A Psalm of David: An Appeal to God Amidst Malicious Accusation

1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, do not be silent, 2 for the mouths of the wicked and the deceitful have opened against me; they have spoken against me with lying tongues. 3 They encircle me with words of hatred and assail me without cause. 4 In return for my love they accuse me, though I remain devoted to prayer. 5 They repay me evil for good, and hatred for my love.

Imprecations Against the Unrighteous Adversary

6 Appoint a wicked man over him, and let an accuser stand at his right hand. 7 When he is brought to judgment, let him emerge condemned, and let his prayer be reckoned as sin. 8 May his days be few, and may another take his office. 9 May his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow. 10 May his children wander as beggars and implore alms; may they seek sustenance from their ruined homes. 11 May the creditor seize all that he possesses, and may strangers plunder the fruit of his toil. 12 May no one extend mercy to him, nor show favor to his fatherless children. 13 May his posterity be cut off, and may their name be blotted out in the next generation.

14 May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and may the sin of his mother never be blotted out. 15 May they stand perpetually before the LORD, that He may cut off their memory from the earth. 16 For he did not remember to show kindness, but pursued the poor and needy and the brokenhearted, seeking to put them to death. 17 Because he loved cursing, may it come upon him; because he took no delight in blessing, may it remain far from him. 18 He clothed himself in cursing as in a garment; may it enter his inward parts like water and penetrate his bones like oil. 19 May it be to him like the cloak he wears and like the belt he constantly fastens around himself. 20 May this be the LORD’s retribution to my accusers, to those who speak evil against my life.

A Plea for Divine Mercy and Vindication

21 But You, O the LORD, the Lord, deal graciously with me for the sake of Your name; because Your steadfast love is good, deliver me. 22 For I am poor and needy, and my heart is stricken within me. 23 I vanish like a shadow at its lengthening; I am shaken off like a locust. 24 My knees grow weak from fasting, and my flesh has grown lean for lack of oil. 25 I have become a reproach to them; when they see me, they shake their heads. 26 Help me, O the LORD my God; save me according to Your lovingkindness. 27 Let them know that this is Your hand, that You, O the LORD, have done it. 28 They may curse, but You will bless; when they rise up, they will be put to shame, while Your servant rejoices. 29 May my accusers be clothed with disgrace and wrapped in their own shame as in a robe.

Praise for the LORD’s Faithful Deliverance

30 With my mouth I will give great thanks to the LORD; I will praise Him in the midst of the multitude. 31 For He stands at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those who condemn his soul.
