David Assembles Israel’s Leaders

1 David assembled all the officials of Israel at Jerusalem: the officials of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions serving the king, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, together with the court officials, the mighty men, and all the valiant warriors.

David’s Address to the Assembly and to Solomon

2 David rose to his feet and said, “Hear me, my brothers and my people. I had it in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and for the footstool of our God, and I made preparations to build. 3 But God said to me, ‘You shall not build a house for My name, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.’ 4 Yet the LORD, the God of Israel, chose me from all my father’s house to be king over Israel forever. For He chose Judah as leader, and from the house of Judah He chose my father’s house, and from my father’s sons He was pleased to make me king over all Israel. 5 And of all my sons—for the LORD has given me many sons—He has chosen my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the kingdom of the LORD over Israel.

“6 He said to me, ‘Solomon your son is the one who will build My house and My courts, for I have chosen him to be My son, and I will be his Father. 7 I will establish his kingdom forever, if he is steadfast to observe My commandments and My ordinances, as he is this day.’ 8 So now, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and in the hearing of our God, observe and seek out all the commandments of the LORD your God, so that you may possess the good land and leave it as an inheritance to your children after you forever. 9 And you, Solomon my son, know the God of your father, and serve Him with a whole heart and a willing mind, for the LORD searches all hearts and understands the intent of every thought. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will reject you forever. 10 Consider now that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it.”

The Temple Plans and Their Divine Origin

11 Then David gave to his son Solomon the plan of the portico and its buildings, its treasuries, its upper rooms, its inner chambers, and the room for the mercy seat; 12 and the plan of all that he had in mind for the courts of the house of the LORD, for all the surrounding chambers, for the treasuries of the house of God, and for the treasuries of the dedicated things; 13 and for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, and for all the work of the service of the house of the LORD, and for all the utensils of service in the house of the LORD.

14 for the gold by weight for all the articles for every kind of service, and for all the silver articles by weight for every kind of service; 15 the weight of the gold lampstands and their lamps, including the weight of each lampstand and its lamps; and the weight of the silver lampstands and their lamps, according to the use of each lampstand; 16 the weight of gold for the tables of the showbread, for each table, and the weight of silver for the silver tables; 17 the pure gold for the forks, the bowls, and the pitchers; the weight of each gold dish; and the silver for each silver dish and its kind.

18 and for the altar of incense, refined gold by weight; and gold for the chariot of the cherubim that spread their wings and overshadow the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

19 “All this,” David said, “The LORD made me understand in writing by His hand upon me, all the works of this pattern.”

David’s Final Charge to Solomon

20 David said to Solomon, “Be strong and courageous, and do it. Do not be afraid or dismayed, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not leave you or forsake you until all the work for the service of the house of the LORD is finished. 21 And behold, the divisions of the priests and the Levites are for all the service of the house of God, and with you in all the work will be every willing man skilled in any craft; also the officials and all the people will be at your every command.”
