The Proverbs of Solomon: Wisdom, Folly, and the Moral Order of Daily Life

1 The proverbs of Solomon: a prudent son gladdens his father, whereas a foolish son brings sorrow to his mother. 2 Treasures amassed through wickedness yield no enduring benefit, but righteousness rescues from death. 3 The LORD will not allow the righteous to hunger, yet He frustrates the craving of the wicked. 4 A slack hand begets poverty, while the diligent hand acquires wealth. 5 A son who gathers during summer acts prudently, but a son who slumbers through harvest brings disgrace.

Blessing, Reputation, and the Power of Speech

6 Blessings rest upon the head of the righteous, whereas the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. 7 The remembrance of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked decays. 8 A wise-hearted person accepts commandments, but a prating fool is overthrown. 9 Whoever walks in integrity proceeds securely, but whoever twists his ways will be exposed. 10 One who winks with the eye brings grief, and a prating fool will be overthrown.

The Tongue, Love, and the Economy of Character

11 The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. 12 Hatred incites contention, but love overlooks all offenses. 13 Wisdom is found upon the lips of the discerning, but a rod is reserved for the back of one devoid of understanding. 14 The wise store up knowledge, but the mouth of a fool hastens ruin. 15 A rich man's wealth is his stronghold, but the poverty of the poor is their ruin.

Work, Speech, and the Blessing of the LORD

16 The work of the righteous leads to life, whereas the gain of the wicked leads to sin. 17 Whoever keeps instruction walks in the way of life, but whoever forsakes correction wanders into error. 18 Whoever conceals hatred speaks with deceitful lips, and whoever broadcasts slander is a fool. 19 Where words abound, transgression is scarcely absent; but whoever restrains his lips acts prudently. 20 The tongue of the righteous is as choice silver, but the heart of the wicked is worth little. 21 The lips of the righteous sustain many, but fools perish for want of discernment. 22 The blessing of the LORD makes rich, and He adds no grief to it.

The Fate of the Wicked and the Security of the Righteous

23 To a fool, doing wrong is like sport, but wisdom delights a person of understanding. 24 What the wicked dread will befall them, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. 25 When the tempest sweeps by, the wicked vanish, but the righteous stand as an everlasting foundation. 26 As vinegar sets the teeth on edge and smoke irritates the eyes, so is the sluggard to those who dispatch him. 27 Reverence for the LORD lengthens days, but the years of the wicked are truncated. 28 The hope of the righteous is joy, but the expectation of the wicked will perish. 29 The LORD's way is a refuge for the upright, but destruction for those who practice iniquity. 30 The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked shall not inhabit the land. 31 The mouth of the righteous yields wisdom, but the perverse tongue will be cut off. 32 The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, but the mouth of the wicked speaks perversity.
