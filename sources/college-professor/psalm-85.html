A Psalm of Restoration, Reconciliation, and Renewed Divine Favor

1 To the choirmaster—a psalm of the sons of Korah: You were propitious toward Your land, O the LORD, and You restored Jacob’s fortunes; 2 You bore away the iniquity of Your people and veiled all their transgressions. Selah. 3 You gathered up all Your wrath and averted the fierceness of Your anger.

4 Restore us again, O God of our salvation, and put away Your indignation toward us. 5 Will You be indignant with us forever, and will You prolong Your wrath from generation to generation? 6 Will You not revive us again, so that Your people may exult in You? 7 Show us Your steadfast love, O the LORD, and grant us Your salvation.

8 I will listen to what God the the LORD speaks, for He will speak peace to His people and to His faithful ones, lest they relapse into folly. 9 Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that glory may dwell in our land. 10 Steadfast love and faithfulness have met; righteousness and peace have kissed. 11 Faithfulness springs from the earth, while righteousness looks down from heaven. 12 Indeed, the the LORD will give what is good, and our land will yield its harvest. 13 Righteousness goes before Him and prepares a way for His steps.
