The Restoration of Israel and the Circumcision of the Heart

1 When all these things have come upon you—the blessing and the curse that I have set before you—and you call them to mind among all the nations to which the LORD your God has driven you, 2 you and your children shall return to the LORD your God and obey His voice with all your heart and all your soul, in accordance with all that I command you this day.

3 Then the LORD your God will restore you from captivity and have compassion on you, gathering you again from all the peoples among whom the LORD your God has scattered you. 4 Even if your exiles are at the ends of heaven, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there He will take you. 5 the LORD your God will bring you into the land your fathers possessed, and you shall possess it; He will prosper you and multiply you beyond your fathers.

6 the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your descendants, so that you love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, and thereby live. 7 the LORD your God will place all these curses upon your enemies and those who hate you, who have persecuted you.

8 You shall turn again and obey the voice of the LORD, doing all His commandments that I command you this day. 9 the LORD your God will make you abundantly prosperous in all the work of your hands, in the fruit of your womb, your livestock, and your soil, for good; for the LORD will once more rejoice over you for good, as He rejoiced over your fathers. 10 This is contingent upon your obeying the voice of the LORD your God by keeping His commandments and statutes written in this Book of the Law, and upon your returning to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.

The Immediacy and Accessibility of the Commandment

11 For this commandment that I am commanding you today is not too abstruse for you, nor is it remote. 12 It is not in heaven, that you should say, "Who will ascend to heaven for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?" 13 Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, "Who will cross the sea for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?" 14 Rather, the word is very near you—in your mouth and in your heart—so that you may do it.

The Solemn Election Between Life and Death

15 See, I have set before you today life and good, death and evil. 16 If you heed what I command you today—loving the LORD your God, walking in His ways, and keeping His commandments, statutes, and judgments—then you shall live and multiply, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are entering to possess. 17 But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are drawn away to bow down to other gods and serve them,

18 I declare to you today that you shall surely perish. You shall not prolong your days upon the land that you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess. 19 I call heaven and earth to witness against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse; therefore choose life, that you and your descendants may live, 20 by loving the LORD your God, obeying His voice, and clinging to Him, for He is your life and the length of your days, that you may dwell in the land that the LORD swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—to give them.
