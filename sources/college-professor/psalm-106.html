A Communal Hymn of Thanksgiving, Confession, and Supplication

1 Hallelujah. Render thanksgiving unto the LORD, for He is benevolent; indeed, His covenantal lovingkindness persists in perpetuity. 2 Who possesses the eloquence to articulate the LORD’s mighty deeds, or to proclaim the entirety of His praise? 3 Blessed are they who uphold judicial equity and practice righteousness in every season. 4 Remember me, O the LORD, in Your favor toward Your people; visit me with Your salvation, 5 that I might behold the felicity of Your elect, exult in the jubilation of Your nation, and triumph alongside Your heritage.

6 We have sinned in concert with our forebears; we have wrought iniquity; we have acted with consummate wickedness. 7 Our forebears in Egypt did not apprehend Your marvelous deeds; they failed to remember the plenitude of Your mercy, and instead rebelled at the Red Sea. 8 Nevertheless, He delivered them for the sake of His name, to make manifest His omnipotence. 9 He rebuked the Red Sea, so that it desiccated, and He conducted them through the abyss as though through a desert. 10 Thus He rescued them from the grasp of the adversary and ransomed them from the power of the enemy. 11 The waters overwhelmed their oppressors, so that not a single one survived. 12 Then they gave credence to His utterances and intoned His praise.

13 Yet they swiftly forgot His deeds and refused to await His counsel. 14 Rather, they indulged a licentious craving in the wilderness and subjected God to trial in the wasteland. 15 He granted their petition, yet dispatched a wasting sickness into their souls. 16 When envy arose in the encampment against Moses and against Aaron, the holy one of the LORD, 17 the earth yawned open and engulfed Dathan, then concealed the assembly of Abiram. 18 Moreover, a conflagration erupted among their company, and the blaze devoured the wicked. 19 At Horeb they fashioned a calf and prostrated themselves before a molten image. 20 They bartered away their glory for the likeness of a grass-consuming ox. 21 They forgot God, their Savior, who had wrought stupendous deeds in Egypt, 22 marvelous works in the land of Ham, and terrifying wonders at the Red Sea. 23 Consequently, He declared that He would annihilate them—had not Moses, His elect, stationed himself in the breach before Him to avert His wrath from their destruction. 24 Thereafter they disdained the desirable land, placing no confidence in His promise. 25 They grumbled within their tents and refused to heed the voice of the LORD. 26 Therefore He lifted His hand and swore that He would cause them to perish in the wilderness, 27 and that He would cast their seed among the nations, dispersing them throughout foreign territories. 28 Then they yoked themselves to Baal of Peor and partook of sacrifices consecrated to the dead. 29 By their practices they provoked the LORD to wrath, and a pestilence erupted in their midst. 30 Then Phinehas arose and interposed, and the plague was arrested. 31 And this was reckoned unto him as righteousness for all generations in perpetuity. 32 They provoked Him at the waters of Meribah, and Moses suffered affliction on their account, 33 because they embittered his spirit, so that he uttered precipitous words with his lips.

34 They failed to exterminate the peoples, as the LORD had commanded them, 35 but instead intermingled with the nations and assimilated their practices. 36 They venerated their idols, which devolved into a snare for them. 37 They immolated their sons and daughters unto demons. 38 They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters—whom they offered to the idols of Canaan, so that the land was defiled by bloodshed. 39 Thus they were polluted by their conduct and prostituted themselves through their practices. 40 Then the wrath of the LORD was inflamed against His people, and He abominated His inheritance; 41 He delivered them into the hand of the nations, so that their adversaries exercised dominion over them. 42 Their enemies subjugated and afflicted them, reducing them to submission beneath their power. 43 On numerous occasions He rescued them, yet they persisted in rebellious counsel and were debased by their iniquity. 44 Nevertheless, He regarded their affliction when He heard their supplication. 45 For their sake He remembered His covenant and relented in accordance with the magnitude of His mercy. 46 He also caused them to find compassion in the sight of all their captors.

47 Deliver us, O the LORD our God, and assemble us from among the nations, that we may render thanksgiving to Your holy name and exult in Your praise. 48 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from eternity unto eternity! And let all the people declare, “Amen!” Hallelujah!
