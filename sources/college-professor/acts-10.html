Cornelius’s Vision and the Summons to Peter

1 In Caesarea there dwelt a certain man named Cornelius, a centurion of the cohort styled Italian, 2 together with his entire household: a devout and God-fearing man, who wrought many alms among the people and petitioned God continually. 3 About the ninth hour of the day he beheld plainly, in a vision, an angel of God entering unto him and saying, “Cornelius.”

4 And he, having fixed his gaze upon him and being seized with fear, said, “What is it, Lord?”

And he said to him, “Your prayers and your alms have ascended as a memorial before God. 5 And now send men to Joppa and summon a certain Simon who is called Peter. 6 This man is lodging with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea.”

7 And when the angel who had been speaking to him had departed, he called two of his servants and a devout soldier from among those who attended him, 8 and, having rehearsed all these things to them, dispatched them to Joppa.

Peter’s Vision and the Messengers from Caesarea

9 The next day, as they were drawing near to the city while on their journey, Peter went up onto the housetop about the sixth hour to pray.

10 And he became hungry and desired to eat; but while they were preparing it, a trance fell upon him.

11 And he beholds heaven opened and a certain vessel descending, like a great sheet let down to the earth by four corners. 12 In it were all kinds of four-footed beasts, creeping things of the earth, and birds of heaven.

13 And a voice came to him: “Rise, Peter; kill and eat.”

14 But Peter said, “By no means, Lord; for I have never eaten anything common or unclean.”

15 And a voice came to him again a second time: “What God has cleansed, you must not treat as common.”

16 And this occurred thrice; and straightway the vessel was taken up into heaven.

17 Now while Peter was inwardly perplexed as to what the vision he had seen might signify, behold, the men sent by Cornelius, having inquired for Simon’s house, stood at the gate. 18 And, calling out, they asked whether Simon who is called Peter was lodging there.

19 And while Peter was musing upon the vision, the Spirit said to him, “Behold, three men seek you. 20 But arise, go down, and journey with them, doubting nothing, for I have sent them.”

21 And Peter, having gone down to the men, said, “Behold, I am the one you seek. What is the cause for which you are here?”

22 And they said, “Cornelius, a centurion—a righteous and God-fearing man, well testified to by the whole nation of the Jews—was divinely instructed by a holy angel to summon you to his house and to hear words from you.”

23 So, having invited them in, he lodged them. And on the morrow Peter arose and went forth with them, and certain of the brethren from Joppa accompanied him.

Peter Received in Cornelius’s House

24 And on the following day he entered Caesarea. Now Cornelius was expecting them, having called together his kinsmen and intimate friends.

25 And as Peter was about to enter, Cornelius met him and fell at his feet and worshiped.

26 But Peter raised him, saying, “Rise; I myself also am a man.”

27 And conversing with him, he entered and found many assembled.

28 And he said to them, “You know how unlawful it is for a Jewish man to associate with or approach one of another nation; yet God has shown me that I should call no man common or unclean. 29 Wherefore, having been summoned, I came without gainsaying. I ask, therefore, for what reason you summoned me.”

30 And Cornelius said, “Four days ago, up to this very hour, I was in my house praying at the ninth hour, and, behold, a man stood before me in dazzling raiment, and said,

31 “‘Cornelius, your prayer has been heard and your alms remembered before God. 32 Send therefore to Joppa and summon Simon who is called Peter; this man lodges in the house of Simon the tanner by the sea.’

33 “So I sent to you at once, and you did well to come. Now, therefore, we are all present before God to hear all things that have been commanded you by the Lord.”

Peter’s Proclamation and the Outpouring of the Spirit

34 And Peter, opening his mouth, said, “In truth I now perceive that God shows no partiality. 35 But in every nation he who fears Him and works righteousness is acceptable to Him. 36 He sent the word to the sons of Israel, preaching peace through Jesus Christ—He is Lord of all.

37 “You know the word that went forth throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism that John proclaimed, 38 how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for God was with Him. 39 And we are witnesses of all that He did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem, whom they killed by hanging Him on a cross. 40 God raised Him up on the third day and granted that He should be made manifest,

41 “not to all the people, but to witnesses chosen beforehand by God—to us, who ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead. 42 And He commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that He is the One ordained by God to be Judge of the living and the dead. 43 To Him all the prophets bear witness, that every one who believes in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name.”

44 While Peter was yet speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all those who heard the word. 45 And the believers of the circumcision who had come with Peter were amazed, because the gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even upon the Gentiles.

46 For they heard them speaking in tongues and magnifying God. Then Peter answered, 47 “Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit just as we have?”

48 And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to tarry certain days.
