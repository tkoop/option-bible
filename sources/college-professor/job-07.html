The Frailty of Mortal Existence

1 Is not human existence upon earth an arduous campaign, and are not one’s days like those of a hired laborer? 2 Like a bondservant panting for the shade, and like a wage-earner awaiting his stipend, 3 I have been bequeathed months of futility, and nights of affliction have been apportioned to me. 4 When I lie down, I think, “When shall I arise?” The night drags on, and I am saturated with restlessness until dawn.

5 My flesh is arrayed in worms and clods of dust; my skin crusts over, only to fester anew. 6 My days pass more swiftly than a weaver’s shuttle, and they are consumed in hopelessness.

7 Remember that my life is but a breath; my eye will never again behold prosperity. 8 The eye that now beholds me will behold me no more; when Your eyes seek me, I shall be no more. 9 As a cloud dissipates and passes away, so he who descends to Sheol does not ascend again. 10 He shall never return to his house, nor shall his dwelling place acknowledge him again.

Job’s Complaint Against Divine Scrutiny

11 Accordingly, I shall not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit, and I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. 12 Am I the sea, or some leviathan, that You should station a guard over me? 13 When I say, “My bed will comfort me; my couch will bear my lament,” 14 then You frighten me with dreams and terrify me with visions, 15 so that my very soul prefers strangulation and death rather than these bones.

16 I despise it; I would not live forever. Leave me alone, for my days are but a breath.

17 What is man that You should magnify him, and that You should set Your heart upon him, 18 that You visit him each morning and test him every instant? 19 How long will You not look away from me, nor release me even long enough to swallow my saliva? 20 If I have sinned, what have I done to You, O Watcher of men? Why have You made me Your mark, so that I have become a burden to myself? 21 And why do You not pardon my transgression and remove my iniquity? For now I shall lie down in the dust; You will seek me, but I shall be no more.
