The Prophet Amos and the Word Concerning Israel

1 The words of Amos, who was among the shepherds of Tekoa, which he beheld concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel—two years before the earthquake.

The LORD Roars from Zion

2 And he said, “The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem; the pastures of the shepherds mourn, and the summit of Carmel withers.”

Oracles Against Damascus, Gaza, and Tyre

3 Thus says the LORD: “For three transgressions of Damascus—and indeed for four—I will not revoke its punishment, because they threshed Gilead with iron threshing sledges. 4 So I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it will devour the citadels of Ben-hadad. 5 I will shatter the gate-bar of Damascus, cut off the inhabitant from the Valley of Aven, and the scepter-bearer from Beth-eden; and the people of Aram shall be exiled to Kir,” declares the LORD.

6 Thus says the LORD: “For three transgressions of Gaza—and indeed for four—I will not revoke its punishment, because they deported an entire population in order to hand them over to Edom. 7 So I will send fire against the wall of Gaza, and it will consume her citadels. 8 I will cut off the inhabitant from Ashdod and the scepter-bearer from Ashkelon; I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish,” declares the Lord the LORD.

9 Thus says the LORD: “For three transgressions of Tyre—and indeed for four—I will not revoke its punishment, because they delivered up an entire captivity to Edom and did not remember the covenant of brotherhood. 10 So I will send fire against the wall of Tyre, and it will consume her citadels,” declares the LORD.

Oracles Against Edom and Ammon

11 Thus says the LORD: “For three transgressions of Edom—and indeed for four—I will not revoke its punishment, because he pursued his brother with the sword and stifled his compassion; his anger raged continually, and his wrath he nursed forever. 12 So I will send fire upon Teman, and it will devour the palaces of Bozrah,” declares the LORD.

13 Thus says the LORD: “For three transgressions of the Ammonites—and indeed for four—I will not revoke its punishment, because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead in order to enlarge their territory. 14 So I will kindle a fire in the wall of Rabbah, and it will devour her palaces, amid shouting on the day of battle and a storm on the day of the whirlwind. 15 And their king shall go into exile, he and his princes together,” declares the LORD.
