Prophecy Against Israel's False Prophets

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2 "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who prophesy, and say to those who prophesy from their own heart, 'Hear the word of the LORD:'"

3 Thus says the Lord the LORD: "Woe to the fatuous prophets who follow their own spirit, though they have seen nothing. 4 O Israel, your prophets have been like foxes among ruins. 5 You have not ascended into the breaches, nor erected a wall for the house of Israel, that it might stand in battle on the day of the LORD."

6 "They have envisioned vanity and deceit, and say, 'The The LORD declares,' though the LORD has not sent them, and they have caused the people to hope that the word will be confirmed. 7 Did you not see a vain vision and utter a deceitful divination, saying, 'The The LORD declares,' although I have not spoken?"

8 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: "Because you have spoken vanity and seen deceit, therefore I am against you," declares the Lord the LORD. 9 "My hand will be against the prophets who envision vanity and deceit; they shall not be in the council of My people, nor be inscribed in the register of the house of Israel, nor shall they enter the land of Israel; and you shall know that I am the Lord the LORD."

The Whitewashed Wall and the Coming Storm

10 "Because, indeed because they have led My people astray, saying, 'Peace,' when there is no peace; and when one builds a wall, they plaster it with whitewash. 11 Say to those who plaster it with whitewash that it will collapse: a deluging rain will come, and you, O hailstones, will fall, and a tempestuous wind will rend it apart. 12 Behold, when the wall collapses, will it not be said to you, 'Where is the plaster with which you plastered it?'"

13 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: "In My wrath I will let loose a tempestuous wind; in My anger a deluging rain and hailstones will come to destroy. 14 I will demolish the wall that you plastered with whitewash and bring it down to the ground, to its foundation, so that it is laid bare; it will collapse, and you will be consumed in its midst; and you will know that I am the LORD. 15 I will expend My wrath upon the wall and upon those who plastered it with whitewash, and I will say to you, 'The wall is no more, and those who plastered it are no more'— 16 namely, the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem and envision peace for her, but there is no peace," declares the Lord the LORD.

Judgment on the False Prophetesses

17 "And you, Son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own heart, and prophesy against them, 18 and say, 'Thus says the Lord the LORD: Woe to the women who sew magic bands upon all wrists and fashion veils for heads of every stature, to ensnare souls! Will you ensnare the souls of My people and preserve your own souls alive? 19 You have profaned Me among My people for handfuls of barley and pieces of bread, by your lying to My people who give heed to lies, to put to death souls that should not die and to keep alive souls that should not live.'"

20 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: "Behold, I am against your magic bands with which you ensnare souls there like birds, and I will tear them from your arms, and I will release the souls that you ensnare like birds. 21 I will tear away your veils and deliver My people from your hand, and they will no longer be prey in your hand; and you will know that I am the LORD. 22 Because you have grieved the heart of the righteous with falsehood, although I did not grieve him, and you have strengthened the hands of the wicked so that he does not turn from his wicked way to save his life, 23 therefore you will no longer see vain visions nor practice divination; and I will deliver My people from your hand, and you will know that I am the LORD."
