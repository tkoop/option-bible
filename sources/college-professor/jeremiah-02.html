The LORD’s Remembrance of Israel’s Early Devotion

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2 “Go and proclaim in the hearing of Jerusalem: Thus says the LORD: I remember you—the devotion of your youth, the love of your betrothals, your following after Me in the wilderness, in a land not sown. 3 Israel is holy to the LORD, the firstfruits of His harvest; all who devour him shall be held guilty, and calamity shall come upon them, declares the LORD.”

The Charge of Apostasy Against the House of Jacob

4 Hear the word of the LORD, O house of Jacob, and all the families of the house of Israel.

5 Thus says the LORD: “What wrong did your fathers find in Me, that they went far from Me and walked after emptiness and became empty? 6 They did not say, ‘Where is the LORD who brought us up from the land of Egypt, who led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and pits, through a land of drought and of the shadow of death, through a land through which no one passed and in which no one dwelt?’ 7 I brought you into a land of abundant yield to eat its fruit and its goodness; but you came and defiled My land, and made My heritage an abomination. 8 The priests did not say, ‘Where is the LORD?’ and those who handle the law did not know Me; the shepherds transgressed against Me; and the prophets prophesied by Baal and walked after what does not profit.

“9 Therefore I will still contend with you, declares the LORD; I will even contend with your children’s children. 10 Cross over to the coasts of Kittim and see; send to Kedar and consider carefully; see whether such a thing has ever happened. 11 Has a nation ever exchanged its gods, though they are no gods? But My people have exchanged their Glory for what does not profit. 12 Be appalled at this, O heavens, and shudder with great horror; be utterly desolate, declares the LORD. 13 For My people have committed two evils: they have forsaken Me, the fountain of living waters, and hewn out cisterns for themselves—broken cisterns that can hold no water.

The Futility of Foreign Alliances and Idolatry

“14 Is Israel a servant? Is he a home-born slave? Why has he become plunder? 15 Young lions have roared against him; they have raised their voices. They have made his land a waste; his cities are burned and without inhabitant. 16 Moreover, the men of Noph and Tahpanhes have ravaged the crown of your head. 17 Have you not brought this upon yourself by forsaking the LORD your God when He led you in the way? 18 And now what have you to do with the way of Egypt, to drink the waters of the Shihor? Or what have you to do with the way of Assyria, to drink the waters of the River? 19 Your own wickedness will discipline you, and your backslidings will rebuke you. Know then and see that it is an evil and bitter thing that you have forsaken the LORD your God and that you have no fear of Me, declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

“20 For long ago I broke your yoke and tore off your bonds, but you said, ‘I will not serve!’ For upon every high hill and under every green tree you bowed down, prostituting yourself. 21 Yet I planted you as a choice vine, a seed of utmost integrity. How then have you turned degenerate before Me into a wild vine? 22 For though you wash yourself with lye and use much soap, yet your guilt is stained before Me, declares the Lord GOD. 23 How can you say, ‘I am not defiled; I have not gone after the Baals’? Look at your way in the valley; know what you have done. You are a swift young camel, restlessly tracing her paths. 24 A wild donkey accustomed to the wilderness, sniffing the wind in her craving—who can restrain her lust? All who seek her will not weary themselves; in her month they will find her. 25 Keep your feet from going bare and your throat from thirst. But you said, ‘It is hopeless! No, for I have loved strangers, and after them I will go.’ 26 As a thief is ashamed when he is caught, so is the house of Israel ashamed—they, their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets— 27 saying to a tree, ‘You are my father,’ and to a stone, ‘You gave birth to me.’ For they have turned their back to Me and not their face; yet in the time of their trouble they say, ‘Arise and save us!’ 28 But where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them arise, if they can save you in your time of trouble; for according to the number of your cities are your gods, O Judah.

Judgment Pronounced Upon a Rebellious Generation

“29 Why will you contend with Me? You have all transgressed against Me, declares the LORD. 30 In vain I have struck your children; they accepted no discipline. Your own sword has devoured your prophets as a ravaging lion. 31 O generation, behold the word of the LORD: Have I been a wilderness to Israel, or a land of deep darkness? Why do My people say, ‘We are free; we will come to You no more’? 32 Can a virgin forget her ornaments, or a bride her wedding attire? Yet My people have forgotten Me for days without number. 33 How skillfully do you arrange your way to seek love! Therefore you have also taught the wicked women your ways. 34 Also on your skirts is found the blood of innocent lives; I did not find it by digging, but it is upon all these. 35 Yet you say, ‘I am innocent; surely His anger has turned from me.’ Behold, I will enter into judgment with you, because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’ 36 Why do you wander so far to change your way? You shall be ashamed of Egypt as you were ashamed of Assyria. 37 From there also you will go out with your hands on your head, for the LORD has rejected those in whom you trusted, and you will not prosper through them.”
