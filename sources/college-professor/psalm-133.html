The Felicity of Fraternal Concord

1 A Song of Ascents, by David. Behold, how salutary and how delightful it is when brethren abide together in concord! 2 It is comparable to the precious oil upon the head, cascading down upon the beard—even Aaron's beard—descending to the collar of his garments. 3 It is comparable to the dew of Hermon descending upon the mountains of Zion; for there the the LORD ordained the blessing, even life unto eternity.
