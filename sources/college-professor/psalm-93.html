The LORD's Majestic Reign Over Creation

1The LORD reigns; He is robed in majesty. the LORD has girded Himself with strength, so that the world is firmly established and cannot be moved. 2Your throne has been established from of old; You are from everlasting.

The Roaring Waters Subdued by Divine Might

3The rivers have lifted up, O the LORD; the rivers have lifted up their voice; the rivers lift up their roaring. 4Above the roar of many waters, above the mighty breakers of the sea, the LORD on high is mighty.

The Surety of His Testimonies and the Beauty of His House

5Your testimonies are exceedingly sure; holiness adorns Your house, O the LORD, for length of days.
