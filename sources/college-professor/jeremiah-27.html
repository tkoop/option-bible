Jeremiah's Symbolic Yoke and the Nations' Submission

1 In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD: 2 The LORD said to me: Make for yourself straps and yoke-bars, and put them on your neck. 3 Then send them to the king of Edom, to the king of Moab, to the king of the Ammonites, to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon, by the hand of the envoys who have come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah. 4 Give them this charge to deliver to their masters: ‘Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Thus you shall say to your masters:

The LORD's Sovereign Disposition of Nebuchadnezzar

5 ‘“I made the earth—the human being and the beast that are upon the face of the earth—by My great power and by My outstretched arm, and I give it to whomever seems right in My eyes. 6 And now I have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant; I have likewise given him the beasts of the field to serve him. 7 All nations shall serve him, and his son, and his grandson, until the time appointed for his own land arrives; then many nations and great kings shall enslave him. 8 And the nation or kingdom that does not serve him—Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon—and does not put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will punish that nation with sword, famine, and pestilence, declares the LORD, until I have consumed it by his hand.”’

Warning Against False Prophets

9 And as for you, do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your dreamers, your soothsayers, or your sorcerers, who say to you, ‘You shall not serve the king of Babylon.’ 10 For they prophesy to you a lie, in order to remove you far from your land; I will drive you out, and you will perish. 11 But the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will leave it in its own land, declares the LORD, and it shall till the soil and dwell in it.

12 And to Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke in accordance with all these words, saying, ‘Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live.’ 13 Why should you and your people die by sword, famine, and pestilence, as the LORD has spoken concerning the nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?

14 Do not listen to the words of the prophets who say to you, ‘You shall not serve the king of Babylon,’ for they prophesy to you a lie. 15 For I have not sent them, declares the LORD, and they prophesy falsely in My name, to drive you out, so that you will perish—you and the prophets who prophesy to you.

The Fate of the Temple Vessels

16 And to the priests and to all this people I spoke, saying, ‘Thus says the LORD: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, saying, “Behold, the vessels of the house of the LORD are being brought back from Babylon soon,” for they prophesy a lie to you.’

17 Do not listen to them; serve the king of Babylon, and live. Why should this city become a ruin?

18 And if they are prophets, and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them now intercede with the LORD of Hosts, that the vessels left in the house of the LORD, in the house of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem may not be carried away to Babylon.

19 For thus says the LORD of Hosts concerning the pillars, the sea, the stands, and the rest of the vessels that remain in this city, 20 which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon, together with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem. 21 For thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, concerning the vessels that remain in the house of the LORD, in the house of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem: 22 They shall be carried to Babylon, and there they shall remain until the day I attend to them, declares the LORD; then I will bring them up and restore them to this place.
