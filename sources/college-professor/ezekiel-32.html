A Lamentation Over Pharaoh and the Cosmic Judgment of Egypt

1 In the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month, the word of the LORD came to me:

2 “Son of man, raise a lamentation over Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and say to him: You are likened to a lion among the nations, yet you are like a monster in the seas; you thrashed in your rivers, churning the waters with your feet and fouling their streams.”

3 Thus says the Lord the LORD: “I will spread My net over you with a great assembly of peoples, and they shall haul you up in My dragnet.”

4 “I will leave you on the ground and cast you upon the open field; I will cause all the birds of heaven to settle upon you, and all the beasts of the earth to gorge themselves on you.”

5 “I will lay your flesh upon the mountains and fill the valleys with your remains.”

6 “I will drench the land with your flowing blood, as far as the mountains, and the ravines shall be filled from you.”

7 “When I extinguish you, I will cover the heavens and darken their stars; I will veil the sun with a cloud, and the moon shall not give its light.”

8 “All the bright luminaries of heaven I will darken over you, and I will set darkness upon your land, declares the Lord the LORD.”

9 “I will agitate the hearts of many peoples when I bring your ruin among the nations, into lands you have not known.”

10 “Many peoples shall be appalled at you; their kings shall shudder in horror at you when I brandish My sword before them, and on the day of your fall each man shall tremble for his life, moment by moment.”

Babylon’s Sword and Egypt’s Desolation

11 “For thus says the Lord the LORD: The sword of the king of Babylon shall come upon you.”

12 “By the swords of the mighty, all of them the most ruthless of nations, I will bring down your hordes; they shall ravage the pride of Egypt, and all her multitudes shall be destroyed.”

13 “I will destroy all her livestock beside the abundant waters; no human foot shall muddy them again, nor shall the hoof of any beast muddy them.”

14 “Then I will let her waters settle and make her rivers flow like oil, declares the Lord the LORD.”

15 “When I make the land of Egypt a desolation, empty of all that filled it, when I strike down all who dwell in it, then they shall know that I am the LORD.”

16 “This is the lamentation they shall chant over her; the daughters of the nations shall chant it; over Egypt and all her multitudes they shall chant it, declares the Lord the LORD.”

Egypt Consigned to the Pit Among the Nations

17 In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:

18 “Son of man, wail over the multitude of Egypt, and bring her down—her and the daughters of mighty nations—to the underworld, to those who descend to the Pit.”

19 “Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down and be laid to rest with the uncircumcised.”

20 “They shall fall among those slain by the sword; the sword is drawn; let them drag her off with all her multitudes.”

21 “The mighty chiefs shall speak to him from the midst of Sheol with his allies: ‘They have gone down; they lie with the uncircumcised, slain by the sword.’”

22 “Assyria is there with all her company; her graves are all around her; all of them are slain, fallen by the sword.”

23 “Her graves are set in the recesses of the Pit; her company is all around her grave; all of them are slain, fallen by the sword—those who spread terror in the land of the living.”

24 “Elam is there with all her multitudes around her grave; all of them are slain, fallen by the sword—those who went down uncircumcised to the earth below, who spread their terror in the land of the living; they bear their shame with those who descend to the Pit.”

25 “Among the slain they prepare a resting place for her with all her multitudes; her graves are all around her; all of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, though their terror was once spread in the land of the living; they bear their shame with those who descend to the Pit; she is put among the slain.”

26 “Meshech and Tubal are there with all their multitudes, with their graves all around them; all of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, because they spread their terror in the land of the living.”

27 “They do not lie with the fallen warriors of old, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, their swords placed beneath their heads and their shields resting upon their bones, although the terror of the mighty was once in the land of the living.”

28 “But you too shall be shattered and lie down among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword.”

29 “Edom is there, and all her kings and princes, who despite their might are laid among those slain by the sword; they lie with the uncircumcised, with those who descend to the Pit.”

30 “All the leaders of the north and all the Sidonians are there; they went down in disgrace with the slain, despite the terror of their might; they lie uncircumcised with those slain by the sword, and bear their shame with those who descend to the Pit.”

31 “Pharaoh will see them and be comforted over all his multitudes—Pharaoh and all his army, slain by the sword, declares the Lord the LORD.”

32 “For I have put his terror in the land of the living, yet he shall be laid to rest among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword—Pharaoh and all his multitudes, declares the Lord the LORD.”
