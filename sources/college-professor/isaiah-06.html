Isaiah’s Vision of the Lord Enthroned in Glory

1 In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and exalted, and the train of His robe filled the temple. 2 Above Him stood the seraphim; each had six wings: with two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew. 3 And one called to another, saying, “Holy, Holy, Holy is the the LORD of Hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory.” 4 The foundations of the thresholds trembled at the voice of the caller, and the temple was filled with smoke.

The Prophet’s Confession and Purification

5 And I said, “Woe to me, for I am ruined! For I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell among a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the King, the the LORD of Hosts.”

6 Then one of the seraphim flew to me, carrying in his hand a glowing coal that he had taken with tongs from the altar. 7 And he touched my mouth and said, “Behold, this has touched your lips; your iniquity is removed, and your sin is atoned for.”

The Prophet’s Commission and the People’s Judicial Hardening

8 And I heard the voice of the Lord saying, “Whom shall I send, and who will go for Us?”

And I said, “Here am I; send me.”

9 And He said, “Go and tell this people: ‘Hear indeed, but understand not; see indeed, but perceive not.’ 10 Make the heart of this people callous, their ears heavy, and their eyes shut, lest they see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, and turn and be healed.”

11 And I said, “How long, O Lord?”

And He said, “Until cities are laid waste without inhabitant, houses without people, and the land is utterly desolate, 12 and the the LORD has carried the people far away, and great desolation reigns in the midst of the land. 13 And though a tenth remains in it, it will be burned again; like the terebinth and the oak, whose stump remains when they are felled, so the holy seed is its stump.”
