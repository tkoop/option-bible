Divine Ordinances Concerning Vows and Dedications

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

Valuation of Persons Vowed to the LORD

2 “Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: If anyone makes a special vow to the LORD, involving the valuation of persons, 3 then your valuation of a male from twenty to sixty years of age shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary. 4 And if it is a female, your valuation shall be thirty shekels.

5 “If the person is from five to twenty years old, your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels. 6 If the person is from one month to five years old, your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver, and for the female three shekels of silver. 7 And if the person is sixty years old or more, your valuation for the male shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels. 8 But if he is too impoverished to meet your valuation, then he shall present himself before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to what the one making the vow can afford, the priest shall value him.

Dedication of Animals, Dwellings, and Fields

9 “If the vowed gift is an animal that may be brought as an offering to the LORD, everything of it that is given to the LORD shall be holy. 10 He shall not exchange it or substitute for it, good for bad or bad for good; and if he does substitute one animal for another, then both it and its substitute shall be holy.

11 “If it is any unclean animal that may not be offered as an offering to the LORD, then he shall present the animal before the priest. 12 And the priest shall value it, whether it is good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall be. 13 But if he wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth to your valuation.

14 “When a man dedicates his house as holy to the LORD, the priest shall value it, whether it is good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall stand. 15 And if he who dedicated it wishes to redeem his house, he shall add a fifth of the valuation in silver, and it shall become his again.

16 “If a man dedicates to the LORD part of the field of his possession, then your valuation shall be in proportion to its seed: a homer of barley seed shall be valued at fifty shekels of silver. 17 If he dedicates his field from the Year of Jubilee, according to your valuation it shall stand. 18 But if he dedicates his field after the Jubilee, then the priest shall calculate the price for him according to the years remaining until the Year of Jubilee, and a deduction shall be made from your valuation. 19 And if he who dedicated the field wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth of the value in silver, and it shall be restored to him.

20 “But if he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any further. 21 But when the field is released in the Jubilee, it shall be holy to the LORD, like a devoted field; the priest shall take possession of it. 22 If he dedicates to the LORD a field which he has bought, which is not of the field of his possession, 23 then the priest shall calculate for him the amount of your valuation up to the Year of Jubilee, and he shall give the valuation on that day as holy to the LORD. 24 In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to him from whom it was bought, to whom the land belonged as a possession. 25 Every valuation shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs to the shekel.

Firstborn Animals, Devoted Things, and Tithes

26 “But the firstborn among animals, which as a firstborn belongs to the LORD, no one shall dedicate; whether ox or sheep, it belongs to the LORD. 27 And if it is an unclean animal, then he shall redeem it according to your valuation, and shall add a fifth to it; or if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation. 28 But anything that a man devotes to the LORD, of all that he has, whether man or beast, or of the field of his possession, shall not be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to the LORD. 29 No person devoted to destruction among humankind shall be redeemed; he shall surely be put to death.

30 “Every tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the trees, is the LORD's; it is holy to the LORD. 31 If a man redeems any of his tithe, he shall add a fifth to it. 32 And every tithe of the herd or flock, every tenth that passes under the rod, shall be holy to the LORD. 33 He shall not inquire whether it is good or bad, nor shall he exchange it; and if he does exchange it, then both it and its substitute shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.”

34 These are the commandments which the LORD commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai.
