A Song of Thanksgiving for Deliverance

1 A Psalm. A Song for the Dedication of the House. Of David. I will exalt You, O the LORD, for You have lifted me up, and have not allowed my enemies to rejoice over me. 2 O the LORD my God, unto You I cried, and You healed me. 3 O the LORD, You brought my soul up from Sheol; You spared me from descending into the Pit.

4 Sing unto the LORD, O His saints, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness. 5 For His anger endures but a moment, whereas His favor lasts a lifetime; weeping may tarry through the evening, but joy arrives in the morning.

The Danger of Self-Sufficient Prosperity

6 In my prosperity I said, "I shall never be moved."

A Plea for Mercy in Affliction

7 O the LORD, by Your favor You established my mountain as a stronghold; You hid Your face, and I was dismayed. 8 Unto You, O the LORD, I cry; to the Lord I make supplication. 9 What profit is there in my blood, in my descent to the Pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness? 10 Hear, O the LORD, and be gracious to me; O the LORD, be my helper.

From Mourning to Joyful Praise

11 You have turned my mourning into dancing; You have loosed my sackcloth and girded me with gladness, 12 so that my glory may sing praise to You and not be silent. O the LORD my God, I will give thanks to You forever.
