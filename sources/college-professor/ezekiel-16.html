Jerusalem’s Desolate Birth and Divine Adoption

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2 “Son of man, make known to Jerusalem her abominations, 3 and say, Thus says the Lord the LORD to Jerusalem: Your origin and your birth are from the land of the Canaanites; your father was an Amorite and your mother a Hittite.

4 “On the day you were born, your umbilical cord was not cut, you were not bathed in water to cleanse you, you were not rubbed with salt, nor were you wrapped in swaddling cloths. 5 No eye looked upon you with pity to do any of these things for you out of compassion; rather, you were cast out into the open field, abhorred on the day of your birth. 6 When I passed by you and saw you wallowing in your blood, I said to you, ‘Live!’—yes, I said to you in your blood, ‘Live!’ 7 I made you flourish like a plant of the field; you grew and became tall, and you attained full adornment. Your breasts were formed and your hair grew, yet you remained naked and bare.

8 “When I passed by you again and saw you, your time was the time of love. I took you to Myself in marriage and covered your nakedness; I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares the Lord the LORD, and you became Mine. 9 Then I bathed you with water, washed your blood from you, and anointed you with oil. 10 I clothed you in embroidered cloth, shod you with fine leather, bound a linen turban about your head, and covered you with silk. 11 I adorned you with ornaments, placed bracelets upon your wrists, and hung a chain about your neck. 12 I put a ring in your nose, earrings in your ears, and a splendid crown upon your head. 13 Thus you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was of fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey, and oil, and you grew exceedingly beautiful, advancing to royalty. 14 Your renown went out among the nations because of your beauty, for it was perfect through the splendor I had bestowed upon you, declares the Lord the LORD.

The City’s Adulterous Betrayal and Idolatrous Prostitution

15 “But you trusted in your beauty and prostituted yourself because of your renown, lavishing your harlotries upon every passerby; your beauty became theirs. 16 You took some of your garments and made for yourself colorful shrines, and upon them you prostituted yourself—such a thing had never occurred and never shall again. 17 You also took your beautiful jewels, My gold and My silver that I had given you, and made for yourself male images, and with them you prostituted yourself. 18 You took your embroidered garments and covered them, and you set My oil and My incense before them. 19 My bread too—the fine flour, oil, and honey with which I fed you—you set before them as a pleasing aroma, declares the Lord the LORD.

20 “You took your sons and your daughters, whom you had borne to Me, and sacrificed them to be devoured. Were your acts of prostitution so insignificant 21 that you slaughtered My children and delivered them as burnt offerings to these idols? 22 In all your abominations and harlotries, you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, wallowing in your blood.

23 “After all your wickedness—woe, woe to you, declares the Lord the LORD— 24 you built yourself a vaulted chamber and erected a lofty shrine in every square. 25 At the head of every street you built your lofty shrine, rendered your beauty an abomination, and offered yourself to every passerby, multiplying your prostitution.

26 “You prostituted yourself to the Egyptians, your lustful neighbors, multiplying your prostitution to provoke Me to anger. 27 Therefore I stretched out My hand against you, diminished your allotment, and delivered you to the desire of your enemies, the daughters of the Philistines, who were ashamed of your lewd conduct. 28 You also prostituted yourself to the Assyrians, because you were not satisfied; you prostituted yourself to them and still were not satisfied. 29 You multiplied your prostitution with Chaldea, the land of commerce, and even then you were not satisfied.

30 “How sick is your heart, declares the Lord the LORD, for doing all these things, the deeds of a shameless prostitute! 31 When you built your vaulted chamber at the head of every street and your lofty shrine in every square, you were unlike a prostitute, because you scorned payment. 32 O adulterous wife, who takes strangers instead of her husband! 33 Men give gifts to all prostitutes, but you gave your gifts to all your lovers, bribing them to come to you from every side in your prostitution. 34 Thus you differed from other women in your prostitution: no one solicited you to prostitute yourself, and you gave payment, whereas they receive payment from you. So you were different.

Judgment, Humiliation, and the Sisters’ Comparison

35 “Therefore, O prostitute, hear the word of the LORD: 36 Thus says the Lord the LORD, Because your lust was poured out and your nakedness uncovered in your prostitution with your lovers and with all your abominable idols, and because of the blood of your children that you gave to them, 37 therefore I will gather all your lovers with whom you took pleasure, together with all those you loved and all those you hated. I will gather them against you from every side and uncover your nakedness to them, so that they may see all your nakedness.

38 “I will judge you with the judgments of adulteresses and of those who shed blood, and I will bring upon you the blood of wrath and jealousy. 39 I will deliver you into their hands, and they will demolish your vaulted chamber, tear down your lofty shrines, strip off your clothes, take your beautiful jewels, and leave you naked and bare. 40 They will bring a crowd against you, stone you, and cut you to pieces with their swords. 41 They will burn your houses with fire and execute judgments against you in the sight of many women. I will make you cease from prostitution, and you shall no longer give payment. 42 Thus I will satisfy My wrath against you, and My jealousy will depart from you; I will be calm and will be angry no more. 43 Because you did not remember the days of your youth but enraged Me in all these things, I have brought your conduct upon your own head, declares the Lord the LORD. Have you not committed lewdness in addition to all your abominations?

44 “Behold, everyone who uses proverbs will apply this proverb to you: ‘Like mother, like daughter.’ 45 You are your mother’s daughter, who despised her husband and her children; and you are the sister of your sisters, who despised their husbands and their children. Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite. 46 Your elder sister is Samaria, who lived with her daughters to the north of you; your younger sister is Sodom, who lived with her daughters to the south of you. 47 Yet you did not merely walk in their ways or commit their abominations; within a very little time you became more corrupt than they in all your ways.

48 “As I live, declares the Lord the LORD, your sister Sodom and her daughters have not done as you and your daughters have done. 49 Behold, this was the guilt of your sister Sodom: she and her daughters had pride, an abundance of food, and prosperous ease, but they did not aid the poor and needy. 50 They were haughty and committed an abomination before Me, so I removed them when I saw it. 51 Samaria has not committed even half of your sins; you have committed more abominations than they, and by all your abominations you have made your sisters appear righteous. 52 You too must bear your disgrace, because you have intervened on behalf of your sisters. Because of your sins, in which you acted more abominably than they, they are more righteous than you. So be ashamed and bear your disgrace, because you have made your sisters appear righteous.

53 “I will restore the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters and the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters, and I will restore your fortunes among them, 54 so that you may bear your disgrace and be ashamed of all that you have done, becoming a consolation to them. 55 As for your sisters, Sodom and her daughters shall return to their former state, Samaria and her daughters shall return to their former state, and you and your daughters shall return to your former state. 56 Was not your sister Sodom a byword in your mouth in the day of your pride, 57 before your wickedness was uncovered? Now you have become an object of reproach for the daughters of Edom and all her neighbors, and for the daughters of the Philistines, all around you who despise you. 58 You bear the penalty of your lewdness and your abominations, declares the LORD.

Covenant Remembrance and Everlasting Restoration

59 “For thus says the Lord the LORD: I will deal with you as you have done, you who despised the oath by breaking the covenant. 60 Yet I will remember My covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant. 61 Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your sisters, both your elder and your younger, and I give them to you as daughters, but not on account of your covenant. 62 I will establish My covenant with you, and you shall know that I am the LORD, 63 so that you may remember and be confounded, and never open your mouth again because of your shame, when I make atonement for you for all that you have done, declares the Lord the LORD.”
