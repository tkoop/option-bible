The LORD's Zealous Restoration of Zion

1 The word of the LORD of Hosts came, saying, 2 “Thus says the LORD of Hosts: I have been consumed with zeal for Zion, a zeal of surpassing magnitude, and with great and burning wrath I have been jealous for her. 3 Thus says the LORD: I have returned to Zion and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; Jerusalem shall be called the City of Truth, and the mountain of the LORD of Hosts the Holy Mountain. 4 Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Old men and old women shall again sit in the streets of Jerusalem, each with his staff in hand by reason of his many days. 5 And the streets of the city shall be filled with boys and girls playing in its thoroughfares. 6 Thus says the LORD of Hosts: If it should seem marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, should it also seem marvelous in My eyes? declares the LORD of Hosts. 7 Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Behold, I will deliver My people from the land of the east and from the land of the west. 8 I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; they shall be My people, and I will be their God in truth and righteousness.”

Exhortation to Rebuild and Prosper

9 Thus says the LORD of Hosts: “Let your hands be strong, you who in these days hear these words from the mouths of the prophets, words spoken on the day the foundation of the house of the LORD of Hosts was laid, that the temple might be rebuilt. 10 For in those former days there was no hire for man or beast, nor was there any peace for anyone either going out or coming in, because of the adversary, and I unleashed every man against his neighbor. 11 But now I will not treat the remnant of this people as in the former days, declares the LORD of Hosts. 12 For the seed of peace shall be sown: the vine shall yield its fruit, the land shall yield its produce, and the heavens shall yield their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to inherit all these things. 13 And just as you were a curse among the nations, O house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you shall be a blessing. Do not fear; let your hands be strong.”

Covenant Faithfulness and Transformed Fasts

14 “For thus says the LORD of Hosts: Just as I purposed to bring disaster upon you when your fathers provoked Me to wrath, and I did not relent, 15 so in these days I have purposed to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah; do not fear. 16 These are the things you shall do: speak truth, each to his neighbor; render judgment in your gates with truth and the judgment of peace. 17 Let none of you devise evil in your hearts against his neighbor, and love no false oath, for all these things I hate,” declares the LORD.

18 The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying, 19 “Thus says the LORD of Hosts: The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth shall become for the house of Judah joy and gladness and cheerful feasts; therefore love truth and peace.”

The Nations Seek the LORD

20 “Thus says the LORD of Hosts: It shall yet come to pass that peoples and the inhabitants of many cities shall come. 21 The inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, ‘Let us go at once to entreat the favor of the LORD and to seek the LORD of Hosts; I myself will go as well.’ 22 Indeed, many peoples and mighty nations shall come to seek the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem and to entreat the favor of the LORD. 23 Thus says the LORD of Hosts: In those days ten men from all the languages of the nations shall seize the robe of a Jewish man, saying, ‘Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.’”
