Consecration of Aaron and His Sons

“1 This is what you are to do to consecrate them, so that they may serve Me as priests: Take a young bull and two rams without blemish, 2 together with unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil. You shall make them from fine wheat flour, 3 and you shall put them in a basket and present them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

“4 Then bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and wash them with water. 5 Take the garments and dress Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece, fastening the ephod with its woven waistband. 6 Put the turban on his head and attach the holy crown to the turban. 7 Then take the anointing oil and pour it on his head to anoint him. 8 Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 9 Wrap the sashes around Aaron and his sons, put headbands on them, and ordain them in this manner; the priesthood shall be theirs by a perpetual statute. Thus you shall ordain Aaron and his sons.

The Sin Offering and Burnt Offering

“10 Bring the bull to the front of the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on its head. 11 You shall slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 12 Take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger; then pour out all the remaining blood at the base of the altar. 13 Take all the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe of the liver, both kidneys and the fat on them, and burn them on the altar. 14 But burn the flesh of the bull, its hide, and its dung outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

“15 Take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on its head. 16 Slaughter the ram and take its blood and sprinkle it on all sides of the altar. 17 Cut the ram into pieces, wash its entrails and legs, and place them with its head and the other pieces. 18 Burn the entire ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

The Ordination Ram and Its Sacred Portions

“19 Take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on its head. 20 Slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the right earlobes of Aaron and his sons, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Then sprinkle the blood on all sides of the altar. 21 Take some of the blood from the altar and some of the anointing oil and sprinkle both on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and their garments. Then he and his garments will be consecrated, along with his sons and their garments.

“22 Take the fat from the ram—the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe of the liver, both kidneys with the fat on them, and the right thigh, since this is a ram for ordination— 23 along with one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD. 24 Put all these in the hands of Aaron and his sons and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. 25 Then take them from their hands and burn them on the altar along with the burnt offering, as a pleasing aroma before the LORD. It is an offering made by fire to the LORD.

“26 Take the breast of the ram of ordination and wave it before the LORD as a wave offering, and it will be your portion. 27 Consecrate for Aaron and his sons the breast of the wave offering that is waved and the thigh of the heave offering that is lifted up from the ram of ordination. 28 This will belong to Aaron and his sons as a perpetual portion from the Israelites, for it is the heave offering that the Israelites present to the LORD from their peace offerings.

“29 The holy garments that belong to Aaron will belong to his sons after him, so that they may be anointed and ordained in them. 30 The son who succeeds him as priest and enters the Tent of Meeting to minister in the Holy Place must wear them for seven days. 31 You are to take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place. 32 At the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, Aaron and his sons are to eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket. 33 They must eat those things by which atonement was made for their ordination and consecration, but no outsider may eat them, because they are holy. 34 If any of the meat of ordination or any of the bread is left until morning, you are to burn up the remainder; it must not be eaten, because it is holy.

Completion of the Ordination and the Daily Sacrifices

“35 This is what you are to do for Aaron and his sons, in accordance with all that I have commanded you. You are to ordain them for seven days. 36 Each day you are to prepare a bull as a sin offering for atonement. Purify the altar by making atonement for it, and anoint it to consecrate it. 37 For seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it. Then the altar will be most holy; anything that touches it will become holy.

“38 This is what you are to offer regularly on the altar: two lambs a year old, every day, continually. 39 Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight. 40 With the first lamb offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour, mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives, and a drink offering of a quarter hin of wine. 41 You shall offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

“42 For the generations to come, this burnt offering shall be made regularly at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak with you there. 43 There I will meet with the Israelites, and the place will be consecrated by My glory. 44 So I will consecrate the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests. 45 Then I will dwell among the Israelites and be their God. 46 And they will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I might dwell among them. I am the LORD their God.”
