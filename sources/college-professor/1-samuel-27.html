David's Refuge among the Philistines

1 David said within himself, "Sooner or later I shall be consumed by Saul's hand; no prospect is more advantageous to me than to escape into the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will cease searching for me throughout all the territory of Israel, and I shall slip from his grasp." 2 Accordingly, David arose and crossed over with the six hundred men who accompanied him to Achish son of Maoch, king of Gath. 3 David and his men settled in Gath with Achish, each with his household, and David with his two wives—Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail, Nabal's widow from Carmel. 4 When Saul was informed that David had fled to Gath, he ceased searching for him.

The Grant of Ziklag and David's Sojourn

5 Then David said to Achish, "If I have found favor in your sight, let them assign me a residence in one of the country towns, that I may dwell there. Why should your servant reside in the royal city with you?" 6 So on that day Achish gave him Ziklag; therefore Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah to this day. 7 The number of days David lived in the Philistine countryside was a year and four months.

David's Raids and Achish's Misapprehension

8 Now David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites—for from ancient times these peoples had inhabited the land extending to Shur and Egypt. 9 Whenever David attacked a region, he left neither man nor woman alive, but took sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and garments; then he returned to Achish.

10 When Achish asked, "Where did you raid today?"

David replied, "Against the Negev of Judah," or "Against the Negev of the Jerahmeelites," or "Against the Negev of the Kenites." 11 David left no man or woman alive to be brought to Gath, for he thought, "They might inform against us, saying, 'This is what David did.'" And such was his custom throughout all the time he lived in the Philistine territory.

12 So Achish trusted David, thinking, "He has made himself utterly abhorrent to his people Israel; therefore he shall be my servant forever."
