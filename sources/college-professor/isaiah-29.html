Ariel Besieged and Humbled

1 Woe unto Ariel—Ariel, the city of David! Pile year upon year; let the festivals complete their circuits. 2 Yet I will distress Ariel, and there shall be mourning and lamentation, and she shall be unto Me as an altar hearth. 3 I shall encamp about you in a ring; I shall besiege you with a siege mound and raise fortifications against you. 4 You shall be brought low; you shall speak from the earth, and your speech shall issue from the dust. Your voice shall be faint, and your speech shall be like a spirit’s muttering from the ground, chirping from the dust.

The Dissolution of the Nations’ Siege

5 The throng of your enemies shall be reduced to fine dust, and the horde of the ruthless shall pass like chaff—suddenly, in an instant. 6 You shall be visited by the LORD of Hosts with thunder and earthquake and great noise, with whirlwind and tempest and the flame of devouring fire. 7 It shall be as a dream, a night vision: the whole horde of the nations warring against Ariel—all her besiegers, her stronghold, and her oppressors. 8 As when a hungry man dreams that he is eating, but awakens and his soul is empty; or as when a thirsty man dreams that he is drinking, but awakens and is faint, his soul still craving—so shall be the whole horde of the nations warring against Mount Zion.

Sealed Revelation and Spiritual Slumber

9 Pause and be astonished; blind yourselves and be blind. They are drunk, but not with wine; they reel, but not with strong drink. 10 For the LORD has poured out upon you a spirit of deep sleep; He has shut your eyes—the prophets—and covered your heads—the seers.

11 The whole vision has become to you like the words of a sealed book. If it is given to one who can read, saying, “Please read this,”

He says, “I cannot, for it is sealed.”

12 Or if the book is given to one who cannot read, saying, “Please read this,”

He says, “I do not know how to read.”

13 Then the Lord said: “Because this people draws near to Me with its mouth and honors Me with its lips, while its heart is far from Me, and its reverence for Me is but a human commandment learned by rote— 14 therefore, behold, I shall once more work wonders among this people—wonder upon wonder—and the wisdom of their wise shall perish, and the discernment of their discerning shall be hidden.”

Concealment, Reversal, and Jacob’s Vindication

15 Woe to those who dig deep to hide their counsel from the LORD, whose works are in darkness, and who say, “Who sees us? Who knows us?”

16 You turn things upside down! Shall the potter be counted as clay, that the thing made should say of its maker, “He did not make me,”

Or the thing formed say of its former, “He has no understanding”?

17 In but a very little while, shall not Lebanon be turned into a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted as forest? 18 In that day the deaf shall hear the words of a book, and the eyes of the blind shall see out of obscurity and out of darkness. 19 The humble shall increase their joy in the LORD, and the needy among mankind shall rejoice in the Holy One of Israel. 20 For the ruthless shall cease, and the scorner shall be consumed, and all who watch for iniquity shall be cut off— 21 those who make a man guilty for a word, and snare him who rebukes in the gate, and turn aside the righteous with empty charges.

22 Therefore thus says the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, concerning the house of Jacob: “Jacob shall not now be ashamed, neither shall his face now grow pale. 23 For when he sees his children, the work of My hands, in his midst, they will sanctify My name; they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob and stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24 Those who err in spirit shall come to understanding, and those who murmur shall learn instruction.”
