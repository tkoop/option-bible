The Artisans and the Surplus of Offerings

1 Bezalel, Oholiab, and every artisan of wise heart in whom the LORD had deposited wisdom and understanding—so that they comprehended how to execute the entirety of the work pertaining to the service of the sanctuary—were to act in accordance with all that the LORD had commanded, for the LORD had imparted to them the wisdom and understanding requisite for every task of the sacred labor. 2 Moses summoned Bezalel, Oholiab, and every wise-hearted person whose heart the LORD had endowed with wisdom—every one whose heart stirred him to draw near and undertake the labor.

3 They received from Moses all the contribution that the Israelites had brought for the work of the service of making the sanctuary, while the people continued to bring him freewill offerings morning after morning. 4 All the skilled artisans engaged in the sanctuary work came, each departing from the task at which he was laboring, 5 and they said to Moses, "The people are bringing far more than is sufficient for the service of the work that the LORD has commanded to be done."

6 Moses commanded that a proclamation be made throughout the camp: "Let no man or woman perform any further labor for the offering of the sanctuary." Thus the people were restrained from bringing more. 7 The materials were already sufficient for all the work to be done, and even exceeded what was required.

The Curtains, Tent, and Coverings of the Tabernacle

8 All the skilled artisans among the workers fashioned the tabernacle from ten curtains of fine twined linen, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, with cherubim skillfully worked into them. 9 Each curtain measured twenty-eight cubits in length and four cubits in width, all the curtains being of one measure. 10 Five curtains were joined to one another, and the remaining five curtains were joined to one another. 11 He fashioned loops of blue along the edge of the one curtain at the end of the first set, and he fashioned them likewise along the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set. 12 He made fifty loops on the one curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set, the loops being opposite one another. 13 He made fifty clasps of gold and coupled the curtains to one another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle became a single whole.

14 He made curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle—eleven curtains in all. 15 The length of one curtain was thirty cubits, and its width four cubits; all eleven curtains were of one measure. 16 He joined five curtains by themselves and six curtains by themselves. 17 He made fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the one set and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set. 18 He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together, so that it became one. 19 He made a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and an outer covering of fine leather.

The Frames and Bars of the Tabernacle

20 He made the upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 21 Each frame was ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 22 Each frame had two tenons, fitted to one another; he did this for all the frames of the tabernacle. 23 He made twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle, toward the south. 24 He made forty silver bases beneath the twenty frames: two bases under one frame for its two tenons, and two bases under another frame for its two tenons. 25 For the second side of the tabernacle, toward the north, he made twenty frames, 26 with their forty silver bases, two bases under one frame and two bases under another. 27 For the west side of the tabernacle he made six frames. 28 He made two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear. 29 They were coupled beneath and coupled together at the top into one ring; he did this for both of them in the two corners. 30 There were eight frames with their sixteen silver bases, two bases under each frame.

31 He made bars of acacia wood: five for the frames of one side of the tabernacle, 32 five bars for the frames of the other side, and five bars for the frames of the tabernacle at the rear toward the west. 33 He made the middle bar to pass through the midst of the frames from end to end. 34 He overlaid the frames with gold and made their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.

The Veil and the Entrance Screen

35 He made the veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twined linen, fashioning it with cherubim skillfully worked. 36 He made four pillars of acacia wood for it and overlaid them with gold; their hooks were of gold, and he cast for them four silver bases. 37 For the entrance of the tent he made a screen of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer, 38 along with its five pillars and their hooks; he overlaid their capitals and their bands with gold, and their five bases were bronze.
