Jonah’s Prayer from the Belly of the Fish

1 Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the belly of the fish.

2 He said, “Out of my distress I invoked the LORD, and He answered me; from the belly of Sheol I cried out, and You heard my voice. 3 You cast me into the deep, into the heart of the seas, and the current surrounded me; all Your breakers and Your waves passed over me. 4 And I said, ‘I am driven away from before Your eyes; yet I will look again toward Your holy temple.’ 5 The waters closed over me, threatening my very life; the abyss surrounded me; seaweed was bound about my head.

From the Pit to the LORD’s Temple

6 “I descended to the roots of the mountains; the earth with its bars closed about me forever; yet You brought up my life from the pit, O the LORD my God. 7 When my soul fainted within me, I remembered the LORD; my prayer came to You, into Your holy temple. 8 Those who cling to worthless idols forfeit the mercy that could be theirs.

Thanksgiving and Deliverance

9 “But I, with shouts of thanksgiving, will sacrifice to you; what I have vowed I will pay. Deliverance belongs to the LORD.”

10 And the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah onto dry land.
