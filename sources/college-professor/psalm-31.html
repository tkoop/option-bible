A Petition for Divine Refuge and Deliverance

1 In You, the LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame; deliver me in Your righteousness. 2 Incline Your ear to me; rescue me swiftly. Be my rock of refuge, a fortified stronghold to save me. 3 For You are my rock and my fortress; for the sake of Your name, lead and guide me. 4 Free me from the snare they have hidden for me, for You are my refuge.

5 Into Your hands I commit my spirit; You have redeemed me, the LORD, God of truth. 6 I abhor those who cling to worthless idols, but as for me, I trust in the LORD. 7 I will exult and rejoice in Your loving devotion, for You have seen my affliction and You have known the anguish of my soul. 8 You have not surrendered me to the enemy; You have set my feet in a spacious place.

9 Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am in distress; my eyes grow dim with sorrow, and my soul and body with grief. 10 For my life is consumed with anguish and my years with groaning; my strength falters because of my iniquity, and my bones waste away. 11 Because of all my adversaries I have become a reproach—especially to my neighbors—and an object of dread to my acquaintances; those who see me in the street flee from me. 12 I am forgotten as though dead; I have become like a shattered vessel. 13 For I hear the whispering of many—“Terror on every side!”—as they conspire together against me and plot to take my life.

14 But I trust in You, the LORD; I say, “You are my God.” 15 My times are in Your hands; deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from my pursuers. 16 Let Your face shine upon Your servant; save me in Your steadfast love.

17 The LORD, let me not be put to shame, for I call upon You; let the wicked be put to shame and be silenced in Sheol. 18 Let lying lips be silenced—those that speak arrogantly against the righteous with pride and contempt.

19 How abundant is Your goodness, which You have treasured up for those who fear You and lavished before the children of men upon those who take refuge in You! 20 You hide them in the shelter of Your presence from the plots of man; You shelter them in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.

21 Blessed be the LORD, for He has shown me the wonders of His steadfast love in a besieged city. 22 In my alarm I said, “I am cut off from Your sight.” Yet You heard the voice of my supplications when I cried to You for help.

23 Love the LORD, all His faithful ones! The LORD preserves the faithful, but to the proud He repays in full. 24 Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all you who hope in the LORD.
