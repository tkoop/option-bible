The Covenant Identity of the LORD’s People and the Repudiation of Pagan Mourning

1 You are sons of the LORD your God. You shall not lacerate yourselves, nor shall you create a tonsured baldness between your eyes as a funerary lamentation. 2 For you are a holy people to the LORD your God, and the LORD has chosen you to be a people for His own possession out of all the peoples who are upon the face of the earth.

Dietary Distinctions and the Separation of the Clean from the Unclean

3 You shall not eat any detestable thing. 4 These are the animals that you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat; 5 the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep. 6 And every beast that has a divided hoof and is cloven-footed and chews the cud among the beasts, that you may eat. 7 Nevertheless, of those that chew the cud or have the hoof divided, you shall not eat these: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger, because they chew the cud but do not divide the hoof; they are unclean to you. 8 And the pig, because it divides the hoof but does not chew the cud, is unclean to you. You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall not touch their carcasses. 9 These you may eat of all that are in the waters: all that have fins and scales you may eat. 10 But all that have not fins and scales you shall not eat; they are unclean to you. 11 Every clean bird you may eat. 12 But these are those that you shall not eat: the eagle, the vulture, and the black vulture; 13 the red kite, the falcon, and the buzzard of any kind; 14 every raven of any kind; 15 the ostrich, the short-eared owl, the gull, and the hawk of any kind; 16 the little owl, the great owl, and the white owl; 17 the pelican, the Egyptian vulture, and the cormorant; 18 the stork, the heron of any kind, the hoopoe, and the bat. 19 And every winged swarming thing is unclean to you; they shall not be eaten. 20 Every clean flying creature you may eat. 21 You shall not eat any carcass. You may give it to the sojourner who is within your gates, that he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner. For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother’s milk.

The Tithe, Its Conversion, and Joyful Feasting Before the LORD

22 You shall surely tithe all the yield of your seed that goes out of the field year by year. 23 And you shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place that He will choose to make His name dwell there, the tithe of your grain, your wine, and your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and flock, that you may learn to fear the LORD your God all your days. 24 And if the way is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry it, because the place is too far from you which the LORD your God chooses, to set His name there, when the LORD your God blesses you, 25 then you shall turn it into money, and bind the money in your hand, and go to the place that the LORD your God chooses. 26 And you shall spend the money for whatever your soul desires—oxen, sheep, wine, strong drink, or whatever your soul asks—and you shall eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household. 27 And you shall not forsake the Levite who is within your gates, for he has no portion or inheritance with you.

The Third-Year Tithe and Blessing Through Care for the Needy

28 At the end of every three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your produce in that year and lay it up within your gates. 29 And the Levite, because he has no portion or inheritance with you, and the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates, shall come and eat and be satisfied, that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands that you do.
