The Heavenly Portal and the Summons to Ascend

1 After these things I beheld, and lo, a portal stood ajar in heaven, while the voice I had first heard discoursing with me—resonant as a trumpet—declared, “Ascend hither, and I shall disclose unto you the things that must needs come to pass hereafter.”

The Throne and Its Radiant Attendants

2 Forthwith I found myself in the Spirit, and, behold, a throne was set in heaven, with One seated upon it. 3 And He who sat there presented an appearance like jasper and carnelian, while a rainbow, resembling an emerald in appearance, encircled the throne in its entirety. 4 And round about the throne were twenty-four thrones, upon which sat twenty-four elders, arrayed in white raiments, with golden crowns upon their heads. 5 And from the throne proceeded lightnings, voices, and thunders; moreover, before the throne burned seven lamps of fire, which are the seven Spirits of God.

The Sea of Glass and the Four Living Creatures

6 Before the throne lay something like a sea of glass, resembling crystal; and in the midst of the throne, and round about it, stood four living creatures, replete with eyes before and behind. 7 The first creature resembled a lion, the second a calf, the third bore a face as of a man, and the fourth was like an eagle in flight.

The Unceasing Doxology of Heaven

8 And each of the four living creatures, having six wings, was full of eyes within and without; and they rest not day nor night, saying, “Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty, who was, and who is, and who is to come.”

9 And whenever the living creatures render glory, honor, and thanksgiving to Him who sits upon the throne, who lives unto the ages of ages, 10 the twenty-four elders prostrate themselves before Him who sits upon the throne, and worship Him who lives unto the ages of ages, casting their crowns before the throne, saying,

11 “Worthy are You, our Lord and God, to receive glory, honor, and power, for You created all things, and by Your will they existed and were created.”
