A Father's Exhortation to Treasure Wisdom

1 My son, keep my words and treasure my commandments within you; 2 keep my commandments that you may live, and guard my teaching as the most precious object of your regard. 3 Bind them upon your fingers; inscribe them upon the tablet of your heart. 4 Say to wisdom, "You are my sister," and call understanding your intimate friend, 5 that they may shield you from the adulteress, from the foreign woman who flatters with her words.

The Naive Youth Observed from the Lattice

6 For at the window of my house I looked out through my lattice, 7 and I beheld among the naive, discerning among the youths, a young man devoid of understanding, 8 traversing the street beside her corner and setting his course toward her dwelling, 9 in the twilight, in the evening, in the blackness of night and in darkness. 10 And behold, a woman comes to meet him, dressed as a harlot and wily of heart. 11 She is clamorous and defiant; her feet never settle in her own house. 12 Now in the street, now in the squares, she lurks by every corner.

The Adulteress's Seductive Oration

13 So she seized him and kissed him, and with an impudent face she said to him:

14 "I am bound to offer peace offerings; today I have paid my vows. 15 Therefore I have come out to meet you, to seek you out earnestly, and I have found you. 16 I have spread my couch with coverings, with colored linens of Egypt. 17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.

18 "Come, let us sate ourselves with love until morning; let us revel in caresses. 19 For my husband is not at home; he has gone far away. 20 He has taken a bag of silver with him; he will return only at the full moon."

The Fatal Consequence of Folly

21 With her many persuasions she seduces him; with her flattering lips she leads him astray. 22 Suddenly he follows her, as an ox goes to the slaughter, or as one fettered for the chastisement of a fool, 23 until an arrow pierces his liver; like a bird rushing into a snare, he does not know that it will cost him his life.

24 And now, my sons, listen to me, and attend to the words of my mouth. 25 Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. 26 For many are the victims she has cast down, and countless are all her slain. 27 Her house is the way to Sheol, descending to the chambers of death.
