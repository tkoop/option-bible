The Ark Is Brought to Jerusalem

1They brought the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 2When David had finished offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. 3He distributed to every Israelite, man and woman alike, a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake.

Levites Appointed for the Ark's Service

4He appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the LORD, to invoke, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel. 5Asaph was the chief, then Zechariah, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel, with harps and lyres; and Asaph sounded the cymbals. 6Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were to blow trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God. 7On that day David first committed to Asaph and his brothers the task of thanking the LORD.

A Psalm of Thanksgiving

8Give thanks to the LORD; call upon His name; make known His deeds among the peoples. 9Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; recount all His wondrous works. 10Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. 11Seek the LORD and His strength; seek His face continually. 12Remember the wonders He has done, His marvels and the judgments of His mouth.

13O offspring of Israel, His servant, children of Jacob, His chosen ones, 14He is the LORD our God; His judgments are in all the earth. 15Remember His covenant forever, the word He commanded for a thousand generations, 16the covenant He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac, 17which He established for Jacob as a statute, and for Israel as an everlasting covenant, saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance," 18when you were few in number, indeed very few, and sojourners in it, 19wandering from nation to nation and from one kingdom to another people. 20He allowed no one to oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf, 21saying, "Do not touch My anointed ones; do My prophets no harm."

22Sing to the LORD, all the earth; proclaim His salvation from day to day. 23Declare His glory among the nations, His marvelous works among all the peoples. 24For great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; He is to be revered above all gods. 25For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 26Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and joy are in His place. 27Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. 28Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and come before Him; worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness. 29Tremble before Him, all the earth; the world is firmly established and cannot be moved. 30Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let them say among the nations, "The LORD reigns." 31Let the sea roar and all that fills it; let the field exult and everything in it. 32Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth. 33Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His steadfast love endures forever. 34And say, "Save us, O God of our salvation, and gather us and deliver us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name and glory in Your praise." 35Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. 36And all the people said, "Amen!" and praised the LORD.

Worship Maintained in Jerusalem and Gibeon

37So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark continually, as the daily service required. 38Obed-edom and his brothers, sixty-eight, together with Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah, served as gatekeepers. 39Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests served before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place in Gibeon, 40to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, and to do all that is written in the Law of the LORD, which He commanded Israel. 41With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest who were chosen and expressly named to give thanks to the LORD, because His steadfast love endures forever. 42Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets and cymbals and the other instruments for the songs of God, and the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate. 43Then all the people departed, each to his own home, and David went home to bless his household.
