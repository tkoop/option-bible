A Shiggaion of David, Which He Sang to the LORD Concerning the Words of Cush the Benjamite

1 The LORD my God, in You I have taken refuge; save me and deliver me from all my pursuers. 2 Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it apart, with no one to deliver.

3 The LORD my God, if I have done this, if there is iniquity in my hands, 4 if I have repaid my ally with evil, or plundered my adversary without cause, 5 then let the enemy pursue and overtake my soul, trample my life to the ground, and lay my glory in the dust. Selah.

6 Arise, the LORD, in Your anger; lift Yourself up against the fury of my adversaries, and awake for me the judgment You have commanded. 7 Let the congregation of the peoples surround You; return, then, and take Your seat above them. 8 The LORD judges the peoples; judge me, the LORD, according to my righteousness and according to my integrity.

9 Let the evil of the wicked come to an end, and establish the righteous—O righteous God, who tests hearts and minds. 10 My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart. 11 God is a righteous judge, a God who is indignant every day.

12 If he does not repent, He will whet His sword; He has bent and strung His bow. 13 He has prepared for him the instruments of death; He fashions His arrows against the pursuers.

14 Behold, he travails with iniquity, conceives mischief, and gives birth to falsehood. 15 He has dug a pit and hollowed it out, and has fallen into the hole he made. 16 His toil shall return upon his own head, and his violence shall descend upon his crown.

17 I will give thanks to the LORD according to His righteousness, and I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.
