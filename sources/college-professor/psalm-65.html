Praise, Vows, and Forgiveness in Zion

1 For the choirmaster—a psalm of David, a song. Praise is Your due in Zion, O God, and to You vows will be discharged. 2 O You who hear prayer, unto You all humanity shall come. 3 When iniquities prevail against me, You make atonement for them. 4 How blessed is the one You choose and bring near, that he may dwell in Your courts! We shall be sated with the beneficence of Your house, Your holy temple.

The Righteous God of Universal Salvation

5 By awe-inspiring acts of righteousness You answer us, O God of our salvation, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. 6 You establish the mountains by Your strength, being girded with might. 7 You quell the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of the peoples. 8 Thus those who dwell at the ends of the earth stand in awe of Your signs; You make the dawn and the sunset shout for joy.

The Creator’s Bounty Upon the Earth

9 You attend to the earth and saturate it; You enrich it immeasurably; the river of God is brimful of water; You prepare their grain, for thus You prepare the earth. 10 You drench its furrows; You level its ridges; You soften it with gentle rains; You bless its burgeoning. 11 You crown the year with Your bounty, and Your paths overflow with abundance. 12 The wilderness pastures drip with plenty, and the hills gird themselves with rejoicing. 13 The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy, they also sing.
