The Widow’s Offering

1 Casting His gaze upward, He beheld the affluent depositing their oblations into the treasury, 2 and He likewise discerned a certain indigent widow contributing two diminutive copper coins.

3 “Truly I tell you, this penurious widow has contributed more than all the rest; 4 for they all have given out of their superfluity, whereas she, out of her destitution, has contributed the entirety of her subsistence.”

The Destruction of the Temple and the End Times

5 And while some were discoursing about the temple—how it was adorned with beautiful stones and votive offerings—He said,

6 “As for these things that you behold, days will come in which not one stone shall be left upon another; the whole edifice shall be demolished.”

7 “Teacher,” they inquired of Him, “when shall these things come to pass, and what sign shall herald their imminent occurrence?”

8 “Take heed lest you be deceived, for many will come in My name, professing, ‘I am He,’ and declaring, ‘The time is at hand.’ Do not follow them. 9 And when you hear of wars and upheavals, be not terrified, for these things must first occur, yet the consummation shall not be immediate.

10 Then He said to them, “Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. 11 There shall be great earthquakes, and in diverse places famines and pestilences; moreover, there shall be portents and great signs from heaven.

12 “But before all these things, they shall lay hands upon you and persecute you, delivering you into synagogues and prisons, and arraigning you before kings and governors on account of My name. 13 This shall redound to you as an occasion for testimony.

14 “Resolve therefore in your hearts not to premeditate a defense beforehand, 15 for I will endow you with utterance and wisdom, which none of your adversaries shall be able to withstand or gainsay.

16 “You shall be betrayed even by parents and brothers and kinsfolk and friends, and some of you shall be put to death. 17 You shall be detested by all on account of My name. 18 Yet not a single hair of your head shall perish. 19 By your steadfast endurance you shall secure your lives.

20 “But when you behold Jerusalem invested by armies, then recognize that her desolation is imminent. 21 Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains, let those within her walls evacuate, and let not those in the countryside enter her. 22 For these are days of retribution, that all things written may be fulfilled. 23 Woe to those with child and to those nursing in those days! For there shall be great calamity upon the earth and wrath against this people. 24 They shall fall by the edge of the sword and be led captive into all nations, and Jerusalem shall be trodden down by the Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles reach their fulfillment.

The Coming of the Son of Man

25 “And there shall be signs in sun and moon and stars, and upon the earth distress of nations, bewildered by the roaring of the sea and its waves. 26 People shall faint from terror and from apprehension of the things impending upon the world, for the celestial powers shall be shaken. 27 And then they shall see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory. 28 But when these things begin to come to pass, stand erect and lift up your heads, because your redemption is drawing near.

29 And He propounded to them a parable: “Look at the fig tree and all the trees. 30 When they already put forth leaves, you observe for yourselves and know that summer is already near. 31 So likewise, when you see these things coming to pass, you know that the kingdom of God is at hand. 32 Truly I tell you, this generation shall not pass away until all these things have occurred. 33 Heaven and earth shall pass away, but My words shall not pass away.

34 “But take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts be overburdened with dissipation and drunkenness and the anxieties of this life, and that day overtake you unawares, as a snare. 35 For it shall come upon all who dwell upon the face of the whole earth. 36 But be vigilant at all times, praying that you may possess the strength to escape all these things that are destined to occur, and to stand before the Son of Man.”

Jesus’ Daily Teaching in the Temple

37 And during the days He was teaching in the temple, but at night He would go out and lodge on the mount called Olivet. 38 And all the people would come early in the morning to Him in the temple to hear Him.
