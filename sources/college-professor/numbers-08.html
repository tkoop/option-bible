The Illumination of the Sanctuary Lampstand

1 The LORD instructed Moses, saying, 2 “Speak to Aaron and tell him, ‘When you kindle the seven lamps, they are to cast their radiance before the lampstand.’”

3 Aaron did precisely so, setting up the lamps before the lampstand, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 4 The lampstand itself was a work of beaten gold, wrought from its base to its blossoms, according to the pattern that the LORD had shown Moses.

The Purification and Consecration of the Levites

5 Then the LORD said to Moses, 6 “Take the Levites from among the Israelites and purify them. 7 Thus you shall deal with them in order to cleanse them: sprinkle upon them the water of purification; have them shave their whole body and launder their garments, and so let them purify themselves. 8 Then let them take a young bull, together with its grain offering of fine flour mingled with oil, and you are to take a second young bull for a sin offering. 9 Bring the Levites before the Tent of Meeting and assemble the entire congregation of the Israelites. 10 You are to present the Levites before the LORD, and the Israelites shall lay their hands upon them. 11 Aaron shall present the Levites before the LORD as a wave offering from the Israelites, so that they may be consecrated to the service of the LORD. 12 Then the Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you are to offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites. 13 You are to have the Levites stand before Aaron and his sons, and then present them as a wave offering to the LORD. 14 In this way you shall separate the Levites from the rest of the Israelites, and the Levites shall be Mine. 15 After you have cleansed them and presented them as a wave offering, they are to come and perform their service at the Tent of Meeting. 16 For they are wholly given to Me from among the Israelites; I have taken them for Myself in place of every firstborn among the Israelites, that is, the first issue of every womb. 17 For every firstborn in Israel is Mine, whether human or animal; I consecrated them to Myself on the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt. 18 But I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites. 19 And I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the Israelites, to carry out the service of the Israelites at the Tent of Meeting, and to make atonement for the Israelites, so that no plague may afflict the Israelites when they approach the sanctuary.”

20 So Moses, Aaron, and the entire congregation of the Israelites did for the Levites everything that the LORD had commanded Moses. 21 The Levites purified themselves and laundered their garments, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD; Aaron also made atonement for them to cleanse them. 22 Afterward the Levites came to perform their service at the Tent of Meeting before Aaron and his sons; they did exactly as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites.

The Age and Service of the Levites

23 The LORD said to Moses, 24 “This is what pertains to the Levites: from twenty-five years of age and upward they shall enter to perform the work of service in the Tent of Meeting. 25 But at the age of fifty they shall withdraw from the company of those who serve and shall labor no more. 26 They may, however, assist their brothers in the Tent of Meeting by keeping guard, though they shall not perform the work. This is how you are to administer the Levites with respect to their duties.”
