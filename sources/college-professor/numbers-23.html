Balaam’s First Oracle and the Altars of Moab

1 Balaam instructed Balak, “Erect seven altars for me upon this height, and make ready seven bulls and seven rams.” 2 Balak discharged that injunction with scrupulous fidelity, and the two of them presented a bull and a ram upon every altar.

3 Balaam then said to Balak, “Remain here beside your burnt offering while I withdraw to a solitary eminence; perhaps the LORD will vouchsafe an encounter with me, and whatever He discloses I shall report to you.” So he ascended to a barren height.

4 God encountered him, and Balaam said, “I have arrayed seven altars, and upon each I have offered a bull and a ram.” 5 Then the LORD implanted a word in Balaam’s mouth and commanded, “Return to Balak and deliver this message.” 6 Accordingly, he returned to Balak, who stood beside his burnt offering in the company of all the princes of Moab.

7 And Balaam lifted up his oracle, declaring, “Balak has summoned me from Aram—the king of Moab from the mountains of the east—saying, ‘Come, curse Jacob on my behalf; come, denounce Israel!’ 8 How, then, shall I execrate those whom God has not execrated? How shall I denounce those whom the LORD has not denounced? 9 For from the summit of the rocks I behold them, and from the hills I survey them: behold, a people dwelling in seclusion, not reckoned among the nations. 10 Who can enumerate the dust of Jacob, or tally the fourth part of Israel? Let my soul perish among the righteous, and let my final estate be commensurate with theirs!”

11 Balak said to Balaam, “What have you wrought against me? I brought you to curse my adversaries, yet—behold—you have blessed them abundantly!”

12 He answered, “Am I not constrained to utter precisely what the LORD places in my mouth?”

The Second Oracle from the Field of Zophim

13 Balak said to him, “I entreat you to accompany me to another prospect, from which you shall descry but a portion of them—not their totality—and from that station curse them on my behalf.”

14 So he conducted him to the field of Zophim, to the summit of Pisgah, where he erected seven altars and offered a bull and a ram upon each.

15 Balaam said to Balak, “Station yourself here beside your burnt offering while I encounter the LORD yonder.”

16 The LORD encountered Balaam and placed a word in his mouth, commanding, “Return to Balak and deliver this message.” 17 He returned to Balak, who stood beside his burnt offering with the princes of Moab, and Balak demanded, “What has the LORD spoken?”

18 Then Balaam lifted up his oracle, saying, “Arise, Balak, and attend; give ear to me, son of Zippor. 19 God is not a man, that He should prevaricate, nor a son of man, that He should relent. Has He declared and not executed? Has He spoken and not established it? 20 Behold, I have received a mandate to bless; He has blessed, and I cannot revoke it. 21 He has discerned no iniquity in Jacob, nor perceived perversity in Israel. The LORD his God is with him, and the acclamation of a king resounds among them. 22 God brought them forth from Egypt; He possesses strength like that of a wild ox. 23 There is no augury in Jacob, no divination in Israel; at the appointed time it shall be proclaimed concerning Jacob and Israel, ‘What has God wrought!’ 24 Behold, the people rise like a lioness and rouse themselves like a lion; they will not recline until they have devoured the prey and drunk the blood of the slain.”

25 Balak said to Balaam, “Utter no curse against them, and no blessing either!”

26 Balaam answered Balak, “Did I not admonish you that whatever the LORD speaks, I am obliged to perform?”

The Final Prospect from Peor

27 Balak said to Balaam, “Pray, come—I shall conduct you to yet another site; perhaps it will commend itself to God, and from there you may curse them on my behalf.”

28 So Balak led Balaam to the summit of Peor, which overlooks the wasteland.

29 Balaam said to Balak, “Construct seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams.” 30 Balak complied with Balaam’s instruction and offered a bull and a ram upon each altar.
