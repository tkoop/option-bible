The Tarnishing of Zion's Splendor

1 How the gold has dimmed, and the finest ore grown tarnished; the hallowed stones lie strewn across every thoroughfare. 2 How the prized sons of Zion, once weighed against pure gold, are reckoned mere earthenware, the handiwork of a potter.

Famine, Cruelty, and the Inversion of Nature

3 Even jackals bare the breast and suckle their whelps, yet the daughter of my people has grown merciless, akin to ostriches of the wasteland. 4 The suckling's tongue adheres to its palate in thirst, while children implore bread and find none to bestow. 5 Those who once feasted on delicacies are destitute in the streets; those reared in purple now embrace ash heaps.

6 For the guilt of my people's daughter exceeds Sodom's sin, overthrown in an instant without a hand laid upon her. 7 Her Nazirites were purer than snow, whiter than milk; their limbs glowed redder than coral, their visage like sapphire. 8 Their form is darker than soot; they are unrecognized in the streets; their skin has shriveled upon their bones and become dry as wood.

The Horrors of Starvation and Maternal Desperation

9 Those slain by the sword are better off than those slain by famine, who waste away, pierced by the lack of the field's produce. 10 Compassionate women have boiled their own children, making them sustenance amid the shattering of my people's daughter.

Divine Wrath, Human Disbelief, and Priestly Guilt

11 The LORD has accomplished His fury; He has poured out His fierce anger. He kindled a fire in Zion, and it consumed her foundations. 12 The kings of the earth and all the world's inhabitants never believed that adversary and foe could enter Jerusalem's gates. 13 It was because of the sins of her prophets and the iniquities of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous within her.

14 They stagger like blind men through the streets, defiled with blood, so that none may touch their garments. 15 People shouted for them to turn away and remain untouched, since they were unclean; so they fled and wandered, and among the nations it was said that they would no longer sojourn. 16 The LORD's countenance has scattered them; He will regard them no more, for they showed no honor to priests and no favor to elders.

17 Our eyes still fail, watching in vain for help; in our watch we looked for a nation that could not save. 18 They stalked our every step, so that we could not walk our streets; our end drew near, our days were fulfilled, for our doom had arrived. 19 Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of heaven; they pursued us over the mountains and ambushed us in the wilderness.

20 The breath of our nostrils, the LORD's Anointed, was caught in their traps; we had said that under his shadow we should live among the nations. 21 Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz! The cup will pass to you as well; you will become drunk and expose yourself. 22 O daughter of Zion, your guilt is at an end; He will not exile you again. O daughter of Edom, He will punish your guilt and expose your sins.
