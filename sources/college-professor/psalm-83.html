An Imprecatory Psalm Against the Confederated Hostility of the Nations

1 A song; a psalm of Asaph. O God, do not remain silent; do not be quiet, O God; do not be still. 2 For behold, Your enemies are in tumult, and those who hate You have grown arrogantly defiant. 3 They scheme with cunning against Your people, and they conspire against Your treasured ones.

4 They say, “Come, let us annihilate them as a nation, so that the name of Israel may be remembered no more.”

5 For they have conspired with one accord; they have made a covenant against You— 6 the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, 7 Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre. 8 Assyria also has joined them; they have become the arm of the children of Lot. Selah.

9 Do to them as to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin at the River Kishon— 10 those who were destroyed at Endor, who became manure for the soil. 11 Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, 12 who said, “Let us seize for ourselves the pastures of God.”

13 Make them like whirling dust, O my God, like chaff before the wind. 14 As fire consumes a forest, as a flame sets the mountains ablaze, 15 so pursue them with Your tempest and terrify them with Your storm.

16 Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek Your name, O the LORD. 17 Let them be put to shame and dismayed forever; let them perish in disgrace. 18 Let them know that You alone are the LORD, the Most High over all the earth.
