David's Confidence in the LORD Amidst Rebellion

1 The LORD, how my adversaries have multiplied! Many rise up against me. 2 Many are declaring concerning me, “There is no salvation for him in God.” Selah.

3 But You, the LORD, are a shield encircling me—my glory and the One who exalts me. 4 I cry aloud to the LORD, and He answers me from His holy mountain. Selah.

5 I lay down and slept; I awoke, for the LORD sustains me. 6 I will not dread myriads of people who have arrayed themselves against me round about.

7 Arise, the LORD! Deliver me, my God! For You have smitten all my enemies upon the cheek; You have shattered the teeth of the wicked. 8 Salvation belongs to the LORD; Your blessing be upon Your people. Selah.
