Joshua's Charge to the Eastern Tribes

1 Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, 2 and said to them, "You have kept all that Moses, the servant of the LORD, commanded you, and you have obeyed my voice in all that I commanded you. 3 You have not deserted your brothers these many days; rather, you have kept the charge of the commandment of the LORD your God. 4 And now the LORD your God has given rest to your brothers, as He promised them. So now turn and go to your tents, to the land of your possession, which Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you beyond the Jordan. 5 Above all, be exceedingly vigilant to observe the commandment and the law that Moses, the servant of the LORD, commanded you: to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments, to hold fast to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and all your soul." 6 So Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents.

7 To the half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had given an inheritance in Bashan, while Joshua gave the other half an inheritance west of the Jordan among their brothers. When Joshua sent them to their tents, he blessed them, 8 saying, "Return to your tents with great wealth—with very many livestock, with silver, gold, bronze, iron, and an abundance of garments. Share the spoils of your enemies with your brothers."

The Altar at Geliloth and Israel's Alarm

9 Thus the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh departed from the Israelites at Shiloh in Canaan and set out for Gilead, the land of their possession, which they had acquired in accordance with the command of the LORD through Moses. 10 When they came to Geliloth of the Jordan in Canaan, the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan—an altar of formidable size and imposing appearance. 11 The Israelites heard that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had built an altar on the border of Canaan at Geliloth of the Jordan, on the Israelite side. 12 When the whole congregation of Israel heard of it, they assembled at Shiloh to march against them in war.

The Congregation's Accusation

13 The Israelites sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to Gilead, to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, 14 together with ten leaders—one leader from each ancestral house, a leader for every tribe of Israel, each the head of an ancestral house among the clans of Israel. 15 They went to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead and spoke to them, saying: 16 "Thus says the whole congregation of the LORD: What is this treachery that you have committed today against the God of Israel by turning away from the LORD and building an altar for yourselves, so that you might rebel against the LORD? 17 Is the iniquity of Peor not enough for us—a sin from which we have not been cleansed to this day, even though a plague struck the congregation of the LORD? 18 And now you would turn away from the LORD? If you rebel against the LORD today, He will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel tomorrow. 19 If, however, the land of your possession is unclean, then cross over into the land of the LORD's possession, where the LORD's tabernacle dwells, and take possession among us. But do not rebel against the LORD, nor against us, by building an altar for yourselves besides the altar of the LORD our God. 20 Did not Achan son of Zerah commit a trespass in the devoted things, so that wrath fell upon the whole congregation of Israel? And he was but one man; yet he did not perish alone for his iniquity."

The Eastern Tribes' Defense and Reconciliation

21 Then the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the leaders of the clans of Israel: 22 "The LORD, the God of gods—the LORD, the God of gods—He knows, and Israel will know. If this was rebellion or breach of faith against the LORD, do not spare us today. 23 If we have built this altar in order to turn away from the LORD, or to offer burnt offerings or grain offerings upon it, or to sacrifice fellowship offerings upon it, then let the LORD Himself require it.

24 "But we did it out of anxious solicitude, fearing that in days to come your descendants might say to our descendants, 'What claim do you have upon the LORD, the God of Israel? 25 For the LORD has made the Jordan a boundary between us and you, O Reubenites and Gadites! You have no portion in the LORD.' So your descendants might cause our descendants to abandon the fear of the LORD.

26 "Therefore we said, 'Let us build an altar—not for burnt offerings or sacrifices— 27 but as a witness between us and you and the generations after us, that we may perform the service of the LORD in His presence with our burnt offerings, sacrifices, and peace offerings, so that your descendants will not say to our descendants in the future, "You have no portion in the LORD." '.

28 "And we thought, 'If they ever say this to us or to our descendants, we will reply, "Look at the pattern of the LORD's altar that our fathers made—not for burnt offerings or sacrifices, but as a witness between us and you."' 29 Far be it from us to rebel against the LORD or to turn away from Him today by building an altar for burnt offerings, grain offerings, or sacrifices, other than the altar of the LORD our God that stands before His tabernacle."

30 When Phinehas the priest and the leaders of the congregation and the heads of the clans of Israel who were with him heard the words that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had spoken, it pleased them. 31 Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, "Today we know that the LORD is among us, because you have not committed this breach of faith against the LORD. Now you have delivered the Israelites from the LORD's wrath." 32 Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from Gilead to Canaan, to the Israelites, and reported to them. 33 The matter pleased the Israelites, and they praised God; and they did not speak of going up against them in war to destroy the land in which the Reubenites and Gadites dwelt. 34 The Reubenites and Gadites named the altar "Witness," for they said, "It is a witness between us that the LORD is God."
