Statutes and Judgments for the Promised Land

1 These are the statutes and judgments that you shall be scrupulous to observe throughout all the days that you live upon the earth, in the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has granted to you to possess. 2 You shall utterly destroy all the places where the nations whom you are dispossessing served their gods—upon the high mountains, upon the hills, and beneath every verdant tree. 3 You shall tear down their altars, smash their pillars, burn their Asherah poles with fire, hew down the carved images of their gods, and obliterate their name from that place. 4 You shall not do thus unto the LORD your God.

The One Sanctuary for Worship and Rejoicing

5 Rather, you shall seek the place that the LORD your God will choose from among all your tribes, to establish His name there as His dwelling; thither you shall go. 6 And there you shall bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, your vows and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock. 7 There, before the LORD your God, you and your households shall eat and rejoice in all your undertakings, inasmuch as the LORD your God has blessed you. 8 You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today, each doing whatever is right in his own eyes. 9 For you have not yet come to the rest and to the inheritance that the LORD your God is giving you. 10 But when you cross the Jordan and dwell in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to inherit, and when He gives you rest from all your enemies round about, so that you dwell in security, 11 then to the place that the LORD your God will choose, to make His name dwell there, you shall bring all that I command you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, and all the choicest of your vow offerings that you vow to the LORD. 12 And you shall rejoice before the LORD your God—you, your sons and daughters, your male and female servants, and the Levite within your gates—because he has no portion or inheritance with you.

Regulations for Offerings, Meat, and Blood

13 Take heed to yourself, lest you offer your burnt offerings in any place that you see; 14 but only in the place that the LORD will choose in one of your tribes shall you do all that I command you. 15 Nevertheless, you may slaughter and eat meat within any of your gates, according to all your soul's desire, as the LORD your God has blessed you; the clean and the unclean alike may eat it, as the gazelle and as the deer. 16 Only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out on the earth like water. 17 You may not eat within your gates the tithe of your grain, or of your wine, or of your oil, or the firstborn of your herd or of your flock, or any of your vows that you vow, or your freewill offerings, or the contribution of your hand; 18 but you shall eat them before the LORD your God in the place that the LORD your God will choose—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, and the Levite within your gates—and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God in all your undertakings. 19 Take heed to yourself, lest you forsake the Levite all your days upon your land. 20 When the LORD your God enlarges your territory, as He has promised you, and you say, 'I will eat meat,' because your soul craves meat, you may eat meat, according to all your soul's desire. 21 If the place that the LORD your God chooses to put His name there is too far from you, then you may slaughter from your herd and from your flock, which the LORD has given you, as I have commanded you, and you may eat within your gates, according to all your soul's desire. 22 Just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you may eat of it; the unclean and the clean alike may eat of it. 23 Only be resolute not to eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the flesh. 24 You shall not eat it; you shall pour it out on the earth like water. 25 You shall not eat it, that it may go well with you and with your children after you, when you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD. 26 But the holy things that are yours, and your vows, you shall take and go to the place that the LORD will choose; 27 and you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, upon the altar of the LORD your God; and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out upon the altar of the LORD your God, but the flesh you may eat. 28 Observe and hear all these words that I command you, that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, when you do what is good and right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

Warning Against Idolatrous Assimilation

29 When the LORD your God cuts off from before you the nations that you are entering to dispossess, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land, 30 take heed to yourself, lest you be ensnared after them, after they are destroyed from before you, and lest you inquire concerning their gods, saying, 'How did these nations serve their gods? I too will do likewise.' 31 You shall not do so to the LORD your God, for every abomination that the LORD hates they have done for their gods; for they even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire to their gods. 32 Everything that I command you, you shall be careful to do; you shall not add to it, nor shall you take away from it.
