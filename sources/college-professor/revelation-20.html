The Binding of Satan and the Millennial Reign

1 I saw an angel descending from heaven, bearing the key to the abyss and wielding a great chain in his hand. 2 He seized the dragon—that primordial serpent, who is the devil and Satan—and bound him for a thousand years. 3 Then he hurled him into the abyss, locking and sealing it over him, so that he might no longer deceive the nations until the thousand years had run their course; afterward he must be released for a brief span.

4 I also saw thrones, and upon them sat those to whom judgment had been given. I saw the souls of those beheaded for their testimony to Jesus and for the word of God, together with those who had not worshiped the beast or its image and had not borne its mark upon forehead or hand; they came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years. 5 The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were completed. This constitutes the first resurrection. 6 Blessed and holy is anyone who shares in the first resurrection: the second death has no power over them, and they shall be priests of God and of Christ, reigning with Him for a thousand years.

Satan's Final Insurrection and Destruction

7 When the thousand years are fulfilled, Satan will be released from his prison, 8 and he will emerge to deceive the nations at the four corners of the earth—Gog and Magog—gathering them for battle, a host as innumerable as the sand of the sea. 9 They ascended over the breadth of the earth and encircled the camp of the saints and the beloved city, but fire descended from heaven and consumed them. 10 The devil, their deceiver, was cast into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet already are, and there they shall be tormented day and night forever and ever.

The Great White Throne Judgment

11 Then I saw a great white throne and the One seated upon it; from His presence earth and heaven fled, and no place was found for them. 12 I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened as well—the book of life—and the dead were judged according to what was written in the books, by their deeds.

13 The sea surrendered the dead within it, and Death and Hades surrendered the dead within them; each was judged according to his deeds. 14 Then Death and Hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death—the lake of fire. 15 If anyone was not found written in the book of life, he was cast into the lake of fire.
