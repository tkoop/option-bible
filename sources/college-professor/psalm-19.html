For the choirmaster. A psalm of David.

The Heavens Recount the Glory of God

1 The heavens recount the glory of God, and the expanse proclaims the work of His hands. 2 Day after day they pour forth speech, and night after night they disclose knowledge. 3 There is no speech, there are no words; their voice is unheard. 4 Yet their sound has gone out through all the earth, and their utterances to the end of the world. In them He has pitched a tent for the sun.

5 And it is like a bridegroom coming forth from his chamber; it rejoices like a mighty man to run its course. 6 Its rising is from one end of the heavens, and its circuit reaches the other; and nothing is hidden from its heat.

The Perfection of the LORD's Torah

7 The law of the LORD is perfect, restoring the soul; the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple. 8 The precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes. 9 The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring forever; the judgments of the LORD are true and altogether righteous. 10 More to be desired are they than gold, even than much fine gold; sweeter also than honey and the drippings of the honeycomb.

A Servant's Prayer for Integrity

11 Moreover, by them Your servant is warned; in keeping them there is great reward. 12 Who can discern his own errors? Acquit me of hidden faults. 13 Restrain Your servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me. Then I shall be blameless and innocent of great transgression. 14 Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O the LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer.
