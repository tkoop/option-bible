A Profound Lament in the Shadow of Death

1 A song, a psalm of the sons of Korah, for the director of music, according to Mahalath Leannoth; a Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. The LORD, God of my salvation, day and night I have cried out before You. 2 May my prayer come before You; incline Your ear to my supplication.

3 For my soul is overwhelmed with troubles, and my life has drawn near to Sheol. 4 I am reckoned among those who descend into the pit; I have become as a man bereft of strength. 5 I am forsaken among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom You remember no longer, cut off from Your care. 6 You have laid me in the lowest pit, in dark places, in the depths. 7 Your wrath presses hard upon me, and You have afflicted me with all Your waves. Selah.

8 You have removed my companions far from me; You have made me an object of loathing to them; I am imprisoned and cannot go forth. 9 My eye wastes away from affliction; every day I call upon You, the LORD; I spread out my hands to You. 10 Will You work wonders for the dead? Will the shades arise to praise You? Selah. 11 Is Your steadfast love recounted in the grave, Your faithfulness in Abaddon? 12 Are Your wonders known in the darkness, Your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

13 But I cry out to You, the LORD; in the morning my prayer comes before You. 14 Why, the LORD, do You reject my soul? Why do You hide Your face from me? 15 From my youth I have been afflicted and near death; I bear Your terrors; I am overwhelmed. 16 Your burning anger has swept over me; Your terrors have destroyed me. 17 They surround me like water all day long; they encompass me on every side. 18 You have removed friend and companion far from me; darkness is my only companion.
