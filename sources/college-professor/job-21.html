Job’s Rejoinder to His Friends

1 Then Job answered and said: 2 “Listen attentively to my discourse, and let this serve as your consolation. 3 Bear with me while I speak, and after I have finished, mock on. 4 Is my complaint directed against a mortal? If so, why should my spirit not chafe? 5 Turn toward me and be appalled; place a hand over the mouth. 6 Even when I recall these things, I am seized with terror, and trembling grips my flesh.

The Paradox of Wicked Prosperity

7 Why do the wicked continue to live, grow old, and even wax mighty in power? 8 Their progeny stand secure before them, and their descendants are before their eyes. 9 Their households are secure from dread, and the rod of God is not upon them. 10 Their bull breeds without failure, and their cow calves without miscarriage. 11 They send out their little ones like a flock, and their children skip about. 12 They lift up the tambourine and lyre, and make merry to the sound of the pipe. 13 They spend their days in prosperity, and in a moment they descend to Sheol.

14 Yet they say to God, “Depart from us, for we do not desire knowledge of Your ways. 15 What is the Almighty, that we should serve Him? What profit would we gain if we entreat Him?” 16 Behold, their prosperity is not in their own hand; the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

17 How often is the lamp of the wicked extinguished, and how often does their calamity come upon them? God distributes sorrows in His anger. 18 They become like stubble before the wind, and like chaff swept away by a storm. 19 God stores up his iniquity for his children; He repays him, and he shall know it. 20 His own eyes will see his destruction, and he will drink of the wrath of the Almighty. 21 For what delight has he in his household after him, when the number of his months has been cut off? 22 Shall anyone teach God knowledge, seeing that He judges those who are exalted?

Mortality, Judgment, and the Levelling Grave

23 One man dies in his full vigor, wholly at ease and secure; 24 his frame is well nourished, and the marrow of his bones is moistened. 25 Another dies in bitterness of soul, having never tasted prosperity. 26 Together they lie down in the dust, and worms cover them.

27 Behold, I know your thoughts and the schemes you devise against me. 28 For you say, “Where is the house of the nobleman, and where are the tents of the wicked?” 29 Have you not inquired of those who travel the road, and do you not recognize their signs? 30 For the wicked is spared for the day of calamity; he is led away to the day of wrath. 31 Who will declare his way to his face, and who will repay him for what he has done? 32 Yet he is carried to the grave, and a watch is kept over his tomb. 33 The clods of the valley are sweet to him; all humanity follows after him, and innumerable precede him. 34 How then can you comfort me with emptiness, when your answers leave nothing but falsehood?
