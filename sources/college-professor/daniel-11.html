The Angel's Foretelling of Persian and Greek Empires

1 In the first year of Darius the Mede, I arose to strengthen and fortify him. 2 Now I shall disclose to you the truth: three further kings will arise in Persia, and the fourth will amass wealth eclipsing all his predecessors; by the might of his riches he will rouse the whole realm against the kingdom of Greece. 3 Then a mighty king will emerge, wielding vast dominion and acting in accordance with his own volition. 4 Yet scarcely has he arisen before his kingdom is shattered and apportioned toward the four winds of heaven; it will not pass to his posterity, nor preserve the dominion he wielded, for his realm will be uprooted and conferred upon others.

The Long War Between the Kings of the South and the North

5 The king of the South will wax powerful, yet one of his commanders will become still more formidable and govern a kingdom of vast extent. 6 After some years they will enter into an alliance, and the daughter of the king of the South will come to the king of the North to secure an equitable settlement; but she will not preserve her influence, nor will his power endure. She, together with her retinue, her child, and her partisan, will be surrendered. 7 Nevertheless, a descendant from her lineage will rise in his place, advance against the army, and penetrate the fortress of the king of the North; he will fight against them and prevail. 8 He will also carry off to Egypt their gods, together with their molten images and precious vessels of silver and gold; for several years he will refrain from assailing the king of the North. 9 Then the king of the North will invade the domain of the king of the South, but will withdraw to his own land.

10 His sons will wage war and muster an immense host—a multitude of troops that will advance like a deluge, overflow, and pass through; he will again wage war as far as his fortress. 11 The king of the South will be exasperated and march out to fight the king of the North, who will raise a great army, but it will be delivered into his hand. 12 When the multitude is carried away, the king of the South will be exalted, and he will cast down tens of thousands, yet he will not be victorious. 13 For the king of the North will return and raise a multitude greater than the first; after some years he will advance with a great army and abundant materiel. 14 In those times many will rise against the king of the South; violent men among your people will exalt themselves to fulfill a vision, but they will stumble.

15 Then the king of the North will come, erect a siege ramp, and capture a fortified city. The forces of the South will not stand; even its choicest troops will lack the strength to resist. 16 The invader will act according to his own pleasure, and no one will stand before him; he will establish himself in the Glorious Land, with ruin in his hand. 17 He will set his face to come with the strength of his entire kingdom, and upright allies with him; he will accomplish this, and give him a daughter in marriage to overthrow it, but she will not stand with him or belong to him. 18 Then he will turn his face toward the coastlands and capture many; but a commander will bring his insolence to an end and turn it back upon him. 19 He will turn his face back toward the strongholds of his own land, but he will stumble, fall, and be found no more. 20 In his place one will arise who will send an oppressor through the glorious kingdom; yet within a few days he will be broken, though not in open battle.

The Rise of a Contemptible King and His Assault on the Covenant

21 In his place a contemptible person will arise, upon whom royal splendor has not been conferred; he will come in a time of tranquility and seize the kingdom through intrigue. 22 Overwhelming forces will be swept away before him and shattered, together with the prince of the covenant. 23 After an alliance is made with him, he will act deceitfully and rise to power with a small following. 24 In a time of ease he will invade the richest provinces and do what neither his fathers nor his forefathers did; he will distribute plunder, spoil, and wealth among them, and he will devise plans against their strongholds—but only for a time.

25 He will stir up his power and courage with a great army against the king of the South, and the king of the South will wage war with an exceedingly great and mighty army, but he will not stand, because plots will be devised against him. 26 His own confidants will destroy him; his army will be swept away, and many will fall slain. 27 The two kings, with their hearts inclined to evil, will speak deceitfully at the same table, but it will come to nothing, for the end will still arrive at the appointed time. 28 He will return to his land with great wealth, but his heart will be set against the holy covenant; he will take action and then return to his own land.

29 At the appointed time he will invade the South again, but this time will not be like the first or the last. 30 Ships of Kittim will come against him, and he will lose heart; then he will turn back and vent his fury against the holy covenant, taking action. He will turn back and show favor to those who forsake the holy covenant. 31 Forces from him will arise, desecrate the sanctuary fortress, abolish the daily sacrifice, and set up the abomination that desolates.

32 With flattery he will corrupt those who violate the covenant, but the people who know their God will stand firm and act. 33 Those with insight will instruct many, though for a time they will fall by sword and flame, be taken captive, and be plundered. 34 In their stumbling they will receive a little help, and many will join them insincerely. 35 Some of the wise will fall, so that they may be refined, purified, and made spotless until the time of the end, for it is still for the appointed time.

The Final Arrogance and Doom of the King

36 Then the king will do as he pleases, exalt and magnify himself above every god, and speak astonishing things against the God of gods. He will prosper until the indignation is completed, for what is decreed will be done. 37 He will show no regard for the gods of his fathers, nor for the desire of women, nor for any god, for he will magnify himself above them all. 38 Instead, he will honor a god of fortresses—a god his fathers did not know—with gold, silver, precious stones, and valuables. 39 He will attack the strongest fortresses with the help of a foreign god; he will greatly honor those who acknowledge him, and he will distribute land at a price.

40 At the time of the end, the king of the South will engage him in battle, and the king of the North will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships. He will invade many countries and sweep through them like a flood. 41 He will also enter the Glorious Land, and many countries will fall, but Edom, Moab, and the leaders of the Ammonites will be delivered from his hand. 42 He will stretch out his hand against the countries, and the land of Egypt will not escape. 43 He will gain control of the hidden treasures of gold and silver and all the riches of Egypt, with the Libyans and Cushites in his train. 44 But reports from the east and the north will alarm him, and he will set out with great fury to destroy and annihilate many. 45 He will pitch his royal tent between the seas at the beautiful holy mountain; yet he will come to his end, and there will be no one to help him.
