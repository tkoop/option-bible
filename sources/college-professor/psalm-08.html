A Psalm Celebrating the Majesty of the LORD and the Dignity of Humankind

1 O the LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens.

2 From the mouths of infants and nursing children You have established strength, because of Your adversaries, to silence the enemy and the avenger.

The Heavens and the Human Condition

3 When I contemplate Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars that You have set in place, 4 what is man that You are mindful of him, or the son of man that You attend to him?

5 Yet You have made him a little lower than the heavenly beings and crowned him with glory and honor. 6 You have given him dominion over the works of Your hands; You have put all things under his feet: 7 all sheep and oxen, and also the beasts of the field, 8 the birds of the air, and the fish of the sea, all that traverse the paths of the seas.

9 O the LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth!
