The Wilderness Journey and the Command Concerning Seir

1 Then we turned and journeyed into the wilderness by way of the Red Sea, just as the LORD had spoken to me, and for many days we circled Mount Seir. 2 Then the LORD said to me,

3 “You have circled this mountain long enough; turn northward. 4 Give this charge to the people: ‘You are passing through the territory of your brothers, the sons of Esau, who inhabit Seir; they will be exceedingly afraid of you, so guard yourselves carefully.’ 5 Do not provoke them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even a footstep's breadth, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as an inheritance. 6 You must buy food from them with silver so that you may eat, and you must also purchase water from them with silver so that you may drink. 7 For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has watched over your journey through this vast wilderness. The LORD your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing these forty years.”

8 So we passed by our brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. We turned from the Arabah road, which comes from Elath and Ezion-geber, and traveled along the road through the wilderness of Moab.

The Passing by Moab and Ammon

9 Then the LORD said to me, “Do not harass Moab or provoke them to war, for I will not give you any of their land as an inheritance, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as an inheritance.”

10 The Emim had formerly lived there—a people great and numerous and tall, like the Anakim. 11 They too were regarded as Rephaim, like the Anakim, but the Moabites called them Emim. 12 The Horites also formerly inhabited Seir, but the descendants of Esau drove them out and annihilated them before them and settled in their place, just as Israel did in the land the LORD gave them as a possession.

13 “Now arise and cross the Brook Zered.” So we crossed the Brook Zered.

14 The days we spent traveling from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Brook Zered were thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of men of war had perished from the camp, as the LORD had sworn to them. 15 Indeed, the hand of the LORD was against them, to destroy them from the camp until they were consumed. 16 When all the men of war had died off from among the people,

17 the LORD spoke to me:

18 “Today you are to pass by the border of Moab at Ar. 19 When you come near the Ammonites, do not distress them or provoke them to war, for I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites as an inheritance, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as an inheritance.”

20 It too is regarded as a land of the Rephaim; the Rephaim formerly dwelt there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim— 21 a people great and numerous and tall, like the Anakim; yet the LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, who then dispossessed them and settled in their place, 22 just as He did for the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites before them; they drove them out and settled in their place, and they remain there to this day. 23 As for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor, destroyed them and settled in their place.

The Defeat of Sihon and the Conquest of Heshbon

24 “Arise, set out, and cross the Arnon Valley. See, I have delivered Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land into your hand. Begin to take possession of it, and engage him in battle. 25 This day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you upon all the peoples under heaven, so that when they hear the report of you, they will tremble and be in anguish before you.”

26 From the wilderness of Kedemoth I sent messengers with words of peace to Sihon king of Heshbon, saying,

27 “Let me pass through your land. I will travel only along the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left. 28 Sell me food for silver so that I may eat, and give me water for silver so that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot, 29 just as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land the LORD our God is giving us.”

30 But Sihon king of Heshbon was unwilling to let us pass through, because the LORD your God had hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, in order to deliver him into your hand, as is now the case.

31 Then the LORD said to me, “See, I have begun to deliver Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession of his land.”

32 Then Sihon and all his people came out to meet us in battle at Jahaz. 33 The LORD our God delivered him over to us, and we struck him down, along with his sons and all his people. 34 At that time we captured all his cities and devoted to destruction every city—men, women, and children—leaving no survivor. 35 We took only the livestock and the spoil of the cities we had captured as our own. 36 From Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, and from the city that is in the valley, as far as Gilead, there was no city too strong for us; the LORD our God delivered all of them over to us. 37 But you did not go near the land of the Ammonites—anywhere along the Jabbok River, the cities of the hill country, or anything else the LORD our God had commanded.
