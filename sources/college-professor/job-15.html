Eliphaz Confronts Job’s Presumption

1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: 2 “Should a wise man answer with vacuous counsel, or sate his belly with hollow wind? 3 Should he contend with unprofitable discourse, or with words that yield no benefit? 4 Indeed, you even undermine piety and diminish devotion before God. 5 For your transgression tutors your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the crafty. 6 Your own mouth condemns you, not I; your own lips testify against you.”

Human Frailty Before the Divine

7 “Were you the firstborn of mankind? Were you brought forth before the hills? 8 Do you listen in on God’s council, and confine wisdom to yourself? 9 What do you know that we do not? What do you understand that is absent from us? 10 Both the gray-haired and the aged are on our side—men far older than your father. 11 Are God’s consolations too slight for you, or the gentle word addressed to you? 12 Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash with defiance, 13 so that you turn your spirit against God and pour forth such words from your mouth? 14 What is man, that he should be pure, or one born of woman, that he should be righteous? 15 If God places no trust in His holy ones, and the heavens are not pure in His sight, 16 how much less man, who is vile and corrupt, who drinks iniquity like water!”

The Doom of the Wicked

17 “Listen to me; I will explicate to you; what I have seen I will declare—what wise men have declared, concealing nothing received from their fathers—to whom alone the land was given, when no foreigner passed among them. 18 19 The wicked man writhes in pain all his days; the span of years allotted to the ruthless remains hidden. 20 Sounds of terror are in his ears; in his prosperity the destroyer attacks him. 21 He does not believe he will return from darkness; he is marked for the sword. 22 He wanders about for food, saying, ‘Where is it?’ He knows that a day of darkness is at hand. 23 Distress and anguish terrify him; they overwhelm him, like a king poised for battle. 24 For he has stretched out his hand against God and vaunts himself against the Almighty, 25 rushing headlong at Him with his thick, embossed shield. 26 Though his face is covered with fat and his waist bulges with flesh, 27 he will dwell in ruined cities, in houses not his own, which are destined to become rubble. 28 He will not grow rich; his wealth will not endure; his possessions will not overspread the land. 29 He will not escape the darkness; a flame will wither his shoots, and the breath of God's mouth will carry him away. 30 Let him not deceive himself by trusting in emptiness, for emptiness will be his recompense. 31 It will be paid in full before his time, and his branch will not flourish. 32 He will be like a vine stripped of its unripe grapes, like an olive tree casting off its blossoms. 33 For the company of the godless will be barren, and fire will consume the tents of bribery. 34 They conceive trouble and give birth to iniquity; their womb prepares deceit.”
