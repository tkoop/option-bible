On Stubbornness, Justice, and the Common Good

1 A stiff-necked man, after repeated reproofs, is suddenly shattered, and no remedy avails. 2 When the righteous increase, the people rejoice; but when the wicked rule, the people groan. 3 He who loves wisdom gladdens his father, whereas one who consorts with prostitutes squanders his substance. 4 Through justice a king establishes the land, but a man given to exactions brings it to ruin. 5 He who flatters his neighbor spreads a snare beneath his steps. 6 An evil man is caught in his own transgression, while the righteous exults and rejoices. 7 The righteous discerns the cause of the poor, but the wicked is devoid of such understanding.

On Scoffing, Contention, and the Life of the Blameless

8 Scoffers set a city ablaze, but the wise allay wrath. 9 When a wise man contends with a fool, the fool only rages and scoffs, and there is no respite. 10 Bloodthirsty men hate the blameless, but the upright concern themselves with his life. 11 A fool gives vent to all his spirit, but a wise man restrains it. 12 If a ruler hearkens to falsehood, all his servants become wicked.

On Poverty, Discipline, and Social Order

13 The poor and the oppressor meet together: The LORD gives light to the eyes of both. 14 A king who judges the poor with truth will have his throne established forever. 15 The rod and reproof impart wisdom, but a child left to himself shames his mother. 16 When the wicked multiply, transgression increases, but the righteous will witness their downfall. 17 Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will bring delights to your soul. 18 Where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint, but blessed is he who keeps the law. 19 A servant is not disciplined by words alone; though he understands, he will not answer. 20 Do you see a man hasty in his words? There is more hope for a fool than for him. 21 A servant pampered from youth will, in the end, prove a grief.

On Pride, Fear, and Final Justice

22 An angry man stirs up strife, and a hot-tempered man abounds in transgression. 23 A man's pride brings him low, but a humble spirit upholds honor. 24 He who shares with a thief hates his own soul; though he hears the curse, he does not tell. 25 The fear of man lays a snare, but he who trusts in the LORD is set securely on high. 26 Many seek the favor of a ruler, but a man's justice comes from the LORD. 27 An unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, and he whose way is upright is an abomination to the wicked.
