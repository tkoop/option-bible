The Final Blessing of Moses

1 This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the children of Israel before his death.

The LORD's Coming and the Law Given

2 He said, "The LORD came from Sinai and dawned upon them from Seir; He shone forth from Mount Paran. He came with myriads of holy ones, and from His right hand issued a flaming law for them. 3 Indeed, He loves the peoples—all His holy ones are in Your hand. They bow at Your feet and receive Your words. 4 Moses commanded us a law, an inheritance for the assembly of Jacob. 5 He was king in Jeshurun when the heads of the people assembled, the tribes of Israel together.

Blessings upon the Tribes of Israel

6 "Let Reuben live and not die, nor let his people be few.

7 "And this for Judah: 'Hear, O the LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. Let his hands be sufficient for him, and be a help against his adversaries.'

8 "And of Levi he said: 'Your Thummim and Urim belong to Your godly one, whom You tested at Massah and contended with at the waters of Meribah. 9 He said of his father and mother, 'I have not seen them,' and did not acknowledge his brothers or know his own children, for they kept Your word and guarded Your covenant. 10 They shall teach Jacob Your ordinances and Israel Your law. They shall put incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar. 11 Bless, O the LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands. Crush the loins of those who rise against him and of those who hate him, so that they rise no more.'

12 "And of Benjamin he said: 'The beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety by Him; He shelters him all the day, and he dwells between His shoulders.'

13 "And of Joseph he said: 'Blessed by the LORD is his land, with the choicest gifts of heaven, with the dew, and with the deep that lies beneath, 14 with the choicest fruits of the sun and the finest yield of the months, 15 with the best of the ancient mountains and the choicest gifts of the everlasting hills, 16 with the choice things of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of Him who dwelt in the bush. May these rest on the head of Joseph, on the brow of the one set apart among his brothers. 17 His firstborn ox has majesty, and his horns are the horns of a wild ox; with them he shall gore the peoples, all at once, to the ends of the earth. They are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh.'

18 "And of Zebulun he said: 'Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents. 19 They shall call peoples to the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness, for they shall suck the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand.'

20 "And of Gad he said: 'Blessed be he who enlarges Gad! He dwells like a lion and tears off arm and scalp. 21 He chose the best for himself, for there a commander's portion was reserved; and he came with the heads of the people. He executed the righteousness of the LORD and His judgments with Israel.'

22 "And of Dan he said: 'Dan is a lion's cub; he shall leap from Bashan.'

23 "And of Naphtali he said: 'Naphtali, satisfied with favor and full of the blessing of the LORD, take possession of the west and the south.'

24 "And of Asher he said: 'Most blessed of sons is Asher; let him be favored by his brothers, and let him dip his foot in oil. 25 Your bars shall be iron and bronze, and as your days, so shall your strength be.'

Israel's Security in the Eternal God

26 "There is none like God, O Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to your help, and in His majesty through the skies. 27 The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He will drive out the enemy before you and say, 'Destroy!' 28 So Israel shall dwell in safety, the fountain of Jacob alone, in a land of grain and new wine; yes, his heavens shall drop down dew. 29 Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help and the sword of your majesty! Your enemies shall cower before you, and you shall tread upon their high places."
