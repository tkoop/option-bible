The Blessedness of the Forgiven

1 How blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered! 2 How blessed is the person to whom the LORD does not reckon iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no guile!

Confession, Chastisement, and Deliverance

3 When I kept silent, my bones wasted away through my groaning all day long. 4 For day and night Your hand lay heavy upon me; my vitality was turned into the drought of summer. Selah.

5 I acknowledged my sin to You, and I did not conceal my iniquity; I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and You forgave the iniquity of my sin. Selah.

6 Therefore let every godly person pray to You while You may be found; surely, when the great waters surge, they will not overwhelm him. 7 You are my hiding place; You preserve me from distress; You surround me with shouts of deliverance. Selah.

Divine Instruction and Joyful Praise

8 “I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will counsel you with My eye upon you. 9 Do not be like the horse or the mule, which lack understanding, whose mouth must be controlled with bit and bridle, or they will not come near you.”

10 Many are the afflictions of the wicked, but steadfast love encompasses the one who trusts in the LORD. 11 Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, you righteous, and shout for joy, all you upright in heart!
