On Integrity, Poverty, and the Fragility of Friendship

1 An impoverished man who walks in his integrity is better than a fool whose lips are perverse. 2 Moreover, a soul bereft of knowledge is no blessing, and he who hastens with his feet sins. 3 A man's folly perverts his path, yet his heart chafes against the LORD.

4 Wealth multiplies companions, whereas the impoverished man is estranged from his neighbor. 5 A false witness shall not go unpunished, and he who breathes forth lies shall not escape. 6 Many solicit the favor of a prince, and all are friends to a benefactor. 7 All the kin of the impoverished despise him; how much more do his companions withdraw! He pursues them with entreaties, yet they have vanished.

8 He who acquires wisdom loves his own soul, and he who keeps discernment will find good. 9 A false witness shall not go unpunished, and he who breathes forth lies shall perish.

On Prudence, Patience, and the Ordering of the Household

10 Luxury is unbecoming to a fool, and still less is it seemly for a servant to rule over princes. 11 A man's prudence makes him slow to anger, and his glory lies in overlooking a transgression. 12 The king's wrath is as the roaring of a lion, but his favor is as dew upon the grass. 13 A foolish son is the ruin of his father, and a contentious wife is as a continual dripping.

14 Houses and riches are an inheritance from fathers, but a prudent wife comes from the LORD. 15 Sloth casts one into a deep sleep, and an idle soul shall suffer hunger. 16 He who keeps the commandment preserves his soul, but he who is careless in his ways shall die. 17 He who is gracious to the poor lends to the LORD, and He will repay him for his good deed.

On Discipline, Divine Counsel, and the Fate of the Wicked

18 Discipline your son while there is hope, and do not be complicit in his death. 19 A man of great wrath shall bear the penalty; if you rescue him, you will have to do it again. 20 Hear counsel and receive instruction, that you may be wise in your latter days. 21 Many are the plans in a man's heart, but the counsel of the LORD shall stand.

22 What a man desires is his kindness, and a poor man is better than a liar. 23 The fear of the LORD tends toward life, so that one may rest satisfied and remain untouched by trouble. 24 The sluggard buries his hand in the dish, yet he will not so much as bring it back to his mouth. 25 Strike a scoffer, and the simple will become wise; reprove a discerning man, and he will gain knowledge.

26 He who plunders his father and chases away his mother is a son who causes shame and reproach. 27 Cease, my son, to listen to instruction, and you will wander from the words of knowledge. 28 A worthless witness scoffs at justice, and the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity. 29 Judgments are prepared for scoffers, and stripes for the backs of fools.
