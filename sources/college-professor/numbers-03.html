The Priestly Lineage and the Death of Nadab and Abihu

1 These are the genealogies of Aaron and Moses on the day when the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. 2 These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 3 These are the names of the sons of Aaron—the anointed priests whose hands had been consecrated to priestly service. 4 But Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD when they offered unauthorized fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons; therefore Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.

The Levites Appointed and Substituted for the Firstborn

5 And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 6 “Bring the tribe of Levi near and set it before Aaron the priest, that it may minister to him. 7 They shall keep his charge and the charge of the entire congregation before the tent of meeting, to perform the service of the tabernacle. 8 They shall keep all the utensils of the tent of meeting and the charge of the sons of Israel, to perform the service of the tabernacle. 9 And you shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons; they are wholly given to him from among the sons of Israel. 10 And you shall appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall keep their priesthood; but any outsider who approaches shall be put to death.” 11 And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 12 “Behold, I have taken the Levites from among the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn that opens the womb among the sons of Israel; and the Levites shall be Mine. 13 For every firstborn is Mine: on the day that I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to Myself every firstborn in Israel, from human being to beast; they shall be Mine. I am the LORD.”

The Census and Duties of the Levites

14 And the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying, 15 “Take a census of the sons of Levi by their ancestral houses and by their clans; every male from one month old and upward you shall number.” 16 So Moses numbered them in accordance with the LORD's command, as he had been instructed.

17 And these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 18 And these are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei. 19 And the sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 20 And the sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by their ancestral houses.

21 For Gershon: the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites; these are the Gershonite clans. 22 Those of them who were numbered, all males from one month old and upward, were seven thousand five hundred. 23 The Gershonite clans shall encamp behind the tabernacle on the west. 24 And the head of the ancestral house of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael. 25 And the custody of the sons of Gershon in the tent of meeting was the tabernacle and the tent, its covering, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting, 26 together with the hangings of the court, the screen for the entrance of the court that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, and its cords, for all its service.

27 And for Kohath: the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites, and the clan of the Uzzielites; these are the Kohathite clans. 28 In number, all males from one month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, guardians of the charge of the sanctuary. 29 The clans of the sons of Kohath shall encamp on the south side of the tabernacle. 30 And the head of the ancestral house of the Kohathites, by their clans, was Elizaphan son of Uzziel. 31 And their custody was the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary with which they minister, together with the veil and all its service. 32 And the chief over the chiefs of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, with oversight of the guardians of the charge of the sanctuary.

33 For Merari: the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites; these are the Merarite clans. 34 And those of them who were numbered, all males from one month old and upward, were six thousand two hundred. 35 And the head of the ancestral house of the Merarites, by their clans, was Zuriel son of Abihail; they shall encamp on the north side of the tabernacle. 36 And the appointed custody of the sons of Merari was the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its pillars, its bases, all its vessels, and all its service, 37 together with the pillars of the court all around, their bases, their pegs, and their cords. 38 And those encamping eastward, before the tabernacle toward the sunrise—Moses and Aaron and his sons—were guardians of the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the sons of Israel; and any outsider who approached was to be put to death. 39 All the numbered of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the LORD's command by their clans, all males from one month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.

The Redemption of the Surplus Firstborn

40 And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, “Number all the firstborn males among the sons of Israel from one month old and upward, and take the number of their names. 41 And you shall take the Levites for Me—I am the LORD—in place of all the firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the cattle of the sons of Israel.” 42 So Moses numbered all the firstborn among the sons of Israel, as the LORD had commanded him. 43 And all the firstborn males, by the number of their names, from one month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three. 44 And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 45 “Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites in place of their cattle; and the Levites shall be Mine. I am the LORD. 46 And as for the redemption of the two hundred seventy-three of the firstborn of the sons of Israel who are in excess over the Levites, 47 you shall take five shekels apiece; by the shekel of the sanctuary you shall take them—twenty gerahs to the shekel— 48 and give the money to Aaron and to his sons as the redemption price for those who are in excess among them.” 49 So Moses took the redemption money from those who were in excess of the number redeemed by the Levites; 50 from the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the money: one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary. 51 And Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and to his sons, according to the word of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
