A Summons to Return to the LORD

1 “Come, let us return to the LORD, for He has torn us, yet He will heal us; He has struck us, but He will bind us up. 2 After two days He will revive us; on the third day He will raise us up, that we may live in His presence. 3 Let us press on to know the LORD; His appearing is as certain as the dawn. He will come to us like the rains, like the spring showers that water the earth.”

The LORD's Reproach of Ephemeral Devotion

4 “What shall I do with you, Ephraim? What shall I do with you, Judah? For your steadfast love is like a morning cloud, like the dew that vanishes at daybreak. 5 Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth; and your judgments go forth like the light. 6 For I desire steadfast love rather than sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings. 7 But they, like Adam, transgressed the covenant; there they betrayed Me.”

Corruption in Israel and Judah

8 Gilead is a city of evildoers, tracked with blood. 9 Like bandits lying in ambush for a man, so a company of priests murders along the road to Shechem, for they have committed an outrage.

A Harvest Appointed and a People Restored

10 “In the house of Israel I have seen an abomination: Ephraim's harlotry is there, and Israel is defiled. 11 And you too, Judah, have a harvest appointed for you, when I restore My people.”
