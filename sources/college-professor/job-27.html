Job’s Oath of Integrity

1 Job resumed his discourse and said:

“2 As God lives, who has deprived me of justice, and the Almighty, who has embittered my soul— 3 so long as breath still animates me, and the spirit of God remains in my nostrils— 4 my lips shall not articulate wickedness, nor shall my tongue promulgate deceit.

“5 Far be it from me to vindicate you; I shall not relinquish my integrity until I expire. 6 I cleave to my righteousness and will not let it go; throughout my days my conscience shall not reproach me.

The Futility of the Godless

“7 May my enemy be reckoned among the wicked, and my adversary among the unrighteous. 8 For what hope has the godless when he is cut off, when God takes away his soul? 9 Will God heed his outcry when distress overtakes him? 10 Will he find delight in the Almighty? Will he invoke God continually?

“11 I shall instruct you concerning the hand of God; I shall not conceal what pertains to the Almighty. 12 Behold, you all have seen; why then do you utter such vanity?

The Portion of the Wicked

“13 This is the portion allotted to the wicked by God, and the inheritance the ruthless receive from the Almighty: 14 if his sons proliferate, it is for the sword, and his offspring shall not be sated with bread. 15 His survivors shall be buried in death, and his widows shall not lament.

“16 Though he amass silver like dust and prepare garments like clay, 17 he may accumulate them, yet the righteous shall wear them, and the innocent shall apportion the silver. 18 He builds his house like a moth’s web, and like a shelter a watchman erects. 19 He lies down wealthy, yet he shall not be gathered; he opens his eyes, and he is gone.

“20 Terrors assail him like waters; a tempest snatches him away in the night. 21 The east wind bears him off, and he vanishes; it whirls him from his place. 22 It hurls itself upon him without pity; he seeks to escape its force. 23 It claps its hands at him and hisses at him from its own place.”
