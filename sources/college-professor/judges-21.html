The Oath and the Grief at Bethel

1 The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, saying, “No man among us shall give his daughter to Benjamin in marriage.” 2 The people went to Bethel and sat there before God until evening; then they raised their voices and wept with great sorrow. 3 And they said, “Why, O the LORD God of Israel, has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe is missing from Israel?” 4 The next day the people rose early, built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

5 The Israelites said, “Who among all the tribes of Israel failed to come up to the assembly before the the LORD?” For they had sworn a solemn oath that anyone who did not assemble before the the LORD at Mizpah would surely be put to death. 6 The Israelites grieved for their brother Benjamin and said, “Today one tribe has been cut off from Israel.” 7 “What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since we have sworn by the the LORD not to give them any of our daughters in marriage?”

The Judgment upon Jabesh-gilead

8 They said, “Who among the tribes of Israel did not come up to the the LORD at Mizpah?” And indeed, no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp, to the assembly. 9 For when the people were mustered, no inhabitant of Jabesh-gilead was found there. 10 So the congregation sent twelve thousand men of valor and commanded them, “Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the sword, including the women and children.” 11 “This is what you are to do: you shall utterly destroy every male, and every woman who has known a man intimately.” 12 They found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young women who were virgins, who had not known a man intimately; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, in the land of Canaan.

13 Then the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the Benjaminites who were at the rock of Rimmon, proclaiming peace to them. 14 Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the women of Jabesh-gilead, but there were not enough for them. 15 The people grieved for Benjamin, because the the LORD had made a breach among the tribes of Israel.

Provision for the Survivors of Benjamin

16 Then the elders of the congregation said, “What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?” 17 They added, “There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe is not wiped out from Israel.” 18 “But we cannot give them any of our daughters as wives, for the Israelites have sworn, saying, ‘Cursed is he who gives a wife to Benjamin.’” 19 They observed, “There is the annual festival of the the LORD at Shiloh, which lies north of Bethel, east of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.”

20 They commanded the Benjaminites, “Go and lie in wait in the vineyards, 21 and when you see the daughters of Shiloh coming out to dance, then you are to come out of the vineyards and seize for yourselves each a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin. 22 And it shall be that, when their fathers or brothers come to complain to us, we will say to them, ‘Do us the favor of helping them, because we did not take a wife for each man in the battle; for you did not give them wives at this time—otherwise you would be guilty.’” 23 The Benjaminites did so: they carried off wives, according to their number, from the dancers whom they seized, and went and returned to their inheritance, rebuilt the cities, and dwelt in them.

24 The Israelites departed from there at that time, each to his tribe and family, and went out from there, each to his own inheritance. 25 In those days there was no king in Israel; every man did what was right in his own eyes.
