Salutation and Apostolic Thanksgiving

1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, according to the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus, 2 to Timothy, my beloved child: grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. 3 I give thanks to God, whom I serve with a pure conscience, as my forebears did, since I retain an unceasing remembrance of you night and day in my prayers, 4 remembering your tears and longing to see you, so that I may be filled with joy. 5 I have been reminded of the unfeigned faith that dwells in you, which first took residence in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that it dwells in you as well. 6 For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands. 7 For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love, and self-discipline.

Courage in Suffering and the Gospel's Revelation

8 Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord, nor of me, His prisoner; rather, share in suffering for the gospel according to the power of God, 9 who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to His own purpose and grace, granted to us in Christ Jesus before the ages began, 10 and now revealed through the appearing of our Savior, Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel. 11 For this gospel I was appointed a herald, an apostle, and a teacher. 12 For this reason I also suffer these things, yet I am not ashamed, for I know the One in whom I have placed my trust, and I am persuaded that He is able to guard the deposit entrusted to me until that day.

Sound Teaching and the Spirit's Custody

13 Hold fast the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. 14 Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit, who dwells in us.

Desertion, Loyalty, and Final Commendation

15 You know that all who are in Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes. 16 May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain. 17 On the contrary, when he was in Rome, he searched diligently and found me. 18 May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord in that day—and you know better than I how many services he rendered in Ephesus.
