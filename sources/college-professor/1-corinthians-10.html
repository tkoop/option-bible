Typological Warnings from Israel's Wilderness Generation

1 For I am unwilling that you should remain unaware, brothers, that all our ancestors were beneath the cloud and all traversed the sea, 2 and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea, 3 and all partook of the same spiritual nourishment, 4 and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ. 5 Nevertheless, God took no pleasure in most of them, for they were laid low in the wilderness. 6 Now these things transpired as examples for us, that we might not crave evil, as they craved it. 7 Do not be idolaters, as some of them were; as it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to make merry." 8 We must not engage in sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in a single day. 9 We must not put Christ to the test, as some of them did and were destroyed by serpents, 10 nor grumble, as some of them did and were destroyed by the Destroyer. 11 Now these things happened to them as an example, but they were written for our instruction, upon whom the ends of the ages have come. 12 Therefore, let anyone who supposes himself to stand take heed, lest he fall. 13 No temptation has overtaken you except what is common to humanity. God is faithful, and He will not permit you to be tempted beyond your ability; but with the temptation He will also furnish the way of escape, so that you may be able to endure it.

Idolatry, the Lord's Table, and Fellowship with Demons

14 Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry. 15 I address you as discerning people; judge for yourselves what I say. 16 The cup of blessing that we bless—is it not a participation in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break—is it not a participation in the body of Christ? 17 Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread. 18 Consider Israel according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices participants in the altar? 19 What then do I mean? That food offered to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything? 20 No, but I do say that what the Gentiles sacrifice they sacrifice to demons and not to God; and I do not want you to become participants with demons. 21 You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; You cannot partake of the table of the Lord and the table of demons. 22 Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?

Liberty Tempered by Love and Devotion to God's Glory

23 "Everything is permissible," but not everything is beneficial. "Everything is permissible," but not everything is edifying. 24 Let no one seek his own interest, but that of his neighbor. 25 Eat whatever is sold in the meat market, making no inquiry on the ground of conscience. 26 For "the earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof." 27 If an unbeliever invites you to dinner and you are disposed to go, eat whatever is set before you, making no inquiry on the ground of conscience. 28 But if someone says to you, "This has been offered in sacrifice," then do not eat it, for the sake of the one who informed you, and for conscience' sake. 29 I do not mean his own conscience, but the other's. For why should my liberty be judged by another's conscience? 30 If I partake with thankfulness, why am I slandered concerning that for which I give thanks? 31 So whether you eat or you drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God. 32 Give no offense to Jews or to Greeks or to the church of God, 33 just as I seek to please all people in all things, not seeking my own advantage, but that of the many, that they may be saved.
