A Psalm of Thanksgiving

1 Shout joyfully unto the the LORD, all the earth. 2 Serve the the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with exultant song. 3 Know that the the LORD—He is God; it is He who fashioned us, and not we ourselves; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture. 4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks unto Him, bless His Name. 5 For the the LORD is good; His steadfast love endures forever, and His faithfulness unto all generations.
