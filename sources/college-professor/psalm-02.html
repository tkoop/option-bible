The Futile Conspiracy of Nations and the Sovereign Installation of the LORD's Anointed

1 Why do the nations tumultuously assemble, and why do the peoples devise emptiness? 2 The kings of the earth take their stand, and the rulers take counsel together against the LORD and against His Anointed, saying, 3 “Let us shatter their fetters and hurl their cords away from us.”

4 He who is enthroned in heaven laughs; the Lord derides them. 5 Then He addresses them in His wrath and terrifies them in His burning anger, saying, 6 “Yet I have installed My King upon Zion, My holy mountain.”

7 I will proclaim the decree: the LORD said to me, “You are My Son; today I have begotten you. 8 Ask of Me, and I will give the nations as Your inheritance and the ends of the earth as Your possession. 9 You shall shatter them with an iron rod; you shall dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”

10 Now therefore, exercise prudence, O kings; receive correction, O judges of the earth. 11 Serve the LORD with reverent awe, and rejoice with trembling. 12 Do homage to the Son, lest He be incensed and you perish along the way, for His wrath flares up in an instant. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.
