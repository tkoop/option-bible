Prologue and the Announcement to Zechariah

1 Inasmuch as many have undertaken to compile a narrative concerning the matters that have been fulfilled among us, 2 just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and ministers of the word delivered them to us, 3 it seemed good to me also, having followed all things closely from the outset, to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus, 4 that you may know the certainty of the things concerning which you have been instructed.

5 In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah, of the division of Abijah, whose wife Elizabeth was descended from the daughters of Aaron. 6 Both were righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord. 7 Yet they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both had advanced far in years.

8 Now while he was serving as priest before God in the order of his division, 9 it fell to him by lot, according to priestly custom, to enter the sanctuary of the Lord and burn incense. 10 At the hour of incense, the whole multitude of the people was praying outside.

11 Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense. 12 Zechariah, upon seeing him, was deeply shaken, and dread seized him.

13 The angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your supplication has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, whom you are to name John. 14 You shall have joy and exultation, and many will rejoice at his birth. 15 For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord; he shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother’s womb. 16 He shall turn many of the sons of Israel back to the Lord their God. 17 He shall go before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers toward their children, and the disobedient toward the prudence of the righteous, thereby preparing for the Lord a people made ready.”

18 Zechariah said to the angel, “How am I to know this? For I am an aged man, and my wife has advanced far in years.”

19 The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God and has been sent to speak with you and to announce this good news to you. 20 Behold, you shall be mute and unable to speak until the day these things come to pass, because you did not give credence to my words, which shall be fulfilled in their appointed season.”

21 Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah and marveled that he lingered so long in the sanctuary. 22 When he emerged, he could not speak to them, and they perceived that he had beheld a vision in the sanctuary; he kept making signs to them and remained mute. 23 When the days of his service had been completed, he returned to his home.

24 After these days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and for five months she secluded herself, saying, 25 “Thus has the Lord dealt with me in the days when He looked upon me, to take away my reproach among men.”

The Annunciation to Mary

26 In the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was dispatched by God to a city of Galilee called Nazareth, 27 to a virgin betrothed to a man named Joseph, of the house of David; the virgin’s name was Mary.

28 He entered where she was and said, “Hail, you who are highly favored; the Lord is with you.” 29 She was deeply unsettled by this utterance and pondered what manner of salutation it might be.

30 The angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found grace with God. 31 Behold, you shall conceive in your womb and bring forth a Son, whom you are to name Jesus. 32 He shall be great and shall be called the Son of the Most High; and the Lord God shall give Him the throne of His father David. 33 He shall reign over the house of Jacob forever, and His kingdom shall know no end.”

34 Mary said to the angel, “How can this be, seeing that I am a virgin?”

35 The angel answered her, “The Holy Spirit shall come upon you, and the power of the Most High shall overshadow you; therefore the child to be born shall be called holy—the Son of God. 36 Behold, even your kinswoman Elizabeth has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren. 37 For with God nothing shall be impossible.”

38 Mary said, “Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word.” And the angel departed from her.

Mary’s Visit to Elizabeth and the Birth of John

39 In those days Mary set out and journeyed with haste into the hill country, to a city of Judah, 40 where she entered the house of Zechariah and saluted Elizabeth. 41 When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the babe leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. 42 She exclaimed with a loud cry, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb! 43 And how is it granted to me that the mother of my Lord should come to me? 44 For behold, as soon as the voice of your greeting reached my ears, the babe in my womb leaped for joy. 45 Blessed is she who believed that the things spoken to her by the Lord would be fulfilled.”

46 Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord, 47 and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior, 48 for He has looked upon the lowliness of His handmaid; for behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed, 49 for He who is mighty has done great things for me, and holy is His name; 50 and His mercy is unto generations and generations of those who fear Him. 51 He has shown strength with His arm; He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts; 52 He has brought down the mighty from their thrones and exalted the lowly; 53 He has filled the hungry with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty; 54 He has helped His servant Israel, in remembrance of His mercy, 55 as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed forever.”

56 Mary stayed with her about three months and then returned to her own home.

57 Now the time was fulfilled for Elizabeth to be delivered, and she gave birth to a son. 58 Her neighbors and kinsfolk heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy toward her, and they rejoiced with her. 59 On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were about to name him Zechariah after his father, 60 but his mother answered, “No; he is to be called John.”

61 They said to her, “None of your kindred bears this name.”

62 They made signs to his father, asking what he would have him called. 63 He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” And they all marveled. 64 Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue freed, and he spoke, blessing God. 65 Awe fell upon all their neighbors, and all these things were noised abroad throughout the hill country of Judea. 66 All who heard them kept them in their hearts, saying, “What then will this child become?” For the hand of the Lord was with him.

Zechariah’s Prophecy

67 His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying, 68 “Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for He has visited and redeemed His people 69 and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of His servant David, 70 as He spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from of old, 71 that we should be saved from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us; 72 to show the mercy promised to our fathers and to remember His holy covenant, 73 the oath that He swore to our father Abraham, to grant us 74 that we, being delivered from the hand of our enemies, might serve Him without fear, 75 in holiness and righteousness before Him all our days. 76 And you, child, will be called the prophet of the Most High; for you will go before the Lord to prepare His ways, 77 to give knowledge of salvation to His people in the forgiveness of their sins, 78 because of the tender mercy of our God, whereby the sunrise shall visit us from on high 79 to give light to those who sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.”

80 The child grew and waxed strong in spirit, and he remained in the desolate places until the day of his manifestation to Israel.
