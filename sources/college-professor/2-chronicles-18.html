Jehoshaphat's Alliance with Ahab

1 Now Jehoshaphat possessed wealth and honor in extraordinary abundance, and he cemented a marriage alliance with Ahab. 2 After several years had elapsed, he descended to Ahab in Samaria; Ahab slaughtered sheep and oxen in prodigious numbers for him and for the retinue accompanying him, and he induced Jehoshaphat to ascend against Ramoth-gilead.

The Inquiry of the Prophets

3 Ahab, king of Israel, said to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, "Will you go with me to Ramoth-gilead?"

Jehoshaphat replied, "I am as you are, my people as your people; I shall be with you in the war."

4 Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please inquire today for the word of the LORD."

5 Then the king of Israel assembled the prophets, four hundred men, and asked them, "Shall we go to war against Ramoth-gilead, or shall I forbear?"

They answered, "Go up, for God will give it into the hand of the king."

6 Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not yet a prophet of the LORD here, that we may inquire of Him?"

7 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is yet one man by whom we may inquire of the LORD—Micaiah son of Imlah—but I hate him, for he never prophesies good concerning me, but evil."

Jehoshaphat said, "Let not the king say so."

8 Then the king of Israel summoned an officer and said, "Bring Micaiah son of Imlah quickly."

9 Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, were sitting each upon his throne, arrayed in robes, at the threshing floor by the entrance of the gate of Samaria, while all the prophets prophesied before them.

10 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made for himself horns of iron and said, "Thus says the LORD: 'With these you shall gore Aram until they are consumed.'"

11 All the prophets prophesied likewise, saying, "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and prosper, for the LORD will give it into the hand of the king."

12 The messenger who had gone to summon Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Behold, the words of the prophets are with one accord favorable to the king; let your word, I pray you, be like one of theirs, and speak favorably."

Micaiah's Vision and the Lying Spirit

13 Micaiah said, "As the LORD lives, what my God says, that I will speak."

14 When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear?"

Micaiah said, "Go up and prosper; they shall be given into your hand."

15 The king said to him, "How many times shall I adjure you to speak to me nothing but the truth in the name of the LORD?"

16 Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains, like sheep that have no shepherd; and the LORD said, 'These have no master; let each return to his house in peace.'"

17 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but evil?"

18 Micaiah said, "Therefore hear the word of the LORD: I saw the LORD sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven standing on His right hand and on His left. 19 And the LORD said, 'Who will entice Ahab, king of Israel, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?' One spoke saying this, and another saying that. 20 Then a spirit came forward and stood before the LORD and said, 'I will entice him.' And the LORD said to him, 'By what means?' 21 He said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' And the LORD said, 'You shall entice him and shall succeed; go out and do so.' 22 Now therefore, behold, the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of these prophets of yours, and the LORD has spoken evil concerning you.'"

23 Then Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek and said, "Which way did the Spirit of the LORD go from me to speak to you?"

24 Micaiah said, "Behold, you shall see on the day when you go into an inner chamber to hide yourself."

25 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Amon, governor of the city, and to Joash, the king's son, 26 and say, 'Thus says the king: Put this fellow in prison and feed him with bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace.'"

27 Micaiah said, "If you return in peace, the LORD has not spoken by me." And he said, "Hear, all you peoples."

The Battle at Ramoth-gilead

28 So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, went up to Ramoth-gilead.

29 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into battle; but you put on your robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went into battle.

30 Now the king of Aram had commanded the captains of his chariots, "Fight neither with the small nor with the great, but only with the king of Israel."

31 When the captains saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "It is the king of Israel." They turned to fight against him; Jehoshaphat cried out, and the LORD helped him; God moved them away from him.

32 When the captains saw that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back from pursuing him.

33 A certain man drew his bow at random and struck the king of Israel between the joints of the armor and the breastplate. He said to the charioteer, "Turn your hand and carry me out of the camp, for I am wounded."

34 The battle increased that day; the king of Israel was propped up in the chariot facing the Arameans until evening; and at sunset he died.
