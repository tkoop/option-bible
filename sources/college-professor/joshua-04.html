The Consummation of the Crossing and the Institution of Memorial Stones

1 Upon the completion of the entire nation’s passage across the Jordan, the LORD spoke to Joshua, saying, 2 “Appropriate for yourselves twelve men from the people—one representative from each tribe— 3 and charge them thus: ‘Take up for yourselves twelve stones from this place, from the very midst of the Jordan, from the locus where the priests’ feet stood firm; transport them with you and deposit them in the encampment where you lodge this night.’”

Joshua Summons the Representatives and Issues His Charge

4 So Joshua summoned the twelve men whom he had appointed from among the Israelites—one man from each tribe. 5 And Joshua said to them, “Cross over before the ark of the LORD your God into the midst of the Jordan, and let each man hoist a stone upon his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Israel, 6 that this may serve as a sign among you; when your children inquire tomorrow, saying, ‘What mean these stones to you?’ 7 then you shall answer them, ‘Because the waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD; when it crossed over in the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off. And these stones shall be for a memorial to the sons of Israel forever.’” 8 And the sons of Israel did so as Joshua commanded: they lifted twelve stones out of the midst of the Jordan, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Israel, as the LORD had spoken to Joshua; and they carried them over with them to the encampment and laid them down there. 9 Joshua also erected twelve stones in the midst of the Jordan, at the place where the feet of the priests bearing the ark of the covenant had stood; and they remain there to this day. 10 For the priests bearing the ark stood in the midst of the Jordan until everything was finished that the LORD commanded Joshua to speak to the people, according to all that Moses had commanded Joshua; and the people hastened and crossed over. 11 And when all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the LORD crossed over, and the priests, before the people. 12 The Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over in martial array before the Israelites, as Moses had spoken to them, 13 about forty thousand equipped for battle crossing over before the LORD to the plains of Jericho. 14 On that day the LORD exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they revered him, as they had revered Moses, all the days of his life.

The Priests Are Commanded to Ascend from the River

15 Then the LORD spoke to Joshua, saying, 16 “Command the priests bearing the ark of the testimony to ascend out of the Jordan.”

17 So Joshua commanded the priests, saying, “Ascend out of the Jordan.” 18 And when the priests bearing the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up out of the midst of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests’ feet were lifted up to the dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and surged over all its banks as before. 19 The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and encamped at Gilgal on the eastern edge of Jericho. 20 And those twelve stones which they had taken out of the Jordan Joshua set up at Gilgal.

The Meaning of the Stones Declared to Israel

21 And he said to the sons of Israel, “When your children ask their fathers tomorrow, saying, ‘What are these stones?’ 22 then you shall make known to your children, saying, ‘Israel crossed over this Jordan on dry land, 23 because the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan from before you until you crossed over, as the LORD your God did to the Red Sea, which He dried up from before us until we crossed over, 24 so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is mighty, that you may fear the LORD your God forever.’”
