A Song of Ascents: From the Abyss to the Assurance of Redemption

1 From the abyssal depths I have cried out to You, the LORD; 2 O Lord, give heed to my voice, and let Your ears be attentively inclined to the voice of my supplications.

3 If You, the LORD, should keep a strict reckoning of iniquities, O Lord, who could stand? 4 Yet with You there is forgiveness, that You may be revered.

5 I wait expectantly for the LORD; my soul waits, and in His word I place my hope. 6 My soul waits for the Lord more than sentinels wait for the dawn—more than sentinels for the morning.

7 O Israel, place your hope in the LORD, for with the LORD there is covenantal lovingkindness, and with Him is plenteous redemption. 8 And He will redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
