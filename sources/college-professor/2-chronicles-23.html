Jehoiada’s Covenant and the Restoration of the Davidic King

1 In the seventh year, Jehoiada strengthened himself and entered into covenant with the commanders of hundreds—Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri. 2 They traversed Judah, assembling the Levites from every city of Judah together with the patriarchal heads of Israel, and they converged upon Jerusalem.

3 The whole assembly then covenanted with the king in the house of God. Jehoiada declared to them, “Indeed, the king’s son shall reign, precisely as the LORD has spoken concerning the descendants of David. 4 This is what you are to do: one-third of you who enter on the Sabbath—namely the priests and Levites serving as gatekeepers at the thresholds—shall guard the thresholds. 5 Another third shall be at the king’s house, and another third at the Foundation Gate, while all the people remain in the courts of the house of the LORD. 6 No one may enter the house of the LORD except the priests and those Levites who minister, for they are consecrated; yet all the people shall keep the watch of the LORD. 7 The Levites must encircle the king, each man bearing his weapon in hand; anyone entering the house shall be put to death. They are to remain with the king in his coming and going.”

8 The Levites and all Judah executed everything that Jehoiada the priest had commanded: each man took his contingent, both those coming on duty and those departing on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada had not dismissed the divisions.

9 Jehoiada the priest distributed to the commanders of hundreds the spears, shields, and bucklers that had belonged to King David and were kept in the house of God.

10 He stationed all the people, every man with his weapon in hand, around the king—from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and the house.

The Coronation and the People’s Acclamation

11 Then they brought out the king’s son, placed the crown upon him, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and they cried, “Long live the king!”

Athaliah’s Downfall

12 When Athaliah heard the clamor of the people running and acclaiming the king, she came to the people in the house of the LORD.

13 She beheld the king standing by his pillar at the entrance, with the officers and trumpeters beside the king; all the people of the land rejoiced and sounded trumpets, while the singers, accompanied by musical instruments, led the praise. Then Athaliah tore her garments and cried, “Treason! Treason!”

14 Jehoiada the priest brought forth the commanders of hundreds who were over the army and said to them, “Bring her out between the ranks, and put to the sword anyone who follows her.” For the priest had said, “She must not be put to death in the house of the LORD.”

15 So they seized her, and she went by way of the entrance of the Horse Gate to the king’s house, where they put her to death.

Covenant Renewal, Temple Reform, and the King’s Enthronement

16 Jehoiada then covenanted between himself, all the people, and the king, that they should be the people of the LORD.

17 All the people went to the temple of Baal, tore it down, smashed its altars and images, and slew Mattan, priest of Baal, before the altars.

18 Jehoiada appointed the priests and Levites over the house of the LORD, according to the divisions David had established for the house of the LORD, to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, as written in the Law of Moses, with rejoicing and singing, according to David’s ordinance.

19 He stationed gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD, so that no one unclean in any respect might enter.

20 He took the captains of hundreds, the nobles, the governors of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They passed through the Upper Gate into the king’s house and seated the king upon the throne of the kingdom.

21 All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet, for Athaliah had been put to the sword.
