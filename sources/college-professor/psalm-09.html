A Psalm of David, for the Choirmaster, upon the Melody “Death of the Son”

1I shall render thanksgiving unto the LORD with the totality of my heart; I shall recount all Your prodigious deeds. 2I shall exult and be jubilant in You; I shall chant praise unto Your name, O Most High. 3When my enemies retreat, they stumble and perish before You. 4For You have vindicated my right and my cause; You sit enthroned as righteous Judge. 5You have rebuked the nations; You have annihilated the wicked; You have effaced their name forever and ever. 6The enemy is brought to utter ruin—perpetual desolations; You have uprooted their cities, and even their remembrance has perished.

7But the LORD sits enthroned forever; He has established His throne for judgment. 8He judges the world in righteousness; He renders judgment upon the peoples with equity. 9The LORD is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of distress. 10Those who know Your name trust in You, for You, O the LORD, have not forsaken those who seek You. 11Sing praises to the LORD, enthroned in Zion; proclaim among the peoples His deeds. 12For He who avenges bloodshed remembers them; He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.

13Be gracious to me, O the LORD; behold my affliction from those who hate me, You who lift me up from the gates of death. 14That I may declare all Your praise in the gates of the Daughter of Zion, I shall rejoice in Your salvation. 15The nations have sunk into the pit they made; their foot is caught in the net they concealed. 16The LORD has made Himself known; He has executed judgment; the wicked are snared by the work of their own hands. Higgaion. Selah.

17The wicked shall return to Sheol—all the nations that forget God. 18For the needy shall not be forgotten forever, nor shall the hope of the afflicted perish in perpetuity. 19Arise, O the LORD; let not man prevail; let the nations be judged before You. 20Strike them with terror, O the LORD; let the nations know that they are but men. Selah.
