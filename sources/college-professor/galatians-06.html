On the Restoration of the Fallen and the Fulfillment of Christ's Law

1 Brethren, should any person be overtaken in a transgression, you who are spiritual must restore such a one in a spirit of meekness, scrutinizing yourself, lest you too be tempted. 2 Bear one another's burdens, and in this manner you will fulfill the law of Christ. 3 For if anyone reckons himself to be something while he is nothing, he beguiles himself. 4 Rather, let each person prove his own work, and then he shall have ground for exultation in himself alone, and not in comparison with another. 5 For each shall bear his own proper load. 6 Moreover, let the one instructed in the word share in all good things with the instructor.

The Inevitable Harvest of Sowing and the Call to Persevering Good

7 Do not be deceived: God is not mocked, for whatever a person sows, that he shall also reap. 8 For whoever sows unto his own flesh shall reap from the flesh corruption; but whoever sows unto the Spirit shall reap from the Spirit life everlasting. 9 And let us not grow weary in well-doing, for at the appointed season we shall reap, if we do not lose heart. 10 Therefore, as we have opportunity, let us work good toward all, and especially toward those of the household of faith.

Paul's Concluding Admonition Concerning the Cross and the New Creation

11 Behold with what prodigious script I have written to you by my own hand. 12 Those who wish to present a seemly appearance in the flesh compel you to be circumcised, solely that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ. 13 For not even those who are circumcised observe the law; rather, they desire that you be circumcised, that they may boast in your flesh. 14 But far be it from me to boast, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world is crucified to me, and I to the world. 15 For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision avails anything, but a new creation. 16 And as many as shall walk by this rule, peace and mercy rest upon them, and upon the Israel of God. 17 Henceforth let no one trouble me, for I bear in my body the marks of Jesus. 18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brethren. Amen.
