A New Song of Divine Triumph and Cosmic Praise

1 Sing to the LORD a new song, for He has wrought wonders; His right hand and holy arm have secured triumph for Him. 2 The LORD has made known His salvation, and He has unveiled His righteousness before the eyes of the nations. 3 He has remembered His loving devotion and faithfulness toward the house of Israel, and all the ends of the earth have beheld the salvation of our God.

4 Shout for joy to the LORD, all the earth; break forth into jubilant song and make music. 5 Make music to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and the resonance of song. 6 With trumpets and the blast of the ram’s horn, shout in triumph before the LORD, the King.

7 Let the sea roar, and all that fills it; let the world and its inhabitants resound. 8 Let the rivers clap their hands; let the mountains sing together for joy. 9 Let them do so before the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth; He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples with equity.
