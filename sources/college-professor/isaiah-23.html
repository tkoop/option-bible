The Oracle Against Tyre and the Coastlands

1 The oracle concerning Tyre: Lament, O ships of Tarshish, for Tyre is devastated, bereft of dwelling and haven; from the land of Cyprus this is disclosed to them. 2 Be silent, O inhabitants of the coastland; you merchants of Sidon, whose traders have traversed the sea and enriched you. 3 By mighty waters the grain of the Shihor, the harvest of the Nile, was her revenue, and she became the emporium of the nations.

4 Be put to shame, O Sidon, fortress of the sea, for the sea itself has declared: "I have not travailed, nor brought forth children; I have not reared young men, nor nurtured maidens." 5 When the tidings reach Egypt, they will be seized with anguish at the tidings of Tyre. 6 Pass over to Tarshish; howl, O inhabitants of the coastland.

7 Is this your exultant city, whose antiquity extends to the days of old, whose own feet bore her to sojourn afar?

The Divine Counsel Behind Tyre's Humiliation

8 Who has devised this against Tyre, the bestower of crowns, whose merchants are princes, whose traders are the honored of the earth? 9 The LORD of Hosts has purposed it, to profane all prideful glory, to abase all the honored of the earth.

10 Overflow your land like the Nile, O Daughter of Tarshish; there is no longer any restraint. 11 He stretched out His hand over the sea; He agitated the kingdoms. The LORD has issued a decree concerning Canaan to demolish its strongholds. 12 And He said, "You shall exult no more, O oppressed Virgin Daughter of Sidon. Arise, pass over to Cyprus; even there you shall find no repose."

13 Behold the land of the Chaldeans—this people is no more; Assyria destined it for the beasts of the desert. They erected their siege towers; they dismantled its palaces; they reduced it to ruin. 14 Wail, O ships of Tarshish, for your stronghold is devastated.

Tyre's Seventy-Year Exile and Restoration

15 In that day Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years—the lifetime of a single king. Then, at the end of seventy years, it will befall Tyre as in the song of the prostitute:

16 "Take a harp, go about the city, O forgotten prostitute; play skillfully, sing a multitude of songs, that you may be remembered."

17 And at the end of seventy years, the LORD will visit Tyre. Then she will return to her hire and will prostitute herself with all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth. 18 Yet her profits and her earnings will be consecrated to the LORD; they will not be treasured up or hoarded, but her profits will belong to those who dwell before the LORD, to provide abundant food and fine clothing.
