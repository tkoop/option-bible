A Psalm of David Composed in the Wilderness of Judah

1 O God, You are my God; I seek You with ardent devotion. My soul thirsts for You; my flesh languishes for You in a parched and exhausted land, devoid of water. 2 I have beheld You in the sanctuary, to gaze upon Your power and Your glory. 3 Because Your steadfast love is better than life itself, my lips will extol You. 4 Thus I will bless You throughout my days; in Your name I will lift up my hands.

5 My soul shall be sated as with the richest fare; my mouth will praise You with exultant lips. 6 When I remember You upon my bed, I meditate upon You through the watches of the night. 7 For You have been my help; and in the protective shadow of Your wings I will sing for joy. 8 My soul clings to You; Your right hand sustains me.

9 But those who seek my life in order to destroy it will descend into the lowest regions of the earth. 10 They will be delivered to the sword; they will become carrion for jackals. 11 Yet the king will rejoice in God; everyone who swears by Him will exult, for the mouths of those who speak falsehood will be stopped.
