A Supplication for Divine Refuge and the Perpetuation of Royal Favor

1 Hear my cry, O God; attend to my prayer. 2 From the farthest reaches of the earth I call to You when my heart is overwhelmed; lead me to the rock that rises above me. 3 For You have been my refuge, a fortified tower against the foe.

4 I shall dwell in Your tent forevermore; I shall take refuge beneath the shelter of Your wings. Selah. 5 For You, O God, have heard my vows; You have granted me the inheritance of those who fear Your name.

6 You shall add days to the king; his years shall extend through generation upon generation. 7 He shall sit in the presence of God forever; steadfast love and truth shall preserve him. 8 So I will sing praise to Your name forever, that I may discharge my vows day by day.
