The Statutory Law of the Guilt Offering

1 This is the law of the guilt offering, which is most holy: 2 the guilt offering shall be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, and its blood shall be sprinkled round about the altar. 3 All of its fat shall be offered—namely, the fat tail and the fat enveloping the entrails, 4 together with the two kidneys and the fat upon them near the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which the priest shall excise along with the kidneys. 5 The priest shall incinerate them upon the altar as an offering made by fire to the LORD; it is a guilt offering. 6 Every male among the priests may partake of it; it shall be consumed in a holy place, for it is most holy. 7 As the sin offering is, so is the guilt offering; one law governs both, and it pertains to the priest who effects atonement thereby.

8 As for the priest who offers a burnt offering on behalf of another, the hide of the burnt offering he has presented shall accrue to that priest. 9 Likewise, every grain offering baked in an oven, or prepared upon a griddle or in a pan, shall belong to the priest who presents it. 10 Every grain offering, whether commingled with oil or dry, shall be apportioned equally among all the sons of Aaron.

Regulations for Peace Offerings and Their Consumption

11 This is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings that one may present to the LORD: 12 if he presents it as a thanksgiving, then, in conjunction with the sacrifice of thanksgiving, he shall offer unleavened cakes mingled with oil, unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mingled with oil. 13 With cakes of leavened bread he shall present his offering, together with the sacrifice of his peace offering of thanksgiving. 14 From these he shall present one cake from each kind as a contribution to the LORD; it shall belong to the priest who sprinkles the blood of the peace offerings.

15 The flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offering of thanksgiving shall be eaten on the day of his offering; he shall leave none of it until morning. 16 If, however, the sacrifice of his offering is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he presents his sacrifice, and what remains of it may be eaten on the next day. 17 But the remainder of the flesh of the sacrifice shall be consumed by fire on the third day. 18 If any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings is eaten on the third day, it shall not be accepted, nor shall it be credited to him who offered it; it shall be an abomination, and the person who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.

19 The flesh that touches anything unclean shall not be eaten; it shall be burned in the fire. As for the flesh, all who are clean may eat of it. 20 But the person who eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings that belong to the LORD, while his uncleanness is upon him, that person shall be cut off from his people. 21 If a person touches anything unclean—whether human uncleanness, an unclean beast, or any unclean detestable thing—and then eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings that belong to the LORD, that person shall be cut off from his people.

Prohibitions Against Fat and Blood

22 The LORD said to Moses, 23 “Speak to the Israelites and say: You are not to eat any of the fat of an ox, a sheep, or a goat. 24 The fat of an animal found dead or torn by beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must not eat it. 25 For anyone who eats the fat of an animal from which an offering by fire is presented to the LORD, the person who eats it shall be cut off from his people. 26 You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal in any of your dwellings. 27 Whoever eats blood shall be cut off from his people.”

Priestly Portions and Concluding Summary

28 The LORD said to Moses, 29 “Speak to the Israelites and say: He who presents the sacrifice of his peace offering to the LORD is to bring to the LORD an offering from it. 30 With his own hands he is to bring the offerings made by fire to the LORD; he is to bring the fat together with the breast, and wave the breast before the LORD as a wave offering. 31 The priest shall burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons. 32 You are to give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offerings. 33 The priest who offers the blood and fat of the peace offering shall have the right thigh as his portion. 34 I have taken from the Israelites the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution from their peace offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as a perpetual portion from the Israelites. 35 This is the portion of the offerings made by fire to the LORD that belongs to Aaron and his sons; it has been assigned to them from the day they are presented to the LORD as priests. 36 On the day he was anointed, the LORD commanded that this be given to them by the Israelites as a perpetual statute for the generations to come.”

37 This is the law of the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering, and the peace offering, 38 which the LORD commanded Moses on Mount Sinai on the day He commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.
