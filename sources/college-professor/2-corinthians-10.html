Paul’s Appeal in the Meekness of Christ

1 Now I, Paul, entreat you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ—I who am lowly in your presence, yet bold toward you when absent.

2 I beseech you that, when I am present, I may not be compelled to exercise the boldness with which I intend to confront those who suppose that we walk according to the flesh.

3 For although we live in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.

4 The weapons of our warfare are not carnal; rather, they are divinely powerful for the demolition of strongholds.

5 We cast down arguments and every lofty pretension raised against the knowledge of God, and we take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ.

6 We stand ready to punish every disobedience once your obedience is complete.

Discerning Who Belongs to Christ

7 Consider what lies plainly before your eyes. If anyone is confident that he belongs to Christ, let him reconsider in himself that, just as he is Christ’s, so are we.

8 For even if I boast somewhat more abundantly concerning the authority that the Lord gave us for your edification and not for your destruction, I shall not be put to shame.

9 I do not wish to seem as though I were terrifying you by my letters.

10 For they say, “His letters are weighty and forceful, but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech is contemptible.”

11 Let such a person reckon that what we are in word by letters when absent, we shall be in deed when present.

Boasting Within God’s Assigned Sphere

12 For we do not dare to number ourselves or compare ourselves with some of those who commend themselves; but they, measuring themselves by themselves and comparing themselves with themselves, are without understanding.

13 We, however, will not boast beyond measure, but according to the measure of the rule that God apportioned to us—a measure that reaches even to you.

14 For we are not overextending ourselves, as though we had not reached you; indeed, we were the first to come even to you with the gospel of Christ.

15 We do not boast beyond measure in the labors of others; rather, we have hope that, as your faith increases, our sphere among you may be greatly enlarged,

16 so that we may proclaim the gospel in the regions beyond you, and not boast in work already prepared in another’s sphere.

17 But let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.

18 For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.
