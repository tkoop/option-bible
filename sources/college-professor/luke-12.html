Warnings Against Hypocrisy and the Fear of God

1 Amidst the thronging of myriads, so densely packed that they were trampling one another, He began by addressing His disciples first: “Beware the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy. 2 For there is nothing so veiled that it shall not be unveiled, and nothing so concealed that it shall not be disclosed. 3 Hence, whatever you have uttered in the darkness shall be heard in the light, and what you have murmured in the inner chambers shall be proclaimed from the rooftops.”

4 “I say to you, My friends, be not afraid of those who kill the body and afterward can do nothing further. 5 But I shall forewarn you whom you ought to fear: fear Him who, after He has killed, possesses authority to cast into Gehenna; yes, I say to you, fear Him! 6 Are not five sparrows sold for two assaria? Yet not one of them is forgotten in the sight of God. 7 Nay, even the hairs of your head are all numbered; therefore fear not, for you are of far greater worth than many sparrows.”

8 “Moreover, I say to you, everyone who confesses Me before men, the Son of Man shall also confess before the angels of God; 9 but whoever denies Me before men shall be denied before the angels of God. 10 And everyone who utters a word against the Son of Man shall be forgiven; but he who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit shall not be forgiven.”

11 “And when they bring you before synagogues and rulers and authorities, be not anxious concerning how you shall defend yourselves or what you shall say; 12 for the Holy Spirit shall teach you in that very hour what you ought to say.”

Covetousness, the Rich Fool, and Trust in Providence

13 Then someone from the crowd said to Him, “Teacher, bid my brother to share the inheritance with me.”

14 But He said to him, “Man, who appointed Me judge or divider over you?”

15 Then He said to them, “Take heed and be on your guard against every form of covetousness, for a man's life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.”

16 And He related to them a parable, saying, “The field of a certain rich man brought forth abundantly; 17 and he deliberated within himself, saying, ‘What shall I do, seeing that I have no place wherein to store my crops?’ 18 And he said, ‘This will I do: I shall pull down my barns and build greater ones, and there I shall store all my grain and my goods. 19 And I shall say to my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years; take your ease, eat, drink, and be merry.”’”

20 “But God said to him, ‘Fool! This very night your soul is demanded of you; and the things you have prepared—whose shall they be?’ 21 Thus is it with him who hoards treasure for himself and is not rich toward God.”

22 And He said to His disciples, “Therefore I say to you, be not anxious for your life, as to what you shall eat, nor for your body, as to what you shall wear. 23 For life is more than food, and the body more than raiment. 24 Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap; they have neither storehouse nor barn; yet God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than the birds! 25 And which of you, by being anxious, can add a single cubit to his stature? 26 If then you are not able to accomplish even so small a thing as that, why are you anxious concerning the rest? 27 Consider the lilies, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin; yet I say to you, not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these. 28 But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which exists today and tomorrow is cast into the oven, how much more will He clothe you, O you of little faith! 29 And seek not what you shall eat or what you shall drink, nor be of anxious mind. 30 For all the nations of the world seek after these things, and your Father knows that you have need of them. 31 But seek His kingdom, and all these things shall be added unto you.”

32 “Fear not, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom. 33 Sell your possessions and give alms; make for yourselves purses that wax not old, a treasure in the heavens that fails not, where no thief draws near and no moth corrupts. 34 For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.”

Watchfulness, Faithful Stewardship, and the Coming of the Son of Man

35 “Let your loins be girded and your lamps burning; 36 and be like men awaiting their master's return from the wedding feast, that, when he comes and knocks, they may open to him immediately. 37 Blessed are those servants whom the master shall find watching when he comes. Truly I say to you, he shall gird himself, make them recline at table, and come forth to serve them. 38 And if he shall come in the second watch, or in the third, and find them so, blessed are those servants! 39 But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would not have suffered his house to be broken into. 40 You also be ready, for the Son of Man comes at an hour you do not expect.”

41 Then Peter said to Him, “Lord, do You speak this parable to us, or to all?”

42 And the Lord said, “Who then is the faithful and wise steward, whom his master shall set over his household, to give them their portion of food in due season? 43 Blessed is that servant whom his master, when he comes, shall find so doing. 44 Truly I say to you, he shall set him over all his possessions. 45 But if that servant shall say in his heart, ‘My master delays his coming,’ and shall begin to beat the menservants and maidservants, and to eat and drink and be drunken, 46 the master of that servant shall come in a day when he looks not for him, and at an hour when he is not aware, and shall cut him asunder and appoint him his portion with the unbelievers. 47 And that servant who knew his master's will and prepared not himself, neither did according to his will, shall be beaten with many stripes. 48 But he who knew not and did things worthy of stripes shall be beaten with few. And to whomsoever much is given, of him shall much be required; and to whom men have committed much, of him they will ask the more.”

Division, Discernment, and Reconciliation Before Judgment

49 “I came to cast fire upon the earth; and how I wish it were already kindled! 50 But I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how greatly I am distressed until it is accomplished! 51 Do you suppose that I came to give peace on earth? No, I tell you, but rather division. 52 For from henceforth in one house there shall be five divided: three against two, and two against three. 53 They shall be divided: father against son, and son against father; mother against daughter, and daughter against mother; mother-in-law against daughter-in-law, and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law.”

54 And He said also to the multitudes, “When you see a cloud rising in the west, you say immediately, ‘A shower is coming’; and so it comes to pass. 55 And when you see the south wind blowing, you say, ‘There will be a scorching heat’; and it comes to pass. 56 You hypocrites! You know how to discern the face of the earth and of the sky; but how is it that you do not discern this time? 57 And why do you not even of yourselves judge what is just? 58 As you go with your adversary to the magistrate, give diligence to be delivered from him on the way, lest he hale you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the officer, and the officer cast you into prison. 59 I tell you, you shall not depart thence until you have paid the very last mite.”
