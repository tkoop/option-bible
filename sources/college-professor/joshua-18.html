The Congregation’s Assembly at Shiloh

1 The whole congregation of the Israelites assembled at Shiloh and erected the Tent of Meeting there, for the land had been subjugated before them. 2 Seven tribes among the Israelites had not yet obtained their inheritance.

Joshua’s Rebuke and the Commanded Survey

3 Joshua remonstrated with the Israelites, saying, “How long will you remain indolent in going to take possession of the land that the LORD God of your fathers has given you? 4 Designate for yourselves three men from each tribe, and I will send them; let them arise, traverse the land, record it according to their inheritances, and return to me. 5 They shall partition it into seven allotments: Judah shall maintain its southern frontier, and the house of Joseph shall maintain its northern frontier. 6 You shall inscribe the seven portions of the land and bring them here to me, and I will cast lots on your behalf here before the LORD our God. 7 For the Levites possess no allotment among you, since the priesthood of the LORD constitutes their inheritance; and Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already secured their inheritance east of the Jordan, which Moses, the servant of the LORD, bestowed upon them.”

The Survey, the Lots, and the Apportionment

8 The men arose and went, and Joshua charged those tasked with surveying the land, saying, “Traverse the land, record it, and return to me, so that I may cast lots for you here before the LORD in Shiloh.” 9 The men therefore passed through the land and recorded it in a scroll, enumerating the cities into seven allotments, and returned to Joshua in the camp at Shiloh. 10 Joshua cast lots for them before the LORD in Shiloh, and there he distributed the land to the Israelites according to their divisions.

The Patrimony of Benjamin

11 The allotment for the tribe of Benjamin, by its clans, came up, and their territory fell between the Judahites and the Josephites. 12 Their northern boundary began at the Jordan, ascended to the shoulder of Jericho on the north, climbed westward into the hill country, and terminated in the wilderness of Beth-aven. 13 From there the boundary passed to Luz, to the south side of Luz (that is, Bethel), and descended to Ataroth-addar upon the hill south of Lower Beth-horon. 14 The boundary then arced southward from the hill confronting Beth-horon on the south, and its extremities reached Kiriath-baal—that is, Kiriath-jearim—a city of the Judahites; this constituted the western frontier. 15 The southern side began at the extremity of Kiriath-jearim and extended westward, emerging at the spring of Nephtoah. 16 The boundary descended to the terminus of the mountain overlooking the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, situated in the Valley of Rephaim to the north, and descended the Valley of Hinnom to the southern flank of the Jebusite, finally reaching En-rogel. 17 It curved northward, emerged at En-shemesh, extended to Geliloth opposite the Ascent of Adummim, and descended to the stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 18 It continued northward over against the Arabah and descended into the Arabah. 19 The boundary proceeded northward to the shoulder of Beth-hoglah, and its termini reached the northern gulf of the Salt Sea at the southern extremity of the Jordan; this was the southern frontier. 20 The Jordan formed its eastern limit. Such was the inheritance of the Benjamites according to their clans, bounded on every side.

21 The cities of the Benjamites, according to their clans, were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, the Valley of Keziz, 22 Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 23 Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 24 Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba—twelve cities with their villages— 25 Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 26 Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah, 27 Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 28 Zelah, Eleph, the Jebusite city (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeath, and Kiriath—fourteen cities with their villages. This was the inheritance of the Benjamites.
