The Covenant with Levi and the Priests’ Failure

1 “And now this commandment is for you, O priests. 2 If you will not listen, and if you will not take it to heart to give glory to My name,” says the the LORD of hosts, “then I will send the curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings; indeed, I have already cursed them, because you do not take it to heart. 3 Behold, I am rebuking your offspring, and I will scatter dung upon your faces—the dung of your festivals—and you shall be carried away with it. 4 Then you will know that I have sent this commandment to you, that My covenant with Levi may continue,” says the the LORD of hosts.

5 “My covenant with him was one of life and peace, and I gave them to him; it was a covenant of reverence, and he revered Me and stood in awe of My name. 6 True instruction was in his mouth, and no injustice was found on his lips; he walked with Me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many away from iniquity. 7 For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek instruction from his mouth, for he is the messenger of the the LORD of hosts.”

8 “But you have turned aside from the way; you have caused many to stumble in the law; you have corrupted the covenant of Levi,” says the the LORD of hosts. 9 “So I have made you despised and humiliated before all the people, because you have not kept My ways, and you show partiality in the law.”

Judah’s Treachery in Marriage and Divorce

10 Do we not all have one Father? Did not one God create us? Why then do we deal treacherously with one another, profaning the covenant of our fathers? 11 Judah has dealt treacherously, and an abomination has been committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah has profaned the sanctuary of the the LORD, which He loves, and has married foreign women. 12 May the the LORD cut off from the tents of Jacob the man who does this—awake or answering—though he present an offering to the the LORD of hosts.

13 And this is the second thing you do: you cover the altar of the the LORD with tears, with weeping and groaning, because He no longer regards the offering or accepts it with favor from your hand. 14 You ask, “Why?” Because the the LORD has been witness between you and the wife of your youth, to whom you have been faithless, though she is your companion and the wife of your covenant.

15 Did not one God make us? And does He not have the remnant of the Spirit? And what does the one seek? Godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be faithless to the wife of your youth. 16 “For I hate divorce,” says the the LORD, the God of Israel, “and him who is clothed in violence,” says the the LORD of hosts. “So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be faithless.”

The People’s Wearisome Words

17 You have wearied the the LORD with your words. Yet you ask, “How have we wearied Him?” By saying, “Everyone who does evil is good in the sight of the the LORD, and He delights in them,” or, “Where is the God of justice?”
