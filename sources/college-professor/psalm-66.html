A Triumphal Hymn of Universal Acclamation and Personal Deliverance

1 Raise a jubilant shout to God, all the earth; 2 extol the splendor of His name; accord to Him glory befitting His majesty. 3 Declare to God, “How awe-inspiring are Your deeds! So great is Your power that Your adversaries cringe before You.” 4 All the earth bows in worship to You and makes music to You; they sing praise to Your name. Selah

5 Come and behold the works of God: He is awe-inspiring in His dealings with humankind. 6 He transformed the sea into dry ground; they crossed the river on foot. There we exulted in Him. 7 He governs the ages by His might; His eyes scrutinize the nations—let the rebellious not exalt themselves. Selah

8 Bless our God, O peoples, and make the voice of His praise resound, 9 for He has preserved our life and has not permitted our feet to slip.

10 For You, O God, have put us to the proof; You have refined us as silver is refined. 11 You led us into the snare; You imposed a crushing weight upon our loins. 12 You allowed men to ride over our heads; we traversed fire and water, yet You brought us forth into abundance.

13 I shall enter Your house with burnt offerings; I shall fulfill my vows to You, 14 the utterances my lips pronounced and my mouth spoke in my distress. 15 I shall offer You burnt offerings of fattened beasts, together with the smoke of rams; I shall sacrifice bulls and goats. Selah

16 Come and listen, all you who fear God, and I will recount what He has accomplished for my soul. 17 I cried to Him with my mouth, and exalted praise was upon my tongue. 18 Had I cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. 19 Yet God has indeed listened; He has given heed to the voice of my prayer. 20 Blessed be God, for He has not spurned my prayer nor withdrawn His steadfast love from me!
