Amaziah's Accession and the Limits of His Piety

1 Amaziah was twenty-five years old at his accession to the throne in Jerusalem, and his reign extended over twenty-nine years; his mother was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem. 2 He executed what was right in the sight of the LORD, albeit not with a whole heart. 3 Once the kingdom had been firmly established under his authority, he put to death the servants who had assassinated his father the king. 4 Yet he did not execute their children, inasmuch as it is written in the Law—in the Book of Moses—that the LORD commanded, "Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor shall children be put to death for their fathers; each shall die for his own sin."

Mobilization, Mercenaries, and Prophetic Counsel

5 Amaziah mustered Judah, appointing officers over thousands and hundreds; he enrolled those from twenty years of age upward throughout Judah and Benjamin, and discovered three hundred thousand select warriors, proficient in spear and shield. 6 He also retained one hundred thousand mighty men of valor from Israel at the cost of one hundred talents of silver.

7 A man of God then approached him, saying, "O king, do not let the army of Israel accompany you, for the LORD is not with Israel—not with any descendant of Ephraim. 8 Yet if you persist in going, then go and be strong for battle; nevertheless, God will cause you to falter before the enemy, for God has power both to help and to overthrow."

9 Amaziah inquired of the man of God, "But what is to be done concerning the hundred talents I have already disbursed to the Israelite troop?" The man of God replied, "The LORD is able to give you far more than this."

10 Accordingly, Amaziah separated out the contingent that had come to him from Ephraim, dismissing them to their homes; they departed in furious anger against Judah, returning to their own places in a rage. 11 Amaziah, however, took courage and led his people into the Valley of Salt, where he defeated ten thousand of the sons of Seir. 12 The men of Judah also captured ten thousand alive, conveyed them to the summit of the rock, and cast them down from its height, so that all were dashed to pieces. 13 As for the troops whom Amaziah had dismissed from the campaign, they made raids upon the cities of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon, struck down three thousand, and carried off a great spoil.

Idolatry after Edom and the Prophet's Rebuke

14 After Amaziah returned from smiting the Edomites, he brought back the gods of the sons of Seir, erected them as his own gods, bowed down before them, and burned incense to them. 15 Therefore the anger of the LORD was kindled against Amaziah, and He sent a prophet to him, who said, "Why have you sought the gods of a people who could not deliver their own people from your hand?" 16 While the prophet was still speaking, the king said to him, "Have we appointed you counselor to the king? Desist; why should you be struck down?" So the prophet refrained, but said, "I know that God has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not heeded my counsel."

War with Israel, Jerusalem's Humiliation, and Amaziah's Demise

17 Then Amaziah king of Judah, having taken counsel, sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us confront one another in battle." 18 Joash king of Israel sent back to Amaziah king of Judah: "A thistle in Lebanon sent to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son for a wife'; but a wild beast of Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle." 19 "You say, 'Behold, I have struck Edom,' and your heart has lifted you up to boast. Now stay at home; why provoke disaster, that you should fall—you and Judah with you?"

20 Amaziah, however, would not listen, for this was from God, that He might deliver them into the hand of Joash, because they had sought the gods of Edom. 21 So Joash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah faced one another at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 22 Judah was defeated before Israel, and the men fled, each to his tent. 23 Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh; he brought him to Jerusalem and broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate, for a distance of four hundred cubits. 24 He took all the gold and silver, all the vessels found in the house of God together with those in the keeping of Obed-edom, the treasures of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

25 Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 26 The rest of the acts of Amaziah, from first to last, are they not written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel? 27 From the time Amaziah turned away from following the LORD, a conspiracy arose against him in Jerusalem; he fled to Lachish, but they sent after him to Lachish and killed him there. 28 They carried him upon horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah.
