A Universal Summons to Acclaim the Sovereign LORD

1 Clap your hands, all you peoples; lift up a jubilant shout unto God.

The LORD's Transcendent Kingship over the Nations

2 For the LORD Most High is awe-inspiring, the great King over all the earth. 3 He subjugates nations beneath us, and peoples beneath our feet. 4 He ordains our inheritance for us, the glory of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah 5 God has ascended amid shouts of jubilation, the LORD amid the sounding of trumpets.

Praise Rendered unto the Exalted King

6 Sing praises unto God, sing praises; sing praises unto our King, sing praises. 7 For God is King over all the earth; sing praises with discernment. 8 God reigns over the nations; God is seated upon His holy throne. 9 The nobles of the nations have assembled as the people of the God of Abraham, for the shields of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted.
