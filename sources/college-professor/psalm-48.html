A Song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah.

The LORD’s Greatness in Zion

1 The LORD is supremely great, and most worthy of exalted praise in the city of our God, upon His holy mountain. 2 Mount Zion—beautiful in its loftiness and the delight of all the earth—lies in the northern reaches, the city of the great King. 3 God dwells within her strongholds; He is renowned as a fortress.

The Panic of the Hostile Kings

4 For the kings gathered together and advanced in concert. 5 They beheld it and were seized with astonishment; they fled in terror. 6 Trembling overtook them there—anguish akin to a woman’s labor pangs. 7 With an east wind You shattered the ships of Tarshish. 8 As we have heard, so now have we beheld in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish her forever. Selah.

Worship, Righteousness, and Divine Judgment

9 Within Your temple, O God, we contemplate Your steadfast love. 10 As Your name, O God, so Your praise extends to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is filled with righteousness. 11 Mount Zion rejoices, and the daughters of Judah exult, on account of Your judgments.

Zion’s Testimony to Future Generations

12 Circumnavigate Zion and tally her towers; inspect her ramparts and scrutinize her citadels, 13 so that you may recount them to the next generation, 14 declaring that this God is our God forever and ever; He will guide us even unto death.
