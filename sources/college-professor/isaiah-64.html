A Cry for the LORD to Rend the Heavens

1 If only You would rend the heavens and come down, so that the mountains might melt at Your presence— 2 as fire kindles brushwood, as fire causes water to boil—to make Your name known to Your adversaries, so that the nations might tremble at Your presence! 3 When You did awesome things that we did not expect, You came down, and the mountains melted at Your presence. 4 From of old no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides You, who acts for those who wait for Him.

Confession of Sin and Spiritual Unworthiness

5 You meet the one who rejoices in doing righteousness, those who remember You in Your ways. Behold, You were angry, and we sinned; in these ways we have continued from of old—how then shall we be saved? 6 We have all become like one who is unclean, and all our righteous deeds are like a polluted garment; we all wither like a leaf, and our iniquities carry us away like the wind. 7 No one calls upon Your name, no one rouses himself to take hold of You; for You have hidden Your face from us and have caused us to melt away in the hand of our iniquity.

Appeal to the LORD as Father and Potter

8 Yet now, O the LORD, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You are our potter; we are all the work of Your hand. 9 Do not be exceedingly angry, O the LORD, nor remember iniquity forever; behold, please look, for we are all Your people.

Lament over the Ruined Sanctuary and Plea for Mercy

10 Your holy cities have become a wilderness; Zion has become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation. 11 Our holy and glorious temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned with fire, and all our pleasant places have become a ruin. 12 After all this, O the LORD, will You restrain Yourself? Will You keep silent and afflict us beyond measure?
