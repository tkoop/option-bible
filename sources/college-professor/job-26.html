Job Rebukes Bildad’s Empty Counsel

1 Then Job answered: 2 “How you have helped the powerless! How you have saved the arm that has no strength! 3 How you have counseled the unwise and abundantly declared sound wisdom! 4 To whom have you uttered words? Whose spirit has gone forth from you?

The Trembling of Sheol and Abaddon

“5 The shades of the dead shudder beneath the waters, together with those who dwell therein. 6 Sheol lies bare before Him, and Abaddon is without any covering.

God’s Sovereign Ordering of the Cosmos

“7 He stretches the north over the void; He suspends the earth upon nothing. 8 He confines the waters within His clouds, yet the cloud is not rent asunder beneath them. 9 He veils the face of the full moon, spreading His cloud over it. 10 He has traced a circle upon the face of the waters, at the boundary between light and darkness. 11 The pillars of heaven quake and are astounded at His rebuke. 12 By His power He calmed the sea; by His understanding He shattered Rahab. 13 By His breath the skies are cleared; His hand has pierced the fleeing serpent.

The Incomprehensible Thunder of Divine Power

“14 Indeed, these are but the outskirts of His ways; how faint a whisper of Him is heard! Who, then, can comprehend the thunder of His power?”
