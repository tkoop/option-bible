The Afflicted Voice Under Divine Discipline

1 I am the man who has seen affliction beneath the rod of His wrath. 2 He has led me and brought me into darkness, and not into light. 3 Surely He turns His hand against me again and again, all day long.

4 He has worn away my flesh and my skin; He has shattered my bones. 5 He has besieged me and encircled me with bitterness and tribulation. 6 He has made me dwell in darkness, like those long dead.

7 He has walled me in so that I cannot escape; He has made my fetters heavy. 8 Even when I cry out and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer. 9 He has barred my ways with hewn stone and made my paths crooked.

10 He is to me like a bear lying in wait, like a lion in secret places. 11 He has turned me aside from my path and torn me to pieces; He has made me desolate. 12 He has bent His bow and set me as a mark for His arrow.

13 He has driven the arrows of His quiver into my inmost parts. 14 I have become a derision to all my people, the subject of their song all day long. 15 He has filled me with bitterness and made me drunk with wormwood.

16 He has ground my teeth with gravel and made me wallow in ashes. 17 My soul has been banished from peace; I have forgotten prosperity. 18 Then I said, 'My strength has perished, together with my hope in the LORD.'

19 Remember my affliction and my wandering, the wormwood and the gall. 20 My soul remembers and is bowed down within me. 21 Yet this I bring to mind, and therefore I have hope:

Hope Rekindled by Covenant Mercy

22 Because of the LORD’s loving devotion we are not consumed, for His compassions never fail. 23 They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

24 'The LORD is my portion,' says my soul, 'therefore I will hope in Him.' 25 The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him. 26 It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD. 27 It is good for a man to bear the yoke in his youth.

28 Let him sit alone and keep silence, because He has laid it upon him. 29 Let him bury his face in the dust—there may yet be hope. 30 Let him offer his cheek to the one who strikes him; let him be filled with reproach.

31 For the Lord will not cast us off forever. 32 Though He brings grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant loving devotion. 33 For He does not willingly afflict or grieve the children of men.

34 To crush under His feet all the prisoners of the earth, 35 to deny a man justice before the Most High, 36 to subvert a man in his cause—the Lord does not approve.

37 Who is it that speaks, and it comes to pass, unless the Lord has commanded it? 38 Do not both calamity and good come from the mouth of the Most High? 39 Why should a living man complain, a man in view of the punishment for his sins?

Repentance and the Search for God's Face

40 Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD. 41 Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven. 42 We have sinned and rebelled; You have not forgiven.

43 You have covered Yourself with anger and pursued us; You have slain without pity. 44 You have covered Yourself with a cloud so that no prayer can pass through. 45 You have made us scum and refuse among the nations.

46 All our enemies open their mouths against us. 47 Panic and pitfall have come upon us, devastation and destruction.

Tears, Entrapment, and the Cry for Deliverance

48 Streams of tears flow from my eyes because of the destruction of the daughter of my people. 49 My eyes will flow unceasingly, without relief, 50 until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees. 51 My eyes bring grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city.

52 Those who were my enemies without cause hunted me like a bird. 53 They silenced my life in the pit and cast stones upon me. 54 Waters flowed over my head, and I said, 'I am cut off!'

55 I called on Your name, O the LORD, from the depths of the pit. 56 You heard my voice; do not hide Your ear from my cry for relief. 57 You came near when I called on You; You said, 'Do not be afraid.'

58 You redeemed my life, O Lord; You have pleaded my cause. 59 You have seen, O the LORD, the wrong done to me; judge my cause. 60 You have seen all their vengeance, all their plots against me. 61 You have heard their reproach, O the LORD, all their plots against me—

62 the lips and muttered schemes of those who rise against me all day long. 63 Whether they sit or rise, behold, I am their song.

64 Repay them, O the LORD, according to the work of their hands. 65 Give them anguish of heart; may Your curse be upon them. 66 Pursue them in anger and destroy them from under the heavens of the LORD.
