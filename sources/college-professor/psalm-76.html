God's Renown in Judah and Zion

1 For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song. God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel. 2 His abode has been established in Salem, His dwelling place in Zion. 3 There He shattered the fiery arrows, the shield, the sword, and the weapons of war. Selah

The Overthrow of the Proud and Mighty

4 Glorious are You, more majestic than the mountains of prey. 5 The stout-hearted were despoiled; they sank into slumber; none of the warriors could so much as raise a hand. 6 At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, horse and rider alike were stunned. 7 You, You are to be feared! Who can stand before You when Your anger is roused?

Judgment Uttered from Heaven

8 From heaven You uttered judgment; the earth feared and was still, 9 when God arose to establish judgment, to save all the humble of the earth. Selah 10 Surely the wrath of man shall praise You; the remnant of wrath You shall gird about Yourself.

Vows and Tribute to the Feared One

11 Make your vows to the LORD your God and perform them; let all who surround Him bring tribute to Him who is to be feared, 12 who breaks the spirit of princes and is feared by the kings of the earth.
