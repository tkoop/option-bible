Israel Mobilizes and the Ark Is Brought into the Camp

1 The word of Samuel came to all Israel. Israel then went out to meet the Philistines in battle and encamped beside Ebenezer, while the Philistines encamped at Aphek. 2 The Philistines arrayed themselves for battle against Israel; when the fighting spread, Israel was defeated before the Philistines, who struck down about four thousand men upon the field. 3 When the people came into the camp, the elders of Israel said, “Why has the LORD defeated us today before the Philistines? Let us bring from Shiloh the ark of the covenant of the LORD, that it may come among us and deliver us from the hand of our enemies.”

4 So the people sent to Shiloh and brought from there the ark of the covenant of the LORD of Hosts, who is enthroned above the cherubim; and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of God. 5 When the ark of the covenant of the LORD entered the camp, all Israel raised a great shout, and the earth resounded. 6 The Philistines heard the sound of the shout and said, “What does this great shout in the camp of the Hebrews mean?” Then they learned that the ark of the LORD had come into the camp.

The Philistines Rally and Israel Is Routed

7 The Philistines were afraid, for they said, “God has come into the camp.” And they said, “Woe to us! Nothing like this has happened before. 8 Woe to us! Who will deliver us from the hand of these mighty gods? These are the gods who struck the Egyptians with every plague in the wilderness.” 9 “Be strong and conduct yourselves like men, O Philistines, lest you become enslaved to the Hebrews as they have been to you. Be men, then, and fight.” 10 The Philistines fought; Israel was defeated, and every man fled to his tent. The slaughter was exceedingly great: thirty thousand foot soldiers of Israel fell. 11 The ark of God was taken, and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

The Death of Eli and the Naming of Ichabod

12 On that day a man of Benjamin ran from the battle line and came to Shiloh, with his garments torn and dust upon his head in mourning. 13 When he came, Eli was sitting on his seat by the roadside, watching, because his heart trembled for the ark of God. The man entered the city and told the news, and the whole city cried out. 14 Eli heard the sound of the outcry and said, “What does the sound of this tumult mean?” The man hurried in and told Eli.

15 Now Eli was ninety-eight years old, and his eyesight had failed him, so that he could not see. 16 The man said to Eli, “I am the one who came from the battle line, and I fled from the battle today.”

And Eli said, “What happened, my son?”

17 The messenger answered, “Israel has fled before the Philistines, and there has been a great slaughter among the people; your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and the ark of God has been taken.” 18 When he mentioned the ark of God, Eli fell backward beside the gate; his neck was broken, and he died, for the man was old and heavy. He had judged Israel forty years.

19 His daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and near her time. When she heard the report that the ark of God had been taken and that her father-in-law and husband were dead, she knelt and gave birth, for her labor pangs had seized her. 20 About the time of her death, the women standing by said to her, “Do not be afraid, for you have borne a son.” But she did not answer or pay heed.

21 She named the child Ichabod, saying, “Glory has departed from Israel,” because the ark of God had been taken and because of her father-in-law and her husband. 22 And she said, “The glory has departed from Israel, for the ark of God has been taken.”
