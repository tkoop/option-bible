Solomon Consolidates His Kingdom and Seeks the LORD at Gibeon

1 Solomon, the son of David, consolidated his authority over the kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and exalted him mightily. 2 Then Solomon addressed all Israel—the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, the judges, and every leader throughout Israel, the patriarchal heads. 3 Solomon, together with the whole assembly, repaired to the high place at Gibeon, because the tent of meeting of God was located there, the very tabernacle that Moses, the servant of the LORD, had constructed in the wilderness. 4 David, however, had conveyed the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim to the site he had prepared for it, having pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem. 5 The bronze altar fashioned by Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, stood there before the tabernacle of the LORD; and Solomon, with the assembly, sought Him there. 6 Solomon ascended to the bronze altar before the LORD at the tent of meeting and offered upon it a thousand burnt offerings.

The LORD's Nocturnal Promise of Wisdom and Wealth

7 That night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I shall give you."

8 Solomon replied to God, "You have dealt with my father David in great and steadfast love, and You have enthroned me in his stead. 9 Now, O the LORD God, let Your word to my father David be established, for You have installed me as king over a people as innumerable as the dust of the earth. 10 Now grant me wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people, for who is capable of judging this great people of Yours?"

11 God said to Solomon, "Because this purpose was in your heart, and you did not request riches, wealth, or honor, nor the life of those who hate you, nor did you request longevity, but you requested wisdom and knowledge for yourself, that you might judge My people over whom I have made you king— 12 wisdom and knowledge are conferred upon you. I will also give you riches, wealth, and honor, such as no king before you has possessed and none after you will possess."

Solomon's Royal Prosperity and International Commerce

13 So Solomon returned to Jerusalem from the high place at Gibeon, from before the tent of meeting, and he reigned over Israel. 14 Solomon amassed chariots and horsemen: fourteen hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem. 15 The king made silver and gold as plentiful in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar as abundant as the sycamores in the foothills. 16 Solomon's horses were brought from Egypt and from Kue; the king's merchants procured them from Kue at a fixed price. 17 A chariot was imported from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty, and they likewise furnished all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.
