The Israelites in Egypt

1The following are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt with Jacob, each accompanied by his household: 2Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah; 3Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin; 4Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 5All the persons descended from Jacob numbered seventy, Joseph having already been in Egypt. 6Joseph died, as did all his brothers and that entire generation. 7Yet the sons of Israel were fruitful, increased superabundantly, multiplied, and grew exceedingly mighty, until the land teemed with them.

A New King Oppresses Israel

8A new king, unfamiliar with Joseph, arose over Egypt. 9He said to his people, “Behold, the people of the sons of Israel are more numerous and mightier than we.” 10“Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, lest they multiply and, should war break out, ally themselves with our enemies, fight against us, and depart from the land.” 11Therefore they appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with their burdens, and they built for Pharaoh the store cities Pithom and Raamses. 12Yet the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and spread abroad; and the Egyptians were dismayed because of the sons of Israel. 13The Egyptians subjected the sons of Israel to harsh servitude. 14They embittered their lives with strenuous labor in mortar and brick and with every manner of field service; all the work they imposed upon them was exacting.

The Hebrew Midwives Defy Pharaoh

15The king of Egypt addressed the Hebrew midwives, one named Shiphrah and the other Puah. 16He said, “When you assist the Hebrew women in childbirth and observe them upon the birthstools, if the child is a son, you shall kill him; but if a daughter, she shall live.” 17The midwives, however, feared God and did not comply with the king of Egypt’s command; they preserved the male infants alive. 18The king of Egypt summoned the midwives and asked, “Why have you done this, preserving the male infants alive?” 19The midwives replied to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women; they are vigorous and give birth before the midwives reach them.” 20So God treated the midwives well, and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly mighty. 21And because the midwives feared God, He granted them households of their own.

Pharaoh’s Final Decree

22Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, “Every son born you shall cast into the Nile, but every daughter you shall spare.”
