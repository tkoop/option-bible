The Oracle Concerning the Nations and Egypt

1The word of the LORD concerning the nations, which came to Jeremiah the prophet— 2concerning Egypt, the army of Pharaoh Necho, king of Egypt, which was at Carchemish on the Euphrates, and which Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, struck in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

The Mustering and Defeat of Egypt's Army at Carchemish

3Arrange the shield and buckler, and draw near to the battle. 4Harness the horses and mount, O horsemen; take your positions in helmets; furbish the spears; put on the coats of mail.

5Why have I seen them dismayed and turned backward? Their warriors are beaten down; they have fled in haste without looking back, and terror is on every side, declares the LORD. 6Let not the swift flee, nor let the mighty escape; northward, by the river Euphrates, they stumble and fall.

7Who is this rising like the Nile, like rivers whose waters toss themselves? 8Egypt rises like the Nile, and its waters churn like rivers; and it says, “I will ascend, I will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and its inhabitants.”

9Advance, O horses; rage, O chariots; go forth, O mighty men: Cush and Put, who bear the shield, and the Ludim, who draw the bow. 10For that day belongs to the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts—a day of vengeance, to avenge Himself upon His adversaries. The sword shall devour and be satiated and be drunk with their blood, for the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts, has a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

11Go up to Gilead and take balm, O Virgin Daughter of Egypt; in vain you have multiplied remedies for yourself; there is no healing for you. 12The nations have heard your disgrace, and your outcry has filled the earth; for warrior has stumbled upon warrior, and both have fallen together.

Babylon's Decreed Invasion of Egypt

13The word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet, that Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, would come to strike the land of Egypt. 14Declare in Egypt, and proclaim in Migdol; proclaim also in Noph and in Tahpanhes. Say, “Stand fast and prepare yourself, for the sword devours all around you.”

15Why have your mighty ones been swept away? They did not stand, because the LORD thrust them down. 16He has multiplied the stumbling; indeed, one has fallen against another, and they said, “Arise, and let us return to our people and to the land of our birth, away from the sword of the oppressor.”

17There they cried out, “Pharaoh, king of Egypt, is but a noise; he has let the appointed time pass by.” 18“As I live,” declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, “surely, as Tabor stands among the mountains and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come.”

19Pack your bags for exile, O daughter dwelling in Egypt; for Noph shall become a desolation and be burned, without inhabitant. 20Egypt is a beautiful heifer, but a gadfly from the north is coming—it is coming! 21Even her mercenaries in her midst are like fattened calves; they too will turn back, and together they will flee; they will not stand their ground, for the day of their calamity is upon them—the time of their punishment.

22Egypt will hiss like a serpent, for they march with an army and come against her with axes, as woodcutters. 23They shall cut down her forest, declares the LORD, though it be impenetrable; for they are more than locusts and without number. 24The Daughter of Egypt shall be put to shame; she shall be delivered into the hand of the people of the north.

25The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I will punish Amon of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, and her gods and her kings, and those who trust in Pharaoh. 26And I will deliver them into the hand of those who seek their lives, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and into the hand of his servants; and afterward she shall be inhabited, as in the days of old,” declares the LORD.

Jacob's Deliverance and Discipline

27“But you, O Jacob My servant, do not fear, and do not be dismayed, O Israel; for behold, I will save you from afar, and your seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and be quiet and at ease, and none shall make him afraid. 28Fear not, O Jacob My servant,” declares the LORD, “for I am with you; for I will make a full end of all the nations to which I have driven you, but I will not make a full end of you; but I will discipline you justly, and will not leave you unpunished.”
