David's Prayer for Preservation Amid the Wicked

1 I cry out to You, O the LORD; hasten to my succor. Give ear to my voice when I invoke You. 2 May my prayer be established before You as incense, and the lifting up of my hands as the evening oblation.

3 Set a sentinel, O the LORD, over my mouth; guard the portal of my lips. 4 Do not permit my heart to be drawn toward evil, nor let me occupy myself with wicked machinations alongside men who perpetrate iniquity; and do not let me partake of their dainties.

5 Let the righteous strike me—it is an act of lovingkindness; let him rebuke me—it is oil upon my head; let not my head spurn it. For my prayer remains set against their wickedness. 6 Their magistrates are hurled down the crags, yet they heed my utterances, for they are pleasing.

7 As one who plows and fractures the soil, so our bones are strewn at the jaws of Sheol. 8 But my eyes are fixed upon You, O GOD the Lord; in You I seek refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless.

9 Preserve me from the snare they have contrived for me, and from the traps of evildoers. 10 Let the wicked fall into their own nets together, while I pass by unscathed.
