A Summons to Imitate God and to Walk in Love

1 Become, therefore, imitators of God, as His dearly beloved children, 2 and conduct yourselves in love, even as Christ loved us and surrendered Himself on our behalf—an offering and sacrifice to God, a fragrant aroma.

3 Yet among you, as befits the saints, let not even a whisper of sexual immorality, nor any impurity, nor covetousness be named; 4 neither obscenity, nor foolish discourse, nor scurrilous jesting—things unbecoming—but rather thanksgiving.

5 For you know this with certainty: that no sexually immoral person, nor impure person, nor covetous person—that is, an idolater—possesses any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. 6 Let no one beguile you with vain words, for on account of these things the wrath of God descends upon the sons of disobedience. 7 Therefore do not become co-participants with them.

From Darkness to Light: Living as Children of Light

8 For once you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord; walk, therefore, as children of light, 9 for the fruit of the light is found in all goodness, righteousness, and truth, 10 discerning what is well-pleasing to the Lord. 11 And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them; 12 for it is shameful even to recount the things wrought by them in secret. 13 Yet all things, when brought to light, are made manifest, for whatever is made manifest is light. 14 Hence it is said, “Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ shall shine upon you.”

Wisdom, Sobriety, and Spirit-Filled Worship

15 Look carefully, then, how you walk—not as the unwise, but as the wise— 16 making the most of your time, for the days are evil. 17 Wherefore do not be foolish, but apprehend what the will of the Lord is. 18 And do not be intoxicated with wine, wherein lies debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit, 19 addressing one another in psalms, hymns, and spiritual odes, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord; 20 always rendering thanks for all things, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to God the Father; 21 and submitting yourselves to one another in the reverence of Christ.

The Mystery of Marriage and Christ’s Love for the Church

22 Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands, as unto the Lord. 23 For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church, He Himself being the Savior of the body. 24 But as the church is subject to Christ, so also wives ought to be subject to their husbands in all things.

25 Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ loved the church and delivered Himself up for her, 26 that He might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word, 27 that He might present her to Himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such blemish, but that she should be holy and without blemish.

28 So too ought husbands to love their own wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself; 29 for no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, even as Christ does the church, 30 for we are members of His body.

31 “For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh.”

32 This mystery is profound; yet I am speaking concerning Christ and the church. 33 Nevertheless, let each one of you love his own wife even as himself, and let the wife see that she reverence her husband.
