The Gileadite Clan's Petition Regarding Tribal Inheritance

1 The patriarchal chieftains of the clan of Gilead—son of Machir, son of Manasseh—drawn from the Josephite houses, approached Moses and the leaders, namely the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelites, and spoke before them. 2 They said, "The LORD commanded our lord to apportion the land as an inheritance to the Israelites by lot, and our lord was commanded by the LORD to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters. 3 Now, should those daughters marry into one of the tribes of the Israelites, their inheritance will be alienated from our ancestral estate and annexed to the estate of the tribe into which they marry; thus our allotted portion will be diminished. 4 Moreover, when the jubilee arrives for the Israelites, their estate will be permanently incorporated into the tribe to which they have married and thereby subtracted from the estate of our paternal tribe."

Moses' Adjudication and the Law of Endogamous Marriage

5 Moses then instructed the Israelites in accordance with the word of the LORD, saying, "The tribe of Joseph has spoken justly." 6 He further declared that this is the ordinance the LORD has decreed concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: they may marry whomever they deem suitable, provided they marry within the clan of their father's tribe. 7 Hence no Israelite inheritance shall circulate from tribe to tribe, since each Israelite must remain tenaciously attached to the patrimony of his ancestral tribe. 8 Furthermore, every daughter who inherits property among the Israelites must marry within her father's tribal clan, so that the Israelites may each retain the patrimony of his forefathers. 9 No estate, therefore, shall pass from one tribe to another, because each Israelite tribe must hold fast to its own allotment.

Compliance of Zelophehad's Daughters and the Concluding Statute

10 Zelophehad's daughters complied exactly with what the LORD had commanded through Moses. 11 Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah—the daughters of Zelophehad—married their paternal cousins. 12 They married within the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained with their father's tribal house. 13 These are the statutes and ordinances the LORD enjoined upon the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan across from Jericho.
