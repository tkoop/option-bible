The LORD's Judgment upon Moab

1 Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Moab, and even for four, I will not revoke My judgment, because he burned the bones of Edom's king to lime. 2 I will send fire into Moab, and it shall devour the strongholds of Kerioth; Moab shall perish amid tumult, amid shouting, and amid the sound of the trumpet. 3 I will cut off the ruler from its midst, and I will slay all its officials together with him," declares the LORD.

The LORD's Judgment upon Judah

4 Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Judah, and even for four, I will not revoke My judgment, because they rejected the law of the LORD and did not keep His statutes, but their lies led them astray—the very falsehoods their ancestors followed. 5 I will send fire upon Judah, and it shall consume the palaces of Jerusalem."

The LORD's Judgment upon Israel

6 Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Israel, and even for four, I will not revoke My judgment, because they sell the righteous for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals. 7 They trample the heads of the poor into the dust of the earth and pervert the way of the humble; a man and his father consort with the same young woman, thereby profaning My holy name. 8 They recline beside every altar upon garments taken in pledge, and in the house of their god they drink wine exacted as fines."

The LORD's Reckoning with Israel

9 "Yet I destroyed the Amorite before them, whose stature was like that of cedars and whose strength was like that of oaks; I destroyed his fruit from above and his roots from below. 10 I brought you up from the land of Egypt and led you forty years through the wilderness, to take possession of the land of the Amorite. 11 I raised up prophets from among your sons and Nazirites from among your young men. Is it not so, O children of Israel?" declares the LORD.

12 "But you made the Nazirites drink wine and commanded the prophets, 'You shall not prophesy.'"

13 "Behold, I will crush you in your place, as a cart laden with sheaves crushes. 14 Flight will be lost to the swift, the strong shall not retain his strength, and the mighty shall not save his life. 15 The archer shall not stand, the swift-footed shall not escape, and the horseman shall not save his life. 16 The stout-hearted among the mighty shall flee naked on that day," declares the LORD.
