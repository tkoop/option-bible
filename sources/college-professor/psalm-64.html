David’s Plea for Deliverance from Secret Adversaries

1 Hear my voice, O God, in my lamentation; preserve my life from the terror of the enemy. 2 Conceal me from the clandestine counsel of evildoers, from the tumult of those who practice iniquity, 3 who whet their tongues like swords and aim bitter words as arrows, 4 to shoot in secret at the blameless; suddenly they discharge them, and they do not fear. 5 They fortify one another in an evil purpose; they recount their scheme to hide snares, saying, “Who will see us?” 6 They search out iniquities and complete a diligent investigation, probing the inner thought and the deep heart of man.

God’s Sudden Judgment and the Exultation of the Righteous

7 But God will shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they will be stricken. 8 Their own tongues will cause them to stumble; all who behold them will shake their heads in derision. 9 Then all mankind will fear and proclaim the work of God, and they will discern His deed. 10 The righteous will rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him; all the upright in heart will glory.
