Warnings Against Envy and the Company of Evildoers

1 Do not envy malefactors, nor covet fellowship with them; 2 for their hearts brood upon violence, and their lips give utterance to mischief.

Wisdom, Counsel, and the Building of the House

3 By wisdom a house is erected, and by discernment it is made firm; 4 by knowledge its chambers are filled with every precious and delectable possession.

5 A sagacious man is mighty, and a man of understanding augments his power; 6 for by prudent counsel you shall wage your war, and in a multitude of counselors there is victory.

7 Exalted wisdom lies beyond a fool's grasp, so he does not open his mouth at the city gate; 8 one who plots evil will be called a master of schemes; 9 the conception of folly is sin, and the scorner is an abomination to humankind.

10 If you faint in the day of adversity, your strength is meager; 11 rescue those being led away to death, and restrain those staggering toward slaughter; 12 if you say, "Behold, we did not know this," does not He who weighs hearts perceive it? Does not He who guards your soul know? And will He not requite each person according to his deeds?

Filial Exhortations, Righteous Resilience, and Reverence for Authority

13 Consume honey, my son, for it is wholesome, and the honeycomb is sweet upon your palate; 14 know that wisdom is such for your soul: if you find it, there shall be a future, and your hope shall not be severed.

15 Do not lie in ambush, O wicked man, against the abode of the righteous, nor ravage his resting place; 16 for though the righteous man falls seven times, he rises again, whereas the wicked are brought down by adversity.

17 Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, and let not your heart exult when he stumbles; 18 lest the the LORD see and it displease Him, and He turn His wrath away from him.

19 Do not be vexed by evildoers, nor envy the wicked; 20 for the evil man shall have no future, and the lamp of the wicked shall be extinguished.

21 My son, revere the the LORD and the king, and do not consort with the seditious; 22 for their ruin shall arise suddenly, and who can know the downfall of them both?

Justice, Honest Speech, Neighborly Conduct, and the Lesson of the Sluggard's Field

23 These too are the sayings of the wise: it is not good to show partiality in judgment; 24 he who says to the wicked, "You are righteous," the peoples will curse him, and the nations will abhor him; 25 but those who rebuke the wicked shall find delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them; 26 he who returns a straightforward answer kisses the lips.

27 Prepare your work outdoors, and make ready for yourself in the field; and afterwards build your house; 28 do not bear witness against your neighbor without cause, nor deceive with your lips; 29 do not say, "I will do to him as he has done to me; I will repay the man according to his work."

30 I passed by the field of a sluggard and by the vineyard of a man devoid of understanding; 31 and lo, it was all overgrown with thorns, nettles had covered its surface, and its stone wall lay in ruins; 32 I observed and took it to heart; I saw and received instruction: 33 "A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest— 34 and your poverty shall come like a robber, and your need like an armed man."
