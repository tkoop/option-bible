Commendation of Phoebe and Salutations to the Roman Saints

1 I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a deacon of the church at Cenchreae, 2 so that you may receive her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and may assist her in whatever affair she may require of you; for she herself has been a patroness to many, and to myself as well.

3 Greet Prisca and Aquila, my coworkers in Christ Jesus, 4 who imperiled their own lives on my behalf—men to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles. 5 Greet also the church that meets in their house. Greet my beloved Epaenetus, the first convert in Asia to Christ.

6 Greet Mary, who toiled exceedingly on your behalf. 7 Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and fellow prisoners, who are illustrious among the apostles and who were in Christ before me. 8 Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord. 9 Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved.

10 Greet Apelles, the approved in Christ. Greet those of the household of Aristobulus. 11 Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet those of the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord. 12 Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet the beloved Persis, who has labored much in the Lord.

13 Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who is a mother to me as well. 14 Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them. 15 Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them. 16 Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.

Admonition Against Divisive Intruders

17 Now I exhort you, brothers, to keep watch over those who engender dissensions and stumbling blocks contrary to the teaching you have learned, and to turn away from them. 18 For such persons do not serve our Lord Christ, but their own appetites, and by smooth speech and flattery they deceive the hearts of the guileless. 19 For your obedience has become known to all; therefore I rejoice over you, yet I desire you to be wise in what is good and innocent in what is evil. 20 The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

Final Greetings from Paul's Associates

21 Timothy, my coworker, greets you, as do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my kinsmen. 22 I, Tertius, who penned this letter, greet you in the Lord. 23 Gaius, my host and host to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, greets you, and Quartus the brother.

Doxology to the Only Wise God

25 Now to Him who is able to establish you according to my gospel and the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery kept secret for eternal ages, 26 but now manifested and made known through the prophetic writings, according to the commandment of the eternal God, for the obedience of faith among all the nations— 27 to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, be glory forever and ever. Amen.
