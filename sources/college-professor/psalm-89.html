A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite: The LORD’s Covenant Love and the Lament of Its Apparent Collapse

1 I will sing of the LORD’s steadfast love forever; with my mouth I will make known Your faithfulness to every generation.

2 For I said, “Steadfast love will be built up forever; in the heavens You will establish Your faithfulness.”

3 You have said, “I have made a covenant with My chosen one; I have sworn to David My servant: 4 ‘I will establish your offspring forever, and build your throne for all generations.’” Selah

The Incomparable Majesty of the LORD

5 The heavens praise Your wonders, O the LORD, Your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones. 6 For who in the skies can be compared to the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD, 7 a God greatly feared in the council of the holy ones, and awesome beyond all who surround Him? 8 O the LORD God of Hosts, who is mighty like You, O the LORD? Your faithfulness surrounds You.

9 You rule the raging sea; when its waves surge, You still them. 10 You crushed Rahab as though it were a corpse; You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm. 11 The heavens are Yours, and the earth is Yours; the world and all that fills it—You founded them. 12 The north and the south You created; Tabor and Hermon rejoice in Your name. 13 You possess might; strong is Your hand, and high is Your right hand. 14 Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne; steadfast love and faithfulness go before You.

15 Blessed are the people who know the festal shout; they walk, O the LORD, in the light of Your face, 16 they exult in Your name all day long, and in Your righteousness they are exalted. 17 For You are the glory of their strength; by Your favor our power is exalted. 18 For our shield belongs to the LORD, and our king to the Holy One of Israel.

The Divine Oracle to David

19 Long ago You spoke in a vision to Your godly ones and said, “I have granted help to one who is mighty; I have exalted one chosen from the people. 20 I have found David My servant; with My holy oil I have anointed him, 21 so that My hand may be established with him, and My arm may strengthen him. 22 No enemy shall deceive him, nor shall a son of wickedness afflict him. 23 I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him. 24 My faithfulness and My steadfast love shall be with him, and in My name his power shall be exalted. 25 I will set his hand on the sea and his right hand on the rivers.

“26 He will call to Me, ‘You are my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation.’ 27 I will also make him My firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth. 28 My steadfast love I will keep for him forever, and My covenant with him shall remain firm. 29 I will establish his descendants forever, and his throne as long as the heavens endure. 30 If his children forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments, 31 if they violate My statutes and do not keep My commandments, 32 then I will punish their transgression with the rod and their iniquity with blows. 33 But I will not withdraw My steadfast love from him, nor will I prove false to My faithfulness. 34 I will not violate My covenant, nor alter the word that has gone forth from My lips. 35 Once for all I have sworn by My holiness; I will not lie to David. 36 His descendants shall endure forever, and his throne before Me like the sun. 37 Like the moon it shall be established forever, a faithful witness in the heavens.” Selah

The Lament over the Rejection of the Anointed King

38 But now You have cast off and rejected him; You are full of wrath against Your anointed. 39 You have renounced the covenant with Your servant; You have profaned his crown in the dust. 40 You have broken down all his walls; You have brought his strongholds to ruin. 41 All who pass by plunder him; he has become a scorn to his neighbors. 42 You have exalted the right hand of his foes; You have made all his enemies rejoice. 43 You have turned back the edge of his sword and have not upheld him in battle. 44 You have ended his splendor and cast his throne to the ground. 45 You have shortened the days of his youth; You have covered him with shame. Selah

46 How long, O the LORD? Will You hide Yourself forever? How long will Your wrath burn like fire? 47 Remember how brief my life is; for what futility have You created all the children of man? 48 What man can live and never see death? Who can deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah

49 O Lord, where are Your former acts of steadfast love, which You swore to David in Your faithfulness? 50 Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants; how I bear in my heart the insults of all the many peoples, 51 with which Your enemies mock, O the LORD, with which they mock the footsteps of Your anointed. 52 Blessed be the LORD forever! Amen and Amen.
