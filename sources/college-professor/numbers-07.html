The Anointing and Consecration of the Tabernacle

1 On the day Moses finished erecting the tabernacle, he anointed and consecrated it, together with all its furnishings, the altar, and every utensil thereof; he anointed them and set them apart as holy.

Carts and Oxen for the Levites

2 The leaders of Israel—heads of their ancestral houses, chieftains of the tribes, those who presided over the enumeration—brought an offering. 3 They brought before the LORD six covered carts and twelve oxen, one cart for every two leaders and one ox for each, and presented them before the tabernacle.

4 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 5 "Accept these from them, that they may be employed in the service of the tent of meeting, and give them to the Levites, to each according to his service."

6 So Moses took the carts and the oxen and gave them to the Levites. 7 He gave two carts and four oxen to the sons of Gershon, according to their service; 8 and four carts and eight oxen to the sons of Merari, according to their service, under the supervision of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest. 9 But to the sons of Kohath he gave none, because the service of the holy things was theirs to carry on their shoulders.

The Dedication Offerings of Israel's Leaders

10 The leaders brought the dedication offering for the altar on the day it was anointed, and they presented their offering before the altar. 11 And the LORD said to Moses, "One leader shall present his offering each day for the dedication of the altar."

12 On the first day Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah, presented his offering. 13 His offering was one silver plate weighing one hundred thirty shekels, and one silver bowl of seventy shekels, both by the sanctuary shekel, both filled with fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering; 14 one gold dish of ten shekels, filled with incense; 15 one bull, one ram, and one male lamb a year old, for a burnt offering; 16 one male goat for a sin offering; 17 and for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs a year old. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

18 On the second day Nethanel son of Zuar, of the tribe of Issachar, presented his offering. 19 His offering was one silver plate weighing one hundred thirty shekels, and one silver bowl of seventy shekels, both by the sanctuary shekel, both filled with fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering; 20 one gold dish of ten shekels, filled with incense; 21 one bull, one ram, and one male lamb a year old, for a burnt offering; 22 one male goat for a sin offering; 23 and for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs a year old. This was the offering of Nethanel son of Zuar.

84 This was the dedication offering of the leaders of Israel for the altar on the day it was anointed: twelve silver plates, twelve silver bowls, and twelve gold dishes. 85 Each silver plate weighed one hundred thirty shekels, and each bowl seventy, so that all the silver vessels amounted to two thousand four hundred shekels by the sanctuary shekel. 86 The twelve gold dishes, filled with incense, weighed ten shekels each by the sanctuary shekel, so that all the gold of the dishes amounted to one hundred twenty shekels. 87 All the animals for the burnt offering were twelve bulls, twelve rams, and twelve male lambs a year old, with their grain offering, together with twelve male goats for a sin offering. 88 All the animals for the sacrifice of peace offerings were twenty-four bulls, sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty male lambs a year old. This was the dedication of the altar after it was anointed.

The LORD Speaks from the Mercy Seat

89 When Moses entered the tent of meeting to speak with Him, he heard the voice speaking to him from above the mercy seat that is on the ark of the testimony, from between the two cherubim; and He spoke to him.
