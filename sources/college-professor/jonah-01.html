The LORD's Summons and Jonah's Flight to Tarshish

1 The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying, 2 “Arise, proceed to Nineveh, that great city, and cry out against it, for its wickedness has ascended before Me.” 3 Yet Jonah rose to flee toward Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. Descending to Joppa, he found a vessel bound for Tarshish; he paid its fare and embarked with its company, intent on reaching Tarshish apart from the presence of the LORD.

The Tempest, the Sailors' Terror, and Their Interrogation of Jonah

4 Then the LORD hurled a violent wind upon the sea, and a tempest of extraordinary magnitude arose upon the waters, so that the ship seemed on the verge of disintegration. 5 The mariners, seized by terror, each cried out to his own god, and they cast the ship's cargo into the sea to lighten their burden; meanwhile Jonah had descended into the vessel's lowest recesses, lain down, and sunk into a profound slumber.

6 The shipmaster approached him and said, “What are you doing, sleeper? Arise and call upon your God! Perhaps God will consider us, so that we may not perish.”

7 They said to one another, “Come, let us cast lots, so that we may ascertain on whose account this calamity has befallen us.” They cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

8 They said to him, “Tell us, we pray, on whose account this calamity has come upon us. What is your occupation? Whence do you come? What is your native land? And to what people do you belong?”

9 He said to them, “I am a Hebrew, and I revere the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land.”

10 The men were then seized by an exceedingly great fear, and they said to him, “What have you done?” For they knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, because he had disclosed it to them.

Jonah's Self-Offering and the Mariners' Prayer

11 The sea was becoming increasingly tempestuous, so they said to him, “What shall we do to you, that the sea may be calm for us?”

12 He said to them, “Lift me up and cast me into the sea; then the sea will be calm for you, for I know that it is on my account that this great storm has come upon you.”

13 Nevertheless, the men rowed strenuously to bring the vessel back to land, but they could not, for the sea grew ever more tempestuous against them. 14 Therefore they cried out to the LORD and said, “Please, O the LORD, do not let us perish on account of this man's life, and do not charge us with innocent blood; for You, O the LORD, have done as You pleased.”

The Cessation of the Storm and Jonah's Swallowing by the Great Fish

15 So they lifted Jonah up and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its raging. 16 Then the men feared the LORD with great fear, and they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows.

17 And the LORD appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah remained in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.
