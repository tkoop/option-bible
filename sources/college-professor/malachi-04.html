The Eschatological Day: Judgment for the Wicked and Radiant Salvation for the Righteous

1 “For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, and all the arrogant together with every worker of iniquity shall be as stubble; the day that is coming will set them ablaze,” says the LORD of Hosts, “so that it leaves them neither root nor branch.”

The Sun of Righteousness and the Triumph of Those Who Revere His Name

2 But to you who revere My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings, and you will go forth and leap like calves released from the stall. 3 “Then you will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes beneath the soles of your feet on the day that I am preparing,” says the LORD of Hosts.

Covenant Memory and the Coming Forerunner

4 Remember the Law of Moses My servant, the statutes and ordinances that I commanded him for all Israel at Horeb. 5 Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and awe-inspiring day of the LORD. 6 And he will turn the hearts of fathers to their children, and the hearts of children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the land with a curse.
