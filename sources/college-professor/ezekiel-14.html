Idolatry in the Heart and the Refusal of Consultation

Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me. Then the word of the the LORD came to me, saying:

"Son of man, these men have brought their idols up into their hearts and have placed the stumbling block of their iniquity before their faces. Should I be inquired of by them at all? Therefore speak to them and say to them, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Every man of the house of Israel who sets up his idols in his heart and places the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and then comes to the prophet, I, the the LORD, will answer him according to the multitude of his idols, in order that I may seize the hearts of the house of Israel, who have all become estranged from Me through their idols.'

"Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Repent and turn away from your idols, and turn your faces away from all your abominations. For every man of the house of Israel, or of the aliens who sojourn in Israel, who separates himself from Me and sets up his idols in his heart and places the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and yet comes to the prophet to inquire of Me on his own behalf, I, the the LORD, will answer him Myself. I will set My face against that man and make him a sign and a byword, and I will cut him off from the midst of My people; and you shall know that I am the the LORD. And if the prophet is deceived and speaks a word, I, the the LORD, have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out My hand against him and destroy him from the midst of My people Israel. And they shall bear their punishment; the punishment of the inquirer shall be like the punishment of the prophet, so that the house of Israel may no longer stray from Me and no longer defile themselves with all their transgressions, but may be My people and I may be their God, declares the Lord GOD.'

The Limits of Righteousness Amid National Judgment

The word of the the LORD came to me, saying:

"Son of man, when a land sins against Me by acting treacherously, and I stretch out My hand against it and break its supply of bread and send famine upon it and cut off from it man and beast, even if these three men—Noah, Daniel, and Job—were in it, they would deliver only themselves by their righteousness, declares the Lord GOD."

"If I cause wild beasts to pass through the land and they bereave it, so that it becomes desolate and no one passes through because of the beasts, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, even if these three men were in it, they would deliver neither sons nor daughters; they alone would be delivered, but the land would be desolate."

"Or if I bring a sword upon that land and say, 'Sword, pass through the land,' and I cut off from it man and beast, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, those three men would not deliver sons or daughters, but only themselves."

"Or if I send pestilence into that land and pour out My wrath upon it in blood, to cut off from it man and beast, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, they would deliver neither son nor daughter; they would deliver only themselves by their righteousness."

The Vindication of Divine Justice and the Remnant

"For thus says the Lord GOD: How much more when I send upon Jerusalem My four severe judgments—sword, famine, wild beasts, and pestilence—to cut off from it man and beast! Yet behold, survivors will be left in it, sons and daughters who will be brought out; behold, they are coming out to you, and when you see their ways and their deeds, you will be consoled concerning the disaster that I have brought upon Jerusalem, concerning all that I have brought upon it. They will console you when you see their ways and their deeds, and you will know that I have not done without cause all that I have done to it, declares the Lord GOD."
