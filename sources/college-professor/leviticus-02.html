General Regulations for the Grain Offering

1 Whensoever a person presents an oblation of grain unto the LORD, his offering shall consist of fine flour; he shall pour oil upon it and place frankincense upon it. 2 He shall bring it unto Aaron’s sons, the priests, and the priest shall take from it a handful of the fine flour and of its oil, together with all the frankincense thereof, and shall burn the memorial portion upon the altar as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma unto the LORD. 3 The remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy portion among the offerings made by fire unto the LORD.

Preparations Baked or Cooked in Various Vessels

4 Should you present a grain offering baked in an oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mingled with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil. 5 If your offering is a grain offering upon a griddle, it shall be of fine flour, unleavened, mingled with oil. 6 You shall crumble it into pieces and pour oil upon it; it is a grain offering. 7 If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil. 8 When you bring unto the LORD the grain offering prepared in any of these manners, you shall present it unto the priest, who shall convey it unto the altar. 9 The priest shall lift from the grain offering its memorial portion and burn it upon the altar as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma unto the LORD. 10 The rest of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy portion among the offerings made by fire unto the LORD.

Prohibitions, Covenant Salt, and Related Obligations

11 No grain offering that you present unto the LORD shall be made with leaven, for you must not burn any leaven or honey as an offering made by fire unto the LORD. 12 You may present them as an offering of firstfruits unto the LORD, but they shall not be offered upon the altar as a pleasing aroma. 13 You shall season all your grain offerings with salt; you must not allow the salt of the covenant of your God to be absent from your grain offering; with all your offerings you shall present salt.

Firstfruits and the Memorial Portion

14 If you present a grain offering of firstfruits unto the LORD, you shall offer crushed new grain, roasted in the fire, as the grain offering of your firstfruits. 15 You shall put oil upon it and place frankincense upon it; it is a grain offering. 16 The priest shall burn as its memorial portion some of the crushed grain and oil, together with all its frankincense, as an offering made by fire unto the LORD.
