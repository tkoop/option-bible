Rejoice in the Lord and Guard against the Mutilators

1 Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. It imposes no onerous burden upon me to reiterate these same admonitions to you; indeed, such repetition constitutes a safeguard for you. 2 Beware of the dogs; beware of the malevolent laborers; beware of the mutilation. 3 For we are the authentic circumcision—those who render worship by the Spirit of God, boast in Christ Jesus, and repose no confidence in the flesh.

Paul’s Pedigree and the Transcendent Value of Knowing Christ

4 Though I myself might repose confidence in the flesh—if any other presumes to repose confidence in the flesh, I possess still greater warrant: 5 circumcised on the eighth day, of the stock of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew sprung from Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee; 6 as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to righteousness derived from the law, blameless. 7 Yet whatever things were gains to me, these I have reckoned as loss for Christ’s sake. 8 Indeed, I count all things to be loss on account of the transcendent excellence of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord, for whose sake I have suffered the loss of all things and regard them as rubbish, that I may gain Christ. 9 and be found in Him, not possessing a righteousness of my own derived from the law, but that which is through faith in Christ—the righteousness that proceeds from God on the basis of faith— 10 that I may know Him and the power of His resurrection, and the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to His death, 11 if somehow I may attain to the resurrection from the dead.

Pressing On toward the Upward Call

12 Not that I have already obtained it or have already been perfected; but I press on, if indeed I may lay hold of that for which Christ Jesus also laid hold of me. 13 Brothers, I do not reckon myself to have laid hold of it; but one thing I do: forgetting the things behind and stretching forward to the things ahead, 14 I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward calling of God in Christ Jesus. 15 Let as many of us as are mature hold this view; and if in anything you think differently, God will reveal even this to you. 16 Nevertheless, let us walk by the same standard to which we have attained.

Enemies of the Cross and Our Heavenly Citizenship

17 Brothers, become fellow imitators of me, and observe those who walk in this manner, just as you have us as a pattern. 18 For many walk—of whom I have often told you, and now tell you even weeping—as enemies of the cross of Christ. 19 Whose end is destruction, whose god is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame—those who set their minds on earthly things. 20 For our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, 21 who will transform our lowly body to be conformed to His glorious body, according to the working of His power, by which He is able even to subject all things to Himself.
