Israel's Hidden Iniquity and the King's Delight

“1 Whenever I would bring healing to Israel, Ephraim's iniquity is disclosed and Samaria's depravities are uncovered; for they have perpetrated falsehood, and a thief breaks in, while a marauding band pillages the street. 2 Yet they do not reckon within their hearts that I remember all their evil; now their deeds have encircled them, and they stand exposed before My face. 3 They delight the king with their wickedness, and the princes with their falsehoods.

The Oven of Conspiracy and the Fall of Kings

“4 They are all adulterers, resembling an oven heated by the baker, who ceases stoking from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened. 5 On the day of our king, the princes became inflamed with wine, and he stretched forth his hand in fellowship with scoffers. 6 For they have drawn near with hearts like an oven, while they lie in ambush; all night their baker slumbers, and by morning it blazes like a flaming fire. 7 They are all hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers; all their kings have fallen; there is none among them who calls upon Me.

Ephraim's Half-Baked Folly among the Nations

“8 Ephraim mingles among the nations; Ephraim has become a cake left unturned—half-baked and worthless. 9 Foreigners have consumed his vigor, yet he does not know it; gray hairs are sprinkled upon him, yet he remains unaware. 10 Israel's arrogance bears witness against him; yet they have not returned to the the LORD their God, nor have they sought Him in all this. 11 Ephraim has become like a gullible dove, devoid of understanding; they call to Egypt, they make their way to Assyria.

The LORD's Net and the Final Reckoning

“12 When they go, I will spread My net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of heaven; I will chastise them in accordance with the report given to their assembly. 13 Woe to them, for they have strayed from Me; ruin to them, for they have rebelled against Me! Though I would redeem them, they speak falsehoods against Me. 14 They do not cry out to Me from their hearts, but they wail upon their beds; for grain and new wine they slash themselves; they turn away from Me. 15 I indeed trained and strengthened their arms, yet they devise evil against Me. 16 They turn, but not to the Most High; they are like a faulty bow. Their leaders shall fall by the sword because of the insolence of their tongue; this shall be their mockery in the land of Egypt.”
