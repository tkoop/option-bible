Praise for the Wondrous Judgments of the LORD

1 O the LORD, You are my God; I will exalt You, and I will praise Your name, for You have wrought wonders—designs ordained from of old in perfect faithfulness. 2 For You have consigned the city to a heap and the fortified city to ruin; the palace of aliens is a city no more, and it shall never be rebuilt. 3 Therefore a mighty people will glorify You, and the cities of ruthless nations will revere You.

4 For You have been a fortress to the poor, a fortress to the needy in his distress, a refuge from the storm and a shade from the heat; for the breath of the ruthless is like a storm driving against a wall. 5 Like heat in a desiccated land, You will subdue the tumult of foreigners; like heat stilled beneath a cloud, the song of the ruthless will be silenced.

The Eschatological Banquet and the Defeat of Death

6 On this mountain the LORD of Hosts will prepare for all peoples a feast of rich food, a banquet of aged wines—rich food full of marrow, aged wine well refined. 7 On this mountain He will destroy the shroud that is cast over all peoples and the veil that is spread over all nations. 8 He will swallow up death forever; and the Lord the LORD will wipe away tears from every face; He will remove the reproach of His people from all the earth, for the LORD has spoken.

9 In that day it will be said, "Behold, this is our God; we have waited for Him, and He will save us. This is the LORD; we have waited for Him. Let us rejoice and be glad in His salvation."

Moab's Humiliation and the Triumph of Zion

10 For the hand of the LORD will rest upon this mountain, while Moab is trampled in its place, as straw is trampled in the waters of a dunghill. 11 He will spread out his hands in its midst, as a swimmer spreads his hands to swim; yet his pride will be brought low together with the craftiness of his hands. 12 He will bring down the high fortifications of your walls, lay them low, and cast them to the ground, even to the dust.
