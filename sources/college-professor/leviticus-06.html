Restitution, Guilt, and the Restoration of Wronged Neighbors

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "If a person sins and acts unfaithfully against the LORD by deceiving his neighbor concerning a deposit, or a pledge, or a stolen thing, or by extorting from his neighbor, 3 or if he has found what was lost and lied about it, swearing falsely concerning any one of all the things that a person may do and thereby sin— 4 then, when he has sinned and incurred guilt, he shall restore what he took by robbery, or the thing extorted, or the deposit entrusted to him, or the lost thing that he found. 5 Or, in regard to anything about which he swore falsely, he shall restore it in full and add a fifth to it; to the one to whom it belongs he shall give it on the day he presents his guilt offering. 6 And he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: a ram without blemish from the flock, according to your valuation, for a guilt offering. 7 The priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD, and he shall be forgiven for any of the things by which one may incur guilt.

The Burnt Offering and the Perpetual Fire

8 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 9 "Command Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the burnt offering—the burnt offering shall remain on the hearth of the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it. 10 The priest shall put on his linen tunic and linen undergarments on his flesh; he shall remove the ashes from the burnt offering consumed by fire on the altar and place them beside the altar. 11 Then he shall take off his garments, put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. 12 The fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. Every morning the priest shall burn wood on it, arrange the burnt offering on it, and burn the fat of the peace offerings on it. 13 Fire shall be kept burning on the altar continually; it shall not go out.

The Grain Offering and the Priests' Portion

14 "And this is the law of the grain offering: Aaron's sons shall present it before the LORD in front of the altar. 15 He shall take from it a handful of the fine flour of the grain offering, together with its oil and all the frankincense on the grain offering, and burn it on the altar as a memorial portion, a soothing aroma to the LORD. 16 Aaron and his sons shall eat the remainder of it; it shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place, in the court of the tent of meeting. 17 It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion from My offerings by fire; it is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering. 18 Every male among the sons of Aaron may eat it; it is a perpetual statute throughout your generations from the LORD's offerings by fire. Everyone who touches them shall be holy."

19 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 20 "This is the offering of Aaron and his sons, which they shall offer to the LORD on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening. 21 It shall be made with oil on a griddle; you shall bring it well stirred; as a grain offering of baked pieces you shall offer it, a soothing aroma to the LORD. 22 The priest who is anointed in his place from among his sons shall offer it; it is a perpetual statute to the LORD; it shall be wholly burned. 23 Every grain offering of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten.

The Sin Offering and Its Sacred Consumption

24 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 25 "Speak to Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the sin offering: In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before the LORD; it is most holy. 26 The priest who offers it for sin shall eat it; it shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the tent of meeting. 27 Whatever touches its flesh shall be holy, and when some of its blood is sprinkled on a garment, you shall wash what was sprinkled in a holy place. 28 An earthenware vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; and if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be scoured and rinsed with water. 29 Every male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy. 30 But no sin offering may be eaten from which any of its blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place; it shall be burned with fire."
