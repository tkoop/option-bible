The LORD's Triumph over Leviathan

1 In that day the LORD will bring judgment upon Leviathan, the fleeing serpent, with His fierce, great, and mighty sword—upon Leviathan, the coiling serpent—and He will slay the dragon that is in the sea.

The LORD's Cherished Vineyard

2 In that day: “Sing of a delightful vineyard. 3 I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually. Lest anyone harm it, I guard it night and day. 4 I have no wrath. If only thorns and briers confronted Me! I would march against them in battle and set them all ablaze together. 5 Or let them seize My stronghold and make peace with Me—yes, let them make peace with Me.”

6 In the days to come, Jacob will take root; Israel will bud and blossom and fill the whole world with fruit.

The Purging of Jacob and the Ruin of the Fortified City

7 Has He struck them as He struck their strikers? Or have they been slain as their slayers were slain? 8 By measured exile You contend with her; He drives her away with His fierce blast in the day of the east wind.

9 Therefore by this Jacob's iniquity will be atoned for, and this is all the fruit of removing his sin: when he makes all the altar stones like pulverized chalk, no Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing. 10 For the fortified city is desolate—a forsaken dwelling, abandoned like the wilderness. There the calf grazes; there it lies down and strips its boughs. 11 When its boughs wither, they are broken; women come and use them for kindling. For this is a people devoid of understanding; therefore their Maker will not pity them, and their Creator will not show them grace.

The Regathering of the Dispersed

12 In that day the LORD will thresh from the River Euphrates to the Brook of Egypt, and you shall be gathered one by one, O sons of Israel. 13 In that day a great trumpet shall be blown, and those perishing in Assyria and those banished in Egypt shall come and worship the LORD on the holy mountain at Jerusalem.
