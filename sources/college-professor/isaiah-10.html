Woe to Those Who Decree Injustice

1 Woe to those who legislate iniquity, and to the scribes who codify oppression— 2 who turn aside the needy from justice and despoil the poor of My people of their rightful claim, so that widows become their spoil and orphans are plundered.

3 What, then, will you do on the day of reckoning, when devastation arrives from afar? To whom will you flee for succor, and where will you abandon your glory? 4 Nothing remains but to crouch among the captives or fall among the slain. Yet for all this His anger is not turned away, and His hand remains outstretched.

Assyria, the Rod of Divine Wrath

5 Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger; the staff in their hand is My indignation. 6 I send him against a godless nation; I commission him against a people destined for My wrath, to seize loot and snatch plunder, and to trample them like clay in the streets. 7 Yet this is not his purpose, nor does he so intend; rather, it is in his heart to annihilate and to cut off many nations.

8 “Are not my commanders all kings?” he says. 9 “Is not Calno like Carchemish? Is not Hamath like Arpad? Is not Samaria like Damascus? 10 As my hand has seized the kingdoms of the idols—whose graven images surpassed those of Jerusalem and Samaria— 11 shall I not do to Jerusalem and its idols as I have done to Samaria and its idols?”

12 When the Lord has completed all His work against Mount Zion and Jerusalem, He will punish the king of Assyria for the fruit of his arrogant heart and the glory of his haughty eyes. 13 For he says: “By the strength of my hand I have accomplished this, and by my wisdom, for I am discerning. I have removed the boundaries of nations and plundered their treasures; and, like a mighty one, I have brought down their inhabitants. 14 My hand has found, as a nest, the wealth of the peoples; and as one gathers forsaken eggs, I have gathered all the earth. There was none that moved a wing, opened its mouth, or chirped.”

15 Does the axe boast against the one who cuts with it? Does the saw magnify itself against the one who wields it? As if a rod could wield him who lifts it! As if a staff could lift him who is not wood! 16 Therefore the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will send a wasting disease among his stalwart warriors; and beneath his glory a fire will be kindled like a blazing flame. 17 And the Light of Israel will be for a fire, and his Holy One for a flame; and it will burn and consume his thorns and briers in a single day. 18 The glory of his forest and his fruitful field—He will consume both soul and body; and it will be as when a sick man wastes away. 19 The rest of the trees of his forest will be so few that a child could write them down.

The Remnant of Israel and the Deliverance of Zion

20 In that day the remnant of Israel and the survivors of the house of Jacob will no longer depend on the one who struck them, but will truly depend on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel. 21 A remnant will return, the remnant of Jacob, to the Mighty God. 22 For though your people, O Israel, be as the sand of the sea, only a remnant will return. Destruction is decreed, overflowing with righteousness. 23 For the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will carry out the destruction decreed upon the whole earth.

24 Therefore thus says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts: “O My people who dwell in Zion, do not fear the Assyrian. He will strike you with a rod and lift up his staff against you, as Egypt did.” 25 For in a very little while My indignation will be spent, and My anger will be turned to their destruction. 26 And the LORD of Hosts will brandish a scourge against him, as in the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb; and He will raise His staff over the sea, as He did against Egypt. 27 In that day his burden will be lifted from your shoulder, and his yoke from your neck; the yoke will be broken because of the anointing oil.

The Assyrian Advance and the LORD’s Judgment

28 He has come to Aiath; he has passed through Migron; at Michmash he stores his supplies. 29 They have crossed the pass; their lodging is at Geba. Ramah trembles; Gibeah of Saul has fled. 30 Cry aloud, O daughter of Gallim! Listen, O Laishah! O poor Anathoth! 31 Madmenah has fled; the inhabitants of Gebim have sought refuge. 32 This very day he will halt at Nob; he will shake his fist at the mountain of the daughter of Zion, the hill of Jerusalem.

33 Behold, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will lop off the boughs with terror; and those tall in stature will be cut down, and the lofty will be humbled. 34 He will clear the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon will fall by the Mighty One.
