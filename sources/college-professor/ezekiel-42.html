The Northern Chambers and Their Architectural Arrangement

1 Then the guide conducted me outward through the northern gate into the outer court, bringing me to the suite of chambers situated opposite the segregated precinct and opposite the edifice on the north. 2 Along the northern façade of the outer structure extended a chamber one hundred cubits in length and fifty cubits in breadth. 3 The uppermost chambers were recessed in relation to those on the lower and middle levels, and they were withdrawn from the wall of the inner court. 4 Before these chambers lay a passage ten cubits wide and one hundred cubits long, with their entrances oriented toward the north.

5 The upper chambers were more contracted, because the galleries appropriated greater space from them than from the lower and middle floors. 6 For they were disposed in three stories and lacked columns such as those in the courts; consequently, the upper chambers were set back from the ground more than the lower and middle ones. 7 Before the chambers stood an outer wall fifty cubits long, running parallel to the chambers and the outer court. 8 Indeed, the chambers opening onto the outer court were fifty cubits long, whereas those facing the temple were one hundred cubits long.

The Southern Chambers and Their Corresponding Access

9 Below these chambers was an entrance on the east side, as one enters them from the outer court. 10 On the south side, along the wall of the outer court, there were chambers facing the separate area and the building, with a passageway before them analogous to that of the northern chambers. 11 They possessed the same length and breadth, with comparable exits, entrances, and arrangements. 12 Corresponding to the doors of the southern chambers was an entrance at the beginning of the passageway, the passageway before the corresponding wall on the east, as one enters.

The Sanctity of the Priestly Chambers

13 Then the man said to me, “The northern and southern chambers facing the temple precinct are holy chambers, where the priests who approach the LORD shall eat the most holy offerings. There they shall deposit the most holy offerings—the grain offerings, the sin offerings, and the guilt offerings—because the place itself is holy.”

14 When the priests enter, they must not depart from the holy area into the outer court without first relinquishing the garments in which they minister, for those garments are holy. They are to don other clothes before they approach the people.

The Measured Enclosure of the Holy Precinct

15 Now when the man had completed measuring the inner temple, he led me out by way of the east gate and measured the surrounding area. 16 He measured the east side with the measuring rod: five hundred cubits. 17 He measured the north side: five hundred cubits. 18 He measured the south side: five hundred cubits. 19 He turned and measured the west side: five hundred cubits. 20 Thus he measured the area on all four sides; it had a wall all around, five hundred cubits in length and five hundred cubits in breadth, to demarcate the holy from the common.
