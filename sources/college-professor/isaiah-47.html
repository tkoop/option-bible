Babylon's Descent from Sovereignty into Dust

1 Descend and seat yourself upon the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon; settle upon the ground bereft of a throne, O daughter of the Chaldeans, for you shall no longer be acclaimed as tender and delicate. 2 Take up the millstones and grind flour; strip away your veil, bare your skirt, expose your thigh, and ford the rivers. 3 Your nakedness shall be uncovered, and your disgrace shall be exposed to view; I will exact retribution, sparing no mortal.

4 Our Redeemer—the LORD of Hosts is His name—the Holy One of Israel.

The Holy One's Retribution for Cruelty and Arrogance

5 Sit in silence and enter the darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans, for you shall no longer be called mistress of kingdoms. 6 I was angry with My people; I profaned My inheritance and delivered them into your hand. You showed them no mercy; upon the aged you made your yoke exceedingly heavy. 7 You said, "I shall be mistress forever," so that you did not lay these things to heart, nor remember their final outcome.

8 Now, then, hear this, you who luxuriate in pleasures, who dwell in security, who say in your heart, 'I am, and there is no one besides me; I shall not sit as a widow, nor know the loss of children.' 9 These two things shall befall you in a moment, in a single day: bereavement and widowhood. They shall come upon you in all their fullness, notwithstanding your many sorceries and the great abundance of your enchantments. 10 You have trusted in your wickedness; you have said, 'No one sees me.' Your wisdom and your knowledge have led you astray, and you have said in your heart, 'I am, and there is no one besides me.' 11 But evil shall come upon you, which you shall not know how to charm away; and calamity shall fall upon you, which you cannot expiate; and desolation shall overtake you suddenly, beyond your foreknowledge.

The Impotence of Babylon's Occult Arts

12 Stand now among your enchantments and in the multitude of your sorceries, in which you have toiled since your youth; perhaps you can derive some profit, perhaps you can prevail. 13 You have wearied yourself with your many counsels; let them stand now and save you—those who divide the heavens, who gaze upon the stars, who announce by the months what shall befall you. 14 Behold, they are like stubble; fire consumes them; they cannot deliver themselves from the power of the flame; there is no ember to warm them, no fire to sit before. 15 Thus have they become to you—those with whom you have trafficked, your merchants from your youth—each strays to his own quarter; there is no one to save you.
