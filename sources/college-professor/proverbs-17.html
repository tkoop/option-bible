Proverbs Seventeen: Domestic Peace and the Testing of Hearts

1 A parched morsel accompanied by tranquility surpasses a house replete with sacrificial feasts embroiled in contention. 2 A discerning servant shall govern a shameful son and apportion the inheritance among the brethren. 3 As the crucible refines silver and the furnace gold, so the LORD assays the hearts of men. 4 A wicked man gives heed to iniquitous lips, and a deceitful tongue hearkens to a destructive tongue. 5 He who derides the indigent reproaches his Maker; he who exults in disaster shall not be held guiltless.

Lineage, Speech, and the Power of Concealment

6 Grandchildren constitute the crown of the aged, and the splendor of children is their fathers. 7 Eloquent discourse ill befits a fool; how much less do mendacious lips befit a noble. 8 A bribe operates as a talisman in the eyes of its purveyor; whithersoever he turns, he prospers. 9 He who veils a transgression seeks love, but he who rehearses a matter alienates an intimate.

Reproof, Rebellion, and the Folly of Contention

10 A single reproof penetrates a discerning man more than a hundred blows penetrate a fool. 11 A wicked man seeks only rebellion, and a merciless messenger shall be dispatched against him. 12 Better to encounter a bereaved she-bear than a fool in his folly. 13 He who requites evil for good shall find that evil never departs from his household. 14 The inception of strife resembles the bursting of a dam; therefore abandon contention before it erupts. 15 To justify the wicked and to condemn the righteous—both alike are an abomination to the LORD. 16 Wherefore should a fool possess the price of wisdom in his hand, when he is devoid of understanding? 17 A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.

Suretyship, Friendship, and the Discipline of Speech

18 A man devoid of understanding strikes hands in pledge and becomes surety for his neighbor. 19 He who loves transgression loves contention; he who exalts his threshold seeks ruin. 20 A perverse heart finds no good, and a deceitful tongue precipitates ruin. 21 He who begets a fool does so to his grief; the father of a fool shall have no joy. 22 A joyful heart is a salutary remedy, but a downcast spirit desiccates the bones. 23 The wicked man takes a bribe from the bosom to pervert the paths of justice. 24 Wisdom abides before the face of the discerning, but the eyes of a fool wander to the ends of the earth. 25 A foolish son is a vexation to his father and bitterness to her who bore him. 26 It is likewise unseemly to punish the righteous and to scourge the noble on account of uprightness. 27 A man of knowledge restrains his utterances; a man of understanding is cool of spirit. 28 Even a fool, when he holds his peace, is reputed wise; he who seals his lips is deemed discerning.
