Pastoral Deportment toward the Congregation's Generations

1 Do not rebuke an older man harshly, but exhort him as a father; address younger men as brothers, 2 older women as mothers, and younger women as sisters, with uncompromising purity.

Discerning True Widows and Familial Obligation

3 Honor widows who are genuinely widows. 4 Yet if any widow has children or grandchildren, let these first learn to manifest piety in their own household and to render due recompense to their progenitors, for this is acceptable in the sight of God. 5 Now the widow who is truly bereft of support has set her hope upon God and persists in supplications and prayers night and day. 6 But she who lives in voluptuous indulgence is dead while she still lives. 7 Command these things, so that they may be irreproachable. 8 But if anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for those of his household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

Enrollment and Stewardship of Widows

9 Let a widow be enrolled only if she has reached at least sixty years, having been the wife of one husband, 10 and is attested by good works: whether she has reared children, shown hospitality, washed the feet of the saints, relieved the afflicted, and devoted herself to every good work. 11 But refuse younger widows; for when their passions draw them away from Christ, they desire to marry, 12 incurring condemnation because they have repudiated their first pledge. 13 At the same time they learn idleness, going from house to house; and not only idle, but gossips and busybodies, speaking what should not be spoken. 14 Therefore I wish younger widows to marry, bear children, manage their households, and afford the adversary no occasion for slander. 15 For some have already turned aside after Satan. 16 If any believing woman has widows, let her assist them, and let not the church be burdened, so that it may assist those who are truly widows.

Honoring and Adjudicating Elders

17 Let elders who preside well be counted worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in word and teaching. 18 For the Scripture says, You shall not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain, and, The laborer is worthy of his wages. 19 Do not receive an accusation against an elder except on the testimony of two or three witnesses. 20 Those who sin, rebuke before all, that the rest also may be seized by fear.

Final Charge and Personal Counsel

21 I charge you before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels to keep these things without prejudice, doing nothing by partiality. 22 Do not lay hands on anyone hastily, nor share in others' sins; keep yourself pure. 23 No longer drink water only, but use a little wine because of your stomach and your frequent ailments. 24 The sins of some men are evident, going before them to judgment; but for others they follow after. 25 Likewise, good works are evident, and those that are otherwise cannot be concealed.
