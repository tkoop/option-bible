The LORD's Compassion and Israel's Restoration

1 For the LORD will have compassion on Jacob and will choose Israel again; He will settle them upon their own land, and the foreigner will join them and attach himself to the house of Jacob. 2 The nations will conduct them and restore them to their homeland, and the house of Israel will possess them as bondmen and bondwomen in the land of the LORD; they will take captive their former captors and exercise dominion over their oppressors. 3 On the day the LORD gives you rest from your pain and turmoil and from the hard service imposed upon you,

The Taunt Against the King of Babylon

4 you will take up this taunt against the king of Babylon and say, "How the oppressor has ceased! How the golden city has ceased!" 5 The LORD has broken the staff of the wicked, the scepter of rulers, 6 who smote the peoples in fury with incessant blows and subjugated the nations in wrath without relenting. 7 The whole earth reposes in tranquility; it bursts into song. 8 Even the cypresses and the cedars of Lebanon exult over you, saying, "Since you were laid low, no woodcutter has ascended against us."

9 Sheol beneath is agog to receive you at your coming; it rouses the shades to welcome you, even all the chieftains of the earth, and makes all the kings of the nations rise from their thrones. 10 They all answer you: "You also have been enfeebled as we are; you have been made like us." 11 Your pomp is brought down to Sheol, together with the music of your lyres; maggots are spread beneath you, and worms envelop you.

12 How you have fallen from heaven, O Day Star, son of Dawn! How you have been hewn down to the earth, you who prostrated the nations! 13 You said in your heart, "I will ascend to heaven; I will exalt my throne above the stars of God; I will sit upon the mount of assembly in the uttermost north. 14 I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will make myself comparable to the Most High." 15 Yet you are brought down to Sheol, to the uttermost depths of the pit.

16 Those who behold you gaze upon you and scrutinize you: "Is this the man who made the earth tremble, who shook kingdoms, 17 who rendered the world a wasteland and demolished its cities, who would not release his captives to their homes?" 18 All the kings of the nations repose in glory, each in his own sepulcher. 19 But you are cast forth from your grave like a despised shoot, clothed with the slain, those pierced by the sword, who descend to the stones of the pit, like a trampled corpse. 20 You will not be united with them in burial, because you have laid waste your land and massacred your people. The seed of evildoers will never be named. 21 Prepare a place of slaughter for his sons on account of the iniquity of their fathers; they shall never arise and take possession of the land or populate the face of the world with cities.

The LORD's Decree Against Babylon and Assyria

22 "I will rise against them," declares the LORD of Hosts. "I will cut off from Babylon name and remnant, progeny and posterity," declares the LORD. 23 "I will make it an inheritance of hedgehogs and marshes of water, and I will sweep it with the broom of destruction," declares the LORD of Hosts. 24 The LORD of Hosts has sworn: "Surely, as I have devised, so shall it come to pass; and as I have purposed, so shall it stand. 25 I will shatter Assyria in My land; I will tread him underfoot upon My mountains. His yoke shall be removed from their shoulders, and his burden from their backs." 26 This is the counsel devised for the whole earth, and this is the hand stretched forth over all the nations. 27 For the LORD of Hosts has counseled, and who shall frustrate it? His hand is stretched out, and who shall turn it back?"

Oracle Concerning Philistia

28 In the year of King Ahaz's demise, this oracle was delivered: 29 "Rejoice not, all you Philistia, that the rod which smote you is broken, for from the root of the serpent shall come forth a viper, and its offspring shall be a flying serpent. 30 The firstborn of the poor shall pasture, and the needy shall lie down in safety; but I will slay your root with famine, and your remnant I will put to death. 31 Wail, O gate; cry out, O city; dissolve in terror, all you Philistia; for smoke comes from the north, and there is no straggler in his ranks." 32 What answer, then, will be given to the messengers of the nation? "The LORD has founded Zion, and in her His afflicted people shall find refuge."
