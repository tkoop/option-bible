The Lord’s Zealous Commitment to Zion’s Vindication

1 For Zion’s sake I will not remain silent, and for Jerusalem’s sake I will not be still, until her righteousness radiates like the dawn and her salvation blazes like a torch. 2 The nations shall behold your righteousness, and all kings your glory; you shall be called by a new name that the mouth of the the LORD will designate. 3 You shall be a crown of splendor in the hand of the the LORD, a royal diadem in the hand of your God.

Jerusalem’s Transformed Identity and Divine Delight

4 No longer shall you be called Forsaken, nor shall your land be called Desolate; rather, you shall be called “My Delight Is in Her,” and your land “Married,” because the the LORD delights in you, and your land shall be married. 5 For as a young man marries a virgin, so your sons shall marry you; and as a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, so your God shall rejoice over you.

Watchmen, Intercession, and the Lord’s Oath

6 Upon your walls, O Jerusalem, I have stationed sentinels; day and night they shall never fall silent. You who invoke the the LORD, grant yourselves no respite, 7 and give Him no rest until He establishes Jerusalem and makes her the praise of the earth. 8 The the LORD has sworn by His right hand and by His mighty arm: “Never again will I consign your grain to your enemies as food, nor shall foreigners drink the new wine for which you have labored.” 9 But those who reap it shall eat it and praise the the LORD, and those who gather it shall drink it in My sacred courts.

The Highway of Redemption and the Proclaimed Salvation

10 Pass through, pass through the gates; clear the way for the people. Raise up, raise up the highway; clear away the stones; lift high a standard for the peoples. 11 Behold, the the LORD has proclaimed to the ends of the earth: “Say to Daughter Zion, ‘See, your salvation is coming; His reward is with Him, and His recompense precedes Him.’” 12 They shall be called the Holy People, the Redeemed of the the LORD; and you shall be called Sought Out, a City Not Forsaken.
