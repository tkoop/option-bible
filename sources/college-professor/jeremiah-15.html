The LORD Declares Irrevocable Judgment upon Judah

1 The LORD said to me, “Even if Moses and Samuel were to stand before Me, My soul would not be inclined toward this people. Banish them from My presence, and let them depart.” 2 “When they say to you, ‘Where shall we go?’ you shall answer, ‘Thus says the LORD: Those destined for death, to death; those for the sword, to the sword; those for famine, to famine; and those for captivity, to captivity.’” 3 “I will summon four agents of ruin against them,” declares the LORD, “the sword to slay, dogs to drag them away, and the birds of the sky together with the beasts of the earth to consume and to ravage.” 4 “I will make them an object of horror to every kingdom of the earth, because of the atrocities Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, perpetrated in Jerusalem.”

The Desolation of Jerusalem and Its People

5 “For who will pity you, O Jerusalem? Who will grieve for you? Who will turn aside to inquire after your welfare?” 6 “you have forsaken Me,” declares the LORD, “you have turned your back upon Me. Therefore I have stretched out My hand against you and brought you to ruin, and I am weary of relenting.” 7 “I will winnow them with a winnowing fork at the gates of the land; I will bereave them and destroy My people, because they have not turned from their ways.” 8 “Their widows shall outnumber the sand of the seas; I will send a ravager against the mothers of youths at noonday, and I will make anguish and dread descend upon them of a sudden.” 9 “The mother of seven shall pine away and yield up her spirit; her sun shall go down while it is yet daytime; she shall be disgraced and humiliated. As for their remnant, I will give them to the sword before their foes,” declares the LORD.

Jeremiah's Lament and the LORD's Promise of Deliverance

10 “Woe is me, my mother, that you brought me forth—a man of strife and discord to the entire earth! Though I have neither lent nor borrowed, all men revile me.”

11 The LORD said, “Surely I will set you free for your welfare; surely I will cause your enemies to supplicate you in the hour of disaster and in the hour of tribulation. 12 Can iron shatter iron that comes from the north, or bronze? 13 Your substance and your treasures I will surrender to plunder, without price, for all your sins, throughout all your territory. 14 I will cause your enemies to carry you into a land you have not known, for a fire is kindled in My anger; it will burn against you.”

Jeremiah's Prayer and the LORD's Commission

15 “O the LORD, You know; remember me and take note of me, and avenge me upon my persecutors. In Your forbearance do not remove me; know that I suffer reproach for Your sake. 16 Your words were found, and I devoured them; they became my rejoicing and the gladness of my heart, for Your name is called upon me, O the LORD God of Hosts. 17 I did not sit in the council of revelers, nor did I make merry; under the pressure of Your hand I sat solitary, for You had filled me with wrath. 18 Why has my anguish become perpetual, and my wound incurable, declining to be healed? Will You be to me as a deceitful torrent, as waters that run dry?”

19 Therefore thus says the LORD, “If you return, I will restore you, and you shall stand in My presence. If you extract the precious from the worthless, you shall be as My mouth. They shall turn to you, but you shall not turn to them. 20 I will make you to this people a fortified wall of bronze; they will fight against you, but they shall not prevail over you, for I am with you to save you and to deliver you,” declares the LORD. 21 “I will deliver you from the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.”
