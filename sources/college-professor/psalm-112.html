The Blessedness of the God-Fearing Man

1 Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD and finds profound delight in His commandments. 2 His descendants shall be mighty in the land, and the generation of the upright shall be blessed. 3 Wealth and abundance reside in his household, while his righteousness stands in perpetuity. 4 For the upright, light arises amid the darkness—gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

5 It is good for the man who is gracious and lends; he conducts his affairs with justice. 6 For he shall never be shaken, and the righteous shall be remembered forever. 7 He will not fear evil tidings; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.

8 His heart is secure; he will not be afraid until he beholds his adversaries with triumph. 9 He has dispersed his bounty to the poor; his righteousness endures forever; his renown will be exalted in honor.

10 The wicked man will see and be vexed; he will gnash his teeth and waste away; the desire of the wicked will perish.
