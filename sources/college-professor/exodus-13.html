The Consecration of Israel’s Firstborn

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Consecrate to Me every firstborn male. The first issue of every womb among the Israelites is Mine, whether human or beast.”

The Memorial of Deliverance and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

3 Moses said to the people, “Remember this day, the day you came out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, because the the LORD brought you out from there with a mighty hand. No leavened bread shall be eaten. 4 Today, in the month of Aviv, you are leaving. 5 When the the LORD brings you into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—the land He swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey—you shall observe this service in this month:

“6 For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a feast to the the LORD. 7 Unleavened bread shall be eaten throughout the seven days, and no leavened thing shall be seen among you, nor shall leaven be seen anywhere within your borders. 8 On that day you shall tell your son, ‘I do this because of what the the LORD did for me when I came out of Egypt.’ 9 It shall be a sign for you upon your hand and a memorial between your eyes, so that the law of the the LORD may be in your mouth; for with a mighty hand the the LORD brought you out of Egypt. 10 You shall therefore keep this statute at its appointed time from year to year.

The Redemption of Firstborn Sons and Beasts

“11 When the the LORD brings you into the land of the Canaanites and gives it to you, as He swore to you and to your fathers, 12 you shall devote to the the LORD every first issue of the womb, and every firstborn male of your livestock shall belong to the the LORD. 13 Every firstborn donkey you shall redeem with a lamb; if you do not redeem it, you shall break its neck. Every firstborn among your sons you shall redeem. 14 When, in time to come, your son asks you, ‘What does this mean?’ you shall say to him, ‘With a mighty hand the the LORD brought us out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. 15 When Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, the the LORD struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of man to the firstborn of beast. Therefore I sacrifice to the the LORD every male first issue of the womb, but every firstborn of my sons I redeem.’ 16 It shall be a sign upon your hand and as frontlets between your eyes that the the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.”

The Route of the Exodus and the Pillars of Cloud and Fire

17 When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them by the way of the land of the Philistines, though that route was shorter; for God said, “Lest, upon seeing war, the people change their minds and return to Egypt.” 18 So God led the people around by the way of the wilderness toward the Red Sea. The Israelites went up out of Egypt arrayed for battle. 19 Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, for Joseph had made the Israelites swear an oath, saying, “God will surely visit you, and you shall carry up my bones with you from here.” 20 They set out from Succoth and encamped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness. 21 The LORD went before them by day in a pillar of cloud to lead them along the way, and by night in a pillar of fire to give them light, that they might travel by day and by night. 22 The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not depart from before the people.
