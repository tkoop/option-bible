Wisdom’s Call in the Public Square

1 Does not wisdom cry aloud, and understanding raise her voice? 2 At the summit of the heights beside the way, at the crossroads she stands. 3 Beside the gates, at the city’s mouth, at the entrances of the portals she cries out.

Wisdom’s Direct Address and the Excellence of Her Counsel

“4 To you, O men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of men. 5 O simple ones, acquire shrewdness; O fools, gain understanding. 6 Listen, for I speak noble things, and from the opening of my lips comes what is right.

“7 For my mouth utters truth, and wickedness is an abomination to my lips. 8 All the words of my mouth are righteous; there is nothing crooked or perverse in them. 9 They are all straightforward to the discerning, and upright to those who attain knowledge.

“10 Receive my instruction rather than silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold. 11 For wisdom is better than rubies, and nothing one might desire can be set beside her.”

Wisdom’s Authority, Gifts, and Loving Invitation

“12 I, wisdom, make my dwelling with shrewdness, and I attain knowledge of sound designs. 13 The fear of the LORD is to hate evil; pride and arrogance, the evil way and the perverse mouth, I hate. 14 Counsel and sound wisdom are mine; I am understanding, and strength is mine.

“15 By me kings reign, and rulers enact what is just. 16 By me princes govern, and nobles—all who judge in righteousness. 17 I love those who love me, and those who seek me diligently find me.

“18 With me are riches and honor, enduring wealth and righteousness. 19 My fruit is better than gold, yes, than fine gold, and my yield than choice silver. 20 I walk in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of justice. 21 To bestow an inheritance upon those who love me and to fill their treasuries.”

Wisdom’s Primordial Role in Creation

“22 The LORD possessed me at the beginning of His way, before His works of old. 23 I was established from eternity, from the beginning, before the earth existed. 24 When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no springs heavy with water.

“25 Before the mountains were set in place, before the hills, I was brought forth. 26 Before He had made the earth or its fields, or the first dust of the world. 27 When He prepared the heavens, I was there; when He drew a circle upon the face of the deep.

“28 When He made firm the skies above, when He strengthened the fountains of the deep. 29 When He gave the sea its bound, so that the waters should not transgress His command, when He marked out the foundations of the earth.

“30 Then I was beside Him, as a master craftsman, and I was His daily delight, rejoicing before Him always. 31 Rejoicing in His inhabited world, and delighting in the sons of men.”

The Blessing of Heeding Wisdom

“32 Now therefore, O children, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways. 33 Hear instruction and be wise; do not turn away from it. 34 Blessed is the man who listens to me, watching daily at my gates, waiting beside my doorposts.

“35 For whoever finds me finds life and obtains favor from the LORD. 36 But he who sins against me injures his own soul; all who hate me love death.”
