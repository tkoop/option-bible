A Song of Ascents: The LORD, the Protector of Israel

1 I raise my eyes toward the mountains—whence shall my help come? 2 My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

3 He will not suffer your foot to slip; your Protector will not slumber. 4 Behold, the Protector of Israel neither slumbers nor sleeps.

5 The LORD is your Protector; the LORD is your Shade at your right hand. 6 The sun shall not smite you by day, nor the moon by night.

7 The LORD will keep you from all evil; He will preserve your soul. 8 The LORD will guard your going out and your coming in, from this time forth and forevermore.
