Salutation and Apostolic Charge

1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope, 2 to Timothy, my genuine child in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

3 As I urged you while I was going into Macedonia, remain in Ephesus, so that you may command certain persons not to teach a different doctrine, 4 nor to devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies, which foster speculations rather than the stewardship of God that is by faith.

5 The aim of our charge is love that proceeds from a pure heart, a good conscience, and a sincere faith. 6 Certain persons, having strayed from these, have turned aside to vain discussion, 7 desiring to be teachers of the law, though they understand neither what they are saying nor the matters about which they make confident assertions.

The Law’s Proper Function

8 Now we know that the law is good, if one uses it lawfully, 9 knowing this: that the law is not laid down for the righteous, but for the lawless and disobedient, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for patricides and matricides, for murderers, 10 for the sexually immoral, for men who practice homosexuality, for enslavers, for liars, for perjurers, and for whatever else is contrary to sound doctrine, 11 in accordance with the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.

Paul’s Testimony of Mercy

12 I give thanks to Him who has strengthened me, Christ Jesus our Lord, because He judged me faithful by appointing me to His service, 13 though formerly I was a blasphemer, a persecutor, and an insolent opponent. But I received mercy because I had acted ignorantly in unbelief, 14 and the grace of our Lord overflowed for me with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. 15 The saying is trustworthy and deserving of full acceptance: that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost. 16 But I received mercy for this reason, that in me, as the foremost, Christ Jesus might display His perfect patience as an example to those who are to believe in Him for eternal life. 17 To the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.

Entrusted Charge to Timothy

18 This charge I entrust to you, Timothy, my child, in accordance with the prophecies previously made concerning you, that by them you may wage the good warfare, 19 holding faith and a good conscience. By rejecting these, some have made shipwreck of their faith, 20 among whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan that they may learn not to blaspheme.
