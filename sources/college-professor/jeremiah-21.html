Zedekiah’s Envoys and Their Plea

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the the LORD when King Zedekiah sent Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah the priest, son of Maaseiah, to him with this message: 2 “Please inquire of the the LORD on our behalf, for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is waging war against us. Perhaps the the LORD will deal with us according to all His wonders and cause him to withdraw from us.”

The LORD’s Refusal to Deliver Jerusalem from Babylon

3 Jeremiah said to them, “You shall say to Zedekiah, ‘4 Thus says the the LORD, the God of Israel: Behold, I am turning back the weapons of war that are in your hand—the very weapons with which you fight against the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who besiege you outside the wall—and I will gather them into the midst of this city. 5 I Myself will fight against you with manifest power and irresistible might, in anger, in fury, and in great wrath. 6 I will strike down the inhabitants of this city, both people and animals, and they will die of a great pestilence. 7 Afterward, declares the the LORD, I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, the people, and those who survive in this city from the pestilence, the sword, and the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their lives. He will put them to the sword without pity, without compassion, and without mercy.’”

Life and Death Set Before the People

8 And to this people you shall say, “Thus says the the LORD: Behold, I set before you the way of life and the way of death. 9 Whoever remains in this city will die by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence; but whoever goes out and surrenders to the Chaldeans who besiege you will live, and his life will be his prize. 10 For I have set Myself against this city for disaster and not for prosperity, declares the the LORD; it will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.”

A Summons to the House of David and the Inhabitants of the Valley

11 And to the house of the king of Judah: Hear the word of the the LORD. 12 “O house of David, thus says the the LORD: Render just judgment each morning, and rescue the plundered from the hand of the oppressor, lest My wrath go forth like fire and burn with none to quench it, because of the evil of your deeds. 13 Behold, I am against you, O inhabitant of the valley, O rocky height of the plain, declares the the LORD—those who say, ‘Who shall come down against us, or who shall enter our dwellings?’ 14 I will punish you according to the consequences of your deeds, declares the the LORD; I will kindle a fire in her forest, and it will consume all that surrounds her.”
