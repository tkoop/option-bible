The Advent of Christ and the Gathering of the Saints

1 Now, regarding the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering unto Him, we entreat you, brothers; 2 do not be precipitously unsettled in mind, nor be terrified, whether by spirit, or by discourse, or by epistle as if from us, to the effect that the day of the Lord has already arrived.

The Apostasy and the Man of Lawlessness

3 Let no one beguile you in any manner, for that day shall not arrive unless the apostasy come first and the man of lawlessness be disclosed, the son of perdition, 4 who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of veneration, so that he seats himself in the temple of God, exhibiting himself as though he were God.

5 Do you not recollect that, while I was yet with you, I was declaring these things to you?

The Restraining and Revelation of the Lawless One

6 And you know what now restrains him, so that he may be disclosed in his appointed season. 7 For the mystery of lawlessness is already operative; only the one who now restrains will continue to do so until he is removed from the midst.

8 And then the lawless one will be disclosed, whom the Lord Jesus will slay with the breath of His mouth and annihilate by the epiphany of His coming— 9 the coming whose operation is according to Satan, with all power and spurious signs and portents, 10 and with every deceit of unrighteousness among those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so as to be saved.

11 Therefore God sends them an operative delusion, that they should believe the lie, 12 in order that all may be judged who did not believe the truth but took pleasure in unrighteousness.

Thanksgiving, Exhortation, and Benediction

13 But we are bound always to give thanks to God for you, brothers beloved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning unto salvation through sanctification by the Spirit and belief in the truth, 14 unto which He also called you through our gospel, for the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

15 So then, brothers, stand firm and hold fast the traditions which you were taught, whether by word or by our epistle.

16 Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave us everlasting consolation and good hope through grace, 17 comfort your hearts and establish them in every good word and work.
