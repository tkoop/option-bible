The Prophet's Commission to Love Again

1 The LORD said to me, "Go, love a woman who is still the beloved of a paramour and is an adulteress, even as the LORD loves the sons of Israel, though they turn toward other gods and are enamored of sacred raisin cakes."

2 So I acquired her for myself for fifteen shekels of silver, together with a homer and a lethech of barley.

The Terms of Restored Fidelity

3 And I said to her, "Remain with me for many days; you shall not be promiscuous, nor shall you belong to any man; and I, too, shall be yours."

Israel's Exile and Eschatological Return

4 For the sons of Israel shall dwell for many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred pillar, and without ephod or household idols. 5 Afterward the sons of Israel shall return and seek the LORD their God and David their king; and they shall turn toward the LORD and toward His goodness with reverent dread in the latter days.
