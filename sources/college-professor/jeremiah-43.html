The Remnant Rejects Jeremiah's Warning

1 When Jeremiah had finished speaking to all the people all the words of the LORD their God, which the LORD their God had sent him to them—all these words.

2 Then Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the insolent men said to Jeremiah, "You are speaking falsely; the LORD our God did not send you to say, 'Do not go to Egypt to sojourn there.' 3 Rather, Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, so that he may hand us over to the Chaldeans to be put to death and exiled to Babylon."

4 Yet Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces, together with all the people, refused to obey the voice of the LORD and to remain in the land of Judah.

The Remnant's Flight to Egypt

5 Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces took all the remnant of Judah—those who had returned from all the nations to which they had been driven, in order to sojourn in the land of Judah— 6 the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, every person whom Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, along with Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah. 7 They entered the land of Egypt, because they had not obeyed the voice of the LORD, and they went as far as Tahpanhes.

The LORD's Prophetic Sign at Tahpanhes

8 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying, 9 "Take large stones in your hand and hide them in mortar in the brickwork that is at the entrance of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah, 10 and say to them, 'Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I am about to send for and take Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he will spread his royal pavilion over them. 11 He shall come and strike the land of Egypt: those destined for death to death, those for captivity to captivity, and those for the sword to the sword. 12 I will kindle fire in the temples of the gods of Egypt, and he shall burn them and carry them away captive; he shall envelop the land of Egypt as a shepherd wraps himself in his mantle, and he shall go forth from there in peace. 13 He shall also break the pillars of Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt, and the temples of the gods of Egypt he shall burn with fire.'"
