Israel Seeks the LORD's Direction After Joshua's Death

1 After Joshua's death, the Israelites sought direction from the LORD, saying, "Who shall first ascend on our behalf against the Canaanites to wage war against them?" 2 The LORD replied, "Judah shall go up; behold, I have consigned the land into his hand."

Judah and Simeon's Southern Campaign

3 Judah said to Simeon his brother, "Ascend with me into my allotted inheritance, and let us make war upon the Canaanites; I, in turn, will accompany you into your allotted inheritance." So Simeon went with him.

4 Judah ascended, and the LORD delivered the Canaanite and the Perizzite into their hand; they struck down ten thousand men at Bezek. 5 They found Adoni-bezek at Bezek, fought against him, and struck the Canaanite and the Perizzite. 6 Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued and apprehended him, and amputated the thumbs of his hands and his great toes. 7 Adoni-bezek said, "Seventy kings, with the thumbs of their hands and their great toes amputated, used to scavenge scraps beneath my table. As I have done, so God has requited me." Then they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

Judah's Further Victories and Caleb's Portion

8 The sons of Judah fought against Jerusalem, captured it, struck it with the edge of the sword, and consigned the city to fire. 9 Afterward the sons of Judah descended to fight against the Canaanites dwelling in the hill country, the Negev, and the Shephelah. 10 Judah went against the Canaanites dwelling in Hebron (formerly Kiriath-arba) and struck Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. 11 From there he went against the inhabitants of Debir (formerly Kiriath-sepher).

12 Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as wife." 13 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it; and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as wife.

14 When she arrived, she incited him to ask her father for a field; and as she dismounted from the donkey, Caleb said to her, "What is your request?"

15 She said to him, "Grant me a favor: since you have given me land in the Negev, give me also springs of water." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

16 The descendants of Moses' father-in-law, the Kenite, ascended with the sons of Judah from the City of Palms into the wilderness of Judah, which lies in the Negev of Arad; and they went and dwelt among the people.

17 Judah went with Simeon his brother; they struck the Canaanites inhabiting Zephath and devoted it to destruction, calling the city Hormah. 18 Judah captured Gaza and its territory, Ashkelon and its territory, and Ekron and its territory. 19 The LORD was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country; yet he could not dispossess the inhabitants of the plain, because they possessed iron chariots. 20 They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had spoken, and he drove out from there the three sons of Anak.

The Incomplete Conquest of Canaan

21 The sons of Benjamin did not dispossess the Jebusites inhabiting Jerusalem; so the Jebusites dwell with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day. 22 The house of Joseph also ascended against Bethel, and the LORD was with them. 23 The house of Joseph spied out Bethel (formerly called Luz). 24 The spies saw a man emerging from the city and said to him, "Show us the entrance to the city, and we will deal kindly with you." 25 He showed them the entrance, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword; yet they spared the man and all his family. 26 The man went to the land of the Hittites, built a city, and named it Luz; that remains its name to this day.

27 Manasseh did not dispossess Beth-shean and its towns, Taanach and its towns, Dor and its towns, Ibleam and its towns, or Megiddo and its towns; the Canaanites persisted in dwelling in that land. 28 When Israel grew strong, they subjected the Canaanites to forced labor, but they did not utterly dispossess them. 29 Ephraim did not dispossess the Canaanites dwelling in Gezer; the Canaanites dwelt among them in Gezer. 30 Zebulun did not dispossess the inhabitants of Kitron and Nahalol; the Canaanites dwelt among them and became forced labor.

31 Asher did not dispossess the inhabitants of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob. 32 The Asherites dwelt among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, for they did not dispossess them. 33 Naphtali did not dispossess the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath; Naphtali dwelt among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, and the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath became forced labor for them. 34 The Amorites confined the Danites to the hill country and would not permit them to descend into the plain. 35 The Amorites persisted in dwelling in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim; yet the house of Joseph prevailed, and they became forced labor. 36 The border of the Amorites ran from the ascent of Akrabbim, from the rock, and upward.
