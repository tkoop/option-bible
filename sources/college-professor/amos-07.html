The Vision of the Locust Swarm

1 Thus the Lord the LORD showed me: behold, He was fashioning locusts at the commencement of the latter growth's springing up; and behold, it was the latter growth after the king's mowings. 2 When they had finished devouring the grass of the land, I said, "O Lord the LORD, forgive, I beseech You! How can Jacob endure? For he is small." 3 The LORD relented concerning this: "It shall not be," said the LORD.

4 Thus the Lord the LORD showed me: behold, the Lord the LORD was calling for judgment by fire, and it consumed the great deep and devoured the allotted portion. 5 And I said, "O Lord the LORD, desist, I beseech You! How can Jacob endure? For he is small." 6 The LORD relented concerning this: "This also shall not be," said the Lord the LORD.

The Vision of the Plumb Line

7 Thus He showed me, and behold, the Lord was standing by a wall constructed with a plumb line, with a plumb line in His hand. 8 And the the LORD said to me, "Amos, what do you see?"

And I said, "A plumb line."

Then the Lord said, "Behold, I am setting a plumb line in the midst of My people Israel; I will not pass by him again. 9 The high places of Isaac shall be desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

Amaziah's Confrontation with Amos

10 Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land cannot endure all his words. 11 For thus Amos says, 'Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall surely go into exile from its land.'"

12 And Amaziah said to Amos, "O seer, go, flee to the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and prophesy there. 13 But never again prophesy at Bethel, for it is the king's sanctuary and a royal residence."

14 Then Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I was not a prophet, nor was I a prophet's son; but I was a herdsman and a tender of sycamore figs. 15 And the the LORD took me from following the flock, and the the LORD said to me, 'Go, prophesy to My people Israel.'"

16 Now therefore hear the word of the the LORD: You say, "Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not speak against the house of Isaac." 17 Therefore thus says the the LORD: 'Your wife shall play the harlot in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be divided by a measuring line, and you shall die in an unclean land; and Israel shall surely go into exile from its land.'"
