Joseph Brings His Sons to the Dying Patriarch

1After these occurrences, intelligence reached Joseph: “Behold, your father is ailing.” Thereupon he took with him his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim.

2When Jacob was informed, “Behold, your son Joseph has arrived,” Israel rallied his strength and sat upright upon the bed.

Jacob Recounts the Divine Promise and Adopts Joseph's Sons

3Jacob said to Joseph, “God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan and blessed me. 4He said to me, ‘Behold, I am rendering you fruitful and will multiply you; I will constitute you a company of peoples, and I will give this land to your offspring after you as an everlasting possession.’

5“And now your two sons, born to you in the land of Egypt before I came to you in Egypt, are mine; Ephraim and Manasseh shall be mine, as Reuben and Simeon are. 6The offspring you beget after them shall be yours; they shall bear the names of their brothers in their inheritance. 7As for me, upon my return from Paddan, Rachel died on the journey in the land of Canaan, while we were still some distance from Ephrath; and I interred her there on the road to Ephrath—that is, Bethlehem.”

Joseph Presents His Sons for Blessing

8Israel beheld Joseph's sons and asked, “Who are these?”

9Joseph replied to his father, “They are my sons, whom God has granted me here.”

Israel said, “Bring them to me, I pray, that I may bless them.”

10Israel's eyes had grown dim with age, so that he could not see; Joseph therefore brought them near, and he kissed them and embraced them.

11Israel said to Joseph, “I never anticipated seeing your face again, yet behold, God has even permitted me to see your offspring.”

12Joseph removed them from his knees and prostrated himself with his face to the earth.

The Crossing of Hands and the Prophetic Blessing

13Joseph took both of them—Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel's left, and Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel's right—and brought them near to him.

14Israel extended his right hand and placed it upon Ephraim's head, though he was the younger, and his left hand upon Manasseh's head, crossing his hands deliberately, because Manasseh was the firstborn.

15He blessed Joseph, saying, “The God before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac walked, the God who has been my shepherd all my life to this day, 16the Angel who has redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be invoked upon them, and the names of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them teem abundantly in the midst of the earth.”

17When Joseph perceived that his father had placed his right hand on Ephraim's head, it displeased him; so he seized his father's hand to remove it from Ephraim's head onto Manasseh's head.

18Joseph said to his father, “Not so, my father, for this one is the firstborn; place your right hand upon his head.”

19His father declined and said, “I know, my son, I know. He too shall become a people, and he too shall be great; nevertheless, his younger brother shall surpass him, and his descendants shall become a multitude of nations.”

20He blessed them that day, saying, “By you Israel will bless, saying, ‘May God make you like Ephraim and like Manasseh.’” And he placed Ephraim before Manasseh.

Israel's Farewell Promise and Bequest

21Israel said to Joseph, “Behold, I am dying, but God will be with you and restore you to the land of your fathers. 22Moreover, I bequeath to you one portion more than your brothers, which I wrested from the hand of the Amorite with my sword and my bow.”
