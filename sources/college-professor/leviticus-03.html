Peace Offerings from the Herd

1 If a person’s offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, let him present it from the herd, whether male or female, unblemished before the LORD. 2 He shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting; then Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall throw the blood around against the altar. 3 From the sacrifice of peace offerings he shall present, as an offering by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails. 4 He shall also remove the two kidneys with the fat upon them, which is on the loins, together with the lobe of the liver beside the kidneys. 5 Aaron’s sons shall burn it on the altar upon the burnt offering that is on the wood over the fire, as an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

Peace Offerings from the Flock

6 If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD is from the flock, he shall present it, male or female, unblemished. 7 If he presents a lamb as his offering, he shall bring it before the LORD. 8 He shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; then Aaron’s sons shall throw its blood around against the altar. 9 From the sacrifice of peace offerings he shall present, as an offering by fire to the LORD, its fat—the entire fat tail removed close to the backbone—along with the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails. 10 He shall also remove the two kidneys with the fat upon them, which is on the loins, together with the lobe of the liver beside the kidneys. 11 The priest shall burn it on the altar as food, an offering by fire to the LORD.

Peace Offerings from Goats

12 If his offering is a goat, he shall present it before the LORD. 13 He shall lay his hand upon its head and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; then Aaron’s sons shall throw its blood around against the altar. 14 From it he shall present his offering, as an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails. 15 He shall also remove the two kidneys with the fat upon them, which is on the loins, together with the lobe of the liver beside the kidneys. 16 The priest shall burn them on the altar as bread, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma; all the fat belongs to the LORD.

A Perpetual Statute Regarding Fat and Blood

17 It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations, in all your dwellings: you shall eat neither fat nor blood.
