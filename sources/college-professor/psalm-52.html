A Maskil of David on the Folly of Malignant Boasting

1 For the choirmaster: a didactic psalm of David, occasioned by Doeg the Edomite’s arrival before Saul, wherein he reported, “David has come to the house of Ahimelech.” Why do you exult in wickedness, O mighty man? The steadfast love of God endures throughout the day.

2 Your tongue contrives devastation, sharpened like a razor, you architect of treachery. 3 You cleave to evil in preference to good, and to falsehood in preference to righteous discourse. Selah. 4 You delight in every devouring utterance, O tongue of guile.

5 Yet God will demolish you forever; He will seize you and rend you from your tent; He will extirpate you from the land of the living. Selah.

The Righteous Witness the Downfall of the Arrogant

6 The righteous shall behold and be seized with reverent dread, and they shall mock him: 7 “Behold the man who did not make God his stronghold, but reposed his confidence in the abundance of his riches and fortified himself in his iniquity.”

8 As for me, I am like a verdant olive tree in the house of God; I have reposed my trust in the steadfast love of God forever and ever. 9 I will give You thanks in perpetuity, because You have brought it to pass; I will wait upon Your name, for it is good, in the presence of Your saints.
