Daniel Discerns the Seventy Years

1 In the inaugural year of Darius, son of Ahasuerus, of Median lineage, who had been enthroned over the Chaldean realm, 2 in the opening year of his reign, I, Daniel, discerned from the sacred writings the tally of years concerning which the word of the LORD had come to Jeremiah the prophet: that Jerusalem's desolations were to fulfill seventy years.

Daniel's Penitential Prayer

3 I turned my countenance toward the Lord God, earnestly seeking Him through prayer and supplication, accompanied by fasting, sackcloth, and ashes.

4 I prayed to the LORD my God and confessed, saying, "O Lord, the great and awe-inspiring God, who keeps covenant and steadfast love with those who love Him and keep His commandments, 5 we have sinned, acted perversely, wrought wickedness, and rebelled; indeed, we have turned aside from Your precepts and Your judgments. 6 Nor have we heeded Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

7 "O Lord, righteousness belongs to You, but to us belongs open shame, as it is this day—to the men of Judah, to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, near and far, throughout all the lands to which You have driven them, because of the treachery by which they have acted treacherously against You. 8 O Lord, open shame belongs to us—to our kings, our princes, and our fathers—because we have sinned against You. 9 To the Lord our God belong mercies and forgiveness, though we have rebelled against Him.

10 "Nor have we obeyed the voice of the LORD our God, to walk in His laws, which He set before us by His servants the prophets. 11 Indeed, all Israel has transgressed Your law and turned aside, refusing to obey Your voice; therefore the curse and the oath written in the Law of Moses, the servant of God, have been poured out upon us, because we have sinned against Him. 12 And He has confirmed His words, which He spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, by bringing upon us a great calamity; for under the whole heaven nothing has been done like what has been done to Jerusalem.

13 "As it is written in the Law of Moses, all this calamity has come upon us; yet we have not entreated the favor of the LORD our God, that we might turn from our iniquities and gain insight into Your truth. 14 The LORD has kept watch over the calamity and brought it upon us, for the LORD our God is righteous in all His works that He has done, and we have not obeyed His voice.

15 "And now, O Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand and made for Yourself a name, as it is this day—we have sinned, we have acted wickedly. 16 O Lord, according to all Your righteousness, let Your anger and Your wrath turn away from Your city Jerusalem, Your holy mountain; for because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people have become a reproach to all around us.

17 "And now, our God, hear the prayer of Your servant and his supplications, and for the Lord's sake let Your face shine upon Your desolate sanctuary. 18 O my God, incline Your ear and hear; open Your eyes and behold our desolations and the city that is called by Your name; for we do not present our supplications before You because of our righteous deeds, but because of Your great mercies. 19 O Lord, hear; O Lord, forgive; O Lord, pay heed and act; do not delay, for Your own sake, O my God, for Your city and Your people are called by Your name."

Gabriel's Arrival and the Seventy Weeks

20 While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my supplication before the LORD my God concerning the holy mountain of my God, 21 indeed, while I was still speaking in prayer, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the earlier vision, came to me in swift flight at the time of the evening offering.

22 And he instructed me and spoke with me, saying, "O Daniel, I have now come forth to impart to you insight and understanding.

23 "At the beginning of your supplications a word went forth, and I have come to declare it, for you are greatly beloved; therefore consider the word and understand the vision.

24 "Seventy weeks are decreed concerning your people and your holy city: to finish transgression, to put an end to sin, to atone for iniquity, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophecy, and to anoint the Most Holy Place.

25 "Know, therefore, and understand: from the issuing of the decree to restore and rebuild Jerusalem until the Anointed One, the Prince, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; the plaza and the moat will be rebuilt, though in times of distress. 26 And after the sixty-two weeks, the Anointed One will be cut off and have nothing; and the people of the coming prince will destroy the city and the sanctuary; its end will come like a flood, and until the end there will be war; desolations are decreed. 27 He will enforce a covenant with many for one week; in the middle of that week he will bring sacrifice and offering to an end; and upon the wing of abominations will come a desolator, until the decreed consummation is poured out upon the desolate."
