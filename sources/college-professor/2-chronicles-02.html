Solomon’s Resolve to Build the Temple and Royal Palace

1 Solomon resolved to build a house for the name of the LORD and a royal palace for his dominion. 2 Solomon assigned seventy thousand men to bear burdens, eighty thousand to hew stone in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred to serve as overseers superintending them.

Solomon’s Appeal to Hiram of Tyre

3 Solomon sent to Hiram, king of Tyre, with this message: “You once dealt kindly with my father David and sent him cedars for the house in which he was to dwell. 4 Behold, I am building a house for the name of the LORD my God, to consecrate it to Him, to burn fragrant incense before Him, to keep the bread of the Presence in perpetual array, and to offer burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths, at the new moons, and at the appointed feasts of the LORD our God. This is an ordinance for Israel forever. 5 The house that I am building is great, for our God is greater than all gods. 6 Yet who is able to build Him a house, when heaven itself, even the highest heaven, cannot contain Him? Who am I, then, that I should build Him a house, except to burn incense before Him? 7 Send me, therefore, a man skillful in working gold, silver, bronze, iron, purple, crimson, and blue, who knows how to execute engravings, to work alongside the skilled artisans who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David provided. 8 Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut the timber of Lebanon; and behold, my servants will be with your servants, 9 to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house I am building is to be great and wondrous. 10 And behold, I will give to your servants, the woodcutters who fell the timber, twenty thousand cors of ground wheat, twenty thousand cors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil.”

Hiram’s Reply and the Dispatch of Huram-abi

11 Hiram, king of Tyre, replied in writing and sent his answer to Solomon: “Because the LORD loves His people, He has made you king over them. 12 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given King David a wise son, endowed with discretion and understanding, to build a house for the LORD and a royal palace for himself. 13 And now I have sent you Huram-abi, a skillful man endowed with understanding, 14 the son of a woman of the daughters of Dan, whose father was a Tyrian, skilled to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, timber, purple, blue, fine linen, and crimson, and to engrave any engraving, and to devise any design that may be assigned to him, together with your wise men and the wise men of my lord David your father. 15 Now therefore the wheat, the barley, the oil, and the wine of which my lord has spoken, let him send to his servants. 16 And we will cut wood from Lebanon according to all your need, and bring it to you in rafts by sea to Joppa, and you shall carry it up to Jerusalem.”

The Census and Organization of the Labor Force

17 Solomon numbered all the resident aliens who were in the land of Israel, after the census that David his father had taken of them, and there were found to be one hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred. 18 He made seventy thousand of them bearers of burdens, eighty thousand hewers in the mountain, and three thousand six hundred overseers, to set the people to work.
