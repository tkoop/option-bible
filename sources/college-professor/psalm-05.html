To the choirmaster, with flutes: a psalm of David.

A Morning Supplication for Righteous Guidance and Deliverance from the Wicked

1 Listen to my words, the LORD; consider my inward meditation. 2 Attend to the voice of my cry, my King and my God, for to You I pray. 3 In the morning, the LORD, You hear my voice; at dawn I lay my plea before You and wait in expectation.

4 For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; no evil can dwell with You. 5 The boastful cannot stand in Your presence; You hate all workers of iniquity. 6 You destroy those who speak lies; the LORD abhors the bloodthirsty and the deceitful.

7 But as for me, by Your great loving devotion I will enter Your house; in reverence for You I will bow down toward Your holy temple. 8 Lead me, the LORD, in Your righteousness because of my enemies; make Your way straight before me.

9 For there is no truth in their mouths; their inward parts are bent on destruction; their throats are open graves; they flatter with their tongues. 10 Declare them guilty, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out for their many transgressions, for they have rebelled against You.

11 But let all who take refuge in You rejoice; let them ever shout for joy, because You shelter them; and let those who love Your name exult in You. 12 For You, the LORD, bless the righteous; You surround him with favor as with a shield.
