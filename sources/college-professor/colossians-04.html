Exhortations on Masterly Obligation, Vigilant Prayer, and Prudent Engagement with Outsiders

1 Masters, furnish your slaves with what is just and equitable, recognizing that you too have a Master in heaven. 2 Devote yourselves steadfastly to prayer, remaining watchful therein with thanksgiving, 3 while concurrently interceding for us, that God may open to us a door for the word, so that we may proclaim the mystery of Christ, on account of which I am in chains. 4 And pray that I may disclose it with the clarity incumbent upon me.

5 Conduct yourselves with wisdom toward outsiders, making the most of the occasion. 6 Let your speech be invariably gracious and aptly seasoned, so that you may discern how you ought to respond to each person.

Greetings and Commendations from the Apostle’s Collaborators

7 Tychicus will apprise you of all my circumstances; he is the beloved brother, a faithful minister, and a fellow servant in the Lord. 8 I have dispatched him to you for this very end, that you may learn of our circumstances and that he may console your hearts. 9 He is accompanied by Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will disclose to you everything here.

10 Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, as does Mark, the cousin of Barnabas. (You have received instructions concerning him; if he comes to you, receive him.) 11 And Jesus, who is called Justus. These alone, being of the circumcision, are my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a solace to me.

12 Epaphras, who is one of you and a servant of Christ Jesus, greets you. He is always wrestling in prayer on your behalf, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God. 13 For I bear witness to him that he exerts himself greatly on your behalf, and on behalf of those in Laodicea and Hierapolis. 14 Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas greet you.

Final Injunctions, Ecclesial Circulation of the Epistle, and Apostolic Benediction

15 Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and Nympha, and the church in her house. 16 And when this letter has been read among you, arrange for it to be read also in the church of the Laodiceans, and see that you yourselves read the one from Laodicea.

17 And tell Archippus, “See that you fulfill the ministry you have received in the Lord.”

18 The greeting is in my own hand—Paul. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
