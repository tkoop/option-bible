The Divine Reaffirmation of Deliverance

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, “Now you shall witness what I will do to Pharaoh; for by My strong hand he will release them, and by My strong hand he will expel them from his realm.”

2 God further addressed Moses, saying, “I am the LORD. 3 I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty, but by My name the LORD I did not make Myself known to them. 4 I also established My covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan—the land in which they sojourned. 5 Moreover, I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians are subjecting to servitude, and I have remembered My covenant. 6 Therefore declare to the Israelites: ‘I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from beneath Egypt's burdens, and I will deliver you from their servitude. I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with formidable judgments. 7 I will take you to Myself as a people, and I will be your God; then you shall know that I am the LORD your God, who brings you out from beneath Egypt's burdens. 8 I will bring you into the land that I swore, with an uplifted hand, to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and I will give it to you as a heritage. I am the LORD.’”

9 Moses spoke thus to the Israelites, but they did not heed him, because of their despondency and oppressive toil.

10 Then the LORD said to Moses, 11 “Go and tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to release the Israelites from his land.”

12 But Moses replied to the LORD, “If the Israelites have not heeded me, how then will Pharaoh heed me, since I am not an adept orator?”

13 Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, and He gave them a charge concerning the Israelites and Pharaoh, king of Egypt: to bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.

The Ancestry of Moses and Aaron

14 These are the heads of their ancestral houses: the sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn son—Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi—these are the clans of Reuben. 15 The sons of Simeon are Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman; these are the clans of Simeon.

16 These are the names of the sons of Levi by their lineages: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari; Levi lived one hundred and thirty-seven years. 17 The sons of Gershon are Libni and Shimei, by their clans. 18 The sons of Kohath are Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel; Kohath lived one hundred and thirty-three years. 19 The sons of Merari are Mahli and Mushi; these are the clans of the Levites by their lineages. 20 Amram took as his wife his father's sister Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses; Amram lived one hundred and thirty-seven years. 21 The sons of Izhar are Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri. 22 The sons of Uzziel are Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

23 Aaron took as his wife Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 24 The sons of Korah are Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the clans of the Korahites. 25 Aaron's son Eleazar took as his wife one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas; these are the heads of the Levite fathers' houses by their clans.

26 It was this Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, “Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt by their divisions.” 27 They are the ones who spoke to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to bring the Israelites out of Egypt—this Moses and Aaron.

The Renewed Command to Confront Pharaoh

28 Now on the day the LORD spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt, 29 the LORD said to Moses, “I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, everything I am telling you.”

30 But Moses said to the LORD, “Since I am not an adept orator, how will Pharaoh listen to me?”
