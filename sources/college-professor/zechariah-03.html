A Vision of Joshua the High Priest before the Angel of the LORD

1 Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the LORD, with Satan stationed at his right hand to indict him. 2 The LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan! Indeed, the LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you. Is this not a brand snatched from the fire?”

The Soiled Garments and the Cleansing of the Priest

3 Now Joshua was arrayed in soiled garments while standing before the Angel.

4 So He answered and said to those standing before Him, “Remove the soiled garments from him.” Then He said to him, “See, I have taken away your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you in sumptuous apparel.”

5 Then I said, “Let them place a pure turban upon his head.” So they placed a pure turban upon his head and clothed him, while the Angel of the LORD stood by.

The Oracle of the LORD of Hosts Concerning Joshua and the Branch

6 Then the Angel of the LORD solemnly charged Joshua: 7 “Thus says the LORD of Hosts: ‘If you walk in My ways and keep My charge, then you shall administer My house and keep watch over My courts, and I will grant you a place among these who stand here. 8 Hear now, O Joshua the high priest—you and your companions who sit before you—for they are men who serve as signs; behold, I am bringing My Servant, the Branch. 9 For behold, the stone that I have set before Joshua—upon that single stone are seven eyes. Behold, I will engrave its inscription,’ declares the LORD of Hosts, ‘and I will remove the iniquity of that land in a single day.’ 10 ‘In that day,’ declares the LORD of Hosts, ‘you will invite one another beneath the vine and beneath the fig tree.’”
