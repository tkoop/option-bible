The Heavenly Council and Satan's Renewed Challenge

1 Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan also came among them to present himself before the LORD. 2 The LORD said to Satan, "From where do you come?"

Satan answered the LORD and said, "From roaming the earth and walking about in it." 3 The LORD said to Satan, "Have you considered My servant Job? For there is no one like him on earth—a man blameless and upright, who fears God and shuns evil. He still holds fast his integrity, though you have incited Me against him to destroy him without cause."

4 Satan answered the LORD, "A man will relinquish all his possessions to preserve his own life. But stretch out Your hand and strike his bone and his flesh, and he will surely curse You to Your face."

6 The LORD said to Satan, "Very well, he is in your hand; only spare his life."

Job's Affliction and His Wife's Counsel

7 So Satan went out from the presence of the LORD and afflicted Job with grievous boils from the sole of his foot to the crown of his head. 8 He took a potsherd with which to scrape himself while he sat among the ashes. 9 Then his wife said to him, "Do you still hold fast your integrity? Curse God and die!"

10 He said to her, "You speak as one of the witless women speaks. Shall we receive good from God and not receive adversity?" In all this Job did not sin with his lips.

The Arrival and Mourning of Job's Friends

11 Now when Job's three friends heard of all the calamity that had befallen him, each came from his own place—Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They arranged to meet together to go and mourn with him and to comfort him. 12 When they lifted up their eyes from afar, they did not recognize him; they raised their voices and wept, and each tore his robe and cast dust heavenward upon his head. 13 They sat with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no one spoke a word to him, for they saw that his suffering was exceedingly great.
