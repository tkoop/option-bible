The Women at the Tomb and the Angelic Annunciation

1 Once the Sabbath had elapsed, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome procured aromatic spices, intending to come and anoint Him. 2 Very early on the first day of the week, at sunrise, they came to the tomb.

3 And they were saying among themselves, “Who will roll the stone away for us from the entrance of the tomb?” 4 And looking up, they observed that the stone had been rolled away—for it was exceedingly large.

5 And entering the tomb, they saw a young man enveloped in a white robe, seated on the right side, and they were overwhelmed with astonishment. 6 And he said to them, “Do not be alarmed; you seek Jesus the Nazarene, the Crucified One. He has been raised; He is not here. Behold the place where they laid Him. 7 But go, report to His disciples and to Peter that He goes before you into Galilee; there you will see Him, just as He said to you.”

8 And they went out and fled from the tomb, for trembling and bewilderment had seized them; and they said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid.

Post-Resurrection Appearances of the Lord

9 Now after He had risen, early on the first day of the week, He appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom He had expelled seven demons. 10 She went and announced it to those who had been with Him, who were mourning and weeping. 11 And when they heard that He was alive and had been seen by her, they refused to believe.

12 Afterward He appeared in another form to two of them as they were walking on their way into the country. 13 And they went and reported it to the rest, but they did not believe them either. 14 Later He appeared to the eleven as they reclined at table, and He reproached them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they had not believed those who had seen Him after He had risen.

The Commissioning, Ascension, and Apostolic Proclamation

15 And He said to them, “Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to all creation. 16 Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. 17 And these signs will accompany those who believe: in My name they will cast out demons; they will speak with new tongues. 18 They will take up serpents with their hands; and if they drink anything deadly, it will not harm them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover.”

19 So then, after the Lord had spoken to them, He was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God. 20 And they went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed.
