The Ziphites Report David's Concealment

1 The Ziphites repaired to Saul at Gibeah, reporting, “Is not David concealed upon the height of Hakilah, opposite the wasteland?” 2 Saul arose, and with him three thousand select men of Israel, and descended into the wilderness of Ziph to seek David. 3 Saul encamped beside the road upon the hill of Hakilah, facing the wilderness, while David remained in the wilderness and perceived that Saul had come in pursuit of him there. 4 David dispatched scouts and ascertained with certainty that Saul had indeed arrived.

David and Abishai Enter Saul's Camp

5 David arose and advanced to the place where Saul was encamped; he observed the site where Saul and Abner son of Ner, commander of his army, were reclining—Saul lying within the encampment's circle, with the troops bivouacked around him. 6 David addressed Ahimelech the Hittite and Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, saying, “Who will descend with me into Saul's camp?”

Abishai answered, “I will accompany you.”

7 That night David and Abishai penetrated the people's lines and found Saul sleeping within the encampment, his spear thrust into the ground at his head, while Abner and the troops lay round about him.

8 Abishai said to David, “Today God has surrendered your adversary into your grasp; permit me, I pray, to transfix him to the earth with one thrust of the spear, and I shall not need to strike again.”

9 David replied, “Do not annihilate him; for who may lay hand upon the LORD's anointed and remain innocent?” 10 He added, “As the LORD lives, either the LORD will smite him, or his appointed day will arrive and he will die, or he will descend into battle and be consumed.” 11 “Far be it from me, by the LORD, to stretch forth my hand against the LORD's anointed; but now take the spear beside his head and the water jar, and let us depart.”

12 David seized the spear and the water jar from beside Saul's head, and they withdrew; no one saw, knew, or awoke, for all were asleep, since a profound slumber from the LORD had fallen upon them.

David Confronts Abner from Afar

13 David crossed to the opposite side and took his stand upon the summit of the hill at a distance, with a great interval between them.

14 David called out to the people and to Abner son of Ner, saying, “Will you not answer, Abner?”

Abner answered, “Who are you that you call to the king?”

15 David said to Abner, “Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not guarded your lord the king? For one of the people came to destroy your lord the king. This thing that you have done is not good.” 16 “As the LORD lives, you are men doomed to die, because you have not guarded your lord, the LORD's anointed. And now behold the king's spear and the water jar that was at his head.”

Saul Confesses and David Departs

17 Saul recognized David's voice and said, “Is this your voice, my son David?”

David answered, “It is my voice, my lord the king.” 18 “Why does my lord pursue his servant? For what have I done, and what evil is in my hand? 19 Now therefore, please, my lord the king, hear the words of his servant. If the LORD has incited you against me, let Him accept an offering; but if it be the sons of men, cursed are they before the LORD, for they have driven me out this day from sharing in the inheritance of the LORD, saying, ‘Go, serve other gods.’ 20 Now therefore, let not my blood fall to the earth away from the presence of the LORD; for the king of Israel has come out to seek a flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains.”

21 Saul said, “I have sinned; return, my son David, for I will no more do you harm, because my life was precious in your eyes this day. Behold, I have played the fool and erred exceedingly.”

22 David answered, “Here is the king's spear; let one of the young men come over and take it.” 23 “The LORD will render to every man his righteousness and his faithfulness; for the LORD delivered you into my hand today, but I would not put forth my hand against the LORD's anointed.” 24 “And behold, as your life was precious this day in my eyes, so may my life be precious in the eyes of the LORD, and may He deliver me out of all tribulation.”

25 Saul said to David, “Blessed are you, my son David; you will surely accomplish great things and indeed prevail.” Then David went his way, and Saul returned to his place.
