The Sovereign Governance of Human Affairs

1 The heart of a sovereign is like a watercourse in the hand of the LORD, and He inclines it toward whatever He delights in. 2 Every man's course appears upright in his own eyes, yet the LORD examines the motives of hearts. 3 To execute righteousness and justice is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.

The Moral Pathology of Arrogance and Avarice

4 Arrogant eyes and an expansive heart—the very lamp of the wicked—are sin. 5 The deliberations of the industrious redound to surplus, whereas every precipitate impulse issues in penury. 6 Treasures amassed by a mendacious tongue are a dissipating vapor and a deadly snare; 7 the violence of the wicked sweeps them away, because they refuse to execute justice.

Domestic Concord, Social Compassion, and the Discipline of Fools

8 The path of the perverse is devious, but the conduct of the pure is upright. 9 It is preferable to inhabit a corner of the rooftop than to share a dwelling with a contentious wife. 10 The wicked soul covets evil, and his neighbor finds no favor in his eyes. 11 When a scoffer is chastised, the naive acquires prudence; and when a wise man is admonished, he apprehends knowledge. 12 The Righteous One considers the household of the wicked and subverts the wicked unto calamity. 13 Whoever seals his ears against the outcry of the indigent shall himself cry out and receive no answer.

Secret Generosity, Judicial Joy, and the Perils of Dissipation

14 A clandestine gift appeases wrath, and a bribe concealed in the bosom pacifies fierce indignation. 15 To administer justice is joy to the righteous and dismay to workers of iniquity. 16 A man who strays from the path of discernment shall abide among the congregation of the shades. 17 He who delights in merriment shall become impoverished; whoever loves wine and oil shall not grow wealthy. 18 The wicked serves as a ransom for the righteous, and the faithless for the upright. 19 It is better to dwell in a wilderness than with a contentious and vexatious wife.

Wisdom, Speech, and the Supremacy of Divine Counsel

20 Desirable treasure and oil reside in the abode of the wise, but a foolish man squanders them. 21 Whoever pursues righteousness and steadfast love discovers life, righteousness, and honor. 22 A wise man ascends the city of the mighty and overthrows the stronghold in which they confide. 23 Whoever guards his mouth and his tongue preserves his soul from calamities. 24 Proud, haughty, mocker—such is the designation of him who acts in arrogant wrath. 25 The desire of the sluggard slays him, for his hands decline to labor. 26 All the day he covets greedily, yet the righteous gives and does not withhold. 27 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination; how much more when he offers it with wicked design! 28 A mendacious witness shall perish, but the attentive man speaks in perpetuity. 29 A wicked man hardens his countenance, but the upright considers his conduct. 30 There is no wisdom, nor understanding, nor counsel that can avail against the LORD. 31 The horse is made ready for the day of battle, but deliverance belongs to the LORD.
