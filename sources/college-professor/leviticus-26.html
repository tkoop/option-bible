Prohibitions Against Idolatry and Reverence for the Sanctuary

1 You shall not fabricate idols for yourselves, nor shall you erect a carved image or a sacred pillar; neither shall you install a sculpted stone within your land so as to prostrate yourselves before it, for I am the LORD your God. 2 You shall keep My Sabbaths and hold My sanctuary in awe, for I am the LORD.

Blessings Promised for Covenant Fidelity

3 If you walk according to My statutes, observe My commandments, and carry them into practice, 4 I will grant your rains in their due season, so that the land yields its produce and the trees of the field give their fruit. 5 Your harvests shall be so abundant that threshing overtakes vintage and vintage overtakes sowing; you shall eat your bread to fullness and dwell securely in your land.

6 I will establish peace in the land, so that you lie down without fear; I will rid the land of dangerous beasts, and no sword shall traverse your land. 7 You shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword. 8 Five of you shall put a hundred to flight, and a hundred of you ten thousand; and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

9 I will turn My regard toward you, make you fruitful, multiply you, and establish My covenant with you. 10 You shall eat grain long stored, and you shall clear out the old to make way for the new. 11 I will set My dwelling in your midst, and My soul shall not abhor you. 12 I will walk among you and be your God, and you shall be My people. 13 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt so that you should not remain their slaves; I shattered the bars of your yoke and caused you to walk upright.

Escalating Judgments for Defiance and Covenant Breach

14 But if you do not listen to Me and do not perform all these commandments, 15 and if you spurn My statutes and your soul abhors My ordinances, so that you fail to perform all My commandments and thereby break My covenant, 16 then I will do this to you: I will appoint over you sudden terror, wasting disease, and burning fever, which shall waste the eyes and consume the soul; you shall sow your seed in vain, and your enemies shall devour it. 17 I will set My face against you, and you shall be smitten before your enemies; those who hate you shall dominate you, and you shall flee though no one pursues.

18 And if, in spite of these things, you do not listen to Me, I will chastise you sevenfold for your sins. 19 I will break the pride of your power and make your heavens like iron and your earth like bronze. 20 Your strength shall be expended in vain, for your land shall not yield its produce, and the trees of the land shall not bear their fruit.

21 And if you persist in opposing Me and refuse to listen to Me, I will multiply plagues upon you sevenfold, according to your sins. 22 I will send wild beasts among you, which shall rob you of your children, destroy your cattle, diminish you, and leave your roads desolate.

23 And if by these things you are not corrected by Me, but persist in opposing Me, 24 then I too will oppose you and strike you sevenfold for your sins. 25 I will bring upon you a sword that avenges the covenant; you shall be gathered into your cities, and I will send pestilence among you, and you shall be delivered into the enemy's hand. 26 When I withhold the bread that sustains you, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven and shall return your bread by weight; you shall eat and not be satisfied.

27 And if, notwithstanding this, you will not listen to Me, but persist in opposing Me, 28 then I will oppose you in furious wrath, and I too will chastise you sevenfold for your sins. 29 You shall eat the flesh of your sons and the flesh of your daughters. 30 I will destroy your high places, cut down your incense altars, and cast your corpses upon the corpses of your idols, and My soul shall abhor you.

31 I will reduce your cities to ruins and desolate your sanctuaries, and I will take no delight in the fragrance of your offerings. 32 I Myself will desolate the land, so that your enemies who dwell in it shall be appalled at it. 33 I will scatter you among the nations and draw out the sword after you; your land shall become a desolation, and your cities shall become ruins.

34 Then the land shall enjoy its Sabbaths throughout the days of its desolation, while you are in your enemies' land; then the land shall rest and enjoy its Sabbaths. 35 Throughout all the days of its desolation it shall rest, because it did not rest during your Sabbaths when you dwelt upon it.

36 As for those who survive among you, I will send faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; the sound of a driven leaf shall put them to flight, and they shall flee as from the sword and fall though no one pursues. 37 They shall stumble, one over another, as before the sword, though no one pursues; and you shall have no power to stand before your enemies. 38 You shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall devour you. 39 Those who survive among you shall waste away in their iniquity in the lands of your enemies, and they shall also waste away in the iniquities of their fathers with them.

Repentance, Restoration, and Remembrance of the Covenant

40 They shall confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in the treachery by which they betrayed Me, and also that they persisted in opposing Me. 41 So I also persisted in opposing them and brought them into the land of their enemies—if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled and they accept the punishment of their iniquity, 42 then I will remember My covenant with Jacob, and also My covenant with Isaac, and also My covenant with Abraham will I remember, and I will remember the land.

43 The land shall be abandoned by them and shall enjoy its Sabbaths while it lies desolate without them; and they shall accept the punishment of their iniquity, because they rejected My ordinances and their soul abhorred My statutes. 44 Yet even so, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them or abhor them so as to destroy them utterly and break My covenant with them, for I am the LORD their God. 45 But I will remember for them the covenant of their forefathers, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that I might be their God: I am the LORD. 46 These are the statutes and the ordinances and the laws which the LORD made between Him and the children of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.
