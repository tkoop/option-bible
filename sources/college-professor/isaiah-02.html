The Vision Concerning Judah and Jerusalem

1 The vision that Isaiah son of Amoz beheld concerning Judah and Jerusalem.

The Mountain of the LORD in the Last Days

2 In the last days, the mountain of the house of the the LORD shall be established as the chief of the mountains and exalted above the hills, and all nations shall stream toward it. 3 Many peoples shall come and say, “Come, let us ascend to the mountain of the the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, that He may instruct us in His ways and that we may walk in His paths.” For from Zion shall issue forth instruction, and the word of the the LORD from Jerusalem. 4 He shall judge among the nations and arbitrate for many peoples; they shall beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks; nation shall not lift up sword against nation, nor shall they learn war any longer. 5 O house of Jacob, come, let us walk in the light of the the LORD.

The LORD’s Indictment of His People

6 For You have forsaken Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are replete with customs imported from the East and practice divination like the Philistines, and they enter into alliances with the children of foreigners. 7 Their land is glutted with silver and gold, and their treasuries know no limit; their land is filled with horses, and their chariots are beyond numbering. 8 Their land is likewise overrun with idols; they prostrate themselves before the work of their own hands, before that which their fingers have fashioned. 9 Thus humankind is humbled, and man is brought low; do not pardon them.

The Day of the LORD and the Humbling of the Proud

10 Enter the rock and hide in the dust from the terror of the the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty. 11 The haughty eyes of man shall be brought low, and the lofty pride of men shall be abased; the the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day. 12 For the day of the the LORD of Hosts shall come against all that is proud and lofty, against all that is exalted and abased; 13 against all the cedars of Lebanon, high and lifted up, and against all the oaks of Bashan; 14 against all the high mountains and all the lifted-up hills; 15 against every high tower and every fortified wall; 16 against all the ships of Tarshish and against all desirable things. 17 The haughtiness of man shall be brought low, and the pride of men shall be abased; the the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day. 18 The idols shall utterly vanish. 19 They shall go into the caves of the rocks and into the holes of the dust from the terror of the the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He arises to terrify the earth. 20 In that day a man shall cast away his silver idols and his gold idols, which they made for themselves to worship, to the moles and to the bats, 21 to enter the clefts of the rocks and the crevices of the cliffs from the terror of the the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He arises to terrify the earth. 22 Cease from man, whose breath is in his nostrils, for of what account is he?
