Tola and Jair: Minor Deliverers of Israel

1 Following the demise of Abimelech, Tola—son of Puah, son of Dodo, and a man of Issachar—arose to effect Israel's deliverance; he took up residence in Shamir, within the hill country of Ephraim. 2 He adjudicated Israel for twenty-three years; upon his death, he was interred in Shamir. 3 After him, Jair the Gileadite arose and judged Israel for twenty-two years. 4 He had thirty sons who rode upon thirty donkeys, and they held thirty towns in the land of Gilead, still designated Havvoth-jair to this day. 5 Jair died and was buried in Kamon.

Israel's Relapse into Idolatry and Oppression

6 The Israelites once more perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD: they venerated the Baals and the Ashtoreths, together with the deities of Aram, Sidon, Moab, the Ammonites, and the Philistines; they abandoned the LORD and rendered Him no service. 7 The LORD's wrath was kindled against Israel, and He consigned them into the power of the Philistines and the Ammonites. 8 In that year they shattered and crushed the Israelites; for eighteen years they oppressed all the Israelites beyond the Jordan in Gilead, in the land of the Amorites. 9 The Ammonites likewise crossed the Jordan to wage war against Judah, Benjamin, and the house of Ephraim, and Israel was exceedingly distressed.

Divine Rebuke and Israel's Contrition

10 The Israelites cried out to the LORD, saying, "We have sinned against you, for we have forsaken our God and served the Baals."

11 The LORD said to the Israelites, "Did I not deliver you from the Egyptians, the Amorites, the Ammonites, and the Philistines? 12 The Sidonians, Amalek, and Maon oppressed you; you cried out to Me, and I delivered you from their hand. 13 Yet you have forsaken Me and served other gods; consequently I shall no longer deliver you. 14 Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen; let them deliver you in your time of distress."

15 The Israelites said to the LORD, "We have sinned; do unto us whatever seems good in Your sight; only deliver us this day."

16 So they removed the foreign gods from their midst and served the LORD, and His soul was grieved by Israel's affliction.

The Ammonite Crisis and the Quest for a Leader

17 The Ammonites assembled and encamped in Gilead, while the Israelites gathered and encamped at Mizpah.

18 The people and the leaders of Gilead said to one another, "Who is the man who will take the lead in fighting the Ammonites? He shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead."
