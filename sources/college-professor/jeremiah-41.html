The Assassination of Gedaliah at Mizpah

1 In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah—of royal stock and one of the king's chief officers, himself the son of Elishama—arrived at Mizpah with ten companions to visit Gedaliah son of Ahikam, and there they shared a meal. 2 Then Ishmael son of Nethaniah, together with those ten men, rose and struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, with the sword, murdering the man whom the king of Babylon had appointed governor over the land.

3 Ishmael also slew all the Jews who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah, together with the Chaldean warriors found there. 4 On the day after Gedaliah's assassination, before the crime had become known, 5 eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria, their beards shaved, their garments torn, and their bodies lacerated, bearing grain offerings and incense to present at the house of the the LORD.

The Massacre of the Pilgrims

6 Ishmael son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them, weeping as he advanced; and as he encountered them, he said, "Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam."

7 When they had entered the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah and his associates slaughtered them and cast their bodies into a cistern.

8 Yet ten of them pleaded with Ishmael, "Do not put us to death, for we have concealed treasures in the field—wheat, barley, oil, and honey." So he desisted and did not slay them along with their companions.

9 The cistern into which Ishmael had cast all the corpses of the men he had struck down, along with Gedaliah, was the great reservoir that King Asa had excavated for defense against Baasha king of Israel; Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with the slain.

Captivity and Johanan's Pursuit

10 Ishmael then carried off captive the entire remnant of the people at Mizpah—the king's daughters and all who remained there, whom Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had entrusted to Gedaliah son of Ahikam—and set out to cross over to the Ammonites.

11 When Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces with him heard of the atrocities Ishmael son of Nethaniah had committed, 12 they mobilized their entire company and marched to confront Ishmael; they found him beside the great pool at Gibeon.

13 All the people who were with Ishmael rejoiced when they saw Johanan son of Kareah and the commanders accompanying him. 14 Consequently, the whole company whom Ishmael had seized at Mizpah turned about and went over to Johanan son of Kareah.

15 Ishmael son of Nethaniah, however, escaped from Johanan with eight men and fled to the Ammonites.

The Survivors' Flight Toward Egypt

16 Then Johanan son of Kareah and all his commanders took the entire remnant they had recovered from Ishmael son of Nethaniah at Mizpah after the murder of Gedaliah son of Ahikam—the warriors, the women, the children, and the eunuchs whom he had brought back from Gibeon. 17 They journeyed onward and settled at Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem, intending to proceed into Egypt, 18 for they feared the Chaldeans, because Ishmael son of Nethaniah had slain Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land.
