Hallelujah: A Cosmic Summons to Extol the Creator

1 Hallelujah! Praise the LORD from the heavens; extol Him in the loftiest heights. 2 Praise Him, all His angels; praise Him, all His celestial hosts. 3 Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, every shining star. 4 Praise Him, highest heavens, and the waters beyond the heavens.

5 Let them extol the name of the LORD, for He issued the command, and they were brought into being. 6 He established them for all eternity; He promulgated a decree that shall never be revoked.

Terrestrial and Human Acclamation of the Divine Sovereign

7 Praise the LORD from the earth—great sea creatures and all oceanic depths, 8 fire and hail, snow and vapor, and the storm wind executing His word; 9 mountains and every hill, fruit trees and all cedars; 10 wild beasts and all livestock, creeping things and winged birds.

11 Kings of the earth and all nations, princes and every judge of the earth; 12 young men and maidens together, the aged alongside children.

The Exaltation of the LORD's Name and the Elevation of His People

13 Let them extol the name of the LORD, for His name alone is exalted; His splendor surpasses earth and heaven. 14 He has exalted a horn for His people—a renown for all His faithful, for the children of Israel, a people intimate with Him. Hallelujah!
