Pashhur Persecutes Jeremiah

1 Pashhur the priest—son of Immer and chief officer in the house of the LORD—heard Jeremiah prophesying these words. 2 So Pashhur struck Jeremiah the prophet and put him in the stocks in the upper gate of Benjamin at the house of the LORD.

3 The next day, when Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks, Jeremiah said to him, “The LORD has not called your name Pashhur, but Magor-missabib—‘Terror on Every Side.’ 4 For thus says the LORD: ‘Behold, I am making you a terror to yourself and to all your friends; they shall fall by the sword of their enemies while your eyes behold it. I will also give all Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon; he shall carry them captive to Babylon and strike them down with the sword. 5 I will give all the wealth of this city—all its labor and all its precious things—and all the treasures of the kings of Judah into the hand of their enemies; they shall plunder them, seize them, and carry them off to Babylon. 6 And you, Pashhur, and all who dwell in your house, shall go into captivity; to Babylon you shall come, and there you shall die and be buried—you and all your friends to whom you have prophesied falsely.’”

Jeremiah’s Anguished Confession

7 You have deceived me, O the LORD, and I was deceived; You are stronger than I, and You have prevailed. I have become a laughingstock all day long; everyone mocks me. 8 For whenever I speak, I cry out and proclaim violence and destruction, because the word of the LORD has become for me a reproach and a derision all day long. 9 If I say, “I will not mention Him, nor will I speak anymore in His name,” then His word becomes like a burning fire shut up in my bones; I grow weary of holding it in, and I cannot.

Conspiracy Against Jeremiah and His Vindication

10 For I have heard the whispering of many: “Terror on every side! Report him, and we will report him.” All my trusted friends watch for my stumbling, saying, “Perhaps he will be enticed, so that we may prevail against him and take vengeance upon him.” 11 But the LORD is with me like a mighty, awesome warrior; therefore my persecutors will stumble and not prevail. They will be utterly ashamed, because they have not prospered; their everlasting disgrace will never be forgotten. 12 O the LORD of Hosts, You who test the righteous and see the kidneys and the heart, let me see Your vengeance upon them, for to You I have committed my cause. 13 Sing to the LORD! Praise the LORD, for He has delivered the soul of the needy from the hand of evildoers.

Jeremiah’s Lament over His Birth

14 Cursed be the day on which I was born; the day my mother bore me, let it not be blessed. 15 Cursed be the man who brought news to my father, saying, “A son is born to you,” and made him exceedingly glad. 16 Let that man be like the cities that the LORD overthrew without relenting; let him hear an outcry in the morning and a war cry at noon. 17 Because He did not kill me in the womb, so that my mother would have been my grave and her womb forever great with child. 18 Why did I come forth from the womb to see toil and sorrow and to spend my days in shame?
