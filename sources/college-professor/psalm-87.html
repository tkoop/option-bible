A psalm of the sons of Korah—a song.

A Hymn of Zion’s Exalted Foundations and Cosmopolitan Destiny

1 His foundation is established upon the holy mountains. 2 The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. 3 Glorious things are spoken of you, O city of God. Selah.

The Nations Enrolled Among Zion’s Children

4 “I will recount Egypt and Babylon among those who acknowledge Me—along with Philistia, Tyre, and Cush—each one born there.”

5 And concerning Zion it shall be said, “This one and that one were born in her,” and the Most High Himself will establish her.

6 The LORD will record in the register of the nations, “This one was born there.” Selah.

7 Singers and dancers will declare, “All my springs are in you.”
