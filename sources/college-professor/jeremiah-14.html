The Oracle Concerning the Drought

1 The word of the the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the drought.

2 Judah mourns, and her gates languish; they are bowed down to the ground in mourning, and the cry of Jerusalem ascends. 3 Her magnates have dispatched their servants to draw water; they have reached the cisterns and found them dry; they have returned with their vessels unfilled; they have been put to shame and confounded, and have veiled their heads. 4 Because the soil is fractured—for no rain has fallen upon the land—the tillers of the soil have been disgraced; they have covered their heads. 5 Even the doe in the field has calved and forsaken her offspring, because no herbage remains. 6 The wild asses have taken their stand upon the bare heights; they have snuffed the wind like jackals; their eyes have been consumed, because no verdure is to be found.

Jeremiah's Intercession and the LORD's Refusal

7 Although our iniquities bear witness against us, O the LORD, act for the sake of Your name; for our apostasies are numerous, and we have sinned against You. 8 O Hope of Israel, its Deliverer in the time of distress, why should You be as a sojourner in the land, as a wayfarer who turns aside to lodge for a night? 9 Why should You be like one bewildered, like a warrior unable to deliver? Yet You are in our midst, O the LORD, and we are called by Your name; do not forsake us.

10 Thus says the the LORD concerning this people: "They have loved to wander; they have not withheld their feet. Therefore the the LORD does not accept them; now He will remember their iniquity and visit their sins upon them."

11 The LORD said to me, "Do not intercede for the well-being of this people. 12 When they fast, I will not hearken to their cry; and when they present burnt offering and grain offering, I will not receive them with favor. Rather, I am bringing them to an end by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence."

False Prophets and the Coming Judgment

13 Then I said, "Ah, Lord the LORD! Behold, the prophets are declaring to them, 'You shall not see the sword, nor shall you have famine, but I will give you enduring peace in this place.'"

14 The LORD said to me, "The prophets are prophesying a lie in My name. I have not sent them, neither have I commanded them, nor have I spoken to them. They are prophesying to you a false vision, divination, vanity, and the delusion of their own hearts. 15 Therefore thus says the the LORD concerning the prophets who prophesy in My name, although I did not send them, and who say, 'Neither sword nor famine shall befall this land'—by sword and famine those prophets shall be consumed. 16 And the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword, and none shall bury them—neither them, nor their wives, nor their sons, nor their daughters—for I will pour out their own evil upon them."

Jeremiah's Lament and Final Supplication

17 You shall say this word to them: "Let my eyes stream with tears night and day, and let them not be still; for the virgin daughter of my people has been shattered with a great fracture, with an exceedingly grievous stroke." 18 If I go forth into the field, behold, those slain by the sword; and if I enter the city, behold, those languishing with famine. For both prophet and priest have roamed into a land they do not know.

19 Have You utterly rejected Judah? Has Your soul abhorred Zion? Why have You smitten us so that there is no healing? We looked for peace, but no good came; and for a time of healing, but behold, terror. 20 We acknowledge our wickedness, O the LORD, and the iniquity of our fathers; for we have sinned against You.

21 For Your name's sake, do not despise us; do not dishonor the throne of Your glory; remember Your covenant, and do not annul it with us. 22 Are there any among the futile idols of the nations that can cause rain? Or can the heavens themselves give showers? Are You not He, O the LORD our God? Therefore we place our hope in You, for You have made all these things.
