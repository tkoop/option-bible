The Divine Repudiation of Saul and the Prophetic Commission to Bethlehem

1 The LORD said to Samuel, “How long will you mourn for Saul, seeing that I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go; I am sending you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have discerned among his sons a king for Myself.”

2 Samuel said, “How can I go? Saul will hear of it and kill me.”

The the LORD said, “Take a heifer with you and say, ‘I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD.’ 3 And you shall summon Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will disclose to you what you are to do; you shall anoint for Me whomever I designate to you.”

4 Samuel did what the the LORD had spoken, and he came to Bethlehem. The elders of the city came trembling to meet him and asked, “Do you come in peace?”

5 He replied, “Peace. I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD. Consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice.” So he consecrated Jesse and his sons and summoned them to the sacrifice.

The Discernment of the Heart and the Anointing of David

6 When they arrived, he beheld Eliab and said, “Surely the the LORD’s anointed stands before Him.”

7 The The LORD said to Samuel, “Do not regard his appearance or his lofty stature, for I have rejected him; for the the LORD does not perceive as a mortal perceives: a mortal looks upon the outward appearance, but the the LORD looks upon the heart.”

8 Then Jesse summoned Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel, who said, “The the LORD has not chosen this one either.” 9 Next Jesse made Shammah pass by, and Samuel said, “The the LORD has not chosen this one either.” 10 Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel, and Samuel said to Jesse, “The the LORD has not chosen any of these.”

11 Samuel asked Jesse, “Are these all the sons you have?”

Jesse answered, “The youngest still remains; behold, he is tending the sheep.”

Samuel said, “Send for him and bring him here, for we will not sit down until he arrives.”

12 So Jesse sent and brought him in. He was ruddy, with beautiful eyes, and handsome in appearance. The The LORD said, “Arise and anoint him, for this is he.” 13 Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers, and the Spirit of the the LORD rushed upon David from that day forward. Then Samuel rose and went to Ramah.

The Affliction of the Rejected King and the Soothing Ministry of the Shepherd-Musician

14 Now the Spirit of the the LORD had departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the the LORD tormented him.

15 Saul’s servants said to him, “Behold, an evil spirit from God is terrifying you. 16 Let our lord now command your servants before you to seek out a man skilled in playing the harp, so that when the evil spirit from God comes upon you, he may play with his hand, and you will be well.”

17 Saul said to his servants, “Find me a man who plays well, and bring him to me.”

18 One of the young men answered, “Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite who knows how to play, a mighty man of valor, a warrior, discerning in speech, handsome in form, and the the LORD is with him.”

19 So Saul sent messengers to Jesse, saying, “Send me David your son, who is with the sheep.”

20 Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat, and sent them with his son David to Saul.

21 David came to Saul and entered his service; Saul loved him greatly, and David became his armor-bearer.

22 Saul sent word to Jesse: “Let David remain before me, for he has found favor in my eyes.”

23 Whenever the spirit from God came upon Saul, David would take the harp and play with his hand; Saul would be relieved and feel better, and the evil spirit would depart from him.
