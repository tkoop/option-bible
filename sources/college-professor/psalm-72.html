A Prayer for the King's Righteous Reign

1 Of Solomon. Grant to the king Your judgments, O God, and Your righteousness to the king's son. 2 He shall judge Your people in righteousness and Your afflicted in justice. 3 Let the mountains bear peace to the people, and the hills, righteousness. 4 He shall vindicate the afflicted of the people; he shall save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor.

Universal Reverence and the King's Beneficent Rule

5 May reverence for You endure as long as the sun shines, and before the moon, throughout all generations. 6 He shall descend like rain upon freshly mown grass, like showers that irrigate the earth. 7 In his days the righteous shall flourish, and an abundance of peace, until the moon is no more. 8 He shall hold dominion from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth. 9 Before him shall bow the denizens of the desert, and his enemies shall be reduced to abject humiliation. 10 The kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands shall render tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba shall present offerings. 11 All kings shall bow down to him, and all nations shall serve him.

Mercy to the Needy and Enduring Prosperity

12 For he rescues the needy who cry for help, and the afflicted who has no helper. 13 He will show compassion to the poor and needy, and will save the lives of the needy. 14 He will redeem their lives from oppression and violence, and their blood shall be precious in his sight. 15 May he live long; may gold from Sheba be bestowed upon him; may prayer be offered on his behalf continually, and may he be blessed throughout the day. 16 Let grain abound in the land; may it sway upon the mountain peaks; its fruit shall be like Lebanon; and they shall flourish from the city like grass of the earth. 17 May his name endure in perpetuity; may it be continued as long as the sun shines. All nations shall be blessed in him, and they shall pronounce him blessed.

Doxology and Conclusion

18 Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone performs wonders. 19 And blessed be His glorious name forever; let the whole earth be filled with His glory. Amen and Amen. 20 The prayers of David, the son of Jesse, are concluded.
