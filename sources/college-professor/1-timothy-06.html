Slaves, Masters, and the Perils of False Teaching

1 All who remain beneath the yoke of servitude must regard their own masters as meriting every species of honor, so that the name of God and the doctrine may not be subjected to blasphemy. 2 Those whose masters are believers are not to treat them with contempt on the pretext that they are brothers; rather, they are to render service all the more, precisely because those who partake of their beneficence are faithful and beloved. Teach and exhort these things.

3 If anyone propagates heterodox doctrine and refuses to acquiesce in the wholesome words of our Lord Jesus Christ and in the teaching consonant with piety, 4 he is inflated with conceit, knowing nothing, but is diseased with controversies and verbal strife, from which arise envy, contention, blasphemy, and baseless suspicions— 5 the chronic friction of people corrupted in mind and bereft of truth, who imagine that piety is a lucrative enterprise.

Godliness, Contentment, and the Snare of Wealth

6 Yet piety accompanied by contentment constitutes a considerable gain, 7 for we imported nothing into the world, and it is certain that we can export nothing from it; 8 and having sustenance and raiment, we shall be satisfied with these.

9 Those who are determined to become wealthy precipitate themselves into temptation and a snare, together with a multitude of senseless and deleterious cravings that submerge people in ruin and perdition. 10 For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil, and through their craving for it some have strayed from the faith and transfixed themselves with manifold anguish.

The Noble Contest and the Appearing of Christ

11 But you, O man of God, flee these things and pursue righteousness, piety, faith, love, endurance, and meekness. 12 Contend in the noble contest of the faith; lay hold of eternal life, to which you were summoned and concerning which you rendered the noble confession before many witnesses.

13 I charge you before God, who gives life to all things, and before Christ Jesus, who bore witness to the noble confession under Pontius Pilate, 14 that you keep the commandment unblemished and irreproachable until the manifestation of our Lord Jesus Christ, 15 which He will reveal in His own seasons—He who is the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of kings and Lord of lords, 16 who alone possesses immortality and inhabits light unapproachable, whom no human being has seen or is able to see; to Him be honor and everlasting dominion. Amen.

Counsel for the Affluent and the Final Charge

17 Charge those who are affluent in the present age not to be arrogant, nor to fix their confidence upon the instability of wealth, but upon God, who lavishly furnishes us with all things for enjoyment. 18 They are to perform beneficence, to be affluent in good works, to be munificent and disposed to share, 19 thereby amassing for themselves a noble foundation for the time to come, in order that they may lay hold upon what is truly life.

20 O Timothy, safeguard the deposit committed to your trust, shunning profane and empty discourses and the antitheses of what is mendaciously styled "knowledge," 21 for by professing such, certain persons have deviated from the faith. Grace be with you.
