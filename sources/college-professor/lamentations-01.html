The Desolation of Jerusalem

1 How desolately sits the city that was once populous! She who was eminent among the nations has become like a widow; the princess among provinces has been reduced to vassalage. 2 She weeps bitterly in the night, and her tears stain her cheeks; among all her lovers there is none to console her. All her companions have dealt treacherously with her and have become her adversaries. 3 Judah has gone into exile under affliction and grievous servitude; though she dwells among the nations, she finds no repose, and all her persecutors overtook her in the straits.

4 The roads to Zion mourn, for none come to the appointed feasts; all her gates are desolate; her priests groan; her maidens are grieved, and she herself is in bitterness. 5 Her adversaries have become her masters; her enemies are at ease, because the LORD has afflicted her for the abundance of her transgressions. Her little ones have gone into captivity before the adversary. 6 All her splendor has departed from the daughter of Zion. Her princes have become like stags that find no pasture; they have gone forth without strength before the pursuer.

The Spoiling of Zion and Her Confession

7 In the days of her affliction and wandering, Jerusalem remembers all her treasures from the days of old, when her people fell into the enemy's hand and no one helped her. The adversaries saw her and mocked at her ruin. 8 Jerusalem has sinned grievously; therefore she has become an object of revulsion. All who honored her despise her, for they have seen her nakedness; she herself groans and turns away. 9 Her uncleanness clings to her skirts; she did not consider her end. She has come down astonishingly, and there is no comforter for her. See, the LORD, my affliction, for the enemy has magnified himself.

10 The adversary has stretched out his hand over all her pleasant things, for she has seen the nations enter her sanctuary—those whom You commanded should not enter Your assembly. 11 All her people groan as they seek bread; they have given their treasures for food to revive their souls. See, the LORD, and consider, for I have become despised. 12 Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by? Look and see whether there is any sorrow like my sorrow, which was brought upon me, which the LORD inflicted in the day of His fierce anger.

The Yoke of Divine Judgment

13 From on high He sent fire into my bones and subdued them; He spread a net for my feet and turned me back; He has made me desolate and faint all the day. 14 The yoke of my transgressions is bound by His hand; they are knit together and placed upon my neck. He has made my strength fail; the Lord has delivered me into hands from which I cannot rise. 15 The Lord has trampled all my mighty men in my midst; He has summoned an assembly against me to crush my young men. The Lord has trodden the virgin daughter of Judah as in a winepress.

16 For these things I weep; my eyes, my eyes overflow with tears, because the comforter who should restore my soul is far from me. My children are desolate because the enemy has prevailed. 17 Zion stretches out her hands, yet there is no one to comfort her. The LORD has commanded concerning Jacob that his adversaries should be round about him. Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them. 18 The LORD is righteous, for I have rebelled against His command. Hear, I pray, all peoples, and see my sorrow; my maidens and my young men have gone into captivity.

A Plea for Vindication

19 I called to my lovers, but they deceived me. My priests and my elders perished in the city while they sought food to revive their souls. 20 See, the LORD, for I am in distress; my inmost being is in turmoil; my heart is overturned within me, for I have been very rebellious. Outside, the sword bereaves; at home, it is like death. 21 They have heard that I groan, and there is no comforter for me. All my enemies have heard of my trouble and rejoice that You have done it. You have brought the day You proclaimed, and they shall become like me. 22 Let all their wickedness come before You, and do to them as You have done to me for all my transgressions. For my groans are many, and my heart is faint.
