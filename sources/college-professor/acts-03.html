The Lame Man's Healing at the Beautiful Gate

1 Peter and John were ascending to the temple at the hour of prayer, namely the ninth hour. 2 A certain man, lame from his mother's womb, was being carried to the gate of the temple called the Beautiful Gate; he was deposited there each day so that he might solicit alms from those entering the sanctuary. 3 Perceiving Peter and John about to enter the temple, he entreated them for an alms.

4 Peter, fixing his gaze upon him together with John, said, “Fix your eyes upon us.”

5 He fastened his gaze upon them, anticipating some benefaction from their hands.

6 Peter said, “Silver and gold are not at my disposal; nevertheless, that which I do possess I give to you: in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, arise and walk.”

7 Grasping him by the right hand, Peter lifted him up, and forthwith his feet and ankles were fortified. 8 He sprang to his feet, stood firm, and began to walk; then he accompanied them into the temple, proceeding with walking and leaping and praising God.

9 All the populace beheld him ambulating and glorifying God. 10 They recognized him as the very man who had been wont to sit begging at the Beautiful Gate, and they were suffused with astonishment and ecstasy at the occurrence befalling him. 11 While he maintained his hold upon Peter and John, the entire crowd, convulsed with amazement, converged upon them in the portico denominated Solomon's.

Peter's Apostolic Address to the Astonished Multitude

12 Perceiving this, Peter responded to the people: “Men of Israel, why do you regard this occurrence with such wonder, or why do you fix your gaze upon us, as though by our own potency or piety we had enabled this man to walk? 13 The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—the God of our fathers—has glorified His Servant Jesus, whom you surrendered and repudiated before Pilate, notwithstanding that he had adjudged Him worthy of release.

14 “Yet you disowned the Holy and Righteous One, and petitioned that a murderer be granted unto you. 15 You put to death the Author of life, whom God raised from the dead; of this we are witnesses.

16 “And by faith in His name, this man—whom you behold and recognize—has been strengthened by that very name; moreover, the faith that comes through Him has conferred upon him this complete soundness in the presence of all of you. 17 And now, brethren, I am cognizant that you acted in ignorance, even as your rulers did. 18 Yet God has fulfilled what He foreannounced through the mouths of all the prophets, namely that His Christ should suffer.

The Prophetic Summons to Repentance and Restoration

19 “Repent, therefore, and return, that your sins may be expunged, 20 so that seasons of refreshing may emanate from the presence of the Lord, and that He may dispatch Jesus, the Christ designated for you. 21 Heaven must receive Him until the times of restoration of all things, which God declared from of old through the mouths of His holy prophets.

22 “For Moses said, ‘The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to Him in everything He says to you.’ 23 And it shall come to pass that every soul who refuses to hearken unto that Prophet shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.

24 “Indeed, all the prophets from Samuel onward, as many as have spoken, have foretold these days. 25 And you are sons of the prophets and of the covenant that God covenanted with your fathers, when He said to Abraham, ‘In your seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.’ 26 God, having raised up His Servant, sent Him first unto you, to bless you by turning each of you away from your iniquities.”
