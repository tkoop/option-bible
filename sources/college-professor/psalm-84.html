The Surpassing Beauty of the Divine Dwelling Place

1 How delectable are Your sanctuaries, O the LORD of Hosts! 2 My soul has languished with longing and is utterly spent for the courts of the the LORD; my heart and my flesh exult in jubilation toward the living God. 3 Even the sparrow has discovered a dwelling, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young—at Your altars, O the LORD of Hosts, my King and my God. 4 How blessed are those who inhabit Your house; they perpetually praise You. Selah.

The Blessed Pilgrimage of the Faithful

5 How blessed is the man whose might is in You, in whose heart lie the highways to Zion. 6 As they traverse the Valley of Baca, they transform it into a spring; the early rain likewise mantles it with blessings. 7 They advance from strength to strength; each appears before God in Zion.

A Supplication for Divine Favor and Protection

8 O the LORD God of Hosts, hear my prayer; attend, O God of Jacob. Selah. 9 Behold our shield, O God, and regard the face of Your Anointed.

The Surpassing Worth of the Divine Courts

10 For a single day in Your courts surpasses a thousand elsewhere; I would rather serve as a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of wickedness. 11 For the the LORD God is a sun and shield; the the LORD bestows grace and glory; He withholds no good thing from those who walk in integrity. 12 O the LORD of Hosts, how blessed is the man who trusts in You!
