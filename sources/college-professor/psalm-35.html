A Psalm of David: A Prayer for Vindication

1 May the LORD contend with those who contend with me; may He fight against those who fight against me. 2 Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise to my aid.

3 Draw out the spear and bar the way against my pursuers; say to my soul, “I am your salvation.”

4 Let those who seek my life be confounded and disgraced; let those who devise evil against me be repelled and humiliated. 5 Let them be like chaff before the wind, with the angel of the LORD driving them on. 6 May their path be dark and treacherous, while the angel of the LORD pursues them.

7 For without provocation they concealed their net for me; without provocation they dug a pit for my life. 8 Let ruin overtake him unawares; let the net he concealed ensnare him; let him fall into that very ruin.

Confidence in the LORD's Deliverance

9 Then my soul shall exult in the LORD and rejoice in His salvation. 10 All my bones shall declare, “Who is like You, the LORD, delivering the poor from the one too strong for him, and the poor and needy from their plunderer?”

11 Ruthless witnesses rise up; they interrogate me concerning things I do not know. 12 They repay me evil for good, seeking the bereavement of my soul.

13 Yet when they were ill, I clothed myself in sackcloth; I afflicted my soul with fasting, and my prayer returned to me unanswered. 14 I went about as though for a friend or brother; I bowed down in grief as one mourning for a mother.

15 Yet when I stumbled, they rejoiced and assembled; attackers gathered against me without my knowledge; they slandered me without ceasing. 16 Like profane mockers at a banquet, they gnashed their teeth at me.

A Plea for Rescue and Thanksgiving

17 How long, O Lord, will You look on? Deliver my soul from their desolations, my precious life from the lions. 18 I will give You thanks in the great assembly; I will praise You among a mighty throng.

19 Let not my enemies rejoice over me wrongfully, nor let those who hate me without cause wink maliciously. 20 For they do not speak peaceably; they devise deceitful plots against the tranquil in the land. 21 They gaped at me and cried, “Aha! Aha! Our eyes have seen it.”

22 The LORD, You have seen; do not be silent. O Lord, do not be far from me. 23 Rouse Yourself and awake to my vindication, to my cause, my God and my Lord. 24 Judge me, the LORD my God, according to Your righteousness, and let them not exult over me.

25 Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha! This is what we desired.” Let them not say, “We have swallowed him up.” 26 Let those who rejoice at my calamity be ashamed and confounded together; let those who magnify themselves against me be clothed with shame and dishonor.

27 Let those who delight in my vindication shout for joy and be glad; let them say continually, “The LORD be magnified, who delights in the well-being of His servant.” 28 Then my tongue shall recount Your righteousness and Your praise all the day long.
