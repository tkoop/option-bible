The Eradication of Idolatry and the Restoration of Priestly Order

1 After all these events, once every Israelite who could be found had completed the celebration, they went out to the cities of Judah and shattered the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherah poles, and demolished the high places and altars throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh until they had utterly destroyed them; then all Israel returned to their own cities, each to his own possession. 2 Hezekiah reestablished the divisions of the priests and Levites according to their divisions, assigning each to his proper service, for the priests and Levites were to present burnt offerings and peace offerings, to minister, to give thanks, and to praise within the gates of the the LORD.

3 The king also set aside from his own property a portion for the burnt offerings—those offered morning and evening—and for the burnt offerings of the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the appointed feasts, as it is written in the law of the the LORD. 4 He commanded the people, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to give the priests and Levites their portion so that they might devote themselves with renewed strength to the law of the the LORD.

The People’s Abundant Offerings of Firstfruits and Tithes

5 When the command spread abroad, the Israelites brought in abundance the firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and every product of the field; indeed, they brought in a tithe of everything in great abundance. 6 The Israelites and the Judahites who dwelt in the cities of Judah likewise brought tithes of cattle and sheep, together with tithes of the holy things consecrated to the the LORD their God, and they laid them heap upon heap. 7 They began laying the foundations of these heaps in the third month and completed them in the seventh month.

8 When Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, they blessed the the LORD and His people Israel. 9 Hezekiah then inquired of the priests and Levites concerning the heaps, 10 and Azariah, the chief priest of the house of Zadok, answered him, “Since the contributions began to be brought into the house of the the LORD, the people have eaten and been satisfied, with a great abundance left over, because the the LORD has blessed His people; indeed, this vast remainder is still here.”

The Organization of Sacred Contributions and Priestly Allotments

11 Hezekiah commanded that chambers be prepared in the house of the the LORD, and they prepared them. 12 They brought the contributions, the tithes, and the consecrated things in faithfully; Conaniah the Levite was the overseer over them, and his brother Shimei was second. 13 Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were supervisors under Conaniah and Shimei, by the appointment of King Hezekiah and of Azariah, the chief priest of the house of God.

14 Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the gatekeeper on the east, was over the freewill offerings of God, to distribute the contribution of the the LORD and the most holy things. 15 Under his authority were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah, acting in faithfulness to distribute to their brothers in the priestly cities, according to their divisions, to great and small alike. 16 In addition to those enrolled by genealogy, males from three years old and upward—everyone who entered the house of the the LORD daily for service—were included in these allotments, according to the duties of their divisions.

17 The priests were enrolled according to their fathers’ houses, and the Levites from twenty years old and upward according to their duties and divisions. 18 They enrolled their entire assembly—their little ones, wives, sons, and daughters—for in faithfulness they had consecrated themselves as holy. 19 For the sons of Aaron, the priests in the pasturelands of their cities, in every city, men were designated by name to give portions to every male among the priests and to all the Levites who were enrolled.

Hezekiah’s Faithfulness and Prosperity

20 Thus Hezekiah acted throughout all Judah, and he did what was good, right, and true before the the LORD his God. 21 In every work that he began in the service of the house of God, in the law and the commandment, to seek his God, he prospered.
