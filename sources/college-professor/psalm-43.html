A Supplication for Vindication and Guiding Light

1 Vindicate me, O God, and champion my cause against an impious nation; deliver me from the machinations of deceitful and iniquitous men. 2 For You are the God of my strength; why have You cast me aside? Why must I traverse my days in mourning beneath the oppression of my adversary?

3 Dispatch Your light and Your truth; let them conduct me, and let them usher me into Your holy mountain and into Your dwelling places. 4 Then shall I approach the altar of God, even God, the exultation of my gladness; and I shall extol You with the harp, O God, my God.

5 Why are you downcast, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall yet extol Him, the salvation of my countenance and my God.
