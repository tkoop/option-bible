Samson's Philistine Alliance and Providential Design

1 Samson descended to Timnah, where he espied a woman among the daughters of Timnah who was of Philistine extraction. 2 He ascended and disclosed to his father and mother, saying, "I have seen a woman among the daughters of the Philistines in Timnah; therefore procure her for me as my wife."

3 His father and mother said to him, "Is there no woman among the daughters of your brothers, or among all my people, that you must go to take a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines?"

Samson said to his father, "Take her for me, for she is pleasing to me."

4 His father and mother did not apprehend that this matter derived from the LORD, since He was seeking a pretext against the Philistines at a time when the Philistines exercised dominion over Israel.

The Lion, the Honey, and the Riddle

5 Samson descended to Timnah with his father and mother, and they came as far as the vineyards of Timnah, where a young lion roared to confront him. 6 Then the Spirit of the LORD surged upon him, and he tore the lion as one tears a young goat, although nothing was in his hand; yet he did not disclose to his father or mother what he had done.

7 He descended and spoke to the woman, and she proved pleasing to Samson.

8 After some days he returned to take her, and he turned aside to inspect the carcass of the lion, only to discover a swarm of bees and honey within the carcass.

9 He scooped it into his palms and ate while proceeding; he also gave some to his father and mother, and they ate, yet he did not disclose that he had scooped the honey from the lion's carcass.

10 His father descended to the woman, and Samson prepared a banquet there, as was customary among the young men.

11 When they saw him, they brought thirty companions to be with him.

The Banquet Riddle and Samson's Vengeance

12 Samson said to them, "Let me now propound a riddle to you: if you can fully declare it to me within the seven days of the feast and solve it, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothing."

13 "But if you cannot tell me, then you shall give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothing."

They said, "Propound your riddle, and we will hear it."

14 He said to them, "Out of the devourer proceeded nourishment, and out of the mighty proceeded sweetness." For three days they were unable to explicate the riddle.

15 On the seventh day they said to Samson's wife, "Coerce your husband into divulging the riddle, lest we incinerate you and your father's household; have you summoned us merely to despoil us?"

16 Samson's wife wept before him and said, "You merely hate me and do not love me; you have posed a riddle to the sons of my people and have not told it to me."

He said, "Behold, I have not told it to my father or my mother; shall I tell you?"

17 She wept in his presence throughout the seven days of their feast; and on the seventh day he disclosed it to her, because she importuned him; whereupon she revealed the riddle to the sons of her people.

18 Before sunset on the seventh day, the men of the city said to him, "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?"

He said, "Had you not exploited my wife, you would never have unraveled my riddle."

19 Then the Spirit of the LORD came powerfully upon him, and he descended to Ashkelon, struck down thirty of their men, took their plunder, and gave the changes of clothing to those who had explained the riddle. Burning with anger, he returned to his father's house.

20 Meanwhile, Samson's wife was consigned to his companion, the one who had attended him.
