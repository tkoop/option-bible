The Philistines Defeat Israel on Mount Gilboa

1 When the Philistines waged war against Israel, the Israelite host fled before them and fell slain upon Mount Gilboa. 2 The Philistines pressed hard after Saul and his sons, striking down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua. 3 The battle pressed crushingly upon Saul; the archers found him, and he was grievously wounded.

The Death of Saul and the Collapse of Israelite Resistance

4 Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me with it, lest these uncircumcised men come and pierce me and abuse me." But his armor-bearer would not, for he was terrified; so Saul took the sword and fell upon it. 5 When the armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he likewise fell upon his sword and died with him. 6 Thus Saul died, together with his three sons, his armor-bearer, and all his men, on that same day. 7 When the Israelites across the valley and beyond the Jordan saw that their countrymen had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities and fled; the Philistines came and occupied them.

The Desecration of Saul's Corpse

8 On the following day the Philistines came to strip the slain, and they found Saul and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa. 9 They cut off his head, stripped his armor, and sent messengers throughout the Philistine country to proclaim the tidings in the temples of their idols and among the people. 10 They placed his armor in the temple of Ashtaroth and fastened his body to the wall of Beth-shan.

The Men of Jabesh-gilead Recover and Bury Saul's Remains

11 When the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, 12 every valiant man arose and marched through the night, took the bodies of Saul and his sons from the wall of Beth-shan, brought them to Jabesh, and burned them there. 13 Then they took their bones and buried them beneath the tamarisk at Jabesh, and they fasted seven days.
