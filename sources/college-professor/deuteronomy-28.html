The Covenant Blessings Attending Obedience

1If you diligently heed the voice of the LORD your God, scrupulously keeping and performing all His commandments that I enjoin upon you this day, then the LORD your God will exalt you above all the nations of the earth. 2All these blessings shall come upon you and overtake you, provided that you heed the voice of the LORD your God.

3You shall be blessed in the city, and blessed shall you be in the field. 4Blessed shall be the fruit of your womb, the produce of your ground, and the offspring of your beasts, the increase of your herds and the young of your flocks. 5Blessed shall be your basket and your kneading bowl. 6You shall be blessed in your coming in, and blessed shall you be in your going out.

7The LORD will cause your enemies who rise against you to be struck down before you; they shall come out against you by one road and flee before you by seven roads. 8The LORD will command the blessing upon your storehouses and upon all the labor of your hands, and He will bless you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

9The LORD will establish you as a people holy to Himself, as He swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in His ways. 10Then all the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of the LORD is proclaimed over you, and they shall stand in awe of you. 11Moreover, the LORD will make you abound in prosperity—in the fruit of your womb, in the offspring of your beasts, and in the produce of your ground—in the land that the LORD swore to your fathers to give you.

12The LORD will open to you His good treasury, the heavens, to give rain upon your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hands; you shall lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow. 13The LORD will make you the head and not the tail; you shall be only above and never below, if you obey the commandments of the LORD your God, which I command you this day, being careful to observe them. 14And do not turn aside, to the right or to the left, from all the words that I command you this day, to go after other gods and serve them.

The Covenant Curses Attending Disobedience

15But if you do not heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep and perform all His commandments and statutes that I enjoin upon you this day, then all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you. 16You shall be cursed in the city, and cursed shall you be in the field. 17Cursed shall be your basket and your kneading bowl. 18Cursed shall be the fruit of your womb, the produce of your ground, the increase of your herds and the young of your flocks. 19You shall be cursed in your coming in, and cursed shall you be in your going out.

20The LORD will send upon you the curse, confusion, and rebuke in every undertaking of your hand, until you are destroyed and perish swiftly, because of the evil of your deeds, whereby you have forsaken Me. 21The LORD will make pestilence cling to you until He has consumed you from the land that you are entering to possess. 22The LORD will smite you with consumption, fever, inflammation, burning heat, sword, blight, and mildew; they shall pursue you until you perish. 23Your heavens shall be brass, and the earth beneath you iron. 24The LORD will make the rain of your land powder and dust; from heaven it shall descend upon you until you are destroyed.

25The LORD will give you up to be defeated before your enemies; you shall go out against them by one road and flee before them by seven roads, and you shall become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth. 26Your carcass shall be food for every bird of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, with none to frighten them away. 27The LORD will smite you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, scabs, and itch, from which you cannot be healed. 28The LORD will smite you with madness, blindness, and dismay of heart. 29You shall grope at noonday as the blind grope in darkness, and you shall not prosper in your ways; you shall be only oppressed and robbed continually, and there shall be no one to save you.

30You shall betroth a wife, but another man shall violate her; you shall build a house, but you shall not dwell in it; you shall plant a vineyard, but you shall not enjoy its fruit. 31Your ox shall be slaughtered before your eyes, but you shall not eat of it; your donkey shall be seized from before you and shall not be restored to you; your sheep shall be given to your enemies, and there shall be no one to save you. 32Your sons and daughters shall be given to another people, while your eyes look on and fail with longing for them all day long, but your hand shall be powerless. 33A people whom you have not known shall eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor, and you shall be only oppressed and crushed continually. 34You shall be driven mad by the sight that your eyes shall see.

35The LORD will smite you with an incurable boil upon your knees and upon your legs, from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head. 36The LORD will drive you and the king whom you set over you to a nation that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you shall serve other gods, of wood and stone. 37You shall become a horror, a proverb, and a byword among all the peoples to whom the LORD shall lead you. 38You shall carry much seed out into the field, but you shall gather in little, for the locust shall consume it. 39You shall plant vineyards and dress them, but you shall neither drink the wine nor gather the grapes, for the worm shall eat them. 40You shall have olive trees throughout all your territory, but you shall not anoint yourself with the oil, for your olives shall drop off.

41You shall beget sons and daughters, but they shall not be yours, for they shall go into captivity. 42The cricket shall possess all your trees and the fruit of your ground. 43The sojourner who is among you shall rise above you higher and higher, while you sink lower and lower. 44He shall lend to you, but you shall not lend to him; he shall be the head, and you shall be the tail.

45All these curses shall come upon you and pursue you and overtake you, until you are destroyed, because you did not heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and statutes that He commanded you. 46They shall be a sign and a wonder upon you and upon your descendants forever. 47Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joyfulness and gladness of heart, when all things abounded, 48therefore you shall serve your enemies whom the LORD shall send against you, in hunger and thirst, in nakedness and in want of all things; and He shall put an iron yoke upon your neck until He has destroyed you.

Siege, Famine, and Exile

49The LORD will bring against you a nation from afar, from the end of the earth, swooping down like an eagle, a nation whose language you do not understand. 50A nation of fierce countenance that does not regard the person of the old or show favor to the young. 51It shall eat the fruit of your beasts and the produce of your ground until you are destroyed, leaving you neither grain, nor wine, nor oil, nor the increase of your herds nor the young of your flocks, until it has caused you to perish.

52It shall besiege you in all your gates, throughout all your land, until your high and fortified walls, in which you trust, come down; it shall besiege you in all your gates throughout all the land that the LORD your God has given you. 53You shall eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of your sons and daughters, whom the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy shall distress you. 54The man who is tender and very delicate among you shall have an evil eye toward his brother, toward the wife of his bosom, and toward the remnant of his children whom he has spared. 55So that he will not give to any of them any of the flesh of his children that he eats, because nothing else is left to him, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy shall distress you in all your gates. 56The woman who is tender and delicate, who would not venture to set the sole of her foot upon the ground out of delicacy and tenderness, shall have an evil eye toward the husband of her bosom, toward her son, and toward her daughter. 57And toward her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet, and toward her children whom she bears, because she shall eat them secretly for want of all things, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy shall distress you in your gates.

The Consummation of the Curses

58If you do not carefully observe all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and awesome Name, the LORD your God, 59then the LORD will make your plagues and the plagues of your descendants extraordinary—great and enduring plagues, and severe and enduring sicknesses. 60He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt, of which you were afraid, and they shall cling to you. 61The LORD will also bring upon you every sickness and every plague that is not written in the book of this law, until you are destroyed.

62Whereas you were as numerous as the stars of heaven, you shall be left few in number, because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God. 63And just as the LORD rejoiced over you to do you good and to multiply you, so the LORD will rejoice over you to destroy you and to bring you to nothing; and you shall be plucked up from the land that you are entering to possess. 64The LORD will scatter you among all peoples, from one end of the earth to the other, and there you shall serve other gods, of wood and stone, which neither you nor your fathers have known. 65Among those nations you shall find no ease, and there shall be no rest for the sole of your foot; but the LORD will give you there a trembling heart, failing eyes, and a despairing soul. 66Your life shall hang in doubt before you; you shall fear night and day and shall have no assurance of your life. 67In the morning you shall say, “Would that it were evening!” and in the evening, “Would that it were morning!”—because of the dread of your heart that you shall dread, and because of the sight of your eyes that you shall see. 68The LORD will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said to you, “You shall never see it again”; and there you shall offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, but there shall be no buyer.
