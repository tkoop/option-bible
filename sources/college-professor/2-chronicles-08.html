Solomon's Building Program and Settlements

1 At the conclusion of the twenty years during which Solomon had erected the house of the LORD and his own palace, 2 he rebuilt the cities that Huram had given him and settled the Israelites in them. 3 Solomon then marched against Hamath-zobah and subjugated it. 4 He built Tadmor in the wilderness, together with all the store-cities he had constructed in Hamath. 5 He also fortified Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, transforming them into walled cities with gates and bars. 6 Moreover, he built Baalath and all the store-cities belonging to Solomon, along with the chariot cities, the cavalry cities, and every other project that Solomon desired to undertake in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout the whole domain of his sovereignty.

Conscription of the Remaining Canaanites

7 All the people who remained from the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—those not belonging to Israel— 8 namely, their descendants who had survived in the land after them, whom the Israelites had not annihilated, Solomon conscripted into forced labor, and they remain subject to it to this day. 9 Yet Solomon did not consign any Israelite to servitude for his labor, because they were warriors, chief officers, commanders of his chariots, and cavalrymen. 10 These were the two hundred fifty chief officers who supervised the people on the king's behalf.

Pharaoh's Daughter and Temple Worship

11 Solomon brought Pharaoh's daughter up from the City of David to the house he had built for her, for he reasoned, "My wife shall not dwell in the palace of David, king of Israel, because the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy." 12 Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD upon the altar of the LORD that he had erected before the portico. 13 He did so day by day, in accordance with the commandment of Moses, for the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the three annual festivals—the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tabernacles. 14 In accordance with the ordinance of David his father, he established the divisions of the priests for their service and the Levites for their duties: to praise and to minister before the priests, as each day required, and the gatekeepers in their divisions at every gate, for such was the command of David, the man of God. 15 Nor did they deviate from the king's injunction to the priests and Levites in any matter, including the treasuries. 16 Thus all of Solomon's work was brought to completion, from the day the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid until the house of the LORD was finished.

The Ophir Expedition

17 Then Solomon traveled to Ezion-geber and Eloth on the seashore of Edom. 18 Huram dispatched ships to him through his servants—mariners who knew the sea—and they sailed with Solomon's servants to Ophir, whence they brought back four hundred fifty talents of gold and delivered them to King Solomon.
