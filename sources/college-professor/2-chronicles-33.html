Manasseh’s Ascension and the Renewal of Idolatry

1 Manasseh ascended the throne at twelve years of age and reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. 2 He perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD, emulating the abominations of the nations that the LORD had dispossessed before the Israelites. 3 He rebuilt the high places that his father Hezekiah had demolished, erected altars to the Baals, fashioned Asherahs, and bowed down to and served the entire host of heaven.

The Profanation of the Temple and Occult Transgressions

4 He even constructed altars within the house of the LORD, concerning which the LORD had declared, “My Name shall be in Jerusalem forever.” 5 In the two courtyards of the LORD’s house he built altars to the whole host of heaven. 6 He consigned his sons to the flames in the Valley of Ben-Hinnom, practiced soothsaying, divination, and sorcery, and consulted mediums and spiritists; by these and kindred abominations he provoked the LORD to anger.

7 He placed the carved image of the idol he had fashioned in the house of God, concerning which God had said to David and to his son Solomon, “In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will establish My Name forever. 8 I will never again uproot Israel from the land I gave to your fathers, provided that you are careful to observe all that I commanded them through Moses—the whole law, the statutes, and the ordinances.” 9 Thus Manasseh led Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem into such delusion that they did more evil than the nations the LORD had destroyed before the Israelites.

Divine Judgment, Penitence, and Restoration

10 The LORD spoke to Manasseh and to his people, but they refused to listen. 11 Therefore the LORD brought against them the commanders of the Assyrian king’s army; they captured Manasseh with hooks, bound him in bronze shackles, and carried him off to Babylon. 12 In his affliction he earnestly sought the favor of the LORD his God and humbled himself profoundly before the God of his fathers. 13 He prayed to Him, and the LORD was moved by his plea; He heard his supplication and restored him to Jerusalem and to his kingdom. Then Manasseh recognized that the LORD is God.

14 Afterward he built an outer wall for the City of David on the west side of Gihon, in the valley, extending to the entrance of the Fish Gate and surrounding the Ophel; he raised it to a great height. He also posted military commanders in all the fortified cities of Judah. 15 He removed the foreign gods and the idol from the house of the LORD, together with all the altars he had built on the temple mount and in Jerusalem, and he cast them outside the city. 16 He restored the altar of the LORD and offered on it sacrifices of peace offerings and thank offerings; he also commanded Judah to serve the LORD, the God of Israel. 17 Nevertheless, the people continued to sacrifice at the high places, though only to the LORD their God.

18 As for the rest of Manasseh’s acts, his prayer to his God, and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, they are recorded in the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 19 His prayer and God’s gracious response to it, along with all his sin and treachery, and the sites where he had built high places and erected Asherahs and carved images before his humiliation—all of this is recorded in the records of the seers.

The Demise of Manasseh and the Wicked Reign of Amon

20 Manasseh died and was buried with his ancestors, and his son Amon succeeded him as king. 21 Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. 22 He did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as his father Manasseh had done; Amon sacrificed to all the carved images his father Manasseh had made, and he served them. 23 Unlike his father Manasseh, he did not humble himself before the LORD; instead, Amon increased his guilt. 24 His servants conspired against him and assassinated him in his own house. 25 But the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and they made his son Josiah king in his place.
