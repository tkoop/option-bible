A Psalm of David: Thanksgiving for the LORD’s Faithfulness

1 I shall render thanksgiving unto You with the entirety of my heart; in the presence of the gods I shall chant praises unto You. 2 I shall prostrate myself toward Your holy sanctuary, and I shall give thanks unto Your name for Your lovingkindness and Your faithfulness, for You have magnified Your word above all Your name.

3 On the day I called, You answered me; You emboldened me with strength within my soul.

The Universal Recognition of the LORD’s Glory

4 All the kings of the earth shall give You thanks, O the LORD, for they have heard the words of Your mouth. 5 And they shall sing of the ways of the LORD, for great is the glory of the LORD. 6 For though the LORD is on high, He regards the lowly, but the haughty He knows from afar.

Assurance of Divine Deliverance and Enduring Mercy

7 Though I walk in the midst of trouble, You will revive me; You will stretch out Your hand against the wrath of my enemies, and Your right hand will save me. 8 The LORD will bring to completion that which concerns me; Your lovingkindness, O the LORD, endures forever; do not forsake the works of Your hands.
