The Tabernacle Curtains and Their Joining

1 You shall make the tabernacle of ten curtains of finely twisted linen, interwoven with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and embroidered with cherubim by the hand of a skilled artisan. 2 Each curtain shall measure twenty-eight cubits in length and four cubits in breadth, every curtain being of one identical measure. 3 Five curtains shall be joined together, edge to edge, and five curtains joined together in like manner. 4 You shall make loops of blue yarn along the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, at the place of joining, and you shall make corresponding loops along the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set. 5 You shall make fifty loops upon the one curtain and fifty upon the edge of the curtain in the second set, the loops being disposed opposite one another. 6 You shall make fifty clasps of gold and join the curtains to one another by means of the clasps, so that the tabernacle becomes a single whole.

The Goat-Hair Tent and Its Coverings

7 You shall make eleven curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle. 8 The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and its breadth four cubits; one measure shall obtain for all eleven curtains. 9 You shall join five curtains together by themselves, and six together by themselves, and you shall double over the sixth curtain at the front of the tent. 10 You shall make fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, and fifty on the edge of the curtain in the second set. 11 You shall make fifty clasps of bronze, insert the clasps into the loops, and join the tent together so that it becomes one. 12 The surplus half-curtain of the tent covering shall hang over the rear of the tabernacle. 13 A cubit on this side and a cubit on that side of the overhanging length shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle, on this side and on that, to cover it. 14 You shall make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and above it a covering of fine leather.

The Acacia Frames, Bases, and Bars

15 You shall make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 16 Each frame shall be ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 17 Each frame shall possess two tenons, fitted together one to the other; you shall make them thus for every frame of the tabernacle. 18 You shall make twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle. 19 You shall make forty silver bases beneath the twenty frames: two bases beneath each frame for its two tenons. 20 For the second side, toward the north, there shall be twenty frames. 21 Their forty silver bases shall be two beneath each frame. 22 For the rear of the tabernacle, toward the west, you shall make six frames. 23 You shall make two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear. 24 They shall be twinned below and joined together at the top by one ring; so shall it be for both corners. 25 Thus there shall be eight frames, with sixteen silver bases, two bases beneath each frame. 26 You shall make five bars of acacia wood for the frames of one side of the tabernacle. 27 Five bars for the frames of the second side, and five for the frames of the rear toward the west. 28 The middle bar shall run through the midst of the frames from end to end. 29 You shall overlay the frames with gold, fashion gold rings to receive the bars, and overlay the bars with gold. 30 You shall erect the tabernacle according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain.

The Veil, Furnishings, and Entrance Screen

31 You shall make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen, with cherubim skillfully worked into it. 32 You shall hang it upon four pillars of acacia wood overlaid with gold, whose hooks are of gold, standing upon four bases of silver. 33 You shall hang the veil beneath the clasps and bring the ark of the Testimony there behind the veil; the veil shall separate for you the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place. 34 You shall put the mercy seat upon the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place. 35 You shall set the table outside the veil on the north side of the tabernacle, and you shall place the lampstand opposite the table on the south side. 36 You shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer. 37 You shall make five pillars of acacia wood for the screen, overlay them with gold, fashion their hooks of gold, and cast five bases of bronze for them.
