Hananiah's False Oracle in the Temple

1 In the fifth month of the fourth year, early in the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, Hananiah son of Azzur, the prophet from Gibeon, spoke to me in the house of the the LORD before the priests and all the people, saying:

2 “Thus says the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: I have shattered the yoke of the king of Babylon. 3 Within two years I shall restore to this place all the vessels of the house of the the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon removed from here and carried off to Babylon. 4 I shall likewise restore to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, together with all the exiles of Judah who were carried to Babylon, declares the the LORD, for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.”

Jeremiah's Prophetic Counter-Address

5 Then the prophet Jeremiah spoke to the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests and all the people who were standing in the house of the the LORD:

6 “Amen! May the the LORD do so; may the the LORD establish the words you have prophesied, bringing back the vessels of the house of the the LORD and all the exiles from Babylon to this place. 7 Nevertheless, hear now this word that I speak in your ears and in the ears of all the people: 8 The prophets who preceded me and you from ancient times prophesied against many lands and great kingdoms—war, calamity, and pestilence. 9 As for the prophet who prophesies peace, only when his word comes to pass shall that prophet be recognized as one whom the the LORD has truly sent.”

The Breaking of the Wooden Yoke

10 Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke from the neck of Jeremiah the prophet and broke it.

11 And Hananiah spoke in the sight of all the people, saying, “Thus says the the LORD: In this very manner, within two years, I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all the nations.” And Jeremiah the prophet went his way.

The Iron Yoke and Hananiah's Sentence

12 After Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke from the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah:

13 “Go and speak to Hananiah, saying, ‘Thus says the the LORD: You have broken wooden yokes, but in their place you have fashioned yokes of iron. 14 For thus says the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: I have placed an iron yoke upon the neck of all these nations, that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon; and they shall serve him. I have given him even the beasts of the field. 15 Listen, Hananiah: The the LORD has not sent you, and you have led this people to trust in a lie. 16 Therefore thus says the the LORD: Behold, I will remove you from the face of the earth. This year you shall die, because you have spoken rebellion against the the LORD.’”

17 In the seventh month of that same year, the prophet Hananiah died.
