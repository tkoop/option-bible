Salutation and Apostolic Commission

1 Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, in accordance with the faith of God’s elect and the knowledge of the truth that accords with godliness, 2 in hope of eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before the ages began, 3 and at the proper time manifested His word through proclamation—a trust committed to me by the command of God our Savior— 4 to Titus, my genuine child in a common faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

Qualifications for Overseers

5 For this reason I left you in Crete, that you might set in order what remained unfinished and appoint elders in every city, just as I directed you, 6 if anyone is above reproach, the husband of one wife, with children who are faithful and not accused of dissipation or insubordination. 7 For an overseer, as God’s steward, must be above reproach—not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not given to wine, not violent, not greedy for dishonest gain— 8 but hospitable, devoted to goodness, self-controlled, upright, holy, and disciplined, 9 clinging to the trustworthy word as taught, so that he may be competent both to exhort by sound doctrine and to refute those who contradict it.

Silencing False Teachers and Restoring Sound Faith

10 For there are many insubordinate people—empty talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision— 11 whose mouths must be silenced, since they subvert entire households by teaching what they ought not, all for sordid gain. 12 One of their own prophets has said, “Cretans are always liars, vicious beasts, lazy gluttons.” 13 This testimony is true; therefore rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith, 14 paying no heed to Jewish myths or to human commandments issued by those who turn away from the truth. 15 To the pure, all things are pure; but to the defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure—indeed, both their mind and their conscience are defiled. 16 They profess to know God, yet by their deeds they deny Him; they are abominable, disobedient, and disqualified for every good work.
