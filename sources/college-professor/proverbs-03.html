Counsel for the Son and the Rewards of Obedience

1 My son, do not forget my instruction, but let your heart safeguard my commandments; 2 for they shall add to you length of days, years of life, and peace. 3 Let not steadfast love and faithfulness abandon you; keep them ever near to your heart, and engrave them upon the tablet of your inner being. 4 Then you shall find favor and sound discernment in the sight of God and of humanity.

5 Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not repose confidence in your own understanding; 6 in every path acknowledge Him, and He will render your ways straight. 7 Do not deem yourself wise in your own judgment; revere the LORD and shun evil. 8 This shall be healing to your flesh and refreshment to your bones.

9 Honor the LORD with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your produce; 10 then your barns shall be filled with plenty, and your vats shall overflow with new wine.

11 My son, do not despise the discipline of the LORD, nor grow weary of His reproof; 12 for whom the LORD loves He disciplines, just as a father delights in the son whom he has chosen.

The Incomparable Worth of Wisdom

13 Blessed is the one who finds wisdom, and the one who acquires understanding; 14 for her gain surpasses the gain of silver, and her yield exceeds fine gold. 15 She is more precious than rubies, and nothing you desire can be set beside her. 16 Long life is in her right hand; in her left are riches and honor. 17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. 18 She is a tree of life to those who seize her, and those who hold her fast are blessed.

Wisdom in Creation and the Security of the Upright

19 The LORD founded the earth by wisdom; He established the heavens by understanding. 20 By His knowledge the deeps were cleft, and the skies distill dew. 21 My son, do not let them slip from your sight; keep prudence and discretion, 22 for they shall be life to your soul and favor to your life. 23 Then you shall walk securely, and you shall not falter. 24 When you lie down, you shall not be afraid; indeed, your sleep shall be sweet. 25 Do not fear sudden terror, nor the calamity that befalls the wicked when it comes; 26 for the LORD shall be your confidence, and He will keep you from the snare.

Ethical Obligations and Divine Retribution

27 Do not withhold good from its rightful claimants when it is in your power to do it. 28 Do not say to your neighbor, "Go, and come back, and tomorrow I will give it," when you already have it with you. 29 Do not devise evil against your neighbor while he dwells beside you in security. 30 Do not contend with anyone without cause, if he has done you no harm. 31 Do not envy a man of violence, and choose none of his ways.

32 For the perverse are an abomination to the LORD, but His intimate counsel is with the upright. 33 The curse of the LORD is upon the house of the wicked, but He blesses the dwelling of the righteous. 34 He scorns the scornful, but gives grace to the humble. 35 The wise shall inherit glory, but fools exalt their own disgrace.
