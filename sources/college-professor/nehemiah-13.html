The Exclusion of Ammonites and Moabites from the Assembly

1 On that day, the Book of Moses was publicly read in the hearing of the people, wherein it was discovered written that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever be admitted into the assembly of God, 2 because they had not met the children of Israel with bread and water, but had hired Balaam to curse them—though our God transformed the curse into a blessing. 3 When they heard the law, they separated from Israel all who were of mixed descent.

Eliashib's Compromise and Nehemiah's Purification of the Temple

4 Now before this, Eliashib the priest, who had been entrusted with the chambers of the house of our God and was closely allied with Tobiah, 5 had prepared for him a capacious chamber in which there had formerly been stored the grain offering, the frankincense, the vessels, the tithe of grain, new wine, and oil—the portions ordained for the Levites, the singers, and the gatekeepers—together with the contribution for the priests. 6 But during all this I was not in Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes, king of Babylon, I had gone to the king, and after some time I requested leave from him. 7 When I returned to Jerusalem, I discerned the evil that Eliashib had done for Tobiah by making him a chamber in the courts of the house of God. 8 It grieved me profoundly, so I cast all of Tobiah's household goods out of the chamber. 9 I issued orders, and they cleansed the chambers; then I restored to that place the vessels of the house of God, together with the grain offering and the frankincense.

10 I also perceived that the Levites had not been given their portions, so the Levites and singers who performed the work had fled, each to his own field. 11 I contended with the officials and said, "Why has the house of God been forsaken?" Then I gathered them together and restored them to their posts. 12 All Judah then brought the tithe of grain, wine, and oil into the storehouses. 13 I appointed over the storehouses Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites, with Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, beside them, because they were reckoned faithful, and their duty was to distribute to their brethren. 14 Remember me, O my God, concerning this, and do not blot out the good deeds I have done for the house of my God and for its services.

Sabbath Reform and the Restriction of Commerce

15 In those days I saw in Judah men treading winepresses on the Sabbath, bringing in heaps of grain and loading them on donkeys, along with wine, grapes, figs, and every kind of burden, which they brought into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day; and I warned them against selling provisions on that day. 16 Men of Tyre also dwelt there, bringing in fish and all kinds of merchandise, and selling them on the Sabbath to the children of Judah, and in Jerusalem.

17 I contended with the nobles of Judah and said to them, "What is this evil thing that you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day? 18 Did not your fathers do the same, and did not our God bring all this disaster upon us and upon this city? Yet you are adding more wrath upon Israel by profaning the Sabbath."

19 And as the gates of Jerusalem grew dark before the Sabbath, I commanded that the doors be shut, and I ordered that they not be opened until after the Sabbath; I stationed some of my servants at the gates, so that no burden might be brought in on the Sabbath day.

20 So the merchants and sellers of all kinds of wares spent the night outside Jerusalem on one or two occasions. 21 I testified against them and said to them, "Why do you camp outside the wall? If you do so again, I will lay hands on you." From that time on they came no more on the Sabbath.

22 I commanded the Levites to purify themselves and to come and guard the gates, in order to keep the Sabbath day holy. Remember me, O my God, concerning this also, and spare me according to the greatness of Your mercy.

Mixed Marriages and the Preservation of Covenant Identity

23 In those days I also saw Jews who had married women of Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab. 24 Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod, and they could not speak the language of Judah, but spoke according to the tongue of each people.

25 I contended with them, cursed them, struck some of them, pulled out their hair, and made them swear by God, saying, "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, nor take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves."

26 "Did not Solomon king of Israel sin on account of these things? Yet among many nations there was no king like him; he was beloved of his God, and God made him king over all Israel—nevertheless, foreign women led him into sin. 27 Shall we then heed you, to do all this great evil, to act treacherously against our God by marrying foreign women?"

28 One of the sons of Joiada, son of Eliashib the high priest, was son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite; therefore I drove him away from me. 29 Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood, the covenant of the priesthood, and the Levites. 30 Thus I purified them from all that is foreign, and I assigned the priests and Levites to their duties, each to his task. 31 I also made provision for the wood offering at the appointed times, and for the firstfruits. Remember me, O my God, for good.
