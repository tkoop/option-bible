The Word of the LORD to Micah

1 The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

The LORD's Coming in Judgment

2 Hear, all peoples; listen, O earth and all that is in it. Let the Lord, the LORD, be a witness against you—the Lord from His holy temple. 3 For behold, the LORD is coming out of His place; He will come down and tread upon the high places of the earth. 4 The mountains will melt beneath Him, and the valleys will split apart, like wax before the fire, like water poured down a slope. 5 All this is for the transgression of Jacob and for the sins of the house of Israel. What is the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what is the high place of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

The Devastation of Samaria

6 “Therefore I will make Samaria a heap of rubble in the open field, a planting place for vineyards; I will pour out her stones into the valley and expose her foundations. 7 All her carved images will be smashed to pieces; all her wages will be burned in the fire; and I will lay waste all her idols. Because she gathered them from a prostitute's wages, they will return to a prostitute's wages.”

Micah's Lament and the Doom of the Cities

8 Because of this I will lament and wail; I will go barefoot and naked. I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich. 9 For her wound is incurable, for it has reached Judah; it has come to the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem. 10 Do not tell it in Gath; do not weep at all. Roll in the dust at Beth-le-aphrah. 11 Pass on, O inhabitants of Shaphir, in naked shame. The inhabitant of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel mourns; its support is taken from you. 12 For the inhabitant of Maroth waits in anguish for good, but disaster has come down from the LORD to the gate of Jerusalem. 13 Harness the chariot to the swift steed, O inhabitant of Lachish. She was the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion, for the transgressions of Israel were found in you. 14 Therefore you shall give parting gifts to Moresheth-gath. The houses of Achzib will be a deception to the kings of Israel. 15 I will yet bring a conqueror against you, O inhabitant of Mareshah; the glory of Israel will come to Adullam. 16 Make yourself bald and cut off your hair for the children you delight in; enlarge your baldness like the eagle, for they have gone into exile from you.
