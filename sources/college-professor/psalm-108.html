A Steadfast Heart and a Confident Prayer

1 A song, a psalm of David. My heart is steadfast, O God; I will sing and make music—indeed, with all my glory. 2 Awake, O harp and lyre! I will rouse the dawn. 3 I will extol You, O the LORD, among the peoples; I will sing Your praises among the nations. 4 For Your steadfast love is exalted above the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches unto the clouds. 5 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens, and let Your glory be above all the earth. 6 Answer me, and save with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

The Divine Oracle of Conquest

7 God has spoken in His holiness: “I will exult; I will apportion Shechem and measure out the Valley of Succoth. 8 Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is the helmet upon My head; Judah is My scepter. 9 Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I will cast My sandal; over Philistia I will shout in triumph.”

A Petition for Help Against the Foe

10 Who will conduct me into the fortified city? Who will guide me unto Edom? 11 Have You not rejected us, O God? And will You not march forth with our armies? 12 Grant us succor against the adversary, for vain is the salvation of man. 13 Through God we shall do valiantly, and He will tread down our adversaries.
