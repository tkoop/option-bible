The Collapse of Babylon's Idols

1 Bel has bowed down, Nebo stoops; their idols are laid upon beasts and cattle. The burdens you bear are onerous, a load for the weary beast. 2 They stoop and bow down together; they are powerless to deliver the burden, and their very selves have gone into captivity.

The The LORD's Sustaining Care for His People

3 “Listen to Me, O house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, you who have been borne from the belly and carried from the womb: 4 Even to your old age I am He, and even to your gray hairs I will bear you; I have made you, and I will bear you; I will carry you and deliver you.

The Vanity of Idolatry

5 “To whom, then, will you liken Me, or make Me equal, or compare Me, that we should be alike? 6 They lavish gold from the purse, weigh out silver on the scale, employ a goldsmith, who fashions it into a god; then they bow down and worship. 7 They lift it to the shoulder, carry it, set it in its place, and there it stands; it does not budge from its spot. One cries to it, yet it does not answer or save him from his distress.

The The LORD's Sovereign Purpose and Salvation

8 “Remember this, and be resolute; lay it to heart, you transgressors. 9 Remember the former things of old; for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like Me, 10 declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times things not yet done, saying, ‘My counsel shall stand, and all My pleasure I will accomplish,’ 11 summoning a bird of prey from the east, the man of My counsel from a far country. Indeed, I have spoken, and I will bring it to pass; I have purposed, and I will do it.

12 “Listen to Me, you who are stout of heart, you who are far from righteousness: 13 I bring near My righteousness; it shall not be far off, and My salvation shall not delay; and I will put salvation in Zion for Israel, My glory.”
