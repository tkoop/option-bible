The Superscription and the Bride's Opening Longing

1 The Song of Songs, which is Solomon's.

2 "Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth, for your love is more delectable than wine. 3 Your ointments exhale a fragrance most exquisite; your name itself is as oil poured forth; therefore the maidens cherish you. 4 Draw me after you, and we shall run; the king has conducted me into his chambers. We shall exult and take delight in you; we shall extol your love above wine; with rectitude do they cherish you."

The Bride's Self-Portrayal and Her Search for the Shepherd

5 "I am dark, yet comely, O daughters of Jerusalem—like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon. 6 Do not stare at me because I am swarthy, because the sun has scorched me. My mother's sons were incensed against me; they appointed me keeper of the vineyards, yet my own vineyard I have not kept."

7 "Tell me, you whom my soul loves, where you pasture your flock, where you make it lie down at noon; for why should I be like one who veils herself beside the flocks of your companions?"

The Bridegroom's Counsel and Praise, with the Companions' Promise and the Bride's Remembrance

8 "If you do not know, O fairest among women, go forth, follow in the tracks of the flock, and pasture your young goats beside the shepherds' tents."

9 "I compare you, my love, to a mare among Pharaoh's chariots. 10 Your cheeks are comely with ornaments, your neck with strings of jewels."

11 "We will make you circlets of gold studded with silver."

12 "While the king was upon his couch, my nard diffused its fragrance. 13 My beloved is to me a sachet of myrrh resting between my breasts. 14 My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms in the vineyards of En-gedi."

Mutual Admiration and the Verdant Couch of Love

15 "Behold, you are beautiful, my love; behold, you are beautiful; your eyes are doves."

16 "Behold, you are handsome, my beloved, truly delightful. Our couch is verdant. 17 The beams of our house are cedars; our rafters are firs."
