The Wedding at Cana and the Revelation of Glory

1 On the third day a wedding feast was celebrated in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was in attendance there. 2 Jesus Himself, moreover, was invited to the nuptials, together with His disciples.

3 When the wine failed, the mother of Jesus said to Him, “They have no wine.”

4 Jesus said to her, “Woman, why does this concern you and Me? My hour has not yet arrived.”

5 His mother said to the servants, “Do whatever He tells you.”

6 Now six stone water jars were set there in accordance with the Jewish rites of purification, each holding two or three measures. 7 Jesus said to them, “Fill the jars with water.” And they filled them to the brim. 8 Then He said to them, “Draw some out now and carry it to the master of the banquet.” And they carried it.

9 When the master of the banquet had tasted the water that had become wine, not knowing whence it came—though the servants who had drawn the water knew—the master of the banquet summoned the bridegroom. 10 He said to him, “Everyone first sets out the good wine, and when the guests have drunk freely, the inferior; but you have kept the good wine until now.”

11 This, the first of His signs, Jesus performed in Cana of Galilee; He manifested His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.

12 Afterward He descended to Capernaum with His mother, His brothers, and His disciples, and they abode there but a few days.

Zeal for the Temple and the Sign of Resurrection

13 The Passover of the Jews was at hand, and Jesus ascended to Jerusalem. 14 In the temple He found those who were selling oxen, sheep, and doves, and the money changers seated there.

15 And having made a scourge of cords, He drove them all, together with the sheep and oxen, out of the temple; He poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.

16 To the dove sellers He said, “Take these things hence; do not make My Father’s house a house of trade!”

17 His disciples remembered that it is written, “Zeal for Your house will consume Me.”

18 The Jews therefore answered and said to Him, “What sign do You show us, that You do these things?”

19 Jesus answered and said to them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”

20 The Jews then said, “This temple was built in forty-six years, and You will raise it up in three days?”

21 But He was speaking concerning the temple of His body. 22 When, therefore, He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had said this, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus had spoken.

Signs, Belief, and the Knowledge of Christ

23 Now while He was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many, beholding the signs which He did, believed in His name.

24 But Jesus Himself did not commit Himself to them, because He knew all men. 25 He had no need that anyone should bear witness concerning man, for He Himself knew what was in man.
