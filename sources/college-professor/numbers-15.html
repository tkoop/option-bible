Regulations for Sacrificial Fire Offerings in the Promised Land

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: ‘When you enter the land I am giving you as your dwelling, 3 and you present to the LORD an offering by fire—whether a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a vow, as a freewill offering, or at your appointed times—to produce a pleasing aroma to the LORD, from the herd or from the flock, 4 then the one presenting his offering to the LORD shall also present a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one-fourth of a hin of oil. 5 With the burnt offering or sacrifice of each lamb, you are to prepare one-fourth of a hin of wine as a drink offering. 6 Or for a ram, you shall prepare a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one-third of a hin of oil, 7 and one-third of a hin of wine as a drink offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

8 “‘When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice, to fulfill a vow or as a peace offering to the LORD, 9 you shall present with it a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one-half of a hin of oil, 10 and one-half of a hin of wine as a drink offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 11 This is to be done for each bull, ram, lamb, or goat. 12 You shall prepare them in proportion to the number you offer. 13 Every native-born Israelite shall do these things in this way when presenting an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 14 And if a sojourner resides among you, or whoever is among you throughout your generations, and he wishes to present an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, he shall do as you do. 15 As for the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the sojourner who resides among you, a perpetual statute throughout your generations; as you are, so shall the sojourner be before the LORD. 16 There is to be one law and one ordinance for you and for the sojourner who resides among you.’”

The Firstfruits Offering, Inadvertent Sin, and Defiant Transgression

17 Then the LORD said to Moses, 18 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: ‘When you enter the land to which I am bringing you, 19 and you eat of the bread of the land, you shall lift up an offering to the LORD. 20 From the first of your dough you shall lift up a cake as an offering; you shall lift it up as an offering from the threshing floor. 21 Throughout your generations, you shall give this offering to the LORD from the first of your dough.

22 “‘And if you stray inadvertently and do not obey all these commandments that the LORD has spoken to Moses— 23 all that the LORD has commanded you through Moses, from the day the LORD gave the commandments and onward throughout your generations— 24 and if it is done inadvertently without the knowledge of the congregation, then the whole congregation shall offer one young bull as a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, with its grain offering and its drink offering according to the ordinance, and one male goat as a sin offering. 25 The priest shall make atonement for the whole congregation of Israel, so that they may be forgiven, for it was inadvertent, and they have brought their offering, an offering by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD for their inadvertent sin. 26 Then the whole congregation of Israel and the sojourner who resides among them shall be forgiven, because all the people acted inadvertently.

27 “‘Also, if one person sins inadvertently, he shall present a year-old female goat as a sin offering. 28 The priest shall make atonement before the LORD for the person who erred, when he sinned inadvertently, to make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven. 29 You shall have one law for the native-born and for the sojourner who resides among you, for anyone who acts inadvertently. 30 But the person who acts defiantly, whether native-born or sojourner, blasphemes the LORD; that person shall be cut off from among his people. 31 Because he has despised the word of the LORD and broken His commandment, that person shall be utterly cut off; his guilt remains upon him.’”

The Sabbath Wood-Gatherer and the Tassels of Remembrance

32 While the Israelites were in the wilderness, a man was found gathering wood on the Sabbath day. 33 Those who found him gathering wood brought him before Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation. 34 They put him in custody, because it had not been declared what should be done to him.

35 Then the LORD said to Moses, “The man must be put to death; the whole congregation must stone him outside the camp.” 36 So the whole congregation took him outside the camp and stoned him to death, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

37 Then the LORD said to Moses, 38 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: ‘Throughout your generations, you are to make tassels on the corners of your garments, with a blue cord on each tassel. 39 These will serve as tassels for you to look at, so that you may remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and not stray after your own heart and your own eyes, by which you are led astray. 40 Then you will remember and obey all My commandments, and you will be holy to your God. 41 I am the LORD your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God.’”
