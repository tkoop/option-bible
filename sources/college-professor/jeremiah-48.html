The Oracle Against Moab

1 Concerning Moab, thus declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Woe to Nebo, for it is laid waste; Kiriathaim is seized; Misgab is humiliated and confounded. 2 Moab's renown is no more; in Heshbon they plot calamity against her, saying, “Come, let us eradicate her as a nation.” You likewise, Madmen, shall be hushed; the sword shall pursue you. 3 A cry of lamentation rises from Horonaim: devastation and immense destruction. 4 Moab is shattered; her little ones raise a wail. 5 For the ascent of Luhith is accompanied by weeping upon weeping; for at the descent of Horonaim the adversaries have heard the cry of ruin.

6 Flee, preserve your lives, and resemble a desolate shrub in the wilderness. 7 For because you reposed trust in your works and in your treasures, you too shall be seized; Chemosh shall go into exile together with his priests and his princes. 8 The devastator shall assail every city, and no city shall elude him; the valley shall perish, and the plain shall be laid waste, as the LORD has spoken. 9 Bestow wings upon Moab, for she shall surely take flight; her cities shall become a desolation, devoid of inhabitants. 10 Cursed is he who executes the work of the LORD with negligence, and cursed is he who restrains his sword from blood.

Moab's Complacency and the Coming Devastation

11 Moab has been tranquil from his youth and has settled upon his lees; he has not been poured from vessel to vessel, nor has he gone into exile; therefore his savor remains in him, and his scent is unaltered. 12 Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send to him pourers, and they shall pour him out; they shall empty his vessels and shatter his jars. 13 Then Moab shall be ashamed of Chemosh, as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their confidence. 14 How can you assert, “We are mighty men, valiant men of war”? 15 Moab is devastated, and her cities have been invaded; her chosen young men have descended to the slaughter, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts.

16 Moab's calamity is imminent, and his disaster hastens exceedingly. 17 Lament for him, all you who dwell around him; all you who know his name. Say, “How broken is the mighty staff, the glorious rod!” 18 Descend from your glory, and sit in thirst, O inhabitant of Daughter Dibon; for the destroyer of Moab has come against you; he has destroyed your fortresses.

19 Stand by the way and keep watch, O inhabitant of Aroer; inquire of him who flees and of her who escapes; say, “What has happened?” 20 Moab is humiliated, for it is broken; wail and cry out! Proclaim in Arnon that Moab is devastated. 21 And judgment has come upon the land of the plain, upon Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath; 22 upon Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim; 23 upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon; 24 and upon Kerioth, Bozrah, and all the cities of the land of Moab, far and near. 25 The horn of Moab is cut off, and his arm is broken, declares the LORD.

The Humiliation, Mourning, and Ruin of Moab

26 Intoxicate him, for he magnified himself against the LORD; Moab shall wallow in his vomit, and he too shall become a laughingstock. 27 For was not Israel a derision to you? Was he found among thieves? For whenever you speak concerning him, you shake your head. 28 Abandon the cities, and dwell in the rock, O inhabitants of Moab, and be like the dove that nests in the clefts of a gorge. 29 We have heard of the pride of Moab—he is exceedingly proud—of his haughtiness, his pride, his arrogance, and the insolence of his heart. 30 I know his insolence, declares the LORD, but it is not right; his boasts have not availed.

31 Therefore I will wail for Moab; I will cry out for all Moab; for the men of Kir-heres I will mourn. 32 I will weep for you, O vine of Sibmah, with the weeping of Jazer; your tendrils crossed the sea, they reached to the sea of Jazer; upon your summer fruit and your vintage the destroyer has fallen. 33 Gladness and joy have been removed from the fruitful field and from the land of Moab; I have caused the wine to cease from the winepresses; no one treads with shouts of joy; the shout is not a shout of joy. 34 From the outcry of Heshbon to Elealeh, to Jahaz they lift their voice; from Zoar to Horonaim, to Eglath-shelishiyah; for the waters of Nimrim also shall become desolations. 35 And I will cause to cease in Moab, declares the LORD, him who ascends the high place and him who burns incense to his god.

36 Therefore My heart moans like flutes for Moab, and My heart moans like flutes for the men of Kir-heres; therefore the abundance he made has perished. 37 For every head is bald, and every beard is clipped; upon all hands are gashes, and upon the loins sackcloth. 38 On all the rooftops of Moab and in her streets there is lamentation everywhere; for I have broken Moab like a vessel in which there is no delight, declares the LORD. 39 How it is shattered! They wail. How Moab has turned his back in shame! So Moab shall be a derision and a terror to all who are around him.

Final Destruction and Eventual Restoration

40 For thus says the LORD: Behold, he shall fly like an eagle, and shall spread his wings over Moab. 41 Kerioth is taken, and the strongholds are seized; and the heart of the mighty men of Moab in that day shall be as the heart of a woman in travail. 42 And Moab shall be destroyed from being a people, because he magnified himself against the LORD. 43 Terror, and the pit, and the snare are upon you, O inhabitant of Moab, declares the LORD. 44 He who flees from the terror shall fall into the pit, and he who climbs out of the pit shall be caught in the snare; for I will bring upon her, upon Moab, the year of their visitation, declares the LORD. 45 In the shadow of Heshbon the fugitives stand powerless; for fire has gone out from Heshbon, and a flame from the midst of Sihon, and it has devoured the corner of Moab, and the crown of the sons of tumult. 46 Woe to you, Moab! The people of Chemosh has perished; for your sons are taken captive, and your daughters into captivity. 47 Yet I will restore the fortunes of Moab in the latter days, declares the LORD. Thus far is the judgment of Moab.
