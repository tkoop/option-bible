A Psalm of David: Mercy, Sickness, and the Treachery of Friends

1 For the choirmaster—a psalm of David. Blessed is the one who exhibits prudent discernment toward the indigent; the LORD will deliver him in the day of adversity. 2 The LORD will guard him and preserve his life; he will be acclaimed as blessed in the land, and You will not surrender him to the machinations of his adversaries. 3 The LORD will sustain him upon a bed of sickness; You have transformed his entire couch throughout his illness.

4 As for me, I said, "the LORD, be gracious to me; heal my soul, for I have sinned against You!"

5 My enemies speak malevolently concerning me: "When will he die, and his name perish?" 6 And if one comes to visit me, he utters falsehood; his heart hoards iniquity within itself; when he departs, he disseminates it. 7 All who hate me conspire in whispers against me; against me they devise calamity.

8 "A deadly plague is poured out upon him, and now that he lies down, he will not rise again." 9 Even my intimate friend, in whom I trusted, who shared my bread, has betrayed me.

10 But You, the LORD, be gracious to me, and raise me up, that I may requite them! 11 By this I know that You delight in me: my enemy will not shout in triumph over me. 12 But You have upheld me because of my integrity, and You have established me in Your presence forever.

13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! Amen and Amen.
