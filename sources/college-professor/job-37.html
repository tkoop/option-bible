Elihu Extols the Thunder and Sovereignty of God

"1 At this my heart likewise trembles and leaps from its place. 2 Listen attentively to the thunder of His voice and to the rumbling that issues from His mouth. 3 He sends it forth beneath the whole heaven, and His lightning to the very ends of the earth. 4 Afterward a voice roars; He thunders with the majesty of His voice, and He does not restrain the lightning when His voice is heard. 5 God thunders marvelously with His voice; He does great things that we do not know.

"6 For He commands the snow, 'Fall upon the earth,' and the rain shower, 'Be a mighty downpour.' 7 He seals the hand of every person, so that all mankind may recognize His work. 8 Then the beasts retreat into their lairs and remain in their dens. 9 From its chamber comes the tempest, and cold from the scattering winds. 10 By the breath of God ice is formed, and the broad waters are frozen.

"11 He also burdens the thick cloud with moisture and scatters His lightning through the clouds. 12 And it turns about at His direction, that they may do whatever He commands them upon the face of the inhabited earth. 13 Whether for correction, for His land, or for mercy, He brings it to pass.

Job Is Summoned to Contemplate Divine Wonders

"14 Hear this, O Job; stand still and consider the wondrous works of God. 15 Do you know how God ordains them, and causes the light of His cloud to shine? 16 Do you know the balancing of the clouds, the wondrous works of Him who is perfect in knowledge? 17 Your garments grow warm when the earth is stilled by the south wind.

"18 Can you, with Him, spread out the skies, strong as a cast metal mirror? 19 Teach us what we should say to Him; we cannot arrange our case because of darkness. 20 Shall it be reported to Him that I would speak? If a man speaks, surely he will be swallowed up.

The Unsearchable Glory of the Almighty

"21 And now they do not see the light, though it is bright in the skies; but the wind passes and clears them. 22 From the north comes golden splendor; God is clothed with awesome majesty. 23 The Almighty is beyond our reach; He is exalted in power; and in justice and abundant righteousness He does not oppress. 24 Therefore men fear Him; He does not regard any who are wise of heart."
