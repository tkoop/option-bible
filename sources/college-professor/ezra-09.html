The Leaders’ Report of Israel’s Unlawful Intermarriage

1 Once these matters had been brought to completion, the leaders drew near to me and declared, “Neither the people of Israel nor the priests and the Levites have kept themselves distinct from the peoples of the lands in respect to their abominations—namely, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians, and the Amorites.” 2 For they have taken some of their daughters as wives for themselves and for their sons, so that the holy lineage has become commingled with the peoples of the lands; indeed, the hand of the officials and governors has been foremost in this trespass.

Ezra’s Grief and the Assembly of the God-Fearing

3 When I heard this report, I tore my garment and my robe, plucked hair from my head and my beard, and sat down in stunned desolation. 4 Then all who trembled at the words of the God of Israel gathered to me because of the unfaithfulness of the exiles, while I remained seated in appalled silence until the evening oblation.

Ezra’s Penitential Prayer at the Evening Offering

5 At the time of the evening oblation, I arose from my fasting, with my garment and robe torn, fell upon my knees, and spread out my hands to the LORD my God.

6 I said, “My God, I am ashamed and abashed to lift up my face before You, my God, for our iniquities have multiplied beyond measure and our guilt has ascended to the heavens. 7 From the days of our ancestors to this very day we have been in great guilt; and because of our iniquities, we—our kings and our priests—have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, to plunder, and to open disgrace, as is evident this day. 8 Yet now, for a brief moment, grace has been shown us by the LORD our God, leaving us a remnant and granting us a secure foothold in His holy place, so that our God may afford us a modest measure of revival in our servitude. 9 For although we are servants, our God has not forsaken us in our bondage; rather, He has extended steadfast love before the kings of Persia, granting us preservation, restoring the house of our God, repairing its ruins, and establishing a protective bulwark in Judah and Jerusalem.”

10 “And now, our God, what can we say after all this? For we have forsaken Your commandments. 11 which You commanded through Your servants the prophets, saying, ‘The land that you are entering to possess is a land polluted by the impurity of the peoples of the lands, by their abominations with which they have filled it from end to end in their uncleanness.’ 12 Therefore do not give your daughters to their sons, nor take their daughters for your sons, and never seek their welfare or prosperity, so that you may grow strong and enjoy the good of the land and leave it as an inheritance to your sons forever. 13 After all that has come upon us because of our evil deeds and our great guilt, You, our God, have punished us less than our iniquities deserve and have given us such a remnant as this. 14 Shall we again break Your commandments and intermarry with the peoples who practice these abominations? Would You not then be angry with us until You had consumed us, leaving no remnant or survivor? 15 The LORD, the God of Israel, You are righteous, for we are left a remnant as we are this day. Behold, we stand before You in our guilt, for no one can stand in Your presence on account of this.”
