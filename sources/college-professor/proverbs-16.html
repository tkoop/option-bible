Divine Sovereignty over Human Designs and Conduct

1 The designs of the human heart are a mortal prerogative, yet the utterance of the tongue is granted by the LORD. 2 Every course a person elects appears immaculate in his own estimation, but the LORD weighs the motives. 3 Entrust your undertakings to the LORD, and your designs shall be firmly established.

4 The LORD has fashioned all things for His own end; even the wicked are reserved for the day of calamity. 5 Every proud-hearted individual is an abomination to the LORD; rest assured, such a one shall not evade retribution. 6 Through steadfast love and fidelity, iniquity is expiated; and through reverence for the LORD, one turns away from evil.

7 When a person's conduct pleases the LORD, He reconciles even that person's enemies to him. 8 Better a modest portion conjoined with righteousness than abundant revenue procured through injustice. 9 A mortal may chart his course in his heart, yet the LORD determines his steps.

Kingship, Justice, and Reverence for the LORD

10 A divine verdict rests upon a king's lips; in judgment, his mouth must not prove treacherous. 11 Just balances and scales belong to the LORD; all the weights in the pouch are His concern. 12 Wicked conduct is an abomination to kings, for a throne is secured through righteousness. 13 Righteous speech is a king's delight, and the one who speaks with integrity is beloved.

14 A king's wrath is a harbinger of death, yet a wise person will propitiate it. 15 In the radiance of a king's countenance there is life; His favor is like a cloud bringing the late spring rain. 16 How far superior it is to acquire wisdom rather than gold, and to choose discernment rather than silver!

The Divergent Ways of Wisdom and Folly

17 The highway of the upright veers away from evil; whoever guards his course preserves his life. 18 Pride precedes ruin, and an arrogant spirit precedes a downfall. 19 Better to be humble in spirit among the oppressed than to divide spoil with the arrogant.

20 Whoever gives heed to instruction prospers, and blessed is the one who trusts in the LORD. 21 The wise of heart are deemed discerning, and gracious speech augments learning. 22 Discernment is a wellspring of life to its possessor, but the chastisement of fools is mere folly. 23 The heart of the wise renders his speech judicious, and his lips further instruction. 24 Gracious words are a honeycomb: sweet to the soul and medicinal to the bones.

25 There is a path that appears upright to a mortal, yet its terminus is the way of death. 26 A laborer's appetite works on his behalf, because his hunger impels him onward. 27 A scoundrel excavates evil, and his speech is as a scorching fire. 28 A perverse person sows strife, and a whisperer alienates intimate friends. 29 A violent person seduces his neighbor and conducts him along a path that is not good.

30 He who winks his eye contrives perversities; he who purses his lips brings evil to pass. 31 Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is found along the path of righteousness. 32 Better is the patient than the warrior, and he who rules his spirit surpasses one who captures a city. 33 The lot may be cast into the lap, yet its every determination proceeds from the LORD.
