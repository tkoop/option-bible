The Withdrawal of Judah’s Supports

1 For behold, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, is withdrawing from Jerusalem and from Judah both prop and stay—every prop of bread and every prop of water— 2 the warrior and the man of war, the judge and the prophet, the diviner and the elder, 3 the commander of fifty and the man of distinction, the counselor, the consummate craftsman, and the adept in incantations. 4 I will appoint callow youths as their princes, and petulant children shall rule over them. 5 The people will be oppressed, one by another and each by his neighbor; the youth will behave insolently toward the elder, and the despised toward the honorable.

6 A man will seize his brother in his father’s house, saying, “You have a garment; you shall be our leader, and this ruin shall be under your hand.”

7 In that day he will lift up his voice, saying, “I will not be a healer; in my house there is neither bread nor garment; you shall not make me leader of the people.”

8 For Jerusalem has stumbled and Judah has fallen, because their tongue and their deeds are against the LORD, defying the radiance of His glory. 9 The cast of their countenance testifies against them; they proclaim their sin as Sodom does and do not conceal it. Woe to them, for they have brought calamity upon themselves! 10 Say of the righteous that it shall be well with them, for they shall eat the fruit of their doings. 11 Woe to the wicked! It shall be ill with him, for the recompense of his hands shall be rendered to him. 12 As for My people, children are their taskmasters, and women rule over them. O My people, your guides lead you astray and have swallowed up the course of your paths.

The LORD’s Indictment of the Nation’s Leaders

13 The LORD arises to plead His cause and stands to judge the peoples. 14 The LORD will enter into judgment with the elders and princes of His people: “It is you who have devoured the vineyard; the plunder of the poor is in your houses. 15 What do you mean by crushing My people and grinding the faces of the poor?” says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

Judgment upon the Daughters of Zion

16 Moreover, the LORD says: “Because the daughters of Zion are haughty and walk with outstretched necks, glancing wantonly with their eyes, mincing along as they go, tinkling with their feet, 17 therefore the Lord will bring sores upon the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and the LORD will lay bare their secret parts.”

18 In that day the Lord will take away the finery of the anklets, the headbands, and the crescents; 19 the pendants, the bracelets, and the veils; 20 the headdresses, the armlets, the sashes, the perfume boxes, and the charms; 21 the rings and the nose rings; 22 the festal robes, the mantles, the cloaks, and the handbags; 23 the mirrors, the linen garments, the turbans, and the veils.

24 Instead of perfume there will be stench; instead of a belt, a rope; instead of well-set hair, baldness; instead of a rich robe, a girding of sackcloth; and branding instead of beauty. 25 Your men shall fall by the sword, and your mighty men in battle. 26 And her gates shall lament and mourn; and she, desolate, shall sit upon the ground.
