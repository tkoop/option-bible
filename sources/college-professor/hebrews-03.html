The Superior Glory of Christ over Moses

1 Consequently, holy brothers, fellow participants in a heavenly summons, fix your attention upon Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession, 2 who proved faithful to Him who appointed Him, even as Moses was throughout His entire household. 3 For this One has been deemed worthy of glory surpassing that of Moses, inasmuch as the founder of a household commands greater esteem than the household itself. 4 For every dwelling is constructed by someone, yet the Architect of all things is God. 5 Indeed, Moses was faithful throughout God's household as a servant, bearing witness to the things destined to be declared afterward, 6 whereas Christ is faithful as a Son over His household; and we ourselves constitute that household, provided we retain our boldness and the exultation of our hope steadfast to the end.

The Holy Spirit's Admonition from Israel's Wilderness Provocation

7 Accordingly, as the Holy Spirit declares, "Today, if you heed His voice, 8 do not harden your hearts as in the provocation, on the day of trial in the wilderness, 9 where your fathers tested Me and tried Me, and beheld My works for forty years. 10 Therefore I was indignant with that generation, and I said, 'They are perpetually wandering in their heart; they have not come to know My ways.' 11 Hence I swore in My wrath, 'They shall not enter My rest.'"

Guarding Against an Unbelieving Heart

12 Take heed, brothers, lest an evil heart of unbelief should arise in any one of you, turning away from the living God. 13 Rather, exhort one another day by day, as long as it is called 'Today,' so that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin. 14 For we have become partakers of Christ, if we hold fast the beginning of our confidence firm to the end, 15 as it is said, "Today, if you heed His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation." 16 For who, after hearing, rebelled? Was it not indeed all those who came out of Egypt under Moses? 17 And with whom was He indignant for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness? 18 And to whom did He swear that they should not enter His rest, if not to the disobedient? 19 Thus we perceive that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.
