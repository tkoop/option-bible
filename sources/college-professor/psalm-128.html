A Song of Ascents: The Felicity of the God-Fearing Household

1 A Song of Ascents. Blessed is every one who fears the LORD, who walks in His ways. 2 You shall eat the fruit of your toil; you shall be blessed, and it shall be well with you. 3 Your wife shall be like a fruitful vine within the innermost recesses of your house, and your children like olive saplings encircling your table. 4 Behold, thus is the man blessed who fears the LORD.

Benediction from Zion and the Peace of Israel

5 The LORD bless you from Zion, that you may behold the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life. 6 And may you behold your children’s children. Peace be upon Israel.
