A Penitential Lament of David amid Sickness and Reproach

1 O the LORD, do not rebuke me in Your wrath, nor chasten me in Your blazing anger. 2 For Your arrows have transfixed me, and Your hand has descended heavily upon me.

3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of Your indignation; there is no peace in my bones because of my sin. 4 For my iniquities have surged above my head; like an unbearable burden, they weigh me down. 5 My wounds have putrefied and festered because of my folly.

6 I am bent double and bowed down exceedingly; all day long I walk about in mourning. 7 For my loins are inflamed with affliction, and there is no soundness in my flesh. 8 I am utterly enfeebled and crushed; I groan because of the tumult of my heart.

9 O Lord, all my yearning is before You, and my sighing is not concealed from You. 10 My heart throbs, my strength has forsaken me, and even the light of my eyes has failed.

11 My friends and companions stand aloof from my affliction, and my kinsmen keep their distance. 12 Those who seek my life set snares; those who pursue my ruin speak of devastation and meditate deceit all day long.

13 But I am like a deaf man who does not hear, and like a mute who does not open his mouth. 14 Thus I have become as one who hears nothing, and in whose mouth there is no reproof.

15 For in You, O the LORD, I wait in hope; You will answer, O Lord my God. 16 For I have said, “Lest they rejoice over me; when my foot slips, they magnify themselves against me.” 17 For I am ready to fall, and my pain is continually before me.

18 For I will declare my iniquity; I am anxious because of my sin. 19 But my enemies are vigorous and strong, and those who hate me without cause are multiplied. 20 Those who repay evil for good are hostile to me, because I pursue what is good.

21 Do not forsake me, O the LORD; O my God, do not be far from me. 22 Make haste to my aid, O Lord, my salvation.
