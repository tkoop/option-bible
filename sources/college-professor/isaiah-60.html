Zion’s Radiant Dawn and the Ingathering of the Nations

1 Arise and be illumined, for your light has arrived, and the glory of the the LORD has dawned upon you. 2 For behold, darkness shall enshroud the earth, and a dense gloom the peoples; yet the the LORD shall arise upon you, and His glory shall be manifested upon you.

3 Nations shall journey toward your light, and kings toward the radiance of your dawn. 4 Lift up your eyes and behold: all are gathered together; they come to you. Your sons shall arrive from afar, and your daughters shall be borne upon the hip.

The Wealth of the Nations and Zion’s Radiance

5 Then you shall perceive and radiate; your heart shall tremble and dilate with joy, because the fullness of the sea shall be turned toward you, and the wealth of the nations shall come to you. 6 A profusion of camels shall overspread you—the young camels of Midian and Ephah; all of them from Sheba shall come. They shall bear gold and frankincense, and shall proclaim the praises of the the LORD.

7 All the flocks of Kedar shall be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth shall serve you. They shall ascend upon My altar as an acceptable offering, and I will adorn the house of My glory. 8 Who are these that fly like a cloud, and like doves to their windows?

9 Surely the coastlands shall wait for Me, and the ships of Tarshish first, to bring your sons from afar, together with their silver and gold, for the name of the the LORD your God, and for the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you. 10 Foreigners shall rebuild your walls, and their kings shall serve you; for in My wrath I struck you, yet in My favor I have shown you mercy. 11 Your gates shall remain open continually; they shall not be shut by day or by night, so that the wealth of the nations may be brought to you, with their kings led in procession. 12 For the nation and kingdom that will not serve you shall perish; indeed, those nations shall be utterly desolated.

Everlasting Peace, Light, and Righteous Glory

13 The glory of Lebanon shall come to you—the cypress, the plane tree, and the pine—together to adorn the place of My sanctuary, and I will glorify the place of My feet. 14 The sons of your oppressors shall come bending low to you, and all who despised you shall bow down at the soles of your feet; and they shall call you the City of the the LORD, the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

15 Whereas you were forsaken and hated, with no passerby, I will make you an everlasting majesty, a joy from generation to generation. 16 You shall suck the milk of nations; you shall nurse at the breast of kings; and you shall know that I am the the LORD, your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob. 17 Instead of bronze I will bring gold, and instead of iron, silver; instead of wood, bronze, and instead of stones, iron. I will make your overseers peace and your taskmasters righteousness.

18 Violence shall no longer be heard in your land, nor devastation and destruction within your borders; but you shall call your walls Salvation, and your gates Praise. 19 The sun shall no longer be your light by day, nor shall the moon give you light for brightness; but the the LORD shall be to you an everlasting light, and your God shall be your glory. 20 Your sun shall no more set, nor your moon withdraw; for the the LORD shall be to you an everlasting light, and the days of your mourning shall be completed.

21 All your people shall be righteous; they shall inherit the land forever—the branch of My planting, the work of My hands—that I may be glorified. 22 The least shall become a thousand, and the small one a mighty nation; I am the the LORD; in its appointed time I will hasten it.
