The LORD's Promise to His Servant Israel

1 Now, therefore, attend, O Jacob, My servant, and Israel, whom I have elected. 2 Thus says the LORD, your Maker, and He who formed you from the womb, who will help you: “Fear not, O Jacob My servant, and Jeshurun, whom I have chosen. 3 For I will pour water upon the parched, and flowing streams upon the arid ground; I will pour out My Spirit upon your offspring, and My blessing upon your descendants. 4 They shall burgeon amid the grass, like poplars beside watercourses.”

5 One will declare, “I belong to the LORD,” another will invoke the name of Jacob, and another will inscribe upon his hand, “The LORD’s,” and adopt the name of Israel.

The LORD Alone Is God

6 Thus says the LORD, the King of Israel and his Redeemer, the LORD of Hosts: “I am the First and I am the Last; apart from Me there is no God. 7 Who is like Me? Let him proclaim it. Let him declare and set it before Me, since I appointed an ancient people. Let them declare what is to come and what will happen. 8 Fear not, nor be afraid. Have I not told you from of old and declared it? You are My witnesses. Is there any God besides Me? There is no other Rock; I know none.”

The Absurdity of Idolatry

9 All fashioners of idols are nothing, and their cherished things profit nothing. Their witnesses neither see nor know, to their own confusion. 10 Who fashions a god or casts an idol, to no profit? 11 Behold, all his associates shall be ashamed, and the craftsmen are but mortals. Let them all gather and stand together; they shall tremble and be put to shame.

12 The smith works with iron, laboring over the coals; he shapes it with hammers and exerts his strength with his arm. He grows hungry and his strength fails; he drinks no water and is weary. 13 The woodworker stretches a line; he outlines it with a stylus; he carves it with planes and traces it with a compass; he fashions it in the likeness of a man, with the comeliness of a man, to dwell in a house. 14 He fells cedars, or selects a cypress or an oak, and makes it strong among the trees of the forest; he plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

15 It serves a man for fuel: he takes some of it and warms himself; he kindles a fire and bakes bread; he also makes a god of it and worships it; he fashions an idol and bows down to it. 16 Half of it he burns in the fire; over that half he roasts meat, eats, and is satisfied; he also warms himself and says, “Ah, I am warm; I behold the blaze.” 17 The remainder he makes into a god, his idol; he bows to it and worships; he prays to it and says, “Rescue me, for you are my god.”

18 They know not and discern not, for He has blinded their eyes so that they cannot see, and their hearts so that they cannot understand. 19 No one takes it to heart, nor is there knowledge or understanding to say, “Half of it I burned in the fire; I also baked bread upon its coals; I roasted meat and ate. Shall I make the rest an abomination? Shall I bow down to a log of wood?” 20 He feeds on ashes; a deluded heart has led him astray, so that he cannot deliver his soul nor say, “Is there not a falsehood in my right hand?”

Redemption and the LORD's Sovereign Purpose

21 “Remember these things, O Jacob, and Israel, for you are My servant. I formed you; you are My servant, O Israel; you shall not be forgotten by Me. 22 I have blotted out your transgressions like a cloud, and your sins like a mist. Return to Me, for I have redeemed you.” 23 Sing, O heavens, for the LORD has wrought it; shout, O depths of the earth; burst into song, O mountains, O forest, and every tree therein! For the LORD has redeemed Jacob, and in Israel He will be glorified.

24 Thus says the LORD, your Redeemer, and He who formed you from the womb: “I am the LORD, who made all things, who alone stretched out the heavens, who by Myself spread out the earth— 25 who frustrates the omens of false prophets and makes fools of diviners, who turns the wise backward and makes their knowledge foolish; 26 who confirms the word of His servant and fulfills the counsel of His messengers; who says of Jerusalem, ‘She shall be inhabited,’ and of the cities of Judah, ‘They shall be rebuilt, and I will raise up their ruins’; 27 who says to the deep, ‘Be dry, and I will dry up your rivers’; 28 who says of Cyrus, ‘He is My shepherd, and he shall fulfill all My purpose,’ and of Jerusalem, ‘She shall be built,’ and of the temple, ‘Your foundation shall be laid.’”
