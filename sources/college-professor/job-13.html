Job's Rebuke of His Friends' Worthless Counsel

1 Indeed, my eye has seen all this, and my ear has heard and understood it. 2 According to your knowledge, I too possess knowledge; I am by no means inferior to you. 3 Yet I would speak to the Almighty, and I am eager to argue my case with God. 4 But you are forgers of falsehood, and all of you are physicians of no value.

5 Would that you might keep absolute silence; then would wisdom be yours. 6 Hear now my reproof, and give heed to the contentions of my lips. 7 Will you utter iniquity for God, and speak deceit on His behalf? 8 Will you show Him favoritism? Will you plead God's cause?

9 Would it be well for Him to scrutinize you? Or will you mock Him as one mocks a mortal? 10 He will surely reprove you if you show partiality in secret. 11 Will not His exaltation affright you, and His terror descend upon you? 12 Your remembered sayings are proverbs of ashes, and your bulwarks are bulwarks of clay.

Job's Resolve to Plead His Case Before God

13 Hold your peace before me, and let me speak; then let what may come upon me. 14 Why should I endanger my own flesh and place my life at hazard? 15 Though He slay me, I will still hope in Him; yet I will defend my ways before Him. 16 He Himself shall be my salvation, for no godless man shall come into His presence.

17 Hear diligently my speech, and let my declaration reach your ears. 18 Behold, I have ordered my case; I know that I shall be justified. 19 Who is he that will strive with me? For now, if I hold my peace, I shall expire.

Job's Plea for Divine Scrutiny and Mercy

20 Only do not two things unto me; then will I not hide myself from You. 21 Withdraw Your hand far from me, and let not Your dread make me afraid. 22 Then summon me, and I will answer; or let me speak, and do You reply. 23 How many are my iniquities and sins? Make me know my transgression and my sin. 24 Why do You hide Your face and regard me as Your enemy?

25 Will You terrorize a windblown leaf and pursue dry chaff? 26 For You write bitter things against me and make me inherit the sins of my youth. 27 You put my feet in the stocks and observe all my paths; You inscribe a mark upon the soles of my feet. 28 And he wastes away like a rotten thing, like a garment that moths have consumed.
