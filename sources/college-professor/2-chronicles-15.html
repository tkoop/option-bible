Azariah’s Prophetic Exhortation

1 The Spirit of God came upon Azariah son of Oded.

2 He went out to meet Asa and said, “Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: the LORD is with you while you are with Him. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will forsake you.

3 “For a long time Israel has been without the true God, without a priest to instruct, and without law. 4 Yet in their distress they turned to the LORD God of Israel, sought Him, and He was found by them.

5 “In those days there was no peace for him who went out or for him who came in, for great disturbances afflicted all the inhabitants of the lands. 6 Nation was crushed by nation, and city by city, for God confounded them with every kind of distress. 7 But as for you, be strong and do not let your hands be weak, for there is reward for your labor.”

Asa’s Purge and Renewal of Worship

8 When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the abominations from all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and from the cities he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim; he also renewed the altar of the LORD that stood before the portico of the LORD.

9 He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, together with those from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who sojourned among them, for a multitude from Israel had defected to him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

The Solemn Assembly at Jerusalem

10 They assembled at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa’s reign.

11 On that day they sacrificed to the LORD seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep from the spoil.

12 They entered into a covenant to seek the LORD God of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul.

13 Whoever would not seek the LORD God of Israel was to be put to death, whether small or great, man or woman.

14 They swore to the LORD with a loud voice, with shouting, with trumpets, and with horns.

15 All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and sought Him with their whole desire, and He was found by them; and the LORD gave them rest on every side.

Asa’s Final Reforms and Enduring Peace

16 Asa also removed Maacah his mother from being queen mother, because she had made an abomination for Asherah; Asa cut down her abomination, crushed it, and burned it in the Kidron Valley.

17 The high places, however, were not removed from Israel; nevertheless, Asa’s heart was wholly devoted to the LORD all his days.

18 He brought into the house of God the dedicated things of his father and his own dedicated things—silver, gold, and vessels.

19 There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa’s reign.
