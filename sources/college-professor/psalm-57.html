For the choirmaster: according to “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul in the cave.

1 Be gracious to me, O God, be gracious to me, for in You my soul takes refuge; in the shadow of Your wings I shall seek shelter until the calamities pass by. 2 I shall call upon God Most High, unto God who fulfills His purpose for me. 3 He shall send from heaven and rescue me; He shall put to shame the one who tramples upon me. Selah. God shall send forth His lovingkindness and His truth. 4 My soul is in the midst of lions; I lie down among ravenous beasts—men whose teeth are spears and arrows, and whose tongues are sharp swords.

Exaltation of God Above the Heavens

5 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let Your glory be over all the earth. 6 They have spread a net for my steps; my soul is bowed down. They have dug a pit before me, yet they themselves have fallen into it. Selah.

A Steadfast Heart and the Awakening of Praise

7 My heart is steadfast, O God; my heart is steadfast. I will sing and make melody. 8 Awake, my glory; awake, O harp and lyre! I shall rouse the dawn.

Thanksgiving Among the Nations

9 I will give thanks unto You, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises unto You among the nations. 10 For Your steadfast love is great even unto the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches unto the clouds. 11 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let Your glory be over all the earth.
