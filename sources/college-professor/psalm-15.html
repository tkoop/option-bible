The Qualifications for Dwelling in the LORD’s Sanctuary

1 A psalm of David. The LORD, who may sojourn in Your tent, and who may abide upon Your holy mountain? 2 The one who walks in integrity and practices righteousness, and who speaks truth from the heart.

3 He does not slander with his tongue, nor does he do evil to his neighbor, nor does he take up a reproach against his friend. 4 In his eyes a vile person is despised, yet he honors those who fear the LORD; he keeps his oath even when it costs him dearly. 5 He does not lend his money at interest, nor does he accept a bribe against the innocent. The one who does these things shall never be shaken.
