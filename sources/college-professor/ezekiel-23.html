The Word of the LORD to Ezekiel

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

2 "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother, 3 and they prostituted themselves in Egypt. They engaged in prostitution from their youth; their breasts were fondled there, and their virgin bosoms were caressed. 4 The older was named Oholah, and her sister Oholibah. They became Mine and bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.

Oholah's Unfaithfulness and Fall

5 "Oholah prostituted herself while she was Mine. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, who were near at hand— 6 warriors clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding upon horses. 7 She lavished her harlotry upon all the choice men of Assyria and defiled herself with every idol of those she coveted. 8 She did not abandon the prostitution she had begun in Egypt, when men lay with her in her youth, caressed her virgin bosom, and poured out their lust upon her. 9 Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, the Assyrians, after whom she lusted. 10 They exposed her nakedness, seized her sons and daughters, and put her to the sword. She became a byword among women, and they executed judgment upon her.

Oholibah's Greater Corruption

11 "Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she was even more depraved in her lust than Oholah, and she surpassed her sister in promiscuity. 12 She lusted after the Assyrians—governors and commanders, warriors arrayed in full armor, horsemen riding upon horses—all of them desirable young men. 13 I saw that both of them were defiled; they had taken the same path. 14 But Oholibah carried her prostitution even further. She saw men portrayed upon the wall—images of the Chaldeans, incised in red, 15 with belts around their waists and flowing turbans upon their heads, all of them resembling officers, the very image of Babylonians of Chaldea, their native land. 16 At the sight of them she lusted after them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea. 17 Then the Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and defiled her with their lust. After she had been defiled by them, she turned away from them in disgust. 18 When she uncovered her prostitution and exposed her nakedness, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister. 19 Yet she multiplied her prostitution, remembering the days of her youth, when she had prostituted herself in the land of Egypt. 20 She lusted after their paramours, whose flesh is like the flesh of donkeys and whose issue is like the issue of horses. 21 So you revisited the lewdness of your youth, when the Egyptians caressed your bosom and fondled your young breasts.

Judgment Pronounced upon Oholibah

22 "Therefore, Oholibah, thus says the Lord, the LORD: I will incite your lovers against you—those from whom you turned away in disgust—and I will bring them upon you from every side— 23 the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them—desirable young men, governors and commanders, officers and men of renown, all of them riding upon horses. 24 They will come against you with a host of peoples, with weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will array themselves against you on every side with buckler, shield, and helmet. I will commit judgment to them, and they will judge you according to their own standards. 25 I will vent My jealous rage against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will cut off your nose and your ears, and your survivors will fall by the sword. They will seize your sons and your daughters, and your remnant will be consumed by fire. 26 They will strip you of your clothes and take your fine jewelry. 27 So I will put an end to your lewdness and your prostitution, which began in the land of Egypt, so that you will no longer lift your eyes to them or remember Egypt.

28 "For this is what the Lord, the LORD says: I am about to deliver you into the hands of those you hate, into the hands of those from whom you turned away in disgust. 29 They will deal with you in hatred and take all you have worked for; they will leave you naked and bare, and the nakedness of your prostitution will be exposed. Your lewdness and your prostitution will be brought to light. 30 I will do these things to you because you have prostituted yourself with the nations and defiled yourself with their idols. 31 You have followed the path of your sister, so I will put her cup into your hand. 32 This is what the Lord, the LORD says: You will drink your sister's cup, a cup deep and wide. It will bring you scorn and derision, for it holds so much. 33 You will be filled with drunkenness and grief, with a cup of devastation and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria. 34 You will drink it and drain it; you will shatter it and tear your breasts. For I have spoken, declares the Lord, the LORD. 35 Therefore this is what the Lord, the LORD says: Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you must bear the consequences of your lewdness and your prostitution.

36 The LORD said to me: "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then confront them with their abominations.

37 "For they have committed adultery; blood is on their hands. They have committed adultery with their idols, and they have even sacrificed their children, whom they bore to Me, in the fire as food for them. 38 And they have done this to Me as well: On the same day they defiled My sanctuary and profaned My Sabbaths. 39 On the very day they sacrificed their children to their idols, they entered My sanctuary to profane it. Yes, this is what they did inside My house. 40 Furthermore, they sent for men who came from afar. When the messengers were sent, you bathed yourself, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with jewelry. 41 You sat on a stately couch with a table spread before it, on which you had placed My incense and My oil. 42 The sound of a carefree crowd accompanied them; Sabeans were brought in from the desert along with men from the rabble, and they put bracelets on your wrists and beautiful crowns on your head. 43 Then I said of her who was worn out by adulteries, 'Now they will commit adultery with her, and she with them.'

44 "Yet men went to her as men go to a prostitute; so they went to Oholah and Oholibah, the lewd women. 45 But righteous men will judge them and sentence them as adulteresses and murderers, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands. 46 For this is what the Lord, the LORD says: I will bring a mob against them and give them over to terror and plunder. 47 The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn down their houses. 48 So I will put an end to lewdness in the land, that all women may be warned and not imitate your lewdness. 49 They will repay you for your lewdness, and you will bear the penalty of your idolatry. Then you will know that I am the Lord, the LORD."
