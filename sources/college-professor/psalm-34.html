Praise for the LORD's Deliverance

Of David, when he feigned madness before Abimelech, so that Abimelech drove him out and he departed. 1I will bless the LORD at all times; His praise shall continually be in my mouth. 2My soul shall exult in the LORD; let the humble hear and rejoice. 3Oh, magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt His name together!

4I sought the LORD, and He answered me and delivered me from all my fears. 5Those who look to Him are radiant, and their faces shall never be put to shame. 6This poor man cried, and the LORD heard him and saved him from all his troubles. 7The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear Him, and rescues them.

Taste and See that the LORD Is Good

8Oh, taste and see that the LORD is good! Blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him! 9Fear the LORD, you His saints, for there is no lack for those who fear Him. 10Young lions suffer want and hunger, but those who seek the LORD shall not lack any good thing.

Instruction in the Fear of the LORD

11Come, children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD. 12What man is there who desires life and loves to see good days? 13Guard your tongue from evil and your lips from speaking deceit. 14Turn away from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it.

The LORD's Care for the Righteous and Judgment upon the Wicked

15The eyes of the LORD are toward the righteous, and His ears toward their cry. 16The face of the LORD is against evildoers, to cut off their remembrance from the earth. 17When the righteous cry out, the LORD hears them and delivers them from all their troubles. 18The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and saves those crushed in spirit.

19Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the LORD delivers him from them all. 20He guards all his bones; not one of them is broken. 21Evil shall slay the wicked, and those who hate the righteous shall be condemned. 22The LORD redeems the life of His servants; none of those who take refuge in Him shall be condemned.
