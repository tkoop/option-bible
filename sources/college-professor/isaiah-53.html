The Servant’s Humiliation and Rejection

1Who has believed our report, and to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed? 2He shot up before Him like a tender shoot, and like a root from parched soil; He possessed no stately form or splendor, and when we beheld Him, there was no comeliness that we should covet Him. 3He was despised and rejected by men, a man of sorrows and acquainted with grief; and as one from whom men hide their faces, He was despised, and we esteemed Him not.

The Servant’s Substitutionary Suffering

4Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we esteemed Him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. 5But He was pierced for our transgressions, He was crushed for our iniquities; the chastisement that brought us peace was upon Him, and by His wounds we are healed. 6We all, like sheep, have gone astray; each of us has turned to his own way; and the LORD has laid upon Him the iniquity of us all.

The Servant’s Silent Suffering and Death

7He was oppressed and afflicted, yet He did not open His mouth; He was led like a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is silent, so He did not open His mouth. 8By oppression and judgment He was taken away; and who among His generation considered that He was cut off from the land of the living for the transgression of My people, to whom the stroke was due? 9He was assigned a grave with the wicked, and with a rich man in His death, although He had done no violence, nor was any deceit in His mouth.

The Servant’s Vindication and Exaltation

10Yet it was the LORD’s will to crush Him and to cause Him to suffer; when His soul is made a guilt offering, He will see His offspring, He will prolong His days, and the good pleasure of the LORD will prosper in His hand. 11After the anguish of His soul, He will see and be satisfied; by His knowledge My righteous Servant will justify many, and He will bear their iniquities. 12Therefore I will allot Him a portion with the great, and He will divide the spoils with the strong, because He poured out His life unto death and was numbered with the transgressors; yet He bore the sin of many and made intercession for the transgressors.
