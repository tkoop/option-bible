Famine and a Family's Migration

1 In the days when Israel was governed by judges, a famine ravaged the land, and a certain man from Bethlehem in Judah—accompanied by his wife and two sons—set out to sojourn in the fields of Moab. 2 The man was named Elimelech; his wife was Naomi, and his sons were Mahlon and Chilion, Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. Having arrived in the fields of Moab, they established residence there.

3 Then Elimelech, Naomi's husband, died, leaving her with her two sons. 4 The sons took Moabite wives—Orpah and Ruth—and the family dwelt there for roughly a decade. 5 In time, Mahlon and Chilion also died, so the woman was bereft of both her children and her husband.

6 Having heard in the fields of Moab that the LORD had visited His people by granting them bread, Naomi rose, together with her daughters-in-law, to return from the fields of Moab. 7 Accompanied by her two daughters-in-law, she departed from the place where she had been living, and they set out along the road to return to the land of Judah.

Naomi's Counsel and the Daughters' Grief

8 Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go, return each of you to her mother's house. May the LORD deal kindly with you, as you have dealt with the dead and with me. 9 May the LORD grant that you find repose, each in the house of her husband." Then she kissed them, and they raised their voices and wept.

10 They replied, "No; we will return with you to your people."

11 Naomi said, "Turn back, my daughters; why would you accompany me? Have I still sons in my womb who could become husbands for you? 12 Turn back, my daughters; go your way, for I am too old to have a husband. Even if I were to say that I still have hope, even if I were to have a husband tonight and bear sons, 13 would you wait for them until they grew up? Would you refrain from marrying for their sake? No, my daughters; my bitterness is far greater than yours, for the LORD has turned against me."

14 They lifted their voices and wept again. Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but Ruth clung to her.

Ruth's Devotion

15 Naomi said, "Look, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods; return after your sister-in-law."

16 Ruth replied, "Do not press me to abandon you or to turn back from following you. Where you go, I will go; where you lodge, I will lodge. Your people shall be my people, and your God my God. 17 Where you die, I will die, and there I will be buried. May the LORD do so to me, and more besides, if anything but death separates me from you."

18 When Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her, she ceased speaking to her.

A Bitter Homecoming

19 So the two of them traveled on until they reached Bethlehem. When they arrived in Bethlehem, the whole city was stirred on their account, and the women asked, "Is this Naomi?"

20 She answered, "Do not call me Naomi; call me Mara, for the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me. 21 I went away full, but the LORD has brought me back empty. Why then would you call me Naomi, when the LORD has testified against me and the Almighty has afflicted me?"

22 Thus Naomi returned, with Ruth the Moabitess, her daughter-in-law, who had accompanied her from the fields of Moab. They arrived in Bethlehem at the beginning of the barley harvest.
