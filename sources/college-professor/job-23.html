Job's Longing to Present His Case Before God

1 Then Job answered and said: 2 “Even today my complaint is bitter; my affliction outweighs my groaning. 3 If only I knew where to find Him, that I might come to His seat. 4 I would set my case in order before Him and fill my mouth with arguments. 5 I would know the words with which He would answer me, and discern what He would say to me. 6 Would He contend with me in His great power? No—He would pay heed to me. 7 There the upright could reason with Him, and I should be delivered forever from my Judge.

Job's Integrity amid God's Elusiveness

8 “If I go east, He is not there; if I go west, I cannot discern Him. 9 When He works in the north, I cannot behold Him; when He turns to the south, I cannot see Him. 10 Yet He knows the way I have taken; when He has tested me, I shall come forth as gold. 11 My feet have held fast to His steps; I have kept His way and have not turned aside. 12 I have not departed from the command of His lips; I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my daily bread.

The Terror of God's Inscrutable Decrees

13 “But He is unchangeable, and who can turn Him back? He does whatever His soul desires. 14 For He carries out what He has decreed for me, and He has many such designs. 15 Therefore I am dismayed in His presence; when I ponder this, I dread Him. 16 God has made my heart faint; the Almighty has terrified me. 17 For I am not silenced by the darkness, nor by the thick darkness that covers my face.”
