Paul’s Zeal for Israel’s Salvation

1 Brothers, the earnest longing of my heart and my supplication to God on behalf of my fellow Israelites is that they might attain salvation. 2 For I bear them witness that they possess a zeal for God, albeit one not informed by true knowledge. 3 For, being ignorant of God’s righteousness and seeking to establish a righteousness of their own, they have not submitted themselves to the righteousness of God. 4 For Christ is the culmination of the law, so that righteousness may be granted to everyone who believes.

The Righteousness That Comes by Faith

5 For Moses writes concerning the righteousness that derives from the law: “The one who does these things shall live by them.” 6 But the righteousness that comes by faith declares, “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’” (that is, to bring Christ down) 7 “or, ‘Who will descend into the abyss?’” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead). 8 But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith that we proclaim). 9 For if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. 10 For with the heart one believes unto righteousness, and with the mouth one confesses unto salvation.

The Universal Lord and the Call to Salvation

11 For the Scripture says, “Everyone who believes in Him will not be put to shame.” 12 For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, since the same Lord is Lord of all, abounding in riches to all who call upon Him. 13 For “everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.” 14 How then will they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in Him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching? 15 And how are they to preach unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the good news!” 16 But not all have obeyed the gospel. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?” 17 So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

Israel’s Inexcusable Unbelief

18 But I ask, have they not heard? Indeed they have, for “Their voice has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the inhabited world.” 19 But I ask, did Israel not understand? First Moses says, “I will make you jealous of those who are not a nation; with a foolish nation I will provoke you to anger.” 20 Then Isaiah is so bold as to say, “I was found by those who did not seek Me; I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me.” 21 And concerning Israel he says, “All day long I have held out My hands to a disobedient and obstinate people.”
