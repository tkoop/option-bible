Hiram’s Embassy and Solomon’s Temple Initiative

1 Now Hiram, king of Tyre, dispatched his servants to Solomon upon learning that Solomon had been anointed king in his father’s stead, for Hiram had ever been a friend to David.

2 Solomon, in turn, sent word to Hiram:

3 “You know that my father David was unable to erect a house for the Name of the LORD his God, because of the wars that encircled him, until the LORD subdued his foes beneath the soles of his feet. 4 But now the LORD my God has granted me tranquility on every side; there is neither adversary nor calamity. 5 Therefore I am resolved to build a house for the Name of the LORD my God, in accordance with what the LORD declared to my father David, saying, ‘Your son, whom I shall set upon your throne in your stead, shall build the house for My Name.’ 6 Consequently, command that cedars be hewn for me from Lebanon; my servants shall labor alongside your servants, and I shall remunerate your servants according to whatever wage you stipulate, for you know that none among us possesses the timber-cutting expertise of the Sidonians.”

Hiram’s Joyful Reply and the Covenant of Peace

7 When Hiram heard Solomon’s words, he rejoiced exceedingly and said, “Blessed be the LORD this day, who has granted David a wise son to preside over this great people.”

8 Then Hiram sent a response to Solomon: “I have heard the message you sent me; I shall fulfill all your desire concerning cedar and cypress timber. 9 My servants shall convey the timber from Lebanon seaward, and I shall bind it into rafts upon the sea, directing it to whatever place you designate; there I shall have it dismantled, and you may remove it. In exchange, you shall attend to my needs by supplying provisions for my household.”

10 Thus Hiram supplied Solomon with all the cedar and cypress timber he desired, 11 while Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat as provisions for his household, together with twenty cors of beaten olive oil; Solomon rendered this to Hiram on a yearly basis.

12 Moreover, the LORD endowed Solomon with wisdom, as He had promised, and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon; the two of them concluded a treaty.

Solomon’s Levies and Preparations for the Temple

13 Then King Solomon levied a forced-labor contingent of thirty thousand men from all Israel.

14 He sent them to Lebanon in monthly rotations of ten thousand, whereby each cohort labored one month in Lebanon and then enjoyed two months at home; and Adoniram superintended the levy.

15 Solomon had seventy thousand porters and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountains, 16 in addition to three thousand three hundred overseers who directed the laborers.

17 The king also commanded that they quarry massive, costly stones, wherewith to lay the foundation of the temple.

18 Consequently, the builders of Solomon and Hiram, along with the Gebalites, quarried the stone and prepared timber and stone for the erection of the temple.
