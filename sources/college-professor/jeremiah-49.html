Oracle of Judgment Against the Ammonites

1 Concerning the Ammonites, thus says the LORD: "Does Israel have no sons? Has he no heir? Why then has Milcom taken possession of Gad, and his people settled in its cities? 2 Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will cause a battle cry to be heard against Rabbah of the Ammonites; and it shall become a desolate heap, and its surrounding towns shall be burned with fire. Then Israel shall dispossess those who dispossessed him, says the LORD."

3 Wail, Heshbon, for Ai is devastated! Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! Gird yourselves with sackcloth, mourn, and run back and forth along the walls, for Milcom shall go into exile, together with his priests and his officials.

4 "Why do you boast in your valleys, your valleys that flow with water, O faithless daughter? You trust in your treasures and ask who can come against you."

5 "Behold, I am bringing terror upon you, declares the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, from all around you; and you shall be driven out, each straight ahead, and there will be no one to gather the fugitives. 6 But afterward I will restore the fortunes of the Ammonites, declares the LORD."

Oracle of Judgment Against Edom

7 Concerning Edom, thus says the LORD of Hosts: "Is wisdom no longer found in Teman? Has counsel perished from the prudent? Has their wisdom decayed? 8 Flee, turn back, dwell in the depths, inhabitants of Dedan! For I bring the calamity of Esau upon him, the time when I punish him. 9 If grape gatherers came to you, would they not leave gleanings? If thieves came by night, would they not steal only as much as they needed? 10 But I have stripped Esau bare; I have uncovered his hiding places, so that he cannot hide. His offspring are devastated, along with his brothers and neighbors, and he is no more."

11 "Leave your orphans; I will keep them alive; and let your widows trust in Me."
