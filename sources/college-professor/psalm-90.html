Moses' Petition: The Eternal God and Mortal Humanity

1 Lord, You have been our dwelling place throughout all generations. 2 Before the mountains were born, before You brought forth the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting, You are God.

The Transience of Human Life

3 You consign mortals to dust, declaring, "Return, you children of Adam." 4 For in Your sight a thousand years are as yesterday when it passes, or as a watch in the night. 5 You sweep them away; they are like a sleep, like grass that flourishes. 6 In the morning it blossoms and withers; by evening it is cut down and dries.

Divine Wrath, Human Sin, and Life's Limits

7 For we are consumed by Your anger and dismayed by Your wrath. 8 You have set our iniquities before You, our hidden sins in the light of Your face. 9 All our days pass away beneath Your wrath; we bring our years to an end with a sigh. 10 The span of our years is seventy, or eighty if we are vigorous; yet their boast is but toil and sorrow, for they are soon cut off, and we fly away. 11 Who can fathom the might of Your anger, or Your wrath in proportion to the reverence due You?

A Plea for Wisdom, Mercy, and Enduring Work

12 So teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom. 13 Return, O the LORD; how long? Have compassion on Your servants. 14 Satisfy us in the morning with Your steadfast love, that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.

15 Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for the years in which we have seen evil. 16 Let Your work be revealed to Your servants, and Your glory to their children. 17 May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us; establish the work of our hands for us—yes, establish the work of our hands.
