A Prayer for Restoration amid Devastation

1 Remember, the LORD, what has befallen us; look upon us and behold our humiliation. 2 Our inheritance has passed into the hands of strangers, and our dwellings to foreigners. 3 We have been reduced to fatherless orphans, and our mothers are as widows.

4 We are compelled to purchase water for our drink, and our timber is furnished only at a price. 5 Our persecutors are at our throats; we labor and are afforded no repose. 6 We have pledged ourselves to Egypt and to Assyria merely to secure bread.

The Weight of Inherited Guilt and Oppression

7 Our ancestors sinned and are gone, yet we suffer the penalty of their iniquities. 8 Bondsmen dominate us, and none rescues us from their grasp. 9 We obtain our bread at the hazard of our lives, exposed to the sword of the desert. 10 Our skin is scorched like an oven by the searing blasts of famine.

The Humiliation of the Covenant Community

11 Women are ravished in Zion, and virgins in the cities of Judah. 12 Princes have been hanged by the hand, and the countenances of the aged have not been revered. 13 Young men are yoked to the millstone, and youths stagger under burdens of timber. 14 The aged have withdrawn from the gate, and the young have abandoned their songs. 15 Joy has departed from our hearts; our dancing has been turned into mourning. 16 Our crown has fallen from our head; woe unto us, for we have sinned.

17 For this cause our heart is sick; for these reasons our eyes have grown dim. 18 Mount Zion lies desolate, and foxes roam across it.

Hope in the LORD’s Eternal Reign

19 Yet You, the LORD, abide forever; Your throne is established from generation to generation. 20 Why do You forget us in perpetuity, and forsake us for days unnumbered? 21 Turn us back to Yourself, the LORD, and we shall return; renew our days as of old. 22 Unless You have indeed rejected us utterly and are enraged with us beyond measure.
