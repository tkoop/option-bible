David's Song of Deliverance

1 To the choirmaster: a psalm of David, the servant of the the LORD, who spoke to the the LORD the words of this song on the day when the the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. And he said, "I love You, the the LORD, my strength. 2 The The LORD is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge; my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold. 3 I call upon the the LORD, who is worthy to be praised, and I am saved from my enemies.

"4 The cords of death encompassed me, and the torrents of destruction terrified me. 5 The cords of Sheol surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me.

"6 In my distress I called upon the the LORD; to my God I cried for help. From His temple He heard my voice, and my cry came before Him, into His ears.

The LORD's Mighty Deliverance

"7 Then the earth reeled and rocked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled and quaked, because He was angry. 8 Smoke went up from His nostrils, and devouring fire from His mouth; glowing coals blazed forth from Him. 9 He bowed the heavens and came down, with thick darkness under His feet. 10 He rode on a cherub and flew; He soared on the wings of the wind. 11 He made darkness His covering; His canopy around Him was dark waters and thick clouds of the skies.

"12 Out of the brightness before Him His thick clouds passed, with hailstones and coals of fire. 13 The The LORD thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered His voice, with hailstones and coals of fire. 14 He sent out His arrows and scattered them; He flashed forth lightnings and routed them. 15 Then the channels of the sea were seen, and the foundations of the world were laid bare, at Your rebuke, the the LORD, at the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.

"16 He reached down from on high and took me; He drew me out of many waters. 17 He rescued me from my strong enemy, and from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me. 18 They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the the LORD was my support. 19 He brought me out into a broad place; He rescued me, because He delighted in me.

The Integrity of the Righteous King

"20 The The LORD has dealt with me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He has recompensed me. 21 For I have kept the ways of the the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God. 22 For all His judgments were before me, and His statutes I did not put away from me. 23 I was blameless before Him, and I kept myself from my iniquity. 24 So the the LORD has recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.

"25 With the faithful You show Yourself faithful; with the blameless You show Yourself blameless. 26 With the pure You show Yourself pure, and with the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. 27 For You save an afflicted people, but the haughty eyes You bring down. 28 For You light my lamp; the the LORD my God illumines my darkness. 29 For by You I can run against a troop; by my God I can leap over a wall. 30 As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the the LORD is refined; He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

"31 For who is God but the the LORD? And who is a rock except our God? 32 It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way blameless. 33 He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me secure on the heights. 34 He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 35 You have given me the shield of Your salvation, and Your right hand has supported me, and Your gentleness has made me great. 36 You enlarge my steps beneath me, so that my feet do not slip.

Victory, Dominion, and Praise

"37 I pursued my enemies and overtook them, and did not turn back until they were consumed. 38 I struck them down, so that they were not able to rise; they fell beneath my feet. 39 For You girded me with strength for the battle; You made those who rose against me sink under me. 40 You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hated me I destroyed. 41 They cried for help, but there was none to save; they cried to the the LORD, but He did not answer them. 42 I beat them as fine as dust before the wind; I cast them out like mud in the streets.

"43 You delivered me from strife with the people; You made me the head of the nations; a people I had not known served me. 44 As soon as they heard of me, they obeyed me; foreigners cringed before me. 45 Foreigners lost heart and came trembling out of their fortresses.

"46 The The LORD lives, and blessed be my rock, and exalted be the God of my salvation. 47 The God who gives me vengeance and subdues peoples under me. 48 He delivers me from my enemies; indeed, You exalt me above those who rise against me; You rescue me from the violent man. 49 Therefore I will give thanks to You among the nations, the the LORD, and sing praises to Your name. 50 Great salvation He gives to His king, and shows steadfast love to His Anointed, to David and his offspring forever."
