A Maskil of Asaph: Lament for the Devastated Sanctuary

1 Why have You cast us off in perpetuity, O God, so that Your wrath smolders against the sheep of Your pasture? 2 Remember Your congregation, which You acquired in antiquity, which You redeemed to be the tribe of Your inheritance—Mount Zion, wherein You have dwelt. 3 Lift Your steps toward the everlasting desolations, toward every atrocity the enemy has perpetrated in the sanctuary.

4 Your adversaries roar in the midst of Your meeting place; they have set up their ensigns as signs. 5 They are recognized as men who wield axes amid a thicket of trees. 6 And now, with hatchet and hammers, they batter down all its carved work alike.

7 They have committed Your sanctuary to the flames; they have profaned the dwelling place of Your name to the ground. 8 They said in their hearts, “Let us crush them all at once”; they have burned every meeting place of God in the land. 9 We no longer behold our signs; no prophet remains, and none among us knows how long.

10 How long, O God, will the adversary taunt? Will the enemy blaspheme Your name in perpetuity? 11 Why do You withdraw Your hand, even Your right hand? Withdraw it from Your bosom and consume them.

Recalling the LORD’s Ancient Deeds of Salvation

12 Yet God is my King from of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth. 13 You divided the sea by Your strength; You shattered the heads of the sea monsters upon the waters. 14 You crushed the heads of Leviathan; You consigned him as food to the peoples of the wilderness.

15 You split open fountain and torrent; You dried up ever-flowing rivers. 16 The day is Yours, and Yours also the night; You established the light and the sun. 17 You have set all the boundaries of the earth; You have ordained summer and winter.

Appeal to Covenant Mercy Amid Blasphemy

18 Remember this: the enemy has reproached the LORD, and a foolish people has blasphemed Your name. 19 Do not deliver the soul of Your turtledove to the beasts; do not forget the life of Your afflicted in perpetuity. 20 Have regard to the covenant, for the dark places of the earth are full of habitations of violence.

21 Let not the oppressed return ashamed; let the poor and needy praise Your name. 22 Arise, O God, plead Your cause; remember how the foolish man reproaches You all day long. 23 Do not forget the voice of Your adversaries, the uproar of those who rise against You, which ascends continually.
