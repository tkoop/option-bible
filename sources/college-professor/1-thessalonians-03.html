Paul’s Anxious Concern and Timothy’s Mission

1 Accordingly, when our fortitude could endure the separation no longer, we resolved to remain alone in Athens. 2 Moreover, we dispatched Timothy—our brother and God’s collaborator in proclaiming the gospel of Christ—to establish and console your faith, 3 so that no one might be agitated by these tribulations; for you yourselves know that we are destined for this. 4 Indeed, even while we were among you, we forewarned you that we were destined to suffer affliction, precisely as it has transpired, and you are aware.

Timothy’s Encouraging Report and Paul’s Consolation

5 Therefore, when my own endurance was exhausted, I dispatched inquiry concerning your faith, lest the tempter had somehow assailed you and our labor should be rendered vain. 6 But now Timothy has returned to us from you, bearing glad tidings of your faith and love, and of your perpetual favorable remembrance of us, longing to see us, even as we likewise long for you. 7 Thus, brethren, in the midst of every exigency and tribulation, we found consolation regarding you, by virtue of your faith. 8 For now we genuinely live, provided that you remain steadfast in the Lord.

Thanksgiving for the Thessalonians’ Faith

9 For what adequate thanksgiving could we possibly offer to God on your behalf, for all the exultation with which we rejoice because of you in the presence of our God, 10 supplicating night and day with superabundant earnestness that we might behold your countenance and remedy the deficiencies of your faith?

Apostolic Prayer for Growth in Love and Holiness

11 Now may our God and Father Himself, together with our Lord Jesus, make straight our way unto you. 12 And may the Lord cause you to proliferate and superabound in love toward one another and toward all, even as we also toward you, 13 so that He may establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all His saints. Amen.
