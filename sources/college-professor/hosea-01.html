The Prophetic Word in the Days of the Kings

1 The word of the LORD that came to Hosea, son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

The Command to Take a Wife of Harlotry

2 At the commencement of the LORD’s speaking through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a wife devoted to harlotry, together with children of harlotry, for the land itself has practiced egregious whoredom, forsaking the LORD.” 3 Accordingly, he went and took Gomer, daughter of Diblaim; and she conceived and bore him a son.

4 Then the LORD said to him, “Name him Jezreel, for in but a little while I will requite the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and I will terminate the kingdom of the house of Israel. 5 And on that day I will shatter the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel.”

Children Named in Judgment and Mercy

6 She conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him, “Name her Lo-ruhamah, for I will no longer extend compassion to the house of Israel; indeed, I will not forgive them at all. 7 But I will have compassion upon the house of Judah, and I will deliver them by the LORD their God; I will not deliver them by bow, or by sword, or by warfare, or by horses, or by horsemen.”

8 When she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived again and bore a son. 9 And He said, “Name him Lo-ammi, for you are not My people, and I am not your God.”

Restoration Beyond Judgment

10 Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea, immeasurable and innumerable; and in the very place where it was said to them, ‘You are not My people,’ it shall be said to them, ‘Children of the living God.’ 11 And the children of Judah and the children of Israel shall be gathered together, and they shall appoint for themselves one head, and they shall ascend from the land, for great shall be the day of Jezreel.
