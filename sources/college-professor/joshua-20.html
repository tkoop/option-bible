The LORD Institutes Cities of Refuge

1 Then the the LORD spoke to Joshua, saying, 2 “Speak to the Israelites and declare, ‘Appoint for yourselves the cities of refuge, concerning which I spoke to you through Moses, 3 so that the manslayer who strikes down a life inadvertently, without knowledge, may flee thither; and they shall constitute for you an asylum from the avenger of blood. 4 He shall flee to one of these cities, take his stand at the portal of the city gate, and plead his case in the hearing of that city’s elders; then they shall receive him into the city, allot him a dwelling, and he shall reside among them. 5 Should the avenger of blood pursue him, they must not surrender the manslayer into his hand, because he struck his neighbor unwittingly and had not been his enemy beforehand. 6 He shall dwell in that city until he stands in judgment before the congregation, and until the death of the high priest who shall serve in those days; thereafter the manslayer may return to his own city and house, to the city from which he fled.’”

The Consecrated Cities Are Named

7 They consecrated Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—in the hill country of Judah. 8 Beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they gave Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.

9 These cities were designated for all the Israelites and for the resident alien dwelling among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally might flee there and not die at the hand of the avenger of blood before standing before the congregation.
