The Sacred Allotment of the Land

1 When you cast lots to apportion the land as an inheritance, you shall set apart for the LORD a holy contribution from the land: a sacred district twenty-five thousand cubits in length and twenty thousand cubits in breadth, and the whole district shall be holy. 2 Within this district there shall be a square of five hundred cubits by five hundred cubits for the sanctuary, with an open border of fifty cubits encircling it. 3 From this holy portion you shall measure off a length of twenty-five thousand cubits and a width of ten thousand cubits, and within it shall stand the sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.

4 It shall be a holy portion of the land for the priests who minister in the sanctuary and draw near to minister before the LORD; it shall furnish them with dwellings and constitute a holy precinct for the sanctuary. 5 An area twenty-five thousand cubits long and ten thousand cubits wide shall belong to the Levites who minister in the temple, and they shall possess twenty chambers. 6 You shall allot to the city a tract five thousand cubits wide and twenty-five thousand cubits long, adjacent to the holy portion, and it shall belong to the whole house of Israel.

7 The prince shall hold a portion on both sides of the holy portion and the city, extending westward from its western side and eastward from its eastern side, running continuously from the western boundary to the eastern boundary. 8 This land shall be his possession in Israel; and My princes shall no longer oppress My people, but shall allot the remainder of the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes.

Righteous Governance and Honest Measures

9 Thus says the Lord GOD: "You have gone quite far enough, O princes of Israel! Put away violence and oppression, execute justice and righteousness, and cease dispossessing My people," declares the Lord GOD.

10 You shall employ just balances, a just ephah, and a just bath. 11 The ephah and the bath shall be of one capacity: the bath shall contain a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer, so that the homer shall be the standard measure. 12 The shekel shall comprise twenty gerahs; twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels, and fifteen shekels shall constitute your mina.

Prescribed Contributions and the Prince's Obligations

13 This is the contribution that you shall offer: a sixth of an ephah from every homer of wheat, and a sixth of an ephah from every homer of barley. 14 The ordinance concerning oil, measured by the bath, shall be a tenth of a bath from each cor—a cor being ten baths, that is, one homer, since ten baths make a homer.

15 From Israel's well-watered pastures you shall take one sheep from every two hundred; these shall serve for the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make atonement for the people, declares the Lord GOD. 16 All the people of the land shall join in this contribution for the prince in Israel. 17 It shall be the prince's duty to furnish the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the festivals, the new moons, and the Sabbaths—for all the appointed feasts of the house of Israel. He shall provide the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make atonement on behalf of the house of Israel.

Purification of the Sanctuary and Festal Sacrifices

18 Thus says the Lord GOD: "On the first day of the first month you shall take a young bull without blemish and purify the sanctuary."

19 The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering and apply it to the doorposts of the temple, to the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and to the gateposts of the inner court. 20 You shall do the same on the seventh day of the month for anyone who has sinned through error or ignorance; so shall you make atonement for the temple.

21 On the fourteenth day of the first month you shall observe the Passover, a festival of seven days, during which unleavened bread shall be eaten. 22 On that day the prince shall provide a bull as a sin offering for himself and for all the people of the land. 23 Throughout the seven days of the festival, he shall provide seven bulls and seven rams without blemish as a burnt offering to the LORD each day, together with a male goat as a sin offering. 24 He shall provide as a grain offering an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, along with a hin of oil for each ephah. 25 On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, during the festival, he shall make the same provision for seven days: the same sin offering, burnt offering, grain offering, and oil.
