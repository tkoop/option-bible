A Lament over the Arrogance of the Wicked and an Appeal for Divine Justice

1 Why, O the LORD, do You stand so aloof, and why do You hide Yourself in the hour of affliction? 2 Through the arrogance of the wicked, the afflicted are relentlessly pursued, and they are caught in the plots those evildoers have devised. 3 For the wicked exults in the cravings of his soul; he applauds the greedy and reviles the the LORD.

4 In the haughtiness of his countenance the wicked does not seek God; all his thoughts are, “There is no God.” 5 His ways prosper at every turn; Your judgments are exalted beyond his ken; he sneers at all his adversaries. 6 He says in his heart, “I shall never be shaken; throughout the generations I shall know no calamity.” 7 His mouth is brimming with cursing, deceit, and oppression; beneath his tongue lurk mischief and iniquity.

8 He lurks in the hiding places of the villages; in secret he slays the innocent; his eyes are ever on the watch for the helpless. 9 He lurks in his covert like a lion in his thicket; he lurks to snatch away the afflicted; he seizes the afflicted as he drags them into his net. 10 The helpless are crushed and brought low; they fall by his might. 11 He says in his heart, “God has forgotten; He has hidden His face; He will never behold it.”

12 Arise, O the LORD; O God, lift up Your hand; do not forget the afflicted. 13 Why does the wicked revile God, saying in his heart, “You will not call it to account”?

14 Yet You have seen, for You behold trouble and grief, to take it into Your hand. The helpless entrusts himself to You; You have been the helper of the fatherless. 15 Shatter the might of the wicked and the evildoer; search out his wickedness until You find none. 16 The the LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land. 17 O the LORD, You have heard the desire of the afflicted; You strengthen their hearts; You incline Your ear to render justice to the fatherless and the oppressed, that mortal man may terrify no more. 18
