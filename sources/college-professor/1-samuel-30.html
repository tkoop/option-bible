The Amalekite Devastation of Ziklag

1 On the third day, when David and his men arrived at Ziklag, the Amalekites had already raided the Negev and Ziklag, assaulting the town and burning it with fire. 2 They had carried off the women who were in it, from the least to the greatest; they had killed no one, but had driven them away and gone on their own way.

3 When David and his men came to the city and found it burned with fire, their wives, sons, and daughters having been taken captive, 4 David and the people with him raised their voices in lamentation until their grief had exhausted their strength.

5 David’s two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel, had been taken captive.

6 David was sorely distressed, for the people spoke of stoning him, each man being bitter of soul over his sons and daughters; yet David fortified himself in the LORD his God.

David Seeks Divine Counsel and Pursues

7 David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, “Please bring me the ephod.” So Abiathar brought the ephod to David.

8 Then David inquired of the LORD, saying, “Shall I pursue this raiding party? Shall I overtake them?”

He answered him, “Pursue, for you will surely overtake them and rescue them.”

9 So David went, he and his six hundred men, and they came to the Brook of Besor, where those left behind remained. 10 Two hundred men stayed behind because they were too exhausted to cross the brook; David and the four hundred men continued in pursuit.

11 They found an Egyptian in the field and brought him to David; they gave him water to drink and bread to eat. 12 They also gave him a piece of fig cake and two clusters of raisins. He ate, and his vigor returned to him, for he had not eaten bread or drunk water for three days and three nights.

13 David said to him, “To whom do you belong, and where are you from?”

The man replied, “I am an Egyptian, the servant of an Amalekite; my master abandoned me three days ago because I became ill. 14 We raided the Negev of the Cherethites, the territory of Judah, and the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire.”

15 David said to him, “Will you bring me down to this raiding party?”

He replied, “Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master, and I will bring you down to this raiding party.”

The Rout of the Amalekites and Recovery of Captives

16 So he brought him down, and there they were, dispersed across the countryside, eating, drinking, and reveling because of all the great plunder they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

17 David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day; not one of them escaped, except four hundred young men who fled on camels. 18 David recovered everything the Amalekites had taken, and he rescued his two wives. 19 Nothing was missing, from the least to the greatest, sons and daughters, plunder and everything they had taken; David brought back all of it.

20 David took all the flocks and herds, and his men drove them ahead of the other livestock, saying, “This is David’s plunder.”

Equitable Distribution of Plunder and Gifts to Judah

21 When David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow him and had been left at the Brook of Besor, they came out to meet him and the people with him. David approached them and greeted them.

22 Then all the wicked and worthless men among those who had gone with David said, “Because they did not go with us, we will not share with them the plunder we recovered, except that each man may take his wife and children and lead them away.”

23 David replied, “My brothers, you shall not do so with what the LORD has given us, for He has preserved us and delivered into our hand the raiding party that came against us. 24 Who will listen to you in this matter? For as is the share of the one who goes down to the battle, so shall be the share of the one who stays with the baggage; they shall share alike.”

25 And so it has been from that day forward; David made this a statute and an ordinance for Israel to this very day.

26 When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah, his friends, saying, “Here is a gift for you from the plunder of the enemies of the LORD.”

27 He sent it to those in Bethel, in Ramoth of the Negev, and in Jattir; 28 to those in Aroer, in Siphmoth, and in Eshtemoa; 29 to those in Racal, in the cities of the Jerahmeelites, and in the cities of the Kenites; 30 to those in Hormah, in Bor-ashan, and in Athach; 31 and to those in Hebron, and to all the places where David and his men had roamed.
