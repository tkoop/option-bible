The Vision of the Glassy Sea and the Song of the Redeemed

1 And I beheld another sign in heaven, great and wondrous: seven angels having the seven last plagues, in which the wrath of God is brought to its completion. 2 And I saw, as it were, a sea of glass mingled with fire, and those who had triumphed over the beast, and over its image, and over the number of its name, standing upon the glassy sea, bearing the harps of God.

3 And they sing the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, “Great and marvelous are Your works, Lord God Almighty! Just and true are Your ways, King of the nations! 4 Who shall not fear You, O Lord, and glorify Your name? For You alone are holy. All the nations shall come and worship before You, for Your righteous acts have been made manifest.”

The Temple Opened and the Seven Last Plagues Dispensed

5 And after these things I looked, and the temple of the tabernacle of testimony in heaven was opened. 6 And out of the temple came forth the seven angels who had the seven plagues, arrayed in pure, radiant linen, and girded about their breasts with golden sashes.

7 And one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls brimming with the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever. 8 And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from His power, and no one was able to enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels should be accomplished.
