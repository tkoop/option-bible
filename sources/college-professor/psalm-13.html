A Davidic Lament Ascending into Confident Praise

1 To the choirmaster: a psalm of David. How long, O the LORD, will You consign me to oblivion? How long will You veil Your countenance from me? 2 How long must I deliberate within my soul, bearing grief in my heart throughout the day? How long shall my adversary exalt himself above me?

3 Regard and respond to me, O the LORD my God; illumine my eyes, lest I succumb to the slumber of death, 4 lest my foe proclaim, “I have prevailed against him,” and my adversaries exult because I have faltered.

5 Yet I have trusted in Your steadfast love; my heart shall exult in Your deliverance. 6 I shall sing unto the LORD, for He has dealt munificently with me.
