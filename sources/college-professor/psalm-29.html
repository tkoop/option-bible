A Psalm of David: The Thunderous Voice of the LORD

1 Attribute to the LORD, O sons of God, attribute to the LORD glory and might. 2 Attribute to the LORD the glory due His name; bow before the LORD in the splendor of holiness.

3 The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is over many waters. 4 The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic.

5 The voice of the LORD shatters the cedars; indeed, the LORD splinters the cedars of Lebanon. 6 He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox.

7 The voice of the LORD hews forth flames of fire. 8 The voice of the LORD convulses the wilderness; the LORD convulses the wilderness of Kadesh.

9 The voice of the LORD brings the deer to labor and lays the forests bare; and in His temple all proclaim, "Glory!"

The LORD Enthroned over the Flood and Blessing His People

10 The LORD sat enthroned over the flood; the LORD sits as King forever. 11 The LORD imparts strength to His people; the LORD blesses His people with peace.
