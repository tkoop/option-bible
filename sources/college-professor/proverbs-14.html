The Architecture of Wisdom and the Ruin of Folly

1 A woman's wisdom constructs her house, whereas folly demolishes it with her own hands. 2 He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but he who is devious in his ways despises Him. 3 Pride issues from the fool's mouth, yet the lips of the wise safeguard them. 4 In the absence of oxen, the feeding trough remains clean, but an abundant harvest arises from the ox's strength.

5 A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies. 6 A scoffer seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge comes easily to the discerning. 7 Withdraw from the fool, for you will discover no discernment upon his lips. 8 The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way, but the folly of fools is deceit.

The Paths of the Righteous and the Wicked

9 Fools scoff at guilt, yet goodwill dwells among the upright. 10 The heart alone knows its own bitterness, and no outsider partakes of its joy. 11 The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish.

12 There is a path that appears upright to a person, yet its terminus is the way of death. 13 Even in laughter the heart may ache, and the end of joy may be grief. 14 The backslider in heart is sated with his own conduct, whereas a good person finds satisfaction from within himself. 15 The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps. 16 A wise person fears evil and withdraws from it, but a fool rages and remains self-assured. 17 One who is quick to anger acts foolishly, and a person who schemes maliciously is hated. 18 The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

Poverty, Riches, and the Ethics of Neighborliness

19 The wicked bow before the good, and the malevolent prostrate themselves at the gates of the righteous. 20 A poor person is hated even by his neighbor, but the rich have many friends. 21 Whoever scorns his neighbor sins, but blessed is the one who shows mercy to the poor. 22 Do not those who contrive evil stray from the path? Yet mercy and truth belong to those who plan good. 23 Every form of labor yields profit, but mere talk conduces only to poverty. 24 The wise person's crown is wealth, yet the folly of fools remains mere folly. 25 A truthful witness delivers lives, but a deceitful witness utters lies.

The Fear of the LORD and the Fate of Kingdoms

26 In the fear of the LORD there is strong confidence, and His children will have a refuge. 27 The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning a person from the snares of death. 28 A king's glory lies in a multitude of people, but a prince is ruined without a populace. 29 Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding, but he who has a hasty temper exalts folly. 30 A tranquil heart is life to the flesh, but envy is rottenness to the bones. 31 Whoever oppresses the poor insults his Maker, but whoever is generous to the needy honors Him.

32 The wicked is driven away in his wickedness, but the righteous finds refuge even in death. 33 Wisdom rests in the heart of the discerning, but among fools it becomes conspicuous. 34 Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people. 35 A king's favor is toward a wise servant, but his wrath is against the one who causes shame.
