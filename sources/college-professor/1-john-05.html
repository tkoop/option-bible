Faith, Love, and the Conquest of the World

1 Every person who believes that Jesus is the Messiah has been begotten of God; and everyone who cherishes the One who begat also cherishes the one begotten from Him. 2 By this we discern that we cherish the offspring of God: whenever we cherish God and execute His precepts. 3 For this constitutes the love of God—that we preserve His commandments; and His commandments are by no means onerous. 4 For every offspring begotten of God vanquishes the world; and this is the triumph that has subdued the world—our faith. 5 And who is the conqueror of the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

The Divine Testimony and the Gift of Eternal Life

6 This is He who came through water and blood—Jesus Christ—not through water alone, but through water and through blood; and the Spirit is the witness, inasmuch as the Spirit is truth itself. 7 For there are three witnesses— 8 the Spirit, the water, and the blood—and these three concur as one. 9 If we accept human testimony, the testimony of God is greater still, because this is God's testimony: He has borne witness concerning His Son. 10 Whoever believes in the Son of God possesses that testimony within himself; whoever does not believe God has rendered Him a liar, because he has not trusted the testimony that God bore concerning His Son. 11 Moreover, this is the testimony: God has conferred upon us life everlasting, and that life resides in His Son. 12 Whoever possesses the Son possesses life; whoever lacks the Son of God is devoid of life. 13 I have inscribed these words for you who trust in the name of the Son of God, that you may be assured you possess life everlasting.

Confidence in Prayer and Final Admonitions

14 And this is the boldness we possess before Him: that if we petition Him for anything consonant with His will, He hearkens to us. 15 And if we know that He hears us in whatever we petition, we know that we possess the petitions we have requested from Him. 16 Should anyone perceive his brother committing a transgression not incurring death, let him petition, and God will grant life to that brother—not, however, for transgressions incurring death. There is a transgression incurring death; concerning that, I do not enjoin him to inquire. 17 All unrighteousness is sin, yet there is sin that does not incur death. 18 We know that everyone begotten of God does not persist in sin; rather, the One begotten of God guards him, and the evil one cannot lay hold of him. 19 We know that we derive from God, whereas the whole world is enthralled to the evil one. 20 Moreover, we know that the Son of God has come and has imparted to us discernment, so that we may know the True One; and we abide in the True One, in His Son Jesus Christ. This One is the true God and life everlasting. 21 Little children, keep yourselves unblemished from idols.
