A Lament over the Desecration of Jerusalem and the Reproach of God's People

1 O God, the nations have invaded your inheritance; they have defiled your holy sanctuary and reduced Jerusalem to a heap of rubble. 2 They have consigned the corpses of your servants to the birds of the sky for food, and the flesh of your faithful to the beasts of the earth. 3 They have poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, with no one to bury them. 4 We have become a reproach to our neighbors, an object of taunting and derision to those who surround us.

5 How long, O the LORD? Will you be indignant forever? Will your jealousy blaze like fire? 6 Pour out your wrath upon the nations that do not know you, and upon the kingdoms that never invoke your name. 7 For they have ravaged Jacob and desolated his homeland.

8 Do not charge us with the iniquities of former times; let your compassion hasten to meet us, for we have been brought to utter destitution. 9 Come to our aid, O God of our deliverance, for the glory of your name; rescue us and expiate our sins for the honor of your name. 10 Why should the nations taunt, "Where is their God?" May the retribution for your servants' shed blood be displayed among the nations before our eyes. 11 Let the groaning of the captive reach your presence; by the might of your arm, preserve those condemned to die. 12 Repay our neighbors sevenfold, letting their reproach recoil upon their own heads, O Lord. 13 But we, your people and the flock of your pasture, will render thanks to you in perpetuity; from generation to generation we shall proclaim your praise.
