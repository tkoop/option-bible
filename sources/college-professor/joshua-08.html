Divine Assurance and the Stratagem Against Ai

1 Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid, nor be dismayed; take all your fighting force and ascend against Ai. Behold, I have delivered into your hand the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his territory. 2 You shall deal with Ai and its king as you dealt with Jericho and its king, except that you may plunder its spoil and livestock for yourselves. Deploy an ambush behind the city.”

Joshua’s Nocturnal Orders and Ambush

3 So Joshua, together with the entire martial host, arose to ascend against Ai; and Joshua chose thirty thousand men of valor and sent them out under cover of night. 4 He charged them, “Behold, you are to lie in ambush behind the city; do not withdraw very far from it, and let all of you be prepared. 5 I, with all the people accompanying me, will approach the city; and when they sally forth against us, as formerly, we shall flee before them. 6 They will pursue us until we have drawn them away from the city, for they will say, ‘They are fleeing before us, as at first’; and we shall flee before them. 7 Then you shall rise from the ambush and dispossess the city, for the LORD your God will deliver it into your hand. 8 When you have seized the city, you shall set it ablaze; according to the word of the LORD you shall act. Behold, I have commanded you.”

The Trap Springs and Ai Is Destroyed

9 Joshua then sent them forth, and they proceeded to the place of ambush, taking position between Bethel and Ai, westward of Ai; but Joshua lodged that night among the people. 10 Joshua rose early in the morning, mustered the people, and he and the elders of Israel went up before the people to Ai. 11 All the fighting men with him went up, drew near to the city, and encamped north of Ai, with the valley between them and Ai. 12 He had taken about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. 13 So they stationed the whole camp north of the city and its rearguard west of the city; and that night Joshua went into the midst of the valley. 14 When the king of Ai saw this, he and all his people hastened and rose early in the morning to meet Israel in battle at the appointed place before the Arabah; yet he did not know that an ambush had been set against him behind the city. 15 Joshua and all Israel feigned retreat before them and fled toward the wilderness. 16 All the men of Ai were summoned to pursue them, and they pursued Joshua, being drawn away from the city. 17 Not a man remained in Ai or Bethel who did not go out after Israel; they left the city unguarded and pursued Israel. 18 Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Extend toward Ai the javelin in your hand, for I will give it into your hand.” So Joshua extended toward the city the javelin in his hand. 19 As soon as he extended his hand, the ambush arose quickly from its place, ran into the city, captured it, and hastily set the city ablaze. 20 The men of Ai turned and saw the smoke of the city ascending to heaven, and they had no power to flee this way or that; and the people who had been fleeing toward the wilderness turned back against their pursuers. 21 When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had captured the city and that smoke was ascending, they turned back and struck down the men of Ai. 22 The ambush also came out of the city against them, so they were caught in the middle, with Israelites on both sides; and Israel struck them down until no survivor or fugitive remained. 23 But they took the king of Ai alive and brought him to Joshua. 24 When Israel had finished slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the open country and in the wilderness where they had pursued them, and all had fallen by the edge of the sword, all Israel returned to Ai and put it to the sword. 25 All who fell that day, from man to woman, were twelve thousand—every inhabitant of Ai. 26 For Joshua did not withdraw the hand with which he had extended the javelin until he had devoted to destruction all the inhabitants of Ai. 27 Only the livestock and the spoil of that city Israel took as plunder for themselves, according to the word of the LORD, which He had commanded Joshua. 28 Joshua burned Ai and made it an everlasting heap of ruins, a desolation to this day. 29 He hanged the king of Ai on a tree until evening; and at sunset Joshua commanded that his corpse be taken down from the tree and cast at the entrance of the city gate, and they raised over him a great heap of stones, which remains to this day.

Covenant Renewal and the Reading of the Law

30 Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal. 31 as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the Book of the Law of Moses: an altar of whole stones, upon which no iron tool had been wielded. They offered burnt offerings to the LORD and sacrificed peace offerings. 32 There, in the presence of the children of Israel, he wrote on the stones a copy of the law of Moses, which he had written. 33 All Israel, with their elders, officers, and judges, stood on either side of the ark of the covenant of the LORD, facing the Levitical priests who bore the ark—half of them before Mount Gerizim and half before Mount Ebal—as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded at first, to bless the people of Israel. 34 Afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessing and the curse, according to all that is written in the Book of the Law. 35 There was not a word of all that Moses had commanded that Joshua did not read before the whole assembly of Israel, together with the women, the little ones, and the sojourners who walked among them.
