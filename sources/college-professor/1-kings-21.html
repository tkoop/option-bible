Ahab's Covetous Desire for Naboth's Vineyard

1 Following these events, Naboth the Jezreelite possessed a vineyard in Jezreel, situated adjacent to the palace of Ahab, king of Samaria. 2 Ahab said to Naboth, "Grant me your vineyard, that I may convert it into a vegetable garden, for it lies near, beside my house; and I will give you a superior vineyard in exchange, or, if you prefer, I will remunerate you with its price in silver."

3 Naboth replied to Ahab, "The LORD forbid that I should surrender to you the inheritance of my fathers."

4 So Ahab entered his house sullen and resentful because of the word that Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him; he said, "I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers." Then he lay upon his bed, turned away his face, and refused to eat.

5 His wife Jezebel came to him and said, "Why is your spirit so dejected that you refuse food?"

6 He said to her, "Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for silver, or, if you prefer, I will give you another vineyard in its place,' and he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

7 Jezebel his wife said to him, "Do you now exercise sovereignty over Israel? Arise, eat, and let your heart be glad. I myself will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite."

Jezebel's Machinations against Naboth

8 Accordingly, she wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and dispatched them to the elders and nobles residing in Naboth's city. 9 In the letters she wrote, "Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth at the head of the people. 10 Set two scoundrels before him to testify against him, saying, 'You have cursed God and the king.' Then bring him out and stone him to death."

11 The men of his city, the elders and nobles who dwelt in his city, did as Jezebel had instructed them, precisely as it was written in the letters she had sent them. 12 They proclaimed a fast and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

13 Then two scoundrels entered and sat opposite him; and these scoundrels testified against Naboth before the people, saying, "Naboth cursed God and the king." Thereupon they carried him outside the city and stoned him to death.

14 They sent word to Jezebel, saying, "Naboth has been stoned and is dead."

15 When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, she said to Ahab, "Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for silver; for Naboth is no longer alive—he is dead."

16 When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he arose to go down and take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.

Elijah's Prophetic Indictment of Ahab

17 Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 18 "Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria; behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to take possession of it. 19 You shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: Have you murdered and also taken possession?' And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: In the place where the dogs licked the blood of Naboth, the dogs shall lick your blood—even yours.'"

20 Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, O my enemy?"

And he answered, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do evil in the sight of the LORD."

21 "Behold, I will bring disaster upon you, and I will utterly sweep you away, and I will cut off from Ahab every male in Israel, whether slave or free. 22 And I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah, for the provocation with which you have provoked Me to anger, and for having made Israel sin. 23 And concerning Jezebel the LORD also said, 'The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.' 24 Whoever belongs to Ahab and dies in the city, the dogs shall eat; and whoever dies in the field, the birds of the sky shall eat."

25 There was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do evil in the sight of the LORD, because Jezebel his wife incited him. 26 He acted very abominably in going after idols, according to all that the Amorites had done, whom the LORD drove out before the children of Israel.

Ahab's Contrition and the LORD's Deferred Judgment

27 When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes, put sackcloth upon his flesh, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and went about quietly.

28 Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 29 "Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before Me? Because he has humbled himself before Me, I will not bring the disaster in his days; but in the days of his son I will bring the disaster upon his house."
