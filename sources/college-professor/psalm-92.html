A Psalm. A Song for the Sabbath.

A Sabbath Hymn of Thanksgiving to the LORD

1 It is fitting to give thanks to the LORD, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High; 2 to declare Your steadfast love in the morning and Your faithfulness through the night, 3 with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre. 4 For You, O LORD, have made me glad by Your work; I will sing for joy at the works of Your hands. 5 How magnificent are Your works, O LORD! How unfathomably deep are Your thoughts!

6 A senseless man does not know, nor does a fool understand this.

The Transience of the Wicked and the Exaltation of the LORD

7 When the wicked spring up like grass and all evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever. 8 But You, O LORD, are exalted forevermore. 9 For behold, Your enemies, O LORD—for behold, Your enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered.

The Anointing and Empowerment of the Psalmist

10 But You have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox; I have been anointed with fresh oil. 11 My eyes have looked upon the downfall of my foes; my ears have heard the ruin of my wicked assailants.

The Flourishing of the Righteous in the Courts of God

12 The righteous will flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. 13 Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God. 14 In old age they will still bear fruit; they will remain vigorous and verdant, 15 to declare that the LORD is upright; He is my Rock, and in Him there is no unrighteousness.
