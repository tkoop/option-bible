The Miraculous Catch and the Summons of the First Disciples

1 On one occasion, while He was standing beside the Lake of Gennesaret and the crowd was pressing in upon Him so as to hear the word of God, 2 He observed two boats moored at the water’s edge, and the fishermen—having disembarked from them—were washing their nets. 3 Stepping into one of the boats, namely Simon’s, He asked him to put out a short distance from the shore; and, having sat down, He taught the crowds from the boat.

4 When He had finished speaking, He said to Simon, “Put out into the deep water and let down your nets for a catch.”

5 Simon answered, “Master, we have toiled through the whole night and caught nothing; yet, at Your word, I will let down the nets.”

6 When they had done so, they enclosed a vast multitude of fish, and their nets began to tear. 7 They therefore signaled to their partners in the other boat to come and help them; and they came and filled both boats, so that they began to sink.

8 Seeing this, Simon Peter fell at Jesus’ knees, saying, “Depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinful man.”

9 For astonishment had seized him and all who were with him because of the catch of fish that they had taken, 10 and likewise James and John, the sons of Zebedee, who were Simon’s partners. Then Jesus said to Simon, “Do not be afraid; from now on you will be catching men.”

11 When they had brought the boats to land, they left everything and followed Him.

A Leper Is Cleansed and the Report Spreads Abroad

12 While He was in one of the cities, behold, a man full of leprosy; and when he saw Jesus, he fell on his face and implored Him, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.”

13 And stretching out His hand, He touched him, saying, “I am willing; be cleansed.” And immediately the leprosy left him.

14 And He charged him, “Tell no one; rather, go, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing the sacrifices that Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.”

15 But the word concerning Him spread all the more, and great crowds assembled to hear Him and to be healed of their ailments. 16 Yet He Himself was withdrawing into the desolate places and praying.

Authority to Forgive Sins Displayed in the Paralytic’s Healing

17 On one of those days, while He was teaching, Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting by—men who had come from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem—and the power of the Lord was with Him to heal.

18 And behold, men were carrying a man who was paralyzed, lying on a bed, and they were seeking to bring him in and to lay him before Him. 19 But, not finding by what way they might bring him in because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and lowered him with his pallet through the tiles into the middle, before Jesus.

20 Seeing their faith, He said, “Man, your sins are forgiven you.”

21 The scribes and Pharisees began to reason, saying, “Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who can forgive sins except God alone?”

22 Jesus, knowing their reasonings, answered and said to them, “Why do you reason in your hearts? 23 Which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven you,’ or to say, ‘Rise and walk’? 24 But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins.” He said to the paralyzed man, “I say to you, rise, take up your pallet, and go to your house.”

25 And immediately, having risen before them, he took up that on which he had lain and departed to his house, glorifying God. 26 And amazement seized them all, and they glorified God; they were filled with fear, saying, “We have seen extraordinary things today.”

The Calling of Levi and Disputations Concerning Fasting

27 After these things He went out and observed a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax booth, and He said to him, “Follow Me.” 28 And he, having risen, left everything and followed Him.

29 Levi made a great feast for Him in his house; and there was a large crowd of tax collectors and others reclining at table with them.

30 The Pharisees and their scribes were grumbling at His disciples, saying, “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”

31 Jesus answered them, “It is not the healthy who need a physician, but those who are ill. 32 I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”

33 They said to Him, “John’s disciples fast often and make petitions, and likewise those of the Pharisees, but yours eat and drink.”

34 Jesus said to them, “Can you make the attendants of the bridegroom fast while the bridegroom is with them? 35 But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them; then, in those days, they will fast.”

36 He also set forth a parable to them: “No one tears a patch from a new garment and puts it on an old garment; otherwise he will tear the new, and the patch from the new will not agree with the old. 37 And no one puts new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the new wine will burst the skins, and it will be spilled, and the skins will be destroyed. 38 Rather, new wine must be put into new wineskins. 39 And no one, having drunk old wine, desires new; for he says, ‘The old is better.’”
