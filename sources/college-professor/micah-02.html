Woe to Those Who Contrive Iniquity

1 Woe to those who contrive iniquity and perpetrate wickedness upon their couches! At the first blush of dawn they bring it to fruition, for it lies within their power. 2 They covet estates and seize them, together with houses, and carry them off; they crush a man together with his household, a man together with his patrimony.

Decreed Disaster

3 Therefore thus says the the LORD: “Behold, I am devising against this family an evil from which you cannot extricate your necks; you shall not walk haughtily, for it is an evil time.”

4 In that day people will raise a taunt against you, and lament with a bitter lamentation, saying, “We are utterly ruined! He has changed the portion of my people; how utterly He has removed it from me; He has allotted our fields to traitors.” 5 Therefore you shall have no one to cast the measuring line by lot in the assembly of the the LORD.

False Prophets and Coming Exile

6 “Do not prophesy!” they say. “Do not prophesy concerning these things; disgrace shall not overtake us.”

7 O house of Jacob, is the Spirit of the the LORD impatient? Are these His doings? Do not My words do good to him who walks uprightly?

8 But lately My people have risen as an enemy; you strip the robe from those who pass by securely, as men returning from war. 9 The women of My people you cast out from their pleasant houses; from their children you take away My glory forever.

10 Arise and depart, for this is no rest for you; because of uncleanness that destroys, with a grievous destruction.

11 If a man should walk in a false spirit and speak lies, saying, “I will prophesy to you of wine and strong drink,” he would be the prophet of this people.

Promised Gathering and Deliverance

12 I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will put them together like sheep of Bozrah, like a flock in the midst of its fold; they shall make a great noise because of the multitude of men. 13 The Breaker goes up before them; they break through and pass the gate, and go out by it; their king passes before them, with the the LORD at their head.
