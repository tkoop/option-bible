The Conditions for Receiving Wisdom

1 My son, if you receive my words and store up my commandments within you, 2 inclining your ear to wisdom and directing your heart toward understanding— 3 indeed, if you call out for insight and cry aloud for understanding, 4 and if you seek her as silver and search for her as for hidden treasures— 5 then you will comprehend the fear of the LORD and discover the knowledge of God.

The LORD's Gift and Wisdom's Indwelling

6 For the LORD gives wisdom; from His mouth proceed knowledge and understanding. 7 He stores up sound wisdom for the upright; He is a shield to those who walk in integrity, 8 guarding the paths of justice and preserving the way of His saints. 9 Then you will discern righteousness, justice, and equity—indeed, every good path.

10 For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will be pleasing to your soul. 11 Discretion will watch over you, and understanding will guard you.

Deliverance from Perverse Men and the Adulteress

12 To rescue you from the way of evil and from men who speak perversities: those who forsake the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness, who delight in doing evil and exult in the perversities of evil, whose paths are crooked and who are devious in their ways. 13 14 15

16 To rescue you from the adulterous woman, from the wayward woman whose words are smooth— 17 she who forsakes the companion of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God. 18 For her house sinks down to death, and her paths lead to the dead. 19 None who go in to her return, nor do they attain the paths of life.

The Destinies of the Righteous and the Wicked

20 Thus you will walk in the way of good men and keep to the paths of the righteous. 21 For the upright will dwell in the land, and the blameless will remain in it; 22 but the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the treacherous will be uprooted from it.
