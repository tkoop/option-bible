The LORD's Restoration of Judah and Judgment of the Nations

1 For behold, in those days and at that very time, when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem— 2 I shall gather all the nations and conduct them down into the Valley of Jehoshaphat, and there I shall contend with them in judgment on behalf of My people and My inheritance, Israel, whom they dispersed among the nations and whose land they partitioned. 3 They cast lots for My people; they bartered a boy for a harlot’s hire and sold a girl for wine, which they then drank.

4 And what claim have you upon Me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the districts of Philistia? Are you requiting Me? If you are requiting Me, I shall swiftly return your recompense upon your own heads. 5 For you appropriated My silver and My gold, and conveyed My choicest treasures into your temples. 6 You sold the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem to the Greeks, so as to exile them far from their native territory. 7 Behold, I shall rouse them from the place to which you sold them, and I shall return your recompense upon your own heads. 8 Moreover, I shall sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of Judah, and they shall sell them to the Sabeans, to a nation far away, for the LORD has spoken.

The Nations Summoned to Holy War

9 Proclaim this among the nations: Consecrate a war; awaken the mighty men; let all the men of war draw near and ascend. 10 Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears; let even the weakling declare, “I am a mighty man.” 11 Hasten and come, all you nations from every side, and gather yourselves there. Bring down Your mighty ones, O the LORD.

12 Let the nations rouse themselves and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I shall sit in judgment over all the surrounding nations. 13 Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe; come and tread the grapes, for the winepress is full and the vats overflow, because their wickedness is great.

The Day of the LORD and the Renewal of Zion

14 Multitudes, multitudes in the Valley of Decision! For the day of the LORD is near in the Valley of Decision. 15 The sun and the moon are darkened, and the stars withhold their radiance.

16 The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem, so that the heavens and the earth quake. Yet the LORD is a refuge for His people and a stronghold for the sons of Israel. 17 Thus you shall know that I am the LORD your God, dwelling in Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem shall be holy, and no stranger shall ever again pass through her.

18 And in that day the mountains shall drip with new wine, the hills shall flow with milk, and all the streambeds of Judah shall run with water; a fountain shall issue from the house of the LORD and irrigate the Valley of Shittim. 19 Egypt shall become a desolation, and Edom a desolate wilderness, because of the violence done to the sons of Judah, in whose land they shed innocent blood. 20 But Judah shall abide forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation. 21 I shall avenge their blood, blood that I have not yet avenged, for the LORD dwells in Zion.
