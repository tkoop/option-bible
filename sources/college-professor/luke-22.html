The Plot to Betray Jesus

1 Now the Feast of Unleavened Bread, designated the Passover, was approaching; 2 and the chief priests and scribes were anxiously seeking some means by which they might put Him to death, for they stood in fear of the people. 3 Then Satan entered into Judas, who was called Iscariot, one of the number of the Twelve; 4 and he went away and conferred with the chief priests and the captains of the temple guard concerning how he might betray Him to them. 5 They were gratified and covenanted to give him silver. 6 He consented, and thereafter sought an opportune moment to betray Him to them apart from the crowd.

Preparations for the Passover

7 The day of Unleavened Bread arrived, on which the Passover lamb was obligatory to be sacrificed; 8 and He dispatched Peter and John, saying, "Go and prepare the Passover for us, that we may partake of it."

9 They inquired of Him, "Where do You wish us to prepare it?"

10 He replied, "Behold, upon your entering the city, a man bearing a water jar will meet you; follow him into the house he enters, 11 and say to the master of that house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest chamber in which I may eat the Passover with My disciples?"' 12 And he will show you a large upper room already furnished; make your preparations there."

13 They departed and discovered everything precisely as He had foretold, and they prepared the Passover.

The Last Supper and Its Teachings

14 When the appointed hour arrived, He reclined at table, and the apostles with Him.

15 He said to them, "I have ardently longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer; 16 for I tell you that I shall not eat of it again until it is consummated in the kingdom of God."

17 Receiving a cup, He gave thanks and said, "Take this and distribute it among yourselves."

18 "For I tell you that from this moment I will not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

19 Taking bread, He gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of Me."

20 In like manner, after supper, He took the cup, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out for you."

21 "But behold, the hand of My betrayer is with Mine on the table."

22 "For the Son of Man goes as it has been ordained; nevertheless, woe to that man through whom He is betrayed!"

23 They began to deliberate among themselves which of them could be about to perpetrate this; 24 and a contention arose among them as to which of them should be esteemed the greatest.

25 He said to them, "The kings of the nations lord it over them, and those exercising authority over them are styled benefactors; 26 but it shall not be so among you. Rather, let the greatest among you become as the youngest, and the leader as one who serves. 27 For who is greater: the one reclining at table, or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining? Yet I am in your midst as One who serves."

28 "You are those who have remained with Me throughout My trials; 29 and I confer on you a kingdom, just as My Father conferred one on Me, 30 that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and sit upon thrones adjudicating the twelve tribes of Israel."

31 "Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has demanded to sift you like wheat; 32 but I have prayed for you that your faith may not lapse; and when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."

33 Peter said to Him, "Lord, I am prepared to go with You both to prison and to death."

34 Jesus said, "I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know Me."

35 He said to them, "When I sent you forth without purse, bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?"

They replied, "Nothing."

36 He said to them, "But now let him who has a purse take it, and likewise a bag; and let him who has no sword sell his cloak and purchase one."

37 "For I tell you that this Scripture must be fulfilled in Me: 'And He was reckoned among the transgressors.' Indeed, what is written concerning Me is reaching its consummation."

38 They said, "Look, Lord, here are two swords."

He said to them, "That suffices."

Gethsemane, Arrest, and the Sanhedrin

39 Going out, He proceeded, as was His custom, to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed Him; 40 and when He reached the place, He said to them, "Pray that you may not enter into temptation."

41 He withdrew from them about a short distance and, kneeling down, prayed, 42 saying, "Father, if You are willing, remove this cup from Me; nevertheless, not My will, but Yours, be done."

43 An angel from heaven appeared to Him, strengthening Him; 44 and being in agony, He prayed still more fervently, and His sweat became like great drops of blood falling to the ground.

45 Rising from prayer, He came to the disciples and found them sleeping from sorrow; 46 and He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Rise and pray that you may not enter into temptation."

47 While He was still speaking, behold, a crowd appeared, and Judas, one of the Twelve, was leading them; he drew near to Jesus to kiss Him.

48 Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?"

49 Those around Him, perceiving what was imminent, said, "Lord, shall we strike with the sword?"

50 One of them struck the servant of the high priest and severed his right ear. 51 Jesus answered, "No more of this!" And touching the ear, He healed him.

52 Jesus said to the chief priests, the officers of the temple, and the elders who had come out against Him, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs? 53 When I was with you daily in the temple, you did not stretch out your hands against Me; but this is your hour, and the power of darkness."

54 They seized Him, led Him away, and brought Him into the high priest's house. Peter followed at a distance; 55 and when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter sat down among them.

56 A servant girl, seeing him as he sat in the light and looking intently at him, said, "This man too was with Him."

57 He denied it, saying, "Woman, I do not know Him."

58 A short while later another saw him and said, "You also are one of them."

Peter said, "Man, I am not."

59 About an hour afterward another insisted, saying, "Truly this man was with Him, for he is a Galilean as well."

60 Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

61 The Lord turned and looked at Peter; and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how He had said to him, "Before the rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times."

62 He went out and wept bitterly.

63 The men who were holding Jesus in custody mocked Him as they beat Him; 64 and blindfolding Him, they kept demanding, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck You?" 65 They uttered many other blasphemies against Him.

66 When day came, the elders of the people, together with the chief priests and scribes, assembled and led Him away to their council, saying,

67 "If You are the Christ, tell us."

He said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe; 68 and if I question you, you will not answer. 69 But from now on the Son of Man shall be seated at the right hand of the power of God."

70 They all said, "Are You then the Son of God?"

He said to them, "You say that I am."

71 They said, "What need have we of further testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from His own mouth."
