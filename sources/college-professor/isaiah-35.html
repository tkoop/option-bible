The Efflorescence of the Wasteland and the Manifestation of the LORD’s Glory

1 The wilderness and the parched land shall exult; the desert shall rejoice and burst into bloom like the crocus. 2 It shall blossom abundantly and rejoice with exultation and song; the glory of Lebanon shall be conferred upon it, together with the majesty of Carmel and Sharon. They shall behold the glory of the LORD, the majesty of our God.

Exhortation to the Faint-Hearted and Fearful

3 Strengthen the enfeebled hands, and fortify the tottering knees. 4 Say to those whose hearts are dismayed, “Take courage; do not be afraid. Behold, your God is coming with vengeance, with divine recompense; He will come and save you.”

The Miraculous Restoration of Creation and the Holy Highway

5 Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. 6 Then the lame shall leap like a deer, and the tongue of the mute shall sing for joy. For waters shall burst forth in the wilderness, and streams in the desert. 7 The burning sand shall become a pool, and the thirsty ground, springs of water; in the haunt of jackals, where they recline, grass shall become reeds and rushes.

8 A highway shall be there, and it shall be called the Way of Holiness. The unclean shall not traverse it, and it shall be for those who walk the way; even fools shall not stray. 9 No lion shall be there, nor shall any ravenous beast ascend it; none shall be found there, but the redeemed shall walk there. 10 And the ransomed of the LORD shall return and come to Zion with singing; everlasting joy shall crown their heads. They shall obtain gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.
