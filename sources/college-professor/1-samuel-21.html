David Seeks Provisions at Nob

1 David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech came trembling to meet David and said to him, "Why are you alone, with no one accompanying you?"

2 David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king has charged me with a confidential matter and said to me, 'Let no one know anything of the matter about which I am sending you, or of the command I have given you.' As for the young men, I have directed them to such-and-such a place. 3 Now what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever is available."

4 The priest answered David, "There is no ordinary bread at hand, but there is consecrated bread—provided the young men have abstained from women."

5 David answered the priest, "Women have indeed been kept from us, as is customary when I set out. The vessels of the young men are holy even on an ordinary journey, and how much more today will they be sanctified."

6 So the priest gave him the consecrated bread, for no bread was there except the bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before the LORD, to be replaced by hot bread on the day it was taken away.

7 Now one of Saul's servants was there that day, detained before the LORD; his name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul's herdsmen.

8 David said to Ahimelech, "Do you have a spear or sword here at hand? I brought neither my sword nor my weapons with me, for the king's business was urgent."

9 The priest said, "Here is the sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah; it is wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you wish to take it, take it, for there is no other here."

David said, "There is none like it; give it to me."

David Flees to Gath

10 On that day David arose and fled from Saul, and he came to Achish, king of Gath. 11 The servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? Did they not sing of him in the dances, saying, 'Saul has slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

12 David took these words to heart and was greatly afraid of Achish, king of Gath.

David Feigns Madness before Achish

13 He altered his conduct before them, feigned insanity in their presence, scrawled on the doors of the gate, and let his spittle run down his beard.

14 Achish said to his servants, "Behold, you see a man who is mad. Why have you brought him to me? 15 Do I lack madmen, that you have brought this fellow to act the madman before me? Shall this man come into my house?"
