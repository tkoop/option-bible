The Divine Summons and the Covenantal Promise to Abram

1Now the LORD said to Abram, “Go forth from your country, from your kindred, and from your father’s house, to the land that I will show you. 2I will make of you a great nation, I will bless you, I will make your name great, and you shall be a blessing. 3I will bless those who bless you, and whoever dishonors you I will curse; and in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.”

Abram’s Obedient Pilgrimage and the Erection of Memorial Altars

4So Abram departed, precisely as the LORD had spoken to him, and Lot went with him; Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran. 5Abram took Sarai his wife, Lot his nephew, all the wealth they had accumulated, and the people they had acquired in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan; thus they entered the land of Canaan.

6Abram traversed the land as far as the place at Shechem, to the oak of Moreh, while the Canaanites were then dwelling in the land. 7Then the LORD appeared to Abram and said, “To your offspring I will give this land.” Thereupon Abram built an altar there to the LORD, who had appeared to him. 8From there he moved to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent, with Bethel to the west and Ai to the east; there he built an altar to the LORD and invoked the name of the LORD. 9Then Abram continued his journey, pressing steadily southward toward the Negev.

Famine, Fear, and Providential Deliverance in Egypt

10Now a severe famine afflicted the land, so Abram descended into Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine was grievous in the land. 11As he was about to enter Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, “Behold, I know that you are a woman of striking beauty; 12when the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife,’ and they will kill me, but they will spare you. 13Say that you are my sister, so that it may be well with me on your account and my life may be preserved for your sake.”

14When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was exceedingly beautiful. 15The princes of Pharaoh saw her and extolled her to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh’s house. 16For her sake he treated Abram well: he received sheep, cattle, male and female donkeys, male and female servants, and camels. 17But the LORD afflicted Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues because of Sarai, Abram’s wife. 18So Pharaoh summoned Abram and said, “What is this you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife? 19Why did you say, ‘She is my sister,’ so that I took her as my wife? Now, behold, here is your wife; take her and depart.” 20Pharaoh then commanded his men concerning him, and they sent him away, together with his wife and all that he possessed.
