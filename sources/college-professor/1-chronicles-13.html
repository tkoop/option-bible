David’s Counsel with the Leaders and the Assembly

1 David conferred with all his leaders—the commanders of thousands and of hundreds. 2 David said to the whole assembly of Israel, “If it seems good to you, and if this proceeds from the LORD our God, let us send word throughout to our brothers who remain in all the territories of Israel, together with the priests and Levites in their cities and pasturelands, that they may assemble with us. 3 And let us bring back the ark of our God to us, for we did not seek Him in the days of Saul.” 4 The whole assembly consented to act, for the matter seemed right in the eyes of all the people.

The Retrieval of the Ark from Kiriath-jearim

5 David assembled all Israel—from the Shihor of Egypt to Hamath—to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim. 6 David and all Israel went up to Baalah, that is, to Kiriath-jearim in Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim—the ark that is called by the Name. 7 They conveyed the ark of God from the house of Abinadab on a new cart, while Uzzah and Ahio guided the cart. 8 David and all Israel celebrated before God with all their might, with songs and with harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

The Death of Uzzah and the Ark’s Sojourn with Obed-edom

9 When they reached the threshing floor of Kidon, Uzzah stretched out his hand to steady the ark, for the oxen had stumbled. 10 Then the anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and He struck him down because he had laid his hand upon the ark; so he died there before God. 11 David was incensed because the LORD had broken out against Uzzah, and he called that place Perez-uzzah, as it is still known today. 12 David feared God that day and said, “How, then, shall I bring the ark of God to me?” 13 So David did not remove the ark to the City of David; instead, he turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite. 14 The ark of God remained in the house of Obed-edom for three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and all his household.
