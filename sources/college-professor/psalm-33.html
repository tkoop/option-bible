An Exhortation to Exultant Praise

1 Exult in the LORD, O righteous; praise is comely for the upright. 2 Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre; make melody to Him with the ten-stringed harp. 3 Sing to Him a new song; play skillfully with a shout of triumph.

The Creative Efficacy of the Divine Word

4 For the word of the LORD is upright, and all His work is done in faithfulness. 5 He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the LORD's steadfast love. 6 By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and by the breath of His mouth all their host. 7 He gathers the waters of the sea as a heap; He puts the deeps in storehouses. 8 Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of Him. 9 For He spoke, and it came to be; He commanded, and it stood firm.

The Futility of Human Counsel and the Permanence of Divine Purpose

10 The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nothing; He frustrates the thoughts of the peoples. 11 The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the thoughts of His heart to all generations. 12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people He has chosen as His heritage.

The LORD's Omniscient Regard and the Hope of the Faithful

13 The LORD looks down from heaven; He sees all the children of man. 14 From His dwelling place He looks out on all the inhabitants of the earth— 15 He who fashions the hearts of them all and observes all their deeds. 16 The king is not saved by his great army; a warrior is not delivered by his great strength. 17 The horse is a false hope for safety, and by its great might it cannot rescue. 18 Behold, the eye of the LORD is on those who fear Him, on those who hope in His steadfast love, 19 that He may deliver their souls from death and keep them alive in famine. 20 Our souls wait for the LORD; He is our help and our shield. 21 For our hearts rejoice in Him, because we trust in His holy name. 22 Let Your steadfast love, O the LORD, be upon us, as we hope in You.
