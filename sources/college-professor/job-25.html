Bildad’s Final Rejoinder: The Overwhelming Majesty of the Divine

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

The Innumerable Hosts and Universal Light of God

2 “Dominion and dread attend Him; He establishes tranquility in His exalted realms. 3 His hosts are beyond numbering, and His light shines upon all

Human Frailty and the Impossibility of Righteousness

4 “How, then, can mortal man be justified before God? How can one born of woman attain purity? 5 Behold, even the moon lacks luster, and the stars are not spotless in His sight. 6 How much less, then, is man, who is but a maggot, and the son of man, who is but a worm!”
