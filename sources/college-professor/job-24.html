The Withholding of Divine Seasons of Judgment

1 Why does the Almighty not reserve times for judgment? Why do those who know Him not see His days? 2 Some displace boundary markers and pasture stolen flocks. 3 They drive off the orphan’s donkey and seize the widow’s ox as collateral. 4 They thrust the indigent from the road, and the afflicted of the land are forced into concealment.

The Despoliation of the Vulnerable and the Indigent

5 Behold, like wild asses in the wilderness, they go forth to their labor, foraging for prey; the wasteland itself becomes sustenance for their children. 6 By night they reap in the field, and they glean the vineyard of the wicked. 7 Without raiment they lie down naked, having no covering against the cold. 8 Drenched by mountain torrents, they cling to the rock for want of shelter. 9 They tear the orphan from the breast and exact a pledge from the poor. 10 Naked and unclothed, they wander about; famished, they carry sheaves of grain. 11 They press oil within their enclosures and tread the winepresses, yet they thirst. 12 From the city the dying groan, and the souls of the wounded cry out, yet God charges no one with wrongdoing.

Crimes Perpetrated Beneath the Veil of Night

13 Then there are those who rebel against the light; they do not know its ways or stay on its paths. 14 At dawn the murderer rises; he slays the poor and needy, and by night he is like a thief. 15 The adulterer’s eye waits for twilight, saying, “No eye will see me,” and he conceals his face. 16 In darkness they break into houses; by day they seal themselves in; they never see the light. 17 For to them, deep darkness is morning; they are friends with the terrors of darkness.

The Ephemeral Exaltation of the Wicked

18 They are but foam upon the surface of the water; their portion of the land is cursed, so that no one turns toward their vineyards. 19 As drought and heat consume the snow waters, so Sheol consumes those who have sinned. 20 The womb forgets them; worms feed sweetly upon them; they are no longer remembered, and wickedness is broken like a tree. 21 They prey upon the barren and childless, and show no kindness to the widow. 22 Yet God, by His power, sustains the mighty; they rise up, though they do not trust in their own life. 23 He grants them security, and they rest in it, but His eyes are upon their ways. 24 They are exalted for a moment, then they are gone; they are brought low and gathered up like all others; they are cut off like heads of grain. 25 If this is not so, then who can prove me a liar and reduce my words to nothing?
