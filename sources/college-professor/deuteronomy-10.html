The Renewal of the Covenant Tablets

1At that time the LORD said to me, “Carve for yourself two stone tablets like the former ones, and ascend to Me upon the mountain, and fashion for yourself an ark of wood. 2And I will inscribe upon the tablets the words that were upon the first tablets, which you shattered, and you shall deposit them in the ark.”

3So I fashioned an ark of acacia wood, and I carved two stone tablets like the first ones, and I ascended the mountain with the two tablets in my hand. 4And He inscribed upon the tablets according to the first writing the Ten Commandments, which the LORD had spoken to you on the mountain out of the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly; and the LORD gave them to me. 5Then I turned and descended from the mountain, and I placed the tablets in the ark that I had made, as the LORD had commanded me, and there they remain.

Journeys, Priesthood, and the Consecration of the Levites

6Now the Israelites journeyed from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah; there Aaron died and was buried, and Eleazar his son ministered as priest in his stead. 7From there they traveled to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of watercourses.

8At that time the LORD set apart the tribe of Levi to bear the ark of the covenant of the LORD, to stand before the LORD to minister to Him and to bless in His name, unto this day. 9Therefore Levi has no portion or inheritance among his brothers; the LORD is his inheritance, as the LORD your God spoke to him.

10And I remained upon the mountain forty days and forty nights, as at the first time; and the LORD hearkened to me that time also, and the LORD would not destroy you. 11Then the LORD said to me, “Arise, proceed on your journey before the people, that they may enter and possess the land that I swore to their fathers to give them.”

The LORD's Requirements and Israel's Distinctive Calling

12And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God require of you, but to fear the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, and to love Him, and to serve the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, 13to keep the commandments of the LORD and His statutes, which I am enjoining upon you today, for your well-being?

14Behold, to the LORD your God belong the heavens, even the highest heavens, and the earth with all that is therein. 15Yet the LORD set His love upon your fathers to love them, and He chose their offspring after them—you—above all peoples, as it is this day.

16Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be obstinate no longer. 17For the LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great, the mighty, and the awesome God, who shows no favoritism and accepts no bribe.

18He executes justice for the orphan and the widow, and loves the sojourner, giving him food and clothing. 19So you must love the sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

20You shall fear the LORD your God; you shall serve Him, and to Him you shall cleave, and by His name you shall swear. 21He is your praise and He is your God, who has wrought for you these great and awesome things that your eyes have beheld. 22Your fathers descended into Egypt with seventy persons, and now the LORD your God has made you as numerous as the stars of heaven.
