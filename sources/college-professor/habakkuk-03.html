Habakkuk's Prayer and Reverent Plea

1 A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, set to Shigionoth. 2 The LORD, I have heard the report concerning You; I stand in awe, the LORD, of Your work. Revive it in the midst of the years; in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath remember mercy.

The Theophany of the LORD in Judgment

3 God comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His glory covers the heavens, and His praise fills the earth. 4 His radiance is like the light; rays flash from His hand, and there His power is hidden.

5 Plague goes before Him, and pestilence follows in His steps. 6 He stood and measured the earth; He looked and startled the nations. The ancient mountains crumbled; the everlasting hills collapsed. His ways are eternal.

7 I saw the tents of Cushan in distress; the curtains of Midian trembled. 8 Were You angry with the rivers, the LORD? Was Your wrath against the streams? Did You rage against the sea when You rode upon Your horses, Your chariots of salvation?

9 You bared Your bow; sworn were the rods of chastisement. Selah. You split the earth with rivers. 10 The mountains saw You and writhed; torrents of water swept by; the deep roared and lifted its hands on high.

11 Sun and moon stood still in their places at the flash of Your flying arrows, at the gleam of Your glittering spear. 12 In wrath You strode through the earth; in anger You threshed the nations.

13 You went out to save Your people, to save Your anointed. You crushed the head of the house of the wicked and stripped him from head to foot. Selah. 14 With his own spear You pierced his head when his warriors stormed out to scatter me, gloating as though to devour the poor in secret. 15 You trampled the sea with Your horses, churning the great waters.

The Prophet's Trembling and Resolute Faith

16 I heard, and my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the sound; decay entered my bones; I trembled where I stood. Yet I will wait in quietness for the day of distress to come upon the nation that invades us.

17 Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls, 18 yet I will rejoice in the LORD; I will exult in the God of my salvation. 19 The LORD God is my strength; He makes my feet like those of a deer and enables me to walk on the heights. For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments.
