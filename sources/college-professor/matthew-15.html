The Pharisees Challenge Jesus concerning the Elders' Traditions

1 Then the Pharisees and the scribes approached Jesus from Jerusalem, saying, 2 “Why do your disciples contravene the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread.”

3 But He answered and said to them, “Why do you also contravene the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition? 4 For God said, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’ and, ‘Whoever reviles father or mother shall surely be put to death.’ 5 But you say, ‘Whoever says to father or mother, “Whatever benefit you might have received from me is a gift”— 6 he need not honor his father or his mother.’ Thus you have nullified the word of God for the sake of your tradition.

7 “You hypocrites! Isaiah prophesied rightly concerning you when he said: 8 ‘These people honor Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me. 9 In vain do they worship Me, teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’”

The True Source of Defilement

10 Then, having summoned the crowd, He said to them, “Hear and understand: 11 it is not what enters the mouth that defiles a person, but what proceeds from the mouth; this is what defiles a person.”

12 Then the disciples came and said to Him, “Do You know that the Pharisees were scandalized when they heard this word?”

13 But He answered and said, “Every plant that My heavenly Father has not planted will be uprooted. 14 Leave them alone; they are blind guides. And if a blind man guides a blind man, both will fall into a pit.”

15 But Peter answered and said to Him, “Explain this parable to us.”

16 “Are you also still without understanding?” He said.

17 “Do you not yet understand that everything entering the mouth passes into the stomach and is expelled into the sewer? 18 But what proceeds out of the mouth comes from the heart, and these things defile a person. 19 For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, acts of sexual immorality, thefts, false testimonies, and blasphemies. 20 These are the things that defile a person; but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a person.”

The Canaanite Woman's Great Faith

21 Then Jesus went out from there and withdrew into the regions of Tyre and Sidon. 22 And behold, a Canaanite woman from those borders came out and cried out, saying, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on me! My daughter is severely demon-possessed.”

23 But He did not answer her a word. And His disciples came and begged Him, saying, “Send her away, for she is crying out after us.”

24 But He answered and said, “I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”

25 But she came and worshiped Him, saying, “Lord, help me!”

26 But He answered and said, “It is not good to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs.”

27 And she said, “Yes, Lord; yet even the dogs eat from the crumbs that fall from their masters' table.”

28 Then Jesus answered and said to her, “O woman, great is your faith! Let it be done for you as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that hour.

Healing and the Miraculous Feeding in Galilee

29 Then Jesus, having departed from there, came beside the Sea of Galilee, and having gone up on the mountain, sat there. 30 And great crowds came to Him, having with them lame, blind, crippled, mute, and many others, and they cast them at His feet, and He healed them. 31 So that the crowd marveled, seeing the mute speaking, the crippled made whole, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.

32 Then Jesus, having called His disciples, said, “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have remained with Me now three days and have nothing to eat; and I do not want to send them away hungry, lest they faint on the way.”

33 And the disciples said to Him, “Where in a desolate place could we get so many loaves as to satisfy so great a crowd?”

34 And Jesus said to them, “How many loaves do you have?”

And they said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”

35 And He commanded the crowd to sit down on the ground; 36 and taking the seven loaves and the fish, and having given thanks, He broke them and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds. 37 And all ate and were satisfied, and they took up seven baskets full of the broken pieces that remained.

38 Now those eating were four thousand men, besides women and children. 39 And having dismissed the crowds, He got into the boat and came to the region of Magadan.
