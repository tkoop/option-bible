The Restoration of Worship and the Feast of Booths

1 In the seventh month, after the Israelites had settled in their cities, the people gathered at Jerusalem as a single body. 2 Then Jeshua son of Jozadak, with his priestly brethren, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, with his associates, arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, so that they might offer burnt offerings upon it, as it is written in the Law of Moses, the man of God. 3 They established the altar upon its bases, and although they were in dread of the peoples of the lands, they offered burnt offerings upon it to the LORD, both morning and evening burnt offerings. 4 They kept the Feast of Booths, as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings in their prescribed number, according to the ordinance for each day. 5 Afterward they offered the continual burnt offering, together with the offerings of the new moons and of all the the LORD's appointed holy convocations, as well as every freewill offering willingly presented to the LORD. 6 From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, although the foundation of the LORD's temple had not yet been laid.

Procurement of Materials and Appointment of Overseers

7 Then they gave silver to the stonecutters and carpenters, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and Tyrians, so that cedar timber from Lebanon might be conveyed by sea to Joppa, in accordance with the authorization of Cyrus, king of Persia. 8 In the second month of the second year after their arrival at the house of God in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the rest of their brethren—the priests, the Levites, and all who had come from the captivity to Jerusalem—began the work and appointed the Levites, from twenty years old and upward, to oversee the work of the house of the LORD. 9 Then Jeshua with his sons and brethren, Kadmiel with his sons, the sons of Judah, and the sons of Henadad with their sons and brethren, the Levites, stood as one to supervise the workmen in the house of God.

The Foundation Laid and the People's Acclamation

10 When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, the priests stood in their vestments with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD, according to the ordinance of David, king of Israel. 11 They sang in praise and thanksgiving to the LORD, “He is good, for His loving devotion to Israel endures forever,” and all the people shouted with a great shout of praise to the LORD, because the foundation of the house of the LORD had been laid. 12 Yet many of the aged priests, Levites, and heads of fathers' houses, who had seen the first house, wept with a loud voice when this house was founded before their eyes, while many others shouted aloud for joy. 13 Thus the people could not distinguish the sound of the joyful shout from the sound of the weeping, for the people shouted with a great shout, and the sound was heard afar off.
