The LORD Announces the Final Plague and the Plundering of Egypt

1 The LORD declared to Moses, “I shall inflict one further plague upon Pharaoh and upon Egypt; thereafter he will release you from this place. When he releases you, he will drive you out altogether from here.” 2 “Now speak in the hearing of the people, that every man request from his neighbor and every woman from her neighbor articles of silver and articles of gold.”

Israel's Favor and Moses' Eminence

3 The LORD granted the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians. Moreover, Moses was exceedingly eminent in the land of Egypt, in the sight of Pharaoh's servants and in the sight of the people.

The Midnight Judgment Foretold

4 Moses said, “Thus says the LORD: ‘About midnight I shall go out into the midst of Egypt, 5 and every firstborn in the land of Egypt shall perish, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sits upon his throne to the firstborn of the maidservant who is behind the millstones, and all the firstborn of the beasts. 6 There shall be a great cry throughout all the land of Egypt, such as has never occurred nor shall occur again. 7 But against all the children of Israel not a dog shall move its tongue, against man or beast, so that you may know that the LORD distinguishes between Egypt and Israel. 8 All these your servants shall come down to me and prostrate themselves before me, saying, “Go out, you and all the people who follow you,” and after that I will go out.’”

He went out from Pharaoh in fierce anger.

Pharaoh's Intransigence and the Hardening of His Heart

9 The LORD said to Moses, “Pharaoh will not heed you, so that My wonders may be multiplied in the land of Egypt.” 10 Moses and Aaron performed all these wonders before Pharaoh, yet the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not release the children of Israel from his land.
