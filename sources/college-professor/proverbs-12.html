The Loving Discipline of Wisdom

1 He who embraces discipline embraces knowledge, while he who spurns correction betrays a brutish deficiency of understanding. 2 A good man draws favor from the LORD, but He condemns the man whose designs are wicked. 3 No human being is rendered secure by wickedness, whereas the righteous remain immovable. 4 A wife of valor is her husband’s diadem, whereas she who brings disgrace is like putrefaction in his bones.

5 The designs of the righteous are equitable, but the counsels of the wicked are duplicitous. 6 The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood, whereas the mouth of the upright rescues them. 7 The wicked are overturned and are no more, but the house of the righteous endures.

Work, Words, and the Ordering of Life

8 A man is commended according to his prudence, but a perverted heart is held in contempt. 9 Better to be of humble station and possess a servant than to be vainglorious and destitute of bread. 10 The righteous has regard for the life of his beast, but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel. 11 He who tills his soil shall be satisfied with bread, but he who pursues vain illusions lacks discernment.

12 The wicked covet the spoils of malefactors, but the root of the righteous flourishes. 13 An evil man is snared by the transgression of his lips, but the righteous comes forth from distress. 14 From the fruit of his mouth a man is satisfied with good, and the recompense of his hands returns to him.

Truth, Peace, and the Delight of the LORD

15 The way of a fool seems right in his own eyes, but a wise man heeds counsel. 16 A fool’s vexation is disclosed at once, but a shrewd man conceals disgrace. 17 He who utters truth proclaims what is just, whereas a false witness speaks deceit. 18 There is one whose rash words are like the thrusts of a sword, but the tongue of the wise is a remedy.

19 A lip of truth stands forever, but a tongue of falsehood endures but an instant. 20 Deceit resides in the heart of those who plot evil, but counselors of peace have joy. 21 No calamity shall befall the righteous, but the wicked are glutted with evil.

Diligence, Friendship, and the Way of Life

22 Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD, but doers of faithfulness are His delight. 23 A shrewd man conceals knowledge, but the heart of fools proclaims folly. 24 The hand of the industrious shall bear rule, but indolence becomes servile labor. 25 Anxiety in a man’s heart weighs it down, but a good word makes it glad.

26 A righteous man is cautious in friendship, but the way of the wicked leads them astray. 27 A slothful man does not roast his prey, but the wealth of a diligent man is precious. 28 In the path of righteousness there is life, and in its pathway there is no death.
