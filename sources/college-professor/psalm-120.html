A Song of Ascents: Deliverance from Deceit and Dwelling Among the Hostile

1In my distress I invoked the LORD, and He responded to me. 2Rescue my soul, O the LORD, from mendacious lips and from a perfidious tongue.

3What retribution shall be meted out to you, and what further affliction shall be added to you, O deceitful tongue? 4The sharpened arrows of a mighty one, together with the glowing embers of the broom shrub.

5Alas for me, for I sojourn in Meshech and dwell among the tents of Kedar. 6For far too long my soul has dwelt with those who abhor peace. 7I am disposed toward peace; yet whenever I speak, they are disposed toward war.
