Desolation and the Plea for a Name

1 On that day seven women will seize a single man, declaring, “We will consume our own bread and attire ourselves in our own garments; only let your name be invoked over us—remove our disgrace!” 2 On that day the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious, and the land’s fruit will be the pride and splendor of Israel’s remnant.

The Sanctification of the Remnant

3 Then every survivor in Zion and every remnant in Jerusalem will be proclaimed holy—each one inscribed for life in Jerusalem—

Purification and the Divine Canopy

4 when the Lord has washed away the filth of Zion’s daughters and purged the bloodshed from Jerusalem’s midst by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning. 5 Then the the LORD will create over all the site of Mount Zion and over her assemblies a cloud and smoke by day, and the radiance of a flaming fire by night; for over all the glory there will be a canopy. 6 It will be a booth affording shade from the heat by day, and a refuge and hiding place from storm and rain.
