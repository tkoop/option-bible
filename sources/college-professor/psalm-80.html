An Asaphite Lament and Petition for Restoration

1 For the director of music, to the melody “Lilies”—a testimony of Asaph. Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, You who shepherd Joseph like a flock; You who are enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth. 2 Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, stir up Your might and come to our salvation. 3 Restore us, O God; cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.

4 the LORD God of Hosts, how long will You let Your wrath smolder against the prayers of Your people? 5 You have fed them with the bread of tears, and You have made them drink tears by the bowlful. 6 You have made us a cause of contention to our neighbors, and our enemies deride us. 7 Restore us, O God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine, that we may be saved.

The Vine of Israel and Its Devastation

8 You removed a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and planted it. 9 You cleared ground before it, and it took root and filled the land. 10 The mountains were mantled with its shade, and the cedars of God with its boughs. 11 It stretched its branches to the Sea and its shoots to the River.

12 Why have You torn down its walls, so that every passerby plucks its fruit? 13 The boar from the forest ravages it, and the beasts of the field feed upon it.

Petition for the Restoration of the Davidic Son

14 Return, O God of Hosts; look down from heaven and see; attend to this vine, 15 the root that Your right hand planted, and the son You have raised up for Yourself. 16 It is cut down and burned with fire; at the rebuke of Your countenance Your people perish.

17 Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, upon the son of man whom You have made strong for Yourself. 18 Then we shall not turn away from You; revive us, and we shall invoke Your name. 19 Restore us, the LORD God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine, that we may be saved.
