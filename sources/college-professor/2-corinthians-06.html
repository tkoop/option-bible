Fellow Workers and the Acceptable Time

1 As fellow laborers with God, we entreat you not to receive the grace of God to no purpose. 2 For He says, “In a propitious hour I hearkened to you, and on the day of salvation I came to your aid. Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.”

3 We afford no occasion for stumbling in anything, so that the ministry may not be reproached. 4 But, as servants of God, we commend ourselves in everything—in great endurance, in tribulations, in necessities, in straits— 5 in beatings, in imprisonments, in tumults, in toils, in wakeful nights, in fastings; 6 in purity, in knowledge, in long-suffering, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in sincere love; 7 in the word of truth, in the power of God; through the weapons of righteousness, whether in the right hand or in the left; 8 through glory and dishonor, through ill report and good report; as deceivers, yet truthful; 9 as unknown, yet recognized; as dying, and behold, we live; as chastened, yet not put to death; 10 as sorrowful, yet ever rejoicing; as impoverished, yet enriching many; as having nothing, yet holding fast all things.

An Open Heart and Reciprocal Affection

11 Our mouth stands open to you, O Corinthians; our heart has been enlarged. 12 You are not constrained in us, but you are constrained in your own affections. 13 Now, in like recompense—I speak as to children—do you also widen your hearts.

The Call to Separation from Unbelief

14 Do not become yoked together with unbelievers; for what partnership has righteousness with lawlessness? or what communion has light with darkness? 15 And what concord has Christ with Belial? or what share has a believer with an unbeliever? 16 And what agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, as God said, “I will dwell in them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they shall be My people.”

17 Therefore, “Come out from the midst of them and be separate,” says the Lord, “and touch no unclean thing; and I will receive you.” 18 “And I will be a Father to you, and you shall be sons and daughters to Me,” says the Lord Almighty.
