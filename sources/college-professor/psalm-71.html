A Prayer for Refuge and Deliverance in Old Age

1 In You, O the LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame. 2 In Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline Your ear to me and save me. 3 Be to me a rock of refuge to which I may continually resort; You have ordained salvation for me, for You are my rock and my fortress. 4 Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and cruel man.

Confidence Born of Lifelong Faithfulness

5 For You, O Lord, the LORD, are my hope, my confidence from my youth. 6 From birth I have leaned upon You; You drew me forth from my mother's womb; my praise is ever of You. 7 I have become, as it were, a portent to multitudes, yet You remain my mighty refuge. 8 My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your glory all the day long. 9 Do not cast me off in the time of old age; do not forsake me when my strength is exhausted.

Enemies' Taunts and an Urgent Petition for Help

10 For my enemies speak concerning me, and those who watch for my life take counsel together, 11 saying,

"God has forsaken him; pursue and seize him, for there is no one to deliver."

12 O God, do not be far from me; O my God, make haste to help me.

Vindication of the Righteous and a Vow of Perpetual Praise

13 Let those who accuse my soul be put to shame and brought to ruin; let those who seek my calamity be clothed with reproach and dishonor. 14 But I shall hope continually, and I shall praise You yet more and more. 15 My mouth shall recount Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, for their number is beyond my reckoning. 16 I will come with the mighty acts of the Lord, the LORD; I will remember Your righteousness, Yours alone. 17 O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your wondrous deeds. 18 And even to old age and gray hair, O God, do not forsake me, until I proclaim Your might to a generation, Your power to all who are to come. 19 Your righteousness, O God, reaches the heavens; You who have done great things, O God, who is like You? 20 You, who have made me see many and bitter troubles, will revive me again; from the depths of the earth You will bring me up again. 21 You will increase my honor and comfort me once more. 22 I will also praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel. 23 My lips shall shout for joy when I sing praises to You, and my soul, which You have redeemed. 24 My tongue also shall recount Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my hurt are put to shame and confounded.
