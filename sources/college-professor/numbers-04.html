The Kohathite Service and Its Safeguards

1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2 “Take a census of the Kohathites among the Levites, by their clans and ancestral houses, 3 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who enters upon service to perform work in the Tent of Meeting.” 4 This is the service of the Kohathites in the Tent of Meeting: the most holy objects.

5 When the camp prepares to set out, Aaron and his sons shall go in and remove the veil of the screen and cover the ark of the testimony with it. 6 They shall place over it a covering of fine leather, spread over it a cloth wholly of blue, and insert its poles. 7 Over the table of the Presence they shall spread a blue cloth and place upon it the dishes, the pans, the bowls, and the pitchers for the drink offering; the perpetual bread shall be upon it. 8 They shall spread over these a scarlet cloth, cover them with a fine-leather covering, and insert its poles. 9 They shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light, its lamps, its tongs, its snuffers, and all its oil vessels with which they serve it. 10 They shall put it and all its vessels in a fine-leather covering and place them on the carrying frame. 11 Over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, cover it with a fine-leather covering, and insert its poles. 12 They shall take all the service vessels with which they serve in the sanctuary, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with a fine-leather covering, and place them on the carrying frame. 13 They shall remove the ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it. 14 They shall place upon it all its vessels—the firepans, the forks, the shovels, and the basins, all the vessels of the altar—and spread over it a fine-leather covering and insert its poles.

15 When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the sanctuary and all its vessels, as the camp sets out, the Kohathites shall come to carry them; but they must not touch the holy things, lest they die. These are the Kohathites' burden in the Tent of Meeting.

16 Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has oversight of the oil for the light, the spices, the continual incense, the anointing grain offering, and the oil for the whole Tabernacle and everything in it, in the sanctuary and its vessels.

17 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 18 “Do not let the Kohathite clans be cut off from among the Levites. 19 Do this for them, that they may live and not die when they approach the most holy objects: Aaron and his sons shall go in and assign each man to his service and burden. 20 But they must not go in to look at the holy things even for a moment, lest they die.”

The Gershonite Service

21 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 22 “Take a census of the Gershonites also, by their ancestral houses and clans, 23 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who enters upon service to perform work in the Tent of Meeting. 24 This is the service of the Gershonite clans, in work and in carrying burdens: 25 they shall carry the curtains of the Tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting with its covering, the fine-leather covering above it, the screen for the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, 26 the hangings of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance of the courtyard gate that is around the Tabernacle and the altar, their cords, and all the equipment for their service; and they shall perform all the work connected with these things. 27 All the service of the Gershonites, their carrying burdens and all their work, shall be under the direction of Aaron and his sons; and you shall assign to them all their duties as a charge. 28 This is the service of the Gershonite clans in the Tent of Meeting, and their charge is under the supervision of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.”

The Merarite Service

29 “The Merarites you shall number by their clans and ancestral houses, 30 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who enters upon service to perform work in the Tent of Meeting. 31 This is their charge for all their service in the Tent of Meeting: the frames of the Tabernacle, its bars, its pillars, and its bases; 32 the pillars of the courtyard all around, their bases, their pegs, and their cords, with all their equipment and all their service; and you shall assign by name the vessels of their charge. 33 This is the service of the Merarite clans, their entire service in the Tent of Meeting, under the supervision of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.”

The Census of the Levites

34 Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of the congregation numbered the Kohathites by their clans and ancestral houses, 35 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who entered the service for work in the Tent of Meeting. 36 Their numbered men by clans were 2,750. 37 These were the numbered men of the Kohathite clans, everyone who served in the Tent of Meeting, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the LORD's command through Moses. 38 The numbered men of the Gershonites by their clans and ancestral houses, 39 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who entered the service for work in the Tent of Meeting, 40 were 2,630 by their clans and ancestral houses. 41 These were the numbered men of the Gershonite clans, everyone who served in the Tent of Meeting, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the LORD's command. 42 The numbered men of the Merarites by their clans and ancestral houses, 43 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who entered the service for work in the Tent of Meeting, 44 were 3,200 by their clans. 45 These were the numbered men of the Merarite clans, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the LORD's command through Moses.

46 All the numbered men of the Levites, whom Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel numbered by their clans and ancestral houses, 47 from thirty years of age and upward to fifty, every man who came to do the service of ministry and the service of carrying burdens in the Tent of Meeting, 48 were 8,580. 49 According to the LORD's command, they were numbered by Moses, each man assigned to his service and burden; thus they were numbered by him, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
