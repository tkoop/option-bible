A Litany of Thanksgiving for the LORD’s Enduring Covenant Love

1 Offer thanksgiving to the LORD, for He is good; for His loving devotion endures forever. 2 Offer thanksgiving to the God of gods, for His loving devotion endures forever. 3 Offer thanksgiving to the Lord of lords, for His loving devotion endures forever.

4 To Him who alone does great wonders, for His loving devotion endures forever. 5 To Him who by understanding made the heavens, for His loving devotion endures forever. 6 To Him who spread out the earth upon the waters, for His loving devotion endures forever. 7 To Him who made the great luminaries, for His loving devotion endures forever. 8 The sun to govern the day, for His loving devotion endures forever. 9 The moon and stars to govern the night, for His loving devotion endures forever.

10 To Him who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, for His loving devotion endures forever. 11 And brought Israel out from among them, for His loving devotion endures forever. 12 With a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, for His loving devotion endures forever. 13 To Him who divided the Red Sea, for His loving devotion endures forever. 14 And led Israel through its midst, for His loving devotion endures forever. 15 But swept Pharaoh and his host into the Red Sea, for His loving devotion endures forever.

16 To Him who led His people through the wilderness, for His loving devotion endures forever. 17 To Him who struck down great kings, for His loving devotion endures forever. 18 And slew mighty kings, for His loving devotion endures forever. 19 Sihon king of the Amorites, for His loving devotion endures forever. 20 And Og king of Bashan, for His loving devotion endures forever. 21 And gave their land as an inheritance, for His loving devotion endures forever. 22 A heritage to Israel His servant, for His loving devotion endures forever.

23 To Him who remembered us in our low estate, for His loving devotion endures forever. 24 And delivered us from our enemies, for His loving devotion endures forever. 25 Who gives food to all flesh, for His loving devotion endures forever. 26 Offer thanksgiving to the God of heaven, for His loving devotion endures forever.
