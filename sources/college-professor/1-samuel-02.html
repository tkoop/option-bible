Hannah’s Prophetic Song of Reversal and Divine Sovereignty

1 Hannah prayed and said, “My heart exults in the LORD; my horn is exalted in the LORD; my mouth opens wide in triumph over my adversaries, for I rejoice in Your salvation. 2 No one is holy like the LORD, for there is none besides You, and no Rock is like our God. 3 Do not persist in speaking with such lofty arrogance; let no insolence issue from your mouth, for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him deeds are weighed. 4 The bows of the mighty are shattered, while those who stumble gird themselves with strength. 5 The sated hire themselves out for bread, while the hungry cease from their hunger; the barren woman has borne seven, but she who abounded in children has been left desolate. 6 The LORD puts to death and gives life; He brings down to Sheol and raises up. 7 The LORD impoverishes and enriches; He humbles and exalts. 8 He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, to seat them with princes and to endow them with a throne of glory; for the pillars of the earth belong to the LORD, and upon them He has set the world. 9 He guards the feet of His faithful ones, but the wicked are silenced in darkness, for not by might does a man prevail. 10 The LORD judges the ends of the earth; He gives strength to His king and exalts the horn of His anointed.”

11 Then Elkanah departed to his house at Ramah, while the boy continued to minister before the LORD in the presence of Eli the priest.

The Corruption of Eli’s Priesthood and Samuel’s Early Ministry

12 Now the sons of Eli were scoundrels; they had no knowledge of the LORD. 13 And this was the custom of the priests with the people: whenever any man offered a sacrifice, the priest’s servant would come while the meat was boiling, holding a three-pronged fork in his hand. 14 He would thrust it into the basin, pot, cauldron, or kettle, and whatever the fork lifted the priest would take for himself. Thus they treated all Israel who came to Shiloh. 17 So the sin of the young men was exceedingly great in the sight of the LORD, because they treated the offering of the LORD with contempt.

15 Moreover, before the fat was burned, the priest’s servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, “Give meat for the priest to roast; for he will not accept boiled meat from you, but raw.”

16 If the man said, “Let them burn the fat first, then take whatever you desire,”

the servant would reply, “No; you must give it now, or I will seize it by force.”

18 But Samuel, though still a boy, ministered before the LORD, wearing a linen ephod. 19 Each year his mother made him a small robe and brought it up when she went up with her husband to offer the annual sacrifice. 20 Then Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, “May the LORD give you offspring from this woman in place of the one lent to the LORD.” And they would return to their home. 21 The LORD indeed visited Hannah; she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. Meanwhile the boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the LORD.

22 Now Eli was exceedingly old; he heard of all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and that they were lying with the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

23 He said to them, “Why do you do such things? For I hear from all this people of your evil deeds. 24 No, my sons; the report I hear is not good; you cause the LORD’s people to transgress. 25 If a man sins against a man, God can arbitrate; but if a man sins against the LORD, who shall intercede for him?” But they would not listen to their father’s voice, for the LORD was pleased to put them to death.

26 Meanwhile the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favor with both the LORD and with people.

The Prophetic Oracle Against the House of Eli

27 A man of God came to Eli and said, “Thus says the LORD: ‘Did I not reveal Myself to your father’s house when they were in Egypt under Pharaoh? 28 Did I not choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to ascend to My altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod before Me? Did I not give to your father’s house all the fire offerings of the children of Israel? 29 Why then do you spurn My sacrifice and My offering, which I commanded for My dwelling, and honor your sons above Me by fattening yourselves on the choicest parts of every offering of My people Israel? 30 Therefore the LORD, the God of Israel, declares: I indeed said that your house and your father’s house should walk before Me forever; but now the LORD declares, “Far be it from Me! For those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed.” 31 Behold, the days are coming when I will cut off your arm and the arm of your father’s house, so that no old man will be in your house. 32 You will see distress in My dwelling, in all that I do good to Israel; and there shall never be an old man in your house. 33 Yet I will not cut off every one of your line from My altar; rather, your eyes will fail and your soul will grieve, and all the increase of your house shall die in the flower of their manhood. 34 And this shall be the sign to you: both your sons, Hophni and Phinehas, shall die on the same day. 35 Then I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who shall do according to what is in My heart and My mind; I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before My anointed forever. 36 And it shall come to pass that everyone who is left of your house will come and bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, and will say, ‘Please appoint me to one of the priestly offices, that I may eat a piece of bread.’”
