A Psalm of David: Waiting, Deliverance, and a New Song

1 I waited with unwavering patience for the LORD, and He inclined toward me and heard my cry for help. 2 He drew me up from the pit of tumult, from the miry clay, and He set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure. 3 He placed a new song in my mouth—a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and stand in awe, and they will place their confidence in the LORD.

Trust, Wonders, and the Incomparability of God

4 Blessed is the person who makes the LORD his refuge, who does not turn toward the arrogant or to those who stray into falsehood. 5 Many are the wonders You have wrought, O the LORD my God, and Your purposes toward us are beyond reckoning; none can be compared to You. Were I to proclaim and recount them, they would elude any complete enumeration.

Obedience, Testimony, and Supplication

6 You have not delighted in sacrifice and offering; You have opened my ears to hear. You have not demanded burnt offerings and sin offerings. 7 Then I said, “Here I am; I have come, as it is written concerning me in the scroll of the book. 8 I take delight in doing Your will, O my God; Your law resides in the depths of my being.” 9 I have proclaimed abundant righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I have not restrained my lips—O the LORD, You know it well. 10 I have not hidden Your righteousness within my heart; I have declared Your faithfulness and Your deliverance. I have not withheld Your steadfast love and Your truth from the great assembly. 11 Do not withhold Your mercy from me, O the LORD; may Your steadfast love and faithfulness continually guard me. 12 For innumerable calamities have encircled me; my iniquities have overtaken me, and I can no longer lift up my eyes. They outnumber the hairs of my head, and my heart fails within me.

Deliverance, Enemies, and the Hope of the Needy

13 Be pleased, O the LORD, to deliver me; hasten, O the LORD, to my aid. 14 Let those who seek to destroy my life be altogether ashamed and confounded; let those who delight in my harm be turned back and disgraced.

15 Let them be appalled because of their disgrace—those who say to me, “Aha, aha!”

16 May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation continually declare, “The LORD be magnified!”

17 As for me, I am poor and needy, but the Lord takes thought for me. You are my help and my deliverer; do not delay, O my God.
