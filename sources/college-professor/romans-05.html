Peace, Hope, and the Love of God

1 Consequently, having been declared righteous through faith, we possess peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ, 2 through whom we have also gained entry, by faith, into this grace in which we now stand, and we exult in the hope of God's glory. 3 Moreover, we even boast in our afflictions, recognizing that affliction begets endurance; 4 endurance, in turn, produces proven character, and proven character produces hope. 5 Nor does such hope disappoint, for God's love has been lavished upon our hearts through the Holy Spirit who has been bestowed upon us.

Reconciliation Through the Death of Christ

6 Indeed, while we were yet powerless, Christ died for the ungodly at the appointed time. 7 For scarcely will anyone lay down his life for a righteous man, though perchance someone might summon the courage to die for a truly good man. 8 Yet God demonstrates His own love toward us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died on our behalf. 9 How much more, then, having now been justified by His blood, shall we be delivered from wrath through Him. 10 For if, while we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, how much more, having been reconciled, shall we be saved by His life. 11 Moreover, we even exult in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now obtained reconciliation.

From Adam's Fall to Christ's Gift

12 Hence, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, so death spread to all humanity, inasmuch as all sinned. 13 For sin was indeed present in the world before the law; yet sin is not reckoned where no law exists. 14 Nevertheless, death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those who had not sinned after the pattern of Adam's transgression—Adam being a figure of the One who was to come.

15 Yet the free gift is not commensurate with the trespass. For if, by the trespass of the one, the many died, how much more did God's grace—and the gift bestowed in grace through the one man, Jesus Christ—abound to the many. 16 Nor is the gift like the consequence of the single sinner's act: one trespass brought judgment unto condemnation, whereas the gift, arising amid many trespasses, brings justification. 17 For if, through one man's trespass, death reigned by means of that one, how much more will those who receive the superabundance of grace and the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man, Jesus Christ.

18 Consequently, just as one trespass brought condemnation to all humanity, so one righteous act brings justification unto life to all humanity. 19 For as, through the disobedience of the one man, the many were constituted sinners, so, through the obedience of the One, the many will be constituted righteous. 20 Furthermore, the law was introduced so that the trespass might multiply; yet where sin multiplied, grace superabounded. 21 Thus, just as sin reigned in death, grace too might reign through righteousness unto eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
