The Divine Command Concerning the Encampment

1 The LORD addressed Moses and Aaron, saying, 2 “The Israelites shall encamp around the Tent of Meeting, setting themselves over against it at a distance; every man shall take his place by his standard, beneath the ensigns of his ancestral house; and they shall encamp facing it on all sides.”

The Eastern Host Under Judah

3 Eastward, in the direction of the sunrise, the standard of the camp of Judah shall be pitched according to their hosts; and the leader of the sons of Judah shall be Nahshon son of Amminadab. 4 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number seventy-four thousand six hundred. 5 Adjacent to him shall encamp the tribe of Issachar, whose leader shall be Nethanel son of Zuar. 6 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number fifty-four thousand four hundred. 7 Likewise the tribe of Zebulun shall encamp, whose leader shall be Eliab son of Helon. 8 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number fifty-seven thousand four hundred. 9 The total of those mustered in the camp of Judah shall be one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred, according to their hosts; and they shall set out first.

The Southern Host Under Reuben

10 Southward, the standard of the camp of Reuben shall be pitched according to their hosts; and the leader of the sons of Reuben shall be Elizur son of Shedeur. 11 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number forty-six thousand five hundred. 12 Adjacent to him shall encamp the tribe of Simeon, whose leader shall be Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. 13 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number fifty-nine thousand three hundred. 14 And the tribe of Gad shall encamp, whose leader shall be Eliasaph son of Reuel. 15 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty. 16 The total of those mustered in the camp of Reuben shall be one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty, according to their hosts; and they shall set out second.

The Levitical Center and the Remaining Hosts

17 Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps; as they encamp, so shall they set out, each man in his place, by their standards. 18 Westward, the standard of the camp of Ephraim shall be pitched according to their hosts; and the leader of the sons of Ephraim shall be Elishama son of Ammihud. 19 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number forty thousand five hundred. 20 Adjacent to him shall encamp the tribe of Manasseh, whose leader shall be Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. 21 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number thirty-two thousand two hundred. 22 And the tribe of Benjamin shall encamp, whose leader shall be Abidan son of Gideoni. 23 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number thirty-five thousand four hundred. 24 The total of those mustered in the camp of Ephraim shall be one hundred eight thousand one hundred, according to their hosts; and they shall set out third. 25 Northward, the standard of the camp of Dan shall be pitched according to their hosts; and the leader of the sons of Dan shall be Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. 26 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number sixty-two thousand seven hundred. 27 Adjacent to him shall encamp the tribe of Asher, whose leader shall be Pagiel son of Ocran. 28 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number forty-one thousand five hundred. 29 And the tribe of Naphtali shall encamp, whose leader shall be Ahira son of Enan. 30 His host, those of them who were mustered, shall number fifty-three thousand four hundred. 31 The total of those mustered in the camp of Dan shall be one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred, according to their hosts; and they shall set out last, by their standards. 32 These are the numbered of the sons of Israel by their fathers' houses; all the numbered of the camps by their hosts were six hundred three thousand five hundred and fifty. 33 But the Levites were not numbered among the sons of Israel, as the LORD commanded Moses. 34 Thus the sons of Israel did according to all that the LORD commanded Moses: they encamped by their standards, and so they set out, each man by his family, according to his father's house.
