The Vexation of the Righteous Amid the Flourishing of the Wicked, and the Consolation of Divine Justice

1 A psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. 2 But as for me, my feet had almost slipped; my steps had nearly given way. 3 For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

4 They have no pangs in their death, and their bodies remain well nourished. 5 They are free from the burdens common to mortals, nor are they afflicted as others are. 6 Therefore pride is their necklace, and violence envelops them like a garment. 7 Their eyes protrude from sheer abundance, while the conceits of their hearts overflow unchecked. 8 They scoff and utter evil; from their lofty station they threaten oppression. 9 They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongues strut across the earth. 10 Therefore their people turn to them and drink in their overflowing words.

11 They say, “How can God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?”

12 Behold, these are the wicked—always at ease, they amass wealth.

13 Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure and washed my hands in innocence. 14 All day long I am afflicted, and every morning brings new chastening. 15 If I had said, “I will speak this way,” I would have betrayed the generation of Your children. 16 When I sought to comprehend this, it proved wearisome to my sight.

17 Until I entered the sanctuary of God, then I discerned their end. 18 Surely You set them on slippery ground; You cast them down to ruin. 19 How suddenly they are laid waste, utterly consumed by terrors! 20 Like a dream when one awakes, O Lord, when You arise, You despise their form.

21 When my heart was grieved and my inmost being was pierced, 22 I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before You.

23 Yet I am always with You; You have held my right hand. 24 You guide me with Your counsel, and afterward You will take me into glory. 25 Whom have I in heaven but You? And on earth I desire no one besides You. 26 My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.

27 Those far from You will perish; You destroy all who are unfaithful to You. 28 But as for me, it is good to draw near to God. I have made the Lord the LORD my refuge, that I may declare all Your works.
