A Psalm of David: The Elevation of the Soul unto the the LORD

1Unto You, O the LORD, do I lift up my very soul. 2In You, O my God, have I reposed my trust; let me not be brought to shame, neither let my adversaries exult triumphantly over me. 3Yea, none who wait upon You shall suffer disgrace; but those who practice perfidy without provocation shall be put to shame.

4Make known to me Your ways, O the LORD, and instruct me in Your paths. 5Lead me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; the whole day long I wait upon You. 6Remember, O the LORD, Your tender mercies and Your steadfast love, for they are from everlasting. 7Remember not the transgressions of my youth, nor my rebellious deeds; according to Your steadfast love remember me, for the sake of Your goodness, O the LORD.

8The The LORD is good and upright; therefore He instructs sinners in the way. 9He guides the meek in judgment and teaches the meek His way. 10All the paths of the the LORD are steadfast love and faithfulness to those who keep His covenant and His testimonies. 11For Your name’s sake, O the LORD, pardon my iniquity, for it is great.

12Who, then, is the man that fears the the LORD? Him will He instruct in the way he should choose. 13His soul shall dwell in prosperity, and his offspring shall inherit the land. 14The The LORD takes into His confidence those who fear Him, and makes known to them His covenant. 15My eyes are ever toward the the LORD, for He shall pluck my feet out of the net.

16Turn to me and be gracious to me, for I am solitary and afflicted. 17The distresses of my heart are enlarged; bring me forth out of my straits. 18Consider my affliction and my toil, and forgive all my sins. 19Behold how numerous are my enemies, and with cruel hatred they detest me. 20Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I have taken refuge in You. 21Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for You. 22Redeem Israel, O God, from all its troubles.
