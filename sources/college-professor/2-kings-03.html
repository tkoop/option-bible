Jehoram’s Reign and Moab’s Revolt

1 In the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat’s reign over Judah, Jehoram son of Ahab became king of Israel, and he reigned in Samaria twelve years. 2 He perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD, though not to the degree of his father and mother, for he removed the sacred pillar of Baal that his father had erected. 3 Nevertheless, he adhered to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which that king had led Israel into sin, and he did not depart from them.

4 Now Mesha, king of Moab, was a sheep breeder, and he rendered to the king of Israel a tribute of one hundred thousand lambs and one hundred thousand rams, together with their wool. 5 But upon Ahab’s death, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

The Three Kings’ Campaign and the Prophet Elisha

6 At that juncture King Jehoram marched out of Samaria and mustered all Israel. 7 He sent to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, saying, “Moab has rebelled against me. Will you accompany me in war against Moab?”

Jehoshaphat replied, “I am as you are; my people are as your people; my horses are as your horses.”

8 Jehoshaphat asked, “By which route shall we ascend?”

Jehoram answered, “Through the wilderness of Edom.”

9 So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom set out; they circled about for seven days, and no water was found for the army or for the beasts that accompanied them.

10 Then the king of Israel exclaimed, “Alas! For the LORD has summoned these three kings only to deliver them into the hand of Moab.”

11 But Jehoshaphat said, “Is there no prophet of the LORD here, through whom we may inquire of the LORD?”

One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here—the one who poured water upon Elijah’s hands.”

12 Jehoshaphat said, “The word of the LORD is with him.” So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom descended to him.

Elisha’s Rebuke and Prophetic Oracle

13 Elisha said to the king of Israel, “What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother.”

The king of Israel said, “No, for the LORD has called these three kings to deliver them into the hand of Moab.”

14 Elisha said, “As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, were it not that I regard the presence of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, I would not look upon you or acknowledge you.”

15 “But now bring me a musician.” And when the musician played, the hand of the LORD came upon him.

16 And he said, “Thus says the LORD: Make this valley full of trenches. 17 For thus says the LORD: You shall not see wind, nor shall you see rain; yet this valley shall be filled with water, so that you may drink—you, your livestock, and your beasts. 18 This is a trifling matter in the sight of the LORD; He will also deliver Moab into your hand. 19 And you shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, and you shall fell every good tree, and stop up all springs of water, and mar every good piece of land with stones.”

The Miracle of Water and the Defeat of Moab

20 In the morning, when the grain offering was being offered, behold, water came by way of Edom, and the land was filled with water. 21 When all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, they summoned all who could bear arms, from the least to the greatest, and took their stand on the border.

22 They rose early in the morning, and the sun shone upon the water; and Moab, looking from the opposite side, saw the water red as blood.

23 They said, “This is blood! The kings have surely slain one another. Now, Moab, to the spoil!”

24 When they came to the camp of Israel, Israel rose up and struck Moab, so that they fled before them; and they pursued them into the land, striking Moab as they went. 25 They demolished the cities, and every man cast a stone upon every good field until it was filled; they stopped up all the springs of water and felled all the good trees, until only Kir-hareseth remained, with its stones; and the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

26 When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, he took seven hundred swordsmen to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not. 27 Then he took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him as a burnt offering upon the wall; and great wrath came upon Israel, so they withdrew from him and returned to their own land.
