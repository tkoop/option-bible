David Designates the Site and Gathers Materials for the Temple

1 Then David declared, “This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel.” 2 David gave orders to assemble the resident aliens in Israel, and from among them he appointed stonecutters to quarry dressed stone for building the house of God. 3 He provided iron in abundance for nails for the doors of the gates and for the fittings, and bronze beyond measure. 4 He also procured cedar timber without stint, for the Sidonians and the Tyrians conveyed an immense quantity of cedar to David.

5 David said, “My son Solomon is youthful and unseasoned, and the house to be built for the the LORD must be exceedingly magnificent—renowned and resplendent among all the nations. I shall now make provision for it.” So David made copious preparations before his death.

David Charges Solomon with the Temple's Construction

6 Then he summoned his son Solomon and charged him to build a house for the the LORD, the God of Israel. 7 David said to Solomon, “My son, it was my heartfelt resolve to build a house for the Name of the the LORD my God. 8 But the word of the the LORD came to me, saying,

“‘You have effused abundant blood and waged mighty wars; you shall not erect a house for My Name, because you have poured out much blood upon the earth in My sight. 9 Behold, a son shall be born to you; he shall be a man of repose, and I will grant him respite from all his surrounding foes. His name shall be Solomon, and I will bestow upon Israel tranquility and security throughout his days. 10 He it is who shall build a house for My Name. He shall be My son, and I shall be his Father; I will establish the throne of his kingship over Israel in perpetuity.’

11 “Now then, my son, may the the LORD be with you, that you may thrive and erect the house of the the LORD your God, according to His word concerning you. 12 May the the LORD endow you with discernment and understanding, and commission you over Israel, that you may observe the law of the the LORD your God. 13 Then you shall prosper, provided you are meticulous in observing the statutes and ordinances that the the LORD enjoined upon Moses for Israel. Be resolute and valiant; do not succumb to fear or dismay. 14 Now behold, in my affliction I have prepared for the house of the the LORD one hundred thousand talents of gold, one million talents of silver, and bronze and iron beyond measure. I have likewise furnished timber and stone, and you may augment them. 15 Moreover, abundant workmen are with you—stonecutters, masons, carpenters, and artisans skilled in every material— 16 gold, silver, bronze, and iron beyond enumeration. Arise, then, and execute the work, and may the the LORD be with you.”

David Exhorts Israel's Leaders to Support the Work

17 David moreover enjoined all the leaders of Israel to assist his son Solomon. 18 “Is not the the LORD your God with you? Has He not granted you repose on every side? For He has delivered the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land is subdued before the the LORD and before His people. 19 Now devote your heart and your soul to seeking the the LORD your God. Arise and construct the sanctuary of the the LORD God, that you may convey the ark of the covenant of the the LORD and the sacred vessels of God into the house erected for the Name of the the LORD.”
