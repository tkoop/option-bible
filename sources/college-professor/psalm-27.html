David's Unshakable Confidence in the LORD

1 The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom, then, shall I fear? The LORD is the bulwark of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? 2 When malefactors draw near to devour my flesh—my adversaries and foes—they stumble and fall. 3 Though a host encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war be raised against me, in this I put my trust.

The Singular Desire of the Psalmist's Heart

4 One thing I have asked of the LORD, and that I will seek: that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD and to inquire in His sanctuary. 5 For in the day of trouble He will conceal me in His shelter; in the covert of His tent He will hide me; He will set me high upon a rock. 6 And now my head shall be lifted up above my enemies round about me; and in His tent I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make melody to the LORD.

A Supplication for Divine Favor and Deliverance

7 Hear, O LORD, when I cry aloud; be gracious to me and answer me.

8 You have said, "Seek My face."

My heart says to you, "Your face, O LORD, do I seek."

9 Hide not Your face from me; turn not Your servant away in anger, You who have been my help. Cast me not off, nor forsake me, O God of my salvation. 10 For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the LORD will receive me. 11 Teach me Your way, O LORD, and lead me on a level path because of my enemies. 12 Give me not up to the will of my adversaries; for false witnesses have risen against me, and they breathe out violence.

Hopeful Waiting and Courageous Endurance

13 I should have despaired, had I not believed that I would behold the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living. 14 Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; yes, wait for the LORD.
