The Bridegroom's Exquisite Admiration of the Bride

1 How exquisite are your steps in sandals, O daughter of nobility! The sinuous contours of your thighs resemble precious gems, the masterpiece of an artisan's hands. 2 Your navel is a rounded goblet that never lacks blended wine; your belly is a mound of wheat, encircled by lilies. 3 Your breasts are like twin fawns, the offspring of a gazelle. 4 Your neck resembles an ivory tower; your eyes are pools in Heshbon, beside the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose is like the tower of Lebanon, overlooking Damascus. 5 Your head crowns you like Carmel, and the flowing locks of your head are like royal purple; a king is held captive by your tresses. 6 How beautiful and pleasant you are, O love, amid your delights! 7 Your stature is like a palm tree, and your breasts are like its clusters. 8 I said, "I will climb the palm tree and seize its fruit." May your breasts be like clusters of the vine, and the fragrance of your breath like apples, 9 and your palate be like the finest wine, flowing smoothly to my beloved, gliding over the lips of sleepers.

The Bride's Devoted Response and Rustic Invitation

10 I am my beloved's, and his desire is for me. 11 Come, my beloved, let us go out into the countryside; let us spend the night among the henna blossoms. 12 Let us go early to the vineyards, to see whether the vine has budded, whether the blossom has opened, and whether the pomegranates are in bloom; there I will give you my love. 13 The mandrakes give forth their fragrance, and at our door are all manner of delicacies, both new and old, which I have treasured up for you, my beloved.
