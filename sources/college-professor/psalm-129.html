A Song of Ascents: Israel's Remembrance of Affliction and the LORD's Righteous Deliverance

1 “They have grievously afflicted me from my youth,” let Israel now declare— 2 “they have grievously afflicted me from my youth, yet they have not prevailed against me. 3 The plowmen furrowed my back and lengthened their furrows.”

4 The LORD is righteous; He has severed the bonds of the wicked.

5 May all who hate Zion be humiliated and repulsed! 6 Let them become like grass upon the rooftops, which perishes before attaining maturity, 7 with which no reaper fills his hand and no binder of sheaves his arms, 8 so that wayfarers never say, “May the blessing of the LORD rest upon you! We bless you in the name of the LORD.”
