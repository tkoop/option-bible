The Resurrection Morning and the Angelic Proclamation

1 At the close of the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to inspect the tomb. 2 And suddenly there arose a violent earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, approached, rolled away the stone, and sat upon it. 3 His appearance was like lightning, and his raiment was white as snow. 4 The guards, seized with terror at the sight of him, trembled and became as dead men.

5 But the angel, answering, said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are seeking Jesus, who was crucified. 6 He is not here, for He has risen, just as He said. Come, see the place where He lay. 7 Then go quickly and tell His disciples that He has risen from the dead and is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see Him. Behold, I have told you.”

8 So they departed swiftly from the tomb, trembling with fear yet exulting with great joy, and ran to report the matter to His disciples.

The Risen Lord Appears to the Women

9 And behold, Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” And they approached, seized His feet, and worshiped Him. 10 Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid. Go and tell My brothers to proceed to Galilee, and there they will see Me.”

The Guard's Fabricated Report and the Bribe

11 While they were on their way, behold, some of the guard entered the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had occurred. 12 And when they had assembled with the elders and taken counsel, they gave the soldiers a substantial sum of silver, 13 saying, “Say, ‘His disciples came by night and stole Him away while we were asleep.’ 14 And if this comes to the governor's attention, we will appease him and render you free from anxiety.” 15 So they took the money and acted as they had been instructed. And this account has been widely disseminated among the Jews to this very day.

The Great Commission on the Mountain

16 Then the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain that Jesus had appointed for them. 17 And when they saw Him, they worshiped Him, though some harbored doubts. 18 And Jesus approached them and spoke, saying, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me. 19 Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, 20 teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, even to the consummation of the age.”
