The Angel of the LORD at Bochim

1 The Angel of the LORD ascended from Gilgal to Bochim and declared, “I brought you up out of Egypt and brought you into the land that I swore to your fathers, and I said, ‘I will never break My covenant with you.’ 2 You shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you shall tear down their altars. But you have not obeyed My voice. What is this that you have done? 3 So I also said, ‘I will not drive them out before you; instead, they shall be a continual source of distress to you, and their gods shall become a snare for you.’”

4 When the Angel of the LORD spoke these words to all the Israelites, the people raised a great cry and wept. 5 Accordingly, they named that place Bochim, and there they offered sacrifice to the LORD.

The Death of Joshua and the Passing of a Generation

6 Upon Joshua's dismissal of the people, the Israelites departed, each to his own inheritance, to take possession of the land. 7 The people served the LORD throughout the days of Joshua and throughout the days of the elders who survived Joshua, those who had witnessed all the great works that the LORD had wrought for Israel. 8 Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at the age of one hundred and ten years. 9 They interred him within the bounds of his inheritance at Timnath-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash. 10 That entire generation was gathered to its fathers, and a subsequent generation arose that knew neither the LORD nor the work that He had accomplished for Israel.

Israel's Apostasy and Subjugation

11 The Israelites perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD and served the Baals. 12 They abandoned the LORD, the God of their fathers, who had led them out of the land of Egypt, and pursued other gods from among the gods of the neighboring peoples, prostrating themselves before them; thus they provoked the LORD. 13 They abandoned the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtoreths. 14 Therefore the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, and He delivered them into the hands of plunderers, who despoiled them; He sold them into the hands of their enemies on every side, so that they could no longer withstand their foes. 15 Wherever they marched forth, the hand of the LORD was against them for calamity, as the LORD had declared and as He had sworn to them; and they were sorely distressed.

The Institution of Judges and the Testing of Israel

16 Then the LORD raised up judges, who delivered them from the hand of those who plundered them. 17 Yet they did not heed their judges, for they were unfaithful, pursuing other gods and bowing down to them; they swiftly deviated from the path their fathers had trodden in obeying the commandments of the LORD; they did not act accordingly. 18 Whenever the LORD raised up a judge for them, the LORD was with that judge and delivered them from the hand of their enemies throughout the judge's days; for the LORD was moved to pity by their groaning on account of those who oppressed and crushed them. 19 But upon the judge's death, they reverted and behaved even more corruptly than their fathers, going after other gods to serve them and bow down to them; they did not relinquish any of their practices or their obstinate course. 20 So the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, and He said, “Because this nation has transgressed My covenant that I commanded their fathers and has not obeyed My voice, 21 I will no longer expel before them any of the nations that Joshua left when he died, 22 in order to test Israel, whether they will preserve the way of the LORD by walking in it, as their fathers preserved it.” 23 Thus the LORD left those nations, not expelling them at once, and He did not deliver them into Joshua's hand.
