A Supplicant's Plea for Deliverance and a Doxology of Confidence

1 Unto You, the LORD, I cry aloud; be not deaf unto me, O my Rock, lest, should You remain silent toward me, I be reckoned among those who descend into the pit. 2 Hear the voice of my supplications when I cry out unto You, when I elevate my hands toward Your holy sanctuary. 3 Do not draw me away with the wicked, with the workers of iniquity, who speak peace to their neighbors while malice resides in their hearts.

4 Deal with them according to their deeds and according to the wickedness of their endeavors; repay them according to the work of their hands; render unto them their due. 5 For they do not regard the works of the LORD, nor the operation of His hands; He shall tear them down and shall not rebuild them. 6 Blessed be the LORD, for He has heard the voice of my supplications.

7 The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart has trusted in Him, and I was helped; therefore my heart exults, and with my song I will praise Him. 8 The LORD is strength unto them, and He is the saving stronghold of His anointed. 9 Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and bear them up forever.
