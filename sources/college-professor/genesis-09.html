The Renewed Mandate and Dominion of Humanity

1 God blessed Noah and his sons, and He addressed them thus: “Procreate and proliferate, and replenish the earth. 2 A reverential terror and dread of you shall fall upon every terrestrial beast, upon every avian creature of the heavens, upon every creeping thing of the ground, and upon all the fish of the sea; they are consigned to your dominion. 3 Every living creature that moves shall constitute sustenance for you; even as I conferred upon you the verdure of vegetation, so now I bestow upon you all things. 4 Nevertheless, you shall not consume flesh together with its life—that is, its blood. 5 Moreover, for your lifeblood I shall demand an accounting: from every beast I shall require it, and from humanity; from each man's fellow I shall exact retribution for the life of a man. 6 Whoever sheds the blood of a human being, by a human being shall his blood be shed, for God fashioned humanity in His own image. 7 And you, procreate and multiply; teem abundantly upon the earth and proliferate therein.”

The Everlasting Covenant and Its Rainbow Sign

8 Then God spoke to Noah and to his sons who were with him, saying, 9 “Behold, I am establishing My covenant with you and with your descendants after you, 10 and with every living creature that is with you—the birds, the cattle, and every beast of the earth with you, as many as emerged from the ark; it is ordained for every beast of the earth. 11 I establish My covenant with you: never again shall all flesh be extirpated by the waters of the deluge, nor shall there ever again be a flood to devastate the earth.”

12 And God said, “This is the token of the covenant that I am ratifying between Me and you and every living creature that is with you, for all successive generations: 13 I have set My bow in the cloud, and it shall serve as a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth. 14 When I bring clouds over the earth and the bow becomes visible in the clouds, 15 I will remember My covenant, which is between Me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall never again swell into a flood to annihilate all flesh. 16 When the bow appears in the clouds, I shall behold it and remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is upon the earth.”

17 God said to Noah, “This is the token of the covenant that I have established between Me and all flesh that is upon the earth.”

Noah's Descendants and the Oracle of Canaan

18 The sons of Noah who disembarked from the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; Ham was the progenitor of Canaan. 19 These three were Noah's sons, and from them the entirety of the earth was peopled. 20 Noah, now a cultivator of the soil, planted a vineyard. 21 He imbibed of the wine, became inebriated, and lay exposed within his tent. 22 Ham, the father of Canaan, beheld his father's nakedness and reported it to his two brothers outside. 23 Then Shem and Japheth took a garment, draped it across their shoulders, walked backward, and covered their father's nakedness; with their faces averted, they did not behold their father's nakedness. 24 When Noah awakened from his wine and discerned what his youngest son had perpetrated against him,

25 he declared, “Cursed be Canaan; the lowliest of servants shall he become to his brothers. 26 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem; and let Canaan be his servant. 27 May God enlarge Japheth, and may he dwell in the tents of Shem, and let Canaan be his servant.”

28 After the deluge, Noah lived 350 years. 29 All the days of Noah amounted to 950 years, and then he died.
