The Glory of the LORD Returns to the Temple

1 He then conducted me to the gate—the gate that faces east. 2 And behold, the glory of the God of Israel was advancing from the east, His voice resonant as the sound of many waters, and the earth was irradiated by His glory. 3 The vision I beheld resembled the vision I had seen when the LORD came to destroy the city, and it recalled the visions I had seen by the Kebar River; whereupon I prostrated myself upon my face. 4 The glory of the LORD entered the temple by way of the gate facing east. 5 The Spirit bore me aloft and brought me into the inner court, and behold, the glory of the LORD pervaded the house. 6 A man stood beside me, and I heard a voice addressing me from within the temple.

The LORD's Rebuke and Eternal Promise

7 He said to me, "Son of man, this is the locus of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet, where I shall dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel forever. The house of Israel—and their kings—shall no longer defile My holy name through their spiritual prostitution or through the corpses of their kings upon their high places. 8 When they placed their threshold beside My threshold and their doorpost beside My doorpost, with only a wall between Me and them, they defiled My holy name by the abominations they committed; therefore I consumed them in My wrath. 9 Now let them remove far from Me their spiritual prostitution and the corpses of their kings, and I will dwell among them forever."

10 "As for you, son of man, describe the temple to the house of Israel, that they may be ashamed of their iniquities, and let them measure the plan. 11 If they are ashamed of all that they have done, make known to them the design of the temple—its arrangement, its exits and entrances, all its forms, all its statutes, every one of its designs, and the whole of its law—and write it before their eyes, so that they may preserve its entire design and all its statutes and put them into practice. 12 This is the law of the temple: its entire boundary all around, upon the summit of the mountain, is most holy. Behold, this is the law of the temple."

The Altar's Measurements and Consecration

13 These are the measurements of the altar in cubits—the cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth: its base is one cubit high and one cubit wide, with a rim of one span around its edge; and this is the height of the altar. 14 From the base on the ground to the lower ledge is two cubits, with a width of one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge is four cubits, with a width of one cubit. 15 The altar hearth is four cubits high, with four horns rising from it. 16 The altar hearth is square on all four sides: twelve cubits long by twelve cubits wide. 17 The ledge is square on all four sides: fourteen cubits long by fourteen cubits wide, with a border of half a cubit all around, a base of one cubit all around, and a stairway facing east.

18 He said to me, "Son of man, thus says the Lord the LORD: These are the statutes of the altar on the day it is fashioned, for offering burnt offerings upon it and for sprinkling blood upon it. 19 You shall give a young bull as a sin offering to the Levitical priests of the line of Zadok, who draw near to Me to minister to Me—declares the Lord the LORD. 20 You shall take some of its blood and apply it to the four horns of the altar, to the four corners of the ledge, and to the border all around; thus you shall purify it and make atonement for it. 21 You shall take the sin-offering bull and burn it in the designated place of the temple, outside the sanctuary. 22 On the second day you shall offer an unblemished male goat as a sin offering, and they shall purify the altar just as they purified it with the bull. 23 When you have finished purifying it, you shall offer an unblemished young bull and an unblemished ram from the flock. 24 You shall present them before the LORD; the priests shall cast salt upon them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD. 25 For seven days you shall offer a goat each day as a sin offering, together with an unblemished young bull and an unblemished ram from the flock; they shall offer these. 26 For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar, purify it, and consecrate it. 27 When these days are completed, on the eighth day and thereafter, the priests shall offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings on the altar; and I will accept you, declares the Lord the LORD."
