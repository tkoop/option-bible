The Miraculous Feeding of the Four Thousand

1 In those days, when a vast multitude had once more assembled and were destitute of anything to eat, He summoned His disciples and addressed them, 2 “I am seized with profound compassion for this multitude, because they have already remained in My company three days and have nothing to eat. 3 If I dismiss them to their homes fasting, they will faint upon the journey, and some of them have come from a great distance.”

4 His disciples answered Him, “Whence, in this wilderness, could anyone procure sufficient bread to satiate so great a multitude?”

5 He asked them, “How many loaves do you have?” They replied, “Seven.”

6 He then directed the multitude to recline upon the earth, and, taking the seven loaves, He gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to His disciples to distribute among the people; and they did so. 7 They also had a few small fish, and after blessing them He instructed them to set these before the multitude as well. 8 So they ate and were fully satisfied, and they gathered seven baskets of surplus fragments. 9 There were about four thousand people, and He dismissed them.

10 Immediately He embarked in the boat with His disciples and went to the region of Dalmanutha.

Confrontation with the Pharisees and the Warning against Leaven

11 The Pharisees came out and began to contend with Him, testing Him by demanding a sign from heaven. 12 He groaned deeply in His spirit and said, “Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I say to you, no sign will be given to this generation.” 13 Then, leaving them, He embarked again and crossed to the other side.

14 Now they had forgotten to take bread, and except for one loaf they had none with them in the boat. 15 He cautioned them, saying, “Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.”

16 They began to deliberate among themselves that they had no bread. 17 Knowing this, He said to them, “Why do you deliberate that you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or comprehend? Is your heart obdurate? 18 Having eyes, do you not see? Having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember? 19 When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of fragments did you take up?” They said to Him, “Twelve.” 20 “And when I broke the seven for the four thousand, how many basketfuls of fragments did you take up?” They said to Him, “Seven.” 21 He said to them, “Do you not yet understand?”

The Healing of the Blind Man at Bethsaida

22 They came to Bethsaida, and some people brought a blind man to Him and begged Him to touch him. 23 Taking the blind man by the hand, He led him outside the village, and, after spitting on his eyes and laying His hands on him, He asked him, “Do you see anything?” 24 He looked up and said, “I see people, but they look like trees walking.” 25 Then He laid His hands on his eyes again, and the man gazed intently and was restored, and he saw everything clearly. 26 Jesus sent him to his home, saying, “Do not even enter the village.”

Peter's Confession and the Way of the Cross

27 Jesus went out, along with His disciples, to the villages of Caesarea Philippi, and on the way He asked His disciples, “Whom do men declare Me to be?” 28 They told Him, “John the Baptist; and others say Elijah; and still others, one of the prophets.” 29 He asked them, “But whom do you declare Me to be?” Peter answered Him, “You are the Christ.” 30 He sternly admonished them to tell no one about Him.

31 He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer manifold things, be rejected by the elders, the chief priests, and the scribes, be killed, and after three days rise again. 32 He spoke the saying with candor, and Peter took Him aside and began to remonstrate with Him. 33 But, turning and looking at His disciples, He rebuked Peter and said, “Get behind Me, Satan! For you are not setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of men.”

34 Calling the crowd to Him, along with His disciples, He said to them, “If anyone desires to come after Me, let him renounce himself and take up his cross and follow Me. 35 For whoever wishes to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake and for the gospel's will save it. 36 For what does it profit a man to gain the whole world and forfeit his soul? 37 For what can a man give in exchange for his soul? 38 For whoever is ashamed of Me and of My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels.”
