The Monstrous Beast Ascending from the Sea

1 I beheld a beast ascending from the sea, endowed with ten horns and seven heads; upon its horns rested ten diadems, and upon its heads were inscribed blasphemous names. 2 The beast I witnessed resembled a leopard, its feet evoked those of a bear, and its maw was like that of a lion; the dragon conferred upon it his own power, his throne, and expansive dominion. 3 One of its heads appeared to have sustained a mortal wound, yet the lethal injury was healed, whereupon the entire earth stood in awestruck admiration of the beast.

4 The populace prostrated themselves before the dragon, since he had delegated dominion to the beast, and they likewise prostrated themselves before the beast, exclaiming, “Who can be likened unto this beast, and who possesses the martial capacity to contend with it?”

Blasphemous Dominion and the Saints’ Endurance

5 To this creature was granted a mouth uttering grandiose and blasphemous pronouncements, together with jurisdiction to act for a span of forty-two months. 6 It opened its maw in blasphemous imprecations against God, reviling His name, His tabernacle, and those who sojourn in heaven. 7 Moreover, it was empowered to wage war against the saints and to overcome them, and jurisdiction was conferred upon it over every tribe, people, language, and nation.

8 All who dwell upon the earth will worship it—everyone whose name has not been written in the book of life of the Lamb, the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world.

9 If anyone is endowed with hearing, let him hear. 10 “If anyone is destined for captivity, into captivity he shall be led; if anyone is appointed to perish by the sword, by the sword he must perish.” Herein resides the steadfast endurance and the fidelity of the saints.

The Second Beast, the Image, and the Mark

11 Then I beheld another beast ascending from the earth, possessing two horns resembling those of a lamb, yet speaking with the voice of a dragon. 12 It exercises the entirety of the first beast’s authority in its presence, and it induces the earth and its inhabitants to worship the first beast, whose mortal wound had been healed. 13 It performs prodigious signs, even causing fire to descend from heaven to earth in the presence of humanity.

14 By the signs it was empowered to perform before the beast, it deceives the inhabitants of the earth, instructing them, “Fashion an image for the beast that received the sword-wound and yet lived.” 15 It was granted the capacity to animate the image of the beast, so that the image might speak and orchestrate the execution of all who refused to worship it.

16 It compels everyone—small and great, rich and poor, free and enslaved alike—to receive a mark upon the right hand or upon the forehead, 17 so that no one may engage in commerce, buying or selling, unless bearing the mark, which is either the name of the beast or the numerical cipher of its name. 18 Herein lies wisdom: let the discerning mind compute the beast’s number, for it is a human number, and its sum is six hundred sixty-six.
