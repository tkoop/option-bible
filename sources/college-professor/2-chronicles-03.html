Solomon Commences the Temple on Mount Moriah

1 Solomon commenced the erection of the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, upon Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to David, his father, in the place that David had prepared upon the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 2 He began this work on the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

The Dimensions and Adornment of the Sanctuary

3 These, then, are the foundations that Solomon laid for the construction of the house of God: a length of sixty cubits and a breadth of twenty cubits. 4 The portico that stood before the temple corresponded in length to the breadth of the house, measuring twenty cubits, while its height reached one hundred twenty cubits; and he overlaid its interior with pure gold.

5 The greater house he paneled with cypress timber and overlaid with fine gold, embellishing it with carved palm trees and chains. 6 Moreover, he adorned the temple with precious stones for beauty, and its gold was the gold of Parvaim. 7 He overlaid the beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold, and he carved cherubim upon the walls.

The Most Holy Place and Its Cherubim

8 He fashioned the Most Holy Place, whose length, measured against the breadth of the house, was twenty cubits, and whose breadth was likewise twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with six hundred talents of fine gold. 9 The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold, and he overlaid the upper chambers with gold.

10 Within the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculptured work and overlaid them with gold. 11 The wings of the cherubim extended twenty cubits: the wing of the first measured five cubits and reached to the wall of the house, while the other wing, likewise five cubits, touched the wing of the second cherub. 12 The wing of the second cherub measured five cubits and reached to the wall of the house, while its other wing, five cubits, was joined to the wing of the first. 13 Thus the wings of these cherubim were spread abroad for twenty cubits; they stood upon their feet, and their faces were turned toward the house.

The Veil and the Pillars Before the Temple

14 He made the veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, with cherubim worked into it.

15 Before the temple he made two pillars, thirty-five cubits in height, each crowned with a capital of five cubits. 16 He fashioned chains for the inner sanctuary and placed them upon the tops of the pillars; he made one hundred pomegranates and affixed them to the chains. 17 He erected the pillars before the temple, one on the right and one on the left; the right-hand pillar he named Jachin, and the left-hand pillar he named Boaz.
