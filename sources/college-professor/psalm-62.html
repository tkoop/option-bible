Psalm 62: Quiet Confidence in God Alone

1 My soul waits in silence for God alone; from Him comes my salvation. 2 He alone is my Rock and my salvation, my stronghold; I shall not be greatly shaken. 3 How long will all of you assail a single man, intent on murdering him—like a wall that bulges outward or a fence that totters toward collapse? 4 They conspire only to thrust him down from his exalted station; they take delight in falsehood, blessing with their mouths while cursing in their hearts. Selah.

5 Rest in God alone, O my soul, for my hope is from Him. 6 He alone is my Rock and my salvation, my stronghold; I shall not be shaken. 7 My salvation and my honor rest upon God; my mighty Rock, my refuge, is in God.

8 Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before Him. God is our refuge. Selah.

9 Mere mortals are but a breath; even the highborn are a deception; placed upon the scales, they rise together, lighter than a mere vapor. 10 Do not trust in oppression, nor set vain hope upon plunder; though your riches increase, do not fix your heart upon them.

11 Once God has spoken—twice I have heard it—that power belongs to God, 12 and that steadfast love belongs to You, O Lord; for You repay each person according to his deeds.
