Redeeming Comfort for Jacob and Israel

1 But now thus says the LORD, He who created you, O Jacob, and He who formed you, O Israel: “Do not be afraid, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine. 2 When you pass through the waters, I will be with you, and through the rivers they shall not overwhelm you; when you walk through fire, you shall not be scorched, and the flame shall not burn you. 3 For I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior; I have given Egypt as your ransom, Cush and Seba in your place. 4 Because you are precious in My sight, and honored, and I love you, I will give people in your place and nations in exchange for your life.

5 “Do not be afraid, for I am with you; I will bring your offspring from the east and gather you from the west. 6 I will say to the north, ‘Give them up,’ and to the south, ‘Do not withhold them.’ Bring My sons from afar and My daughters from the ends of the earth— 7 everyone who is called by My name, whom I created for My glory, whom I formed and made.”

The One True God

8 Bring out the people who are blind, yet have eyes, and the deaf, yet have ears. 9 Let all the nations be gathered together, and let the peoples be assembled. Who among them can declare this and show us the former things? Let them bring their witnesses to prove themselves right, and let them hear and say, “It is true.”

10 “You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and My servant whom I have chosen, so that you may know and believe Me and understand that I am He. Before Me no god was formed, nor will there be one after Me. 11 I, yes I, am the LORD, and apart from Me there is no Savior. 12 I have revealed and saved and proclaimed—I, and not some foreign god among you. You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “that I am God. 13 Even from eternity I am He, and none can deliver out of My hand. I act, and who can reverse it?”

Deliverance from Babylon and a New Creation

14 Thus says the LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “For your sake I will send to Babylon and bring them all down as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, in the ships over which they rejoice. 15 I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, your King.” 16 Thus says the LORD, who makes a way in the sea and a path through the mighty waters, 17 who brings out chariot and horse, army and mighty man; they lie down together and do not rise; they are extinguished, quenched like a wick.

18 “Do not remember the former things, nor consider the things of old. 19 Behold, I am doing a new thing; now it springs up. Do you not perceive it? I will make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the wasteland. 20 The beasts of the field will honor Me, the jackals and the ostriches, because I give water in the wilderness and rivers in the wasteland, to give drink to My people, My chosen, 21 the people I formed for Myself, that they may declare My praise.”

Unfaithfulness, Judgment, and Forgiveness

22 “Yet you have not called upon Me, O Jacob; you have grown weary of Me, O Israel. 23 You have not brought Me sheep for your burnt offerings, nor honored Me with your sacrifices. I have not burdened you with grain offerings, nor wearied you with frankincense. 24 You have bought Me no sweet cane with money, nor satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices. Instead, you have burdened Me with your sins; you have wearied Me with your iniquities.

25 I, yes I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake, and I will not remember your sins. 26 Remind Me, let us argue the case together; state your case, that you may be proved right. 27 Your first father sinned, and your mediators transgressed against Me. 28 So I will profane the princes of the sanctuary, and I will devote Jacob to destruction and Israel to reproach.”
