The Divine Summons from Zion

1 A psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God, the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from the east to the west. 2 From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth. 3 Our God comes and does not keep silence; a devouring fire goes before Him, and a fierce tempest encircles Him. 4 He summons the heavens above and the earth, to judge His people.

5 “Gather to Me My faithful ones, who have solemnized My covenant by sacrifice.”

6 The heavens declare His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah.

The LORD’s Admonition to His People

7 “Hear, O My people, and I will speak; O Israel, I will testify against you: I am God, your God. 8 I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices, nor for your burnt offerings, which are ever before Me. 9 I will take no bull from your house, nor goats from your folds. 10 For every beast of the forest is Mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills. 11 I know every bird of the mountains, and the creatures of the field are Mine. 12 If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is Mine, and all its fullness. 13 Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats? 14 Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and pay your vows to the Most High. 15 Call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you shall honor Me.”

The Indictment of the Wicked

16 But to the wicked God says, “What right have you to recite My statutes, or to take My covenant upon your lips? 17 For you hate discipline and cast My words behind you. 18 When you see a thief, you make common cause with him, and your portion is with adulterers. 19 You have unleashed your mouth for evil, and your tongue frames deceit. 20 You sit and speak against your brother; you slander your mother’s son. 21 These things you have done, and I kept silent; you thought that I was altogether like you. But now I rebuke you and set the case before your eyes.”

The Way of Deliverance and Salvation

22 “Consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you to pieces, with no one to deliver. 23 He who offers a sacrifice of thanksgiving honors Me, and to him who orders his way aright I will show the salvation of God.”
