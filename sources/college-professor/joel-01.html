The Oracle Delivered to Joel

1 The word of the LORD that came to Joel, son of Pethuel. 2 Hear this, O elders, and give ear, all you inhabitants of the land: has anything comparable occurred in your days, or in the days of your fathers? 3 Recount it to your children, and let your children recount it to their children, and their children to another generation.

The Locust Devastation

4 What the cutting locust has left, the swarming locust has devoured; what the swarming locust has left, the hopping locust has devoured; and what the hopping locust has left, the destroying locust has devoured. 5 Awake, you drunkards, and weep; wail, all you drinkers of wine, over the new wine, for it is snatched from your mouths. 6 For a nation has ascended against my land—mighty and beyond number—whose teeth are the teeth of a lion and whose fangs are those of a lioness. 7 It has made my vine a desolation and splintered my fig tree; it has stripped it utterly bare and cast it away, so that its branches are whitened. 8 Lament as a virgin girded in sackcloth for the bridegroom of her youth.

The Ruin of Worship and Sustenance

9 The grain offering and the libation are cut off from the house of the LORD; the priests mourn, the ministers of the LORD. 10 The field is ravaged, the soil mourns, because the grain is ruined, the new wine is withered, and the oil languishes. 11 Be confounded, you farmers; wail, you vinedressers, over the wheat and the barley, for the harvest of the field has perished. 12 The vine is withered, the fig tree languishes; the pomegranate, the palm, and the apple—indeed, all the trees of the field are dried up—because gladness is withered from the children of men. 13 Gird yourselves and lament, you priests; wail, you ministers of the altar. Come, spend the night in sackcloth, you ministers of my God, for the grain offering and the libation are withheld from the house of your God. 14 Sanctify a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly; gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land into the house of the LORD your God, and cry out to the LORD.

The Day of the LORD and the Plea for Mercy

15 Alas for the day! For the day of the LORD is near, and as devastation from the Almighty it comes. 16 Is not the food cut off before our eyes—gladness and joy from the house of our God? 17 The seed shrivels beneath their clods; the storehouses are desolate; the granaries are broken down, because the grain is withered. 18 How the beasts groan! The herds of oxen are bewildered, for they have no pasture; indeed, the flocks of sheep are made desolate. 19 To you, the LORD, I cry, for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and flame has scorched all the trees of the field. 20 Even the beasts of the field pant after you, for the watercourses are dried up, and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.
