The Vision of the Basket of Summer Fruit

1 This is what the Lord the LORD showed me: Behold, a basket of summer fruit. 2 He said, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will not spare them any longer." 3 "In that day," declares the Lord the LORD, "the songs of the temple will become wailings; many will be the dead bodies—cast out in every place. Silence!"

The Indictment of Social Injustice

4 Hear this, you who trample the needy and bring the humble of the land to ruin, 5 saying, "When will the new moon be over, that we may sell grain? And the Sabbath, that we may put the wheat up for sale?—making the ephah small and the shekel large, and falsifying the scales with deceit— 6 so that we may buy the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, and sell the sweepings of the wheat?"

The LORD's Sworn Judgment and Cosmic Upheaval

7 the LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob: "Surely I will never forget any of their deeds." 8 Will not the land tremble on account of this, and every inhabitant mourn? Indeed, all of it will rise like the Nile, surge, and subside again, like the river of Egypt.

9 "And on that day," declares the Lord the LORD, "I will make the sun set at noon and darken the land in broad daylight. 10 I will turn your festivals into mourning and all your songs into dirges; I will put sackcloth on every waist and baldness on every head; I will make it like mourning for an only child, and its end like a bitter day."

The Famine of the Word of the LORD

11 "Behold, days are coming," declares the Lord the LORD, "when I will send a famine upon the land—not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD. 12 They will wander from sea to sea and from north to east; they will rove about seeking the word of the LORD, but they will not find it." 13 In that day the beautiful young women and the young men will faint from thirst. 14 Those who swear by the shame of Samaria and say, "As your god lives, O Dan," and, "As the way of Beersheba lives"—they will fall, never to rise again.
