The Feast of Wisdom

1 Wisdom has built her house and hewn out her seven pillars. 2 She has slaughtered her beasts, mixed her wine, and set her table in order. 3 She has dispatched her maidens; from the loftiest places of the city she proclaims her invitation. 4 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to him who lacks understanding. 5 “Come, partake of my bread and drink of the wine I have mingled.” 6 “Forsake your simple ways and live, and walk in the way of understanding.”

The Discipline of Wisdom

7 He who disciplines a scoffer procures disgrace for himself, and he who reproves the wicked incurs a stain. 8 Do not reprove a scoffer, lest he hate you; reprove a wise man, and he will love you. 9 Impart instruction to a wise man, and he will become wiser still; make known to a righteous man, and he will augment his learning.

10 The fear of the LORD constitutes the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. 11 For by me your days will be multiplied, and years of life will be added to you. 12 If you are wise, you are wise for yourself; but if you scoff, you alone will bear it.

The Snare of Folly

13 The woman Folly is clamorous; she is ingenuous and devoid of knowledge. 14 She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the highest places of the city, 15 calling to those who pass by, who go straight on their way: 16 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” And to him who lacks sense she says, 17 “Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.” 18 But he does not know that the dead are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
