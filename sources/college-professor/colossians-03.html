Seeking the Heavenly Realities in Union with Christ

1 Therefore, if you have been raised with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God. 2 Set your minds on the things above, not on terrestrial concerns. 3 For you died, and your life is concealed with Christ in God. 4 When Christ, who is your life, is manifested, then you also will be manifested with Him in glory.

Mortifying Earthly Vices and Renewing the Inner Person

5 Therefore, put to death your earthly members: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed—which is idolatry. 6 On account of these things the wrath of God is coming upon the sons of disobedience. 7 You once walked among them, when you were living in them. 8 But now you must also rid yourselves of all these: anger, wrath, malice, slander, and obscene language from your mouth. 9 Do not deceive one another, having stripped off the old self with its practices 10 and having clothed yourselves with the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its Creator. 11 In this renewal there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave or free, but Christ is all and in all.

Embodying Christlike Virtues and Cultivating Communal Worship

12 Therefore, as God's chosen, holy and beloved, array yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience. 13 Bear with one another and forgive one another; if anyone has a grievance against another, forgive as the Lord forgave you. 14 And above all these, put on love, which is the bond of perfection. 15 And let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts, to which you were indeed called in one body; and be thankful. 16 Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, as you teach and admonish one another in all wisdom, singing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs with grace in your hearts to God. 17 And whatever you do, in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through Him.

Ordering Household and Servile Relations under the Lordship of Christ

18 Wives, be subject to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. 19 Husbands, love your wives and do not grow bitter toward them. 20 Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is well-pleasing in the Lord. 21 Fathers, do not exasperate your children, lest they become discouraged. 22 Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything—not with eye-service, as people-pleasers, but in sincerity of heart, fearing the Lord. 23 Whatever you do, labor with your whole soul, as for the Lord and not for men, 24 knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward; you serve the Lord Christ. 25 For the wrongdoer will receive back what he has done wrong, and there is no partiality.
