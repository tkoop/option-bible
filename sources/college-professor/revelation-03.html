Admonition to the Assembly at Sardis

1 “To the angel of the church in Sardis write: Thus says the One who holds the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your deeds—you bear a reputation for being alive, yet you are dead. 2 Become alert and fortify what remains, which is about to die, for I have not found your works complete in the sight of My God. 3 Remember, then, how you received and heard; keep it, and repent. If you do not stay alert, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come upon you

4 “Yet you have a few people in Sardis who have not soiled their garments; and because they are worthy, they will walk with Me in white. 5 Thus the one who conquers will be clothed in white garments, and I will never blot his name from the Book of Life; indeed, I will confess his name before My Father and before His angels

6 “Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”

Admonition to the Assembly at Philadelphia

7 “To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: Thus says the Holy One, the True One, who has the key of David—He who opens and no one shuts, and shuts and no one opens. 8 I know your deeds. Behold, I have set before you an open door that no one can shut, because you have but little power, yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name. 9 Behold, I will cause those of the synagogue of Satan—who claim to be Jews yet are not, but lie—to come and prostrate themselves before your feet, and to know that I have loved you

10 “Because you have kept My word of patient endurance, I too will keep you from the hour of trial that is coming upon the whole inhabited world, to test those who dwell on the earth. 11 I am coming soon; hold fast to what you have, so that no one may rob you of your crown

12 “The one who conquers I will make a pillar in the temple of My God, never to leave it again; and I will inscribe upon him the name of My God, and the name of the city of My God—the new Jerusalem descending from heaven from My God—and My own new name. 13 Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”

Admonition to the Assembly at Laodicea

14 “To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: Thus says the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the origin of God’s creation. 15 I know your deeds: you are neither cold nor hot. Would that you were either cold or hot! 16 So, because you are lukewarm—neither hot nor cold—I am about to vomit you out of My mouth

17 “For you say, ‘I am wealthy, I have grown rich, and I lack nothing,’ yet you do not know that you are wretched, pitiable, impoverished, blind, and naked. 18 I advise you to purchase from Me gold refined by fire, that you may become truly rich; and white garments, that you may clothe yourself and keep the shame of your nakedness from being exposed; and eye salve to anoint your eyes, that you may see. 19 Those whom I love I reprove and discipline; therefore be earnest and repent

20 “Behold, I stand at the door and knock; if anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me. 21 To the one who conquers I will give the right to sit with Me on My throne, just as I conquered and sat down with My Father on His throne. 22 Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”
