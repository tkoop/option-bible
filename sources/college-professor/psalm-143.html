A Supplication for Deliverance and Righteous Guidance

1 Hear my prayer, O the LORD; incline Your ear to my supplications, and answer me in Your faithfulness and righteousness. 2 Do not bring Your servant into judgment, for no one living can be righteous before You.

3 For the enemy has pursued my soul, crushed my life to the ground, and consigned me to darkness like those long dead. 4 Consequently, my spirit is overwhelmed within me, and my heart is desolate within me.

5 I remember the days of antiquity; I meditate upon all Your works; I muse upon the work of Your hands. 6 I spread out my hands to You; my soul thirsts for You as a parched land. Selah.

7 Answer me quickly, O the LORD; my spirit is spent. Do not hide Your face from me, lest I be reckoned among those who descend into the pit. 8 Cause me to hear Your lovingkindness in the morning, for I trust in You; make known to me the way I should walk, for I lift up my soul to You.

9 Deliver me, O the LORD, from my enemies; I have taken refuge in You. 10 Teach me to do Your will, for You are my God; may Your good Spirit guide me on level ground.

11 Revive me, O the LORD, for Your name's sake; in Your righteousness bring my soul out of trouble. 12 In Your mercy, cut off my enemies and destroy all who afflict my soul, for I am Your servant.
