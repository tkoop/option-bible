The Sudden and Unforeseen Arrival of the Lord’s Day

1 Now, concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no necessity for anything to be inscribed for you, 2 for you yourselves know with meticulous precision that the day of the Lord arrives unexpectedly and furtively. 3 When they are declaring, “Peace and security,” then abrupt ruination overtakes them, just as the acute pangs of childbirth seize a woman heavy with child, and they shall in no wise elude it.

4 But you, brothers, are not in darkness, so that that day should overtake you unawares; 5 for you are all sons of light and sons of day—we are not of night, nor of darkness. 6 Therefore let us not slumber, as the rest do, but let us keep watch and remain sober. 7 For those who slumber slumber by night, and those who are intoxicated are intoxicated by night. 8 But since we belong to the day, let us be sober, having put on the breastplate of faith and love, and, as a helmet, the hope of salvation.

Divine Election unto Salvation and Mutual Edification

9 For God did not appoint us to wrath, but to acquire salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, 10 who died for us, so that whether we are awake or asleep we might live together with Him. 11 Therefore encourage one another and edify one another, just as you are already doing.

Ecclesial Recognition, Care, and Concord

12 Now we ask you, brothers, to acknowledge those who toil among you and exercise oversight over you in the Lord and admonish you, 13 and to regard them with superabundant esteem in love because of their work. Cultivate peace among yourselves. 14 We urge you, brothers, to admonish the unruly, to comfort the fainthearted, to sustain the infirm, and to be long-suffering toward all. 15 See to it that no one recompenses evil for evil, but always pursue what is good, both toward one another and toward all.

Final Exhortations, Benediction, and Closing Charge

16 Rejoice perpetually; 17 pray incessantly; 18 render thanksgiving in all circumstances, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you. 19 Do not stifle the Spirit; 20 do not treat prophecies with contempt; 21 but scrutinize all things, and retain tenaciously that which is good. 22 Abstain from every species of evil. 23 Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept irreproachable at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. 24 The One who calls you is faithful, and He will assuredly bring it to pass. 25 Brothers, intercede for us. 26 Greet all the brothers with a sacred kiss. 27 I adjure you by the Lord that this epistle be recited to all the brothers. 28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ remain with you.
