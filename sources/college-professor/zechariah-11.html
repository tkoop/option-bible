Judgment upon Lebanon and Its Forests

1 Open your gates, O Lebanon, that fire may devour your cedars. 2 Wail, O cypress, for the cedar has fallen, because the majestic trees have been devastated; wail, O oaks of Bashan, for the impenetrable forest has been brought low. 3 Listen to the wailing of the shepherds, for their glory has been ruined; listen to the roaring of the lions, for the pride of the Jordan has been ruined.

The Flock Doomed to Slaughter

4 Thus says the LORD my God: “Shepherd the flock destined for slaughter—the very flock whose buyers slaughter them with impunity, whose sellers proclaim, ‘Blessed be the LORD, for I have grown wealthy,’ and whose shepherds show them no compassion. 5 For I will no longer have compassion on the inhabitants of the land,” declares the LORD. “Behold, I am about to deliver mankind, each into the hand of his neighbor and into the hand of his king; they shall crush the land, and I will not deliver anyone from their power.”

The Shepherd's Ministry and the Broken Covenant

7 So I shepherded the flock destined for slaughter—indeed, the afflicted of the flock—and I took two staffs, calling one Favor and the other Union, and I shepherded the flock. 8 In a single month I removed three shepherds; yet I grew impatient with them, and they likewise came to despise me. 9 Then I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. Let the dying die, let the perishing perish, and let those who remain devour one another's flesh.”

10 Then I took my staff, Favor, and broke it, thereby annulling the covenant I had made with all the peoples. 11 So it was annulled on that very day; and the afflicted of the flock who were watching me recognized that this was the word of the LORD.

12 Then I said to them, “If it seems good to you, give me my wages; but if not, withhold them.” So they weighed out my wages: thirty pieces of silver.

13 Then the LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter—the magnificent price at which they valued Me.” So I took the thirty pieces of silver and cast them into the house of the LORD, to the potter.

14 Then I broke my second staff, Union, annulling the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

The Worthless Shepherd

15 Then the LORD said to me, “Take once more the implements of a foolish shepherd. 16 For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not attend to the perishing, will not seek the young, will not heal the broken, and will not sustain the standing; instead, he will devour the flesh of the fat and tear off their hooves.”

17 Woe to the worthless shepherd who abandons the flock! A sword shall be upon his arm and upon his right eye; his arm shall wither utterly, and his right eye shall be completely darkened.
