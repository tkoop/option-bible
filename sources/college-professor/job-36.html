Elihu's Continued Address

1 Elihu resumed his discourse and declared:

2 “Grant me a brief forbearance, and I shall disclose to you what remains to be said on God's behalf. 3 I shall fetch my understanding from afar and ascribe righteousness to my Maker. 4 For, in truth, my words are not mendacious; one consummate in knowledge stands with you.

Divine Justice and Discipline

5 “Behold, God is mighty, yet He does not despise anyone; He is mighty in the sagacity of His understanding. 6 He does not preserve the wicked in life, but accords justice to the afflicted. 7 He does not avert His eyes from the righteous; He enthrones them with kings and exalts them in perpetuity.

8 “If, moreover, they are bound in fetters and ensnared by cords of affliction, 9 then He discloses to them their deeds and their transgressions, inasmuch as they have acted with overweening pride. 10 He uncovers their ears to instruction and enjoins them to turn back from iniquity. 11 If they heed and serve Him, they shall consummate their days in prosperity and their years in delights. 12 But if they do not heed, they shall perish by the sword and expire without understanding.

13 “The godless in heart treasure up wrath; they do not cry out when He binds them. 14 They perish in their youth, and their life ends among the male shrine prostitutes. 15 Yet He delivers the afflicted through their affliction and uncovers their ears in oppression.

Deliverance and Solemn Warning

16 “Indeed, He drew you from the maw of distress into a spacious place devoid of constraint, with your table laden with rich fare. 17 Yet you are replete with the judgment due the wicked; judgment and justice have seized you. 18 Let not wrath entice you into scoffing, nor let the magnitude of the ransom turn you aside. 19 Will your cry for help avail you, or all your mighty endeavors? 20 Do not yearn for the night, when peoples are cut off in their place. 21 Beware; do not turn toward iniquity, for you have chosen this rather than affliction.

The Majesty of God in Nature

22 “Behold, God is exalted in His power; who is an instructor like Him? 23 Who has prescribed His way for Him, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? 24 Remember to magnify His work, which men have lauded, 25 that all humanity has beheld; man regards it from afar. 26 Behold, God is great, beyond our ken; the number of His years is unsearchable.

27 “For He draws up the drops of water; they distill as rain from His mist, 28 which the clouds pour down and shower abundantly upon mankind. 29 Can anyone comprehend the spreading of the clouds, the thunderings of His pavilion? 30 Behold, He spreads His lightning over it and covers the roots of the sea. 31 For by these He judges the peoples and gives food in abundance. 32 He fills His hands with lightning and commands it to strike its mark. 33 His thunder announces His presence; even the cattle regard the approaching storm.”
