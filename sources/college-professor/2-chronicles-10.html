Rehoboam’s Royal Progress to Shechem

1 Rehoboam traveled to Shechem, since all Israel had gathered there to install him as king. 2 When Jeroboam son of Nebat learned of these events, he returned from Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon.

The People’s Petition for Relief

3 They sent word and summoned him; Jeroboam and all Israel came to Rehoboam and spoke, saying, 4 “Your father imposed a severe yoke upon us; now lighten the harsh servitude and the heavy yoke that your father laid upon us, and we shall serve you.” 5 He said to them, “Return to me after three days.” So the people departed.

Rehoboam’s Fateful Deliberation

6 King Rehoboam consulted the elders who had attended his father Solomon while he lived, asking, “How do you advise me to answer this people?” 7 They replied, “If you will deal kindly with this people and gratify them, speaking gracious words to them, then they will be your servants forever.” 8 Yet he disregarded the elders’ counsel and consulted the young men who had grown up with him and stood in his service.

9 He asked them, “What counsel do you offer, that we may answer this people who said to me, ‘Lighten the yoke your father placed upon us’?” 10 The young men who had grown up with him replied, “Thus you shall say to the people who have spoken to you, ‘Your father made our yoke heavy, but you make it lighter for us’—thus you shall tell them, ‘I am far more formidable than my father.’ 11 And now my father loaded you with a heavy yoke, yet I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with stinging lashes.”

12 On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, as the king had directed, saying, “Return to me on the third day.” 13 The king answered them harshly; King Rehoboam abandoned the elders’ counsel. 14 He spoke to them according to the young men’s counsel: “I will make your yoke heavier; I will add to it. My father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with stinging lashes.”

15 The king did not heed the people, for this turn of events came from God, so that the LORD might fulfill the word He had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

The Revolt of the Northern Tribes

16 When all Israel saw that the king did not listen to them, the people answered the king: “What share have we in David? We have no inheritance in the son of Jesse. To your tents, O Israel! Now look to your own house, David.” So all Israel went to their tents. 17 Yet Rehoboam reigned over the Israelites who dwelt in the cities of Judah. 18 King Rehoboam sent Hadoram, who was in charge of the forced labor; but Israel stoned him to death. King Rehoboam then made haste to mount his chariot and flee to Jerusalem. 19 Thus Israel has remained in rebellion against the house of David to this day.
