The LORD's Tender Love for His Son Israel

“1When Israel was yet in its youth, I loved him, and out of Egypt I summoned My son. 2They were summoned, yet they withdrew from those who summoned them; they sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to carved images. 3Nevertheless, it was I who instructed Ephraim to walk, lifting him upon My arms; yet he failed to recognize that I was his healer. 4I drew them with human cords, with bonds of love; I was to them as one who removes the yoke from the jaw; I stooped toward him and nourished him.

Judgment Upon Israel's Stubborn Rebellion

“5He shall not return to the land of Egypt; rather, Assyria shall be his sovereign, because they refused to turn back. 6The sword shall whirl through his cities, consuming their fortifications and devouring them because of their own counsels. 7My people are resolutely bent upon apostasy from Me; though they cry out to the Most High, not one truly exalts Him.

The Compassion of the Holy One

“8How could I surrender you, Ephraim? How could I give up you, Israel? How could I render you as Admah, or appoint you as Zeboiim? My heart turns within Me, and My compassions are kindled in concert. 9I will not execute the heat of My anger; I will not again bring Ephraim to ruin, for I am God and not a man—the Holy One in your midst—and I will not come upon them in wrath.

Restoration and the LORD's Final Word

“10They shall follow the LORD, who will roar like a lion; when He roars, the children shall come trembling from the west. 11They shall flutter like a bird from Egypt, and like a dove from the land of Assyria; and I will cause them to dwell in their homes, declares the LORD. 12Ephraim has encircled Me with deceit, and the house of Israel with treachery; yet Judah still walks with God and remains faithful among the holy ones.”
