Jesus Forewarns His Disciples of Impending Persecution and the Advocate’s Convicting Ministry

1 I have imparted these teachings to you so that you might not be precipitated into apostasy. 2 They will expel you from the synagogues; indeed, an hour is approaching in which everyone who slays you will imagine that he is rendering sacred service to God. 3 They will perpetrate these actions because they have neither known the Father nor Me. 4 Yet I have apprised you of these things so that, when their hour arrives, you may recall that I forewarned you of them. I did not disclose these things to you from the outset, because I was abiding with you.

5 Now, however, I am departing to Him who sent Me, and not one of you interrogates Me, “Where are you going?” 6 Rather, grief has saturated your hearts because I have spoken these things to you. 7 Nevertheless, I speak the truth to you: it is expedient for you that I withdraw, for if I do not withdraw, the Advocate will not come to you; but if I depart, I will dispatch Him to you.

8 And upon His arrival, He will convict the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment: 9 concerning sin, inasmuch as they do not believe in Me; 10 concerning righteousness, because I am departing to the Father and you behold Me no longer; 11 and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world stands condemned.

12 I retain yet many things to communicate to you, but you are presently incapable of bearing them. 13 Yet when that One, the Spirit of truth, arrives, He will guide you into all truth; for He will not discourse from His own initiative, but will articulate whatsoever He hears, and He will announce to you the things that are impending. 14 He will glorify Me, for He will receive from what is Mine and will declare it to you. 15 All things whatsoever the Father possesses are Mine; therefore I declared that He receives from what is Mine and announces it to you.

The Disciples’ Perplexity and the Promise of Joy Transformed from Sorrow

16 A brief interval, and you no longer behold Me; and again a brief interval, and you shall see Me.

17 Some of His disciples therefore said to one another, “What is this that He says to us, ‘A brief interval, and you do not behold Me, and again a brief interval, and you shall see Me,’ and, ‘because I am going to the Father’?” 18 Thus they were saying, “What does He mean by ‘a brief interval’? We do not comprehend what He is saying.”

19 Jesus perceived that they desired to interrogate Him, and He said to them, “Are you inquiring among yourselves concerning this, because I said, ‘A brief interval and you do not behold Me, and again a brief interval and you shall see Me’?”

20 Truly, truly, I say to you, you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice; you will be grieved, yet your grief will be transmuted into joy. 21 When a woman is in travail, she experiences sorrow because her hour has arrived; but when she has borne the child, she no longer recalls the affliction, on account of the joy that a human being has been brought forth into the world. 22 So also you presently experience sorrow; yet I shall see you again, and your hearts will rejoice, and no one will wrest your joy from you.

Prayer in Jesus’ Name and the Father’s Abiding Love

23 And in that day you will interrogate Me concerning nothing. Truly, truly, I say to you, whatsoever you request of the Father in My name, He will grant it to you. 24 Hitherto you have requested nothing in My name; request, and you shall receive, so that your joy may be fulfilled. 25 I have spoken these things to you in figurative language; an hour is coming when I shall no longer address you in figurative language, but I will announce to you plainly concerning the Father.

26 In that day you will request in My name; and I do not say to you that I will petition the Father on your behalf; 27 for the Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and have believed that I proceeded from God. 28 I proceeded from the Father and have come into the world; again I am leaving the world and going to the Father.

29 His disciples said, “Behold, now You speak plainly and utter no figure of speech!” 30 “Now we know that You know all things and have no need that anyone should interrogate You; by this we believe that You came from God.”

31 Jesus answered them, “Do you now believe?”

32 Behold, an hour is coming, and has already arrived, when you will be dispersed, each to his own dwelling, and you will abandon Me alone. Yet I am not solitary, because the Father is with Me. 33 I have spoken these things to you so that in Me you may possess peace. In the world you have tribulation; but be of good courage—I have conquered the world.
