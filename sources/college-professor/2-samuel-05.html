David’s Anointing as King over All Israel

1 Then all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said, “Behold, we are your own flesh and blood. 2 In times past, when Saul was king over us, you were the one who led Israel out and brought them in; and the LORD said to you, ‘You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be prince over Israel.’ 3 So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David made a covenant with them before the LORD; then they anointed David king over Israel.

4 David was thirty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in all. 5 In Hebron he reigned over Judah seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years over all Israel and Judah.

David’s Capture of Jerusalem

6 The king and his men marched on Jerusalem against the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land, who said to David, “You shall not come in here; rather, the blind and the lame will turn you away,” thinking, “David cannot come in here.” 7 Nevertheless, David captured the stronghold of Zion—that is, the City of David.

8 On that day David said, “Whoever strikes the Jebusites must go up by the water shaft and strike the lame and the blind, who are despised by David’s soul.” Therefore it is said, “The blind and the lame shall not enter the house.”

9 David dwelt in the stronghold and called it the City of David; and David built all around from the Millo inward. 10 David grew steadily greater, and the LORD God of Hosts was with him.

David’s House and Family in Jerusalem

11 Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, together with cedar timber, carpenters, and stonemasons, and they built a house for David. 12 Then David knew that the LORD had established him as king over Israel and had exalted his kingdom for the sake of His people Israel.

13 After David came from Hebron, he took still more concubines and wives in Jerusalem, and more sons and daughters were born to him. 14 These are the names of those born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon; 15 Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia; 16 and Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

David’s Victories over the Philistines

17 When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David; but David heard of it and went down to the stronghold. 18 The Philistines had come and spread themselves in the Valley of Rephaim.

19 David inquired of the LORD, saying, “Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will You give them into my hand?” And the LORD said to David, “Go up, for I will surely give the Philistines into your hand.”

20 So David came to Baal-perazim and struck them there. Then he said, “The LORD has broken through my enemies before me like a breakthrough of waters.” Therefore he named that place Baal-perazim. 21 They abandoned their idols there, and David and his men carried them away.

22 The Philistines came up again and spread themselves in the Valley of Rephaim. 23 David inquired of the LORD, and He said, “You shall not go up; circle around behind them and come upon them in front of the balsam trees. 24 When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then rouse yourself, for then the LORD has gone out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines.” 25 David did as the LORD commanded him, and he struck the Philistines from Geba to Gezer.
