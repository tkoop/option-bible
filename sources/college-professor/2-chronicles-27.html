Jotham's Accession and Righteous Reign

1 Jotham was twenty-five years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; his mother was Jerushah, the daughter of Zadok. 2 He acted uprightly in the sight of the LORD, following in every respect the example of his father Uzziah, though, unlike him, he did not enter the temple of the LORD; nevertheless, the people persisted in their corrupt practices.

Jotham's Building Projects and Victory over Ammon

3 He rebuilt the Upper Gate of the house of the LORD and carried out extensive construction on the wall of Ophel. 4 He also built cities throughout the hill country of Judah, together with fortresses and towers in the wooded regions. 5 He waged war against the king of the Ammonites and subdued them; in that year the Ammonites gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley, and they rendered him the same tribute in the second and third years.

The Record and End of Jotham's Reign

6 Thus Jotham grew powerful, for he ordered his ways before the LORD His God. 7 The remainder of Jotham's acts, including all his military campaigns and his conduct, are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. 8 He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. 9 Jotham died and was buried in the City of David; his son Ahaz succeeded him as king.
