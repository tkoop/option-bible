The Blessed Path of the Righteous and the Perishing of the Wicked

1 How blessed is the man who does not follow the counsel of the wicked, nor take his stand among sinners, nor settle into the company of scoffers; 2 but whose delight resides in the law of the the LORD, and who meditates upon His law day and night. 3 He shall be like a tree transplanted beside streams of water, yielding its fruit in its season, its leaf never withering, and in all that he undertakes he prospers.

4 The wicked are not so; rather, they are like chaff that the wind drives away. 5 Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous; 6 for the the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked shall perish.
