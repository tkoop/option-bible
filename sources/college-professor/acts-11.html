The Apostles' Confrontation with Peter over His Fellowship with the Uncircumcised

1 Now the apostles and the brothers dispersed throughout Judea heard that the Gentiles, too, had received the word of God. 2 When Peter ascended to Jerusalem, those of the circumcision contended with him, 3 saying, "You went in to men possessing uncircumcision and ate with them."

4 But Peter commenced and expounded the matter to them in orderly sequence, saying,

5 "I was in the city of Joppa, engaged in prayer, when, in an ecstasy, I beheld a vision: a certain vessel, resembling a great sheet, descending as though let down from heaven by four corners, and it came even to me. 6 Fixing my gaze upon it, I observed and discerned the four-footed creatures of the earth, the wild beasts, the creeping things, and the birds of the sky. 7 Then I heard a voice saying to me, 'Rise, Peter; slaughter and eat.'"

8 "But I replied, 'By no means, Lord; for nothing common or unclean has ever entered my mouth.' 9 Yet the voice answered from heaven a second time, 'What God has cleansed, you must not deem common.' 10 This occurred thrice, and then all was drawn back up into heaven."

11 "And behold, at that very moment, three men who had been dispatched to me from Caesarea stood at the house where we lodged. 12 The Spirit instructed me to accompany them without discrimination; moreover, these six brothers went with me, and we entered the man's house."

13 "He recounted to us how he had seen an angel standing in his house and saying, 'Send to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter, 14 who will speak words to you by which you and all your household will be saved.'"

15 "And as I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell upon them, even as it had upon us at the beginning. 16 Then I remembered the word of the Lord, how He said, 'John indeed baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.'"

17 "If, then, God bestowed upon them the identical gift that He also bestowed upon us who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I to be able to hinder God?"

18 When they heard these things, they fell silent and glorified God, saying, "Then to the Gentiles also God has granted repentance unto life."

The Gospel's Flourishing among the Hellenists at Antioch

19 Now those who had been scattered by the tribulation that arose over Stephen passed through as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch, speaking the word to no one except Jews alone. 20 But some of them, men of Cyprus and Cyrene, upon coming to Antioch, spoke to the Hellenists, proclaiming the Lord Jesus. 21 And the hand of the Lord was with them, so that a great number believed and turned to the Lord.

22 The report concerning them reached the ears of the church in Jerusalem, and they dispatched Barnabas as far as Antioch. 23 When he arrived and perceived the grace of God, he rejoiced and exhorted them all to remain with the Lord with resolute purpose of heart. 24 For he was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and faith, and a considerable crowd was added to the Lord.

25 He then departed for Tarsus to seek Saul, 26 and having found him, he brought him to Antioch. It came to pass that for a whole year they assembled with the church and instructed a substantial multitude; and it was in Antioch that the disciples were first called Christians.

Prophetic Famine Warning and the Disciples' Benevolent Response

27 In those days prophets descended from Jerusalem to Antioch. 28 One of them, named Agabus, stood up and signified through the Spirit that a great famine was about to come upon the whole inhabited world—which indeed came to pass in the days of Claudius. 29 So the disciples determined, each according to his means, to send relief to the brothers dwelling in Judea. 30 This they also did, sending it to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul.
