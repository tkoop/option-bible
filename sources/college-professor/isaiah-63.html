The Divine Warrior's Return from Edom

1 Who is this coming from Edom, from Bozrah, with garments stained crimson? This One, glorious in apparel, striding in the fullness of His strength?

“It is I, speaking in righteousness, mighty to save.”

2 Why are Your garments red, and Your apparel like that of one treading the winepress?

3 I have trodden the winepress alone, and from the peoples no one was with Me. I trod them in My anger and trampled them in My wrath; their blood bespattered My garments, and I have defiled all My raiment. 4 For the day of vengeance was in My heart, and the year of My redemption had come. 5 I looked, but there was no one to help; I was astonished that none sustained Me. So My own arm brought Me salvation, and My wrath upheld Me. 6 I trampled the nations in My anger; in My wrath I made them drunk and poured out their lifeblood upon the earth.

Israel's Remembrance of the LORD's Former Mercies

7 I shall recount the steadfast love of the LORD, the praises of the LORD, according to all that the LORD has bestowed upon us, and the great goodness to the house of Israel that He has granted according to His compassion and the abundance of His steadfast love.

8 He said, “Surely they are My people, children who will not deal falsely.” So He became their Savior. 9 In all their affliction He was afflicted, and the Angel of His Presence saved them. In His love and in His compassion He redeemed them; He lifted them up and carried them all the days of old. 10 But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit; so He turned and became their enemy, and He Himself fought against them.

11 Then His people remembered the days of old, the days of Moses: Where is He who brought them up out of the sea with the shepherds of His flock? Where is He who put His Holy Spirit in their midst— 12 the One who made His glorious arm go at the right hand of Moses, who divided the waters before them to make for Himself an everlasting name— 13 who led them through the depths like a steed in the desert, so that they did not stumble? 14 Like cattle descending into the vale, the Spirit of the LORD gave them rest. So You led Your people, to make for Yourself a glorious name.

A Penitent Plea for Restoration

15 Look down from the heavens and see, from Your holy and glorious habitation. Where are Your zeal and Your might? Your yearning and Your compassion are withheld from us. 16 For You are our Father, though Abraham does not know us and Israel does not acknowledge us; You, O LORD, are our Father, our Redeemer from antiquity is Your name. 17 Why, O LORD, do You cause us to wander from Your ways and harden our heart from fearing You? Return, for the sake of Your servants, the tribes of Your inheritance. 18 For a brief while Your people possessed Your holy place, but our adversaries have trampled Your sanctuary. 19 We have become like those over whom You have never ruled, like those not called by Your name.
