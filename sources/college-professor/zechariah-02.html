The Vision of the Man with the Measuring Line

1 I raised my eyes and beheld a man with a measuring line in his hand. 2 "Where are you going?" I asked. "To measure Jerusalem," he replied, "to see how wide and how long it is." 3 Then the angel who had been speaking with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him.

The Promise of an Unwalled Jerusalem and Divine Protection

4 The other angel said, "Run, tell that young man, 'Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the multitude of people and livestock in it.'" 5 "And I will be a wall of fire around her," declares the the LORD, "and I will be the glory in her midst."

The Summons to Return from the North

6 "Come! Come! Flee from the land of the north," declares the the LORD, "for I have dispersed you like the four winds of heaven," declares the the LORD. 7 "Up, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with Daughter Babylon."

8 Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: "After His glory, He has sent Me to the nations that plundered you—for whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye— 9 behold, I will wave My hand over them, and they shall become plunder for their servants. Then you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent Me."

The LORD's Glory Among the Nations

10 "Sing and rejoice, O Daughter of Zion, for behold, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst," declares the the LORD, 11 "and on that day many nations will join themselves to the the LORD and become My people, and I will dwell in your midst. Then you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you."

12 The LORD will take possession of Judah as His portion in the holy land, and will again choose Jerusalem. 13 Be silent before the the LORD, all humanity, for He has roused Himself from His holy dwelling.
