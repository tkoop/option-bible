The LORD Commands Vengeance upon Midian

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Avenge the Israelites upon the Midianites; afterward you shall die.”

3 Moses spoke to the people, saying, “Arm men from among you for the war, that they may go against Midian to execute the LORD’s vengeance upon Midian. 4 You shall send a thousand from each tribe of Israel to the war.”

5 So from the thousands of Israel they furnished a thousand from each tribe—twelve thousand men armed for war. 6 Moses sent them to the war, a thousand from each tribe, along with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest; he took with him the holy vessels and the trumpets for the alarm.

The Campaign and Its Spoils

7 They made war against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and killed every male. 8 They also killed the kings of Midian—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian—and Balaam son of Beor they slew with the sword.

9 The Israelites took captive the women of Midian and their little ones, and they plundered all their cattle, all their flocks, and all their goods. 10 They burned with fire all their cities where they dwelt, and all their encampments. 11 They took all the spoil and all the prey, both of man and of beast. 12 They brought the captives, the prey, and the spoil to Moses and to Eleazar the priest, and to the congregation of the Israelites, at the camp on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

Moses’ Anger and the Purification of the Spoil

13 Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp. 14 Moses was angry with the officers of the host—the commanders of thousands and of hundreds who were returning from the war. 15 Moses said to them, “Have you allowed all the women to live? 16 See, it was these who, on Balaam’s counsel, caused the Israelites to trespass against the LORD in the matter of Peor, so that the plague came upon the congregation of the LORD.

17 “Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has known a man by lying with him. 18 But keep alive for yourselves all the young girls who have not known a man by lying with him. 19 And you shall camp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has killed any person and everyone who has touched any slain person shall purify yourselves and your captives on the third day and on the seventh day. 20 You shall also purify every garment, every article of skin, everything made of goats’ hair, and every article of wood.”

21 Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who had gone to the battle, “This is the statute of the law that the LORD has commanded Moses: 22 only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead— 23 everything that can withstand fire, you shall pass through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless it shall be purified with the water of purification; and everything that cannot withstand fire you shall pass through water. 24 You shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you shall be clean; and afterward you may come into the camp.”

The Division of the Plunder and the Officers’ Offering

25 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 26 “Take the sum of the prey that was taken, both of man and of beast—you and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the fathers’ houses of the congregation— 27 and divide the prey into two parts, between the men of war who went out to battle and all the congregation. 28 And levy a tribute for the LORD from the men of war who went out to battle: one soul out of five hundred, both of persons and of cattle, of donkeys and of sheep. 29 From their half you shall take it and give it to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s heave offering. 30 And from the half of the Israelites you shall take one portion of fifty, of persons, of cattle, of donkeys, and of sheep, of all the beasts, and give them to the Levites who keep charge of the tabernacle of the LORD.”

31 Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD had commanded Moses. 32 The prey that remained from the spoil taken by the men of war amounted to 675,000 sheep, 33 72,000 cattle, 34 61,000 donkeys, 35 and 32,000 persons in all—every woman who had not known a man by lying with him.

36 The half, the portion of those who went out to war, numbered 337,500 sheep; 37 the LORD’s tribute from the sheep was 675; 38 the cattle were 36,000, of which the LORD’s tribute was 72; 39 the donkeys were 30,500, of which the LORD’s tribute was 61; 40 and the persons were 16,000, of which the LORD’s tribute was 32 persons. 41 Moses gave the tribute, the LORD’s heave offering, to Eleazar the priest, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

42 From the half belonging to the Israelites, which Moses had divided off from the men who had gone to war— 43 the congregation’s half was 337,500 sheep, 44 36,000 cattle, 45 30,500 donkeys, 46 and 16,000 persons— 47 Moses took one portion of fifty from the Israelites’ half, of persons and of beasts, and gave them to the Levites who kept charge of the tabernacle of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

48 The officers over the thousands of the army, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, came near to Moses 49 and said to him, “Your servants have taken a count of the men of war under our command, and not one of us is missing. 50 So we have brought an offering to the LORD—what each man found: gold articles, anklets, bracelets, rings, earrings, and pendants—to make atonement for ourselves before the LORD.”

51 Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them—all the crafted articles. 52 The gold of the heave offering that the commanders of thousands and of hundreds offered to the LORD was 16,750 shekels. 53 The men of war had each taken plunder for himself. 54 Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD.
