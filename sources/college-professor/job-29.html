Job Recalls His Former Eminence and Divine Favor

1Job again took up his discourse and said, 2“Would that I might be restored to the months of old, to the days when God kept watch over me, 3when His lamp shone above my head and by His light I walked through darkness, 4as in the days of my prime, when the intimate counsel of God rested upon my tent, 5while the Almighty remained in my company and my children encircled me, 6when my steps were bathed in cream and the rock poured out for me rivers of oil.

The Reverence He Commanded in the City

7“When I proceeded to the city gate and established my seat in the square, 8the young men perceived me and withdrew, while the elders rose and stood; 9nobles restrained their speech and placed their hands over their mouths; 10the voices of leaders were subdued, and their tongues adhered to their palates. 11For the ear that heard me pronounced me blessed, and the eye that beheld me bore witness on my behalf.

His Righteousness and Benevolence Toward the Vulnerable

12“Because I rescued the afflicted in his outcry, and the orphan bereft of any helper; 13the benediction of the perishing came upon me, and I caused the widow’s heart to exult. 14I arrayed myself in righteousness, and it invested me; my justice was as a robe and a turban. 15I served as eyes to the blind and feet to the lame. 16I was a father to the indigent, and I investigated the plea of one unknown to me. 17I shattered the jaws of the wicked and wrested the prey from between his teeth.

His Confidence in Enduring Prosperity

18“Then I concluded, ‘I shall expire in my nest, and I shall multiply my days like the sand,’ 19my roots extending to the waters, while the dew lodged upon my branches throughout the night. 20My glory was renewed within me, and my bow was perpetually renewed in my hand. 21Men hearkened unto me and waited expectantly, and they fell silent for my counsel. 22After my words, they spoke no further, and my discourse distilled upon them. 23They awaited me as for rain, and opened their mouths as for the latter rain. 24I smiled upon them, yet they could scarcely credit it, and they did not suffer the radiance of my countenance to be cast down. 25I selected their path and sat at their head; I dwelt as a king amid his troops, like one who consoles mourners.”
