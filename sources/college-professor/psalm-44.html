A Communal Lament Recalling Past Deliverance and Pleading for Renewed Mercy

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah.

1 We have heard with our ears, O God, what our fathers recounted to us—the deeds You performed in their days, in days of old. 2 With Your hand You drove out the nations and planted them; You crushed the peoples and cast them out. 3 For they did not take the land by their sword, nor did their arm secure victory for them; rather, it was Your right hand, Your arm, and the radiance of Your face, because You favored them.

4 You are my King, O God; ordain victories for Jacob. 5 Through You we repel our adversaries; in Your name we trample our foes. 6 For I do not trust in my bow, nor does my sword deliver me. 7 For You save us from our foes and put to shame those who hate us. 8 In God we have boasted all day long, and we will give thanks to Your name forever. Selah

9 Yet You have rejected and humiliated us; You no longer go forth with our armies. 10 You made us retreat before the enemy, and those who hate us have plundered us. 11 You gave us up like sheep to be devoured; You scattered us among the nations. 12 You sold Your people for nothing; You gained no profit from their sale. 13 You made us a reproach to our neighbors, a scorn and derision to those around us. 14 You made us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples.

15 All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face, 16 because of the voice of the taunter and reviler, because of the enemy and avenger.

17 All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten You or dealt falsely with Your covenant. 18 Our hearts have not turned back; our steps have not strayed from Your path. 19 Yet You have crushed us in the haunt of jackals and covered us with deepest darkness.

20 If we had forgotten the name of our God or spread out our hands to a foreign god, 21 would not God have discovered it, since He knows the secrets of the heart? 22 Yet for Your sake we are killed all day long; we are regarded as sheep for the slaughter.

23 Awake, O Lord! Why do You sleep? Arise! Do not reject us forever. 24 Why do You hide Your face and forget our misery and oppression? 25 For our soul has sunk to the dust; our bodies cling to the earth. 26 Rise up; be our help! Redeem us for the sake of Your loving devotion.
