The Siege and Fall of Jerusalem

1 In the ninth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, advanced against Jerusalem with his entire army and besieged it. 2 On the ninth day of the fourth month of Zedekiah’s eleventh year, the city was breached. 3 Then all the officers of the king of Babylon entered and took their seats in the Middle Gate—Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsechim the chief eunuch, Nergal-sharezer the chief magus, and all the remaining officials of the king of Babylon.

4 When Zedekiah, king of Judah, and all the men of war saw them, they fled and departed from the city by night through the king’s garden gate, between the walls, and took the road toward the Arabah. 5 But the Chaldean army pursued them, overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho, seized him, and escorted him to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he pronounced judgment upon him.

6 At Riblah, the king of Babylon executed Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes; he also executed all the nobles of Judah. 7 Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, bound him in bronze fetters, and carried him off to Babylon. 8 The Chaldeans burned the royal palace and the houses of the people with fire, and they tore down the walls of Jerusalem.

The Deportation of the Survivors

9 Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, carried into exile to Babylon the remnant left in the city, the deserters who had gone over to him, and the rest of the populace. 10 But Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, left in the land of Judah some of the poorest people, who had nothing, and on that day he gave them vineyards and fields.

Jeremiah’s Deliverance and Commission

11 Now Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had given orders concerning Jeremiah through Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, saying, 12 “Take him and set your eyes upon him, and do him no harm; rather, deal with him exactly as he asks of you.” 13 So Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, Nebushazban the chief eunuch, Nergal-sharezer the chief magus, and all the chief officers of the king of Babylon sent and took Jeremiah from the court of the guard. 14 They committed him to Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, to escort him to his residence, and so he remained among the people.

A Promise to Ebed-melech the Cushite

15 While Jeremiah had been confined in the court of the guard, the word of the LORD came to him: 16 “Go and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite, ‘Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I am about to bring My words upon this city for disaster and not for prosperity, and they shall be fulfilled before you on that day. 17 But I will deliver you on that day, declares the LORD, and you shall not be given into the hand of the men whom you dread. 18 For I will surely save you, and you shall not fall by the sword; your life will be spared as plunder, because you have trusted in Me, declares the LORD.’”
