God’s Righteous and Impartial Judgment

1 Consequently, you are without defense, O man—every one who judges—for in the very act by which you condemn another, you condemn yourself; for you, the judge, perpetrate the same offenses. 2 We know, moreover, that God’s judgment against those who practice such things is rendered in accordance with truth. 3 Do you suppose, O man—you who judge those who perpetrate such deeds while committing them yourself—that you will elude the judgment of God? 4 Or do you disdain the wealth of His kindness, forbearance, and patience, failing to recognize that God’s kindness is intended to lead you to repentance? 5 Yet, in accordance with your obduracy and unrepentant heart, you are accumulating wrath for yourself against the day of wrath and the revelation of God’s righteous judgment.

6 He will render to each according to his works: 7 to those who, by steadfast endurance in good works, seek glory, honor, and immortality—eternal life; 8 but to those driven by self-seeking ambition, who disobey the truth while yielding to unrighteousness—wrath and fury. 9 There will be affliction and distress upon every human soul that perpetrates evil—to the Jew first, and also to the Greek; 10 but glory, honor, and peace to everyone who practices good—to the Jew first, and likewise to the Greek. 11 For God exhibits no partiality.

Law, Conscience, and the Coming Judgment

12 For all who have sinned apart from the law will likewise perish apart from the law, and all who have sinned under the law will be judged by the law. 13 For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law who will be justified. 14 For whenever Gentiles, who lack the law, perform by nature what the law requires, they—though lacking the law—are a law unto themselves. 15 They demonstrate that the work of the law is inscribed upon their hearts, while their conscience bears witness and their thoughts alternately accuse or exonerate them, 16 on the day when God judges the hidden things of humanity through Jesus Christ, in accordance with my gospel.

The Hypocrisy of Boasting in the Law

17 But if you are called a Jew, repose upon the law, and make your boast in God, 18 and know His will, and discern what is excellent, having been instructed from the law, 19 and are persuaded that you yourself are a guide to the blind, a light for those in darkness, 20 an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, possessing in the law the embodiment of knowledge and truth— 21 you, therefore, who instruct another, do you not instruct yourself? You who proclaim that one must not steal—do you steal? 22 You who say, ‘Do not commit adultery’—do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols—do you rob temples? 23 You who boast in the law—do you dishonor God by transgressing the law?

24 For, as it is written, ‘The name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.’

The Circumcision That Matters

25 For circumcision is advantageous if you observe the law; but if you are a transgressor of the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision. 26 If, then, the uncircumcised observe the ordinances of the law, will not his uncircumcision be reckoned as circumcision? 27 And will not the naturally uncircumcised, who fulfills the law, judge you—you who transgress the law despite possessing the letter and circumcision? 28 For a person is not a Jew who is one merely outwardly, nor is circumcision that which is external in the flesh. 29 Rather, the true Jew is one inwardly, and circumcision is of the heart—by the Spirit, not by the letter—whose commendation comes not from human beings but from God.
