1 The word of the the LORD came to me, saying:

The Prophet’s Celibacy and the Land’s Coming Devastation

2 “You shall not take a wife for yourself, nor shall you have sons or daughters in this place.” 3 For thus says the the LORD concerning the sons and daughters born in this place, and concerning their mothers who bear them and their fathers who beget them in this land: 4 They shall die of deadly diseases. They shall not be lamented or buried; they shall be as dung upon the face of the earth. They shall perish by sword and famine, and their corpses shall become food for the birds of the heavens and the beasts of the earth.

5 For thus says the the LORD: “Do not enter the house of mourning, nor go to lament, nor bemoan them; for I have withdrawn My peace from this people,” declares the the LORD, “even lovingkindness and compassion.” 6 Both great and small shall die in this land. They shall not be buried, nor shall they be lamented; nor shall anyone gash himself or make himself bald for them. 7 Nor shall men break bread for them in mourning, to comfort them concerning the dead, nor give them the cup of consolation to drink on account of father or mother. 8 You shall not enter the house of feasting, to sit with them, to eat and to drink.

The End of Festivity and the People’s Protest

9 For thus says the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: “Behold, I am about to remove from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of mirth, the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride.”

10 And when you tell this people all these words, and they say to you, “Why has the the LORD pronounced all this great evil against us? What is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have committed against the the LORD our God?”

11 Then say to them, “Because your fathers have forsaken Me,” declares the the LORD, “and have walked after other gods and served them and bowed down to them, and have forsaken Me and have not kept My law. 12 And you have done worse than your fathers; for behold, every one of you walks after the stubbornness of his evil heart, so as not to listen to Me. 13 Therefore I will hurl you out of this land into a land that you and your fathers have not known; and there you shall serve other gods day and night, where I will not show you favor.”

Exile, Restoration, and Inescapable Judgment

14 “Therefore behold, days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when it shall no longer be said, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt,’ 15 but, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up the sons of Israel from the land of the north and from all the lands where He had driven them.’ And I will bring them back to their land that I gave to their fathers.”

16 “Behold, I am sending many fishermen,” declares the the LORD, “and they shall fish for them; and afterward I will send many hunters, and they shall hunt them from every mountain, from every hill, and from the clefts of the rocks. 17 For My eyes are upon all their ways; they are not hidden from My face, nor is their iniquity concealed from My eyes. 18 And first I will doubly repay their iniquity and their sin, because they have polluted My land with the carcasses of their detestable things, and have filled My inheritance with their abominations.”

Jeremiah’s Prayer and the Divine Response

19 “O the LORD, my strength and my stronghold, and my refuge in the day of distress! To You the nations shall come from the ends of the earth and say, ‘Our fathers have inherited only lies, vanity, and things in which there is no profit.’ 20 Can a man make gods for himself? Yet they are not gods.”

21 “Therefore behold, I will make them know—this once I will make them know My hand and My might, and they shall know that My name is the the LORD.”
