The Worth of a Good Name and the Divine Ordering of Human Relations

1 A distinguished reputation is preferable to vast wealth; gracious esteem surpasses both silver and gold. 2 The affluent and the indigent encounter one another, yet the LORD is the Maker of them all. 3 The shrewd perceive calamity and conceal themselves, whereas the naive press forward and incur penalty. 4 The recompense of humility and reverence for the LORD comprises riches, honor, and life.

5 Thorns and snares beset the way of the perverse; whoever preserves his soul remains at a distance from them. 6 Instruct a youth according to his proper path, and even in senescence he will not deviate from it. 7 The affluent exercise dominion over the impoverished, and the debtor becomes servant to the creditor. 8 Whoever sows iniquity shall harvest calamity, and the rod of his wrath shall be consumed.

Generosity, Purity, and the Expulsion of Folly

9 The generous shall be blessed, since he dispenses his bread to the needy. 10 Expel the mocker, and quarreling will subside; indeed, litigation and ignominy will abate. 11 Whoever cherishes purity of heart and possesses gracious speech shall find the king as his companion. 12 The LORD's eyes safeguard knowledge, yet He subverts the utterances of the treacherous.

13 The sluggard protests, “A lion lurks without; I shall be murdered in the thoroughfares!”

14 The mouth of a forbidden woman is a bottomless pit; one under the LORD's wrath will plunge therein. 15 Folly is tethered to a child's heart, yet the rod of correction banishes it from him. 16 Whoever extorts the indigent to aggrandize himself, or tenders gifts to the affluent, shall surely be reduced to penury.

The Sage's Pedagogy and Confidence in the LORD

17 Incline your ear and hearken to the sages' words; devote your heart to my understanding. 18 For it is delightful when you treasure them inwardly; let them be ever upon your lips. 19 That your confidence may repose in the LORD, I acquaint you this day—even you. 20 Have I not committed to writing for you thirty counsels and insights? 21 to impart to you the verity of words, that you may return truthful answers to those who sent you.

Justice for the Poor, Temperament, and the Preservation of Boundaries

22 Do not plunder the indigent because he is poor, nor oppress the afflicted at the gate. 23 For the LORD shall champion their cause and despoil those who despoil them. 24 Do not fraternize with a choleric man, nor consort with one of furious temper, 25 lest you assimilate his conduct and ensnare your own soul.

26 Do not join those who pledge themselves as sureties for debts. 27 If you lack means to discharge the debt, why should your bed be seized from beneath you? 28 Do not displace an ancient landmark that your ancestors established. 29 Do you perceive a man diligent in his craft? He shall attend kings, not remain among the obscure.
