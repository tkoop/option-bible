The Fabrication of the Bronze Altar and the Molten Sea

1 He fashioned a bronze altar twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in breadth, and ten cubits in height. 2 He also cast the Sea, a circular vessel whose rim extended ten cubits from one edge to the opposite edge and whose height was five cubits; a line of thirty cubits encompassed its circumference. 3 Beneath its rim, bovine figures encircled it, ten in each cubit, running continuously around the whole perimeter; these bulls were cast in two rows as an integral part of the same molten piece. 4 The Sea rested upon twelve oxen, three facing north, three west, three south, and three east, so that the vessel stood above them while their hindquarters were turned inward. 5 Its wall was a handbreadth thick, and its rim was fashioned like the rim of a cup, resembling a lily blossom; the vessel held three thousand baths.

The Bronze Appointments and the Temple Courts

6 He likewise made ten basins for washing, placing five on the right and five on the left; in them were rinsed the implements used for the burnt offering, whereas the Sea served the priests for their ablutions. 7 He made ten lampstands of gold according to their prescribed pattern and set them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left. 8 He also made ten tables and positioned them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left, together with a hundred gold sprinkling bowls.

9 He constructed the priests' courtyard, the great court, and the doors of the court, overlaying those doors with bronze. 10 He set the Sea on the south side, at the southeast corner.

The Completion and Casting of Huram's Bronze Work

11 Huram made the pots, the shovels, and the sprinkling bowls; thus Huram brought to completion the work he had undertaken for King Solomon in the house of God: 12 the two pillars; the two bowl-shaped capitals atop the pillars; the two networks intended to cover the two bowl-shaped capitals atop the pillars; 13 the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, arranged in two rows on each network to cover the two bowl-shaped capitals on the faces of the pillars; 14 the stands and the basins resting upon the stands; 15 the Sea and the twelve oxen beneath it.

16 The pots, the shovels, the forks, and all their utensils Huram-abi fashioned for King Solomon for the house of the LORD out of polished bronze. 17 The king had them cast in clay molds in the plain of the Jordan, between Succoth and Zeredah. 18 Solomon produced all these articles in such prodigious abundance that the weight of the bronze was never determined.

The Golden Appointments of the Sanctuary

19 Solomon also made all the furnishings for the house of God: the golden altar; the tables upon which the Bread of the Presence was laid; 20 the lampstands of pure gold, with their lamps, to be kindled before the inner sanctuary in accordance with the ordinance; 21 the floral ornaments, the lamps, and the tongs, all of pure gold; 22 the snuffers, the sprinkling bowls, the dishes, and the censers of pure gold; and the gold doors of the innermost chamber, the Most Holy Place, together with the doors of the main hall.
