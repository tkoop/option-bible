The Allotment of the Northern Tribes

1 These are the names of the tribes. Beginning at the northern boundary, Dan shall receive one portion, whose frontier runs from the road to Hethlon through Lebo-hamath to Hazar-enan and the border of Damascus, with Hamath to the north, extending from the eastern boundary to the sea. 2 South of Dan, Asher shall receive one portion, stretching from the eastern boundary to the western. 3 South of Asher, Naphtali shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 4 South of Naphtali, Manasseh shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 5 South of Manasseh, Ephraim shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 6 South of Ephraim, Reuben shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 7 South of Reuben, Judah shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western.

The Sacred Portion and the City

8 Bordering Judah on the south, from east to west, there shall be the special contribution you are to set apart: twenty-five thousand cubits in width, with a length equal to one tribal portion from the eastern boundary to the western, and with the sanctuary in its midst. 9 The contribution that you offer to the LORD shall be twenty-five thousand cubits in length and ten thousand cubits in width. 10 This shall be the holy portion for the priests: twenty-five thousand cubits on the north, ten thousand cubits on the west, ten thousand cubits on the east, and twenty-five thousand cubits on the south, with the sanctuary of the LORD in its midst. 11 It shall belong to the consecrated priests, the descendants of Zadok, who kept My charge and did not go astray as the Levites did when the Israelites went astray. 12 It shall be a special contribution for them from the land's contribution, a most holy portion adjoining the territory of the Levites. 13 Alongside the priests' territory, the Levites shall have an area twenty-five thousand cubits in length and ten thousand cubits in width; the whole length shall be twenty-five thousand cubits, and the width ten thousand cubits. 14 They shall not sell any part of it, nor exchange it, nor alienate the firstfruits of the land, because it is holy to the LORD. 15 The remaining area—five thousand cubits in width and twenty-five thousand in length—shall be common land for the city, for dwellings and pasture, and the city shall be in its midst. 16 These shall be the city's dimensions: four thousand five hundred cubits on the north, four thousand five hundred on the south, four thousand five hundred on the east, and four thousand five hundred on the west. 17 The city's pastureland shall extend two hundred fifty cubits to the north, two hundred fifty to the south, two hundred fifty to the east, and two hundred fifty to the west. 18 The remainder of the holy portion, bordering the holy allotment, shall be ten thousand cubits to the east and ten thousand to the west; its produce shall provide food for the city's workers. 19 The city's workers, drawn from all the tribes of Israel, shall cultivate it. 20 The entire portion shall be a square, twenty-five thousand cubits by twenty-five thousand cubits; you shall set apart the holy portion together with the city's property. 21 The remainder on both sides of the holy portion and the city's property shall belong to the prince: eastward from the twenty-five thousand cubits of the holy portion to the eastern boundary, and westward from the twenty-five thousand cubits to the western boundary, with the holy portion and the sanctuary of the temple in its midst. 22 Thus the Levitical property and the city's property shall lie in the center of the area belonging to the prince, whose territory shall lie between the borders of Judah and Benjamin.

The Allotment of the Southern Tribes

23 As for the remaining tribes, Benjamin shall receive one portion, south of the prince's allotment, extending from the eastern boundary to the western. 24 South of Benjamin, Simeon shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 25 South of Simeon, Issachar shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 26 South of Issachar, Zebulun shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 27 South of Zebulun, Gad shall receive one portion, from the eastern boundary to the western. 28 Gad's southern boundary shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, then along the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea. 29 “This is the land that you are to allot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these are their portions,” declares the Lord the LORD.

The City's Gates and Its Name

30 “These are the exits of the city: on the north side, which shall be four thousand five hundred cubits long, 31 the gates of the city shall be named after the tribes of Israel. There shall be three gates on the north: the gate of Reuben, the gate of Judah, and the gate of Levi. 32 On the east side, which shall be four thousand five hundred cubits long, there shall be three gates: the gate of Joseph, the gate of Benjamin, and the gate of Dan. 33 On the south side, which shall be four thousand five hundred cubits long, there shall be three gates: the gate of Simeon, the gate of Issachar, and the gate of Zebulun. 34 On the west side, which shall be four thousand five hundred cubits long, there shall be three gates: the gate of Gad, the gate of Asher, and the gate of Naphtali. 35 The perimeter shall be eighteen thousand cubits, and from that day the name of the city shall be: The LORD Is There.”
