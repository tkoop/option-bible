The Genealogical Record of Israel and the Exiles’ Return

1 Thus all Israel was enrolled by genealogies, and behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah; Judah, however, was carried away to Babylon for its unfaithfulness. 2 The first inhabitants who dwelt in their holdings in their cities were Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the Nethinim. 3 From among the descendants of Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh, some dwelt in Jerusalem.

4 Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, of the sons of Perez son of Judah. 5 Of the Shilonites, Asaiah the firstborn and his sons. 6 Of the sons of Zerah, Jeuel, together with his brethren, six hundred and ninety.

7 Of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah. 8 Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah. 9 Nine hundred and fifty-six, together with their brethren according to their generations—all these men being heads of ancestral houses for their respective clans.

Priests, Levites, and the Service of the House of God

10 Of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin. 11 Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, chief officer of the house of God. 12 Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer. 13 One thousand seven hundred and sixty, together with their brethren, heads of their ancestral houses—valiant men engaged in the service of the house of God.

14 Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari. 15 Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph. 16 Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who dwelt in the villages of the Netophathites.

Gatekeepers, Chambers, and Sacred Duties

17 The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, and Ahiman, with their brethren; Shallum was the chief. 18 To this day they are gatekeepers at the king’s gate on the east, for the camp of the sons of Levi. 19 Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his brethren of the Korahite house, were over the work of the service, keepers of the thresholds of the tent; their fathers had been keepers of the entrance to the camp of the the LORD. 20 In former times Phinehas son of Eleazar had been their chief, and the the LORD was with him. 21 Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the door of the tent of meeting. 22 All these, selected as gatekeepers at the thresholds, numbered two hundred and twelve. They were enrolled by genealogy in their villages, for David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to their trusted office.

23 They and their sons were in charge of the gates of the house of the the LORD, that is, the house of the tent, according to their watches. 24 The gatekeepers were stationed on the four sides—east, west, north, and south. 25 Their brethren in their villages were to come in at appointed times, seven days at a time, to serve with them. 26 For the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were over the chambers and treasuries of the house of God. 27 They lodged round about the house of God, because the watch was committed to them; they were in charge of the key and opened it morning by morning.

28 Some of them were in charge of the vessels of service, for they brought them in and took them out by count. 29 Some were appointed over the vessels and over all the holy vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices. 30 Some of the sons of the priests compounded the ointment of spices. 31 Mattithiah, one of the Levites—the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite—was entrusted with the making of the baked cakes. 32 Some of the sons of the Kohathites, their brethren, were over the showbread, to prepare it Sabbath by Sabbath. 33 These are the singers, heads of the Levitical ancestral houses, who lodged in the chambers and were free from other duty, because they were engaged in their work day and night. 34 These were heads of the fathers of the Levites, according to their generations, chief men; these dwelt at Jerusalem.

The Lineage of Saul from Gibeon to Jerusalem

35 Jeiel, the father of Gibeon, dwelt in Gibeon; his wife’s name was Maacah. 36 His firstborn son was Abdon, and then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, and Nadab. 37 Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth. 38 Mikloth fathered Shimeam. These too dwelt alongside their brethren in Jerusalem, opposite their kinsmen. 39 Ner fathered Kish, Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 40 The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal fathered Micah. 41 The sons of Micah were Pithon, Melech, and Tarea. 42 Ahaz fathered Jarah, Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri fathered Moza. 43 Moza fathered Binea; Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son. 44 Azel had six sons, whose names were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan; these were the sons of Azel.
