Abram Returns from Egypt to Bethel

1 Abram ascended from Egypt toward the Negev—he together with his wife, all that belonged to him, and Lot in his company. 2 Abram was exceedingly wealthy in livestock, silver, and gold. 3 Proceeding by stages from the south, he came to Bethel, to the place between Bethel and Ai where his tent had first been pitched. 4 It was the site of the altar he had originally erected; there Abram invoked the name of the LORD.

Abram and Lot Part Ways Amid Growing Prosperity

5 Lot, who traveled with Abram, likewise possessed flocks, herds, and tents. 6 The land could not sustain both men dwelling together, for their holdings had become too vast; they could not remain in the same territory. 7 Strife arose between Abram’s herdsmen and Lot’s, while the Canaanites and Perizzites were still dwelling in the land.

8 Abram said to Lot, “Let there be no strife between me and you, nor between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are brothers. 9 Is not the whole land before you? Separate, I pray, from me: if you go to the left, I will go to the right; and if you go to the right, I will go to the left.”

Lot Chooses the Jordan Plain

10 Lot lifted his eyes and beheld the whole plain of the Jordan, that it was well watered throughout—before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah—like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt as you approach Zoar. 11 Lot chose for himself the entire plain of the Jordan, and he journeyed eastward; thus the two men separated from each other. 12 Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled in the cities of the plain and pitched his tent as far as Sodom.

13 Now the men of Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful against the LORD.

The LORD Promises the Land to Abram

14 After Lot had separated from him, the LORD said to Abram, “Lift your eyes from the place where you are, and look northward, southward, eastward, and westward. 15 For all the land that you see, I will give to you and to your offspring forever. 16 I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone can count the dust of the earth, your offspring also shall be counted. 17 Arise, walk through the land in its length and breadth, for I will give it to you.”

18 Abram moved his tent and came to dwell by the oaks of Mamre, which are at Hebron, and there he built an altar to the LORD.
