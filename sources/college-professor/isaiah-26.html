A Song of Salvation in the Land of Judah

1 In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: “We have a strong city; He sets up salvation as walls and ramparts. 2 Open the gates, that the righteous nation may enter—the one that keeps faith. 3 You will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are stayed on You, because they trust in You.

Trust in the Rock of Ages and the Humbling of the Proud

“4 Trust in the the LORD forever, for the the LORD, even the the LORD Himself, is the Rock of ages. 5 For He has brought low those who dwell on high; He lays the exalted city low, bringing it down to the ground and casting it into the dust. 6 Feet trample it—the feet of the poor, the steps of the needy.

The Righteous Path and the Discipline of the Wicked

“7 The path of the righteous is level; O Upright One, You make the way of the righteous smooth. 8 Indeed, in the path of Your judgments, O the LORD, we wait for You; Your name and renown are the desire of our souls. 9 My soul yearns for You in the night; my spirit within me seeks You earnestly. For when Your judgments are upon the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness. 10 Though favor is shown to the wicked, he does not learn righteousness; in a land of uprightness he deals perversely, and does not perceive the majesty of the the LORD. 11 O the LORD, Your hand is lifted high, yet they do not see it. Let them see Your zeal for Your people and be ashamed; let the fire reserved for Your enemies consume them.

Peace, Discipline, and the Hope of Resurrection

“12 O the LORD, You will establish peace for us, for You have accomplished all our works for us. 13 O the LORD our God, other lords besides You have ruled over us, but Your name alone we honor. 14 The dead shall not live; the departed shall not arise. Therefore You have visited them with destruction and annihilated them, and You have blotted out all remembrance of them. 15 You have increased the nation, O the LORD; You have increased the nation. You are glorified; You have extended all the borders of the land. 16 O the LORD, in distress they sought You; they poured out a whispered prayer when Your chastening was upon them. 17 As a woman with child who draws near to her time of delivery writhes and cries out in her pangs, so were we before You, O the LORD. 18 We were with child; we writhed in pain, yet we brought forth only wind. We have wrought no deliverance in the earth, and the inhabitants of the world have not fallen. 19 Your dead shall live; their bodies shall rise. Awake and sing, you who dwell in the dust! For Your dew is as the dew of the dawn, and the earth shall cast forth her dead. 20 Go, My people, enter your chambers and shut your doors behind you; hide yourselves for a little moment until the indignation has passed. 21 For behold, the the LORD is coming forth from His place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity; the earth will disclose the blood shed upon her, and will no longer cover her slain.”
