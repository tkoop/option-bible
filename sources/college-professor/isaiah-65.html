A Prophetic Indictment of Israel's Rebellion

1 "I was sought by those who did not petition Me; I was found by those who did not quest after Me. To a nation that did not invoke My name, I said, 'Here am I, here am I.'" 2 "All day long I have extended My hands to an obdurate people, who pursue a path that is not good, walking after their own machinations." 3 "A people who perpetually provoke Me to My face, sacrificing in gardens and burning incense upon bricks; 4 who sit among tombs and lodge in secluded places; who consume swine's flesh, and whose vessels hold the putrid broth of abominations; 5 who say, 'Keep away from me; do not approach me, for I am too sacred for you.' Such people are a provocation that rankles in My nostrils, a fire that burns all the day. 6 Behold, it is inscribed before Me: I will not be silent, but I will requite, and I will requite into their bosom, 7 your iniquities and the iniquities of your fathers together, says the LORD, because they burned incense on the mountains and reviled Me on the hills; therefore I will measure their retribution into their bosom."

A Preserved Remnant and a Renewed Inheritance

8 "Thus says the LORD: 'As fresh wine is found in a cluster, and one says, "Do not destroy it, for a blessing is in it," so will I act for the sake of My servants, that I may not destroy them all. 9 I will bring forth descendants from Jacob, and from Judah an heir of My mountains; My chosen shall inherit it, and My servants shall dwell there. 10 Sharon shall become a pasture for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a repose for herds, for My people who have sought Me.'"

Judgment upon the Apostate and the Joy of the Servants

11 "But you who forsake the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who spread a table for Fortune and pour mixed wine for Destiny— 12 I will allot you to the sword, and all of you shall stoop to the slaughter, because when I called, you did not answer; when I spoke, you did not hearken; but you did what was evil in My sight and chose that in which I did not delight."

13 "Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: 'Behold, My servants shall eat, but you shall hunger; behold, My servants shall drink, but you shall thirst; behold, My servants shall rejoice, but you shall be put to shame. 14 Behold, My servants shall exult with gladness of heart, but you shall cry out in anguish of heart and wail for a crushed spirit. 15 You shall leave your name as an execration to My chosen, and the Lord the LORD will put you to death, but He will call His servants by another name, 16 so that whoever blesses himself in the land shall bless himself by the God of truth, and whoever swears in the land shall swear by the God of truth, because the former afflictions are forgotten and hidden from My eyes.'"

The New Creation and Eschatological Peace

17 "For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth, and the former things shall not be remembered nor arise in the mind. 18 But be glad and rejoice forever in what I create; for behold, I create Jerusalem as a rejoicing and her people as a joy. 19 I will rejoice in Jerusalem and be glad in My people; no more shall the sound of weeping or the cry of distress be heard in it. 20 No more shall an infant there perish after a mere handful of days, nor an old man fail to complete his years; for one who dies at a hundred shall be reckoned a mere youth, and the sinner who attains a hundred shall be accursed. 21 They shall build houses and inhabit them; they shall plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 22 They shall not build and another inhabit; they shall not plant and another eat; for as the days of a tree, so shall be the days of My people, and My chosen shall long enjoy the work of their hands. 23 They shall not labor in vain, nor bear children for calamity; for they are the offspring of the blessed of the LORD, and their descendants with them. 24 Before they invoke Me, I will answer; while yet they are speaking, I will hear. 25 The wolf and the lamb shall feed together, the lion shall eat straw like the ox, and dust shall be the serpent's food. They shall not do harm nor destroy in all My holy mountain, says the LORD."
