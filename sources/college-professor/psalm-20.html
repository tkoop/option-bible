A Liturgical Petition for the King's Deliverance

1 May the LORD respond to you in the day of affliction; may the renown of the God of Jacob establish you securely. 2 May He dispatch succor to you from the sanctuary, and uphold you from Zion. 3 May He remember all your grain offerings, and regard your burnt offerings with favor. Selah.

4 May He bestow upon you the longing of your heart, and bring all your plans to fruition. 5 May we exult in your deliverance, and raise our banners in the name of our God. May the LORD satisfy all your petitions.

Confidence in the LORD's Anointed and His Saving Might

6 Now I am assured that the LORD delivers His anointed; He answers him from His holy heaven by the triumphant might of His right hand.

7 Some place their confidence in chariots and others in horses, but we invoke the name of the LORD our God. 8 They stumble and are laid low, whereas we arise and remain steadfast.

9 The LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call.
