The Circumstances of Jeremiah’s Oracle to Baruch

1 The oracle that Jeremiah the prophet delivered to Baruch son of Neriah, when Baruch committed these words to a scroll from Jeremiah’s dictation, during the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

The LORD’s Counsel to Baruch in a Season of National Dissolution

2 “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning you, Baruch:

3 “You have said, ‘Alas for me now! For the LORD has compounded grief upon my affliction; I am exhausted by my groaning, and I have found no respite.’

4 “Thus you shall say to him, ‘Thus says the LORD: Behold, what I have erected I am dismantling, and what I have planted I am uprooting—even this entire land.

5 “‘And do you seek exalted things for yourself? Do not seek them; for behold, I am bringing calamity upon all flesh, says the LORD. Yet I will grant you your life as spoil in every place to which you go.’”
