A Psalm of David: The LORD as Shepherd and Host

1 A psalm of David. The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. 2 He makes me lie down in verdant pastures; He leads me beside tranquil waters. 3 He restores my soul; He guides me along righteous paths for His name's sake.

4 Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.

5 You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. 6 Surely goodness and mercy shall pursue me all the days of my life, and I shall dwell in the house of the LORD forever.
