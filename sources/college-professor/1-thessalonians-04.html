Exhortations to a Holy and God-Pleasing Walk

1 Finally, then, brothers, we request and exhort you in the Lord Jesus that, just as you received from us how you must walk and please God—indeed, as you are walking—so you may abound all the more. 2 For you know what injunctions we delivered to you through the Lord Jesus.

The Will of God: Sanctification and Abstention from Immorality

3 For this is the will of God: your sanctification, namely, that you abstain from sexual immorality; 4 that each of you know how to acquire his own body in sanctification and honor, 5 not in the passion of lust, as do the Gentiles who do not know God; 6 that no one transgress and defraud his brother in this matter, because the Lord is an avenger concerning all these things, as we also forewarned you and solemnly testified. 7 For God did not call us to impurity but in sanctification. 8 Therefore, whoever rejects this does not reject a human being but God, who also gives His Holy Spirit to you.

Brotherly Love and a Quiet, Industrious Life

9 Now concerning brotherly love, you have no need for us to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another. 10 Indeed, you are doing so toward all the brothers throughout Macedonia. But we exhort you, brothers, to abound still more, 11 and to aspire to live quietly, to mind your own affairs, and to work with your own hands, just as we instructed you, 12 so that you may walk decorously toward outsiders and be in need of nothing.

Comfort Concerning Those Who Have Fallen Asleep

13 But we do not want you to be uninformed, brothers, concerning those who are asleep, so that you may not grieve as the rest do who have no hope. 14 For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so too will God bring with Him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus.

The Lord's Coming and the Gathering of His People

15 For this we declare to you by a word of the Lord: we who are alive, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will not precede those who have fallen asleep. 16 For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a cry of command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God; and the dead in Christ will rise first. 17 Then we who are alive, who remain, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air, and so we will always be with the Lord. 18 Therefore encourage one another with these words.
