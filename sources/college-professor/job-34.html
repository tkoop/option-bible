Elihu Summons the Wise to Weigh Job’s Claims

1 Then Elihu resumed his discourse and said: 2 “Hear my words, you who are wise; attend to me, you who possess learning. 3 For the ear assays words even as the palate savors food. 4 Let us choose justice for ourselves and discern among ourselves what is good. 5 For Job has declared, ‘I am righteous, yet the LORD has deprived me of justice. 6 Should I lie concerning my right? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’ 7 What mortal is like Job, who drinks in derision as though it were water? 8 He keeps company with evildoers and walks in the way of the wicked. 9 For he has said, ‘It profits a man nothing that he should delight in God.’

The LORD’s Justice and Retribution

10 “Therefore listen to me, you men of understanding: far be it from the LORD to do evil, and from the Almighty to do wrong. 11 For according to a man’s deeds He repays him, and according to his ways He lets it find him. 12 Indeed, the LORD does not act wickedly, and the Almighty does not pervert justice. 13 Who gave Him charge over the earth? Who appointed Him over the whole world? 14 If He were to set His heart upon it and gather to Himself His Spirit and breath, 15 all flesh would perish together, and man would return to dust. 16 If you have understanding, hear this; give ear to the sound of my words. 17 Can one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the just and mighty One? 18 Is it fitting to say to a king, ‘Worthless one!’ or to nobles, ‘Wicked men!’? 19 Yet He is not partial to princes, nor does He regard the rich more than the poor, for they are all the work of His hands. 20 In a moment they die, even at midnight; the people are shaken and pass away, and the mighty are removed without a hand.

God’s Omniscient Governance

21 “For His eyes are upon the ways of a man, and He sees all his steps. 22 There is no darkness nor shadow of death where the workers of iniquity may hide. 23 For He need not examine a man further, that he should go before the LORD in judgment. 24 He breaks the mighty without inquiry and sets others in their place. 25 Therefore He knows their deeds, and He overthrows them in the night, and they are crushed. 26 He strikes them as wicked men in the sight of others, 27 because they turned aside from following Him and had no regard for any of His ways, 28 so that they caused the cry of the poor to reach Him, and He heard the cry of the afflicted. 29 When He gives quietness, who then can condemn? And when He hides His face, who then can see Him? Whether a nation or a man alone, 30 He prevents the godless man from reigning, so that the people are not ensnared.

Elihu’s Final Summons and Rebuke

31 “Suppose someone says to the LORD, ‘I have endured chastening; I will offend no more. 32 Teach me what I cannot see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’ 33 Should He repay you on your terms when you refuse to repent? You must decide, not I; so tell me what you know. 34 Men of understanding will say to me—indeed, a wise man who hears me— 35 ‘Job speaks without knowledge; his words lack insight.’ 36 If only Job were tried to the utmost, for his answers are those of wicked men. 37 For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against the LORD.”
