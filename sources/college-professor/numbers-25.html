Israel’s Seduction at Shittim and the Outbreak of Plague

1 While Israel was sojourning in Shittim, the people began to prostitute themselves with the daughters of Moab. 2 The Moabite women invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to those gods. 3 Thus Israel devoted itself to Baal of Peor, and the anger of the LORD blazed against Israel.

4 The LORD said to Moses, “Take all the leaders of the people and execute them in broad daylight before the LORD, so that the fierce anger of the LORD may be turned away from Israel.”

5 So Moses said to the judges of Israel, “Let every one of you put to death those among his people who have devoted themselves to Baal of Peor.”

6 Then, behold, an Israelite man came and brought a Midianite woman to his kinsmen in the sight of Moses and the whole congregation of the Israelites, while they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 7 When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this, he rose from the midst of the congregation, seized a spear in his hand, 8 pursued the Israelite man into the tent, and thrust both of them through—the Israelite man and the woman—right through her belly, so that the plague was stayed from the Israelites. 9 Those who died in the plague numbered twenty-four thousand.

The LORD’s Covenant with Phinehas

10 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 11 “Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned My wrath away from the Israelites, because he was zealous with My zeal among them, so that I did not destroy the Israelites in My zeal. 12 Therefore declare: Behold, I am giving him My covenant of peace. 13 It shall be to him and to his descendants after him a covenant of perpetual priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the Israelites.”

14 Now the name of the slain Israelite man who was slain with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, a leader of a Simeonite ancestral house. 15 And the name of the slain Midianite woman was Cozbi daughter of Zur, a tribal chief of a clan in Midian.

The Mandate to Oppose Midian

16 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 17 “Harass the Midianites and strike them down, 18 for they are harassing you with their wiles, with which they have beguiled you in the affair of Peor and in the affair of Cozbi, the daughter of a Midianite leader, their sister, who was slain on the day of the plague because of Peor.”
