Israel Without a King and Dan's Unsettled Inheritance

1 In those days Israel had no king, and the tribe of Dan was seeking an inheritance in which to settle, because no allotment had yet fallen to it among the tribes of Israel.

The Reconnaissance of Laish and Micah's Priest

2 The Danites sent five men from their clans, from the outlying districts—valiant men—from Zorah and Eshtaol, to spy out the land and reconnoiter it. They said to them, "Go and reconnoiter the land." So they went up into the hill country of Ephraim, to Micah's house, and lodged there.

3 While they were with Micah, they recognized the young Levite's voice and turned aside there, asking, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What do you have here?"

4 He answered, "Micah has done such and such for me: he hired me, and I became his priest."

5 They said to him, "Please inquire of God, that we may know whether the way on which we are going will prosper."

6 The priest said to them, "Go in peace; the LORD looks with favor on your path, the one in which you are going."

7 The five men departed and came to Laish, where they saw a people dwelling securely, in the manner of the Sidonians—quiet and secure—with no authority in the land to humiliate them in any way. They were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

8 They returned to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brothers asked them, "What do you report?"

9 They answered, "Arise and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good. And will you remain idle? Do not be remiss in going to take possession of the land."

10 When you go, you will come upon a secure people, and the land is broad, for God has given it into your hand—a place lacking nothing that is in the earth.

The Danite Expedition and the Seizure of Micah's Shrine

11 Six hundred men of the Danites set out from there, from Zorah and Eshtaol, girded with weapons of war. 12 They went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah; therefore they called that place Mahaneh-dan to this day—it lies west of Kiriath-jearim. 13 From there they passed on into the hill country of Ephraim and came to Micah's house.

14 The five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod, teraphim, a carved image, and a cast image? Now therefore consider what you will do."

15 They turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite at Micah's house, and greeted him.

16 The six hundred Danites, armed with their weapons of war, were stationed at the entrance of the gate. 17 The five men who had gone to spy out the land went in and took the carved image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the cast image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men armed for war.

18 These men entered Micah's house and took the carved image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the cast image. Then the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

19 They said to him, "Hush. Come with us and be a father and priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to one man's house, or to a tribe and clan in Israel?"

20 The priest's heart was glad; he took the ephod, the teraphim, and the carved image, and went in the midst of the people. 21 They turned and departed, putting the little ones, the livestock, and the baggage ahead of them.

22 When they had gone far from Micah's house, the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were called together and overtook the Danites.

23 They called to the Danites, who turned their faces and said to Micah, "What troubles you, that you have raised such a cry?"

24 He said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and gone away; and what do I have left? How then do you say to me, 'What troubles you?'"

25 The Danites said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest fierce men fall upon you and you lose your life and the lives of your household."

26 The Danites went on their way; and when Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.

The Capture of Laish and the Establishment of Dan

27 They took what Micah had made, and the priest who had belonged to him, and came to Laish, to a people quiet and secure, and put them to the sword and burned the city with fire. 28 There was no deliverer, because it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone; it lay in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the city and dwelt in it.

29 They called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; however, the name of the city formerly was Laish.

30 The Danites set up for themselves the carved image; and Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day the land was carried into exile. 31 They kept Micah's carved image, which he had made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh.
