A Hymn of Praise to the LORD for Jerusalem’s Restoration and His Sovereign Providence

1 Praise the LORD, for it is good; sing praises to our God, for it is pleasant, and praise is befitting. 2 The LORD builds up Jerusalem; He gathers the exiles of Israel. 3 He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

4 He counts the number of the stars; He calls them all by name. 5 Great is our Lord and mighty in power; His understanding is beyond measure. 6 The LORD lifts up the humble; He casts the wicked to the ground.

7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the lyre. 8 He covers the sky with clouds; He provides rain for the earth and makes grass grow on the hills. 9 He gives food to the beasts and to the young ravens when they cry.

10 He does not delight in the strength of the horse, nor take pleasure in the legs of a man. 11 The LORD takes pleasure in those who fear Him, in those who hope in His steadfast love.

12 Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion! 13 For He strengthens the bars of your gates; He blesses your children within you. 14 He makes peace within your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat.

15 He sends forth His command to the earth; His word runs swiftly. 16 He gives snow like wool; He scatters frost like ashes; He casts forth His hail like crumbs. 17 Who can stand before His cold? 18 He sends out His word and melts them; He makes His wind blow, and the waters flow.

19 He declares His word to Jacob, His statutes and ordinances to Israel. 20 He has not dealt thus with any nation; they do not know His ordinances. Praise the LORD!
