Shepherding the Flock and the Oversight of Elders

1 I exhort the elders among you, being myself a fellow elder and witness to the sufferings of Christ, and likewise a participant in the glory that is about to be disclosed. 2 Shepherd the flock of God that is among you, exercising oversight not under compulsion but willingly, in accordance with God’s will, and not for sordid gain but with eager readiness. 3 Do not lord it over those allotted to your charge, but become patterns for the flock. 4 And when the Chief Shepherd is manifested, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.

Humility, Vigilance, and Resistance to the Adversary

5 Likewise, you who are younger, be subject to the elders; and all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble. 6 Humble yourselves, therefore, under the mighty hand of God, so that He may exalt you in due time. 7 casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He cares for you.

8 Be sober-minded; be watchful. Your adversary the devil prowls about like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour. 9 Resist him, firm in faith, knowing that the same sufferings are being endured by your brethren throughout the world.

The God of All Grace and His Restorative Purpose

10 And after you have suffered a little while, the God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Christ, will Himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you. 11 To Him be the dominion forever and ever. Amen.

Final Exhortations, Fraternal Greetings, and Peace

12 Through Silvanus, whom I regard as a faithful brother, I have written to you briefly, exhorting and testifying that this is the true grace of God; stand firm in it. 13 The fellow-chosen one in Babylon greets you, and so does my son Mark. 14 Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.
