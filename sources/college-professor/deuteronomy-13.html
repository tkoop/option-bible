The Peril of Prophetic Deception

1If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams should arise in your midst and give you a sign or a wonder, 2and the sign or wonder of which he spoke to you comes to pass, while he says, “Let us follow other gods whom you have not known, and let us serve them,”

3you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer of dreams, for the the LORD your God is testing you, to ascertain whether you love the the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul. 4You shall walk after the the LORD your God and fear Him and keep His commandments and obey His voice; you shall serve Him and cling to Him. 5But that prophet or that dreamer of dreams shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against the the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery, in order to turn you aside from the way in which the the LORD your God commanded you to walk. Thus shall you purge the evil from your midst.

The Seduction of Intimate Companions

6If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter or the wife of your bosom or your friend who is as your own soul secretly entices you, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods,” which neither you nor your fathers have known— 7gods of the peoples around you, whether near you or far from you, from one end of the earth to the other—

8you shall not yield to him or listen to him, nor shall your eye pity him, nor shall you spare him, nor shall you conceal him. 9Rather, you shall surely kill him; your hand shall be first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people. 10You shall stone him to death with stones, because he sought to draw you away from the the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. 11Then all Israel shall hear and fear, and they shall never again perpetrate such wickedness among you.

The Destruction of an Apostate City

12If you hear in one of your cities, which the the LORD your God is giving you to dwell in, 13that worthless men have gone out from among you and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods,” which you have not known, 14then you shall inquire and search diligently and ask carefully; and behold, if the matter proves true and certain, that such an abomination has been committed among you,

15you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, devoting it and all that is in it, including its livestock, to utter destruction by the sword. 16You shall gather all its spoil into the midst of its open square and burn the city and all its spoil with fire as a whole burnt offering to the the LORD your God; it shall remain a perpetual ruin, never to be rebuilt. 17None of the devoted things shall cling to your hand, so that the the LORD may turn from the fierceness of His anger and grant you mercy and have compassion on you and multiply you, as He swore to your fathers. 18If you obey the voice of the the LORD your God, keeping all His commandments that I am commanding you today, and doing what is right in the eyes of the the LORD your God.
