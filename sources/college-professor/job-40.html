The LORD’s Initial Challenge to Job

1 Then the LORD answered Job, saying: 2 "Shall one who contends with the Almighty presume to correct Him? Let him who reproves God answer."

3 Then Job answered the LORD and said: 4 "Behold, I am of no account; what reply can I make to You? I have laid my hand upon my mouth. 5 I have spoken once, and I will not answer again; twice, yet I will add nothing further."

The LORD’s Whirlwind Discourse

6 Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said: 7 "Brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall answer Me. 8 Will you indeed nullify My judgment? Will you condemn Me so that you may be vindicated? 9 Have you an arm comparable to God’s? Can you thunder with a voice like His? 10 Adorn yourself with majesty and exaltation; clothe yourself with glory and splendor. 11 Unleash the torrents of your wrath; look upon every proud man and abase him. 12 Regard every proud man and bring him low; trample the wicked in their place. 13 Bury them all in the dust; imprison them in the grave. 14 Then I too will acknowledge you, for your own right hand can save you."

Behemoth, the Preeminent of God’s Works

15 "Behold now Behemoth, whom I made along with you; he feeds on grass like an ox. 16 Behold, his vigor resides in his loins, and his power in the muscles of his belly. 17 He makes his tail rigid like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are interwoven. 18 His bones are like tubes of bronze; his limbs resemble bars of iron. 19 He is the preeminent work of God; only his Maker can draw near him with a sword. 20 The mountains yield him their produce, and all the beasts of the field disport themselves there. 21 He reclines under the lotus plants, in the covert of the reeds and in the marsh. 22 The lotus plants shade him with their shadow; the willows of the brook encircle him. 23 Though the river be turbulent, he is not dismayed; he remains secure, even should the Jordan surge against his mouth. 24 Can anyone seize him while he keeps watch, or pierce his nose with a snare?"
