The Healing of the Man Born Blind

1 As He was passing by, He noticed a man who had been blind from birth. 2 His disciples inquired of Him, "Rabbi, who sinned—this man or his parents—that he should be born blind?"

3 Jesus responded, "Neither this man nor his parents sinned; rather, his condition exists so that the works of God might be manifested in him. 4 As long as it is day, we must accomplish the works of Him who sent Me; night approaches, when no one is able to labor. 5 While I remain in the world, I am the light of the world."

6 Having uttered these words, He expectorated upon the ground, fashioned mud from the spittle, and anointed the man's eyes with that mud. 7 He said to him, "Go, wash in the Pool of Siloam" (a name meaning "Sent"). Accordingly, he departed, washed, and returned with his sight restored.

8 His neighbors and those who had previously observed him begging began to inquire, "Is this not the man who used to sit and beg?"

9 Some affirmed, "Yes, he is."

Others countered, "No, but he resembles him."

Yet he himself persisted in saying, "I am he."

10 So they asked him, "How, then, were your eyes opened?"

11 He replied, "The man called Jesus prepared mud, smeared it upon my eyes, and instructed me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' So I went, washed, and received my sight."

12 "Where is He?" they asked.

"I do not know," he replied.

The Pharisees Investigate the Healing

13 They conducted the formerly blind man to the Pharisees. 14 Now the day on which Jesus had fashioned the mud and opened his eyes was a Sabbath.

15 So the Pharisees also asked him how he had received his sight. He told them, "He placed mud upon my eyes; I washed, and now I see."

16 Some of the Pharisees declared, "This man is not from God, for He does not observe the Sabbath."

Others inquired, "How can a sinful man perform such signs?"

Thus a schism arose among them.

17 So they again questioned the blind man, "What do you say about Him, inasmuch as He opened your eyes?"

He responded, "He is a prophet."

The Parents' Testimony and the Man's Expulsion

18 The Jews refused to credit that he had been blind and had received sight until they summoned his parents.

19 They interrogated them, asking, "Is this your son, whom you allege was born blind? How, then, does he see now?"

20 "We know that this is our son and that he was born blind," his parents replied. 21 "Yet how he now sees, we do not know, nor do we know who opened his eyes. Interrogate him; he is of age and will testify on his own behalf."

22 His parents spoke thus because they feared the Jews, who had already resolved that anyone confessing Him to be the Christ would be expelled from the synagogue. 23 Therefore his parents said, "He is of age; question him."

24 So a second time they summoned the man who had been blind and said to him, "Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner."

25 He answered, "Whether He is a sinner, I know not. One thing I know: I was blind, and now I see."

26 They then asked him, "What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?"

27 He answered, "I have already told you, and you did not listen. Why do you wish to hear it again? Do you also desire to become His disciples?"

28 They reviled him, saying, "You are His disciple; we are disciples of Moses." 29 "We know that God spoke to Moses, but we do not know whence this man comes."

30 The man replied, "This is a marvel indeed: you do not know whence He comes, yet He opened my eyes. 31 We know that God does not heed sinners, but He heeds the devout person who does His will. 32 Never from the dawn of creation has it been heard that anyone opened the eyes of a person born blind. 33 Were this man not from God, He could accomplish nothing."

34 They retorted, "You were born wholly in sin, and do you presume to instruct us?" And they expelled him.

Spiritual Blindness and Judgment

35 Jesus heard that they had expelled him, and upon finding him, He asked, "Do you believe in the Son of Man?"

36 He answered, "Who is He, Lord, that I may believe in Him?"

37 Jesus said to him, "You have seen Him, and He is the One conversing with you."

38 He said, "Lord, I believe," and he prostrated himself before Him.

39 Jesus declared, "For judgment I came into this world, so that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind."

40 Some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these words and asked, "Are we also blind?"

41 Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would have no sin. But now that you say, 'We see,' your sin remains."
