The Alliance of Ahab and Jehoshaphat and the Question of Ramoth-gilead

1 Three full years elapsed in which no hostilities disturbed the peace between Aram and Israel. 2 In the third year, Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, descended to the king of Israel.

3 The king of Israel said to his servants, "Are you aware that Ramoth-gilead is ours, yet we remain passive, failing to reclaim it from the king of Aram?"

4 He said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you accompany me to wage war at Ramoth-gilead?"

Jehoshaphat replied to the king of Israel, "I am as you are; my people are as your people; my horses are as your horses."

The Consultation of Prophets and the Summons of Micaiah

5 Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please inquire today for the word of the LORD."

6 The king of Israel gathered the prophets—about four hundred men—and said to them, "Shall I march against Ramoth-gilead in battle, or shall I forbear?"

They answered, "Go up, for the Lord will give it into the king's hand."

7 Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no longer a prophet of the LORD here, that we might consult him?"

8 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There remains one man through whom we may inquire of the LORD—Micaiah son of Imlah—but I detest him, for he never prophesies prosperity for me, only calamity."

Jehoshaphat said, "Let not the king say so."

9 The king of Israel called one of his officials and said, "Fetch Micaiah son of Imlah without delay."

10 Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting each upon his throne, robed in regalia, at the threshing floor beside the gate of Samaria, while all the prophets prophesied before them.

11 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah fashioned iron horns for himself and declared, "Thus says the LORD: With these you shall gore the Arameans until you make an end of them."

12 All the prophets prophesied likewise, saying, "Ascend to Ramoth-gilead and prosper, for the LORD will deliver it into the king's hand."

13 The messenger who had gone to summon Micaiah said to him, "Now observe: the prophets speak with one accord in the king's favor. Please let your word be like the word of one of them, and speak favorably."

14 Micaiah said, "As the LORD lives, only what the LORD speaks to me will I utter."

Micaiah's Vision of the Heavenly Council and Ahab's Doom

15 When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall we march against Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall we desist?"

He answered, "Go up and prosper, for the LORD will deliver it into the king's hand."

16 The king said to him, "How many times must I make you swear that you will speak to me nothing but the truth in the name of the LORD?"

17 He said, "I saw all Israel dispersed upon the mountains, like sheep without a shepherd; and the LORD said, 'These have no master; let each return to his own house in peace.'"

18 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would prophesy no good concerning me, but only evil?"

19 Micaiah said, "Therefore hear the word of the LORD: I saw the LORD seated upon His throne, and all the host of heaven standing beside Him, on His right and on His left."

20 And the LORD said, "Who will persuade Ahab to go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?" One spoke in this manner, and another in that.

21 Then a spirit came out and stood before the LORD and said, "I will entice him." And the LORD said to him, "By what means?"

22 He said, "I will go forth and become a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets." And He said, "You shall entice him, and you shall also succeed; go forth and do so."

23 Now, then, behold: the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the LORD has decreed disaster concerning you."

24 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek, saying, "By what way did the Spirit of the LORD pass from me to speak to you?"

25 Micaiah said, "Behold, you will see on that day when you enter an inner room to hide yourself."

26 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the ruler of the city and to Joash the king's son, 27 and say, 'Thus says the king: Imprison this man and feed him on bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace.'"

28 Micaiah said, "If you indeed return in peace, the LORD has not spoken by me." Then he said, "Hear, all peoples!"

Ahab's Death at Ramoth-gilead and the Aftermath of His Reign

29 So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead. 30 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and enter the battle, but you wear your robes." And the king of Israel disguised himself and went into the battle.

31 The king of Aram commanded the thirty-two captains of his chariots, "Do not engage small or great, but the king of Israel alone."

32 When the chariot captains saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "Surely that is the king of Israel." They turned aside to attack him, and Jehoshaphat cried out.

33 But when the chariot captains saw that he was not the king of Israel, they turned back from pursuing him. 34 But a man drew his bow at a venture and struck the king of Israel between the scale armor and the breastplate. He said to his charioteer, "Turn about and carry me out of the camp, for I am wounded."

35 The battle intensified that day, and the king was held upright in his chariot facing the Arameans, while the blood of the wound ran into the floor of the chariot; he died at evening. 36 As the sun was setting, a cry went through the camp: "Every man to his city, and every man to his land!"

37 The king died and was brought to Samaria, where they buried him. 38 They washed the chariot at the pool of Samaria, and the dogs licked up his blood, while the prostitutes bathed, in accordance with the word of the LORD that He had spoken.

39 Now the rest of the acts of Ahab, and all that he did, and the ivory house that he built, and all the cities that he built—are they not recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 40 Ahab slept with his fathers, and his son Ahaziah reigned in his place.

41 In the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel, Jehoshaphat son of Asa began to reign over Judah. 42 Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah, daughter of Shilhi. 43 He walked in all the way of Asa his father and did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of the LORD. Yet the high places were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places.

44 Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel. 45 Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, and his might that he displayed, and his wars—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

46 He also removed from the land the rest of the male shrine prostitutes who had survived from the days of his father Asa. 47 There was no king in Edom; a deputy reigned. 48 Jehoshaphat constructed a fleet of Tarshish ships to fetch gold from Ophir, but they never sailed, for the ships were wrecked at Ezion-geber.

49 Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants sail with your servants in the ships." But Jehoshaphat refused.

50 Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers and was buried with them in the city of David his father; his son Jehoram reigned in his stead. 51 In the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Ahaziah son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned over Israel two years. 52 He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD and walked in the way of his father and mother and in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had made Israel sin. 53 He served Baal and worshiped him, provoking the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger, just as his father had done.
