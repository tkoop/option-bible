Eliphaz the Temanite Answers Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

2“If one should presume to address you, would you grow weary? Yet who could restrain himself from speaking? 3Surely you have instructed multitudes and fortified their faltering hands. 4Your words have sustained those who were stumbling, and you have strengthened knees that were buckling. 5But now adversity comes to you, and you are wearied; it touches you, and you are dismayed. 6Is not your reverence your confidence, and the integrity of your ways your hope?

The Innocent Are Not Destroyed

7“Remember now, who has ever perished while innocent? Or where have the upright been destroyed? 8As I have seen, those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap the same. 9By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of His anger they are consumed. 10The roar of the lion and the voice of the fierce lion are silenced, and the teeth of the young lions are broken. 11The old lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

Eliphaz's Nocturnal Vision

12“Now a word was brought to me stealthily, and my ear caught a whisper of it. 13In disquieting dreams in the night, when deep sleep falls upon men, 14fear and trembling seized me and made all my bones shudder. 15Then a spirit glided past my face, and the hair of my flesh stood on end. 16It stood still, but I could not discern its form; a figure was before my eyes, and I heard a hushed voice:

The Voice Declares Human Frailty

17“‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God, or a man more pure than his Maker? 18If God places no trust in His servants and charges even His angels with error, 19how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundations rest upon dust and who are crushed like a moth! 20They are shattered from morning to evening; without anyone's notice, they perish forever. 21Are not their tent cords pulled up, so that they die devoid of wisdom?’”
