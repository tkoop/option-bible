A Psalm of David

1 I shall sing of steadfast love and justice; to You, the LORD, I shall sing. 2 I shall conduct myself with wisdom along the blameless way; when shall You come to me? I shall walk within my house in the integrity of my heart.

A Vow of Personal Integrity

3 I shall set no base thing before my eyes; I hate the practice of those who turn aside, and it shall not cling to me. 4 A perverse heart shall turn away from me; I shall know nothing of evil.

The Purging of the Wicked

5 Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, him I will destroy; the proud of eye and haughty of heart, I cannot endure. 6 My eyes are upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the blameless way, he shall minister to me. 7 No one who practices deceit shall dwell within my house; no one who speaks falsehood shall stand before my eyes. 8 Morning by morning I will destroy all the wicked of the land, to cut off from the city of the LORD every worker of iniquity.
