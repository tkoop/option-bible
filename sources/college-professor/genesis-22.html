The Trial of Abraham's Faith

1 After these events, God subjected Abraham to a trial. He called out to him, "Abraham!"

Abraham replied, "Here I am."

2 Then He declared, "Take now your son, your only son, whom you love—Isaac—and go to the land of Moriah; offer him there as a burnt offering upon one of the mountains that I will tell you."

The Ascent to Moriah

3 Abraham rose at dawn, saddled his donkey, and took with him two of his young men and Isaac his son. Having split wood for the burnt offering, he set out for the place God had indicated.

4 On the third day, Abraham raised his eyes and perceived the appointed place from afar.

5 Abraham said to his young men, "Remain here with the donkey; the boy and I will go yonder to worship, and we shall return to you."

6 Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and laid it upon Isaac his son; bearing the fire and the knife in his hand, the two advanced together.

7 Isaac addressed his father Abraham, saying, "My father!"

Abraham answered, "Here I am, my son."

Isaac continued, "Here are the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for the burnt offering?"

8 Abraham replied, "God Himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son." Thus the two continued on together.

9 Upon arriving at the place God had designated, Abraham erected an altar there, arranged the wood, bound his son Isaac, and placed him upon the altar, above the wood.

10 Abraham extended his hand and seized the knife to slay his son.

The Angelic Intervention and the Renewed Covenant

11 The angel of the LORD called to him from heaven, "Abraham, Abraham!"

Abraham answered, "Here I am."

12 The angel declared, "Do not raise your hand against the boy, nor do him any harm; for now I know that you fear God, seeing that you have not withheld your son, your only son, from Me."

13 Abraham lifted his eyes and beheld a ram behind him, caught by its horns in a thicket. He went and took the ram and offered it up as a burnt offering in lieu of his son.

14 Abraham named that place the LORD Will Provide; hence it is said to this day, "On the mountain of the LORD it will be provided."

15 The angel of the LORD called to Abraham from heaven a second time.

16 He said, "By Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that because you have done this and have not withheld your son, your only son, 17 I will surely bless you and multiply your offspring like the stars of heaven and like the sand upon the seashore; your offspring shall possess the gates of their enemies. 18 Through your offspring all the nations of the earth shall be blessed, because you have obeyed My voice."

19 Abraham returned to his young men, and they journeyed together to Beersheba; Abraham took up residence in Beersheba.

The Lineage of Nahor

20 After these things, Abraham was informed, "Milcah also has borne sons to your brother Nahor: 21 Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram, 22 Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel." 23 Bethuel fathered Rebekah. These eight Milcah bore to Nahor, Abraham's brother.

24 His concubine, Reumah by name, likewise bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.
