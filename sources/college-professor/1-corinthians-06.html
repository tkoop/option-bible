The Adjudication of Grievances among the Saints

1 If any among you, having a dispute with another, dares to seek adjudication before the unrighteous rather than before the saints, 2 or do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you unworthy to preside over the most trivial tribunals? 3 Do you not know that we are to judge angels—how much more, then, the mundane affairs of this life?

4 Therefore, if you have disputes concerning ordinary life, appoint as judges those who are held in least esteem within the church. 5 I say this to your shame: is there truly no one among you wise enough to arbitrate between brothers? 6 Instead, one brother brings suit against another, and that before unbelievers.

7 The very fact that you have lawsuits among you already constitutes a defeat. Why not rather suffer wrong? Why not rather be defrauded? 8 On the contrary, you yourselves perpetrate fraud and injustice, and even against your own brothers.

The Kingdom of God and the Transformation of the Assembly

9 Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor the effeminate, nor those who practice homosexuality, 10 nor thieves, nor the avaricious, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God. 11 And such were some of you. But you were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by the Spirit of our God.

Christian Liberty and the Sanctity of the Body

12 “All things are permissible for me,” but not all things are expedient; “all things are permissible for me,” but I will not be brought under the power of any. 13 Food is for the stomach and the stomach for food, but God will bring both to nothing; and the body is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body. 14 And God raised the Lord, and He will also raise us by His power.

15 Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? Never! 16 Or do you not know that he who is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For it says, “The two will become one flesh.” 17 But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit with Him.

18 Flee sexual immorality. Every other sin that a person commits is outside the body, but the one who is sexually immoral sins against his own body. 19 Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God, and that you are not your own? 20 For you were bought at a price; therefore glorify God in your body.
