A Solemn Charge in the Presence of God and Christ

1 I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is destined to judge the living and the dead, and in view of His appearing and His kingdom: 2 proclaim the word; stand ready whether the occasion is opportune or inopportune; expose error, censure, and exhort, exercising complete forbearance and doctrinal instruction. 3 For a season will arrive in which they will no longer tolerate wholesome teaching; rather, their ears, craving pleasing novelties, will prompt them to amass for themselves instructors consonant with their own cravings. 4 They will avert their ears from the truth and be diverted into fables. 5 But as for you, maintain sober-mindedness in every circumstance, endure affliction, perform the labor of an evangelist, and discharge your ministry in full.

Paul’s Imminent Departure and the Crown of Righteousness

6 For I am already being poured out as a libation, and the season of my dissolution is at hand. 7 I have waged the noble contest, completed the course, and preserved the faith. 8 Consequently, the crown of righteousness is reserved for me, which the Lord—the righteous Judge—will bestow upon me in that day, and not solely upon me but upon all who have cherished His appearing.

Final Instructions, Personal Remarks, and Greetings

9 Strive to come to me without delay. 10 For Demas, enamored of the present age, has abandoned me and departed for Thessalonica; Crescens has gone to Galatia; and Titus to Dalmatia. 11 Luke alone remains with me. Retrieve Mark and bring him along with you, for he proves serviceable to me in ministry. 12 Tychicus I have dispatched to Ephesus. 13 When you arrive, bring the cloak I left with Carpus in Troas, together with the books—above all, the parchments.

14 Alexander the coppersmith inflicted considerable injury upon me; the Lord will recompense him commensurate with his works. 15 You likewise must be on guard against him, since he vehemently resisted our message. 16 At my initial defense, no one stood by me; rather, all forsook me. May it not be reckoned against them. 17 Yet the Lord stood beside me and empowered me, so that through me the proclamation might be brought to full fruition and all the nations might hear it; and I was delivered from the lion's mouth. 18 The Lord will deliver me from every malevolent work and will usher me safely into His heavenly kingdom; to Him belongs glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.

19 Convey my greetings to Prisca and Aquila, and to the household of Onesiphorus. 20 Erastus stayed behind in Corinth, while I left Trophimus ailing in Miletus. 21 Strive to arrive before winter. Eubulus sends you greetings, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brethren. 22 The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.
