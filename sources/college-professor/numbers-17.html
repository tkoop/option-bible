The Divine Ordinance of the Rods

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and take from them twelve rods, one rod for each ancestral household, from all their chieftains according to their fathers’ houses; let every man inscribe his name upon his rod. 3 Inscribe Aaron’s name upon the rod of Levi, for one rod is to represent the head of their ancestral house. 4 You shall deposit them in the tent of meeting before the testimony, the place where I shall meet with you. 5 The rod of the man whom I select shall blossom, and I shall quell, before Me, the Israelites’ grumblings, by which they murmur against you."

The Miraculous Budding of Aaron’s Rod

6 Moses conveyed this to the Israelites, and their chieftains furnished him with rods—one for each chieftain, according to their ancestral houses—twelve rods in total; Aaron’s rod lay among them. 7 Moses deposited the rods before the LORD in the tent of the testimony. 8 On the morrow, Moses entered the tent of the testimony, and behold, Aaron’s rod—pertaining to the Levitical house—had sprouted, put forth buds, blossomed into flowers, and produced almonds. 9 Moses brought forth all the rods from before the LORD to the Israelites; they inspected them, and each man reclaimed his own rod.

The Rod Preserved as a Sign

10 The LORD said to Moses, "Return Aaron’s rod before the testimony, that it be preserved as a sign against the rebellious; and their grumblings shall be removed from before Me, lest they die." 11 Moses complied; exactly as the LORD had commanded him, so he acted.

Israel’s Terror Before the Tabernacle

12 The Israelites addressed Moses, saying, "Behold, we are perishing; we are ruined—all of us are ruined. 13 Everyone who approaches, who draws near to the tabernacle of the LORD, shall die. Will death consume us all?"
