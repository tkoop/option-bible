A Prayer for Deliverance from Treacherous Adversaries

1 For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments—a Maskil of David—composed when the Ziphites approached Saul and declared, “Is David not concealed among us?” Deliver me, O God, by Your name, and vindicate me by Your might.

2 Hear my prayer, O God; attend to the utterances of my mouth. 3 For strangers have risen against me, and ruthless men seek my life, inasmuch as they have no regard for God. Selah.

4 Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul. 5 He will repay my enemies with evil; in Your faithfulness, bring them to ruin.

6 I will freely sacrifice to You; I will praise Your name, O the LORD, for it is good. 7 For He has delivered me from every distress, and my eyes have looked in triumph upon my foes.
