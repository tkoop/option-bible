The Prophet's Warning and the Officials' Demand

1 Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur son of Malchijah hearkened to the words Jeremiah was proclaiming to the entire populace: 2 "Thus says the LORD: Whoever persists in dwelling within this city shall meet death by sword, famine, and pestilence; whereas whoever surrenders to the Chaldeans shall live, and his life shall be secured to him as spoil, so that he may continue to live. 3 Thus says the LORD: This city shall assuredly be delivered into the hand of the Babylonian king's martial host, and he shall subjugate it."

4 Then the officials said to the king, "This man merits execution, for he is enervating the resolve of the soldiers who remain in this city, as well as of the entire populace, by promulgating such words to them; indeed, this man does not seek the welfare of this people but their ruin."

5 King Zedekiah answered, "Behold, he is in your hands; for the king is powerless to resist you."

Imprisonment in the Cistern

6 So they seized Jeremiah and hurled him into the cistern of Malchiah, the king's son, situated in the courtyard of the guard; and they lowered Jeremiah with ropes. Now the cistern contained no water, only mire, and Jeremiah sank into the mire.

Ebed-Melech's Intercession and Rescue

7 Now Ebed-melech the Cushite, a eunuch in the royal palace, heard that they had consigned Jeremiah to the cistern. While the king was seated at the Gate of Benjamin, 8 Ebed-melech emerged from the palace and addressed the king, saying, 9 "My lord the king, these men have perpetrated wickedness in all they have done to Jeremiah the prophet, inasmuch as they have cast him into the cistern; and he will perish there of hunger, for there is no longer any bread in the city."

10 Thereupon the king commanded Ebed-melech the Cushite, "Take thirty men from here and extricate Jeremiah the prophet from the cistern before he expires."

11 So Ebed-melech took the men with him and went to the royal palace, to a place beneath the treasury; and from there he took tattered rags and worn-out garments and lowered them by ropes into the cistern to Jeremiah. 12 Then Ebed-melech the Cushite cried out to Jeremiah, "Place these worn-out rags and garments beneath your armpits, under the ropes." And Jeremiah complied.

13 So they hoisted Jeremiah by the ropes and lifted him out of the cistern; and Jeremiah remained in the courtyard of the guard.

Zedekiah's Confidential Consultation

14 Then King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah the prophet and had him brought to the third entrance of the house of the LORD. And the king said to Jeremiah, "I am about to interrogate you concerning a matter; do not withhold anything from me."

15 Jeremiah replied, "If I tell you, will you not assuredly put me to death? And if I proffer counsel to you, you will not heed me."

16 But King Zedekiah swore secretly to Jeremiah, "As surely as the LORD lives, who granted us this life, I will not put you to death, nor will I surrender you into the hand of these men who seek your life."

17 Then Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Hosts, the God of Israel: If you will indeed surrender to the officers of the king of Babylon, your life shall be spared, this city shall not be consumed by fire, and you and your household shall live. 18 But if you do not surrender to the officers of the king of Babylon, this city shall be delivered into the hand of the Chaldeans, and they shall burn it with fire, and you yourself shall not escape from their grasp."

19 King Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "I am apprehensive of the Jews who have defected to the Chaldeans, lest they deliver me into their hand and abuse me."

20 Jeremiah replied, "They will not deliver you over. Obey the voice of the LORD in what I speak to you, that it may be well with you and your life be spared. 21 But if you refuse to go forth, this is the word that the LORD has revealed to me:

"22 All the women who remain in the house of the king of Judah shall be brought out to the officers of the king of Babylon, and they shall say, 'They beguiled you and prevailed over you, those confidants of yours. Your feet sank in the mire, and they recoiled.' 23 All your wives and children shall be brought out to the Chaldeans, and you yourself shall not escape from their hand, but shall be seized by the king of Babylon, and this city shall be burned with fire."

24 Then Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "Let no one know of this colloquy, or you will die.

25 And if the officials hear that I have conversed with you and come to you and say, 'What did you say to the king, and what did the king say to you? Do not withhold it from us, or we will put you to death,' 26 then you shall say to them, 'I was presenting my petition before the king, that he would not return me to the house of Jonathan to die there.'"

27 When all the officials came to Jeremiah and interrogated him, he reported to them in accordance with all these words that the king had commanded; and they fell silent, because the matter had not been overheard.

28 And Jeremiah remained in the courtyard of the guard until the day Jerusalem was captured.
