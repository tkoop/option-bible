David's Supplication from the Cave

A maskil of David, when he was in the cave—a prayer.

1 I cry aloud to the LORD; with my voice I make supplication to the LORD. 2 I pour out my complaint in His presence; I declare my distress before Him.

3 When my spirit faints within me, You know my path; in the way I walk they have concealed a snare for me. 4 Look to my right and see—there is no one who recognizes me; refuge has failed me; no one inquires after my life.

5 I have cried out to You, O the LORD; I have said that You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living. 6 Give ear to my supplication, for I am brought very low; deliver me from my persecutors, for they are stronger than I.

7 Bring my life out of confinement, that I may give thanks to Your name; the righteous will surround me, for You will deal bountifully with me.
