The LORD’s Instructions to Moses After the Death of Aaron’s Sons

1 The LORD spoke to Moses after the death of Aaron’s two sons, who had drawn near to the LORD and perished. 2 The LORD said to Moses, “Tell your brother Aaron that he must not enter the Most Holy Place behind the veil at any time, before the mercy seat that is upon the ark, lest he die; for I will appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat.

The Offerings and the Lots for the Two Goats

“3 Aaron shall enter the Most Holy Place with a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering. 4 He shall be vested in the holy linen tunic, with linen undergarments upon his flesh; he shall gird himself with a linen sash and wrap a linen turban about his head. These are the holy garments. He shall bathe his body in water and then put them on. 5 From the congregation of the Israelites he shall take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering. 6 Aaron shall present the bull of the sin offering that is for himself and make atonement for himself and for his household. 7 He shall take the two goats and set them before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 8 Aaron shall cast lots over the two goats—one lot for the LORD and one lot for Azazel, the scapegoat. 9 Aaron shall bring the goat upon which the lot for the LORD fell and offer it as a sin offering. 10 But the goat upon which the lot for Azazel fell shall be presented alive before the LORD, to make atonement over it, that it may be sent away into the wilderness to Azazel.

The Atonement Within the Sanctuary

“11 Aaron shall bring the bull of the sin offering that is for himself and shall make atonement for himself and for his household, and he shall slaughter the bull of the sin offering that is for himself. 12 He shall take a censer full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD, with his hands full of finely ground sweet incense, and bring them within the veil. 13 He shall put the incense upon the fire before the LORD, so that the cloud of incense covers the mercy seat that is over the testimony, lest he die. 14 He shall take some of the blood of the bull and sprinkle it with his finger on the east side of the mercy seat; before the mercy seat he shall sprinkle some of the blood with his finger seven times. 15 Then he shall slaughter the goat of the sin offering that is for the people and bring its blood within the veil, and he shall do with its blood as he did with the blood of the bull, sprinkling it upon the mercy seat and before the mercy seat. 16 Thus he shall make atonement for the sanctuary from the uncleannesses of the Israelites and from their transgressions, for all their sins; and so shall he do for the tent of meeting, which dwells among them in the midst of their uncleanness. 17 No person shall be in the tent of meeting when he enters to make atonement in the sanctuary until he comes out, having made atonement for himself, for his household, and for all the assembly of Israel. 18 Then he shall go out to the altar that is before the LORD and make atonement for it, taking some of the blood of the bull and some of the blood of the goat, and putting it on the horns of the altar all around. 19 He shall sprinkle some of the blood upon it with his finger seven times, and cleanse it and consecrate it from the uncleannesses of the Israelites.

The Scapegoat and the Everlasting Statute of Atonement

“20 When he has finished making atonement for the sanctuary, the tent of meeting, and the altar, he shall bring the live goat. 21 Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat and confess over it all the iniquities of the Israelites and all their transgressions, even all their sins, putting them upon the head of the goat; and he shall send it away into the wilderness by the hand of a man appointed for the task. 22 The goat shall bear upon itself all their iniquities to a desolate, uninhabited region; and he shall release the goat in the wilderness. 23 Then Aaron shall come into the tent of meeting and remove the linen garments that he put on when he went into the sanctuary, and he shall leave them there. 24 He shall bathe his body in water in a holy place and put on his garments, and he shall come out and offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for the people. 25 The fat of the sin offering he shall burn upon the altar. 26 He who releases the goat for Azazel shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and afterward he may come into the camp. 27 The bull of the sin offering and the goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the sanctuary, shall be carried outside the camp; their hides, their flesh, and their offal shall be burned with fire. 28 He who burns them shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and afterward he may come into the camp.

“29 This shall be a statute forever for you: in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, you shall deny yourselves and do no work at all, whether the native-born or the foreigner sojourning among you. 30 For on this day atonement shall be made for you to cleanse you; before the LORD you shall be clean from all your sins. 31 It is a sabbath of solemn rest for you, and you shall deny yourselves; it is a statute forever. 32 The priest who is anointed and consecrated to minister in his father’s place shall make atonement, wearing the holy linen garments. 33 He shall make atonement for the holy sanctuary, for the tent of meeting, for the altar, for the priests, and for all the people of the assembly.”

34 “This shall be an everlasting statute for you, to make atonement for the Israelites once in the year for all their sins.” And Moses did as the LORD commanded him.
