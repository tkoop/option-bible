The Earth and All That Fills It Belong to the LORD

1 A psalm of David. The earth is the LORD’s, together with all that fills it; the world and all who dwell within it are His. 2 For He founded it upon the seas and established it upon the rivers.

Those Qualified to Stand in the LORD’s Holy Place

3 Who shall ascend the mountain of the LORD, and who shall stand in His holy place? 4 The one whose hands are clean and whose heart is pure, who has not devoted his soul to vanity, and who has not sworn deceitfully. 5 He shall receive blessing from the LORD and righteousness from the God of his salvation. 6 Such is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek Your face, O Jacob. Selah.

The King of Glory’s Triumphant Entrance

7 Lift up your heads, O gates, and be lifted up, O everlasting doors, that the King of Glory may come in. 8 Who is this King of Glory? The LORD, strong and mighty; the LORD, mighty in battle. 9 Lift up your heads, O gates; lift them up, O everlasting doors, that the King of Glory may come in. 10 Who is this King of Glory? The LORD of hosts—He is the King of Glory. Selah.
