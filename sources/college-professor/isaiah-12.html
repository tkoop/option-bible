Thanksgiving for the LORD's Mercy and Salvation

1 In that day you will say, “O the LORD, I will give thanks to You, for though You were angry with me, Your anger has turned away, and You comfort me. 2 Surely God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid, for Yah the LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation.”

3 With joy you will draw water from the springs of salvation.

Proclaiming the LORD's Exalted Name among the Nations

4 In that day you will say, “Give thanks to the the LORD, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations; proclaim that His name is exalted.”

5 Sing to the the LORD, for He has done glorious things; let this be known in all the earth.

Zion's Rejoicing in the Holy One of Israel

6 Cry out and shout, O inhabitant of Zion, for great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel.
