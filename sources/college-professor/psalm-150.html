A Final Doxology of Universal Praise

1 Praise the LORD! Praise God in His sanctuary; praise Him in the firmament of His power. 2 Praise Him for His mighty deeds; praise Him according to His surpassing greatness.

3 Praise Him with the blast of the shofar; praise Him with lute and harp. 4 Praise Him with timbrel and dance; praise Him with strings and flute.

5 Praise Him with clashing cymbals; praise Him with resounding cymbals. 6 Let every being that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
