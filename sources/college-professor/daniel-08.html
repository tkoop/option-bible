Daniel’s Vision by the Ulai

1 In the third year of the reign of King Belshazzar, a vision appeared to me—Daniel—after the one that had appeared to me at the first. 2 In the vision I was in Susa the citadel, in the province of Elam, and I was standing by the Ulai canal.

3 I lifted my eyes and looked, and behold, a ram was standing before the canal, and it had two horns; the horns were high, but one was higher than the other, and the higher one came up last. 4 I saw the ram goring westward, northward, and southward; no beast could withstand it, and none could deliver any from its hand; it acted according to its will and magnified itself.

5 As I was considering this, behold, a male goat came from the west, sweeping across the face of the whole earth without touching the ground; and the goat had a conspicuous horn between its eyes. 6 It came to the ram with the two horns, which I had seen standing before the canal, and it charged at it with the fury of its might. 7 I saw it reach the ram; enraged against it, it struck the ram and shattered its two horns, and the ram had no strength to stand before it; it hurled the ram to the ground and trampled it, and there was no one to rescue the ram from its power.

8 The male goat grew exceedingly great; but when it had become strong, the great horn was broken, and in its place four conspicuous horns sprang up toward the four winds of heaven.

The Little Horn’s Assault on the Sanctuary

9 From one of them came forth a little horn, which grew exceedingly toward the south, toward the east, and toward the Beautiful Land. 10 It grew up to the host of heaven, threw down some of the host and some of the stars to the earth, and trampled them. 11 It magnified itself even to the Prince of the host; it took away the daily sacrifice from Him, and His sanctuary was thrown down. 12 Because of transgression, a host was given over to it together with the daily sacrifice; it cast truth to the ground and prospered in all that it did.

13 I heard a holy one speaking, and another holy one said to the one who was speaking, “How long will the vision last—the daily sacrifice, the desolating transgression, and the surrender of both the sanctuary and the host to be trampled underfoot?”

14 He said to me, “For two thousand three hundred evenings and mornings; then the sanctuary shall be restored to its rightful state.”

Gabriel Interprets the Vision

15 When I, Daniel, had seen the vision, I sought to understand it; and behold, there stood before me one whose appearance was like that of a man. 16 I heard a human voice calling across the Ulai, “Gabriel, make this man understand the vision.”

17 He came near where I stood, and when he came I was terrified and fell on my face. He said to me, “Understand, O son of man, that the vision concerns the time of the end.” 18 While he was speaking with me, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground; but he touched me and set me on my feet.

19 He said, “Behold, I will make known to you what is to happen at the end of the wrath, for it concerns the appointed time of the end. 20 The ram that you saw with its two horns represents the kings of Media and Persia. 21 The shaggy goat is the king of Greece, and the great horn between its eyes is the first king. 22 As for the horn that was broken, in whose place four arose, four kingdoms shall arise from that nation, but not with his power.

23 “In the latter part of their kingdom, when the transgressors have reached their full measure, a king of fierce countenance, adept in intrigue, shall arise. 24 His power shall be mighty, though not by his own strength; he shall work fearful destruction, prosper in all that he does, destroy mighty men, and afflict the holy people. 25 By his cunning he shall make deceit prosper under his control; in his heart he shall magnify himself; while men are at ease he shall destroy many; and he shall rise up against the Prince of princes, but he shall be broken by no human hand. 26 The vision of the evenings and mornings that has been declared is true; but seal up the vision, for it concerns days far in the future.”

27 And I, Daniel, was overcome and lay sick for several days; then I rose and attended to the king’s business, but I was appalled by the vision, and no one could explain it.
