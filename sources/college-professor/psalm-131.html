A Psalm of Humble Trust and Quiet Hope

1 My heart, O the LORD, is not haughty; my eyes are not raised on high; neither have I busied myself in matters too great or too wondrous for me. 2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul, as a weaned child upon his mother; my soul is as a weaned child. 3 O Israel, hope in the LORD from this time forth and forevermore.
