A Davidic Psalm of Evening Confidence

1 For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments—a Psalm of David. Answer me when I call, O God of my righteousness; You have granted me relief in adversity. Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

2 How long, O sons of men, will you transmute my honor into disgrace? How long will you cleave to emptiness and pursue falsehood? Selah. 3 But know that the LORD has set apart the godly for Himself; the LORD hears when I call to Him. 4 Tremble, yet sin not; commune within your heart upon your bed, and be silent. Selah. 5 Offer sacrifices of righteousness, and trust in the LORD.

6 Many are saying, "Who will show us good? Let the light of Your face shine upon us, O the LORD."

7 You have put gladness in my heart, more than in the season when their grain and new wine abound. 8 In peace I will both lie down and sleep, for You alone, O the LORD, make me dwell in safety.
