Job’s Anguish and Plea for Release

1 Then Job answered and said: 2 “Oh, that my vexation were weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances together! 3 For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore my words have been rash. 4 For the arrows of the Almighty are in me; my spirit drinks their venom; the terrors of God are arrayed against me. 5 Does the wild donkey bray over grass, or the ox low over its fodder? 6 Can tasteless food be eaten without salt, or is there flavor in the white of an egg? 7 My soul refuses to touch them; they are as loathsome food to me.

8 “Oh, that my request might come to pass, and that God would grant my desire! 9 That it would please God to crush me, that He would loose His hand and cut me off! 10 Then I would still have comfort; I would exult in pain unsparing, for I have not denied the words of the Holy One. 11 What is my strength, that I should wait? What is my end, that I should prolong my life? 12 Is my strength the strength of stones, or is my flesh bronze? 13 Is there no help within me, and has sound wisdom been driven from me?

The Faithlessness of Friends Likened to Vanishing Brooks

14 “He who withholds kindness from a friend forsakes the fear of the Almighty. 15 My brothers have dealt treacherously like a torrent, like the torrents of wadis that pass away. 16 They are darkened because of ice; snow is hidden upon them. 17 When they are scorched, they vanish; when it is hot, they disappear from their place. 18 The caravans turn aside from their course; they go up into the waste and perish. 19 The caravans of Tema looked; the travelers of Sheba hoped in them. 20 They were ashamed because they trusted; they came there and were confounded. 21 For now you are nothing; you see a dreadful sight and are afraid.

Job’s Rebuttal and Appeal for Honest Judgment

22 “Did I say, ‘Give me something,’ or, ‘From your wealth offer a bribe on my behalf’? 23 Or, ‘Deliver me from the hand of the adversary,’ or, ‘Redeem me from the hand of the ruthless’? 24 Teach me, and I will be silent; show me how I have gone astray. 25 How forceful are upright words! But what does your reproof prove? 26 Do you intend to reprove mere words, the speeches of a despairing man that are but wind? 27 You would even gamble away an orphan and barter over your friend. 28 But now be pleased to look at me; I will not lie to your face. 29 Reconsider, I pray; let there be no injustice; reconsider, for my righteousness is still at stake. 30 Is there injustice on my tongue? Can my palate not discern calamity?”
