Paul’s Proclamation in Corinth

1 When I came to you, brothers, I did not come proclaiming to you the testimony of God with any rhetorical superiority or wisdom, 2 for I had resolved to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified. 3 I was present with you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling, 4 and my discourse and my proclamation were not in persuasive words of wisdom, but in a demonstration of the Spirit and of power, 5 so that your faith might not rest upon human wisdom but upon the power of God.

The Hidden Wisdom of God

6 Yet among the mature we do speak wisdom—not the wisdom of this age, nor that of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing— 7 but we speak, in a mystery, the hidden wisdom of God, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory. 8 None of the rulers of this age has known it; for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. 9 But, as it is written, “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor has entered into the heart of man—what God has prepared for those who love Him.”

Revelation Through the Spirit

10 For to us God has revealed them through the Spirit, for the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God. 11 For who among men knows the things of a man except the spirit of that man which is in him? Even so no one knows the things of God except the Spirit of God. 12 Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit that is from God, that we might know the things freely given to us by God. 13 And the things we speak we do not utter in words taught by human wisdom, but in words taught by the Spirit, comparing spiritual realities with spiritual discernment.

Spiritual Discernment

14 But the natural man does not accept the things of the Spirit of God, for to him they are folly; neither can he know them, because they are spiritually appraised. 15 But the spiritual person appraises all things, while he himself is appraised by no one. 16 For “Who has known the mind of the Lord, that he should instruct Him?” But we have the mind of Christ.
