The Boundaries of the Land of Canaan

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

2 "Command the Israelites and say to them, 'When you are entering the land of Canaan, this is the land that shall fall to you as an inheritance—the land of Canaan according to its borders.' 3 Your southern side shall be from the wilderness of Zin along the border of Edom, and your southern boundary shall be from the end of the Salt Sea eastward.

4 "Your border shall turn from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim and pass on to Zin, and its exits shall be from the south to Kadesh-barnea; and it shall go out to Hazar-addar and pass on to Azmon. 5 Then the border shall turn from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and its exits shall be to the sea. 6 For the western border, you shall have the Great Sea and its coast; this shall be your western border.

7 "This shall be your northern border: from the Great Sea you shall mark out for yourselves Mount Hor. 8 From Mount Hor you shall mark out to the entrance to Hamath, and the exits of the border shall be to Zedad. 9 Then the border shall go out to Ziphron, and its exits shall be at Hazar-enan. This shall be your northern border.

10 "You shall mark out for yourselves your eastern border from Hazar-enan to Shepham. 11 The border shall descend from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain, and the border shall descend and reach the eastern shore of the Sea of Chinnereth eastward. 12 The border shall descend to the Jordan, and its exits shall be at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land according to its borders all around."

The Inheritance Allotted by Lot

13 Moses commanded the Israelites, saying, "This is the land that you shall inherit by lot, which the LORD has commanded to give to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe. 14 For the tribe of the children of Reuben, according to their ancestral houses, and the tribe of the children of Gad, according to their ancestral houses, have received their inheritance; and the half-tribe of Manasseh has received its inheritance. 15 The two and a half tribes have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho, eastward, toward the sunrise."

Leaders Appointed to Divide the Land

16 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

17 "These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun. 18 You shall take one leader from each tribe to apportion the land. 19 These are the names of the men: for the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh; 20 for the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud; 21 for the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon; 22 for the tribe of the sons of Dan, Bukki son of Jogli; 23 for the sons of Joseph—for the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, Hanniel son of Ephod, 24 and for the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan; 25 for the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, Elizaphan son of Parnach; 26 for the tribe of the sons of Issachar, Paltiel son of Azzan; 27 for the tribe of the sons of Asher, Ahihud son of Shelomi; 28 and for the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, Pedahel son of Ammihud. 29 These are the ones whom the LORD has commanded to apportion the inheritance to the Israelites in the land of Canaan."
