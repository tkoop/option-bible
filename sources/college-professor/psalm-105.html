A Summons to Celebrate the LORD's Covenant Faithfulness

1 Give thanks to the LORD, invoke His name, and proclaim His mighty deeds among the peoples. 2 Sing to Him, chant psalms to Him, and meditate upon all His wondrous works. 3 Exult in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

4 Seek the LORD and His strength; pursue His presence without ceasing. 5 Remember the marvels He has wrought, His portents and the judgments uttered by His mouth, 6 O offspring of Abraham His servant, children of Jacob His chosen. 7 He is the LORD our God; His judgments prevail throughout the whole earth.

The Everlasting Covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob

8 He remembers His covenant forever, the promise He ordained for a thousand generations— 9 the covenant He struck with Abraham and the oath He swore to Isaac. 10 He ratified it for Jacob as an ordinance, for Israel as a covenant in perpetuity, 11 saying, "I will give you the land of Canaan as the measured allotment of your inheritance."

12 When they were but a handful, few in number and sojourners within it, 13 they wandered from nation to nation, from one realm to another people. 14 He allowed no mortal to oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf.

15 "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do My prophets no harm."

Joseph's Exaltation and Israel's Sojourn in Egypt

16 He summoned famine upon the land and shattered every staff of bread. 17 He sent a man before them—Joseph—sold into slavery. 18 They afflicted his feet with shackles; his soul was confined in iron. 19 Until the appointed time, when his word came to pass, the word of the LORD tested him. 20 The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free. 21 He appointed him master of his household and steward of all his possessions, 22 to bind his princes at his discretion and to impart wisdom to his elders.

23 Then Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. 24 The LORD made His people exceedingly fruitful and stronger than their adversaries. 25 He turned their hearts to hate His people, to deal treacherously with His servants.

The Plagues, the Exodus, and Wilderness Provision

26 He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron whom He had chosen. 27 They performed His signs among them and wonders in the land of Ham. 28 He sent darkness and made it dark, and they did not defy His words. 29 He turned their waters into blood and slew their fish. 30 Their land teemed with frogs, even in the royal chambers. 31 He spoke, and swarms of flies came, and gnats throughout all their territory. 32 He gave them hail for rain and blazing fire upon their land. 33 He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their borders. 34 He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts beyond number— 35 and they devoured every herb in their land and consumed the fruit of their soil.

36 He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their strength. 37 He brought His people out with silver and gold, and none among His tribes stumbled. 38 Egypt rejoiced at their departure, for terror of them had fallen upon it. 39 He spread a cloud for a covering and fire to illumine the night. 40 They asked, and He brought quail and satisfied them with bread from heaven. 41 He opened the rock, and waters gushed forth; they ran in the arid wastes like a river. 42 For He remembered His holy promise to Abraham His servant.

43 He brought out His people with gladness, His chosen with shouts of joy. 44 He gave them the lands of the nations, and they inherited the toil of the peoples, 45 that they might keep His statutes and observe His laws. Praise the LORD!
