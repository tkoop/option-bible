An Invitation to the Thirsty and the Destitute

1 Behold, all you who thirst—come to the waters; and you who possess no silver, come, buy and eat! Come, buy wine and milk without silver and without price. 2 Why do you expend silver upon that which is not bread, and your toil upon that which cannot satisfy? Listen attentively to me, and eat what is good, and let your soul delight in rich abundance.

The Everlasting Covenant and the Vindication of the Servant

3 Incline your ear and come to me; listen, that your soul may live. I will establish with you an everlasting covenant—the faithful lovingkindness promised to David. 4 See, I have appointed him a witness to the nations, a ruler and commander of peoples. 5 Behold, you will summon a nation you do not know, and nations that do not know you will hasten to you, because of the the LORD your God and the Holy One of Israel, for He has glorified you.

Repentance, Pardon, and the Incomparability of Divine Thought

6 Seek the the LORD while He may be found; call upon Him while He is near. 7 Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts; let him return to the the LORD, and He will have mercy on him, and to our God, for He will abundantly pardon. 8 “For My thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways My ways,” declares the the LORD.

The Efficacy of the Divine Word and the Joy of Restoration

9 For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are My ways higher than your ways and My thoughts than your thoughts. 10 For as the rain and snow descend from heaven and do not return thither, but water the earth and make it bring forth and bud, yielding seed to the sower and bread to the eater— 11 so shall My word be that proceeds from My mouth: it shall not return to Me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in that for which I sent it. 12 For you shall go forth with joy and be led forth in peace; the mountains and hills shall break forth into singing before you, and all the trees of the field shall clap their hands. 13 Instead of the thorn the cypress shall rise, and instead of the brier the myrtle shall rise; and it shall be to the the LORD for a name, for an everlasting sign that shall not be cut off.
