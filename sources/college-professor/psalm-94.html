A Psalm Invoking Divine Vengeance and Affirming God's Righteous Judgment

1 The LORD, God of vengeance; God of vengeance, shine forth. 2 Lift Yourself up, Judge of the earth; repay to the proud their recompense. 3 How long, O the LORD, will the wicked? How long will the wicked exult? 4 They pour forth insolent speech; all workers of iniquity speak; they boast.

5 They crush Your people, O the LORD; they afflict Your inheritance. 6 They slay the widow and the sojourner; they murder orphans. 7 And they say, "The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob does not regard."

8 Understand, you brutish among the people; and you fools, when will you be wise? 9 He who planted the ear, does He not hear? He who formed the eye, does He not see? 10 He who disciplines the nations, does He not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge? 11 The LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are vanity.

12 Blessed is the man whom You, the LORD, discipline, whom You teach from Your law, 13 to give him quiet from days of evil, until a pit is dug for the wicked. 14 For the LORD will not abandon His people; nor will He forsake His inheritance. 15 For judgment will return to righteousness; and all the upright in heart will follow.

16 Who will rise for me against evildoers? Who will stand with me against workers of iniquity? 17 If the LORD had not been my help, my soul would soon have dwelt in silence. 18 If I said, "My foot has slipped," Your mercy, O the LORD, supported me. 19 In the multitude of my thoughts within me, Your comforts delight my soul.

20 Can a throne of destruction be allied with You, one that frames mischief by statute? 21 They band together against the soul of the righteous; they condemn innocent blood. 22 But the LORD has been my stronghold; and my God the rock of my refuge. 23 And He will turn their iniquity upon them; and in their evil the LORD our God will cut them off.
