Stewards of God’s Mysteries

1 Let every person, therefore, regard us as servants of Christ and as stewards of the mysteries of God. 2 Moreover, it is required of stewards that one be found faithful.

3 But to me it is a matter of the least consequence that I should be examined by you or by any human tribunal; indeed, I do not even examine myself. 4 For I am conscious of nothing against myself, yet on that account I am not justified; rather, the Lord is the One who examines me. 5 Therefore judge nothing before the appointed time, until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the things hidden in darkness and disclose the purposes of the hearts; and then each person will receive praise from God.

Against Partisan Boasting

6 Now these things, brothers, I have applied to myself and to Apollos for your sake, that in us you may learn the lesson, “Do not go beyond what is written,” so that none of you may be inflated with pride on behalf of one against another. 7 For who makes you superior? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as though you had not?

8 Already you are sated; already you have grown wealthy; apart from us you have already begun to reign—and would that you truly did reign, so that we also might reign with you.

The Apostles’ Present Humiliation

9 For it seems to me that God has exhibited us apostles last of all, as men condemned to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, to angels, and to human beings. 10 We are fools for Christ’s sake, but you are prudent in Christ; we are weak, but you are strong; you are held in honor, but we are dishonored.

11 To the present hour we both hunger and thirst, we are poorly clothed, we are buffeted, and we have no settled home. 12 We toil, working with our own hands; when reviled, we bless; when persecuted, we endure. 13 When slandered, we entreat; we have become, and remain, as the refuse of the world—the scum of all things.

Paternal Counsel and Impending Visit

14 I do not write these things to shame you, but to admonish you as my beloved children. 15 For though you may have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the gospel. 16 I urge you, therefore, to be imitators of me.

17 For this reason I have sent Timothy to you, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord; he will remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, just as I teach everywhere in every church. 18 Now some have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.

19 But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will ascertain not the speech of those who are puffed up but their power. 20 For the kingdom of God does not consist in speech but in power. 21 What do you wish? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love and a spirit of gentleness?
