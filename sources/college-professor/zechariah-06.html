The Vision of the Four Chariots

1 I turned again, lifted my eyes, and saw: behold, four chariots were issuing forth from between two mountains, and those mountains were mountains of bronze. 2 The first chariot was drawn by red horses, the second by black horses; 3 the third by white horses, and the fourth by dappled, vigorous horses. 4 And I answered and said to the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”

5 And the angel answered and said to me, “These are the four spirits of heaven, going out from their station before the Lord of all the earth. 6 The black horses in it go out to the land of the north, and the white go out after them, and the dappled go out to the land of the south. 7 And the vigorous horses went out and sought to go and patrol the earth; and he said, ‘Go, patrol the earth.’ So they patrolled the earth. 8 And he cried out to me and spoke, saying, ‘Behold, those who go out to the land of the north have set My Spirit at rest in the land of the north.’”

The Crowning of Joshua the High Priest

9 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 10 “Take silver and gold from the exiles—from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon—and you yourself shall go, on that very day, into the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. 11 And you shall take the silver and gold, make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

The Branch Who Shall Build the Temple

“12 And you shall say to him, ‘Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Behold, the man whose name is Branch shall branch out from His place, and He shall build the temple of the LORD. 13 Yes, He shall build the temple of the LORD, and He shall be invested with majesty, and He shall sit and rule upon His throne; and He shall be a priest upon His throne, and the counsel of peace shall be between the two.’ 14 And the crowns shall be a memorial in the temple of the LORD for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen son of Zephaniah. 15 And those who are far off shall come and build in the temple of the LORD; and you shall know that the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you. And it shall come to pass if you diligently heed the voice of the LORD your God.”
