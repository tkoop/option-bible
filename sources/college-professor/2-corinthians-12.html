Paul’s Reluctant Boasting and Heavenly Vision

1 It is incumbent upon me to boast, though such boasting is unprofitable; nevertheless, I will proceed to visions and revelations of the Lord. 2 I know a man in Christ who, fourteen years ago—whether in the body I do not know, or whether out of the body I do not know; God knows—was caught up to the third heaven. 3 And I know such a man—whether in the body or apart from the body I do not know; God knows— 4 that he was caught up into Paradise and heard inexpressible words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter. 5 On behalf of such a one I will boast, but on my own behalf I will not boast, except in my weaknesses. 6 For if I should wish to boast, I would not be foolish, for I would be speaking the truth; yet I refrain, lest anyone credit me with more than he sees in me or hears from me. 7 And lest I should be exalted beyond measure by the surpassing greatness of the revelations, a thorn in the flesh was given to me—a messenger of Satan to buffet me—lest I should be exalted beyond measure. 8 Three times I pleaded with the Lord concerning this, that it might depart from me.

9 And He said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness.”

Therefore I will boast all the more gladly in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest upon me. 10 Therefore I am well content, for Christ’s sake, with weaknesses, with insults, with necessities, with persecutions, and with distresses; for when I am weak, then I am strong.

Paul’s Defense of His Apostolic Ministry

11 I have become a fool; you compelled me. For I ought to have been commended by you, for I was in no way inferior to the super-apostles, even though I am nothing. 12 The signs of a true apostle were performed among you with all perseverance, by signs and wonders and mighty works. 13 For in what respect were you treated as inferior to the other churches, except that I myself did not burden you? Forgive me this wrong.

14 Behold, I am ready to come to you a third time, and I will not be burdensome to you; for I do not seek what is yours but you yourselves. For children ought not to treasure up for their parents, but parents for their children. 15 And I will most gladly spend and be spent for your souls; if I love you more abundantly, am I loved the less?

Answers to Suspicions and Final Concerns

16 But be it so, I did not burden you; nevertheless, being crafty, I took you by guile. 17 Did I take advantage of you by any of those whom I sent to you? 18 I urged Titus to go, and sent the brother with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit and in the same footsteps?

19 Do you suppose that all this time we have been defending ourselves to you? We speak before God in Christ; and all things, beloved, are for your edification. 20 For I fear that, when I come, I may not find you such as I wish, and that I may be found by you such as you do not wish—lest there be strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalries, slanders, whisperings, conceits, and disorders. 21 Lest, when I come again, my God should humble me before you, and I should mourn over many who sinned previously and have not repented of the impurity, fornication, and licentiousness which they practiced.
