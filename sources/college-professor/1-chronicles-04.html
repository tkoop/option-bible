The Descendants of Judah and Their Early Clans

1 Judah’s sons were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 2 Reaiah, the son of Shobal, fathered Jahath; Jahath, in turn, fathered Ahumai and Lahad, and these men constituted the clans of the Zorathites. 3 Etam’s sons were Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash, and their sister was Hazzelelponi. 4 Penuel fathered Gedor, whereas Ezer fathered Hushah; these were Hur’s sons, Hur being Ephrathah’s firstborn and Bethlehem’s progenitor. 5 Ashhur, Tekoa’s father, had two wives, Helah and Naarah. 6 Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari, and these were Naarah’s sons. 7 Helah’s sons were Zereth, Izhar, and Ethnan. 8 Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, along with the clans of Aharhel, Harum’s son.

The Prayer of Jabez

9 Jabez surpassed his brothers in honor, and his mother named him Jabez, declaring, “Because I bore him in pain.”

10 Jabez invoked the God of Israel, saying, “If You will indeed bless me and enlarge my territory, and if Your hand will accompany me, and if You will preserve me from harm so that I might not suffer pain.” And God granted his petition.

Further Judahite Lineages and Settlements

11 Chelub, Shuhah’s brother, fathered Mehir, who became Eshton’s father. 12 Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, Ir-nahash’s father; these were Recah’s men. 13 Kenaz’s sons were Othniel and Seraiah, and Othniel’s son was Hathath. 14 Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab, Ge-harashim’s father, because they were artisans. 15 Caleb, Jephunneh’s son, had sons Iru, Elah, and Naam, and Elah’s son was Kenaz. 16 Jehallelel’s sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel. 17 Ezrah’s sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon; Mered’s wife, Bithiah, Pharaoh’s daughter, bore Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, Eshtemoa’s father. 18 His Judean wife bore Jered, Gedor’s father; Heber, Soco’s father; and Jekuthiel, Zanoah’s father. These were the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh’s daughter, whom Mered married. 19 The sons of Hodiah’s wife, Naham’s sister, were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite. 20 Shimon’s sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon; Ishi’s sons were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth. 21 Shelah, Judah’s son, had sons Er, Lecah’s father; Laadah, Mareshah’s father; and the families of the linen workers at Beth-ashbea. 22 Jokim, the men of Cozeba, Joash, and Saraph—who ruled in Moab—and Jashubi-lehem: these are matters of antiquity. 23 They were potters and residents of Netaim and Gederah, and they dwelt there in royal service.

The Simeonites: Genealogy, Towns, and Conquests

24 Simeon’s sons were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 25 Shallum was his son, Mibsam his son, and Mishma his son. 26 Mishma’s sons were Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, and Shimei his son. 27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, though his brothers did not have numerous children, and their entire family did not multiply as abundantly as Judah’s sons. 28 They settled in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual, 29 Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad, 30 Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag, 31 Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim; these were their cities until David reigned. 32 Their villages included Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five cities—together with 33 all their surrounding villages as far as Baal. These were their settlements, and they maintained their genealogical registers.

34 Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah the son of Amaziah, 35 Joel, Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel, 36 Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah, 37 and Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah. 38 These named individuals were clan leaders, and their ancestral houses increased prodigiously. 39 They journeyed to Gedor’s entrance, east of the valley, seeking pasture for their flocks. 40 They discovered rich, excellent pasture, and the land was broad, tranquil, and secure, because Hamites had previously inhabited it. 41 These men, recorded by name, acted in Hezekiah’s reign, king of Judah; they attacked their tents and the Meunites found there, devoted them to destruction to this day, and occupied their place, since pasture for their flocks was there. 42 Five hundred of them—men of the Simeonites—went to Mount Seir, led by Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, sons of Ishi. 43 They struck down the remnant of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have resided there to this day.
