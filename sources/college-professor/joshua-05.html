The Terror of the Nations before Israel’s Advance

1 When all the Amorite kings who were beyond the Jordan to the west, together with all the Canaanite kings who were by the sea, heard that the LORD had dried up the waters of the Jordan before the children of Israel until we had passed over, their hearts melted, and no spirit remained in them any longer because of the children of Israel.

The Renewal of Circumcision in the Promised Land

2 At that time the LORD said to Joshua, “Make flint knives for yourself, and circumcise the children of Israel a second time.” 3 So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the children of Israel at the Hill of the Foreskins. 4 Now this is the reason Joshua circumcised them: all the males who had come out of Egypt, all the men of war, had died in the wilderness along the way after they came out of Egypt. 5 For although all the people who came out had been circumcised, none of those born in the wilderness along the way after they left Egypt had been circumcised. 6 For the children of Israel traveled in the wilderness forty years, until the whole nation of men of war who came out of Egypt was consumed, because they did not obey the voice of the LORD; to them the LORD had sworn that He would not let them see the land that the LORD had sworn to their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey. 7 So He raised up their children in their place, and Joshua circumcised these, for they were uncircumcised because they had not been circumcised on the journey. 8 When the whole nation had finished being circumcised, they remained in their places in the camp until they were healed.

9 The LORD said to Joshua, “Today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt from you.” Therefore the name of that place is called Gilgal to this day.

The Passover and the Cessation of Manna

10 The children of Israel camped at Gilgal and observed the Passover on the evening of the fourteenth day of the month in the plains of Jericho. 11 On the day after the Passover, on that very day, they ate unleavened bread and roasted grain from the produce of the land. 12 The manna ceased on the day after they ate from the produce of the land, and the children of Israel no longer had manna; instead, they ate from the produce of the land of Canaan that year.

The Mysterious Commander at Jericho

13 Now when Joshua was near Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, a man was standing opposite him with his sword drawn in his hand. Joshua went to him and said, “Are you for us, or for our adversaries?”

14 “No,” he replied, “but as Commander of the army of the LORD I have now come.”

Then Joshua fell on his face to the ground and worshiped, and said to him, “What does my Lord say to His servant?”

15 The Commander of the army of the LORD said to Joshua, “Take off your sandal from your foot, for the place where you are standing is holy.” And Joshua did so.
