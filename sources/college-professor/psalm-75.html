A Psalm of Thanksgiving and Divine Sovereignty

1We give thanks to You, O God; we give thanks, for Your name is near. The people declare Your wondrous works.

The LORD's Oracle in the Appointed Time

2“When I choose the appointed time, I will judge with equity. 3When the earth and all its inhabitants dissolve, it is I who uphold its pillars. Selah. 4I said to the boastful, ‘Do not boast,’ and to the wicked, ‘Do not exalt your strength.’ 5Do not exalt your strength on high, nor speak with haughty insolence.”

The LORD as Judge of Exaltation and Wrath

6For exaltation comes neither from the east nor from the west, nor from the mountainous wilderness; 7rather, it is God who judges—He brings one down and exalts another. 8For a cup is in the hand of the LORD, full of foaming wine mixed with spices; He pours it out, and all the wicked of the earth drink it down to the dregs.

The Psalmist's Perpetual Praise and Confidence

9But I will proclaim forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. 10I will cut off all the strength of the wicked, but the strength of the righteous will be exalted.
