The Reign of Amaziah in Judah

1 In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash became king of Judah. 2 He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother was Jehoaddan, from Jerusalem. 3 He did what was upright in the eyes of the LORD, though not with the wholehearted fidelity of his ancestor David; rather, he conducted himself in every respect as his father Joash had done. 4 Yet the high places were not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and to burn incense upon them.

5 When the kingdom was firmly established in his hand, he put to death the servants who had assassinated his father the king. 6 But he did not execute the children of the murderers, in accordance with what is written in the Book of the Law of Moses, wherein the LORD commanded, “Fathers shall not be put to death because of their children, nor shall children be put to death because of their fathers; each shall die for his own sin.” 7 He struck down ten thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt, seized Sela in battle, and renamed it Joktheel, a name it bears to this day.

Amaziah's Defeat by Israel

8 Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come, let us confront one another face to face.”

9 Jehoash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, “The thistle that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son for a wife’; but a wild beast that was in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle. 10 You have indeed struck Edom, and your heart has lifted you up. Glory in it and stay at home; why should you stir up trouble so that you fall, you and Judah with you?”

11 But Amaziah would not listen, so Jehoash king of Israel advanced; he and Amaziah king of Judah confronted one another at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 12 Judah was routed before Israel, and every man fled to his tent. 13 Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Jehoash son of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh; then he came to Jerusalem and tore down the wall of Jerusalem from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate—a breach of four hundred cubits. 14 He carried off all the gold and silver and all the vessels found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the royal palace, together with hostages, and returned to Samaria.

15 The rest of the acts of Jehoash—what he did, his might, and his war with Amaziah king of Judah—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 16 Jehoash slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; his son Jeroboam reigned in his place.

The Death of Amaziah and the Rise of Azariah

17 Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 18 The rest of the acts of Amaziah—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 19 A conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but they sent men after him to Lachish and put him to death there. 20 They brought him back on horses and buried him in Jerusalem with his fathers in the City of David. 21 Then all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. 22 He rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah, after the king had been laid to rest with his fathers.

Jeroboam II and the Restoration of Israel

23 In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash became king of Israel in Samaria, and he reigned forty-one years. 24 He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had made Israel sin. 25 He restored the border of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, in accordance with the word that the LORD, the God of Israel, had spoken through His servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher. 26 For the LORD saw the very bitter affliction of Israel: there was no one, whether confined or at large, and no helper for Israel. 27 Yet the LORD had not decreed that He would blot out the name of Israel from under heaven, so He saved them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash. 28 The rest of the acts of Jeroboam—all that he did, his might, how he waged war, and how he recovered Damascus and Hamath, which had belonged to Judah, for Israel—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 29 Jeroboam slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel; his son Zechariah reigned in his place.
