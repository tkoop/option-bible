Sarai’s Scheme and Hagar’s Conception

1 Now Sarai, Abram’s wife, had borne him no children, but she had an Egyptian maidservant whose name was Hagar. 2 Sarai said to Abram, “Behold, the LORD has restrained me from bearing; please go in to my servant, that perhaps I may have children through her.” And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai.

3 After Abram had dwelt ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram’s wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her maidservant, and gave her to Abram her husband as a wife. 4 He went in to Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, her mistress became contemptible in her eyes.

5 Sarai said to Abram, “The wrong done to me is upon you. I gave my maidservant into your bosom, and when she saw that she had conceived, I was despised in her eyes. May the LORD judge between me and you.”

6 Abram said to Sarai, “Behold, your maidservant is in your hand; do to her what is good in your eyes.” Then Sarai dealt harshly with her, and she fled from her presence.

Hagar’s Flight and the Angel’s Counsel

7 The angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the road to Shur.

8 He said, “Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?”

She said, “I am fleeing from my mistress Sarai.”

9 The angel of the LORD said to her, “Return to your mistress, and humble yourself under her authority. 10 I will greatly multiply your offspring, so that they cannot be counted for multitude. 11 Behold, you are with child and shall bear a son, and you shall name him Ishmael, because the LORD has heard your affliction. 12 He shall be a wild donkey of a man: his hand against everyone, and everyone’s hand against him; and he shall live in hostility toward all his brothers.”

13 She called the name of the LORD who spoke to her, “You are the God who sees me,” for she said, “Truly, here I have seen the One who sees me.”

14 Therefore the well was called Beer-lahai-roi; it lies between Kadesh and Bered.

The Birth of Ishmael

15 Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael. 16 Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.
