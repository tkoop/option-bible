Galilean Ministry and the Challenge of His Brothers

1 Thereafter, Jesus walked throughout Galilee, for He was disinclined to traverse Judea, since the Jews were intent upon putting Him to death. 2 Now the feast of the Jews, the Feast of Tabernacles, was imminent. 3 So His brothers said to Him, “Depart from here and go into Judea, so that your disciples may also behold the works you are performing. 4 For no one acts in secret while desiring public recognition; if you are indeed doing these things, manifest yourself to the world.”

5 For even His brothers did not believe in Him. 6 Jesus said to them, “My appointed time has not yet arrived, but your time is perpetually at hand. 7 The world cannot hate you, but it hates Me, because I testify concerning it that its works are evil. 8 Go up to the feast; I am not yet going up to this feast, because My time has not yet been fulfilled.” 9 After saying these things, He remained in Galilee.

A Covert Pilgrimage to the Feast

10 But when His brothers had gone up to the feast, then He also went up, not openly, but as it were in secret. 11 The Jews were seeking Him at the feast and saying, “Where is He?” 12 There was considerable murmuring among the crowds concerning Him.

“He is good,” some said.

“No,” others replied, “He leads the people astray.”

13 Yet no one spoke openly about Him, for fear of the Jews.

Temple Discourse and the Sabbath Controversy

14 Now when the feast was already half over, Jesus went up into the temple and began to teach. 15 The Jews therefore marveled, saying, “How does this man possess such learning, having never been taught?”

16 Jesus answered them, “My teaching is not Mine, but His who sent Me. 17 If anyone is willing to do His will, he will know concerning the teaching, whether it is from God or whether I speak from Myself. 18 He who speaks from himself seeks his own glory, but he who seeks the glory of the One who sent him is true, and there is no unrighteousness in him.”

19 Jesus said, “Did not Moses give you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why do you seek to kill Me?”

20 The crowd answered, “You have a demon! Who seeks to kill You?”

21 Jesus answered and said to them, “I performed one work, and you all marvel. 22 For this reason Moses gave you circumcision—not that it is from Moses, but from the fathers—and on the Sabbath you circumcise a man. 23 If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with Me because I made an entire man well on the Sabbath? 24 Do not judge according to appearance, but judge with righteous judgment.”

Popular Schism, Messianic Expectation, and the Failure of the Officers

25 Some of those from Jerusalem were saying, “Is this not the man they seek to kill? 26 And look, He speaks openly, and they say nothing to Him. Have the rulers indeed come to know that this is the Christ? 27 But we know where this man is from; yet when the Christ comes, no one knows where He is from.”

28 Then Jesus, teaching in the temple, cried out, “You know Me, and you know where I am from; and I have not come of Myself, but He who sent Me is true, whom you do not know. 29 I know Him, because I am from Him, and He sent Me.”

30 So they were seeking to seize Him, but no one laid a hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come. 31 Many among the crowd believed in Him and were saying, “When the Christ comes, will He not perform more signs than this man has done?”

32 The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about Him, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to seize Him. 33 Jesus said, “I am with you a little while longer, and then I go to Him who sent Me. 34 You will seek Me and will not find Me; and where I am, you cannot come.”

35 The Jews said to one another, “Where is this man about to go, that we shall not find Him? Is He going to the Dispersion of the Greeks and to teach the Greeks?”

36 “What is this saying that He said, ‘You will seek Me and will not find Me, and where I am you cannot come’?”

37 On the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, “If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink. 38 He who believes in Me, as the Scripture has said, rivers of living water will flow from his innermost being.” 39 Now this He said concerning the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were about to receive; for the Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.

40 Some of the crowd, hearing these words, were saying, “This is truly the Prophet.”

41 Others were saying, “This is the Christ.”

But others were saying, “Surely the Christ does not come from Galilee, does He? 42 Has not the Scripture said that the Christ comes from the seed of David and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?”

43 So a division arose among the crowd because of Him. 44 Some of them wanted to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him.

45 Then the officers came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, “Why did you not bring Him?”

46 The officers answered, “Never has a man spoken like this man.”

47 The Pharisees answered them, “Have you also been led astray? 48 Has any of the rulers or of the Pharisees believed in Him? 49 But this crowd, which does not know the law, is accursed.”

50 Nicodemus, who had previously come to Him, being one of them, said to them, 51 “Does our law judge a man unless it first hears from him and knows what he is doing?”

52 They answered and said to him, “Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that no prophet arises from Galilee.” 53 And each went to his own house.
