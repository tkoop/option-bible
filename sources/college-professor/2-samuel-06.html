David Assembles Israel for the Ark’s Ascent

1 David again mustered the elect of Israel’s soldiery, thirty thousand strong. 2 David, together with the entire host accompanying him, departed from Baale-judah to convey thence the ark of God, which bears the Name, even the name of the LORD of Hosts, enthroned upon the cherubim. 3 They set the ark of God upon a newly fashioned cart and bore it from Abinadab’s hillside dwelling; Uzzah and Ahio, Abinadab’s sons, were guiding the new cart. 4 They conveyed it, together with the ark of God, from Abinadab’s house on the hill, Ahio proceeding before the ark. 5 David and the whole house of Israel were celebrating before the LORD with every instrument fashioned of cypress wood—lyres, harps, tambourines, castanets, and cymbals.

Uzzah’s Death and the Ark’s Sojourn

6 Upon arriving at the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah stretched forth his hand and laid hold of the ark of God, for the oxen had stumbled. 7 Then the LORD’s anger flared against Uzzah, and God struck him down there on account of his irreverence; he perished there beside the ark of God. 8 David was incensed because the LORD had broken out against Uzzah; therefore he named that place Perez-uzzah, a designation it retains to this day. 9 David feared the LORD that day and asked, “How shall the ark of the LORD ever come to me?”

10 Accordingly, David would not remove the ark of the LORD into the City of David; rather, he turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite. 11 The ark of the LORD abode in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite for three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and his entire household.

The Ark’s Triumphant Entry into Jerusalem

12 King David was told, “The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that pertains to him on account of the ark of God.” Thereupon David went and brought up the ark of God from Obed-edom’s house to the City of David with great rejoicing. 13 After those bearing the ark of the LORD had advanced six paces, he sacrificed an ox and a fatted beast. 14 David, girded with a linen ephod, was dancing before the LORD with all his might. 15 Thus David and all the house of Israel were conveying the ark of the LORD upward amid shouting and the blast of the trumpet.

16 As the ark of the LORD was entering the City of David, Michal, daughter of Saul, looked down from the window and beheld King David leaping and dancing before the LORD; and she despised him in her heart. 17 They brought the ark of the LORD and stationed it in its appointed place within the tent David had pitched for it; and David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD. 18 When David had completed the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of Hosts. 19 He apportioned to the entire assemblage of Israel, men and women alike, one loaf of bread, one portion of meat, and one raisin cake for each person; thereafter the whole people dispersed, every man to his own dwelling.

Michal’s Reproach and David’s Defense

20 As David returned to bless his household, Michal daughter of Saul came forth to meet him and exclaimed, “How gloriously the king of Israel has distinguished himself today, exposing himself before the eyes of his servants’ handmaids, like one of the vulgar rabble who shamelessly bares himself!”

21 David replied to Michal, “It was before the LORD, who chose me above your father and above all his house, to appoint me ruler over the people of the LORD, over Israel; therefore I will celebrate before the LORD. 22 I will demean myself still further than this, and I will be lowly in my own sight; yet with the handmaids of whom you have spoken, with them I shall be held in honor.”

23 And Michal, daughter of Saul, remained childless until the day of her death.
