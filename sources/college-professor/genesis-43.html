The Famine Forces a Second Descent into Egypt

1 Now the famine bore heavily upon the land. 2 And when they had consumed the grain that they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, "Return; procure for us a modest quantity of provisions."

3 But Judah said to him, "The man solemnly warned us, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.' 4 If you send our brother with us, we will descend and buy food for you. 5 Yet if you will not send him, we will not descend, for the man said to us, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

6 Then Israel said, "Why have you dealt so grievously with me by telling the man that you had yet another brother?"

7 They answered, "The man questioned us closely concerning ourselves and our kindred, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Have you a brother?' Thus we answered him according to these words. How could we have known that he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?"

Judah’s Suretyship and Israel’s Reluctant Consent

8 Then Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the boy with me, and we will arise and go, that we may live and not die—both we and you and our little ones. 9 I myself will be surety for him; from my hand you shall require him. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, then let me bear the blame before you all my days. 10 For had we not delayed, we would surely have returned by now a second time."

11 Then Israel their father said to them, "If it must be so, then do this: take some of the best produce of the land in your bags and carry down a present to the man—a little balm, a little honey, gum, myrrh, pistachio nuts, and almonds. 12 Take double money with you, and take back in your hand the money that was returned in the mouths of your sacks; perhaps it was an oversight. 13 Take also your brother and arise; go again to the man. 14 May God Almighty grant you mercy before the man, that he may send back your other brother and Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."

The Brothers Stand Before Joseph

15 So the men took this present, and they took double money with them, and Benjamin. They arose and went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.

16 When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the steward of his house, "Bring the men into the house, and slaughter an animal and make ready, for the men shall dine with me at noon."

17 The man did as Joseph had commanded and brought the men into Joseph's house.

18 Now the men were afraid because they had been brought into Joseph's house, and they said, "It is because of the money that was returned in our sacks the first time that we are brought in, so that he may assault us and fall upon us and take us for slaves, together with our donkeys."

19 So they drew near to the steward of Joseph's house and spoke to him at the door of the house, 20 saying, "Oh, my lord, we came down the first time to buy food. 21 And when we came to the lodging place and opened our sacks, behold, each man's money was in the mouth of his sack—our money in full weight—so we have brought it back in our hand. 22 We have also brought down other money with us to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks."

23 He replied, "Peace be to you; do not fear. Your God and the God of your father has put treasure in your sacks for you. I have received your money." Then he brought Simeon out to them.

24 And the man brought the men into Joseph's house and gave them water; they washed their feet, and he gave their donkeys fodder. 25 They prepared the present for Joseph's arrival at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat bread there.

Joseph’s Compassion and the Feast

26 When Joseph came into the house, they brought into the house to him the present that was in their hand, and bowed down to him to the ground.

27 He inquired after their welfare and said, "Is your father well, the old man of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?"

28 They said, "Your servant our father is well; he is still alive." And they bowed down and prostrated themselves.

29 He lifted up his eyes and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son, and said, "Is this your younger brother, of whom you spoke to me?" And he said, "God be gracious to you, my son."

30 Then Joseph hurried out, for his compassion was stirred for his brother, and he sought a place to weep. He went into his chamber and wept there.

31 Then he washed his face, came out, restrained himself, and said, "Serve the food."

32 They served him by himself, and them by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves, because the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination to the Egyptians.

33 They sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men looked at one another in astonishment. 34 He took portions from his own table to them, but Benjamin's portion was five times as much as any of theirs. And they drank and were merry with him.
