Oracle Against the Philistines

1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.

The Northern Flood and the Ruin of Philistia

2 Thus says the LORD: “Behold, waters are rising from the north and shall become an overflowing torrent; they shall inundate the land and all its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it. Humanity will cry out, and every inhabitant of the land will wail. 3 At the sound of the stamping of the hoofs of his stallions, at the thunder of his chariots and the rumble of his wheels, fathers will not turn back for their children, for their hands have become utterly feeble. 4 This is the day that comes to destroy all the Philistines, to cut off from Tyre and Sidon every surviving helper; for the LORD is destroying the Philistines, the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor.”

Mourning in Gaza and Ashkelon

5 “Baldness has come upon Gaza; Ashkelon is silenced. O remnant of their valley, how long will you gash yourself?”

The Sword of the LORD

6 “Alas, sword of the LORD! How long until you are quiet? Sheathe yourself in your scabbard; be tranquil and silent. 7 How can it be quiet when the LORD has commanded it? Against Ashkelon and against the seacoast, there He has appointed it.”
