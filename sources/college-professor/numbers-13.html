Divine Commissioning of Canaan's Reconnaissance

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Send men to spy out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites; from each ancestral tribe you shall send one leader.” 3 At the LORD's command Moses sent them from the wilderness of Paran; all of them were men and heads of the Israelites.

The Twelve Tribal Leaders Appointed as Spies

4 From the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur; 5 from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori; 6 from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh; 7 from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph; 8 from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun; 9 from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu; 10 from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi; 11 from the tribe of Manasseh, that is, of Joseph, Gaddi son of Susi; 12 from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli; 13 from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael; 14 from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi; 15 and from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

16 These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses called Hoshea son of Nun Joshua.

Mosaic Instructions for the Expedition

17 Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan and said to them, “Go up by way of the Negev, and then ascend into the hill country. 18 See what the land is like, and whether the people who dwell in it are strong or weak, few or many. 19 And see what the land is like in which they dwell, whether good or bad; and what the cities are like in which they dwell, whether in camps or in fortified places. 20 And see what the land is like, whether rich or poor; whether there is timber in it or not. Take courage and bring back some of the fruit of the land.” Now the season was that of the first ripe grapes.

21 So they went up and spied out the land from the wilderness of Zin to Rehob, near Lebo-hamath. 22 They went up into the Negev and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai—the descendants of Anak—were. (Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.) 23 They reached the Valley of Eshcol and cut down from there a branch bearing a single cluster of grapes; they carried it on a pole between two of them, and they also brought some pomegranates and figs. 24 That place was called the Valley of Eshcol because of the cluster the Israelites cut down from there. 25 At the end of forty days they returned from spying out the land.

The Spies' Report and the Crisis of Resolve

26 They came to Moses and Aaron and to the whole congregation of the Israelites in the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh; they brought back word to them and to the whole congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land. 27 They told him, “We came to the land to which you sent us; it indeed flows with milk and honey, and this is its fruit. 28 Nevertheless, the people who inhabit the land are strong, and its cities are fortified and exceedingly large; moreover, we saw the descendants of Anak there. 29 Amalek dwells in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, the Jebusites, and the Amorites dwell in the hill country; and the Canaanites dwell by the sea and along the Jordan.”

30 Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, “Let us go up at once and take possession of it, for we are well able to overcome it.”

31 But the men who had gone up with him said, “We are not able to go up against the people, for they are stronger than we.”

32 And they brought an evil report to the Israelites concerning the land they had spied out, saying, “The land through which we passed to spy it out is a land that devours its inhabitants; and all the people we saw in it are of great stature. 33 Moreover, we saw the Nephilim there—the sons of Anak, who descend from the Nephilim—and we seemed to ourselves like grasshoppers, and so we seemed to them.”
