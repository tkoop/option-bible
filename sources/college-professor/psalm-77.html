For the choirmaster, according to Jeduthun: a psalm of Asaph.

A Cry in the Night

1 I cried aloud to God for deliverance; I cried aloud to God, that He might hear me. 2 In the day of my distress I sought the Lord; throughout the night my outstretched hand did not grow weary, and my soul refused to be comforted. 3 I remembered God, and I groaned; I mused, and my spirit fainted. Selah 4 You have kept my eyes from closing; I was too distraught to speak. 5 I considered the days of old, the years of antiquity. 6 At night I remember my song; I meditate within my heart, and my spirit searches:

Questioning the Lord's Favor

7 “Will the Lord cast off forever? Will He never again show favor? 8 Has His steadfast love ceased forever? Has His promise failed for all generations? 9 Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has He in anger withheld His compassion?” Selah

Remembering the Works of the Most High

10 Then I said, “This is my anguish: the years of the right hand of the Most High.” 11 I will remember the deeds of Yah; indeed, I will remember Your wonders of old. 12 I will meditate on all Your work; I will ponder Your mighty deeds. 13 Your way, O God, is in holiness; what god is as great as our God? 14 You are the God who works wonders; You have made known Your strength among the peoples. 15 With Your mighty arm You redeemed Your people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah

The God Who Commands the Waters

16 The waters saw You, O God; the waters saw You and were convulsed; even the depths trembled. 17 The clouds poured down water; the skies thundered; Your arrows flashed back and forth. 18 The sound of Your thunder was in the whirlwind; Your lightnings illuminated the world; the earth trembled and quaked. 19 Your way was in the sea, and Your path through the mighty waters, yet Your footsteps were not known. 20 You led Your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
