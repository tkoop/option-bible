A Benjamite Lineage and a Search for Lost Donkeys

1 There was once a man of Benjamin—Kish son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah—a Benjamite of formidable valor. 2 He had a son named Saul, select in bearing and strikingly comely; among the Israelites no man surpassed him in handsomeness, and from the shoulders upward he towered over all the people.

3 Now the donkeys belonging to Saul's father Kish had strayed away. Kish therefore said to his son Saul, "Please take one of the servants with you and go; seek out the donkeys."

4 So Saul traversed the hill country of Ephraim and the territory of Shalishah, yet they did not find them; they then passed through the land of Shaalim, but the animals were not there; and when they crossed into Benjamin, they still found nothing.

The Servant's Counsel and the Seer's City

5 When they reached the land of Zuph, Saul said to the servant accompanying him, "Come, let us turn back, lest my father abandon his concern for the donkeys and grow disquieted on our account."

6 The servant replied, "Look, in this city there is a man of God, a person of considerable honor; every word he utters is fulfilled. Let us proceed there; perhaps he will disclose to us the route we ought to take."

7 Saul said to the servant, "If we go, what shall we present to the man? The bread in our sacks has been consumed, and we have no offering for the man of God. What remains at our disposal?"

8 The servant answered, "Look, I have in my possession a quarter shekel of silver; I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our route."

9 (In former times in Israel, when one went to consult God, one would say, "Come, let us go to the seer," for the figure now termed a prophet was then called a seer.)

10 Saul said to his servant, "Your words are sound; come, let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God resided.

11 As they ascended the hill toward the city, they encountered young women emerging to draw water, and they asked them, "Is the seer here?"

12 They answered, "He is; he is just ahead of you. Make haste, for he has arrived in the city today, because the people are holding a sacrifice at the high place today. 13 As soon as you enter the city, you will find him before he ascends to the high place to eat; for the people will not eat until he arrives, since he must bless the sacrifice, and only afterward will those invited eat. Go up now; today you will find him."

14 So they went up to the city, and as they were entering, Samuel came out to meet them as he was going up to the high place.

Divine Revelation of Israel's Chosen King

15 Now on the day preceding Saul's arrival, the the LORD had revealed to Samuel: 16 "Tomorrow at about this hour I will send you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as leader over My people Israel, and he shall deliver My people from the hand of the Philistines; for I have regarded My people, because their cry has reached Me."

17 When Samuel saw Saul, the the LORD said to him, "Here is the man of whom I spoke to you. He shall govern My people."

18 Then Saul approached Samuel in the gateway and said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."

19 Samuel answered, "I am the seer. Go up ahead of me to the high place, for today you are to eat with me, and in the morning I will send you on your way and tell you all that is in your heart. 20 As for the donkeys that were lost to you three days ago, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. And to whom does all the desire of Israel belong? Is it not to you and to your father's house?"

21 Saul answered, "Am I not a Benjamite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and is not my clan the least of all the clans of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me in this manner?"

A Feast and a Private Word from God

22 Then Samuel took Saul and his servant and brought them into the chamber and seated them at the head of those invited, about thirty in number.

23 Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the portion that I gave you, the one I told you to set aside." 24 So the cook brought up the thigh with its attached portion and set it before Saul. And Samuel said, "Here is what was reserved for you. Eat, for it was kept for you for this appointed time, for I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel that day.

25 After they descended from the high place into the city, Samuel spoke with Saul on the roof.

26 They rose early in the morning, and at dawn Samuel called to Saul on the roof, "Get up, and I will send you on your way." So Saul got up, and the two of them went out together. 27 As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the servant to go on ahead of us; but you remain here for a while, so that I may make known to you the word of God."
