David’s Supplication for Mercy and Deliverance

1 Incline Your ear, O the LORD; answer me, for I am poor and needy. 2 Preserve my life, for I am godly; O my God, save Your servant who trusts in You. 3 Be gracious to me, O Lord, for to You I cry all day long. 4 Gladden the soul of Your servant, for to You, O Lord, I lift up my soul.

5 For You, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in steadfast love to all who call on You. 6 Give ear to my prayer, O the LORD, and attend to the voice of my supplications. 7 In the day of my distress I will call upon You, for You will answer me.

The Incomparability of the LORD

8 Among the gods there is none like You, O Lord, nor are there works like Yours. 9 All the nations You have made will come and bow down before You, O Lord, and they will glorify Your name. 10 For You are great and perform wonders; You alone are God.

11 Teach me Your way, O the LORD; I will walk in Your truth. Grant me an undivided heart, that I may fear Your name. 12 I will praise You, O Lord my God, with all my heart, and I will glorify Your name forever. 13 For great is Your steadfast love toward me; You have delivered my soul from the depths of Sheol.

Deliverance from the Arrogant and Final Comfort

14 O God, the arrogant have risen against me; a band of ruthless men seeks my life; they have no regard for You. 15 But You, O Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. 16 Turn to me and have mercy on me; grant Your strength to Your servant, and save the son of Your maidservant. 17 Show me a sign of Your favor, that my enemies may see it and be put to shame, because You, O the LORD, have helped me and comforted me.
