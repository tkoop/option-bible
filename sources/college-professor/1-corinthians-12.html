Concerning Spiritual Gifts and the Test of the Spirit

1 Now, concerning spiritual gifts, brothers, I would not have you remain in ignorance. 2 You know that when you were pagans, you were led astray unto mute idols, in whatever manner you were led. 3 Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking by the Spirit of God declares, "Jesus is accursed," and no one is able to declare, "Jesus is Lord," except by the Holy Spirit.

The Varieties of Gifts and the Unity of the Spirit

4 Now there are varieties of gifts, yet the same Spirit; 5 and there are varieties of ministries, yet the same Lord; 6 and there are varieties of operations, yet the same God, who works all things in all. 7 But to each is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the common good. 8 For to one is given through the Spirit a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge according to the same Spirit; 9 to another faith by the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healing by the one Spirit; 10 and to another the working of miracles, and to another prophecy, and to another the discerning of spirits, to another various kinds of tongues, and to another the interpretation of tongues. 11 But all these are worked by the one and the same Spirit, distributing to each severally even as He wills.

The Body of Christ and Its Many Members

12 For as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so also is Christ. 13 For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, and we were all given to drink of one Spirit. 14 For the body is not one member, but many.

15 If the foot should say, "Because I am not a hand, I am not of the body," it is not on that account any less a part of the body. 16 And if the ear should say, "Because I am not an eye, I am not of the body," it is not on that account any less a part of the body. 17 If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be? 18 But now God has set the members, each one of them, in the body, as it pleased Him. 19 If they were all one member, where would the body be? 20 But now there are many members, yet one body.

21 And the eye cannot say to the hand, "I have no need of you"; nor again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you." 22 Nay, much rather, those members of the body which seem to be weaker are indispensable; 23 and those members of the body which we deem less honorable, upon these we bestow more abundant honor, and our less presentable members attain far greater presentability; 24 whereas our more presentable members have no such need. But God has tempered the body together, giving more abundant honor to that part which lacked it, 25 so that there should be no schism in the body, but that the members should cherish the same care for one another. 26 And whether one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; or one member is honored, all the members rejoice with it.

God's Appointments in the Church

27 Now you are the body of Christ, and members in particular. 28 And God has set some in the church: first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues. 29 Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Are all workers of miracles? 30 Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak with tongues? Do all interpret? 31 But earnestly desire the greater gifts. Moreover, I will show you a way that is far more excellent.
