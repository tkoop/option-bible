The Eighth Day and the Summons to Worship

1 On the eighth day, Moses summoned Aaron, together with his sons and the elders of Israel.

2 Moses said to Aaron, “Take a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering, both unblemished, and present them before the LORD. 3 Say to the Israelites, ‘Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a calf and a lamb, each a year old and unblemished, for a burnt offering. 4 Bring also an ox and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice before the LORD, together with a grain offering mixed with oil, for today the LORD will appear to you.’”

5 They brought what Moses had commanded to the front of the tent of meeting, and the whole congregation drew near and stood before the LORD.

6 Moses said, “This is what the LORD has commanded you to do, so that the glory of the LORD may appear to you.”

7 Moses said to Aaron, “Approach the altar and offer your sin offering and your burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself and for the people; then present the people’s offering and make atonement for them, as the LORD has commanded.”

Aaron’s Sin and Burnt Offerings

8 Aaron approached the altar and slaughtered the calf for his sin offering. 9 Aaron’s sons brought the blood to him; he dipped his finger in the blood and put it on the horns of the altar, and he poured out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. 10 The fat, the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver from the sin offering he burned on the altar, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 11 The flesh and the hide he burned with fire outside the camp.

12 He slaughtered the burnt offering, and Aaron’s sons handed him the blood, which he sprinkled all around the altar. 13 They handed him the burnt offering, cut into its pieces, together with the head, and he burned them on the altar. 14 He washed the entrails and the legs and burned them on the altar, on top of the burnt offering.

The People’s Sacrifices and Wave Offering

15 Then he brought the people’s offering: he took the goat of the sin offering for the people, slaughtered it, and offered it for sin, as he had done with the first. 16 He brought the burnt offering and offered it according to the ordinance. 17 He brought the grain offering, filled his hand with some of it, and burned it on the altar, in addition to the morning burnt offering.

18 He slaughtered the ox and the ram for the people as a peace offering; Aaron’s sons handed him the blood, and he sprinkled it around the altar. 19 They also set forth the fat portions from the ox and the ram: the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver. 20 They placed the fat portions on the breasts, and he burned the fat portions on the altar. 21 Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh as a wave offering before the LORD, as Moses had commanded.

The Divine Glory Revealed

22 Then Aaron lifted his hands toward the people and blessed them; and, having finished offering the sin offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, he came down. 23 Moses and Aaron entered the tent of meeting; when they came out, they blessed the people, and the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people. 24 Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed the burnt offering and the fat on the altar; when all the people saw it, they shouted and fell on their faces.
