The Outer Sanctuary and Its Measurements

1 Then he brought me to the outer sanctuary and measured the side pillars; they were six cubits in breadth on one side and six cubits on the other—the breadth of the tabernacle. 2 The entrance was ten cubits wide, and its shoulder walls were five cubits on either side. He measured the outer sanctuary: forty cubits in length and twenty cubits in breadth.

The Inner Sanctuary

3 Then he entered the inner sanctuary and measured the jambs of the entrance: two cubits; the entrance itself: six cubits; and the breadth of the entrance: seven cubits. 4 He measured the inner sanctuary: twenty cubits in length and twenty cubits in breadth, corresponding to the outer sanctuary. And he said to me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”

The Side Chambers and Temple Enclosure

5 Then he measured the wall of the temple: six cubits; and the breadth of each side chamber: four cubits, all around the temple. 6 The side chambers were arranged in three stories, thirty chambers to each story, stacked one above another. Ledges ran all around the wall of the temple to support the side chambers, so that the supports were not inserted into the wall of the temple itself. 7 The side chambers surrounding the temple grew wider at each successive level, because the surrounding structure was built in tiers; consequently, the width of the temple increased as one ascended, the upper chambers being formed from space taken from the lower and middle levels.

8 I observed that the temple had a raised base all around it; these were the foundations of the side chambers, a full rod—six cubits—in height. 9 The outer wall of the side chambers was five cubits thick, and the open area between the side chambers of the temple 10 and the outer chambers was twenty cubits wide all around the temple. 11 The side chambers opened onto this open area, one door facing north and another facing south, and the open area was five cubits wide all around.

12 The building facing the temple yard on the west was seventy cubits wide; its wall was five cubits thick all around, and its length was ninety cubits. 13 He measured the temple: one hundred cubits in length; and the separate place, the building, and its walls: one hundred cubits in length. 14 The separate place on the east, facing the temple, was one hundred cubits wide.

15 He measured the building facing the separate place at the rear, together with its galleries on either side: one hundred cubits. The outer sanctuary, the inner sanctuary, and the porticoes of the court 16 were paneled with wood all around, and the thresholds, the narrow windows, and the galleries around all three were likewise paneled with wood—everything from the threshold up to the windows, which were covered. 17 From the space above the door up to the inner sanctuary, and all around the outer and inner walls, everything was measured.

Carved Ornamentation, Doors, and the Table

18 They were carved with cherubim and palm trees: a palm tree stood between two cherubim, and each cherub had two faces. 19 One face was human, turned toward the palm tree on one side, and the other was a lion’s face, turned toward the palm tree on the other side. These carvings ran all around the temple. 20 From the floor to the space above the door, cherubim and palm trees were carved on the walls of the sanctuary.

21 The outer sanctuary had a square doorframe, and the front of the sanctuary looked like 22 an altar of wood—three cubits high and two cubits long—with corners; its length and its walls were of wood. And he said to me, “This is the table that is before the LORD.”

23 The outer sanctuary and the inner sanctuary each had a double door, 24 and each door had two panels—two folding doors for one door and two for the other. 25 Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the doors of the sanctuary, as on the walls, and a canopy of wood stood over the front of the portico outside. 26 Narrow windows and palm trees appeared on either side of the portico’s shoulders, along with the side chambers of the temple and the canopies.
