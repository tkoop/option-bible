The Passover Commanded in the Wilderness of Sinai

1 In the first month of the second year after the Israelites had departed from the land of Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying:

2 “Command the Israelites to observe the Passover at its appointed time. 3 You are to observe it at the appointed time, at twilight on the fourteenth day of this month, in accordance with all its statutes and ordinances.”

4 So Moses instructed the Israelites to observe the Passover, 5 and they did so in the wilderness of Sinai at twilight on the fourteenth day of the first month. The Israelites executed everything precisely as the LORD had commanded Moses.

Provision for Those Unable to Keep the First Passover

6 Yet certain men had become unclean through contact with a human corpse, and therefore could not observe the Passover on that day. They accordingly presented themselves before Moses and Aaron on that same day

7 and said to Moses, “We are unclean because of a dead body; but why should we be excluded from presenting the LORD’s offering among the Israelites at its appointed time?”

8 Moses replied to them, “Remain here, and I will ascertain what the LORD commands concerning you.”

9 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

10 “Tell the Israelites: ‘When any one of you or your descendants is unclean because of a dead body, or is on a distant journey, he may nevertheless observe the Passover to the LORD. 11 Such persons shall observe it at twilight on the fourteenth day of the second month; they shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. 12 They shall leave none of it until morning, nor break any of its bones; they shall observe the Passover in accordance with all its statutes. 13 But if a man is clean and is not on a journey, yet neglects to observe the Passover, that person shall be cut off from his people, because he did not present the LORD’s offering at its appointed time; that man shall bear his iniquity. 14 If an alien sojourning among you wishes to observe the Passover to the LORD, he shall do so according to the statute and ordinance of the Passover. You shall have one statute for the alien and for the native of the land.’”

The Cloud and the Movements of Israel

15 On the day the tabernacle was erected, the cloud enveloped the tabernacle—the Tent of the Testimony—and from evening until morning the cloud above the tabernacle had the appearance of fire. 16 Thus it was continually: the cloud covered it by day, and by night it had the appearance of fire.

17 Whenever the cloud was lifted from above the Tent, the Israelites would set out; and wherever the cloud settled, there the Israelites would encamp. 18 At the command of the LORD the Israelites set out, and at the command of the LORD they encamped. As long as the cloud rested over the tabernacle, they remained encamped.

19 Even when the cloud lingered over the tabernacle for many days, the Israelites kept the charge of the LORD and did not set out. 20 Sometimes the cloud remained over the tabernacle for only a few days; at the command of the LORD they would encamp, and at the command of the LORD they would set out. 21 Sometimes the cloud remained only from evening until morning, and when it lifted in the morning, they set out. Whether by day or by night, whenever the cloud lifted, they set out.

22 Whether the cloud lingered for two days, a month, or longer, the Israelites remained encamped and did not set out as long as the cloud rested over the tabernacle; but when it lifted, they set out. 23 They encamped at the command of the LORD and set out at the command of the LORD. They kept the charge of the LORD, according to the command of the LORD through Moses.
