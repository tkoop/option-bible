An Oracle Concerning Egypt

1 An oracle concerning Egypt: Behold, the LORD rides upon a swift cloud and comes to Egypt; the idols of Egypt tremble before Him, and the heart of Egypt melts within it.

2 I will incite Egyptian against Egyptian—brother against brother, neighbor against neighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom. 3 The spirit of the Egyptians will be drained from within them; I will confound their counsel, and they will consult idols, spirits of the dead, mediums, and spiritists. 4 I will deliver Egypt into the hand of a cruel master, and a fierce king will rule over them, declares the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

The Desolation of Egypt's Waters and Labor

5 The waters of the sea will dry up, and the river will be parched and desiccated. 6 The canals will grow fetid; the streams of Egypt will dwindle and run dry, and the reeds and rushes will wither. 7 The papyrus beside the Nile, at the mouth of the Nile, and every sown field along the Nile, will wither, be driven away, and cease to be.

8 The fishermen will mourn, and all who cast a hook into the Nile will lament; those who spread nets upon the waters will languish. 9 The workers in flax will be dismayed, and the weavers of fine linen will turn pale. 10 Her foundations will be crushed, and all who labor for wages will be grieved.

The Collapse of Egyptian Wisdom

11 The princes of Zoan are fools, and Pharaoh's wise counselors proffer witless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, "I am a son of the wise, a son of ancient kings"? 12 Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and disclose what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.

13 The princes of Zoan have become fools; the princes of Memphis are deceived, and the leaders of her tribes have led Egypt astray. 14 The LORD has poured out upon her a spirit of confusion, so that they have made Egypt stagger in all her undertakings, as a drunkard staggers in his own vomit. 15 There will be nothing Egypt can accomplish—neither by its leaders nor by its common people, neither by the strong nor by the weak.

Egypt's Humiliation and Future Redemption

16 In that day the Egyptians will be overcome by fearful helplessness; they will tremble and be terrified before the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, which He brandishes over them. 17 The land of Judah will become a terror to Egypt; everyone who mentions it will be afraid, because of the plan that the LORD of Hosts has devised against them.

18 In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts; one will be called the City of Destruction. 19 In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the heart of Egypt, and a monument to the LORD near its border. 20 It will be a sign and witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt; when they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, He will send them a Savior and Defender, and He will rescue them. 21 The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and in that day the Egyptians will acknowledge the LORD. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings; they will make vows to the LORD and fulfill them. 22 The LORD will strike Egypt with a plague; He will strike and heal. They will turn to the LORD, and He will respond to their pleas and heal them.

23 In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will go to Egypt, and the Egyptians to Assyria, and the Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together. 24 In that day Israel will be the third, along with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth. 25 The LORD of Hosts will bless them, saying, "Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria My handiwork, and Israel My inheritance."
