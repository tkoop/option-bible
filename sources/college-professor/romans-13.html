Submission to Governing Authorities

1 Every soul must submit to the governing authorities, for no authority exists save that which derives from God, and those authorities that do exist stand ordained by God. 2 Consequently, whoever arrays himself against authority opposes the divine ordinance, and those who set themselves in opposition shall bring judgment upon themselves.

3 For rulers are not a terror to good conduct but to evil. Do you desire to be free from fear of authority? Practice the good, and you shall garner praise from it. 4 For he is God’s minister unto your good; yet if you perpetrate evil, fear, for he does not wield the sword without cause, since he is God’s minister, an avenger inflicting wrath upon the one who practices evil.

5 Hence necessity compels submission, not merely on account of wrath but also because of conscience. 6 For this reason you likewise render taxes, for they are God’s ministers, devoting themselves assiduously to this very office. 7 Render to all their due: taxes to whom taxes are due, revenue to whom revenue, respect to whom respect, honor to whom honor.

The Debt of Love and the Fulfillment of the Law

8 Let no debt remain outstanding to anyone, save the debt of mutual love; for the one who loves another has fulfilled the law. 9 For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery, You shall not murder, You shall not steal, You shall not covet,” together with any other commandment, are summed up in this saying: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.” 10 Love does no wrong to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.

Wakefulness, Light, and the Lord Jesus Christ

11 Moreover, you know the season, that it is already the hour for you to rouse yourselves from slumber; for now our salvation stands nearer than when we first embraced the faith. 12 The night has advanced, and the day draws nigh. Let us therefore discard the deeds of darkness and array ourselves in the armor of light. 13 Let us comport ourselves honorably, as in the day: not in carousals and intoxication, not in sexual immorality and sensuality, not in strife and jealousy. 14 Rather, clothe yourselves in the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no forethought for the flesh to gratify its lusts.
