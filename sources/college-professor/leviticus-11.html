Divine Commission and the Classification of Terrestrial Beasts

1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them: 2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: This is the law concerning all the beasts upon the earth. 3 Among the beasts, you may eat any creature that has a fully cloven hoof and chews the cud.

4 "Nevertheless, you shall not eat these among the ruminants or among the cloven-hoofed: the camel, for though it ruminates, it does not divide the hoof; it is unclean to you. 5 The rock badger, for it ruminates but does not divide the hoof; it is unclean to you. 6 The hare, for it ruminates but does not divide the hoof; it is unclean to you. 7 And the swine, for it divides the hoof and is cloven-footed, yet does not ruminate; it is unclean to you. 8 Their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch; they are unclean to you.

Aquatic and Avian Restrictions

9 "From all that inhabit the waters, in seas and rivers, you may eat these: every creature that possesses fins and scales. 10 But every creature in seas or rivers that lacks fins and scales—among all swarming aquatic life and every living thing in the waters—is an abomination to you. 11 They shall remain an abomination to you; you shall not eat their flesh, and their carcasses you shall hold in abhorrence. 12 Every aquatic creature lacking fins and scales is detestable to you.

13 "And these you shall hold in abhorrence among the birds; they are not to be eaten, for they are an abomination: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the black vulture; 14 the kite and the falcon according to its kind; 15 every raven according to its kind; 16 the ostrich, the short-eared owl, the gull, and the hawk according to its kind; 17 the little owl, the cormorant, and the great owl; 18 the white owl, the desert owl, and the osprey; 19 the stork according to its kind, the heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.

20 "Every winged insect that goes upon four legs is an abomination to you. 21 Yet you may eat these among the winged insects that go upon four legs: those possessing jointed legs above their feet, wherewith to leap upon the earth. 22 Namely, the locust according to its kind, the katydid according to its kind, the cricket according to its kind, and the grasshopper according to its kind. 23 But every other winged insect with four feet is detestable to you.

Carcass Contamination and Ritual Purification

24 "By these you shall be rendered unclean: whoever touches their carcass shall be unclean until evening. 25 And whoever bears any part of their carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening. 26 Every beast that has a hoof without a cloven cleft, or that does not ruminate, is unclean to you; whoever touches them becomes unclean. 27 Every quadruped that walks upon its paws, among all quadrupedal creatures, is unclean to you; whoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening. 28 And he who carries its carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening; they are unclean to you.

29 "And these are unclean to you among the swarming things that swarm upon the earth: the mole rat, the mouse, and the great lizard according to its kind; 30 the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon. 31 These are unclean to you among all swarming things; whoever touches them after they die shall be unclean until evening. 32 And upon whatever any of them falls when dead, it becomes unclean—whether a wooden vessel, a garment, a skin, or a sack—every vessel employed for work; it must be brought into water and remains unclean until evening, after which it becomes clean. 33 If one of them falls into an earthen vessel, everything within it becomes unclean, and you shall break the vessel. 34 Any food that may be eaten, if water has touched it, becomes unclean; and any drink that may be drunk from any vessel becomes unclean. 35 Moreover, everything upon which a carcass falls becomes unclean; an oven or stove shall be demolished; they are unclean and shall be unclean to you. 36 Nevertheless, a spring or a cistern of water remains clean, though whoever touches their carcass becomes unclean.

37 "If a carcass falls upon seed intended for sowing, it remains clean; 38 but if water has been put upon the seed and a carcass falls upon it, it is unclean to you. 39 If any beast that you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening. 40 And he who eats of its carcass, or carries its carcass, shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening.

Swarming Creatures and the Call to Holiness

41 "Every swarming thing that swarms upon the earth is detestable; it shall not be eaten. 42 You shall not eat any swarming creature that moves on the earth—whether it creeps upon its belly, walks on four legs, or has many feet—for they are detestable. 43 Do not render your souls abominable by any swarming creature, nor defile yourselves through them, lest you become defiled.

44 "For I am the LORD your God. Consecrate yourselves therefore, and be holy, for I am holy; and do not defile yourselves with any swarming thing that moves upon the earth. 45 For I am the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; you shall therefore be holy, for I am holy. 46 This is the law concerning beasts and birds and every living creature that moves in the waters and every creature that swarms upon the earth, 47 to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the living thing that may be eaten and the living thing that may not be eaten."
