The LORD’s Servant and His Redemptive Mission

“1 Behold My Servant, whom I uphold; My Chosen One, in whom My soul delights. I have placed My Spirit upon Him, and He shall promulgate justice among the nations. 2 He shall not cry out, nor lift up His voice, nor make Himself heard in the thoroughfares. 3 A bruised reed He shall not shatter, nor will He extinguish a dimly burning wick; He shall establish justice with fidelity. 4 He will neither falter nor be crushed until He has established justice upon the earth, and the remotest coastlands shall await His law.”

“5 Thus says God, the LORD, who created the heavens and stretched them out, who spread forth the earth and its offspring, who gives breath to the people upon it and spirit to those who walk therein. 6 I, the LORD, have summoned You in righteousness; I will grasp Your hand, preserve You, and constitute You as a covenant for the people, a light unto the nations, 7 to open the eyes of the blind, to liberate captives from confinement, and to bring forth from the dungeon those who dwell in darkness. 8 I am the LORD; that is My name. My glory I will not yield to another, nor My praise to graven images. 9 Behold, the former things have come to pass; now I announce new things. Before they spring into being, I proclaim them to you.”

A New Song and the LORD’s Zealous Theophany

10 Sing to the LORD a new song; let His praise resound from the ends of the earth—you who descend to the sea and all its fullness, the coastlands and their dwellers. 11 Let the wilderness and its towns raise their voices; let the settlements of Kedar exult; let the inhabitants of Sela shout for joy; let them cry out from the mountain peaks. 12 Let them ascribe glory to the LORD and proclaim His praise throughout the coastlands.

13 The LORD marches forth like a mighty warrior; like a man of war He rouses His zeal. He shouts—indeed, He raises a war cry—and He triumphs over His adversaries.

“14 Long have I held My peace; I have been still and restrained Myself. But now I will cry out like a woman in travail; I will gasp and pant together. 15 I will devastate mountains and hills, and wither all their vegetation; I will transform rivers into islands and desiccate the pools. 16 I will lead the blind along a path they have not known; through unfamiliar paths I will guide them. I will turn darkness into light before them and make the crooked ways level. These things I will accomplish, and I will not abandon them.”

Israel’s Obduracy and Divine Discipline

17 They shall be turned back and utterly put to shame—those who trust in carved images and say to molten idols, “You are our gods.”

“18 Hear, you deaf; behold, you blind, that you may see. 19 Who is blind but My Servant, or deaf as My messenger whom I send? Who is blind like the one consecrated to Me, blind like the Servant of the LORD? 20 You have seen many things, but you have not observed them; your ears are open, yet you do not hear.”

21 The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness, to magnify the law and make it glorious. 22 But this is a people plundered and despoiled; all of them are snared in holes and hidden in prisons. They have become plunder, with no one to rescue them, and spoil, with no one to say, “Restore!” 23 Who among you will give heed to this, who will attend and hear what is to come? 24 Who delivered Jacob to the spoiler and Israel to plunderers? Was it not the LORD, against whom we have sinned, in whose ways they refused to walk, and whose law they would not obey? 25 Therefore He poured out upon him the fury of His anger and the violence of war. It blazed all around him, yet he did not know it; it consumed him, yet he did not lay it to heart.
