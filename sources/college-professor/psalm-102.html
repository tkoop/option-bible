A Prayer of the Afflicted: Lament, Divine Compassion, and the Eternity of the LORD

1 Hear my prayer, O the LORD; let my cry for help come before You. 2 Do not conceal Your countenance from me in the day of my distress; incline Your ear toward me; answer me speedily on the day I call. 3 For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are scorched as though by fire. 4 My heart is blighted and withered like grass, because I have forgotten to eat my bread.

5 At the sound of my groaning, my bones cleave to my flesh. 6 I am likened to a pelican of the wilderness, like an owl dwelling among ruins. 7 I keep vigil and am like a solitary bird upon a rooftop. 8 All day long my enemies reproach me; those who rail against me invoke my name as a curse.

9 For I have eaten ashes as though they were bread, and mingled my drink with tears. 10 Because of Your indignation and wrath, for You have lifted me up and cast me down. 11 My days are like an evening shadow, and I wither like grass.

12 But You, O the LORD, sit enthroned forever, and Your renown endures throughout all generations. 13 You will arise and show compassion to Zion, for it is the season to be gracious to her; the appointed time has arrived. 14 For Your servants cherish her stones and take pity on her dust. 15 The nations will fear the name of the the LORD, and all the kings of the earth will revere Your glory. 16 For the the LORD will rebuild Zion and reveal Himself in His glory. 17 He will attend to the prayer of the destitute and will not despise their supplication.

18 Let this be written for the generation to come, that a people yet to be created may praise the the LORD. 19 For He has looked down from His holy height; from heaven the the LORD has beheld the earth, 20 to hear the groaning of the captive and to release those condemned to death. 21 that the name of the the LORD may be declared in Zion and His praise in Jerusalem, 22 when peoples and kingdoms assemble together to serve the the LORD.

23 He has enfeebled my strength along the way and shortened my days. 24 I say, "O my God, do not take me away in the midst of my days; Your years endure through all generations." 25 Long ago You founded the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands. 26 They shall perish, but You shall remain; they shall all wear out like a garment. You will change them like clothing, and they will be changed. 27 But You are the same, and Your years will never come to an end. 28 The children of Your servants will dwell in Your presence, and their offspring will be established before You.
