The Reign of Manasseh and the Defilement of Judah

1 Manasseh was twelve years of age when he assumed the throne, and he reigned in Jerusalem for fifty-five years; his mother was Hephzibah. 2 He perpetrated what was evil in the sight of the the LORD, emulating the abominations of the nations that the the LORD had dispossessed before the Israelites.

3 He restored the high places that his father Hezekiah had demolished, erected altars to Baal, fashioned an Asherah after the manner of Ahab, king of Israel, and prostrated himself before them in worship and service. 4 He likewise constructed altars within the house of the the LORD, concerning which the the LORD had declared that He would establish His Name in Jerusalem. 5 In the two courts of the the LORD’s house he erected altars to the entire celestial host. 6 He even sacrificed his son by fire, practiced sorcery and divination, consulted mediums and spiritists, and thereby multiplied evil in the sight of the the LORD, provoking Him to anger.

7 He installed the carved image of the Asherah that he had fabricated within the house concerning which the the LORD had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, I will put My Name forever. 8 I will not again cause the feet of Israel to wander from the land that I gave to their fathers, provided they are meticulous in doing all that I have commanded them and all the law that My servant Moses commanded them." 9 Nevertheless, they did not listen; Manasseh led them astray, so that they acted more wickedly than the nations whom the the LORD had destroyed before the Israelites.

The Prophetic Indictment and the Decree of Judgment

10 The the LORD spoke through His servants the prophets, saying, 11 "Because Manasseh, king of Judah, has committed these abominations—acting more wickedly than the Amorites who preceded him—and has caused Judah to sin through his idols, 12 therefore thus says the the LORD, the God of Israel: Behold, I am bringing calamity upon Jerusalem and Judah, so dreadful that every hearer’s ears will tingle. 13 I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria and the plumb line of the house of Ahab, and I will wipe out Jerusalem as one wipes a dish—wiping it and turning it upside down. 14 I will forsake the remnant of My inheritance and deliver them into the hand of their enemies, so that they become plunder and spoil to all their adversaries. 15 For they have done evil in My eyes and have provoked Me from the day their fathers departed from Egypt until this day."

16 Moreover, Manasseh spilled so much innocent blood that he filled Jerusalem from one end to the other, apart from the sin whereby he caused Judah to sin, doing evil in the sight of the the LORD. 17 As for the remaining acts of Manasseh, all that he did and the sin he committed, are they not recorded in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 18 Manasseh died and was buried with his fathers in the garden of his house, the garden of Uzza; and his son Amon reigned in his stead.

The Brief and Violent Reign of Amon

19 Amon was twenty-two years of age when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for two years. His mother was Meshullemeth, daughter of Haruz of Jotbah. 20 He did evil in the sight of the the LORD, as his father Manasseh had done. 21 He walked in every way his father had walked, serving the idols his father had served and bowing down to them. 22 He abandoned the the LORD, the God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the the LORD.

23 The servants of Amon conspired against him and assassinated the king in his own house. 24 But the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and the people installed his son Josiah as king in his place. 25 As for the remaining acts of Amon, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 26 He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and his son Josiah reigned in his place.
