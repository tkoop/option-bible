Prophecy against Gog, Prince of Meshech and Tubal

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying:

2 "Son of man, set your face toward Gog, in the land of Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy against him. 3 Say, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: "Behold, I am against you, O Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. 4 I will turn you about, fasten hooks in your jaws, and drag you forth with all your host—horses and riders, every one accoutred in panoply, a vast assemblage bearing buckler and shield, all of them wielding swords. 5 Persia, Cush, and Put are in their ranks, all equipped with shield and helmet. 6 Gomer and all its hordes, Beth-togarmah from the uttermost north and all its hordes—many peoples are allied with you. 7 Be at the ready; indeed, make yourself ready—you and all your company mustered about you—and assume command over them.'"

Gog's Invasion in the Latter Years

8 "After many days you will be summoned. In the latter years you will invade the land recovered from the sword, gathered from many peoples, upon the mountains of Israel, which had long been a desolation; yet its people were brought forth from the nations, and now they dwell in security, every one of them. 9 You will advance, coming like a tempest; you will be like a cloud enveloping the land—you and all your troops, and many peoples with you."

10 "Thus says the Lord GOD: On that day thoughts will arise in your heart, and you will devise a nefarious scheme. 11 You will say,

"'I will march against a land of unfortified villages; I will come upon those who are tranquil, who dwell in security, all of them living without walls and having neither bars nor gates'—

12 "to seize spoil and carry off plunder, to turn your hand against the desolate places now inhabited, and against a people gathered from the nations, who have amassed livestock and goods and dwell in the heart of the earth. 13 Sheba, Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish with all its villages will say to you,

"'Have you come to seize spoil? Have you mustered your company to take plunder, to carry away silver and gold, to take away livestock and goods, to take great spoil?'"

The LORD's Summons and Vindication

14 "Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: On that day when My people Israel are dwelling in security, will you not take notice? 15 You will come from your place, from the farthest north, you and many peoples with you, all of them riding on horses—a vast company and a mighty army. 16 You will advance against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. In the latter days, O Gog, I will bring you against My land, so that the nations may know Me when I am sanctified in you before their eyes.'"

Divine Judgment and Self-Revelation

17 "Thus says the Lord GOD: Are you the one of whom I spoke in former days through My servants, the prophets of Israel, who prophesied in those days for years that I would bring you against them? 18 On that day, when Gog comes against the land of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, My wrath will flare up in My anger. 19 For in My zeal and in the fire of My wrath I have spoken: Surely on that day there will be a great earthquake in the land of Israel. 20 The fish of the sea, the birds of the heavens, the beasts of the field, all creeping things that creep upon the earth, and all mankind upon the face of the earth will tremble at My presence. The mountains will be overturned, the cliffs will collapse, and every wall will fall to the ground. 21 I will summon a sword against him throughout all My mountains, declares the Lord GOD. Every man's sword will be against his brother. 22 I will enter into judgment with him by pestilence and bloodshed, and I will rain down upon him, upon his troops, and upon the many peoples who are with him torrential rain, hailstones, fire, and brimstone. 23 Thus I will magnify Myself and sanctify Myself, and I will make Myself known in the sight of many nations; and they will know that I am the LORD."
