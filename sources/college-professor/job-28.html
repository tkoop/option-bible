The Subterranean Extraction of Earth's Hidden Treasures

1 Indeed, silver possesses its source, and gold has its appointed place of refinement. 2 Iron is wrested from the dust, and copper is smelted from the rock. 3 He imposes an end upon darkness and probes to the utmost extremity every stone shrouded in gloom and the shadow of death.

4 He cuts a shaft far from human habitation, in regions forgotten by the foot; there men dangle and sway, far from their fellows. 5 Bread comes forth from the earth, yet beneath it the ground is upheaved as though by fire. 6 Its stones are the very seat of sapphires, and its dust contains gold.

7 No bird of prey knows that path, and no falcon's eye has beheld it. 8 The proud beasts have not trodden it, nor has the lion passed over it.

9 He lays his hand upon the flint and overturns mountains from their roots. 10 He hews channels through the rock, and his eye discerns every precious thing. 11 He dams the streams so that they do not trickle, and brings the hidden thing into the light.

Wisdom's Elusiveness Beyond Material Wealth

12 Yet where shall wisdom be found, and where is the place of understanding? 13 Mortals do not know its worth, nor is it found in the land of the living.

14 The deep says, "It is not in me."

And the sea says, "It is not with me."

15 It cannot be bought with gold, nor can silver be weighed out as its price. 16 It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir, with precious onyx, or with sapphire.

17 Gold and glass cannot equal it, nor can it be exchanged for vessels of fine gold. 18 Coral and crystal are not to be mentioned; the price of wisdom exceeds that of pearls.

19 The topaz of Cush cannot be compared with it, nor can it be valued in pure gold. 20 Whence, then, comes wisdom, and where is the place of understanding?

21 It is concealed from the eyes of all the living and hidden from the birds of the heavens. 22 Abaddon and Death say, "We have heard a rumor of it with our ears."

Divine Omniscience and the Fear of the Lord

23 God understands its way, and He knows its place. 24 For He looks to the ends of the earth and sees all that is beneath the heavens.

25 When He assigned weight to the wind and measured out the waters, 26 when He made a decree for the rain and a path for the thunderbolt, 27 then He saw it and declared it; He established it and investigated it.

28 And He said to man, "Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom, and to turn away from evil is understanding."
