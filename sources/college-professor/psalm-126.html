A Song of Ascents: Zion’s Restoration and the Harvest of Joy

1 When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dream. 2 Then our mouths were filled with laughter, and our tongues with songs of joy; then it was said among the nations, “The LORD has done great things for them.” 3 The LORD has done great things for us; we are glad.

4 Restore our fortunes, O the LORD, like torrents in the Negev! 5 Those who sow in tears shall reap with shouts of joy! 6 He who goes out weeping, carrying seed for sowing, shall come home with shouts of joy, bearing his sheaves.
