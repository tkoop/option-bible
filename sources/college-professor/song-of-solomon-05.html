The Bridegroom's Garden and Nuptial Feast

1 I have come into my garden, my sister, my bride; I have gathered my myrrh with my spice; I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey; I have drunk my wine with my milk. Eat, O companions; drink, and be intoxicated, O lovers.

The Bride's Restless Night and Search

2 I slept, but my heart was awake. A sound! My beloved is knocking.

"Open to me, my sister, my darling, my dove, my flawless one, for my head is drenched with dew, my tresses with the drops of night."

3 I have removed my garment; how am I to don it anew? I have cleansed my feet; how am I to soil them? 4 My beloved thrust his hand through the latch-opening, and my inmost being was stirred for him.

5 I rose to open to my beloved, and my hands dripped with myrrh, my fingers with liquid myrrh, upon the handles of the bolt. 6 I opened to my beloved, but my beloved had withdrawn and gone. My spirit faltered when he spoke. I sought him, but did not find him; I called him, but he gave no answer.

7 The watchmen who go about the city found me; they struck me, they wounded me; they took away my veil from me, those watchmen of the walls.

The Daughters of Jerusalem and the Bride's Adjuration

8 I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my beloved, what will you tell him?—that I am faint with love.

9 What is your beloved more than another beloved, O fairest among women? What is your beloved more than another beloved, that you so adjure us?

The Bride's Panegyric of Her Beloved

10 My beloved is radiant and ruddy, preeminent among ten thousand.

11 His head is finest gold; his locks are wavy, black as a raven.

12 His eyes are like doves beside channels of water, washed in milk, resting beside a brimming pool.

13 His cheeks are like beds of spices, towers of perfumes; his lips are lilies, dropping liquid myrrh.

14 His arms are rods of gold set with topaz; his torso is polished ivory inlaid with sapphires.

15 His legs are pillars of alabaster founded upon bases of gold; his aspect is like Lebanon, select as the cedars.

16 His palate is sweetness itself, and he is altogether desirable. This is my beloved, and this is my friend, O daughters of Jerusalem.
