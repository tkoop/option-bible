The Shunammite’s Return and the Restoration of Her Estate

1 Elisha had spoken to the woman whose son he had restored to life, saying, “Arise, go you and your household, and sojourn wherever you are able to sojourn, for the LORD has summoned a famine, and it is coming upon the land for seven years.” 2 The woman arose and acted in accordance with the word of the man of God; she departed with her household and sojourned in the land of the Philistines for seven years.

3 When the seven years had run their course, the woman returned from the land of the Philistines and went out to the king to cry out concerning her house and her field.

4 Now the king was speaking to Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, saying, “Please recount to me all the great deeds that Elisha has performed.” 5 While he was recounting to the king how he had restored the dead to life, behold, the woman whose son he had restored to life came crying to the king about her house and her field. Gehazi said, “My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha restored to life.” 6 The king inquired of the woman, and she recounted it to him; then the king appointed to her a certain officer, saying, “Restore to her all that was hers, together with all the produce of the field, from the day she left the land until now.”

Hazael’s Destiny Revealed in Damascus

7 Elisha went to Damascus while Ben-hadad, king of Aram, was ill, and it was reported to him, saying, “The man of God has come here.” 8 The king said to Hazael, “Take a gift in your hand and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the LORD through him, saying, ‘Shall I recover from this sickness?’” 9 So Hazael went to meet him, taking in his hand a gift of forty camel-loads and every good thing of Damascus, and he came and stood before him and said, “Your son Ben-hadad, king of Aram, has sent me to you, saying, ‘Shall I recover from this sickness?’”

10 Elisha said to him, “Go, say to him, ‘You shall certainly recover,’ but the LORD has shown me that he shall surely die.” 11 He fixed his gaze upon him and held it until he was ashamed, and the man of God wept. 12 Hazael said, “Why does my lord weep?” He said, “Because I know the evil that you will do to the children of Israel: you will set their fortresses on fire, you will slay their young men with the sword, you will dash their little ones to pieces, and you will rip open their pregnant women.” 13 Hazael said, “But what is your servant, this dog, that he should do so great a thing?” Elisha said, “The LORD has shown me that you are to be king over Aram.”

14 So he departed from Elisha and came to his master, who said to him, “What did Elisha say to you?” And he said, “He said to me, ‘You shall certainly recover.’” 15 On the next day he took the thick cloth and dipped it in water and spread it over his face, so that he died; and Hazael reigned in his place.

Jehoram of Judah and the Revolt of Edom

16 In the fifth year of Joram the son of Ahab, king of Israel—Jehoshaphat being then king of Judah—Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat began to reign over Judah. 17 He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem. 18 He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD. 19 Yet the LORD was unwilling to destroy Judah, for the sake of David His servant, as He had promised to give him a lamp for his sons always.

20 In his days Edom revolted from under Judah’s rule and set up a king over themselves. 21 Then Joram crossed over to Zair with all his chariots; by night he arose and struck down the Edomites who surrounded him and the commanders of the chariots, but the troops fled to their tents. 22 So Edom has remained in revolt against Judah to this day, and at the same time Libnah revolted. 23 As for the rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 24 Joram slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the City of David; and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

Ahaziah’s Brief Reign and Alliance with Israel

25 In the twelfth year of Joram the son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah the son of Jehoram, king of Judah, began to reign. 26 Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri, king of Israel. 27 He walked in the way of the house of Ahab and did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, like the house of Ahab, for he was son-in-law to the house of Ahab. 28 He went with Joram the son of Ahab to war against Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram. 29 King Joram returned to Jezreel to be healed of the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael, king of Aram; and Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Joram son of Ahab, because he was sick.
