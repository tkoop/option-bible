The Valley of Dry Bones

1 The hand of the LORD was upon me, and the LORD brought me out by the Spirit and set me down in the midst of the valley, which was full of bones. 2 He led me all around among them, and, behold, there were very many on the surface of the valley, and, behold, they were exceedingly dry.

3 He said to me, "Son of man, can these bones live?"

And I answered, "O Lord GOD, You know."

4 Then He said to me, "Prophesy over these bones and say to them, 'O dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.'

5 Thus says the Lord GOD to these bones: "Behold, I will cause breath to enter you, and you shall live. 6 I will put sinews upon you and bring flesh upon you and cover you with skin and put breath in you, and you shall live, and you shall know that I am the LORD."

7 So I prophesied as I was commanded; and as I prophesied, there was a sound, and, behold, a rattling, and the bones came together, bone to its bone. 8 And I looked, and, behold, sinews were on them, and flesh had come up, and skin had covered them above; but there was no breath in them.

9 Then He said to me, "Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, Son of man, and say to the breath, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe on these slain, that they may live.'

10 So I prophesied as He commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived and stood on their feet, an exceedingly great army.

11 Then He said to me, "Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. Behold, they say, 'Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost; we are cut off.'

12 Therefore prophesy and say to them, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I will open your graves and raise you from your graves, O My people, and I will bring you into the land of Israel. 13 And you shall know that I am the LORD, when I open your graves and raise you from your graves, O My people. 14 I will put My Spirit in you, and you shall live, and I will place you on your own land. Then you shall know that I am the LORD; I have spoken, and I will do it, declares the LORD.'

The Sign of the Two Sticks

15 The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

16 "And you, Son of man, take for yourself one stick and write on it, 'For Judah and for the children of Israel, his companions'; then take another stick and write on it, 'For Joseph—the stick of Ephraim—and for all the house of Israel, his companions.' 17 And join them to one another into one stick, that they may become one in your hand."

18 And when the children of your people say to you, 'Will you not tell us what these things mean to you?'

19 say to them, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am taking the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel, his companions, and I will put them on the stick of Judah and make them one stick, and they shall be one in My hand.' 20 The sticks on which you write shall be in your hand before their eyes.

The Reunited Kingdom and Everlasting Covenant

21 And say to them, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I will take the children of Israel from among the nations to which they have gone, and I will gather them from all around and bring them into their own land. 22 I will make them one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel, and one king shall be king over all of them; they shall no longer be two nations, and they shall no longer be divided into two kingdoms. 23 They shall no longer defile themselves with their idols, their detestable things, and all their transgressions; and I will save them from all their dwelling places in which they have sinned, and I will cleanse them; and they shall be My people, and I will be their God. 24 And My servant David shall be king over them, and there shall be one shepherd over all of them; they shall walk in My judgments and keep My statutes and observe them. 25 They shall dwell in the land that I gave to My servant Jacob, in which your fathers dwelt; and they shall dwell in it, they, their children, and their children's children, forever; and My servant David shall be their prince forever. 26 I will make a covenant of peace with them; it shall be an everlasting covenant with them; and I will establish them and multiply them, and I will set My sanctuary in their midst forever. 27 My dwelling place shall be with them; I will be their God, and they shall be My people. 28 Then the nations shall know that I am the LORD who sanctifies Israel, when My sanctuary is in their midst forever.'
