Salutation and Thanksgiving for Truthful Conduct

1 The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in the truth—not I alone, but likewise all who have come to know the truth— 2 on account of the truth abiding in us and destined to remain with us in perpetuity. 3 Grace, mercy, and peace shall be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

4 I rejoiced exceedingly upon discovering that some of your children conduct themselves in the truth, precisely as we received commandment from the Father.

The Imperative of Mutual Love

5 And now I entreat you, lady, not as though I were inscribing a novel injunction for you, but rather the one we have possessed from the beginning: that we love one another. 6 And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments; this is the commandment, even as you heard from the beginning, that you walk in it.

Admonition Against Antichristian Deceivers

7 For many deceivers have issued forth into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in flesh; this one is the deceiver and the antichrist. 8 Take heed to yourselves, that you do not forfeit what we have labored to accomplish, but that you may receive a complete recompense. 9 Everyone who advances beyond and does not abide in the teaching of Christ possesses no God; the one who abides in the teaching—this one possesses both the Father and the Son.

10 If anyone comes to you and does not bear this teaching, do not receive him into your house, and do not extend a salutation to him. 11 For the one who extends a salutation to him participates in his wicked works.

Epistolary Conclusion and Valediction

12 Having numerous matters to inscribe to you, I was disinclined to do so through paper and ink; instead, I hope to arrive and discourse face to face, so that our joy may be made full. 13 The children of your elect sister extend greetings to you.
