The Majesty of the Creator and His Handiwork

1 Bless the LORD, O my soul. O the LORD my God, You are surpassingly great; You are clothed with splendor and majesty. 2 He wraps Himself in light as with a garment; He stretches out the heavens like a tent. 3 He lays the beams of His upper chambers in the waters; He makes the clouds His chariot; He walks upon the wings of the wind. 4 He makes the winds His messengers, flames of fire His ministers.

The Foundations of Earth and Sea

5 He founded the earth upon its foundations, that it should not be moved forever and ever. 6 You covered it with the deep as with a garment; the waters stood above the mountains. 7 At your rebuke they fled; at the thunder of your voice they hastened away. 8 The mountains rose up; the valleys sank down to the place You had appointed for them. 9 You set a boundary they shall not transgress, nor shall they return to overwhelm the earth.

Providence, Order, and the Sustenance of Life

10 He sends forth springs into the watercourses; they wind among the hills. 11 They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild asses slake their thirst. 12 The birds of the heavens make their abode beside them and utter their voices among the foliage. 13 He waters the mountains from His upper chambers; the earth is satiated with the fruit of His works. 14 He causes grass to spring up for the cattle, and herbage for the service of man, that he may bring forth bread from the earth, 15 and wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil to make his face radiant, and bread that fortifies the heart.

16 The trees of the the LORD are abundantly watered, the cedars of Lebanon that He planted. 17 There the birds build their nests; the stork makes its dwelling in the fir trees. 18 The high mountains are for the wild goats; the crags are a refuge for the rock badgers. 19 He appointed the moon for seasons; the sun knows its setting. 20 You make darkness, and it becomes night, when all the beasts of the forest creep forth. 21 The young lions roar after their prey, seeking their food from God. 22 When the sun rises, they withdraw and lie down in their lairs. 23 Man goes forth to his work and to his labor until evening.

The Wisdom of the LORD and the Praise of His People

24 How manifold are your works, O the LORD! In wisdom You have made them all; the earth is full of your creatures. 25 There is the sea, vast and spacious, wherein are innumerable creeping things, beasts both small and great. 26 There go the ships; Leviathan, which You formed to sport in it. 27 All of these look to You to give them their food in due season. 28 When You give to them, they gather it up; when You open your hand, they are filled with good things. 29 When You hide your face, they are dismayed; when You take away their breath, they expire and return to their dust. 30 When You send forth your Spirit, they are created, and You renew the face of the ground.

31 May the glory of the the LORD endure forever; may the the LORD rejoice in His works. 32 He looks upon the earth, and it trembles; He touches the mountains, and they smoke. 33 I will sing to the the LORD throughout my life; I will sing praise to my God while I have being. 34 May my meditation be pleasing to Him; I will rejoice in the the LORD. 35 May sinners be consumed from the earth, and the wicked be no more. Bless the the LORD, O my soul. Praise the the LORD!
