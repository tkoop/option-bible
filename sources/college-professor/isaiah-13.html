Oracle Concerning Babylon

1 An oracle concerning Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz beheld. 2 Raise a banner upon the barren height; call aloud to them; wave your hand, that they may enter the gates of the nobles.

3 I have commanded My consecrated ones; I have likewise summoned My warriors to execute My wrath—those who exult in My majesty. 4 A tumult resounds upon the mountains, like that of a great multitude; an uproar among the kingdoms, like nations assembled together. The LORD of Hosts is mustering a host for war. 5 They come from a distant land, from the ends of heaven—the the LORD and the instruments of His wrath—to devastate the whole earth.

The Day of the LORD

6 Wail, for the day of the the LORD is near; it comes as devastation from the Almighty. 7 Therefore every hand will grow feeble, and every human heart will melt away. 8 They will be seized with terror; pangs and anguish will grip them. They will writhe like a woman in labor, staring at one another in astonishment, their faces aflame.

9 Behold, the day of the the LORD comes—cruel, with wrath and fierce anger—to render the earth a desolation and to exterminate its sinners from it. 10 For the stars of heaven and their constellations will not give their light; the sun will be darkened as it rises, and the moon will not shed its light.

11 I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity; I will bring to an end the arrogance of the proud and abase the haughtiness of the ruthless. 12 I will make mortals rarer than pure gold, and humankind rarer than the gold of Ophir. 13 Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth will be displaced from its place, because of the wrath of the the LORD of Hosts and the day of His fierce anger.

The Flight and Slaughter of the Nations

14 And it will be as with a hunted gazelle, or as sheep without a shepherd: each will turn to his own people, and each will flee to his own land. 15 Everyone who is found will be pierced; everyone who is captured will fall by the sword. 16 Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses will be plundered, and their wives will be ravished.

The Medes and the Desolation of Babylon

17 Behold, I am stirring up the Medes against them—men who have no regard for silver and no delight in gold. 18 Their bows will dash the young men to pieces; they will show no mercy to the fruit of the womb, and their eyes will not pity the children.

19 Babylon, the jewel of kingdoms, the glory and pride of the Chaldeans, will be overthrown by God like Sodom and Gomorrah. 20 It will never be inhabited or settled from generation to generation; no Arab will pitch his tent there, and no shepherd will make his flock lie down there. 21 But desert creatures will lie down there, and their houses will be full of owls; ostriches will dwell there, and wild goats will frolic there. 22 Hyenas will howl in their citadels, and jackals in their luxurious palaces. Her time is near, and her days will not be prolonged.
