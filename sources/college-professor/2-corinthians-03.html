Paul’s Commendation Inscribed upon Hearts

1 Are we commencing anew to commend ourselves, or do we require—as certain persons do—letters of commendation addressed to you or emanating from you? 2 You constitute our epistle, engraved within our hearts, recognized and perused by all humanity, 3 being manifested that you are an epistle of Christ, ministered through us, inscribed not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not upon tablets of stone but upon tablets of fleshly hearts.

4 Such confidence we possess toward God through Christ. 5 Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to reckon anything as deriving from ourselves, but our sufficiency is from God, 6 who has also rendered us sufficient ministers of a new covenant—not of the letter but of the Spirit—for the letter kills, whereas the Spirit vivifies.

The Surpassing Glory of the New Covenant

7 For if the ministry of death, engraved in letters upon stones, arrived in glory, so that the sons of Israel could not fix their gaze upon the face of Moses because of the glory of his countenance—a glory being brought to naught— 8 how shall not rather the ministry of the Spirit be in glory? 9 For if the ministry of condemnation possesses glory, how much more does the ministry of righteousness abound in glory. 10 For even that which was glorified has not been glorified in this respect, on account of the superabundant glory. 11 For if that which is being brought to naught was through glory, how much more is that which remains in glory.

The Veil Removed and the Spirit’s Transforming Freedom

12 Therefore, possessing such hope, we employ great boldness, 13 and not as Moses placed a veil over his face, so that the sons of Israel might not gaze upon the consummation of that which is being abolished. 14 But their minds were hardened; for until this very day the same veil remains upon the reading of the old covenant, not being unveiled, because in Christ it is abolished. 15 But until today, whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their heart. 16 But whenever one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed. 17 Now the Lord is the Spirit; and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty. 18 And we all, with unveiled face, beholding as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transfigured into the same image from glory to glory, even as from the Lord Spirit.
