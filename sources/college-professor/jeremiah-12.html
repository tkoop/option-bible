Jeremiah’s Perplexity over the Prosperity of the Wicked

1 Righteous are You, O the LORD, when I plead my cause before You; yet I would reason with You concerning justice: why does the way of the wicked prosper, and why do all the treacherous live at ease? 2 You have planted them, and they have struck root; they have grown and even borne fruit. You are ever upon their lips, yet remote from their inmost being.

3 But You know me, O the LORD; You see me and test my heart toward You. Drag them away like sheep to the slaughter, and set them apart for the day of slaughter. 4 How long must the land mourn and the grass of every field wither? Because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it, beasts and birds are swept away; for they say, “He will not see our final end.”

5 If you have run with footmen and they have wearied you, how then will you compete with horses? If you have stumbled in a land of peace, what will you do in the thickets of the Jordan? 6 For even your brothers and your father’s house—even they have betrayed you; even they have cried out after you. Do not trust them, though they speak kindly to you.

The LORD’s Lament over His Inheritance

“7 I have forsaken My house; I have abandoned My inheritance. I have given the beloved of My soul into the hands of her enemies. 8 My inheritance has become to Me like a lion in the forest; she has raised her voice against Me; therefore I have hated her.

“9 Is not My inheritance to Me like a speckled bird of prey, with other birds of prey circling against her? Go, gather all the beasts of the field; bring them to devour her. 10 Many shepherds have destroyed My vineyard; they have trampled My plot of land. They have turned My pleasant field into a desolate wasteland. 11 They have made it a desolation; desolate, it mourns to Me. The whole land is laid waste, because no one takes it to heart.

“12 On all the barren heights in the wilderness destroyers have come, for the sword of the the LORD devours from one end of the earth to the other. No one has peace. 13 They sowed wheat but harvested thorns; they exhausted themselves to no avail. Be ashamed of your harvests because of the fierce anger of the the LORD.”

Oracle Against the Neighboring Nations

“14 Thus says the the LORD concerning all My wicked neighbors who touch the inheritance that I gave My people Israel: Behold, I will uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them. 15 And after I have uprooted them, I will again have compassion on them and bring them back, each to his inheritance and each to his land.

“16 And if they will diligently learn the ways of My people—to swear by My name, ‘As the the LORD lives,’ just as they taught My people to swear by Baal—then they will be established among My people. 17 But if they will not obey, then I will uproot that nation and destroy it, declares the the LORD.”
