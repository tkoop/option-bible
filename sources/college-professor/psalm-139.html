The All-Seeing Sovereign and His Intimate Knowledge

1 The LORD, You have searched me and known me. 2 You know my sitting down and my rising up; You understand my thoughts from afar. 3 You scrutinize my path and my reclining, and You are intimately acquainted with all my ways. 4 For no utterance yet rests upon my tongue, but behold, the LORD, You know it in its entirety. 5 You have hemmed me in behind and before, and laid Your hand upon me. 6 Such knowledge is too wondrous for me; it is exalted, and I cannot attain unto it.

The Inescapable Presence of the Divine

7 Whither shall I go from Your Spirit? Or whither shall I flee from Your presence? 8 If I ascend into heaven, You are there; if I spread my couch in Sheol, behold, You are there. 9 If I take the wings of the dawn, if I dwell at the sea's farthest bound, 10 even there Your hand shall guide me, and Your right hand shall hold me fast. 11 If I should say, "Surely darkness shall cover me, and the light turn to night about me," 12 even the darkness is not dark to You; the night is bright as the day, for darkness is as light to You.

Fearfully and Wonderfully Fashioned

13 For You formed my inmost parts; You wove me together in my mother's womb. 14 I will praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made; wondrous are Your works, and my soul knows it full well. 15 My frame was not hidden from You when I was fashioned in secret, intricately wrought in the depths of the earth. 16 Your eyes beheld my unformed substance; in Your book were written all the days fashioned for me, before any of them came to pass. 17 How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God! How vast is the sum of them! 18 Were I to count them, they would outnumber the sand; when I awake, I am still with You.

Zeal for the LORD and a Prayer for Purity

19 O that You would slay the wicked, O God! Depart from me, you men of bloodshed! 20 They speak against You with malicious intent; Your enemies misuse Your name. 21 Do I not hate those who hate You, the LORD? And do I not contend with those who rise up against You? 22 I hate them with a perfect hatred; I count them my foes. 23 Search me, O God, and know my heart; try me, and know my anxious thoughts. 24 And see whether any way of grief is in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.
