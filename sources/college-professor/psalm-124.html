A Song of Ascents; of David

1 If the LORD had not been our ally—let Israel now declare— 2 if the LORD had not been our ally when mortals rose up against us, 3 then, when their wrath flared against us, they would have swallowed us alive.

4 Then the waters would have overwhelmed us, and the torrent would have swept over our soul; 5 then the arrogant waters would have passed over our soul.

Praise for the LORD's Deliverance

6 Blessed be the LORD, who has not consigned us to be prey to their teeth. 7 Our soul has escaped like a bird from the fowler's snare; the snare is shattered, and we have been delivered.

8 Our help rests in the name of the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
