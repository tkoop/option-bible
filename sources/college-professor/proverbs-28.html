The Flight of the Wicked and the Courage of the Righteous

1 The wicked flee though no pursuer is at their heels, whereas the righteous possess a leonine intrepidity. 2 When a realm transgresses, it proliferates rulers; yet a person endowed with understanding and knowledge perpetuates its order. 3 An impoverished oppressor of the poor resembles a torrential rain that devastates the harvest and leaves no sustenance. 4 Those who abandon the law extol the wicked, whereas those who keep the law contend against them.

5 Men of evil do not comprehend justice, but those who seek the LORD apprehend it in its totality. 6 A poor man who walks in integrity surpasses a wealthy man whose conduct is crooked. 7 A son of discernment keeps the law, but one who consorts with gluttons brings shame upon his father.

Wealth, Usury, and the Vindication of the Upright

8 Whoever augments his wealth through usury and interest ultimately accumulates it for one who will be gracious to the poor. 9 Whoever refuses to heed the law renders even his prayer an abomination. 10 Whoever seduces the upright into an evil course plummets into his own pit, while the blameless inherit prosperity.

11 A wealthy man is wise in his own estimation, but a discerning poor man penetrates his pretensions. 12 When the righteous flourish, great glory abounds; but when the wicked ascend, people conceal themselves. 13 Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but whoever confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy. 14 Blessed is the one who continually reveres the LORD, but whoever is obdurately unyielding falls into calamity.

Rulers, Labor, and the Perils of Avarice

15 A wicked ruler over a destitute people is like a roaring lion or a charging bear. 16 A ruler devoid of discernment is a grievous oppressor, but one who abhors illicit gain prolongs his days. 17 A man laden with bloodguilt will flee until death; let no one sustain him.

18 Whoever walks in integrity will be delivered, but whoever is crooked in his ways will fall suddenly. 19 Whoever cultivates his land will have abundant food, but whoever pursues vain fantasies will be sated with poverty. 20 A faithful man will abound in blessings, but one who hastens to be rich will not be held guiltless.

21 To show partiality is not good, yet for a trivial inducement a man will transgress. 22 A stingy man hastens after wealth and remains oblivious to the poverty that will overtake him.

Rebuke, Filial Theft, and the Triumph of the Righteous

23 Whoever rebukes a man will afterward find greater favor than one who flatters with his tongue. 24 Whoever robs his father or mother and declares, “It is no transgression,” is a companion to a destroyer.

25 A greedy man stirs up strife, but whoever trusts in the LORD will flourish. 26 Whoever trusts in his own heart is a fool, but whoever walks in wisdom will be delivered. 27 Whoever gives to the poor will lack nothing, but whoever willfully ignores them will be laden with curses. 28 When the wicked rise, people hide themselves; but when they perish, the righteous increase.
