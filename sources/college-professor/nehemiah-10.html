The Sealed Covenant and Its Signatories

1 Among those who set their seal were Nehemiah the governor, son of Hachaliah, and Zedekiah: 2 Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah; 3 Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah; 4 Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch; 5 Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah; 6 Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch; 7 Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin; 8 Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah—these were the priests.

9 And the Levites: Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, Kadmiel; 10 and their brothers Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan; 11 Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah; 12 Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah; 13 Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

14 The chiefs of the people: Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani; 15 Bunni, Azgad, Bebai; 16 Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin; 17 Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur; 18 Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai; 19 Hariph, Anathoth, Nobai; 20 Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir; 21 Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua; 22 Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah; 23 Hoshea, Hananiah, Hashub; 24 Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek; 25 Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah; 26 Ahijah, Hanan, Anan; 27 Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

The Community’s Solemn Pledge

28 The rest of the people—the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the Law of God—together with their wives and all their sons and daughters capable of discernment, 29 joined their brothers, the nobles, and bound themselves by oath and curse to walk in the Law of God given by the hand of Moses, the servant of God, and to do and to keep all the commandments, judgments, and statutes of the LORD our God. 30 We will not give our daughters in marriage to the peoples of the land, nor take their daughters for our sons. 31 When the peoples of the land bring merchandise or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and in the seventh year we will forgo every debt.

Provisions for the Temple Service

32 We have also obligated ourselves to contribute one-third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God: 33 for the showbread, the continual grain offering and the continual burnt offering, the Sabbath offerings, the new moons, the appointed feasts, the holy offerings, the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of the house of our God. 34 We have cast lots among the priests, the Levites, and the people concerning the wood offering, so that the households of our fathers may bring it at designated times, year after year, to be burned upon the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the Law. 35 We will also bring, year after year, the firstfruits of our soil and the firstfruits of all the fruit of every tree to the house of the LORD. 36 And we will bring the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock—as it is written in the Law—together with the firstborn of our herds and flocks, to the house of our God, to the priests who minister there.

37 Moreover, we will bring to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God, the first of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every tree, the new wine and the oil; and the tithe of our ground to the Levites, for they, the Levites, are the ones who collect the tithes in all the towns where we labor. 38 A priest, a son of Aaron, shall accompany the Levites when they receive the tithe, and the Levites shall bring up a tenth of the tithe to the house of our God, to the chambers of the treasury. 39 For the Israelites and the Levites are to bring the contributions of grain, new wine, and oil to the chambers where the vessels of the sanctuary are stored and where the ministering priests, the gatekeepers, and the singers reside; and we will not neglect the house of our God.
