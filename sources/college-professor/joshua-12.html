Kings Defeated East of the Jordan

1 Here are set forth the kings of the land whom the Israelites subdued, whose territories they appropriated east of the Jordan, extending from the gorge of the Arnon to Mount Hermon and encompassing the whole eastern Arabah. 2 Sihon, king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, ruled from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon gorge, through the midst of that gorge, as far as the Jabbok gorge, the frontier of the Ammonites, together with half of Gilead. 3 The eastern Arabah extended to the Sea of Chinnereth and to the Sea of the Arabah—that is, the Salt Sea—eastward along the route toward Beth-jeshimoth, and southward beneath the slopes of Pisgah.

4 The domain of Og, king of Bashan—one of the last survivors of the Rephaim—who held court at Ashtaroth and Edrei, 5 included Mount Hermon, Salcah, and all of Bashan, reaching to the frontier of the Geshurites and Maacathites, as well as half of Gilead up to the border of Sihon, king of Heshbon. 6 Moses, the servant of the the LORD, together with the Israelites, vanquished them; and Moses, the servant of the the LORD, allotted their land as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

Kings Defeated West of the Jordan

7 These are the kings whom Joshua and the Israelites struck down west of the Jordan, from Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, distributed according to their tribal allotments; Joshua assigned their territories as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel. 8 The lands comprised the hill country, the Shephelah, the Arabah, the slopes, the wilderness, and the Negev, inhabited by the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

Enumeration of the Western Kings

9 The king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, near Bethel, one; 10 the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one; 11 the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one; 12 the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one; 13 the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one; 14 the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one; 15 the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one; 16 the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one.

17 The king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one; 18 the king of Aphek, one; the king of Sharon, one; 19 the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one; 20 the king of Shimron-meron, one; the king of Achshaph, one; 21 the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one; 22 the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jokneam in Carmel, one; 23 the king of Dor in Naphath-dor, one; the king of Goiim in Gilgal, one; 24 the king of Tirzah, one—thirty-one kings in all.
