The Priestly Vestments and Their Consecration

1 You shall bring near unto you your brother Aaron, together with his sons from among the Israelites—Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar—that they may minister to Me as priests. 2 You shall fashion holy garments for your brother Aaron, garments of glory and beauty. 3 You shall speak unto all the wise-hearted whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, that they may fashion Aaron’s garments to consecrate him for ministry unto Me. 4 These are the garments they shall fashion: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a checkered tunic, a turban, and a sash. They shall fashion holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons, that they may serve Me as priests. 5 They shall take gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, together with fine linen.

6 You shall fashion the ephod of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and of finely twisted linen, the work of a skillful artisan. 7 It shall possess two shoulder pieces joined to its two ends, and it shall be joined together. 8 The skillfully woven waistband that is upon it shall be of one workmanship with it—of gold, blue, purple, scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen.

9 You shall take two onyx stones and engrave upon them the names of the sons of Israel: 10 six of their names upon one stone, and the remaining six names upon the other, according to their births. 11 As the work of a stone engraver, like the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel; you shall set them in settings of gold. 12 You shall put the two stones upon the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel, and Aaron shall bear their names before the LORD upon his two shoulders for a remembrance.

13 You shall make settings of gold, 14 and two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords, and you shall attach the chains to the settings.

The Breastpiece of Judgment

15 You shall make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a skillful artisan; like the work of the ephod you shall make it, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and of finely twisted linen. 16 It shall be square and doubled, a span in length and a span in width. 17 You shall set in it a mounting of stones, four rows of stones: the first row a sardius, a topaz, and an emerald; 18 the second row a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond; 19 the third row a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst; 20 and the fourth row a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. They shall be set in gold settings. 21 The stones shall be twelve, according to the names of the sons of Israel, each like the engraving of a signet, each according to its name, for the twelve tribes.

22 You shall make on the breastpiece chains like cords, of pure gold, twisted. 23 You shall make two gold rings for the breastpiece and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece. 24 You shall put the two gold chains on the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece. 25 You shall attach the two ends of the chains to the two settings and put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front. 26 You shall make two gold rings and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece, on the edge toward the ephod. 27 You shall make two gold rings and put them below on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, toward the front, corresponding to its seam, above the waistband of the ephod. 28 They shall bind the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so that it may rest on the waistband of the ephod and not swing away from the ephod.

29 Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel on the breastpiece of judgment over his heart when he enters the holy place, for a remembrance before the LORD continually. 30 You shall put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece of judgment, and they shall be over Aaron’s heart when he goes before the LORD. Aaron shall bear the judgment of the sons of Israel over his heart before the LORD continually.

The Robe, Turban, and Undergarments

31 You shall make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue. 32 It shall have an opening for the head in the middle; around the opening there shall be a binding like the neck of a garment, woven, so that it does not tear. 33 You shall make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet upon its hem all around, and gold bells between them all around. 34 A gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe. 35 It shall be upon Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he enters and exits the sanctuary before the LORD, so that he does not die.

36 You shall make a plate of pure gold and engrave upon it, like the engraving of a signet, “Holy to the LORD.” 37 You shall put it on a blue cord and set it upon the turban, at the front of the turban. 38 It shall be upon Aaron’s forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the holy things that the Israelites consecrate, all their holy gifts; and it shall be upon his forehead continually, for acceptance before the LORD.

39 You shall weave the tunic of fine linen and make the turban of fine linen, and you shall make the sash, the work of an embroiderer. 40 For Aaron’s sons you shall make tunics, sashes, and caps, for glory and for beauty.

41 You shall clothe Aaron your brother and his sons with him, and you shall anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them, that they may serve Me as priests. 42 You shall make linen undergarments to cover the flesh of their nakedness; they shall extend from the hips to the thighs. 43 They shall be upon Aaron and his sons when they enter the tent of meeting or approach the altar to minister in the holy place, so that they do not bear iniquity and die. It is a statute forever for him and for his descendants.
