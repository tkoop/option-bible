Israel's Arrival at Sinai and the Covenant Summons

1 In the third month after the Israelites' exodus from the land of Egypt—on that very day—they entered the wilderness of Sinai. 2 Upon departing Rephidim, they came into the wilderness of Sinai and encamped there in the wilderness, opposite the mountain.

3 Moses ascended to God, and the LORD summoned him from the mountain, saying, "Thus you shall say to the house of Jacob and declare to the sons of Israel: 4 'You yourselves have witnessed what I wrought upon Egypt, and how I bore you aloft with swift and sheltering deliverance and brought you to Myself. 5 Now therefore, if you will indeed hearken to My voice and keep My covenant, you shall be My treasured possession among all peoples, for all the earth is Mine. 6 And you shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.' These are the words that you shall speak to the Israelites."

The People's Assent and the Promise of Divine Presence

7 So Moses came and summoned the elders of the people, and laid before them all these words that the LORD had enjoined upon him. 8 And all the people responded in unison, saying, "All that the LORD has spoken we will perform." And Moses conveyed the people's words back to the LORD.

9 The LORD said to Moses, "Behold, I am about to come to you in a dense cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you, and may also repose enduring trust in you." And Moses reported to the LORD the words of the people.

Consecration for the Divine Descent

10 The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them launder their garments, 11 and let them be prepared for the third day; for on the third day the LORD will descend upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people. 12 And you shall demarcate boundaries for the people round about, saying, 'Take care that you do not ascend the mountain or touch its extremity. Whoever touches the mountain shall assuredly be put to death. 13 No hand shall touch him, but he shall assuredly be stoned or transfixed with arrows; whether human or beast, he shall not survive. When the trumpet emits a prolonged blast, they may ascend the mountain.'"

14 So Moses descended from the mountain to the people and sanctified the people, and they laundered their garments. 15 And he said to the people, "Be prepared for the three days; abstain from sexual relations."

The Theophany and the Sanctified Boundaries

16 On the third day, at daybreak, there were thunderings and lightnings, and a heavy cloud upon the mountain, and an exceedingly loud trumpet blast, so that all the people in the camp quaked. 17 Then Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stationed themselves at the foot of the mountain. 18 Mount Sinai was altogether enveloped in smoke because the LORD had descended upon it in fire; its smoke ascended like the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mountain quaked violently. 19 And as the trumpet's sound waxed louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him with thunder.

20 The LORD descended to the summit of Mount Sinai, and the LORD summoned Moses to the summit, and Moses ascended. 21 And the LORD said to Moses, "Descend and admonish the people, lest they break through to the LORD to gaze, and many of them perish. 22 Let the priests also, who draw near to the LORD, sanctify themselves, lest the LORD erupt against them."

23 And Moses said to the LORD, "The people cannot ascend Mount Sinai, for You Yourself admonished us, saying, 'Set boundaries around the mountain and sanctify it.'"

24 And the LORD said to him, "Go, descend; then you shall ascend, you and Aaron with you; but let not the priests and the people break through to ascend to the LORD, lest He erupt against them." 25 So Moses descended to the people and addressed them.
