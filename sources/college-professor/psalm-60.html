David’s Lament and Appeal for Restoration

1 For the choirmaster, to the melody “Lily of the Testimony”; a Miktam of David for instruction—when he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down twelve thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt. You have rejected us, O God; You have breached our defenses; You have been angry; restore us! 2 You have made the earth quake and split it asunder; heal its fissures, for it is tottering. 3 You have shown Your people harsh affliction; You have made us drink a wine that makes us reel. 4 You have raised a standard for those who revere You, to be unfurled in the cause of truth. Selah. 5 Answer us! Save with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

God’s Oracle of Conquest

6 God has spoken in His sanctuary: “I will exult; I will apportion Shechem and measure off the Valley of Succoth. 7 Gilead is Mine, Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, and Judah is My scepter. 8 Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I fling My sandal; over Philistia I raise a shout of triumph.”

A Final Plea for Deliverance

9 Who will conduct me to the fortified city? Who will guide me into Edom? 10 Have You not, O God, cast us off? And will You not go forth with our armies? 11 Grant us succor against the adversary, for human deliverance is futile. 12 In God we shall act with valor, and He will trample down our adversaries.
