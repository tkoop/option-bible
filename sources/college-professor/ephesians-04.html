Walking Worthy of the Vocation to Which One Has Been Summoned

1 As a prisoner in the Lord, therefore, I exhort you to conduct your lives in a manner worthy of the vocation to which you have been called, 2 with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love, 3 striving to preserve the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace. 4 There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope in your calling; 5 one Lord, one faith, one baptism; 6 one God and Father of all, who is over all, through all, and in all.

Christ’s Gifts and the Church’s Maturation

7 Yet to each of us grace has been apportioned according to the measure of Christ’s gift. 8 Hence it is said, “When He ascended on high, He led captives in His train and gave gifts to humanity.” 9 Now what is the significance of “He ascended,” if not that He first descended into the lower regions of the earth? 10 The One who descended is Himself also the One who ascended far above all the heavens, so that He might fill all things. 11 And He Himself appointed some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as pastors and teachers, 12 for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, toward the edification of the body of Christ, 13 until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to mature adulthood, to the measure of the stature of Christ’s fullness. 14 Thus we are no longer to be infants, tossed to and fro and carried about by every wind of teaching, by the cunning and craftiness of people in their deceitful scheming. 15 Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in all respects into Him who is the head—Christ Himself. 16 From Him the whole body, being fitted and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love as each part does its work according to its proper measure.

Renouncing the Former Way and Embracing the New Self

17 So I declare and testify in the Lord that you must no longer live as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their thinking. 18 Their understanding is darkened; they are alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance within them, stemming from the hardness of their hearts. 19 Having lost all sensitivity, they have surrendered themselves to sensuality so as to indulge in every kind of impurity with insatiable greed. 20 But you did not so learn Christ, 21 assuming that you have heard Him and been taught in Him, as truth resides in Jesus. 22 You were instructed to put off the old self, which belongs to your former way of life and is being corrupted by deceitful desires, 23 and to be renewed in the spirit of your minds, 24 and to put on the new self, created in God’s likeness in true righteousness and holiness.

The Ethical Imperatives of the Renewed Life

25 Therefore, having laid aside falsehood, let each of you speak truth with his neighbor, for we are members of one another. 26 “Be angry, yet do not sin; do not let the sun set upon your wrath.” 27 And give no opportunity to the devil. 28 Let the thief steal no longer; rather, let him toil, doing honest work with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with those in need. 29 Let no corrupt word proceed from your mouth, but only what is good for edification according to the need, that it may impart grace to those who hear. 30 And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption. 31 Let all bitterness, rage, anger, clamor, and slander be removed from you, together with all malice. 32 Rather, be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as God in Christ forgave you.
