The Benjamite Genealogical Register

1 Benjamin fathered Bela, his firstborn, together with Ashbel, the second, and Aharah, the third; 2 Nohah, the fourth, and Rapha, the fifth, complete the enumeration of his principal sons. 3 Bela's progeny included Addar, Gera, and Abihud; 4 Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah; 5 and Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. 6 These were the sons of Ehud, who constitute the ancestral heads of the inhabitants of Geba and who were deported to Manahath. 7 Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera effected that deportation, and Gera fathered Uzza and Ahihud.

8 Shaharaim fathered children in the territory of Moab after having dismissed his wives Hushim and Baara. 9 By his wife Hodesh he fathered Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcham, together with Jeuz, Sachiah, and Mirmah—these being his sons and heads of ancestral houses. 10 11 From Hushim he also fathered Abitub and Elpaal. 12 Elpaal's sons were Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod, including its dependent settlements. 13 Beriah and Shema served as ancestral heads of the inhabitants of Aijalon and routed the inhabitants of Gath.

14 The register proceeds with Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth; 15 then Zebadiah, Arad, and Eder; 16 then Michael, Ishpah, and Joha, the sons of Beriah; 17 then Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, and Heber; 18 then Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab, the sons of Elpaal; 19 then Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi; 20 and finally Elienai, Zillethai, and Eliel. 21 Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei; 22 then Ishpan, Eber, and Eliel; 23 then Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan; 24 then Hananiah, Elam, and Anthothijah; 25 then Iphdeiah and Penuel, the sons of Shashak; 26 then Shamsherai, Shehariah, and Athaliah; 27 and then Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri, the sons of Jeroham. 28 These were the chiefs of their ancestral houses, enumerated according to their generations, and they resided in Jerusalem.

The Gibeonite Patriarchal Household

29 The father of Gibeon resided in Gibeon, and his wife's name was Maacah. 30 His firstborn was Abdon, followed by Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab; 31 likewise Gedor, Ahio, and Zechariah. 32 Mikloth fathered Shimeah. These men dwelt over against their kinsmen in Jerusalem, together with those kinsmen.

The Royal Lineage of Saul and Jonathan

33 Ner fathered Kish; Kish fathered Saul; and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 34 Jonathan's son was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal fathered Micah. 35 Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. 36 Ahaz fathered Jehoaddah; Jehoaddah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Moza. 37 Moza fathered Binea, whose son was Raphah, whose son was Eleasah, whose son was Azel. 38 Azel had six sons: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. 39 The sons of his brother Eshek were Ulam the firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third. 40 Ulam's sons were mighty men of valor and archers; they were prolific in sons and grandsons, one hundred fifty in all, and all these were descendants of Benjamin.
