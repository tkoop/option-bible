A Thanksgiving for Deliverance and a Vow of Grateful Service

1I cherish the LORD, for He has heard my voice and my supplications. 2Because He inclined His ear toward me, I shall invoke Him throughout all my days. 3The cords of death encompassed me, and the pangs of Sheol seized me; I encountered distress and grief. 4Then I called upon the name of the LORD: "O the LORD, I implore You, rescue my soul!"

5The LORD is gracious and righteous, and our God is full of compassion. 6The LORD preserves the guileless; I was brought low, and He delivered me. 7Return, O my soul, to your rest, for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you. 8For You have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from stumbling, 9that I may walk before the LORD in the lands of the living.

10I kept faith even while I confessed, "I am sorely afflicted." 11In my consternation I said, "Every human being is a liar." 12How shall I repay the LORD for all His beneficence toward me? 13I will lift up the cup of salvation and invoke the name of the LORD. 14I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people.

15Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His faithful ones. 16O the LORD, truly I am Your servant; I am Your servant, the son of Your maidservant; You have loosed my bonds. 17I will offer You a sacrifice of thanksgiving and invoke the name of the LORD. 18I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people, 19in the courts of the LORD's house, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!
