A Supplication for Expedient Deliverance

For the director of music. A Psalm of David, for remembrance. 1 Hasten, O God, to rescue me; make haste, O the LORD, to my aid. 2 May those who seek my life be shamed and confounded; may those who delight in my ruin turn back and be dishonored. 3 May those who cry, “Aha! Aha!” recoil in their own disgrace. 4 Let all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; let those who love Your salvation say continually, “God is great!” 5 But I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; O the LORD, do not delay.
