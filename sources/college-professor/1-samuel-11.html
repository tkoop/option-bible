The Ammonite Threat and the Plea of Jabesh-gilead

1 Nahash the Ammonite advanced and laid siege to Jabesh-gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to him, "Make a treaty with us, and we will serve you."

2 But Nahash the Ammonite replied, "I will make a treaty with you on this condition: that I gouge out the right eye of every one of you and thereby bring disgrace upon all Israel."

3 The elders of Jabesh said to him, "Grant us seven days so that we may send messengers throughout all Israel; and if no one comes to deliver us, we will surrender to you."

Saul's Mobilization in Response to the Crisis

4 When the messengers arrived at Gibeah of Saul, they reported these words to the people, and all the people wept aloud.

5 Just then Saul was returning from the fields behind his oxen. "What troubles the people?" he asked. "Why are they weeping?" They told him what the men of Jabesh had said.

6 When Saul heard these words, the Spirit of God rushed upon him, and his anger burned fiercely.

7 He took a yoke of oxen, dismembered them, and sent the pieces by messengers throughout all the territory of Israel, proclaiming, "This is what will be done to the oxen of anyone who does not follow Saul and Samuel." Then the terror of the LORD fell upon the people, and they mustered as one man.

The Battle and Defeat of the Ammonites

8 When he had mustered them at Bezek, the Israelites numbered three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand. 9 They said to the messengers who had come, "Say to the men of Jabesh-gilead, 'Tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, deliverance will be yours.'" When the messengers reported this to the men of Jabesh, they rejoiced.

10 The men of Jabesh said, "Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you may deal with us as you see fit."

11 The next day Saul divided the people into three companies. They broke into the Ammonite camp during the morning watch and struck them down until the heat of the day. The survivors scattered, so that no two of them were left together.

The Aftermath and Renewal of the Kingship

12 The people then said to Samuel, "Who was it who said, 'Must Saul reign over us?' Hand those men over, and we will put them to death."

13 But Saul said, "No one will be put to death today, for today the LORD has brought deliverance to Israel."

14 Then Samuel said to the people, "Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingship there."

15 So all the people went to Gilgal, and there they made Saul king in the presence of the LORD. There they sacrificed peace offerings before the LORD, and Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced exceedingly.
