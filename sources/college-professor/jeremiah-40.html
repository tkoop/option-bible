Jeremiah Released and Commissioned to Remain

1 The following oracle came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan, commander of the imperial guard, released him at Ramah, where he had discovered him fettered among the entire company of exiles from Jerusalem and Judah being deported to Babylon.

2 The captain of the guard found Jeremiah and said to him, “The LORD your God decreed this calamity upon this place, 3 and now the LORD has brought it to pass and fulfilled what He threatened; because you people sinned against the LORD and refused to heed His voice, this catastrophe has befallen you. 4 But now, behold, I release you this day from the chains upon your wrists. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will watch over you; but if it seems wrong to you to come with me to Babylon, then stay. See, the whole land lies before you: wherever it seems good and right to you to go, go there.”

5 But before Jeremiah turned to depart, Nebuzaradan added, “Return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah, and dwell with him among the people, or go wherever it seems right to you.” Then the captain of the guard gave him provisions and a gift and sent him away.

6 So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and dwelt with him among the people who remained in the land.

The Remnant Leaders Assemble at Mizpah

7 When all the commanders of the armies in the field, together with their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over the land and had entrusted to him men, women, children, and the poorest of the land—those who had not been exiled to Babylon— 8 they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, together with their men.

9 Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, swore an oath to them and to their men, saying, “Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Remain in the land, serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you. 10 As for me, I will remain in Mizpah to represent you before the Chaldeans when they come to us. But you, gather wine, summer fruit, and oil, store them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities that you have occupied.”

11 When all the Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and in all the other lands heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant in Judah and had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over them, 12 they returned from all the places to which they had been scattered and came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and gathered a very great abundance of wine and summer fruit.

A Plot Against Gedaliah Is Disclosed

13 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah 14 and said to him, “Do you not know that Baalis, king of the Ammonites, has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to take your life?” But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.

15 Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah, “Let me go and slay Ishmael son of Nethaniah; no one will know. Why should he take your life and scatter all the Jews gathered to you, so that the remnant of Judah would perish?”

16 But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, “Do not do this thing! What you are saying about Ishmael is false.”
