The Oracle of Nahum Concerning Nineveh

1 An oracle concerning Nineveh: the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

The LORD's Jealous Wrath and Steadfast Goodness

2 The LORD is a jealous and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and fierce in wrath. The LORD takes vengeance upon His adversaries, and He reserves indignation for His enemies. 3 The LORD is long-suffering and possessed of great might; the LORD will not at all acquit the guilty. His way is in the whirlwind and in the tempest, and the clouds are the dust of His feet.

4 He rebukes the sea and desiccates it, and He dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel languish, and the blossom of Lebanon withers. 5 The mountains tremble before Him, and the hills dissolve; the earth heaves before Him, together with the world and all its inhabitants.

6 Who can stand before His indignation? Who can rise up amid the fierceness of His anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before Him.

7 The LORD is good—a stronghold in the day of distress—and He knows those who seek refuge in Him. 8 With an overflowing flood He will bring its place to an utter end, and darkness will pursue His enemies.

The Oracle Against Nineveh

9 What do you devise against the LORD? He is effecting an utter end; affliction shall not rise up a second time. 10 For though they are entangled like thorns, and though they are drunk as drunkards, they shall be consumed like stubble fully dried. 11 From you has gone forth one who devises evil against the LORD, a counselor of worthlessness.

12 Thus says the LORD: “Though they stand intact and in great numbers, yet they shall be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more. 13 And now I will break his yoke from upon you, and I will tear your bonds asunder.”

14 The LORD has commanded concerning you: “You shall have no descendants to bear your name. From the house of your gods I will cut off the carved image and the molten image. I will prepare your grave, for you are contemptible.”

Deliverance and Peace for Judah

15 Behold, upon the mountains, the feet of a messenger bringing good tidings, announcing peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah; fulfill your vows. For the wicked one shall no more pass through you; he has been utterly cut off.
