The Northern Kings Form a Coalition

1 When Jabin, monarch of Hazor, received intelligence of these developments, he dispatched envoys to Jobab, monarch of Madon, and to the monarchs of Shimron and Achshaph, and to the sovereigns of the northern territories in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinnereth, in the lowland, and in the elevations of Dor by the sea, 2 and to the Canaanites on the east and on the west, and to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, and the Jebusites in the hill country, and to the Hivites beneath Hermon in the land of Mizpah. 3 They emerged with all their hosts, a people as innumerable as the sand upon the seashore, accompanied by horses and chariots in prodigious abundance. 4 All these monarchs convened and encamped together at the waters of Merom to prosecute war against Israel. 5

Divine Assurance and Sudden Victory

6 And the LORD declared to Joshua, "Do not be apprehensive of them, for tomorrow at this hour I shall deliver all of them slain before Israel; you shall hamstring their horses and incinerate their chariots with fire." 7 Accordingly, Joshua and all the martial host with him descended upon them precipitously at the waters of Merom and fell upon them. 8 The LORD delivered them into Israel's hand, and they smote them and pursued them as far as Great Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, eastward to the valley of Mizpeh, smiting them until no survivor remained. 9 Joshua then executed against them precisely what the LORD had prescribed: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

The Devastation of Hazor and the Northern Cities

10 At that juncture Joshua turned and seized Hazor, striking its king with the sword, for Hazor had formerly been the paramount power among all those kingdoms. 11 They struck down every inhabitant with the edge of the sword, devoting them to utter destruction; no breathing soul was left, and he burned Hazor with fire. 12 Joshua captured all these cities and their kings and struck them with the edge of the sword; he devoted them to destruction, as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had enjoined. 13 Yet Israel did not incinerate any of the cities that stood upon their mounds, save Hazor alone, which Joshua burned. 14 All the spoil of these cities and the livestock the Israelites appropriated as plunder; but every person they struck down with the edge of the sword until they were annihilated, leaving no one breathing. 15 As the LORD had commanded Moses His servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua complied; he left nothing unfulfilled of all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Conquest Completed and the Land Allotted

16 Thus Joshua annexed all that land: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowland, the Arabah, the hill country of Israel and its lowland, 17 from Mount Halak, which ascends toward Seir, to Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon beneath Mount Hermon; and he captured all their kings, struck them, and put them to death. 18 Joshua protracted war against all these kings for an extended duration. 19 No city sought accommodation with the Israelites except the Hivites inhabiting Gibeon; all the others were seized in battle. 20 For the LORD hardened their hearts to confront Israel in battle, so that they might be devoted to destruction and receive no quarter, but be annihilated, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 21 At that time Joshua advanced and extirpated the Anakim from the hill country—from Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and all the hill country of Israel—and devoted them and their cities to destruction. 22 No Anakim survived in the land of the Israelites except in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod, where remnants persisted. 23 So Joshua took possession of the entire land in accordance with all that the LORD had spoken to Moses; and Joshua allotted it as an inheritance to Israel according to their tribal divisions; and the land enjoyed respite from war.
