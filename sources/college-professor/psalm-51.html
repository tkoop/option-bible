For the choirmaster: a psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came to him after he had gone in to Bathsheba.

A Contrite Confession and Plea for Cleansing

1 Be gracious to me, O God, according to Your steadfast love; according to the plenitude of Your compassions, efface my transgressions. 2 Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and purify me from my sin. 3 For I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin remains perpetually before me. 4 Against You—You alone—have I sinned, and I have wrought that which is evil in Your sight, so that You may be vindicated in Your utterance and acquitted in Your judgment. 5 Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity, and in sin did my mother conceive me. 6 Behold, You delight in truth in the inward parts, and in the hidden place You cause me to know wisdom. 7 Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow. 8 Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones that You have crushed rejoice. 9 Hide Your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.

Renewal of Spirit and Joyful Restoration

10 Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me. 11 Do not cast me away from Your presence, and do not take Your Holy Spirit from me. 12 Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and uphold me with a willing spirit.

Vows of Instruction, Praise, and True Sacrifice

13 Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners shall return to You. 14 Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, the God of my salvation, and my tongue shall sing aloud of Your righteousness. 15 O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth shall declare Your praise. 16 For You do not delight in sacrifice, else would I bring it; You take no pleasure in burnt offerings. 17 The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, You will not despise. 18 In Your favor do good to Zion; build the walls of Jerusalem. 19 Then will You delight in righteous sacrifices, in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings; then bulls will be offered upon Your altar.
