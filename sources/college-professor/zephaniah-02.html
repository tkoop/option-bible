A Summons to Repentance Before the Day of the LORD

1 Gather together, yes, gather, O shameless nation, 2 before the decree takes effect—before the day is borne away like chaff—before the fierce anger of the the LORD descends upon you, before the day of the the LORD overtakes you. 3 Seek the the LORD, all you humble of the land, who have performed His justice; seek righteousness, seek humility; perhaps you will be concealed in the day of the the LORD’s anger.

Oracles of Judgment Against the Philistine Cities

4 For Gaza shall be forsaken, and Ashkelon shall become a desolation; Ashdod shall be driven out at noon, and Ekron shall be uprooted. 5 Woe to you inhabitants of the seacoast, you nation of the Cherethites! The word of the the LORD is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines; I will destroy you, so that no inhabitant remains. 6 The seacoast shall become pastures, with meadows for shepherds and folds for flocks. 7 It shall belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; they shall pasture upon it, and in the houses of Ashkelon they shall lie down at evening, for the the LORD their God will visit them and restore their fortunes.

The Doom of Moab, Ammon, and the Surrounding Nations

8 “I have heard the taunt of Moab and the revilings of the Ammonites, how they taunted My people and magnified themselves against their border.

9 Therefore, as I live,” declares the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, “Moab shall be like Sodom, and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—a realm of nettles and salt pits, a desolation forever. The remnant of My people shall plunder them, and the survivors of My nation shall possess them.” 10 This shall be their recompense for their pride, because they taunted and magnified themselves against the people of the the LORD of Hosts. 11 The the LORD will be formidable against them, for He will famish all the gods of the earth; and all the coastlands of the nations shall bow down to Him, each in its own place. 12 You also, O Cushites, shall be slain by My sword.

The Desolation of Assyria and Nineveh

13 He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy Assyria; He will make Nineveh a desolation, dry as the wilderness. 14 Herds shall lie down in her midst—all kinds of beasts; even the owl and the hedgehog shall lodge among her capitals; a voice shall hoot in the window; devastation shall be on the threshold, for He has laid bare her cedar work. 15 This is the exultant city that dwelt in security, that said in her heart, “I am, and there is no one else.” How she has become a desolation, a lair for wild beasts! Everyone who passes by her hisses and shakes his fist.
