A New Song of Praise for the Faithful

1Praise the LORD! Sing to the LORD a new song, His praise in the assembly of the saints. 2Let Israel exult in its Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King. 3Let them praise His name with dance; let them make music to Him with tambourine and lyre.

4For the LORD takes pleasure in His people; He adorns the humble with salvation. 5Let the saints exult in glory; let them sing for joy upon their beds.

The Faithful as Instruments of Divine Judgment

6Let the high praises of God be in their throats, and a two-edged sword in their hands, 7to execute vengeance upon the nations and retribution upon the peoples, 8to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with iron fetters, 9to execute the judgment written against them. This is an honor for all His saints. Praise the LORD!
