Israel’s Forbidden Rejoicing and Impending Exile

1 Do not exult, O Israel, after the manner of the nations, for you have been unfaithful to your God; you have set your affection upon a harlot’s hire upon every threshing floor of grain. 2 Neither threshing floor nor winepress shall nourish them, and the new wine shall prove false to them. 3 They shall not remain in the land of the LORD; Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria they shall consume that which is unclean.

Corrupted Worship and the Folly of Prophets

4 They shall not pour out wine offerings to the LORD, nor shall their sacrifices be acceptable to Him. Their bread shall be as the bread of mourners; all who partake of it shall be defiled, for their bread is for their own sustenance and shall not enter the house of the LORD. 5 What will you do on the day of the appointed festival, on the day of the LORD’s feast? 6 For behold, they have departed because of devastation; Egypt shall gather them, and Memphis shall bury them. Their cherished silver shall be inherited by nettles, and thorns shall dwell in their tents. 7 The days of visitation have arrived, the days of recompense have arrived; Israel shall know it. The prophet is a fool, and the man of the spirit is mad, because of the magnitude of your iniquity and the abundance of your hostility. 8 Ephraim’s prophet is a watchman with my God, yet a snare of the fowler is upon all his ways, and hostility is in the house of his God. 9 They have sunk to the depths of corruption, as in the days of Gibeah; He will remember their iniquity and visit their sins upon them.

From Early Favor to Barrenness and Slaughter

10 I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness; I beheld your fathers as the first-ripe fruit upon the fig tree in its first season. But they went to Baal-peor and consecrated themselves to Shame, and became abominations in proportion to their love. 11 Ephraim’s glory shall take wing like a bird—no birth, no pregnancy, no conception. 12 Even if they rear their children, I will bereave them of every one; yes, woe to them when I withdraw from them. 13 I have seen Ephraim, like Tyre, planted in a pleasant place; yet Ephraim shall lead forth his children to the slayer. 14 Give them, O LORD—what shall You give? Give them a womb that miscarries and breasts that yield no milk.

Final Rejection and Wandering Among the Nations

15 All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I hated them. On account of the evil of their deeds I will expel them from My house; I will no longer love them; all their princes are rebels. 16 Ephraim is stricken; their root has dried up; they shall produce no fruit. Even if they beget children, I will put to death the darlings of their womb. 17 My God will reject them, because they have not hearkened to Him; they shall become wanderers among the nations.
