Prudential Counsel on Banqueting and Wealth

1 When you sit at the table of a sovereign, scrutinize with meticulous circumspection the provisions arrayed before you; 2 and, should you be a person of ravenous appetite, impose upon yourself the most stringent restraint. 3 Do not covet his delicacies, for that fare is fundamentally deceptive. 4 Do not exhaust yourself in the pursuit of riches; be sufficiently discerning to desist. 5 When your gaze alights upon wealth, it is already vanishing, for it sprouts wings for itself and takes flight like an eagle toward the heavens.

The Stingy Host and Foolish Speech

6 Do not partake of the bread of a man with a grudging eye, nor covet his delicacies; 7 for as he reckons within himself, so he truly is. Though he says, “Eat and drink,” his heart is not with you. 8 You will vomit up the morsel you have consumed and squander your gracious words. 9 Do not speak in a fool’s hearing, for he will despise the sagacity of your words.

Justice, Discipline, and Filial Piety

10 Do not displace an ancient boundary marker, and do not encroach upon the fields of orphans, 11 for their Redeemer is mighty; He will plead their cause against you.

12 Apply your heart to discipline and your ears to words of knowledge. 13 Do not withhold correction from a child; though you strike him with a rod, he will not die. 14 You shall strike him with a rod and deliver his soul from Sheol.

15 My son, if your heart is wise, my heart will rejoice—indeed, I too. 16 My inmost being will exult when your lips speak what is right.

17 Let not your heart envy sinners, but abide in the fear of the LORD all day long, 18 for surely there is a future, and your hope will not be severed.

19 Hear, my son, and be wise, and direct your heart in the way. 20 Do not associate with winebibbers, nor with gluttonous devourers of flesh; 21 for the drunkard and the glutton will be impoverished, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags.

22 Heed your father, who begot you, and do not despise your mother when she is old. 23 Acquire truth and do not sell it; acquire wisdom, instruction, and understanding. 24 The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who begets a wise son will delight in him. 25 Let your father and mother be glad, and let her who bore you rejoice.

The Snares of Lust and the Ruin of Wine

26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes delight in my ways. 27 For a prostitute is a deep pit, and an adulteress is a narrow well. 28 She lies in wait like a robber and multiplies the faithless among men.

29 Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaints? Who has needless bruises? Who has bloodshot eyes? 30 Those who linger over wine, those who go to sample mixed drinks. 31 Do not gaze upon wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and descends smoothly.

32 In the end it bites like a serpent and stings like a viper. 33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your heart will utter perversities. 34 You will be like one lying in the heart of the sea, or like one lying atop a mast.

35 “They struck me, yet I was not sick; they beat me, yet I did not know. When will I awake, that I may seek another drink?”
