A Supplication for Vindication, Protection, and the Vision of God

1 Hear, the LORD, my righteous plea; attend to my cry, and give ear to my prayer, uttered by lips untainted by deceit. 2 Let my vindication proceed from Your presence; let Your eyes discern what is upright. 3 You have probed my heart; You have visited me in the night; You have refined me and found no iniquity within me; I have resolved that my mouth shall not transgress. 4 As for the deeds of mortals, by the word of Your lips I have shunned the paths of the violent. 5 My steps have held fast to Your courses; my feet have not faltered.

6 I call upon You, O God, for You will answer me; incline Your ear to me and hear my prayer. 7 Show forth the wonders of Your steadfast love, You who save by Your right hand those who seek refuge from their adversaries. 8 Keep me as the apple of Your eye; conceal me in the shadow of Your wings.

9 from the wicked who despoil me, from my mortal enemies who encircle me. 10 Their hearts have grown callous; their mouths utter arrogance. 11 They have tracked me down; they now surround me; their eyes are set to cast me to the ground. 12 Like a lion he longs to tear his prey, like a young lion lurking in ambush.

13 Arise, the LORD; confront them and bring them to their knees; deliver my soul from the wicked by Your sword. 14 from mortals, the LORD, by Your hand—from men of this world whose portion is in this life. May You fill their bellies with Your treasure; may their sons have abundance, and may they leave their surplus to their children. 15 As for me, I shall behold Your face in righteousness; when I awake, I shall be satisfied with Your presence.
