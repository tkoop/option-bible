Moses’ Final Charge to Israel

1 Moses went forth and proclaimed these words to the whole of Israel. 2 He said to them, “I have attained the age of one hundred twenty years this day; I am no longer capable of active leadership. Furthermore, the the LORD has declared to me, ‘You shall not cross this Jordan.’ 3 The The LORD your God—He Himself is crossing over before you; He will annihilate these nations from before your face, and you shall take possession of their land. Joshua, too, is crossing over before you, just as the the LORD has spoken. 4 The The LORD will deal with them as He dealt with Sihon and Og, kings of the Amorites, and with their land, which He laid waste. 5 The The LORD will deliver them before you, and you shall treat them in accordance with every commandment I have enjoined upon you. 6 Be strong and courageous; do not fear, nor be dismayed before them, for the the LORD your God is the One who goes with you. He will not fail you, nor will He forsake you.”

The Law Entrusted to the Levitical Priests

9 Moses wrote down this law and delivered it to the Levitical priests who bear the ark of the covenant of the the LORD, and to all the elders of Israel. 10 Moses charged them, saying, “At the end of every seven years, at the designated time of the year of release, during the Feast of Tabernacles, 11 when all Israel assembles to appear before the the LORD your God in the place He will choose, you shall read this law aloud in the hearing of all Israel. 12 Assemble the people—men, women, and children—and the sojourner within your gates, so that they may hear, learn, and fear the the LORD your God, and carefully observe all the words of this law, 13 and that their children who have not known it may hear and learn to fear the the LORD your God for as long as you live upon the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess.”

The LORD Foretells Israel’s Apostasy and Ordains a Song

14 The The LORD said to Moses, “Behold, the days of your death draw near. Summon Joshua and present yourselves in the tent of meeting, that I may commission him.” So Moses and Joshua went and took their stand in the tent of meeting. 15 The The LORD appeared in the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood at the entrance of the tent.

16 The The LORD said to Moses, “You are about to die and be gathered to your fathers. This people will then arise and turn to idolatry, pursuing the foreign gods of the land they are entering, living in its midst; they will forsake Me and violate the covenant I have made with them. 17 In that day My anger will be kindled against them; I will forsake them and hide My face from them, so that they will be consumed. Many evils and troubles will befall them, and they will say in that day, ‘Have not these calamities come upon us because our God is not in our midst?’ 18 I will surely hide My face in that day on account of all the evil they have committed, for they will have turned to other gods.”

19 The The LORD said, “Now, therefore, write this song for yourselves and teach it to the children of Israel; place it upon their lips, so that this song may stand as a witness for Me against the children of Israel. 20 For when I bring them into the land I swore to their fathers—a land flowing with milk and honey—and they eat their fill and grow prosperous, they will turn to other gods, serve them, provoke Me, and break My covenant. 21 And when many evils and troubles befall them, this song will testify against them as a witness, for it will not be forgotten from the mouths of their descendants. I know the inclination of their hearts even now, before I bring them into the land I have sworn to give them.” 22 So Moses wrote this song that very day and taught it to the children of Israel.

The Law Deposited Beside the Ark

23 The The LORD then commissioned Joshua son of Nun, saying, “Be strong and courageous, for you shall lead the children of Israel into the land I swore to them, and I will be with you.”

24 When Moses had finished writing all the words of this law in a book, to their very completion, 25 Moses commanded the Levites who bear the ark of the covenant of the the LORD, saying, 26 “Take this book of the law and place it beside the ark of the covenant of the the LORD your God, so that it may remain there as a witness against you. 27 For I know your rebellion and your stiff neck. Even while I am alive with you today, you have been rebellious against the the LORD; how much more so after my death! 28 Assemble before me all the elders of your tribes and your officers, that I may speak these words in their hearing and call heaven and earth to witness against them. 29 For I know that after my death you will utterly corrupt yourselves and turn aside from the way I have commanded you. In the latter days evil will befall you, because you will do what is evil in the sight of the the LORD, provoking Him to anger by the work of your hands.” 30 Moses then recited the words of this song in the hearing of the whole assembly of Israel, until he had finished.
