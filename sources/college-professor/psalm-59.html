A Prayer for Deliverance from Conspiratorial Enemies

For the choirmaster, to the melody “Do Not Destroy”; a miktam of David, composed when Saul dispatched men to surveil his house in order to assassinate him.

1 Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; lift me beyond the reach of those who rise against me. 2 Deliver me from evildoers and save me from bloodthirsty men.

The Nocturnal Malice of the Wicked

3 For behold, they lie in wait for my life; fierce men conspire against me—not for any transgression or sin of mine, O LORD. 4 Though I am innocent, they rush to array themselves against me; rouse Yourself to meet me, and see. 5 You, O LORD God of Hosts, God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to any who treacherously practice iniquity. Selah.

6 They return at evening; they howl like dogs and prowl around the city. 7 See how they pour forth cutting words from their mouths; their lips are like drawn swords, for they say, “Who will hear?” 8 But You, O LORD, laugh at them; You hold all the nations in derision.

Confident Waiting upon God’s Strength

9 O my Strength, I will keep watch for You, for God is my fortress. 10 My God of loving devotion will go before me; God will let me gaze in triumph upon my enemies. 11 Do not kill them, lest my people forget; scatter them by Your power and bring them down, O Lord, our shield. 12 For the sin of their mouths and the words of their lips, let them be ensnared in their pride, on account of the cursing and lies they utter. 13 Consume them in wrath; consume them until they are no more, that the ends of the earth may know that God rules over Jacob. Selah.

14 They return at evening; they howl like dogs and prowl around the city. 15 They roam in search of food and growl if they remain unsatisfied.

Praise for God’s Steadfast Protection

16 But I will sing of Your strength and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning, for You have been my fortress and my refuge in the day of my distress. 17 O my Strength, I will sing praises to You, for God is my fortress, my God of loving devotion.
