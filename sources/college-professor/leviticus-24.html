The Perpetual Lamp and Showbread

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Command the sons of Israel to bring to you clear oil of pressed olives for the light, that the lamp may burn continually. 3 Outside the veil of the Testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall arrange it from evening until morning before the LORD continually; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations. 4 He shall arrange the lamps on the pure lampstand before the LORD continually.”

5 “And you shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve cakes; two-tenths of an ephah shall be in each cake. 6 You shall set them in two rows, six in a row, on the pure table before the LORD. 7 You shall put pure frankincense on each row, that it may be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to the LORD. 8 Every Sabbath day he shall arrange it before the LORD continually, as a covenant forever from the sons of Israel. 9 It shall belong to Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to them among the offerings by fire to the LORD, a perpetual statute.”

The Blasphemer in the Camp

10 Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the sons of Israel; and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man quarreled in the camp. 11 The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed; so they brought him to Moses. His mother was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan. 12 They put him in custody, that the adjudication might be declared to them from the mouth of the LORD.

The Divine Decree on Blasphemy and Retribution

13 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 14 “Bring the blasphemer outside the camp, and let all who heard him lay their hands on his head; then let all the congregation stone him. 15 And speak to the sons of Israel, saying, ‘Whoever curses his God shall bear his sin. 16 Whoever blasphemes the Name of the LORD shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall stone him. The foreigner and the native-born alike shall be put to death when he blasphemes the Name.’”

17 “Whoever strikes any person mortally shall surely be put to death. 18 Whoever strikes an animal shall make restitution for it, life for life. 19 If a man causes a disfigurement in his neighbor, as he has done, so shall it be done to him: 20 fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As he caused a disfigurement in a man, so shall it be given to him. 21 Whoever strikes an animal shall make restitution for it, and whoever strikes a man shall be put to death. 22 You shall have one standard of judgment for the foreigner and for the native-born, for I am the LORD your God.”

The Execution of the Blasphemer

23 Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they brought the blasphemer outside the camp and stoned him with stones. The sons of Israel did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
