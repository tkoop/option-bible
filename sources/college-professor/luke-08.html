Jesus' Itinerant Ministry and the Parable of the Sower

1 Thereafter, He Himself traversed every city and village, heralding and announcing the glad tidings of the kingdom of God, with the Twelve accompanying Him. 2 Certain women, moreover, who had been restored to health from malign spirits and from various infirmities, attended upon Him—Mary, called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had been expelled; 3 Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward; Susanna; and many others—who ministered to them out of their own resources.

4 When an immense multitude had coalesced and people were converging upon Him from every city, He addressed them through a parable: 5 "A sower went forth to sow his seed, and as he sowed, some of it fell beside the path, where it was trampled underfoot and the birds of heaven devoured it. 6 Another portion fell upon rocky ground, and, having sprung up, it withered because it possessed no moisture. 7 Still another fell among thorns, and the thorns, springing up with it, choked it. 8 Yet another fell into good soil, and, having sprung up, it produced fruit a hundredfold." As He uttered these things, He cried out, "He who has ears to hear, let him hear."

9 His disciples then inquired of Him what this parable might signify. 10 He replied, "To you it has been granted to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to the rest they are conveyed in parables, so that, seeing, they may not see, and hearing, they may not understand."

11 "Now this is the parable: the seed is the word of God. 12 Those beside the path are the ones who hear, whereupon the devil comes and snatches the word from their hearts, lest they should believe and be saved. 13 Those upon the rock are the ones who, upon hearing, receive the word with joy; yet these have no root, and they believe for a season, but in the hour of trial they fall away. 14 That which fell among thorns—these are the ones who hear, but as they proceed they are choked by the anxieties, riches, and pleasures of life, and they bring no fruit to maturity. 15 But that in the good soil—these are the ones who, with a noble and good heart, hear the word, retain it, and bear fruit with patient endurance."

16 "No one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel or places it beneath a bed; rather, he sets it upon a lampstand, so that those entering may see the light. 17 For nothing is concealed that shall not be revealed, nor anything hidden that shall not be known and brought to light. 18 Take heed, therefore, how you hear; for to him who has, more shall be given, and from him who does not have, even that which he supposes he has shall be taken away."

True Kinship and the Calming of the Storm

19 Then His mother and His brothers came to Him, yet they could not gain access to Him because of the crowd. 20 And it was announced to Him, Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wishing to see You."

21 He answered them, "My mother and My brothers are these who hear the word of God and do it."

22 On one of those days He said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side of the lake." So He embarked in a boat with His disciples, and they set sail. 23 While they were sailing, He fell asleep, and a tempest of wind descended upon the lake; the boat was being swamped, and they were in peril.

24 They approached and awakened Him, crying, "Master, Master, we are perishing!" Then, having roused Himself, He rebuked the wind and the surge of the water, and they subsided, and a calm ensued.

25 He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And, seized with fear, they marveled, saying to one another, "Who then is this, that He commands even the winds and the water, and they obey Him?"

Deliverance of the Gerasene Demoniac

26 They sailed to the region of the Gerasenes, which lies opposite Galilee. 27 When He stepped ashore, a man from the city who was possessed by demons met Him; for a considerable time he had worn no garment and had not dwelt in a house, but among the tombs.

28 Seeing Jesus, he cried out and fell down before Him, and with a loud voice he said, "What have you to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beseech you, do not torment me."

29 For He had been commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man; for many a time it had seized him, and he was kept under guard, bound with chains and fetters, yet he would break the bonds and be driven by the demon into the deserts.

30 Jesus asked him, "What is your name?"

He said, "Legion," because many demons had entered him. 31 And they entreated Him not to command them to depart into the abyss.

32 Now a large herd of swine was feeding on the mountain; and they begged Him to permit them to enter those, and He permitted them. 33 The demons, having gone out of the man, entered the swine, and the herd rushed down the precipice into the lake and was drowned.

34 When the herdsmen saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the city and in the country. 35 The people went out to see what had happened, and they came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting at Jesus' feet, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.

36 Those who had seen it reported how the demoniac had been saved. 37 Then the whole multitude of the surrounding region of the Gerasenes asked Him to depart from them, for they were seized with great fear. So He embarked in a boat and returned.

38 The man from whom the demons had gone out begged to be with Him; but He sent him away, saying, "Return to your house, and declare how much God has done for you." 39 And he went away, proclaiming throughout the whole city how much Jesus had done for him.

Healing of the Woman with the Hemorrhage and the Raising of Jairus's Daughter

40 When Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed Him, for they were all waiting for Him. 41 And behold, a man named Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue, came and fell at Jesus' feet, imploring Him to come into his house, 42 for he had an only daughter about twelve years old, and she was dying. While He was going, the crowds were pressing in upon Him. 43 And a woman who had been afflicted with a hemorrhage for twelve years, and who had spent her entire livelihood on physicians, yet could not be healed by anyone, 44 came up behind Him and touched the fringe of His garment, and immediately her flow of blood ceased.

45 Jesus said, "Who is it that touched Me?"

While everyone was denying it, Peter said, "Master, the crowds are surrounding and pressing You."

46 But Jesus said, "Someone touched Me, for I perceive that power has gone out from Me."

47 When the woman saw that she had not remained concealed, she came trembling and fell down before Him, and in the presence of all the people she declared the reason she had touched Him and how she had been healed immediately.

48 He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you; go in peace."

49 While He was still speaking, someone came from the house of the synagogue ruler, saying, "Your daughter has died; do not trouble the Teacher any longer."

50 Hearing this, Jesus answered him, "Do not fear; only believe, and she will be saved."

51 When He came to the house, He allowed no one to enter with Him except Peter, John, James, and the father and mother of the child. 52 Everyone was weeping and lamenting for her; but He said, "Do not weep, for she is not dead but sleeping."

53 They laughed at Him, knowing that she was dead.

54 But He took her by the hand and called out, "Child, arise." 55 Her spirit returned, and immediately she rose up; and He directed that something be given her to eat.

56 Her parents were astonished; but He charged them to tell no one what had happened.
