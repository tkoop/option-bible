Joshua Secretly Dispatches Scouts to Reconnoiter Jericho

1 Joshua son of Nun clandestinely dispatched two men from Shittim to spy out the land, saying, "Go, reconnoiter the land, especially Jericho." So they went and entered the house of a prostitute named Rahab and lodged there.

2 It was reported to the king of Jericho, "Behold, men from among the Israelites have come here tonight to search out the land."

3 Then the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you, who entered your house, for they have come to search out all the land."

Rahab Conceals the Israelite Scouts

4 But the woman had taken the two men and concealed them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know whence they came. 5 When dusk fell and the gate was being shut, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue them quickly; you may overtake them." 6 But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them among the stalks of flax she had arranged in order on the roof. 7 So the men pursued them along the way to the fords of the Jordan; and as soon as the pursuers had gone out, the gate was shut behind them.

Rahab’s Plea and the Spies’ Solemn Oath

8 Before the spies had lain down, she came up to them on the roof. 9 She said to the men, "I know that the the LORD has given you the land, and that dread of you has fallen upon us, and all the inhabitants of the land are overcome with dread before you. 10 For we have heard how the the LORD dried up the waters of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to Sihon and Og, the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, whom you devoted to destruction. 11 When we heard these things, our courage failed, and no one retained any resolve because of you; for the the LORD your God, He is God in heaven above and on earth beneath. 12 Now therefore swear to me by the the LORD that, since I have dealt kindly with you, you will deal kindly with my father's house, and give me a trustworthy pledge. 13 Spare my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and all who belong to them, and deliver our lives from death."

14 The men said to her, "We pledge our lives for yours, provided you do not disclose this business of ours. And when the the LORD gives us the land, we will deal with you in kindness and truth."

15 Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was built into the city wall, and she dwelt in the wall. 16 She said to them, "Go to the hill country, lest the pursuers meet you; hide there three days until the pursuers have returned, and afterward go your way."

17 The men said to her, "We will be innocent of this oath that you have made us swear, 18 provided that, when we come into the land, you tie this scarlet cord in the window through which you let us down, and gather into your house your father and mother, your brothers, and all your father's household. 19 Whoever goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be upon his own head, and we shall be innocent; but if anyone with you in the house is harmed, his blood shall be upon our heads. 20 But if you disclose this business of ours, we shall be innocent of the oath you made us swear."

21 She said, "According to your words, so be it." Then she sent them away, and they departed; and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

The Scouts Return to Joshua with Their Report

22 They went and came to the hill country and remained there three days, until the pursuers had returned. The pursuers searched all along the road but did not find them. 23 Then the two men returned and came down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him all that had happened to them.

24 They said to Joshua, "Truly the the LORD has given all the land into our hand; indeed, all the inhabitants of the land are overcome with dread before us."
