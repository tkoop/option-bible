David’s Song of Deliverance

1 David spoke these words to the LORD in song on the day the LORD delivered him from the grasp of all his enemies and from Saul’s hand. 2 “The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. 3 God is my rock, in whom I take refuge; He is my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold and my refuge, my Savior, who saves me from violence. 4 I call upon the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

Delivered from the Waters of Death

5 “For the breakers of death surrounded me; the torrents of destruction terrified me. 6 The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me. 7 In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried out to my God, and from His temple He heard my voice; my cry came into His ears.

8 “Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of the heavens quaked and were shaken, because He was angry. 9 Smoke ascended from His nostrils, and devouring fire came from His mouth; coals were kindled by it. 10 He bowed the heavens and descended, with thick darkness beneath His feet. 11 He rode upon a cherub and flew; He was seen upon the wings of the wind. 12 He made darkness His canopy around Him—dark waters and thick clouds of the skies. 13 From the brightness before Him coals of fire burned. 14 The LORD thundered from heaven; the Most High uttered His voice. 15 He shot forth arrows and scattered them; He flashed lightning and routed them. 16 Then the channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils.

17 “He sent from on high and took me; He drew me out of many waters. 18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me. 19 They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the LORD was my support. 20 He brought me out into a broad place; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

Righteousness, Recompense, and Strength

21 “The LORD has dealt with me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He has repaid me. 22 For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. 23 For all His ordinances are before me, and from His statutes I have not turned aside. 24 I have been blameless before Him, and I have kept myself from my iniquity. 25 Therefore the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in His sight.

26 “With the faithful You show Yourself faithful; with the blameless man You show Yourself blameless. 27 With the pure You show Yourself pure, and with the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. 28 You save an afflicted people, but Your eyes are upon the haughty to bring them down. 29 For You are my lamp, O the LORD; and the LORD lightens my darkness. 30 For by You I run against a troop; by my God I leap over a wall. 31 As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is proven; He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

32 “For who is God besides the LORD? And who is a rock besides our God? 33 God is my strength and fortress, and He makes my way perfect. 34 He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me upon my high places. 35 He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 36 You have also given me the shield of Your salvation, and Your gentleness has made me great. 37 You enlarge my steps beneath me, so that my feet do not slip.

Triumph, Dominion, and Everlasting Praise

38 “I pursued my enemies and destroyed them; I did not turn back until they were consumed. 39 I consumed them and shattered them, so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet. 40 For You have girded me with strength for battle; You subdued under me those who rose up against me. 41 You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and I silenced those who hated me. 42 They looked, but there was no savior; they cried to the LORD, but He did not answer them. 43 I beat them as fine as the dust of the earth; I crushed and trampled them like mud in the streets.

44 “You have delivered me from the strivings of my people; You have kept me as head of the nations; a people I did not know serves me. 45 Foreigners cringe before me; as soon as they hear of me, they obey me. 46 Foreigners fade away and come trembling from their strongholds.

47 “The LORD lives, and blessed be my rock; and exalted be God, the rock of my salvation— 48 the God who gives me vengeance and brings down peoples under me, 49 who brings me out from my enemies. You exalt me above those who rise against me; You deliver me from the violent man. 50 Therefore I will give thanks to You, O the LORD, among the nations, and I will sing praises to Your name. 51 He is a tower of salvation to His king, and shows steadfast love to His anointed, to David and to his offspring forever.”
