Purification of the Encampment

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Command the Israelites to send out of the camp every leper, every person with a discharge, and everyone defiled by contact with a corpse. 3 Both male and female shall be sent out; they shall be removed outside the camp, so that they do not defile the camps in whose midst I dwell among them.” 4 The Israelites did so: they sent them outside the camp, just as the LORD had spoken to Moses, so the Israelites did.

Restitution and Sacred Offerings

5 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 6 “Speak to the Israelites: When a man or woman commits any of the transgressions by which mortals offend the LORD, and thereby incurs guilt, 7 they shall confess the sin they have committed, make full restitution for their guilt, add one-fifth to it, and give it to the person against whom they have offended. 8 But if the injured party has no kinsman-redeemer to whom restitution may be made, the restitution due to the LORD shall be given to the priest, in addition to the ram of atonement by which atonement is made for the offender. 9 Every contribution of sacred things that the Israelites present to the priest shall belong to him. 10 Each person's consecrated gifts shall remain his own, but whatever anyone gives to the priest shall become the priest's.”

The Ordeal of Suspected Adultery

11 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 12 “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: If any man's wife goes astray and acts unfaithfully against him, 13 and another man lies with her carnally, and the matter is hidden from her husband's eyes, and she is defiled, and there is no witness against her, nor was she apprehended, 14 and a spirit of jealousy comes upon him, so that he is jealous of his wife, whether she is defiled or not, 15 then the man shall bring his wife to the priest and bring her offering for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He shall pour no oil upon it and put no frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, recalling iniquity. 16 The priest shall bring her near and set her before the LORD. 17 The priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel and take some of the dust from the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water. 18 The priest shall set the woman before the LORD, uncover her head, and place in her hands the grain offering of remembrance, that is, the grain offering of jealousy; and the priest shall hold in his hand the water of bitterness that brings a curse.

19 “Then the priest shall make her swear and say to her: ‘If no man has lain with you, and if you have not gone astray to uncleanness while under your husband, be free from this water of bitterness that brings a curse.’ 20 But if you have gone astray while under your husband, and if you are defiled, and a man other than your husband has lain with you, 21 then the priest shall make the woman swear by the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to her: ‘May the LORD make you a curse and an oath among your people, when the LORD causes your belly to swell and you to miscarry.’

22 “These waters of bitterness that bring a curse shall enter your bowels, to make your belly swell and cause you to miscarry. And the woman shall say, ‘Amen, Amen.’

23 “Then the priest shall write these curses in a book and wash them off into the water of bitterness. 24 He shall make the woman drink the water of bitterness that brings a curse, and the water that brings a curse shall enter her for bitterness. 25 The priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman's hand, wave it before the LORD, and bring it to the altar. 26 The priest shall take a handful of the grain offering, its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar; afterward he shall make the woman drink the water. 27 When he has made her drink the water, then, if she is defiled and has acted unfaithfully against her husband, the water that brings a curse shall enter her for bitterness, her belly shall swell, she shall miscarry, and the woman shall become a curse among her people. 28 But if the woman is not defiled, but is clean, she shall be free and shall conceive offspring.

29 “This is the law of jealousy, when a wife goes astray while under her husband and is defiled, 30 or when a spirit of jealousy comes upon a man and he is jealous of his wife. Then he shall set the woman before the LORD, and the priest shall do to her according to all this law. 31 The man shall be free from iniquity, and that woman shall bear her iniquity.”
