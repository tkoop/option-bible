The Genealogical Record and Migration of Esau

1 The following constitutes the genealogical register of Esau, whose identity is inextricably bound to Edom. 2 Esau contracted matrimonial alliances among the Canaanite population, taking Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite; Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite; 3 and Basemath, daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth. 4 Through Adah he begot Eliphaz; through Basemath, Reuel; 5 and through Oholibamah, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah—all of whom were born to him within Canaan.

6 Esau thereafter assembled his wives, sons, daughters, every constituent of his household, his livestock and beasts, together with all property he had amassed in Canaan, and he migrated to a region removed from his brother Jacob. 7 Their aggregated possessions had become too prodigious for communal habitation; the land of their sojourn could not sustain them because of their herds. 8 Consequently, Esau established himself in the hill country of Seir, and Esau is Edom.

9 Such, then, are the generations of Esau, father of Edom, in the hill country of Seir.

The Descendants and Chieftains of Esau

10 These are the names of Esau's sons: Eliphaz, son of Esau by Adah, and Reuel, son of Esau by Basemath. 11 Eliphaz's sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 12 Timna, who functioned as concubine to Eliphaz, son of Esau, bore Amalek to him; these are the sons associated with Adah, Esau's wife. 13 Reuel's sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah—the sons associated with Basemath, Esau's wife. 14 The sons of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, Esau's wife, were Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

15 These are the chiefs among the sons of Esau: from the line of Eliphaz, Esau's firstborn, emerged Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, and Chief Kenaz; 16 Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, and Chief Amalek likewise belonged to this lineage. These were the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Edom, descending from Adah. 17 The sons of Reuel, Esau's son, produced Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, and Chief Mizzah; these were the chiefs of Reuel in Edom, descending from Basemath, Esau's wife. 18 The sons of Oholibamah, Esau's wife, produced Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, and Chief Korah; these were the chiefs of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and wife of Esau. 19 These are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs; he is Edom.

The Horite Inhabitants of Seir

20 These are the sons of Seir the Horite, the autochthonous inhabitants of the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 21 Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites, the sons of Seir in the land of Edom. 22 The sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam, and Lotan's sister was Timna. 23 The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 24 The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah; this is the Anah who discovered the hot springs in the wilderness while pasturing the donkeys of his father Zibeon. 25 The sons of Anah were Dishon and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah. 26 The sons of Dishon were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 27 The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 28 The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran. 29 These are the chiefs of the Horites: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah, 30 Chief Dishon, Chief Ezer, and Chief Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites, enumerated according to their chiefs in the land of Seir.

The Kings and Territorial Chiefs of Edom

31 These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: 32 Bela, son of Beor, reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah. 33 Upon Bela's death, Jobab, son of Zerah of Bozrah, succeeded him. 34 Upon Jobab's death, Husham from the land of the Temanites succeeded him. 35 Upon Husham's death, Hadad, son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the field of Moab, succeeded him, and the name of his city was Avith. 36 Upon Hadad's death, Samlah from Masrekah succeeded him. 37 Upon Samlah's death, Saul from Rehoboth on the River succeeded him. 38 Upon Saul's death, Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, succeeded him. 39 Upon the death of Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, Hadar succeeded him; the name of his city was Pau, and his wife was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezahab.

40 These are the names of the chiefs of Esau, according to their families and localities, by their names: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth, 41 Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon, 42 Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar, 43 Chief Magdiel, and Chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom, according to their settlements in the land of their possession. He is Esau, the father of Edom.
