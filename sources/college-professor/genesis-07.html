The Divine Mandate to Enter the Ark

1 The LORD said to Noah, "Come into the ark—you and your entire household—for I have found you righteous before Me in this generation. 2 You are to take with you seven pairs of every clean animal, a male with its mate, and one pair of every unclean animal, a male with its mate, 3 as well as seven pairs of the birds of the sky, male and female, so that their offspring may be preserved upon the face of the whole earth. 4 For in seven days I will cause rain to fall upon the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will eradicate from the face of the earth every living thing that I have made."

5 Noah executed all that the LORD had commanded him. 6 Noah was six hundred years old when the deluge came upon the earth. 7 Noah entered the ark with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives, to find refuge from the waters of the flood.

8 Clean and unclean animals, birds, and every creature that crawls upon the ground 9 came to Noah and entered the ark, two by two, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.

The Onset of the Deluge

10 After seven days, the floodwaters came upon the earth. 11 In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, on the seventeenth day of the second month—on that very day—all the fountains of the great deep burst forth, and the floodgates of heaven were opened. 12 Rain fell upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.

The Household and the Creatures Enter the Ark

13 On that very day Noah and his sons—Shem, Ham, and Japheth—together with Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons entered the ark. 14 They were joined by every beast according to its kind, all livestock according to their kinds, every creature that moves along the ground according to its kind, and every bird according to its kind, every winged creature. 15 They came to Noah and entered the ark, two by two, of all flesh in which there was the breath of life. 16 Those that entered, male and female of every living creature, came as God had commanded Noah; then the LORD shut him in.

The Flood Prevails over the Earth

17 The deluge remained upon the earth for forty days, and the waters increased and lifted the ark high above the earth. 18 The waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the earth, and the ark drifted upon the surface of the waters. 19 The waters prevailed so overwhelmingly upon the earth that all the high mountains beneath the whole heaven were covered. 20 The waters rose fifteen cubits above the mountains.

21 Every living thing that moved on the earth perished—birds, livestock, beasts, every creature that swarms upon the earth, and all humanity. 22 Everything that had the breath of life in its nostrils and lived on dry land died. 23 Thus He obliterated every living thing upon the face of the earth—humanity and livestock, creeping things and birds of the air—and they were blotted out from the earth. Only Noah and those with him in the ark survived. 24 The waters prevailed upon the earth for one hundred fifty days.
