The Covenant Renewed in Moab

1 These are the words of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to make with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant He had made with them at Horeb.

Moses Summons Israel and Recalls the LORD’s Deliverance

2 Moses summoned all Israel and said to them, “You have seen all that the LORD accomplished before your eyes in the land of Egypt—to Pharaoh, to all his servants, and to all his land. 3 Your eyes saw the great trials, the signs, and those great wonders. 4 Yet to this day the LORD has not granted you a heart to know, eyes to see, or ears to hear.

“5 I have led you forty years through the wilderness; your garments have not worn out upon you, nor have your sandals worn off your feet. 6 You have eaten no bread and drunk neither wine nor strong drink, so that you might know that I am the LORD your God.

“7 When you arrived at this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out against us to battle, and we defeated them. 8 We seized their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 9 Therefore observe the words of this covenant and put them into practice, that you may prosper in all that you do.

The Solemn Covenant Assembly

“10 You are all standing today before the LORD your God—your heads, your tribes, your elders, and your officers, every man in Israel— 11 your little ones, your wives, and the resident alien in the midst of your camps, from the hewer of wood to the drawer of water, 12 to enter into the covenant of the LORD your God and into His oath, which the LORD your God is making with you today, 13 that He may establish you today as His people and be your God, just as He spoke to you and as He swore to your fathers—to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

“14 And it is not with you alone that I am making this covenant and this oath, 15 but with him who stands here with us today before the LORD our God and with him who is not here with us today.

“16 For you know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt and how we passed through the midst of the nations through which you passed. 17 You saw their abominations and their idols—wood and stone, silver and gold—that were among them. 18 Lest there be among you a man or woman, or family or tribe, whose heart turns away today from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations; lest there be among you a root bearing poisonous fruit and wormwood. 19 And when such a one hears the words of this curse, he blesses himself in his heart, saying, ‘I shall have peace, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart,’ thereby bringing disaster upon the watered land as well as the dry.

Divine Retribution for Apostasy and Exile

“20 The LORD will not consent to forgive him; rather, the anger of the LORD and His jealousy will burn against that man, and all the curse written in this book will settle upon him, and the LORD will blot out his name from under heaven. 21 The LORD will single him out for disaster from all the tribes of Israel, in accordance with all the curses of the covenant written in this Book of the Law.

“22 And the generation to come—your children who rise after you, and the foreigner who comes from a distant land—will say, when they see the plagues of that land and the diseases that the LORD has inflicted upon it, 23 ‘Its whole land is sulfur and salt, a burnt wasteland, unsown and unproductive, where no grass grows, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in His anger and wrath.’

“24 All nations will say, ‘Why has the LORD done thus to this land? What is the meaning of this great and burning anger?’ 25 Then they will say, ‘Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He made with them when He brought them out of the land of Egypt. 26 They went and served other gods and worshiped them—gods they did not know and that He had not allotted to them. 27 Therefore the anger of the LORD burned against this land, to bring upon it every curse written in this book. 28 The LORD uprooted them from their land in anger, wrath, and great indignation, and cast them into another land, as it is this day.’

“29 The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things revealed belong to us and to our children forever, that we may do all the words of this law.”
