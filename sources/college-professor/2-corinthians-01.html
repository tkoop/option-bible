Apostolic Salutation and Thanksgiving

1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy our brother, to the church of God that is in Corinth, together with all the saints throughout Achaia: 2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and the God of all comfort, 4 who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction with the comfort by which we ourselves are comforted by God. 5 For just as the sufferings of Christ overflow to us, so also, through Christ, our comfort overflows. 6 If we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which proves effective as you patiently endure the same sufferings that we also suffer. 7 And our hope for you is unshaken, because we know that as you share in our sufferings, so you will also share in our comfort.

Deliverance from Mortal Peril in Asia

8 For we do not want you, brothers, to be ignorant of the affliction that befell us in Asia, that we were burdened beyond measure, beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of life itself. 9 Indeed, we felt within ourselves the sentence of death, so that we might not place our confidence in ourselves but in God, who raises the dead. 10 He delivered us from so great a death, and He will deliver us; in Him we have set our hope that He will deliver us still. 11 You also must help us by your prayers, so that many may give thanks on our behalf for the gracious gift bestowed upon us through the prayers of many.

Integrity of Conscience and Travel Plans

12 For our boast is this: the testimony of our conscience that we conducted ourselves in the world, and more abundantly toward you, in holiness and sincerity from God, not by fleshly wisdom but by the grace of God. 13 For we write to you nothing other than what you read and understand; and I hope that you will understand fully, even to the end, 14 just as you have understood us in part, that we are your boast, just as you are ours, in the day of our Lord Jesus. 15 In this confidence I intended to come to you first, so that you might receive a double benefit, 16 and to pass through you into Macedonia, and again to come to you from Macedonia, and to be sent on by you to Judea. 17 So when I planned this, did I act with frivolity? Or do I make my plans according to the flesh, so that with me there is Yes, yes, and No, no? 18 But God is faithful, and our word to you is not Yes and No. 19 For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, whom we proclaimed among you—through me, Silvanus, and Timothy—was not Yes and No, but in Him it has always been Yes. 20 For every promise of God finds its Yes in Him; and through Him we say Amen to the glory of God. 21 Now God, who establishes us with you in Christ and who anointed us, 22 has also sealed us and given us the Spirit in our hearts as a pledge. 23 But I call God as witness against my own soul that it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth. 24 Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers with you for your joy, for you stand firm by faith.
