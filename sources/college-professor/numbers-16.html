Korah’s Rebellion and the Challenge to Leadership

1 Now Korah son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, together with Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth, descendants of Reuben, took certain Israelite men and set themselves in open defiance; 2 they presented themselves before Moses with two hundred fifty leaders of the congregation—men of renown, regularly summoned to the assembly.

3 They said to Moses and Aaron, “You have carried your presumption too far, for the entire congregation is holy, every one of them, and the LORD dwells among them. Why then do you exalt yourselves above the assembly of the LORD?”

4 When Moses heard this, he fell upon his face.

The Test of Divine Election

5 Then he spoke to Korah and to all his faction, saying, “In the morning the LORD will make known who belongs to Him and who is holy, and He will bring that one near to Himself; indeed, the one whom He chooses He will bring near to Himself. 6 Do this: take censers for yourselves, Korah and all your faction, 7 put fire in them, and lay incense upon them before the LORD tomorrow. The man whom the LORD chooses shall be the holy one. You Levites have carried your presumption too far! 8 Hear now, you sons of Levi: 9 is it too small a thing for you that the God of Israel has set you apart from the congregation of Israel, to bring you near to Himself for the service of the tabernacle of the LORD and to stand before the congregation to minister to them? 10 He has brought you near, along with all your brothers, the sons of Levi, and now you seek the priesthood as well. 11 Therefore you and all your faction are gathered together against the LORD; and what is Aaron, that you murmur against him?”

12 Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and they replied, “We will not come up. 13 Is it a trivial matter that you brought us up from a land of abundant prosperity only to kill us in the wilderness, and now you would also lord it over us? 14 Besides, you have not brought us into a land of abundant prosperity or given us an inheritance of fields and vineyards. Would you blind these men? We will not come up.”

15 Moses was exceedingly angry, and he said to the LORD, “Do not regard their offering. I have not taken one donkey from them, nor have I wronged a single one of them.”

16 Then Moses said to Korah, “You and all your faction must appear before the LORD tomorrow—you, they, and Aaron. 17 Let each man take his censer, put incense on it, and bring it before the LORD—two hundred fifty censers in all; you and Aaron shall each bring his own.”

18 So each man took his censer, put fire in it, laid incense on it, and stood at the entrance of the tent of meeting, together with Moses and Aaron. 19 Korah assembled the whole congregation against them at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD appeared to the entire congregation.

20 Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 21 “Separate yourselves from among this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment.” 22 But they fell upon their faces and said, “O God, God of the spirits of all flesh, shall one man sin, and will You be angry with the entire congregation?”

23 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 24 “Speak to the congregation, saying, ‘Withdraw from the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.’” 25 So Moses rose and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel followed him. 26 He warned the congregation, “Depart, I beg you, from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing that belongs to them, lest you be swept away in all their sin.” 27 Thus they withdrew from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram; and Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrances of their tents, with their wives, their sons, and their little ones.

The Earth Swallows the Rebels

28 And Moses said, “By this you shall know that the LORD has sent me to do all these works, for I have not acted of my own accord. 29 If these men die the common death of all men, or if they suffer the ordinary fate of all mankind, then the LORD has not sent me. 30 But if the LORD creates something new, and the earth opens its mouth and swallows them up, with all that belongs to them, so that they descend alive into Sheol, then you shall understand that these men have despised the LORD.”

31 As soon as he finished speaking all these words, the ground beneath them split open. 32 The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up, together with their households and all the people who belonged to Korah, along with all their possessions. 33 They and all that belonged to them went down alive into Sheol; the earth closed over them, and they perished from the midst of the assembly. 34 All Israel around them fled at their outcry, for they said, “Lest the earth swallow us up as well.” 35 Then fire came out from the LORD and consumed the two hundred fifty men who had offered the incense.

36 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 37 “Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to remove the censers from the burning and scatter the fire abroad, for they are holy. 38 As for the censers of these sinners against their own lives, let them be hammered into plates as a covering for the altar, because they were offered before the LORD and are holy; thus they shall serve as a sign to the children of Israel.” 39 So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers that had been offered by those who were burned, and they were hammered into a covering for the altar, 40 as a memorial to the children of Israel, so that no outsider who is not of the seed of Aaron might draw near to offer incense before the LORD, lest he become like Korah and his faction, just as the LORD had spoken to him through Moses.

The Plague and Aaron’s Intercession

41 But on the next day the whole congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have killed the people of the LORD.” 42 And when the congregation assembled against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the tent of meeting, and behold, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the LORD appeared. 43 Then Moses and Aaron came to the front of the tent of meeting.

44 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 45 “Get away from among this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment.” And they fell upon their faces.

46 And Moses said to Aaron, “Take the censer, put fire on it from the altar, lay incense on it, and hurry to the congregation and make atonement for them; for wrath has gone out from the LORD—the plague has begun.” 47 So Aaron took it, as Moses had spoken, and ran into the midst of the assembly; and behold, the plague had already begun among the people. He put on the incense and made atonement for the people. 48 He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was arrested. 49 Those who died by the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died on account of Korah. 50 Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the plague was arrested.
