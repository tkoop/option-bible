The Vision of the Golden Lampstand

1 Then the angel who had been speaking with me returned and awakened me, as one is awakened from his slumber.

2 He said to me, "What do you see?"

I replied, "I see, and behold, a lampstand all of gold, with a bowl upon its summit, and its seven lamps upon it, with seven spouts for each of the lamps that rest upon its summit; 3 and two olive trees beside it, one to the right of the bowl and the other to its left."

4 I answered and spoke to the angel who had been speaking with me, saying, "What are these, my lord?"

5 The angel who had been speaking with me answered and said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

The Word of the the LORD to Zerubbabel

6 Then he answered and spoke to me, saying, "This is the word of the the LORD to Zerubbabel: 'Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit,' says the the LORD of Hosts. 7 Who are you, O great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you shall become a plain. And he shall bring forth the capstone amid shouts of 'Grace, grace to it!'"

The Completion of the Temple

8 Moreover, the word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 9 "The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands shall bring it to completion. Then you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you. 10 For who has despised the day of small beginnings? These seven are the eyes of the the LORD, which roam throughout the whole earth; they will rejoice when they see the plumb line in the hand of Zerubbabel."

The Two Olive Trees and the Anointed Ones

11 Then I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees to the right of the lampstand and to its left?" 12 And I answered a second time and said to him, "What are these two olive branches that lie beside the two golden pipes that pour out the golden oil?"

13 He answered me and said, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

14 Then he said, "These are the two anointed ones who stand by the Lord of all the earth."
