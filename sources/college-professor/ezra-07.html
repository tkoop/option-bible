Ezra's Sacerdotal Genealogy and Postexilic Repatriation

1 Subsequent to these occurrences, during the reign of Artaxerxes, king of Persia, Ezra—son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah, 2 son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub, 3 son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth, 4 son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki, 5 son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest—ascended from Babylon.

6 This Ezra undertook the ascent from Babylon as a scribe preeminently proficient in the law of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given; and the king granted him every petition, in accordance with the hand of the LORD his God upon him. 7 Moreover, certain of the Israelites, together with priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants, ascended to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

Scribal Erudition and the Zionward Pilgrimage

8 He reached Jerusalem in the fifth month, in the seventh year of the king. 9 For he had commenced the ascent from Babylon on the first day of the first month, and he arrived in Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month, by the gracious hand of his God upon him. 10 For Ezra had devoted his heart to studying the law of the LORD, to practicing it, and to teaching in Israel statutes and ordinances.

The Imperial Decree and Cultic Provisions

11 This is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe—a scribe of the words of the commandments of the LORD and of His statutes for Israel:

12 “Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of the Law of the God of heaven: Greetings. 13 I hereby decree that anyone of the people of Israel within my realm, including their priests and Levites, who freely volunteers to accompany you to Jerusalem, may go.

14 “For you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to investigate Judah and Jerusalem according to the Law of your God that is in your hand, 15 and to convey the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem, 16 together with all the silver and gold that you may find throughout the province of Babylon, along with the freewill offerings of the people and of the priests who freely offer for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

17 “With this money, therefore, you shall diligently purchase bulls, rams, and lambs, together with their grain offerings and drink offerings, and you shall offer them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. 18 Whatever seems good to you and to your brothers to do with the remaining silver and gold, you may do according to the will of your God.

19 “The vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in full before the God of Jerusalem. 20 Whatever else is needed for the house of your God that you may need to provide, provide it from the royal treasury.

21 “And I, King Artaxerxes, issue a decree to all the treasurers in the province Beyond the River: Whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the Law of the God of heaven, asks of you, let it be done diligently, 22 up to one hundred talents of silver, one hundred cors of wheat, one hundred baths of wine, one hundred baths of oil, and salt without prescribed limit.

23 “Whatever is commanded by the God of heaven, let it be done with diligence for the house of the God of heaven, lest wrath fall upon the realm of the king and his sons. 24 We also inform you that it shall not be lawful to impose tribute, custom, or toll upon any of the priests, Levites, singers, doorkeepers, temple servants, or other servants of this house of God.

25 “And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges to judge all the people in the province Beyond the River—all who know the laws of your God—and instruct those who do not know them. 26 Whoever will not obey the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgment be executed upon him diligently, whether by death, banishment, confiscation of goods, or imprisonment.”

Doxological Thanksgiving and Communal Mobilization

27 Blessed be the LORD, the God of our fathers, who put such a thing into the heart of the king, to adorn the house of the LORD that is in Jerusalem, 28 and who extended covenant mercy to me before the king, his counselors, and all the king's mighty nobles. Thus I was strengthened according to the hand of the LORD my God upon me, and I gathered the principal men of Israel to go up with me.
