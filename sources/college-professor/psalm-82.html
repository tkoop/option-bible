The Divine Council and the Rebuke of Unjust Judges

1 A Psalm of Asaph. God takes His stand in the divine council; in the midst of the gods He renders judgment.

2 “How long will you render unjust judgments and show partiality to the wicked? 3 Vindicate the powerless and the fatherless; uphold the rights of the afflicted and the oppressed. 4 Deliver the weak and the needy; rescue them from the hand of the wicked.” Selah.

The Blindness of the Wicked and the Mortality of the So-Called Gods

5 They know nothing and understand nothing; they walk about in darkness, and all the foundations of the earth are destabilized.

6 “I said, ‘You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High.’ 7 Yet you will perish like mortals; you will fall like any of the princes.”

A Plea for God’s Righteous Judgment

8 Rise up, O God, and judge the earth, for all the nations are Your inheritance.
