The Moral Economy of Righteousness and Wickedness

1 Dishonest scales are an abomination to the LORD, whereas an honest weight is His delight. 2 When pride arrives, disgrace follows; yet wisdom resides with the humble. 3 The integrity of the upright guides them, but the perversity of the treacherous destroys them. 4 Wealth proves utterly inefficacious in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from death.

5 The righteousness of the blameless keeps his way straight, but the wicked falls by his own wickedness. 6 The righteousness of the upright delivers them, whereas the treacherous are ensnared by their own desires. 7 When a wicked man dies, his hope perishes, and the expectation vested in his strength vanishes. 8 The righteous is delivered from trouble, and the wicked takes his place.

Speech, Counsel, and Communal Well-Being

9 By his mouth the godless destroys his neighbor, but through knowledge the righteous are delivered. 10 When righteousness prospers, the city rejoices; when the wicked perish, jubilation ensues. 11 By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but by the mouth of the wicked it is destroyed. 12 Whoever belittles his neighbor lacks sense, but a man of understanding holds his peace.

13 A talebearer reveals secrets, but he who is trustworthy in spirit conceals a matter. 14 In the absence of guidance, a people falls, but in an abundance of counselors there is safety.

Generosity, Security, and the Fruits of Character

15 He who puts up security for a stranger will surely suffer, but he who hates pledging himself as surety remains secure. 16 A gracious woman obtains honor, and ruthless men obtain riches. 17 A kind man benefits himself, but a cruel man harms himself. 18 The wicked earns deceptive wages, but he who sows righteousness reaps a true reward. 19 He who is steadfast in righteousness attains life, but he who pursues evil meets death.

20 The LORD abhors those of crooked heart, but those whose way is blameless are His delight. 21 Be assured that the wicked will not go unpunished, but the descendants of the righteous will be delivered. 22 Like a gold ring in a pig's snout is a beautiful woman without discretion. 23 The desire of the righteous results only in good, but the expectation of the wicked brings wrath. 24 One gives freely, yet grows all the richer; another withholds unduly, but comes to poverty.

25 A generous person will be enriched, and one who waters others will himself be watered. 26 The people curse the hoarder of grain, but a blessing rests upon the head of the seller. 27 He who diligently seeks good finds favor, but evil comes to him who searches for it.

Final Recompense and the Tree of Life

28 He who trusts in his riches will fall, but the righteous will flourish like a green leaf. 29 He who troubles his own household will reap only emptiness, and the fool will be servant to the wise in heart. 30 The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and he who wins people over is wise. 31 If the righteous are recompensed on earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner!
