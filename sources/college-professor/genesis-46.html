Jacob's Departure and Divine Reassurance at Beersheba

1 Israel departed with all his possessions and came to Beersheba, where he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.

2 During the night God spoke to Israel in a vision, saying, “Jacob, Jacob.”

“Here I am,” he replied.

3 And He said, “I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for there I will make of you a great nation. 4 I will go down with you to Egypt, and I will surely bring you up again; Joseph will close your eyes.”

5 Jacob then arose from Beersheba, and the sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, along with their children and wives, in the wagons Pharaoh had sent to transport him. 6 They took their livestock and the property they had acquired in the land of Canaan, and Jacob came into Egypt with all his descendants. 7 He brought with him his sons and grandsons, his daughters and granddaughters—in short, all his progeny.

The Genealogical Register of Jacob's Household

8 These are the names of the Israelites who entered Egypt—Jacob and his sons—with Reuben as Jacob's firstborn. 9 The sons of Reuben were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 10 The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman. 11 The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 12 The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah; Er and Onan died in Canaan, and the sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.

13 The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puvah, Job, and Shimron. 14 The sons of Zebulun were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. 15 These were the sons of Leah whom she bore to Jacob in Paddan-aram, together with his daughter Dinah; all his sons and daughters by her numbered thirty-three persons.

16 The sons of Gad were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. 17 The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah; the sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel. 18 These were the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Leah; she bore them to Jacob—sixteen persons.

19 The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife, were Joseph and Benjamin. 20 In Egypt, Joseph's sons Manasseh and Ephraim were born to him by Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of On. 21 The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard. 22 These were the descendants of Rachel born to Jacob—fourteen persons in all.

23 The son of Dan was Hushim. 24 The sons of Naphtali were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem. 25 These were the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel; she bore them to Jacob—seven persons.

26 All the persons who came with Jacob to Egypt—those begotten of his body, apart from the wives of his sons—numbered sixty-six. 27 Joseph had two sons born to him in Egypt. Thus all the persons of Jacob's household who came to Egypt were seventy.

Reunion in Goshen and Counsel for the Royal Audience

28 Judah was sent ahead of him to Joseph to guide him to Goshen, and they entered the land of Goshen. 29 Joseph prepared his chariot and went up to Goshen to meet his father Israel; when he saw him, he fell upon his neck and wept there at length.

30 Israel said to Joseph, “Now I may die in peace, for I have seen your face and you still live.”

31 Joseph said to his brothers and to his father, “I will go up and inform Pharaoh, and I will say to him, ‘My brothers and my father's household, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me. 32 The men are shepherds, for they have been keepers of livestock; they have brought their flocks, their herds, and all their possessions.’ 33 When Pharaoh summons you and asks, ‘What is your occupation?’ 34 you are to say, ‘Your servants have tended livestock from our youth until now, as our fathers did before us,’ so that you may settle in Goshen, for shepherds are an abomination to the Egyptians.”
