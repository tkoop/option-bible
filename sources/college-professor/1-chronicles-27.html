David’s Rotating Military Divisions

1 The sons of Israel were enumerated according to their number—heads of ancestral houses, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officers who served the king in every matter concerning the divisions that came in and went out month by month throughout all the months of the year—each division comprising twenty-four thousand men. 2 Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was over the first division for the first month, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 3 He was descended from Perez and served as chief of all the army commanders for the first month.

4 Dodai the Ahohite was over the division for the second month, with Mikloth as leader; and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 5 The third commander, for the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 6 This Benaiah was a mighty man among the Thirty and was over the Thirty, and his son Ammizabad was in charge of his division.

7 The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel, Joab’s brother, with Zebadiah his son succeeding him; and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 8 The fifth, for the fifth month, was Shamhuth the Izrahite, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 9 The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

10 The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, of the Ephraimites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 11 The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 12 The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjamites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

13 The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 14 The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, of the Ephraimites, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 15 The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

Tribal Princes of Israel

16 Furthermore, over the tribes of Israel: Eliezer son of Zichri was chief officer of the Reubenites; Shephatiah son of Maacah, of the Simeonites; 17 Hashabiah son of Kemuel, of the Levites; Zadok, of Aaron; 18 Elihu, one of David’s brothers, of Judah; Omri son of Michael, of Issachar; 19 Ishmaiah son of Obadiah, of Zebulun; Jerimoth son of Azriel, of Naphtali; 20 Hoshea son of Azaziah, of the Ephraimites; Joel son of Pedaiah, of the half-tribe of Manasseh; 21 Iddo son of Zechariah, of the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead; Jaasiel son of Abner, of Benjamin; 22 Azarel son of Jeroham, of Dan. These were the princes of the tribes of Israel.

The Census and Its Consequences

23 But David did not number those twenty years old and under, because the LORD had said He would multiply Israel like the stars of heaven. 24 Joab son of Zeruiah began to number them but did not finish, for wrath came upon Israel; nor was the total recorded in the chronicles of King David.

Royal Administrators and Counselors

25 Over the king’s treasuries was Azmaveth son of Adiel; over the storehouses in the fields, in the cities, in the villages, and in the towers was Jonathan son of Uzziah; 26 over those who did the work of the field in tilling the ground was Ezri son of Chelub; 27 over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite; over the produce of the vineyards for the wine cellars was Zabdi the Shiphmite; 28 over the olive trees and the sycamore trees in the Shephelah was Baal-hanan the Gederite; over the storehouses of oil was Joash; 29 over the cattle that fed in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite; over the cattle in the valleys was Shaphat son of Adlai; 30 over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite; over the donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite; 31 over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were the officials of King David.

32 Jonathan, David’s uncle, was also a counselor—a man of understanding and a scribe—and Jehiel son of Hachmoni was with the king’s sons. 33 Ahithophel was the king’s counselor, and Hushai the Archite was the king’s friend. 34 After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah and Abiathar. The commander of the king’s army was Joab.
