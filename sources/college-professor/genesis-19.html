The Arrival of the Angels at Sodom

1 In the evening, two angels arrived at Sodom while Lot was sitting in the city gate; upon seeing them, Lot rose to meet them and bowed with his face to the ground. 2 He said, “Please, my lords, turn aside into your servant’s house, wash your feet, spend the night, and rise early to continue your journey.”

They replied, “No; we shall spend the night in the square.” 3 Yet he urged them so insistently that they turned toward him and entered his house; there he prepared a feast, baking unleavened bread, and they ate.

The Wickedness of Sodom and Lot's Intercession

4 Before they retired, the men of the city—the men of Sodom, from the youngest to the oldest, every last one of them—surrounded the house. 5 They called to Lot and demanded, “Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us, that we may have sexual relations with them.”

6 Lot went out to them at the entrance and closed the door behind him. 7 He pleaded, “Please, my brothers, do not act so wickedly.” 8 “Look, I have two daughters who have never known a man; let me bring them out to you, and you may do with them as seems good to you. Only do nothing to these men, for they have come under the shelter of my roof.”

9 They retorted, “Stand back!” and added, “This fellow came as an alien, and now he presumes to judge us? We shall treat you worse than them.” They pressed Lot so violently that they nearly broke down the door.

The Deliverance of Lot and the Destruction of Sodom

10 The men reached out, drew Lot back into the house, and shut the door. 11 Then they struck the men at the doorway, from the least to the greatest, with blindness, so that they exhausted themselves trying to find the entrance.

12 The men said to Lot, “Whom else do you have here? Bring out your sons-in-law, your sons, your daughters, and anyone else belonging to you in the city; take them out of this place. 13 For we are about to destroy it, because the outcry against its people has grown so great before the LORD that the LORD has sent us to annihilate it.”

14 Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, the men betrothed to his daughters: “Get up and leave this place, for the LORD is about to destroy the city.” But his sons-in-law thought he was jesting.

15 At dawn the angels urged Lot, “Arise, take your wife and your two daughters who are here, lest you be swept away in the city’s punishment.”

16 When he hesitated, the men seized him, his wife, and his two daughters by the hand and brought them outside the city, for the LORD had compassion on him. 17 As soon as they had brought them out, one said, “Flee for your life! Do not look behind you, and do not stop anywhere in the plain; flee to the mountains, lest you be swept away.”

18 Lot answered them, “No, my lords, please! 19 Behold, your servant has found favor with you, and you have shown your abundant mercy to me by preserving my life; yet I cannot flee to the mountains, lest disaster overtake me and I perish. 20 Look, that city is near enough to flee to, and it is small. Please let me escape there—is it not a small place?—so that my life may be spared.”

21 He said to him, “Very well, I grant you this request as well: I will not overthrow the city of which you have spoken. 22 Hurry and escape there, for I cannot do anything until you arrive.” Therefore the city was called Zoar.

23 The sun had risen over the land when Lot entered Zoar. 24 Then the LORD rained sulfur and fire upon Sodom and Gomorrah from the LORD out of heaven. 25 He overthrew those cities, the entire plain, all the inhabitants of the cities, and the vegetation of the ground. 26 But Lot’s wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt. 27 Early in the morning, Abraham went to the place where he had stood before the LORD. 28 He looked down upon Sodom and Gomorrah and upon the whole plain, and he saw smoke rising from the land like the smoke of a furnace. 29 So when God destroyed the cities of the plain, God remembered Abraham and brought Lot out from the midst of the overthrow, when He overthrew the cities in which Lot had lived.

The Aftermath and the Origins of Moab and Ammon

30 Lot went up from Zoar and settled in the mountains with his two daughters, for he was afraid to remain in Zoar; so he and his daughters lived in a cave. 31 The older daughter said to the younger, “Our father is old, and there is no man in the land to marry us according to the custom of all humankind. 32 Come, let us make our father drink wine, and we will lie with him, so that we may preserve offspring through our father.” 33 That night they made their father drink wine, and the older daughter went in and lay with her father; he did not know when she lay down or when she rose. 34 The next day the older daughter said to the younger, “I lay with my father last night. Let us make him drink wine tonight as well; then you go in and lie with him, so that we may preserve offspring through our father.” 35 So they made their father drink wine that night too, and the younger daughter arose and lay with him; but he did not know when she lay down or when she rose.

36 Thus both of Lot’s daughters conceived by their father. 37 The older bore a son and named him Moab; he is the ancestor of the Moabites to this day. 38 The younger also bore a son and named him Ben-ammi; he is the ancestor of the Ammonites to this day.
