Samson's Nocturnal Exploit at Gaza

1 Samson repaired to Gaza, where, upon esping a prostitute, he entered her abode. 2 The Gazites were apprised of his arrival and therefore encompassed the city, lying in ambush for him throughout the night at the gate; they kept silent the while, determining, "We shall put him to death at daybreak." 3 Samson, however, lay only until midnight, then arose in the dead of night, gripped the doors of the city gate together with its two posts, wrenched them free, bar and all, laid them upon his shoulders, and carried them to the crest of the hill facing Hebron.

Delilah's Enticement and Samson's Dissembling

4 Thereafter he became enamored of a woman in the Valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah. 5 The Philistine lords approached her and said, "Entice him, and ascertain the source of his prodigious strength and by what means we may subdue him, so that we may bind him in order to torment him; each of us will bestow upon you eleven hundred pieces of silver." 6 Delilah therefore said to Samson, "Please reveal to me whence your great strength derives, and how you may be restrained so as to be tormented."

7 Samson replied, "If they bind me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not been dried, I shall be rendered powerless and become like any other man."

8 The Philistine lords accordingly brought her seven fresh bowstrings that had not been dried, and she bound him with them. 9 Now an ambush was concealed in an inner chamber; she exclaimed, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" Yet he broke the bowstrings as a thread of tow snaps at the scent of flame, so his strength remained a mystery.

10 Delilah then said to him, "Behold, you have deceived me and lied to me. Now, I pray, tell me how you may be bound."

11 He answered, "If they bind me fast with new ropes that have never been employed for any task, I shall be rendered powerless and become like any other man."

12 Delilah therefore took new ropes and bound him with them; then she cried, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" while the ambush waited in the chamber. Yet he snapped them from his arms as if they were thread.

13 Delilah said to Samson, "You have hitherto deceived me and lied to me. Now, I beseech you, tell me how you may be bound."

He said, "If you weave the seven locks of my hair into the fabric on the loom."

14 So she secured them with a pin and said, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" He awoke from his sleep and tore away the pin together with the web.

15 She said to him, "How can you say that you love me, when your heart is not with me? This is the third time you have deceived me and have not told me wherein your great strength lies." 16 And because she harassed him daily with her importunities and pressed him, his very soul was worn down to the point of death.

17 He therefore revealed to her his inmost heart and said, "No razor has ever touched my head, for from my mother's womb I have been consecrated to God as a Nazirite. If I am shaven, my strength will depart from me, and I shall be rendered powerless and become like any other man."

18 When Delilah discerned that he had told her his whole heart, she dispatched a messenger to summon the Philistine lords, saying, "Come up once more, for he has told me all his heart." The Philistine lords therefore came up to her, bringing the silver with them.

Betrayal, Bondage, and Degradation

19 She lulled him to sleep upon her knees, called for a man, and sheared the seven locks of his head; thereby she began to subdue him, and his strength left him.

20 She cried, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!"

He awoke from his sleep and said, "I will go forth as before and shake myself free." But he did not know that the LORD had departed from him.

21 The Philistines seized him, put out his eyes, brought him down to Gaza, fettered him with bronze chains, and compelled him to grind grain in the prison house. 22 Yet the hair of his head began to grow again once it had been shorn.

Samson's Final Vengeance in the Temple of Dagon

23 The Philistine lords assembled to present a great sacrifice to Dagon, their god, and to make merry, saying, "Our god has delivered Samson, our foe, into our power."

24 When the populace beheld him, they extolled their god, saying, "Our god has delivered into our hands our enemy, the devastator of our land, who slew so many of us."

25 When their hearts were gladdened, they said, "Summon Samson, that he may amuse us." They therefore brought Samson out of the prison, and he performed for them; then they set him between the pillars.

26 Samson said to the youth who supported his hand, "Let me feel the pillars on which the house rests, that I may lean upon them."

27 The house was thronged with men and women, and all the Philistine lords were present, while upon the roof stood some three thousand men and women who were watching Samson's performance.

28 Samson called to the LORD and said, "O Lord the LORD, remember me, I pray, and strengthen me, O God, only this once, that I may be avenged upon the Philistines for my two eyes."

29 Then Samson grasped the two middle pillars upon which the house rested, and leaned his weight upon them, one with his right hand and the other with his left.

30 Samson said, "Let me die together with the Philistines." Then he thrust with all his strength, and the house fell upon the lords and upon all the people who were in it. Thus the dead he slew in his dying exceeded those he had slain in his lifetime.

31 Then his brothers and all his father's house came down, carried him up, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father. He had judged Israel for twenty years.
