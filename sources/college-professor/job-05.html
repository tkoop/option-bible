Eliphaz’s Rebuke and the Fate of the Foolish

1 Call out now: is there anyone who will answer you? And to which of the holy ones will you turn? 2 For indignation slays the fool, and jealousy destroys the simple. 3 I have observed the fool taking root, yet suddenly I pronounced a curse upon his dwelling. 4 His sons are driven far from deliverance, crushed at the gate, with no one to rescue them.

5 The hungry devour his harvest, snatching it even from among thorns, while the thirsty pant after his wealth. 6 For misfortune does not spring from the dust, nor does toil burgeon from the soil; 7 rather, man is born to hardship, as sparks inevitably soar upward.

The Inscrutable Works of God

8 But I, for my part, would seek God and entrust my case to God, 9 who accomplishes great and unsearchable things, wonders beyond numbering; 10 who gives rain upon the earth and sends waters across the fields; 11 who exalts the lowly and raises mourners to deliverance; 12 who frustrates the schemes of the crafty, so that their hands cannot bring their designs to completion; 13 who ensnares the wise in their own cunning, while the counsel of the perverse is precipitated into ruin. 14 They meet with darkness in the daytime and grope at noon as though it were night. 15 Yet He delivers the needy from the sword, from their devouring words, and from the hand of the mighty. 16 Thus the poor retain hope, while injustice closes its mouth.

The Discipline, Deliverance, and Restoration Promised to the Afflicted

17 Observe: blessed is the mortal whom God reproves; therefore do not despise the discipline of the Almighty, 18 for He wounds yet binds up, He shatters yet His hands heal. 19 He will deliver you from six calamities; indeed, in seven no harm shall touch you. 20 In famine He will redeem you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. 21 You will be sheltered from the lash of the tongue, and you will not fear devastation when it arrives. 22 You will laugh at devastation and famine, and you will not fear the beasts of the earth.

23 For you will be allied with the stones of the field, and the beasts of the field will be at peace with you. 24 You will know that your tent is secure; you will survey your homestead and lack nothing. 25 You will also know that your progeny will be numerous and your descendants like the grass of the earth. 26 You will come to your grave in ripe old age, as a sheaf is gathered in its season. 27 Observe, we have searched this out; it is true. Hear it and know it for yourself.
