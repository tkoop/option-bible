The Exhumation of the Dead and the Ruin of the Remnant

1 At that time, declares the LORD, they shall exhume the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its princes, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem from their sepulchers.

2 They shall spread them before the sun, the moon, and all the host of heaven, which they loved and served, after which they walked, which they sought, and before which they bowed down. They shall not be gathered or buried; they shall be left as dung upon the face of the earth. 3 And in every place where the survivors remain, wherever I have driven them, death shall be chosen rather than life by all the remnant of this wicked family, declares the LORD of Hosts.

The Obdurate Refusal of Judah to Repent

4 You shall say to them, "Thus says the LORD: Do men fall and not rise again? If one turns away, does he not return?" 5 Why then has this people—Jerusalem itself—turned away in perpetual backsliding? They cling to deceit and refuse to return. 6 I have listened and heard, yet they do not speak aright; no one repents of his wickedness, saying, 'What have I done?' Each one rushes headlong in his own course, like a horse charging into battle.

7 Even the stork in the heavens knows her appointed seasons, and the turtledove, the swift, and the crane observe the time of their coming; but My people do not know the judgment of the LORD. 8 How can you say, "We are wise, and the law of the LORD is with us"? Behold, the lying pen of the scribes has made it a lie. 9 The wise are put to shame; they are dismayed and taken. Behold, they have rejected the word of the LORD; what wisdom, then, is in them?

10 Therefore I will give their wives to others and their fields to those who shall inherit them, because from the least to the greatest everyone is greedy for gain; from prophet to priest everyone practices deceit. 11 They have healed the brokenness of My people superficially, saying, "Peace, peace," when there is no peace. 12 Were they ashamed when they committed abomination? No, they were not at all ashamed; they did not even know how to blush. Therefore they shall fall among the fallen; at the time of their visitation they shall stumble, says the LORD.

The Harvest Removed and the Impending Invasion

13 I will surely gather them, declares the LORD. There are no grapes on the vine, no figs on the fig tree, and even the leaf has withered; and whatever I have given them shall be taken from them. 14 "Why are we sitting here? Assemble, and let us flee to the fortified cities and perish there, for the LORD our God has doomed us. He has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD. 15 We hoped for peace, but no good has come; for a time of healing, but behold, terror." 16 From Dan is heard the snorting of his horses; at the sound of the neighing of his stallions the whole land trembles. They come and devour the land and all that fills it, the city and its inhabitants.

17 "For behold, I am sending among you serpents, adders that cannot be charmed, and they shall bite you," declares the LORD.

Jeremiah's Lament over the Ruin of His People

18 My sorrow is beyond healing; my heart is faint within me. 19 Behold, the cry of the daughter of My people from a distant land: "Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her King not in her?" Why have they provoked Me with their carved images, with their worthless foreign idols? 20 The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved. 21 For the shattering of the daughter of My people I am shattered; I mourn, and horror has seized Me. 22 Is there no balm in Gilead, no physician there? Why then has the healing of My people not come?
