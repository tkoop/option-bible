A Wisdom Psalm on the Futility of Wealth

1 Hear this, all you peoples; give ear, all you who dwell in the world. 2 Both those of humble birth and those of exalted station, the rich and the poor together. 3 My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart shall yield understanding. 4 I will incline my ear to a proverb and expound my riddle to the accompaniment of the lyre.

The Helplessness of Riches Before Death

5 Why should I fear in evil days, when treacherous wickedness surrounds me? 6 Those who trust in their wealth and boast in their great riches— 7 no one can redeem a brother, nor give God a ransom for him— 8 for the redemption of a life is too costly, and no payment is ever enough— 9 that he should live on forever and never see decay.

The Mortality of the Powerful

10 For all can see that the wise die, and that the fool and the senseless perish alike, leaving their wealth to others. 11 Their graves are their homes forever, their dwellings for endless generations, though they have named lands after themselves. 12 Yet a man, however rich, does not endure; he is like the beasts that perish. 13 Such is the way of the self-assured, and those who follow them applaud their words. Selah. 14 Like sheep they are destined for Sheol; death shall be their shepherd. The upright shall rule over them in the morning, and their form shall waste away in Sheol, far from their splendor.

Divine Redemption and Final Admonition

15 But God will redeem my soul from the power of Sheol, for He will receive me. Selah. 16 Do not be afraid when a man grows rich, when the splendor of his house increases; 17 for when he dies he will carry nothing away, and his wealth will not descend with him. 18 For while he lives he congratulates himself, and people praise you whenever you prosper; 19 he will join the generation of his fathers, who will never again see the light. 20 A man who has riches without understanding is like the beasts that perish.
