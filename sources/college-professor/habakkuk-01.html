The Prophet’s Burden and the Erosion of Justice

1 The prophetic oracle that Habakkuk the prophet beheld.

2 How long, O the LORD, must I cry for help, but You do not listen? I cry out to You, “Violence!” but You do not rescue? 3 Why do You cause me to behold iniquity and compel me to gaze upon misery? Ruin and violence confront me; strife and contention arise. 4 Therefore the law is enfeebled, and justice never emerges; for the wicked hem in the righteous, so judgment issues forth distorted.

The LORD’s Staggering Reply and the Ascendancy of the Chaldeans

5 “Look among the nations and observe; be confounded, utterly bewildered. For I am effecting a work in your days that you would not believe even if it were recounted.”

6 For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and impetuous nation, who traverse the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own. 7 They are dreaded and fearsome; their justice and sovereignty proceed from themselves. 8 Their horses are swifter than leopards, fiercer than wolves of the evening; their horsemen charge ahead, and their riders come from afar, flying like an eagle swooping to devour. 9 They all come for violence; their faces press eastward, and they gather captives like sand. 10 They scoff at kings, and rulers are a derision to them; they laugh at every fortress, for they heap up earth and capture it. 11 Then they surge onward like the wind and pass by—guilty men whose might is their god.

The Prophet’s Second Plea Against the Triumph of the Wicked

12 Are You not from everlasting, O the LORD my God, my Holy One? We shall not die. O the LORD, You have appointed them for judgment; O Rock, You have established them for chastisement. 13 Your eyes are too pure to behold evil, and You cannot gaze upon iniquity. Why do You regard the treacherous and remain silent while the wicked devours one more righteous than himself? 14 You make humankind like the fish of the sea, like crawling creatures without a ruler.

15 He draws them all up with a hook; he catches them in his net and gathers them in his dragnet; therefore he rejoices and exults. 16 Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his dragnet, for by them his portion is fat and his food is rich. 17 Will he then empty his net and continue slaughtering nations without pity?
