The LORD's Promise to Abram

1 After these things, the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision, saying, “Do not fear, Abram; I am your shield; your recompense shall be exceedingly great.”

2 Abram said, “O Lord GOD, what will You give me, since I continue childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus? 3 Behold, You have given me no offspring, and behold, one born in my house is my heir.”

4 Then the word of the LORD came to him, saying, “This man shall not be your heir; rather, one who proceeds from your own body shall be your heir.” 5 He brought him outside and said, “Look toward heaven and count the stars, if you are able to count them.” He said to him, “So shall your offspring be.”

6 Abram believed in the LORD, and He reckoned it to him as righteousness.

The Covenant Ceremony and the Promise of the Land

7 He said to him, “I am the LORD, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans, to give you this land as your possession.”

8 He said, “O Lord GOD, how shall I know that I shall possess it?”

9 He said to him, “Take for Me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a turtledove, and a young pigeon.”

10 Abram brought all these to Him, split each down the middle, and laid the halves opposite one another; but he did not cut the birds in half. 11 Then birds of prey descended upon the carcasses, and Abram drove them away.

The Vision of Bondage and the Promise of Return

12 As the sun was setting, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and behold, a great and fearsome darkness overwhelmed him.

13 He said to Abram, “Know with certainty that your descendants shall be sojourners in a land not their own, and that they shall be enslaved and oppressed for four hundred years. 14 Yet I shall judge the nation whom they serve, and afterward they shall depart with great wealth. 15 As for you, you shall join your fathers in peace; you shall be buried at a good old age. 16 In the fourth generation they shall return hither, for the iniquity of the Amorites has not yet reached its full measure.”

The Ratification of the Covenant and the Boundaries of the Land

17 When the sun had set and darkness had fallen, behold, a smoking firepot and a blazing torch passed between those pieces.

18 On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, “To your descendants I have given this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the Euphrates— 19 the land of the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, 20 the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, 21 and the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.”
