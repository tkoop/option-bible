Prophetic Summons to Confront Covenant Hypocrisy

1 “Cry aloud; do not hold back; lift up your voice like a trumpet, and declare to My people their transgression, and to the house of Jacob their sins. 2 Yet they seek Me day by day and delight in drawing near to God, as though they were a nation that had practiced righteousness and had not forsaken the justice of its God. They ask Me for righteous judgments; they delight in the nearness of God.”

The Fast God Rejects and the Fast He Requires

3 “Why have we fasted, and You have not seen? Why have we afflicted our souls, and You have not noticed?”

“Behold, on the day of your fast you find pleasure, and you exploit all your laborers. 4 Behold, you fast for strife and contention, to strike with a wicked fist. You cannot fast as you do today and expect your voice to be heard on high. 5 Is this the fast I have chosen—a day for a person to afflict his soul, to bow his head like a reed, and to spread sackcloth and ashes? Will you call this a fast, a day acceptable to the LORD? 6 Is not this the fast I choose: to loose the bonds of wickedness, to untie the cords of the yoke, to set the oppressed free, and to break every yoke? 7 Is it not to share your bread with the hungry, and to bring the homeless poor into your house; when you see the naked, to cover him, and not to hide yourself from your own kin?”

Promises of Radiant Restoration and Renewal

8 “Then your light will break forth like the dawn, and your healing will spring up speedily; your righteousness will go before you, and the glory of the LORD will be your rear guard. 9 Then you will call, and the LORD will answer; you will cry out, and He will say, ‘Here I am.’ If you remove the yoke from your midst, and abandon the pointing of the finger and malicious speech, 10 and if you pour out your soul for the hungry and satisfy the afflicted soul, then your light will rise in the darkness, and your gloom will be like the noonday. 11 The LORD will guide you continually; He will satisfy your soul in a sun-scorched land and strengthen your frame. You will be like a well-watered garden, like a spring whose waters never fail. 12 Your people will rebuild the ancient ruins; you will restore the foundations of generations. You will be called a repairer of the breach, a restorer of streets for habitation.”

Sabbath Delight and Covenant Blessing

13 “If you turn your foot from the Sabbath, from pursuing your own pleasure on My holy day, and if you call the Sabbath a delight, the holy day of the LORD honorable, and honor it by not going your own way, not seeking your own pleasure, and not speaking idle words, 14 then you will delight in the LORD, and I will make you ride on the heights of the land and feed you with the heritage of your father Jacob; for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.”
