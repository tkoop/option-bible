The Fifth Trumpet and the Opening of the Abyss

1 When the fifth angel sounded his trumpet, I beheld a star that had fallen from heaven to earth, and to it was given the key to the shaft of the abyss. 2 It opened the shaft of the abyss, and smoke ascended from the shaft like the smoke of a great furnace, so that the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke rising from the shaft. 3 Out of the smoke came locusts upon the earth, and to them was granted authority commensurate with that possessed by the scorpions of the earth. 4 They were instructed not to harm the grass of the earth, nor any green plant, nor any tree, but only those human beings who do not bear the seal of God upon their foreheads. 5 They were not permitted to kill them; rather, they were to torment them for five months, and their torment was like the torment inflicted when a scorpion strikes a person. 6 In those days people will seek death and will not find it; they will long to die, and death will flee from them.

The Apocalyptic Locust Plague

7 The locusts resembled horses prepared for battle; upon their heads were something like golden crowns, and their faces were like human faces. 8 They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth. 9 They wore breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of many horses and chariots rushing into battle. 10 They had tails and stings like scorpions, and in their tails lay their power to harm people for five months. 11 They have over them a king, the angel of the abyss, whose name in Hebrew is Abaddon and in Greek is Apollyon. 12 The first woe has passed; two more woes are still to come.

The Sixth Trumpet and the Bound Angels at the Euphrates

13 When the sixth angel sounded his trumpet, I heard a voice from the four horns of the golden altar before God, 14 saying to the sixth angel who held the trumpet, "Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates." 15 So the four angels, who had been prepared for that hour, day, month, and year, were released in order to kill a third of humanity.

16 The number of the mounted troops was two hundred million; I heard their number. 17 And this is how I saw the horses and their riders in the vision: they had breastplates of fire, hyacinth, and sulfur; the horses' heads were like lions' heads; and from their mouths came fire, smoke, and sulfur. 18 A third of humanity was killed by these three plagues—by the fire, the smoke, and the sulfur that issued from their mouths. 19 For the power of the horses resides in their mouths and in their tails, for their tails are like serpents with heads, and with them they inflict injury.

The Unrepentant Remnant

20 The rest of humanity, who were not killed by these plagues, did not repent of the works of their hands—namely, their worship of demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood, which cannot see or hear or walk. 21 Nor did they repent of their murders, their sorceries, their sexual immorality, or their thefts.
