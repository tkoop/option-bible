The Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread: Memorial of Deliverance

1 Observe the month of Abib and celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, for in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night. 2 You shall sacrifice the Passover to the LORD your God—sheep or cattle—at the place that the LORD will choose as a dwelling for His name. 3 You shall eat no leavened bread with it; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, the bread of affliction, because you departed from Egypt in haste, so that you may remember the day of your departure from Egypt throughout all the days of your life. 4 No leaven shall be seen with you in any of your territory for seven days, nor shall any of the flesh that you sacrifice on the evening of the first day remain until morning. 5 You may not sacrifice the Passover within any of your towns that the LORD your God is giving you. 6 Rather, you shall sacrifice the Passover at the place that the LORD your God will choose to establish His name, in the evening at sunset, at the appointed time of your departure from Egypt. 7 You shall cook it and eat it at the place that the LORD your God will choose, and in the morning you may turn and go to your tents. 8 For six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a solemn assembly to the LORD your God; you shall do no work.

The Pilgrim Feasts of Weeks and Booths: Joyful Worship Before the LORD

9 You shall count seven weeks for yourself; begin counting the seven weeks from the moment the sickle is first put to the standing grain. 10 Then you shall celebrate the Feast of Weeks to the LORD your God with a freewill offering from your hand, in proportion to how the LORD your God has blessed you. 11 You shall rejoice before the LORD your God at the place that He will choose to establish His name—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite within your towns, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow who are among you. 12 You shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and you shall be careful to observe these statutes. 13 You shall celebrate the Feast of Booths for seven days when you gather in your threshing floor and your winepress. 14 You shall rejoice in your feast—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow within your towns. 15 For seven days you shall celebrate to the LORD your God at the place that He will choose, because the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, and you shall be wholly joyful. 16 Three times a year all your males shall appear before the LORD your God at the place that He will choose—at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, and at the Feast of Booths—and none shall appear before the LORD empty-handed. 17 Each shall give as he is able, according to the blessing that the LORD your God has given you.

Justice and Pure Worship: Righteous Rule and Rejected Idolatry

18 You shall appoint judges and officers for your tribes in all your towns that the LORD your God is giving you, and they shall judge the people with righteous judgment. 19 You shall not pervert justice; you shall not show partiality; and you shall not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and subverts the words of the righteous. 20 Justice, and only justice, you shall pursue, so that you may live and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you. 21 You shall not plant for yourself any tree as an Asherah beside the altar of the LORD your God that you are making. 22 You shall not set up for yourself a sacred pillar, which the LORD your God hates.
