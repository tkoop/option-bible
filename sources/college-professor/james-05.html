A Prophetic Denunciation of Oppressive Wealth

1 Come now, you who are rich—weep and wail over the miseries that are bearing down upon you. 2 Your wealth has putrefied, and your garments have become moth-eaten. 3 Your gold and silver have corroded, and their rust shall stand as testimony against you, devouring your flesh as though it were fire; you have amassed treasure in the final days. 4 Behold, the wages of the laborers who mowed your fields, which you withheld by fraud, are crying out against you, and the cries of the harvesters have reached the ears of the LORD of Hosts. 5 Upon the earth you have reveled in luxury and wanton indulgence; you have nourished your hearts for a day of slaughter. 6 You have condemned and slain the righteous one, who does not resist you.

Patience, Endurance, and the Nearness of the Lord's Coming

7 Be patient, therefore, brethren, until the advent of the Lord. Behold, the husbandman awaits the precious produce of the soil, enduring patiently for it until it has received the early and the latter rains. 8 You too must be patient; fortify your hearts, for the Lord's coming is near. 9 Do not murmur against one another, brethren, lest you be judged; behold, the Judge stands at the doors. 10 As an exemplar of suffering and endurance, brethren, take the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord. 11 Behold, we deem blessed those who have endured. You have heard of Job's perseverance, and you have beheld the consummation wrought by the Lord, that the Lord is full of compassion and mercy. 12 But above all, my brethren, do not swear, neither by heaven nor by earth nor by any other oath; let your "yes" be yes and your "no" be no, lest you fall under judgment.

The Ministry of Prayer, Confession, and Restoration

13 Is anyone among you afflicted? Let him pray. Is anyone of good cheer? Let him sing psalms. 14 Is anyone among you ill? Let him summon the elders of the congregation, and let them pray over him, having anointed him with oil in the name of the Lord. 15 And the prayer of faith shall deliver the ailing one, and the Lord will raise him up; and if he has committed sins, they shall be forgiven him. 16 Therefore confess your transgressions to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The petition of a righteous person, being effectual in its operation, is mighty. 17 Elijah was a man of like nature with us, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain, and no rain fell upon the land for three years and six months. 18 And again he prayed, and the heavens yielded rain, and the earth brought forth its fruit. 19 My brethren, if any among you should stray from the truth, and one should turn him back, 20 let him know that he who turns a sinner from the error of his way shall save a soul from death and shall cover a multitude of sins.
