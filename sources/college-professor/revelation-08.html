The Seventh Seal and the Golden Censer

1 When the Lamb opened the seventh seal, silence fell upon heaven for about half an hour. 2 I beheld the seven angels standing before God, to whom seven trumpets were entrusted. 3 Another angel, bearing a golden censer, approached and took his place at the altar; abundant incense was given to him, that he might present it with the prayers of all the saints upon the golden altar before the throne. 4 The smoke of the incense, ascending with the prayers of the saints, rose before God from the angel's hand. 5 The angel then took the censer, filled it with fire from the altar, and cast it upon the earth; whereupon there followed thunderclaps, rumblings, flashes of lightning, and an earthquake. 6 The seven angels who held the seven trumpets then made ready to sound them.

The First Four Trumpet Judgments

7 The first angel sounded, and there came hail and fire mingled with blood, and it was cast upon the earth; a third of the earth was consumed by fire, as were a third of the trees and all the green grass. 8 The second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain blazing with fire was hurled into the sea, so that a third of the sea became blood. 9 A third of the living creatures in the sea perished, and a third of the ships were destroyed. 10 The third angel sounded, and a great star, blazing like a torch, fell from heaven upon a third of the rivers and upon the springs of water. 11 The star's name is Wormwood, and a third of the waters became wormwood; many people died from the waters, because they had been made bitter. 12 The fourth angel sounded, and a third of the sun, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars were struck, so that a third of them were darkened; a third of the day was deprived of light, and the night as well.

The Eagle's Threefold Woe

13 Then I looked, and I heard an eagle flying in midheaven, crying out in a loud voice, "Woe, woe, woe to the inhabitants of the earth, because of the remaining trumpet blasts that the three angels are about to sound!"
