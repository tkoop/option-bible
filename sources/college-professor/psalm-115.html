A Doxology That Redirects Glory from Mortals to God

1 Not unto us, O the LORD, not unto us, but unto Your name give glory, for the sake of Your steadfast love, for the sake of Your faithfulness. 2 Why should the nations say, “Where, pray, is their God?”

3 Our God is in the heavens; He does whatever He pleases.

The Futility of Idolatry

4 Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands. 5 They have mouths, but do not speak; eyes, but do not see. 6 They have ears, but do not hear; noses, but do not smell. 7 They have hands, but do not feel; feet, but do not walk; nor do they utter a sound through their throats. 8 Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.

Israel’s Trust, Blessing, and Eternal Praise

9 O Israel, trust in the the LORD; He is their help and their shield. 10 O house of Aaron, trust in the the LORD; He is their help and their shield. 11 You who fear the the LORD, trust in the the LORD; He is their help and their shield.

12 The LORD has remembered us; He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron. 13 He will bless those who fear the the LORD, both the small and the great.

14 May the the LORD increase you— you and your children. 15 You are blessed by the the LORD, Maker of heaven and earth. 16 The heavens are the heavens of the the LORD, but the earth He has given to the children of men.

17 The dead do not praise the the LORD, nor any who descend into silence. 18 But we will bless the the LORD from this time forth and forevermore. Praise the the LORD!
