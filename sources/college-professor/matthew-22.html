The Parable of the Wedding Feast

1 Once more Jesus replied to them in parables, saying, “The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who prepared a wedding banquet for his son. 2 He sent his servants to summon the invited guests to the banquet, but they refused to come. 3 Again he dispatched other servants with this message: ‘Tell those who have been invited, “Behold, my banquet is prepared; my oxen and fattened cattle have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding.”’

“5 But those invitees, paying the summons no heed, went away—one to his farm, another to his business. 6 The rest seized his servants, abused them, and killed them. 7 The king was enraged; he sent his troops, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.

“8 Then he told his servants, ‘The feast is prepared, but those invited proved unworthy.’ 9 Go therefore to the intersections of the highways and invite to the banquet whomever you encounter. 10 Those servants went out into the streets and gathered everyone they could find, wicked and righteous alike, so that the banquet hall was filled with guests.

“11 When the king entered to inspect the guests, he noticed a man not attired in wedding garments. 12 He said to him, ‘Friend, how did you gain entrance here without wedding attire?’ And the man was struck mute. 13 Then the king ordered his attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness, where there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ 14 For many are summoned, yet few are chosen.”

The Question of the Tribute

15 Then the Pharisees went away and took counsel how they might entrap Him in speech. 16 They sent their disciples to Him, together with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that You are truthful and teach the way of God in truth, and that You are not swayed by anyone, for You do not regard the person of men. 17 Tell us, therefore, what You think: Is it lawful to pay the tribute to Caesar, or not?”

18 But Jesus, knowing their wickedness, said, “Why are you testing Me, hypocrites? 19 Show Me the coin of the tax.” And they brought Him a denarius.

20 He asked them, “Whose image is this, and whose inscription?”

21 They answered, “Caesar’s.”

Then He said to them, “Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.”

22 Hearing this, they marveled; and leaving Him, they departed.

The Resurrection and the Great Commandment

23 On that same day Sadducees—who maintain that there is no resurrection—came to Him and questioned Him, 24 saying, “Teacher, Moses said, ‘If a man dies childless, his brother shall marry his widow and raise up offspring for his brother.’ 25 Now among us there were seven brothers. The first married and died, leaving no offspring, and gave his wife to his brother. 26 The same happened to the second, and to the third, down to the seventh. 27 Last of all, the woman died. 28 In the resurrection, therefore, whose wife will she be? For all seven had her.”

29 Jesus answered them, “You are mistaken, because you know neither the Scriptures nor the power of God. 30 For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven. 31 But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, saying, 32 ‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? He is not the God of the dead, but of the living.”

33 When the crowds heard this, they were astonished at His teaching.

34 But when the Pharisees heard that He had silenced the Sadducees, they gathered together. 35 And one of them, a lawyer, tested Him by asking,

36 “Teacher, which commandment is greatest in the Law?”

37 He said to him, “You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind. 38 This is the first and greatest commandment. 39 And the second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself. 40 On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.”

David’s Son and David’s Lord

41 While the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them, 42 “What do you think about the Christ? Whose Son is He?”

They answered Him, “David’s.”

43 He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call Him Lord, saying,

“44 ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand, until I put Your enemies under Your feet.”’

“45 If then David calls Him Lord, how is He his son?”

46 And no one was able to answer Him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day on to question Him any further.
