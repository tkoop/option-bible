The Allotment of Canaan Among the Tribes

1 These are the territories that the Israelites inherited in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelite tribes apportioned to them. 2 Their inheritance was determined by lot, exactly as the LORD had commanded through Moses, for the nine and one-half tribes.

3 For Moses had already granted an inheritance to the two and one-half tribes beyond the Jordan, yet he had granted no inheritance among them to the Levites. 4 The sons of Joseph had become two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, and the Levites received no share in the land except cities in which to dwell, together with their pasturelands for livestock and property.

5 Thus the Israelites acted precisely as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they partitioned the land.

Caleb's Petition for the Mountainous Region of Hebron

6 Then the sons of Judah approached Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, “You know the word that the LORD spoke to Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea, concerning you and me.

7 “I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to reconnoiter the land, and I brought back a report precisely as I had perceived it in my heart.

8 “Yet my brothers who went up with me demoralized the people, whereas I unreservedly followed the LORD my God.

9 “Therefore on that day Moses swore, saying, ‘Surely the land upon which your foot has trodden shall be an inheritance for you and your children forever, because you have unreservedly followed the LORD my God.’

10 “And now, mark well that the LORD has preserved me alive, just as He promised, these forty-five years since the LORD spoke this word to Moses, while Israel wandered in the wilderness; and now, mark well that I am eighty-five years old today.

11 “I am today as vigorous as on the day Moses sent me; my strength then is my strength now, for warfare and for every undertaking of life.

12 “Now, therefore, grant me this mountainous region that the LORD promised on that day; for you heard on that day that the Anakim were there, with great fortified cities. Perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I shall dispossess them, just as the LORD said.”

Hebron Conferred Upon Caleb

13 So Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh as an inheritance. 14 Therefore Hebron has remained the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he unreservedly followed the LORD, the God of Israel.

15 Now the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath-arba; Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. And the land had rest from war.
