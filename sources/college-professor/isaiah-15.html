An Oracle against Moab

1 A prophetic oracle concerning Moab: in a single night Ar of Moab is devastated and brought to silence, and in a single night Kir of Moab is devastated and brought to silence. 2 Dibon ascends to its sanctuary, to the high places, to weep; Moab wails over Nebo and over Medeba; upon all its heads there is baldness, and every beard is shorn.

The Mourning of Moab's Cities

3 In its streets they gird themselves with sackcloth; upon its rooftops and in its broad plazas everyone wails, descending in tears. 4 Heshbon and Elealeh cry aloud; their voice is heard as far as Jahaz; therefore the armed men of Moab shout, and his soul quails within him.

The Prophet's Grief and Moab's Flight

5 My heart cries out for Moab; her fugitives flee to Zoar and to Eglath-shelishiyah. For along the ascent of Luhith they go up weeping, and along the road to Horonaim they raise a cry of ruin. 6 For the waters of Nimrim shall be turned to desolation; for the grass has withered, the verdure has perished, and no green thing remains. 7 Therefore they carry away the abundance they have gained, together with their hoarded stores, over the Brook of the Willows.

The Final Devastation at Dimon

8 For the cry has encircled the border of Moab; its wailing reaches Eglaim, and its wailing reaches Beer-elim. 9 For the waters of Dimon are full of blood; for I will bring upon Dimon even more—a lion upon the remnant of Moab and upon the survivors of the land.
