The Tower of Babel and the Dispersion of Nations

1 Initially, the entire earth possessed a single language and a unified lexicon. 2 As humanity migrated eastward, its members discovered a plain in the land of Shinar and established residence there.

3 They exhorted one another: "Come now, let us fabricate bricks and fire them thoroughly." Thus brick served them as stone, and bitumen as mortar.

4 Then they declared, "Come now, let us erect for ourselves a city and a tower whose summit reaches the heavens, and let us secure an enduring renown, lest we be dispersed across the face of the entire earth."

5 But the LORD descended to inspect the city and the tower that humankind had constructed.

6 The LORD said, "Observe, they constitute one people with one language, and this enterprise is merely the prelude to all they intend; henceforth nothing they scheme to accomplish will be unattainable for them. 7 Come, let us descend and there confound their speech, so that no one may comprehend his neighbor's tongue."

8 So the LORD scattered them from that place across the face of the whole earth, and they abandoned the construction of the city.

9 Therefore it was named Babel, because there the LORD confounded the speech of the whole earth, and from there the LORD dispersed them across the face of the whole earth.

The Lineage from Shem to Terah

10 These are the generations of Shem. When Shem had lived one hundred years, he fathered Arpachshad two years after the Flood. 11 After fathering Arpachshad, Shem lived five hundred years and fathered sons and daughters.

12 Arpachshad lived thirty-five years and fathered Shelah. 13 After fathering Shelah, Arpachshad lived four hundred and three years and fathered sons and daughters.

14 Shelah lived thirty years and fathered Eber. 15 After fathering Eber, Shelah lived four hundred and three years and fathered sons and daughters.

16 Eber lived thirty-four years and fathered Peleg. 17 After fathering Peleg, Eber lived four hundred and thirty years and fathered sons and daughters.

18 Peleg lived thirty years and fathered Reu. 19 After fathering Reu, Peleg lived two hundred and nine years and fathered sons and daughters.

20 Reu lived thirty-two years and fathered Serug. 21 After fathering Serug, Reu lived two hundred and seven years and fathered sons and daughters.

22 Serug lived thirty years and fathered Nahor. 23 After fathering Nahor, Serug lived two hundred years and fathered sons and daughters.

24 Nahor lived twenty-nine years and fathered Terah. 25 After fathering Terah, Nahor lived one hundred and nineteen years and fathered sons and daughters.

26 Terah lived seventy years and fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

Terah's Descendants and the Migration toward Canaan

27 These are the generations of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran fathered Lot. 28 Haran died before his father Terah in his native land, in Ur of the Chaldeans. 29 Abram and Nahor took wives: Abram's wife was Sarai, and Nahor's wife was Milcah, daughter of Haran, who was the father of Milcah and Iscah. 30 Sarai was barren and bore no child.

31 Terah took Abram his son, Lot the son of Haran, his grandson, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son, and they set out together from Ur of the Chaldeans to travel to the land of Canaan; but when they reached Haran, they settled there. 32 The days of Terah totaled two hundred and five years, and Terah died in Haran.
