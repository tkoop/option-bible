The Fading Crown of Ephraim's Drunkards

1 Alas for the proud diadem of Ephraim—the drunkards whose splendor is withering—and for the fading blossom of its glorious beauty, situated upon the head of the fertile valley, those smitten by wine. 2 Behold, the Lord has one who is mighty and strong: like a hailstorm of destruction, like a tempest, like a torrent of mighty waters overflowing, He will cast it down to the earth with His hand. 3 The proud diadem of Ephraim, even its drunkards, shall be trampled underfoot. 4 And the fading blossom of its glorious beauty, which crowns the head of the fertile valley, shall be like the first-ripe fig before summer: no sooner does one behold it than, while it is still in his hand, he devours it.

5 In that day the LORD of Hosts will be a crown of glory and a diadem of beauty to the remnant of His people. 6 and a spirit of justice to him who sits in judgment, and strength to those who repel the assault at the gate.

Drunken Priests, Prophets, and the LORD's Instruction

7 These also have gone astray through wine and have staggered from strong drink; priest and prophet alike have gone astray through strong drink, have been engulfed by wine, have staggered from strong drink; they have gone astray in vision, they have stumbled in judgment. 8 For every table is covered with vomit, with filth, so that there is no clean place.

9 To whom, then, will He impart knowledge, and to whom will He render the message intelligible? To those weaned from milk, to those drawn from the breast. 10 For it is precept upon precept, precept upon precept, line upon line, line upon line, a little here, a little there. 11 For by stammering lips and a foreign tongue He will speak to this people. 12 To whom He said, “This is the rest with which you may give rest to the weary, and this is the repose”—yet they would not listen. 13 Thus the word of the LORD became to them precept upon precept, precept upon precept, line upon line, line upon line, a little here, a little there, so that they might go and stumble backward, be broken, be snared, and be taken.

The Covenant with Death and the Precious Cornerstone

14 Therefore hear the word of the LORD, you scoffers who rule this people in Jerusalem. 15 For you have said, “We have made a covenant with death, and with Sheol we have made an agreement; when the overflowing scourge passes through, it will not reach us, for we have made lies our refuge, and in falsehood we have hidden ourselves.”

16 Therefore thus says the Lord, the LORD: “Behold, I am laying in Zion a stone, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation; he who believes will not be in haste.” 17 I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the plumb line; hail will sweep away the refuge of lies, and waters will overflow the hiding place. 18 Your covenant with death will be annulled, and your pact with Sheol will not stand; when the overflowing scourge passes through, you will be trampled underfoot. 19 As often as it passes through, it will seize you; for morning by morning, by day and by night, it will pass through, and it will be sheer terror to comprehend the message.

20 For the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the covering too narrow to wrap oneself in. 21 For the LORD will rise up as at Mount Perazim; He will be enraged as in the Valley of Gibeon—to do His work, His strange work, and to perform His task, His alien task. 22 Now therefore do not mock, lest your bonds be tightened; for I have heard from the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, a decree of utter destruction upon the whole land.

The Farmer's Wisdom and the LORD's Counsel

23 Give ear and hear my voice; listen closely and hear my speech. 24 Does the plowman plow all day to sow? Does he continually break up and harrow his ground? 25 When he has leveled its surface, does he not scatter dill and sow cumin, and put wheat in rows, barley in its designated place, and spelt along its border? 26 For his God instructs him and teaches him the principles of justice. 27 For dill is not threshed with a threshing sledge, nor is a cartwheel rolled over cumin; rather, dill is beaten out with a stick, and cumin with a rod. 28 Bread grain is crushed, but not forever; though the wheel of his cart and his horses rumble over it, they do not pulverize it. 29 This too comes from the LORD of Hosts, who is wonderful in counsel and great in wisdom.
