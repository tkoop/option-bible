Exhortations to Civic Deference and Universal Benevolence

1 Admonish them to be subject to magistrates and authorities, to be obedient, and to remain prepared for every beneficent undertaking; 2 to calumniate no one, to be pacific and equitable, exhibiting complete forbearance toward all humanity.

3 For we ourselves were formerly insensate, recalcitrant, and led astray, enslaved to multifarious cravings and pleasures, conducting our lives in malice and envy, detestable and mutually abhorrent.

The Manifestation of Divine Kindness and Regenerative Renewal

4 Yet when the benignity of God our Savior and His philanthropy toward humankind was disclosed, 5 He delivered us—not by deeds of righteousness that we had performed, but in accordance with His own mercy—through the laver of regeneration and the renovation wrought by the Holy Spirit, 6 whom He lavished upon us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Savior, 7 in order that, having been justified by His grace, we might become heirs in hope of life everlasting.

Doctrinal Fidelity, Profitable Labor, and the Exclusion of Divisive Speculation

8 This saying is trustworthy, and I desire that you affirm these matters with conviction, so that those who have placed their trust in God may be solicitous to take the lead in noble works; for such pursuits are honorable and advantageous to humankind. 9 But shun foolish disputations, genealogies, contentions, and legalistic quarrels, for they are otiose and vain. 10 After a first and second admonition, have nothing to do with a divisive person, 11 knowing that such an individual is perverted and sinful, being self-condemned.

Itinerant Arrangements, Communal Provision, and Apostolic Benediction

12 When I dispatch Artemas or Tychicus to you, endeavor to come to me at Nicopolis, for I have resolved to winter there. 13 Expedite the departure of Zenas the jurist and Apollos, so that they may lack nothing. 14 Moreover, let our own people learn to take the lead in noble works for pressing necessities, lest they be fruitless. 15 All who are with me send greetings to you. Greet those who cherish us in faith. Grace be with all of you.
