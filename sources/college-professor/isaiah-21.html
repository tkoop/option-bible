Oracle Concerning the Wilderness of the Sea

1 The oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea: as whirlwinds sweep through the Negev, so it advances from the wilderness, from a land of terror. 2 A stern vision has been declared to me: the traitor continues to betray, and the devastator continues to devastate. Ascend, O Elam; besiege, O Media; I have brought to an end all her sighing.

3 Therefore my loins are filled with anguish; pangs have seized me, like the pangs of a woman in travail. I am so bowed down that I cannot hear; I am so dismayed that I cannot see. 4 My heart reels; horror has appalled me; the twilight I longed for has been transformed for me into trembling.

5 They lay the table, they spread the carpets, they eat, they drink. Arise, you princes, anoint the shield!

The Watchman's Vision of Babylon's Fall

6 For thus the Lord said to me: "Go, station a watchman; let him announce what he sees. 7 When he sees riders, horsemen in pairs, riders on donkeys, riders on camels, let him listen with the utmost vigilance."

8 Then the watchman cried out, "Upon a watchtower I stand, O Lord, continually by day, and at my post I am stationed throughout the nights. 9 And behold, here comes a chariot of men, horsemen in pairs!" And he answered, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon, and all the carved images of her gods He has shattered to the ground."

10 O my threshed and winnowed one, what I have heard from the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, I announce to you.

Oracle Concerning Dumah

11 The oracle concerning Dumah: someone calls to me from Seir, "Watchman, what hour of the night? Watchman, what hour of the night?"

12 The watchman replies, "Morning comes, and yet night follows. If you will inquire, then inquire; return once more."

Oracle Concerning Arabia

13 The oracle concerning Arabia: in the thickets of Arabia you will lodge, O caravans of the Dedanites.

14 To the thirsty bring water; meet the fugitive with bread, O inhabitants of the land of Tema. 15 For they have fled from the swords, from the drawn sword, from the bent bow, and from the crush of battle.

16 For thus the Lord said to me, "Within a year, according to the years of a hired worker, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end. 17 And the remainder of the number of the archers, the mighty men of the sons of Kedar, will be few, for the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken."
