Paul’s Defense Before the Jerusalem Crowd

1 “Men, brothers and fathers, hear, I pray you, the defense I now offer before you.” 2 And when they heard that he was addressing them in the Hebrew dialect, they were seized by an even more profound silence; and he said: 3 “I am a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, yet nurtured in this city, educated at the feet of Gamaliel in punctilious adherence to the law of our fathers, and being a zealot for God, as are all of you this day. 4 I persecuted this Way even unto death, binding men and women alike and delivering them into custody, 5 as the high priest and the whole council of elders can attest; for, having received letters from them to the brothers, I was journeying to Damascus, that I might bring in bonds those who were there and convey them to Jerusalem for punishment.”

The Damascus Road Revelation

6 “As I was journeying and drawing near to Damascus, about midday, a great light from heaven suddenly blazed about me. 7 I fell to the earth and heard a voice saying to me, ‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me?’ 8 I answered, ‘Who are You, Lord?’ And He said to me, ‘I am Jesus of Nazareth, whom you are persecuting.’ 9 Those who were with me indeed saw the light, yet they did not comprehend the voice of Him who spoke to me. 10 I said, ‘What shall I do, Lord?’ And the Lord said to me, ‘Arise and go into Damascus, and there it shall be told you all things that are appointed for you to do.’ 11 And because I could not see for the glory of that light, I was led by the hand by my companions and came into Damascus.”

Ananias and the Temple Vision

12 “And a certain Ananias, a devout man according to the law, well attested by all the Jews dwelling there, 13 came to me; and standing by, he said to me, ‘Brother Saul, receive your sight.’ And in that same hour I received my sight and beheld him. 14 And he said, ‘The God of our fathers has appointed you to know His will, and to see the Righteous One, and to hear a voice from His mouth. 15 For you shall be His witness unto all men of the things you have seen and heard. 16 And now why do you delay? Arise and be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling upon His name.

17 “And when I had returned to Jerusalem and was praying in the temple, I fell into a trance, 18 and saw Him saying to me, ‘Hasten and depart from Jerusalem quickly, because they will not receive your testimony concerning Me.’ 19 I said, ‘Lord, they themselves know that in one synagogue after another I imprisoned and beat those who believed in You.’ 20 And when the blood of Stephen Your witness was being poured out, I also was standing by and consenting, and guarding the garments of those who slew him. 21 And He said to me, ‘Go, for I will send you far away unto the Gentiles.’”

The Crowd’s Outrage and Paul’s Roman Citizenship

22 They listened to him up to this word; then they lifted up their voices and said, “Away with such a fellow from the earth! For it is not fitting that he should live.” 23 And while they were crying out, and casting off their garments, and throwing dust into the air, 24 the tribune commanded that he be conducted into the barracks, directing that he be examined by scourging, so that he might ascertain the cause for which they were shouting against him in this manner.

25 But as they stretched him out with the thongs, Paul said to the centurion standing by, “Is it lawful for you to scourge a man who is a Roman and uncondemned?”

26 And when the centurion heard it, he went to the tribune and reported, saying, “What are you about to do? For this man is a Roman.”

27 So the tribune came and said to him, “Tell me, are you a Roman?”

And he said, “Yes.”

28 The tribune answered, “I acquired this citizenship for a great sum.”

But Paul said, “I, however, have been born a citizen.”

29 Immediately those who were about to examine him withdrew from him; and the tribune also was afraid, having learned that he was a Roman and that he had bound him. 30 But on the next day, wishing to know with certainty the charge on which he was accused by the Jews, he loosed him, and commanded the chief priests and all the council to assemble; and he brought Paul down and set him before them.
