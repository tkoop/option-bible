A Pilgrimage Supplication for Divine Mercy amid Scorn

1 A song of ascents: I elevate my gaze unto You, enthroned in the heavens. 2 Behold, as the eyes of servants are fixed upon the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maidservant upon the hand of her mistress, so our eyes are fixed upon the LORD our God, until He grants us grace.

3 Be gracious to us, O the LORD, be gracious to us, for we are sated to overflowing with contempt. 4 Our soul is sated to overflowing with the derision of the complacent, with the scorn of the arrogant.
