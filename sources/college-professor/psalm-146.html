A Hymn Extolling the LORD’s Everlasting Dominion

1 Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD, O my soul! 2 I shall extol the LORD throughout my days; I shall chant psalms to my God while breath yet remains within me.

3 Do not repose your confidence in nobles, nor in mortal offspring, in whom no deliverance resides. 4 When his spirit departs, he returns to the dust; on that very day his designs perish.

5 Blessed is the one whose succor is the God of Jacob, whose hope is fixed upon the LORD his God— 6 the Artificer of heaven and earth, the sea, and all that dwells therein, who preserves fidelity in perpetuity; 7 who administers justice for the oppressed and dispenses sustenance to the famished. The LORD liberates captives;

8 the LORD grants sight to the blind; the LORD raises up those bowed beneath their burdens; the LORD cherishes the righteous. 9 The LORD safeguards sojourners; He sustains the orphan and the widow, yet He subverts the way of the wicked. 10 The LORD shall reign in perpetuity—your God, O Zion, throughout all generations. Praise the LORD!
