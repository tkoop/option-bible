Filial Obedience and Paternal Instruction

1 Children, render obedience to your parents in the Lord, for this is the essence of righteousness. 2 “Honor your father and mother”—the first commandment that carries a promise— 3 that it may fare well with you and that you may be long-lived upon the earth. 4 And fathers, do not provoke your children to wrath; rather, nurture them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

Servitude and Mastery under Christ

5 Slaves, obey your earthly masters with reverential fear and trembling, in the sincerity of your heart, as unto Christ; 6 not as men-pleasers rendering service solely under scrutiny, but as slaves of Christ, executing the will of God from the soul; 7 serving with benevolence, as to the Lord and not to mortals, 8 knowing that whatever good each one performs, he will receive recompense from the Lord, whether slave or free. 9 And masters, act reciprocally toward them, relinquishing threats, cognizant that their Master and yours is in heaven, and that there is no partiality with Him.

The Panoply of God and Spiritual Warfare

10 Finally, be empowered in the Lord and in the might of His strength. 11 Clothe yourselves in the full armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the stratagems of the devil. 12 For our contest is not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against authorities, against the cosmic rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly realms.

13 Therefore take up the full armor of God, that in the evil day you may be able to withstand, and having accomplished all, to stand. 14 Stand firm, therefore, having girded your loins with truth and having put on the breastplate of righteousness; 15 and having shod your feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace; 16 in all circumstances taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the flaming darts of the evil one; 17 and take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God; 18 praying in the Spirit at all times with every prayer and supplication, and to this end keeping alert with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints.

Apostolic Commission and Final Benediction

19 And for me, that utterance may be granted me in the opening of my mouth, to proclaim with boldness the mystery of the gospel, 20 for which I am an ambassador in chains, that I may speak boldly therein, as it behooves me to speak.

21 Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, will acquaint you with all things, so that you too may know my circumstances and what I am doing. 22 I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know our affairs and that he may comfort your hearts. 23 Peace be to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 24 Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with incorruptible love.
