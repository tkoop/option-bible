The LORD’s Fierce Indignation against Zion

1 How the Lord has enshrouded Daughter Zion in the cloud of His indignation! He has hurled from heaven to earth the splendor of Israel, and He has not remembered His footstool in the day of His wrath. 2 Without mercy the Lord has engulfed all the habitations of Jacob; in His fury He has razed the fortresses of Daughter Judah; He has brought her sovereignty and her princes down to the dust, humiliating them. 3 In the heat of His anger He has cut off every horn of Israel; He has withdrawn His right hand before the enemy; He has kindled in Jacob a conflagration that devours on every side.

4 He has bent His bow like an enemy; His right hand stands poised like an adversary; He has slain all who were pleasing to the eye; in the tent of Daughter Zion He has poured out His wrath like fire. 5 The Lord has become like an enemy; He has swallowed up Israel; He has swallowed up all her palaces; He has destroyed her strongholds; and He has multiplied mourning and lamentation in Daughter Judah.

The Desolation of Sanctuary and City

6 He has ravaged His tabernacle as though it were a garden; He has ruined His appointed meeting place; the LORD has caused Zion to forget her sacred seasons and her Sabbaths; in the fury of His anger He has rejected both king and priest. 7 The Lord has spurned His altar; He has profaned His sanctuary; He has delivered the walls of her citadels into the hand of the foe; they have shouted in the house of the LORD as though it were a day of festival. 8 The LORD resolved to lay waste the wall of Daughter Zion; He stretched out the measuring line; He did not withdraw His hand from devouring; rampart and wall alike lamented; together they wasted away.

9 Her gates have sunk into the earth; He has destroyed and shattered her bars; her king and her princes are exiled among the nations; instruction is no more; even her prophets obtain no vision from the LORD. 10 The elders of Daughter Zion sit on the ground in silence; they have cast dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth; the virgins of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

The Prophet’s Anguish and the People’s Cry

11 My eyes are consumed with weeping; my inmost being churns within me; my heart is poured out upon the earth over the shattering of my people, as little ones and infants languish in the city’s streets.

12 They cry to their mothers, “Where is grain and wine?” as they swoon like the mortally wounded in the city’s streets, their lives ebbing away in their mothers’ arms.

13 What testimony can I offer on your behalf? To what shall I liken you, O Daughter Jerusalem? To what shall I compare you, that I might console you, O Virgin Daughter Zion? For your breach is as wide as the sea; who can heal you? 14 Your prophets have envisioned for you vanity and whitewash; they have not laid bare your guilt so as to restore your fortunes; instead, they have proclaimed for you oracles that are false and deluding.

15 All who pass by clap their hands at you; they hiss and shake their heads at Daughter Jerusalem: “Is this the city that was called the perfection of beauty, the joy of the whole earth?”

16 All your enemies open their mouths against you; they hiss and gnash their teeth; they say, “We have swallowed her up! Surely this is the day we have longed for; we have found it; we have seen it.”

17 The LORD has done what He purposed; He has fulfilled His word, which He decreed in days of old; He has overthrown without pity; He has made the enemy rejoice over you; He has exalted the horn of your adversaries.

A Plea for Mercy in the Day of Wrath

18 Their hearts cried out to the Lord. O wall of Daughter Zion, let your tears stream down like a river day and night; grant yourself no relief; do not let your eyes cease. 19 Arise, cry out in the night, at the beginning of the watches; pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord; lift up your hands to Him for the lives of your little ones, who faint from hunger at the head of every street.

20 Behold the LORD, and consider: to whom have You ever done this? Should women devour their offspring, the children of their tender care? Should priest and prophet be slain in the sanctuary of the Lord? 21 Young and old lie together in the dust of the streets; my young men and my virgins have fallen by the sword; You have slain them in the day of Your wrath; You have slaughtered without mercy. 22 You have summoned terrors on every side, as on a day of festival; in the day of the LORD’s anger no one escaped or survived; those whom I dandled and reared, my enemy has consumed.
