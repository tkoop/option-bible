Christ Our Advocate and Propitiation

1 My dear little ones, I am inscribing these admonitions for you, lest you fall into sin; yet should any among you transgress, we possess an Advocate before the Father—Jesus Christ, the Righteous One. 2 He Himself constitutes the propitiation for our transgressions—not solely for ours, but likewise for the sins of the entire world.

Obedience and the Perfection of Love

3 By this we ascertain that we have attained intimate knowledge of Him: whenever we observe His commandments. 4 Whoever professes, “I have known Him,” yet fails to observe His commandments, is a mendacious figure in whom the truth does not dwell. 5 Conversely, whoever keeps His word has the love of God truly perfected within him; by this we recognize that we abide in Him. 6 He who alleges that he abides in Him is obligated to order his life precisely as that One Himself did.

The Old and New Commandment of Love

7 Beloved, I am not promulgating a novel injunction for you, but rather an antiquated commandment which you have possessed from the inception; this venerable mandate is the very word you have heard. 8 Yet again I inscribe for you a new commandment—something veracious in Him and in you—because the darkness is passing away and the genuine light already shines.

9 Whoever professes to dwell in the light while harboring hatred toward his brother remains, even now, in darkness. 10 But he who cherishes his brother abides in the light, and no occasion for stumbling exists within him. 11 Conversely, whoever hates his brother inhabits darkness and conducts himself therein; he is unaware of his destination, for darkness has blinded his eyes.

Generations, the World, and the Antichrists

12 I inscribe this to you, little children, because your sins have been remitted for the sake of His name. 13 I write to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who exists from the beginning; I write to you, young men, because you have prevailed over the evil one; I have written to you, little children, because you have come to know the Father. 14 I have written to you, fathers, because you have known Him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, the word of God resides within you, and you have conquered the evil one.

15 Do not set your affection upon the world, nor upon the things that are in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father does not dwell within him. 16 For everything that belongs to the world—the craving of the flesh, the craving of the eyes, and the vainglory of life—does not originate from the Father, but is derived from the world. 17 Moreover, the world is passing away, and with it its desires; yet the one who performs the will of God abides forever.

18 Little ones, it is the final hour; and as you have heard that an antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have arisen; hence we recognize that it is indeed the final hour. 19 They departed from our fellowship, yet they were never truly of us; for had they been of us, they would have persisted with us. Rather, their exodus serves to manifest that not all of them belonged to us. 20 Yet you possess an anointing from the Holy One, and you all possess knowledge. 21 I did not write to you on account of your ignorance of the truth, but because you are acquainted with it, and because no falsehood derives from the truth.

22 Who, then, is the liar, if not the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? Such a person is the antichrist—one who denies both the Father and the Son. 23 Everyone who denies the Son is devoid of the Father; conversely, whoever confesses the Son possesses the Father also. 24 As for you, let that which you heard from the beginning abide in you; if it does, you too will abide in the Son and in the Father. 25 And this is the promise which He Himself pledged to us—life everlasting.

26 I have committed these words to writing for you concerning those who seek to lead you astray. 27 As for you, the anointing which you received from Him abides within you, and you have no need for anyone to instruct you; rather, as His anointing instructs you concerning all things—and it is authentic, not fallacious—so, having been taught by it, remain in Him. 28 And now, little children, abide in Him, so that whenever He is manifested we may possess boldness and not be put to shame before Him at His advent. 29 If you know that He is righteous, you also recognize that everyone who practices righteousness has been begotten of Him.
