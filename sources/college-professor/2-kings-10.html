Jehu’s Epistolary Ultimatum to Samaria’s Leadership

1 Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Jehu composed letters and sent them to Samaria, addressing the officials of Jezreel, the elders, and the guardians of Ahab’s sons, with this message:

2 “When this letter reaches you, since your master’s sons are with you, and you possess chariots, horses, a fortified city, and weapons, 3 choose the best and most upright among your master’s sons, set him upon his father’s throne, and fight on behalf of your master’s house.”

4 They were terrified—indeed, exceedingly so—and said, “Behold, two kings could not withstand him; how then shall we stand?”

5 The steward of the household, the city overseer, the elders, and the guardians sent word to Jehu: “We are your servants; whatever you command we will do, and we will install no man as king. Do what seems good in your eyes.”

6 He then wrote a second letter to them: “If you are mine and will obey my voice, take the heads of your master’s sons and come to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow.” Now the king’s sons, seventy in number, were with the great men of the city, who were raising them. 7 When the letter reached them, they seized the king’s sons, slaughtered seventy men, placed their heads in baskets, and sent them to him at Jezreel.

8 A messenger came and reported, “They have brought the heads of the king’s sons.”

He replied, “Lay them in two heaps at the gate’s entrance until morning.”

The Massacre at Jezreel and the Vindication of Prophecy

9 In the morning he went out, stood before all the people, and said, “You are righteous. Behold, I conspired against my master and killed him; but who struck down all these? 10 Know, then, that not one word of the LORD that the LORD spoke concerning the house of Ahab shall fall to the ground, for the LORD has accomplished what He spoke through His servant Elijah.”

11 Jehu struck down everyone remaining of Ahab’s house in Jezreel, together with all his great men, his intimates, and his priests, until he left him no survivor.

12 Then he rose, departed, and went toward Samaria; along the way, at Beth-eked of the Shepherds,

13 Jehu encountered the brothers of Ahaziah, king of Judah, and asked, “Who are you?”

They replied, “We are brothers of Ahaziah; we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother.”

14 He commanded, “Take them alive.” They seized them alive and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked—forty-two men—and left not one of them.

15 He left that place and found Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. He greeted him and asked, “Is your heart upright, as my heart is toward your heart?”

Jehonadab answered, “It is.”

“If it is,” Jehu said, “give me your hand.” He gave his hand, and Jehu took him up into the chariot.

16 He said, “Come with me and witness my zeal for the LORD.” So they made him ride in his chariot.

17 When he reached Samaria, he killed all who remained to Ahab in Samaria until he had annihilated them, in accordance with the word of the LORD spoken to Elijah.

The Deceptive Convocation of Baal’s Prophets

18 Jehu assembled all the people and said to them, “Ahab served Baal but little; Jehu will serve him much.”

19 “Now summon to me all the prophets of Baal, all his servants, and all his priests; let none be absent, for I intend a great sacrifice to Baal. Whoever is absent shall not live.” Jehu, however, acted deceitfully, in order to destroy the worshippers of Baal. 20 He said, “Consecrate a solemn assembly for Baal.” And they proclaimed it.

21 Jehu sent throughout all Israel; all the worshippers of Baal came, so that not one was left who did not come. They entered the house of Baal, and it was filled from end to end.

22 He said to the keeper of the wardrobe, “Bring out garments for all the worshippers of Baal.” So he brought out garments for them.

23 Jehu went in with Jehonadab son of Rechab into the house of Baal and said to the worshippers of Baal, “Search and see that none of the servants of the LORD is here with you, but only worshippers of Baal.”

24 They went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed eighty men outside and said, “If any of the men whom I bring into your hands escapes, the one who lets him escape shall forfeit his life in exchange for the fugitive’s.”

25 As soon as he finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu commanded the guards and the officers, “Go in and strike them down; let no one get out.” The guards and officers struck them with the edge of the sword, cast the bodies out, and proceeded to the city of the house of Baal.

26 They brought out the sacred pillars of the house of Baal and burned them.

27 They demolished the pillar of Baal and tore down the house of Baal, making it a latrine to this day.

28 Thus Jehu eradicated Baal from Israel.

Jehu’s Incomplete Reformation and the Erosion of Israel

29 Yet Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had caused Israel to sin—the golden calves at Bethel and at Dan.

30 The LORD said to Jehu, “Because you have acted well in doing what is right in My eyes, and have carried out against the house of Ahab all that was in My heart, your sons shall sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.”

31 But Jehu was not careful to walk in the law of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, by which he had caused Israel to sin.

32 In those days the LORD began to cut Israel down, and Hazael defeated them throughout all the territory of Israel: 33 from the Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead—the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the Manassites—from Aroer by the Arnon Valley, including Gilead and Bashan.

34 Now the rest of the acts of Jehu, all that he did and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

35 Jehu slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria. His son Jehoahaz reigned in his place.

36 The time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.
