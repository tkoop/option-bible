Indictment of Israel’s Leadership and People

1 Hear this, O priests; attend, O house of Israel; give ear, O king—for judgment is upon you, because you have become a snare at Mizpah and a net spread over Tabor. 2 The rebels have sunk profoundly into slaughter, yet I remain a chastisement to them all. 3 I know Ephraim, and Israel is not concealed from Me; for now Ephraim has prostituted itself, and Israel is defiled. 4 Their deeds will not permit them to return to their God, for a spirit of promiscuity dwells within them, and they have no knowledge of the LORD.

The Pride and Fall of the Northern Kingdoms

5 Israel’s pride bears witness against him; Israel and Ephraim stumble in their iniquity, and Judah stumbles with them. 6 They go with their flocks and herds to seek the LORD, but they will not find Him; He has withdrawn from them. 7 They have dealt treacherously with the LORD; for they have begotten alien offspring. Now a single month will devour them together with their fields.

The Alarm Sounded Against Benjamin

8 Sound the horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; raise the battle cry in Beth-aven: “Behind you, Benjamin!”

The Futile Appeal to Assyria

9 Ephraim shall be desolate on the day of rebuke; among the tribes of Israel I have announced what is certain. 10 The princes of Judah are like those who displace boundary stones; I will pour out My wrath upon them like water. 11 Ephraim is oppressed, crushed in judgment, because it willingly pursued a worthless decree. 12 I will be to Ephraim like a moth and to the house of Judah like rot. 13 When Ephraim perceived its sickness and Judah its wound, Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to the great king; yet he cannot heal you or cure your wound. 14 For I will be like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to the house of Judah; I Myself will tear and depart; I will carry away, and no one will rescue them. 15 I will go and return to My place until they acknowledge their guilt and seek My face; in their distress they will seek Me earnestly.
