The LORD's Chosen Servant

1 Behold My Servant, whom I uphold, My chosen one, in whom My soul delights; I have put My Spirit upon Him; He will bring forth justice to the nations. 2 He will not cry out or raise His voice, nor make His voice heard in the street. 3 A bruised reed He will not break, and a smoldering wick He will not snuff out; He will faithfully bring forth justice. 4 He will not grow faint or be discouraged until He has established justice on the earth, and the coastlands will wait for His law.

The LORD's Covenant and Light for the Nations

5 Thus says the the LORD God, who created the heavens and stretched them out, who spread out the earth and its offspring, who gives breath to the people on it and spirit to those who walk in it:

“6 I, the the LORD, have called You in righteousness; I will take hold of Your hand. I will keep You and appoint You as a covenant for the people, a light for the nations, 7 to open the eyes of the blind, to bring prisoners out of the dungeon, and those who sit in darkness out of the prison. 8 I am the the LORD; that is My name. I will not give My glory to another, nor My praise to idols. 9 See, the former things have come to pass, and now I declare new things; before they spring forth I announce them to you.”

A New Song of Praise

10 Sing to the the LORD a new song, His praise from the ends of the earth, you who go down to the sea and all that fills it, the coastlands and their inhabitants. 11 Let the wilderness and its cities lift up their voices, the villages where Kedar dwells; let the inhabitants of Sela sing for joy; let them shout from the tops of the mountains. 12 Let them give glory to the the LORD and declare His praise in the coastlands.

The LORD's Zeal and Israel's Blindness

13 The The LORD will go forth like a warrior; He will stir up His zeal like a man of war. He will shout; yes, He will roar. He will prevail against His enemies. 14 “I have been silent for a long time; I have been still and restrained Myself. Now I will cry out like a woman in labor; I will gasp and pant together. 15 I will lay waste the mountains and hills and dry up all their vegetation; I will turn the rivers into islands and dry up the pools. 16 I will lead the blind by a way they do not know; I will guide them in paths they have not known. I will turn darkness into light before them and make the crooked places straight. These things I will do, and I will not forsake them.”

17 They will be turned back and utterly put to shame—those who trust in carved images, who say to molten images, ‘You are our gods.’

18 Hear, you deaf, and look, you blind, that you may see. 19 Who is blind but My servant, or deaf like My messenger whom I send? Who is blind like the one devoted to Me, or blind like the servant of the the LORD? 20 You have seen many things, but you do not observe them; your ears are open, but you do not hear.

21 The the LORD was pleased, for His righteousness' sake, to magnify the law and make it glorious.

22 But this is a people plundered and looted, all of them trapped in holes or hidden in prisons. They have become plunder with no one to rescue them, spoil with no one to say, ‘Bring them back!’ 23 Who among you will pay attention to this, who will listen and hear hereafter? 24 Who gave Jacob to the plunderer, and Israel to the looters? Was it not the the LORD, against whom we have sinned? They were unwilling to walk in His ways, and they did not obey His law. 25 So He poured out on him the heat of His anger and the violence of war. It enveloped him in flames, yet he did not understand; it burned him, but he did not take it to heart.
