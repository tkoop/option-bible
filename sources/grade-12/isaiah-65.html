The Outstretched Hand of The LORD to a Rebellious Nation

1 I allowed Myself to be sought by those who never petitioned Me; I was discovered by those who never pursued Me. To a nation that did not invoke My name, I declared, "Here I am, here I am." 2 Throughout the whole day I have extended My hands toward an intractable people, who pursue a path devoid of goodness and follow their own schemes. 3 They are a people who incessantly provoke Me to My face, sacrificing in gardens and offering incense upon bricks. 4 They dwell among tombs and lodge in concealed places; they consume swine's flesh, and their containers hold broth of abominable things. 5 They say, "Keep to yourself; do not approach me, for I am holier than you." Such people are a continual irritation to Me, a fire that burns all day long.

6 Behold, it is inscribed before Me: I will not remain silent, but I will recompense; indeed, I will repay them in full. 7 Both your iniquities and your fathers' iniquities together, says the LORD—those who burned incense upon the mountains and defied Me upon the hills—I will mete out their retribution in full.

A Preserved Remnant and the Blessing of the LORD's Servants

8 Thus says the LORD: Just as new wine is discovered in the cluster, and someone says, "Do not destroy it, for a blessing resides within it," so I will act on behalf of My servants and not annihilate all of them. 9 I will bring forth posterity from Jacob, and from Judah an heir for My mountains; My elect will inherit it, and My servants will reside there. 10 Sharon will become pastureland for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds, for My people who have sought Me.

11 But you who abandon the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who prepare a table for Fortune and fill bowls of mixed wine for Destiny— 12 I will appoint you to the sword, and all of you will bow down to slaughter, because I called and you did not answer; I spoke and you did not listen. You perpetrated evil in My sight and chose what did not please Me.

13 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: My servants will eat, but you will hunger; My servants will drink, but you will thirst; My servants will rejoice, but you will be disgraced. 14 My servants will exult from a joyful heart, but you will shriek from anguish of heart and wail from a crushed spirit. 15 You will leave your name to My chosen ones as a curse, and the Lord the LORD will put you to death, but to His servants He will grant another name. 16 Whoever invokes a blessing upon himself in the land will bless himself by the God of truth, and whoever swears an oath in the land will swear by the God of truth, because the former afflictions are forgotten and concealed from My eyes.

The New Creation and Jerusalem's Everlasting Joy

17 For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth, and the former things will neither be remembered nor ascend into the mind. 18 But rejoice and exult forever in what I create; for behold, I am making Jerusalem a rejoicing and her people a joy. 19 I will exult in Jerusalem and rejoice in My people; no longer will the sound of weeping or the cry of distress be heard within her. 20 No longer will there be an infant who lives only a few days, nor an elder who fails to complete his days; for the youth will die at a hundred years, and the sinner who reaches a hundred years will be accursed.

21 They will construct houses and dwell in them; they will plant vineyards and consume their fruit. 22 They will not build only for another to inhabit, nor plant only for another to consume; for as the days of a tree, so will be the days of My people, and My chosen ones will enjoy the labor of their hands. 23 They will not toil in vain nor bear children for calamity; for they are the offspring blessed by the LORD, and their descendants along with them. 24 Before they call, I will answer; while they are still speaking, I will hear. 25 The wolf and the lamb will graze together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox, but dust will be the serpent's food. They will neither harm nor destroy on all My holy mountain, says the LORD.
