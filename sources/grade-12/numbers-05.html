Purity Within the Camp

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Command the Israelites to expel from the camp every leper, every person afflicted with a discharge, and everyone defiled by contact with a corpse. 3 You shall remove both men and women; you shall send them outside the camp so that they do not defile the dwelling place where I reside among them.” 4 And the Israelites complied, sending them outside the camp exactly as the LORD had spoken to Moses.

Restitution for Wrongs

5 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 6 “Speak to the Israelites: whenever a man or woman commits any transgression common to humanity, thereby acting treacherously against the LORD, that individual stands guilty. 7 The offender must confess the sin committed, render full restitution for the guilt, augment it by one-fifth, and deliver the total to the person wronged. 8 Yet if the wronged man has no redeemer to receive restitution, the amount owed to the LORD shall be given to the priest, along with the ram of atonement that procures atonement for him. 9 Furthermore, every contribution of holy things that the Israelites present to the priest shall belong to him. 10 Each person’s sacred gifts shall remain his own; whatever anyone offers to the priest shall belong to him.”

The Ordeal of Jealousy

11 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 12 “Speak to the Israelites and declare to them: If a man’s wife strays and proves unfaithful to him, 13 and another man lies with her, and the affair is concealed from her husband’s eyes so that she is defiled without detection—no witness appearing against her and she not having been apprehended in the act— 14 and if a spirit of jealousy overcomes him so that he is jealous of his wife, whether she has defiled herself or not, 15 then the man shall bring his wife to the priest and bring her offering for her: one-tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He shall pour no oil upon it and place no frankincense upon it, because it is a grain offering of jealousy, a memorial offering that summons iniquity to remembrance.

“16 The priest shall bring her near and station her before the LORD; 17 he shall take holy water in an earthen vessel, take some dust from the floor of the tabernacle, and put it into the water. 18 Then the priest shall stand the woman before the LORD, loosen the hair of her head, and place in her hands the memorial grain offering, which is the grain offering of jealousy; in the priest’s hand shall be the water of bitterness that brings a curse.

“19 The priest shall administer an oath to her and say to the woman, ‘If no man has lain with you and you have not strayed into uncleanness while under your husband, you shall be immune to these waters of bitterness that bring a curse; 20 but if you have strayed while under your husband and have defiled yourself, and a man other than your husband has lain with you’— 21 then the priest shall bind the woman under the oath of the curse and say to her, ‘May the LORD make you a curse and an oath among your people when the LORD makes your thigh waste away and your belly swell; 22 may these waters of bitterness that bring a curse enter your stomach and make your belly swell and your thigh waste away.’ And the woman shall say, ‘Amen, Amen.’

“23 The priest shall write these curses in a book and wash them into the water of bitterness. 24 He shall make the woman drink the water of bitterness that brings a curse, so that it may enter her. 25 The priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman’s hand, wave the grain offering before the LORD, and bring it to the altar.

“26 The priest shall take a handful of the grain offering, its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar; afterward he shall make the woman drink the water. 27 When she has drunk it, if she has defiled herself and been unfaithful to her husband, the waters of bitterness that bring a curse shall enter her and produce bitterness, her belly shall swell and her thigh shall waste away, and the woman shall become a curse among her people. 28 But if the woman has not defiled herself and is clean, she shall be free and shall bear children.

“29 This is the law of jealousy when a wife, while under her husband, goes astray and defiles herself, 30 or when a spirit of jealousy comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife; then he shall make the woman stand before the LORD, and the priest shall carry out for her all this law. 31 The man shall be free from guilt, but the woman shall bear her guilt.”
