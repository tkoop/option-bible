The King Imposes Tribute

1 King Ahasuerus imposed a tribute upon the mainland and upon the islands of the sea.

Mordecai's Rise to Prominence

2 As for all the power and might of Mordecai, together with the full account of his greatness to which the king advanced him, are not these things written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia? 3 For Mordecai the Jew was second only to King Ahasuerus; he was great among the Jews and well regarded by the majority of his brothers, seeking the good of his people and speaking peace to all his descendants.
