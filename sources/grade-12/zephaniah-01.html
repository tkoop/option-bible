A Prophetic Message to Zephaniah

1 The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

The LORD Announces Judgment on the Earth

2 "I will utterly sweep away everything from the face of the earth," declares the LORD. 3 "I will sweep away both man and beast; I will sweep away the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, along with the stumbling blocks of the wicked. I will cut off mankind from the face of the earth," declares the LORD.

Judgment on Judah and Its Leaders

4 "I will stretch out My hand against Judah and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem. I will cut off from this place every remnant of Baal, the names of the pagan priests along with the priests, 5 those who bow down on the rooftops to the host of heaven, those who bow down and swear to the LORD but also swear by Milcom, 6 and those who turn back from following the LORD, and those who neither seek the LORD nor inquire of Him," declares the LORD.

7 Be silent before the Lord, the LORD, for the Day of the LORD is near. Indeed, the LORD has prepared a sacrifice; He has consecrated His guests.

8 "On the Day of the LORD, I will punish the officials and the sons of the king and all who dress in foreign clothes. 9 On that day I will punish all who leap over the threshold, who fill the house of their master with violence and deceit," declares the LORD.

10 "On that day," declares the LORD, "a cry will go up from the Fish Gate, a wailing from the Second District, and a loud crash from the hills." 11 Wail, O inhabitants of the Hollow, for all the merchants are ruined; all who weigh out silver are cut off.

12 "At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps and punish the men who are complacent, who say in their hearts, 'The LORD will do nothing, either good or evil.' 13 Their wealth will be plundered and their houses laid waste. They will build houses but not inhabit them, and plant vineyards but not drink their wine," declares the LORD.

The Day of the LORD Approaches

14 The great Day of the LORD is near—near and coming quickly. The sound of the Day of the LORD is bitter; there the mighty warrior cries out. 15 That day is a day of wrath, a day of trouble and distress, a day of ruin and desolation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and blackness, 16 a day of trumpet blast and battle cry against the fortified cities and against the high corner towers.

17 "I will bring distress upon mankind, so that they walk like the blind, because they have sinned against the LORD. Their blood will be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung." 18 Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to deliver them on the Day of the LORD's wrath. The whole earth will be consumed by the fire of His jealousy. For He will make a sudden end of all who dwell on the earth.
