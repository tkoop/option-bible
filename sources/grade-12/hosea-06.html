A Call to Return to God

1 “Come, let us return to the LORD, for He has torn us, and He will heal us; He has wounded us, and He will bind up our wounds. 2 After two days He will revive us; on the third day He will raise us up, that we may live before Him. 3 Let us know; let us press on to know the LORD. His going forth is as sure as the dawn; He will come to us like the rain, like the spring rain that waters the earth.”

God's Indictment of Unfaithful Israel

4 “What shall I do with you, Ephraim? What shall I do with you, Judah? Your steadfast love is like a morning cloud, like the dew that goes away early. 5 Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth, and your judgments go forth as the light. 6 For I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice, and knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings. 7 But they, like Adam, broke the covenant; there they betrayed Me.

Corruption in Gilead and Among the Priests

8 “Gilead is a city of evildoers, tracked with blood. 9 Like marauders lying in wait for a man, a band of priests murders on the road to Shechem; surely they have committed an outrage. 10 In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing: Ephraim has been unfaithful there, and Israel is defiled. 11 Also for you, Judah, a harvest is appointed when I restore the fortunes of My people.”
