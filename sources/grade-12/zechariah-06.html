The Four Chariots

1 I turned again, lifted my eyes, and looked, and behold, four chariots were coming out from between two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of bronze. 2 The first chariot had red horses, and the second had black horses. 3 The third had white horses, and the fourth had dappled horses—all of them strong. 4 I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"

5 The angel answered and said to me, "These are the four spirits of heaven, going out after they have presented themselves before the Lord of all the earth." 6 The black horses are going to the north country, the white horses are following after them, and the dappled horses are going to the south country. 7 The strong horses went out and sought to range throughout the earth, and He said, "Go, range throughout the earth." So they ranged throughout the earth. 8 Then He called to me and said, "Look, those going to the north country have given My Spirit rest in the north country."

The Word of the the LORD Concerning Joshua

9 The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 10 "Take an offering from the exiles—from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have arrived from Babylon—and go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. 11 Take silver and gold, make crowns, and set them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

12 "And say to him, 'Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: Behold, a man whose name is the Branch; He will branch out from His place and build the temple of the the LORD. 13 He will build the temple of the the LORD; He will be clothed in majesty and will sit and rule on His throne. He will be a priest on His throne, and there will be peaceful counsel between the two.'

14 "The crowns will be a memorial in the temple of the the LORD for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen son of Zephaniah. 15 Even those far away will come and build the temple of the the LORD, and you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you. This will happen if you diligently obey the voice of the the LORD your God."
