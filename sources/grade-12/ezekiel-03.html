Ezekiel Consumes the Scroll and Receives His Commission

1 And He said to me, “Son of man, eat what you find; eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel.” 2 So I opened my mouth, and He fed me the scroll. 3 Then He said to me, “Son of man, eat this scroll that I am giving you, and fill your stomach with it.” So I ate it, and it was as sweet as honey in my mouth.

The Stubbornness of Israel and the Prophet's Resolve

4 Then He said to me, “Son of man, go to the house of Israel and speak My words to them. 5 For you are not being sent to a people of unfamiliar speech and difficult language, but to the house of Israel— 6 not to many peoples of unfamiliar speech and difficult language whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you. 7 But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, because they are not willing to listen to Me. For the whole house of Israel is stubborn and obstinate. 8 Behold, I have made your face as hard as their faces, and your forehead as hard as their foreheads. 9 I have made your forehead like diamond, harder than flint. Do not fear them or be dismayed by their presence, though they are a rebellious house.” 10 Moreover, He said to me, “Son of man, take all My words that I speak to you to heart, and listen with your ears. 11 Go now to your people in exile and speak to them. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says,’ whether they listen or refuse.”

Ezekiel Among the Exiles and the Watchman's Duty

12 Then the Spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me a great rumbling: “Blessed be the glory of the the LORD from His place!” 13 It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures brushing against one another and the sound of the wheels beside them—a great rumbling. 14 So the Spirit lifted me up and carried me away, and I went in bitterness and in the anger of my spirit, while the hand of the the LORD was strong upon me. 15 I came to the exiles at Tel Abib, who were living by the Kebar River, and I sat among them, overwhelmed, for seven days.

16 At the end of seven days the word of the the LORD came to me: 17 “Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from My mouth, warn them for Me. 18 When I say to a wicked person, ‘You will surely die,’ and you do not warn him or speak to turn him from his wicked way so that he may live, that wicked person will die in his iniquity, and I will hold you accountable for his blood. 19 But if you warn a wicked person and he does not turn from his wickedness and his wicked way, he will die in his iniquity, but you will have saved yourself. 20 Again, if a righteous person turns from his righteousness and commits iniquity, and I place a stumbling block before him, he will die. Since you did not warn him, he will die in his sin, and his righteous deeds will not be remembered; but I will hold you accountable for his blood. 21 But if you warn a righteous person not to sin, and he does not sin, he will live because he heeded the warning, and you will have saved yourself.”

The Prophet's Silence and Commission to Speak

22 Then the hand of the the LORD was upon me, and He said, “Get up and go out to the plain, and there I will speak with you.” 23 So I got up and went out to the plain, and there stood the glory of the the LORD, like the glory I had seen by the Kebar River, and I fell on my face. 24 Then the Spirit entered me and set me on my feet. He spoke to me and said, “Go, shut yourself inside your house. 25 And you, Son of man, they will put ropes on you and bind you with them, so that you cannot go out among them. 26 I will make your tongue cling to the roof of your mouth, so that you will be mute and unable to rebuke them, for they are a rebellious house. 27 But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you will say to them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says.’ Whoever listens, let him listen; and whoever refuses, let him refuse, for they are a rebellious house.”
