Job Reaffirms His Integrity

1 Job resumed his discourse and said: 2 "As God lives, who has deprived me of justice, and the Almighty, who has embittered my soul— 3 as long as my breath is within me and the spirit of God is in my nostrils, 4 my lips will not speak wickedness, nor will my tongue utter deceit. 5 I will never concede that you are right; I will not relinquish my integrity. 6 I cling to my righteousness and will not let it go; my heart will not reproach me as long as I live.

The Hopeless End of the Godless

7 "Let my enemy be like the wicked, and my adversary like the unrighteous. 8 For what is the hope of the godless when he is cut off, when God takes away his life? 9 Will God hear his cry when distress comes upon him? 10 Will he delight in the Almighty? Will he call upon God at all times? 11 I will teach you about the hand of God; I will not conceal what is with the Almighty. 12 Behold, all of you have seen it; why then do you talk such nonsense?

The Portion of the Wicked

13 "This is the wicked man's portion from God, and the inheritance that oppressors receive from the Almighty. 14 If his sons are many, they are for the sword, and his offspring will never have enough bread. 15 Those who survive him are carried off by plague, and his widows do not weep. 16 Though he heaps up silver like dust and piles up clothing like clay, 17 he may prepare it, but the righteous will wear it, and the innocent will divide his silver. 18 He builds his house like a moth's cocoon, like a booth that a watchman sets up. 19 He lies down wealthy, but will not do so again; he opens his eyes, and he is gone. 20 Terrors overtake him like a flood; a tempest snatches him away by night. 21 The east wind carries him off, and he is gone; it sweeps him out of his place. 22 It hurls itself at him without pity; he flees in vain from its power. 23 It claps its hands at him and hisses at him from its place."
