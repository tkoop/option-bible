1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them,

Clean and Unclean Land Animals

2 "Speak to the Israelites: These are the living creatures among all the beasts on the earth that you may eat. 3 Among the beasts, you may eat any that has a divided hoof—cloven-footed and chewing the cud.

4 "But among those that chew the cud or have a divided hoof, you must not eat these: the camel, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you. 5 The rock badger, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you. 6 The hare, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you. 7 And the pig, because it has a divided hoof and is cloven-footed but does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you. 8 You must not eat their flesh or touch their carcasses; they are unclean for you.

Creatures of the Water and Air

9 "These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the seas and in the rivers—you may eat them. 10 But all that do not have fins and scales in the seas and rivers, among all the swarming creatures in the waters and all living creatures in the waters, are detestable to you. 11 They shall be detestable to you; you must not eat their flesh, and you must detest their carcasses. 12 Whatever in the waters does not have fins and scales is detestable to you.

13 "These you must detest among the birds—they must not be eaten, because they are detestable: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture, 14 the kite, any kind of falcon, 15 any kind of raven, 16 the ostrich, the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk, 17 the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl, 18 the white owl, the desert owl, the osprey, 19 the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.

20 "All winged insects that go on all fours are detestable to you. 21 Yet you may eat these among all the winged insects that go on all fours: those that have jointed legs above their feet for hopping on the ground. 22 Of these you may eat: the locust of any kind, the bald locust of any kind, the cricket of any kind, and the grasshopper of any kind. 23 But all other winged insects that have four feet are detestable to you.

Uncleanness from Carcasses

24 "By these you will become unclean; whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening, 25 and whoever carries any part of their carcasses shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening. 26 Every animal that has a divided hoof but is not cloven-footed or does not chew the cud is unclean for you; everyone who touches them shall be unclean. 27 All that go on their paws among the four-footed animals are unclean for you; whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening. 28 And whoever carries their carcasses shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening; they are unclean for you.

29 "These are unclean for you among the swarming creatures that swarm on the ground: the mole, the mouse, and any kind of great lizard, 30 the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon. 31 These are unclean for you among all the swarming creatures; whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening. 32 And anything on which one of them falls when dead—whether wood, cloth, hide, or sackcloth, or any article used for work—must be rinsed with water and will be unclean until evening; then it will be clean.

33 "If any of them falls into a clay pot, everything in it shall be unclean, and you must break the pot. 34 Any food that may be eaten, on which water has come, and any drink that may be drunk from such a vessel, shall be unclean. 35 And everything on which a carcass falls shall be unclean: an oven or stove shall be broken up; they are unclean and shall be unclean for you. 36 Nevertheless, a spring or a cistern holding water shall be clean, but whoever touches a carcass in them shall be unclean. 37 If a carcass falls on any seed that is to be sown, it is clean; 38 but if water has been put on the seed and a carcass falls on it, it is unclean for you.

39 "If an animal that you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening. 40 And whoever eats of its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening, and whoever carries its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.

Holiness and Distinction

41 "Every swarming creature that swarms on the ground is detestable; it must not be eaten. 42 You must not eat any creature that moves along the ground, whether it goes on its belly, or on all fours, or on many feet; all such are detestable. 43 Do not make yourselves detestable by any swarming creature that swarms; do not defile yourselves with them, or you will become unclean by them.

44 "For I am the LORD your God. Consecrate yourselves therefore, and be holy, for I am holy. You must not defile yourselves with any swarming creature that moves along the ground. 45 For I am the LORD who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; you shall be holy, for I am holy.

46 "This is the law concerning the animals, birds, every living thing that moves in the waters, and every creature that swarms on the ground, 47 to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creature that may be eaten and the living creature that may not be eaten."
