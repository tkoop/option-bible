Jeremiah's Anguish Over a Corrupt People

1 If only my head were a spring of water and my eyes a fountain of tears, I would weep day and night for the slain among my people. 2 If only I had a traveler's shelter in the wilderness, I would leave my people and go far from them, for they are all adulterers, a crowd of treacherous men.

3 "They bend their tongues like bows to shoot lies; they grow strong in the land, but not for faithfulness. They advance from one evil to the next, and they do not acknowledge Me," declares the LORD.

4 "Let every man guard yourselves, and trust no neighbor, for every brother is a schemer and every neighbor goes about slandering. 5 Each man deceives his neighbor and refuses to speak the truth; they have trained their tongues to lie and wear themselves out doing wrong. 6 Your dwelling is amid deceit; in deceit they refuse to know Me," declares the LORD.

Refining a Rebellious People

7 Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says: "See, I will refine and test them, for what else can I do because of My people? 8 Their tongues are drawn bows that shoot lies; deceit fills their mouths. They speak peace to their neighbors while setting ambushes in their hearts. 9 Should I not punish them for these things? Should I not take vengeance on a nation like this?" declares the LORD.

The Land Laid Waste

10 I will take up weeping and wailing for the mountains and a lament for the wilderness pastures, because they are burned over so that no one passes through, and the lowing of cattle is not heard. From the birds of the sky to the beasts, all have fled and gone. 11 I will make Jerusalem a heap of ruins, a haunt of jackals, and the cities of Judah a desolation without inhabitant.

12 Who is the wise man who understands this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he might declare it? Why has the land perished and been scorched like a wilderness, with no one passing through? 13 The LORD said, "Because they have forsaken My law, which I set before them, and have not obeyed My voice or walked in it. 14 Instead, they have followed the stubbornness of their hearts and the Baals, as their fathers taught them."

15 Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: "See, I will feed this people wormwood and give them bitter water to drink. 16 I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send the sword after them until I have finished them off."

Mourning and the Coming Reckoning

17 This is what the LORD of Hosts says: "Consider, and call for the mourning women, that they may come; send for the skilled women, that they may come. 18 Let them hurry and raise a wailing over us, so that our eyes may overflow with tears and our eyelids gush water. 19 For a voice of wailing is heard from Zion: 'How ruined we are! We are deeply ashamed, because we have left the land, because our dwellings have been thrown down.'"

20 "Hear, women, the word of the LORD, and let your ear receive the word of His mouth. Teach your daughters to wail, and each woman her neighbor to lament. 21 For death has climbed through our windows and entered our palaces, cutting off children from the streets and young men from the squares."

22 Speak: "This is what the LORD says: 'The corpses of men will fall like dung on the open field, like sheaves behind the reaper, with no one to gather them.'"

23 "This is what the LORD says: 'Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, nor the mighty man boast in his might, nor the rich man boast in his riches. 24 But let the one who boasts boast in this: that he understands and knows Me, that I am the LORD, who practices steadfast love, justice, and righteousness on the earth, for in these things I delight,' declares the LORD."

25 "See, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will punish all who are circumcised yet uncircumcised: 26 Egypt, Judah, Edom, the Ammonites, Moab, and all who live in the wilderness and clip the hair at their temples. For all the nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart."
