The Prosperity of the God-Fearing Household

1 Blessed is everyone who fears the LORD and walks in His ways. 2 When you eat the fruit of your labor, you are blessed, and it goes well with you.

3 Your wife shall be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children shall be like olive shoots around your table. 4 Thus is the man blessed who fears the LORD.

5 May the LORD bless you from Zion, that you may see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life. 6 May you see your children's children; peace be upon Israel.
