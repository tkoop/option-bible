Oracle Against Tyre

1 An oracle concerning Tyre: Wail, you ships of Tarshish, for Tyre has been destroyed; there is no house left and no harbor, and from the land of Cyprus the news has been revealed to them. 2 Be still, you inhabitants of the coastland, you merchants of Sidon, whose traders have crossed the sea. 3 On the great waters came the grain of Shihor; the harvest of the Nile was her revenue, and she became the marketplace of the nations.

Sidon's Shame and Egypt's Anguish

4 Be ashamed, Sidon, fortress of the sea, for the sea has declared, “I have not labored, nor given birth; I have not raised young men, nor brought up virgins.” 5 When the report reaches Egypt, they will writhe in anguish at the report of Tyre. 6 Cross over to Tarshish; wail, you inhabitants of the coastland.

7 Is this your jubilant city, whose origin reaches back to ancient days, whose feet carried it to settle in a distant land?

The LORD's Purpose for Tyre's Humiliation

8 Who has planned this against Tyre, the bestower of crowns, whose merchants are princes and whose traders are the renowned of the earth? 9 The LORD of Hosts has planned it, to profane all pride of beauty and to humble all the honored of the earth. 10 Cross your land like the Nile, daughter of Tarshish, for there is no harbor left. 11 He stretched out His hand over the sea and shook the kingdoms; the the LORD has commanded concerning Canaan to destroy its strongholds. 12 And He said, “You shall exult no more, O oppressed virgin daughter of Sidon. Arise, cross over to Cyprus; even there you will find no rest.”

13 Behold, the land of the Chaldeans—this is the people that was not; Assyria appointed it for the beasts of the desert. They raised up its siege towers, they overthrew its palaces, and they made it a ruin. 14 Wail, ships of Tarshish, for your stronghold is destroyed.

Seventy Years of Obscurity and Return

15 In that day Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, like the days of one king. At the end of seventy years it will be with Tyre as in the song of the prostitute: 16 “Take a harp, go about the city, O forgotten prostitute; make sweet melody, sing many songs, that you may be remembered.”

17 At the end of seventy years the the LORD will visit Tyre, and she will return to her hire, and she will prostitute herself with all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth. 18 Her merchandise and her hire will be holy to the the LORD. It will not be stored or hoarded, but her merchandise will be for those who dwell before the the LORD, for sufficient food and for durable clothing.
