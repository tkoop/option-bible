God Summons the Nations to Judgment

1 “Maintain your silence before Me, O coastlands, and let the peoples renew their strength; let them draw near and then speak, and let us draw near together for judgment. 2 Who has stirred up one from the east, calling him in righteousness to His service? He delivers nations into his hand and subjugates kings before him, reducing them to dust beneath his sword and to wind-driven chaff before his bow. 3 He pursues them and advances unscathed along a path his feet have never before trodden. 4 Who has accomplished this and brought it to pass, summoning the generations from the beginning? I, the LORD—the First and the Last—I am He.”

5 The coastlands behold and tremble, and the ends of the earth are seized with dread as they draw near and assemble. 6 Each one strengthens his neighbor and says to his brother, “Be strong!” 7 The craftsman encourages the goldsmith, and the one who smooths the metal with a hammer spurs on the striker of the anvil; he declares the soldering good and fastens it with nails so that it will not topple.

Comfort and Vindication for God’s Servant

8 “But you, O Israel, My servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, descendant of Abraham My friend— 9 I brought you from the ends of the earth and summoned you from its remotest corners. I said, ‘You are My servant.’ I have chosen you and have not rejected you. 10 Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you; I will indeed help you; I will uphold you with My righteous right hand. 11 Behold, all who rage against you will be put to shame and disgraced. Those who contend with you will be brought to nothing and will perish. 12 You will seek them, yet you will not find them; those who war against you will be reduced to nothing. 13 For I am the LORD your God, who grasps your right hand and says to you, ‘Do not fear; I will help you.’”

14 “Do not fear, O worm Jacob, O few men of Israel. I will help you,” declares the LORD. “Your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel.”

Empowerment for Israel and Restoration for the Needy

15 “Behold, I will make you a new, sharp threshing sledge with many teeth. You will thresh the mountains and crush them, and you will reduce the hills to chaff. 16 You will winnow them, and the wind will carry them away; the gale will scatter them. But you will rejoice in the LORD; you will glory in the Holy One of Israel.”

17 The poor and needy seek water, yet there is none; their tongues are parched with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them; I, the God of Israel, will not abandon them. 18 I will open rivers on the barren heights and springs in the midst of the valleys. I will transform the desert into a pool of water and the dry land into flowing springs. 19 I will plant cedars in the wilderness, along with acacias, myrtles, and olive trees; I will set pines in the desert, together with fir and cypress, 20 so that all may see and know, may consider and understand, that the hand of the LORD has accomplished this and that the Holy One of Israel has created it.

The Futility of Idols and God’s Vindication

21 “Present your case,” says the LORD.

“Bring forth your strong arguments,” says the King of Jacob. 22 “Let them come and declare to us what will happen. Tell us what the former things were, so that we may reflect upon them and know their outcome. Or announce to us the things that are to come. 23 Tell us what is to come hereafter, that we may know that you are gods. Yes, do good or do evil, that we may be dismayed and filled with terror. 24 Behold, you are nothing, and your work amounts to nothing. He who chooses you is detestable.”

25 I have raised up one from the north, and he has come—from the east he calls upon My name. He treads upon rulers as upon mortar, as a potter tramples clay. 26 Who has declared this from the beginning, that we may know, and from times past, that we may say, ‘He is righteous’? No one proclaimed it, no one foretold it, no one heard your words. 27 I was the first to say to Zion, ‘Behold, here they are!’ and I gave to Jerusalem a herald of good news. 28 But when I look, there is no one; there is no counselor among them who, when I ask, can return an answer. 29 Behold, they are all a delusion; their works are nothing; their images are but wind and confusion.
