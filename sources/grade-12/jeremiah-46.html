The LORD's Word Concerning the Nations

1 The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations. 2 Concerning Egypt: the army of Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, which was defeated at Carchemish on the Euphrates by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

Egypt's Army and Its Defeat at Carchemish

3 Prepare shield and buckler, and advance to battle. 4 Harness the horses and mount, O horsemen; take your stand with helmets; polish the spears and put on the coats of mail.

5 Why have I seen it? They are dismayed and turn backward; their warriors are beaten down and flee headlong; they do not look back; terror is on every side, declares the LORD. 6 Let not the swift escape, nor the mighty man get away; northward by the river Euphrates they stumble and fall.

7 Who is this rising like the Nile, like rivers whose waters surge? 8 Egypt rises like the Nile, and its waters surge like rivers; it says, "I will rise, I will cover the earth, I will destroy the city and its inhabitants."

9 Advance, O horses; rage, O chariots; let the warriors go out—Cush and Put, who handle the shield, and the Ludim, who draw the bow. 10 That day is to the Lord the LORD of Hosts a day of vengeance, to avenge Himself on His foes; the sword shall devour and be satisfied, and drink its fill of their blood; for the Lord the LORD of Hosts has a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

Egypt's Wounds and Futile Remedies

11 Go up to Gilead and take balm, O virgin daughter of Egypt! In vain you have multiplied remedies; there is no healing for you. 12 The nations have heard of your shame, and your cry has filled the land; for warrior has stumbled against warrior, and both have fallen together.

Babylon's Invasion and Egypt's Humiliation

13 The word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about the coming of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to strike the land of Egypt: 14 "Declare in Egypt and proclaim in Migdol; proclaim also in Memphis and in Tahpanhes; say, 'Take your stand and prepare yourself, for the sword devours around you.'" 15 Why are your mighty ones swept away? They did not stand, because the LORD drove them. 16 He multiplied the stumbling; indeed, one fell upon another, and they said, "Arise, let us return to our people and to the land of our birth, away from the sword of the oppressor."

17 There they cried out, "Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he has let the appointed moment pass by." 18 As I live, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, surely, as Tabor among the mountains and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come. 19 Prepare your baggage for exile, O daughter who dwells in Egypt, for Memphis shall become a desolation and be burned, without inhabitant. 20 Egypt is a beautiful heifer, but a gadfly from the north has come upon her.

21 Even her hired soldiers in her midst are like fattened calves; they too have turned and fled together; they did not stand, for the day of their calamity has come upon them, the time of their punishment. 22 Her sound is like a serpent's hiss, for they march in force and come against her with axes, like woodcutters. 23 They shall cut down her forest, declares the LORD, though it is impenetrable, because they are more numerous than locusts and without number. 24 The daughter of Egypt shall be put to shame; she shall be delivered into the hand of a people from the north.

Judgment on Egypt and Restoration

25 The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "Behold, I will punish Amon of Thebes, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, and her gods, and her kings—Pharaoh and those who trust in him. 26 I will deliver them into the hand of those who seek their lives, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and into the hand of his servants. Afterward Egypt shall be inhabited as in the days of old," declares the LORD.

Comfort and Restoration for Israel

27 But as for you, Jacob My servant, do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed, O Israel, for I will save you from afar and your offspring from the land of their captivity. Jacob will return and be at rest and secure, and no one will make him afraid. 28 You, Jacob My servant, do not be afraid, declares the LORD, for I am with you; I will make a full end of all the nations among whom I have driven you, but I will not make a full end of you. I will discipline you justly, and I will by no means leave you unpunished.
