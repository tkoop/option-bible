Joseph Prospers in Potiphar’s House

1 Joseph had been brought down to Egypt, where Potiphar—an Egyptian official of Pharaoh and captain of the guard—purchased him from the Ishmaelites who had transported him there. 2 The LORD was with Joseph, and he became a prosperous man, residing in the house of his Egyptian master. 3 His master perceived that the LORD was with him and that the LORD brought prosperity to everything he undertook. 4 Joseph won his master’s favor and served him; his master appointed him steward of the household and entrusted to him all his possessions. 5 From the moment he placed Joseph in charge of his household and all that he owned, the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s house for Joseph’s sake, and the LORD’s blessing rested upon everything he had, both indoors and in the fields. 6 Consequently, he relinquished oversight of all his property to Joseph and concerned himself with nothing except the food he consumed. Now Joseph was strikingly handsome and well proportioned.

Potiphar’s Wife Tempts Joseph

7 After these events, his master’s wife looked at Joseph and said, “Have sexual relations with me.”

8 But he refused and said to his master’s wife, “Look, my master does not know what is in the house with me, and he has entrusted everything he owns to my care. 9 No one in this household is greater than I am, and he has withheld nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How then could I commit this great wickedness and sin against God?”

10 Although she spoke to Joseph day after day, he would not listen to her, nor would he lie beside her or be with her. 11 One day, however, he entered the house to perform his duties, and none of the household servants was present.

12 She caught him by his cloak and said, “Have sexual relations with me,” but he left his cloak in her hand and ran outside.

Joseph Is Falsely Accused

13 As soon as she saw that he had left his cloak in her hand and had fled outside, 14 she summoned the men of her household and said, “See, he has brought a Hebrew into our house to mock us. He came in to me to have sexual relations with me, but I cried out in a loud voice. 15 When he heard me raise my voice and cry out, he left his cloak beside me and ran outside.”

16 She kept his cloak beside her until his master came home. 17 Then she said to him, “The Hebrew servant whom you brought to us came in to me to mock me. 18 But when I raised my voice and cried out, he left his cloak beside me and ran outside.”

19 When his master heard the words his wife had spoken to him—“This is what your servant did to me”—his anger burned. 20 Joseph’s master took him and confined him in prison, the place where the king’s prisoners were held, and he remained there.

The LORD Blesses Joseph in Prison

21 But the LORD was with Joseph and showed him steadfast love, and He gave him favor in the sight of the prison keeper. 22 The prison keeper entrusted to Joseph all the prisoners in the prison, and whatever was done there, he was the one who did it. 23 The prison keeper paid no attention to anything under Joseph’s care, because the LORD was with him, and the LORD made everything he did prosper.
