A Devastating Drought Afflicts Judah

1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the droughts. 2 Judah mourns, and her gates languish; they bow to the ground in blackness, and the cry of Jerusalem rises. 3 Her nobles send their servants for water; they come to the cisterns and find none; they return with empty vessels, ashamed and confounded, and they cover their heads. 4 Because the ground is dismayed—there has been no rain in the land—the plowmen are ashamed and cover their heads. 5 Even the doe in the field gives birth and abandons her young, because there is no grass. 6 The wild donkeys stand on the bare heights, panting like jackals; their eyes fail because there is no vegetation.

Judah Confesses Its Sin and Pleads for Mercy

7 Though our iniquities testify against us, do, O the LORD, for Your name's sake; for our backslidings are many, and we have sinned against You. 8 O Hope of Israel, her Savior in time of distress, why should You be like a stranger in the land, like a traveler who turns aside to spend the night? 9 Why should You be like a man stunned, like a mighty one who cannot save? Yet You are in our midst, O the LORD, and we are called by Your name; do not forsake us.

The LORD Rejects the People and Condemns False Prophets

10 Thus says the LORD to this people: “They love to wander; they have not restrained their feet. Therefore the LORD does not accept them; now He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins.”

11 The LORD said to me, “Do not pray for the welfare of this people. 12 When they fast, I will not hear their cry; when they offer burnt offering and grain offering, I will not accept them; rather, I will consume them by sword, famine, and pestilence.”

13 Then I said, “Ah, Lord, the LORD! Behold, the prophets say to them, ‘You will not see the sword, nor will there be famine in this place; but I will give you true peace in this place.’”

14 “The prophets prophesy lies in My name,” the LORD said to me. “I have not sent them, nor commanded them, nor spoken to them. They prophesy to you a lying vision, worthless divination, and the deceit of their own minds. 15 Therefore thus says the LORD concerning the prophets who prophesy in My name, though I did not send them, and who say, ‘No sword or famine shall come upon this land’: By sword and famine those prophets shall be consumed. 16 And the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword, with no one to bury them—their wives, their sons, and their daughters. I will pour out their evil upon them.”

Jeremiah's Grief and Final Intercession

17 The LORD told Jeremiah to declare to them, “My eyes overflow with tears night and day, and let them not cease, for the virgin daughter of my people has been shattered by a great breach, a very grievous wound.”

18 If I go out into the field, behold, those slain by the sword; if I enter the city, behold, diseases from famine. For both prophet and priest ply their trade throughout the land, but they have no knowledge.

19 Have You utterly rejected Judah? Has Your soul loathed Zion? Why have You struck us so that there is no healing? We looked for peace, but no good came; for a time of healing, but behold, terror. 20 We acknowledge our wickedness, O the LORD, the iniquity of our fathers; for we have sinned against You. 21 For Your name's sake, do not spurn us; do not dishonor the throne of Your glory. Remember; do not break Your covenant with us. 22 Are there any among the idols of the nations that can bring rain? Or can the heavens give showers? Is it not You, O the LORD our God? Therefore we hope in You, for You have made all these things.
