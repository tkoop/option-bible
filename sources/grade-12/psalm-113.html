Praise for the LORD’s Exaltation and Care

1 Praise the LORD! Praise, servants of the LORD; praise the name of the LORD. 2 Blessed be the name of the LORD from this time forth and forevermore. 3 Across the entire world, the name of the LORD is to be praised.

4 The LORD is high above all nations, and His glory is above the heavens. 5 Who is like the LORD our God, who is seated on high, 6 who looks far down upon the heavens and the earth? 7 He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, 8 to seat them with princes, with the princes of His people. 9 He settles the childless woman in a home, making her a joyful mother of children. Praise the LORD!
