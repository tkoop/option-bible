Abraham and Sarah in Gerar

1 Abraham traveled from there toward the Negev and settled between Kadesh and Shur, and he sojourned in Gerar. 2 Abraham said of Sarah his wife, "She is my sister." Then Abimelech, king of Gerar, sent for her and took Sarah.

3 But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night and said to him, "You are as good as dead because of the woman you have taken, for she is another man's wife."

4 Now Abimelech had not approached her, so he said, "Lord, will You destroy even a righteous nation? 5 Did he not say to me, 'She is my sister'? And she herself said, 'He is my brother.' I have done this with an upright heart and innocent hands."

6 Then God said to him in the dream, "I know that you did this with an upright heart, and I also kept you from sinning against Me; therefore I did not let you touch her. 7 Now then, restore the man's wife, for he is a prophet, and he will pray for you, and you will live. But if you do not restore her, know that you will surely die, you and all that is yours."

8 So Abimelech rose early in the morning, called all his servants, and told them all these things; and the men were exceedingly afraid.

Abimelech Confronts Abraham

9 Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? How have I sinned against you, that you have brought a great sin upon me and my kingdom? You have done to me things that ought not to be done. 10 What were you thinking, that you did this thing?"

11 Abraham said, "I thought, 'There is surely no fear of God in this place, and they will kill me because of my wife.' 12 Besides, she really is my sister, the daughter of my father though not of my mother, and she became my wife. 13 When God caused me to wander from my father's house, I said to her, 'This is the kindness you can show me: everywhere we go, say of me, "He is my brother."'"

14 Then Abimelech took sheep, oxen, male and female servants, and gave them to Abraham, and he restored Sarah his wife to him.

15 Abimelech said, "Look, my land is before you; settle wherever seems best to you."

16 And to Sarah he said, "Look, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver. It is your vindication before all who are with you, and you are fully cleared."

God Heals Abimelech's Household

17 Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants, and they bore children. 18 For the LORD had closed every womb in Abimelech's household because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
