Oracle Against Moab

1 Concerning Moab, this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Woe to Nebo, for it has been devastated! Kiriathaim has been captured; the stronghold is disgraced and dismayed. 2 No longer will Moab be praised. In Heshbon they plotted evil against her, saying, 'Come, let us cut her off from being a nation.' You too, Madmen, will be silenced; the sword will pursue you."

3 A cry of anguish rises from Horonaim: 'Plunder and great destruction!' 4 Moab is shattered; even her little ones raise a cry. 5 On the ascent to Luhith they climb weeping, and on the descent to Horonaim they hear cries of distress.

6 "Flee and save your lives; make yourselves like a shrub in the desert. 7 Because you have trusted in your works and treasures, you too will be captured; Chemosh will go into exile together with his priests and officials."

The Fall of Moab's Strongholds

8 The destroyer will come against every city, and no city will escape. The valley will be ruined and the plateau destroyed, because the LORD has spoken.

9 "Give Moab wings, for she will take flight; her towns will become a wasteland, without a single inhabitant. 10 Cursed is he who does the work of the LORD deceitfully, and cursed is he who withholds his sword from blood."

11 Moab has been at ease from his youth; he has settled on his dregs and has not been poured from vessel to vessel, nor has he gone into exile. Therefore his flavor remains, and his aroma has not changed. 12 "Therefore, behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send to him those who pour, and they will pour him out; they will empty his vessels and shatter his wine jars." 13 Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their confidence.

14 "How can you say, 'We are mighty men, warriors valiant in battle'?"

15 Moab has been destroyed, and her cities have gone up in smoke; her best young men have gone down to the slaughter, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts. 16 The disaster of Moab is near at hand; his calamity has come quickly.

17 Mourn for him, all you who surround him, all you who know his name. Say, "How the mighty staff is broken, the glorious scepter!"

18 "Come down from your glory and sit in thirst, O daughter dwelling in Dibon, for the destroyer of Moab has come up against you; he has destroyed your fortresses. 19 Stand by the road and watch, O inhabitant of Aroer; ask him who flees and her who escapes; say, 'What has happened?'" 20 Moab is put to shame, for it is broken down; wail and cry out! Declare by the Arnon that Moab is destroyed.

21 Judgment has come upon the plateau: upon Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath, 22 upon Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim, 23 upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon, 24 upon Kerioth, Bozrah, and all the cities of the land of Moab, far and near.

Moab's Pride and Coming Judgment

25 The horn of Moab is cut off, and his arm is broken, declares the LORD. 26 "Make him drunk, for he has exalted himself against the LORD; Moab will wallow in his vomit and become a laughingstock."

27 "Was not Israel a laughingstock to you? Whenever you spoke of him, you shook your head. 28 Leave the cities and dwell among the rocks, O inhabitants of Moab; be like a dove that nests in the clefts of a gorge."

29 We have heard of the pride of Moab—his exceeding pride—his arrogance, pride, and haughtiness, and the loftiness of his heart. 30 "I know his insolence, declares the LORD, but it is futile; his boastings have accomplished nothing."

Lament and Future Restoration

31 "Therefore I wail for Moab; I cry out for all Moab; I mourn for the men of Kir-heres. 32 O vine of Sibmah, I weep for you more than for Jazer. Your branches have spread to the sea and reached the Sea of Jazer. The destroyer has fallen upon your summer fruit and your grape harvest. 33 Joy and gladness are removed from the fruitful field and from the land of Moab. I have made the wine to cease from the winepresses; no one treads them with shouting; their shouting is not shouting. 34 From the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh, they raise their voices to Jahaz; from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah; for even the waters of Nimrim will become desolate."

35 "And I will bring an end to Moab, declares the LORD, to the one who offers sacrifice on the high place and burns incense to his god. 36 Therefore My heart laments for Moab like flutes; My heart laments for the men of Kir-heres like flutes, because the wealth he acquired has perished."

37 For every head is shaved and every beard clipped; on every hand are gashes, and on every waist is sackcloth. 38 On all the rooftops of Moab and in its streets there is mourning, for "I have broken Moab like a vessel no one wants," declares the LORD. 39 How shattered it is! How they wail! How Moab has turned his back in shame! So Moab will become a laughingstock and a terror to all around him.

40 For thus says the LORD: "Behold, one will fly like an eagle and spread his wings over Moab. 41 Kerioth will be captured and the strongholds taken. The heart of Moab's warriors will be like the heart of a woman in labor. 42 Moab will be destroyed as a people, because he magnified himself against the LORD. 43 Terror, pit, and snare await you, O inhabitant of Moab, declares the LORD. 44 He who flees from the terror will fall into the pit, and he who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the snare; for I will bring upon them, upon Moab, the year of their punishment, declares the LORD."

45 In the shadow of Heshbon the refugees stand exhausted, for a fire has gone out from Heshbon, a flame from the midst of Sihon; it has consumed the forehead of Moab and the crown of the sons of tumult. 46 "Woe to you, Moab! The people of Chemosh have perished, for your sons have been taken captive and your daughters into captivity."

47 "Yet I will restore the fortunes of Moab in the latter days, declares the LORD." Thus far is the judgment on Moab.
