A Prayer for Deliverance and Rest

1 Answer me when I call, O God of my righteousness; You have brought me into a broad place amid my distress. Be gracious to me and hear my prayer. 2 How long, O men, will my honor be turned to shame? How long will you love vanity and pursue falsehood? Selah. 3 But know that the LORD has set apart the godly for Himself; the LORD hears when I call to Him.

4 Tremble, yet do not sin; commune with your own hearts upon your beds, and be still. Selah. 5 Offer sacrifices of righteousness, and trust in the LORD.

6 Many say, "Who will show us any good?" Let the light of Your face shine upon us, O the LORD. 7 You have put more joy in my heart than they have when their grain and new wine abound. 8 I will both lie down and sleep in peace, for You alone, O the LORD, make me dwell in safety.
