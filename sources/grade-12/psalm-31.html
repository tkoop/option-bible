A Prayer for Refuge and Deliverance

1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. In You, O the LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame; deliver me in Your righteousness. 2 Turn Your ear to me and rescue me quickly; be my rock of refuge, a strong fortress that saves me. 3 For You are my rock and my fortress; lead me and guide me for Your name's sake. 4 Free me from the net they have hidden for me, for You are my stronghold.

5 Into Your hand I commit my spirit; You have redeemed me, O the LORD, God of truth. 6 I hate those who devote themselves to worthless idols, but as for me, I trust in the LORD. 7 I will be glad and rejoice in Your steadfast love, for You have seen my affliction; You have known the anguish of my soul. 8 You have not handed me over to the enemy; You have set my feet in a safe, open place.

9 Be gracious to me, O the LORD, for I am in distress; my eyes grow weak with sorrow, as do my soul and body. 10 For my life is consumed with grief and my years with groaning; my iniquity has sapped my strength, and my bones waste away. 11 Because of all my adversaries I have become a reproach, especially to my neighbors; I am dreaded by my friends, and those who see me on the street flee from me. 12 I am forgotten as though I were dead, out of mind; I am like a broken vessel. 13 For I hear the slander of many; terror is on every side. They conspire together against me and plot to take my life.

14 But I trust in You, O the LORD; I say, "You are my God." 15 My times are in Your hands; deliver me from my enemies and from those who pursue me. 16 Look upon Your servant with favor; save me by Your steadfast love. 17 O the LORD, let me not be put to shame, for I call upon You. Let the wicked be put to shame; let them be silenced in the grave. 18 Let lying lips be silenced—those that speak arrogantly against the righteous with pride and contempt.

19 How great is Your goodness, which You have stored up for those who fear You and shown openly before all people for those who take refuge in You! 20 You hide them in the shelter of Your presence from human plots; You conceal them in Your pavilion from contentious tongues. 21 Blessed be the LORD, for He has shown me His wonderful steadfast love in a besieged city. 22 In my alarm I said, "I am cut off from Your sight!" Yet You heard my cry for mercy when I called to You. 23 Love the LORD, all His godly ones! The LORD preserves the faithful, but He fully repays the arrogant. 24 Be strong and let your heart take courage, all you who hope in the LORD.
