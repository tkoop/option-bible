The Danites Seek an Inheritance

1 In those days there was no king in Israel. In those days the tribe of Dan was seeking an inheritance for itself in which to dwell, for until that day they had not received an inheritance among the tribes of Israel. 2 So the Danites sent five men from their clans, from the borders of their territory—men of valor from Zorah and Eshtaol—to spy out the land and explore it. The Danites said to them, “Go and search the land.” So they went up into the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and lodged there.

3 While they were staying at Micah’s house, they recognized the voice of the young Levite. They turned aside and said to him, “Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What do you have here?”

4 He said to them, “Micah has done this and that for me, and he hired me, and I became his priest.”

5 They said to him, “Please inquire of God for us, so that we may know whether the journey we are undertaking will succeed.”

6 The priest said to them, “Go in peace; the LORD is favorable to your journey.”

The Spies Discover a Vulnerable City

7 So the five men departed and came to Laish, where they saw the people living securely, after the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and secure. There was no ruler in the land to put them to shame in any way, and they were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

8 When they returned to Zorah and Eshtaol, their brothers asked them, “What do you have to report?”

9 They said, “Arise, let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good. Will you sit still? Do not be slack in going to enter and possess the land. 10 When you go, you will come to a secure people and a spacious land, for God has given it into your hand—a place where there is no lack of anything on earth.”

11 So six hundred men of the Danite clan, armed with weapons of war, set out from Zorah and Eshtaol. 12 They went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. Therefore that place is called Mahaneh-dan to this day; behold, it lies behind Kiriath-jearim. 13 From there they passed through the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.

The Danites Seize Micah’s Idols and Priest

14 The five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish said to their brothers, “Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod, household gods, a carved image, and a metal image? Now consider what you should do.”

15 They turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite, the house of Micah, and greeted him.

16 The six hundred Danites, armed with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate. 17 The five men who had gone to spy out the land went in and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the metal image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred armed men.

18 When these men entered Micah’s house and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the metal image, the priest said to them, “What are you doing?”

19 They said to him, “Be silent. Put your hand over your mouth and come with us, and be a father and priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and clan in Israel?”

20 The priest’s heart was glad. He took the ephod, the household gods, and the carved image, and went in the midst of the people. 21 They turned and departed, putting the little ones, the livestock, and the valuables ahead of them.

22 When they had gone far from Micah’s house, the men who lived in the houses near Micah’s house were called together and overtook the Danites. 23 They called to the Danites, who turned around and said to Micah, “What is wrong with you, that you have gathered a company?”

24 He said, “You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and have gone away. What do I have left? How can you say to me, ‘What is wrong with you?’”

25 The Danites said to him, “Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest bitter men attack you, and you lose your life and the lives of your household.”

26 So the Danites went on their way, and Micah saw that they were stronger than he was, and he turned and went back to his house.

The Conquest and Settlement of Laish

27 They took what Micah had made, and the priest who belonged to him, and came to Laish, to a quiet and secure people. They struck them with the edge of the sword and burned the city with fire. 28 There was no deliverer, because it was far from Sidon, and they had no dealings with anyone. It lay in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the city and dwelt in it.

29 They named the city Dan, after the name of Dan their ancestor, who was born to Israel; but the name of the city formerly was Laish. 30 The Danites set up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land. 31 They set up for themselves the carved image that Micah had made, all the time the house of God was at Shiloh.
