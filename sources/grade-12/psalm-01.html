The Way of the Righteous and the Wicked

1 How blessed is the one who does not follow the advice of the wicked, adopt the ways of sinners, or join the company of mockers. 2 Rather, his delight is in the law of the the LORD, and he meditates on His law day and night. 3 He is like a tree planted beside streams of water, producing fruit in its season, whose leaves never wither; everything he does prospers.

4 The wicked are not like this; they are like chaff blown away by the wind. 5 Therefore the wicked will not be acquitted in judgment, and sinners will have no place among the righteous. 6 For the the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked leads to ruin.
