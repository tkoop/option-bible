The Priestly Garments and Their Makers

“1 The LORD said to Moses, “Bring near to yourself Aaron your brother, along with his sons—Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar—from among the Israelites, that he may serve Me as priest. 2 Make holy garments for your brother Aaron, for glory and for beauty. 3 Speak to all who are wise in heart, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and let them make Aaron’s garments to consecrate him for his priesthood to Me. 4 These are the garments they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, an embroidered tunic, a turban, and a sash. They shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons, that he may serve Me as priest. 5 They shall take the gold, the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and the fine linen.

The Ephod and Its Memorial Stones

“6 You shall make the ephod of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and of finely twisted linen, the work of a skilled craftsman. 7 It shall have two shoulder pieces attached at its two ends, so that it is joined. 8 The skillfully woven waistband that is on it shall be of one piece with it—of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen. 9 You shall take two onyx stones. 10 Engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel—six of the names on one stone and the remaining six on the other stone—in the order of their birth. 11 You shall engrave the two stones like a signet ring with the names of the sons of Israel, and mount them in gold filigree settings. 12 Fasten both stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel. Aaron shall bear their names before the the LORD on his two shoulders for a memorial. 13 You shall make gold filigree settings. 14 You shall make two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords, and attach them to the settings.

The Breastpiece of Judgment

“15 You shall make the breastpiece of judgment, skillfully worked like the ephod; you shall make it of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen. 16 It shall be square and doubled, a span in length and a span in width. 17 You shall set in it four rows of stones: 18 a first row of ruby, topaz, and emerald; 19 a second row of turquoise, sapphire, and diamond; 20 a third row of jacinth, agate, and amethyst; and a fourth row of beryl, onyx, and jasper. They shall be mounted in gold filigree settings. 21 The stones shall bear the names of the sons of Israel, twelve according to their names, engraved like signets, one for each of the twelve tribes. 22 You shall make for the breastpiece chains of pure gold, twisted like cords. 23 You shall make two gold rings for the breastpiece and attach them to its two ends. 24 You shall fasten the two gold chains to the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece. 25 you shall fasten the other ends of the chains to the two settings, attaching them to the front shoulder pieces of the ephod. 26 You shall make two gold rings and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece on the edge that is toward the ephod. 27 You shall make two gold rings and attach them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod on its front, near its seam, above the waistband. 28 They shall bind the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so that it rests on the waistband and does not swing loose from the ephod. 29 Aaron shall bear the names of the Israelites on the breastpiece of judgment over his heart when he enters the Holy Place, as a continual memorial before the the LORD. 30 You shall put the Urim and the Thummim in the breastpiece of judgment, and they shall be over Aaron’s heart when he goes before the the LORD. Thus Aaron shall bear the judgment of the Israelites over his heart before the the LORD continually.

The Robe, Turban, and Additional Garments

“31 You shall make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue. 32 Its opening for the head shall be in the middle; it shall have a woven binding around the opening, like the opening of a coat of mail, so that it will not tear. 33 You shall make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn around its hem, with gold bells between them. 34 A gold bell and a pomegranate shall alternate all around the hem of the robe. 35 It shall be on Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he enters the Holy Place before the the LORD and when he comes out, so that he will not die.

“36 You shall make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it, like the engraving of a signet, ‘Holy to the the LORD.’ 37 You shall fasten it on a blue cord and put it on the turban; it shall be on the front of the turban. 38 It shall be on Aaron’s forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the holy things that the Israelites consecrate, all their holy gifts. It shall be on his forehead continually, to make them acceptable before the the LORD. 39 You shall weave the tunic of fine linen, make the turban of fine linen, and make the sash with embroidered work.

“40 You shall make tunics, sashes, and caps for Aaron’s sons, for glory and for beauty. 41 You shall dress Aaron your brother and his sons with them; you shall anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them, that they may serve Me as priests. 42 You shall make linen undergarments to cover their nakedness; they shall extend from the waist to the thighs. 43 Aaron and his sons shall wear them when they enter the tent of meeting or when they approach the altar to minister in the Holy Place, so that they do not incur guilt and die. It shall be a perpetual statute for him and for his offspring after him.”
