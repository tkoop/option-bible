Wise Speech and Its Effects

1 A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. 2 The tongue of the wise commends knowledge, whereas the mouth of fools spews nonsense. 3 The eyes of the LORD are in every place, observing both the wicked and the good. 4 A healing tongue is a tree of life, yet perversity in it crushes the spirit. 5 A fool despises his father's discipline, but one who heeds reproof acts prudently. 6 The house of the righteous holds great treasure, while the income of the wicked brings trouble. 7 Wise lips disseminate knowledge, but foolish hearts do not.

The LORD's Regard for Righteousness

8 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is His delight. 9 The LORD detests the way of the wicked, yet He loves the one who pursues righteousness. 10 Harsh discipline awaits anyone who forsakes the path, and whoever hates reproof will die. 11 Even Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD; how much more, then, the hearts of human beings. 12 A mocker resents correction and will not consult the wise. 13 A joyful heart brightens the countenance, but sorrow of heart crushes the spirit. 14 The discerning heart seeks knowledge, whereas the mouth of fools feeds on folly. 15 All the days of the oppressed are wretched, but a cheerful heart enjoys a continual feast. 16 Better a little with reverence for the LORD than great wealth accompanied by turmoil. 17 Better a simple meal of vegetables where love dwells than a fattened ox served with hatred. 18 A hot-tempered person stirs up strife, but patience quiets contention. 19 The way of the lazy person is blocked with thorns, whereas the path of the upright is a highway.

Character, Counsel, and Conduct

20 A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish man treats his mother with contempt. 21 Folly delights those who lack sense, yet a person of understanding walks uprightly. 22 Plans fail without counsel, but with many advisers they succeed. 23 A person finds joy in a fitting reply; how good is a timely word. 24 The path of life leads upward for the discerning, so that he may avoid the grave below. 25 The LORD demolishes the house of the proud, but He sets the widow's boundary firm. 26 The LORD detests the thoughts of the wicked, but pure words are pleasing to Him. 27 Whoever is greedy for gain troubles his own household, but one who hates bribes will live. 28 The heart of the righteous weighs how to answer, while the mouth of the wicked gushes evil. 29 The LORD is far from the wicked, yet He hears the prayer of the righteous. 30 The light of the eyes rejoices the heart, and good news nourishes the bones. 31 The ear that heeds life-giving reproof will dwell among the wise. 32 Whoever disregards discipline despises himself, but one who heeds correction gains understanding. 33 The fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom, and humility comes before honor.
