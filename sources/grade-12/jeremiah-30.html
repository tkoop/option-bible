The LORD Commands Jeremiah to Record His Promise

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying: 2 This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "Write in a book all the words I have spoken to you. 3 For behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will restore the prosperity of My people Israel and Judah," declares the LORD, "and I will bring them back to the land I gave their fathers, and they will possess it."

4 These are the words the LORD spoke concerning Israel and Judah.

The Terror and Distress of the Coming Day

5 For thus says the LORD: "We have heard a cry of terror, of fear, and of no peace. 6 Ask now, and see: Can a man give birth? Why then do I see every man with his hands on his loins like a woman in labor, and every face drained of color? 7 Alas, that day is so great that none is like it; it is a time of distress for Jacob, yet he will be saved out of it."

8 "And in that day," declares the LORD of Hosts, "I will break the yoke from your neck and snap your bonds, so that foreigners will no longer enslave him. 9 Instead, they will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them."

10 "So do not fear, O Jacob My servant," declares the LORD, "and do not be dismayed, O Israel; for behold, I will save you from afar, and your offspring from the land of their captivity. Jacob shall return and be tranquil and secure, with no one to terrify him. 11 For I am with you to save you," declares the LORD, "For I will make a complete end of all the nations among whom I scattered you, but I will not make a complete end of you; yet I will discipline you justly, and I will by no means leave you unpunished."

Israel's Incurable Wound and Promised Healing

12 For thus says the LORD: "Your injury is incurable; your wound is severe. 13 There is no one to plead your case, no remedy to heal your wound; there is no recovery for you. 14 All your allies have forgotten you; they do not seek you. For I have struck you with an enemy's blow, with the discipline of a cruel oppressor, because of the greatness of your iniquity; your sins have increased. 15 Why do you cry out over your injury? Your pain is incurable. Because of the greatness of your iniquity and your many sins, I have done these things to you. 16 Therefore all who devour you shall be devoured, and all your adversaries, every one of them, shall go into captivity; those who plunder you shall be plundered, and all who prey upon you I will give as plunder. 17 For I will restore health to you and heal you of your wounds, declares the LORD, because they have called you an outcast, saying, 'This is Zion, whom no one cares for.'"

The Restoration and Renewal of Zion

18 Thus says the LORD: "Behold, I will restore the prosperity of Jacob's tents and have compassion on his dwellings; the city shall be rebuilt on its mound, and the palace shall remain on its proper site. 19 From them shall come thanksgiving and the sound of those who celebrate; I will multiply them, and they shall not be diminished; I will honor them, and they shall not be lightly esteemed. 20 Their children shall be as in former days, and their congregation shall be established before Me; and I will punish all their oppressors. 21 Their leader shall be one of their own, and their ruler shall come forth from their midst; I will bring him near, and he shall approach Me. For who is the one who would devote his heart to approach Me? declares the LORD. 22 And you shall be My people, and I will be your God."

23 Behold, the storm of the LORD has gone forth in fury, a sweeping whirlwind; it will burst upon the head of the wicked. 24 The fierce anger of the LORD will not turn back until He has accomplished and established the intentions of His heart. In the latter days you will understand this.
