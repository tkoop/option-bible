Household Obligations in the Lord

1 Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. 2 “Honor your father and your mother”—the first commandment accompanied by a promise—“so that well-being may attend you and you may enjoy longevity upon the earth.” 4 Fathers, do not exasperate your children, but nurture them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

Slaves and Masters under Christ

5 Slaves, obey your earthly masters with reverent awe, and with singleness of heart, as though you were serving Christ. 6 Do not render eye-service merely to ingratiate yourselves with people, but as slaves of Christ, executing the will of God from your inmost being. 7 Serve with goodwill, as if you were serving the Lord rather than human beings. 8 because you know that the Lord will recompense each person for whatever good he has done, whether slave or free. 9 And masters, treat them in the same way. Refrain from threats, since you know that He who is both their Master and yours is in heaven, and with Him there is no partiality.

Spiritual Warfare and the Armor of God

10 Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of His power. 11 Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil. 12 For our struggle is not against human adversaries, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers of this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms. 13 Therefore take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand firm. 14 Stand firm, therefore, with the belt of truth fastened around your waist and the breastplate of righteousness in place. 15 and having shod your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace. 16 In all circumstances take up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the flaming darts of the evil one. 17 And take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. 18 praying in the Spirit at all times with all kinds of prayers and supplications. To that end, keep alert with all perseverance, making supplication for all the saints.

Final Greetings and Benediction

19 Pray also for me, that a word may be given to me when I open my mouth, so that I may boldly proclaim the mystery of the gospel, 20 for which I am an ambassador in chains, that I may declare it boldly, as I ought to speak. 21 Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, will make known to you everything, so that you also may know about me and what I am doing. 22 I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know our circumstances and that he may encourage your hearts. 23 Peace to the brothers and sisters, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 24 Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.
