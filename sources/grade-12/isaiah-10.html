Woe to Those Who Decree Injustice

1 Woe to those who decree injustice, who draft oppressive statutes and commit them to writing. 2 They turn aside the needy from justice and rob the poor of My people of their rights, so that widows become their spoil and they plunder the fatherless.

3 What will you do on the day of reckoning, when destruction comes from afar? To whom will you flee for help, and where will you leave your glory? 4 Nothing will remain but to crouch among the captives or fall among the slain. In all this, His anger is not turned away, and His hand is still stretched out.

Assyria as the Instrument of Divine Wrath

5 Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger; the staff in their hands is My wrath. 6 I will send him against a godless nation and dispatch him against a people under My wrath, to seize the spoil, to plunder the plunder, and to trample them down like mud in the streets. 7 Yet he does not intend this, and his heart does not think so; rather, it is in his heart to destroy and to cut off not a few nations.

8 For he says, "Are not my princes all kings? 9 Is not Calno like Carchemish? Is not Hamath like Arpad? Is not Samaria like Damascus? 10 As my hand has reached the kingdoms of the idols, whose carved images exceeded those of Jerusalem and Samaria, 11 shall I not do to Jerusalem and her idols as I have done to Samaria and her idols?"

12 When the Lord has finished all His work on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will punish the king of Assyria for the arrogant pride of his heart and the haughty look in his eyes.

13 For he says, "By the strength of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom, for I am prudent. I have removed the boundaries of nations and plundered their treasures; like a mighty man I have brought down their inhabitants. 14 My hand has found, as a nest, the wealth of the peoples; and as one gathers forsaken eggs, I have gathered all the earth; and there was none that moved a wing, or opened the mouth, or chirped."

15 Shall the axe boast over him who hews with it, or shall the saw magnify itself against him who wields it? As if a rod should wield him who lifts it, or as if a staff should lift up him who is not wood! 16 Therefore the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will send leanness among his stout ones, and beneath his glory a fire will be kindled like a blazing flame.

17 The Light of Israel will become a fire, and His Holy One a flame; it will burn and devour his thorns and his briers in one day. 18 The glory of his forest and his fruitful field He will consume, both soul and body; and it will be as when a sick man wastes away. 19 The remnant of the trees of his forest will be so few that a child could write them down.

The Remnant of Israel Will Return

20 In that day the remnant of Israel and the survivors of the house of Jacob will no longer rely on him who struck them, but will truly rely on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel. 21 A remnant will return, the remnant of Jacob, to the Mighty God. 22 Though your people, O Israel, be as the sand of the sea, a remnant of them will return. Destruction is decreed, overflowing with righteousness. 23 For the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will carry out the destruction decreed upon the whole earth.

Deliverance for Zion and the Fall of Assyria

24 Therefore thus says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts: "O My people, who dwell in Zion, do not be afraid of the Assyrian. He will strike you with a rod and lift up his staff against you, as in the way of Egypt. 25 For in a very little while My fury will be spent, and My anger will be directed to their destruction. 26 The LORD of Hosts will stir up a scourge against him, as in the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb; and He will raise His staff over the sea, and lift it as in Egypt. 27 In that day his burden will depart from your shoulder, and his yoke from your neck; and the yoke will be broken because the neck has grown too fat for it."

28 He has come to Aiath; he has passed through Migron; at Michmash he stores his supplies. 29 They have gone over the pass; they have taken up their lodging at Geba. Ramah trembles; Gibeah of Saul has fled. 30 Lift up your voice, O daughter of Gallim! Give ear, O Laishah! O poor Anathoth! 31 Madmenah is in flight; the inhabitants of Gebim take to flight. 32 This very day he will halt at Nob; he will shake his fist at the mountain of the house of Zion, at the hill of Jerusalem.

33 Behold, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will lop off the boughs with terror; and the tall ones will be hewn down, and the lofty will be brought low. 34 He will cut down the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon will fall by the Mighty One.
