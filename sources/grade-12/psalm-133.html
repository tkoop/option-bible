The Blessing of Unity Among Brothers

1 Behold, how good and how pleasant it is when brothers dwell together in unity! 2 It is like precious oil poured on the head, running down on the beard—Aaron’s beard—flowing even to the collar of his robes. 3 It is like the dew of Hermon descending upon the mountains of Zion; for there the LORD has bestowed the blessing of life forevermore.
