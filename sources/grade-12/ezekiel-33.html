The Watchman’s Duty

1 The word of the the LORD came to me: 2 “Son of man, speak to your people and say to them: When I bring the sword against a land, and its people appoint one of their own as watchman, 3 and he sees the sword coming against the land and blows the trumpet to warn the people—

4 “if anyone hears the trumpet yet disregards the warning, and the sword comes and carries him off, his blood is on his own head. 5 He heard the trumpet but failed to heed the warning; his blood is upon him. Had he heeded the warning, he would have preserved his life. 6 But if the watchman sees the sword approaching and does not sound the trumpet, so that the people remain unwarned, and the sword comes and claims one of them, that individual is taken away in his guilt, yet I will hold the watchman accountable for his blood.

7 “So you, son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from My mouth, you shall give them warning from Me. 8 If I say to the wicked, ‘You will surely die,’ and you do not speak to warn the wicked to turn from his way, that wicked person will die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood at your hand. 9 But if you warn the wicked to turn from his way, and he does not turn, he will die in his iniquity; yet you will have delivered your soul.”

God’s Call to Repentance

10 “And you, son of man, say to the house of Israel, ‘This is what you have said: “Our transgressions and our sins weigh upon us, and we are wasting away because of them. How, then, can we live?”’ 11 Say to them, ‘As I live, declares the Lord the LORD, I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked; rather, I desire that the wicked turn from their ways and live. Turn back, turn back from your evil ways! Why will you die, O house of Israel?’”

12 “And you, son of man, say to the sons of your people, ‘The righteousness of the righteous will not deliver him when he sins, nor will the wickedness of the wicked cause him to stumble when he turns from it; moreover, the righteous will not be able to live by his righteousness when he sins. 13 Though I promise the righteous that he will surely live, yet if he relies upon his righteousness and commits injustice, none of his righteous deeds will be remembered; he will die because of the injustice he has committed.’”

14 “Again, though I declare to the wicked, ‘You will surely die,’ yet if he turns from his sin and does what is just and right— 15 if the wicked restores the pledge, returns what he has stolen, and walks in the statutes of life without committing injustice, he will surely live; he will not die. 16 None of the sins he has committed will be remembered against him. Because he has done what is just and right, he will surely live.’”

17 “Yet the sons of your people say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just,’ when in fact it is their own way that is not just. 18 When the righteous turns from his righteousness and commits injustice, he will die for it. 19 And when the wicked turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will live by doing so. 20 Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ O house of Israel, I will judge each of you according to your ways.”

News of Jerusalem’s Fall and Judgment on the Survivors

21 In the twelfth year of our exile, on the fifth day of the tenth month, a fugitive from Jerusalem came to me and reported, “The city has been struck down.” 22 Now the hand of the the LORD had been upon me the evening before the fugitive arrived, and He had opened my mouth by the time the man reached me in the morning; thus my mouth was opened, and I was no longer mute.

23 The word of the the LORD came to me: 24 “Son of man, the inhabitants of these ruins in the land of Israel are saying, ‘Abraham was only one man, yet he took possession of the land; but we are many, and the land has been given to us as a possession.’ 25 Therefore say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: You consume meat with its blood still in it, lift up your eyes to your idols, and shed blood; shall you then possess the land? 26 You rely upon your sword, you commit abominations, and each of you defiles his neighbor’s wife; shall you then possess the land?’

27 “Say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: As I live, surely those who are in the ruins will fall by the sword; those in the open field I will give to the beasts to be devoured; and those in strongholds and caves will die of plague. 28 I will make the land a desolation and a waste, and its proud strength will come to an end; the mountains of Israel will be desolate, so that no one passes through. 29 Then they will know that I am the the LORD, when I make the land a desolation and a waste because of all the abominations they have committed.’”

The People Hear but Do Not Obey

30 “As for you, son of man, your people are talking about you beside the walls and at the doors of their houses, saying to one another, ‘Come and hear the word that comes from the the LORD.’ 31 They come to you as people come, and they sit before you and hear your words, but they will not act upon them; for with their mouths they express devotion, yet their hearts pursue dishonest gain.

32 “Indeed, to them you are like one who sings love songs with a beautiful voice and plays an instrument skillfully, for they hear your words, but they will not act upon them. 33 When this comes to pass—and it will come to pass—then they will know that a prophet has been among them.”
