The Inheritance of Simeon

1 The second lot fell to the clans of the tribe of Simeon, and their inheritance lay within the territory of Judah.

2 Their inheritance included Beersheba (or Sheba), Moladah, 3 Hazar-shual, Balah, Ezem, 4 Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah, 5 Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susah, 6 Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen—thirteen cities with their villages. 7 They also received Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan—four cities with their villages— 8 along with all the villages surrounding these cities as far as Baalath-beer and Ramah of the Negev. This was the inheritance of the tribe of Simeon according to its clans.

9 The inheritance of Simeon was taken from Judah's portion, because Judah's allotment was too large for them; therefore Simeon received an inheritance within Judah's inheritance.

The Inheritances of Zebulun and Issachar

10 The third lot fell to the clans of Zebulun, and the boundary of their inheritance reached as far as Sarid. 11 Their boundary ascended westward to Maralah and touched Dabbasheth, then reached the brook east of Jokneam. 12 From Sarid it turned eastward toward the sunrise to the border of Chisloth-tabor, proceeded to Daberath, and ascended to Japhia. 13 From there it passed eastward to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, went out to Rimmon, and turned toward Neah. 14 The northern boundary turned at Hannathon and ended at the Valley of Iphtah-el.

15 The inheritance included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem—twelve cities with their villages. 16 This was the inheritance of the tribe of Zebulun according to its clans—these cities and their villages.

17 The fourth lot fell to the clans of Issachar. 18 Their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem, 19 Hapharaim, Shion, and Anaharath, 20 Rabbith, Kishion, and Abez, 21 Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 22 The boundary touched Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, and ended at the Jordan—sixteen cities with their villages. 23 This was the inheritance of the tribe of Issachar according to its clans—these cities and their villages.

The Inheritances of Asher and Naphtali

24 The fifth lot fell to the clans of Asher. 25 Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph, 26 Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal; westward it touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath. 27 It turned eastward to Beth-dagon, touched Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el on the north, then Beth-emek and Neiel, and went out to Cabul on the left. 28 It included Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as Great Sidon. 29 The boundary turned to Ramah and the fortified city of Tyre, then turned to Hosah and ended at the sea in the region of Achzib. 30 The inheritance also included Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob—twenty-two cities with their villages. 31 This was the inheritance of the tribe of Asher according to its clans—these cities and their villages.

32 The sixth lot fell to the clans of Naphtali. 33 Their boundary ran from Heleph, from the oak at Zaanannim, through Adami-nekeb and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum, and ended at the Jordan. 34 The boundary turned westward to Aznoth-tabor, went out to Hukkok, touched Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan on the east. 35 The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth. 36 It also included Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor, 37 Kedesh, Edrei, and En-hazor, 38 Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh—nineteen cities with their villages. 39 This was the inheritance of the tribe of Naphtali according to its clans—these cities and their villages.

The Inheritance of Dan and Joshua

40 The seventh lot fell to the clans of Dan. 41 Their territory included Zorah, Eshtaol, and Ir-shemesh, 42 Shaalabbin, Aijalon, and Ithlah, 43 Elon, Timnah, and Ekron, 44 Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath, 45 Jehud, Bene-berak, and Gath-rimmon, 46 Me-jarkon, Rakkon, and the territory opposite Joppa. 47 The territory allotted to Dan was too small for them, so the Danites went up and fought against Leshem, captured it, struck it with the sword, took possession of it, settled in it, and renamed Leshem Dan after their ancestor Dan. 48 This was the inheritance of the tribe of Dan according to its clans—these cities and their villages.

49 When Israel finished allotting the land by its boundaries, they gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them. 50 At the LORD's command they gave him the city he requested—Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim—and he rebuilt the city and settled in it. 51 These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of Israel distributed by lot at Shiloh before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land.
