The LORD Commands the Twelve Staffs

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Speak to the Israelites and obtain from them twelve staffs, one from the leader of each ancestral house; write each man’s name on his staff. 3 Write Aaron’s name on the staff of Levi, for there is one staff for the head of each ancestral house. 4 Place them in the Tent of Meeting before the Testimony, where I meet with you. 5 The staff of the man I choose will sprout, and I will rid Myself of the Israelites’ complaints that they are making against you.”

6 So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each of their leaders gave him a staff—one for each leader of their ancestral houses—twelve staffs in all. Aaron’s staff was among them. 7 Moses placed the staffs before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony.

Aaron’s Staff Blossoms

8 The next day Moses entered the Tent of the Testimony and saw that Aaron’s staff for the house of Levi had sprouted, putting forth buds, blossoming, and bearing ripe almonds. 9 Then Moses brought all the staffs out from before the LORD to all the Israelites. They looked at them, and each man took his own staff.

10 The LORD said to Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back before the Testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebellious, so that their complaints against Me may cease and they will not die.”

11 Moses did just as the LORD had commanded him.

The Israelites’ Despair

12 Then the Israelites said to Moses, “Behold, we are perishing! We are lost; we are all lost! 13 Anyone who approaches the tabernacle of the LORD will die. Are we all going to perish?”
