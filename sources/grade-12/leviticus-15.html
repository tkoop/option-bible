1 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

Laws Concerning a Man's Discharge

2 " 'Speak to the Israelites and say to them: Whenever a man has a bodily discharge, that discharge renders him ceremonially unclean. 3 This is the nature of his uncleanness with respect to the discharge: whether his body secretes it or retains it, he is unclean. 4 Every bed on which the man with the discharge lies shall be unclean, and every article on which he sits shall be unclean.

5 " 'Anyone who touches his bed must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain ceremonially unclean until evening. 6 Whoever sits on any object on which the man with the discharge has sat must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 7 Whoever touches the flesh of the man with the discharge must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 8 If the man with the discharge spits upon a person who is clean, that person must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening.

9 " 'Every saddle on which the man with the discharge rides shall be unclean. 10 Anyone who touches anything beneath him shall be unclean until evening, and whoever carries such articles must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 11 If the man with the discharge touches another person without first having rinsed his hands in water, the person touched must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 12 Any clay vessel touched by the man with the discharge must be broken, and any wooden article must be rinsed with water.

13 " 'When the man with the discharge has been cleansed of his discharge, he must count seven days for his purification, launder his garments, and bathe his body in fresh water; then he shall be clean. 14 On the eighth day he must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest. 15 The priest shall sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, and in this way he shall make atonement for him before the LORD because of his discharge.

Purification After Seminal Emission

16 " 'When a man has an emission of semen, he must bathe his entire body in water and remain unclean until evening. 17 Any garment or leather on which there is an emission of semen must be washed with water and shall remain unclean until evening. 18 If a man lies with a woman and an emission of semen occurs, both must bathe in water and remain unclean until evening.

Regulations for a Woman's Discharge

19 " 'Whenever a woman has a discharge of blood from her body, she shall be ceremonially unclean for seven days, and anyone who touches her shall remain unclean until evening. 20 Anything on which she lies during her menstrual impurity shall be unclean, and anything on which she sits shall be unclean. 21 Anyone who touches her bed must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 22 Anyone who touches anything on which she has sat must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 23 Whether the contact occurs with the bed or with an object on which she was sitting, the person who touches it shall be unclean until evening. 24 If a man lies with her and her menstrual flow touches him, he shall be unclean for seven days, and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

25 " 'If a woman has a discharge of blood for many days outside her menstrual period, or if it continues beyond her regular period, she shall be unclean throughout all the days of her unclean discharge, just as she is during her menstrual period. 26 Any bed on which she lies during the days of her discharge shall be unclean, as shall everything on which she sits. 27 Anyone who touches these things shall be unclean; he must launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 28 When she is cleansed of her discharge, she must count seven days, and afterward she shall be clean. 29 On the eighth day she must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. 30 The priest shall sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, and in this way he shall make atonement for her before the LORD for her unclean discharge.

Separation of Israel from Uncleanness

31 " ' You must keep the Israelites separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die by defiling My tabernacle that is among them. 32 This is the law for a man with a discharge, for one who has an emission of semen and becomes unclean. 33 for a woman in her menstrual period, for anyone with a discharge, whether male or female, and for a man who lies with an unclean woman.'
