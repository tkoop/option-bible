A Call to Worship the LORD

1 Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. 2 Sing to the LORD and bless His name; proclaim His salvation from day to day. 3 Declare His glory among the nations and His wonders among all peoples.

4 For the LORD is great and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods. 5 For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 6 Splendor and majesty stand before Him; strength and beauty fill His sanctuary.

7 Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. 8 Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and enter His courts. 9 Worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness; tremble before Him, all the earth.

10 Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity.”

11 Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea roar and everything that fills it. 12 Let the field exult and all that is in it; then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy 13 before the LORD, for He is coming—for He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in His faithfulness.
