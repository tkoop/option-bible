Bildad Rebukes Job's Reckless Words

“1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: 2 How long will you persist in this torrent of words? Your speech is but a gale of empty rhetoric. 3 Would God subvert justice, or would the Almighty distort righteousness? 4 If your children sinned against Him, He delivered them into the power of their own transgression.

The Testimony of the Ancients

“5 If you would seek God with earnest diligence and plead with the Almighty, 6 if you are pure and upright, He will surely rouse Himself on your behalf and restore your rightful estate. 7 Though your beginnings were humble, your latter days would abound in prosperity. 8 For inquire, I pray, of the former generations, and give heed to what their fathers discovered. 9 For we are but of yesterday and know nothing, because our days upon the earth are but a shadow. 10 Will they not teach you and declare to you, bringing forth words from their inmost understanding?

The Fragility of the Godless

“11 Can papyrus grow without a marsh? Can reeds flourish without water? 12 While yet in bloom and not cut down, they wither before any other vegetation. 13 So are the paths of all who forget God; the hope of the godless man perishes. 14 His confidence proves frail, and his trust is but a spider's web. 15 He leans upon his house, yet it does not stand; he clings to it, yet it does not endure. 16 He is verdant before the sun, and his shoots spread across his garden. 17 His roots entwine a heap of stones; he gazes upon a house of stones. 18 If he is swept away from his place, then it disowns him, saying, ‘I have never seen you.’ 19 Behold, this is the joy of his course; and out of the dust others spring up.

The Vindication of the Blameless

“20 Behold, God will not spurn a blameless man, nor will He uphold the hand of evildoers. 21 He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouts of joy. 22 Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.”
