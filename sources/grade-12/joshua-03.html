Israel Prepares to Cross the Jordan

1 Early in the morning Joshua and all the Israelites set out from Shittim and went to the Jordan, where they camped before crossing over. 2 After three days the officers went through the camp.

3 They commanded the people, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God being carried by the Levitical priests, you must break camp and follow it. 4 But keep a distance of about two thousand cubits between yourselves and the ark; do not approach it, so that you may know the way you are to go, since you have never traveled this road before."

5 Joshua said to the people, "Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you."

6 Then Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant and go on ahead of the people." So they took up the ark of the covenant and went ahead of the people.

The LORD Exalts Joshua

7 The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to exalt you in the sight of all Israel, so that they may know that, just as I was with Moses, so I will be with you."

8 You are to command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, 'When you reach the edge of the Jordan's waters, you must stand still in the Jordan.'"

Joshua's Charge to Israel

9 Then Joshua said to the Israelites, "Come closer and listen to the words of the LORD your God. 10 By this you will know that the living God is among you and that He will surely drive out before you the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites. 11 Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing over ahead of you into the Jordan. 12 Now choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one from each tribe. 13 When the soles of the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the LORD—the Lord of all the earth—touch the waters of the Jordan, the waters flowing down from above will be cut off and stand up in a heap."

The Jordan Is Divided

14 So when the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant ahead of them, 15 the Jordan was overflowing all its banks throughout the harvest season. But as soon as the priests carrying the ark reached the Jordan and their feet touched the water's edge, 16 the waters flowing down from above stood still and rose up in a heap a great distance away at Adam, the city beside Zarethan, while the waters flowing into the Sea of the Arabah—the Salt Sea—were completely cut off. So the people crossed over opposite Jericho. 17 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firmly on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, and all Israel passed over on dry ground until the entire nation had finished crossing the Jordan.
