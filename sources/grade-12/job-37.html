Elihu Trembles at God's Thunder and Lightning

1 At this my heart pounds and leaps from its place. 2 Listen carefully to the thunder of His voice and the rumbling that comes from His mouth. 3 He unleashes it beneath the whole sky, and His lightning flashes to the ends of the earth. 4 After it His voice roars; He thunders with His majestic voice. He does not hold back the lightning when His voice is heard. 5 God thunders wondrously with His voice; He does great things we cannot comprehend.

God Commands the Snow, Rain, and Storm

6 For He says to the snow, "Fall on the earth," and to the rain shower, "Be a mighty downpour." 7 He seals up the hand of every man, so that all men may know His work. 8 Then the beasts take cover in their lairs and remain in their dens. 9 The tempest comes from its chamber, and the cold from the driving winds. 10 By the breath of God ice is formed, and the broad waters are frozen solid. 11 He also loads the clouds with moisture and scatters His lightning through the clouds. 12 And they swirl about, turning round and round at His direction, accomplishing whatever He commands them over the face of the whole earth. 13 Whether for punishment, or for His land, or for mercy, He makes it happen.

Job Is Challenged to Understand God's Wonders

14 Listen to this, Job; stand still and consider the wondrous works of God. 15 Do you know how God directs them, making the lightning flash in His cloud? 16 Do you know the balancing of the clouds, the wondrous works of Him who is perfect in knowledge— 17 you whose garments grow hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? 18 Can you, with Him, spread out the skies, hard as a mirror of bronze? 19 Teach us what we should say to Him; we cannot draw up our case because of darkness. 20 Should it be reported to Him that I would speak? If a man speaks, surely he would be swallowed up. 21 Now no one can look at the sun when it is bright in the skies, after the wind has swept through and cleared them.

The Unsearchable Majesty of the Almighty

22 Out of the north comes golden splendor; God is clothed with awesome majesty. 23 The Almighty—we cannot find Him; He is great in power and justice, and abundant righteousness He does not oppress. 24 Therefore men fear Him; He does not regard any who are wise in their own heart.
