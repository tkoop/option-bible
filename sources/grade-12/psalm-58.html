The Wicked Condemned and the Righteous Vindicated

For the choirmaster: to the tune "Do Not Destroy." A miktam of David.

1 Do you indeed speak justice, O rulers? Do you judge fairly, O sons of men? 2 No, in your hearts you devise injustice; your hands mete out violence in the land.

3 The wicked are estranged from the womb; those who speak lies go astray from birth.

4 Their venom is like a serpent's venom, like a deaf adder that stops its ear, 5 so that it cannot hear the voice of charmers or of the skillful enchanter.

6 O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O the LORD, tear out the fangs of the young lions. 7 May they vanish like water that runs off; when He bends His bow, may His arrows be blunted.

8 Like a slug that melts as it crawls, like a stillborn child that never sees the sun.

9 Before your pots can feel the heat of the thorns—whether green or dry—He will sweep them away in a whirlwind.

10 The righteous will rejoice when they see vengeance; they will bathe their feet in the blood of the wicked. 11 Then men will say, "Surely the righteous have a reward; surely there is a God who judges on the earth."
