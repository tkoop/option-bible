Psalm 91: Refuge in the Almighty

1 He who dwells in the sanctuary of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. 2 I will say to the LORD, “He is my refuge and my stronghold, my God, in whom I place my trust.”

3 For He will surely rescue you from the fowler's snare and from the deadly pestilence. 4 He will cover you with His feathers, and beneath His wings you will find refuge; His faithfulness is your shield and rampart. 5 You will not dread the terror of night, nor the arrow that flies by day, 6 nor the plague that stalks in darkness, nor the devastation that lays waste at midday. 7 Though a thousand fall at your side and ten thousand at your right hand, it shall not approach you. 8 You will only observe it with your eyes and behold the recompense of the wicked.

9 Because you have made the LORD your dwelling—your refuge, the Most High— 10 no evil shall befall you, nor shall any plague draw near your tent. 11 For He will command His angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways. 12 They will bear you up in their hands, lest you strike your foot against a stone. 13 You will tread upon the lion and the cobra; You will trample the young lion and the serpent.

14 “Because he loves Me, I will deliver him; because he knows My name, I will protect him. 15 When he calls upon Me, I will answer him; I will be with him in distress; I will rescue him and honor him. 16 With long life I will satisfy him and show him My salvation.”
