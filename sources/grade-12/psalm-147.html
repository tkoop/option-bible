Praise for the LORD's Restoration and Care

1 Praise the LORD! It is good to sing praises to our God; how pleasant and fitting it is to praise Him! 2 The LORD builds up Jerusalem; He gathers the exiles of Israel. 3 He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. 4 He counts the number of the stars and calls them all by name. 5 Great is our Lord and mighty in power; His understanding is beyond measure. 6 The LORD sustains the humble and casts the wicked down to the ground.

7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the harp. 8 He covers the sky with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains. 9 He provides food for the beasts and for the young ravens when they call. 10 He takes no pleasure in the strength of a horse, nor does He delight in a man's physical strength. 11 The LORD delights in those who fear Him, in those who hope in His unfailing love.

12 Praise the LORD, Jerusalem; praise your God, Zion. 13 He strengthens the bars of your gates and blesses your people within you. 14 He grants peace within your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat. 15 He sends His command to the earth; His word runs swiftly. 16 He gives snow like wool and scatters frost like ashes. 17 He hurls down His hail like pebbles. Who can withstand His icy blast? 18 He sends His word and melts them; He stirs up His wind, and the waters flow. 19 He declares His word to Jacob, His statutes and judgments to Israel. 20 He has not done this for any other nation; they do not know His laws. Praise the LORD!
