A Reflective Psalm of Asaph

1 Listen attentively, my people, to my instruction; turn your ears toward the words of my mouth. 2 I will open my mouth in a parable; I will utter enigmatic sayings handed down from antiquity, 3 things that we have heard and known, that our fathers have told us. 4 We will not hide them from their children, but we will recount to the succeeding generations the renowned acts of the LORD, His power, and the wonders that He has done.

5 He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed instruction in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to teach to their children, 6 so that the generation to come might know them, even children yet unborn, and that they might arise and declare them to their children, 7 so that they might place their confidence in God and not forget God's deeds, but observe His commandments. 8 They were not to be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation whose heart was not resolute and whose spirit was not faithful toward God.

Israel's Unfaithfulness in the Wilderness

9 The Ephraimites, though equipped with bows, retreated on the day of battle. 10 They did not keep God's covenant, but refused to walk in accordance with His law. 11 They forgot His works and the wonders that He had shown them. 12 In the sight of their fathers He performed wonders in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. 13 He divided the sea and enabled them to pass through it, causing the waters to stand like a wall. 14 By day He led them with a cloud, and throughout the night with a blazing fire. 15 He split rocks in the wilderness and gave them water to drink in abundance, as from the ocean depths. 16 He brought forth streams from the rock and made waters flow down like rivers. 17 Yet they continued to sin against Him, rebelling against the Most High in the wilderness. 18 They tested God in their hearts by demanding the food they craved. 19 They spoke against God, saying, "Can God set a table in the wilderness? 20 He struck the rock so that water gushed out and streams overflowed. Can He also give bread, or provide meat for His people?"

21 Therefore, when the LORD heard, He was filled with wrath; a fire was kindled against Jacob, and His anger rose against Israel, 22 because they did not believe in God and did not trust in His saving power. 23 Yet He commanded the skies above and opened the doors of heaven, 24 and He rained down manna upon them to eat and gave them the grain of heaven. 25 Mortals ate the bread of the mighty; He sent them provisions in abundance. 26 He caused the east wind to blow through the heavens, and by His power He led forth the south wind. 27 He rained meat upon them like dust, winged birds like the sand of the seas. 28 He let them fall in the midst of their camp, all around their dwellings. 29 And they ate and were well filled, for He gave them what they craved. 30 But before they had satisfied their craving, while the food was still in their mouths, 31 the anger of God rose against them, and He struck down the strongest among them and laid low the young men of Israel.

32 In spite of all this, they still sinned; despite His wonders, they did not believe. 33 So He caused their days to vanish like a breath, and their years to end in terror. 34 When He struck them down, they sought Him; they repented and sought God earnestly. 35 They remembered that God was their rock, and that the Most High God was their Redeemer. 36 But they flattered Him with their mouths and lied to Him with their tongues. 37 Their hearts were not steadfast toward Him, and they were not faithful to His covenant. 38 Yet He, being compassionate, atoned for their iniquity and did not destroy them; He restrained His anger often and did not stir up all His wrath. 39 He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and does not return. 40 How often they rebelled against Him in the wilderness and grieved Him in the desert! 41 They tested God again and again and provoked the Holy One of Israel. 42 They did not remember His power or the day when He redeemed them from the foe.

The Plagues and Deliverance from Egypt

43 He performed His signs in Egypt and His marvels in the fields of Zoan. 44 He turned their rivers to blood, so that they could not drink from their streams. 45 He sent among them swarms of flies that devoured them, and frogs that destroyed them. 46 He gave their crops to the destroying locust and the fruit of their labor to the locust. 47 He destroyed their vines with hail and their sycamore figs with frost. 48 He gave their cattle over to the hail and their flocks to thunderbolts. 49 He let loose upon them His burning anger, wrath, indignation, and distress—a company of destroying angels. 50 He made a path for His anger; He did not spare them from death, but gave their lives over to the plague. 51 He struck down every firstborn in Egypt, the firstfruits of their strength in the tents of Ham. 52 Then He led out His people like sheep and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. 53 He led them in safety, so that they were not afraid, but the sea overwhelmed their enemies. 54 And He brought them to His holy land, to the mountain that His right hand had won. 55 He drove out nations before them; He apportioned them a possession and settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

Renewed Apostasy, Judgment, and the Choice of David

56 Yet they tested and rebelled against the Most High God and did not keep His testimonies, 57 but turned back and acted treacherously like their fathers; they proved as treacherous as a warped bow. 58 For they provoked Him to anger with their high places and moved Him to jealousy with their idols. 59 When God heard, He was filled with wrath, and He utterly rejected Israel. 60 He forsook His dwelling at Shiloh, the tent where He dwelt among mankind, 61 and delivered His power to captivity and His glory into the hand of the foe. 62 He gave His people over to the sword and showed wrath against His heritage. 63 Fire devoured their young men, and their maidens had no nuptial song. 64 Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows made no lamentation. 65 Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a warrior roused by wine. 66 And He put His adversaries to rout; He consigned them to everlasting shame. 67 He rejected the tent of Joseph; He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim, 68 but He chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which He loves. 69 He built His sanctuary like the high heavens, like the earth, which He has founded forever. 70 He chose David His servant and took him from the sheepfolds; 71 from following the nursing ewes He brought him to shepherd Jacob His people, Israel His inheritance. 72 With an upright heart he shepherded them and guided them with skillful hand.
