Job's Covenant with His Eyes and His Integrity

1 "I made a solemn pledge to my eyes never to look with lust upon a young woman; how, then, could I have fixed my gaze upon a virgin? 2 For what portion would God allot me from above, and what inheritance would the Almighty grant me from the heights? 3 Is not ruin the lot of the wicked, and disaster the portion of those who practice iniquity? 4 Does He not see my ways and count every step I take?"

5 "If I have walked in falsehood, or if my foot has hurried toward deceit, 6 let God weigh me on honest scales and know my integrity. 7 If my steps have strayed from the path, if my heart has followed the craving of my eyes, or if any stain has clung to my hands, 8 then let me sow while another reaps, and let all that I have planted be uprooted."

9 "If my heart has been seduced by a woman, or if I have lurked at my neighbor's doorway in pursuit of adultery, 10 then let my wife grind grain for another man, and let strangers lie with her. 11 For that would be a heinous crime, an iniquity deserving judgment; 12 it would be a fire consuming down to Abaddon, devouring all my increase to the root."

Job's Justice Toward the Vulnerable

13 "If I have dismissed the complaint of my male or female servant when they contended with me, 14 what could I do when God arises? When He examines me, what answer could I give Him? 15 Did not He who formed me in the womb form him as well? Did not one and the same God fashion us both in the womb?"

16 "If I have withheld from the poor what they needed, or made a widow's eyes grow dim with longing, 17 or eaten my bread alone while the orphan had none of it— 18 though from my youth the orphan grew up beside me as though I were his father, and from my mother's womb I guided the widow— 19 if I have seen anyone perish for lack of clothing, or the needy without a covering, 20 if his loins have not blessed me, or if he was not warmed by the fleece of my sheep, 21 if I have lifted my hand against an orphan because I knew I had allies at the gate, 22 then let my shoulder blade fall from my shoulder and my arm be broken from its socket. 23 For calamity from God was a terror to me, and because of His majesty I could not endure it."

Job's Rejection of Wealth, Idolatry, and Vengeance

24 "If I have made gold my confidence, or called fine gold my security, 25 if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great or because my hand had gained much, 26 if I have looked upon the sun in its brilliance or the moon moving in splendor 27 and been secretly enticed, so that my mouth kissed my hand in homage, 28 that too would be an iniquity deserving judgment, for I would have been false to God above."

29 "If I have rejoiced at the ruin of my enemy, or exulted when misfortune overtook him— 30 though I never let my mouth sin by demanding his life with a curse— 31 if the men of my household could not say, 'Who has ever gone hungry at his table?'— 32 for no stranger had to sleep in the street, since I opened my doors to the traveler— 33 if I have hidden my transgressions like other men, concealing my iniquity in my heart, 34 because I dreaded the multitude and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silent and never stepped outside my door—"

Job's Final Appeal to God

35 "Oh, that someone would hear me! Here is my signature—let the Almighty answer me! Oh, that my accuser had written an indictment! 36 Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and bind it upon myself like a crown; 37 I would give Him an account of every step I have taken and approach Him like a prince."

38 "If my land has cried out against me, or its furrows have wept together, 39 if I have eaten its produce without payment and caused its owners to lose their lives, 40 then let thorns grow in place of wheat and foul weeds in place of barley." Thus the words of Job came to an end.
