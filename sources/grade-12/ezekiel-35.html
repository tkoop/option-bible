Prophecy Against Mount Seir

1 The word of the the LORD came to me: 2 “Son of man, direct your attention to Mount Seir and prophesy against it.”

The LORD's Sentence Against Mount Seir

3 “Thus says the Lord the LORD: I am against you, Mount Seir; I will take decisive action against you and make you a wasteland, utterly desolate. 4 I will reduce your cities to ruins, and you will become a desolation. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.

Judgment for Ancient Hostility

5 “Because you have harbored a perpetual enmity and delivered the Israelites to the sword in their time of calamity, at the time of their final punishment, 6 therefore, as surely as I live, declares the Lord the LORD, I will destine you for bloodshed, and bloodshed will pursue you. Since you did not despise bloodshed, bloodshed will pursue you. 7 I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a waste, and I will remove from it every traveler and resident. 8 I will fill its mountains with its slain; those struck down by the sword will fall across your hills, valleys, and ravines. 9 I will make you an everlasting desolation, and your cities will never be inhabited. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.

Retribution for Arrogant Claims

10 “Because you said, ‘These two nations and these two lands will be mine, and we will possess them,’ although the the LORD was there, 11 therefore, as surely as I live, declares the Lord the LORD, I will deal with you according to the anger and jealousy you displayed in your hatred toward them, and I will make Myself known among them when I judge you. 12 Then you will know that I, the the LORD, have heard all the contemptuous words you spoke against the mountains of Israel when you said, ‘They are desolate; they have been given to us to devour.’ 13 You vaunted yourselves against Me with your mouths and multiplied your words against Me; I have heard them.

Desolation in the Day of Rejoicing

14 “Thus says the Lord the LORD: When the whole earth rejoices, I will make you a desolation. 15 As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it was desolate, so I will do to you. You will be a desolation, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it. Then they will know that I am the the LORD.”
