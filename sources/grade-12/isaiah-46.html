Babylon’s Idols Collapse

1 Bel has bowed down; Nebo has stooped. Their idols are borne by beasts and cattle. Your carried things are burdens, a heavy load for the weary. 2 They collapsed and bowed down together; they could not rescue the burden, and their own lives went into captivity.

The LORD Carries His People

3 Listen to Me, house of Jacob, and all you who remain of the house of Israel—you who have been borne by Me from the womb and carried from birth. 4 Even to old age I am He; even to gray hair I will carry you. I have made you, and I will bear you; I will carry and rescue you.

The Folly of Idolatry

5 To whom will you liken Me, or make Me equal? With whom will you compare Me, that we should be alike? 6 They pour gold from the purse and weigh silver on the scale; they hire a goldsmith to make it into a god. Then they bow down and worship it. 7 They lift it onto their shoulders and carry it; they set it in its place, and it stands there. It does not move from its spot. If someone cries out to it, it does not answer or save him from his distress.

The LORD Alone Declares the Future

8 Remember this, and take courage; bring it to mind again, you transgressors. 9 Remember the former things of old, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like Me. 10 I declare the end from the beginning and from ancient times what is still to come. I say, “My counsel will stand, and I will accomplish all My purpose.” 11 I call a bird of prey from the east, a man to carry out My purpose from a far country. Yes, I have spoken; yes, I will bring it to pass. I have planned it; yes, I will do it.

Salvation Near for Israel

12 Listen to Me, you stubborn of heart, you who are far from righteousness. 13 I have brought My righteousness near; it is not far off, and My salvation will not delay. I will give salvation in Zion to Israel, My glory.
