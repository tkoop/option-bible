God Presides Over the Divine Assembly

1 God presides in the divine assembly; He judges among the gods. 2 “How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah.

A Mandate to Defend the Vulnerable

3 Defend the weak and the fatherless; vindicate the afflicted and the needy. 4 Rescue the weak and needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked.

5 They know nothing; they understand nothing. They walk in darkness, and all the foundations of the earth are shaken.

The Mortality of Those Called Gods

6 I said, “You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High. 7 Yet you will die like mortals; you will fall like any prince.”

A Prayer for Universal Justice

8 Arise, O God, and judge the earth, for You will inherit all the nations.
