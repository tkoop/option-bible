Job Remembers the Days of Divine Favor

1 Job again took up his discourse and said: 2 “If only I were as in months gone by, in the days when God watched over me— 3 when His lamp shone above my head, and by His light I walked through darkness; 4 when I was in my prime, and God’s intimate friendship rested upon my tent; 5 when the Almighty was still with me, and my children surrounded me; 6 when prosperity bathed my steps, and abundance poured out for me like oil from the rock.

His Standing Among the People

7 When I went out to the city gate and took my seat in the square, 8 the young men saw me and stepped aside, while the aged rose to their feet. 9 Princes refrained from speaking and laid their hands over their mouths; 10 the voices of nobles fell silent, and their tongues clung to their palates. 11 For the ear that heard me called me blessed, and the eye that saw me bore witness to me.

His Care for the Vulnerable

12 Because I delivered the poor man who cried for help, and the fatherless who had no advocate; 13 the blessing of the perishing came upon me, and I made the widow’s heart sing for joy. 14 I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; my justice was my robe and my turban. 15 I was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame. 16 I was a father to the needy, and I investigated cases I did not know. 17 I broke the fangs of the wicked and snatched the prey from his teeth.

His Confidence in a Lasting Future

18 Then I thought, “I would die in my nest, with my days as numerous as the sand. 19 My roots reached to the waters, and dew rested upon my branches. 20 My glory stayed fresh within me, and my bow was renewed in my hand.” 21 Men listened to me and waited; they kept silent for my counsel. 22 After I spoke, they had nothing more to say, and my words settled upon them. 23 They waited for me as for rain, and opened their mouths wide as for the latter rain. 24 I smiled at them when they had no confidence, and they did not diminish the light of my countenance. 25 I chose their way and sat as chief; I dwelt like a king among his troops, like one who comforts mourners.
