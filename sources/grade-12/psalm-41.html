A Prayer for Healing, Vindication, and Deliverance from Treachery

1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. Blessed is the one who considers the poor! In the day of trouble, the LORD delivers him. 2 The LORD protects him and keeps him alive; he is counted blessed in the land, and You do not surrender him to the desire of his enemies. 3 The LORD sustains him upon his sickbed; You restore him from every illness.

4 As for me, I said, "O the LORD, be gracious to me; heal my soul, for I have sinned against You!"

5 My enemies speak evil concerning me: "When will he die, and his name perish?" 6 If one comes to see me, he speaks falsehood; he gathers iniquity within his heart, and when he departs, he proclaims it abroad. 7 All who hate me whisper together against me; they devise harm for me.

8 "A deadly thing is poured out upon him," they say; "he will not rise again from where he lies." 9 Even my close friend, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted his heel against me.

10 But You, the LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, that I may repay them. 11 By this I know that You delight in me: my enemy does not triumph over me. 12 As for me, You uphold me in my integrity and set me in Your presence forever.

13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! Amen and Amen.
