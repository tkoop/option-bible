Praise and Vows in Zion

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A Song. 1 Praise awaits You, O God, in Zion, and to You our vows shall be fulfilled. 2 You who hear prayer, to You all flesh will come. 3 My iniquities have overwhelmed me, yet You atone for our transgressions. 4 Blessed is the one You choose and bring near to dwell in Your courts. We shall be satisfied with the goodness of Your house, the holiness of Your temple.

God's Awesome Deeds and Sovereign Power

5 You answer us with awesome deeds in righteousness, O God of our salvation, You who are the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. 6 You formed the mountains by Your power, girded with strength. 7 You stilled the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of the nations. 8 Thus the inhabitants of the ends of the earth fear Your signs; You make the dawn and the sunset shout for joy.

God's Abundant Provision for the Earth

9 You visit the earth and water it; You greatly enrich it. The river of God is full of water; You prepare their grain, for so You have prepared it. 10 You drench its furrows and level its ridges; You soften it with showers and bless its growth. 11 You crown the year with Your goodness, and Your paths overflow with abundance. 12 The pastures of the wilderness overflow, and the hills gird themselves with joy. 13 The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are decked with grain; they shout and even sing.
