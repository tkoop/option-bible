The Bride's Beauty Extolled

1 "How beautiful your feet are in sandals, daughter of a noble! The curves of your hips are like jewels, fashioned by a master craftsman. 2 Your navel is a rounded goblet that never lacks mixed wine; your belly is a heap of wheat encircled by lilies. 3 Your breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle. 4 Your neck is like an ivory tower; your eyes are the pools of Heshbon beside the gate of Bath-rabbim; your nose is like the tower of Lebanon that looks toward Damascus. 5 Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel, and the tresses of your head are like purple; a king is held captive in its tresses. 6 How beautiful and pleasant you are, my love, with your delights! 7 Your stature resembles a palm tree, and your breasts are like its clusters. 8 I said, 'I will climb the palm tree and take hold of its fruit.' May your breasts be like clusters of the vine, and the fragrance of your breath be like apples; 9 and may your mouth be like the finest wine, flowing smoothly for my beloved and gliding over the lips of those who sleep."

The Bride's Invitation

10 "I am my beloved's, and his desire is for me. 11 Come, my beloved, let us go out into the fields and lodge in the villages. 12 Let us rise early and go to the vineyards; let us see whether the vine has budded, whether the blossom has opened, and whether the pomegranates are in bloom—there I will give you my love. 13 The mandrakes give forth their fragrance, and at our doors are all choice fruits, new and old, which I have treasured up for you, my beloved."
