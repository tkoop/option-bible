The LORD's Command to Ezekiel

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying: 2 “Son of man, set your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them.”

Judgment on Israel's Idolatrous High Places

3 “Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord the LORD! Thus says the Lord the LORD to the mountains and hills, to the ravines and valleys: I am about to bring a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places. 4 Your altars will be desolate, your incense altars will be smashed, and I will cast down your slain before your idols. 5 I will lay the corpses of the Israelites before their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars. 6 In all your dwellings the cities will be laid waste, and the high places will be desolate, so that your altars may be ruined and desolate, your idols broken and brought to nothing, your incense altars cut down, and your works blotted out. 7 The slain will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the LORD.”

A Remnant Will Remember the LORD

8 “Yet I will leave a remnant, so that you will have some who escape the sword among the nations when you are scattered among the countries. 9 Then those of you who escape will remember Me among the nations where they are carried captive, because I was crushed by their adulterous heart that turned away from Me, and by their eyes that lusted after their idols; and they will loathe themselves for the evils they have committed, for all their abominations. 10 They will know that I am the LORD; I have not spoken in vain when I said I would bring this evil upon them.”

The Coming Devastation and Famine

11 Thus says the Lord the LORD: “Clap your hand and stamp your foot, and say, ‘Alas!’ because of all the evil abominations of the house of Israel, for they will fall by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence. 12 He who is far away will die by pestilence, he who is near will fall by the sword, and he who remains and is besieged will die by famine; and I will spend My fury upon them. 13 Then you will know that I am the LORD, when their slain lie among their idols around their altars, on every high hill and on all the mountaintops, under every green tree and under every leafy oak—wherever they offered a pleasing aroma to all their idols. 14 I will stretch out My hand against them and make the land a desolation and a waste, from the wilderness to Diblah; and they will know that I am the LORD.”
