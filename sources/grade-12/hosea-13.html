Ephraim's Rise and Idolatry

1 When Ephraim spoke, trembling seized Israel, and he was exalted among his people. Yet he became guilty through Baal and died. 2 Now they sin more and more; they make molten images for themselves, idols skillfully crafted from their silver, all of them the work of craftsmen. It is said of them, "Men who offer sacrifices worship calf idols." 3 Therefore they will be like the morning cloud, like dew that vanishes, like chaff swept from the threshing floor, and like smoke rising from a chimney.

The LORD's Deliverance and Judgment

4 "But I am the LORD your God from the land of Egypt. You know no God but Me, and there is no Savior besides Me. 5 I knew you in the wilderness, in the land of drought. 6 When they were pastured, they became full; they were satisfied, and their heart grew proud; therefore they forgot Me. 7 So I will be like a lion to them, like a leopard lurking along the path. 8 I will confront them like a bear robbed of her cubs; I will tear open their chests. There I will devour them like a lion; wild beasts will tear them apart.

Israel's Self-Ruin

9 "You are destroyed, O Israel, because you are against Me, against your Helper. 10 Where is your king now, that he may save you in all your cities? And where are your judges, whom you said, 'Give me a king and princes'? 11 I gave you a king in My anger, and I took him away in My wrath. 12 The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is stored away. 13 Labor pains come upon him, yet he is not a wise son, because when the time comes to be born, he does not come out of the womb.

Redemption and Final Judgment

14 "I will ransom them from the power of Sheol; I will redeem them from death. O Death, I will be your plague; O Sheol, I will be your destruction. Compassion is hidden from My eyes.

15 Though he flourishes among his brothers, an east wind will come—the wind of the LORD rising from the wilderness. His spring will dry up, and his fountain will be parched. It will plunder his treasury of every precious article. 16 Samaria will be held guilty, because she rebelled against her God. They will fall by the sword; their little ones will be dashed to pieces, and their pregnant women will be ripped open.
