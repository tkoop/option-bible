The LORD Is My Shepherd

1 The LORD is my shepherd; I lack nothing. 2 He lets me rest in verdant meadows and leads me beside tranquil waters. 3 He revives my spirit and guides me along righteous paths for the sake of His name.

4 Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff comfort me. 5 You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. 6 Surely goodness and faithful love will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the the LORD forever.
