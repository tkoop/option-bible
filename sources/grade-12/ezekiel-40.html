Ezekiel's Vision of the Restored Temple

1 In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth day of the month—in the fourteenth year after the city was struck down—on that very day the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He brought me there. 2 In visions of God He brought me to the land of Israel and set me down on a very high mountain, on whose southern slope stood a structure resembling a city. 3 When He brought me there, I saw a man whose appearance was like bronze; he was standing in the gateway with a linen cord and a measuring reed in his hand. 4 The man said to me, "Son of man, look with your eyes, hear with your ears, and set your heart on all that I show you, for you have been brought here so that I may show it to you. Declare to the house of Israel all that you see."

The Outer Court and Its Gates

5 I saw a wall surrounding the outside of the temple area. The measuring reed in the man's hand was six cubits long—each cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth—and he measured the wall's thickness as one reed and its height as one reed. 6 He then entered the gateway facing east, ascended its steps, and measured the threshold of the gate as one reed wide. 7 Each gate chamber measured one reed in length and one reed in width, with five cubits between adjacent chambers; the inner threshold of the gate beside the portico facing inward measured one reed. 8 He measured the portico of the gateway toward the temple at one reed. 9 The portico of the gateway measured eight cubits, and its jambs two cubits; the portico of the gateway faced the temple. 10 The east gate had three gate chambers on each side, all with identical measurements, and the gateposts on either side shared those same measurements. 11 He measured the gateway's entrance at ten cubits wide and the gateway itself at thirteen cubits long. 12 A barrier one cubit wide stood in front of the gate chambers on each side, and each gate chamber was six cubits square. 13 He measured the gateway from the roof of one gate chamber to the roof of the opposite chamber: its width was twenty-five cubits, from doorway to doorway. 14 He measured the gateposts at sixty cubits, and the gateway extended around to the gatepost of the courtyard. 15 From the front of the gate at the entrance to the front of the inner portico of the gate was fifty cubits. 16 The gate chambers and their posts had beveled windows all around within the gateway, and windows encircled the inner portico; palm trees were carved on each side. 17 He then brought me into the outer court, where chambers and a paved surface extended all around the court; thirty chambers faced the pavement. 18 The pavement lay beside the gates, corresponding in length to the gates; this was the lower pavement. 19 He measured the width from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner court: one hundred cubits on the east and one hundred cubits on the north. 20 He also measured the length and width of the outer court's gate that faced north. 21 Its three gate chambers on each side, its posts, and its portico had the same measurements as the first gate: the gate was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 22 Its windows, portico, and palm trees had the same measurements as those of the gate facing east; seven steps led up to it, with its portico opposite them. 23 The inner court also had a gate facing the north gate, just as there was toward the east, and he measured the distance from gate to gate at one hundred cubits. 24 He led me to the south side, where a gate faced south, and he measured its posts and portico; they had the same measurements as the others. 25 Both the gate and its portico had windows all around, like the other windows; the gate was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 26 Seven steps led up to it, with its portico opposite them, and palm trees were carved on its posts, one on each side. 27 The inner court also had a gate facing south, and he measured the distance from gate to gate toward the south at one hundred cubits.

The Inner Court and Its Gates

28 He brought me to the inner court through the south gate and measured the south gate; it had the same measurements as the others. 29 Its chambers, posts, and portico had the same measurements as the others, and both it and its portico had windows all around; the gate was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 30 Porticoes extended all around, twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide. 31 Its portico faced the outer court, palm trees were carved on its posts, and eight steps led up to it. 32 He brought me to the east side of the inner court and measured the gate; it had the same measurements as the others. 33 Its chambers, posts, and portico had the same measurements as the others, and both it and its portico had windows all around; the gate was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 34 Its portico faced the outer court, palm trees were carved on its posts, one on each side, and eight steps led up to it. 35 He brought me to the north gate and measured it; it had the same measurements as the others. 36 Its chambers, posts, and portico had the same measurements as the others, and it had windows all around; the gate was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 37 Its portico faced the outer court, palm trees were carved on its posts, one on each side, and eight steps led up to it.

Sacrificial Furnishings and Priestly Chambers

38 A chamber with a doorway stood by the gateposts of the gates; there they wash the burnt offering. 39 In the portico of the gate were two tables on each side, on which to slaughter the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. 40 Outside, beside the portico at the entrance of the north gate, were two tables on one side and two on the other, on which to slaughter the burnt offering. 41 At the side of the portico were four tables on each side—eight tables in all—on which to slaughter the sacrifices. 42 Four tables of cut stone served for the burnt offering, each one cubit and a half long, one cubit and a half wide, and one cubit high; on them they placed the utensils used to slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice. 43 Hooks, each a handbreadth long, were fastened all around the inside, and the flesh of the offerings was to be placed on the tables. 44 Outside the inner gate were chambers for the singers in the inner court—one beside the north gate facing south, and one beside the south gate facing north. 45 The man said to me, "This chamber facing south is for the priests who keep charge of the temple, 46 and the chamber facing north is for the priests who keep charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, who alone among the Levites may approach the LORD to minister to Him." 47 He measured the court: a perfect square, one hundred cubits long and one hundred cubits wide. The altar stood in front of the temple. 48 He brought me to the portico of the temple and measured the jambs of the portico: five cubits on each side. The width of the gateway was three cubits on each side. 49 The portico was twenty cubits long and eleven cubits wide, with steps by which they ascended it. Pillars stood by the jambs, one on each side.
