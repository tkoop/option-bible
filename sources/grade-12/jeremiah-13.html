The Ruined Linen Belt

1 Thus says the LORD to me, "Go and purchase a linen belt for yourself, fasten it around your waist, and do not wash it in water." 2 So I bought the belt according to the word of the LORD and fastened it around my waist.

3 Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying, 4 "Take the belt that you bought, which is on your waist, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a cleft of the rock." 5 So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, as the LORD had commanded me.

6 After many days the LORD said to me, "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the belt that I commanded you to hide there." 7 So I went to the Euphrates, dug, and took the belt from the place where I had hidden it; and there it was, the belt ruined and utterly useless.

The Pride of Judah

8 Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 9 "Thus says the LORD: 'In this way I will destroy the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem. 10 This evil people, who refuse to hear My words, who walk in the stubbornness of their heart and have gone after other gods to serve them and bow down to them, will be like this belt, which is good for nothing. 11 For as the belt clings to the waist of a man, so I have made the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah cling to Me, declares the LORD, that they might be for Me a people, and a name, and a praise, and a glory; but they would not hear.'"

The Parable of the Wine Jars

12 And you shall say to them this word: "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'Every jar will be filled with wine.'"

And they will say to you, "Do we not indeed know that every jar will be filled with wine?"

13 And you shall say to them, "Thus says the LORD: 'Behold, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land—the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem—with drunkenness. 14 And I will dash them one against another, fathers and sons together, declares the LORD. I will not pity, nor spare, nor have compassion, but will destroy them.'"

Judgment on Judah and Jerusalem

15 "Hear and give ear; do not be proud, for the LORD has spoken." 16 "Give glory to the LORD your God before He brings darkness, and before your feet stumble on the dark mountains, and while you hope for light, He turns it into deathly gloom and makes it deep darkness."

17 But if you will not hear it, my soul will weep in secret because of your pride; my eye will weep bitterly and run down with tears, because the LORD's flock has been taken captive.

18 "Say to the king and to the queen mother, 'Humble yourselves and sit down, for your glorious crown has fallen from your heads. 19 The cities of the Negev will be shut up, with no one to open them; all Judah will be carried into exile, carried completely away.'"

20 "Lift up your eyes and see those who come from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, your beautiful flock? 21 What will you say when He appoints over you those whom you have taught to be your allies at your head? Will not pangs seize you like a woman in labor? 22 And if you say in your heart, 'Why have these things come upon me?' it is because of the greatness of your iniquity. Your skirts are uncovered, your heels are made bare."

23 "Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard its spots? Then neither can you do good—you who are accustomed to doing evil. 24 Therefore I will scatter them like chaff driven by the desert wind. 25 This is your lot, the portion measured out to you from Me, declares the LORD, because you have forgotten Me and trusted in falsehood. 26 I will also uncover your skirts over your face, and your shame will be seen. 27 I have seen your adulteries and your lustful neighings, the shameless lewdness of your prostitution; on the hills and in the fields I have seen your detestable acts. Woe to you, O Jerusalem! How long will you remain unclean?"
