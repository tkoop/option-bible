A Warning Against Many Teachers

1My brothers, not many of you should become teachers, knowing that we who teach will be judged more strictly. 2For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone doesn't stumble in what he says, that person is mature and able to bridle the whole body.

Taming the Tongue

3If we put bits into horses' mouths so that they obey us, we can turn their whole bodies as well. 4Look at ships, too: although they're so large and are driven by fierce winds, they're steered by a very small rudder wherever the pilot's will directs. 5So the tongue is also a small member, yet it boasts of great things. See how a tiny spark can set a great forest ablaze! 6The tongue is a fire, a world of unrighteousness among our members; it stains the whole body, sets the course of life on fire, and is itself set on fire by hell. 7Every kind of beast, bird, reptile, and sea creature can be tamed and has been tamed by mankind, 8but no human being can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison. 9With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse people who have been made in God's likeness. 10From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be. 11Does a spring pour out fresh water and bitter water from the same opening? 12My brothers, can a fig tree produce olives, or a grapevine produce figs? Neither can salt water yield fresh water.

True Wisdom from Above

13Who among you is wise and understanding? Let him show it by his good conduct, by deeds done in the humility of wisdom. 14But if you harbor bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your heart, don't boast and lie against the truth. 15This kind of wisdom doesn't come down from above; it's earthly, unspiritual, and demonic. 16For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there is disorder and every evil practice. 17But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits, impartial and sincere. 18And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace.
