The People's Outcry Against Oppression

1 A great outcry arose among the people and their wives against their fellow Jews.

2 Some said, "We have many sons and daughters, so we must obtain grain in order to eat and survive."

3 Some said, "We are mortgaging our fields, vineyards, and houses to buy grain during the famine."

4 Some said, "We have borrowed money for the king's tax on our fields and vineyards."

5 "Now our very flesh is the same as our brothers' flesh, and our children are like their children; yet we are forcing our sons and daughters into slavery, and some of our daughters have already been enslaved, while we are powerless, because our fields and vineyards belong to others."

Nehemiah Rebukes the Nobles and Officials

6 When I heard their outcry and these words, I was very angry. 7 After careful deliberation, I rebuked the nobles and officials, saying, "You are exacting usury from your own brothers!" Then I convened a great assembly against them.

8 I said to them, "We have, according to our ability, redeemed our Jewish brothers who were sold to the nations. Now would you even sell your brothers, that they might be sold back to us?" They were silent and found nothing to say.

9 Again I said, "What you are doing is not good. Should you not walk in the fear of our God, to avoid the reproach of the nations, our enemies?"

10 I, my brothers, and my servants are also lending them money and grain. Please, let us stop charging this interest. 11 Please restore to them this very day their fields, vineyards, olive groves, and houses, along with the hundredth part of the money, grain, new wine, and oil that you have been exacting from them.

12 Then they said, "We will restore it and require nothing from them; we will do exactly as you say." So I called the priests and made them swear to do according to this promise.

13 I also shook out the fold of my garment and said, "Thus may God shake out every man from his house and from his labor who does not fulfill this promise; even so may he be shaken out and emptied."

And all the assembly said, "Amen!" And they praised the LORD. Then the people did according to this promise.

Nehemiah's Selfless Governance

14 Moreover, from the day King Artaxerxes appointed me to be governor in the land of Judah—from the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of his reign, twelve years—neither I nor my brothers ate the food allotted to the governor.

15 But the former governors who preceded me laid heavy burdens on the people and took from them bread and wine, besides forty shekels of silver; even their servants lorded it over the people. But I did not do so, because of the fear of God.

16 I also devoted myself to the work on this wall, and all my servants were gathered there for the work; we did not acquire any field.

17 Moreover, there were at my table one hundred fifty Jews and officials, besides those who came to us from the nations around us. 18 Now what was prepared for each day was one ox, six choice sheep, and birds; and every ten days an abundance of all kinds of wine was provided. Yet with all this I did not demand the governor's food allowance, because the service was heavy on this people.

19 Remember me, O my God, for good, according to all that I have done for this people.
