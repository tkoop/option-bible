The LORD's Indictment of Israel

1 Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, children of Israel—against the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt: 2 You alone have I known among all the families of the earth; therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

Warnings, Causes, and Prophetic Revelation

3 Do two walk together unless they have agreed to meet? 4 Does a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey? Does a young lion cry out from his den unless he has caught something? 5 Does a bird fall into a snare on the ground unless a trap has been set for it? Does a snare spring up from the earth unless it has caught something? 6 If a trumpet is blown in a city, do the people not tremble? If calamity comes upon a city, has not the LORD done it? 7 Surely the Lord, the LORD, does nothing without revealing His secret to His servants the prophets. 8 The lion has roared; who will not fear? The Lord, the LORD, has spoken; who will not prophesy?

Judgment on Samaria

9 Proclaim over the palaces of Ashdod and over the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, “Assemble on the mountains of Samaria and observe the great tumult within her and the oppressions in her midst.” 10 “They do not know how to do what is right,” declares the LORD—“those who hoard violence and plunder in their palaces.” 11 Therefore thus says the Lord, the LORD: “An enemy will surround the land, strip you of your strength, and plunder your palaces.” 12 Thus says the LORD: “As a shepherd rescues from the mouth of a lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so the children of Israel who dwell in Samaria will be rescued—with only a corner of a bed and a piece of a Damascene couch.”

Sentence Against Bethel and the Great Houses

13 Hear and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord, the LORD, the God of Hosts: 14 “For on the day when I punish Israel for its transgressions, I will punish the altars of Bethel as well; the horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground. 15 I will strike the winter house along with the summer house; the houses of ivory will perish, and many houses will come to an end,” declares the LORD.
