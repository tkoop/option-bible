A Joyful Call to Worship

1 Raise a joyful shout to the the LORD, all the earth. 2 Serve the the LORD with gladness; come before Him with joyful singing.

3 Know that the the LORD—He is God. He made us, and we did not make ourselves; we are His people and the sheep of His pasture.

4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name. 5 For the the LORD is good; His steadfast love endures forever, and His faithfulness extends to every generation.
