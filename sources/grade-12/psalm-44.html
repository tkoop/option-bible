A Remembrance of God's Ancient Deliverance

1 O God, our ears have heard what our fathers recounted to us—the mighty deeds You performed in their days, in those ancient times. 2 With Your own hand You drove out the nations and planted our ancestors; You crushed the peoples and made our forebears flourish. 3 For they did not conquer the land by their own sword, nor did their own arm save them; rather, Your right hand and Your arm, together with the light of Your face, secured their triumph, because You favored them. 4 You are my King, O God; ordain salvation for Jacob! 5 Through You we push down our foes; through Your name we trample those who rise against us. 6 For I do not trust in my bow, and my sword cannot save me. 7 But You have saved us from our foes and shamed those who hate us. 8 In God we boast all day long, and we will give thanks to Your name forever. Selah

A Lament Over Present Defeat and Humiliation

9 Yet You have rejected us and brought us to shame, and You no longer go out with our armies. 10 You turn us back before the foe, and those who hate us plunder us. 11 You hand us over like sheep to be slaughtered and scatter us among the nations. 12 You sell Your people for a pittance, demanding no great price for them. 13 You make us a reproach to our neighbors, a scorn and a derision to those around us. 14 You make us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples. 15 All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face, 16 at the taunt and reproach of the scoffer, before the enemy and the avenger.

A Protest of Covenant Fidelity

17 All this has befallen us, yet we have not forgotten You, and we have not dealt falsely with Your covenant. 18 Our heart has not turned back, nor have our steps strayed from Your path; 19 yet You have shattered us in the haunt of jackals and covered us with the shadow of death. 20 If we had forgotten the name of our God or stretched out our hands to a foreign god, 21 would not God have discovered it? For He knows the secrets of the heart. 22 Yet for Your sake we are killed all day long; we are reckoned as sheep for the slaughter.

A Plea for Divine Redemption

23 Awake! Why do You sleep, O Lord? Rouse Yourself! Do not reject us forever! 24 Why do You hide Your face? Why do You forget our affliction and our oppression? 25 For our soul is bowed down to the dust; our body clings to the ground. 26 Rise up and help us! Redeem us for the sake of Your steadfast love!
