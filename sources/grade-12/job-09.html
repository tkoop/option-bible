Job Replies to Bildad

1 Then Job answered: 2 "Truly I know that it is so, but how can a mortal be righteous before God? 3 If He should choose to contend with him, he could not answer Him one time in a thousand.

God's Unsearchable Power

4 "He is wise in heart and mighty in strength; who has hardened himself against Him and prospered? 5 He moves mountains without their knowing it and overturns them in His anger. 6 He shakes the earth from its place, and its pillars tremble. 7 He commands the sun, and it does not rise; He seals up the stars. 8 He alone stretches out the heavens and treads upon the waves of the sea. 9 He makes the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the hidden constellations of the south. 10 He does great things beyond searching out and wonders beyond number.

Job's Helplessness Before God

11 "If He passes by me, I do not see Him; if He moves on, I do not perceive Him. 12 If He snatches away, who can restrain Him? Who can say to Him, 'What are You doing?' 13 God does not turn back His anger; the allies of Rahab bow beneath Him.

14 "How much less, then, can I answer Him and choose my words before Him? 15 Though I were righteous, I could not answer; I would plead for mercy to my Judge. 16 If I called and He answered me, I would not believe that He was listening to my voice. 17 For He crushes me with a tempest and multiplies my wounds without cause. 18 He will not let me catch my breath, but fills me with bitterness. 19 If it is a matter of strength, behold, He is mighty; if of justice, who can summon Him? 20 If I am righteous, my own mouth would condemn me; though I am blameless, He would declare me perverse. 21 I am blameless, yet I do not know myself; I despise my life. 22 It is all one; therefore I say, 'He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.' 23 When calamity strikes suddenly, He mocks the suffering of the innocent. 24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; He covers the faces of its judges. If it is not He, then who is it?

Job's Lament and Plea

25 "My days are swifter than a runner; they flee away without seeing good. 26 They pass by like papyrus skiffs, like an eagle swooping down on prey. 27 If I say, 'I will forget my complaint; I will change my expression and be cheerful,' 28 I dread all my suffering; I know You will not acquit me. 29 I am guilty; why then should I toil in vain? 30 If I wash myself with snow and cleanse my hands with lye, 31 You will plunge me into the pit, and my own clothes will abhor me. 32 For He is not a man like me, that I should answer Him, that we should come together in judgment. 33 There is no arbiter between us, to lay his hand upon us both. 34 Let Him remove His rod from me, and let not His terror make me afraid. 35 Then I would speak without fear of Him, but that is not how I am."
