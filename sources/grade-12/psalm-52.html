The Boastful Wicked Man and the Flourishing Righteous

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of David. When Doeg the Edomite went to Saul and said, “David has gone to the house of Ahimelech.”

1 Why do you boast of wickedness, O mighty man? God’s loving devotion endures all day long. 2 Your tongue plots ruin, sharp as a razor, O worker of deceit. 3 You love evil rather than good, and falsehood rather than speaking what is right. Selah. 4 You love every word that devours, O treacherous tongue.

5 But God will bring you down forever; He will snatch you up and tear you from your tent; He will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah.

6 The righteous will see and fear, and they will laugh at him. 7 Behold, the man who did not make God his refuge, but trusted in the abundance of his riches and strengthened himself in his wickedness.

8 But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God; I trust in the loving devotion of God forever and ever. 9 I will praise You forever, because You have done it. I will wait on Your name, for it is good, in the presence of Your saints.
