The Supremacy of Love

1 If I speak in the tongues of human beings and of angels, yet lack love, I have become nothing more than a resounding gong or a clanging cymbal. 2 If I possess prophetic power and comprehend every mystery and all knowledge, and if I have faith sufficient to move mountains, yet lack love, I am nothing. 3 If I give away all my possessions, and if I surrender my body so that I may boast, yet lack love, I gain nothing.

The Character of Love

4 Love is patient and kind; love does not envy or boast; it is not arrogant. 5 It does not behave disgracefully; it does not pursue its own interests; it is not easily provoked; it keeps no record of wrongs. 6 It does not rejoice in injustice, but rejoices with the truth. 7 It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, and endures all things.

The Permanence of Love

8 Love never fails. But if there are prophecies, they will be abolished; if there are tongues, they will cease; if there is knowledge, it will be abolished. 9 For we know only in part, and we prophesy only in part; 10 but when perfection comes, what is partial will pass away. 11 When I was a child, I spoke like a child, I thought like a child, and I reasoned like a child; when I became an adult, I put away childish things. 12 For now we see in a mirror, dimly, but then face to face. Now I know only in part, but then I shall know fully, even as I have been fully known. 13 And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love; but the greatest of these is love.
