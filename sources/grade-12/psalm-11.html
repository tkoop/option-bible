Refuge in the LORD Amid Wicked Threats

1 In the LORD I have taken refuge. How can you say to me, “Flee like a bird to your mountain”? 2 For indeed, the wicked bend the bow; they set their arrows on the string to shoot from the darkness at the upright in heart. 3 If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?

4 The LORD is in His holy temple; the LORD’s throne is in heaven. His eyes observe; His eyelids examine the sons of men. 5 The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked, and His soul hates the one who loves violence. 6 Upon the wicked He will rain down fiery coals and sulfur; a scorching wind will be their allotted portion. 7 For the LORD is righteous; He loves righteous deeds. The upright will see His face.
