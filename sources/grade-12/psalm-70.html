An Urgent Prayer for Deliverance

1 Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, the LORD. 2 Let those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; let those who delight in my calamity be turned back and disgraced.

3 Let those who cry, "Aha! Aha!" recoil in disgrace. 4 Let all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; let those who love Your salvation say continually, "May God be exalted!"

5 Yet I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; the LORD, do not delay.
