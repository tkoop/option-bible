Israel's Hidden Sins Laid Bare

1 When I heal Israel, Ephraim's iniquity is exposed, together with Samaria's crimes; for they have practiced deceit, thieves break in, and marauding bands raid the streets. 2 Yet they do not reflect within their hearts that I remember all their wickedness; now their deeds encompass them, and they are ever before My face. 3 They gladden the king with their evil and the princes with their lies.

The Oven of Rebellion

4 They are all adulterers, like an oven heated by a baker, who need not stir the fire from the kneading of the dough until it has leavened. 5 On the day of our king, the princes become inflamed with wine, and he makes common cause with mockers. 6 For they prepare their hearts like an oven while they lie in wait; all night their rage smolders, and in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire. 7 All of them are as hot as an oven, and they devour their judges; all their kings have fallen, and none of them calls upon Me.

Ephraim's Futile Alliances

8 Ephraim mixes himself among the nations; Ephraim has become like a cake left unturned—burned on one side and doughy on the other. 9 Strangers devour his strength, yet he does not know it; gray hair is sprinkled upon him, yet he remains unaware. 10 Israel's pride testifies against him, yet they do not return to the LORD their God, nor seek Him, despite all this. 11 Ephraim is like a senseless dove, devoid of understanding, calling to Egypt and going to Assyria. 12 As they go, I will spread My net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of the sky. I will discipline them as soon as I hear them flocking together.

The LORD's Rejected Redemption

13 Woe to them, for they have strayed from Me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against Me! Though I would redeem them, they speak lies against Me. 14 They do not cry out to Me from their hearts, but they wail upon their beds; they gash themselves for grain and new wine, yet they turn away from Me. 15 Although I trained and strengthened their arms, they plot evil against Me. 16 They turn, but not to the Most High; they are like a bow that fails to shoot true. Their leaders will fall by the sword because of the fury of their tongues. In the land of Egypt they will be mocked.
