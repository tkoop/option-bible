The Righteous Branch from Jesse

1 A shoot shall spring from the stump of Jesse, and a Branch from his roots shall bear fruit. 2 The Spirit of the LORD shall rest upon Him—a Spirit of wisdom and understanding, a Spirit of counsel and might, a Spirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD. 3 His delight shall be in the fear of the LORD; He shall not judge by the sight of His eyes, nor decide by the hearing of His ears.

4 Rather, with righteousness He shall judge the poor, and with equity He shall decide for the meek of the earth. He shall strike the earth with the rod of His mouth, and with the breath of His lips He shall slay the wicked. 5 Righteousness shall be the belt about His waist, and faithfulness the sash about His hips.

Peace in the Messiah's Kingdom

6 The wolf shall dwell with the lamb, the leopard shall lie down with the goat, the calf and the young lion and the fattened calf together, and a little child shall lead them. 7 The cow and the bear shall graze; their young shall lie down together; and the lion shall eat straw like the ox. 8 The nursing child shall play over the hole of the cobra, and the weaned child shall put his hand into the viper's den. 9 They shall not hurt nor destroy in all My holy mountain, for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.

The Gathering of God's Remnant

10 In that day the Root of Jesse shall stand as a banner for the peoples; the nations shall seek Him, and His resting place shall be glorious. 11 On that day the Lord shall extend His hand a second time to recover the remnant of His people from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the coastlands of the sea. 12 He shall raise a banner for the nations and gather the outcasts of Israel; He shall assemble the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

13 The jealousy of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off. Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not harass Ephraim. 14 They shall swoop down upon the Philistines in the west; together they shall plunder the people of the east. They shall lay their hands on Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites shall obey them. 15 The LORD shall dry up the gulf of the Sea of Egypt; with a scorching wind He shall wave His hand over the Euphrates and strike it into seven streams, so that people may cross in sandals. 16 There shall be a highway for the remnant of His people who remain from Assyria, as there was for Israel when they came up from the land of Egypt.
