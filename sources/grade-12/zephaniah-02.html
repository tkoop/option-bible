A Summons to the Shameless Nation

1 Gather yourselves, gather together, O nation without shame. 2 Before the decree is born, before the day passes like chaff, before the fierce anger of the LORD comes upon you, before the Day of the LORD comes upon you. 3 Seek the LORD, all you humble people of the land who have done what His justice requires. Seek righteousness; seek humility. Perhaps you will be sheltered on the day of the LORD's wrath.

Judgment on the Philistines

4 For Gaza will be abandoned, and Ashkelon left a desolation; Ashdod will be emptied by midday, and Ekron will be uprooted. 5 Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast, O nation of the Cherethites! The word of the LORD is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines; and I will destroy you, and no one will remain. 6 The seacoast will become pastureland, with shepherd encampments and sheepfolds. 7 It will belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; there they will pasture and lie down in the evening within the houses of Ashkelon, for the LORD their God will attend to them and restore their fortunes.

Judgment on Moab and Ammon

8 I have heard the taunting of Moab and the reviling of the Ammonites, who have taunted My people and threatened their borders. 9 Therefore, as I live, declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, Moab will become like Sodom and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—a land of weeds and salt pits, a desolation forever. The remnant of My people will plunder them, and the survivors of My nation will inherit them. 10 This is what they will receive in return for their pride, for taunting and exalting themselves against the people of the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD's Triumph over the Nations

11 The LORD will be fearsome to them when He reduces to nothing all the gods of the earth; then every nation will bow down to Him, each in its own place, even all the coastlands of the nations. 12 You too, O Cushites, will be slain by My sword. 13 He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy Assyria, and He will make Nineveh a desolation, dry as the wilderness. 14 Flocks will lie down in her midst, every beast of the nations; both the desert owl and the screech owl will lodge in her pillars. Their calls will echo through the windows; desolation will be on the threshold, for He has laid bare the cedar beams. 15 This is the exultant city that dwelt securely, that said in her heart, "I am, and there is no one besides me." How she has become a desolation, a lair for wild beasts! Everyone who passes by her hisses and shakes his fist.
