The Steadfast Security of the Righteous

1 A Song of Ascents. Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved but abides forever. 2 As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds His people from this time forth and forever. 3 For the scepter of wickedness shall not rest on the allotment of the righteous, lest the righteous stretch out their hands to iniquity. 4 Do good, O the LORD, to those who are good, and to those upright in their hearts. 5 But those who turn aside to their crooked ways, the LORD will lead away with the workers of iniquity. Peace be upon Israel.
