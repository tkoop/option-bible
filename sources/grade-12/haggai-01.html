The Command to Rebuild the Temple

1 In the second year of King Darius, on the first day of the sixth month, the word of the the LORD came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest:

2 "This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: 'These people say, "The time has not yet come to rebuild the house of the the LORD."'

The LORD Rebukes Their Neglect

3 Then the word of the the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying:

4 "Is it time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?

5 "Now, therefore, this is what the the LORD of Hosts says: Consider your ways.

6 "You have sown much, but harvested little. You eat, but you never have enough; you drink, but you never have your fill. You clothe yourselves, but no one is warm. And he who earns wages earns them only to see them vanish.

7 "This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: Consider your ways.

8 "Go up to the mountain and bring wood and build the house, so that I may be pleased with it and be glorified, says the the LORD.

9 "You expected much, but it amounted to little. What you brought home, I blew away. Why? declares the the LORD of Hosts. Because of My house, which remains in ruins, while each of you is busy with his own house.

10 "Therefore, because of you, the heavens have withheld their dew and the earth has withheld its crops.

11 "I called for a drought on the land and the mountains, on the grain, the new wine, the olive oil, on what the ground produces, on people and livestock, and on all the labor of your hands."

The People Obey the LORD

12 Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and all the remnant of the people obeyed the voice of the the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, as the the LORD their God had sent him. And the people feared the the LORD.

13 Then Haggai, the messenger of the the LORD, delivered the the LORD's message to the people: "I am with you, declares the the LORD."

The Work Resumes

14 So the The LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people. They came and worked on the house of the the LORD of Hosts, their God, 15 on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.
