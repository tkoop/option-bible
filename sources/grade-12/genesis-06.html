The Corruption of Humanity

1 When humanity began to multiply across the face of the earth, daughters were born to them. 2 The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful, and they took wives for themselves from among all whom they chose.

3 Then the LORD said, “My Spirit shall not contend with man forever, for he is flesh; his days shall be one hundred twenty years.” 4 The Nephilim were on the earth in those days—and afterward as well—when the sons of God came in to the daughters of men and they bore children to them. These were the mighty men of antiquity, men of renown.

God’s Grief and Judgment

5 The the LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. 6 And the the LORD regretted that He had made man on the earth, and He was grieved in His heart. 7 So the the LORD said, “I will wipe out man, whom I have created, from the face of the earth—both man and beast, creeping thing and bird of heaven—for I regret that I have made them.” 8 But Noah found favor in the eyes of the the LORD.

Noah’s Righteousness and the Earth’s Corruption

9 These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a righteous man, blameless among his contemporaries; Noah lived in close fellowship with God. 10 Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. 11 Now the earth was corrupted before God, and the earth was filled with violence. 12 God saw the earth, and indeed it was corrupted; for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth.

God’s Command to Build the Ark

13 God said to Noah, “The end of all flesh has come before Me, for the earth is filled with violence because of them; and behold, I am about to destroy them along with the earth. 14 Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood; make compartments in the ark, and seal it inside and out with pitch.

15 And this is how you shall make it: the length of the ark shall be three hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits. 16 Make a roof for the ark, and finish it to within a cubit of the top; put a door in the side of the ark; and make it with lower, second, and third decks. 17 And behold, I am bringing a flood of waters upon the earth, to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life from under heaven; everything that is on the earth shall perish. 18 But I will establish My covenant with you; and you shall enter the ark—you, your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives with you. 19 And of every living thing, of all flesh, you shall bring two of every sort into the ark, to keep them alive with you; they shall be male and female. 20 Of the birds according to their kinds, and of the beasts according to their kinds, of every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind, two of every sort shall come to you, to keep them alive. 21 And you shall take for yourself every kind of food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself; and it shall be food for you and for them.”

22 Noah did this; he did all that God had commanded him.
