The LORD's Covenant Promise to Jacob

1 But now listen, O Jacob, My servant, and Israel, whom I have chosen. 2 Thus says the the LORD, your Maker and Former, who formed you in the womb and will help you: "Do not fear, O Jacob, My servant, and Jeshurun, whom I have chosen."

3 For I will pour water upon the thirsty land and streams upon the dry ground; I will pour My Spirit upon your offspring and My blessing upon your descendants. 4 They will spring up among the grass like willows beside flowing streams.

5 One will say, "I belong to the LORD," another will call himself by the name of Jacob, and another will write on his hand, "The LORD's," and will surname himself by the name of Israel.

6 Thus says the the LORD, Israel's King and Redeemer, the the LORD of Hosts: "I am the First and I am the Last, and there is no God but Me. 7 Who then is like Me? Let him call out and declare it; let him set it in order before Me, since I established the ancient people. Let them declare the things to come and what will happen. 8 Do not fear or be dismayed. Have I not announced it to you long ago and declared it? You are My witnesses. Is there any God but Me? There is no Rock; I know not one."

The Emptiness of Idol Making

9 All who fashion idols are nothing, and their precious things are worthless. Their witnesses see nothing and know nothing, so they will be put to shame. 10 Who fashions a god or casts an idol to no profit? 11 Behold, all his companions will be put to shame, and the craftsmen are but men. Let them all gather and stand up; they will tremble and be put to shame.

12 The blacksmith takes a tool, works in the coals, and fashions it with hammers; he works with his strong arm. Even he grows hungry and loses strength; he drinks no water and becomes faint. 13 The woodworker extends a measuring line, outlines it with chalk, shapes it with planes, and marks it with a compass. He makes it like the form of a man, like the beauty of man, to dwell in a house. 14 He cuts down cedars, or he takes a cypress or an oak and makes it strong for himself among the trees of the forest. He plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

15 It becomes fuel for man. He takes some and warms himself; he kindles a fire and bakes bread. He also makes a god and worships it; he makes an idol and bows down to it. 16 Half of it he burns in the fire; on that half he eats meat; he roasts meat and is satisfied. He warms himself and says, "Ah, I am warm; I have seen the fire." 17 The rest of it he makes into a god, into his idol; he bows to it, worships it, prays to it, and says, "Deliver me, for you are my god."

18 They know nothing and understand nothing, for their eyes are plastered shut so they cannot see, and their hearts cannot understand. 19 No one considers, nor is there knowledge or understanding to say, "I have burned half of it in the fire, and I have baked bread on its coals; I have roasted meat and eaten it. Shall I make the rest of it into an abomination? Shall I bow down to a block of wood?" 20 He feeds on ashes; a deluded heart has led him astray, and he cannot deliver his soul or say, "Is there not a lie in my right hand?"

The LORD Redeems and Restores His People

21 Remember these things, O Jacob and Israel, for you are My servant. I formed you; you are My servant, O Israel, you will not be forgotten by Me. 22 I have blotted out your transgressions like a cloud, and your sins like a mist. Return to Me, for I have redeemed you. 23 Sing, O heavens, for the LORD has done it; shout, O depths of the earth; break forth into singing, O mountains, forest, and every tree in it; for the LORD has redeemed Jacob and will be glorified in Israel.

24 Thus says the the LORD, your Redeemer, who formed you from the womb: "I am the the LORD, who does all things, who stretches out the heavens alone, who spreads out the earth by Myself, 25 who frustrates the signs of false prophets and makes fools of diviners, who turns back the wise and makes their knowledge foolish, 26 who confirms the word of His servant and fulfills the counsel of His messengers, who says to Jerusalem, 'You will be inhabited,' and to the cities of Judah, 'You will be rebuilt,' and I will raise up her ruins. 27 who says to the deep, 'Be dry,' and I will dry up your rivers, 28 who says to Cyrus, 'My shepherd,' and he will fulfill all My desire, saying to Jerusalem, 'You will be built,' and to the temple, 'Your foundation will be laid.'"
