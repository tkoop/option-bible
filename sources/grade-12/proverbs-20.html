Warnings Against Drunkenness, Anger, and Sloth

1 Wine is a mocker, strong drink a brawler, and whoever is led astray by them is not wise. 2 A king's terror is like a lion's roar; whoever provokes his anger forfeits his life. 3 It is honorable for a man to refrain from strife, but every fool quarrels. 4 The lazy man will not plow in winter; at harvest he seeks a crop and finds none.

The Depths of the Heart and the King's Judgment

5 A plan in a man's heart is deep water, but a man of understanding draws it out. 6 Many proclaim their own loyalty, but who can find a faithful man? 7 The righteous man walks in his integrity; blessed are his children after him. 8 A king seated on the throne of judgment winnows all evil with his eyes.

9 Who can say, "I have purified my heart; I am cleansed from my sin"?

10 Differing weights and differing measures are both an abomination to the LORD. 11 Even a child is known by his deeds, whether his conduct is pure and upright.

Honesty in Commerce, Counsel, and Speech

12 The hearing ear and the seeing eye—the LORD has made them both. 13 Do not love sleep, lest you come to poverty; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of bread.

14 "Bad, bad!" says the buyer, but when he has gone away, he boasts.

15 There is gold and an abundance of costly stones, but lips of knowledge are a precious jewel. 16 Take the garment of one who has become security for a stranger, and hold it in pledge when he becomes security for foreigners. 17 Bread gained by deceit is sweet to a man, but afterward his mouth will be full of gravel. 18 Plans are established by counsel, and war is waged by wise guidance. 19 Whoever gossips reveals secrets, so do not associate with a babbler.

Vows, Retribution, and the LORD's Sovereignty

20 Whoever curses his father or his mother—his lamp will be put out in utter darkness. 21 An inheritance gained hastily at first will not be blessed in the end.

22 Do not say, "I will repay evil"; wait for the LORD, and He will save you.

23 Differing weights are an abomination to the LORD, and dishonest scales are not good. 24 A man's steps are from the LORD; how then can man understand his way?

25 It is a snare to say rashly, "It is holy," and only afterward to consider one's vows.

26 A wise king winnows the wicked and drives the threshing wheel over them. 27 The spirit of man is the lamp of the LORD, searching all his innermost parts. 28 Steadfast love and faithfulness preserve the king, and by steadfast love his throne is upheld. 29 The glory of young men is their strength, but the splendor of old men is their gray hair. 30 Blows that wound cleanse away evil, and strokes make clean the innermost parts.
