Woe to Ariel and the Coming Siege

1 Woe to Ariel, Ariel, the city where David camped! Year after year, let the festivals run their course. 2 I will besiege Ariel, and she will mourn and lament; she will be to Me like an altar hearth. 3 I will encamp all around you, and besiege you with towers; I will raise siegeworks against you. 4 You will be brought low, and you will speak from the earth; your words will be muffled in the dust. Your voice will be like a ghost rising from the ground, and your speech will whisper from the dust. 5 But the multitude of your enemies will be like fine dust, and the horde of the ruthless like blowing chaff. Then suddenly, in an instant, 6 you will be judged by the LORD of Hosts with thunder and earthquake and a great roar, with whirlwind and storm and a consuming fire. 7 The multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel—all who war against her and her stronghold and distress her—will be like a dream, a vision in the night. 8 It will be as when a hungry man dreams he is eating, but awakens to find his soul empty; or as when a thirsty man dreams he is drinking, but awakens faint, with his thirst unquenched. So will it be with all the nations that wage war against Mount Zion.

Spiritual Blindness and Hollow Worship

9 Be stunned and amazed; blind yourselves and remain sightless! They are drunk, but not on wine; they stagger, but not from strong drink. 10 For the LORD has poured out on you a spirit of deep sleep; He has shut your eyes—the prophets—and veiled your heads—the seers. 11 So the entire vision is to you like the words of a sealed scroll. If it is handed to someone who can read, and he is asked to read it, he will say, "I cannot, because it is sealed." 12 Or if the scroll is handed to one who cannot read, and he is asked to read it, he will say, "I cannot read."

13 Therefore the Lord said: "Because this people draw near to Me with their mouths and honor Me with their lips, while their hearts are far from Me, and their reverence for Me is merely a commandment taught by men, 14 I will again confound this people with wonder upon wonder. The wisdom of the wise will vanish, and the discernment of the discerning will be hidden."

Judgment on Secret Schemes

15 Woe to those who dig deep to hide their plans from the LORD! In darkness they do their work and say, "Who sees us? Who knows us?" 16 You have turned everything upside down. Shall the potter be regarded as clay? Shall what is made say of its maker, "He did not make me"? Or shall what is formed say of Him who formed it, "He has no understanding"?

Restoration and Redemption

17 In just a little while, will not Lebanon be turned into a fertile field, and the fertile field seem like a forest? 18 In that day the deaf will hear the words of the scroll, and out of darkness and gloom the eyes of the blind will see. 19 The humble will increase their joy in the LORD, and the poor among men will rejoice in the Holy One of Israel. 20 For the ruthless will vanish, the mockers will disappear, and all who look for evil will be cut down: 21 those who with a word make a person out to be a sinner, who ensnare the mediator at the gate, and who with false charges deprive the innocent of justice.

22 Therefore this is what the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, says to the house of Jacob: "No longer will Jacob be ashamed; no longer will his face grow pale. 23 For when he sees his children, the work of My hands, among him, they will honor My name; they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob and stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24 Those who stray in spirit will come to understanding, and those who complain will accept instruction."
