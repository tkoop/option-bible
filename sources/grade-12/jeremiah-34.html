The Siege of Jerusalem and Zedekiah’s Fate

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, together with all his army, all the kingdoms of the earth under his dominion, and all the peoples, were waging war against Jerusalem and all its cities:

2 The LORD, the God of Israel, said, "Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah and tell him, 'This is what the LORD says: Behold, I am about to hand this city over to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire. 3 And you will not escape from his hand but will surely be captured and handed over to him. You will see the king of Babylon eye to eye and speak with him face to face, and you will go to Babylon. 4 Yet hear the word of the LORD, O Zedekiah king of Judah. This is what the LORD says concerning you: You will not die by the sword; 5 you will die in peace. As spices were burned for your fathers, the former kings who preceded you, so a fire will be kindled for you, and they will lament, 'Alas, O master!' For I have spoken this word, declares the LORD.'"

6 Then Jeremiah the prophet proclaimed all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem, 7 while the Babylonian army was besieging Jerusalem and every remaining city of Judah—Lachish and Azekah—because those were the only fortified cities still left in Judah.

The Covenant to Free Hebrew Slaves

8 After King Zedekiah had covenanted with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim liberty, 9 so that each man would release his Hebrew slaves, both male and female, and no one would keep a fellow Hebrew in bondage, 10 all the officials and all the people who entered into the covenant consented to free their male and female slaves and to hold them in bondage no longer. They heeded the agreement and set them free. 11 But afterward they reversed course and re-enslaved the men and women they had released.

The LORD’s Rebuke and Judgment

12 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,

13 "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: I established a covenant with your ancestors when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, instructing them that 14 at the end of seven years each of you must release his fellow Hebrew who has sold himself to you. He may serve you six years, and then you must send him away free. But your ancestors neither listened nor inclined their ears. 15 Recently you repented and did what was right in My sight by proclaiming freedom to one another, and you ratified a covenant before Me in the house called by My name. 16 But then you turned back and desecrated My name. Each of you reclaimed the male and female slaves you had released to go wherever they pleased, and you subdued them into slavery again.

17 "Therefore this is what the LORD says: You have not obeyed Me; you have not proclaimed freedom to your brothers and neighbors. So now I proclaim freedom for you, declares the LORD—freedom to the sword, to pestilence, and to famine! I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth. 18 And those who violated My covenant and failed to uphold the terms of the covenant they made before Me, I will treat like the calf they cut in two, walking between its pieces. 19 The officials of Judah and Jerusalem, the court officials, the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the pieces of the calf, 20 I will deliver into the hands of their enemies, into the hands of those who seek their lives. Their corpses will become food for the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth. 21 And Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials I will deliver into the hands of their enemies, into the hands of those who seek their lives, and into the hand of the Babylonian army, which has withdrawn from you. 22 Behold, I will issue a command, declares the LORD, and I will bring them back to this city; they will fight against it, capture it, and burn it with fire. And I will devastate the cities of Judah so that no one can dwell there."
