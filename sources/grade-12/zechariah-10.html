Seek Rain from the LORD and Reject False Shepherds

1 Ask the LORD for rain in the season of the spring rains; the LORD makes storm clouds and gives them showers—rain for every plant in the field. 2 For the household idols speak deceit, and the diviners see false visions; they utter empty dreams and offer vain comfort. Therefore the people wander like sheep and suffer, because there is no shepherd.

The LORD Rebukes the Shepherds and Strengthens His People

3 My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders, for the LORD of Hosts has visited His flock, the house of Judah, and will make them like His majestic horse in battle. 4 From Judah will come the cornerstone, the tent peg, the battle bow, and every ruler together. 5 They will be like warriors trampling mud in the streets during battle; they will fight because the LORD is with them, and the horsemen will be put to shame.

6 I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph; I will restore them, for I have shown them compassion. They will be as though I had never rejected them, for I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them. 7 Ephraim will be like a mighty warrior; their hearts will rejoice as if with wine; their children will see it and rejoice; their hearts will exult in the LORD.

The LORD Gathers, Enlarges, and Delivers His People

8 I will whistle for them and gather them in, for I have redeemed them; and they will be as numerous as before. 9 Though I scatter them among the nations, they will remember Me in distant lands; they and their children will live and return. 10 I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria; I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon, and there will be no room for them. 11 He will pass through the sea of distress and strike the waves of the sea, so that all the depths of the Nile dry up; Assyria's pride will be brought low, and Egypt's scepter will pass away. 12 I will strengthen them in the LORD, and they will walk in His name, declares the LORD.
