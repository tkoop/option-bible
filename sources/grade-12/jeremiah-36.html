Jeremiah Is Commanded to Write a Scroll

1 In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this proclamation came to Jeremiah from the the LORD: 2 "Take a scroll and inscribe upon it all the words I have spoken to you concerning Israel, Judah, and all the nations—from the day I first addressed you, in the days of Josiah, until this very day. 3 Perhaps the people of Judah will heed all the calamity I intend to inflict upon them, so that each may turn from his wicked course. Then I will forgive their iniquity and their sin."

4 Accordingly, Jeremiah summoned Baruch son of Neriah, and while Jeremiah dictated, Baruch inscribed upon a scroll all the words the the LORD had spoken to him.

5 "I am confined; I cannot enter the temple of the the LORD. 6 Therefore, you must go to the the LORD's house on a day of fasting and read aloud to the people at the temple the words of the the LORD from the scroll you wrote at my dictation. Read them also to all the people of Judah who come from their towns. 7 Perhaps their supplication will find favor before the the LORD, and each will turn from his wicked course; for great is the anger and wrath the the LORD has pronounced against these people."

8 Baruch son of Neriah carried out everything Jeremiah the prophet commanded; he read the words of the the LORD from the scroll in the the LORD's house.

Baruch Reads the Scroll to the People

9 In the ninth month of the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, a fast was proclaimed before the the LORD for all the people in Jerusalem and for all those arriving from the towns of Judah.

10 From the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court at the entrance of the New Gate of the the LORD's house, Baruch read from the scroll the words of Jeremiah in the hearing of all the people.

11 When Micaiah son of Gemariah son of Shaphan heard all the words of the the LORD from the scroll, 12 he descended to the scribe's chamber in the king's palace, where all the officials were seated: Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Akbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.

13 Micaiah reported to them everything he had heard Baruch read from the scroll to the people.

The Officials Bring the Scroll to the King

14 Then all the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah son of Shelemiah son of Cushi to Baruch, saying, "Bring the scroll you have read to the people, and come." So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and went to them. 15 They said to him, "Sit down, please, and read it to us." So Baruch read it to them.

16 When they heard all the words, they looked at one another in alarm and said to Baruch, "We must surely report all these words to the king."

17 Then they asked Baruch, "Tell us, how did you write all these words? Did Jeremiah dictate them to you?"

18 Baruch answered them, "He dictated all these words to me, and I wrote them with ink on the scroll."

19 The officials said to Baruch, "You and Jeremiah, go and hide; do not let anyone know where you are."

20 After they had deposited the scroll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, they went to the king in the courtyard and reported everything to him. 21 The king sent Jehudi to retrieve the scroll, and he brought it from the chamber of Elishama the scribe and read it to the king and to all the officials standing beside him.

22 It was the ninth month, and the king was sitting in the winter house, with a fire burning in the brazier before him. 23 Whenever Jehudi had read three or four columns, the king cut them off with a scribe's knife and cast them into the fire in the brazier, until the entire scroll was consumed by the flames.

24 The king and all his attendants who heard all these words were not afraid, nor did they tear their garments. 25 Even though Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urged the king not to burn the scroll, he would not listen to them.

26 Instead, the king commanded Jerahmeel, a son of the king, Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to arrest Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet; but the the LORD had hidden them.

The Scroll Is Rewritten and Judgment Pronounced

27 After the king had burned the scroll containing the words Baruch had written at Jeremiah's dictation, the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah: 28 "Take another scroll and inscribe upon it all the former words that were on the first scroll, which Jehoiakim king of Judah burned. 29 Tell Jehoiakim king of Judah, 'This is what the the LORD says: You burned this scroll and said, "Why did you write on it that the king of Babylon will certainly come and destroy this land and remove from it both man and beast?" 30 Therefore this is what the the LORD says about Jehoiakim king of Judah: He will have no one to sit on the throne of David, and his dead body will be cast out to the heat by day and to the frost by night. 31 I will punish him, his offspring, and his attendants for their iniquity; I will bring on them and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem and the people of Judah every disaster I have pronounced against them, because they did not listen.'"

32 Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to the scribe Baruch son of Neriah, and as Jeremiah dictated, Baruch wrote on it all the words of the scroll that Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire. And many similar words were added to them.
