Prophecy Against Tyre

1 In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me:

2 “Son of man, because Tyre has exulted over Jerusalem, saying that the gateway of the nations has been shattered, that its commerce has turned to Tyre, and that Tyre will prosper now that Jerusalem is ruined,

3 “therefore this is what the Lord the the LORD says: Behold, I am against you, O Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea brings up its waves.

4 “They will destroy your walls and tear down your towers; I will scrape away your rubble and make you a bare rock.

5 “You will become a place in the sea where nets are spread, for I have spoken, declares the Lord the the LORD. 6 Your daughters in the open country will be killed by the sword, and they will know that I am the the LORD.”

Nebuchadnezzar's Siege and Tyre's Ruin

7 “For this is what the Lord the the LORD says: Behold, I will bring against Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of kings, from the north, with horses, chariots, and horsemen, a great company, and many people.

8 “He will kill your daughters in the open country with the sword. He will set up a siege wall against you, pour out a siege ramp against you, and raise shields against you. 9 He will direct the blow of his battering rams against your walls and break down your towers with his swords.

10 “The dust of his horses will cover you. When he enters your gates as through a breached city, your walls will tremble at the sound of horsemen, wagons, and chariots. 11 With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.

12 “They will plunder your wealth and loot your merchandise. They will break down your walls and demolish your fine houses, and they will throw your stones, timber, and rubble into the sea. 13 I will put an end to the sound of your songs, and the music of your harps will be heard no more. 14 I will make you a bare rock; you will become a place where nets are spread; you will never be rebuilt, for I, the the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord the the LORD.”

The Coastlands Mourn Tyre's Fall

15 “This is what the Lord the the LORD says to Tyre: Will not the coastlands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan and slaughter takes place in your midst?

16 “Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, remove their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. They will clothe themselves with trembling, sit on the ground, tremble continually, and be appalled at you.

17 “They will take up a lament for you, asking how you have perished, O renowned city, inhabited by seafaring men—you who were mighty on the sea, you and your inhabitants, who imposed your terror on all who dwell there.

18 “Now the coastlands tremble on the day of your fall; the coastlands by the sea are dismayed at your passing.”

Tyre Consigned to the Pit

19 “For this is what the Lord the the LORD says: When I make you a desolate city, like cities that are not inhabited, when I bring the deep over you and the great waters cover you,

20 “then I will bring you down with those who go down to the pit, to the people of old, and I will make you dwell in the lowest parts of the earth, among the ancient ruins, with those who go down to the pit, so that you will not be inhabited. Yet I will bestow beauty in the land of the living.

21 “I will make you a horror, and you will be no more. Though you are sought, you will never be found again, declares the Lord the the LORD.”
