Asa Succeeds Abijah and Reforms Judah

1 Abijah died and was interred with his ancestors in the City of David; his son Asa ascended the throne in his place, and throughout his reign the land enjoyed a decade of peace. 2 Asa did what was good and right in the sight of the LORD his God. 3 He eradicated the foreign altars and the high places, shattered the sacred pillars, and hewed down the Asherah poles. 4 He charged Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to observe the law and the commandment. 5 He removed the high places and the incense altars from every city in Judah, and the kingdom remained tranquil under his rule.

Asa Fortifies Judah During a Season of Peace

6 Because the land was at rest, he fortified cities throughout Judah during those years; no war threatened him, for the LORD had granted him rest. 7 He urged Judah, “Let us build these cities and encircle them with walls, towers, doors, and bars. The land is still ours, for we have sought the LORD our God; we have sought Him, and He has granted us rest on every side.” Thus they built and prospered.

Zerah the Cushite Invades and Asa Prays

8 Asa mustered an army of three hundred thousand men from Judah, equipped with shields and spears, and two hundred eighty thousand from Benjamin, equipped with shields and bows; all were valiant warriors. 9 Zerah the Cushite marched against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots, advancing as far as Mareshah. 10 Asa went out to confront him, and they drew up battle lines in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah. 11 Asa cried out to the LORD his God: “LORD, it is as easy for You to help the powerless as the mighty. Help us, O LORD our God, for we rely on You, and in Your name we have come against this multitude. O LORD, You are our God; do not let mortal man prevail against You.”

The LORD Defeats the Cushites and Grants Plunder

12 The LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and they fled. 13 Asa and his people pursued them to Gerar. The Cushites collapsed until none could recover, for they were crushed before the LORD and before His army. They seized an enormous quantity of plunder. 14 They assaulted every city surrounding Gerar, because the terror of the LORD had fallen upon them, and they plundered all the cities, for the spoil was great. 15 They also attacked the tents of the herdsmen, carrying off sheep and camels in abundance, and then returned to Jerusalem.
