Enemies Plot Against Nehemiah and the Wall

1 Now when Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of our enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall and that no gap remained in it—though at that time I had not yet set up doors in the gates— 2 Sanballat and Geshem sent me a message, saying, “Come, let us meet together in one of the villages on the plain of Ono.” But they were planning to harm me.

3 So I sent messengers to them with this reply: “I am engaged in a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it to come down to you?” 4 They sent me the same message four times, and each time I answered them in the same way.

5 Then, for the fifth time, Sanballat sent his servant to me with an open letter in his hand, 6 and this is what it said: “It is reported among the nations—and Gashmu agrees—that you and the Jews are plotting rebellion; therefore you are building the wall. Moreover, according to these reports, you are to become their king. 7 You have also appointed prophets in Jerusalem to proclaim concerning you, ‘A king is in Judah.’ Now these matters will be reported to the king. So come, let us confer together.”

8 I sent word back to him: “Nothing like the things you are alleging has happened; you are inventing them in your own mind.” 9 For they were all trying to intimidate us, saying, “Their hands will be weakened from the work, and it will not be done.” But now, strengthen my hands!

Shemaiah's False Prophecy

10 I then went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah, the son of Mehetabel, who was confined to his home. He said, “Let us meet in the house of God, inside the temple. Let us shut the doors of the temple, for they are coming to kill you—by night they are coming to kill you!”

11 But I replied, “Should a man like me run away? Or should one like me enter the temple to save his life? I will not go in!”

12 Then I realized that God had not sent him, but that he had prophesied against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him. 13 He had been hired to intimidate me so that I would sin by doing this, and then they would have grounds to discredit me.

14 O my God, remember Tobiah and Sanballat for what they have done, and also the prophetess Noadiah and the rest of the prophets who have been trying to intimidate me.

The Wall Is Completed

15 So the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of Elul, in fifty-two days. 16 When all our enemies heard about this, all the nations around us were afraid and lost their confidence, for they realized that this work had been accomplished by our God.

Tobiah's Influence Among the Nobles

17 Also in those days, the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah's letters came to them. 18 For many in Judah were bound to him by oath, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah. 19 Moreover, they kept speaking of his good deeds in my presence and reporting my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.
