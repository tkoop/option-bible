Ahaz Begins His Reign in Judah

1 In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham became king of Judah. 2 Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. Unlike David his forefather, he did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD his God. 3 Instead, he walked in the way of the kings of Israel and even made his son pass through the fire, following the abominations of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel. 4 He sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and beneath every leafy tree.

Judah Threatened by Aram and Israel

5 Then Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, marched up to wage war. They besieged Jerusalem against Ahaz, yet they could not overpower him. 6 At that time Rezin king of Aram recovered Elath for Aram and drove the Judeans from Elath; the Arameans came to Elath and have lived there to this day.

Ahaz Appeals to Assyria

7 Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria, saying, "I am your servant and your son. Come up and rescue me from the hand of the king of Aram and from the hand of the king of Israel, who are rising against me."

8 Ahaz took the silver and gold found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the king's house, and he sent them as a bribe to the king of Assyria. 9 The king of Assyria listened to him and marched against Damascus, captured it, deported its people to Kir, and put Rezin to death.

A Foreign Altar in the Temple

10 King Ahaz traveled to Damascus to meet Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria. There he saw the altar at Damascus, and King Ahaz sent to Uriah the priest a sketch of the altar, along with a model of its design and all its workmanship. 11 Uriah the priest built the altar in accordance with everything King Ahaz had sent from Damascus, finishing it before King Ahaz returned from Damascus.

12 When the king returned from Damascus and saw the altar, he approached it and offered sacrifices on it. 13 He burned his burnt offering and his grain offering, poured out his drink offering, and sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings on the altar.

14 He moved the bronze altar that stood before the LORD from the front of the temple—between the new altar and the house of the LORD—and set it on the north side of the altar.

15 King Ahaz commanded Uriah the priest, "Burn on the great altar the morning burnt offering and the evening burnt offering, the king's grain offering and his burnt offering, and the grain offering and burnt offerings of all the people of the land, with their drink offerings. Sprinkle on it all the blood of the burnt offerings and all the blood of the sacrifices. The bronze altar, however, will be for me to seek guidance." 16 Uriah the priest did everything King Ahaz commanded.

17 King Ahaz cut off the frames of the movable stands and removed the basins from them. He took down the Sea from the bronze bulls that supported it and set it on a stone pavement. 18 He also removed from the house of the LORD the Sabbath canopy that had been built there, along with the royal entryway, because of the king of Assyria.

19 As for the rest of the acts of Ahaz, all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 20 Ahaz died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David, and his son Hezekiah reigned in his place.
