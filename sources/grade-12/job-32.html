Elihu's Anger at Job and His Friends

1 Now these three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. 2 Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, became very angry; he burned with anger at Job for justifying himself rather than God. 3 He was also angry with Job’s three friends, because they had found no answer, yet they had condemned Job. 4 Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job because they were older than he. 5 But when Elihu saw that the three men had no answer in their mouths, his anger burned.

Elihu Begins to Speak

6 So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, “I am young in years, and you are old; therefore I was timid and afraid to declare what I know to you. 7 I thought, ‘Age should speak, and a multitude of years should teach wisdom.’ 8 But there is a spirit in man, and the breath of the Almighty gives them understanding. 9 It is not only the old who are wise, or the aged who understand justice. 10 Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me; I too will declare what I know.’

11 “Behold, I waited for your words; I listened to your reasonings while you searched out what to say. 12 I gave you my full attention, yet not one of you has refuted Job or answered his claims. 13 Beware lest you say, ‘We have found wisdom; God, not man, will refute him.’ 14 Now he has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your arguments.

15 “They are dismayed; they answer no more; words have failed them. 16 I have waited, because they do not speak, because they stand still and answer no more. 17 I too will have my say; I too will declare what I know. 18 For I am full of words; the spirit within my belly compels me. 19 Behold, my belly is like wine that has no outlet; it is ready to burst like new wineskins. 20 I must speak, so that I may find relief; I must open my lips and respond. 21 I will be partial to no one, nor will I flatter any man. 22 For I do not know how to flatter, or my Maker would soon take me away.”
