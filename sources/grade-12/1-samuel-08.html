Israel Demands a King

1When Samuel had advanced in years, he appointed his sons as judges over Israel. 2His firstborn was named Joel, and his second was Abijah; they administered justice in Beersheba. 3Yet his sons did not conduct themselves according to his ways. Instead, they turned aside after dishonest gain, accepted bribes, and perverted justice.

4All the elders of Israel assembled and came to Samuel at Ramah. 5They said to him, “Look, you are advanced in years, and your sons do not conduct themselves according to your ways. Therefore, appoint a king to judge us, as all the other nations have.” 6The matter displeased Samuel when they said, “Give us a king to judge us.” So Samuel prayed to the the LORD.

7The The LORD said to Samuel, “Listen to the voice of the people in all that they say to you, for they have not rejected you; rather, they have rejected Me as their King. 8They are doing to you precisely what they have done to Me from the day I brought them up from Egypt until now: they have forsaken Me and served other gods. 9Now listen to their voice, but solemnly warn them and explain the manner of the king who will reign over them.”

Samuel Warns About the Cost of a King

10Samuel communicated all the words of the the LORD to the people who had requested a king. 11He said, “This is how the king who will reign over you will treat you: he will take your sons and assign them to his chariots and cavalry, and they will run before his chariot. 12He will appoint commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties, and he will set some to plow his ground, reap his harvest, and manufacture his weapons of war and the equipment for his chariots. 13He will take your daughters to serve as perfumers, cooks, and bakers. 14He will seize the best of your fields, vineyards, and olive groves and bestow them upon his servants. 15He will exact a tenth of your grain and your vineyards and give it to his officials and servants. 16He will take your male and female servants, your finest young men, and your donkeys, and employ them in his service. 17He will exact a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will become his servants. 18On that day you will cry out because of the king you have chosen for yourselves, but the the LORD will not answer you in that day.”

The People Refuse to Listen

19But the people refused to heed Samuel’s voice. They said, “No! We must have a king over us, 20so that we may be like all the other nations, and so that our king may judge us, go out before us, and fight our battles.” 21Samuel heard all the people’s words and repeated them to the the LORD.

22The The LORD said to Samuel, “Listen to their voice and appoint a king for them.”

Then Samuel told the men of Israel, “Go back, each of you, to his own city.”
