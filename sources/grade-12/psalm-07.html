A shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning the words of Cush, a Benjamite.

David's Appeal for Refuge and Vindication

1 The LORD my God, in You I seek refuge; save me from all my pursuers and deliver me, 2 lest my soul be torn apart as by a lion, rent with no one to rescue.

3 The LORD my God, if I have done this, if iniquity clings to my hands, 4 if I have requited my friend with evil or plundered my adversary unprovoked, 5 then let my enemy hunt down my life and overtake it; let him crush my life into the dust and consign my glory to the dust. Selah.

The LORD's Just Judgment of the Nations

6 Rise up, the LORD, in Your wrath; lift Yourself against the rage of my adversaries. Awaken the justice You have ordained for me. 7 Let the congregation of nations assemble around You, and over them be exalted on high.

8 The LORD judges the peoples. Vindicate me, the LORD, according to my righteousness and integrity. 9 Terminate the malice of the wicked, yet establish the righteous—You who examine hearts and minds, O God. 10 My shield rests with God, who saves the upright in heart. 11 God is a righteous judge, a God who is indignant every day.

The Self-Destruction of the Wicked

12 If a man will not repent, He sharpens His sword; He has bent His bow and made it ready. 13 He has prepared instruments of death; He makes His arrows fiery shafts. 14 Behold, the wicked travails in iniquity, conceives trouble, and births deception. 15 He excavated a pit and deepened it, only to fall into the snare he contrived. 16 His mischief returns upon his own head, and his violence descends upon his own crown.

Gratitude for Divine Righteousness

17 I will render thanks to the LORD for His righteousness, and I will sing to the name of the LORD Most High.
