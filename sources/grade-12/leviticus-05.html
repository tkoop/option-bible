Guilt Offerings for Specific Offenses

1 When a person sins by failing to testify after hearing a sworn summons, though he is a witness who saw or knew something, he shall bear his guilt. 2 Or if a person touches anything ceremonially unclean—whether the carcass of an unclean wild animal, an unclean domestic animal, or an unclean creeping thing—and is unaware of it, he is unclean and guilty. 3 Or if he touches human uncleanness of any kind by which a person becomes unclean, and is unaware of it, he is guilty once he realizes it. 4 Or if a person swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do good or evil—whatever a man may utter in an oath—and is unaware of it, he is guilty in any of these matters once he realizes it. 5 When he is guilty in any of these matters, he must confess the sin he has committed.

6 He must bring his guilt offering to the LORD for the sin he has committed: a female lamb or goat from the flock as a sin offering. Then the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin. 7 If he cannot afford a lamb, he shall bring to the LORD two turtledoves or two young pigeons—one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. 8 He shall bring them to the priest, who shall first present the one for the sin offering; he shall wring its head at the front of its neck without severing it. 9 Then he shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering against the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering.

10 The second bird he shall prepare as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he shall be forgiven. 11 But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he shall bring as his offering for the sin he has committed a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. He shall not put oil on it or place frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering. 12 He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take a handful of it as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar on top of the offerings made by fire to the LORD. It is a sin offering. 13 In this way the priest shall make atonement for him for any of these sins he has committed, and he shall be forgiven. The remainder shall belong to the priest, as with the grain offering.

Guilt Offering for Sacred Things

14 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, “15 If a person acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against the LORD’s holy things, he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, valued in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel, as a guilt offering. 16 He must make restitution for the holy thing in which he sinned, adding a fifth to its value, and give it to the priest. Then the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he shall be forgiven.”

Guilt Offering for Unknown Violations

17 If a person sins and does any of the things the LORD has commanded not to be done, though he does not know it, he is guilty and bears his guilt. 18 He shall bring to the priest a ram without blemish from the flock, of proper value, as a guilt offering. Then the priest shall make atonement for him for the error he committed unintentionally, and he shall be forgiven. 19 It is a guilt offering; he has indeed been guilty before the LORD.
