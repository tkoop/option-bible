A Prayer of David in the Cave

For the choirmaster: to the tune “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul in the cave.

1 Be gracious to me, O God, be gracious to me, for in You my soul takes refuge; in the shadow of Your wings I take refuge, until the calamities pass by. 2 I cry out to God Most High, to God who brings His purpose to completion for me. 3 He will send from heaven and save me; He will rebuke those who trample on me. Selah. God will send forth His steadfast love and His faithfulness.

4 My soul is among lions; I lie down among ravenous beasts—sons of men whose teeth are spears and arrows, and whose tongues are sharp swords. 5 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let Your glory be over all the earth. 6 They have prepared a net for my steps; my soul is bowed down. They have dug a pit before me, yet they have fallen into it themselves. Selah.

Steadfast Praise and Thanksgiving

7 My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast. I will sing and make music. 8 Awake, my glory! Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn. 9 I will give thanks to You, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises to You among the nations. 10 For Your loving devotion is great to the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. 11 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let Your glory be over all the earth.
