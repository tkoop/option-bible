Regulations for Offerings in the Promised Land

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land I am giving you to inhabit, 3 and you present to the LORD a fire offering from the herd or the flock—an aroma pleasing to the LORD—whether a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a vow or as a freewill offering, or at your appointed festivals, 4 then the one who presents the offering shall bring to the LORD his offering: a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil; 5 and for the drink offering you shall add a quarter of a hin of wine for each lamb, with the burnt offering or the sacrifice. 6 For a ram, you shall prepare a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil, 7 and for the drink offering you shall offer a third of a hin of wine, an aroma pleasing to the LORD. 8 When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice, to fulfill a vow or as a fellowship offering to the LORD, 9 then with the bull you shall present a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil, 10 and you shall offer half a hin of wine for the drink offering, a fire offering, an aroma pleasing to the LORD. 11 Thus it shall be done for each bull, ram, lamb, or young goat. 12 According to the number you prepare, so you shall do for each one. 13 Every native-born Israelite shall do these things in this way when presenting a fire offering, an aroma pleasing to the LORD. 14 And throughout your generations, if a foreigner residing among you or among your people makes a fire offering, an aroma pleasing to the LORD, he shall do just as you do. 15 For the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the foreigner residing among you; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations. Before the LORD, you and the foreigner shall be the same. 16 One law and one ordinance shall apply to you and to the foreigner residing among you."

The Contribution from the First Dough

17 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 18 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you come into the land to which I am bringing you, 19 and you eat of the bread of the land, you shall set apart a contribution to the LORD. 20 From the first of your dough you shall set apart a cake as a contribution; like the contribution from the threshing floor, so you shall set it apart. 21 Throughout your generations you shall give to the LORD a contribution from the first of your dough."

Atonement for Unintentional Sins

22 "If you unintentionally fail to observe all these commandments that the LORD has spoken to Moses— 23 everything that the LORD has commanded you through Moses, from the day the LORD gave the command and onward throughout your generations— 24 and if it was done unintentionally without the knowledge of the community, then the whole community shall offer one young bull as a burnt offering, an aroma pleasing to the LORD, with its grain offering and drink offering according to the ordinance, and one male goat for a sin offering. 25 The priest shall make atonement for the whole community of Israelites, and they shall be forgiven, because it was unintentional, and they have brought their offering, a fire offering to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD for their unintentional sin. 26 The whole community of Israelites shall be forgiven, and the foreigner residing among them, because all the people acted unintentionally.

27 "If one person sins unintentionally, he shall offer a female goat a year old for a sin offering. 28 The priest shall make atonement before the LORD for the person who erred through unintentional sin, to make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven. 29 You shall have one law for the native-born among the Israelites and for the foreigner residing among them, for anyone who acts unintentionally.

30 "But the person who acts defiantly, whether native-born or foreigner, reviles the LORD; that person shall be removed from among his people. 31 Because he has despised the word of the LORD and has broken His commandment, his guilt shall remain on him."

The Sabbath Wood-Gatherer and the Tassels

32 While the Israelites were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day. 33 Those who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses, Aaron, and the whole community. 34 They put him in custody, because it had not been declared what should be done to him.

35 The LORD said to Moses, "The man shall surely be put to death; the whole community shall stone him with stones outside the camp." 36 So the whole community brought him outside the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

37 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 38 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: Throughout your generations you shall make tassels on the corners of your garments, and you shall put a blue cord on the tassel of each corner. 39 The tassels shall be for you a reminder, so that you will remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and so that you will not follow after your own hearts and your own eyes, by which you are led into unfaithfulness. 40 Thus you shall remember and do all My commandments and be holy to your God. 41 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God."
