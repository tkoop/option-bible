The King's Triumph in the LORD's Delivering Strength

1 The king rejoices in Your might, O LORD; how profoundly he exults in Your salvation! 2 You have granted him his heart’s desire and have not withheld the supplication of his lips. Selah. 3 For You welcomed him with abundant blessings; You set a crown of pure gold upon his head. 4 He petitioned You for life, and You bestowed it upon him—length of days forever and ever. 5 Great is his glory through Your salvation; You confer upon him splendor and majesty. 6 For You grant him blessings forever; You gladden him with joy in Your presence. 7 For the king trusts in the LORD; through the loving devotion of the Most High, he shall not be shaken.

The LORD's Vengeance upon His Adversaries

8 Your hand will lay hold of all Your enemies; Your right hand will seize those who hate You. 9 You will make them like a fiery furnace at the time of Your appearing. In His wrath the LORD will engulf them, and fire will consume them. 10 You will obliterate their descendants from the earth and their offspring from among humanity. 11 Though they intended evil against You, the schemes they devised will not prevail. 12 For You will rout them when You aim Your bow at their faces.

13 Be exalted, O LORD, in Your might; we will sing and celebrate Your power.
