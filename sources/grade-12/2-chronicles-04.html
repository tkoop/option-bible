The Bronze Altar and the Molten Sea

1 Solomon made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high. 2 He also made the Sea of cast metal, round, ten cubits from rim to rim, five cubits high, with a circumference of thirty cubits; a measuring line of thirty cubits encircled it. 3 Beneath it were figures of oxen all around, ten per cubit, completely surrounding the Sea; the oxen were cast in two rows when the Sea itself was cast. 4 The Sea stood on twelve oxen: three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east, with the Sea resting on them and all their hindquarters turned inward. 5 It was a handbreadth thick, and its rim was shaped like the rim of a cup, like a lily blossom; it could hold three thousand baths.

The Basins, Lampstands, and Tables

6 He made ten basins for washing and set five on the right and five on the left. In them they washed the articles used for the burnt offering, but the Sea was for the priests to wash in. 7 He made ten golden lampstands according to their prescribed pattern and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left. 8 He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left, and he made one hundred golden bowls.

The Court, Pillars, and Bronze Vessels

9 He made the priests' courtyard, the great court, and doors for the court, and he overlaid their doors with bronze. 10 He set the Sea at the southeast corner, east of the south side. 11 Huram made the pots, shovels, and basins. Huram finished all the work he had done for King Solomon in the house of God. 12 He made the two pillars, the two bowl-shaped capitals on top of the pillars, and the two lattice networks covering the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars. 13 He also made four hundred pomegranates for the two networks—two rows of pomegranates on each network—to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars.

14 He made the stands and the basins on the stands. 15 He made one Sea with the twelve oxen beneath it. 16 The pots, shovels, meat forks, and all their articles that Huram-abi made for King Solomon for the house of the the LORD were of polished bronze. 17 The king cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zeredah. 18 Solomon made all these articles in such great abundance that the weight of the bronze could not be determined.

The Gold Furnishings of the Temple

19 Solomon also made all the furnishings for the house of God: the golden altar; the tables for the bread of the Presence; 20 the lampstands with their lamps to burn before the inner sanctuary, according to the ordinance, of pure gold; 21 the flowers, the lamps, and the tongs, all of pure gold, and of the finest gold; 22 the snuffers, basins, spoons, and censers, of pure gold; and the entrance of the house, with its inner doors to the Most Holy Place, and the doors of the temple, were of gold.
