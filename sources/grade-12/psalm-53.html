The Foolishness of the Godless and Israel's Hope

1 The fool declares in his heart, "There is no God." They are thoroughly corrupt; they have committed abominable deeds; no one does good.

2 God gazes down from heaven upon humankind to see whether anyone understands, whether anyone seeks God. 3 All have turned aside; together they have become corrupt; no one does good, not even one.

4 Will the evildoers never comprehend? They consume my people as though eating bread; they do not call upon God.

5 There they are, overwhelmed with dread where no dread existed. God has scattered the bones of those who besieged you; You have put them to shame, for God has rejected them.

6 If only salvation for Israel would come from Zion! When God restores the fortunes of His captive people, let Jacob rejoice; let Israel be glad.
