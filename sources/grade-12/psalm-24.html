The LORD's Ownership of the Earth

1 A psalm of David. The earth, together with everything that fills it, belongs to the LORD, as does the world and all who dwell within it. 2 For He laid its foundations upon the seas and secured it above the rivers.

The Character Required for Worship

3 Who may ascend the hill of the LORD, and who may stand in His holy sanctuary? 4 The one whose hands are clean and whose heart is pure, who has not devoted his soul to falsehood or sworn deceitfully. 5 He will receive a blessing from the LORD and righteousness from God his Savior. 6 Such is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek Your face, O God of Jacob. Selah.

The Arrival of the King of Glory

7 Lift up your heads, O gates, and be lifted up, O everlasting doors, so that the King of Glory may enter. 8 Who is this King of Glory? The LORD, strong and mighty; the LORD, mighty in battle.

9 Lift up your heads, O gates; lift them up, O everlasting doors, so that the King of Glory may enter. 10 Who is this King of Glory? The LORD of Hosts—He is the King of Glory. Selah.
