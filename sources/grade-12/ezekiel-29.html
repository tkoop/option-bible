Prophecy Against Pharaoh and Egypt

1 In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the word of the the LORD came to me: 2 “Son of man, oppose Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.”

3 Speak and say, “This is what the Lord the LORD says: Behold, I am against you, Pharaoh, king of Egypt, the great beast that lies among his rivers, who says, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it.’ 4 I will put hooks in your jaws and make the fish of your rivers cling to your scales. I will pull you out of your rivers, and all the fish of your rivers will cling to your scales. 5 I will cast you into the wilderness, you and all the fish of your rivers. You will fall on the open field; you will not be gathered or buried. I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the heavens. 6 Then all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am the the LORD, because they have been an unreliable support for the house of Israel. 7 When they took hold of you by the hand, you splintered and tore their shoulders; when they leaned on you, you broke and made their hips give way.”

Egypt Humbled and Scattered

8 Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: “Behold, I will bring the sword against you and cut off from you both people and animals. 9 The land of Egypt will become a desolation and a waste, and they will know that I am the the LORD, because he said, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it.’ 10 Therefore I am against you and against your rivers, and I will make the land of Egypt an utter waste and desolation, from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush. 11 No human foot or animal hoof will pass through it, and it will remain uninhabited for forty years. 12 I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolate lands, and its cities a desolation among ruined cities for forty years. I will scatter Egypt among the nations and disperse them among the countries.”

Egypt Restored but Humbled

13 For this is what the Lord the LORD says: “At the end of forty years I will gather Egypt from the peoples among whom they were scattered. 14 I will restore Egypt's fortunes and bring them back to the land of Pathros, their homeland, and there they will be a lowly kingdom. 15 It will be the lowliest of the kingdoms and will never again exalt itself above the nations; I will make them so small that they will not rule over the nations. 16 Egypt will no longer be a source of confidence for the house of Israel, a reminder of their guilt when they turn to it for help; then they will know that I am the Lord the LORD.”

Babylon's Reward and Israel's Renewal

17 In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me: 18 “Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made his army labor hard against Tyre; every head was worn bald and every shoulder rubbed bare; yet neither he nor his army received wages from Tyre for the labor they performed against it.”

19 Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: “Behold, I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. He will carry off its wealth, plunder its stores, and seize its goods; it will be wages for his army. 20 I have given him the land of Egypt as payment for his labor, because they served Me, declares the Lord the LORD. 21 On that day I will give new strength to the house of Israel, and I will give you a voice among them; then they will know that I am the the LORD.”
