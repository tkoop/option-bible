Eliphaz Challenges Job’s Innocence

1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: 2 “Can a mortal be of any use to God? Can even a wise man add to His prosperity? 3 Does the Almighty find pleasure in your righteousness? Does He gain anything when your ways are blameless? 4 Is it out of fear of you that He rebukes you, or that He brings you into judgment? 5 Is not your wickedness immense, and are not your iniquities without end?

6 For you have exacted pledges from your brothers without cause and stripped the naked of their clothing. 7 You have given no water to the weary and withheld bread from the hungry. 8 Yet the land belongs to the powerful, and the favored dwell in it. 9 You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the arms of the fatherless have been crushed.

10 Therefore snares surround you, and sudden terror overwhelms you; 11 or darkness, so that you cannot see, and a flood of water covers you.

God Sees All from the Heights of Heaven

12 Is not God in the heights of heaven? Look at the highest stars—how lofty they are!

13 Yet you say, “What does God know? Can He judge through the thick darkness? 14 Thick clouds veil Him, so that He does not see; He walks about on the vault of heaven.”

15 Will you keep to the ancient path that wicked men have trodden— 16 men who were snatched away before their time, whose foundations were washed away by a flood?

17 They said to God, “Depart from us,” and, “What can the Almighty do to us?”

18 Yet He filled their houses with good things. Far be it from me to embrace the counsel of the wicked. 19 The righteous see it and rejoice, and the innocent mock them,

20 saying, “Surely our adversaries are cut off, and fire has consumed their wealth.”

The Path to Restoration and Blessing

21 Reconcile now with Him and be at peace; thereby good will come to you. 22 Receive instruction from His mouth, and lay up His words in your heart.

23 If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored; if you banish iniquity from your tent, 24 and lay your gold in the dust—even the gold of Ophir among the stones of the brooks— 25 then the Almighty will be your gold and your precious silver.

26 For then you will delight yourself in the Almighty and lift up your face to God. 27 You will pray to Him, and He will hear you; you will fulfill your vows. 28 You will decide on a matter, and it will be established for you, and light will shine on your ways.

29 When men are brought low, you will say, “There is exaltation!” He will save the humble. 30 He will deliver the innocent; yes, they will be delivered by the purity of your hands.
