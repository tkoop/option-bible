The LORD Summons Moses at the Tent of Meeting

1 The LORD summoned Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting, saying, 2 “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: ‘When any of you brings an offering to the the LORD, you may bring your offering of livestock from the herd or the flock.

Burnt Offerings from the Herd

3 “‘If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he must present an unblemished male. He shall bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting, so that he may be accepted before the the LORD. 4 He shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted on his behalf to make atonement for him.

5 “‘He shall slaughter the young bull before the the LORD, and Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall present the blood and sprinkle it all around the altar at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 6 He shall skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces. 7 The sons of Aaron the priest shall kindle a fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire. 8 Then Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall arrange the pieces—including the head and the fat—on the burning wood that is on the altar. 9 He shall wash the entrails and the legs with water, and the priest shall burn all of it on the altar as a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD.

Burnt Offerings from the Flock

10 “‘If, however, his offering is a burnt offering from the flock—whether from the sheep or the goats—he must present an unblemished male. 11 He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the the LORD, and Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar. 12 He shall cut it into pieces, and the priest shall arrange them—including the head and the fat—on the burning wood that is on the altar. 13 He shall wash the entrails and the legs with water, and the priest shall bring all of it and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD.

Burnt Offerings of Birds

14 “‘If his offering to the the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he must present either a turtledove or a young pigeon. 15 The priest shall bring it to the altar, wring off its head, and burn it on the altar; its blood shall be drained out on the side of the altar. 16 He shall remove its crop with its feathers and throw it to the east side of the altar, to the place for ashes. 17 He shall tear it open by its wings without severing it completely, and the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD.’”
