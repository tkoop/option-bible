A Summons to Exultant Worship and Covenant Remembrance

1 Sing aloud to God, our strength; shout for joy to the God of Jacob! 2 Raise a song; sound the tambourine; play the sweet-sounding lyre together with the harp. 3 Blow the ram’s horn at the new moon, at the full moon, on the day of our feast. 4 For it is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob.

God’s Testimony of Deliverance and Covenant Appeal

5 He established it as a testimony for Joseph when He went out over the land of Egypt, where I heard a tongue I had not known:

6 “I relieved his shoulder of the burden; his hands were freed from the basket. 7 In your distress you called, and I rescued you; I answered you from the thundercloud; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah

8 “Hear, O My people, and I will testify against you—Israel, if only you would listen to Me! 9 There shall be no foreign god among you; you shall not bow down to a foreign god. 10 I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

11 “But My people would not listen to My voice; Israel refused to submit to Me. 12 So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts, to walk in their own counsels.

13 “If only My people would listen to Me, if Israel would walk in My ways, 14 how swiftly I would subdue their enemies and turn My hand against their foes! 15 Those who hate the LORD would feign obedience to Him, and their doom would last forever. 16 But I would feed you with the finest wheat and satisfy you with honey from the rock.”
