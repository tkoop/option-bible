A Psalm of David: Thanksgiving for the the LORD's Mercy

1 Bless the the LORD, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless His holy name. 2 Bless the the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all His benefits: 3 He forgives all your iniquities and heals all your diseases; 4 He redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with steadfast love and tender mercy; 5 He satisfies you with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's.

6 The The LORD executes righteousness and justice for all who are oppressed. 7 He made known His ways to Moses and His deeds to the children of Israel. 8 The The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. 9 He will not always accuse, nor will He harbor His anger forever. 10 He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our iniquities.

11 For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His steadfast love toward those who fear Him. 12 As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us. 13 As a father has compassion on his children, so the the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him. 14 For He knows how we are formed; He remembers that we are dust.

15 As for man, his days are like grass; he flourishes like a flower of the field. 16 When the wind passes over it, it is gone, and its place remembers it no more. 17 But the steadfast love of the the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon those who fear Him, and His righteousness to their children's children, 18 to those who keep His covenant and remember to do His commandments.

19 The The LORD has established His throne in heaven, and His kingdom rules over all. 20 Bless the the LORD, you His angels, mighty in strength, who do His word, obeying the voice of His word. 21 Bless the the LORD, all you His hosts, His servants who do His will. 22 Bless the the LORD, all His works, in all places of His dominion. Bless the the LORD, O my soul.
