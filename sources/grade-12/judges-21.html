Israel’s Oath and the People’s Grief

1 Now the Israelites had sworn at Mizpah, “No one among us shall give his daughter to Benjamin in marriage.”

2 The people went to Bethel and sat there before God until evening, lifting up their voices and weeping bitterly. 3 They said, “O the LORD, God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe is lacking in Israel?”

4 The next day the people rose early, built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Seeking Wives for Benjamin

5 Then the Israelites asked, “Which of all the tribes of Israel did not come up to the assembly to the the LORD?” For a solemn oath had been taken against anyone who did not come up to the the LORD at Mizpah: “He shall surely be put to death.”

6 And the Israelites had compassion on Benjamin their brother and said, “Today one tribe has been wiped out in Israel. 7 What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since we have sworn by the the LORD not to give them any of our daughters in marriage?”

8 So they asked, “Which one of the tribes of Israel did not come up to the the LORD at Mizpah?” And behold, no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp to the assembly. 9 For when the people were mustered, behold, none of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead was there.

The Destruction of Jabesh-gilead

10 So the congregation sent twelve thousand of their most valiant warriors there and commanded them, “Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the sword, including the women and children. 11 This is what you shall do: you shall utterly destroy every male and every woman who has had sexual relations with a man.”

12 And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young women who were virgins, who had never had sexual relations with a man; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan. 13 Then the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the Benjamites who were at the rock of Rimmon, and offered them peace. 14 And Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the women of Jabesh-gilead; but they were not enough for them. 15 And the people grieved for Benjamin, because the the LORD had caused a rift among the tribes of Israel.

Seeking Wives for the Remaining Benjamites

16 Then the elders of the congregation said, “What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?” 17 And they said, “There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe may not be wiped out from Israel.” 18 “But we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for the Israelites have sworn, saying, ‘Cursed is he who gives a wife to Benjamin.’”

19 So they said, “Behold, there is the annual feast of the the LORD in Shiloh, which is north of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.”

20 And they commanded the Benjamites, saying, “Go and lie in ambush in the vineyards. 21 Keep watch; if the daughters of Shiloh come out to take part in the dances, then come out of the vineyards and seize for yourselves each man a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin. 22 And when their fathers or brothers come to complain to us, we will say to them, ‘Be gracious to them, for we did not take a wife for each man in the war, and you did not give them wives, so you will not now be guilty.’”

23 And the Benjamites did so, and took wives for themselves from the women who were dancing, whom they carried off, according to their number; and they went and returned to their inheritance, and rebuilt the cities and dwelt in them. 24 And the Israelites departed from there at that time, every man to his tribe and family; and they went out from there, every man to his inheritance. 25 In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did as he saw fit.
