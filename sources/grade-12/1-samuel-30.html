The Amalekite Raid on Ziklag

1 On the third day, David and his men arrived at Ziklag, but the Amalekites had raided the Negev and Ziklag, attacking Ziklag and burning it to the ground. 2 They had taken captive the women there, young and old alike; they killed no one, but carried them off and went their way. 3 When David and his men reached the city, they found it burned, and their wives, sons, and daughters had been taken captive.

4 Then David and the people with him lifted up their voices and wept until they had no strength left to weep. 5 David's two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite, had been taken captive. 6 David was in great distress because the people were embittered and spoke of stoning him, for every man was bitter in soul over his sons and daughters; but David strengthened himself in the LORD his God.

David Seeks the LORD's Guidance

7 David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Bring me the ephod." So Abiathar brought the ephod to David. 8 Then David inquired of the LORD, "Shall I pursue this raiding band? Shall I overtake them?" And He answered, "Pursue them, for you will surely overtake them and rescue the captives."

9 So David set out with his six hundred men and came to the Brook Besor, where those who were left behind stayed. 10 Two hundred men were too exhausted to cross the brook, so David continued the pursuit with four hundred men.

The Pursuit and Defeat of the Amalekites

11 They found an Egyptian in the field and brought him to David. They gave him water to drink and bread to eat, 12 and they gave him a piece of a fig cake and two clusters of raisins. After he had eaten, his strength returned, for he had eaten no bread and drunk no water for three days and three nights.

13 David asked him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?"

He replied, "I am an Egyptian, the servant of an Amalekite. My master abandoned me three days ago because I became ill. 14 We raided the Negev of the Cherethites and the territory belonging to Judah and the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag."

15 David asked him, "Will you lead me down to this raiding band?"

He answered, "Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master, and I will lead you down to this band."

16 So he led him down, and there the Amalekites were spread out over the countryside, eating, drinking, and celebrating because of the great spoil they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from Judah.

17 David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day. Not one of them escaped, except four hundred young men who fled on camels.

18 David recovered everything the Amalekites had taken, including his two wives. 19 Nothing was missing, small or great, sons or daughters, or any of the plunder; David brought back everything.

20 David took all the flocks and herds, and his men drove them ahead of the other livestock, saying, "This is David's spoil."

Dividing the Spoil and Rewarding Judah

21 When David reached the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow him and had been left at the Brook Besor, they came out to meet him and the people with him. David approached them and greeted them.

22 But all the wicked and worthless men among those who had gone with David said, "Because they did not go with us, we will not give them any of the spoil we recovered, except each man's wife and children. Let them take them and go."

23 David replied, "My brothers, you shall not do this with what the LORD has given us. He has protected us and delivered into our hand the raiding band that came against us. 24 Who would listen to you in this matter? The share of the one who goes down to battle shall be the same as the share of the one who stays with the supplies; they shall share alike."

25 From that day forward, David made this a statute and ordinance for Israel, and it stands to this day.

26 When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the spoil to the elders of Judah, his friends, saying, "Here is a gift for you from the spoil of the LORD's enemies." 27 He sent it to those in Bethel, Ramoth of the Negev, Jattir, 28 Aroer, Siphmoth, Eshtemoa, 29 Racal, the cities of the Jerahmeelites, the cities of the Kenites, 30 Hormah, Bor-ashan, Athach, 31 Hebron, and to all the places where David and his men had roamed.
