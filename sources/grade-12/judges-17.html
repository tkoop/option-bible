Micah and His Mother's Dedicated Silver

1 A man named Micah resided in the hill country of Ephraim. 2 He said to his mother, "The eleven hundred shekels of silver that were taken from you, and concerning which you pronounced a curse—even declaring within my hearing, 'Look, the silver is with me; I took it'—well, I have the silver; it is here."

His mother responded, "Blessed be my son by the LORD."

3 He restored the eleven hundred shekels to his mother, whereupon she declared, "I had solemnly dedicated this silver to the LORD from my own hand for my son, to fashion a carved image and a molten image; therefore, I now restore it to you."

4 Accordingly, he returned the silver to his mother. She took two hundred shekels from it and gave them to a silversmith, who fashioned a carved image and a molten image; these were then installed in Micah's house.

Micah's Household Shrine and Priest

5 Now this man Micah possessed a house of gods. He fashioned an ephod and teraphim, and he consecrated one of his sons, who served as his priest.

6 In those days Israel lacked a king, so each man did what was right in his own eyes.

A Wandering Levite Enters Micah's Service

7 A young Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, belonging to the clan of Judah, was sojourning there.

8 The man departed from Bethlehem in Judah to sojourn wherever he might secure a livelihood, and he journeyed as far as the hill country of Ephraim, reaching Micah's house.

9 Micah inquired, "Where do you come from?"

The Levite answered, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am traveling to sojourn wherever I may find opportunity."

10 Micah said, "Remain with me and serve as father and priest to me, and I will give you ten shekels of silver per annum, a suit of garments, and your sustenance." Thus the Levite accompanied him.

11 The Levite consented to dwell with the man, and the young man became as one of his sons.

12 Micah consecrated the Levite; the young man became his priest and remained in Micah's household.

13 Then Micah declared, "Now I know that the LORD will prosper me, because I have a Levite serving as my priest."
