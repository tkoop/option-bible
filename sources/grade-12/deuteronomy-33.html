Moses Pronounces His Final Blessing

1 This is the blessing that Moses, the man of God, pronounced over the Israelites before his death.

2 He said: “The LORD came from Sinai and dawned upon us from Seir; He shone forth from Mount Paran and came with myriads of holy ones, with flaming fire at His right hand. 3 Surely You love the peoples; all Your holy ones are in Your hand, and they sit at Your feet, receiving Your words. 4 Moses gave us the law as a possession for the assembly of Jacob. 5 So He became King in Jeshurun when the leaders of the people assembled, when the tribes of Israel came together.”

Blessings for Reuben, Judah, and Levi

6 “Let Reuben live and not die, nor his men be few.”

7 And this he said of Judah: “Hear, O the LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. With his hands he contends for them, and You shall be a help against his foes.”

8 And of Levi he said: “Your Thummim and Urim belong to Your godly one, whom You tested at Massah and contended with at the waters of Meribah, 9 who said of his father and mother,”

“I do not consider them.”

“He disregarded his brothers and did not acknowledge his own children. For they obeyed Your word and kept Your covenant. 10 They will teach Your ordinances to Jacob and Your law to Israel. They will set incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar. 11 Bless, O the LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands. Strike the loins of those who rise against him and of his foes, so they rise no more.”

12 Of Benjamin he said: “The beloved of the LORD rests secure in Him; He shields him all day long, and he rests between His shoulders.”

Joseph's Abundant Inheritance

13 And of Joseph he said: “May his land be blessed by the LORD with the precious dew from heaven and the deep waters beneath, 14 with the bountiful harvest of the sun and the rich yield of the months, 15 with the best of the ancient mountains and the bounty of the everlasting hills, 16 with the choice gifts of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of Him who dwelt in the burning bush. May these rest on the head of Joseph, on the brow of the prince among his brothers. 17 In majesty he is like a firstborn bull; his horns are the horns of a wild ox; with them he will gore the nations, even to the ends of the earth. Such are the myriads of Ephraim, and such are the thousands of Manasseh.”

Blessings for the Remaining Tribes and Israel's Security

18 And of Zebulun he said: “Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents. 19 They will summon peoples to the mountain; there they will offer sacrifices of righteousness, for they will feast on the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand.”

20 And of Gad he said: “Blessed is He who enlarges Gad! He dwells as a lion and tears off arm and scalp. 21 He chose the best land for himself, for there a commander's portion was reserved. He came with the leaders of the people; he carried out the LORD's justice and His ordinances for Israel.”

22 And of Dan he said: “Dan is a lion's cub, leaping out of Bashan.”

23 And of Naphtali he said: “Naphtali is abounding with favor, full of the blessing of the LORD; he will take possession of the sea and the south.”

24 And of Asher he said: “Most blessed of sons is Asher; let him be favored by his brothers, and let him dip his foot in oil. 25 Your bars shall be iron and bronze, and your strength shall match your days.”

26 “There is no one like the God of Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to your help, and in His majesty through the skies. 27 The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He will drive out the enemy before you, saying,”

“Destroy them!”

28 “So Israel will dwell in safety; Jacob will dwell secure in a land of grain and new wine, where the heavens drop down dew. 29 Blessed are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? He is the shield that protects you, the sword that exalts you. Your enemies will cower before you, and you will trample their high places.”
