Psalm 60: A Lament After Defeat and a Promise of Victory

1 For the choir director. To the tune of “Lily of the Covenant.” A Miktam of David for instruction. When he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down twelve thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt. O God, You have rejected us; You have shattered us; You have been incensed; restore us!

2 You have shaken the earth and torn it open; heal its breaches, for it is tottering.

3 You have made Your people experience affliction; You have given us a wine that makes us reel.

4 You have given a banner to those who fear You, to be raised for the sake of truth. Selah

5 Save us by Your right hand and answer us, that those You love may be rescued.

God's Oracle of Victory

6 God has spoken in His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth.

7 “Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter.

8 “Moab is My washbasin; I claim Edom as My possession; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”

A Prayer for Deliverance in Battle

9 Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?

10 Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You not march forth with our armies?

11 Grant us help in our distress, for human help is futile.

12 With God we will act valiantly, and He will trample our enemies.
