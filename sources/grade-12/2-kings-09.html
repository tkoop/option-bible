Jehu Is Anointed King of Israel

1 Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Prepare yourself and take this flask of oil in your hand, then go to Ramoth-gilead. 2 When you arrive there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Then go in, get him away from his companions, and take him into an inner room. 3 Then take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and say, 'This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee without lingering. 4 So the young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead.

5 When he arrived, the commanders of the army were sitting there. He said, "I have a message for you, commander."

Jehu asked, "For which of us?"

He replied, "For you, commander."

6 Then Jehu got up and went into the house. The prophet poured the oil on his head and declared, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'I anoint you king over the people of the LORD, over Israel.'"

7 He continued, "You are to strike down the house of Ahab, your master, so that I may avenge the blood of My servants the prophets and of all the LORD's servants shed by Jezebel. 8 The whole house of Ahab will perish, and I will cut off from Ahab every male, both slave and free, in Israel. 9 I will make the house of Ahab like the houses of Jeroboam son of Nebat and Baasha son of Ahijah. 10 And the dogs will devour Jezebel in the plot of ground at Jezreel, and no one will bury her." Then he opened the door and fled.

Jehu's Officers Declare Him King

11 When Jehu went out to his master's servants, they asked him, "Is everything all right? Why did this madman come to you?"

He said to them, "You know the man and his talk."

12 "That's a lie," they said. "Tell us now."

So Jehu said, "He told me, 'This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.'"

13 Then each man quickly took his garment and put it under Jehu on the bare steps, blew the trumpet, and proclaimed, "Jehu is king!"

Jehu Rides to Jezreel and Kills Joram

14 Thus Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Now Joram and all Israel had been defending Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram. 15 But King Joram had returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds the Arameans had inflicted on him in his battle with Hazael king of Aram. So Jehu said, "If you are willing, let no fugitive leave the city to go and report in Jezreel."

16 Then Jehu mounted his chariot and went to Jezreel, because Joram was lying there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had gone down to see Joram.

17 When the watchman standing on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu's troops approaching, he called out, "I see a company!"

Joram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them, and have him ask, 'Is it peace?'"

18 So a horseman rode off to meet Jehu and said, "This is what the king says: 'Is it peace?'"

Jehu replied, "What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me."

The watchman reported, "The messenger reached them, but he is not coming back."

19 Then the king sent a second horseman, who went to them and said, "This is what the king says: 'Is it peace?'"

And Jehu answered, "What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me."

20 Again the watchman reported, "He reached them, but he is not coming back. And the driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi—he drives like a madman."

21 Then Joram said, "Hitch up my chariot." So they hitched it up, and Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah rode out, each in his own chariot, to meet Jehu. They met him in the field of Naboth the Jezreelite.

22 When Joram saw Jehu, he asked, "Is it peace, Jehu?"

Jehu replied, "How can there be peace as long as the idolatry and witchcraft of your mother Jezebel abound?"

23 Joram turned his chariot and fled, calling to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

24 Then Jehu drew his bow and shot Joram between the shoulders. The arrow pierced his heart, and he collapsed in his chariot.

25 Then Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, "Pick him up and throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember that when you and I were riding side by side behind his father Ahab, the LORD lifted up this prophecy against him: 26 'As surely as I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday,' declares the LORD, 'I will repay you in this very field,' declares the LORD. Now, therefore, pick him up and throw him into the field, in accordance with the word of the LORD."

27 When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by the road to Beth-haggan. Jehu pursued him, shouting, "Shoot him too!" So they shot him in his chariot on the ascent to Gur, near Ibleam, and he fled to Megiddo and died there.

28 Then his servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him with his fathers in his tomb in the City of David. 29 In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah had become king over Judah.

Jezebel's Death

30 When Jehu came to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it. She painted her eyes, arranged her hair, and looked out of a window.

31 As Jehu entered the gate, she called out, "Is it peace, Zimri, you murderer of your master?"

32 He looked up at the window and called, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three eunuchs looked down at him.

33 "Throw her down!" he said. So they threw her down, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and Jehu trampled her underfoot.

34 Then Jehu went in and ate and drank. Afterward he said, "Attend to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter." 35 But when they went to bury her, they found nothing but her skull, her feet, and the palms of her hands.

36 They returned and told Jehu, who said, "This is the word of the LORD that He spoke through His servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'In the plot of ground at Jezreel the dogs will devour the flesh of Jezebel. 37 Jezebel's corpse will be like dung on the surface of the field in the plot at Jezreel, so that no one will say, "This is Jezebel."'"
