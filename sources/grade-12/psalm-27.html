A Psalm of David: Confidence in the LORD

1 The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the fortress of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? 2 When evildoers advance to devour my flesh, my adversaries and my foes stumble and fall. 3 Though an army encamp against me, my heart will not fear; though war erupt against me, in this I trust.

4 One thing I have asked of the LORD, and this I seek: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD and to seek Him in His sanctuary. 5 For in the day of adversity He will conceal me in His shelter; He will hide me in the secret place of His tent; He will set me high upon a rock. 6 Then my head will be exalted above my enemies on every side. In His tent I will offer sacrifices with joyful shouts; I will sing and make music to the LORD.

7 Hear my voice when I call, the LORD; be gracious to me and answer me. 8 My heart says of You, "Seek His face." Your face, the LORD, I will seek. 9 Do not avert Your face from me; do not reject Your servant in anger; You have been my help. Do not abandon or forsake me, O God my Savior.

10 Though my father and mother forsake me, the LORD will receive me. 11 Teach me Your way, the LORD, and lead me on level ground because of my adversaries.

12 Do not surrender me to the desire of my foes, for false witnesses have risen against me, uttering violent threats. 13 I am confident that I will see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living. 14 Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; wait for the LORD.
