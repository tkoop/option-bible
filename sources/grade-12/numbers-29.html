The Festival of Trumpets

1On the first day of the seventh month, you shall have a sacred assembly. You shall not do any regular work. It is a day for you to sound the trumpets. 2As a pleasing aroma to the the LORD, you shall offer one burnt offering: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, without blemish. 3Its grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 4and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 5Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you. 6In addition to the monthly burnt offering and its grain offering, and the daily burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings, according to their ordinance, these shall be offered as a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the the LORD.

The Day of Atonement

7On the tenth day of this seventh month, you shall have a sacred assembly. You must deny yourselves; you shall do no work. 8You shall offer as a pleasing aroma to the the LORD one burnt offering: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, without blemish. 9Its grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 10and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 11Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the sin offering of atonement, the continual burnt offering, and its grain offering and drink offerings.

The Festival of Tabernacles

12On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you shall have a sacred assembly; you shall not do any regular work. You shall celebrate a feast to the the LORD for seven days. 13You shall offer as an offering made by fire, a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish. 14Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths for each of the two rams, 15and one-tenth for each of the fourteen lambs. 16Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

17On the second day, you shall offer twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 18with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 19Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings.

20On the third day, you shall offer eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 21with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 22Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

23On the fourth day, you shall offer ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 24with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 25Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

26On the fifth day, you shall offer nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 27with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 28Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

29On the sixth day, you shall offer eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 30with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 31Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings.

32On the seventh day, you shall offer seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, without blemish, 33with the grain offering and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to their number, as prescribed. 34Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

The Solemn Assembly

35On the eighth day, you shall hold a solemn assembly; you shall do no regular work. 36You shall offer as an offering made by fire, a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, without blemish. 37Their grain offering and drink offerings shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 38Also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

39These you shall offer to the the LORD at your appointed feasts, in addition to your vows and freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings, and peace offerings. 40Moses told the Israelites everything the the LORD had commanded him.
