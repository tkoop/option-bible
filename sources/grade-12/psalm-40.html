For the choirmaster. A psalm of David.

Praise for the LORD's Deliverance

1 I waited patiently for the LORD, and He inclined to me and heard my cry. 2 He drew me up from the pit of destruction, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, steadying my steps. 3 He placed a new song in my mouth—a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and stand in awe, and they will trust in the LORD.

4 Blessed is the man who makes the LORD his trust and does not turn to the arrogant or to those who stray after falsehood. 5 Many are the wonders You have performed, O the LORD my God, and the plans You have devised for us are beyond reckoning; if I tried to proclaim and recount them, they would exceed all number.

Obedience Greater Than Sacrifice

6 Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but You have opened my ears to hear Your will; burnt offering and sin offering You did not require. 7 Then I said, "Here I am; I have come, as it is written of me in the scroll of the book." 8 I delight to do Your will, O my God; Your law is written within my heart.

9 I have proclaimed righteousness in the great assembly; I have not held back my lips, as You know, O the LORD. 10 I have not hidden Your righteousness in my heart; I have spoken of Your faithfulness and Your salvation; I have not concealed Your steadfast love and Your truth from the great assembly. 11 You, O the LORD, will not withhold Your compassion from me; Your steadfast love and truth will continually guard me.

A Cry for Help Amid Overwhelming Trouble

12 For evils beyond number have surrounded me; my iniquities have overtaken me, until I cannot see; they outnumber the hairs of my head, and my heart has failed me. 13 Be pleased, O the LORD, to deliver me; hurry, O the LORD, to help me.

14 May those who seek my life be ashamed and dismayed together; may those who desire my ruin be turned back and disgraced. 15 May they be appalled because of their shame—those who say to me, "Aha! Aha!"

16 May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation say continually, "The LORD be magnified!" 17 But I am poor and needy; may the Lord consider me. You are my help and my deliverer; do not delay, O my God.
