Oracle Against Moab

1 An oracle concerning Moab: Ar is laid waste and silenced in a single night; Kir is laid waste and silenced in a single night.

2 Dibon ascends to its high places to weep; over Nebo and Medeba Moab wails. Every head is shaved, and every beard is cut off. 3 In its streets they wear sackcloth; on its rooftops and in its squares everyone wails, collapsing in tears.

The Prophet's Lament

4 Heshbon and Elealeh cry out; their voices are heard as far as Jahaz. Therefore Moab's warriors cry aloud, and their souls tremble within them. 5 My heart cries out for Moab; her fugitives flee to Zoar, to Eglath-shelishiyah. They ascend the slope of Luhith weeping, and on the road to Horonaim they raise a cry of destruction.

6 The waters of Nimrim run dry; the grass withers, the tender growth dies, and no greenery remains. 7 Therefore they carry away the abundance they have gained and their stored-up treasures across the Brook of the Willows.

8 For the cry has encircled the borders of Moab; her wailing reaches Eglaim, and her wailing reaches Beer-elim.

Final Judgment on Moab

9 The waters of Dimon are full of blood; yet I will bring still more upon Dimon—a lion against the fugitives of Moab and against the remnant of the land.
