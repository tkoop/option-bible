Counsel Against Envy and Violent Scheming

1 Do not be envious of evildoers, nor desire to consort with them. 2 For their hearts devise violence, and their lips give voice to trouble. 3 By wisdom a house is built, and by understanding it is established; 4 by knowledge its chambers are filled with every precious and pleasant treasure. 5 A wise man is strong, and a man of knowledge fortifies his power, 6 for by wise counsel you shall wage war, and in an abundance of counselors there is victory. 7 Wisdom is too lofty for a fool, so he does not open his mouth in the gate. 8 He who plots to do evil will be called a schemer, 9 and the scheme of folly is sin, while the scoffer is an abomination to mankind.

Deliverance, Strength, and Wisdom's Reward

10 If you falter in the day of distress, your strength is small. 11 Rescue those being led away to death, and hold back those staggering toward slaughter. 12 If you say, "See, we did not know this," does not He who weighs the hearts perceive it? He who keeps your soul—does He not know? And will He not repay each person according to his deeds?

13 My son, eat honey, for it is good; yes, the honeycomb is sweet upon your tongue. 14 Know that wisdom is like this for your soul: if you find it, there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.

Righteousness, the Fear of the LORD, and Impartial Judgment

15 Do not lie in wait, O wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous, and do not ravage his resting place, 16 for a righteous man falls seven times and rises again, whereas the wicked are brought down by calamity. 17 Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, and do not let your heart be glad when he stumbles, 18 lest the LORD see it and be displeased, and turn away His wrath from him. 19 Do not fret because of evildoers, nor be envious of the wicked, 20 for the evil man has no future, and the lamp of the wicked will be extinguished.

21 My son, fear the LORD and the king, and do not associate with those given to change, 22 for their calamity will arise suddenly, and who knows the ruin that both will bring?

23 These also are sayings of the wise: to show partiality in judgment is not good. 24 He who says to the wicked, "You are righteous," peoples will curse him, and nations will abhor him; 25 but to those who rebuke the wicked will be delight, and a good blessing will come upon them. 26 He who gives a right answer kisses the lips.

27 Prepare your work outside and make it ready for yourself in the field; afterward, then build your house. 28 Do not be a witness against your neighbor without cause, and do not deceive with your lips.

29 Do not say, "I will do to him as he has done to me; I will repay the man according to his deeds."

The Sluggard's Ruined Field

30 I passed by the field of the sluggard and by the vineyard of the man lacking sense, 31 and behold, it was all overgrown with thorns; nettles had covered its surface, and its stone wall was broken down. 32 When I saw it, I reflected upon it; I looked, and received instruction:

33 A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, 34 and your poverty will come like a robber, and your want like an armed man.
