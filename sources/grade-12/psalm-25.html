A Prayer of David: Seeking Guidance, Mercy, and Deliverance

1 To You, O the LORD, I lift up my soul. 2 In You, my God, I trust; let me not be put to shame, and let not my enemies triumph over me. 3 Indeed, none who wait for You will be put to shame; but those who act treacherously without cause will be ashamed.

4 Make known to me Your ways, O the LORD; teach me Your paths. 5 Lead me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You.

6 Remember Your compassion, O the LORD, and Your steadfast love, for they have been from of old. 7 Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions; according to Your steadfast love remember me, for the sake of Your goodness, O the LORD.

8 Good and upright is the the LORD; therefore He instructs sinners in the way. 9 He leads the humble in justice and teaches the humble His way. 10 All the paths of the the LORD are steadfast love and faithfulness to those who keep His covenant and His testimonies.

11 For Your name's sake, O the LORD, pardon my guilt, for it is great. 12 Who is the man who fears the the LORD? He will instruct him in the way He chooses. 13 His soul will abide in prosperity, and his offspring will inherit the land. 14 The LORD confides in those who fear Him, and He makes known to them His covenant.

15 My eyes are ever toward the the LORD, for He will pluck my feet out of the net. 16 Turn to me and be gracious to me, for I am lonely and afflicted. 17 The troubles of my heart have enlarged; bring me out of my distresses.

18 Look upon my affliction and my trouble, and forgive all my sins. 19 Consider how many are my foes, and with what violent hatred they hate me. 20 Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You.

21 May integrity and uprightness preserve me, for I wait for You. 22 Redeem Israel, O God, from all his troubles.
