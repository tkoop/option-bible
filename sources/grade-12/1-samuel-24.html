Saul Pursues David in the Wilderness of En-gedi

1 When Saul returned from his pursuit of the Philistines, he was informed, “Behold, David is in the wilderness of En-gedi.” 2 Saul selected three thousand choice men from all Israel and set out to seek David and his men among the crags of the wild goats.

David Spares Saul in the Cave

3 He came to the sheepfolds along the road; there was a cave there, and Saul entered it to relieve himself. David and his men, meanwhile, were sitting in the innermost recesses of the cave. 4 David’s men said to him, “Behold, this is the day of which the LORD said to you, ‘Behold, I am giving your enemy into your hand, and you shall do to him as seems good in your eyes.’” David rose and covertly cut off the corner of Saul’s robe. 5 Afterward David’s conscience smote him because he had cut off the corner of Saul’s robe.

6 He said to his men, “The LORD forbid that I should do this thing to my lord, the LORD’s anointed, or stretch out my hand against him, since he is the LORD’s anointed.” 7 With these words David restrained his men and did not allow them to rise against Saul. Then Saul rose from the cave and went on his way.

David Confronts Saul after He Leaves the Cave

8 Afterward David arose, left the cave, and called after Saul, saying, “My lord the king.” When Saul looked behind him, David bowed his face to the ground and prostrated himself. 9 David said to Saul, “Why do you heed the words of men who say, ‘David seeks your harm’? 10 Behold, this day your eyes have seen how the LORD delivered you into my hand in the cave; and though someone urged me to kill you, I spared you and said, ‘I will not stretch out my hand against my lord, for he is the LORD’s anointed.’ 11 And see, my father, see the corner of your robe in my hand; for by cutting off the corner of your robe I did not kill you. Know and see that there is no evil or transgression in my hand, and I have not sinned against you, yet you hunt my life to take it. 12 May the LORD judge between me and you, and may the LORD avenge me on you; but my hand shall not be against you. 13 As the proverb of the ancients says, ‘Out of the wicked comes forth wickedness’; but my hand shall not be against you. 14 After whom has the king of Israel come out? After whom do you pursue? After a dead dog? After a flea? 15 May the LORD therefore be judge, and judge between me and you, and see and plead my cause, and deliver me out of your hand.”

Saul Acknowledges David’s Righteousness

16 When David had finished speaking these words to Saul, Saul said, “Is this your voice, my son David?” And Saul lifted up his voice and wept. 17 He said to David, “You are more righteous than I, for you have repaid me with good, whereas I have repaid you with evil. 18 You have declared this day how you have dealt well with me, in that when the LORD gave me into your hand you did not kill me. 19 For if a man finds his enemy, will he let him go away unharmed? And may the LORD reward you with good for what you have done to me this day. 20 And now, behold, I know that you shall surely be king, and that the kingdom of Israel shall be established in your hand. 21 Swear to me now by the LORD that you will not cut off my descendants after me and that you will not destroy my name from my father’s house.”

22 David swore to Saul. Saul went to his house, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.
