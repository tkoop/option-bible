Hosea's Command to Love Again

1 The LORD said to me, "Go, love a woman who is loved by another and is an adulteress, just as the LORD loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and delight in cakes offered to idols." 2 So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a half of barley.

3 Then I told her, "You must live with me for many days. You must not be promiscuous, and you must not belong to any other man; and I will be faithful to you."

Israel's Future Restoration

4 For the Israelites will live for many days without a king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred pillar, without an ephod or household gods. 5 Afterward the Israelites will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. They will come trembling to the LORD and to His goodness in the last days.
