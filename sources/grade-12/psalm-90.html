God's Eternal Refuge and the Frailty of Mortals

1 Lord, You have been our dwelling place throughout every generation. 2 Before the mountains were born, before You brought forth the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting You are God. 3 You return mortals to dust and say, “Return, O children of men.” 4 For in Your sight a thousand years are like a day that has already passed, or like a watch in the night. 5 You sweep them away; they sleep. They are like grass that springs up: 6 in the morning it blossoms and fades, but by evening it is cut down and withers.

The Weight of Divine Wrath upon Human Sin

7 For we are consumed by Your anger, and by Your wrath we are dismayed. 8 You have set our iniquities before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence. 9 For all our days pass away under Your wrath; we bring our years to an end like a sigh. 10 The days of our lives are seventy years, or eighty if we are strong; yet their best is toil and sorrow, for they pass quickly, and we fly away. 11 Who can comprehend the power of Your anger? Your wrath is as great as the reverence You deserve.

A Prayer for Wisdom, Mercy, and Lasting Joy

12 So teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom. 13 Return, the LORD—how long?—and have compassion on Your servants. 14 Satisfy us in the morning with Your lovingkindness, that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days. 15 Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for the years we have seen evil. 16 Let Your work appear to Your servants, and Your splendor to their children. 17 May the favor of the Lord our God be upon us; establish the work of our hands for us—yes, establish the work of our hands.
