Submission to Governing Authorities

1 Let every soul be subject to the governing authorities, for no authority exists except from God, and those that are constituted have been ordained by God. 2 Consequently, whoever resists authority opposes the divine ordinance, and those who resist will incur judgment against themselves.

3 For rulers are not a terror to good conduct but to evil. Do you desire to be without fear of authority? Do what is good, and you will receive its approval. 4 For the authority is God’s servant for your good; but if you do evil, be afraid, for it does not bear the sword in vain. It is God’s servant, an avenger executing wrath upon the evildoer.

5 Therefore necessity compels submission, not merely to escape wrath but also for conscience’ sake. 6 For this reason you also render tribute, for the authorities are God’s public servants, attending diligently to this very office. 7 Render to all their dues: taxes to whom taxes are due, customs to whom customs are due, fear to whom fear is due, and honor to whom honor is due.

Love as the Fulfillment of the Law

8 Owe nothing to anyone except to love one another, for whoever loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law. 9 For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery, you shall not murder, you shall not steal, you shall not covet,” and any other commandment, are summed up in this word: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.” 10 Love works no ill to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.

Awakening to the Nearness of Salvation

11 And do this, knowing the season, that it is already time for you to rouse yourselves from sleep, for our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed. 12 The night has advanced, and the day has drawn near. Let us therefore lay aside the works of darkness and clothe ourselves with the armor of light. 13 Let us conduct ourselves honorably as in daylight: not in carousing and drunkenness, not in lewdness and debauchery, not in contention and envy. 14 But clothe yourselves with the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh to fulfill its lusts.
