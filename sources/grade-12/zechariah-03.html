Joshua the High Priest before the Angel

1Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the the LORD, with Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him.

The Accuser Rebuked

2And the LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan! Indeed, the the LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you! Is this not a man rescued from destruction?”

The Priest Cleansed and Charged

3Now Joshua was clothed in filthy garments as he stood before the Angel. 4So He answered and spoke to those standing before Him, saying, “Remove the filthy garments from him.” Then He said to Joshua, “See, I have removed your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you in rich robes.”

5Then I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” So they put a clean turban on his head and clothed him, while the Angel of the the LORD stood by.

The Branch and the Stone

6And the Angel of the the LORD gave this charge to Joshua: 7“This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: ‘If you walk in My ways and keep My charge, then you will govern My house and keep My courts, and I will give you a place among these who are standing here. 8Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, you and your friends seated before you, who are a sign: For behold, I am going to bring My Servant, the Branch. 9For behold, the stone that I have set before Joshua: on one stone are seven eyes. Behold, I will engrave its inscription,’ declares the the LORD of Hosts, ‘and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day. 10On that day,’ declares the the LORD of Hosts, ‘you will invite one another to sit under your vine and under your fig tree.’”
