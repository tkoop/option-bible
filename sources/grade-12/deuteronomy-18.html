Provision for the Priests and Levites

1 The Levitical priests—that is, the whole tribe of Levi—shall have no land allotment or inheritance among the Israelites. They shall eat the offerings made by fire to the the LORD and His inheritance. 2 They shall have no inheritance among their brothers; the the LORD is their inheritance, just as He promised them.

3 This shall be the priests’ prescribed share from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether an ox or a sheep: they shall give to the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the stomach. 4 The firstfruits of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, and the first wool sheared from your flock, you shall give to him. 5 For the the LORD your God has chosen him and his sons from among all your tribes to stand and serve the the LORD all their days.

6 If a Levite comes from one of your towns, from anywhere in Israel where he has been sojourning, and comes with all his heart’s desire to the place that the the LORD will choose, 7 then he shall minister in the name of the the LORD his God, like all his fellow Levites who stand there before the the LORD. 8 They shall eat equal portions, apart from what comes from the sale of his ancestral property.

Forbidden Practices of the Nations

9 When you enter the land that the the LORD your God is giving you, you must not learn to imitate the abominations of those nations. 10 No one among you shall be found who sacrifices his son or daughter in the fire, or who practices divination, soothsaying, enchantment, or witchcraft, 11 or who casts spells, or consults a medium or spiritist, or seeks guidance from the dead.

12 For everyone who does these things is an abomination to the the LORD, and because of these abominations the the LORD your God is driving them out before you. 13 You shall be blameless before the the LORD your God. 14 For these nations that you are dispossessing listen to soothsayers and diviners, but as for you, the the LORD your God has not permitted you to do so.

The Promised Prophet

15 The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers; you shall listen to him.

16 This is in accordance with all that you asked of the the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, “Let me not hear again the voice of the the LORD my God, nor see this great fire any longer, lest I die.”

17 And the the LORD said to me, “They have spoken well.”

18 I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers, and I will put My words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him. 19 And whoever does not listen to My words that he speaks in My name, I Myself will require it of him. 20 But the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods—that prophet shall die.

21 And if you say in your heart, “How shall we know the word that the the LORD has not spoken?” 22 When a prophet speaks in the name of the the LORD, if the thing does not happen or come to pass, that is the word that the the LORD has not spoken; the prophet has spoken it presumptuously. You shall not be afraid of him.
