Miracles of Healing and Authority

1 After Jesus descended from the mountain, great crowds followed Him. 2 A leper approached, knelt before Him, and said, "Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean."

3 Jesus stretched out His hand, touched the man, and said, "I am willing; be cleansed." Immediately the leprosy left him, and he was cleansed. 4 Jesus told him, "See that you tell no one; instead, go and show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift Moses prescribed, as a testimony to them."

5 When Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion came to Him, pleading with Him. 6 He said, "Lord, my servant lies paralyzed at home, suffering terribly."

7 Jesus said to him, "I will come and heal him."

8 The centurion replied, "Lord, I am not worthy for You to come under my roof; but simply speak the word, and my servant will be healed. 9 For I myself am a man under authority, with soldiers under me; I tell this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

10 When Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those following, "Truly I tell you, I have not found such great faith in anyone in Israel. 11 I tell you that many will come from east and west and recline with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven. 12 But the sons of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

13 Jesus said to the centurion, "Go; as you have believed, let it be done for you." And his servant was healed at that very hour.

14 When Jesus came into Peter's house, He saw Peter's mother-in-law lying ill with a fever. 15 He touched her hand, the fever left her, and she rose and began to serve Him.

16 That evening they brought to Him many who were demon-possessed; He drove out the spirits with a word and healed all who were sick. 17 This happened so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled: "He Himself took our infirmities and carried our diseases."

The Cost of Discipleship

18 When Jesus saw the crowd around Him, He gave orders to cross to the other side. 19 A scribe approached and said to Him, "Teacher, I will follow You wherever You go."

20 Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens and birds of the air have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay His head."

21 Another disciple said to Him, "Lord, first let me go and bury my father."

22 Jesus said to him, "Follow Me, and let the dead bury their own dead."

Jesus Calms the Storm

23 When He got into the boat, His disciples followed Him. 24 Suddenly a violent storm arose on the sea, so that the boat was being swamped by the waves; yet He was asleep. 25 The disciples came and woke Him, saying, "Lord, save us! We are perishing!"

26 He said to them, "Why are you afraid, O you of little faith?" Then He rose and rebuked the winds and the sea, and a great calm followed.

27 The men were amazed and said, "What kind of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey Him?"

Deliverance in the Country of the Gadarenes

28 When He reached the other side, in the country of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men met Him as they came out of the tombs; they were so violent that no one could pass along that road. 29 They cried out, "What do You want with us, Son of God? Have You come here to torment us before the appointed time?"

30 Now a large herd of pigs was feeding at some distance from them. 31 The demons begged Him, saying, "If You drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs."

32 He said to them, "Go!" They came out and entered the pigs, and the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the water. 33 The herdsmen fled, went into the city, and reported everything, including what had happened to the demon-possessed men. 34 Then the whole city came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw Him, they begged Him to leave their region.
