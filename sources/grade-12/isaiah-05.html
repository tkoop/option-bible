The Song of the Vineyard

1 Let me sing for my beloved a song of my beloved concerning His vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a very fertile hill. 2 He dug it and cleared it of stones, and planted it with choice vines; He built a watchtower in its midst, and hewed out a wine vat in it. He expected it to yield grapes, but it yielded only worthless, sour fruit. 3 And now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem and men of Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard. 4 What more could have been done for My vineyard that I did not do in it? When I expected grapes, why did it yield worthless fruit? 5 Now I will tell you what I am going to do to My vineyard: I will remove its hedge, and it shall be devoured; I will break down its wall, and it shall be trampled. 6 I will make it a wasteland; it shall not be pruned or hoed, and briers and thorns shall grow up. I will also command the clouds to withhold rain from it. 7 For the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are His pleasant planting. He expected justice, but found bloodshed; righteousness, but found an outcry!

Woes upon the Oppressors

8 Woe to those who accumulate house after house and field after field, until no space remains, so that you are left to dwell alone in the land! 9 The LORD of hosts has sworn in my hearing: "Surely many houses shall become desolate, great and beautiful houses, without inhabitant. 10 For ten acres of vineyard will yield only a bath of wine, and a homer of seed will yield only an ephah of grain."

Woes upon the Reckless and the Unjust

11 Woe to those who rise early in the morning to pursue strong drink, who linger late into the evening until wine inflames them! 12 They have lyre and harp, tambourine and flute and wine at their feasts, but they do not regard the work of the LORD, nor do they see the deeds of His hands. 13 Therefore My people go into exile for lack of knowledge; their honored men go hungry, and their multitude is parched with thirst. 14 Therefore Sheol has enlarged its appetite and opened its mouth without limit; down go her splendor, her masses, her revelry, and those who rejoice in her. 15 So man is humbled, and each one is brought low, and the eyes of the haughty are brought low. 16 But the LORD of hosts is exalted in justice, and the Holy God shows Himself holy in righteousness. 17 Then lambs shall graze as in their pasture, and nomads shall eat among the ruins of the rich.

18 Woe to those who drag iniquity along with ropes of deceit and haul sin as with cart ropes, 19 who say, "Let Him act quickly, let Him hasten His work, that we may see it; let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel draw near and come, that we may know it!" 20 Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness, who put bitter for sweet and sweet for bitter! 21 Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes and shrewd in their own sight! 22 Woe to those who are champions at drinking wine and valiant men at mixing strong drink, 23 who acquit the guilty for a bribe and deprive the innocent of their right! 24 Therefore, as the tongue of fire devours stubble and as dry grass is consumed by the flame, so their root shall be as rottenness and their blossom shall go up like dust; for they have rejected the law of the LORD of hosts and have despised the word of the Holy One of Israel. 25 Therefore the anger of the LORD was kindled against His people, and He stretched out His hand against them and struck them; the mountains quaked, and their corpses were as refuse in the midst of the streets. For all this His anger has not turned away, and His hand is still stretched out. 26 He will raise a signal for nations far away and whistle to summon them from the ends of the earth; and suddenly they come, swift and speedy! 27 None among them is weary, none stumbles; no one slumbers or sleeps; no waistband is loose, nor is a sandal strap broken. 28 Their arrows are sharp, all their bows are bent; their horses' hoofs are reckoned like flint, and their wheels like the whirlwind. 29 Their roaring is like a lion's; like young lions they roar; they growl and seize prey; they carry it off, and there is none to deliver. 30 In that day they will roar over it like the roaring of the sea. If one looks to the land, there is darkness and distress; and the light is darkened by its clouds.
