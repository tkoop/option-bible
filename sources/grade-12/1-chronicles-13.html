David Consults Israel's Leaders

1 David conferred with the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and with every leader. 2 Then David said to the whole assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it is from the the LORD our God, let us send word throughout all the lands of Israel to our brothers who remain, together with the priests and Levites in their cities and pasturelands, that they may gather to us. 3 Let us bring back the ark of our God to us, for we did not seek it in the days of Saul." 4 The whole assembly agreed to do this, because it seemed right to all the people.

The Ark Is Brought from Kiriath-Jearim

5 So David assembled all Israel, from the Shihor in Egypt to the entrance of Hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim. 6 Then David and all Israel went up to Baalah—that is, Kiriath-jearim—in Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, the the LORD who is enthroned between the cherubim and is called by His Name. 7 They transported the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Abinadab, with Uzza and Ahio driving the cart. 8 David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all their might, with songs and with harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

Uzzah Struck Down

9 When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzzah reached out his hand to steady the ark, because the oxen stumbled. 10 Then the anger of the the LORD burned against Uzzah, and He struck him down because he had put out his hand to the ark; so he died there before God. 11 David was angry because the the LORD had unleashed His wrath against Uzzah, and to this day that place is called Perez-Uzza. 12 David was afraid of God that day and said, "How can I bring the ark of God to myself?"

The Ark at Obed-Edom's House

13 So David did not take the ark with him into the City of David; instead, he diverted it to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite. 14 The ark of God remained with the family of Obed-Edom in his house for three months, and the the LORD blessed Obed-Edom and all his household.
