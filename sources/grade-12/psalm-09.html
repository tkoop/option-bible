A Psalm of David: Thanksgiving for the LORD’s Justice

1I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all Your wonders. 2I will be glad and exult in You; I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High.

3When my enemies turn back, they stumble and perish before You. 4For You have upheld my just cause; You sat enthroned, judging in righteousness. 5You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked; You have blotted out their name forever and ever. 6The enemy has come to an end in everlasting ruins; You have uprooted their cities, and even their memory has perished.

7But the LORD sits enthroned forever; He has established His throne for judgment. 8He judges the world with righteousness and governs the peoples with equity.

9The LORD is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. 10Those who know Your name trust in You, for You, the the LORD, have not forsaken those who seek You.

11Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion; declare His deeds among the nations. 12For the One who avenges blood remembers; He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.

13Be gracious to me, O the LORD; see my affliction from those who hate me. You who lift me up from the gates of death, 14that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of Daughter Zion and rejoice in Your salvation.

15The nations have sunk into the pit they made; their foot is caught in the net they hid. 16The LORD is known by the justice He executes; the wicked are snared by the work of their own hands. Meditation. Selah.

17The wicked will return to Sheol—all the nations who forget God. 18For the needy will not always be forgotten, nor will the hope of the afflicted perish forever.

19Arise, O the LORD! Do not let man prevail; let the nations be judged in Your presence. 20Put them in fear, O the LORD; let the nations know that they are but men. Selah.
