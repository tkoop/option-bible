A Psalm of David When He Fled from Absalom

1 O the LORD, how numerous are my foes! Many rise up against me. 2 Many say of me, "God will not deliver him." Selah.

3 But You, O the LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the One who lifts my head. 4 I cried aloud to the LORD, and He answered me from His holy hill. Selah.

5 I lay down and slept; I awoke again, for the LORD sustained me. 6 I will not fear ten thousands of people who have set themselves against me all around.

7 Arise, O the LORD! Save me, O my God! For You strike all my enemies on the cheek; You break the teeth of the wicked. 8 Salvation belongs to the LORD; Your blessing be on Your people! Selah.
