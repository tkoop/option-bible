A Sign of Jerusalem's Siege

1 And you, son of man, take a brick for yourself, set it before you, and inscribe on it a city—Jerusalem. 2 Lay siege to it: build a siege wall against it, raise a siege ramp against it, pitch camps against it, and station battering rams around it on every side. 3 Then take an iron plate and set it as an iron wall between you and the city; fix your face against it, and let it be besieged while you besiege it. This shall be a sign to the house of Israel.

Bearing the Iniquity of Israel and Judah

4 Lie on your left side and place the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it; you shall bear their iniquity for the number of days you lie on it. 5 For I have assigned you three hundred and ninety days, corresponding to the years of their iniquity, and you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel. 6 When you have completed these, lie again on your right side and bear the iniquity of the house of Judah. I have assigned it to you for forty days, a day for each year. 7 Set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm bared, and prophesy against it. 8 Behold, I will bind you with cords, so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.

Rationed Food and Water

9 And you, take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt, and put them into one vessel, and make bread from them; you shall eat it for the three hundred and ninety days that you lie on your side. 10 The food you eat shall be by weight—twenty shekels a day—and you shall eat it at fixed times. 11 You shall drink water by measure, a sixth of a hin, and you shall drink it at fixed times. 12 You shall eat it as a barley cake baked publicly over human dung.

Defiled Bread and Coming Famine

13 The LORD said, "Thus shall the sons of Israel eat their defiled bread among the nations, where I will drive them."

14 Then I said, "Ah, Lord the LORD! Behold, I have never defiled myself; from my youth until now I have not eaten what died of itself or was torn by beasts, nor has any abominable flesh entered my mouth."

15 He said to me, "See, I give you cow dung instead of human dung, and you shall prepare your bread over it."

16 And He said to me, "Son of man, behold, I am breaking the supply of bread in Jerusalem. They shall eat bread by weight and in anxiety, and drink water by measure and in dismay, 17 so that they may lack bread and water, be appalled at one another, and waste away because of their iniquity."
