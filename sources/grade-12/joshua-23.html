Joshua’s Final Assembly of Israel

1 Following a lengthy interval after the LORD had granted Israel respite from all surrounding enemies, Joshua had become elderly and advanced in years. 2 Joshua convened all Israel—its elders, chieftains, judges, and officers—and addressed them, “I have become elderly and advanced in years.

The LORD’s Past Faithfulness and Israel’s Obligation

“3 You have witnessed everything the LORD your God accomplished among these nations on your behalf, for the LORD your God Himself waged war for you. 4 Observe that I have assigned to your tribes, as their inheritance, the nations that remain, together with every nation I annihilated from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west. 5 The LORD your God will expel them before you and dispossess them, enabling you to occupy their land, precisely as the LORD your God promised you.

“6 Therefore, be exceedingly resolute in observing and executing everything inscribed in the Book of the Law of Moses, without deviating from it to the right or to the left. 7 Do not assimilate with these remaining nations; do not invoke their gods, swear by them, serve them, or bow down to them. 8 Rather, remain devoted to the LORD your God, as you have done to this day.

“9 For the LORD has driven out before you great and formidable nations, and to this day no one has been able to withstand you. 10 A single one of you can put a thousand to flight, because the LORD your God fights for you, just as He promised. 11 Therefore, be exceedingly vigilant in loving the LORD your God.

The Danger of Assimilation

“12 If you turn back and ally yourselves with the remnant of these nations still among you, intermarrying with them until you are thoroughly entangled with them and they with you, 13 then know with certainty that the LORD your God will no longer drive these nations out before you; instead, they will become a snare and a trap for you, a scourge at your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that the LORD your God has given you.

Joshua’s Imminent Death and Final Warning

“14 Now I am about to die, as all mortals do, and you know with all your heart and soul that not one of the good promises the LORD your God made concerning you has failed; every one has been fulfilled, and not a single word has fallen short. 15 But just as every good promise the LORD your God made to you has come to pass, so the LORD will bring upon you every calamity until He has destroyed you from this good land that the LORD your God has given you, 16 if you violate the covenant of the LORD your God that He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then the LORD’s anger will burn against you, and you will quickly perish from the good land He has given you.”
