The LORD's Command and Pharaoh's Pursuit

1 Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and bid them turn back and encamp before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea; they shall camp by the sea, directly opposite Baal-zephon. 3 Pharaoh will conclude that the Israelites are wandering in confusion throughout the land and that the wilderness has hemmed them in. 4 I will harden Pharaoh's heart, so that he pursues them, and I will win glory for Myself through Pharaoh and all his army; then the Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD." So the Israelites did so.

5 When the king of Egypt was informed that the people had fled, Pharaoh and his servants reversed their disposition toward the people and exclaimed, "What is this we have done, releasing Israel from our service?" 6 So Pharaoh prepared his chariot and took his people with him. 7 He selected six hundred of the finest chariots, together with every chariot in Egypt, and officers over them all. 8 The LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and he pursued the Israelites, who were marching out with confident boldness. 9 The Egyptians pursued them—all Pharaoh's horses and chariots, his horsemen and his army—and overtook them encamped by the sea, near Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon.

Israel's Fear and Moses' Reassurance

10 As Pharaoh drew near, the Israelites lifted their eyes and beheld the Egyptians advancing behind them; they were seized with great terror and cried out to the LORD. 11 They said to Moses, "Was it for lack of graves in Egypt that you brought us into the wilderness to die? What have you done to us by leading us out of Egypt? 12 Did we not tell you in Egypt, 'Leave us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians'? It would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians than to perish in the wilderness!"

13 Moses answered the people, "Do not be afraid. Stand firm, and you shall see the salvation that the LORD will accomplish for you today; for the Egyptians whom you see today, you shall never see again. 14 The LORD will fight for you, and you need only be still."

The Parting of the Sea and Israel's Crossing

15 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to Me? Tell the Israelites to move on. 16 And you, raise your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea and divide it, so that the Israelites may go through the sea on dry ground. 17 I will harden the hearts of the Egyptians, so that they go in after them, and I will win glory for Myself through Pharaoh and all his army, through his chariots and his horsemen. 18 Then the Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD, when I have won glory through Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen."

19 Then the angel of God, who had been going before the camp of Israel, moved and went behind them; the pillar of cloud likewise moved from before them and stood behind them. 20 It came between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel: the cloud was darkness to one side, yet it gave light by night to the other, so that neither approached the other all night. 21 Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the LORD drove back the sea all night by a strong east wind and made the sea dry land; the waters were divided. 22 The Israelites went into the midst of the sea on dry ground, with the waters as a wall for them on their right and on their left.

The Destruction of the Egyptian Army

23 The Egyptians pursued and went into the sea after them—all Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and horsemen. 24 In the morning watch, the LORD looked down upon the Egyptian army from the pillar of fire and cloud and threw the army into confusion. 25 He jammed the wheels of their chariots, so that they dragged heavily, and the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from Israel, for the LORD is fighting for them against Egypt!"

26 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the waters may return upon the Egyptians, upon their chariots and horsemen." 27 So Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and as morning broke, the sea returned to its normal course; the Egyptians fled before it, and the LORD swept them into the sea. 28 The waters returned and covered the chariots and the horsemen—the whole army of Pharaoh that had followed them into the sea; not one of them remained. 29 But the Israelites had walked through the sea on dry ground, with the waters as a wall on their right and on their left.

30 Thus the LORD saved Israel that day from the hand of the Egyptians, and Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the seashore. 31 When Israel saw the great power that the LORD had wielded against the Egyptians, the people feared the LORD and believed in the LORD and in Moses His servant.
