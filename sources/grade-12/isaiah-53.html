The Servant's Unremarkable Origins and Rejection

1 Who has believed what we have heard? And to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed? 2 He grew up before Him like a tender shoot, and like a root from parched ground. He had no stately form or splendor that we should gaze upon Him, and no beauty that we should desire Him. 3 He was despised and forsaken by men, a man of sorrows and acquainted with grief; and like one from whom people hide their faces, He was despised, and we did not esteem Him.

The Servant's Substitutionary Suffering

4 Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we regarded Him as stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. 5 But He was pierced for our transgressions, He was crushed for our iniquities; the chastisement that brought us peace was upon Him, and by His wounds we are healed. 6 All of us, like sheep, have gone astray; each of us has turned to his own way; and the LORD has laid upon Him the iniquity of us all.

Silent Suffering, Death, and Burial

7 He was oppressed and afflicted, yet He did not open His mouth; like a lamb led to the slaughter, and like a sheep before its shearers, He was silent, so He did not open His mouth. 8 By oppression and judgment He was taken away; and who among His contemporaries considered that He was cut off from the land of the living, stricken for the transgression of my people? 9 They assigned His grave among the wicked, and His burial place among the rich, although He had done no violence, and there was no deceit in His mouth.

The Servant's Vindication and Exaltation

10 Yet the LORD was pleased to crush Him and make Him suffer; when He makes His life a guilt offering, He will see His offspring and prolong His days, and the will of the LORD will prosper in His hand. 11 After the anguish of His soul, He will see the outcome and be satisfied; by His knowledge My righteous Servant will justify many, and He will bear their iniquities. 12 Therefore I will allot Him a portion among the great, and He will divide the spoils with the strong, because He poured out His life unto death and was numbered with the transgressors; yet He bore the sin of many and made intercession for the transgressors.
