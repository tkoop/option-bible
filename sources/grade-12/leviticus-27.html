1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

Vows Concerning Persons

2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When a person makes a special vow to the LORD, the value of the person vowed shall be set according to your valuation. 3 For a male between twenty and sixty years of age, your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the sanctuary shekel. 4 For a female, your valuation shall be thirty shekels. 5 If the person is from five to twenty years old, your valuation for a male shall be twenty shekels, and for a female ten shekels. 6 If the person is from one month to five years old, your valuation for a male shall be five shekels of silver, and for a female three shekels of silver. 7 If the person is sixty years old or older, your valuation for a male shall be fifteen shekels, and for a female ten shekels. 8 If he is too poor to pay your valuation, he shall be brought before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to what the one who vowed can afford, the priest shall value him.

Dedicated Animals and Houses

9 "If it is an animal of the kind that may be offered as an offering to the LORD, all that is given to the LORD shall be holy. 10 He shall not exchange it or substitute another for it, whether good for bad or bad for good; and if he does substitute animal for animal, then both it and its substitute shall be holy. 11 If it is any unclean animal that is not offered as an offering to the LORD, he shall bring the animal before the priest. 12 The priest shall value it, whether good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall be. 13 But if he indeed redeems it, he shall add a fifth of its value to your valuation. 14 When a man dedicates his house as holy to the LORD, the priest shall value it, whether good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall stand. 15 If the one who dedicated redeems his house, he shall add a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be his.

Dedicated Fields

16 "If a man dedicates to the LORD a field from his inherited land, the valuation shall be according to the amount of seed required for it: fifty shekels of silver for each homer of barley seed. 17 If he dedicates his field from the year of Jubilee, it shall stand according to your valuation. 18 But if he dedicates his field after the Jubilee, the priest shall calculate the money for him according to the years remaining until the year of Jubilee, and a deduction shall be made from your valuation. 19 If the one who dedicated redeems the field, he shall add a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be confirmed to him. 20 If he does not redeem the field, or if he sells the field to another man, it shall no longer be redeemed. 21 When the field is released in the Jubilee, it shall be holy to the LORD, like a field devoted to the LORD; the priest shall take possession of it. 22 If he dedicates to the LORD a field that he has bought, which is not part of his inherited land, 23 then the priest shall calculate for him the amount of your valuation up to the year of Jubilee, and he shall give your valuation on that day as a holy thing to the LORD. 24 In the year of Jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom he bought it, to whom the land's possession belongs. 25 All of your valuations shall be according to the sanctuary shekel: twenty gerahs to the shekel.

Firstborn, Devoted Things, and Tithes

26 "However, no one may dedicate a firstborn among animals, since it is firstborn to the LORD; whether ox or sheep, it belongs to the LORD. 27 If it is an unclean animal, he shall redeem it according to your valuation and add a fifth to it; if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold according to your valuation. 28 Nevertheless, anything that a man devotes to the LORD of all that he has, whether person, animal, or inherited field, shall not be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to the LORD. 29 No person devoted to destruction from among mankind shall be redeemed; he shall surely be put to death. 30 All the tithe of the land, whether from the seed of the land or from the fruit of the trees, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD. 31 If a man redeems any of his tithe, he shall add a fifth to it. 32 All the tithe of the herd or the flock, every tenth animal that passes under the rod, shall be holy to the LORD. 33 He shall not examine whether it is good or bad, nor shall he substitute for it; and if he does substitute for it, then both it and its substitute shall be holy, and it shall not be redeemed."

34 These are the commandments that the LORD commanded Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
