Daniel's Vision in the Third Year of Cyrus

1 In the third year of Cyrus, king of Persia, a message was revealed to Daniel, who was called Belteshazzar. The message was true and concerned a great conflict, and understanding of it was given to him in a vision. 2 In those days I, Daniel, mourned for three weeks. 3 I ate no choice food; no meat or wine passed my lips, and I did not anoint myself with oil until the three weeks were completed. 4 On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was standing beside the great river, the Tigris.

The Heavenly Messenger's Appearance

5 I lifted my eyes and looked, and there stood a man clothed in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz around his waist. 6 His body was like beryl, his face like lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and legs like polished bronze, and the sound of his words like the roar of a multitude.

Daniel's Weakness and the Messenger's Encouragement

7 I, Daniel, alone saw the vision; the men with me did not see it, but a great terror seized them, and they fled to hide. 8 So I was left alone, gazing at this great vision. My strength deserted me; my vigor turned to decay, and I retained no strength. 9 Then I heard the sound of his words, and as I heard them, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground.

10 Suddenly a hand touched me and set me trembling on my hands and knees. 11 He said to me, "Daniel, man greatly loved, understand the words I am speaking to you, and stand upright, for I have now been sent to you." While he spoke this word to me, I stood up trembling. 12 "Do not be afraid, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard, and I have come because of your words. 13 But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, for I had been left there with the kings of Persia. 14 Now I have come to make you understand what will happen to your people in the latter days, for the vision concerns those days."

15 While he was speaking these words to me, I turned my face toward the ground and was silent. 16 Then one in the likeness of the sons of men touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke, saying to him who stood before me, "My lord, because of the vision my anguish has turned upon me, and I have no strength. 17 For how can this servant of my lord speak with my lord? Now no strength remains in me, and no breath is left in me."

The Messenger's Final Words

18 Again one having the appearance of a man touched me and strengthened me. 19 He said, "Do not be afraid, man greatly loved. Peace be to you; be strong and courageous."

When he spoke to me, I was strengthened and said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

The Conflict Among the Princes

20 He said, "Do you know why I have come to you? Now I will return to fight the prince of Persia, and when I leave, the prince of Greece will come. 21 But I will tell you what is inscribed in the book of truth. No one strengthens me against these except Michael, your prince."
