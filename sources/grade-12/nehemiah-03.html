Rebuilding the Gates and Northern Wall

1 Eliashib the high priest, together with his fellow priests, rebuilt the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set its doors in place, dedicating the work as far as the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel. 2 The men of Jericho built beside it, and Zaccur son of Imri built beside them. 3 The sons of Hassenaah rebuilt the Fish Gate; they laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 4 Beside them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, made repairs; beside him Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, made repairs; and beside him Zadok son of Baana made repairs.

5 Beside him the men of Tekoa made repairs, yet their nobles refused to submit to the labor demanded by their masters. 6 Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate; they laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 7 Beside them Melatiah the Gibeonite, Jadon the Meronothite, and the men of Gibeon and Mizpah repaired the section under the authority of the governor of the province beyond the River. 8 Beside them Uzziel son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs; beside him Hananiah, one of the perfumers, made repairs. They fortified Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

Repairing the Southern and Western Sections

9 Beside them Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs. 10 Beside them Jedaiah son of Harumaph made repairs opposite his house; beside him Hattush son of Hashabneiah made repairs. 11 Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section, together with the Tower of the Ovens. 12 Beside him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of the other half of the district of Jerusalem, made repairs, he and his daughters.

13 Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate; they rebuilt it and installed its doors, bolts, and bars, and they repaired a thousand cubits of the wall as far as the Dung Gate. 14 Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccerem, repaired the Dung Gate; he rebuilt it and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. 15 Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate; he rebuilt it, roofed it, and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Siloam by the king's garden, as far as the stairs descending from the City of David. 16 After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-zur, made repairs up to the point opposite the tombs of David, as far as the artificial pool and the House of the Mighty.

Levites, Priests, and Residents at Work

17 After him the Levites made repairs under Rehum son of Bani; beside him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, made repairs for his district. 18 After him their brothers made repairs under Binnui son of Henadad, ruler of the other half of the district of Keilah. 19 Beside him Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section opposite the ascent to the armory at the angle. 20 After him Baruch son of Zabbai zealously repaired another section, from the angle to the doorway of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

21 After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the doorway of Eliashib's house to the end of the house. 22 Beside him the priests from the surrounding region made repairs. 23 After them Benjamin and Hasshub made repairs opposite their house; beside them Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, made repairs beside his house. 24 After him Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from the house of Azariah to the angle and the corner.

Completing the Circuit to the Sheep Gate

25 Palal son of Uzai made repairs opposite the angle and the tower projecting from the upper palace of the king, by the courtyard of the guard. Next to him, Pedaiah son of Parosh 26 and the temple servants living on the Ophel made repairs up to the point opposite the Water Gate toward the east and the tower that juts out. 27 Next to them the men of Tekoa repaired another section, from the great tower that juts out to the wall of Ophel. 28 Above the Horse Gate the priests made repairs, each in front of his own house.

29 After them Zadok son of Immer made repairs opposite his house. After him Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the guard of the East Gate, made repairs. 30 After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah, along with Hanun the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. After them Meshullam son of Berechiah made repairs opposite his chamber. 31 After him Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs as far as the house of the temple servants and merchants, opposite the Inspection Gate, and as far as the upper chamber of the corner. 32 Between the upper chamber of the corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and merchants made repairs.
