Job's Rebuttal to His Friends

1 Then Job answered:

2 "Truly you are the people, and wisdom will perish with you. 3 I also possess understanding, just as you do; I am not inferior to you. Who does not know such things? 4 I have become a laughingstock to my neighbor, though I called upon God and He answered me—a righteous and blameless man made into a spectacle. 5 In the mind of one at ease, calamity is contemptible, yet it is prepared for those whose feet slip. 6 The tents of robbers remain secure, and those who provoke God dwell in safety, because they trust in their own power.

Wisdom Revealed in Creation

7 "But ask the beasts, and they will teach you; the birds of the heavens will tell you. 8 Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; the fish of the sea will declare it to you. 9 Who among all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has done this? 10 In His hand is the life of every living creature and the breath of all mankind. 11 Does not the ear test words as the palate tastes food? 12 Wisdom is found with the aged, and understanding in length of days.

God's Sovereign Power Over Human Affairs

13 "With Him are wisdom and might; counsel and understanding belong to Him. 14 If He tears down, no one can rebuild; if He imprisons a man, no one can release him. 15 If He withholds the waters, they dry up; if He sends them forth, they overwhelm the earth. 16 Strength and effective wisdom are His; both the deceived and the deceiver belong to Him. 17 He leads counselors away stripped of their dignity and makes judges into fools. 18 He looses the bonds of kings and binds a sash around their waists. 19 He leads priests away stripped and overthrows the mighty. 20 He removes the speech of trusted men and takes discernment from the elders. 21 He pours contempt upon princes and weakens the strong. 22 He reveals deep things out of darkness and brings utter darkness into the light. 23 He makes nations great and then destroys them; He enlarges nations and leads them away. 24 He takes understanding from the leaders of the earth and makes them wander in a pathless wasteland. 25 They grope in darkness without light, and He makes them stagger like drunkards."
