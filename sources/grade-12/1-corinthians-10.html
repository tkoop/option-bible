Warnings from Israel's History

1 For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that our ancestors were all under the cloud and all crossed the sea. 2 They were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea. 3 They all ate the same spiritual food, 4 and they all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual Rock that accompanied them, and that Rock was Christ. 5 Nevertheless, God was not pleased with most of them, for they were struck down in the wilderness.

6 Now these things happened as examples for us, so that we might not set our hearts on evil things as they did. 7 Do not become idolaters, as some of them were; as it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to revel.” 8 Let us not indulge in sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in a single day. 9 Let us not test Christ, as some of them tested Him and were destroyed by snakes. 10 Neither let us grumble, as some of them grumbled and were destroyed by the destroyer.

11 Now these things happened to them as a warning, and they were written for our instruction, on whom the ends of the ages have come. 12 Therefore, let the one who thinks he stands take heed, lest he fall. 13 No temptation has seized you except what is common to humanity; but God is faithful, and He will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you can bear. Instead, with the temptation He will also make a way of escape, so that you may be able to endure it.

Flee from Idolatry

14 Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry. 15 I speak as to sensible people; judge for yourselves the force of what I say. 16 Is not the cup of blessing that we bless a sharing in the blood of Christ? Is not the bread that we break a sharing in the body of Christ? 17 Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

The Lord's Table and Demonic Fellowship

18 Consider the people of Israel in the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices sharers in the altar? 19 What then? Do I mean that food offered to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything? 20 No; rather, I say that what the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God, and I do not want you to become sharers with demons. 21 You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the table of the Lord and the table of demons. 22 Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?

Freedom, Conscience, and God's Glory

23 “All things are lawful,” but not all things are beneficial; “all things are lawful,” but not all things are edifying. 24 Let no one seek his own advantage, but the advantage of his neighbor. 25 Eat anything sold in the meat market without raising questions of conscience, 26 for “The earth is the Lord's, and its fullness.”

27 If one of the unbelievers invites you to a meal and you are willing to go, eat whatever is set before you without raising questions of conscience. 28 But if someone says to you, “This has been offered in sacrifice,” then do not eat it, for the sake of the one who informed you and for conscience's sake. 29 I do not mean your conscience, but his. For why should my liberty be judged by another's conscience? 30 If I partake with thanksgiving, why am I slandered concerning that for which I give thanks?

31 So, whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God. 32 Give no offense to Jews or to Greeks or to the church of God, 33 just as I also try to please everyone in everything, not seeking my own advantage but that of many, so that they may be saved.
