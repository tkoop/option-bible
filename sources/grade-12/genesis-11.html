The Tower of Babel

1 At that time, the whole earth spoke a single language and shared a common vocabulary. 2 As people migrated eastward, they discovered an expansive plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.

3 They said to one another, "Come, let us mold bricks and fire them thoroughly." They used brick instead of stone and tar instead of mortar.

4 Then they said, "Come, let us build a city for ourselves, with a tower whose summit reaches the heavens, so that we may win renown and not be scattered across the face of the whole earth."

5 The LORD descended to inspect the city and the tower that humankind was building.

6 The LORD said, "Look, they are one people with a single language, and this is merely the beginning of their undertakings. Nothing they propose to do will now be beyond their reach. 7 Come, let Us go down and confuse their language, so that no one may understand another's speech."

8 So the LORD dispersed them from there over the face of the entire earth, and they ceased building the city. 9 Therefore it was called Babel, because there the LORD confused the language of the entire earth, and from there the LORD dispersed them over the face of the entire earth.

The Lineage of Shem

10 These are the generations of Shem. Shem was one hundred years old when he fathered Arpachshad two years after the flood; afterward he lived five hundred years and fathered sons and daughters.

12 Arpachshad was thirty-five years old when he fathered Shelah. 13 Afterward he lived four hundred and three years and fathered sons and daughters.

14 Shelah was thirty years old when he fathered Eber. 15 Afterward he lived four hundred and three years and fathered sons and daughters.

16 Eber was thirty-four years old when he fathered Peleg. 17 Afterward he lived four hundred and thirty years and fathered sons and daughters.

18 Peleg was thirty years old when he fathered Reu. 19 Afterward he lived two hundred and nine years and fathered sons and daughters.

20 Reu was thirty-two years old when he fathered Serug. 21 Afterward he lived two hundred and seven years and fathered sons and daughters.

22 Serug was thirty years old when he fathered Nahor. 23 Afterward he lived two hundred years and fathered sons and daughters.

24 Nahor was twenty-nine years old when he fathered Terah. 25 Afterward he lived one hundred and nineteen years and fathered sons and daughters.

Terah's Family and the Journey to Haran

26 Terah was seventy years old when he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran. 27 These are the generations of Terah: Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot.

28 Haran died in the presence of his father Terah in his native land, Ur of the Chaldeans.

29 Abram and Nahor took wives: Abram's wife was Sarai, and Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, who fathered both Milcah and Iscah.

30 Sarai, however, was barren and bore no children.

31 Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot son of Haran, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, wife of his son Abram, and they set out from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. They came to Haran and settled there. 32 Terah lived two hundred and five years and died in Haran.
