The Promise of Restoration

1 When the blessings and curses that I have set before you come upon you, and you take them to heart among all the nations where the the LORD your God has driven you, 2 you and your children will return to the the LORD your God and obey His voice in all that I command you today, with all your heart and with all your soul. 3 Then the the LORD your God will restore you from captivity, have compassion on you, and gather you again from all the peoples among whom He has scattered you.

4 Even if you have been banished to the farthest horizon, the the LORD your God will gather you from there and take you back. 5 He will bring you into the land your fathers possessed, so that you may possess it; He will prosper you and multiply you beyond your fathers. 6 The the LORD your God will also circumcise your heart and the hearts of your descendants, so that you love the the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, and so that you may live.

7 The the LORD your God will place all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate and persecute you. 8 You, for your part, will return and obey the voice of the the LORD, keeping all the commandments I am giving you today. 9 The the LORD your God will make you abundantly prosperous in all your undertakings—in the fruit of your womb, your livestock, and your land—to your good, for the the LORD will once again delight in prospering you, as He delighted in your fathers. 10 This will happen when you obey the voice of the the LORD your God and keep His commandments and statutes recorded in this Book of the Law, and when you return to the the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

The Commandment Is Within Reach

11 For this commandment that I am giving you today is not too difficult for you, nor is it beyond your reach. 12 It is not in heaven, that you should say, “Who will ascend to heaven for us and retrieve it, so that we may hear it and obey it?” 13 Nor is it beyond the sea, that you should say, “Who will cross the sea for us and retrieve it, so that we may hear it and obey it?” 14 On the contrary, the word is very near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it.

The Choice Between Life and Death

15 See, I have set before you today life and prosperity, as well as death and disaster. 16 If you love the the LORD your God, walk in His ways, and keep His commandments, statutes, and ordinances, you will live and multiply, and the the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are entering to possess. 17 But if your heart turns away and you refuse to listen, if you are lured into bowing down to other gods and serving them, 18 I declare to you today that you will certainly perish; you will not live long in the land you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess. 19 I call heaven and earth to witness against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Therefore choose life, so that you and your offspring may live— 20 loving the the LORD your God, obeying His voice, and holding fast to Him—for He is your life and the length of your days, and He will let you dwell in the land the the LORD swore to give your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
