The Angels Arrive in Sodom

1 In the evening, two angels arrived in Sodom, and Lot was seated at the city gate. When Lot saw them, he rose to meet them and bowed with his face to the ground. 2 He said, "Please, my lords, turn aside now to your servant's house and wash your feet; then stay the night and rise early to continue your journey."

They replied, "No; we will spend the night in the square." 3 Yet he importuned them so vehemently that they turned aside and entered his house. He prepared a feast for them, baking unleavened bread, and they ate.

The Men of Sodom Surround the House

4 Before they lay down, every man in the city—young and old alike, from every quarter—surrounded the house. 5 They called to Lot and demanded, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us, so that we may have sexual relations with them." 6 Lot went out to them at the entrance and shut the door behind him. 7 He said, "Please, my brothers, do not act so wickedly. 8 Look, I have two daughters who have never had sexual relations with a man. Let me bring them out to you, and do with them as you see fit; only do nothing to these men, for they have come under the shelter of my roof."

9 They said, "Step aside!" and added, "This man came here as a sojourner, and now he presumes to judge us? We will treat you worse than them." Then they pressed hard against Lot and moved in to break down the door. 10 But the men inside reached out, pulled Lot back into the house, and shut the door. 11 Then they struck the men at the door, young and old, with blindness, so that they groped about in vain for the entrance.

Judgment Pronounced on Sodom

12 The angels said to Lot, "Whom else do you have here? A son-in-law, sons, daughters, or anyone else in the city—bring them out of this place, 13 for we are about to destroy it. The outcry against its people has grown so great before the LORD that the LORD has sent us to destroy it."

14 So Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who were to marry his daughters. "Get up and leave this place," he said, "for the LORD is about to destroy the city." But to his sons-in-law he seemed to be joking. 15 When dawn broke, the angels urged Lot on, saying, "Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here, or you will be swept away in the punishment of the city." 16 When he hesitated, the men seized his hand, his wife's hand, and the hands of his two daughters—the the LORD being merciful to him—and led them out, setting them down outside the city.

17 As they brought them out, one of them said, "Flee for your life! Do not look back, and do not stop anywhere in the plain. Flee to the hills, or you will be swept away!" 18 But Lot said to them, "No, please, my lords! 19 Look, your servant has found favor in your sight, and you have shown me great kindness by sparing my life. But I cannot flee to the hills, lest the disaster overtake me and I die. 20 Look, this city is near enough to flee to, and it is small. Please let me escape there—is it not small?—and my life will be spared."

21 He said to him, "Very well, I grant you this favor too: I will not overthrow the city you have mentioned. 22 Hurry and escape there, for I cannot do anything until you arrive." So the city was called Zoar. 23 The sun had risen over the earth by the time Lot reached Zoar.

The Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah

24 Then the LORD rained down sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah—from the LORD out of heaven. 25 He overthrew those cities and the whole plain, along with all the inhabitants of the cities and everything that grew on the ground. 26 But Lot's wife looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

27 Early the next morning, Abraham went to the place where he had stood before the LORD. 28 He looked toward Sodom and Gomorrah and over the whole plain, and he saw smoke rising from the land like smoke from a furnace. 29 So when God destroyed the cities of the plain, He remembered Abraham and brought Lot out of the catastrophe, as He overthrew the cities where Lot had lived.

Lot and His Daughters in the Hills

30 Lot went up from Zoar and settled in the hill country with his two daughters, for he was afraid to stay in Zoar. He lived in a cave with his two daughters. 31 The older daughter said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man on earth to marry us, as is customary throughout the world. 32 Come, let us make our father drink wine, and we will lie with him, so that we may preserve offspring through our father." 33 So they made their father drink wine that night, and the older daughter went in and lay with her father. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

34 The next day, the older daughter said to the younger, "Last night I lay with my father. Let us make him drink wine tonight as well, and you go in and lie with him, so that we may preserve offspring through our father." 35 So they made their father drink wine that night too, and the younger daughter went in and lay with him. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up. 36 Thus both of Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father. 37 The older daughter bore a son and named him Moab; he is the father of the Moabites to this day. 38 The younger daughter also bore a son and named him Ben-ammi; he is the father of the Ammonites to this day.
