God Tests Abraham

1 After these things, God tested Abraham and said to him, "Abraham!"

Abraham replied, "Here I am."

2 God said, "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will show you."

3 So Abraham rose early in the morning, saddled his donkey, and took two of his young servants along with his son Isaac. He split the wood for the burnt offering, set out, and traveled to the place God had designated.

4 On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the place in the distance.

5 Abraham said to his servants, "Stay here with the donkey while the boy and I go over there. We will worship and then return to you."

6 Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and laid it on his son Isaac. He carried the fire and the knife in his own hand, and the two of them walked on together.

7 Isaac spoke to his father Abraham: "My father."

Abraham answered, "Here I am, my son."

Isaac asked, "The fire and the wood are here, but where is the lamb for the burnt offering?"

8 Abraham replied, "God will provide the lamb for Himself, my son." So the two of them continued together.

9 When they reached the place God had told him about, Abraham built an altar there, arranged the wood, bound his son Isaac, and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood.

10 Abraham reached out his hand, took the knife, and was about to slaughter his son.

11 But the Angel of the LORD called to him from heaven, "Abraham! Abraham!"

Abraham answered, "Here I am."

12 The Angel said, "Do not lay your hand on the boy, and do nothing to him, for now I know that you fear God, because you have not withheld your son, your only son, from Me."

Provision on the Mountain

13 Abraham looked up and saw a ram caught by its horns in a thicket. He went over, took the ram, and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son.

14 Abraham named that place "The LORD Will Provide." To this day it is said, "On the mountain of the LORD it will be provided."

The Promise Renewed

15 The Angel of the LORD called to Abraham a second time from heaven.

16 He said, "By Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that because you have done this and have not withheld your son, your only son, 17 I will surely bless you, and I will surely multiply your offspring as the stars of heaven and as the sand on the seashore. Your offspring shall possess the gates of their enemies. 18 And in your offspring all the nations of the earth shall be blessed, because you have obeyed My voice."

19 Abraham returned to his servants, and they set out together for Beersheba, where Abraham settled.

Nahor's Descendants

20 After these things, Abraham was told, "Look, Milcah has also borne children to your brother Nahor: 21 Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram, 22 Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel."

23 Bethuel became the father of Rebekah. These eight children Milcah bore to Nahor, Abraham's brother.

24 Nahor's concubine, Reumah, also bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.
