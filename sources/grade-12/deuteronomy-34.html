Moses Beholds the Promised Land

1 Moses ascended from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah opposite Jericho, and the the LORD showed him the entire land—Gilead as far as Dan, 2 all of Naphtali, the lands of Ephraim and Manasseh, all of Judah as far as the western sea, 3 the Negev, the plain of the Jericho Valley, the City of Palms, and as far as Zoar. 4 The The LORD said to him, "This is the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have granted you a view of it with your own eyes, but you shall not cross over into it."

The Death and Burial of Moses

5 So Moses, the servant of the the LORD, died there in the land of Moab at the the LORD's command. 6 He was buried in a valley in Moab, opposite Beth-peor, and no one knows his grave to this day. 7 Moses was one hundred twenty years old when he died; his eyesight had not failed, nor had his strength diminished. 8 The Israelites mourned Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days, until the period of mourning had ended.

Joshua's Succession and Moses' Unparalleled Legacy

9 Joshua son of Nun was filled with the Spirit of wisdom because Moses had laid hands on him; the Israelites obeyed him and carried out everything the the LORD had commanded Moses. 10 No prophet has since arisen in Israel like Moses, with whom the the LORD spoke directly. 11 He performed all the signs and wonders the the LORD had sent him to do in Egypt against Pharaoh, his officials, and his whole land; 12 and he displayed all the mighty power and great terror that Moses manifested before all Israel.
