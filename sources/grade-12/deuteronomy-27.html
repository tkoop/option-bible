Moses Commands Israel to Keep the Law and Erect Memorial Stones

1 Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, “Keep every commandment that I am giving you today.”

2 On the day you cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD your God is giving you, you shall erect large stones and coat them with plaster.

3 You shall write on them all the words of this law as you cross over, so that you may enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you.

4 When you cross the Jordan, you shall set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I command you today, and you shall coat them with plaster.

5 You shall build an altar there to the LORD your God, an altar of stones that no iron tool has touched.

6 You shall build the altar of the LORD your God of unhewn stones, and you shall offer burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.

7 You shall sacrifice peace offerings there, eat them, and rejoice before the LORD your God.

8 You shall write clearly on the stones all the words of this law.

Israel’s Covenant Identity and Obedience

9 Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, “Be quiet and listen, O Israel: today you have become the people of the LORD your God.”

10 You shall therefore obey the voice of the LORD your God and carry out His commandments and statutes, which I am commanding you today.

Blessings and Curses on the Two Mountains

11 Moses also charged the people on that day,

12 “When you cross the Jordan, these shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin. 13 And these shall stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.”

The Levites Pronounce Curses upon Disobedience

14 Then the Levites shall declare in a loud voice to all the men of Israel: 15 “Cursed is anyone who makes a carved or molten image, an abomination to the LORD, the work of a craftsman’s hands, and sets it up in secret. 16 Cursed is anyone who dishonors his father or his mother. 17 Cursed is anyone who moves his neighbor’s boundary marker. 18 Cursed is anyone who leads a blind person astray on the road.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

19 “Cursed is anyone who perverts justice for the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow. 20 Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his father’s wife, because he has violated his father’s bed. 21 Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with an animal. 22 Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his sister, whether she is his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

23 “Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with his mother-in-law. 24 Cursed is anyone who strikes down his neighbor in secret. 25 Cursed is anyone who accepts a bribe to kill an innocent person. 26 Cursed is anyone who does not uphold the words of this law by carrying them out.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.” And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”
