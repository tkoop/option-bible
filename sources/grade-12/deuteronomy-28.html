Blessings for Obedience

1 If you diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God, being careful to observe all His commands that I am giving you today, then the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth. 2 All these blessings will come upon you and overtake you, if you obey the voice of the LORD your God.

3 You will be blessed in the city and blessed in the country. 4 Blessed will be the fruit of your womb, the produce of your ground, and the young of your livestock—the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks. 5 Blessed will be your basket and your kneading bowl. 6 You will be blessed when you come in and blessed when you go out.

7 The LORD will cause the enemies who rise against you to be defeated before you. They will march out against you by one road but flee before you by seven. 8 The LORD will command the blessing to be with you in your barns and in everything you put your hand to, and He will bless you in the land the LORD your God is giving you. 9 The LORD will establish you as His holy people, as He swore to you, if you keep the commands of the LORD your God and walk in His ways.

10 All the peoples of the earth will see that you are called by the name of the LORD, and they will stand in awe of you. 11 The LORD will grant you abundant prosperity—in the fruit of your womb, the offspring of your livestock, and the produce of your ground—in the land the LORD swore to your fathers to give you. 12 The LORD will open for you the heavens, His good treasury, to send rain on your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hands. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow. 13 The LORD will grant you preeminence, not subordination; you will be only above and never beneath, if you obey the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you today. 14 Do not turn aside from any of the words I am commanding you today, either to the right or to the left, by following other gods to serve them.

Curses for Disobedience

15 But if you do not obey the voice of the LORD your God, to keep all His commands and statutes that I am giving you today, then all these curses will come upon you and overtake you. 16 You will be cursed in the city and cursed in the country. 17 Cursed will be your basket and your kneading bowl. 18 Cursed will be the fruit of your womb, the produce of your ground, the calves of your herds, and the lambs of your flocks. 19 You will be cursed when you come in and cursed when you go out.

20 The LORD will send against you curses, confusion, and rebuke in every undertaking you pursue, until you are destroyed and quickly perish, because of the evil of your deeds, by which you have forsaken Me. 21 The LORD will make pestilence cling to you until He has consumed you from the land you are entering to possess. 22 The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, fiery heat, the sword, blight, and mildew; and they will pursue you until you perish. 23 The sky above your head will be bronze, and the earth beneath you will be iron. 24 The LORD will make the rain of your land dust and powder; it will come down on you from the sky until you are destroyed.

25 The LORD will cause you to be defeated before your enemies. You will march out against them by one road but flee before them by seven, and you will become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth. 26 Your carcasses will be food for every bird of the sky and for the beasts of the earth, with no one to frighten them away. 27 The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, scabs, and itch, from which you cannot be healed. 28 The LORD will strike you with madness, blindness, and confusion of heart. 29 You will grope at noon as the blind grope in darkness, and you will not prosper in your ways. You will be only oppressed and robbed continually, and there will be no one to save you.

30 You will betroth a wife, but another man will sleep with her. You will build a house, but not live in it. You will plant a vineyard, but not enjoy its fruit. 31 Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, but you will not eat any of it. Your donkey will be seized from before you and will not be returned to you. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and there will be no one to rescue you. 32 Your sons and daughters will be given to another people, while your eyes look on and waste away with longing for them all day long; but you will be powerless to help. 33 A people you do not know will eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor, and you will be only oppressed and crushed continually. 34 You will be driven mad by the sights your eyes see.

35 The LORD will strike you with severe boils on your knees and legs, from the sole of your foot to the top of your head, an affliction from which you cannot recover. 36 The LORD will bring you and your king, whom you set over you, to a nation neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you will serve other gods—wood and stone. 37 You will become an object of horror, a proverb, and a byword among all the peoples where the LORD drives you. 38 You will sow much seed in the field, but gather little, because locusts will consume it. 39 You will plant vineyards and tend them, but you will not drink the wine or gather the grapes, because worms will eat them. 40 You will have olive trees throughout your territory, but you will not anoint yourself with oil, because your olives will drop off. 41 You will father sons and daughters, but they will not remain yours, because they will go into captivity. 42 Locusts will take possession of all your trees and the produce of your ground.

43 The foreigner residing among you will rise higher and higher above you, while you sink lower and lower. 44 He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him; he will be your superior, and you will be his subordinate. 45 All these curses will come upon you and pursue and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God or keep His commands and statutes that He gave you. 46 They will be a sign and a wonder against you and your descendants forever. 47 Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and gladness of heart in the midst of all abundance, 48 You will serve your enemies, whom the LORD will send against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and lack of everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until He has destroyed you.

Invasion and Siege

49 The LORD will bring against you a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, swooping down like an eagle—a nation whose language you do not understand, 50 a grim-faced nation that will show no respect to the elderly and no mercy to the young. 51 It will devour the offspring of your livestock and the produce of your soil until you are destroyed; it will leave you no grain, new wine, or oil, no calves or lambs, until it has annihilated you. 52 It will besiege you in all your towns throughout your land until your high and fortified walls, in which you trust, come down; it will besiege you in every town throughout the land the LORD your God has given you. 53 In the siege and distress that your enemy will inflict upon you, you will eat the fruit of your womb—the flesh of your sons and daughters whom the LORD your God has given you.

54 The most tender and delicate man among you will begrudge his brother, the wife of his bosom, and the children he has left, 55 refusing to give any of them the flesh of his children that he is eating, because he has nothing else left in the siege and distress your enemy will inflict upon you in all your cities. 56 The most tender and delicate woman among you—so delicate that she would not venture to set the sole of her foot on the ground—will begrudge the husband of her bosom, her son, and her daughter, 57 as well as the afterbirth that comes from between her feet and the children she bears, because she will eat them in secret for lack of everything, in the siege and distress your enemy will inflict upon you in your cities.

Exile and Final Judgment

58 If you do not carefully observe all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and awesome name—the LORD your God— 59 then the LORD will bring upon you and your descendants extraordinary plagues—great and lasting plagues—and severe and lasting illnesses. 60 He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt that you dreaded, and they will cling to you. 61 The LORD will also bring upon you every sickness and plague not recorded in this Book of the Law, until you are destroyed.

62 You were as numerous as the stars of heaven, but you will be left few in number, because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God. 63 Just as the LORD rejoiced over you to do you good and multiply you, so the LORD will now rejoice over you to destroy you and annihilate you; and you will be uprooted from the land you are entering to possess. 64 The LORD will scatter you among all the nations, from one end of the earth to the other, and there you will serve other gods—wood and stone—that neither you nor your fathers have known.

65 Among those nations you will find no rest, nor will the sole of your foot find a resting place; there the LORD will give you a trembling heart, failing eyes, and a despairing soul. 66 Your life will hang in doubt before you; you will be in dread night and day, never sure of your life. 67 In the morning you will say, 'If only it were evening!' and in the evening, 'If only it were morning!'—because of the terror in your heart and the sights your eyes will see. 68 The LORD will send you back to Egypt in ships, by the route I said you would never see again. There you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, but no one will buy you.
