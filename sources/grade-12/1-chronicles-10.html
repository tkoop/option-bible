The Philistines Defeat Israel and Kill Saul

1 The Philistines waged war against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before them; many fell slain on Mount Gilboa. 2 The Philistines pressed hard after Saul and his sons, and they killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul. 3 The battle grew fierce against Saul; the archers overtook him, and he was wounded by their arrows.

4 Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised men come and abuse me." But his armor-bearer was terrified and would not do it, so Saul took the sword and fell on it.

5 When the armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died. 6 Thus Saul died, together with his three sons and all his household. 7 When all the Israelites in the valley saw that their forces had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities, and the Philistines came and occupied them.

The Philistines Desecrate Saul's Body

8 The next day the Philistines came to strip the slain, and they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa. 9 They stripped him, took his head and his armor, and sent messengers throughout the land of the Philistines to proclaim the news to their idols and to the people. 10 They placed his armor in the temple of their gods and fastened his skull in the temple of Dagon.

Jabesh-gilead Buries Saul

11 When all Jabesh-gilead heard everything the Philistines had done to Saul, 12 all their valiant men arose, took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons, brought them to Jabesh, buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

The Reason for Saul's Death

13 So Saul died for his unfaithfulness to the the LORD: he did not keep the word of the the LORD, and he consulted a medium for guidance. 14 He did not seek the the LORD, so the the LORD put him to death and turned the kingdom over to David son of Jesse.
