Micah Laments the Moral Decay of His Nation

1 Woe to me! I have become like one who gathers summer fruit after the vintage has been gleaned: no cluster remains to eat, and no first-ripe fig that my soul craves. 2 The faithful have vanished from the land; not one upright person remains among humanity. They all lurk for blood, and each man hunts his brother as though casting a net. 3 Both hands are adept at evil; the ruler demands a bribe, the judge is open to a price, and the powerful man speaks whatever his soul craves, so they weave it all together.

4 The best of them is like a briar; the most upright is worse than a thorn hedge. The day of your watchmen has come; now your punishment will be their confusion. 5 Do not trust a neighbor; do not place confidence in a friend; guard the words of your mouth from the one who lies in your bosom. 6 For a son dishonors his father, a daughter rises against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's enemies are those of his own household.

The Prophet Places His Hope in the LORD

7 But I will look to the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation; my God will hear me. 8 Do not rejoice over me, my enemy: though I have fallen, I will rise again; though I dwell in darkness, the LORD will be light to me. 9 I will bear the indignation of the LORD, for I have sinned against Him, until He pleads my cause and executes judgment on my behalf; He will bring me out into the light, and I will see His righteousness. 10 Then my enemy will see it, and shame will cover the one who said to me, "Where is the LORD your God?" My eyes will gaze upon her; now she will be trampled like mud in the streets.

The LORD Will Restore and Vindicate His People

11 A day for building your walls! On that day the boundary will be extended far. 12 In that day they will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt, from Egypt to the Euphrates, from sea to sea and mountain to mountain. 13 Yet the earth will be desolate because of its inhabitants, as the fruit of their deeds.

14 Shepherd your people with your staff, the flock of your inheritance, who dwell alone in a forest in the midst of Carmel; let them graze in Bashan and Gilead as in the days of old. 15 As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt, I will show them wonders. 16 The nations will see and be ashamed of all their might; they will put their hands over their mouths; their ears will be deaf. 17 They will lick the dust like a serpent, like the crawling things of the earth; they will come trembling from their strongholds; they will turn in dread to the LORD our God, and they will fear You.

God's Unmatched Mercy and Faithfulness

18 Who is a God like You, who pardons iniquity and passes over the transgression of the remnant of His heritage? He does not hold His anger forever, for He delights in steadfast love. 19 He will again have compassion on us; He will subdue our iniquities; and You will cast all their sins into the depths of the sea. 20 You will give truth to Jacob and steadfast love to Abraham, as You swore to our fathers from the days of old.
