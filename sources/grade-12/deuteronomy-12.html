Destroying Pagan Worship and Choosing the LORD’s Place

1 These are the statutes and ordinances you must keep and perform all the days you live on the earth—in the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, gives you to possess. 2 You must utterly destroy all the places where the nations you are dispossessing served their gods—on the high mountains, on the hills, and under every leafy tree. 3 You must tear down their altars, smash their sacred pillars, burn their Asherah poles with fire, and cut down the carved images of their gods; you must also wipe out their names from that place. 4 You shall not do this to the LORD your God.

5 Instead, you must seek the place that the LORD your God chooses from among all your tribes to establish His name as His dwelling, and you must go there. 6 There you must bring your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tithes, the heave offering of your hand, your vows, your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herd and flock. 7 There you shall eat before the LORD your God, and you shall rejoice in all your undertakings—you and your households—as the LORD your God has blessed you. 8 You must not do as we are doing here today, each person doing whatever is right in his own eyes. 9 For you have not yet come to the rest and to the inheritance that the LORD your God is giving you. 10 But when you cross the Jordan and dwell in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to inherit, and He gives you rest from all your enemies around you, so that you dwell securely, 11 then to the place that the LORD your God chooses to make His name dwell, you shall bring all that I command you: your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tithes, the heave offering of your hand, and all your choice vows that you vow to the LORD. 12 And you shall rejoice before the LORD your God—you, your sons, your daughters, your male and female servants, and the Levite who is within your gates—because he has no portion or inheritance with you. 13 Take heed to yourself that you do not offer your burnt offerings in every place you see. 14 But in the place that the LORD chooses in one of your tribes, there you shall do all that I command you.

Permitted Meat, the Prohibition of Blood, and Joyful Worship

15 However, you may slaughter and eat meat in all your towns, according to the blessing of the LORD your God that He has given you; the unclean and the clean alike may eat it, as the gazelle and the deer. 16 You shall not eat the blood; you must pour it on the earth like water. 17 You may not eat within your towns the tithe of your grain, your new wine, or your oil, nor the firstborn of your herd or flock, nor any vow you make, nor your freewill offerings, nor the heave offering of your hand. 18 Instead, you must eat them before the LORD your God in the place that the LORD your God chooses—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, and the Levite who is within your towns—and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God in all your undertakings. 19 Take heed to yourself that you do not forsake the Levite as long as you live in your land. 20 When the LORD your God enlarges your territory, as He has promised you, and you say, “I want to eat meat,” because your soul craves meat, you may eat meat whenever you desire. 21 If the place that the LORD your God chooses to put His name is too far from you, then you may slaughter from your herd and flock that the LORD has given you, as I have commanded you, and you may eat within your towns whenever you desire. 22 Just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you may eat them; the unclean and the clean alike may eat them. 23 Only be sure that you do not eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the flesh. 24 You shall not eat it; you must pour it on the earth like water. 25 You shall not eat it, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, because you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD. 26 But your holy things that are yours, and your vows, you shall take and go to the place that the LORD chooses. 27 And you shall offer your burnt offerings—the flesh and the blood—on the altar of the LORD your God; and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of the LORD your God, and you shall eat the flesh. 28 Observe and obey all these words that I command you, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, because you do what is good and right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

Guarding Against the Nations’ Abominations

29 When the LORD your God cuts off before you the nations that you are going in to dispossess, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land, 30 take care that you are not ensnared after them, after they are destroyed from before you, and that you do not inquire about their gods, saying, “How did these nations serve their gods? I will do the same.” 31 You shall not do so to the LORD your God, for everything that is an abomination to the LORD, which He hates, they have done for their gods; for they even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire to their gods. 32 You shall keep everything that I command you to do; you shall not add to it, nor shall you take away from it.
