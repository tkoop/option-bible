Jeremiah’s Prophetic Ministry Begins

1 These are the words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests residing in Anathoth in the territory of Benjamin. 2 The word of the the LORD came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign. 3 It also came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, and until Jerusalem was carried into exile in the fifth month.

Jeremiah’s Divine Appointment

4 The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 5 “Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you; before you were born, I set you apart; I appointed you a prophet to the nations.”

6 Then I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! I do not know how to speak, for I am merely a youth.”

7 But the the LORD said to me, “Do not say, ‘I am a youth,’ for you must go to everyone to whom I send you, and you must speak whatever I command you. 8 Do not fear them, for I am with you to rescue you,” declares the the LORD. 9 Then the the LORD stretched out His hand, touched my mouth, and said to me, “See, I have placed My words in your mouth. 10 See, today I have appointed you over nations and kingdoms, to uproot and demolish, to destroy and overthrow, to build and to plant.”

Visions of Judgment

11 The word of the the LORD came to me: “Jeremiah, what do you see?”

I said, “I see a branch of an almond tree.”

12 The the LORD said to me, “You have seen correctly, for I am watching over My word to fulfill it.”

13 The word of the the LORD came to me a second time: “What do you see?”

I answered, “I see a boiling pot, tilted toward us from the north.”

14 The the LORD said to me, “From the north, disaster will be unleashed upon all the inhabitants of the land.”

The Coming Invasion from the North

15 “For behold, I am summoning all the families of the kingdoms of the north,” declares the the LORD. “They will come and each will set his throne at the entrance of Jerusalem's gates, against all its walls on every side, and against all the cities of Judah. 16 I will pronounce My judgments against them for all their wickedness, because they have forsaken Me, burned incense to other gods, and worshiped the work of their hands. 17 But you, prepare yourself for action; arise and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be terrified before them, or I will terrify you before them. 18 Behold, today I have made you a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the entire land—against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests, and the people of the land. 19 They will fight against you, but they will not prevail against you, for I am with you to deliver you,” declares the the LORD.
