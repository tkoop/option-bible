David Seeks Provisions at Nob

1 David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech trembled when he met David and asked him, "Why are you alone, with no one accompanying you?"

2 David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king has commanded me on a matter and told me, 'Let no one know anything about the mission I am sending you on or the orders I have given you.' I have directed the young men to a specified place. 3 Now, what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever you can provide."

4 Ahimelech answered David, "I have no ordinary bread on hand; there is only consecrated bread, provided the young men have kept themselves from women."

5 David answered the priest, "Women have indeed been kept from us, as is customary when I set out. The young men's bodies are holy even on missions that are not holy, and how much more so today!"

6 So the priest gave him the consecrated bread, because no bread was there except the bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before the LORD to be replaced by hot bread on the day it was taken away. 7 Now one of Saul's servants was there that day, detained before the LORD; his name was Doeg the Edomite, chief of Saul's shepherds.

David Takes Goliath's Sword

8 David said to Ahimelech, "Do you have a spear or sword on hand? I have brought neither my sword nor my equipment, because the king's business was urgent."

9 The priest replied, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah, is here, wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want it, take it, for there is no other here."

David said, "There is none like it; give it to me."

David Feigns Madness in Gath

10 David rose and fled that day from Saul and went to Achish, king of Gath.

11 The servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? Is it not he of whom they sing in the dances, 'Saul has slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

12 David took these words to heart and was very afraid of Achish, king of Gath.

13 So he changed his behavior before them and feigned madness while in their presence; he scribbled on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down his beard.

14 Achish said to his servants, "Look, you see a man behaving like a madman. Why did you bring him to me? 15 Do I lack madmen, that you have brought this one to rave before me? Must this man come into my house?"
