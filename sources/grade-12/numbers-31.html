The LORD Commands Vengeance on Midian

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Avenge the Israelites on the Midianites. Afterward you will die and be gathered to your ancestors.” 3 So Moses spoke to the people, saying, “Arm some of your men for war, that they may go against Midian to execute the LORD’s vengeance on Midian. 4 You shall send a thousand from each tribe of Israel to the war.” 5 So from the thousands of Israel, twelve thousand armed for war were delivered, a thousand from each tribe. 6 Moses sent them to the war, a thousand from each tribe, with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the war, with the holy vessels and the trumpets for the alarm in his hand.

Israel Defeats Midian

7 They fought against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and killed every male. 8 They killed the kings of Midian: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword. 9 The Israelites took captive the women of Midian and their little ones, and plundered all their livestock, all their herds, and all their wealth. 10 They burned all their cities where they dwelt and all their encampments with fire. 11 They took all the spoil and all the plunder, both man and beast. 12 They brought the captives, the plunder, and the spoil to Moses and Eleazar the priest and to the congregation of the Israelites, to the camp on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.

Purification After the Battle

13 Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp. 14 Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds who returned from the war. 15 Moses said to them, “Have you spared all the women? 16 Behold, these were the ones who, on Balaam’s advice, caused the Israelites to act treacherously against the LORD in the matter of Peor, so that the plague came upon the congregation of the LORD. 17 Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has known a man by lying with him. 18 But all the young girls who have not known a man by lying with him, keep alive for yourselves. 19 And you, camp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has killed a person and everyone who has touched a slain person shall purify themselves on the third day and on the seventh day, both you and your captives. 20 And every garment, every leather thing, everything made of goats’ hair, and every article of wood, you shall purify.”

21 Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who had gone into battle, “This is the statute of the law that the LORD commanded Moses: 22 Only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead— 23 everything that can go into fire, you shall pass through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless it shall be purified with the water of purification; and everything that cannot go into fire you shall pass through water. 24 You shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you shall be clean; and afterward you may come into the camp.”

Division of the Plunder

25 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 26 “Take a count of the plunder that was taken, both of man and of beast, you and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the fathers’ houses of the congregation; 27 and divide the plunder between the warriors who went out to battle and all the congregation. 28 And levy a tribute for the LORD from the men of war who went out to battle: one soul out of five hundred, both of man and of cattle, of donkeys and of sheep. 29 Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as the LORD’s offering. 30 And from the half of the Israelites take one portion out of fifty, of man, of cattle, of donkeys, and of sheep, of all the livestock, and give them to the Levites who keep guard over the tabernacle of the LORD.” 31 So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD commanded Moses.

32 The rest of the plunder that the people of the army had plundered was 675,000 sheep, 33 72,000 cattle, 34 61,000 donkeys, 35 and 32,000 persons in all, women who had not known a man by lying with him. 36 The half, the portion of those who went out to war, was 337,500 sheep, 37 and the tribute to the LORD from the sheep was 675; 38 the cattle were 36,000, of which the tribute to the LORD was 72; 39 the donkeys were 30,500, of which the tribute to the LORD was 61; 40 the persons were 16,000, of which the tribute to the LORD was 32 persons. 41 Moses gave the tribute, the LORD’s offering, to Eleazar the priest, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

42 From the half of the Israelites, which Moses had divided from the men who had gone to war— 43 the congregation’s half was 337,500 sheep, 44 36,000 cattle, 45 30,500 donkeys, 46 and 16,000 persons. 47 Moses took from the half of the Israelites one portion out of fifty, of man and of beast, and gave them to the Levites who kept guard over the tabernacle of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Officers’ Offering

48 The officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, came near to Moses 49 and said to Moses, “Your servants have taken a count of the men of war who are under our command, and not one of us is missing. 50 We have brought as an offering to the LORD the gold articles that each man found: armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and pendants, to make atonement for ourselves before the LORD.” 51 Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, all the articles of worked gold. 52 The gold of the offering that the commanders of thousands and of hundreds raised for the LORD was 16,750 shekels. 53 (Each man of the army had plundered for himself.) 54 Moses and Eleazar the priest brought the gold into the tent of meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD.
