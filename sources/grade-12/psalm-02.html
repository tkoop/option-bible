The Nations Rage Against the LORD and His Anointed

1 Why do the nations rage, and why do the peoples plot in vain? 2 The kings of the earth take their stand, and the rulers conspire together against the LORD and against His Anointed, 3 saying, "Let us break their chains and cast off their cords."

4 He who sits in heaven laughs; the Lord holds them in derision. 5 Then He speaks to them in His wrath and terrifies them in His fury: 6 "As for Me, I have installed My King on Zion, My holy mountain."

7 I will proclaim the decree:

"The LORD said to Me, 'You are My Son; today I have begotten You. 8 Ask of Me, and I will make the nations Your inheritance and the ends of the earth Your possession. 9 You shall break them with an iron rod and shatter them like a potter's vessel.'"

10 Now then, O kings, be wise; be warned, O judges of the earth. 11 Serve the LORD with fear and rejoice with trembling. 12 Pay homage to the Son, lest He be angry and you perish in the way, for His wrath can flare up in a moment. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.
