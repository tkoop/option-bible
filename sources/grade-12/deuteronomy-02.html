The Journey Through the Wilderness

1 Then we reversed course and set out toward the wilderness along the route to the Red Sea, as the LORD had directed me, and for many days we roamed about Mount Seir.

Divine Directions Concerning Seir

2 Then the LORD said to me, 3 "You have circled this hill country long enough; turn northward. 4 Command the people that they shall pass through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who inhabit Seir. They will be exceedingly afraid of you, so exercise utmost caution. 5 Do not antagonize them, for I will not grant you any of their land—not even a foothold—because I have assigned Mount Seir to Esau as his possession. 6 You are to purchase food from them with silver so that you may eat, and you are to purchase water from them with silver so that you may drink. 7 For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has watched over your journey through this vast wilderness. The LORD your God has accompanied you these forty years, and you have not been deprived of anything."

8 So we passed by our brothers, the descendants of Esau, who inhabit Seir. We turned away from the Arabah road, which comes up from Elath and Ezion-geber, and traveled along the road of the wilderness of Moab.

Commands Regarding Moab and Ammon

9 Then the LORD said to me, "Do not afflict Moab or incite them to war, for I will not give you any of their land, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as their possession."

10 The Emim formerly inhabited that region—a people great, numerous, and tall, like the Anakim. 11 Like the Anakim, they too are classified as Rephaim, although the Moabites designate them Emim. 12 The Horites once dwelt in Seir, but the descendants of Esau expelled and exterminated them and occupied their territory, just as Israel did in the land that the LORD granted them as their possession.

13 "Now rise up and cross the Zered Valley." So we crossed the Zered Valley.

14 The interval we spent traveling from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered Valley amounted to thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of warriors had perished from the camp, as the LORD had sworn to them. 15 Indeed, the LORD's hand was against them, to eradicate them from the camp, until they were eliminated.

16 Now when all the fighting men among the people had died, 17 the LORD said to me, 18 "Today you are to cross the border of Moab at Ar. 19 When you approach the Ammonites, do not afflict them or incite them to war, for I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as their possession."

20 That land too was considered the domain of the Rephaim, who once inhabited it, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim. 21 They were a people great, numerous, and tall, like the Anakim. Yet the LORD annihilated them before the Ammonites, who expelled them and occupied their territory. 22 This is what He did for the descendants of Esau who reside in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites before them. They expelled them and occupied their territory, and they remain there to this day. 23 Moreover, the Avvim, who inhabited villages as far as Gaza—the Caphtorites originated in Caphtor and destroyed them and occupied their territory.

The Defeat of Sihon of Heshbon

24 "Arise, set out, and cross the Arnon Valley. Observe, I have delivered Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his domain into your hand. Begin to take possession of it, and engage him in battle. 25 This day I will commence instilling the dread and fear of you among all the nations beneath heaven, who will hear the report concerning you and tremble and be distressed because of you."

26 So I dispatched messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with overtures of peace, saying, 27 "Permit me to pass through your land; I will remain on the main road. I will not deviate to the right or to the left. 28 You may sell me food for money so that I may eat, and provide me water for money so that I may drink. Just let me pass through on foot, 29 as the descendants of Esau who dwell in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD our God is bestowing upon us."

30 But Sihon king of Heshbon refused to allow us passage, for the LORD your God had hardened his spirit and rendered his heart obstinate, so that He might deliver him into your hand, as is the case this very day.

31 Then the LORD said to me, "Behold, I have begun to deliver Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession of his land."

32 Then Sihon and all his people advanced to meet us in battle at Jahaz. 33 And the LORD our God delivered him over to us, and we struck him down, together with his sons and all his people. 34 At that time we captured all his cities and devoted every city to destruction, along with its men, women, and children. We left no survivors. 35 We carried off for ourselves only the livestock and the spoil of the cities we had captured. 36 From Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, as far as Gilead, no city proved too fortified for us. The LORD our God gave us all of them. 37 But we did not approach the land of the Ammonites, or the land along the Jabbok River, or the cities of the hill country, or any place that the LORD our God had forbidden us.
