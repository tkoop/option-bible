Hezekiah Seeks the Prophet's Counsel

1 When King Hezekiah heard the report, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and entered the house of the LORD. 2 He sent Eliakim, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and the senior priests, all clothed in sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.

3 They told him, "This is what Hezekiah says: 'Today is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace. Children have come to the point of birth, but there is no strength to deliver them.' 4 Perhaps the the LORD your God has heard the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master, the king of Assyria, sent to defy the living God, and He will rebuke the words that the the LORD your God has heard. Therefore pray for the remnant that still survives."

5 So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

6 Isaiah said to them, "Tell your master: This is what the the LORD says: Do not be afraid of the words you have heard, with which the servants of the king of Assyria have blasphemed Me. 7 I am going to put a spirit in him so that he hears a rumor and returns to his own land; there I will make him fall by the sword."

Sennacherib's Renewed Threat

8 When the Rabshakeh heard that the king of Assyria had left Lachish, he returned and found him fighting against Libnah. 9 Now Sennacherib heard that Tirhakah, king of Cush, had marched out to fight against him, so he sent messengers to Hezekiah with this message:

"Say to Hezekiah king of Judah: Do not let your God, in whom you trust, deceive you by saying, 'Jerusalem will not be handed over to the king of Assyria.' 11 Surely you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the lands—how they devoted them to destruction. And will you be rescued? 12 Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed rescue them—Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar? 13 Where is the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, or Ivvah?"

Hezekiah's Prayer

14 Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers' hands, read it, and went up to the house of the LORD. There he spread it out before the LORD. 15 And Hezekiah prayed to the LORD:

"O the LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, enthroned above the cherubim, You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made heaven and earth. 17 Incline Your ear, O the LORD, and hear; open Your eyes, O the LORD, and see. Hear all the words Sennacherib has sent to defy the living God. 18 It is true, O the LORD, that the kings of Assyria have laid waste all the lands and their territories. 19 They have thrown their gods into the fire, for they were not gods at all, but the work of human hands—wood and stone—so they destroyed them. 20 Now, O the LORD our God, save us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone are the LORD."

The Lord's Answer and Assyria's Defeat

21 Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent word to Hezekiah: "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Because you have prayed to Me about Sennacherib king of Assyria, 22 this is the word the LORD has spoken concerning him: The virgin daughter of Zion despises you and mocks you; the daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind you. 23 Whom have you reproached and blasphemed? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes to the heights? Against the Holy One of Israel. 24 By your servants you have reproached the Lord and said, 'With my many chariots I have ascended the heights of the mountains, the farthest reaches of Lebanon; I will cut down its tallest cedars and its finest cypresses; I will reach its farthest height, the forest of Carmel. 25 I have dug wells and drunk foreign water; with the soles of my feet I have dried up all the streams of Egypt.'

"26 Have you not heard? Long ago I ordained it; in ancient days I planned it. Now I have brought it to pass, that you should turn fortified cities into heaps of rubble. 27 Their inhabitants are weak; they are dismayed and put to shame. They are like grass in the field, like tender green shoots, like grass on the rooftops—blighted before it can grow. 28 But I know your sitting down, your going out and your coming in, and your raging against Me. 29 Because you rage against Me and your complacency has reached My ears, I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth, and I will turn you back by the way you came.

"30 This will be a sign for you: This year you will eat what grows by itself; in the second year, what springs from that; but in the third year you will sow and reap, plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 31 The surviving remnant of the house of Judah will take root below and bear fruit above. 32 For out of Jerusalem will come a remnant, and survivors from Mount Zion. The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this.

"33 Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning the king of Assyria: He will not enter this city or shoot an arrow here; he will not advance against it with a shield or build a siege ramp against it. 34 He will return by the way he came; he will not enter this city, declares the LORD. 35 I will defend this city and save it for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David."

36 Then the angel of the LORD went out and struck down 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. When the people arose early in the morning, there lay all the dead bodies. 37 So Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and withdrew; he returned and stayed in Nineveh. 38 One day, while he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer struck him with the sword and escaped to the land of Ararat. His son Esarhaddon reigned in his place.
