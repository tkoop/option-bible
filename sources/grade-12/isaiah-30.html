Woe to the Rebellious Children Who Trust in Egypt

1 Woe to the rebellious children, declares the the LORD, who take counsel, but not from Me, and who devise plans, but not by My Spirit, adding sin to sin. 2 They go down to Egypt without asking My counsel, to take refuge in Pharaoh’s strength and to trust in Egypt’s shadow. 3 Therefore Pharaoh’s strength will be your shame, and trust in Egypt’s shadow will be your humiliation. 4 For his princes are at Zoan, and his envoys reach Hanes; 5 yet they will all be put to shame by a people who cannot help them, who bring neither aid nor profit, but only shame and reproach.

The Burden of the Beasts of the South

6 An oracle concerning the beasts of the South: through a land of hardship and distress, of lioness and lion, of viper and flying serpent, they carry their riches on donkeys’ backs and their treasures on camels’ humps, to a people who cannot help. 7 Egypt’s help is futile and empty; therefore I have called her “Rahab the Do-Nothing.”

The Rebellious People Reject the LORD’s Instruction

8 Now go, write it on a tablet before them, and inscribe it in a book, that it may stand for the time to come, forever and ever. 9 For this is a rebellious people, deceitful children, children who refuse to hear the instruction of the the LORD. 10 They say to the seers, “Do not see,” and to the prophets, “Do not prophesy to us what is right; speak to us pleasant things, prophesy illusions. 11 Turn aside from the path, get out of the way, and let us hear no more about the Holy One of Israel.”

12 Therefore thus says the Holy One of Israel: “Because you reject this word and trust in oppression and deceit, and rely on them, 13 this iniquity will be to you like a breach ready to fall, a bulge in a high wall, whose collapse comes suddenly, in an instant. 14 He will smash it like a potter’s jar, crushed so thoroughly that no fragment will be found among its pieces large enough to take fire from the hearth or draw water from the cistern.”

15 For thus says the Lord, the the LORD, the Holy One of Israel: “In returning and rest you will be saved; in quietness and trust will be your strength. But you were unwilling.”

16 Instead, you said, “No, we will flee on horses”—so you will flee—and, “We will ride on swift horses”—so swift will be those who pursue you. 17 A thousand will flee at the threat of one; at the threat of five you will flee, until you are left like a flagstaff on a mountaintop and like a banner on a hill.

The LORD’s Gracious Restoration and Final Judgment

18 Therefore the the LORD waits to be gracious to you; therefore He will rise up to show you mercy. For the the LORD is a God of justice; blessed are all who wait for Him. 19 O people of Zion, who dwell in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. He will be gracious to you at the sound of your cry; when He hears it, He will answer you. 20 Although the Lord gives you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, your Teacher will no longer hide Himself, but your eyes will see your Teacher. 21 Your ears will hear a word behind you, saying, “This is the way; walk in it,” whenever you turn to the right or to the left. 22 You will defile your silver-plated idols and your gold-plated images. You will throw them away like menstrual cloths and say, “Be gone!” 23 Then He will give rain for the seed you sow in the ground, and the bread that the ground yields will be rich and plentiful. In that day your livestock will graze in broad pastures. 24 The oxen and donkeys that work the soil will eat salted fodder winnowed with shovel and pitchfork. 25 On every high mountain and every lofty hill there will be streams and watercourses in the day of great slaughter, when the towers fall. 26 The light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be seven times brighter, like the light of seven days, in the day the the LORD binds up the brokenness of His people and heals the wound He inflicted.

27 Behold, the name of the the LORD comes from afar, burning with His anger and heavy with smoke. His lips are full of fury, and His tongue is like a devouring fire. 28 His breath is like an overflowing stream that reaches to the neck, to sift the nations in a sieve of destruction and to put a misleading bridle in the jaws of the peoples. 29 You will have a song as in the night of a consecrated feast, and gladness of heart as when one goes with a flute to the mountain of the the LORD, to the Rock of Israel. 30 The LORD will make the splendor of His voice heard and show the descent of His arm, with the indignation of His anger and the flame of a devouring fire, with a cloudburst, a tempest, and hailstones. 31 For at the voice of the the LORD Assyria will be shattered, as He strikes with the rod. 32 Every stroke of the appointed rod that the the LORD brings down on him will be to the sound of tambourines and harps, as He fights with His uplifted arm. 33 For Topheth has long been prepared; it is made ready for the king. Its pyre is deep and wide, with plenty of fire and wood. The breath of the the LORD, like a stream of brimstone, sets it ablaze.
