The Wise Woman and the Foolish One

1 Every wise woman builds her house, but a foolish one tears it down with her own hands. 2 He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but he who is crooked in his ways despises Him. 3 In a fool’s mouth is a rod of pride, but wise lips preserve them.

4 Where no oxen are, the manger is clean, but an abundant harvest comes by the strength of the ox. 5 A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies. 6 A mocker seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge comes easily to the discerning. 7 Leave the presence of a fool, for you will gain no knowledge from his lips.

8 The wisdom of the prudent is to discern his way, but the folly of fools is deceit. 9 Fools mock at guilt, but goodwill is found among the upright. 10 The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares its joy. 11 The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish.

The Fear of the LORD and the Path of Life

12 There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death. 13 Even in laughter the heart may ache, and joy may end in grief. 14 The backslider in heart will be filled with his own ways, but a good man will be satisfied from above. 15 The simple believes every word, but the prudent gives thought to his steps.

16 A wise man fears and turns from evil, but a fool is reckless and overconfident. 17 A quick-tempered man acts foolishly, and a man of wicked schemes is hated. 18 The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge. 19 Evil men bow down before the good, and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

20 A poor man is hated even by his neighbor, but the rich have many friends. 21 He who despises his neighbor sins, but blessed is he who is kind to the poor. 22 Do not those who plan evil go astray? But lovingkindness and truth belong to those who plan good. 23 In all toil there is profit, but mere talk leads only to poverty. 24 The crown of the wise is their wealth, but the folly of fools is only folly.

Righteousness, Wealth, and the King

25 A truthful witness saves lives, but a false witness breathes deceit. 26 In the fear of the LORD there is strong confidence, and His children will have a place of refuge. 27 The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning one away from the snares of death. 28 In a multitude of people is a king’s glory, but without people a prince is ruined.

29 He who is slow to anger has great understanding, but he who is quick-tempered exalts folly. 30 A tranquil heart gives life to the flesh, but envy makes the bones rot. 31 He who oppresses the poor taunts his Maker, but he who is kind to the needy honors Him. 32 The wicked are brought down by their own calamity, but the righteous have a refuge when they die.

33 Wisdom rests in the heart of the discerning, but among fools it is made known. 34 Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to any people. 35 The king’s favor rests on a wise servant, but his wrath falls on one who brings shame.
