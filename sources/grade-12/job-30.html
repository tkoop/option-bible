Job Laments the Mockery of Worthless Young Men

“1 But now they deride me—men younger than I, whose fathers I would have deemed unworthy even to run with my sheepdogs. 2 What value could the strength of their hands have offered me, when their vitality had already withered? 3 Blighted by want and hard hunger, they gnaw the parched earth by night amid waste and desolation; 4 they gather saltwort among the shrubs and broom roots for sustenance.

“5 They are banished from human society and shouted at as though they were thieves. 6 They must dwell in the gullies of torrents, in holes of earth and rock. 7 Among the brush they bray; beneath the nettles they cluster. 8 A witless, nameless brood, they have been scourged from the land.

Job Becomes Their Byword and the Mob Advances

“9 Now I have become their song of mockery, a byword among them. 10 They detest me and keep their distance; they do not hesitate to spit when they see me. 11 Because God has loosed my cord and humbled me, they cast off all restraint before me. 12 On my right the rabble rise; they push away my feet and throw up causeways of ruin against me. 13 They tear up my path; they advance my ruin, and they need no accomplice. 14 They pour through a wide breach; amid the crashing ruin they roll on. 15 Terrors overwhelm me; my dignity is driven before the wind, and my prosperity has vanished like a cloud.

Job's Soul Is Poured Out and God's Hand Is Upon Him

“16 Now my very soul is poured out within me, and days of affliction have seized me. 17 Night pierces my bones; the pain that gnaws at me grants no rest. 18 My garment is distorted by violent force; it clings to me like the collar of my tunic. 19 God has cast me into the mire, and I have become like dust and ashes.

“20 I cry out to You for help, but You do not answer; I stand before You, and You merely look at me. 21 You have turned cruel toward me; with the might of Your hand You persecute me. 22 You lift me up into the wind and make me ride upon it; You toss me about in the roar of the storm. 23 For I know that You will bring me down to death, to the house appointed for all the living. 24 Yet does not a man in ruins stretch out his hand, crying for help in his calamity?

“25 Did I not weep for those whose days were hard? Was my soul not grieved for the needy? 26 But when I hoped for good, evil came; when I waited for light, darkness came. 27 My inward parts churn and find no rest; days of affliction come upon me. 28 I walk about darkened, though not by the sun; I stand in the assembly and cry for help.

“29 I have become a brother to jackals and a companion of ostriches. 30 My skin grows black and peels from me, and my bones are scorched with heat. 31 My lyre is tuned to mourning, and my flute to the sound of weeping.”
