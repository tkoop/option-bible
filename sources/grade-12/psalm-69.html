A Desperate Plea for Rescue from Overwhelming Waters

1 Save me, O God, for the waters have risen to my very soul. 2 I have sunk in the mire of the deep, where there is no foothold; I have entered the depths of the waters, and the flood has swept me away. 3 I am weary with my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes fail while I wait for my God. 4 Those who hate me without cause are more than the hairs of my head; mighty are my destroyers, my enemies who attack me with lies. What I did not steal, I am forced to restore.

5 O God, You know my folly, and my guilt is not hidden from You. 6 Let not those who hope in You be put to shame through me, O Lord, the LORD of Hosts; let not those who seek You be dishonored through me, O God of Israel.

7 For Your sake I have borne reproach; shame has covered my face. 8 I have become a stranger to my brothers, an alien to my mother’s sons. 9 For zeal for Your house has consumed me, and the reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen on me. 10 I wept and humbled my soul with fasting, but it became a reproach to me. 11 I made sackcloth my clothing, and I became a byword to them. 12 Those who sit at the gate gossip about me, and drunkards make songs about me.

13 But as for me, my prayer is to You, O the LORD, at an acceptable time. O God, in the abundance of Your mercy, answer me in the truth of Your salvation. 14 Deliver me from the mire, and let me not sink; let me be rescued from those who hate me and from the depths of the waters. 15 Let not the flood of waters sweep over me, nor the deep swallow me up, nor the pit close its mouth over me. 16 Answer me, O the LORD, for Your lovingkindness is good; according to the greatness of Your mercies, turn to me. 17 Hide not Your face from Your servant, for I am in distress; answer me quickly. 18 Draw near to my soul and redeem it; ransom me because of my enemies.

19 You know my reproach, my shame, and my dishonor; all my adversaries are before You. 20 Reproach has broken my heart, and I am sick; I looked for pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none. 21 They gave me gall for my food, and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

22 Let their table become a snare before them, and let their well-being become a trap. 23 Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and let their loins tremble continually. 24 Pour out Your indignation upon them, and let Your burning anger overtake them. 25 Let their camp be desolate; let no one dwell in their tents. 26 For they persecute him whom You have struck, and they recount the pain of those You have wounded. 27 Add iniquity to their iniquity, and let them not come into Your righteousness. 28 Let them be blotted out of the book of life; let them not be recorded among the righteous.

29 But I am poor and in pain; let Your salvation lift me up. 30 I will praise the Name of God with a song, and I will magnify Him with thanksgiving. 31 This will please the LORD more than an ox or a bull with horns and hooves. 32 The humble will see it and be glad; you who seek God, let your heart live. 33 For the LORD hears the needy and does not despise His prisoners. 34 Let heaven and earth praise Him, the seas and everything that moves in them. 35 For God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah, that they may dwell there and possess it. 36 The descendants of His servants will inherit it, and those who love His Name will dwell in it.
