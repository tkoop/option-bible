The Ziphites Betray David’s Hideout

1 The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, “Is David not hiding on the hill of Hachilah, facing the wasteland?” 2 So Saul rose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph with three thousand chosen men of Israel to search for David. 3 Saul camped beside the road on the hill of Hachilah, overlooking the wasteland, while David remained in the wilderness. When David saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness, 4 he sent out spies and learned for certain that Saul had indeed come. 5 Then David rose and went to the place where Saul had camped. He saw the spot where Saul lay, along with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army. Saul was lying in the camp, and the troops were camped around him.

David and Abishai Enter Saul’s Camp

6 David said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab’s brother, “Who will go down with me to Saul in the camp?”

Abishai said, “I will go down with you.”

7 So David and Abishai came to the troops by night. There lay Saul, sleeping in the camp, with his spear stuck in the ground at his head. Abner and the troops were lying around him.

8 Abishai said to David, “God has delivered your enemy into your hand today. Now let me pin him to the earth with one stroke of the spear; I will not need to strike him twice.”

9 But David said to Abishai, “Do not destroy him, for who can lay a hand on the the LORD’s anointed and be guiltless? 10 As the the LORD lives, either the the LORD will strike him down, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go down into battle and perish. 11 The LORD forbid that I should stretch out my hand against the the LORD’s anointed. Now, please take the spear that is at his head and the water jar, and let us go.”

12 So David took the spear and the water jar from beside Saul’s head, and they went away. No one saw or knew or woke, for they were all asleep; a deep sleep from the the LORD had fallen on them.

David Calls to Abner and Saul

13 David crossed to the other side and stood on top of the hill at a distance; there was a wide space between them.

14 David called out to the troops and to Abner son of Ner, “Will you not answer, Abner?”

Abner answered, “Who are you that calls to the king?”

15 David said to Abner, “You are a man, and who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not guarded your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy the king your lord. 16 This thing that you have done is not good. As the the LORD lives, you deserve to die, because you have not guarded your lord, the the LORD’s anointed. And now see where the king’s spear is and the water jar that was at his head.”

17 Saul recognized David’s voice and said, “Is this your voice, my son David?”

David said, “It is my voice, my lord the king.”

18 David said, “Why does my lord pursue his servant? What have I done? What wrong have I done?”

19 David said, “Now therefore, please, my lord the king, hear the words of your servant. If the the LORD has stirred you up against me, let Him accept an offering. But if it is men, they are cursed before the the LORD, for they have driven me out today from sharing in the the LORD’s inheritance, saying, ‘Go, serve other gods.’”

20 David said, “Now do not let me be killed far from the presence of the the LORD. For the king of Israel has come out to hunt a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains.”

Saul Confesses and Blesses David

21 Saul said, “I have sinned. Return, my son David, for I will harm you no more, because my life was precious in your eyes today. Behold, I have acted foolishly and erred greatly.”

22 David answered, “Here is the king’s spear. Let one of the young men come over and take it. 23 The The LORD will repay each man for his righteousness and his faithfulness, for the the LORD delivered you into my hand today, but I would not stretch out my hand against the the LORD’s anointed.”

24 David said, “And as your life was highly valued in my eyes today, so may my life be highly valued in the eyes of the the LORD, and may He deliver me from all trouble.”

25 Saul said to David, “Blessed are you, my son David! You will surely do great things and will surely prevail.” Then David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.
