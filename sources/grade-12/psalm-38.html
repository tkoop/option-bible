A Psalm of David: Confession, Suffering, and Hope

1 A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. The LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger, nor discipline me in Your wrath. 2 For Your arrows have sunk deep into me, and Your hand has fallen heavily upon me. 3 There is no health in my body because of Your indignation; there is no wholeness in my bones because of my sin. 4 For my iniquities have risen above my head; like a heavy burden, they weigh me down more than I can bear. 5 My wounds reek and fester because of my folly. 6 I am bent over and brought low; all day long I go about in mourning. 7 For my sides are filled with searing pain, and there is no health in my flesh. 8 I am feeble and utterly crushed; I groan because of the anguish of my heart.

Forsaken by Friends and Pursued by Enemies

9 O Lord, all my longing is before You, and my sighing is not hidden from You. 10 My heart pounds, my strength has left me, and even the light of my eyes has failed me. 11 My friends and companions keep their distance from my affliction, and my relatives stand far off. 12 Those who seek my life set traps for me; those who seek my ruin speak of destruction and plot deceit all day long. 13 But I am like a deaf man who does not hear, like a mute man who cannot open his mouth. 14 I have become like one who cannot hear, and in whose mouth there is no defense.

A Plea for the LORD's Deliverance

15 But I put my hope in You, the LORD; You will answer, O Lord my God. 16 For I said, "Do not let them gloat over me, those who boast against me when my foot slips." 17 For I am about to collapse, and my pain is always before me. 18 I confess my guilt; I am distressed over my sin. 19 My enemies are strong and active, and those who hate me without cause have multiplied. 20 Those who repay good with evil oppose me because I pursue what is good. 21 The LORD, do not forsake me; O my God, do not be far from me. 22 Come quickly to my aid, O Lord, my salvation.
