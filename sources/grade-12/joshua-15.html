The Territory Allotted to Judah

1 The allotment for the clans of the tribe of Judah extended southward to the border of Edom, to the wilderness of Zin at the farthest south. 2 Their southern boundary began at the end of the Salt Sea, at the bay that faces southward. 3 It went out southward of the ascent of Akrabbim, crossed Zin, ascended south of Kadesh-barnea, passed Hezron, rose to Addar, and turned toward Karka. 4 Then it passed Azmon, went out by the Brook of Egypt, and ended at the sea. This shall be your southern boundary.

5 The eastern boundary was the Salt Sea as far as the mouth of the Jordan, and the northern boundary ran from the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan. 6 It went up to Beth-hoglah and passed north of Beth-arabah. Then it ascended to the stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 7 From there it went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor, turning northward toward Gilgal, opposite the ascent of Adummim on the south side of the valley. It crossed to the waters of En-shemesh and ended at En-rogel. 8 Then it went up by the Valley of the Son of Hinnom at the southern shoulder of the Jebusite city, that is, Jerusalem, and ascended to the top of the mountain that lies west of the Valley of Hinnom, at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim.

9 From the top of the mountain the boundary extended to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah and reached the cities of Mount Ephron. Then it bent around to Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim. 10 From there it went westward to Mount Seir, passed along the northern shoulder of Mount Jearim, that is, Chesalon, descended to Beth-shemesh, and passed Timnah. 11 It went out northward to the shoulder of Ekron, bent around to Shikkeron, passed Mount Baalah, went out to Jabneel, and ended at the sea. 12 The western boundary was the Great Sea with its coastline. This is the boundary surrounding the clans of the people of Judah.

Caleb's Portion and the Conquest of Debir

13 According to the command of the LORD to Joshua, Caleb son of Jephunneh received a portion among the people of Judah: Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron. Arba was the father of Anak. 14 Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak—Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak.

15 He then went up against the inhabitants of Debir, whose former name was Kiriath-sepher. 16 Caleb said, "Whoever attacks Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as wife." 17 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, captured it, and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as wife.

18 When she arrived, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She dismounted from her donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you want?"

19 She said, "Give me a blessing. Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me also springs of water." And he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

20 This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Judah according to their clans.

The Cities of Judah

21 The cities belonging to the tribe of the people of Judah in the extreme south, toward the border of Edom, were Kabzeel, Eder, Jagur, Kinah, Dimonah, Adadah, Kedesh, Hazor, Ithnan, Ziph, Telem, Bealoth, Hazor-hadattah, Kerioth-hezron (that is, Hazor), Amam, Shema, Moladah, Hazar-gaddah, Heshmon, Beth-pelet, Hazar-shual, Beersheba, Bizjothjah, Baalah, Iim, Azem, Eltolad, Chesil, Hormah, Ziklag, Madmannah, Sansannah, Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon—twenty-nine cities with their villages.

33 In the lowland: Eshtaol, Zorah, Ashnah, Zanoah, En-gannim, Tappuah, Enam, Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh, Azekah, Shaaraim, Adithaim, Gedera, and Gederothaim—fourteen cities with their villages.

37 Zenan, Hadashah, Migdal-gad, Dilean, Mizpeh, Joktheel, Lachish, Bozkath, Eglon, Cabbon, Lahmas, Kithlish, Gederoth, Beth-dagon, Naamah, and Makkedah—sixteen cities with their villages.

42 Libnah, Ether, Ashan, Iphthah, Ashnah, Nezib, Keilah, Achzib, and Mareshah—nine cities with their villages.

45 Ekron, with its towns and villages; 46 from Ekron to the sea, all that lies beside Ashdod, with its villages; 47 Ashdod, with its towns and villages; Gaza, with its towns and villages; to the Brook of Egypt and the Great Sea with its boundary.

48 In the hill country: Shamir, Jattir, Socoh, Dannah, Kiriath-sannah (that is, Debir), Anab, Eshtemoh, Anim, Goshen, Holon, and Giloh—eleven cities with their villages.

52 Arab, Dumah, Eshan, Janim, Beth-tappuah, Aphekah, Humtah, Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron), and Zior—nine cities with their villages.

55 Maon, Carmel, Ziph, Juttah, Jezreel, Jokdeam, Zanoah, Kain, Gibeah, and Timnah—ten cities with their villages.

58 Halhul, Beth-zur, and Gedor; 59 Maarath, Beth-anoth, and Eltekon—six cities with their villages. 60 Kiriath-baal (that is, Kiriath-jearim) and Rabbah—two cities with their villages.

61 In the wilderness: Beth-arabah, Middin, Secacah, Nibshan, the City of Salt, and En-gedi—six cities with their villages.

The Jebusites Remain in Jerusalem

63 But the people of Judah could not drive out the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so the Jebusites live among the people of Judah in Jerusalem to this day.
