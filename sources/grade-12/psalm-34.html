David's Vow to Bless the LORD

1 I will bless the LORD at all times; His praise shall be continually in my mouth. 2 My soul boasts in the LORD; let the humble hear and rejoice. 3 Oh, magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt His name together.

The LORD Delivers Those Who Seek Him

4 I sought the LORD, and He answered me; He delivered me from all my fears. 5 Those who look to Him are radiant, and their faces shall never be put to shame. 6 This poor man cried out, and the LORD heard him and saved him from all his troubles. 7 The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear Him, and He delivers them.

Taste and See the LORD's Goodness

8 Oh, taste and see that the LORD is good! Blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him. 9 Fear the LORD, you His saints, for those who fear Him have no lack. 10 The young lions suffer want and hunger, but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing.

Wisdom for Life and Peace

11 Come, O children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD. 12 What man is there who desires life and loves many days, that he may see good? 13 Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from deceit. 14 Turn away from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it.

The LORD's Nearness to the Righteous

15 The eyes of the LORD are toward the righteous, and His ears are toward their cry. 16 The face of the LORD is against those who do evil, to cut off their memory from the earth. 17 When the righteous cry for help, the LORD hears and delivers them from all their troubles. 18 The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit. 19 Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the LORD delivers him from them all. 20 He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken. 21 Evil shall slay the wicked, and those who hate the righteous will be condemned. 22 The LORD redeems the life of His servants; none of those who take refuge in Him will be condemned.
