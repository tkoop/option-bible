David Shows Kindness to Hanun

1 After this, the king of the Ammonites died, and his son Hanun reigned in his place.

2 David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, just as his father showed kindness to me.” So David sent his servants to comfort him concerning his father, and they came to the land of the Ammonites.

3 But the Ammonite leaders said to Hanun their lord, “Do you really think David honors your father by sending comforters? Hasn’t David sent his servants to you to spy out the city, scout it, and overthrow it?”

David’s Servants Are Humiliated

4 So Hanun seized David’s servants, shaved off half of each man’s beard, cut their garments in half at the buttocks, and sent them away.

5 When this was reported to David, he sent messengers to meet them, for the men were deeply humiliated. The king said, “Stay in Jericho until your beards have grown back; then return.”

The Ammonites and Syrians Prepare for War

6 When the Ammonites realized they had made themselves contemptible to David, they sent and hired the Syrians of Beth-rehob and Zobah—twenty thousand foot soldiers—as well as the king of Maacah with one thousand men, and twelve thousand men from Tob.

7 When David heard of it, he sent Joab and the entire army of mighty men.

8 The Ammonites came out and drew up in battle formation at the entrance of the gate, while the Syrians of Zobah and Rehob, and the men of Tob and Maacah, were by themselves in the open country.

9 When Joab saw that the battle was set against him both in front and behind, he chose some of the best men in Israel and arrayed them against the Syrians.

10 He put the rest of the troops under his brother Abishai and arrayed them against the Ammonites.

11 Joab said, “If the Syrians overpower me, come and help me; but if the Ammonites overpower you, I will come and help you. 12 Be strong! Let us be strong for our people and for the cities of our God, and may the LORD do what seems good in His eyes.”

Joab Defeats the Syrians and Ammonites

13 So Joab and the people with him advanced to fight the Syrians, and they fled before him.

14 When the Ammonites saw that the Syrians had fled, they also fled before Abishai and entered the city. Then Joab returned from fighting the Ammonites and came to Jerusalem.

David Defeats the Syrians at Helam

15 When the Syrians saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they gathered together.

16 Hadadezer sent and brought out the Syrians who were beyond the River, and they came to Helam, with Shobach, the commander of Hadadezer’s army, leading them.

17 When this was reported to David, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, and came to Helam. The Syrians arrayed themselves against David and fought him.

18 But the Syrians fled before Israel, and David killed seven hundred charioteers and forty thousand horsemen. He also struck down Shobach, the commander of their army, and he died there.

19 When all the kings who were servants of Hadadezer saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them. So the Ammonites no longer received help from them.
