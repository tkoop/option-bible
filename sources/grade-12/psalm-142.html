A Prayer from the Cave

1 I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift my voice to the LORD and plead for mercy. 2 I pour out my complaint before Him, and I declare my trouble before Him. 3 When my spirit grows faint within me, You know my path; in the way I walk, they have hidden a snare for me.

4 Look to my right and see—no one acknowledges me. Refuge has failed me; no one cares for my life. 5 I cry to You, O the LORD; I say, “You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living.”

6 Listen to my cry, for I am brought very low; rescue me from my persecutors, for they are too strong for me. 7 Bring my soul out of prison, that I may give thanks to Your name; the righteous will surround me, for You will deal bountifully with me.
