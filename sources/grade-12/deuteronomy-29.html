The Covenant Renewed in Moab

1 These are the terms of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to ratify with the Israelites in the land of Moab, supplementing the covenant He established with them at Horeb.

2 Moses summoned all Israel and declared to them, “You have witnessed everything the LORD accomplished before your eyes in the land of Egypt—against Pharaoh, against all his servants, and against all his territory. 3 Your eyes have beheld the great trials, the signs, and the great wonders. 4 Yet to this day the LORD has not granted you a heart to comprehend, eyes to perceive, or ears to apprehend.

“5 I guided you through the wilderness for forty years; your garments did not deteriorate upon you, nor did your sandals wear out beneath your feet. 6 You consumed no bread and drank no wine or intoxicating drink, so that you might recognize that I am the LORD your God.

“7 When you arrived at this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan advanced to meet us in battle, and we defeated them. 8 We seized their land and apportioned it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 9 Therefore observe the stipulations of this covenant and execute them, so that you may prosper in all your undertakings.

All Israel Stands Before the LORD

“10 All of you stand today before the LORD your God—your leaders, your tribes, your elders, and your officers, every Israelite— 11 your children, your wives, and the resident alien in your camp, from the woodcutter to the water drawer— 12 so that you may enter the covenant of the LORD your God and the oath He is making with you today, 13 that He may constitute you today as His people and be your God, exactly as He promised you and swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

“14 I am not ratifying this covenant and this oath with you alone, 15 but with those standing here with us today before the LORD our God, and with those absent from us today.

A Warning Against Idolatry

“16 You are aware how we resided in the land of Egypt and how we traversed the nations through which you passed. 17 You witnessed their abominations and their idols of wood and stone, silver and gold, that were among them. 18 Ensure that no man or woman, family or tribe among you today possesses a heart turning away from the LORD our God to serve the gods of those nations, and that no root among you matures into a toxic, bitter weed.

“19 When anyone hears the words of this oath and congratulates himself in his heart, saying, ‘I shall enjoy peace, though I persist in the stubbornness of my heart,’ he thereby brings calamity upon both the watered and the dry.

“20 The LORD will not deign to forgive him; rather, His anger and jealousy will smolder against that man, and every curse inscribed in this book will descend upon him, and the LORD will expunge his name from under heaven. 21 The LORD will set him apart for calamity from all the tribes of Israel, in accordance with all the curses of the covenant recorded in this Book of the Law.

The Ruin of the Land and Exile

“22 A later generation—your descendants who arise after you—and the foreigner who arrives from a distant land will declare, when they behold the afflictions of that land and the maladies the LORD has imposed upon it: 23 all its terrain scorched with sulfur and salt, unsown and unproductive, with no vegetation sprouting in it—resembling the cataclysm of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overturned in His fierce anger and wrath.

“24 All the nations will inquire, ‘Why has the LORD perpetrated such a thing against this land? What explains this great conflagration of His anger?’

“25 And they will respond, ‘Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He established with them when He liberated them from the land of Egypt. 26 They proceeded to serve other gods and prostrated themselves before them—gods they had not known and that He had not apportioned to them.’

“27 Therefore the LORD’s anger ignited against that land, bringing upon it all the curses recorded in this book. 28 In fierce anger and great wrath the LORD uprooted them from their soil and hurled them into another land, as is evident to this day.

“29 The concealed things belong to the the LORD our God, but the revealed things belong to us and to our children in perpetuity, so that we may execute all the words of this law.”
