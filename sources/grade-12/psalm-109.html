David's Plea in the Face of False Accusations

1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, do not remain silent. 2 For the mouths of the wicked and the deceitful have opened against me; they have spoken against me with lying tongues. 3 They have surrounded me with words of hatred and attacked me without cause. 4 In return for my love they slander me, though I am devoted to prayer. 5 They repay me evil for good and hatred for my love.

A Call for Judgment upon the Wicked

6 Appoint a wicked man to oppose him, and let an accuser stand at his right hand. 7 When he is tried, let him be found guilty, and may his prayer be counted as sin. 8 May his days be few, and may another usurp his position. 9 May his children be orphaned and his wife widowed. 10 May his children wander as vagrants and beg, scavenging sustenance from their desolate homes. 11 May a creditor seize all that he possesses, and may strangers plunder the fruit of his labor. 12 May no one extend mercy to him, nor may anyone favor his fatherless children. 13 May his posterity be cut off; may his name be blotted out in the succeeding generation.

14 May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and may the sin of his mother not be blotted out. 15 May they stand continually before the LORD, so that He may eradicate their memory from the earth. 16 For he never remembered to show kindness; instead, he persecuted the poor and needy and sought to slay the brokenhearted. 17 He loved cursing, so let curses overtake him; he took no pleasure in blessing, so let blessing remain far from him. 18 He clothed himself in cursing as in a garment; may it enter his inward parts like water and penetrate his bones like oil. 19 May it cling to him like a cloak and gird him like a belt he never removes. 20 May this be the LORD's recompense to my accusers, to those who speak evil against my life.

David's Prayer for Deliverance

21 But You, O the LORD, my Lord, deal with me for Your name's sake; because Your steadfast love is good, deliver me. 22 For I am poor and needy, and my heart is wounded within me. 23 I am passing away like a declining shadow; I am shaken off like a locust. 24 My knees give way from fasting, and my flesh has grown lean for lack of oil. 25 I have become a reproach to them; when they see me, they shake their heads.

26 Help me, O the LORD my God; save me according to Your steadfast love. 27 Let them know that this is Your hand, that You, O the LORD, have done it. 28 They may curse, but You will bless; they arise and are put to shame, while Your servant rejoices. 29 May my accusers be clothed with disgrace and wrapped in their shame as in a cloak. 30 With my mouth I will greatly thank the LORD; in the midst of many I will praise Him. 31 For He stands at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those who condemn his soul.
