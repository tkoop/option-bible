A Levite and His Concubine

1 In those days Israel had no king, and a Levite was sojourning in the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim. He took for himself a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah. 2 But his concubine was unfaithful to him; she left him and returned to her father's house in Bethlehem in Judah, where she remained for four months.

The Levite Seeks Reconciliation

3 Her husband then set out after her to speak kindly to her and bring her back, taking with him a servant and a pair of donkeys. When he reached her father's house, the girl's father saw him and rejoiced at his coming. 4 His father-in-law, the young woman's father, detained him, and he stayed with him three days; they ate, drank, and lodged there.

5 On the fourth day they rose early in the morning, and he prepared to depart; but the girl's father said to his son-in-law, "Refresh yourself with a morsel of bread, and afterward you may go." 6 So the two of them sat down and ate and drank together. Then the girl's father said to the man, "Please be content and stay the night, and be cheerful." 7 When the man rose to go, his father-in-law urged him, so he stayed there another night.

8 On the fifth day he rose early to depart, and the girl's father said, "Refresh yourself, please, and tarry until the day declines." So the two of them ate. 9 Then the man rose to go, he and his concubine and his servant; but his father-in-law, the girl's father, said to him, "Look, the day is fading toward evening. Please stay the night; the day is drawing to a close. Stay here, that you may be cheerful, and tomorrow rise early for your journey, and go to your home."

A Night in Gibeah

10 Yet the man was unwilling to stay the night, so he arose and departed. He came as far as opposite Jebus—that is, Jerusalem—and with him were two saddled donkeys and his concubine. 11 When they were near Jebus, the day had declined greatly, and the servant said to his master, "Come, let us turn aside into this city of the Jebusites and lodge in it." 12 But his master said to him, "We will not turn aside into a foreign city that does not belong to the Israelites; we will pass on to Gibeah." 13 He said to his servant, "Come, let us draw near to one of these places and lodge in Gibeah or in Ramah."

14 So they passed on and traveled, and the sun set as they came near Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin. 15 They turned aside there to lodge in Gibeah, and when they entered the city they sat down in the open square, but no one took them into his house to spend the night.

16 Then in the evening an old man came in from his work in the field; he was from the hill country of Ephraim and was sojourning in Gibeah, though the men of the place were Benjamites. 17 He lifted up his eyes and saw the traveler in the city square, and the old man said, "Where are you going, and from where have you come?"

18 He answered him, "We are passing from Bethlehem in Judah to the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim. I am from there, and I went to Bethlehem in Judah, and now I am going to the house of the LORD, but no one has received me into his house. 19 Yet there is both straw and fodder for our donkeys, and bread and wine for me, for your maidservant, and for the young man with us; we lack nothing."

20 The old man said, "Peace be to you; let me attend to all your needs; only do not spend the night in the square." 21 So he brought him into his house and gave fodder to the donkeys; they washed their feet, ate, and drank.

The Crime at Gibeah

22 While they were making their hearts merry, behold, the worthless men of the city, Benjamites, surrounded the house, beating on the door. They said to the old man, the master of the house, "Bring out the man who came into your house, that we may sexually assault him."

23 The master of the house went out to them and said, "No, my brothers, do not act so wickedly; since this man has come into my house, do not do this disgraceful thing. 24 Behold, here is my daughter, a virgin, and his concubine. I will bring them out now, and you may humble them and do to them what seems good in your eyes; but to this man do not do this vile thing."

25 But the men would not listen to him. So the man seized his concubine and brought her out to them, and they raped her and abused her all night until morning; at dawn they sent her away.

26 As morning was breaking, the woman came and fell down at the door of the man's house where her master was, and she lay there until it was light. 27 When her master rose in the morning and opened the doors of the house and went out to continue his journey, behold, his concubine was lying at the door of the house with her hands on the threshold.

28 He said to her, "Get up, and let us go." But there was no answer. So he took her up on the donkey, and the man arose and went to his place.

29 When he came to his house, he took a knife, seized his concubine, and cut her into twelve pieces, and sent her throughout all the territory of Israel.

30 Everyone who saw it said, "Such a thing has never been seen or done from the day the Israelites came up out of the land of Egypt until this day. Consider it, take counsel, and speak."
