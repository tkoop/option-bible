Justice Between Men

1 When a dispute arises between men, they are to go to court, and the judges shall decide the case, acquitting the innocent and condemning the guilty. 2 If the guilty man deserves a beating, the judge must have him lie down and be flogged in his presence with the number of lashes his crime deserves. 3 Do not give him more than forty lashes, so that your brother is not degraded in your sight. 4 Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain.

Levirate Marriage and Family Obligation

5 If brothers live together and one of them dies without a son, the widow must not marry outside the family; her husband's brother shall take her as his wife and perform the duty of a brother-in-law for her. 6 The first son she bears will carry on the name of the dead brother, so that his name will not be blotted out from Israel.

7 If the man does not want to marry his brother's widow, she shall go to the elders at the city gate and say, "My husband's brother refuses to carry on his brother's name in Israel. He will not fulfill the duty of a brother-in-law to me."

8 Then the elders of his city shall summon him and speak with him. If he persists and says, "I do not want to marry her,"

9 then his brother's widow shall go up to him in the presence of the elders, remove his sandal from his foot, spit in his face, and declare, "This is what is done to the man who will not build up his brother's family line." 10 And in Israel he will be called "The Family of the Unsandaled."

A Woman's Intervention in a Fight

11 If two men are fighting and the wife of one comes to rescue her husband from his assailant, and she reaches out and seizes the man by his private parts, 12 You are to cut off her hand. Show her no pity.

Honest Weights and Measures

13 You shall not have two differing weights in your bag, one heavy and one light. 14 You shall not have two differing measures in your house, one large and one small. 15 You must have accurate and honest weights and measures, so that you may live long in the land the the LORD your God is giving you. 16 For everyone who does these things and acts unjustly is an abomination to the the LORD your God.

Remembering Amalek

17 Remember what the Amalekites did to you on the way when you came out of Egypt. 18 How they attacked you on the way when you were faint and weary, striking down those who lagged behind; they did not fear God. 19 When the the LORD your God gives you rest from all your enemies around you in the land that He is giving you as an inheritance to possess, you must blot out the memory of Amalek from under heaven. Do not forget.
