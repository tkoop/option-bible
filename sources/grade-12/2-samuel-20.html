Sheba’s Rebellion Against David

1 Now a worthless man named Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite, happened to be there. He blew a trumpet and shouted, “We have no share in David, and no inheritance in Jesse’s son. Every man of Israel, return home!”

2 So all the men of Israel withdrew from following David and followed Sheba son of Bichri, while the men of Judah remained loyal to their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.

David Provides for His Concubines

3 When David returned to his house in Jerusalem, the king took the ten concubines he had left to keep the house, placed them in a guarded residence, provided for them, but did not sleep with them. They remained confined until the day they died, living as widows.

4 The king said to Amasa, “Summon the men of Judah for me within three days, and you be present here.”

5 Amasa went to summon Judah, but he delayed beyond the appointed time.

6 David said to Abishai, “Now Sheba son of Bichri will harm us more than Absalom did. Take your lord’s servants and pursue him, or he will find fortified cities and escape from us.”

7 Joab’s men went out after him, along with the Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the mighty warriors. They left Jerusalem to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.

Joab Kills Amasa

8 They were at the great stone in Gibeon when Amasa came to meet them. Joab was wearing his military tunic, with a belt and a sword in its sheath fastened at his waist; as he stepped forward, the sword slipped out.

9 Joab said to Amasa, “Are you well, my brother?” Then Joab took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him.

10 Amasa did not notice the sword in Joab’s hand. Joab struck him in the abdomen, and his entrails spilled onto the ground; he died without a second blow. Then Joab and his brother Abishai pursued Sheba son of Bichri.

11 One of Joab’s young men stood beside Amasa and cried, “Whoever is for Joab, and whoever is for David, follow Joab!”

12 Amasa lay wallowing in his blood in the middle of the road. When the man saw that everyone who passed by stopped, he dragged Amasa from the road into a field and threw a garment over him.

13 Once he had been removed from the road, all the men passed on after Joab to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.

The Wise Woman of Abel

14 Sheba passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel Beth-maachah and to all the Berites; they assembled and followed him as well.

15 They came and besieged him in Abel Beth-maachah. They built a siege ramp against the city, and it reached the outer wall; all the troops with Joab were battering the wall to bring it down.

16 A wise woman called out from the city, “Listen! Listen! Please tell Joab to come here so I can speak with you.”

17 Joab approached her, and the woman asked, “Are you Joab?”

“I am,” he replied.

“Listen to the words of your maidservant,” she said.

“I am listening,” he answered.

18 Then she said, “In former times people would say, ‘Let them seek counsel at Abel,’ and that settled the matter.”

19 “I am one of the peaceful and faithful in Israel. You are trying to destroy a city that is a mother in Israel. Why would you swallow up the LORD’s inheritance?”

20 Joab answered, “Far be it from me! Far be it from me to swallow up or destroy!”

21 “The matter is not so. A man from the hill country of Ephraim, Sheba son of Bichri, has rebelled against King David. Hand him over alone, and I will withdraw from the city.”

“Look, his head will be thrown to you over the wall,” the woman replied.

22 The woman went to all the people with her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it to Joab. He blew the trumpet, and they dispersed from the city, each to his own home. Then Joab returned to the king in Jerusalem.

David’s Royal Officials

23 Now Joab was over all the army of Israel; Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites.

24 Adoram was over the forced labor; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.

25 Sheva was the scribe; Zadok and Abiathar were the priests.

26 Ira the Jairite was also David’s priest.
