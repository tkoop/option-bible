For the choirmaster: to the melody “Lilies”—a testimony of Asaph; a psalm.

A Plea for the Restoration of God’s People

1 Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, You who guide Joseph like a flock; You who are enthroned between the cherubim, reveal Your glory. 2 Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, awaken Your might and come to our salvation. 3 Restore us, O God; look upon us with favor, and we shall be saved.

4 O the LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your wrath burn against the prayers of Your people? 5 You have given them tears instead of bread and compelled them to drink tears in abundance. 6 You have made us an object of contention to our neighbors, and our enemies deride us. 7 Restore us, O God of Hosts; look upon us with favor, and we shall be saved.

The Vine Transplanted from Egypt

8 You uprooted a vine from Egypt; You expelled the nations and planted it. 9 You cleared the ground before it, and it took root and filled the land. 10 The mountains were covered by its shade, and the mighty cedars were overshadowed by its branches. 11 Its branches reached the Sea, and its shoots stretched to the River.

12 Why have You torn down its walls, so that every passerby plucks its grapes? 13 The wild boar from the forest ravages it, and the creatures of the field feed upon it.

A Prayer for Renewal and Deliverance

14 Return, O God of Hosts! Look down from heaven and behold; attend to this vine, 15 the root Your right hand planted, the son You strengthened for Yourself. 16 It is burned with fire and cut down; they perish at Your fierce rebuke. 17 Let Your hand rest upon the man at Your right hand, upon the son of man You have strengthened for Yourself. 18 Then we shall not turn back from You; revive us, and we will call upon Your name. 19 Restore us, O the LORD God of Hosts; look upon us with favor, and we shall be saved.
