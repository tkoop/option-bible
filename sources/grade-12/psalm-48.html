The Magnificence of God's Holy City

A song; a psalm of the sons of Korah. 1 Great is the LORD, and highly to be praised in the city of our God, upon His holy mountain. 2 Beautiful in its lofty situation, the joy of all the earth, Mount Zion stands on the northern slopes—the city of the great King. 3 God is known in her citadels as a stronghold.

The Terror of the Assembled Kings

4 For behold, the kings assembled and advanced together. 5 They looked, and they were thunderstruck; seized by panic, they fled. 6 Trembling seized them on the spot, anguish like that of a woman in labor. 7 By an east wind You shatter the ships of Tarshish.

God's Eternal Establishment of Zion

8 As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish it forever. Selah

9 Within Your temple, O God, we meditate on Your loving devotion. 10 As Your name, O God, so Your praise extends to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness. 11 Mount Zion rejoices, and the daughters of Judah exult because of Your judgments.

A Call to Remember and Proclaim

12 Walk about Zion, encircle her, and count her towers. 13 Consider her ramparts; pass through her citadels, that you may recount them to the next generation— 14 that this God is our God forever and ever; He will guide us even to death.
