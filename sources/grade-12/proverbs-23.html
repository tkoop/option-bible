Wisdom for Dining with Rulers

1 When you sit down to dine with a ruler, scrutinize with meticulous care whatever is set before you, 2 and place a knife to your throat if you are a man of voracious appetite. 3 Do not crave his delicacies, for such food is deceptive. 4 Do not exhaust yourself in the pursuit of riches; have the discernment to desist. 5 When your eyes light upon wealth, it vanishes, for it sprouts wings like an eagle and flies away to heaven.

6 Do not partake of the bread of a man with an evil eye, nor crave his delicacies; 7 for as he reckons within himself, so he truly is. “Eat and drink,” he says to you, yet his heart is not with you. 8 You will vomit up the morsel you have eaten and squander your gracious words. 9 Do not speak into a fool’s ears, for he will despise the wisdom of your words.

10 Do not remove an ancient boundary stone, and do not encroach upon the fields of the fatherless, 11 for their Redeemer is mighty; He will plead their cause against you.

Receiving Discipline and Instruction

12 Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to words of knowledge. 13 Do not withhold discipline from a child; if you strike him with the rod, he will not die. 14 You shall strike him with the rod and deliver his soul from Sheol.

15 My son, if your heart is wise, my heart too will rejoice. 16 My inmost being will rejoice when your lips speak what is upright. 17 Do not let your heart envy sinners, but remain in the fear of the LORD all the day. 18 Surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.

19 Listen, my son, and be wise, and direct your heart in the way. 20 Do not join those who drink too much wine or gorge themselves on meat, 21 for the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags.

22 Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old. 23 Buy the truth and do not sell it—wisdom, instruction, and understanding as well. 24 The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who begets a wise son will delight in him. 25 Let your father and your mother be glad, and let her who bore you rejoice!

A Father's Appeal and the Dangers of Folly

26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes delight in my ways. 27 For a prostitute is a deep pit, and an adulteress is a narrow well. 28 She lies in wait like a robber and multiplies the faithless among men.

29 Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaints? Who has wounds without cause? Who has bloodshot eyes? 30 Those who linger over wine, those who go to sample mixed drinks. 31 Do not gaze at wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup, when it goes down smoothly. 32 In the end it bites like a serpent and stings like an adder. 33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your heart will utter perversities. 34 You will be like one sleeping in the heart of the sea, or like one at the helm of a ship. 35 “They struck me, but I did not feel it! They beat me, but I did not know it! When will I wake up so I can find another drink?”
