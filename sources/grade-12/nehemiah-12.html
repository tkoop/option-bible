Priests and Levites Who Returned with Zerubbabel

1 These are the priests and Levites who returned with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and with Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra, 2 Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, 3 Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, 4 Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah, 5 Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah, 6 Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, 7 Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. These were the chief priests and their associates in the days of Jeshua.

Levitical Genealogies and Duties

8 The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who, together with his brothers, led the songs of thanksgiving. 9 Bakbukiah and Unni, their kinsmen, stood opposite them in their appointed watches.

10 Jeshua was the father of Joiakim, Joiakim the father of Eliashib, and Eliashib the father of Joiada. 11 Joiada was the father of Jonathan, and Jonathan was the father of Jaddua.

12 In the days of Joiakim, the heads of the priestly families were as follows: for Seraiah, Meraiah; for Jeremiah, Hananiah; 13 for Ezra, Meshullam; for Amariah, Jehohanan; 14 for Malluchi, Jonathan; for Shebaniah, Joseph; 15 for Harim, Adna; for Meraioth, Helkai; 16 for Iddo, Zechariah; for Ginnethon, Meshullam; 17 for Abijah, Zichri; for Miniamin and Moadiah, Piltai; 18 for Bilgah, Shammua; for Shemaiah, Jehonathan; 19 for Joiarib, Mattenai; for Jedaiah, Uzzi; 20 for Sallai, Kallai; for Amok, Eber; 21 for Hilkiah, Hashabiah; and for Jedaiah, Nethanel.

22 The heads of the Levite and priestly families were recorded in the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, continuing until the reign of Darius the Persian. 23 The Levites’ ancestral heads were inscribed in the Book of the Chronicles down to the days of Johanan son of Eliashib. 24 The chiefs of the Levites—Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua son of Kadmiel, and their associates—stood opposite one another to chant praise and thanksgiving, watch corresponding to watch, as David, the man of God, had commanded. 25 Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers who guarded the storerooms at the gates. 26 These served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and scribe.

Dedication of Jerusalem’s Wall

27 At the dedication of Jerusalem’s wall, the Levites were summoned from every place where they resided and brought to Jerusalem to celebrate the dedication with gladness, thanksgiving, and song, accompanied by cymbals, harps, and lyres. 28 The singers assembled from the surrounding district of Jerusalem and from the villages of the Netophathites, 29 and from Beth-gilgal and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth, because the singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem. 30 The priests and Levites purified themselves, and then they purified the people, the gates, and the wall.

31 Then I led Judah’s leaders up onto the wall and stationed two large thanksgiving choirs, which processed along the wall to the right toward the Dung Gate. 32 Hoshaiah and half of Judah’s leaders followed them, 33 along with Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam, 34 Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah, 35 and some of the priests’ sons bearing trumpets—Zechariah son of Jonathan son of Shemaiah son of Mattaniah son of Micaiah son of Zaccur son of Asaph— 36 and his brothers Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani, with the musical instruments of David, the man of God. Ezra the scribe walked before them. 37 At the Fountain Gate they ascended straight up the steps of the City of David, along the wall’s ascent, above David’s house, to the Water Gate on the east.

38 The second thanksgiving choir proceeded to the left, and I followed it with half the people along the wall, above the Tower of the Ovens, to the Broad Wall. 39 It passed above the Gate of Ephraim, above the Old Gate, above the Fish Gate, and past the Tower of Hananel and the Tower of the Hundred, reaching the Sheep Gate; there they stopped at the Gate of the Guard. 40 So both thanksgiving choirs took their stand in the house of God, and I stood with half the officials, 41 while the priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah blew trumpets, 42 and Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer joined them. The singers sang under the direction of Jezrahiah.

43 On that day they offered great sacrifices and rejoiced, for God had granted them great joy; the women and children rejoiced as well, so that Jerusalem’s joy was heard from afar.

Provision for Priests and Levites

44 On that day men were appointed to oversee the storerooms for the contributions, firstfruits, and tithes, gathering from the fields of the cities the portions prescribed by the Law for the priests and Levites, because Judah rejoiced over the priests and Levites who served. 45 They kept the service of their God and the service of purification, as did the singers and gatekeepers, according to the command of David and his son Solomon. 46 For in former days, under David and Asaph, the chief singers had led songs of praise and thanksgiving to God. 47 In the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel contributed the daily portions for the singers and gatekeepers; they consecrated the sacred portion for the Levites, and the Levites consecrated the sacred portion for the sons of Aaron.
