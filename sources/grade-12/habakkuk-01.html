The Oracle of Habakkuk

1 The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet received in a vision.

Habakkuk's First Complaint

2 “How long, the LORD, must I cry for help, and You do not hear? I cry out to You, ‘Violence!’ and You do not save. 3 Why do You make me see iniquity, and why do You look at oppression? For devastation and violence are before me; strife arises, and contention is stirred up. 4 Therefore the law is paralyzed, and justice never goes forth. For the wicked hem in the righteous, so that justice is perverted.”

The LORD's Response

“5 Look among the nations and observe; be utterly astonished, for I am doing a work in your days that you would not believe even if it were told. 6 For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans—that bitter and impetuous nation—who march through the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own. 7 They are dreaded and feared; their justice and dignity proceed from themselves.”

“8 Their horses are swifter than leopards, fiercer than wolves at dusk. Their horsemen charge ahead; they come from afar, flying like an eagle swooping to devour. 9 They all come for violence; their hordes advance like the east wind; they gather captives like sand.”

“10 They mock kings, and rulers are a laughingstock to them; they laugh at every fortress, pile up earth, and capture it. 11 Then they sweep by like the wind and pass on—guilty men whose strength is their god.”

Habakkuk's Second Complaint

“12 Are You not from everlasting, the LORD my God, my Holy One? We will not die. the LORD, You have appointed them for judgment; and You, O Rock, have established them for correction. 13 Your eyes are too pure to look on evil; You cannot tolerate wrongdoing. Why do You look on the treacherous and keep silent when the wicked devours one more righteous than himself? 14 You have made mankind like the fish of the sea, like creeping things with no ruler. 15 He brings all of them up with a hook; he catches them in his net and gathers them in his dragnet. Therefore he rejoices and is glad. 16 Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his dragnet, for by them his portion is rich and his food is plentiful. 17 Will he then empty his net and continue slaughtering the nations without mercy?”
