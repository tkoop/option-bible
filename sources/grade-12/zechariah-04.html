The Golden Lampstand and Olive Trees

1 The angel who had been speaking with me returned and awakened me as a man roused from sleep. 2 He asked, "What do you see?" I answered, "I see a lampstand entirely of gold, with a bowl on its top and seven lamps on it, along with seven pipes to the lamps on its top." 3 Beside it stood two olive trees, one on the right of the bowl and one on its left.

4 I asked the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?" 5 He replied, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."

The Word to Zerubbabel

6 Then he answered and said to me, "This is the word of the the LORD to Zerubbabel: 'Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit,' says the the LORD of Hosts. 7 'Who are you, O great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become a plain. He will bring out the capstone amid shouts of "Grace, grace to it!"'"

The Completion of the Temple

8 The word of the the LORD came to me: 9 "The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will finish it. Then you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you. 10 For who despises the day of small things? These seven are the eyes of the the LORD, which roam throughout the earth; they will rejoice and see the plumb line in Zerubbabel's hand."

The Two Anointed Ones

11 I asked him, "What are these two olive trees on the right and left of the lampstand?" 12 I asked him a second time, "What are these two olive branches beside the two golden pipes that pour out golden oil?" 13 He replied, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord." 14 He said, "These are the two anointed ones who stand beside the Lord of all the earth."
