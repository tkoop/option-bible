Wisdom, Folly, and Family Joy

1 A wise son gladdens his father, whereas a foolish son brings grief to his mother. 2 Treasures gained through wrongdoing profit nothing, but righteousness delivers from death. 3 The LORD will not allow the righteous to hunger; instead, He frustrates the craving of the wicked. 4 Indolence impoverishes a person, while diligent labor brings wealth. 5 A son who gathers during summer demonstrates prudence, but a son who sleeps through harvest brings disgrace.

Blessing, Memory, and the Power of Speech

6 Blessings crown the head of the righteous, whereas the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. 7 The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot. 8 Those wise in heart receive instruction, whereas a garrulous fool hastens toward ruin. 9 One who walks in integrity walks securely, but one who distorts his ways will be exposed. 10 One who signals deceit with a wink causes grief, and a garrulous fool comes to ruin. 11 The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, while the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.

12 Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all transgressions. 13 Wisdom is found on the lips of the discerning, but the undiscerning invite chastisement. 14 The wise store up knowledge, but the mouth of the fool invites destruction. 15 The rich man's wealth is his fortified city, whereas poverty ruins the poor.

Labor, Instruction, and Wealth

16 The labor of the righteous leads to life, but the gain of the wicked leads to sin. 17 Whoever heeds instruction walks the path of life, but whoever rejects reproof goes astray. 18 One who conceals hatred has lying lips, and one who spreads slander is a fool. 19 Where words abound, transgression is never absent, but one who restrains his lips acts wisely. 20 The tongue of the righteous is choice silver, but the heart of the wicked is of little value. 21 The lips of the righteous nourish many, but fools perish for want of discernment.

22 The blessing of the LORD enriches, and He adds no sorrow to it. 23 A fool finds pleasure in wicked schemes, but a person of understanding delights in wisdom. 24 What the wicked dreads will overtake him, but the desire of the righteous will be granted.

The Enduring Righteous and the Passing Wicked

25 When the whirlwind passes, the wicked are no more, but the righteous stand as an everlasting foundation. 26 Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to those who send him. 27 The fear of the LORD prolongs life, but the years of the wicked are cut short. 28 The hope of the righteous is joy, but the expectation of the wicked perishes.

29 The way of the LORD is a stronghold to the upright, but destruction to the workers of iniquity. 30 The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked will not dwell in the land. 31 The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but a perverse tongue will be silenced. 32 The lips of the righteous know what is fitting, but the mouth of the wicked is perverse.
