Atonement for an Unsolved Homicide

1 If a homicide victim is discovered fallen in a field within the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess, and the perpetrator remains unknown, 2 then your elders and judges shall go out and measure the distance from the victim to the surrounding cities. 3 The city nearest the victim shall have its elders take a heifer from the herd—one that has never been worked and has never drawn a yoke. 4 The elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a valley with a perennial stream, land that has neither been plowed nor sown, and there they shall break the heifer's neck in the valley. 5 Then the priests, the sons of Levi, shall approach, because the LORD your God has chosen them to minister to Him and to bless in the name of the LORD, and every dispute and every assault shall be settled by their verdict. 6 All the elders of the city nearest the victim shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley.

7 They shall declare, "Our hands did not shed this blood, nor did our eyes witness the deed. 8 Atone, O the LORD, for Your people Israel, whom You have redeemed, and do not let innocent blood remain among Your people Israel." Then the bloodguilt shall be atoned for them. 9 So you shall purge the innocent blood from your midst, for you must do what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

Marriage to a Captive Woman

10 When you march out to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God delivers them into your hand, and you take captives, 11 and you see among the captives a woman of striking beauty and desire her, you may take her as your wife. 12 Then you shall bring her into your house, and she shall shave her head and trim her nails. 13 She shall remove the garments of her captivity, remain in your house, and mourn her father and mother for a full month; after that you may consummate the marriage with her, and she shall be your wife. 14 But if you no longer take pleasure in her, you shall release her to go wherever she wishes; you shall not sell her for money, and you shall not reduce her to servitude, because you have dishonored her.

Inheritance Rights and a Rebellious Son

15 If a man has two wives, one beloved and the other despised, and both bear him sons, and the firstborn son is the child of the despised wife, 16 then on the day he distributes his inheritance among his sons, he may not grant the son of the beloved wife the status of firstborn in preference to the firstborn son of the despised wife. 17 Rather, he shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the despised wife, by allotting him a double share of all that he possesses, for he is the first yield of his vigor; the right of the firstborn belongs to him.

18 If a man has a stubborn and intractable son who will not obey his father or mother, and even when they chastise him he still refuses to heed them, 19 then his father and mother shall seize him and bring him before the elders of his city and to the gate of his hometown. 20 They shall say to the elders, "This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he will not obey us; he is a glutton and a drunkard." 21 Then all the men of his city shall stone him to death. So you shall purge the evil from your midst, and all Israel shall hear and fear.

Execution and Proper Burial

22 If a man is guilty of a capital offense and is put to death, and you hang him on a tree, 23 his corpse shall not remain on the tree overnight; you shall certainly bury him that same day, because a hanged man is accursed of God. You shall not defile the land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance.
