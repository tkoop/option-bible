Jeroboam's Deception and the Prophet's Summons

1 At that time, Abijah, the son of Jeroboam, fell gravely ill. 2 Jeroboam said to his wife, "Go and disguise yourself so that no one will recognize you as Jeroboam's wife. Then travel to Shiloh, where the prophet Ahijah resides—the man who declared that I would reign over this people. 3 Take with you ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey, and go to him; he will tell you what will become of the boy."

4 Jeroboam's wife did as he instructed: she arose, traveled to Shiloh, and came to Ahijah's house. Now Ahijah could not see, for his eyes had grown dim with age.

Ahijah's Prophecy of Judgment

5 Yet the LORD had said to Ahijah, "Behold, the wife of Jeroboam is coming to inquire of you concerning her son, for he is ill. Speak to her thus and so, and when she arrives, she will be disguised."

6 When Ahijah heard the sound of her footsteps at the door, he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why do you disguise yourself? For I have been sent to you with a severe message.

7 "Go and tell Jeroboam that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Because I exalted you from among the people and appointed you ruler over My people Israel, 8 and because I tore the kingdom from the house of David and gave it to you—yet you have not been like My servant David, who kept My commandments and followed Me with all his heart, doing only what was right in My eyes— 9 but you have done more evil than all who preceded you. You have gone and made for yourself other gods and molten images to provoke Me to anger, and you have cast Me behind your back— 10 therefore, behold, I am bringing disaster upon the house of Jeroboam. I will cut off from Jeroboam every male, whether slave or free, in Israel, and I will sweep away the house of Jeroboam as completely as dung is swept away. 11 Anyone belonging to Jeroboam who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs, and anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by the birds of the sky, for the LORD has spoken.'

"12 Now arise and go to your house. When your feet enter the city, the child will die. 13 All Israel will mourn for him and bury him, for he alone among Jeroboam's household will receive a burial, because in him something good was found toward the LORD, the God of Israel, within the house of Jeroboam. 14 Moreover, the LORD will raise up for Himself a king over Israel who will cut off the house of Jeroboam that very day. What then—even now? 15 The LORD will strike Israel as a reed is shaken in water; He will uproot Israel from this good land that He gave to their fathers, and He will scatter them beyond the Euphrates River, because they have made their Asherah poles, provoking the LORD. 16 He will give up Israel because of the sins of Jeroboam, which he committed and by which he made Israel sin."

The Death of Abijah and Jeroboam's End

17 Then Jeroboam's wife arose, departed, and came to Tirzah. As she reached the threshold of the house, the child died. 18 All Israel buried him and mourned for him, in accordance with the word that the LORD had spoken through His servant Ahijah the prophet.

19 The rest of the acts of Jeroboam—how he waged war and how he reigned—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 20 Jeroboam reigned twenty-two years; then he died and was buried with his ancestors, and his son Nadab reigned in his place.

Rehoboam's Reign, Judah's Apostasy, and Egypt's Plunder

21 Meanwhile, Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned over Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the LORD had chosen from all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite.

22 Judah did evil in the sight of the LORD, and they provoked Him to jealousy with their sins, which they committed beyond all that their fathers had done.

23 For they also built high places, pillars, and Asherah poles for themselves on every high hill and under every green tree.

24 There were even male cult prostitutes in the land; they did according to all the abominations of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel.

25 In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem. 26 He took away the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king's house; he took everything, including all the gold shields that Solomon had made.

27 King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place and entrusted them to the captains of the guard who kept the door of the king's house. 28 Whenever the king went into the house of the LORD, the guards would carry them and then return them to the guardroom.

29 The rest of the acts of Rehoboam and all that he did—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 30 There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam throughout their days. 31 Rehoboam died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. And his son Abijam reigned in his place.
