The LORD's Triumph over Leviathan

1 In that day the LORD will punish Leviathan the fleeing serpent with His hard, great, and strong sword; He will punish Leviathan the twisting serpent and slay the dragon that is in the sea.

The LORD's Care for His Vineyard

2 In that day, “Sing of a delightful vineyard: 3 I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually. I guard it night and day, lest anyone harm it. 4 I have no wrath. If only thorns and briers confronted Me, I would march against them in battle and burn them all together. 5 Or let him take hold of My stronghold and make peace with Me—yes, let him make peace with Me.”

6 In days to come Jacob will take root; Israel will blossom and put forth shoots, and the face of the world will be filled with fruit.

The LORD's Measured Judgment

7 Has He struck them as He struck their attackers? Or have they been slain as their slayers were slain? 8 In exact measure, when you sent her away, you contended with her. He removed her with His fierce blast in the day of the east wind. 9 Therefore by this the guilt of Jacob will be atoned for, and this will be the full fruit of removing his sin: when he makes all the stones of the altar like crushed chalkstones, so that no Asherah poles or incense altars remain standing.

10 The fortified city stands desolate, an abandoned settlement forsaken like the wilderness; there the calf grazes, there it lies down and strips its branches bare. 11 When its boughs are dry, they are broken; women come and set them ablaze. For this is a people without understanding; therefore their Maker will not have compassion on them, and their Creator will not be gracious to them.

The Gathering of God's People

12 In that day the LORD will thresh from the Euphrates River to the Brook of Egypt, and you will be gathered one by one, O people of Israel. 13 In that day a great trumpet will be blown, and those who were lost in the land of Assyria and those who were driven out to the land of Egypt will come and worship the LORD on the holy mountain at Jerusalem.
