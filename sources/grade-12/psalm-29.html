The Majesty of the LORD’s Voice

1 A psalm of David. Ascribe to the LORD glory and strength, O sons of God; ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name. 2 Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness.

3 The voice of the LORD resounds over the waters; the God of Glory thunders; the LORD presides over the many waters. 4 The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic.

5 The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; indeed, the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. 6 He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox.

7 The voice of the LORD hews forth flames of fire.

8 The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. 9 The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare; and in His temple all cry, “Glory!”

10 The LORD sat enthroned over the deluge; the LORD is enthroned as King forever.

11 The LORD gives strength to His people; the LORD blesses His people with peace.
