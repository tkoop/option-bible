A Prayer for Deliverance from Treacherous Adversaries

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A contemplative psalm of David, composed when the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?”

1 Deliver me, O God, by Your name; vindicate me by Your might. 2 Hear my prayer, O God; attend to the words of my mouth. 3 For strangers have risen against me, and ruthless men seek my life, giving no thought to God. Selah.

Confidence in God and a Vow of Thanksgiving

4 Surely God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul. 5 He will requite my foes with evil; in Your faithfulness, annihilate them. 6 With a willing heart I will sacrifice to You; I will give thanks to Your name, the LORD, for it is good. 7 For He has delivered me from all trouble, and my eyes have looked upon my foes.
