Nehemiah Receives Distressing News from Jerusalem

1 The words of Nehemiah son of Hachaliah: In the month of Kislev, in the twentieth year, while I was in the citadel of Susa, 2 Hanani, one of my brothers, arrived with several men from Judah, and I inquired of them about the Jews who had survived the captivity and about Jerusalem. 3 They replied, "The remnant there in the province suffers great affliction and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem lies in ruins, and its gates have been consumed by fire."

Nehemiah's Prayer of Confession and Supplication

4 Upon hearing these words, I sat down and wept. I mourned for days, fasting and praying before the God of heaven. 5 Then I said, "I beseech the LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and steadfast love with those who love Him and keep His commandments, 6 let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to hear the prayer of Your servant that I pray before You day and night for Your servants, the Israelites, confessing the sins of the Israelites, which we have committed against You. Both I and my father's house have sinned. 7 We have acted very corruptly toward You and have not kept the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that You commanded Your servant Moses.

8 "Remember, I pray, the word that You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, 'If you act unfaithfully, I will scatter you among the nations; 9 but if you return to Me and keep and do My commandments, then even if your exiles are at the farthest horizon, I will gather them from there and bring them to the place I have chosen to set My Name there.' 10 They are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great power and Your strong hand. 11 O Lord, let Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants who desire to fear Your Name. Grant Your servant success today, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man." For I was cupbearer to the king.
