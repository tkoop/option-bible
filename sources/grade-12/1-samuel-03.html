The LORD Calls Samuel

1 Now the youth Samuel was ministering to the LORD in Eli's presence, and in those days revelations from the LORD were scarce; visions were not commonly granted. 2 By then Eli's eyesight had begun to fail, rendering him unable to see, and he was reclining in his customary place. 3 The lamp of God had not yet been extinguished, and Samuel was reclining within the sanctuary of the LORD, where the ark of God rested.

4 Then the LORD summoned Samuel, and he responded, "Here I am!"

5 He hastened to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me."

But Eli said, "I did not call; lie down again." So Samuel went and lay down.

6 And the LORD called again, "Samuel!"

Samuel arose, went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me."

But Eli said, "I did not call, my son; lie down again."

7 Now Samuel had not yet come to know the LORD, nor had the word of the LORD yet been disclosed to him.

8 The LORD summoned Samuel a third time. He arose, went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me." Then Eli discerned that the LORD was calling the boy.

9 Therefore Eli instructed Samuel, "Go, lie down; and should He call you, you are to say, 'Speak, the LORD, for Your servant hears.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

10 Then the LORD came and stood by him, calling as He had before, "Samuel! Samuel!"

And Samuel said, "Speak, for Your servant hears."

Judgment on Eli's House

11 Then the LORD said to Samuel, "Behold, I am about to accomplish a deed in Israel that will cause all who hear of it to shudder. 12 On that day I will execute against Eli every word I have spoken concerning his household, from first to last. 13 I have declared to him that I will judge his house in perpetuity because of the iniquity he acknowledged: his sons were blaspheming God, and he failed to restrain them. 14 Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the guilt of Eli's house shall never be expiated by sacrifice or offering."

15 Samuel remained there until morning, when he opened the doors of the house of the LORD; yet he dreaded disclosing the vision to Eli.

Samuel Tells Eli the Vision

16 Eli summoned Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son."

Samuel answered, "Here I am."

17 Then Eli said, "What is the word that He spoke to you? Do not conceal it from me. May God deal with you, be it ever so severely, if you withhold from me any part of all that He said to you."

18 Samuel recounted everything and concealed nothing from him. Then Eli said, "It is the LORD; let Him do what seems good in His sight."

Samuel Recognized as a Prophet

19 Samuel continued to grow, and the LORD was with him, permitting none of his words to go unfulfilled. 20 All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, recognized that Samuel had been established as a prophet of the LORD.

21 The LORD appeared again at Shiloh, for He revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh through the word of the LORD.
