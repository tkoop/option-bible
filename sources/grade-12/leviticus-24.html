The Perpetual Lamp and the Showbread

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Command the Israelites to bring to you clear oil of beaten olives for the light, so that a lamp may burn continually. 3 Outside the veil of the Testimony, in the Tent of Meeting, Aaron shall arrange it continually from evening until morning before the LORD, a perpetual statute throughout your generations. 4 He shall arrange the lamps on the pure lampstand before the LORD continually.”

5 “You shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve loaves; two-tenths of an ephah shall be in each loaf. 6 You shall set them in two rows, six in a row, on the pure table before the LORD. 7 You shall put pure frankincense on each row, so that it may be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to the LORD. 8 Every Sabbath day he shall arrange it before the LORD continually, from the Israelites as an everlasting covenant. 9 It shall be for Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to him from the offerings by fire to the LORD, a perpetual statute.”

The Blasphemer in the Camp

10 Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the Israelites; and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp. 11 The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed, so they brought him to Moses. His mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan. 12 They put him in custody until it was declared to them according to the mouth of the LORD.

Judgment for Blasphemy and Bodily Injury

13 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 14 “Bring the blasphemer outside the camp, and let all who heard him lay their hands on his head, and let all the congregation stone him. 15 You shall speak to the Israelites, saying, ‘Any man who curses his God shall bear his sin. 16 Whoever blasphemes the Name of the LORD shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall stone him. The foreigner and the native alike, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.’ 17 Whoever strikes any person mortally shall surely be put to death. 18 Whoever strikes an animal shall make restitution for it, life for life. 19 If a man causes a defect in his neighbor, as he has done, so shall it be done to him: 20 fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he has caused a defect in a man, so shall it be done to him. 21 Whoever strikes an animal shall make restitution for it, and whoever strikes a man shall be put to death. 22 You shall have one judgment for the foreigner and for the native, for I am the LORD your God.”

23 Moses spoke to the Israelites, and they brought the blasphemer outside the camp and stoned him with stones. The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
