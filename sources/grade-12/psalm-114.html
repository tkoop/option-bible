Israel's Deliverance and God's Dwelling

1When Israel departed from Egypt, and the house of Jacob from a people whose language they did not understand, 2Judah became His sanctuary, and Israel His dominion.

3The sea beheld and fled; the Jordan reversed its course. 4The mountains leapt like rams, and the hills like lambs.

5What afflicted you, O sea, that you fled? O Jordan, that you recoiled? 6O mountains, that you leapt like rams? O hills, like lambs?

The Earth Trembles before the LORD

7Tremble, O earth, before the Lord, before the God of Jacob, 8who transformed the rock into a pool of water and the flint into a spring of water.
