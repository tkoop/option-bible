The Spirit's Warning About Coming Apostasy

1 Now the Spirit expressly declares that in later times some will fall away from the faith, devoting themselves to deceitful spirits and to demonic teachings, 2 through the hypocrisy of liars whose consciences have been seared as with a hot iron. 3 They forbid marriage and require abstinence from foods that God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth. 4 For every creature of God is good, and nothing is to be rejected when it is received with thanksgiving, 5 because it is sanctified by the word of God and by prayer.

A Good Servant of Christ Jesus

6 If you set these things before the brothers, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourished on the words of the faith and of the good teaching that you have followed. 7 But reject profane and foolish myths, and instead train yourself for godliness. 8 For physical training is of some value, but godliness is valuable in every respect, holding promise for the present life and also for the life to come.

Trustworthy Sayings and Apostolic Labor

9 This saying is trustworthy and worthy of full acceptance: 10 for this reason we labor and strive, because we have set our hope on the living God, who is the Savior of all people, especially of those who believe.

Directives for Timothy's Conduct and Ministry

11 Command and teach these things. 12 Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, and in purity. 13 Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of Scripture, to exhortation, and to teaching. 14 Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy when the council of elders laid their hands on you. 15 Practice these things; be absorbed in them, so that your progress may be evident to everyone. 16 Pay close attention to yourself and to the teaching; persevere in them, for by doing so you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
