Longing for the Courts of the LORD

For the choirmaster; according to Gittith. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. 1 How delightful are Your dwellings, O the LORD of Hosts!

2 My soul yearns—indeed, it faints—for the courts of the the LORD; my heart and my flesh cry out for the living God. 3 Even the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young—beside Your altars, O the LORD of Hosts, my King and my God. 4 Blessed are those who dwell in Your house; they are ever praising You. Selah.

The Blessing of Pilgrimage

5 Blessed are those whose strength is in You, whose hearts are set on pilgrimage. 6 As they pass through the Valley of Baca, they make it a place of springs; indeed, the autumn rains cover it with blessings. 7 They go from strength to strength, until each appears before God in Zion.

Prayer for God's Favor

8 O the LORD God of Hosts, hear my prayer; give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah. 9 Behold our shield, O God, and look with favor upon the face of Your anointed.

The Excellence of God's House

10 For a day in Your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere; I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of the wicked. 11 For the the LORD God is a sun and a shield; the the LORD bestows favor and honor; He withholds no good thing from those who walk in integrity. 12 O the LORD of Hosts, blessed is the man who trusts in You.
