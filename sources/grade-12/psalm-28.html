A Psalm of David: Deliverance, Thanksgiving, and Intercession

1 To You, O the LORD, I call; my Rock, do not be deaf to me. For if You remain silent, I shall become like those who descend into the pit. 2 Hear the voice of my supplications when I cry to You for help, when I lift my hands toward Your holy sanctuary.

3 Do not drag me away with the wicked, with those who practice iniquity, who speak peace to their neighbors while malice fills their hearts. 4 Repay them according to their deeds and the wickedness of their actions; repay them according to the work of their hands, and render to them their due. 5 Because they do not comprehend the works of the the LORD or the work of His hands, He will tear them down and never rebuild them.

6 Blessed be the the LORD, for He has heard the voice of my supplications. 7 The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart has trusted in Him, and I was helped. Therefore my heart exults, and with my song I will praise Him.

8 The LORD is strength to His people and a stronghold of salvation for His Anointed. 9 Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and carry them forever.
