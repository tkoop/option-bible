A Psalm of David: From Anguish to Universal Praise

1 My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? Why are you so far from saving me, and so far from heeding my anguished cries? 2 I cry out to you by day, yet you do not answer; I call by night, but find no rest. 3 Yet you are enthroned as the Holy One, the praise of Israel. 4 In you our ancestors trusted; they trusted, and you delivered them. 5 They cried to you and were rescued; they trusted in you and were never put to shame.

6 But I am a worm, and not a man—scorned by everyone, despised by the people. 7 All who see me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads.

8 "He trusts in the the LORD," they say. "Let the the LORD rescue him. Let him deliver him, since he delights in him."

9 Yet you brought me forth from the womb; you made me trust in you, even at my mother's breast. 10 From birth I was cast upon you; from my mother's womb you have been my God. 11 Do not be far from me, for trouble is near and no helper is at hand.

Surrounded by Fierce and Mocking Enemies

12 Many bulls surround me; the strong bulls of Bashan encircle me. 13 They open their mouths against me like roaring lions that tear their prey. 14 I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint. My heart has turned to wax; it melts within me. 15 My strength is dried up like a shard of pottery, and my tongue cleaves to the roof of my mouth; you lay me in the dust of death.

16 Dogs surround me; a band of evildoers encircles me. They have pierced my hands and my feet. 17 I can count all my bones; people stare and gloat over me. 18 They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.

19 But you, the the LORD, do not be far from me. You are my strength; come quickly to help me. 20 Deliver me from the sword, my precious life from the power of the dogs. 21 Rescue me from the mouth of the lions; save me from the horns of the wild oxen. You have answered me.

A Vow of Praise and the Gathering of the Nations

22 I will proclaim your name to my brothers; in the assembly I will praise you. 23 You who fear the the LORD, praise Him! All you descendants of Jacob, honor Him! Revere Him, all you descendants of Israel! 24 For He has not despised or scorned the suffering of the afflicted one; He has not hidden His face from him but has listened to his cry for help. 25 From you comes the theme of my praise in the great assembly; before those who fear Him I will fulfill my vows.

26 The poor will eat and be satisfied; those who seek the the LORD will praise Him—may your hearts live forever! 27 All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the the LORD, and all the families of the nations will bow down before you, 28 for dominion belongs to the the LORD and He rules over the nations. 29 All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who go down to the dust will kneel before Him—those who cannot keep themselves alive. 30 Posterity will serve Him; future generations will be told about the Lord. 31 They will proclaim His righteousness, declaring to a people yet unborn: He has done it!
