The Day of the LORD

1 Blow the trumpet in Zion; shout aloud on My holy mountain. Let all who dwell in the land tremble, for the day of the the LORD is coming; indeed, it is near. 2 A day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness, like dawn spread over the mountains—a great and mighty people; there has never been anything like it from eternity, nor will there be again through generations. 3 Before them fire devours; behind them flame blazes. The land before them is like Eden; behind them, a desolate wilderness. Indeed, nothing escapes them. 4 Their appearance is like horses; like warhorses they charge. 5 Like the rumble of chariots they leap over the mountain peaks; like the crackle of a devouring fire; like a mighty army arrayed for battle. 6 Before them the peoples are in anguish; every face grows pale. 7 They run like warriors; they scale walls like soldiers; each marches straight ahead and does not break rank. 8 They do not jostle one another; each proceeds in his path; and they burst through the defenses without breaking ranks. 9 They rush into the city; they run upon the walls; they climb into the houses; they enter through the windows like a thief. 10 Before them the earth quakes; the heavens tremble; the sun and moon are darkened, and the stars withdraw their shining. 11 The the LORD utters His voice before His army; His camp is very great; mighty is the One who carries out His word. For the day of the the LORD is great and very awesome; who can endure it?

A Call to Repentance

12 “Yet even now,” declares the the LORD, “return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning.” 13 “Rend your hearts, not your garments, and return to the the LORD your God, for He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love, and He relents from bringing disaster. 14 Who knows? He may turn and relent and leave a blessing behind Him—a grain offering and a drink offering for the the LORD your God.”

A Solemn Assembly

15 Blow the trumpet in Zion; consecrate a fast; proclaim a solemn assembly. 16 Gather the people; consecrate the congregation; assemble the elders; gather the children, even nursing infants; let the bridegroom leave his chamber and the bride her canopy. 17 Let the priests, the ministers of the the LORD, weep between the portico and the altar, and let them say, “Spare Your people, O the LORD, and do not make Your inheritance a reproach, a byword among the nations. Why should they say among the peoples, ‘Where is their God?’”

The LORD's Restoration

18 Then the the LORD became jealous for His land and had pity on His people.

“19 Behold, I am sending you grain, new wine, and oil, and you will be satisfied; I will never again make you a reproach among the nations. 20 I will remove the northerner far from you and drive him into a parched and desolate land, his front toward the eastern sea and his rear toward the western sea; his stench will rise, and his foul odor will ascend, for he has done great things. 21 Do not be afraid, O land; rejoice and be glad, for the the LORD has done great things.

“22 Do not be afraid, you beasts of the field, for the pastures of the wilderness are green; the trees bear their fruit; the fig tree and the vine yield their strength. 23 Rejoice, O children of Zion, and be glad in the the LORD your God, for He has given you the autumn rain for your vindication; He has poured down rain for you, the autumn and spring rains, as before. 24 The threshing floors will be full of grain; the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.

“25 I will repay you for the years the swarming locust has eaten—the young locust, the destroying locust, and the consuming locust—My great army that I sent among you. 26 You will eat in plenty and be satisfied, and you will praise the name of the the LORD your God, who has dealt wondrously with you; and My people will never again be put to shame. 27 Then you will know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the the LORD your God, and there is no other; and My people will never again be put to shame.

“28 And afterward I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh; your sons and your daughters will prophesy, your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions. 29 Even on the male and female servants I will pour out My Spirit in those days. 30 I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth—blood, fire, and columns of smoke. 31 The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood before the coming of the great and awesome day of the the LORD. 32 And everyone who calls on the name of the the LORD will be saved; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be deliverance, as the the LORD has said, and among the survivors whom the the LORD calls.”
