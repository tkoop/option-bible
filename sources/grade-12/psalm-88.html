A Lament from the Depths of Despair

A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the director of music. According to Mahalath Leannoth. A contemplative poem of Heman the Ezrahite. 1 The LORD, God of my salvation, I have cried out before You day and night. 2 Let my prayer come before You; incline Your ear to my supplication. 3 For my soul is saturated with troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol.

4 I am reckoned among those who descend into the pit; I am like a man bereft of strength. 5 Abandoned among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom You no longer remember, I am severed from Your care. 6 You have placed me in the lowest depths, in realms of darkness, in the abyss.

7 Your wrath bears down heavily upon me, and You have assaulted me with all Your billows. Selah. 8 You have alienated my acquaintances from me; You have made me loathsome to them. I am imprisoned and cannot escape. 9 My eyes are dimmed by affliction. I have called upon You, the LORD, daily; I have spread out my hands to You.

10 Will You perform wonders for the dead? Will the departed rise to praise You? Selah. 11 Will Your steadfast love be declared in the grave, Your faithfulness in Abaddon? 12 Will Your wonders be known in the darkness, and Your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

13 Yet to You, the LORD, I cry out; in the morning my prayer comes before You. 14 Why, the LORD, do You reject my life? Why do You hide Your face from me? 15 Afflicted and close to death from my youth, I have suffered Your terrors; I am in despair.

16 Your burning anger has swept over me; Your terrors have destroyed me. 17 They surround me like water all day long; they close in on me from every side. 18 You have removed far from me friend and companion; darkness is my only companion.
