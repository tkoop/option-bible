Oracle Concerning the Valley of Vision

1 An oracle concerning the Valley of Vision: What ails you now, that you have all gone up to the rooftops? 2 O city of commotion, O town of revelry! Your slain are not slain by the sword, nor killed in battle. 3 All your leaders have fled together, captured without a bow; all your fugitives were captured together, having fled far away. 4 Therefore I said, "Turn away from me; let me weep bitterly. Do not try to console me over the destruction of the daughter of my people."

The LORD's Day of Tumult in the Valley

5 For the Lord the LORD of Hosts has a day of tumult, trampling, and confusion in the Valley of Vision—tearing down walls and crying out to the mountain. 6 Elam takes up the quiver, with chariots and horsemen, and Kir uncovers the shield. 7 Your choicest valleys are full of chariots, and horsemen are posted at the gates.

8 He has uncovered the defense of Judah. In that day you looked to the weapons in the House of the Forest. 9 You saw that the breaches in the City of David were many, and you collected the water of the lower pool. 10 You counted the houses of Jerusalem and tore down houses to fortify the wall. 11 You made a reservoir between the walls for the water of the old pool, but you did not look to Him who made it, nor did you consider Him who planned it long ago.

12 On that day the Lord the LORD of Hosts called for weeping and wailing, for shaving the head and wearing sackcloth. 13 But look, there is joy and gladness, slaughtering cattle and killing sheep, eating meat and drinking wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!" 14 The LORD of Hosts has revealed in my ears: "Surely this iniquity will not be forgiven you until you die," says the Lord the LORD of Hosts.

Shebna Replaced by Eliakim

15 This is what the Lord the LORD of Hosts says: "Go, say to this steward, to Shebna, who is in charge of the palace: 'What are you doing here, and who gave you permission to cut out a tomb for yourself here—cutting your tomb on the height and chiseling a resting place for yourself in the rock? Look, the the LORD is about to hurl you away, O man. He will seize you firmly and roll you up tightly like a ball and throw you into a large country. There you will die, and there your glorious chariots will be—the shame of your master's house. I will depose you from your office, and you will be ousted from your position.'"

20 "In that day I will summon My servant, Eliakim son of Hilkiah. 21 I will clothe him with your robe and fasten your sash around him. I will hand your authority over to him, and he will be a father to the residents of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. 22 I will place on his shoulder the key to the house of David. What he opens no one will shut, and what he shuts no one will open. 23 I will drive him like a peg into a firm place, and he will become a throne of honor for his father's house. 24 All the glory of his father's house will hang on him: the offspring and the offshoots—all the small vessels, from bowls to every kind of jar."

25 "In that day," declares the the LORD of Hosts, "the peg driven into a firm place will give way; it will be cut down and fall, and the load hanging on it will be cut off." For the the LORD has spoken.
