Famine and the Journey to Moab

1 In the days when the judges governed Israel, a severe famine afflicted the land, and a man from Bethlehem in Judah departed with his wife and two sons to sojourn in the territory of Moab. 2 The man was named Elimelech; his wife was Naomi, and his two sons were Mahlon and Chilion, Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. They entered Moab and settled there.

3 Then Elimelech, Naomi’s husband, died, leaving her with her two sons. 4 Her sons married Moabite women—one named Orpah and the other Ruth—and they resided there for about ten years. 5 Then both Mahlon and Chilion died as well, and the woman was bereaved of her husband and her two sons.

Naomi Prepares to Return to Judah

6 When Naomi heard in Moab that the LORD had attended to His people by granting them food, she arose with her daughters-in-law to return from the territory of Moab. 7 Accompanied by her two daughters-in-law, she left the place where she had been living and set out on the road back to the land of Judah.

Naomi Urges Her Daughters-in-Law to Remain

8 Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “Go, return each to her mother’s house. May the LORD deal kindly with you, as you have dealt with the dead and with me. 9 May the LORD grant you security, each in the home of a husband.” Then she kissed them, and they wept aloud.

10 They said, “We will surely return with you to your people.”

11 Naomi said, “Return, my daughters; why should you go with me? Do I still have sons in my womb who could become your husbands? 12 Return, my daughters; go your way, for I am too old to have a husband. Even if I said I still had hope—even if I had a husband tonight and bore sons— 13 would you wait for them to grow up? Would you refrain from marrying because of them? No, my daughters, for it is far more bitter for me than for you, because the LORD has afflicted me.”

Ruth’s Vow and the Return to Bethlehem

14 They raised their voices and wept again. Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but Ruth clung to her.

15 Naomi said, “See, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods. Return after your sister-in-law.”

16 Ruth said, “Do not urge me to leave you or to turn back from following you. Where you go I will go, and where you lodge I will lodge. Your people will be my people, and your God my God. 17 Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the LORD do so to me, and more also, if anything but death separates me from you.”

18 When Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her, she ceased speaking to her.

19 So the two traveled on until they reached Bethlehem. When they arrived in Bethlehem, the whole town was stirred because of them, and the women asked, “Is this Naomi?”

20 She said, “Do not call me Naomi; call me Mara, for the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me. 21 I went away full, but the LORD has brought me back empty. Why do you call me Naomi, when the LORD has brought this suffering upon me and the Almighty has afflicted me?”

22 So Naomi returned, accompanied by Ruth the Moabitess, her daughter-in-law, who came back with her from Moab. They reached Bethlehem at the beginning of the barley harvest.
