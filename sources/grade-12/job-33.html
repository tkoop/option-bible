Elihu Appeals to Job

1 But now, Job, hear my words, please; listen to all that I have to say. 2 Behold, I have opened my mouth; my tongue speaks. 3 My words declare the uprightness of my heart, and my lips utter pure knowledge. 4 The Spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life.

5 Answer me, if you can; arrange your arguments before me and take your stand. 6 Behold, I am as you are before God; I too was formed from clay. 7 Behold, my terror will not frighten you, nor will my burden weigh heavily on you. 8 Surely you spoke in my hearing, and I heard the sound of your words.

9 "I am pure, without transgression; I am clean, and there is no iniquity in me. 10 Look, He finds reasons to oppose me; He counts me as His enemy. 11 He puts my feet in the stocks and watches all my paths."

12 Look, in this you are not right; I will answer you, for God is greater than man. 13 Why do you contend with Him, saying that He will not answer any of your words? 14 For God speaks once, and even twice, yet man does not perceive it.

God Speaks Through Dreams and Discipline

15 In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls on men, while they slumber on their beds, 16 then He opens the ears of men and seals His discipline upon them, 17 to turn a man from his deed and hide pride from him. 18 He spares his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing by the sword.

19 He is also chastened with pain on his bed, and with strife in his bones, 20 so that his life abhors bread, and his soul craves delicacies. 21 His flesh wastes away so that it cannot be seen, and his bones, once hidden, protrude. 22 His soul draws near to the pit, and his life to those who destroy.

The Mediator and Restoration

23 If there is an angel for him, one mediator among a thousand, to declare to a man his uprightness, 24 and He is gracious to him and says, "Deliver him from going down to the pit; I have found a ransom,"

25 his flesh is restored like a child's; he returns to the days of his youth. 26 He prays to God, and He accepts him; he sees His face with a shout of joy, and He restores his righteousness to man. 27 Then he sings before men and says, "I have sinned and perverted what is right, yet it was not repaid to me." 28 He has redeemed my soul from going down to the pit, and my life will see the light.

29 Behold, God does all these things two or three times with a man, 30 to bring back his soul from the pit, that he may be enlightened with the light of life.

Elihu's Final Challenge

31 Pay attention, Job, and listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. 32 If you have words, answer me; speak, for I desire to justify you. 33 If not, listen to me; be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.
