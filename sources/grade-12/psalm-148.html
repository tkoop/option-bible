All Creation's Call to Worship the LORD

1 Hallelujah! Praise the the LORD from the heavens; praise Him in the loftiest realms. 2 Praise Him, all His angels; praise Him, all His celestial hosts. 3 Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, all you radiant stars. 4 Praise Him, you highest heavens, and waters beyond the skies.

5 Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for at His command they were created. 6 He established them for all eternity; He decreed a law that shall never be revoked.

7 Praise the the LORD from the earth, you great sea creatures and all ocean depths, 8 fire and hail, snow and mist, stormy winds executing His word; 9 you mountains and all hills, fruit-bearing trees and all cedars; 10 wild beasts and all livestock, creeping things and winged birds.

11 Kings of the earth and all nations, you princes and all judges of the earth, 12 young men and maidens, elders and children.

13 Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for His name alone is exalted; His majesty surpasses earth and heaven. 14 He has raised up a horn for His people, the praise of all His saints, of Israel, a people near to Him. Hallelujah!
