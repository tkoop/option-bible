Judah Subjugated and Jehoiakim's Rebellion

1 During Jehoiakim’s reign, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon invaded Judah; Jehoiakim served him for three years, but then he rebelled. 2 The LORD sent raiding bands of Chaldeans, Arameans, Moabites, and Ammonites against him; He sent them against Judah to destroy it, in accordance with the word that the LORD had spoken through His servants the prophets. 3 Surely this disaster came upon Judah at the LORD’s command, to remove them from His presence, because of the sins of Manasseh and everything he had done. 4 Moreover, because of the innocent blood Jehoiakim had shed—filling Jerusalem with it—the LORD was unwilling to forgive.

5 As for the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 6 Jehoiakim died and was buried with his ancestors, and his son Jehoiachin succeeded him. 7 The king of Egypt did not march out of his land again, because the king of Babylon had taken all that had belonged to the king of Egypt, from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River.

Jehoiachin's Surrender and the First Exile

8 Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem three months. His mother’s name was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan; she was from Jerusalem. 9 He did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as his father had done. 10 At that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon advanced on Jerusalem, and the city came under siege. 11 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon himself arrived while his servants were besieging it. 12 Jehoiachin king of Judah, together with his mother, his servants, his commanders, and his officials, surrendered to the king of Babylon. The king of Babylon took him captive in the eighth year of his reign.

13 He carried out all the treasures from the house of the LORD and from the royal palace, and he cut to pieces all the gold articles that Solomon king of Israel had made for the temple of the LORD, just as the LORD had foretold. 14 He carried into exile all Jerusalem: all the officials, all the mighty warriors, all the craftsmen and smiths—ten thousand captives in all. Only the poorest people of the land remained. 15 He exiled Jehoiachin to Babylon, along with the king’s mother, the king’s wives, his eunuchs, and the leading men of the land; he led them all from Jerusalem into exile in Babylon. 16 The king of Babylon brought to Babylon all seven thousand men of valor, one thousand craftsmen and smiths, and all who were strong and fit for war.

Zedekiah's Reign and Final Defiance

17 Then the king of Babylon made Mattaniah, Jehoiachin’s uncle, king in his place and changed his name to Zedekiah. 18 Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eleven years. His mother’s name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah; she was from Libnah. 19 He did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as Jehoiakim had done. 20 Because of the LORD’s anger against Jerusalem and Judah, He finally banished them from His presence, and Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.
