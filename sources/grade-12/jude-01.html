Greeting and Purpose

1 Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, beloved in God the Father and kept for Jesus Christ: 2 May mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you. 3 Beloved, although I was eager to write to you about the salvation we share, I felt compelled to write and urge you to contend for the faith once for all entrusted to the saints. 4 For certain men have crept in unnoticed—ungodly men, long ago designated for this condemnation—who turn the grace of our God into licentiousness and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

Warnings from Israel's History

5 Now I want to remind you, though you already know all this, that Jesus, having once saved a people out of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe. 6 And the angels who did not keep their own domain but abandoned their proper dwelling, He has kept in eternal chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day. 7 Just as Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, having indulged in sexual immorality and gone after strange flesh, are set forth as an example, undergoing the punishment of eternal fire.

The Character and Doom of the Ungodly

8 Yet in the same way these dreamers also defile the flesh, reject authority, and blaspheme glorious beings. 9 But when the archangel Michael contended with the devil and disputed about the body of Moses, he did not dare to pronounce a reviling judgment, but said, "The Lord rebuke you!" 10 But these men blaspheme whatever they do not understand; and in whatever they know by instinct, like unreasoning animals, they corrupt themselves. 11 Woe to them! For they have gone in the way of Cain and rushed for profit into the error of Balaam and perished in the rebellion of Korah.

12 These are hidden reefs in your love feasts, carousing fearlessly with you, shepherding themselves; waterless clouds carried along by winds; fruitless autumn trees, twice dead, uprooted. 13 They are wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame; wandering stars, for whom the blackness of darkness is reserved forever.

14 Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about these men, saying, "Behold, the Lord came with His holy myriads 15 to execute judgment on all and to convict all the ungodly of all their ungodly deeds that they have committed in ungodliness, and of all the harsh things that ungodly sinners have spoken against Him." 16 These are grumblers and malcontents, following their own desires; their mouths speak swelling words, flattering people to gain advantage.

Exhortations and Final Praise

17 But you, beloved, remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, 18 for they told you, "In the last time there will be scoffers, following their own ungodly passions." 19 These are the ones who cause divisions, worldly, lacking the Spirit.

20 But you, beloved, building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit, 21 keep yourselves in the love of God, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life. 22 And have mercy on those who doubt. 23 Save others by snatching them out of the fire; and to others show mercy with fear, hating even the garment stained by the flesh.

24 Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you blameless before the presence of His glory with great joy, 25 to the only God our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority, before all time and now and forever. Amen.
