A Lament for Pharaoh of Egypt

1 In the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month, the word of the the LORD came to me:

2 “Son of man, take up a lamentation for Pharaoh king of Egypt, and say to him: ‘You are like a young lion among the nations, yet you are like a monster in the seas; you thrash about in your rivers, churn the water with your feet, and muddy their rivers.’

3 “This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘I will spread My net over you with a great assembly of peoples, and they will haul you up in My dragnet.

4 “‘I will leave you on the land and cast you upon the open field. I will settle all the birds of heaven on you, and I will sate every beast of the earth with you.

5 “‘I will lay your flesh upon the mountains and fill the valleys with your remains. 6 I will drench the land with the outflow of your blood, even to the mountains, and the ravines will be filled with you.

7 “‘When I extinguish you, I will cover the heavens and darken their stars; I will veil the sun with a cloud, and the moon will withhold its light. 8 All the luminaries of heaven I will darken over you, and I will bring darkness upon your land,’ declares the Lord GOD.

9 “‘I will also trouble the hearts of many peoples when I bring your ruin among the nations, in lands you have not known.

10 “‘I will make many peoples aghast at you, and their kings will shudder with horror because of you when I brandish My sword before them. On the day of your downfall each will tremble unceasingly for his life.’

11 “For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘The sword of the king of Babylon will come against you.

12 “‘By the swords of the mighty I will bring down your hordes; the most ruthless of nations, all of them, will pillage the pride of Egypt, and all her hordes will be annihilated.

13 “‘I will destroy all her livestock beside abundant waters, so that no human foot or beast's hoof will ever muddy them again. 14 Then I will make their waters settle and cause their rivers to flow like oil,’ declares the Lord GOD.

15 “‘When I make the land of Egypt a desolation, emptied of all that fills it, and when I strike down all who dwell in it, then they will know that I am the the LORD.’

16 “‘This is the lamentation that will be chanted; the daughters of the nations will chant it. Over Egypt and all her hordes they will chant it,’ declares the Lord GOD.”

Egypt Consigned to the Pit

17 In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me, saying:

18 “Son of man, wail over the hordes of Egypt and consign her, with the daughters of mighty nations, to the lowest parts of the earth, to those who descend into the Pit. 19 ‘Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down and be laid among the uncircumcised.’ 20 They will fall among those slain by the sword. The sword is given; drag her away with all her hordes.

21 “Mighty chiefs will speak to him from the midst of Sheol concerning his allies: ‘They have come down; they lie with the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword.’

The Slain Nations in Sheol

22 “Assyria is there with her whole company; her graves surround her. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword.

23 “Her graves are set in the depths of the Pit, and her company surrounds her grave. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword—those who once spread terror in the land of the living.

24 “Elam is there with all her hordes around her grave. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword—those who went down uncircumcised to the depths of the earth, who once spread terror in the land of the living. They bear their disgrace with those who descend into the Pit.

25 “They have made a bed for her among the slain, with all her hordes. Her graves surround her. All of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, because their terror was spread in the land of the living. They bear their disgrace with those who descend into the Pit; she has been placed among the slain.

26 “Meshech and Tubal are there with all their hordes. Their graves surround them. All of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, because they spread their terror in the land of the living.

27 “They do not lie with the fallen warriors of old, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, whose swords were placed beneath their heads, and whose shields rested on their bones, though the terror of the mighty was in the land of the living.

28 “But you too will be broken and lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword.

29 “Edom is there, her kings and all her princes, who despite their might are laid among those slain by the sword. They lie with the uncircumcised and with those who descend into the Pit.

30 “All the chiefs of the north and all the Sidonians are there, who went down in shame with the slain, despite the terror of their might. They lie uncircumcised with those slain by the sword, and bear their disgrace with those who descend into the Pit.

31 “Pharaoh will see them and be comforted over all his hordes—Pharaoh and all his army, slain by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

32 “For I have put his terror in the land of the living, yet he will be laid among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword—Pharaoh and all his hordes, declares the Lord GOD.”
