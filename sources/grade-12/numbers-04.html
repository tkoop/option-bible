The Kohathites’ Sacred Transport Duties

1 Then the the LORD addressed Moses and Aaron, saying, 2 “Conduct a census of the Kohathites among the Levites, by their clans and ancestral families— 3 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those eligible to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 4 This is the service of the Kohathites at the Tent of Meeting: the most holy things.

5 “Whenever the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall enter, take down the veil of the screen, and cover the ark of the Testimony with it. 6 Then they shall place over it a covering of fine leather, spread a cloth of solid blue over it, and insert its carrying poles. 7 Over the table of the Presence they shall spread a blue cloth and place on it the plates, dishes, bowls, and pitchers for the drink offering, together with the continual bread offering. 8 And they shall spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover them with fine leather, and insert the poles. 9 They shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand that gives light, along with its lamps, wick trimmers, trays, and all the oil vessels used in its service. 10 Then they shall place it, with all its utensils, inside a covering of fine leather and set it upon the transport frame. 11 Over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, cover it with fine leather, and insert its poles. 12 They shall take all the utensils employed in sanctuary service, place them in a blue cloth, cover them with fine leather, and set them upon a transport frame. 13 They shall remove the ashes from the bronze altar and spread a purple cloth over it. 14 And they shall place on it all the vessels used for service—the firepans, meat forks, shovels, and sprinkling bowls, every utensil of the altar. Over it they shall spread a covering of fine leather and insert its poles.

15 “When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy objects and all their utensils, as the camp sets out the Kohathites shall come and bear them; yet they must not touch the holy objects, lest they die. These are the bearing responsibilities of the Kohathites concerning the Tent of Meeting. 16 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest shall have charge of the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the regular grain offering, and the anointing oil. He shall have oversight of the entire tabernacle and everything within it, including the holy objects and their utensils.”

Safeguards for the Kohathites

17 Then the the LORD addressed Moses and Aaron, saying, 18 “Do not permit the Kohathite clans to be severed from among the Levites. 19 So that they may live and not die when they approach the most holy things, do this for them: Aaron and his sons shall enter and assign each man his task and what he is to carry. 20 But the Kohathites shall not enter to look upon the holy objects, even for a moment, or they will die.”

The Gershonites and Merarites Assigned

21 Then the the LORD addressed Moses, saying, 22 “Likewise, register the Gershonites by their families and clans, 23 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those eligible to perform service in the Tent of Meeting. 24 This is the service of the Gershonite clans pertaining to labor and transport: 25 They shall carry the curtains of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting with its covering and the covering of fine leather above it, and the screen for the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, 26 the curtains of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance to the courtyard surrounding the tabernacle and altar, and their ropes, together with all the equipment for their service and everything related to them. They shall perform every aspect of this work.

27 “All the service of the Gershonites—their loads and their labor—shall be executed under the directive of Aaron and his sons. You shall designate for them every article they are charged to bear. 28 This is the service of the Gershonite clans at the Tent of Meeting, and their duties shall be under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

29 “Regarding the Merarites, you shall enumerate them by their clans and families, 30 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those eligible to perform service in the Tent of Meeting. 31 This is the obligation encompassing their entire service at the Tent of Meeting: to carry the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, posts, and bases, 32 as well as the posts of the surrounding courtyard with their bases, tent pegs, and ropes, including all their equipment and all the work related to them. You shall designate by name the articles each individual must transport. 33 This is the service of the Merarite clans according to all their work at the Tent of Meeting, under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.”

Numbering the Levitical Clans

34 So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of the congregation enumerated the Kohathites by their clans and families, 35 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those able to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 36 And the number of those registered by their clans was 2,750. 37 These were the enumerated men of the Kohathite clans, all who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron numbered them according to the command of the the LORD through Moses.

38 Then the Gershonites were enumerated by their clans and families, 39 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those able to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 40 And the number of those registered by their clans and families was 2,630. 41 These were the enumerated men of the Gershonite clans, all who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron numbered them according to the command of the the LORD.

42 And the Merarites were enumerated by their clans and families, 43 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those able to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 44 The number of those registered was 3,200. 45 These were the enumerated men of the Merarite clans, all who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron numbered them according to the command of the the LORD through Moses.

46 So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel enumerated all the Levites by their clans and families, 47 men from thirty to fifty years of age, all those able to serve in the labor at the Tent of Meeting and in its transport. 48 And the number of those registered was 8,580. 49 At the the LORD’s command, they were numbered by Moses, each man according to his service and his responsibility, as the the LORD had commanded Moses.
