The Closed East Gate

1 He brought me back by way of the outer gate of the sanctuary facing east, and it was shut. 2 The LORD said to me, "This gate will remain shut; it will not be opened, and no one will enter through it, because the LORD, the God of Israel, has entered through it. Therefore it will remain shut." 3 Only the prince may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD; he will enter by way of the porch of the gate and go out by the same way.

The Glory of the LORD and Israel's Rebuke

4 He brought me to the front of the temple by way of the north gate, and I saw the glory of the LORD filling the house of the LORD; I fell on my face. 5 The LORD said to me, "Son of man, pay close attention and look with your eyes and listen with your ears to everything I tell you concerning all the statutes and laws of the house of the LORD. Pay close attention to the entrance of the house and to all its exits."

6 Say to the rebellious house of Israel, "Thus says the Lord the LORD: House of Israel, you have had enough of all your abominations. 7 When you brought foreigners—uncircumcised in heart and flesh—into My sanctuary, profaning My house, while you offered My food, the fat and the blood, they broke My covenant in addition to all your abominations. 8 You have not kept charge of My holy things; instead, you have appointed others to keep My charge in My sanctuary for yourselves. 9 Thus says the Lord the LORD: No foreigner uncircumcised in heart or flesh will enter My sanctuary—not any foreigner among the Israelites."

The Demoted Levites and the Faithful Zadokites

10 But the Levites who went far from Me when Israel strayed from Me after their idols will bear their guilt. 11 They will serve in My sanctuary, keeping charge at the gates of the house and ministering to the house. They will slaughter burnt offerings and sacrifices for the people and stand before them to serve them. 12 Because they served the people in the presence of their idols and caused the house of Israel to stumble into iniquity, I have sworn against them, declares the Lord the LORD, and they will bear their iniquity. 13 They must not come near Me to serve as priests or approach any of My holy things, especially the most holy things; they will bear their shame and the abominations they committed. 14 I will appoint them to keep charge of the temple, for all its service and all that is done in it.

15 But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept charge of My sanctuary when the Israelites went astray from Me, will come near to Me to serve Me. They will stand before Me to offer Me fat and blood, declares the Lord the LORD. 16 They will enter My sanctuary and come near My table to serve Me, and they will keep My charge.

Priestly Vestments, Conduct, and Provisions

17 When they enter the gates of the inner court, they must wear linen garments; no wool may be on them while they serve at the gates of the inner court or inside. 18 They must wear linen turbans on their heads and linen undergarments on their waists, and they must not wear anything that makes them sweat. 19 When they go out to the outer court to the people, they must remove the garments in which they served, leave them in the holy rooms, and put on other garments, so that they do not consecrate the people with their garments. 20 They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long; they must trim their hair.

21 No priest may drink wine when he enters the inner court. 22 They must not marry widows or divorced women, but only virgins from Israel's descendants, or a widow of a priest. 23 They will teach My people the difference between holy and profane and help them distinguish unclean from clean. 24 In disputes they will stand to judge according to My ordinances; they will keep My laws and statutes at all My appointed feasts and sanctify My Sabbaths. 25 They must not defile themselves by approaching a dead person, except for father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or an unmarried sister. 26 Afterward he must count seven days for his purification. 27 On the day he enters the sanctuary, into the inner court, to serve in the sanctuary, he must offer his sin offering, declares the Lord the LORD.

28 It will be their inheritance: I am their inheritance. You must give them no possession in Israel; I am their possession. 29 They will eat the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering, and every devoted thing in Israel will belong to them. 30 The first of all firstfruits and every contribution from all your contributions will belong to the priests; you must also give the priest the first of your dough, so that a blessing may rest on your house. 31 The priests must not eat anything found dead or torn by beasts, whether bird or animal.
