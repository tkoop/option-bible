The Amorite Coalition Against Gibeon

1 When Adoni-zedek, king of Jerusalem, heard that Joshua had captured Ai and devoted it to destruction, treating Ai and its king as he had treated Jericho and its king, and that the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and were living among them, 2 he was greatly alarmed. Gibeon was a great city, like one of the royal cities, larger than Ai, and all its men were mighty warriors. 3 So Adoni-zedek, king of Jerusalem, sent word to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish, and Debir king of Eglon, saying, 4 “Come up to me and help me, so that we may strike Gibeon, because it has made peace with Joshua and with the Israelites.”

5 So these five Amorite kings—the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon—gathered their forces, marched up with all their armies, encamped against Gibeon, and attacked it.

6 The men of Gibeon sent word to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal: “Do not abandon your servants. Come up to us quickly and save us and help us, for all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hill country have gathered against us.” 7 So Joshua marched up from Gilgal with all his troops, including all the mighty warriors.

The LORD Fights for Israel

8 The LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them, for I have given them into your hand. Not one of them will be able to stand against you.” 9 Joshua therefore came upon them suddenly, having marched all night from Gilgal.

10 The LORD threw them into confusion before Israel, and Israel struck them with a great slaughter at Gibeon. Israel pursued them along the road up to Beth Horon and struck them as far as Azekah and Makkedah. 11 While they fled before Israel down the descent from Beth Horon, the LORD hurled large hailstones from heaven upon them as far as Azekah, and they died. More died from the hailstones than the Israelites killed with the sword.

12 On the day the LORD delivered the Amorites to the Israelites, Joshua spoke to the LORD in the sight of Israel: “Sun, stand still over Gibeon, and moon, over the Valley of Aijalon.” 13 So the sun stood still and the moon stopped, until the nation avenged itself on its enemies. Is this not written in the Book of Jashar? The sun stood still in the middle of the sky and did not hurry to set for about a whole day. 14 There has never been a day like it before or since, when the LORD listened to the voice of a man, for the LORD fought for Israel. 15 Then Joshua returned, with all Israel, to the camp at Gilgal.

The Five Kings Are Captured and Executed

16 Those five kings fled and hid in the cave at Makkedah. 17 Joshua was told, “The five kings have been found; they are hiding in the cave at Makkedah.” 18 Joshua said, “Roll large stones against the mouth of the cave and station men there to guard them.” 19 “But do not stay there yourselves. Pursue your enemies and attack them from the rear. Do not let them reach their cities, for the LORD your God has given them into your hand.” 20 When Joshua and the Israelites had finished striking them down with a very great slaughter, until they were wiped out, the survivors entered the fortified cities. 21 Then all the people returned safely to Joshua at the camp at Makkedah, and no one spoke against the Israelites. 22 Joshua said, “Open the mouth of the cave and bring those five kings out to me.” 23 They did so and brought out the five kings—the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon. 24 When they brought them out to Joshua, he summoned all the Israelites and said to the commanders of the soldiers who had marched with him, “Come near and put your feet on the necks of these kings.” They came near and put their feet on their necks. 25 Joshua said to them, “Do not be afraid or discouraged. Be strong and courageous, for the LORD will do this to all your enemies against whom you fight.” 26 Then Joshua struck them down and killed them, and he hanged them on five trees, where they hung until evening. 27 At sunset Joshua gave the command, and they took them down from the trees and threw them into the cave where they had hidden. They placed large stones at the mouth of the cave, and they remain there to this day.

The Conquest of the Southern Cities

28 That same day Joshua captured Makkedah. He struck it and its king with the sword, devoting every inhabitant to destruction and leaving no survivor. He treated the king of Makkedah as he had treated the king of Jericho. 29 Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved on from Makkedah to Libnah and fought against it. 30 The LORD also gave it and its king into Israel’s hand. Joshua struck it and every person in it with the sword, leaving no survivor, and he treated its king as he had treated the king of Jericho. 31 Then Joshua and all Israel with him moved on from Libnah to Lachish, besieged it, and fought against it. 32 The LORD gave Lachish into Israel’s hand, and Joshua captured it on the second day. He struck it and every person in it with the sword, just as he had done to Libnah. 33 Then Horam, king of Gezer, came up to help Lachish, but Joshua struck him and his people until no survivor remained. 34 From Lachish, Joshua and all Israel with him moved on to Eglon. They besieged it and fought against it. 35 They captured it that day and struck it with the sword, devoting every inhabitant to destruction, just as they had done to Lachish. 36 Then Joshua and all Israel with him went up from Eglon to Hebron and fought against it. 37 They captured it and struck it with the sword—its king, all its towns, and every inhabitant—leaving no survivor, just as they had done to Eglon. Joshua devoted the city and every inhabitant to destruction. 38 Then Joshua and all Israel with him turned back to Debir and fought against it. 39 He captured it, along with its king and all its towns. They struck them with the sword and devoted every inhabitant to destruction, leaving no survivor. Just as he had done to Hebron, to Libnah, and to their kings, so he did to Debir and to its king. 40 Thus Joshua struck the entire land—the hill country, the Negev, the lowlands, and the slopes—along with all their kings. He left no survivor, but devoted to destruction everything that breathed, just as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded. 41 Joshua struck them from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza, and all the land of Goshen as far as Gibeon. 42 Joshua captured all these kings and their land at one time, because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel. 43 Then Joshua returned, with all Israel, to the camp at Gilgal.
