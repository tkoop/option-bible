Israel's Forbidden Rejoicing

1 Do not rejoice, O Israel, with exultation like the nations, for you have committed spiritual adultery, turning away from your God; you have loved the payment received for harlotry on every threshing floor. 2 Threshing floor and winepress will not sustain them, and the new wine will fail her.

3 They will not remain in the land of the the LORD; Ephraim will return to Egypt and eat unclean food in Assyria. 4 They will not pour out wine offerings to the the LORD, nor will their sacrifices please Him. Their bread will be like the bread eaten in mourning; all who eat it will be defiled, for their bread is for themselves and will not enter the house of the the LORD.

Exile and the Loss of Festivals

5 What will you do on the day of the appointed festival and on the day of the the LORD's feast? 6 For behold, they have fled from devastation. Egypt will gather them; Memphis will bury them. Weeds will take possession of their silver treasures, and thorns will fill their tents.

7 The days of punishment have come; the days of recompense have come. Israel will know. The prophet is a fool; the man of the spirit is insane, because of your great iniquity and great hostility. 8 Ephraim's watchman is with my God, yet a fowler's snare lies on all his ways, and hostility is in the house of his God. 9 They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah; He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins.

Ephraim's Fading Glory

10 I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers like the first ripe fig on a fig tree in its first season. But they went to Baal Peor and consecrated themselves to shame, and they became detestable, like the thing they loved.

11 As for Ephraim, their glory will fly away like a bird—no birth, no pregnancy, no conception. 12 Even if they raise children, I will bereave them; yes, woe to them when I depart from them! 13 I have seen Ephraim planted like Tyre in a pleasant place, but Ephraim will bring forth his children to the slayer.

Barrenness and Rejection

14 Give them, O the LORD—what will You give? Give them a miscarrying womb and breasts that give no milk. 15 All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I hated them. Because of the evil of their deeds, I will drive them out of My house. I will love them no more; all their princes are rebels.

16 Ephraim is stricken; their root is dried up; they will bear no fruit. Even if they give birth, I will put to death the beloved of their womb. 17 My God will reject them, because they have not obeyed Him; and they will be wanderers among the nations.
