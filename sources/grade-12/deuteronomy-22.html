Returning Lost Property and Aiding Neighbors

1 If you see your brother's ox or sheep going astray, do not ignore them; return them to your brother. 2 If your brother is not nearby and you do not know him, bring the animal home and keep it with you until he comes looking for it; then restore it to him. 3 Do the same with his donkey, his cloak, or anything else your brother loses that you find; you must not ignore it. 4 If you see your brother's donkey or ox fallen on the road, do not ignore it; help him lift it up.

Holiness in Daily Conduct

5 A woman must not wear a man's clothing, and a man must not put on a woman's garment, for everyone who does these things is detestable to the LORD your God. 6 If you come across a bird's nest along the road, in any tree or on the ground, with chicks or eggs, and the mother is sitting on the chicks or on the eggs, do not take the mother with the young. 7 You may take the young for yourself, but you must let the mother go, so that it may go well with you and you may live long. 8 When you build a new house, make a parapet around your roof, so that you do not bring bloodguilt on your house if anyone falls from it. 9 Do not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, or the whole harvest will be defiled—both the seed you sow and the vineyard's fruit. 10 Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together. 11 Do not wear clothing made of wool and linen woven together. 12 Make tassels on the four corners of the cloak you wear.

Marriage and the Proof of Virginity

13 If a man marries a woman and, after sleeping with her, comes to hate her, 14 and he accuses her of shameful conduct and gives her a bad name by saying, “I married this woman, and when I approached her, I did not find evidence of her virginity,” 15 then the girl's father and mother must take the evidence of her virginity and bring it to the elders at the city gate.

16 The girl's father must say to the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man as a wife, and he has come to hate her. 17 Now he has accused her of shameful conduct, saying, ‘I did not find your daughter a virgin.’ But here is the evidence of my daughter's virginity.” Then they must spread the cloth before the elders of the city.

18 The elders of that city must take the man and punish him. 19 They must fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give them to the girl's father, because he has given a bad name to a virgin in Israel. She must remain his wife, and he may never divorce her.

20 But if the charge is true and no evidence of virginity is found for the girl, 21 they must bring her to the door of her father's house, and the men of her city must stone her to death, because she committed a disgraceful act in Israel by prostituting herself in her father's house. So you must purge the evil from your midst.

Sexual Offenses and Their Penalties

22 If a man is found lying with a woman who is married to another man, both of them must die—the man who lay with the woman and the woman. So you must purge the evil from Israel.

23 If a virgin is betrothed to a man and another man meets her in the city and sleeps with her, 24 you must bring both of them to the gate of that city and stone them to death—the girl because she did not cry out for help though she was in the city, and the man because he violated his neighbor's wife. So you must purge the evil from your midst.

25 But if a man finds the betrothed girl in the open country and seizes her and sleeps with her, only the man who lay with her must die. 26 Do nothing to the girl; she has committed no sin deserving death, for this case is like that of a man who attacks his neighbor and murders him. 27 For the man found her in the open country, and although the betrothed girl cried out, there was no one to save her.

28 If a man finds a virgin who is not betrothed and seizes her and sleeps with her, and they are discovered, 29 the man who lay with her must give the girl's father fifty shekels of silver, and she must become his wife, because he has humiliated her. He may never divorce her.

30 A man must not take his father's wife or uncover his father's nakedness.
