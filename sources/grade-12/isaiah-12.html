A Song of Thanksgiving for Salvation

1 In that day you will say, ‘The LORD, I will praise You, for though You were angry with me, Your anger has turned away, and You comfort me.’ 2 Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid. For Yah, the LORD, is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation. 3 With joy you will draw water from the springs of salvation.

Praise Proclaimed Among the Nations

4 And in that day you will say, ‘Give thanks to the LORD; call on His name; make known His deeds among the peoples; proclaim that His name is exalted.’ 5 Sing to the LORD, for He has done glorious things; let this be known throughout the earth. 6 Shout aloud and sing for joy, O inhabitant of Zion, for great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel.
