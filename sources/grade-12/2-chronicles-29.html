Hezekiah’s Righteous Reign Begins

1 Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah. 2 He acted uprightly in the sight of the the LORD, following the example of his father David in every respect. 3 During the first month of the first year of his reign, he threw open the doors of the the LORD’s house and restored them. 4 He summoned the priests and the Levites and assembled them in the eastern square.

5 “Listen to me, Levites,” he said. “Consecrate yourselves at once, and consecrate the house of the the LORD, the God of your fathers. Purge every impurity from the sanctuary. 6 For our fathers acted treacherously and did evil in the sight of the the LORD our God. They abandoned Him, averted their faces from the dwelling place of the the LORD, and turned their backs upon Him. 7 They also closed the doors of the portico and extinguished the lamps; they offered neither incense nor burnt offerings in the Holy Place to the God of Israel. 8 Therefore the wrath of the the LORD has fallen upon Judah and Jerusalem, and He has made them an object of terror, horror, and derision, as you can plainly see. 9 Our fathers have fallen by the sword, while our sons, daughters, and wives have been carried into captivity. 10 Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with the the LORD, the God of Israel, so that His fierce anger may turn away from us. 11 My sons, do not now be negligent, for the the LORD has chosen you to stand before Him, to serve Him, to minister before Him, and to burn incense.”

The Levites Purify the Temple

12 Then the Levites rose to the task: from the Kohathites, Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah; from the Merarites, Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehalelel; from the Gershonites, Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah; 13 from the Asaphites, Zechariah and Mattaniah; from the Hemanites, Jehiel and Shimei; and from the Jeduthunites, Shemaiah and Uzziel. 14 They assembled their brothers, consecrated themselves, and entered to cleanse the house of the the LORD, in accordance with the king’s command and the words of the the LORD. 15 They began the consecration on the first day of the first month; by the eighth day they had reached the vestibule of the the LORD, and in eight more days they had consecrated the the LORD’s house, finishing on the sixteenth day of the first month. 16 Then the priests entered the inner part of the the LORD’s house to purify it. They removed into the courtyard of the the LORD’s house every unclean thing they discovered in the temple, and the Levites bore them away to the Kidron Valley.
