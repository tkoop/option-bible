Light Dawns on a People in Darkness

1 Nevertheless, those who were in anguish will no longer know gloom: in former times He humbled the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the latter time He will bring honor to the road by the sea, beyond the Jordan, to Galilee of the nations. 2 The people walking in darkness have seen a great light; upon those dwelling in the land overshadowed by death, a light has dawned.

3 You have expanded the nation and heightened its joy; they rejoice before You as people rejoice at the harvest, and as men exult when dividing the plunder. 4 For as in the day of Midian’s defeat, You have shattered the yoke that burdened them, the bar laid across their shoulders, and the rod of their oppressor. 5 Every trampling boot of battle and every garment stained with blood will be fuel for the flames.

The Child Who Will Govern in Peace

6 For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given, and the government will rest upon His shoulders; and His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. 7 Of the increase of His government and of peace there will be no end. He will reign upon David’s throne and over His kingdom, to establish it and to uphold it with justice and righteousness from that time forth and forevermore. The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this.

The LORD’s Judgment on Arrogant Israel

8 The Lord has sent a message against Jacob, and it has fallen upon Israel. 9 All the people will know it—Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria—who speak with pride and arrogance of heart, saying: 10 “The bricks have fallen, but we will rebuild with dressed stones; the sycamores have been cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.”

11 Therefore the LORD has raised up Rezin’s foes against him and stirred up his enemies. 12 Arameans from the east and Philistines from the west have devoured Israel ravenously. Despite all this, His anger is not turned away, and His hand is still upraised.

13 Yet the people have not returned to Him who struck them, nor have they sought the LORD of Hosts. 14 So the LORD will cut off from Israel both head and tail, palm branch and reed, in a single day. 15 The elder and honorable man is the head, and the prophet who teaches lies is the tail.

16 For those who guide this people mislead them, and those who are guided are swallowed up. 17 Therefore the Lord does not rejoice in their young men, and He shows no compassion to their fatherless and widows; for every one of them is godless and wicked, and every mouth speaks folly. Despite all this, His anger is not turned away, and His hand is still upraised.

18 For wickedness burns like a fire that consumes thorns and briers; it sets the thickets of the forest ablaze, so that it rolls upward in a column of smoke. 19 By the wrath of the LORD of Hosts the land is scorched, and the people become fuel for the fire; no one spares his brother. 20 They carve meat on the right but remain hungry; they eat on the left but are not satisfied. Each preys upon his own kin. 21 Manasseh devours Ephraim, and Ephraim devours Manasseh; together they turn against Judah. Despite all this, His anger is not turned away, and His hand is still upraised.
