Judgment on Moab

1 This is what the LORD says: “For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not revoke its punishment, because he burned the bones of the king of Edom to lime. 2 So I will send fire against Moab, and it will consume the citadels of Kerioth. Moab will perish amid uproar, amid shouting, and amid the sound of the trumpet. 3 I will cut off the judge from its midst and slay all its princes along with him,” declares the LORD.

Judgment on Judah

4 This is what the LORD says: “For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not revoke its punishment, because they rejected the law of the LORD and have not kept His statutes, but their false gods led them astray—the false gods their fathers followed. 5 So I will send fire against Judah, and it will consume the palaces of Jerusalem,” declares the LORD.

Judgment on Israel

6 This is what the LORD says: “For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not revoke its punishment, because they sell the righteous for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals. 7 They trample the heads of the poor into the dust of the earth and deny justice to the oppressed. A man and his father go in to the same young woman, thereby profaning My holy name. 8 They lie down beside every altar on garments taken in pledge, and in the house of their God they drink the wine of those who have been fined.

9 “Yet it was I who destroyed the Amorite before them, whose height was like that of cedars and who was as strong as oaks. I destroyed his fruit above and his roots below. 10 It was I who brought you up from the land of Egypt and led you forty years in the wilderness, so that you might possess the land of the Amorite. 11 I raised up prophets from among your sons and Nazirites from among your young men. Is this not so, O children of Israel?” declares the LORD. 12 “But you made the Nazirites drink wine and commanded the prophets, ‘You shall not prophesy.’”

13 “Behold, I will crush you in your place, as a cart is crushed when loaded with sheaves. 14 The swift will have no escape; the strong will not be able to use his strength, and the warrior will not save his life. 15 The archer will not hold his ground, the swift runner will not escape, and the horseman will not save his life. 16 Even the bravest among the mighty will flee naked on that day,” declares the LORD.
