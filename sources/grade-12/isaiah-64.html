A Plea for Divine Intervention

1 Oh, that You would tear open the heavens and come down, that the mountains would tremble before You— 2 as fire kindles brushwood and fire makes water boil—to make Your name known to Your adversaries, so that the nations would tremble before You! 3 When You did awesome things we did not expect, You came down, and the mountains trembled before You. 4 From ancient times no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides You, who acts on behalf of those who wait for Him.

Confession of Sin and Need for Salvation

5 You meet with favor those who joyfully practice righteousness and remember Your ways. Indeed, You were angry, for we sinned; we have long continued in these ways, and we need to be saved. 6 All of us have become like one who is unclean, and all our righteous deeds are like filthy garments; we all wither like a leaf, and our iniquities carry us away like the wind. 7 No one calls on Your name or rouses himself to take hold of You, for You have hidden Your face from us and given us over to our iniquities.

8 But now, the LORD, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You are our potter; we are all the work of Your hand. 9 Do not be angry beyond measure, the LORD; do not remember our iniquity forever. Look, please, and see that we are all Your people.

Lament over the Ruined Holy Places

10 Your holy cities have become a wilderness; Zion has become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation. 11 Our holy and beautiful temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned with fire, and all our pleasant places have become ruins. 12 After all this, the LORD, will You restrain Yourself? Will You keep silent and punish us beyond measure?
