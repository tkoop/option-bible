Paul Recalls His Ministry in Thessalonica

1 For you yourselves know, brothers, that our coming to you was not in vain. 2 As you know, we had already suffered and been shamefully treated at Philippi; yet, amid great conflict, we were bold in our God to speak to you the gospel of God.

3 For our exhortation does not spring from error, impurity, or deceit. 4 Rather, as those approved by God to be entrusted with the gospel, we speak not to please people but God, who examines our hearts. 5 For, as you know, we never resorted to flattery or employed a pretext for greed—God is our witness. 6 Nor did we seek glory from people, whether from you or from anyone else, although, as apostles of Christ, we could have been a burden.

7 Instead, we were gentle among you, like a nursing mother tenderly caring for her own children. 8 So, affectionately longing for you, we were delighted to share with you not only the gospel of God but our very lives, because you had become dear to us. 9 For you remember, brothers, our labor and hardship: we worked night and day so as not to burden any of you, while we proclaimed to you the gospel of God.

10 You are witnesses, as is God, of how devoutly, justly, and blamelessly we conducted ourselves among you who believe. 11 For you know how we dealt with each of you as a father deals with his own children— 12 exhorting, encouraging, and urging you to walk in a manner worthy of God, who calls you into His own kingdom and glory.

The Thessalonians Embrace God’s Word

13 And we also thank God without ceasing, because when you received the word of God from us, you accepted it not as the word of men but as it truly is—the word of God, which is also at work in you who believe. 14 For you, brothers, became imitators of the churches of God in Christ Jesus that are in Judea; for you suffered from your own countrymen the same things they suffered from the Jews, 15 who killed both the Lord Jesus and the prophets, drove us out, displease God, and are hostile to all people, 16 hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles so that they might be saved. Thus they always fill up the full measure of their sins, but wrath has come upon them to the uttermost.

Paul’s Longing to Return

17 But we, brothers, though torn away from you for a short time—in person, not in heart—were all the more eager, with great longing, to see you face to face. 18 For we wanted to come to you—I, Paul, tried more than once—but Satan hindered us. 19 For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting? Is it not you, in the presence of our Lord Jesus at His coming? 20 For you are our glory and joy.
