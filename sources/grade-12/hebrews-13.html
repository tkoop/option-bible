Final Exhortations for Christian Living

1 Let brotherly love continue. 2 Never neglect hospitality, for through it some have entertained angels unaware. 3 Remember those in prison as if you were bound with them, and those who suffer mistreatment as if you yourselves were suffering in your own bodies.

4 Let marriage be held in honor by all, and let the marriage bed remain undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.

5 Keep your conduct free from the love of money, and be content with what you have, for He has said, "I will never leave you, nor will I ever forsake you."

6 Hence we may say with confidence, "The Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?"

Christ’s Unchanging Truth

7 Remember your leaders, those who proclaimed God's word to you; and, weighing the outcome of their conduct, imitate their faith. 8 Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. 9 Do not be swept away by all kinds of strange teachings, for it is admirable for the heart to be established by grace rather than by foods, which have never benefited those preoccupied with them.

The Altar Outside the Camp

10 We possess an altar from which those who minister at the tabernacle have no authority to eat. 11 For when the high priest brings the blood of animals into the holy places as a sin offering, their bodies are consumed by fire outside the camp. 12 Consequently, Jesus suffered outside the gate, so that He might sanctify the people through His own blood. 13 Let us therefore go forth to Him outside the camp, bearing His reproach. 14 For we have no enduring city here; rather, we seek the city that is to come.

15 Through Him, therefore, let us offer up to God continually a sacrifice of praise—that is, the fruit of lips that acknowledge His name. 16 And do not forget to do good and to share with others, for God is well pleased with such sacrifices.

Closing Exhortations and Benediction

17 Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they keep watch over your souls as those who will give an account; do this so that their work may be a joy and not a burden of groaning, for that would be of no advantage to you.

18 Pray for us, for we are persuaded that we maintain a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves honorably in everything. 19 I urge you even more earnestly to do this, so that I may be restored to you sooner.

20 Now may the God of peace—who, through the blood of the eternal covenant, brought back from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great Shepherd of the sheep—21 equip you with every good thing to accomplish His will, and may He accomplish in us what is pleasing in His sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.

22 I exhort you, brothers, to endure patiently this word of exhortation, for I have written to you concisely. 23 Know that our brother Timothy has been released; if he arrives soon, I will come with him to see you. 24 Greet all your leaders and all the saints. Those in Italy send their greetings to you. 25 Grace be with all of you.
