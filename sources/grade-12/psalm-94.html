The LORD, God of Vengeance and Refuge

1 The LORD, God of vengeance, God of vengeance, shine forth! 2 Rise up, O Judge of the earth; repay to the proud what they deserve.

3 How long, O the LORD, will the wicked—how long will the wicked exult? 4 They pour out arrogant words; all the evildoers boast. 5 They crush Your people, O the LORD, and afflict Your heritage. 6 They slay the widow and the sojourner, and murder the fatherless.

7 And they say, "The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob does not perceive."

8 Understand, you senseless among the people! And you fools, when will you be wise? 9 He who planted the ear, does He not hear? He who formed the eye, does He not see? 10 He who disciplines the nations, does He not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge— 11 the LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath.

12 Blessed is the man whom You discipline, O the LORD, and whom You teach out of Your law, 13 to give him rest from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked. 14 For the LORD will not forsake His people; He will not abandon His heritage. 15 For justice will return to righteousness, and all the upright in heart will follow it.

16 Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? Who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity? 17 If the LORD had not been my help, my soul would soon have dwelt in the land of silence. 18 If I say, "My foot slips," Your steadfast love, O the LORD, holds me up. 19 When the cares of my heart are many, Your consolations cheer my soul.

20 Shall the throne of iniquity, which devises evil by statute, have fellowship with You? 21 They band together against the life of the righteous and condemn the innocent to death. 22 But the LORD has been my stronghold, and my God the rock of my refuge. 23 He will bring back on them their own iniquity and wipe them out for their wickedness; the LORD our God will wipe them out.
