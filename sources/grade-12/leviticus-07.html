The Law of the Guilt Offering

1Now this is the law of the guilt offering, which is most holy: 2the guilt offering must be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, and its blood must be sprinkled on all sides of the altar. 3All its fat is to be offered—the fat tail and the fat that covers the entrails— 4along with both kidneys and the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys. 5The priest shall burn them on the altar as an offering made by fire to the the LORD; it is a guilt offering. 6Every male among the priests may eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.

7The guilt offering is like the sin offering; one law applies to both. The priest who makes atonement with it shall have it. 8As for the priest who presents a man's burnt offering, the hide of that burnt offering belongs to him. 9Likewise, every grain offering baked in an oven or prepared in a pan or on a griddle belongs to the priest who presents it. 10And every grain offering, whether dry or mixed with oil, belongs equally to all the sons of Aaron.

The Law of the Peace Offering

11Now this is the law of the peace offering that one may present to the the LORD: 12If he offers it in thanksgiving, then along with the sacrifice of thanksgiving he shall offer unleavened cakes mixed with oil, unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mixed with oil. 13Along with his peace offering of thanksgiving, he is to present an offering with cakes of leavened bread. 14From the cakes he shall present one portion of each offering as a contribution to the the LORD. It belongs to the priest who sprinkles the blood of the peace offerings.

15The meat of the sacrifice of his peace offering of thanksgiving must be eaten on the day he offers it; none of it may be left until morning. 16If, however, the sacrifice he offers is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he presents his sacrifice, but what is left over may be eaten on the next day. 17But any meat of the sacrifice remaining until the third day must be burned up. 18If any of the meat of his peace offering is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to the one who presented it; it shall be an abomination, and the one who eats it shall bear the guilt of his sin.

19Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten; it must be burned up. As for the meat, anyone who is clean may eat it, 20but if anyone who is unclean eats meat from the peace offering that belongs to the the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people. 21If anyone touches anything unclean—whether human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean, detestable thing—and then eats meat from the peace offering that belongs to the the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people.

Prohibitions Against Eating Fat and Blood

22Then the the LORD said to Moses, 23"Speak to the Israelites and say: 'You must not eat any fat of an ox, a sheep, or a goat. 24The fat of an animal found dead or torn by wild beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must not eat it. 25If anyone eats the fat of an animal from which an offering made by fire is presented to the the LORD, the one who eats it must be cut off from his people. 26You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal in any of your dwellings. 27If anyone eats blood, that person must be cut off from his people.'"

Portions for the Priests

28Then the the LORD said to Moses, 29"Speak to the Israelites and say: 'Anyone who presents a peace offering to the the LORD must bring to the the LORD his offering from it. 30With his own hands he is to bring the offerings made by fire to the the LORD; he shall bring the fat with the breast, to wave the breast as a wave offering before the the LORD. 31The priest is to burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons. 32And you are to give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offerings. 33The son of Aaron who presents the blood and fat of the peace offering shall have the right thigh as his portion. 34For I have taken from the Israelites the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as a perpetual portion from the Israelites.'"

35This is the portion of the offerings made by fire to the the LORD for Aaron and his sons on the day they were presented to serve as priests to the the LORD. 36On the day they were anointed, the the LORD commanded that this be given them by the Israelites. It is a permanent statute for the generations to come. 37This is the law of the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering, and the peace offering, 38which the the LORD commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day He commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.
