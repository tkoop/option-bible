The LORD Speaks to Moses

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 “Say to the Israelites: Any Israelite or any foreigner residing in Israel who gives any of his children to Molech must be put to death. The people of the land are to stone him. 3 I will oppose that man and remove him from his people, because he has given his children to Molech, defiling My sanctuary and profaning My holy name. 4 If the people of the land deliberately overlook that man when he gives his children to Molech and do not put him to death, 5 then I will oppose that man and his family. I will remove him from his people, along with all who follow him in prostituting themselves to Molech. 6 I will also oppose anyone who turns to mediums or spiritists and prostitutes himself after them, and I will remove him from his people.”

A Call to Holiness

7 “Consecrate yourselves and be holy, for I am the LORD your God. 8 Keep My statutes and practice them; I am the LORD, who sanctifies you.”

Penalties for Immorality and Other Sins

9 “If anyone curses his father or mother, he must be put to death. He has cursed his father or mother; he is responsible for his own death. 10 If a man commits adultery with another man's wife—with his neighbor's wife—both the adulterer and the adulteress must be put to death. 11 If a man has sexual relations with his father's wife, he has dishonored his father. Both of them must be put to death; they are responsible for their own deaths. 12 If a man has sexual relations with his daughter-in-law, both of them must be put to death. They have committed a perversion; they are responsible for their own deaths. 13 If a man has sexual relations with another man as with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination. They must be put to death; they are responsible for their own deaths.”

14 “If a man marries a woman and her mother, it is depravity. He and both women must be burned with fire, so that no depravity will exist among you. 15 If a man has sexual relations with an animal, he must be put to death, and you must kill the animal. 16 If a woman approaches an animal to mate with it, you must kill the woman and the animal. They must be put to death; they are responsible for their own deaths. 17 If a man marries his sister, whether his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and has sexual relations with her, it is a disgrace. They must be publicly removed from their people. He has violated his sister; he will bear his guilt. 18 If a man has sexual relations with a woman during her menstrual period, he has exposed her flow and she has exposed the source of her blood. Both of them must be removed from their people.”

19 “You must not have sexual relations with your mother's sister or your father's sister, for that exposes the nakedness of a close relative. They will bear their guilt. 20 If a man has sexual relations with his aunt, he has dishonored his uncle. They will bear their sin; they will die childless. 21 If a man marries his brother's wife, it is impurity. He has dishonored his brother; they will be childless.”

Holiness and Separation from the Nations

22 “You must therefore keep all My statutes and all My judgments and do them, so that the land I am bringing you into will not expel you. 23 You must not follow the customs of the nation I am driving out before you, because they did all these things, and I abhorred them. 24 I have told you, ‘You will inherit their land, and I will give it to you to possess—a land flowing with milk and honey.’ I am the LORD your God, who has set you apart from the nations. 25 You must therefore distinguish between clean and unclean animals and between unclean and clean birds. Do not defile yourselves by any animal or bird or by anything that creeps on the ground, which I have set apart as unclean for you. 26 You must be holy to Me, for I, the LORD, am holy, and I have separated you from the nations to be My own. 27 Any man or woman who is a medium or spiritist must be put to death. They must be stoned; they are responsible for their own deaths.”
