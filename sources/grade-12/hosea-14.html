Israel's Summons to Repentance

1 Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God, for you have stumbled because of your iniquity.

2 Take words with you and return to the LORD; say to Him, “Take away all iniquity and receive what is good, and we will offer the fruit of our lips. 3 Assyria will not save us; we will not rely on horses for deliverance; and we will no longer call the work of our hands our god, for in you the orphan finds mercy.”

God's Promise to Heal and Restore

4 “I will heal their apostasy; I will love them freely, for My anger has turned away from them. 5 I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like the lily and send down his roots like the cedars of Lebanon.

6 “His shoots will spread, and his splendor will be like the olive tree, with a fragrance like the cedars of Lebanon. 7 They will return and dwell in his shade; they will revive the grain and blossom like the vine. His renown will be like the wine of Lebanon.”

Ephraim, Wisdom, and the Ways of God

8 Ephraim, what have I to do any more with idols? I have answered him and will watch over him. I am like a green cypress; your fruit is found in Me.

9 Who is wise, that he may understand these things? Who is discerning, that he may know them? For the ways of the LORD are right, and the righteous will walk in them, but transgressors will stumble in them.
