Athaliah’s Massacre and the Concealment of Joash

1 When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, she rose up and destroyed the whole royal line. 2 But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Jehoram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash, the son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king’s sons who were being put to death. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom, concealing him from Athaliah so that he was not killed. 3 So Joash remained hidden with his nurse in the house of the LORD for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

Jehoiada’s Covenant and the King’s Restoration Plan

4 In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent for the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, and the guards, and had them brought to him into the house of the LORD, and in the house of the LORD he made a covenant with them, put them under oath, and showed them the king’s son.

5 He commanded them, “This is what you are to do: A third of you who come on duty on the Sabbath are to guard the king’s palace. 6 A third are to be at the Sur gate, and a third at the gate behind the guards. You are to guard the temple. 7 The two divisions that would normally go off duty on the Sabbath are to guard the house of the LORD for the king. 8 You must surround the king on every side, each man with his weapon in hand. Anyone who breaches the ranks is to be put to death. Stay with the king wherever he goes, when he leaves and when he returns.”

9 So the commanders of hundreds did everything that Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each man took his men—those coming on duty on the Sabbath and those going off duty—and came to Jehoiada the priest. 10 Then the priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears and shields that had belonged to King David and were kept in the house of the LORD. 11 The guards stood with weapons in hand around the king, from the south side of the house of the LORD to the north side, near the altar and the house of the LORD.

The Coronation of Joash and the Fall of Athaliah

12 Then Jehoiada brought out the king’s son, placed the crown on him, gave him the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. They anointed him, and the people clapped their hands and shouted, “Long live the king!”

13 When Athaliah heard the noise of the guards and the people, she went into the house of the LORD to see the people. 14 She looked and saw the king standing by the pillar, according to custom, with the officers and trumpeters beside the king. All the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and cried out, “Treason! Treason!”

15 Jehoiada the priest ordered the commanders of hundreds who were in charge of the army, “Bring her out between the ranks, and put to death anyone who follows her.” For the priest had said, “She must not be put to death in the house of the LORD.”

16 So they seized her, and she went by way of the horse gate to the king’s palace, where she was put to death.

Renewal of the Covenant and Reform of Worship

17 Then Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they would be the LORD’s people, and also between the king and the people. 18 All the people of the land went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and idols to pieces, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, before the altars. Then Jehoiada the priest posted guards over the house of the LORD. 19 He took with him the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the guards, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD and entered the king’s palace by way of the gate of the guards. The king sat on the royal throne. 20 All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. They put Athaliah to death with the sword at the king’s palace. 21 Joash was seven years old when he became king.
