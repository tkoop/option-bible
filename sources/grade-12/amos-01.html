The Words of Amos

1 The words of Amos, who was among the shepherds of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel two years before the earthquake, in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel.

The LORD Roars from Zion

2 The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem; the pastures of the shepherds mourn, and the summit of Carmel withers.

Judgments on Damascus, Gaza, and Tyre

3 Thus says the LORD: "For their many transgressions, I will not revoke the punishment, because they have brutally crushed Gilead. 4 I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it will consume the palaces of Ben-hadad. 5 I will break the defenses of Damascus, cut off the inhabitants of the Valley of Aven, and remove the one who holds the scepter from Beth-eden; the people of Aram will go into exile to Kir," says the LORD.

6 Thus says the LORD: "For their many transgressions, I will not revoke the punishment, because they carried an entire population into exile to hand it over to Edom. 7 I will send fire upon the wall of Gaza, and it will consume its palaces. 8 I will cut off the inhabitants of Ashdod and the one who holds the scepter from Ashkelon; I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines will perish," declares the Lord, the LORD.

9 Thus says the LORD: "For their many transgressions, I will not revoke the punishment, because they delivered up an entire captive population to Edom and did not remember the covenant of brotherhood. 10 I will send fire upon the wall of Tyre, and it will consume its palaces."

Judgments on Edom and Ammon

11 Thus says the LORD: "For their many transgressions, I will not revoke the punishment, because he pursued his brother with the sword and showed no mercy; his anger raged continually, and his wrath he kept forever. 12 I will send fire upon Teman, and it will consume the palaces of Bozrah."

13 Thus says the LORD: "For their many transgressions, I will not revoke the punishment, because they ripped open pregnant women in Gilead to enlarge their territory. 14 I will set fire to the wall of Rabbah, and it will consume its palaces, amid shouting on the day of battle and amid a tempest on the day of the whirlwind. 15 Their king will go into exile, he and his princes together," says the LORD.
