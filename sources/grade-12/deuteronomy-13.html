Warning Against False Prophets and Dreamers

1 If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises among you and proclaims a sign or wonder to you, 2 and the sign or wonder he spoke to you comes to pass, and he says, “Let us follow other gods, whom you have not known, and let us serve them,” 3 you must not heed the words of that prophet or dreamer. For the the LORD your God is testing you to discover whether you love the the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul. 4 You are to follow the the LORD your God and fear Him. Keep His commandments and obey His voice, and you shall serve Him and cling to Him. 5 But that prophet or dreamer must be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against the the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery, to turn you from the way the the LORD your God commanded you to walk in. So you shall purge the evil from your midst.

Resisting Secret Enticement to Idolatry

6 If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or daughter, or the wife you embrace, or your closest friend secretly entices you, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods”—whom neither you nor your fathers have known— 7 the gods of the peoples around you, whether near to you or far from you, from one end of the earth to the other— 8 you must not yield to him or listen to him. Show him no pity, and do not spare him or shield him. 9 Instead, you must surely put him to death. Your hand must be first against him to put him to death, and then the hands of all the people. 10 Stone him to death, because he has tried to turn you away from the the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. 11 Then all Israel will hear and fear, and they will never again do such an evil thing among you.

The Destruction of an Idolatrous City

12 If you hear it said about one of the cities the the LORD your God is giving you to live in, 13 that wicked men have gone out from among you and led the inhabitants of their city astray, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods”—whom you have not known— 14 then you must investigate, probe, and inquire thoroughly. If it is true and confirmed that such an abomination has been committed among you, 15 you must surely put to the sword all who live in that city. Devote it and everything in it, including its livestock, to complete destruction. 16 And you are to gather all its plunder into the middle of its square and burn the city and all its plunder as a whole burnt offering to the the LORD your God. It must remain a heap of ruins forever; it must never be rebuilt. 17 Nothing devoted to destruction may cling to your hand, so that the the LORD will turn from His fierce anger, give you mercy, and show you compassion, and multiply you as He swore to your fathers. 18 because you obey the voice of the the LORD your God by keeping all His commandments that I am giving you today, and by doing what is right in the eyes of the the LORD your God.
