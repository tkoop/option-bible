Reuben and Gad Request Land East of the Jordan

1 The Reubenites and Gadites owned an exceptionally large number of livestock, and when they saw that the territory of Jazer and Gilead was ideally suited to pastoral use, 2 they came to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the congregation and said, 3 “Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon— 4 the land that the LORD subdued before the congregation of Israel—is pastoral country, and your servants possess livestock. 5 If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be granted to your servants as a possession; do not require us to cross the Jordan.”

Moses Rebukes Their Request

6 Moses said to the Gadites and Reubenites, “Shall your brothers march to war while you remain here? 7 Why would you sap the courage of the Israelites and deter them from crossing into the land the LORD has given them? 8 This is precisely what your fathers did when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to inspect the land. 9 They ascended to the Valley of Eshcol, surveyed the land, and discouraged the Israelites from entering the land the LORD had given them. 10 So the LORD’s anger was kindled that day, and He swore that not one of the men twenty years old or more who came up from Egypt would see the land He had sworn to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, because they had not followed Him wholeheartedly— 11 except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, for they followed the LORD wholeheartedly. 12 Thus the LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and He made them wander in the wilderness for forty years, until the whole generation of those who had committed evil in the sight of the LORD had perished. 14 And now you, a brood of sinful men, have arisen in your fathers’ stead to intensify still further the LORD’s fierce anger against Israel. 15 If you turn away from following Him, He will once again abandon this people in the wilderness, and you will bring ruin upon all of them.”

The Tribes Pledge to Fight for Canaan

16 They came closer and said, “We will build sheepfolds here for our livestock and cities for our children. 17 But we will arm ourselves and march as the vanguard before the Israelites until we have brought them to their place. Our children will dwell in fortified cities because of the inhabitants of the land. 18 We will not return to our homes until every Israelite has received his inheritance. 19 We will not claim an inheritance with them beyond the Jordan, because our inheritance has fallen to us on this side, east of the Jordan.”

20 Moses said to them, “If you do this—if you arm yourselves before the LORD for battle, 21 and every armed man among you crosses the Jordan before the LORD until He has driven out His enemies before Him, 22 and the land is subdued before the LORD—then afterward you may return, and you will be absolved of obligation to the LORD and to Israel, and this land will be your possession before the LORD. 23 But if you do not do this, you will have sinned against the LORD, and be assured that your sin will overtake you. 24 Build cities for your little ones and pens for your flocks, but do what you have promised.”

25 The Gadites and Reubenites said to Moses, “Your servants will do as my lord commands. 26 Our children, wives, livestock, and all our animals will remain here in the cities of Gilead, 27 while your servants, all armed for war, cross over before the LORD to battle, as my lord says.”

28 Moses commanded them—to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelites—saying, 29 “If the Gadites and Reubenites cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead as a possession. 30 But if they do not cross over with you armed, they shall receive their inheritance among you in the land of Canaan.”

31 The Gadites and Reubenites answered, “We will do what the LORD has spoken. 32 We will cross over into Canaan armed before the LORD, and our inheritance will remain with us east of the Jordan.”

The Land East of the Jordan Is Allotted

33 So Moses gave to the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan—the land with its cities and the territories surrounding them. 34 The Gadites built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 35 Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 36 Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran as fortified cities, and they built sheepfolds. 37 The Reubenites built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim, 38 Nebo, Baal-meon (whose name they changed), and Sibmah, and they renamed the cities they built. 39 The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it, and drove out the Amorites who were there. 40 So Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he settled there. 41 Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their villages and called them Havvoth-jair. 42 Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and called it Nobah after his own name.
