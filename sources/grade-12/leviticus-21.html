Priestly Holiness in Mourning and Marriage

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them: 'No one shall defile himself for a dead person among his people, 2 except for his close relative—his mother, father, son, daughter, or brother— 3 or his unmarried sister who is near to him, since she has had no husband; for her he may defile himself. 4 He must not defile himself for a leader among his people, thereby profaning himself. 5 They must not shave their heads or trim the edges of their beards or make cuts in their flesh. 6 They must be holy to their God and not profane the name of their God. For they present the offerings made by fire to the LORD, the food of their God. So they must be holy.

"'They must not marry a woman defiled by prostitution, nor a woman divorced from her husband, for the priest is holy to his God. 8 You shall consecrate him, for he presents the food of your God. He shall be holy to you, because I the LORD am holy who sanctifies you. 9 If a priest's daughter defiles herself by prostituting herself, she defiles her father; she must be burned in the fire.

The High Priest's Special Restrictions

"'The high priest, the one among his brothers on whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to wear the garments, must not let his hair hang loose or tear his garments. 11 He must not go near any dead body or defile himself, even for his father or mother. 12 He must not leave the sanctuary or desecrate the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am the LORD.

"'He must marry a virgin. 14 He must not marry a widow, a divorced woman, or a woman defiled by prostitution. He must marry a virgin from his own people, 15 so that he does not defile his offspring among his people. For I am the LORD who sanctifies him.'"

Disqualifications for Priestly Service

16 Then the LORD said to Moses, 17 "Speak to Aaron and tell him: 'No man among your descendants for all generations who has a physical defect may approach to offer the food of his God. 18 No man who has a defect may approach—no blind man, no lame man, no man with a disfigured face, and no man with a deformed limb.

"' 19 No man with a broken foot or hand, 20 no hunchback, no dwarf, no man with an eye defect, no man with a scab or rash, and no man with crushed testicles. 21 No descendant of Aaron the priest who has a defect may approach to present the offerings made by fire to the LORD. He has a defect; he must not approach to offer the food of his God.

"'22 He may eat the food of his God, both the most holy and the holy. 23 But he must not go near the curtain or approach the altar, because he has a defect, so that he does not desecrate My sanctuary. For I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'

24 So Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites.
