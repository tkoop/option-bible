The Families Returning with Ezra

1 These are the heads of their ancestral houses and the genealogical records of those who went up with me from Babylon during the reign of King Artaxerxes. 2 From the sons of Phinehas: Gershom. From the sons of Ithamar: Daniel. From the sons of David: Hattush. 3 From the sons of Shecaniah, namely from the sons of Parosh: Zechariah, with whom one hundred fifty men were registered. 4 From the sons of Pahath-moab: Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, with two hundred men. 5 From the sons of Shecaniah son of Jahaziel: three hundred men. 6 From the sons of Adin: Ebed son of Jonathan, with fifty men. 7 From the sons of Elam: Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, with seventy men. 8 From the sons of Shephatiah: Zebadiah son of Michael, with eighty men. 9 From the sons of Joab: Obadiah son of Jehiel, with two hundred eighteen men. 10 From the sons of Shelomith son of Josiphiah: one hundred sixty men. 11 From the sons of Bebai: Zechariah son of Bebai, with twenty-eight men. 12 From the sons of Azgad: Johanan son of Hakkatan, with one hundred ten men. 13 From the last sons of Adonikam, whose names were Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah, with sixty men. 14 From the sons of Bigvai: Uthai and Zabbud, with seventy men.

Ezra Secures Levites and Temple Servants

15 I assembled them at the river that flows to Ahava, and we encamped there for three days. When I examined the people and the priests, I found that no Levites were among them. 16 So I sent for the leaders Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, and for Joiarib and Elnathan, men of discernment. 17 I dispatched them to Iddo, the chief at Casiphia, and I instructed them what to say to Iddo and his relatives, the temple servants at Casiphia, so that they might bring us ministers for the house of our God. 18 Because the good hand of our God was upon us, they brought us a man of understanding from the sons of Mahli son of Levi son of Israel—Sherebiah—together with his sons and brothers, eighteen in all. 19 They also brought Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the sons of Merari, along with his relatives and their sons, twenty in all. 20 From the temple servants, whom David and the officials had appointed to assist the Levites, came two hundred twenty, all designated by name.

Fasting and Prayer for the Journey

21 There, at the river Ahava, I proclaimed a fast, that we might humble ourselves before our God and seek from Him a straight way for ourselves, our children, and all our possessions. 22 For I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us from enemies on the road, because we had said to the king, "The hand of our God is for good upon all who seek Him, but His power and His wrath are against all who forsake Him." 23 So we fasted and petitioned our God concerning this, and He answered our plea.

The Treasure, the Journey, and the Worship in Jerusalem

24 Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests—Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers—and weighed out to them the silver, the gold, and the vessels, the offering that the king, his counselors, his officials, and all Israel present had contributed for the house of our God. 25 I weighed into their hands six hundred fifty talents of silver, silver articles weighing one hundred talents, and one hundred talents of gold, 26 along with twenty gold basins valued at a thousand darics and two vessels of fine bronze, precious as gold. 27 Then I said to them, "You are holy to the the LORD, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are a freewill offering to the the LORD, the God of your fathers. 28 Guard them carefully until you weigh them out in the storerooms of the house of the the LORD in Jerusalem, before the chief priests, the Levites, and the leaders of the fathers' houses of Israel." 29 So the priests and the Levites received the silver, the gold, and the vessels by weight, to bring them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.

30 Then we departed from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month, to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was upon us, and He delivered us from the hand of the enemy and from ambush along the way. 31 We came to Jerusalem and remained there three days. 32 On the fourth day, the silver, the gold, and the vessels were weighed in the house of our God into the hands of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest; with him were Eleazar son of Phinehas, and with them Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui, the Levites. 33 Everything was accounted for by number and by weight, and the total weight was recorded at that time. 34 The exiles who had returned from captivity offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering. All of this was a burnt offering to the the LORD. 35 They also delivered the king's decrees to the royal satraps and to the governors of the province beyond the River, and these officials supported the people and the house of God.
