The Birth of Isaac

1 The LORD attended to Sarah as He had promised, and the LORD accomplished for her what He had spoken. 2 Sarah conceived and bore a son to Abraham in his old age, at the appointed time God had fixed. 3 Abraham named his son Isaac, the child Sarah had borne to him. 4 When his son Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him, as God had commanded. 5 Abraham was one hundred years old when his son Isaac was born.

6 Sarah said, "God has given me laughter; everyone who hears of this will laugh with me." 7 She added, "Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet I have borne him a son in his old age."

Isaac Is Weaned, and Ishmael Mocks

8 The child grew and was weaned, and Abraham held a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned. 9 But Sarah saw the son whom Hagar the Egyptian had borne to Abraham mocking. 10 So she said to Abraham, "Drive out this slave woman and her son, for the son of this slave woman will not share the inheritance with my son Isaac."

11 The matter distressed Abraham deeply, because it concerned his son. 12 But God said to Abraham, "Do not be distressed about the boy and your maidservant. Listen to everything Sarah tells you, for through Isaac your offspring will be reckoned. 13 I will also make the son of the maidservant into a nation, because he too is your offspring."

Hagar and Ishmael in the Wilderness

14 Early the next morning Abraham took bread and a skin of water, gave them to Hagar, and laid them on her shoulder; then he sent her away with the boy. She departed and wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba. 15 When the water in the skin was exhausted, she put the boy under one of the bushes. 16 Then she went and sat down opposite him, about a bowshot away, for she said, "I cannot bear to watch the boy die." As she sat there, she lifted up her voice and wept.

17 God heard the boy crying, and the angel of God called to Hagar from heaven, "What troubles you, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has heard the boy's voice where he is. 18 Rise, lift the boy, and take him by the hand, for I will make him into a great nation." 19 Then God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water. She went and filled the skin with water and gave the boy a drink.

20 God was with the boy as he grew up. He lived in the wilderness and became an archer. 21 He settled in the wilderness of Paran, and his mother took a wife for him from Egypt.

A Covenant at Beersheba

22 At that time Abimelech and Phicol, commander of his army, said to Abraham, "God is with you in everything you do. 23 Now swear to me here by God that you will not deal falsely with me, my children, or my grandchildren, but that you will show me and the land where you reside the same kindness I have shown you."

24 Abraham said, "I swear it."

25 Then Abraham rebuked Abimelech concerning a well that Abimelech's servants had seized. 26 Abimelech replied, "I do not know who did this. You did not tell me, and I heard nothing about it until today."

27 Abraham took sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech, and the two of them made a covenant. 28 Abraham set apart seven ewe lambs from the flock. 29 Abimelech asked, "What is the meaning of these seven ewe lambs you have set apart?" 30 Abraham answered, "Accept these seven ewe lambs from my hand as witness that I dug this well." 31 Therefore that place was called Beersheba, because there the two of them swore an oath. 32 After they made the covenant at Beersheba, Abimelech and Phicol, commander of his army, returned to the land of the Philistines.

33 Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba, and there he called on the name of the LORD, the Eternal God. 34 Abraham stayed in the land of the Philistines for a long time.
