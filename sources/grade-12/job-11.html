Zophar Accuses Job of Empty Words

1 Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

2 "Should a torrent of words go unanswered, and should a man of many speeches be vindicated? 3 Should your idle talk silence men, and when you scoff, should no one put you to shame? 4 For you say, 'My teaching is pure, and I am clean in Your eyes.'

God's Wisdom Beyond Human Reach

5 "But oh, that God would speak and open His lips against you, 6 and disclose to you the secrets of wisdom—for true wisdom has a double measure. Know, then, that God exacts from you less than your guilt deserves. 7 Can you discover the deep things of God? Can you find the limit of the Almighty? 8 It is higher than the heavens—what can you do? Deeper than Sheol—what can you know? 9 Its measure is longer than the earth and broader than the sea. 10 If He passes through, imprisons, and summons to judgment, who can restrain Him? 11 For He knows deceitful men; when He sees iniquity, does He not take note? 12 An empty-headed man will never gain understanding, no more than a wild donkey can bear a human child.

A Call to Repentance and Restoration

13 "If you prepare your heart and spread out your hands to Him, 14 if you put away the iniquity in your hand and do not let wickedness dwell in your tents, 15 then you will lift up your face without blemish; you will stand firm and not fear. 16 You will forget your misery, remembering it only as water that has flowed past. 17 your life will shine brighter than noonday; even its darkness will be like the morning. 18 You will be secure, because there is hope; you will look about and lie down in safety. 19 You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid; many will seek your favor.

The Fate of the Wicked

20 "But the eyes of the wicked will fail, escape will elude them, and their hope will be a dying gasp."
