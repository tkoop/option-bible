Pursue Love and Spiritual Gifts

1 Pursue love, and be zealous for spiritual gifts, but above all that you may prophesy. 2 For the one who speaks in a tongue does not address human beings but God; no one comprehends him, yet in spirit he utters mysteries. 3 But the one who prophesies speaks to human beings for their edification, encouragement, and consolation. 4 The tongue-speaker edifies himself, whereas the prophet edifies the church. 5 I wish that all of you could speak in tongues, but I wish even more that you would prophesy; for the prophet is greater than the tongue-speaker unless the latter interprets, so that the church may be edified.

6 Now, brothers, if I were to come to you speaking in tongues, what benefit would I confer upon you unless I addressed you through revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching? 7 Even inanimate instruments that emit sound—whether flute or lyre—unless they articulate distinct notes, how will anyone recognize the melody being played? 8 And if a trumpet emits an ambiguous blast, who will prepare for battle? 9 So it is with you: unless you produce intelligible speech through the tongue, how will anyone understand what is being said? For you would be speaking into the void. 10 There are, doubtless, many varieties of languages in the world, and none lacks significance. 11 Yet if I am ignorant of a language's meaning, I shall be a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker a foreigner to me. 12 So also you, since you are zealous for spiritual gifts, strive to excel in edifying the church.

Prophecy, Tongues, and Edification

13 Therefore, the tongue-speaker should pray for the ability to interpret. 14 For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind remains unfruitful. 15 What then is to be done? I will pray with my spirit, and I will pray with my mind as well; I will sing with my spirit, and I will sing with my mind as well. 16 Otherwise, if you pronounce blessing in spirit, how can the one who occupies the position of the unlearned say "Amen" to your thanksgiving, since he does not understand what you are saying? 17 For you may indeed give thanks admirably, yet the other is not edified. 18 I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you. 19 Nevertheless, in the assembly I would rather articulate five words with my mind, so as to instruct others, than ten thousand words in a tongue.

20 Brothers, do not be childish in your reasoning; in malice be infants, but in reasoning be mature. 21 In the Law it is written: "By men of strange tongues and by the lips of foreigners I will speak to this people, and even then they will not listen to Me," says the Lord. 22 Consequently, tongues are a sign not for believers but for unbelievers, while prophecy is a sign not for unbelievers but for believers.

23 Therefore, if the entire church assembles and all speak in tongues, and outsiders or unbelievers enter, will they not conclude that you are deranged? 24 But if all prophesy, and an unbeliever or outsider enters, he is convicted by all and judged by all; 25 the hidden things of his heart are brought to light, and so, falling upon his face, he will worship God, proclaiming, "God is truly among you."

Orderly Worship in the Assembly

26 What then, brothers? Whenever you assemble, each one contributes a psalm, a teaching, a revelation, a tongue, or an interpretation. Let everything be done for edification. 27 If anyone speaks in a tongue, let there be two, or at most three, speaking in succession, and let one interpret. 28 But if no interpreter is present, let him remain silent in the assembly and speak to himself and to God. 29 Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others discern. 30 If a revelation is given to another who is seated, let the first speaker fall silent. 31 For you can all prophesy individually, so that all may learn and all be encouraged. 32 And the spirits of prophets are subject to prophets. 33 For God is not a God of disorder but of peace, as in all the churches of the saints.

34 Let the women keep silent in the churches, for they are not permitted to speak; rather, let them be in submission, as the Law also declares. 35 If they wish to learn anything, let them question their own husbands at home, for it is disgraceful for a woman to speak in the assembly. 36 Or did the word of God originate from you? Or has it reached you alone?

37 If anyone regards himself as a prophet or as spiritual, let him acknowledge that what I am writing to you is the Lord's command. 38 But if anyone is ignorant, he is not recognized. 39 Therefore, my brothers, be zealous for prophecy, and do not hinder speaking in tongues. 40 But let all things be done with propriety and in order.
