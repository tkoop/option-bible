Agur's Oracle and Confession of Ignorance

1 The words of Agur son of Jakeh—an oracle. The man declares, "I am weary, O God; I am weary, O God, and faint." 2 Surely I am more brutish than any man, and human understanding eludes me. 3 I have not learned wisdom, nor do I possess knowledge of the Holy One. 4 Who has ascended to heaven and descended? Who has gathered the wind into His hands? Who has bound the waters in His cloak? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is His name, and what is the name of His Son? Surely you know!

5 Every utterance of God is flawless; He is a shield to those who take refuge in Him. 6 Do not add to His words, lest He rebuke you and demonstrate that you are a liar.

Agur's Prayer for a Measured Life

7 Two things I ask of You; do not withhold them from me before I die: 8 Keep falsehood and deceit far from me. Grant me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the bread allotted to me. 9 Otherwise, I might have too much and deny You, saying, "Who is the LORD?" Or I might become poor and steal, thereby profaning the name of my God.

10 Do not slander a servant to his master, lest he curse you and you be held guilty.

Four Generations, Insatiable Things, and Mysterious Paths

11 There is a generation that curses its father and does not bless its mother. 12 There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes yet is not cleansed of its filth. 13 There is a generation whose eyes are so haughty, whose eyelids are raised high. 14 There is a generation whose teeth are swords and whose jaws are knives, devouring the poor from the earth and the needy from among men.

15 The leech has two daughters who cry, "Give! Give!" There are three things that are never satisfied—four that never say, "Enough!" 16 Sheol, the barren womb, land that is never satisfied with water, and fire that never says, "Enough!"

17 An eye that mocks a father and scorns a mother's discipline will be pecked out by the ravens of the valley and devoured by vultures.

18 There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot comprehend: 19 the path of an eagle through the sky, the path of a serpent upon a rock, the course of a ship through the sea, and the way of a man with a maiden. 20 This is the way of an adulterous woman: she eats, wipes her mouth, and says, "I have done nothing wrong."

Four Upheavals, Four Wise Creatures, Four Stately Walkers, and a Warning

21 Under three things the earth trembles; under four it cannot bear up: 22 a servant who becomes king, a fool who is filled with food, 23 an unloved woman who marries, and a maidservant who supplants her mistress.

24 Four things on earth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise: 25 the ants are not a powerful people, yet they store up their food in summer; 26 the rock badgers are not a mighty people, yet they make their dwellings in the cliffs; 27 the locusts have no king, yet they all march out in formation; 28 the lizard can be seized with the hands, yet it is found in the palaces of kings.

29 There are three things that are stately in their stride, four that are impressive in their gait: 30 a lion, mightiest among beasts, who does not retreat before anyone; 31 a strutting rooster, a goat, and a king with his army around him.

32 If you have acted foolishly by exalting yourself, or if you have devised evil, put your hand over your mouth. 33 For as churning milk produces butter, and wringing a nose produces blood, so stirring up anger produces strife.
