Indictment of Israel's Corrupt Leaders

1 I said, "Hear now, you leaders of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel: Is it not your obligation to know justice? 2 You hate what is good and love what is evil; you tear the skin from My people and the flesh from their bones. 3 You devour the flesh of My people, strip off their skin, break their bones, and spread them out like meat in a pot, like stew in a cauldron. 4 Then they will cry out to the LORD, but He will not answer them; at that time He will hide His face from them, because they have done evil deeds."

Judgment on False Prophets

5 Thus says The LORD concerning the prophets who lead My people astray, who bite with their teeth and cry, "Peace!" but declare holy war against anyone who does not feed them. 6 Therefore night will come upon you without visions, and darkness without divination; the sun will set on the prophets, and the day will grow dark over them. 7 The seers will be ashamed, the diviners confounded, and all of them will cover their mouths, for there is no answer from God.

Micah's Rebuke and the Ruin of Zion

8 But I am full of power by the Spirit of the LORD, and of justice and might, to declare to Jacob his transgression and to Israel his sin. 9 Hear this, you leaders of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, you who abhor justice and pervert every right. 10 You build Zion with blood and Jerusalem with injustice. 11 Her leaders judge for a bribe, her priests teach for a price, and her prophets divine for money; yet they lean on the LORD and say, "Is not the LORD among us? No calamity will overtake us." 12 Therefore, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the temple mount will become a forested hill.
