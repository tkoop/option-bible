David’s Pledge to Walk in Integrity

1 I will sing of steadfast love and justice; to You, the LORD, I will sing. 2 I will give heed to the way of the blameless; when will You come to me? I will walk within my house with integrity of heart. 3 I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. I hate the deeds of those who turn aside, and they shall not cling to me. 4 A perverse heart shall depart from me; I will know no evil.

Purging Wickedness from the Court

5 Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, I will silence; the one with haughty eyes and a proud heart, I will not tolerate. 6 My eyes are upon the faithful in the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way of integrity shall serve me. 7 No practitioner of deceit shall dwell in my house; no speaker of falsehoods shall stand in my presence. 8 Each morning I will silence all the wicked in the land, cutting off every evildoer from the city of the LORD.
