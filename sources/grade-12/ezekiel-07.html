1 The word of the the LORD came to me, saying,

The End Has Come upon the Land

2 “Son of man, this is what the Lord the LORD says to the land of Israel: The end! The end has come upon the four corners of the land. 3 The end is now upon you, and I will send My anger against you; I will judge you according to your ways and repay you for all your abominations. 4 My eye will not spare you, nor will I have pity; I will bring your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

An Unprecedented Disaster

5 “Thus says the Lord the LORD: Disaster! An unprecedented disaster! Behold, it is coming. 6 The end has come; the end has come; it has roused itself against you. Behold, it is coming. 7 Doom has come upon you, O inhabitant of the land. The time has come; the day is near—tumult rather than joyful shouting on the mountains.

8 “Now I will soon pour out My wrath upon you and spend My anger against you; I will judge you according to your ways and repay you for all your abominations. 9 My eye will not spare you, nor will I have pity; I will repay you according to your ways, while your abominations are in your midst. Then you will know that I, the LORD, am the One who strikes.

The Day of Reckoning

10 “Behold, the day! Behold, it comes! Doom has gone forth; the rod has blossomed; arrogance has sprouted. 11 Violence has risen into a rod of wickedness; none of them shall remain—neither their multitude nor their wealth—and there will be no lamentation among them.

12 “The time has come; the day has arrived. Let not the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn, for wrath is upon all their multitude. 13 For the seller shall not return to what he has sold, as long as they live; for the vision concerning all their multitude will not be revoked, and because of their iniquity no one among them will preserve his life. 14 They have blown the trumpet and prepared everything, but no one goes to battle, for My wrath is upon all their multitude.

15 “The sword is outside, and pestilence and famine are within; he who is in the field will die by the sword, and he who is in the city will be consumed by famine and pestilence. 16 But those who escape will flee to the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning, each one for his own iniquity.

17 “All hands will falter, and every knee will buckle. 18 They will gird themselves with sackcloth, and shuddering will cover them; shame will be on every face, and baldness on all their heads.

19 “They will cast their silver into the streets, and their gold will become an unclean thing; their silver and gold will not be able to deliver them in the day of the the LORD's wrath. They will not satisfy their appetites or fill their stomachs, for their wealth has become the stumbling block of their iniquity. 20 They took pride in their beautiful jewelry and made from it their detestable images and abominations; therefore I have made it an unclean thing to them.

21 “I will hand it over to foreigners as plunder, and to the wicked of the earth as spoil, and they will profane it. 22 I will turn My face away from them, and they will profane My treasured place; robbers will enter it and profane it.

Chains, Exile, and Desolation

23 “Forge the chain, for the land is full of bloodshed and the city is full of violence. 24 I will bring the most ruthless of the nations, and they will take possession of their houses; I will put an end to the pride of the mighty, and their holy places will be profaned.

25 “Anguish is coming; they will seek peace, but there will be none. 26 Disaster upon disaster will come, and rumor upon rumor; they will seek a vision from a prophet, but the law will vanish from the priest and counsel from the elders. 27 The king will mourn, the prince will be clothed in despair, and the hands of the people of the land will tremble. I will deal with them according to their conduct, and I will judge them by their own standards. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
