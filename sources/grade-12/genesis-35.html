Jacob Returns to Bethel

1 God said to Jacob, “Arise, go up to Bethel, and dwell there, and erect an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau.”

2 Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, “Remove the foreign gods that are among you, sanctify yourselves, and change your garments. 3 Let us arise and go up to Bethel, and I will build an altar there to the God who answered me in the day of my distress and has accompanied me wherever I have traveled.”

4 So they surrendered to Jacob all the foreign gods in their possession and the rings in their ears, and Jacob concealed them beneath the oak near Shechem. 5 As they journeyed, the terror of God fell upon the surrounding cities, so that none pursued Jacob’s sons. 6 Jacob and all the people with him arrived at Luz—that is, Bethel—in the land of Canaan. 7 There he built an altar and named the place El Bethel, because God had revealed Himself to him there when he fled from his brother. 8 Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died and was buried beneath the oak below Bethel, and he called it Allon-bacuth.

God Renews His Covenant with Jacob

9 After Jacob returned from Paddan-aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.

10 God said to him, “Your name is Jacob, but you will no longer be called Jacob; your name will be Israel.” So He called him Israel.

11 God also said, “I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation and an assembly of nations will come from you, and kings will descend from your line. 12 The land I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give it to your descendants after you.”

13 Then God ascended from him in the place where He had spoken with him. 14 Jacob erected a stone pillar in the place where God had spoken with him; he poured out a drink offering upon it and anointed it with oil. 15 Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

Rachel Dies in Childbirth

16 They departed from Bethel, and while they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel began to give birth, and her labor was arduous.

17 During her difficult labor, the midwife said to her, “Do not be afraid, for you have this son as well.”

18 As her soul was departing—for she was dying—she named him Ben-oni, but his father named him Benjamin. 19 Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem. 20 Jacob erected a pillar over her tomb; it remains the pillar of Rachel’s tomb to this day. 21 Israel traveled onward and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-eder.

22 While Israel was residing in that land, Reuben went in and lay with Bilhah, his father’s concubine, and Israel learned of it. Now Jacob had twelve sons. 23 The sons of Leah were Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 24 The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin. 25 The sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s maidservant, were Dan and Naphtali. 26 The sons of Zilpah, Leah’s maidservant, were Gad and Asher. These were the sons born to Jacob in Paddan-aram.

Isaac's Death and Burial

27 Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—where Abraham and Isaac had sojourned. 28 Isaac lived a hundred and eighty years. 29 He breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his ancestors; he was old and full of years, and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
