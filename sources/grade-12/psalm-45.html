A Royal Wedding Song

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Lilies.” A Maskil of the sons of Korah. A love song.

1 My heart overflows with a noble theme; I recite my verses to the king, and my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer.

The King’s Glory and Victory

2 You are the most handsome of men; grace is poured out upon Your lips; therefore God has blessed You forever. 3 Strap Your sword upon Your thigh, O mighty one, in Your splendor and majesty. 4 In Your majesty ride forth in victory on behalf of truth, humility, and righteousness; let Your right hand perform awesome deeds. 5 Your arrows pierce the hearts of the king’s enemies; the nations fall beneath You.

6 Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever, and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom. 7 You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You above Your companions with the oil of joy.

8 All Your garments are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from palaces of ivory the music of strings makes You glad. 9 The daughters of kings are among Your honored women; the queen stands at Your right hand in gold of Ophir.

The Queen’s Beauty and Counsel

10 Listen, O daughter! Consider and incline your ear: Forget your people and your father’s house, 11 and the king will desire your beauty. Bow to Him, for He is your Lord. 12 The daughter of Tyre will come with a gift, and the wealthy among the people will seek your favor.

13 All glorious is the princess in her chamber, with gold embroidery. 14 In colorful garments she is led to the king; her virgin companions are brought to You. 15 They are led in with joy and gladness; they enter the palace of the king.

16 Your sons will take the place of your fathers; You will make them princes throughout the land. 17 I will perpetuate Your name through all generations; therefore the nations will praise You forever and ever.
