The LORD's Deliverance of His People

1 A Song of Ascents. Of David. Had the LORD not been on our side—let Israel now declare—

2 had the LORD not been on our side when our foes rose up against us, 3 then they would have swallowed us alive in their burning wrath; 4 then the floodwaters would have overwhelmed us, and the torrent would have swept over our souls; 5 indeed, the haughty waters would have swept over our souls.

Praise for the LORD's Rescue

6 Blessed be the LORD, who has not handed us over to be devoured by our foes. 7 We have escaped like a bird from the fowler's snare; the snare is shattered, and we have been set free. 8 Our help is in the name of the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
