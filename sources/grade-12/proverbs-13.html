Wisdom, Speech, and the Discipline of Life

1 A wise son heeds his father's correction, but a scoffer ignores rebuke. 2 From his words a man enjoys good things, but the appetite of the treacherous craves violence. 3 He who guards his mouth preserves his life, but one who speaks rashly brings ruin upon himself. 4 The indolent crave yet receive nothing, but the diligent are richly satisfied. 5 The righteous hate falsehood, but the wicked bring shame and disgrace. 6 Righteousness guards the blameless along their path, but wickedness overthrows the sinner.

Wealth, Pride, and the Fruit of Diligence

7 Some feign wealth yet possess nothing; others feign poverty yet hold great riches. 8 A man's wealth may ransom his life, but a poor man hears no rebuke. 9 The light of the righteous brings joy, but the lamp of the wicked is extinguished. 10 Arrogance breeds only contention, but wisdom belongs to those who accept counsel. 11 Wealth gained hastily dwindles, but whoever gathers steadily multiplies it. 12 A deferred hope sickens the heart, but a longing fulfilled is a tree of life.

Instruction, Companionship, and the Rewards of Righteousness

13 Whoever despises the word brings destruction upon himself, but whoever fears the commandment is rewarded. 14 The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life, turning one away from the snares of death. 15 Sound judgment wins favor, but the way of the treacherous is harsh. 16 Every prudent person acts with knowledge, but a fool parades folly. 17 A wicked messenger falls into adversity, but a faithful envoy brings healing. 18 Poverty and shame befall those who spurn discipline, but whoever heeds correction is honored. 19 A longing fulfilled is sweet to the soul, but fools abhor turning from evil. 20 Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools suffers harm. 21 Calamity pursues sinners, but prosperity rewards the righteous.

Inheritance, Justice, and the Nourishment of the Righteous

22 A good person leaves an inheritance to grandchildren, but the sinner's wealth is stored up for the righteous. 23 The fallow ground of the poor yields abundant food, yet injustice sweeps it away. 24 Whoever spares the rod hates his son, but whoever loves him disciplines him diligently. 25 The righteous eat to their hearts' content, but the stomach of the wicked goes hungry.
