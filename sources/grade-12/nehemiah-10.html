Those Who Sealed the Covenant

1 Nehemiah the governor, son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah; 2 Seraiah, Azariah, and Jeremiah; 3 Pashhur, Amariah, and Malchijah; 4 Hattush, Shebaniah, and Malluch; 5 Harim, Meremoth, and Obadiah; 6 Daniel, Ginnethon, and Baruch; 7 Meshullam, Abijah, and Mijamin; 8 Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah—these are the priests.

9 The Levites: Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, and Kadmiel; 10 and their brothers Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, and Hanan; 11 Mica, Rehob, and Hashabiah; 12 Zaccur, Sherebiah, and Shebaniah; 13 Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

14 The leaders of the people: Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, and Bani; 15 Bunni, Azgad, and Bebai; 16 Adonijah, Bigvai, and Adin; 17 Ater, Hezekiah, and Azzur; 18 Hodiah, Hashum, and Bezai; 19 Hariph, Anathoth, and Nebai; 20 Magpiash, Meshullam, and Hezir; 21 Meshezabeel, Zadok, and Jaddua; 22 Pelatiah, Hanan, and Anaiah; 23 Hoshea, Hananiah, and Hashub; 24 Hallohesh, Pilha, and Shobek; 25 Rehum, Hashabnah, and Maaseiah; 26 Ahijah, Hanan, and Anan; 27 Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

The People’s Covenant Oath

28 The rest of the people—the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the Law of God, together with their wives, their sons, and their daughters, everyone who had knowledge and understanding— 29 joined with their nobles and their brothers and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the Law of God, which was given by the hand of Moses, the servant of God, and to observe and to do all the commandments of the LORD our Lord, together with His ordinances and His statutes. 30 They swore that they would not give their daughters to the peoples of the land, nor take their daughters for their sons. 31 If the peoples of the land brought merchandise or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, they would not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and they would forgo the harvest of the seventh year and cancel every debt.

Provision for the House of God

32 We also take upon ourselves the commandment to give a third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God: 33 for the showbread, for the regular grain offering, for the regular burnt offering, for the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the appointed feasts, for the holy things, for the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God. 34 We have cast lots among the priests, the Levites, and the people for the wood offering, to bring it into the house of our God according to our ancestral houses at appointed times year by year, to burn on the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the Law. 35 We also obligate ourselves to bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of every fruit tree to the house of the LORD year by year. 36 We will bring the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the Law, and the firstborn of our herds and flocks, to the house of our God, to the priests who minister there. 37 We will bring the first portion of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every tree, the new wine, and the oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God; and we will bring the tithe of our ground to the Levites, for the Levites are the ones who collect the tithes in all the towns where we work. 38 The priest, the son of Aaron, shall be with the Levites when they receive the tithe, and the Levites shall bring up a tenth of the tithe to the house of our God, to the chambers of the storehouse. 39 For the people of Israel and the sons of Levi shall bring the contribution of grain, new wine, and oil to the chambers where the vessels of the sanctuary are, together with the priests who minister, the gatekeepers, and the singers. We will not neglect the house of our God.
