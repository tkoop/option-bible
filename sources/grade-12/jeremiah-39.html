Jerusalem Falls to Babylon

1 In the ninth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came against Jerusalem with his entire army and besieged it. 2 In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was breached. 3 Then all the officials of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the remaining officials of the king of Babylon.

Zedekiah Captured and Punished

4 When Zedekiah, king of Judah, and all the soldiers saw them, they fled and left the city by night, taking the way of the king’s garden through the gate between the two walls, and they went out toward the Arabah. 5 But the Chaldean army pursued them and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho; they seized him and brought him to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he pronounced judgment on him. 6 At Riblah the king of Babylon slaughtered Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes, and he also killed all the nobles of Judah. 7 Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, bound him with bronze fetters, and took him to Babylon. 8 The Chaldeans burned the king’s house and the houses of the people, and they broke down the walls of Jerusalem. 9 Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, carried into exile to Babylon the remnant of the people who remained in the city, along with those who had deserted to him and the rest of the people. 10 But Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, left in the land of Judah some of the poor people who had nothing, and at that time he gave them vineyards and fields.

Jeremiah Released by the Babylonians

11 Now Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had given orders concerning Jeremiah through Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, saying, 12 “Take him and look after him; do him no harm, but do for him whatever he requests of you.” 13 So Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, Nebushazban the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the chief officers of the king of Babylon sent and took Jeremiah out of the courtyard of the guard. 14 They entrusted him to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, to take him home, and so Jeremiah remained among the people.

The LORD’s Promise to Ebed-Melech

15 While Jeremiah had been confined to the courtyard of the guard, the word of the LORD came to him: 16 “Go and tell Ebed-melech the Cushite that this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘I am about to fulfill My words against this city for disaster and not for prosperity, and all of them will be fulfilled before your eyes on that day. 17 But I will rescue you on that day, declares the LORD, and you will not be delivered into the hands of the men you fear. 18 For I will surely save you, and you will not fall by the sword; because you have trusted in Me, you will escape with your life, declares the LORD.’”
