Ahaziah's Accession to the Throne

1 The inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah, his youngest son, king in his place, because the raiding party that had come with the Arabs to the camp had killed all his older brothers. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, began to reign. 2 Ahaziah was forty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah, daughter of Omri. 3 He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother counseled him to act wickedly. 4 He did evil in the sight of the the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, for after his father's death they became his counselors to his ruin.

Ahaziah's Alliance with Israel

5 He also followed their counsel and went with Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, to war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram. 6 Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds he had received at Ramah in the battle against Hazael king of Aram. Then Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to visit Joram son of Ahab, because Joram was ill.

Jehu's Purge and Ahaziah's Death

7 But Ahaziah's downfall came from God: when he came to Joram, he went out with Joram to meet Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the the LORD had anointed to exterminate the house of Ahab. 8 While Jehu was executing judgment on the house of Ahab, he discovered the princes of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who served Ahaziah, and he killed them. 9 He searched for Ahaziah, and they caught him while he was hiding in Samaria. They brought him to Jehu and killed him, then buried him, because they said, "He is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the the LORD with all his heart." Thus the house of Ahaziah had no strength to retain the kingdom.

Athaliah Seizes the Throne

10 When Athaliah, mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal heirs of the house of Judah. 11 But Jehoshabeath, the king's daughter, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from the king's sons who were being slain, and she placed him and his nurse in a bedroom. Because Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram and wife of Jehoiada the priest, was Ahaziah's sister, she hid him from Athaliah so that she did not kill him. 12 He remained hidden with them in the house of God for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.
