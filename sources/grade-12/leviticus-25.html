The Sabbath Year for the Land

1 The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: 3 For six years you may sow your field, prune your vineyard, and gather its produce. 4 But in the seventh year the land is to have a complete Sabbath rest, a Sabbath to the LORD: you must not sow your field or prune your vineyard. 5 You must not reap the aftergrowth of your harvest or gather the grapes of your unpruned vine; it is to be a year of complete rest for the land. 6 The Sabbath produce of the land will be food for you—for you, your male and female servants, your hired worker, and the temporary resident living among you— 7 and for your livestock and the wild animals in your land. All its yield is to be eaten."

The Year of Jubilee

"8 You are to count off seven Sabbath years—seven times seven years—so the seven Sabbath years amount to forty-nine years. 9 Then you must sound a ram's horn with a loud blast on the tenth day of the seventh month, on the Day of Atonement; you must sound the horn throughout your land. 10 You must consecrate the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty throughout the land to all its inhabitants. It will be a Jubilee for you: each of you is to return to his own property, and each is to return to his clan. 11 The fiftieth year will be a Jubilee for you: you must not sow or reap the aftergrowth or gather grapes from the unpruned vines. 12 Because it is a Jubilee, it is to be holy to you; you may eat its produce from the field. 13 In this Year of Jubilee, you are to return, each to his own property."

Property Sales and Redemption

"14 If you sell property to your neighbor or buy from your neighbor, you must not wrong one another. 15 You are to buy from your neighbor according to the number of years after the Jubilee, and he is to sell to you according to the number of years of crops. 16 If the years are many, you must increase the price; if they are few, you must decrease it, because he is selling you a number of harvests. 17 You must not wrong one another, but fear your God, for I am the LORD your God. 18 You must keep My statutes and observe My ordinances, and then you will dwell securely in the land. 19 The land will yield its fruit, you will eat your fill, and you will dwell securely in it. 20 If you ask, ‘What will we eat in the seventh year if we do not sow or gather our produce?’ 21 I will command My blessing to you in the sixth year, and it will produce a crop sufficient for three years. 22 You will sow in the eighth year, but you will eat from the old crop until the ninth year; until its crop comes in, you will eat the old.”

"23 The land must not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is Mine; you are strangers and sojourners with Me. 24 In all the land you possess, you must allow redemption of the land. 25 If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his property, his nearest redeemer must come and redeem what his brother sold. 26 If a man has no one to redeem it, but he himself prospers and finds enough to redeem it, 27 then he must calculate the years since its sale and refund the excess to the man to whom he sold it, and then return to his property. 28 But if he cannot secure enough to refund him, what he sold remains in the buyer's hand until the Year of Jubilee; in the Jubilee it is released, and he returns to his property. 29 If a man sells a house in a walled city, his right of redemption lasts until the end of the year of its sale; for a full year he has the right of redemption. 30 If it is not redeemed within a full year, the house in the walled city belongs permanently to the buyer and his descendants; it is not released in the Jubilee. 31 But houses in villages without walls around them are to be counted as fields of the land; they have redemption and are released in the Jubilee. 32 As for the cities of the Levites, the houses in their possessed cities, the Levites have a permanent right of redemption. 33 If one of the Levites redeems it, the house that was sold and the city of his possession are released in the Jubilee, because the houses of the Levites' cities are their possession among the Israelites. 34 But the fields of pastureland around their cities may not be sold, for that is their permanent possession."

Care for the Poor and Servants

"35 If your brother becomes poor and cannot maintain himself with you, you must support him as a stranger and a sojourner, and he will live with you. 36 You must not take interest or profit from him, but you must fear your God, so that your brother may live with you. 37 You must not lend him your money at interest or give him your food for profit. 38 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God."

"39 If your brother becomes poor beside you and sells himself to you, you must not make him serve as a slave. 40 He shall be with you as a hired worker and as a sojourner; he shall serve with you until the Year of Jubilee. 41 Then he and his children with him shall go out from you and return to his clan and to the property of his fathers. 42 For they are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt; they shall not be sold as slaves. 43 You must not rule over him ruthlessly, but you must fear your God. 44 As for your male and female slaves whom you may have—from the nations around you, from them you may buy male and female slaves. 45 You may also buy from the children of the strangers who sojourn among you, and from their clans that are with you, which they have begotten in your land; they may become your property. 46 You may leave them as an inheritance to your children after you, to inherit as property; you may make them serve forever. But as for your brothers, the children of Israel, no man shall rule over his brother ruthlessly. 47 If a stranger or sojourner with you becomes rich, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to the stranger or sojourner with you, or to a member of the stranger's clan, 48 after he is sold, he retains the right of redemption; one of his brothers may redeem him, 49 or his uncle or his cousin may redeem him, or any close relative from his clan may redeem him; or if he prospers, he may redeem himself. 50 He and his buyer shall count the time from the year he sold himself to him until the Year of Jubilee, and the price of his redemption shall be based on the number of years, with his time treated as that of a hired worker. 51 If there are still many years, he shall refund his redemption money in proportion to them. 52 If few years remain until the Year of Jubilee, he shall calculate and refund accordingly. 53 He shall be with him as a hired worker year by year; he shall not rule ruthlessly over him in your sight. 54 And if he is not redeemed by these means, then he and his children with him shall go out in the Year of Jubilee. 55 For the children of Israel are servants to Me; they are My servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God."
