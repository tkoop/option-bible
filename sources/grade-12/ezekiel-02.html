Ezekiel's Commissioning

1 He said to me, “Mortal man, rise to your feet, and I will speak with you.” 2 As He spoke to me, the Spirit entered me and raised me to my feet, and I heard Him speaking.

Sent to a Rebellious People

3 He said to me, “Mortal man, I am sending you to the Israelites, to a rebellious nation that has rebelled against Me. They and their ancestors have transgressed against Me to this very day. 4 They are brazen and hard-hearted descendants; to them I am sending you, and you must tell them, ‘This is what the Lord, the LORD, says.’ 5 Whether they listen or refuse—for they are a rebellious people—they will know that a prophet has been among them.”

Courage Amid Hostility

6 “And you, mortal man, do not fear them or their words, though you are surrounded by people as hostile as thorns and as dangerous as scorpions. Do not fear their words, and do not be dismayed by their looks, for they are a rebellious people. 7 You must speak My words to them, whether they listen or refuse, for they are a rebellious people. 8 But you, mortal man, hear what I am saying to you. Do not be rebellious like that rebellious people; open your mouth and eat what I give you.”

The Scroll of Woe

9 Then I looked, and, to my astonishment, a hand was extended toward me, holding a scroll. 10 He spread it before me; it was written on both sides, and inscribed upon it were lamentations, mourning, and woe.
