Hannah’s Song of Thanksgiving

1 Hannah prayed and said, “My heart rejoices in the the LORD; my strength is lifted up in the the LORD. My mouth speaks boldly against my enemies, for I rejoice in Your salvation. 2 There is no one holy like the the LORD, for there is none besides You, and there is no rock like our God. 3 Do not go on speaking so proudly; do not let arrogance come from your mouth, for the the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him deeds are weighed. 4 The bows of the mighty are broken, but those who stumbled are armed with strength. 5 The well-fed hire themselves out for bread, while the hungry are filled; the barren woman bears seven children, but she who has many sons grows feeble. 6 The LORD brings death and gives life; He sends down to Sheol and raises up. 7 The LORD makes poor and makes rich; He humbles and He exalts. 8 He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, to seat them with princes and give them an inheritance of honor. For the pillars of the earth are the the LORD’s, and He has set the world upon them. 9 He guards the feet of His faithful ones, but the wicked are silenced in darkness, for not by might does a man prevail. 10 The LORD shatters those who contend with Him; He thunders against them from heaven. The LORD judges the ends of the earth; He gives strength to His king and exalts the power of His anointed.”

11 Then Elkanah went home to Ramah, while the boy ministered to the the LORD in the presence of Eli the priest.

The Corruption of Eli’s Sons

12 Now the sons of Eli were worthless men; they did not know the the LORD. 13 The custom of the priests with the people was this: whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest’s servant came while the meat was boiling, carrying a three-pronged fork in his hand. 14 He would thrust it into the pan, kettle, cauldron, or pot, and whatever the fork brought up the priest took for himself. This is what they did to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

15 Moreover, before the fat was burned, the priest’s servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, “Give the priest meat to roast; he will not accept boiled meat from you, but only raw.”

16 If the man said to him, “Let them burn the fat first, then take as much as you desire,” he would answer,

“No, give it now; if not, I will take it by force.”

17 Thus the sin of the young men was very great before the the LORD, for they treated the the LORD’s offering with contempt.

Samuel’s Growth and Eli’s Rebuke

18 But Samuel was ministering before the the LORD, a boy wearing a linen ephod. 19 Moreover, his mother made him a little robe and brought it to him year by year when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

20 Then Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, “May the The LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she dedicated to the the LORD.” Then they would go home. 21 Indeed, the The LORD attended to Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the the LORD.

22 Now Eli was very old, and he heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they lay with the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

23 So he said to them, “Why do you do such things? I hear from all the people about your evil deeds. 24 No, my sons; the report that I hear is not good, for you are causing the people of the the LORD to transgress. 25 If one man sins against another, God may mediate for him; but if a man sins against the the LORD, who can intercede for him?” But they did not listen to their father, because the the LORD had decided to put them to death.

26 And the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favor with the the LORD and with men.

A Prophet’s Warning to Eli

27 Then a man of God came to Eli and said to him, “Thus says the the LORD: Did I not clearly reveal Myself to your father’s house when they were in Egypt under Pharaoh’s rule? 28 Did I not choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to go up to My altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod before Me? And did I not give to your father’s house all the offerings made by fire by the children of Israel? 29 Why then do you scorn My sacrifice and My offering that I have commanded for My dwelling, and honor your sons above Me by fattening yourselves on the best of all the offerings of My people Israel? 30 Therefore the the LORD, the God of Israel, declares: ‘I did indeed say that your house and your father’s house would walk before Me forever.’ But now the the LORD declares: ‘I would never permit that; those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me will be lightly esteemed.’ 31 Behold, the days are coming when I will cut off your strength and the strength of your father’s house, so that there will not be an old man in your house. 32 And you will see distress in My dwelling in place of all the good that was done to Israel, and there will never be an old man in your house. 33 Yet not every man of yours will I cut off from My altar, so that your eyes will fail and your heart will grieve; and all the increase of your house will die in the prime of life. 34 And this will be a sign to you that will come upon your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas: in one day both of them shall die. 35 Then I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who shall do according to what is in My heart and in My mind. I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before My anointed forever. 36 And everyone who is left in your house will come and bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, and will say, ‘Please appoint me to one of the priestly offices, that I may eat a piece of bread.’”
