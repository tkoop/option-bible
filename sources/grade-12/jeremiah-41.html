Ishmael Assassinates Gedaliah

1 In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah—descended from the royal line and formerly one of the king’s principal officers—arrived at Mizpah with ten companions to visit Gedaliah son of Ahikam. There they shared a meal, and while Gedaliah governed the land under Babylonian appointment, Ishmael turned hospitality into treachery. 2 He and his ten men rose, struck Gedaliah down with the sword, and murdered the very official whom the king of Babylon had entrusted with authority over the land. 3 Ishmael also slaughtered every Jew who had gathered with Gedaliah at Mizpah, together with the Chaldean soldiers stationed there. 4 The crime remained concealed for two days, so that no one yet knew what had happened.

Pilgrims Are Massacred at Mizpah

5 Eighty men from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria—men whose beards had been shaved, whose garments were torn, and whose bodies bore the marks of mourning—came bearing grain offerings and frankincense to the house of the LORD. 6 Ishmael son of Nethaniah emerged from Mizpah to intercept them, weeping as he advanced, and when he reached them he urged, “Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam.”

7 Once they had entered the city, Ishmael and his accomplices slaughtered the pilgrims and cast their bodies into a cistern. 8 Ten of the men, however, pleaded, “Do not kill us; we have concealed stores of wheat, barley, oil, and honey in the fields.” Ishmael spared them and did not execute them with their kinsmen. 9 The cistern into which he had hurled all the corpses of those he killed alongside Gedaliah was the large reservoir that King Asa had constructed as a defense against Baasha king of Israel; Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with the slain.

Johanan Rescues the Captives

10 Ishmael then seized the entire remnant of Mizpah’s population—the king’s daughters and everyone else left there, whom Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had placed under Gedaliah’s supervision—and led them away as captives toward the Ammonites. 11 When Johanan son of Kareah and all the military commanders with him heard of every atrocity Ishmael son of Nethaniah had committed, 12 they mobilized their men and marched to confront him. They overtook him beside the great pool at Gibeon. 13 At the sight of Johanan and his commanders, the people who had been with Ishmael rejoiced. 14 The captives Ishmael had seized from Mizpah broke away and rallied to Johanan son of Kareah. 15 Ishmael himself escaped with eight men and fled to the Ammonites. 16 Johanan son of Kareah and his commanders then gathered the whole remnant they had recovered from Ishmael after the murder of Gedaliah son of Ahikam—soldiers, women, children, and eunuchs—and brought them back from Gibeon.

The Survivors Seek Refuge in Egypt

17 They traveled onward and settled at Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem, intending to continue into Egypt, 18 because they feared the Chaldeans. Ishmael son of Nethaniah had struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land, and they dreaded retaliation from Babylon.
