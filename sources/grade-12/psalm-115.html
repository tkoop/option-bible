To the LORD Alone Belongs the Glory

1 Not to us, O the LORD, not to us, but to Your name give glory, for the sake of Your steadfast love and for the sake of Your faithfulness. 2 Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?" 3 Our God is in heaven; He does whatever He pleases.

4 Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands. 5 They have mouths, yet they cannot speak; they have eyes, yet they cannot see. 6 They have ears, yet they cannot hear; they have noses, yet they cannot smell. 7 They have hands, yet they cannot feel; they have feet, yet they cannot walk; nor can they utter a sound with their throats. 8 Those who make them become like them, as does everyone who trusts in them.

9 O Israel, trust in the LORD; He is their help and shield. 10 O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD; He is their help and shield. 11 You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; He is their help and shield.

12 The LORD remembers us and will bless us; He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron. 13 He will bless those who fear the LORD, both the least and the greatest.

14 May the LORD increase you—more and more, you and your children. 15 May you be blessed by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth. 16 The highest heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth He has given to humanity.

17 It is not the dead who praise the LORD, nor those who descend into silence. 18 But we will bless the LORD from this time forth and forevermore. Praise the LORD!
