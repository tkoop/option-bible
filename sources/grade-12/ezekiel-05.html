Ezekiel's Sign of the Siege

1 "And you, son of man, take for yourself a sharp sword; take it as a barber's razor and pass it over your head and over your beard; then take scales for weighing and divide them. 2 During the days of the siege, when they are completed, burn a third in the fire in the midst of the city; take a third and strike around it with the sword; scatter a third to the wind, and I will draw the sword after them. 3 Take from there a few in number and bind them in your skirts. 4 Take some of them again and throw them into the midst of the fire and burn them in the fire; from it a fire will go out to all the house of Israel.

The LORD's Indictment of Jerusalem

5 "Thus says the Lord the LORD: This is Jerusalem; I have set her in the midst of the nations, with countries all around her. 6 She has rebelled against My ordinances by doing wickedness more than the nations, and against My statutes more than the countries around her, for they have rejected My ordinances and have not walked in them. 7 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Because you were more turbulent than the nations around you, and you have not walked in My statutes, nor have you done My ordinances, but you have done according to the ordinances of the nations around you. 8 Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I, even I, am against you, and I will execute judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations. 9 Because of all your abominations, I will do to you what I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again.

The Fourfold Punishment

10 "Therefore fathers will eat their sons in your midst, and sons will eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments on you, and I will scatter all your remnant to every wind. 11 Therefore, as I live, says the Lord the LORD, surely because you have defiled My sanctuary with all your detestable things and with all your abominations, I will also withdraw My gaze; My eye will not spare, and I will have no pity. 12 A third of you will die by pestilence and be consumed by famine in your midst; a third will fall by the sword around you; and a third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw the sword after them. 13 Thus My anger will be spent, and I will cause My wrath to rest on them, and I will be appeased; and they will know that I am the LORD, that I have spoken in My zeal when I have spent My wrath on them. 14 I will make you a desolation and a reproach among the nations that are around you, in the sight of all who pass by. 15 It will be a reproach and a taunt, an instruction and an astonishment to the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments on you in anger and in wrath and in furious rebukes. I, the LORD, have spoken. 16 When I send against them the deadly arrows of famine, which are for destruction, which I will send to destroy you, I will increase the famine upon you and break your staff of bread. 17 I will send upon you famine and evil beasts, and they will bereave you; pestilence and blood will pass through you, and I will bring the sword upon you. I, the LORD, have spoken."
