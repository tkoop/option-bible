Praise the LORD and Remember His Wondrous Deeds

1 Give thanks to the LORD; call upon His name, and proclaim His deeds among the nations. 2 Sing to Him; sing praises to Him, and recount all His wonders. 3 Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. 4 Seek the LORD and His strength; seek His face always. 5 Remember the wonders He has done, His miracles, and the judgments He has spoken.

The LORD's Covenant with the Patriarchs

6 O descendants of Abraham His servant, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones, 7 He is the LORD our God; His judgments reach throughout the earth. 8 He remembers His covenant forever, the word He commanded for a thousand generations— 9 the covenant He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac, 10 which He established as a statute for Jacob, an everlasting covenant for Israel— 11 saying, “I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.”

12 When they were but few in number, insignificant, and strangers in that land, 13 they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another. 14 He allowed no one to oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf, 15 saying, “Do not touch My anointed ones; do no harm to My prophets.”

From Joseph to the Plagues of Egypt

16 He called a famine upon the land and broke every source of bread. 17 He sent a man before them—Joseph, sold as a slave. 18 They bruised his feet with shackles, and his neck was placed in irons, 19 until his word came to pass and the word of the LORD proved him true. 20 The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free. 21 He made him master of his household and ruler over all his possessions, 22 to instruct his princes as he pleased and to teach his elders wisdom.

23 Then Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. 24 He made His people very fruitful and stronger than their adversaries. 25 He turned their hearts to hate His people and to conspire against His servants. 26 He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron, whom He had chosen. 27 They performed His signs among them and wonders in the land of Ham. 28 He sent darkness, and it became dark, and they did not rebel against His words. 29 He turned their waters into blood and caused their fish to die. 30 Their land teemed with frogs, even in their royal chambers. 31 He spoke, and insects swarmed—gnats throughout their country. 32 He gave them hail for rain, with lightning throughout their land. 33 He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country. 34 He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts without number. 35 They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil. 36 Then He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigor.

The Exodus and the Promised Land

37 He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and none among His tribes stumbled. 38 Egypt was glad when they departed, for the dread of Israel had fallen upon them. 39 He spread a cloud as a covering and a fire to light the night. 40 He summoned quail and satisfied them with bread from heaven. 41 He opened the rock, and water gushed out; it flowed in the desert like a river. 42 For He remembered His holy promise to Abraham His servant. 43 He brought forth His people with rejoicing, His chosen with shouts of joy. 44 He gave them the lands of the nations, that they might inherit the fruit of others' labor, 45 that they might keep His statutes and obey His laws. Hallelujah!
