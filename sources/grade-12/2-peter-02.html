Warning Against False Teachers

1 But there were also false prophets among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you, who will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them and bringing swift destruction on themselves. 2 Many will follow their depraved conduct, and because of them the way of truth will be blasphemed. 3 In their greed they will exploit you with fabricated words. Their condemnation, decreed long ago, is not idle, and their destruction does not slumber.

God's Judgment on the Ungodly

4 For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into hell and committed them to chains of gloomy darkness to be kept until the judgment; 5 and if He did not spare the ancient world, but brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly and preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness, with seven others; 6 and if He condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction by reducing them to ashes, making them an example of what is coming to the ungodly; 7 and if He rescued righteous Lot, distressed by the depraved conduct of the lawless— 8 for that righteous man, living among them day after day, was tormented in his righteous soul by the lawless deeds he saw and heard— 9 then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials and to hold the unrighteous for punishment on the day of judgment.

The Character and Corruption of False Teachers

10 especially those who follow the flesh in its corrupt desires and despise authority. Bold and arrogant, they do not tremble as they blaspheme glorious ones, 11 whereas angels, though greater in strength and power, do not bring a slanderous charge against them before the Lord. 12 But these people are like irrational animals, creatures of instinct born to be caught and destroyed; they blaspheme about things they do not understand, and in their destruction they too will be destroyed, 13 receiving harm as the wages of their wrongdoing. They consider it pleasure to carouse in broad daylight. They are stains and blemishes, carousing in their deceit while they feast with you. 14 Their eyes are full of adultery, insatiable for sin; they entice unstable souls; they have hearts trained in greed—accursed children! 15 They have forsaken the straight way and wandered off, following the way of Balaam son of Beor, who loved the wages of wrongdoing, 16 but was rebuked for his own transgression: a speechless donkey spoke with a human voice and restrained the prophet's madness.

The Empty Promises of False Freedom

17 These men are springs without water and mists driven by a storm; blackest darkness is reserved for them. 18 For by speaking arrogant, empty words, they entice by sensual passions of the flesh those who have barely escaped from people living in error. 19 They promise them freedom, while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for whatever overcomes a person enslaves him. 20 If they have escaped the defilements of the world through the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ and are again entangled in them and overcome, their last condition is worse than the first. 21 For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than, having known it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them. 22 As the true proverb says, "A dog returns to its own vomit," and, "a sow, after washing, wallows in the mud."
