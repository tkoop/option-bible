God Renews His Covenant with Abram

1 When Abram had reached ninety-nine years of age, the LORD appeared to him and declared, “I am God Almighty; walk in My presence and remain blameless. 2 I will establish My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you immeasurably.”

3 Abram prostrated himself, and God conversed with him, saying,

“4 As for Me, behold, My covenant stands with you, and you shall become the father of a multitude of nations. 5 your name shall no longer be Abram; instead, you shall be called Abraham, because I have appointed you the father of many nations. 6 I will render you exceedingly fruitful; I will constitute nations from you, and kings shall issue from you. 7 I will establish My covenant as an everlasting covenant between Me and you and your descendants after you, to be God to you and to your descendants after you. 8 I will bestow upon you and your descendants after you the land of your sojourn—all the land of Canaan—as an everlasting possession, and I will be their God.”

The Covenant of Circumcision

9 God also said to Abraham, “As for you, you must observe My covenant—you and your descendants after you throughout their generations. 10 This is My covenant with you and your descendants after you, which you are to observe: every male among you must be circumcised. 11 You shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall serve as the sign of the covenant between Me and you. 12 Throughout the generations to come, every male among you must be circumcised on the eighth day, including those born within the household and those acquired from a foreigner—even those not sprung from your offspring. 13 Whether born within your household or acquired, they must be circumcised; My covenant shall be inscribed in your flesh as an everlasting covenant. 14 Any uncircumcised male who fails to circumcise the flesh of his foreskin shall be cut off from his people, for he has violated My covenant.”

The Promise of Isaac and Ishmael

15 God also said to Abraham, “As for Sarai your wife, do not call her Sarai, for her name is Sarah. 16 I will bless her and assuredly give you a son through her. I will bless her, and she shall become the mother of nations; kings of peoples shall descend from her.”

17 Abraham prostrated himself and laughed, musing in his heart whether a child could be born to a man of a hundred years and whether Sarah, at ninety, could bear one. 18 Abraham said to God, “If only Ishmael might live before You!”

19 God replied, “No; rather, your wife Sarah will bear you a son, and you shall name him Isaac. I will establish My covenant with him as an everlasting covenant for his descendants after him. 20 As for Ishmael, I have heard you: I will indeed bless him, make him fruitful, and multiply him exceedingly. He shall father twelve princes, and I will make him a great nation. 21 But I will establish My covenant with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this same time next year.”

22 When God concluded speaking with Abraham, He ascended from him.

Abraham Obeys the Command

23 On that very day Abraham took his son Ishmael and all who were born in his household or acquired—all the males among Abraham's household—and circumcised them, exactly as God had instructed him. 24 Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised. 25 His son Ishmael was thirteen years old when he was circumcised. 26 On that very day Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised. 27 All the men of his household, both those born in the household and those acquired from a foreigner, were circumcised with him.
