Allotments for the Northern Tribes

1 These are the names of the tribes: at the northern frontier, Dan shall receive one portion, bordering the road of Hethlon to the entrance of Hamath and reaching Hazar-enan on the border of Damascus, with Hamath to the north; and Dan’s territory shall extend from the east side to the west side. 2 Asher shall receive one portion along the border of Dan, from the east side to the west side. 3 Naphtali shall receive one portion along the border of Asher, from the east side to the west side. 4 Manasseh shall receive one portion along the border of Naphtali, from the east side to the west side. 5 Ephraim shall receive one portion along the border of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side. 6 Reuben shall receive one portion along the border of Ephraim, from the east side to the west side. 7 Judah shall receive one portion along the border of Reuben, from the east side to the west side.

The Sacred District, the City, and the Prince’s Portion

8 Along the border of Judah, from the east side to the west side, shall be the offering you set apart: 25,000 cubits in width and as long as one of the portions; the sanctuary shall be in its midst. 9 The offering you set apart to the the LORD shall be 25,000 cubits in length and 10,000 cubits in width. 10 This holy offering shall belong to the priests: 25,000 cubits on the north, 10,000 cubits on the west in width, 10,000 cubits on the east in width, and 25,000 cubits on the south in length; and the sanctuary of the the LORD shall be in its center. 11 It shall be for the consecrated priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept My charge and did not go astray when the Israelites went astray, as the Levites did. 12 It shall be for them an offering from the offering of the land, a most holy portion, bordering the territory of the Levites. 13 Alongside the border of the priests, the Levites shall have 25,000 cubits in length and 10,000 cubits in width; the entire area shall be 25,000 by 10,000 cubits. 14 They shall not sell any of it, nor exchange it, nor transfer this choice portion of the land, because it is holy to the the LORD. 15 The remaining land, 5,000 cubits in width alongside the 25,000, shall be common land for the city—for dwelling and for pasture—and the city shall be in its midst. 16 These shall be its measurements: 4,500 cubits on the north side, 4,500 cubits on the south side, 4,500 cubits on the east side, and 4,500 cubits on the west side. 17 The pastureland around the city shall be 250 cubits on the north, 250 cubits on the south, 250 cubits on the east, and 250 cubits on the west. 18 The remainder of the length bordering the holy offering—10,000 cubits eastward and 10,000 cubits westward—shall belong to the workers of the city, and its produce shall be food for them. 19 The workers of the city who cultivate it shall come from all the tribes of Israel. 20 The entire portion shall be a square—25,000 cubits by 25,000 cubits—and you shall set apart the holy offering together with the property of the city. 21 The remaining land on both sides of the holy offering and of the property of the city shall belong to the prince; it shall extend eastward from the 25,000 cubits of the holy offering to the eastern border, and westward from the 25,000 cubits to the western border, and the holy offering and the sanctuary of the temple shall be in the center. 22 Thus the property of the Levites and the property of the city shall lie in the center of the area belonging to the prince; the prince’s portion shall lie between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin.

Allotments for the Southern Tribes

23 As for the rest of the tribes, Benjamin shall receive one portion from the east side to the west side. 24 Simeon shall receive one portion along the border of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side. 25 Issachar shall receive one portion along the border of Simeon, from the east side to the west side. 26 Zebulun shall receive one portion along the border of Issachar, from the east side to the west side. 27 Gad shall receive one portion along the border of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side. 28 The southern border of Gad shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, then along the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea. 29 This is the land you shall allot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these are their portions, declares the Lord the LORD.

The City’s Gates and Its New Name

30 These shall be the exits of the city: on the north side, 4,500 cubits. 31 The gates of the city shall be named after the tribes of Israel: on the north, three gates—the gate of Reuben, the gate of Judah, and the gate of Levi. 32 On the east side, 4,500 cubits, three gates—the gate of Joseph, the gate of Benjamin, and the gate of Dan. 33 On the south side, 4,500 cubits, three gates—the gate of Simeon, the gate of Issachar, and the gate of Zebulun. 34 On the west side, 4,500 cubits, three gates—the gate of Gad, the gate of Asher, and the gate of Naphtali. 35 The circumference of the city shall be 18,000 cubits, and from that day on the name of the city shall be: the LORD Is There.
