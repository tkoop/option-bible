The Inner Gate and the Prince’s Offerings

1 Thus says the Lord the LORD: “The inner gate of the courtyard facing east shall be shut during the six working days, but it shall be opened on the Sabbath and on the day of the New Moon. 2 The prince shall enter from outside by way of the vestibule of the gate and stand at the gatepost; the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall worship at the threshold of the gate. Then he shall go out, but the gate shall not be closed until evening. 3 On the Sabbaths and on the New Moons, the people of the land shall worship at the entrance of that gate before the the LORD.”

4 The burnt offering that the prince presents to the the LORD on the Sabbath day shall be six lambs without blemish and a ram without blemish. 5 The grain offering for the ram shall be an ephah, and the grain offering for the lambs shall be as much as he can afford, together with a hin of oil per ephah.

6 On the day of the New Moon he shall offer a young bull without blemish, six lambs, and a ram; they shall be without blemish. 7 He shall provide a grain offering of an ephah for the bull, an ephah for the ram, and as much as he can afford for the lambs, together with a hin of oil per ephah.

8 When the prince enters, he shall enter by way of the vestibule of the gate and go out by the same way. 9 But when the people of the land come before the the LORD at the appointed feasts, whoever enters by the north gate to worship shall go out by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate shall go out by the north gate. No one shall return by the gate through which he came, but each shall go out straight ahead. 10 The prince shall be among them; when they enter, he shall enter, and when they go out, he shall go out.

11 At the festivals and the appointed feasts, the grain offering shall be an ephah with a bull, an ephah with a ram, and as much as he can afford with the lambs, together with a hin of oil per ephah. 12 When the prince provides a freewill offering, either a burnt offering or peace offerings, as a freewill offering to the the LORD, the gate facing east shall be opened for him. He shall offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings as he does on the Sabbath, and then he shall go out; after he has gone out, the gate shall be shut.

The Daily Burnt Offering

13 You shall present a lamb a year old without blemish as a burnt offering to the the LORD each day; morning by morning you shall present it. 14 You shall also provide with it a grain offering each morning: one-sixth of an ephah, and one-third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour, as a grain offering to the the LORD. This is a perpetual statute. 15 So the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil shall be prepared every morning as a continual burnt offering.

The Prince’s Inheritance

16 Thus says the Lord the LORD: “If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance and shall belong to his sons; it is their possession by inheritance. 17 But if he gives a gift of his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the Year of Liberty; then it shall return to the prince. His inheritance, however, shall belong to his sons. 18 The prince shall not take any of the people’s inheritance or evict them from their property. He shall give an inheritance to his sons from his own property, so that none of My people shall be scattered from his property.”

The Priests’ Cooking Chambers

19 Then he brought me through the entrance beside the gate into the holy chambers for the priests, which faced north; and behold, there was a place at the western extremity. 20 He said to me, “This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offering and the sin offering and bake the grain offering, so that they do not bring them out into the outer court and sanctify the people.”

21 Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me around to the four corners of the court; and behold, in every corner of the court there was an inner court. 22 In the four corners of the court were enclosed courts, forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide; the four of them in the corners had the same dimensions. 23 A row of masonry ran all around them—around the four of them—and boiling places had been made beneath the rows all around.

24 Then he said to me, “These are the boiling houses where the ministers of the temple shall boil the sacrifices of the people.”
