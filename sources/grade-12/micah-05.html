The Siege and the Stricken Judge

1 Now, city of soldiers, muster your forces, for a siege is laid against us; with a rod they strike the judge of Israel on the cheek.

The Ruler from Bethlehem

2 And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are small among the clans of Judah, out of you will come for Me One to be ruler over Israel, whose origins are from of old, from ancient days. 3 Therefore He will hand them over until the woman in labor gives birth; then the rest of His brothers will return to the Israelites. 4 He will stand and shepherd His flock in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD His God. And they will live securely, for now He will be great to the ends of the earth.

Peace and Deliverance from Assyria

5 He will be their peace. When Assyria invades our land and treads in our citadels, we will raise against him seven shepherds and eight leaders of men. 6 They will rule the land of Assyria with the sword and the land of Nimrod at its gates; He will deliver us when the Assyrian invades our land and marches within our borders.

The Remnant and Divine Purging

7 The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers on the grass, which do not wait for man or linger for mankind. 8 The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep; if it passes through, it tramples and tears, and there is no one to rescue. 9 Your hand will be raised against your adversaries, and all your enemies will be cut off.

10 And on that day, declares the LORD, I will cut off your horses from among you and destroy your chariots. 11 I will cut off the cities of your land and tear down all your fortresses. 12 I will cut off sorcery from your hand, and you will have no soothsayers. 13 I will cut off your carved images and your sacred pillars from among you, and you will no longer bow down to the work of your hands. 14 I will uproot your Asherah poles from among you and destroy your cities. 15 I will execute vengeance in anger and wrath on the nations that have not obeyed.
