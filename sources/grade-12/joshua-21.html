The Levites Request Their Inheritance

1 Then the heads of the Levite families approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the Israelite tribal families, 2 while Israel was at Shiloh in Canaan, and said to them, “The The LORD commanded through Moses that you give us towns in which to live, together with pasturelands for our livestock.” 3 So, at the the LORD’s command, the Israelites gave the Levites these towns and their pasturelands from their own inheritance:

4 The first lot fell to the Kohathite clans. The priestly descendants of Aaron received thirteen towns from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin. 5 The remaining Kohathites received ten towns from the clans of Ephraim, Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 6 The descendants of Gershon received thirteen towns from the clans of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan. 7 The descendants of Merari, by their clans, received twelve towns from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. 8 Thus the Israelites gave the Levites these towns and their pasturelands, just as the the LORD had commanded through Moses, by casting lots.

Cities Given to the Priests

9 From the tribes of Judah and Simeon they gave these towns, which are named: 10 to the descendants of Aaron, who belonged to the Kohathite clans of the Levites, because the first lot fell to them: 11 Kiriath Arba—that is, Hebron—with its surrounding pastureland in the hill country of Judah. Arba was the father of Anak. 12 But the fields and villages around the city had been given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession. 13 To the priestly descendants of Aaron they gave Hebron, a city of refuge for the manslayer, together with its pastureland; Libnah and its pastureland; 14 Jattir and its pastureland; Eshtemoa and its pastureland; 15 Holon and its pastureland; Debir and its pastureland; 16 Ain and its pastureland; Juttah and its pastureland; and Beth Shemesh and its pastureland—nine towns from these two tribes. 17 From the tribe of Benjamin they gave Gibeon and its pastureland, Geba and its pastureland, 18 Anathoth and its pastureland, and Almon and its pastureland—four towns. 19 In all, the priests, the descendants of Aaron, received thirteen towns, together with their pasturelands.

Cities for the Remaining Levite Clans

20 The rest of the Kohathite clans among the Levites received towns from the tribe of Ephraim by lot: 21 Shechem, a city of refuge in the hill country of Ephraim, with its pastureland; Gezer and its pastureland; 22 Kibzaim and its pastureland; and Beth Horon and its pastureland—four towns. 23 From the tribe of Dan they received Eltekeh and its pastureland, Gibbethon and its pastureland, 24 Aijalon and its pastureland, and Gath Rimmon and its pastureland—four towns. 25 From the half-tribe of Manasseh they received Taanach and its pastureland and Gath Rimmon and its pastureland—two towns. 26 These ten towns, with their pasturelands, were given to the remaining Kohathite clans.

27 The Gershonite clans among the Levites received from the half-tribe of Manasseh Golan in Bashan, a city of refuge, with its pastureland, and Beeshterah with its pastureland—two towns. 28 From the tribe of Issachar they received Kishion and its pastureland, Daberath and its pastureland, 29 Jarmuth and its pastureland, and En Gannim and its pastureland—four towns. 30 From the tribe of Asher they received Mishal and its pastureland, Abdon and its pastureland, 31 Helkath and its pastureland, and Rehob and its pastureland—four towns. 32 From the tribe of Naphtali they received Kedesh in Galilee, a city of refuge, with its pastureland; Hammoth Dor and its pastureland; and Kartan and its pastureland—three towns. 33 In all, the Gershonite clans received thirteen towns, together with their pasturelands.

34 The Merarite clans, the rest of the Levites, received from the tribe of Zebulun Jokneam and its pastureland, Kartah and its pastureland, 35 Dimnah and its pastureland, and Nahalal and its pastureland—four towns. 36 From the tribe of Reuben they received Bezer and its pastureland, Jahzah and its pastureland, 37 Kedemoth and its pastureland, and Mephaath and its pastureland—four towns. 38 From the tribe of Gad they received Ramoth in Gilead, a city of refuge, with its pastureland; Mahanaim and its pastureland; 39 Heshbon and its pastureland; and Jazer and its pastureland—four towns. 40 In all, twelve towns were allotted to the Merarite clans, the rest of the Levites, together with their pasturelands.

41 The Levites’ cities within the Israelites’ inheritance totaled forty-eight cities, together with their pasturelands. 42 Each of these cities had pasturelands all around it; so it was with all these cities.

The The LORD Fulfills His Promises to Israel

43 Thus the the LORD gave Israel all the land He had sworn to give to their fathers, and they took possession of it and settled in it. 44 The the LORD gave them rest on every side, according to all that He had sworn to their fathers, and none of their enemies could stand against them; the the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand. 45 Not one of all the good promises the the LORD had made to the house of Israel failed; everything came to pass.
