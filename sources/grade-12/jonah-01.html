The LORD Commands Jonah to Go to Nineveh

1 The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai: 2 "Rise and go to Nineveh, that great city, and proclaim against it, for their wickedness has reached My presence."

Jonah's Flight from the LORD

3 But Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the LORD. He went down to Joppa, found a ship bound for Tarshish, paid its fare, and went down into it to sail with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. 4 Then the LORD hurled a violent wind upon the sea, and a mighty tempest arose upon the waters, so that the ship threatened to break apart.

The Storm and the Sailors' Inquiry

5 The sailors grew terrified, and each cried out to his own god. They hurled the ship's cargo into the sea to lighten the vessel. Meanwhile, Jonah had gone down into the innermost hold, lain down, and fallen into a deep sleep. 6 The captain approached him and said, "How can you sleep? Rise and call upon your god! Perhaps that god will be mindful of us, so that we may not perish."

7 Then they said to one another, "Come, let us cast lots, that we may discover on whose account this calamity has befallen us." So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah. 8 Then they said to him, "Please tell us on whose account this calamity has befallen us. What is your occupation? Where have you come from? What is your country? And from what people are you?"

9 He said to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I revere the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land."

10 Then the men were seized with great fear and said to him, "What is this you have done?" For they knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.

Jonah Is Cast into the Sea

11 Then they said to him, "What must we do to you, that the sea may become calm for us?" For the sea was growing ever more tempestuous.

12 He said to them, "Lift me up and throw me into the sea; then the sea will become calm for you, for I know that this great storm has come upon you because of me."

13 Nevertheless, the men rowed strenuously to return the ship to land, but they could not, for the sea grew increasingly tempestuous against them.

14 Therefore they cried out to the LORD and said, "Please, O the LORD, let us not perish on account of this man's life, and do not hold us guilty of innocent blood; for You, O the LORD, have done as You pleased."

15 So they lifted Jonah and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its raging.

16 Then the men feared the LORD greatly, and they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows.

17 And the LORD appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah remained in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.
