The Inheritance Allotted to Joseph's Descendants

1 The lot apportioned to the descendants of Joseph extended from the Jordan at Jericho to the waters of Jericho on the east, traversing the wilderness that ascends from Jericho into the hill country of Bethel. 2 It proceeded from Bethel to Luz, crossed over to the border of the Archites at Ataroth, 3 and descended westward to the border of the Japhletites, as far as the border of Lower Beth-horon and onward to Gezer, terminating at the sea. 4 Thus Ephraim and Manasseh, the sons of Joseph, received their inheritance.

The Territory Assigned to Ephraim

5 The territory of the Ephraimites, according to their clans, extended eastward from Ataroth-addar to Upper Beth-horon. 6 From there the border issued westward: from Michmethath on the north it turned eastward toward Taanath-shiloh, passed by it, and reached Janoah on the east. 7 From Janoah it descended to Ataroth and Naarah, touched Jericho, and emerged at the Jordan.

8 From Tappuah the border ran westward to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the sea. This constituted the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim by its clans, 9 together with all the cities and villages allotted to the Ephraimites within the inheritance of the Manassites.

The Unconquered Canaanites in Gezer

10 Yet they did not dispossess the Canaanites dwelling in Gezer, so the Canaanites remain among the Ephraimites to this day, though they are subjected to forced labor.
