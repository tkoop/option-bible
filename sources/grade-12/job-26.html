Job Rebukes Bildad’s Empty Counsel

1 Then Job answered: 2 “How have you helped the powerless? How have you rescued an arm bereft of strength? 3 How have you counseled one devoid of wisdom, and how have you made sound insight abundantly known? 4 To whom have you uttered words, and whose breath proceeded from you?”

The Power of God in Creation and the Depths

5 “The shades tremble beneath the waters, together with those who dwell there. 6 Sheol lies naked before Him, and Abaddon has no covering. 7 He stretches out the north over empty space and hangs the earth upon nothing. 8 He gathers the waters into His thick clouds, yet the cloud does not burst beneath them. 9 He obscures the face of His throne, spreading His cloud upon it. 10 He has drawn a circle on the face of the waters, at the boundary of light and darkness. 11 The pillars of heaven tremble and are astounded at His rebuke. 12 By His power He quieted the sea, and by His understanding He shattered Rahab. 13 By His Spirit the heavens were adorned, and His hand pierced the fleeing serpent. 14 Behold, these are but the fringes of His ways; what a faint whisper we hear of Him! Yet who can comprehend the thunder of His might?”
