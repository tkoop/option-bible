A Sabbath Song of Thanksgiving and Righteous Flourishing

1 A psalm—a song for the Sabbath. It is good to give thanks to the LORD, and to sing praise to Your name, O Most High— 2 to proclaim Your steadfast love in the morning and Your faithfulness at night, 3 with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre.

4 For You, O the LORD, have made me glad by Your work; I will sing for joy at the works of Your hands. 5 How great are Your works, O the LORD, how very deep are Your thoughts!

6 A brutish man does not know, and a fool does not understand 7 that though the wicked sprout like grass and all evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever.

8 But You, O the LORD, are exalted forever.

9 For indeed, Your enemies, O the LORD—indeed, Your enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered. 10 You have given me the strength of a wild ox, and You have refreshed me with new oil. 11 My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies; my ears have heard the ruin of my wicked foes.

12 The righteous will flourish like a palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. 13 Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God. 14 In old age they will still bear fruit; they will remain fresh and green, 15 to proclaim that the LORD is upright; He is my Rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
