A Song. A Psalm of Asaph.

A Prayer for Deliverance from a Hostile Coalition

1 O God, do not keep silent; do not be quiet, O God, and do not be still. 2 See how Your enemies are in an uproar, and those who hate You have exalted themselves. 3 They devise crafty schemes against Your people and conspire against Your treasured ones.

4 They say, “Come, let us wipe them out as a nation, so that the name of Israel will be remembered no more.”

5 For with one mind they plot together; they form an alliance against You— 6 the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, 7 Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre. 8 Even Assyria has joined them; they lend their strength to the descendants of Lot. Selah

9 Do to them as You did to Midian, as to Sisera and Jabin at the torrent of Kishon, 10 who perished at Endor and became like dung for the ground. 11 Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, 12 who said, “Let us take possession of God’s pastures for ourselves.”

13 Make them like whirling dust, O my God, like chaff before the wind. 14 As fire consumes a forest, as a flame sets the mountains ablaze, 15 so pursue them with Your tempest and terrify them with Your storm.

16 Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek Your name, the LORD. 17 Let them be ashamed and dismayed forever; let them be humiliated and perish. 18 Let them know that You alone—whose name is the LORD—are the Most High over all the earth.
