The Vision of the Measuring Line

1 I lifted my eyes and looked, and behold, a man was standing there with a measuring line in his hand. 2 I asked, "Where are you going?"

He said, "I am going to measure Jerusalem, to find out how wide and how long it is."

3 Then the angel who had been speaking with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him.

4 He said to him, "Run and tell that young man: 'Jerusalem will be inhabited without walls because of the multitude of people and livestock within it.'"

5 "And I will be a wall of fire around her," declares the LORD, "and I will be the glory within her."

A Call to Flee from Babylon

6 "Up! Up! Flee from the land of the north," declares the LORD, "for I have scattered you like the four winds of heaven," declares the LORD. 7 "Up, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the Daughter of Babylon."

8 For thus says the LORD of Hosts: "After His glory has sent Me against the nations that have plundered you—for whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye— 9 I will surely raise My hand against them, so that they will become plunder for their own servants. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me."

The LORD's Return to Dwell Among His People

10 "Shout for joy and be glad, O Daughter of Zion, for behold, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst," declares the LORD.

11 "On that day many nations will join themselves to the LORD and become My people, and I will dwell in your midst. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you."

12 The LORD will take possession of Judah as His portion in the holy land, and He will once again choose Jerusalem.

13 Be silent before the LORD, all people, for He has roused Himself from His holy dwelling.
