The Lord's Intimate Knowledge of His Servant

1 O the LORD, You have scrutinized me and come to know me. 2 You know when I sit and when I rise; You discern my thoughts from afar. 3 You sift my path and my resting place; You are intimately acquainted with all my ways. 4 Before a word takes shape on my tongue, You know it completely, O the LORD. 5 You enclose me behind and before; You have placed Your hand upon me. 6 Such knowledge is too sublime for me, too exalted for me to reach.

The Inescapability of the Divine Presence

7 Where could I go to elude Your Spirit? Where could I flee from Your presence? 8 If I ascend to the heavens, You are there; if I spread my couch in Sheol, You are there. 9 If I take the wings of the dawn and settle at the farthest reaches of the sea, 10 even there Your hand will lead me, and Your right hand will uphold me. 11 If I should say, “Surely darkness will conceal me, and the light around me will become night”— 12 even darkness is not dark to You; the night shines like the day, for darkness and light are alike to You.

The Wonder of Being Formed by God

13 For You formed my innermost being; You wove me together in my mother's womb. 14 I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made; Your works are wondrous, and my soul knows it very well. 15 My frame was not hidden from You when I was fashioned in secret, when I was intricately wrought in the depths of the earth. 16 Your eyes saw my unformed body; all my days were written in Your book and ordained for me before one of them came to be. 17 How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God! How immense is their sum! 18 Were I to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; when I awake, I am still with You.

A Plea for Righteous Judgment and Lasting Guidance

19 O God, if only You would slay the wicked! Depart from me, you men of bloodshed! 20 They speak against You with malicious intent; Your adversaries invoke Your name in vain. 21 Do I not hate those who hate You, O the LORD? Do I not abhor those who rise up against You? 22 I hate them with a consummate hatred; I count them my enemies. 23 Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. 24 See whether any grievous way is in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.
