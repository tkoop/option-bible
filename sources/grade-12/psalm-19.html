The Heavens Declare God's Glory

1The heavens declare the glory of God, and the expanse proclaims the work of His hands. 2Day after day they pour forth speech, and night after night they reveal knowledge. 3They have no speech and no words; their voice is not heard. 4Yet their message goes out through all the earth, and their words reach to the ends of the world. In the heavens He has pitched a tent for the sun—

5like a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, like a champion rejoicing to run his course. 6It rises at one end of the heavens and travels to the other, and nothing is hidden from its heat.

The LORD's Law Is Perfect

7The law of the LORD is perfect, restoring the soul; the testimony of the LORD is trustworthy, making wise the simple. 8The precepts of the LORD are right, bringing joy to the heart; the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes. 9The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring forever; the judgments of the LORD are true and altogether righteous. 10They are more desirable than gold, even much fine gold; they are sweeter than honey, even honey from the comb.

A Prayer for Purity and Acceptance

11Moreover, by them Your servant is warned; in keeping them there is great reward. 12Who can discern his own errors? Cleanse me from hidden faults. 13Keep back Your servant also from presumptuous sins; do not let them rule over me. Then I shall be blameless and innocent of great transgression. 14May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O the LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer.
