A Rebuke of Samaria’s Oppressors

1 Hear this word, you pampered women of Bashan on Mount Samaria, who oppress the poor and crush the needy, who say to your masters, “Bring us something to drink.”

2 The Lord the LORD has sworn by His holiness: “Behold, days are coming upon you when they will take you away with hooks, and your descendants with fishhooks. 3 You will go out through the breaches, each woman straight ahead, and you will be cast out toward Harmon,” declares the LORD.

Empty Worship at Bethel and Gilgal

4 “Come to Bethel and transgress; at Gilgal multiply your transgression; bring your sacrifices in the morning, your tithes every three days. 5 Offer a thanksgiving sacrifice of leavened bread, and proclaim your freewill offerings and make them known; for this is what you love to do, O children of Israel,” declares the Lord the LORD.

The LORD’s Repeated Discipline

6 “I gave you famine in all your cities and a shortage of bread in every place, yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.

7 “I also withheld rain from you when the harvest was still three months away. I sent rain on one city but withheld it from another; one field received rain, while the field that did not receive rain withered. 8 Two or three cities wandered to another city for water but were not satisfied; yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.

9 “I struck you with blight and mildew; the locust devoured your many gardens and vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees; yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.

10 “I sent a plague among you as I did to Egypt; I killed your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses; I filled your nostrils with the stench of your camps; yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.

11 “I overthrew some of you as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a burning stick snatched from the fire; yet you did not return to Me,” declares the LORD.

Prepare to Meet Your God

12 “Therefore, this is what I will do to you, O Israel; because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel.”

13 For behold, He who forms the mountains and creates the wind, who reveals His thoughts to man, who turns the dawn into darkness and treads on the heights of the earth—the LORD, the God of Hosts, is His name.
