The Daily Burnt Offerings

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Command the children of Israel, and say to them, 'My offering, My food for My offerings made by fire, My soothing aroma, you shall be careful to offer to Me at the appointed time.' 3 And say to them, 'This is the fire offering that you shall offer to the the LORD:' 4 Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight, 5 with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter of a hin of pressed oil. 6 It is the continual burnt offering instituted at Mount Sinai, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the the LORD. 7 Its drink offering shall be a quarter of a hin for each lamb; in the holy place pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the the LORD. 8 Offer the second lamb at twilight, with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD.

Sabbath and New Moon Offerings

9 "On the Sabbath day, two male lambs a year old without blemish, with two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and its drink offering. 10 This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the continual burnt offering with its drink offering. 11 At the beginnings of your months, you shall present a burnt offering to the the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old without blemish, 12 with three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each bull, and two-tenths for the ram, 13 and one-tenth of an ephah for each lamb, a grain offering mixed with oil. This is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the the LORD. 14 Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for a ram, and a quarter of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering for each month throughout the months of the year. 15 Also one male goat shall be offered to the the LORD as a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering with its drink offering.

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

16 "On the fourteenth day of the first month is the LORD's Passover. 17 On the fifteenth day of this month is a feast; unleavened bread shall be eaten for seven days. 18 On the first day there shall be a holy convocation; you shall not do any ordinary work. 19 And you shall present a fire offering as a burnt offering to the the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs a year old; they shall be without blemish for you. 20 Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: you shall offer three-tenths of an ephah for a bull, and two-tenths for a ram, 21 and one-tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 22 Also one goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you. 23 You shall offer these besides the continual burnt offering of the morning. 24 In this manner you shall offer the food of the fire offering daily for seven days, a pleasing aroma to the the LORD; it shall be offered besides the continual burnt offering and its drink offering. 25 And on the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not do any ordinary work.

The Feast of Weeks

26 "Also on the day of the firstfruits, when you offer a new grain offering to the the LORD at your Feast of Weeks, you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not do any ordinary work. 27 You shall present a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to the the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs a year old. 28 Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each bull, two-tenths for the ram, 29 and one-tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 30 Also one male goat to make atonement for you. 31 You shall offer them, with their drink offerings, besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering; they shall be without blemish for you."
