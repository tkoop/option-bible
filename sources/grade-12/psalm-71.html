A Prayer for Refuge and Help in Old Age

1 In You, the LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame. 2 In Your righteousness rescue me and deliver me; incline Your ear to me and save me. 3 Be my rock of refuge, to which I may continually come; You have given command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress. 4 Deliver me, my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and cruel.

Confidence from Youth

5 For You are my hope, O Lord, the LORD, my confidence from my youth. 6 Upon You I have leaned from birth; You took me from my mother's womb. My praise is continually of You. 7 I have become a wonder to many, but You are my strong refuge. 8 My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long.

Enemies and Renewed Plea

9 Do not cast me off in the time of old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails. 10 For my enemies speak against me, and those who watch for my life consult together, 11 saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue and seize him, for there is no one to deliver.” 12 O God, do not be far from me; my God, hasten to help me. 13 Let those who accuse my soul be put to shame and consumed; let those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.

Praise and Trust in God's Righteousness

14 But as for me, I will hope continually and will praise You more and more. 15 My mouth will tell of Your righteousness and of Your salvation all day long, for I do not know their full number. 16 I will come in the strength of the Lord the LORD; I will make mention of Your righteousness, Yours alone. 17 O God, You have taught me from my youth, and until now I declare Your wondrous works. 18 And even to old age and gray hair, O God, do not forsake me, until I declare Your strength to this generation, Your power to everyone who is to come.

19 Your righteousness, O God, reaches to the heavens; You who have done great things, O God, who is like You? 20 You who have shown me many and grievous troubles will revive me again; from the depths of the earth You will bring me up again. 21 You will increase my greatness and comfort me on every side. 22 I will also praise You with the harp, my God; I will sing praises to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel. 23 My lips will shout for joy when I sing praises to You, and my soul, which You have redeemed. 24 My tongue also will tell of Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are put to shame and disgraced.
