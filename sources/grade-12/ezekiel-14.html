Idolatry in the Hearts of Israel's Elders

1 Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me. 2 Then the word of the the LORD came to me, saying,

3 “Son of man, these men have brought their idols into their hearts and have set the stumbling block of their iniquity before their faces. Should I allow Myself to be consulted by them at all?

4 “Therefore speak to them and say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the the LORD: Every man of the house of Israel who sets up his idols in his heart and puts the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and then comes to the prophet, I the the LORD will answer him according to the multitude of his idols, 5 so that I may lay hold of the hearts of the house of Israel, because they have all turned away from Me through their idols.’

6 “Therefore say to the house of Israel, ‘Thus says the Lord the the LORD: Repent and turn away from your idols, and turn your faces away from all your abominations.’ 7 For every man of the house of Israel, or of the strangers who sojourn in Israel, who separates himself from Me and sets up his idols in his heart and puts the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and comes to the prophet to inquire of Me concerning himself, I the the LORD will answer him Myself. 8 I will set My face against that man and make him a sign and a byword, and I will cut him off from the midst of My people, and you shall know that I am the the LORD.

9 “And if a prophet is deceived and speaks a word, I the the LORD have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out My hand against him and destroy him from the midst of My people Israel. 10 They shall bear their iniquity; the iniquity of the one who inquires will be the same as the iniquity of the prophet. 11 so that the house of Israel may no longer stray from Me, nor defile themselves with all their transgressions, but that they may be My people and I may be their God, declares the Lord the the LORD.”

Judgment on a Sinful Land

12 The word of the the LORD came to me, saying,

13 “Son of man, when a land sins against Me by trespassing grievously, and I stretch out My hand against it and break its supply of bread and send famine into it and cut off from it both people and animals, 14 even if these three men—Noah, Daniel, and Job—were in it, they could deliver only their own lives by their righteousness, declares the Lord the the LORD.

15 “If I cause wild beasts to pass through the land and they rob it of its children, so that it becomes desolate and no one passes through because of the beasts, 16 as I live, declares the Lord the the LORD, these three men in its midst would not deliver sons or daughters; they alone would be delivered, while the land would become desolate.

17 “Or if I bring a sword upon that land and say, ‘Let a sword pass through the land,’ and I cut off from it both people and animals, 18 as I live, declares the Lord the the LORD, these three men in its midst would not deliver sons or daughters; they alone would be delivered.

19 “Or if I send a pestilence into that land and pour out My wrath upon it in blood, to cut off from it both people and animals, 20 as I live, declares the Lord the the LORD, Noah, Daniel, and Job in its midst would not deliver son or daughter; they, by their righteousness, would deliver only their own lives.

Jerusalem's Four Dreadful Judgments

21 “For thus says the Lord the the LORD: ‘How much more when I send upon Jerusalem My four dreadful judgments—sword, famine, wild beasts, and pestilence—to cut off from it both people and animals.’ 22 Yet behold, survivors will be left in it, sons and daughters who will be brought out; behold, they will come out to you, and you will see their ways and their deeds, and you will be comforted concerning the disaster that I have brought upon Jerusalem, all that I have brought upon it. 23 They will comfort you when you see their ways and their deeds, and you will know that I have not done without cause all that I have done in it, declares the Lord the the LORD.”
