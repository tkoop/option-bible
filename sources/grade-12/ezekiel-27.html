A Lament over Tyre

1 The LORD spoke to me: 2 "And you, son of man, raise a lamentation over Tyre. 3 Say to Tyre, you who dwell at the entrances of the sea, merchant of the peoples to many coastlands, 'Thus says the Lord, the LORD: You, Tyre, have said, "I am perfect in beauty."'"

4 Your borders are in the heart of the seas; your builders have perfected your beauty. 5 They built all your planks of cypress from Senir; they took cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you. 6 Of oaks from Bashan they made your oars; of ivory inlaid with boxwood from the coasts of Kittim they made your deck. 7 Fine embroidered linen from Egypt was your sail, to serve as your banner; blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah were your awning.

8 The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers; your skilled men, Tyre, were your pilots. 9 The elders of Gebal and its skilled men were within you, repairing your seams; all the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you to barter for your merchandise. 10 Men of Persia, Lud, and Put were in your army, your men of war; they hung shield and helmet in you; they gave you splendor. 11 Men of Arvad and your army were on your walls all around, and the Gammadim were in your towers; they hung their shields on your walls all around; they perfected your beauty.

Tyre's Far-Reaching Trade

12 Tarshish traded with you because of your great wealth; for your wares they exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead. 13 Javan, Tubal, and Meshech traded with you; they exchanged human beings and bronze vessels for your merchandise. 14 From Beth-togarmah they exchanged horses, war horses, and mules for your wares. 15 The Dedanites traded with you; many coastlands were your market; they brought you ivory tusks and ebony in payment.

16 Aram traded with you because of your many products; they exchanged turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and agate for your wares. 17 Judah and the land of Israel traded with you; they exchanged wheat of Minnith, meal, honey, oil, and balm for your merchandise. 18 Damascus traded with you because of your many products and your great wealth: wine of Helbon and wool of Zahar. 19 Dan and Javan from Uzal traded wrought iron, cassia, and cane in your market; these were among your merchandise.

20 Dedan traded with you in saddlecloths for riding. 21 Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were your customers; they traded with you in lambs, rams, and goats. 22 The merchants of Sheba and Raamah traded with you; for your wares they exchanged the finest spices, all kinds of precious stones, and gold. 23 Haran, Canneh, and Eden, and the merchants of Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad traded with you. 24 They traded with you in fine garments of blue, embroidered work, and multicolored rugs with cords tightly twisted and knotted.

The Fall of Tyre

25 The ships of Tarshish were the carriers for your merchandise. So you were filled and heavily laden in the heart of the seas. 26 Your rowers have brought you into great waters; the east wind has broken you in the heart of the seas. 27 Your wealth, your wares, your merchandise, your mariners, your pilots, your caulkers, your traders, all your men of war who are in you, and your whole assembly within you will fall into the heart of the seas on the day of your ruin.

28 The countryside will quake at the sound of your pilots' cry. 29 All who handle the oar will abandon their ships; the mariners and all the pilots of the sea will stand on the shore. 30 They will raise their voices over you and cry out bitterly; they will throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes. 31 They will shave their heads for you and put on sackcloth; they will weep for you with anguish of soul and bitter mourning.

32 In their wailing they will take up a lamentation for you and lament over you: "Who is like Tyre, silenced in the midst of the sea?"

33 When your wares went out from the seas, you satisfied many peoples; with your abundant wealth and merchandise you enriched the kings of the earth. 34 Now you are broken by the seas in the depths of the waters; your merchandise and all your company have fallen within you.

35 All the inhabitants of the coastlands are appalled at you; their kings shudder with horror; their faces are contorted. 36 The merchants among the peoples hiss at you; you have become a horror and will be no more forever.
