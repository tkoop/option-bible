1 The word of the LORD came to me:

The Proverb of Inherited Guilt Is Rejected

2 “What do you mean by repeating this proverb in the land of Israel: ‘The parents have sinned, and the children suffer the consequences’?

3 “As surely as I live, declares the Lord, the LORD, you will no longer repeat this proverb in Israel. 4 Indeed, every life belongs to Me; the life of the father and the life of the son alike are Mine. The person who sins is the one who will die.

Righteousness, Wickedness, and Generational Responsibility

5 “Suppose a man is righteous, practicing justice and righteousness— 6 if he does not join in sacrificial meals on the high places or worship the idols of the house of Israel, does not commit adultery with his neighbor’s wife or have sexual relations with a woman during her menstrual period, 7 does not oppress anyone, but returns a debtor’s collateral, commits no robbery, gives bread to the hungry, and clothes the naked, 8 does not lend at interest or take profit, refrains from injustice, and administers true justice between one person and another, 9 and lives by My statutes and faithfully keeps My ordinances—he is righteous; he will certainly live, declares the Lord, the LORD.

10 “Now suppose he fathers a son who is violent and sheds blood, committing any of these acts, although the father himself did none of them. 11 This son eats at the high places, commits adultery with his neighbor’s wife, 12 oppresses the poor and needy, commits robbery, refuses to return collateral, worships idols, and commits abominations, 13 lends at interest and takes profit. Shall such a man live? He will not live. Because he has committed all these abominations, he will certainly die, and his blood will be on his own head.

14 “Now suppose that wicked man has a son who sees all the sins his father committed; he observes them, yet refuses to imitate them. 15 He does not join in sacrificial meals on the high places or worship the idols of Israel, and does not commit adultery with his neighbor’s wife. 16 He does not oppress anyone, demands no collateral, commits no robbery, but gives bread to the hungry and clothes the naked. 17 He refrains from wrongdoing, takes no interest or profit, obeys My ordinances, and lives by My statutes. He will not die for his father’s guilt; he will certainly live. 18 As for his father, because he extorted, robbed his fellow citizen, and did what was evil among his people, he will die for his own iniquity.

19 “Yet you ask, ‘Why should the son not bear the father’s guilt?’ When the son has acted justly and rightly and has carefully observed all My statutes, he will certainly live. 20 The one who sins is the one who dies. A son will not bear his father’s guilt, and a father will not bear his son’s guilt. The righteousness of a righteous person will rest on him, and the wickedness of a wicked person will rest on him.

Repentance and the Promise of Life

21 “But if a wicked person turns away from all the sins he has committed and keeps all My statutes, practicing justice and righteousness, he will certainly live; he will not die. 22 None of the transgressions he committed will be remembered against him; because of the righteousness he has practiced, he will live. 23 Do I take pleasure in the death of the wicked? declares the Lord, the LORD. Is it not rather My desire that he turn from his ways and live? 24 But if a righteous person turns away from his righteousness and commits injustice, doing the same abominations as the wicked, will he live? None of the righteous deeds he has done will be remembered; because of the treachery he has committed and the sin he has sinned, he will die.

25 “Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ Listen, house of Israel: Is My way not just? Are not your ways the ones that are unjust? 26 When a righteous person turns away from his righteousness and dies, he dies because of the iniquity he has committed. 27 And when a wicked person turns away from the wickedness he has committed and practices justice and righteousness, he will preserve his life. 28 Because he considered and turned away from all the transgressions he committed, he will certainly live; he will not die. 29 Yet the house of Israel says, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ House of Israel, are My ways not just? Are not your ways the ones that are unjust?

30 “Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel—each person according to his ways, declares the Lord, the LORD. Repent and turn away from all your transgressions, so that iniquity will not be your downfall. 31 Throw off from yourselves all the transgressions you have committed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why should you die, house of Israel? 32 For I take no pleasure in the death of anyone, declares the Lord, the LORD; therefore turn and live.”
