Paul’s Vision of the Third Heaven and Paradise

1 I must continue boasting, though it confers no benefit; yet I will proceed to visions and revelations from the Lord. 2 I know a man in Christ who, fourteen years ago, was caught up to the third heaven—whether in the body I do not know, or out of the body I do not know; God knows. 3 And I know that this man—whether in the body or apart from the body I do not know; God knows— 4 was caught up into Paradise and heard inexpressible words, utterances that no human being is permitted to speak.

Paul’s Affliction and God’s Sufficient Grace

5 On behalf of such a man I will boast, but on my own behalf I will not boast, except in my weaknesses. 6 For if I were to choose to boast, I would not be acting as a fool, because I would be speaking the truth; nevertheless, I refrain, so that no one may estimate me beyond what he observes in me or hears from me. 7 And because of the extraordinary magnitude of the revelations, a painful affliction was given to me in my body—a messenger of Satan to torment me—lest I should become conceited. 8 Three times I implored the Lord concerning this, that it might depart from me. 9 But He said to me, My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness. Therefore I will boast all the more gladly in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may dwell upon me. 10 For the sake of Christ, I take pleasure in weaknesses, in insults, in hardships, in persecutions, and in distresses; for when I am weak, then I am strong.

Paul’s Vindication of His Apostleship

11 I have become a fool; you compelled me to it, for I ought to have been commended by you. For I was not at all inferior to the super-apostles, even though I am nothing. 12 The signs of a true apostle were accomplished among you with all endurance, by signs and wonders and mighty works. 13 For in what respect were you disadvantaged in comparison with the other churches, except that I myself did not burden you? Forgive me this injustice!

14 Look, I am prepared to come to you a third time, and I will not be a burden to you, for I seek not your possessions but you. For children ought not to accumulate treasures for their parents, but parents for their children. 15 And I will most gladly expend and be expended for your souls. If I love you more abundantly, am I to be loved less? 16 Granted, I did not burden you; nevertheless, being crafty, I caught you by deceit.

17 Did I exploit you through any of those whom I sent to you? 18 I urged Titus to go, and I sent the brother with him. Did Titus exploit you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit? Did we not follow the same course? 19 Do you suppose that all this time we have been defending ourselves to you? We are speaking in the sight of God in Christ, and everything, beloved, is for your edification.

Paul’s Apprehension for the Corinthian Church

20 For I fear that when I arrive I may not find you as I desire, and that you may not find me as you desire; lest there be strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalries, slander, gossip, arrogance, and disorder. 21 I fear that when I come again my God may humble me before you, and that I may grieve over many who sinned previously and have not repented of the impurity, sexual immorality, and licentiousness that they practiced.
