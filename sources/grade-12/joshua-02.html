Joshua Sends Spies to Jericho

1 Then Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two spies from Shittim and told them, "Go and scout the land, especially Jericho." They went and entered the house of a prostitute named Rahab, where they lodged.

2 The king of Jericho was told, "Tonight men from Israel have come here to spy out the land."

3 So the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, for they have come to spy out the whole land."

Rahab Hides the Spies

4 But the woman had already taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from. 5 They left near dusk, just as the gate was about to close, and I do not know where they went. Pursue them quickly, and you may catch them."

6 She had, however, brought them up to the roof and hidden them under stalks of flax that she had arranged on the roof. 7 The pursuers chased them along the road toward the fords of the Jordan, and as soon as the pursuers had gone out, the gate was shut.

Rahab's Plea and the Spies' Oath

8 Before the spies had lain down for the night, she went up to them on the roof.

9 She said to the men, "I know that the LORD has given you this land and that a deep dread of you has fallen on us, so that everyone living in the land is overcome with fear of you. 10 For we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan, whom you devoted to destruction. 11 When we heard these things, our courage failed, and everyone lost heart before you, because the LORD your God is God in heaven above and on the earth below. 12 Now please swear to me by the LORD that, because I have shown kindness to you, you will also show kindness to my father's household. Give me a reliable sign 13 that you will spare my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and all who belong to them, and that you will rescue us from death."

14 The men answered her, "We pledge our lives for your lives! If you do not reveal our mission, we will show you kindness and faithfulness when the LORD gives us the land."

15 Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was built into the city wall; she lived in the wall.

16 She said to them, "Go to the hill country so the pursuers do not find you. Hide there for three days until the pursuers return, and then go on your way."

17 The men said to her, "We will be released from this oath you made us swear 18 unless, when we enter the land, you have tied this scarlet cord in the window through which you lowered us, and have gathered into your house your father, mother, brothers, and all your family. 19 Anyone who goes out the door of your house will bear responsibility for his own death, and we will be innocent. But if anyone with you in the house is harmed, his death will be our responsibility. 20 If you report our mission, however, we will be released from the oath you made us swear."

21 "Agreed," she replied. "Let it be as you say." So she sent them away, and they departed; and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

The Spies Return to Joshua

22 So they went up into the hills and stayed there three days, until the pursuers had searched all along the road and returned without finding them.

23 Then the two men came down from the hills, crossed over, and returned to Joshua son of Nun. They reported to him everything that had happened to them.

24 They said to Joshua, "Surely the LORD has given the whole land into our hands; indeed, everyone living in the land is overcome with fear of us."
