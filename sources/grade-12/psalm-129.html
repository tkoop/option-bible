A Song of Ascents.

Persecution, Deliverance, and Retribution

1 Many a time they have afflicted me from my youth—let Israel now declare— 2 many a time they have afflicted me from my youth, yet they have not prevailed against me.

3 The plowmen plowed over my back; they made their furrows long. 4 The LORD is righteous; He has cut me loose from the cords of the wicked.

5 May all who hate Zion be turned back in shame. 6 May they be like grass on the rooftops, which withers before it can grow, 7 so that no reaper fills his hand, nor any binder of sheaves his arms.

8 And may none who pass by say, “The blessing of the LORD be upon you; we bless you in the name of the LORD.”
