David’s Thirst for God in the Wilderness

1 O God, You are my God; with ardent devotion I seek You. My soul thirsts for You, and my flesh languishes for You in a parched and weary land devoid of water. 2 Thus I have beheld You in the sanctuary, to gaze upon Your power and glory.

3 Because Your loving devotion surpasses life itself, my lips will extol You. 4 Therefore I will bless You throughout my days; in Your name I will lift up my hands.

Satisfaction and Confidence in God

5 My soul shall be sated as with the richest fare, and my mouth shall praise You with exultant lips. 6 When I remember You upon my bed, I meditate upon You through the night watches. 7 For You have been my help, and in the shadow of Your wings I will sing for joy.

8 My soul clings to You; Your right hand sustains me.

The Fate of David’s Enemies and the King’s Joy

9 Yet those who seek my life to destroy it shall descend into the depths of the earth. 10 They will be delivered to the power of the sword; they will become sustenance for jackals. 11 But the king will rejoice in God; all who swear by Him will glory, for the mouths of liars will be silenced.
