The LORD's Second Word to Jeremiah

1 While Jeremiah was still confined in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the LORD came to him a second time:

2 "Thus says the LORD, who made the earth, the LORD who formed it and established it—the LORD is His name—" 3 "Call to Me, and I will answer you, and I will tell you great and hidden things that you do not know."

Restoration After Judgment

4 "For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the houses of this city and the palaces of the kings of Judah that have been torn down to make defenses against the siege ramps and the sword: 5 The Chaldeans are coming to fight and to fill those houses with the dead bodies of the people whom I will strike down in My anger and wrath, because I have hidden My face from this city on account of all its wickedness.

6 "Nevertheless, I will bring health and healing to it; I will heal them and reveal to them an abundance of peace and truth. 7 I will restore Judah and Israel from captivity and rebuild them as they were at first. 8 I will cleanse them from all the iniquity by which they have sinned against Me, and I will pardon all their iniquities by which they have sinned against Me and rebelled against Me. 9 Then this city will bring Me renown, joy, praise, and glory before all the nations of the earth who hear of all the good that I do for them; they will tremble and quake at all the good and all the prosperity that I provide for it."

Joy Returns to the City and the Land

10 "This is what the LORD says: In this place, which you say is a wasteland without man or beast—in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem that are desolate, without inhabitant, without man, and without beast—there will again be heard 11 the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, and the voices of those who bring thank offerings to the house of the LORD, saying, 'Give thanks to the LORD of Hosts, for the LORD is good; His steadfast love endures forever.' For I will restore the land from captivity as it was at first," says the LORD.

12 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: In this desolate place, without man or beast, and in all its cities, there will again be pastures for shepherds resting their flocks. 13 In the cities of the hill country, the cities of the foothills, the cities of the Negev, the land of Benjamin, the outskirts of Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, the flocks will again be counted," says the LORD.

The Davidic Covenant and God's Unbreakable Promises

14 "The days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will fulfill the gracious promise I made concerning the house of Israel and the house of Judah. 15 In those days and at that time I will cause a righteous Branch to sprout for David, and He will execute justice and righteousness in the land. 16 In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will dwell in safety, and this is the name by which it will be called: The LORD Our Righteousness."

17 "For this is what the LORD says: David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel, 18 and the Levitical priests will never lack a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, to burn grain offerings, and to make sacrifices."

19 And the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 20 "This is what the LORD says: If you can break My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night, so that day and night no longer come at their appointed time, 21 then My covenant with David My servant may be broken, and with the Levitical priests My ministers, so that David will not have a descendant to reign on his throne. 22 As the host of heaven cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will multiply the offspring of David My servant and the Levites who minister to Me."

23 And the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 24 "Have you not noticed what these people are saying: 'The LORD has rejected the two families that He chose'? Thus they despise My people and no longer regard them as a nation. 25 This is what the LORD says: If I have not established My covenant with day and night and the fixed order of heaven and earth, 26 then I would reject the descendants of Jacob and of David My servant, and I would not choose a ruler from the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. For I will restore them from captivity and have compassion on them."
