Woe to the Oppressors

1 Woe to those who devise wickedness and plot evil on their beds; at daybreak they carry it out, because power lies in their hands. 2 They covet fields and seize them, and houses, and take them; they oppress a man and his household, a person and his inheritance.

The LORD's Coming Judgment

3 Therefore thus says the LORD: "Behold, I am planning disaster against this family, from which you cannot escape; and you shall not walk haughtily, for it is an evil time. 4 In that day they will take up a taunt against you and chant a bitter lamentation: 'We are utterly ruined! He changes the portion of my people; how He takes it from me! To the faithless He apportions our fields.' 5 Therefore you will have no one in the assembly of the LORD to apportion land by lot."

False Prophets and True Rebuke

6 "Do not prophesy," their prophets say. "Do not prophesy about these things; disgrace will not overtake us."

7 "Should it be said, O house of Jacob, 'Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient? Are these His doings?' Do not My words bring good to him who walks uprightly? 8 But lately My people have risen up as an enemy; you strip the robe from peaceful passersby, from those returning from war. 9 You drive the women of My people from their pleasant homes; you take My splendor from their children forever. 10 Arise and depart, for this is not your resting place, because it is defiled; it brings destruction, even a grievous destruction. 11 If a man, walking in falsehood and lies, says, 'I will prophesy to you of wine and strong drink,' he would be the prophet for this people."

Restoration of the Remnant

12 "I will surely gather all of you, Jacob; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel. I will put them together like sheep in a fold, like a flock in its pasture; it will be noisy with people. 13 The Breaker goes up before them; they break through and pass the gate and go out by it. Their king passes on before them, with the LORD at their head."
