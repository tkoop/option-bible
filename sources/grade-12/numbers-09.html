Observance of the Passover

1 In the first month of the second year after the Israelites had departed from the land of Egypt, the the LORD spoke to Moses in the Wilderness of Sinai, saying, 2 “Command the Israelites to observe the Passover at its appointed time. 3 You are to celebrate it at that appointed time—at twilight on the fourteenth day of this month—in full accordance with all its statutes and ordinances.”

4 Accordingly, Moses instructed the Israelites to observe the Passover, 5 and they did so in the Wilderness of Sinai at twilight on the fourteenth day of the first month. The Israelites carried out everything precisely as the the LORD had commanded Moses.

Provision for a Second Passover

6 Yet certain men had become ceremonially unclean through contact with a human corpse, so they could not observe the Passover on that day. They approached Moses and Aaron that same day, 7 and said to Moses, “Although we are unclean because of a corpse, why should we be deprived of presenting the the LORD’s offering alongside the rest of the Israelites at the appointed time?”

8 Moses answered them, “Wait here while I ascertain what the the LORD commands concerning you.”

9 Then the the LORD said to Moses, 10 “Tell the Israelites that if any one of you or your descendants is unclean because of a corpse, or is away on a distant journey, he may nevertheless observe the Passover to the the LORD. 11 They are to observe it on the fourteenth day of the second month at twilight, eating it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. 12 They must not leave any of it until morning, nor may they break any of its bones. They are to observe the Passover in accordance with all its statutes.

13 “But the man who is ceremonially clean and is not on a journey, yet fails to observe the Passover, must be excluded from his people, because he did not present the the LORD’s offering at its appointed time; that man shall bear the guilt of his sin. 14 If a foreigner residing among you wishes to observe the Passover to the the LORD, he must do so according to the Passover’s statutes and ordinances. You are to have the same statute for the foreigner and for the native of the land.”

Guidance by Cloud and Fire

15 On the day the tabernacle was erected, the cloud covered the tabernacle of the Testimony; from evening until morning it had the appearance of fire above the tabernacle. 16 So it was continually: the cloud covered the tabernacle by day, and by night it appeared like fire. 17 Whenever the cloud was lifted from over the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out; and wherever the cloud settled, there the Israelites would encamp.

18 At the command of the the LORD the Israelites set out, and at the command of the the LORD they encamped. As long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle, they remained in camp. 19 Even when the cloud lingered over the tabernacle for many days, the Israelites kept the the LORD’s charge and did not set out.

20 At times the cloud remained over the tabernacle for only a few days; at the command of the the LORD they would encamp, and at the command of the the LORD they would set out. 21 At times the cloud remained only from evening until morning, and when it lifted in the morning, they set out. Whether by day or by night, whenever the cloud was taken up, they set out.

22 Whether the cloud remained over the tabernacle for two days, a month, or a full year, the Israelites would encamp and not set out; but when it was taken up, they would set out. 23 They encamped at the command of the the LORD, and they set out at the command of the the LORD. They kept the the LORD’s charge, in accordance with the the LORD’s command through Moses.
