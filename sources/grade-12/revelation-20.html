The Angel Binds Satan

1 Then I saw an angel descending from heaven, bearing the key to the Abyss and a great chain in his hand. 2 He seized the dragon—that ancient serpent, who is the devil and Satan—and bound him for a thousand years. 3 He hurled him into the abyss, locked it, and sealed it over him, so that he might no longer deceive the nations until the thousand years were completed; afterward he must be released for a short time.

The Saints Reign with Christ

4 Then I saw thrones, and those seated on them were granted authority to judge. I also saw the souls of those who had been beheaded for their testimony to Jesus and for the word of God—those who had neither worshiped the beast nor its image and had not received its mark on their foreheads or hands. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years. 5 The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years had ended. This is the first resurrection. 6 Blessed and holy is the one who has a share in the first resurrection; over such persons the second death holds no power. On the contrary, they will serve as priests of God and of Christ, and they will reign with Him for a thousand years.

Satan's Final Rebellion and Defeat

7 When the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his confinement. 8 He will emerge to deceive the nations throughout the world—Gog and Magog—in order to assemble them for war, and their number will be like the sand of the sea. 9 They will march across the breadth of the earth and encircle the camp of the saints and the beloved city, but fire will descend from heaven and consume them. 10 Then the devil who had deceived them will be cast into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet are, and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.

The Great White Throne Judgment

11 Then I saw a great white throne and the One enthroned upon it. Earth and heaven fled from His presence, and no place was found for them. 12 I saw the dead, both great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened—the Book of Life—and the dead were judged according to their works, as recorded in the books. 13 The sea surrendered the dead within it, and Death and Hades surrendered the dead within them, and each was judged according to his works. 14 Then Death and Hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death—the lake of fire. 15 Anyone whose name was not found written in the Book of Life was cast into the lake of fire.
