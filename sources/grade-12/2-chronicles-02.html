Solomon's Temple Project

1 Solomon determined to build a temple for the Name of the LORD and a royal palace for himself. 2 He numbered seventy thousand men to carry loads, eighty thousand to quarry stone in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred to supervise them.

Solomon's Correspondence with Huram

3 Solomon sent this message to Huram king of Tyre: "Just as you dealt with my father David, sending him cedar to build a house in which to live, please do the same now. 4 I am building a temple for the Name of the LORD my God, to dedicate it to Him, to burn incense before Him, to maintain the continual bread offering, and to present burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths, at the new moons, and on the festivals of the LORD our God. This is a perpetual ordinance for Israel.

5 "The temple I am building will be great, because our God is greater than every other god. 6 Yet who can truly build a house for Him, when even the heavens—and the highest heavens—cannot contain Him? And who am I, that I should build a house for Him, except to burn incense before Him?

7 "Therefore send me a skilled craftsman who can work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, purple, crimson, and blue yarn, and who knows how to execute engraved designs. He will work with the skilled artisans already with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David appointed. 8 Also send me cedar, cypress, and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know your servants are skilled in cutting Lebanese timber. My servants will work alongside yours. 9 They will prepare timber in abundance for me, because the temple I am building will be great and extraordinary. 10 I will give your servants, the woodcutters, twenty thousand cors of ground wheat, twenty thousand cors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil."

Huram's Response and the Craftsman

11 Huram king of Tyre replied in writing and sent it to Solomon: "Because The LORD has loved His people, He has made you king over them. 12 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, and who has given King David a wise son endowed with discretion and understanding, to build a house for the LORD and a royal palace for himself.

13 "So now I am sending you Huram-abi, a skilled man endowed with understanding. 14 He is the son of a Danite woman and a Tyrian father, and he is skilled in working gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, wood, purple, blue, fine linen, and crimson. He can execute any engraved design and devise whatever plan is assigned to him. He will work with your skilled artisans and with those of my lord David, your father. 15 Now let my lord send his servants the wheat, barley, oil, and wine he mentioned. 16 We will cut as much timber from Lebanon as you need, float it by sea to Joppa, and you can transport it up to Jerusalem."

The Resident Alien Workforce

17 Solomon also took a census of all the foreigners residing in Israel, following the census his father David had taken, and found 153,600. 18 He assigned 70,000 as carriers, 80,000 as stonecutters in the mountains, and 3,600 as supervisors to direct the people's labor.
