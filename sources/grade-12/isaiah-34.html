The LORD’s Judgment on the Nations

1 Come near, you nations, to listen; pay attention, you peoples! Let the earth hear, along with all that fills it—the world and everything that springs from it. 2 The LORD is angry with all the nations and furious with all their armies. He will devote them to destruction; He will give them over to slaughter. 3 Their slain will be left unburied, and the stench of their corpses will rise; the mountains will be drenched with their blood. 4 All the host of heaven will rot away, and the skies will be rolled up like a scroll. All their stars will wither, like a leaf withering on the vine, like foliage on a fig tree.

The LORD’s Sword Against Edom

5 When My sword has drunk its fill in the heavens, then it will come down on Edom, on the people I have devoted to destruction, for judgment. 6 The LORD’s sword is bathed in blood; it is covered with fat—the blood of lambs and goats, the fat of the kidneys of rams. For the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah, a great slaughter in the land of Edom. 7 And the wild oxen will fall with them, the young bulls with the mighty bulls. Their land will be drenched with blood, and their dust will be fattened with fat. 8 For the LORD has a day of vengeance, a year of recompense for the cause of Zion.

The Desolation of Edom

9 Her streams will be turned to pitch, and her dust to sulfur; her land will become burning pitch. 10 It will not be quenched night or day; its smoke will rise forever. From generation to generation it will lie desolate; no one will pass through it forever and ever. 11 The pelican and the porcupine will possess it, and the owl and the raven will dwell in it. He will stretch over it the measuring line of chaos and the plumb line of destruction. 12 No nobles will be left to proclaim a kingdom, and all her princes will come to nothing. 13 Thorns will overrun her palaces, nettles and thistles her fortresses. She will become a haunt of jackals, an abode for ostriches. 14 The desert creatures will meet with the hyenas, and one wild goat will cry to another; there too the night creature will settle and find a resting place. 15 There the owl will make her nest, lay eggs, hatch them, and gather her young under her shadow. Yes, the kites will be gathered there, each with its mate.

The LORD’s Decree Fulfilled

16 Search the book of the LORD and read: not one of these will be missing; none will lack her mate. For His mouth has commanded, and His Spirit has gathered them. 17 He has allotted their portions, and His hand has divided it among them. They will possess it forever; from generation to generation they will dwell there.
