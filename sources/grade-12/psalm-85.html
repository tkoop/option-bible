A Prayer for Restoration and Peace

1 You, the LORD, have shown favor to Your land; You have restored the fortunes of Jacob. 2 You have taken away the iniquity of Your people; You have covered all their sins. Selah. 3 You have withdrawn all Your wrath; You have turned from the fierceness of Your anger.

4 Restore us, God of our salvation, and put away Your displeasure toward us. 5 Will You be angry with us forever? Will You prolong Your anger through every generation? 6 Will You not revive us again, so that Your people may rejoice in You? 7 Show us Your unfailing love, the LORD, and grant us Your salvation.

8 I will listen to what God the LORD says, for He will speak peace to His people and to His faithful servants—but let them not return to folly. 9 Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that His glory may dwell in our land.

10 Loving devotion and truth have met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other. 11 Truth springs up from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven. 12 Indeed, the LORD will give what is good, and our land will yield its harvest. 13 Righteousness will go before Him and prepare the way for His steps.
