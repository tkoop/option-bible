A Prayer for Guarded Speech and Deliverance

1 I call upon You, the LORD; hasten to me. Hear my voice when I call to You. 2 May my prayer be established before You like incense, and my uplifted hands like the evening offering.

3 Set a guard, the LORD, over my mouth; keep watch over the door of my lips. 4 Do not let my heart incline toward any evil, or permit me to participate in wicked deeds with evildoers; do not let me indulge in their luxuries. 5 Let a righteous person strike me—that is kindness; let him rebuke me—that is a refreshing favor, which I will not reject. Yet my prayer remains against the deeds of the wicked.

6 Their judges are hurled from the cliffs, and they will hear that my words are pleasing. 7 As one plows and breaks up the earth, so our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol. 8 But my eyes are fixed on You, the LORD, my Sovereign; in You I take refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless.

9 Keep me from the snares they have laid for me, and from the traps of evildoers. 10 Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I pass by in safety.
