A Cry in Times of Trouble

1 Why do You, the LORD, stand so far off? Why do You hide Yourself in times of trouble?

The Arrogance and Predation of the Wicked

2 In the wicked man's insolence, the poor are hunted relentlessly; may he be ensnared by the very stratagems he has conceived. 3 Indeed, he exults in his own cravings, commends greed, and treats the LORD with contempt. 4 In his overweening pride, he refuses to seek God, and every design he entertains assumes that God is absent.

5 His undertakings prosper at all times; Your judgments are loftier than his vision can reach; he sneers at every adversary. 6 He assures himself, "I shall never be shaken; age after age, I shall remain untouched by calamity." 7 His speech brims with curses, deception, and violence; mischief and wickedness lurk beneath his words.

8 He lurks in ambush near the settlements; in concealed places he murders the innocent; his eyes search furtively for the vulnerable. 9 He crouches in ambush like a lion in its cover; he lurks to capture the destitute, and he drags them into his snare. 10 The vulnerable are crushed; they sink down and fall beneath his power. 11 He tells himself, "God has forgotten; He has turned away His face; He will never notice."

A Plea for the LORD's Justice

12 Rise up, the LORD; lift up Your hand, O God; do not forget the afflicted. 13 Why does the wicked man spurn God and tell himself, "You will never call me to account"? 14 Yet You do see, for You observe trouble and vexation, in order to take them into Your hands; the helpless entrusts himself to You; You have been the protector of the fatherless. 15 Shatter the strength of the wicked and the evildoer; pursue his wickedness until You have found nothing left of it.

The LORD's Everlasting Reign

16 The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land. 17 You hear, the LORD, the longing of the afflicted; You will strengthen their hearts; You will incline Your ear. 18 to vindicate the fatherless and the oppressed, so that earthly man may no longer inspire terror.
