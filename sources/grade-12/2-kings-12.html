Jehoash's Reign and the Temple Repairs

1 In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zibiah of Beersheba. 2 Jehoash did what was right in the eyes of the LORD throughout all his days, because Jehoiada the priest had instructed him. 3 Nevertheless, the high places were not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places.

4 Jehoash said to the priests, “All the silver brought into the house of the LORD as sacred gifts—the silver assessed for each person, and all the silver that anyone willingly brings to the house of the LORD— 5 let the priests take it, each from his associate, and use it to repair every breach found in the house of the LORD.”

6 But by the twenty-third year of King Jehoash, the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house of the LORD. 7 So King Jehoash summoned Jehoiada the priest and the other priests and asked them, “Why have you not repaired the breaches of the house of the LORD? Now take no more silver from your associates; instead, hand it over for the repair of the house of the LORD.” 8 The priests agreed to receive no more silver from the people and to stop repairing the house themselves.

9 Then Jehoiada the priest took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar on the right side as one enters the house of the LORD. The priests who guarded the threshold put into it all the silver brought into the house of the LORD. 10 Whenever they saw that the chest held a large amount of silver, the king’s secretary and the high priest would come, count the silver found in the house of the LORD, and put it in bags. 11 They then gave the weighed silver to those supervising the work on the house of the LORD, and these men paid it to the carpenters and builders working on the house of the LORD, 12 as well as to the masons and stonecutters. They also bought timber and dressed stone to repair the breaches of the house of the LORD, and they funded everything else needed to strengthen the house.

13 However, no silver cups, snuffers, basins, trumpets, or any gold or silver vessels were made for the house of the LORD from the money brought into the house of the LORD. 14 Instead, they gave it to the workmen, who used it to repair the house of the LORD. 15 Moreover, they did not require an accounting from the men into whose hands they entrusted the silver to pay the workmen, because those men acted faithfully. 16 The silver from guilt offerings and sin offerings was not brought into the house of the LORD; it belonged to the priests.

Hazael's Campaign and Jehoash's Tribute

17 Then Hazael king of Aram marched up, fought against Gath, and captured it. Afterward, Hazael turned his forces toward Jerusalem. 18 King Jehoash of Judah took all the sacred objects that his ancestors Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, kings of Judah, had dedicated, along with his own sacred objects, and all the gold found in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and the royal palace, and sent them to Hazael king of Aram; so Hazael withdrew from Jerusalem. 19 As for the rest of the acts of Joash and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

The Assassination of Jehoash

20 His servants conspired against him and struck down Joash at the house of Millo, on the road that descends to Silla. 21 Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the City of David, and his son Amaziah reigned in his place.
