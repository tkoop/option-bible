A Solemn Charge to Preach the Word

1 I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is about to judge the living and the dead, and by His appearing and His kingdom: 2 preach the word; be prepared whether the occasion is favorable or not; reprove, rebuke, and exhort with complete patience and instruction. 3 For the time will come when people will not tolerate sound teaching; instead, because their ears crave novelty, they will gather around themselves teachers to suit their own passions. 4 They will turn their ears away from the truth and wander off into myths. 5 But you, remain sober-minded in all things, endure hardship, do an evangelist's work, and fully discharge your ministry.

Paul's Impending Departure

6 For I am already being poured out like a libation, and the time of my departure is at hand. 7 I have fought the good fight; I have finished the race; I have kept the faith. 8 Henceforth the crown of righteousness is reserved for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day—and not to me alone, but to all who have loved His appearing.

Final Requests and Warnings

9 Do your utmost to come to me quickly, 10 for Demas, in love with the present age, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica; Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia. 11 Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me in service. 12 I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus. 13 When you come, bring the cloak I left with Carpus in Troas, along with the books—especially the parchments.

14 Alexander the coppersmith caused me great harm; the Lord will repay him according to his deeds. 15 You too must be on guard against him, for he strongly opposed our message. 16 At my first defense no one stood by me; everyone deserted me. May it not be held against them. 17 But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the message might be fully proclaimed and all the Gentiles might hear it; and I was rescued from mortal danger. 18 The Lord will rescue me from every evil work and bring me safely into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be glory forever and ever. Amen.

Final Greetings and Blessing

19 Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus. 20 Erastus stayed in Corinth, and I left Trophimus ill in Miletus. 21 Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers greet you. 22 The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with all of you.
