Joshua Sends the Eastern Tribes Home

1 Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

2 He said to them, “You have kept all that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, commanded you, and you have obeyed my voice in everything I commanded you. 3 For many days you have not abandoned your brothers until this day, and you have kept the charge of the commandment of the the LORD your God. 4 And now the the LORD your God has given rest to your brothers, as He promised them. Therefore turn and go to your tents, to the land of your possession, which Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave you beyond the Jordan. 5 Only be very careful to observe the commandment and the law that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, commanded you: to love the the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments, to cling to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and all your soul.”

6 Then Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents. 7 Now to the half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had given an inheritance in Bashan, but to the other half Joshua gave land west of the Jordan, along with their brothers. When Joshua sent them home, he blessed them.

8 He said to them, “Return to your homes with your great wealth—with very large herds, with silver, gold, bronze, and iron, and with a great quantity of clothing. Divide with your brothers the spoil of your enemies.”

9 So the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned and left the Israelites at Shiloh in Canaan to go to Gilead, the land of their possession, which they had acquired according to the word of the the LORD through Moses.

An Altar by the Jordan Provokes Alarm

10 When they reached the districts of the Jordan in Canaan, the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan, an altar impressive in size. 11 The Israelites heard that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had built an altar on the frontier of Canaan, in the districts of the Jordan, on the Israelite side. 12 When the whole congregation of Israelites heard this, they assembled at Shiloh to march against them.

Phinehas Confronts the Eastern Tribes

13 The Israelites sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to Gilead, to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 14 With him went ten leaders, one from each ancestral house of the tribes of Israel; each was the head of his ancestral house among the thousands of Israel.

15 They came to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead and spoke to them: 16 “Thus says the whole congregation of the the LORD: What is this treachery you have committed today against the God of Israel by turning away from following the the LORD, by building an altar for yourselves, so that you might rebel today against the the LORD? 17 Is the iniquity of Peor not enough for us, from which we have not cleansed ourselves to this day, although a plague came upon the congregation of the the LORD? 18 Yet today you turn away from following the the LORD. If you rebel today against the the LORD, tomorrow He will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel. 19 If the land of your possession is unclean, then cross over to the land of the possession of the the LORD, where the the LORD’s tabernacle dwells, and take possession among us. But do not rebel against the the LORD, and do not rebel against us by building an altar for yourselves apart from the altar of the the LORD our God. 20 Did not Achan son of Zerah commit a trespass in the devoted thing, and wrath fell on the whole congregation of Israel? He did not perish alone for his iniquity.”

21 Then the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the heads of the clans of Israel: 22 “The the LORD God of gods—the the LORD God of gods—He knows, and Israel will know: if it was in rebellion or in trespass against the the LORD, do not save us today. 23 If we have built this altar to turn away from following the the LORD, or to offer burnt offerings, grain offerings, or peace offerings on it, may the the LORD Himself require it. 24 No, we did this out of concern, from fear that in time to come your children might say to our children, ‘What have you to do with the the LORD, the God of Israel?’ 25 For the the LORD has made the Jordan a border between us and you, Reubenites and Gadites. You have no portion in the the LORD, so your children might make our children cease from fearing the the LORD. 26 Therefore we said, ‘Let us now build an altar, not for burnt offering or sacrifice.’ 27 But it shall be a witness between us and you and our generations after us, that we may perform the service of the the LORD before Him with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices, and our peace offerings, so that your children will not say to our children in time to come, ‘You have no portion in the the LORD.’ 28 Therefore we said that, when in time to come they say this to us or to our generations, we will answer, ‘Look at the pattern of the altar of the the LORD that our fathers made—not for burnt offering or sacrifice, but as a witness between us and you.’ 29 Far be it from us to rebel against the the LORD and turn away today from following the the LORD by building an altar for burnt offering, grain offering, or sacrifice, besides the altar of the the LORD our God that stands before His tabernacle.”

Peace Is Restored

30 Phinehas the priest, the leaders of the congregation, and the heads of Israel with him heard the words that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites had spoken, and it pleased them. 31 Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites, “Today we know that the the LORD is among us, because you have not committed this trespass against the the LORD. Now you have delivered the Israelites from the hand of the the LORD.” 32 Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the Reubenites and Gadites in Gilead to Canaan, to the Israelites, and brought back a report. 33 The matter pleased the Israelites, and they blessed God and spoke no more of going up against them in war to destroy the land where the Reubenites and Gadites lived.

34 The Reubenites and Gadites called the altar Witness, for they said, “It is a witness between us that the the LORD is God.”
