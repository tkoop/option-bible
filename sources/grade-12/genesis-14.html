The Kings' War in the Valley of Siddim

1 During the reigns of Amraphel, king of Shinar; Arioch, king of Ellasar; Chedorlaomer, king of Elam; and Tidal, king of Goiim, 2 these monarchs waged war against Bera, king of Sodom; Birsha, king of Gomorrah; Shinab, king of Admah; Shemeber, king of Zeboiim; and the king of Bela—that is, Zoar. 3 All these allied forces converged in the Valley of Siddim, which is now the Salt Sea. 4 For twelve years they had been subject to Chedorlaomer, but in the thirteenth year they revolted.

5 In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings allied with him invaded and struck down the Rephaim at Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim at Ham, the Emim at Shaveh-kiriathaim, 6 and the Horites throughout the hill country of Seir, extending to El-paran, which borders the wilderness. 7 Then they reversed course and came to En-mishpat—that is, Kadesh—where they struck the whole territory of the Amalekites and also the Amorites dwelling at Hazazon-tamar.

8 Thereupon the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela—that is, Zoar—marched out and arrayed themselves for battle against them in the Valley of Siddim: 9 Chedorlaomer, king of Elam; Tidal, king of Goiim; Amraphel, king of Shinar; and Arioch, king of Ellasar—four kings against five. 10 Now the Valley of Siddim was replete with tar pits; the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, and some perished in them, while the rest escaped to the hills.

11 The invaders seized all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah, along with all their provisions, and withdrew. 12 They also abducted Lot, Abram's nephew, who resided in Sodom, together with his possessions, and withdrew.

Abram Rescues Lot

13 Then a fugitive arrived and reported to Abram the Hebrew, who was dwelling among the oaks of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and the brother of Aner; these men were Abram's confederates. 14 Upon hearing that his kinsman had been captured, Abram mobilized his three hundred eighteen trained men, born in his household, and pursued the captors as far as Dan.

15 He divided his forces against them under cover of night—he and his servants—and routed them, pursuing them to Hobah, north of Damascus. 16 He recovered all the goods and restored his kinsman Lot, with his possessions, together with the women and the other captives.

Melchizedek Blesses Abram

17 After Abram returned from defeating Chedorlaomer and the kings allied with him, the king of Sodom came out to meet him in the Valley of Shaveh—that is, the King's Valley. 18 Melchizedek, king of Salem, presented bread and wine; he was priest of God Most High.

19 And he blessed him, saying, “Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth, 20 and blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies into your hand.” Then Abram gave him a tenth of all.

Abram Refuses the King of Sodom's Offer

21 The king of Sodom said to Abram, “Give me the people, and keep the goods for yourself.”

22 But Abram replied to the king of Sodom, “I have sworn an oath to the LORD, God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth, 23 that I will not take so much as a thread or a sandal strap from anything that is yours, lest you should say, ‘I have made Abram rich.’ 24 I will accept nothing except what the young men have eaten and the portion due to the men who accompanied me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them receive their share.”
