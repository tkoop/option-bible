Esther Approaches the King

1 On the third day, Esther put on her royal robes and stood in the inner courtyard of the king's palace, directly opposite the king's residence. The king was seated on his royal throne in the hall of the palace, facing the entrance.

2 When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the courtyard, she won his favor, and he extended the golden scepter in his hand toward her. Esther approached and touched the tip of the scepter.

3 The king asked her, "What is it, Queen Esther? What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom will be given to you."

The Banquet and the Request

4 Esther replied, "If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come today to the banquet I have prepared for him."

5 The king said, "Bring Haman quickly, so that we may carry out Esther's request." So the king and Haman went to the banquet Esther had prepared.

6 At the banquet of wine, the king said to Esther, "What is your petition? It will be granted. What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be done."

7 8 Esther answered, "My petition and my request are these: if I have found favor in your sight, O king, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and fulfill my request, then let the king and Haman come tomorrow to the banquet I will prepare for them, and I will do as the king has said."

Haman's Pride and Fury

9 Haman left that day joyful and glad of heart. But when he saw Mordecai at the king's gate refusing to stand or tremble before him, he was filled with rage against Mordecai.

10 Nevertheless, Haman restrained himself and went home. He sent for his friends and his wife Zeresh.

11 12 Haman boasted to them about his glorious wealth, his many sons, and all the honors the king had bestowed on him, including his promotion above the officials and servants of the king. He added, "Even Queen Esther invited no one but me to accompany the king to the banquet she prepared, and tomorrow I am invited again with the king."

13 "Yet all of this means nothing to me," he said, "as long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king's gate."

14 His wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Have a gallows built, seventy-five feet high, and in the morning ask the king to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then go to the banquet with the king in good spirits." This advice pleased Haman, so he had the gallows constructed.
