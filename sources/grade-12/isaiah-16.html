Moab’s Tribute and Appeal for Refuge

1 Send the lamb to the ruler of the land, from Sela, by way of the desert, to the mount of Daughter Zion. 2 Like fluttering birds driven from the nest, so the daughters of Moab are at the fords of the Arnon. 3 Give counsel, render justice; make your shadow like night at noonday; hide the outcasts; do not betray the wanderer. 4 Let My outcasts dwell with you; be a refuge for Moab from the destroyer. For the oppressor is at an end, destruction has ceased, and the oppressors have vanished from the land. 5 A throne will be established in steadfast love, and in the tent of David one will sit on it in faithfulness—a judge who seeks justice and is swift to do righteousness.

Moab’s Pride and the Prophet’s Lament

6 We have heard of the pride of Moab—how proud he is!—of his arrogance, his pride, and his wrath; but his boasting is false. 7 Therefore Moab will wail for Moab; all of Moab will moan. You will mourn for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth, utterly stricken. 8 For the fields of Heshbon languish, and the vine of Sibmah; the rulers of the nations have trampled its choicest vines, which reached as far as Jazer and strayed toward the desert. Its shoots spread abroad and passed over the sea. 9 Therefore I weep with the weeping of Jazer for the vine of Sibmah; I drench you with my tears, O Heshbon and Elealeh, for the shouts of joy have fallen silent over your summer fruit and your harvest. 10 Joy and gladness are removed from the fruitful field; in the vineyards no one sings or shouts; no treader treads out wine in the presses; I have put an end to the shouting. 11 Therefore my heart laments for Moab like a harp, my inmost being for Kir-hareseth. 12 And when Moab presents himself, when he wearies himself on the high place, and when he comes to his sanctuary to pray, he will not prevail.

The LORD’s Verdict on Moab

13 This is the word that the LORD has spoken concerning Moab in the past. 14 But now the LORD has spoken, saying, “Within three years, as a hired worker counts the years, Moab’s splendor and all her great multitude will be despised, and her remnant will be very small and feeble.”
