David's Desire to Build a Temple

1 After the king was settled in his house and the LORD had given him rest from all his surrounding enemies, 2 he said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of God remains in a tent." 3 Nathan replied to the king, "Go and do everything that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you."

The LORD's Covenant with David

4 That night the word of the LORD came to Nathan: 5 "Go and say to My servant David, 'Will you build Me a house in which to dwell? 6 From the day I brought the Israelites up from Egypt until now, I have not lived in a house; rather, I have moved with them in a tent and a tabernacle. 7 Wherever I traveled with all Israel, did I ever ask any of the tribes I commanded to shepherd My people Israel why they had not built Me a cedar house?'"

8 "Now then, say this to My servant David: 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be ruler over My people Israel. 9 I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies before you. I will make your name great, like the names of the greatest on earth. 10 I will provide a place for My people Israel and plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more. Wicked people will no longer oppress them, as they did at the beginning, 11 from the time I appointed judges over My people Israel. I will give you rest from all your enemies. The LORD also declares to you that He will make a house for you.'"

12 "When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, who will come from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom. 13 He will build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever. 14 I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me. When he sins, I will discipline him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of men. 15 But My steadfast love will not depart from him, as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed from before you. 16 Your house and your kingdom will endure forever before you; your throne will be established forever."

17 Nathan told David all these words and this entire vision.

David's Prayer of Thanksgiving

18 Then King David went in and sat before the LORD and said, "Who am I, Lord, the LORD, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far? 19 And yet this was a small thing in your sight, Lord, the LORD; you have also spoken about the future of your servant's house. Is this how you deal with mankind, Lord, the LORD? 20 What more can David say to you? You know your servant, Lord, the LORD. 21 For your word's sake and according to your own heart, you have done all this greatness to make your servant know it.

22 "Therefore you are great, Lord, the LORD; for there is none like you, and there is no God besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears. 23 And who is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth whom God went out to redeem for Himself as a people, to make a name for Himself, and to do for them great and awesome things by driving out nations and their gods before your people, whom you redeemed from Egypt? 24 You established your people Israel for yourself forever, and you, the LORD, became their God.

25 "Now, the LORD God, confirm forever the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and his house, and do as you have spoken. 26 Let your name be magnified forever, saying, 'The the LORD of hosts is God over Israel,' and let the house of your servant David be established before you. 27 For you, the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, have revealed to your servant, saying, 'I will build you a house.' Therefore your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to you.

28 "And now, Lord, the LORD, you are God, and your words are true, and you have promised this good thing to your servant. 29 Now therefore be pleased to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue forever before you; for you, Lord, the LORD, have spoken, and with your blessing the house of your servant will be blessed forever."
