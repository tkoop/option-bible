A Psalm of David: A Song for the Dedication of the House

1 I will extol You, O the LORD, for You have lifted me up and have not allowed my enemies to gloat over me. 2 O the LORD my God, I cried to You, and You healed me. 3 O the LORD, You brought my soul up from Sheol; You preserved my life from among those going down to the pit.

4 Sing to the LORD, you His faithful ones, and give thanks to His holy name. 5 For His anger lasts but a moment, while His favor lasts a lifetime; weeping may lodge for the night, but joy comes with the morning.

6 In my prosperity I said, “I shall never be shaken.” 7 O the LORD, by Your favor You made my mountain stand firm; You hid Your face, and I was dismayed.

8 I cried to You, O the LORD; I sought mercy from my Lord: 9 “What profit is there in my death if I go down to the pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it declare Your faithfulness?”

10 Hear, O the LORD, and be gracious to me; O the LORD, be my helper. 11 You have turned my mourning into dancing; You have removed my sackcloth and clothed me with gladness, 12 so that my whole being may sing Your praise and never be silent. O the LORD my God, I will give thanks to You forever.
