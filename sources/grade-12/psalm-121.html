A Song of Ascents: The LORD Our Protector

1 I lift my eyes toward the mountains and wonder where my help will come from. 2 My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

3 He will not allow your foot to slip, and your Protector will not slumber. 4 Behold, the Protector of Israel neither slumbers nor sleeps.

5 The LORD is your Protector; the LORD is the shade at your right hand. 6 The sun will not strike you by day, nor will the moon by night.

7 The LORD will protect you from every evil; He will preserve your life. 8 The LORD will watch over your going out and your coming in, from this time forth and forevermore.
