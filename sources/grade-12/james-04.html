The Source of Conflict

1 From what source do wars and quarrels arise among you? Do they not originate from the passions that wage war within your members? 2 You desire and lack; you murder and covet, yet you cannot obtain; you contend and make war. You have not because you do not ask. 3 You ask and do not receive, because you ask with corrupt motives, intending to squander it on your pleasures.

Friendship with the World

4 You adulteresses! Do you not know that friendship with the world constitutes enmity toward God? Consequently, whoever determines to be a friend of the world renders himself an enemy of God. 5 Or do you suppose that Scripture speaks emptily when it says, "The Spirit whom He caused to dwell in us yearns jealously"? 6 Yet He bestows greater grace; therefore it declares, "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble."

Humble Submission to God

7 Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. 8 Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Purify your hands, you sinners, and cleanse your hearts, you double-minded. 9 Lament, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be transformed into mourning and your joy into dejection. 10 Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will exalt you.

Judging and Boasting

11 Brothers, do not speak evil against one another. Whoever speaks evil of a brother or judges his brother speaks evil of the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a practitioner of the law but a judge. 12 There is a single Lawgiver and Judge, who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor? 13 Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will journey to this or that city, remain there a year, trade, and turn a profit"— 14 you who do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? You are a vapor that appears briefly and then dissipates. 15 Instead, you should say, "If the Lord wills, we will live and undertake this or that." 16 But as it is, you boast in your arrogance; all such boasting is evil. 17 Therefore, for the one who knows the good he ought to perform and fails to perform it, it is sin.
