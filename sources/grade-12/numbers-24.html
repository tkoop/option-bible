Balaam’s Final Blessing of Israel

1 When Balaam saw that it pleased the LORD to bless Israel, he did not resort to sorcery as he had on previous occasions, but turned his face toward the wilderness. 2 Balaam lifted his eyes and saw Israel encamped tribe by tribe, and the Spirit of God came upon him.

3 He lifted up an oracle and said: “This is the oracle of Balaam son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eyes are open— 4 the oracle of one who hears the words of God, who sees a vision from the Almighty, who falls prostrate with eyes uncovered: 5 How beautiful are your tents, O Jacob, your tabernacles, O Israel! 6 They spread out like valleys, like gardens beside a river, like aloes the LORD has planted, like cedars beside the waters. 7 Water will flow from their buckets, and their seed will have abundant water. Their king will be greater than Agag, and their kingdom will be exalted. 8 God brought them out of Egypt; they have the strength of a wild ox. They devour hostile nations and crush their bones; they pierce them with arrows. 9 They crouch and lie down like a lion, like a lioness—who dares to rouse them? Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you!”

Balak’s Anger and Balaam’s Refusal

10 Then Balak’s anger burned against Balaam, and he struck his hands together. Balak said to Balaam, “I summoned you to curse my enemies, but you have persisted in blessing them these three times! 11 Now get up and go home! I said I would richly reward you, but the LORD has withheld your reward.”

12 Balaam answered Balak, “Did I not tell the messengers you sent me: 13 ‘Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the command of the LORD to do good or evil of my own accord; I must speak only what the LORD says’? 14 Now I am returning to my people, but come, let me warn you what this people will do to your people in the days to come.”

The Oracle of the Star and Scepter

15 Then he lifted up an oracle and said: “This is the oracle of Balaam son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eyes are open— 16 the oracle of one who hears the words of God and knows the knowledge of the Most High, who sees a vision from the Almighty, who falls prostrate with eyes uncovered: 17 I see him, but not now; I behold him, but not near. A star will come forth from Jacob, and a scepter will arise from Israel. He will crush the foreheads of Moab and destroy all the sons of Sheth. 18 Edom will become a possession; Seir, his enemies, will also become a possession; but Israel will perform valiantly. 19 One from Jacob will rule and destroy the survivors of the city.”

Oracles Concerning the Nations

20 Then he looked at Amalek and lifted up an oracle, saying: “Amalek was first among the nations, but his end will be destruction.” 21 Then he looked at the Kenites and lifted up an oracle, saying: “Your dwelling place is secure, and your nest is set in a cliff. 22 Yet Kain will be destroyed when Asshur takes you captive.” 23 Then he lifted up an oracle and said: “Alas, who can live when God does this? 24 Ships will come from the coast of Kittim; they will subdue Asshur and Eber, but they too will perish.”

25 Then Balaam arose and departed, returning to his home, and Balak also went his own way.
