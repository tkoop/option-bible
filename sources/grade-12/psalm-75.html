For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.

Thanksgiving and God’s Sovereign Judgment

1 We give thanks to You, O God; we give thanks, for Your name is near. People declare Your wonders.

2 “When I choose an appointed time, I will judge with equity. 3 When the earth and all its inhabitants tremble, I am the One who holds up its pillars. Selah. 4 I say to the proud, ‘Do not boast,’ and to the wicked, ‘Do not exalt yourselves. 5 Do not exalt yourselves on high, nor speak arrogantly.’”

6 For exaltation comes not from the east or the west, nor from the wilderness, 7 but from God, who judges; He lowers one and exalts another. 8 For a cup is in the hand of the LORD, full of foaming wine mixed with spices. He pours it out, and all the wicked of the earth will drink it down to the dregs. 9 But I will proclaim forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

10 “I will cut off all the power of the wicked, but the righteous will be exalted.”
