Wisdom and the Fear of God

1 Who is like the wise man, and who understands the interpretation of a matter? A man’s wisdom makes his face shine, and the sternness of his face is changed.

2 Keep the king’s command, I say, because of your oath before God. 3 Do not be hasty to leave his presence, and do not persist in an evil matter, for he does whatever he pleases. 4 Since a king’s word is supreme, who can say to him, “What are you doing?”

5 Whoever keeps a command will experience no harm, and a wise heart will know the proper time and procedure. 6 For there is a proper time and procedure for every matter, though a man’s misery weighs heavily upon him. 7 Since no one knows what will happen, who can tell him what is to come?

Human Limitations and Injustice

8 As no one has power over the wind to contain it, so no one has power over the day of death. There is no discharge from that war, nor will wickedness deliver those who practice it. 9 All this I have seen, and I applied my heart to every deed that is done under the sun, at a time when one man has power over another to his harm.

10 Then I saw the wicked buried—those who used to come and go from the holy place and received praise in the city where they did this. This too is futility. 11 When the sentence for a crime is not speedily executed, the hearts of men become fully set on doing evil.

12 Although a sinner does evil a hundred times and still lives long, yet I know that it will be well with those who fear God, who stand in awe before Him. 13 Yet it will not be well with the wicked, nor will he prolong his days like a shadow, because he does not fear God. 14 There is a futility that is done on the earth: there are righteous men who get what the wicked deserve, and there are wicked men who get what the righteous deserve. I say that this too is futility.

Joy in God’s Gifts and Unsearchable Works

15 So I commended joy, for there is nothing better for a man under the sun than to eat and drink and be merry. For this will accompany him in his labor during the days of his life that God has given him under the sun. 16 When I applied my heart to know wisdom and to observe the activity that is done on the earth—even though one’s eyes do not see sleep day or night— 17 I saw every work of God, and that a man cannot find out the work that is done under the sun. For though a man labors to seek it out, yet he will not find it; indeed, even if a wise man claims to know it, he cannot find it.
