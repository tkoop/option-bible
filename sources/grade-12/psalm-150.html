A Final Summons to Universal Praise

1 Praise the LORD! Praise God in His sanctuary; praise Him in His mighty heavens. 2 Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him for His excellent greatness.

3 Praise Him with the sound of the trumpet; praise Him with harp and lyre. 4 Praise Him with tambourine and dancing; praise Him with strings and flute.

5 Praise Him with clashing cymbals; praise Him with resounding cymbals. 6 Let everything that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
