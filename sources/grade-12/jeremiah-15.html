The LORD Rejects Judah

1 Then the LORD said to me, “Even if Moses and Samuel should stand before Me, My heart would not turn toward this people. Send them away from My presence, and let them go. 2 If they ask you, ‘Where shall we go?’ you must tell them that this is what the LORD says: ‘Those destined for death, to death; those for the sword, to the sword; those for famine, to famine; and those for captivity, to captivity.’ 3 I will appoint over them four kinds of destroyers, declares the LORD: the sword to kill, the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the air and the beasts of the earth to devour and destroy. 4 I will make them an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem.

“5 Who will have pity on you, O Jerusalem? Who will mourn for you? Who will turn aside to inquire after your welfare? 6 You have rejected Me, declares the LORD. You have turned away from Me. So I have stretched out My hand against you and destroyed you; I am weary of showing compassion. 7 I will winnow them with a winnowing fork at the gates of the land. I will bereave and destroy My people, for they did not turn from their ways. 8 Their widows will be more numerous than the sand of the sea. I will bring a destroyer at noon against the mothers of young men. I will suddenly bring upon them anguish and terror. 9 The mother of seven will grow faint and breathe her last; her sun will set while it is still day; she will be disgraced and humiliated. And the rest of them I will put to the sword in the presence of their enemies, declares the LORD.”

Jeremiah Laments His Calling

“10 Ah, woe to me, my mother, that you bore me, a man of strife and contention to all the land! I have neither lent nor borrowed, yet everyone curses me.”

The LORD Answers Jeremiah

11 The LORD said, “Surely I will deliver you for a good purpose; surely I will make your enemies plead with you in time of disaster and in time of distress. 12 Can anyone smash iron—iron from the north—or bronze? 13 Your wealth and your treasures I will give as plunder, without cost, for all your sins, throughout your territory. 14 I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know, for My anger has blazed like fire; it will burn against you.”

Jeremiah's Prayer and the the LORD's Commission

“15 O the LORD, You know; remember me and take vengeance for me on my persecutors. In Your patience, do not take me away. Know that I bear reproach for Your sake. 16 Your words were found, and I ate them; Your words became to me a joy and the delight of my heart, because I am called by Your name, O the LORD God of Hosts. 17 I never sat with the merrymakers, nor did I celebrate. Because of Your hand, I sat alone, for You had filled me with indignation. 18 Why is my pain perpetual and my wound incurable, refusing to be healed? You have indeed become to me a deceitful brook—water that is not reliable.”

19 Therefore this is what the LORD says: “If you return, I will restore you; you will stand before Me. And if you extract the precious from the worthless, you will be as My mouth. They will turn to you, but you must not turn to them. 20 I will make you a fortified wall of bronze to this people; they will fight against you but will not overcome you, for I am with you to save you and deliver you, declares the LORD. 21 I will deliver you from the hand of the wicked and redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.”
