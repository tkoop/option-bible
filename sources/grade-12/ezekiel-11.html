Judgment on Jerusalem's Leaders

1 The Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the the LORD, which faces east. And behold, at the entrance of the gateway there were twenty-five men. Among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people. 2 And He said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and give wicked counsel in this city."

3 They say, "We need not build houses yet; this city is like a secure pot, and we are safe within it."

4 Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man.

5 Then the Spirit of the the LORD fell upon me, and He said to me, "Say: Thus says the the LORD: That is exactly what you are thinking, O house of Israel, for I know what comes into your mind. 6 You have multiplied your slain in this city and filled its streets with the slain.

7 "Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Your slain whom you have laid in its midst, they are the meat, and this city is the pot; but I will bring you out of its midst. 8 You fear the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you, declares the Lord the LORD.

9 "I will bring you out of its midst and give you into the hand of foreigners, and execute judgments among you. 10 You shall fall by the sword; I will judge you at the border of Israel, and you shall know that I am the the LORD.

11 "This city shall not be your pot, nor shall you be the meat in its midst. I will judge you at the border of Israel. 12 And you shall know that I am the the LORD, for you have not walked in My statutes, nor executed My judgments, but have done according to the judgments of the nations that are around you."

13 When I prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Ah, Lord the LORD! Will You make a full end of the remnant of Israel?"

A Promise of Restoration for the Exiles

14 Again the word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 15 "Son of man, your brothers—your brothers, the men of your kindred and all the house of Israel, all of them—are those of whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'Go far away from the the LORD; this land has been given to us for a possession.'

16 "Therefore say: Thus says the Lord the LORD: Although I have removed them far off among the nations and scattered them among the countries, yet I have been a sanctuary to them for a little while in the countries where they have gone.

17 "Therefore say: Thus says the Lord the LORD: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel. 18 And when they come there, they will remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations.

19 "And I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within them. I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh, 20 that they may walk in My statutes and keep My rules and obey them. And they shall be My people, and I will be their God.

21 "But as for those whose heart goes after their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their conduct down upon their own heads, declares the Lord the LORD."

The Departure of God's Glory

22 Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above. 23 And the glory of the the LORD went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is on the east side of the city.

24 Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me in a vision by the Spirit of God into Chaldea, to the exiles. Then the vision that I had seen went up from me. 25 And I spoke to the exiles all the things that the the LORD had shown me.
