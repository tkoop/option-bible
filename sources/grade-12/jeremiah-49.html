Judgment on the Ammonites

1 Concerning the Ammonites, this is what the LORD says: “Does Israel have no sons? Is there no heir? Why then has their king inherited Gad, and his people settled in its cities?” 2 “Therefore, behold, days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will cause the battle cry to be heard against Rabbah of the Ammonites; she will become a heap of ruins, and her daughters will be burned with fire. Then Israel will dispossess those who dispossessed him,” says the LORD.

3 “Wail, Heshbon, for Ai is ruined! Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! Put on sackcloth, lament, and run back and forth among the walls, for their king will go into exile, together with his priests and officials.” 4 “Why do you boast in the valleys, your valley flowing away, O faithless daughter, who trusts in her treasures, saying, ‘Who will come against me?’”

5 “Behold, I am bringing terror upon you from all around you,” declares the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, “and you will be driven out, each man straight ahead, and no one will gather the fugitives.” 6 “But afterward I will restore the fortunes of the Ammonites,” declares the LORD.

Judgment on Edom

7 Concerning Edom, this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Is wisdom no more in Teman? Has counsel perished from the prudent? Has their wisdom decayed?” 8 “Flee, turn back, and hide deep, O inhabitants of Dedan! For I will bring the calamity of Esau upon him, the time when I punish him.” 9 “If grape gatherers came to you, would they not leave gleanings? If thieves came by night, would they not steal only enough for themselves?”

10 “But I have stripped Esau bare; I have uncovered his hiding places, and he cannot conceal himself. His offspring are devastated, along with his brothers and neighbors, and he is no more.” 11 “Leave your orphans; I will preserve them alive, and let your widows trust in Me.” 12 “For this is what the the LORD says: ‘If those who do not deserve to drink the cup must drink it, will you go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, but you will surely drink it.’”

13 “For I have sworn by Myself,” declares the LORD, “that Bozrah will become a desolation, a reproach, a waste, and a curse, and all its cities will be perpetual ruins.” 14 “I have heard a message from the the LORD, and a messenger is sent among the nations: ‘Gather yourselves together and come against her; rise up for battle!’” 15 “Behold, I will make you small among the nations, despised among men.” 16 “The terror you inspire has deceived you, and the pride of your heart, O you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, who hold the height of the hill. Though you make your nest as high as the eagle's, from there I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.

17 “Edom will become an object of horror; everyone who passes by her will be appalled and will hiss at all her wounds.” 18 “As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors,” says the the LORD, “no man will dwell there, nor will any son of man reside in it.” 19 “Behold, he will come up like a lion from the thickets of the Jordan against the enduring pasture; for I will suddenly make him run away from her, and I will appoint over her whomever I choose. For who is like Me? Who will summon Me? And who is the shepherd who can stand before Me?”

20 “Therefore hear the plan that the the LORD has devised against Edom and the strategies He has plotted against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely the little ones of the flock will be dragged away; surely their pasture will be appalled at them.” 21 “At the sound of their fall the earth will tremble; the sound of their cry will be heard at the Red Sea.” 22 “Behold, he will come up and fly like an eagle and spread his wings over Bozrah; and the heart of the warriors of Edom will be in that day like the heart of a woman in labor.”

Judgment on Damascus

23 “Concerning Damascus: Hamath and Arpad are put to shame, for they have heard a bad report; they are dismayed. There is anxiety by the sea; it cannot be calmed.” 24 “Damascus has become feeble; she has turned to flee, and panic has seized her; anguish and pain have taken hold of her, like a woman in labor.” 25 “How is the city of praise not forsaken, the city of My joy?”

26 “Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all the men of war will be silenced in that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 27 “I will set fire to the wall of Damascus, and it will consume the palaces of Ben-hadad.”

Judgment on Kedar, Hazor, and Elam

28 “Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated, this is what the the LORD says: ‘Arise, go up against Kedar, and destroy the people of the East.’” 29 “They will take their tents and their flocks, their tent curtains and all their goods; they will carry off their camels for themselves, and will shout to them, ‘Terror on every side!’”

30 “Flee, wander far away, hide in the depths, O inhabitants of Hazor,” declares the LORD, “for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has taken counsel against you and devised a plan against you.” 31 “Arise, go up against a nation at ease, that dwells securely,” declares the LORD, “that has no gates or bars, that dwells alone.”

32 “Their camels will become plunder, and their multitude of livestock a spoil, and I will scatter to every wind those who have shaved temples, and I will bring their disaster from every side,” declares the LORD. 33 “Hazor will become a haunt of jackals, a desolation forever; no one will dwell there, nor will any son of man reside in it.”

34 The word of the the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah: 35 “This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Behold, I will break the bow of Elam, the chief of their might.’” 36 “I will bring the four winds from the four quarters of heaven against Elam, and I will scatter them to all these winds, and there will be no nation to which the outcasts of Elam will not go.”

37 “I will shatter Elam before their enemies and before those who seek their life; I will bring disaster upon them, My fierce anger,” declares the LORD, “and I will send the sword after them until I have consumed them.” 38 “I will set My throne in Elam and destroy from there king and officials,” declares the LORD. 39 “But in the latter days I will restore the fortunes of Elam,” declares the LORD.
