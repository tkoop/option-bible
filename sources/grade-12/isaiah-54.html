Joy for the Barren Woman

1 “Shout for joy, barren woman who never bore a child; burst into song and cry aloud, you who have never labored! For the children of the desolate woman will outnumber the children of the married one,” says the LORD.

Enlarge Your Dwelling

2 “Enlarge the place of your tent, stretch out the curtains of your dwellings, and do not hold back. Lengthen your cords and strengthen your stakes. 3 For you will spread out to the right and to the left, and your descendants will possess nations and inhabit desolate cities. 4 Do not be afraid, for you will not be put to shame; do not be discouraged, for you will not be disgraced. You will forget the shame of your youth and remember no more the reproach of your widowhood.”

The LORD as Husband and Redeemer

5 “For your Maker is your Husband—the the LORD of Hosts is His name—the Holy One of Israel is your Redeemer; He is called the God of all the earth. 6 For the the LORD has called you back, like a wife deserted and wounded in spirit—a wife of one’s youth who has been rejected,” says your God.

7 “For a brief moment I forsook you, but with great compassion I will bring you back. 8 In a surge of anger I hid My face from you for a moment, but with everlasting kindness I will have compassion on you,” says the the LORD your Redeemer.

9 “This is like the days of Noah to Me; I swore that the waters of Noah would never again cover the earth. So I have sworn that I will not be angry with you or rebuke you. 10 Though the mountains may be removed and the hills may be shaken, My loving devotion will not depart from you, and My covenant of peace will not be shaken,” says the the LORD, who has compassion on you.

Rebuilding the Afflicted City

11 “O afflicted city, lashed by storms and not comforted, I will rebuild you with turquoise, lay your foundations with sapphires, 12 make your pinnacles of rubies, your gates of sparkling jewels, and all your walls of precious stones. 13 Then all your children will be taught by the the LORD, and great will be their peace. 14 In righteousness you will be established, far from oppression; you will have no fear. Terror will be far removed from you.”

The LORD's Protection

15 “If anyone stirs up strife, it is not from Me; whoever attacks you will fall in your presence. 16 Behold, I have created the smith who fans the coals into flame and forges a weapon fit for its task; and I have created the destroyer to wreak havoc. 17 No weapon formed against you shall prosper, and you will refute every tongue that accuses you. This is the heritage of the servants of the the LORD, and their vindication is from Me,” declares the the LORD.
