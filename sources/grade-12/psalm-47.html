A Summons to Universal Acclaim

1Clap your hands, all peoples; shout to God with a voice of jubilation. 2For the LORD Most High is awesome, a great King over all the earth. 3He subdues peoples beneath us and nations under our feet. 4He chooses our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah.

God's Triumphant Ascension

5God has ascended amid shouting, the LORD amid the sound of a trumpet. 6Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises. 7For God is King of all the earth; sing praises with understanding.

The Nations Acknowledge Divine Sovereignty

8God reigns over the nations; God sits on His holy throne. 9The nobles of the peoples gather as the people of the God of Abraham, for the shields of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted.
