1 The word of the LORD came to me, declaring:

Judgment Against the Ammonites

2 “Son of man, confront the Ammonites and prophesy against them. 3 Say to the Ammonites: ‘Hear the word of the Lord GOD: Thus says the Lord GOD: Because you said, “Aha!” over My sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of Israel when it was desolate, and over the house of Judah when they went into exile, 4 therefore, behold, I am delivering you into the possession of the eastern peoples, and they shall pitch their encampments among you and establish their dwellings within you. They shall eat your fruit, and they shall drink your milk. 5 I will transform Rabbah into a pasture for camels and the Ammonites into a fold for flocks. Then you shall know that I am the LORD.’

6 “For thus says the Lord GOD: Because you clapped your hands, stamped your feet, and rejoiced with all the scorn in your soul against the land of Israel, 7 therefore, behold, I have stretched out My hand against you, and I will deliver you as plunder to the nations; I will excise you from the peoples and annihilate you among the countries. I will destroy you. Then you shall know that I am the LORD.”

Judgment Against Moab and Seir

8 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Moab and Seir said, ‘Look, the house of Judah is like all the nations,’ 9 therefore, behold, I will lay open Moab’s flank from its cities to its frontier—the splendor of the land—Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim. 10 I will consign it, together with the Ammonites, to the eastern peoples as a possession, so that the Ammonites will no longer be remembered among the nations. 11 I will execute judgments against Moab. Then they shall know that I am the LORD.”

Judgment Against Edom

12 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Edom acted vindictively toward the house of Judah and incurred grievous guilt by exacting vengeance upon them, 13 therefore thus says the Lord GOD: I will stretch out My hand against Edom, and I will cut off from it both human and beast, and I will render it desolate from Teman; moreover, Dedan shall fall by the sword. 14 I will execute My vengeance upon Edom through My people Israel, and they shall deal with Edom according to My anger and according to My wrath; thus they shall know My vengeance, declares the Lord GOD.”

Judgment Against the Philistines

15 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because the Philistines acted vengefully and took vengeance with spiteful malice, seeking to destroy with perpetual enmity, 16 therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am stretching out My hand against the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethites and annihilate the remnant of the coast. 17 I will execute great vengeance upon them with furious rebukes. Then they shall know that I am the LORD when I lay My vengeance upon them.”
