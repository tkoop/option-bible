Prophecy Against Gog

1 And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog and say, "Thus says the Lord, the LORD: Behold, I am against you, O Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. 2 I will turn you around, urge you onward, bring you up from the far north, and lead you against the mountains of Israel. 3 I will strike your bow from your left hand and knock your arrows from your right hand. 4 On the mountains of Israel you will fall—you and all your troops and the nations with you. I will give you as food to every bird of prey and wild animal. 5 You will fall in the open field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord, the LORD. 6 I will send fire on Magog and on those who dwell securely in the coastlands, and they will know that I am the LORD. 7 I will make My holy name known among My people Israel, and I will no longer allow My holy name to be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel. 8 Behold, it is coming, and it will surely happen, declares the Lord, the LORD. This is the day of which I have spoken."

The Aftermath in Israel

9 Then the inhabitants of the cities of Israel will go out and use the weapons for fuel and burn them—the shields, small and large, the bows and arrows, the clubs and spears. For seven years they will use them for fuel. 10 They will not need to gather wood from the fields or cut it from the forests, because they will use the weapons for fuel. They will loot those who looted them and plunder those who plundered them, declares the Lord, the LORD.

The Burial of Gog's Army

11 On that day I will give Gog a burial place in Israel—the Valley of the Travelers east of the sea. It will obstruct the travelers, because Gog and all his multitude will be buried there. So it will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog. 12 For seven months the house of Israel will be burying them in order to cleanse the land. 13 All the people of the land will bury them, and it will be a day of renown for Me when I am glorified, declares the Lord, the LORD. 14 Men will be continually appointed to travel through the land and bury the invaders left on its surface; after the seven months, they will conduct a further search. 15 As they pass through the land, anyone who sees a human bone will set up a marker beside it, until those assigned to bury have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog. 16 A city there will also be called Hamonah. Thus they will cleanse the land.

The Great Sacrifice

17 Now as for you, son of man, this is what the Lord, the LORD says: Tell every kind of bird and every wild animal: "Assemble and come, gather together from all around to My sacrifice that I am preparing for you—a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel—and you will eat flesh and drink blood. 18 You will eat the flesh of warriors and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, as though they were rams, lambs, goats, and bulls—all of them fattened animals of Bashan. 19 At the sacrifice I am preparing, you will eat fat until you are satiated and drink blood until you are drunk. 20 At My table you will gorge yourselves on horses and charioteers, on mighty warriors and all the men of war, declares the Lord, the LORD."

Israel's Restoration

21 I will display My glory among the nations, and all the nations will see My judgment that I have executed and My hand that I have laid upon them. 22 From that day forward the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God. 23 And the nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their iniquity, because they were treacherous toward Me. So I turned away from them and delivered them into the hands of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword. 24 I dealt with them according to their impurity and transgressions, and I turned away from them. 25 Therefore this is what the Lord, the LORD says: Now I will bring back the exiles of Jacob and have compassion on the whole house of Israel, and I will be zealous for My holy name. 26 They will forget their shame and all the treachery they committed against Me, when they dwell securely in their land, with no one to make them afraid. 27 When I bring them back from the nations and gather them out of their enemies' lands, I will reveal My holiness through them in the sight of many nations. 28 Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, for though I sent them into exile among the nations, I will gather them back to their own land, not leaving any of them behind. 29 And I will no longer turn away from them, for I will pour out My Spirit on the house of Israel, declares the Lord, the LORD.
