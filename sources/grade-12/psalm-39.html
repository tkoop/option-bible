For the choirmaster. To Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.

Restraint, Anguish, and Speech

1 I said, "I will guard my ways, lest I sin with my tongue; I will muzzle my mouth while the wicked stand before me." 2 I was struck dumb with silence; I refrained from speaking even what was good, and my grief was aggravated. 3 My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, fire burned, and I spoke with my tongue.

Human Frailty and Fleeting Wealth

4 Make known to me, O the LORD, my end and the measure of my days, that I may know how fleeting I am. 5 Behold, You have made my days a few handbreadths, and my lifetime is as nothing before You; surely every man stands as a mere breath. Selah. 6 Surely a man goes about as a mere shadow; surely they bustle in vain; he amasses wealth without knowing who will collect it.

Petition for Mercy and Deliverance

7 And now, O Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in You. 8 Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me the reproach of a fool. 9 I am mute; I do not open my mouth, for You have done it. 10 Remove Your plague from me; I am consumed by the blow of Your hand. 11 You discipline a man with rebukes for iniquity, consuming his treasure like a moth; surely every man is but a breath. Selah.

12 Hear my prayer, O the LORD, and attend to my cry; do not be deaf to my tears, for I am a sojourner with You, a temporary resident like all my fathers. 13 Turn Your gaze away from me, that I may smile again, before I depart and am no more.
