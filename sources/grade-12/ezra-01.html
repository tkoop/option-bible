Cyrus’s Decree Fulfills Jeremiah’s Prophecy

1 In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, so that the word of the LORD spoken through Jeremiah might be fulfilled, the LORD stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia. He made a proclamation throughout his entire kingdom and also put it in writing, saying:

2 “Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: The LORD, the God of heaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has appointed me to build Him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. 3 Who among you is of all His people? May his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel—He is the God who is in Jerusalem. 4 And every survivor, wherever he sojourns, shall be assisted by the men of his place with silver, gold, goods, and livestock, together with the freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem.”

The Exiles Respond and Prepare to Rebuild

5 Then the heads of the fathers’ houses of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites, rose up—everyone whose spirit God had stirred—to go up and build the house of the LORD that is in Jerusalem. 6 And all their neighbors strengthened their hands with vessels of silver and gold, with goods and livestock, and with precious things, besides all that was freely offered.

The Sacred Vessels Are Restored

7 King Cyrus also brought out the vessels of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar had carried away from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his god. 8 Cyrus king of Persia brought them out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, who counted them out to Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.

9 This was their number: thirty gold basins, a thousand silver basins, twenty-nine knives, 10 thirty gold bowls, four hundred ten silver bowls of a second kind, and a thousand other articles. 11 All the vessels of gold and silver numbered five thousand four hundred. Sheshbazzar brought up all of them with the exiles who were brought up from Babylon to Jerusalem.
