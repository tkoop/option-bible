God as Refuge and Strength amid Cosmic Upheaval

For the choirmaster. A composition of the Sons of Korah, set to alamoth. A song. 1 God is our refuge and our strength, a help exceedingly present in every affliction. 2 Therefore we shall not fear though the earth be rent asunder and the mountains be cast into the depths of the sea, 3 though its waters roar and foam and the mountains quake before its swelling surge. Selah.

The River That Gladdens the Holy City

4 There is a river whose streams gladden the city of God, the holy dwelling of the Most High. 5 God is in her midst; she shall not be shaken; God will help her at the break of dawn. 6 The nations rage and the kingdoms totter; He lifts His voice, and the earth dissolves. 7 The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our stronghold. Selah.

The LORD’s Desolations and the Cessation of War

8 Come, behold the works of the LORD, the desolations He has wrought upon the earth. 9 He causes wars to cease to the very ends of the earth; He shatters the bow and breaks the spear; He burns the chariots with fire.

10 “Cease your striving and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.”

11 The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our stronghold. Selah.
