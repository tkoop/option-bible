Opening Greeting

1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace and peace to you.

Thanksgiving for Their Faith, Love, and Hope

2 We always give thanks to God for all of you, remembering you in our prayers, 3 as we unceasingly recall before our God and Father your work produced by faith, your labor motivated by love, and your endurance inspired by hope in our Lord Jesus Christ. 4 Brothers loved by God, we know that He has chosen you,

The Gospel's Power and Their Example

5 for our gospel came to you not merely in word but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with deep conviction—just as you know what kind of people we were among you for your sake. 6 And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, receiving the word amid much affliction with the joy of the Holy Spirit, 7 so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia.

Their Witness and Future Hope

8 For not only has the word of the Lord sounded forth from you in Macedonia and in Achaia, but your faith toward God has gone out to every place, so that we have no need to say anything more. 9 For they themselves report what kind of welcome we received among you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God, 10 and to wait for His Son from heaven, whom He raised from the dead—Jesus, who delivers us from the wrath to come.
