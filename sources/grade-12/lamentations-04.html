The Ruin of Jerusalem's Glory

1 Alas, how the gold has dimmed, the finest gold altered; the sacred stones lie scattered at every street corner. 2 Zion's precious sons, once valued like pure gold, are now reckoned as earthen vessels, the work of a potter's hands. 3 Even jackals offer the breast to nurse their young, but the daughter of my people has become cruel, like ostriches in the wilderness. 4 The nursing child's tongue clings to the roof of its mouth for thirst; little children beg for bread, yet no one gives them any. 5 Those who once feasted on delicacies perish in the streets; those reared in scarlet embrace ash heaps. 6 The punishment of the daughter of my people exceeds the sin of Sodom, which was overthrown in an instant without human hands. 7 Her nobles were purer than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were ruddier than coral, their forms like sapphire. 8 Now their faces are darker than soot; they are not recognized in the streets; their skin has shriveled upon their bones and become as dry as wood. 9 Those slain by the sword are better off than those slain by famine, who waste away, pierced through, for lack of the field's produce. 10 The hands of compassionate women have boiled their own children; they became food for them in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

The LORD's Wrath and the Fall of Jerusalem

11 The LORD has vented His wrath; He has poured out His fierce anger. He set fire to Zion, and it consumed her foundations. 12 The kings of the earth and all the inhabitants of the world did not believe that foe or enemy could enter the gates of Jerusalem. 13 It was because of the sins of her prophets and the iniquities of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst. 14 They wandered blind through the streets, defiled with blood, so that no one could touch their garments. 15 "Depart! Unclean!" people cried to them. "Depart, depart, do not touch!" So they fled and wandered; among the nations it was said, "They shall dwell here no longer." 16 The LORD Himself scattered them; He will no longer regard them. They did not honor the priests, nor show favor to the elders.

The End of Hope and the Triumph of Enemies

17 Our eyes still fail, watching in vain for help; from our watchtowers we watched for a nation that could not save. 18 They stalked our steps so that we could not walk in our streets; our end drew near, our days were finished, for our end had come. 19 Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of heaven; they chased us over the mountains and ambushed us in the wilderness. 20 The LORD's Anointed, the breath of our life, was caught in their pits. We had said, "Under his shadow we shall live among the nations."

Edom's Reckoning and Zion's Restoration

21 Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz; yet the cup will pass to you; you will be drunk and exposed. 22 O daughter of Zion, your iniquity is accomplished; He will not exile you again. He will punish your iniquity, O daughter of Edom; He will expose your sins.
