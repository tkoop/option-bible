Jesus Remains in Galilee Before the Feast

1 Following these occurrences, Jesus traveled throughout Galilee, for He was unwilling to travel throughout Judea, since the Jews were intent upon killing Him. 2 Now the Jewish festival, the Feast of Tabernacles, was imminent.

3 Accordingly, His brothers said to Him, "Leave this place and go into Judea, so that Your disciples may also witness the works You are performing. 4 For no one who seeks public recognition acts in secret; if You do these things, reveal Yourself to the world."

5 Indeed, even His brothers did not believe in Him.

6 "My time has not yet arrived, but your time is perpetually at hand. 7 The world cannot hate you, but it hates Me, because I testify concerning it that its works are evil. 8 Go up to this feast; I am not going up to this feast, because My time has not yet been fulfilled."

9 After addressing them thus, He remained in Galilee.

10 But when His brothers had ascended to the feast, He also ascended, not publicly but as though in concealment.

11 The Jews therefore sought Him at the feast and kept inquiring, "Where is He?"

12 And there was considerable murmuring among the crowds concerning Him.

Some declared, "He is a good man."

Others asserted, "No, He deceives the crowd."

13 Nevertheless, no one spoke openly about Him for fear of the Jews.

Jesus Instructs the People in the Temple

14 When the festival was already half over, Jesus ascended into the temple and began to teach.

15 The Jews therefore were astonished, saying, "How does this man possess learning, having never studied?"

16 "My teaching is not Mine, but His who sent Me. 17 If anyone wills to do His will, he will know concerning the teaching, whether it derives from God or whether I speak from Myself. 18 He who speaks from himself seeks his own glory; but he who seeks the glory of Him who sent him is true, and there is no unrighteousness in him. 19 Did not Moses give you the Law? Yet none of you keeps the Law. Why do you seek to kill Me?"

20 The crowd answered, "You have a demon. Who seeks to kill You?"

21 "I performed one work, and you all marvel. 22 Therefore Moses gave you circumcision—not that it is from Moses, but from the fathers—and on the Sabbath you circumcise a man. 23 If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the Law of Moses may not be violated, are you indignant with Me because I made a whole man well on the Sabbath? 24 Do not judge according to appearance, but render righteous judgment."

Division Among the People

25 Some of the residents of Jerusalem said, "Is this not the man they seek to kill? 26 Behold, He speaks openly, and they say nothing to Him. Have the rulers truly concluded that this is the Christ? 27 Yet we know where this man originates; but when the Christ comes, no one knows where He is from."

28 "You both know Me and know where I am from. I have not come of Myself, but He who sent Me is true, and you do not know Him. 29 I know Him, because I am from Him, and He sent Me."

30 Therefore they sought to seize Him, but no one laid a hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come.

31 Many of the crowd believed in Him and said, "When the Christ comes, will He perform more signs than this man has performed?"

32 The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about Him, and the Pharisees and the chief priests dispatched officers to seize Him.

33 "I am with you a little while longer, and then I go to Him who sent Me. 34 You will seek Me and will not find Me, and where I am you cannot come."

35 The Jews therefore said among themselves, "Where is this man about to go, that we will not find Him? Is He going to the Dispersion among the Greeks and teach the Greeks?"

36 They asked, "What does He mean by saying, 'You will seek Me and will not find Me, and where I am you cannot come'?"

37 On the last day, the great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, saying, "If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink. 38 Whoever believes in Me, as the Scripture has said, out of his innermost being will flow rivers of living water."

39 He said this concerning the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were to receive, for the Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.

40 When some of the crowd heard these words, they said, "This is truly the Prophet."

41 Others said, "This is the Christ." But some said, "Does the Christ come from Galilee? 42 Has not the Scripture said that the Christ comes from the seed of David and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?"

43 So a division arose among the crowd because of Him.

44 Some of them wanted to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him.

The Officers Report to the Religious Leaders

45 The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and those leaders asked them, "Why did you not bring Him?"

46 The officers answered, "Never has a man spoken like this man."

47 The Pharisees answered them, "Are you also deceived? 48 Has any of the rulers or of the Pharisees believed in Him? 49 But this crowd, which does not know the Law, is accursed."

50 Nicodemus, who had come to Him earlier and was one of them, said to them, 51 "Does our Law judge a man unless it first hears from him and learns what he is doing?"

52 They answered him, "Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that no prophet arises from Galilee."

53 Then each departed to his own house.
