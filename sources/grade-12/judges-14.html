Samson Seeks a Philistine Wife

1 Samson went down to Timnah, where he saw a woman among the daughters of the Philistines. 2 When he returned, he told his father and mother, “I have seen a Philistine woman in Timnah; get her for me as a wife.”

3 His father and mother asked him, “Is there no woman among your brothers’ daughters or among all our people? Must you go to the uncircumcised Philistines to take a wife?”

But Samson said to his father, “Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes.”

4 His father and mother did not know that this matter was from the LORD, who was seeking an occasion against the Philistines; for at that time the Philistines ruled over Israel.

Samson Slays a Lion

5 Samson went down to Timnah with his father and mother, and as they came to the vineyards of Timnah, a young lion came roaring toward him. 6 Then the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him, and he tore the lion apart with his bare hands as one tears a young goat. He did not tell his father or mother what he had done. 7 He went down and spoke to the woman, and she was pleasing in Samson’s eyes.

8 After some days he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion. In it was a swarm of bees and honey. 9 He scooped the honey into his hands and ate it as he walked. When he came to his father and mother, he gave them some, and they ate it; but he did not tell them that he had taken the honey from the lion’s carcass.

The Wedding Feast and Samson’s Riddle

10 His father went down to the woman, and Samson made a feast there, for young men used to do so. 11 When the people saw him, they brought thirty companions to be with him.

12 Samson said to them, “Let me now propound a riddle to you. If you can fully explain it to me within the seven days of the feast, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes. 13 But if you cannot explain it to me, then you shall give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes.”

They said to him, “Propound your riddle, that we may hear it.”

14 He said to them, “Out of the eater came something to eat, and out of the strong came something sweet.” For three days they could not explain the riddle.

15 On the fourth day they said to Samson’s wife, “Entice your husband to explain the riddle to us, or we will burn you and your father’s household with fire. Have you invited us here to rob us?”

16 Samson’s wife wept before him and said, “You only hate me; You do not love me. You have propounded a riddle to the sons of my people, and You have not explained it to me.”

He said to her, “Behold, I have not explained it to my father or my mother, and shall I explain it to you?”

17 She wept before him during the seven days while their feast lasted. On the seventh day he told her, because she pressed him; and she explained the riddle to the sons of her people. 18 Before the sun went down on the seventh day, the men of the city said to him, “What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?”

He said to them, “If you had not plowed with my heifer, you would not have found out my riddle.”

Samson’s Vengeance and the Loss of His Wife

19 Then the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon and struck down thirty of their men. He took their garments and gave them as the changes of clothes to those who had explained the riddle. His anger burned, and he went up to his father’s house. 20 But Samson’s wife was given to his companion who had been his attendant.
