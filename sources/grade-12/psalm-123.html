A Pilgrim’s Prayer for Mercy

1 A song of ascents. I lifted my eyes to You, the One enthroned in heaven.

2 Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maidservant look to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to the LORD our God, until He shows us favor.

3 Be gracious to us, O the the LORD, be gracious to us, for we have had more than enough of contempt. 4 Our soul has had more than enough of the scorn of the complacent and the contempt of the proud.
