The LORD Sends Samuel to Anoint David

1 The LORD said to Samuel, "How long will you mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse of Bethlehem, because I have selected one of his sons to be king for Me."

2 Samuel said, "How can I go? Saul will hear of it and kill me."

The LORD answered, "Take a heifer with you and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD.' 3 Invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show you what you are to do. You are to anoint for Me the one I designate."

4 Samuel did what the the LORD had said and went to Bethlehem. The elders of the city came trembling to meet him and asked, "Do you come in peace?"

5 "Peace," he replied. "I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD. Consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice." Then he consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.

David Is Chosen Among Jesse's Sons

6 When they arrived, Samuel saw Eliab and thought, "Surely the the LORD's anointed stands here before Him."

7 But the the LORD said to Samuel, "Do not look at his appearance or his height, for I have rejected him. Man does not see what the the LORD sees; man looks at the outward appearance, but the the LORD looks at the heart."

8 Then Jesse called Abinadab and had him pass before Samuel. But Samuel said, "The The LORD has not chosen this one either." 9 Then Jesse had Shammah pass by, but Samuel said, "The The LORD has not chosen this one either." 10 Jesse had seven of his sons pass before Samuel, but Samuel said to Jesse, "The The LORD has not chosen any of these."

11 Samuel asked Jesse, "Are these all the sons you have?"

"There is still the youngest," Jesse answered, "but he is tending the sheep."

Samuel said, "Send for him; we will not sit down until he arrives."

12 So Jesse sent for him and brought him in. He was ruddy, with beautiful eyes and a handsome appearance. Then the the LORD said, "Rise and anoint him, for he is the one."

13 So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the presence of his brothers. From that day on, the Spirit of the the LORD came powerfully upon David. Then Samuel left and went to Ramah.

An Evil Spirit Torments Saul

14 Now the Spirit of the the LORD had departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the the LORD tormented him.

15 Saul's servants said to him, "Surely an evil spirit from God is tormenting you. 16 Let our lord command your servants who are here before you to seek out a man who plays the lyre. Whenever the evil spirit from God comes upon you, he will play, and you will feel better."

17 Saul said to his servants, "Find me someone who plays well and bring him to me."

David Is Brought to Saul

18 One of the servants answered, "I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who knows how to play the lyre. He is a mighty warrior, valiant in battle, discerning in speech, and handsome. And the the LORD is with him."

19 So Saul sent messengers to Jesse, saying, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep."

20 Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat, and sent them with his son David to Saul.

21 David came to Saul and entered his service. Saul loved him greatly, and David became his armor-bearer.

22 Then Saul sent word to Jesse: "Let David remain in my service, for he has found favor in my eyes."

23 Whenever the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, David would take his harp and play. Then Saul would find relief and feel better, and the evil spirit would leave him.
