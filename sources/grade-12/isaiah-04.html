The Aftermath of Judgment and the Hope of Restoration

1 In that day seven women will cling to one man and say, “We will provide our own bread and clothing; only let your name be called upon us. Take away our disgrace.”

2 In that day the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land will be the pride and glory of the remnant of Israel. 3 And it will come to pass that whoever remains in Zion and survives in Jerusalem will be called holy—everyone who is recorded among the living in Jerusalem.

Cleansing, Glory, and Divine Shelter

4 When the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and cleansed the bloodstains from the midst of Jerusalem by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.

5 Then the LORD will create over all of Mount Zion and over her assemblies a cloud and smoke by day, and the brightness of a flaming fire by night; for over all the glory there will be a canopy. 6 It will be a shelter for shade from the heat by day, and a refuge and hiding place from the storm and rain.
