Israel’s Prosperity Turned to Idolatry

1 Israel is a luxuriant vine that bears fruit for itself; as its harvest multiplied, it multiplied altars, and as its land prospered, it embellished sacred pillars. 2 Their hearts are divided; now they must bear their guilt. He will tear down their altars and shatter their sacred pillars. 3 For now they say, “We have no king, because we did not fear the the LORD. What then can a king do for us?” 4 They utter hollow words and forge covenants with false oaths, so that justice springs up like a venomous weed in the furrows of the field.

The Humiliation of Samaria and Its Idols

5 The residents of Samaria will dread the calves of Beth-aven. Its people will mourn over it, and its idolatrous priests will rejoice over its glory, because that glory has departed from it. 6 It too will be carried to Assyria as tribute to the great king. Ephraim will be seized with shame; Israel will be ashamed of its own counsel. 7 Samaria and its king will be swept away like foam upon the water. 8 The high places of Aven, the very sin of Israel, will be destroyed; thorns and thistles will overrun their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall on us!”

God’s Discipline and Israel’s Stubbornness

9 Since the days of Gibeah you have sinned, O Israel; there you have remained. Will not war in Gibeah overtake the evildoers? 10 When I desire, I will discipline them; nations will be gathered against them when they are bound for their double transgression. 11 Ephraim is a trained heifer that loves to thresh; I spared her fair neck, but now I will harness her. Judah will plow; Jacob will break up the fallow ground.

A Summons to Righteousness and Its Refusal

12 Sow for yourselves righteousness; reap according to steadfast love. Break up your fallow ground, for it is time to seek the the LORD, until He comes and rains righteousness upon you.

13 You have plowed wickedness, reaped iniquity, and eaten the fruit of deception, because you trusted in your own way and in the multitude of your mighty men. 14 Therefore a tumult will arise among your people, and all your fortresses will be destroyed, as Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel in the day of battle, dashing mothers to pieces with their children. 15 So it will be done to you, O Bethel, because of your great wickedness. At dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.
