The Wedding at Cana

1 On the third day a wedding was held at Cana in Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there. 2 Jesus and His disciples had also been invited to the wedding.

3 When the wine ran out, the mother of Jesus said to Him, "They have no wine."

4 Jesus answered, "Woman, what does this have to do with Me? My hour has not yet come."

5 His mother said to the servants, "Do whatever He tells you."

6 Now six stone water jars had been set there for the Jewish rites of purification; each could hold from twenty to thirty gallons. 7 Jesus said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water." So they filled them to the brim. 8 Then He told them, "Now draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet." And they did.

9 When the master of the banquet tasted the water that had become wine—and he did not know where it had come from, though the servants who had drawn the water knew—he called the bridegroom aside. 10 Then he said, "Everyone serves the choice wine first and then the cheaper wine after the guests have had too much to drink; but you have kept the best wine until now."

11 This, the first of His signs, Jesus performed at Cana in Galilee. He thus revealed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him. 12 Afterward He went down to Capernaum with His mother, His brothers, and His disciples, and they stayed there a few days.

Jesus Cleanses the Temple

13 Now the Jewish Passover was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 14 In the temple courts He found people selling cattle, sheep, and doves, and money changers seated at their tables. 15 So He made a whip out of cords and drove all of them from the temple courts, along with the sheep and cattle; He poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.

16 To those selling doves He said, "Take these things away! Do not make My Father's house a marketplace!"

17 His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for Your house will consume Me."

18 So the Jews replied, "What sign can You show us for doing these things?"

19 Jesus answered, "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up."

20 Then the Jews said, "It took forty-six years to build this temple, and will You raise it up in three days?"

21 But He was speaking about the temple of His body. 22 After He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had said this; then they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.

Jesus Knows What Is in Man

23 While He was in Jerusalem at the Passover Feast, many people saw the signs He was performing and believed in His name.

24 But Jesus would not entrust Himself to them, for He knew all people. 25 He did not need anyone to testify about man, for He knew what was in man.
