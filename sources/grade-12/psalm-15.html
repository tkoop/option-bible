The Character of Those Who Dwell in God's Sanctuary

1 A psalm of David. The LORD, who may sojourn in Your tent? Who may dwell upon Your holy hill?

2 He who walks in integrity and practices righteousness, 3 who does not slander with his tongue, who does no evil to his neighbor, and who does not take up a reproach against his friend; 4 who regards the vile as contemptible but honors those who fear the the LORD, who keeps his oath even when it proves costly; 5 who lends his money without usury and does not accept a bribe against the innocent. Whoever does these things shall never be shaken.
