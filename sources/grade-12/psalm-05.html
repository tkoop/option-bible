For the choirmaster, with flutes—a psalm of David.

1 Give ear to my words, O LORD; ponder my meditation. 2 Attend to the voice of my cry, my King and my God, for to You I pray. 3 In the morning, O LORD, You hear my voice; in the morning I set my prayer in order before You and wait expectantly.

4 For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; evil cannot dwell with You. 5 The arrogant cannot stand before Your eyes; You hate all who practice iniquity. 6 You destroy those who speak lies; the LORD abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful.

7 But I, through the abundance of Your steadfast love, will enter Your house; in reverence for You I will bow toward Your holy temple. 8 Lead me, O LORD, in Your righteousness because of my adversaries; make Your path straight before me.

9 For nothing reliable dwells in their mouths; destruction fills their inmost being; their throats are open graves; their tongues deal in flattering deceit. 10 Declare them guilty, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; drive them out for the multitude of their transgressions, for they have rebelled against You.

11 But let all who take refuge in You rejoice; let them sing aloud forever. Spread Your protection over them, so that those who love Your name may exult in You. 12 For You, O LORD, bless the righteous; You surround him with favor as with a shield.
