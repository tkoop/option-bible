God's Renown and Triumph

For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments—a psalm of Asaph, a song. 1 God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel.

2 His tabernacle stood in Salem, and His dwelling place in Zion. 3 There He shattered the flashing arrows—the shield, the sword, and the instruments of war. Selah

The Rout of the Mighty

4 You are radiant and majestic, surpassing the mountains where beasts of prey seize their quarry. 5 The stout-hearted were despoiled; they sank into their final slumber, and no warrior could even raise his hands.

6 At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, horse and chariot alike sank into stupor. 7 You are fearsome indeed; who can stand before You once Your wrath is kindled? 8 From heaven You proclaimed judgment, and the earth feared and fell silent 9 when God arose to render judgment and to rescue all the oppressed of the earth. Selah

Human Wrath and the Call to Worship

10 Surely human wrath will redound to Your praise; You will gird Yourself with the remnant of that fury. 11 Vow and pay to the the LORD your God; let all who surround Him bring tribute to the One who is to be feared. 12 He will cut off the spirit of princes; He is to be feared by the kings of the earth.
