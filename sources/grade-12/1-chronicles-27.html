The Monthly Army Divisions

1 Now the Israelites were counted according to their number: the heads of the ancestral houses, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and their officers who served the king in all the affairs of the divisions that rotated in and out month by month throughout the year, each division numbering twenty-four thousand men. 2 Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was over the first division for the first month, and his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 3 He was one of the sons of Perez and was chief of all the commanders of the army for the first month.

4 Dodai the Ahohite commanded the second division for the second month, and Mikloth was his chief officer; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 5 Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest was chief of the third division for the third month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 6 This Benaiah was a mighty man among the Thirty and their commander, and his son Ammizabad was over his division.

7 Joab, brother of Asahel, commanded the fourth division for the fourth month, and after him Zebadiah his son; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 8 Shamhuth the Izrahite commanded the fifth division for the fifth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 9 Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite commanded the sixth division for the sixth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

10 Helez the Pelonite, one of the sons of Ephraim, commanded the seventh division for the seventh month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 11 Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites, commanded the eighth division for the eighth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 12 Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjamites, commanded the ninth division for the ninth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

13 Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites, commanded the tenth division for the tenth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 14 Benaiah the Pirathonite, one of the sons of Ephraim, commanded the eleventh division for the eleventh month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand. 15 Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel, commanded the twelfth division for the twelfth month; his division numbered twenty-four thousand.

Leaders of the Tribes

16 These were the leaders over the tribes of Israel: for Reuben, Eliezer son of Zichri was leader; for Simeon, Shephatiah son of Maacah. 17 For Levi, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; for Aaron, Zadok. 18 For Judah, Elihu, one of David's brothers; for Issachar, Omri son of Michael. 19 For Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel.

20 For the sons of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Azaziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah. 21 For the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; for Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner. 22 For Dan, Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the tribes of Israel.

23 David did not number those who were under twenty years of age, because the LORD had said that He would make Israel as numerous as the stars of heaven. 24 Joab son of Zeruiah began to count, but he did not finish, and wrath came upon Israel because of this; and the number was not recorded in the chronicles of King David.

Overseers of the King's Property

25 Azmaveth son of Adiel was over the king's treasuries; Jonathan son of Uzziah was over the treasuries in the fields, in the cities, in the villages, and in the towers. 26 Ezri son of Chelub was over those who worked the fields for tilling the ground. 27 Shimei the Ramathite was over the vineyards; Zabdi the Shiphmite was over the wine in the storehouses.

28 Baal-hanan the Gederite was over the olive and sycamore trees in the Shephelah; Joash was over the oil stores. 29 Shitrai the Sharonite was over the cattle that grazed in Sharon; Shaphat son of Adlai was over the cattle in the valleys. 30 Obil the Ishmaelite was over the camels; Jehdeiah the Meronothite was over the donkeys. 31 Jaziz the Hagrite was over the flocks. All these were stewards of King David's property.

David's Counselors and Officials

32 Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding and a scribe; Jehiel son of Hachmoni was with the king's sons. 33 Ahithophel was counselor to the king; Hushai the Archite was the king's friend. 34 After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah and Abiathar; Joab was commander of the king's army.
