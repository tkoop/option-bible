David Sets Apart the Temple Musicians

1 David and the commanders of the army set apart for service the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, who prophesied with harps, lyres, and cymbals. The number of those assigned to this work was as follows:

The Families of the Prophetic Musicians

2 From the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asharelah. The sons of Asaph served under Asaph's direction; he prophesied under the king's authority.

3 From the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah—six in all—under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the harp, giving thanks and praise to the LORD.

4 From the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. 5 All these were sons of Heman, the king's seer in the words of God, to exalt his strength; and God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

6 All these served under their father's direction in the house of the LORD, with cymbals, harps, and lyres, for the service of the house of God, under the king's authority—Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman. 7 Together with their relatives, all of them trained and skilled in singing to the LORD, their number was two hundred eighty-eight.

The Divisions by Lot

8 They cast lots for their assignments, treating the lesser and the greater alike, teacher and student together.

9 The first lot fell to Asaph and was assigned to Joseph; the second to Gedaliah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 10 The third to Zaccur, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 11 The fourth to Izri, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 12 The fifth to Nethaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 13 The sixth to Bukkiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 14 The seventh to Jesharelah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 15 The eighth to Jeshaiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 16 The ninth to Mattaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 17 The tenth to Shimei, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 18 The eleventh to Azarel, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 19 The twelfth to Hashabiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 20 The thirteenth to Shubael, with his sons and brothers—twelve.

21 The fourteenth to Mattithiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 22 The fifteenth to Jerimoth, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 23 The sixteenth to Hananiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 24 The seventeenth to Joshbekashah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 25 The eighteenth to Hanani, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 26 The nineteenth to Mallothi, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 27 The twentieth to Eliathah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 28 The twenty-first to Hothir, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 29 The twenty-second to Giddalti, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 30 The twenty-third to Mahazioth, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 31 The twenty-fourth to Romamti-ezer, with his sons and brothers—twelve.
