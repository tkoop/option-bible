Job Longs to Present His Case Before God

1 Then Job answered and said: 2 “Even today my complaint is bitter; my hand weighs heavily because of my groaning. 3 Oh, that I knew where I might find Him, that I might come to His seat! 4 I would lay my case before Him and fill my mouth with arguments. 5 I would know what words He would answer me with, and understand what He would say to me. 6 Would He contend with me in great power? No—He would pay attention to me. 7 There an upright man could reason with Him, and I would be delivered forever from my Judge.”

Job Affirms His Integrity

8 “Behold, I go forward, but He is not there; I go backward, but I do not perceive Him. 9 When He works on the left, I cannot grasp Him; He turns to the right, but I do not see Him. 10 But He knows the way that I take; when He has tried me, I shall come out as gold. 11 My foot has held fast to His steps; I have kept His way and not turned aside. 12 I have not departed from the commandment of His lips; I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my daily bread.”

God’s Unchangeable Purpose Terrifies Job

13 “But He is unchangeable, and who can turn Him? What His soul desires, that He does. 14 For He will complete what He has appointed for me, and many such things are with Him. 15 Therefore I am terrified at His presence; when I consider, I am in dread of Him. 16 God has made my heart faint, and the Almighty has terrified me, 17 for I am not silenced by the darkness, nor by the thick darkness that covers my face.”
