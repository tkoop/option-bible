A Plea for Vindication and Renewed Hope

1 Vindicate me, O God, and plead my case against an ungodly nation; deliver me from the deceitful and unjust man. 2 For You are the God of my strength; why have You rejected me? Why must I go about in mourning because of the enemy’s oppression?

3 Send forth Your light and Your truth; let them lead me and bring me to Your holy mountain and to Your dwelling places. 4 Then I will approach the altar of God—to God, my supreme delight—and I will praise You with the harp, O God, my God.

5 Why are you downcast, O my soul? And why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I will yet praise Him—my Savior and my God.
