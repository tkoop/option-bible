The Lampstand and Its Lamps

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Speak to Aaron and tell him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall shine toward the front of the lampstand.'"

3 Aaron did so: he set up its lamps so that they shone toward the front of the lampstand, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 4 The lampstand itself was hammered gold, from its base to its blossoms; it was hammered work, fashioned according to the pattern the LORD had shown Moses.

The Consecration of the Levites

5 The LORD spoke to Moses: 6 "Take the Levites from among the Israelites and purify them. 7 This is how you shall purify them: sprinkle on them the water of purification; have them shave their entire bodies and wash their clothes, and they shall be purified. 8 Then let them take a young bull with its grain offering—fine flour mixed with oil—and you shall take a second young bull for a sin offering. 9 You shall bring the Levites before the tent of meeting and assemble the whole congregation of the Israelites. 10 You shall bring the Levites before the LORD, and the Israelites shall lay their hands on the Levites. 11 Aaron shall present the Levites before the LORD as an offering on behalf of the Israelites, so that they may perform the service of the LORD. 12 The Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls; then you shall offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites. 13 You shall set the Levites before Aaron and his sons, and present them before the LORD as an offering. 14 Thus you shall separate the Levites from among the Israelites, and the Levites shall be Mine. 15 After that the Levites shall come to serve in the tent of meeting; you shall purify them and present them as an offering. 16 For they are wholly given to Me from among the Israelites; in place of every firstborn who opens the womb—every firstborn of the Israelites—I have taken them for Myself. 17 For all the firstborn among the Israelites are Mine, both human and animal; on the day I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated them to Myself. 18 I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the Israelites. 19 I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the Israelites, to perform the service of the Israelites in the tent of meeting and to make atonement for the Israelites, so that no plague may strike the Israelites when they approach the sanctuary."

20 Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation of the Israelites did for the Levites everything the LORD had commanded Moses concerning them; the Israelites did so for them. 21 The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothes; Aaron presented them as an offering before the LORD, and Aaron made atonement for them to purify them. 22 After that the Levites came to perform their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and his sons, just as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites.

Age Limits for Levitical Service

23 The LORD spoke to Moses: 24 "This is what applies to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward they shall enter to perform service in the work of the tent of meeting. 25 But from the age of fifty years they shall withdraw from the service of the work and shall work no more. 26 They may, however, assist their brothers in the tent of meeting by keeping guard, but they shall perform no service. Thus you shall deal with the Levites in regard to their duties."
