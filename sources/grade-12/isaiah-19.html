Oracle Against Egypt

1 An oracle concerning Egypt: Behold, the LORD rides upon a swift cloud and comes to Egypt; the idols of Egypt tremble before Him, and the heart of Egypt melts within it.

2 I will stir up Egypt against Egypt, and they will fight—each against his brother, each against his neighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom. 3 The spirit of Egypt will be emptied within it, and I will confound its counsel; then they will inquire of the idols, the sorcerers, the mediums, and the necromancers. 4 I will deliver Egypt into the hand of a harsh master, and a fierce king will rule over them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

The Ruin of Egypt’s Nile and Counsel

5 The waters of the sea will dry up, and the river will be drained and parched. 6 Its canals will become foul, and the streams of Egypt will diminish and dry up; reeds and rushes will wither. 7 The meadows by the Nile, on the bank of the river, and all that is sown along the Nile will dry up, be driven away, and be no more.

8 The fishermen will mourn, and all who cast hooks into the Nile will lament; those who spread nets upon the water will pine away. 9 The workers in flax will be ashamed, and the weavers of fine linen will be dismayed. 10 Her foundations will be crushed, and all who earn wages will be sick at heart.

11 The princes of Zoan are utterly foolish; Pharaoh’s wisest counselors give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am a son of the wise, a son of ancient kings”? 12 Where, then, are your wise men? Let them tell you, and let them make known what the LORD of Hosts has purposed against Egypt.

13 The princes of Zoan have become fools, and the princes of Memphis are deluded; the cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray. 14 The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion, so that they make Egypt stagger in everything it does, as a drunkard staggers in his vomit. 15 Egypt will be left with no one capable of acting—neither leader nor follower, neither the mighty nor the lowly.

Egypt’s Humiliation and Future Allegiance

16 In that day the Egyptians will be like women, trembling and terrified before the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, which He shakes over them. 17 The land of Judah will become a source of dread to Egypt; whenever it is mentioned, they will fear because of the purpose that the LORD of Hosts has purposed against them.

18 In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts; one of them will be called the City of Destruction.

19 In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD at its border. 20 It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry to the LORD because of oppressors, He will send them a Savior and Champion, and He will deliver them.

21 The LORD will make Himself known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians will know the LORD in that day. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings, and they will make vows to the LORD and fulfill them. 22 The LORD will strike Egypt, striking and healing, and they will return to the LORD. He will listen to their pleas and heal them.

A Highway of Peace Among the Nations

23 In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria, and Assyria will come into Egypt, and Egypt into Assyria, and the Egyptians will worship together with the Assyrians. 24 In that day Israel will be the third partner with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth, 25 whom the LORD of Hosts has blessed, saying, “Blessed be Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands, and Israel My inheritance.”
