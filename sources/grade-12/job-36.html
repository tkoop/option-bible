Elihu Continues His Defense of God’s Justice

1 Elihu continued and said:

“2 Bear with me a little longer, and I will show you; for I still have words to speak on God’s behalf. 3 I will carry my knowledge from afar and ascribe righteousness to my Maker. 4 For truly my words are not false; one perfect in knowledge is with you.

“5 Behold, God is mighty and does not despise anyone; He is mighty in strength of understanding. 6 He does not keep the wicked alive, but He gives justice to the afflicted. 7 He never withdraws His eyes from the righteous; He enthrones them with kings forever and exalts them.

God’s Discipline and Deliverance

“8 If they are bound in chains and caught in the cords of affliction, 9 then He declares to them their deeds and their transgressions, that they have acted arrogantly. 10 He opens their ears to instruction and commands them to turn back from iniquity. 11 If they obey and serve Him, they complete their days in prosperity and their years in pleasantness. 12 But if they do not obey, they perish by the sword and die without knowledge.

“13 The godless in heart harbor anger; they do not cry for help when He binds them. 14 They die in their youth, and their life ends among the male shrine prostitutes. 15 He delivers the afflicted through their affliction and opens their ears by adversity. 16 He is even drawing you out of distress into a broad place where there is no cramping, and the table set before you is laden with rich food.

“17 Yet you are filled with the judgment due the wicked; judgment and justice have seized you. 18 Beware lest wealth entice you, and do not let a great ransom turn you aside. 19 Will your riches avail you, or all your mighty strength? 20 Do not long for the night, when peoples are cut off in their place. 21 Be careful; do not turn to iniquity, for you have chosen it rather than affliction.

God’s Power Displayed in Creation

“22 Behold, God is exalted in His power; who is a teacher like Him? 23 Who has prescribed His way for Him, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? 24 Remember to extol His work, of which men have sung. 25 All mankind has looked upon it; men behold it from afar.

“26 Behold, God is great, and we do not know Him; the number of His years is unsearchable. 27 For He draws up the drops of water; they distill rain from His mist. 28 The clouds pour it down and drop it abundantly upon mankind. 29 Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds, the thunderings of His pavilion?

“30 Behold, He spreads His lightning over it and covers the depths of the sea. 31 For by these He judges the peoples and gives food in abundance. 32 He covers His hands with lightning and commands it to strike its mark. 33 Its thunder announces Him, and even the cattle sense what is rising.”
