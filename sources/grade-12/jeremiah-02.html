1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

The LORD Remembers Israel’s Early Faithfulness

“2 Go and proclaim in the hearing of Jerusalem: Thus says the LORD, ‘I remember your devotion in your youth, your love as a bride—how you followed Me in the wilderness, in a land not sown.’ 3 Israel was holy to the LORD, the firstfruits of His harvest. All who devoured it were held guilty; disaster came upon them, declares the LORD.

“4 Hear the word of the LORD, house of Jacob, and all the families of the house of Israel. 5 Thus says the LORD: What wrong did your fathers find in Me, that they went far from Me, followed worthless idols, and became worthless themselves? 6 They did not say, ‘Where is the LORD, who brought us up from the land of Egypt, who led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and ravines, through a land of drought and deep darkness, through a land no one passes through and where no one lives?’ 7 I brought you into a fertile land to eat its fruit and its goodness. But you came and defiled My land, and you made My inheritance an abomination. 8 The priests did not ask, ‘Where is the LORD?’ Those who handle the law did not know Me. The shepherds rebelled against Me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal and followed what does not profit.

The LORD Brings Charges Against His People

“9 Therefore I will contend with you, declares the LORD, and I will contend with your children’s children. 10 Cross to the coasts of Cyprus and see; send to Kedar and observe carefully; see whether such a thing has happened. 11 Has a nation ever changed its gods, though they are not gods? But My people have exchanged their Glory for what does not profit. 12 Be appalled at this, O heavens; shudder with great horror, declares the LORD. 13 For My people have committed two evils: they have forsaken Me, the fountain of living water, and hewn out for themselves cisterns—broken cisterns that hold no water.

The Consequences of Forsaking the LORD

“14 Is Israel a slave, or a home-born servant? Why has he become plunder? 15 Young lions roared at him; they raised their voices. They made his land a wasteland; his cities are burned without inhabitant. 16 Even the men of Memphis and Tahpanhes have humiliated you. 17 Have you not brought this on yourself by forsaking the LORD your God while He led you along the way? 18 And now what do you gain by going to Egypt to drink the waters of the Shihor? Or what do you gain by going to Assyria to drink the waters of the Euphrates? 19 Your own wickedness will discipline you, and your backsliding will rebuke you. Know and see that it is evil and bitter to forsake the LORD your God, and that you have no fear of Me, declares the Lord, the LORD of hosts.

“20 For long ago I broke your yoke and tore off your bonds, but you said, ‘I will not serve.’ Yes, on every high hill and under every green tree you bowed down as a prostitute. 21 I planted you as a choice vine, a vine of pure stock. How then have you turned into a degenerate vine, a wild vine to Me? 22 Even if you wash yourself with lye and use much soap, your guilt is still stained before Me, declares the Lord, the LORD. 23 How can you say, ‘I am not defiled; I have not gone after the Baals’? Look at your way in the valley; know what you have done. You are a swift young camel, twisting and turning in her course. 24 A wild donkey at home in the wilderness, panting for breath in her desire; who can restrain her passion? All who seek her will not weary themselves; in her month they will find her. 25 Keep your feet from going barefoot and your throat from thirst. But you said, ‘It is hopeless; no, for I have loved strangers, and after them I will go.’

Judgment on Idolatry and False Trust

“26 As a thief is ashamed when caught, so the house of Israel is ashamed—their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets. 27 They say to wood, ‘You are my father,’ and to stone, ‘You gave birth to me.’ For they have turned their back to Me and not their face. Yet in the time of their trouble they say, ‘Arise and save us.’ 28 But where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them arise, if they can save you in your time of trouble. For you have as many gods as you have cities, O Judah. 29 Why do you contend with Me? You have all transgressed against Me, declares the LORD. 30 In vain I struck your children; they accepted no discipline. Your own sword devoured your prophets like a destroying lion. 31 O generation, see the word of the LORD! Have I been a wilderness to Israel, or a land of thick darkness? Why do My people say, ‘We are free; we will come to You no more’?

“32 Does a virgin forget her ornaments, or a bride her attire? Yet My people have forgotten Me for days without number. 33 How skillfully you direct your course to seek love! Even the wicked women have learned your ways. 34 Also on your skirts is found the blood of the souls of the innocent poor. You did not find them breaking in. Yet in spite of all this 35 you say, ‘I am innocent; surely His anger has turned away from me.’ Behold, I will judge you, because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’ 36 How you gad about, changing your way! You will be put to shame by Egypt as you were put to shame by Assyria. 37 From there also you will go out with your hands on your head, for the LORD has rejected those you trust; you will not prosper by them.”
