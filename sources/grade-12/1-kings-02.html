David's Final Instructions to Solomon

1 As David's death drew near, he commanded his son Solomon, 2 "I am about to die, as all people do. Be strong and act like a man.

3 "Keep the charge of the LORD your God, walking in His ways and keeping His statutes, His commandments, His ordinances, and His testimonies, as it is written in the Law of Moses, so that you may prosper in everything you do and wherever you turn, 4 so that the LORD may establish His word that He spoke concerning me: 'If your sons keep their way, walking before Me in truth with all their heart and all their soul, then you shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel.'

5 "Moreover, you know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me—what he did to Abner son of Ner and to Amasa son of Jether, the two commanders of Israel's armies. He killed them, shedding the blood of war in peacetime, and stained his belt and sandals with their blood.

6 "So act according to your wisdom, and do not let him die peacefully in old age. 7 But show kindness to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them eat at your table, because they came to me when I fled from your brother Absalom. 8 Also, look—Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim, is with you. He cursed me with a grievous curse on the day I went to Mahanaim. Yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the LORD, saying, 'I will not put you to death by the sword.' 9 Now do not let him go unpunished, for you are a wise man and you will know what you should do to him. Bring him to the grave in blood."

10 Then David died and was buried in the City of David. 11 The days David reigned over Israel were forty years: seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. 12 Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was firmly established.

Adonijah's Petition and Solomon's Judgment

13 Then Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. She asked, "Do you come in peace?"

"Peace," he answered.

14 Then he said, "I have a word for you."

She replied, "Speak."

15 He said, "You know that the kingdom was mine and all Israel looked to me to reign. But the kingdom passed to my brother, for the LORD gave it to him. 16 Now I ask one request of you; do not refuse me."

She said to him, "Speak."

17 He said, "Please speak to King Solomon, for he will not refuse you, that he may give me Abishag the Shunammite as wife."

18 Bathsheba said, "Very well; I will speak to the king for you."

19 Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak for Adonijah. The king rose to meet her, bowed to her, and sat on his throne; then he had a throne set for the king's mother, and she sat at his right hand.

20 She said, "I am asking one small request; do not refuse me."

The king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not refuse you."

21 She said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as wife."

22 King Solomon answered his mother, "Why do you ask Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for him the kingdom as well—for he is my older brother—and for Abiathar the priest and Joab son of Zeruiah."

23 Then King Solomon swore by the LORD, saying, "May God do so to me and more if Adonijah has not spoken this word against his own life.

24 "Now therefore, as the LORD lives, who established me and set me on the throne of David my father and made me a house as He promised, surely Adonijah shall be put to death today."

25 King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he struck him down, and he died.

Abiathar's Exile and Joab's Death

26 To Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to Anathoth, to your field, for you are a man worthy of death. But I will not put you to death today, because you carried the ark of Lord the LORD before David my father and because you shared in all my father suffered."

27 So Solomon expelled Abiathar from being priest to the LORD, to fulfill the word of the LORD that He had spoken concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

28 The news reached Joab, for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he had not turned after Absalom. Joab fled to the tent of the LORD and took hold of the horns of the altar.

29 It was told to King Solomon that Joab had fled to the tent of the LORD and was beside the altar. Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him down."

30 Benaiah came to the tent of the LORD and said to him, "Thus says the king: 'Come out.'"

He replied, "No; I will die here."

Benaiah brought back word to the king, saying, "Thus spoke Joab, and thus he answered me."

31 The king said to him, "Do as he said, and strike him down and bury him, so that you may remove from me and from my father's house the innocent blood that Joab shed.

32 "The LORD will hold him accountable for that blood, because he fell upon two men more righteous and better than he and killed them with the sword—Abner son of Ner, commander of Israel's army, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of Judah's army—without my father David knowing.

33 "That bloodguilt will rest on the head of Joab and on the head of his descendants forever; but for David and his descendants and his house and his throne, there shall be peace forever from the LORD."

34 Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and struck him down and killed him, and he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.

35 The king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada in his place over the army, and Zadok the priest the king appointed in place of Abiathar.

Shimei's Broken Oath and Solomon's Secure Throne

36 The king sent and called for Shimei, and said to him, "Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and live there, and do not go out from there anywhere.

37 "On the day you go out and cross the Brook Kidron, know for certain that you will die; you will bear responsibility for your own death."

38 Shimei said to the king, "The word is good. As my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." Shimei lived in Jerusalem many days.

39 But after three years, two of Shimei's servants fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. They told Shimei, saying, "Behold, your servants are in Gath."

40 Shimei rose and saddled his donkey and went to Gath to Achish to seek his servants. Shimei went and brought his servants from Gath.

41 It was told to Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had returned.

42 The king sent and called for Shimei, and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the LORD and warn you, saying, 'On the day you go out and walk anywhere, know for certain that you shall surely die'? And you said to me, 'The word I have heard is good.' 43 Why then have you not kept the oath of the LORD and the commandment that I commanded you?"

44 The king said to Shimei, "You know in your heart all the evil that you did to David my father. The LORD will hold you accountable for your evil. 45 But King Solomon shall be blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the LORD forever."

46 The king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck him down, and he died. So the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon.
