A Psalm of Praise by David

1 I shall exalt You, my God and King; I shall bless Your name forever and ever. 2 Each day I shall bless You, and I shall praise Your name forever and ever. 3 Great is the LORD and exceedingly to be praised; His greatness is unsearchable.

4 One generation shall commend Your works to the next, proclaiming Your mighty acts. 5 They shall recount the glorious splendor of Your majesty, while I meditate upon Your wondrous works. 6 They shall proclaim the power of Your awesome deeds, and I shall declare Your greatness. 7 They shall pour forth the renown of Your abundant goodness and sing exultantly of Your righteousness.

The LORD's Compassion and Goodness

8 The LORD is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. 9 The LORD is good to all, and His compassion rests upon all that He has made. 10 All Your works shall give thanks to You, the LORD, and Your faithful ones shall bless You.

The Glory of His Eternal Kingdom

11 They shall speak of the glory of Your kingdom and declare Your power, 12 to make known to humanity Your mighty acts and the glorious splendor of Your kingdom. 13 Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and Your dominion endures throughout all generations.

The LORD's Sustaining Care

14 The LORD upholds all who stumble and raises up all who are bowed down. 15 The eyes of all look to You, and You give them their food in due season. 16 You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing. 17 The LORD is righteous in all His ways and kind in all His works. 18 The LORD is near to all who call upon Him, to all who call upon Him in truth. 19 He fulfills the desire of those who fear Him; He hears their cry and saves them. 20 The LORD preserves all who love Him, but all the wicked He will destroy.

21 My mouth shall declare the praise of the LORD, and let all flesh bless His holy name forever and ever.
