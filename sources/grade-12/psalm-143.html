A Prayer for Deliverance

1 The LORD, hear my prayer; in Your faithfulness listen to my supplications; in Your righteousness answer me. 2 Do not bring Your servant into judgment, for no one living is righteous before You.

3 For the enemy has pursued my soul; he has crushed my life to the ground and forced me to dwell in darkness like those who have been dead for ages. 4 My spirit is overwhelmed within me; my heart is appalled.

5 I remember the days of old; I meditate on all Your works; I ponder the work of Your hands. 6 I stretch out my hands to You; my soul thirsts for You like parched land. Selah.

7 Answer me quickly, the LORD; my spirit fails. Do not turn away from me, or I will be like those who descend into the pit. 8 Let me hear Your steadfast love in the morning, for I trust in You. Show me the way I should go, for to You I lift up my soul.

9 Deliver me from my enemies, the LORD; I take refuge in You. 10 Teach me to do Your will, for You are my God. Let Your good Spirit lead me on level ground.

11 For Your name's sake, the LORD, preserve my life. In Your righteousness bring me out of distress. 12 In Your steadfast love, destroy my enemies; annihilate all who afflict me, for I am Your servant.
