David’s Song of Deliverance

1 David composed this song to the the LORD on the day the the LORD delivered him from the grasp of all his enemies and from Saul’s hand. 2 He said: The the LORD is my Rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. 3 God is my Rock, in whom I take refuge; my shield, the horn of my salvation, my stronghold, my refuge, and my Savior: You rescue me from violence. 4 I call upon the the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies. 5 For the breakers of death encircled me; torrents of ruin overwhelmed me. 6 The cords of Sheol coiled around me; the snares of death confronted me. 7 In my distress I called to the the LORD; I cried to my God. From His temple He heard my voice, and my cry reached His ears.

The LORD’s Thunderous Rescue

8 Then the earth shook and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled and were shaken, because He was angry. 9 Smoke ascended from His nostrils, and consuming fire issued from His mouth; burning coals were kindled by Him. 10 He bowed the heavens and descended, with thick darkness beneath His feet. 11 He rode upon a cherub and flew; He was seen upon the wings of the wind. 12 He made darkness His covering around Him—dark waters and thick clouds of the skies. 13 Out of the brightness before Him, coals of fire burned. 14 The the LORD thundered from heaven; the Most High uttered His voice.

15 He sent out arrows and scattered them; He flashed forth lightning and routed them. 16 The channels of the sea were exposed, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of the the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils. 17 He reached down from on high and seized me; He drew me out of many waters. 18 He rescued me from my fierce enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me. 19 They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the the LORD was my support. 20 He brought me out into a broad place; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

Righteousness and Retribution

21 The the LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He has repaid me. 22 For I have kept the ways of the the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. 23 For all His ordinances are before me, and I have not turned aside from His statutes. 24 I have been blameless before Him, and I have kept myself from iniquity. 25 Therefore the the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my purity in His sight.

26 To the faithful You show Yourself faithful; to the blameless You show Yourself blameless. 27 To the pure You show Yourself pure; to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. 28 You save an afflicted people, but Your eyes are upon the proud, that You may bring them down. 29 For You are my lamp, O the LORD; the the LORD illumines my darkness. 30 For by You I can run through a troop; by my God I can leap over a wall. 31 As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him. 32 For who is God besides the the LORD? And who is a Rock except our God? 33 God is my strength and power; He makes my way perfect. 34 He makes my feet like the feet of deer and sets me on my high places. 35 He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 36 You have given me the shield of Your salvation, and Your gentleness exalts me. 37 You enlarge my steps beneath me, so my feet have not slipped.

Triumph and Thanksgiving

38 I pursued my enemies and destroyed them; I did not turn back until they were consumed. 39 I consumed and crushed them, so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet. 40 For You girded me with strength for battle; You subdued under me those who rose against me. 41 You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and I annihilated those who hated me. 42 They cried for help, but there was no one to save; they cried to the the LORD, but He did not answer them. 43 I beat them as fine as the dust of the earth; I trampled them like mud in the streets.

44 You delivered me from the strife of my people; You kept me as head of the nations. A people I did not know serves me. 45 Foreigners cringe before me; at the mere report of me they obey. 46 Foreigners lose heart and come trembling out of their strongholds. 47 The the LORD lives! Blessed be my Rock, and exalted be God, the Rock of my salvation. 48 God, who gives me vengeance and subdues peoples under me— 49 He delivers me from my enemies; You exalt me above my adversaries; You rescue me from violent men. 50 Therefore I will give thanks to You among the nations, O the LORD, and I will sing praises to Your name. 51 He gives great salvation to His king and shows steadfast love to His anointed, to David and to his descendants forever.
