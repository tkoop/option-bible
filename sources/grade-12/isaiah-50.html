The LORD's Challenge to Israel

1 Thus says the LORD: "Where is your mother's certificate of divorce, with which I sent her away? Or which of My creditors is it to whom I have sold you? Look, for your iniquities you were sold, and for your transgressions your mother was sent away. 2 Why, when I came, was there no man? When I called, why was there no one to answer? Is My hand too short to redeem? Or do I lack the power to deliver? Look, at My rebuke I dry up the sea and make rivers a wilderness; their fish stink for lack of water and die of thirst. 3 I clothe the heavens with blackness and make sackcloth their covering."

The Servant's Obedience and Vindication

4 The Lord the LORD has given Me a tongue of the learned, that I may know how to sustain the weary with a word. He awakens Me morning by morning; He awakens My ear to hear as the learned. 5 The Lord the LORD has opened My ear, and I was not rebellious; I did not turn back. 6 I gave My back to those who strike, and My cheeks to those who pluck out the beard; I did not hide My face from disgrace and spitting.

7 For the Lord the LORD helps Me; therefore I have not been disgraced; therefore I have set My face like flint, and I know that I will not be put to shame. 8 He who vindicates Me is near. Who will contend with Me? Let us stand together. Who is My adversary? Let him come near to Me. 9 Look, the Lord the LORD helps Me; who is he who will condemn Me? Look, they all will wear out like a garment; the moth will eat them.

A Call to Trust the LORD

10 Who among you fears the the LORD and obeys the voice of His Servant, though he walks in darkness and has no light? Let him trust in the name of the the LORD and rely on his God. 11 Look, all you who kindle a fire, who equip yourselves with firebrands, walk in the light of your fire and in the firebrands you have kindled! This you will receive from My hand: you will lie down in torment.
