Salutation and Thanksgiving

1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy, our brother, to the church of God in Corinth, together with all the saints throughout Achaia: 2Grace and peace be granted to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

The God of All Comfort

3Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and the God of all consolation, 4who consoles us in every affliction, so that we may be enabled to console those who are in any affliction with the consolation that we ourselves receive from God. 5For just as the sufferings of Christ overflow to us, so also our consolation abounds through Christ.

6If we are afflicted, it is for your consolation and salvation; and if we are consoled, it is for your consolation, which becomes operative as you patiently endure the very sufferings we endure. 7Our hope concerning you is steadfast, because we know that as you share in our sufferings, you will likewise share in our consolation.

Deliverance from Deadly Peril in Asia

8For we do not wish you to remain ignorant, brothers, of the affliction that befell us in Asia: we were burdened beyond all measure, beyond our own strength, so that we despaired even of life. 9Indeed, we felt within ourselves the sentence of death, yet this occurred so that we would not place confidence in ourselves, but in God, who raises the dead. 10He delivered us from so formidable a death, and He will deliver us again; in Him we have set our hope that He will continue to deliver us. 11You also are cooperating with us through your prayers, so that thanksgiving may be offered by many on our behalf for the gracious gift bestowed upon us through the prayers of many.

Integrity of Conscience and Revised Itinerary

12For our boast is this: the testimony of our conscience that we conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you, with holiness and sincerity that come from God, not with fleshly wisdom but by the grace of God. 13For we write nothing to you other than what you read and comprehend; and I hope that you will comprehend fully, even to the end, 14just as you have comprehended us in part, so that on the day of our Lord Jesus you may boast of us, even as we boast of you.

15Because I was confident of this, I intended to visit you first, so that you might receive a second measure of grace; 16and to pass through you on my way to Macedonia, and then to return to you from Macedonia, and to be sent by you on my way to Judea. 17When I planned this, did I act with fickleness? Or do I make my plans according to the flesh, so that with me there is "Yes, yes" and "No, no" simultaneously?

18But as God is faithful, our word to you is not "Yes" and "No." 19For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, whom Silvanus, Timothy, and I proclaimed among you, was not "Yes" and "No," but in Him it is always "Yes." 20For every promise of God finds its "Yes" in Him; therefore, through Him we also utter "Amen" to God for His glory.

21Now it is God who establishes us with you in Christ and has anointed us; 22He has also sealed us and given us the Spirit in our hearts as a pledge. 23But I call God as witness against my soul that it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth. 24Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers for your joy, because you stand firm in your faith.
