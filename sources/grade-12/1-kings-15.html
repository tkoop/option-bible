Abijam Reigns over Judah

1 In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam son of Nebat’s reign, Abijam assumed the throne over Judah. 2 He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom.

3 He followed all the sins his father had committed before him, and his heart was not wholly devoted to the LORD his God, as the heart of David his father had been. 4 Yet, for David’s sake, the LORD his God preserved a royal line for him in Jerusalem, raising up his son after him and strengthening the city. 5 For David had done what was right in the eyes of the LORD and had not turned aside from anything the LORD commanded throughout his life, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

6 War persisted between Rehoboam and Jeroboam throughout Abijam’s lifetime. 7 The remaining acts of Abijam, and all that he did, are recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. War also continued between Abijam and Jeroboam. 8 Abijam died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David, and his son Asa succeeded him.

Asa Reigns over Judah

9 In the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa became king of Judah. 10 He reigned in Jerusalem for forty-one years. His mother was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom. 11 Asa did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, as his father David had done.

12 He banished the male shrine prostitutes from the land and removed all the idols his ancestors had made. 13 He also deposed his mother Maacah from her position as queen mother, because she had made an abhorrent image for Asherah. Asa cut down her image and burned it in the Kidron Valley. 14 The high places were not removed, yet Asa’s heart was fully devoted to the LORD throughout his life. 15 He brought into the house of the LORD the silver, gold, and vessels that his father had consecrated, along with his own consecrated gifts.

16 War continued between Asa and Baasha king of Israel throughout their reigns. 17 Baasha king of Israel advanced against Judah and fortified Ramah, intending to block all movement into and out of the territory of Asa king of Judah.

18 Then Asa took all the silver and gold that remained in the treasuries of the LORD’s house and the royal palace, and he entrusted them to his servants. King Asa sent them to Ben-Hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, king of Aram, who resided in Damascus, with this message:

19 “Let there be a treaty between you and me, as there was between my father and your father. I am sending you a gift of silver and gold. Go and break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will withdraw from me.”

20 Ben-Hadad listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel. He struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Chinnereth, together with the whole land of Naphtali. 21 When Baasha heard of it, he ceased fortifying Ramah and withdrew to Tirzah. 22 Then King Asa issued a proclamation throughout Judah—no one was exempt—and the people carried away the stones and timber of Ramah that Baasha had used. With them King Asa built Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah.

23 The rest of Asa’s acts, his might, all he accomplished, and the cities he built are recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. In his old age, however, he became diseased in his feet. 24 Asa died and was buried with his fathers in the city of his father David, and his son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place.

Nadab Reigns over Israel

25 Nadab son of Jeroboam became king of Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned over Israel for two years. 26 He did evil in the sight of the LORD and followed his father’s example and the sin he had caused Israel to commit. 27 Baasha son of Ahijah, of the house of Issachar, conspired against him and struck him down at Gibbethon, a Philistine city, while Nadab and all Israel were besieging it. 28 Baasha killed Nadab in the third year of Asa king of Judah and reigned in his place.

29 As soon as he became king, Baasha struck down the entire house of Jeroboam. He did not leave Jeroboam a single survivor, but destroyed them all, according to the word the LORD had spoken through His servant Ahijah the Shilonite. 30 This happened because of the sins Jeroboam had committed and had caused Israel to commit, and because he had provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger. 31 The rest of Nadab’s acts and all he did are recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 32 War continued between Asa and Baasha king of Israel throughout their days.

Baasha Reigns over Israel

33 In the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king over all Israel in Tirzah, and he reigned for twenty-four years. 34 He did evil in the sight of the LORD and followed Jeroboam’s example and the sin he had caused Israel to commit.
