Jezebel Threatens Elijah

1 Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah had done, including how he had killed all the prophets with the sword. 2 Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, threatening, "May the gods strike me down and worse if, by this time tomorrow, I have not put you to death like one of them."

Elijah Flees and Despairs in the Wilderness

3 When Elijah saw this, he fled for his life and came to Beersheba in Judah, where he left his servant. 4 He went a day's journey into the wilderness and sat down under a broom tree. Then he asked that he might die, saying, "I have had enough, the LORD; take my life, for I am no better than my fathers."

5 He lay down and slept under the broom tree, and suddenly an angel touched him and said, "Get up and eat." 6 He looked, and there at his head was a loaf baked on hot stones and a jar of water. He ate and drank, and then lay down again.

7 The angel of the LORD came back a second time, touched him, and said, "Get up and eat, for the journey is too much for you." 8 He got up, ate and drank, and strengthened by that food he traveled forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

The LORD Speaks to Elijah at Mount Horeb

9 There he came to a cave and spent the night in it. Then the word of the LORD came to him, and He said, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

10 Elijah replied, "I have been intensely zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts, because the Israelites have forsaken Your covenant, torn down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I alone am left, and they are seeking my life to take it."

11 The LORD said, "Go out and stand on the mountain before the LORD." Then the LORD passed by. A great and mighty wind tore through the mountains and shattered the rocks before the LORD, but the LORD was not in the wind. After the wind came an earthquake, but the LORD was not in the earthquake. 12 After the earthquake came a fire, but the LORD was not in the fire; and after the fire came a soft, hushed whisper.

13 When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his cloak, went out, and stood at the entrance of the cave. Then a voice came to him and said, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

14 He answered, "I have been intensely zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts, because the Israelites have forsaken Your covenant, torn down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I alone am left, and they are seeking my life to take it."

The LORD Commissions Elijah's Successors and Calls Elisha

15 The LORD said to him, "Go back the way you came toward the wilderness of Damascus. When you arrive, anoint Hazael king over Aram. 16 Also anoint Jehu son of Nimshi king over Israel, and anoint Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah to be prophet in your place. 17 Jehu will put to death whoever escapes the sword of Hazael, and Elisha will put to death whoever escapes the sword of Jehu. 18 Yet I will spare seven thousand in Israel—all whose knees have not bowed to Baal and every mouth that has not kissed him."

19 So Elijah left there and found Elisha son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen ahead of him, while he himself drove the twelfth. Elijah passed by him and threw his cloak over him.

20 Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah, saying, "Please let me kiss my father and mother goodbye, and then I will follow you."

Elijah replied, "Go back; I have not compelled you." 21 So Elisha returned, took the yoke of oxen, and sacrificed them. He boiled their meat using the oxen's yokes as fuel and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he got up and followed Elijah and served him.
