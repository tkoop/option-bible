The LORD’s Zealous Return to Zion

1 The word of the the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying: 2 “Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: I am jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I am jealous for her with fierce wrath.”

3 “Thus says the the LORD: I have returned to Zion and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem. Jerusalem shall be called the City of Truth, and the mountain of the the LORD of Hosts, the Holy Mountain.”

Jerusalem Restored in Peace and Joy

4 “Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: Old men and old women shall again sit in the streets of Jerusalem, each leaning on a staff because of advanced age. 5 The streets of the city shall be filled with boys and girls playing in them.”

6 Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “If it seems beyond belief to the remnant of this people in those days, should it also seem beyond belief to Me?” declares the the LORD of Hosts.

7 “Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: Behold, I will save My people from the land of the east and from the land of the west. 8 I will bring them back, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem. They shall be My people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.”

Strengthened Hands and Renewed Prosperity

9 Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “Let your hands be strong, you who in these days hear these words from the mouth of the prophets who were present when the foundation of the house of the the LORD of Hosts was laid, so that the temple might be built. 10 For before those days neither man nor beast had wages, nor was there safety for anyone who came or went, because of the adversary; for I turned every man against his neighbor.”

11 “But now I will not deal with the remnant of this people as in the former days,” declares the the LORD of Hosts. 12 “For the sowing of peace will prosper: the vine shall give its fruit, the earth shall give its produce, and the heavens shall give their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to inherit all these things.”

13 “And as you have been a curse among the nations, O house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you shall be a blessing. Do not be afraid; let your hands be strong.”

Truth, Peace, and the Nations’ Pilgrimage

14 For thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “Just as I purposed to bring disaster upon you when your fathers provoked Me to anger, and I did not relent,” says the the LORD of Hosts. 15 “So now in these days I have purposed to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Do not be afraid.”

16 “These are the things you are to do: Speak truth to one another; render in your gates judgments that are true and make for peace. 17 Let none of you devise evil in your hearts against your neighbor, and love no false oath; for all these things I hate,” declares the the LORD.

18 And the word of the the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying, 19 “Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: The fasts of the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months shall become joyful and glad occasions, and cheerful feasts for the house of Judah. Therefore love truth and peace.”

20 Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “It shall yet come to pass that peoples and the inhabitants of many cities shall come. 21 The inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying,”

“Let us go at once to entreat the favor of the the LORD and to seek the the LORD of Hosts. I too will go.”

22 “And many peoples and strong nations shall come to seek the the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem, and to entreat the favor of the the LORD.”

23 Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “In those days ten men from every language among the nations shall take hold of the garment of a Jew, saying,”

“Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.”
