The LORD's Love for Israel

1 An oracle: The word of the LORD to Israel through Malachi.

2 "I have loved you," says the LORD. "But you ask,

"How have You loved us?"

"Is not Esau Jacob's brother?" declares the LORD. "Yet I have loved Jacob, 3 but Esau I have hated. I have laid waste his hill country and given his inheritance to the jackals of the desert."

4 If Edom says, "We are devastated, but we will rebuild the ruins,"

the LORD of Hosts says, "They may build, but I will tear down, and they will be called the territory of wickedness, and the people with whom the LORD is angry forever."

5 You will see it with your own eyes and say, "Great is the LORD beyond the border of Israel!"

The Priests Despise the LORD's Name

6 "A son honors his father, and a servant his master. If then I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am a master, where is My fear?" says the LORD of Hosts to you, O priests, who despise My name.

"But you ask, 'How have we despised Your name?'"

7 "By offering defiled food on My altar. But you say,

"How have we defiled You?"

"By saying that the table of the LORD may be despised." 8 "When you offer the blind for sacrifice, is that not evil? And when you offer the lame or sick, is that not evil? Present that to your governor; will he accept you or show you favor?" says the LORD of Hosts. 9 "And now entreat the favor of God, that He may be gracious to us. With such a gift from your hand, will He show favor to any of you?" says the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD Rejects Defiled Offerings

10 "Oh, that there were one among you who would shut the doors, that you might not kindle fire on My altar in vain! I have no pleasure in you," says the LORD of Hosts, "and I will not accept an offering from your hand." 11 "For from the rising of the sun to its setting My name will be great among the nations, and in every place incense will be offered to My name, and a pure offering. For My name will be great among the nations," says the LORD of Hosts. 12 "But you profane it when you say that the table of the LORD is polluted and its fruit, that is, its food, may be despised."

13 "But you say,

"What a weariness this is,"

"and you sniff at it," says the LORD of Hosts. "You bring what has been taken by violence or is lame or sick, and this you bring as your offering! Shall I accept that from your hand?" says the LORD.

14 "Cursed be the deceiver who has a male in his flock and vows it, and yet sacrifices to the LORD what is blemished. For I am a great King," says the LORD of Hosts, "and My name will be feared among the nations."
