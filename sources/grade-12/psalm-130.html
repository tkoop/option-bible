A Song of Ascents: From the Depths to Hope

1 Out of the depths I have cried to You, O the LORD. 2 O Lord, hear my voice; let Your ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications. 3 If You, O the LORD, kept a record of iniquities, O Lord, who could stand? 4 But with You there is forgiveness, that You may be revered.

Waiting in Hope for the LORD

5 I wait for the LORD; my soul waits, and in His word I hope. 6 My soul waits for the Lord more than watchmen wait for the morning—indeed, more than watchmen for the morning.

Israel’s Hope in the LORD’s Redemption

7 O Israel, hope in the LORD, for with the LORD there is steadfast love, and with Him abundant redemption. 8 And He will redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
