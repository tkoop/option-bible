The Vision Concerning Judah and Jerusalem

1 The message that Isaiah son of Amoz beheld concerning Judah and Jerusalem.

The Mountain of the LORD in the Last Days

2 In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD will be established as the chief of the mountains; it will be raised above the hills, and all the nations will stream to it. 3 Many peoples will come and say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways, so that we may walk in His paths." For out of Zion will go forth the law, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem. 4 He will arbitrate among the nations and render decisions for many peoples. They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not take up sword against nation, nor will they train for war anymore.

5 Come, O house of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the LORD.

The LORD's Indictment of His People

6 For You have abandoned Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled with influences from the east and with soothsayers like the Philistines, and they make alliances with foreigners. 7 Their land is full of silver and gold, and there is no end to their treasures. Their land is full of horses, and there is no end to their chariots. 8 Their land is full of idols; they bow down to the work of their hands, to what their fingers have made. 9 So mankind is humbled and brought low; do not forgive them.

The Day of the LORD

10 Go into the rocks and hide in the dust from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty. 11 The proud look of man will be humbled, and the loftiness of men will be brought low; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day.

12 For the Day of the LORD of Hosts will come against all that is proud and lofty, against all that is lifted up, and it will be brought low— 13 against all the cedars of Lebanon, lofty and lifted up, and against all the oaks of Bashan; 14 against all the high mountains and all the lofty hills; 15 against every high tower and every fortified wall; 16 against all the ships of Tarshish and every stately vessel. 17 So the pride of man will be brought low, and the loftiness of men will be humbled; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day. 18 And the idols will vanish completely.

19 People will flee to caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth. 20 In that day people will throw their silver and gold idols to the moles and bats—idols they made to worship. 21 They will enter the crevices of the rocks and the clefts of the cliffs, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth.

22 Stop trusting in man, whose life is but a breath in his nostrils. What is he worth?
