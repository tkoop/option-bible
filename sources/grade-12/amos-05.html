A Lament for Fallen Israel

1 Hear this word, O house of Israel—the lament I raise over you: 2 The virgin Israel has fallen; she will rise no more. She lies forsaken on her own soil, and no one lifts her up. 3 For thus says the Lord, the LORD: "The city that sends out a thousand men will have only a hundred remain, and the one that sends out a hundred will have only ten remain for the house of Israel."

A Call to Seek the LORD and Do Justice

4 For thus says the LORD to the house of Israel: "Seek Me and live. 5 Do not seek Bethel, do not enter Gilgal, and do not cross over to Beersheba; for Gilgal will surely go into exile, and Bethel will be reduced to nothingness." 6 Seek the LORD and live, lest He break out like fire in the house of Joseph and devour it, with no one to quench it in Bethel. 7 You who turn justice into wormwood and cast righteousness to the ground!

8 He who made the Pleiades and Orion, who turns deep darkness into morning and darkens day into night, who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out upon the face of the earth—the the LORD is His name. 9 He who makes devastation burst upon the strong, so that ruin overtakes the fortress.

10 They hate the one who reproves in the gate, and they abhor the one who speaks with integrity. 11 Therefore, because you trample the poor and exact from him a levy of wheat, you have built houses of hewn stone, but you will not dwell in them; you have planted pleasant vineyards, but you will not drink their wine. 12 For I know that your transgressions are many and your sins are mighty—you who oppress the righteous, accept bribes, and push aside the needy in the gate.

13 Therefore the prudent keep silent in such a time, for it is an evil time. 14 Seek good and not evil, that you may live, and so the the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, as you have said. 15 Hate evil and love good, and establish justice in the gate; perhaps the the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.

The LORD's Decree of Mourning

16 Therefore thus says the the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord: "In all the squares there will be wailing, and in all the streets they will cry, 'Alas! Alas!' They will summon the farmer to mourning and those skilled in lamentation to wailing. 17 In all vineyards there will be wailing, for I will pass through your midst," says the the LORD.

The Day of the LORD: Darkness, Not Light

18 Woe to you who desire the day of the the LORD! Why do you desire the day of the the LORD? It is darkness, and not light— 19 as when a man flees from a lion and a bear meets him, or he enters the house and leans his hand against the wall and a serpent bites him. 20 Is not the day of the the LORD darkness, and not light, and gloom with no brightness in it?

True Worship and the Coming Exile

21 "I hate, I despise your feasts, and I take no delight in your solemn assemblies. 22 Even though you offer Me your burnt offerings and your grain offerings, I will not accept them; and the peace offerings of your fattened animals I will not regard. 23 Take away from Me the noise of your songs; I will not listen to the melody of your harps. 24 But let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream."

25 "Did you bring to Me sacrifices and offerings during the forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel? 26 You will carry Sikkuth your king, and Kiyyun your star god—your images that you made for yourselves— 27 and I will send you into exile beyond Damascus," says the the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts.
