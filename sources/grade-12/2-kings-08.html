Elisha Protects a Woman from Famine

1 Elisha had said to the woman whose son he had restored to life, "Arise, and go—you and your household—and dwell wherever you can dwell, for the LORD has decreed a famine, and it will come upon the land for seven years." 2 The woman arose and did as the man of God had said; she went with her household and dwelt in the land of the Philistines for seven years.

3 When the seven years had ended, the woman returned from the land of the Philistines and went to the king to appeal for her house and her field.

4 The king was speaking with Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, and said, "Please tell me all the great things that Elisha has done."

5 While Gehazi was recounting to the king how Elisha had restored a dead man to life, the woman whose son he had restored to life came to appeal to the king for her house and her field. Gehazi said, "My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha restored to life."

6 The king asked the woman about it, and she told him. Then the king appointed an official for her and said, "Restore to her everything that was hers, together with all the produce of the field from the day she left the land until now."

Elisha Prophesies Hazael's Rise to Power

7 Elisha came to Damascus while Ben-hadad, king of Aram, was ill. When it was reported to him, "The man of God has come here,"

8 the king said to Hazael, "Take a gift in your hand and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the LORD through him, saying, 'Shall I recover from this illness?'"

9 So Hazael went to meet him, taking with him a gift of every good thing of Damascus, forty camel loads. He came and stood before Elisha and said, "Your son Ben-hadad, king of Aram, has sent me to you to ask, 'Shall I recover from this illness?'"

10 Elisha said to him, "Go and say to him, 'You will certainly recover.' Yet the LORD has shown me that he will certainly die."

11 Elisha fixed his gaze on him and stared until Hazael felt ashamed; then the man of God wept.

12 Hazael asked, "Why does my lord weep?" Elisha answered, "Because I know the evil you will do to the Israelites: you will set their fortresses on fire, you will kill their young men with the sword, you will dash their little ones to pieces, and you will rip open their pregnant women."

13 Hazael said, "What is your servant, a dog, that he should do so great a thing?" Elisha answered, "The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Aram."

14 Hazael left Elisha and returned to his master. When Ben-hadad asked, "What did Elisha say to you?" he answered, "He told me that you would certainly live."

15 But the next day Hazael took a thick cloth, dipped it in water, and spread it over the king's face, so that he died. Then Hazael reigned in his place.

Jehoram Reigns over Judah

16 In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, while Jehoshaphat was king of Judah, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat became king of Judah. 17 He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.

18 He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as the house of Ahab had done, for Ahab's daughter was his wife; and he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD.

19 Yet the LORD was unwilling to destroy Judah, for the sake of His servant David, because He had promised to preserve a lasting royal line for him and his descendants forever.

20 In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah and set up a king over themselves. 21 Then Joram crossed over to Zair with all his chariots. He rose by night and struck the Edomites who had surrounded him and his chariot commanders, but his troops fled to their tents.

22 So Edom has been in revolt against Judah to this day. Then Libnah revolted at the same time.

23 The rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

24 Joram slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

Ahaziah Succeeds Jehoram

25 In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, began to reign. 26 Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah, the granddaughter of Omri, king of Israel.

27 He walked in the way of the house of Ahab and did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, because he was related by marriage to the house of Ahab.

28 He went with Joram son of Ahab to war against Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram. 29 King Joram returned to Jezreel to be healed of the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael, king of Aram. And Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Joram son of Ahab, because Joram was sick.
