Collection for the Saints

1 Now concerning the collection for the saints, so you, too, should do as I directed the churches of Galatia. 2 On the first day of every week, each of you should set something aside and store it up, as he may prosper, so that no collections will be necessary when I come. 3 When I arrive, I will send with letters those whom you approve to carry your gift to Jerusalem. 4 If it seems advisable for me to go as well, they will travel with me.

Travel Plans and Fellow Workers

5 I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am going through Macedonia. 6 Perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go. 7 I do not want to see you now merely in passing; rather, I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits. 8 But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost, 9 because a great and effective door has opened for me, though many oppose me.

10 When Timothy arrives, see that you make him feel at ease among you, for he is doing the Lord's work, just as I am. 11 Therefore, let no one despise him; help him on his way in peace, so that he may return to me, for I am expecting him along with the brothers. 12 As for our brother Apollos, I urged him repeatedly to visit you with the brothers, but he was not at all willing to come now; he will come when he has an opportunity.

Final Exhortations and Greetings

13 Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, be courageous, and be strong. 14 Let everything you do be done in love. 15 You know that the household of Stephanas were the first converts in Achaia and that they have devoted themselves to serving the saints. I urge you, brothers, 16 to submit to such people and to everyone who works and labors alongside them. 17 I rejoice in the arrival of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus, because they supplied what was lacking on your part. 18 They refreshed my spirit as well as yours; therefore, recognize such people.

19 The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the church that meets in their home. 20 All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss. 21 I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 22 If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. Our Lord, come! 23 The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. 24 My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
