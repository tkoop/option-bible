Rehoboam Prepares for War and Receives a Warning

1 When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin—one hundred eighty thousand chosen warriors—to fight against Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam. 2 Then the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God: 3 "Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, 4 'Thus says the LORD: You shall not go up or fight against your brothers. Let every man return to his house, for this matter is from Me.'" So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from marching against Jeroboam.

Fortifying Judah and Benjamin

5 Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built cities for defense in Judah. 6 He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, 7 Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam, 8 Gath, Mareshah, Ziph, 9 Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah, 10 Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron—the fortified cities of Judah and Benjamin. 11 He strengthened the fortresses, placed commanders in them, and stored food, oil, and wine there. 12 He also put shields and spears in every city and strengthened them greatly. Thus Judah and Benjamin remained under his rule.

Priests and Levites Flee to Judah

13 Meanwhile, the priests and Levites throughout Israel stood with Rehoboam, coming to him from all their districts. 14 For the Levites had left their pasturelands and holdings and gone to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them from serving as priests to the LORD. 15 Jeroboam had appointed his own priests for the high places, for the goat idols, and for the calves he had made. 16 People from every tribe of Israel who set their hearts to seek the LORD, the God of Israel, followed them to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their fathers. 17 They strengthened the kingdom of Judah and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, because during those three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon.

Rehoboam's Family and Succession

18 Rehoboam married Mahalath, daughter of Jerimoth son of David, and Abihail, daughter of Eliab son of Jesse. 19 She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. 20 After her, he took Maacah daughter of Absalom, who bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith. 21 Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his other wives and concubines; in all he had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters. 22 He appointed Abijah son of Maacah as chief prince among his brothers, intending to make him king. 23 Acting wisely, he dispersed some of his sons throughout the territories of Judah and Benjamin, to every fortified city, providing them with abundant supplies and seeking wives for them.
