Woe to the Shepherds Who Scatter the Flock

1 Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture! declares the the LORD.

2 Therefore this is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the shepherds who tend My people: “You have scattered My flock and driven them away, and you have not attended to them. Behold, I will attend to you for the evil of your deeds,” declares the the LORD.

3 “Then I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock from all the lands to which I have driven them, and I will bring them back to their pasture, where they will be fruitful and multiply. 4 I will raise up shepherds over them who will tend them, and they will no longer be afraid or dismayed, nor will any go missing,” declares the the LORD.

The Promised Righteous Branch

5 “Behold, the days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch, and He will reign wisely and do justice and righteousness in the land. 6 In His days Judah will be saved, and Israel will dwell securely. And this is His name by which He will be called: The LORD Our Righteousness.”

7 “So then, the days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when they will no longer say, ‘As surely as the the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of Egypt,’ 8 but, ‘As surely as the the LORD lives, who brought and led the descendants of the house of Israel up out of the land of the north and from all the other lands to which I had banished them.’ Then they will dwell in their own land.”

Jeremiah’s Grief and the Condemnation of False Prophets

9 As for the prophets: My heart is broken within me; all my bones tremble. I am like a drunken man, like a man overcome by wine, because of the the LORD and because of His holy words. 10 For the land is full of adulterers; because of the curse the land mourns; the pastures of the wilderness are withered. Their course is evil, and their might is not right.

11 “For both prophet and priest are ungodly; even in My house I have found their wickedness,” declares the the LORD. 12 “Therefore their path will be like slippery paths in the darkness; they will be driven away and fall in it. For I will bring disaster upon them in the year of their punishment,” declares the the LORD.

13 “I have seen a horrible thing in the prophets of Samaria: They prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel astray. 14 And among the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen a horrible thing: They commit adultery and walk in falsehood; they strengthen the hands of evildoers, so that no one turns from his wickedness. They are all like Sodom to Me, and their inhabitants like Gomorrah.”

15 Therefore this is what the the LORD of Hosts says about the prophets: “I will feed them wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink, because from the prophets of Jerusalem ungodliness has spread throughout the land.”

16 This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: “Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you. They are filling you with false hopes; they speak visions from their own minds, not from the mouth of the the LORD.”

17 “They keep saying to those who despise Me, ‘The the LORD says you will have peace,’ and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his own heart, ‘No disaster will befall you.’” 18 “But who has stood in the council of the the LORD to see and hear His word? Who has given heed to His word and obeyed?”

19 “Behold, the storm of the the LORD has gone forth in wrath, even a whirling tempest; it will swirl about the head of the wicked. 20 The anger of the the LORD will not turn back until He has fully accomplished the purposes of His heart. In the latter days you will understand it clearly.”

21 “I did not send these prophets, yet they have run with their message; I did not speak to them, yet they have prophesied. 22 But if they had stood in My council, they would have proclaimed My words to My people and turned them back from their evil ways and their evil deeds.”

The LORD Sees All and Judges False Prophets

23 “Am I a God who is only near,” declares the the LORD, “and not a God far away? 24 Can a man hide in secret places where I cannot see him?” declares the the LORD. “Do I not fill the heavens and the earth?” declares the the LORD.

25 “I have heard what the prophets are saying who prophesy lies in My name, saying, ‘I had a dream! I had a dream!’ 26 How long will this continue in the hearts of these prophets who prophesy lies, these prophets of deceitful dreams? 27 They suppose they can make My people forget My name by their dreams, which they recount to one another, just as their fathers forgot My name for Baal.”

28 “Let the prophet who has a dream retell his dream, but let the one who has My word speak My word truthfully. What does straw have in common with grain?” declares the the LORD. 29 “Is not My word like fire,” declares the the LORD, “and like a hammer that smashes a rock?”

30 “Therefore behold, I am against the prophets,” declares the the LORD, “who steal My words from one another. 31 I am against the prophets,” declares the the LORD, “who wag their own tongues and declare, ‘The the LORD declares.’ 32 I am against the prophets who prophesy false dreams,” declares the the LORD. “They tell them and lead My people astray with their reckless lies, yet I did not send them or command them, and they do not profit these people at all,” declares the the LORD.

The Oracle of the LORD

33 “Now when these people or a prophet or priest asks you, ‘What is the oracle of the the LORD?’ you are to tell them, ‘You are the oracle—I will forsake you,’” declares the the LORD. 34 “As for the prophet or priest or anyone who says, ‘This is the oracle of the the LORD,’ I will punish that man and his household.”

35 “This is what you are to say to one another and to your brothers: ‘What has the the LORD answered? What has the the LORD spoken?’ 36 But never again use the phrase, ‘The oracle of the the LORD,’ for every man’s own word becomes his oracle, and you have perverted the words of the living God, the the LORD of Hosts, our God.”

37 “This is what you should ask the prophet: ‘What has the the LORD answered you? What has the the LORD spoken?’ 38 But if you say, ‘The oracle of the the LORD,’ then this is what the the LORD says: ‘Because you have used this phrase, “The oracle of the the LORD,” when I specifically told you not to, I will surely forget you.’ 39 I will surely forget you and cast you out of My presence, both you and the city I gave to you and your fathers. 40 I will bring upon you everlasting shame and perpetual disgrace that will not be forgotten.”
