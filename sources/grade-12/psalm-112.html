The Prosperity and Steadfastness of the Righteous

1 Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD and takes great delight in His commandments. 2 His descendants will be mighty in the land, and the generation of the upright will be blessed. 3 Wealth and riches will dwell in his house, and his righteousness will endure forever. 4 Light dawns in the darkness for the upright; he is gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

5 It is good for a man to be gracious and to lend; he will sustain his cause in judgment. 6 Surely he will never be shaken, and the righteous will be remembered forever. 7 He will not fear dire tidings; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD. 8 His heart is secure; he will not fear until he beholds his adversaries in triumph.

9 He has distributed his gifts freely to the needy; his righteousness endures forever, and his horn will be exalted in honor. 10 The wicked will see and be incensed; he will gnash his teeth and waste away, and the desire of the wicked will perish.
