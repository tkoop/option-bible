David's Thanksgiving for God's Steadfast Love

1 Of David. I will praise You with all my heart; before God I will sing Your praise. 2 I will bow down toward Your holy temple and give thanks to Your name for Your steadfast love and Your faithfulness, because You have exalted Your name and Your word above everything. 3 On the day I called, You answered me; You strengthened my resolve and fortified my soul.

4 All the kings of the earth will give You thanks, O the LORD, when they hear the words from Your mouth. 5 They will sing about the ways of the LORD, for great is the glory of the LORD.

6 Although the LORD is exalted, He cares for the humble; yet the proud He recognizes only from a distance. 7 Though I walk through trouble, You keep me alive; against my enemies' fury You extend Your hand, and Your right hand delivers me. 8 The LORD will accomplish His purpose for me. O the LORD, Your steadfast love endures forever—do not abandon what Your hands have made.
