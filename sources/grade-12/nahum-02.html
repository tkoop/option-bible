The Enemy Advances on Nineveh

1 The scatterer has come up against you. Guard the fortress, watch the road, brace for action, and marshal all your strength. 2 For the LORD will restore the splendor of Jacob like the splendor of Israel, though destroyers have laid them waste and ruined their vines.

The Battle for Nineveh

3 The shields of his mighty men are red; the warriors are clad in scarlet. The chariots flash with fire on the day they are made ready, and the spears of cypress are brandished. 4 The chariots dash through the streets; they race madly through the squares. Their appearance is like torches; they dart like lightning. 5 He summons his nobles; they stumble as they advance. They hasten to its wall; the siege shield is set up. 6 The river gates are thrown open, and the palace collapses. 7 It is decreed: she is exiled and led away; her maidservants moan like doves and beat upon their breasts.

The City Is Emptied and Plundered

8 Nineveh has been like a pool of water throughout her days, but now they are fleeing. "Stop! Stop!" they cry, but no one turns back. 9 Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! There is no end to the treasure, an abundance of every precious thing. 10 She is emptied! Yes, she is desolate and laid waste! Hearts melt, knees knock, bodies tremble, and every face grows pale.

The End of the Lion's Den

11 Where is the lions' den, the feeding ground of the young lions, where the lion and lioness prowled with their cubs, with nothing to make them afraid? 12 The lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled prey for his lionesses. He filled his lairs with prey and his dens with torn flesh. 13 "Behold, I am against you," declares the LORD of Hosts. "I will burn up your chariots in smoke, and the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will no longer be heard."
