The Beast Rising from the Sea

1 And I saw a beast rising out of the sea, bearing ten horns and seven heads, with ten diadems on its horns and blasphemous names on its heads. 2 The beast I saw resembled a leopard; its feet were like a bear's, and its mouth was like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave it his power, his throne, and great authority.

3 One of its heads seemed to have been mortally wounded, but its deadly wound was healed, and the whole earth marveled and followed the beast. 4 They worshiped the dragon because he had given authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who can make war against it?"

5 The beast was given a mouth to utter great and blasphemous words, and authority was given to it to act for forty-two months. 6 It opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme His name, His tabernacle, and those who dwell in heaven.

7 It was permitted to wage war against the saints and to conquer them, and authority was given to it over every tribe, people, language, and nation. 8 All who dwell on the earth will worship it—everyone whose name has not been written, from the foundation of the world, in the Book of Life of the Lamb who was slain.

9 Whoever has an ear, let him hear. 10 If anyone is destined for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone is to be killed by the sword, by the sword he must be killed. Here is the perseverance and faith of the saints.

The Beast Rising from the Earth

11 Then I saw another beast rising out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb, but it spoke like a dragon. 12 It exercises all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and it compels the earth and its inhabitants to worship the first beast, whose deadly wound was healed.

13 It performs great signs, even making fire descend from heaven to earth in the sight of people. 14 By the signs it was permitted to perform before the beast, it deceives the inhabitants of the earth, telling them to make an image for the beast that was wounded by the sword and yet lived.

15 It was granted to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast could speak and cause all who refused to worship it to be put to death.

The Mark and Number of the Beast

16 It also compels everyone—small and great, rich and poor, free and slave—to receive a mark on the right hand or on the forehead, 17 so that no one may buy or sell unless he has the mark: the name of the beast or the number of its name. 18 Here is wisdom: let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is a human number, and its number is 666.
