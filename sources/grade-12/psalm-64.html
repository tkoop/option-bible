A Prayer for Deliverance from Hidden Enemies

1 Hear my voice, O God, in my lamentation; preserve my life from the dread inspired by my enemy. 2 Conceal me from the clandestine counsel of evildoers, from the tumult of those who perpetrate iniquity.

3 They have whetted their tongues like swords; they have aimed bitter words as though they were arrows. 4 From ambush they shoot at the blameless; suddenly they loose their shafts, and they entertain no fear. 5 They fortify one another in wickedness; they conspire to conceal snares, saying, “Who will see us?” 6 They scrutinize iniquities and complete a meticulous search; the inward thought of each man is profound, and his heart is unfathomable.

7 But God will shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they will be wounded. 8 Their own tongues will precipitate their ruin; all who behold them will recoil.

9 Then all humanity will fear and proclaim the work of God, for they will discern His deed. 10 The righteous will rejoice in the LORD and trust in Him; all the upright in heart will glory.
