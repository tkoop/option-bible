The Mountain of God Exalted

1 In the latter days, the mountain of the house of the the LORD will stand firm as the foremost of the mountains, raised high above the hills, and peoples will stream to it. 2 Many nations will go and say, “Come, let us ascend to the mountain of the the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, that He may teach us His ways and that we may walk in His paths; for teaching will go forth from Zion, and the word of the the LORD from Jerusalem.” 3 He will judge between many peoples and settle disputes for mighty nations afar; they will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not raise sword against nation, nor will they train for war again. 4 Each man will sit beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, and no one will make him afraid, for the mouth of the the LORD of Hosts has spoken. 5 For every people walks in the name of its god, but we will walk in the name of the the LORD our God forever and ever.

The Remnant Gathered and Restored

6 In that day, declares the the LORD, I will gather the lame and assemble the outcast—those whom I have afflicted. 7 I will make the lame into a remnant and the distant into a mighty nation, and the the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion from now on and forever. 8 And you, tower of the flock, stronghold of the daughter of Zion, to you the former sovereignty will come; the kingdom will come to the daughter of Jerusalem.

Zion’s Distress and Triumph

9 Why do you cry aloud? Is there no king within you? Has your counselor perished, that pain has seized you like a woman in labor? 10 Writhe and groan, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in labor, for now you will leave the city and dwell in the open field; you will go as far as Babylon. There you will be rescued; there the the LORD will redeem you from the power of your enemies. 11 Now many nations are gathered against you, saying, “Let her be defiled, and let our eyes gloat over Zion.” 12 But they do not know the thoughts of the the LORD, nor do they understand His counsel, that He has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor. 13 Arise and thresh, O daughter of Zion, for I will make your horn iron and your hooves bronze; you will crush many peoples, and I will devote their gain to the the LORD and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.
