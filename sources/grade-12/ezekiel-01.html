The Vision by the Kebar River

1 In the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, while I was among the exiles by the river Chebar, the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God. 2 On the fifth day of the month—it was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin— 3 the word of the the LORD came to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar; and the hand of the the LORD was upon him there.

The Four Living Creatures

4 As I looked, a storm wind came from the north—a great cloud with fire flashing forth continually, and brightness all around it; and from its midst came something like gleaming metal, from the midst of the fire. 5 From its midst also came the likeness of four living creatures, and this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man. 6 Each had four faces and four wings. 7 Their legs were straight, and the soles of their feet were like the hooves of a calf; they gleamed like burnished bronze. 8 Human hands were beneath their wings on their four sides; and the four of them had faces and wings. 9 Their wings touched one another; they did not turn when they went, but each went straight ahead. 10 As for the likeness of their faces, each had a human face; on the right, a lion's face; on the left, an ox's face; and all four had an eagle's face. 11 Their faces and wings were separated above; two wings of each touched one another, and two covered their bodies. 12 Each went straight ahead; wherever the spirit was about to go, they went, without turning as they went. 13 As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like torches moving back and forth among the living creatures; the fire was radiant, and lightning flashed out of it. 14 The living creatures darted to and fro like flashes of lightning.

The Wheels Beside the Creatures

15 Now as I looked at the living creatures, a wheel was on the ground beside each living creature, corresponding to its four faces. 16 The appearance of the wheels and their construction was like the gleam of beryl, and all four had the same likeness; their appearance and construction were as though a wheel were within a wheel. 17 When they went, they went in any of their four directions without turning as they went. 18 As for their rims, they were high and awe-inspiring, and their rims were full of eyes all around the four of them. 19 When the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up. 20 Wherever the spirit was about to go, they went in that direction, and the wheels rose close beside them; for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels. 21 When those went, these went; and when those stood still, these stood still; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up close beside them; for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

The Expanse and the Throne

22 Over the heads of the living creatures there was the likeness of an expanse, shining like crystal, spread out above their heads. 23 Under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward the other; each had two which covered their bodies on this side and two which covered on that side. 24 When they moved, I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty—a sound of turmoil like the sound of an army. When they stood still, they lowered their wings. 25 A voice came from above the expanse that was over their heads; when they stood still, they lowered their wings. 26 Above the expanse over their heads was the likeness of a throne, in appearance like a sapphire stone; and on the throne, high up, was a figure with the appearance of a man. 27 From what appeared to be His waist upward, I saw something like gleaming metal, like fire enclosed all around; and from His waist downward, I saw what looked like fire, with brightness surrounding it. 28 Like the appearance of the rainbow in the clouds on a rainy day, so was the appearance of the surrounding radiance. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the the LORD. And when I saw it, I fell on my face and heard a voice speaking.
