Jonah’s Second Commission

1 The word of the the LORD came to Jonah a second time, saying, 2 “Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and proclaim to it the message I am telling you.”

Jonah Obeys and Warns Nineveh

3 So Jonah arose and went to Nineveh according to the word of the the LORD. Now Nineveh was an exceedingly great city—a journey of three days to cross. 4 Jonah began to enter the city, going a day’s journey, and he cried out, “Yet forty days, and Nineveh will be overthrown!”

Nineveh Repents

5 The people of Nineveh believed God, and they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest to the least. 6 When the word reached the king of Nineveh, he rose from his throne, removed his robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes.

7 Then he issued a proclamation in Nineveh: “By the decree of the king and his nobles, let no human being or animal—neither herd nor flock—taste anything at all. They must not eat or drink water. 8 Furthermore, let both people and animals be covered with sackcloth, and let them cry out earnestly to God. Let each one turn from his evil way and from the violence in his hands. 9 Who knows? God may turn and relent; He may turn from His fierce anger, so that we will not perish.”

God Relents from Judgment

10 When God saw their actions—that they had turned from their evil way—He relented from the calamity He had threatened to bring upon them, and He did not carry it out.
