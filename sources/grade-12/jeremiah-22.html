The LORD Commands Justice from the Throne

1 Thus says the the LORD: “Go down to the house of the king of Judah and speak this word there: 2 ‘Hear the word of the the LORD, O king of Judah, who sits on the throne of David—you, your servants, and your people who enter these gates. 3 Thus says the the LORD: Do justice and righteousness, and rescue the robbed from the hand of the oppressor. Do not mistreat or violently oppress the resident alien, the fatherless, or the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place. 4 For if you truly do this, then kings who sit on David’s throne will enter the gates of this house, riding in chariots and on horses—they, their servants, and their people. 5 But if you will not obey these words, I swear by Myself, declares the the LORD, that this house will become a desolation.’”

Judgment on the House of Judah

6 For thus says the the LORD concerning the house of the king of Judah: “You are like Gilead to Me, like the summit of Lebanon; yet I will surely make you a wilderness, like cities that are not inhabited. 7 I will appoint destroyers against you, each with his weapons; they will cut down your choicest cedars and cast them into the fire.”

8 Many nations will pass by this city, and every man will say to his neighbor, “Why has the the LORD done such a thing to this great city?” 9 And they will answer, “Because they have forsaken the covenant of the the LORD their God and have worshiped and served other gods.”

10 Do not weep for the dead, nor grieve for him; weep bitterly for the one who goes away, for he will never return or see his native land again. 11 For thus says the the LORD concerning Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who reigned in place of his father Josiah and who went away from this place: He shall not return here, 12 but he will die in the place to which they have carried him captive, and he will never see this land again.

Woe to the King Who Builds by Injustice

13 Woe to the one who builds his house by unrighteousness and his upper rooms by injustice, who makes his neighbor serve him for nothing and does not give him his wages, 14 who says, “I will build myself a spacious house with large upper rooms,” and cuts out windows for it, paneling it with cedar and painting it with vermilion.

15 Do you think you reign because you excel in cedar? Did not your father eat and drink and do justice and righteousness? Then it was well with him. 16 He judged the cause of the poor and needy; then it was well. Is not this to know Me? declares the the LORD. 17 But your eyes and heart are set only on your dishonest gain, on shedding innocent blood, and on practicing oppression and violence.

The Rejection of Jehoiakim and Coniah

18 Therefore thus says the the LORD concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, my brother!” or “Alas, my sister!” They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, lord!” or “Alas, his majesty!” 19 He will be buried like a donkey—dragged out and thrown beyond the gates of Jerusalem.

20 Go up to Lebanon and cry out, and lift up your voice in Bashan; cry out from Abarim, for all your lovers have been destroyed. 21 I spoke to you in your prosperity, but you said, “I will not listen.” This has been your way from your youth, for you have not obeyed My voice. 22 The wind will sweep away all your shepherds, and your lovers will go into captivity; then you will be ashamed and confounded because of all your evil. 23 O inhabitant of Lebanon, nested among the cedars, how you will groan when pangs come upon you, pain like that of a woman in labor!

24 As I live, declares the the LORD, though Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were the signet ring on My right hand, yet I would tear you from there. 25 I will give you into the hand of those who seek your life, and into the hand of those of whom you are afraid, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of the Chaldeans. 26 I will hurl you and the mother who bore you into another country, where you were not born, and there you will die. 27 They will never return to the land for which they long.

28 Is this man Coniah a despised, broken pot, a vessel no one wants? Why are he and his children hurled and cast into a land they do not know? 29 O land, land, land, hear the word of the the LORD! 30 Thus says the the LORD: Write this man down as childless, a man who will not prosper in his days, for none of his offspring will prosper, sitting on the throne of David or ruling again in Judah.
