A Prayer for the King's Triumph

1 May the LORD answer you in your day of distress; may the name of the God of Jacob set you securely on high. 2 May He send you help from the sanctuary and sustain you from Zion. 3 May He remember all your sacrifices and regard your burnt offerings with favor. Selah 4 May He grant you the desires of your heart and bring all your plans to fruition. 5 Let us shout for joy at your deliverance and raise our banners in the name of our God. May the LORD fulfill all your petitions.

Confidence in God's Anointed

6 Now I know that the LORD delivers His anointed; He answers him from His holy heaven with the mighty salvation of His right hand.

Trust in the LORD Alone

7 Some put their trust in chariots and others in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God. 8 They collapse and fall, but we rise and stand firm.

A Plea for the King

9 The LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call.
