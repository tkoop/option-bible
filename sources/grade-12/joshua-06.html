Jericho Sealed Under Siege

1 Now Jericho was shut tight because of the Israelites; no one went out, and no one came in.

2 Then the LORD said to Joshua, “See, I have delivered Jericho into your hands, along with its king and its elite warriors. 3 March around the city once with all the men of war; do this for six days. 4 Have seven priests carry seven rams' horns before the ark, and on the seventh day march around the city seven times while the priests blow the horns. 5 When you hear them sound a long blast on the horns, have all the people raise a great shout; then the city wall will collapse, and every man will charge straight in.”

Israel's Seven-Day March

6 So Joshua son of Nun summoned the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and have seven priests carry seven trumpets ahead of it.”

7 And he commanded the people, “Move out! March around the city, with an armed vanguard going ahead of the ark of the LORD.”

8 When Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven trumpets before the LORD advanced and blew their trumpets, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them. 9 The armed guard marched ahead of the priests who were blowing the trumpets, and the rearguard followed the ark, with the trumpets sounding continually.

10 But Joshua had commanded the people, “Do not raise a war cry, do not lift your voices, and do not let a word escape your lips until the day I tell you to shout; then shout!”

11 So he had the ark of the LORD carried around the city, circling it once; then they returned to camp and spent the night there. 12 Joshua rose early the next morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD. 13 The seven priests carrying the seven trumpets advanced, marching before the ark of the LORD and blowing the trumpets; the armed men went ahead of them, and the rearguard followed the ark of the LORD, while the trumpets kept sounding. 14 On the second day they marched around the city once and returned to camp. They did this for six days. 15 On the seventh day they rose at dawn and marched around the city seven times in the same manner; on that day alone they circled it seven times.

The Fall of the Walls

16 The seventh time around, when the priests sounded the trumpet blast, Joshua commanded the people, “Shout! For the LORD has given you the city! 17 The city and everything in it are to be devoted to the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute and all who are with her in her house will be spared, because she hid the messengers we sent. 18 But keep away from the devoted things, so that you will not bring about your own destruction by taking any of them. Otherwise you will make the camp of Israel liable to destruction and bring trouble on it. 19 All the silver and gold and the articles of bronze and iron are sacred to the LORD and must go into His treasury.”

20 When the trumpets sounded, the people shouted; at the sound of the trumpet the people raised a great shout, and the wall collapsed. Then everyone charged straight in, and they captured the city. 21 They devoted the city to the LORD and destroyed with the sword everything in it—men and women, young and old, cattle, sheep, and donkeys.

Rahab Spared and Joshua's Curse

22 Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, “Go into the prostitute's house and bring out the woman and all who belong to her, as you swore to her.”

23 So the young men who had been spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father and mother, her brothers and sisters, and all who belonged to her. They brought out her whole family and settled them outside the camp of Israel. 24 Then they burned the entire city and everything in it, but the silver and gold and the articles of bronze and iron they put into the treasury of the LORD's house. 25 But Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, along with her family and all who belonged to her, because she hid the men Joshua had sent to spy on Jericho—and she lives among the Israelites to this day.

26 At that time Joshua pronounced this curse: “Cursed before the LORD is the one who undertakes to rebuild this city, Jericho. At the cost of his firstborn he will lay its foundations, and at the cost of his youngest he will set up its gates.”

27 So the LORD was with Joshua, and his renown spread throughout the land.
