Ahab Covets Naboth's Vineyard

1 After these events, Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard in Jezreel, beside the palace of Ahab, king of Samaria.

2 Ahab said to Naboth, “Give me your vineyard, that I may make it a vegetable garden, for it is near my house; and I will give you a better vineyard in its place. Or, if it seems good to you, I will give you its price in silver.”

3 Naboth said to Ahab, “The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my ancestors.”

4 Ahab went into his house sullen and angry because Naboth the Jezreelite had said to him, “I will not give you the inheritance of my ancestors.” He lay down on his bed, turned his face away, and refused to eat.

5 Jezebel his wife came to him and said, “Why is your spirit so sullen that you are not eating?”

6 He said to her, “I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said to him, ‘Give me your vineyard for silver, or, if you wish, I will give you another vineyard in its place.’ But he said, ‘I will not give you my vineyard.’”

7 Jezebel his wife said to him, “Do you now rule Israel? Arise, eat, and let your heart be cheerful. I will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.”

Jezebel Plots Naboth's Death

8 So she wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to the elders and nobles who lived in Naboth's city.

9 In the letters she wrote, “Proclaim a fast and seat Naboth at the head of the people. 10 Set two worthless men opposite him, and let them testify against him, saying, ‘You have cursed God and the king.’ Then take him out and stone him to death.”

11 The men of his city—the elders and nobles who lived there—did as Jezebel had instructed them, just as it was written in the letters she had sent. 12 They proclaimed a fast and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

13 Then two worthless men came in and sat opposite him, and these worthless men testified against Naboth before the people, saying, “Naboth cursed God and the king.” So they carried him outside the city and stoned him to death with stones.

14 Then they sent word to Jezebel: “Naboth has been stoned and is dead.”

15 When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, she said to Ahab, “Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for money; for Naboth is no longer alive, but dead.”

16 When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he arose and went down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite to take possession of it.

Elijah Pronounces Judgment on Ahab

17 Then the word of the the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite:

18 “Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. Behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to take possession of it. 19 You shall speak to him, saying, ‘Thus says the the LORD: Have you killed and also taken possession?’ And you shall speak to him, saying, ‘Thus says the the LORD: In the place where the dogs licked the blood of Naboth, the dogs shall lick your blood—even yours.’”

20 Ahab said to Elijah, “Have you found me, my enemy?”

Elijah answered, “I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do evil in the sight of the the LORD. 21 Behold, I am bringing calamity upon you. I will consume your descendants and cut off from Ahab every male in Israel, slave or free. 22 I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah, because of the provocation with which you have provoked Me to anger, and because you have caused Israel to sin. 23 And concerning Jezebel the the LORD has declared: ‘The dogs will devour Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.’ 24 Anyone of Ahab's who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs, and anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by the birds of the air.”

25 Surely there was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do evil in the sight of the the LORD, because Jezebel his wife incited him. 26 He acted most abominably by going after idols, as the Amorites had done, whom the the LORD drove out before the Israelites.

Ahab's Repentance Delays Judgment

27 When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes, put sackcloth on his body, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and walked about dejectedly.

28 Then the word of the the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite: 29 “Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before Me? Because he has humbled himself before Me, I will not bring the disaster in his days; but in the days of his son I will bring the disaster upon his house.”
