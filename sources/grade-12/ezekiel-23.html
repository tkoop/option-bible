The Parable of Two Unfaithful Sisters

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2 "Son of man, two women were born to the same mother.

3 "They prostituted themselves in Egypt and gave themselves to promiscuity in their youth; there their breasts were caressed, and there their virginity was violated. 4 The elder was named Oholah, and her sister Oholibah. They became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.

5 "Oholah prostituted herself while she was still Mine. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, her neighbors— 6 warriors clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all desirable young men, horsemen riding horses. 7 She lavished her prostitution upon all of them, the choicest men of Assyria, and defiled herself with every idol of everyone she coveted. 8 She did not abandon the prostitution she had begun in Egypt, when men lay with her in her youth, caressed her virgin breasts, and poured out their desire upon her.

9 "Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, the Assyrians she had coveted. 10 They exposed her shame, seized her sons and daughters, and killed her with the sword. She became a byword among women, and they executed judgment upon her.

11 "Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she was even more depraved in her lust than her sister, and her prostitution exceeded her sister's. 12 She lusted after the Assyrians—governors and commanders, warriors arrayed in splendor, horsemen riding horses, all desirable young men. 13 I saw that both were defiled and had followed the same path.

14 "Yet she increased her prostitution. She saw men carved upon a wall, images of Chaldeans drawn in vermilion, 15 girded with belts around their waists and flowing turbans upon their heads, all resembling officers—the likeness of Babylonians, Chaldeans from their native land. 16 As soon as she saw them, she lusted after them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

17 "Then the Babylonians came to her bed of love and defiled her with their lust. After she had been defiled by them, she turned away from them in disgust. 18 When she flaunted her prostitution and exposed her shame, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister. 19 Yet she increased her prostitution, recalling the days of her youth when she prostituted herself in Egypt. 20 She lusted after lovers there whose flesh is like the flesh of donkeys and whose emission is like the emission of horses. 21 So you revisited the lewdness of your youth, when the Egyptians handled your breasts and caressed your youthful bosom.

Judgment Against Oholibah

22 "Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to rouse your lovers against you—those from whom you turned away in disgust—and I will bring them against you from every side: 23 the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans—Pekod, Shoa, and Koa—and all the Assyrians with them, desirable young men, governors and commanders, officers and men of renown, all riding horses.

24 "They will come against you with an assembly of peoples, with weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will surround you on every side with buckler, shield, and helmet. I will place judgment before them, and they will judge you according to their own standards. 25 I will pour out My jealousy upon you, and they will deal with you in wrath. They will cut off your nose and your ears, and your survivors will fall by the sword. They will seize your sons and daughters, and your survivors will be consumed by fire. 26 They will strip off your clothes and take away your fine ornaments. 27 So I will put an end to your lewdness and your prostitution, which began in Egypt. You will no longer lift your eyes to them, nor will you remember Egypt again.

28 "For this is what the Lord GOD says: I am going to deliver you into the hands of those you hate, into the hands of those from whom you turned away in disgust. 29 They will treat you with hatred and take all your wealth. They will leave you naked and bare, and the shame of your prostitution will be exposed. Your lewdness and prostitution will be uncovered. 30 I will do these things to you because you prostituted yourself with the nations and defiled yourself with their idols. 31 You have walked in the way of your sister; therefore I will put her cup into your hand.

32 "This is what the Lord GOD says: You will drink your sister's cup, a cup deep and wide; it will bring you scorn and derision, for it holds much. 33 You will be filled with drunkenness and grief—a cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria. 34 You will drink it and drain it; you will scrape its shards with your teeth and tear your breasts. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

35 "Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you must bear the consequences of your lewdness and prostitution."

The Abominations Declared

36 The LORD said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their abominations.

37 "For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They committed adultery with their idols, and they even sacrificed to them the children they bore to Me. 38 They have also done this to Me: they defiled My sanctuary on that day and profaned My Sabbaths. 39 On the very day they sacrificed their children to their idols, they entered My sanctuary to profane it. Yes, this is what they did inside My house.

40 "Furthermore, they sent for men who came from afar. When they arrived, you bathed yourself for them, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with jewelry. 41 You sat on a stately couch with a table spread before it, on which you had placed My incense and My oil. 42 The sound of a carefree crowd was there; and with ordinary men, drunkards were brought in from the desert. They put bracelets on the women's wrists and beautiful crowns upon their heads.

43 "Then I said of her who was worn out by adulteries, 'Will they now commit adultery with her, and she with them?' 44 Men went in to her as to a prostitute. So they went in to Oholah and Oholibah, those lewd women.

45 "But righteous men will judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and murderers, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands. 46 For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will bring a mob against them and consign them to terror and plunder. 47 The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn their houses.

48 "So I will put an end to lewdness in the land, and all the women will be warned not to imitate your lewdness. 49 They will repay you for your lewdness, and you will bear the penalty of your idolatry. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD."
