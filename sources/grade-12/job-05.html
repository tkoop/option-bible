Eliphaz Challenges Job to Call on the Holy Ones

“1 Call out now—will anyone answer you? To which of the holy ones will you turn? 2 For wrath slays the fool, and envy destroys the simple. 3 I have seen a fool take root, yet suddenly I cursed his dwelling. 4 His sons are driven far from safety; they are crushed in the gate, and no one delivers them. 5 The hungry consume his harvest, seizing it even from among thorns, while the thirsty pant after his wealth. 6 For misery does not spring from the dust, nor does trouble sprout from the soil. 7 Yet man is born to trouble, as surely as sparks fly upward.”

God’s Power to Exalt and Overthrow

“8 But I would seek God and lay my case before Him. 9 He does great things beyond searching out, wonders without number. 10 He gives rain to the earth and sends water over the fields. 11 He sets the lowly on high, and those who mourn He lifts to safety. 12 He frustrates the plans of the crafty, so their hands accomplish nothing. 13 He catches the wise in their own cunning, and the counsel of the crooked is swept away. 14 Darkness overtakes them at noon; at midday they grope as in the night. 15 He saves the needy from their cutting words and from the hand of the mighty. 16 So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts its mouth.”

The Blessing of Divine Discipline

“17 Blessed is the man whom God corrects; therefore do not despise the discipline of the Almighty. 18 For He wounds, yet He binds up; He shatters, yet His hands heal. 19 He will rescue you from six calamities; in seven no harm will befall you. 20 In famine He will redeem you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. 21 You will be shielded from the scourge of the tongue and will not fear when destruction comes. 22 You will laugh at destruction and famine, and you will not fear the beasts of the earth. 23 For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field, and the beasts of the earth will be at peace with you. 24 You will know that your tent is secure; you will inspect your property and find nothing missing. 25 You will know that your children are many, and your descendants like the grass of the earth. 26 You will come to the grave in full vigor, like a sheaf gathered in its season.”

Eliphaz’s Final Counsel

“27 We have searched this out, and it is true. Hear it, then, and apply it to yourself.”
