God’s Love for Israel and Their Rebellion

1When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called My son. 2The more they called them, the more they went from them. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to idols.

3Yet it was I who taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by the arms; but they did not know that I had healed them. 4I drew them with cords of human kindness, with ropes of love. I was to them like one who lifts a yoke from their necks, and I bent down to feed them.

Judgment for Persistent Rebellion

5They will not return to Egypt; Assyria will be their king, because they refused to return to Me. 6The sword will flash in their cities, destroy the bars of their gates, and devour them because of their own counsel. 7My people are bent on turning away from Me. Though they call to the Most High, He will not exalt them.

Divine Compassion and Restoration

8How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I surrender you, Israel? How can I make you like Admah, or treat you like Zeboiim? My heart is turned within Me; My compassion is stirred. 9I will not carry out the fierceness of My anger; I will not destroy Ephraim again. For I am God and not a man—the Holy One among you—and I will not come in wrath.

10They will walk after the the LORD; He will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west. 11They will come trembling like birds from Egypt, like doves from the land of Assyria, and I will settle them in their homes, declares the the LORD. 12Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit; but Judah still walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One.
