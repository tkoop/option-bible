The Potter and the Clay

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD said, 2 "Arise and go down to the potter's house, and there I will let you hear My words."

3 So I went down to the potter's house, and there he was working at the wheels. 4 The vessel he was fashioning from clay was spoiled in the potter's hand, so he reworked it into another vessel, as it seemed good to the potter to make.

God's Conditional Judgment

5 Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 6 "O house of Israel, can I not do with you as this potter does?" declares the LORD. "Look, as the clay is in the potter's hand, so are you in My hand, O house of Israel!

7 "The instant I speak concerning a nation and concerning a kingdom, to uproot and to tear down and to destroy it, 8 if that nation against which I have spoken turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I intended to bring upon it.

9 "And the instant I speak concerning a nation and concerning a kingdom, to build and to plant it, 10 if it does evil in My sight, so that it does not obey My voice, then I will relent concerning the good with which I said I would benefit it.

A Call to Repentance

11 "Now therefore speak to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: Behold, I am fashioning disaster against you and devising a plan against you. Return now, every man, from his evil way, and make your ways and your deeds good.'"

12 But they said, "It is utterly futile! For we will walk according to our own plans, and each of us will follow the stubborn dictates of his evil heart."

Judah's Appalling Defection

13 Therefore thus says the LORD: "Ask now among the nations, 'Who has heard such things?' The virgin of Israel has committed an appalling atrocity.

14 "Would anyone abandon the snow of Lebanon that descends from the rocky slopes? Or would the cold waters flowing from a distant source be forsaken? 15 Yet My people have forgotten Me; they burn incense to worthless idols, and they have caused themselves to stumble from the ancient paths and to walk in bypaths rather than on the main road.

16 "They have made their land a desolation and a lasting object of scorn; everyone who passes by it is appalled and shakes his head. 17 I will scatter them before the enemy like an east wind; in the day of their calamity I will turn My back on them and not My face."

Conspiracy and Jeremiah's Plea

18 Then they said, "Come, let us devise plans against Jeremiah, for instruction will not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, let us destroy him with slander, and let us not heed any of his words."

19 Attend to me, O the LORD. Listen to what my accusers are saying!

20 Should evil be repaid with good? For they have laid a trap for my life. Remember how I stood before You to speak good on their behalf, to turn away Your wrath from them.

21 Therefore hand their children over to famine and deliver them to the sword. Let their wives be bereaved and widowed; let their men be put to death, their young men struck down by the sword in battle.

22 Let a cry be heard from their houses when You suddenly bring raiders against them, for they have laid a trap to capture me and have hidden traps for my feet.

23 But You, O the LORD, know all their counsel against me to slay me. Do not pardon their iniquity, nor blot out their sin from Your sight. Let them stumble before You; deal with them in the time of Your anger.
