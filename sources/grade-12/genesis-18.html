The LORD Appears to Abraham

1 The LORD appeared to Abraham by the oaks of Mamre while he sat at the entrance of his tent in the heat of the day. 2 He raised his eyes and saw three men standing nearby. When he saw them, he ran from the tent entrance to meet them and bowed low to the ground.

3 Abraham said, "My Lord, if I have found favor in your sight, please do not pass by your servant.

4 "Let a little water be brought, and wash your feet, and refresh yourselves under the tree.

5 "I will bring a morsel of bread, and restore your strength; then you may pass on, for that is why you have come to your servant."

And they said, "Do as you have said."

6 Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Quickly prepare three seahs of fine flour, knead it, and make cakes."

7 Abraham ran to the herd and took a calf, tender and choice, and gave it to the young man, who hurried to prepare it. 8 He took curds and milk and the calf that he had prepared, and set it before them; he stood by them under the tree while they ate.

Sarah Is Promised a Son

9 Then they said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?"

He said, "She is in the tent."

10 And He said, "I will surely return to you about this time next year, and behold, Sarah your wife shall have a son."

Sarah was listening at the tent door behind him.

11 Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years; Sarah had passed beyond childbearing age.

12 So Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "After I am worn out, and my husband is old, shall I have such pleasure?"

13 And the LORD said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh and say, 'Shall I indeed bear a child, now that I am old?' 14 Is anything too difficult for the LORD? At the appointed time I will return to you, about this time next year, and Sarah shall have a son."

15 But Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid.

He said, "No, but you did laugh."

The LORD Reveals His Plan for Sodom

16 Then the men rose from there and looked down toward Sodom, and Abraham went with them to send them on their way.

17 And the LORD said, "Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do, 18 seeing that Abraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him? 19 For I have known him, that he may command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the LORD by doing righteousness and justice, so that the LORD may bring to Abraham what He has spoken concerning him."

20 And the LORD said, "Because the outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and their sin is very grave, 21 I will go down now and see whether they have done altogether according to the outcry that has come to Me; and if not, I will know."

22 So the men turned from there and went toward Sodom, but Abraham still stood before the LORD.

Abraham Intercedes for Sodom

23 Then Abraham drew near and said, "Will you destroy the righteous along with the wicked? 24 Suppose there are fifty righteous within the city; will you then destroy the place and not spare it for the fifty righteous who are in it? 25 It would be unthinkable for You to do such a thing, to put the righteous to death with the wicked, so that the righteous fare as the wicked! It would be unthinkable for You! Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?"

26 And the LORD said, "If I find at Sodom fifty righteous within the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake."

27 Abraham answered and said, "Behold, I have undertaken to speak to the Lord, though I am but a mortal man. 28 Suppose five of the fifty righteous are lacking—will you destroy the whole city for lack of five?"

And He said, "If I find forty-five there, I will not destroy it."

29 Again he spoke to Him and said, "Suppose forty are found there."

He said, "For the sake of forty I will not do it."

30 Then he said, "Oh, let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak: suppose thirty are found there."

He said, "I will not do it, if I find thirty there."

31 And he said, "Behold, now I have taken it upon myself to speak to the Lord: suppose twenty are found there."

He said, "For the sake of twenty I will not destroy it."

32 Then he said, "Oh, let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak just once more: suppose ten are found there."

He said, "For the sake of ten I will not destroy it."

33 And when the LORD had finished speaking to Abraham, Abraham returned to his place.
