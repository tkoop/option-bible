Jeremiah's Twenty-Three Years of Warning

1 The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah—the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 2 This is what Jeremiah the prophet said to all the people of Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem:

3 "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this very day—twenty-three years—the word of the the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened. 4 And the the LORD has sent to you all His servants the prophets again and again, but you have not listened or inclined your ears to hear."

5 They said, "Turn now, each of you, from your evil way and deeds, and you can dwell in the land that the the LORD has given to you and your fathers forever and ever. 6 Do not follow other gods to serve and worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands. Then I will not harm you."

7 "But you did not listen to Me," declares the the LORD, "so as to provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands to your own harm."

8 Therefore this is what the the LORD of Hosts says:

"Because you have not obeyed My words, 9 behold, I will summon all the families of the north," declares the the LORD, "and I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and I will bring them against this land and its inhabitants and against all the surrounding nations. I will devote them to destruction and make them an object of horror and scorn, an everlasting desolation. 10 Moreover, I will banish from them the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp. 11 And this whole land will become a desolate wasteland, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years. 12 But when seventy years are complete, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans, for their guilt," declares the the LORD, "and I will make it an everlasting desolation. 13 I will bring upon that land all the words I have pronounced against it, everything written in this book that Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations. 14 For many nations and great kings will enslave them, and I will repay them according to their deeds and the work of their hands."

The Cup of Wrath for the Nations

15 For this is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me:

"Take from My hand this cup of the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it. 16 And they will drink and stagger and go mad because of the sword that I will send among them."

17 So I took the cup from the the LORD's hand and made all the nations drink it, to whom the the LORD had sent me: 18 Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and officials, to make them a desolation, an object of horror and scorn, and a curse, as they are this day; 19 Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials, his people, 20 and all the mixed tribes; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod; 21 Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites; 22 all the kings of Tyre and Sidon; the kings of the coastlands across the sea; 23 Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who cut the corners of their hair; 24 all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed tribes who dwell in the desert; 25 all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media; 26 all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another, and all the other kingdoms of the earth. And after all of them, the king of Sheshach will drink it too.

27 Then you are to tell them that this is what the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says:

"Drink, get drunk, and vomit; fall and rise no more, because of the sword I will send among you."

28 If they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, then you are to tell them that this is what the the LORD of Hosts says:

"You must drink it! 29 For behold, I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears My name, and do you think you will go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am summoning a sword against all the inhabitants of the earth," declares the the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD's Roar and Universal Judgment

30 "Now as for you, prophesy all these words to them and say to them that the the LORD will roar from on high; He will raise His voice from His holy dwelling. He will roar loudly over His pasture; He will shout like those who tread the grapes against all the inhabitants of the earth. 31 The tumult will resound to the ends of the earth, because the the LORD has an indictment against the nations; He will execute judgment on all mankind; He will put the wicked to the sword," declares the the LORD.

32 This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: "Behold, disaster is going out from nation to nation; a great storm is being stirred up from the farthest ends of the earth." 33 Those slain by the the LORD on that day will be spread from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried; they will be like dung on the face of the ground.

34 Wail, you shepherds, and cry out; roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock, for the days of your slaughter have come, and you will be shattered like a precious vessel. 35 Flight will elude the shepherds, and escape will elude the leaders of the flock. 36 Hear the sound of the shepherds' cries and the wails of the leaders of the flock, for the the LORD is destroying their pasture. 37 The peaceful pastures will become desolate because of the fierce anger of the the LORD. 38 Like a lion He has left His den, for their land has become a desolation because of the wrath of the oppressor and because of His fierce anger.
