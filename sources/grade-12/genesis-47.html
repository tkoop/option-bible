Joseph Presents His Family to Pharaoh

1 Joseph went to Pharaoh and reported, "My father and my brothers, along with their flocks, herds, and everything they own, have come from Canaan and are now in Goshen." 2 He selected five of his brothers and presented them to Pharaoh.

3 Pharaoh asked them, "What is your occupation?"

His brothers answered, "Your servants are shepherds, as our fathers were." 4 They also said to Pharaoh, "We have come to reside temporarily in this land because your servants' flocks have no pasture; the famine is severe in Canaan. Please let your servants settle in Goshen."

5 Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Your father and your brothers have come to you. 6 The land of Egypt lies before you; settle your father and your brothers in the best part of the land. Let them live in Goshen, and if you know any capable men among them, appoint them as overseers of my livestock."

Jacob Blesses Pharaoh

7 Then Joseph brought his father Jacob and presented him to Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

8 Pharaoh asked Jacob, "How many are the days of the years of your life?"

9 Jacob replied to Pharaoh, "The days of the years of my sojournings are one hundred thirty years. Few and difficult have been the days of my life, and they have not reached the days of the years of the lives of my fathers during their sojournings." 10 So Jacob blessed Pharaoh and withdrew from his presence.

11 Joseph settled his father and his brothers and gave them a holding in Egypt, in the best part of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded. 12 Joseph also provided his father, his brothers, and all his father's household with food, according to the number of their dependents.

Joseph Administers the Famine

13 There was no bread in all the land, because the famine was very severe; the lands of Egypt and Canaan languished because of the famine. 14 Joseph collected all the money that was found in Egypt and Canaan in payment for the grain they were buying, and he brought the money into Pharaoh's house.

15 When the money in Egypt and Canaan was spent, all the Egyptians came to Joseph and said, "Give us bread! Why should we die before you? Our money is gone."

16 Joseph answered, "Give your livestock, and I will give you bread in exchange for your livestock, since your money is gone." 17 So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for the horses, the flocks, the cattle, and the donkeys. He sustained them with bread throughout that year in exchange for all their livestock.

18 When that year ended, they came to him in the second year and said, "We will not conceal from my lord that our money is gone and our livestock belong to my lord. Nothing remains before my lord except our bodies and our land. 19 Why should we die before your eyes, both we and our land? Buy us and our land for bread, and we and our land will be servants to Pharaoh. Give us seed, that we may live and not die, and that the land may not become desolate."

20 So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh, because every Egyptian sold his field when the famine grew severe over them, and the land became Pharaoh's. 21 As for the people, he moved them to the cities from one end of Egypt to the other. 22 Only the land of the priests did he not buy, because the priests had an allotment from Pharaoh, and they lived on the allotment that Pharaoh gave them; therefore they did not sell their land.

23 Then Joseph said to the people, "Today I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you; sow the land. 24 When the crops come in, you shall give a fifth to Pharaoh, and the remaining four-fifths shall be yours for seed for the field, for your food, for those in your households, and for feeding your little ones." 25 They said, "You have saved our lives. May we find favor in the eyes of my lord, and we will be Pharaoh's servants." 26 So Joseph established it as a statute concerning the land of Egypt to this day: a fifth belongs to Pharaoh, except that the land of the priests alone did not become Pharaoh's.

27 Thus Israel settled in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen, and they acquired property there and were exceedingly fruitful and multiplied. 28 Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years, so the days of Jacob's life were one hundred forty-seven years.

Jacob's Final Request

29 When the time drew near for Israel to die, he called his son Joseph and said, "If now I have found favor in your eyes, put your hand under my thigh and deal with me in kindness and truth: do not bury me in Egypt. 30 When I lie down with my fathers, carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their burial place."

"I will do as you have spoken."

31 Jacob said, "Swear to me." So Joseph swore to him, and Israel bowed in worship at the head of his bed.
