A Prayer for the Righteous King

1 Give the king your judgments, O God, and your righteousness to the king's son. 2 He will judge your people in righteousness and your afflicted with justice. 3 The mountains will bring peace to the people, and the hills will bring righteousness. 4 He will vindicate the afflicted among the people; He will save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor. 5 They will fear you while the sun endures, and as long as the moon shines, throughout all generations. 6 He will come down like rain upon freshly mown grass, like showers that water the earth. 7 In His days the righteous will flourish, and peace will abound until the moon is no more.

The King's Universal Dominion

8 He will rule from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth. 9 Desert nomads will bow before Him, and His enemies will be humbled to the dust. 10 The kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands will bring tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba will present gifts. 11 All kings will bow down to Him, and all nations will serve Him.

Compassion for the Needy and National Prosperity

12 For He will deliver the needy who cry out, and the afflicted who have no helper. 13 He will have compassion on the poor and needy and preserve the lives of the needy. 14 He will redeem their lives from oppression and violence, and their blood will be precious in His sight. 15 Long may He live! May gold from Sheba be given to Him. May people pray for Him continually, and may they bless Him all day long. 16 May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it wave upon the mountaintops. May its fruit be like the abundance of Lebanon, and may people flourish in the cities like grass upon the earth. 17 May His name endure forever; may it increase as long as the sun shines. May all nations be blessed in Him, and may they call Him blessed.

Doxology and Conclusion

18 Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonders. 19 And blessed be His glorious name forever; may the whole earth be filled with His glory. Amen and Amen. 20 The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended.
