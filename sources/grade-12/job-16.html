Job Replies to His Friends

1 Then Job answered: 2 "I have heard plenty of talk like this; all of you are miserable comforters. 3 Will these windy speeches never end? What drives you to keep answering?

4 "I could speak as you do if you were in my place; I could pile up words against you and shake my head at you. 5 I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the solace of my lips would ease your pain. 6 If I speak, my pain is not eased; and if I fall silent, how much of it leaves me?

God Has Made Me His Target

7 "Surely now He has worn me out; You have devastated my entire household. 8 You have bound me, and it has become a witness; my frailty rises up against me and testifies to my face. 9 His anger has torn and hounded me; He has gnashed His teeth at me; my adversary sharpens His eyes against me. 10 They gape at me with their mouths; they strike my cheeks with contempt; they mass together against me. 11 God has delivered me to the ungodly and cast me into the hands of the wicked. 12 I was at ease, but He shattered me; He seized me by the neck and broke me apart. He has set me up as His target. 13 His archers surround me; He pierces my kidneys without mercy and pours my gall on the ground. 14 He breaks me with wound upon wound; He rushes at me like a warrior.

15 "I have sewn sackcloth over my skin and buried my dignity in the dust. 16 My face is flushed with weeping, and the shadow of death lies over my eyelids. 17 Although my hands are free of violence and my prayer is pure.

Job Appeals to Heaven for Vindication

18 "O earth, do not cover my blood, and let my cry find no resting place. 19 Even now my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high. 20 My friends mock me, but my eyes pour out tears to God. 21 Oh, that a man might plead with God as he pleads with his fellow man! 22 For when a few years have passed, I will go the way of no return."
