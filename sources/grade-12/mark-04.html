Teaching by the Sea and the Parable of the Sower

1 Once more He began teaching beside the sea, and such an immense crowd gathered around Him that He stepped into a boat and sat down on the sea, while the entire throng remained on the shore.

2 He taught them many things through parables, and in His teaching He said to them:

3 "Listen! A sower went out to sow.

4 "As he sowed, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured it. 5 Other seed fell on rocky ground, where it lacked deep soil, so it sprang up at once; 6 yet when the sun rose it was scorched, and because it had no root it withered. 7 Still other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it, so that it produced no fruit. 8 But other seeds fell into good soil, and as they grew and multiplied they yielded fruit—some thirtyfold, some sixtyfold, and some a hundredfold."

9 He said, "Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

The Purpose of Parables

10 When He was alone, those around Him, together with the Twelve, asked Him about the parables.

11 He said to them, "To you the mystery of the kingdom of God has been given, but to those outside everything comes in parables, 12 so that seeing they may see and not perceive, and hearing they may hear and not understand, lest they turn and be forgiven."

13 He said to them, "Do you not know this parable? Then how will you understand all the parables?"

14 "The sower sows the word. 15 These are the ones along the path where the word is sown: when they hear, Satan immediately comes and takes away the word sown in them. 16 Likewise, these are the ones sown on rocky ground: when they hear the word, they immediately receive it with joy, 17 but they have no root within themselves and endure only briefly; then, when tribulation or persecution arises because of the word, they immediately fall away. 18 Others are those sown among thorns: they hear the word, 19 but worldly anxieties, the deceitfulness of wealth, and cravings for other things enter in and choke the word, so that it becomes unfruitful. 20 And these are the ones sown on good soil: they hear the word, accept it, and bear fruit—some thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred."

21 He said to them, "Is a lamp brought to be placed under a basket or under a bed? Is it not meant to be placed on a lampstand? 22 For nothing is hidden except to be disclosed, and nothing has been concealed except to come into the light. 23 If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear."

24 He said to them, "Pay attention to what you hear: with the measure you use, it will be measured to you, and more will be added to you. 25 For to the one who has, more will be given; but from the one who has not, even what he has will be taken away."

Further Parables of the Kingdom

26 He said, "The kingdom of God is as if a man should scatter seed on the ground. 27 He sleeps and rises night and day, and the seed sprouts and grows, though he does not know how. 28 The earth produces fruit of itself—first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear. 29 But when the grain is ripe, at once he sends in the sickle, because the harvest has come."

30 He said, "With what shall we compare the kingdom of God, or what parable shall we use for it? 31 It is like a mustard seed, which, when sown on the ground, is the smallest of all the seeds on earth; 32 yet when it is sown it grows up and becomes greater than all the garden plants, putting out large branches, so that the birds of the air can nest in its shade."

33 With many such parables He spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it. 34 He did not speak to them without a parable, but privately to His own disciples He explained everything.

Jesus Calms a Storm on the Sea

35 On that day, when evening had come, He said to them, "Let us go across to the other side." 36 Leaving the crowd behind, they took Him with them in the boat, just as He was; and other boats were with Him.

37 A great windstorm arose, and the waves beat against the boat, so that it was already filling. 38 He Himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion. They woke Him and said, "Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?"

39 He awoke and rebuked the wind, and He said to the sea, "Peace! Be still!" Then the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.

40 He said to them, "Why are you so cowardly? Do you still have no faith?"

41 They were filled with great fear and said to one another, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?"
