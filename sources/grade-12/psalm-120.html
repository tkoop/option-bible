A Song of Ascents: Deliverance from Deceitful Speech

1 In my distress I called to the LORD, and He answered me. 2 Deliver my soul, O the LORD, from lying lips and from a deceitful tongue.

3 What will He do to you, and what more besides, O deceitful tongue? 4 Sharp arrows of the warrior, with burning coals of the broom tree!

5 Woe is me, for I sojourn in Meshech and dwell among the tents of Kedar! 6 Too long has my soul dwelt with those who hate peace. 7 I am for peace, but when I speak, they are for war.
