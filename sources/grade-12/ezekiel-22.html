The City of Blood and Its Abominations

1 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2 “Son of man, will you judge, will you judge the city of blood? Then make known to her all her abominations.”

3 Say, “Thus says the Lord GOD: A city that sheds blood within herself and makes idols to defile herself has drawn near her time. 4 You are guilty because of the blood you have shed, and you are defiled by the idols you have made; you have brought your days near and reached your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all the lands. 5 Those near and far will mock you, you whose name is defiled and who are great in turmoil.”

6 Behold, the princes of Israel in you—each one according to his power—have been there to shed blood. 7 In you they have dishonored father and mother; in your midst they have oppressed the sojourner; in you they have wronged the orphan and the widow. 8 You have despised My holy things and profaned My Sabbaths.

9 Slanderers are in you to shed blood, and they eat on the mountains; they commit lewdness in your midst. 10 In you they have uncovered their father's nakedness; in you they have humiliated the woman during her menstrual uncleanness. 11 One man has committed an abomination with his neighbor's wife; another has lewdly defiled his daughter-in-law; another has violated his sister, his father's daughter, in you. 12 In you they have taken bribes to shed blood; you have taken interest and usury; you have extorted your neighbors by violence; and you have forgotten Me, declares the Lord GOD.

13 And behold, I have clapped My hands at your dishonest gain and at the blood that is in your midst. 14 Can your heart endure, or can your hands be strong, in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it. 15 I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the lands, and I will remove your uncleanness from you. 16 You will be profaned in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD.

Israel Like Dross in the Furnace

17 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 18 “Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to Me; all of them—bronze, tin, iron, and lead—are in the furnace; they are the dross of silver.”

19 Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Because all of you have become dross, behold, I am gathering you into Jerusalem. 20 As one gathers silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin into a furnace to blow fire upon it and melt it, so I will gather you in My anger and wrath, and I will put you in and melt you. 21 I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of My wrath, and you will be melted in it. 22 As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted in it; and you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out My wrath upon you.

Corrupt Leaders and the Failure to Stand in the Gap

23 The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 24 “Son of man, say to her, ‘You are a land that has not been cleansed and has not been rained upon in the day of wrath.’”

25 A conspiracy of her prophets is in her midst, like a roaring lion tearing its prey; they devour lives, seize treasure and valuables, and make many widows in her midst. 26 Her priests have done violence to My law and have profaned My holy things; they have made no distinction between the holy and the common, and they have not taught the difference between the unclean and the clean; they have hidden their eyes from My Sabbaths, so that I am profaned among them. 27 Her princes in her midst are like wolves tearing prey, shedding blood and destroying lives to make dishonest gain.

28 Her prophets plaster them with whitewash, seeing false visions and divining lies for them, saying, “Thus says the Lord GOD,” when the LORD has not spoken. 29 The people of the land practice extortion and commit robbery; they oppress the poor and needy and mistreat the foreigner without justice. 30 I sought for a man among them who would build up the wall and intercede before Me for the land, that I might not destroy it, but I found none. 31 So I poured out My indignation upon them; I consumed them with the fire of My wrath; I brought their ways upon their own heads, declares the Lord GOD.
