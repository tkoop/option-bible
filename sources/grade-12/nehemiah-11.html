The Repopulation of Jerusalem

1 The civic leaders took up residence in Jerusalem, while the remaining populace cast lots to select one in ten to dwell in Jerusalem, the holy city; the other nine-tenths remained in their respective towns. 2 The people blessed every man who volunteered to reside in Jerusalem.

Leaders and Residents of Jerusalem

3 These are the provincial administrators who settled in Jerusalem. (Throughout Judah’s cities, each man settled on his own property within their towns—the Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants, and descendants of Solomon’s servants— 4 while some descendants of Judah and Benjamin settled in Jerusalem.)

5 and Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, son of the Shilonite; 6 the descendants of Perez who settled in Jerusalem totaled 468 men of valor.

7 From the descendants of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah; 8 and after him, Gabbai and Sallai—928. 9 Joel son of Zichri served as chief officer over them, and Judah son of Hassenuah oversaw the city as second-in-command.

Priests, Levites, and Gatekeepers

10 From the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib; Jachin; 11 Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, ruler of the house of God; 12 and their associates who performed the temple service—822. Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; 13 and his associates, the heads of families—242. Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer; 14 and their associates—128 mighty men of valor. Zabdiel son of Haggedolim was their overseer.

15 From the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni; 16 Shabbethai and Jozabad, leaders of the Levites, who supervised the exterior work of the house of God; 17 Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, leader of the praise who led in thanksgiving and prayer; Bakbukiah, second among his associates; and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun. 18 The Levites in the holy city totaled 284. 19 And the gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their associates, who kept watch at the gates—172.

20 The rest of the Israelites, priests, and Levites were in all the cities of Judah, each on his own inheritance. 21 The temple servants lived on the hill of Ophel, with Ziha and Gishpa over them. 22 Now the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Mica, one of Asaph’s descendants, who were the singers assigned to the service of the house of God. 23 For there was a command from the king concerning them, and a fixed provision for the singers, day by day. 24 Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, a descendant of Zerah son of Judah, was the king’s agent in every matter concerning the people.

Settlements Beyond Jerusalem

25 As for the villages with their fields, some of the people of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba and its villages, Dibon and its villages, Jekabzeel and its villages, 26 Jeshua, Moladah, Beth-pelet, 27 Hazar-shual, Beersheba and its villages, 28 Ziklag, Meconah and its villages, 29 En-rimmon, Zorah, Jarmuth, 30 Zanoah, Adullam and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its villages. So they settled from Beersheba all the way to the Valley of Hinnom.

31 The descendants of Benjamin from Geba lived in Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its villages, 32 Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah, 33 Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim, 34 Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat, 35 Lod, Ono, and the Valley of Craftsmen. 36 And some of the Levites of Judah settled in Benjamin.
