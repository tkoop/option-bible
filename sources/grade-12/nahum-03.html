Woe to the Bloodstained City

1 Woe to the city of blood, full of lies, full of plunder, never without prey. 2 The crack of the whip, the rumble of wheels, galloping horses, and bounding chariots! 3 Charging horsemen; flashing sword and gleaming spear; countless slain, heaps of corpses, bodies without number—they stumble over the dead. 4 All because of the countless harlotries of the harlot, the charming one, mistress of sorceries, who sells nations through her harlotries and families through her sorceries.

The LORD Opposes Nineveh

5 Behold, I am against you, declares the LORD of Hosts, and I will lift your skirts over your face; I will show the nations your nakedness and the kingdoms your shame. 6 I will pelt you with filth, treat you with contempt, and make you a spectacle. 7 Then everyone who sees you will shrink back and say, "Nineveh is devastated; who will grieve for her?" Where can I find comforters for you?

The Fall of Thebes

8 Are you better than Thebes, situated on the Nile, with water all around her, whose defense was the sea and whose wall was the water? 9 Cush and Egypt were her boundless strength; Put and Libya were her helpers. 10 Yet she was carried away; she went into captivity. Her infants were dashed to pieces at the head of every street. They cast lots for her nobles, and all her great men were bound in chains.

The Siege and Final Ruin

11 You too will become drunk; you will go into hiding; you too will seek refuge from the enemy. 12 All your fortresses are like fig trees with first-ripe figs: if shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater. 13 Look, your troops are women in your midst; the gates of your land are wide open to your enemies; fire has consumed your gate bars. 14 Draw water for the siege, strengthen your fortresses, work the clay, tread the mortar, and repair the brick kiln. 15 There fire will consume you; the sword will cut you down and devour you like the locust. Multiply yourself like the locust; multiply yourself like the grasshopper. 16 You have increased your merchants more than the stars of heaven. The locust strips the land and flies away. 17 Your guards are like locusts, and your officials are like swarms of locusts that settle in the walls on a cold day; when the sun rises, they flee, and no one knows where they are. 18 Your shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria; your nobles sleep. Your people are scattered on the mountains, and no one gathers them. 19 There is no healing for your wound; your injury is fatal. Everyone who hears the report about you claps their hands over you, for who has not endured your relentless cruelty?
