The Genealogy of Judah

1 The sons of Judah were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 2 Reaiah, the son of Shobal, fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad; these were the clans of the Zorathites. 3 These were the sons of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; and their sister was named Hazzelelponi. 4 Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah and the father of Bethlehem.

5 Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives, Helah and Naarah. 6 Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari; these were the sons of Naarah. 7 The sons of Helah were Zereth, Izhar, and Ethnan. 8 Koz fathered Anub, Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel, the son of Harum.

Jabez’s Prayer and the Honorable Line

9 Jabez was more honorable than his brothers. His mother named him Jabez, saying, “Because I bore him in pain.” 10 Jabez called on the God of Israel, “If only You would bless me and enlarge my territory! May Your hand be with me and keep me from harm, so that I may be free from pain.” And God granted his request.

11 Chelub, the brother of Shuhah, fathered Mehir, who fathered Eshton. 12 Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Rechah.

13 The sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah, and Othniel’s son was Hathath. 14 Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of Ge-harashim, because they were craftsmen. 15 The sons of Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, were Iru, Elah, and Naam; Elah’s son was Kenaz. 16 The sons of Jehallelel were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

17 The sons of Ezrah were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. Mered’s wife Bithiah bore Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, the father of Eshtemoa. His wife Hodiah bore Jared, the father of Gedor, Heber, the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel, the father of Zanoah. 18 These were the sons of Pharaoh’s daughter Bithiah, whom Mered had married. 19 The sons of Hodiah’s wife, the sister of Naham, were the father of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite. 20 The sons of Shimon were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. The sons of Ishi were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

21 The sons of Shelah, the son of Judah, were Er, the father of Lecah; Laadah, the father of Mareshah; and the clans of the linen workers at Beth-ashbea. 22 Jokim, the men of Cozeba, Joash, and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem—these details are from ancient records. 23 They were potters and inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah; they lived there in the king’s service.

The Clans of Simeon

24 The sons of Simeon were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 25 Shallum was Shaul’s son, Mibsam his son, and Mishma his son. 26 The sons of Mishma were Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, and Shimei his son. 27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers had few children, so their entire clan was not as numerous as the sons of Judah.

28 They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual, Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad, Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David’s reign. 29 Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five cities—and all the surrounding villages as far as Baal. 30 These were their settlements, and they preserved a genealogical record.

31 Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah the son of Amaziah, Joel, Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel, Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah, Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah. 32 These were the leaders of their clans, and their families increased greatly. 33 They journeyed to the entrance of Gedor, to the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.

Simeon’s Expansion and Settlement

34 They found rich, good pasture, and the land was spacious, quiet, and peaceful, because Hamites had formerly lived there. 35 Those recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah, king of Judah, and struck their tents and the Meunites found there; they devoted them to destruction to this day and settled in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks. 36 Five hundred of them, men of Simeon, went to Mount Seir with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, as their leaders. 37 They struck down the remnant of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.
