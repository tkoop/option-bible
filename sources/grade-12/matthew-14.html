Herod Executes John the Baptist

1 At that time Herod the tetrarch heard reports concerning Jesus, 2 and he said to his attendants, "This is John the Baptist, risen from the dead; consequently, miraculous powers are operating through him."

3 Herod had previously arrested John, bound him, and confined him in prison on account of Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip, 4 because John had repeatedly told him, "It is not lawful for you to have her." 5 Although Herod wished to execute him, he feared the multitude, since they regarded John as a prophet.

6 When Herod's birthday arrived, the daughter of Herodias danced before the company and captivated Herod, 7 prompting him to swear an oath that he would grant her whatever she requested. 8 Prompted by her mother, she demanded, "Give me here, on a platter, the head of John the Baptist." 9 The king, though distressed, commanded that her request be fulfilled because of his oaths and his dinner guests. 10 He sent orders, and John was beheaded in prison; 11 his head was conveyed on a platter to the girl, who delivered it to her mother.

12 Thereafter, John's disciples came, removed the body, buried it, and reported the matter to Jesus.

Jesus Feeds the Five Thousand

13 Upon hearing this, Jesus withdrew from there by boat to a solitary place; yet when the crowds learned of it, they followed Him on foot from the surrounding towns. 14 When He disembarked, He saw a vast multitude, was moved with compassion for them, and healed their infirmities.

15 When evening approached, the disciples came to Him, saying, "This place is desolate, and the hour has already passed; dismiss the crowds so they may go into the villages and purchase food for themselves."

16 Jesus replied, "They need not depart; you give them something to eat."

17 They answered, "We have here only five loaves and two fish."

18 He said, "Bring them to Me."

19 Then He directed the crowds to recline on the grass; and taking the five loaves and the two fish, He looked toward heaven, blessed them, broke the loaves, and distributed them to the disciples, who in turn distributed them to the crowds. 20 Everyone ate and was satisfied, and they collected twelve baskets filled with the leftover fragments. 21 Those who had eaten numbered about five thousand men, not counting women and children.

Jesus Walks on Water

22 Immediately He insisted that His disciples embark and precede Him to the opposite shore while He dismissed the crowds. 23 After sending the crowds away, He ascended the mountain alone to pray; and when evening fell, He remained there in solitude.

24 Meanwhile, the boat was already many stadia from shore, tormented by the waves, because the wind was contrary. 25 During the fourth watch of the night, He approached them, walking upon the sea. 26 Seeing Him walk upon the sea, the disciples were alarmed and exclaimed, "It is a specter!" and they screamed in terror.

27 But Jesus at once addressed them: "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid."

28 Peter responded, "Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water."

29 He said, "Come." So Peter descended from the boat and walked on the water toward Jesus.

30 But perceiving the fierce wind, he became frightened and, as he began to sink, cried out, "Lord, save me!"

31 Immediately Jesus extended His hand, seized him, and said, "O you of little faith, why did you doubt?"

32 When they climbed into the boat, the wind subsided. 33 Then those in the boat worshipped Him, saying, "Truly, You are the Son of God."

Healing at Gennesaret

34 After crossing over, they landed at Gennesaret. 35 The men of that locality recognized Him and dispatched messengers throughout the surrounding district, bringing all the sick to Him. 36 They entreated Him that they might merely touch the hem of His garment; and everyone who touched it was healed.
