Rehoboam Forsakes the Law of the LORD

1 When Rehoboam had established his kingdom and strengthened himself, with all Israel standing beside him, he abandoned the Law of the the LORD. 2 In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, because they had been unfaithful to the the LORD, Shishak king of Egypt marched against Jerusalem— 3 with twelve hundred chariots and sixty thousand horsemen, and an innumerable host of Libyans, Sukkiites, and Cushites who had come with him from Egypt. 4 He captured the fortified cities of Judah and advanced as far as Jerusalem.

Shemaiah Warns Judah and the Nation Humbles Itself

5 Then Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and to the leaders of Judah who had assembled at Jerusalem because of Shishak, and he said to them, "This is what the the LORD says: 'You have abandoned Me, so I have abandoned you to Shishak.'"

6 The leaders of Israel and the king humbled themselves and said, "The LORD is righteous."

7 When the the LORD saw that they had humbled themselves, the word of the the LORD came to Shemaiah: "They have humbled themselves; I will not destroy them, but I will grant them a little deliverance, and My wrath will not be poured out upon Jerusalem by Shishak. 8 Nevertheless, they will become his servants, so that they may learn the difference between serving Me and serving the kingdoms of other lands."

Shishak Plunders Jerusalem

9 So Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem and took the treasures of the house of the the LORD and the treasures of the king's house. He took everything, including the gold shields that Solomon had made. 10 King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place and entrusted them to the commanders of the guard who kept watch at the entrance of the king's house. 11 Whenever the king entered the house of the the LORD, the guard would carry the shields and then return them to the guardroom. 12 When he humbled himself, the wrath of the the LORD turned from him, so that he was not destroyed completely; and conditions in Judah were favorable.

Rehoboam's Reign and Death

13 King Rehoboam strengthened himself in Jerusalem and reigned. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the the LORD had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonitess. 14 He did evil, for he did not set his heart to seek the the LORD. 15 Now the acts of Rehoboam, first and last, are they not written in the records of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the seer concerning genealogies? And there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam throughout all his days. 16 Rehoboam rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. Abijah his son reigned in his place.
