The Bronze Altar

1 Construct an altar of acacia wood, square in plan: five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high. 2 Make horns on its four corners, forming them as one piece with the altar, and overlay the whole altar with bronze. 3 Make all its utensils of bronze—its pots for removing ashes, its shovels, its basins, its forks, and its firepans.

4 Make a bronze grating for it, a network of bronze, and attach four bronze rings to the four corners of the net. 5 Set the grating beneath the altar's ledge so that the mesh rises halfway up the altar. 6 Make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with bronze. 7 Insert the poles through the rings so that they project from the two sides of the altar whenever it is carried. 8 Construct the altar as a hollow framework of boards, exactly as He showed you on the mountain.

The Courtyard of the Tabernacle

9 Construct the court of the tabernacle. On the south side, toward the south, the curtains of fine twisted linen shall be one hundred cubits long for that side. 10 It shall have twenty pillars with twenty bronze bases, and the hooks and bands of the pillars shall be silver. 11 The north side shall be the same in length: curtains one hundred cubits long, with twenty pillars and twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands for the pillars.

12 The width of the court on the west side shall be fifty cubits, with ten pillars and ten bases. 13 The width of the court on the east side, toward the east, shall be fifty cubits. 14 The curtains for one side of the entrance shall be fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three bases. 15 The curtains for the other side shall likewise be fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three bases.

16 For the gate of the court there shall be a screen twenty cubits long, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer, with four pillars and four bases. 17 All the pillars around the court shall be banded with silver; their hooks shall be bronze, and their bases bronze. 18 The court shall be one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide, with curtains of fine twisted linen five cubits high and bronze bases. 19 All the utensils used in the service of the tabernacle, together with all the pegs of the court, shall be bronze.

Oil for the Sanctuary Lamps

20 You are to command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of pressed olives for the light, so that the lamps may burn continually. 21 In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil that is before the Testimony, Aaron and his sons shall keep the lamps burning before the LORD from evening until morning. This is to be a perpetual statute for the Israelites throughout their generations.
