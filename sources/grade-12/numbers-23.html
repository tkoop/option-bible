Balaam's First Altars and Oracle

1 Balaam said to Balak, “Construct seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me.” 2 Balak did exactly as Balaam requested, and the two of them offered a bull and a ram on each altar.

3 “Remain here beside your burnt offering,” Balaam told Balak, “while I step aside. Perhaps the the LORD will meet me, and whatever He reveals, I will report to you.” So Balaam went off to a barren height.

4 God met Balaam, and Balaam said to Him, “I have arranged seven altars, and I have offered a bull and a ram on each.”

5 Then the the LORD put a word in Balaam's mouth and said, “Return to Balak and speak as I command.”

6 So Balaam returned to Balak, who was standing beside his burnt offering with all the princes of Moab.

7 Then Balaam lifted up his oracle and said, “Balak, king of Moab, has brought me from Aram, from the mountains of the east, saying, ‘Come, curse Jacob for me; come, denounce Israel.’ 8 How can I curse those whom God has not cursed? How can I denounce those whom the the LORD has not denounced? 9 For I see them from the summit of the rocks, and from the heights I behold them. Behold, a people dwelling apart, not counted among the nations. 10 Who can number Jacob's countless descendants, or count even a quarter of Israel? Let me die the death of the righteous, and may my end be like theirs!”

Balak's Disappointment and a Second Attempt

11 Then Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, yet you have blessed them thoroughly!”

12 Balaam answered, “Must I not speak exactly what the the LORD puts in my mouth?”

13 Balak said to him, “Please come with me to another place, from which you can see them. You will see only a portion of them, not all of them; and from there curse them for me.”

14 So Balak took him to the field of Zophim, on the summit of Pisgah, and built seven altars there, offering a bull and a ram on each.

15 “Remain here beside your burnt offering,” Balaam said to Balak, “while I meet the the LORD over there.”

16 The The LORD met Balaam and put a word in his mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and speak as I command.”

17 So Balaam returned to Balak, who was standing beside his burnt offering with the princes of Moab. “What did the the LORD say?” Balak asked.

Balaam's Second Oracle

18 Then Balaam lifted up his oracle and said, “Arise, Balak, and listen; give ear to me, son of Zippor. 19 God is not a man, that He should lie, nor a son of man, that He should change His mind. Does He speak and not act? Does He promise and not fulfill? 20 Behold, I have received a command to bless; He has blessed, and I cannot revoke it. 21 He has seen no iniquity in Jacob, and no trouble in Israel. The The LORD their God is with them, and the shout of a king is among them. 22 God brought them out of Egypt; they have the strength of a wild ox. 23 For there is no sorcery against Jacob and no divination against Israel. It will now be said of Jacob and Israel, ‘What has God wrought!’ 24 Behold, the people rise like a lioness; they rouse themselves like a lion. They will not lie down until they devour the prey and drink the blood of the slain.”

Balak's Final Rejection and Third Attempt

25 Then Balak said to Balaam, “Do not curse them at all, and do not bless them at all!”

26 Balaam answered, “Did I not tell you, ‘All that the the LORD speaks, I must do’?”

27 Balak said to Balaam, “Please come; I will take you to another place. Perhaps it will please God to let you curse them for me from there.”

28 So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, overlooking the wasteland.

29 Balaam said to Balak, “Construct seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me.” 30 Balak did as Balaam said and offered a bull and a ram on each altar.
