The Sign of Maher-shalal-hash-baz

1 Then the the LORD said to me, "Take a large tablet and write on it with an ordinary stylus: 'Maher-shalal-hash-baz.'" 2 I will take faithful witnesses to record it—Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah.

3 I approached the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. Then the the LORD said to me, "Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz." 4 Before the boy knows how to say "My father" or "My mother," the wealth of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria will be carried off by the king of Assyria.

Judgment Through Assyria

5 The the LORD spoke to me again: 6 "Because this people has rejected the gently flowing waters of Shiloah and puts its confidence in Rezin and the son of Remaliah, 7 therefore, behold, the Lord is bringing against them the floodwaters of the River—mighty and many—the king of Assyria and all his glory. It will overflow all its channels and run over all its banks. 8 It will sweep through Judah, overflow, and pass through; it will reach up to the neck. The spreading of his wings will fill the breadth of your land, O Immanuel."

The LORD as Sanctuary and Stumbling Stone

9 Band together, you peoples, and be shattered; listen, all you distant lands; arm yourselves, and be shattered; arm yourselves, and be shattered. 10 Devise a plan, but it will be thwarted; speak a word, but it will not stand, for God is with us.

11 For this is what the the LORD said to me with His strong hand upon me, warning me not to follow the way of this people: 12 "Do not call conspiracy everything this people calls conspiracy. Do not fear what they fear, and do not dread what they dread. 13 The the LORD of Hosts is the One you are to regard as holy; He is the One you are to fear; He is the One you are to dread."

14 He will be a sanctuary, but a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense to both houses of Israel, a trap and a snare to the people of Jerusalem. 15 Many will stumble over them; they will fall and be broken; they will be snared and captured.

The Testimony and Coming Darkness

16 Preserve the testimony and seal the instruction among my disciples. 17 I will wait for the the LORD, who is hiding His face from the house of Jacob, and I will put my hope in Him. 18 Here I am, with the children the the LORD has given me. We are signs and wonders in Israel from the the LORD of Hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

19 When people tell you to consult the spirits of the dead and the spiritists who whisper and mutter, shouldn't a people consult their God? Why consult the dead on behalf of the living? 20 Turn to the instruction and the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no light of dawn.

21 They will roam through the land, dejected and hungry. When they are famished, they will become enraged; and looking upward, they will curse their king and their God. 22 Then they will look to the earth and see only distress and darkness and fearful gloom, and they will be driven into utter darkness.
