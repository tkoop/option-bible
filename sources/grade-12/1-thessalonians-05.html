The Day of the Lord Will Come Without Warning

1 Concerning the times and seasons, you do not need us to write anything to you, 2 for you yourselves know perfectly well that the Day of the Lord will come without warning. 3 When people declare, "Peace and security," sudden destruction will assail them, as labor pains seize a woman with child, and they will not escape.

4 Yet you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness, so that the Day should catch you off guard. 5 For all of you are children of light and children of the day; we are not children of night or of darkness.

6 Therefore, let us not slumber as the rest do, but let us remain vigilant and sober. 7 For those who slumber do so at night, and those who become drunk do so at night. 8 But because we belong to the day, let us be sober, having clothed ourselves with the breastplate of faith and love and with the helmet of the hope of salvation.

9 For God did not appoint us to wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, 10 who died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with Him. 11 Therefore, encourage one another and strengthen one another, just as you are already doing.

Exhortations for a Holy Life

12 We implore you, brothers and sisters, to acknowledge those who toil among you and preside over you in the Lord and admonish you, 13 and to esteem them exceedingly in love on account of their labor. Live in peace with one another.

14 We also exhort you, brothers and sisters, to admonish the disorderly, comfort the discouraged, uphold the weak, and exercise patience toward all. 15 See that no one renders evil for evil, but always pursue what is good, both toward one another and toward everyone.

16 Rejoice perpetually; 17 pray continually; 18 give thanks in every circumstance, for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.

19 Do not stifle the Spirit; 20 do not despise prophecies; 21 but examine all things and retain what is good. 22 Abstain from every form of evil.

Final Blessing and Apostolic Charge

23 Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you entirely, and may your whole spirit, soul, and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. 24 Faithful is He who calls you, and He will surely accomplish it.

25 Brothers and sisters, pray for us. 26 Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss. 27 I solemnly charge you in the name of the Lord that this epistle be read to all the brothers and sisters. 28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
