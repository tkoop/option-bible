Laws Concerning Divorce and Remarriage

1 When a man takes a wife and marries her, and she does not find favor in his eyes because he has found some indecency in her, he may write her a certificate of divorce, place it in her hand, and send her away from his house. 2 If she leaves his house and becomes the wife of another man, 3 and the latter husband hates her and writes her a certificate of divorce, places it in her hand, and sends her away from his house—or if the latter husband dies, who took her as his wife— 4 then her former husband, who sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife after she has been defiled; for that is an abomination before the LORD, and you must not bring sin upon the land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance.

Military Exemption and Protection of Livelihood

5 When a man takes a new wife, he shall not go out with the army, nor shall any duty be laid upon him; he shall be free at home for one year and cheer his wife whom he has taken. 6 No one shall take a handmill or an upper millstone as security for a debt, since that would be taking a man's livelihood as security. 7 If a man is found kidnapping one of his fellow Israelites and treating him as a slave or selling him, that kidnapper shall die; so you shall purge the evil from among you. 8 Take care in the case of leprous disease to observe and do according to all that the Levitical priests teach you; as I have commanded them, so you shall be careful to do. 9 Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the way as you came out of Egypt.

Lending, Wages, and Justice

10 When you lend your neighbor anything, you shall not go into his house to take his pledge. 11 You shall stand outside, and the man to whom you lend shall bring the pledge out to you. 12 And if the man is poor, you shall not sleep with his pledge. 13 You shall surely return the pledge to him when the sun goes down, that he may sleep in his garment and bless you; and it shall be righteousness for you before the LORD your God. 14 You shall not oppress a hired servant who is poor and needy, whether he is one of your brothers or a foreigner in your land within your gates. 15 You must pay his wages on the same day, before the sun goes down, because he is poor and depends on them for his life; otherwise he may cry out to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin. 16 Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor shall children be put to death for their fathers; each shall be put to death for his own sin. 17 You shall not pervert justice for the foreigner or the fatherless, nor take a widow's garment as security. 18 But you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and the LORD your God redeemed you from there; therefore I command you to do this thing.

Gleaning and Remembrance of Deliverance

19 When you reap your harvest in your field and forget a sheaf in the field, you shall not go back to take it; it shall be for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands. 20 When you beat your olive trees, you shall not go over the branches again; it shall be for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow. 21 When you gather the grapes of your vineyard, you shall not glean it afterward; it shall be for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow. 22 And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt; therefore I command you to do this thing.
