The Wicked Oppress the Poor and Vulnerable

“1 Why does the Almighty not reserve times for judgment? Why may those who know Him not see His days? 2 There are those who move boundary stones; they pasture stolen flocks. 3 They drive away the donkey of the fatherless and take the widow’s ox in pledge. 4 They push the needy off the road; the poor of the land are forced into hiding.

“5 Indeed, like wild donkeys in the desert, the poor go out to their toil, seeking food; the wasteland is food for their children. 6 They gather fodder in the fields and glean the vineyards of the wicked. 7 Without clothing, they spend the night naked; they have no covering against the cold. 8 Drenched by mountain rains, they huddle against the rock for lack of shelter.

“9 The fatherless are snatched from the breast, and the infant of the poor is taken as a pledge. 10 Without clothing, they wander about naked. They carry the sheaves, but still go hungry. 11 They crush olives within their walls; they tread the winepresses, but still are thirsty. 12 From the city, men groan, and the souls of the wounded cry out, yet God charges no one with wrongdoing.

Sinners Who Shun the Light

“13 Then there are those who rebel against the light; they do not know its ways or stay on its paths. 14 The murderer rises at daybreak to kill the poor and needy, and in the night he is like a thief. 15 The eye of the adulterer watches for twilight. ‘No eye will see me,’ he says, and he conceals his face. 16 In the dark they dig through houses; by day they seal themselves in; they never see the light. 17 For to them, deep darkness is their morning; they are friends with the terrors of darkness.

The Inevitable Downfall of the Wicked

“18 He is but foam on the surface of the water; his portion of the land is cursed, so that no one turns toward his vineyards. 19 As drought and heat consume the melted snow, so Sheol steals those who have sinned. 20 The womb forgets him; the worm feeds on him; he is no longer remembered. So injustice is broken like a tree. 21 He preys on the barren and childless, and does not show kindness to the widow. 22 Yet God drags away the mighty by His power; though rising up, they have no assurance of life. 23 He gives them a sense of security, and they rest in it, but His eyes are on their ways. 24 They are exalted for a moment, then they are gone; they are brought low and gathered up like all others; they wither like heads of grain. 25 If this is not so, then who can prove me a liar and reduce my words to nothing?”
