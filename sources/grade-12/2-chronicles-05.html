Solomon Completes the Temple and Gathers Israel

1 Thus all the labor Solomon performed for the house of the LORD reached completion; moreover, Solomon brought in the sacred treasures of David his father—the silver, the gold, and all the vessels—and placed them in the treasuries of the house of God. 2 Then Solomon convened the elders of Israel, along with all the heads of the tribes and the leaders of the ancestral houses of the Israelites, in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the City of David, which is Zion. 3 All the men of Israel assembled before the king at the festival held in the seventh month.

4 When all the elders of Israel had arrived, the Levites lifted the ark. 5 They carried up the ark, the tent of meeting, and all the holy utensils that were in the tent; the Levitical priests brought them up. 6 King Solomon and the entire assembly of Israel, gathered before him at the ark, sacrificed sheep and oxen in such abundance that they could not be counted or numbered.

The Ark Is Placed in the Most Holy Place

7 Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house—to the Most Holy Place—beneath the wings of the cherubim. 8 The cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark, and they covered the ark and its poles from above. 9 The poles were so long that their ends were visible from the front of the inner sanctuary, yet they could not be seen from outside; and they remain there to this day. 10 There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses had placed there at Horeb, where the LORD made a covenant with the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

The Priests and Levites Praise God

11 Now when the priests came out of the Holy Place—for all the priests who were present had consecrated themselves, without regard to their divisions— 12 and all the Levitical singers—Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and relatives—stood east of the altar, clothed in fine linen, with cymbals, harps, and lyres, together with one hundred twenty priests sounding trumpets,

13 in unison, as the trumpeters and singers made themselves heard with one voice in praising and thanking the LORD. When they lifted up their voice, accompanied by trumpets, cymbals, and other musical instruments, and when they praised the LORD, saying, “He indeed is good, for His lovingkindness endures forever,” then the house of the LORD was filled with a cloud.

The Glory of God Fills the Temple

14 Consequently, the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of God.
