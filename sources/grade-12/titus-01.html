Paul's Apostolic Greeting

1 Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes in accordance with the faith of God's elect and with the knowledge of the truth that leads to godliness, 2 grounded in the hope of eternal life, which God—who cannot lie—promised before time itself began. 3 At the proper time, He disclosed His word through the preaching entrusted to me by the command of God our Savior. 4 To Titus, my true child in our common faith: grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

Qualifications for Elders in Every City

5 For this reason I left you in Crete, so that you might set in order what remained unfinished and appoint elders in each city, just as I directed you. 6 An elder must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, with faithful children who are not accused of debauchery or insubordination. 7 For an overseer, as God's steward, must be above reproach: not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not addicted to wine, not violent, and not greedy for shameful gain. 8 Rather, he must be hospitable, a lover of what is good, sober-minded, just, devout, and self-controlled, 9 holding firmly to the trustworthy word as taught, so that he may be able both to encourage others with sound doctrine and to refute those who oppose it.

Silencing Rebellious False Teachers

10 For there are many rebellious people—idle talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision— 11 whose mouths must be silenced, since they ruin whole households by teaching what they ought not to teach, and do so for shameful gain. 12 One of their own prophets has said, "Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons." 13 This testimony is true. Therefore rebuke them sharply, so that they may become sound in the faith, 14 and pay no attention to Jewish myths or to the commands of people who turn away from the truth. 15 To the pure, all things are pure; but to those who are defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure. Indeed, both their mind and their conscience are defiled. 16 They profess to know God, yet by their works they deny Him, being detestable, disobedient, and disqualified for every good work.
