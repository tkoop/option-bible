Jotham Assumes the Throne

1 Upon ascending the throne at twenty-five years of age, Jotham reigned in Jerusalem for sixteen years; his mother was Jerushah, the daughter of Zadok.

Righteous Rule and Building Projects

2 He conducted himself uprightly in the sight of the LORD, emulating all that his father Uzziah had done, with the sole exception that he did not enter the temple of the LORD; nevertheless, the populace persisted in corrupt conduct.

3 He erected the Upper Gate of the house of the LORD and undertook extensive work on the wall of Ophel. 4 He also established cities throughout the hill country of Judah, together with fortresses and watchtowers, and he constructed edifices within the forested regions.

War Against the Ammonites

5 He waged war against the king of the Ammonites and subdued them; in that same year, the Ammonites rendered to him one hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley. They remitted an identical tribute during the second and third years as well.

Jotham's Strength, Records, and Death

6 Thus Jotham increased in power, for he established his conduct before the LORD his God.

7 The remaining deeds of Jotham—encompassing all his military campaigns and his personal conduct—are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.

8 He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and his reign in Jerusalem spanned sixteen years. 9 Jotham slept with his fathers and was entombed in the City of David, whereupon his son Ahaz succeeded him.
