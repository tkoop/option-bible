Thanksgiving for the LORD's Enduring Love

1 Give thanks to the the LORD, for He is good; His faithful love endures forever. 2 Let the redeemed of the the LORD proclaim it—those He ransomed from the hand of the foe. 3 He gathered them from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.

4 Some wandered in the wilderness, in a barren wasteland, and found no road to an inhabited city. 5 Hungry and thirsty, their spirits failed within them. 6 Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered them from their distress. 7 He led them along a straight path until they reached a city where they could settle.

8 Let them thank the the LORD for His faithful love, for His wonders to humankind. 9 For He satisfies the longing soul and fills the hungry with good things.

10 Some sat in darkness and mortal gloom, prisoners in misery and chains, 11 because they had rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the Most High. 12 So He humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and no one came to help.

13 Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress. 14 He brought them out of darkness and mortal gloom and shattered their bonds.

15 Let them thank the the LORD for His faithful love, for His wonders to humankind. 16 For He shattered bronze doors and severed iron bars.

17 Some became fools through their rebellious ways and suffered affliction because of their sins. 18 They loathed all food and drew near to the brink of death.

19 Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress. 20 He sent His word and healed them, rescuing them from destruction.

21 Let them thank the the LORD for His faithful love, for His wonders to humankind. 22 Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and proclaim His deeds with joyful songs.

23 Some went down to the sea in ships, carrying on trade upon the great waters. 24 They saw the works of the the LORD and His wonders in the deep. 25 For He spoke and stirred up a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea. 26 They rose to the heavens and plunged to the depths; their courage melted in the crisis. 27 They staggered and reeled like drunkards, and all their skill availed nothing.

28 Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and He brought them out of their distress. 29 He stilled the storm and hushed the waves. 30 Then they rejoiced in the calm, and He guided them to the harbor they longed for.

31 Let them thank the the LORD for His faithful love, for His wonders to humankind. 32 Let them exalt Him in the congregation of the people and praise Him in the assembly of the elders.

33 He turns rivers into desert and springs of water into thirsty ground, 34 a fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it. 35 He turns a desert into pools of water and parched land into springs of water.

36 There He settles the hungry, and they build a city to live in. 37 They sow fields and plant vineyards, and they gather a fruitful harvest. 38 He blesses them so that they multiply greatly, and He does not let their herds diminish.

39 Yet when they are diminished and brought low by oppression, calamity, and sorrow, 40 He pours contempt on princes and makes them wander in trackless wastes. 41 But He lifts the needy out of affliction and makes their families like flocks. 42 The upright see it and rejoice, while all wickedness is silenced.

43 Whoever is wise, let him heed these things and consider the faithful love of the the LORD.
