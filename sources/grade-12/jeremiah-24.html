Two Baskets of Figs

1 After Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into exile—along with the officials of Judah, the craftsmen, and the metalworkers from Jerusalem—and had brought them to Babylon, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs placed before the temple of the LORD. 2 One basket contained very good figs, like first-ripe figs, but the other contained very bad figs, so spoiled they could not be eaten.

3 Then the LORD asked me, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” I answered, “Figs—the good ones very good, and the bad ones very bad, so spoiled they cannot be eaten.”

The LORD Interprets the Vision

4 Then the word of the LORD came to me: 5 “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Like these good figs, so I will regard with favor the exiles of Judah, whom I have sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans. 6 I will set My eyes on them for good, and I will bring them back to this land. I will build them up and not tear them down; I will plant them and not uproot them. 7 I will give them a heart to know Me, for I am the LORD. They will be My people, and I will be their God, for they will return to Me with all their heart.”

Judgment on Those Who Remain

8 “But like the bad figs, so spoiled they cannot be eaten,” says the LORD, “so I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and the remnant of Jerusalem—those remaining in this land and those living in Egypt. 9 I will make them a horror and an offense to all the kingdoms of the earth, a reproach and a byword, a taunt and a curse in all the places to which I banish them. 10 I will send against them sword, famine, and plague until they have perished from the land I gave to them and their fathers.”
