David Seeks Refuge Among the Philistines

1 David said to himself, "Sooner or later Saul will destroy me; I have no prospect here but ruin. If I flee to Philistine territory, Saul will abandon his search for me throughout Israel, and I will slip from his grasp." 2 So David and the six hundred men with him crossed over to Achish son of Maoch, king of Gath. 3 David and his men settled in Gath with Achish, each man with his household; David brought his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the Carmelite widow of Nabal. 4 When Saul learned that David had fled to Gath, he ceased searching for him.

Achish Grants David Ziklag

5 David said to Achish, "If I have found favor in your eyes, grant me a place in one of the rural towns, where I may live. Why should your servant remain in the royal city with you?" 6 So Achish gave him Ziklag that very day, and from that time Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah. 7 David lived in Philistine territory for a year and four months.

David's Raids and Deception

8 David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites, peoples who had inhabited the land from Shur to Egypt since ancient times. 9 He devastated the region and left no one alive, neither man nor woman; he carried off sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothing, then returned to Achish. 10 Achish asked, "Where did you raid today?"

David replied, "Against the Negev of Judah, against the Negev of the Jerahmeelites, and against the Negev of the Kenites."

11 David spared no man or woman who might be brought to Gath, lest they report what he had done and the policy he maintained throughout his time in Philistine territory.

12 Achish trusted David and concluded, "He has made himself utterly detestable to his people Israel; therefore he will serve me forever."
