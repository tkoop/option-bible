Gatekeeper Families

1 These were the groups of gatekeepers. From the Korahites: Meshelemiah son of Kore, one of the sons of Asaph. 2 Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, and Jathniel the fourth. 3 Elam was the fifth, Johanan the sixth, and Elioenai the seventh. 4 The sons of Obed-edom were Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth, 5 Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peulthai the eighth. For God had blessed Obed-edom.

6 Shemaiah, Obed-edom’s son, also had sons who led their father’s family, because they were men of great ability. 7 Shemaiah’s sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His brothers Elihu and Semachiah were also men of great ability. 8 All these men came from Obed-edom. They, their sons, and their brothers were strong men, able to do the work—62 in all from Obed-edom. 9 Meshelemiah also had sons and brothers who were capable men—18 in all.

10 Hosah, a Merarite, also had sons: Shimri the first. Although he was not the firstborn, his father made him the leader. 11 Hilkiah was the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. Hosah had 13 sons and brothers in all.

Gatekeeper Duties and Lots

12 These groups of gatekeepers, through their chief men, served in the house of the LORD, just as their relatives did. 13 They cast lots for each gate, by families, for both young and old. 14 The lot for the east gate fell to Shelemiah. Then they cast lots for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor, and his lot came out for the north gate. 15 The lot for the south gate fell to Obed-edom, and the lot for the storehouses fell to his sons. 16 The lot for the west gate and the Shalleketh Gate on the road going up fell to Shuppim and Hosah. Guards were stationed at every watch.

17 On the east there were six Levites each day, on the north four each day, on the south four each day, and at the storehouses two pairs. 18 At the Parbar on the west there were four at the road and two at the Parbar. 19 These were the groups of gatekeepers from the sons of Korah and Merari.

Treasuries and Dedicated Gifts

20 Their fellow Levites were in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated things. 21 Jehieli came from the family of Ladan, a Gershonite, and was head of that family. 22 Jehieli’s sons were Zetham and his brother Joel. They were in charge of the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

23 From the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites: 24 Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, was the officer in charge of the treasuries. 25 His relatives through Eliezer were Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son. 26 This Shelomith and his brothers were in charge of all the treasuries of the dedicated things that King David, the heads of families, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the other army commanders had dedicated. 27 They had dedicated some of the plunder from their battles to repair the house of the LORD. 28 Everything that Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated—everything anyone had dedicated—was under the care of Shelomith and his brothers.

Officials Over Israel

29 From the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were assigned to outside duties as officers and judges over Israel. 30 From the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his relatives—1,700 capable men—were put in charge of Israel’s affairs west of the Jordan. They handled all the work of the LORD and the service of the king. 31 As for the Hebronites, Jerijah was the chief according to the family records. In the fortieth year of David’s reign, a search was made, and men of great ability were found among them at Jazer in Gilead. 32 Jerijah had 2,700 relatives who were capable men and heads of families. King David appointed them to oversee the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh for all the matters of God and of the king.
