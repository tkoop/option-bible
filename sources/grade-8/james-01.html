Greetings to God's People

1 James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, sends greetings to the twelve tribes scattered among the nations.

Joy and Wisdom in Trials

2 My brothers and sisters, think of it as pure joy whenever you face trials of many kinds. 3 You know that the testing of your faith produces perseverance. 4 Let perseverance finish its work so that you may be mature and complete, not lacking anything.

5 If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you. 6 But when you ask, you must believe and not doubt, because the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed by the wind. 7 That person should not expect to receive anything from the Lord. 8 Such a person is double-minded and unstable in all they do.

9 A believer who is in humble circumstances should be proud of being raised up. 10 But rich people should be proud that God has humbled them, because they will fade away like a wildflower. 11 The sun rises with scorching heat and withers the plant; its flower falls, and its beauty is destroyed. In the same way, the rich will fade away even while they go about their business. 12 Blessed is the person who keeps going under trial, because after standing the test, that person will receive the crown of life that the Lord has promised to those who love Him.

Temptation and God's Good Gifts

13 When tempted, no one should say, "God is tempting me." For God cannot be tempted by evil, nor does He tempt anyone. 14 But each person is tempted when they are dragged away by their own evil desire and enticed. 15 Then, after desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full-grown, gives birth to death.

16 Don't be fooled, my dear brothers and sisters. 17 Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights, who does not change like shifting shadows. 18 He chose to give us birth through the word of truth, so that we would be a kind of first harvest of everything He created.

Hearing and Doing the Word

19 My dear brothers and sisters, remember this: Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to become angry. 20 For human anger does not produce the righteousness that God desires. 21 So get rid of all moral filth and the evil that is so common, and humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save you.

22 Don't just listen to the word and fool yourselves. Do what it says. 23 Anyone who listens to the word but does not do what it says is like someone who looks at their face in a mirror. 24 They look at themselves and then go away, and right away they forget what they look like. 25 But whoever looks closely into the perfect law that gives freedom and keeps on doing it—not forgetting what they have heard, but doing it—will be blessed in what they do.

26 Those who think they are religious but don't control their tongues fool themselves, and their religion is worthless. 27 Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world.
