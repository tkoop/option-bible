Jeroboam's Sick Son

1At that time, Abijah, Jeroboam's son, became sick.

2Jeroboam said to his wife, "Please get up and disguise yourself so no one knows you're Jeroboam's wife. Go to Shiloh. Ahijah the prophet is there—the one who told me I would be king over this people. 3Take ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey with you, and go to him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy."

4Jeroboam's wife did as she was told. She got up and went to Shiloh and came to Ahijah's house. Ahijah could not see, because his eyes were dim with age.

Ahijah's Prophecy Against Jeroboam

5Now the LORD had said to Ahijah, "Look, Jeroboam's wife is coming to ask you about her son, for he is sick. Say such and such to her. When she comes, she will be disguised."

6When Ahijah heard the sound of her feet as she came in the door, he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why do you disguise yourself? I have been sent to you with hard news. 7Go tell Jeroboam, 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I raised you up from among the people and made you leader over My people Israel. 8I tore the kingdom away from David's house and gave it to you. But you have not been like My servant David, who kept My commands and followed Me with all his heart, doing only what was right in My eyes. 9You have done more evil than all who were before you. You made other gods and metal images to anger Me, and you turned your back on Me. 10Therefore I am bringing disaster on Jeroboam's house. I will cut off from Jeroboam every male in Israel, slave or free. I will burn up Jeroboam's house completely, like dung burned until it is gone. 11Anyone belonging to Jeroboam who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs, and anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by birds of the sky, for the LORD has spoken.' 12As for you, go home. When your feet enter the city, the child will die. 13All Israel will mourn for him and bury him, because he alone of Jeroboam's family will go to the grave, since in him the LORD, the God of Israel, found something good in Jeroboam's house. 14The LORD will raise up a king over Israel who will cut off Jeroboam's house this very day—and even now. 15The LORD will strike Israel, so it shakes like a reed in water. He will uproot Israel from this good land He gave their fathers, and scatter them beyond the Euphrates River, because they made their Asherah poles, angering the LORD. 16He will give Israel up because of Jeroboam's sins, which he committed and which he made Israel commit."

17Jeroboam's wife got up and left, and came to Tirzah. As she stepped over the threshold of the house, the child died. 18They buried him, and all Israel mourned for him, just as the LORD had said through His servant Ahijah the prophet. 19The rest of Jeroboam's acts, how he fought and how he reigned, are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 20Jeroboam reigned twenty-two years. Then he died and was buried with his ancestors, and his son Nadab reigned in his place.

Rehoboam's Reign in Judah

21Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the LORD chose out of all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. 22Judah did evil in the LORD's eyes. They sinned more than their fathers had, and they made Him jealous with their sins. 23They also built for themselves high places, sacred stones, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 24There were even male shrine prostitutes in the land. They did all the detestable things the nations had done that the LORD drove out before the Israelites.

Shishak Plunders Jerusalem

25In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt marched against Jerusalem. 26He took the treasures of the LORD's house and the treasures of the king's house. He took everything, including all the gold shields Solomon had made. 27King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place and put them in the care of the captains of the guard who watched the entrance to the king's house. 28Whenever the king went into the LORD's house, the guards carried them and brought them back to the guardroom.

29The rest of Rehoboam's acts and all he did are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 30There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all their days. 31Rehoboam died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. His son Abijam reigned in his place.
