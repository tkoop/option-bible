A Warning to Israel's Leaders

1 Hear this, you priests; listen, people of Israel; pay attention, king. Judgment is coming for you, because you have been a trap at Mizpah and a net spread over Tabor. 2 The rebels have gone deep into slaughter, but I will punish them all. 3 I know Ephraim well, and Israel is not hidden from Me. Now, Ephraim, you have been unfaithful; Israel is defiled. 4 Their actions do not let them return to their God, because a spirit of unfaithfulness is in them, and they do not know the the LORD.

Israel's Sin Brings Judgment

5 Israel's pride speaks against them. Israel and Ephraim stumble in their sin, and Judah stumbles with them. 6 They will go with their flocks and herds to seek the the LORD, but they will not find Him; He has withdrawn from them. 7 They have been unfaithful to the the LORD; they have had children by strangers. Now the new moon will destroy them and their fields.

A Warning and a Call to Arms

8 Blow the horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; shout the alarm in Beth-aven: "Look behind you, Benjamin!" 9 Ephraim will be ruined on the day of punishment; among Israel's tribes I have made known what is sure. 10 The princes of Judah are like men who move boundary stones; I will pour out My wrath on them like water. 11 Ephraim is oppressed and crushed in judgment, because he was determined to follow false gods. 12 So I am like a moth to Ephraim and like decay to the house of Judah.

No Help from Assyria

13 When Ephraim saw his sickness and Judah saw his wound, Ephraim went to Assyria and sent a message to King Jareb. But he cannot heal you or take away your wound. 14 For I will be like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to the house of Judah. I, even I, will tear them to pieces and go away; I will carry them off, and no one will rescue them. 15 I will go back to My place until they admit their guilt and seek My face; in their trouble they will earnestly seek Me.
