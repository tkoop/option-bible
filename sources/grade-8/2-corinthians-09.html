Help for God's People

1 It is not necessary for me to write to you about the help being sent to God's people. 2 I know how eager you are. I boast about you to the people of Macedonia. I tell them that you in Achaia have been ready since last year. Your excitement has stirred many of them to act. 3 But I sent the brothers ahead so our boasting about you in this matter would not be empty. I want you to be ready, just as I said you would be. 4 If some Macedonians come with me and find you not ready, we would be ashamed of our confidence in you. 5 So I thought it was necessary to ask the brothers to visit you first and get your promised gift ready. Then it will be a real gift given freely, not something taken by pressure.

Give Cheerfully

6 Remember this: Whoever plants only a little will gather only a little. But whoever plants generously will gather generously. 7 Each person should give what he has decided in his heart to give. He should not give with sadness or because he feels forced, because God loves a cheerful giver.

God's Grace Brings Thanks

8 God is able to give you more than enough grace. Then you will always have what you need and be able to do every good work. 9 As it is written, “He scattered His gifts to the poor. His goodness lasts forever.” 10 God, who gives seed to the farmer and bread for food, will give you seed and make it grow. He will increase the harvest that comes from your right living. 11 You will be made rich in every way so you can be generous. Your generosity will lead many people, through us, to thank God. 12 This service does more than provide for the needs of God's people. It also causes many people to thank God. 13 Because you have proved your faith by this service, people will praise God. They will see that you obey the good news of Christ and share generously with them and with everyone. 14 They will pray for you. They will long to see you because of the great grace God has given you. 15 Thanks be to God for His gift that is too wonderful for words!
