A contemplative song of Ethan the Ezrahite.

God's Faithful Love and Covenant

1 I will sing of the LORD's faithful love forever; with my mouth I will make known Your faithfulness to every generation. 2 For I said, "Faithful love will last forever; in the heavens You will make Your faithfulness firm."

3 "I have made a covenant with My chosen one; I have sworn to David My servant: 4 'I will establish your offspring forever and build your throne for all generations.'" Selah.

5 Let the heavens praise Your wonders, the LORD, and Your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones. 6 For who in the skies can be compared to the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD? 7 God is greatly feared in the council of the holy ones, and He is awesome above all who are around Him. 8 the LORD God of Hosts, who is mighty like You? Your faithfulness surrounds You.

9 You rule the raging of the sea; when its waves rise, You still them. 10 You crushed Rahab like a corpse; You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm.

11 The heavens are Yours, and the earth is Yours; the world and all that is in it, You founded them. 12 The north and the south, You created them; Tabor and Hermon sing for joy at Your name. 13 You have a mighty arm; Your hand is strong, Your right hand is lifted high. 14 Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne; faithful love and truth go before Your face.

15 Blessed are the people who know the joyful shout; they walk in the light of Your face, the LORD. 16 They rejoice in Your name all day long, and in Your righteousness they are lifted up. 17 For You are the glory of their strength, and by Your favor our strength is lifted high. 18 For the LORD is our shield, and our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel.

God's Promise to David

19 Then You spoke in a vision to Your faithful ones and said, "I have given help to a warrior; I have exalted one chosen from the people. 20 I have found David My servant; with My holy oil I have anointed him. 21 My hand will be with him, and My arm will strengthen him. 22 The enemy will not deceive him, nor will the son of wickedness afflict him. 23 I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him. 24 My faithfulness and My faithful love will be with him, and in My name his strength will be lifted high. 25 I will set his hand over the sea and his right hand over the rivers."

"26 He will cry to Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and my saving refuge.' 27 I will make him My firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth. 28 I will keep My faithful love for him forever, and My covenant with him will stand firm. 29 I will establish his offspring forever and his throne as the days of heaven. 30 If his sons forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments, 31 if they break My statutes and do not keep My commandments,

"32 then I will punish their wrongdoing with a rod and their sin with stripes. 33 But I will not take My faithful love from him, nor will I be false to My faithfulness. 34 I will not violate My covenant or change what My lips have spoken. 35 I have sworn once by My holiness; I will not lie to David: 36 his offspring will endure forever, and his throne will be before Me like the sun. 37 Like the moon it will be established forever, a faithful witness in the sky." Selah.

When God Seems to Have Rejected His People

38 But You have cast off and rejected him; You have been very angry with Your anointed one. 39 You have renounced the covenant with Your servant; You have profaned his crown in the dust. 40 You have broken through all his walls; You have brought his strongholds to ruin. 41 All who pass by plunder him; he has become a disgrace to his neighbors.

42 You have lifted up the right hand of his foes; You have made all his enemies glad. 43 You have turned back the edge of his sword and have not supported him in battle. 44 You have ended his splendor and cast his throne to the ground. 45 You have cut short the days of his youth; You have covered him with shame. Selah.

A Prayer for Mercy

46 How long, the LORD? Will You hide Yourself forever? How long will Your wrath burn like fire? 47 Remember how short my life is; have You created all people for nothing? 48 What man can live and never see death? Who can save his soul from the power of the grave? Selah.

49 Lord, where are Your former acts of faithful love that You swore to David in Your faithfulness? 50 Remember, Lord, the shame of Your servants; how I carry in my heart the insults of many nations, 51 the insults Your enemies have hurled, the LORD, the insults they have hurled at the footsteps of Your anointed one. 52 Blessed be the LORD forever! Amen and Amen.
