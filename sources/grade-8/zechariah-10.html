Ask the LORD for Rain

1 Ask the LORD for rain when the spring rains come. The LORD makes the lightning, and He will give you showers of rain, and the fields will grow grass.

2 Household gods speak lies, and fortune-tellers see false visions. They tell empty dreams and give false comfort. So the people wander like sheep and suffer because there is no shepherd.

The LORD Will Strengthen His People

3 “My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders. For the LORD of Hosts cares for His flock, the people of Judah. He will make them like a proud war horse. 4 From Judah will come the cornerstone, the tent peg, the battle bow, and every ruler. 5 They will be like warriors trampling mud in the streets. They will fight because the LORD is with them, and they will put the horsemen to shame.

God Will Restore His People

6 “I will strengthen the people of Judah and save the people of Joseph. I will bring them back because I have had mercy on them. They will be as if I had never rejected them, for I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them. 7 Ephraim will be like a strong man. Their hearts will be glad, like people who have drunk wine. Their children will see it and be glad; their hearts will rejoice in the LORD. 8 I will whistle for them and gather them, for I have set them free. They will grow as they grew before.

The LORD Will Bring Them Home

9 “I will plant them among the nations. In faraway lands they will remember Me, and they and their children will live and return. 10 I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria. I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon, but there will not be enough room for them. 11 He will pass through the sea of trouble and strike the waves of the sea. All the deep places of the Nile will dry up. The pride of Assyria will be brought down, and Egypt’s rod of power will pass away. 12 I will make them strong in the LORD, and they will walk in His name,” says the LORD.
