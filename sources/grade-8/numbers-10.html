The Silver Trumpets

1 The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 2 "Make two silver trumpets for yourself. Hammer them from one piece of silver. They will be for you to call the community together and to signal the camps to move. 3 When both trumpets sound, the whole community must gather at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 4 If only one trumpet sounds, the leaders, the heads of Israel's clans, must gather before you. 5 When you blow a loud blast, the camps on the east side must set out. 6 When you blow a second loud blast, the camps on the south side must set out. They must blow the blast as the signal for their journey. 7 To gather the assembly, blow the trumpets, but do not sound the alarm. 8 Aaron's sons, the priests, must blow the trumpets. This is a lasting rule for you and your descendants. 9 When you go to war in your land against an enemy who attacks you, sound the alarm on the trumpets. Then the LORD your God will remember you, and He will save you from your enemies. 10 On your joyful days, your appointed festivals, and the first days of your months, blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and peace offerings. They will be a reminder for you before your God. I am the LORD your God."

Israel Leaves Mount Sinai

11 On the twentieth day of the second month of the second year, the cloud lifted from above the tabernacle of the covenant. 12 Then the Israelites set out from the Desert of Sinai and traveled from place to place. The cloud came to rest in the Desert of Paran.

The Order of the March

13 They set out for the first time at the LORD's command through Moses. 14 The divisions of the camp of Judah went first, under their standard. Nahshon son of Amminadab was in command. 15 Nethanel son of Zuar was over the division of the tribe of Issachar. 16 Eliab son of Helon was over the division of the tribe of Zebulun. 17 The tabernacle was taken down, and the Gershonites and Merarites set out, carrying it. 18 The divisions of the camp of Reuben set out next, under their standard. Elizur son of Shedeur was in command. 19 Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai was over the division of the tribe of Simeon. 20 Eliasaph son of Deuel was over the division of the tribe of Gad. 21 The Kohathites set out next, carrying the holy things. The tabernacle was to be set up before they arrived. 22 The divisions of the camp of Ephraim set out next, under their standard. Elishama son of Ammihud was in command. 23 Gamaliel son of Pedahzur was over the division of the tribe of Manasseh. 24 Abidan son of Gideoni was over the division of the tribe of Benjamin. 25 Finally, the divisions of the camp of Dan set out last, under their standard. They were the rear guard for all the camps. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai was in command. 26 Pagiel son of Okran was over the division of the tribe of Asher. 27 Ahira son of Enan was over the division of the tribe of Naphtali. 28 This was the order in which the Israelites set out, division by division.

Moses Invites Hobab

29 Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law, "We are setting out for the place the LORD promised to give us. Come with us, and we will treat you well, for the LORD has promised good things for Israel."

30 But Hobab said, "No, I will not go. I will return to my own country and my own people."

31 Moses said, "Please do not leave us. You know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you can be our guide. 32 If you come with us, we will share with you whatever good things the LORD gives us."

The Ark Leads the Way

33 So they set out from the mountain of the LORD and traveled for three days. The ark of the covenant of the LORD went before them for those three days to find a resting place for them. 34 The cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp.

35 Whenever the ark set out, Moses said, "Rise up, the LORD! May Your enemies be scattered, and may those who hate You flee before You."

36 When it came to rest, he said, "Return, the LORD, to the countless thousands of Israel."
