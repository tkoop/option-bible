The LORD Reaches Out to His People

1 I let Myself be found by people who did not ask for Me. I let Myself be found by people who did not look for Me. I said, "Here I am, here I am," to a nation that did not call on My name.

2 All day long I held out My hands to stubborn people. They walk on a path that is not good. They follow their own thoughts.

3 They are people who always make Me angry to My face. They sacrifice in gardens and burn incense on brick altars. 4 They sit among graves and stay in hidden places. They eat pig meat, and their bowls hold broth made from unclean things.

5 They say, "Stay away from me. Do not come near me, because I am holier than you." These people are like smoke in My nose. They are like a fire that burns all day.

6 Look, it is written before Me. I will not keep quiet. I will pay them back. I will pay them back in full. 7 The LORD says, "You and your fathers have sinned together. They burned incense on the mountains and insulted Me on the hills. So I will give them full payment for what they did before."

God Will Preserve His Servants

8 The LORD says, "When new wine is found in a bunch of grapes, people say not to destroy it, because there is a blessing in it. In the same way, I will act for the good of My servants. I will not destroy everyone."

9 I will bring children from Jacob. From Judah I will bring someone to take over My mountains. My chosen people will own the land, and My servants will live there. 10 Sharon will become a pasture for sheep. The Valley of Achor will become a resting place for cattle. This will be for My people who looked for Me.

11 But you who leave the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune and fill cups of mixed wine for Destiny— 12 I will hand you over to the sword. All of you will bow down to be killed. I called, but you did not answer. I spoke, but you did not listen. You did evil in My eyes. You chose what I did not want.

The LORD Promises Justice

13 Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: "My servants will eat, but you will go hungry. My servants will drink, but you will go thirsty. My servants will rejoice, but you will be put to shame. 14 My servants will sing for joy from a glad heart, but you will cry out from a hurting heart. You will wail from a broken spirit. 15 You will leave your name for a curse to My chosen ones. The Lord the LORD will put you to death, but He will call His servants by another name. 16 Whoever blesses himself in the land will bless himself by the God of truth. Whoever swears in the land will swear by the God of truth. The first troubles will be forgotten. They will be hidden from My eyes."

A New Creation and Lasting Peace

17 Look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth. The old things will not be remembered. They will not come to mind. 18 Be glad and rejoice forever in what I create. Look, I am creating Jerusalem to be a joy and its people to be a delight. 19 I will rejoice in Jerusalem and be glad in My people. The sound of weeping and crying will not be heard there anymore.

20 No more will a child die young. No old person will fail to live a full life. A person who dies at one hundred will be thought young. A sinner who dies at one hundred will be cursed.

21 They will build houses and live in them. They will plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 22 They will not build houses for someone else to live in. They will not plant vineyards for someone else to eat from. My people will live as long as trees. My chosen people will enjoy the work of their hands. 23 They will not work for nothing. They will not have children who are doomed to trouble. They will be a people blessed by the LORD, and their children will be with them.

24 Before they call, I will answer. While they are still speaking, I will hear. 25 The wolf and the lamb will eat together. The lion will eat straw like an ox. Dust will be the snake's food. They will not hurt or destroy on all My holy mountain, says the LORD.
