A Morning Prayer

1 Listen to my words, the LORD; consider my groaning. 2 Listen to my cry for help, my King and my God, for I pray to You. 3 In the morning, the LORD, You hear my voice; in the morning I bring my prayers to You and wait in hope.

The LORD Hates Evil

4 For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; no evil can dwell with You. 5 Proud people cannot stand before You; You hate all who do evil. 6 You destroy those who speak lies; the LORD detests bloodthirsty and deceitful men.

Lead Me in What Is Right

7 But I will enter Your house because of Your great mercy; in reverence I will bow down toward Your holy temple. 8 Lead me, the LORD, in Your righteousness because of my enemies; make Your way straight before me. 9 For there is no truth in their mouths; their hearts are full of ruin; their throats are open graves; they flatter with their tongues. 10 Declare them guilty, O God; let them fall by their own plans; throw them out for their many sins, for they have rebelled against You.

Joy for Those Who Trust in the LORD

11 But let everyone who trusts in You rejoice; let them shout for joy forever, and shelter them, so that those who love Your name may be glad in You. 12 For surely, the LORD, You bless those who do right; You surround them with favor like a shield.
