Samuel Hears God's Call

1 Now the boy Samuel was serving the LORD before Eli. In those days the word of the LORD was rare, and visions were not common. 2 One day Eli, whose eyes were so weak he could not see, was lying down in his place. 3 The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was lying down in the LORD's temple, where the ark of God was.

4 Then the LORD called Samuel. He answered, "Here I am."

5 Samuel ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me."

But Eli said, "I did not call you. Go back and lie down." So Samuel went back and lay down.

6 The LORD called Samuel again. Samuel got up and went to Eli. He said, "Here I am, because you called me."

But Eli said, "My son, I did not call you. Go back and lie down."

7 Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, and the word of the LORD had not yet been shown to him.

8 The LORD called Samuel a third time. Samuel got up, went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, because you called me." Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy.

9 So Eli said to Samuel, "Go lie down. If He calls you, say, 'Speak, the LORD, for Your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

God Gives Samuel a Message

10 Then the LORD came and stood there. He called as before, "Samuel! Samuel!"

Samuel answered, "Speak, for your servant is listening."

11 The LORD said to Samuel, "Look, I am about to do something in Israel that will make both ears of everyone who hears it tingle.

"On that day I will carry out against Eli everything I spoke about his family, from beginning to end.

"I have told him that I will judge his family forever because of the sin he knew about. His sons cursed God, and he did not stop them.

"Therefore I swore to Eli's family that their sin will never be forgiven by sacrifice or offering."

15 Samuel lay down until morning. Then he opened the doors of the LORD's house. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.

Samuel Shares the Message with Eli

16 Eli called Samuel and said, "My son."

Samuel answered, "Here I am."

17 Eli asked, "What did He say to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do to you and even more if you hide from me one word of all that He said to you."

18 Samuel told him everything and hid nothing. Then Eli said, "He is the LORD. Let Him do what seems good to Him."

Samuel Becomes God's Prophet

19 Samuel grew up, and the LORD was with him. He did not let any of Samuel's words fail. 20 All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew that Samuel was a trusted prophet of the LORD. 21 The LORD kept appearing at Shiloh, because He revealed Himself to Samuel there by His word.
