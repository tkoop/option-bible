Babylon Will Be Brought Low

1 Come down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter of Babylon. Sit on the ground with no throne, daughter of the Chaldeans. People will no longer call you tender and delicate. 2 Take millstones and grind grain. Take off your veil, lift your skirts, bare your legs, and wade through rivers. 3 People will see your nakedness and your shame. I will take revenge and show no mercy. 4 Our Redeemer—the the LORD of Hosts is His name—the Holy One of Israel.

The LORD's Anger and Babylon's Pride

5 Sit quietly and go into the dark, daughter of the Chaldeans. People will no longer call you queen of kingdoms. 6 I was angry with My people. I let their land be ruined, and I gave them into your hands. You showed them no mercy. You made your heavy yoke very hard, even on old people. 7 You said, “I will be queen forever,” but you did not think about these things or remember how it would end.

False Security and Coming Disaster

8 Now listen to this, you who love pleasure and feel safe. You say in your heart, “I am the only one; there is no one else. I will never be a widow or lose my children.” 9 Both of these things will hit you in one moment, in one day: losing children and becoming a widow. They will come on you fully, even with all your magic and your strong spells. 10 You trusted in your evil ways. You said, “No one sees me.” Your wisdom and knowledge tricked you. You said in your heart, “I am the only one; there is no one else.”

11 Disaster will come on you, and you will not know how to make it go away. Ruin will fall on you, and you cannot pay for it. Destruction will come on you suddenly, and you will not expect it.

Magic and Stars Cannot Save Babylon

12 Keep standing with your spells and your many charms, the things you worked hard at since you were young. Maybe they will help you. Maybe they will scare people. 13 You are tired from all your advice. Let them stand and save you—those who study the sky, who watch the stars, who tell each month what will happen to you. 14 Look, they are like dry grass. Fire will burn them up. They cannot save themselves from the flames. There is no warm coal for them, no fire to sit beside. 15 That is how they will be to you—the people you worked with since you were young. Each one wanders off his own way. No one can save you.
