The Tower of Babel

1 At that time, everyone in the world spoke the same language and used the same words. 2 As people moved east, they found a flat plain in Shinar and settled there.

3 They said to one another, "Come, let's make bricks and bake them hard." They used brick instead of stone, and tar for mortar. 4 Then they said, "Come, let's build a city for ourselves, with a tower that reaches the sky. We want to make a name for ourselves so we won't be scattered all over the earth."

5 But the LORD came down to see the city and the tower the people were building. 6 The LORD said, "They are one people, and they all speak the same language. This is just the beginning of what they will do. Soon nothing they plan will be too hard for them. 7 Come, let's go down and mix up their language so they won't understand each other."

8 So the LORD scattered them from there over the whole earth, and they stopped building the city. 9 That is why it was called Babel—because there the LORD mixed up the language of the whole world. From there the LORD scattered them over the whole earth.

The Family Line from Shem to Terah

10 These are the family records of Shem. Shem was 100 years old when he became the father of Arphaxad, two years after the flood. 11 After Arphaxad was born, Shem lived 500 more years and had other sons and daughters.

12 Arphaxad was 35 years old when he became the father of Shelah. 13 After Shelah was born, Arphaxad lived 403 more years and had other sons and daughters.

14 Shelah was 30 years old when he became the father of Eber. 15 After Eber was born, Shelah lived 403 more years and had other sons and daughters.

16 Eber was 34 years old when he became the father of Peleg. 17 After Peleg was born, Eber lived 430 more years and had other sons and daughters.

18 Peleg was 30 years old when he became the father of Reu. 19 After Reu was born, Peleg lived 209 more years and had other sons and daughters.

20 Reu was 32 years old when he became the father of Serug. 21 After Serug was born, Reu lived 207 more years and had other sons and daughters.

22 Serug was 30 years old when he became the father of Nahor. 23 After Nahor was born, Serug lived 200 more years and had other sons and daughters.

24 Nahor was 29 years old when he became the father of Terah. 25 After Terah was born, Nahor lived 119 more years and had other sons and daughters.

26 Terah was 70 years old when he became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

Terah's Family

27 These are the family records of Terah: Terah became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran became the father of Lot. 28 Haran died while his father Terah was still alive, in the land where he was born, in Ur of the Chaldeans.

29 Abram and Nahor married. Abram's wife was named Sarai, and Nahor's wife was named Milcah. Milcah was the daughter of Haran, who was the father of both Milcah and Iscah. 30 Sarai could not have children.

31 Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot (Haran's son), and his daughter-in-law Sarai (Abram's wife), and they left Ur of the Chaldeans to go to Canaan. When they reached Haran, they settled there. 32 Terah lived 205 years and died in Haran.
