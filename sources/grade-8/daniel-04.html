Nebuchadnezzar’s Letter to All Peoples

1 King Nebuchadnezzar to all peoples, nations, and languages that live in all the earth: Peace be multiplied to you. 2 It seemed good to me to show the signs and wonders that the Most High God has done for me. 3 How great are His signs, and how mighty His wonders! His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and His rule lasts from generation to generation.

The King’s Troubling Dream

4 I, Nebuchadnezzar, was at rest in my house and doing well in my palace. 5 I had a dream that made me afraid. The thoughts on my bed and the visions in my head troubled me. 6 So I gave an order to bring all the wise men of Babylon before me, so they could tell me what the dream meant. 7 Then the magicians, enchanters, Chaldeans, and astrologers came in. I told them the dream, but they could not explain it to me.

8 At last Daniel came in before me. His name is Belteshazzar, after the name of my god, and the spirit of the holy gods is in him. I told him the dream and said, 9 “Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians, I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in you and that no secret is too hard for you. Tell me the visions I saw in my dream and what they mean.

10 “These were the visions in my mind as I lay on my bed: I saw a tree in the middle of the earth, and it was very tall. 11 The tree grew and became strong. Its top reached to heaven, and it could be seen from the ends of the whole earth. 12 Its leaves were beautiful, and its fruit was plentiful. It had food for everyone. The wild animals found shade under it, and the birds of the sky lived in its branches. All living things were fed from it.

13 “In the visions of my head as I lay on my bed, I saw a watcher, a holy one, come down from heaven. 14 He called out loudly: ‘Cut down the tree and cut off its branches. Strip off its leaves and scatter its fruit. Let the animals run away from under it and the birds from its branches. 15 But leave the stump and its roots in the ground, bound with a band of iron and bronze, in the grass of the field. Let him be wet with the dew of heaven, and let him live with the animals in the grass of the earth. 16 Let his mind be changed from a man’s mind, and let him be given an animal’s mind. Let seven periods of time pass over him. 17 This sentence comes by the decree of the watchers, and this decision is a command of the holy ones, so that living people may know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men and gives them to anyone He chooses, even to the lowliest of men.’

18 “This is the dream that I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw. Now, Belteshazzar, tell me what it means, because none of the wise men of my kingdom can explain it. But you can, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.”

Daniel Gives the Meaning

19 Then Daniel, whose name is Belteshazzar, was silent for a while, and his thoughts frightened him. The king said, “Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or its meaning frighten you.”

Belteshazzar answered, “My lord, may the dream be for your enemies and its meaning for your foes!

20 “The tree you saw, which grew and became strong, whose top reached to heaven and could be seen by all the earth, 21 whose leaves were beautiful and whose fruit was plentiful, and which had food for everyone—under it the wild animals lived, and in its branches the birds of the sky nested— 22 it is you, O king. You have grown and become strong. Your greatness has reached to heaven, and your rule reaches to the ends of the earth.

23 “You also saw a watcher, a holy one, come down from heaven. He said, ‘Cut down the tree and destroy it, but leave the stump and its roots in the ground, bound with a band of iron and bronze, in the grass of the field. Let him be wet with the dew of heaven, and let him live with the wild animals, until seven periods of time pass over him.’

24 “This is the meaning, O king. It is the decree of the Most High that has come upon my lord the king: 25 You will be driven away from people, and you will live with the wild animals. You will be made to eat grass like an ox. You will be wet with the dew of heaven, and seven periods of time will pass over you, until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men and gives them to anyone He chooses. 26 Just as it was commanded to leave the stump with the roots of the tree, your kingdom will be given back to you when you know that Heaven rules. 27 So, O king, please accept my advice: Stop your sins by doing right, and stop your wrongs by being kind to the poor. Then your good times may last longer.”

The Dream Comes True

28 All this happened to King Nebuchadnezzar. 29 Twelve months later, he was walking on the roof of the royal palace in Babylon. 30 The king said, “Is this not great Babylon, which I built by my great power as a royal home and for the glory of my majesty?”

31 While the words were still in the king’s mouth, a voice from heaven said, “King Nebuchadnezzar, your kingdom has been taken from you. 32 You will be driven away from people and live with the wild animals. You will be made to eat grass like an ox, and seven periods of time will pass over you, until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men and gives them to anyone He chooses.”

33 Right away the word was fulfilled against Nebuchadnezzar. He was driven away from people and ate grass like an ox. His body was wet with the dew of heaven, until his hair grew like eagle feathers and his nails like bird claws.

34 At the end of that time, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up to heaven, and my mind came back to me. Then I blessed the Most High and praised and honored Him who lives forever. His rule is an everlasting rule, and His kingdom lasts from generation to generation. 35 All the people on earth are like nothing before Him. He does what He wants with heaven’s armies and the people of the earth. No one can hold back His hand or say to Him, “What have You done?”

36 At that time my mind came back to me. My honor and glory were given back to me for the glory of my kingdom. My advisors and nobles came to me, and I was put back on my throne. I became even greater than before. 37 Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise, lift up, and honor the King of Heaven, because all His works are true and all His ways are just. He is able to bring down those who walk in pride.
