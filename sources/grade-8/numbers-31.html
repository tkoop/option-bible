God Commands War Against Midian

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 "Take revenge on the Midianites for the Israelites. After that, you will die and join your ancestors. 3 So Moses said to the people, "Arm some of your men to go to war against Midian. They will carry out the the LORD's revenge on them. 4 Send one thousand men from each tribe of Israel into battle." 5 So twelve thousand men armed for battle were supplied from Israel's clans—one thousand from each tribe. 6 Moses sent them into battle, one thousand from each tribe, along with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, who took with him holy items and the trumpets used to sound the attack.

Israel Defeats Midian

7 They fought against Midian, as the the LORD had commanded Moses, and killed every male. 8 Among those killed were the kings of Midian—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba—the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword. 9 The Israelites captured the Midianite women and children and took all their herds, flocks, and goods as plunder. 10 They burned all the towns where the Midianites had lived, along with all their camps. 11 They took all the plunder and spoils, both people and animals. 12 They brought the captives, spoils, and plunder to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the Israelite community at the camp on the plains of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho.

Moses Corrects the Army

13 Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the community went out to meet them outside the camp. 14 But Moses was angry with the army officers—the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds—who had returned from the battle.

15 "Have you spared all the women?" he asked them. 16 "Look, these women led the Israelites, on Balaam's advice, to be unfaithful to the the LORD at Peor, and a plague broke out among the the LORD's people. 17 Now kill every male child, and kill every woman who has slept with a man. 18 But keep for yourselves every girl who has never slept with a man. 19 All of you who have killed anyone or touched a dead body must stay outside the camp for seven days. Purify yourselves and your captives on the third day and the seventh day. 20 Purify every garment, everything made of leather, everything made of goat hair, and every article of wood."

21 Then Eleazar the priest said to the soldiers who had gone into battle, "This is the rule of the law that the the LORD has commanded Moses. 22 Only things made of gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, or lead— 23 everything that can withstand fire—you must pass through the fire, and it will be clean. But it must also be purified with purifying water. Everything that cannot withstand fire you must pass through the water. 24 On the seventh day you must wash your clothes, and then you will be clean. After that you may enter the camp."

Dividing the Spoils

25 The LORD said to Moses, 26 "You and Eleazar the priest and the family leaders of the community are to count the spoils that were taken—both the people and the animals. 27 Then divide the spoils between the soldiers who went out to battle and the rest of the community. 28 From the soldiers who fought, take one out of every five hundred, whether people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep, as a tribute to the the LORD. 29 Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as the the LORD's contribution. 30 From the Israelites' half, take one out of every fifty, whether people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep, and give them to the Levites who guard the the LORD's tabernacle."

31 So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the the LORD had commanded Moses.

32 The plunder remaining from the spoils taken by the fighting men was 675,000 sheep, 33 72,000 cattle, 34 61,000 donkeys, 35 and 32,000 women—all of whom had never slept with a man.

36 The half given to those who had gone out to war numbered 337,500 sheep, 37 and the the LORD's tribute from the sheep was 675. 38 There were 36,000 cattle, and their tribute to the the LORD was 72. 39 There were 30,500 donkeys, and their tribute to the the LORD was 61. 40 There were 16,000 people, and the the LORD's tribute was 32. 41 Moses gave the tribute to Eleazar the priest as a contribution to the the LORD, just as the the LORD had commanded Moses. 42 From the Israelites' half, which Moses had separated from that of the men who had gone to battle, 43 the community's share was 337,500 sheep, 44 36,000 cattle, 45 30,500 donkeys, 46 and 16,000 people. 47 Moses took one out of every fifty from the Israelites' share and gave them to the Levites who maintained the the LORD's tabernacle, as the the LORD had commanded Moses.

48 Then the army officers—the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds—came to Moses 49 and said, "Your servants have counted the men under our command, and not one of us is missing. 50 So we have brought an offering to the the LORD from the gold articles each man found—armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces—to make atonement for ourselves before the the LORD." 51 Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from them, all the articles made of gold. 52 The gold that the commanders of thousands and hundreds offered as a contribution to the the LORD weighed 16,750 shekels. 53 (Each soldier had taken plunder for himself.) 54 Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold and brought it to the tent of meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the the LORD.
