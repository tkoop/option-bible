A Song of Ascents. By David.

Thanking God for Keeping Us Safe

1 If the LORD had not been on our side—let Israel say it now— 2 if the LORD had not been on our side when people rose up against us, 3 then they would have swallowed us alive when their anger flared against us.

4 Then the flood would have swept us away, and the rushing stream would have covered us. 5 Yes, the proud, raging waters would have swept over us.

Praise for Our Rescue

6 Praise the LORD, who did not let our enemies devour us. 7 We have escaped like a bird from a hunter's trap. The trap is broken, and we have escaped.

8 Our help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
