Woe to Nineveh, the Bloody City

1 Woe to the city of blood, full of lies, full of plunder, never without victims! 2 The crack of the whip and the rumble of wheels, galloping horses and bouncing chariots! 3 Charging horsemen, flashing swords, and shining spears! Many are slain, piles of dead bodies, corpses without end—they trip over their dead. 4 Because of the prostitute's many acts of prostitution—she is charming but deadly—she sells whole nations through her prostitution and families through her sorcery.

The LORD's Judgment on Nineveh

5 "I am against you," declares the LORD of Hosts. "I will lift your skirt over your face; I will show your nakedness to the nations and your shame to the kingdoms. 6 I will throw filth on you and treat you with contempt; I will make you a public disgrace." 7 Then everyone who sees you will shrink back and say, 'Nineveh is destroyed! Who will mourn for her?' Where can I find anyone to comfort you?

Nineveh Compared to Thebes

8 Are you better than Thebes, which sits by the Nile with water all around it? The river was her protection, and water was her wall. 9 Cush and Egypt were her endless strength; Put and Libya were her allies. 10 Yet she became an exile and went into captivity. Her infants were dashed to pieces at the head of every street. They cast lots for her nobles, and all her great men were bound in chains.

11 You too will become drunk; you will go into hiding. You too will seek refuge from the enemy. 12 All your fortresses are like fig trees with ripe figs: when shaken, the figs fall into the mouth of the eater. 13 Look, your people are women in your midst; the gates of your land are wide open to your enemies; fire has eaten away your bars.

The Fall of Nineveh

14 Draw water for the siege; strengthen your fortresses. Go into the clay and tread the mortar; repair the brick kiln. 15 There the fire will consume you; the sword will cut you down. It will devour you like the young locust. Multiply like the young locust; multiply like the swarming locust! 16 You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of heaven. The young locust strips the land and flies away. 17 Your leaders are like swarming locusts, and your officials like clouds of locusts that settle on the walls on a cold day. When the sun rises, they fly away, and no one knows where. 18 O king of Assyria, your shepherds slumber; your leaders are asleep. Your people are scattered on the mountains with no one to gather them. 19 There is no healing for your wound; your injury is fatal. All who hear the news of you clap their hands over you, for who has not felt your endless cruelty?
