David Decides to Guard His Words

1 I said, "I will watch how I live so I don't sin with my tongue. I will keep my mouth under control like with a muzzle while wicked people are near me." 2 I stayed quiet and said nothing, not even good things, but my pain grew worse. 3 My heart burned inside me. As I thought, the fire grew, and then I spoke.

David Asks About Life's Shortness

4 The LORD, show me when my life will end and how long my days are, so I can know how weak I am. 5 Look, You made my days only a few hand-widths long, and my whole life is nothing compared to You. Truly, every person, even at their best, is only a breath. Selah. 6 Surely everyone walks around like a shadow. They worry for nothing. They pile up wealth, but they don't know who will take it. 7 And now, Lord, what am I waiting for? My hope is in You.

David Pleads for Mercy

8 Save me from all my sins; don't make me an insult to foolish people. 9 I stayed quiet and didn't open my mouth, because You did this. 10 Take Your punishment away from me; I am dying under Your heavy hand. 11 When You correct people for sin, You wear away what they treasure, like a moth eats cloth. Surely everyone is only a breath. Selah. 12 Hear my prayer, the LORD, and listen to my cry; don't ignore my tears. I am a stranger living with You, a visitor like all my ancestors. 13 Look away from me so I can be cheerful again before I go and am no more.
