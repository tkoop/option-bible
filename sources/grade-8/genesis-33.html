Jacob and Esau Meet Again

1 Jacob looked up and saw Esau coming toward him with four hundred men. He divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two female servants.

2 He put the female servants and their children first, Leah and her children next, and Rachel and Joseph last.

3 Then Jacob went ahead of them and bowed to the ground seven times as he came near his brother.

4 But Esau ran to meet him. He hugged Jacob, threw his arms around his neck, kissed him, and they both cried.

Jacob Introduces His Family

5 Esau looked up and saw the women and children. He asked, "Who are these with you?"

Jacob answered, "They are the children God has graciously given your servant."

6 The female servants and their children came forward and bowed down.

7 Then Leah and her children came forward and bowed down. After them, Joseph and Rachel came forward and bowed down.

Esau Accepts Jacob's Gift

8 Esau asked, "What do you mean by all this group I met?"

Jacob replied, "I sent it so I would find favor in my lord's eyes."

9 Esau said, "I already have plenty, my brother. Keep what you have."

10 Jacob said, "No, please. If I have found favor in your eyes, then accept this gift from my hand. I have seen your face, and it was like seeing the face of God, and you were pleased with me."

11 Jacob said, "Please take the blessing I brought you, because God has been gracious to me, and I have everything I need." Jacob urged him until Esau took it.

12 Esau said, "Let us travel on together. I will go ahead of you."

13 Jacob said, "My lord knows the children are weak, and the flocks and herds have young with them. If they are driven hard for one day, all the animals will die."

14 Jacob said, "Please let my lord go ahead of your servant. I will travel slowly, at the pace of the animals ahead of me and at the pace of the children, until I reach my lord at Seir."

15 Esau said, "Then let me leave some of my men with you."

Jacob replied, "Why do that? Just let me find favor in my lord's eyes."

16 So that day Esau started back to Seir.

Jacob Settles Near Shechem

17 Jacob traveled to Succoth. He built a house for himself and made shelters for his animals. That is why the place was called Succoth.

18 When Jacob came from Paddan Aram, he arrived safely at the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan. He camped near the city.

19 He bought the piece of land where he had pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for one hundred pieces of silver.

20 There he set up an altar and named it "God, the God of Israel."
