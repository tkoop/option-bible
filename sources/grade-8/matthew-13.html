Jesus Teaches by the Sea

1 That day, Jesus left the house and sat by the sea. 2 Large crowds gathered around Him, so He got into a boat and sat down. The whole crowd stood on the shore.

3 He told them many things in parables, saying, "A farmer went out to sow seed. 4 As he sowed, some seed fell along the path, and birds came and ate it. 5 Other seed fell on rocky ground, where there wasn't much soil. It sprang up quickly because the soil was shallow. 6 But when the sun came up, it was scorched. Since it had no root, it dried up. 7 Other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them. 8 Other seed fell on good soil and produced grain—some a hundred times, some sixty, and some thirty. 9 Whoever has ears should listen."

Why Jesus Used Parables

10 The disciples came to Him and asked, "Why do you speak to them in parables?"

11 He answered, "It has been given to you to know the secrets of the kingdom of heaven, but it has not been given to them. 12 Whoever has will be given more, and he will have plenty. Whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away. 13 That is why I speak to them in parables: They see, but they do not really see. They hear, but they do not really hear or understand.

14 "In them the words of the prophet Isaiah come true: 'You will hear but never understand. You will see but never really see.' 15 For these people's hearts have become hard. They hear with difficulty, and they have closed their eyes. Otherwise, they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, turn to Me, and be healed.

16 "But your eyes are blessed, because they see, and your ears are blessed, because they hear. 17 I tell you the truth: Many prophets and good people longed to see what you see, but they did not see it. They longed to hear what you hear, but they did not hear it."

18 "Listen, then, to the parable of the sower. 19 When anyone hears the message about the kingdom and does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away what was planted in that person's heart. That is the seed sown along the path. 20 The seed sown on rocky ground is the person who hears the message and quickly accepts it with joy. 21 But he has no root in himself. He lasts only a short time. When trouble or persecution comes because of the message, he quickly falls away. 22 The seed sown among thorns is the person who hears the message, but the worries of this life and the deceitfulness of wealth choke the message, and it does not bear fruit. 23 The seed sown on good soil is the person who hears the message and understands it. He bears fruit and produces a crop—some a hundred times, some sixty, and some thirty."

More Parables About the Kingdom

24 He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like a man who planted good seed in his field. 25 But while everyone was sleeping, his enemy came and planted weeds among the wheat, and then went away. 26 When the wheat grew and formed heads of grain, the weeds also appeared.

27 "The owner's servants came to him and said, 'Sir, didn't you sow good seed in your field? How then does it have weeds?' 28 He answered, 'An enemy did this.' The servants asked him, 'Do you want us to go and pull them up?'

29 "He said, 'No. If you pull up the weeds, you might pull up the wheat with them. 30 Let both grow together until the harvest. Then I will tell the harvesters, "First collect the weeds and tie them in bundles to be burned. Then gather the wheat into my barn."'

31 He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a man planted in his field. 32 It is the smallest of all seeds. But when it grows, it is larger than the garden plants and becomes a tree. Then the birds of the sky come and nest in its branches."

33 He told them another parable: "The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman mixed into three measures of flour until all of it was leavened."

34 Jesus said all these things to the crowds in parables. He did not say anything to them without using a parable. 35 This happened to fulfill what the prophet said: "I will open My mouth in parables. I will speak things hidden since the world was made."

36 Then He left the crowds and went into the house. His disciples came to Him and said, "Explain to us the parable of the weeds in the field."

37 He answered, "The one who sows the good seed is the Son of Man. 38 The field is the world. The good seed stands for the children of the kingdom. The weeds are the children of the evil one. 39 The enemy who planted them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the harvesters are angels.

40 "Just as the weeds are collected and burned in the fire, so it will be at the end of the age. 41 The Son of Man will send out His angels. They will gather from His kingdom everything that causes sin and everyone who does evil. 42 They will throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be crying and terrible pain. 43 Then the good people will shine like the sun in their Father's kingdom. Whoever has ears should listen."

44 "The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field. A man found it, hid it again, and then, full of joy, went and sold everything he had and bought that field."

45 "Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant looking for fine pearls. 46 When he found one pearl of great value, he went and sold everything he had and bought it."

47 "Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a net thrown into the sea that caught all kinds of fish. 48 When it was full, the fishermen pulled it to shore, sat down, and collected the good fish into baskets. They threw the bad ones away."

49 "This is how it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come and separate the evil people from the good people. 50 They will throw the evil people into the fiery furnace, where there will be crying and terrible pain."

51 "Do you understand all this?"

They answered, "Yes."

52 He said to them, "Then every teacher of the law who has been trained for the kingdom of heaven is like the owner of a house. He brings out of his storeroom both new things and old things."

53 When Jesus had finished these parables, He left that place.

Jesus Visits His Hometown

54 He came to His hometown and taught in their synagogue. The people were amazed and asked, "Where did this man get this wisdom and these powerful works?"

55 "Isn't this the carpenter's son? Isn't His mother called Mary? Aren't His brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas? 56 Aren't all His sisters here with us? Where, then, did He get all these things?"

57 They were offended by Him. But Jesus said to them, "A prophet is honored everywhere except in his hometown and in his own home."

58 He did not do many powerful works there because they did not believe.
