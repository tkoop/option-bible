Elihu Trembles at God's Power

1At this my heart trembles and leaps out of its place. 2Listen carefully to the thunder of His voice and the rumble that comes from His mouth. 3He sends it out under the whole sky, and His lightning to the ends of the earth. 4Afterward His voice roars. He thunders with His majestic voice, and He does not hold back the lightning when His voice is heard. 5God thunders marvelously with His voice; He does great things we cannot understand.

God Commands the Weather

6He tells the snow, "Fall on the earth," and to the rain shower, "Pour down hard." 7He stops every person's work, so that everyone may know what He has done. 8Then wild animals go into their dens and stay in their hiding places. 9The storm comes from its chamber, and cold comes from the scattering winds. 10By the breath of God, ice is made, and the wide waters are frozen solid. 11He fills the thick cloud with water, and the cloud scatters His lightning. 12They turn around and around by His guidance, to carry out everything He commands on the face of the whole world. 13He sends it for correction, for His land, or as an act of mercy.

Elihu Challenges Job

14Listen to this, O Job; stand still and think about the wonderful works of God. 15Do you know how God gives them their orders and makes the lightning flash from His cloud? 16Do you know how the clouds are balanced, the amazing works of Him who is perfect in knowledge—17 you whose clothes grow hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? 18Can you spread out the sky with Him, hard as a mirror made of cast metal? 19Teach us what we should say to Him; we cannot put our case in order because of the darkness. 20Should someone tell Him that I want to speak? If a person spoke, would he not be swallowed up?

No One Can Stand Before the Almighty

21Now people cannot look at the bright light in the sky after the wind has passed and cleared the skies. 22Out of the north comes golden splendor; God is clothed with terrifying majesty. 23The Almighty—we cannot find Him; He is great in power, and in justice and great goodness He will not wrong anyone. 24So people fear Him; He does not look with favor on those who are wise in their own eyes.
