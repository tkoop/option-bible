Comfort for God's People

1 "Comfort, comfort my people," says your God. 2 Speak kindly to Jerusalem and tell her that her hard service is over, her sin has been paid for, and she has received from the the LORD's hand double for all her sins.

3 A voice calls out, "Prepare the way for the the LORD in the wilderness. Make a straight road in the desert for our God." 4 Every valley will be raised up, and every mountain and hill will be made low. The rough ground will become level, and the rugged places will become a plain. 5 Then the glory of the the LORD will be shown, and all people will see it together. The the LORD has spoken.

6 A voice says, "Cry out!"

And I asked, "What should I cry?"

"All people are like grass, and all their glory is like the flowers of the field. 7 The grass dries up and the flowers fall when the the LORD's breath blows on them. Yes, the people are like grass. 8 The grass dries up and the flowers fall, but the word of our God lasts forever."

Good News: The Lord Is Coming

9 Go up on a high mountain, messenger of good news to Zion. Lift up your voice with strength, messenger of good news to Jerusalem. Lift it up; do not be afraid. Say to the towns of Judah, "Here is your God!" 10 Look, the Lord the the LORD comes with power, and His arm rules for Him. Look, His reward is with Him, and His pay is before Him. 11 He will feed His flock like a shepherd. He will gather the lambs in His arms and carry them close to His heart. He will gently lead the mothers with their young.

Who Can Measure or Match God?

12 Who has measured the waters in the hollow of His hand and marked off the heavens with His fingers? Who has measured the dust of the earth and weighed the mountains and hills on a scale? 13 Who can measure the Spirit of the the LORD, or tell Him what to do? 14 Who did He ask for advice? Who made Him understand and taught Him the right way? Who taught Him knowledge and showed Him the path of wisdom? 15 Look, the nations are like a drop in a bucket. They are counted like dust on a scale. Look, He lifts up the islands like fine dust. 16 Lebanon's trees would not be enough for fuel, and its animals would not be enough for a burnt offering. 17 All the nations are nothing before Him. They are counted by Him as less than nothing, as empty. 18 To whom, then, will you compare God? What image will you make for Him? 19 An idol? A worker shapes it, and a goldsmith covers it with gold and makes silver chains for it. 20 A poor person chooses wood that will not rot and looks for a skilled worker to make an idol that will not fall over. 21 Do you not know? Have you not heard? Has it not been told you from the beginning? Have you not understood since the earth was founded? 22 He sits above the circle of the earth, and its people are like grasshoppers. He stretches out the heavens like a curtain and spreads them like a tent to live in. 23 He brings princes to nothing and makes the rulers of the earth empty. 24 They are barely planted and barely sown. Their stem has barely taken root in the earth when He blows on them and they dry up, and a storm carries them away like straw. 25 "To whom, then, will you compare Me?" asks the Holy One. "Who is equal to Me?" 26 Look up at the sky. Who created all these? He brings out the stars by number and calls each one by name. By His great power and strength, not one of them is missing.

Strength for the Weary

27 Why do you say, O Jacob, and speak, O Israel, "My way is hidden from the the LORD, and my God overlooks my cause"? 28 Have you not known? Have you not heard? The the LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. He does not get tired or weary. No one can understand His wisdom. 29 He gives strength to the tired and increases the power of the weak. 30 Even young people get tired and weary, and young men stumble and fall. 31 But those who wait for the the LORD will renew their strength. They will soar on wings like eagles. They will run and not get tired. They will walk and not grow weary.
