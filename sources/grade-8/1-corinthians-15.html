The Good News About Christ's Resurrection

1 Now, brothers and sisters, I want to remind you of the good news I preached to you. You received it, and you are standing firm in it. 2 Through it you are being saved, if you hold on to the message I preached to you—unless you believed for no reason. 3 I passed on to you what I received first: Christ died for our sins, just as the Scriptures say. 4 He was buried, and He rose again on the third day, just as the Scriptures say. 5 He appeared to Cephas, then to the Twelve. 6 After that, He appeared to more than five hundred brothers and sisters at once. Most of them are still alive, though some have died.

7 Then He appeared to James, then to all the apostles. 8 Last of all, He appeared to me too, as if I were born at the wrong time. 9 I am the least of the apostles. I am not even fit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted God's church. 10 But by God's grace I am what I am. His grace to me was not wasted. I worked harder than all of them, yet it was not really me, but God's grace with me. 11 So whether it was I or they, this is what we preach, and this is what you believed.

Why the Resurrection Matters

12 Now, if we preach that Christ rose from the dead, how can some of you say there is no resurrection of the dead? 13 If there is no resurrection of the dead, then Christ has not been raised. 14 If Christ has not been raised, our preaching is empty, and your faith is empty too. 15 Then we are found to be false witnesses about God, because we said God raised Christ. But if the dead are not raised, He did not raise Him. 16 If the dead are not raised, Christ has not been raised either. 17 If Christ has not been raised, your faith is useless, and you are still in your sins. 18 Then those who died in Christ are lost. 19 If our hope in Christ is only for this life, we are the most pitiful of all people.

20 But Christ has been raised from the dead! He is the first of those who have died to be raised. 21 Since death came through a man, the resurrection of the dead also comes through a man. 22 Just as everyone dies because of Adam, everyone will be made alive because of Christ. 23 But each in his own turn: Christ first, then those who belong to Christ when He comes. 24 Then comes the end, when He hands over the kingdom to God the Father, after He has destroyed every ruler, authority, and power. 25 Christ must rule until He has defeated all His enemies. 26 The last enemy to be destroyed is death. 27 For Scripture says, "He has put everything under His control." But when it says everything is under His control, it does not include the One who gave Him that control. 28 When everything is under His control, then the Son Himself will submit to the One who gave Him control over everything, so that God may be all in all.

29 What will those do who are baptized for the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why are people baptized for them? 30 And why do we put ourselves in danger every hour? 31 I face death every day, brothers and sisters. As surely as I boast about you in Christ Jesus our Lord, this is true. 32 If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus for human reasons, what good did it do me? If the dead are not raised, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die." 33 Do not be fooled: bad company ruins good habits. 34 Wake up and do what is right, and stop sinning. Some of you do not know God. I say this to shame you.

What Our Resurrection Bodies Will Be Like

35 But someone will ask, "How are the dead raised? What kind of body will they have?"

36 You fool! What you plant does not come to life unless it dies. 37 When you plant, you do not plant the body that will grow. You plant a bare seed, maybe wheat or something else. 38 But God gives it a body just as He wants. He gives each kind of seed its own body. 39 Not all flesh is the same. People have one kind of flesh, animals another, birds another, and fish another. 40 There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. The glory of the heavenly bodies is one kind, and the glory of the earthly bodies is another. 41 The sun has one kind of glory, the moon another, and the stars another. Even one star differs from another in glory. 42 So it is with the resurrection of the dead. What is planted decays; what is raised will never decay. 43 It is planted in dishonor; it is raised in glory. It is planted in weakness; it is raised in power. 44 It is planted a natural body; it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.

45 That is why Scripture says, "The first man Adam became a living being." The last Adam became a life-giving spirit. 46 The spiritual did not come first. The natural came first, then the spiritual. 47 The first man was made from dust. The second man is from heaven. 48 Just as the earthly man was, so are the earthly people. Just as the heavenly man is, so will the heavenly people be. 49 We wore the image of the earthly man, and we will wear the image of the heavenly man. 50 I tell you this, brothers and sisters: flesh and blood cannot inherit God's kingdom. What decays cannot inherit what will never decay.

Victory Over Death

51 Listen! I tell you a secret: we will not all die, but we will all be changed. 52 In a flash, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. The trumpet will sound, the dead will be raised so they will never decay, and we will be changed. 53 This body that can decay must become a body that will never decay, and this mortal body must become immortal. 54 When this body that can decay becomes a body that will never decay, and this mortal body becomes immortal, then the saying that is written will come true: "Death has been defeated by victory." 55 "Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?"

56 The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law. 57 But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ. 58 So, my dear brothers and sisters, stand firm. Let nothing move you. Always work hard for the Lord, because you know your work in the Lord is not wasted.
