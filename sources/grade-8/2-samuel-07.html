David Wants to Build a House for the LORD

1 After the king was living in his house and the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies around him, 2 the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of God is staying in a tent." 3 Nathan said to the king, "Go and do everything in your heart, for the LORD is with you."

The LORD's Promise to David

4 That night the word of the LORD came to Nathan.

5 "Go and tell My servant David, 'This is what the LORD says: Will you build Me a house to live in? 6 I have not lived in a house since the day I brought the Israelites up from Egypt until now. I have been moving around in a tent and a tabernacle. 7 Everywhere I went with all the Israelites, did I ever say a word to any tribe I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, asking, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedar?"'

8 "Now then, tell My servant David, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be ruler over My people Israel. 9 I have been with you wherever you went. I cut off all your enemies before you. I have made your name great, like the names of the greatest men on earth. 10 I will set a place for My people Israel and plant them. They will live in their own place and not be disturbed anymore. Wicked people will not trouble them as they did before. 11 This is also from the day I appointed judges over My people Israel. I will give you rest from all your enemies. The LORD has also told you that He will make a royal house for you. 12 When your days are over and you die and join your ancestors, I will raise up your offspring after you, one who comes from your body. I will establish his kingdom. 13 He will build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever. 14 I will be a Father to him, and he will be a Son to Me. When he sins, I will correct him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of men. 15 My faithful love will not leave him, as I took it away from Saul, whom I removed from before you. 16 Your house and your kingdom will be sure forever before you. Your throne will be established forever.'

17 Nathan told David all these words and this vision.

David's Prayer of Thanks

18 Then King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, "Who am I, Lord GOD, and what is my family, that You have brought me this far? 19 And yet this was a small thing in Your eyes, Lord GOD. You have also spoken about Your servant's family line for a long time to come. This is how You deal with people, Lord GOD. 20 What more can David say to You? You know Your servant, Lord GOD. 21 For Your word's sake and according to Your heart, You have done all this greatness and made it known to Your servant. 22 Therefore You are great, Lord GOD. There is no one like You, and there is no God besides You, according to all we have heard with our ears. 23 And who is like Your people Israel? It is one nation on earth that God went out to redeem for Himself, to make a name for Himself, and to do great and awesome things for Your land, before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt, from the nations and their gods. 24 You established Your people Israel as Your own people forever, and You, the LORD, became their God.

25 "Now, the LORD God, let the word You spoke about Your servant and his house be established forever. Do as You have spoken. 26 Let Your name be great forever, so that people say, 'The the LORD of Hosts is God over Israel,' and let the house of Your servant David be established before You. 27 For You, the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, have revealed to Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you.' That is why Your servant found courage to pray this prayer to You. 28 Now, Lord GOD, You are God, and Your words are true. You have promised this good thing to Your servant. 29 Now be pleased to bless the house of Your servant, so it may be before You forever. For You, Lord GOD, have spoken, and with Your blessing the house of Your servant will be blessed forever."
