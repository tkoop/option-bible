Opposition to the Wall

1 When Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he got angry and very upset. He mocked the Jews. 2 In front of his brothers and the army of Samaria, he said, "What are these weak Jews doing? Do they think they can rebuild it? Will they offer sacrifices? Can they finish in one day? Can they bring stones back to life from piles of burned rubble?"

3 Tobiah the Ammonite was standing beside him, and he said, "Even if a fox climbed on it, their stone wall would fall apart!"

Nehemiah Prays

4 Hear us, our God, because we are looked down on. Turn their insults back on them, and let them be plundered in a land where they are captives. 5 Do not cover up their guilt, and do not wipe away their sin from Your sight, because they have made You angry in front of the builders.

Enemies Plot an Attack

6 So we built the wall until all of it was joined together up to half its height, because the people were eager to work. 7 But when Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites, and the people of Ashdod heard that the walls of Jerusalem were being repaired and that the gaps were being closed, they became very angry. 8 They all plotted together to come and fight against Jerusalem and cause trouble there. 9 We prayed to our God and posted guards day and night to protect ourselves from them.

10 In Judah people were saying, "The workers are worn out, and there is too much rubble. We can't rebuild the wall by ourselves."

11 Our enemies said, "They won't know or see anything until we are right among them. Then we'll kill them and stop the work."

12 The Jews who lived near them came and told us ten times over, "Wherever you turn, they will attack us."

13 So I stationed people behind the lowest parts of the wall, at the open spots. I placed them by families, with their swords, spears, and bows. 14 After I looked things over, I stood up and said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord, who is great and awesome. Fight for your brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your homes." 15 When our enemies heard that we knew about their plan and that God had ruined it, we all went back to the wall, each to his own work.

The Builders Work Armed

16 From that day on, half of my men worked on the building, and half held the spears, shields, bows, and armor. The leaders stood behind all the people of Judah. 17 The builders and the carriers worked with one hand and held a weapon with the other. 18 Each builder had his sword strapped at his side while he worked. The man who blew the trumpet stayed beside me.

19 I said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, "The work is big and spread out, and we are far apart on the wall. 20 Wherever you hear the trumpet sound, gather to us there. Our God will fight for us."

21 So we worked from dawn until the stars came out, while half of them held spears.

22 I also told the people at that time, "Let every man and his servant stay inside Jerusalem at night. Then they can guard us at night and work during the day."

23 Neither I, nor my brothers, nor my servants, nor the guards with me took off our clothes. Each man kept his weapon with him, even when he went for water.
