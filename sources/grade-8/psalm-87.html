Zion, the City of God

1 A psalm of the sons of Korah. A song. Its foundation rests on the holy mountains. 2 The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the homes of Jacob. 3 Glorious things are said about you, city of God. Selah.

4 "I will name Rahab and Babylon to those who know Me. Look, Philistia and Tyre are there with Cush. This one was born there." 5 "And about Zion it will be said, 'This one and that one were born in her,' and the Most High Himself will establish her."

6 The LORD will count the nations in His book: "This one was born there." Selah.

7 Singers and dancers will say, "All my fountains are in you."
