The East Gate Is Shut

1He brought me back to the outer gate of the sanctuary, the one facing east, and it was shut. 2The LORD said to me, “This gate will stay shut. It will not be opened, and no one will enter through it, because the LORD, the God of Israel, came in through it. So it will stay shut.” 3Only the prince may sit there to eat bread before the LORD. He will enter through the porch of the gate and leave the same way.

The Glory of the LORD in the Temple

4He brought me to the front of the house through the north gate. I saw that the glory of the LORD filled the LORD’s house, and I fell facedown. 5The LORD said to me, “Son of man, pay attention. Look with your eyes and listen with your ears to everything I tell you about all the rules and laws of the LORD’s house. Pay close attention to who may enter the house and to all the ways out of the sanctuary.”

A Warning to Israel

6“Say to the stubborn house of Israel, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: You have gone too far with all your terrible sins, house of Israel. 7You let foreigners who were not circumcised in heart or body into My sanctuary, and they made My house unclean. You offered My food—fat and blood—and broke My covenant with all your terrible sins. 8You did not take care of My holy things. Instead, you put others in charge of My sanctuary for your own benefit.’ 9‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: No foreigner who is not circumcised in heart or body may enter My sanctuary—not even a foreigner living among the Israelites.’”

The Levites and the Priests

10But the Levites who wandered away from Me when Israel went astray after their idols will be punished for their sin. 11They can still serve in My sanctuary. They can guard the gates of the house and work in the house. They will slaughter the burnt offerings and sacrifices for the people and stand before them to serve them. 12Because they served the people in front of their idols and led Israel into sin, I swore an oath against them, and they will be punished for their sin, says the Lord the LORD. 13They must not come near Me to serve as priests or come near any of My holy things, especially the most holy things. They must be disgraced and punished for the terrible things they did. 14I will put them in charge of all the work in the house and everything that needs to be done there.

15But the priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept charge of My sanctuary when Israel went astray, will come near to serve Me. They will stand before Me to offer the fat and the blood, says the Lord the LORD. 16They will enter My sanctuary and come near My table to serve Me, and they will keep My rules. 17When they enter the inner courtyard, they must wear linen clothes. They must not wear wool while they serve at the gates of the inner courtyard or inside the house. 18They must wear linen turbans on their heads and linen undergarments around their waists. They must not wear anything that makes them sweat. 19When they go out to the outer courtyard to the people, they must take off the clothes they served in and leave them in the holy rooms. They must put on other clothes so they do not make the people holy with their clothes. 20They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long. They must keep their hair neatly trimmed. 21No priest may drink wine when he enters the inner courtyard. 22They must not marry a widow or a divorced woman. They may marry only a virgin from the family of Israel, or a widow of a priest.

23They must teach My people the difference between what is holy and what is common, and between what is clean and what is unclean. 24When there is a disagreement, they must stand and judge it by My laws. They must follow My instructions and My rules. They must keep My special days holy and respect My Sabbaths. 25A priest must not make himself unclean by going near a dead person, unless it is his father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or an unmarried sister. For these, he may become unclean. 26After he is made clean again, he must wait seven days. 27On the day he goes into the inner courtyard of the sanctuary to serve, he must offer his sin offering, says the Lord the LORD. 28The LORD will be their inheritance; I am their inheritance. You must not give them any land in Israel, because I am their share. 29They may eat the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. Everything set apart to the LORD in Israel will belong to them. 30The first of all firstfruits and every contribution from all your gifts will belong to the priests. You must also give the priest the first of your dough so a blessing can rest on your house. 31The priests must not eat any bird or animal that died on its own or was torn apart by wild animals.
