Don't Take Church Disputes to Unbelievers

1 If one of you has a disagreement with another believer, does he dare to bring it before people who do wrong instead of before God's holy people? 2 Don't you know that God's holy people will judge the world? And if the world is judged by you, aren't you good enough to handle small cases? 3 Don't you know that we will judge angels? How much more, then, the things of this life!

4 So if you have ordinary disputes, why do you set as judges people whom the church looks down on? 5 I say this to make you ashamed. Is there really not one wise person among you who can settle a disagreement between believers? 6 Instead, one brother takes another to court, and that before unbelievers.

7 The fact that you have lawsuits with one another already means you have lost. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated? 8 Instead, you cheat and wrong others, even your own brothers.

People Who Do Wrong Won't Inherit God's Kingdom

9 Don't you know that people who do wrong will not inherit God's kingdom? Don't be fooled. No sexually immoral person, idol worshiper, adulterer, or man who submits to or performs homosexual acts will inherit it. 10 Neither will thieves, greedy people, drunkards, people who insult others, or swindlers.

11 Some of you used to be like that. But you were washed clean, set apart for God, and made right with God in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by the Spirit of our God.

Your Body Belongs to the Lord

12 "Everything is allowed for me," but not everything is helpful. "Everything is allowed for me," but I will not be controlled by anything. 13 Food is for the stomach, and the stomach is for food, but God will destroy both. The body is not for sexual sin but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.

14 By His power, God raised the Lord and will raise us too. 15 Don't you know that your bodies are parts of Christ? Should I take parts of Christ and join them to a prostitute? Never!

16 Or don't you know that anyone who joins himself to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For it is written, "The two will become one flesh." 17 But anyone who joins himself to the Lord becomes one spirit with Him.

Run Away from Sexual Sin

18 Run away from sexual sin. Every other sin a person commits is outside the body, but the person who is sexually immoral sins against his own body. 19 Don't you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, who is in you and whom you received from God, so you are not your own. 20 For you were bought with a price, so honor God with your body.
