A Reminder to Stay Focused

1 Dear friends, this is the second letter I have written to you. In both of them I am stirring your honest minds by reminding you. 2 I want you to remember the words spoken long ago by the holy prophets and the command our Lord and Savior gave through His apostles.

Mockers in the Last Days

3 First, understand this: In the last days, mockers will come, following their own evil desires. 4 They will say, “Where is the promise that He will come again? Ever since our ancestors died, everything goes on just as it has since the beginning of creation.”

5 They choose to forget that long ago God spoke, and the heavens and earth were made. The earth was formed from water and through water. 6 By these waters the world of that time was destroyed in a flood. 7 But by the same word, the present heavens and earth are stored up for fire. They are being kept for the day of judgment, when ungodly people will be destroyed.

God's Patience and the Coming Day

8 But do not forget this one thing, dear friends: With the Lord, a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day. 9 The Lord is not slow to keep His promise, as some people think. Instead, He is patient with you. He does not want anyone to be destroyed, but He wants everyone to turn from sin and be saved.

10 But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On that day the heavens will disappear with a loud noise. The elements will be destroyed by fire, and the earth and everything done on it will be laid bare. 11 Since all these things will be destroyed, what kind of people should you be? You should live holy lives and honor God. 12 Look forward to the day of God and be eager for it to come. On that day the heavens will be destroyed by fire, and the elements will melt in the heat. 13 But we are looking forward to what He promised: new heavens and a new earth where everything is right.

Living Ready for His Return

14 So, dear friends, since you are looking forward to these things, make every effort to be found living at peace with Him, without spot or blame. 15 Think of our Lord's patience as an opportunity for people to be saved. Our dear brother Paul wrote to you about this too, using the wisdom God gave him.

16 He talks about these things in all his letters. Some of what he says is hard to understand. People who are uneducated and unstable twist his words, just as they do with the rest of Scripture. That leads to their own destruction.

17 So, dear friends, since you already know this, be on guard. Do not be led away by the errors of people who break God's law. Then you will not lose your firm footing. 18 Instead, grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be glory now and forever. Amen.
