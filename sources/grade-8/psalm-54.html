For the choir director. With stringed instruments. A teaching song of David. When the people of Ziph went to Saul and said, "Isn't David hiding among us?"

David Asks God for Help

1 Save me, O God, by your name; show I am right by your power. 2 Hear my prayer, O God; listen to the words of my mouth. 3 Strangers have risen against me, and cruel men are trying to kill me. They do not care about God.

David Trusts God's Help

4 Surely God is my helper; the Lord keeps me alive. 5 Let evil turn back on those who attack me; destroy them by your faithfulness.

David Promises to Praise God

6 I will freely sacrifice to you; I will praise your name, O the LORD, because it is good. 7 For He has rescued me from every trouble, and I have seen my enemies defeated.
