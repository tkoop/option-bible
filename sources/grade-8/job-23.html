Job Speaks Again

1 Then Job answered:

2 "Even today, my complaint is bitter, and my burden feels heavy despite my groans.

3 "If only I knew where to find Him, I would go to His throne. 4 I would plead my case before Him and fill my mouth with arguments. 5 I would know how He would answer me and understand what He would say.

6 "Would He use His great power against me? No—He would pay attention to me. 7 There an upright person could argue with Him, and I would be set free forever from my Judge.

Job Searches for God

8 "If I go east, He is not there; if I go west, I cannot see Him. 9 If He turns to the north, I cannot find Him; if He turns to the south, I do not see Him.

Job Trusts His Upright Life

10 "Yet He knows the path I have taken; when He has tested me, I will come out like gold. 11 My feet have followed His tracks, and I have kept His way without turning aside. 12 I have not turned away from the commands of His lips; I have treasured His words more than my daily bread.

Job Fears God's Power

13 "But He never changes, and who can oppose Him? He does what He wants. 14 He carries out His plan for me, and He has many plans like it. 15 So I am terrified in His presence; when I think about it, I am afraid of Him. 16 God has made my heart weak; the Almighty has terrified me. 17 Yet I am not silenced by the darkness, or by the thick darkness that covers my face."
