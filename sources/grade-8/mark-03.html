Jesus Heals a Man on the Sabbath

1 Jesus went into the synagogue again. A man was there whose hand was withered and useless. 2 Some people watched Jesus closely to see if He would heal the man on the Sabbath. They wanted a reason to accuse Him. 3 Jesus said to the man with the withered hand, "Stand up in front of everyone." 4 Then He asked them, "Is it lawful to do good on the Sabbath, or to do evil? Is it lawful to save a life, or to kill?" They said nothing. 5 Jesus looked around at them with anger. He was sad because their hearts were hard. He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." The man stretched it out, and his hand was healed. 6 The Pharisees left right away and began to plan with the Herodians how they could kill Jesus.

Great Crowds Follow Jesus

7 Jesus and His disciples went to the lake. A large crowd from Galilee and Judea followed Him. 8 People also came from Jerusalem, Idumea, the land across the Jordan, and the areas around Tyre and Sidon. They had heard what Jesus was doing, and they came in large numbers. 9 Jesus told His disciples to keep a small boat nearby for Him, so the crowd would not crush Him. 10 He had healed many people, so those who were sick pushed forward to touch Him. 11 Whenever unclean spirits saw Him, they fell down before Him and shouted, "You are the Son of God!" 12 But Jesus gave them strict orders not to tell who He was.

Jesus Chooses the Twelve Apostles

13 Jesus went up on a mountain and called the people He wanted. They came to Him. 14 He chose twelve and named them apostles. They were to stay with Him and go out and preach. 15 He also gave them authority to cast out demons. 16 These were the twelve: Simon, whom He named Peter; 17 James son of Zebedee and John, James's brother. Jesus named them Boanerges, which means "Sons of Thunder." 18 Then came Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, and Simon the Zealot. 19 Last was Judas Iscariot, who later betrayed Him.

Jesus Faces Opposition

20 Jesus went home, and such a large crowd gathered that He and His disciples could not even eat. 21 When His family heard about it, they came to take charge of Him, because they said, "He has lost His mind."

22 The teachers of the law who had come from Jerusalem said, "He is controlled by Beelzebul. He drives out demons by the power of the prince of demons."

23 Jesus called them together and spoke to them in parables. He said, "How can Satan drive out Satan? 24 If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand. 25 If a home is divided against itself, that home cannot stand. 26 If Satan is divided against himself, he cannot stand, but his end has come. 27 No one can enter a strong man's house and steal his things unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can rob the house. 28 Truly I tell you, people will be forgiven all their sins and every insult they speak against God. 29 But anyone who speaks against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. That person is guilty of a sin that lasts forever."

30 Jesus said this because they were saying, "He has an evil spirit."

31 His mother and brothers came and stood outside. They sent someone to call Him. 32 A crowd was sitting around Him. They said, "Look, Your mother and Your brothers are outside looking for You."

33 Jesus asked, "Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?" 34 He looked at those sitting around Him and said, "Look, here are My mother and My brothers." 35 He added, "Whoever does God's will is My brother and sister and mother."
