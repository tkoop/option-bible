Light in a Dark Land

1 But there won't be gloom forever for people who were in trouble. In the past, He humbled the lands of Zebulun and Naphtali. But later He will bring honor to the road by the sea, across the Jordan, and to Galilee, where the nations live. 2 The people walking in darkness have seen a great light. Light has shone on those living in the land of the shadow of death.

Joy and a Coming King

3 You have made the nation larger, but You have not made its joy greater. They rejoice before You like people rejoice at harvest, or like people celebrate when they divide the plunder. 4 For as in the day of Midian, You have broken the yoke that burdened them, the bar on their shoulders, and the rod of the one who oppressed them. 5 Every boot that tramples in battle and every coat rolled in blood will be burned as fuel for the fire.

6 For to us a child is born, to us a son is given, and the government will rest on His shoulders. And His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. 7 His government and peace will keep growing, and there will be no end. He will sit on David's throne and rule His kingdom. He will make it strong and support it with justice and right living from now on and forever. The strong desire of the the LORD of Hosts will do this.

Pride Brings Judgment

8 The Lord has sent a message against Jacob, and it has fallen on Israel. 9 All the people will know it—Ephraim and those who live in Samaria. In pride and with an arrogant heart they say: 10 “The bricks have fallen, but we will rebuild with cut stones. The sycamore trees have been cut down, but we will replace them with cedar trees.”

11 So the the LORD has raised up Rezin's enemies against him and stirred up his foes. 12 Arameans from the east and Philistines from the west have swallowed Israel up. Even after all this, His anger has not turned away; His hand is still raised.

The Lord's Anger Is Not Turned Away

13 But the people did not turn back to Him who struck them. They did not seek the the LORD of Hosts. 14 In one day the the LORD will cut off Israel's head and tail—both the leaders and the ordinary people. 15 The elder and respected man is the head, and the prophet who teaches lies is the tail. 16 The leaders of this people lead them the wrong way, and those who follow them are ruined.

17 So the Lord will not be happy with their young men, and He will not have mercy on their orphans and widows. Everyone is ungodly and does evil, and every mouth speaks foolish things. Even after all this, His anger has not turned away; His hand is still raised.

18 Wickedness burns like a fire. It eats up thorns and briars and sets the forest thickets on fire, so smoke rises in columns. 19 Because of the anger of the the LORD of Hosts, the land is burned, and the people become fuel for the fire. No one spares even his own brother.

20 They grab food on the right but are still hungry. They eat on the left but are not full. Each one even turns against his own people. 21 Manasseh fights Ephraim, and Ephraim fights Manasseh. Together they turn against Judah. Even after all this, His anger has not turned away; His hand is still raised.
