God Is Our Refuge and Strength

For the director of music. A song of the sons of Korah. To the tune of Alamoth.

1 God is our refuge and strength. He is always ready to help when we are in trouble. 2 So we will not be afraid, even if the earth changes and mountains crash into the sea. 3 Even if the water roars and foams and the mountains tremble as the seas rise. Selah.

4 A river with streams that bring joy to God's city, the holy place where the Most High lives. 5 God is in that city, so it will not be shaken. God will help it at dawn.

6 Nations are in uproar and kingdoms fall. He speaks, and the earth melts away. 7 The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.

8 Come and see what the LORD has done. See the destruction He has brought on the earth. 9 He stops wars all over the earth. He breaks bows and shatters spears. He burns chariots in the fire.

10 "Be still, and know that I am God. I will be honored among the nations. I will be honored all over the earth."

11 The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.
