David Prays for a Fair Judgment

1 Judge me, the LORD, for I have walked in my honesty. I have trusted in the LORD, and I will not slip. 2 Test me, the LORD, and try me; refine my heart and mind. 3 For Your faithful love is before my eyes, and I have walked in Your truth.

4 I have not sat with deceitful people, and I will not go with hypocrites. 5 I have hated the crowd of evildoers, and I will not sit with wicked people.

6 I will wash my hands in innocence and walk around Your altar, the LORD. 7 I do this to give thanks out loud and to tell about all Your wonders. 8 The LORD, I have loved the house where You live, the place where Your glory stays.

9 Do not gather my soul with sinners, or my life with men who shed blood, 10 whose hands are full of evil plans and whose right hands are full of bribes. 11 But I will walk in my honesty; redeem me and be gracious to me. 12 My foot stands on level ground; in the assemblies I will bless the LORD.
