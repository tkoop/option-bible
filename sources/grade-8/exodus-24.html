God Calls Moses and the Elders

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, “Come up to the LORD, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel. You are to worship at a distance, 2 but Moses alone may approach the LORD. The others must not come near, and the people may not come up with him.”

The People Agree to the Covenant

3 When Moses went and told the people all the LORD's words and laws, they answered with one voice, “All the words the LORD has spoken, we will do.” 4 Moses then wrote down everything the LORD had said. He got up early the next morning and built an altar at the foot of the mountain. He also set up twelve stone pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel.

The Covenant Is Sealed with Blood

5 Then he sent young Israelite men, and they offered burnt offerings and sacrificed young bulls as fellowship offerings to the LORD.

6 Moses took half of the blood and put it in bowls. The other half he sprinkled on the altar.

7 Then he took the Book of the Covenant and read it aloud to the people. They said, “We will do everything the LORD has said, and we will obey.”

8 Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people. He said, “This is the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you in keeping with all these words.”

9 Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and the seventy elders of Israel went up 10 and saw the God of Israel. Under His feet was something like a pavement made of sapphire, as clear as the sky itself. 11 But God did not raise His hand against the leaders of the Israelites. They saw God, and they ate and drank.

Moses Goes Up the Mountain

12 The LORD said to Moses, “Come up to Me on the mountain and stay there, and I will give you the stone tablets with the law and the commands I have written to teach them.”

13 Then Moses set out with his assistant, Joshua, and Moses went up the mountain of God. 14 He said to the elders, “Wait here for us until we return to you. Aaron and Hur are with you, and anyone who has a dispute can go to them.”

15 When Moses went up the mountain, the cloud covered it. 16 The glory of the LORD settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered the mountain for six days. On the seventh day the LORD called to Moses from inside the cloud. 17 To the Israelites, the glory of the LORD looked like a consuming fire on top of the mountain. 18 Moses entered the cloud as he went farther up the mountain, and he stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights.
