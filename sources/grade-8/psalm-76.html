God Is Known in Judah

1 For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song. God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel. 2 His tent is in Salem, His dwelling place in Zion. 3 There He broke the flaming arrows, the shield and sword and weapons of war. Selah

4 You are radiant with light, more majestic than mountains full of prey. 5 The valiant lie plundered; they sleep their last sleep; none of the warriors can lift a hand. 6 At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, both horse and chariot lie still.

7 You alone are to be feared. Who can stand before You when You are angry? 8 From heaven You pronounced judgment, and the earth feared and was still 9 when God rose up to judge, to save all the afflicted of the earth. Selah

10 Surely human anger brings You praise; You keep the rest of it under control. 11 Make and fulfill your vows to the LORD your God; let all who surround Him bring tribute to the Awesome One. 12 He breaks the spirit of princes; He is feared by the kings of the earth.
