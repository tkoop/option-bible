Instructions for the Promised Land

1 These are the rules and decisions you must follow. Follow them every day you live in the land the the LORD, the God of your fathers, gave you to own. 2 You must completely destroy every place where the nations you are driving out worship their gods. Destroy their places on high mountains, on hills, and under every green tree. 3 Tear down their altars. Smash their sacred stone pillars. Burn their Asherah poles. Cut down the carved images of their gods. Erase their names from those places. 4 You must not worship the the LORD your God in that way.

One Place for Worship

5 Instead, look for the place the the LORD your God will choose from among all your tribes. He will put His Name there as His home. Go to that place. 6 There you must bring your burnt offerings and sacrifices. Bring your tithes and special gifts. Bring the offerings you promised. Bring your voluntary offerings. Bring the firstborn of your herds and flocks. 7 There, in the presence of the the LORD your God, you and your families must eat and rejoice. Rejoice in everything you do, because the the LORD your God has blessed you.

8 You are not to do what we are doing here today. Here, each person does what seems right in his own eyes. 9 You have not yet reached the resting place. You have not yet reached the land the the LORD your God is giving you. 10 But when you cross the Jordan and live in the land the the LORD your God is giving you as your own, He will give you rest. He will give you rest from all your enemies around you. Then you will live in safety. 11 Then you must bring everything I command you to the place the the LORD your God will choose. He will make it a home for His Name. Bring your burnt offerings and sacrifices. Bring your tithes and contributions. Bring all the best vow offerings you promise to the the LORD. 12 You must rejoice before the the LORD your God. Rejoice—you, your sons and daughters, your male and female servants, and the Levite who lives in your towns. He has no share of land among you. 13 Be careful not to offer your burnt offerings just anywhere you see. 14 Instead, offer them only in the place the the LORD will choose in one of your tribes. There you must do everything I command you.

Eating Meat and Blood

15 But whenever you want, you may slaughter and eat meat in any of your towns. The LORD your God has blessed you. Both people who are ritually clean and those who are unclean may eat it. Eat it just like a gazelle or a deer. 16 But you must not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 17 In your towns you may not eat the tithe of your grain, new wine, or oil. You may not eat the firstborn of your herd or flock. You may not eat any offering you promised, your voluntary offerings, or your special gifts. 18 Instead, you must eat them in the presence of the the LORD your God. Eat them at the place the the LORD your God will choose. Eat with your sons and daughters, your male and female servants, and the Levite in your towns. Rejoice before the the LORD your God in everything you do. 19 Be careful not to ignore the Levite. Do this as long as you live in your land.

20 When the the LORD your God gives you more land, as He promised, you may crave meat. You may say, "I want to eat meat." Then you may eat meat whenever you want. 21 If the place the the LORD your God chooses for His Name is too far from you, you may slaughter an animal. Take it from the herd or flock the the LORD has given you. Do this as I have commanded you. Then eat it in your towns, as much as you want. 22 You may eat it the way you would eat a gazelle or a deer. Both clean and unclean people may eat it. 23 But be sure you do not eat the blood. The blood is the life. You must not eat the life with the meat. 24 You must not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 25 Do not eat it. Then things will go well for you and your children after you. You will be doing what is right in the eyes of the the LORD. 26 But you must take your holy things and your promised offerings. Go to the place the the LORD will choose. 27 Offer the meat and blood of your burnt offerings on the altar of the the LORD your God. Pour the blood of your other sacrifices beside the altar of the the LORD your God. But you may eat the meat. 28 Be careful to obey all these things I am commanding you. Then it will go well with you and your children after you forever. Do this when you do what is good and right in the eyes of the the LORD your God.

Warning Against Idolatry

29 When the the LORD your God removes the nations before you, you will drive them out. You will settle in their land. 30 Be careful not to be trapped by their ways after they have been destroyed before you. Do not ask about their gods. Do not say, "How do these nations serve their gods? I will do the same." 31 You must not do this to the the LORD your God. They do for their gods every disgusting thing the the LORD hates. They even burn their sons and daughters in the fire for their gods. 32 See that you do everything I command you. Do not add to it. Do not take anything away from it.
