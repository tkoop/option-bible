A New Song for the LORD

1 Sing a new song to the LORD, because He has done amazing things. His right hand and holy arm have won the victory for Him. 2 The LORD has made His saving power known. He has shown His justice to the nations. 3 He remembered His faithful love and His faithfulness to Israel. Everyone to the ends of the earth has seen the saving power of our God.

All the Earth Praises Him

4 Shout for joy to the LORD, all the earth. Burst into song, sing for joy, and make music. 5 Sing praises to the LORD with the harp, with music and a joyful song. 6 Play trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn. Shout for joy before the LORD, the King.

Creation Joins the Praise

7 Let the sea roar, and everything in it. Let the world and everyone who lives in it do the same. 8 Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the mountains sing together with joy. 9 Let them stand before the LORD, because He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with justice and the peoples with fairness.
