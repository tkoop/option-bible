Zophar Answers Job

1 Then Zophar the Naamathite answered:

2 "My thoughts push me to answer because I am deeply upset. 3 I hear a harsh correction that shames me, and my understanding makes me answer.

The Wicked Prosper Only a Short Time

4 "Do you know this from long ago, since people were placed on the earth? 5 It is that the success of wicked people is short, and the joy of those who ignore God lasts only a moment. 6 Though his pride reaches the heavens and his head touches the clouds, 7 he will be gone forever, like his own waste; people who saw him will ask, 'Where did he go?' 8 He will vanish like a dream and not be found; he will be chased away like a vision in the night.

9 "The eye that saw him will not see him again, and his home will not see him again. 10 His children will beg from poor people, and his own hands will have to give back his wealth. 11 His bones, once strong in youth, will lie with him in the dust.

Secret Sin Turns to Poison

12 "Though evil is sweet in his mouth and he hides it under his tongue, 13 though he enjoys it and won't let it go, but keeps it in his mouth, 14 yet his food will turn sour in his stomach; it will become cobra venom inside him. 15 He swallows riches but throws them up; God will force them out of his stomach. 16 He will suck cobra poison; a viper's fangs will kill him.

17 "He will not see streams, the rivers flowing with honey and cream. 18 He must give back his gains without enjoying them; he cannot enjoy the wealth he has earned. 19 For he crushed poor people and left them; he seized houses he did not build. 20 Because he is never satisfied inside, he cannot hold on to what he wants. 21 Nothing is left for him to eat, so his good times will not last.

God's Judgment on the Wicked

22 "When he has plenty, he will still be in trouble; all the force of misery will come on him. 23 When his stomach is full, God will pour out His fierce anger on him and rain blows on him while he eats. 24 If he runs from an iron weapon, a bronze arrow will pierce him. 25 The arrow is pulled out from his back, its shiny tip from his liver. Terror comes over him.

26 "Complete darkness is stored up for his treasures; a fire no one fans will burn him and eat what is left in his tent. 27 The sky will show his sin, and the earth will turn against him. 28 What he owns will be taken away, flowing off on the day of God's anger. 29 This is what God gives the wicked person; this is the share God sets for him."
