A Prophet Warns Jeroboam

1 Jeroboam was standing by the altar to burn incense. A man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the word of the the LORD. 2 He cried out against the altar by the word of the the LORD: “Altar, altar! This is what the the LORD says: ‘A son named Josiah will be born to David’s family. He will sacrifice on you the priests of the high places who burn incense on you, and human bones will be burned on you.’” 3 That day he gave a sign and said, “This is the sign the the LORD has spoken: The altar will split open, and the ashes on it will spill out.”

4 When the king heard what the man of God cried out against the altar at Bethel, he stretched out his hand from the altar and said, “Seize him!” But the hand he stretched out against the man dried up, and he could not pull it back. 5 The altar split open, and the ashes spilled out from the altar, just as the man of God had said would happen by the word of the the LORD.

6 The king said to the man of God, “Please ask the the LORD your God to show me favor and pray for me, so my hand will be restored.” The man of God prayed to the the LORD, and the king’s hand was restored and became as it was before.

7 The king said to the man of God, “Come home with me, eat, and I will give you a gift.” 8 But the man of God said to the king, “Even if you gave me half your house, I would not go with you. I will not eat bread or drink water in this place. 9 The LORD commanded me by His word: ‘Do not eat bread, do not drink water, and do not go back the way you came.’” 10 So he left by another road and did not return by the road he had taken to Bethel.

The Old Prophet Deceives the Man of God

11 An old prophet lived in Bethel. His sons came and told him everything the man of God had done that day in Bethel. They also told him the words he had spoken to the king. 12 Their father asked them, “Which way did he go?” His sons showed him the road the man of God from Judah had taken.

13 He said to his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” They saddled it, and he rode on it. 14 He went after the man of God and found him sitting under an oak tree. He asked, “Are you the man of God who came from Judah?” He answered, “I am.”

15 The old prophet said to him, “Come home with me and eat bread.”

16 The man of God said, “I cannot go back with you or come with you. I will not eat bread or drink water with you in this place. 17 The LORD told me by His word, ‘Do not eat bread, do not drink water, and do not return by the road you took to get here.’”

18 The old prophet said, “I am also a prophet like you. An angel spoke to me by the word of the the LORD and said, ‘Bring him back with you to your house so he can eat bread and drink water.’” But he was lying to him.

19 So he went back with the old prophet and ate bread in his house and drank water. 20 While they were sitting at the table, the word of the the LORD came to the prophet who had brought him back.

21 He cried out to the man of God who had come from Judah, “This is what the the LORD says: ‘Because you disobeyed the the LORD’s word and did not keep the command the the LORD your God gave you, 22 you came back and ate bread and drank water in the place where He told you, “Do not eat bread and do not drink water,” your body will not be buried in your fathers’ tomb.’”

The Man of God Dies

23 After he had eaten bread and drunk water, the old prophet saddled the donkey for the prophet he had brought back. 24 The man left, and a lion met him on the road and killed him. His body was thrown in the road. The donkey stood beside it, and the lion stood beside the body.

25 Some men passed by and saw the body thrown in the road, with the lion standing beside it. They went and told what they had seen in the city where the old prophet lived. 26 When the prophet who had brought him back heard this, he said, “He is the man of God who disobeyed the the LORD’s word. That is why the the LORD gave him to the lion, which tore him and killed him, just as the the LORD had said.”

27 He said to his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” They saddled it. 28 He went and found the body thrown in the road. The donkey and the lion were standing beside the body. The lion had not eaten the body or torn the donkey.

29 The prophet picked up the body of the man of God, laid it on the donkey, and brought it back. The old prophet came into the city to mourn and bury him. 30 He laid the body in his own grave and mourned over him, saying, “Oh, my brother!”

31 After he buried him, he said to his sons, “When I die, bury me in the grave where the man of God is buried. Lay my bones beside his bones. 32 The word he cried out by the the LORD’s word against the altar in Bethel and against all the shrines in the cities of Samaria will surely come true.”

Jeroboam's Sin Continues

33 Even after this, Jeroboam did not turn from his evil way. He again made priests for the high places from all kinds of people. Anyone who wanted to serve, he made a priest for the high places. 34 This became the sin of Jeroboam’s family. It led to their ruin and destruction from the face of the earth.
