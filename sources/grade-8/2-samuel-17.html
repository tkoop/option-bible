Ahithophel's Plan to Catch David

1 Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me choose twelve thousand men. I will get up tonight and chase David. 2 I will attack him while he is tired and weak. I will terrify all the people with him so they run away, and then I will strike down only the king. 3 I will bring all the people back to you. The man you are after is the only one we need to kill. After that, everyone else can come home in peace." 4 This plan sounded good to Absalom and to all the elders of Israel.

5 But Absalom said, "Call Hushai the Arkite too. Let us hear what he has to say." 6 When Hushai came to Absalom, Absalom said to him, "Ahithophel has given this advice. Should we follow his plan? If not, you speak."

Hushai's Better Advice

7 Hushai said to Absalom, "The advice Ahithophel gave this time is not good. 8 You know your father and his men. They are brave and fierce. They are like a bear in the field whose cubs have been taken. Your father is a seasoned warrior, and he will not stay with the troops at night. 9 Right now he is probably hiding in a cave or some other place. If a few of your men fall at the start, people will hear and say, 'Absalom's followers have been defeated.' 10 Then even the bravest men, with hearts like a lion's, will lose their nerve, because all Israel knows your father is a mighty warrior and the men with him are brave. 11 So I advise that all Israel be gathered to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as many as the sand by the sea. Then you yourself should go into battle. 12 We will come upon him wherever he is. We will fall on him like dew on the ground. Not one man will be left, not one of all the men with him. 13 If he retreats into a city, all Israel will bring ropes to that city. We will drag it into the valley until not even a small stone is left."

14 Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The advice of Hushai the Arkite is better than the advice of Ahithophel." For the LORD had decided to ruin Ahithophel's good advice, so that He could bring disaster on Absalom.

Hushai Warns David

15 Then Hushai told Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "Ahithophel advised Absalom and the elders of Israel like this, but I advised them like this." 16 "Now send quickly and tell David, 'Do not spend the night in the desert plains, but cross over at once, or the king and all his people may be wiped out.'"

17 Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at En Rogel. A female servant went and told them. They were to go and report to King David, since they could not be seen going into the city. 18 But a young man saw them and told Absalom. So the two hurried away and came to the house of a man in Bahurim. He had a well in his courtyard, and they climbed down into it.

19 The man's wife took a covering and spread it over the well's opening. Then she scattered grain over it, so no one would know. 20 When Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house, they asked, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?"

The woman said, "They crossed the brook." The men searched but did not find them, so they returned to Jerusalem.

21 After they left, the two men came up out of the well and went to tell King David. They said to David, "Get up and cross the water quickly, because Ahithophel has advised this against you."

22 So David and all the people with him got up and crossed the Jordan. By dawn everyone had crossed; not one was left behind.

Ahithophel's Death and David's Escape

23 When Ahithophel saw that his advice was not followed, he saddled his donkey and went home to his town. He put his house in order and hanged himself. He died and was buried in his father's tomb.

24 David went to Mahanaim. Absalom crossed the Jordan with all the men of Israel.

25 Absalom had made Amasa commander of the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of Ithra the Israelite. Ithra had married Abigail, daughter of Nahash and sister of Zeruiah, Joab's mother.

26 Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.

27 When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo Debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim 28 brought beds, basins, pottery, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils, honey, curds, sheep, and cheese. 29 They brought these for David and his people to eat, because they said, "The people are hungry, tired, and thirsty in the desert."
