Paul Writes to the Churches in Galatia

1 Paul, an apostle—not from people or through a person, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him from the dead— 2 and all the brothers who are with me, to the churches in Galatia: 3 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, 4 who gave Himself for our sins to save us from this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father, 5 to Him be glory forever and ever. Amen.

A Warning About a Different Gospel

6 I'm amazed that you are turning away so quickly from the One who called you by the grace of Christ and are following a different gospel. 7 It is not that there is another gospel, but some people are troubling you and want to twist the gospel of Christ. 8 But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach to you a gospel different from the one we preached to you, let that person be cursed. 9 As we said before, I now say again: If anyone preaches to you a gospel different from the one you received, let that person be cursed.

10 Am I now trying to win the approval of people or of God? Am I trying to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I would not be a servant of Christ.

Paul's Calling and Early Travels

11 For I want you to know, brothers, that the gospel I preached is not a human message. 12 I did not receive it from a person, and I was not taught it. I received it through a revelation from Jesus Christ. 13 For you have heard about my former life in Judaism—how I persecuted the church of God terribly and tried to destroy it. 14 I was moving ahead in Judaism faster than many others my age among my people, and I was extremely devoted to the traditions of my ancestors.

15 But when God, who set me apart before I was born and called me by His grace, was pleased 16 to reveal His Son in me so that I might preach Him among the nations, I did not immediately ask advice from any person. 17 I did not go up to Jerusalem to see those who were apostles before me. Instead, I went to Arabia, and later I returned to Damascus.

18 Then, after three years, I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas, and I stayed with him for fifteen days. 19 I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother. 20 Before God, I'm not lying in what I'm writing to you.

21 Then I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia. 22 The churches in Judea that belong to Christ did not know me in person. 23 They had only heard that the man who once persecuted us was now preaching the faith he had tried to destroy. 24 And they praised God because of me.
