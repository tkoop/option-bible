Jehu Challenges Samaria’s Leaders

1 Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the rulers of Jezreel, the elders, and the guardians of Ahab’s children. The letters said, 2 “When this letter reaches you, you have your master’s sons with you. You also have chariots, horses, a fortified city, and weapons. 3 So choose the best and most honest of your master’s sons. Put him on his father’s throne. Then fight to defend your master’s family.”

4 When the leaders read the letter, they were very afraid. They said, “Two kings could not stand against Jehu. How can we stand?”

5 So the palace manager, the city governor, the elders, and the guardians sent this message to Jehu: “We are your servants. We will do whatever you tell us. We will not make anyone king. Do what you think is best.”

Jehu Demands the Heads of Ahab’s Sons

6 Jehu wrote a second letter and said, “If you are on my side and will obey me, take the heads of your master’s sons and bring them to me in Jezreel by this time tomorrow.” The king had seventy sons. The leaders of the city were raising them.

7 When the letter came, they took the king’s sons and killed all seventy. They put their heads in baskets and sent them to Jehu in Jezreel.

8 A messenger came and told Jehu, “They have brought the heads of the king’s sons.”

Jehu said, “Put them in two piles at the city gate until morning.”

9 In the morning, Jehu went out and stood before all the people. He said, “You are innocent. I am the one who plotted against my master and killed him. But who killed all these? 10 Know this: Not one word that the LORD spoke against Ahab’s family will fail. The LORD has done what He said through His servant Elijah.”

11 Then Jehu killed everyone left from Ahab’s family in Jezreel. He killed all the important men, close friends, and priests. He left no survivor.

12 After that, Jehu set out for Samaria. On the way, he stopped at Beth-eked of the Shepherds.

13 There he met the relatives of Ahaziah, king of Judah. Jehu asked, “Who are you?”

They answered, “We are relatives of Ahaziah. We are going down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother.”

14 Jehu said, “Take them alive!” His men captured them alive and killed them at the pit of Beth-eked. There were forty-two men, and he did not leave one alive.

15 Jehu left there and found Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. Jehu blessed him and asked, “Is your heart right with mine, as my heart is with yours?”

Jehonadab answered, “It is.”

Jehu said, “If it is, give me your hand.” Jehonadab gave him his hand, and Jehu pulled him up into the chariot.

16 Jehu said, “Come with me and see my strong devotion to the LORD.” So they rode together in his chariot.

17 When Jehu reached Samaria, he killed everyone left from Ahab’s family there. He destroyed them all, just as the LORD had said through Elijah.

Jehu Destroys the Worship of Baal

18 Then Jehu gathered all the people and said to them, “Ahab worshiped Baal a little. Jehu will worship him much more.”

19 Jehu said, “Now call together all the prophets of Baal, all his worshipers, and all his priests. Do not let anyone miss the meeting, because I have a great sacrifice for Baal. Anyone who does not come will die.” Jehu said this to trick them so he could destroy the worshipers of Baal.

20 Jehu said, “Set apart a holy meeting for Baal.” So they announced it.

21 Jehu sent word through all Israel. Every worshiper of Baal came. Not one stayed away. They went into the temple of Baal until it was full from one end to the other.

22 Jehu told the man in charge of the robes, “Bring out robes for all the worshipers of Baal.” So he brought out the robes.

23 Then Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab went into the temple of Baal. Jehu said to the worshipers, “Look carefully and make sure no servants of the LORD are here with you. Only worshipers of Baal should be here.”

24 Then they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Jehu had placed eighty men outside and said, “If any man I hand over to you gets away, you will pay with your own life.”

25 As soon as Jehu finished offering the burnt offering, he said to the guards and officers, “Go in and kill them. Do not let anyone escape.” The guards and officers killed them with their swords. They threw the bodies outside. Then they went into the inner room of the temple.

26 They brought out the sacred stone pillars from the temple of Baal and burned them.

27 They tore down the pillar of Baal and tore down the temple of Baal. They made it a toilet, and it is still used that way today.

28 So Jehu wiped out the worship of Baal from Israel.

Jehu’s Later Rule and Israel’s Losses

29 But Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. He kept the golden calves at Bethel and Dan.

30 The LORD said to Jehu, “You have done well. You did what is right in My eyes, and you did to Ahab’s family everything I wanted. So your sons, down to the fourth generation, will sit on the throne of Israel.”

31 But Jehu was not careful to follow the law of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, who had led Israel to sin.

32 In those days, the LORD began to reduce Israel’s territory. Hazael defeated Israel throughout its borders. 33 He took all the land east of the Jordan: the whole land of Gilead, and the regions of Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh. He took it from Aroer by the Arnon Gorge all the way through Gilead and Bashan.

34 The rest of the acts of Jehu, everything he did, and all his power are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

35 Jehu died and was buried with his ancestors in Samaria. His son Jehoahaz became king in his place. 36 Jehu ruled over Israel in Samaria for twenty-eight years.
