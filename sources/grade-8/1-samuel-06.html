The Ark Among the Philistines

1 The ark of the LORD stayed in the land of the Philistines for seven months.

2 The Philistines called their priests and fortune-tellers and asked, "What should we do with the ark of the LORD? Tell us how to send it back to its place."

3 They answered, "If you send back the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty. You must return a guilt offering with it. Then you will be healed, and you will know why His hand has not left you."

4 "What guilt offering should we send to Him?" they asked.

"Five gold tumors and five gold mice," they answered. "Send one for each Philistine ruler, because the same plague came on all of you and your rulers. 5 Make gold models of your tumors and of your mice that are ruining the land. Give honor to the God of Israel. Maybe He will stop being so hard on you, your gods, and your land."

6 "Why are you hardening your hearts, like Pharaoh and the Egyptians did? When the LORD punished them, didn't they send the people away?"

The Philistines Send the Ark Home

7 "Now get a new cart and two cows that have never been yoked. Hitch the cows to the cart, and take their calves away from them and keep them at home."

8 "Put the ark of the LORD on the cart. Put the gold objects you are sending back as a guilt offering in a box beside it. Then send it on its way."

9 "Watch what happens. If it goes up the road to Beth Shemesh, then the LORD has brought this terrible trouble on us. But if it does not, then we will know His hand did not touch us, and it just happened by chance."

10 The men did just that. They took two milk cows, hitched them to the cart, and kept the calves at home.

11 They put the ark of the LORD on the cart, along with the box containing the gold mice and the models of their tumors.

12 The cows went straight down the road toward Beth Shemesh, staying on the main road. They mooed as they walked and did not turn right or left. The Philistine rulers followed them to the border of Beth Shemesh.

The Ark Returns to Beth Shemesh

13 The people of Beth Shemesh were harvesting wheat in the valley. They looked up and saw the ark, and they were glad to see it.

14 The cart came to the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh and stopped by a large stone. The people split the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD.

15 The Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the box with the gold objects, and they placed them on the large stone. That day the men of Beth Shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrificed to the LORD.

16 The five Philistine rulers saw all this and went back to Ekron that same day.

17 These are the gold tumors the Philistines sent back as a guilt offering to the LORD: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, and one for Ekron.

18 The gold mice matched the number of Philistine cities belonging to the five rulers, from fortified cities to small villages. The large stone of Abel, where they set the ark of the LORD, still stands in the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh.

Judgment at Beth Shemesh

19 But the LORD struck some of the men of Beth Shemesh because they looked into the ark of the LORD. He struck down seventy men, and the people mourned because the LORD had struck them with a terrible plague.

20 The men of Beth Shemesh said, "Who can stand before this Holy God? Who will take the ark away from us?"

21 So they sent messengers to the people of Kiriath Jearim and said, "The Philistines have returned the ark of the LORD. Come down and take it up to you."
