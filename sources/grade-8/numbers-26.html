A New Census After the Plague

1 After the plague, the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, 2 "Take a census of the whole Israelite community by family groups. Count every man twenty years old or older who can serve in Israel's army." 3 So on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River, across from Jericho, Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke to the people and said, 4 "Count the men twenty years old or older, just as the LORD commanded Moses and the Israelites who came out of Egypt."

The Clans of Israel

5 Reuben was Israel's firstborn. His sons and clans were Hanoch (clan of Hanoch) and Pallu (clan of Pallu). 6 Hezron (clan of Hezron) and Carmi (clan of Carmi) were also included. 7 These Reubenite clans totaled 43,730. 8 Pallu's son was Eliab. 9 Eliab's sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. These Dathan and Abiram were leaders chosen by the community. They rebelled against Moses and Aaron with Korah's group when they rebelled against the LORD. 10 The ground opened and swallowed them and Korah. Fire killed the 250 men, and this became a warning. 11 But Korah's sons did not die. 12 Simeon's sons and clans were Nemuel (clan of Nemuel), Jamin (clan of Jamin), and Jachin (clan of Jachin). 13 Zerah (clan of Zerah) and Shaul (clan of Shaul) were also included. 14 These Simeonite clans totaled 22,200. 15 Gad's sons and clans were Zephon (clan of Zephon), Haggi (clan of Haggi), and Shuni (clan of Shuni). 16 Ozni (clan of Ozni) and Eri (clan of Eri) were also included. 17 Arod (clan of Arod) and Areli (clan of Areli) were also included. 18 These Gadite clans totaled 40,500. 19 Judah's sons Er and Onan died in Canaan. 20 Judah's sons and clans were Shelah (clan of Shelah), Perez (clan of Perez), and Zerah (clan of Zerah). 21 Perez's sons were Hezron (clan of Hezron) and Hamul (clan of Hamul). 22 These Judahite clans totaled 76,500. 23 Issachar's sons and clans were Tola (clan of Tola) and Puah (clan of Puah). 24 Jashub (clan of Jashub) and Shimron (clan of Shimron) were also included. 25 These Issacharite clans totaled 64,300. 26 Zebulun's sons and clans were Sered (clan of Sered), Elon (clan of Elon), and Jahleel (clan of Jahleel). 27 These Zebulunite clans totaled 60,500. 28 Joseph's sons by families were Manasseh and Ephraim. 29 Manasseh's sons were Machir (clan of Machir). Machir was the father of Gilead (clan of Gilead). 30 Gilead's sons were Iezer (clan of Iezer) and Helek (clan of Helek). 31 Asriel (clan of Asriel) and Shechem (clan of Shechem) were also included. 32 Shemida (clan of Shemida) and Hepher (clan of Hepher) were also included. 33 Hepher's son Zelophehad had no sons, only daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 34 These Manassehite clans totaled 52,700. 35 Ephraim's sons and clans were Shuthelah (clan of Shuthelah), Becher (clan of Becher), and Tahan (clan of Tahan). 36 Shuthelah's son Eran (clan of Eran) was also included. 37 These Ephraimite clans totaled 32,500. These were Joseph's sons by families. 38 Benjamin's sons and clans were Bela (clan of Bela), Ashbel (clan of Ashbel), and Ahiram (clan of Ahiram). 39 Shupham (clan of Shupham) and Hupham (clan of Hupham) were also included. 40 Bela's sons were Ard (clan of Ard) and Naaman (clan of Naaman). 41 These Benjaminite clans totaled 45,600. 42 Dan's sons and clans were Shuham (clan of Shuham). These were the Danite clans by their families. 43 All Shuhamite clans totaled 64,400. 44 Asher's sons and clans were Imnah (clan of Imnah), Ishvi (clan of Ishvi), and Beriah (clan of Beriah). 45 Beriah's sons were Heber (clan of Heber) and Malchiel (clan of Malchiel). 46 Asher's daughter was Serah. 47 These Asherite clans totaled 53,400. 48 Naphtali's sons and clans were Jahzeel (clan of Jahzeel) and Guni (clan of Guni). 49 Jezer (clan of Jezer) and Shillem (clan of Shillem) were also included. 50 These Naphtali clans totaled 45,400. 51 The total number of Israelites counted was 601,730.

Dividing the Promised Land

52 The LORD spoke to Moses: 53 "Divide the land among these people as an inheritance, based on the number of names. 54 Give more land to a larger group and less to a smaller one. Each group's land should match its number of counted men. 55 But divide the land by lot among the tribes of their fathers' families. 56 Divide the inheritance between the larger and smaller groups by lot."

The Levites and the End of the First Generation

57 These were the counted Levites by families: Gershon (clan of Gershon), Kohath (clan of Kohath), and Merari (clan of Merari). 58 These were Levi's families: Libni (clan of Libni), Hebron (clan of Hebron), Mahli (clan of Mahli), Mushi (clan of Mushi), and Korah (clan of Korah). Kohath was the father of Amram. 59 Amram's wife was Jochebed, a daughter of Levi born to Levi in Egypt. She bore Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam to Amram. 60 Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 61 Nadab and Abihu died when they brought unauthorized fire before the LORD. 62 The Levites counted were 23,000 males one month old or older. They were not counted among the Israelites because they received no inheritance among them. 63 These were the men Moses and Eleazar the priest counted among the Israelites on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River across from Jericho. 64 Among this group, none were from those Moses and Aaron had counted in the Sinai wilderness. 65 For the LORD had said they would die in the wilderness. None survived except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
