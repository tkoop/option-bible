Paul in Ephesus

1 While Apollos was in Corinth, Paul traveled through the upper regions and came to Ephesus. He found some disciples there. 2 He asked them, "Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?"

They answered, "No, we haven't even heard that there is a Holy Spirit."

3 Paul asked, "Then what baptism did you have?"

They said, "John's baptism."

4 Paul said, "John baptized with a baptism of repentance. He told the people to believe in the One coming after him. That One is Jesus."

5 When they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. 6 Paul laid his hands on them, and the Holy Spirit came on them. They spoke in tongues and prophesied. 7 There were about twelve men in all.

Paul Teaches in Ephesus

8 Paul went into the synagogue and spoke boldly for three months. He argued and persuaded people about the kingdom of God. 9 But some became stubborn and refused to believe. In front of the crowd, they spoke against the Way. So Paul left them and took the disciples with him. He talked every day in the lecture hall of Tyrannus. 10 This went on for two years, so all the Jews and Greeks who lived in Asia heard the word of the Lord.

11 God did extraordinary miracles through Paul's hands. 12 People even carried handkerchiefs and aprons that had touched his skin to the sick. Their diseases left them, and evil spirits went out of them.

The Sons of Sceva

13 Some Jewish exorcists who traveled around tried to use the name of the Lord Jesus over people who had evil spirits. They said, "I command you by Jesus, whom Paul preaches."

14 Seven sons of a Jewish chief priest named Sceva were doing this.

15 But the evil spirit answered them, "I know Jesus, and I know about Paul. But who are you?"

16 Then the man with the evil spirit jumped on them and overpowered them both. He beat them so badly that they ran out of the house naked and wounded.

17 Everyone in Ephesus, both Jews and Greeks, heard about this. Fear came over all of them, and the name of the Lord Jesus was held in high honor. 18 Many believers came and confessed their sins. They also told about the things they had done. 19 Many who had practiced magic brought their books and burned them in front of everyone. They added up the value of the books and found it was fifty thousand silver coins. 20 In this way the word of the Lord spread and grew strong.

Paul's Travel Plans

21 After these things were finished, Paul decided in the Spirit to go to Jerusalem. He planned to travel through Macedonia and Achaia. He said, "After I have been there, I must also see Rome."

22 He sent two of his helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia. Paul stayed in Asia for a while.

The Riot in Ephesus

23 About that time there was a serious disturbance about the Way. 24 A silversmith named Demetrius made silver shrines of Artemis. This gave the craftsmen a lot of work.

25 He called them together, along with the workers in related trades. He said, "Men, you know that we make our living from this business. 26 You can see and hear that this Paul has persuaded many people, not only in Ephesus but almost all of Asia. He says that gods made by human hands are not really gods. 27 This is dangerous for us. Our trade could be ruined. Also, the temple of the great goddess Artemis could be thought worthless, and her greatness could be destroyed. All of Asia and the whole world worship her."

28 When they heard this, they were furious. They shouted, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!" 29 The whole city was filled with confusion. The crowd rushed together into the theater. They dragged along Gaius and Aristarchus, Paul's travel companions from Macedonia.

30 Paul wanted to go in among the crowd, but the disciples would not let him. 31 Even some of the Asiarchs, who were his friends, sent a message urging him not to risk going into the theater.

32 The assembly was in confusion. Some people shouted one thing, and others shouted another. Most of them did not know why they had come together. 33 The Jews pushed Alexander forward. He motioned with his hand and wanted to make a defense to the crowd. 34 But when they found out he was a Jew, they all shouted the same thing for about two hours: "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!"

35 The city clerk quieted the crowd. He said, "Men of Ephesus, who doesn't know that the city of the Ephesians is the temple keeper of the great Artemis and of the image that fell from heaven? 36 Since these things can't be denied, you should be calm and not do anything rash. 37 You have brought these men here, but they are not temple robbers, and they have not spoken against our goddess. 38 So if Demetrius and the craftsmen with him have a complaint against anyone, the courts are open, and there are proconsuls. Let them bring charges against each other. 39 If you want anything more, it must be settled in the lawful assembly. 40 We are in danger of being charged with rioting today. There is no reason for this commotion, and we won't be able to explain it."

41 After he said this, he dismissed the assembly.
