The Altar of Incense

1 Make an altar for burning incense out of acacia wood. 2 It must be square—one cubit long, one cubit wide, and two cubits high—and its horns must be made as one piece with it. 3 Cover it with pure gold: the top, all around the sides, and the horns. Make a gold rim around it. 4 Make two gold rings for it below the rim on two opposite sides to hold the poles used to carry it. 5 Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.

6 Put the altar in front of the curtain that is before the ark of the covenant, in front of the mercy seat over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

7 Aaron must burn fragrant incense on it every morning when he tends the lamps. 8 He must also burn incense at twilight when he lights the lamps, so that incense burns regularly before the LORD for all your generations. 9 Do not burn any other incense on it, or any burnt offering or grain offering, and do not pour out a drink offering on it. 10 Once a year Aaron must make atonement on its horns with the blood of the sin offering. He will make atonement for it once a year for all your generations. It is most holy to the LORD.

The Census Offering and Wash Basin

11 The LORD said to Moses, 12 "When you take a census of the Israelites to count them, each man must give a ransom for his life to the LORD when he is counted, so that no plague will strike them.

13 "Everyone who is counted must give half a shekel by the sanctuary shekel—twenty gerahs to the shekel. This half shekel is an offering to the LORD. 14 Everyone twenty years old or older who is counted must give this offering to the LORD.

15 "The rich must not give more, and the poor must not give less, than this half shekel when you make the offering to the LORD to atone for your lives. 16 Take this atonement money from the Israelites and use it for the work of the tent of meeting. It will be a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD, to atone for your lives."

17 The LORD said to Moses, 18 "Make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. Put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it.

19 "Aaron and his sons are to wash their hands and feet with water from it. 20 Whenever they enter the tent of meeting or approach the altar to serve and burn an offering to the LORD, they must wash with water so they will not die. 21 They must wash their hands and feet so they will not die. This is to be a lasting rule for Aaron and his descendants for all generations."

The Holy Anointing Oil

22 The LORD said to Moses, 23 "Take the finest spices: five hundred shekels of liquid myrrh, two hundred fifty shekels of fragrant cinnamon, two hundred fifty shekels of fragrant cane, five hundred shekels of cassia—all measured by the sanctuary shekel—and a hin of olive oil.

25 "Make from these a holy anointing oil, a perfume blended by a perfumer. It will be a holy anointing oil. 26 Use it to anoint the tent of meeting, the ark of the covenant, 27 the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, and the incense altar. 28 Anoint the burnt offering altar and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand. 29 You must set them apart so they will be most holy; whatever touches them will become holy. 30 Anoint Aaron and his sons and set them apart to serve Me as priests.

31 "Say to the Israelites, 'This is to be My holy anointing oil for all your generations. 32 Do not pour it on the body of an ordinary man, and do not make any other oil like it with the same formula. It is holy, and it must be holy to you. 33 Anyone who mixes a similar perfume or puts some of it on an outsider must be cut off from his people.'"

The Sacred Incense

34 The LORD said to Moses, "Take spices: gum resin, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense, with each spice in equal amount. 35 Make incense from them, a perfume blended by a perfumer. It must be seasoned with salt, pure, and holy.

36 "Grind some of it into a very fine powder and put it in front of the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. It is to be most holy to you. 37 Do not make any incense like it for yourselves using the same formula. It is holy to the LORD. 38 Anyone who makes something like it to enjoy its fragrance must be cut off from his people."
