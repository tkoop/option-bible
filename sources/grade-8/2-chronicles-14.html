Asa's Good Reign

1 Abijah died and was buried in the City of David. His son Asa became king after him. In his days the land had peace for ten years. 2 Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God.

3 He took away the foreign altars and high places. He broke the sacred stones and cut down the Asherah poles. 4 He told the people of Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to obey the law and His commands. 5 He removed the high places and incense altars from all the cities of Judah. The kingdom was at peace under him.

Asa Prepares for War

6 Because the land was at peace, Asa built fortified cities in Judah. There was no war in those years, because the LORD gave him rest. 7 He said to Judah, “Let us build up these cities and surround them with walls, towers, gates, and bars. The land is still ours, because we have sought the LORD our God. We have sought Him, and He has given us rest on every side.” So they built and did well. 8 Asa had an army of 300,000 men from Judah. They carried shields and spears. From Benjamin there were 280,000 men who carried shields and bows. All these men were brave soldiers.

Zerah Invades Judah

9 Zerah the Cushite marched against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots. He came as far as Mareshah. 10 Asa went out to meet him, and they set up for battle in the Valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.

Asa Prays and Wins the Battle

11 Asa cried out to the LORD his God, “the LORD, no one but You can help, whether an army is large or weak. Help us, the LORD our God, because we trust in You. We have come against this huge army in Your name. the LORD, You are our God. Do not let people win against You.”

12 So the LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and the Cushites ran away. 13 Asa and his men chased them all the way to Gerar. The Cushites fell until no one was left alive, because they were crushed before the LORD and His army. Asa's men carried off a great deal of plunder.

14 They attacked all the cities around Gerar, because the fear of the LORD had fallen on them. They plundered all the cities, because there was much wealth in them. 15 They also attacked the tents where livestock were kept. They took many sheep and camels and went back to Jerusalem.
