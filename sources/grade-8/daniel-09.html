Daniel Learns About the Seventy Years

1 In the first year of King Darius, son of Ahasuerus, who was from the Medes and had been made king over the Babylonian kingdom, 2 I, Daniel, was reading the Scriptures. I understood the number of years that the LORD had spoken about through Jeremiah the prophet: Jerusalem would lie in ruins for seventy years.

Daniel's Prayer of Confession

3 So I turned to the Lord God and sought Him in prayer and in requests for mercy. I fasted and showed deep sorrow for my sins.

4 I prayed to the LORD my God and confessed my sins. I said, "Lord, You are great and awesome. You keep Your covenant and show faithful love to everyone who loves You and obeys Your commands.

5 "We have sinned and done wrong. We have acted wickedly and rebelled against You. We have turned away from Your commands and laws.

6 "We did not listen to Your servants the prophets. They spoke in Your name to our kings, our leaders, our ancestors, and all the people of the land.

7 "Lord, You are right and good, but we are filled with shame. This is true for the people of Judah, the people of Jerusalem, and all Israel—both those nearby and those far away in every country where You sent us because we broke faith with You.

8 "The LORD, we are covered with shame—our kings, our leaders, and our ancestors—because we sinned against You.

9 "The Lord our God is full of mercy and forgiveness, even though we have rebelled against Him.

10 "We have not obeyed the LORD our God. We have not followed the laws He gave us through His servants the prophets.

11 "All Israel has broken Your law and turned away from You. We refused to obey You. So the curse and the oath written in the Law of Moses, God's servant, have come down on us, because we sinned against You.

12 "You kept Your word against us and our rulers. You brought a terrible disaster on us. Nothing like it has ever happened anywhere under heaven, not even to Jerusalem.

13 "Just as it is written in the Law of Moses, all this disaster came on us. But we did not ask the LORD our God for help. We did not turn from our sins or learn Your truth.

14 "So the LORD kept this disaster ready and brought it on us. The LORD our God is right in everything He does. But we did not obey Him.

15 "Now, Lord our God, You brought Your people out of Egypt with great power and made Your name famous, even to this day. But we have sinned and done wickedly.

16 "Lord, because You always do what is right, please turn Your anger and wrath away from Jerusalem, Your holy mountain. Our sins and the sins of our ancestors have made Jerusalem and Your people an object of shame to everyone around us.

17 "So now, our God, listen to the prayer and request of Your servant. For Your sake, Lord, make Your face shine on Your holy place, which is now in ruins.

18 "My God, listen and hear. Open Your eyes and see our ruins and the city that bears Your name. We are not asking You for help because we have done right, but because You are so merciful.

19 "Lord, hear us! Lord, forgive us! Lord, pay attention and act! For Your own sake, my God, do not wait, because Your city and Your people bear Your name."

Gabriel Gives Daniel Insight

20 While I was still speaking, praying, confessing my sins and the sins of my people Israel, and asking the LORD my God for help for His holy mountain, 21 Gabriel, the man I had seen in the earlier vision, came to me in swift flight about the time of the evening sacrifice.

22 He explained things to me and said, "Daniel, I have come to give you understanding and insight.

23 "As soon as you began to pray, an answer was given. I have come to tell you, because you are greatly loved. So pay attention to the message and understand the vision.

24 "Seventy sets of seven years are set for your people and your holy city. They will put an end to rebellion, put an end to sin, pay for wrongdoing, bring in lasting right living, complete the vision and prophecy, and anoint the Most Holy Place.

25 "So know and understand this: From the time the order is given to restore and rebuild Jerusalem until the Messiah, the ruler, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks. The city will be rebuilt with streets and a trench, but it will be a time of trouble.

26 "After those sixty-two weeks, the Messiah will be put to death and have nothing. Then the people of the coming ruler will destroy the city and the holy place. The end will come like a flood. War and destruction will continue until the end, as has been decreed.

27 "He will make a strong covenant with many for one week. But in the middle of that week, he will put an end to sacrifice and offering. And on the wing of the temple he will set up a disgusting thing that causes ruin, until the destruction that has been decreed is poured out."
