A Helper When We Sin

1 My dear children, I am writing this to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have a Helper with the Father—Jesus Christ, who is right with God. 2 He is the sacrifice that pays for our sins. This is not only for us, but also for the sins of the whole world.

Knowing God and Loving Others

3 We know that we have come to know Him if we keep His commands. 4 Anyone who says, "I know Him," but does not keep His commands is a liar. The truth is not in that person. 5 But if anyone keeps His word, God's love is made complete in that person. This is how we know we are in Him: 6 whoever says he stays in Him must live the way Jesus lived.

7 Dear friends, I am not writing you a new command. I am writing an old command that you have had since the beginning. This old command is the message you heard. 8 Yet I am also writing you a new command. It is true in Him and in you, because the darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining.

9 Anyone who says he is in the light but hates his brother is still in the dark. 10 Anyone who loves his brother stays in the light, and there is nothing in him that makes others stumble. 11 But anyone who hates his brother is in the dark. He walks in the dark. He does not know where he is going, because the dark has blinded his eyes.

Growing in Faith and Not Loving the World

12 I am writing to you, dear children, because your sins are forgiven through His name. 13 I am writing to you, fathers, because you know Him who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one. I have written to you, children, because you know the Father. 14 I have written to you, fathers, because you know Him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, God's word stays in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

15 Do not love the world or anything in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in that person. 16 All that is in the world comes down to these things: the cravings of the body, the cravings of the eyes, and pride in what we own. These things are not from the Father. They are from the world. 17 The world and its desires are passing away. But whoever does God's will lives forever.

The Last Hour and False Teachers

18 Children, this is the last hour. You have heard that the antichrist is coming. Even now many antichrists have come. This is how we know it is the last hour. 19 They went out from us, but they did not really belong to us. If they had belonged to us, they would have stayed with us. But they left so it would be clear that not all of them belong to us.

20 But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know the truth. 21 I did not write to you because you do not know the truth. I wrote because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.

22 Who is the liar? It is the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ. This person is the antichrist. He denies the Father and the Son. 23 No one who denies the Son has the Father. Anyone who accepts the Son has the Father too.

24 Let what you heard from the beginning stay in you. If it does, you will stay in the Son and in the Father. 25 This is the promise He made to us: eternal life.

26 I have written these things to you about those who are trying to lead you astray. 27 The anointing you received from Him stays in you. You do not need anyone to teach you. His anointing teaches you about all things. It is true and not a lie. Stay in Him, just as it taught you.

28 And now, dear children, stay in Him. Then when He appears, we can be confident and not be ashamed before Him when He comes. 29 If you know that He is right with God, you also know that everyone who does what is right has been born of Him.
