Israel Sings to the LORD

1 Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to the the LORD: "I will sing to the the LORD, for He is great and glorious. The horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea. 2 The LORD is my strength and my song; He has become my salvation. This is my God, and I will praise Him, my father's God, and I will lift Him up. 3 The LORD is a warrior; the LORD is His name. 4 Pharaoh's chariots and his army He has thrown into the sea; his best officers sank in the Red Sea. 5 The deep waters covered them; they sank to the bottom like a stone. 6 Your right hand, LORD, is strong and glorious. Your right hand, LORD, smashed the enemy. 7 In the greatness of Your majesty You threw down those who stood against You. You sent out Your burning anger; it burned them up like dry grass. 8 By the power of Your breath the waters piled up. The rushing water stood like a wall; the deep waters became solid in the heart of the sea." 9 The enemy said,

"I will chase them, I will catch them, I will take their treasures. I will get all I want. I will pull out my sword; my hand will destroy them."

"10 But You blew with Your breath, and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in the mighty waters. 11 Who among the gods is like You, LORD? Who is like You—glorious in holiness, awesome in glory, doing wonders? 12 You stretched out Your right hand, and the earth swallowed them. 13 In Your faithful love You lead the people You rescued. In Your strength You guide them to Your holy place. 14 The nations will hear and tremble; terror will grip the people of Philistia. 15 The leaders of Edom will be afraid; the strong men of Moab will shake with fear; the people of Canaan will melt away. 16 Fear and dread will fall on them. By the power of Your arm they will be still as stone until Your people pass by, LORD, until the people You bought pass by. 17 You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain You promised them. LORD, You made that place for Your home; it is the holy place, LORD, that Your hands built. 18 The LORD will rule forever and ever."

19 When Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and riders went into the sea, the LORD brought the sea water back over them. But the Israelites walked on dry ground through the sea.

Miriam Leads the Women in Song

20 Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took a tambourine in her hand. All the women followed her, with tambourines and dancing.

21 Miriam sang to them: "Sing to the LORD, for He is great and glorious. The horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea."

Bitter Water at Marah

22 Then Moses led Israel away from the Red Sea into the Desert of Shur. They traveled three days in the desert and found no water.

23 When they came to Marah, they could not drink the water because it was bitter. That is why the place is called Marah.

24 The people complained to Moses and asked, "What are we going to drink?"

God Sweetens the Water and Promises Healing

25 Moses cried out to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a piece of wood. He threw it into the water, and the water became sweet. There the LORD gave them a rule and a law, and there He tested them.

26 He said, "If you listen carefully to the voice of the the LORD your God and do what is right in His eyes, if you pay attention to His commands and keep all His rules, I will not bring on you any of the diseases I brought on Egypt, for I am the LORD, who heals you."

27 Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs and seventy palm trees. They camped there by the water.
