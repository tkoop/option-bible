Israel Breaks Faith

1 The Israelites did not keep faith with the things set apart for the LORD. Achan son of Karmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah, took some of those things. So the LORD was very angry with Israel.

Defeat at Ai

2 Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, a town near Beth Aven, east of Bethel. He said, "Go up and spy on the land." The men went up and spied on Ai. 3 When they came back to Joshua, they said, "Not all the people need to go up against Ai. Send about two or three thousand men to attack it. Do not make all the people tired, because Ai has only a few people."

4 So about three thousand men went up. But the men of Ai beat them and chased them away. 5 The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them. They chased the Israelites from the city gate to the stone pits and struck them down on the slopes. Then the people lost all courage and were terrified.

Joshua Prays to the LORD

6 Then Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown on the ground before the ark of the LORD. He stayed there until evening. The leaders of Israel did the same. They put dust on their heads.

7 Joshua said, "O Lord, the LORD, why did You bring these people across the Jordan? Was it to hand us over to the Amorites so they could destroy us? If only we had stayed on the other side of the Jordan! 8 Lord, what can I say now that Israel has turned and run from its enemies? 9 The Canaanites and everyone else in the land will hear about this. They will surround us and wipe out our name from the earth. Then what will You do for Your great name?"

The LORD Reveals the Sin

10 The LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying facedown? 11 Israel has sinned. They broke My covenant that I gave them. They took some of the things set apart for Me. They stole them, lied about them, and hid them with their own things. 12 That is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They turn and run from them. They themselves are now set apart for destruction. I will not be with you anymore unless you destroy the devoted things among you."

13 The LORD said, "Get up and set the people apart for Me. Tell them, 'Set yourselves apart for tomorrow. This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: There are devoted things among you, Israel. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove them.' 14 In the morning, bring your tribes forward. The tribe the LORD chooses will come forward by clans. The clan the LORD chooses will come forward by families. The family the LORD chooses will come forward man by man. 15 Whoever is caught with the devoted things will be burned. He and everything he owns will be destroyed, because he broke the LORD's covenant and did a shameful thing in Israel."

16 Early the next morning, Joshua brought Israel forward by tribes. The tribe of Judah was chosen. 17 He brought the clans of Judah forward, and the Zerahites were chosen. Then he brought the Zerahite clans forward by families, and the family of Zabdi was chosen. 18 Joshua brought Zabdi's family forward man by man, and Achan son of Karmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah, was chosen.

19 Then Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give glory and praise to the LORD, the God of Israel. Tell me what you have done. Do not hide it from me." 20 Achan answered, "It is true. I have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did: 21 I saw a beautiful robe from Babylon, about two hundred shekels of silver, and a gold bar weighing fifty shekels. I wanted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground in my tent, with the silver under them."

22 Joshua sent messengers. They ran to the tent, and there were the things, hidden in the tent, with the silver underneath. 23 They took the things out of the tent and brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites. They spread them out before the LORD.

24 Then Joshua and all Israel took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the robe, the gold bar, his sons and daughters, his oxen, donkeys, and sheep, his tent, and everything he owned. They brought them to the Valley of Achor. 25 Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you today." Then all Israel stoned Achan. Afterward, they burned him and all he owned. 26 They piled a large heap of stones over Achan. It is still there today. Then the LORD turned from His fierce anger. That is why the place is called the Valley of Achor to this day.
