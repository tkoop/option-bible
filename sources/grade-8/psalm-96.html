A New Song for the LORD

1 Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. 2 Sing to the LORD, praise His name; tell of His salvation day after day. 3 Tell of His glory among the nations, and His wonderful works among all peoples.

4 For great is the LORD and worthy of much praise; He is to be feared above all gods. 5 For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 6 Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and beauty are in His sanctuary.

7 Give to the LORD, you families of nations, give to the LORD glory and strength. 8 Give to the LORD the glory His name deserves; bring an offering and come into His courts. 9 Worship the LORD in the beauty of His holiness; tremble before Him, all the earth.

10 Say among the nations, "The LORD reigns! The world is firmly set; it cannot be shaken. He will judge the peoples fairly."

11 Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea roar, and everything in it. 12 Let the fields and everything in them be joyful; let all the trees of the forest sing for joy. 13 Let all creation rejoice before the LORD, for He comes, He comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in His faithfulness.
