Paul Greets the Ephesians

1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, writes to God's holy people in Ephesus who are faithful in Christ Jesus. 2 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

God's Blessings in Christ

3 Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! He has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places. 4 He chose us in Him before the world was made, so that we would be holy and blameless before Him in love. 5 He planned ahead of time to adopt us as His children through Jesus Christ. This was His good and happy will. 6 So we praise the glory of His grace, which He freely gave us in His dearly loved Son.

7 In Him we have freedom through His blood. He forgave our sins because His grace is so rich. 8 He poured out that grace on us with all wisdom and understanding. 9 He made known to us the secret of His will. It matched His good plan, which He set in Christ. 10 This plan will bring everything together in Christ when the time is right. That includes things in heaven and things on earth.

11 In Christ we were chosen. God planned this ahead of time. He works everything out by His own will and plan. 12 We were the first to hope in Christ, so we would bring praise to His glory. 13 You also heard the true message, the good news of your salvation. When you believed, you were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit. 14 The Spirit is a down payment on our inheritance. He guarantees it until God fully redeems His people. This brings praise to His glory.

Paul Prays for the Ephesians

15 I have heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God's holy people. 16 So I never stop thanking God for you. I keep you in my prayers. 17 I pray that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, will give you a spirit of wisdom and understanding. Then you will know Him better. 18 I pray that the eyes of your hearts will be opened. Then you will know the hope He has called you to. You will know the riches of His glorious inheritance among His holy people.

19 You will also know His great power for us who believe. It works by His mighty strength. 20 He used that power in Christ when He raised Him from the dead. He seated Him at His right hand in heaven. 21 Christ is far above every ruler, authority, power, and leader. He is above every name that is named, now and in the future. 22 God put everything under His feet. He made Christ head over everything for the church. 23 The church is His body. It is filled by Him who fills everything in every way.
