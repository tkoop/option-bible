Jesus Before Pilate and Herod

1 The whole crowd got up and led Him to Pilate.

2 They began to accuse Him, saying, “We found this man misleading our nation, telling people not to pay taxes to Caesar, and claiming to be the Christ, a king.”

3 Pilate asked Him, “Are you the King of the Jews?”

He answered, “You have said so.”

4 Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I find no reason to charge this man with anything.”

5 But they kept insisting, “He stirs up the people all over Judea by teaching. He started in Galilee and has come here.”

6 When Pilate heard this, he asked if the man was a Galilean. 7 Learning that He was under Herod’s authority, he sent Him to Herod, who was in Jerusalem at that time.

8 Herod was very glad to see Jesus. He had wanted to see Him for a long time because he had heard about Him, and he hoped to see Him do a miracle.

9 He questioned Jesus with many words, but Jesus gave him no answer. 10 The chief priests and teachers of the law stood there, strongly accusing Him.

11 Herod and his soldiers made fun of Jesus. They dressed Him in a bright robe and sent Him back to Pilate. 12 That same day Herod and Pilate became friends. Before that, they had been enemies.

Pilate Tries to Release Jesus

13 Pilate called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people. 14 He said to them, “You brought me this man as someone who stirs up the people. I have examined Him in front of you and found no reason for the charges you bring against Him. 15 Herod found nothing either, because he sent Him back to us. Indeed, He has done nothing that deserves death. 16 So I will punish Him and let Him go.”

18 But they all shouted, “Take this man away! Release Barabbas to us!”

19 Barabbas had been put in prison for starting a riot in the city and for murder.

20 Pilate wanted to release Jesus, so he spoke to them again.

21 But they shouted, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!”

22 A third time he said to them, “What has this man done wrong? I have found no reason to put Him to death. So I will punish Him and let Him go.”

23 But they kept shouting loudly, demanding that He be crucified, and their voices won out.

24 So Pilate decided to grant their request. 25 He released the man who had been in prison for riot and murder, the one they asked for, and he handed Jesus over to be treated as they wanted.

Jesus Is Crucified

26 As they led Him away, they seized Simon from Cyrene, who was coming in from the country. They put the cross on him and made him carry it behind Jesus.

27 A large crowd of people followed Him, including women who mourned and cried for Him.

28 Jesus turned to them and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me. Weep for yourselves and for your children. 29 For the days are coming when people will say, ‘Blessed are the women who could not have children, the wombs that never gave birth, and the breasts that never nursed.’ 30 Then they will say to the mountains, ‘Fall on us,’ and to the hills, ‘Cover us.’ 31 For if people do this to the innocent, what will happen to the guilty?”

32 Two other criminals were led out to be put to death with Him.

33 When they came to the place called the Skull, they crucified Him there, along with the criminals, one on His right and one on His left.

34 Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.” Then they divided His clothes by casting lots.

35 The people stood there watching. The rulers sneered at Him, saying, “He saved others; let Him save Himself if He is God’s Messiah, the Chosen One.”

36 The soldiers also mocked Him. They came up and offered Him vinegar. 37 They said, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself.”

38 There was a sign above Him that said, “This is the King of the Jews.”

39 One of the criminals hanging there insulted Him, saying, “Aren’t you the Messiah? Save yourself and us!”

40 But the other criminal answered and scolded him, saying, “Don’t you fear God, since you are under the same sentence? 41 We are punished fairly, because we are getting what our deeds deserve. But this man has done nothing wrong.” 42 Then he said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.”

43 Jesus said to him, “Truly I tell you, today you will be with Me in paradise.”

Jesus Dies and Is Buried

44 It was about noon, and darkness covered the whole land until three in the afternoon. 45 The sun stopped shining, and the curtain in the temple was torn down the middle.

46 Jesus cried out in a loud voice, “Father, into your hands I commit My spirit.” After saying this, He died.

47 When the centurion saw what happened, he praised God and said, “Surely this was a righteous man.”

48 All the crowds who had come to watch saw what happened. They went home, beating their chests in grief. 49 But all those who knew Him, including the women who had followed Him from Galilee, stood at a distance and watched these things.

50 There was a man named Joseph, a member of the council. He was a good and righteous man. 51 He had not agreed with their decision and action. He was from the Jewish town of Arimathea, and he was waiting for the kingdom of God.

52 He went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. 53 He took it down, wrapped it in linen cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut into rock, where no one had ever been laid.

54 It was Preparation Day, and the Sabbath was about to begin. 55 The women who had come with Him from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how His body was placed. 56 Then they went home and prepared spices and perfumes. They rested on the Sabbath, as the commandment said.
