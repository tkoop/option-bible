Hezekiah Turns to God

1 When King Hezekiah heard this, he tore his clothes, covered himself with sackcloth, and went into the house of the LORD. 2 He sent Eliakim, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the secretary, and the older priests, all wearing sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.

3 They said to him, "This is what Hezekiah says: Today is a day of trouble, rebuke, and disgrace. Children have come to the point of birth, but there is no strength to deliver them. 4 Maybe the LORD your God will hear all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master the king of Assyria sent to insult the living God, and will correct him for the words the LORD your God has heard. So pray for the remnant that is left."

5 So the servants of King Hezekiah went to Isaiah.

6 Isaiah said to them, "Tell your master, 'This is what the LORD says: Do not be afraid of the words you have heard, the words the servants of the king of Assyria have used to insult Me.' 7 Look, I will put a spirit in him. He will hear a report and go back to his own country. There I will make him fall by the sword."

8 When the Rabshakeh heard that the king of Assyria had left Lachish, he went back and found the king fighting against Libnah.

Sennacherib Threatens Jerusalem

9 The king of Assyria heard that Tirhakah, king of Cush, had come out to fight against him. So he sent messengers to Hezekiah again and said,

"10 Say to Hezekiah king of Judah: Do not let your God, whom you trust, fool you by saying, 'Jerusalem will not be handed over to the king of Assyria.' 11 Look, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the other lands. They destroyed them completely. Do you think you will be rescued?

"12 Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed save them—Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar? 13 Where is the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?"

14 Hezekiah took the letters from the messengers and read them. Then he went up to the house of the LORD and spread them out before the LORD.

15 Hezekiah prayed to the LORD: "The LORD, God of Israel, who sits enthroned between the cherubim, You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made heaven and earth. 16 The LORD, turn Your ear to me and hear. The LORD, open Your eyes and see. Listen to the words Sennacherib has sent to insult the living God.

"17 It is true, the LORD, that the kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations and their lands. 18 They have thrown their gods into the fire and destroyed them, because they were not gods at all. They were only wood and stone, made by human hands. 19 Now, the LORD our God, save us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone, the LORD, are God."

God Answers Through Isaiah

20 Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah: "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I have heard your prayer about Sennacherib king of Assyria.

"21 This is the word the LORD has spoken against him: 'Virgin Daughter Zion despises you and mocks you; Daughter Jerusalem shakes her head at you.'

"22 Whom have you insulted and spoken against? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes in pride? Against the Holy One of Israel!

"23 By your messengers you have insulted the Lord. You said, 'With my many chariots I have climbed to the tops of the mountains, to the farthest parts of Lebanon. I cut down its tallest cedars and its best cypresses. I reached its farthest shelter, its thickest forest.' 24 'I have dug wells and drunk foreign water. With the soles of my feet I have dried up all the streams of Egypt.'

"25 Have you not heard? Long ago I planned this; in ancient times I set it in motion. Now I have brought it to pass, so that you could turn fortified cities into piles of rubble. 26 Their people were weak and afraid. They were like grass in a field, like tender green shoots, like grass on a roof, burned before it grows.

"27 But I know when you sit and when you go out and come in. I know how you rage against Me. 28 Because you rage against Me and your pride has reached My ears, I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth. I will make you go back the way you came.

"29 This will be a sign for you: This year you will eat what grows by itself. Next year you will eat what grows from that. But in the third year you will plant and harvest, plant vineyards, and eat their fruit. 30 The remnant of Judah that survives will take root below and bear fruit above. 31 From Jerusalem a remnant will go out, and from Mount Zion a band of survivors. The zeal of the LORD will do this.

"32 So this is what the LORD says about the king of Assyria: He will not enter this city. He will not shoot an arrow here. He will not come against it with a shield or build a siege ramp. 33 He will go back the way he came. He will not enter this city, declares the LORD. 34 I will defend this city and save it for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David."

The Assyrian Army Is Destroyed

35 That night the angel of the LORD went out and struck down 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. In the morning, when the people got up, they saw all the dead bodies. 36 So Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and went home. He returned to Nineveh and stayed there. 37 One day, while he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with the sword. Then they escaped to the land of Ararat, and his son Esarhaddon became king after him.
