A Prayer for Help in Battle

1 You have rejected us, God. You broke through our defenses, and You were angry. Restore us! 2 You shook the land and tore it open. Heal its cracks, for it is quaking. 3 You made Your people suffer. You made us drink wine that makes us stagger. 4 You raised a flag for those who fear You, so they can run to it and escape the arrows. 5 Save us with Your right hand. Answer us, so those You love may be rescued.

6 God has spoken from His holy place: "I will triumph. I will divide Shechem and measure the Valley of Succoth. 7 Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is My helmet, and Judah is My scepter. 8 Moab is My washbasin. I will throw My sandal on Edom, and I will shout in triumph over Philistia."

9 Who will bring me to the strong city? Who will lead me to Edom? 10 Haven't You rejected us, God? Won't You march out with our armies? 11 Give us help against the foe, for help from people is worthless. 12 With God we will fight bravely, and He will trample our enemies.
