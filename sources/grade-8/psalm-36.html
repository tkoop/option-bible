The Wicked Person Has No Fear of God

1 For the choir director. A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD. Deep in my heart I know this about the wicked person: There is no fear of God before his eyes. 2 He flatters himself and thinks his sin will not be found out or hated. 3 The words from his mouth are evil and deceitful; he has stopped being wise and doing good. 4 He plans evil while lying in bed; he chooses a path that is not good; he does not turn away from evil.

The LORD's Faithful Love Reaches the Heavens

5 Your faithful love, O the LORD, reaches to the heavens; Your faithfulness reaches to the skies. 6 Your goodness is like the mountains of God; Your judgments are like the deep sea. O the LORD, You keep people and animals safe. 7 How precious is Your faithful love, O God! People find safety in the shadow of Your wings. 8 They feast on the plenty in Your house; You give them drink from the river of Your delights. 9 For with You is the fountain of life; in Your light we see light.

A Prayer for God's Protection

10 Show Your faithful love to those who know You, and Your goodness to those with honest hearts. 11 Do not let the proud step on me, and do not let the wicked drive me away. 12 There the evildoers have fallen; they are thrown down and cannot get up.
