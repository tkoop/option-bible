The Elders Come to God

1 In the seventh year, on the tenth day of the fifth month, some of the elders of Israel came to ask the LORD a question. They sat down in front of me.

2 Then the word of the LORD came to me:

3 “Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel. Tell them this is what the Lord the LORD says: ‘Have you come to ask Me questions? As surely as I live, I will not let you ask Me anything,’ says the Lord the LORD. 4 Son of man, will you judge them? Will you judge them? Then make known to them the disgusting things their fathers did.”

Israel’s Rebellion in Egypt and the Desert

5 “Tell them this is what the Lord the LORD says: On the day I chose Israel, I raised My hand in an oath to the family of Jacob. I made Myself known to them in Egypt. I raised My hand and said, ‘I am the LORD your God.’ 6 On that day I raised My hand to bring them out of Egypt into a land I had chosen for them. It was a rich land, the finest of all lands. 7 I told them, ‘Each of you must throw away the disgusting things you look at. Do not make yourselves unclean with the idols of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.’”

8 “But they refused to listen to Me. No one threw away the disgusting things he looked at. No one gave up the idols of Egypt. So I said I would pour out My anger on them and destroy them in Egypt. 9 But I acted for the sake of My name, so it would not be dishonored among the nations where they lived. There I had shown them who I was by bringing them out of Egypt. 10 So I brought them out of Egypt and led them into the desert.”

11 “I gave them My laws and taught them My rules. If a person follows them, he will live. 12 I also gave them My Sabbaths. They were a sign between Me and them, so they would know that I am the LORD, who makes them holy. 13 But the people of Israel rebelled against Me in the desert. They did not follow My laws. They rejected My rules, even though a person who follows them will live. They badly dishonored My Sabbaths. So I said I would pour out My anger on them and destroy them in the desert. 14 But I acted for the sake of My name, so it would not be dishonored among the nations who saw Me bring them out.”

15 “I also raised My hand in the desert. I swore I would not bring them into the land I had given them, a rich land, the finest of all lands. 16 They rejected My rules and did not follow My laws. They dishonored My Sabbaths, because their hearts went after their idols. 17 But I felt sorry for them. I did not destroy them completely in the desert.”

18 “I told their children in the desert, ‘Do not follow your parents’ ways. Do not keep their rules. Do not make yourselves unclean with their idols.’ 19 I am the LORD your God. Follow My laws. Keep My rules and do them. 20 Keep My Sabbaths holy. They are a sign between Me and you, so you will know that I am the LORD your God. 21 But the children also rebelled against Me. They did not follow My laws. They did not keep My rules, even though a person who follows them will live. They dishonored My Sabbaths. So I said I would pour out My anger on them and destroy them in the desert. 22 But I held back My hand. I acted for the sake of My name, so it would not be dishonored among the nations who saw Me bring them out.”

23 “I also raised My hand in the desert. I swore I would scatter them among the nations and spread them through other countries. 24 They did not follow My rules. They rejected My laws. They dishonored My Sabbaths. Their eyes were set on their fathers’ idols. 25 So I also gave them rules that were not good and laws by which they could not live. 26 I let them become unclean through their gifts. They sacrificed their firstborn children by passing them through the fire. I did this to make them guilty and to show them that I am the LORD.”

27 “Therefore, Son of man, speak to the people of Israel. Tell them this is what the Lord the LORD says: ‘Your fathers also insulted Me by being unfaithful to Me.’ 28 I brought them into the land I had promised to give them. When they saw every high hill and every green tree, they offered their sacrifices there. They made Me angry by placing their gifts there. They burned their sweet-smelling offerings there. They poured out their drink offerings there. 29 I asked them, ‘What is this high place you keep going to?’ So it is called High Place to this day.”

30 “Therefore tell the people of Israel this is what the Lord the LORD says: ‘You are making yourselves unclean the same way your fathers did. You are chasing after their disgusting things.’ 31 When you offer your gifts and sacrifice your children in the fire, you make yourselves unclean with all your idols to this day. Should I let you ask Me questions, people of Israel? As surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will not let you ask Me anything. 32 What you are thinking will never happen. You say, ‘Let us be like the other nations, like the families of other lands. Let us worship wood and stone.’”

God Will Rule and Restore Israel

33 “As surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will rule over you with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out anger. 34 I will bring you out from the nations. I will gather you from the countries where you were scattered. I will rule over you with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out anger. 35 I will bring you into the desert of the nations. There I will judge you face to face. 36 I will judge you the same way I judged your fathers in the desert of Egypt, says the Lord the LORD. 37 I will make you pass under My rod. I will bring you into the bond of the covenant. 38 I will remove from you those who rebel and sin against Me. I will bring them out of the land where they live, but they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

39 “As for you, people of Israel, this is what the Lord the LORD says: Go and serve your idols, every one of you. But later you will surely listen to Me. You will no longer make My holy name unclean with your gifts and your idols. 40 For on My holy mountain, on the high mountain of Israel, says the Lord the LORD, all the people of Israel will serve Me. There, in the land, I will accept them. There I will ask for your offerings and the best of your gifts, along with all your holy things. 41 When I bring you out from the nations and gather you from the countries where you were scattered, I will accept you like a pleasing smell. I will show My holiness through you before the nations. 42 Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, the land I raised My hand to give to your fathers. 43 There you will remember your ways and all your actions by which you made yourselves unclean. You will hate yourselves because of all the evil you have done. 44 Then you will know, people of Israel, that I am the LORD. I will deal with you for the sake of My name, not according to your evil ways or your corrupt actions, says the Lord the LORD.”

A Fire in the Southern Forest

45 Then the word of the LORD came to me: 46 “Son of man, turn your face toward the south. Speak against the south. Prophesy against the forest of the south.”

47 “Say to the forest of the south, ‘Hear the word of the LORD. This is what the Lord the LORD says: I am about to set a fire in you. It will burn up every green tree and every dry tree. The bright flame will not be put out. Every face from south to north will be scorched by it.’ 48 Everyone will see that I, the LORD, have set it on fire. It will not be put out.”

49 Then I said, “Ah, Lord the LORD! They are saying about me, ‘Is he not just telling parables?’”
