The LORD Is Our Help and Shield

1 Not to us, the LORD, not to us, but give glory to Your name, because of Your loving devotion, because of Your faithfulness. 2 Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?" 3 Our God is in heaven; He does whatever He wants.

4 Their idols are silver and gold, made by human hands. 5 They have mouths, but they do not speak; they have eyes, but they do not see. 6 They have ears, but they do not hear; they have noses, but they do not smell. 7 They have hands, but they do not feel; they have feet, but they do not walk; and they cannot make a sound with their throats. 8 Those who make them become like them, and so do all who trust in them.

9 O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield. 10 O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield. 11 You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.

12 The LORD has remembered us; He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron. 13 He will bless those who fear the LORD, both the small and the great. 14 May the LORD give you more and more, you and your children! 15 You are blessed by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

16 The heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth He has given to people. 17 The dead do not praise the LORD, nor do those who go down into silence. 18 But we will bless the LORD from now on and forever. Praise the LORD!
