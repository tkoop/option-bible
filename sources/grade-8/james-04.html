Why Do You Fight?

1 Where do wars and fights among you come from? Don't they come from the desires inside you that battle in your bodies? 2 You want things and don't have them. You kill and are jealous, but you still can't get them. You fight and go to war. You don't have, because you don't ask. 3 When you do ask, you don't receive, because you ask for the wrong things. You just want to spend them on your own pleasures.

Friendship with the World

4 You unfaithful people! Don't you know that friendship with the world makes you an enemy of God? So anyone who wants to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God. 5 Do you think Scripture says this for no reason? The Spirit He placed in us longs for us with jealous love. 6 But He gives even more grace. That is why it says, "God opposes the proud, but He gives grace to the humble."

Humble Yourselves Before God

7 So submit to God. Stand up to the devil, and he will run away from you. 8 Come near to God, and He will come near to you. Wash your hands, you sinners, and make your hearts pure, you who can't make up your minds. 9 Be sad, mourn, and cry. Let your laughter turn into mourning, and your joy turn into gloom. 10 Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will lift you up.

Do Not Judge or Boast

11 Brothers and sisters, don't speak badly about each other. Anyone who speaks against a brother or sister, or judges them, speaks against the law and judges the law. If you judge the law, you are not obeying it but judging it. 12 There is only one Lawgiver and Judge, the One who can save and destroy. So who are you to judge your neighbor?

13 Now listen, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go to this city and stay there a year, trading and making a profit." 14 You don't even know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? You are like a mist that shows up for a little while and then disappears.

15 Instead, you should say, "If the Lord wants us to, we will live and do this or that." 16 But now you brag about your proud plans. All that kind of bragging is evil. 17 So if someone knows the good they should do and doesn't do it, that is sin.
