God Calls Judah to Repent

1 "If you return, Israel, return to Me," says the LORD. "If you remove your detestable idols from My presence, you will not be driven away." 2 If you take an oath in the LORD's name—"As the LORD lives"—with truth, justice, and righteousness, then nations will be blessed through Him and praise Him.

3 For this is what the LORD says to the people of Judah and to Jerusalem: "Prepare your hearts for Me; don't keep doing things that only bring trouble." 4 "Give your hearts to the LORD; get rid of the stubbornness in your hearts, people of Judah and residents of Jerusalem. Otherwise My anger will blaze like fire and burn with no one to put it out, because of your evil deeds."

Warning of an Enemy from the North

5 "Announce in Judah, shout in Jerusalem, and say: Blow the trumpet throughout the land. Call out loudly, 'Gather together! Let us run to the walled cities.'" 6 "Raise a warning flag toward Zion. Seek shelter; don't delay! I am bringing terrible trouble from the north, a great disaster."

7 "A lion has come out of his thicket; a destroyer of nations is on his way. He has left his den to turn your land into a wasteland and destroy your cities so no one lives there."

8 "So put on rough mourning clothes, mourn and wail, because the LORD's fierce anger has not turned away from us."

9 "On that day," says the LORD, "the king and the officials will lose heart. The priests will be shocked, and the prophets will be confused."

10 Then I said, "Alas, Lord, the LORD! You have completely deceived this people and Jerusalem by saying, 'You will have peace,' when the sword is at our throats."

11 At that time people will say to this nation and to Jerusalem, "A hot wind from the bare hills of the desert blows toward My people, but not to separate the grain or to cleanse."

12 "A wind too strong for that comes from Me. Now I will also pronounce judgments against them."

13 "Look, he advances like clouds; his chariots are like a whirlwind. His horses are faster than eagles. Woe to us, for we are ruined!"

14 "O Jerusalem, wash the evil from your heart, so you may be saved. How long will you keep wicked thoughts within you?"

15 "A voice shouts from Dan, and trouble is announced from the hills of Ephraim."

16 "Tell the nations, and announce to Jerusalem: 'Attackers are coming from a distant land, shouting against the cities of Judah.'"

17 "They surround her like guards around a field, because she has rebelled against Me," says the LORD. 18 "Your own ways and deeds have brought this on you. This is your punishment. How bitter it is, because it cuts to your heart!"

Jeremiah's Sorrow and Vision of Ruin

19 "My pain, my pain! I twist in pain. Oh, my heart! My heart pounds within me. I cannot stay silent, for I have heard the trumpet, the alarm of war." 20 "Disaster after disaster is announced, for the whole land is ruined. Suddenly my tents are destroyed; my tent curtains are torn in a moment." 21 "How long must I see the battle flag and hear the trumpet?"

22 "For My people are foolish; they do not know Me. They are senseless children with no understanding. They are skilled at doing evil, but they do not know how to do good."

23 "I looked at the earth, and it was a wasteland with nothing in it. I looked at the heavens, and their light was gone." 24 "I looked at the mountains, and they were quaking; all the hills were swaying." 25 "I looked, and no one was there; all the birds of the sky had flown away." 26 "I looked, and the fruitful land had become a desert; all its cities were torn down before the LORD, before His fierce anger."

The LORD's Final Judgment on Judah

27 "For this is what the LORD says: 'The whole land will be desolate, but I will not completely destroy it.'" 28 "The earth will mourn, and the heavens above will grow dark, because I have spoken. I have planned, and I will not change My mind or turn back."

29 "Every city runs from the sound of the horseman and archer. People hide in the bushes and climb among the rocks. Every city is abandoned; no one lives in them."

30 "And you, ruined one, what will you do? Though you wear scarlet, though you deck yourself with gold, though you put makeup on your eyes, you make yourself beautiful for nothing. Your allies reject you; they want to kill you."

31 "For I hear a cry like a woman in labor, a cry of anguish like a woman giving birth to her first child—the cry of the daughter of Zion gasping for breath and stretching out her hands: 'Woe is me, for I am worn out by killers!'"
