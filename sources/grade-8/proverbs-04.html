A Father's Wise Advice

1 Listen, my sons, to your father's teaching. Pay close attention so you can understand. 2 I'm giving you good lessons, so don't give up my teaching. 3 When I was a boy, I was my father's son. I was young and the only child my mother had. 4 He taught me and said, "Hold on to my words with all your heart. Keep my commands, and you will live."

Get Wisdom

5 Get wisdom and get understanding. Don't forget them, and don't turn away from what I say. 6 Don't leave wisdom behind, and she will keep you safe. Love her, and she will guard you. 7 Wisdom is the most important thing, so get wisdom. With everything you gain, get understanding. 8 Value wisdom highly, and she will lift you up. She will honor you when you hold her close. 9 She will put a graceful garland on your head and give you a beautiful crown. 10 Listen to me, my son, and accept my words. Then you will live many years. 11 I've taught you the way of wisdom and led you on the path of what's right. 12 When you walk, nothing will slow you down. If you run, you will not trip. 13 Hold on to instruction and don't let go. Guard wisdom, because she is your life.

Stay Away from the Wicked

14 Don't go where wicked people go, and don't follow the path of those who do wrong. 15 Stay away from it. Don't even go near it. Turn from it and keep moving. 16 Wicked people can't sleep unless they've done something wrong. They lose sleep unless they make someone fall. 17 They feed on evil and drink in violence. 18 But the path of those who do right shines like the morning light. It grows brighter until the day is fully here. 19 The way of wicked people is like deep darkness. They don't even know what makes them stumble.

Guard Your Heart

20 My son, listen to my words. Pay attention to what I say. 21 Don't let them get away from you. Keep them in your heart. 22 They bring life to everyone who finds them and healing to the whole body. 23 Guard your heart more than anything else, because everything in life flows from it. 24 Keep dishonest talk far from you, and keep sly words far from you. 25 Look straight ahead, and keep your eyes focused on what's in front of you. 26 Think carefully about where you step, and then everything you do will be steady. 27 Don't turn to the right or the left. Keep your foot away from evil.
