Warning to the Women of Samaria

1 Listen to this, you pampered women of Samaria who live on Mount Samaria. You crush the poor and push down the needy. You say to your husbands, "Bring us something to drink."

2 The Lord the LORD has sworn by His holiness: "Days are coming when enemies will drag you away with hooks, and your descendants with fishhooks. 3 You will go out through the breaks in the wall, each woman straight ahead, and you will be thrown toward Harmon," declares the LORD.

Empty Worship in Israel

4 "Go to Bethel and sin; go to Gilgal and sin even more. Bring your sacrifices every morning, your tithes every three days. 5 Offer a thank offering made with yeast, and brag about your freewill offerings. This is what you love to do, people of Israel," declares the Lord the LORD.

The LORD's Repeated Warnings

6 "I gave you empty stomachs in every city and a lack of bread in every place, yet you did not return to Me," declares the LORD. 7 "I also held back the rain from you when the harvest was still three months away. I sent rain on one city, but not on another. One field got rain, while another field without rain dried up. 8 People from two or three cities wandered to another city for water, but they did not get enough to drink. Yet you did not return to Me," declares the LORD.

9 "I struck you with blight and mildew. Locusts ate your many gardens and vineyards, your fig trees and olive trees. Yet you did not return to Me," declares the LORD. 10 "I sent a plague on you like the plagues of Egypt. I killed your young men with the sword, and your horses were taken. I made the stench of your camps rise into your nostrils. Yet you did not return to Me," declares the LORD. 11 "I destroyed some of you like I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. You were like a burning stick pulled from a fire. Yet you did not return to Me," declares the LORD.

Prepare to Meet Your God

12 "Therefore this is what I will do to you, O Israel: Because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel!"

13 For behold, He who forms the mountains, creates the wind, and reveals His thoughts to man, who turns the dawn to darkness and treads on the heights of the earth—the LORD, the God of Hosts, is His name.
