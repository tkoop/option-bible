Nehemiah Secures Jerusalem

1 When the wall was finished and the doors were set in place, Nehemiah appointed the gatekeepers, singers, and Levites. 2 He put his brother Hanani and Hananiah, the commander of the citadel, in charge of Jerusalem, because Hananiah was a faithful man who feared God more than most people. 3 He told them, "Do not open the gates of Jerusalem until noon. While the gatekeepers are still on duty, have them shut and bar the doors. Also appoint guards from the people of Jerusalem, each at his post and each near his own house." 4 The city was large and spacious, but few people lived in it, and the houses had not yet been rebuilt.

Nehemiah Registers the People

5 My God put it into my heart to gather the nobles, the officials, and the people so they could be listed by family. I found the family records of those who had first returned, and this is what was written in them.

6 These are the people of the province who returned from exile. Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had carried them away, but they came back to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his own town. 7 They came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bigvai, Nehum, and Baanah. Here is the number of the men of Israel:

8 The sons of Parosh, 2,172. 9 The sons of Shephatiah, 372. 10 The sons of Arah, 652. 11 The sons of Pahath-moab, through the line of Jeshua and Joab, 2,818. 12 The sons of Elam, 1,254. 13 The sons of Zattu, 845. 14 The sons of Zaccai, 760. 15 The sons of Binnui, 648. 16 The sons of Bebai, 628. 17 The sons of Azgad, 2,322. 18 The sons of Adonikam, 667. 19 The sons of Bigvai, 2,067. 20 The sons of Adin, 655. 21 The sons of Ater, through Hezekiah, 98. 22 The sons of Hashum, 328. 23 The sons of Bezai, 324. 24 The sons of Hariph, 112. 25 The sons of Gibeon, 95.

26 The men of Bethlehem and Netophah, 188. 27 The men of Anathoth, 128. 28 The men of Beth-azmaveth, 42. 29 The men of Kiriath-jearim, Chephirah, and Beeroth, 743. 30 The men of Ramah and Geba, 621. 31 The men of Michmas, 122. 32 The men of Bethel and Ai, 123. 33 The men of the other Nebo, 52. 34 The sons of the other Elam, 1,254. 35 The sons of Harim, 320. 36 The sons of Jericho, 345. 37 The sons of Lod, Hadid, and Ono, 721. 38 The sons of Senaah, 3,930.

39 The priests: the sons of Jedaiah, from the family of Jeshua, 973; 40 the sons of Immer, 1,052; 41 the sons of Pashhur, 1,247; 42 the sons of Harim, 1,017.

43 The Levites: the sons of Jeshua, through Kadmiel, from the line of Hodevah, 74. 44 The singers: the sons of Asaph, 148. 45 The gatekeepers: the sons of Shallum, Ater, Talmon, Akkub, Hatita, and Shobai, 138.

46 The temple servants: the sons of Ziha, Hasupha, Tabbaoth, 47 Keros, Sia, Padon, 48 Lebana, Hagaba, Shalmai, 49 Hanan, Giddel, Gahar, 50 Reaiah, Rezin, Nekoda, 51 Gazzam, Uzza, Paseah, 52 Besai, Meunim, Nephusesim, 53 Bakbuk, Hakupha, Harhur, 54 Bazlith, Mehida, Harsha, 55 Barkos, Sisera, Temah, 56 Neziah, and Hatipha.

57 The sons of Solomon's servants: Sotai, Sophereth, Perida, 58 Jaala, Darkon, Giddel, 59 Shephatiah, Hattil, Pochereth-hazzebaim, and Amon. 60 All the temple servants and the sons of Solomon's servants totaled 392.

61 Some people came up from Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer, but they could not prove their family lines or show that they were Israelites. 62 They were the sons of Delaiah, Tobiah, and Nekoda, 642 in all. 63 Some priests also could not prove their family line: the sons of Hobaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai, who had married a daughter of Barzillai the Gileadite and took that family's name. 64 They searched the family records, but their names were not found, so they were not allowed to serve as priests. 65 The governor told them, "Do not eat any of the most holy things until a priest can use the Urim and Thummim to decide the matter."

66 The whole group numbered 42,360. 67 Besides them, there were 7,337 male and female servants, and 245 singers, men and women. 69 They had 435 camels and 6,720 donkeys.

Gifts for the Work

70 Some of the family leaders gave money for the work. The governor gave 1,000 darics of gold, 50 bowls, and 530 priestly garments to the treasury. 71 Other family leaders gave 20,000 darics of gold and 2,200 minas of silver to the treasury. 72 The rest of the people gave 20,000 darics of gold, 2,000 minas of silver, and 67 priestly garments.

The People Settle in Their Towns

73 The priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and some of the people, along with all Israel, settled in their towns. When the seventh month came, the people of Israel were living in their towns.
