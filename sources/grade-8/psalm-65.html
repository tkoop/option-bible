A Song of Praise

1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A Song. Praise waits for You, O God, in Zion, and to You our vows will be fulfilled. 2 You who hear prayer, all people will come to You. 3 When my sins overpower me, You forgive our wrongs. 4 Blessed is the one You choose and bring near to live in Your courts! We are filled with the good things in Your house, Your holy temple.

5 You answer us with awesome acts of justice, O God of our salvation, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. 6 You formed the mountains by Your power; You are armed with strength. 7 You calm the roar of the seas, the roar of their waves, and the uproar of the nations. 8 Those who live at the ends of the earth fear Your signs. You make the dawn and sunset shout for joy.

9 You visit the earth and water it; You make it very rich. The river of God is full of water. You provide grain, for so You have prepared it. 10 You water its rows abundantly; You settle its ridges. You soften it with showers and bless its growth. 11 You crown the year with Your bounty, and Your paths overflow with plenty. 12 The pastures of the wilderness overflow; the hills are clothed with joy. 13 The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are covered with grain. They shout for joy; yes, they sing.
