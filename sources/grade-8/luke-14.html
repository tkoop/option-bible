Jesus Heals on the Sabbath

1 One Sabbath, He went to eat at the home of one of the leading Pharisees. They were watching Him closely. 2 A man with a swelling disease stood in front of Him.

3 Jesus asked the experts in the law and the Pharisees, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath, or not?"

4 They said nothing. So He took the man, healed him, and sent him away.

5 Then He asked them, "Which of you, if his son or ox falls into a well on the Sabbath, would not pull him out at once?"

6 They could not answer Him.

A Lesson in Humility

7 He noticed how the guests chose the best seats. So He told them this story:

8 "When you are invited to a wedding feast, do not sit in the best seat. Someone more important than you may have been invited too.

9 "Then the host who invited both of you will tell you to give that person your seat. You will be ashamed and have to take the last seat.

10 "But when you are invited, go and sit in the last seat. Then your host may ask you, as a friend, to move to a better place. That will bring you honor in front of everyone eating with you.

11 "For everyone who lifts himself up will be brought down, and everyone who brings himself down will be lifted up."

The Great Banquet

12 "When you give a lunch or dinner, do not invite your friends, your brothers, your relatives, or rich neighbors. They might invite you back, and that would pay you back.

13 "Instead, when you give a feast, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, and the blind. 14 You will be blessed, because they cannot pay you back. You will be paid back when good people are raised to life."

15 One of the guests heard this and said to Him, "Blessed is anyone who eats bread in the kingdom of God!"

16 "A man gave a great dinner and invited many people.

17 "When it was time to eat, he sent his servant to tell the guests to come, because everything was ready.

18 "But they all began to make excuses. The first said he had bought a field and had to go see it, and he asked to be excused.

19 "Another said he had bought five pairs of oxen and was going to try them out, and he asked to be excused.

20 "Still another said he had just married, so he could not come.

21 "The servant came back and told his master this. The master was angry and told his servant to go quickly into the streets and alleys of the town and bring in the poor, the crippled, the blind, and the lame.

22 "The servant reported that he had done as ordered, but there was still room.

23 "Then the master told his servant to go out to the roads and country lanes and make people come in, so his house would be full.

24 "I tell you, none of those men who were invited will taste my dinner."

The Cost of Following Jesus

25 Large crowds were traveling with Him. He turned and said to them:

26 "If anyone comes to Me and is not ready to put Me before his father, mother, wife, children, brothers, and sisters, and even his own life, he cannot be My disciple.

27 "Whoever does not carry his own cross and follow Me cannot be My disciple.

28 "Suppose one of you wants to build a tower. Won't he first sit down and figure out the cost, to see if he has enough money to finish?

29 "If he lays the foundation but cannot finish, everyone who sees it will laugh at him.

30 "They will say he started to build but could not finish.

31 "Or suppose a king is going to war against another king. Won't he first sit down and decide if he can face an army of twenty thousand with only ten thousand?

32 "If he cannot, he will send messengers while the other king is still far away and ask for peace.

33 "In the same way, none of you can be My disciple unless you give up everything you own.

34 "Salt is good. But if salt loses its flavor, how can it be made salty again?

35 "It is not good for the soil or even for the manure pile. People throw it out. Anyone who can hear should listen."
