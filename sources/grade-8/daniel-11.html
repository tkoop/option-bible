Daniel's Vision of Future Kings

1 In the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to support and strengthen him. 2 Now I will tell you the truth. Look, three more kings will stand up for Persia. The fourth will be richer than all of them. When he becomes strong through his riches, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece. 3 Then a mighty king will stand up and rule with great authority. He will do as he pleases. 4 But as soon as he stands up, his kingdom will be broken and split toward the four winds of heaven. It won't go to his children, and it won't be ruled with the power he had, because his kingdom will be uprooted and given to others.

The Struggle Between the North and South

5 The king of the South will grow strong, but one of his commanders will grow stronger than him and rule a greater kingdom. 6 After some years they will make an alliance. The daughter of the king of the South will come to the king of the North to make an agreement, but she won't keep her power. Neither will he and his power last. She will be given up, along with those who brought her, her father, and the one who supported her in those times. 7 But a branch from her roots will stand up in his place. He will come against the army of the king of the North, enter his fortress, fight against them, and overcome. 8 He will also carry off their gods to Egypt, along with their metal images and their valuable vessels of silver and gold. Then for some years he will stay away from the king of the North. 9 Then the king of the North will invade the realm of the king of the South, but he will return to his own land.

10 His sons will stir up war and gather a great army. They will advance, sweep through, and then return. They will stir up war again, all the way to his fortress. 11 The king of the South will be furious. He will go out and fight against the king of the North. The king of the North will raise a great army, but it will be handed over to him. 12 When the army is carried away, his heart will be lifted up. He will throw down tens of thousands, but he won't grow stronger. 13 The king of the North will raise another army, greater than the first. After some years, he will come with a great army and much equipment. 14 In those times, many will stand against the king of the South. Also violent men among your people will lift themselves up to fulfill a vision, but they will fail.

15 Then the king of the North will come. He will build siege ramps and capture a fortified city. The forces of the South won't be able to stand. Even his best troops won't have strength to stand. 16 The one who comes against him will do as he pleases, and no one will stand before him. He will stand in the Beautiful Land, and destruction will be in his hand. 17 He will set his face to come with the strength of his whole kingdom. He will make an agreement with him, and he will give him a daughter in marriage to ruin the kingdom. But she won't stand with him or be for him. 18 After this, he will turn his face to the coastlands and capture many. But a commander will put an end to his taunting. Instead, he will turn the taunt back on him. 19 Then he will turn his face toward the fortresses of his own land. But he will stumble and fall, and he won't be found. 20 Then someone will stand in his place. He will send a tax collector through the glorious kingdom, but within a few days he will be broken, not in anger or in battle.

The Rise of a Contemptible King

21 Then a contemptible person will stand in his place. The honor of the kingdom won't be given to him, but he will come in peacefully and seize the kingdom by flattery. 22 An overwhelming army will be swept away before him. Both it and a prince of the covenant will be broken. 23 After an alliance is made with him, he will act deceitfully. He will become strong with a small nation. 24 In a time of peace, he will invade the richest parts of the province. He will do what his fathers and his fathers' fathers never did. He will scatter plunder, spoil, and wealth among them. He will plan attacks against fortresses, but only for a time.

25 He will stir up his strength and courage against the king of the South with a great army. The king of the South will wage war with an exceedingly great and mighty army, but he won't stand, because plots will be made against him. 26 Those who eat his food will destroy him. His army will be swept away, and many will fall slain. 27 Both kings will have evil in their hearts. They will speak lies at the same table, but it won't succeed, because the end is still set for the appointed time. 28 He will return to his land with great wealth, but his heart will be set against the holy covenant. He will take action and then return to his own land. 29 At the appointed time, he will return and go into the South, but it won't be like the first time or the last.

30 Ships from Kittim will come against him, and he will be discouraged. He will turn back and vent his rage against the holy covenant. He will take action and show favor to those who forsake the holy covenant. 31 His forces will rise up and desecrate the sanctuary fortress. They will remove the daily sacrifice and set up the abomination that causes desolation. 32 With smooth words he will corrupt those who break the covenant, but the people who know their God will be strong and take action. 33 Those who are wise among the people will instruct many, though for a time they will fall by sword and flame, by captivity and plunder. 34 When they fall, they will receive a little help, and many will join them by flattery. 35 Some of the wise will stumble, so that they may be refined, purified, and made white until the time of the end, for it is still for the appointed time.

The King's Arrogance and End

36 The king will do as he pleases. He will exalt and magnify himself above every god. He will speak astonishing things against the God of gods. He will prosper until the wrath is completed, for what is determined will be done. 37 He won't regard the gods of his fathers, or the desire of women, or any god, for he will magnify himself above all. 38 Instead, he will honor a god of fortresses. A god his fathers didn't know, he will honor with gold, silver, precious stones, and valuable things. 39 He will attack the strongest fortresses with the help of a foreign god. He will give great honor to those who acknowledge him. He will make them rulers over many and divide the land for a price.

40 At the time of the end, the king of the South will charge at him. The king of the North will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships. He will invade countries and sweep through them. 41 He will also enter the Beautiful Land, and many countries will fall. But Edom, Moab, and the leaders of the Ammonites will escape from his hand. 42 He will stretch out his hand against the countries. The land of Egypt won't escape. 43 He will gain control of the treasures of gold and silver and all the valuables of Egypt. The Libyans and Cushites will follow at his heels. 44 But news from the east and the north will alarm him. He will go out with great fury to destroy and devote many to destruction. 45 He will pitch his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain. Yet he will come to his end, and no one will help him.
