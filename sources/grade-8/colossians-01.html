Greetings from Paul and Timothy

1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, and Timothy, our brother, 2 to the holy people and faithful brothers and sisters in Christ at Colossae: Grace and peace to you from God our Father.

Thanksgiving and Prayer

3 We always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, when we pray for you. 4 We have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all God's people. 5 This faith and love come from the hope stored up for you in heaven, which you first heard about in the true message, the good news. 6 That good news has come to you. It is bearing fruit and growing all over the world, just as it has been doing among you since the day you heard it and truly understood God's grace. 7 You learned it from Epaphras, our dear fellow servant. He is a faithful helper of Christ for you. 8 He also told us about your love in the Spirit.

9 Because of this, since the day we heard about you, we have not stopped praying for you. We ask that you may be filled with the knowledge of His will, with all spiritual wisdom and understanding. 10 Then you can live in a way that honors the Lord. You can please Him in every way by bearing fruit in every good work and by growing in the knowledge of God. 11 You will be strengthened with all power, according to His glorious might, so you can have great endurance and patience, and you can be joyful. 12 Give thanks to the Father, who has made you able to share in the inheritance of God's people in the light.

13 He has rescued us from the power of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of His dear Son. 14 In Him we have freedom, the forgiveness of sins.

Christ Is Supreme Over All

15 The Son is the image of the invisible God. He is the firstborn over all creation. 16 For in Him all things were created: things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or powers or rulers or authorities. All things were created through Him and for Him. 17 He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together. 18 He is the head of the body, which is the church. He is the beginning and the firstborn from the dead, so that He may have first place in everything. 19 For God was pleased to have all His fullness live in Him. 20 Through Him God made peace and brought all things back to Himself, whether things on earth or things in heaven, by the blood of His cross.

Paul's Ministry for the Church

21 At one time you were far from God and were enemies in your minds because of your evil deeds. 22 But now He has brought you back to Himself through Christ's body and His death. He did this to present you holy, without fault, and blameless before Him. 23 This is true if you keep on believing. Stay strong and steady, and do not move away from the hope of the good news you heard. That good news has been preached to all creation under heaven, and I, Paul, became its servant.

24 Now I am glad to suffer for you. In my body I am filling up what is still needed in Christ's sufferings for His body, which is the church. 25 I became a servant of this message by the job God gave me: to fully share God's word with you. 26 This message was a secret hidden for ages and generations, but now it has been shown to God's holy people. 27 God chose to make known to the nations the glorious riches of this secret. The secret is Christ in you, the hope of glory. 28 We tell people about Him. We warn and teach everyone with all wisdom, so that we can present everyone fully grown in Christ. 29 For this reason I work hard and struggle with all His strength, which works powerfully in me.
