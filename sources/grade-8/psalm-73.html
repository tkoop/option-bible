God Is Good to Israel

1 Surely God is good to Israel, to those with pure hearts. 2 But as for me, my feet almost slipped; my steps nearly gave way.

3 For I envied the proud when I saw how well the wicked were doing. 4 They have no pain when they die; their bodies are well fed. 5 They don't suffer like other people, and they aren't troubled like everyone else. 6 So pride is their necklace, and violence covers them like a robe. 7 Their wealth leads them to sin, and their minds overflow with wild plans. 8 They mock others and speak evil; from their high place they threaten people. 9 They set their mouths against heaven, and their tongues strut through the earth. 10 So their people turn to them and drink in their words. 11 They say, "How can God know? Does the Most High really understand?" 12 Look, these are the wicked—always at ease, they grow richer and richer.

13 Surely I kept my heart pure for nothing; I washed my hands in innocence for no reason. 14 I am troubled all day long, and every morning brings new punishment. 15 If I had said, "I will talk like that," I would have betrayed your children. 16 When I tried to understand all this, it was too hard for me.

17 Then I went into God's holy place, and I understood what would happen to them in the end. 18 Surely You set them on slippery ground; You send them sliding down to ruin. 19 How suddenly they are destroyed, completely swept away by terror! 20 Like a dream when someone wakes up, so when You wake up, O Lord, You will despise their form.

21 When my heart was bitter and I felt pain deep inside, 22 I was stupid and didn't understand; I was like a wild animal before You. 23 Yet I am always with You; You hold my right hand. 24 You guide me with Your advice, and afterward You will bring me into glory.

25 Whom do I have in heaven but You? And on earth I want no one but You. 26 My body and heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my share forever. 27 Those far from You will die; You destroy everyone who is unfaithful to You. 28 But as for me, it is good to be close to God. I have made the Lord, the LORD, my refuge, so I can tell about all Your works.
