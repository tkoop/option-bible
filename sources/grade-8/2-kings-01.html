Ahaziah's Fall and Question

1 After Ahab died, Moab rebelled against Israel. 2 Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upper room in Samaria and was injured. He sent messengers with this order: "Go and ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, if I will recover from this sickness."

Elijah's Message to the King

3 But the angel of the the LORD said to Elijah the Tishbite, "Get up and go meet the messengers of the king of Samaria. Say to them, 'Is it because there is no God in Israel that you go to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron?'"

4 So this is what the the LORD says: "You will not get down from the bed you went up to. You will die." Then Elijah left.

5 The messengers returned to him, and he asked, "Why have you come back?"

6 They said, "A man came up to meet us and said, 'Go back to the king who sent you and say to him, "This is what the the LORD says: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you send to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore you will not get down from the bed you went up to. You will die."'"

7 The king asked, "What did the man look like who met you and said these things to you?"

8 They answered, "He wore a garment of hair with a leather belt around his waist."

The king said, "That is Elijah the Tishbite."

Fire from Heaven

9 Then the king sent a captain with his fifty men to Elijah. The captain went up to him while Elijah was sitting on top of the hill. He said, "Man of God, the king says, 'Come down.'"

10 Elijah answered the captain, "If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and burn you and your fifty men." Then fire came down from heaven and burned him and his fifty men.

11 The king sent another captain with his fifty men. This captain said to Elijah, "Man of God, the king says, 'Come down at once.'"

12 Elijah answered, "If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and burn you and your fifty men." Then God's fire came down from heaven and burned him and his fifty men.

13 The king sent a third captain with his fifty men. The third captain went up, knelt before Elijah, and begged, "Man of God, please let my life and the lives of these fifty servants of yours be important to you.

14 Fire has already come down from heaven and burned the first two captains and their fifties. So please let my life be important to you too."

15 The angel of the the LORD said to Elijah, "Go down with him. Do not be afraid of him." So Elijah got up and went down with him to the king.

Ahaziah's Death

16 Elijah said to the king, "This is what the the LORD says: 'Because you sent messengers to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, is it because there is no God in Israel to ask for His word? So you will not get down from the bed you went up to. You will die.'"

17 So Ahaziah died, just as the the LORD had said through Elijah. Because Ahaziah had no son, Jehoram became king after him. This was in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah.

18 The rest of what Ahaziah did is written in the book of the history of the kings of Israel.
