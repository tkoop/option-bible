Jesus Is Lord of the Sabbath

1 One Sabbath, Jesus was walking through grainfields, and His disciples were picking heads of grain, rubbing them in their hands, and eating them. 2 Some Pharisees asked, "Why are you doing what isn't allowed on the Sabbath?"

3 Jesus answered, "Haven't you read what David did when he and his men were hungry? 4 He went into God's house, took the bread of the Presence, ate it, and gave some to his men. That bread is allowed only for the priests to eat. 5 The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath."

Healing on the Sabbath

6 On another Sabbath, Jesus went into the synagogue and taught. A man was there whose right hand was withered. 7 The scribes and Pharisees watched Him closely to see if He would heal on the Sabbath, so they could accuse Him.

8 But Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said to the man with the withered hand, "Get up and stand here in the middle." The man got up and stood there.

9 Then Jesus said to them, "I ask you: Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do harm, to save a life or to destroy it?"

10 He looked around at all of them and said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." The man did, and his hand was restored.

11 But they were filled with rage and talked with each other about what they might do to Jesus.

Jesus Chooses the Twelve Apostles

12 In those days Jesus went out to a mountain to pray, and He spent the whole night praying to God. 13 When day came, He called His disciples and chose twelve of them, whom He also named apostles: 14 Simon, whom He named Peter, and his brother Andrew; James and John; Philip and Bartholomew; 15 Matthew and Thomas; James son of Alphaeus and Simon called the Zealot; 16 Judas son of James and Judas Iscariot, who became the traitor.

Jesus Teaches on the Plain

17 He came down with them and stood on a level place. A large crowd of His disciples was there, along with a huge crowd from all Judea, Jerusalem, and the coast of Tyre and Sidon. 18 They came to hear Him and to be healed of their diseases. Those troubled by unclean spirits were healed. 19 The whole crowd tried to touch Him, because power came from Him and healed everyone.

20 He looked at His disciples and said, "Blessed are you who are poor, for yours is the kingdom of God. 21 Blessed are you who are hungry now, for you will be filled. Blessed are you who weep now, for you will laugh. 22 Blessed are you when people hate you, avoid you, insult you, and reject your name as evil because of the Son of Man. 23 Rejoice in that day and jump for joy, because your reward is great in heaven. That is how their ancestors treated the prophets.

24 "But woe to you who are rich, for you have already received your comfort. 25 Woe to you who are full now, for you will be hungry. Woe to you who laugh now, for you will mourn and weep. 26 Woe to you when everyone speaks well of you, for that is how their ancestors treated the false prophets.

27 "But I say to you who are listening: Love your enemies. Do good to those who hate you. 28 Bless those who curse you. Pray for those who mistreat you. 29 If someone strikes you on the cheek, offer the other cheek too. If someone takes your coat, don't stop him from taking your shirt. 30 Give to everyone who asks you, and if someone takes what is yours, don't demand it back. 31 Treat others the way you want them to treat you.

32 "If you love those who love you, what credit is that to you? Even sinners love those who love them. 33 If you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is that to you? Even sinners do that. 34 If you lend to those you expect to pay you back, what credit is that to you? Even sinners lend to sinners to get the same amount back. 35 But love your enemies, do good, and lend without expecting anything back. Then your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Most High, because He is kind to the ungrateful and the wicked. 36 Be merciful, just as your Father is merciful.

37 "Don't judge, and you won't be judged. Don't condemn, and you won't be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven. 38 Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over, will be poured into your lap. The way you measure to others is the way it will be measured back to you.

39 He also told them a parable: "Can a blind man lead a blind man? Won't they both fall into a pit? 40 A student is not above his teacher, but everyone who is fully trained will be like his teacher. 41 Why do you see the speck in your brother's eye but fail to notice the log in your own eye? 42 How can you say to your brother, 'Brother, let me take the speck out of your eye,' when you don't even see the log in your own eye? You hypocrite! First take the log out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly enough to take the speck out of your brother's eye.

43 "No good tree makes bad fruit, and no bad tree makes good fruit. 44 Every tree is known by its own fruit. People don't pick figs from thornbushes, and they don't gather grapes from brambles. 45 A good person brings good things out of the good stored in his heart, and an evil person brings evil things out of the evil stored there. What a person says flows from what fills his heart.

46 "Why do you call Me 'Lord, Lord' and not do what I say? 47 I will show you what everyone is like who comes to Me, hears My words, and does them. 48 He is like a man who builds a house. He digs down deep and lays the foundation on rock. When a flood comes and the river beats against that house, it can't shake it, because it was well built. 49 But the one who hears My words and doesn't do them is like a man who builds a house on the ground without a foundation. When the river beats against it, it collapses at once, and the ruin of that house is great."
