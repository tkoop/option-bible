Land Allotted to Manasseh

1 This was the land share for the tribe of Manasseh, because he was Joseph's firstborn. Makir, Manasseh's firstborn and father of Gilead, received Gilead and Bashan because he was a warrior. 2 Land was also given to the rest of Manasseh's family groups: the families of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, listed by their family groups.

Land for Zelophehad's Daughters

3 Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 4 They came to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders. They said, "The LORD told Moses to give us land among our brothers." So Joshua gave them land among their father's brothers, just as the LORD had commanded.

5 Besides Gilead and Bashan east of the Jordan, Manasseh received ten shares of land. 6 Manasseh's daughters received land among his sons. The land of Gilead belonged to the rest of Manasseh's sons.

Borders of Manasseh's Land

7 Manasseh's border started at Asher and went to Michmethath, east of Shechem. Then it turned south to the people who lived at En Tappuah. 8 The land around Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but the town of Tappuah on Manasseh's border belonged to Ephraim. 9 The border went down to the Kanah Valley. The towns south of the valley belonged to Ephraim, even though they were among Manasseh's towns. Manasseh's border was north of the valley and ended at the sea. 10 The land south of the border belonged to Ephraim, and the land north belonged to Manasseh. The sea was their western border. To the north they touched Asher, and to the east they touched Issachar.

11 In Issachar and Asher, Manasseh had Beth Shan, Ibleam, Dor, Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo, along with their surrounding villages. 12 But Manasseh could not drive out the people of those cities. The Canaanites kept living in that land. 13 Later, when the Israelites grew strong, they made the Canaanites work for them, but they did not drive them out completely.

Joseph's Tribes Ask for More Land

14 The descendants of Joseph spoke to Joshua. They said, "Why did you give us only one share of land? We are a large people, and the LORD has blessed us greatly."

15 Joshua said, "If you are a large people, go up into the forest and clear land for yourselves in the land of the Perizzites and Rephaites, because the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you."

16 "The hill country is not enough for us," the descendants of Joseph replied. "All the Canaanites in the lowlands have iron chariots. This is true in Beth Shan and its villages, and it is true in the Jezreel Valley."

17 Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are a large and powerful people. You will not have just one share of land. 18 The hill country will be yours. Clear it, and its farthest borders will be yours. You can drive out the Canaanites, even though they have iron chariots and are strong."
