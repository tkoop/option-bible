Judah's Plan to Trust Egypt

1 "Woe to the rebellious children," declares the LORD. "You carry out a plan that is not Mine. You make an agreement, but not by My Spirit, so you keep adding sin to sin. 2 You go down to Egypt without asking Me. You seek safety in Pharaoh's protection and hide in Egypt's shadow. 3 But Pharaoh's protection will become your shame, and Egypt's shelter will become your disgrace. 4 Even though his officials are in Zoan and his messengers have reached Hanes, 5 everyone will be put to shame by a people that cannot help them. They bring no help and no gain, only shame and disgrace."

The Hard Journey to Egypt

6 This is a message about the animals of the southern desert: The people carry their wealth on donkeys' backs and their treasures on camels' humps. They travel through a hard and dangerous land, where lions and young lions live and vipers and flying serpents are found. They go to a people who cannot help them. 7 Egypt's help is empty and useless. That is why I call her "Rahab the Do-Nothing." 8 Now go and write this on a tablet in front of them. Write it in a book so it will stand as a witness for the future, forever.

A Rebellious People

9 These people are rebellious. They are deceitful children who refuse to listen to the LORD's teaching. 10 They tell the seers, "Do not see visions," and the prophets, "Do not tell us what is right. Speak smooth words to us. Prophesy pleasant lies. 11 Get out of the way! Turn off the road! Keep the Holy One of Israel away from us."

God Warns and Promises Mercy

12 So the Holy One of Israel says, "Because you have rejected this message and relied on oppression and deceit, 13 this wrong of yours is like a wall about to fall. It bulges out and will break apart all at once, in a single moment. 14 It will shatter like a clay jar smashed to pieces, so completely that no broken piece will be left big enough to scoop coals from a fire or draw water from a well."

15 The Lord the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, has said, "By turning back and resting, you would be saved. Your strength would be quiet trust. But you were not willing. 16 You said you would flee on horses, so you will flee. You said you would ride fast horses, so your pursuers will be fast. 17 A thousand of you will run from one enemy. Five enemies will make you run, until you are left like a pole on a mountaintop or a flag on a hill."

18 So the LORD waits to be kind to you. He rises to show you mercy. The LORD is a God of justice. Blessed are all who wait for Him. 19 O people in Zion, who live in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. When you cry for help, He will be kind to you. When He hears, He will answer you. 20 The Lord will give you bread of hardship and water of trouble, but your Teacher will not hide Himself anymore. Your eyes will see your Teacher. 21 Whether you turn right or left, your ears will hear a voice behind you saying, "This is the way. Walk in it."

22 Then you will defile your silver-plated idols and your gold-covered statues. You will throw them away like dirty rags. You will say to them, "Get out of here!"

23 Then He will send rain for the seed you sow in the ground, and the food the land produces will be rich and plentiful. On that day your cattle will graze in wide pastures. 24 The oxen and donkeys that work the soil will eat salted fodder, winnowed with shovel and fork. 25 On every high mountain and every hill, streams of water will flow on the day of great slaughter, when the towers fall. 26 The moon's light will be like the sun's light, and the sun's light will be seven times brighter, like the light of seven days. This will happen on the day the LORD binds up His people's brokenness and heals the wounds He gave them.

God's Judgment on Assyria

27 Look, the name of the LORD comes from far away. His anger burns, and thick smoke comes with Him. His lips are full of fury, and His tongue is like a fire that eats everything. 28 His breath is like a rushing flood that rises to the neck. He comes to sift the nations in a sieve that destroys, and He puts a bridle on the peoples' jaws to lead them astray. 29 You will sing as on the night of a holy feast. Your heart will rejoice like someone walking to the sound of a flute, going up to the mountain of the LORD, to the Rock of Israel. 30 The LORD will make His glorious voice heard. He will show His arm coming down with fierce anger, with a flame of fire that consumes, with cloudburst, storm, and hailstones.

31 Assyria will be terrified at the voice of the LORD, when He strikes with His rod. 32 Every stroke of the rod He appoints will be to the sound of tambourines and harps. He will fight them with His whirling weapons. 33 Topheth has been ready for a long time. It was prepared for the king. Its fire pit is deep and wide, with plenty of fire and wood. The breath of the LORD, like a stream of burning sulfur, sets it on fire.
