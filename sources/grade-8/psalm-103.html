David Praises the LORD

1 Bless the LORD, O my soul; with all my heart, bless His holy name. 2 Bless the LORD, O my soul, and don't forget all His blessings.

3 He forgives all your sins; He heals all your diseases. 4 He rescues your life from destruction; He crowns you with faithful love and compassion. 5 He fills your years with good things, so your youth is renewed like the eagle's.

The LORD's Compassion for His People

6 The LORD does what is right and fair for all who are treated unfairly. 7 He showed Moses His ways and the people of Israel His deeds. 8 The LORD is kind and gracious, slow to anger, and full of faithful love. 9 He will not always accuse, and He will not hold His anger forever. 10 He has not treated us as our sins deserve, and He has not paid us back for our wrongs.

11 For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His faithful love for those who fear Him. 12 As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our wrongdoings from us. 13 As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him. 14 For He knows how weak we are; He remembers that we are dust.

15 A person's days are like grass; he blooms like a wildflower. 16 When the wind blows over it, it is gone, and its place no longer remembers it. 17 But the faithful love of the LORD lasts forever for those who fear Him, and His goodness reaches to their children and grandchildren. 18 It is for those who keep His promise and remember to obey His commands.

All Creation Praises the LORD

19 The LORD has set up His throne in heaven, and His kingdom rules over all. 20 Bless the LORD, you His angels, mighty in strength, who do what He says and listen to His word. 21 Bless the LORD, all you His heavenly armies, His servants who do His will. 22 Bless the LORD, all His works, in every place He rules. Bless the LORD, O my soul.
