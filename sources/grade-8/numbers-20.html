Miriam Dies at Kadesh

1 In the first month, the whole Israelite community came to the Desert of Zin and stayed at Kadesh. There Miriam died and was buried. 2 There was no water for the community, so they gathered against Moses and Aaron. 3 The people argued with Moses and said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before the LORD! 4 Why did you bring the LORD's people into this desert to die here along with our animals? 5 Why did you bring us up out of Egypt to bring us to this awful place? It is not a place for grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink."

The LORD Gives Water from the Rock

6 Moses and Aaron left the assembly and went to the entrance of the tent of meeting. They fell facedown, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them. 7 The LORD spoke to Moses: 8 "Take the staff and gather the community, you and your brother Aaron. Speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give its water. You will bring water out of the rock for them and give the community and their animals a drink." 9 So Moses took the staff from before the LORD, as He had commanded him.

10 Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and Moses said to them, "Listen now, you rebels! Must we bring water out of this rock for you?"

11 Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff. Water gushed out, and the community and their animals drank.

12 But the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not trust Me enough to honor Me as holy before the Israelites, you will not lead this community into the land I gave them." 13 These were the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites quarreled with the LORD, and He showed His holiness among them.

Edom Refuses Israel Passage

14 Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "This is what your brother Israel says: You know all the hardship we have faced. 15 Our ancestors went down to Egypt, and we lived there many years. The Egyptians mistreated us and our ancestors. 16 We cried out to the LORD, and He heard our voice. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now we are at Kadesh, a town on the edge of your territory. 17 Please let us pass through your land. We will not go through fields or vineyards, and we will not drink water from your wells. We will stay on the king's road and not turn aside to the right or left until we have passed through your territory."

18 But Edom answered, "You may not pass through my land, or I will come out against you with the sword."

19 The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the main road. If we or our livestock drink your water, we will pay for it. We only want to pass through on foot—nothing else."

20 Edom said, "You shall not pass through." Then Edom came out against them with a large and powerful army. 21 So Edom refused to let Israel pass through his territory, and Israel turned away from him.

Aaron Dies on Mount Hor

22 The whole Israelite community set out from Kadesh and came to Mount Hor. 23 At Mount Hor, near the border of Edom, the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 24 "Aaron will die and join his ancestors, because he will not enter the land I gave the Israelites. You both rebelled against My command at the waters of Meribah. 25 Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and bring them up Mount Hor. 26 Remove Aaron's garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will die there."

27 Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole community. 28 Moses removed Aaron's garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron died there on top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down the mountain. 29 When the whole community saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.
