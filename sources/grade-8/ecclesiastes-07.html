A Good Name and the Day of Death

1 A good name is better than fine perfume, and the day of death is better than the day of birth. 2 It is better to go to a house of mourning than to go to a house of feasting, because death is the end of every person, and the living should take that to heart. 3 Sorrow is better than laughter, because a sad face can make the heart better. 4 The heart of wise people is in the house of mourning, but the heart of fools is in the house of pleasure.

Wisdom and Folly

5 It is better to listen to a wise person's warning than to listen to the songs of fools. 6 For the laughter of fools is like the crackling of thorns under a pot. That too is meaningless. 7 Oppression can make a wise person foolish, and a bribe can ruin the heart. 8 The end of a matter is better than its beginning, and patience is better than pride. 9 Do not be quick to get angry in your spirit, because anger rests in the lap of fools. 10 Do not say, "Why were the old days better than these?" For you do not ask that out of wisdom. 11 Wisdom is good when it comes with an inheritance, and it helps those who see the sun. 12 Wisdom is a shelter, just as money is a shelter, but the advantage of knowledge is that wisdom keeps its owner alive.

God's Work and Human Limits

13 Look at the work of God: Who can straighten what He has made crooked? 14 When times are good, be happy; when times are bad, think about this: God made both, so no one can know what will come next. 15 In my meaningless life I have seen both of these: a righteous person can die even though they are righteous, and a wicked person can live a long time in wickedness. 16 Do not be too good, and do not make yourself too wise. Why would you ruin yourself? 17 Do not be too wicked, and do not be a fool. Why would you die before your time? 18 It is good to hold on to one and not let the other slip from your hand, because the person who fears God will follow both warnings. 19 Wisdom makes a wise person stronger than ten rulers in a city. 20 Surely there is no one on earth who always does good and never sins. 21 Do not listen to everything people say, or you may hear your servant cursing you. 22 For you know in your heart that you too have cursed others many times.

The Search for Wisdom

23 I tested all of this with wisdom. I said, "I will be wise," but it was beyond me. 24 What exists is far away and very deep. Who can find it? 25 I turned my mind to know, to study, and to seek wisdom and an explanation. I also wanted to understand the foolishness of wickedness and the madness of folly. 26 I find a woman whose heart is a trap and whose hands are chains more bitter than death. The person who pleases God will escape her, but the sinner will be caught by her. 27 "Look," says the Teacher, "I have found this by adding one thing to another to find an explanation." 28 While I was still searching but not finding, I found one upright man among a thousand, but among all these I did not find one upright woman. 29 See, I have found only this: God made people upright, but they have looked for many schemes.
