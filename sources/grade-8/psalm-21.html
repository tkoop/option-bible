The King Rejoices in the LORD's Strength

1 O the LORD, the king rejoices in Your strength. How greatly he exults in Your salvation! 2 You have granted his heart’s desire and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah.

3 You welcome him with rich blessings and place a crown of pure gold on his head. 4 He asked You for life, and You gave it to him—length of days forever and ever. 5 His glory is great because of Your salvation; You have given him honor and majesty. 6 You bless him forever, and You fill him with joy by Your presence.

7 The king trusts in the LORD, and because of the faithful love of the Most High, he will not be shaken. 8 Your hand will catch all Your enemies; Your right hand will seize those who hate You.

9 You will make them like a hot oven when You appear. In His wrath the the LORD will swallow them up, and fire will consume them. 10 You will destroy their children from the earth and their descendants from among people. 11 They planned evil against You and made a plot, but they cannot carry it out. 12 You will make them run away, and You will aim Your arrows at them.

13 Be lifted high, O the LORD, in Your strength! We will sing and praise Your power.
