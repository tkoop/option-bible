Praise the LORD and Tell of His Wonders

1 Give thanks to the LORD. Call on His name. Make His deeds known among the nations. 2 Sing to Him; sing praise to Him. Tell about all His wonderful acts. 3 Glory in His holy name. Let the hearts of those who seek the LORD be glad. 4 Seek the LORD and His strength; seek His face always. 5 Remember the wonders He has done, His miracles, and the judgments He spoke.

The LORD's Covenant with Abraham

6 Descendants of Abraham, His servant, and sons of Jacob, His chosen ones. 7 He is the LORD our God. His judgments are over all the earth. 8 He remembers His covenant forever—the word He commanded for a thousand generations. 9 He made this covenant with Abraham and swore an oath to Isaac. 10 He made it a law for Jacob and an everlasting covenant for Israel. 11 He said, "I will give you the land of Canaan as your inheritance."

12 When they were only a few people—very few—and strangers in that land, 13 they wandered from nation to nation and from one kingdom to another. 14 He let no one oppress them. For their sake He warned kings: 15 "Do not touch My anointed ones; do no harm to My prophets."

Joseph Sent Ahead to Egypt

16 He called famine on the land and broke every supply of bread. 17 He sent a man ahead of them—Joseph, who was sold as a slave. 18 They hurt his feet with chains, and iron pierced his soul. 19 He stayed there until the time came for the LORD's word to come true; His promise tested him.

20 Then the king sent for him and set him free; the ruler of the peoples released him. 21 He made Joseph master of his house and ruler over all he owned. 22 He did this so Joseph could guide the king's officials as he pleased and teach wisdom to the elders.

From Egypt to the Promised Land

23 Then Israel came to Egypt; Jacob lived as a foreigner in the land of Ham. 24 The LORD made His people grow very strong and more numerous than their enemies. 25 He turned the enemies' hearts to hate His people and to plot against His servants.

26 He sent Moses, His servant, and Aaron, whom He had chosen. 27 They did His signs and wonders in the land of Ham. 28 He sent darkness, and it became dark; they did not disobey His words. 29 He turned their water into blood and killed their fish. 30 Their land swarmed with frogs, even in the royal bedrooms. 31 He spoke, and flies came; gnats spread through all their country.

32 He gave them hail instead of rain, and lightning flashed across their land. 33 He struck their grapevines and fig trees and broke the trees throughout their country. 34 He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts too many to count. 35 They ate every plant in the land and the fruit of the soil. 36 He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the first and best of their strength.

37 He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and no one among His tribes was weak. 38 Egypt was glad when they left, because fear of Israel had fallen on them. 39 He spread a cloud to cover them and fire to give light at night. 40 They asked, and He brought quail; He filled them with bread from heaven. 41 He opened the rock, and water poured out; it ran like a river in the dry land.

42 He did this because He remembered His holy promise to Abraham, His servant. 43 So He brought out His people with joy and His chosen ones with singing. 44 He gave them the lands of the nations, and they took over what other peoples had worked for. 45 He did this so they would keep His laws and obey His teachings. Praise the LORD!
