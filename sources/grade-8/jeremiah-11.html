The Broken Covenant

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the the LORD, saying: 2 "Listen to the words of this covenant, and speak them to the men of Judah and to the people living in Jerusalem. Tell them, 'This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "Cursed is the man who does not obey the words of this covenant—the covenant I commanded your fathers when I brought them out of Egypt, out of the iron furnace. I said, 'Obey My voice, and do everything I command you. Then you will be My people, and I will be your God.' This is so I can keep the promise I made to your fathers, to give them a rich and fertile land, as it is today.'"

Then I answered, "Amen, the LORD."

Proclaim the Covenant in Judah

6 The The LORD said to me, "Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem: 'Listen to the words of this covenant and do them.' 7 From the day I brought your fathers out of Egypt until today, I warned them again and again, saying, 'Obey My voice.' 8 But they did not listen or pay attention. Each man followed his stubborn, evil heart. So I brought on them all the curses of this covenant, which I commanded them to obey, but they did not."

A Conspiracy in Judah

9 The The LORD said to me, "A conspiracy has been found among the men of Judah and the people of Jerusalem. 10 They have turned back to the sins of their fathers, who refused to listen to My words. They have followed other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken My covenant, which I made with their fathers."

Judgment on Idolatry

11 Therefore this is what the the LORD says: "I am bringing disaster on them that they cannot escape. They will cry out to Me, but I will not listen to them. 12 The cities of Judah and the people of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods they burn incense to, but those gods will not save them in their time of trouble. 13 Judah, you have as many gods as you have cities, and Jerusalem has as many altars as its streets—altars you people have set up to burn incense to that shameful thing, to Baal."

Do Not Pray for This People

14 "So do not pray for these people. Do not lift up a cry or prayer for them, because I will not listen when they call to Me in their trouble. 15 What is My beloved doing in My house? She has done many evil things. Can holy meat take away your guilt? When you do evil, you are happy. 16 The The LORD called your name, 'Green Olive Tree, Beautiful with Fruit.' But with the roar of a great storm He will set fire to it, and its branches will be broken. 17 The The LORD of Hosts, who planted you, has spoken disaster against you because of the evil of the house of Israel and the house of Judah. They made Me angry by burning incense to Baal."

A Plot Against Jeremiah

18 The The LORD made it known to me, and I knew. Then He showed me what they were doing. 19 I was like a gentle lamb led to be killed. I did not know they had planned against me, saying, "Let us destroy him and everything he has. Let us kill him so his name will be forgotten." 20 But the the LORD of Hosts judges fairly. He tests the heart and mind. Let me see Your vengeance on them, for I have brought my case to You.

21 Therefore this is what the the LORD says about the men of Anathoth, who seek your life and say, "Do not prophesy in the name of the the LORD, or you will die by our hand"— 22 this is what the the LORD of Hosts says: "I will punish them. The young men will die by the sword. Their sons and daughters will die by famine. 23 No one will be left, because I will bring disaster on the men of Anathoth in the year of their punishment."
