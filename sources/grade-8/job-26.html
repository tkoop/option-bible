Job Replies to Bildad

1 Then Job answered:

Questions for Bildad

2 “How have you helped the powerless? How have you saved the weak arm? 3 What advice have you given to someone without wisdom? What knowledge have you shown so clearly? 4 To whom have you spoken these words? Whose breath came from you?”

God's Power Over Creation

5 “The dead tremble beneath the waters, and so do those who live there. 6 Sheol is naked before Him, and Abaddon has no cover.

7 “He stretches out the north over empty space; He hangs the earth on nothing. 8 He wraps the waters in His thick clouds, but the clouds do not burst under their weight. 9 He covers the face of the full moon, spreading His cloud over it. 10 He has marked a boundary on the water's surface, where light and darkness meet.

11 “The pillars of heaven tremble and are amazed at His rebuke. 12 By His power He stilled the sea; by His wisdom He shattered Rahab. 13 By His breath the skies were cleared; His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

The Mystery of God's Power

14 “Indeed, these are only the edges of His ways. What a faint whisper we hear of Him! Who can understand the thunder of His power?”
