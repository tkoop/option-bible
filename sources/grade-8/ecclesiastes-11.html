Be Generous and Plan Ahead

1 Be generous with what you have, because after many days you will see good come from it. 2 Spread your resources among seven or even eight projects, since you do not know what trouble may come to the land.

3 If clouds fill with water, they pour rain on the earth. A tree may fall to the south or to the north, but it stays where it falls. 4 If you wait for perfect weather, you will never plant or harvest.

Trust God's Hidden Work

5 You do not know how the wind moves or how a baby forms in the womb, so you cannot understand the work of God, who makes everything. 6 Sow your seed in the morning, and do not let your hands rest in the evening, because you do not know which effort will succeed—this one, that one, or both.

Enjoy Life, but Remember God's Judgment

7 Light is sweet, and it is good for the eyes to see the sun. 8 If a person lives many years, he should enjoy all of them, but he should remember the dark days, because they will be many. Everything that is coming is meaningless.

9 Be happy, young man, while you are young, and let your heart give you joy in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways your heart chooses and in what your eyes see, but know that God will bring you into judgment for all these things. 10 Remove sorrow from your heart, and put away pain from your body, because youth and strength are meaningless.
