Jesus Teaches by the Sea

1 Jesus began to teach by the sea again. A very large crowd gathered around Him, so He got into a boat on the water and sat there, while all the crowd stood on the shore. 2 He taught them many things in parables. In His teaching He said,

3 "Listen! A farmer went out to sow seed. 4 As he sowed, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and ate it up. 5 Other seed fell on rocky ground, where it did not have much soil. It sprang up quickly because the soil was shallow. 6 But when the sun came up, it was scorched and withered because it had no root. 7 Other seed fell among thorns. The thorns grew up and choked it, and it gave no fruit. 8 Still other seed fell on good soil. It grew, increased, and produced a crop—some thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred times what was sown. 9 Whoever has ears to hear, let them hear."

Jesus Explains the Parables

10 When He was alone, the Twelve and the others around Him asked Him about the parables. 11 He said to them, "The mystery of the kingdom of God has been given to you, but to those outside everything comes in parables. 12 That way, 'They may look and look but not see; they may listen and listen but not understand. Otherwise they might turn and be forgiven.' 13 Then He said to them, "Do you not understand this parable? Then how will you understand any parable?"

14 "The sower sows the word. 15 These are the ones along the path where the word is sown: as soon as they hear it, Satan comes and takes away the word that was sown in them. 16 In the same way, these are the ones sown on rocky places. They hear the word and receive it with joy right away. 17 But they have no root in themselves, so they last only a short time. When trouble or persecution comes because of the word, they fall away at once. 18 Others are sown among thorns. They are the ones who hear the word, 19 but the worries of this life, the deceitfulness of wealth, and the desire for other things come in and choke the word, so it becomes unfruitful. 20 And these are the ones sown on good soil: they hear the word, accept it, and produce fruit—some thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred times."

21 "Does a lamp come in to be put under a basket or under a bed? No—it is put on a lampstand. 22 For nothing is hidden except to be brought into the open, and nothing is kept secret except to come to light. 23 Whoever has ears to hear, let them hear." 24 "Pay attention to what you hear. The measure you use will be measured to you, and even more will be added to you. 25 For whoever has will be given more, but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away."

More Parables About God's Kingdom

26 "The kingdom of God is like this: a man scatters seed on the ground. 27 Night and day he sleeps and gets up, and the seed sprouts and grows, though he does not know how. 28 The earth produces a crop by itself—first the blade, then the head, then the full grain in the head. 29 As soon as the grain is ripe, he cuts it with a sickle, because the harvest has come."

30 "What can we compare the kingdom of God to? What parable shall we use for it? 31 It is like a mustard seed. When it is sown, it is the smallest of all seeds on earth. 32 Yet when it is sown, it grows and becomes the largest of all garden plants. It puts out large branches, so the birds of the air can nest in its shade."

33 With many parables like these, He spoke the word to them, as much as they could understand. 34 He did not speak to them without a parable, but in private He explained everything to His own disciples.

Jesus Calms the Storm

35 That same day, when evening came, He said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side." 36 Leaving the crowd behind, they took Him along, just as He was, in the boat. Other boats were with Him.

37 A furious windstorm came up, and the waves broke over the boat, so that it was nearly swamped. 38 Jesus was in the back of the boat, sleeping on a cushion. They woke Him and said to Him, "Teacher, don't you care that we are dying?"

39 He got up, rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, "Peace! Be still!" The wind died down, and there was a great calm.

40 He said to them, "Why are you afraid? Do you still have no faith?"

41 They were terrified and asked one another, "Who is this? Even the wind and the sea obey Him!"
