Commands for the Promised Land

1 These are the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that the the LORD your God commanded me to teach you, so you can obey them in the land you are crossing over to possess, 2 so that you, your children, and your grandchildren may fear the the LORD your God all the days of your life and keep all His statutes and commands that I am giving you today, so that your days may be long. 3 Listen, Israel, and be careful to do them, so that things go well for you and you multiply greatly in a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the the LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you.

The Greatest Commandment

4 Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the the LORD is One. 5 You shall love the the LORD your God with all your heart, all your soul, and all your strength. 6 These words that I command you today are to be on your heart. 7 Teach them carefully to your children. Talk about them when you sit in your house, when you walk along the road, when you lie down, and when you get up. 8 Tie them as a sign on your hand, and let them be reminders between your eyes. 9 Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

Remembering the LORD's Goodness

10 When the the LORD your God brings you into the land He swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—to give you, you will find large and good cities that you did not build, 11 along with houses full of good things you did not fill, wells you did not dig, and vineyards and olive groves you did not plant. Then you will eat and be full. 12 Be careful not to forget the the LORD, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery. 13 Fear the the LORD your God and serve Him, and swear by His name. 14 Do not follow other gods, the gods of the peoples around you.

15 For the the LORD your God among you is a jealous God. If His anger burns against you, the the LORD your God will wipe you from the face of the earth. 16 Do not test the the LORD your God, as you tested Him at Massah. 17 Carefully obey the commandments of the the LORD your God, along with His testimonies and statutes that He commanded you. 18 Do what is right and good in the the LORD's sight, so that things go well for you and you take possession of the good land the the LORD promised to your fathers. 19 He will drive out all your enemies before you, just as the the LORD has said.

Teaching Your Children

20 In the future, when your son asks you, "What do these testimonies, statutes, and ordinances mean that the the LORD our God commanded you?"

21 Tell him, "We were slaves to Pharaoh in Egypt, but the the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

"22 The The LORD showed great and terrible signs and wonders in Egypt against Pharaoh and all his household, right before our eyes. 23 He brought us out of there to bring us into this land and give it to us, the land He promised to our fathers.

"24 The The LORD commanded us to obey all these statutes and to fear the the LORD our God, so that things will always go well for us and we will live, as we do today. 25 If we are careful to obey all this command before the the LORD our God, as He commanded us, it will be our righteousness."
