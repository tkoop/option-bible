Jacob Sends His Sons to Egypt

1 When Jacob saw that there was grain in Egypt, he said to his sons, "Why are you just staring at each other? 2 I heard there is grain in Egypt. Go down there and buy some for us, so we can live and not die."

3 So ten of Joseph's brothers went down to buy grain from Egypt. 4 But Jacob did not send Benjamin, Joseph's brother, with them, because he said, "Something bad might happen to him." 5 The sons of Israel came with the others who were buying grain, because the famine was also in Canaan.

Joseph Recognizes His Brothers

6 Now Joseph was the ruler of the land, and he sold grain to everyone. When Joseph's brothers arrived, they bowed down to him with their faces to the ground.

7 Joseph saw his brothers and recognized them, but he acted like a stranger and spoke harshly to them. "Where do you come from?" he asked.

They answered, "From the land of Canaan, to buy food."

8 Joseph recognized his brothers, but they did not recognize him.

9 Joseph remembered the dreams he had had about them and said, "You are spies! You came to find weak spots in our land."

10 "No, my lord," they said. "Your servants came to buy food. 11 We are all sons of one man. We are honest men; your servants are not spies."

12 "No," he said. "You came to find weak spots in our land."

13 They said, "Your servants are twelve brothers, sons of one man in Canaan. The youngest is with our father today, and one is gone."

14 Joseph said to them, "It is just as I said: you are spies."

Joseph Tests His Brothers

15 Joseph said, "By this you will be tested: by the life of Pharaoh, you will not leave here unless your youngest brother comes here. 16 Send one of you to get your brother, while the rest are kept in custody, so your words may be tested and we can see if you are telling the truth. If not, by the life of Pharaoh, you are spies."

17 He put them all in custody for three days.

18 On the third day, Joseph said to them, "Do this and live, for I fear God. 19 If you are honest men, let one brother be kept in prison while you go and take grain to feed your families. 20 But bring your youngest brother to me, so your words can be proven true and you will not die." And they did so.

21 Then they said to one another, "Truly we are guilty because of our brother. We saw his trouble when he begged us, but we would not listen. That is why this trouble has come on us."

22 Reuben answered, "Didn't I tell you, 'Do not sin against the boy'? But you would not listen. Now we must answer for his blood."

23 They did not know Joseph understood them, because an interpreter was between them.

24 He turned away from them and wept. Then he came back and spoke to them. He took Simeon from them and tied him up in front of them.

25 Joseph gave orders to fill their bags with grain, to return each man's silver to his sack, and to give them food for the journey. They did as he said. 26 They loaded the grain on their donkeys and left.

27 At the place where they stopped for the night, one of them opened his sack to feed his donkey and saw his silver at the top of the sack. 28 He said to his brothers, "My silver has been returned! Here it is in my sack!"

They panicked and trembled. They said to one another, "What has God done to us?"

The Brothers Return to Jacob

29 When they reached their father Jacob in Canaan, they told him everything that had happened. They said, 30 "The man who rules the land spoke harshly to us and treated us like spies. 31 We said to him, 'We are honest men; we are not spies. 32 We are twelve brothers, sons of one father. One is gone, and the youngest is with our father in Canaan.' 33 Then the man who rules the land said, 'By this I will know you are honest: leave one brother with me, take grain for the hunger in your homes, and go. 34 Bring your youngest brother to me, and then I will know you are not spies but honest men. I will give your brother back to you, and you can trade in the land.'"

35 As they emptied their sacks, each man found his bundle of silver in his sack. When they and their father saw the silver, they were afraid.

36 Their father Jacob said to them, "You have taken my children from me. Joseph is gone, Simeon is gone, and now you want to take Benjamin. Everything is against me."

37 Reuben said to his father, "You can kill my two sons if I do not bring Benjamin back to you. Put him in my care, and I will bring him back to you."

38 Jacob said, "My son will not go down with you, for his brother is dead and he is the only one left. If something bad happens to him on the journey you are taking, you will send me to my grave in grief."
