Job Promises to Keep His Eyes Pure

1 I made a promise to my eyes not to look at a young woman with lust. How could I stare at her? 2 Otherwise, what blessing could I expect from God above? What gift would the Almighty send from heaven? 3 Doesn't disaster come to the wicked, and ruin to those who do wrong? 4 Doesn't He see everything I do and count every step I take?

Job's Warning Against Adultery and Cheating

5 If I have lived by lies, or my feet have rushed to cheat others, 6 let God weigh me on fair scales and know that I am innocent. 7 If I have wandered off the right path, or let my heart chase what my eyes wanted, or if guilt has stuck to my hands, 8 then let someone else eat what I plant, and let my children be torn up by the roots.

9 If my heart has been led astray by a woman, or I have lurked at my neighbor's door, 10 then let my wife serve another man, and let others sleep with her. 11 That would be a terrible sin, a crime for judges to punish. 12 It is like a fire that burns everything up and destroys all I have earned.

Job's Care for Servants and the Needy

13 If I have ignored the complaints of my servants, male or female, 14 what will I do when God rises to judge me? When He questions me, what will I answer? 15 Didn't the God who made me in the womb make them too? Didn't the same God form us both?

16 If I have kept the poor from what they needed, or made a widow lose hope; 17 if I have eaten my food alone and not shared it with an orphan, 18 even though I have cared for the orphan like a father since I was young, and guided the widow all my life— 19 if I have seen someone freeze for lack of clothes, or a poor person with nothing to cover them; 20 and they were not warmed by wool from my sheep, 21 if I have raised my hand against an orphan because I had powerful friends in court, 22 then let my arm fall from my shoulder and break at the joint. 23 For I was terrified of disaster from God, and I could not stand against His greatness.

Job's Final Defense Before God

24 If I have trusted in gold, or called fine gold my safety; 25 if I have been glad just because I was rich or because I gained a lot; 26 if I have admired the sun in its brightness or the moon in its glory, 27 and my heart was secretly drawn to them, and I kissed my hand to them in worship, 28 that too would be a crime for judges to punish, because I would have denied the God above.

29 If I have been glad when my enemy was ruined, or felt proud when trouble hit him, 30 I have not let my mouth sin by asking God to curse his life. 31 The men in my tent never complained that they weren't filled with my food. 32 No stranger had to sleep in the street, because I opened my doors to travelers.

33 If I have hidden my sins like other people, keeping my guilt secret in my heart, 34 because I was afraid of the crowd and dreaded the scorn of families, so I kept quiet and stayed inside— 35 if only someone would hear me! Here is my signature; let the Almighty answer me, and let my accuser write his charge. 36 Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown. 37 I would tell Him every step I have taken; I would go to Him like a prince. 38 If my land accuses me and its furrows weep with it, 39 if I have eaten its crops without paying, or worn out the people who worked it, 40 then let thorns grow instead of wheat, and weeds instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.
