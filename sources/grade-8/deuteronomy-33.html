Moses Blesses Israel

1 This is the blessing Moses, the man of God, spoke to the Israelites before he died.

The Lord Comes with His Law

2 He said, “The LORD came from Sinai. He rose over them from Seir. He shone from Mount Paran. He came with thousands of holy ones, and fire burned at His right hand. 3 Surely You love the people; all Your holy ones are in Your hand. They bow at Your feet and receive Your words. 4 Moses gave us the law, an inheritance for Jacob's people. 5 The LORD was king in Jeshurun when the leaders of the people gathered, the tribes of Israel together.”

Blessings for the Tribes

6 “Let Reuben live and not die, and let his people be many.”

7 And this he said about Judah: “Hear, O the LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. His hands are strong for him, and be his help against his foes.”

8 About Levi he said, “Your Thummim and Your Urim belong to Your faithful man, the one You tested at Massah and challenged at the waters of Meribah.” He said about his father and mother that he did not recognize them, and he did not know his brothers or acknowledge his own children. For they kept Your word and guarded Your covenant. 10 They will teach Your laws to Jacob and Your instructions to Israel. They will offer incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar. 11 “Bless, O the LORD, his strength, and accept the work of his hands. Crush those who rise against him and those who hate him, so they cannot rise again.”

12 About Benjamin he said, “The LORD's loved one rests safely with Him. The LORD covers him all day and rests between his shoulders.”

13 About Joseph he said, “May the LORD bless his land with the best from heaven, with dew, and with water from the deep below, 14 with the best the sun can give and the best each month brings, 15 with the best from the ancient mountains and the best from the everlasting hills, 16 with the best the earth can give and all that fills it, and with the favor of the One who lived in the bush. May these blessings rest on Joseph's head, on the crown of the one set apart among his brothers. 17 His bull has great strength, and its horns are like a wild ox's horns. With them he will push the peoples to the ends of the earth. They are the thousands of Ephraim and the ten thousands of Manasseh.”

18 About Zebulun he said, “Rejoice, Zebulun, when you go out, and Issachar, in your tents. 19 They will call the peoples to the mountain and offer right sacrifices there. For they will feed on the wealth of the seas and on the treasures hidden in the sand.”

20 About Gad he said, “Blessed is the one who expands Gad's territory. Gad lives like a lion and tears off arm and head. 21 He chose the best land for himself, for a leader's portion was kept there. He came with the leaders of the people and carried out the LORD's justice and His laws for Israel.”

22 About Dan he said, “Dan is a lion cub that leaps from Bashan.”

23 About Naphtali he said, “Naphtali, full of favor and filled with the LORD's blessing, take the west and the south.”

24 About Asher he said, “Asher is blessed with children. Let him be favored by his brothers and dip his foot in oil. 25 Your sandals will be iron and bronze, and your strength will last as long as you live.”

The Lord Is Israel's Refuge

26 There is no one like the God of Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to help you and through the skies in His majesty. 27 The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are His everlasting arms. He will drive the enemy out before you and destroy them. 28 Israel will live safely alone. Jacob's spring is in a land of grain and wine, and the heavens will drip with dew. 29 How happy you are, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? He is your shield and your help, and the sword that gives you honor. Your enemies will cringe before you, and you will walk on their high places.
