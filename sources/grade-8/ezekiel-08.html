Ezekiel's Vision of Idols in Jerusalem

1 In the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was sitting in my house, and the elders of Judah were sitting in front of me. There the hand of the Lord the LORD fell on me. 2 I looked, and I saw a figure that looked like a man. From the waist down, it looked like fire. From the waist up, it looked like bright, glowing metal.

3 He reached out something that looked like a hand and took me by the hair of my head. The Spirit lifted me up between the earth and the sky and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem. He brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the inner courtyard, where the idol of jealousy sits, the one that stirs up jealousy. 4 There I saw the glory of the God of Israel, just like the vision I had seen in the valley.

5 "Son of man," He said to me, "look toward the north." So I looked toward the north, and there at the entrance of the gate by the altar was this idol of jealousy.

6 He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they are doing? The people of Israel are doing terrible, detestable things here. They are driving Me far away from My sanctuary. But you will see even more detestable things."

Secret Idol Worship in the Temple

7 Then He brought me to the entrance of the courtyard, and I looked and saw a hole in the wall. 8 He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall." So I dug through the wall and saw an entrance.

9 He said to me, "Go in and see the wicked, detestable things they are doing here."

10 So I went in and looked, and I saw that all around the wall were carved pictures of crawling creatures, unclean animals, and all the idols of the people of Israel. 11 In front of them stood seventy of the elders of Israel, and Jaazaniah son of Shaphan was standing among them. Each man held a censer in his hand, and a thick cloud of incense was rising.

12 He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen what the elders of Israel are doing in the dark? Each man is in his own room of idols. They say, 'The LORD does not see us. The LORD has abandoned the land.'"

13 He also said to me, "You will see them doing even more detestable things."

Women Weeping for Tammuz

14 Then He brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the LORD, and there women were sitting, weeping for Tammuz.

15 He said to me, "Son of man, do you see this? You will see even more detestable things than these."

Sun Worship in the Inner Court

16 He brought me into the inner courtyard of the house of the LORD. There, at the entrance to the temple of the LORD, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men. Their backs were toward the temple of the LORD, and their faces were toward the east. They were bowing down to the sun in the east.

17 He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen this? Is it not enough for the people of Judah to do these detestable things here? They have filled the land with violence, and they keep provoking Me to anger. Look, they are thumbing their noses at Me."

18 So I will act in My anger. My eye will not spare them, and I will have no pity. Even if they shout loudly to Me, I will not listen to them.
