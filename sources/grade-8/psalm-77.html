A Cry for Help

1 For the director of music, according to Jeduthun. A psalm of Asaph. I cried out to God for help; I cried out to God, and He listened to me.

2 In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord; at night my hands stayed stretched out and did not grow tired, but my soul refused to be comforted. 3 I remembered God and groaned; I thought about Him, and my spirit grew faint. Selah

4 You kept my eyes open; I was too troubled to speak. 5 I thought about the days of old, the years long ago. 6 At night I remembered my song; I thought deeply in my heart, and my spirit searched.

Questioning God's Favor

7 Will the Lord reject forever? Will He never show favor again? 8 Has His faithful love ended forever? Has His promise failed for all generations? 9 Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has He in anger withheld His compassion? Selah

10 Then I said, "This is my grief, that the right hand of the Most High has changed."

Remembering God's Works

11 I will remember the works of the LORD; yes, I will remember Your wonders from long ago. 12 I will meditate on all Your works and think about Your mighty deeds.

13 Your way, O God, is holy. What god is as great as our God? 14 You are the God who works wonders; You have made known Your strength among the peoples. 15 With Your arm You redeemed Your people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah

God's Power Over the Waters

16 The waters saw You, O God; the waters saw You and trembled; the depths also shook. 17 The clouds poured out water; the skies thundered; Your arrows flew back and forth. 18 The sound of Your thunder was in the whirlwind; Your lightning lit up the world; the earth trembled and quaked.

19 Your way was in the sea, and Your path in the mighty waters, but Your footprints were not known. 20 You led Your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
