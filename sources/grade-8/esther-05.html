Esther Approaches the King

1 On the third day, Esther put on her royal robes and stood in the inner courtyard of the palace, facing the king's hall. The king was sitting on his royal throne in the throne room, facing the doorway. 2 When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the courtyard, she won his favor. He held out the gold scepter in his hand toward her, and Esther came near and touched the top of the scepter.

3 The king asked her, "What is it, Queen Esther? What do you want? I'll give it to you, even up to half my kingdom."

4 Esther answered, "If it pleases the king, may the king and Haman come today to the feast I have prepared for him."

5 The king said, "Hurry and get Haman so he can do what Esther asked." So the king and Haman went to the feast Esther had prepared.

6 At the wine feast, the king asked Esther, "What do you want? I'll give it to you. What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, I'll do it."

7 Esther answered, "Here is my request and my petition: 8 If I have found favor with the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my request and do what I ask, may the king and Haman come to the feast I will prepare for them tomorrow. Then I'll tell the king what I want."

Haman's Pride and Anger

9 That day Haman left happy and in a good mood. But when he saw Mordecai at the king's gate, and Mordecai didn't stand up or move for him, Haman was filled with anger toward Mordecai. 10 Haman held back his anger. When he got home, he sent for his friends and his wife Zeresh.

11 Haman bragged to them about his great wealth, his many children, and all the ways the king had honored him. He told how the king had promoted him above the other officials and servants. 12 Haman also said, "Queen Esther didn't let anyone come with the king to the feast she prepared except me. Tomorrow I'm invited to go with the king to another feast she's giving. 13 But all of this means nothing to me as long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king's gate."

A Plan to Kill Mordecai

14 His wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Have a gallows built fifty cubits high. In the morning, ask the king to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then you can go happily with the king to the feast." Haman liked the idea, so he had the gallows made.
