The Day of the LORD Is Coming

1 Look, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, and all the proud and all who do evil will be like dry straw. The day that is coming will burn them up, says the LORD of Hosts, so that nothing is left of them—not even a root or a branch. 2 But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its rays. You will go out and leap like calves released from the stall. 3 You will trample the wicked, because they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day I do this, says the LORD of Hosts.

Remember the Law of Moses

4 Remember the law of My servant Moses—the rules and decisions I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel.

Elijah Will Prepare the Way

5 Look, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and frightening day of the LORD comes. 6 He will turn the hearts of fathers to their children and the hearts of children to their fathers. Otherwise, I will come and strike the land with total destruction.
