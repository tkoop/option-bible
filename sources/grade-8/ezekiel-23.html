Two Sisters Who Were Unfaithful

1 The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 2 "Son of man, there were two women. They were daughters of the same mother. 3 They prostituted themselves in Egypt. They did this even in their youth. Their breasts were fondled there, and their virgin breasts were caressed. 4 The older one was named Oholah, and her sister was Oholibah. They became Mine, and they had sons and daughters. Oholah stands for Samaria, and Oholibah stands for Jerusalem.

Oholah's Lust for Assyria

5 "Oholah was unfaithful while she belonged to Me. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, 6 warriors dressed in blue—governors and commanders. They were all handsome young men, horsemen riding horses. 7 She offered herself to all of them, even the best of the Assyrians. She made herself unclean with every idol of the people she lusted after. 8 She did not stop the prostitution she had started in Egypt. In her youth, men slept with her. They caressed her virgin breasts and poured out their lust on her.

9 "So I handed her over to her lovers, the Assyrians she lusted after. 10 They stripped her naked. They took her sons and daughters and killed them with swords. She became a warning to other women, and they carried out judgment against her.

Oholibah's Greater Betrayal

11 "Her sister Oholibah saw this. But she was even more corrupt in her lust than Oholah. She outdid her sister's unfaithfulness. 12 She lusted after the Assyrians too—governors and commanders, warriors dressed in fine clothes, and handsome young horsemen. All of them were desirable young men. 13 I saw that both of them had made themselves unclean. They had taken the same path.

14 "But Oholibah went even further in her prostitution. She saw men carved on a wall—pictures of Chaldeans, carved in red, 15 wearing belts around their waists and flowing turbans on their heads. They all looked like officers. They looked like Babylonians from Chaldea, the land where they were born. 16 When she saw them, she lusted after them. She sent messengers to them in Chaldea. 17 Then the Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and made her unclean with their lust. After she had been defiled by them, she turned away from them in disgust. 18 When she uncovered her prostitution and exposed her nakedness, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister. 19 Yet she increased her prostitution. She remembered the days of her youth, when she had prostituted herself in the land of Egypt. 20 She lusted after their lovers, whose genitals were like those of donkeys and whose flow was like that of stallions. 21 So you revisited the indecency of your youth. The Egyptians caressed your bosom and pressed your young breasts.

God's Judgment on Both Sisters

22 "Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord the LORD says: I will stir up your lovers against you—the ones you turned away from in disgust. I will bring them against you from every side. 23 They are the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them. They are handsome young men—governors and commanders, officers and famous men. All of them ride horses. 24 They will come against you with a crowd of peoples. They will bring weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will surround you on every side with shields and helmets. I will hand you over to them for judgment, and they will judge you by their own laws. 25 I will set My jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will cut off your nose and your ears. Your survivors will fall by the sword. They will take your sons and daughters, and your survivors will be consumed by fire. 26 They will strip off your clothes and take your fine jewelry. 27 So I will put an end to your indecency and prostitution, which you began in the land of Egypt. You will not look to them anymore, and you will not remember Egypt.

28 "For this is what the Lord the LORD says: I will hand you over to those you hate, to those you turned away from in disgust. 29 They will treat you with hatred and take everything you worked for. They will leave you naked and bare. The nakedness of your prostitution will be exposed. Your indecency and prostitution will be revealed. 30 I will do these things to you because you were unfaithful with the nations and became unclean with their idols. 31 You have walked in your sister's way, so I will put her cup into your hand.

32 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: You will drink your sister's cup. It is deep and wide, and it holds much. It will bring you much mockery and scorn. 33 You will be filled with drunkenness and grief. It is a cup of horror and ruin—the cup of your sister Samaria. 34 You will drink it and drain it. You will smash it to pieces, and you will tear your breasts. For I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD. 35 Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you forgot Me and cast Me behind your back, you must bear your indecency and prostitution."

36 Then the LORD said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their evil practices. 37 For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They committed adultery with their idols. They even sacrificed the children they bore to Me, passing them through the fire. 38 On that same day they made My sanctuary unclean and dishonored My Sabbaths. 39 On the very day they sacrificed their children to their idols, they entered My sanctuary to make it unclean. Yes, this is what they did inside My house. 40 They even sent messengers for men who came from far away. Look, those men came! You bathed for them, painted your eyes, and put on jewelry. 41 You sat on a fancy couch with a table set before it. On it you had placed My incense and My oil. 42 The sound of a noisy crowd went with them. Men from the desert, called Sabeans, were brought in, along with ordinary men from the crowd. They put bracelets on the women's wrists and beautiful crowns on their heads.

43 "Then I said, 'Will she now add these shameful acts to her prostitution?' 44 Men went to her as they would go to a prostitute. So they went to Oholah and Oholibah, those wicked women. 45 But righteous men will judge them. They will judge them as unfaithful wives and murderers are judged, because they are unfaithful and blood is on their hands. 46 For this is what the Lord the LORD says: Bring a mob against them. Hand them over to terror and plunder. 47 The mob will stone them and cut them down with swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn down their houses. 48 So I will put an end to indecency in the land. All the women will be warned not to copy your indecency. 49 They will repay you with your own indecency. You will bear the punishment for your idols. Then you will know that I am the Lord the LORD."
