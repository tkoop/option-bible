A Song of Praise to God

1 Shout for joy to God, all the earth! 2 Sing about the glory of His name; make His praise glorious. 3 Say to God, "How awesome are Your deeds! So great is Your power that Your enemies cringe before You. 4 All the earth bows down to You and sings praise to You; they sing praises to Your name." Selah

5 Come and see what God has done. His works for people are amazing! 6 He turned the sea into dry land, and they crossed the river on foot. Come, let us rejoice in Him. 7 He rules forever by His power. His eyes watch the nations. Do not let rebels rise up against Him. Selah

8 Praise our God, all you peoples; let the sound of His praise be heard. 9 He has kept us alive and has not let our feet slip.

10 For You, God, have tested us and refined us like silver. 11 You brought us into a trap and laid heavy burdens on our backs. 12 You let people walk all over us. We went through fire and water, but You brought us out to a place of abundance.

A Promise to Worship

13 I will come to Your temple with burnt offerings and fulfill my vows to You— 14 vows my lips promised and my mouth spoke when I was in trouble. 15 I will sacrifice fat animals as burnt offerings, with the smoke of rams. I will offer bulls and goats. Selah

16 Come and hear, all you who fear God, and I will tell you what He has done for me. 17 I cried out to Him with my mouth, and praise was on my tongue. 18 If I had held on to sin in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. 19 But God has surely listened; He has heard my prayer. 20 Praise be to God, who has not rejected my prayer or withheld His love from me!
