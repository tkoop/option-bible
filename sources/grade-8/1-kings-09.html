Solomon Finishes the Temple and Palace

1After Solomon finished building the the LORD's temple, the royal palace, and everything else he wanted to build, 2the the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, just as He had appeared to him at Gibeon.

3And the the LORD said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea, which you have made before Me; I have dedicated this temple you have built to put My name there forever. My eyes and My heart will always be there. 4As for you, if you walk before Me as your father David walked, with an honest and upright heart, doing everything I have commanded you, and if you keep My laws and rules, 5then I will establish your royal throne over Israel forever, as I promised your father David: 'You will never fail to have a man on the throne of Israel.'

6"But if you and your sons turn away from Me and do not keep My commands and laws that I have set before you, and if you go and serve other gods and worship them, 7then I will cut Israel off from the land I have given them. I will remove this temple from My sight, and Israel will become a thing of scorn and ridicule among all peoples.

8"This temple will become a pile of ruins. Everyone who passes by will be shocked and hiss, saying, 'Why has the the LORD done this to this land and to this temple?' 9And people will answer, 'Because they left the the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of Egypt. They turned to other gods, worshiped them, and served them. That is why the the LORD has brought all this trouble on them.'"

Solomon and King Hiram

10At the end of twenty years, during which Solomon built the two houses—the house of the the LORD and the king's house— 11King Hiram of Tyre had supplied Solomon with cedar and cypress timber and gold for all he desired. At that time King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee. 12So Hiram left Tyre to look at the cities Solomon had given him, but they did not please him. 13He said, "What are these cities you have given me, my brother?" So he called them the Land of Cabul, and they are still called that today. 14Hiram sent the king 120 talents of gold.

Solomon's Building Projects

15This is the account of the forced labor King Solomon raised to build the house of the the LORD, his own house, the terraces, the wall of Jerusalem, Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer. 16Pharaoh king of Egypt had attacked and captured Gezer. He burned it with fire, killed the Canaanites who lived in the city, and gave it as a wedding gift to his daughter, Solomon's wife.

17So Solomon rebuilt Gezer, Lower Beth-horon, 18Baalath, and Tamar in the wilderness in the land. 19He also built all his store cities, the cities for his chariots and horsemen, and everything else he wanted to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout his whole kingdom.

Solomon's Workers and Trade

20As for all the people left from the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—people who were not Israelites— 21Solomon forced their descendants who remained in the land after them, the ones the Israelites could not completely destroy, to do forced labor. They still do this work today. 22But Solomon did not force any Israelite to work. They were his soldiers, servants, officials, commanders, and officers over his chariots and cavalry. 23These were the chief officers in charge of Solomon's work: 550 officials who supervised the people doing the labor.

24Pharaoh's daughter moved from the City of David to the house Solomon had built for her. Then he built the terraces. 25Three times a year Solomon offered burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar he had built for the the LORD, and he burned incense before the the LORD. So he finished the temple.

26King Solomon also built a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, near Eloth in the land of Edom, on the shore of the Red Sea. 27Hiram sent his servants, men who knew the sea, to serve in the fleet with Solomon's servants. 28They sailed to Ophir and brought back 420 talents of gold, which they delivered to King Solomon.
