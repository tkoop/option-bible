Jesus Before Pilate

1 Very early in the morning, the chief priests met with the elders, the teachers of the law, and the whole Sanhedrin. They made a plan, tied up Jesus, and took Him away. Then they handed Him over to Pilate.

2 Pilate asked Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?"

Jesus answered, "You have said so."

3 The chief priests accused Him of many things. 4 Pilate asked Him again, "Won't You answer anything? See how many things they accuse You of." 5 But Jesus still didn't answer, so Pilate was amazed.

6 At the feast, Pilate used to release one prisoner whom the people asked for. 7 A man named Barabbas was in prison with the rebels who had killed someone during a riot. 8 The crowd came up and began to ask Pilate to do what he usually did for them.

9 Pilate answered, "Do you want me to release the King of the Jews for you?"

10 He knew the chief priests had handed Jesus over because they were jealous. 11 But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to ask for Barabbas instead.

12 Pilate asked them again, "Then what do you want me to do with the One you call the King of the Jews?"

13 They shouted, "Crucify Him!"

14 Pilate said, "Why? What crime has He done?"

But they shouted even louder, "Crucify Him!"

15 Wanting to please the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them. He had Jesus whipped, and then he handed Him over to be crucified.

Soldiers Mock Jesus

16 The soldiers led Him away into the governor's palace. They called together the whole company of soldiers. 17 They put a purple robe on Him. They twisted together a crown of thorns and put it on Him.

18 They began to greet Him, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

19 They hit His head with a reed. They spit on Him. They knelt down and bowed before Him. 20 After they mocked Him, they took off the purple robe and put His own clothes back on Him. Then they led Him out to crucify Him.

Jesus Is Crucified

21 They forced a man named Simon from Cyrene to carry His cross. Simon was the father of Alexander and Rufus. He was coming in from the country. 22 They brought Jesus to a place called Golgotha, which means "Place of the Skull." 23 They offered Him wine mixed with myrrh, but He didn't take it. 24 They crucified Him. They divided His clothes by casting lots to see who would get what. 25 It was nine in the morning when they crucified Him. 26 The sign with the charge against Him read, "The King of the Jews." 27 They crucified two robbers with Him, one on His right and one on His left.

29 People walking by insulted Him. They shook their heads and said, "Aha! You said You could destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days." 30 "Come down from the cross and save Yourself!"

31 The chief priests and teachers of the law also mocked Him among themselves. They said, "He saved others, but He can't save Himself!" 32 "Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now, so we can see and believe." The men crucified with Him also insulted Him.

33 From noon until three in the afternoon, darkness came over the whole land. 34 At three in the afternoon, Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" This means, "My God, My God, why have You abandoned Me?"

35 Some people standing there heard it and said, "Listen, He's calling Elijah."

36 Someone ran and filled a sponge with sour wine. He put it on a reed and gave it to Jesus to drink. He said, "Wait, let's see if Elijah comes to take Him down."

37 Jesus let out a loud cry and breathed His last. 38 The curtain in the temple tore in two from top to bottom. 39 When the Roman officer standing in front of Jesus saw how He died, he said, "Truly this Man was the Son of God!"

40 Some women were watching from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome. 41 They had followed Jesus and helped Him while He was in Galilee. Many other women had also come with Him to Jerusalem.

Jesus Is Buried

42 It was already evening. Since it was Preparation Day, the day before the Sabbath, 43 Joseph of Arimathea came. He was a respected member of the council, and he was waiting for God's kingdom. He was brave enough to go to Pilate and ask for Jesus' body. 44 Pilate was surprised that Jesus was already dead. He called the Roman officer and asked if Jesus had been dead for a while. 45 When the Roman officer told him, Pilate gave the body to Joseph. 46 Joseph bought a linen cloth. He took Jesus down, wrapped Him in the cloth, and placed Him in a tomb cut out of rock. Then he rolled a stone against the entrance of the tomb. 47 Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where Jesus was placed.
