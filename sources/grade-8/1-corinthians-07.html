Marriage and Purity

1 Now about what you wrote: It is good for a man not to have sexual relations with a woman. 2 But because of sexual sin, each man should have his own wife, and each woman her own husband. 3 A husband should fulfill his duty to his wife, and a wife should do the same for her husband. 4 A wife does not have authority over her own body; her husband does. In the same way, a husband does not have authority over his own body; his wife does.

5 Do not refuse to be intimate with each other, except when you both agree to take time apart to pray. Then come together again, so Satan will not tempt you because you lack self-control. 6 I say this as a suggestion, not as a command. 7 I wish everyone were like me. But each person has a different gift from God.

8 To unmarried people and widows I say: It is good to stay single, as I am. 9 But if they cannot control themselves, they should get married. It is better to marry than to burn with desire.

Divorce and Mixed Marriages

10 To married people I give this command—not I, but the Lord: A wife must not separate from her husband. 11 But if she does leave, she should stay single or make peace with her husband. A husband must not divorce his wife.

12 To the rest I say—I, not the Lord: If a Christian brother has an unbelieving wife, and she is willing to live with him, he must not divorce her. 13 And if a woman has an unbelieving husband, and he is willing to live with her, she must not divorce her husband.

14 For the unbelieving husband is made holy through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy through her husband. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is, they are holy. 15 But if the unbeliever leaves, let them leave. A believing brother or sister is not bound in such cases. God has called you to live in peace. 16 Wife, how do you know if you will save your husband? Husband, how do you know if you will save your wife?

Living as God Called You

17 Each person should live the life the Lord has given them, just as God called them. This is the rule I give in all the churches. 18 Was a man circumcised when he was called? He should not try to undo it. Was a man uncircumcised when he was called? He should not be circumcised. 19 Circumcision does not matter, and uncircumcision does not matter. What matters is obeying God's commands.

20 Each person should stay in the situation they were in when God called them. 21 Were you a slave when you were called? Do not worry about it. But if you can become free, take that chance. 22 For a slave who was called in the Lord is the Lord's freed person. In the same way, a free person who was called is Christ's slave. 23 You were bought at a price; do not become slaves of human beings. 24 Brothers and sisters, each person should remain with God in the state in which they were called.

Advice for Unmarried People

25 Now about unmarried people: I have no command from the Lord, but I give my opinion as someone the Lord has shown mercy to and made trustworthy. 26 Because of the present trouble, I think it is good for a person to stay as they are. 27 Are you bound to a wife? Do not try to get free. Are you free from a wife? Do not look for a wife. 28 But if you marry, you have not sinned; and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. But those who marry will have trouble in this life, and I want to spare you.

29 What I mean, brothers and sisters, is that the time is short. From now on, those who have wives should live as if they did not. 30 Those who mourn should live as if they were not mourning; those who are happy should live as if they were not; those who buy things should live as if they did not own them. 31 And those who use the things of this world should not become too attached to them. For this world in its present form is passing away.

32 I want you to be free from worry. An unmarried man cares about the Lord's work and how to please the Lord. 33 But a married man cares about the things of this world and how to please his wife. 34 His attention is divided. An unmarried woman or virgin cares about the Lord's work, wanting to be holy in both body and spirit. But a married woman cares about the things of this world and how to please her husband.

35 I am saying this for your own good, not to restrict you, but so that you can live in a proper way and serve the Lord without being pulled in many directions.

36 If a man thinks he is not treating the woman he is engaged to fairly, and if she is past the usual age to marry, and he feels he should marry, he may do what he wants. He does not sin. They should get married. 37 But the man who has made up his mind and is under no pressure, who has control over his own will, and who has decided to keep her as she is, does well. 38 So the man who marries the woman he is engaged to does right, but the man who does not marry her does even better.

39 A woman is bound to her husband as long as he lives. But if her husband dies, she is free to marry anyone she wishes, as long as he belongs to the Lord. 40 In my opinion, she is happier if she stays as she is. And I think I too have the Spirit of God.
