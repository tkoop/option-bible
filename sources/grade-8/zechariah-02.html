A Man Measures Jerusalem

1 I looked up and saw a man. He was holding a measuring line in his hand. 2 I asked him, “Where are you going?” He said, “I am going to measure Jerusalem. I want to see how wide it is and how long it is.”

3 Then the angel who had been talking with me came out. Another angel came out to meet him. 4 The other angel said to him, “Run and tell that young man, ‘Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the many people and animals living in it.’”

5 “For I will be a wall of fire around her,” declares the LORD. “I will be the glory inside her.”

A Call to Return from Babylon

6 “Get up! Get up! Run away from the land of the north,” declares the LORD. “For I have scattered you in every direction,” declares the LORD.

7 “Get up! Escape to Zion, you who live in Babylon!”

8 For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “After He was honored, He sent me to the nations that robbed you. Anyone who touches you touches something very dear to Him. 9 I will wave My hand over them, so they will become loot for their own servants. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me.”

God Will Live Among His People

10 “Sing and be happy, people of Zion, for I am coming to live among you,” declares the LORD. 11 “On that day many nations will join the LORD and become My people. I will live among you, and you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you.”

12 The LORD will take Judah as His own land in the Holy Land, and He will choose Jerusalem again. 13 Be quiet before the LORD, all people, for He has awakened from His holy home.
