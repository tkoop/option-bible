Going to War

1 When you go out to war against your enemies and see horses and chariots and an army bigger than you, don't be afraid of them. The LORD your God, who brought you up out of Egypt, is with you.

Preparing for Battle

2 When you get close to the battle, the priest will come forward and speak to the people. 3 He will say to them, "Listen, Israel! Today you are getting close to fight your enemies. Don't let your courage fail. Don't be afraid, don't panic, and don't be terrified of them. 4 The LORD your God is He who goes with you to fight for you against your enemies and to save you."

5 Then the officers will speak to the people. They will say, "Is there any man who has built a new house but hasn't dedicated it? He should go home so he won't die in battle and someone else dedicate it. 6 Is there any man who has planted a vineyard but hasn't enjoyed its fruit? He should go home so he won't die in battle and someone else enjoy its fruit. 7 Is there any man who has promised to marry a woman but hasn't married her? He should go home so he won't die in battle and someone else marry her."

8 The officers will also say, "Is there any man who is afraid and has a weak heart? He should go home so he won't make his brothers lose courage too." 9 When the officers finish speaking to the people, they will appoint commanders to lead the army.

Approaching a City

10 When you get close to a city to fight it, first offer it peace. 11 If it answers you with peace and opens its gates to you, then all the people in it will be forced to work for you and serve you. 12 But if it doesn't make peace with you and fights against you, then lay siege to it. 13 When the the LORD your God hands it over to you, kill all the men in it with the sword. 14 But you may take the women, children, animals, and everything else in the city as plunder. You may eat the goods taken from your enemies, which the the LORD your God has given you.

15 Do this to all the cities that are very far from you and don't belong to these nations. 16 But in the cities of these peoples that the the LORD your God is giving you as your own land, don't let anything that breathes survive. 17 You must completely destroy them—the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—just as the the LORD your God commanded you. 18 Do this so they won't teach you to copy all the terrible things they do for their gods, and so you won't sin against the the LORD your God.

Trees During a Siege

19 When you surround a city for a long time to fight and capture it, don't destroy its trees by swinging an axe on them. You may eat from them, but don't cut them down. Are trees in the field human, that they should be besieged by you? 20 You may destroy and cut down only trees that you know aren't food trees. Use them to build siege works against the city that is fighting you until it falls.
