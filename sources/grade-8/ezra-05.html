Prophets Encourage the Rebuilding

1 The prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem. They spoke in the name of the God of Israel. 2 Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak began to rebuild the house of God in Jerusalem. The prophets of God were with them and helped them.

Officials Question the Builders

3 At that time Tattenai, the governor of the province Beyond the River, Shethar-boznai, and their associates came to the builders and said, “Who gave you permission to build this house and finish it?” 4 Then we asked them, “What are the names of the men who are building this building?” 5 But God was watching over the Jewish leaders, so no one stopped them until the report reached King Darius and an answer came back.

Letter Sent to King Darius

6 This is a copy of the letter that Tattenai, governor of Beyond the River, Shethar-boznai, and their associates, the officials in the province Beyond the River, sent to King Darius.

7 They sent this report to him, and it said, “To King Darius: Peace and prosperity. 8 Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with large stones, and timber is being placed in the walls. The work is moving forward quickly and is going well for them. 9 Then we asked those leaders, ‘Who gave you permission to build this house and finish it?’ 10 We also asked for their names, so we could report to you the names of the men in charge.

The Jews Explain Their History

“‘11 We are servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the house that was built many years ago. A great king of Israel built and finished it. 12 But because our ancestors angered the God of heaven, He handed them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean. He destroyed this house and carried the people away to Babylon. 13 In the first year of King Cyrus of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree to rebuild this house of God. 14 He also brought out from the temple in Babylon the gold and silver objects that belonged to the house of God. Nebuchadnezzar had taken them from the temple in Jerusalem and brought them to Babylon. King Cyrus gave them to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he had appointed governor.

“‘15 He told him, “Take these objects and go put them in the temple in Jerusalem. Let the house of God be rebuilt on its original site.”

“‘16 Then Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundation of the house of God in Jerusalem. From that time until now it has been under construction, but it is not yet finished.’

“17 Now if it pleases the king, let a search be made in the royal archives in Babylon to see whether King Cyrus really did issue a decree to rebuild this house of God in Jerusalem. Then let the king send us his decision about this.”
