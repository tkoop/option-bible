The LORD Promises Healing and Restoration

1 While Jeremiah was still held in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the the LORD came to him a second time. 2 It said, "This is what the the LORD says: He made the earth and shaped it to set it in place. The the LORD is His name. 3 Call to Me, and I will answer you. I will tell you great and hidden things you do not know."

4 "For this is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the houses in this city and the houses of the kings of Judah: They have been torn down to defend against siege ramps and swords. 5 The Babylonians are coming to fight and to fill these places with the dead bodies of the people I struck down in My anger and wrath. I also hid My face from this city because of all their evil."

6 "Look, I will bring healing and cure to this city. I will heal them and show them plenty of peace and truth. 7 I will bring Judah and Israel back from captivity and rebuild them as they were before. 8 I will cleanse them from all their sin against Me and forgive all their sins and rebellions against Me. 9 Then this city will be a name of joy to Me, a praise and glory to all nations on earth who hear about all the good I do for them. They will tremble and shake because of all the good and peace I give this city."

Joy Returns to Jerusalem

10 'This is what the the LORD says: "In this place you say is a ruin, without people or animals—in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem that are empty, without people, animals, or anyone living there—people will again hear 11 the voice of joy and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the bride, and the voice of those who bring thank offerings to the house of the the LORD. They will say, "Give thanks to the the LORD of Hosts, for the the LORD is good; His faithful love endures forever." For I will restore the land from captivity as before,' says the the LORD."

12 'This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: "Again in this empty place, without people or animals, and in all its cities, there will be pastures where shepherds rest their flocks. 13 In the cities of the hill country, the western foothills, the Negev, the land of Benjamin, the area around Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them," says the the LORD.'

The Righteous Branch

14 "Look, the days are coming, declares the the LORD, when I will fulfill the good promise I made to the house of Israel and the house of Judah. 15 In those days and at that time I will make a righteous Branch grow for David. He will do what is just and right in the land. 16 In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will live in safety. This is the name by which she will be called: The LORD Our Righteousness."

17 "For this is what the the LORD says: 'David will never fail to have a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel, 18 and the priests from the tribe of Levi will never fail to have a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, grain offerings, and sacrifices.'"

The LORD's Covenant Stands Forever

19 The word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 20 "If you can break My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night, so that day and night no longer come at their appointed time, 21 then My covenant with David My servant can be broken, so that he will not have a son to reign on his throne, and My covenant with the priests from the tribe of Levi who serve Me. 22 Just as the host of heaven cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will multiply the descendants of David My servant and the Levites who serve Me."

23 The word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 24 "Have you not noticed what these people are saying: 'The the LORD has rejected the two families He chose'? So they despise My people and no longer regard them as a nation. 25 This is what the the LORD says: If I have not established My covenant with day and night and the fixed laws of heaven and earth, 26 then I will reject the descendants of Jacob and David My servant, and will not choose a ruler from his descendants to rule over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. For I will restore them from captivity and have compassion on them."
