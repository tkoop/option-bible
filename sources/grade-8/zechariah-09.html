The LORD Judges the Nations

1 An oracle: The word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach and Damascus, its resting place. For the eyes of all people and all the tribes of Israel are on the LORD. 2 Hamath too borders it; Tyre and Sidon, though very wise. 3 Tyre has built herself a fortress; she has piled up silver like dust and gold like mud in the streets.

4 Look, the Lord will take her over and strike her wealth into the sea; she will be consumed by fire. 5 Ashkelon will see and be afraid; Gaza will writhe in pain; Ekron too, because her hope is gone. The king will die in Gaza, and Ashkelon will no longer be lived in.

6 A mixed people will live in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines. 7 I will remove blood from their mouths and forbidden things from between their teeth. The survivors will belong to our God and be like a clan in Judah; Ekron will be like the Jebusites. 8 I will camp around My house to guard it from armies. No one will pass through or return, and no oppressor will pass over them again, for now I have seen with My own eyes.

The Coming King

9 Rejoice greatly, Daughter Zion! Shout, Daughter Jerusalem! Look, your King is coming to you. He is righteous and brings salvation. He is humble and rides on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.

10 I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem. The bow of war will be cut off, and He will speak peace to the nations. His rule will stretch from sea to sea and from the River to the ends of the earth.

The LORD Rescues and Restores His People

11 As for you, by the blood of your covenant I have sent your prisoners out of the pit with no water. 12 Return to the stronghold, you prisoners of hope. Even today I declare that I will restore double to you. 13 For I have bent Judah as My bow and filled it with Ephraim. I will stir up your sons, Zion, against the sons of Greece, and I will make you like a warrior's sword.

14 Then the LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will shoot out like lightning. The Lord the LORD will blow the trumpet and march in the storms of the south. 15 The LORD of Hosts will protect them. They will devour and conquer sling stones. They will drink and roar like wine. They will be filled like bowls, like the corners of the altar.

16 On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of His people, for they are like jewels in a crown lifted over His land. 17 How great is His goodness and beauty! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.
