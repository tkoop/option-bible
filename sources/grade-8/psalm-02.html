Why the Nations Rage Against the LORD

1 Why do the nations rage and the peoples plot in vain? 2 The kings of the earth take their stand, and the rulers gather together against the the LORD and against His Anointed. 3 "Let us break their chains and throw off their ropes."

4 The One who sits in heaven laughs; the Lord mocks them. 5 Then He speaks to them in His anger and terrifies them in His fury:

6 "As for me, I have set my King on Zion, my holy hill."

7 I will tell of the decree the the LORD gave me: "You are my Son; today I have become your Father. 8 Ask me, and I will give the nations to you as your inheritance, and the ends of the earth as your possession. 9 You will break them with an iron rod; you will shatter them like pottery."

A Warning to Earthly Rulers

10 So now, kings, be wise; rulers of the earth, take warning. 11 Serve the the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling. 12 Show honor to the Son, or He may become angry and you may be destroyed on your way, for His anger can flare up quickly. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.
