God Calls Jacob to Bethel

1 God said to Jacob, "Get up, go up to Bethel, and live there. Make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau."

2 Jacob said to his household and to everyone with him, "Put away the foreign gods among you, make yourselves clean, and change your clothes. 3 Let's get up and go up to Bethel. I will make an altar there to the God who answered me on the day I was in trouble and stayed with me on the road I traveled."

4 So they gave Jacob all the foreign gods they had and the earrings in their ears. Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem. 5 They traveled on, and God put fear into the cities around them, so no one chased Jacob's sons. 6 Jacob and all the people with him came to Luz, which is Bethel, in the land of Canaan. 7 There he built an altar and named the place El Bethel, because God had shown Himself to him there when he fled from his brother Esau. 8 Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died. She was buried under the oak below Bethel, and Jacob named it Allon Bacuth.

God Renews His Promise

9 When Jacob came back from Paddan-aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.

10 God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. You will not be called Jacob anymore. Your name will be Israel." Then He called him Israel.

11 God also said, "I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation and a group of nations will come from you, and kings will come from your descendants. 12 The land I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give it to your descendants after you."

13 God went up from him in the place where He had spoken to him. 14 Jacob set up a stone pillar there. He poured a drink offering and oil on it. 15 Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

Rachel Dies on the Road

16 They left Bethel. While they were still a little way from Ephrath, Rachel began to give birth, and she had a hard labor. 17 When the labor was hardest, the midwife said to her, "Do not be afraid, for this is another son for you." 18 As Rachel was dying, she named her son Ben-oni, but his father named him Benjamin.

19 Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem. 20 Jacob set up a pillar over her grave. It marks Rachel's tomb to this day. 21 Israel moved on and set up his tent beyond Migdal Eder. 22 While Israel lived in that land, Reuben went in and slept with Bilhah, his father's concubine. Israel heard about it. Jacob now had twelve sons.

Jacob's Sons and Isaac's Death

23 The sons of Leah: Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 24 The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin. 25 The sons of Bilhah, Rachel's servant: Dan and Naphtali. 26 The sons of Zilpah, Leah's servant: Gad and Asher. These were the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-aram.

27 Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath Arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had lived. 28 Isaac lived 180 years. 29 Isaac took his last breath and died. He was old and had lived a full life. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
