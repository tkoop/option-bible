Greeting to the Chosen Lady

1 The elder writes to the chosen lady and her children. I love them in the truth. I am not the only one—everyone who knows the truth loves them too. 2 We love them because the truth stays in us and will be with us forever. 3 Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Father's Son. They will stay with us in truth and love.

Walking in Truth and Love

4 I was very glad to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the Father commanded us. 5 Now I ask you, lady, not to write a new command for you, but to remember the command we have had from the start: love one another. 6 This is love: walk by His commands. This is the command you have heard from the start, so you should walk in it.

Beware of Deceivers

7 Many deceivers have gone out into the world. They do not admit that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. Anyone like that is a deceiver and an antichrist. 8 Watch yourselves so you do not lose what we worked for. Instead, you should receive a full reward. 9 Everyone who runs ahead and does not stay in Christ's teaching does not have God. The person who stays in that teaching has both the Father and the Son. 10 If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not take him into your house, and do not even greet him. 11 Anyone who greets him shares in his evil deeds.

Final Words

12 I have a lot to write to you, but I do not want to use paper and ink. I hope to visit you and talk face to face, so our joy will be complete. 13 The children of your chosen sister send their greetings to you.
