Amaziah Becomes King

1 Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan, from Jerusalem. 2 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, but not with a whole heart.

3 When his kingdom was strong, Amaziah killed the servants who had murdered his father the king. 4 But he did not kill their sons, because it is written in the Law, in the Book of Moses, where the LORD commanded: "Fathers must not be put to death for their children, and children must not be put to death for their fathers; each person must die for his own sin."

Amaziah's Military Plans

5 Amaziah gathered the people of Judah and organized them by family groups under commanders of thousands and hundreds. He counted everyone twenty years old and older in Judah and Benjamin and found 300,000 choice men who could serve in the army. They carried spears and shields.

6 He also hired 100,000 strong soldiers from Israel for 100 talents of silver. 7 But a man of God came to him and said, "O king, do not let Israel's army go with you, because the LORD is not with Israel—not with any of the Ephraimites. 8 But if you do go, be strong for battle; yet God will make you stumble before the enemy, because God has power to help and to make stumble."

9 Amaziah asked the man of God, "But what about the hundred talents I gave to the Israelite troops?"

The man of God answered, "The LORD is able to give you much more than this."

10 So Amaziah dismissed the troops that had come from Ephraim. They were to go home. But they were furious with Judah and returned home in a rage.

War with Edom and Israel

11 Amaziah strengthened himself and led his people out. He went to the Valley of Salt and struck down 10,000 men of Seir. 12 The men of Judah captured another 10,000 alive and took them to the top of a cliff. They threw them down, and all were dashed to pieces.

13 But the troops Amaziah had sent back, not letting them go with him to battle, raided towns of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon. They killed 3,000 people and carried off much plunder.

14 After Amaziah returned from defeating the Edomites, he brought back the gods of the people of Seir and set them up as his gods. He bowed down to them and burned incense to them.

15 Therefore the LORD was angry with Amaziah, and He sent a prophet to him, who said, "Why have you sought the gods of a people who could not save their own people from your hand?"

16 While the prophet was still speaking, Amaziah said to him, "Have we made you an advisor to the king? Stop! Why should you be struck down?"

So the prophet stopped, but not before saying, "I know that God has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."

17 After some time, Amaziah king of Judah made plans and sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us meet face to face."

18 Joash king of Israel replied to Amaziah king of Judah: "A thornbush in Lebanon sent to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son in marriage.' Then a wild beast in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thornbush. 19 You say, 'Look, I have struck down Edom,' and your heart has lifted you up to boast. Now stay at home. Why should you stir up trouble so that you fall and Judah with you?"

20 But Amaziah would not listen, for it was from God, so that He might deliver them into the hand of Joash, because they had sought the gods of Edom.

21 So Joash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah faced each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 22 Judah was defeated by Israel, and every man fled to his tent.

23 Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh. He brought him to Jerusalem and broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate, a distance of four hundred cubits. 24 He took all the gold and silver and all the articles found in the house of God with Obed-edom, and the treasures of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

Amaziah's Fall and Death

25 Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 26 The rest of the acts of Amaziah, from beginning to end, are they not written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel?

27 From the time Amaziah turned away from following the LORD, a conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish. But they sent men after him to Lachish and killed him there. 28 They carried him back on horses and buried him with his fathers in the City of Judah.
