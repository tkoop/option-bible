Great Is the LORD in Zion

1 A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. Great is the LORD, and He is greatly praised in the city of our God, His holy mountain. 2 Beautiful in height, the joy of all the earth—Mount Zion, in the far north, the city of the great King. 3 God is in her strongholds; He is known as a fortress.

The Kings Flee in Fear

4 For look, the kings gathered together and passed by as one. 5 They saw it and were amazed; fear seized them, and they ran away. 6 Trembling took hold of them there, like pain for a woman giving birth. 7 With an east wind You shatter the ships of Tarshish.

8 Just as we heard, so we have seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will make it stand forever. Selah. 9 Within Your temple, O God, we think about Your faithful love. 10 Like Your name, O God, so Your praise reaches the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness. 11 Mount Zion is glad; the daughters of Judah rejoice because of Your judgments.

Walk Around Zion

12 Walk around Zion and go all the way around her; count her towers. 13 Look at her walls and walk through her strong towers, so you can tell the next generation. 14 For this God is our God forever and ever; He will guide us even to death.
