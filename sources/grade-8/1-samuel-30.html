Amalekites Burn Ziklag

1 On the third day, David and his men reached Ziklag. The Amalekites had attacked the Negev and Ziklag, and they had burned Ziklag to the ground. 2 They took the women captive, from the youngest to the oldest, but they did not kill anyone. They took them away and went on their way. 3 When David and his men came to the city, they found it burned down. Their wives, sons, and daughters had been taken captive. 4 David and the people with him cried out loud and wept until they had no strength left to cry. 5 David's two wives, Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, Nabal's widow from Carmel, had been taken captive. 6 David was in great trouble because the men were bitter and talked about stoning him. Each man was upset about his own sons and daughters. But David found strength in the LORD his God.

David Asks the LORD for Help

7 David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Bring me the ephod." Abiathar brought the ephod to David. 8 David asked the LORD, "Should I chase this raiding party? Will I catch them?"

The LORD answered, "Chase them. You will catch them, and you will rescue the captives."

9 David and his six hundred men set out and came to the Besor Valley. The men who were left behind stayed there. 10 Two hundred men were too tired to cross the valley, so they stayed behind. David and four hundred men went on after the enemy.

David Finds and Defeats the Amalekites

11 They found an Egyptian in a field. They brought him to David and gave him water to drink. They also gave him food, and he ate. 12 They gave him a piece of fig cake and two clusters of raisins. After he ate, he felt stronger. He had not eaten or drunk anything for three days and three nights.

13 David asked him, "Who do you belong to? Where are you from?"

He said, "I am a young Egyptian. I serve an Amalekite. My master left me behind three days ago because I got sick. 14 We raided the Negev of the Cherethites and the land that belongs to Judah. We also raided the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag."

15 David asked him, "Can you lead me down to that raiding party?"

He said, "Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master. Then I will lead you down to them."

16 He led David down, and there they were, spread out over the land. They were eating, drinking, and celebrating because of the large amount of plunder they had taken from the Philistines and from Judah.

17 David attacked them from dusk until the next evening. No one escaped except four hundred young men who rode away on camels. 18 David got back everything the Amalekites had taken. He also rescued his two wives. 19 Nothing was missing, from the smallest to the greatest, sons, daughters, and all the plunder. David brought everything back. 20 He took all the flocks and herds and drove them ahead of the other animals. The men said, "This is David's plunder."

David Shares the Spoils and Sends Gifts

21 David came to the two hundred men who had been too tired to follow him. They had stayed at the Besor Valley. They came out to meet David and the people with him. David greeted them. 22 But some of the worthless and wicked men who had gone with David said, "They did not go with us, so we should not give them any of the plunder we recovered. Each man can take his wife and children and go."

23 David said, "No, my brothers. You must not do that with what the LORD has given us. He protected us and gave the raiding party into our hands. 24 Who will listen to you in this? The share of the man who goes into battle will be the same as the share of the man who stays with the supplies. They will share alike."

25 From that day on, David made this a rule and law for Israel, and it is still followed today.

26 When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah. They were his friends. He said, "Here is a gift for you from the plunder taken from the LORD's enemies." 27 He sent gifts to those in Bethel, Ramoth in the Negev, Jattir, 28 Aroer, Siphmoth, Eshtemoa, 29 Racal, the cities of the Jerahmeelites, the cities of the Kenites, 30 Hormah, Bor Ashan, Athach, 31 and Hebron. He also sent gifts to all the other places where David and his men had traveled.
