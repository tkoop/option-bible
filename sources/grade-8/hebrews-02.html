Pay Close Attention to the Message

1 So we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, or we may drift away. 2 For if the message given through angels was binding, and every sin and disobedience received a fair punishment, 3 how can we escape if we ignore such a great salvation? It was first announced by the Lord, and those who heard Him confirmed it to us. 4 God also gave His witness with signs, wonders, and all kinds of miracles, and with gifts from the Holy Spirit given as He chose.

Jesus Was Made Lower Than the Angels

5 For God did not put the world to come under angels' control, the world we are talking about. 6 But someone has testified somewhere, saying, "What is man that You are mindful of him, or the son of man that You care for him? 7 You made him a little lower than the angels; You crowned him with glory and honor. 8 You put everything under his feet." Now when He put everything under him, He left nothing outside his rule. But right now we do not yet see everything under his rule.

9 But we do see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, now crowned with glory and honor because He suffered death, so that by God's grace He could taste death for everyone.

10 For it was fitting that God, who brings many sons to glory, should make Jesus—the leader of their salvation—perfect through suffering. He is the One for whom and through whom everything exists. 11 For the One who makes people holy and those who are made holy all come from one family. That is why Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers.

12 He says, "I will declare Your name to My brothers; I will sing Your praises in the middle of the assembly." 13 And again, "I will put My trust in Him." And again, "Here am I, and the children God has given Me."

Jesus Shared Our Humanity

14 Since the children share flesh and blood, Jesus also shared in their humanity, so that by His death He might destroy the one who holds the power of death—that is, the devil. 15 He did this to free those who were held in slavery all their lives by their fear of death.

16 For surely He does not help angels; He helps Abraham's descendants. 17 So He had to be made like His brothers in every way, so that He could become a merciful and faithful high priest in service to God, to pay for the sins of the people. 18 Because He Himself suffered when He was tempted, He is able to help those who are being tempted.
