Israel Travels Around Mount Seir

1 Then we turned and set out for the wilderness by way of the Red Sea, as the the LORD had told me, and for many days we traveled around Mount Seir. 2 Then the the LORD spoke to me and said, 3 You have circled this mountain long enough; now turn north. 4 Command the people: 'You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be very afraid of you, so watch yourselves carefully. 5 Do not provoke them, for I will not give you any of their land—not even a foot of ground—because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as his possession. 6 You may buy food from them with money so that you may eat, and you may buy water from them with money so that you may drink.' 7 For the the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has watched over your journey through this great wilderness. These forty years the the LORD your God has been with you, and you have lacked nothing. 8 So we passed by our brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. We turned away from the Arabah road that comes from Elath and Ezion-geber, and we traveled along the road through the wilderness of Moab.

Commands About Moab and Ammon

9 Then the the LORD said to me, "Do not trouble Moab or provoke them to war, for I will not give you any of their land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as a possession." 10 The Emim used to live there—a people great, numerous, and tall, like the Anakim. 11 Like the Anakim, they were also regarded as Rephaim, but the Moabites called them Emim. 12 The Horites also lived in Seir formerly, but the descendants of Esau drove them out and destroyed them and settled in their place, just as Israel did to the land the the LORD gave them as their possession. 13 "Now get up and cross the Zered Valley." So we crossed the Zered Valley. 14 The time we spent traveling from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered Valley was thirty-eight years, until the whole generation of fighting men had died off from the camp, as the the LORD had sworn to them. 15 Indeed, the hand of the the LORD was against them, to destroy them from the camp, until they were gone. 16 So when all the fighting men had finally died among the people, 17 the the LORD spoke to me and said, 18 "Today you are to pass by the territory of Moab at Ar. 19 When you come near the Ammonites, do not harass them or provoke them, for I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites as a possession, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as a possession." 20 That too is regarded as a land of the Rephaim. The Rephaim formerly lived there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim. 21 They were a people great, numerous, and tall, like the Anakim, but the the LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, who drove them out and settled in their place. 22 He did the same for the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites before them. They drove them out and settled in their place, and they remain there to this day. 23 The Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, were destroyed by the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor and settled in their place.

The Defeat of Sihon

24 "Arise, set out, and cross the Arnon Valley. See, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to take possession of it and engage him in battle. 25 This day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you upon all the peoples under the whole heaven, who will hear your report and tremble and be in anguish because of you." 26 So I sent messengers from the Wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying, 27 "Let me pass through your land. I will stay on the main road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left. 28 Sell me food for money so that I may eat, and give me water for money so that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot, 29 just as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land the the LORD our God is giving us." 30 But Sihon king of Heshbon refused to let us pass through, for the the LORD your God hardened his spirit and made his heart stubborn, that He might give him into your hand, as he is this day. 31 And the the LORD said to me, "See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession, that you may inherit his land." 32 Then Sihon and all his people came out to meet us in battle at Jahaz. 33 And the the LORD our God gave him over to us, and we struck him down, along with his sons and all his people. 34 At that time we captured all his cities and completely destroyed every city—its men, women, and children. We left no survivors. 35 We took only the livestock and the spoil of the cities we captured as plunder for ourselves. 36 From Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and from the city in the valley, even as far as Gilead, there was not a city too strong for us. The The LORD our God gave everything into our hands. 37 But we did not go near the land of the Ammonites, or anywhere along the Jabbok River, or the cities of the hill country, or anywhere the the LORD our God had forbidden us.
