The LORD Questions His People

1 This is what the the LORD says: "Where is the paper that says I divorced your mother? Or which of My creditors did I sell you to? Look, you were sold because of your sins, and your mother was sent away because of your rebellion."

The LORD's Power to Save

2 "Why was no one here when I came? Why did no one answer when I called? Is My hand too short to buy you back? Or do I have no power to save you? Look, with one strong word I can dry up the sea and turn rivers into a desert. The fish rot without water and die of thirst. 3 I can cover the sky with darkness and dress it in sackcloth."

The Servant's Suffering and Trust

4 The Lord the the LORD has given Me the tongue of a student, so I know how to help tired people with words. He wakes Me up each morning; He opens My ear to listen like a student.

5 The Lord the the LORD has opened My ear, and I did not refuse. I did not turn back.

6 I gave My back to those who strike, and My cheeks to those who pull out My beard. I did not hide My face from shame and spit.

7 The Lord the the LORD helps Me, so I have not been disgraced. That is why I set My face like hard stone and know I will not be put to shame.

8 The One who says I am right is near. Who will argue with Me? Let us face each other. Who is My accuser? Let him come near Me.

9 Look, the Lord the the LORD helps Me. Who can say I am guilty? Look, they will all wear out like old clothes, and moths will eat them up.

A Warning and a Call to Trust

10 "Who among you fears the the LORD and obeys His Servant? Who walks in the dark without light? Let that person trust in the the LORD's name and lean on his God."

11 "Look, all of you who start a fire and arm yourselves with flaming arrows—walk in the light of your own fire and in the flames you lit. This is what you will get from My hand: You will lie down in torment."
