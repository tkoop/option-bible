The LORD Calls Jonah

1 The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai. 2 The LORD said, "Get up and go to the great city of Nineveh. Speak out against it, because I have seen how evil its people are." 3 But Jonah got up to run away to Tarshish, away from the LORD. He went down to Joppa, found a ship going to Tarshish, paid his fare, and got on board to sail with them to Tarshish, away from the LORD.

A Storm Hits the Ship

4 Then the LORD sent a powerful wind on the sea. A huge storm came up, and the ship was about to break apart. 5 The sailors were afraid. Each man prayed to his own god. They threw the cargo into the sea to make the ship lighter. But Jonah had gone down into the bottom of the ship. He lay down and fell into a deep sleep. 6 The captain came to him and said, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray to your god! Maybe your god will notice us and keep us from dying."

7 The sailors said to each other, "Come, let's throw lots to find out who caused this trouble." They threw lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

8 They said to him, "Tell us, please, who brought this trouble on us. What do you do for a living? Where do you come from? What country are you from? What people do you belong to?"

9 He answered, "I am a Hebrew. I worship the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land."

10 The men were very afraid. They asked him, "What have you done?" They knew he was running away from the LORD, because he had told them.

Jonah Is Thrown into the Sea

11 The sea was getting rougher and rougher. So they asked him, "What should we do to you so the sea will calm down for us?"

12 He said to them, "Pick me up and throw me into the sea. Then it will calm down for you. I know this terrible storm has come on you because of me."

13 Instead, the men rowed hard to get back to land. But they could not, because the sea was getting rougher and rougher against them.

14 Then they cried out to the LORD, "O LORD, please do not let us die for taking this man's life. Do not blame us for killing an innocent man, because You, O LORD, have done what You wanted."

15 Then they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea, and the sea calmed down.

16 The men were very afraid of the LORD. They offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made promises to Him.

Jonah Swallowed by a Fish

17 The LORD sent a huge fish to swallow Jonah. Jonah was inside the fish for three days and three nights.
