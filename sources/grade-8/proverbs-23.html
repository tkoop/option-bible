Be Careful with Rulers and Wealth

1 When you sit down to eat with a ruler, pay close attention to what is in front of you. 2 If you have a big appetite, be very careful not to overeat. 3 Do not long for his rich food, for it can fool you. 4 Do not wear yourself out trying to get rich; be wise enough to stop. 5 When you look at wealth, it can disappear. It seems to grow wings and fly away like an eagle.

Avoid Greedy and Foolish People

6 Do not eat with someone who is stingy, and do not crave his rich food. 7 He is the kind of person who keeps track of every bite. He says, “Eat and drink,” but he does not really care about you. 8 You will throw up the food you ate, and your kind words will be wasted.

9 Do not speak to a fool, for he will laugh at your wise words. 10 Do not move an old boundary stone, and do not take over the fields of orphans. 11 Their Redeemer is strong, and He will take up their case against you.

Accept Discipline and Fear the LORD

12 Give your heart to instruction and your ears to words of knowledge. 13 Do not refuse to discipline a child. If you discipline him with a rod, he will not die. 14 You may strike him with a rod and save his life from the grave.

15 My son, if your heart is wise, my heart will be glad. 16 My whole heart will rejoice when you speak what is right. 17 Do not envy sinners in your heart, but keep on living in fear of the LORD all day long. 18 Surely there is a future for you, and your hope will not be taken away.

Choose Wisdom and Avoid Temptation

19 Listen, my son, and be wise, and guide your heart on the right path. 20 Do not spend time with people who drink too much wine or eat too much food. 21 Drunkards and gluttons become poor, and laziness leaves them in rags.

22 Listen to your father, who gave you life, and do not look down on your mother when she is old. 23 Buy the truth and do not sell it. Get wisdom, discipline, and understanding. 24 The father of a righteous child will be very glad, and the one who has a wise son will delight in him. 25 May your father and mother be glad, and may the one who gave birth to you rejoice.

26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes be happy with my ways. 27 A prostitute is a deep pit, and an unfaithful woman is a narrow well. 28 She waits like a robber and leads many men to be unfaithful.

29 Who has trouble? Who has sorrow? Who has arguments? Who has complaints? Who has wounds for no reason? Who has red eyes? 30 Those who stay late over wine and go to taste mixed drinks. 31 Do not stare at wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup, and when it goes down smoothly.

32 In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a viper. 33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind will say twisted things. 34 You will be like someone sleeping in the middle of the sea, or like someone sleeping on top of the mast. 35 “They struck me, but I did not feel it. They beat me, but I did not know it. When will I wake up? I will seek it again.”
