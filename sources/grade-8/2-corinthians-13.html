Paul's Final Warning to the Corinthians

1 This is the third time I am coming to you. Every charge must be proved by the words of two or three witnesses. 2 I already warned you the second time when I was with you, and now, while I am away, I warn those who sinned before and all the rest: If I come again, I will not spare you. 3 Since you are looking for proof that Christ is speaking in me, He is not weak toward you, but He is powerful among you. 4 He was crucified in weakness, but He lives by God's power. We too are weak in Him, but we will live with Him by God's power for you.

Examine Yourselves

5 Test yourselves to see if you are in the faith. Check yourselves. Do you not know that Jesus Christ is in you, unless you fail the test? 6 I hope you will know that we are not failing. 7 I pray to God that you do no evil. I do not pray this so we may look approved, but so you may do what is good, even if we seem disapproved. 8 We cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth. 9 We are glad when we are weak and you are strong. This is what we pray: that you may be made complete. 10 That is why I write these things while I am away, so that when I am with you, I will not have to use sharp words, using the authority the Lord gave me to build you up, not tear you down.

Final Words and Blessing

11 Finally, brothers and sisters, rejoice. Aim to be restored. Be comforted. Agree with one another. Live in peace. And the God of love and peace will be with you. 12 Greet one another with a holy kiss. 13 All the saints greet you. 14 May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sharing of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.
