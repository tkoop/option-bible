The Last Plagues and the Victorious Singers

1 Then I saw another great and amazing sign in heaven: seven angels holding the seven last plagues. God’s anger is completed in them. 2 I saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire. Those who had won the victory over the beast, its image, and the number of its name were standing on that glassy sea. They had God’s harps. 3 They sang the song of God’s servant Moses and the song of the Lamb, saying, “Great and amazing are Your works, Lord God Almighty! Fair and true are Your ways, King of the nations! 4 Who will not fear, Lord, and give glory to Your name? For You alone are holy. All nations will come and worship before You, because Your right actions have been shown.”

The Temple Opens in Heaven

5 After this I looked, and the temple of the tent of witness was opened in heaven. 6 The seven angels who had the seven plagues came out of the temple. They were dressed in clean, bright linen and wore gold sashes around their chests.

Bowls of God’s Wrath

7 One of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven gold bowls. The bowls were full of the anger of God, who lives forever and ever. 8 The temple was filled with smoke from God’s glory and power. No one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.
