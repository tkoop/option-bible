The LORD Knows All About Me

1 The LORD, You have searched me and know me. 2 You know when I sit down and when I get up. You understand my thoughts even from far away. 3 You see me when I go out and when I lie down. You are familiar with all my ways. 4 Before a word is on my tongue, You, the LORD, know all about it. 5 You are all around me, behind and before, and You have placed Your hand on me. 6 This knowledge is too amazing for me. It is too high for me to reach.

God Is Everywhere

7 Where can I go from Your Spirit? Where can I run from Your presence? 8 If I go up to the heavens, You are there. If I make my bed in the place of the dead, You are there too. 9 If I fly with the wings of the morning or settle far across the sea, 10 even there Your hand will guide me, and Your right hand will hold me. 11 If I say, "Surely the darkness will hide me, and the light will become night around me," 12 even the darkness is not dark to You. The night shines like the day, because darkness is like light to You.

God Made Me

13 You created my deepest self. You knit me together in my mother's womb. 14 I praise You because I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Your works are wonderful, and I know that very well. 15 My body was not hidden from You when I was made in secret, when I was woven together deep in the earth. 16 Your eyes saw my unfinished form. All my days were written in Your book before even one of them happened.

17 How precious Your thoughts are to me, God! How great is the number of them! 18 If I tried to count them, they would be more than the grains of sand. When I wake up, I am still with You.

19 If only You, God, would kill the wicked! Get away from me, you bloodthirsty people! 20 They speak against You with evil plans. Your enemies misuse Your name for no good reason. 21 the LORD, do I not hate those who hate You? Do I not hate those who rise up against You? 22 I hate them completely. I count them as my enemies.

Search My Heart

23 Search me, God, and know my heart. Test me and know my worried thoughts. 24 See if there is any hurtful way in me, and lead me in the way that lasts forever.
