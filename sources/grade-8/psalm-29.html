Give Glory to the LORD

A Psalm of David. 1 Give to the LORD, O sons of God, give to the LORD glory and strength. 2 Give to the LORD the glory His name deserves; worship the LORD in the beauty of His holiness.

3 The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is over many waters. 4 The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is full of majesty.

5 The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; yes, the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. 6 He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox. 7 The voice of the LORD strikes with flames of fire.

8 The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the Wilderness of Kadesh. 9 The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare, and in His temple all cry, "Glory!"

10 The LORD sits as King over the flood; the LORD is King forever. 11 the LORD gives strength to His people; the LORD blesses His people with peace.
