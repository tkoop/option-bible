King Lemuel's Mother Speaks

1 "The words of King Lemuel — an oracle his mother taught him: 2 What, my son? What, son of my womb? What, son of my vows?

3 "Don't spend your strength on women, or your ways on those who ruin kings.

4 "It isn't for kings, Lemuel — it isn't for kings to drink wine, or for rulers to crave strong drink, 5 lest they drink and forget what's been decreed, and twist the rights of all who are oppressed. 6 Give strong drink to those who are dying, and wine to those who are in anguish. 7 Let them drink and forget their poverty, and remember their misery no more.

A Call to Defend the Needy

8 "Speak up for those who can't speak for themselves, for the rights of all who have nothing. 9 Speak up and judge fairly; defend the rights of the poor and needy."

The Wife of Noble Character

10 A wife of noble character — who can find her? She is worth far more than rubies. 11 Her husband trusts her completely, and he lacks nothing of value. 12 She brings him good, not harm, all the days of her life.

13 She looks for wool and flax and works with eager hands. 14 She is like the merchant ships, bringing her food from far away. 15 She rises while it's still night and provides food for her household, and portions for her servant girls.

16 She considers a field and buys it; from her earnings she plants a vineyard. 17 She dresses herself with strength and makes her arms strong. 18 She sees that her trading is profitable, and her lamp doesn't go out at night. 19 She holds the distaff in her hands and grips the spindle with her fingers.

20 She opens her arms to the poor and reaches out her hands to the needy. 21 When it snows, she isn't afraid for her household, for all of them are clothed in scarlet. 22 She makes coverings for her bed; she's dressed in fine linen and purple.

23 Her husband is respected at the city gate, where he sits among the elders of the land. 24 She makes linen garments and sells them, and supplies the merchants with sashes. 25 She's clothed with strength and dignity, and she can laugh at the days to come.

26 She opens her mouth with wisdom, and faithful teaching is on her tongue. 27 She watches over the affairs of her household and doesn't eat the bread of idleness.

28 Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband too, and he praises her: 29 "Many women do noble things, but you surpass them all."

30 Charm is misleading and beauty doesn't last, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised. 31 Give her the reward she's earned, and let her works bring her praise at the city gate.
