The LORD Warns Israel About Idols

1 Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, O house of Israel. 2 This is what the LORD says: "Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by signs in the heavens, though the nations are terrified by them. 3 For the customs of the peoples are worthless; they cut a tree out of the forest, and a craftsman shapes it with his chisel. 4 They adorn it with silver and gold; they fasten it with hammer and nails so it will not topple. 5 Like a scarecrow in a cucumber field, their idols cannot speak; they must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no harm, nor can they do any good."

6 No one is like You, O the LORD; You are great, and Your name is mighty in power. 7 Who would not fear You, O King of the nations? For this is Your due. Among all the wise leaders of the nations and in all their kingdoms, there is no one like You. 8 They are altogether senseless and foolish; instruction from idols is wood! 9 Hammered silver is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz. The work of the craftsman and of the goldsmith is clothed in blue and purple—all the work of skilled men. 10 But the LORD is the true God; He is the living God and eternal King. The earth trembles at His wrath, and the nations cannot endure His anger. 11 Tell them this: 'These gods, who did not make the heavens and the earth, will perish from the earth and from under the heavens.'

The LORD Made Everything

12 He made the earth by His power; He established the world by His wisdom and stretched out the heavens by His understanding. 13 When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar; He causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning with the rain and brings the wind from His storehouses. 14 Every man is senseless and without knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idols. For his molten images are a fraud, and there is no breath in them. 15 They are worthless, a work of mockery. In the time of their punishment they will perish. 16 The Portion of Jacob is not like these, for He is the Maker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of His inheritance. the LORD of Hosts is His name.

Judgment Is Coming on Judah

17 Gather up your belongings from the ground, O daughter living under siege. 18 For this is what the LORD says: "Look, I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this time, and I will bring distress on them so that they may be captured." 19 Woe to me because of my injury; my wound is painful. Yet I said, 'This is my sickness, and I must bear it.' 20 My tent is destroyed, and all my ropes are snapped. My children have left me and are no more; no one is left to pitch my tent or to set up my curtains. 21 For the shepherds are senseless and do not seek the LORD; therefore they do not prosper, and all their flock is scattered. 22 Listen! The sound of a report is coming—a great commotion from the land of the north to make the cities of Judah a wasteland, a haunt of jackals.

Jeremiah Prays to the LORD

23 I know, O the LORD, that a man's way is not his own; no one who walks directs his own steps. 24 Correct me, O the LORD, but only with justice—not in Your anger, or You will bring me to nothing. 25 Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not acknowledge You, and on the families that do not call on Your name. For they have devoured Jacob; they have devoured him and consumed him, and they have laid waste his homeland.
