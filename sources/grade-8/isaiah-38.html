Hezekiah's Illness and Prayer

1 In those days Hezekiah became sick and was near death. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, "This is what the the LORD says: Put your house in order, because you are going to die. You will not live."

2 Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the the LORD.

3 He said, "Please, the LORD, remember how I have walked before You in truth and with a whole heart. I have done what is good in Your sight." Then Hezekiah cried and wept bitterly.

God Promises Healing and a Sign

4 Then the word of the the LORD came to Isaiah:

5 "Go and say to Hezekiah, 'This is what the the LORD, the God of your father David, says: I have heard your prayer and seen your tears. I will add fifteen years to your life. 6 I will rescue you and this city from the king of Assyria, and I will protect this city. 7 This will be the sign to you from the the LORD that the the LORD will do what He has promised: 8 I will make the shadow on the steps of Ahaz go back ten steps.' So the sun moved back the ten steps it had gone down.

Hezekiah's Song of Thanks

9 This is the writing of Hezekiah king of Judah after he was sick and got well:

10 I said, "In the middle of my life I must go through the gates of the grave. I am robbed of the rest of my years." 11 I said, "I will not see the the LORD in the land of the living. I will no longer look at people who live in this world."

12 My home is pulled up and carried away from me like a shepherd's tent. I have rolled up my life like a weaver. He cuts me off from the loom. Day and night, You bring me to an end. 13 I waited until morning. Like a lion, He breaks all my bones. Day and night, You bring me to an end.

14 I chirp like a swallow or a crane. I moan like a dove. My eyes are weak as I look to the heights. Lord, I am in trouble; be my guarantee. 15 What can I say? He has spoken to me, and He has done it. I will walk carefully all my years because my soul is bitter.

16 Lord, by these things people live. My spirit also lives by them. You restore me to health and keep me alive. 17 I went through bitter pain, but it led to peace. You have loved my soul and saved it from the pit of destruction, because You have thrown all my sins behind Your back.

18 The grave does not thank You. Death does not praise You. Those who go down into the pit do not hope for Your truth. 19 The living, the living, they thank You, as I do today. Fathers tell their children about Your truth. 20 The the LORD came to save me. We will sing my songs with stringed instruments all the days of our lives in the house of the the LORD.

Hezekiah Is Healed

21 Isaiah had said, "Let them take a cake of figs and put it on the boil, and he will live." 22 Hezekiah had asked, "What is the sign that I will go up to the house of the the LORD?"
