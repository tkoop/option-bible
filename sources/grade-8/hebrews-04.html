God's Rest Is Still Open

1 So, since the promise still stands that we can enter God's rest, let us be careful. None of you should think you have missed it. 2 We heard the good news too, just like those people did. But the message they heard did not help them, because they did not believe it.

3 We who believe are entering that rest. God said, "As I swore in My anger, 'They will never enter My rest.'" Yet His work was finished when the world was made. 4 In one place He said about the seventh day, "God rested on the seventh day from all His work." 5 And again He said, "They will never enter My rest."

6 So it is still open for some people to enter. Those who first heard the good news did not enter because they would not obey. 7 So God set another day, called "Today." Long after that time, He spoke through David, as it was said before: "Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts."

8 If Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day. 9 So a Sabbath rest is still left for God's people. 10 Anyone who enters God's rest also rests from his own work, just as God rested from His. 11 So let us work hard to enter that rest. Then no one will fall into the same kind of disobedience.

God's Word Sees Everything

12 God's word is alive and powerful. It is sharper than any sword with two edges. It cuts deep, even to the place where soul and spirit meet, and to joints and marrow. It judges what we think and want. 13 Nothing is hidden from God. Everything is open and uncovered before the eyes of the One we must answer to.

Jesus Is Our Great High Priest

14 Since we have a great high priest who has gone through the heavens—Jesus, the Son of God—let us hold firmly to what we believe. 15 Our high priest can feel our weaknesses. He was tested in every way, just like we are, but He never sinned. 16 So let us come boldly to the throne of grace. Then we will receive mercy and find grace to help us when we need it.
