Elihu Speaks to the Wise

1 Then Elihu spoke up and said:

2 "Listen to my words, you wise men; hear me, you who know. 3 The ear tests words just like the tongue tastes food. 4 Let's choose what is right. Let's decide among ourselves what is good.

5 "For Job has said,

6 'I am right, but God has taken away my justice. Even though I am right, people call me a liar. My wound will not heal, even though I haven't sinned.'

7 "What man is like Job? He drinks up scorn like water. 8 He keeps company with evildoers and walks with wicked people. 9 He said,

"'It does no good for a person to try to please God.'

God Is Always Just

10 "Therefore, listen to me, you men of understanding: God would never do evil, and the Almighty would never do wrong. 11 He pays back a person for what they do and makes them face the results of their ways. 12 Truly, God does not do evil, and the Almighty does not twist justice. 13 Who put Him in charge of the earth? Who laid the whole world on Him? 14 If He set His heart on it and gathered His spirit and breath to Himself, 15 all people would die at once, and everyone would return to dust.

God Sees Everything

16 "If you have understanding, hear this; listen to what I say. 17 Could someone who hates justice rule? Will you condemn the righteous and mighty One? 18 It is wrong to say such things to a king or noble. 19 How much more is it wrong to say them to God, who shows no favor to princes and doesn't value the rich over the poor, since all are the work of His hands. 20 They die in a moment; at midnight people are shaken and pass away; the mighty are removed without human hand.

21 "His eyes are on a person's ways, and He sees every step. 22 There is no darkness or deep shadow where evildoers can hide. 23 God doesn't need to investigate a person more before judging him. 24 He breaks the mighty without asking questions and puts others in their place. 25 Because He knows what they do, He overthrows them at night, and they are crushed. 26 He punishes them as wicked people where everyone can see, 27 because they turned away from following Him and didn't care about any of His ways. 28 So they made the cry of the poor reach Him, and He heard the cry of those who suffer. 29 If He keeps quiet, who can accuse Him? If He hides His face, who can see Him? This is true for a nation or a person. 30 So godless people don't rule and don't trap the people.

Job's Words Lack Wisdom

31 "Has anyone ever said to God, 'I have been punished; I won't sin anymore'? 32 'Teach me what I don't see; if I have done wrong, I won't do it again'? 33 Should God repay you the way you want, just because you refuse His way? You have to decide, not I. So tell me what you know.

34 "Men of understanding will say to me, and wise people who hear me will say,

35 'Job speaks without knowledge; his words show no insight.'

36 "I wish Job would be tested to the end, because he answers like wicked men. 37 He adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God."
