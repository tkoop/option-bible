Hiram Helps David

1 Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David. He also sent cedar wood, builders, and stone workers to build David a house. 2 David knew the the LORD had made him king over Israel. He also knew his kingdom was raised high for the sake of His people Israel.

David's Family Grows

3 David took more wives in Jerusalem, and he had more sons and daughters. 4 These are the names of the children born to him there: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon; 5 Ibhar, Elishua, and Elpelet; 6 Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia; 7 Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.

David Defeats the Philistines

8 When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, they came to look for him. David heard about it and went out to meet them. 9 The Philistines arrived and spread out across the Valley of Rephaim.

10 David asked God, "Should I go up against the Philistines? Will You give them to me?"

The LORD answered him, "Go up, and I will give them into your hand."

11 David and his men went up to Baal Perazim. David defeated the Philistines there. He said, "God has broken through my enemies like a flood of water." So that place was called Baal Perazim.

12 The Philistines left their gods there. David ordered them burned, and they were burned in the fire.

13 The Philistines came again and spread out in the valley.

14 David asked God again. God said to him, "Do not go up after them. Circle around them and come at them from in front of the balsam trees. 15 When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then go out to battle. God has gone out before you to strike the Philistine camp."

16 David did what God had commanded. He and his men struck the Philistine camp from Gibeon to Gezer.

David's Fame Spreads

17 David became famous in all the lands. The LORD put fear of him in all the nations.
