Judgment on Jerusalem

1 How terrible for the city that oppresses people! It is rebellious and unclean. 2 It did not listen. It did not accept correction. It did not trust in the LORD. It did not draw near to its God. 3 Its leaders are like roaring lions. Its judges are like wolves that hunt at night. They leave nothing for the morning. 4 Its prophets are reckless and treacherous. Its priests have made the holy place unclean and have twisted the law. 5 The LORD is in the city. He is good and does no wrong. Every morning He gives fair judgment, and it never fails. But the wicked do not know shame.

6 I have destroyed nations. Their strong towers are empty. I ruined their streets so no one walks there. Their cities are destroyed, with no one left to live in them. 7 I said, "Surely you will fear Me and accept My correction. Then your home will not be destroyed, and I will not punish you as I planned." But they were quick to do every evil thing.

The LORD Will Judge the Nations

8 "So wait for Me," declares the LORD, "until the day I rise to judge. I have decided to gather the nations and kingdoms and pour out My anger on them—all My burning anger. The whole earth will be burned up by My jealous anger."

9 For then I will give the nations pure speech, so all of them can call on the LORD's name and serve Him side by side. 10 From beyond the rivers of Cush, My worshipers, the children of My scattered people, will bring Me their gifts. 11 On that day you will not be ashamed of the wrong things you did against Me. For then I will remove from you the proud boasters, and you will stop being proud on My holy mountain. 12 I will leave among you people who are humble and poor, and they will trust in the LORD's name. 13 The rest of Israel will not do wrong or tell lies. No deceitful words will be found in their mouths. They will eat and rest, and no one will scare them.

Joy and Restoration for Jerusalem

14 Sing, people of Zion! Shout, Israel! Be glad and celebrate with all your heart, people of Jerusalem! 15 The LORD has taken away your punishment. He has turned your enemy away. The LORD, the King of Israel, is among you. You will never again be afraid of disaster.

16 On that day people will say to Jerusalem, "Do not be afraid, Zion. Do not let your hands become weak."

17 The LORD your God is with you. He is mighty to save. He will take great delight in you. He will calm you with His love. He will rejoice over you with singing.

18 I will gather those among you who are sad because the festivals are gone, so you will not be shamed anymore. 19 At that time I will deal with all who hurt you. I will save the lame and gather the outcast. I will give them praise and honor in every land where they were shamed. 20 "At that time I will bring you home. When I gather you, I will make you famous and praised among all the nations, when I bring your people back from captivity before your eyes," says the LORD.
