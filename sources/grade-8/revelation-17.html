The Great Prostitute on Many Waters

1 Then one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke to me. He said, “Come, you must see the punishment of the great prostitute who sits on many waters. 2 The kings of the earth committed sexual immorality with her, and the people who live on earth got drunk on the wine of her immorality.”

The Woman and the Scarlet Beast

3 He carried me away in the Spirit to a desert, and I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast. The beast was full of names that insult God, and it had seven heads and ten horns. 4 The woman was dressed in purple and scarlet, and she wore gold, precious stones, and pearls. She held a golden cup in her hand, full of detestable things and the unclean things of her sexual immorality. 5 On her forehead a name was written: MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF PROSTITUTES AND OF THE DETESTABLE THINGS OF THE EARTH. 6 I saw the woman drunk with the blood of God’s holy people and with the blood of those who died for Jesus. When I saw her, I was greatly amazed.

The Angel Explains the Vision

7 The angel said to me, “Why are you amazed? I will explain to you the mystery of the woman and of the beast that carries her, the one with seven heads and ten horns. 8 The beast you saw was, and is not, and is about to come up out of the bottomless pit and go to destruction. The people living on earth whose names have not been written in the Book of Life since the world was made will be amazed when they see the beast, because it was, and is not, and yet will come. 9 This calls for a mind with wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains where the woman sits. 10 They are also seven kings. Five have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come. When he comes, he must remain for a little while.”

11 “The beast that was, and is not, is an eighth king. He belongs to the seven, and he goes to destruction. 12 The ten horns you saw are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom, but for one hour they will receive power as kings along with the beast. 13 They have one purpose, and they give their power and authority to the beast. 14 They will make war against the Lamb, but the Lamb will overcome them, because He is Lord of lords and King of kings. Those with Him are called, chosen, and faithful.”

The Waters and the Prostitute’s End

15 Then he said to me, “The waters you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples, crowds, nations, and languages. 16 The ten horns and the beast you saw will hate the prostitute. They will leave her ruined and naked, eat her flesh, and burn her with fire.”

17 “For God has put it into their hearts to carry out His purpose. They will agree to give their kingdom to the beast until God’s words are fulfilled. 18 The woman you saw is the great city that rules over the kings of the earth.”
