Nicodemus Visits Jesus at Night

1 There was a man named Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a leader among the Jews. 2 He came to Jesus at night and said, "Rabbi, we know You are a teacher who has come from God, because no one could do the signs You do unless God is with Him."

3 Jesus answered, "Truly, truly, I tell you, unless someone is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

4 Nicodemus said to Him, "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he go back into his mother's womb and be born a second time?"

5 Jesus answered, "Truly, truly, I tell you, unless someone is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. 6 What is born from the flesh is flesh, and what is born from the Spirit is spirit. 7 Do not be amazed that I said to you, 'You must be born again.' 8 The wind blows where it wants to, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."

9 Nicodemus asked Him, "How can these things be?"

10 Jesus answered, "You are Israel's teacher, and you do not understand these things? 11 Truly, truly, I tell you, we speak about what we know, and we testify about what we have seen, but you do not receive our testimony. 12 If I have told you earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things? 13 No one has gone up to heaven except the One who came down from heaven—the Son of Man.

God's Love for the World

14 "And just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, 15 so that everyone who believes in Him may have eternal life. 16 For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life. 17 For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through Him. 18 Whoever believes in Him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe is condemned already, because he has not believed in the name of God's one and only Son. 19 This is the judgment: Light has come into the world, but people loved darkness instead of light because their deeds were evil. 20 Everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that his deeds will not be exposed. 21 But whoever does what is true comes to the light, so that it may be seen that his deeds are done in God."

Jesus and John the Baptist

22 After this, Jesus and His disciples went into the Judean countryside, and He stayed there with them and baptized. 23 John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because there was plenty of water there, and people kept coming to be baptized. 24 For John had not yet been put in prison.

25 Then a disagreement arose between some of John's disciples and a Jew about ceremonial washing. 26 They came to John and said, "Rabbi, the man who was with you across the Jordan—the one you testified about—look, He is baptizing, and everyone is going to Him."

John Points to Jesus

27 John answered, "A person cannot receive even one thing unless it is given to him from heaven. 28 You yourselves can testify that I said, 'I am not the Christ, but I have been sent ahead of Him.' 29 The bride belongs to the bridegroom. The friend who stands by and listens for Him is overjoyed when he hears the bridegroom's voice. That joy of mine is now complete. 30 He must become greater, and I must become less.

31 "The One who comes from above is above all. The one who is from the earth speaks about earthly things, and the One who comes from heaven is above all. 32 He testifies to what He has seen and heard, but no one accepts His testimony. 33 Whoever accepts His testimony has confirmed that God is true. 34 For the One whom God sent speaks God's words, because God gives the Spirit without limit. 35 The Father loves the Son and has put everything in His hands. 36 Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life, but whoever refuses to obey the Son will not see life; instead, God's wrath remains on him."
