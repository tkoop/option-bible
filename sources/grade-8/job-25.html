Bildad Speaks Again

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

God's Greatness and Human Weakness

2 “God rules with power and fear. He brings peace in His high places. 3 Can anyone count His armies? Is there anyone His light does not reach? 4 How can a person be right with God? How can someone born of a woman be clean? 5 Even the moon does not shine brightly, and the stars are not pure in His eyes. 6 How much less a person, who is like a maggot, and a human being, who is like a worm!”
