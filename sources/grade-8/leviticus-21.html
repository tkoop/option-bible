Priests and the Dead

1 The LORD said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron and his sons and tell them: No priest among his people may make himself unclean by touching a dead body, 2 except for his close relatives—his mother, father, son, daughter, and brother. 3 He may also make himself unclean for his virgin sister who is close to him and has never married. 4 He must not make himself unclean for those related to him by marriage, and so dishonor himself."

Priests Must Stay Holy

5 They must not shave their heads bald, trim the edges of their beards, or cut their bodies. 6 They must be holy to their God and not dishonor His name, because they present the LORD's food offerings, the bread of their God. So they must be holy. 7 They must not marry a woman who has been a prostitute or who has been divorced from her husband, because the priest is holy to his God. 8 You must set him apart as holy, because he offers the bread of your God. He must be holy to you, because I, the LORD, am holy—I am the One who makes you holy. 9 If a priest's daughter makes herself unclean by becoming a prostitute, she disgraces her father; she must be burned with fire.

Rules for the High Priest

10 The priest who is highest among his brothers—the one who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and who has been set apart to wear the holy clothes—must not let his hair hang loose or tear his clothes. 11 He must not go near any dead body or make himself unclean, even for his father or mother. 12 He must not leave the holy place or dishonor the holy place of his God, because the special oil that sets him apart to his God is on him. I am the LORD. 13 He must marry a woman who is still a virgin. 14 He must not marry a widow, a divorced woman, a woman who has been defiled, or a prostitute. He may marry only a virgin from his own people, 15 so that he does not dishonor his children among his people. I am the LORD, who makes him holy.

Priests with Physical Defects

16 The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 17 "Speak to Aaron and say: In every generation, no man among your descendants who has a physical defect may come near to offer the bread of his God. 18 No man with a defect may come near: no blind man, no lame man, no man with a damaged face or a deformed limb, 19 no man with an injured foot or an injured hand, 20 no hunchback or dwarf, no man with an eye defect or a skin disease or scabs, and no man with crushed testicles. 21 No man from the family of Aaron the priest who has a defect may come near to present the LORD's food offerings. Because he has a defect, he must not come near to offer the bread of his God. 22 He may still eat the bread of his God, both from the most holy offerings and from the holy offerings. 23 But he must not go behind the curtain or come near the altar, because he has a defect. He must not dishonor My holy places, for I am the LORD, who makes them holy."

24 So Moses spoke to Aaron, his sons, and all the people of Israel.
