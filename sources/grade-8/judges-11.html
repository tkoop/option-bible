Jephthah, a Mighty Warrior

1 Jephthah the Gileadite was a mighty warrior. He was the son of a prostitute, and Gilead was his father. 2 Gilead's wife also had sons. When they grew up, they drove Jephthah away. They said, "You will not share in our father's inheritance, because you are the son of another woman." 3 So Jephthah ran away from his brothers and lived in the land of Tob. Worthless men gathered around him and went out with him.

The Elders Ask Jephthah to Lead

4 After some time, the Ammonites fought against Israel. 5 When the Ammonites attacked Israel, the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah back from Tob. 6 They said to him, "Come and be our commander, so we can fight the Ammonites."

7 Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "Didn't you hate me and drive me out of my father's house? Why do you come to me now, when you are in trouble?"

8 The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "That is why we are coming back to you now. Go with us and fight the Ammonites, and you will be head over all who live in Gilead."

9 Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "If you bring me back to fight the Ammonites and the LORD gives them into my hand, I will be your head."

10 The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "The LORD is witness between us. We will do just as you say."

11 So Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead. The people made him head and commander over them. Then Jephthah spoke all his words before the LORD at Mizpah.

Jephthah Sends Messengers to the Ammonite King

12 Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites. They said, "What do you have against me, that you come to fight in my land?"

13 The king of the Ammonites answered Jephthah's messengers, "When Israel came up from Egypt, they took my land from the Arnon to the Jabbok and the Jordan. Now give it back peacefully."

14 Jephthah sent messengers again to the king of the Ammonites. 15 He said, "This is what Jephthah says: Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the Ammonites. 16 When Israel came up from Egypt, they traveled through the wilderness to the Red Sea and came to Kadesh. 17 Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, asking to pass through his land, but the king of Edom would not listen. Israel also sent messengers to the king of Moab, but he refused. So Israel stayed at Kadesh. 18 Then they went through the wilderness and around the land of Edom and Moab. They came from the east to the land of Moab and camped beyond the Arnon. They did not enter Moab's border, because the Arnon was Moab's border.

19 "Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, king of Heshbon, asking to pass through his land to their place. 20 But Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his border. He gathered all his people, camped at Jahaz, and fought against Israel. 21 But the LORD, the God of Israel, gave Sihon and all his people into Israel's hand. Israel struck them down and took all the land of the Amorites, where those people lived. 22 Israel took all the territory of the Amorites from the Arnon to the Jabbok and from the wilderness to the Jordan.

23 "Now the LORD, the God of Israel, has driven out the Amorites before His people Israel. Should you take their land? 24 You may keep whatever your god Chemosh gives you. But we will keep everything the LORD our God has given us by driving them out before us. 25 Are you better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever argue with Israel or fight against them? 26 Israel has lived in Heshbon and its villages, Aroer and its villages, and all the cities along the Arnon for three hundred years. Why didn't you take them back during that time? 27 I have not sinned against you, but you are doing me wrong by fighting me. May the LORD, the Judge, judge today between the Israelites and the Ammonites."

28 But the king of the Ammonites did not listen to Jephthah's message.

Jephthah's Vow and His Daughter

29 Then the Spirit of the LORD came upon Jephthah. He passed through Gilead and Manasseh, passed through Mizpah of Gilead, and from Mizpah he crossed over to the Ammonites.

30 Jephthah made a vow to the LORD. He said, "If you will give the Ammonites into my hand, 31 then whatever comes out of the doors of my house to meet me when I return safely from the Ammonites will belong to the LORD, and I will offer it as a burnt offering."

32 So Jephthah crossed over to fight the Ammonites, and the LORD gave them into his hand. 33 He struck them down from Aroer to Minnith, capturing twenty cities, as far as Abel-keramim. It was a very great defeat, and the Ammonites were defeated by the Israelites.

34 When Jephthah came to his home at Mizpah, his daughter came out to meet him with tambourines and dancing. She was his only child; he had no son or daughter besides her.

35 When he saw her, he tore his clothes and said, "Oh, my daughter! You have brought me great trouble. You are the one who has troubled me, because I made a promise to the LORD and I cannot take it back."

36 She said to him, "My father, you made a promise to the LORD. Do to me what you said, because the LORD has taken vengeance for you on your enemies, the Ammonites."

37 She said to her father, "Let me have this one thing: leave me alone for two months, so I can go up and down the mountains and cry because I will never marry. I and my friends will go."

38 He said, "Go," and sent her away for two months. She and her friends went up on the mountains and mourned because she would never marry.

39 After two months she returned to her father, and he did to her what he had promised. She had never been with a man. 40 This became a custom in Israel: every year the young women of Israel spend four days remembering the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite.
