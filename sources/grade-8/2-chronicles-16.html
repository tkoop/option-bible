Baasha Attacks Judah

1 In the thirty-sixth year of Asa's reign, Baasha king of Israel attacked Judah and built up Ramah. He did this so no one could leave or enter Asa's land. 2 Asa took silver and gold from the treasuries of the the LORD's temple and the royal palace. He sent it to Ben-hadad, king of Aram, who ruled in Damascus, and said, 3 "Make a treaty with me, as our fathers did. I have sent you silver and gold. Now go and break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so he will leave me alone."

4 Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent his army leaders against Israel's cities. They captured Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the storage cities of Naphtali. 5 When Baasha heard about this, he stopped building up Ramah and left the work unfinished. 6 Then King Asa brought all Judah together. They carried away the stones and wood Baasha had used in Ramah, and Asa used them to build up Geba and Mizpah.

Hanani Warns Asa

7 At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah and said, "Because you relied on the king of Aram instead of on the the LORD your God, the army of Aram's king has escaped from your hand. 8 Were not the Cushites and Libyans a huge army with many chariots and horsemen? Yet when you relied on the the LORD, He delivered them into your hand. 9 The LORD's eyes move back and forth over the whole earth. He wants to give strength to those whose hearts are fully devoted to Him. You have acted foolishly in this. From now on, you will have wars."

10 Asa was angry with the seer and put him in prison, because he was furious with him over this. At that time Asa also treated some of his people harshly.

Asa's Final Years

11 The things Asa did, from first to last, are written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 12 In the thirty-ninth year of his reign, Asa got a disease in his feet. It became very bad. Even in his sickness, he did not seek the the LORD, but only the doctors. 13 Asa died in the forty-first year of his reign. 14 He was buried in the tomb he had cut for himself in the City of David. He was laid on a funeral bed filled with spices and many mixed perfumes. They also burned a large fire in his honor.
