Freed from the Law Through Christ

1 Or don't you know, brothers and sisters—for I am speaking to those who know the law—that the law has power over a person only as long as that person lives? 2 For example, a married woman is bound by law to her husband while he lives. But if her husband dies, she is released from the law that bound her to him. 3 So then, if she marries another man while her husband is still alive, people call her an adulteress. But if her husband dies, she is free from that law, and she is not an adulteress if she marries another man.

4 In the same way, my brothers and sisters, you died to the law through the body of Christ, so that you could belong to another—to Him who was raised from the dead—so that we can produce good results for God. 5 For when we were ruled by our sinful desires, the sinful passions stirred up by the law were at work in our bodies, and that produced results that led to death. 6 But now we have died to what held us captive, so we are released from the law. We serve in the new way of the Spirit, not in the old way of written rules.

The Law Reveals Sin

7 What shall we say, then? Is the law sin? Not at all! Yet I would not have known what sin was except through the law. For I would not have known what coveting was if the law had not said, "You shall not covet." 8 But sin used the commandment to stir up in me every kind of coveting. For without the law, sin had no power. 9 Once I was alive apart from the law, but when the commandment came, sin became active and I died. 10 I found that the very commandment that was meant to bring life actually brought death. 11 For sin used the commandment to deceive me, and through it put me to death. 12 So the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, right, and good. 13 Did what is good, then, become death to me? By no means! But sin used what is good to bring about my death, so that sin might be shown for what it is. Through the commandment, sin became utterly sinful.

The Inner Struggle

14 We know that the law is spiritual, but I am unspiritual, sold as a slave to sin. 15 I do not understand what I do. For what I want to do, I do not do; but what I hate, I do. 16 And if I do what I do not want to do, I agree that the law is good. 17 As it is, it is no longer I who do it, but sin living in me. 18 For I know that nothing good lives in me—that is, in my sinful nature. For I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot carry it out. 19 For I do not do the good I want to do, but the evil I do not want to do—this I keep on doing. 20 Now if I do what I do not want to do, it is no longer I who do it, but sin living in me. 21 So I find this law at work: When I want to do good, evil is right there with me. 22 For in my inner being I delight in God's law. 23 But I see another law at work in my body, fighting against the law of my mind and making me a prisoner of the law of sin that lives in my body.

Rescue Through Jesus Christ

24 What a miserable person I am! Who will rescue me from this body that is subject to death? 25 Thanks be to God, who rescues me through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then, I myself serve the law of God with my mind, but with my sinful nature I serve the law of sin.
