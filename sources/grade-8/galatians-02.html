Paul Visits the Leaders in Jerusalem

1 Fourteen years later, I went up to Jerusalem again. Barnabas went with me, and I took Titus along. 2 I went because God told me to. I told the leaders there the good news I preach to the Gentiles. But I spoke privately to the ones who seemed important, so my work would not be wasted. 3 Even Titus, who was with me, was not forced to be circumcised, even though he is a Greek.

4 This happened because some false brothers sneaked in. They came in secretly to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus and to make us slaves. 5 We did not give in to them for even a moment, so the truth of the good news would stay with you.

6 The leaders who seemed important did not add anything to my message. It does not matter to me what they once were, because God does not show favoritism. 7 Instead, they saw that God had trusted me with the good news for the Gentiles, just as He had trusted Peter with the good news for the Jews. 8 The same God who worked through Peter as an apostle to the Jews also worked through me for the Gentiles.

9 James, Peter, and John seemed to be pillars. They understood the grace God had given me. They shook hands with Barnabas and me as partners. We would go to the Gentiles, and they would go to the Jews. 10 They only asked us to remember the poor, and that was something I was already eager to do.

Paul Confronts Peter in Antioch

11 But when Peter came to Antioch, I stood up to him face to face, because he was clearly wrong. 12 Before some men came from James, Peter ate with the Gentiles. But when they came, he pulled back and separated himself because he was afraid of the Jewish Christians. 13 The other Jews joined him in this hypocrisy, and even Barnabas was led astray.

14 When I saw that they were not acting in line with the truth of the good news, I said to Peter in front of everyone, "If you are a Jew but live like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you force Gentiles to live like Jews?"

Made Right by Faith, Not by the Law

15 We are Jews by birth, not Gentile sinners. 16 But we know that a person is not made right with God by obeying the law. A person is made right through faith in Jesus Christ. So we have put our faith in Christ Jesus, so we may be made right by faith in Christ and not by obeying the law. No one will be made right by obeying the law.

17 But if we are trying to be made right in Christ and we are still found to be sinners, does that make Christ a servant of sin? Of course not! 18 If I build again what I tore down, I show that I am a lawbreaker.

19 Through the law I died to the law, so I could live for God. 20 I have been crucified with Christ. I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in my body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me. 21 I do not reject God's grace. If being right with God came through the law, then Christ died for nothing.
