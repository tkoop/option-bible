Measuring God's Temple

1 I was given a measuring rod like a staff. A voice said, "Get up and measure God's temple, the altar, and the people who worship there. 2 But leave out the outer courtyard of the temple. Do not measure it, because it has been given to the nations. They will trample the holy city for forty-two months."

The Two Witnesses

3 I will give power to My two witnesses, and they will prophesy for 1,260 days while wearing sackcloth. 4 These witnesses are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth. 5 If anyone tries to hurt them, fire comes from their mouths and burns up their enemies. Anyone who tries to hurt them must be killed. 6 They have power to shut the sky so no rain falls during the days they prophesy. They also have power to turn the waters into blood and to strike the earth with every kind of plague as often as they want.

The Beast Kills the Witnesses

7 When they finish their testimony, the beast that comes up from the bottomless pit will fight against them. It will defeat them and kill them. 8 Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city. That city is called Sodom and Egypt in a spiritual sense, because that is where their Lord was crucified. 9 For three and a half days, people from every nation, tribe, language, and people will look at their bodies. They will not allow the bodies to be put in a tomb. 10 The people who live on the earth will be glad about this. They will celebrate and send gifts to one another, because these two prophets made life hard for everyone on earth.

11 After the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered them. They stood up on their feet, and great fear fell on everyone who saw them. 12 Then they heard a loud voice from heaven say, "Come up here." They went up to heaven in a cloud, and their enemies watched them. 13 At that same hour, there was a great earthquake. A tenth of the city fell, and seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake. The rest were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.

The Seventh Trumpet

14 The second woe has passed. Look, the third woe is coming soon. 15 The seventh angel blew his trumpet, and loud voices spoke in heaven. They said, "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ, and He will reign forever and ever." 16 The twenty-four elders who sit on their thrones before God fell on their faces and worshiped God. 17 They said, "We thank You, Lord God Almighty, who is and who was, because You have taken Your great power and begun to reign. 18 The nations were angry, but Your wrath has come. The time has come to judge the dead and to reward Your servants the prophets, the holy people, and everyone who fears Your name, both small and great. It is also time to destroy those who destroy the earth." 19 Then God's temple in heaven was opened, and the ark of His covenant was seen in His temple. There were flashes of lightning, voices, thunder, an earthquake, and a great hailstorm.
