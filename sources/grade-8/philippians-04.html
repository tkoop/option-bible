Stand Firm in the Lord

1 So, my dear brothers and sisters, whom I love and long to see, you are my joy and my crown. Stand firm in the Lord, dear friends. 2 I urge Euodia, and I urge Syntyche, to agree with each other in the Lord. 3 Yes, and I ask you, my true partner, to help these women. They worked hard with me for the good news, along with Clement and the rest of my coworkers. Their names are in the book of life.

Rejoice, Pray, and Think on What Is Good

4 Rejoice in the Lord always. I will say it again: Rejoice! 5 Let everyone see your gentle spirit. The Lord is near. 6 Do not worry about anything. Instead, in everything, pray and ask God for what you need, with a thankful heart. 7 Then God's peace, which is greater than we can understand, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

8 Finally, brothers and sisters, think about whatever is true, whatever is noble, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, and whatever is admirable. If anything is excellent or worthy of praise, think about those things. 9 Practice what you learned, received, heard, and saw in me. Then the God of peace will be with you.

Paul Thanks the Philippians

10 I rejoiced greatly in the Lord because you finally showed care for me again. Earlier you had cared, but you had no chance to show it. 11 I am not saying this because I need something. I have learned to be content with whatever I have. 12 I know how to live with little, and I know how to live with plenty. I have learned the secret of being content in any and every situation, whether I am full or hungry, whether I have plenty or need. 13 I can do all things through Him who gives me strength.

14 Yet you did well to share in my trouble. 15 And you Philippians know that in the beginning of the good news, when I left Macedonia, no church shared with me in giving and receiving except you alone. 16 Even in Thessalonica you sent help for my needs more than once. 17 I do not say this because I want a gift. I want the reward that grows for your benefit. 18 I have received everything, and I have more than enough. I am fully supplied now that I have received from you the gifts brought by Epaphroditus. They are like a sweet-smelling offering, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God. 19 And my God will supply all your needs according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus. 20 To our God and Father be glory forever and ever. Amen.

Final Greetings

21 Greet every believer in Christ Jesus. The brothers and sisters with me send their greetings to you. 22 All the believers send greetings to you, especially those from Caesar's household. 23 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
