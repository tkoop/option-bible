David Prays for Mercy and Safety

1 Be merciful to me, O God, be merciful to me, for my soul takes refuge in You. In the shadow of Your wings I will take refuge until the danger passes. 2 I cry out to God Most High, to God who does what He has planned for me. 3 He will send help from heaven and save me. He will rebuke the one who tramples me. Selah God will send His faithful love and truth.

4 My soul is among lions. I lie down among beasts that tear apart—people whose teeth are spears and arrows, whose tongues are sharp swords.

5 Be lifted up, O God, above the heavens. Let Your glory be over all the earth. 6 They set a net for my steps. My soul was bowed down. They dug a pit in front of me, but they fell into it themselves. Selah

David's Song of Praise

7 My heart is firm, O God; my heart is firm. I will sing and make music. 8 Wake up, my glory! Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake the dawn.

9 I will praise You, O Lord, among the nations. I will sing Your praises among the peoples. 10 For Your faithful love is great to the heavens, and Your truth reaches to the skies. 11 Be lifted up, O God, above the heavens. Let Your glory be over all the earth.
