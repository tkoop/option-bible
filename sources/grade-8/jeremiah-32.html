Jeremiah in Prison

1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar. 2 At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the courtyard of the guard in the palace of the king of Judah.

3 Zedekiah king of Judah had put Jeremiah there and asked, "Why do you prophesy that the LORD says He is giving this city to the king of Babylon? 4 The LORD says Zedekiah will not escape the Chaldeans. He will be handed over to the king of Babylon, speak with him face to face, and see him eye to eye. 5 He will take Zedekiah to Babylon, where he will stay until the LORD deals with him. The LORD declares that if you fight the Chaldeans, you will not succeed."

Jeremiah Buys a Field

6 Jeremiah said, "The word of the LORD came to me. The LORD said my cousin Hanamel son of Shallum was coming to say, 'Buy my field in Anathoth, because you have the right to redeem it.' 7 Just as the LORD had said, Hanamel came to me in the courtyard of the guard and said, 'Buy my field in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin. You have the right to inherit it and redeem it, so buy it for yourself.' 8 Then I knew this was the word of the LORD."

9 So I bought the field in Anathoth from my cousin Hanamel and weighed out seventeen shekels of silver. 10 I wrote the deed, sealed it, called witnesses, and weighed the silver on scales. 11 I took the deed of purchase, both the sealed copy with its terms and conditions and the open copy. 12 I gave the deed to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, in front of my cousin Hanamel and the witnesses who signed the deed, and in front of all the Jews sitting in the courtyard of the guard.

13 In front of them I gave Baruch this command: 14 "This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Take these deeds, the sealed deed of purchase and the open deed, and put them in a clay jar so they will last many days. 15 For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Houses, fields, and vineyards will be bought again in this land."

Jeremiah's Prayer

16 After I gave the deed to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD: 17 "Ah, Lord the LORD! You made heaven and earth with Your great power and outstretched arm. Nothing is too hard for You. 18 You show faithful love to thousands, but You repay the sin of the fathers by letting its consequences fall on their children. You are the great and mighty God; the LORD of Hosts is Your name. 19 You are great in wisdom and mighty in action. Your eyes are open to all the ways of people, and You reward each one according to his ways and the fruit of his deeds. 20 You performed signs and wonders in Egypt, and You still do to this day, in Israel and among all people. You made a name for Yourself, as it is today. 21 You brought Your people Israel out of Egypt with signs, wonders, a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and great terror. 22 You gave them this land that You swore to give their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey. 23 They came in and took possession of it, but they did not obey Your voice or follow Your law. They did none of the things You commanded, so You brought all this disaster on them. 24 Now the siege ramps have reached the city to capture it. Because of sword, famine, and plague, the city has been handed over to the Chaldeans who attack it. What You said has happened, and You see it. 25 Yet You, Lord the LORD, told me, 'Buy the field with silver and call witnesses,' even though the city has been handed over to the Chaldeans."

The LORD's Answer

26 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah: 27 "I am the LORD, the God of all people. Is anything too hard for Me? 28 So this is what the LORD says: I will hand this city over to the Chaldeans and to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will capture it. 29 The Chaldeans fighting against this city will come and set it on fire. They will burn it down, along with the houses where people burned incense to Baal on the roofs and poured out drink offerings to other gods to make Me angry. 30 From their youth, the people of Israel and Judah have done only evil in My sight. The people of Israel have done nothing but provoke Me to anger by what their hands have made, declares the LORD. 31 This city has provoked My anger and fury from the day they built it until now, so I will remove it from My sight. 32 All the evil done by the people of Israel and Judah to provoke Me to anger—by their kings, officials, priests, prophets, and by the people of Judah and those living in Jerusalem—has brought this on them. 33 They turned their backs on Me instead of facing Me. Though I taught them again and again, they would not listen or accept correction. 34 They set up their disgusting idols in the house that bears My name and made it unclean. 35 They built high places to Baal in the Valley of Ben Hinnom to sacrifice their sons and daughters to Molech. I never commanded this; it never entered My mind. They did this terrible thing and led Judah into sin.

36 "So now, this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says about this city, which you say will be handed over to the king of Babylon by sword, famine, and plague: 37 I will gather them from all the lands where I drove them in My anger, fury, and great wrath. I will bring them back to this place and let them live in safety. 38 They will be My people, and I will be their God. 39 I will give them one heart and one way, so that they will fear Me forever. This will be for their good and for their children after them. 40 I will make an everlasting covenant with them: I will never turn away from doing good to them, and I will put the fear of Me in their hearts so they will not turn away from Me. 41 I will gladly do good to them. I will plant them in this land with all My heart and all My soul. 42 I brought all this great disaster on this people, and in the same way I will bring on them all the good I have promised. 43 Fields will be bought in this land, though you say, 'It is a wasteland with no people or animals. It has been handed over to the Chaldeans.' 44 People will buy fields with silver, write deeds, seal them, and call witnesses in the land of Benjamin, around Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, the hill country, the western foothills, and the Negev. I will restore their fortunes, declares the LORD."
