Joseph Reveals Himself to His Brothers

1 Joseph could not hold back his feelings in front of everyone who was standing near him. He called out, "Send everyone out of the room!" So no one else stayed with Joseph when he told his brothers who he was. 2 He cried so loudly that the Egyptians heard him, and Pharaoh's household heard him too.

3 Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?" His brothers could not answer him because they were so shocked at seeing him. 4 Then Joseph said to his brothers, "Please come closer to me." They came closer, and he said, "I am Joseph, your brother, the one you sold into Egypt.

5 "Now do not be sad, and do not be angry with yourselves, because you sold me here. God sent me ahead of you to save lives. 6 There has been a famine in the land for two years now, and there are five more years when there will be no plowing or harvest. 7 God sent me ahead of you to keep a small group of your family alive in the land and to save you by a great rescue. 8 So it was not you who sent me here, but God. He has made me a father to Pharaoh, lord of all his house, and ruler over all the land of Egypt.

9 "Hurry and go up to my father. Tell him, 'This is what your son Joseph says: "God has made me lord of all Egypt. Come down to me. Do not wait."' 10 You will live in the land of Goshen, and you will be near me. You, your children, and your grandchildren, along with your flocks, your herds, and everything you own, will be close to me. 11 I will take care of you there, because there are still five more years of famine. Otherwise, you, your household, and everything you own could become poor. 12 Look, your own eyes see, and my brother Benjamin's eyes see, that I am the one speaking to you. 13 Tell my father about all my honor in Egypt and about everything you have seen. Then hurry and bring my father down here."

14 Joseph fell on his brother Benjamin's neck and cried, and Benjamin cried on his neck. 15 He kissed all his brothers and cried over them. After that, his brothers talked with him.

Pharaoh Welcomes Joseph's Family

16 The news was heard in Pharaoh's house: "Joseph's brothers have come." Pharaoh and his servants were pleased. 17 Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Tell your brothers, 'Do this: load your animals and go to the land of Canaan. 18 Bring your father and your families and come to me. I will give you the best land in Egypt, and you will eat the best food in the land.' 19 You are also to tell them, 'Take wagons from Egypt for your children and your wives, and bring your father and come. 20 Do not worry about your belongings, because the best of all Egypt will be yours.'"

Jacob Hears Joseph Is Alive

21 The sons of Israel did so. Joseph gave them wagons, as Pharaoh had commanded, and he gave them food for the journey. 22 He gave each of them a change of clothes, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of clothes. 23 He sent his father ten donkeys loaded with the best things of Egypt, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain, bread, and food for his father on the journey. 24 He sent his brothers away, and they left. He said to them, "Do not argue on the way." 25 They went up from Egypt and came to their father Jacob in the land of Canaan.

26 They told him, "Joseph is still alive! In fact, he is the ruler of all the land of Egypt." Jacob was stunned, because he did not believe them. 27 But when they told him everything Joseph had said to them, and when he saw the wagons Joseph had sent to carry him, Jacob's spirit revived. 28 Israel said, "That is enough. My son Joseph is still alive. I will go and see him before I die."
