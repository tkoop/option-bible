Babylon Will Fall

1 This is a message about the Desert by the Sea. Like whirlwinds sweeping through the Negev, an invader comes from the desert, from a land of terror.

2 A harsh vision has been shown to me. The traitor betrays, and the destroyer destroys. Go up, Elam! Lay siege, Media! I will put an end to all her groaning. 3 So my body is full of pain. Sharp pains have seized me, like the pains of a woman in labor. I am confused by what I hear, and I am terrified by what I see. 4 My heart pounds, and fear makes me shake. The evening I hoped for has become a horror to me.

5 They set the table and spread the cloth. They eat and drink. Rise up, you princes, and oil your shields!

6 For the Lord says to me, "Go, post a lookout, and have him report what he sees." 7 When he sees riders, horsemen in pairs, riders on donkeys, and riders on camels, let him watch very carefully.

8 The lookout shouted, "Day after day, my lord, I stand on the watchtower; every night I stay at my post."

9 Then the lookout saw a man in a chariot with a team of horses coming, and he called out, "Babylon has fallen! It has fallen! All the carved images of her gods lie shattered on the ground!"

10 My people, crushed on the threshing floor, I tell you what I have heard from the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel.

A Question from Dumah

11 This is a message about Dumah. Someone calls to me from Seir, "Watchman, what is left of the night? Watchman, what is left of the night?"

12 The watchman replies, "Morning is coming, but night is coming too. If you would ask, then come back and ask."

Trouble for Arabia

13 This is a message about Arabia. You caravans of Dedanites, camp in the thickets of Arabia. 14 Bring water for the thirsty, O people of the land of Tema. Meet the runaway with bread. 15 For they are running from the sword, from the drawn sword, from the bent bow, and from the heavy battle.

16 For the Lord says to me, "Within one year, as a hired worker would count it, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end. 17 The remaining archers and warriors of Kedar will be few." The The LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.
