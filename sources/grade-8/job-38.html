The LORD Speaks from the Whirlwind

1 The LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said:

2 “Who is this who clouds good advice with words that show no knowledge? 3 Get ready like a man. I will question you, and you must answer Me.

Questions About the Earth and Sea

4 “Where were you when I laid the earth's foundation? Tell Me, if you know so much. 5 Who set its size? Surely you know! Or who stretched a measuring line across it? 6 What holds up its pillars? Or who set its cornerstone in place, 7 when the morning stars sang together and all the angels shouted for joy?

8 “Or who shut up the sea behind doors when it rushed out from the womb, 9 when I made clouds its clothing and thick darkness its blanket, 10 and set its limits and put bars and doors in place, 11 and said, ‘You may come this far, but no farther; here is where your proud waves must stop’?

12 “Have you ever given orders to the morning or told the dawn where it should be, 13 so it could grab the edges of the earth and shake the wicked out of it? 14 The land changes like clay stamped with a seal, and its shapes stand out like clothing. 15 The wicked are denied their light, and their raised arm is broken.

16 “Have you gone to the springs of the sea or walked in the deepest parts of the ocean? 17 Have the gates of death been shown to you? Have you seen the gates of deep darkness? 18 Have you understood how wide the earth is? Tell Me, if you know all of it.

Light, Darkness, and the Skies

19 “Where is the road to light's home, and where does darkness live, 20 so you can take them to their borders and find the paths to their houses? 21 You must know, because you were born then, and your days are so many!

22 “Have you entered the storehouses of snow or seen the storehouses of hail, 23 which I have saved for times of trouble, for days of battle and war? 24 What is the path to where light is spread out, or where the east wind is scattered over the earth?

25 “Who dug a channel for the floodwaters and a path for the thunder, 26 to bring rain on land where no one lives, on the desert where no person is, 27 to water the empty, ruined land and make grass sprout?

28 “Does rain have a father? Or who fathered the drops of dew? 29 From whose womb did ice come? And who gave birth to the frost of heaven? 30 The water becomes hard like stone, and the surface of the deep freezes.

31 “Can you tie the chains of the Pleiades or loosen the cords of Orion? 32 Can you bring out the constellations at the right time? Can you lead the Bear with its cubs? 33 Do you know the laws of the heavens? Can you set their rule over the earth?

34 “Can you raise your voice to the clouds and make a flood of water cover you? 35 Can you send out lightning so it goes and says to you, ‘Here we are’? 36 Who put wisdom in the heart? Or who gave understanding to the mind?

37 “Who can count the clouds with wisdom? Or who can tip over the water jars of heaven, 38 when the dust hardens into a mass and the clods stick together?

Wild Animals and Their Food

39 “Can you hunt food for the lion or feed the young lions, 40 when they crouch in their dens or hide in the bushes waiting to attack? 41 Who gives the raven its food when its young cry out to God and wander around hungry?”
