Job Answers His Friends

1 Then Job answered:

2 “I have heard many things like these. You are all terrible comforters. 3 Will your speeches never end? What makes you keep arguing? 4 I could talk like you if you were in my place. I could pile words against you and shake my head at you. 5 But I would encourage you with my mouth, and the comfort from my lips would ease your pain. 6 If I speak, my pain doesn't get better. If I stay quiet, how much of it goes away?

Job Describes His Suffering

7 “But now He has worn me out. You have wiped out my whole family. 8 You have seized me, and that has become a witness. My thin body rises up and testifies against me. 9 His anger has torn me and turned against me. He grinds His teeth at me. My enemy stares at me with His piercing eyes. 10 They open their mouths against me and slap my cheeks in scorn. They gang up on me. 11 God has handed me over to evil men and thrown me into the hands of the wicked.

12 “I was at ease, but He shattered me. He grabbed me by the neck and broke me to pieces. He has set me up as His target. 13 His archers surround me. Without mercy, He pierces my kidneys and pours out my gall on the ground. 14 He breaks me again and again. He charges at me like a mighty warrior.

15 “I have sewn sackcloth over my skin and buried my strength in the dust. 16 My face is red from weeping, and dark shadows cover my eyes. 17 Even so, my hands are free of violence, and my prayer is pure. 18 O earth, do not cover my blood. Let my cry find no rest.

Job's Hope in Heaven

19 “Even now my Witness is in heaven, and my Advocate is on high. 20 My friends mock me, but my eyes pour out tears to God. 21 Oh, that someone could plead a man's case before God, as he would for a friend! 22 For in a few years I will go where I can never return.”
