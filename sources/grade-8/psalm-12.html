A Prayer for Help When Good People Are Gone

For the music leader. To be played on the eight-stringed lyre. A psalm of David. 1 Help, O the LORD, for godly people are gone; the faithful have disappeared from among people.

The Wicked Boast and Lie

2 They lie to each other; they speak with flattering lips and a heart that says one thing but means another. 3 May the LORD cut off all flattering lips and the tongue that boasts.

4 They say, “With our tongues we will win; our lips belong to us. Who is master over us?”

The LORD Promises to Help the Poor

5 “Because the poor are crushed and the needy groan, now I will act,” says the LORD. “I will give safety to the one who longs for My help.”

Pure Words and Sure Protection

6 The LORD’s words are pure, like silver melted in a furnace and cleaned seven times. 7 O the LORD, You will keep them safe; You will guard them from this generation forever. 8 Wicked people walk freely everywhere when what is shameful is praised among people.
