The Plot to Kill Jesus

1 The Feast of Unleavened Bread, also called Passover, was getting close. 2 The chief priests and teachers of the law were looking for a way to kill Jesus, because they were afraid of the people. 3 Then Satan entered Judas, called Iscariot, who was one of the Twelve. 4 He went and talked with the chief priests and temple officers about how he could hand Jesus over to them. 5 They were glad and agreed to give him money. 6 He consented and looked for a chance to betray Jesus when no crowd was around.

The Last Supper

7 Then the day of Unleavened Bread came, the day when the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed. 8 Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, "Go and get everything ready for us to eat the Passover."

9 They asked Him, "Where do You want us to get it ready?"

10 He said, "When you enter the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him to the house he enters. 11 Tell the owner of the house, 'The Teacher asks you, "Where is the room where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?"' 12 He will show you a large upstairs room, already set up. Get it ready there."

13 They left and found everything just as He had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

14 When the hour came, Jesus reclined at the table, and the apostles were with Him. 15 He said to them, "I have really wanted to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. 16 For I tell you, I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God." 17 Then He took a cup, gave thanks, and said, "Take this and share it among yourselves." 18 "For I tell you, I will not drink from the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes." 19 Then He took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, given for you. Do this to remember Me." 20 In the same way, after supper, He took the cup, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out for you." 21 "But look, the hand of the one who betrays Me is with Mine on the table. 22 The Son of Man will go as it has been decided. But woe to that man who betrays Him!" 23 They began to ask each other which of them might do this.

24 A dispute also started among them about which of them seemed to be the greatest. 25 Jesus said, "The kings of the nations rule over their people, and those in power are called benefactors. 26 But it should not be that way with you. Instead, the greatest among you should be like the youngest, and the leader like the one who serves. 27 Who is greater, the one at the table or the one serving? Is it not the one at the table? Yet I am among you as one who serves. 28 You are the ones who have stayed with Me through My trials. 29 I give you a kingdom, just as My Father gave one to Me. 30 You will eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and you will sit on thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel."

31 "Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to test all of you, like wheat being sifted. 32 But I prayed for you that your faith will not fail. And when you turn back, strengthen your brothers."

33 Peter said, "Lord, I am ready to go with You to prison and to death."

34 Jesus said, "I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know Me."

35 He said to them, "When I sent you out without a purse, a bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?"

They said, "Nothing."

36 "But now, let the one who has a purse take it, and a bag too. Let the one who has no sword sell his cloak and buy one." 37 "For I tell you, this Scripture must be fulfilled in Me: 'He was counted among criminals.' Yes, what is written about Me is coming true."

38 They said, "Lord, look, here are two swords."

He said, "That is enough."

Jesus Prays and Is Arrested

39 Jesus went out and walked to the Mount of Olives, as He usually did, and the disciples followed Him.

40 When He reached the place, He said to them, "Pray that you will not fall into temptation."

41 He withdrew from them about a stone's throw, knelt down, and prayed.

42 "Father, if You are willing, take this cup from Me. Yet not My will, but Yours be done."

43 An angel from heaven appeared to Him and strengthened Him. 44 Being in agony, He prayed more earnestly, and His sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground.

45 When He rose from prayer and came to the disciples, He found them sleeping because of sorrow. 46 He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so that you will not fall into temptation."

47 While He was still speaking, a crowd came. Judas, one of the Twelve, was leading them. He came up to Jesus to kiss Him. 48 Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?"

49 Those with Jesus saw what was happening and said, "Lord, should we strike with a sword?" 50 One of them struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his right ear.

51 Jesus answered, "No more of this!" Then He touched the man's ear and healed him.

52 Jesus said to the chief priests, the temple officers, and the elders who had come for Him, "Did you come out with swords and clubs as if I were a robber? 53 Every day I was with you in the temple, you did not lay hands on Me. But this is your hour, and darkness has its power."

Peter Denies Jesus and Jesus Is Questioned

54 They seized Him, led Him away, and brought Him into the high priest's house. Peter followed at a distance. 55 They had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, and Peter sat among them.

56 A servant girl saw him sitting by the firelight. She looked closely at him and said, "This man was with Him too."

57 But Peter denied it: "Woman, I don't know Him."

58 A little later someone else saw him and said, "You are also one of them."

But Peter said, "Man, I am not."

59 About an hour later another person insisted, "Truly, this man was with Him, for he is a Galilean."

60 Peter said, "Man, I don't know what you are talking about." Just as he was speaking, the rooster crowed.

61 The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the Lord's word: "Before the rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times." 62 He went out and wept bitterly.

63 The men who were holding Jesus began to mock Him and beat Him. 64 They blindfolded Him and said, "Prophesy! Who hit you?" 65 They also said many other insulting things against Him.

66 When day came, the elders of the people, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law gathered together and led Him to their council.

67 They said, "If You are the Christ, tell us."

Jesus said, "If I tell you, you won't believe. 68 If I ask you, you won't answer. 69 But from now on the Son of Man will sit at the right hand of God's power."

70 They all said, "Are You then the Son of God?"

He said, "You say that I am."

71 They said, "What more do we need? We have heard it from His own mouth."
