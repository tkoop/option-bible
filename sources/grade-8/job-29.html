Job Remembers His Former Days

1 Job continued speaking and said:

2 "How I wish for the months that have passed, for the days when God watched over me. 3 His lamp shone above my head, and by His light I walked through darkness. 4 I was in my prime, and God's close friendship rested on my tent. 5 The Almighty was still with me, and my children were around me. 6 I had plenty, with cream on my paths and oil pouring from the rocks.

Job's Standing in the City

7 "When I went out to the city gate and took my seat in the square, 8 young men stepped aside when they saw me, and old men stood up. 9 Leaders stopped talking and covered their mouths with their hands. 10 The voices of nobles were hushed, and their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths. 11 Everyone who heard me blessed me, and everyone who saw me praised me.

Job's Care for the Needy

12 "I rescued the poor who cried for help and the orphan who had no helper. 13 The dying man blessed me, and I made the widow's heart glad. 14 I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; justice was my robe and turban. 15 I was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame. 16 I was a father to the needy, and I looked into the stranger's case. 17 I broke the fangs of the wicked and snatched the prey from his teeth. 18 Then I thought, 'I will die at home, and my days will be as many as grains of sand. 19 My roots will spread out to the water, and the dew will rest on my branches. 20 My honor stays new within me, and my bow is renewed in my hand.'

Job's Respected Leadership

21 "People listened to me and waited; they kept silent to hear my advice. 22 After I spoke, they said no more; my words settled on them like dew. 23 They waited for me as for rain and opened their mouths as for spring rain. 24 If I smiled at them, they could hardly believe it; they did not let my favor fade. 25 I chose their way and sat as their chief; I lived like a king among his troops, like one who comforts those who mourn."
