Wise Words and Good Choices

1 A wise son listens to his father's teaching, but a mocker ignores correction. 2 A man is rewarded by the good things he says, but people who betray others crave violence. 3 The person who watches his words protects his life, but someone who talks too much brings trouble on himself.

4 A lazy person wants things but gets nothing, while a hard worker is well fed. 5 A good person hates lies, but an evil person brings shame and disgrace. 6 Righteousness protects the honest person's path, but wickedness trips up the sinner.

Wealth, Work, and Warnings

7 Some people pretend to be rich but have nothing, while others pretend to be poor but are very wealthy. 8 A person's wealth can save his life, but a poor person hears no threat. 9 The light of good people brings joy, but the lamp of the wicked goes out.

10 Pride only causes fights, but wisdom belongs to those who take advice. 11 Money gained quickly shrinks, but money gathered little by little grows. 12 Hope that is delayed makes the heart sick, but a desire that comes true brings life.

Teaching, Friends, and Consequences

13 A person who ignores good advice brings trouble on himself, but one who respects commands will be rewarded. 14 The teaching of a wise person is a fountain of life, turning people away from deadly traps. 15 Good judgment brings favor, but the path of traitors is rough.

16 A wise person acts with knowledge, but a fool shows his foolishness. 17 A wicked messenger falls into trouble, but a faithful messenger brings healing. 18 Poverty and disgrace come to those who ignore instruction, but those who listen to correction are honored.

19 A fulfilled desire is sweet to the soul, but fools hate turning away from evil. 20 Whoever walks with wise people becomes wise, but a companion of fools will be harmed.

Family and Daily Needs

21 Trouble chases sinners, but good people are rewarded with good. 22 A good person leaves an inheritance to his grandchildren, but a sinner's wealth is stored up for the righteous. 23 A poor person's field can produce plenty of food, but injustice sweeps it away.

24 Whoever spares the rod hates his son, but the one who loves him disciplines him. 25 A good person eats enough to satisfy himself, but the wicked person goes hungry.
