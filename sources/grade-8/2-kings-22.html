Josiah Becomes King

1 Josiah was eight years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for thirty-one years. His mother was Jedidah, the daughter of Adaiah from Bozkath. 2 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. He followed the way of his father David in everything. He did not turn to the right or to the left.

Repairing the Temple

3 In the eighteenth year of King Josiah's rule, the king sent Shaphan son of Azaliah son of Meshullam, the secretary, to the the LORD's temple. He said, 4 "Go to Hilkiah the high priest and have him count the money that has been brought into the the LORD's temple. The doorkeepers collected it from the people. 5 Give it to the men in charge of the work on the the LORD's temple. They will pay the workers who are repairing the temple. 6 Give it to the carpenters, builders, and masons. Also use it to buy wood and cut stone to repair the temple. 7 Do not ask them to report on the money they receive, because they work faithfully."

The Book of the Law Is Found

8 Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the secretary, "I have found the Book of the Law in the the LORD's temple." Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and Shaphan read it. 9 Then Shaphan the secretary went to the king and reported, "Your servants have emptied out the money found in the temple. They have given it to the men in charge of the work at the the LORD's temple." 10 Shaphan the secretary also told the king, "Hilkiah the priest has given me a book." Then Shaphan read it to the king. 11 When the king heard the words of the Book of the Law, he tore his clothes. 12 The king commanded Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Achbor son of Micaiah, Shaphan the secretary, and Asaiah the king's servant. He said, 13 "Go and ask the the LORD for me, for the people, and for all Judah. Ask about the words of this book that was found. The the LORD's anger is burning against us because our ancestors did not obey the words of this book. They did not do everything written for us."

Huldah's Prophecy

14 So Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophetess. She was the wife of Shallum son of Tikvah, son of Harhas, who kept the wardrobe. She lived in Jerusalem in the second district. They spoke to her. 15 She said to them, "This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Tell the man who sent you to Me, 16 the the LORD says He is going to bring disaster on this place and on its people. It will be everything written in the book that the king of Judah has read. 17 They have left Me and burned incense to other gods. They have made Me angry with everything they have made. So My anger will burn against this place and will not be put out.' 18 But tell the king of Judah who sent you to ask the the LORD, 'This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: You have heard these words. 19 Because your heart was soft and you humbled yourself before the the LORD when you heard what I said against this place and its people, that it would become a ruin and a curse, you tore your clothes and wept before Me. I have heard you, says the the LORD. 20 So I will let you join your ancestors in peace. You will be buried in peace. Your eyes will not see all the trouble I am bringing on this place.'" Then they brought her answer back to the king.
