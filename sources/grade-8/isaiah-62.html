God Promises to Restore Zion

1 For Zion's sake I will not keep quiet. For Jerusalem's sake I will not rest. I will keep going until her right living shines out like a bright light, and her rescue burns like a torch. 2 The nations will see your right living, and all kings will see your glory. You will be called by a new name that the LORD Himself will give. 3 You will be a glorious crown in the LORD's hand, a royal crown in the hand of your God.

New Names for God's People

4 People will no longer call you "Abandoned," and they will no longer call your land "Empty." Instead, you will be called "My Delight Is in Her," and your land will be called "Married." This is because the LORD delights in you, and your land will be married. 5 As a young man marries a young woman, so your sons will take you as their own. As a groom rejoices over his bride, so your God will rejoice over you.

Watchmen Pray and God Promises Plenty

6 I have put watchmen on your walls, Jerusalem. They will not be quiet day or night. You who call on the LORD, don't let yourselves rest. 7 Don't give Him rest either, until He makes Jerusalem strong and makes her the praise of the whole earth.

8 The LORD has sworn by His strong right hand and His powerful arm: "I will never again give your grain to your enemies for food. Foreigners will never drink your new wine that you worked so hard for. 9 Instead, those who harvest it will eat it and praise the LORD. Those who gather it will drink it in My holy courtyards."

Prepare the Way for God's People

10 Go through, go through the gates! Clear the way for the people. Build up, build up the road! Remove the stones. Raise a flag for the nations. 11 Look, the LORD has announced to the ends of the earth, "Tell Daughter Zion, 'Look, your salvation is coming! Look, His reward is with Him, and His payment goes before Him.'" 12 They will be called God's Holy People, the Redeemed of the LORD. And you will be called "Sought After," a City That Has Not Been Abandoned.
