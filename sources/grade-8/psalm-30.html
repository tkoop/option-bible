A Psalm of Thanksgiving for Deliverance

1 I will lift You up, the LORD, because You have lifted me up. You did not let my enemies celebrate over me. 2 The LORD my God, I called to You for help, and You healed me. 3 The LORD, You brought my soul up from the grave. You kept me alive so I would not go down into the pit.

4 Sing to the LORD, you who are faithful to Him. Give thanks to His holy name. 5 His anger lasts only a moment, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning.

6 When life was going well, I said, “I will never be shaken.” 7 The LORD, by Your favor You made me as steady as a mountain. When You hid Your face, I was terrified. 8 To You, the LORD, I called. To the Lord I cried for mercy:

9 “What good would my death do? If I go down to the pit, will the dust praise You? Will it tell about Your faithfulness? 10 Hear me, the LORD, and be gracious to me. The LORD, be my helper.”

11 You turned my mourning into dancing. You took away my sackcloth and dressed me with joy. 12 So I will sing Your praise with all my heart and not be silent. The LORD my God, I will thank You forever.
