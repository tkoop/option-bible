The Word of Life

1 We are telling you about something that was there from the very beginning. We heard it. We saw it with our own eyes. We looked at it, and our hands touched it. This is about the Word of life. 2 That life showed itself to us. We saw it, and now we tell others about it. We announce to you the life that lasts forever. That life was with the Father, and it showed itself to us. 3 We are telling you what we saw and heard so that you can share life with us. Our shared life is with the Father and with His Son, Jesus Christ. 4 We are writing these things so that our joy may be full.

God Is Light

5 This is the message we heard from Him and now tell you: God is light, and there is no darkness in Him at all. 6 If we say we share life with Him but live in darkness, we are lying. We are not living by the truth. 7 But if we live in the light, as He is in the light, we share life with one another. Also, the blood of Jesus, His Son, washes us clean from every sin.

Confessing Our Sins

8 If we say we have no sin, we fool ourselves, and the truth is not in us. 9 But if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just. He will forgive our sins and make us clean from all wrong. 10 If we say we have never sinned, we make Him out to be a liar, and His word is not in us.
