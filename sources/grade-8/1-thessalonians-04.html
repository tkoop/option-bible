Living to Please God

1 Finally, brothers and sisters, we ask and urge you in the Lord Jesus to live the way we taught you, a way that pleases God. You are already living that way, but we want you to do even more. 2 You already know these instructions, because we gave them to you through the Lord Jesus.

3 God wants you to be holy, so stay away from sexual sin. 4 Each of you should learn to control your own body in a holy and respectful way.

5 Do not let strong desire control you, like people who do not know God. 6 No one should wrong or cheat a fellow believer in this matter, because the Lord punishes all such sins. We have already told you this and warned you.

7 God did not call us to live in uncleanness, but in holiness. 8 So anyone who rejects this teaching is not rejecting a human being, but God, who gives you His Holy Spirit.

Loving One Another

9 Now about loving each other as brothers and sisters, you do not need anyone to write to you, because God Himself has taught you to love one another. 10 In fact, you are already doing this for all the brothers and sisters throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers and sisters, to do it even more.

11 Make it your goal to live a quiet life, mind your own business, and work with your hands, just as we told you. 12 Then you will earn the respect of outsiders, and you will not have to depend on anyone.

Hope for Those Who Have Died

13 Brothers and sisters, we do not want you to be uninformed about those who have died, so that you will not grieve like people who have no hope. 14 Since we believe that Jesus died and rose again, we also believe that God will bring with Jesus those who have died in Him.

15 We tell you this by the Lord's own word: We who are still alive when the Lord comes will not go ahead of those who have died. 16 For the Lord Himself will come down from heaven with a loud command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet call of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first.

17 After that, we who are still alive and are left will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will be with the Lord forever. 18 Therefore encourage one another with these words.
