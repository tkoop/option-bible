Hosea Buys Back His Unfaithful Wife

1 The LORD said to me, "Go, love a woman who is loved by another man and is unfaithful, just as the LORD loves the people of Israel, even though they turn to other gods and love sweet cakes offered to idols."

2 So I bought her for fifteen pieces of silver and about nine bushels of barley.

3 Then I said to her, "Live with me for many days. Don't be unfaithful, and don't belong to another man. I will do the same for you."

Israel's Future Return to the LORD

4 The people of Israel will live for many days without a king or leader, without sacrifices or sacred stone pillars, and without the priest's vest or household idols.

5 Later, the people of Israel will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. They will come trembling to the LORD and to His goodness in the last days.
