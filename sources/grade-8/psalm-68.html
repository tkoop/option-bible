God Rises and His Enemies Scatter

1 For the director of music. A psalm of David. A song. God rises up. His enemies scatter, and those who hate Him run away from Him. 2 As smoke is blown away, You blow them away. As wax melts before a fire, so the wicked perish before God. 3 But those who do right will be glad. They will rejoice before God and celebrate with joy.

Praise the God Who Rides the Clouds

4 Sing to God, sing praises to His name. Praise Him who rides on the clouds. His name is the LORD, and rejoice before Him. 5 God is a father to the fatherless and a defender of widows. He lives in His holy home. 6 God gives a home to those who are alone. He leads prisoners to freedom, but rebels live in a dry land.

God Marched with His People from Sinai

7 God, when You went out ahead of Your people, when You marched through the desert. Selah. 8 The earth shook, and rain poured from the sky before God, the God of Sinai, before God, the God of Israel. 9 You sent plenty of rain, O God; You refreshed Your tired land. 10 Your people settled there. In Your goodness, O God, You provided for the poor.

11 The LORD gives the command, and a large group of women spread the news. 12 Kings and armies run away quickly. Even the woman who stays home shares the plunder. 13 Even if you rest among the sheep pens, you will be like a dove's wings covered with silver, with shining gold feathers. 14 When the Almighty scattered kings, it was like snow falling on Mount Zalmon.

The Lord's Victory Procession

15 God's mountain is like Mount Bashan, a mountain with many peaks. 16 Why do you look with envy, you mountains with many peaks? This is the mountain God chose for His home. The LORD will live there forever. 17 God's chariots number in the tens of thousands—thousands upon thousands. The Lord is among them in His holy place, just as He was at Sinai. 18 You went up to the high place. You took captives with You. You received gifts from people, even from rebels, so that the LORD God may live there.

19 Blessed be the Lord, who carries our loads every day—the God who saves us. Selah. 20 Our God is a God who saves; the Lord—the LORD—gives us a way out of death. 21 Surely God will crush the heads of His enemies, the hairy scalps of those who keep sinning. 22 The Lord said, "I will bring them back from Bashan; I will bring them back from the depths of the sea, 23 so your feet can wade in their blood, and your dogs can lick their share from the enemy."

24 People see Your procession, O God, the procession of my God and King into the holy place. 25 Singers lead the way, musicians follow, and young women play tambourines in the middle. 26 Bless God among the great gathering. Bless the LORD, you who come from Israel's fountain. 27 There is Benjamin, the youngest, leading them; the princes of Judah in their group; the princes of Zebulun and Naphtali.

28 Use Your power, O God; show Your strength, O God, which You have worked for us. 29 Because of Your temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring gifts to You. 30 Rebuke the beast of the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the peoples, until they trample those who want silver. Scatter the peoples who love war. 31 Messengers will come from Egypt. Cush will stretch out her hands to God. 32 Sing to God, kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord—Selah. 33 To Him who rides across the ancient heavens. Listen—He speaks with a mighty voice. 34 Give power to God; His majesty is over Israel, and His power is in the skies. 35 God, You are awesome in Your holy place; the God of Israel gives power and strength to His people. Blessed be God!
