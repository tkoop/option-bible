Paul Before Festus

1 Three days after arriving in the province, Festus went up from Caesarea to Jerusalem. 2 The chief priests and the leading men of the Jews brought charges against Paul to him and urged him 3 to send for Paul to come to Jerusalem, planning to ambush and kill him on the way. 4 Festus answered that Paul was being kept at Caesarea and that he himself was about to leave soon.

5 "So," he said, "let those among you who are leaders go down with me and accuse him, if there is anything wrong with the man."

6 After staying no more than eight or ten days among them, he went down to Caesarea. The next day he sat on the judgment seat and ordered Paul to be brought. 7 When Paul arrived, the Jews who had come down from Jerusalem stood around him, bringing many serious charges that they could not prove.

8 Paul defended himself, saying, "I have done nothing wrong against the Jewish law, or the temple, or Caesar."

Paul Appeals to Caesar

9 Festus, wanting to do the Jews a favor, answered Paul, "Do you want to go up to Jerusalem and be tried there before me about these things?"

10 Paul said, "I am standing before Caesar's judgment seat, where I must be tried. I have done no wrong to the Jews, as you yourself know very well. 11 If I am guilty and have done something worthy of death, I do not refuse to die. But if none of their charges are true, no one can hand me over to them. I appeal to Caesar."

12 Then Festus, after talking with his council, answered, "You have appealed to Caesar; to Caesar you will go."

Festus Tells Agrippa About Paul

13 After some days, King Agrippa and Bernice arrived at Caesarea to greet Festus.

14 While they stayed there many days, Festus told the king about Paul's case, saying, "There is a man left as a prisoner by Felix. 15 When I was at Jerusalem, the chief priests and elders of the Jews informed me about him, asking that he be condemned. 16 I answered them that it is not Roman custom to hand over any man before the accused has faced his accusers and had a chance to answer the charge. 17 So when they came here, I did not delay. The next day I sat on the judgment seat and ordered the man to be brought. 18 When the accusers stood up, they brought no charge of the evil things I had suspected. 19 Instead, they had some questions against him about their own religion and about a dead man named Jesus, whom Paul claimed was alive. 20 Being unsure how to investigate these things, I asked if he wanted to go to Jerusalem and be tried there about them. 21 But Paul appealed to be kept for the decision of the Emperor, and I ordered him kept until I could send him to Caesar."

Paul Brought Before Agrippa

22 Agrippa said to Festus, "I would like to hear the man myself."

"Tomorrow," Festus said, "you will hear him." 23 The next day Agrippa and Bernice came with great show, and they entered the hearing room with the commanders and the leading men of the city. At Festus's command, Paul was brought in.

24 Festus said, "King Agrippa and all the men here with us, you see this man. The whole Jewish crowd has urged me both here and in Jerusalem, shouting that he ought not to live any longer. 25 But I found that he had done nothing deserving death. Since he himself appealed to the Emperor, I decided to send him. 26 I have nothing certain to write to my lord about him. Therefore I have brought him before you, and especially before you, King Agrippa, so that after this examination I may have something to write. 27 For it seems unreasonable to me to send a prisoner without also stating the charges against him."
