Ahaziah Becomes King

1 The people living in Jerusalem made Ahaziah, his youngest son, king in his place. The raiders who came with the Arabs to the camp had killed all his older brothers. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, began to rule. 2 Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri.

Ahaziah's Evil Reign

3 He also followed the ways of Ahab's family, because his mother advised him to do wrong. 4 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as Ahab's family had done. After his father died, they became his advisers and led him to ruin. 5 He also followed their advice and went with Jehoram son of Ahab, king of Israel, to fight Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth Gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram. 6 Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds he got at Ramah while fighting Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Joram son of Ahab, because Joram was sick.

Jehu Kills Ahaziah

7 But God used his trip to bring him to Joram. When he got there, he went out with Joram to meet Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to destroy Ahab's family. 8 While Jehu was carrying out judgment on Ahab's family, he found the leaders of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers, who served Ahaziah, and he killed them. 9 He looked for Ahaziah and caught him while he was hiding in Samaria. They brought him to Jehu and killed him. They buried him, because they said, “He is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart.” After that, Ahaziah's family had no one left to rule the kingdom.

Athaliah Takes the Throne

10 When Athaliah, Ahaziah's mother, saw that her son was dead, she rose up and destroyed all the royal family of Judah. 11 But Jehoshabeath, the king's daughter, took Joash son of Ahaziah and hid him among the king's sons who were being killed. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom. Jehoshabeath was the daughter of King Jehoram and the wife of Jehoiada the priest. She was also Ahaziah's sister. She hid Joash from Athaliah, so she did not kill him. 12 He stayed with them six years, hidden in the temple of God, while Athaliah ruled the land.
