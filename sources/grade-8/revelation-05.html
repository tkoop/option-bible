The Scroll with Seven Seals

1 I saw a scroll in the right hand of the One sitting on the throne. It was written on the inside and the outside, and it was sealed with seven seals. 2 I also saw a strong angel shouting in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?” 3 But no one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth could open the scroll or even look inside it. 4 I cried hard because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look inside it.

The Lamb Who Is Worthy

5 Then one of the elders said to me, “Do not cry. Look! The Lion from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has won the victory. He can open the scroll and its seven seals.” 6 Then I saw a Lamb standing in the middle of the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders. He looked like He had been killed. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent out into all the earth. 7 He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the One sitting on the throne.

Worship in Heaven

8 When He took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each one had a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of God’s holy people.

9 They sang a new song: “You are worthy to take the scroll and open its seals, because You were killed. With Your blood You bought people for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation. 10 You made them a kingdom and priests to serve our God, and they will rule on the earth.”

Praise from All Creation

11 Then I looked and heard the voice of many angels around the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. Their number was thousands and thousands, and ten thousand times ten thousand. 12 They said in a loud voice, “The Lamb who was killed is worthy to receive power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise!”

13 Then I heard every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, and on the sea, and everything in them, saying, “To the One sitting on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing, honor, glory, and power forever and ever!” 14 The four living creatures said, “Amen!” and the elders fell down and worshiped.
