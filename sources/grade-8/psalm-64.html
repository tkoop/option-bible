David Asks for Protection

1 Hear my voice, O God, when I complain; protect my life from the enemy's terror. 2 Hide me from the secret plans of the wicked and from the noisy crowd of evildoers.

The Wicked Plot in Secret

3 They sharpen their tongues like swords and aim bitter words like arrows. 4 From hiding they shoot at the innocent; they shoot suddenly and without fear. 5 They hold firmly to their evil plan and talk about hiding traps. They ask, "Who will see us?" 6 They search for ways to do wrong and say, "We have made a perfect secret plan." A person's inner thoughts and heart are deep.

God Brings the Wicked Down

7 But God will shoot them with arrows; suddenly they will be wounded. 8 Their own tongues will turn against them, and everyone who sees them will shake his head. 9 Then everyone will fear and tell about what God has done; they will think about His work.

The Righteous Rejoice in the LORD

10 Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him. Let all who are upright in heart celebrate.
