David Finds Refuge in God

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. 1 In You, the LORD, I have taken refuge. Let me never be put to shame. Deliver me in Your righteousness. 2 Turn Your ear to me. Come quickly to save me. Be my rock of refuge, a strong fortress to save me.

3 For You are my rock and my fortress. Lead me and guide me for Your name's sake. 4 Free me from the trap they hid for me, for You are my refuge.

5 Into Your hands I commit my spirit. You have redeemed me, the LORD, God of truth. 6 I hate those who cling to worthless idols, but I trust in the LORD. 7 I will be glad and rejoice in Your loving devotion, for You have seen my trouble. You have known my deep sorrow. 8 You have not handed me over to the enemy. You have set my feet in an open place.

A Cry for Mercy

9 Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am in trouble. My eyes are worn out from sorrow, and so are my soul and body. 10 My life is worn out with grief, and my years with groaning. My strength fails because of my sin, and my bones grow weak. 11 I am a disgrace to all my enemies and a terror to my neighbors. Those who know me run away. Those who see me in the street flee from me. 12 I am forgotten like a dead man, out of everyone's mind. I am like a broken pot.

13 For I hear the slander of many. Terror is on every side. They plot together against me and plan to take my life. 14 But I trust in You, the LORD. I say, "You are my God." 15 My future is in Your hands. Save me from my enemies and from those who chase me. 16 Show Your favor to Your servant. Save me in Your loving devotion. 17 The LORD, don't let me be put to shame, for I called on You. Let the wicked be put to shame. Let them be silent in the grave. 18 Let their lying lips be silenced—those that speak arrogantly against the righteous with pride and contempt.

God's Goodness

19 How great is Your goodness! You have stored it up for those who fear You and given it to those who take refuge in You, right in front of everyone! 20 You hide them in the shelter of Your presence from the schemes of men. You conceal them in Your shelter from accusing tongues. 21 Blessed be the LORD, for He has shown His wonderful loving devotion to me in a city under siege. 22 In my panic I said, "I am cut off from Your sight!" But You heard my cries for mercy when I called to You.

23 Love the LORD, all you who are faithful to Him! The LORD protects the faithful and fully punishes the proud. 24 Be strong and take courage, all you who put your hope in the LORD.
