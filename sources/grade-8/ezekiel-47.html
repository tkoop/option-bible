The River Flowing from the Temple

1 He brought me back to the entrance of the house. Water was flowing out from under the threshold of the house toward the east, because the house faced east. The water ran down from under the south side of the house, south of the altar. 2 He brought me out through the north gate and led me around outside to the outer gate that faces east. Water was trickling from the south side.

3 The man went east with a measuring line in his hand. He measured off a thousand cubits and led me through water that was ankle-deep. 4 He measured off another thousand and led me through water that reached my knees. He measured off another thousand and led me through water that reached my waist. 5 He measured off another thousand, but the river was too deep to cross. The water had risen so much that it was deep enough to swim in, and no one could cross it.

6 He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen this?" Then He led me back along the bank of the river.

7 When I returned, I saw a great many trees on both banks of the river.

The River Brings Life

8 He said to me, "This water flows out to the eastern region and goes down into the Arabah. It flows into the sea, and when it enters the sea, the water becomes fresh. 9 Every living creature that moves will live wherever the river flows. There will be very many fish, because this water goes there and makes the water fresh. So everything will live wherever the river goes.

10 "Fishermen will stand along it. From En Gedi to En Eglaim there will be places to spread nets. The fish will be of many kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea. 11 But its swamps and marshes will not be healed; they will be left for salt.

12 "On both banks of the river, every kind of tree that bears fruit will grow. Its leaves will not wither, and its fruit will not fail. It will bear new fruit every month, because its water comes from the sanctuary. Its fruit will be for food, and its leaves will be for healing."

The Borders of the Land

13 This is what the Lord the LORD says: "This is the boundary by which you will divide the land as an inheritance for the twelve tribes of Israel, with Joseph receiving two portions. 14 You will inherit it, each like his brother, because I raised My hand and swore to give it to your fathers. This land will fall to you as your inheritance.

15 "The northern boundary is this: From the Great Sea by the road to Hethlon, as far as Zedad, 16 Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which lies between Damascus and Hamath, and Hazer-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran.

17 "So the boundary will run from the sea to Hazar-enan, along the northern border of Damascus and Hamath. This is the north side. 18 The eastern boundary will run between Hauran and Damascus, along the Jordan between Gilead and the land of Israel, to the eastern sea. This is the east side.

19 "The southern boundary will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribah Kadesh, then along the Brook of Egypt to the Great Sea. This is the south side. 20 The western boundary will be the Great Sea, from the southern border up to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This is the west side.

Sharing the Land with Foreigners

21 "You are to divide this land among yourselves for the tribes of Israel. 22 You are to allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the foreigners living among you who have children among you. They will be to you like native-born Israelites. With you they will receive an inheritance among the tribes of Israel. 23 In whatever tribe a foreigner lives, there you are to give him his inheritance, declares the Lord the LORD."
