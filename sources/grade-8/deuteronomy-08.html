Obey God's Commands

1 Be careful to obey all the commands I am giving you today, so that you may live and grow in number, and go in and take over the land the the LORD promised to your ancestors.

God's Testing in the Wilderness

2 Remember how the the LORD your God led you all the way through the wilderness for forty years. He did it to humble you and test you, to learn what was in your heart and whether you would keep His commands. 3 He let you go hungry, then fed you manna, a food that neither you nor your ancestors had ever known. He did this so you would understand that people do not live on bread alone but on every word that comes from the mouth of the the LORD. 4 Your clothes did not wear out, and your feet did not swell during those forty years.

5 You should know in your heart that the the LORD your God corrects you just as a father corrects his son. 6 So obey the commands of the the LORD your God by walking in His ways and fearing Him.

The Good Land God Promised

7 The LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land with streams and pools of water, with springs flowing out in the valleys and hills. 8 It is a land of wheat and barley, vines and fig trees and pomegranates, a land of olive oil and honey.

9 It is a land where you will eat bread without ever running short, where you will lack nothing. It is a land whose stones are iron and whose hills you can dig copper from. 10 When you have eaten and are full, praise the the LORD your God for the good land He has given you.

Do Not Forget the LORD

11 Be careful not to forget the the LORD your God by failing to keep His commands, laws, and rules that I am giving you today. 12 When you eat and are full, and build good houses and settle down, 13 and when your herds and flocks grow, and your silver and gold increase, and everything you own increases, 14 do not let your heart become proud and forget the the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery.

15 He led you through the great and dangerous wilderness, with its poisonous snakes and scorpions, and its dry ground with no water. He brought water out of hard rock for you. 16 In the wilderness He fed you with manna, which your ancestors had never known, to humble you and test you so that things would go well for you in the end. 17 Do not say in your heart that your strength and your own power made you wealthy. 18 Remember the the LORD your God, because He is the One who gives you power to become wealthy. He does this to keep the promise He made to your ancestors, just as He does today.

19 But if you forget the the LORD your God and follow other gods and worship them, I warn you today that you will certainly be destroyed. 20 Like the nations the the LORD is destroying before you, you too will be destroyed, because you did not obey the voice of the the LORD your God.
