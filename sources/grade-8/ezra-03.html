The Altar Is Rebuilt

1 In the seventh month, the Israelites had settled in their towns, and the people gathered in Jerusalem as one man. 2 Then Jeshua son of Jozadak, his fellow priests, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and his brothers began to build the altar of the God of Israel so they could offer burnt offerings on it, just as it is written in the Law of Moses, the man of God. 3 Even though they were afraid of the peoples around them, they set the altar on its foundations and offered burnt offerings on it to the LORD, morning and evening. 4 They celebrated the Festival of Booths just as it is written, offering the daily burnt offerings in the right number for each day. 5 After that, they offered the regular burnt offering, the new moon offerings, and all the appointed feasts of the LORD. They also offered every freewill offering brought to the LORD. 6 They began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the first day of the seventh month, even though the foundation of the LORD's temple had not yet been laid. 7 They gave money to the stonecutters and carpenters, and food, drink, and oil to the people of Sidon and Tyre, so the cedar logs from Lebanon could be brought by sea to Joppa, as King Cyrus of Persia had allowed.

Work Begins on the Temple

8 In the second month of the second year after they came to the house of God in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the rest of their brothers, the priests and Levites, and all who had returned from captivity to Jerusalem began the work. They appointed Levites twenty years old and older to oversee the work on the house of the LORD. 9 Jeshua, his sons and brothers, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, and the sons of Henadad with their sons and brothers, the Levites, stood together to supervise the workers on the house of God.

The Foundation Is Laid

10 When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, the priests in their robes stood with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, stood with cymbals to praise the LORD, as David, king of Israel, had directed. 11 They sang, praising and thanking the LORD: “He is good; His faithful love to Israel lasts forever.” Then all the people shouted a loud shout of praise to the LORD because the foundation of the house of the LORD had been laid. 12 But many of the older priests, Levites, and family leaders who had seen the first temple wept loudly when they saw the foundation of this temple laid. Many others shouted with joy. 13 The people could not tell the shouts of joy from the sound of weeping, because the people were shouting so loudly. The sound was heard far away.
