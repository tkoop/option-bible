Land Given to the Tribes of Joseph

1 The land given to Joseph's sons began at the Jordan near Jericho. It ran east to the waters of Jericho, through the desert, and up from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel. 2 From Bethel it went to Luz and passed along the border of the Arkites at Ataroth. 3 Then it went down west to the border of the Japhletites, as far as the border of Lower Beth Horon, then to Gezer, and ended at the sea. 4 So the people of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their land.

Ephraim's Territory

5 The border of Ephraim's people, by their families, was this: on the east it ran from Ataroth Addar to Upper Beth Horon. 6 From there it went out to the sea. On the north it reached Michmethath, then turned east to Taanath Shiloh and passed beyond it on the east to Janoah. 7 From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and Naarah, touched Jericho, and ended at the Jordan. 8 From Tappuah the border went west to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the sea. This was the land given to the tribe of Ephraim, by their families.

Cities Shared with Manasseh

9 Ephraim also had cities set apart for them inside the land of Manasseh's people. This included all those cities and their villages.

Canaanites Remain in Gezer

10 They did not drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer. The Canaanites still live among Ephraim's people today, but they became forced laborers.
