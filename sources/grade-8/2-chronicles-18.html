Jehoshaphat and Ahab Become Allies

1 Jehoshaphat had great wealth and honor. He made a marriage alliance with Ahab.

2 After some years, he went down to Ahab in Samaria. Ahab killed many sheep and cattle for him and the people with him. Then Ahab urged him to attack Ramoth Gilead.

Ahab Asks for Help

3 Ahab, king of Israel, said to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, "Will you go with me to Ramoth Gilead?"

Jehoshaphat answered, "I am like you, and my people are like your people. We will be with you in the war."

4 Jehoshaphat said, "Please ask today for the word of the LORD."

5 The king of Israel gathered four hundred prophets. He asked them, "Should we go to war against Ramoth Gilead, or should I hold back?"

They said, "Go up. God will give it into the king's hand."

6 Jehoshaphat asked, "Isn't there another prophet of the LORD here, so we can ask him?"

7 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man we can ask. He is Micaiah son of Imlah. But I hate him, because he never predicts good things for me. He always predicts bad things."

Jehoshaphat said, "The king should not say that."

8 The king of Israel called an officer and said, "Bring Micaiah son of Imlah quickly."

9 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, were sitting on their thrones, wearing royal robes. They were at the threshing floor by the gate of Samaria. All the prophets were speaking in front of them.

10 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. He said, "This is what the LORD says: 'With these you will gore Aram until they are destroyed.'"

11 All the prophets said the same thing: "Go up to Ramoth Gilead and succeed. The LORD will give it into the king's hand."

12 The messenger who had gone to call Micaiah said to him, "All the prophets are saying good things to the king. Please let your words match theirs, and say something good."

13 Micaiah said, "As the LORD lives, I will say only what my God tells me."

Micaiah Warns of Defeat

14 When Micaiah came to the king, the king asked him, "Micaiah, should we go to Ramoth Gilead to fight, or should I hold back?"

Micaiah answered, "Go up and succeed. They will be given into your hand."

15 The king said, "How many times must I make you swear to tell me only the truth in the name of the LORD?"

16 Then Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains like sheep without a shepherd. The LORD said, 'These have no master. Let each one go home in peace.'"

17 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Didn't I tell you he would not predict good things for me, but bad things?"

18 Micaiah said, "So listen to the word of the LORD. I saw the LORD sitting on His throne. All the armies of heaven were standing on His right and on His left.

19 "The The LORD said, 'Who will trick Ahab, king of Israel, into going up to Ramoth Gilead, so he will fall there?' One said one thing, and another said another.

20 "Then a spirit came forward and stood before the LORD. He said, 'I will trick him.' The The LORD asked, 'How?'

21 "The spirit said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouths of all his prophets.' The The LORD said, 'You will trick him, and you will succeed. Go out and do it.'

22 "So now the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouths of these prophets. The The LORD has spoken disaster against you."

23 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek. He said, "Which way did the Spirit of the LORD go from me to speak to you?"

24 Micaiah said, "You will see that on the day you go into a back room to hide."

25 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and send him back to Amon, the governor of the city, and to Joash the king's son."

26 The king also said, "Tell them, 'This is what the king says: Put this man in prison. Feed him only bread and water until I return safely.'"

27 Micaiah said, "If you really come back safely, then the LORD has not spoken through me. Listen, all you people!"

Ahab Dies at Ramoth Gilead

28 So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, went up to Ramoth Gilead.

29 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into battle. But you wear your robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went into battle.

30 The king of Aram had ordered his chariot commanders, "Do not fight anyone, whether important or not. Fight only the king of Israel."

31 When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "That is the king of Israel." They turned to fight him. Jehoshaphat cried out, and the LORD helped him. God turned them away from him.

32 When the chariot commanders saw he was not the king of Israel, they stopped chasing him.

33 A man shot an arrow without aiming. It hit the king of Israel between the joints of his armor and his breastplate. The king said to his chariot driver, "Turn around and take me out of the battle, because I am wounded."

34 The battle grew worse that day. The king of Israel was propped up in his chariot, facing the Arameans, until evening. He died at sunset.
