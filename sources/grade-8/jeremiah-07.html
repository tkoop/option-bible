Jeremiah Speaks at the Temple Gate

1 This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD: 2 "Stand at the gate of the LORD's house and announce this message there. Say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who come through these gates to worship the LORD.'"

God Calls for Real Change

3 "This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Fix your ways and your actions, and I will let you live in this place. 4 Do not trust lying words that say, 'This is the LORD's temple, the LORD's temple, the LORD's temple.'"

5 "But if you truly fix your ways and your actions, if you truly treat each other fairly, 6 if you do not oppress the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not follow other gods to your own harm, 7 then I will let you live in this place, in the land I gave your ancestors forever. 8 But look, you are trusting in lying words that cannot help you. 9 Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear lies, burn incense to Baal, and follow other gods you do not know? 10 Then will you come and stand before Me in this house that bears My name and say, 'We are safe!'—so you can go on doing all these hateful things? 11 Has this house that bears My name become a hideout for robbers in your eyes? I have seen it Myself," declares the LORD.

A Warning from Shiloh

12 "But go now to My place in Shiloh, where I first made My name dwell, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel. 13 And now, because you do all these things," declares the LORD, "and when I spoke to you again and again, you did not listen, and when I called you, you did not answer. 14 I will do to the house that bears My name—the one you trust in—and to the place I gave you and your ancestors, what I did to Shiloh. 15 I will throw you out of My sight, just as I threw out all your brothers, all the descendants of Ephraim."

16 "As for you, do not pray for this people. Do not lift up a cry or prayer for them, and do not plead with Me, because I will not listen to you. 17 Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem? 18 The children gather wood, the fathers light the fire, and the women knead dough to make cakes for the queen of heaven. They also pour out drink offerings to other gods to provoke Me to anger. 19 Are they provoking Me to anger?" declares the LORD. "No, they are hurting themselves, to their own shame."

Judgment on Judah

20 "Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Look, My anger and My wrath will be poured out on this place—on people and animals, on the trees of the field and the fruit of the ground. It will burn and not be put out.'"

21 "This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: 'Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices and eat the meat.'"

22 "For when I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, I did not speak to them or command them about burnt offerings and sacrifices. 23 But I commanded them this: Obey My voice, and I will be your God, and you will be My people. Walk in all the way I command you, so that it may go well with you. 24 But they did not listen or pay attention. Instead they followed the stubbornness of their evil hearts. They turned away instead of moving forward. 25 From the day your ancestors left Egypt until today, I have sent you all My servants the prophets, again and again. 26 Yet they did not listen to Me or pay attention. They became stubborn and did worse than their ancestors. 27 So you will speak all these words to them, but they will not listen to you. You will call to them, but they will not answer you. 28 And you will say to them, 'This is the nation that did not obey the voice of the LORD their God and did not accept correction. Truth has disappeared from their mouths.'"

29 "Cut off your hair and throw it away. Take up a lament on the bare heights, for the LORD has rejected and abandoned the people He is angry with. 30 For the children of Judah have done evil in My eyes," declares the LORD. "They have set their hateful idols in the house that bears My name and defiled it. 31 They built high places at Topheth in the Valley of Ben Hinnom to burn their sons and daughters in the fire. I did not command that, and it never entered My mind. 32 So look, the days are coming," declares the LORD, "when people will no longer call it Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter. They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no room. 33 The dead bodies of this people will become food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth, and no one will scare them away. 34 I will put an end to the sounds of joy and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the bride, in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, because the land will become a wasteland."
