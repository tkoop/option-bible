Listen to My Teaching

1 Listen, my people, to my teaching. Turn your ears to the words of my mouth. 2 I will open my mouth and tell a story. I will share old, hidden truths. 3 We have heard them and known them, and our fathers told them to us. 4 We won't hide them from their children. We'll tell the next generation the praises of the LORD, and His power and the wonders He has done.

5 He made a covenant in Jacob and gave His law in Israel. He commanded our fathers to teach it to their children. 6 Then the next generation would know it, even children not yet born. They would grow up and tell their own children. 7 Then they could trust in God and not forget His works, but keep His commands. 8 They wouldn't be like their stubborn and rebellious fathers, whose hearts weren't steady and whose spirits weren't faithful to God.

Rebellion in the Wilderness

9 The people of Ephraim had bows, but they turned back on the day of battle. 10 They didn't keep God's covenant, and they refused to walk in His law. 11 They forgot His works and the wonders He had shown them.

12 He did wonders for their fathers in Egypt, in the area of Zoan. 13 He split the sea and led them through it. He made the water stand like a wall. 14 He led them with a cloud by day and with a light of fire all night. 15 He split rocks in the desert and gave them water as deep as the ocean. 16 He brought streams from the rock and made water flow like rivers.

17 But they kept sinning against Him. They rebelled against the Most High in the desert. 18 They tested God in their hearts by demanding food they wanted. 19 They spoke against God and said, "Can God give us food in the desert? 20 He struck the rock, and water poured out, and streams flowed. Can He also give bread? Can He provide meat for His people?"

21 So the LORD heard and was very angry. Fire broke out against Jacob, and His anger rose against Israel. 22 That was because they didn't believe in God or trust His power to save. 23 Yet He commanded the clouds above and opened the doors of heaven. 24 He rained manna down for them to eat. He gave them grain from heaven. 25 They ate heavenly food, and He gave them plenty.

26 He made the east wind blow in the sky and drove the south wind by His power. 27 He rained meat on them like dust, and birds like sand on the shore. 28 He made them fall in the middle of their camp, all around their tents. 29 They ate and were full, because He gave them what they wanted. 30 But before they were satisfied, while the food was still in their mouths, 31 God's anger rose against them. He killed their strongest men and struck down Israel's young men.

32 Even after all this, they kept sinning. Even after His wonders, they didn't believe. 33 So He ended their days in emptiness and their years in sudden fear. 34 When He killed them, they looked for Him. They turned back and searched for God. 35 They remembered that God was their Rock and that God Most High was their Redeemer. 36 But they only flattered Him with their mouths and lied to Him with their tongues. 37 Their hearts weren't faithful to Him, and they weren't true to His covenant. 38 Still, He was kind. He forgave their sin and didn't destroy them. He often held back His anger and didn't pour out all His wrath. 39 He remembered that they were only flesh, like a breeze that passes and doesn't come back.

40 How often they rebelled against Him in the desert and made Him sad in the wasteland! 41 Again and again they tested God and provoked the Holy One of Israel. 42 They didn't remember His power, the day He saved them from their enemies.

God's Power in Egypt and the Promised Land

43 He did signs in Egypt and wonders in the area of Zoan. 44 He turned their rivers to blood, so they couldn't drink from their streams. 45 He sent swarms of flies that ate them up, and frogs that ruined them. 46 He gave their crops to grasshoppers and the fruit of their work to locusts.

47 He destroyed their grapevines with hail and their fig trees with ice. 48 He handed their cattle over to hail and their livestock to lightning. 49 He sent His fierce anger against them—wrath, rage, and trouble—through a band of destroying angels. 50 He made a path for His anger. He didn't spare them from death but gave their lives to the plague. 51 He struck down all the firstborn in Egypt, the first children born in the land of Ham.

52 But He led His people like sheep and guided them like a flock in the desert. 53 He led them safely, so they weren't afraid, while the sea covered their enemies. 54 He brought them to His holy land, to the mountain His right hand had won. 55 He drove out nations before them and gave them their land by lot. He settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

From Shiloh to David's Throne

56 But they tested and rebelled against God Most High. They didn't keep His rules. 57 They turned back and were unfaithful, like their fathers. They were as unreliable as a bow that doesn't shoot straight. 58 They made Him angry with their hilltop altars and provoked Him with their idols. 59 God heard and was furious. He completely rejected Israel. 60 He abandoned the tent at Shiloh, the place He had set up among people.

61 He let His strength be taken captive and His glory fall into enemy hands. 62 He gave His people to the sword and poured out His anger on His own land. 63 Fire burned up their young men, and their young women didn't get married. 64 Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows couldn't even weep.

65 Then the LORD woke up, as if from sleep, like a strong warrior full of courage. 66 He beat back His enemies and put them to lasting shame. 67 He rejected Joseph's tent and didn't choose Ephraim's tribe. 68 Instead, He chose Judah's tribe and Mount Zion, which He loved.

69 He built His holy place high like the heavens and firm like the earth He made to last forever. 70 He chose David, His servant, and took him from the sheep pens. 71 He brought him from caring for sheep to shepherd His people, Jacob, and Israel, His inheritance. 72 David led them with an honest heart and guided them with skillful hands.
