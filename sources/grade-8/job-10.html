Job Tells God How He Feels

"1 I am disgusted with my life, so I will speak my complaint and talk from my bitter heart. 2 I say to God, 'Do not condemn me. Tell me why You are fighting me.' 3 Is it right for You to oppress me, to reject what Your hands made, and favor the plans of wicked people?"

Job Questions God's View of Him

"4 Do You have human eyes? Do You see the way people see? 5 Are Your days like those of a human, or Your years like those of a mortal? 6 Why do You look for my guilt and search for my sin? 7 You know I am not guilty, and no one can rescue me from Your hand."

Job Remembers How God Made Him

"8 Your hands shaped me and made me; now You destroy me. 9 Please remember that You made me like clay. Will You now return me to dust? 10 Did You not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese? 11 You clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and tendons. 12 You have given me life and love, and Your care has kept my spirit alive. 13 Yet You hid these things in Your heart; I know this was part of Your plan."

Job Asks for Relief

"14 If I sinned, You would notice, and You would not clear me of my guilt. 15 If I am guilty, I am doomed. If I am innocent, I still cannot lift my head, because I am full of shame. Look at my suffering! 16 If I hold my head high, You hunt me like a lion and show Your power against me again. 17 You bring new witnesses against me and increase Your anger toward me; fresh troubles come against me in waves. 18 Why did You bring me out of the womb? I wish I had died before anyone saw me. 19 Then I would be as if I had never lived, carried from the womb to the grave."

"20 Are not my days few? Leave me alone so I can have a little comfort 21 before I go and never return, to a land of darkness and gloom, 22 a land of deep darkness and disorder, where even the light is like darkness."
