A Lament for Pharaoh, King of Egypt

1 In the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month, the word of the LORD came to me:

2 "Son of man, raise a lament for Pharaoh king of Egypt and say to him, You are like a lion among the nations; you are like a monster in the seas. You thrash about in your rivers, churn the water with your feet, and muddy their streams.

The LORD's Judgment on Egypt

3 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: I will spread My net over you with a crowd of many peoples, and they will haul you up in My dragnet.

4 "I will throw you on the land and hurl you on the open field. I will let all the birds of the sky settle on you, and I will let all the beasts of the earth feed on you.

5 "I will put your flesh on the mountains and fill the valleys with your remains.

6 "I will water the land with your blood as far as the mountains, and the streams will be filled with you.

7 "When I snuff you out, I will cover the heavens and darken their stars. I will cover the sun with a cloud, and the moon will not give its light.

8 "All the shining lights in the heavens I will darken over you, and I will bring darkness on your land, declares the Lord the LORD.

9 "I will trouble the hearts of many peoples when I bring your ruin among the nations, in lands you have not known.

10 "I will make many peoples appalled at you, and their kings will shudder with horror because of you when I brandish My sword before them. On the day of your fall, each man will tremble for his own life every moment.

11 "For this is what the Lord the LORD says: The sword of the king of Babylon will come against you.

12 "I will bring down your hordes with the swords of warriors, the most ruthless of nations. They will plunder the pride of Egypt, and all her hordes will be wiped out.

13 "I will destroy all her livestock near the many waters, and no human foot or animal hoof will stir them up again.

14 "Then I will let her waters calm down and make her rivers flow like oil, declares the Lord the LORD.

15 "When I make the land of Egypt a desolation, stripped of everything in it, when I strike down all who live there, then they will know that I am the LORD.

16 "This is a lament, and the daughters of the nations will chant it. They will chant it over Egypt and over all her hordes, declares the Lord the LORD."

Egypt Sent Down to the Pit

17 In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:

18 "Son of man, wail over the hordes of Egypt and send her down, along with the daughters of mighty nations, to the depths of the earth, to those who go down to the pit.

19 "Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down and lie with the uncircumcised.

20 "They will fall among those slain by the sword. The sword is drawn; drag her away with all her hordes.

21 "From within Sheol, the mighty leaders, along with his allies, will speak to him:

"'They have gone down and lie with the uncircumcised, slain by the sword.'

22 "Assyria is there with all her company; her graves are all around her. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword.

23 "Her graves are placed in the depths of the pit, and her company surrounds her grave. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword, those who once spread terror in the land of the living.

24 "Elam is there with all her hordes around her grave. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword. They went down uncircumcised to the lower parts of the earth, those who spread their terror in the land of the living. They bear their shame with those who go down to the pit.

25 "A bed is made for her among the slain, with all her hordes. Her graves are all around her. All of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, because their terror was spread in the land of the living. They bear their shame with those who go down to the pit; they are placed among the slain.

26 "Meshech and Tubal are there with all their hordes. Their graves are all around them. All of them are uncircumcised, slain by the sword, because they spread their terror in the land of the living.

27 "They do not lie with the fallen warriors of the uncircumcised, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war. They placed their swords under their heads, but their sins rest on their bones, because they terrified the mighty in the land of the living.

28 "But you will be broken and lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword.

29 "Edom is there, her kings and all her leaders, who in their strength were laid among those slain by the sword. They lie with the uncircumcised, with those who go down to the pit.

30 "All the princes of the north are there, and all the Sidonians, who went down in shame with the slain, because of the terror they caused by their might. They lie uncircumcised with those slain by the sword and bear their shame with those who go down to the pit.

31 "Pharaoh will see them and be comforted over all his hordes—Pharaoh and all his army, slain by the sword, declares the Lord the LORD.

32 "For I have put his terror in the land of the living, and he will be laid among the uncircumcised with those slain by the sword—Pharaoh and all his hordes, declares the Lord the LORD."
