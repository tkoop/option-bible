Peter and John Arrested

1 While Peter and John were speaking to the people, the priests, the captain of the temple guard, and the Sadducees came up to them. 2 They were upset because Peter and John were teaching the people and telling them that Jesus had risen from the dead. 3 They seized Peter and John and put them in jail until the next day, because it was already evening.

4 But many who heard the message believed, and the number of men grew to about five thousand.

Peter Speaks Before the Council

5 The next day the rulers, elders, and teachers of the law met in Jerusalem. 6 Annas the high priest was there, along with Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and others from the high priest's family.

7 They had Peter and John stand before them and asked, "By what power or in what name did you do this?"

8 Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, "Rulers and elders of the people, 9 if we are being questioned today about a good deed done for a sick man and asked how he was healed, 10 then let all of you know, and let all the people of Israel know, that this man stands healthy before you because of the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. You crucified Him, but God raised Him from the dead.

"11 Jesus is the stone that you builders rejected, but He has become the cornerstone. 12 Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to people by which we must be saved."

13 The council members saw how bold Peter and John were. They realized Peter and John were uneducated, ordinary men, and they were amazed. They also recognized that these men had been with Jesus. 14 Because the man who had been healed was standing there with them, the council had nothing to say.

The Council Warns the Apostles

15 So they ordered Peter and John to leave the council room, and then the council members talked with one another. 16 They said, "What should we do with these men? Everyone in Jerusalem knows they have done an obvious miracle, and we cannot deny it. 17 But we must keep this from spreading any further among the people. So let's warn them not to speak to anyone else in this name."

18 They called Peter and John back in and ordered them not to speak or teach at all in the name of Jesus.

19 But Peter and John answered, "Decide for yourselves whether it is right in God's sight to listen to you instead of God. 20 For we cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard."

21 After more threats, the council let them go. They could not find a way to punish them, because all the people were praising God for what had happened. 22 The man who had been healed was more than forty years old.

The Believers Pray and Share

23 When Peter and John were released, they went to their own people and reported everything the chief priests and elders had said to them.

24 When the believers heard this, they raised their voices together to God and said, "Sovereign Lord, You made heaven, earth, the sea, and everything in them. 25 You spoke through the Holy Spirit by the mouth of our father David, Your servant: 'Why did the nations rage, and the peoples make empty plans? 26 The kings of the earth stood up, and the rulers gathered together against the Lord and against His Christ.'

"27 In truth, Herod and Pontius Pilate met in this city with the Gentiles and the people of Israel against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed. 28 They did whatever Your hand and Your plan had decided beforehand would happen.

"29 And now, Lord, look at their threats, and give Your servants courage to speak Your word with complete boldness. 30 Stretch out Your hand to heal, and perform signs and wonders through the name of Your holy Servant Jesus."

31 After they prayed, the place where they were gathered shook. They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke the word of God boldly.

32 All the believers were one in heart and mind. No one said that any of his possessions was his own, but they shared everything. 33 The apostles gave their testimony about the resurrection of the Lord Jesus with great power, and great grace was on all of them.

34 There were no needy people among them. Those who owned land or houses sold them and brought the money from the sales 35 and laid it at the apostles' feet. Then it was given to anyone who needed it. 36 Joseph, a Levite from Cyprus, whom the apostles called Barnabas (which means "son of encouragement"), 37 sold a field he owned and brought the money and laid it at the apostles' feet.
