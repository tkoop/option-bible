Persecution Scatters the Believers

1 Saul approved of Stephen’s death. That same day, a great persecution broke out against the church in Jerusalem. Everyone except the apostles was scattered throughout Judea and Samaria. 2 Some devout men buried Stephen and mourned deeply over him. 3 Saul was ruining the church. He went into homes, dragged out men and women, and put them in prison. 4 The believers who were scattered traveled around and told people the good news.

Philip Preaches in Samaria

5 Philip went down to a city in Samaria and preached about Christ to the people there. 6 The crowds listened closely to what Philip said. They heard his words and saw the signs he did. 7 Evil spirits cried out with loud voices and came out of many people. Many who were paralyzed or lame were healed. 8 So the city was filled with great joy.

Simon and the Apostles

9 A man named Simon had been practicing magic in that city. He amazed the people of Samaria and claimed to be someone great. 10 Everyone, from the least to the greatest, paid attention to him. They said, “This man is the power of God called Great.” 11 They followed him because he had amazed them with his magic for a long time. 12 But when they believed Philip as he preached the good news about God’s kingdom and the name of Jesus Christ, men and women were baptized. 13 Simon himself believed and was baptized. Then he stayed close to Philip and was amazed at the great signs and miracles he saw.

14 The apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted God’s word, so they sent Peter and John to them. 15 When Peter and John arrived, they prayed for the new believers to receive the Holy Spirit. 16 The Spirit had not yet come on any of them; they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. 17 Then Peter and John laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit.

18 When Simon saw that the Spirit was given through the apostles’ hands, he offered them money. 19 He said, “Give me this power too, so that anyone I lay my hands on will receive the Holy Spirit.”

20 Peter said to him, “May your money be destroyed along with you, because you thought you could buy God’s gift with money! 21 You have no part or share in this, because your heart is not right with God. 22 So repent of this wickedness and pray to the Lord. Maybe He will forgive the evil thought in your heart. 23 I can see that you are full of bitterness and bound by sin.”

24 Simon answered, “Pray to the Lord for me, so that none of what you said will happen to me.”

25 After Peter and John had warned them and preached the Lord’s word, they returned to Jerusalem. On the way, they preached the good news in many Samaritan villages.

Philip and the Ethiopian

26 An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip. He said, “Get up and go south to the road that leads down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” That road runs through the desert. 27 So Philip got up and went. He met an Ethiopian man, a eunuch who was an important official in charge of all the treasure of Candace, queen of Ethiopia. He had come to Jerusalem to worship. 28 Now he was on his way home. He was sitting in his chariot and reading the prophet Isaiah.

29 The Holy Spirit said to Philip, “Go to that chariot and stay close to it.”

30 Philip ran up and heard the man reading Isaiah the prophet. Philip asked, “Do you understand what you are reading?”

31 The man said, “How can I understand unless someone explains it to me?” Then he invited Philip to climb up and sit with him.

32 The passage he was reading said, “He was led like a sheep to be killed. Like a lamb before the one who shears it, He did not open His mouth. 33 In His humiliation, justice was taken from Him. Who can describe His family? His life was taken from the earth.”

34 The eunuch asked Philip, “Who is the prophet talking about—himself or someone else?” 35 Then Philip began with that passage and told him the good news about Jesus.

36 As they traveled along the road, they came to some water. The eunuch said, “Look, there’s water! What’s stopping me from being baptized?” 38 He ordered the chariot to stop. Both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him. 39 When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord suddenly took Philip away. The eunuch did not see him anymore, but he went on his way rejoicing. 40 Philip was found at Azotus. He traveled through the towns and preached the good news in every city until he reached Caesarea.
