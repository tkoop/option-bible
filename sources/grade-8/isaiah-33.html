Woe to the Destroyer

1 Woe to you, destroyer, who have not been destroyed! Woe to you, traitor, who have not been betrayed! When you finish destroying, you will be destroyed; when you stop betraying, you will be betrayed.

2 The LORD, be gracious to us; we have waited for you. Be our arm every morning, our salvation in time of trouble.

3 At the sound of Your voice, peoples flee; when You are lifted up, nations scatter. 4 Your plunder is gathered like caterpillars gather; like locusts swarm, so people swarm over it.

The LORD's Justice and Salvation

5 The LORD is lifted high, because He lives on high; He has filled Zion with justice and righteousness. 6 He will be the stability of your times, a wealth of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge; the fear of the LORD is His treasure.

7 Look, their brave men cry out in the streets; the messengers of peace weep bitterly. 8 The roads are empty; travelers have stopped using them. The agreement is broken; cities are hated; no one pays attention to people. 9 The land is sad and drying up; Lebanon is ashamed and wilts; Sharon is like a desert; Bashan and Carmel drop their leaves.

The LORD Rises to Judge

10 "Now I will rise up," says the LORD. "Now I will lift Myself up; now I will be honored. 11 You conceive chaff; you give birth to straw. Your breath is a fire that consumes you.

12 "And the peoples will be burned to ashes, like thorns cut down and set on fire. 13 You who are far away, hear what I have done; you who are near, acknowledge My power."

14 Sinners in Zion are terrified; trembling grips the godless: "Who among us can live with the consuming fire? Who among us can live with everlasting burning?"

15 He who walks righteously and speaks honestly, who rejects money gained by cheating, who will not take bribes, who refuses to listen to talk of bloodshed, and who will not look at evil. 16 He will live on high; his safe place will be a mountain fortress; his food will be given to him, and his water will be sure.

God's Promise to Zion

17 Your eyes will see the King in His beauty and view a land that stretches far away. 18 Your heart will ponder the terror: "Where is the scribe? Where is the weigher? Where is the one who counts the towers?"

19 You will no longer see the fierce people, a people with strange speech you cannot understand, stammering in a language you cannot understand. 20 Look at Zion, the city of our festivals. Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a peaceful home, a tent that will not be moved; its stakes will never be pulled up, and none of its ropes will be broken.

21 But there the LORD, our Mighty One, will be for us a place of rivers and wide canals, where no boat with oars will go, and no large ship will pass. 22 For the LORD is our Judge, the LORD is our Lawgiver, the LORD is our King; He will save us.

23 Your ropes are untied; they cannot hold the mast firm, nor spread the sail. Then a great amount of spoil will be divided; even the lame will carry off plunder. 24 And no one living there will say, "I am sick"; the people who live there will be forgiven their sin.
