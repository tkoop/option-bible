Jehoram Becomes King of Israel

1 In the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat's rule over Judah, Jehoram son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled for twelve years. 2 He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD, but not like his father and mother. He removed the sacred stone of Baal that his father had made. 3 Still, he held on to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which had led Israel into sin. He did not turn away from them.

Moab Rebels Against Israel

4 Mesha, king of Moab, raised sheep. He used to send the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs and the wool from one hundred thousand rams. 5 But after Ahab died, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel. 6 So King Jehoram marched out from Samaria and called up all Israel.

7 He sent a message to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah: "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me to fight against Moab?"

Jehoshaphat answered, "I will go. I am like you. My people are like your people, and my horses are like your horses."

8 Then Jehoram asked, "Which way should we go?"

Jehoshaphat replied, "Through the Desert of Edom."

9 So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom set out. After marching around for seven days, there was no water for the army or for the animals with them.

10 The king of Israel said, "Oh no! Has the the LORD brought these three kings together only to let Moab defeat us?"

The Kings Seek a Prophet

11 But Jehoshaphat asked, "Is there no prophet of the the LORD here? Then we could ask the the LORD through him."

One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here. He used to serve Elijah."

12 Jehoshaphat said, "The word of the the LORD is with him." So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to see him.

13 Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What do you and I have to do with each other? Go to the prophets of your father and your mother!"

The king of Israel replied, "No, because the the LORD has brought these three kings together to hand them over to Moab."

14 Elisha said, "As surely as the the LORD of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, if I did not respect Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not look at you or notice you."

15 Elisha said, "But now bring me someone who plays the harp." While the harpist played, the hand of the the LORD came upon Elisha.

16 He said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'Make this valley full of ditches.' 17 The LORD also says, 'You will not see wind or rain, but this valley will be filled with water, and you and your cattle and your animals will drink.' 18 And this is an easy thing for the the LORD. He will also hand Moab over to you. 19 You must attack every walled city and every important city. Cut down every good tree, stop up every spring of water, and ruin every good field by covering it with stones."

Israel Defeats Moab

20 The next morning, about the time of the morning sacrifice, water came flowing from the direction of Edom, and the land was filled with water.

21 All the Moabites heard that the kings had come to fight them. So every man who could carry a weapon was called out, and they stood at the border.

22 When they got up early in the morning, the sun was shining on the water. To the Moabites across the way, the water looked red like blood.

23 They said, "This is blood! The kings must have fought and killed each other. Now, Moab, take the plunder!"

24 But when they came to Israel's camp, the Israelites rose up and struck the Moabites, who ran away from them. The Israelites went into the land and attacked Moab.

25 They destroyed the cities. Each man threw a stone into every good field until it was covered. They stopped up every spring and cut down every good tree. Only Kir Hareseth was left with its stones in place, but the slingers surrounded it and attacked it.

26 When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, he took seven hundred swordsmen to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

27 Then he took his oldest son, who was to rule after him, and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Great anger came against Israel, so they withdrew from him and returned to their own land.
