David Looks for Saul's Family

1 David asked, "Is there anyone still left of Saul's family? I want to show kindness to him because of Jonathan."

2 Saul's family had a servant named Ziba. They called him to David. The king said to him, "Are you Ziba?"

Ziba said, "Yes, I am your servant."

3 The king said, "Is there no one left of Saul's family? I want to show God's kindness to him."

Ziba said to the king, "Jonathan still has a son. Both his feet are lame."

Mephibosheth Is Brought to David

4 The king said to him, "Where is he?"

Ziba said to the king, "He is in Lo-debar, at the house of Machir son of Ammiel."

5 King David sent for him and brought him from Machir's house in Lo-debar.

6 Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, the grandson of Saul, came to David. He bowed with his face to the ground. David said, "Mephibosheth!"

Mephibosheth said, "Here I am, your servant."

David's Promise to Mephibosheth

7 David said to him, "Don't be afraid. I will show kindness to you because of your father Jonathan. I will give back all the land of your father Saul. You will always eat at my table."

8 Mephibosheth bowed and said, "What am I, your servant, that you should care about someone as worthless as me?"

David Restores Saul's Land

9 The king called Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "I have given your master's son everything that belonged to Saul and his family. 10 You, your sons, and your servants will farm the land for him. You will bring in the crops so your master's son will have food to eat. But Mephibosheth, your master's son, will always eat at my table." Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

11 Ziba said to the king, "Your servant will do everything my lord the king commands."

The king said, "Mephibosheth will eat at my table like one of the king's sons."

12 Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica. Everyone living in Ziba's house was a servant to Mephibosheth.

13 Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, because he always ate at the king's table. He was lame in both feet.
