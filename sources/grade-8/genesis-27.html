Isaac Plans to Bless Esau

1When Isaac was old, his eyes were so weak that he could not see well. He called his older son Esau and said, "My son." Esau answered, "Here I am." 2Isaac said, "I am old now, and I do not know when I will die. 3So take your weapons, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field to hunt some wild game for me. 4Then make the kind of tasty food I love, bring it to me, and I will eat it. After that, I will bless you before I die."

Rebekah and Jacob Plan a Trick

5Rebekah was listening while Isaac spoke to his son Esau. When Esau went out to the field to hunt game and bring it back, 6Rebekah said to her son Jacob, "I heard your father tell your brother Esau, 7'Bring me game and make tasty food for me. I will eat it and bless you in the presence of the LORD before I die.' 8Now, my son, listen to me and do what I tell you. 9Go to the flock and bring me two good young goats. I will make tasty food from them for your father, the way he loves it. 10Then take it to your father so he can eat it and bless you before he dies."

11Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Look, my brother Esau is a hairy man, but I am a smooth man. 12What if my father feels me? He would think I am tricking him, and I would bring a curse on myself instead of a blessing."

13His mother said to him, "Let the curse fall on me, my son. Just listen to me. Go and get the goats for me."

14So Jacob went and got the goats and brought them to his mother. She made tasty food, just the way his father liked it. 15Rebekah took the best clothes of her older son Esau, which she had in the house, and put them on her younger son Jacob. 16She put the goatskins on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck. 17Then she put the tasty food and the bread she had made into Jacob's hands.

Isaac Blesses Jacob

18Jacob went to his father and said, "My father." Isaac asked, "Who are you, my son?" 19Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done what you told me. Please get up, sit down, and eat some of my game so you can bless me." 20Isaac said to his son, "How did you find it so quickly, my son?" Jacob said, "Because the LORD your God made it happen for me."

21Isaac said to Jacob, "Please come close so I can feel you, my son, and know whether you really are my son Esau or not." 22Jacob went close to his father Isaac, and Isaac felt him. Isaac said, "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are Esau's hands." 23Isaac did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau's hands, so he blessed him. 24Isaac asked, "Are you really my son Esau?" Jacob said, "I am." 25Isaac said, "Bring it close to me, and I will eat some of my son's game so I can bless you." Jacob brought it close, and Isaac ate. Jacob also brought him wine, and he drank.

26His father Isaac said to him, "Come close now and kiss me, my son." 27So Jacob came close and kissed him. Isaac smelled the smell of his clothes and blessed him. He said, "Ah, the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that the LORD has blessed. 28May God give you the dew of heaven and the richness of the earth, along with plenty of grain and new wine. 29May peoples serve you and nations bow down to you. Be master over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. Cursed be those who curse you, and blessed be those who bless you."

Esau's Plea and Jacob's Flight

30As soon as Isaac finished blessing Jacob, Jacob had barely left his father Isaac's presence when his brother Esau came in from his hunting. 31Esau had also made tasty food and brought it to his father. He said, "Father, please get up and eat some of your son's game so you can bless me." 32His father Isaac asked him, "Who are you?" Esau said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau." 33Isaac trembled violently and said, "Who was it, then, that hunted game and brought it to me? I ate all of it before you came, and I blessed him. Yes, he will be blessed."

34When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out with a loud and very bitter cry. He said to his father, "Bless me too, my father!" 35Isaac said, "Your brother came and tricked me, and he took your blessing." 36Esau said, "Isn't he rightly named Jacob? He has cheated me twice now. He took my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing." Then he asked, "Haven't you saved a blessing for me?"

37Isaac answered Esau, "I have made him your master, and I have given him all his brothers as servants. I have supported him with grain and new wine. What can I do for you now, my son?" 38Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, my father!" Then Esau wept loudly.

39His father Isaac answered him, "Your home will be away from the rich soil of the earth and away from the dew of heaven above. 40You will live by your sword, and you will serve your brother. But when you grow restless, you will throw off his yoke from your neck."

41Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing his father had given him. Esau said to himself, "The days of mourning for my father will soon come, and then I will kill my brother Jacob." 42Someone told Rebekah what her older son Esau had said. So she sent for her younger son Jacob and said to him, "Your brother Esau is comforting himself by planning to kill you. 43Now, my son, listen to me. Run away to my brother Laban in Haran. 44Stay with him for a little while, until your brother's anger calms down. 45When his anger has eased and he forgets what you did to him, I will send for you and bring you back. Why should I lose both of you in one day?"

46Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am tired of living because of these Hittite women. If Jacob marries a Hittite woman like them, one of the women of this land, what good is life to me?"
