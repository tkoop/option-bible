Greeting

1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write to the church in Thessalonica. You are in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 2 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Thanks for Their Growing Faith

3 Brothers and sisters, we always need to thank God for you. That is right, because your faith is growing more and more. Also, each of you loves the others more and more. 4 So we brag about you to God's churches. We tell them about your patience and faith. You stay strong through all the trouble and hardship you face.

5 This shows that God judges fairly. He is making you worthy of His kingdom. You are suffering for that kingdom.

God's Fair Judgment

6 God is fair. He will give trouble to the people who trouble you. 7 He will give rest to you who are troubled, and to us too. This will happen when the Lord Jesus comes from heaven with His powerful angels.

8 He will come in blazing fire. He will punish those who do not know God. He will also punish those who do not obey the good news about our Lord Jesus. 9 They will be punished with lasting destruction. They will be kept away from the Lord's presence and from His glorious power.

10 This will happen on the day He comes. He will be honored among His holy people. Everyone who believed will be amazed at Him. You believed the message we brought you.

Paul's Prayer for Them

11 So we always pray for you. We ask God to make you worthy of His call. We ask Him to use His power to complete every good desire and every act of faith. 12 Then the name of our Lord Jesus will be honored in you. You will also be honored in Him. This comes from the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
