Jehoram Becomes King

1 Jehoshaphat died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Jehoram became king after him. 2 Jehoshaphat had other sons: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, another Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. These were all the sons of Jehoshaphat, king of Israel. 3 Their father gave them many gifts of silver, gold, and valuable things, along with fortified cities in Judah. But he gave the kingdom to Jehoram, because Jehoram was the oldest son. 4 After Jehoram took control of his father's kingdom, he made himself strong. Then he killed all his brothers with a sword, along with some of the leaders of Israel.

Jehoram's Evil Reign

5 Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king. He ruled eight years in Jerusalem. 6 He followed the ways of the kings of Israel, just as Ahab's family did, because Ahab's daughter was his wife. He did what was evil in the eyes of the the LORD. 7 But the the LORD did not want to destroy David's family. He had made an agreement with David and promised that David's family line would continue forever.

Revolt and Warning

8 During his rule, Edom broke free from Judah's control and chose its own king. 9 Jehoram went out with his commanders and all his chariots. The Edomites surrounded him, but he and his chariot commanders attacked at night. 10 Edom has stayed free from Judah to this day. At that same time, Libnah also broke free, because Jehoram had left the the LORD, the God of his fathers. 11 He also built high places in the hills of Judah. He led the people of Jerusalem into unfaithfulness and led Judah away from the the LORD.

12 A letter came to him from Elijah the prophet. It said, "This is what the the LORD, the God of your father David, says: You have not followed the ways of your father Jehoshaphat or the ways of Asa, king of Judah. 13 Instead, you have followed the ways of the kings of Israel. You have led Judah and the people of Jerusalem into unfaithfulness, just as Ahab's family did. You have also killed your brothers, who were better men than you. 14 So the the LORD will strike your people, your children, your wives, and everything you own with a terrible disease. 15 You will become very sick. Your intestines will come out because of the sickness, and this will go on day after day."

Judgment and Death

16 Then the the LORD turned the Philistines and the Arabs who lived near the Cushites against Jehoram. 17 They came up into Judah and broke into it. They carried off everything found in the king's house, along with his sons and wives. Only his youngest son, Jehoahaz, was left. 18 After all this, the the LORD struck Jehoram with a disease in his intestines that could not be healed. 19 After two years, his intestines came out because of the disease, and he died in great pain. His people did not make a fire for him, as they had for his fathers.

20 Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled eight years in Jerusalem. He died without anyone mourning him. He was buried in the City of David, but not in the tombs of the kings.
