More Proverbs of Solomon

1 These are more wise sayings of Solomon, copied by the men of King Hezekiah of Judah.

2 It brings glory to God to keep a matter hidden, and it brings glory to kings to search it out. 3 The sky is high, the earth is deep, and no one can fully understand a king's heart.

4 Remove the impurities from silver, and the refiner can shape it into something useful. 5 Remove wicked people from a king's presence, and his rule will be built on what is right.

6 Don't show off in front of a king or take the place of important people. 7 It's better for someone to say, "Come up here," than for you to be moved down in front of a noble you can see.

Wise Words and Relationships

8 Don't rush into a fight. You may not know what to do at the end, especially if your neighbor puts you to shame. 9 Settle your disagreement with your neighbor directly, and don't reveal someone else's secret. 10 Otherwise, the person who hears it may shame you, and your bad reputation may never go away.

11 The right word at the right time is like golden apples in silver settings. 12 A wise correction to a listening ear is like a gold ring or fine gold jewelry. 13 A faithful messenger refreshes those who send him, just as snow-cooled air refreshes people during harvest.

14 A person who brags about a gift he never gives is like clouds and wind that bring no rain. 15 Patience can persuade a ruler, and gentle words can break strong resistance.

16 If you find honey, eat only what you need. If you eat too much, you may get sick. 17 Don't visit your neighbor's house too often, or he may get tired of you and hate you.

18 A person who lies about his neighbor is like a club, a sword, and a sharp arrow. 19 Trusting an unfaithful person in trouble is like a broken tooth or a sprained ankle. 20 Singing cheerful songs to a sad person is like taking off someone's coat in cold weather or pouring vinegar on baking soda.

Kindness, Self-Control, and Character

21 If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he is thirsty, give him water to drink. 22 Your kindness will make him feel ashamed, and the LORD will reward you.

23 A north wind brings rain, and a gossiping tongue brings angry looks. 24 It's better to live in a corner of the roof than share a house with a nagging wife. 25 Good news from a faraway land is like cold water to a thirsty person.

26 A good person who gives in to the wicked is like a polluted spring. 27 It's not good to eat too much honey, and seeking your own glory isn't glorious. 28 A person who can't control his temper is like a city with broken walls.
