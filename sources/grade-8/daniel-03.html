Nebuchadnezzar's Gold Statue

1 King Nebuchadnezzar made a gold statue. It was about ninety feet tall and nine feet wide. He set it up in the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon. 2 Then King Nebuchadnezzar sent word to gather the satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all the other rulers of the provinces. They were to come to the dedication of the statue he had set up. 3 So all these officials came for the dedication of the statue King Nebuchadnezzar had set up, and they stood in front of it.

4 Then a herald shouted, "People of every nation, tribe, and language, listen to this command: 5 When you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music, you must fall down and worship the gold statue King Nebuchadnezzar has set up. 6 Anyone who does not fall down and worship it will be thrown at once into a blazing furnace." 7 So when all the people heard the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, and every kind of music, they fell down and worshiped the gold statue King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

The Three Jews Refuse to Bow

8 At that time some Babylonians came forward and accused the Jews.

9 They said to King Nebuchadnezzar, "O king, live forever! 10 You gave a command, O king, that everyone who hears the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music must fall down and worship the gold statue. 11 And anyone who does not fall down and worship it must be thrown into a blazing furnace. 12 But there are some Jews you put in charge of the province of Babylon—Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego—who do not obey your command. They do not serve your gods or worship the gold statue you set up."

13 Then Nebuchadnezzar became furious. In his rage he ordered Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to be brought in. So they were brought before the king.

14 Nebuchadnezzar said to them, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is it true that you do not serve my gods or worship the gold statue I set up? 15 Now when you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music, be ready to fall down and worship the statue I made. But if you do not worship it, you will be thrown at once into a blazing furnace. Then what god will be able to rescue you from my power?"

16 Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered the king, "Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to defend ourselves before you about this. 17 If the God we serve is able to save us from the blazing furnace and from your hand, O king, He will save us. 18 But even if He does not, we want you to know, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the gold statue you set up."

The Furnace and God's Rescue

19 Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with anger. His face changed toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual. 20 He ordered some of the strongest soldiers in his army to tie up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and throw them into the blazing furnace. 21 So these men were tied up in their robes, trousers, turbans, and other clothes, and thrown into the blazing furnace. 22 Because the king's command was so harsh and the furnace so hot, the flames killed the men who carried Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego up. 23 And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the blazing furnace while still tied up.

24 Then King Nebuchadnezzar jumped up in alarm. He asked his advisers, "Didn't we throw three men, tied up, into the fire?"

They answered, "Yes, O king."

25 He said, "Look! I see four men walking around in the fire, unharmed. And the fourth looks like a son of the gods."

26 Then Nebuchadnezzar went to the opening of the blazing furnace and shouted, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out!" So they came out of the fire.

27 The satraps, prefects, governors, and royal advisers gathered around. They saw that the fire had not harmed the men's bodies. The hair on their heads was not singed. Their robes were not scorched, and there was no smell of fire on them.

The King's Decree

28 Nebuchadnezzar said, "Praise be to the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He sent His angel and rescued His servants. They trusted in Him and refused to obey the king's command. They were willing to give up their bodies rather than serve or worship any god except their own God."

29 "So I make this decree: Anyone of any nation or language who says anything against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego will be cut into pieces, and his house will be turned into a pile of rubble. For no other god can save like this." 30 And the king gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego more important jobs in the province of Babylon.
