Cities for the Levites

1 The LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho. He said, 2 "Command the Israelites to give the Levites cities to live in from the land they own, and you must also give the Levites pastureland around those cities. 3 The cities will be for the Levites to live in, and the pastureland will be for their cattle, their goods, and all their animals. 4 The pastureland around the cities that you give the Levites must reach a thousand cubits from the city wall all the way around. 5 You must measure two thousand cubits outside the city on the east, two thousand on the south, two thousand on the west, and two thousand on the north, with the city in the middle. This will be the pastureland for the cities. 6 Among the cities you give the Levites, six will be cities of refuge, where a person who has killed someone can run for safety. Besides these, you must give them forty-two more cities. 7 So all the cities you give the Levites will be forty-eight, along with their pastureland. 8 The cities you give the Levites from the Israelites' land must come from the larger tribes more and from the smaller tribes less. Each tribe must give cities to the Levites in proportion to the land it receives."

Cities of Refuge

9 Then the LORD spoke to Moses and said, 10 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'When you cross the Jordan into Canaan, 11 you must choose cities to be your cities of refuge. A person who kills someone by accident can run there for safety. 12 These cities will protect him from the relative who seeks revenge, so the killer will not die until he stands trial before the people. 13 The cities you give will be six cities of refuge for you. 14 You must give three cities east of the Jordan and three in Canaan. They will be cities of refuge. 15 These six cities will be a safe place for Israelites, foreigners, and travelers living among them, so anyone who kills a person by accident can run there.'"

Murder and Accidental Killing

16 If a man hits someone with an iron tool and kills him, he is a murderer and must be put to death. 17 If he hits him with a stone that could kill and the man dies, he is a murderer and must be put to death. 18 If he hits him with a wooden tool that could kill and the man dies, he is a murderer and must be put to death. 19 The relative who seeks revenge must kill the murderer when he finds him. 20 If a man pushes someone out of hatred, or throws something at him on purpose to kill him, 21 or hits him in anger and kills him, the one who hit him must be put to death. He is a murderer, and the relative who seeks revenge must kill him when he finds him.

22 But if he pushes someone suddenly, without hatred, or throws something at him without meaning to kill him, 23 or without seeing drops a stone that could kill on him and he dies, and the man was not his enemy and did not mean to hurt him, 24 then the community must judge between the killer and the relative who seeks revenge, using these rules. 25 The community must rescue the killer from the relative who seeks revenge and send him back to his city of refuge, where he fled. He must live there until the high priest dies, the one who was anointed with holy oil. 26 But if the killer leaves his city of refuge, 27 and the relative who seeks revenge finds him outside the city, that relative may kill him without guilt. 28 The killer must stay in his city of refuge until the high priest dies. After the high priest dies, he may return to his own land.

Justice and the Land

29 These rules will be a law for you and your descendants in every place you live. 30 If anyone kills a person, the murderer must be put to death, but only if witnesses testify against him. One witness is not enough to put someone to death. 31 You must not accept money to spare the life of a murderer who deserves to die. He must be put to death. 32 You must not accept money to let someone who fled to a city of refuge return home before the high priest dies. 33 Do not make the land where you live unclean. Bloodshed makes the land unclean, and the land cannot be made clean except by the blood of the one who shed it. 34 Do not make unclean the land where you live, where I dwell among you. I am the LORD, and I dwell among the Israelites.
