John the Baptist Prepares the Way

1 In the fifteenth year of Emperor Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea. Herod was ruler of Galilee, his brother Philip was ruler of Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was ruler of Abilene. 2 Annas and Caiaphas were high priests. At that time, God's word came to John, son of Zechariah, in the desert. 3 John traveled all around the Jordan area. He preached that people should be baptized and turn from sin so their sins could be forgiven. 4 This was like Isaiah the prophet wrote: "A voice is calling in the desert, 'Prepare the way for the Lord. Make straight paths for Him.'" 5 Every valley will be filled. Every mountain and hill will be made low. Crooked roads will become straight, and rough roads will become smooth. 6 Then all people will see God's saving power.

John Calls People to Turn from Sin

7 John said to the crowds coming to be baptized, "You snakes' children! Who warned you to run from the coming anger? 8 Produce fruit that shows you have truly changed. Do not start saying to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones. 9 The ax is already at the root of the trees. Every tree that does not produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire."

10 The crowds asked him, "What should we do?" 11 He answered, "If anyone has two shirts, share with someone who has none. Do the same with food." 12 Tax collectors also came to be baptized. They said, "Teacher, what should we do?" 13 He said, "Collect no more than you are ordered to collect." 14 Soldiers asked him, "What should we do?" He said, "Do not force money from anyone. Do not make false charges. Be happy with your pay."

John Points to the Messiah

15 The people were waiting. They all wondered in their hearts if John might be the Messiah. 16 John answered everyone, "I baptize you with water. But someone stronger than me is coming. I am not worthy to untie His sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire. 17 His winnowing fork is in His hand. He will clean His threshing floor. He will gather the wheat into His barn. But He will burn the chaff with fire that never goes out." 18 John used many other words like this to encourage people as he preached the good news to them. 19 But Herod the ruler got in trouble with John. John corrected him about Herodias, his brother's wife, and about all the other evil things Herod had done. 20 Herod added this to his other sins: he locked John in prison.

Jesus Is Baptized and His Family Line

21 While all the people were being baptized, Jesus was baptized too. As He prayed, heaven opened. 22 The Holy Spirit came down on Him in bodily form, like a dove. A voice came from heaven: "You are My beloved Son. I am very pleased with you."

23 Jesus was about thirty years old when He began His work. He was thought to be the son of Joseph, son of Heli, 24 son of Matthat, son of Levi, son of Melchi, son of Jannai, son of Joseph, 25 son of Mattathias, son of Amos, son of Nahum, son of Esli, son of Naggai, 26 son of Maath, son of Mattathias, son of Semein, son of Josech, son of Joda, 27 son of Joanan, son of Rhesa, son of Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, son of Neri, 28 son of Melchi, son of Addi, son of Cosam, son of Elmadam, son of Er, 29 son of Joshua, son of Eliezer, son of Jorim, son of Matthat, son of Levi, 30 son of Simeon, son of Judah, son of Joseph, son of Jonam, son of Eliakim, 31 son of Melea, son of Menna, son of Mattatha, son of Nathan, son of David, 32 son of Jesse, son of Obed, son of Boaz, son of Salmon, son of Nahshon, 33 son of Amminadab, son of Admin, son of Arni, son of Hezron, son of Perez, son of Judah, 34 son of Jacob, son of Isaac, son of Abraham, son of Terah, son of Nahor, 35 son of Serug, son of Reu, son of Peleg, son of Eber, son of Shelah, 36 son of Cainan, son of Arphaxad, son of Shem, son of Noah, son of Lamech, 37 son of Methuselah, son of Enoch, son of Jared, son of Mahalaleel, son of Cainan, 38 son of Enos, son of Seth, son of Adam, son of God.
