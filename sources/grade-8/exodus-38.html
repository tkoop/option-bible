The Bronze Altar

1 He made the altar of burnt offering from acacia wood. It was square, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high. 2 He made horns on its four corners. The horns were one piece with the altar, and he covered it with bronze. 3 He made all the altar's utensils of bronze: the pots, shovels, basins, forks, and firepans.

4 He made a bronze grating, a net under the altar's rim, halfway up. 5 He cast four bronze rings for the four corners of the grating, to hold the poles. 6 He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with bronze. 7 He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the altar to carry it. He made the altar hollow, with boards.

The Bronze Basin

8 He made the bronze basin and its bronze stand from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

The Courtyard

9 He made the courtyard. On the south side, it was a hundred cubits long, with curtains of finely twisted linen. 10 There were twenty posts with twenty bronze bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 11 The north side was also a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 12 The west side was fifty cubits long, with ten posts and ten bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 13 The east side was fifty cubits wide.

14 On one side of the entrance, the curtains were fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 15 On the other side of the entrance to the courtyard, the curtains were also fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 16 All the curtains around the courtyard were of finely twisted linen. 17 The bases for the posts were bronze. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver, and their tops were covered with silver. All the posts around the courtyard had silver bands.

18 The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen. It was twenty cubits long and five cubits high, matching the courtyard curtains. 19 It had four posts with four bronze bases. Their hooks were silver, and their tops and bands were covered with silver. 20 All the tent pegs for the tabernacle and the courtyard were bronze.

The Materials Used

21 These are the accounts of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the Testimony. They were recorded at Moses' command. This was the work of the Levites under Ithamar son of Aaron the priest. 22 Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah, made everything the LORD commanded Moses. 23 With him was Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan. He was a craftsman, designer, and embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.

24 All the gold used for the work, in all the work of the sanctuary, was 29 talents and 730 shekels, by the sanctuary shekel. 25 The silver from the people counted in the community was 100 talents and 1,775 shekels, by the sanctuary shekel. 26 This was a beka, or half a shekel, per person, by the sanctuary shekel, for everyone twenty years old or older who was counted: 603,550 men.

27 From the 100 talents of silver, they cast the bases for the sanctuary and the bases for the veil: 100 bases from 100 talents, one talent for each base. 28 From the 1,775 shekels, he made hooks for the posts, covered their tops, and connected them. 29 The bronze from the offering was 70 talents and 2,400 shekels. 30 With it he made the bases for the entrance to the tent of meeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grating, and all the altar's utensils. 31 He also made the bases for the courtyard all around, the bases for the courtyard gate, and all the tent pegs for the tabernacle and the courtyard.
