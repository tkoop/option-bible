Greetings from Paul

1 Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, was called to be an apostle. He was set apart for the gospel of God. 2 God promised this gospel long ago through His prophets in the holy Scriptures. 3 The gospel is about His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord. As a man, He came from the family line of David. 4 But by the power of the Holy Spirit, He was shown to be the Son of God when He was raised from the dead. 5 Through Him, we received grace and the work of being apostles. We do this so people from all nations will obey God because they trust in Him. This brings honor to Jesus' name. 6 You are among those people, since you are called to belong to Jesus Christ. 7 To everyone in Rome who is loved by God and called to be His holy people: Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Paul's Prayer for Rome

8 First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is talked about all over the world. 9 God is my witness. I serve Him with my whole spirit by sharing the gospel about His Son. I never stop praying for you. 10 In my prayers, I always ask that, if it is God's will, I may finally be able to visit you. 11 I really want to see you so I can share a spiritual gift with you. It will make you stronger. 12 I mean that we can encourage each other by the faith we share—you and I. 13 Brothers and sisters, I want you to know that I have planned to come to you many times, but I have been stopped until now. I want to do some good work among you, just as I have done among other nations. 14 I owe a debt to Greeks and non-Greeks, and to wise people and foolish people. 15 That is why I am eager to preach the gospel to you in Rome too.

The Power of the Gospel

16 I am not ashamed of the gospel, because it is God's power to save everyone who believes. It is for the Jew first, and then for the Greek. 17 In the gospel, God shows His way of making people right with Him. It begins with faith and grows into more faith. The Bible says, "The righteous will live by faith."

God's Wrath Against Sin

18 God's anger is shown from heaven against every kind of godlessness and wickedness. People push down the truth by their evil. 19 What can be known about God is clear to them, because God has made it clear. 20 Since the world was made, God's invisible qualities—His lasting power and divine nature—have been easy to see. People can understand them through what God made. So no one has an excuse. 21 They knew God, but they did not honor Him as God or thank Him. Their thinking became useless, and their foolish hearts became dark. 22 They said they were wise, but they became fools. 23 They traded the glory of the God who lives forever for images that look like mortal people, birds, animals, and reptiles.

24 So God let them follow the sinful desires of their hearts. They became impure, and they dishonored their bodies with each other. 25 They traded God's truth for a lie. They worshiped and served created things instead of the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen. 26 Because of this, God let them have shameful desires. Even their women traded natural sexual relations for unnatural ones. 27 The men did the same. They stopped having natural relations with women and burned with desire for each other. Men did shameful things with other men. They received in themselves the punishment their wrong choices deserved.

28 Since they did not think it was important to know God, God let them have a mind that is no good. They do things that should not be done. 29 They are full of every kind of wrong: evil, greed, and meanness. They are full of envy, murder, fighting, trickery, and spite. They gossip 30 and slander. They hate God. They are rude, proud, and boastful. They invent new ways to do evil. They disobey their parents. 31 They are foolish, unfaithful, unloving, and without mercy. 32 They know God's rule: people who do these things deserve death. But they not only do them; they also approve of others who do them.
