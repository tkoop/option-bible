A Prayer for God to Judge the Wicked

For the choir director. To the tune “Do Not Destroy.” A miktam of David.

1 Do you really speak fairly, you powerful ones? Do you judge people fairly, you people? 2 No, in your hearts you plan evil, and your hands spread violence across the land.

3 Wicked people are crooked from birth; liars wander off track from the womb. 4 Their poison is like a snake's poison, like a deaf cobra that plugs its ears. 5 It will not listen to the snake charmer, no matter how skilled the enchanter is.

6 O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O the LORD, tear out the fangs of the young lions. 7 Let them disappear like water that runs away; when they shoot their arrows, let them lose their power. 8 Like a slug that melts as it crawls, like a baby born dead, may they never live to see the light of day.

9 Before your cooking pots can feel the heat of thorn bushes—green or dry—He will sweep them away. 10 Good people will be glad when they see justice done; they will celebrate victory over the wicked. 11 Then people will say, “Surely good people receive a reward; surely God judges the earth.”
