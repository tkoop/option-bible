Joshua Gathers Israel at Shechem

1 Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel at Shechem. He called for the elders, leaders, judges, and officers of Israel. They stood before God.

The LORD Remembers What He Did

2 Joshua said to all the people, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Long ago your ancestors lived beyond the river. Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor, served other gods. 3 But I took your father Abraham from the other side of the river. I led him through all the land of Canaan. I gave him many descendants, and I gave him Isaac. 4 To Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau. I gave Esau the hill country of Seir to own. Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt. 5 Then I sent Moses and Aaron. I brought terrible trouble on Egypt, just as I did among them. After that, I brought you out. 6 I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea. The Egyptians chased your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Red Sea. 7 Your fathers cried out to the LORD. He put darkness between you and the Egyptians. Then He brought the sea over them and covered them. Your eyes saw what I did in Egypt. After that, you lived in the wilderness for a long time. 8 I brought you into the land of the Amorites, who lived east of the Jordan. They fought against you, but I gave them into your hands. You took their land, and I destroyed them before you. 9 Then Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, got ready to fight Israel. He sent for Balaam son of Beor to curse you. 10 But I would not listen to Balaam. Instead, he blessed you, and I saved you from his hand. 11 You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho. The people of Jericho fought against you, along with the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites. But I gave them into your hands. 12 I sent hornets ahead of you. They drove out the two Amorite kings before you. You did not win that land with your swords or bows. 13 I gave you a land you did not work for. You live in cities you did not build. You eat from vineyards and olive groves you did not plant.'"

Choose Whom You Will Serve

14 "Now fear the LORD and serve Him with all your heart and with truth. Throw away the gods your ancestors served beyond the river and in Egypt. Serve the LORD. 15 If it seems wrong to you to serve the LORD, choose today whom you will serve. You may serve the gods your ancestors served beyond the river, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you live. But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."

The People Promise to Serve

16 The people answered, "We would never leave the LORD to serve other gods! 17 For the LORD our God brought us and our ancestors up from Egypt, out of the land of slavery. He did great and wonderful signs before our eyes. He kept us safe on the whole journey and among all the nations we passed through. 18 The LORD drove out all the nations before us, including the Amorites who lived in the land. So we will serve the LORD, because He is our God."

19 Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the LORD. He is a holy God. He is a jealous God. He will not forgive your rebellion and your sins. 20 If you leave the LORD and serve foreign gods, He will turn against you. He will bring harm to you and destroy you, even after He has been good to you."

21 The people said to Joshua, "No! We will serve the LORD."

22 Joshua said, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the LORD to serve Him."

They said, "We are witnesses."

23 Joshua said, "Now throw away the foreign gods among you. Turn your hearts to the LORD, the God of Israel."

24 The people said to Joshua, "We will serve the LORD our God, and we will obey His voice."

Joshua's Final Acts and Death

25 That day Joshua made a covenant with the people at Shechem. He set up a rule and a law for them. 26 Joshua wrote these words in the Book of the Law of God. Then he took a large stone and set it up there under the oak tree near the sanctuary of the LORD. 27 Joshua said to all the people, "Look! This stone will be a witness against us. It has heard all the words the LORD spoke to us. It will be a witness against you, so you do not deny your God."

28 Then Joshua sent the people away, each to his own inheritance.

29 After these things, Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at the age of one hundred ten. 30 They buried him in the border of his inheritance at Timnath Serah, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash. 31 Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who lived after Joshua. They knew all the work the LORD had done for Israel.

32 The bones of Joseph, which the Israelites had brought up from Egypt, were buried at Shechem. They were buried in the piece of land Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for one hundred pieces of silver. It became an inheritance for Joseph's children. 33 Eleazar son of Aaron died too. They buried him at Gibeah, which belonged to his son Phinehas, in the hill country of Ephraim.
