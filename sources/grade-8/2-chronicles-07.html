Fire and Glory Fill the Temple

1When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven and burned up the burnt offering and the sacrifices. The glory of the LORD filled the temple. 2The priests could not enter the LORD’s house because His glory filled it. 3When all the Israelites saw the fire come down and the glory of the LORD on the temple, they knelt with their faces to the ground on the pavement. They worshiped and thanked the LORD, saying, “He is good; His faithful love endures forever.”

Solomon and the People Offer Sacrifices

4Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the LORD. 5King Solomon sacrificed 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep. The king and all the people dedicated God’s house. 6The priests stood at their stations, and the Levites played the LORD’s musical instruments that King David had made to praise the LORD—“His faithful love endures forever”—when David led the praise through them. The priests blew trumpets across from them, and all Israel stood.

7Solomon set apart the middle of the courtyard in front of the LORD’s house. He offered burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings there, because the bronze altar Solomon had made could not hold the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the fat. 8At that time Solomon and all Israel with him celebrated the feast for seven days. The crowd was very large, coming from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt. 9On the eighth day they held a solemn assembly. They had dedicated the altar for seven days and celebrated the feast for seven days. 10On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, Solomon sent the people to their tents. They were joyful and glad in heart because of the good things the LORD had done for David, Solomon, and His people Israel. 11So Solomon finished the LORD’s house and the king’s house. He succeeded in everything he had planned to do in the LORD’s house and in his own house.

God Speaks to Solomon

12The LORD appeared to Solomon at night and said, “I have heard your prayer and chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice. 13If I shut the heavens so that no rain falls, or command locusts to devour the land, or send a plague among My people, 14and if My people who are called by My name humble themselves and pray and seek My face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven and forgive their sin and heal their land. 15Now My eyes will be open and My ears attentive to the prayer offered in this place. 16For I have chosen and set apart this house so that My name may be there forever. My eyes and My heart will be there for all time.

“17As for you, if you walk before Me as your father David walked, doing all I command you and keeping My statutes and rules, 18then I will establish your royal throne, just as I promised your father David: ‘You will never fail to have a man to rule over Israel.’ 19But if you turn away and forsake My statutes and commands that I have given you, and go serve and worship other gods, 20then I will uproot them from the land I gave them and reject this house I set apart for My name. I will make it a warning story that people mock among all nations. 21This temple, now so highly honored, will become a ruin. Everyone who passes by will be appalled and say, ‘Why has the LORD done this to this land and this temple?’ 22They will answer, ‘Because they forsook the LORD, the God of their fathers, who brought them out of Egypt, and clung to other gods, worshiping and serving them. That is why He brought all this disaster on them.’”
