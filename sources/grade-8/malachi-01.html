The LORD's Love for Israel

1 This is a message from the the LORD to Israel, brought by Malachi.

2 "I have loved you," says the the LORD.

But you ask, "How have you loved us?"

"Was not Esau Jacob's brother?" declares the the LORD. "Yet I have loved Jacob, 3 but Esau I have hated. I made his mountains a wasteland and left his inheritance to the jackals of the desert."

4 Edom may say, "We have been crushed, but we will rebuild the ruins."

This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: "They may build, but I will tear it down. People will call them the Wicked Land, a people the the LORD has cursed forever.

5 You will see it with your own eyes and say, "The The LORD is great, even beyond Israel's borders!"

The Priests Dishonor the LORD

6 "A son honors his father, and a servant his master. If I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am a master, where is My respect?" says the the LORD of Hosts. "It is you priests who dishonor My name. But you ask, 'How have we dishonored Your name?'"

Worthless Offerings on the Altar

7 "By offering polluted food on My altar. But you ask, 'How have we polluted You?' By saying that the the LORD's table is worthless."

8 "When you offer blind animals for sacrifice, is that not wrong? When you offer the lame or sick, is that not wrong? Try offering them to your governor. Would he be pleased with you? Would he accept you?" says the the LORD of Hosts.

9 "Now plead with God to be gracious to us. With such offerings from your hands, will He accept you?" says the the LORD of Hosts.

10 "If only one of you would shut the temple doors, so that you would not light useless fires on My altar! I am not pleased with you," says the the LORD of Hosts, "and I will accept no offering from your hands."

The LORD's Name Among the Nations

11 "My name will be great among the nations, from the rising of the sun to its setting. In every place incense and pure offerings will be brought to My name, because My name will be great among the nations," says the the LORD of Hosts.

12 "But you dishonor it when you say, 'The Lord's table is defiled, and its food is worthless.'"

13 "And you say, 'What a burden!' and you treat it with scorn," says the the LORD of Hosts. "When you bring injured, lame, or sick animals, should I accept them from your hands?" says the the LORD.

14 "Cursed is the cheat who has an acceptable male in his flock and vows to give it, but then sacrifices a blemished animal to the Lord. For I am a great King," says the the LORD of Hosts, "and My name is to be feared among the nations."
