God's Blessing and Praise from Every Nation

1 May God be gracious to us and bless us, and show us His favor. Selah. 2 May Your way be known on earth, Your salvation among all nations.

3 Let the peoples praise You, God; let all the peoples praise You. 4 Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for You judge the peoples fairly and guide the nations of the earth. Selah.

5 Let the peoples praise You, God; let all the peoples praise You. 6 The land has given its harvest; God, our God, blesses us. 7 God blesses us, so that all the ends of the earth may fear Him.
