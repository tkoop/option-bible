Warning to Unfair Leaders

1 Woe to those who make unfair laws and write harmful rules. 2 They keep the poor from getting justice and take rights from the needy among my people. They make widows their prize and rob orphans. 3 What will you do when punishment comes, when disaster comes from far away? Who will you run to for help? Where will you leave your wealth? 4 Nothing will be left except to crouch among prisoners or fall among the dead. Even then, His anger will not turn away; His hand will still be raised.

Assyria, God's Rod of Anger

5 Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger! The club in its hand is My wrath. 6 I send him against a godless nation. I order him to attack a people who make Me angry, to take loot and grab plunder, and to trample them down like mud in the streets. 7 But that is not what he plans, and that is not what he thinks. In his heart he plans to destroy and wipe out many nations. 8 For he says, "Are not all my commanders kings? 9 Is not Kalno like Carchemish? Is not Hamath like Arpad? Is not Samaria like Damascus? 10 Just as my hand seized the kingdoms of the idols, whose carved images were greater than those of Jerusalem and Samaria, 11 will I not do to Jerusalem and her idols what I did to Samaria and her images?"

God Will Judge Assyria's Pride

12 When the Lord has finished all His work on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will punish the king of Assyria for his proud heart and his proud eyes. 13 For he boasts, "By my own strength I did this. By my wisdom I have understanding. I moved the borders of nations. I plundered their treasures. Like a mighty man I brought down their kings. 14 My hand found the wealth of the nations like a nest. Like gathering abandoned eggs, I gathered all the countries. No one flapped a wing, opened its beak, or chirped." 15 Does an ax brag over the one who swings it? Does a saw act greater than the one who uses it? It is as if a rod could lift the one who holds it, or a club could wave the one who carries it! 16 So the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will send a wasting sickness among his strong warriors. Under his glory a fire will burn like a blazing flame. 17 The Light of Israel will become a fire, and their Holy One a flame. In one day it will burn and consume his thorns and briers. 18 It will completely destroy the glory of his forests and his fruitful fields, body and soul. It will be like a sick person wasting away. 19 The trees left in his forest will be so few that a child could count them.

A Remnant and Deliverance

20 In that day, the remnant of Israel and the survivors of Jacob's house will no longer depend on the one who struck them. Instead, they will truly depend on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel. 21 A remnant will return, a remnant of Jacob, to the Mighty God. 22 Though your people, Israel, are like the sand by the sea, only a remnant will return. Destruction has been decided, and it will be full of justice. 23 For the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will carry out the destruction that has been decided throughout the whole land. 24 Therefore this is what the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, says: "My people who live in Zion, do not be afraid of the Assyrians. They will beat you with a rod and lift a club against you, as Egypt did. 25 But in a very short time My wrath will end, and My anger will be turned to their destruction." 26 The LORD of Hosts will stir up a whip against them, as when He struck Midian at the rock of Oreb. He will raise His staff over the sea, as He did in Egypt. 27 In that day their burden will be lifted from your shoulders, and their yoke from your neck. The yoke will be broken because your neck has grown fat. 28 He has come to Aiath. He has passed through Migron. At Michmash he stores his supplies. 29 They have crossed over the pass. They camp at Geba. Ramah trembles. Gibeah of Saul runs away. 30 Cry out, O daughter of Gallim! Listen, O Laishah! Poor Anathoth! 31 Madmenah is in flight. The people of Gebim hide. 32 This very day he will stop at Nob. He will shake his fist at the mountain of Daughter Zion, at the hill of Jerusalem. 33 Look, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, will cut off the branches with terrifying power. The tall trees will be cut down, and the lofty ones will be brought low. 34 He will clear the thickets of the forest with an iron tool, and Lebanon will fall by the Mighty One.
