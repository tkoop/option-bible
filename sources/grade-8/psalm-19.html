God's Glory in the Sky

For the director of music. A psalm of David. 1 The heavens tell about God's glory, and the sky shows the work of His hands. 2 Day after day they pour out speech; night after night they show knowledge. 3 They have no speech and use no words. No sound is heard from them. 4 Yet their voice goes out through all the earth, and their words reach the ends of the world. In the heavens God has set a tent for the sun.

5 The sun is like a bridegroom coming out of his room. It runs its path with joy, like a strong man. 6 It rises from one end of the sky and travels to the other. Nothing can hide from its heat.

God's Perfect Law

7 The law of the the LORD is perfect. It gives new life to the soul. The the LORD's testimony is trustworthy. It makes the simple wise. 8 The the LORD's rules are right. They bring joy to the heart. The the LORD's command is pure. It gives light to the eyes. 9 Respect for the the LORD is pure. It lasts forever. The the LORD's decisions are true and right in every way.

10 They are more desirable than gold, even more than much pure gold. They are sweeter than honey, even sweeter than honey from the comb. 11 By them Your servant is warned. Keeping them brings a great reward.

A Prayer for Forgiveness

12 Who can notice his own mistakes? Clear me of hidden faults. 13 Keep Your servant from willful sins. Do not let them rule over me. Then I will be blameless and free from great sin. 14 May the words of my mouth and the thoughts of my heart be pleasing to You, O the LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer.
