Joseph Brings His Family to Pharaoh

1 Joseph went to Pharaoh and said, "My father and my brothers have come from Canaan with their sheep, cattle, and everything they own. They are now in Goshen." 2 He took five of his brothers and presented them to Pharaoh.

3 Pharaoh asked them, "What is your work?"

His brothers answered, "Your servants are shepherds, just as our fathers were." 4 They also said to Pharaoh, "We have come to live here for a while because there is no pasture for your servants' flocks. The famine is very severe in Canaan. Please let your servants live in Goshen."

5 Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Your father and your brothers have come to you. 6 The land of Egypt is before you. Settle your father and your brothers in the best land. Let them live in Goshen. If you know any capable men among them, put them in charge of my livestock."

Jacob Meets Pharaoh

7 Joseph brought his father Jacob and presented him to Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

8 Pharaoh asked Jacob, "How old are you?"

9 Jacob said to Pharaoh, "I have lived as a foreigner for one hundred thirty years. My years have been few and hard, and I have not lived as long as my fathers did."

10 Jacob blessed Pharaoh and left his presence.

11 Joseph settled his father and brothers in Egypt and gave them property in the best land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had ordered. 12 He also provided food for his father, his brothers, and all his father's household, according to the number of their children.

Joseph Manages the Famine

13 There was no food in all the land, because the famine was very severe. Egypt and Canaan were worn out by the famine.

14 Joseph collected all the money in Egypt and Canaan that people paid for grain, and he brought it to Pharaoh's palace.

15 When the money in Egypt and Canaan was gone, all the Egyptians came to Joseph and said, "Give us food! Why should we die in front of you? Our money is gone."

16 Joseph said, "Bring your livestock, and I will give you food in exchange for it, since your money is gone." 17 So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and he gave them food in exchange for horses, flocks, herds, and donkeys. He supported them with food that year in exchange for all their livestock.

18 When that year ended, they came to him the next year and said, "We will not hide from our master that our money is gone and our livestock belongs to our master. Nothing is left for our master except our bodies and our land." 19 "Why should we die before your eyes, both we and our land? Buy us and our land for food, and we and our land will be Pharaoh's servants. Give us seed so we may live and not die, and so the land will not become empty."

20 So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh, because every Egyptian sold his field when the famine became severe. The land became Pharaoh's. 21 He moved the people to the cities from one end of Egypt to the other. 22 He did not buy the priests' land, because the priests received a set allowance from Pharaoh and lived on it. That is why they did not sell their land.

23 Joseph said to the people, "Now that I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh, here is seed for you. Sow the land. 24 At harvest, give one-fifth to Pharaoh. The other four-fifths will be yours for seed for the fields, for food, for your households, and for your children."

25 They said, "You have saved our lives. Let us find favor in your eyes, and we will be Pharaoh's servants."

26 So Joseph made it a law over Egypt that lasts to this day: Pharaoh gets one-fifth. Only the priests' land did not belong to Pharaoh. 27 Israel settled in Egypt, in Goshen. They acquired property there, were very fruitful, and multiplied greatly. 28 Jacob lived in Egypt seventeen years, so he lived one hundred forty-seven years in all.

Jacob's Final Request

29 When the time came for Israel to die, he called his son Joseph and said, "If I have found favor with you, put your hand under my thigh and promise to deal kindly and truly with me: Do not bury me in Egypt.

30 When I die, carry me out of Egypt and bury me where my fathers are buried."

Joseph said, "I will do as you say."

31 "Swear to me," Jacob said. So Joseph swore to him, and Israel bowed at the head of his bed.
