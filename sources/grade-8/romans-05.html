Peace with God Through Faith

1 So, since we have been made right with God by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. 2 Through Him we have also gained access by faith into this grace where we now stand. We boast in the hope of God's glory. 3 Not only that, but we also boast in our troubles. We know that trouble produces patience. 4 Patience produces character, and character produces hope. 5 That hope will not disappoint us, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us.

God's Love Shown in Christ

6 While we were still weak, Christ died for ungodly people at just the right time. 7 It is rare for someone to die for a righteous person. Maybe someone would dare to die for a good person. 8 But God shows His love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us. 9 Since we have now been made right with God by His blood, we will be saved from God's anger through Him even more. 10 If we were brought back to God through the death of His Son while we were enemies, then we will be saved by His life even more, now that we are brought back. 11 Not only that, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through Him we have now been brought back to God.

Sin and Death Through Adam

12 So, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, death spread to all people because all sinned. 13 Sin was in the world before the law was given, but sin is not counted when there is no law. 14 Yet death ruled from Adam to Moses, even over people who did not sin in the same way Adam did. Adam is a pattern of the One who was to come.

Grace and Life Through Christ

15 But God's free gift is not like Adam's sin. If many died because of one man's sin, then God's grace and the gift that come through the grace of one man, Jesus Christ, overflowed to many people even more. 16 The gift is not like the result of one man's sin. One sin brought judgment and punishment, but the free gift brings right standing with God after many sins. 17 If death ruled because of one man's sin, then those who receive God's rich grace and the gift of being made right with Him will rule in life through one man, Jesus Christ, even more.

18 So, just as one sin brought punishment to all people, one act of doing right brings life and being made right with God to all people. 19 Just as one man's disobedience made many people sinners, one man's obedience will make many people right with God. 20 The law was added so that sin would increase. But where sin increased, grace increased even more. 21 Sin once ruled in death. Now grace rules through being made right with God, leading to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
