David Trusts God for Justice

1 A song of David for the the LORD, about Cush, a man from Benjamin. The LORD, my God, I have run to You for safety. Save me from all who chase me, and rescue me. 2 Otherwise, they will tear me apart like a lion, ripping me to pieces with no one to save me.

3 The LORD, my God, if I have done this, if my hands are guilty of wrong, 4 if I have repaid my friend with evil or robbed my enemy for no reason, 5 then let my enemy chase me and catch me. Let him trample my life into the ground and throw my honor in the dust. Selah

6 The LORD, rise up in Your anger. Stand against the fury of those who attack me. Wake up, my God, and bring justice. 7 Let the nations gather around You, and take Your place above them. 8 The LORD judges the peoples. The LORD, judge me by my goodness and honesty. 9 End the evil of the wicked, and strengthen the righteous. You are the God who tests hearts and minds.

10 My shield is with God, who saves those with pure hearts. 11 God is a fair judge, and He is angry with the wicked every day. 12 If a person does not turn back, God will sharpen His sword. He has bent His bow and made it ready.

13 He has prepared deadly weapons. He makes His arrows burn. 14 Look, the wicked person is full of trouble. He plans evil and gives birth to lies. 15 He dug a pit and made it deep, but he fell into the hole he made. 16 His trouble will come back on his own head. His violence will fall on his own head. 17 I will thank the the LORD for His fairness. I will sing praise to the name of the the LORD, the Most High.
