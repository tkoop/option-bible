Younger Men Mock Job

1 But now men younger than I laugh at me—men whose fathers I would not even put with the dogs that guard my sheep. 2 What good is their strength to me? Their power is gone. 3 Thin from want and hunger, they chew on dry ground in the empty wasteland at night. 4 They pick mallow among the bushes, and broom tree roots are their food. 5 People drive them away and shout at them as if they were thieves. 6 They have to live in dry riverbeds, in holes in the earth and rocks. 7 They bray like donkeys among the bushes and huddle under thorn plants. 8 Sons of fools and worthless men, they were driven from the land.

Job Is Treated Like an Enemy

9 Now I am the subject of their songs; I am a joke to them. 10 They hate me and stay away, but they do not hold back from spitting in my face. 11 Because God has unstrung my bow and afflicted me, they have thrown off all restraint before me. 12 On my right the mob rises up; they set traps for my feet and build siege ramps against me. 13 They block my path; they add to my ruin, and no one stops them. 14 They come at me like an army through a broken wall; they roll in through the ruins. 15 Terrors turn against me; my honor is blown away like the wind, and my well-being has passed like a cloud.

Job's Pain Deepens

16 Now my life drains out of me; days of suffering have taken hold of me. 17 At night pain pierces my bones; my aching never stops. 18 By His great power He grips my clothing; He chokes me by the collar of my shirt. 19 He has thrown me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.

Job Cries Out to God

20 I cry out to You, but You do not answer; I stand up, and You merely look at me. 21 You have turned cruel to me; with the strength of Your hand You persecute me. 22 You lift me up into the wind and make me ride it; You dissolve me in the storm. 23 Yes, I know You will bring me down to death, to the place where all living things go. 24 Yet when someone falls into ruins, doesn't he reach out his hand? Or in disaster, doesn't he cry for help? 25 Haven't I wept for those in trouble? Hasn't my heart grieved for the poor? 26 But when I hoped for good, evil came; when I looked for light, darkness came. 27 My stomach churns and never stops; days of suffering face me. 28 I walk around blackened, but not by the sun. I stand up in the crowd and cry for help. 29 I have become like a brother to jackals and a friend to owls. 30 My skin grows black and peels; my bones burn with fever. 31 My harp is tuned to mourning, and my flute to the sound of weeping.
