Jeremiah Asks Why the Wicked Prosper

1 You are righteous, the LORD, even though I bring my case to You. Still, I want to talk with You about Your justice. Why does the way of the wicked succeed? Why are all traitors so comfortable? 2 You planted them, and they took root. They grow and bear fruit. You are always on their lips, but far from their hearts. 3 But You know me, the LORD. You see me and test my heart for You. Drag them away like sheep to be killed, and set them apart for the day of slaughter. 4 How long will the land mourn and the grass in every field dry up? The evil of the people who live there has wiped out the animals and birds, because they said, "He will not see how things end for us."

The LORD Answers Jeremiah

5 "If you have raced with men on foot and they have worn you out, how can you compete with horses? If you stumble in a peaceful land, what will you do in the thick woods by the Jordan? 6 For even your brothers and your father's house have betrayed you; even they have called out against you. Do not trust them, though they speak well of you."

The LORD Mourns His Land

7 "I have forsaken My house; I have abandoned My inheritance. I have given the one I love into the hands of her enemies. 8 My inheritance has become to Me like a lion in the forest. She has roared against Me; therefore I have hated her. 9 Is My inheritance to Me like a speckled bird of prey? Birds of prey surround her. Come, gather all the wild animals; bring them to devour her.

10 "Many shepherds have destroyed My vineyard; they have trampled My plot of ground. They have turned My pleasant field into a desolate wasteland. 11 They have made it a desolation; desolate, it mourns before Me. The whole land is desolate because there is no one who cares. 12 Over all the bare hilltops in the desert, destroyers have come, for the sword of the LORD devours from one end of the earth to the other. There is no peace for all mankind. 13 They have sown wheat but reaped thorns; they have worn themselves out but gained nothing. So be ashamed of your harvest because of the fierce anger of the LORD."

The LORD Promises Mercy to the Nations

14 "This is what the LORD says: As for all My wicked neighbors who touch the inheritance I have given My people Israel, I will uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them. 15 But after I have uprooted them, I will return and have compassion on them and bring them back, each to his inheritance and each to his land.

16 "And if they will diligently learn the ways of My people and swear by My name, 'As surely as the LORD lives'—as they taught My people to swear by Baal—then they will be built up among My people. 17 But if they will not obey, then I will uproot that nation and destroy it," declares the LORD.
