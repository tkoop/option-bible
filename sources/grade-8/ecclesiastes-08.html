Wisdom and Obedience

1Who is like a wise person? Who knows how to explain things? Wisdom lights up a person's face and softens a hard look. 2I say, keep the king's command because of your oath to God. 3Don't be in a hurry to leave his presence, and don't join in an evil plan, because he does whatever he wants. 4Since the king's word has power, who can ask him, "What are you doing?" 5Whoever obeys a command avoids trouble. A wise heart knows the right time and the right way to act. 6There is a time and a way for everything, even when trouble weighs heavy on a person. 7No one knows what will happen next, and no one can tell him what is coming. 8No one can control the wind, and no one can control the day of death. No one is released during war, and evil won't save the person who does it. 9I saw all of this and thought about everything done under the sun. Sometimes one person rules over another and brings harm.

Justice and Frustration

10I also saw wicked people buried. They used to come and go from the holy place, and then they were forgotten in the city where they did those things. This too is meaningless. 11When a sentence against a bad act isn't carried out quickly, people's hearts are set on doing wrong. 12Even though a sinner does wrong a hundred times and lives a long time, I know it will go well for those who fear God, because they fear Him. 13But it won't go well for the wicked. He won't live long, like a shadow that passes quickly, because he doesn't fear God. 14Here is another meaningless thing that happens on earth: good people sometimes get what the wicked deserve, and wicked people sometimes get what the good deserve. I say this too is meaningless.

Enjoying Life

15So I praised joy. A person has nothing better under the sun than to eat, drink, and be happy. That joy stays with him in his work during the life God gives him under the sun. 16When I set my heart to know wisdom and to see the work done on earth—even though a person's eyes get no sleep day or night— 17I saw all the work of God. No person can understand the work done under the sun. Even if someone works hard to find it out, he won't. Even if a wise person says he knows, he can't.
