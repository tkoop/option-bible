A Prayer for Help

1 How long, O the LORD, will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me? 2 How long must I worry and feel sad in my heart all day? How long will my enemy have power over me?

3 Look at me and answer me, O the LORD my God. Give me strength, or I will die. 4 Then my enemy would say, “I have beaten him,” and my foes would be glad because I am shaken.

5 But I trust in your unfailing love; my heart rejoices in your salvation. 6 I will sing to the LORD, for He has been good to me.
