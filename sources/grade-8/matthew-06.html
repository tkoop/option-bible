Giving, Prayer, and Fasting

1 Be careful not to do your good works in front of others just to show off. If you do, your Father in heaven won't reward you. 2 So when you give to the needy, don't make a big show of it, like hypocrites do in the synagogues and streets, to get praise from people. I tell you, they already have their reward. 3 But when you give to the needy, keep it a secret. 4 Give in secret, and your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

5 And when you pray, don't be like the hypocrites. They love to stand and pray in the synagogues and on street corners so people will see them. I tell you, they already have their reward. 6 But when you pray, go into your room, close the door, and pray to your Father, who is unseen. Then your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you. 7 And when you pray, don't keep repeating empty words like the pagans do. They think they'll be heard because they talk a lot. 8 Don't be like them. your Father knows what you need before you ask Him.

9 So pray like this: "Our Father in heaven, may Your name be kept holy. 10 May Your kingdom come. May Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven. 11 Give us today our daily bread. 12 Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us. 13 And keep us from temptation, but rescue us from the evil one."

14 If you forgive others for their wrongs, your heavenly Father will forgive you too. 15 But if you don't forgive others, your Father won't forgive your wrongs either.

16 When you fast, don't look sad like the hypocrites. They make their faces look worse so people will know they're fasting. I tell you, they already have their reward. 17 But when you fast, wash your face and comb your hair. 18 Then your fasting won't be noticed by people, but by your Father, who is unseen. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

Treasures and True Focus

19 Don't store up treasures for yourselves on earth, where moths and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal. 20 Instead, store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where moths and rust don't destroy, and thieves don't break in and steal. 21 Because where your treasure is, there your heart will be too.

22 The eye is the lamp of the body. So if your eye is healthy, your whole body will be full of light. 23 But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If the light inside you is really darkness, how deep that darkness is!

24 No one can serve two masters. Either you will hate one and love the other, or you will be loyal to one and look down on the other. You can't serve both God and money.

Do Not Worry

25 So I tell you, don't worry about your life—what you will eat or drink—or about your body—what you will wear. Isn't life more than food, and the body more than clothes? 26 Look at the birds in the sky. They don't plant or harvest or store food in barns, but your heavenly Father feeds them. Aren't you worth much more than they are? 27 Can any of you add a single hour to your life by worrying?

28 And why do you worry about clothes? Look at how the wildflowers grow. They don't work or make clothes. 29 Yet I tell you that even Solomon in all his glory wasn't dressed as beautifully as one of these. 30 If God clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and gone tomorrow, won't He clothe you too? You have so little faith!

31 So don't worry and say, "What will we eat?" or "What will we drink?" or "What will we wear?"

32 The people who don't know God chase after all these things, but your heavenly Father knows you need them. 33 But seek first God's kingdom and His right way of living, and all these things will be given to you too. 34 So don't worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will have its own worries. Today's trouble is enough for today.
