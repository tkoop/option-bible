Elisha Warns the Shunammite Woman to Leave

1 Elisha had told the woman whose son he brought back to life, "Get up and go with your family. Stay wherever you can, because the LORD has called for a famine. It will come on the land for seven years." 2 So the woman got up and did what the man of God said. She went with her family and stayed in the land of the Philistines for seven years. 3 After seven years, the woman came back from the land of the Philistines and went to the king to ask for her house and her field.

The King Restores the Woman's Property

4 The king was talking to Gehazi, the servant of the man of God. He said, "Please tell me all the great things Elisha has done."

5 While Gehazi was telling the king how Elisha brought a dead man back to life, the woman whose son Elisha brought back to life came to the king and cried out for her house and her field. Gehazi said, "My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha brought back to life."

6 The king asked the woman, and she told him what had happened. Then the king told one of his officials, "Give back everything that belonged to her, and all the crops from her field from the day she left the land until now."

Elisha Foretells Hazael's Rise to Power

7 Elisha came to Damascus while Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, was sick. Someone told him, "The man of God has come here."

8 The king said to Hazael, "Take a gift with you and go meet the man of God. Ask the LORD through him if I will recover from this illness."

9 So Hazael went to meet Elisha. He took a gift with him: all the good things of Damascus, carried by forty camels. He came and stood before Elisha and said, "Your son Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, has sent me to ask if he will recover from this illness."

10 Elisha said to him, "Go and tell him, 'You will surely recover.' But the LORD has shown me that he will surely die."

11 Elisha stared at him until Hazael felt uncomfortable, and then the man of God wept.

12 Hazael asked, "Why is my lord weeping?"

Elisha answered, "Because I know the harm you will do to the people of Israel. You will set their forts on fire, kill their young men with the sword, smash their little ones, and rip open their pregnant women."

13 Hazael said, "What is your servant, a dog, that he should do such a big thing?"

Elisha answered, "The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Aram."

14 So Hazael left Elisha and went to his master. Ben-Hadad asked, "What did Elisha say to you?"

Hazael answered, "He told me that you would surely recover."

15 The next day, Hazael took a thick cloth, dipped it in water, and spread it over Ben-Hadad's face, so that he died. Then Hazael became king in his place.

New Kings Rule Judah

16 In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat became king of Judah. Jehoshaphat was king of Judah at that time. 17 He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled eight years in Jerusalem. 18 He followed the ways of the kings of Israel, as Ahab's family had done, because he married Ahab's daughter. He did what was evil in the LORD's sight.

19 But the LORD did not want to destroy Judah, because of David His servant. He had promised to give David and his descendants a lamp forever.

20 During his rule, Edom broke free from Judah's control and chose a king for themselves. 21 Joram went to Zair with all his chariots. He rose up at night and attacked the Edomites who had surrounded him and the chariot commanders. The Edomites fled to their tents. 22 So Edom has been free from Judah's control to this day. At that same time, Libnah also broke free.

23 The rest of Joram's acts, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. 24 Joram died and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. His son Ahaziah became king in his place.

25 In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah. 26 Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled one year in Jerusalem. His mother was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri, king of Israel. 27 He followed the ways of Ahab's family and did what was evil in the LORD's sight, just as Ahab's family had done, because he was married into Ahab's family.

28 He went with Joram son of Ahab to fight against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-Gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram. 29 So King Joram went back to Jezreel to recover from the wounds the Arameans gave him at Ramah while he fought Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Joram son of Ahab, because Joram was sick.
