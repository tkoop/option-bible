Love One Another and Live Purely

1 Keep loving one another as brothers and sisters. 2 Do not forget to show hospitality to strangers, because some people have welcomed angels without knowing it. 3 Remember people in prison as if you were locked up with them. Remember those who are mistreated, because you also have bodies that can suffer.

4 Marriage should be honored by everyone, and the marriage bed kept pure, because God will judge those who are sexually immoral and those who commit adultery.

5 Keep your lives free from the love of money, and be content with what you have, because God has said, "I will never leave you or abandon you."

6 So we can say with confidence, "The Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid. What can anyone do to me?"

Trust Jesus Christ, Who Never Changes

7 Remember your leaders, who spoke God's word to you. Think about how their lives ended, and copy their faith. 8 Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. 9 Do not be carried away by all kinds of strange teachings. It is good for your heart to be strengthened by grace, not by rules about food, which have not helped those who follow them.

Our Altar and Going Outside the Camp

10 We have an altar, and those who serve in the tabernacle have no right to eat from it. 11 The high priest carries the blood of animals into the holy place to pay for sin, but the animals' bodies are burned outside the camp.

12 So Jesus also suffered outside the city gate to make the people holy through His own blood. 13 So let us go to Him outside the camp and share the shame He suffered. 14 For we do not have a lasting city here. Instead, we look for the city that is coming.

15 Through Jesus, then, let us always offer God a sacrifice of praise. That means words from our lips that honor His name. 16 Do not forget to do good and to share with others, because God is pleased with sacrifices like that.

Final Requests and Greetings

17 Obey your leaders and submit to them, because they watch over your souls and will have to answer to God. Let them do this work with joy, not with groaning, because that would not help you.

18 Pray for us, because we are sure we have a clear conscience. We want to live honorably in every way. 19 I especially urge you to pray this so I can be restored to you sooner.

20 Now may the God of peace, who raised our Lord Jesus from the dead—the great Shepherd of the sheep—through the blood of the eternal covenant, 21 equip you with everything good so you can do His will. May He work in us what pleases Him, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.

22 Brothers and sisters, I beg you to listen patiently to this word of encouragement, for I have written to you only briefly. 23 You should know that our brother Timothy has been released. If he arrives soon, I will come with him to see you.

24 Greet all your leaders and all the saints. Those from Italy send their greetings to you. 25 Grace be with all of you.
