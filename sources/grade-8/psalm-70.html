A Prayer for Quick Help

1 Hurry, God, to save me! Come quickly, the LORD, to help me. 2 May those who seek my life be ashamed and confused. May those who want to harm me be turned back in disgrace. 3 May those who say, "Aha! Aha!" turn back in shame.

4 But may all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You. May those who love Your salvation always say, "Let God be praised!" 5 But I am poor and needy; hurry to me, God. You are my help and my rescuer; the LORD, do not delay.
