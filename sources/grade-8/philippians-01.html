1 Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus, to all God's holy people in Christ Jesus at Philippi, along with the overseers and deacons: 2 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Thanksgiving and Prayer

3 I thank my God every time I remember you. 4 In every prayer for all of you, I always pray with joy, 5 because of your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now. 6 I am confident of this: He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus. 7 It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, because I have you in my heart. In my chains and in my defense and confirmation of the gospel, all of you share in God's grace with me. 8 God is my witness that I long for all of you with the affection of Christ Jesus.

9 And this is my prayer: that your love may grow more and more in knowledge and deep understanding, 10 so that you may know what is best and may be pure and blameless until the day of Christ, 11 filled with the fruit of righteousness that comes through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.

Paul's Chains Advance the Gospel

12 Now I want you to know, brothers, that what has happened to me has really helped the gospel move forward. 13 As a result, it has become clear to the whole palace guard and to everyone else that I am in chains for Christ. 14 Because of my chains, most of the brothers in the Lord have become more confident and dare to speak God's word without fear.

15 It is true that some preach Christ out of envy and rivalry, but others do it out of goodwill. 16 Those who act in love know that I am here to defend the gospel. 17 The others preach Christ out of selfish ambition, not sincerely. They think they can make my chains harder to bear. 18 But what does it matter? The important thing is that Christ is preached in every way, whether from false motives or true. I rejoice in that, and I will keep rejoicing.

19 I know that through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, this will lead to my salvation. 20 I eagerly expect and hope that I will not be ashamed in any way. Instead, I will have complete boldness, so that now as always Christ will be honored in my body, whether by life or by death.

To Live Is Christ

21 For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain. 22 If I go on living in the body, that means fruitful work for me. Yet I do not know which to choose. 23 I am torn between the two. I desire to leave this life and be with Christ, which is far better. 24 But it is more necessary for you that I remain in the body. 25 Convinced of this, I know that I will remain and continue with all of you for your progress and joy in the faith, 26 so that through my coming to you again, your pride in Christ Jesus will grow because of me.

Living Worthy of the Gospel

27 Whatever happens, live in a way worthy of the gospel of Christ. Then, whether I come and see you or only hear about you while I am away, I will know that you stand firm in one spirit, working side by side for the faith of the gospel. 28 Do not be frightened by those who oppose you. This is a sign to them that they will be destroyed, but that you will be saved—and that by God. 29 For it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer for Him. 30 Since you are going through the same struggle you saw I had, and now hear that I still have.
