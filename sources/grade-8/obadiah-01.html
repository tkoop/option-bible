God's Message About Edom

1The vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord, the LORD, says about Edom: We have heard a message from the LORD. A messenger has been sent to the nations, saying, "Get up! Let's rise up and fight against her!"

Edom's Coming Judgment

2Look, I will make you small among the nations. You will be very despised. 3Your pride has tricked you. You live in the cracks of the rock and make your home high up. You say in your heart, "Who can bring me down to the ground?" 4Even if you fly as high as an eagle, even if you make your nest among the stars, I will bring you down from there, declares the LORD.

5If thieves came to you, or robbers at night—how ruined you would be!—wouldn't they steal only what they needed? If grape pickers came, wouldn't they leave a few grapes behind? 6But Esau will be searched out. His hidden treasures will be found. 7All your allies will push you to the border. The men who were at peace with you will trick you and overpower you. Those who ate your bread will set a trap beneath you. He has no understanding.

8On that day, declares the LORD, won't I destroy the wise men from Edom and understanding from Mount Esau? 9Your mighty men, Teman, will be terrified. Every man from Mount Esau will be cut off in the slaughter. 10Because of the violence you did to your brother Jacob, shame will cover you. You will be cut off forever.

11On the day you stood aside, when strangers carried off his wealth and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots for Jerusalem, you were like one of them. 12Do not gloat over your brother on the day of his disaster. Do not rejoice over the people of Judah on the day they are destroyed. Do not boast on the day of trouble. 13Do not enter the gate of My people on the day of their disaster. Do not gloat over their trouble on the day of their disaster. Do not take their wealth on the day of their disaster. 14Do not stand at the crossroads to cut off those who escape. Do not hand over those who survive on the day of trouble.

15For the day of the LORD is near for all nations. As you have done, it will be done to you. Your deeds will return on your own head. 16Just as you drank on My holy mountain, so all the nations will drink without stopping. They will drink and swallow and be as if they had never existed.

Israel's Future Hope

17But on Mount Zion there will be those who escape, and it will be holy. The house of Jacob will take back its own land. 18The house of Jacob will be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame. But the house of Esau will be like straw. They will set it on fire and burn it up. No one will be left from the house of Esau, for the LORD has spoken. 19Those from the Negev will take over Mount Esau. Those from the lowlands will take over Philistia. They will take over the fields of Ephraim and Samaria. Benjamin will take over Gilead. 20The people of Israel who were carried away will take over the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath. The people of Jerusalem who were taken to Sepharad will take over the cities of the Negev. 21Rescuers will go up to Mount Zion to rule Mount Esau. And the kingdom will belong to the LORD.
