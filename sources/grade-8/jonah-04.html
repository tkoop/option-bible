Jonah Is Angry at God's Mercy

1 But Jonah was very upset about this, and he became angry.

2 He prayed to the the LORD and said, "O the LORD, wasn't this what I said while I was still in my own country? That's why I ran away to Tarshish first. I knew that You are a kind and caring God. You are slow to anger and full of faithful love, and You are willing to hold back from sending disaster. 3 And now, O the LORD, please take my life, because it is better for me to die than to live."

4 The the LORD replied, "Do you have a good reason to be angry?"

Jonah Waits Outside the City

5 Then Jonah left the city and sat down east of it. He made himself a shelter and sat in its shade, waiting to see what would happen to the city. 6 The the LORD God made a plant grow up over Jonah. It gave shade over his head and saved him from his discomfort. Jonah was very happy about the plant.

7 But at dawn the next day, God sent a worm. It attacked the plant, and the plant dried up. 8 When the sun came up, God sent a hot east wind. The sun beat down on Jonah's head, and he became faint. He wished he could die and said, "It is better for me to die than to live."

God's Lesson About Mercy

9 Then God said to Jonah, "Do you have a good reason to be angry about the plant?"

Jonah answered, "Yes, I do. I am angry enough to die!"

10 The the LORD said, "You have had pity on the plant. You did not work for it or make it grow. It came up in one night and died in one night. 11 Should I not have pity on Nineveh, that great city? More than 120,000 people live there. They do not know their right hand from their left. Many animals live there too."
