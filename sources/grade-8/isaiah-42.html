The LORD’s Servant Will Bring Justice

1 Here is My Servant, whom I uphold. He is My Chosen One, and I delight in Him. I have put My Spirit on Him, and He will bring justice to the nations. 2 He will not shout or raise His voice. He will not make His voice heard in the streets. 3 He will not break a bruised reed, and He will not put out a smoldering wick. He will bring justice in a faithful way. 4 He will not grow weak or discouraged until He has set justice in the earth. The distant lands will put their hope in His law.

The LORD’s Promise to His Servant

5 This is what God the LORD says—He who made the heavens and stretched them out, who spread out the earth and all that grows from it, who gives breath to the people on it and spirit to those who walk on it:

6 “I, the LORD, have called You in righteousness. I will take hold of Your hand. I will keep You and make You a covenant for the people, a light for the nations, 7 to open the eyes of the blind, to bring prisoners out of the dungeon, and to bring out of prison those who sit in darkness. 8 I am the LORD; that is My name. I will not give My glory to another, or My praise to idols. 9 See, the former things have come to pass, and now I declare new things. Before they spring up, I tell you about them.”

Praise the LORD from the Ends of the Earth

10 Sing to the LORD a new song—His praise from the ends of the earth—you who go down to the sea and all that is in it, you islands and the people who live on them. 11 Let the desert and its towns raise their voices. Let the villages where Kedar lives be glad. Let the people of Sela sing for joy. Let them shout from the tops of the mountains. 12 Let them give glory to the LORD and announce His praise in the distant lands. 13 The LORD will go out like a mighty man. He will stir up His zeal like a warrior. He will shout—yes, He will roar—and He will win against His enemies.

14 “I have been silent for a long time. I have kept still and held Myself back. But now I will cry out like a woman giving birth. I will gasp and pant. 15 I will destroy mountains and hills. I will dry up all their plants. I will turn rivers into islands. I will dry up the pools. 16 I will lead blind people along a road they do not know. I will guide them on paths they have not known. I will turn darkness into light before them. I will make rough places smooth. I will do these things for them, and I will not leave them.”

Israel’s Blindness and Judgment

17 But they will be turned back and put to shame—those who trust in idols, who say to metal statues, “You are our gods.” 18 Hear, you deaf people! Look, you blind people, so that you may see! 19 Who is blind but My servant, or deaf like the messenger I send? Who is blind like the one fully committed, or blind like the servant of the LORD? 20 You have seen many things, but you do not notice them. Your ears are open, but you do not hear.

21 The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness, to make His law great and glorious. 22 But this is a people robbed and plundered, trapped in holes and hidden in prisons. They became plunder with no one to rescue them, and loot with no one to say, “Give it back!” 23 Who among you will pay attention to this? Who will listen and hear for the time to come? 24 Who gave Jacob up to the robber, and Israel to the plunderers? Was it not the LORD, against whom we have sinned? They would not follow His ways, and they did not obey His law. 25 So He poured out on them His burning anger, the fury of war. It surrounded them in flames, but they did not know it. It burned among them, but they did not take it to heart.
