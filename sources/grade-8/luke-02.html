Jesus Is Born in Bethlehem

1 In those days, Caesar Augustus sent out an order that everyone in the empire should be counted in a census. 2 This was the first census taken while Quirinius was governor of Syria. 3 Everyone went to their own town to be registered.

4 Joseph went up from Nazareth in Galilee to Judea, to Bethlehem, the city of David, because he belonged to the family line of David. 5 He went there to register with Mary, who was engaged to him and was expecting a baby. 6 While they were there, the time came for her baby to be born. 7 She gave birth to her firstborn Son, wrapped Him in strips of cloth, and laid Him in a manger, because there was no room for them in the guest room.

Shepherds Hear About Jesus

8 In that same area, shepherds were staying out in the fields and watching over their sheep at night. 9 An angel of the Lord appeared to them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them. They were terrified.

10 The angel said to them, "Do not be afraid. I bring you good news that will bring great joy to all people. 11 Today in the city of David a Savior has been born for you. He is Christ the Lord. 12 This will be a sign for you: You will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth and lying in a manger."

13 Suddenly a great crowd of angels from heaven appeared with the angel. They were praising God and saying, 14 "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and peace on earth to those He favors."

15 When the angels had gone back into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, "Let's go to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has told us about." 16 They hurried and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger. 17 When they saw Him, they told everyone what they had been told about this child. 18 Everyone who heard it was amazed at what the shepherds said.

19 But Mary kept all these things in her heart and thought about them often. 20 The shepherds went back, praising and glorifying God for everything they had heard and seen, just as they had been told.

Jesus Is Named and Presented

21 Eight days later, when it was time to circumcise Him, He was named Jesus. This was the name the angel had given before Mary became pregnant. 22 When the time came for them to be purified, as the Law of Moses required, they took Him to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord.

23 This followed what is written in the Lord's Law: "Every firstborn male must be set apart as holy to the Lord." 24 They also offered a sacrifice, as the Lord's Law says: "a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons."

Simeon and Anna Recognize Jesus

25 In Jerusalem there was a man named Simeon. He was good and faithful, and he was waiting for God to comfort Israel. The Holy Spirit was on him. 26 The Holy Spirit had told him that he would not die before he saw the Lord's Messiah. 27 Led by the Spirit, he went into the temple. When Jesus' parents brought the child in to do what the Law required for Him,

28 Simeon took Him in his arms, praised God, and said, 29 "Lord, now you can let Your servant go in peace, as You promised. 30 For my eyes have seen Your salvation, 31 which You have prepared for all people to see. 32 He will be a light to show the way to the nations and bring glory to Your people Israel."

33 Jesus' parents were amazed at what was said about Him. 34 Then Simeon blessed them and said to Mary, His mother, "This child is meant to cause many in Israel to fall and to rise. He will be a sign that people speak against. 35 A sword will pierce your own soul too, so that the secret thoughts of many hearts will be brought into the open."

36 There was also a prophet named Anna. She was the daughter of Phanuel from the tribe of Asher. She was very old. After she married, she lived with her husband seven years. 37 Then she was a widow until she was eighty-four. She never left the temple. She worshiped God day and night with fasting and prayer. 38 At that very moment, she came up and began to thank God. She spoke about Jesus to everyone who was waiting for God to rescue Jerusalem.

39 When Joseph and Mary had finished everything the Lord's Law required, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth. 40 The child grew and became strong. He was filled with wisdom, and God's grace was on Him.

Jesus as a Boy in the Temple

41 Every year Jesus' parents went to Jerusalem for the Passover Feast. 42 When He was twelve years old, they went up to the feast as usual. 43 After the feast days were over and they started home, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. His parents did not know it.

44 Thinking He was with the group, they traveled for a whole day. Then they began to look for Him among their relatives and friends. 45 When they could not find Him, they went back to Jerusalem to search for Him. 46 Three days later they found Him in the temple. He was sitting with the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions. 47 Everyone who heard Him was amazed at His understanding and His answers.

48 When His parents saw Him, they were astonished. His mother said to Him, "Child, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I have been searching for You in great pain."

49 He said to them, "Why were you looking for Me? Didn't you know that I must be in My Father's house?" 50 But they did not understand what He said to them. 51 Then He went down with them to Nazareth and obeyed them. His mother kept all these things in her heart. 52 And Jesus grew in wisdom and age and in favor with God and people.
