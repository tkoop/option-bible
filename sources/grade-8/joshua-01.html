The LORD Calls Joshua

1 After Moses, the servant of the LORD, died, the LORD said to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant: 2 "Moses My servant is dead. Now get up, you and all the people, and cross this Jordan River into the land I am giving to the Israelites. 3 I have given you every place you set foot, just as I promised Moses. 4 Your territory will run from the desert and Lebanon to the great Euphrates River. It will include all the land of the Hittites and reach west to the Great Sea. 5 No one will be able to stand against you as long as you live. I will be with you, just as I was with Moses. I will not leave you or abandon you."

6 "Be strong and brave, because you will lead these people to take the land I swore to their ancestors I would give them. 7 Only be very strong and brave. Obey all the law that Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn away from it to the right or the left, so you will succeed wherever you go. 8 Keep this Book of the Law with you. Read it day and night so you can obey everything written in it. Then you will be successful and do well. 9 I have commanded you to be strong and brave. Do not be afraid or discouraged, because the LORD your God is with you wherever you go."

Joshua Commands the People

10 Then Joshua commanded the people's leaders: 11 "Go through the camp and tell the people, 'Get your food ready, because in three days you will cross this Jordan River. You will go in and take the land the LORD your God is giving you to own.'"

The Eastern Tribes Promise Support

12 Then Joshua spoke to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh: 13 "Remember what Moses, the servant of the LORD, commanded you. He said that the LORD your God is giving you rest and will give you this land. 14 Your wives, children, and animals may stay in the land Moses gave you east of the Jordan. But all your fighting men must cross over, armed, ahead of your brothers. You must help them. 15 Keep helping them until the LORD gives your brothers rest, just as He gave you. They will take the land the LORD your God is giving them. Then you can return to your own land east of the Jordan and take it. That is the land Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you."

The People Promise to Obey

16 The people answered Joshua, "We will do everything you command us. We will go wherever you send us. 17 We obeyed Moses in everything, and we will obey you in the same way. May the LORD your God be with you, just as He was with Moses. 18 Anyone who rebels against your commands and does not obey everything you tell him will be put to death. Just be strong and brave."
