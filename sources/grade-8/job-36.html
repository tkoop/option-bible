Elihu Urges Job to Listen

1 Elihu also continued and said:

2 “Give me a little time, and I will show you. I still have more to say for God. 3 I will bring knowledge from far away and show that my Maker is right. 4 My words are truly not false. Someone who is fully wise is with you.

God’s Power and Justice

5 “Look, God is powerful, but He does not look down on anyone. He is powerful in understanding. 6 He does not keep wicked people alive, but He gives justice to those who are hurting. 7 He does not take His eyes off the people who do right. He sets them on thrones with kings and lifts them up for all time.

8 “If they are bound in chains and caught in ropes of trouble, 9 He tells them what they did and how they sinned. He shows that they acted proudly. 10 He opens their ears to teaching and tells them to turn away from sin. 11 If they listen and serve Him, they will live out their days in good times and their years in pleasure. 12 But if they do not listen, they will die by the sword and die without understanding.

Elihu Warns Job

13 “People with godless hearts hold on to anger. They do not cry for help when God binds them. 14 They die while young, and their lives end among male temple prostitutes. 15 But God saves suffering people through their suffering and opens their ears while they are in trouble. 16 He drew you out of trouble into a wide, open place, and your table was full of good food.

17 “But you are full of the judgment meant for wicked people. Judgment and justice have caught you. 18 Be careful that anger does not trick you into mocking. Do not let a large payment turn you away. 19 Will your cry for help save you from trouble, or will all your strength? 20 Do not long for the night, when people disappear from their place. 21 Be careful. Do not turn to sin, for you chose this instead of suffering.

God’s Greatness in the Storm

22 “Look, God is lifted up by His power. Who is a teacher like Him? 23 Who has told Him how to live, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? 24 Remember to praise His work, which people have praised. 25 All people have seen it. People look at it from far away. 26 Look, God is great, and we do not know Him. The number of His years cannot be found out.

27 “He lifts up the drops of water, and they turn into rain from His mist. 28 The clouds pour it down and shower it heavily on people. 29 Can anyone understand how He spreads out the clouds or the thunder from His tent? 30 Look, He spreads His lightning all around Him and covers the depths of the sea. 31 He uses these things to judge people and to give plenty of food. 32 He fills His hands with lightning and tells it to hit the target. 33 His thunder announces Him, and even the cattle sense the storm coming.”
