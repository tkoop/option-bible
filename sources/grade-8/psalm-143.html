A Prayer for Mercy and Guidance

1 The LORD, hear my prayer; in Your faithfulness listen to my plea; in Your righteousness answer me. 2 Do not bring Your servant into judgment, for no one living is righteous before You.

3 For the enemy has pursued my soul; he has crushed my life to the ground; he has made me dwell in darkness like those long dead. 4 My spirit grows faint within me; my heart is dismayed within me.

5 I remember the days of old; I think about all Your works; I consider the work of Your hands. 6 I spread out my hands to You; my soul thirsts for You like a parched land. Selah.

7 Answer me quickly, the LORD; my spirit fails. Do not hide Your face from me, or I will be like those who go down to the pit. 8 Let me hear of Your faithful love in the morning, for I trust in You. Make me know the way I should go, for I lift up my soul to You.

9 Deliver me from my enemies, the LORD; I flee to You for refuge. 10 Teach me to do Your will, for You are my God. Let Your good Spirit lead me on level ground.

11 For Your name's sake, the LORD, revive me. In Your righteousness, bring my soul out of trouble. 12 In Your faithful love, cut off my enemies and destroy all who afflict my soul, for I am Your servant.
