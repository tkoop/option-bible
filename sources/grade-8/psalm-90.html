A Prayer of Moses, the Man of God

God Is Eternal

1 Lord, You have been our home for every generation. 2 Before the mountains were born, before You made the earth and the world, You are God from everlasting to everlasting.

Human Life Is Short

3 You turn people back to dust and say, "Return to dust, O people." 4 For a thousand years in Your sight are like yesterday when it passes, or like a few hours of the night. 5 You sweep them away, and they sleep; they are like grass that springs up in the morning. 6 In the morning it blooms and grows, but by evening it is cut down and dries up.

God's Anger at Sin

7 For we are consumed by Your anger and terrified by Your wrath. 8 You have set our sins before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence. 9 All our days pass away under Your wrath; we finish our years with a sigh. 10 The days of our life are seventy years, or eighty if we are strong; yet their best is only trouble and sorrow, for they pass quickly, and we fly away. 11 Who understands the power of Your anger? Your wrath is as great as the fear that is due You.

Ask God for Mercy

12 Teach us to number our days, so that we may gain a heart of wisdom. 13 Return, O the LORD! How long will it be? Have compassion on Your servants. 14 Satisfy us in the morning with Your unfailing love, so that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days. 15 Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for as many years as we have seen trouble. 16 Let Your work be shown to Your servants, and Your splendor to their children. 17 May the favor of the Lord our God rest on us; establish the work of our hands for us—yes, establish the work of our hands.
