Descendants of Issachar

1 The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron. That was four sons. 2 Tola's sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel. They were leaders of their families. In David's time, Tola's family line had 22,600 brave soldiers listed in their family records. 3 Uzzi's son was Izrahiah. Izrahiah's sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah. All five of them were family leaders. 4 Their family lines also had 36,000 soldiers ready for war. They had many wives and children. 5 All their relatives from every Issachar family were brave soldiers. In all, 87,000 men were listed in their family records.

Descendants of Benjamin and Naphtali

6 Benjamin's sons were Bela, Beker, and Jediael. That was three sons. 7 Bela's sons were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri. These five men were family leaders. They were strong fighters, and their family records listed 22,034 men. 8 Beker's sons were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All of these were Beker's sons. 9 Their family records listed them as family leaders. There were 20,200 strong fighters. 10 Jediael's son was Bilhan. Bilhan's sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar. 11 All these men were Jediael's sons. They were family leaders and strong fighters. Their records listed 17,200 men who could go to war. 12 Shuppim and Huppim were sons of Ir. Hushim was the son of Aher. 13 Naphtali's sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum. They were the sons of Bilhah.

Descendants of Manasseh and Ephraim

14 Manasseh's son was Asriel. His Aramean concubine gave birth to him. She also gave birth to Machir, the father of Gilead. 15 Machir took a wife from the Huppites and Shuppites. His sister was named Maacah. Another descendant was named Zelophehad, and he had only daughters. 16 Machir's wife Maacah gave birth to a son. She named him Peresh. His brother was named Sheresh. Sheresh's sons were Ulam and Rakem. 17 Ulam's son was Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh. 18 Machir's sister Hammoleketh gave birth to Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. 19 Shemida's sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

20 Ephraim's sons were Shuthelah, Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, and Tahath his son. 21 Zabad was his son, and Shuthelah was his son. Ezer and Elead were killed by men from Gath who were born in that land. They had gone down to steal livestock. 22 Their father Ephraim mourned for many days. His relatives came to comfort him. 23 Then he slept with his wife. She became pregnant and gave birth to a son. He named him Beriah, because trouble had come to his house. 24 His daughter was Sheerah. She built Lower Beth-horon, Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah. 25 Her descendants included Rephah, Resheph, Telah, and Tahan. 26 Ladan was his son, Ammihud was his son, and Elishama was his son. 27 Nun was his son, and Joshua was his son. 28 Their land and towns included Bethel and its villages, Naaran to the east, Gezer and its villages to the west, and Shechem and its villages as far as Ayyah and its villages. 29 Along the border of Manasseh were Beth-shean and its villages, Taanach and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, and Dor and its villages. The descendants of Joseph, the son of Israel, lived in these towns.

Descendants of Asher

30 Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. Their sister was Serah. 31 Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel. Malchiel was the father of Birzaith. 32 Heber was the father of Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua. 33 Japhlet's sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were Japhlet's sons. 34 Shemer's sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram. 35 The sons of Helem, his brother, were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. 36 Zophah's sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah. 37 Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera were also his sons. 38 Jether's sons were Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. 39 Ulla's sons were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia. 40 All these were Asher's descendants. They were family leaders, chosen strong fighters, and leaders among the princes. Their family records listed 26,000 men who could fight in war.
