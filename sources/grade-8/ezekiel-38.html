Prophecy Against Gog

1 The word of the LORD came to me.

2 "Son of man, oppose Gog, the ruler of the land of Magog and chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. Prophesy against him. 3 Say: This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. 4 I will turn you around and drag you out with hooks in your jaws. I will bring you out with your whole army—horses and horsemen, all wearing full armor, a huge crowd with large and small shields, all holding swords. 5 Persia, Cush, and Put will be with them, all with shields and helmets. 6 Gomer and all its troops; Beth Togarmah from the far north with all its troops—many peoples are with you.

Gog Prepares for Battle

7 "Get ready. Be prepared—you and all your hordes gathered around you. You will be their guard. 8 After many days you will be called to account. In the last years you will come into a land brought back from war, gathered from many nations to the mountains of Israel. That land had long been ruined, but its people were brought out from the nations, and now they live in safety. 9 You and all your troops and many peoples with you will go up like a storm. You will be like a cloud covering the land.

Gog's Evil Plan

10 "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: On that day, thoughts will come into your mind, and you will devise an evil plan. 11 You will say that you are going up against a land of villages without walls, to attack a peaceful and unsuspecting people—all living without walls, gates, or bars. 12 You will plunder and loot, attacking ruined places that are now lived in and a people gathered from the nations, who have livestock and goods and live at the center of the land.

13 "Sheba, Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish and all its towns will ask you if you have come to plunder, if you have gathered your hordes to carry off silver and gold, to take livestock and goods, and to seize a great spoil. 14 Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog: This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: On that day, when My people Israel are living safely, will you not know? 15 You will come from your place in the far north, you and many peoples with you, all riding horses—a huge horde and mighty army. 16 You will advance against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. In the last days, Gog, I will bring you against My land so the nations may know Me when I show My holiness through you before their eyes.

God's Judgment on Gog

17 "This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Are you not the one I spoke of long ago through My servants the prophets of Israel? In those days they prophesied for years that I would bring you against them. 18 On that day, when Gog comes against the land of Israel, declares the Lord, the LORD, My wrath will rise in My anger. 19 In My zeal and fiery wrath I declare that on that day there will be a great earthquake in the land of Israel.

20 "The fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the beasts of the field, every creeping thing that moves on the ground, and every person on the face of the earth will tremble at My presence. The mountains will be thrown down, the cliffs will collapse, and every wall will fall to the ground. 21 I will call for a sword against Gog on all My mountains, declares the Lord, the LORD. Everyone will turn his sword against his brother. 22 I will judge him with plague and bloodshed. I will rain down on him and on his troops and on the many peoples with him a flood of rain, hailstones, fire, and sulfur. 23 I will show My greatness and holiness and make Myself known in the sight of many nations. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
