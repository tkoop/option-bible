The LORD's Anointed Brings Good News

1The Spirit of the Lord, the LORD, is on Me, because the LORD has anointed Me to bring good news to the poor. He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim freedom to the captives, and release to those in prison. 2To announce the year of the LORD's favor and the day of our God's justice, and to comfort all who mourn. 3To give those who grieve in Zion a crown of beauty instead of ashes, the oil of joy instead of mourning, and a garment of praise instead of a heavy spirit. They will be called oaks of righteousness, a planting of the LORD to show His glory.

Rebuilding and Renewal

4They will rebuild the ancient ruins and restore the places destroyed long ago. They will renew the ruined cities that have been empty for generations. 5Strangers will stand and care for your flocks, and foreigners will work your fields and vineyards.

6You will be called priests of the LORD, and people will say you are servants of our God. You will enjoy the wealth of the nations and boast in their riches. 7Instead of your shame you will receive a double portion, and instead of disgrace you will rejoice in your inheritance. So you will inherit a double portion in your land, and everlasting joy will be yours.

The LORD's Covenant and Blessing

8For I, the LORD, love justice; I hate robbery and wrongdoing. In My faithfulness I will reward them and make an everlasting covenant with them. 9Their children will be known among the nations, and their descendants among the peoples. Everyone who sees them will know they are the people the LORD has blessed.

Praise for God's Salvation

10I will rejoice greatly in the LORD; my soul will be joyful in my God. For He has clothed me with garments of salvation and wrapped me in a robe of righteousness, like a groom putting on his wedding turban and like a bride adorning herself with her jewels. 11For just as the earth brings forth its plants and a garden makes its seeds grow, so the Lord, the LORD, will make righteousness and praise spring up before all the nations.
