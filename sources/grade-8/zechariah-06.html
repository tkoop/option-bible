Four Chariots from Between the Mountains

1 I turned and lifted my eyes and saw four chariots coming out from between two mountains. The mountains were made of bronze. 2 The first chariot had red horses, the second had black horses, 3 the third had white horses, and the fourth had strong, spotted horses.

4 I asked the angel who was speaking to me, "What are these, my lord?"

5 The angel answered, "These are the four spirits of heaven. They go out from where they stood before the Lord of all the earth. 6 The chariot with black horses goes to the north country, the white horses follow them, and the spotted horses go to the south country. 7 The strong horses went out and wanted to patrol the earth. He said, 'Go and patrol the earth!' So they patrolled the earth."

8 Then he called out to me and said, "Look! The horses going to the north have brought My Spirit to rest in the north country."

Crowns for the High Priest

9 The word of the the LORD came to me: 10 "Take silver and gold from the exiles—Heldai, Tobiah, and Jedaiah—who came from Babylon. On that same day, go to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. 11 Make crowns from the silver and gold, and put them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest."

The Branch Will Build the Temple

12 Tell him, "This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: 'Here is a man whose name is the Branch. He will grow where He is and build the temple of the the LORD.' 13 He will build the temple of the the LORD, wear royal glory, sit and rule on His throne, and be a priest on His throne. There will be peace between the two.'"

14 The crowns will be kept in the temple of the the LORD as a reminder for Helem, Tobiah, Jedaiah, and Hen son of Zephaniah. 15 People from far away will come and help build the temple of the the LORD. Then you will know that the the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you. This will happen if you carefully listen to the voice of the the LORD your God."
