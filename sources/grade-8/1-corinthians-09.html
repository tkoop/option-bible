Paul Defends His Apostleship

1 Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Haven't I seen Jesus, our Lord? Aren't you my work in the Lord? 2 If I am not an apostle to other people, I am at least an apostle to you. You are the proof that I am an apostle in the Lord. 3 This is how I defend myself to those who question me.

The Right to Be Supported

4 Don't we have the right to eat and drink? 5 Don't we have the right to take a believing wife with us, like the other apostles, the Lord's brothers, and Peter? 6 Or are Barnabas and I the only ones who don't have the right to stop working?

7 Who ever serves as a soldier and pays his own way? Who plants a vineyard and doesn't eat its fruit? Who takes care of a flock and doesn't drink some of the milk? 8 Am I only using human reasoning? Doesn't the Law say the same thing? 9 For it is written in the Law of Moses, "Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain." Is God really concerned about oxen? 10 Or is He speaking entirely for our benefit? Surely it was written for us. The one who plows should plow with hope, and the one who threshes should thresh with hope of sharing in the crop.

11 If we planted spiritual things among you, is it too much to harvest material things from you? 12 If others have this right over you, don't we have an even greater right? But we did not use this right. Instead, we endure everything so that we won't get in the way of the gospel of Christ. 13 Don't you know that those who work in the temple eat from the temple, and those who serve at the altar share in what is offered on it? 14 In the same way, the Lord has commanded that those who preach the gospel should earn their living from the gospel.

Paul Surrenders His Rights

15 But I have not used any of these rights. I am not writing this to get something from you. I would rather die than let anyone take away my reason to boast. 16 If I preach the gospel, I have nothing to boast about. I have to do it. Woe to me if I do not preach the gospel! 17 If I do this because I want to, I get a reward. But if I do it because I have to, I have been trusted with a responsibility. 18 What is my reward then? It is that when I preach the gospel, I can offer it free of charge. That way, I do not make full use of my rights in the gospel.

19 I am free from everyone, but I have made myself a slave to everyone, so that I might win more people. 20 To the Jews I became like a Jew, so that I could win Jews. To those under the law, I became like someone under the law, even though I am not under the law myself, so that I could win those under the law. 21 To those not under the law, I became like someone not under the law. I am not free from God's law, but I am under Christ's law. I did this so that I could win those not under the law. 22 To the weak I became weak, so that I could win the weak. I have become all things to all people, so that I might save some of them. 23 I do all this for the sake of the gospel, so that I can share in its blessings with them.

Running to Win

24 Don't you know that in a race all the runners run, but only one gets the prize? So run in a way that you will win. 25 Everyone who competes trains in every way. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get one that will last forever. 26 So I do not run without a goal. I do not fight like someone who punches the air. 27 Instead, I discipline my body and make it obey me, so that after I preach to others, I won't fail the test myself.
