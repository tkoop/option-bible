Esau’s Family and Move to Seir

1 These are the descendants of Esau, who is also called Edom. 2 Esau married women from Canaan: Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite; Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite; 3 and Basemath, daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth. 4 Adah had Eliphaz for Esau, and Basemath had Reuel. 5 Oholibamah had Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These sons of Esau were born to him in Canaan.

6 Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, and all the people in his house. He also took his livestock, all his animals, and everything he had gained in Canaan. Then he moved to a land away from his brother Jacob. 7 Their belongings were too many for them to stay together. The land where they lived could not hold both of them because of their livestock. 8 So Esau, who is also called Edom, settled in the hill country of Seir. 9 This is the family history of Esau, the father of the Edomites, in the hill country of Seir.

Leaders Among Esau’s Descendants

10 These are the names of Esau’s sons: Eliphaz, son of Esau’s wife Adah, and Reuel, son of Esau’s wife Basemath. 11 The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 12 Timna was a concubine of Esau’s son Eliphaz, and she gave birth to Amalek for him. These are the sons of Esau’s wife Adah. 13 The sons of Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These are the sons of Esau’s wife Basemath. 14 The sons of Esau’s wife Oholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, were Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

15 These are the chiefs among Esau’s sons: the chiefs of Eliphaz, Esau’s firstborn, were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz, 16 Korah, Gatam, and Amalek. These are the chiefs of Eliphaz in Edom, and they are the sons of Adah. 17 The chiefs of Esau’s son Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These are the chiefs of Reuel in Edom, and they are the sons of Esau’s wife Basemath. 18 The chiefs of Esau’s wife Oholibamah were Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These are the chiefs of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah. 19 These are the sons of Esau, that is, Edom, and these are their chiefs.

Chiefs of the Horites in Seir

20 These are the sons of Seir the Horite, who lived in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 21 Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites, the sons of Seir in Edom. 22 The sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam, and Timna was Lotan’s sister. 23 The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 24 The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah. This is the Anah who found hot springs in the wilderness while he was grazing his father Zibeon’s donkeys. 25 The sons of Anah were Dishon and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah. 26 The sons of Dishon were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 27 The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 28 The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran.

29 These are the chiefs of the Horites: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 30 Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites, by their chiefs, in the land of Seir.

Kings and Chiefs of Edom

31 These are the kings who ruled in Edom before any king ruled over the Israelites: 32 Bela son of Beor ruled in Edom. His city was called Dinhabah. 33 When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah ruled in his place. 34 When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites ruled in his place. 35 When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad ruled in his place. He had defeated Midian in the field of Moab. His city was called Avith. 36 When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah ruled in his place. 37 When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the River ruled in his place. 38 When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor ruled in his place. 39 When Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, Hadar ruled in his place. His city was called Pau. His wife’s name was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezahab.

40 These are the names of Esau’s chiefs by their families and places, by their names: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, 41 Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 42 Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 43 Magdiel, and Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom by the places where they lived in the land they owned. Esau was the father of the Edomites.
