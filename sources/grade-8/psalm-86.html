David's Prayer for Help

1 Listen to me, the LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and needy. 2 Guard my life, for I am faithful. Save Your servant who trusts in You, my God. 3 Be gracious to me, Lord, for I call to You all day long. 4 Bring joy to Your servant's soul, for I turn my whole self to You, Lord. 5 For You, Lord, are good and forgiving, and You are full of faithful love for all who call on You.

6 The LORD, listen to my prayer, and pay attention to my cry for mercy. 7 I call on You in my trouble because You answer me. 8 There is no one like You among the gods, Lord, and no works like Yours. 9 All the nations You have made will come and worship before You, Lord, and they will honor Your name. 10 For You are great and do wonderful things; You alone are God.

11 Teach me Your way, the LORD, so I may walk in Your truth. Give me an undivided heart to fear Your name. 12 I will praise You, Lord my God, with all my heart, and I will honor Your name forever. 13 For Your faithful love to me is great; You have saved my life from the grave.

14 God, proud people have risen against me, and a gang of violent men is trying to kill me. They do not keep You in mind. 15 But You, Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, and full of faithful love and truth. 16 Turn to me and be gracious to me. Give Your strength to Your servant, and save the son of Your servant. 17 Show me a sign of Your favor, so my enemies will see it and be put to shame, because You, the LORD, have helped me and comforted me.
