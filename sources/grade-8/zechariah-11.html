Fire Burns the Cedars of Lebanon

1 Open your gates, Lebanon, so fire can burn your cedar trees. 2 Wail, cypress, because the cedar has fallen and the mighty trees are ruined. Wail, oaks of Bashan, because the thick forest has been cut down. 3 Listen to the shepherds wail, because their glory is destroyed. Listen to the young lions roar, because the Jordan's thickets are ruined.

The Flock Doomed to Slaughter

4 This is what the LORD my God says: “Pasture the flock that is marked for slaughter. 5 Their buyers kill them and are not punished. Their sellers say, ‘Praise the LORD, because I am rich!’ Even their shepherds show them no pity. 6 For I will no longer pity the people who live in this land,” says the LORD. “Instead, I will hand every person over to his neighbor and to his king. They will crush the land, and I will not rescue anyone from their power.”

7 So I pastured the flock marked for slaughter, especially the oppressed among them. I took two staffs. I named one Favor and the other Union, and I pastured the flock. 8 In one month I got rid of three shepherds. I grew tired of them, and they hated me. 9 Then I said, “I will not shepherd you anymore. Let the dying die, the perishing perish, and let the survivors eat one another's flesh.”

Two Staffs Are Broken

10 I took my staff called Favor and broke it, ending the covenant I had made with all the nations. 11 So it was broken that day. The poor sheep who watched me knew that this was the LORD's word. 12 Then I said to them, “If it seems good to you, give me my pay; if not, keep it.” So they weighed out my pay: thirty pieces of silver.

13 Then the LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter—the handsome price they put on Me!” So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them to the potter in the LORD's house.

14 Then I broke my second staff, Union, to break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

A Foolish Shepherd Is Raised Up

15 Then the LORD said to me, “Take for yourself the gear of a foolish shepherd. 16 For I am about to raise up a shepherd in the land who will not care for the lost, will not look for the young, will not heal the injured, and will not feed the healthy. Instead, he will eat the meat of the fat sheep and tear off their hooves. 17 Woe to the worthless shepherd who abandons the flock! A sword will strike his arm and right eye. His arm will dry up completely, and his right eye will go blind.”
