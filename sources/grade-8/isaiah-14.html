The LORD Will Restore Israel

1 For the LORD will have mercy on Jacob. Once again He will choose Israel and settle them in their own land. The foreigner will join them and unite with the house of Jacob.

2 Nations will take them and bring them to their own place. The house of Israel will own them as male and female servants in the LORD’s land. They will make captives of their captors and rule over their oppressors.

A Taunt Against Babylon’s King

3 On the day the LORD gives you rest from your pain and torment and the hard labor forced on you, 4 you will take up this taunt against the king of Babylon: “How the oppressor has ceased! The golden city has ceased!”

5 The LORD has broken the staff of the wicked, the scepter of rulers. 6 It struck the peoples in anger with blows that never stopped. It ruled the nations with a fury that never held back. 7 All the earth is at rest and quiet now; they break into singing.

8 Even the cypresses and the cedars of Lebanon rejoice over you: “Since you have been laid low, no woodcutter comes up against us.”

9 Sheol below is stirred up to meet you when you come. It wakes the spirits of the dead to greet you—all the leaders of the earth. It makes all the kings of the nations rise from their thrones. 10 They all answer and say to you, “You too have become weak, as we are. You have become like us.” 11 Your pride is brought down to Sheol, along with the sound of your harps. Maggots are spread out beneath you, and worms cover you.

12 How you have fallen from heaven, O morning star, son of the dawn! You have been cut down to the earth, O destroyer of nations. 13 You said in your heart, “I will ascend to heaven. I will raise my throne above the stars of God. I will sit on the mount of assembly in the far north. 14 I will rise above the tops of the clouds. I will make myself like the Most High.” 15 But you will be brought down to Sheol, to the lowest depths of the pit.

16 Those who see you will stare. They will think, “Is this the man who shook the earth and made kingdoms tremble, 17 who turned the world into a desert and destroyed its cities, who never let his prisoners go home?” 18 All the kings of the nations lie in honor, each in his own tomb. 19 But you are thrown out of your grave like a hated branch. You are covered with the dead, with those pierced by the sword, with those who go down to the stones of the pit, like a body trampled underfoot. 20 You will not join them in burial, because you destroyed your land and killed your people. May the children of evildoers never be named again. 21 Get ready to slaughter his sons for the sins of their fathers, so they will never rise again to take the land or fill the world with cities.

The LORD’s Plan Against Assyria

22 “I will rise up against them,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “I will cut off from Babylon her name and remnant, her children and grandchildren,” declares the LORD. 23 “I will make her a home for owls and swamps. I will sweep her away with the broom of destruction,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

24 The LORD of Hosts has sworn: “Surely, as I have planned, so it will be; as I have purposed, so it will stand— 25 to break Assyria in My land and trample him on My mountains. His yoke will be taken from them, and his burden will be removed from their shoulders.” 26 This is the plan made for the whole earth, and this is the hand stretched out over all the nations. 27 For the LORD of Hosts has purposed, and who can stop Him? His hand is stretched out, and who can turn it back?

A Warning to Philistia

28 In the year King Ahaz died, this message came:

29 Do not rejoice, all you Philistines, that the rod that struck you is broken. For from the root of the snake a viper will come, and its fruit will be a flying serpent. 30 The firstborn of the poor will eat, and the needy will lie down in safety. But I will kill your root with famine, and your remnant I will slay. 31 Wail, O gate! Cry out, O city! Melt in fear, all you Philistines! For smoke comes from the north, and there is no straggler in its ranks.

32 What answer will be given to the messengers of the nations? “The LORD has founded Zion, and in her the afflicted of His people will find refuge.”
