An Attacker Comes Against Nineveh

1 A scatterer advances against you. Guard the fortress! Watch the road! Brace yourself! Gather all your strength! 2 For the LORD will restore Jacob's glory like Israel's glory, because destroyers have stripped them bare and ruined their branches. 3 The shields of his mighty men are red; his soldiers are dressed in scarlet. The chariots flash like fire when he prepares for battle, and the cypress spears are brandished. 4 The chariots race through the streets and rush through the squares. They look like torches; they dart like lightning.

Nineveh Falls

5 He remembers his officers. They stumble as they march. They hurry to the wall; the siege shield is set up. 6 The gates of the rivers are opened, and the palace melts away. 7 It is decided: she is stripped and carried away. Her servant girls moan like doves and beat their breasts. 8 Nineveh is like a pool of water, but its people are fleeing. "Stop! Stop!" they cry, but no one turns back.

Plunder and Ruin

9 Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! There is no end to the treasure, and wealth comes from every precious thing. 10 She is emptied! She is drained! She is stripped! Hearts melt, knees shake, bodies tremble, and every face grows pale.

The Lions and the LORD's Judgment

11 Where is the lions' den, the feeding place of the young lions, where the lion and lioness walked with their cubs, with no one to make them afraid? 12 The lion tore prey for his cubs and strangled it for his lionesses. He filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh. 13 Look, I am against you, declares the LORD of Hosts. I will burn up your chariots in smoke, and the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will be heard no more.
