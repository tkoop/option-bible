Oppression in This World

1 Then I turned again and looked at all the oppression that happens in this world. I saw the tears of the oppressed, and they had no one to comfort them. Power was on the side of their oppressors, and they had no one to comfort them. 2 So I said the dead, who have already died, are better off than the living, who are still alive. 3 But better than both is the person who has never been born, who has not seen the evil things done in this world.

Work, Envy, and Rest

4 Then I saw that all hard work and every skillful deed come from one person envying another. This too is meaningless and useless. 5 The fool folds his hands and destroys himself. 6 It is better to have a little peace and quiet than a lot of hard work that comes to nothing.

7 Then I turned again and saw something meaningless in this world. 8 A man may be alone, with no companion. He has no son and no brother, yet he never stops working. His eyes are never satisfied with riches. He asks, "Who am I working so hard for, and why am I denying myself good things?" This too is meaningless—a miserable way to live.

Two Are Better Than One

9 Two people are better than one, because they get a good reward for their work. 10 If one falls, the other can help him up. But how sad for the person who is alone and falls, with no one to help him up!

11 Also, if two lie down together, they can keep each other warm. But how can one person stay warm alone? 12 If someone attacks one person, two can stand against him. A rope made of three strands is not easily broken.

A Wise Youth and a Foolish King

13 It is better to be a poor but wise young person than an old and foolish king who no longer listens to warnings. 14 The young man came out of prison to rule, even though he was born poor in his kingdom.

15 I saw all the living people in this world, along with the young man who would take the king's place. 16 There is no end to all the people who came before them. Yet the people who come later will not be pleased with him. This too is meaningless and useless.
