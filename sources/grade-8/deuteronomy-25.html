Judging Disputes and Punishment

1 If two men have a disagreement and go to court, the judges will decide who is right and who is wrong. 2 If the guilty man deserves a beating, the judge will order him to lie down and be beaten in front of him. The number of blows must match his crime. 3 He may receive up to forty blows, but no more. If you give more, your brother would be humiliated in front of you. 4 Do not muzzle an ox while it is threshing grain.

When Brothers Live Together

5 If brothers live together and one of them dies without a son, his widow must not marry someone outside the family. Her husband's brother should marry her and do his duty as a brother-in-law. 6 The first son she has will carry the name of the dead brother, so his name will not disappear from Israel.

7 But if the man does not want to marry his brother's widow, she must go to the elders at the town gate and say, "My brother-in-law refuses to keep his brother's name alive in Israel. He will not do his duty for me."

8 The elders of his town will call for him and talk to him. If he stands firm and says, "I do not want to marry her,"

9 then his brother's widow will go up to him in front of the elders, pull his sandal off his foot, spit in his face, and say, "This is what is done to the man who will not build up his brother's family." 10 After that, his family in Israel will be called "the family of the man whose sandal was pulled off."

11 If two men are fighting and the wife of one steps in to save her husband from the man hitting him, and she grabs the attacker by his private parts, 12 cut off her hand. Do not show her any mercy.

Honest Weights and Measures

13 Do not keep two different weights in your bag—one heavy and one light. 14 Do not keep two different measures in your house—one large and one small. 15 Keep a full and fair weight and a full and fair measure. Then you will live a long time in the land the the LORD your God gives you. 16 For everyone who does these things, everyone who acts unfairly, is detestable to the the LORD your God.

Remember Amalek

17 Remember what Amalek did to you on the road when you came out of Egypt. 18 He met you on the way when you were tired and worn out. He attacked everyone who was lagging behind you, and he did not fear God. 19 When the the LORD your God gives you rest from all your enemies around you in the land the the LORD your God is giving you to own, you must wipe out the memory of Amalek from under heaven. Do not forget.
