Two Angels Visit Lot in Sodom

1 Two angels came to Sodom in the evening, and Lot was sitting at the gate of Sodom. When Lot saw them, he got up to meet them and bowed with his face to the ground.

2 He said, "Please, my lords, turn aside to your servant's house. Wash your feet and stay the night. Then you can get up early and go on your way."

"No," they said, "we will stay in the street."

3 But he urged them strongly, so they turned aside to him and entered his house. He made a feast for them and baked bread without yeast, and they ate.

The Men of Sodom Surround Lot's House

4 Before they lay down, the men of the city—the men of Sodom, both young and old, all the people from every part of the city—surrounded the house.

5 They called to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so we can have sex with them."

6 Lot went out to them at the door and shut the door behind him.

7 He said, "Please, my brothers, do not do such a wicked thing. 8 Look, I have two daughters who have never slept with a man. Let me bring them out to you, and you can do to them what seems good to you. Only do nothing to these men, because they are staying in my home."

9 They said, "Get out of the way! This fellow came to live here, and now he wants to play judge! Now we will do worse to you than to them." Then they pushed hard against Lot and came near to break the door.

10 The men reached out their hands, brought Lot into the house, and shut the door.

11 They struck all the men at the door, young and old, with blindness, so that they could not find the door no matter how hard they tried.

The Angels Urge Lot to Leave

12 The men said to Lot, "Do you still have anyone here—sons-in-law, sons, daughters, or anyone else in the city? Bring them out of this place. 13 For we are about to destroy this place, because the outcry against them has become so great before the LORD that the LORD has sent us to destroy it."

14 Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law who were to marry his daughters. He said, "Get up and get out of this place, because the LORD is about to destroy the city." But to his sons-in-law he seemed to be joking.

15 When dawn came, the angels urged Lot, saying, "Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here, or you will be swept away in the punishment of the city."

16 He hesitated, but the men seized his hand, his wife's hand, and his two daughters' hands. The LORD was merciful to him, and they brought him out and set him outside the city.

17 When they brought them out, one said, "Run for your life! Do not look back, and do not stop anywhere in the valley. Run to the hills, or you will be swept away."

18 Lot said to them, "Oh no, my lord! 19 Your servant has found favor in your sight, and you have shown great kindness by saving my life. But I cannot run to the hills. Disaster might catch me, and I would die. 20 Look, this town is near enough to run to, and it is small. Please let me escape there—it is only a little place—so my life may be saved."

21 He said to him, "All right, I have granted your request about this matter too; I will not destroy the town you spoke of. 22 Hurry and escape there, because I cannot do anything until you get there." So the town was called Zoar.

23 The sun had risen over the earth when Lot came to Zoar.

Fire and Sulfur Destroy Sodom and Gomorrah

24 Then the LORD rained sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah—from the LORD out of heaven.

25 He overthrew those cities and the whole valley, and all the people who lived in the cities, and also the plants of the ground.

26 But Lot's wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

27 Abraham got up early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before the LORD.

28 He looked down toward Sodom and Gomorrah and toward all the land of the valley, and he saw smoke rising from the land like smoke from a furnace.

29 When God destroyed the cities of the valley, God remembered Abraham and rescued Lot from the destruction. When He overthrew the cities where Lot had lived, He spared Lot.

30 Lot went up from Zoar with his two daughters and lived in the hills, because he was afraid to live in Zoar. He and his two daughters lived in a cave.

Lot's Daughters and Their Sons

31 The older daughter said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man on earth left to marry us. 32 Come, let us get our father drunk with wine, and we will lie with him, so our family line can continue."

33 That night they got their father drunk with wine. The older daughter went in and lay with her father, and he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

34 The next day the older daughter said to the younger, "Look, I lay with my father last night. Let us get him drunk with wine again tonight, and you go in and lie with him, so our family line can continue."

35 So that night they got their father drunk with wine again. The younger daughter went in and lay with him, and he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

36 So both of Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father.

37 The older daughter gave birth to a son and named him Moab. He is the father of the Moabites to this day.

38 The younger daughter gave birth to a son and named him Ben-ammi. He is the father of the Ammonites to this day.
