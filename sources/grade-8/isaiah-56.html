The LORD Calls for Justice

1 This is what the LORD says: "Keep justice and do what is right, because My salvation is near and My righteousness will soon be shown." 2 Blessed is the person who does this, and the one who holds on to it—keeping the Sabbath without treating it like an ordinary day, and keeping his hand from doing any evil.

The LORD Welcomes Outsiders

3 The foreigner who has joined the LORD should not say, "The LORD will surely separate me from His people."

And the man who cannot have children should not say, "I am like a tree that can never bear fruit."

4 For this is what the LORD says to the men who cannot have children who keep My Sabbaths, choose what pleases Me, and hold on to My covenant: 5 "I will give them a place in My house and within My walls, a name better than sons and daughters. I will give them a lasting name that will never be cut off." 6 And the foreigners who join the LORD to serve Him, to love the name of the LORD, and to be His servants—everyone who keeps the Sabbath without treating it like an ordinary day and holds on to My covenant— 7 I will bring them to My holy mountain and make them joyful in My house of prayer. Their burnt offerings and sacrifices will be accepted on My altar, because My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations." 8 The Lord, the LORD, who gathers Israel's scattered people, declares: "I will gather even more people to those already gathered."

The LORD Warns Israel's Leaders

9 All of you wild animals in the fields, come and eat. All of you wild animals in the forest, come too. 10 Israel's watchmen are blind. They all lack knowledge. They are like dogs that cannot bark. They lie around dreaming and love to sleep. 11 The dogs are greedy and never have enough. They are shepherds who do not understand. They all turn to their own way, each one chasing his own gain. 12 They say, "Come, let's get wine and drink strong drink. Tomorrow will be like today, only much better!"
