A Warning for the Philistines

1The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet about the Philistines. It came before Pharaoh attacked Gaza. 2This is what the LORD said: "Look! Water is rising from the north. It will become a flooding river. It will overflow the land and everything in it—the city and all who live there. People will cry out, and everyone who lives in the land will wail." 3They will hear the sound of horses' hooves and the roar of chariots. They will hear the rumble of wheels. Fathers will not turn to help their children, because their hands will be too weak.

The Day of Destruction

4On the day that is coming, the LORD will destroy all the Philistines. He will cut off every survivor and helper from Tyre and Sidon. The LORD is destroying the Philistines, the remnant from the coast of Caphtor. 5Gaza, your head will be shaved in mourning. Ashkelon will be silenced. You who remain in the valley, how long will you keep cutting yourselves?

The Sword of the LORD

6Ah, sword of the LORD! How long will you not rest? Return to your sheath. Be still and quiet. 7How can it rest when the LORD has commanded it? He has set it against Ashkelon and the seacoast.
