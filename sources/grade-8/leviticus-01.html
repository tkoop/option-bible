The LORD Speaks to Moses

1 The LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting. He said,

2 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them, ‘When any of you brings an offering to the LORD, you shall bring your offering from the livestock, either from the herd or from the flock.

Burnt Offerings from Cattle

3 “‘If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall offer a male without defect. He shall bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting so that he may be accepted before the LORD. 4 He shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted for him to make atonement for him.

5 “‘He shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, shall bring the blood and sprinkle it all around on the altar at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

6 “‘He shall skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces. 7 The sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.

8 “‘Then Aaron's sons, the priests, shall arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat, on the wood that is on the fire on the altar. 9 He shall wash the inner parts and the legs with water. Then the priest shall burn all of it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

Burnt Offerings from Sheep or Goats

10 “‘If his offering is a burnt offering from the flock, from the sheep or the goats, he shall offer a male without defect.

11 “‘He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, shall sprinkle its blood all around on the altar.

12 “‘He shall cut it into pieces. The priest shall arrange them, along with the head and the fat, on the wood that is on the fire on the altar.

13 “‘He shall wash the inner parts and the legs with water. Then the priest shall bring all of it and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

Burnt Offerings from Birds

14 “‘If his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he shall bring his offering of turtledoves or young pigeons.

15 “‘The priest shall bring it to the altar, twist off its head, and burn it on the altar. Its blood shall be drained against the side of the altar.

16 “‘He shall remove its crop with its feathers and throw it on the east side of the altar, in the place for ashes.

17 “‘He shall tear it open by the wings without dividing it completely. Then the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.’”
