Two Baskets of Figs Before the Temple

1 After King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took King Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim of Judah into exile, along with Judah's officials, craftsmen, and metalworkers from Jerusalem, and brought them to Babylon, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs set in front of the temple of the LORD.

2 One basket held very good figs, like early figs. The other held very bad figs, too bad to eat.

3 The LORD said to me, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I answered, "The good figs are very good, and the bad figs are very bad—too bad to eat."

4 Then the word of the LORD came to me:

The Good Figs: The Exiles Who Will Return

5 "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Like these good figs, so I will treat the exiles of Judah for good—the ones I sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans. 6 I will watch over them for good, and I will bring them back to this land. I will build them up, not tear them down; I will plant them, not uproot them. 7 I will give them a heart to know Me, for I am the LORD. They will be My people, and I will be their God, for they will return to Me with all their heart.

The Bad Figs: Those Who Face Judgment

8 "But like the bad figs, so bad they cannot be eaten—thus says the LORD—so I will give up Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, the rest of Jerusalem who remain in this land, and those who live in the land of Egypt. 9 I will make them a horror and an evil to all the kingdoms of the earth—a disgrace, a proverb, a taunt, and a curse in all the places where I drive them. 10 I will send against them sword, famine, and plague until they are gone from the land I gave to them and their fathers."
