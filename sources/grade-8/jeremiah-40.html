Jeremiah Is Freed

1 This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, released him at Ramah. He had taken Jeremiah in chains with all the captives of Jerusalem and Judah who were being carried to Babylon.

2 The captain of the guard took Jeremiah and said to him, "The LORD your God said He would bring this disaster on this place. 3 The LORD has done what He said, because you sinned against the LORD and did not obey His voice. That is why this has happened to you. 4 Now look, I am releasing you today from the chains on your hands. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will take care of you. But if it does not seem good to you to come with me to Babylon, then stay. Look, the whole land is before you. Go wherever you think is good and right."

5 Before Jeremiah could turn to leave, the captain added, "Go to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon appointed over the cities of Judah. Live with him among the people, or go wherever you think is best." Then the captain gave him food and a gift and sent him away.

Gedaliah Is Appointed Governor

6 So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and stayed with him among the people who were left in the land.

7 All the army commanders in the field heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam to govern the land and had put him in charge of the men, women, children, and poor people who had not been taken to Babylon. 8 So they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah; Johanan and Jonathan, the sons of Kareah; Seraiah son of Tanhumeth; the sons of Ephai from Netophah; and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, along with their men.

9 Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, promised them and their men, "Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and things will go well for you. 10 As for me, I will stay at Mizpah to meet the Chaldeans when they come to us. But you, gather wine, summer fruit, and oil, put them in your jars, and live in the cities you have taken."

11 All the Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and other lands also heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant in Judah and had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over them. 12 So they came back from all the places where they had been scattered. They came into the land of Judah to Gedaliah at Mizpah and gathered a very large amount of wine and summer fruit.

A Warning About Ishmael

13 Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah.

14 They said to him, "Do you know that Baalis, king of the Ammonites, sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to kill you?" But Gedaliah did not believe them.

15 Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah. He said, "Let me go kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah. No one will know. Why should he kill you and scatter all the Jews who have gathered to you? Then the rest of Judah would be destroyed."

16 Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, "Do not do this. You are telling lies about Ishmael."
