Elihu Answers Job

1 Then Elihu spoke again: 2 "Do you think this is fair? You say, 'I am more righteous than God.' 3 You ask, 'What good does it do me? What do I gain by not sinning?' 4 I will answer you and your friends who are with you."

Human Sin Does Not Hurt God

5 "Look up at the sky and see; look at the clouds high above you. 6 If you sin, what do you do to Him? If your sins are many, what do you do to Him? 7 If you are righteous, what do you give Him? Or what does He receive from your hand? 8 Your wrongdoing affects only people like you, and your right living affects only other people."

Why God May Not Answer

9 "Because of heavy oppression, people cry out; they call for help against the power of the mighty. 10 But no one says, 'Where is God my Maker, who gives songs in the night, 11 who teaches us more than the animals of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?' 12 There they cry out, but He does not answer, because of the pride of evil people. 13 Surely God does not listen to empty cries, and the Almighty does not pay attention to them. 14 How much less, then, when you say you do not see Him, that your case is before Him and you must wait for Him! 15 And now, since He does not punish in anger and does not pay much attention to wrongdoing, 16 Job opens his mouth for nothing and piles up words without knowledge."
