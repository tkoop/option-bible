A Prayer for God to Restore His People

1Listen to us, Shepherd of Israel. You lead Joseph like a flock. You sit enthroned between the cherubim. Show Yourself to us. 2Rally Your power before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, and come save us. 3Restore us, O God. Make Your face shine on us, so we may be saved.

4O the LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger burn against the prayers of Your people? 5You have fed them tears for bread. You have made them drink tears in large amounts. 6You have made us a joke to our neighbors. Our enemies laugh at us.

7Restore us, O God of Hosts. Make Your face shine on us, so we may be saved.

The Vine God Planted

8You brought a vine out of Egypt. You drove out the nations and planted it. 9You cleared the ground for it. It took root and filled the land. 10The mountains were covered by its shade. The tall cedars were covered by its branches. 11It sent its branches to the Sea, and its new shoots to the River.

12Why have You broken down its walls? Now everyone who passes by picks its fruit. 13The wild boar from the forest tears it apart. The animals of the field feed on it.

A Plea for Revival

14Return, O God of Hosts! Look down from heaven and see. Take care of this vine, 15the root Your right hand planted, the son You raised up for Yourself. 16It is burned with fire and cut down. They die at Your angry look.

17Let Your hand rest on the man at Your right hand, the son of man You have raised up for Yourself. 18Then we will not turn away from You. Give us life, and we will call on Your name. 19Restore us, O the LORD God of Hosts. Make Your face shine on us, so we may be saved.
