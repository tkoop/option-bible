Aaron and His Sons Are Chosen as Priests

1 “Bring your brother Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar near to you from among the Israelites, so they can serve Me as priests. 2 Make holy garments for your brother Aaron, for glory and splendor. 3 You are to speak to all the skilled craftsmen, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, so they may make Aaron's garments to set him apart for serving Me as priest. 4 These are the garments they are to make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a woven tunic, a turban, and a sash. They are to make holy garments for your brother Aaron and his sons, so they may serve Me as priests. 5 They are to use gold, along with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen.

The Ephod

6 “They are to make the ephod of finely spun linen, embroidered with gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. 7 It must have two shoulder pieces attached at two of its corners, so it can be fastened. 8 The woven waistband of the ephod must be one piece with it, made the same way, of finely spun linen embroidered with gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn.

9 “Take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel: 10 six names on one stone and the remaining six on the other, in the order of their birth. 11 Engrave the names of the sons of Israel on the two stones the way a gem cutter engraves a seal. Then mount the stones in gold settings. 12 Fasten both stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel. Aaron must carry their names on his two shoulders before the LORD as a memorial. 13 Make gold settings 14 and two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords, and attach the chains to the settings.

The Breastpiece

15 “You are to make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a skilled craftsman. Make it like the ephod—of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. 16 It must be square when folded over, a span long and a span wide. 17 Mount on it a setting of four rows of stones: The first row must be a ruby, a topaz, and an emerald. 18 The second row must be a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond. 19 The third row must be a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst. 20 The fourth row must be a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. Mount them in gold settings. 21 The stones must match the names of the sons of Israel, twelve stones, one for each name, engraved like a seal for each of the twelve tribes.

22 “For the breastpiece, make braided chains of pure gold, like cords. 23 You are to make two gold rings for the breastpiece and fasten them to its two upper corners. 24 Then attach the two gold chains to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece. 25 Fasten the other ends of the chains to the two settings, attaching them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front. 26 Make two more gold rings and attach them to the other two corners of the breastpiece, on the inside edge next to the ephod. 27 Make two more gold rings and attach them to the lower part of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front, near the seam just above the woven waistband. 28 Tie the rings of the breastpiece to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so the breastpiece stays above the waistband of the ephod and does not swing loose.

29 “Whenever Aaron enters the Holy Place, he must carry the names of the sons of Israel over his heart on the breastpiece of judgment, as a continual memorial before the LORD. 30 Place the Urim and Thummim in the breastpiece of judgment, so they will be over Aaron's heart whenever he enters the presence of the LORD. In this way Aaron will always carry the judgment of the Israelites over his heart before the LORD.

The Robe, Turban, and Other Garments

31 “You are to make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth. 32 It must have an opening for the head in the center. Around the opening, make a woven collar like the opening of a garment, so it will not tear. 33 Make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn around the lower hem, with gold bells between them. 34 The gold bells and pomegranates must alternate around the lower hem of the robe. 35 Aaron must wear it when he serves, and the sound of the bells must be heard when he enters or leaves the Holy Place before the LORD, so he will not die.

36 “You are to make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it as on a seal: HOLY TO the LORD. 37 Fasten a blue cord to it to attach it to the turban; it is to be on the front of the turban. 38 It will be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron will carry the guilt of the holy things that the Israelites set apart with all their holy gifts. It will always be on his forehead, so they may be accepted before the LORD.

39 “You are to weave the tunic of fine linen, make the turban of fine linen, and make the sash the work of an embroiderer. 40 Make tunics, sashes, and headbands for Aaron's sons, to give them glory and splendor. 41 Put these garments on your brother Aaron and his sons, anoint them, ordain them, and set them apart, so they may serve Me as priests.

42 “Make linen undergarments to cover their nakedness, reaching from waist to thigh. 43 Aaron and his sons must wear them whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting or approach the altar to serve in the Holy Place, so they will not bring guilt on themselves and die. This will be a lasting rule for Aaron and his descendants.”
