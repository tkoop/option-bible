A Cry for Help

1 For the director of music. To the tune of "The Doe of the Dawn." A psalm of David. My God, my God, why have You forsaken me? Why are You so far from saving me, and so far from hearing my cries? 2 My God, I cry out by day, but You do not answer; by night I find no rest.

3 Yet You are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel. 4 In You our fathers trusted; they trusted, and You delivered them. 5 They cried out to You and were saved; they trusted in You and were not put to shame.

6 But I am a worm, not a man; I am scorned by men and despised by the people.

Mocked by His Enemies

7 All who see me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads: 8 "He trusts in the the LORD; let the the LORD rescue him. Let Him deliver him, since He delights in him."

9 Yet You brought me out of the womb; You made me trust in You, even at my mother's breast. 10 From birth I was cast on You; from my mother's womb You have been my God. 11 Do not be far from me, for trouble is near and there is no one to help.

Surrounded by Enemies

12 Many bulls surround me; strong bulls of Bashan encircle me. 13 They open their mouths against me like a roaring lion that tears its prey. 14 I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint. My heart is like wax; it melts within me. 15 My strength is dried up like a piece of pottery, and my tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth; You lay me in the dust of death.

16 Dogs surround me; a band of evil men encircles me; they have pierced my hands and feet. 17 I can count all my bones; they stare and gloat over me. 18 They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.

God Answers and Saves

19 But You, the the LORD, do not stay far away; my Strength, come quickly to help me. 20 Save my life from the sword, and my precious life from the dog's power. 21 Save me from the lion's mouth; save me from the horns of the wild oxen. You have answered me.

Praise in the Assembly

22 I will declare Your name to my brothers; in the midst of the assembly I will praise You. 23 You who fear the the LORD, praise Him! All you descendants of Jacob, honor Him! All you descendants of Israel, revere Him! 24 For He has not despised or scorned the suffering of the afflicted; He has not hidden His face from him, but has listened to his cry for help. 25 My praise for You resounds in the great assembly; I will fulfill my vows before those who fear You. 26 The poor will eat and be satisfied; those who seek Him will praise the the LORD. May your hearts live forever!

All Nations Will Worship the LORD

27 All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the the LORD, and all the families of the nations will bow down before Him. 28 For dominion belongs to the the LORD, and He rules over the nations. 29 All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who die will bow before Him—even those who cannot keep themselves alive. 30 Future generations will serve Him; they will declare the Lord to a future generation. 31 They will come and proclaim His righteousness to a people yet unborn—that He has done it!
