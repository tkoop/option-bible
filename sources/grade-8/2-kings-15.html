Azariah Rules Judah

1 In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam, king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah became king of Judah. 2 He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he ruled fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah, from Jerusalem. 3 He did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done. 4 But the high places were not taken away. The people still sacrificed and burned incense on them. 5 The LORD struck the king with leprosy, and he stayed a leper until the day he died. He lived in a separate house, and his son Jotham was in charge of the palace and judged the people of the land. 6 The rest of the acts of Azariah, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. 7 Azariah died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Jotham became king in his place.

Turmoil in Israel

8 In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Zachariah son of Jeroboam became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled six months. 9 He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD, as his fathers had done. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which led Israel to sin. 10 Shallum son of Jabesh made a plot against him. He struck him down and killed him in front of the people, and then he became king in his place. 11 The rest of the acts of Zachariah are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 12 This happened just as the the LORD had told Jehu: "Your descendants will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation." And so it was.

13 Shallum son of Jabesh became king in the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah, king of Judah. He ruled one month in Samaria. 14 Then Menahem son of Gad went up from Tirzah to Samaria. He attacked Shallum son of Jabesh there, killed him, and became king in his place. 15 The rest of the acts of Shallum, and the plot he made, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 16 Then Menahem attacked Tiphsah and everyone in it and its territory. Because they would not open the gates, he destroyed the city and tore open all the pregnant women.

17 In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Menahem son of Gad became king of Israel. He ruled ten years in Samaria. 18 He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. All his days, he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which led Israel to sin. 19 Pul, king of Assyria, came against the land. Menahem gave him a thousand talents of silver so that Pul would support him and help him keep the kingdom. 20 Menahem collected the money from all the wealthy men of Israel. He took fifty shekels of silver from each man and gave it to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria turned back and did not stay in the land. 21 The rest of the acts of Menahem, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 22 Menahem died, and his son Pekahiah became king in his place.

Pekah and the Assyrian Invasion

23 In the fiftieth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled two years. 24 He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which led Israel to sin. 25 Pekah son of Remaliah, his officer, made a plot against him. With Argob and Arieh, and fifty men from Gilead, he struck him down in the king's palace in Samaria and killed him. Then he became king in his place. 26 The rest of the acts of Pekahiah, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

27 In the fifty-second year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled twenty years. 28 He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which led Israel to sin. 29 In the days of Pekah, king of Israel, Tiglath-Pileser, king of Assyria, came and took Ijon, Abel Beth Maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, Galilee, and all the land of Naphtali. He carried the people away to Assyria. 30 Hoshea son of Elah made a plot against Pekah son of Remaliah. He struck him down and killed him, and became king in his place. This was in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah. 31 The rest of the acts of Pekah, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

Jotham Rules Judah

32 In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah became king of Judah. 33 He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jerusha, the daughter of Zadok. 34 He did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD, just as his father Uzziah had done. 35 But the high places were not taken away. The people still sacrificed and burned incense on them. He built the Upper Gate of the house of the the LORD. 36 The rest of the acts of Jotham, and all he did, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. 37 In those days the the LORD began to send Rezin, king of Aram, and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 38 Jotham died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David, his father's city. His son Ahaz became king in his place.
