Wealth Without Enjoyment

1 I have seen something evil in this life, and it weighs heavily on people. 2 God gives a person wealth, property, and honor, so that he lacks nothing he wants. Yet God does not let him enjoy it; someone else gets to enjoy it instead. That is meaningless and a painful sickness.

A Stillborn Child Is Better Off

3 A man may have a hundred children and live a very long life. Still, if he is not satisfied with good things and does not even get a proper burial, I say a stillborn child is better off than he is. 4 That child comes into the world for nothing and leaves in darkness. Its name is lost in darkness. 5 It never sees the sun or knows anything. Yet it is more at rest than he is. 6 Even if he lived two thousand years but never saw good—don’t all people end up in the same place? 7 People work hard to feed themselves, but they are never full.

The Limits of Wisdom

8 What does a wise person gain over a fool? What does a poor person gain by knowing how to live among the living? 9 It is better to enjoy what one has than to keep longing for more. That too is meaningless and pointless. 10 Everything that exists was named long ago. People know what humans are, but no one can argue with someone stronger. 11 The more there is, the more meaningless it all becomes. What good does it do a person? 12 Who knows what is good for a person during his short, meaningless life? His days pass like a shadow. Who can tell him what will happen after he is gone from this life?
