Jephthah and the Men of Ephraim

1 The men of Ephraim got together and went north. They said to Jephthah, "Why did you go to fight the Ammonites without calling us to go with you? We’ll burn your house down with you in it!"

2 Jephthah answered them, "My people and I were in a big fight with the Ammonites. I called you, but you didn't rescue me from them."

3 "When I saw that you wouldn't save me, I risked my life and went to fight the Ammonites. The LORD handed them over to me. So why are you coming to fight me now?"

4 Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought Ephraim. The men of Gilead struck them down because the Ephraimites had said, "You Gileadites are runaways living among Ephraim and Manasseh."

5 The men of Gilead captured the Jordan crossings to stop the Ephraimites. Whenever an escaped Ephraimite said, "Let me cross," the men of Gilead asked, "Are you an Ephraimite?" If he said, "No," 6 they told him, "Say 'Shibboleth.'" He said, "Sibboleth," because he couldn't say it right. Then they grabbed him and killed him at the Jordan crossing. 42,000 Ephraimites died at that time.

7 Jephthah led Israel for six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in a city in Gilead.

Other Judges of Israel

8 After him, Ibzan from Bethlehem led Israel. 9 He had thirty sons and thirty daughters. He married his daughters to men outside his family, and he brought in thirty young women from outside for his sons to marry. He led Israel for seven years.

10 Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

11 After him, Elon from Zebulun led Israel for ten years. 12 Then Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Aijalon in Zebulun.

13 After him, Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon led Israel. 14 He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, and they rode on seventy donkeys. He led Israel for eight years. 15 Then Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon died and was buried in Pirathon in Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.
