The Warrior from Edom

1 Who is this coming from Edom, from Bozrah, with clothes stained bright red? Who is this, dressed in glory, striding forward with great strength?

"It is I, speaking what is right, strong to save."

2 "Why are Your clothes red? Why do Your garments look like someone trampling grapes in a winepress?"

3 "I have trampled the winepress alone. No one from the nations helped Me. In My anger I trampled them, and in My fury I crushed them. Their blood splashed on My clothes, and I stained all My garments. 4 The day of vengeance was in My heart, and the year to rescue My people had come. 5 I looked, but no one helped. I was shocked that no one stood by Me. So My own power saved Me, and My wrath held Me up. 6 I trampled the nations in My anger. In My fury I made them drunk and poured their blood on the ground."

Remembering God's Faithful Love

7 I will tell of the the LORD's faithful love — and praise Him — for all He has done for us, for the many good things He has done for Israel, because of His mercy and His great faithful love.

8 For He said, "Surely they are My people, children who will not lie." So He became their Savior.

9 In all their distress, He too was distressed, and the angel of His presence saved them. In His love and mercy He redeemed them. He lifted them up and carried them all through the old days.

10 But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit. So He turned and became their enemy, and He Himself fought against them.

11 Then His people remembered the old days, the days of Moses: "Where is He who brought them up out of the sea with the shepherd of His flock? Where is He who put His Holy Spirit among them, 12 who sent His glorious arm to lead them by Moses' right hand, who divided the waters before them to win for Himself everlasting fame, 13 who led them through the deep like a horse in the desert, so they did not stumble? 14 Like cattle going down into the valley, the Spirit of the the LORD gave them rest. You led Your people this way to make for Yourself a glorious name."

A Prayer for Mercy

15 Look down from heaven and see, from Your holy and glorious home. Where are Your passion and Your power? Your tender love and mercy are being held back from us.

16 Yet You are our Father. Though Abraham does not know us and Israel does not recognize us, You, O the LORD, are our Father. Our Redeemer from long ago is Your name.

17 Why, O the LORD, do You let us wander from Your ways and harden our hearts so we do not fear You? Come back, for the sake of Your servants, the tribes of Your inheritance.

18 For a short time Your people owned Your holy place, but our enemies have trampled Your sanctuary.

19 We have become like people You never ruled, like people not called by Your name.
