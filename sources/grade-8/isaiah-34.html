The LORD Summons the Nations to Hear

1 Come close, nations, and listen. Listen, peoples! Let the earth hear, and everything in it. Let the world and all its people hear. 2 For the LORD is angry with all the nations and furious with all their armies. He has marked them for destruction, and He has given them over to slaughter. 3 Their dead bodies will be thrown out, and the smell of their corpses will rise. The mountains will run with their blood. 4 All the stars in the sky will rot away, and the sky will be rolled up like a scroll. Every star will fall like withered leaves from a grapevine, like leaves falling from a fig tree.

The LORD's Sword Falls on Edom

5 For My sword has drunk its fill in heaven; look, it will come down on Edom, on the people I have marked for destruction. 6 The sword of the LORD is full of blood. It is rich with fat—from the blood of lambs and goats and the fat of rams' kidneys. For the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah and a great slaughter in Edom. 7 Wild oxen will fall along with them, and young bulls with the mighty ones. Their land will be soaked with blood, and their soil will be rich with fat. 8 For the LORD has a day of revenge, a year of payback to defend Zion.

Edom Becomes a Burning Wasteland

9 The streams of Edom will turn into tar, and its dust into sulfur. The land itself will become burning tar. 10 It will burn day and night and never go out. Its smoke will rise forever. It will be ruined from one generation to the next, and no one will ever pass through it again.

Wild Animals Take Over the Ruins

11 But pelicans and porcupines will take it over. Owls and ravens will live there. He will measure it out for ruin and leave it empty and wasted. 12 There will be no one left to call it a kingdom, and all its princes will be nothing. 13 Thorns will grow in its palaces, and thistles and brambles will fill its forts. It will become a home for jackals and a place for ostriches. 14 Desert animals will meet hyenas there, and wild goats will call to each other. Even the night creature will settle there and find a place to rest. 15 There the owl will nest, lay eggs, hatch them, and gather her young in her shade. Yes, kites will gather there, each with its mate. 16 Search the book of the LORD and read it: Not one of these animals will be missing, and none will be without its mate. His mouth has commanded it, and His Spirit has gathered them. 17 He has cast lots for them, and His hand has measured out their share. They will own it forever; they will live there from generation to generation.
