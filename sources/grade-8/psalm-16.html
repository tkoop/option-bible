David Trusts in the LORD

1 A poem of David. Protect me, God, because I trust in You. 2 I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord; I have no good apart from You.”

3 As for the faithful people in the land, they are the noble ones, and I delight in them. 4 Those who chase after other gods will have more and more sorrow. I won't pour out their blood offerings or even speak their names.

5 The LORD is my share and my cup; You hold my future. 6 My boundaries fell in pleasant places; yes, I have a beautiful inheritance.

7 I will praise the LORD, who advises me; even at night my heart teaches me. 8 I always keep the LORD before me. Because He is at my right hand, I won't be shaken.

9 So my heart is glad and my whole self rejoices; even my body rests safely. 10 For You won't leave my soul in the grave, and You won't let Your Holy One decay. 11 You show me the path of life. In Your presence there is complete joy, and at Your right hand are pleasures that last forever.
