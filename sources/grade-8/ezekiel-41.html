The Outer Sanctuary and Its Entrance

1 Then the man brought me into the outer sanctuary and measured the side pillars. They were six cubits wide on one side and six cubits wide on the other. The entrance was ten cubits wide. 2 The entrance was ten cubits long, with walls five cubits thick on each side. He also measured the outer sanctuary: forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide.

3 He went farther in and measured the jambs of the entrance. They were two cubits wide. The entrance itself was six cubits wide, and each side wall was seven cubits wide. 4 Then he measured the room next to the inner sanctuary: twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. He said to me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”

The Temple Walls and Side Rooms

5 Next he measured the temple wall: six cubits thick. Each side room around the temple was four cubits wide. 6 The side rooms were stacked in three levels, with thirty rooms on each level. Ledges ran all around the temple wall to support the side rooms, so their supports would not be built into the temple wall itself.

7 The side rooms grew wider at each level because the building around the temple rose in stages. So the upper rooms were wider than the lower ones. 8 I saw that the temple had a raised base all around it. This base formed the foundation for the side rooms, and it was six cubits high, the full length of a measuring rod.

9 The outer wall of the side rooms was five cubits thick. There was an open space between the temple's side rooms and the outer chambers. 10 This open space was twenty cubits wide all around the temple. 11 The side rooms opened into this space. One door faced north and another faced south. The open space was five cubits wide all around.

The Courtyard and Surrounding Buildings

12 The building facing the temple courtyard on the west was seventy cubits wide and ninety cubits long. Its walls were five cubits thick all around. 13 Then he measured the temple: one hundred cubits long. The temple courtyard and the building with its walls were also one hundred cubits long.

14 The temple courtyard on the east, including the temple, was one hundred cubits wide. 15 He also measured the building facing the open area on the west, including its galleries on both sides: one hundred cubits. This included the inner temple and the porches of the courtyard.

Decorations, Doors, and Furnishings

16 The thresholds, the windows, and the galleries around all three sides had wood paneling all around. The floor was wood from the threshold to the windows, and the windows were covered. 17 Measurements were taken all around, from above the door to the inner sanctuary and to the outside.

18 Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the walls. A palm tree stood between each pair of cherubim, and each cherub had two faces. 19 On one side of a palm tree was a human face, and on the other side was a lion's face. This pattern covered the whole temple. 20 Cherubim and palm trees were carved from the floor to above the entrance and on the wall of the sanctuary.

21 The outer sanctuary had square doorposts. The front of the sanctuary had the same appearance as the inner sanctuary. 22 The altar was made of wood. It was three cubits high and two cubits square. Its corners, base, and sides were wood. He said to me, “This is the table that is before the LORD.”

23 The outer sanctuary and the inner sanctuary each had double doors. 24 Each door had two panels. There were two panels for one door and two for the other.

25 Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the doors of the outer sanctuary, just as they were on the walls. There was a wooden canopy over the front of the outside porch. 26 The portico had windows with frames and palm trees on both sides. The side rooms of the temple also had canopies.
