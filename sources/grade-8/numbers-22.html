Balak Sends for Balaam

1 The Israelites traveled and camped in the plains of Moab near the Jordan River, across from Jericho. 2 Balak son of Zippor saw everything Israel had done to the Amorites. 3 Moab was terrified of the people because there were so many of them. Moab dreaded the Israelites.

4 Moab said to the elders of Midian, "This crowd will eat up everything around us, like an ox eats grass in a field." Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.

5 He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, by the Euphrates, in the land of his people. He said, "Look, a people has come out of Egypt. They cover the whole land and are living right across from me." 6 "Please come and curse this people for me, because they are too strong for me. Maybe I can defeat them and drive them out of the land. I know that whoever you bless is blessed, and whoever you curse is cursed."

7 So the elders of Moab and Midian went with money for fortune-telling in their hands. They came to Balaam and gave him Balak's message. 8 Balaam said to them, "Stay here tonight, and I will bring back to you whatever the LORD says to me." So Moab's leaders stayed with Balaam.

God Warns Balaam

9 That night God came to Balaam and asked, "Who are these men with you?"

10 Balaam said to God, "Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, sent them to me. 11 He said, 'Look, a people has come out of Egypt and covers the whole land. Come now and curse them for me. Maybe I can fight them and drive them out.'"

12 God said to Balaam, "Do not go with them. You must not curse the people, because they are blessed."

13 In the morning Balaam got up and said to Balak's messengers, "Go back to your land, because the LORD has refused to let me go with you."

14 Moab's leaders got up, went to Balak, and said, "Balaam refused to come with us."

15 Balak sent more officials, and they were more numerous and more important than the first ones. 16 They came to Balaam and said, "This is what Balak son of Zippor says: 'Please do not refuse to come to me. 17 I will honor you greatly, and I will do whatever you say. Please come and curse this people for me.'"

18 Balaam answered Balak's servants, "Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the word of the LORD my God and do anything small or great. 19 Now stay here tonight too, so I can find out what more the LORD will say to me."

20 That night God came to Balaam and said, "If the men have come to call you, get up and go with them. But do only what I tell you."

21 In the morning Balaam got up, saddled his donkey, and went with Moab's leaders.

The Donkey and the Angel

22 But God became angry because Balaam was going. The Angel of the LORD stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam was riding his donkey, and his two servants were with him. 23 The donkey saw the Angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in His hand. She turned off the road and went into a field. So Balaam beat the donkey to guide her back onto the road.

24 Then the Angel of the LORD stood in a narrow path between the vineyards, with a wall on each side. 25 The donkey saw the Angel of the LORD and pressed against the wall, crushing Balaam's foot against it. So he beat her again.

26 Then the Angel of the LORD went ahead and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to turn right or left. 27 The donkey saw the Angel of the LORD and lay down under Balaam. Balaam became angry and beat the donkey with his staff.

28 Then the LORD opened the donkey's mouth, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you that you have beaten me these three times?"

29 Balaam said to the donkey, "Because you have made a fool of me! If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now."

30 The donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey, the one you have ridden all your life? Have I ever done this to you before?"

"No," he said.

31 Then the LORD opened Balaam's eyes, and he saw the Angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in His hand. So Balaam bowed low and fell facedown.

32 The Angel of the LORD said to him, "Why have you beaten your donkey three times? Look, I came out to oppose you because your way is wrong before Me. 33 The donkey saw Me and turned away from Me three times. If she had not turned away, I would have killed you by now, but I would have spared her."

34 Balaam said to the Angel of the LORD, "I have sinned. I did not know you were standing in the road to meet me. Now, if it is wrong in your eyes, I will go back."

35 The Angel of the LORD said to Balaam, "Go with the men, but speak only the word I give you." So Balaam went with Balak's officials.

Balaam Meets Balak

36 When Balak heard that Balaam was coming, he went out to meet him at a Moabite city on the Arnon border, at the edge of his territory. 37 Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not send for you again and again? Why didn't you come to me? Am I really not able to reward you?"

38 Balaam said to Balak, "Look, I have come to you now. But can I say anything on my own? I must speak only the word God puts in my mouth." 39 Then Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.

40 Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep and sent some to Balaam and the officials who were with him. 41 In the morning Balak took Balaam up to Bamoth-baal. From there Balaam could see the edge of the Israelite camp.
