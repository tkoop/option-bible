David Prays for Help Against His Enemies

1 The LORD, fight those who fight me; stand against those who attack me. 2 Take up Your shield and armor, and rise to help me. 3 Draw out Your spear and stop those who chase me. Say to my soul, ‘I am the One who saves you.’

4 Let those who want to kill me be ashamed and disgraced. Let those who plan to hurt me turn back in fear. 5 Let them be like dry husks blown by the wind, while the angel of the LORD drives them away. 6 Let their road be dark and slippery, while the angel of the LORD chases them.

7 For no reason, they hid a net for me. For no reason, they dug a pit to trap me. 8 Let ruin hit them by surprise. Let the net they hid catch them. Let them fall into their own pit and be destroyed.

9 Then I will be glad in the LORD. I will rejoice in His help. 10 My whole being will say, “the LORD, who is like You? You save the poor from those too strong for them. You save the poor and needy from those who rob them.”

11 Lying witnesses stand up and ask me things I do not know. 12 They pay me back with evil for the good I did, and they crush my spirit.

13 But when they were sick, I wore sackcloth and went without food to humble myself. I prayed for them, and my prayers came back to me unanswered. 14 I walked around as if they were my friends or brothers. I bowed down in sorrow as if I were mourning my mother.

15 But when I stumbled, they were happy and gathered together. Strangers I did not know attacked me. They lied about me without stopping. 16 Like people who mock others at a party, they ground their teeth at me.

17 How long, O Lord, will You just watch? Rescue my life from their attacks. Save my one life from these lions. 18 Then I will thank You in the great gathering. I will praise You among many people.

19 Do not let my enemies be glad over me for no reason. Do not let those who hate me without cause wink at me in mockery. 20 They do not speak peace. They plan lies against quiet people in the land. 21 They open their mouths wide against me and say, “Aha! Aha! Our eyes have seen it!”

22 You have seen this, the LORD. Do not stay silent. Lord, do not stay far from me. 23 Wake up and rise to defend my case. My God and my Lord, take up my cause. 24 Judge me by Your righteousness, the LORD my God. Do not let them be glad over me.

25 Do not let them say in their hearts, “Aha! We have swallowed him up!” Do not let them say, “We have eaten him alive!” 26 Let all who are glad when I suffer be ashamed and confused. Let those who brag about me be covered with shame and disgrace.

27 Let those who support my right cause shout with joy and be glad. Let them always say, “The LORD is great! He is happy with the peace of His servant.” 28 Then my tongue will talk about Your righteousness and praise You all day long.
