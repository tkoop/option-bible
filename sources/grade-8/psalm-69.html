A Prayer for Rescue from Deep Trouble

1 Save me, God, because the water has risen to my neck. 2 I sink in deep mud where there is no footing. I have gone into deep water, and the flood sweeps over me. 3 I am tired from crying out; my throat is dry. My eyes grow weak as I look for my God.

4 Those who hate me for no reason are more than the hairs on my head. Many enemies attack me with lies, and they force me to pay back what I did not steal. 5 You know my foolishness, God, and my guilt is not hidden from You. 6 May those who hope in You, O Lord, the LORD of Hosts, not be put to shame because of me. May those who seek You, God of Israel, not be disgraced because of me.

7 I have carried shame for Your sake, and shame has covered my face. 8 I have become a stranger to my brothers, a foreigner to my mother's sons. 9 Zeal for Your house has eaten me up, and the insults of those who insult You have fallen on me.

10 When I cried and fasted, they laughed at me. 11 When I wore sackcloth, I became a joke to them. 12 People sitting at the gate mock me, and drunkards sing about me.

13 But my prayer is to You, O the LORD, at a time You choose to show favor. In Your great faithful love, God, answer me with the truth of Your saving help. 14 Save me from the mud; do not let me sink. Rescue me from my enemies and from the deep water. 15 Do not let the flood sweep me away, or the deep water swallow me. Do not let the pit close its mouth over me.

16 Answer me, O the LORD, because Your faithful love is good. Turn to me because of Your great mercy. 17 Do not hide Your face from Your servant. Answer me quickly, because I am in trouble. 18 Come close to my soul and redeem it. Buy me back because of my enemies.

19 You know my shame and disgrace; all my enemies are before You. 20 Shame has broken my heart, and I am worn out. I looked for pity, but found none. I looked for comfort, but found no one. 21 They gave me bitter food and vinegar to drink when I was thirsty.

22 May their table become a trap, a snare for their friends. 23 May their eyes grow dark so they cannot see, and may their backs always shake. 24 Pour out Your anger on them, and let Your burning wrath catch up with them.

25 May their camp be empty, with no one living in their tents. 26 They chase the ones You have struck, and they talk about the pain of those You have wounded. 27 Add their sin to their sin, and do not let them share in Your goodness. 28 May they be erased from the book of life and not be written with those who do right.

29 I am poor and in pain. May Your saving help, God, keep me safe. 30 I will praise God's name with a song and honor Him with thanks. 31 That will please the LORD more than an ox, more than a bull with horns and hooves.

32 The humble will see it and be glad. You who seek God, let your hearts live. 33 For the LORD hears those in need and does not look down on His prisoners. 34 Let heaven and earth praise Him, and the seas and everything that moves in them. 35 For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah, so people can live there and own it. 36 The children of His servants will receive it, and those who love His name will live there.
