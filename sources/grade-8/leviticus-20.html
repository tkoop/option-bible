Punishment for Child Sacrifice

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. 2 He said, "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: Any Israelite or foreigner living in Israel who gives one of his children to Molech must be put to death. The people of the land must stone him.

3 "I will turn My face against that man and cut him off from his people, because he gave his child to Molech. He made My holy place unclean and treated My holy name with disrespect.

4 "If the people of the land ignore him and do not put him to death when he gives his child to Molech, 5 I will turn My face against that man and his family. I will cut them off from their people. That includes him and everyone who follows him by worshiping Molech.

A Call to Holiness

6 "I will turn My face against anyone who turns to people who talk to spirits and gives himself to them. I will cut that person off from his people.

7 "Set yourselves apart and be holy, because I am the LORD your God. 8 Keep My rules and follow them. I am the LORD, who makes you holy.

Punishments for Sexual Sins

9 "Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death. He cursed his father or mother, so his death is his own fault. 10 If a man sleeps with another man's wife, both of them must be put to death.

11 "If a man sleeps with his father's wife, he has dishonored his father. Both of them must be put to death. They are responsible for their own deaths. 12 If a man sleeps with his daughter-in-law, both of them must be put to death. What they did is a perversion. They are responsible for their own deaths. 13 If a man sleeps with a man as with a woman, both of them have done a hateful thing. They must be put to death. They are responsible for their own deaths.

14 "If a man marries a woman and her mother, it is wicked. He and the two women must be burned in fire, so that wickedness will not be among you. 15 If a man has sex with an animal, he must be put to death, and you must kill the animal. 16 If a woman goes near an animal to have sex with it, kill both the woman and the animal. They must be put to death. They are responsible for their own deaths.

17 "If a man marries his sister, the daughter of either his father or his mother, and they have sex, it is a disgrace. They must be removed from their people in front of everyone. He has dishonored his sister and will answer for it. 18 If a man has sex with a woman during her monthly period, he has exposed the source of her bleeding, and she has uncovered it too. Both of them must be cut off from their people.

19 "Do not have sex with your mother's sister or your father's sister. That would dishonor a close relative, and both people involved would be held responsible. 20 If a man has sex with his aunt, he dishonors his uncle. They will be held responsible and will die without children. 21 If a man marries his brother's wife, it is unclean. He has dishonored his brother. They will have no children.

Living in the Promised Land

22 "Keep all My rules and laws and follow them. Then the land I am bringing you to live in will not throw you out. 23 Do not follow the customs of the nations I am driving out before you. They did all these things, and I was disgusted by them.

24 "But I said to you, 'You will take their land. I will give it to you to own. It is a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God. I set you apart from the nations.'

25 "So you must tell the difference between clean and unclean animals and between unclean and clean birds. Do not make yourselves unclean by any animal or bird or anything that crawls on the ground. I have set those apart as unclean for you. 26 You are to be holy to Me, because I, the LORD, am holy. I have set you apart from the nations to be Mine.

27 "A man or woman among you who is a medium or spiritist must be put to death. You must stone them. They are responsible for their own deaths."
