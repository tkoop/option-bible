David's Vow to Find a Home for the the LORD

1 The LORD, remember David and all his hardships. 2 He swore an oath to the the LORD; he made a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob:

3 "I will not go into my house or go to bed; 4 I will not let my eyes sleep or my eyelids close 5 until I find a place for the the LORD, a home for the Mighty One of Jacob."

The People Worship at the the LORD's Footstool

6 We heard about it in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of Jaar. 7 Let us go to His dwelling; let us worship at His footstool. 8 Arise, the LORD, and go to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. 9 May Your priests be clothed with righteousness, and may Your faithful people shout for joy. 10 For the sake of Your servant David, do not reject Your anointed king.

The LORD's Promise to David and Zion

11 The the LORD made a faithful promise to David, and He will not break it: "I will place one of your descendants on your throne. 12 If your sons keep My covenant and the teachings I give them, their sons will also sit on your throne forever."

13 For the the LORD chose Zion; He wanted it for His home.

14 "This is My resting place forever; here I will live, because I wanted it. 15 I will richly bless her food; I will give her poor people enough bread. 16 I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her faithful people will shout for joy. 17 There I will make David's family strong; I have prepared a light for My anointed. 18 I will make his enemies ashamed, but his crown will flourish."
