1 The word of the LORD came to me:

God's Judgment on Ammon

2 "Son of Man, set your face against the Ammonites and prophesy against them.

3 "Say to the Ammonites: 'Hear the word of the Lord the LORD. This is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you said, "Aha!" against My sanctuary when it was ruined, against the land of Israel when it was desolate, and against the house of Judah when they went into exile,

4 "therefore I will hand you over to the people of the East as a possession. They will set up their camps among you and pitch their tents among you. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.

5 "I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels and Ammon a resting place for sheep. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'

6 "For this is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Because you clapped your hands and stomped your feet and rejoiced over the land of Israel with all your soul and all your contempt,

7 "therefore I will stretch out My hand against you and give you over to the nations as plunder. I will cut you off from the peoples and wipe you out of the countries. I will destroy you, and you will know that I am the LORD.'

God's Judgment on Moab and Seir

8 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Because Moab and Seir said, "Look, the house of Judah is like all the other nations,"

9 "therefore I will open up Moab's side, starting at its border cities—Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim—the glory of the land.

10 "I will give it, along with the Ammonites, to the people of the East as a possession, so that the Ammonites will not be remembered among the nations.

11 "So I will carry out judgments on Moab, and they will know that I am the LORD.'

God's Judgment on Edom

12 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Because Edom took revenge on the house of Judah and became very guilty by taking revenge,

13 "therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: I will stretch out My hand against Edom and cut off from it both people and animals. I will make it a wasteland from Teman to Dedan; they will fall by the sword.

14 "I will take vengeance on Edom through My people Israel. They will deal with Edom according to My anger and wrath, and they will know My vengeance, declares the Lord the LORD.'

God's Judgment on the Philistines

15 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Because the Philistines acted in revenge and took vengeance with malice, to destroy them with ancient hatred,

16 "therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: I will stretch out My hand against the Philistines, cut off the Cherethites, and destroy the rest of the coast.

17 "I will carry out great vengeance against them with furious rebukes. Then they will know that I am the LORD when I take vengeance on them.' "
