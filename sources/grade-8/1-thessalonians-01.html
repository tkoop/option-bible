Greetings and Thanks

1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write to the church in Thessalonica, which belongs to God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. Grace and peace to you. 2 We always thank God for all of you. We mention you in our prayers. 3 Before God our Father, we never stop remembering your work of faith, your hard work of love, and your steady hope in our Lord Jesus Christ. 4 Brothers loved by God, we know that He chose you.

The Power of the Gospel

5 Our good news came to you not only with words, but also with power, with the Holy Spirit, and with strong confidence. You know what kind of people we were among you while we were there for your good. 6 You became followers of us and of the Lord. You received the message with joy from the Holy Spirit, even while you suffered a lot.

An Example to Others

7 So you became an example to all believers in Macedonia and Achaia. 8 The Lord's message spread out from you not only in Macedonia and Achaia. Your faith in God has become known everywhere. So we do not need to say anything.

Turning to the True God

9 People everywhere tell how warmly you welcomed us. They tell how you turned away from idols to serve the living and true God. 10 They also tell how you are waiting for His Son to come from heaven. God raised Him from the dead. His Son is Jesus, and He saves us from the coming wrath.
