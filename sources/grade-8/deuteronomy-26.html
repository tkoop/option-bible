Bringing the Firstfruits to the LORD

1 When you come into the land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and live in it, 2 you shall take some of the firstfruits of all the produce of the ground from your land that the LORD your God gives you, and put it in a basket. Then you shall go to the place the LORD your God chooses to make His name dwell. 3 You shall go to the priest who is in office in those days and say, “I declare today to the LORD your God that I have come into the land the LORD swore to our fathers to give us.” 4 The priest shall take the basket from your hand and set it before the altar of the LORD your God.

5 Then you shall answer and say before the LORD your God, “My father was a wandering Aramean. He went down into Egypt with only a few people and lived there, and there he became a great nation, mighty and numerous. 6 The Egyptians treated us badly and oppressed us, and they put hard work on us. 7 We cried out to the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD heard our voice and saw our suffering, our hard work, and our oppression. 8 The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, and with signs and wonders. 9 He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey. 10 Now look, I have brought the firstfruits of the produce of the ground that the LORD gave me. I set it before the LORD your God and bow down before the LORD your God.”

11 You and the Levite and the foreigner among you shall rejoice in all the good things the LORD your God has given to you and your household.

The Third-Year Tithe

12 When you have finished setting aside a tenth of all your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, you shall give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so they can eat and be full in your towns.

13 Then you shall say before the LORD your God, “I have removed the holy portion from my house, and I have also given it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, just as You commanded me. I have not broken Your commands or forgotten them. 14 I have not eaten any of it while mourning, and I have not removed any of it while unclean, and I have not given any of it for the dead. I have obeyed the voice of the LORD my God. I have done everything You commanded me. 15 Look down from Your holy dwelling, from heaven, and bless Your people Israel and the land You gave us, as You promised our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.”

Israel's Covenant with the LORD

16 Today the LORD your God commands you to follow these statutes and judgments. Keep them and do them with all your heart and all your soul. 17 Today you have declared that the LORD is your God, and that you will walk in His ways, keep His statutes, His commands, and His judgments, and listen to His voice. 18 Today the LORD has declared that you are His people, His treasured possession, as He said, and that you are to keep all His commands. 19 He will make you higher than all the nations He has made, for praise, for a name, and for honor, and you will be a holy people to the LORD your God, as He said.
