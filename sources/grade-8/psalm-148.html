Everything in Heaven Praises the LORD

1Hallelujah! Praise the the LORD from the heavens; praise Him in the heights. 2Praise Him, all His angels; praise Him, all His heavenly hosts. 3Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, all you shining stars. 4Praise Him, highest heavens, and you waters above the skies. 5Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for He commanded and they were created. 6He established them forever and ever; He issued a decree that will never pass away.

Everything on Earth Praises the LORD

7Praise the the LORD from the earth, all great sea creatures and ocean depths, 8lightning and hail, snow and clouds, powerful wind fulfilling His word, 9mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars, 10wild animals and all cattle, crawling creatures and flying birds, 11kings of the earth and all nations, princes and all rulers of the earth, 12young men and maidens, old and young alike.

God Is Exalted Above All

13Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for His name alone is exalted; His splendor is above the earth and the heavens. 14He has given strength to His people, praise for all His saints, for the Israelites, a people near to Him. Hallelujah!
