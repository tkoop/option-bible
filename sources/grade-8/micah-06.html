The LORD's Case Against Israel

1 Hear what the LORD says: "Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice."

2 Hear, O mountains, the LORD's case, and you lasting foundations of the earth. For the LORD has a case against His people, and He will contend with Israel.

3 "My people, what have I done to you? How have I worn you out? Answer Me!

4 "For I brought you up from the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery. I sent Moses before you, along with Aaron and Miriam.

5 "My people, remember what Balak king of Moab planned and what Balaam son of Beor answered him. Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal, so you may know the LORD's righteous acts."

What the LORD Wants from His People

6 "With what shall I come before the LORD when I bow before the God on high? Should I come to Him with burnt offerings, with calves a year old?

7 "Would the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousand rivers of oil? Shall I give my firstborn for my sin, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?"

8 He has shown you, people, what is good. And what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?

The City's Wickedness

9 The voice of the LORD calls to the city (and wisdom fears Your name): "Heed the rod and the One who ordained it.

10 "Are there still treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and a short measure that is cursed?

11 "Can I clear the man with wicked scales and a bag of false weights?

12 "For her rich men are full of violence; her residents speak lies, and their tongues are deceitful in their mouths.

Punishment for Israel

13 "Therefore I am striking you with a severe blow, bringing ruin on you because of your sins.

14 "You will eat but not be satisfied, and your hunger will stay inside you. You will store things up but not save them, and what you save, I will give to the sword.

15 "You will sow but not reap; you will press olives but not anoint yourselves with oil; you will tread grapes but not drink wine.

16 "You have kept the statutes of Omri and all the practices of the house of Ahab; you have followed their advice. Therefore I will make you a ruin, and your people an object of scorn; you will bear the shame of My people."
