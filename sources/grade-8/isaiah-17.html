An Oracle Against Damascus

1 This is a message about Damascus: Look, Damascus will stop being a city and will become a pile of ruins. 2 The cities of Aroer will be left empty. They will be places where flocks rest, and no one will scare them away. 3 The fortified city will be gone from Ephraim, and the kingdom will be gone from Damascus. The rest of Aram will be like Israel's glory, says the LORD of Hosts.

Israel's Glory Will Fade

4 In that day, Jacob's glory will fade, and his body will grow thin. 5 It will be like a worker cutting grain and gathering the heads with his arm. It will be like someone picking grain heads in the Valley of Rephaim. 6 Only a few olives will be left, like olives left after beating a tree—two or three on the top branch, four or five on the fruitful branches, says the LORD, the God of Israel.

People Will Turn to Their Maker

7 In that day, people will look to their Maker, and their eyes will see the Holy One of Israel. 8 They will not look to the altars their hands made, and they will not respect what their fingers made—the Asherah poles and the incense altars. 9 In that day, their strong cities will be like a deserted forest and a leftover high branch, left behind when the Israelites came through. The land will be a wasteland.

A Warning About Forgetting God

10 You forgot the God who saves you, and you did not remember the Rock who protects you. So you plant pleasant plants and sow vines from other lands. 11 On the day you plant them, they grow fast, and in the morning your seed blooms. But the harvest will be gone in a day of sickness and deep pain.

The Nations Will Be Scattered

12 How terrible for the many people who roar like the sea! How terrible for the rushing nations that rush like mighty waters! 13 The nations rush like many waters, but the LORD will warn them, and they will run far away. They will be chased like chaff on a hill before the wind, like a tumbleweed before a storm. 14 In the evening, trouble comes. By morning, it is gone. That is what happens to those who rob us and take our things.
