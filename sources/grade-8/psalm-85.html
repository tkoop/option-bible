A Prayer for God’s Favor

1 The LORD showed favor to Your land, and You restored Jacob from captivity. 2 You forgave the sin of Your people and covered all their wrongs. Selah 3 You set aside all Your anger and turned from Your burning rage.

4 Restore us, O God of our salvation, and put away Your anger toward us. 5 Will You stay angry with us forever? Will You let Your wrath last for every generation? 6 Won’t You give us life again so Your people can rejoice in You? 7 Show us Your faithful love, O the LORD, and grant us Your salvation.

God Promises Peace

8 I will listen to what God the LORD says, because He promises peace to His people and to His faithful followers, so they won’t turn back to foolish ways. 9 Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, so His glory can live in our land. 10 Faithful love and truth will meet; righteousness and peace will kiss. 11 Truth will grow from the earth, and righteousness will look down from heaven.

The LORD Will Bless the Land

12 The LORD will surely give what is good, and our land will produce its crops. 13 Righteousness will walk before Him and make a path for His steps.
