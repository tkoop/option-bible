A Prayer Against Israel's Enemies

1 A song. A psalm of Asaph. God, do not stay silent. Do not be quiet, and do not be still, O God. 2 See, Your enemies make an uproar, and those who hate You lift their heads in pride. 3 They plot secretly against Your people and make plans against those You protect.

4 They say, "Come, let us destroy them as a nation, so that Israel's name is forgotten forever."

Enemies Unite Against God

5 Yes, they are united in purpose. They make an alliance against You: 6 the people of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, 7 Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek, Philistia with the people of Tyre. 8 Even Assyria has joined them; they have become a powerful ally for the descendants of Lot. Selah.

Remembering God's Past Victories

9 Treat them as You treated Midian, Sisera, and Jabin at the Kishon River. 10 They were destroyed at Endor and became like dung on the ground. 11 Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their rulers like Zebah and Zalmunna.

12 They said, "Let us take God's lands for ourselves."

A Prayer for God's Judgment

13 Make them like tumbleweed, my God, like chaff blown by the wind. 14 Like fire burning through a forest, like flames setting mountains ablaze. 15 Chase them with Your storm and terrify them with Your windstorm.

16 Fill their faces with shame so that they will seek Your name, O the LORD. 17 Let them be ashamed and terrified forever; let them be disgraced and destroyed. 18 Let them know that You alone, whose name is the LORD, are the Most High over all the earth.
