The LORD Restores His People and Judges the Nations

1 "For look, in those days and at that time, when I bring Judah and Jerusalem back from captivity, 2 I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will judge them for what they did to My people, My very own Israel, whom they scattered among the nations, and to My land, which they divided.

3 "They gambled for My people. They sold a boy for a prostitute and traded a girl for wine to drink. 4 Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia, what do you have against Me? Are you trying to pay Me back? If you are, I will quickly bring that trouble back on your own heads.

5 "You took My silver and gold and carried My finest treasures into your temples. 6 You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, to send them far from their homeland.

7 "Now I will wake them up from the place where you sold them, and I will pay you back on your own heads. 8 I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation. For the LORD has spoken."

The Nations Are Called to War

9 "Proclaim this among the nations: Get ready for war! Wake up the mighty men! Let all the soldiers step forward and get ready to fight.

10 "Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears. Let the weak say,

"'I am strong.'

11 "Hurry and come, all you nations from every side, and gather there. Bring down Your mighty ones, O the LORD. 12 Let the nations wake up and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations. 13 Swing the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Come and trample the grapes, for the winepress is full and the vats overflow, because their evil is great."

The Day of the LORD

14 "Multitudes, multitudes in the Valley of Decision! For the day of the LORD is near in the Valley of Decision. 15 The sun and moon will grow dark, and the stars will stop shining.

16 "The LORD will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem. The heavens and the earth will shake, but the LORD will be a safe place for His people and a strong tower for the people of Israel."

The LORD Blesses His People

17 "Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who lives in Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy, and no foreigner will ever pass through it again. 18 In that day, the mountains will drip with sweet wine, the hills will flow with milk, and all the streambeds of Judah will run with water. A spring will flow from the house of the LORD and water the Valley of Acacias.

19 "Egypt will become a wasteland and Edom a dry desert, because of the violence done to the people of Judah, in whose land they spilled innocent blood. 20 But Judah will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation. 21 I will avenge their blood, which I have not yet avenged, for the LORD lives in Zion."
