Abraham's Later Family

1 Abraham took another wife, named Keturah. 2 She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. 3 Jokshan was the father of Sheba and Dedan. Dedan's sons were Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim. 4 Midian's sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were sons of Keturah. 5 Abraham gave everything he owned to Isaac. 6 While he was still alive, he gave gifts to the sons of his concubines and sent them away from his son Isaac, eastward to the east country.

Abraham's Death

7 Abraham lived 175 years. 8 He breathed his last and died at a good old age, old and full of years. Then he was gathered to his people. 9 His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, east of Mamre. 10 Abraham had bought that field from the Hittites. There he buried his wife Sarah. 11 After Abraham's death, God blessed his son Isaac. Isaac was living near Beer-lahai-roi.

Ishmael's Descendants

12 These are the family lines of Abraham's son Ishmael, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's slave, bore to Abraham. 13 These are the names of Ishmael's sons, listed in the order of their birth: Nebaioth, the firstborn, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, 14 Mishma, Dumah, Massa, 15 Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. 16 These were Ishmael's sons, and these are their names by their villages and camps. They were twelve leaders of their tribes. 17 Ishmael lived 137 years. He breathed his last and died, and he was gathered to his people. 18 His descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, east of Egypt, as you travel toward Assyria. He died among all his relatives.

Isaac's Family and the Twins

19 This is the family history of Isaac, Abraham's son. Abraham was Isaac's father. 20 Isaac was forty years old when he married Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan-aram and sister of Laban the Aramean. 21 Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife, because she could not have children. The LORD answered his prayer, and his wife Rebekah became pregnant. 22 The babies struggled inside her. She said, "If this is so, why did I get pregnant?" So she went to ask the LORD. 23 The LORD said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples will be separated from your body. One people will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger." 24 When the time came for her to give birth, there were twins in her womb. 25 The first came out red and hairy all over, like a garment. They named him Esau. 26 After that, his brother came out with his hand holding Esau's heel. They named him Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when they were born.

27 The boys grew up. Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the open country. Jacob was a quiet man who stayed among the tents. 28 Isaac loved Esau because he brought him game. Rebekah loved Jacob.

29 Once Jacob was cooking stew. Esau came in from the field, and he was exhausted. 30 Esau said to Jacob, "Let me eat some of that red stew, because I am exhausted!" That is why he was called Edom.

31 Jacob said, "Sell me your birthright today."

32 Esau said, "Look, I am about to die. What good is the birthright to me?"

33 Jacob said, "Swear to me today." So Esau swore and sold his birthright to Jacob. 34 Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. Esau ate and drank, got up, and went away. So Esau treated his birthright as if it meant nothing.
