Rehoboam Turns Back from War

1When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he gathered the men of Judah and Benjamin—180,000 chosen fighting men—to fight against Israel and win the kingdom back for Rehoboam. 2But the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God: 3“Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, 4‘This is what the LORD says: Do not go up to fight against your brothers. Each of you go home, for this is My doing.’” So they obeyed the words of the LORD and turned back from marching against Jeroboam.

Rehoboam Strengthens Fortified Cities

5Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built cities for defense in Judah. 6He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, 7Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam, 8Gath, Mareshah, Ziph, 9Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah, 10Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron. These were fortified cities in Judah and Benjamin. 11He strengthened their defenses and put commanders in them, with supplies of food, olive oil, and wine. 12He put shields and spears in every city and made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin were his.

Priests and Levites Come to Judah

13The priests and Levites from all over Israel came to him. 14The Levites left their pasturelands and property and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them as priests of the LORD. 15Jeroboam appointed his own priests for the high places and for the goat and calf idols he had made. 16Those from every tribe of Israel who were determined to seek the LORD, the God of Israel, followed the Levites to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their ancestors. 17They strengthened the kingdom of Judah and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, following the ways of David and Solomon.

Rehoboam's Family

18Rehoboam married Mahalath, who was the daughter of Jerimoth son of David and of Abihail, the daughter of Eliab son of Jesse. 19She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. 20He later married Maakah daughter of Absalom, who bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith. 21Rehoboam loved Maakah daughter of Absalom more than all his other wives and concubines. He had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters. 22Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maakah as chief prince among his brothers, to make him king. 23He acted wisely by sending some of his sons throughout the districts of Judah and Benjamin, and to all the fortified cities. He gave them abundant provisions and took many wives for them.
