The Ruined Linen Belt

1 The LORD said to me, "Go buy a linen belt and put it around your waist, but do not put it in water." 2 So I bought the belt, just as the the LORD had said, and put it around my waist.

3 Then the word of the the LORD came to me again. 4 "Take the belt you bought, the one around your waist. Go at once to the Euphrates and hide it in a crack in the rocks." 5 So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, just as the the LORD had commanded me.

6 After many days the the LORD said to me, "Get up and go to the Euphrates. Take from there the belt I told you to hide." 7 So I went to the Euphrates and dug up the belt. I took it from the place where I had hidden it. But the belt was ruined; it was good for nothing.

Judah's Pride Will Be Ruined

8 Then the word of the the LORD came to me. 9 "This is what the the LORD says: I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem in the same way. 10 These wicked people refuse to listen to My words. They follow the stubbornness of their hearts and go after other gods to serve and worship them. They will be like this belt—useless. 11 Just as a belt clings to a man's waist, so I made the whole family of Israel and the whole family of Judah cling to Me. I did this so they would be My people and bring Me fame, praise, and glory. But they would not listen."

The Warning of the Wineskins

12 Say to them, "This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Every wineskin will be filled with wine." If they say to you, "Don't we already know that every wineskin will be filled with wine?" 13 then tell them, "This is what the the LORD says: I am going to fill with drunkenness everyone who lives in this land—the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the people of Jerusalem." 14 The the LORD declares, "I will smash them against each other, fathers and sons alike. I will not pity or spare or show mercy. I will destroy them."

A Call to Repent

15 Listen and pay attention. Do not be proud, for the the LORD has spoken. 16 Give glory to the the LORD your God before He brings darkness, and before your feet stumble on the dark mountains. While you wait for light, He turns it into deep darkness. 17 But if you will not listen, I will weep in secret because of your pride. My eyes will weep bitterly and stream with tears, because the the LORD's flock will be taken captive.

18 Tell the king and the queen mother, "Come down and sit in a low place, for your glorious crowns have fallen from your heads." 19 The cities of the south will be shut up, with no one to open them. All Judah will be taken away captive, completely.

20 Lift up your eyes and see those coming from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, your beautiful sheep? 21 What will you say when He brings rulers over you? You yourself taught them to rule over you. Will not pains seize you like a woman in labor? 22 And if you say in your heart, "Why is this happening to me?" it is because of your many sins. Your skirts have been stripped off and your heels exposed. 23 Can an Ethiopian change his skin, or a leopard its spots? Neither can you do good, you who are used to doing evil.

24 I will scatter them like straw blown by the desert wind. 25 This is your lot, the portion I have assigned to you, declares the the LORD, because you have forgotten Me and trusted in lies. 26 I will pull your skirts up over your face, so your shame is seen. 27 I have seen your adulteries and your lustful neighings, your shameless acts on the hills and in the fields. I have seen your disgusting acts. Woe to you, Jerusalem! How long will you remain unclean?
