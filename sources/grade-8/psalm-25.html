David's Prayer for Guidance and Forgiveness

1 To You, the LORD, I give my whole self. 2 My God, I trust in You. Do not let me be disgraced. Do not let my enemies win over me. 3 No one who hopes in You will be disgraced. But those who betray others for no reason will be disgraced.

4 Show me Your ways, the LORD. Teach me Your paths. 5 Lead me in Your truth and teach me. You are the God who saves me. I hope in You all day long. 6 Remember Your mercy and faithful love, the LORD. You have shown them since long ago. 7 Do not remember the sins of my youth or my rebellious acts. Remember me with Your faithful love, the LORD, because of Your goodness.

8 The LORD is good and right. So He teaches sinners the right path. 9 He guides humble people in what is right and teaches them His way. 10 All the the LORD's paths are faithful love and truth for those who keep His agreement and His commands. 11 For Your name's sake, the LORD, forgive my guilt, because it is great.

12 Who is the person who honors the the LORD? The LORD will show him the path to choose. 13 He will live well, and his children will inherit the land. 14 The LORD is a friend to those who honor Him, and He shows them His agreement. 15 My eyes are always on the the LORD, because He will pull my feet out of the trap.

16 Turn to me and be kind to me. I am alone and suffering. 17 My heart's troubles are growing worse. Bring me out of my distress. 18 Look at my suffering and my pain, and forgive all my sins. 19 See how many enemies I have and how cruelly they hate me. 20 Guard my life and rescue me. Do not let me be disgraced, because I run to You for safety. 21 May honesty and right living protect me, because I hope in You.

22 Rescue Israel, God, from all its troubles.
