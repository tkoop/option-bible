God's Love for Israel

1 When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called My son. 2 The more they were called, the more they turned away from Me. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to idols. 3 It was I who taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by the arms, but they did not know it was I who healed them. 4 I led them with cords of human kindness and with ropes of love. I lifted the yoke from their jaws and bent down to feed them.

Israel's Coming Judgment

5 Will they not return to Egypt, and will Assyria not rule over them because they refuse to repent? 6 A sword will sweep through their cities. It will destroy their defenses and consume them because of their own plans. 7 My people are set on turning away from Me. Even when they call to the Most High, He will not lift them up.

God's Compassion for His People

8 How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart is torn within Me; all My compassion is stirred. 9 I will not carry out My fierce anger, nor will I destroy Ephraim again. For I am God, and not a man—the Holy One among you. I will not come against their cities.

Restoration of Israel

10 They will follow the the LORD; He will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west. 11 They will come trembling like birds from Egypt, like doves from Assyria. I will settle them in their homes, declares the the LORD. 12 Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, and the house of Israel surrounds Me with deceit. Judah is still unruly toward God, even toward the faithful Holy One.
