Ezekiel Acts Out the People's Exile

1 The word of the LORD came to me: 2 “Son of man, you live among a rebellious house. They have eyes to see, but they do not see. They have ears to hear, but they do not hear, because they are a rebellious house.”

3 “As for you, Son of man, pack a bag for exile. Go into exile by day while they watch. Travel from your place to another place while they watch. Maybe they will notice, even though they are a rebellious house. 4 Bring out your baggage by day while they watch, like baggage for exile. Then go out in the evening while they watch, like people who must go into exile. 5 While they watch, dig through the wall and bring your baggage out through it. 6 While they watch, lift the baggage onto your shoulder and carry it out at dusk. Cover your face so you cannot see the land, because I have made you a sign for the house of Israel.”

7 I did just as I was told. I brought out my baggage by day, like baggage for exile. In the evening I dug through the wall with my own hands. I brought the baggage out at dusk and carried it on my shoulder while they watched.

The Rebellious House Questions Ezekiel

8 In the morning the word of the LORD came to me: 9 “Son of man, has not the house of Israel, that rebellious house, said to you, ‘What are you doing?’”

10 “Say to them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: This message is about the prince in Jerusalem and all the people of Israel who live there.’ 11 Say, ‘I am a sign for you. Just as I did, it will be done to them. They will go into exile and captivity.’

12 “The prince among them will lift his baggage onto his shoulder at dusk and go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out through it. He will cover his face so he cannot see the land. 13 I will spread My net over him, and he will be caught in My trap. I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, but he will not see it, and he will die there. 14 I will scatter to every wind all who are around him, his helpers and all his troops. I will draw My sword after them. 15 Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I scatter them among the nations and spread them through other lands. 16 But I will spare a few from the sword, famine, and plague, so they can tell about all their terrible sins among the nations where they go. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

A Warning About Jerusalem's Suffering

17 The word of the LORD came to me: 18 “Son of man, eat your bread with shaking, and drink your water with trembling and worry.”

19 “Say to the people of the land, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says about the people living in Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread with worry and drink their water in dismay, because their land will be stripped of everything in it due to the violence of everyone who lives there. 20 The cities where people live will be destroyed, and the land will become empty. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’”

The Proverb About Delayed Visions

21 The word of the LORD came to me: 22 “Son of man, what is this proverb you people have in the land of Israel? You say, ‘The days go by, and every vision comes to nothing.’”

23 “So tell them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: I will stop this proverb. They will not use it in Israel anymore.’ Instead, say, ‘The days are near, and every vision will come true.’ 24 There will be no more false visions or smooth-talking fortune-telling among the people of Israel. 25 For I am the LORD. I will speak, and what I say will happen. It will not be delayed anymore. In your days, you rebellious house, I will speak and do it, declares the Lord the LORD.”

26 The word of the LORD came to me: 27 “Son of man, the people of Israel are saying, ‘The vision he sees is for many days from now. He prophesies about times far away.’ 28 So tell them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: None of My words will be delayed anymore. The word I speak will happen, declares the Lord the LORD.’”
