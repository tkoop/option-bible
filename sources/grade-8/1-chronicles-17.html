David Wants to Build a Temple for the LORD

1 When David was settled in his house, he said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of the the LORD's covenant is under tent curtains."

2 Nathan said to David, "Do everything that is in your heart, for God is with you."

God Speaks to David Through Nathan

3 That night the word of God came to Nathan. He said, 4 "Go and tell My servant David, 'This is what the the LORD says: You will not build a house for Me to live in.'"

5 "For I have not lived in a house since the day I brought Israel up, until this day. I have moved from tent to tent and from one dwelling place to another. 6 In all the places I have moved with all Israel, did I ever say to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people, 'Why have you not built Me a house of cedar?'"

7 "Now then, tell My servant David, 'This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be ruler over My people Israel.' 8 I have been with you wherever you have gone. I have cut off all your enemies before you, and I have made your name like the names of the greatest on earth."

9 "I will provide a place for My people Israel and plant them there, so they can have a home of their own and not be disturbed anymore. Wicked people will no longer trouble them, as they did before. 10 Since the time I appointed judges over My people Israel, I have subdued all your enemies. I also tell you that the the LORD will build a house for you."

11 "When your days are fulfilled and you go to be with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, one of your own sons, and I will establish his kingdom. 12 He will build a house for Me, and I will establish his throne forever. 13 I will be his Father, and he will be My son. I will never take My faithful love away from him, as I removed it from the king who ruled before you. 14 I will set him over My house and My kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever."

15 Nathan spoke to David all these words and this vision.

David Thanks the LORD

16 Then King David went in and sat before the the LORD and said, "Who am I, LORD God, and what is my family, that You have brought me this far? 17 This was a small thing in Your eyes, O God, yet You have spoken about the future of Your servant's house. You have treated me like a man of high rank, LORD God. 18 What more can David say to You to honor Your servant? You know Your servant."

19 "LORD, for Your servant's sake and according to Your will, You have done all this great work and made known all these great things. 20 LORD, there is no one like You, and there is no God besides You, according to all we have heard with our ears. 21 And who is like Your people Israel, the one nation on earth that God went out to redeem as His own people? You made a name for Yourself by doing great and awesome things, driving out nations before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt."

22 "You made Your people Israel Your own people forever, and You, LORD, have become their God. 23 Now, LORD, let the word You have spoken about Your servant and his house come true forever. Do as You have said. 24 Let Your name be established and magnified forever, so people will say, 'The the LORD of Hosts is the God of Israel, the God over Israel,' and the house of Your servant David will be established before You."

25 "For You, my God, have revealed to Your servant that You will build a house for him. That is why Your servant has found courage to pray before You. 26 Now, LORD, You are God, and You have promised this good thing to Your servant. 27 Now You have been pleased to bless the house of Your servant, so it will last forever before You. For You, LORD, have blessed it, and it will be blessed forever."
