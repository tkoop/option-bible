What Faith Is

1 Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and proof of what we do not see. 2 By faith, the people of old won God's approval. 3 By faith we understand that God created the universe by His word, so what we see was not made from things that are visible.

Early Heroes of Faith

4 By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain. Because of it, he was declared right with God. God spoke well of his gifts. Even though Abel died, his faith still speaks. 5 By faith Enoch was taken away so he would not see death. He could not be found because God had taken him. Before that, he was known as someone who pleased God. 6 Without faith, no one can please God. Anyone who comes to Him must believe that He exists and that He rewards those who seek Him. 7 By faith Noah, warned about things not yet seen, built an ark in holy fear to save his family. Through his faith he condemned the world and became an heir of the right standing with God that comes by faith.

Abraham's Faith

8 By faith Abraham obeyed when God called him to go to a place he would later receive as his inheritance. He left, even though he did not know where he was going. 9 By faith he lived in the promised land like a stranger. He stayed in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who shared the same promise. 10 He was looking forward to the city with strong foundations, whose builder and maker is God. 11 By faith Sarah, though she was barren and past the age to have children, received strength to have a child. She trusted God because she believed the One who promised was faithful. 12 So from one man, whose body was as good as dead, came descendants as many as the stars in the sky and as countless as the sand on the seashore.

13 All these people died in faith. They did not receive what was promised, but they saw it and welcomed it from far away. They admitted they were strangers and foreigners on earth. 14 People who talk like that show they are looking for a homeland. 15 If they had been thinking about the country they left, they could have gone back. 16 Instead, they long for a better country, a heavenly one. So God is not ashamed to be called their God, because He has prepared a city for them.

17 By faith Abraham, when tested, offered Isaac. He who had received the promises was ready to sacrifice his only son. 18 God had said to him, "Through Isaac your descendants will be called." 19 Abraham believed God could raise the dead. In a way, he received Isaac back from death.

Faith of Later Heroes

20 By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau about things to come. 21 By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of Joseph's sons and worshiped, leaning on the top of his staff. 22 By faith Joseph, near the end of his life, spoke about the Israelites leaving Egypt and gave orders about his bones.

23 By faith Moses' parents hid him for three months after he was born. They saw he was a beautiful child and were not afraid of the king's order. 24 By faith Moses, when he grew up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter. 25 He chose to suffer with God's people rather than enjoy sin's short-lived pleasures. 26 He thought suffering for Christ was worth more than Egypt's treasures, because he looked ahead to his reward. 27 By faith he left Egypt and did not fear the king's anger. He kept going because he saw the One who cannot be seen. 28 By faith he kept the Passover and sprinkled the blood, so the destroyer of the firstborn would not touch them.

29 By faith the people crossed the Red Sea as if on dry ground. But when the Egyptians tried, they were drowned. 30 By faith the walls of Jericho fell after the people marched around them for seven days. 31 By faith Rahab the prostitute did not die with those who disobeyed, because she welcomed the spies peacefully.

32 What more can I say? Time would run out if I told about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets. 33 Through faith they conquered kingdoms, did what was right, received what was promised, and shut the mouths of lions. 34 They put out raging fires, escaped the sword, became strong after being weak, grew mighty in battle, and made enemy armies run away.

35 Women received their dead back, raised to life again. Others were tortured and refused release, so they might gain a better resurrection. 36 Others suffered mocking, whipping, chains, and prison. 37 They were stoned, sawed in two, and killed by the sword. They went about in sheepskins and goatskins, needy, suffering, and mistreated. 38 The world was not worthy of them. They wandered in deserts and mountains and lived in caves and holes in the ground.

39 All these, though praised for their faith, did not receive what was promised. 40 God had planned something better for us, so they would not be made perfect without us.
