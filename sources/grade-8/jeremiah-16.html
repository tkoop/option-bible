Jeremiah Is Told Not to Marry

1 The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 2 "Do not take a wife for yourself, and do not have sons or daughters in this place."

3 For this is what the LORD says about the sons and daughters born in this place, and about their mothers who bore them and their fathers who fathered them in this land: 4 They will die of terrible diseases. No one will mourn for them or bury them. They will be like dung on the ground. They will be destroyed by sword and famine, and their dead bodies will be food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.

No Mourning or Feasting

5 For this is what the LORD says: Do not go into a house where people are mourning. Do not go to cry with them or comfort them. For I have taken away My peace from this people, declares the LORD—My faithful love and mercy. 6 Both great and small will die in this land. They will not be buried. People will not mourn for them, cut themselves, or shave their heads for them. 7 No one will bring them food to comfort them after a death, and no one will give them a drink of comfort for their father or mother. 8 Do not go into a house where people are feasting, to sit with them and eat and drink. 9 For this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: I am about to stop in this place, before your eyes and in your days, the sound of joy and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

The People Ask Why

10 When you tell this people all these things, they will say to you, "Why has the LORD said all this terrible disaster will come on us? What wrong have we done? What sin have we committed against the LORD our God?"

11 Then you will say to them, "Your fathers left Me, declares the LORD. They followed other gods, served them, and bowed down to them. They left Me and did not keep My law. 12 But you have done even worse than your fathers. Look, each of you follows his own stubborn, evil heart instead of listening to Me. 13 Therefore I will throw you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your fathers have known. There you will serve other gods day and night, because I will not show you favor."

The LORD Promises to Bring Them Back

14 Therefore, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when people will no longer say, "As the LORD lives who brought the people of Israel up from Egypt." 15 Instead they will say, "As the LORD lives who brought the people of Israel up from the land of the north and from all the countries where He scattered them." I will bring them back to their own land, the land I gave their ancestors.

16 Look, I am sending many fishermen to catch them, declares the LORD. Then I will send many hunters to hunt them from every mountain, hill, and rocky cliffs. 17 For My eyes are on all their ways. They are not hidden from Me, and their wrongdoing is not hidden from My eyes. 18 First I will repay them double for their wickedness and sin, because they have defiled My land with the dead bodies of their detestable idols and filled My inheritance with their disgusting practices.

19 The LORD, You are my strength and fortress, my refuge in times of trouble. Nations will come to You from the ends of the earth and say, "Our ancestors inherited only lies, worthless idols that cannot help. 20 Can a person make gods for himself? They are not gods." 21 So I will teach them. This time I will show them My power and strength, and they will know that My name is the LORD.
