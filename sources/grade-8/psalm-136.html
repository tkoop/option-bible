Praise the LORD for His Lasting Love

1 Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good, for His faithful love lasts forever. 2 Give thanks to the God of gods, for His faithful love lasts forever. 3 Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His faithful love lasts forever.

4 Give thanks to Him who alone does great wonders, for His faithful love lasts forever. 5 Give thanks to Him who made the heavens with wisdom, for His faithful love lasts forever. 6 Give thanks to Him who spread out the earth above the waters, for His faithful love lasts forever. 7 Give thanks to Him who made the great lights, for His faithful love lasts forever. 8 Give thanks to Him who made the sun to rule by day, for His faithful love lasts forever. 9 Give thanks to Him who made the moon and stars to rule by night, for His faithful love lasts forever.

10 Give thanks to Him who struck down Egypt's firstborn, for His faithful love lasts forever. 11 Give thanks to Him who brought Israel out from among them, for His faithful love lasts forever. 12 Give thanks to Him who acted with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, for His faithful love lasts forever. 13 Give thanks to Him who divided the Red Sea in two, for His faithful love lasts forever. 14 Give thanks to Him who led Israel through the middle of it, for His faithful love lasts forever. 15 Give thanks to Him who swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea, for His faithful love lasts forever.

16 Give thanks to Him who led His people through the wilderness, for His faithful love lasts forever. 17 Give thanks to Him who struck down great kings, for His faithful love lasts forever. 18 Give thanks to Him who killed mighty kings, for His faithful love lasts forever. 19 Give thanks to Him who defeated Sihon, king of the Amorites, for His faithful love lasts forever. 20 Give thanks to Him who defeated Og, king of Bashan, for His faithful love lasts forever. 21 Give thanks to Him who gave their land as an inheritance, for His faithful love lasts forever. 22 Give thanks to Him who gave that inheritance to Israel His servant, for His faithful love lasts forever.

23 Give thanks to Him who remembered us when we were in trouble, for His faithful love lasts forever. 24 Give thanks to Him who rescued us from our enemies, for His faithful love lasts forever. 25 Give thanks to Him who gives food to all living things, for His faithful love lasts forever. 26 Give thanks to the God of heaven, for His faithful love lasts forever.
