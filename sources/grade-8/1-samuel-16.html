The LORD Chooses a New King

1 The LORD said to Samuel, “How long will you mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him as king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse of Bethlehem, because I have chosen one of his sons to be king for Me.”

2 Samuel said, “How can I go? If Saul hears about it, he will kill me.”

The LORD said, “Take a young cow with you and say, ‘I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD.’ 3 Invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show you what to do. You are to anoint for Me the one I tell you about.”

4 Samuel did what the the LORD said and went to Bethlehem. The elders of the town met him, trembling, and asked, “Are you here peacefully?”

5 He said, “Peacefully. I have come to sacrifice to the the LORD. Make yourselves ready and come with me to the sacrifice.” Then he set Jesse and his sons apart and invited them to the sacrifice.

6 When they arrived, Samuel saw Eliab and said, “Surely the the LORD’s chosen one stands before Him.”

7 But the the LORD said to Samuel, “Do not judge by his looks or height, because I have rejected him. People look at what can be seen, but the the LORD looks at the heart.”

8 Then Jesse called Abinadab and had him walk in front of Samuel. But Samuel said, “The The LORD has not chosen this one either.” 9 Jesse then had Shammah walk by. Samuel said, “The The LORD has not chosen this one either.” 10 Jesse had seven of his sons walk in front of Samuel, but Samuel said to Jesse, “The The LORD has not chosen any of these.”

11 Samuel asked Jesse, “Are these all your sons?”

Jesse answered, “There is still the youngest. He is out tending the sheep.”

Samuel said, “Send for him. We will not sit down to eat until he gets here.”

12 So Jesse sent for him and brought him in. He was reddish, with beautiful eyes and a handsome appearance. The The LORD said, “Get up and anoint him, for this is the one.”

13 So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed David among his brothers. The Spirit of the the LORD came powerfully on David from that day on. Then Samuel went back to Ramah.

An Evil Spirit Troubles Saul

14 Now the Spirit of the the LORD had left Saul, and an evil spirit from the the LORD tormented him.

15 Saul’s servants said to him, “Look, an evil spirit from God is troubling you.”

16 “Let our lord tell his servants who are with you to look for a man who can play the harp well. When the evil spirit from God comes on you, he can play, and you will feel better.”

17 Saul said to his servants, “Find me a man who plays well, and bring him to me.”

David Is Chosen to Play for Saul

18 One of the servants answered, “I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who plays skillfully. He is a brave and mighty warrior, good with words, and handsome. The The LORD is with him.”

19 So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, “Send me your son David, who is with the sheep.”

20 Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat, and sent them to Saul with his son David.

21 David came to Saul and entered his service. Saul loved him very much, and David became his armor-bearer.

22 Saul sent a message to Jesse: “Let David stay with me, because he has found favor with me.”

23 Whenever the evil spirit from God came on Saul, David would take his harp and play. Then Saul would feel relieved and better, and the evil spirit would leave him.
