Collection for God's People

1 Now about the collection for God's people, you should do what I told the churches in Galatia to do. 2 On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside money at home and save as much as you can, so that when I come, we won't need to collect money then. 3 When I arrive, I will send letters with the people you choose to carry your gift to Jerusalem. 4 If it seems right for me to go too, they will travel with me.

Paul's Travel Plans

5 I will come to you after I go through Macedonia, because I am traveling through Macedonia. 6 Maybe I will stay with you for a while, or even spend the winter, so you can help me on my way wherever I go next. 7 I don't want to see you only in passing now. I hope to stay with you for some time, if the Lord allows it. 8 But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost. 9 A big door for useful work has opened for me, but many people oppose me.

10 If Timothy comes, make sure he has nothing to fear while he is with you, because he is doing the Lord's work, just as I am. 11 No one should look down on him. Send him on his way in peace so he can come to me, because I am waiting for him along with the brothers. 12 Now about our brother Apollos: I urged him many times to come to you with the brothers, but he did not want to come now. He will come when he has a chance.

Stand Firm in Faith

13 Be on guard. Stand firm in the faith. Be brave. Be strong. 14 Let everything you do be done in love.

15 You know that the family of Stephanas was the first to believe in Achaia, and they have given themselves to serving God's people. Brothers, I urge you 16 to submit to people like that and to everyone who works hard with them. 17 I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus have come, because they made up for what you could not do. 18 They refreshed my spirit, and yours too. So respect people like them.

Final Greetings

19 The churches in Asia send you their greetings. Aquila and Priscilla greet you warmly in the Lord, along with the church that meets in their home. 20 All the brothers and sisters send greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss. 21 I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 22 If anyone does not love the Lord, let that person be cursed. Maranatha! 23 May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. 24 My love is with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
