The Beast from the Sea

1 I saw a beast come up out of the sea. It had ten horns and seven heads. On its horns were ten crowns, and on its heads were names that insulted God. 2 The beast I saw looked like a leopard. Its feet were like a bear's feet, and its mouth was like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave the beast his power, his throne, and great authority. 3 One of the beast's heads looked as if it had been killed, but its deadly wound was healed. The whole world was amazed by the beast and followed it. 4 People worshiped the dragon because he gave authority to the beast. They also worshiped the beast and said, "Who is like the beast? Who can fight against it?"

5 The beast was given a mouth that spoke proud and insulting words. It was given authority to act for forty-two months. 6 It opened its mouth to insult God. It insulted His name, His home, and those who live in heaven. 7 It was allowed to make war against God's holy people and defeat them. It was given authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation. 8 Everyone living on earth will worship it. This means everyone whose name has not been written, since the world was made, in the book of life that belongs to the Lamb who was killed. 9 If anyone has an ear, let him hear. 10 If anyone is meant to be captured, he will be captured. If anyone is to be killed by the sword, he will be killed by the sword. This calls for patient endurance and faith from God's holy people.

The Second Beast

11 Then I saw another beast coming up out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb's horns, but it spoke like a dragon. 12 It uses all the authority of the first beast while the first beast watches. It makes the earth and everyone living on it worship the first beast, whose deadly wound was healed. 13 It performs great signs. It even makes fire come down from heaven to earth while people watch. 14 It deceives the people living on earth by the signs it is allowed to do in front of the first beast. It tells them to make an image for the beast that was wounded by the sword but lived. 15 It was allowed to give breath to the image of the beast, so the image could speak. It could also cause anyone who does not worship the image of the beast to be killed.

The Mark of the Beast

16 It forces everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to receive a mark on their right hand or on their forehead. 17 No one can buy or sell unless he has the mark. The mark is either the name of the beast or the number of its name. 18 This calls for wisdom. The person who understands should count the number of the beast. It is a human number. Its number is 666.
