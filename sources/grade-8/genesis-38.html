Judah Leaves His Brothers

1 At that time, Judah left his brothers and went down to stay with a man named Hirah from Adullam. 2 There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua. He married her and slept with her. 3 She became pregnant and gave birth to a son, and he named him Er. 4 She conceived again and gave birth to a son and named him Onan. 5 She gave birth to another son and named him Shelah. She had him at Kezib.

Er and Onan

6 Judah got a wife for Er, his firstborn. Her name was Tamar. 7 But Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the LORD's eyes. So the LORD put him to death. 8 Then Judah said to Onan, "Sleep with your brother's wife and do your duty as a brother-in-law to her. Raise up children for your brother." 9 But Onan knew the child would not be his. So whenever he slept with his brother's wife, he spilled his seed on the ground so he would not father children for his brother. 10 What he did was wicked in the LORD's eyes, so the LORD put him to death too. 11 Judah said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, "Live as a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up." He thought, "He might die too, like his brothers." So Tamar went to live in her father's house.

Tamar's Plan

12 After a long time, Judah's wife, the daughter of Shua, died. When Judah had finished mourning, he went up to Timnah with his friend Hirah the Adullamite to see the men who were shearing his sheep. 13 Someone told Tamar, "Your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep." 14 She took off her widow's clothes, covered herself with a veil, and sat at the entrance to Enaim, which is on the road to Timnah. She did this because she saw that Shelah had grown up, but she had not been given to him as a wife. 15 When Judah saw her, he thought she was a prostitute because she had covered her face.

16 He turned to her by the road and said, "Come now, let me sleep with you." He did not know she was his daughter-in-law.

She asked, "What will you give me to sleep with you?"

17 "I'll send you a young goat from my flock," he said.

"Will you give me something as a pledge until you send it?" she asked.

18 He said, "What pledge should I give you?"

"Your seal and its cord, and the staff in your hand," she answered. So he gave them to her and slept with her, and she became pregnant by him.

19 After she left, she took off her veil and put on her widow's clothes again.

20 Meanwhile Judah sent the young goat by his friend the Adullamite to get his pledge back from the woman, but he did not find her.

21 He asked the men where she was and said, "Where is the shrine prostitute who was beside the road at Enaim?"

"There hasn't been any shrine prostitute here," they said.

22 So he went back to Judah and said, "I didn't find her. Besides, the men who live there said, 'There hasn't been any shrine prostitute here.'"

23 Then Judah said, "Let her keep them, or people will make fun of us. After all, I did send her this goat, but you couldn't find her."

The Truth Comes Out

24 About three months later Judah was told, "Your daughter-in-law Tamar is guilty of prostitution, and as a result she is now pregnant."

Judah said, "Bring her out and have her burned to death!"

25 As she was being brought out, she sent a message to her father-in-law. "I am pregnant by the man who owns these," she said. "Please look at these and see whose seal and cord and staff these are."

26 Judah recognized them and said, "She is more in the right than I am, since I wouldn't give her to my son Shelah." And he did not sleep with her again.

27 When the time came for her to give birth, there were twin boys in her womb.

28 As she was giving birth, one of them put out his hand; so the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his wrist and said, "This one came out first."

29 But when he drew his hand back, his brother came out, and she said, "So this is how you have broken out!" And he was named Perez.

30 Then his brother, who had the scarlet thread on his wrist, came out. And he was named Zerah.
