A Prayer for Help When Enemies Attack

For the choir director. To the tune of "A Dove on Distant Oaks." A poem of David, when the Philistines captured him in Gath. 1 Be gracious to me, O God, because people are hunting me down. All day long they fight and crush me. 2 My enemies trample me all day; many proudly fight against me.

3 When I am afraid, I will trust in You. 4 In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

Enemies Twist My Words

5 All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are against me for evil. 6 They conspire and lurk; they watch my steps, waiting to take my life.

7 Will they escape because of their sin? In Your anger, O God, bring down the nations. 8 You have counted my wanderings. Put my tears in Your bottle. Are they not in Your book?

God Is on My Side

9 Then my enemies will retreat on the day I call. This I know: God is for me. 10 In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise, 11 in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

12 Your vows are upon me, O God; I will bring You thank offerings. 13 For You have delivered my soul from death, and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.
