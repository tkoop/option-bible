Greeting to God's People

1 Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and a brother of James, writes to those who are called, who are loved by God the Father and kept in Jesus Christ. 2 Mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you.

Warning About False Teachers

3 Dear friends, while I was trying hard to write to you about the salvation we share, I felt I had to write and urge you to fight for the faith that God gave His holy people once and for all. 4 Some people have slipped in among you without notice. They were marked long ago for judgment. They are ungodly people who take God's grace and use it as an excuse to live wildly, and they deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

5 I want to remind you, even though you already know all this, that Jesus saved a people out of Egypt but later destroyed those who did not believe. 6 And the angels who did not stay in their proper place but left their own home, He has kept in everlasting chains under darkness for judgment on the great day. 7 Just as Sodom and Gomorrah and the nearby cities gave themselves to sexual immorality and pursued unnatural desires, they stand as an example of those who suffer the punishment of eternal fire.

8 In the same way, these dreamers pollute their bodies, reject authority, and speak evil of glorious beings. 9 But Michael the archangel, when he argued with the devil about Moses' body, did not dare to accuse him with insulting words. Instead, he said, "The Lord rebuke you!" 10 But these people speak evil of things they do not understand. Like animals that act without thinking, they are destroyed by the things they know by instinct.

11 Woe to them! They have followed Cain's path. They have rushed into Balaam's error for money. They have been destroyed in Korah's rebellion. 12 These people are hidden reefs at your love feasts. They eat with you without fear, caring only for themselves. They are clouds without rain, blown by the wind. They are autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, pulled up by the roots. 13 They are wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame. They are wandering stars, and the blackest darkness is reserved for them forever.

14 Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about these people. He said, "Look, the Lord is coming with thousands of His holy ones 15 to judge everyone and to convict all ungodly people of every ungodly thing they have done, and of all the harsh words ungodly sinners have spoken against Him." 16 These people are grumblers and complainers. They follow their own evil desires. They speak proud words and flatter others to get what they want.

A Call to Stay Faithful

17 But you, dear friends, remember what the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ said before. 18 They told you, "In the last days, mockers will come who follow their own ungodly desires." 19 These people cause divisions. They follow their natural desires and do not have the Spirit.

20 But you, dear friends, build yourselves up in your most holy faith and pray in the Holy Spirit. 21 Keep yourselves in God's love as you wait for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ to bring you to eternal life. 22 Have mercy on those who doubt. 23 Save others by snatching them from the fire. Have mercy on others with fear, hating even the clothes stained by sin.

Praise to God

24 Now to Him who can keep you from falling and present you blameless before His glorious presence with great joy, 25 to the only God our Savior be glory, majesty, power, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord, before all time, now, and forever. Amen.
