Israel Called to Repent

1 Say to your brothers, "My people," and to your sisters, "My loved one." 2 Plead with your mother, plead, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Let her put away her unfaithfulness from her face and her adultery from between her breasts.

3 Otherwise I will strip her naked and expose her as on the day she was born. I will make her like a desert, turn her into dry land, and kill her with thirst. 4 I will not show love to her children, because they are children born from unfaithfulness. 5 Their mother has been unfaithful. The one who gave birth to them has disgraced herself. She said, "I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and water, my wool and linen, my oil and drinks."

6 So I will block her path with thorns. I will build a wall so she cannot find her way. 7 She will chase her lovers but not catch them. She will look for them but not find them. Then she will say, "I will go back to my first husband, because life was better for me then than now." 8 She did not know that I gave her the grain, new wine, and oil. I gave her plenty of silver and gold, but she used it for Baal.

9 So I will take back My grain in its season and My new wine at harvest time. I will take back My wool and linen that were meant to cover her nakedness. 10 I will expose her shame before her lovers' eyes. No one will rescue her from My hand.

11 I will end all her joy—her festivals, new moons, Sabbaths, and all her special feasts. 12 I will ruin her vines and fig trees, which she said were her wages from her lovers. I will turn them into a thicket, and wild animals will eat them. 13 I will punish her for the days she burned incense to the Baals. She put on rings and jewelry and went after her lovers, but she forgot Me, says the LORD.

God Promises to Restore Israel

14 So I will win her back. I will lead her into the desert and speak tenderly to her. 15 There I will give her back her vineyards. I will make the Valley of Achor a door of hope. There she will sing as she did in her youth, as on the day she came up from Egypt.

16 In that day, says the LORD, you will call Me "My husband," and you will no longer call Me "My master." 17 I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth. Their names will not be remembered anymore. 18 In that day I will make a covenant for them with the wild animals, the birds of the sky, and the creatures that move along the ground. I will remove bow, sword, and war from the land, so they can lie down in safety.

19 I will betroth you to Me forever. I will betroth you in righteousness and justice, in love and compassion. 20 I will betroth you in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.

The Land and People Are Restored

21 In that day, says the LORD, I will answer the heavens, and they will answer the earth. 22 The earth will answer the grain, new wine, and oil, and they will answer Jezreel. 23 I will plant her in the land for Myself. I will show mercy to "No Mercy." I will say to "Not My People," "You are My people," and they will say, "You are my God."
