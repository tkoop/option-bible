Come, Let's Worship the LORD

1 Come, let's sing to the the LORD! Let's shout to the Rock who saves us! 2 Let's come to Him with thanks, and shout to Him with songs of praise!

3 For the the LORD is a great God, a great King over all gods. 4 The deep places of the earth are in His hand, and the mountain peaks belong to Him. 5 The sea is His, because He made it. His hands shaped the dry land.

6 Come, let's worship and bow down. Let's kneel before the the LORD, our Maker.

7 For He is our God, and we are His flock, the sheep He holds in His hand.

"Today, if you hear His voice, 8 do not harden your hearts as at Meribah, as on the day at Massah in the desert, 9 where your ancestors tested Me and tried Me, even though they saw My work.

10 "For forty years I was angry with that generation, and I said, 'They are a people whose hearts go astray, and they do not know My ways.'

11 "So I swore in My anger, 'They will never enter My rest.'"
