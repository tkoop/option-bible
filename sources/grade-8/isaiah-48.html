Listen, House of Jacob

1 "Listen to this, house of Jacob—you who are called Israel. You came from Judah’s line. You swear by the the LORD’s name and call on the God of Israel, but not with truth or right living. 2 For they call themselves after the holy city and depend on the God of Israel. The the LORD of Hosts is His name.

3 "I spoke about things from long ago. They came from My mouth, and I announced them. Then, suddenly, I acted, and they came true. 4 Because I knew you are stubborn. Your neck is like iron, and your forehead is like bronze.

5 "So I told you about them long ago. Before they happened, I announced them to you, so you would not say, ‘My idol did them, and my carved image and my metal image commanded them.’ 6 You have heard; look at all this. Will you not declare it? From now on I announce new things to you, hidden things you have not known. 7 They are new now, not from long ago. You have not heard of them before today, so you cannot say, ‘Look, I knew them.’

8 "Surely you have never heard; surely you have never known. From long ago your ear has not been open. For I knew you would act very unfaithfully, and you were called a rebel from birth.

Tested in the Furnace

9 "For the sake of My name I will hold back My anger. For the sake of My praise I will restrain it for you, so I do not cut you off. 10 Look, I have refined you, but not like silver. I have tested you in the furnace of suffering. 11 For My own sake, for My own sake, I will do it. For how can I let My name be dishonored? I will not give My glory to another.

The LORD Is First and Last

12 "Listen to Me, Jacob, and Israel, whom I have called. I am the One; I am the first, and I am the last. 13 Surely My hand founded the earth, and My right hand spread out the heavens. When I call them, they stand together.

14 "Assemble, all of you, and listen: Who among them has declared these things? The the LORD loves him; he will carry out His plan against Babylon, and His arm will be against the Chaldeans. 15 I, yes I, have spoken. I have called him. I have brought him, and he will succeed. 16 Come near to Me and listen to this: From the beginning I have not spoken in secret. From the time it happened, I was there. And now the Lord, the the LORD, has sent Me, and His Spirit."

The LORD Redeems His People

17 Thus says the the LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: "I am the the LORD your God, who teaches you what is best, who leads you in the way you should go. 18 If only you had paid attention to My commands, your peace would have been like a river, and your right living like the waves of the sea. 19 Your children would have been like the sand, and your descendants like its grains. Their name would never be cut off or destroyed before Me."

20 "Come out of Babylon! Flee from the Chaldeans! Announce it with a shout of joy. Proclaim it. Send it to the ends of the earth. Say, ‘The the LORD has redeemed His servant Jacob!’"

21 They did not get thirsty when He led them through the deserts. He made water flow for them from the rock. He split the rock, and water poured out.

22 "There is no peace," says the the LORD, "for the wicked."
