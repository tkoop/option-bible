Wicked Leaders in Jerusalem

1 The Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD, the gate that faces east. At the entrance of the gate I saw twenty-five men. Among them were Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people.

2 The LORD said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who plan evil and give bad advice in this city."

3 They say, "Isn't it almost time to build houses? This city is like a pot, and we are the meat kept safe inside it."

God Warns the City's Leaders

4 Therefore prophesy against them, Son of man. Prophesy!

5 Then the Spirit of the LORD fell on me, and He said to me, "Say: This is what the LORD says: You people of Israel have said these things, and I know every thought that comes into your minds.

6 "You have killed many people in this city and filled its streets with the dead.

7 "So this is what the Lord the LORD says: The people you killed in this city are the meat, and the city is the pot. But I will bring you out of it.

8 "You have feared the sword, so I will bring the sword against you, declares the Lord the LORD.

9 "I will bring you out of the city and hand you over to foreigners. I will punish you.

10 "You will die by the sword. I will judge you at the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

11 "This city will not be a pot for you, and you will not be the meat inside it. I will judge you at the border of Israel.

12 "Then you will know that I am the LORD. You did not follow My laws or practice My rules. Instead, you followed the rules of the nations around you."

13 While I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. I fell facedown and cried out in a loud voice, "Alas, Lord the LORD! Will You make a complete end of the people of Israel who are left?"

A Promise for the Exiles

14 Then the word of the LORD came to me:

15 "Son of man, your brothers—your brothers, your relatives, and all the house of Israel—are the ones the people of Jerusalem talk about when they say, 'They are far from the LORD; this land has been given to us as our possession.'

16 "So say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: I sent them far away to other nations and scattered them among the countries. Yet for a little while I have been a place of worship for them in the countries where they have gone.'

17 "So say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: I will gather you from the nations and bring you together from the countries where you were scattered. I will give you the land of Israel.'

18 "They will return to it and remove all its disgusting things and all its evil practices.

19 "I will give them one heart and put a new spirit in them. I will take away their stubborn heart of stone and give them a tender heart of flesh.

20 "Then they will follow My laws and keep My rules and do them. They will be My people, and I will be their God.

21 "But those who set their hearts on their disgusting things and evil practices—I will bring the results of their actions down on their own heads, declares the Lord the LORD."

The Glory of God Leaves the City

22 Then the cherubim, with the wheels beside them, lifted their wings, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.

23 The glory of the LORD rose up from inside the city and stood over the mountain east of the city.

24 The Spirit lifted me up and, in a vision from the Spirit of God, brought me to the exiles in Chaldea. Then the vision I had seen left me.

25 I told the exiles everything the LORD had shown me.
