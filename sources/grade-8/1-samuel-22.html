David Escapes to the Cave of Adullam

1David left that place and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's household heard about it, they went down to him there. 2All the men who were in trouble, all the men who owed money, and all the men who were bitter and unhappy gathered to him. He became their leader, and about four hundred men were with him.

David Goes to Moab

3David left there and went to Mizpah in Moab. He said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and mother stay with you until I know what God will do for me." 4He left them in the care of the king of Moab, and they stayed with him the whole time David was in the stronghold.

5The prophet Gad told David, "Do not stay in the stronghold. Go into the land of Judah." So David left and went to the forest of Hereth.

Saul Accuses His Servants

6Saul heard that David and his men had been found. Saul was sitting under the tamarisk tree in Ramah, holding his spear, with all his servants standing around him. 7Saul said to the servants standing around him, "Listen, men of Benjamin! Will Jesse's son give all of you fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and hundreds? 8For all of you have plotted against me. No one tells me when my son makes an agreement with Jesse's son. None of you is concerned about me or tells me that my son has stirred up my servant to ambush me, as he is doing today."

Saul Turns Against the Priests

9Doeg the Edomite, who was standing with Saul's servants, said, "I saw Jesse's son go to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob. 10He asked the the LORD for him, gave him food, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

11Then the king sent for Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and all his father's family—the priests at Nob—and they all came to the king.

12Saul said, "Listen now, son of Ahitub."

He answered, "Here I am, my lord."

13Saul said to him, "Why have you and Jesse's son plotted against me by giving him bread and a sword and asking God for him, so he could turn against me and ambush me, as he is doing today?"

14Ahimelech answered the king, "Who among all your servants is as faithful as David? He is the king's son-in-law, captain of your bodyguard, and honored in your house. 15Have I only now started asking God for him? I would never do that! Do not let the king accuse me or any of my father's household, for your servant knew nothing about this, either small or great."

16The king said, "You will surely die, Ahimelech—you and all your father's household."

17The king said to the guards standing around him, "Go and kill the priests of the the LORD, because they have taken David's side. They knew he was running away and did not tell me." But the king's servants refused to harm the priests of the the LORD.

18Then the king said to Doeg, "You go and strike down the priests!" So Doeg the Edomite went and struck down the priests. That day he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.

19He also put the town of Nob, the priests' town, to the sword, killing men and women, children and babies, and even oxen, donkeys, and sheep.

20But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped and fled to David. His name was Abiathar.

21Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the the LORD.

22David said to Abiathar, "I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would tell Saul. I am responsible for the death of all your father's household. 23Stay with me. Do not be afraid, for the one who is trying to kill me is trying to kill you too. You will be safe with me."
