Working Together with God

1 As God's coworkers, we urge you not to let God's grace go to waste. 2 For He says, “In a time of favor I heard you, and on the day of salvation I helped you. Look, now is the time of favor; look, now is the day of salvation.”

Living as God's Servants

3 We try not to put any obstacle in anyone's way, so our work for God will not be blamed. 4 But as servants of God, we prove ourselves in every way: in great patience, in troubles, in hardships, and in distress. 5 We have faced beatings, prisons, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, and hunger. 6 We have lived in purity, knowledge, patience, and kindness, with the help of the Holy Spirit and sincere love. 7 We speak the truth and rely on God's power, using the weapons of righteousness in our right and left hands.

8 We have been honored and shamed, praised and criticized; people call us deceivers, but we speak the truth. 9 We are treated as unknown, yet we are well known; as dying, and yet we live; as punished, but not killed. 10 We are sad, yet always glad; poor, yet we make many rich; having nothing, yet we have everything.

Open Hearts to the Corinthians

11 We have spoken openly to you, Corinthians; our hearts are wide open. 12 You are not held back by us; you are held back by your own hearts. 13 In return, I speak to you as to my children. Open your hearts wide too.

Do Not Team Up with Unbelievers

14 Do not team up with unbelievers. What do righteousness and lawlessness share? Or what does light have in common with darkness? 15 What agreement can Christ have with Belial? Or what does a believer share with an unbeliever?

16 What does God's temple have in common with idols? For we are the temple of the living God. As God said, “I will live with them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be My people.”

17 Therefore, “Come out from among them and be separate,” says the Lord. “Touch no unclean thing, and I will receive you.”

18 “And I will be a Father to you, and you will be My sons and daughters,” says the Lord Almighty.
