Hiram and Solomon Work Together

1 When Hiram, king of Tyre, heard that Solomon had been made king in place of his father, he sent his servants to Solomon. Hiram had loved David all his life. 2 Solomon sent this message to Hiram: 3 “You know that my father David could not build a temple for the Name of the LORD his God. Wars surrounded him until the LORD gave him victory over his enemies. 4 But now the LORD my God has given me rest on every side. There is no enemy and no trouble. 5 So I plan to build a temple for the Name of the LORD my God, just as the LORD promised my father David. He said, ‘Your son, whom I will put on your throne in your place, will build the house for My Name.’ 6 Now please command that cedars be cut for me from Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants. I will pay your servants whatever wages you ask, because you know that no one among us knows how to cut timber like the people of Sidon.”

Hiram Agrees to Help

7 When Hiram heard Solomon's words, he rejoiced greatly and said, “Blessed be the LORD today! He has given David a wise son to rule over this great people.”

8 Hiram sent this reply to Solomon: “I have heard your request. I will supply all the cedar and cypress wood you want. 9 My servants will bring the logs down from Lebanon to the sea. I will make them into rafts and float them to the place you choose. There I will break them apart, and you can carry them away. You will provide food for my royal household.”

10 So Hiram gave Solomon all the cedar and cypress wood he wanted. 11 Each year Solomon gave Hiram 20,000 measures of wheat for his household and 20 measures of pure olive oil. 12 The LORD gave Solomon wisdom, as He had promised. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them made a treaty.

Workers for the Temple

13 King Solomon forced 30,000 men from all Israel to work. 14 He sent them to Lebanon in shifts of 10,000 a month. They spent one month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was in charge of the forced labor. 15 Solomon also had 70,000 men who carried loads and 80,000 men who cut stone in the hills. 16 Besides these, he had 3,300 supervisors who oversaw the workers.

17 The king ordered large, costly stones to be cut for the temple foundation. They were hewn stones. 18 Solomon's builders, Hiram's builders, and the Giblites cut and prepared the wood and stone for building the temple.
