Joseph Succeeds in Egypt

1 Now Joseph had been taken down to Egypt. Potiphar, an Egyptian officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had taken him there. 2 The LORD was with Joseph, so he prospered, and he stayed in the house of his Egyptian master. 3 His master saw that the LORD was with him and that the LORD made everything he did succeed. 4 So Joseph found favor in his eyes and served him. He put Joseph in charge of his household and gave him authority over all that he owned. 5 From the time he put Joseph in charge of his house and all he had, the LORD blessed the Egyptian's house because of Joseph. The LORD's blessing was on all the Egyptian had, both in the house and in the field. 6 So he left all he owned in Joseph's care. He didn't know what he had except the food he ate. Now Joseph was handsome in form and appearance.

Potiphar's Wife Tempts Joseph

7 After these things, his master's wife looked at Joseph with desire and said, "Lie with me." 8 But he refused. He said to his master's wife, "Look, my master doesn't know what is in the house with me. He has put everything he owns in my hands. 9 No one in this house is greater than I am, and he has withheld nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How could I do this great wickedness and sin against God?" 10 She spoke to Joseph day after day, but he wouldn't listen to her to lie beside her or be with her.

11 One day, he went into the house to do his work, and none of the household servants were there. 12 She grabbed him by his garment and said, "Lie with me." But he left his garment in her hand and ran out of the house. 13 When she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and run outside, 14 she called to the men of her household and said, "Look! He brought us a Hebrew man to make fun of us. He came in to lie with me, and I cried out loudly. 15 When he heard me raise my voice and cry out, he left his garment beside me and ran outside." 16 She kept his garment beside her until his master came home.

17 Then she spoke to him, saying, "The Hebrew servant you brought us came in to me to make fun of me. 18 But when I raised my voice and cried out, he left his garment beside me and ran outside." 19 When his master heard the words his wife spoke to him, saying, "This is what your servant did to me," his anger burned.

Joseph Thrown into Prison

20 Joseph's master took him and put him in the prison, the place where the king's prisoners were bound. He stayed there in prison. 21 But the LORD was with Joseph and showed him kindness. He gave him favor in the eyes of the prison warden. 22 The prison warden put all the prisoners under Joseph's charge. Whatever needed to be done there, Joseph was in charge of doing it. 23 The prison warden didn't worry about anything under Joseph's hand, because the LORD was with him, and the LORD made whatever he did succeed.
