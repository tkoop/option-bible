The Sabbath Year and the Year of Jubilee

1 The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. 2 He told Moses to speak to the Israelites and say that when you entered the land He was giving you, the land had to keep a Sabbath rest for the LORD.

3 For six years you may plant your field, prune your vineyard, and gather its crops. 4 But in the seventh year the land must have a full Sabbath rest, a Sabbath for the LORD. Do not plant your field or prune your vineyard. 5 Do not harvest what grows on its own, and do not pick grapes from vines you have not trimmed. That year is a complete rest for the land.

6 Whatever the land produces during its Sabbath may be food for you, your male and female servants, your hired workers, and the foreigners living with you. It may also feed your livestock and the wild animals in your land. 7 All its crops may be eaten.

8 You must count seven Sabbaths of years—seven times seven years, or forty-nine years. 9 On the tenth day of the seventh month, on the Day of Atonement, sound the trumpet all through your land. 10 Set apart the fiftieth year as holy, and proclaim freedom throughout the land for everyone who lives there. That year is a Jubilee for you. Each of you must return to his own property and to his family.

11 The fiftieth year is a Jubilee for you. Do not plant, do not harvest what grows on its own, and do not pick grapes from untrimmed vines. 12 You may eat what the field produces on its own. 13 In that Year of Jubilee, each of you must return to his own property.

Fair Buying and Selling

14 If you sell land to your neighbor or buy from your neighbor, do not cheat each other. 15 Buy from your neighbor based on the number of years since the Jubilee, and he will sell to you based on the number of harvests. 16 If many years remain, the price will be higher; if few years remain, the price will be lower, because he is selling you a number of harvests. 17 Do not cheat one another. Respect your God, for I am the LORD your God.

18 Keep My laws and obey My rules. Then you will live safely in the land. 19 The land will give its fruit, you will eat until you are full, and you will live safely on it.

20 You may ask what you will eat in the seventh year if you do not plant or gather your crops. 21 But I will send My blessing for you in the sixth year, so the land will produce enough for three years. 22 You will plant in the eighth year and eat from the old crop until the ninth year's harvest comes in.

The Land Belongs to the LORD

23 The land must not be sold for all time, because it is Mine. You are foreigners and temporary residents with Me. 24 In all the land you own, you must allow the land to be bought back.

25 If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his property, his nearest relative may buy it back for him. 26 If no one can buy it back for him but he can afford it himself, he must count the years since he sold it, pay back the extra money to the buyer, and return to his property. 27 If he cannot afford to buy it back, it stays with the buyer until the Year of Jubilee. 28 Then it is released, and he returns to his property.

29 If a man sells a house in a walled city, he may buy it back until one full year after the sale. 30 After a full year, the house in the walled city belongs permanently to the buyer and his descendants; it will not be released in the Jubilee. 31 Houses in villages without walls are treated like open fields. They may be bought back, and they are released in the Jubilee. 32 The Levites may always buy back the houses in their cities, and those houses are released in the Jubilee. 33 This is because the houses in the Levite cities are their permanent possession among the Israelites. 34 The fields around their cities may not be sold, because that is their permanent possession.

Helping the Poor and Protecting Servants

35 If your brother becomes poor and cannot support himself with you, help him as you would a foreigner or temporary resident, so he can live with you. 36 Do not charge him interest or make a profit from him, but respect your God, so your brother can live with you. 37 Do not lend him your money at interest or sell him your food for profit. 38 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.

39 If your brother becomes poor among you and sells himself to you, do not make him work like a slave. 40 Treat him as a hired worker or temporary resident. He is to work for you until the Year of Jubilee. 41 Then he and his children are to leave you and return to his family and to the property of his ancestors. 42 They are My servants, whom I brought out of Egypt. They must not be sold as slaves. 43 Do not rule over him harshly, but respect your God.

44 You may buy male and female slaves from the nations around you. 45 You may also buy them from the children of the foreigners living among you, and from their families born in your land. They may become your property. 46 You may leave them to your children as an inheritance and make them slaves for life. But you must not rule harshly over your brothers, the Israelites.

47 If a foreigner or temporary resident with you becomes rich and your brother becomes poor and sells himself to that foreigner or resident, or to a member of his family, 48 he keeps the right to be bought back after he is sold. One of his brothers may buy him back. 49 An uncle, a cousin, or another close relative may buy him back, or he may buy himself back if he can afford it. 50 He and his buyer must count the years from the year he was sold to the Year of Jubilee. His price is to be set like a hired worker's, based on the number of years left. 51 If many years remain, he must pay a larger part of his purchase price to be bought back. 52 If only a few years remain until the Jubilee, he is to be charged less. 53 He is to be treated as a hired worker from year to year, and his master must not rule over him harshly in your presence. 54 If he is not bought back in any of these ways, he and his children are released in the Year of Jubilee.

55 For the Israelites are My servants. I brought them out of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.
