The Divisions of the Priests

1 These were the divisions of Aaron's descendants. Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 2 But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests. 3 David, along with Zadok from Eleazar's descendants and Ahimelech from Ithamar's descendants, divided them by their duties in service. 4 More family leaders were found among Eleazar's descendants than among Ithamar's. They were divided into sixteen heads of family lines for Eleazar's family and eight for Ithamar's family. 5 They divided them by drawing lots, mixing both groups together, because both groups had officers of the sanctuary and officers of God. 6 Shemaiah son of Nethanel, a Levite scribe, wrote them down before the king, the officials, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the heads of the family lines of the priests and Levites. One family line was taken for Eleazar, and one for Ithamar.

The Priestly Divisions by Lot

7 The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, and the second to Jedaiah. 8 The third fell to Harim, and the fourth to Seorim. 9 The fifth fell to Malchijah, and the sixth to Mijamin. 10 The seventh fell to Hakkoz, and the eighth to Abijah. 11 The ninth fell to Jeshua, and the tenth to Shecaniah. 12 The eleventh fell to Eliashib, and the twelfth to Jakim. 13 The thirteenth fell to Huppah, and the fourteenth to Jeshebeab. 14 The fifteenth fell to Bilgah, and the sixteenth to Immer. 15 The seventeenth fell to Hezir, and the eighteenth to Happizzez. 16 The nineteenth fell to Pethahiah, and the twentieth to Jehezkel. 17 The twenty-first fell to Jachin, and the twenty-second to Gamul. 18 The twenty-third fell to Delaiah, and the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.

19 These were their duties for service: to come into the house of the the LORD according to the rules given by their ancestor Aaron, as the the LORD God of Israel had commanded him.

The Remaining Levite Families

20 For the rest of Levi's descendants: from Amram's descendants came Shubael; from Shubael's descendants came Jehdeiah. 21 For Rehabiah's line, the leader among his sons was Isshiah. 22 For the Izharites: Shelomoth; for Shelomoth's descendants: Jahath. 23 For Hebron's descendants: Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. 24 For Uzziel's descendants: Micah; for Micah's descendants: Shamir. 25 Micah's brother was Isshiah; for Isshiah's descendants: Zechariah. 26 For Merari's descendants: Mahli and Mushi. For Jaaziah's descendants: Beno. 27 For Merari's descendants through Jaaziah: Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri. 28 Mahli's son was Eleazar, who had no sons. 29 Kish's sons were Jerahmeel. 30 For Mushi's descendants: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the Levites listed by their family lines. 31 They also cast lots, just as their relatives, Aaron's descendants, had done. They did this before King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the family lines of the priests and Levites. The families of the oldest brother were treated the same as those of the youngest.
