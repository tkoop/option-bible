The LORD Gives Jericho to Joshua

1 Jericho was shut tight because of the Israelites. No one went out, and no one came in. 2 Then the the LORD said to Joshua, “Look, I have handed Jericho over to your hand, along with its king and its strong warriors. 3 March around the city once with all your fighting men. Do this for six days. 4 Have seven priests carry seven rams’ horns in front of the ark. On the seventh day, march around the city seven times while the priests blow the trumpets. 5 When you hear the long blast of the ram’s horn, all the people must shout loudly. Then the city wall will fall, and everyone will charge straight ahead.”

Marching Around the City

6 Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said, “Take up the ark of the covenant. Let seven priests carry seven rams’ horns in front of the ark of the the LORD.” 7 He told the people, “Move out and march around the city. Let the armed men go ahead of the ark of the the LORD.” 8 As soon as Joshua spoke, the seven priests carrying the seven rams’ horns went forward before the the LORD, blowing their trumpets. The ark of the covenant of the the LORD followed them. 9 The armed men went ahead of the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard followed the ark. The priests kept blowing the trumpets as they marched.

10 Joshua ordered the people, “Do not shout or make a sound with your voices until I tell you to shout. Then you must shout.” 11 So the ark of the the LORD went around the city once. Then they returned to camp and stayed there for the night.

12 Joshua got up early the next morning, and the priests carried the ark of the the LORD. 13 The seven priests carrying the seven rams’ horns went ahead of the ark of the the LORD, blowing their trumpets. The armed men went before them, and the rear guard followed the ark of the the LORD. 14 On the second day, they marched around the city once and returned to camp. They did the same thing for six days.

The Walls Fall

15 On the seventh day, they got up at dawn and marched around the city seven times, just as before. That was the only day they marched around it seven times. 16 On the seventh time, after the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, “Shout! The the LORD has given you the city. 17 The city and everything in it must be completely destroyed for the the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute and everyone in her house will live, because she hid the messengers we sent.

18 “But be careful not to take anything that is set apart for destruction. If you take any of them, you will bring trouble on Israel’s camp. 19 All the silver, gold, bronze, and iron are holy to the the LORD. They must go into the the LORD’s treasury.”

20 So the people shouted, and the priests blew the trumpets. When the people heard the trumpet sound, they gave a loud shout, and the wall fell down. Then everyone charged straight into the city and captured it. 21 They destroyed everything in the city—men, women, young, old, cattle, sheep, and donkeys—by the sword.

Rahab Is Saved and Jericho Is Burned

22 Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, “Go into the prostitute’s house and bring out the woman and everyone with her, just as you promised her.” 23 The young spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father, mother, brothers, and all her relatives. They placed them outside Israel’s camp.

24 Then they burned the city and everything in it. They saved only the silver, gold, bronze, and iron and put them in the the LORD’s treasury. 25 Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute and her family because she hid the spies Joshua sent to Jericho. She still lives among the Israelites today.

26 At that time Joshua made the people promise before the the LORD: “Anyone who rebuilds this city of Jericho is cursed. He will lose his oldest son when he lays the foundation, and his youngest son when he sets up the gates.” 27 So the the LORD was with Joshua, and people all over the country heard about him.
