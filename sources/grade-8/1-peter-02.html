Grow Up in Salvation

1 So get rid of all evil, all lying, pretending, jealousy, and all bad talk about others. 2 Like newborn babies, long for the pure spiritual milk, so that by it you can grow up in your salvation, 3 since you have tasted that the Lord is good.

Jesus, the Living Stone

4 As you come to Him, the living Stone—rejected by people but chosen by God and precious to Him— 5 you also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood, offering spiritual sacrifices that God accepts through Jesus Christ. 6 For in Scripture it says, "See, I lay a stone in Zion, a chosen and precious cornerstone, and whoever trusts in Him will never be put to shame." 7 Now to you who believe, this stone is precious. But to those who do not believe, "The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone," 8 and, "A stone that makes people stumble and a rock that makes them fall." They stumble because they disobey the word, and that is what they were destined for. 9 But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God's own possession, so that you may tell about the wonderful deeds of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light. 10 Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.

Live Honorably Among Outsiders

11 Dear friends, I urge you, as strangers and temporary residents, to stay away from sinful desires that fight against your soul. 12 Keep your behavior good among the people around you, so that when they speak against you as if you were doing wrong, they may see your good deeds and give glory to God on the day He visits us. 13 For the Lord's sake, submit to every human authority, whether to the emperor as the supreme ruler, 14 or to governors, who are sent by Him to punish those who do wrong and to praise those who do right. 15 For it is God's will that by doing good you silence the ignorant talk of foolish people. 16 Live as free people, but do not use your freedom as a cover for evil; live as God's servants. 17 Honor everyone. Love the family of believers. Fear God. Honor the emperor.

Follow Christ's Example in Suffering

18 Slaves, submit to your masters with all respect, not only to those who are good and gentle, but also to those who are harsh. 19 For it is to your credit if you endure pain while suffering unfairly because you are mindful of God. 20 What credit is it if you are beaten for doing wrong and you endure it? But if you suffer for doing good and endure it, this is pleasing to God. 21 For you were called to this, because Christ suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in His steps. 22 "He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in His mouth." 23 When He was insulted, He did not insult back; when He suffered, He did not threaten, but trusted Himself to the One who judges fairly. 24 "He Himself bore our sins" in His body on the tree, so that we might die to sins and live for what is right; "by His wounds you have been healed." 25 For "you were like sheep going astray," but now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.
