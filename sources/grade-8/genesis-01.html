God Creates the World

1 In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth. 2 The earth was empty and dark. Water covered everything, and God's Spirit moved over the water.

3 God said, "Let there be light," and there was light. 4 God saw that the light was good, so He separated the light from the darkness. 5 God called the light "day" and the darkness "night." There was evening, and there was morning. That was the first day.

God Makes the Sky, Land, and Plants

6 God said, "Let there be a sky in the middle of the waters to separate water from water." 7 So God made the sky and separated the water below it from the water above it. And it happened just as He said. 8 God called the sky "heavens." There was evening, and there was morning. That was the second day.

9 God said, "Let the water under the sky gather in one place so dry land can appear." And it happened. 10 God called the dry land "earth" and the gathered water "seas." God saw that it was good.

11 Then God said, "Let the earth grow plants. Let them make seeds, and let fruit trees make fruit with seeds in it, each kind matching its own kind." And it happened. 12 The earth grew plants and trees. Each kind made seeds or fruit with seeds, just as God had said. God saw that it was good. 13 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the third day.

God Makes the Sun, Moon, and Stars

14 God said, "Let there be lights in the sky to separate day from night. Let them be signs for seasons, days, and years. 15 Let them shine on the earth." And it happened. 16 God made two great lights. The bigger one rules the day, and the smaller one rules the night. He also made the stars. 17 God placed them in the sky to shine on the earth. 18 They rule over day and night, and they separate light from darkness. God saw that it was good. 19 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the fourth day.

God Makes Animals and People

20 God said, "Let the water be full of living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the sky." 21 So God created the great sea animals and every living thing that moves in the water. He made them by their kinds. He also made every kind of bird. God saw that it was good. 22 God blessed them and said, "Have many children. Fill the seas, and let the birds grow in number on the earth." 23 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the fifth day.

24 God said, "Let the earth bring out living animals. Let there be farm animals, crawling things, and wild animals, each by its kind." And it happened. 25 God made the wild animals, the farm animals, and everything that crawls on the ground, each by its kind. God saw that it was good.

26 Then God said, "Let Us make people in Our image, like Us. Let them rule over the fish, the birds, the farm animals, and all the earth and every crawling thing." 27 So God created people in His image. He created them male and female. 28 God blessed them and said, "Have many children. Fill the earth and take charge of it. Rule over the fish, the birds, and every living thing that moves on the earth."

29 God said, "Look, I have given you every plant that makes seed and every tree with fruit that has seed. They are for you to eat. 30 I have given every green plant to the animals, the birds, and everything that crawls on the earth—everything with life in it—for food." And it happened. 31 God looked at everything He had made, and it was very good. There was evening, and there was morning. That was the sixth day.
