The LORD’s Mountain in the Last Days

1 This is the message that Isaiah son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem.

2 In the last days, the mountain of the house of the LORD will be set up as the highest of the mountains. It will be lifted above the hills, and all nations will stream to it. 3 Many peoples will come and say, “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways, so we can walk in His paths.” For the law will go out from Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem. 4 He will judge between the nations and settle disputes for many peoples. They will turn their swords into plow blades and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not take up the sword against nation, and they will not learn war anymore.

A Warning to Jacob’s House

5 Come, people of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the LORD. 6 For You have abandoned Your people, the people of Jacob, because they are full of Eastern ways and fortune-tellers like the Philistines, and they make deals with foreigners. 7 Their land is full of silver and gold, with no end to their treasures. Their land is full of horses, with no end to their chariots. 8 Their land is full of idols. They bow down to the work of their own hands, to what their fingers have made. 9 So people are brought low, and everyone is humbled. Do not forgive them!

Hide from the LORD’s Majesty

10 Go into the rocks and hide in the dust, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty. 11 The proud look of man will be humbled, and the pride of people will be brought low. The LORD alone will be raised high on that day.

The Day of the LORD

12 For the Day of the LORD of Hosts will come against all who are proud and high, and against all that is lifted up. It will be brought low. 13 It will come against all the tall cedars of Lebanon and all the oaks of Bashan. 14 It will come against all the high mountains and all the high hills. 15 It will come against every high tower and every strong wall. 16 It will come against every ship from Tarshish and every beautiful treasure. 17 So human pride will be brought low, and the pride of people will be humbled. The LORD alone will be raised high on that day. 18 The idols will vanish completely. 19 People will run to caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth. 20 On that day, people will throw away their silver and gold idols—the idols they made to worship—to the moles and the bats. 21 They will run to cracks in the rocks and gaps in the cliffs, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth. 22 Stop trusting in people. They are only human, with breath in their noses. What are they worth?
