A Cry for Help in Trouble

1 Why do You stand so far away, the LORD? Why do You hide when trouble comes? 2 The wicked proudly hunt down the poor. Let them be caught in the traps they set. 3 The wicked brag about what they want. They praise greedy people and insult the the LORD. 4 In his pride, the wicked man does not seek God. All his plans say, "There is no God." 5 He always seems to get his way. Your judgments are far from his mind. He sneers at everyone who opposes him.

6 He says to himself, "I will never be shaken. I will never face trouble, not for generations."

7 His mouth is full of curses, lies, and threats. Trouble and evil hide under his tongue. 8 He hides near villages and waits. In secret places he kills innocent people. His eyes watch for the helpless. 9 He lurks in secret like a lion in its den. He waits to catch the poor and drags them into his net. 10 The helpless are crushed and beaten down. They fall under his power.

11 He says to himself, "God has forgotten. He has hidden His face. He will never notice."

12 Arise, the LORD! Take action, O God! Do not forget those who are hurting! 13 Why does the wicked man insult God? He tells himself, "You will not hold me accountable."

14 But You have seen the trouble and grief. You take it into Your own hands. The victim trusts You. You have been the helper of orphans. 15 Break the power of the wicked and those who do evil. Search out his wrongdoing until none is left.

16 The LORD is King forever and ever. The nations will perish from His land. 17 You have heard the longing of those who are hurting. You will strengthen their hearts. You will listen closely. 18 You will bring justice to orphans and those who are crushed, so that mere mortals will no longer terrify them.
