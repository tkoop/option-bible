Spies Sent to Jericho

1 Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two spies from Shittim. He told them, "Go and look at the land, especially Jericho." So they went and stayed at the house of Rahab, a prostitute. 2 The king of Jericho was told, "Men from Israel have come here tonight to spy out the land." 3 The king sent this message to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, because they came to spy out the whole land."

Rahab Hides the Spies

4 But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I didn't know where they were from." 5 "They left at dark, just as the gate was about to close. I don't know where they went. Pursue them quickly. You may catch them." 6 But she had taken them up to the roof and hidden them under stalks of flax she had laid out there. 7 The pursuers chased them along the road toward the Jordan River crossings. As soon as the pursuers had gone out, the gate was shut.

Rahab's Confession and Request

8 Before the spies lay down for the night, Rahab went up to the roof to talk to them. 9 She said to the men, "I know the the LORD has given you this land. We are terrified of you, and everyone in the land has melted with fear of you. 10 We heard how the the LORD dried up the Red Sea in front of you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan, whom you completely destroyed. 11 When we heard this, our hearts melted. Everyone lost courage because of you. For the the LORD your God is God in heaven above and on earth below. 12 Now please swear to me by the the LORD that, since I showed kindness to you, you will show kindness to my family. Give me a trustworthy sign 13 that you will spare my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and all who belong to them, and that you will save us from death."

14 "Our lives for yours!" the men said. "If you don't tell us about this mission of ours, we will show you kindness and faithfulness when the the LORD gives us the land."

The Escape and Report

15 Then she let them down by a rope through the window, because her house was built into the city wall. 16 She told them, "Go to the hills so the pursuers won't find you. Hide there for three days until they return. Then go on your way." 17 The men said to her, "We will keep our promise. We will be free from the oath you made us swear."

18 "When we enter the land, tie this scarlet cord in the window you used to lower us. Gather your father and mother, your brothers, and all your father's family into your house. 19 Anyone who goes out of your house into the street will be responsible for his own death, and we will be innocent. But if anyone with you in the house is harmed, we will take the blame. 20 But if you tell what we are doing, we won't be bound by the oath you made us swear." 21 "Agreed," she said. "As your say, so be it." Then she sent them away, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

22 They went into the hills and stayed there three days, until the pursuers returned. The pursuers had searched all along the road but didn't find them. 23 Then the two men came down from the hills, crossed the river, and went to Joshua son of Nun. They told him everything that had happened to them. 24 They said to Joshua, "The LORD has surely given the whole land into our hands. Everyone there is terrified of us."
