A Prayer for Safety and Help

1 In You, the LORD, I have taken refuge. Let me never be put to shame. 2 Rescue me and save me by Your righteousness. Turn Your ear to me and save me. 3 Be my safe rock, a place I can always go. You have ordered my rescue, because You are my rock and my fortress. 4 Save me, my God, from wicked people, from those who are unfair and cruel.

Hope from My Youth

5 You are my hope, Lord, the LORD, my confidence since I was young. 6 I have leaned on You since birth. You brought me out of my mother's womb. I will always praise You. 7 I have become like a sign to many people, but You are my strong safe place. 8 My mouth is full of praise for You, and I talk about Your greatness all day long.

Do Not Leave Me in Old Age

9 Do not throw me away when I am old. Do not leave me when my strength is gone. 10 My enemies speak against me. Those who want to kill me plan together. 11 They say, "God has abandoned him. Chase him and catch him, because no one will save him." 12 Do not stay far from me, my God. Hurry, my God, to help me. 13 Let those who accuse me be ashamed and come to nothing. Let those who want to hurt me be covered with shame and disgrace.

I Will Keep Praising You

14 But I will always hope and praise You more and more. 15 My mouth will tell of Your righteousness and Your saving help all day long, even though I cannot count them all. 16 I will come in the strength of the Lord, the LORD, and talk about Your righteousness. Yours alone. 17 God, You have taught me since I was young, and to this day I tell about Your wonderful works. 18 Even when I am old and gray, do not leave me, my God, until I tell Your power to the next generation and Your strength to everyone who is coming. 19 Your righteousness reaches up to the heavens, God. You have done great things. Who is like You, God? 20 You have shown me many troubles and hard times, but You will bring me back to life. From the deepest parts of the earth You will raise me up again. 21 You will make me more honored and comfort me again. 22 I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, my God. I will sing praise to You with the lyre, Holy One of Israel. 23 My lips will shout for joy when I sing praise to You, along with my soul, which You have saved. 24 My tongue will tell of Your righteousness all day long, because those who wanted to hurt me have been put to shame and confusion.
