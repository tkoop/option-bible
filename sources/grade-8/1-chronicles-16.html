The Ark Is Placed in David's Tent

1 They brought the ark of God and set it in the middle of the tent David had pitched for it. Then they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 2 When David finished offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the the LORD. 3 He gave every man and woman in Israel a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake.

4 David appointed some Levites to serve before the ark of the the LORD, to remember, to thank, and to praise the the LORD, the God of Israel. 5 Asaph was the leader, and Zechariah was second. Then came Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They played harps and lyres, while Asaph played the cymbals. 6 The priests Benaiah and Jahaziel blew trumpets regularly before the ark of the covenant of God.

David's Song of Thanks

7 That day David first gave this song of thanks to the the LORD through Asaph and his brothers:

8 “Give thanks to the the LORD. Call on His name. Tell the nations what He has done. 9 Sing to Him. Sing praise to Him. Tell of all His wonderful works. 10 Glory in His holy name. Let the hearts of those who seek the the LORD rejoice. 11 Seek the the LORD and His strength. Seek His face always. 12 Remember the wonders He has done, His miracles, and the judgments He spoke.

13 You are the descendants of Israel His servant, the children of Jacob, His chosen ones. 14 He is the the LORD our God. His judgments are in all the earth. 15 Remember His covenant forever, the word He commanded for a thousand generations. 16 He made that covenant with Abraham and swore an oath to Isaac. 17 He confirmed it to Jacob as a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant. 18 He said, “I will give you the land of Canaan as your portion to inherit.” 19 When you were few in number, very few, and strangers in that land, 20 they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another. 21 He let no one oppress them. He rebuked kings for their sake: 22 “Do not touch My anointed ones. Do no harm to My prophets.”

23 Sing to the the LORD, all the earth. Proclaim His salvation day after day. 24 Declare His glory among the nations, His wonderful works among all peoples. 25 For the the LORD is great and worthy of great praise. He is to be feared above all gods. 26 For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the the LORD made the heavens. 27 Splendor and majesty are before Him. Strength and joy are in His place. 28 Give the the LORD glory and strength, families of the nations. 29 Give the the LORD the glory due His name. Bring an offering and come before Him. Worship the the LORD in holy splendor. 30 Tremble before Him, all the earth. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved. 31 Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad. Let them say among the nations, “The LORD reigns.” 32 Let the sea roar and all that fills it. Let the fields be joyful and everything in them. 33 Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth.

34 Give thanks to the the LORD, for He is good. His faithful love endures forever. 35 Say, “Save us, O God of our salvation. Gather us and deliver us from the nations, so that we may give thanks to Your holy name and praise You.” 36 Blessed be the the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Then all the people said, “Amen,” and praised the the LORD.

Worship Leaders Are Appointed

37 David left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the the LORD to serve before the ark regularly, day by day. 38 Obed-edom and his sixty-eight relatives, along with Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah, served as gatekeepers. 39 David left Zadok the priest and his brothers, the priests, before the tabernacle of the the LORD at the high place in Gibeon. 40 They were to offer burnt offerings to the the LORD on the altar of burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, and to do everything written in the Law of the the LORD, which He had commanded Israel. 41 With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of those chosen and named to give thanks to the the LORD, because His faithful love endures forever. 42 Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets and cymbals for the musicians, and the other instruments for the songs of God. The sons of Jeduthun were gatekeepers.

The People Return Home

43 Then all the people went to their homes, and David went home to bless his household.
