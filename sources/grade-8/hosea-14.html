Return to the LORD

1 Return, Israel, to the the LORD your God. You have stumbled because of your sin.

2 Take words with you and return to the the LORD. Say to Him, "Take away all our guilt and receive us graciously, so we can offer You praise with our lips. 3 Assyria will not save us. We will not ride on horses, and we will no longer call the work of our hands our god. In You the orphan finds compassion."

God Promises to Restore Israel

4 "I will heal their turning away. I will love them freely, for My anger has turned away from him. 5 I will be like dew to Israel. he will blossom like a lily and put down roots like Lebanon. 6 his branches will spread. his beauty will be like the olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon. 7 Those who live in his shade will return. They will flourish like grain and blossom like a vine. his fame will be remembered like the wine of Lebanon. 8 Ephraim, what do I have to do with idols anymore? I answer him and take care of him. I am like a green cypress; your fruit comes from Me."

The Wise Will Understand

9 Who is wise? Let them understand these things. Who has good judgment? Let them know them. For the ways of the the LORD are right, and the righteous walk in them, but those who do wrong stumble in them.
