Wealth, Friends, and Honesty

1 Better a poor person who walks in honesty than a fool whose lips are crooked. 2 Even eagerness is no good without knowledge, and a person who rushes into things sins. 3 A person's own foolishness ruins his way, yet his heart is angry with the LORD.

4 Wealth brings many friends, but a poor person is deserted by his friend. 5 A false witness will not go unpunished, and a person who breathes out lies will not escape.

6 Many seek the favor of a leader, and everyone is a friend to a person who gives gifts. 7 All a poor person's relatives turn against him; how much more will his friends leave him! He begs them, but they are gone.

Wisdom, Patience, and Family

8 Whoever gains wisdom loves his own life; whoever keeps understanding will find good. 9 A false witness will not go unpunished, and a person who breathes out lies will perish. 10 It is not fitting for a fool to live in luxury—how much less for a servant to rule over princes!

11 A person's good sense makes him slow to anger, and it is his glory to overlook an offense. 12 A king's anger is like a lion's roar, but his favor is like dew on the grass.

13 A foolish son is his father's ruin, and a nagging wife is like a constant drip. 14 Houses and wealth are inherited from fathers, but a wise wife is from the LORD.

Discipline, Plans, and the LORD

15 Laziness brings deep sleep, and an idle person will go hungry. 16 Whoever keeps a command keeps his life, but whoever is careless in his ways will die. 17 Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the LORD, and He will repay him for what he has done.

18 Discipline your son, for there is hope; do not be a party to his death. 19 A hot-tempered person must pay the penalty; if you rescue him, you will only have to do it again. 20 Listen to advice and accept discipline, so that you may be wise for the rest of your days. 21 Many are the plans in a person's heart, but the counsel of the LORD will stand.

Character and Consequences

22 What a person wants is kindness, and a poor person is better than a liar. 23 The fear of the LORD leads to life, so one may sleep satisfied and untouched by evil. 24 A lazy person buries his hand in the dish, but he will not even bring it back to his mouth.

25 Strike a mocker, and the simple will learn good sense; rebuke a wise person, and he will gain knowledge. 26 A son who robs his father and drives out his mother brings shame and disgrace. 27 My son, if you stop listening to instruction, you will stray from words of knowledge.

28 A worthless witness mocks justice, and the mouth of the wicked swallows sin. 29 Judgments are prepared for mockers, and beatings for the backs of fools.
