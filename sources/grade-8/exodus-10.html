The LORD Sends Moses to Pharaoh

1 Then the the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh. I have made his heart and his servants' hearts hard, so I can show My signs among them. 2 You will tell your son and your grandson how I made Egypt look foolish and about the signs I put among them. Then you will know that I am the the LORD."

3 Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said, "This is what the the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: 'How long will you refuse to humble yourself before Me? Let My people go so they can serve Me. 4 If you refuse to let My people go, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your land. 5 They will cover the land so no one can see it. They will eat what the hail left, and they will eat every tree that grows for you in the field. 6 Your houses and your servants' houses and all the houses in Egypt will be full. Your fathers and grandfathers never saw anything like this from the day they lived here until now.'" Then he turned and left Pharaoh.

Pharaoh's Servants Speak Up

7 Pharaoh's servants said to him, "How long will this man keep causing trouble for us? Let the men go so they can serve the the LORD their God. Don't you know yet that Egypt is ruined?"

8 Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh, and he said, "Go, serve the the LORD your God. But who is going?"

9 Moses said, "We will go with our young and old. We will go with our sons and daughters, our sheep and cattle, because we have a festival to the the LORD."

10 He said to them, "May the the LORD be with you if I ever let you go with your little ones! Look, you have evil in mind. 11 No, only the men may go and serve the the LORD, because that is what you asked for." Then he drove them out from his presence.

The Plague of Locusts

12 Then the the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over Egypt for the locusts. Let them come over Egypt and eat every plant in the land, all that the hail left." 13 Moses stretched out his staff over Egypt. The the LORD brought an east wind over the land all that day and night. By morning the east wind had brought the locusts. 14 The locusts came over all Egypt and settled everywhere in Egypt. There had never been such a heavy locust plague before, and there never will be again. 15 They covered the whole land, and the land became dark. They ate every plant and all the fruit on the trees that the hail had left. No green thing was left on the trees or in the fields anywhere in Egypt.

16 Pharaoh hurried to call Moses and Aaron. He said, "I have sinned against the the LORD your God and against you. 17 Now please forgive my sin once more. Plead with the the LORD your God to take this deadly plague away from me."

18 Moses left Pharaoh and pleaded with the the LORD.

19 The the LORD changed the wind to a very strong west wind. It carried the locusts away and drove them into the Red Sea. Not one locust was left anywhere in Egypt. 20 But the the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the Israelites go.

Darkness Over Egypt

21 Then the the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky so darkness will come over Egypt, darkness so thick it can be felt."

22 Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky. Thick darkness covered all Egypt for three days. 23 People could not see each other, and no one got up from where he was for three days. But all the Israelites had light where they lived.

24 Pharaoh called Moses and said, "Go, serve the the LORD. Only leave your flocks and herds behind. Your little ones may go with you."

25 Moses said, "You must also give us animals for sacrifices and burnt offerings, so we can offer them to the the LORD our God. 26 Our livestock must go with us. Not one hoof will be left behind, because we need some of them to serve the the LORD our God. We do not know what we will need to serve the the LORD until we get there."

27 But the the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he would not let them go. 28 Pharaoh said to him, "Get away from me! Be careful never to see my face again, because on the day you see my face, you will die."

29 Moses said, "You are right. I will never see your face again."
