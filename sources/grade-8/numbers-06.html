The Nazirite Vow

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: If a man or a woman makes a special vow, a Nazirite vow, to dedicate himself to the LORD, 3 he must not drink wine or other fermented drink. He must not drink vinegar made from wine or other fermented drink. He must not drink grape juice, and he must not eat fresh grapes or dried grapes. 4 As long as he is a Nazirite, he must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine, from the seeds to the skins. 5 During all the days of his Nazirite vow, no razor may touch his head. He must be holy until the time is finished that he has dedicated himself to the LORD. He must let the hair on his head grow long. 6 During all the days he dedicates himself to the LORD, he must not go near a dead body. 7 He must not make himself unclean for his father, mother, brother, or sister when they die, because his dedication to God is on his head. 8 During all the days of his Nazirite vow, he is holy to the LORD.

9 "If someone dies suddenly near him and makes his head unclean, he must shave his head on the day he becomes clean—on the seventh day. 10 On the eighth day he must bring two doves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 11 The priest must offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. He must make atonement for him because he sinned by being near the dead body. He must make his head holy again that same day. 12 He must dedicate himself to the LORD again. He must bring a year-old male lamb as a guilt offering. The earlier days do not count, because his Nazirite vow was defiled.

Ending the Nazirite Vow

13 "This is the law for the Nazirite: When the days of his dedication are complete, he must be brought to the entrance to the tent of meeting. 14 He must present his offering to the LORD: one year-old male lamb without defect for a burnt offering, one year-old female lamb without defect for a sin offering, and one ram without defect for a peace offering. 15 He must also bring a basket of bread made without yeast—cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, and wafers coated with oil—plus their grain offering and drink offerings. 16 The priest must bring these before the LORD and sacrifice the sin offering and the burnt offering. 17 He must sacrifice the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, along with the basket of bread made without yeast. The priest must also offer the grain offering and the drink offering. 18 The Nazirite must shave off his dedicated hair at the entrance to the tent of meeting. He must take the hair from his head and put it on the fire under the peace offering. 19 The priest must take the boiled shoulder of the ram, one cake of bread made without yeast from the basket, and one wafer made without yeast. He must put them in the hands of the Nazirite after the Nazirite has shaved off his hair. 20 The priest must wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. They are holy and belong to the priest, along with the breast that was waved and the thigh that was lifted up. After that, the Nazirite may drink wine. 21 This is the law for the Nazirite who makes a vow. He must bring to the LORD whatever he vowed for his dedication, in addition to what he can afford. He must do exactly what he vowed, following the law for his Nazirite vow.

The Priestly Blessing

22 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 23 "Tell Aaron and his sons, 'This is how you must bless the Israelites. Say to them:'"

24 "The LORD bless you and keep you. 25 The LORD make His face shine on you and be gracious to you. 26 The LORD turn His face toward you and give you peace."

27 "So they will put My name on the Israelites, and I will bless them."
