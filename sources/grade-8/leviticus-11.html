Clean and Unclean Land Animals

1 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

2 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: Of all the animals that live on the land, 3 you may eat any animal that has a split hoof and chews the cud.

4 But there are animals that chew the cud or have a split hoof, and you must not eat them. The camel chews the cud, but it does not have a split hoof, so it is unclean for you. 5 The rock badger chews the cud, but it does not have a split hoof, so it is unclean for you. 6 The rabbit chews the cud, but it does not have a split hoof, so it is unclean for you. 7 The pig has a split hoof, but it does not chew the cud, so it is unclean for you. 8 You must not eat their meat or touch their dead bodies. They are unclean for you.

Water, Birds, and Insects

9 "Of all the creatures that live in the water, whether in the seas or streams, you may eat any that have fins and scales. 10 But every creature in the seas or streams that does not have fins and scales, whether it swarms or not, is detestable to you. 11 They must stay detestable to you. Do not eat their meat, and detest their dead bodies. 12 Any creature in the water that does not have fins and scales is detestable to you.

13 "These are the birds you must detest and not eat, because they are detestable: the eagle, the vulture, the black vulture, 14 the kite, any kind of falcon, 15 any kind of raven, 16 the horned owl, the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk, 17 the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl, 18 the white owl, the desert owl, the osprey, 19 the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.

20 "All flying insects that walk on four legs are detestable to you. 21 But you may eat those that have jointed legs for hopping on the ground. 22 Of these you may eat any kind of locust, katydid, cricket, or grasshopper. 23 All other flying insects that have four legs are detestable to you.

Touching Dead Animals

24 "You will make yourselves unclean by these animals. Anyone who touches their dead bodies will be unclean until evening. 25 Anyone who picks up one of their dead bodies must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. 26 Every animal that does not have a split hoof or does not chew the cud is unclean for you. Anyone who touches its dead body will be unclean. 27 Of all the animals that walk on four legs, those that walk on their paws are unclean for you. Anyone who touches their dead bodies will be unclean until evening. 28 Anyone who picks up their dead bodies must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. They are unclean for you.

29 "Of the animals that move along the ground, these are unclean for you: the mole rat, the mouse, any kind of large lizard, 30 the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon. 31 Of all these that move along the ground, they are unclean for you. Anyone who touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening. 32 When one of them dies and falls on something, that thing, whatever it is used for, becomes unclean. It may be made of wood, cloth, leather, or sackcloth. Put it in water, and it will be unclean until evening; then it will be clean. 33 If one of them falls into a clay pot, everything in the pot becomes unclean, and you must break the pot. 34 Any food that could be eaten but has water on it from such a pot is unclean, and any liquid in such a pot is unclean. 35 Anything that one of their dead bodies falls on becomes unclean. An oven or cooking pot must be broken. They are unclean, and you must treat them as unclean. 36 A spring, a cistern, or a collection of water stays clean, but anyone who touches one of their dead bodies is unclean. 37 If a dead body falls on any seeds that are to be planted, the seeds stay clean. 38 But if water has been put on the seed and a dead body falls on it, it is unclean for you.

39 "If an animal that you are allowed to eat dies, anyone who touches its dead body will be unclean until evening. 40 Anyone who eats some of its dead body must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. Anyone who picks up its dead body must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening.

41 "Every creature that moves along the ground is detestable. It must not be eaten. 42 You must not eat any creature that moves along the ground, whether it moves on its belly, walks on four legs, or has many feet. All such creatures are detestable. 43 Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these creatures. Do not make yourselves unclean by them, and do not let them make you unclean.

Be Holy Because God Is Holy

44 "I am the LORD your God. Set yourselves apart and be holy, because I am holy. Do not make yourselves unclean by any of the swarming creatures that move along the ground. 45 I am the LORD, who brought you up out of Egypt to be your God. Be holy, because I am holy. 46 These are the rules for animals, birds, every living thing that moves in the water, and every creature that moves along the ground. 47 You must tell the difference between the unclean and the clean, between animals that may be eaten and animals that may not be eaten."
