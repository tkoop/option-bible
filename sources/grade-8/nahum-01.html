Nahum's Vision About Nineveh

1 A message about Nineveh. This is the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

The LORD's Anger and Power

2 The LORD is a jealous and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and full of wrath. The LORD takes vengeance on His foes and stores up wrath for His enemies. 3 The LORD is slow to anger and great in power; the LORD will not let the guilty go unpunished. His path is in the whirlwind and the storm, and clouds are the dust of His feet.

4 He rebukes the sea and dries it up; He dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel wither, and the bloom of Lebanon fades. 5 The mountains shake before Him, and the hills melt away. The earth trembles at His presence, along with the world and everyone who lives in it. 6 Who can stand before His anger? Who can survive the heat of His wrath? His fury is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before Him.

The LORD Is a Refuge in Trouble

7 The LORD is good, a safe place in times of trouble. He knows those who trust in Him. 8 But with a rushing flood He will completely destroy its place, and darkness will chase His enemies.

Judgment on Nineveh and Hope for Judah

9 What do you plan against the LORD? He will completely destroy; trouble will not rise up a second time. 10 For they are tangled like thorns, and like drunkards they are destroyed; they burn like dry stubble. 11 From you came a person who plans evil against the LORD, a worthless counselor.

12 This is what the LORD says: "Though they are strong and many, they will be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more. 13 Now I will break his yoke off you and snap your bonds."

14 The LORD has commanded concerning you: "Your name will no longer be passed on. I will cut off the carved idol and the metal idol from the house of your gods. I will make your grave, for you are worthless."

15 Look, on the mountains are the feet of one who brings good news, who announces peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah; keep your vows. For the wicked will never again march through you; he is completely cut off.
