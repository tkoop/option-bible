The LORD Rules in Glory

1The LORD rules, so let the earth be glad! Let the distant shores rejoice! 2Clouds and thick darkness surround Him. Righteousness and justice are the base of His throne. 3Fire goes ahead of Him and burns up His enemies all around. 4His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees it and trembles. 5The mountains melt like wax before the the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth.

6The heavens declare His righteousness, and all nations see His glory. 7Everyone who worships idols is put to shame—those who brag about worthless gods. Worship Him, all you gods!

God's People Rejoice

8Zion hears and is glad. The daughters of Judah rejoice because of Your judgments, O the LORD. 9For You, O the LORD, are the Most High over all the earth. You are lifted up far above all gods. 10You who love the the LORD, hate evil! He keeps His faithful people safe and rescues them from the wicked. 11Light is given to those who do right, and joy to those with honest hearts. 12Rejoice in the the LORD, you righteous people, and give thanks to His holy name!
