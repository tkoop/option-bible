The Good News Begins

1 This is the beginning of the good news about Jesus Christ, the Son of God. 2 As it is written in Isaiah the prophet: "I will send My messenger ahead of You. He will prepare Your way. 3 A voice calls in the wilderness, 'Get ready for the Lord's way. Make straight paths for Him.'"

John Prepares the Way

4 John the Baptist came to the wilderness. He preached that people should be baptized and turn from their sins, so God would forgive them. 5 People from Jerusalem and all over Judea went out to him. They admitted their sins, and John baptized them in the Jordan River. 6 John wore clothes made from camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.

7 He preached, "After me comes someone stronger than I am. I am not even good enough to bend down and untie His sandals. 8 I baptized you with water, but He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit."

Jesus Is Baptized and Tempted

9 In those days, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee. John baptized Him in the Jordan River. 10 As Jesus came up out of the water, He saw heaven torn open. The Spirit came down on Him like a dove. 11 A voice from heaven said, "You are My Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with You."

12 Right away, the Spirit sent Him out into the wilderness. 13 He was in the wilderness for forty days. Satan tempted Him. He was with the wild animals, and angels took care of Him.

Jesus Begins His Work

14 After John was put in prison, Jesus went to Galilee. He preached the good news from God. 15 He said, "The time has come. God's kingdom is near. Turn from your sins and believe the good news."

16 As Jesus walked beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the lake, because they were fishermen. 17 Jesus said to them, "Come, follow Me, and I will make you fishers of people." 18 At once they left their nets and followed Him.

19 A little farther on, He saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat fixing their nets. 20 Right away He called them. They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed Him.

21 They went to Capernaum. On the Sabbath, Jesus went into the synagogue and began to teach. 22 The people were amazed at His teaching. He taught them like someone who had authority, not like the teachers of the law.

23 Just then, a man in the synagogue cried out. He was controlled by an evil spirit. 24 The man said, "What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!"

25 Jesus told the spirit sharply, "Be quiet! Come out of him!" 26 The evil spirit shook the man violently. Then it came out of him with a loud cry.

27 Everyone was so amazed that they asked each other, "What is this? A new teaching with authority! He even gives orders to evil spirits, and they obey Him." 28 News about Him spread quickly through the whole region of Galilee.

29 As soon as they left the synagogue, they went with James and John to the home of Simon and Andrew. 30 Simon's mother-in-law was in bed with a fever. They told Jesus about her right away. 31 He went to her, took her hand, and helped her up. The fever left her, and she began to serve them.

32 That evening, after sunset, people brought to Him everyone who was sick or controlled by demons. 33 The whole town gathered at the door. 34 He healed many who were sick with different diseases. He also drove out many demons. He would not let the demons speak, because they knew who He was.

35 Very early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up. He left the house and went to a lonely place, where He prayed. 36 Simon and his friends went to look for Him.

37 When they found Him, they said, "Everyone is looking for You."

38 Jesus said, "Let's go somewhere else—to the nearby towns—so I can preach there too. That is why I came." 39 So He traveled all through Galilee. He preached in their synagogues and drove out demons.

40 A man with leprosy came to Him. He begged Him and knelt down, saying, "If You are willing, You can make me clean."

41 Jesus was moved with pity. He reached out His hand and touched the man. He said, "I am willing. Be clean!"

42 Right away the leprosy left him, and he was healed. 43 Jesus sent him away at once. He gave him a strong warning. 44 He said, "Don't tell anyone about this. Instead, go and show yourself to the priest. Offer what Moses commanded for your cleansing, as a proof to them."

45 But the man went out and began to tell everyone. He spread the news so much that Jesus could no longer enter a town openly. He stayed outside in lonely places, but people still came to Him from everywhere.
