Herod Persecutes the Church

1 About that time, King Herod began to harm some people in the church. 2 He had James, the brother of John, killed with a sword. 3 When he saw that this pleased the Jews, he went on to arrest Peter too. This was during the Feast of Unleavened Bread. 4 After arresting him, he put him in prison and handed him over to four groups of four soldiers to guard him. Herod planned to bring him out for a public trial after Passover.

The Church Prays and Peter Escapes

5 So Peter was kept in prison, but the church prayed earnestly to God for him. 6 The night before Herod was going to bring him to trial, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers. He was bound with two chains, and guards stood at the door.

7 Suddenly an angel of the Lord appeared, and a light shone in the cell. He struck Peter on the side and woke him up. "Quick, get up!" he said, and the chains fell off Peter's wrists. 8 Then the angel said to him, "Put on your clothes and sandals." Peter did so. Then the angel said, "Wrap your cloak around you and follow me." 9 Peter followed him out of the prison, but he did not know that what the angel was doing was really happening. He thought he was seeing a vision. 10 They passed the first and second guards and came to the iron gate that led to the city. It opened by itself, and they went out. They walked down one street, and then the angel left him.

11 Then Peter came to himself and said, "Now I know for sure that the Lord sent His angel and rescued me from Herod's power and from all that the Jewish people were expecting." 12 When he realized this, he went to the house of Mary, the mother of John, who was also called Mark. Many people were gathered there and were praying.

13 He knocked at the outer door, and a servant girl named Rhoda came to answer it. 14 When she recognized Peter's voice, she was so happy that she did not open the gate. Instead, she ran in and told them that Peter was standing at the door.

15 "You are out of your mind!" they told her. But she kept insisting it was true. Then they said, "It must be his angel." 16 But Peter kept on knocking. When they opened the door and saw him, they were amazed.

17 Peter motioned with his hand for them to be quiet. Then he told them how the Lord had brought him out of prison. He said, "Tell James and the other believers about this." Then he left for another place.

Herod's Search and the Word of God Spreads

18 When morning came, there was a big uproar among the soldiers about what had happened to Peter. 19 After Herod searched for him and could not find him, he questioned the guards and ordered them to be put to death. Then Herod left Judea and went down to Caesarea, where he stayed.

20 Herod was angry with the people of Tyre and Sidon. They came to him together and, after winning over Blastus, the king's trusted servant, they asked for peace. They depended on the king's country for food. 21 On the set day, Herod put on his royal robes, sat on his throne, and gave a speech to them.

22 The people shouted, "This is the voice of a god, not of a man!" 23 Right away, because Herod did not give glory to God, an angel of the Lord struck him down. He was eaten by worms and died. 24 But the word of God kept spreading and growing. 25 When Barnabas and Saul finished their work, they returned from Jerusalem, bringing with them John, who was also called Mark.
