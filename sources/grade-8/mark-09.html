Jesus' Glory on the Mountain

1 “Truly I tell you, some of the people standing here won't die before they see the kingdom of God come with power.”

2 Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John and led them up a high mountain by themselves. There He was changed in front of them. 3 His clothes became very bright and white, whiter than anyone on earth could bleach them. 4 Elijah and Moses appeared to them and were talking with Jesus.

5 Peter said to Jesus, “Rabbi, it's good for us to be here. Let's make three shelters—one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.” 6 He didn't know what to say, because they were terrified.

7 A cloud came and covered them, and a voice came from the cloud: “This is My Son, whom I love. Listen to Him!” 8 Suddenly they looked around and saw no one else with them except Jesus.

9 As they were coming down the mountain, Jesus told them not to tell anyone what they had seen until the Son of Man rose from the dead. 10 They kept this to themselves and talked about what “rising from the dead” meant.

11 They asked Him, “Why do the teachers of the law say Elijah must come first?”

12 He answered, “Elijah does come first and restores all things. Why then is it written that the Son of Man must suffer much and be rejected? 13 But I tell you, Elijah has already come, and people did to him whatever they wanted, just as it is written about him.”

A Boy with an Evil Spirit

14 When they came back to the other disciples, they saw a large crowd around them and the teachers of the law arguing with them. 15 As soon as all the people saw Jesus, they were amazed and ran to greet Him.

16 He asked them, “What are you arguing with them about?” 17 A man in the crowd answered, “Teacher, I brought You my son. He has a spirit that makes him unable to speak. 18 Whenever it takes hold of him, it throws him down. He foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and becomes stiff. I asked Your disciples to drive it out, but they couldn't.”

19 He said, “You unbelieving generation! How long will I stay with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy to Me.” 20 So they brought him. When the spirit saw Jesus, it immediately threw the boy into a fit. He fell to the ground and rolled around, foaming at the mouth.

21 Jesus asked the boy's father, “How long has this been happening to him?” “From childhood,” the father answered. 22 “It has often thrown him into fire or water to kill him. But if You can do anything, have pity on us and help us.”

23 Jesus said, “‘If You can’? Everything is possible for the one who believes.” 24 The boy's father cried out at once, “I do believe; help me with my unbelief!”

25 When Jesus saw that a crowd was running to the scene, He scolded the evil spirit. “You deaf and mute spirit,” He said, “I command you, come out of him and never enter him again.” 26 The spirit cried out, shook him violently, and came out. The boy looked so much like a dead person that many said, “He's dead.” 27 But Jesus took him by the hand and helped him up, and he stood.

28 After Jesus went inside, His disciples asked Him privately, “Why couldn't we drive it out?” 29 He answered, “This kind can come out only by prayer.”

Jesus Predicts His Death and Teaches About Greatness

30 They left that place and traveled through Galilee. Jesus didn't want anyone to know where they were, 31 because He was teaching His disciples. He told them, “The Son of Man is going to be handed over to people. They will kill Him, and after three days He will rise.” 32 But they didn't understand what He meant and were afraid to ask Him.

33 They came to Capernaum. When Jesus was in the house, He asked them, “What were you arguing about on the road?” 34 But they kept quiet because on the way they had argued about who was the greatest.

35 Sitting down, Jesus called the Twelve and said, “If anyone wants to be first, he must be last of all and servant of all.” 36 He took a little child and had the child stand among them. He took the child in His arms and said to them, 37 “Whoever welcomes one of these little children in My name welcomes Me, and whoever welcomes Me welcomes not just Me but the One who sent Me.”

38 John said to Him, “Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons in Your name, and we tried to stop him because he wasn't one of us.”

39 Jesus said, “Do not stop him, for no one who does a miracle in My name can soon speak evil of Me. 40 For whoever is not against us is for us. 41 Truly I tell you, anyone who gives you a cup of water in My name because you belong to Christ will certainly not lose their reward.”

Warnings About Sin and Salt

42 “If anyone causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it would be better for them to have a large millstone hung around their neck and be thrown into the sea. 43 If your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life maimed than to have two hands and go into hell, into the fire that never goes out. 45 If your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life lame than to have two feet and be thrown into hell. 47 If your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out. It is better for you to enter the kingdom of God with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into hell, 48 where the worms never die and the fire never goes out.”

49 “Everyone will be salted with fire. 50 Salt is good, but if it loses its saltiness, how can you make it salty again? Have salt among yourselves, and be at peace with each other.”
