Listen to the LORD and Remember His Promises

1 Listen to Me, you who chase what is right, you who look for the LORD. Look at the rock you were cut from, and the pit you were dug from. 2 Look at Abraham, your father, and at Sarah, who gave birth to you. I called him when he was only one man. Then I blessed him and made his family grow. 3 The LORD will comfort Zion. He will comfort all her ruined places. He will make her desert like Eden and her dry land like the garden of the LORD. Joy and gladness will be found there, along with thanks and singing.

4 Pay attention to Me, My people, and listen to Me, My nation. A law will come from Me, and My justice will be a light for the nations. I will make it happen soon. 5 My righteousness is near. My salvation has already gone out. My arms will bring justice to the nations. The faraway lands wait for Me, and they put their hope in My arm.

The LORD’s Salvation Lasts Forever

6 Lift your eyes to the sky, and look at the earth below. The sky will disappear like smoke, and the earth will wear out like old clothes. The people who live on it will die like gnats. But My salvation will last forever, and My righteousness will never be broken.

7 Listen to Me, you who know what is right, you people who have My law in your hearts. Do not fear the scorn of people, and do not be crushed by their insults. 8 The moth will eat them like clothes, and worms will eat them like wool. But My righteousness will last forever, and My salvation will go on for all generations.

The LORD Will Rescue His People

9 Wake up, wake up! Put on strength, arm of the LORD! Wake up like in the old days, in years long ago. Was it not You who cut Rahab to pieces and stabbed the dragon? 10 Was it not You who dried up the sea, the water of the great deep, and made a road through the deep sea for the saved to cross? 11 So the saved people of the LORD will return and come to Zion with singing. Everlasting joy will crown their heads. Gladness and joy will catch up with them, and sorrow and sighing will run away.

God Comforts His People

12 I, even I, am the One who comforts you. Who are you that you are afraid of a person who dies, or of a human who fades like grass? 13 You have forgotten the LORD, your Maker, who stretched out the sky and laid the foundations of the earth. You live in fear all day long because of the anger of the one who crushes people, who is ready to destroy. But where is that anger now? 14 The prisoner will soon be set free. He will not die in the pit, and he will not run out of bread. 15 I am the LORD, your God, who stirs up the sea so its waves roar. The LORD of Hosts is His name. 16 I put My words in your mouth and covered you with the shadow of My hand. I did this to plant the sky and lay the foundations of the earth, and to say to Zion, "You are My people."

Wake Up, Jerusalem

17 Wake up, wake up! Stand up, Jerusalem! You drank from the LORD’s hand the cup of His anger. You drank the bowl of staggering and drained it. 18 Out of all the sons she has given birth to, no one is there to guide her. Out of all the sons she has raised, no one takes her hand. 19 These two things have happened to you: ruin and destruction, famine and sword. Who will comfort you? Who will grieve for you? 20 Your sons have fainted. They lie at the head of every street like an animal caught in a net. They are full of the LORD’s anger and the rebuke of your God. 21 So listen to this, you who are hurting, drunk but not from wine.

22 This is what your Lord says—the LORD, your God, who defends His people: "I have taken from your hand the cup of staggering. You will never drink the bowl of My anger again. 23 I will put it in the hands of your tormentors, who said to you, 'Bow down so we can walk over you.' You made your back like the ground, like a street for people to walk on."
