Job Keeps His Integrity

1 Job continued speaking and said, 2 “As surely as God lives, who took away my justice, and the Almighty, who made my soul bitter,

3 as long as I have breath in me and God’s Spirit is in my nostrils, 4 my lips will not speak evil, and my tongue will not say anything false. 5 Far be it from me to say you are right. I will not give up my integrity until I die. 6 I will hold on to my right living and will not let it go. My heart will not shame me as long as I live. 7 Let my enemy be like the wicked, and my opponent like an evildoer.”

8 “For what hope does a godless person have when he is cut off, when God takes away his life? 9 Will God hear his cry when trouble comes on him? 10 Will he delight in the Almighty? Will he call on God at all times?”

Job Teaches About God’s Hand

11 “I will teach you about God’s hand. I will not hide what belongs to the Almighty. 12 Look, all of you have seen it. So why do you talk so pointlessly?”

The Portion of the Wicked

13 “This is what a wicked person receives from God, and the inheritance that oppressors get from the Almighty: 14 If his sons increase, it is for the sword, and his children will not have enough bread. 15 Those who survive him will die and be buried, and his widows will not weep. 16 Though he piles up silver like dust and stores up clothing like clay, 17 he may get it ready, but a righteous person will wear it, and an innocent person will divide the silver. 18 He builds his house like a moth’s, and like a shelter a watchman sets up. 19 He lies down rich, but he will not be gathered up. He opens his eyes, and he is gone. 20 Terrors overtake him like a flood; a storm steals him away at night. 21 The east wind carries him off, and he is gone. It sweeps him out of his place. 22 It hurls itself against him without pity. He runs from its power. 23 It claps its hands at him and hisses at him from its place.”
