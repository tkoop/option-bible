A Warning Against Tyre

1 This is a message about Tyre: Wail, ships of Tarshish, because Tyre is destroyed. There is no house left and no harbor. The news has come to them from the land of Cyprus.

2 Be quiet, you people who live along the coast, and you merchants of Sidon whose traders crossed the sea. 3 On the great waters came the grain of the Nile. The harvest of the river was her wealth, and she became the marketplace of the nations.

4 Be ashamed, Sidon, fortress of the sea, because the sea says, "I have not been in labor or given birth. I have not raised young men or brought up young women." 5 When the news reaches Egypt, they will be in pain at the news about Tyre.

6 Cross over to Tarshish. Wail, you people who live along the coast. 7 Is this your joyful city? It began long ago, and its people carried it to faraway lands to settle.

The LORD Plans Tyre's Fall

8 Who planned this against Tyre, the city that crowns others? Its merchants are like princes, and its traders are honored people of the earth. 9 The LORD of Hosts planned it, to bring down all her pride and beauty and to humble all the honored people of the earth.

10 Flood your land like the Nile, daughter of Tarshish; the harbor is gone. 11 He has stretched out His hand over the sea and shaken kingdoms. The LORD has given a command about Canaan to destroy its strongholds. 12 And He said, "You will no longer rejoice, you crushed virgin daughter of Sidon. Get up and cross over to Cyprus. Even there you will find no rest."

13 Look at the land of the Chaldeans. They were not a people. Assyria made it a home for desert animals. They built its towers, stripped its palaces, and left it in ruins. 14 Wail, ships of Tarshish, because your stronghold is destroyed.

Tyre Forgotten and Restored

15 In that day Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, like the life of one king. At the end of seventy years, Tyre will be like the song of a prostitute. 16 Take a harp and go around the city, you forgotten prostitute. Play well and sing many songs, so you will be remembered.

17 After seventy years, the LORD will pay attention to Tyre. She will go back to earning money and will sell herself to every kingdom on earth. 18 But her profits and her pay will be holy to the LORD. They will not be stored or saved. Instead, her profits will provide food and clothing for those who worship the LORD.
