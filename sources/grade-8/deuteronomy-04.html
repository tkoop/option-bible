Moses Urges Israel to Obey God's Commands

1 "Now, Israel, listen to the laws and rules I am teaching you. Do them so you can live, go in, and take the land the the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you. 2 Do not add anything to what I command you, and do not take anything away from it. Keep the commands of the the LORD your God that I give you. 3 You saw with your own eyes what the the LORD did at Baal Peor. The the LORD your God destroyed from among you everyone who followed Baal of Peor. 4 But all of you who stayed close to the the LORD your God are alive today.

5 "See, I have taught you the laws and rules, just as the the LORD my God commanded me. Follow them in the land you are entering to take. 6 Keep them and do them. They will be your wisdom and understanding before the nations. When those nations hear all these laws, they will say, 'Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.' 7 What great nation has a god so near to it as the the LORD our God is to us whenever we call on Him? 8 What great nation has laws and rules as fair as all this law I am giving you today?

Remember What God Did at Horeb

9 "Only be very careful. Guard your soul closely so you do not forget what your eyes have seen. Do not let these things leave your heart as long as you live. Teach them to your children and grandchildren. 10 Remember the day you stood before the the LORD your God at Horeb. The the LORD said to me, 'Bring the people to Me so I can let them hear My words. Then they will learn to fear Me all the days they live on the earth, and they will teach their children.' 11 You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain. The mountain burned with fire up to the sky. It was dark, with clouds and thick darkness. 12 Then the the LORD spoke to you from the fire. You heard the sound of words, but you saw no form. There was only a voice. 13 He told you His covenant, which He commanded you to keep: the Ten Commandments. He wrote them on two stone tablets. 14 At that time the the LORD commanded me to teach you the laws and rules, so you could do them in the land you are crossing over to take.

Do Not Make Idols or Worship Created Things

15 "So guard yourselves very carefully. You saw no form on the day the the LORD spoke to you from the fire at Horeb. 16 Be careful not to ruin yourselves by making a carved idol. Do not make an image of any shape, male or female. 17 Do not make an image of any animal on earth, any bird that flies in the sky, 18 anything that crawls on the ground, or any fish in the water below. 19 Do not look up to the sky and be led astray. When you see the sun, moon, and stars, all the host of heaven, do not bow down to them or serve them. The the LORD your God has given them to all the nations under the whole sky. 20 But the the LORD took you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt. He made you His own people, as you are today.

21 "Furthermore, the the LORD was angry with me because of you. He swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land the the LORD your God is giving you as your own. 22 I will die in this land. I will not cross the Jordan. But you will cross over and take this good land. 23 Be careful not to forget the covenant of the the LORD your God that He made with you. Do not make a carved idol of any kind that the the LORD your God has forbidden. 24 The the LORD your God is a consuming fire. He is a jealous God.

25 "When you have children and grandchildren, and you have grown old in the land, do not ruin yourselves. Do not make a carved idol of any kind, and do not do evil in the sight of the the LORD your God, because that makes Him angry. 26 I call heaven and earth to witness against you today. You will quickly be destroyed and removed from the land you are crossing the Jordan to take. You will not live there long. You will be completely destroyed. 27 The the LORD will scatter you among the peoples. Only a few of you will survive among the nations where the the LORD drives you. 28 There you will serve gods of wood and stone, made by human hands. They cannot see, hear, eat, or smell.

God's Mercy, Power, and the Cities of Refuge

29 "But from there you will seek the the LORD your God. You will find Him if you search for Him with all your heart and all your soul. 30 When you are in trouble and all these things happen to you in the last days, you will return to the the LORD your God and obey His voice. 31 The the LORD your God is merciful. He will not leave you or destroy you. He will not forget the covenant with your fathers, which He swore to them.

32 "Ask about the past, before your time, since the day God created man on the earth. Ask from one end of the sky to the other. Has anything this great ever happened before? Has anyone ever heard of it? 33 Has any nation ever heard the voice of a god speaking from the fire, as you have heard, and lived? 34 Has any god ever tried to take a nation for himself from another nation by tests, signs, wonders, war, a mighty hand, an outstretched arm, and great terrors? That is what the the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes.

35 "You were shown this so you would know that the the LORD is God. There is no other besides Him. 36 From heaven He let you hear His voice to teach you. On earth He let you see His great fire, and you heard His words from the fire. 37 Because He loved your fathers, He chose their children after them. He brought you out of Egypt with His own presence and great power. 38 He drove out nations greater and stronger than you so He could bring you in and give you their land as your own, as it is today. 39 Know today, and take it to heart, that the the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth below. There is no other. 40 Keep His laws and commands, which I give you today, so it will go well with you and your children after you. Then you will live a long time in the land the the LORD your God is giving you for all your days."

41 Then Moses set apart three cities east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise. 42 A person who killed someone by accident, without hating them before, could run to one of these cities and live. 43 Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau for the Reubenites, Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites, and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.

44 This is the law Moses set before the Israelites. 45 These are the rules, laws, and commands Moses spoke to the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

46 This was east of the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. Moses and the Israelites defeated him when they came out of Egypt. 47 They took his land and the land of Og king of Bashan. These were the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise. 48 Their land ran from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley to Mount Sirion, that is, Hermon. 49 It also included all the Arabah east of the Jordan, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, below the slopes of Pisgah.
