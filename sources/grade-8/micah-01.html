Judgment on Samaria and Jerusalem

1 This is the word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. He saw a vision about Samaria and Jerusalem. 2 Hear, all you peoples; listen, earth and everything in it. Let the Lord, the LORD, be a witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple. 3 Look, the LORD is coming from His place. He will come down and walk on the high places of the earth. 4 The mountains will melt under Him, and the valleys will split open, like wax near a fire, like water poured down a hill. 5 All this is because of Jacob's sin and the sins of Israel's family. What is Jacob's sin? Isn't it Samaria? What is Judah's high place? Isn't it Jerusalem?

6 So I will make Samaria a pile of rubble in an open field, a place to plant vineyards. I will throw her stones into the valley and uncover her foundations. 7 All her carved images will be smashed. All her wages will be burned with fire. I will destroy all her idols. She gathered them like a prostitute's pay, and they will be used that way again.

Micah Mourns the Coming Disaster

8 Because of this I will cry and wail. I will walk barefoot and naked. I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich. 9 Her wound cannot be healed. It has reached Judah. It has touched the gate of my people, all the way to Jerusalem.

Judgment on the Cities of Judah

10 Do not tell it in Gath. Do not weep at all. Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah. 11 Pass on in naked shame, people of Shaphir. The people of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel mourns and takes away its support from you. 12 The people of Maroth wait for something good, but disaster has come down from the LORD to the gate of Jerusalem. 13 Harness the chariot to the fast horse, people of Lachish. She was the beginning of sin for the Daughter of Zion, because Israel's sins were found in you.

14 So you will give farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath. The houses of Achzib will prove false to the kings of Israel. 15 I will bring another conqueror against you, people of Mareshah. The glory of Israel will reach Adullam. 16 Shave your head and cut off your hair for the children you loved. Make yourself as bald as an eagle, because they have gone from you into exile.
