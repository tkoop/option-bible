A Song of Ascents

1 In my trouble I called to the LORD, and He answered me. 2 Deliver my soul, the LORD, from lying lips and from a deceitful tongue.

3 What will be given to you, and what more will be done to you, you deceitful tongue? 4 Sharp arrows of a warrior, with burning coals of a broom bush.

5 Woe to me, for I live in Meshech; I stay among the tents of Kedar. 6 I have lived too long with people who hate peace. 7 I am for peace, but when I speak, they are for war.
