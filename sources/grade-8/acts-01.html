1 In my first book, Theophilus, I wrote about everything Jesus began to do and teach. 2 He kept teaching until the day He was taken up to heaven. Before He left, through the Holy Spirit, He gave commands to the apostles He had chosen. 3 After His suffering, He showed Himself to them alive by many proofs. He appeared to them over forty days and spoke about God's kingdom.

Jesus Promises the Holy Spirit

4 While He was meeting with them, He told them, "Do not leave Jerusalem. Wait for the Father's promise, which you have heard Me speak about. 5 John baptized with water, but in a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit."

6 So when they were together, they asked Him, "Lord, are You going to restore the kingdom to Israel now?"

7 He said to them, "It is not for you to know the times or seasons the Father has set by His own authority. 8 But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you, and you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."

Jesus Ascends to Heaven

9 After He said this, He was lifted up while they watched, and a cloud hid Him from their sight. 10 While they were staring into the sky as He went, two men in white clothes stood beside them. 11 They said, "Men of Galilee, why do you stand here looking into the sky? This Jesus, who was taken from you into heaven, will come back the same way you saw Him go into heaven."

The Apostles Pray Together

12 Then they returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which is near Jerusalem, about a Sabbath day's walk away. 13 When they arrived, they went up to the upper room where they were staying. Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James were there. 14 They all joined together in prayer, along with the women, Mary the mother of Jesus, and His brothers.

15 In those days Peter stood up among the believers. About one hundred twenty people were there. He said, 16 "Brothers, the Scripture had to be fulfilled. The Holy Spirit spoke long ago through David about Judas, who led the men who arrested Jesus. 17 He was counted among us and shared in this ministry. 18 With the pay he got for his wrongdoing, he bought a field. He fell headfirst, his body burst open, and his insides spilled out. 19 Everyone in Jerusalem heard about it, so they called that field in their own language Akeldama, which means Field of Blood. 20 For it is written in the Book of Psalms: 'Let his home be left empty, with no one living in it,' and, 'Let another take his place of leadership.' 21 So we need to choose one of the men who has been with us the whole time the Lord Jesus was among us, from John's baptism until the day Jesus was taken up from us. 22 He will join us as a witness of Jesus' resurrection."

Matthias Is Chosen to Replace Judas

23 They put forward two men: Joseph called Barsabbas, also known as Justus, and Matthias. 24 Then they prayed, "Lord, You know everyone's heart. Show us which of these two You have chosen 25 to take Judas's place in this work and mission, which Judas left to go to his own place." 26 Then they cast lots, and the lot fell to Matthias. So he was added to the eleven apostles.
