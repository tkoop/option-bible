Blessing for Those Who Care for the Poor

1 Blessed is the person who cares for someone in need. The LORD will rescue him in times of trouble. 2 The LORD protects him and keeps him alive. He blesses him in the land, and He won't hand him over to his enemies. 3 The LORD supports him on his sickbed and helps him recover from his illness.

A Prayer for Mercy

4 I said, "O the LORD, be gracious to me. Heal me, because I have sinned against You."

Enemies and Betrayal

5 My enemies say bad things about me: "When will he die and his name be forgotten?" 6 When someone comes to see me, he says empty things. He stores up trouble in his heart, then goes out and spreads it around. 7 All who hate me whisper together against me and imagine the worst for me: 8 "A deadly disease has been poured on him. He will never get up from where he lies."

9 Even my close friend, the one I trusted, who shared my bread, has turned against me.

Confidence in the LORD

10 But You, O the LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, so I can repay them. 11 By this I know You are pleased with me: my enemy does not shout in triumph over me. 12 Because of my integrity, You hold me up and set me in Your presence forever. 13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen.
