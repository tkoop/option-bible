Looking to God for Mercy

1 A song of ascents. I lift my eyes to You, the One who sits in heaven. 2 Look, as servants look to their master's hand, and a servant girl looks to her mistress's hand, so our eyes look to the LORD our God until He shows us mercy.

3 Have mercy on us, O the LORD, have mercy on us, for we have had more than enough contempt. 4 We have had more than enough scorn from those who are carefree, and contempt from the proud.
