David Thanks the LORD

1 I will give You thanks with all my heart. Before the gods I will sing Your praise. 2 I will bow down toward Your holy temple and give thanks to Your name for Your faithful love and Your truth. For You have raised Your name and Your word above all else. 3 On the day I called, You answered me. You strengthened me and filled me with courage.

All Kings Will Praise the LORD

4 All the kings of the earth will thank You, O the LORD, when they hear the words of Your mouth. 5 They will sing about the ways of the LORD, for great is the glory of the LORD.

The LORD Looks on the Lowly

6 Though the LORD is high above all, He cares for the lowly, but He sees the proud from far away.

The LORD Will Protect and Finish His Work

7 Even when I walk through trouble, You keep me alive. You reach out Your hand against my angry enemies, and Your right hand saves me. 8 The LORD will finish what He has planned for me. O the LORD, Your faithful love lasts forever. Do not give up on the work of Your hands.
