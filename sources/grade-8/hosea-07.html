Israel's Hidden Sin

1 When I wanted to heal Israel, Ephraim's guilt and Samaria's crimes came to light. They lie and cheat. Thieves break in, and robbers attack in the streets. 2 They do not think about the fact that I remember all their evil. Their sins surround them, and they are right in front of Me. 3 They make the king happy with their evil, and the leaders happy with their lies.

The Oven of Sin

4 They are all unfaithful. They are like an oven a baker heats. He stops tending the fire while the dough rises, until it is ready. 5 On our king's special day, the leaders got drunk on wine, and he joined hands with mockers. 6 In their hearts they are like that hot oven. They wait to attack. Their baker sleeps all night, but by morning the oven blazes like a fire. 7 All of them are as hot as an oven, and they destroy their judges. All their kings fall, and none of them calls on Me.

Foolish Alliances

8 Ephraim mixes with other nations. Ephraim is like a flatbread that was never flipped, so it is only half-baked. 9 Strangers have eaten up his strength, but he does not know it. Gray hair has spread over him, but he does not know it. 10 Israel's pride speaks against them. Still, they do not return to the LORD their God, and they do not seek Him, even after all this. 11 So Ephraim is like a silly dove with no sense. It calls to Egypt, then flies to Assyria.

God's Net of Judgment

12 As they go, I will spread My net over them. I will bring them down like birds in the sky. I will punish them as their community has heard. 13 It will be terrible for them, because they have wandered away from Me. They will be destroyed, because they rebelled against Me. Even though I wanted to save them, they tell lies about Me. 14 They do not cry out to Me from their hearts. Instead, they wail on their beds. They cut themselves to beg for grain and new wine, but they turn away from Me. 15 I trained them and made their arms strong, but they plan evil against Me. 16 They turn, but not to the Most High. They are like a bow that does not shoot straight. Their leaders will fall by the sword because of their cursing. People in Egypt will laugh at them.
