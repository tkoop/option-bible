David Prepares a Place for the Ark

1 David built houses for himself in the city of David. He prepared a place for the ark of God and set up a tent for it. 2 Then David said, "No one but the Levites may carry the ark of God. the LORD chose them to carry the ark of the LORD and to serve Him forever." 3 David gathered all Israel in Jerusalem to bring the ark of the LORD to the place he had prepared. 4 He also gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites.

5 From the Kohathites, Uriel was the leader, with 120 relatives. 6 From the Merarites, Asaiah was the leader, with 220 relatives. 7 From the Gershonites, Joel was the leader, with 130 relatives. 8 From the Elizaphanites, Shemaiah was the leader, with 200 relatives. 9 From the Hebronites, Eliel was the leader, with 80 relatives. 10 From the Uzzielites, Amminadab was the leader, with 112 relatives.

David Calls the Levites to Serve

11 David called the priests Zadok and Abiathar, along with the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab. 12 He said to them, "You are the heads of the Levite families. You and your brothers must become holy before God. Bring the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, to the place I prepared for it. 13 Because you did not carry it the first time, the LORD our God broke out against us. We did not seek Him in the proper way." 14 So the priests and Levites set themselves apart to bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel. 15 The Levites carried the ark of God on their shoulders with poles, as Moses had commanded by the word of the LORD.

16 David told the Levite leaders to appoint their fellow Levites as singers. They were to play harps, lyres, and cymbals loudly and raise a joyful sound. 17 The Levites appointed Heman son of Joel. From his relatives they appointed Asaph son of Berechiah. From the Merarites they appointed Ethan son of Kushaiah. 18 With them were their relatives of the second rank: Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.

19 The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound the bronze cymbals. 20 Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play harps in a high key. 21 Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to lead with lyres in a low key. 22 Chenaniah, leader of the Levites, was in charge of carrying the ark. He gave instructions because he understood the work. 23 Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark. 24 The priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer blew trumpets before the ark of God. Obed-Edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.

The Ark Is Brought to Jerusalem

25 David, the elders of Israel, and the commanders of thousands went joyfully to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the house of Obed-Edom. 26 Because God helped the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams. 27 David wore a fine linen robe. So did the Levites who carried the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah, who led the carriers. David also wore a linen ephod. 28 All Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the LORD with shouting, the sound of a horn, trumpets, and cymbals. They also played harps and lyres. 29 As the ark of the covenant of the LORD came to the city of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out a window. She saw King David dancing and celebrating, and she despised him in her heart.
