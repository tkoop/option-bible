Remembering God's Goodness and Israel's Sin

1 Praise the the LORD! Thank the the LORD, because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 2 Who can speak of the the LORD's mighty acts? Who can tell all His praise? 3 Happy are the people who keep justice and do what is right all the time. 4 Remember me, the the LORD, when You show kindness to Your people. Come help me with Your saving power. 5 Let me see the good things You give Your chosen people. Let me be glad with the joy of Your nation and join in praising You with the people You own.

6 We have sinned like our ancestors. We have done wrong and acted wickedly. 7 Our ancestors in Egypt did not understand Your miracles. They did not remember Your great kindness. They rebelled by the Red Sea. 8 Yet He saved them to honor His name and show His mighty power. 9 He spoke sharply to the Red Sea, and it dried up. He led them through the deep water like through a desert. 10 He saved them from their enemies and set them free. 11 The water covered their enemies, and not one of them was left. 12 Then they believed His words and sang His praise.

13 But they soon forgot what He had done and did not wait for His plan. 14 They became greedy in the desert and tested God in the dry land. 15 He gave them what they asked for, but He sent a sickness that made them weak. 16 In the camp they became jealous of Moses and Aaron, the the LORD's holy one. 17 The ground opened up and swallowed Dathan. It covered Abiram's followers. 18 Fire broke out among them, and flames burned up the wicked people.

19 At Mount Horeb they made a calf and worshiped a statue made of metal. 20 They traded their glorious God for a statue of a bull that eats grass. 21 They forgot God, their Savior, who did great things in Egypt. 22 He did amazing things in Egypt and terrifying things at the Red Sea. 23 So He said He would destroy them. But Moses, His chosen servant, stepped in and turned His anger away so He would not destroy them.

24 They rejected the good land and did not believe His promise. 25 They grumbled in their tents and did not listen to the the LORD's voice. 26 So He raised His hand and swore He would make them die in the desert. 27 He would scatter their children among the nations and send them to other lands. 28 They gave themselves to the worship of Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to the dead. 29 They made Him angry by what they did, so a plague broke out among them.

30 But Phinehas stood up and stepped in, and the plague stopped. 31 This was counted to him as right living for all time to come. 32 They made the the LORD angry at the waters of Meribah, and Moses suffered because of them. 33 They rebelled against His Spirit, and Moses spoke without thinking. 34 They did not destroy the nations the the LORD had told them to destroy.

35 Instead, they mixed with the nations and learned their ways. 36 They worshiped their idols, which became a trap for them. 37 They sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons. 38 They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters—by sacrificing them to the idols of Canaan. The land was polluted with blood. 39 They became unclean by their actions and were unfaithful in what they did. 40 So the the LORD became angry with His people, and He was disgusted with the people He had chosen.

41 He let other nations take them, and their enemies ruled over them. 42 Their enemies pressed hard on them, and they were brought low under their power. 43 Many times He saved them, but they kept rebelling against His plan, and their sin pulled them down. 44 Yet He saw their trouble and heard their cry. 45 He remembered His promise to them and showed mercy because of His great faithful love. 46 He caused their captors to have mercy on them.

47 Save us, the the LORD our God! Gather us from the nations so we can thank Your holy name and praise You. 48 May the the LORD, the God of Israel, be praised from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, "Amen!" Praise the the LORD!
