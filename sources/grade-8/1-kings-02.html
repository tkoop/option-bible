David Gives Solomon His Final Instructions

1 As David’s time to die drew near, he gave his son Solomon this command:

2 “I am about to go the way of all the earth. Be strong and act like a man. 3 Keep the charge of the LORD, your God. Walk in His ways and keep His rules, commands, decisions, and warnings, as written in the Law of Moses. Then you will do well in everything you do, wherever you go. 4 Then the LORD will keep His promise to me: ‘If your sons live right and walk before Me in truth, with all their heart and all their soul, you will never fail to have a man on the throne of Israel.’ 5 And you know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me. He killed the two commanders of Israel’s armies, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them in peacetime as if in war. He got their blood on the belt around his waist and the sandals on his feet. 6 So deal with him wisely. Do not let his gray head go down to the grave in peace. 7 But show kindness to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite. Let them eat at your table, because they stood by me when I ran from your brother Absalom. 8 And watch Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim, who is with you. He cursed me badly the day I went to Mahanaim. But when he came down to meet me at the Jordan, I swore to him by the LORD, ‘I will not kill you with the sword.’ 9 Now do not let him go unpunished. You are a wise man, and you know what to do to him. Bring his gray head down to the grave in blood.”

10 Then David died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. 11 David had ruled Israel for forty years: seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. 12 So Solomon sat on his father David’s throne, and his kingdom was firmly set.

Adonijah Asks for Abishag

13 Adonijah son of Haggith went to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother.

She asked, “Do you come in peace?”

“Yes, in peace,” he said.

14 Then he said, “I have something to say to you.”

“Speak,” she said.

15 “You know,” he said, “that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel expected me to be king. But the kingdom has turned to my brother, because the LORD gave it to him.”

16 “Now I have just one request of you. Do not turn me down.”

“Speak,” she replied.

17 “Please ask King Solomon—for he will not turn you away—to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife.”

18 “Very well,” Bathsheba replied. “I will speak to the king for you.”

19 So Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him about Adonijah. The king rose to greet her, bowed to her, and sat on his throne. Then he had a throne brought for the king’s mother, and she sat at his right hand.

20 “I have just one small request to make of you,” she said. “Do not turn me down.”

“Ask, my mother,” the king replied, “for I will not turn you down.”

21 So she said, “Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah, your brother, as his wife.”

22 King Solomon answered his mother, “Why do you ask for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Since he is my older brother, you might as well ask for the kingdom for him—and for Abiathar the priest and Joab son of Zeruiah!” 23 Then King Solomon swore by the LORD: “May God punish me, and even more, if Adonijah does not pay for this request with his life! 24 And now, as surely as the LORD lives—the One who set me on my father David’s throne and made a house for me as He promised—Adonijah will be put to death today!”

25 So King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he struck Adonijah down, and Adonijah died.

Solomon Deals with His Enemies

26 Then the king said to Abiathar the priest, “Go to your fields in Anathoth. You deserve to die, but I will not kill you now, because you carried the ark of the Lord GOD before my father David, and you suffered everything my father suffered.”

27 So Solomon removed Abiathar from being priest of the LORD, to fulfill the LORD’s word spoken at Shiloh about the house of Eli.

28 When Joab heard this—he had sided with Adonijah but not with Absalom—he ran to the tent of the LORD and grabbed the horns of the altar.

29 King Solomon was told, “Joab has run to the tent of the LORD and is by the altar.”

So Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, “Go strike him down!”

30 Benaiah went into the tent of the LORD and said to Joab, “The king says, ‘Come out!’”

But Joab replied, “No, I will die here.”

So Benaiah reported to the king, “This is what Joab said, and this is how he answered me.”

31 And the king replied, “Do just as he says. Strike him down and bury him. Remove from me and from my father’s house the innocent blood Joab shed. 32 The LORD will bring his blood back on his own head, because without my father David knowing it, he killed two men more righteous and better than he: Abner son of Ner, commander of Israel’s army, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of Judah’s army. 33 Their blood will come back on Joab’s head and on his family forever. But the LORD will give peace to David, his family, his house, and his throne forever.”

34 So Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up, struck Joab down, and killed him. Joab was buried at his own home in the wilderness.

35 The king put Benaiah son of Jehoiada in charge of the army in Joab’s place, and he put Zadok the priest in Abiathar’s place.

Shimei Breaks His Oath

36 Then the king sent for Shimei and said to him, “Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem and live there. Do not go anywhere else.”

37 “On the day you leave and cross the Kidron Valley, know for sure that you will die. Your blood will be on your own head.”

38 “That is fine,” Shimei replied. “Your servant will do as my lord the king says.” So Shimei lived in Jerusalem for many days.

39 After three years, two of Shimei’s slaves ran away to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, “Look, your slaves are in Gath.”

40 So Shimei saddled his donkey and went to Gath to look for his slaves. He found them and brought them back from Gath.

41 Solomon was told that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and come back.

42 The king summoned Shimei and said to him, “Did I not make you swear by the LORD and warn you, ‘On the day you leave and go anywhere, know for sure that you will die’? Yet you have not kept the LORD’s oath or the command I gave you.”

43 “Why have you not kept the LORD’s oath and the command I gave you?”

44 The king also said to Shimei, “You know in your heart all the harm you did to my father David. Now the LORD will bring your evil back on your own head.”

45 “But King Solomon will be blessed, and David’s throne will stand before the LORD forever.”

46 Then the king ordered Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out, struck Shimei down, and Shimei died. So the kingdom was firmly established in Solomon’s hands.
