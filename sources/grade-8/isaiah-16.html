Moab Asked to Send Tribute

1 Send lambs to the ruler of the land, from Sela in the desert to Mount Zion. 2 Like birds pushed from their nests, the women of Moab will gather at the crossing places of the Arnon River.

Shelter for Refugees

3 Give wise advice and make a fair decision. Hide the refugees so well that even at noon it is like night. Do not give them up. 4 Let my people who were driven out live among you, Moab. Be a safe place for them from the destroyer. The attacker is finished. Destruction has ended. Those who crush the land are gone. 5 Then a throne will be set up in love, and a faithful ruler from the house of David will sit on it. He will judge, seek justice, and be quick to do what is right.

Moab's Pride and Ruin

6 We have heard of Moab's great pride. He is very proud. His pride, his anger, and his boasting are not right. 7 So Moab will wail, and everyone will wail for Moab. You will mourn for the raisin cakes of Kir Hareseth, deeply wounded. 8 The fields of Heshbon are dying. The vines of Sibmah are failing. Foreign rulers have cut down her finest vines. They reached as far as Jazer and wandered into the desert. Her branches spread out and crossed the sea. 9 So I will weep for Jazer and for the vines of Sibmah. I will water you with my tears, Heshbon and Elealeh. The joyful shouts over your summer fruit and harvest have stopped. 10 Joy and gladness are gone from the orchards. No one sings or shouts in the vineyards. The grape treaders do not press wine. I have stopped the harvest shout. 11 So my heart aches for Moab like a harp. My inner being aches for Kir Hareseth. 12 When Moab goes up to his high place, worn out, he will go to his temple to pray, but he will not succeed.

The LORD's Judgment

13 This is the word the LORD spoke long ago about Moab. 14 But now the LORD says, "Within three years—like the years of a hired worker—Moab's glory will be brought low, along with all his great crowd. Only a small remnant will be left, and it will not be strong."
