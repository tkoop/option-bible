Jeremiah Tests the Rechabites

1 This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the the LORD during the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:

2 "Go to the Rechabite family and speak to them. Bring them into one of the rooms in the the LORD's temple, and give them wine to drink."

3 So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, along with his brothers, all his sons, and the whole Rechabite family. 4 I brought them to the the LORD's temple, into the room of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God. That room was next to the room of the officials, above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, who guarded the doorway. 5 Then I set pitchers full of wine and cups before the Rechabites, and I said to them, "Drink wine."

6 They said, "We will not drink wine. Our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab told us, 'You and your children must never drink wine. 7 You must not build houses, plant fields, or plant vineyards for yourselves. None of it will be yours. Instead, you must live in tents all your lives, so you may live a long time on the land where you are foreigners.' 8 We have obeyed our ancestor Jonadab in everything he commanded. We, our wives, our sons, and our daughters have not drunk wine all our lives. 9 We have not built houses to live in. We have no vineyards, fields, or crops. 10 We have lived in tents and done everything our ancestor Jonadab commanded us.

11 "When King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came up against this land, we said, 'Let's go to Jerusalem to escape the Babylonian and Aramean armies.' So we have been living in Jerusalem."

The LORD Warns Judah

12 Then the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah:

13 "This is what the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Go and tell the people of Judah and those living in Jerusalem, 'Will you not learn a lesson and listen to My words?' declares the the LORD."

14 "The words of Jonadab son of Rechab have been carried out. He ordered his sons not to drink wine, and they have not drunk wine to this day, because they obeyed their father's command. But I have spoken to you again and again, and you have not listened to Me. 15 I sent all My servants the prophets to you again and again. I said, 'Turn now, each of you, from your evil ways. Do what is right. Do not follow other gods to serve them. Then you will live in the land I gave to you and your ancestors.' But you did not listen to Me or pay attention. 16 The sons of Jonadab son of Rechab have kept their father's command, but you have not listened to Me."

17 "Therefore this is what the the LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am bringing on Judah and everyone living in Jerusalem all the disaster I have spoken against them, because I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer."

A Promise for the Rechabites

18 Then Jeremiah said to the Rechabite family, "This is what the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Because you obeyed the command of your father Jonadab and kept all his commands, doing everything he told you, 19 therefore this is what the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will never fail to have a descendant to serve Me."
