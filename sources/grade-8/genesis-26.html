God's Promise to Isaac

1 There was a famine in the land, besides the first famine that happened in Abraham's days. Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines, in Gerar. 2 The LORD appeared to him and said, "Do not go down to Egypt. Live in the land I will show you. 3 Stay in this land, and I will be with you and bless you. I will give all these lands to you and your descendants, and I will keep the promise I made to your father Abraham.

"4 I will make your descendants as many as the stars in the sky. I will give your descendants all these lands, and all nations on earth will be blessed through your descendants. 5 I will do this because Abraham obeyed Me and kept My rules, My commands, My laws, and My teachings."

Isaac and Rebekah in Gerar

6 So Isaac stayed in Gerar.

7 The men of that place asked about his wife. Isaac said, "She is my sister," because he was afraid to say, "She is my wife." He thought, "The men of this place might kill me because of Rebekah, because she is beautiful."

8 After Isaac had been there a long time, Abimelech, king of the Philistines, looked out through a window. He saw Isaac caressing his wife Rebekah.

9 Abimelech called Isaac and said, "Look, she is your wife! Why did you say, 'She is my sister'?"

Isaac said to him, "I said it because I thought I might die because of her."

10 Abimelech said, "What have you done to us? One of the people could have slept with your wife, and you would have made us guilty."

11 Abimelech ordered all the people, "Anyone who harms this man or his wife will be put to death."

Isaac Prospers and Digs Wells

12 Isaac planted crops in that land. That same year he gathered a hundred times as much, because the LORD blessed him. 13 Isaac became very rich and kept growing richer. 14 He had large flocks and herds and many servants, so the Philistines became jealous of him. 15 They filled with dirt all the wells his father's servants had dug in Abraham's time.

16 Abimelech said to Isaac, "Leave us. You are much stronger than we are."

17 Isaac left and camped in the Gerar Valley, where he stayed. 18 Isaac dug again the wells that had been dug in Abraham's time. The Philistines had filled them in after Abraham died. Isaac gave them the same names his father had given them. 19 Isaac's servants dug in the valley and found a well of fresh water.

20 The herdsmen of Gerar argued with Isaac's herdsmen. They said, "This water is ours." So Isaac named the well Esek, because they quarreled with him. 21 They dug another well, and they argued over that one too. Isaac named it Sitnah.

22 He moved on and dug another well. No one argued over it. He named it Rehoboth and said, "Now the LORD has made room for us, and we will grow in this land."

23 From there Isaac went up to Beersheba.

24 The LORD appeared to him that night and said, "I am the God of your father Abraham. Do not be afraid, because I am with you. I will bless you and give you many descendants because of My servant Abraham."

25 Isaac built an altar there and worshiped the LORD. He set up his tent there, and his servants dug a well.

Isaac and Abimelech Make Peace

26 Abimelech came from Gerar with Ahuzzath, his friend, and Phicol, the commander of his army.

27 Isaac asked them, "Why have you come to me? You hate me and sent me away."

28 They said, "We see clearly that the LORD has been with you. So we said, 'Let there be a sworn promise between us and you. Let us make an agreement with you, that you will not harm us. 29 Just as we did not hurt you and did only good to you, and sent you away in peace, you are now blessed by the LORD.'"

30 Isaac made a feast for them, and they ate and drank.

31 They got up early the next morning and swore an oath to each other. Isaac sent them away, and they left in peace.

32 That same day, Isaac's servants came and told him about the well they had dug. They said, "We found water."

33 Isaac named the well Shibah. That is why the city is called Beersheba to this day.

34 When Esau was forty years old, he married Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite. 35 These wives made life bitter for Isaac and Rebekah.
