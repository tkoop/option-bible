The Lord Takes Away Jerusalem's Support

1 For look, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, is taking away from Jerusalem and Judah all support and all help—every bit of bread and every drop of water. 2 He is taking away the hero and the soldier, the judge and the prophet, the fortune-teller and the elder, 3 the captain of fifty and the honored man, the counselor, the skilled craftsman, and the expert in secret arts. 4 I will make young people their leaders, and children will rule over them. 5 People will oppress each other—man against man and neighbor against neighbor. The young will be rude to the old, and the lowly will insult the respected.

6 For a man will grab his brother in his father's house and say, "You have a coat. Be our leader, and take charge of this ruin."

7 At that time he will speak up and say, "I can't fix this mess. I have no food and no coat at home, so don't make me leader of the people."

Sin Brings Judgment

8 For Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen, because their words and their actions are against the LORD. They rebel against His glorious presence. 9 The look on their faces testifies against them. They show their sin as openly as Sodom did, and they do not hide it. Woe to them! They have brought disaster on themselves. 10 Tell the righteous that it will go well with them, for they will enjoy the results of their good deeds. 11 Woe to the wicked! It will go badly for him, for he will be paid back for what his hands have done. 12 My people are ruled by children, and women are in charge. O My people, your leaders lead you the wrong way and destroy the path you should follow.

The LORD Judges the Leaders

13 The LORD stands up to accuse; He stands to judge the peoples. 14 The LORD will enter into judgment with the elders and princes of His people: "You have ruined the vineyard; the stolen goods of the poor are in your houses.

15 "What do you mean by crushing My people and oppressing the poor?" declares the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

Judgment on the Women of Zion

16 The LORD also says, "Because the daughters of Zion are proud and walk with stretched-out necks and flirty looks, mincing along and jingling their ankle bracelets,

17 "the Lord will put sores on the heads of the daughters of Zion, and the LORD will make their foreheads bare."

18 In that day the Lord will take away their beautiful ankle chains, headbands, and crescent ornaments. 19 He will take away their pendants, bracelets, and veils. 20 He will take away their headdresses, armlets, sashes, scent bottles, and amulets. 21 He will take away their signet rings and nose rings.

22 He will take away their fine robes, shawls, wraps, and purses. 23 He will take away their mirrors, fine linen, turbans, and veils. 24 Instead of sweet perfume, there will be a stench; instead of a belt, a rope; instead of styled hair, baldness; instead of a fine robe, sackcloth; and a brand instead of beauty.

25 Your men will fall by the sword, and your strong warriors in battle. 26 Her gates will mourn and grieve; she will be left empty and sit on the ground.
