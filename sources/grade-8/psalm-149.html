Praise the LORD with a New Song

1 Hallelujah! Sing to the LORD a new song. Sing His praise among the faithful people gathered together. 2 Let Israel be glad in their Maker. Let the children of Zion rejoice in their King. 3 Let them praise His name with dancing. Let them sing to Him with tambourines and harps.

4 For the LORD is pleased with His people. He gives honor to the humble by saving them. 5 Let those who are faithful to Him shout for joy because of His glory. Let them sing for joy even on their beds. 6 May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a double-edged sword be in their hands.

7 They will carry out God’s judgment on the nations and punishment on the peoples. 8 They will bind their kings with chains and their nobles with iron shackles. 9 They will do the judgment written against them. This honor belongs to all who are faithful to Him. Hallelujah!
