God's Protection for Those Who Trust Him

1 Whoever stays in the secret place of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty. 2 I will say to the LORD, “You are my safe place and my strong tower. My God, I trust in You.” 3 For He will save you from the hunter’s trap and from the deadly disease. 4 He will cover you with His wings, and you will find safety under them. His truth will be your shield and protection.

5 You will not be afraid of the terror at night or of the arrow that flies by day. 6 You will not fear the disease that stalks in the dark or the ruin that strikes at noon. 7 A thousand may fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand, but it will not come near you. 8 You will only look with your eyes and see what happens to the wicked.

9 Because you have made the LORD your home and the Most High your safe place, 10 no harm will come to you, and no sickness will come near your tent. 11 For He will tell His angels to watch over you in all your ways. 12 They will lift you up in their hands so that you do not strike your foot against a stone. 13 You will step on the lion and the snake; you will trample the young lion and the serpent.

14 “Because he loves Me, I will save him. Because he knows My name, I will protect him. 15 When he calls to Me, I will answer him. I will be with him in trouble. I will rescue him and honor him. 16 I will satisfy him with a long life and show him My saving help.”
