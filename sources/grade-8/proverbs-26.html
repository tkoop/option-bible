Honor Doesn't Fit a Fool

1 Honor doesn't fit a fool, just like snow in summer or rain during the harvest.

2 A curse with no reason won't come true, like a bird that never lands. 3 A horse needs a whip, a donkey needs a bridle, and a fool needs a rod on the back.

How to Deal with Fools

4 Don't answer a fool in his own foolish way, or you will become like him. 5 Answer a fool in his foolish way, so he won't think he's wise.

6 Sending a message with a fool is like cutting off your own feet and drinking trouble. 7 A wise saying in a fool's mouth is as useless as lame legs. 8 Giving honor to a fool is like tying a stone in a sling—it doesn't help and can hurt. 9 A wise saying in a fool's mouth is like a thorn in a drunk person's hand.

10 Hiring a fool—or just anyone passing by—is like an archer shooting wildly. 11 A fool who keeps repeating his mistakes is like a dog going back to its vomit. 12 Do you see someone who thinks he's wise? There's more hope for a fool than for him.

Lazy People and Meddling

13 The lazy person says, "There's a lion in the road! A fierce lion is in the streets!"

14 A lazy person turns in bed like a door swings on its hinges. 15 He buries his hand in the dish but is too tired to bring it back to his mouth. 16 A lazy person thinks he's wiser than seven people who give good answers.

17 Grabbing a dog by the ears is like butting into someone else's fight. 18 19 A person who tricks his neighbor and says, "I was only joking!" is like a crazy person throwing burning sticks, arrows, and death.

Gossip and Trouble

20 A fire goes out when there's no wood, and a fight dies down when there's no gossip. 21 Charcoal keeps hot coals glowing, and wood keeps a fire burning; a troublemaker keeps fights going.

22 Gossip is like tasty food—it goes deep inside a person.

23 Burning lips and an evil heart are like a shiny coating over a clay pot—they look good but hide something cheap. 24 A hateful person hides his true feelings behind his words, but he stores up deceit inside.

25 Don't trust him when he sounds kind, because his heart is full of evil. 26 His hatred may be hidden for now, but his evil will be shown in public.

27 Whoever digs a pit for someone else falls into it, and whoever rolls a stone has it roll back on him. 28 A lying tongue hates the people it hurts, and smooth talk brings ruin.
