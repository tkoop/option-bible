How Believers Should Live in Public

1 Remind them to obey rulers and authorities and to be ready for every good work. 2 Tell them not to insult anyone, to be peaceable and gentle, and to show true humility to everyone.

God Saved Us by His Mercy

3 At one time we too were foolish and disobedient. We were led astray and served all kinds of desires and pleasures. We lived in evil and envy, and people hated us while we hated each other. 4 But when the kindness of God our Savior and His love for people appeared, 5 He saved us. It was not because of good things we had done, but because of His mercy. He washed us clean through the new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit. 6 God poured out the Spirit on us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior. 7 Because we were made right by His grace, we can now hope to receive eternal life.

Avoid Foolish Arguments

8 This is a trustworthy saying, and I want you to stress these things. Then believers will be careful to do good works. These things are good and helpful for everyone. 9 But stay away from foolish arguments, family histories, fights, and quarrels about the law. They are useless and empty. 10 Warn a person who causes division once, and then again. After that, have nothing to do with him. 11 Keep in mind that such a person is twisted and keeps on sinning. He brings judgment on himself.

Final Words and Greetings

12 When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, try to come to me at Nicopolis. I have decided to spend the winter there. 13 Help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way, so they will not need anything. 14 Our people must also learn to do good works to meet real needs, so they will not be unproductive. 15 Everyone with me sends you greetings. Greet those who love us in the faith. May grace be with all of you.
