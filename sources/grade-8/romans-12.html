Offering Ourselves to God

1 So I urge you, brothers and sisters, because of God's mercy, to offer your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God. This is your true worship. 2 Do not copy the ways of this world. Instead, let God change you by making your minds new, so you can know God's will—what is good, pleasing, and perfect.

3 Because of the grace God gave me, I tell everyone among you not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think. Instead, he should think clearly and wisely, because God has given each person a measure of faith.

One Body and Many Gifts

4 Just as one body has many parts, and not all parts do the same job, 5 so in Christ we who are many are one body, and each part belongs to the others.

6 We have different gifts, according to the grace God has given us. If a person has the gift of prophecy, let them use it in keeping with their faith. 7 If it is serving, serve; if teaching, teach. 8 If encouraging, encourage; if giving, give generously; if leading, lead with care; if showing mercy, do it cheerfully.

Love in Action

9 Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; hold on to what is good. 10 Love each other deeply as brothers and sisters. Honor one another more than yourselves. 11 Never be lazy in your zeal. Be full of spirit, and serve the Lord. 12 Be joyful in hope, patient in trouble, and faithful in prayer. 13 Share with God's people who are in need, and welcome strangers into your home.

14 Bless those who persecute you; bless them and do not curse them. 15 Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep. 16 Live in harmony with one another. Do not be proud, but be willing to associate with people who are humble. Do not think you are smarter than you really are.

Overcoming Evil with Good

17 Do not pay back anyone evil for evil. Try to do what is right in the eyes of everyone. 18 If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone. 19 Dear friends, do not take revenge. Leave room for God's anger, because it is written: "Vengeance belongs to Me; I will pay it back," says the Lord.

20 Instead, "If your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink. In doing this, you will heap burning coals on his head." 21 Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.
