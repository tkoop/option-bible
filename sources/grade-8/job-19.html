Job Describes His Suffering

1 Then Job answered:

2 "How long will you torment me and crush me with words? 3 You have insulted me over and over, and you are not ashamed to wrong me. 4 Even if I really did go wrong, my mistake is my own. 5 If you really put yourselves above me and use my shame against me, 6 then know that God has treated me unfairly and trapped me in His net. 7 Look, I cry out that I am being wronged, but no one answers; I shout for help, but there is no justice. 8 He has blocked my path so I cannot get through, and He has made my roads dark. 9 He has taken away my honor and removed the crown from my head. 10 He breaks me down on every side, and I am finished; He pulls up my hope like a tree. 11 He has become angry with me and treats me like an enemy. 12 His troops gather, advance against me, and camp around my tent.

Job Feels Abandoned

13 "He has driven my brothers away from me, and those who know me have turned away. 14 My relatives have abandoned me, and my close friends have forgotten me. 15 The people in my house and my servant girls treat me like a stranger; I am a foreigner to them. 16 I call my servant, but he does not answer; I have to beg him with my own words. 17 My breath smells bad to my wife, and even my own brothers find me disgusting. 18 Even little children look down on me; when I get up, they speak against me. 19 All my close friends hate me, and the ones I loved have turned against me. 20 My bones stick to my skin and flesh, and I have barely escaped with my life. 21 Pity me, pity me, my friends, because God's hand has struck me. 22 Why do you keep attacking me like God does, and why aren't you satisfied with my suffering?

Job's Hope in His Redeemer

23 "If only my words were written down! If only they were recorded in a book! 24 If only they were carved with an iron tool and lead into the rock forever! 25 But I know that my Redeemer lives, and in the end He will stand on the earth. 26 Even after my skin is destroyed, I will see God in my own flesh. 27 I will see Him for myself; my own eyes will look at Him, not someone else's. My heart aches within me!

Job Warns His Friends

28 "If you say, 'How can we hunt him down?' and 'The real problem is in him,' 29 then be afraid of the sword, because anger brings the punishment of the sword, so you will know that judgment is real."
