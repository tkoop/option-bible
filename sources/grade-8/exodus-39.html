The Holy Garments Are Made

1 From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, they made woven garments for serving in the holy place. They also made the holy garments for Aaron, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Ephod

2 They made the ephod from gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen. 3 They hammered the gold into thin sheets and cut them into threads. Then they worked the gold into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and into the fine linen. The work was done by a skilled worker. 4 They made shoulder pieces for the ephod, joined together at its two ends. 5 The waistband of the ephod was made from the same material and was attached to the ephod. It was made of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

6 They mounted the onyx stones in gold settings. They were engraved like a seal with the names of the sons of Israel. 7 They fastened them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Breastpiece

8 They made the breastpiece like the ephod: a skilled worker's work, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen. 9 It was square and folded double. It was a span long and a span wide.

10 They set four rows of stones in it. The first row had carnelian, chrysolite, and emerald. 11 The second row had turquoise, sapphire, and diamond. 12 The third row had jacinth, agate, and amethyst. 13 The fourth row had beryl, onyx, and jasper. They were set in gold settings.

14 The stones matched the names of the sons of Israel, twelve in all. Each stone was engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes.

15 They made braided chains of pure gold for the breastpiece. 16 They made two gold settings and two gold rings, and they fastened the two rings to the two upper corners of the breastpiece. 17 They attached the two gold chains to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece. 18 They fastened the other ends of the two chains to the two settings and attached them to the front shoulder pieces of the ephod. 19 They made two gold rings and fastened them to the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on the inner edge toward the ephod. 20 They made two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front, near the seam above the waistband. 21 They tied the rings of the breastpiece to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so the breastpiece would rest on the waistband of the ephod and not come loose. This was as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Robe and Other Garments

22 They made the robe of the ephod entirely of blue yarn, the work of a weaver. 23 It had an opening in the center for the head, with a woven collar around the opening so it would not tear. 24 They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen around the hem of the robe. 25 They made bells of pure gold and put them between the pomegranates around the hem of the robe. 26 There were bells and pomegranates all around the hem of the robe, for serving, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

27 They made tunics of fine linen for Aaron and his sons, woven work. 28 They made the turban of fine linen, the ornate headbands of fine linen, the linen undergarments of finely twisted linen, 29 and the sash of finely twisted linen with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, embroidered work, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

30 They made the holy plate, the pure gold crown. They engraved on it, in seal-like letters, "Holy to the LORD." 31 They fastened a blue cord to it to attach it to the turban above, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Tabernacle Is Finished

32 So all the work on the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, was finished. The Israelites did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

33 They brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings, its clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases; 34 the covering of ram skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather, the inner curtain; 35 the ark of the Testimony with its poles and the atonement cover; 36 the table with all its utensils and the bread of the Presence; 37 the pure gold lampstand with its lamps, the lamps in a row, and all its utensils, and the oil for the light; 38 the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the curtain for the entrance to the tent; 39 the bronze altar with its bronze grate, its poles, and all its utensils; the basin with its stand; 40 the curtains of the courtyard with its posts and bases; the curtain for the courtyard gate; its ropes and tent pegs; and all the utensils for the service of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting; 41 the woven garments for serving in the holy place; the holy garments for Aaron the priest; and the garments for his sons to serve as priests.

42 The Israelites had done all the work just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 43 Moses saw all the work, and indeed they had done it just as the LORD had commanded. So Moses blessed them.
