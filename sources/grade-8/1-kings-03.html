Solomon's Marriage and the High Places

1 Solomon made an alliance with Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He married Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to the City of David. He kept her there until he finished building his palace, the house of the the LORD, and the wall around Jerusalem.

2 The people were still sacrificing at the high places, because a house for the Name of the the LORD had not yet been built. 3 Solomon loved the the LORD and walked in the laws of his father David, but he also sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

Solomon Asks God for Wisdom

4 The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, because it was the most important high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on the altar there.

5 At Gibeon the the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream at night. God said, "Ask what I should give you."

6 Solomon said, "You have shown great and faithful love to Your servant David, my father. He walked before You in truth, in right living, and with a true heart toward You. You kept that great love for him and gave him a son to sit on his throne, just as it is today."

7 "And now, O the LORD my God, You have made Your servant king in place of David, my father. But I am only a young boy. I do not know how to lead Your people."

8 "Your servant is in the middle of Your people, whom You have chosen. They are so many that no one can count them."

9 "So give Your servant an understanding heart to judge Your people and to tell right from wrong. For who can govern this great people?"

10 The Lord was pleased that Solomon asked for this.

11 God said to him, "Because you asked for this and did not ask for a long life or riches or the death of your enemies, but asked for understanding to judge fairly, 12 I will do what you asked. I will give you a wise and understanding heart. No one before you has been like you, and no one after you will be like you."

13 "I will also give you what you did not ask for: riches and honor, so that no other king will be your equal during your whole life. 14 And if you walk in My ways and keep My laws and commands, as your father David did, then I will give you a long life."

15 Then Solomon woke up and realized it had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord. He offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and he held a feast for all his servants.

Two Women Bring a Dispute to the King

16 At that time two prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

17 One woman said, "Please, my lord, this woman and I live in the same house. I gave birth to a baby while she was there. 18 On the third day after I gave birth, she also gave birth. We were alone in the house; no one else was with us. Only the two of us were in the house. 19 During the night this woman's baby died, because she rolled over on him.

20 "She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while I, your servant, slept. She laid him at her breast and laid her dead son at my breast. 21 When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, he was dead. But when I looked at him closely, I saw he was not the son I had given birth to."

22 But the other woman said, "No, the living child is mine, and the dead child is yours."

But the first woman said, "No, the dead child is yours, and the living child is mine." So they argued before the king.

23 Then the king said, "One says, 'This is my son who lives, and your son is dead,' while the other says, 'No, your son is dead, and my son is the living one.'"

24 So the king said, "Bring me a sword." And they brought a sword before the king.

Solomon's Wise Judgment

25 The king said, "Cut the living child in two and give half to one woman and half to the other."

26 The woman whose son was alive spoke to the king, because she loved her son deeply. She said, "Please, my lord, give her the living child. Do not kill him!"

But the other woman said, "He will be neither mine nor yours. Cut him in two!"

27 Then the king said, "Give the living child to the first woman. Do not kill him; she is his mother."

28 All Israel heard about the judgment the king had made. They stood in awe of the king, because they saw that God's wisdom was in him to do what was right.
