Jesus, Our Superior High Priest

1 Now the main point of what we are saying is this: We have a high priest like that. He sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven. 2 He serves in the holy place and in the true tabernacle, which the Lord set up, not people.

3 Every high priest is chosen to offer gifts and sacrifices. So this One must have something to offer too. 4 If He were on earth, He would not be a priest, because there are already priests who offer gifts the way the law says.

5 They serve a copy and a shadow of the heavenly things. That is why God warned Moses when he was about to build the tabernacle: “See that you make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.”

A Better Ministry and Covenant

6 But now He has received a ministry that is much better. He is also the go-between for a better covenant, which is based on better promises. 7 If the first covenant had been perfect, there would have been no need for a second one.

The New Covenant Promised

8 Because God found fault with the people, He says, “The days are coming, says the the LORD, when I will make a new covenant with the people of Israel and with the people of Judah.

9 “It will not be like the covenant I made with their ancestors when I took them by the hand to lead them out of Egypt. They did not keep My covenant, so I turned away from them, says the the LORD.

10 “This is the covenant I will make with the people of Israel after those days, says the the LORD: I will put My laws in their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people.

11 “No one will need to teach his neighbor or his brother, saying, ‘Know the the LORD,’ because they will all know Me, from the least to the greatest.

12 “For I will forgive their wickedness and remember their sins no more.”

13 By calling this covenant “new,” God has made the first one old. What is old and aging is close to disappearing.
