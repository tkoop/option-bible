God Calls Out Jerusalem's Sins

1 The word of the the LORD came to me. He said, 2 "Son of man, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Will you judge it? Then tell her about all her terrible acts. Say this to her:"

3 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: You are a city that sheds blood inside yourself, so your time has come. You make idols that make you unclean. 4 You are guilty because of the blood you shed. You made yourself unclean with the idols you made. You brought your day of judgment near, and your time has run out. So I have made you a shame to the nations and a mockery to all the lands. 5 Those near and far from you will mock you. You are a city with a bad name, and you are full of trouble.

6 'Look, the leaders of Israel in you have used their power to shed blood. 7 In you they have treated father and mother lightly. In your midst they have cheated the stranger. They have hurt the fatherless and the widow. 8 You have shown disrespect for My holy things and treated My Sabbaths as unholy. 9 In you there are people who spread lies so that blood is shed. In you they eat at the mountain shrines. They commit sexual sins among you.

10 'In you men sleep with their father's wife. In you they force themselves on women during their monthly periods. 11 One man does something disgusting with his neighbor's wife. Another man makes his daughter-in-law unclean through sexual sin. Another man in you has sex with his sister, his father's daughter. 12 In you they take bribes to shed blood. You charge interest and take extra money. You get rich by cheating your neighbors. And you have forgotten Me, says the Lord the LORD.

13 'Look, I stand against the dishonest money you have made and the bloodshed among you. 14 Can your heart stay strong? Can your hands stay powerful in the days when I deal with you? I, the the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it. 15 I will scatter you among the nations and spread you through other countries. I will burn away your uncleanness from you. 16 You will be made unclean in front of the nations, and you will know that I am the the LORD.'

Israel Is Like Worthless Metal

17 The word of the the LORD came to me. He said, 18 "Son of man, the people of Israel have become like worthless metal to Me. All of them are like bronze, tin, iron, and lead in a furnace. They are the waste left from silver.

19 Therefore, this is what the Lord the LORD says: 'Because you have all become worthless metal, I will gather you into Jerusalem. 20 Just as people gather silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin into a furnace and blow fire on it to melt it, so I will gather you in My anger and fury. I will put you in and melt you. 21 I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of My anger, and you will be melted in it. 22 As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted in it. Then you will know that I, the the LORD, have poured out My anger on you.'

No One Stands in the Gap

23 The word of the the LORD came to me. He said, 24 "Son of man, say to the land:"

'You are a land that has not been cleansed. No rain has fallen on you in the day of My anger. 25 Her prophets have made a secret plan among themselves. They are like a roaring lion tearing its prey. They devour people. They take treasure and costly things. They make many widows in her midst. 26 Her priests have broken My law. They have treated My holy things as unclean. They have not shown the difference between what is holy and what is common. They have not taught the difference between the unclean and the clean. They have ignored My Sabbaths, and I am treated as unclean among them.

27 'Her leaders in her midst are like wolves tearing prey. They shed blood and destroy lives to make dishonest money. 28 Her prophets cover up their evil with false comfort. They see false visions and tell lies in My name. They say, "This is what the Lord the LORD says," when the the LORD has not spoken. 29 The people of the land have cheated and robbed. They have hurt the poor and needy. They have treated the stranger unfairly.

30 'I looked for someone among them who would rebuild the wall and stand between Me and the land to protect it from destruction. But I found no one. 31 So I poured out My anger on them. I destroyed them with the fire of My anger. I made them suffer for what they had done, says the Lord the LORD.'
