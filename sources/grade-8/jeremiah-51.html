The LORD's Judgment on Babylon

1 This is what the LORD says: "Look, I am going to stir up a destroying wind against Babylon and against the people of Leb Kamai. 2 I will send strangers to Babylon to scatter her and empty her land. They will come against her from every side on the day of trouble. 3 Do not let the archer bend his bow or put on his armor. Do not spare her young men. Destroy all her army. 4 They will fall dead in the land of the Chaldeans, wounded in her streets."

5 Israel and Judah have not been abandoned by their God, the LORD of Armies, even though their land was full of guilt against the Holy One of Israel.

A Call to Flee Babylon

6 Flee from Babylon! Run for your life! Do not be destroyed because of her sin. It is time for the LORD to punish her; He will pay her back.

7 Babylon was a gold cup in the LORD's hand. It made all the earth drunk. The nations drank her wine, so they went mad. 8 Suddenly Babylon has fallen and been broken. Wail for her! Get balm for her pain; maybe she can be healed.

9 We tried to heal Babylon, but she could not be healed. Leave her and let each of us go home, because her judgment reaches up to the heavens and rises to the skies. 10 The LORD has shown that we are right. Come, let us tell in Zion what the LORD our God has done.

11 Sharpen the arrows! Fill the quivers! The LORD has stirred up the spirit of the kings of Media, because His plan is to destroy Babylon. This is the LORD's revenge, revenge for His temple. 12 Raise a banner against the walls of Babylon! Strengthen the guard! Post watchmen! Set up ambushes! For the LORD has planned it and will do what He said against the people of Babylon.

13 You who live by many waters and have great treasure, your end has come. The thread of your life is cut. 14 The LORD of Armies has sworn by Himself: "Surely I will fill you with men, as many as locusts, and they will shout a battle cry against you."

The LORD's Power and Babylon's Fall

15 He made the earth by His power. He set the world in place by His wisdom and stretched out the heavens by His understanding. 16 When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar. He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning with the rain and brings wind out of His storehouses. 17 Every man is foolish and without knowledge. Every metalworker is put to shame by his idol, because his cast image is a lie and has no breath in it. 18 They are worthless, a work of deception. When the time comes to punish them, they will be destroyed. 19 The Portion of Jacob is not like them, for He is the Maker of all things. Israel is the tribe of His inheritance. The LORD of Armies is His name.

20 You are My hammer and My weapon of war. With you I shatter nations and destroy kingdoms. 21 With you I shatter horse and rider. With you I shatter chariot and driver. 22 With you I shatter man and woman, old and young, young man and young woman. 23 With you I shatter shepherd and flock, farmer and team, rulers and officials.

24 But I will repay Babylon and all the people of Chaldea for all the evil they did in Zion, right before your eyes, declares the LORD.

25 I am against you, O destroying mountain, declares the LORD. You destroy the whole earth. I will stretch out My hand against you, roll you down from the rocks, and make you a burned-out mountain. 26 No one will take a cornerstone from you or a stone for a foundation. You will be empty forever, declares the LORD.

27 Raise a banner in the land! Blow the trumpet among the nations! Prepare the nations against her! Call the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz against her! Appoint a commander against her! Bring up horses like a swarm of locusts! 28 Prepare the nations against her—the kings of Media, their governors and officials, and all the land they rule. 29 The land shakes and writhes, because the LORD's plans against Babylon stand. He will make Babylon a wasteland where no one lives.

30 Babylon's warriors have stopped fighting. They stay in their forts. Their strength is gone. They have become like women. Her houses are set on fire, and her bars are broken. 31 One runner tells another runner, and one messenger tells another messenger, to report to the king of Babylon that his city has been taken from end to end. 32 The river crossings are seized, the marshes are burned, and the soldiers are terrified.

33 This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "Daughter Babylon is like a threshing floor at the time it is trampled. In a little while, her harvest time will come."

34 "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devoured us and crushed us. He has made us an empty jar. He swallowed us like a sea monster and filled his belly with our delicacies. He has driven us away."

35 "Let the violence done to me and my family be blamed on Babylon," says the one who lives in Zion.

"Let my blood be on the people of Chaldea," says Jerusalem.

36 So this is what the LORD says: "I will take up your case and avenge you. I will dry up her sea and make her spring run dry."

37 Babylon will become a heap of ruins, a home for jackals, a horror and a hissing, with no one living there. 38 They will roar together like young lions and growl like lion cubs.

39 While they are heated, I will prepare their drink and make them drunk, so they may celebrate and then sleep forever and never wake up, declares the LORD. 40 I will bring them down like lambs to the slaughter, like rams and goats.

41 How Sheshach has been captured! The praise of the whole earth has been seized! How Babylon has become a horror among the nations! 42 The sea has come up over Babylon. She is covered with its roaring waves. 43 Her cities have become a wasteland, a dry and empty land. No one travels through them; no person lives there.

44 I will punish Bel in Babylon and make him spit out what he swallowed. The nations will no longer stream to him. Even Babylon's wall has fallen.

45 Come out of her, My people! Run for your life from the LORD's fierce anger!

46 Do not let your hearts grow weak or be afraid of the rumors you hear in the land. One rumor comes this year, and another comes the next. There is violence in the land, and rulers fight against rulers.

47 So the time is coming when I will punish Babylon's idols. Her whole land will be put to shame, and all her wounded will fall inside her. 48 Then heaven and earth and everything in them will sing for joy over Babylon, because destroyers from the north will come against her, declares the LORD.

49 Babylon must fall because of Israel's dead, just as the dead of all the earth have fallen because of Babylon.

50 You who have escaped the sword, go away! Do not stand still! Remember the LORD from far away, and let Jerusalem come into your mind.

51 We are ashamed because we have heard insults. Shame covers our faces, because foreigners have entered the holy places of the LORD's house.

52 So the time is coming, declares the LORD, when I will punish her idols, and the wounded will groan throughout her land. 53 Even if Babylon climbs to the sky and fortifies her highest stronghold, destroyers from Me will come against her, declares the LORD.

54 A cry of distress comes from Babylon, and great destruction from the land of the Chaldeans! 55 For the LORD is destroying Babylon and silencing her loud noise. When her waves roar like great waters, their voice thunders. 56 For the destroyer has come against her, against Babylon. Her mighty men are captured, and their bows are broken, because the LORD is a God who repays; He will surely repay.

57 I will make her leaders and wise men drunk, along with her governors, officials, and warriors. They will sleep forever and never wake up, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Armies.

58 This is what the LORD of Armies says: "Babylon's thick walls will be completely torn down, and her high gates will be burned. The peoples will work for nothing, and the nations will grow tired in the flames."

Jeremiah's Scroll Against Babylon

59 This is the message Jeremiah the prophet gave to Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when Seraiah went to Babylon with King Zedekiah of Judah in the fourth year of Zedekiah's reign. Seraiah was the officer in charge of supplies. 60 Jeremiah wrote on a scroll all the disaster that would come on Babylon—all these words written against Babylon.

61 Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you get to Babylon, read all these words aloud, and say, 'the LORD, You have said You will destroy this place so that no one will live in it—not people or animals. It will be empty forever.'

"When you finish reading this scroll, tie a stone to it and throw it into the Euphrates River.

"Then say, 'In the same way Babylon will sink and never rise again, because of the disaster I am bringing on her. Her people will grow tired.' The words of Jeremiah end here."
