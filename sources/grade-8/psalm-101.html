David's Promise to Live with Integrity

1 I will sing about Your faithful love and justice. To You, the LORD, I will sing praise. 2 I will be careful to live a blameless life. When will You come to me? I will walk inside my home with a pure heart.

3 I won't put anything worthless in front of my eyes. I hate what people do when they turn away from what's right, and I won't let it stick to me. 4 A twisted heart will leave me, and I won't have anything to do with evil.

5 I will destroy anyone who secretly talks badly about his neighbor. I can't stand a person with proud eyes and a proud heart. 6 I keep my eyes on the faithful people of the land so they can live with me. The person who walks in a blameless way will serve me.

7 No one who practices deceit will live in my house. No one who tells lies will stand in front of me. 8 Every morning I will destroy all the wicked people in the land. I will cut off all evildoers from the city of the LORD.
