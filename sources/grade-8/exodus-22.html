Laws About Theft and Property

1 If a man steals an ox or a sheep and kills it or sells it, he must pay five oxen for an ox and four sheep for a sheep. 2 If a thief is caught breaking in and is struck and killed, no one is guilty of his death. 3 But if the sun has risen, killing him is a crime. The thief must pay in full; if he has nothing, he must be sold to pay for his theft. 4 If the stolen animal is found alive in his possession, whether it is an ox, a donkey, or a sheep, he must pay double.

Laws About Fields, Fire, and Guarded Property

5 If a man lets his animal loose so it grazes in someone else's field or vineyard, he must pay with the best of his own field and vineyard. 6 If a fire breaks out and spreads to thorn bushes, burning up stacked grain, standing grain, or a field, the one who started the fire must make full restitution. 7 If someone gives a neighbor money or goods to keep, and they are stolen from the neighbor's house, the thief, if caught, must pay double. 8 If the thief is not found, the homeowner must go before God to show whether he took his neighbor's property. 9 In any dispute about an ox, donkey, sheep, clothing, or lost item, if one person says, "This is mine," both must go before God. The one God finds guilty must pay double to his neighbor. 10 If a man gives a neighbor a donkey, ox, sheep, or any animal to watch, and it dies, is injured, or is stolen with no one watching, 11 the two men must swear an oath before the LORD that the watcher did not take the animal. The owner must accept it, and the watcher will not have to pay. 12 But if the animal was stolen from him, he must pay the owner. 13 If it was torn by a wild animal, he must bring the remains as proof; he will not have to pay for the torn animal.

Laws About Borrowing, Marriage, and Social Justice

14 If a man borrows an animal from a neighbor and it is injured or dies while the owner is not present, he must pay full compensation. 15 If the owner was present, no payment is required. If the animal was hired, its loss is covered by the rental price. 16 If a man seduces a young woman who is not engaged and sleeps with her, he must pay the bride-price and marry her. 17 If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, the man must still pay the same amount as the bride-price for a virgin. 18 You must not allow a sorceress to live. 19 Anyone who has sexual relations with an animal must be put to death. 20 Anyone who sacrifices to any god except the LORD alone must be destroyed. 21 Do not mistreat or oppress a foreigner, for you were foreigners in Egypt. 22 Do not mistreat any widow or orphan. 23 If you do mistreat them, and they cry out to Me, I will surely hear their cry. 24 My anger will burn, and I will kill you with the sword; your wives will become widows, and your children will become orphans. 25 If you lend money to My people, to the poor among you, do not treat him like a moneylender; do not charge him interest. 26 If you take your neighbor's cloak as a pledge, return it to him by sunset. 27 For that cloak is his only covering; it is his garment for his body. What else will he sleep in? If he cries out to Me, I will hear, for I am gracious.

Laws About Worship and Holy Living

28 You must not blaspheme God or curse a ruler of your people. 29 Do not delay your offerings or your firstfruits. Give Me the firstborn of your sons. 30 Do the same with your oxen and your sheep. Let them stay with their mother for seven days, but give them to Me on the eighth day. 31 You are to be My holy people. Do not eat meat torn by wild animals in the field; throw it to the dogs.
