A Sign Child Is Born

1 Then the LORD said to me, “Take a large scroll and write on it with an ordinary pen, ‘Quick to the Plunder, Swift to the Spoil.’ 2 I will take reliable witnesses for Myself—Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah.”

3 I went to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. Then the LORD said to me, “Call his name ‘Quick to the Plunder, Swift to the Spoil.’ 4 For before the boy knows how to say, ‘my father’ or ‘my mother,’ the wealth of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria will be carried away before the king of Assyria.”

The LORD Warns of Assyria's Flood

5 The LORD spoke to me again: 6 “Because these people have rejected the calm waters of Shiloah and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah’s son, 7 the Lord is bringing against them the strong and mighty waters of the River—the king of Assyria and all his glory. It will rise over all its channels and overflow all its banks. 8 It will sweep through Judah, flooding and passing through, reaching to the neck. Its outstretched wings will fill the breadth of Your land, O Immanuel.”

9 Be shattered, O peoples, and be dismayed; listen, all distant lands. Prepare for battle, and be dismayed; prepare for battle, and be dismayed. 10 Devise a plan, but it will be thwarted; speak a word, but it will not stand, for God is with us.

Do Not Fear Conspiracy

11 For this is what the LORD said to me with a strong hand, instructing me not to walk in the way of this people: 12 “Do not call conspiracy everything these people call conspiracy. Do not fear what they fear, and do not dread it.

13 “The LORD of Hosts is the One you are to regard as holy. Let Him be your fear, and let Him be your dread. 14 He will be a sanctuary, but to both houses of Israel He will be a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense, a trap and a snare to the people of Jerusalem. 15 Many will stumble over them; they will fall and be broken; they will be snared and captured.”

Hope in the LORD Alone

16 Bind up the testimony; seal the law among my disciples. 17 I will wait for the LORD, who is hiding His face from the house of Jacob. I will put my hope in Him. 18 Here I am, with the children the LORD has given me. We are signs and wonders in Israel from the LORD of Hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

19 When people tell you to consult the spirits of the dead and the spiritists who whisper and mutter, should not a people consult their God? Why consult the dead on behalf of the living? 20 To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no hope of a new day. 21 They will roam the land, distressed and hungry. When they are hungry, they will become enraged; and looking upward, they will curse their king and their God. 22 Then they will look to the earth and see only distress, darkness, and the gloom of anguish. And they will be driven into darkness.
