David Praises the Lord as His Rock

1 Blessed be the LORD, my Rock, who trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle. 2 He is my loving devotion and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer. He is my shield, and in Him I take refuge. He subdues my people under me.

David Marvels at God's Care

3 The LORD, what is man, that You regard him? What is the son of man, that You think of him? 4 Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow.

David Asks for Rescue

5 Part Your heavens, the LORD, and come down; touch the mountains so that they smoke. 6 Flash forth lightning and scatter them; shoot Your arrows and rout them. 7 Reach down from on high; rescue me and deliver me from the many waters, from the hand of foreigners, 8 whose mouths speak deceit and whose right hands are full of lies.

9 I will sing a new song to You, O God; on a ten-stringed harp I will make music to You— 10 to the One who gives victory to kings, who delivers His servant David from the deadly sword. 11 Rescue me and deliver me from the hand of foreigners, whose mouths speak deceit and whose right hands are full of lies.

Blessings for God's People

12 Then our sons will be like well-grown plants, and our daughters like pillars carved to adorn a palace. 13 Our barns will be filled with every kind of produce. Our sheep will bring forth thousands and tens of thousands in our fields. 14 Our oxen will bear heavy loads. There will be no broken wall, no one carried away, and no cry of distress in our streets. 15 Blessed are the people who are in such a state; blessed are the people whose God is the LORD.
