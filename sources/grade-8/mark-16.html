The Women Visit the Empty Tomb

1 After the Sabbath, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices. They wanted to go and anoint Jesus' body. 2 Very early on the first day of the week, just after sunrise, they went to the tomb. 3 They asked each other, "Who will roll the stone away from the tomb entrance for us?" 4 When they looked up, they saw that the stone had already been rolled away. It was very large. 5 They went into the tomb and saw a young man sitting on the right side. He was wearing a white robe. They were amazed. 6 He said to them, "Don't be amazed. You are looking for Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has been raised! He is not here. Look at the place where they laid Him. 7 Go and tell His disciples and Peter, 'He is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see Him, just as He told you.'" 8 The women left the tomb and ran away. They were shaking and amazed, and they were afraid. So they said nothing to anyone.

Jesus Appears to His Followers

9 Early on the first day of the week, after He had risen, Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene. He had driven seven demons out of her. 10 She went and told the people who had been with Him. They were mourning and crying. 11 When they heard that He was alive and that she had seen Him, they did not believe her. 12 After that, He appeared in a different form to two of them while they were walking to the country. 13 They went and told the rest, but they did not believe them either. 14 Later He appeared to the eleven while they were eating. He scolded them for not believing and for their stubborn hearts, because they had not believed the people who saw Him after He had been raised.

Jesus Gives His Final Instructions

15 He said to them, "Go into all the world and preach the good news to all creation. 16 Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. 17 These signs will follow those who believe: In My name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new languages; 18 they will pick up snakes with their hands; and if they drink anything deadly, it will not hurt them. They will place their hands on sick people, and those people will get well." 19 After the Lord Jesus had spoken to them, He was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God. 20 The disciples went out and preached everywhere. The Lord worked with them and proved His word true through the miracles that followed.
