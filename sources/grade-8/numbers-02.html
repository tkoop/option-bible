God Commands the Camp Order

1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2 "The Israelites are to camp around the Tent of Meeting, but not right beside it. Each man is to camp under his own banner, with the banners of his family group. They are to camp all around it.

Camps on the East and South Sides

3 "On the east side, toward the sunrise, the divisions of Judah are to camp under their banner. Nahshon son of Amminadab is the leader of the people of Judah. 4 His division has 74,600 men. 5 Next to him, the tribe of Issachar is to camp. Nethanel son of Zuar is the leader of the people of Issachar. 6 His division has 54,400 men. 7 Then the tribe of Zebulun is to camp. Eliab son of Helon is the leader of the people of Zebulun. 8 His division has 57,400 men. 9 The total for the camp of Judah is 186,400 men. They are to move out first.

10 "On the south side, the divisions of Reuben are to camp under their banner. Elizur son of Shedeur is the leader of the people of Reuben. 11 His division has 46,500 men. 12 Next to him, the tribe of Simeon is to camp. Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai is the leader of the people of Simeon. 13 His division has 59,300 men. 14 Then the tribe of Gad is to camp. Eliasaph son of Reuel is the leader of the people of Gad. 15 His division has 45,650 men. 16 The total for the camp of Reuben is 151,450 men. They are to move out second. 17 Then the Tent of Meeting and the Levites' camp will set out in the middle of the camps. They will move in the same order they camp, each man in his place under his banner.

Camps on the West and North Sides

18 "On the west side, the divisions of Ephraim are to camp under their banner. Elishama son of Ammihud is the leader of the people of Ephraim. 19 His division has 40,500 men. 20 Next to him, the tribe of Manasseh is to camp. Gamaliel son of Pedahzur is the leader of the people of Manasseh. 21 His division has 32,200 men. 22 Then the tribe of Benjamin is to camp. Abidan son of Gideoni is the leader of the people of Benjamin. 23 His division has 35,400 men. 24 The total for the camp of Ephraim is 108,100 men. They are to move out third.

25 "On the north side, the divisions of Dan are to camp under their banner. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai is the leader of the people of Dan. 26 His division has 62,700 men. 27 Next to him, the tribe of Asher is to camp. Pagiel son of Ocran is the leader of the people of Asher. 28 His division has 41,500 men. 29 Then the tribe of Naphtali is to camp. Ahira son of Enan is the leader of the people of Naphtali. 30 His division has 53,400 men. 31 The total for the camp of Dan is 157,600 men. They are to move out last, under their banners."

The Total Number of Israelites

32 These are the Israelites counted by their families. The total of all the camps by their divisions is 603,550 men. 33 The Levites were not counted among the Israelites, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 34 So the Israelites did everything the LORD commanded Moses. They camped under their banners, and they set out in the same order. Each man went with his family and his father's house.
