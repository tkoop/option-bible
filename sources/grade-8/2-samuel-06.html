David Brings the Ark of God

1David gathered all the chosen men of Israel again, thirty thousand in all. 2David and all the people with him left Baale Judah to bring up from there the ark of God. It is called by the Name, the name of the the LORD of Hosts, who is enthroned between the cherubim.

3They set the ark of God on a new cart and brought it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio, sons of Abinadab, were guiding the new cart. 4The ark of God was on it, and Ahio was walking in front of it.

5David and all Israel were celebrating with all their might before the the LORD, playing castanets, harps, lyres, tambourines, rattles, and cymbals.

Uzzah Dies for Touching the Ark

6When they came to the threshing floor of Nakon, Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark of God, because the oxen stumbled. 7The LORD's anger burned against Uzzah because of his irreverent act; so God struck him down, and he died there beside the ark of God.

8Then David was angry because the the LORD's wrath had broken out against Uzzah, and to this day that place is called Perez Uzzah. 9David was afraid of the the LORD that day and said, "How can the ark of the the LORD ever come to me?"

The Ark Stays with Obed-Edom

10He was not willing to take the ark of the the LORD to be with him in the City of David. Instead, he took it to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite. 11The ark of the the LORD remained in the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite for three months, and the the LORD blessed him and his entire household. 12Now King David was told, "The The LORD has blessed the household of Obed-Edom and everything he has, because of the ark of God." So David went to bring up the ark of God from the house of Obed-Edom to the City of David with rejoicing.

David Dances Before the the LORD

13When those who were carrying the ark of the the LORD had taken six steps, David sacrificed a bull and a fattened calf. 14Wearing a linen ephod, David was dancing before the the LORD with all his might. 15He and all Israel were bringing up the ark of the the LORD with shouts and the sound of trumpets.

16As the ark of the the LORD was entering the City of David, Michal daughter of Saul watched from a window. When she saw King David leaping and dancing before the the LORD, she despised him in her heart. 17They brought the ark of the the LORD and set it in its place inside the tent that David had pitched for it, and David sacrificed burnt offerings and peace offerings before the the LORD. 18After he had finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the the LORD of Hosts. 19Then he gave a loaf of bread, a cake of dates, and a cake of raisins to each person in the whole crowd of Israelites, both men and women. And all the people went to their homes.

20When David returned home to bless his household, Michal daughter of Saul came out to meet him and said, "How the king of Israel has honored himself today! He uncovered himself before the slave girls of his servants, like a vulgar man would!"

21David said to Michal, "It was before the the LORD, who chose me rather than your father or anyone from his house when he appointed me ruler over the the LORD's people Israel—I will celebrate before the the LORD. 22I will become even more undignified than this, and I will be humiliated in my own eyes. But by these slave girls you spoke of, I will be held in honor."

23And Michal daughter of Saul had no children until the day of her death.
