Spiritual Gifts and the Spirit

1 Now about spiritual gifts, brothers and sisters, I don't want you to be unaware. 2 You know that when you were pagans, you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led. 3 So I make known to you that no one who speaks by the Spirit of God says, "Jesus is cursed," and no one can say, "Jesus is Lord," except by the Holy Spirit.

4 There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit gives them. 5 There are different kinds of service, but the same Lord. 6 There are different kinds of working, but the same God works all of them in everyone.

7 Each person is given a way the Spirit makes Himself known for the good of all. 8 To one person the Spirit gives a message of wisdom; to another, a message of knowledge by the same Spirit. 9 To another, faith by that same Spirit; to another, gifts of healing by the one Spirit. 10 To another, the power to do miracles; to another, prophecy; to another, the ability to tell spirits apart; to another, different kinds of tongues; and to another, the ability to explain tongues. 11 All these gifts are the work of one and the same Spirit. He gives them to each person, just as He chooses.

One Body with Many Parts

12 The body is like this: it is one, but it has many parts. Even though there are many parts, they all make up one body. It is the same with Christ. 13 We were all baptized by one Spirit into one body—whether Jews or Greeks, slaves or free—and we were all given the one Spirit to drink. 14 The body is not just one part; it has many parts.

15 If the foot said, "Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body," that would not make it stop being part of the body.

16 And if the ear said, "Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body," that would not make it stop being part of the body.

17 If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If the whole body were an ear, where would smelling be? 18 But God has placed the parts in the body, each one just as He wanted. 19 If all the parts were the same, where would the body be? 20 As it is, there are many parts, but one body.

21 The eye cannot say to the hand, "I don't need you!"

And the head cannot say to the feet, "I don't need you!"

22 In fact, the parts that seem weaker are needed even more. 23 We give special honor to the parts we think are less honorable. The parts we are ashamed to show are treated with more respect. 24 The parts we are not ashamed to show do not need that. But God has put the body together and given more honor to the part that lacked it. 25 He did this so there would be no division in the body, but so the parts would care for each other. 26 If one part suffers, all the parts suffer with it. If one part is honored, all the parts rejoice with it.

God's Order in the Church

27 You are the body of Christ, and each of you is a part of it. 28 God has placed people in the church: first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then those who do miracles, then gifts of healing, helping, guiding, and different kinds of tongues.

29 Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles? 30 Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

31 But eagerly desire the greater gifts. And I will show you a way that is even better.
