Remember Our Shame, LORD

1 Remember, the LORD, what has happened to us. Look and see our shame. 2 Our land has been given to strangers, and our homes to foreigners. 3 We have become orphans with no father. Our mothers are like widows. 4 We have to pay for the water we drink, and we must buy wood. 5 Our enemies chase us and stay close behind us. We are tired and get no rest.

We Suffer for Our Fathers' Sins

6 We turned to Egypt and Assyria to get enough food. 7 Our fathers sinned, and they are gone, but we suffer for their wrongs. 8 Slaves rule over us, and no one can save us from them. 9 We risk our lives to get bread because of danger in the desert. 10 Our skin is hot like an oven because of the burning hunger.

Our People Are Hurting

11 Women have been abused in Zion, and young women in the towns of Judah. 12 Leaders were hung by their hands, and elders were not respected. 13 Young men grind grain, and boys stumble under loads of wood. 14 The elders no longer sit at the city gate, and the young men have stopped their music. 15 Joy has left our hearts. Our dancing has turned to mourning. 16 The crown has fallen from our head. How terrible for us, because we have sinned!

The LORD Rules Forever

17 Because of this, our hearts are weak. Because of these things, our eyes grow dim. 18 Mount Zion is empty and ruined, and jackals walk over it. 19 You, the LORD, reign forever. Your throne lasts from generation to generation. 20 Why do You forget us forever? Why do You leave us for so long? 21 Bring us back to You, the LORD, so we may return. Make our days new again, like they were before. 22 Unless You have completely rejected us and are very angry with us.
