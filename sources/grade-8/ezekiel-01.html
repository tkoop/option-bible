Ezekiel’s Vision by the Kebar River

1 In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, I was among the captives by the Kebar River. The heavens opened, and I saw visions of God. 2 On the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year of King Jehoiachin’s exile, 3 the word of the the LORD came to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans by the Kebar River. There the hand of the the LORD was on him. 4 I looked, and I saw a windstorm coming from the north. It was a great cloud with fire flashing, and bright light shone all around it. In the middle of the fire, there was something like glowing metal.

Four Living Creatures

5 In the middle of it were four living creatures that looked like humans. 6 Each had four faces and four wings. 7 Their legs were straight, and their feet were like a calf’s hoof. They sparkled like polished bronze. 8 Under their wings on their four sides were human hands. All four had faces and wings. 9 Their wings touched one another. They did not turn when they moved; each one went straight ahead. 10 Their faces looked like this: each had a human face; on the right was a lion’s face; on the left was an ox’s face; and each also had an eagle’s face. 11 Their wings were spread upward. Two wings of each creature touched the wings of the others, and two covered their bodies. 12 Each one went straight ahead. Wherever the Spirit wanted to go, they went, and they did not turn as they moved. 13 The living creatures looked like burning coals of fire, like torches. Fire moved back and forth among the creatures, and lightning flashed out of the fire. 14 The creatures darted back and forth like flashes of lightning.

Wheels Beside the Creatures

15 As I looked at the living creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground beside each creature with its four faces. 16 The wheels looked like sparkling beryl. All four looked alike, and each wheel seemed to be inside another wheel. 17 When they moved, they went on their four sides, and they did not turn. 18 Their rims were high and frightening, and they were full of eyes all around. 19 When the living creatures moved, the wheels moved beside them. When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose too. 20 Wherever the Spirit went, the creatures and wheels went, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels. 21 When the creatures moved, the wheels moved; when they stood still, the wheels stood still; and when they rose from the ground, the wheels rose with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

A Throne Above the Expanse

22 Over the heads of the living creatures was something like a great expanse. It looked like awesome crystal, stretched out above their heads. 23 Under the expanse, their wings were straight out toward one another. Each creature had two wings covering its body. 24 When they moved, I heard the sound of their wings. It sounded like the roar of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty, and like the noise of an army. When they stood still, they lowered their wings. 25 A voice came from above the expanse over their heads. When they stood still, they lowered their wings. 26 Above the expanse over their heads was something like a throne. It looked like sapphire, and on the throne was a figure that looked like a man. 27 From the waist up, He looked like glowing metal with fire all around. From the waist down, He looked like fire, and bright light shone around Him. 28 The bright light around Him looked like a rainbow in a cloud on a rainy day. This was the appearance of the glory of the the LORD. When I saw it, I fell facedown, and I heard a voice speaking.
