Jonah Prays from Inside the Fish

1 From inside the fish, Jonah prayed to the LORD his God. 2 He said, “In my trouble I called to the LORD, and He answered me. From the depths of the grave I cried for help, and You heard my voice. 3 You threw me into the deep water, into the middle of the sea. The currents surrounded me, and all Your waves and breakers swept over me.

4 “I said I was driven from Your sight, but I will look again toward Your holy temple. 5 The waters closed in on me and threatened my life. The deep sea surrounded me, and seaweed wrapped around my head.

The LORD Brings Jonah Up

6 “I sank down to the bottoms of the mountains. The earth locked me in forever. But You, the LORD my God, brought my life up from the pit. 7 When I was about to give up, I remembered the LORD. My prayer reached You in Your holy temple.

Jonah Promises to Worship the LORD

8 “Those who worship worthless idols turn away from God’s faithful love. 9 But I will offer You a sacrifice of thanks. I will keep my promises. Salvation comes from the LORD.”

The LORD Frees Jonah

10 Then the LORD commanded the fish, and it spit Jonah out onto dry land.
