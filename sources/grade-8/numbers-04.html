Duties of the Kohathites

1 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 2 "Take a census of the Kohathite clans among the Levites. Count them by their families and their fathers' houses. 3 Count the men from thirty to fifty years old, everyone who can serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.

4 "This is the work of the Kohathites at the Tent of Meeting: caring for the Most Holy Place. 5 When the camp moves, Aaron and his sons must go in and take down the curtain veil. They must cover the ark of the Testimony with it. 6 They must put a covering of fine leather over it, spread a solid blue cloth over that, and put its poles in place.

7 "They must spread a blue cloth over the table of the Presence. On it they must put the plates, dishes, bowls, and pitchers for the drink offering, along with the regular bread. 8 They must spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover them with fine leather, and put the poles in place. 9 They must take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand that gives light, along with its lamps, wick trimmers, trays, and all the oil containers used to care for it. 10 Then they must put it and all its tools inside a covering of fine leather and place it on the carrying frame.

11 "They must spread a blue cloth over the gold altar and cover it with fine leather. They must put its poles in place. 12 They must take all the tools used for serving in the holy place, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with fine leather, and place them on the carrying frame. 13 They must remove the ashes from the bronze altar and spread a purple cloth over it. 14 They must put on it all the tools used there: the firepans, meat forks, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. These are all the altar's tools. They must spread a covering of fine leather over it and put its poles in place.

15 "When Aaron and his sons finish covering the holy things and all their tools as the camp moves out, the Kohathites may come and carry them. But they must not touch the holy things, or they will die. This is how the Kohathites are to carry the things for the Tent of Meeting. 16 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is to oversee the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the regular grain offering, and the anointing oil. He is in charge of the whole tabernacle and everything in it, including the holy things and their tools."

Protecting the Kohathites

17 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 18 "Do not let the Kohathite clans be cut off from the Levites. 19 So they may live and not die when they come near the Most Holy Place, do this for them: Aaron and his sons must go in and give each man his task and his load. 20 But the Kohathites must not go in to look at the holy things, even for a moment, or they will die."

Duties of the Gershonites and Merarites

21 Then the LORD said to Moses, 22 "Take a census of the Gershonites too, by their families and their fathers' houses. 23 Count the men from thirty to fifty years old, everyone who can serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.

24 "This is the work of the Gershonite clans in carrying and moving things: 25 They are to carry the curtains of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting with its covering, the covering of fine leather over it, and the curtain at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. 26 They are also to carry the curtains of the courtyard, the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard around the tabernacle and altar, their ropes, and all the equipment for their work. They will carry everything that needs to be done.

27 "All the work of the Gershonites, including all their carrying duties and other work, is to be done as Aaron and his sons direct. You are to assign them all their responsibilities. 28 This is the work of the Gershonite clans at the Tent of Meeting. Their duties are under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

29 "You are to count the Merarites by their clans and families. 30 Count the men from thirty to fifty years old, everyone who can serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 31 This is their duty for all their work at the Tent of Meeting: to carry the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, posts, and bases. 32 They are also to carry the posts of the surrounding courtyard, their bases, tent pegs, ropes, and all their equipment and tools. You are to assign by name the items they are to carry. 33 This is the work of the Merarite clans in all their work at the Tent of Meeting. It is under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

Census of the Levites

34 So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of the community counted the Kohathites by their clans and families. 35 They counted the men from thirty to fifty years old who could serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 36 The number counted by their clans was 2,750.

37 These were the counted men of the Kohathite clans, everyone who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had commanded through Moses.

38 Then the Gershonites were counted by their clans and families. 39 They counted the men from thirty to fifty years old who could serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 40 The number counted by their clans was 2,630.

41 These were the counted men of the Gershonites, everyone who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had commanded.

42 Then the Merarites were counted by their clans and families. 43 They counted the men from thirty to fifty years old who could serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting. 44 The number counted by their clans was 3,200.

45 These were the counted men of the Merarite clans, everyone who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had commanded through Moses.

46 So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel counted all the Levites by their clans and families. 47 They counted the men from thirty to fifty years old who could do the work of serving and carrying loads in the Tent of Meeting. 48 Their total number was 8,580.

49 At the LORD's command, Moses counted them. Each man was counted for his work and his load, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
