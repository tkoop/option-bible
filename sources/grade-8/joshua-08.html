The LORD Tells Joshua Not to Fear Ai

1 After Israel's defeat at Ai, the LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid or discouraged. Take all the warriors with you, and go up to Ai. See, I have given into your hand the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his land. 2 Do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king. But you may take its plunder and livestock for yourselves. Set an ambush behind the city."

3 So Joshua and all the fighting men got ready to go up to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand mighty warriors and sent them out at night.

4 He gave them this order: "Watch carefully. You are to hide in ambush behind the city. Do not go very far from it, and all of you must be ready. 5 I and all the people with me will move toward the city. When its men come out to fight us, as they did before, we will run from them. 6 They will chase us until we have drawn them away from the city, because they will say, 'They are running from us again, just as before.' Then we will run from them. 7 You must rise up from your hiding place and take the city. The LORD your God will give it into your hands. 8 When you have taken the city, set it on fire. Do what the LORD has commanded. See, I have given you this order."

9 So Joshua sent them off, and they went to the hiding place. They stayed between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai. Joshua spent that night with the people. 10 Early the next morning, Joshua got up and gathered the people. He and the elders of Israel went up in front of the people toward Ai.

11 All the fighting men who were with him went up and came near the city. They camped north of Ai, with a valley between them and Ai. 12 Joshua took about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. 13 So the main camp was north of the city, and the rear guard was west of the city. Joshua spent that night in the valley.

The Battle for Ai

14 When the king of Ai saw this, he and all his men hurried out early in the morning to meet Israel in battle at the place near the Arabah. He did not know that men were hiding behind the city. 15 Joshua and all Israel let themselves be pushed back by them and ran toward the wilderness. 16 All the men in the city were called out to chase them. They chased Joshua and were drawn away from the city. 17 Not a man was left in Ai or Bethel who did not go after Israel. They left the city open and chased Israel.

18 Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Stretch out the spear in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand." Joshua stretched out the spear in his hand toward the city. 19 As soon as he did, the men in ambush rose quickly from their place and ran into the city. They captured it and quickly set it on fire. 20 The men of Ai looked back and saw smoke rising from the city into the sky. They had no way to escape in either direction. The Israelites who had been running toward the wilderness turned back against their pursuers. 21 When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city and saw the smoke, they turned back and struck down the men of Ai.

22 The other Israelites came out of the city against them, so the men of Ai were caught in the middle. Israel struck them down until no survivor or escapee was left. 23 But they captured the king of Ai alive and brought him to Joshua. 24 When Israel had finished killing all the people of Ai in the open country and in the wilderness where they had chased them, and when all of them had fallen by the sword, all Israel went back to Ai and struck it down with the sword. 25 Twelve thousand men and women of Ai died that day.

26 Joshua did not draw back the hand that held the spear until he had completely destroyed all the people of Ai. 27 Israel took only the livestock and the plunder of that city for themselves, as the LORD had commanded Joshua. 28 So Joshua burned Ai and made it a permanent heap of ruins, which it still is today. 29 He hung the king of Ai on a tree until evening. At sunset, Joshua gave the order, and they took his body down from the tree. They threw it at the entrance to the city gate and piled a large heap of stones over it. That heap is still there today.

Joshua Builds an Altar on Mount Ebal

30 Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal. 31 He did this just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded the Israelites. It is written in the Book of the Law of Moses: an altar of whole stones, on which no iron tool had been used. On it they offered burnt offerings to the LORD and sacrificed peace offerings.

32 There, in front of the Israelites, Joshua wrote on the stones a copy of the Law of Moses, which Moses had written.

33 All Israel, including foreigners and native-born people, along with their elders, officers, and judges, stood on both sides of the ark of the covenant of the LORD. Half of them faced Mount Gerizim, and half faced Mount Ebal, just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded at first, in order to bless the people of Israel. 34 After that, Joshua read all the words of the Law, including the blessings and the curses, exactly as written in the Book of the Law. 35 There was not a word of all that Moses had commanded that Joshua did not read before the whole assembly of Israel, including the women, the children, and the foreigners living among them.
