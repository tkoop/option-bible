Elihu Asks Job to Listen

1 "But now, Job, please listen to my words; pay attention to everything I say. 2 I am about to open my mouth; my tongue is ready to speak. 3 My words come from a sincere heart; my lips speak true knowledge. 4 The Spirit of God made me; the breath of the Almighty gives me life. 5 Answer me if you can; prepare your case and stand before me. 6 I am the same as you in God's sight; I too was formed from clay. 7 So you need not fear me; I will not be harsh with you.

Job's Complaint and Elihu's Reply

8 "Surely you have spoken in my hearing, and I have heard your words:

9 "'I am pure and without sin; I am clean and free of guilt. 10 Yet God finds fault with me; He considers me His enemy. 11 He puts my feet in stocks; He watches all my paths.'

12 "But I tell you, you are wrong in this, for God is greater than people. 13 Why do you argue with Him? He does not have to explain everything He does.

How God Speaks to People

14 "For God speaks in one way and then another, but people may not notice. 15 He may speak in a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls on people as they slumber in their beds. 16 He opens their ears and disciplines them. 17 He does this to turn a person from wrong actions and keep him from pride. 18 He spares his soul from the grave and his life from dying violently.

19 "Or a man is corrected on his bed with pain and constant distress in his bones. 20 He loses his appetite for food, and even his favorite meal turns his stomach. 21 His body wastes away until his bones show. 22 He comes close to the grave, to the place of death.

God's Mercy and Elihu's Final Plea

23 "If there is an angel for him, one out of a thousand, to speak up for a man's uprightness, 24 then God is gracious to him and says,

"'Spare him from going down to the pit; I have found a ransom.'

25 "His flesh is restored like a child's; he returns to the days of his youth. 26 He prays to God and finds favor; he sees His face with a shout of joy, and God restores His righteousness to man. 27 Then he will say to others,

"'I sinned and twisted what was right, but it did me no good. 28 He has redeemed my soul from going down to the pit, and my life will see the light.'

29 "Look, God does all these things to a person two or three times, 30 to bring his soul back from the pit, so that he may be enlightened with the light of life. 31 Pay attention, Job, and listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. 32 If you have something to say, answer me; speak, because I want to clear your name. 33 If not, listen to me; be silent, and I will teach you wisdom."
