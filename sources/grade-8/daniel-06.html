Daniel Rises to Power

1 Darius decided to put 120 governors in charge of different parts of his kingdom. 2 He also chose three supervisors over them, and Daniel was one of them. The governors had to report to these three men so the king would not lose anything. 3 Daniel did so well that he stood out above all the other supervisors and governors, because he had an excellent spirit. So the king planned to put him in charge of the whole kingdom.

A Plot Against Daniel

4 Then the supervisors and governors tried to find a reason to accuse Daniel of wrongdoing in his government work. But they could not find any reason or fault, because he was faithful. They found no mistake or corruption in him.

5 So these men said, "We will not find any reason to accuse Daniel unless it has to do with the law of his God."

6 Then those men rushed to the king and said, "Long live King Darius! 7 All the leaders of the kingdom—the supervisors, governors, advisors, and local officials—agree that the king should make a law and enforce it. For thirty days, no one may pray to any god or person except to you, King. Anyone who does must be thrown into the lions' den. 8 Now, King, put this rule into effect and sign the document so it cannot be changed. It must follow the law of the Medes and Persians, which cannot be revoked."

9 So King Darius signed the document and the law.

Daniel in the Lions' Den

10 When Daniel learned that the document had been signed, he went home. In his upstairs room, the windows were open toward Jerusalem. Three times a day he got down on his knees. He prayed and thanked his God, just as he had always done. 11 Then those men came together and found Daniel praying and asking his God for help.

12 They went to the king and said, "King, didn't you sign a law saying that for thirty days no one may pray to any god or person except to you? Anyone who does must be thrown into the lions' den?"

The king answered, "Yes. That is the law of the Medes and Persians, and it cannot be changed."

13 Then they said to the king, "Daniel, one of the captives from Judah, does not obey you, King, or the law you signed. He still prays three times a day."

14 When the king heard this, he was very upset. He tried hard to save Daniel. He worked until sunset to rescue him.

15 Then those men came together to the king and said, "Remember, King, that under the law of the Medes and Persians, no law or rule the king makes can be changed."

16 Then the king gave the order. They brought Daniel and threw him into the lions' den. The king said to Daniel, "May your God, whom you serve all the time, save you!"

17 A stone was brought and placed over the opening of the den. The king sealed it with his own ring and with the rings of his nobles. That way, no one could change anything about Daniel's case. 18 Then the king went to his palace. He did not eat that night. No entertainment was brought to him, and he could not sleep.

God Rescues Daniel

19 At dawn, the king got up and hurried to the lions' den. 20 As he got close, he called out in a sad voice. The king said to Daniel, "Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God, whom you serve all the time, been able to save you from the lions?"

21 Daniel answered, "Long live the king! 22 My God sent His angel and shut the lions' mouths. They did not hurt me, because I was innocent before Him. I also have done you no wrong, King."

23 The king was very glad. He ordered that Daniel be lifted out of the den. They pulled Daniel out, and he had no injury at all, because he had trusted in his God.

24 Then the king ordered that the men who had falsely accused Daniel be brought and thrown into the lions' den. Their children and wives were thrown in too. Before they even reached the bottom, the lions attacked them and crushed all their bones.

25 Then King Darius wrote to all the nations and peoples of every language: "May you have great peace! 26 I order that in every part of my kingdom, people must tremble and fear before the God of Daniel. He is the living God. He lasts forever. His kingdom will never be destroyed, and His rule will never end. 27 He saves and rescues. He does signs and wonders in heaven and on earth. He saved Daniel from the lions."

28 So Daniel did well during the rule of Darius and during the rule of Cyrus the Persian.
