The Widow's Offering

1 Jesus looked up and saw rich people putting their gifts into the temple treasury. 2 He also saw a poor widow put in two small coins.

3 "Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put in more than all of them. 4 All these people gave from what they had left over, but she gave from what she needed. She put in all she had to live on."

The Temple Will Be Destroyed

5 Some people were talking about the temple. They said it was decorated with beautiful stones and gifts given to God. Jesus said, 6 "As for what you see here, the days will come when not one stone will be left on another. Every stone will be thrown down."

7 They asked Him, "Teacher, when will these things happen? What sign will show that they are about to happen?"

Signs of the End Times

8 "Watch out so you are not fooled. Many will come in My name and say, 'I am he,' or, 'The time is near.' Do not follow them.

9 "When you hear about wars and riots, do not be afraid. These things must happen first, but the end will not come right away.

10 "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. 11 There will be great earthquakes, famines, and deadly diseases in many places. There will also be scary events and great signs from heaven.

12 "But before all this happens, people will arrest you and hurt you because of My name. They will hand you over to synagogues and prisons, and you will be brought before kings and governors. 13 This will be your chance to tell them about Me.

14 "So decide now not to worry ahead of time about how you will answer. 15 For I will give you words and wisdom that none of your enemies will be able to resist or argue against.

16 "You will be betrayed even by your parents, brothers, relatives, and friends. Some of you will be put to death. 17 Everyone will hate you because of My name.

18 "But not even one hair on your head will be lost. 19 By your patient endurance, you will save your lives.

20 "When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, you will know that it is about to be destroyed. 21 Then those in Judea should run to the mountains. Those in the city should leave, and those in the country should not go into the city.

22 "These are the days of punishment, so that everything written will come true. 23 It will be terrible for pregnant women and nursing mothers in those days. There will be great trouble on the earth and anger against this people. 24 They will be killed by the sword and taken as prisoners to all nations. Jerusalem will be trampled by other nations until their time is over.

25 "There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars. On earth, nations will be upset and confused by the roaring and tossing of the sea. 26 People will faint from fear as they wait for what is coming on the world, because the powers of heaven will be shaken. 27 Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory."

Be Ready for Jesus' Return

28 "When these things begin to happen, stand up and lift up your heads, because your rescue is near."

29 He told them this story: 30 "Look at the fig tree and all the trees. As soon as they sprout leaves, you can see for yourselves that summer is near. 31 In the same way, when you see these things happening, you know that God's kingdom is near."

32 "Truly I tell you, this generation will not pass away until all these things have happened. 33 Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will never pass away."

34 "Be careful, or your hearts will be weighed down by wild parties, drunkenness, and the worries of life. Then that day will close in on you suddenly, like a trap. 35 For it will come on everyone who lives on the face of the whole earth. 36 So stay awake at all times and pray that you will have the strength to escape all these things that are going to happen, and to stand before the Son of Man."

37 Each day Jesus taught in the temple, and each evening He went out to spend the night on the hill called the Mount of Olives. 38 All the people came early in the morning to hear Him in the temple.
