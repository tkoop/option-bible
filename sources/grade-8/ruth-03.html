Naomi's Plan for Ruth

1 Naomi, Ruth's mother-in-law, said to her, "My daughter, shouldn't I find a safe home for you, so things go well for you? 2 Now Boaz, whose young women you worked with, is he not our relative? Look, tonight he is winnowing barley at the threshing floor. 3 So wash up, put on perfume, and put on your best clothes. Then go down to the threshing floor, but don't let the man know you are there until he has finished eating and drinking. 4 When he lies down, notice the place where he lies. Then go and uncover his feet and lie down there. He will tell you what you should do."

5 Ruth said, "I will do everything you say."

6 So she went down to the threshing floor and did everything her mother-in-law had told her to do.

Ruth Meets Boaz at the Threshing Floor

7 Boaz ate and drank, and he felt happy. Then he went to lie down at the far end of the grain pile. Ruth came quietly, uncovered his feet, and lay down.

8 In the middle of the night, the man woke up and turned over. He was startled to find a woman lying at his feet.

9 "Who are you?" he asked.

"I am Ruth, your servant," she replied. "Please make me your wife, because you are our family redeemer."

Boaz Promises to Help

10 "May you be blessed by the LORD, my daughter," he said. "You have shown even greater kindness now than before, because you didn't chase after young men, whether poor or rich.

11 "And now, my daughter, don't be afraid. I will do for you everything you ask, because everyone in town knows you are a woman of noble character.

12 "It is true that I am a redeemer, but there is another redeemer who is closer to our family than I am.

13 "Stay here tonight. In the morning, if he wants to redeem you, good—let him do it. But if he is not willing, then, as the LORD lives, I will redeem you myself. Now lie down until morning."

14 So she lay at his feet until morning. She got up before anyone could recognize another person. He said, "Let it not be known that the woman came to the threshing floor."

15 He said, "Bring the shawl you are wearing and hold it out." So she held it, and he measured out six measures of barley and put it on her. Then she went into the city.

Ruth Returns to Naomi

16 When she came to her mother-in-law, Naomi asked, "How did it go, my daughter?" Then Ruth told her everything the man had done for her.

17 Ruth said, "He gave me these six measures of barley, for he said, 'You must not go back empty-handed to your mother-in-law.'"

18 Naomi said, "Wait here, my daughter, until you learn how this turns out. The man won't rest until he settles the matter today."
