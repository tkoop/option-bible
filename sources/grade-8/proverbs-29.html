Wisdom for Daily Life

1 A stubborn person who ignores many warnings will be destroyed suddenly, with no cure. 2 When good people do well, everyone celebrates; when evil people rule, everyone groans. 3 A son who loves wisdom makes his father happy, but a man who spends time with prostitutes wastes his money. 4 A king who rules with justice makes the land stable, but a man who takes bribes tears it down.

5 A man who flatters his neighbor sets a trap for him. 6 An evil man is trapped by his own sin, but a good person sings and is glad. 7 Good people care about justice for the poor, but wicked people do not care about it. 8 Mockers stir up trouble in a city, but wise people calm anger.

9 If a wise man argues with a foolish man, the fool gets angry and laughs, and there is no peace. 10 Violent people hate an innocent person, but good people care about his life. 11 A foolish person lets out all his anger, but a wise person keeps it under control. 12 If a ruler listens to lies, all his workers become wicked.

The LORD Gives Life to All

13 The poor person and the oppressor have this in common: The LORD gives life to both. 14 A king who judges poor people fairly will have a throne that lasts forever. 15 A rod and a warning give wisdom, but a child left on his own disgraces his mother. 16 When wicked people gain power, sin grows, but good people will see them fall. 17 Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will bring joy to your life.

18 Without a vision, people lose control; but happy is the one who keeps the law. 19 A servant cannot be corrected by words alone; even if he understands, he will not answer. 20 Do you see a man who speaks too quickly? There is more hope for a fool than for him. 21 A servant who is spoiled from childhood will bring grief in the end.

Anger, Pride, and Trust in the LORD

22 An angry man stirs up fights, and a hot-tempered man sins a lot. 23 Pride brings a person down, but a humble spirit gains honor. 24 A person who partners with a thief hates his own life; he is sworn in but says nothing. 25 Fear of people is a trap, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe. 26 Many seek a ruler's favor, but justice for a person comes from the LORD. 27 The way of an evil person is disgusting to good people, and the way of a good person is disgusting to evil people.
