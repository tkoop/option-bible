The LORD Will Defeat Leviathan and Care for His People

1 On that day, the LORD will use His fierce, great, and strong sword to punish Leviathan, the fleeing serpent—Leviathan the twisting serpent—and He will kill the dragon in the sea.

2 On that day, people will sing, "A delightful vineyard!"

3 "I, the LORD, watch over it. I water it all the time. I guard it day and night so no one can harm it. 4 I have no anger. If only thorns and briers stood against Me, I would march against them in battle and burn them all up. 5 Or let them take hold of My strength and make peace with Me—yes, let them make peace with Me."

Israel Will Blossom and Be Restored

6 In the days to come, Jacob will take root. Israel will blossom and bud, and fill the whole world with fruit.

7 Did the LORD strike Israel the same way He struck its enemies? Was Israel killed like the people who attacked it?

8 By warfare and exile, You fought against her. He removed her with His fierce wind on the day of the east wind.

9 So Jacob's guilt will be forgiven, and the result of removing his sin will be this: he will make all the altar stones like crushed chalk, and no Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing.

10 The fortified city will be deserted. It will be like a lonely home left in the wilderness. There calves will graze, and there they will lie down and strip its branches bare. 11 When its branches dry up, they will break off. Women will come and use them for firewood. This is a people without understanding, so their Maker will not have compassion on them, and their Creator will show them no favor.

God Will Gather His People

12 On that day, the LORD will thresh from the Euphrates River to the brook of Egypt, and you, people of Israel, will be gathered one by one. 13 On that day, a great trumpet will sound, and those who were perishing in Assyria and those who were exiled in Egypt will come and worship the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
