A Righteous King Is Coming

1 Look, a king will reign with justice, and princes will rule with fairness. 2 Each one will be like a shelter from the wind and a hiding place from the storm. He will be like streams of water in a dry land, and like the shade of a huge rock in a tired land.

3 Then the eyes of those who see will not be closed, and the ears of those who hear will listen. 4 The heart of the rash will understand, and the tongue of those who stutter will speak clearly.

5 No longer will a fool be called noble, or a scoundrel be called respected. 6 A fool speaks foolish things, and his heart plans evil. He does ungodly things and speaks lies about the LORD. He leaves hungry people empty and keeps thirsty people from drink. 7 The weapons of a scoundrel destroy; he plans tricks to ruin poor people with lies, even when the needy ask for justice. 8 But a noble man plans noble things, and he stands firm in them.

Warning to Complacent Women

9 Rise up, you complacent women; listen to my voice. You daughters who feel secure, hear my words. 10 In a little more than a year, you complacent women will tremble. The grape harvest will fail, and the fruit harvest will not come. 11 Tremble, you complacent women; shudder, you daughters who feel secure. Strip off your fine clothes and put on sackcloth.

12 Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields and the fruitful vine. 13 Beat them for the land of my people, now overgrown with thorns and briers. Yes, beat them for every happy house and every joyful city. 14 The palace will be left empty, and the busy city will be abandoned. The hill and the watchtower will become caves forever. They will be a joy for wild donkeys and a pasture for flocks.

The Spirit Brings Renewal

15 This will last until the Spirit is poured out on us from above. Then the desert will become a fertile field, and the fertile field will seem like a forest. 16 Justice will live in the desert, and righteousness will stay in the fertile field.

17 The work of righteousness will be peace, and its service will be quiet confidence forever. 18 Then my people will live in a peaceful place, in safe homes and quiet resting places. 19 But hail will flatten the forest, and the city will sink into the lowlands. 20 Blessed are you who sow beside all waters and let the ox and donkey roam free.
