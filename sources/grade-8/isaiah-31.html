Warning Against Trusting Egypt

1 How terrible it will be for those who go down to Egypt for help, who rely on horses, who trust in many chariots and many horsemen. They do not look to the Holy One of Israel, and they do not seek the LORD. 2 Yet He is also wise and brings disaster. He will not take back His words. He will rise up against the house of evildoers and against the helpers of evildoers. 3 But the Egyptians are only people, not God. Their horses are flesh, not spirit. When the LORD stretches out His hand, the helper will stumble, and the one being helped will fall. They will all be destroyed together.

The LORD Will Defend Jerusalem

4 For this is what the LORD says to me: “As a lion growls, and a young lion over its prey—and though a whole group of shepherds is called against it, it is not scared by their shouts or quieted by their noise—so the LORD of Hosts will come down to fight on Mount Zion and on its hill. 5 Like birds flying overhead, so the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem. He will protect and save it; He will pass over and rescue it.”

Call to Return and Reject Idols

6 Return to Him from whom you have deeply revolted, children of Israel. 7 For on that day each person will reject his silver idols and gold idols, which your own hands have made and which have caused you to sin.

Assyria Will Fall

8 “Then Assyria will fall by a sword that is not from a person. A sword not from a person will destroy him. He will run from the sword, and his young men will become forced labor. 9 His stronghold will pass away in terror, and his princes will panic at the battle flag,” declares the LORD, whose fire is in Zion and whose furnace is in Jerusalem.
