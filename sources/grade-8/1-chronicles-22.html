David Prepares for God's Temple

1 David said, "This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel."

David Gathers Workers and Materials

2 David ordered the foreigners living in Israel to be gathered. He also put stoneworkers in charge of cutting dressed stones to build God's house. 3 He prepared a large amount of iron for nails for the doors and gates and for metal brackets. He prepared so much bronze that it could not be weighed. 4 He had more cedar logs than anyone could count, because the people of Sidon and Tyre brought David a huge amount of cedar wood.

David Instructs Solomon

5 David said, "My son Solomon is young and inexperienced. The house to be built for the LORD must be very great, so that it brings honor and glory to His name in every land. So I will prepare for it." Then David prepared a great amount of material before he died. 6 Then he called for his son Solomon and commanded him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.

7 David said to Solomon, "My son, I wanted to build a house for the name of the LORD my God. 8 But the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 'You have shed much blood and fought great wars. You will not build a house for My name, because you have shed so much blood on the earth before Me. 9 But a son will be born to you. He will be a man of peace, and I will give him rest from all his enemies around him. His name will be Solomon, and I will give peace and quiet to Israel during his days. 10 He will build a house for My name. He will be My son, and I will be his Father. I will make his royal throne firm over Israel forever.' 11 Now, my son, may the LORD be with you. May you succeed and build the house of the LORD your God, as He has spoken concerning you. 12 Only may the LORD give you wisdom and understanding, and give you charge over Israel, so that you may keep the law of the LORD your God. 13 Then you will prosper if you are careful to do the statutes and judgments the LORD commanded Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or discouraged.

"14 Now look, in my hardship I have prepared for the house of the LORD one hundred thousand talents of gold and one million talents of silver. I have also prepared bronze and iron beyond weighing, because there is so much. I have prepared wood and stone, and you may add to them. 15 There are many workers with you—stonecutters, masons, carpenters, and skilled workers of every kind. 16 There is more gold, silver, bronze, and iron than anyone can count. Get up and work, and may the LORD be with you."

David Urges Israel's Leaders to Help

17 David also commanded all the leaders of Israel to help his son Solomon, saying, "18 Isn't the LORD your God with you? Hasn't He given you rest on every side? For He has given the people of the land into my hand, and the land is subdued before the LORD and before His people. 19 Now set your heart and your soul to seek the LORD your God. Get up and build the sanctuary of the LORD God, so that you can bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD and the holy vessels of God into the house built for the name of the LORD."
