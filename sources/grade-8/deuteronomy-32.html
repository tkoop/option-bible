Moses Sings to Israel

1 Listen, heavens, and I will speak. Let the earth hear the words from my mouth. 2 Let my teaching fall like rain. Let my words drop like dew, like gentle rain on new grass, like showers on plants. 3 I will announce the name of the LORD. Give glory to our God!

4 The Rock is perfect in His work, because all His ways are fair. He is a faithful God without evil. He is good and right. 5 They have ruined themselves before Him. They are not His children because of their faults. They are a crooked and twisted generation. 6 Is this how you repay the LORD, you foolish and senseless people? Isn't He your Father who created you? He made you and set you firm.

Remember the LORD's Care

7 Remember the old days. Think about the years of many generations. Ask your father, and he will show you. Ask your elders, and they will tell you. 8 When the Most High gave the nations their land, when He divided the people, He set the borders of the nations to match the number of Israel's people. 9 For the LORD's share is His people. Jacob is His chosen inheritance.

10 He found him in a desert land, in an empty and howling wilderness. He surrounded him and cared for him. He guarded him like His most precious treasure. 11 Like an eagle that stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, He spread His wings and caught him. He carried him on His wings. 12 The LORD alone led him. No foreign god was with him.

13 He made him ride on the high places of the land. He ate the crops of the field. He nursed him with honey from the rock and oil from the hard rock. 14 He gave him curds from cows and milk from sheep, with fat from lambs and goats and rams from Bashan. He fed him with the best wheat and gave him wine from grapes to drink.

Israel Turns Away from God

15 But Israel grew fat and kicked. You grew fat, heavy, and well-fed. Then he left the God who made him and turned away from the Rock who saved him. 16 They made Him jealous with strange gods. They angered Him with disgusting idols. 17 They sacrificed to demons, not to God. These were gods they did not know, new gods that came lately, which your fathers never feared. 18 You forgot the Rock who gave you life. You forgot the God who created you.

19 The LORD saw this and rejected them. He was angry with His sons and daughters. 20 He said, "I will hide My face from them. I will see what happens to them in the end. They are a twisted generation, children who cannot be trusted." 21 They made Me jealous with what is not a god. They angered Me with their useless idols. So I will make them jealous with a people who are not a nation. I will anger them with a foolish nation.

22 For a fire is burning in My anger. It burns down to the deepest grave. It eats up the earth and its crops. It sets fire to the foundations of the mountains. 23 I will bring many troubles on them. I will use up My arrows on them. 24 They will waste away from hunger and be eaten by disease and bitter sickness. I will send wild animals against them, along with the poison of snakes in the dust. 25 Outside, the sword will strike. Inside, there will be terror. It will strike young men and young women, nursing babies and old men.

26 I thought I would scatter them and erase their memory from mankind. 27 But I held back because I feared the enemy would brag. I feared their foes would misunderstand and say, "Our hand won this victory. The LORD did not do all this." 28 They are a nation without good sense, and they have no understanding. 29 If they were wise, they would understand this. They would think about what will happen to them in the end.

30 How could one person chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them and the LORD had given them up? 31 For their rock is not like our Rock. Even our enemies can see the difference. 32 Their vine comes from Sodom's vine. Their grapes come from Gomorrah's fields. Their grapes are bitter and poisonous. 33 Their wine is like snake venom, like deadly cobra poison.

The LORD Declares His Power

34 "Isn't this stored up with Me, sealed in My treasuries?" 35 Vengeance belongs to Me. I will pay them back when their foot slips. Their day of trouble is near. What is coming for them will happen soon. 36 For the LORD will judge His people and have mercy on His servants. He will see that their strength is gone and no one is left, slave or free. 37 Then He will say, "Where are their gods, the rock they trusted? 38 Who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their offerings? Let them rise up and help you. Let them be your protection!"

39 See now that I am He, and there is no god besides Me. I bring death and I give life. I wound and I heal. No one can rescue anyone from My hand. 40 For I raise My hand to heaven and swear that, as I live forever, 41 if I sharpen My flashing sword and take hold of judgment, I will take vengeance on My enemies and repay those who hate Me. 42 My arrows will be soaked in blood, and My sword will devour flesh. It will shed the blood of the slain and the captives, even the enemy leaders. 43 Rejoice, nations, with His people! For He will avenge the blood of His servants. He will take vengeance on His enemies and show mercy to His land and His people.

44 Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and spoke all the words of this song to the people. 45 When Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel,

46 he said to them, "Take to heart all the words I am using to warn you today. Command your children to obey carefully all the words of this law. 47 For these words are not empty for you. They are your very life. By these words you will live a long time in the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

48 That same day the LORD spoke to Moses, 49 "Go up into the Abarim Mountains, to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, across from Jericho. Look at the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their own. 50 Die on the mountain you are climbing, and be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people. 51 This is because you and Aaron broke faith with Me among the Israelites at the waters of Meribah Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin. You did not honor Me as holy among the Israelites. 52 You may see the land from a distance, but you will not enter it, the land I am giving to the Israelites."
