The Earth and All It Holds Belong to the LORD

1 A psalm of David. The earth belongs to the the LORD, and everything in it. The world and all who live in it are His. 2 He founded it on the seas and set it firm on the rivers.

3 Who may go up the the LORD’s holy hill? Who may stand in His holy place? 4 The one with clean hands and a pure heart, who doesn’t lift his soul to an idol or swear falsely.

5 He will receive a blessing from the the LORD and righteousness from God his Savior. 6 Such are the people who seek Him, who seek Your face, O God of Jacob. Selah.

The King of Glory Comes In

7 Lift up your heads, O gates, and be lifted up, O ancient doors, so the King of glory may come in. 8 Who is this King of glory? The The LORD, strong and mighty, the the LORD, mighty in battle.

9 Lift up your heads, O gates, and lift them up, O ancient doors, so the King of glory may come in. 10 Who is this King of glory? The the LORD of Hosts—He is the King of glory. Selah.
