Elkanah and His Two Wives

1 There was a man named Elkanah from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim. He was the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephrathite. 2 He had two wives. One was named Hannah, and the other was named Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none. 3 Every year Elkanah went up from his town to worship and sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts at Shiloh. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests of the LORD there. 4 On the day Elkanah sacrificed, he gave portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters. 5 But to Hannah he gave a double portion, because he loved her, even though the LORD had closed her womb. 6 Because the LORD had closed her womb, her rival kept bothering her and made her angry. 7 This happened year after year. Whenever Hannah went up to the LORD's house, Peninnah provoked her, so Hannah cried and would not eat.

Hannah Prays for a Child

8 Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Hannah, why are you crying? Why won't you eat? Why is your heart sad? Am I not worth more to you than ten sons?" 9 After they had eaten and drunk in Shiloh, Hannah got up. Now Eli the priest was sitting on his seat by the doorpost of the LORD's temple. 10 She was crushed in spirit, and she prayed to the LORD and wept bitterly. 11 She made a vow, saying, "the the LORD of Hosts, if You will look on the trouble of Your servant and remember me, and not forget Your servant, but give Your servant a son, then I will give him to the LORD for all the days of his life, and no razor will touch his head."

12 As she kept praying before the LORD, Eli watched her mouth. 13 Hannah was praying in her heart. Only her lips moved, and her voice was not heard. So Eli thought she was drunk.

14 Eli said to her, "How long will you stay drunk? Get rid of your wine."

15 Hannah answered, "No, my lord. I am a woman with a troubled spirit. I have not drunk wine or strong drink. I have poured out my soul before the LORD. 16 Do not think of your servant as a worthless woman. I have been speaking from my great sorrow and grief."

17 Eli answered, "Go in peace. May the God of Israel grant your petition that you asked of Him."

18 She said, "May your servant find favor in your eyes." Then the woman went on her way and ate, and her face was no longer sad.

Samuel Is Born and Dedicated

19 Early the next morning they worshiped before the LORD and returned home to Ramah. Elkanah was intimate with his wife Hannah, and the LORD remembered her. 20 In time Hannah became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She named him Samuel, saying, "Because I asked him of the LORD."

21 The next year Elkanah and his whole household went up to offer the yearly sacrifice to the LORD and to keep his vow. 22 But Hannah did not go up. She told her husband, "I will not go until the boy is weaned. Then I will take him to appear before the LORD, and he will stay there forever."

23 Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Do what you think is best. Stay until you have weaned him. May the LORD keep His word." So the woman stayed home and nursed her son until she weaned him.

24 After she had weaned him, she took him up with her, along with a three-year-old bull, an ephah of flour, and a skin of wine, and brought him to the house of the LORD at Shiloh. The boy was still young. 25 They slaughtered the bull and brought the boy to Eli.

26 She said, "As surely as you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here beside you praying to the LORD. 27 I prayed for this boy, and the LORD has given me what I asked of Him. 28 So now I give him to the LORD. For as long as he lives, he is given to the LORD." And he worshiped the LORD there.
