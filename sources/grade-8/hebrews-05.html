Every High Priest Is Chosen to Serve

1 Every high priest is chosen from among people and is put in charge of things between God and people. He offers gifts and sacrifices for sins. 2 He can be gentle with people who don't know better and who wander off the path, because he himself struggles with weakness. 3 Because of that weakness, he must offer sacrifices for his own sins as well as for the sins of the people. 4 No one takes this honor for himself. He receives it only when God calls him, just as Aaron was called.

Christ Became Our High Priest

5 In the same way, Christ did not give Himself the glory of becoming high priest. Instead, God said to Him, “You are My Son; today I have become Your Father.” 6 In another place God says, “You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek.”

7 While He lived in a human body, Christ offered prayers and requests to God. He cried out loudly and with tears to the One who could save Him from death, and God heard Him because He deeply respected God. 8 Even though He was the Son, He learned to obey through what He suffered. 9 After He was made complete, He became the source of eternal salvation for everyone who obeys Him. 10 God named Him a high priest in the order of Melchizedek.

Growing from Milk to Solid Food

11 We have a lot to say about this, but it is hard to explain because you have become slow to listen. 12 By now you should be teachers, but you still need someone to teach you the basic truths of God’s word. You need milk, not solid food. 13 Anyone who lives on milk is still a baby and does not understand the teaching about living right. 14 Solid food is for mature believers. Their senses have been trained by practice to tell good from evil.
