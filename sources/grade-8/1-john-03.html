God's Love Makes Us His Children

1 See how much love the Father has given us, so that we can be called children of God. And that is what we are. That is why the world does not know us, because it did not know Him. 2 Dear friends, we are already God's children. We do not yet know what we will be, but we know that when He appears, we will be like Him, because we will see Him as He really is.

3 Everyone who has this hope in Him keeps himself pure, just as He is pure.

Living Right and Avoiding Sin

4 Everyone who keeps on sinning breaks God's law, because sin is lawlessness. 5 And you know that He came to take away our sins, and there is no sin in Him. 6 No one who stays in Him keeps on sinning. No one who keeps on sinning has seen Him or known Him.

7 Dear children, do not let anyone fool you. The one who does what is right is righteous, just as He is righteous. 8 The one who keeps on sinning belongs to the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The Son of God came to destroy the devil's work. 9 No one born of God keeps on sinning, because God's seed stays in him. He cannot keep on sinning, because he has been born of God. 10 This is how we can tell God's children from the devil's children: Anyone who does not do what is right is not from God, and neither is anyone who does not love his brother.

Love One Another

11 This is the message you heard from the beginning: We should love one another. 12 We should not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother. Why did he murder him? Because his own actions were evil, but his brother's actions were right.

13 Do not be surprised, brothers and sisters, if the world hates you. 14 We know that we have moved from death to life, because we love our brothers and sisters. Anyone who does not love stays in death. 15 Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life in him.

16 This is how we know what love is: He gave His life for us, so we should give our lives for our brothers and sisters. 17 If anyone has enough of this world's goods and sees a brother or sister in need but does not help, how can God's love stay in that person? 18 Dear children, let us love not just with words or speech, but with actions and in truth.

Confidence Before God

19 This is how we know we belong to the truth, and this is how we can set our hearts at rest before Him. 20 If our hearts make us feel guilty, God is greater than our hearts, and He knows everything. 21 Dear friends, if our hearts do not make us feel guilty, we can be bold before God. 22 We receive from Him whatever we ask, because we obey His commands and do what pleases Him.

23 His command is this: We must believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as He commanded us. 24 Whoever obeys His commands stays in Him, and He stays in him. We know He stays in us because of the Spirit He gave us.
