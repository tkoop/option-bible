The Ark Stays in Kiriath-jearim

1 Then the men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the ark of the the LORD. They brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill and set apart his son Eleazar to guard the ark of the the LORD. 2 From that day the ark stayed in Kiriath-jearim for a long time—twenty years in all. All the people of Israel longed for the the LORD.

Israel Returns to the LORD

3 Then Samuel said to all the people of Israel, "If you are coming back to the the LORD with all your heart, then get rid of the foreign gods and the Ashtoreths among you. Prepare your hearts for the the LORD, and serve Him alone. Then He will save you from the Philistines."

4 So the Israelites put away the Baals and Ashtoreths and served the the LORD alone.

5 Then Samuel said, "Gather all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray to the the LORD for you."

6 When they had gathered at Mizpah, they drew water and poured it out before the the LORD. That day they fasted and confessed, "We have sinned against the the LORD." And Samuel judged the Israelites at Mizpah.

The Philistines Attack at Mizpah

7 When the Philistines heard that the Israelites had gathered at Mizpah, their rulers came up to attack Israel. The Israelites heard about it and were afraid of the Philistines.

8 The Israelites said to Samuel, "Do not stop crying out to the the LORD our God for us, so that He may save us from the Philistines."

9 Then Samuel took a young lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to the the LORD. He cried out to the the LORD for Israel, and the the LORD answered him.

10 While Samuel was offering the burnt offering, the Philistines came near to fight against Israel. But that day the the LORD thundered with a loud thunder against the Philistines and threw them into confusion, so they were beaten before Israel.

11 Then the men of Israel went out from Mizpah and chased the Philistines. They struck them down all the way to a place below Beth-car.

Samuel Judges Israel

12 Afterward, Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen. He named it Ebenezer, saying, "The The LORD has helped us this far."

13 So the Philistines were subdued and did not come into Israel's territory again. The hand of the the LORD was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel. 14 The cities from Ekron to Gath that the Philistines had taken from Israel were returned to Israel. Israel also took back their territory from the Philistines. And there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

15 Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life. 16 Year after year he traveled around to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, judging Israel in all those places. 17 Then he would return to Ramah, because his home was there, and there he judged Israel too. He built an altar to the the LORD there.
