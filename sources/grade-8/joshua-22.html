Joshua Sends the Eastern Tribes Home

1 Then Joshua called the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 2 He said to them, "You have kept all that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, commanded you. You have also obeyed my voice in everything I commanded you. 3 For many days you have not left your brothers. To this day you have kept the charge of the command of the the LORD your God. 4 Now the the LORD your God has given rest to your brothers, just as He promised them. So turn and go to your tents, to the land you own. Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave it to you east of the Jordan. 5 Only be very careful to obey the command and the law that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave you. Love the the LORD your God. Walk in all His ways. Keep His commands. Hold fast to Him. Serve Him with all your heart and all your soul."

6 Then Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents. 7 Moses had given land in Bashan to the half-tribe of Manasseh, and Joshua had given land west of the Jordan to the other half. Joshua also sent them to their tents among their brothers and blessed them. 8 He said to them, "Return to your tents with great wealth: with very much livestock, silver, gold, bronze, iron, and clothing. Divide the spoil of your enemies with your brothers."

The Eastern Tribes Build an Altar

9 So the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh left the Israelites at Shiloh in Canaan. They went to Gilead, to the land they owned, which they had taken as the the LORD had said through Moses. 10 When they came to the region of the Jordan in Canaan, they built a large altar there by the Jordan. It was big and easy to see.

11 The Israelites heard that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had built an altar on the border of Canaan, at Geliloth near the Jordan, on the Israelite side. 12 When the whole Israelite community heard this, they gathered at Shiloh to go to war against them.

Israel Sends Leaders to Question the Altar

13 Then the Israelites sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to Gilead, to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 14 With him went ten leaders, one from each ancestral house and one for each tribe of Israel. They were heads of their ancestral houses among the clans of Israel.

15 They came to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead and said to them, 16 "This is what the whole community of the the LORD says: What is this wrong you have done today against the God of Israel? You have turned away today from following the the LORD by building an altar for yourselves. Are you rebelling today against the the LORD? 17 Wasn't the sin at Peor bad enough for us? We still have not fully turned from it, and it brought a plague on the the LORD's people. 18 Yet you turn away today from following the the LORD? If you rebel today against the the LORD, tomorrow He will be angry with all Israel."

19 "If the land you own is unclean, cross over to the land the the LORD owns, where the the LORD's tabernacle stands, and take land among us. But do not rebel against the the LORD or against us by building an altar for yourselves besides the altar of the the LORD our God. 20 Didn't Achan son of Zerah break faith by taking devoted things? Wrath came on all Israel, and he was not the only one who died for his sin."

The Eastern Tribes Explain the Altar

21 Then the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the leaders of the clans of Israel. 22 They said, "The the LORD, the God of gods! The the LORD, the God of gods! He knows, and Israel will know too. If we rebelled or broke faith against the the LORD, do not save us today. 23 If we built this altar to turn away from following the the LORD, or to offer burnt offerings or grain offerings on it, or to make peace offerings on it, may the the LORD Himself hold us accountable. 24 No, we did this because we were worried. We thought, 'Someday your children might say to our children, "What do you have to do with the the LORD, the God of Israel?"'"

25 "The the LORD made the Jordan a border between us and you, Reubenites and Gadites. You have no share in the the LORD. We feared your children would make our children stop fearing the the LORD. 26 So we said, 'Let us build an altar—not for burnt offerings or sacrifices— 27 but as a witness between us and you and between our generations after us. Then we can serve the the LORD before Him with our burnt offerings, sacrifices, and peace offerings. That way your children will not say to our children later, "You have no share in the the LORD."'"

28 "We also said, 'If they say this to us or to our generations later, we will say, "Look at the model of the the LORD's altar that our fathers made. It is not for burnt offerings or sacrifices. It is a witness between us and you."' 29 Far be it from us to rebel against the the LORD or turn away from following Him today by building an altar for burnt offerings, grain offerings, or sacrifices, besides the altar of the the LORD our God that stands before His tabernacle."

30 Phinehas the priest, the leaders of the community, and the heads of Israel's clans with him heard what the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassehites said. They were pleased. 31 Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassehites, "Today we know the the LORD is among us, because you have not committed this wrong against the the LORD. Now you have saved the Israelites from the the LORD's hand." 32 Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders left the Reubenites and Gadites in Gilead and returned to Canaan. They brought a report back to the Israelites. 33 The Israelites were pleased with the report. They blessed God and decided not to go to war against them to destroy the land where the Reubenites and Gadites lived.

34 The Reubenites and Gadites named the altar Witness, because they said, "It is a witness between us that the the LORD is God."
