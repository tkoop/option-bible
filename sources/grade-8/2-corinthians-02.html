Paul's Reason for Not Visiting

1 For I decided on my own not to come to you again in sorrow. 2 For if I make you sad, who can make me glad except the one I made sad? 3 I wrote what I did so that when I came, I would not have sorrow from the people who should make me happy. I trust all of you, because I know my joy is your joy. 4 I wrote to you with many tears, out of great trouble and a heavy heart. I did not write to make you sad, but to show you how much love I have for you.

Forgive and Comfort the Offender

5 If anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused it to me alone, but in part to all of you. I say this so I will not be too hard on him. 6 The punishment this man received from the majority is enough. 7 So now you should forgive and comfort him, so he will not be swallowed up by too much sorrow. 8 So I urge you to reaffirm your love for him. 9 I also wrote for this reason: to test you, to see if you obey everything. 10 Anyone you forgive, I also forgive. And if I have forgiven anything, I did it for your sake in the presence of Christ. 11 We do this so Satan will not outsmart us, because we know his evil plans.

Paul Travels to Macedonia

12 When I came to Troas to preach the good news about Christ, the Lord opened a door for me. 13 But I had no peace in my spirit because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and went on to Macedonia.

The Fragrance of Christ

14 But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ and, through us, spreads everywhere the fragrance of the knowledge of Him. 15 For we are to God the pleasing aroma of Christ among those who are being saved and among those who are dying. 16 To some, we are a smell that leads from death to death; to others, a smell that leads from life to life. Who is able to do this work? 17 For we are not like many who sell God's word for profit. Instead, in Christ we speak with sincerity, as from God, in the sight of God.
