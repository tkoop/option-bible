Who May Enter the the LORD's Assembly

1 No man whose private parts are crushed or cut off may enter the assembly of the LORD. 2 No one born from an unlawful union may enter the assembly of the LORD, and none of his descendants may enter, even to the tenth generation. 3 No Ammonite or Moabite may ever enter the assembly of the LORD, not even in the tenth generation.

4 This is because they did not meet you with food and water on your way out of Egypt, and because they hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram Naharaim to curse you. 5 Yet the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam. Instead, the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loves you. 6 Never seek their peace or prosperity as long as you live.

7 Do not hate an Edomite, because he is your brother. Do not hate an Egyptian, because you were a foreigner in his land. 8 The children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of the LORD.

Keeping the Camp Clean

9 When you go out to fight your enemies, keep yourself from every evil thing. 10 If a man among you becomes unclean because of a discharge during the night, he must leave the camp and stay outside it. 11 In the evening he must wash with water, and when the sun goes down he may return to the camp.

12 You must have a place outside the camp where you can go to relieve yourself. 13 You must carry a digging tool with your gear. When you relieve yourself, dig a hole and cover up your waste. 14 For the LORD your God walks through your camp to protect you and hand your enemies over to you. Your camp must be holy, so that He does not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you.

Protecting Runaway Slaves

15 Do not hand over to his master a slave who has escaped to you. 16 He may live among you wherever he chooses, in any of your towns he likes. Do not mistreat him.

Other Laws for Israel

17 No Israelite man or woman may be a temple prostitute. 18 You must not bring a prostitute's earnings or the price of a dog into the house of the LORD your God to pay any vow, because both are disgusting to the LORD your God.

19 Do not charge interest to your brother on money, food, or anything else that can earn interest. 20 You may charge interest to a foreigner, but not to your brother, so that the LORD your God may bless you in everything you do in the land you are entering to possess.

21 If you make a vow to the LORD your God, do not delay in keeping it, because He will certainly require it from you, and it will be counted as sin against you. 22 But if you do not make a vow, you will not be guilty of sin. 23 Be careful to do what you have promised, because you freely vowed to the LORD your God with your own mouth.

24 When you enter your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat as many grapes as you want, but you must not put any in your container. 25 When you enter your neighbor's grainfield, you may pluck heads of grain with your hand, but you must not use a sickle on your neighbor's standing grain.
