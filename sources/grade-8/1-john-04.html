Testing the Spirits

1 Dear friends, do not believe every spirit. Instead, test the spirits to see if they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. 2 This is how you can know the Spirit of God: Every spirit that says Jesus Christ came in the flesh is from God. 3 But every spirit that does not agree that Jesus Christ came in the flesh is not from God. That is the spirit of the antichrist. You have heard it is coming, and it is already in the world.

4 You are from God, dear children. You have won the victory over them, because the One who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world. 5 They belong to the world, so they speak like the world, and the world listens to them. 6 We belong to God. Whoever knows God listens to us. Whoever does not belong to God does not listen to us. This is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

God Is Love

7 Dear friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 8 Anyone who does not love does not know God, because God is love. 9 God showed His love to us by sending His only Son into the world so that we could live through Him. 10 This is love: not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as the sacrifice that takes away our sins.

11 Dear friends, since God loved us that much, we should love one another too. 12 No one has ever seen God. But if we love one another, God lives in us, and His love is made complete in us. 13 We know we live in Him and He lives in us, because He has given us His Spirit. 14 We have seen and tell others that the Father sent His Son to save the world. 15 If anyone says that Jesus is the Son of God, God lives in him, and he lives in God. 16 We have known and believed the love God has for us. God is love. Whoever stays in love stays in God, and God stays in him.

Love Gives Us Confidence

17 Love is made complete among us so that we can be brave on the day of judgment. We can be brave because in this world we are like Him. 18 There is no fear in love. Perfect love throws fear out, because fear brings punishment. The person who is afraid has not been made complete in love. 19 We love because He first loved us.

20 If someone says, “I love God,” but hates his brother, he is a liar. The person who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, cannot love God, whom he has not seen. 21 We have this command from Him: Anyone who loves God must also love his brother.
