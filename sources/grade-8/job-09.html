Job Replies to His Friend

1Then Job answered: 2"I know that is true, but how can a person be right with God? 3If someone wanted to argue with Him, he could not answer even one question out of a thousand. 4He is wise in heart and strong in power. Who has ever stood against Him and won?"

God's Power Is Beyond Human Understanding

5"He moves mountains, and no one even notices. He flips them over in His anger. 6He shakes the earth from its place, and its supports tremble. 7He tells the sun not to rise, and it stays dark. He seals up the stars. 8He alone stretches out the sky and walks on the waves of the sea. 9He made the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the hidden places in the south. 10He does great things we cannot search out and wonders too many to count."

Job Cannot Argue His Case Before God

11"Look, He passes by me, and I do not see Him. He moves on, but I do not notice Him. 12Look, He snatches things away. Who can stop Him? Who can say to Him, 'What are You doing?' 13God does not hold back His anger. Even the proudest powers bow under Him. 14So how could I answer Him or choose my words before Him? 15Even if I were right, I could not answer Him. I would have to beg my Judge for mercy. 16If I called and He answered, I still could not believe He was listening to me. 17He crushes me with a storm and adds to my wounds for no reason. 18He will not let me catch my breath; He fills me with bitterness. 19If it is a test of strength, look, He is mighty! And if it is a matter of justice, who can bring Him to court? 20If I am right, my own mouth would condemn me. If I am blameless, it would show me as twisted. 21I am blameless, yet I do not know myself. I hate my life."

Job Feels God Treats Everyone the Same

22"It is all the same. That is why I say, 'He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.' 23When trouble suddenly brings death, He mocks the pain of the innocent. 24The earth is handed over to the wicked. He covers the faces of its judges. If it is not Him, then who is it? 25My days are faster than a runner. They fly by and see no good. 26They pass like boats made of reeds, like an eagle diving for food. 27If I say, 'I will forget my complaint and stop looking sad and be cheerful,' 28I am afraid of all my pain. I know You will not clear me. 29I am guilty, so why should I work so hard for nothing? 30Even if I wash myself with snow and clean my hands with soap, 31then You will throw me into a pit, and even my clothes will hate me. 32He is not a man like me, so I cannot answer Him or take Him to court. 33There is no one to settle things between us, to put a hand on us both. 34Let Him take His rod away from me, and let fear of Him not terrify me. 35Then I would speak without fearing Him, but that is not how it is with me."
