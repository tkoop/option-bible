Man's Short and Troubled Life

1 Man born of a woman lives only a short time and is full of trouble. 2 He blooms like a flower and then withers; he runs like a shadow and does not last. 3 You open Your eyes on him, and You bring him into judgment with Yourself. 4 Who can bring something clean from something unclean? No one.

5 If his days are set, and the number of his months is with You, You have set his limit, and he cannot pass it. 6 Look away from him so he can rest, until he finishes his day like a hired worker.

A Tree Can Renew, but a Person Dies

7 There is hope for a tree: if it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its new shoots will not stop. 8 Even if its root grows old in the ground and its stump dies in the soil, 9 at the scent of water it will bud and grow branches like a new plant.

10 But a man dies and lies still; a person breathes his last, and where is he? 11 Water dries up from the sea, and a river becomes dry and parched. 12 So a man lies down and does not rise again; he will not wake up until the heavens are gone, nor will he be roused from his sleep.

Job Asks God to Hide Him

13 If only You would hide me in Sheol and keep me hidden until Your anger passes! Set a time for me, then remember me. 14 If a man dies, will he live again? All the days of my hard service I will wait until my change comes. 15 You will call, and I will answer; You will long for the work of Your hands. 16 Then You will count my steps, but not keep watch over my sin. 17 My sin is sealed in a bag, and You cover over my guilt.

God's Power Over Human Life

18 But a mountain falls and crumbles, and a rock is moved from its place. 19 Water wears away stones; floods wash away the soil of the earth; and You destroy man's hope. 20 You overpower him forever, and he is gone; You change his appearance and send him away. 21 His sons are honored, but he does not know it; they are brought low, but he does not see it. 22 He feels only the pain in his body, and his soul mourns within him.
