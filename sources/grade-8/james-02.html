Don't Show Favoritism

1 My brothers and sisters, don't hold on to faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ while showing favoritism. 2 Suppose a man comes into your meeting wearing a gold ring and fine clothes, and a poor man in dirty clothes comes in too. 3 If you give special attention to the well-dressed man and say, "Sit here in a good seat," but say to the poor man, "You stand there," or "Sit on the floor by my feet," 4 haven't you made unfair distinctions among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?

5 Listen, my dear brothers and sisters: Hasn't God chosen poor people in the world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom He promised to those who love Him? 6 But you have insulted the poor. Aren't the rich the ones who oppress you and drag you into court? 7 Don't they speak against the good name that was given to you?

The Royal Law

8 If you really keep the royal law found in Scripture, "Love your neighbor as yourself," you are doing well. 9 But if you show favoritism, you sin. The law convicts you as a lawbreaker. 10 Whoever keeps the whole law but trips up in one part is guilty of breaking all of it. 11 He who said, "Do not commit adultery," also said, "Do not murder." If you don't commit adultery but you do murder, you have become a lawbreaker. 12 So speak and act like people who will be judged by the law that gives freedom. 13 Judgment without mercy will come to anyone who has not shown mercy. Mercy wins over judgment.

Faith Needs Action

14 What good is it, my brothers and sisters, if someone says he has faith but has no good deeds? Can that faith save him? 15 Suppose a brother or sister has no clothes and not enough food for the day. 16 If one of you says to them, "Go in peace. Stay warm and eat well," but doesn't give them what their body needs, what good is that? 17 In the same way, faith by itself, if it has no deeds, is dead.

18 But someone will say, "You have faith and I have deeds." Show me your faith without deeds, and I will show you my faith by my deeds. 19 You believe that God is one. Good for you! Even the demons believe that—and they shake with fear. 20 Do you want proof, you foolish person, that faith without deeds is useless?

Examples of Living Faith

21 Wasn't our ancestor Abraham counted right with God because of what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar? 22 You see that his faith worked together with his deeds, and his faith was made complete by his deeds. 23 Scripture was fulfilled when it said, "Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness." He was also called God's friend. 24 You see, then, that a person is counted right with God by deeds and not by faith alone. 25 In the same way, wasn't Rahab the prostitute counted right with God by deeds when she welcomed the messengers and sent them out by another road? 26 Just as the body without breath is dead, so faith without deeds is dead.
