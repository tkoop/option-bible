The LORD's Judgment on Moab

1 This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says about Moab: “How terrible for Nebo, because it is destroyed! Kiriathaim is disgraced and captured. The stronghold is shattered and shaken.”

2 Moab is no longer praised. In Heshbon they plot evil against her and say, “Come, let us cut her off from being a nation.” You too, Madmen, will be silenced; the sword will chase you.

3 A voice cries out from Horonaim: “Ruin and great destruction!”

4 Moab is shattered; her little ones cry out. 5 On the road up to Luhith, people go up weeping. On the road down to Horonaim, cries of distress are heard.

6 Run! Save yourselves! Be like a shrub in the desert.

7 Because you trusted in your works and treasures, you too will be captured. Chemosh will go into exile, along with his priests and officials.

8 The destroyer will come against every city, and no city will escape. The valley will be ruined, and the plain will be destroyed, because the LORD has spoken.

9 Give Moab wings, for she will fly away. Her cities will become empty, with no one living in them. 10 A curse on anyone who does the LORD’s work carelessly; a curse on anyone who keeps his sword from bloodshed.

Moab's Pride and Weakness

11 Moab has been at ease since he was young. He is like wine left on its dregs; he has not been poured from one jar to another, and he has not gone into exile. So his taste stays the same, and his smell does not change. 12 So look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send wanderers who will pour him out. They will empty his jars and smash his containers. 13 Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, just as Israel was ashamed when they trusted in Bethel.

14 How can you say, “We are strong warriors, ready for battle”?

15 Moab is destroyed, and its cities go up in smoke. The best of its young men go down to the slaughter, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts. 16 Moab’s disaster is near; his ruin is coming quickly.

17 Mourn for him, all who live around him, all who know his name. Say, “How the mighty rod is broken, the glorious staff!”

18 Come down from your glory and sit on dry ground, daughter who lives in Dibon, because the destroyer of Moab has come against you; he has destroyed your fortresses.

19 Stand by the road and watch, person living in Aroer. Ask the man running away and the woman escaping, “What has happened?” 20 Moab is put to shame, for it is shattered. Wail and cry out! Declare by the Arnon that Moab is destroyed.

21 Judgment has come on the plain—upon Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath, 22 upon Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim, 23 upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon, 24 upon Kerioth, Bozrah, and all the cities of Moab, far and near.

25 The horn of Moab has been cut off, and his arm is broken, declares the LORD. 26 Make him drunk, because he has raised himself against the LORD. So Moab will wallow in his own vomit and become a laughingstock.

27 Wasn’t Israel a laughingstock to you? Whenever you spoke about him, you shook your head.

28 Leave the cities and live among the rocks, people of Moab. Be like a dove that nests in the cracks of a cliff.

29 We have heard of Moab’s pride—how very proud and arrogant he is, with a proud and stubborn heart. 30 I know his pride, declares the LORD, but it is empty. His boasting does nothing.

Mourning and Final Judgment

31 So I will cry out for Moab; I will cry out for all of Moab. I will mourn for the people of Kir-heres.

32 Vine of Sibmah, I will weep for you more than for Jazer. Your branches spread past the sea; they reached the Sea of Jazer. The destroyer has fallen on your summer fruit and on your grape harvest.

33 Joy and gladness are taken away from the fertile fields and from Moab. I will stop the wine from flowing out of the winepresses. No one will stomp the grapes with shouts of joy; their shouts will not be shouts of joy. 34 From the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh and Jahaz, they raise their voices; from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah. Even the waters of Nimrim will become dry.

35 I will put an end in Moab, declares the LORD, to the one who offers sacrifices on the high places and burns incense to his gods. 36 So My heart mourns for Moab like flutes; My heart mourns for the people of Kir-heres, because the wealth he gained is gone.

37 Every head is shaved, and every beard is cut off. On every hand there are cuts, and on every waist there is sackcloth. 38 On every rooftop in Moab and in every public square there is mourning, because I have broken Moab like a jar no one wants, declares the LORD.

39 How shattered she is! How they wail! How Moab has turned away in shame! Moab has become a joke and a horror to everyone around her. 40 For this is what the LORD says: “Look, an eagle is swooping down and spreading its wings over Moab.” 41 The cities are captured, and the strongholds are seized. On that day the hearts of Moab’s warriors will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

42 Moab will be destroyed as a nation, because he has raised himself against the LORD. 43 Terror, pit, and trap wait for you, person living in Moab, declares the LORD. 44 Whoever runs from the terror will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs out of the pit will be caught in the trap. For I will bring on Moab the year of their punishment, declares the LORD.

45 Those who run away will stand tired in the shadow of Heshbon, because a fire has gone out from Heshbon and a flame from Sihon. It has burned the foreheads of Moab and the crowns of the noisy people. 46 How terrible for you, Moab! The people of Chemosh have died, because your sons have been taken captive and your daughters have been led away. 47 Yet in the last days I will restore Moab’s fortunes, declares the LORD. This is the end of the judgment on Moab.
