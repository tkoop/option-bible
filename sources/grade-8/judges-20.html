Israel Gathers at Mizpah

1 All the Israelites, from Dan to Beersheba and through the land of Gilead, came together as one man before the LORD at Mizpah. 2 The leaders of all the people and all the tribes of Israel stood in God's assembly. There were 400,000 foot soldiers who carried swords. 3 The Benjaminites heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah. The Israelites said, "Tell us how this terrible thing happened."

The Levite Tells His Story

4 The Levite, the husband of the woman who was killed, answered, "I and my concubine came to Gibeah in Benjamin to spend the night. 5 The men of Gibeah rose up against me. They surrounded the house at night and meant to kill me. They abused my concubine, and she died. 6 I took my concubine, cut her into pieces, and sent the pieces through all the land that belongs to Israel. They did a shameful and terrible thing in Israel. 7 Look, all you Israelites, give your advice and counsel here."

Benjamin Refuses to Hand Over the Guilty

8 All the people rose as one man and said, "None of us will go to his tent. No one will turn back to his house. 9 But this is what we will do to Gibeah: We will go up against it by lot. 10 We will take ten men out of every hundred from all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of every thousand, and a thousand out of every ten thousand. They will get food for the people. Then, when they come to Gibeah in Benjamin, they can punish the men for the terrible thing they did in Israel." 11 So all the men of Israel gathered against the city as one man.

12 The tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribe of Benjamin. They said, "What is this wicked thing that has happened among you? 13 Now hand over the wicked men in Gibeah. We will put them to death and remove this evil from Israel." But the Benjaminites would not listen to their brothers, the Israelites. 14 Instead, the Benjaminites gathered from their cities to Gibeah to go out and fight the Israelites.

15 That day the Benjaminites mustered 26,000 swordsmen from their cities, plus 700 chosen men from Gibeah. 16 Among all these soldiers were 700 left-handed men. Each one could sling a stone and hit a target as small as a hair without missing. 17 The Israelites, not counting Benjamin, mustered 400,000 swordsmen, all trained for war.

Israel's Battles with Benjamin

18 The Israelites went up to Bethel and asked God, "Who should go up first to fight against the Benjaminites?" The LORD said, "Judah first." 19 The Israelites got up in the morning and camped near Gibeah. 20 They went out to fight the Benjaminites and lined up for battle at Gibeah. 21 The Benjaminites came out of Gibeah and killed 22,000 Israelite soldiers that day.

22 The Israelites encouraged each other and took the same positions they had on the first day. 23 They went up and wept before the LORD until evening. They asked, "Should we go up again to fight our brothers, the Benjaminites?" The LORD said, "Go up against them." 24 So the Israelites advanced against the Benjaminites on the second day. 25 The Benjaminites came out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day and killed 18,000 more Israelite soldiers, all of them swordsmen.

26 Then all the Israelites, the whole army, went up to Bethel. They wept and sat before the LORD. They fasted until evening and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to the LORD. 27 The Israelites asked the LORD a question. In those days the ark of God's covenant was there. 28 Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, was serving before it. He asked, "Should we go up once more to fight our brothers the Benjaminites, or should we stop?" The LORD said, "Go, for tomorrow I will hand them over to you."

29 Israel set men in hiding around Gibeah. 30 On the third day the Israelites went up against the Benjaminites. They lined up against Gibeah, just as they had done before. 31 The Benjaminites came out to meet them and were drawn away from the city. They began to strike down Israelites as before, killing about thirty men on the roads. One road goes up to Bethel, and the other goes through the fields to Gibeah.

32 The Benjaminites said, "They are running from us, just like before."

But the Israelites said, "Let's pull back and draw them away from the city to the roads." 33 All the Israelites moved from their places and lined up at Baal Tamar. Israel's men in hiding rushed out from their place west of Gibeah. 34 Then 10,000 chosen men from all Israel advanced against Gibeah. The battle was fierce, and the Benjaminites did not know that disaster was close to them.

35 The LORD defeated the Benjaminites before Israel. The Israelites killed 25,100 Benjaminites that day, all swordsmen. 36 Then the Benjaminites saw that they were beaten. The Israelites gave ground to the Benjaminites because they trusted the men in hiding near Gibeah. 37 The men in hiding rushed in and spread out. They struck the whole city with the sword. 38 The Israelites and the men in hiding had agreed on a signal: a great cloud of smoke would rise from the city.

39 Then the Israelites turned in the battle. The Benjaminites had begun to strike down about thirty Israelites. They thought, "Surely they are defeated before us, just like before." 40 But the smoke signal began to rise from the city. The Benjaminites looked back and saw the whole city going up in smoke to the sky. 41 Then the Israelites turned back, and the Benjaminites were terrified because they saw that disaster had come on them.

42 So they fled from Israel toward the wilderness, but the battle caught up with them. The Israelites from the cities destroyed them in their midst. 43 They surrounded the Benjaminites, chased them, and trampled them down without rest as far as a place east of Gibeah. 44 Eighteen thousand Benjaminites fell, all brave warriors.

45 They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. The Israelites killed 5,000 of them on the roads. They pursued them to Gidom and struck down 2,000 more. 46 So 25,000 Benjaminites fell that day, all swordsmen and brave warriors. 47 But 600 men turned and fled to the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. They stayed there four months. 48 The Israelites went back to the Benjaminites and put to the sword everyone they found in the cities, including people and animals. They also set all the cities on fire.
