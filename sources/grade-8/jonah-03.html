The The LORD Sends Jonah Again

1 The word of the the LORD came to Jonah a second time. 2 The LORD said, "Get up and go to Nineveh, that great city. Proclaim to it the message I am giving you."

Jonah Warns Nineveh

3 Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, just as the the LORD had said. Nineveh was a very large city. It took three days to walk across it.

4 Jonah went into the city and traveled one day. Then he cried out, "In forty days, Nineveh will be destroyed!"

Nineveh Repents

5 The people of Nineveh believed God. They called for a fast, and everyone—from the greatest to the least—put on rough cloth.

6 When the news reached the king of Nineveh, he got up from his throne. He took off his royal robe, put on rough cloth, and sat in ashes.

7 He sent this order through Nineveh: "By the command of the king and his nobles, no person or animal may eat or drink anything. 8 Do not let the herds or flocks graze or drink water. Instead, cover both people and animals with rough cloth. Let everyone pray hard to God. Let each person turn from his evil ways and stop the violence in his hands. 9 Who knows? Maybe God will change His mind, turn from His fierce anger, and not destroy us."

God Shows Mercy

10 When God saw what they did and that they turned from their evil ways, He changed His mind about the disaster He had planned to bring on them, and He did not do it.
