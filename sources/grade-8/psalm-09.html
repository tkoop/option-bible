To the music leader, to the tune "Death of the Son." A psalm of David.

David Praises God

1 I will thank the LORD with all my heart. I will tell about all the amazing things You have done. 2 I will be glad and rejoice in You. I will sing praises to Your name, O Most High.

God Judges the Nations

3 When my enemies run away, they stumble and die before You. 4 You have defended my cause and my rights. You sit on Your throne and judge fairly. 5 You warned the nations and destroyed the wicked. You erased their names forever. 6 The enemy is finished forever. You tore down their cities, and even their memory is gone.

7 But the LORD sits on His throne forever. He set it up to bring justice. 8 He judges the world fairly and rules the nations with justice.

God Is a Safe Place

9 The LORD is a safe place for people who are treated badly, a refuge in times of trouble. 10 Those who know You trust in You, because You, O the LORD, do not abandon those who look for You.

11 Sing praises to the LORD, who rules from Zion! Tell the nations what He has done. 12 He remembers those who were murdered. He does not forget the cries of the suffering.

David Asks for Mercy and Justice

13 Show me mercy, O the LORD! See how my enemies hurt me. You pull me back from the edge of death. 14 Then I will tell of all Your praises in the gates of Zion and rejoice in Your rescue.

15 The nations sank into the pit they dug. Their own feet got caught in the net they hid. 16 The LORD has shown Himself. He has judged fairly. The wicked are trapped by what their own hands have done. Pause and think about this.

17 The wicked will go down to the grave—all the nations that forget God. 18 The needy will not be forgotten forever, and the hope of the poor will not die.

19 Rise up, O the LORD! Do not let people win. Judge the nations in Your presence. 20 Make them afraid, O the LORD! Let the nations know they are only human. Pause.
