Remember the LORD's Power

1 Love the LORD your God and keep His rules, laws, and commands every day. 2 Remember today that your children did not know and did not see the discipline of the LORD your God. They did not see His greatness, His mighty hand, or His outstretched arm. 3 They did not see His signs and the things He did in Egypt to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and to all his land. 4 They did not see what He did to Egypt's army, horses, and chariots when He made the Red Sea cover them as they chased you. The LORD destroyed them, and they are gone to this day. 5 They did not see what He did for you in the desert until you arrived here. 6 They did not see what He did to Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab son of Reuben, when the ground opened its mouth and swallowed them, their houses, their tents, and everything that followed their feet, right in front of all Israel. 7 But your eyes have seen all the great things the LORD has done.

Obey and Receive Blessings

8 So keep all the command I give you today. Then you will be strong, and you will go in and take the land you are crossing over to possess. 9 Then you will live a long time in the land the LORD promised your fathers He would give to them and their children. It is a land flowing with milk and honey. 10 The land you are entering to possess is not like Egypt, where you planted seed and watered it by foot, like a vegetable garden. 11 But the land you are crossing into is a land of hills and valleys. It drinks rain from heaven. 12 It is a land the LORD your God cares for. The eyes of the LORD your God are always on it, from the beginning of the year to the end. 13 If you carefully listen to the commands I give you today, and love the LORD your God and serve Him with all your heart and soul, 14 then I will send rain on your land at the right time, both the fall and spring rains. Then you will gather your grain, new wine, and olive oil. 15 I will give grass in your fields for your animals, and you will eat and be full. 16 Be careful, or your heart will be tricked. Then you will turn away and worship other gods and bow down to them. 17 Then the LORD's anger will burn against you. He will close the sky so there is no rain, and the ground will not give its crops. You will soon be destroyed and removed from the good land the LORD is giving you.

Keep God's Words Close

18 Put these words of Mine in your hearts and minds. Tie them as a sign on your hands, and let them be on your foreheads. 19 Teach them to your children. Talk about them when you sit at home, when you walk along the road, when you lie down, and when you get up. 20 Write them on the doorframes of your house and on your gates. 21 Then your days and your children's days will be many in the land the LORD promised your fathers. They will be as many as the days the sky is above the earth.

The LORD Will Give You the Land

22 If you carefully keep all these commands I am giving you, and love the LORD your God, walk in all His ways, and stay close to Him, 23 then the LORD will drive out all these nations ahead of you. You will take over nations bigger and stronger than you. 24 Every place where you set your foot will belong to you. Your land will stretch from the desert and Lebanon, from the Euphrates River to the western sea. 25 No one will be able to stand against you. The LORD your God will put fear and dread of you on the whole land you walk on, just as He promised you.

Choose Blessing or Curse

26 Look, today I am putting a blessing and a curse in front of you. 27 You will get the blessing if you obey the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you today. 28 You will get the curse if you do not obey the commands of the LORD your God, but turn from the way I command you today, and follow other gods you have not known. 29 When the LORD your God brings you into the land you are entering to possess, put the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal. 30 These mountains are across the Jordan, west of the road toward the sunset, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah. They are opposite Gilgal, near the oaks of Moreh. 31 You are about to cross the Jordan to take the land the LORD your God is giving you. You will possess it and live in it. 32 Be careful to follow all the rules and laws I am putting in front of you today.
