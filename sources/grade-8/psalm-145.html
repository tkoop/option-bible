A Psalm of Praise by David

1 I will lift You up, my God and King; I will bless Your name forever and ever. 2 Every day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name forever and ever.

The Greatness of the LORD

3 The LORD is great and greatly to be praised; His greatness cannot be measured. 4 One generation will praise Your works to the next, and will declare Your mighty acts. 5 I will speak of the glorious splendor of Your majesty, and of Your wonderful works. 6 People will speak of the power of Your awesome deeds, and I will declare Your greatness. 7 They will pour out the memory of Your great goodness, and will sing of Your righteousness.

8 The LORD is gracious and full of compassion, slow to anger and great in mercy. 9 The LORD is good to all, and His tender mercies are over all His works.

All Creation Praises the LORD

10 All Your works will praise You, the LORD, and Your faithful ones will bless You. 11 They will speak of the glory of Your kingdom, and talk of Your power, 12 to make known to the sons of men His mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of His kingdom. 13 Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and Your rule endures through all generations. 14 The LORD upholds all who fall, and raises up all who are bowed down.

The LORD Provides for All

15 The eyes of all look to You in hope, and You give them their food at the right time. 16 You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing. 17 The LORD is righteous in all His ways, and kind in all His works. 18 The LORD is near to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth. 19 He will do what those who fear Him desire; He will hear their cry and save them. 20 The LORD watches over all who love Him, but all the wicked He will destroy.

21 My mouth will speak the praise of the LORD, and all flesh will bless His holy name forever and ever.
