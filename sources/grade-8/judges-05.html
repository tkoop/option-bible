The Song of Deborah and Barak

1 On that day Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam sang:

2 "When the leaders in Israel take the lead, when the people willingly offer themselves, bless the the LORD!

3 "Hear, O kings! Give ear, O rulers! I will sing to the the LORD; I will sing praise to the the LORD, the God of Israel.

The LORD Goes Out Before His People

4 "The LORD, when You went out from Seir, when You marched from the land of Edom, the earth trembled, the heavens poured down rain, and the clouds poured down water.

5 "The mountains flowed before the the LORD, yes, Sinai before the the LORD, the God of Israel.

6 "In the days of Shamgar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the highways were deserted, and travelers took the back roads.

7 "Village life stopped; it ended in Israel, until I, Deborah, rose up, a mother in Israel.

8 "When they chose new gods, then war came to the gates. Not a shield or spear was seen among forty thousand in Israel.

9 "My heart goes out to the commanders of Israel, to the volunteers among the people. Bless the the LORD!

10 "You who ride white donkeys, who sit on saddle blankets, and who walk along the road, think about this!

11 "The voice of the singers at the watering places. There they tell about the righteous acts of the the LORD, the righteous deeds of His warriors in Israel. Then the people of the the LORD went down to the gates.

Deborah Calls Israel to Battle

12 "Awake, awake, Deborah! Awake, awake, sing a song! Arise, Barak, and take your captives, O son of Abinoam!

13 "Then the survivors came down to the nobles; the people of the the LORD came down to me against the mighty.

14 "From Ephraim came those rooted in Amalek; Benjamin followed you with your troops. From Machir the commanders descended, and from Zebulun those who carry the staff of command.

15 "The leaders of Issachar were with Deborah, and Issachar was with Barak, rushing into the valley at his heels. Among the clans of Reuben there was great hesitation.

16 "Why did you sit among the sheepfolds to hear the whistling of the flocks? Among the clans of Reuben there was great hesitation.

17 "Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan, and Dan, why did he linger by the ships? Asher sat still at the coast of the sea and stayed by his harbors.

18 "Zebulun was a people who risked their lives; Naphtali, too, on the heights of the field.

19 "Kings came; they fought. Then the kings of Canaan fought at Taanach by the waters of Megiddo, but they took no plunder of silver.

20 "From the heavens the stars fought; from their courses they fought against Sisera.

21 "The river Kishon swept them away, the ancient river, the river Kishon. March on, my soul, with strength!

22 "Then the hooves of horses thundered—the galloping, galloping of his stallions.

Meroz Is Cursed and Jael Is Blessed

23 "'Curse Meroz,' says the angel of the the LORD, 'curse its people bitterly, for they did not come to help the the LORD, to help the the LORD against the mighty.'

24 "Most blessed of women is Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, of tent-dwelling women most blessed.

25 "He asked for water, and she gave him milk. In a bowl fit for nobles she brought him curdled milk.

26 "She reached for a tent peg with her right hand, and for the workman's hammer with her left. She struck Sisera and crushed his skull; she shattered and pierced his temple.

27 "He sank, he fell, he lay at her feet; he sank, he fell; where he sank, there he fell dead.

28 "Sisera's mother looked through the window and cried out through the lattice: 'Why is his chariot so long in coming? Why are the hoofbeats of his chariots delayed?'

29 "The wisest of her ladies answer; indeed, she says to herself:

30 "'Are they not finding and dividing the plunder—a girl or two for each soldier, dyed garments for Sisera, embroidered garments for the neck of the looter?'

31 "So may all Your enemies perish, O the LORD! But may those who love You shine like the sun at its brightest." And the land had rest forty years.
