Mining the Earth

1 Surely there is a mine for silver, and a place where gold is refined. 2 Iron is taken from the earth, and copper is melted from stone.

3 People end the darkness. They search the deepest places for ore in the blackest dark. 4 They dig a shaft far from where people live. Travelers forget it, and the miners hang far away from others.

5 The earth, which gives food, is changed below as if by fire. 6 Its stones hold sapphires, and its dust contains gold.

7 No bird of prey knows that path; no falcon's eye has seen it. 8 Proud animals have not walked it, and no lion has crossed it.

9 People put their hand to hard rock and overturn mountains at the base. 10 They cut channels through rock, and their eyes see every precious thing. 11 They dam streams so they do not flow, and bring hidden things into the light.

Searching for Wisdom

12 But where can wisdom be found, and where does understanding live? 13 No one knows its worth, and it is not found in the land of the living.

14 The deep sea says, "It is not in me."

The ocean says, "It is not with me."

15 It cannot be bought with gold, and its price cannot be weighed in silver. 16 It cannot be valued with Ophir's gold, with precious onyx, or with sapphire. 17 Neither gold nor crystal can compare to it, and it cannot be traded for fine gold.

18 Coral and quartz are not worth mentioning. Wisdom's price is more than rubies. 19 Cush's topaz cannot compare to it, and it cannot be valued in pure gold.

20 So where does wisdom come from, and where does understanding live? 21 It is hidden from the eyes of every living thing and kept secret from the birds of the sky.

22 Destruction and Death say, "We have heard a rumor about it."

God Knows Wisdom

23 God understands its way, and He knows its place. 24 For He looks to the ends of the earth and sees everything under the heavens.

25 When He set the weight of the wind and measured the waters, 26 when He made a rule for the rain and a path for the thunder, 27 then He saw wisdom and told about it. He set it in place and searched it out.

28 And He said to people, "Look, the fear of the Lord is wisdom, and turning away from evil is understanding."
