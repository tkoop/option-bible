A Prayer for Justice and Protection

1 the LORD, hear my honest prayer; listen to my cry for justice. My lips do not lie. 2 Let my case be judged fairly by You, and let Your eyes see what is right. 3 You have tested my heart and checked on me at night. You have refined me and found nothing wrong; I have decided not to sin with my mouth. 4 As for what people do, by the words of Your lips I have stayed away from the paths of violent men. 5 My steps have stayed on Your paths, and my feet have not slipped.

Surrounded by Enemies

6 I call on You, O God, because You will answer me. Turn Your ear to me and hear my words. 7 Show Your wonderful faithful love, O Savior of those who find safety at Your right hand against those who rise up against them. 8 Guard me as something You treasure deeply; hide me in the shadow of Your wings.

9 Protect me from the wicked who attack me, from my deadly enemies who surround me. 10 They have shut their hearts to pity; their mouths speak with pride. 11 They have tracked me down and now surround me; they watch me, ready to throw me to the ground. 12 They are like a lion eager to tear its prey, like a young lion hiding in ambush.

Hope in God's Presence

13 Rise up, O the LORD; go before them and bring them down. Save my life from the wicked by Your sword. 14 the LORD, save me by Your hand from people of this world whose share is only in this life. You fill their bellies with Your treasure; their sons are satisfied, and they leave what is left to their children. 15 But as for me, I will see Your face in righteousness; when I awake, I will be satisfied with Your presence.
