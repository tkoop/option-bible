1 Then Job answered:

Job Replies to His Friends

2 "So you think you are the only wise people, and that wisdom will die with you! 3 But I have a mind like you; I'm not below you. Who doesn't know things like that?"

4 "I've become a joke to my friends, even though I prayed to God and He answered me. An innocent, honest person has become a joke. 5 People who are comfortable look down on trouble, but trouble is ready for anyone who stumbles. 6 Robbers' tents are safe, and people who anger God feel secure, because God holds them in His hand."

Learn from Creation

7 "But ask the animals, and they will teach you; or the birds in the air, and they will tell you. 8 Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; or let the fish of the sea explain things to you. 9 Which of all these doesn't know that the LORD's hand has done this? 10 The life of every living thing is in His hand, along with the breath of all people."

God's Wisdom and Power

11 "Doesn't the ear test words the way the tongue tastes food? 12 Wisdom is found among the old, and understanding comes with long life. 13 Wisdom and strength belong to God; counsel and understanding are His."

14 "What He tears down can't be rebuilt; the person He locks up can't be set free. 15 If He holds back the water, it dries up; if He sends it out, it floods the land. 16 Strength and success belong to Him; both the deceiver and the deceived are His."

God Brings Down the Proud

17 "He leads advisors away barefoot and makes judges look foolish. 18 He loosens the bonds kings put in place and ties a belt around their waists. 19 He leads priests away barefoot and overthrows those who seemed secure. 20 He takes speech from trusted people and takes away the good judgment of elders. 21 He pours contempt on nobles and weakens the strong."

22 "He reveals deep things hidden in darkness and brings dark shadows into the light. 23 He makes nations great, and He destroys them; He expands nations, and then He leads them away. 24 He takes away the understanding of earth's leaders and leaves them wandering in a land with no path. 25 They feel around in the dark with no light, and He makes them stagger like drunk people."
