The LORD Rejects an Old Saying

1 The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 2 "What do you mean by using this saying in Israel: 'Parents sin, and their children suffer for it'? 3 As surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, you will not use this saying in Israel anymore.

4 "Look, every life belongs to Me. The life of the father and the life of the son are both Mine. The person who sins will die.

Righteous and Wicked Lives

5 "Suppose a man does what is right and fair. 6 He does not eat food offered at mountain shrines. He does not look up to the idols of Israel. He does not sin with his neighbor's wife. He does not have sex with a woman during her monthly period. 7 He does not mistreat anyone. He returns what he took as a pledge. He does not steal. He gives food to the hungry. He gives clothes to people who need them. 8 He does not lend money for interest or make unfair profit. He stays away from wrong. He judges fairly between people. 9 He follows My laws and keeps My rules. He lives faithfully. That man is right with God, and he will surely live, says the Lord the LORD.

10 "But suppose that man has a violent son who murders people and does any of these things, even though his father did none of them. 11 The son eats food at mountain shrines. He sins with his neighbor's wife. 12 He mistreats the poor and needy. He steals. He does not return what he took as a pledge. He looks up to idols. He does terrible things. 13 He lends money for interest and makes unfair profit. Will he live? No, he will not live. He has done all these terrible things, so he will surely die. He is to blame for his own death.

14 "Now suppose that violent son has a son who sees all his father's sins but does not copy them. 15 He does not eat food at mountain shrines. He does not look up to idols. He does not sin with his neighbor's wife. 16 He does not mistreat anyone. He does not take things as a pledge to cheat people. He does not steal. He gives food to the hungry. He gives clothes to those who need them. 17 He does not take advantage of the poor. He does not lend money for interest or make unfair profit. He does what is right and fair. That son will not die for his father's sin. He will live. 18 But his father, because he cheated and robbed people and did evil among his people, will die for his own sin.

19 "But you ask, 'Why doesn't the son pay for his father's sin?' The answer is that the son did what was right and fair. He kept My laws and followed them, so he will live. 20 The person who sins is the one who will die. A son will not be punished for his father's sin, and a father will not be punished for his son's sin. The good person will be rewarded for his own goodness, and the wicked person will be punished for his own wickedness.

Turning from Sin and Living

21 "But if a wicked person turns away from all the sins he has done and keeps My laws and does what is right and fair, he will live. He will not die. 22 None of his past sins will be remembered. He will live because of the right things he does. 23 Do I enjoy seeing a wicked person die? says the Lord the LORD. No, I would rather see him turn from his old ways and live. 24 But if a good person turns away from doing right and starts doing wrong, doing the same terrible things a wicked person does, will he live? No. None of the good things he did will be remembered. He will die because of the wrong he has done.

25 "But you say, 'The Lord's way is not fair.' Listen, people of Israel: Is My way unfair? Isn't it your ways that are unfair? 26 When a good person turns from doing right and does wrong, he will die. He will die for the wrong he has done. 27 When a wicked person turns from his wickedness and does what is right and fair, he will save his life. 28 Because he thought about his sins and turned away from them, he will live. He will not die. 29 But you, people of Israel, say, 'The Lord's way is not fair.' Are My ways unfair, people of Israel? Isn't it your ways that are unfair?

A Call to Repent

30 "So I will judge you, people of Israel. Each of you will be judged by what he has done, says the Lord the LORD. Turn away from all your sins. Stop doing wrong so that sin will not destroy you. 31 Throw away all the sins you have done. Get yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why do you want to die, people of Israel? 32 I do not want anyone to die, says the Lord the LORD. So turn back to Me and live."
