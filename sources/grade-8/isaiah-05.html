A Song About the LORD's Vineyard

1 I will sing a song for my beloved about his vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a fertile hillside. 2 He dug it up, cleared away the stones, and planted the best vines. He built a watchtower in the middle and cut out a winepress. Then He looked for grapes, but it produced only wild grapes.

3 Now you who live in Jerusalem and you men of Judah, judge between me and my vineyard. 4 What more could I have done for my vineyard that I did not do? Why did I look for good grapes but get only wild ones?

5 Now let me tell you what I will do to my vineyard. I will take away its hedge, and it will be burned. I will break down its wall, and it will be trampled. 6 I will make it a wasteland. It will not be pruned or hoed, and briers and thorns will grow there. I will also tell the clouds not to rain on it.

7 The vineyard of the LORD of Hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are the planting He loves. He looked for justice, but He saw bloodshed. He looked for righteousness, but He heard cries of distress.

Woes Against Greed and Drunkenness

8 Woe to you who add house to house and field to field until there is no more room, so that you live alone in the land. 9 The LORD of Hosts has spoken in my ears: "Surely many large and beautiful houses will become empty ruins, with no one living in them." 10 Ten acres of vineyard will produce only one bath of wine, and a homer of seed will yield only an ephah of grain.

11 Woe to those who rise early in the morning to chase strong drink, and who stay up late at night until wine inflames them. 12 They have harps and lyres, tambourines and flutes, and wine at their parties, but they do not look at what the LORD is doing or see the work of His hands.

13 So my people go into exile because they lack knowledge. Their honored men go hungry, and their crowds are parched with thirst. 14 So the grave opens wide and stretches its mouth beyond measure. Down go their nobles, their crowds, their noise, and their revelers. 15 So people are humbled and mankind is brought low; the eyes of the proud are lowered. 16 But the LORD of Hosts is lifted up by justice, and God, the Holy One, is shown holy by righteousness. 17 Then lambs will graze in their pasture, and strangers will feed among the ruins of the rich.

Woes Against Sin and Pride

18 Woe to those who drag sin along with cords of lies and pull wrongdoing like cart ropes. 19 They say,

"Let Him hurry and do His work quickly, so we can see it. Let the plan of the Holy One of Israel come near and happen, so we can know it."

20 Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness in place of light and light in place of darkness, who call bitter sweet and sweet bitter. 21 Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes and clever in their own sight. 22 Woe to those who are heroes at drinking wine and champions at mixing strong drink. 23 They clear the guilty for a bribe and deny justice to the innocent.

The LORD's Judgment and the Coming Army

24 So, just as fire burns up dry grass, their roots will rot and their blossoms will blow away like dust. This will happen because they rejected the law of the LORD of Hosts and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel. 25 That is why the anger of the LORD burned against His people. He stretched out His hand and struck them. The mountains shook, and dead bodies lay like garbage in the streets. Even then, His anger did not turn away, and His hand was still stretched out.

26 He will raise a banner for nations far away and whistle for them from the ends of the earth. Look, they come quickly and swiftly. 27 None of them is tired or stumbles. None of them sleeps or dozes. Their belts are tight, and their sandal straps are not broken. 28 Their arrows are sharp, and all their bows are strung. Their horses' hooves seem like flint, and their wheels spin like a whirlwind. 29 Their roar is like a lion's roar. They growl like young lions. They grab their prey and carry it off, and no one can save it. 30 On that day they will roar over it like the roaring of the sea. If anyone looks at the land, there will be darkness and trouble. Even the light will be darkened by the clouds.
