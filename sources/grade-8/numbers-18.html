Duties of Priests and Levites

1 The LORD said to Aaron, “You and your sons, along with your father’s family, must carry the guilt for the holy place. You and your sons must carry the guilt for your priesthood. 2 Bring your brothers from Levi’s tribe, your father’s tribe, near to you. They will join you and help you and your sons in front of the Tent of the Testimony. 3 They must take care of your needs and the needs of the whole tent. But they must not go near the holy objects or the altar. If they do, both they and you will die. 4 They will join you and care for the Tent of Meeting, doing all the work of the tent. But no outsider may come near you. 5 You must take care of the holy place and the altar, so that God’s anger will not fall on the Israelites again. 6 Look, I have taken your brothers the Levites from among the Israelites. I give them to you as a gift. They are given to the LORD to do the work of the Tent of Meeting. 7 But you and your sons must take care of your priesthood for everything about the altar and behind the curtain. You will serve there. I am giving you the priesthood as a gift. Anyone who is not a priest who comes near must be put to death.”

Offerings Given to the Priests

8 Then the LORD said to Aaron, “Look, I have put you in charge of the offerings I set apart for Myself. I give all the holy things of the Israelites to you and your sons. This is your share, a lasting right. 9 The most holy part of the offerings kept back from the fire will be yours. From all the offerings they bring to Me as most holy—whether grain offerings, sin offerings, or guilt offerings—that part is most holy to you and your sons. 10 You must eat it in a most holy place. Every male may eat it. It is holy to you. 11 This also is yours: the special offering from their gifts, along with all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I have given them to you, your sons, and your daughters as a lasting right. Anyone in your household who is clean may eat it. 12 I give you the best of the fresh oil, the new wine, and the grain that the people give to the LORD as their first crops. 13 The first crops from everything in their land that they bring to the LORD will be yours. Anyone in your household who is clean may eat it. 14 Everything in Israel that is set apart for the LORD will be yours. 15 The firstborn of every womb, whether human or animal, that is offered to the LORD belongs to you. But you must redeem the firstborn son and the firstborn of an unclean animal. 16 You are to redeem them at one month old, at the set price of five shekels of silver, using the holy shekel, which is twenty gerahs. 17 But you must not redeem the firstborn of an ox, a sheep, or a goat. They are holy. You must sprinkle their blood on the altar and burn their fat as an offering made by fire, a pleasing smell to the LORD. 18 Their meat will be yours, like the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh. It is yours. 19 All the special offerings of holy things that the Israelites bring to the LORD, I have given to you, your sons, and your daughters as a lasting right. It is a lasting promise before the LORD for you and your children after you.”

The Levites' Inheritance

20 Then the LORD said to Aaron, “You will not receive land in their country, and you will not have a share among them. I am your share and your inheritance among the Israelites. 21 Look, I have given the Levites all the tithes in Israel as their inheritance, in return for the work they do, the work of the Tent of Meeting. 22 From now on, the Israelites must not come near the Tent of Meeting, or they will be guilty and die. 23 The Levites will do the work of the Tent of Meeting, and they will carry their own guilt. This is a lasting rule for all generations: They will not receive land among the Israelites. 24 For I have given the Levites as their inheritance the tithes that the Israelites bring to the LORD as a special offering. That is why I said they will not receive land among the Israelites.”

The Levites' Offering to the LORD

25 The LORD said to Moses, 26 “Speak to the Levites and tell them: When you receive from the Israelites the tithe I have given you as your inheritance, you must give a tenth of that tithe as an offering to the LORD. 27 Your offering will count for you as if it were grain from the threshing floor or juice from the winepress. 28 In this way you also must give an offering to the LORD from all the tithes you receive from the Israelites. From that offering, you must give the LORD’s share to Aaron the priest. 29 You must give the best part of everything you receive as the LORD’s offering, the best part of it. 30 Say to them: When you have given the best part, it will count for you as if it were produce from the threshing floor or winepress. 31 You and your households may eat the rest anywhere, because it is your pay for your work in the Tent of Meeting. 32 By giving the best part, you will not be guilty. You will not treat the holy offerings of the Israelites as unclean, and you will not die.”
