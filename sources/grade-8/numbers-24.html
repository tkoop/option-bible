Balaam Speaks His Third Blessing

1When Balaam saw that it pleased the LORD to bless Israel, he did not look for omens as he had before. Instead, he turned toward the wilderness. 2Balaam raised his eyes and saw Israel camped by their tribes. The Spirit of God came on him.

3He spoke his message and said, "The message of Balaam son of Beor, the message of the man whose eyes are open, 4the message of one who hears God's words, who sees a vision from the Almighty, falling down with eyes open:

5"How good are your tents, Jacob! How good are your homes, Israel! 6They spread out like valleys, like gardens beside a river, like aloes the LORD has planted, like cedars beside the waters. 7Water will flow from his buckets, and his children will be many. His king will be greater than Agag, and his kingdom will be lifted up. 8God brings him out of Egypt; he has strength like a wild ox. He will defeat enemy nations and crush their bones; he will pierce them with arrows. 9He crouches and lies down like a lion, like a lioness. Who will wake him? Blessed is everyone who blesses you, and cursed is everyone who curses you."

Balak's Anger

10Balak was very angry with Balaam. He clapped his hands and said to Balaam, "I called you to curse my enemies, but look, you have blessed them three times! 11Now go back to your place! I said I would honor you greatly, but look, the LORD has kept you from honor."

12Balaam said to Balak, "Did I not tell your messengers whom you sent to me, 13'If Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the LORD's command. I cannot do good or bad by my own choice. I will say only what the LORD tells me to say.' 14Now I am going back to my people. Come, and I will tell you what these people will do to your people in the days to come."

Balaam's Final Oracles

15He spoke his message and said, "The message of Balaam son of Beor, the message of the man whose eyes are open, 16the message of one who hears God's words and knows the knowledge of the Most High, who sees a vision from the Almighty, falling down with eyes open:

17"I see him, but not now. I look at him, but he is not near. A star will come from Jacob, and a scepter will rise from Israel. He will crush Moab and break down all the descendants of Sheth. 18Edom will become a possession, and Seir, his enemies, will become a possession, while Israel grows strong. 19A ruler will come from Jacob and destroy those left in the city.

20He looked at Amalek and spoke his message: "Amalek was first among the nations, but its end will be destruction.

21He looked at the Kenite and spoke his message: "Your home is secure, and your nest is set in the rock. 22But the Kenites will be destroyed, and Assyria will carry you away.

23He spoke his message: "Alas! Who can live when God does this? 24But ships will come from Kittim and trouble Assyria and Eber, and they too will be destroyed."

25Then Balaam got up and went back to his place, and Balak also went on his way.
