False Teachers Will Come

1 But long ago there were false prophets among God's people. In the same way, there will be false teachers among you. They will secretly bring in destructive teachings that pull people away. They will even deny the Master who bought them, and they will bring quick ruin on themselves. 2 Many people will follow their shameful ways. Because of them, the way of truth will be spoken against. 3 In their greed, they will use you with made-up words. Their judgment was set long ago, and their ruin is not sleeping.

God Rescues and Judges

4 For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but threw them into hell and chained them in dark gloom until judgment; 5 if He did not spare the ancient world, but saved Noah, a preacher of right living, along with seven others, when He brought a flood on a world of ungodly people; 6 if He turned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes and condemned them to ruin, making them a warning for ungodly people in the future; 7 and if He rescued Lot, a good man who was worn down by the evil lives of lawless people— 8 for that good man lived among them and was troubled day after day by the lawless things he saw and heard— 9 then the Lord knows how to rescue godly people from trials and to hold ungodly people for punishment until the day of judgment.

What False Teachers Are Like

10 This is especially true for those who follow dirty desires and look down on authority. They are bold and stubborn. They do not tremble when they speak evil of glorious beings. 11 Even angels, who are stronger and more powerful, do not accuse them with insults before the Lord. 12 But these people are like animals that cannot think. They are born to be caught and destroyed. They speak evil about things they do not understand. They will be destroyed by their own corruption.

13 They will suffer harm as pay for their wrongdoing. They think it is fun to live in luxury during the day. They are spots and stains. They enjoy tricking people while they eat with you. 14 Their eyes are full of adultery, and they never stop sinning. They lure weak souls. Their hearts are trained in greed. They are children under a curse!

15 They have left the straight path and wandered off. They followed the path of Balaam son of Beor, who loved pay for doing wrong. 16 But he was corrected for his own sin. A donkey that could not speak talked with a human voice and stopped the prophet's crazy plan. 17 These people are like wells with no water and like clouds blown by a storm. The blackest darkness is kept for them.

18 They speak proud, empty words. With sinful human desires, they lure people who have only just escaped from those living in error. 19 They promise freedom, but they are slaves to corruption. A person is a slave to whatever controls him.

Turning Back Is Dangerous

20 If people have escaped the world's filth through knowing our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and then get tangled up in it again and are overcome, their last condition is worse than their first. 21 It would have been better for them not to have known the way of right living than to know it and turn away from the holy command given to them.

22 This true saying has come true for them: “A dog returns to its own vomit,” and, “A pig that has washed goes back to rolling in the mud.”
