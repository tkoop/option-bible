Abijah Rules Judah

1 In the eighteenth year of Jeroboam's reign, Abijah became king over Judah. 2 He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother's name was Micaiah, the daughter of Uriel from Gibeah. There was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

3 Abijah went to battle with an army of four hundred thousand chosen men, all strong warriors. Jeroboam lined up against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, also strong warriors.

Abijah Warns Israel

4 Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in the hill country of Ephraim and said, "Listen to me, Jeroboam and all Israel. 5 Don't you know that the the LORD, the God of Israel, gave the kingdom of Israel to David and his sons forever by a lasting agreement? 6 Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, Solomon's servant, rebelled against his master. 7 Some worthless scoundrels gathered around him and opposed Rehoboam son of Solomon when Rehoboam was young and unsure of himself and could not stand against them.

8 "And now you plan to resist the kingdom of the the LORD, which is in the hands of David's descendants. You are a huge crowd, and you have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods. 9 Didn't you drive out the the LORD's priests, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and make priests for yourselves like the peoples of other lands? Anyone who brings a young bull and seven rams to be ordained can become a priest for gods that aren't real.

10 "But as for us, the the LORD is our God, and we have not forsaken Him. The priests who serve the the LORD are Aaron's sons, and the Levites assist in the work. 11 Every morning and evening they burn burnt offerings and fragrant incense to the the LORD. They set out the bread on the table of pure gold, and they light the lamps on the gold lampstand every evening. We are keeping the the LORD our God's requirements, but you have forsaken Him. 12 God is with us; He is our leader. His priests blow the trumpets to sound the battle cry against you. People of Israel, don't fight against the the LORD, the God of your ancestors, because you won't win."

The Battle Turns

13 Meanwhile, Jeroboam had sent soldiers to circle around behind them. So Judah found the enemy in front and the ambush behind. 14 When the men of Judah turned around, they saw they were trapped front and back. They cried out to the the LORD, and the priests blew the trumpets.

15 Then the men of Judah shouted the war cry. As they shouted, God defeated Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah. 16 The Israelites fled from Judah, and God handed them over to Judah. 17 Abijah and his army struck them with a great blow: five hundred thousand of Israel's chosen soldiers fell.

18 So Israel was humbled at that time, and the people of Judah grew strong because they relied on the the LORD, the God of their ancestors.

After the Victory

19 Abijah chased Jeroboam and captured some of his cities: Bethel with its surrounding villages, Jeshanah with its villages, and Ephron with its villages. 20 Jeroboam never regained his power during Abijah's reign. The The LORD struck him, and he died.

21 But Abijah grew stronger. He took fourteen wives and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters. 22 The rest of Abijah's deeds, his ways, and his words are written in the records of the prophet Iddo.
