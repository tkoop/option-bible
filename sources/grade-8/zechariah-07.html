The People Ask About Fasting

1 In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Kislev. 2 Now the people of Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech, along with their men, to seek the the LORD's favor.

3 They asked the priests of the house of the the LORD of Hosts and the prophets, "Should I keep mourning and fasting in the fifth month, as I have done for so many years?"

God Questions Their Fasts

4 Then the word of the the LORD of Hosts came to me:

5 "Say to all the people of the land and to the priests, 'When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months these seventy years, were you really fasting for Me? 6 And when you ate and drank, were you not eating and drinking for yourselves? 7 Are these not the words the the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets when Jerusalem and its surrounding towns were at rest and prosperous, and the Negev and the western foothills were inhabited?'"

What God Really Wants

8 And the word of the the LORD came to Zechariah:

9 "This is what the the LORD of Hosts says: 'Administer true justice, and show mercy and compassion to one another. 10 Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the foreigner or the poor. Do not plot evil against one another in your hearts.'"

The People Refused and Were Scattered

11 But they refused to listen and stubbornly turned away; they would not hear. 12 They hardened their hearts and would not listen to the law or to the words the the LORD of Hosts had sent by His Spirit through the earlier prophets. So great anger came from the the LORD of Hosts.

13 "Therefore, just as they would not listen when I called, so I will not listen when they call," says the the LORD of Hosts. 14 "I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations they had not known, and the land was left so empty and ruined behind them that no one could come or go. Thus they made the pleasant land a wasteland."
