Job's Life and Family

1 There was a man named Job who lived in the land of Uz. He was honest and good. He respected God and stayed away from evil. 2 He had seven sons and three daughters. 3 He owned seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred pairs of oxen, five hundred female donkeys, and many servants. He was the greatest man in the east. 4 His sons took turns having feasts at their homes. They invited their three sisters to eat and drink with them. 5 When the feast days were over, Job would send for his children and make them clean. Early in the morning, he would offer a burnt offering for each of them. He thought, “Maybe my children sinned and turned against God in their hearts.” Job did this all the time.

Satan Challenges Job

6 One day the sons of God came to stand before the LORD, and Satan came with them.

7 The LORD asked Satan, “Where have you come from?”

Satan answered, “I have been walking around the earth.”

8 The LORD said to Satan, “Have you noticed My servant Job? No one on earth is like him. He is honest and good. He respects God and stays away from evil.”

9 Satan replied, “Does Job respect God for no reason? 10 Haven't You protected him, his family, and everything he has? You have blessed his work, and his animals have grown all over the land. 11 But take away everything he has, and he will curse You to Your face.”

12 The LORD said to Satan, “Everything he has is in your power. But do not hurt him.” Then Satan left the the LORD's presence.

Job Loses Everything

13 One day Job's sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine at their oldest brother's house.

14 A messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys were grazing nearby. 15 Then the Sabeans attacked and took them. They killed the servants with swords. I am the only one who escaped to tell you.”

16 While he was still speaking, another messenger came and said, “Fire from God fell from the sky. It burned up the sheep and the servants. I am the only one who escaped to tell you.”

17 While he was still speaking, another messenger came and said, “The Chaldeans split into three groups and attacked the camels. They took them and killed the servants with swords. I am the only one who escaped to tell you.”

18 While he was still speaking, another messenger came and said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine at their oldest brother's house. 19 A strong wind came from the desert and struck the four corners of the house. It fell on the young people, and they died. I am the only one who escaped to tell you.”

Job Worships God

20 Then Job stood up, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped.

21 He said, “I was born with nothing, and I will die with nothing. The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away. May the name of the LORD be praised.”

22 In all this, Job did not sin or blame God.
