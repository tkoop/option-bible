Eliphaz Warns Job About the Foolish

1 Call out now—will anyone answer you? Which of the holy ones will you turn to? 2 Anger kills a foolish person, and jealousy destroys a naive person. 3 I have seen a foolish person settle down and prosper, but suddenly I cursed his home. 4 His children are far from safety. They are crushed at the city gate, and no one rescues them. 5 The hungry eat his harvest, even taking it from among the thorns. Thieves take his wealth. 6 For trouble does not come from the soil, and hardship does not grow from the ground. 7 Yet people are born for trouble, just as sparks fly upward from a fire.

Eliphaz Tells Job to Seek God

8 But I would seek God and bring my case to Him. 9 He does great things that no one can fully understand, and wonders without number. 10 He sends rain on the earth and waters the fields. 11 He lifts up the lowly and brings safety to those who mourn. 12 He stops the plans of the clever, so their hands cannot finish what they start. 13 He catches wise people in their own tricks, and the plans of crooked people are quickly undone. 14 They meet darkness in the daytime and grope around at noon as if it were night. 15 But He saves the poor from the sword, from cruel words, and from the power of the mighty. 16 So the poor have hope, and evil is silenced.

God's Discipline Brings Blessing

17 Look, blessed is the person whom God corrects. Do not despise the discipline of the Almighty. 18 For He wounds and then binds up; He crushes and then His hands heal. 19 He will rescue you from six troubles; in fact, in seven, no harm will touch you. 20 In famine He will redeem you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. 21 You will be hidden from the lash of the tongue, and you will not fear destruction when it comes. 22 You will laugh at destruction and famine, and you will not fear the wild animals. 23 For you will have peace with the stones of the field, and the wild animals will be at peace with you. 24 You will know your tent is safe, and you will check your home and find nothing wrong. 25 You will know your children are many, and your descendants are like the grass of the field. 26 You will come to the grave at a ripe old age, like a pile of grain gathered at the right time. 27 Look, we have studied this, and it is true. Listen, and know it for yourself.
