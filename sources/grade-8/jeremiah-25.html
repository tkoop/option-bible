Jeremiah Warns the People of Judah

1 The word that came to Jeremiah about all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. That was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

2 Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all who lived in Jerusalem. He said,

3 "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this day—twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me. I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened. 4 The LORD has sent all His servants the prophets to you again and again, but you have not listened or turned your ears to hear."

5 They said, "Turn now, each one of you, from your evil way and your evil deeds. Live in the land the LORD gave to you and your fathers forever and ever. 6 Do not follow other gods to serve and worship them. Do not make the LORD angry with the work of your hands, and He will not bring disaster on you."

7 "But you did not listen to Me," declares the LORD, "and you made Me angry with the work of your hands, to your own harm."

Babylon Will Rule for Seventy Years

8 "Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says: 'Because you have not obeyed My words, 9 look, I will summon all the families of the north,' declares the LORD, 'and I will bring My servant Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, against this land, against its people, and against all these nations around. I will completely destroy them and make them an object of horror and scorn, an everlasting ruin. 10 I will take away from them the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of bride and bridegroom, the sound of millstones, and the light of the lamp. 11 This whole land will become a ruined waste, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.'"

12 "But when seventy years are complete, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans, for their guilt," declares the LORD, "and I will make it an everlasting desolation. 13 I will bring on that land all the words I have spoken against it, everything written in this book that Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations. 14 For many nations and great kings will make them serve too, and I will repay them for what they have done and for the work of their hands."

The Cup of God's Wrath

15 "For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to me: 'Take this cup of the wine of wrath from My hand and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it. 16 They will drink and stagger and go mad because of the sword I am sending among them.'"

17 So I took the cup from the LORD's hand and made all the nations drink it, the ones the LORD sent me to: 18 Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, her kings and officials, to make them a ruin, a desolation, a hissing, and a curse—as they are today—

19 Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials, and all his people; 20 all the mixed tribes; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the Philistines—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the rest of Ashdod; 21 Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites; 22 all the kings of Tyre and Sidon; the kings of the coastlands across the sea; 23 Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who trim the hair at their temples; 24 all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed tribes who live in the desert; 25 all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media; 26 all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another; and all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground. After all of them, the king of Sheshach will drink it too.

27 "Then you are to tell them, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Drink, get drunk, vomit, and fall. Never rise again, because of the sword I am sending among you.'"

28 "If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink it, then you are to tell them, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts says: You must drink it! 29 Look, I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears My name. Will you then go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am summoning a sword against all the people who live on the earth,' declares the LORD of Hosts."

The LORD Judges the Nations

30 "So you are to prophesy all these words against them and say to them: 'The LORD will roar from on high; He will raise His voice from His holy dwelling. He will roar loudly against His shepherds. He will shout like those who tread grapes, against all who live on the earth. 31 The noise will reach the ends of the earth, for the LORD has a case against the nations. He will judge all people. He will put the wicked to the sword,' declares the LORD."

32 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: 'Look, disaster is going out from nation to nation, and a great storm is being stirred up from the farthest ends of the earth. 33 Those killed by the LORD on that day will be spread from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried. They will be like dung lying on the ground.'"

34 "Wail, you shepherds, and cry out; roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock. For the days of your slaughter have come; you will fall and be shattered like fine pottery. 35 Flight will be lost to the shepherds, and escape to the leaders of the flock. 36 Hear the cries of the shepherds and the wails of the leaders of the flock, for the LORD is destroying their pasture. 37 The peaceful meadows will be silent because of the fierce anger of the LORD. 38 Like a lion He has left His thicket, for their land has become a desolation because of the sword of the oppressor and because of His fierce anger."
