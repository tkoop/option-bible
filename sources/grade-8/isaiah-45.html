The LORD Calls Cyrus to Free Israel

1 This is what the LORD says to Cyrus, His anointed one. I have taken hold of your right hand to bring nations under your control and to strip kings of their power. I will open doors for you so that gates will not be shut. 2 I will go before you and level the mountains. I will break the bronze doors and cut the iron bars.

3 I will give you treasures hidden in darkness and riches stored in secret places. Then you will know that I am the LORD, the God of Israel, who calls you by name. 4 For the sake of Jacob My servant and Israel My chosen one, I called you by name. I gave you a title, even though you do not know Me.

The LORD Alone Is God

5 I am the LORD, and there is no other. Besides Me there is no God. I will strengthen you, even though you do not know Me. 6 I do this so that people from the east and from the west may know that there is no one besides Me. I am the LORD, and there is no other. 7 I form light and create darkness. I bring peace and create disaster. I, the LORD, do all these things.

8 Let the heavens rain down from above, and let the clouds pour down righteousness. Let the earth open and bring forth salvation, and let righteousness grow with it. I, the LORD, have created it.

9 Woe to those who argue with their Maker. They are like a clay pot among many pots. Does the clay say to the potter, "What are you making?" Does your work say, "He has no hands"? 10 Woe to those who say to their father, "What have you fathered?" or to their mother, "What have you brought into the world?"

The LORD Is the Maker of All

11 This is what the LORD says, the Holy One of Israel and its Maker: "Will you question Me about My children, or give Me orders about the work of My hands? 12 I made the earth and created mankind on it. My hands stretched out the heavens, and I commanded all the stars. 13 I have raised him up in righteousness, and I will make all his paths straight. He will rebuild My city and set My exiles free, not for money or reward," says the LORD of Hosts.

Salvation for Israel and the Nations

14 This is what the LORD says: "The wealth of Egypt and the trade of Cush, along with the Sabeans—tall men—will come over to you and will be yours. They will follow behind you and bow down to you in chains. They will pray to you and say, 'Surely God is with you, and there is no other; there is no other God.'" 15 Truly You are a God who hides Himself, O God of Israel, the Savior. 16 All of them will be put to shame and disgraced. The makers of idols will go away together in shame. 17 But Israel will be saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation. You will never be put to shame or disgraced, forever and ever.

18 For this is what the LORD says, the Creator of the heavens—He is God. He formed the earth and made it; He set it in place. He did not create it to be empty; He formed it to be lived in. "I am the LORD, and there is no other. 19 I did not speak in secret or in a dark place. I did not say to the descendants of Jacob, 'Seek Me in vain.' I, the LORD, speak the truth and declare what is right.

"20 Come, gather together, and draw near, you survivors of the nations. Those who carry wooden idols and pray to a god that cannot save know nothing. 21 Speak up and present your case; yes, let them take counsel together. Who foretold this long ago? Who declared it from ancient times? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no other God but Me, a righteous God and Savior; there is none but Me.

"22 Turn to Me and be saved, all the ends of the earth, for I am God, and there is no other. 23 By Myself I have sworn; truth has gone out from My mouth, a word that will not be taken back: Every knee will bow before Me, every tongue will swear allegiance.

"24 They will say of Me, 'In the LORD alone are righteousness and strength.' All who rage against Him will come to Him and be put to shame. 25 In the LORD all descendants of Israel will be justified and will glory."
