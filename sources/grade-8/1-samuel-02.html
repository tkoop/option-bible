Hannah's Prayer

1 Hannah prayed, “My heart rejoices in the LORD; The LORD has made me strong. My mouth speaks boldly against my enemies, because I rejoice in Your salvation. 2 There is no one holy like the LORD; there is no one besides You. There is no Rock like our God. 3 Stop speaking so proudly; do not let arrogance come from your mouths. The LORD is a God who knows, and He weighs what people do. 4 The bows of the warriors are broken, but those who stumbled are armed with strength. 5 Those who were full now hire themselves out for bread, but those who were hungry are hungry no more. The woman who could not have children now has seven, but the woman with many sons wastes away.”

“6 The LORD brings death and gives life; He brings people down to the grave and raises them up. 7 The LORD makes people poor and makes them rich; He brings them low and lifts them up. 8 He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap; He seats them with princes and gives them a seat of honor. The foundations of the earth belong to the LORD, and He has set the world on them. 9 He guards the feet of His faithful servants, but the wicked are silenced in the darkness. No one wins by strength alone. 10 Those who fight against the LORD will be broken. The Most High thunders from heaven; the LORD will judge the ends of the earth. He will give strength to His king and raise up the power of His Anointed.”

Samuel Serves the LORD

11 Then Elkanah went home to Ramah, but the boy stayed to serve the LORD under Eli the priest.

Eli's Wicked Sons

12 Eli's sons were worthless men who did not know the LORD.

13 This was the priests' custom with the people: Whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come with a three-pronged fork in his hand while the meat was boiling. 14 He would stick it into the pot, kettle, cauldron, or pan, and the priest would take whatever the fork brought up. They did this to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

15 Even before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give the priest meat to roast. He will not take boiled meat from you, only raw meat."

16 If the man said that the fat should be burned first and then the priest could take whatever he wanted, the servant would answer, "No, give it now. If not, I will take it by force." 17 So the young men's sin was very great before the LORD, because they treated the LORD's offering with contempt.

18 Samuel served the LORD as a boy. He wore a linen ephod. 19 Each year his mother made him a little robe and brought it when she went with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

20 Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, "May the LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she gave to the LORD." Then they would go home.

21 The LORD visited Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. The boy Samuel grew up with the LORD.

22 Eli was very old. He heard everything his sons were doing to all Israel, and that they were sleeping with the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

23 So he said to them, "Why do you do these things? I hear from all the people about your evil deeds. 24 No, my sons; the report I hear is not good. You are making the LORD's people sin. 25 If one person sins against another, God may mediate for them. But if a person sins against the LORD, who can pray for them?" Yet they would not listen to their father, because the LORD wanted to put them to death.

26 The boy Samuel kept growing. He was in favor with the LORD and with people.

A Man of God Warns Eli

27 A man of God came to Eli and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'When your ancestors were in Egypt under Pharaoh, did I not reveal Myself to your father's family? 28 I chose them out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priests, to go up to My altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod before Me. I also gave your father's family all the food offerings from the Israelites. 29 Why do you scorn My sacrifice and offering that I commanded for My dwelling? Why do you honor your sons more than Me by fattening yourselves with the best of every offering from My people Israel?'"

30 "Therefore the LORD, the God of Israel, declares: 'I promised that your family and your father's family would serve Me forever.' But now the LORD declares: 'Far be it from Me! Those who honor Me I will honor, but those who despise Me will be treated with contempt.' 31 The time is coming when I will take away your strength and your father's family's strength, so that no one in your family will live to old age. 32 You will see trouble in My dwelling. Israel will prosper, but no one in your family will live to old age."

33 "Any of your descendants who are not cut off from My altar will bring sorrow and grief to you, and all the rest of your family will die young. 34 This will be a sign to you: Both of your sons, Hophni and Phinehas, will die on the same day. 35 I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest who will do what is in My heart and mind. I will build a lasting house for him, and he will walk before My Anointed One always. 36 Then everyone left in your family will come and bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, saying, 'Please give me a priest's job so I can have something to eat.'"
