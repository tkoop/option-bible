The Time of the End

1 At that time Michael, the great prince who stands up for your people, will rise up. There will be a time of trouble like none since nations began until then. At that time your people—everyone found written in the book—will be rescued.

2 Many who sleep in the dust of the earth will wake up. Some will rise to live forever, and others will rise to shame and lasting contempt. 3 Those who are wise will shine like the bright sky, and those who lead many to do right will shine like stars forever and ever.

4 But you, Daniel, close up these words and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will travel everywhere, and knowledge will grow.

A Vision by the River

5 Then I, Daniel, looked and saw two other men standing there. One stood on this bank of the river, and the other stood on the other bank.

6 One of them said to the man clothed in linen, who stood above the water of the river, "How long will it be until these amazing things are finished?"

7 The man clothed in linen, who stood above the water, raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven. He swore by Him who lives forever, "It will be three and a half years. When the power of the holy people has been completely broken, all these things will end."

8 I heard this, but I did not understand. So I asked, "My lord, what will happen at the end?"

9 He said, "Go, Daniel. These words are closed up and sealed until the time of the end."

10 Many will be made clean, made white, and refined. The wicked will keep doing wrong, and none of the wicked will understand. But those who are wise will understand.

The Final Days

11 From the time the daily sacrifice is stopped and the terrible thing that causes ruin is set up, there will be 1,290 days. 12 Blessed is the one who waits and reaches 1,335 days.

13 But you, go to the end. You will rest, and then you will rise to receive your share at the end of the days.
