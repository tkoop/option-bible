Athaliah Tries to Destroy the Royal Family

1 When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, she got up and killed all the royal family. 2 But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash, the son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being killed. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom, so they hid him from Athaliah, and he was not killed. 3 He stayed hidden with her in the house of the the LORD for six years while Athaliah ruled the land.

Jehoiada Plans to Crown Joash

4 In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent for the captains of the Carites and the guards. He brought them into the house of the the LORD and made a covenant with them. He made them swear an oath, and he showed them the king's son. 5 He commanded them, "This is what you must do: A third of you who come on duty on the Sabbath will guard the king's house. 6 A third will be at the gate of Sur, and a third at the gate behind the guards. You will guard the temple. 7 The other two groups, who are off duty on the Sabbath, will guard the house of the the LORD around the king. 8 You must surround the king on every side, each man with his weapon in his hand. Anyone who comes inside your lines must be put to death. Stay with the king wherever he goes out or comes in."

Joash Is Crowned King

9 The captains did everything Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each man brought his own men, both those coming on duty on the Sabbath and those going off duty, and they came to Jehoiada the priest. 10 The priest gave the captains the spears and shields that had belonged to King David and were in the house of the the LORD. 11 The guards stood, each with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side, around the altar and the temple, to protect the king. 12 Then Jehoiada brought out the king's son, put the crown on his head, gave him the covenant scroll, and made him king. They anointed him, clapped their hands, and shouted, "Long live the king!"

13 When Athaliah heard the noise of the guards and the people, she went to the people at the house of the the LORD. 14 She looked and saw the king standing by the pillar, as was the custom, with the captains and trumpeters beside the king. All the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and shouted, "Treason! Treason!" 15 Jehoiada the priest ordered the captains in charge of the army, "Take her out between the ranks and kill anyone who follows her with the sword." For the priest had said, "She must not be killed in the house of the the LORD." 16 So they seized her, and she went by way of the horses' entrance to the king's palace, and there she was put to death.

The Covenant Is Renewed

17 Then Jehoiada made a covenant between the the LORD, the king, and the people, that they would be the the LORD's people. He also made a covenant between the king and the people. 18 Then all the people of the land went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and idols completely, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. Then the priest posted guards over the house of the the LORD. 19 He took the captains, the Carites, the guards, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the the LORD. They went through the gate of the guards to the king's palace, and he sat on the throne of the kings. 20 All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. They had killed Athaliah with the sword at the king's palace. 21 Joash was seven years old when he became king.
