God Tells Noah to Enter the Ark

1 The LORD said to Noah, “Come into the ark, you and all your family, because I have seen that you are right with Me in this generation. 2 Take with you seven of every clean animal, a male and its mate, and two of every unclean animal, a male and its mate. 3 Also take seven of every bird, males and females, so life can continue on the earth. 4 In seven days I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights. I will wipe out every living thing I have made.”

5 Noah did everything the LORD commanded him. 6 Noah was six hundred years old when the floodwaters came on the earth. 7 Noah, his sons, his wife, and his sons’ wives went into the ark to escape the floodwaters.

8 Clean animals and unclean animals, birds, and everything that crawls on the ground 9 came to Noah in pairs, male and female, just as God had commanded Noah. 10 After seven days, the floodwaters came on the earth.

The Flood Begins

11 In Noah’s six hundredth year, in the second month, on the seventeenth day, the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of heaven opened. 12 Rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights.

13 On that very day, Noah and his sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, Noah’s wife, and the three wives of his sons went into the ark with them. 14 Every kind of wild animal, livestock, crawling creature, and bird—each after its kind—went with them. 15 They came to Noah in pairs, two by two, from all living things that had breath. 16 The males and females of all flesh entered, as God had commanded Noah. Then the LORD shut him in.

The Waters Cover the Earth

17 The flood lasted forty days on the earth. The waters rose and lifted the ark, and it floated high above the ground. 18 The waters grew stronger and rose greatly on the earth, and the ark floated on the surface of the waters. 19 The waters rose even more and covered all the high mountains under the whole sky. 20 The waters rose fifteen cubits above the mountains, and the mountains were covered.

21 Every living thing that moved on the earth died—birds, livestock, wild animals, every creature that swarms on the earth, and all people. 22 Everything on dry land that had the breath of life in its nostrils died. 23 God wiped out every living thing on the ground—people, livestock, crawling creatures, and birds. They were wiped out from the earth. Only Noah and those with him in the ark were left. 24 The waters stayed strong on the earth for one hundred fifty days.
