A Song of Praise to the LORD

1 Sing for joy in the LORD, you righteous people. Praise looks good on those who are upright. 2 Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre. Sing praises to Him with the ten-stringed harp. 3 Sing a new song to Him. Play well with a joyful shout.

4 For the word of the LORD is upright, and all His work is done in faithfulness. 5 He loves righteousness and justice. The earth is full of the faithful love of the LORD.

6 By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all their stars by the breath of His mouth. 7 He gathers the waters of the sea like a heap. He stores the deep waters in warehouses. 8 Let all the earth fear the LORD. Let all who live in the world stand in awe of Him. 9 For He spoke, and it happened. He gave the command, and it stood firm.

10 The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations. He stops the schemes of the peoples. 11 The plans of the LORD stand forever. His heart's thoughts last from generation to generation. 12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people He chose as His own inheritance.

13 The LORD looks down from heaven. He sees all people. 14 From where He lives, He watches all who live on the earth. 15 He forms the hearts of all. He understands all their deeds.

16 No king is saved by a large army. No warrior is rescued by great strength. 17 The horse is a false hope for rescue. Even with its great strength, it cannot save.

18 The eyes of the LORD are on those who fear Him, on those who hope in His faithful love. 19 He saves their lives from death and keeps them alive in famine.

20 We wait for the LORD. He is our help and our shield. 21 For our hearts rejoice in Him, because we trust in His holy name. 22 May Your faithful love be upon us, O the LORD, as we wait for You.
