Jesus Rides into Jerusalem

1 As Jesus and His disciples came near Jerusalem and reached Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples ahead. 2 He said, "Go into the village ahead of you. You will find a donkey tied there, with her colt beside her. Untie them and bring them to Me. 3 If anyone asks you why, say, 'The Lord needs them,' and he will send them right away."

4 This happened to fulfill what the prophet had said: 5 "Say to the Daughter of Zion, 'Look, your King is coming to you. He is gentle and rides on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.'"

6 The disciples went and did exactly what Jesus told them. 7 They brought the donkey and the colt, spread their coats over them, and Jesus sat on them.

8 A large crowd spread their own coats on the road. Others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.

9 The crowds walking ahead of Him and following behind shouted, "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!"

10 When Jesus entered Jerusalem, the whole city was stirred up and asked, "Who is this?"

11 The crowds answered, "This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee."

Jesus Clears the Temple

12 Jesus went into the temple and drove out everyone who was buying and selling there. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves.

13 He said to them, "It is written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer,' but you are making it a den of robbers."

14 The blind and the lame came to Him in the temple, and He healed them.

15 The chief priests and teachers of the law were angry when they saw the miracles He did and heard the children shouting in the temple, "Hosanna to the Son of David!"

16 They asked Him, "Do you hear what these children are saying?"

"Yes," He answered. "Haven't you ever read, 'From the mouths of little children and babies You have prepared praise'?"

17 Then Jesus left them and went out of the city to Bethany, where He stayed the night.

The Fig Tree and Faith

18 Early in the morning, as Jesus was heading back to the city, He was hungry.

19 He saw a fig tree by the road and went up to it, but He found nothing on it except leaves. He said to it, "May no fruit ever grow on you again!" Immediately the fig tree dried up.

20 When the disciples saw this, they were amazed and asked, "How did the fig tree dry up so fast?"

21 Jesus answered, "Truly I tell you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree. You can even say to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,' and it will happen. 22 And whatever you ask for in prayer, if you believe, you will receive."

Questions and Parables in the Temple

23 When Jesus returned to the temple and was teaching, the chief priests and the elders of the people came to Him and asked, "By what authority are You doing these things? And who gave You this authority?"

24 Jesus answered, "I will ask you one question too. If you answer Me, I will tell you by what authority I do these things. 25 Was John's baptism from heaven or from people?"

They talked it over among themselves and said, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will ask, 'Then why didn't you believe him?' 26 But if we say, 'From people,' we are afraid of the crowd, because everyone thinks John was a prophet."

27 So they answered Jesus, "We don't know."

He said, "Then I won't tell you by what authority I do these things either."

28 "What do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first and said, 'Son, go work in the vineyard today.' 29 The son answered, 'I won't go,' but later he changed his mind and went. 30 Then the father went to the second son and said the same thing. He answered, 'I will, sir,' but he did not go."

31 "Which of the two did what his father wanted?"

They said, "The first."

"Truly I tell you, tax collectors and prostitutes are going into the kingdom of God ahead of you. 32 For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, but tax collectors and prostitutes did believe him. Even after you saw this, you did not change your minds and believe him."

33 "Listen to another parable. A man owned some land. He planted a vineyard, put a wall around it, dug a winepress, and built a watchtower. Then he rented it to farmers and went on a trip. 34 When harvest time came, he sent his servants to the farmers to collect his share of the fruit. 35 But the farmers grabbed his servants. They beat one, killed another, and stoned a third. 36 So he sent more servants than before, but the farmers treated them the same way. 37 Finally, he sent his son, thinking, 'They will respect my son.' 38 But when the farmers saw the son, they said to each other, 'This is the heir. Let's kill him and take his inheritance.' 39 They grabbed him, threw him out of the vineyard, and killed him."

40 "When the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?"

41 They said, "He will bring those evil men to a terrible end and rent the vineyard to other farmers who will give him his fruit at harvest time."

42 Jesus said, "Haven't you ever read in the Scriptures, 'The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. The Lord has done this, and it is amazing in our eyes'? 43 So I tell you that God's kingdom will be taken away from you and given to a people who will produce its fruit. 44 Anyone who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, and anyone it falls on will be crushed."

45 When the chief priests and the Pharisees heard His parables, they knew He was talking about them. 46 They wanted to arrest Him, but they were afraid of the crowds, because the people thought He was a prophet.
