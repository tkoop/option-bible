The Useless Vine Wood

1 The word of The LORD came to me, saying, 2 “Son of man, what is vine wood compared with all other wood? What is a vine branch among the trees of the forest? 3 Can wood be taken from it to make something useful? Can a peg be taken from it to hang things on? 4 See, it is given to the fire for fuel. The fire burns both ends, and the middle is charred. Can it be used for anything then? 5 Even when it was whole, it could not be used for work. How much less after fire has burned it and charred it!”

Judgment on Jerusalem

6 “Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Like vine wood among the trees of the forest, which I gave to the fire for fuel, so I have given the people of Jerusalem. 7 I will set My face against them. They have come out of the fire, but the fire will consume them. Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I set My face against them. 8 I will make the land desolate because they have been unfaithful,” declares the Lord the LORD.
