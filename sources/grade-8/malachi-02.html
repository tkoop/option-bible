A Warning to the Priests

1 And now this command is for you priests. 2 "If you do not listen, and if you do not set your heart on honoring My name," says the LORD of Hosts, "I will send a curse on you, and I will curse your blessings. Yes, I have already cursed them, because you do not take it to heart." 3 "Look, I am going to rebuke your descendants. I will smear dung on your faces—the dung from your festival offerings—and you will be carried away with it."

4 "Then you will know that I sent this command to you, so that My covenant with Levi can continue," says the LORD of Hosts. 5 "My covenant with him was life and peace. I gave them to him. It called for respect, and he respected Me and stood in awe of My name." 6 True teaching was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. He walked with Me in peace and honesty, and he turned many people away from sin.

7 A priest's lips should guard knowledge, and people should seek teaching from his mouth, because he is the messenger of the LORD of Hosts. 8 "But you have turned from the way. You have caused many to stumble in your teaching. You have broken the covenant of Levi," says the LORD of Hosts. 9 "So I have made you hated and low before all the people, because you do not keep My ways. You also play favorites when you teach the law."

Breaking Faith

10 Don't we all have one Father? Didn't one God create us? Then why do we turn against each other and break the covenant our fathers made? 11 Judah has been unfaithful. A terrible thing has been done in Israel and Jerusalem. Judah has made unclean the holy place the LORD loves. He has married the daughter of a foreign god. 12 May the LORD remove from Jacob's tents anyone who does this, no matter who he is, even if he brings an offering to the LORD of Hosts.

13 Here is another thing you do: You cover the LORD's altar with tears, weeping, and groaning, because He no longer looks at your offering or accepts it with favor from your hand. 14 You ask, "Why?" Because the LORD has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth. You have been unfaithful to her, even though she is your companion and the wife of your covenant.

15 Didn't God make them one, with a share of His Spirit? And what is He looking for? Godly children. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not betray the wife of your youth.

16 "For I hate divorce," says the LORD, the God of Israel. "I also hate when a man harms the wife he should protect," says the LORD of Hosts. "So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be unfaithful."

Wearing Out the LORD

17 You have worn out the LORD with your words. Yet you ask, "How have we worn Him out?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the LORD's eyes, and He is pleased with them," or, "Where is the God of justice?"
