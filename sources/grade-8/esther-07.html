The Second Banquet

1 So the king and Haman went to eat with Queen Esther. 2 On the second day of the wine feast, the king asked Queen Esther again, "What is your petition, Queen Esther? It will be given to you. What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be done."

Esther's Request

3 Queen Esther answered, "If I have found favor with you, O king, and if it pleases the king, grant me my life; that is my petition. And spare my people; that is my request. 4 For I and my people have been sold to be destroyed, killed, and wiped out. If we had only been sold as male and female slaves, I would have kept quiet, because no such trouble would justify disturbing the king."

Haman Is Exposed

5 King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther, "Who is he, and where is he—the man who has dared to do this?" 6 Esther said, "An enemy and foe is this wicked Haman!" Then Haman was terrified before the king and queen.

Haman Begs for Mercy

7 The king rose in his anger from the wine feast and went into the palace garden. Haman stayed to beg Queen Esther for his life, because he saw that the king meant to harm him. 8 When the king returned from the palace garden to the banquet hall, Haman had fallen on the couch where Esther was. The king said, "Will he even attack the queen while I am in the house?" As soon as the words left the king's mouth, they covered Haman's face.

Haman Is Hanged

9 Then Harbona, one of the eunuchs serving the king, said, "Look! The gallows fifty cubits high stands by Haman's house. He had it built for Mordecai, who spoke up to help the king." The king said, "Hang him on it!" 10 So they hanged Haman on the gallows he had prepared for Mordecai. Then the king's anger died down.
