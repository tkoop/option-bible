The LORD Calls Ezekiel

1 He said to me, "Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak with you." 2 As He spoke to me, the Spirit came into me and set me on my feet, and I heard Him speaking to me.

A Message for Rebellious Israel

3 He said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the Israelites, to a rebellious nation that has rebelled against Me. They and their fathers have sinned against Me to this very day. 4 The people I am sending you to are stubborn and hard-hearted children. Say to them, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says.' 5 Whether they listen or refuse to listen—for they are a rebellious people—they will know that a prophet has been among them.

Do Not Be Afraid

"6 And you, Son of man, do not be afraid of them or their words. Do not be afraid, though hostile people surround you like briers and thorns, and you live among dangerous enemies like scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words or dismayed by their looks, for they are a rebellious people. 7 Speak My words to them, whether they listen or refuse to listen, for they are rebellious. 8 And you, Son of man, listen to what I tell you. Do not rebel like those rebellious people. Open your mouth and eat what I give you."

The Scroll of Warnings

9 Then I looked and saw a hand stretched out toward me. In it was a scroll. 10 He unrolled it before me, and it was written on both sides. On it were written words of mourning, grief, and woe.
