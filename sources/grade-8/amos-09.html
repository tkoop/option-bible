The Lord's Judgment on Israel

1 I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and He said, "Strike the tops of the pillars so the thresholds shake. Bring them down on the heads of all the people. Those who are left I will kill with the sword. Not one will escape; no fugitive will get away. 2 If they dig down to the grave, My hand will take them from there. If they climb up to heaven, I will bring them down from there. 3 If they hide on top of Mount Carmel, I will search and take them from there. If they hide from My eyes at the bottom of the sea, I will command the serpent to bite them there. 4 If they are led away captive by their enemies, I will command the sword to kill them there. I will set My eyes on them for harm, not for good."

5 The Lord the LORD of Hosts touches the earth, and it melts. All who live in it mourn. It rises like the Nile and sinks like the river of Egypt. 6 He builds His upper rooms in heaven and sets their foundation on the earth. He calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out over the earth. The LORD is His name.

Israel Is No Better Than Other Nations

7 "Are you not like the Cushites to Me, O people of Israel?" declares the LORD. "Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Arameans from Kir?" 8 "Behold, the eyes of the Lord the LORD are on the sinful kingdom. I will destroy it from the face of the earth. Yet I will not completely destroy the house of Jacob," declares the LORD.

Israel Will Be Sifted Among the Nations

9 "For behold, I am commanding, and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations, as grain is shaken in a sieve, but not a single pebble will fall to the ground. 10 All the sinners of My people will die by the sword—those who say, 'Disaster will not catch up with us or happen to us.'"

The Restoration of David's Fallen Tent

11 "In that day I will raise up David's fallen tent. I will repair its broken walls, rebuild its ruins, and make it as it was in days of old. 12 I do this so they may possess what is left of Edom and all the nations that are called by My name," declares the LORD.

A Future of Plenty

13 "Behold, the days are coming," declares the LORD, "when the plowman will catch up to the reaper, and the grape treader will catch up to the seed sower. The mountains will drip with sweet wine, and all the hills will flow with it. 14 I will restore My people Israel. They will rebuild the ruined cities and live in them. They will plant vineyards and drink their wine. They will make gardens and eat their fruit. 15 I will plant them in their own land, and they will never again be uprooted from the land I have given them," says the LORD your God.
