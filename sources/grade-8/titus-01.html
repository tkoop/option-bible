Greetings from Paul

1Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes for the faith of God’s chosen people and their knowledge of the truth that leads to godliness. 2He writes in the hope of eternal life, which God, who never lies, promised before time began. 3At the right time, He made His word known through the preaching He trusted to me, by the command of God our Savior. 4To Titus, my true son in our shared faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

Choosing Church Leaders

5I left you in Crete so you could put in order what still needed work and appoint elders in every town, as I told you. 6An elder must be without blame. He must have one wife, and his children must be believers. They must not be accused of wild living or of refusing to obey. 7Because an overseer manages God’s work, he must be without blame. He must not be proud, quick to anger, drunk, violent, or eager for dishonest gain. 8Instead, he must welcome strangers, love what is good, be self-controlled, right, holy, and disciplined. 9He must hold firmly to the trustworthy message, as it was taught, so he can encourage others with sound teaching and correct those who argue against it.

False Teachers in Crete

10There are many rebellious people who say empty things and fool others. This is especially true of those who insist on circumcision. 11They must be stopped from talking, because they upset whole families by teaching what they should not teach, just to make dishonest money.

12One of their own prophets even said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, and lazy gluttons.”

13This statement is true. So rebuke them sharply, so they may be sound in their faith. 14They should not pay attention to Jewish myths or to human commands from people who turn away from the truth. 15To pure people, everything is pure. But to corrupt and unbelieving people, nothing is pure. Even their minds and consciences are stained. 16They claim to know God, but they deny Him by what they do. They are detestable, disobedient, and useless for any good work.
