Jehoiada Makes a Covenant

1 In the seventh year, Jehoiada grew strong and made a covenant with the commanders of hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri. 2 They traveled through Judah and gathered the Levites from all its cities, along with the heads of Israel's families, and they came to Jerusalem. 3 The whole group made a covenant with the king in the house of God. Jehoiada said to them, "Look, the king's son will rule, just as the LORD promised about David's sons. 4 This is what you must do: One third of you priests and Levites who come on duty on the Sabbath are to guard the gates. 5 One third are to guard the royal palace, and one third are to guard the Foundation Gate. All the people are to stay in the courtyards of the house of the the LORD. 6 No one may enter the house of the the LORD except the priests and Levites on duty. They may enter because they are holy, but all the people must keep the the LORD's rules. 7 The Levites must surround the king with weapons in their hands. Anyone who enters the temple must be put to death. Stay close to the king wherever he goes."

The People Follow the Plan

8 The Levites and all Judah did everything Jehoiada the priest ordered. Each man brought his own group, both those coming on duty on the Sabbath and those going off duty, because Jehoiada had not dismissed any of the divisions. 9 Then Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears and the large and small shields that had belonged to King David and were kept in the house of God. 10 He placed all the people, each with a weapon in hand, from the south side of the temple to the north side, around the altar and the temple. 11 Then they brought out the king's son. They put the crown on him and gave him the Testimony. They proclaimed him king, and Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and said, "Long live the king!"

Athaliah Is Put to Death

12 When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and cheering the king, she went to the people in the house of the the LORD. 13 She saw the king standing by his pillar at the entrance. The captains and trumpeters stood beside the king. All the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets, and the singers were leading the praise with instruments. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, "Treason! Treason!"

14 Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders who were in charge of the army. He said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and put to death with the sword anyone who follows her."

The priest had said, "Do not put her to death in the house of the the LORD."

15 So they seized her. When she reached the entrance of the Horse Gate of the king's house, they put her to death there.

The People Return to the LORD

16 Then Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, that they would be the the LORD's people. 17 All the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and images, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. 18 Jehoiada put the Levitical priests in charge of the house of the the LORD, as David had assigned them, to offer burnt offerings to the the LORD, as it is written in the Law of Moses, with joy and song, as David had ordered. 19 He also stationed gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the the LORD, so that no one who was unclean in any way could enter. 20 He took the commanders, the nobles, the rulers of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought the king down from the house of the the LORD. They entered the king's house through the Upper Gate and seated the king on the royal throne. 21 All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet, because Athaliah had been killed with the sword.
