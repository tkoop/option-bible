Moses Begins His Final Speech

1 These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel in the wilderness east of the Jordan, in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab. 2 It takes eleven days to travel from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by way of Mount Seir. 3 In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses told the Israelites everything the the LORD had commanded him to say to them. 4 This was after he had defeated Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei. 5 East of the Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law. He said:

The LORD Commands Israel to Leave Horeb

6 The The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb. He said, "You have stayed at this mountain long enough. 7 Resume your journey and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all their neighbors in the Arabah, the hill country, the foothills, the Negev, and along the coast of the Mediterranean Sea. Go to the land of the Canaanites and to Lebanon, as far as the great river, the Euphrates. 8 See, I have given the land ahead of you. Go in and take it, the land the the LORD promised to give to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—and to their descendants after them."

Leaders Are Appointed

9 At that time I said to you, "I cannot carry you by myself. 10 The The LORD your God has made you many, and today you are as many as the stars in the sky. 11 May the the LORD, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times more and bless you, as He has promised you. 12 How can I carry your troubles, your loads, and your arguments by myself? 13 Choose wise, understanding, and respected men from each of your tribes, and I will put them over you as leaders."

14 You answered me, "What you say is good."

15 So I took the leaders of your tribes—wise and respected men—and put them over you. I made them commanders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens, and officers for your tribes.

16 At that time I told your judges, "Listen to the cases between your brothers and judge fairly between a man and his brother or a foreigner living among you." 17 Do not show favoritism in judging. Hear the small and the great alike. Do not be afraid of anyone, because judgment belongs to God. Bring me any case that is too hard for you, and I will hear it.

18 At that time I gave you all the commands you are to follow.

Spies, Rebellion, and Judgment

19 Then we set out from Horeb and traveled through all that great and frightening wilderness you saw, and we came to Kadesh-barnea.

20 I said to you, "You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which the the LORD our God is giving us. 21 See, the the LORD your God has given you the land. Go up and take it, as the the LORD, the God of your fathers, told you. Do not be afraid or discouraged."

22 Then all of you came to me and said, "Let us send men ahead of us to explore the land for us and bring back a report about the way we should go and the cities we will enter."

23 The idea seemed good to me, so I chose twelve men from among you, one from each tribe. 24 They set out and went up into the hill country and came to the Valley of Eshcol and spied it out.

25 They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands, brought it down to us, and reported, "The land the the LORD our God is giving us is good."

26 But you were not willing to go up. You rebelled against the command of the the LORD your God.

27 You grumbled in your tents and said, "The The LORD hates us. That is why He brought us out of Egypt—to hand us over to the Amorites to be destroyed. 28 Where can we go? Our brothers have discouraged us. They say the people are stronger and taller than we are, the cities are large with walls up to the sky, and they saw the sons of Anak there."

29 Then I said to you, "Do not be terrified or afraid of them. 30 The The LORD your God, who goes before you, will fight for you, just as you saw Him do for you in Egypt 31 and in the wilderness, where the the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way you traveled until you reached this place."

32 But even so, you did not believe the the LORD your God, 33 who went before you on your journey to find a place for you to camp and to show you the way you should go, with fire by night and cloud by day.

34 When the the LORD heard your words, He was angry and swore an oath: 35 "Not one of the men of this evil generation will see the good land I swore to give your fathers, 36 except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He will see it, and I will give him and his descendants the land he walked on, because he followed the the LORD wholeheartedly."

37 The The LORD was also angry with me because of you, and He said, "You will not enter it either. 38 But Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, will enter it. Encourage him, for he will lead Israel to take it. 39 And your little ones, whom you said would become plunder, and your children who do not yet know good from evil, will enter it. I will give it to them, and they will take it. 40 But as for you, turn around and set out for the wilderness by way of the Red Sea."

41 Then you replied, "We have sinned against the the LORD. We will go up and fight, as the the LORD our God commanded us." So each of you put on his weapons of war, and you were eager to go up into the hill country.

42 But the the LORD said to me, "Tell them: Do not go up and do not fight, for I am not with you. If you do, you will be defeated by your enemies."

43 So I spoke to you, but you did not listen. You rebelled against the the LORD's command and went up into the hill country anyway. 44 Then the Amorites who lived in the hill country came out against you and chased you like a swarm of bees. They crushed you in Seir all the way to Hormah. 45 You returned and wept before the the LORD, but the the LORD would not listen to your voice or pay attention to you. 46 And you stayed in Kadesh for many days—the same number of days you had stayed before.
