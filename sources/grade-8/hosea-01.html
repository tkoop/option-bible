Hosea's Call and His Unfaithful People

1 This is the message from the the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri. It came during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and during the reign of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel. 2 When the the LORD first spoke through Hosea, He said, "Go and find yourself a wife who will be unfaithful, and have children with her. The people of this land have completely turned away from following the the LORD." 3 So Hosea went and married Gomer, daughter of Diblaim. She became pregnant and had a son.

4 Then the the LORD said to him, "Name the boy Jezreel, because soon I will punish the family of Jehu for the bloodshed at Jezreel. I will put an end to the kingdom of Israel. 5 On that day I will break Israel's bow in the Valley of Jezreel."

6 Gomer became pregnant again and had a daughter. The the LORD said to Hosea, "Name her Lo-Ruhamah, meaning 'No Mercy,' because I will no longer show mercy to the people of Israel. I will completely take them away. 7 But I will show mercy to the people of Judah. I will save them, not with bows, swords, or war, and not with horses or horsemen, but by the the LORD their God."

8 After Gomer stopped nursing Lo-Ruhamah, she became pregnant again and had a son. 9 Then the the LORD said, "Name him Lo-Ammi, because you are not My people, and I am not your God."

A Future Hope for God's People

10 Yet the number of Israelites will be like the sand on the seashore, too many to measure or count. In the very place where it was said, "You are not My people," they will be called "children of the living God." 11 The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together. They will choose one leader for themselves, and they will go up from the land. For the day of Jezreel will be great.
