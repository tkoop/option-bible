1 David spoke the words of this song to the LORD when the LORD saved him from all his enemies and from Saul.

David's Song of Deliverance

2 "The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. 3 My God is my rock, where I take refuge. He is my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold and my refuge. My Savior, You save me from violence. 4 I call on the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

God Rescues David

5 "The waves of death surrounded me, and the floods of wickedness terrified me. 6 The cords of the grave wrapped around me; the snares of death confronted me. 7 In my distress I called to the LORD; I cried out to my God. From His temple He heard my voice, and my cry reached His ears.

8 "Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of the heavens quaked, because He was angry. 9 Smoke rose from His nostrils, and consuming fire came from His mouth; burning coals blazed from it. 10 He parted the heavens and came down, with thick darkness under His feet. 11 He rode on a cherub and flew; He was seen on the wings of the wind. 12 He made darkness His covering, surrounding Him with rain clouds and thick clouds of the skies. 13 From the brightness before Him, coals of fire blazed. 14 The LORD thundered from heaven, and the Most High raised His voice. 15 He shot arrows and scattered them; lightning routed them. 16 The channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of breath from His nostrils.

17 "He reached down from on high and took hold of me; He drew me out of deep waters. 18 He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me. 19 They confronted me in my day of disaster, but the LORD was my support. 20 He brought me out into open space; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

The LORD Rewards Righteousness

21 "The LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; He has repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands. 22 For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly turned from my God. 23 For all His ordinances are before me, and I have not turned away from His statutes. 24 I have been blameless before Him and have kept myself from my sin. 25 So the LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in His sight.

26 "To the faithful You show Yourself faithful; to the blameless You show Yourself blameless. 27 To the pure You show Yourself pure, but to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. 28 You save an afflicted people, but Your eyes are on the haughty to bring them down. 29 For You are my lamp, O the LORD, and the LORD lights up my darkness. 30 With Your help I can run through a troop; with my God I can leap over a wall. 31 As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

32 "For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the Rock except our God? 33 God is my strong fortress, and He makes my way blameless. 34 He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me on the heights. 35 He trains my hands for battle, so my arms can bend a bronze bow. 36 You have given me the shield of Your salvation, and Your gentleness has made me great. 37 You widen the path beneath me, so my feet do not slip.

Victory and Praise

38 "I pursued my enemies and destroyed them; I did not turn back until they were consumed. 39 I crushed them and pierced them so they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet. 40 You armed me with strength for battle; You made my foes bow before me. 41 You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and I destroyed those who hated me. 42 They looked, but there was no one to save them—to the LORD, but He did not answer them. 43 I ground them as fine as dust of the earth; I crushed and trampled them like mud in the streets. 44 You have delivered me from the attacks of my people; You have preserved me as head of nations. A people I did not know serves me. 45 Foreigners submit to me; as soon as they hear, they obey me. 46 Foreigners fade away and come trembling from their strongholds. 47 The LORD lives! Blessed be my Rock! May God, the Rock of my salvation, be exalted! 48 He is the God who avenges me and brings peoples under my rule. 49 He delivers me from my enemies. You lift me up above my foes; You rescue me from violent men. 50 Therefore I will praise You among the nations, O the LORD, and I will sing praises to Your name. 51 He gives great victories to His king and shows loving devotion to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever."
