A Song for Judah

1 In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: “We have a strong city. God will make salvation its walls and defenses. 2 Open the gates so the righteous nation can come in—the nation that stays faithful. 3 You will keep him in perfect peace when his mind stays on You, because he trusts in You. 4 Trust in the LORD forever, because the LORD God is a rock that lasts forever.

The LORD Brings Down the Proud

“5 He has brought down the people who live in high places and the proud city. He lays it low to the ground and brings it down to dust. 6 The poor and needy walk all over it. 7 The path of those who do right is smooth. You make the road straight for those who do right.

“8 Yes, we wait for You, the LORD, on the path of Your justice. We long for Your name and Your fame. 9 My soul longs for You at night, and my spirit searches for You at dawn. When Your judgments come to the earth, the people of the world learn to do right. 10 Even if the wicked are treated kindly, they still do not learn to do right. In a land of honest people, they act unfairly and ignore the LORD’s greatness.

“11 The LORD, You lift Your hand, but they do not see it. Let them see how much You care for Your people so they will be ashamed. Let the fire meant for Your enemies burn them up. 12 The LORD, You will give us peace, because You have done all our work for us. 13 The LORD our God, other rulers besides You have ruled over us, but we will remember only Your name.

“14 The dead will not live again. The spirits of the dead will not rise. So You punished and destroyed them, and wiped out all memory of them. 15 You have made the nation bigger, the LORD. You have made it bigger and are honored. You have pushed its borders to the ends of the earth. 16 The LORD, when they were in trouble, they looked for You. They whispered a prayer when Your discipline was on them.

The LORD Will Raise the Dead

“17 Like a pregnant woman who twists and cries out in pain, so we were before You, the LORD. 18 We were pregnant and in pain, but we gave birth only to wind. We did not bring salvation to the earth, and we did not bring new life into the world. 19 Your dead will live. Their bodies will rise. Wake up and sing, you who live in the dust! For Your dew is like the morning dew, and the earth will give birth to the dead.”

A Call to Hide

20 “Go, My people. Enter your rooms and shut your doors behind you. Hide yourselves for a little while until the anger passes.”

21 For the LORD is coming out of His place to punish the people of the earth for their sin. The earth will show its blood and will not hide its murdered people anymore.
