The Majesty of God's Name

1The LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens.

2From the mouths of children and infants You have established strength, because of Your enemies, to silence the foe and the avenger.

Man's Place in God's Creation

3When I look at Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars, which You have set in place, 4what is man that You remember him, and the son of man that You care for him?

5You made him a little lower than the angels, and crowned him with glory and honor. 6You made him ruler over the works of Your hands; You put everything under his feet: 7all sheep and cattle, and even the beasts of the field, 8the birds of the air and the fish of the sea, all that swim the paths of the seas.

9The LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth!
