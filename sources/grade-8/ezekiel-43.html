The Glory of the LORD Returns to the Temple

1 Then he brought me to the gate that faces east. 2 I saw the glory of the God of Israel coming from the east. His voice sounded like rushing water, and the earth shone with His glory. 3 The vision I saw was like the one I had seen when He came to destroy the city. It was also like the vision I saw by the Kebar River. I fell facedown. 4 The glory of the LORD entered the temple through the gate facing east. 5 The Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and the glory of the LORD filled the temple.

The LORD Speaks About the Temple

6 A man stood beside me, and I heard someone speaking to me from inside the temple. 7 He said to me, “Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place for the soles of My feet. I will live among the Israelites forever. The house of Israel and their kings must never again defile My holy name with their unfaithfulness or with the dead bodies of their kings on their high places. 8 When they put their threshold next to My threshold and their doorposts beside My doorposts, with only a wall between Me and them, they defiled My holy name by the detestable things they did. So I destroyed them in My anger. 9 Now let them keep their unfaithfulness and the dead bodies of their kings far from Me, and I will live among them forever.”

10 “Son of man, describe the temple to the house of Israel so they will be ashamed of their sins. Let them measure its design. 11 If they are ashamed of all they have done, show them the design of the temple—its layout, exits, and entrances, and its whole design. Tell them all its rules and forms, and write them down before their eyes so they will keep its whole design and all its rules and do them. 12 This is the law of the temple: The whole area around it on top of the mountain is most holy. This is the law of the temple.”

Measurements of the Altar

13 These are the measurements of the altar in cubits (the cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth): Its base shall be a cubit high and a cubit wide, with a rim around its edge of one span. This is the height of the altar. 14 From the base on the ground to the lower ledge shall be two cubits, and the width one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge shall be four cubits, and the width one cubit. 15 The altar hearth shall be four cubits high, with four horns projecting upward from the hearth. 16 The altar hearth shall be square, twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide. 17 The ledge shall be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide, with a rim around it of half a cubit and a base of a cubit all around. Its steps shall face east.

Consecrating the Altar

18 He said to me, “Son of man, this is what the Lord the LORD says: These are the statutes for the altar on the day it is built, so that burnt offerings may be offered on it and blood may be sprinkled on it. 19 You are to give a young bull as a sin offering to the Levitical priests of the family of Zadok, who approach Me to minister before Me, declares the Lord the LORD. 20 You shall take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge, and all around the rim. Thus you shall cleanse the altar and make atonement for it. 21 Then take the bull for the sin offering and burn it in the designated place of the temple, outside the sanctuary. 22 On the second day you are to present an unblemished male goat as a sin offering, and they shall purify the altar just as they purified it with the bull. 23 When you have finished the sin offering, you are to present an unblemished young bull and an unblemished ram from the flock. 24 You shall present them before the LORD, and the priests shall sprinkle salt on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD. 25 For seven days you are to provide a male goat daily for a sin offering; also provide an unblemished young bull and an unblemished ram from the flock. 26 For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it; thus they shall consecrate it. 27 When these days are complete, on the eighth day and thereafter, the priests shall offer your burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar. Then I will accept you, declares the Lord the LORD.”
