Israel Turns Away from God Again

1 After Ehud died, the Israelites again did evil in the eyes of the LORD. 2 So the LORD handed them over to Jabin, king of Canaan, who ruled from Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth Haggoyim. 3 The Israelites cried out to the LORD because Jabin had nine hundred iron chariots and had oppressed them for twenty years.

Deborah Calls Barak

4 At that time Deborah, a prophetess and the wife of Lappidoth, was judging Israel. 5 She held court under the Palm of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came to her for decisions.

6 She sent for Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali and said to him, "The LORD, the God of Israel, commands you: 'Go, take ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun with you, and lead them up to Mount Tabor. 7 I will bring Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, to you at the Kishon River, along with his chariots and troops, and I will hand him over to you.'"

8 Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go. But if you don't go with me, I won't go."

9 Deborah said, "I will certainly go with you. But because of the way you are going, you won't get the honor. The LORD will hand Sisera over to a woman." So Deborah went with Barak to Kedesh.

10 There Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali to come, and ten thousand men went up with him. Deborah went up with him too. 11 Now Heber the Kenite had moved away from the other Kenites, the descendants of Hobab, Moses' father-in-law. He had pitched his tent by the great tree in Zaanannim near Kedesh.

Sisera Marches to Battle

12 When Sisera was told that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor, 13 he gathered all his chariots—nine hundred iron chariots—and all his men from Harosheth Haggoyim to the Kishon River.

14 Deborah said to Barak, "Get up! This is the day the LORD has handed Sisera over to you. Hasn't the LORD gone out ahead of you?" So Barak went down Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him.

15 At Barak's advance, the LORD threw Sisera and all his chariots and troops into panic, and they were defeated by the sword. Sisera got down from his chariot and ran away on foot. 16 Barak chased the chariots and army all the way to Harosheth Haggoyim. Every soldier of Sisera fell by the sword; not one was left.

Sisera Flees to Jael's Tent

17 Sisera ran on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, because there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and Heber's family.

18 Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Come in, my lord. Come in. Don't be afraid." So he went into her tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

19 He said, "I'm thirsty. Please give me a little water." She opened a skin of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him up.

20 He told her, "Stand at the entrance to the tent. If someone comes and asks you, 'Is anyone here?' say, 'No.'"

21 But Jael, Heber's wife, took a tent peg and a hammer. She went quietly to Sisera while he was fast asleep and worn out. She drove the peg through the side of his head into the ground, and he died.

22 Just then Barak came by, chasing Sisera. Jael went out to meet him and said, "Come, I will show you the man you are looking for." So he went in with her, and there lay Sisera, dead, with the tent peg through the side of his head.

23 On that day God defeated Jabin king of Canaan for the Israelites. 24 The Israelites kept getting stronger against Jabin king of Canaan until they destroyed him.
