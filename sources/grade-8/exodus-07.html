God Appoints Moses and Aaron

1 The LORD said to Moses, "See, I have made you like God to Pharaoh, and your brother Aaron will be your prophet. 2 You must speak everything I command you, and your brother Aaron must tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites leave his country. 3 But I will harden Pharaoh's heart, and I will multiply My signs and wonders in the land of Egypt.

4 "Pharaoh will not listen to you. Then I will lay My hand on Egypt, and with mighty judgments I will bring out My people, the Israelites, from the land of Egypt. 5 And the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD when I stretch out My hand against Egypt and bring the Israelites out from among them."

6 So Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded them. 7 Moses was eighty years old and Aaron was eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh.

Aaron's Staff Becomes a Snake

8 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 9 "When Pharaoh says to you, 'Show us a miracle,' you must tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down in front of Pharaoh, and it will become a snake.'"

10 So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did as the LORD had commanded. Aaron threw his staff down in front of Pharaoh and his officials, and it became a snake. 11 Then Pharaoh also called for the wise men and sorcerers, and the Egyptian magicians did the same thing by their magic. 12 Each one threw down his staff, and they became snakes. But Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs. 13 Yet Pharaoh's heart became hard, and he would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

The First Plague: The Nile Turns to Blood

14 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is stubborn; he refuses to let the people go. 15 Go to Pharaoh in the morning. You will see him going out to the water. Stand on the bank of the Nile to meet him. Take in your hand the staff that was turned into a snake. 16 Say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you to say, "Let My people go, so that they may worship Me in the wilderness." But until now you have not listened.' 17 This is what the LORD says: 'By this you will know that I am the LORD: I am going to strike the water of the Nile with the staff in My hand, and it will turn to blood.' 18 The fish in the Nile will die, the river will stink, and the Egyptians will be unable to drink its water."

19 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt—over their rivers, canals, ponds, and all their pools of water—so that they may become blood. There will be blood throughout the land of Egypt, even in wooden buckets and stone jars.'"

20 Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded. In front of Pharaoh and his officials, Aaron raised the staff and struck the water of the Nile, and all the water in the Nile turned to blood. 21 The fish in the Nile died, and the river smelled so bad that the Egyptians could not drink its water. There was blood throughout the land of Egypt. 22 But the Egyptian magicians did the same thing by their magic, and Pharaoh's heart became hard. He would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said. 23 Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and he did not take this to heart either. 24 All the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink the water of the river. 25 Seven days passed after the LORD struck the Nile.
