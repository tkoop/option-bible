Seek Wisdom with All Your Heart

1 My son, if you accept my words and store up my commands inside you, 2 turning your ear to wisdom and giving your heart to understanding— 3 yes, if you call out for insight and cry aloud for understanding, 4 and if you search for it like silver and hunt for it like hidden treasure, 5 then you will understand what it means to revere the the LORD, and you will find the knowledge of God.

The LORD Gives Wisdom and Protection

6 For the the LORD gives wisdom; knowledge and understanding come from His mouth. 7 He stores up wise guidance for those who do right; He is a shield for people who live without blame. 8 He guards the paths of justice and protects the way of His faithful people.

9 Then you will understand what is right, fair, and just—every good path. 10 For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will bring joy to your soul. 11 Good judgment will protect you, and understanding will keep you safe.

Wisdom Keeps You from Evil

12 Wisdom will save you from the ways of evil people, from those who speak twisted words, 13 who have left the straight path to walk in dark ways, 14 who enjoy doing wrong and celebrate twisted evil, 15 whose paths are crooked and whose ways are devious.

16 It will also save you from the wayward woman, from the smooth-talking woman who is unfaithful to her husband, 17 who has left the partner of her youth and forgotten the promise she made to her God. 18 Surely her house leads down to death, and her paths lead to the spirits of the dead. 19 None who go to her come back; they never reach the paths of life.

The Reward of Righteous Living

20 So you will walk in the ways of good people and keep to the paths of those who do right. 21 For those who do right will live in the land, and those without blame will stay in it; 22 but the wicked will be cut off from the land, and those who break faith will be uprooted from it.
