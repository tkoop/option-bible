The Words of Amos

1 These are the words of Amos. He was one of the shepherds from Tekoa. He saw this message about Israel during the time of Uzziah, king of Judah, and Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

The LORD Roars from Zion

2 The LORD roars from Zion and speaks from Jerusalem. The shepherds' pastures mourn, and the top of Mount Carmel dries up.

Judgment on Israel's Neighbors

3 This is what the LORD says: "Damascus has sinned again and again, so I will not hold back My punishment. They crushed Gilead with iron threshing sledges. 4 So I will send fire on the house of Hazael, and it will burn up Ben-Hadad's palaces. 5 I will break the bar of Damascus's gate and remove the ruler from the Valley of Aven and the one who holds the scepter from Beth Eden. The people of Aram will be taken captive to Kir, says the LORD."

6 This is what the LORD says: "Gaza has sinned again and again, so I will not hold back My punishment. They dragged off whole communities and sold them to Edom. 7 So I will send fire on the wall of Gaza, and it will burn up its palaces. 8 I will remove the ruler from Ashdod and the one who holds the scepter from Ashkelon. I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the rest of the Philistines will be wiped out, says the Lord the LORD."

9 This is what the LORD says: "Tyre has sinned again and again, so I will not hold back My punishment. They sold whole communities into exile to Edom and forgot their treaty of brotherhood. 10 So I will send fire on the wall of Tyre, and it will burn up its palaces."

11 This is what the LORD says: "Edom has sinned again and again, so I will not hold back My punishment. He chased his brother with a sword and showed no mercy. His anger burned all the time, and his wrath never ended. 12 So I will send fire on Teman, and it will burn up the palaces of Bozrah."

13 This is what the LORD says: "Ammon has sinned again and again, so I will not hold back My punishment. They ripped open pregnant women in Gilead to enlarge their territory. 14 So I will set fire to the wall of Rabbah, and it will burn up its palaces amid war cries on the day of battle and a storm on the day of a whirlwind. 15 Their king will go into exile, along with his officials, says the LORD."
