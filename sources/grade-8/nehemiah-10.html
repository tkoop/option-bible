Those Who Sealed the Covenant

1 Those who put their seal on the covenant were Nehemiah the governor, son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah; 2 Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah; 3 Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah; 4 Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch; 5 Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah; 6 Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch; 7 Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin; 8 Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah. These were the priests.

9 The Levites were Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, and Kadmiel. 10 Their relatives were Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan, 11 Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah, 12 Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah, 13 Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

14 The leaders of the people were Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani, 15 Bunni, Azgad, Bebai, 16 Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin, 17 Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur, 18 Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai, 19 Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai, 20 Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir, 21 Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua, 22 Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah, 23 Hoshea, Hananiah, Hashub, 24 Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek, 25 Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah, 26 Ahijah, Hanan, Anan, 27 Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

The People Promise to Follow God's Law

28 The rest of the people—the priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and everyone who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to follow the Law of God, along with their wives, sons, and daughters, all who could understand— 29 joined their fellow Israelites and leaders. They took on a curse and an oath to walk in God's Law, which was given through Moses, God's servant, and to carefully obey all the commandments of the the LORD our Lord, along with His rules and laws.

30 “We will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land, and we will not take their daughters for our sons. 31 If the peoples of the land bring goods or grain to sell on the Sabbath, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on any holy day. We will also let the land rest in the seventh year and cancel every debt.

Support for the House of God

32 “We take on the duty of giving one third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God: 33 for the bread set out before God, the regular grain offering, the regular burnt offering on Sabbaths, new moon festivals, and appointed feasts, and for holy things and sin offerings to pay for Israel's sins, and for all the work in the house of our God.

34 “We, the priests, Levites, and people, have cast lots for the wood offering. We will bring it to the house of our God by family groups at set times each year, to burn on the altar of the the LORD our God, as it is written in the Law. 35 We also promise to bring the first crops from our land and the first fruit from every tree each year to the house of the the LORD.

36 “We will bring to the house of our God, to the priests who serve there, the firstborn of our sons and of our cattle, as the Law says, along with the firstborn of our herds and flocks. 37 We will bring the first portion of our dough, our gifts, the fruit of every tree, wine, and oil to the priests in the rooms of the house of our God. We will also bring the Levites a tenth of our crops, because the Levites collect the tithes in all the towns where we work.

38 “A priest, a descendant of Aaron, will be with the Levites when they receive the tithe. The Levites will bring a tenth of the tithes to the house of our God, to the rooms of the storehouse. 39 The people of Israel and the Levites will bring the gifts of grain, wine, and oil to those rooms, where the holy vessels are kept, along with the priests who serve, the gatekeepers, and the singers. We will not neglect the house of our God.”
