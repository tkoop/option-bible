Jehoahaz Rules Israel

1 In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu began to rule over Israel in Samaria. He ruled seventeen years. 2 He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and he followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which Jeroboam had made Israel commit. He did not turn away from them. 3 So the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and He kept handing them over to Hazael king of Aram and to Ben-Hadad son of Hazael all those days. 4 Jehoahaz begged the LORD, and the LORD listened to him, because He saw how Israel was being crushed. The king of Aram was crushing them. 5 The LORD gave Israel a rescuer, and they escaped from under Aram's power. The people of Israel lived in their tents again, as they had before. 6 But they did not leave the sins of the house of Jeroboam, which he had made Israel commit. They kept walking in those sins. The Asherah pole also stayed in Samaria. 7 Jehoahaz was left with no army except fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers, because the king of Aram had destroyed the rest and crushed them like dust. 8 The rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, and all he did, and his strength, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel? 9 Jehoahaz died and was buried with his ancestors in Samaria. His son Jehoash became king in his place.

Jehoash Rules Israel

10 In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz began to rule over Israel in Samaria. He ruled sixteen years. 11 He did evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had made Israel commit, but he walked in them. 12 The rest of the acts of Jehoash, and all he did, and his strength in his war with Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel? 13 Jehoash died and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel. Jeroboam took his place on the throne.

Elisha's Last Days

14 Elisha had become sick with the illness that would kill him. Jehoash king of Israel came down to him and wept over his face. He said, "My father, my father! You were Israel's true defense and strength!"

15 Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." So he took a bow and arrows. 16 Elisha said to the king of Israel, "Put your hand on the bow." He put his hand on it, and Elisha put his hands on the king's hands.

17 Elisha said, "Open the window eastward." He opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot!" He shot. Then Elisha said, "This is the LORD's arrow of victory, the arrow of victory over Aram. You will strike Aram at Aphek until you destroy them."

18 Elisha said, "Take the arrows." He took them. Then Elisha said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground." He struck three times and stopped.

19 The man of God was angry with him and said, "You should have struck five or six times. Then you would have struck Aram until you destroyed them. But now you will strike Aram only three times."

Elisha Dies and Israel Recovers

20 Elisha died, and they buried him. Bands of Moabites used to raid the land every spring. 21 As some men were burying a man, they saw a raiding band. They threw the man into Elisha's tomb. When the man touched Elisha's bones, he came back to life and stood up on his feet. 22 Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz. 23 But the LORD was kind to them and had compassion on them. He turned to them because of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He would not destroy them, and He had not yet driven them from His presence. 24 Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-Hadad became king in his place. 25 Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from Ben-Hadad son of Hazael the cities that he had taken from Jehoahaz his father in war. Jehoash defeated him three times and restored the cities of Israel.
