Israel Loses the First Battle

1 Samuel’s message came to all Israel. Israel went out to fight the Philistines and camped at Ebenezer, while the Philistines camped at Aphek. 2 The Philistines lined up to fight Israel, and as the battle spread, Israel was defeated. The Philistines killed about four thousand Israelite soldiers in the field.

Israel Brings the Ark to Battle

3 When the troops came back to camp, the elders of Israel asked, “Why has the LORD let us be defeated today by the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from Shiloh so that He may go with us and save us from our enemies.” 4 So the people sent men to Shiloh, and they brought back the ark of the covenant of the LORD of Hosts, who sits between the cherubim. Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of God.

5 When the ark of the covenant of the LORD came into the camp, all Israel gave such a loud shout that the ground shook. 6 The Philistines heard the shout and asked, “What is this loud shouting in the Hebrew camp?” When they learned that the ark of the LORD had come into the camp, they were afraid. 7 They said, “A god has come into the camp!” They also said, “Woe to us! Nothing like this has happened before. 8 Woe to us! Who will save us from these mighty gods? These are the gods who struck the Egyptians with every kind of plague in the wilderness. 9 Be strong and act like men, Philistines, or you will become slaves to the Hebrews, as they were to you. Be men and fight!”

The Ark Is Captured

10 So the Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated. Every man fled to his tent. The slaughter was very great—thirty thousand Israelite foot soldiers fell. 11 The ark of God was captured, and Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

Eli and His Family Hear the News

12 That same day a man from Benjamin ran from the battle line all the way to Shiloh. His clothes were torn, and dust was on his head. 13 When he arrived, Eli was sitting on a chair beside the road, watching, because his heart trembled for the ark of God. The man went into the city to report, and the whole city cried out. 14 Eli heard the outcry and asked, “What is this noise?” So the man hurried in and told Eli.

15 Eli was ninety-eight years old, and his eyes were so weak that he could not see. 16 The man said to Eli, “I am the one who came from the battle. I fled from the battle today.” “What happened, my son?” Eli asked. 17 The messenger answered, “Israel has fled before the Philistines, and many people have been killed. Your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and the ark of God has been captured.”

18 As soon as he mentioned the ark of God, Eli fell backward off his chair beside the gate. His neck was broken, and he died, for he was an old, heavy man. He had judged Israel for forty years. 19 Now Eli’s daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and about to give birth. When she heard that the ark of God had been captured and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead, she knelt down and gave birth, because her labor pains came on. 20 As she was dying, the women beside her said, “Do not be afraid, for you have given birth to a son!” But she did not answer or pay attention. 21 She named the boy Ichabod, saying, “The glory has departed from Israel,” because the ark of God had been captured and because of her father-in-law and her husband. 22 “The glory has departed from Israel,” she said, “for the ark of God has been captured.”
