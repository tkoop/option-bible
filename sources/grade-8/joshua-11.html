Northern Kings Join Forces Against Israel

1 When Jabin, king of Hazor, heard about this, he sent messages to Jobab, king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph. 2 He also sent messages to the kings in the north who lived in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinneroth, in the western foothills, and in Naphoth-dor by the sea. 3 He sent to the Canaanites in the east and west, the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites below Mount Hermon in the land of Mizpah. 4 They came out with all their troops. Their army was very large, like the sand on the seashore, and they had many horses and chariots. 5 All these kings met and camped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

The LORD Promises Victory to Joshua

6 The LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them, because by this time tomorrow I will hand all of them over to Israel, dead. You are to hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire.”

7 So Joshua and all his fighting men came against them by the waters of Merom suddenly and attacked them. 8 The LORD gave them into the hand of Israel. They struck them down and chased them to Great Sidon, to Misrephoth-maim, and to the Valley of Mizpah in the east. They struck them down until no survivors were left. 9 Joshua did to them as the LORD had commanded. He hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

Joshua Captures Hazor and the Northern Cities

10 At that time Joshua turned back and captured Hazor and struck down its king with the sword. Hazor had been the head of all those kingdoms. 11 They put to the sword every person in it, destroying them completely. No one was left alive. Then they burned Hazor with fire.

12 Joshua captured all these cities and their kings. He struck them with the sword and destroyed them, just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded. 13 But Israel did not burn any of the cities that stood on their mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua burned. 14 The Israelites took for themselves all the plunder and livestock of these cities. But they put all the people to the sword until they destroyed them, leaving no one alive. 15 As the LORD had commanded His servant Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua did it. He did not fail to do anything the LORD had commanded Moses.

Joshua Takes the Land of Canaan

16 So Joshua took all that land: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the western foothills, the Arabah, the hill country of Israel and its lowlands. 17 It went from Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, as far as Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings, struck them down, and put them to death. 18 Joshua waged war against all these kings for a long time. 19 No city made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites who lived in Gibeon. They took all the others in battle. 20 For it was the LORD who hardened their hearts to fight against Israel, so that they would be destroyed completely and receive no mercy, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Anakim Are Defeated

21 At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, Debir, and Anab, from all the hill country of Judah, and from all the hill country of Israel. Joshua destroyed them and their cities completely. 22 No Anakim were left in the land of the Israelites except in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod. 23 Joshua took all the land, just as the LORD had spoken to Moses. He gave it to Israel as an inheritance, divided among their tribes. Then the land rested from war.
