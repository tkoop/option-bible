Some Men Say Circumcision Is Needed

1 Some men came down from Judea and taught the brothers, "Unless you are circumcised the way Moses taught, you cannot be saved."

2 This led to a serious argument between Paul and Barnabas and those men. So the church decided to send Paul, Barnabas, and a few others to Jerusalem. They would ask the apostles and elders about this question. 3 The church sent them on their way. They traveled through Phoenicia and Samaria, telling how the Gentiles had turned to God. This brought great joy to all the brothers. 4 When they reached Jerusalem, the church, the apostles, and the elders welcomed them. Then they reported everything God had done through them.

5 But some of the believers who belonged to the Pharisees stood up and said, "The Gentiles must be circumcised and told to obey the law of Moses."

The Church Leaders Meet in Jerusalem

6 The apostles and elders met to consider this issue.

7 After much discussion, Peter stood up and said, "Brothers, you know that long ago God chose me from among you to tell the Gentiles the good news so they could believe. 8 God knows everyone's heart. He showed that He accepted them by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as He did to us. 9 He made no difference between us and them, because He made their hearts clean by faith. 10 So why are you testing God by putting a heavy load on the disciples? Neither we nor our fathers could carry that load. 11 Instead, we believe we are saved by the grace of the Lord Jesus, just like they are."

12 The whole crowd became quiet. They listened to Barnabas and Paul tell about the signs and wonders God had done among the Gentiles through them.

13 When they finished, James spoke up: "Brothers, listen to me. 14 Simeon has told us how God first cared for the Gentiles by choosing some of them to belong to Him. 15 The words of the prophets agree with this, as it is written: 16 'After this I will return and rebuild David's fallen tent. I will repair its ruins and set it up again. 17 Then the rest of humanity may seek the Lord. That includes all the Gentiles who are called by My name,' says the Lord, who does these things. 18 These things have been known for ages. 19 So my decision is not to make things hard for the Gentiles who are turning to God. 20 Instead, we should write to them and tell them to stay away from food offered to idols, from sexual sin, from meat of strangled animals, and from blood. 21 For Moses has been preached in every city for many generations, and his words are read in the synagogues every Sabbath."

A Letter Is Sent to the Gentile Churches

22 Then the apostles and elders, along with the whole church, decided to choose some men from their group and send them to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas, called Barsabbas, and Silas. These were leaders among the brothers.

23 They sent this letter with them: "The apostles and elders, your brothers, send greetings to the Gentile brothers in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia. 24 We have heard that some men from our group went out without our permission. They upset you and troubled your minds with their words. 25 So we all agreed to choose some men and send them to you with our dear friends Barnabas and Paul.

26 "These men have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 27 So we are sending Judas and Silas. They will tell you the same things in person. 28 It seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us not to place any heavier burden on you than these necessary things: 29 Stay away from food sacrificed to idols, from blood, from strangled meat, and from sexual sin. If you keep away from these things, you will do well. Goodbye."

30 So the men were sent off. They went down to Antioch, gathered the church together, and gave them the letter. 31 When the people read it, they were glad because of its encouraging message.

32 Judas and Silas were prophets. They said many words to encourage and strengthen the brothers. 33 After staying there for a while, they were sent back in peace to the men who had sent them.

Paul and Barnabas Separate

35 But Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch. They and many others taught and preached the word of the Lord.

36 After some days, Paul said to Barnabas, "Let's go back and visit the brothers in every city where we preached the word of the Lord. We can see how they are doing."

37 Barnabas wanted to take John, also called Mark. 38 But Paul did not think it was right to take him, because he had left them in Pamphylia and had not continued with them in the work.

39 They disagreed so sharply that they separated. Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus. 40 Paul chose Silas and left. The brothers sent him off, trusting him to the Lord's grace. 41 He traveled through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.
