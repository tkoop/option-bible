Moses Prepares Israel for the Future

1 Then Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel. 2 He said, "I am now one hundred twenty years old. I can no longer go out and come in. the LORD has said to me, 'You shall not cross this Jordan.' 3 The LORD your God Himself will cross over ahead of you. He will destroy these nations before you, and you will take their land. Joshua will also go ahead of you, as the LORD has spoken. 4 The LORD will do to them what He did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, whom He destroyed along with their land. 5 The LORD will hand them over to you, and you must do to them exactly as I have commanded you. 6 Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or terrified of them, because the LORD your God goes with you. He will never leave you or forsake you."

Joshua Is Commissioned to Lead

7 Then Moses called Joshua and said to him where all Israel could see, "Be strong and courageous, because you will go with this people into the land the LORD promised to give their fathers, and you will give it to them as their land. 8 The LORD Himself goes before you. He will be with you. He will never leave you or forsake you. Do not be afraid or discouraged."

The Law Is Given to the Priests

9 So Moses wrote down this law and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the LORD's covenant, and to all the elders of Israel. 10 Then Moses commanded them, "At the end of every seven years, at the set time in the year of release, during the Feast of Tabernacles, 11 when all Israel comes to appear before the LORD your God in the place He chooses, you must read this law out loud so all Israel can hear it. 12 Gather the people—men, women, children, and the foreigners living in your towns—so they may hear and learn to fear the LORD your God and carefully obey all the words of this law. 13 Then their children, who do not know it, will hear and learn to fear the LORD your God as long as you live in the land you are crossing the Jordan to take."

God Warns Israel About Its Future

14 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Look, the time for your death is near. Call Joshua and stand together at the Tent of Meeting, so I can give him his orders." So Moses and Joshua went and stood at the Tent of Meeting. 15 The LORD appeared at the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the cloud stood above the entrance.

16 The LORD said to Moses, "You are about to lie down with your fathers. These people will rise up and be unfaithful to Me by chasing the gods of the land they are entering. They will leave Me and break the covenant I made with them. 17 On that day My anger will burn against them. I will leave them and hide My face from them, and they will be destroyed. Many troubles and disasters will come on them. On that day they will say, 'Have not these disasters found us because our God is not with us?' 18 I will surely hide My face on that day because of all the evil they have done, for they will have turned to other gods.

19 "So now write down this song and teach it to the Israelites. Put it in their mouths, so this song will be a witness for Me against them. 20 When I bring them into the land I promised their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey, they will eat and be full and grow comfortable. Then they will turn to other gods and worship them. They will reject Me and break My covenant. 21 When many troubles and disasters come on them, this song will speak against them, because it will not be forgotten by their children. I know what they plan to do, even before I bring them into the land I promised to give them."

22 So that same day Moses wrote down this song and taught it to the Israelites. 23 Then the LORD gave Joshua son of Nun his orders and said, "Be strong and courageous, because you will bring the Israelites into the land I promised to give them, and I will be with you."

24 When Moses finished writing all the words of this law in a book, from beginning to end, 25 he commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the LORD's covenant, 26 "Take this Book of the Law and place it beside the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, so it will stay there as a witness against you. 27 For I know your rebellion and your stubborn neck. Even while I am still alive with you, you have been rebelling against the LORD. How much more will you do so after I die!"

28 "Bring together for me all the elders of your tribes and your officers, so I can speak these words to them and call heaven and earth to witness against them. 29 For I know that after I die you will become completely corrupt and turn away from the path I commanded you to follow. In the days to come, disaster will come on you because you will do what is evil in the LORD's sight and make Him angry by the work of your hands."

30 Then Moses recited the words of this song from beginning to end while all Israel listened.
