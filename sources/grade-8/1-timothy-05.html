How to Treat People in the Church

1 Do not scold an older man. Instead, speak to him as a father. Treat younger men like brothers. 2 Treat older women like mothers and younger women like sisters, with complete purity.

Caring for Widows

3 Honor widows who are truly alone. 4 If a widow has children or grandchildren, they should first learn to care for their own family and repay their parents. This pleases God.

5 A widow who is truly alone puts her hope in God and keeps praying day and night. 6 But a widow who lives only for pleasure is spiritually dead, even though she is still alive.

7 Give these commands so no one can be blamed. 8 If anyone does not take care of his own family, especially those in his home, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

9 A widow should be added to the list only if she is at least sixty years old and has been married to only one husband. 10 She must be known for good works, such as raising children, welcoming strangers, washing the feet of God's people, helping those in trouble, and doing every kind of good deed.

11 But do not add younger widows to the list. When their strong desires pull them away from Christ, they want to marry again. 12 They bring judgment on themselves because they have broken their first promise to Christ. 13 Besides that, they learn to be lazy and go from house to house. They not only become lazy but also talk too much and meddle in other people's business, saying things they should not say.

14 So I want younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their homes. That way they give the enemy no reason to speak badly about us. 15 Some have already turned away and followed Satan. 16 If a believing woman has widows in her family, she should help them. Then the church is not weighed down and can help the widows who are truly alone.

Honoring Church Leaders

17 Elders who lead well deserve to be respected and well paid, especially those who work hard at preaching and teaching. 18 For the Scripture says, "Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading grain," and, "The worker deserves his pay."

19 Do not listen to an accusation against an elder unless two or three witnesses support it. 20 But those who keep sinning, correct in front of everyone, so the others will be afraid to sin.

Final Instructions

21 I charge you before God, Christ Jesus, and the chosen angels to keep these rules without bias, doing nothing out of favoritism. 22 Do not quickly appoint anyone to leadership, and do not share in other people's sins. Keep yourself pure.

23 Stop drinking only water. Use a little wine for your stomach and your frequent illnesses. 24 The sins of some people are easy to see and lead the way to judgment. The sins of others follow later. 25 In the same way, good deeds are easy to see. Even those that are not obvious cannot stay hidden.
