Message to the Church in Sardis

1 "To the angel of the church in Sardis write: These things says He who has the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars. I know your deeds. You have a reputation for being alive, but you are dead. 2 Wake up and strengthen what is left, because it is about to die. I have not found your deeds complete before My God. 3 So remember what you received and heard. Keep it and turn back to Me. If you do not stay awake, I will come like a thief, and you will not know when I will come to you.

4 "Yet you have a few people in Sardis who have not stained their clothes. They will walk with Me in white, because they are worthy. 5 The one who overcomes will be dressed in white. I will not erase his name from the Book of Life, and I will acknowledge his name before My Father and before His angels.

6 "The one who has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

Message to the Church in Philadelphia

7 "To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: These things says the Holy and True One, who holds the key of David. What He opens no one can shut, and what He shuts no one can open. 8 I know your deeds. Look, I have placed before you an open door that no one can shut. You have little strength, yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name. 9 Look, I will make those who belong to Satan's synagogue—people who say they are Jews but are not, because they lie—come and bow down at your feet. Then they will know that I have loved you.

10 "Because you kept My command to endure, I will also keep you from the time of testing that is coming on the whole world, to test those who live on the earth. 11 Look, I am coming soon. Hold on to what you have, so no one takes your crown. 12 The one who overcomes, I will make a pillar in the temple of My God, and he will never leave it. I will write on him the name of My God and the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down from heaven from My God. I will also write on him My new name.

13 "The one who has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

Message to the Church in Laodicea

14 "To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: These things says the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Originator of God's creation. 15 I know your deeds. You are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were one or the other. 16 So because you are lukewarm—neither hot nor cold—I am about to spit you out of My mouth.

17 "You say, 'I am rich; I have grown wealthy and need nothing.' But you do not know that you are miserable, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked. 18 I advise you to buy from Me gold refined by fire, so you can be truly rich. Buy white clothes so your shameful nakedness will not show. Also buy eye medicine to put on your eyes so you can see. 19 I correct and train everyone I love. So be eager and turn back to Me.

20 "Look, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in and eat with him, and he will eat with Me. 21 To the one who overcomes, I will give the right to sit with Me on My throne, just as I overcame and sat down with My Father on His throne.

22 "The one who has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
