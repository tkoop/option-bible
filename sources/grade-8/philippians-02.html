United in Christ

1 So if there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any sharing in the Spirit, and any kindness and mercy, 2 then make my joy complete by being of one mind. Have the same love, be united in spirit, and think the same way.

3 Do nothing out of selfish ambition or pride. Instead, in humility, think of others as more important than yourselves. 4 Let each of you look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others.

Christ's Humility and Glory

5 Have the same attitude among yourselves that Christ Jesus had. 6 Though He was in God's very nature, He did not cling to His equality with God. 7 Instead, He gave up His divine privileges and became a servant. He was born as a human. 8 And being found as a man, He humbled Himself and obeyed God all the way to death, even death on a cross.

9 Because of this, God raised Him to the highest place and gave Him the name that is above every other name. 10 So that at the name of Jesus every knee will bow, in heaven, on earth, and under the earth. 11 And every tongue will say that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

Living as God's Children

12 So, my dear friends, just as you have always obeyed, not only when I was with you but even more now that I am away, work hard to live out the salvation God has given you, with deep respect and awe. 13 For God is the One working in you, giving you the desire and the power to do what pleases Him.

14 Do everything without complaining or arguing. 15 Then you will be pure and without fault, God's children without blame in the middle of a crooked and twisted generation. Among them, you shine like lights in the world.

16 Hold tightly to the message that gives life. Then on the day Christ returns, I can be proud that my work and effort were not wasted. 17 Even if I give my life for your faith, I am glad and share your joy. 18 In the same way, you should be glad and share my joy.

Timothy and Epaphroditus

19 I hope, with the Lord Jesus' help, to send Timothy to you soon, so I can be encouraged by news about you. 20 I have no one else like him who truly cares about your well-being. 21 Everyone else looks out for their own interests, not Christ's. 22 But you know how he has proven himself. He served with me in spreading the good news, like a son serving with his father. 23 So I hope to send him as soon as I know how things will turn out for me. 24 I trust in the Lord that I will come to you soon too.

25 I felt I had to send Epaphroditus back to you. He is my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier. He is also your messenger and the one who helped meet my needs. 26 He has been longing to see all of you, and he was upset because you heard he was sick. 27 He really was sick, almost to the point of death. But God had mercy on him, and on me too, so I would not have grief on top of grief. 28 So I am even more eager to send him, so you can be glad to see him again and I can worry less. 29 Welcome him in the Lord with great joy, and honor people like him. 30 He almost died for Christ's work. He risked his life to make up for the help you were unable to give me.
