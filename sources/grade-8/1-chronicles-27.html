David's Monthly Army Divisions

1 The Israelites were listed by number: family leaders, commanders of thousands and hundreds, and officers who served the king in every matter of the divisions. These divisions came and went month by month through the year, and each one had 24,000 men. 2 Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was in charge of the first division for the first month, and his division had 24,000 men. 3 He was from the family of Perez and was chief of all the army captains for the first month. 4 Dodai the Ahohite was in charge of the division for the second month, and Mikloth was its leader. That division had 24,000 men. 5 Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest was commander for the third month. He was a chief leader, and his division had 24,000 men. 6 This Benaiah was one of the Thirty mighty men and was their leader. His son Ammizabad was in charge of his division. 7 Asahel, Joab's brother, was commander for the fourth month, and his son Zebadiah followed him. His division had 24,000 men. 8 Shamhuth the Izrahite was commander for the fifth month, and his division had 24,000 men.

9 Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite was commander for the sixth month, and his division had 24,000 men. 10 Helez the Pelonite, from the family of Ephraim, was commander for the seventh month, and his division had 24,000 men. 11 Sibbecai the Hushathite, a Zerahite, was commander for the eighth month, and his division had 24,000 men. 12 Abiezer the Anathothite, a Benjamite, was commander for the ninth month, and his division had 24,000 men. 13 Maharai the Netophathite, a Zerahite, was commander for the tenth month, and his division had 24,000 men. 14 Benaiah the Pirathonite, from the family of Ephraim, was commander for the eleventh month, and his division had 24,000 men. 15 Heldai the Netophathite, from Othniel's family, was commander for the twelfth month, and his division had 24,000 men.

Leaders of Israel's Tribes

16 Over Israel's tribes: Eliezer son of Zichri was leader of the Reubenites, and Shephatiah son of Maacah was leader of the Simeonites. 17 Hashabiah son of Kemuel was leader of the Levites, and Zadok was leader of Aaron's line. 18 Elihu, one of David's brothers, was leader of Judah, and Omri son of Michael was leader of Issachar. 19 Ishmaiah son of Obadiah was leader of Zebulun, and Jerimoth son of Azriel was leader of Naphtali. 20 Hoshea son of Azaziah was leader of the sons of Ephraim, and Joel son of Pedaiah was leader of the half-tribe of Manasseh. 21 Iddo son of Zechariah was leader of the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, and Jaasiel son of Abner was leader of Benjamin. 22 Azarel son of Jeroham was leader of Dan. These were the leaders of Israel's tribes.

David's Unfinished Census

23 David did not count those younger than twenty years old, because the LORD had promised to make Israel as numerous as the stars in the sky. 24 Joab son of Zeruiah started the census, but he did not finish, because this brought wrath on Israel. The total was not recorded in the official records of King David.

Royal Officials and Counselors

25 Azmaveth son of Adiel was in charge of the king's treasuries. Jonathan son of Uzziah was in charge of the storehouses in the fields, cities, villages, and towers. 26 Ezri son of Chelub was in charge of the field workers who farmed the land. 27 Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards. Zabdi the Shiphmite was in charge of the vineyard produce for the wine cellars. 28 Baal-hanan the Gederite was in charge of the olive and sycamore trees in the lowlands. Joash was in charge of the oil stores. 29 Shitrai the Sharonite was in charge of the herds grazing in Sharon. Shaphat son of Adlai was in charge of the herds in the valleys. 30 Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels. Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the donkeys. 31 Jaziz the Hagrite was in charge of the flocks. All these men were officials over King David's property. 32 Jonathan, David's uncle, was an advisor, a man of understanding, and a scribe. Jehiel son of Hachmoni was with the king's sons. 33 Ahithophel was the king's counselor, and Hushai the Archite was the king's friend. 34 After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah, and then Abiathar. Joab was commander of the king's army.
