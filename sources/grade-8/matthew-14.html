Herod Kills John the Baptist

1 At that time Herod the ruler heard the news about Jesus. 2 He said to his servants, "This is John the Baptist. He has risen from the dead, and that is why these powers are at work in him."

3 Herod had arrested John, tied him up, and put him in prison. He did this because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife. 4 John had told him, "It is not lawful for you to have her." 5 Herod wanted to kill him, but he was afraid of the crowd, because they thought John was a prophet.

6 On Herod's birthday, Herodias's daughter danced for the guests, and Herod was pleased. 7 So he promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked. 8 After her mother told her what to ask for, she said, "Give me John the Baptist's head on a platter right here." 9 The king was sad, but because of his oaths and his guests, he ordered it to be done. 10 He sent someone and had John beheaded in the prison. 11 John's head was brought on a platter and given to the girl, and she took it to her mother. 12 John's disciples came, took his body, buried it, and then went and told Jesus.

Jesus Feeds the Five Thousand

13 When Jesus heard this, He left there by boat for a quiet place alone. But the crowds heard about it and followed Him on foot from the towns. 14 When He came ashore, He saw a large crowd. He felt sorry for them and healed their sick.

15 That evening the disciples came to Him and said, "This is a remote place, and the hour is already late. Send the crowds away so they can go to the villages and buy food for themselves."

16 Jesus said, "They do not need to go away. You give them something to eat."

17 They said, "We have only five loaves of bread and two fish here."

18 Jesus said, "Bring them here to Me." 19 Then He told the crowds to sit down on the grass. He took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to heaven, blessed them, broke the loaves, and gave them to the disciples. The disciples gave them to the crowds. 20 Everyone ate and was full. They picked up twelve baskets of leftover pieces. 21 About five thousand men ate, not counting women and children.

Jesus Walks on the Water

22 Right away He made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of Him to the other side, while He sent the crowds away. 23 After He sent the crowds away, He went up on a mountain by Himself to pray. When evening came, He was there alone. 24 By now the boat was far from land. It was being tossed by the waves, because the wind was against them.

25 During the fourth watch of the night, Jesus came to them, walking on the sea. 26 When the disciples saw Him walking on the sea, they were terrified and said, "It is a ghost!" They cried out in fear.

27 But Jesus spoke to them at once: "Take courage! It is I. Do not be afraid."

28 Peter answered, "Lord, if it is You, tell me to come to You on the water."

29 Jesus said, "Come." Peter got out of the boat, walked on the water, and came toward Jesus. 30 But when he saw the strong wind, he was afraid. As he began to sink, he cried out, "Lord, save me!"

31 Jesus reached out His hand at once and caught him. He said, "You of little faith, why did you doubt?"

32 When they got into the boat, the wind died down. 33 Then those in the boat worshiped Him and said, "Truly You are the Son of God."

Healing at Gennesaret

34 They crossed over and came to land at Gennesaret. 35 When the men of that place recognized Him, they sent word to all the surrounding area. People brought all who were sick to Him. 36 They begged Him to let them just touch the edge of His cloak, and everyone who touched it was healed.
