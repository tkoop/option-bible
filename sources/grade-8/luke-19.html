Zacchaeus Meets Jesus

1 Jesus entered Jericho and was passing through. 2 A man named Zacchaeus was there. He was a chief tax collector and was rich. 3 He wanted to see who Jesus was, but he could not because of the crowd, since he was short. 4 So he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus, because Jesus was going to pass that way. 5 “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, because I must stay at your house today.” 6 So Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed Him with joy. 7 When everyone saw this, they grumbled and said, “He has gone to be the guest of a sinful man.” 8 But Zacchaeus stood up and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, I give half of my possessions to the poor. And if I have cheated anyone, I will pay back four times as much.” 9 “Today salvation has come to this house, because he too is a son of Abraham. 10 For the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost.”

The Parable of the Ten Minas

11 While they were listening, Jesus told them a parable, because He was near Jerusalem and the people thought the kingdom of God would appear right away. 12 “A nobleman went to a far country to get a kingdom for himself and then return. 13 Before he left, he called ten of his servants and gave them ten minas. He told them, ‘Put this money to work until I come back.’ 14 But his subjects hated him and sent a message after him, saying, ‘We do not want this man to rule over us.’ 15 When he returned after receiving the kingdom, he sent for the servants he had given the money to, to find out what they had gained. 16 The first one came and said, ‘Sir, your mina has earned ten more minas.’ 17 The master said, ‘Well done, good servant! Because you have been faithful in a small thing, you will have authority over ten cities.’ 18 The second came and said, ‘Sir, your mina has earned five minas.’ 19 The master said to him, ‘You too will rule over five cities.’ 20 Then another servant came and said, ‘Sir, here is your mina, which I kept put away in a cloth. 21 I was afraid of you, because you are a hard man. You take what you did not put there and harvest what you did not plant.’ 22 The master said, ‘I will judge you, you wicked servant! You knew that I am a hard man, taking what I did not put there and harvesting what I did not plant.’ 23 Then why didn’t you put my money in the bank? When I came back, I could have collected it with interest.’ 24 Then he said to those standing there, ‘Take the mina from him and give it to the one who has ten minas.’ 25 They said, ‘Sir, he already has ten minas.’ 26 He replied, ‘I tell you, everyone who has will be given more, but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away. 27 But bring those enemies of mine who did not want me to rule over them, and kill them in front of me.’”

Jesus Enters Jerusalem

28 After He said this, Jesus went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem. 29 As He came near Bethphage and Bethany at the hill called the Mount of Olives, He sent two of His disciples. 30 “Go into the village ahead of you. As you enter it, you will find a colt tied there that no one has ever ridden. Untie it and bring it here. 31 If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ say, ‘The Lord needs it.’” 32 Those who were sent went and found it just as He had told them. 33 As they were untying the colt, its owners asked them, “Why are you untying the colt?” 34 They replied, “The Lord needs it.” 35 They brought it to Jesus, threw their cloaks on the colt, and had Jesus ride on it. 36 As He went along, people spread their cloaks on the road.

37 As He was getting close to the place where the road goes down the Mount of Olives, the whole crowd of disciples began to praise God joyfully and loudly for all the miracles they had seen. 38 They said, “Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”

39 Some of the Pharisees in the crowd said to Him, “Teacher, tell Your disciples to be quiet.”

40 “I tell you, if they keep quiet, the stones will cry out.”

41 As He came near and saw the city, He wept over it. 42 “If you had known today what would bring you peace—but now it is hidden from your eyes. 43 Days will come against you when your enemies will build a wall around you. They will surround you and trap you on every side. 44 They will level you to the ground, you and your children with you. They will not leave one stone on another, because you did not recognize the time when God came to help you.”

Jesus Clears the Temple

45 Then He went into the temple and began driving out those who were selling. 46 “It is written, ‘My house will be a house of prayer,’ but you have made it a den of robbers.” 47 Every day He was teaching in the temple. But the chief priests, teachers of the law, and leaders of the people were trying to kill Him. 48 Yet they could not find a way to do it, because all the people were hanging on His words.
