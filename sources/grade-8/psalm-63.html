A Psalm of David in the Wilderness of Judah

1 God, You are my God; I seek You eagerly. My soul thirsts for You; my body longs for You in a dry, weary land with no water. 2 I have seen You in the holy place, to see Your power and Your glory. 3 Because Your faithful love is better than life, my lips will praise You. 4 So I will bless You as long as I live; in Your name I will lift my hands. 5 My soul will be satisfied as with the richest food; with joyful lips my mouth will praise You.

6 When I remember You on my bed, I think about You through the night watches. 7 Because You have been my help, I will sing for joy in the shadow of Your wings. 8 My soul clings to You; Your right hand holds me up.

9 But those who want to kill me will go down into the depths of the earth. 10 They will be handed over to the sword; they will become food for jackals. 11 The king will rejoice in God; all who swear by Him will boast, for the mouths of liars will be silenced.
