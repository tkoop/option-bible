Babylonian Messengers Visit Hezekiah

1At that time, Merodach-Baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, because he had heard that Hezekiah had been sick and had gotten better. 2Hezekiah was glad to see them and showed them his treasure house—the silver, the gold, the spices, the fine oil, all his weapons, and everything in his storerooms. Nothing in his house or in his whole kingdom was left hidden from them.

Isaiah Questions Hezekiah

3Then Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and asked him, “What did these men say? And where did they come from?” Hezekiah answered, “They came to me from far away, from Babylon.” 4Isaiah asked, “What did they see in your house?” Hezekiah replied, “They saw everything in my house. I did not hide anything in my storerooms from them.”

The LORD Warns of Future Exile

5Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Listen to the word of the LORD of Hosts: 6‘The days are coming when everything in your house, and everything your fathers saved up until now, will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left,’ says the LORD. 7‘Some of your own sons, born to you, will be taken away. They will serve as eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.’”

Hezekiah Accepts the Warning

8Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The word of the LORD that you spoke is good.” For he thought, “There will be peace and truth in my days.”
