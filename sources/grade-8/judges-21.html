Israel Grieves for Benjamin

1 The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, "None of us will give his daughter to a Benjamite as a wife." 2 The people went to Bethel and sat there before God until evening. They cried out and wept bitterly. 3 They said, "Why, the LORD God of Israel, has this happened in Israel? Why is one tribe missing today?" 4 The next day the people got up early and built an altar there. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

5 Then the Israelites asked, "Who among all the tribes of Israel did not come up to the the LORD's gathering?" They had sworn a serious oath: anyone who did not come up to the the LORD at Mizpah would be put to death. 6 The Israelites felt sorry for their brother Benjamin. They said, "Today one tribe has been cut off from Israel." 7 They asked, "What can we do to get wives for the men who are left? We swore by the the LORD that we would not give them our daughters."

Benjamin Gets Wives from Jabesh-gilead

8 They asked, "Which tribe of Israel did not come up to the the LORD at Mizpah?" Then they found that no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the gathering. 9 When they counted the people, not one person from Jabesh-gilead was there. 10 The community sent 12,000 brave soldiers and told them, "Go and kill the people of Jabesh-gilead with your swords, even the women and children." 11 "Here is what you must do: destroy every male and every woman who has been with a man." 12 They found four hundred young women in Jabesh-gilead who had never been with a man. They brought them to the camp at Shiloh, in the land of Canaan.

13 The whole community sent messengers to the Benjamites at the Rock of Rimmon and offered them peace. 14 Benjamin came back then, and the Israelites gave them the women they had spared from Jabesh-gilead. But there were not enough women for all of them. 15 The people felt sorry for Benjamin, because the the LORD had broken one of Israel's tribes.

16 The elders of the community asked, "What can we do to find wives for the men who are left? The women of Benjamin have been wiped out. 17 The men of Benjamin who are left must keep their family line, so one tribe will not disappear from Israel. 18 But we cannot give them our daughters, because the Israelites swore, 'Anyone who gives a wife to a Benjamite is cursed.'"

Benjamin Gets Wives from Shiloh

19 They said, "There is a yearly festival to the the LORD in Shiloh. It is north of Bethel, east of the road that goes from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah." 20 They told the Benjamites, "Go and hide in the vineyards." 21 "Watch, and when the young women of Shiloh come out to dance, rush out of the vineyards. Each man can grab a wife from the Shiloh women and take her back to Benjamin." 22 "If their fathers or brothers come to complain, we will say, 'Please be kind to them. We did not take a wife for each man during the war, and you did not give them wives either, so you will not be at fault.'"

23 The Benjamites did that. They took as many wives as they needed from the dancers, carried them off, went back to their land, rebuilt their cities, and lived there. 24 Then the Israelites left and went back to their own tribes and families, each to his own land.

Israel Has No King

25 In those days Israel had no king, and everyone did what seemed right to him.
