Jesus Is Transfigured on the Mountain

1 Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John—James's brother—and led them up a high mountain by themselves. 2 There He was changed before their eyes. His face shone like the sun, and His clothes became as white as light. 3 Suddenly Moses and Elijah appeared and were talking with Him.

4 Peter said to Jesus, "Lord, it's good for us to be here. If You want, I'll make three shelters here—one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

5 While he was still speaking, a bright cloud covered them. A voice from the cloud said, "This is My Son, whom I love. I'm pleased with Him. Listen to Him!" 6 When the disciples heard this, they fell facedown and were very afraid.

7 Jesus came and touched them. "Get up," He said. "Don't be afraid." 8 When they looked up, they saw no one except Jesus.

9 As they were going down the mountain, Jesus told them, "Don't tell anyone about this vision until the Son of Man rises from the dead."

10 The disciples asked Him, "Then why do the teachers of the law say that Elijah must come first?"

11 He answered, "Elijah does come, and he'll restore all things. 12 But I tell you that Elijah has already come. People didn't recognize him, and they did to him whatever they wanted. In the same way, the Son of Man will suffer at their hands."

13 Then the disciples understood that He was talking about John the Baptist.

Jesus Heals a Boy with a Demon

14 When they came to the crowd, a man came up to Jesus and knelt before Him.

15 "Lord," he said, "have mercy on my son. He has seizures and suffers badly. He often falls into the fire or into the water. 16 I brought him to Your disciples, but they couldn't heal him."

17 Jesus answered, "You unbelieving and twisted generation! How long will I be with you? How long will I put up with you? Bring him here to Me."

18 Jesus scolded the demon, and it came out of the boy. The boy was healed from that hour.

19 Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and asked, "Why couldn't we drive it out?"

20 He said, "Because of your little faith. Truly I tell you, if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you."

Jesus Predicts His Death Again

22 While they were staying in Galilee, Jesus told them, "The Son of Man is going to be handed over to people. 23 They'll kill Him, and on the third day He'll be raised." The disciples were very sad.

The Temple Tax

24 When they came to Capernaum, the temple tax collectors came to Peter and asked, "Does your teacher pay the temple tax?"

25 "Yes," Peter said.

When Peter came into the house, Jesus spoke first. "What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth collect taxes or fees—from their own children or from strangers?"

26 "From strangers," Peter said.

Jesus said, "Then the children are free. 27 But so we won't offend them, go to the lake and throw in a hook. Take the first fish you catch. Open its mouth, and you will find a coin. Take it and give it to them for Me and for you."
