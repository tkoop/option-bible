A Prayer for God to Come Down

1 If only You would tear open the heavens and come down, so the mountains would shake before You! 2 Fire burns brushwood, and fire makes water boil. Make Your name known to Your enemies, so the nations would tremble before You! 3 When You did amazing things we did not expect, You came down, and the mountains shook before You.

Our Sin and Need for Rescue

4 Since long ago, no one has heard or listened, and no eye has seen any God besides You, who does such things for those who wait for Him. 5 You meet those who are glad to do right and who remember You by walking in Your ways. Yet You were angry because we sinned. We have been in our sins a long time. Can we still be saved? 6 All of us have become unclean, and all our good deeds are like dirty rags. We all fade like a leaf, and our sins blow us away like the wind. 7 No one calls on Your name or tries to hold on to You, because You have hidden Your face from us and let our sins sweep us away.

God Is Our Father and Potter

8 Yet You, the LORD, are our Father. We are the clay, and You are our potter. We are all the work of Your hand. 9 the LORD, do not be angry with us beyond measure, and do not remember our sin forever. Look, we are all Your people.

The Ruined Temple

10 Your holy cities have become a desert; Zion has become a desert, and Jerusalem is in ruins. 11 Our holy and beautiful temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned with fire, and all our treasured places lie in ruins. 12 After all this, will You still hold back, the LORD? Will You keep silent and let us suffer beyond measure?
