The LORD Gives Rules for Cleansing a Skin Disease

1 Then the LORD said to Moses,

“2 This is the law for a person who has a skin disease when he is made clean: He must be brought to the priest.

“3 The priest must go outside the camp and look at him. If the skin disease has healed, 4 the priest will order that two live clean birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop be brought for the person being made clean.

“5 The priest will order that one of the birds be killed over fresh water in a clay pot. 6 He must take the live bird, along with the cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop, and dip them in the blood of the bird killed over the fresh water. 7 Seven times he must sprinkle the person being made clean from the skin disease. Then he must announce that the person is clean and let the live bird go free in the open field.

“8 The person being made clean must wash his clothes, shave off all his hair, and bathe in water. Then he will be clean. After that he may come into the camp, but he must stay outside his tent for seven days. 9 On the seventh day he must shave off all his hair—his head, beard, eyebrows, and all the rest of his hair. He must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and then he will be clean.

Offerings for the Person Being Made Clean

“10 On the eighth day he must take two male lambs without flaws, a female lamb one year old without flaws, three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and one log of oil. 11 The priest who makes him clean must present the person and these offerings before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. 12 Then the priest must take one of the male lambs and offer it as a guilt offering, along with the log of oil. He must wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. 13 He must kill the lamb in the holy place where the sin offering and burnt offering are killed. Like the sin offering, the guilt offering belongs to the priest. It is most holy.

“14 The priest must take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 15 Then the priest must take some of the log of oil and pour it into his left palm. 16 The priest must dip his right index finger into the oil in his left palm and sprinkle some of the oil with his finger seven times before the LORD. 17 The priest must put some of the oil left over on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot, on top of the blood of the guilt offering. 18 The priest must put the rest of the oil in his palm on the head of the person being made clean. This makes atonement for him before the LORD.

“19 Then the priest must offer the sin offering and make atonement for the person being made clean from his uncleanness. After that, the priest must kill the burnt offering. 20 He must offer the burnt offering on the altar, along with the grain offering, to make atonement for him, and he will be clean.

“21 If the person being made clean is poor and cannot afford these offerings, he must take one male lamb as a guilt offering to be waved, to make atonement for him. He must also take a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and a log of oil. 22 He must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford—one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering. 23 On the eighth day he must bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting before the LORD for his cleansing. 24 The priest must take the lamb for the guilt offering, along with the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. 25 Then he must kill the lamb for the guilt offering and take some of its blood and put it on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

“26 Then the priest must pour some of the oil into his left palm 27 and sprinkle with his right index finger some of the oil in his left palm seven times before the LORD. 28 He must also put some of the oil on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot, in the same places as the blood of the guilt offering. 29 The priest must put the rest of the oil in his palm on the head of the person being made clean, to make atonement for him before the LORD. 30 Then he must offer one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford. 31 He must offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, along with the grain offering. In this way the priest will make atonement before the LORD for the person being made clean. 32 This is the law for someone who has a skin disease and cannot afford the cost of his cleansing.”

33 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

Rules for Mold in a House

“34 When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as your own land, I will put mold in a house in the land you own. 35 The owner of the house must go and tell the priest, ‘Something like mold has appeared in my house.’ 36 The priest must order that the house be emptied before he goes in to look at the mold, so that nothing in the house becomes unclean. After this, the priest may enter to inspect the house.

“37 He must look at the mold. If it is greenish or reddish spots that seem to be deeper than the wall, 38 the priest must go out to the doorway of the house and close it up for seven days. 39 On the seventh day the priest must return and inspect it. If the mold has spread in the walls of the house,

“40 he must order that the stones with the mold be removed and thrown into an unclean place outside the city. 41 He must have the inside of the house scraped all around, and the scraped plaster must be dumped in an unclean place outside the city. 42 Then they must take other stones to replace the ones removed and take new mortar to replaster the house.

“43 If the mold comes back in the house after the stones have been removed and the house has been scraped and replastered, 44 the priest must come and inspect it. If the mold has spread in the house, it is a bad mold. The house is unclean. 45 It must be torn down—its stones, wood, and all the plaster—and taken outside the city to an unclean place.

“46 Anyone who enters the house during any of the days it is closed up will be unclean until evening. 47 Anyone who sleeps in the house or eats in it must wash his clothes. 48 If, however, the priest comes and inspects it and the mold has not spread in the house after it was plastered, the priest must announce that the house is clean, because the mold is healed.

“49 To make the house clean, he must take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop. 50 He must kill one of the birds over fresh water in a clay pot. 51 Then he must take the cedar wood, hyssop, scarlet yarn, and live bird, dip them in the blood of the killed bird and in the fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times.

“52 In this way he will cleanse the house with the blood of the bird, the fresh water, the live bird, the cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet yarn. 53 Then he must let the live bird go free in the open field outside the city. In this way he will make atonement for the house, and it will be clean.

Summary of the Laws About Skin Diseases

“54 This is the law for any infectious skin disease, for a scaly outbreak, 55 for mold in clothing or in a house, 56 and for a swelling, rash, or bright spot, 57 to decide when something is clean or unclean. This is the law about skin diseases.”
