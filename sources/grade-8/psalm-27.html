The LORD Is My Light and Salvation

1 The LORD is my light and my salvation. Who should I fear? The LORD is the strength of my life. Who can make me afraid? 2 When evil people came close to attack me, my enemies and foes stumbled and fell. 3 Even if an army camps against me, my heart will not be afraid. Even if war rises against me, I will trust in Him.

Seeking the LORD's Presence

4 I have asked the LORD for one thing, and that is what I seek: to live in the LORD's house all my life, to see how beautiful the LORD is, and to look for guidance in His temple. 5 For in the day of trouble He will hide me in His shelter. He will conceal me in the secret place of His tent. He will lift me up on a rock. 6 Now my head will be lifted above my enemies all around me. In His tent I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy. I will sing and make music to the LORD.

A Prayer for Help

7 Hear my voice when I call, the LORD. Be gracious to me and answer me. 8 My heart says to You, “Seek His face.” The LORD, I will seek Your face. 9 Do not hide Your face from me. Do not turn Your servant away in anger. You have been my help. Do not leave me or abandon me, O God of my salvation. 10 Even if my father and mother leave me, the LORD will take me in. 11 Teach me Your way, the LORD, and lead me on a straight path because of my enemies. 12 Do not hand me over to the desire of my foes, for false witnesses have risen against me, breathing out violence.

Waiting on the LORD

13 I would have lost hope unless I believed I would see the LORD's goodness in the land of the living. 14 Wait for the LORD. Be strong, and let your heart take courage. Wait for the LORD.
