Jesus Sends Out the Seventy-Two

1 After this, the Lord chose seventy-two others and sent them two by two ahead of Him to every town and place He was about to go. 2 He told them, "The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Ask the Lord of the harvest to send out workers into His harvest. 3 Go! I am sending you out like lambs among wolves. 4 Do not carry a money bag, a travel bag, or sandals, and do not greet anyone on the road."

5 "When you enter a house, first say, 'Peace to this house.' 6 If someone there loves peace, your peace will rest on him. If not, it will come back to you. 7 Stay in that same house, eating and drinking what they give you, because the worker deserves his pay. Do not move from house to house."

8 "When you enter a town and the people welcome you, eat what is set before you. 9 Heal the sick there and tell them, 'God's kingdom has come near to you.'"

10 "But when you enter a town and the people do not welcome you, go into its streets and say, 11 'We wipe off even the dust of your town that stuck to our feet. But know this: God's kingdom has come near.' 12 I tell you that on that day it will be easier for Sodom than for that town."

13 "Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! If the miracles done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago. 14 But it will be easier for Tyre and Sidon at the judgment than for you."

15 "And you, Capernaum, will you be lifted up to heaven? No, you will go down to Hades. 16 Whoever listens to you listens to Me, and whoever rejects you rejects Me. Whoever rejects Me rejects the One who sent Me."

The Seventy-Two Return with Joy

17 The seventy-two returned with joy and said, "Lord, even the demons obey us when we use Your name!" 18 He said to them, "I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven."

19 "Look, I have given you power to walk on snakes and scorpions, and power over all the enemy's strength. Nothing will harm you. 20 But do not rejoice that the spirits obey you. Rejoice instead that your names are written in heaven."

21 At that same hour, Jesus was full of joy through the Holy Spirit and said, "I praise You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You hid these things from the wise and learned, and showed them to little children. Yes, Father, this is what You wanted. 22 My Father has given Me everything. No one knows who the Son is except the Father, and no one knows who the Father is except the Son and those the Son chooses to reveal Him to."

23 Then He turned to His disciples and said privately, "Blessed are the eyes that see what you see. 24 I tell you that many prophets and kings wanted to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it."

The Good Samaritan

25 A law expert stood up to test Him and asked, "Teacher, what must I do to gain eternal life?" 26 He asked him, "What is written in the Law? How do you read it?"

27 He answered, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your strength, and with all your mind. Love your neighbor as yourself." 28 He said to him, "You have answered correctly. Do this, and you will live."

29 But he wanted to show he was right, so he asked Jesus, "And who is my neighbor?"

30 Jesus replied, "A man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho. Robbers attacked him, stripped him, beat him, and left him half dead. 31 By chance, a priest was going down that road. When he saw the man, he passed by on the other side. 32 A Levite also came to that place. When he saw the man, he passed by on the other side."

33 "But a Samaritan who was traveling came to where the man was. When he saw him, he felt deep pity. 34 He went to him, bandaged his wounds, and poured oil and wine on them. Then he put the man on his own animal and took him to an inn, where he cared for him."

35 "The next day he took out two silver coins and gave them to the innkeeper. He said, 'Take care of him. When I come back, I will pay you back for anything extra you spend.' 36 Which of these three do you think was a neighbor to the man who fell among robbers?"

37 The man said, "The one who showed mercy to him."

He said to him, "Go and do the same."

Mary and Martha

38 As they traveled, Jesus entered a village. A woman named Martha welcomed Him into her home. 39 She had a sister named Mary, who sat at the Lord's feet and listened to His teaching.

40 Martha was busy with all the work to be done. She came to Jesus and said, "Lord, don't You care that my sister has left me to serve alone? Tell her to help me."

41 The Lord answered, "Martha, Martha, you are worried and upset about many things. 42 But only one thing is needed. Mary has chosen what is better, and it will not be taken away from her."
