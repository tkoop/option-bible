A Warning About the Forbidden Woman

1 My son, pay attention to my wisdom; turn your ear to my understanding. 2 So your lips may keep knowledge.

3 For the lips of a forbidden woman drip honey, and her speech is smoother than oil. 4 But in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a double-edged sword. 5 Her feet go down to death; her steps lead straight to the grave. 6 She does not think about the path of life; she does not know that her ways are unsteady.

A Father’s Warning

7 So now, my sons, listen to me, and do not turn aside from the words of my mouth. 8 Keep your path far from her; do not go near the door of her house, 9 or you will give your strength to others, and your years to a cruel man. 10 Strangers will feast on your wealth, and your hard work will enrich a stranger’s house.

11 At the end of your life you will groan when your flesh and body are spent. 12 And you will say, “How I hated discipline, and my heart despised correction! 13 I did not listen to my teachers, or turn my ear to my instructors. 14 I was almost ruined among the whole crowd.”

Faithfulness in Marriage

15 Drink water from your own cistern, and running water from your own well. 16 Should your springs overflow into the streets, streams of water in the public squares? 17 Let them be yours alone, never to be shared with strangers.

18 May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth: 19 a loving doe, a graceful deer— may her breasts satisfy you always; may you be captivated by her love forever.

The LORD Sees Every Path

20 Why be captivated, my son, by an adulteress, or embrace the bosom of a stranger? 21 For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD watches all his paths. 22 The sins of the wicked trap him; the cords of his sin entangle him. 23 He dies for lack of discipline, led astray by his own great folly.
