A Call to Return to the LORD

1“Come, let us return to the the LORD. He has torn us apart, but He will heal us. He has wounded us, but He will bandage our wounds. 2After two days He will bring us back to life. On the third day He will raise us up, so we can live in His presence. 3Let us know the LORD. Let us press on to know Him. He will appear as surely as the dawn. He will come to us like rain, like the spring rain that waters the earth.”

The LORD Questions Israel and Judah

4“What shall I do with you, Ephraim? What shall I do with you, Judah? Your faithfulness is like morning fog and like early dew that disappears. 5Therefore I have cut them down by the prophets. I have killed them by the words of My mouth, and My judgments go forth like the light. 6For I desire mercy, not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings. 7But, like Adam, they broke the covenant; there they betrayed Me.”

Israel's Sins Are Exposed

8Gilead is a city of evildoers, stained with blood. 9Like robbers waiting to ambush someone, priests band together. They murder on the road to Shechem and commit terrible crimes. 10In Israel I have seen a horrible thing: Ephraim prostitutes itself, and Israel is defiled.

Judgment and Restoration for Judah

11“Also for you, Judah, a harvest is appointed when I bring My people back from captivity.”
