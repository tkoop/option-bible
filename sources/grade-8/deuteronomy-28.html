Blessings for Obedience

1If you fully obey the the LORD your God and carefully follow all His commands that I am giving you today, the the LORD your God will put you above all the nations on earth. 2All these blessings will come to you and catch up with you, because you obey the the LORD your God.

3You will be blessed in the city and blessed in the country. 4Your children, your crops, and your animals will be blessed. 5Your basket and your kneading bowl will be blessed. 6You will be blessed when you come in and when you go out.

7The The LORD will defeat your enemies who attack you. They will come at you one way and run away seven ways. 8The The LORD will command His blessing on your stores and on everything you do. The The LORD your God will bless you in the land He is giving you.

9The The LORD will make you His holy people, as He promised, if you keep His commands and walk in His ways. 10Then all the peoples of the earth will see that you are called by the the LORD's name, and they will fear you.

11The The LORD will give you plenty of good things: children, animals, and crops in the land He promised your fathers. 12He will open the skies to send rain on your land at the right time and bless all your work. You will lend to many nations, but you will not need to borrow.

13The The LORD will make you the head, not the tail. You will be on top and not on the bottom, if you obey the commands of the the LORD your God that I am giving you today. 14Do not turn aside to the right or left from any of the commands I am giving you today, and do not follow other gods to serve them.

Curses for Disobedience

15But if you do not obey the the LORD your God and carefully follow all His commands and rules that I am giving you today, all these curses will come on you and catch up with you. 16You will be cursed in the city and cursed in the country. 17Your basket and your kneading bowl will be cursed. 18Your children, your crops, your calves, and your lambs will be cursed. 19You will be cursed when you come in and when you go out.

20The The LORD will send curses, confusion, and trouble on everything you do, until you are destroyed and quickly lost because of your evil actions in leaving Him. 21The The LORD will make disease stick to you until He removes you from the land you are entering to possess. 22The The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, burning heat, sword, blight, and mildew. They will chase you until you die. 23The sky above you will be like bronze, and the ground beneath you will be like iron. 24The The LORD will turn the rain on your land into dust and powder. It will fall from the sky until you are destroyed.

25The The LORD will let your enemies defeat you. You will march out against them one way and flee seven ways. You will become a warning to all the kingdoms of the earth. 26Your dead body will be food for birds and wild animals, and no one will scare them away.

27The The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, rash, and itch that cannot be healed. 28The The LORD will strike you with madness, blindness, and confusion of heart. 29At noon you will feel around like a blind person in the dark, and you will not succeed in anything. You will be robbed and cheated all your days, with no one to save you.

30You will be engaged to a woman, but another man will sleep with her. You will build a house but not live in it. You will plant a vineyard but not enjoy its fruit. 31Your ox will be killed before your eyes, but you will not eat it. Your donkey will be stolen from you and not returned. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and no one will save you.

32Your sons and daughters will be given to another people. You will look for them all day with failing eyes, and you will have no power to help. 33A people you do not know will eat the fruit of your land and all your hard work. You will be crushed and treated badly all your days. 34The sights you see will drive you mad.

35The The LORD will strike you with painful boils from the soles of your feet to the top of your head, boils that cannot be healed. 36The The LORD will send you and your king to a nation neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you will serve other gods made of wood and stone. 37You will become a horror, an example, and a joke among all the peoples where the the LORD sends you.

38You will plant much seed in the field, but harvest little, because locusts will eat it. 39You will plant and care for vineyards, but you will not drink the wine or gather the grapes, because worms will eat them. 40You will have olive trees everywhere in your land, but you will not use the oil, because your olives will drop off. 41You will have sons and daughters, but they will not stay with you, because they will be taken captive. 42Locusts will take over all your trees and crops.

43The foreigner among you will rise higher and higher, while you sink lower and lower. 44He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.

45All these curses will come on you, chase you, and catch you until you are destroyed, because you did not obey the the LORD your God or keep His commands and rules. 46They will be a sign and wonder against you and your children forever.

47Because you did not serve the the LORD your God with joy and gladness of heart, even though you had plenty of everything, you will serve your enemies whom the the LORD sends against you. 48You will be hungry, thirsty, naked, and lacking everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until He destroys you.

49The The LORD will bring a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, flying like an eagle. It will be a nation whose language you do not understand. 50It will be a fierce nation that shows no respect for the old and no pity for the young. 51It will eat the young of your animals and the fruit of your land until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine, or oil, no calves or lambs, until it has destroyed you.

Siege and Starvation

52It will surround you in all your cities and all your land, until your high, fortified walls that you trust in come down. It will besiege you in all your cities throughout the land the the LORD your God has given you. 53In the terrible trouble of being surrounded by your enemy, you will eat your own children, the flesh of your sons and daughters whom the the LORD your God has given you.

54The most gentle and kind man among you will have a hard heart toward his brother, his wife, and his remaining children. 55He will not share with any of them the flesh of his children that he is eating, because he has nothing left in the terrible trouble your enemy brings on you in all your cities. 56The most delicate and gentle woman among you, who would not even put her foot on the ground because she was so pampered, will look with evil eyes at her husband, her son, and her daughter. 57She will secretly eat her afterbirth and the children she gives birth to, because she has nothing else in the terrible trouble your enemy brings on you in your cities.

58If you are not careful to obey all the words of this law written in this book, and if you do not fear this glorious and awesome name, the the LORD your God, 59then the the LORD will send terrible plagues on you and your children. They will be long-lasting and severe, sicknesses and diseases that last. 60He will bring back on you all the diseases of Egypt that you feared, and they will cling to you. 61The The LORD will also bring on you every sickness and plague not written in this Book of the Law, until you are destroyed.

62You may have been as many as the stars in the sky, but only a few of you will be left, because you did not obey the the LORD your God. 63Just as the the LORD was glad to do good to you and make you grow, so He will be glad to destroy you and wipe you out. You will be pulled out of the land you are entering to possess.

Exile and Fear

64The The LORD will scatter you among all the peoples from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods of wood and stone that neither you nor your fathers have known. 65Among those nations you will find no rest, and your foot will have no resting place. The The LORD will give you an anxious heart, your failing eyes, and a sick soul. 66Your life will hang in doubt before you. You will be afraid night and day and will not trust that you will live. 67In the morning you will say, "If only it were evening!" and in the evening you will say, "If only it were morning!" because of the fear in your heart and the sights your eyes see.

68The The LORD will send you back to Egypt by ship, by the way I told you that you would never see again. There you will try to sell yourselves to your enemies as slaves, but no one will buy you.
