The LORD Will Ruin the Earth

1 Look, the LORD is going to empty the earth and ruin it. He will twist its surface and scatter the people who live there.

2 Everyone will face the same fate: the people and the priests, servants and masters, maids and mistresses, buyers and sellers, lenders and borrowers, and creditors and debtors.

3 The earth will be completely emptied and stripped bare, because the LORD has spoken this word.

The Earth Mourns

4 The earth dries up and withers. The world wastes away and withers. Even the proud leaders of the earth waste away.

5 The earth is polluted by those who live on it, because they broke God's laws, changed His rules, and broke His lasting agreement.

6 So a curse eats up the earth, and the people who live there are guilty. That is why the people of the earth are burned up, and only a few are left.

7 The new wine is gone, the vine droops, and everyone who was happy is groaning.

8 The happy sound of tambourines stops. The noise of party people ends. The joy of the harp stops.

9 No one sings while drinking wine. Strong drink tastes bitter to those who drink it.

10 The ruined city is broken down. Every house is shut tight, and no one can enter.

11 In the streets people cry out because there is no wine. All joy has faded. The happiness of the earth has gone away.

12 The city is left in ruins. Its gate is smashed to pieces.

13 This is how it will be on earth among the nations: like shaking an olive tree, or like the last grapes picked after the harvest.

Songs of Praise

14 They raise their voices and sing. They shout from the sea about the majesty of the LORD.

15 So give glory to the LORD in the east. In the islands of the sea, honor the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

16 From the ends of the earth we hear songs: "Glory to the Righteous One."

But I said, "I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Woe to me! Traitors betray! Traitors betray completely!"

Terror and the Final Shaking

17 Terror, pits, and traps wait for you, people of the earth!

18 Anyone who runs from the sound of terror will fall into a pit. Anyone who climbs out of the pit will be caught in a trap. The windows of heaven are open, and the foundations of the earth shake.

19 The earth is completely broken. It is split apart. It shakes violently.

20 The earth staggers like a drunk person and sways like a small shelter. Its sin is heavy on it. It falls and will not rise again.

21 In that day, the LORD will punish the armies of heaven above and the kings of the earth below.

22 They will be gathered like prisoners in a pit. They will be locked in a prison. After many days, they will be punished.

23 Then the moon will be ashamed, and the sun will be embarrassed, because the LORD of Hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem. His glory shines before His elders.
