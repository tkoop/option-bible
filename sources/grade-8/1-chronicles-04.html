Descendants of Judah

1 The sons of Judah were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 2 Reaiah, Shobal's son, was the father of Jahath, and Jahath was the father of Ahumai and Lahad. These were the families of the Zorathites.

3 These were Etam's sons: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. Their sister was named Hazzelelponi.

4 Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, Ephrathah's firstborn and the father of Bethlehem.

5 Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives, Helah and Naarah. 6 Naarah gave birth to Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were Naarah's sons. 7 Helah's sons were Zereth, Izhar, and Ethnan.

8 Koz was the father of Anub and Zobebah, and of the families of Aharhel son of Harum.

Jabez's Prayer

9 Jabez was more honored than his brothers. His mother named him Jabez, saying, "I gave birth to him in pain."

10 Jabez called on the God of Israel. He said, "If You will truly bless me and enlarge my territory, and if Your hand is with me, and You keep me from harm, then I won't be in pain." And God granted his request.

11 Chelub, Shuhah's brother, was the father of Mehir, who was the father of Eshton. 12 Eshton was the father of Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, who was the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

13 Kenaz's sons were Othniel and Seraiah. Othniel's son was Hathath. 14 Meonothai was the father of Ophrah. Seraiah was the father of Joab, who was the father of Ge-harashim, because they were craftsmen.

15 Caleb son of Jephunneh had sons named Iru, Elah, and Naam. Elah's son was Kenaz. 16 Jehallelel's sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

17 Ezrah's sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. Mered's wife gave birth to Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, who was the father of Eshtemoa. 18 His Judahite wife gave birth to Jered, who was the father of Gedor; Heber, who was the father of Soco; and Jekuthiel, who was the father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Pharaoh's daughter Bithiah, whom Mered had married. 19 The sons of Hodiah's wife, Naham's sister, were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

20 Shimon's sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. Ishi's sons were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

21 Shelah's sons, Judah's son, were Er, who was the father of Lecah, and Laadah, who was the father of Mareshah. The families of linen workers at Beth-ashbea also came from them.

22 There were also Jokim, the men of Cozeba, Joash, and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem. These records are old. 23 They were potters who lived in Netaim and Gederah. They stayed there with the king and worked for him.

Descendants of Simeon

24 Simeon's sons were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 25 Shaul's son was Shallum. His son was Mibsam. His son was Mishma. 26 Mishma's son was Hammuel. His son was Zaccur. His son was Shimei. 27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters. His brothers didn't have many children, so their whole family didn't grow as much as Judah's children.

28 They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual, 29 Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad, 30 Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag, 31 Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their towns until David became king. 32 Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five towns in all. 33 They also had all the villages around these towns as far as Baal. These were their homes, and they kept their family records.

34 Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah, 35 Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah son of Seraiah son of Asiel, 36 Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah, 37 Ziza son of Shiphi son of Allon son of Jedaiah son of Shimri son of Shemaiah. 38 These men, listed by name, were leaders in their families, and their fathers' houses grew greatly.

39 They went to the entrance of Gedor, to the east side of the valley, to find pasture for their flocks. 40 They found rich, good pasture. The land was wide, quiet, and peaceful, because the Hamites had lived there before. 41 These men, listed by name, came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. They attacked the tents and the Meunites found there. They destroyed them completely, and they have stayed there to this day, because there was pasture for their flocks. 42 Five hundred of these Simeonites went to Mount Seir. Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, led them. 43 They struck down the rest of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.
