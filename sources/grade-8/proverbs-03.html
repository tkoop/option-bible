Trust the the LORD

1 My son, don't forget my teaching. Keep my commands in your heart. 2 They will add years to your life and bring you peace. 3 Don't let kindness and truth leave you. Tie them around your neck, and write them on your heart. 4 Then you will find favor and good understanding with God and people.

5 Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and don't depend on your own understanding. 6 In everything you do, know Him, and He will make your paths straight. 7 Don't think you are wise on your own. Fear the LORD, and turn away from evil. 8 This will bring health to your body and strength to your bones.

9 Honor the LORD with your wealth and with the first part of all your crops. 10 Then your barns will be full, and your winepresses will overflow with new wine.

11 My son, don't reject the LORD's discipline, and don't get tired of His correction. 12 For the LORD corrects those He loves, just as a father corrects the son He delights in.

The Value of Wisdom

13 Happy is the person who finds wisdom and gains understanding. 14 Wisdom brings more profit than silver, and her gain is worth more than gold. 15 She is more precious than rubies. Nothing you want can compare with her.

16 She holds long life in her right hand, and riches and honor in her left. 17 Her ways are pleasant, and all her paths bring peace. 18 She is a tree of life to those who hold on to her. Those who keep her are happy.

The The LORD's Wisdom and Safety

19 The LORD used wisdom to lay the earth's foundation. He used understanding to set the heavens in place. 20 By His knowledge the deep waters burst forth, and the clouds drop dew.

21 My son, don't lose sight of these things. Guard good judgment and common sense. 22 They will give life to you and bring you favor. 23 Then you will walk safely, and your foot won't trip. 24 When you lie down, you won't be afraid, and your sleep will be sweet.

25 Don't be afraid of sudden danger or the ruin that comes on the wicked. 26 For the LORD will be your support, and He will keep your foot from being caught.

Love Your Neighbor

27 Don't hold back good from those who deserve it when you have the power to do it. 28 Don't tell your neighbor, "Go and come back tomorrow; I'll give it to you then," when you already have it with you.

29 Don't plan harm against your neighbor who lives safely beside you. 30 Don't argue with someone for no reason if he hasn't harmed you. 31 Don't envy a violent person, and don't choose any of his ways.

32 For the LORD hates dishonest people, but He shares His plans with those who do right. 33 The LORD's curse is on the wicked person's house, but He blesses the home of those who do right. 34 He mocks those who mock, but He gives grace to the humble. 35 Wise people will inherit honor, but fools will be covered with shame.
