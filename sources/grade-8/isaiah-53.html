Who Has Believed Our Message?

1 Who has believed our message? To whom has the the LORD's power been revealed? 2 He grew up before Him like a tender shoot, like a root from dry ground. He had no beauty or majesty to attract us; there was nothing in His appearance that we should desire Him. 3 He was despised and rejected by people. He was a man of sorrows, familiar with suffering. People turned their faces away from Him. He was despised, and we did not value Him.

He Carried Our Sorrows

4 Surely He took up our weaknesses and carried our pain, yet we thought God had struck Him, beaten Him, and made Him suffer. 5 But He was pierced for our sins and crushed for our guilt. The punishment that brought us peace fell on Him, and by His wounds we are healed. 6 We all have wandered like sheep; each of us has gone his own way. But the the LORD has placed on Him the sin of us all.

Silent Like a Lamb

7 He was treated harshly and suffered, yet He did not say a word. He was led like a lamb to be killed, and like a sheep that is quiet before those who shear it, He did not open His mouth. 8 He was taken away through oppression and unfair judgment. Who thought about His future? He was cut off from the living; He was punished for the sin of My people. 9 He was given a grave with the wicked and a tomb with the rich, although He had done no violence and had spoken no lies.

God's Plan to Save Many

10 Yet it was the the LORD's will to crush Him and cause Him to suffer. When His life is made a guilt offering, He will see His descendants, He will live a long life, and the plan of the the LORD will succeed through Him. 11 After His suffering, He will see life and be satisfied. By His knowledge, My righteous Servant will make many right with God, and He will carry their sins. 12 So I will give Him a place among the great, and He will share the reward with the strong, because He poured out His life to death and was counted among sinners. He bore the sin of many and pleaded for the sinners.
