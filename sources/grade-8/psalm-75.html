A Song of Thanks and God's Judgment

1 We give thanks to you, O God; we give thanks, for Your presence is near. People tell of Your wonderful works.

2 “At the time I choose, I will judge fairly.

3 “When the earth shakes and all who live on it, I hold its pillars steady. Selah

4 “I say to those who brag, ‘Do not brag,’ and to the wicked, ‘Do not lift up your horn.

5 “Do not lift up your horn on high, or speak arrogantly.’”

6 Honor does not come from the east or the west, or from the desert. 7 It is God who judges: He brings one down and lifts another up. 8 For in the hand of the LORD is a cup full of foaming wine, mixed with spices, and He pours it out. All the wicked people of the earth will drink it to the last drop.

9 But I will tell of this forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. 10 I will cut off all the power of the wicked, but the power of those who do right will be raised up.
