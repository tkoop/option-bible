The Blessing of Forgiveness

1 Blessed is the one whose wrongs are forgiven and whose sins are covered. 2 Blessed is the person the the LORD does not count as guilty, and in whose spirit there is no deceit.

3 When I kept silent, my bones wore out through my groaning all day long. 4 For day and night Your hand was heavy on me; my strength dried up like the heat of summer. Selah

5 Then I told You about my sin and did not hide my guilt. I said, “I will confess my wrongs to the the LORD,” and You forgave the guilt of my sin. Selah

6 So let every godly person pray to You while You can be found. Surely, when great waters rise, they will not reach him. 7 You are my hiding place; You keep me safe from trouble; You surround me with songs of rescue. Selah

8 “I will teach you and show you the way to go; I will give you advice; My eye is on you. 9 Do not be like a horse or mule, which has no understanding and must be controlled with a bit and bridle, or it will not come near you.”

10 Many are the sorrows of the wicked, but faithful love surrounds the one who trusts in the the LORD. 11 Be glad in the the LORD and rejoice, you righteous; shout for joy, all you upright in heart.
