Elders Come to Ezekiel

1 Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat down in front of me. 2 Then the word of the LORD came to me:

3 “Son of man, these men have brought their idols into their hearts. They have put the stumbling block of their sin right in front of their faces. Should I be asked by them? I will answer them.”

God Answers Those Who Worship Idols

4 “So speak to them and tell them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: When any Israelite sets up idols in his heart and puts the stumbling block of his sin right in front of his face, and then comes to the prophet, I, the LORD, will answer him according to how many idols he has. 5 I will do this to win back the hearts of the people of Israel, who have all turned away from Me because of their idols.’”

A Call to Turn from Idols

6 “So tell the people of Israel, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: Turn back! Turn away from your idols and from all your disgusting sins. 7 When any Israelite or any foreigner living in Israel turns away from Me and sets up idols in his heart, puts the stumbling block of his sin right in front of his face, and then comes to a prophet to ask something of Me, I, the LORD, will answer him Myself. 8 I will turn against that man. I will make him a warning sign and a story people tell. I will cut him off from My people. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 9 If a prophet is tricked into giving a message, I, the LORD, am the One who tricked that prophet. I will raise My hand against him and destroy him from among My people Israel. 10 They will suffer for their guilt. The guilt of the person who asks will be the same as the guilt of the prophet. 11 Then the people of Israel will no longer wander away from Me. They will no longer make themselves unclean with all their sins. They will be My people, and I will be their God, says the Lord the LORD.’”

Judgment on a Sinful Land

12 The word of the LORD came to me: 13 “Son of man, if a country sins against Me by being unfaithful, I will reach out My hand against it. I will break its supply of bread. I will send famine into it and cut off both people and animals. 14 Even if these three men—Noah, Daniel, and Job—were in it, they would save only themselves by their righteousness, says the Lord the LORD. 15 If I send wild animals through the land and they make it childless and empty, so that no one travels through it because of the animals, 16 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, even those three men would not save sons or daughters. They alone would be saved, but the land would be empty. 17 Or if I bring a sword against that land and say, ‘Let a sword pass through the land,’ and I cut off both people and animals from it, 18 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, those three men would not save sons or daughters. They alone would be saved. 19 Or if I send a plague into that land and pour out My wrath on it in blood, killing both people and animals, 20 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, they would save neither son nor daughter. They would save only themselves by their righteousness. 21 For this is what the Lord the LORD says: How much worse will it be when I send My four terrible judgments—sword, famine, wild animals, and plague—against Jerusalem to cut off both people and animals from it! 22 Yet there will be survivors left in it—sons and daughters. They will come out to you, and you will see how they live and what they do. Then you will be comforted about the disaster I brought on Jerusalem, about everything I brought on it. 23 They will comfort you when you see their ways and their deeds. Then you will know that I did not act without a reason for everything I did in it, says the Lord the LORD.”
