Luke’s Purpose in Writing

1 Many people have tried to write about the things that have been fulfilled among us. 2 They passed on to us what the first eyewitnesses and servants of the message handed down from the beginning. 3 So, after I had carefully studied everything from the start, it seemed good to me to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus. 4 I did this so you could know how sure these things are—the things you were taught.

The Birth of John Is Foretold

5 In the days of King Herod of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah. He was from the priestly group of Abijah. His wife was Elizabeth, from Aaron’s family. 6 Both were right with God. They lived without blame in all the Lord’s commands and rules. 7 But they had no child, because Elizabeth could not have children. Both were very old.

8 One day, while Zechariah was serving as priest before God in his group’s turn, 9 he was chosen by lot, as priests usually were, to go into the Lord’s temple and burn incense. 10 The whole crowd of people was praying outside while the incense was offered.

11 Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the incense altar. 12 Zechariah was shaken and afraid when he saw him.

13 But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will have a son, and you will name him John. 14 He will bring you joy and gladness, and many people will be glad when he is born. 15 He will be great in the Lord’s sight. He will not drink wine or strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he is born. 16 He will turn many of the people of Israel back to the Lord their God. 17 He will go before the Lord in the spirit and power of Elijah. He will turn the hearts of fathers to their children. He will turn people who do not obey to the wise thinking of those who do right. He will make ready a people prepared for the Lord.”

18 Zechariah said to the angel, “How can I know this? I am an old man, and my wife is old too.”

19 The angel answered, “I am Gabriel. I stand before God, and I was sent to speak to you and bring you this good news. 20 But now you will be silent. You will not be able to speak until these things happen, because you did not believe my words. They will come true at the right time.”

21 The people were waiting for Zechariah. They wondered why he stayed so long in the temple. 22 When he came out, he could not speak to them. They knew he had seen a vision in the temple. He kept making signs to them, and he stayed unable to speak.

23 When his time of service was over, he went home. 24 After that, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She stayed hidden for five months. 25 She said, “The Lord has done this for me. He has shown me favor and taken away my shame among people.”

The Birth of Jesus Is Foretold

26 In the sixth month, God sent the angel Gabriel to a town in Galilee called Nazareth. 27 He was sent to a virgin named Mary. She was engaged to a man named Joseph, from David’s family.

28 The angel came to her and said, “Greetings, you who are highly favored! The Lord is with you.” 29 Mary was troubled by his words and wondered what kind of greeting this was.

30 The angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary. You have found favor with God. 31 You will become pregnant and have a son. You will name Him Jesus. 32 He will be great. He will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David. 33 He will rule over Jacob’s family forever. His kingdom will never end.”

34 Mary said to the angel, “How will this happen? I have not been with a man.”

35 The angel answered, “The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will cover you. So the holy Child will be called the Son of God. 36 Look, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age. This is the sixth month for her, the one called unable to have children. 37 For nothing is impossible with God.”

38 Mary said, “I am the Lord’s servant. Let it happen to me as you have said.” Then the angel left her.

Mary Visits Elizabeth and John Is Born

39 Soon after that, Mary got up and traveled quickly to a town in the hill country of Judea. 40 She went into Zechariah’s home and greeted Elizabeth.

41 When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped in her womb. Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. 42 She called out loudly, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the child you are carrying! 43 Why am I so favored that the mother of my Lord should come to me? 44 As soon as I heard your greeting, the baby in my womb leaped for joy. 45 Blessed is she who believed that the Lord would keep His promise to her.”

46 Mary said, “My soul praises the Lord. 47 My spirit rejoices in God my Savior. 48 He has looked on the lowly state of His servant. From now on all generations will call me blessed. 49 The Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is His name. 50 His mercy is for those who fear Him, from generation to generation.

51 “He has shown strength with His arm. He has scattered those who are proud in their thoughts. 52 He has brought down rulers from their thrones and lifted up the humble. 53 He has filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty. 54 He has helped His servant Israel, remembering His mercy. 55 He promised this to our fathers, to Abraham and his children forever.”

56 Mary stayed with Elizabeth for about three months. Then she went back home.

57 When it was time for Elizabeth to have her baby, she gave birth to a son. 58 Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her great mercy. They rejoiced with her.

59 On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child. They were going to name him after his father, Zechariah. 60 But his mother said, “No. He will be called John.” 61 They said to her, “No one in your family has that name.” 62 Then they motioned to his father to ask what he wanted to name the child. 63 He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” Everyone was amazed.

64 Right away his mouth was opened and his tongue was freed. He spoke and praised God. 65 All the neighbors were filled with awe. Throughout the hill country of Judea, people talked about all these things. 66 Everyone who heard about them wondered, “What will this child become?” They said, “The Lord’s power was with him.”

67 Zechariah, his father, was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, 68 “Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, because He has come to help His people and set them free. 69 He has raised up a mighty Savior for us from the house of His servant David. 70 He spoke through His holy prophets long ago. 71 He promised to save us from our enemies and from everyone who hates us. 72 He promised to show mercy to our fathers and remember His holy covenant. 73 He swore to our father Abraham 74 to rescue us from our enemies, so we could serve Him without fear. 75 We would be holy and right before Him all our days.

76 “And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High. You will go before the Lord to prepare His ways. 77 You will give His people knowledge of salvation through the forgiveness of their sins. 78 Because of our God’s tender mercy, the rising sun will visit us from heaven. 79 It will shine on those who live in darkness and in the shadow of death. It will guide our feet into the path of peace.”

80 The child grew and became strong in spirit. He lived in the wilderness until he was shown to Israel.
