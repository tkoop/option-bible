Israel Destroys Idols

1When all this had ended, the Israelites who were there went out to the towns of Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. They smashed the stone pillars, cut down the Asherah poles, and tore down the high places and altars throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. After they had destroyed all of them, the Israelites went back to their own towns and their own property.

Hezekiah Organizes Temple Service

2Hezekiah appointed the priests and Levites to their divisions. Each group had its own duties. The priests and Levites were to offer burnt offerings and fellowship offerings, to serve, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD at the gates of His temple. 3The king gave part of his own wealth for the morning and evening burnt offerings and for the burnt offerings on the Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts, as the Law of the LORD required. 4He also told the people living in Jerusalem to give the priests and Levites their share, so they could give all their time to the Law of the LORD.

The People Bring Their Offerings

5As soon as the order spread, the Israelites gave large amounts of the first crops of grain, new wine, olive oil, and honey, and all the produce of the fields. They brought a large tithe of everything. 6The men of Israel and Judah who lived in the towns of Judah also brought a tithe of their herds and flocks and a tithe of the holy things dedicated to the LORD their God. They piled them in heaps. 7They began piling them up in the third month and finished in the seventh month. 8When Hezekiah and his officials came and saw the heaps, they praised the LORD and blessed His people Israel. 9Hezekiah asked the priests and Levites about the heaps. 10Azariah the chief priest, from the family of Zadok, answered, "Since the people began to bring their contributions into the temple of the LORD, we have had plenty to eat, with much left over, because the LORD has blessed His people. This large amount is what is left."

11Then Hezekiah ordered them to prepare storerooms in the temple of the LORD, and they did. 12They faithfully brought in the contributions, tithes, and dedicated gifts. Conaniah the Levite was in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second. 13Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were supervisors under Conaniah and Shimei. King Hezekiah and Azariah, the official in charge of the temple of God, had appointed them.

14Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the gatekeeper at the East Gate, was in charge of the freewill offerings given to God. He distributed the contributions made to the LORD and the most holy gifts. 15Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah served under him. They faithfully gave out the supplies to their fellow Levites in the priestly towns, by divisions, to great and small alike. 16Besides the males three years old and older who were listed by family records, everyone who came to the temple of the LORD each day for service received a share, according to their divisions.

17The priests were enrolled by their ancestral families, and the Levites twenty years old and older were enrolled according to their duties and divisions. 18The enrollment included all the little children, wives, sons, and daughters of the whole assembly, because they had faithfully set themselves apart as holy. 19For the priests, the descendants of Aaron, who lived in the pasturelands around their towns, men were named in each town to distribute portions to every male among the priests and to every Levite who was enrolled.

Hezekiah's Faithful Service

20This is what Hezekiah did throughout Judah. He did what was good, right, and faithful before the LORD his God. 21In everything he did for the service of God's temple, for the law and commands, and in seeking his God, he worked with all his heart, and he prospered.
