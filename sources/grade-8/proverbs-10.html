Wise Sons and Hard Work

1 A wise son makes his father glad, but a foolish son brings grief to his mother. 2 Treasures gained by wrong do no good, but doing right saves from death. 3 The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry, but He blocks the wicked from getting what they crave.

4 Lazy hands lead to poverty, but hard work brings wealth. 5 A son who gathers crops in summer is wise, but a son who sleeps during harvest brings shame.

Speech and Wisdom

6 Blessings rest on the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked hides violence. 7 The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot. 8 A wise heart accepts commands, but a fool who talks too much comes to ruin.

9 Whoever walks with honesty walks safely, but whoever twists his ways will be found out. 10 A person who winks to plot trouble brings grief, and a fool who talks too much comes to ruin. 11 The mouth of the righteous is a spring of life, but the mouth of the wicked hides violence.

12 Hatred stirs up fights, but love covers all wrongs. 13 Wisdom is found on the lips of the wise, but a rod is for the back of one who lacks sense. 14 Wise people store up knowledge, but the mouth of a fool brings ruin near.

Wealth and Security

15 The rich person's wealth is his strong city, but poverty ruins the poor. 16 The work of the righteous leads to life, but the gain of the wicked leads to sin. 17 Whoever listens to correction stays on the path to life, but whoever ignores correction goes astray.

18 Whoever hides hatred has lying lips, and whoever spreads slander is a fool. 19 When words are many, sin is not far away, but whoever holds back his words is wise. 20 The tongue of the righteous is like fine silver, but the heart of the wicked is worth little. 21 The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools die for lack of sense.

22 The blessing of the LORD brings wealth, and He adds no sorrow to it. 23 A fool enjoys wicked plans, but a person of understanding delights in wisdom. 24 What the wicked fear will come to them, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. 25 When the storm passes, the wicked are gone, but the righteous have a lasting foundation.

The Righteous and the Wicked

26 Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes, so is a lazy person to those who send him. 27 The fear of the LORD adds days to life, but the years of the wicked will be cut short. 28 The hope of the righteous brings joy, but the hope of the wicked will come to nothing.

29 The way of the LORD is a safe place for the blameless, but it brings ruin to those who do wrong. 30 The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked will not live in the land. 31 The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but a twisted tongue will be cut off. 32 The lips of the righteous know what is pleasing, but the mouth of the wicked speaks twisted things.
