The LORD Is My Shepherd

1 The LORD is my shepherd. I will not be in need. 2 He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside quiet waters. 3 He gives me new strength. He guides me on the right paths for the sake of His name.

4 Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, because You are with me. Your rod and Your staff comfort me.

5 You prepare a feast for me where my enemies can see. You pour oil on my head, and my cup runs over. 6 Goodness and faithful love will follow me all my life, and I will live in the house of the LORD forever.
