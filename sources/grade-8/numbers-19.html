The Red Heifer Sacrifice

1 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

2 "This is the rule of the law that the LORD has commanded: Tell the Israelites to bring you a red heifer that has no defect or blemish and has never been under a yoke. 3 Give it to Eleazar the priest. He will take it outside the camp and slaughter it in front of him. 4 Eleazar the priest must take some of its blood on his finger and sprinkle it seven times toward the front of the Tent of Meeting. 5 Then the heifer must be burned while he watches. Its hide, meat, blood, and dung must be burned.

6 "The priest must take cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet wool and throw them into the fire where the heifer is burning. 7 Then the priest must wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. After that he may enter the camp, but he will be unclean until evening. 8 The person who burns the heifer must also wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. He too will be unclean until evening. 9 A clean person must gather the ashes of the heifer and put them in a clean place outside the camp. They must be kept for the Israelite community to use in the water of purification. It is for purification from sin. 10 The man who gathers the ashes of the heifer must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. This is a permanent rule for the Israelites and for the foreigner living among them.

Becoming Unclean by Death

11 "Anyone who touches a dead body will be unclean for seven days. 12 He must make himself clean with the water on the third day and on the seventh day. Then he will be clean. But if he does not make himself clean on the third and seventh days, he will not be clean. 13 Anyone who touches a dead body, the body of a person who has died, and does not make himself clean, defiles the the LORD's tabernacle. That person must be removed from Israel, because the water of purification was not sprinkled on him. He is unclean, and his uncleanness stays with him.

14 "This is the law when a man dies in a tent: Everyone who enters the tent and everyone already in the tent will be unclean for seven days. 15 Any open container that has no lid fastened on it is unclean. 16 Anyone in the open field who touches someone killed by a sword, or any dead person, or a human bone, or a grave, will be unclean for seven days.

Cleansing with Purification Water

17 "For the unclean person, take some ashes from the burnt sin offering and put them in a container with fresh water. 18 Then a clean person must take hyssop, dip it in the water, and sprinkle it on the tent and on all the furnishings and people who were there. He must also sprinkle it on anyone who touched a bone, a dead body, or a grave. 19 The clean person must sprinkle the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day. After the unclean person has made himself clean on the seventh day, he must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That evening he will be clean. 20 But if a person who is unclean does not make himself clean, he must be removed from the community, because he has defiled the the LORD's sanctuary. The water of purification was not sprinkled on him; he is unclean.

Final Rules for Purification

21 "This is a permanent rule for them. The person who sprinkles the water of purification must wash his clothes, and anyone who touches the water of purification will be unclean until evening. 22 Anything the unclean person touches will be unclean, and anyone who touches it will be unclean until evening."
