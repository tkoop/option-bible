A Reflection by Asaph

Why Has God Rejected His People?

1Why have You rejected us forever, O God? Why does Your anger burn against the sheep of Your pasture? 2Remember Your people, whom You bought long ago and redeemed to be the tribe You own—Mount Zion, where You dwell.

3Turn Your steps toward the ruins that last forever; the enemy has destroyed everything in Your sanctuary. 4Your enemies shouted in Your meeting place and planted their flags as signs of victory.

5They acted like men swinging axes in a forest. 6They smashed all the carved wood with hatchets and axes.

7They burned Your holy place to the ground and dishonored the place where Your name lives. 8They said to themselves, “Let’s wipe them out completely!” They burned every place where God’s people met in the land.

9We do not see our signs; there is no longer any prophet, and none of us knows how long this will last. 10How long, God, will the enemy mock us? Will our foe insult Your name forever? 11Why do You pull back Your hand, even Your right hand? Take it out of Your cloak and destroy them!

Remember God's Mighty Acts

12But God has been my King since long ago, bringing salvation on the earth. 13You divided the sea by Your strength and crushed the heads of the sea monsters in the waters. 14You crushed the heads of Leviathan and fed him to the wild animals of the desert.

15You broke open the springs and streams, and You dried up the rivers that never stop. 16The day is Yours, and so is the night; You set the sun and moon in place. 17You fixed all the borders of the earth and made summer and winter.

Call on God to Keep His Promise

18Remember that the enemy has mocked the LORD, and that a foolish people has reviled Your name. 19Do not hand over the life of Your dove to wild animals; do not forget the lives of Your suffering people forever. 20Look at Your covenant, for the dark places of the earth are full of violence.

21Do not let the oppressed go away in shame; may the poor and needy praise Your name. 22Rise up, God, and defend Your cause; remember how the foolish insult You all day long. 23Do not forget the noise of Your enemies, the uproar of those who rise against You that never stops.
