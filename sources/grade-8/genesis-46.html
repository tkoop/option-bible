Jacob Travels to Egypt

1 Israel set out with everything he owned and came to Beersheba. There he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. 2 That night God spoke to Israel in a vision and said, "Jacob, Jacob!" Jacob answered, "Here I am." 3 God said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, because I will make you into a great nation there. 4 I will go down to Egypt with you, and I will bring you back again. Joseph will be with you when you die."

5 Jacob got up from Beersheba. The sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, along with their little children and wives, in the carts Pharaoh had sent. 6 They took their livestock and everything they had gained in Canaan. Jacob and all his family went with him to Egypt. 7 He brought with him his sons and grandsons, his daughters and granddaughters—in fact, all his descendants.

Jacob's Family Who Went to Egypt

8 These are the names of Israel's sons and their families who came to Egypt. Reuben was Jacob's oldest son. 9 Reuben's sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. 10 Simeon's sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite woman. 11 Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 12 Judah's sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. Er and Onan died in Canaan. Perez's sons were Hezron and Hamul. 13 Issachar's sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron. 14 Zebulun's sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. 15 These were the sons Leah had for Jacob in Paddan Aram, along with his daughter Dinah. In all, her sons and daughter were 33 people.

16 Gad's sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. 17 Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and their sister was Serah. Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel. 18 These were the children of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Leah. She had 16 children for Jacob.

19 The sons of Jacob's wife Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin. 20 In Egypt, Joseph had two sons by Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of On. Their names were Manasseh and Ephraim. 21 Benjamin's sons were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard. 22 In all, Rachel's children and grandchildren who were counted were 14 people.

23 Dan's son was Hushim. 24 Naphtali's sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem. 25 These were the children born to Jacob by Bilhah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel. In all, they were 7 people. 26 All those who came to Egypt with Jacob—his own descendants, not counting his sons' wives—were 66 people. 27 Joseph had two sons born in Egypt. So all the people in Jacob's household who came to Egypt were 70.

Jacob Meets Joseph in Goshen

28 Jacob sent Judah ahead to Joseph to show the way to Goshen. So they came to the land of Goshen. 29 Joseph got his chariot ready and went to Goshen to meet his father Israel. He saw him, hugged him, and wept for a long time. 30 Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die, because I have seen your face and know that you are alive."

Joseph Prepares His Family for Pharaoh

31 Joseph said to his brothers and his father, "I will go up and tell Pharaoh, 'My brothers and my father's household, who were in Canaan, have come to me. 32 The men are shepherds. They take care of animals and have brought their sheep, cattle, and everything they own.' 33 When Pharaoh calls you and asks, 'What is your work?' 34 say, 'Your servants have been keepers of livestock from our youth until now, both we and our fathers.' Then you may live in Goshen, because every shepherd is detestable to the Egyptians."
