Job Asks Why God Waits to Judge

1 "Why does the Almighty not set times for judgment? Why do those who know Him look in vain for His days?

The Wicked Hurt the Poor

2 "Some move boundary stones and pasture stolen flocks. 3 They drive away the orphan's donkey and take the widow's ox as a pledge. 4 They push the needy off the road, so the poor of the land must hide. 5 Like wild donkeys in the desert, the poor go out to work, searching for food; the wasteland gives food to their children. 6 They gather feed in the fields and pick leftover grapes from the wicked man's vineyard. 7 Without clothes, they spend the night naked, with no covering in the cold. 8 Mountain rains soak them, and they cling to rocks because they have no shelter. 9 They snatch the fatherless from the breast and take a pledge from the poor. 10 Without clothes, they walk naked; they carry sheaves but still go hungry. 11 They press olives among the terraces and tread the winepresses, yet they are thirsty. 12 The dying groan in the city, and the wounded cry for help, but God does not call it a crime.

Those Who Hide in Darkness

13 "There are those who rebel against the light; they do not know its ways or stay on its paths. 14 The murderer rises at dawn to kill the poor and needy, and at night he is like a thief. 15 The adulterer's eye watches for twilight; he says, 'No eye will see me,' and he covers his face. 16 In the dark they break into houses; by day they shut themselves in; they do not know the light. 17 For them, deep darkness is like morning; they are friends with the terrors of darkness.

What Happens to the Wicked

18 "They are like foam on the water. Their land is cursed, so no one goes to their vineyards. 19 As drought and heat melt the snow, so the grave takes away sinners. 20 The womb forgets them; worms feast on them; they are remembered no more; and wickedness is broken like a tree. 21 They mistreat the childless woman and do no good to the widow. 22 God drags away the mighty by His power; though they rise up, they have no assurance of life. 23 He may let them rest in safety, but His eyes are on their ways. 24 For a little while they are lifted up, then they are gone; they are brought down and gathered up like everyone else; they are cut off like heads of grain. 25 If this is not so, who can prove me a liar and make my words worthless?"
