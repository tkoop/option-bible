A Message About Egypt

1 A message about Egypt: Look, the LORD rides on a swift cloud and comes to Egypt. Egypt’s idols tremble before Him, and the hearts of the Egyptians melt inside them.

2 “I will stir up Egyptian against Egyptian—brother against brother, neighbor against neighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom. 3 The spirit of the Egyptians will be emptied out inside them. I will confuse their plans, and they will turn to idols, spirits of the dead, mediums, and spiritists. 4 I will hand the Egyptians over to a harsh master, and a fierce king will rule over them,” declares the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

5 The waters of the sea will dry up, and the river will be parched and dry. 6 The canals will stink; the streams of Egypt will shrink and dry up. The reeds and rushes will wither. 7 The papyrus by the Nile, at the mouth of the Nile, and all the fields planted by the Nile will dry up, blow away, and be gone. 8 The fishermen will mourn, and all who cast hooks into the Nile will grieve; those who spread nets on the water will waste away. 9 The workers in flax will be ashamed, and the weavers of fine linen will turn pale. 10 Its pillars will be crushed, and all who work for wages will be sick at heart.

Egypt’s Leaders Are Foolish

11 The princes of Zoan are fools; Pharaoh’s wise advisors give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am a son of wise men, a son of ancient kings”? 12 Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and reveal what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt. 13 The princes of Zoan have become fools; the princes of Memphis are deluded. The cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray. 14 The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion. Egypt has been led astray in all she does, like a drunkard staggering around in his vomit. 15 Egypt will be helpless; no one, from the greatest to the least, will be able to do anything.

Egypt Turns to the LORD

16 In that day the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble and fear at the waving of the hand of the LORD of Hosts, which He will raise against them. 17 The land of Judah will bring terror to Egypt; everyone who hears Judah mentioned will be afraid, because of what the LORD of Hosts is planning against them. 18 In that day five cities in Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear loyalty to the LORD of Hosts. One of them will be called the City of Destruction. 19 In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the heart of Egypt and a pillar to the LORD near her border. 20 It will be a sign and witness to the LORD of Hosts in Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, He will send them a Savior and Defender, and He will rescue them. 21 So the LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and on that day the Egyptians will know the LORD. They will worship Him with sacrifices and offerings; they will make vows to the LORD and keep them. 22 The LORD will strike Egypt with a plague; He will strike and heal. So they will turn to the LORD, and He will answer their prayers and heal them.

Egypt, Assyria, and Israel United

23 In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will go to Egypt, and the Egyptians to Assyria. The Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together. 24 In that day Israel will be third, along with Egypt and Assyria—a blessing in the middle of the earth. 25 The LORD of Hosts will bless them and say, “Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria My handiwork, and Israel My inheritance.”
