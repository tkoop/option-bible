Joseph in Prison

1 After these things, Pharaoh's cupbearer and baker wronged their master, the king of Egypt. 2 Pharaoh was angry with his two officers, the chief cupbearer and the chief baker. 3 He put them in prison in the house of the captain of the guard, in the same prison where Joseph was held. 4 The captain of the guard put Joseph in charge of them, and Joseph served them. They stayed in prison for a while.

5 Both of them, the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were confined in the prison, had a dream the same night. Each man had his own dream, and each dream had its own meaning.

6 When Joseph came to them in the morning, he saw that they were upset.

7 He asked Pharaoh's officers who were with him in custody in his master's house, "Why do your faces look so sad today?"

8 They said, "We have had a dream, and there is no one to interpret it."

Joseph said, "Don't interpretations come from God? Please tell them to me."

The Cupbearer's Dream

9 Then the chief cupbearer told his dream to Joseph: "In my dream there was a vine before me.

10 "On the vine were three branches. As soon as it budded, its blossoms came out, and its clusters ripened into grapes.

11 "Pharaoh's cup was in my hand. I took the grapes and pressed them into Pharaoh's cup, and I placed the cup in Pharaoh's hand."

12 Joseph said, "This is its meaning: The three branches are three days. 13 Within three days Pharaoh will restore you to favor and give you back your position. You will put Pharaoh's cup in his hand, as you used to do when you were his cupbearer.

14 "But when it goes well with you, please remember me and show me kindness. Mention me to Pharaoh, and get me out of this house. 15 For I was kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and here also I have done nothing to deserve being thrown into this prison."

The Baker's Dream

16 When the chief baker saw that the interpretation was good, he said to Joseph, "I also had a dream. There were three baskets of bread on my head.

17 "In the top basket were all kinds of baked goods for Pharaoh, and the birds were eating them out of the basket on my head."

18 Joseph answered, "This is its meaning: The three baskets are three days.

19 "Within three days Pharaoh will have you put to death and hang you on a tree. The birds will eat your flesh."

Pharaoh's Birthday

20 On the third day, which was Pharaoh's birthday, he made a feast for all his servants. He brought the chief cupbearer and the chief baker before his servants.

21 He restored the chief cupbearer to his position, and he placed the cup in Pharaoh's hand. 22 But he hanged the chief baker, just as Joseph had interpreted to them. 23 Yet the chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph; he forgot him.
