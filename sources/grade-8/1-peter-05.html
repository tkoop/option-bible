Encouragement for Church Leaders

1 I am a fellow elder and a witness of Christ’s sufferings, and I will share in the glory that is about to be revealed. So I urge the elders among you: 2 Care for God’s flock among you. Watch over them—not because you must, but because you want to. Don’t do it for shameful profit, but do it with eagerness. 3 Don’t act like bosses over the people God has given you. Instead, be examples to the flock. 4 Then, when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the crown of glory that never fades.

Be Humble and Trust God

5 In the same way, younger people, submit to the elders. All of you, put on humility toward one another, because God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble. 6 So humble yourselves under God’s mighty hand, so that He may lift you up at the right time. 7 Throw all your worries on Him, because He cares for you.

Resist the Devil and Stay Strong

8 Be clear-minded and watchful. Your enemy the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour. 9 Resist him. Stand firm in your faith. Remember that believers all over the world are going through the same kinds of suffering. 10 After you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Christ, will Himself restore you, make you strong, give you power, and set you on a firm foundation. 11 To Him be the power forever and ever. Amen.

Final Greetings

12 With the help of Silvanus, whom I trust as a faithful brother, I have written this short letter to encourage you and to show that this is God’s true grace. Stand firm in it. 13 The believers in Babylon, who are chosen along with you, send greetings, and so does my son Mark. 14 Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.
