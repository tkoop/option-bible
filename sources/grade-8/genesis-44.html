Joseph Hides His Silver Cup

1 Joseph told the man who ran his house, "Fill the men's sacks with as much food as they can carry. Put each man's silver in the mouth of his sack. 2 Put my silver cup in the mouth of the youngest brother's sack, along with the silver for his grain." The servant did exactly what Joseph said.

3 At dawn, the men were sent away with their donkeys.

The Brothers Are Stopped

4 They had not gone far from the city when Joseph said to his house manager, "Get up and chase the men. When you catch them, say to them, 'Why have you paid back good with evil? 5 Isn't this the cup my master drinks from and uses to tell the future? You have done wrong.'"

6 The manager caught up with them and said these words to them.

7 They said to him, "Why does my lord say such things? Your servants would never do anything like that! 8 Look, we brought back the silver we found in our sacks from Canaan. How could we steal silver or gold from your lord's house? 9 If any of your servants is found with it, he will die, and the rest of us will become my lord's slaves."

10 He said, "Very well, as you say. The one who has it will be my slave, but the rest of you are innocent."

11 Each man quickly lowered his sack to the ground and opened it.

12 The steward searched, beginning with the oldest and ending with the youngest. The cup was found in Benjamin's sack.

13 They tore their clothes. Each man loaded his donkey, and they went back to the city.

The Brothers Return to Joseph

14 Judah and his brothers came to Joseph's house. He was still there, and they fell to the ground before him.

15 Joseph said to them, "What is this you have done? Don't you know that a man like me can find things out?"

16 Judah said, "What can we say to my lord? What can we speak? How can we prove we are innocent? God has found out our guilt. Here we are, my lord's slaves, both we and the one who had the cup."

17 Joseph said, "Far be it from me to do that! The man who had the cup will be my slave. The rest of you go up in peace to your father."

Judah Pleads for Benjamin

18 Then Judah went up to him and said, "Please, my lord, let your servant speak a word in my lord's ears. Do not let your anger burn against your servant, for you are like Pharaoh. 19 My lord asked his servants, 'Do you have a father or a brother?'

20 "We said to my lord, 'We have an old father, and a young brother born in his old age. His brother is dead, and he is the only son left of his mother. His father loves him.' 21 You said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me so I can see him.' 22 We said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father. If he leaves, his father will die.' 23 But you said to your servants, 'Unless your youngest brother comes with you, you will not see my face again.'

24 "When we went back to your servant my father, we told him what my lord said. 25 Our father said, 'Go back and buy us a little food.' 26 We said, 'We cannot go down unless our youngest brother is with us. We cannot see the man's face unless our youngest brother is with us.' 27 Your servant our father said to us, 'You know that my wife had two sons for me. 28 One left me, and I said, "He must have been torn apart," and I have not seen him since. 29 If you take this one from me too, and harm comes to him, you will send me to my grave in sorrow.'

30 "Now if I go back to your servant my father without the boy, and his life is bound up with the boy's life, 31 he will die when he sees the boy is gone. Your servants will send him to his grave in sorrow. 32 Your servant promised my father, 'If I do not bring him back to you, I will bear the blame before my father all my life.' 33 Now let your servant stay here as my lord's slave instead of the boy. Let the boy go back with his brothers. 34 How can I go back to my father if the boy is not with me? I cannot bear to see the trouble that would come to my father."
