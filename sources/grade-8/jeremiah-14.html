Drought in Judah

1 This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah about the droughts. 2 Judah is in mourning, and her city gates grow weak. Her people sit in black on the ground, and Jerusalem cries out. 3 The leaders send their servants for water. They come to the cisterns, but find no water. They return with empty jars, ashamed and disgraced, and they cover their heads. 4 The ground is cracked because no rain has fallen in the land. The farmers are ashamed and cover their heads. 5 Even the doe in the field gives birth and leaves her fawn, because there is no grass. 6 Wild donkeys stand on the bare hills and pant like jackals. Their eyes grow weak because there is no grass.

Jeremiah Prays for the People

7 Even though our sins speak against us, O the LORD, do something for the honor of Your name. We have turned away from You many times, and we have sinned against You. 8 You are the hope of Israel and its Savior in times of trouble. Why do You seem like a stranger in the land, like a traveler who stops for the night? 9 Why do You seem like a man who is stunned, like a strong man who cannot rescue anyone? Yet You are among us, O the LORD, and we are called by Your name. Do not leave us.

The LORD Rejects the People and Their Prophets

10 The LORD said to this people, "They have loved to wander away. They did not hold back their feet. So the LORD does not accept them. Now He will remember their guilt and punish their sins." 11 The LORD said to me, "Do not pray for good for these people. 12 When they fast, I will not listen to their cry. When they offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will destroy them by war, hunger, and disease."

13 I said, "Oh, Lord the LORD! Look, the prophets are telling them, 'You will not see war, and you will not have famine. Instead, I will give you lasting peace in this place.'"

14 The LORD said to me, "The prophets are telling lies in My name. I did not send them, command them, or speak to them. They are giving you false visions, fortune-telling, empty hopes, and lies from their own hearts."

15 "Therefore this is what the LORD says about the prophets who speak in My name, though I did not send them, and who say, 'There will be no war or famine in this land.' Those same prophets will be destroyed by war and famine. 16 The people they prophesy to will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and war. No one will bury them, or their wives, sons, and daughters. I will pour out their own evil on them."

A Plea for Mercy

17 The LORD said to me, "Say this to the people: 'My eyes overflow with tears day and night, and they do not stop, because the young women of my people have been broken by a terrible blow, a very bad wound. 18 If I go out to the fields, I see people killed by war. If I go into the city, I see people sick with hunger. Even prophets and priests have gone to a land they do not know.'"

19 Have You completely rejected Judah? Has Your soul turned against Zion? Why have You struck us with no healing? We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for a time of healing, but there is only terror. 20 We know our wickedness, O the LORD, and the sins of our fathers, because we have sinned against You. 21 For Your name's sake, do not reject us. Do not dishonor Your glorious throne. Remember Your covenant with us, and do not break it. 22 Can any of the worthless idols of the nations bring rain? Can the skies give showers? Are You not the LORD our God? So we will wait for You, because You made all these things.
