Praise for the LORD's Wonderful Works

1 The LORD, you are my God. I will lift you up. I will praise your name, because you have done amazing things. Your plans from long ago are faithful and true. 2 For you have turned the city into a pile of rubble. You have destroyed the fortified city. The palace of foreigners is gone forever. It will never be rebuilt. 3 So strong people will honor you, and nations that are cruel will fear you.

4 For you have been a safe place for the poor, a safe place for people in need when they are in trouble. You have been shelter from the storm and shade from the heat. The cruel people's angry breath is like a storm hitting a wall. 5 Like heat in a dry land, you quiet the noise of foreigners. Like heat cooled by the shade of a cloud, you bring down the song of cruel people.

A Feast for All Nations

6 On this mountain, the LORD of Hosts will make a feast of rich food for all people. It will be a feast of aged wine, of rich food full of flavor, and of the finest wine. 7 He will destroy on this mountain the covering spread over all peoples, the veil that is over all nations. 8 He will swallow up death forever. The Lord the LORD will wipe away tears from every face. He will take away the shame of His people from the whole earth, because the the LORD has spoken.

9 On that day people will say, "Look, this is our God! We waited for Him, and He saved us. This is the LORD; we waited for Him. Let us be glad and rejoice in His salvation!"

Judgment on Moab

10 For the hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain, and Moab will be trampled down where it stands, like straw is trampled in a manure pile. 11 He will spread his hands in the middle of them, like a swimmer spreading his hands to swim. But the the LORD will bring down their pride, along with all their clever work. 12 The high walls of your fortress He will bring down. He will level them and throw them to the ground, down to the dust.
