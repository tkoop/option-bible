Offerings for the Promised Land

1 The LORD said to Moses,

2 "Speak to the Israelites and say: When you enter the land I am giving you to settle in, 3 and you present a food offering to the LORD from your herds or flocks—to make an offering that pleases the LORD as a burnt offering or sacrifice, to keep a special promise or as a free gift, or at your appointed festivals— 4 the one who brings the offering must also bring a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil. 5 With a burnt offering or sacrifice for each lamb, also bring a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering. 6 For a ram, bring a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil, 7 and a third of a hin of wine as a drink offering. It is an offering that pleases the LORD. 8 When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice, to keep a special promise or as a peace offering to the LORD, 9 bring with the bull a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil, 10 and half a hin of wine as a drink offering. It is an offering that pleases the LORD. 11 Do this for each bull, ram, lamb, or goat. 12 However many you offer, do this for each one. 13 Every native-born Israelite must do this when presenting a food offering, an offering that pleases the LORD. 14 For all your generations, if a foreigner living among you wants to present a food offering, an offering that pleases the LORD, he must do it the same way you do. 15 The whole community has one rule for you and for the foreigner living among you. It is a lasting rule for your generations. The foreigner is the same as you before the LORD. 16 There is one law and one rule for you and for the foreigner living among you."

The First Dough Offering

17 The LORD said to Moses,

18 "Speak to the Israelites and say: When you enter the land I am bringing you into, 19 and you eat food from that land, set aside a portion for the LORD. 20 Present a cake from the first of your dough as an offering. Offer it like the grain from your threshing floor. 21 For all your generations, give the LORD an offering from the first of your dough.

Sins and Their Consequences

22 "If you fail to obey all these commands the LORD gave you through Moses— 23 all the LORD commanded you through Moses from the day He gave them and for your generations to come— 24 and if the whole community sins by mistake, and they do not realize it, the whole community must bring one young bull as a burnt offering, an offering that pleases the LORD, with its grain and drink offerings as required, and one male goat as a sin offering. 25 The priest will make atonement for the whole Israelite community. They will be forgiven, because it was a mistake, and they brought their offering to the LORD—a burnt offering—and their sin offering before the LORD for their mistake. 26 The whole community of Israelites and the foreigner living among them will be forgiven, because all the people sinned by mistake. 27 If one person sins by mistake, he must bring a year-old female goat as a sin offering. 28 The priest will make atonement before the LORD for the person who sinned by mistake. When atonement is made for him, he will be forgiven. 29 One law applies to the native-born Israelite and to the foreigner living among you for sins done by mistake. 30 But anyone who sins on purpose, whether native-born or foreigner, speaks against the LORD. That person must be removed from his people. 31 Because he has rejected the LORD's word and broken His command, his guilt remains."

32 While the Israelites were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day. 33 Those who found him brought him to Moses, Aaron, and the whole community. 34 They kept him in custody because it was not clear what should be done with him.

35 The LORD said to Moses, "The man must be put to death. The whole community must stone him outside the camp."

36 So the community brought him outside the camp and stoned him to death, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

Tassels to Remember God's Commands

37 The LORD said to Moses,

38 "Speak to the Israelites and say: For all your generations, make tassels on the corners of your garments and put a blue cord on each tassel. 39 The tassels will remind you of all the LORD's commands, so you will obey them and not follow your own desires and eyes, which lead you away from God. 40 Then you will remember and obey all My commands and be holy to your God. 41 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God."
