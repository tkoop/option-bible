David Trusts God in Trouble

A song of David, written when he fled from his son Absalom. 1 O LORD, how many enemies I have! Many are rising against me.

2 Many say, “God won’t save him.” Selah.

3 But you, O LORD, are my protector. You are my honor, and You give me new courage. 4 I call out to the LORD, and He answers me from His holy hill. Selah.

5 I lie down and sleep. I wake again, for the LORD sustains me. 6 I won’t be afraid of the thousands of people who surround me on every side.

7 Arise, O LORD! Save me, O my God! For You defeat all my enemies and take away the power of the wicked. 8 Salvation belongs to the LORD. May Your blessing rest on Your people! Selah.
