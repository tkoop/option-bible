David Counts the Israelites

1Satan turned against Israel and pushed David to count the Israelites. 2David said to Joab and the commanders of the troops, "Go count the Israelites from Beersheba to Dan, and bring me the total so I will know how many there are."

3Joab replied, "May the LORD make His people a hundred times more numerous. But, my lord the king, are they not all your servants? Why do you want this? Why should this bring guilt on Israel?"

4But the king's order won out over Joab. So Joab left and traveled all over Israel. Then he came back to Jerusalem.

5Joab gave David the number of men able to fight. In all Israel there were 1,100,000 men who could use a sword. Judah had 470,000 of them. 6But Joab did not count Levi and Benjamin, because he hated the king's order. 7God saw this order as evil, and He struck Israel.

8David said to God, "I have sinned greatly by doing this. Now please take away the sin of Your servant. I have acted very foolishly."

God Gives David a Choice

9The LORD spoke to Gad, David's prophet, and said, 10"Go tell David, 'This is what the LORD says: I am giving you three choices. Pick one, and I will do it to you.'"

11So Gad went to David and said, "This is what the LORD says: Choose one of these: 12three years of famine; three months of being beaten by your enemies, with their swords chasing you; or three days of the LORD's sword and sickness in the land, while the angel of the LORD destroys all over Israel. Now decide what answer I should give the One who sent me."

13David said to Gad, "I am in great trouble. Let me fall into the LORD's hand, because His mercy is very great. But do not let me fall into human hands."

The Plague and David's Altar

14So the LORD sent a plague on Israel, and 70,000 Israelites died. 15God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem. But as he was about to destroy it, the LORD saw it and changed His mind about the disaster. He said to the destroying angel, "That is enough! Stop! Put down your hand." The angel of the LORD was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 16David looked up and saw the angel of the LORD standing between heaven and earth with a sword drawn in his hand, stretched out over Jerusalem. David and the elders, wearing sackcloth, fell facedown.

17David said to God, "Was it not I who ordered the people counted? I am the one who sinned and did wrong. But these sheep—what have they done? The LORD my God, please let Your hand fall on me and my family, not on Your people as a plague."

18The angel of the LORD told Gad to tell David to go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 19So David went up, just as Gad had said in the LORD's name. 20Ornan was threshing wheat. He turned and saw the angel, and he and his four sons hid. 21When David came to Ornan, Ornan looked and saw David. He left the threshing floor and bowed with his face to the ground before David.

22David said to Ornan, "Sell me the site of your threshing floor so I can build an altar to the LORD. Sell it to me at the full price, so the plague may stop for the people."

23Ornan said to David, "Take it, my lord the king. Do what seems good to you. Look, I will give the oxen for the burnt offerings, the threshing boards for wood, and the wheat for the grain offering. I have given everything."

24King David replied to Ornan, "No, I insist on paying the full price. I will not take for the LORD what is yours, or offer a burnt offering that costs me nothing."

25So David paid Ornan six hundred shekels of gold by weight for the site. 26David built an altar to the LORD there. He offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and called on the LORD. The LORD answered him with fire from heaven on the altar of burnt offering. 27Then the LORD told the angel, and the angel put his sword back in its sheath. 28At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he sacrificed there.

29The tabernacle of the LORD that Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offering, were at that time at the high place in Gibeon. 30But David could not go there to seek God, because he was afraid of the sword of the angel of the LORD.
