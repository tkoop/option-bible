Wisdom for Leaders and the Poor

1 The wicked run away even when no one chases them, but godly people are as brave as a lion. 2 When a country rebels, it has many rulers, but a wise leader with understanding keeps things in order. 3 A poor leader who crushes the poor is like a driving rain that leaves no food.

4 People who turn away from God's law praise the wicked, but those who obey God's law stand against them. 5 Evil people do not understand justice, but those who seek the the LORD understand everything. 6 It is better to be poor and live with honesty than to be rich and walk a crooked path.

Honesty, Mercy, and Courage

7 A wise son obeys the law, but a friend of gluttons brings shame to his father. 8 Someone who grows rich by charging interest and fees saves it for a person who will show kindness to the poor. 9 If someone refuses to listen to God's law, even their prayer is offensive to God.

10 Whoever leads good people down an evil road will fall into his own trap, but honest people will receive good things. 11 A rich person may think he is wise, but a poor person with understanding can see through him. 12 When good people win, there is great honor; when wicked people rise to power, people hide.

13 Whoever hides their sins will not succeed, but whoever admits them and leaves them behind will receive mercy. 14 A person who always respects God is blessed, but someone who hardens his heart will fall into trouble.

Warnings About Greed and Oppression

15 A wicked ruler over poor people is like a roaring lion or a charging bear. 16 A ruler who lacks good judgment is a cruel oppressor, but one who hates dishonest gain will live a long time. 17 A person with blood on his hands will run to the grave; do not support him.

18 Whoever walks with honesty will be saved, but whoever is crooked in his ways will suddenly fall. 19 Whoever works his land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases empty dreams will have plenty of poverty. 20 A faithful person will be richly blessed, but someone in a hurry to get rich will not go unpunished.

21 It is not good to show favoritism, yet some people will do wrong for a piece of bread. 22 A greedy person rushes after wealth and does not know poverty is on its way.

Trust in the LORD

23 Someone who corrects another person will later find more favor than someone who flatters with smooth words. 24 Whoever robs his father or mother and says, "It is not a sin," is a friend to a destroyer. 25 A greedy person stirs up trouble, but whoever trusts in the the LORD will prosper.

26 Whoever trusts in his own heart is a fool, but whoever walks in wisdom will be kept safe. 27 Whoever gives to the poor will not lack, but whoever ignores them will receive many curses. 28 When wicked people rise, people hide; when they are destroyed, good people increase.
