Ziba Brings Supplies to David

1 David had gone just a little past the top of the hill when Ziba, Mephibosheth's servant, met him. Ziba had two donkeys loaded with two hundred loaves of bread, one hundred clusters of raisins, one hundred pieces of summer fruit, and a skin of wine.

2 The king asked Ziba, "What are these for?"

Ziba answered, "The donkeys are for the king's family to ride. The bread and fruit are for the young men to eat, and the wine is for anyone who gets tired in the desert to drink."

3 The king asked, "Where is your master?"

Ziba said to the king, "He is staying in Jerusalem."

He added that his master had said, "Today the people of Israel will give me back my father's kingdom."

4 The king said to Ziba, "Everything that belonged to Mephibosheth is now yours."

Ziba bowed and said, "I bow before you. May I find favor in your eyes, my lord the king."

Shimei Curses David

5 When David reached Bahurim, a man from Saul's family came out cursing. His name was Shimei son of Gera.

6 He threw stones at David and at all the king's servants, and at all the people and mighty men on David's right and left.

7 Shimei cursed him and shouted, "Get out! Get out, you murderer! You worthless man! 8 The LORD has paid you back for all the blood of Saul's family. You took his place as king, but the LORD has given the kingdom to your son Absalom. Now you are caught in your own evil, because you are a murderer."

9 Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, "Why should this worthless man curse my lord the king? Let me go over and cut off his head."

10 The king said, "What business is this of yours, sons of Zeruiah? If the LORD has told him to curse David, no one can ask why he is doing it."

11 David said to Abishai and all his servants, "Look, my own son is trying to kill me. How much more should I expect this man from Benjamin to curse me! Leave him alone and let him curse, for the LORD has told him to. 12 Perhaps the LORD will see my trouble and repay me with good for this cursing today."

13 So David and his men went on their way. Shimei walked along the hillside opposite him, cursing as he went, throwing stones and flinging dust.

14 The king and all the people with him arrived tired, and there he rested.

Absalom Enters Jerusalem

15 Absalom and all the men of Israel came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel was with him.

16 When Hushai the Archite, David's friend, came to Absalom, Hushai said, "Long live the king! Long live the king!"

17 Absalom said to Hushai, "Is this your loyalty to your friend? Why didn't you go with your friend?"

18 Hushai said to Absalom, "No! I will serve the one whom the LORD and all Israel have chosen, and I will stay with him. 19 Besides, whom should I serve? Should I not serve his son? Just as I served your father, I will serve you."

Ahithophel Advises Absalom

20 Absalom said to Ahithophel, "Give your advice. What should we do?"

21 Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Go in to your father's concubines, whom he left to watch the house. All Israel will hear that you have made yourself hated by your father, and everyone with you will feel stronger."

22 So they set up a tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.

23 In those days, Ahithophel's advice was like getting advice from God Himself. Both David and Absalom treated it that way.
