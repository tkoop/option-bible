Rejoice in the Lord and Watch Out

1 Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. It is no trouble for me to write the same things to you, and it keeps you safe. 2 Watch out for the dogs, watch out for evil workers, and watch out for those who mutilate the flesh. 3 For we are the true circumcision. We worship by the Spirit of God, we boast in Christ Jesus, and we do not put our trust in human effort.

Paul's Past and New Goal

4 I could put confidence in human effort too. If anyone else thinks he has reason to trust in human effort, I have even more. 5 I was circumcised on the eighth day. I am from the people of Israel and the tribe of Benjamin. I am a Hebrew, born of Hebrews. As for the law, I was a Pharisee. 6 As for zeal, I persecuted the church. As for righteousness under the law, I was blameless. 7 But whatever used to be gain to me, I now count as loss for Christ. 8 Yes, I count everything as loss because knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth far more. For His sake I have lost everything, and I count it all as garbage so I can gain Christ. 9 I want to be found in Him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but one that comes through faith in Christ. It is the righteousness that comes from God and depends on faith. 10 I want to know Him and the power of His resurrection. I want to share in His sufferings and become like Him in His death. 11 Then I hope to reach the resurrection from the dead.

Pressing On Toward the Goal

12 I have not already reached it or already become perfect. But I press on to take hold of that for which Christ Jesus took hold of me. 13 Brothers, I do not think I have taken hold of it yet. But one thing I do: I forget what is behind and reach forward to what is ahead. 14 I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God's upward call in Christ Jesus. 15 All of us who are mature should think this way. And if you think differently about anything, God will make that clear to you too. 16 Only let us live up to what we have already reached. 17 Brothers, join in following my example, and watch those who live the way we do. You have us as a model. 18 Many people walk as enemies of the cross of Christ. I have told you this often, and now I tell you again with tears.

Our Heavenly Citizenship

19 Their end is destruction. Their god is their stomach. They are proud of what should shame them. They think only about earthly things. 20 But our citizenship is in heaven. We eagerly wait for a Savior from there, the Lord Jesus Christ. 21 He will change our weak bodies to be like His glorious body. He does this by the power that lets Him bring everything under His control.
