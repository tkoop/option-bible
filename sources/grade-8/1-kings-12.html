Rehoboam Goes to Shechem

1 Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel had gathered there to make him king. 2 Jeroboam son of Nebat heard about this while he was still in Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon, and he stayed there.

The People Ask for Relief

3 So they sent for Jeroboam. He and all Israel came and spoke to Rehoboam. 4 They said, "Your father made our work very hard. Now make the heavy work and the hard burden your father put on us lighter, and we will serve you." 5 Rehoboam said to them, "Go away for three days, then come back to me." So the people went away.

6 King Rehoboam asked the elders who had served his father Solomon while he was alive, "How do you advise me to answer these people?" 7 They answered, "If today you will be a servant to these people and serve them, and if you answer them and speak kind words to them, they will be your servants forever." 8 But Rehoboam rejected the elders' advice and asked the young men who had grown up with him and were serving him. 9 He said to them, "What advice do you give? How should we answer these people who said, 'Make the work your father put on us lighter'?"

10 The young men who grew up with him said, "Tell the people who told you, 'Your father made our load heavy, but make it lighter for us': 'I will be much harder on you than my father was. 11 My father put a heavy burden on you, but I will make it heavier. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you with scorpions.'"

12 On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, just as the king had said, "Come back to me on the third day." 13 The king answered the people harshly, rejecting the elders' advice. 14 He spoke to them using the young men's advice and said, "My father made your burden heavy, but I will make it heavier. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you with scorpions." 15 The king did not listen to the people, because this turn of events came from the the LORD, to carry out the word He had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

The Kingdom Divides

16 When all Israel saw that the king would not listen to them, they answered the king, "What share do we have in David? We have no part in Jesse's son. Go to your tents, Israel! Look after your own house, David!" Then Israel went home. 17 But Rehoboam still ruled over the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah.

18 King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was in charge of forced labor, but all Israel stoned him to death. King Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and fled to Jerusalem. 19 So Israel rebelled against David's family, and that is still true today. 20 When all Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned, they sent for him and made him king over all Israel. Only the tribe of Judah stayed with David's family.

21 Rehoboam came to Jerusalem and gathered all the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin—180,000 chosen warriors—to fight against Israel and win the kingdom back for Rehoboam son of Solomon. 22 But the word of God came to Shemaiah, the man of God: 23 "Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Judah and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people: 24 This is what the the LORD says: Do not go up and fight against your brothers, the Israelites. Each of you go back home, for this is My doing." Then they obeyed the word of the the LORD and went home, as the the LORD had said.

Jeroboam Sets Up False Worship

25 Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived there. He also went out from there and built Penuel. 26 Jeroboam said in his heart, "Now the kingdom may go back to David's family. 27 If these people go up to offer sacrifices in the house of the the LORD in Jerusalem, their hearts will turn back to their master, Rehoboam king of Judah. Then they will kill me and go back to Rehoboam king of Judah."

28 The king asked for advice and made two golden calves. He said to the people, "It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem. Here are your gods, Israel, who brought you up out of Egypt." 29 He put one calf in Bethel and the other in Dan. 30 This became a sin. The people went as far as Dan to worship before one of the calves.

31 Jeroboam built shrines on high places and made priests from all kinds of people who were not Levites. 32 Jeroboam set a feast on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, like the feast in Judah. He offered sacrifices on the altar he had built at Bethel to the calves he had made. He also put priests in Bethel for the high places he had made. 33 On the fifteenth day of the eighth month—the month he had chosen on his own—he offered sacrifices on the altar he had built at Bethel. He set a festival for the Israelites and went up to the altar to burn incense.
