David Plans to Move the Ark

1 David met with the commanders of thousands and hundreds and with all the other leaders.

2 David said to all the people of Israel, "If this seems good to you, and if it is from the LORD our God, let us send word to our brothers who are left in all the lands of Israel. Let the priests and Levites who live in their towns with pasturelands join them, and let them gather to us. 3 Let us bring back the ark of our God to us, because we did not ask about it during the days of Saul."

4 All the people agreed to do it, because they thought it was right.

The Ark Is Brought Toward Jerusalem

5 David gathered all Israel from the Shihor in Egypt to Hamath to bring the ark of God from Kiriath Jearim.

6 David and all Israel went up to Baalah, which is Kiriath Jearim in Judah, to bring up the ark of God. It is the ark of the LORD, who sits between the cherubim—the ark that is called by His Name.

7 They carried the ark of God from Abinadab's house on a new cart. Uzzah and Ahio were guiding the cart.

8 David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all their might. They used songs, harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

Uzzah Is Struck Down

9 When they came to the threshing floor at Kidon, Uzzah reached out his hand to hold the ark, because the oxen stumbled.

10 The LORD was angry with Uzzah and struck him down because he had put his hand on the ark. He died there before God.

11 David was angry because the LORD had punished Uzzah. So that place has been called Perez Uzzah to this day.

12 David was afraid of God that day. He said, "How can I bring the ark of God to me?"

The Ark Stays with Obed-Edom

13 So David did not take the ark to the City of David. Instead, he sent it to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.

14 The ark of God stayed in Obed-Edom's house for three months. The LORD blessed Obed-Edom and all his household.
