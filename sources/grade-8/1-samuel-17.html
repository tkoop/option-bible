The Armies Face Each Other

1The Philistines gathered their armies for war. They camped at Socoh in Judah, between Socoh and Azekah, at Ephes-dammim. 2Saul and the men of Israel gathered too. They camped in the Valley of Elah and lined up for battle against the Philistines. 3The Philistines stood on one hill, and Israel stood on another hill, with the valley between them.

4A champion came out of the Philistine camp. His name was Goliath, from Gath. He was about nine feet, nine inches tall. 5He wore a bronze helmet and a bronze coat of armor that weighed about 125 pounds. 6He had bronze armor on his legs and a bronze spear between his shoulders. 7The shaft of his spear was like a weaver's beam, and its iron point weighed about 15 pounds. A man carrying his shield walked in front of him.

Goliath Challenges Israel

8Goliath stood and shouted to Israel's battle lines, "Why do you come out to fight? Am I not a Philistine, and aren't you Saul's servants? Choose a man and let him come down to me. 9If he can fight me and kill me, we will be your servants. But if I beat him and kill him, you will be our servants and serve us. 10I challenge Israel's army today. Give me a man, and we will fight one on one." 11When Saul and all Israel heard this, they were very afraid.

David Comes to the Camp

12David was the son of Jesse, an Ephrathite from Bethlehem in Judah. Jesse had eight sons. By then Jesse was an old man. 13His three oldest sons—Eliab, Abinadab, and Shammah—had gone with Saul to war. 14David was the youngest. The three oldest followed Saul. 15David went back and forth from Saul to tend his father's sheep at Bethlehem. 16For forty days the Philistine came out every morning and evening and took his stand.

17Jesse said to his son David, "Take this basket of roasted grain and these ten loaves of bread to your brothers in the camp. 18Take these ten cheeses to the commander of their thousand. See how your brothers are doing, and bring back a report." 19Saul and all the men of Israel were in the Valley of Elah, fighting the Philistines.

20David got up early, left the sheep with a keeper, and went as Jesse had told him. He reached the camp just as the army was marching out to fight. They were shouting the war cry. 21Israel and the Philistines lined up against each other. 22David left his supplies with the supply keeper, ran to the battle line, and greeted his brothers. 23While he was talking with them, Goliath, the Philistine champion from Gath, stepped out from the Philistine lines and shouted the same challenge. David heard it. 24All the Israelites ran from him and were very afraid.

25They said, "Have you seen this man? He keeps coming up to challenge Israel. The king will give great riches to the man who kills him. He will also give him his daughter and make his family free from taxes in Israel."

26David asked the men standing near him, "What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and takes away this shame from Israel? Who is this uncircumcised Philistine that he should defy the armies of the living God?"

27The people answered him the same way: "This is what will be done for the man who kills him."

28When Eliab, David's oldest brother, heard him talking to the men, he got angry. He said, "Why did you come down here? Who is watching those few sheep in the desert? I know how proud and wicked your heart is. You came down just to watch the battle."

29David said, "What have I done now? Can't I even ask a question?" 30He turned away from Eliab to another man and asked the same thing. The people gave him the same answer. 31When Saul heard what David said, he sent for him.

David Offers to Fight Goliath

32David said to Saul, "Don't let anyone lose heart because of this Philistine. Your servant will go and fight him."

33Saul said, "You can't go fight this Philistine. You are only a boy, and he has been a warrior since he was young."

34David said to Saul, "Your servant has been keeping his father's sheep. When a lion or a bear came and carried off a sheep from the flock, 35I went after it, struck it, and saved the sheep from its mouth. When it turned on me, I grabbed it by its jaw and struck and killed it. 36Your servant has killed both the lion and the bear. This uncircumcised Philistine will be like one of them, because he has insulted the armies of the living God."

37David added, "The LORD who saved me from the paw of the lion and the bear will save me from this Philistine."

Saul said, "Go, and may the LORD be with you."

38Saul put his own clothes on David. He put a bronze helmet on his head and dressed him in armor. 39David strapped Saul's sword over the armor, but he could not walk because he was not used to them. David said to Saul, "I can't go in these, because I'm not used to them." So he took them off. 40He took his staff in his hand and picked five smooth stones from the stream. He put them in his shepherd's bag. With his sling in his hand, he approached the Philistine.

41The Philistine came closer and closer to David, with his shield bearer in front of him. 42When he looked and saw David, he despised him because he was only a boy, ruddy and handsome. 43The Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come at me with sticks?" Then he cursed David by his gods. 44"Come here," the Philistine said, "and I'll give your flesh to the birds and the wild animals."

45David said to him, "You come to me with sword, spear, and javelin, but I come to you in the name of the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel's armies, whom you have insulted. 46Today the LORD will hand you over to me. I will strike you down and cut off your head. I will give the dead bodies of the Philistines to the birds and the wild animals. Then all the earth will know that there is a God in Israel. 47Everyone here will know that the LORD does not save with sword or spear. The battle belongs to the LORD, and He will hand all of you over to us."

48As the Philistine moved closer to attack, David ran quickly toward him. 49David reached into his bag, took out a stone, and slung it. It hit the Philistine on the forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell facedown on the ground. 50So David won over the Philistine with a sling and a stone. He struck him down and killed him, even though David had no sword. 51David ran and stood over the Philistine. He took the Philistine's sword, drew it, and killed him by cutting off his head. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they ran away.

Israel Pursues the Philistines

52The men of Israel and Judah shouted and chased the Philistines all the way to the entrance of Gath and to the gates of Ekron. The Philistines fell dead along the road to Shaaraim, all the way to Gath and Ekron. 53When the Israelites came back from chasing them, they plundered the Philistine camp. 54David took the Philistine's head and brought it to Jerusalem. He put the Philistine's weapons in his own tent.

55When Saul saw David going out to meet the Philistine, he asked Abner, the commander of the army, "Whose son is this young man, Abner?"

Abner answered, "As surely as you live, O king, I don't know."

56The king said, "Find out whose son this young man is." 57When David came back after killing the Philistine, Abner brought him to Saul. David was still holding the Philistine's head.

58Saul asked him, "Whose son are you, young man?"

David answered, "I am the son of your servant Jesse, from Bethlehem."
