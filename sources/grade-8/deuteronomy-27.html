Keep the Commands and Set Up Stones

1 Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, “Keep every command I give you today.”

2 On the day you cross the Jordan into the land the LORD your God gives you, set up large stones for yourself and coat them with plaster.

3 Write on them all the words of this law when you cross over, so that you may enter the land the LORD your God gives you, a land rich with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, said to you.

4 When you cross the Jordan, set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I command you today, and coat them with plaster.

5 Build an altar there to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. Do not use iron tools on them.

6 Build the altar of the LORD your God with whole stones, and offer burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.

7 Sacrifice peace offerings there, eat them, and rejoice before the LORD your God.

8 Write all the words of this law on the stones very clearly.

9 Moses and the priests, who are Levites, spoke to all Israel, saying, “Be quiet and listen, Israel! Today you have become the people of the LORD your God.

10 So obey the voice of the LORD your God and do His commands and rules that I give you today.”

Blessings and Curses on Two Mountains

11 That same day Moses commanded the people, saying, 12 “When you cross the Jordan, these tribes shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin. 13 These tribes shall stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.”

The Levites Pronounce Curses

14 The Levites shall say in a loud voice to all the men of Israel:

15 “Cursed is the man who makes a carved image or a metal image, something the LORD hates, made by a craftsman's hands, and sets it up in secret.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

16 “Cursed is anyone who dishonors his father or his mother.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

17 “Cursed is anyone who moves his neighbor's boundary stone.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

18 “Cursed is anyone who misleads a blind person on the road.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

19 “Cursed is anyone who twists justice for a foreigner, an orphan, or a widow.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

20 “Cursed is anyone who sleeps with his father's wife and shames his father.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

21 “Cursed is anyone who has sexual relations with any animal.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

22 “Cursed is anyone who sleeps with his sister, whether she is his father's daughter or his mother's daughter.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

23 “Cursed is anyone who sleeps with his mother-in-law.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

24 “Cursed is anyone who attacks his neighbor in secret.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

25 “Cursed is anyone who takes a bribe to kill an innocent person.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”

26 “Cursed is anyone who does not keep the words of this law by doing them.”

And all the people shall answer, “Amen.”
