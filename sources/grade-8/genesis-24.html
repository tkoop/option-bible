Abraham Sends His Servant to Find a Wife for Isaac

1 Abraham was old and had lived a long life. The LORD had blessed him in everything.

2 Abraham said to his oldest servant, the one in charge of everything he owned, "Put your hand under my thigh. 3 Swear by the LORD, the God of heaven and the God of earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites among whom I live. 4 Instead, go to my country and my family, and get a wife for my son Isaac."

5 The servant asked him, "What if the woman does not want to follow me to this land? Should I take your son back to the land you came from?"

6 Abraham said, "Make sure you do not take my son back there. 7 The LORD, the God of heaven, who took me from my father's house and my homeland, spoke to me and promised me with an oath that He would give this land to my descendants. He will send His angel ahead of you, and you will bring back a wife for my son from there. 8 If the woman is not willing to follow you, then you are free from this oath. Only do not take my son back there."

9 So the servant put his hand under Abraham's thigh and swore to him about this matter.

The Servant Meets Rebekah at the Well

10 The servant took ten of his master's camels and left, carrying all kinds of good things from his master. He set out for the city of Nahor in Mesopotamia. 11 He had the camels kneel down by the well outside the city. It was evening, the time when women come out to draw water.

12 He said, "the LORD, God of my master Abraham, please give me success today and show kindness to my master Abraham. 13 See, I am standing by the spring, and the daughters of the men of this city are coming out to draw water. 14 Let the young woman to whom I say, 'Please lower your jar so I can drink,' and who answers, 'Drink, and I will water your camels too,' be the one You have chosen for Your servant Isaac. By this I will know that You have shown kindness to my master."

15 Before he finished speaking, Rebekah came out. She was born to Bethuel son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham's brother Nahor. She carried a jar on her shoulder. 16 The young woman was very beautiful, a virgin, and no man had been with her. She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came back up.

17 The servant ran to meet her and said, "Please let me have a little water from your jar."

18 "Drink, my lord," she said. She quickly lowered her jar onto her hand and gave him a drink. 19 When she had finished giving him a drink, she said, "I will draw water for your camels too, until they have had enough to drink." 20 She quickly emptied her jar into the trough, ran back to the well to draw more water, and drew enough for all his camels.

21 The man watched her quietly to learn whether the LORD had made his journey successful or not. 22 When the camels had finished drinking, the man took a gold nose ring weighing a beka and two gold bracelets weighing ten shekels, and put them on her wrists.

23 He asked, "Whose daughter are you? Please tell me. Is there room in your father's house for us to spend the night?"

24 "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor," she answered. 25 She also said to him, "We have plenty of straw and feed, and room for you to spend the night."

26 Then the man bowed down and worshiped the LORD.

27 He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not stopped showing kindness and truth to my master. As for me, the LORD has led me on the right road to the house of my master's relatives."

Rebekah's Family Welcomes the Servant

28 The young woman ran and told her mother's household about these things.

29 Rebekah had a brother named Laban. He ran out to the man at the spring. 30 When he saw the nose ring and the bracelets on his sister's wrists, and when he heard Rebekah say what the man had told her, he went out to the man. There he was, standing by the camels at the spring.

31 Laban said, "Come in, you who are blessed by the LORD. Why are you standing outside? I have prepared the house and a place for the camels."

32 So the man went into the house. He unloaded the camels and gave them straw and feed. He also brought water to wash his feet and the feet of the men with him.

33 Food was set before him, but he said, "I will not eat until I have said what I need to say."

"Speak," they said.

34 He said, "I am Abraham's servant.

35 "The the LORD has greatly blessed my master, and he has become very rich. He has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, male and female servants, camels and donkeys. 36 My master's wife Sarah bore a son to him in her old age, and my master has given him everything he owns.

37 "My master made me swear, 'You must not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, where I live. 38 Instead, go to my father's house and my family, and get a wife for my son.' 39 I asked my master, 'What if the woman will not follow me?' 40 He answered, 'The the LORD, before whom I have walked, will send His angel with you and make your journey successful, so you can get a wife for my son from my family and my father's house.' 41 You will be free from my oath if you go to my family and they refuse to give her to you. Then you will be free from my oath.'

42 "Today I came to the spring and said, 'The the LORD, God of my master Abraham, if You are going to make my journey successful, please help me.' 43 See, I am standing by the spring. When the young woman comes out to draw water, I will say to her, 'Please give me a little water from your jar.' 44 She will answer, 'Drink, and I will draw water for your camels too.' Let that woman be the one the LORD has chosen for my master's son.

45 "Before I finished speaking in my heart, Rebekah came out with her jar on her shoulder. She went down to the spring and drew water. I said to her, 'Please give me a drink.' 46 She quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will water your camels too.' So I drank, and she watered the camels. 47 I asked her, 'Whose daughter are you?' She said, 'I am the daughter of Bethuel son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.' Then I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her wrists. 48 Then I bowed down and worshiped the LORD. I praised the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who led me on the right road to get the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

49 "Now, if you are going to show kindness and truth to my master, tell me. If not, tell me, so I will know which way to turn."

50 Laban and Bethuel answered, "This matter comes from the LORD. We cannot say anything to you, either good or bad." 51 "Rebekah is here in front of you. Take her and go, and let her be the wife of your master's son, as the LORD has spoken."

52 When Abraham's servant heard their words, he bowed to the ground before the LORD.

53 Then the servant brought out silver and gold jewelry and clothes and gave them to Rebekah. He also gave costly gifts to her brother and her mother.

54 He and the men with him ate and drank, and they spent the night there. When they got up in the morning, he said, "Send me back to my master."

55 Her brother and her mother said, "Let the young woman stay with us for ten days or so. After that, she can go."

56 But he said, "Do not delay me. The LORD has made my journey successful. Send me on my way so I can go to my master."

57 They said, "Let us call the young woman and ask her."

58 They called Rebekah and asked her, "Will you go with this man?" She said, "I will go."

59 So they sent their sister Rebekah on her way, along with her nurse and Abraham's servant and his men.

60 They blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, may you become the mother of thousands upon thousands. May your descendants take over the cities of their enemies."

61 Then Rebekah and her young women got up, rode on the camels, and followed the man. So the servant took Rebekah and left.

Isaac Meets Rebekah and Takes Her as His Wife

62 Now Isaac had come from Beer-lahai-roi and was living in the Negev. 63 In the evening Isaac went out to the field to pray. He looked up and saw camels coming.

64 Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac. She got down from her camel.

65 She asked the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?"

The servant said, "He is my master." So she took her veil and covered herself.

66 Then the servant told Isaac everything he had done.

67 Isaac brought her into the tent of his mother Sarah. He married Rebekah, and she became his wife. He loved her, and she comforted him after his mother's death.
