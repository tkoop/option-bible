For the director of music. A psalm of David.

A Prayer for Protection from Evil People

1 Rescue me, the LORD, from evil men; protect me from men of violence, 2 who plan evil in their hearts and stir up war all day long. 3 They sharpen their tongues like a snake; viper's venom is under their lips. Selah 4 Guard me, the LORD, from the hands of the wicked; protect me from men of violence who plan to make me stumble. 5 Proud men have hidden a trap for me; they have spread cords as a net along my path and set snares for me. Selah

A Cry for Mercy

6 I said to the LORD, “You are my God.” Hear my cry for mercy, the LORD. 7 the LORD, my Lord, You are my strong Savior; You shield my head in the day of battle. 8 Do not give the wicked what they want, the LORD; do not let their plans succeed, or they will become proud. Selah

Judgment on the Wicked

9 May the heads of those who surround me be covered with the trouble their own lips have caused. 10 May burning coals fall on them; may they be thrown into the fire, into deep pits, never to rise again. 11 May slanderers not last in the land; may disaster hunt down men of violence.

The LORD Defends the Needy

12 I know that the LORD brings justice to the poor and stands up for the needy. 13 Surely the righteous will praise Your name; the upright will live in Your presence.
