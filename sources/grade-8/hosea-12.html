Ephraim Chases Empty Things

1 Ephraim chases empty, destructive things all day long. He piles up lies and violence. He makes a treaty with Assyria and sends oil to Egypt.

The LORD’s Case and Jacob’s Struggle

2 The LORD has a case against Judah. He will punish Jacob for what he has done. He will pay him back for his deeds.

3 In the womb, Jacob grabbed his brother’s heel. In his strength, he wrestled with God.

4 He wrestled with the angel and won. He cried and begged for God’s favor. God found him at Bethel, and there He spoke with us.

5 The LORD is the God of Hosts. The LORD is His name.

A Call to Return

6 So you should return to your God. Keep showing love and doing what is right. Wait for your God all the time.

Pride, Sin, and Punishment

7 A merchant loves to cheat with false scales in his hand.

8 Ephraim says, “I have become very rich. I have found wealth for myself. No one will find any wrong or sin in all my work.”

9 “I am the LORD, your God, who brought you out of Egypt. I will make you live in tents again, like in the days of the festival.

10 I spoke through the prophets. I gave many visions. I used the prophets to teach lessons.”

11 If there is sin in Gilead, it is worthless. In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls. Their altars are like piles of stones in a plowed field.

12 Jacob fled to the land of Aram. Israel worked for a wife, and he tended sheep for a wife.

13 The LORD used a prophet to bring Israel out of Egypt. He used a prophet to protect them.

14 Ephraim has made the LORD very angry. So his Lord will hold him guilty for his bloodshed and repay him for his scorn.
