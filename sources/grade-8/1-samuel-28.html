Philistines Prepare for War

1 In those days the Philistines gathered their forces to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "You need to know that you and your men will march out with me in the army."

2 David said to Achish, "Then you will know what your servant will do."

Achish said to David, "Then I will make you my bodyguard for life."

Saul Seeks a Medium

3 Now Samuel was dead. All Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, in his own town. Saul had removed the mediums and spiritists from the land.

4 The Philistines gathered and camped at Shunem. Saul gathered all Israel, and they camped at Gilboa.

5 When Saul saw the Philistine camp, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly.

6 Saul asked the LORD for help, but the LORD did not answer him, not by dreams, not by the Urim, and not by prophets.

7 Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium, so I can go to her and ask her about this."

His servants said to him, "There is a woman who is a medium at Endor."

The Medium at Endor

8 So Saul disguised himself and put on other clothes. He went with two men, and they came to the woman at night. He said, "Please call up a spirit for me, and bring up for me the one I name."

9 The woman said to him, "You know what Saul has done. He has cut off the mediums and spiritists from the land. Why are you trying to trap me and get me killed?"

10 Saul swore to her by the LORD, "As the LORD lives, no punishment will come to you for this."

11 Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?"

He said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

12 When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out loudly. She said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!"

13 The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?"

The woman said to Saul, "I see a god coming up out of the earth."

14 He said, "What does he look like?"

She said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wearing a robe."

Then Saul knew it was Samuel. He bowed with his face to the ground.

Samuel Speaks to Saul

15 Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?"

Saul said, "I am in great trouble. The Philistines are fighting against me, and God has turned away from me. He no longer answers me, neither by prophets nor by dreams. So I have called you to tell me what I should do."

16 Samuel said, "Why do you ask me, since the LORD has turned from you and become your enemy?"

17 The LORD has done to you what He said through me. He has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbor David.

18 Because you did not obey the LORD and did not carry out His fierce anger against Amalek, the LORD has done this to you today.

19 The LORD will also give Israel and you into the hand of the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The LORD will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines.

20 Then Saul quickly fell full length on the ground. He was very afraid because of Samuel's words. He had no strength, because he had not eaten food all day and all night.

21 The woman came to Saul and saw that he was very upset. She said, "Look, your servant has listened to your voice. I put my life in my hands and obeyed what you said to me."

22 "Now please listen to your servant. Let me set a piece of bread before you, and eat, so you will have strength when you go on your way."

23 But he refused and said, "I will not eat." His servants and the woman urged him, and he listened to their voice. Then he got up from the ground and sat on the bed.

24 The woman had a fattened calf in the house. She hurried and slaughtered it. She took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread.

25 She set it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they got up and went away that night.
