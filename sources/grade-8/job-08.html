1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:

God's Justice and Job's Children

2 "How long will you say such things? Your words are just hot air. 3 Does God twist justice? Does the Almighty twist what is right? 4 If your children sinned against Him, He let their own wrongdoing destroy them.

5 "But if you seek God and plead with the Almighty, 6 if you are pure and upright, surely now He will rouse Himself for you and restore your rightful place. 7 Your beginnings will seem small, but your future will grow very great.

Lessons from the Past

8 "Ask the former generation and learn from what their fathers discovered. 9 For we have lived only a short time and know nothing, because our days on earth are like a shadow. 10 Will they not teach you and tell you? Will they not share what they know?

The Godless and the Blameless

11 "Can papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Can reeds flourish without water? 12 While still green and uncut, they wither faster than grass. 13 Such is the fate of all who forget God; so the hope of the godless dies.

14 "His confidence is fragile; his security is a spider's web. 15 He leans on his house, but it gives way; he clings to it, but it does not hold.

16 "He is like a well-watered plant in the sunshine, with shoots spreading over his garden. 17 His roots wrap around a rock pile; he looks for a place among the stones. 18 If he is uprooted from his place, it will disown him, saying, 'I never saw you.' 19 Surely this is the joy of his way, and out of the earth others will spring up.

20 "See, God does not reject a blameless person, and He will not help those who do evil. 21 He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouts of joy. 22 Your enemies will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more."
