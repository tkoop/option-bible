Four Kings Fight Five Kings

1 In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim, 2 these kings made war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar. 3 All of them joined together in the Valley of Siddim, which is the Salt Sea. 4 They had served Chedorlaomer for twelve years, but in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

5 In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings with him came and defeated the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim, 6 and the Horites in their hill country of Seir, all the way to El-paran near the desert. 7 Then they turned back and came to En-mishpat, which is Kadesh, and defeated all the country of the Amalekites and also the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.

8 The king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out and lined up for battle against them in the Valley of Siddim. 9 They fought four kings against five. 10 The Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits, and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled and fell into them, while the rest fled to the hills. 11 The invaders took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food and left. 12 They also took Lot, Abram's nephew, and his possessions, because he was living in Sodom, and they left.

Abram Rescues Lot

13 Then a man who had escaped came and told Abram the Hebrew. Abram was living near the oak trees of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and the brother of Aner. These men had made an agreement with Abram. 14 When Abram heard that his relative had been captured, he led out his trained men, born in his house—318 of them—and chased the enemy as far as Dan. 15 He split his forces against them at night, he and his servants, and defeated them. He chased them as far as Hobah, north of Damascus. 16 He brought back all the goods. He also brought back his relative Lot and his goods, and the women and the people.

Melchizedek Blesses Abram

17 After Abram returned from defeating Chedorlaomer and the kings with him, the king of Sodom came out to meet him in the Valley of Shaveh, which is the King's Valley. 18 Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought out bread and wine. He was priest of God Most High. 19 He blessed Abram and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth; 20 and blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies into your hand." Then Abram gave him a tenth of everything.

Abram Refuses the King of Sodom's Offer

21 The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people, but you may keep the goods." 22 Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I have sworn to the LORD, God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth, 23 that I will not take a thread or a sandal strap from anything that is yours, so that you cannot say, 'I made Abram rich.' 24 I will take nothing except what the young men have eaten, and the share for the men who went with me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them take their share."
