Jesus Turns Water into Wine

1 On the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee. Jesus' mother was there, 2 and Jesus and His disciples were also invited.

3 When the wine ran out, Jesus' mother said to Him, "They have no more wine."

4 "Woman, why do you concern Me?" Jesus replied. "My hour has not yet come."

5 His mother said to the servants, "Do whatever He tells you."

6 Nearby stood six stone water jars used for Jewish ceremonial washing. Each held about twenty or thirty gallons.

7 Jesus said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water." So they filled them to the top. 8 Then He told them, "Now draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet." They did so.

9 The master of the banquet tasted the water that had become wine. He did not know where it came from, but the servants who had drawn the water knew. Then he called the bridegroom over. 10 He said, "Everyone serves the best wine first, and then the cheaper wine after the guests have had too much to drink. But you have saved the best wine until now."

11 This was the first sign Jesus did in Cana of Galilee. It showed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.

12 After this, He went down to Capernaum with His mother, His brothers, and His disciples. They stayed there for a few days.

Jesus Clears the Temple

13 The Jewish Passover was near, so Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 14 In the temple courts He found people selling cattle, sheep, and doves. Others were sitting at tables exchanging money.

15 He made a whip from cords and drove everyone out of the temple courts, along with the sheep and cattle. He scattered the coins of the money changers and turned over their tables.

16 To those selling doves He said, "Get these out of here! Do not turn My Father's house into a market!"

17 His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for Your house will consume Me."

18 The Jews then asked Him, "What sign can You show us to prove You have authority to do all this?"

19 Jesus answered them, "Destroy this temple, and I will raise it again in three days."

20 They replied, "It took forty-six years to build this temple, and You are going to raise it in three days?"

21 But the temple He meant was His body. 22 After He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered what He had said. Then they believed the Scripture and the words Jesus had spoken.

Jesus Knows What Is in People

23 While He was in Jerusalem for the Passover Festival, many people saw the signs He was doing and believed in His name. 24 But Jesus did not trust Himself to them, because He knew all people. 25 He did not need anyone to tell Him about a person, for He knew what was in each one.
