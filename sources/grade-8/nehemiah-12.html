Priests and Levites Who Returned

1 These are the priests and Levites who came up with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra, 2 Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, 3 Shechaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, 4 Iddo, Ginnetho, Abijah, 5 Miamin, Maadiah, Bilgah, 6 Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, 7 Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. These were the heads of the priests and their relatives in the days of Jeshua.

8 The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who led the thanksgiving songs, he and his relatives. 9 Bakbukiah and Unni, their relatives, stood across from them during their shifts.

10 Jeshua was the father of Joiakim, Joiakim was the father of Eliashib, and Eliashib was the father of Joiada. 11 Joiada was the father of Jonathan, and Jonathan was the father of Jaddua.

Family Lines of the Priests

12 In the days of Joiakim, these were the family heads of the priests: of Seraiah, Meraiah; of Jeremiah, Hananiah; 13 of Ezra, Meshullam; of Amariah, Jehohanan; 14 of Melicu, Jonathan; of Shebaniah, Joseph; 15 of Harim, Adna; of Meraioth, Helkai; 16 of Iddo, Zechariah; of Ginnethon, Meshullam; 17 of Abijah, Zichri; of Miniamin, Piltai; 18 of Bilgah, Shammua; of Shemaiah, Jonathan; 19 of Joiarib, Mattenai; of Jedaiah, Uzzi; 20 of Sallai, Kallai; of Amok, Eber; 21 of Hilkiah, Hashabiah; and of Jedaiah, Nethaneel.

22 In the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, the Levites and priests were recorded as family heads until the reign of Darius the Persian. 23 The sons of Levi, the family heads, were written in the Book of the Chronicles up to the days of Johanan son of Eliashib.

24 The leaders of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua son of Kadmiel, and their relatives. They stood opposite each other to praise and give thanks, one group facing another, as David, the man of God, had commanded. 25 Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers who guarded the storerooms at the gates. 26 These served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and scribe.

Dedication of Jerusalem's Wall

27 At the dedication of Jerusalem's wall, they sought the Levites from all their places to bring them to Jerusalem. They were to celebrate the dedication with joy, thanksgiving, singing, and with cymbals, harps, and lyres. 28 The singers gathered from the area around Jerusalem and from the villages of the Netophathites. 29 They also came from Beth-gilgal and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth, because the singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem. 30 The priests and Levites purified themselves, and then they purified the people, the gates, and the wall.

31 Then I brought the leaders of Judah up onto the wall and set up two large thanksgiving choirs. One went to the right on top of the wall toward the Dung Gate. 32 Hoshaiah and half the leaders of Judah followed them: 33 Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam, 34 Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah. 35 Some of the priests' sons played trumpets: Zechariah son of Jonathan, son of Shemaiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Micaiah, son of Zaccur, son of Asaph. 36 His relatives were Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethaneel, Judah, and Hanani. They played the musical instruments of David, the man of God. Ezra the scribe went before them. 37 At the Fountain Gate they went straight up the steps of the City of David, along the ascent of the wall, above David's house, to the Water Gate on the east.

38 The second thanksgiving choir went the other way. I followed it with the other half of the people on top of the wall. We passed the Tower of the Furnaces to the Broad Wall. 39 We passed above the Gate of Ephraim, the Old Gate, the Fish Gate, the Tower of Hananel, the Tower of the Hundred, and stopped at the Sheep Gate. We stood at the Gate of the Guard. 40 So the two thanksgiving choirs stood in God's house. I stood there too, along with half the officials. 41 The priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah played trumpets. 42 Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Johanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer also played. The singers sang loudly, led by Jezrahiah. 43 That day they offered great sacrifices and rejoiced, because God had given them great joy. The women and children rejoiced too, and the joy of Jerusalem was heard far away.

Support for the Temple Workers

44 On that day men were appointed over the storerooms for the contributions, firstfruits, and tithes. They were to gather into them, from the fields of the cities, the portions required by the Law for the priests and Levites. Judah rejoiced over the priests and Levites who served. 45 They performed the service of their God and the service of purification, as did the singers and gatekeepers. They followed the commands of David and his son Solomon. 46 For in the days of David and Asaph, long ago, there were leaders of the singers and songs of praise and thanksgiving to God. 47 In the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel gave daily portions to the singers and gatekeepers. They set apart what was holy for the Levites, and the Levites set apart what was holy for the sons of Aaron.
