The Lord's Messenger and the Refiner

1 The LORD says, "Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will prepare the way before Me. Then the Lord you are looking for will suddenly come to His temple. The messenger of the covenant, whom you want, is coming," says the LORD of Hosts.

2 But who can survive the day He comes? Who can stand when He appears? He will be like a fire that refines metal or like strong soap that cleans. 3 He will sit and refine silver. He will purify the Levites and refine them like gold and silver. Then they will bring offerings to the LORD in the right way. 4 Then the offerings of Judah and Jerusalem will please the LORD, as they did long ago, in earlier years.

5 "So I will come to judge you. I will be quick to speak against those who practice witchcraft, those who commit adultery, those who lie under oath, those who cheat workers out of their pay, those who take advantage of widows and orphans, and those who treat foreigners among you unfairly and do not fear Me," says the LORD of Hosts.

Return to God

6 "I am the LORD, and I do not change. That is why you, Jacob's descendants, have not been destroyed. 7 Since the days of your ancestors, you have turned away from My laws and have not obeyed them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the LORD of Hosts. "But you ask, 'How can we return?'"

8 "Will a person rob God? Yet you rob Me. But you ask, 'How do we rob You?' In tithes and offerings."

9 Your whole nation is under a curse because you are robbing Me.

Bring the Whole Tithe

10 "Bring all the tithes into the storehouse so there will be food in My house. Test Me in this," says the LORD of Hosts. "See if I will not open the windows of heaven and pour out so much blessing that you will not have enough room for it." 11 "I will stop the pests from destroying your crops, and your vines in the field will not lose their fruit before harvest," says the LORD of Hosts. 12 "Then all the nations will call you blessed, because your land will be a delight," says the LORD of Hosts.

Words Against the the LORD

13 "You have spoken harsh words against Me," says the LORD. "Yet you ask, 'What have we said against You?' 14 You have said, 'Serving God is useless. What do we gain by obeying His rules and walking around sadly before the LORD of Hosts? 15 But now we call proud people blessed. Evildoers get ahead. They even test God and escape punishment.'"

16 Then those who feared the LORD spoke with each other. The LORD listened and heard them. A book of remembrance was written before Him for those who feared the LORD and honored His name.

17 "They will be Mine," says the LORD of Hosts, "on the day I act. I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him." 18 "And you will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between those who serve God and those who do not."
