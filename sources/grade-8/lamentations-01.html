Jerusalem Sits Alone

1 How lonely the city sits now! It once had many people. It was great among the nations, but now it is like a widow. It was a princess among the regions, but now it pays heavy taxes like a slave. 2 She cries hard at night, and tears run down her cheeks. No one comforts her, not even those who once loved her. All her friends betrayed her and became her enemies. 3 Judah was carried away because of suffering and hard work. Now she lives among other nations and finds no rest. Her enemies caught her in narrow places. 4 The roads to Zion are sad because no one comes for the festivals. The city gates are empty. The priests groan. The young women suffer, and Zion itself is bitter.

The LORD Punishes His People

5 Her enemies are now in charge. They succeed because the LORD punished her for her many sins. Her children were taken away as prisoners. 6 All the beauty has left Zion. Her leaders are like deer with no grass to eat. They had no strength and ran from the hunter. 7 In her suffering and wandering, Jerusalem remembers all the good things she had long ago. When her people fell to the enemy, no one helped her. Her enemies saw her and laughed at her ruin. 8 Jerusalem sinned badly, so she became unclean. Everyone who honored her now hates her, because they saw her shame. She groans and turns away.

Jerusalem Cries for Mercy

9 Her filth was on her clothes. She did not think about what would happen next. Her fall was terrible, and no one comforts her. "Look, the LORD, at my suffering, because the enemy has won!" 10 The enemy took all her treasures. She saw nations enter her holy place, even though You had said they could not join Your people. 11 All her people groan while looking for bread. They give up their treasures for food to stay alive. "Look, the LORD, and see, for I am treated with contempt."

12 Does my pain mean nothing to you who pass by? Look and see if anyone has pain like mine. The LORD brought this on me in His fierce anger. 13 From above He sent fire into my bones and overpowered me. He spread a net for my feet and turned me back. He left me empty and weak all day. 14 My sins were tied into a heavy yoke. By His hand they were fastened together and put on my neck. He made my strength fail. The Lord handed me over to people I cannot resist. 15 The Lord threw away all my strong men. He gathered an army to crush my young men. The Lord trampled the young women of Judah like grapes in a winepress. 16 Because of this I cry. My eyes pour out tears. No one comforts me or gives me strength. My children are ruined because the enemy won.

17 Zion reaches out her hands, but no one comforts her. The LORD ordered that Jacob's neighbors become his enemies. Jerusalem is treated like something dirty among them. 18 The LORD is right, because I rebelled against Him. Listen, all you nations, and see my pain. My young men and young women were taken captive. 19 I called to my allies, but they tricked me. My priests and leaders died in the city while searching for food to stay alive. 20 Look, the LORD, I am in trouble. My stomach turns, and my heart aches inside me, because I rebelled badly. Outside, the sword kills; inside, it feels like death. 21 They heard my groaning, yet no one comforts me. All my enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that You have done it. You brought the day You announced; now let them be like me. 22 Let all their evil come before You. Treat them as You treated me because of all my sins. My groans are many, and my heart is weak.
