All Nations Praise the LORD

1 Clap your hands, all you nations; shout to God with cries of joy! 2 For the LORD Most High is awesome; He is the great King over all the earth. 3 He gave us power over nations and peoples. 4 He chose our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah.

God Rules Over All the Earth

5 God has gone up with a shout of joy, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet. 6 Sing praises to God, sing praises! Sing praises to our King, sing praises! 7 For God is the King of all the earth; sing praises with understanding. 8 God rules over the nations; God sits on His holy throne. 9 The leaders of the nations gather together as the people of the God of Abraham, for the rulers of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted.
