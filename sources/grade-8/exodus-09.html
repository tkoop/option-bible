The Plague on Egypt's Livestock

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and tell him, 'This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let My people go so they can serve Me. 2 But if you refuse to let them go and still keep them, 3 then the hand of the LORD will bring a terrible disease on your livestock in the fields—on the horses, donkeys, camels, cattle, and sheep. 4 The LORD will make a difference between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, so none of the animals that belong to the Israelites will die. 5 The LORD set a time and said, 'Tomorrow the LORD will do this in the land.' 6 The next day the LORD did it. All the livestock of the Egyptians died, but not one animal from the Israelites' livestock died. 7 Pharaoh sent men to check, and not one animal from the Israelites' livestock was dead. But Pharaoh's heart was hard, and he would not let the people go.

The Plague of Boils

8 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Take handfuls of soot from a furnace, and let Moses throw it toward the sky in front of Pharaoh. 9 It will become fine dust over all the land of Egypt, and it will turn into boils that break out into sores on people and animals all over Egypt." 10 So they took soot from the furnace and stood in front of Pharaoh. Moses threw it up toward the sky, and it became boils that broke out into sores on people and animals. 11 The magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils, because the boils were on the magicians and on all the Egyptians. 12 But the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had said to Moses.

The Plague of Hail

13 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Get up early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh. Tell him, 'This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let My people go so they can serve Me. 14 This time I will send all My plagues on you personally, on your officials, and on your people, so you will know there is no one like Me in all the earth. 15 By now I could have stretched out My hand and struck you and your people with disease, and you would have been wiped off the earth. 16 But I have let you live for this reason: to show you My power and to make My name known in all the earth. 17 You are still setting yourself up against My people and will not let them go. 18 So tomorrow at this time I will send a very heavy hailstorm. There has never been a storm like it in Egypt since the day it was founded. 19 Now send word and bring your livestock and everything you have in the fields into shelter. Every person and animal left in the fields will die when the hail comes down on them.'"

20 Some of Pharaoh's officials who feared the LORD's word hurried their servants and livestock into the houses. 21 But those who did not take the LORD's word seriously left their servants and livestock in the fields.

22 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, so hail will fall on all the land of Egypt—on people, animals, and every plant in the fields throughout Egypt." 23 Moses stretched out his staff toward the sky, and the LORD sent thunder and hail, with lightning running across the ground. The LORD rained hail on the land of Egypt. 24 The hail was very heavy, with lightning flashing in it. There had never been a storm like it in Egypt since it became a nation. 25 The hail struck everything in the fields throughout Egypt—people, animals, every plant, and every tree in the fields. 26 Only in the land of Goshen, where the Israelites lived, there was no hail.

Pharaoh Confesses and Asks for Relief

27 Then Pharaoh sent for Moses and Aaron and said to them, "This time I have sinned. The LORD is right, and my people and I are wrong. 28 Pray to the LORD, because there has been enough of God's thunder and hail. I will let you go, and you will not have to stay any longer."

29 Moses said to him, "As soon as I leave the city, I will spread out my hands to the LORD. The thunder will stop, and there will be no more hail, so you may know that the earth belongs to the LORD. 30 But I know that you and your officials still do not fear the LORD God."

31 The flax and barley were ruined because the barley was ripe and the flax was in bloom. 32 But the wheat and spelt were not ruined because they grow later. 33 Moses left Pharaoh and went out of the city. He spread out his hands to the LORD, and the thunder and hail stopped. Rain no longer poured down on the earth. 34 When Pharaoh saw that the rain, hail, and thunder had stopped, he sinned again. He and his officials hardened their hearts. 35 Pharaoh's heart was hard, and he did not let the Israelites go, just as the LORD had said through Moses.
