Respect Government Authority

1 Everyone must submit to the governing authorities, because no authority exists except from God, and the authorities that do exist have been appointed by God. 2 So whoever resists the authority resists what God has appointed, and those who resist will bring judgment on themselves. 3 For rulers are not a terror to good conduct, but to bad. Do you want to be free from fear of the authority? Do what is good, and you will receive praise from it. 4 For it is God's servant for your good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, for it does not bear the sword in vain. It is God's servant, an avenger who brings wrath on the one who practices evil.

5 So you must submit, not only to avoid punishment, but also to keep a clear conscience. 6 For this reason you also pay taxes, because the authorities are servants of God who give their full time to this work. 7 Pay everyone what you owe: taxes to those who collect taxes, customs to those who collect customs, respect to those who deserve respect, and honor to those who deserve honor.

Love One Another

8 Owe no one anything, except to love one another, for the one who loves another has fulfilled the law. 9 For the commandments, "You shall not commit adultery," "You shall not murder," "You shall not steal," "You shall not covet," and any other commandment, are summed up in this word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." 10 Love does no wrong to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.

Wake Up and Live in the Light

11 Do this, understanding the present time: it is already the hour for you to wake up from sleep, because our salvation is closer now than when we first believed. 12 The night is far gone; the day is at hand. So let us cast off the works of darkness and put on the armor of light. 13 Let us live decently, as in the daytime, not in wild parties or drunkenness, not in sexual sin or shameless behavior, not in fighting or jealousy. 14 But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and do not make provision for the flesh, to gratify its desires.
