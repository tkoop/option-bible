David Gathers Israel's Leaders

1 David assembled all the leaders of Israel in Jerusalem: the leaders of the tribes, the leaders of the divisions that served the king, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, the officials in charge of all the king's property and livestock, the palace officials, the warriors, and all the brave fighting men.

David's Farewell Speech

2 David stood up and said, "Listen to me, my brothers and my people. I had it in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD and for the footstool of our God, and I made plans to build it. 3 But God told me that I was not to build a house for His name, because I was a man of war who had shed blood.

4 "The LORD, the God of Israel, chose me from all my father's family to be king over Israel forever. He chose Judah to lead, and from Judah's family He chose my father's family. Among my father's sons, He was pleased to make me king over all Israel. 5 And from all my sons—for the LORD has given me many sons—He chose my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the kingdom of the LORD over Israel.

6 "He told me that my son Solomon would build His house and His courts, for He chose him to be His son, and He would be his Father. 7 He promised to make his kingdom last forever, if he stayed strong to obey His commands and His rules, as he does today.

8 "So now, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and while our God listens, keep and seek all the commands of the LORD your God. Then you will possess this good land and leave it as an inheritance to your children after you forever.

9 "And you, my son Solomon, know the God of your father. Serve Him with a whole heart and a willing mind. For the LORD searches every heart and understands every thought. If you seek Him, He will be found by you. But if you forsake Him, He will cast you off forever. 10 Consider now that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it."

Plans for the Temple

11 David gave Solomon the plans for the porch and its buildings, storerooms, upper rooms, inner rooms, and the room for the mercy seat. 12 He gave him the plans for the courts of the LORD's house and all the rooms around it, for the storerooms of God's house and the storerooms for the dedicated things. 13 He gave plans for the divisions of the priests and Levites and for all the work of service in the LORD's house, and for all the articles used in that service.

14 He gave the weight of gold for all the gold articles used in every kind of service, and the weight of silver for all the silver articles used in every kind of service. 15 He gave the weight for the gold lampstands and their lamps, for each lampstand and its lamps, and for the silver lampstands and their lamps, for each lampstand and its lamps, according to the work of each one. 16 He gave the weight of gold for the tables of the showbread, for each table, and silver for the silver tables. 17 He gave pure gold for the forks, bowls, and cups, and the weight of gold for each gold basin; and silver for the silver bowls, with the weight for each one. 18 He gave the weight of refined gold for the altar of incense, and gold for the plan of the chariot—the cherubim that spread their wings and cover the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

19 David said, "The LORD made all this clear to me in writing by His hand upon me—all the details of the plan."

David Encourages Solomon

20 David said to Solomon, "Be strong and courageous, and do the work. Do not be afraid or discouraged, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you or forsake you until all the work for the service of the LORD's house is finished. 21 The divisions of the priests and Levites are ready for all the work on God's house. Every willing person skilled in any craft will help you, and the officials and all the people will obey your every command."
