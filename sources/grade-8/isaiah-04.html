Shame and the Need for a Name

1 On that day seven women will seize one man and say, “We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothes. Just let us be called by your name. Take away our shame.”

The Branch of the Lord

2 On that day the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious, and the land’s crops will be the pride and glory of Israel’s survivors. 3 Everyone left in Zion and everyone remaining in Jerusalem will be called holy—everyone recorded among the living in Jerusalem.

Cleansing and Protection

4 When the Lord has washed away the filth of Zion’s daughters and cleansed Jerusalem’s bloodstains from within her, by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning, 5 then the LORD will create over every dwelling place of Mount Zion and over her gatherings a cloud of smoke by day and a shining flame of fire by night. Over all the glory there will be a covering. 6 It will be a shelter from the heat by day and a refuge and hiding place from storm and rain.
