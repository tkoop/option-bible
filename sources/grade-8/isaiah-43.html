The LORD Promises to Rescue Israel

1 But now, the LORD says—He who created you, Jacob, and formed you, Israel: “Don’t be afraid, for I have rescued you. I have called you by name; you are Mine. 2 When you pass through the water, I will be with you, and the rivers won’t sweep you away. When you walk through fire, you won’t be burned, and the flames won’t hurt you.

3 “For I am the LORD, your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior. I gave Egypt to buy you back, and Cush and Seba to take your place. 4 Because you are precious to Me and honored, and I love you, I will give other people in your place and other nations in exchange for your life. 5 Don’t be afraid, for I am with you. I will bring your children from the east and gather you from the west.

6 “I will say to the north, ‘Give them up!’ and to the south, ‘Don’t hold them back.’ Bring My sons from far away and My daughters from the ends of the earth— 7 everyone who is called by My name, whom I created for My glory, whom I formed and made.”

The LORD Is the Only True God

8 Bring out the people who are blind but have eyes, and the deaf but have ears. 9 Let all the nations gather together, and let the peoples assemble. Who among them can tell us this and show us what happened before? Let them bring their witnesses to prove they are right, and let them hear and say, “It is true.”

10 “You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and My servant whom I have chosen, so that you may know and believe Me and understand that I am He. Before Me no god was formed, and after Me none will come.” 11 “I, yes I, am the LORD, and apart from Me there is no savior.”

12 “I have revealed and saved and proclaimed—I, and not some foreign god among you. You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and I am God.” 13 “Even from ancient times I am He, and no one can rescue anyone from My hand. When I act, who can undo it?”

The LORD Will Make a New Way

14 This is what the LORD says—your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “For your sake I will send to Babylon and bring them all down as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, in the ships they are so proud of.” 15 “I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, your King.”

16 This is what the LORD says—“He who makes a way through the sea and a path through the mighty waters, 17 who brings out chariot and horse, army and strong warrior. They lie down together; they do not rise again. They are snuffed out, quenched like a wick.”

18 “Don’t remember the former things; don’t think about the old things. 19 Look, I am doing something new; now it is springing up. Don’t you see it? I will make a way in the desert and rivers in the wasteland. 20 The wild animals will honor Me—the jackals and the ostriches—because I give water in the desert and streams in the wasteland, to give drink to My chosen people. 21 The people I formed for Myself will tell of My praise.”

Israel Has Not Been Faithful

22 “But you haven’t called on Me, O Jacob, and you have grown tired of Me, O Israel. 23 You haven’t brought Me sheep for your burnt offerings, and you haven’t honored Me with your sacrifices. I haven’t burdened you with grain offerings, and I haven’t wearied you with frankincense. 24 You haven’t bought Me sweet cane with money, and you haven’t satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices. But you have burdened Me with your sins, and you have wearied Me with your wrongs.”

25 “I, yes I, am He who blots out your sins for My own sake, and I will not remember your sins. 26 Remind Me; let us argue the case together. State your case, so that you may be proved right.”

27 “Your first father sinned, and your leaders rebelled against Me. 28 So I disgraced the leaders of the holy place, and I gave Jacob over to be destroyed and Israel to be mocked.”
