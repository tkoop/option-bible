Wisdom Calls Out

1 Does not Wisdom call out? Does not Understanding raise her voice? 2 She stands on the high places along the road, at the crossroads. 3 Beside the city gates, at the entrance, she cries out: 4 “To you, I call out, and my voice is for everyone. 5 Simple ones, learn to be wise; fools, gain understanding. 6 Listen, because I speak noble things, and my lips say what is right. 7 My mouth speaks truth, and wickedness is hateful to my lips. 8 All my words are right; none are twisted or crooked. 9 They are clear to anyone who understands, and right to those who find knowledge.”

Wisdom Is Better Than Wealth

10 Receive my teaching instead of silver, and knowledge instead of fine gold. 11 Wisdom is better than rubies, and nothing anyone desires compares with her. 12 I, Wisdom, live with Prudence, and I find knowledge and good judgment. 13 To fear the the LORD is to hate evil. I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and crooked speech. 14 I have good advice and sound judgment; I have understanding and strength. 15 By me kings rule, and rulers make just laws. 16 By me princes govern, and nobles judge with justice. 17 I love those who love me, and those who seek me early find me. 18 With me are riches and honor, lasting wealth and righteousness. 19 My fruit is better than gold, even pure gold, and what I produce is better than silver. 20 I walk in the way of righteousness, along the paths of justice. 21 I give wealth to those who love me and fill their treasuries.

Wisdom and Creation

22 The The LORD possessed me at the beginning of His work, before His deeds of old. 23 I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth began. 24 When there were no depths, I was born; when there were no springs heavy with water. 25 Before the mountains were settled and before the hills, I was born. 26 Before He made the earth and its fields, or the first dust of the world. 27 I was there when He established the heavens and drew a circle on the face of the deep. 28 When He made the clouds above and made the springs of the deep strong. 29 When He set a boundary for the sea so its waters would not cross His command, and when He laid the earth’s foundations. 30 Then I was beside Him like a skilled worker, and I was His delight day by day, rejoicing before Him at all times. 31 I rejoiced in His whole world and delighted in people.

The Blessing of Finding Wisdom

32 Now, my sons, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways. 33 Listen to instruction and be wise; do not ignore it. 34 Blessed is the person who listens to me, watching daily at my doors and waiting at my doorway. 35 For whoever finds me finds life and receives favor from the the LORD. 36 But whoever sins against me harms himself; all who hate me love death.
