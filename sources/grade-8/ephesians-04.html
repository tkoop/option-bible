Live Worthy of Your Calling

1 I am a prisoner because I serve the Lord, and I beg you to live in a way that matches the call you have received. 2 Be completely humble and gentle. Be patient, and put up with each other in love. 3 Work hard to keep the unity that the Spirit gives, joined together by peace. 4 There is one body and one Spirit. You were also called to one hope when you were called. 5 There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism. 6 There is one God and Father of all. He is over everything, through everything, and in everything.

Christ Gives Gifts to His Church

7 But each of us received grace as Christ gave it, measured out by His gift. 8 That is why Scripture says, "When He went up to heaven, He led captives with Him and gave gifts to people." 9 What does "He went up" mean? It means He also came down to the lower parts of the earth. 10 The One who came down is the same One who went up above all the heavens, so He could fill everything. 11 Christ gave some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to be evangelists, and some to be pastors and teachers. 12 He did this to prepare God's people for service and to build up the body of Christ. 13 We will keep growing until we all reach unity in the faith and in knowing the Son of God. Then we will be mature, fully grown like Christ. 14 Then we won't be like little children anymore, tossed around by waves and blown by every new teaching. We won't be tricked by clever people who make evil plans. 15 Instead, by speaking the truth in love, we will grow in every way into Christ, who is the head. 16 From Him the whole body is joined and held together. Every part does its work, and the body grows and builds itself up in love.

Live Differently Than the World

17 So I say this, and I say it strongly in the Lord: You must no longer live like the people who don't know God. They live in empty thinking. 18 Their minds are dark, and they are cut off from the life God gives because they don't know Him. Their hearts are hard. 19 They have lost all feeling and have given themselves over to wild living. They are greedy to do every kind of impure thing.

Put On the New Self

20 But that is not how you learned about Christ. 21 If you truly heard Him and were taught in Him, then the truth is in Jesus. 22 You were taught to take off your old self, which is ruined by lying desires. 23 Let your mind be made new. 24 Put on the new self, created to be like God in true right living and holiness.

Rules for Everyday Life

25 So each of you must stop lying and speak truth to your neighbor, because we are parts of one another. 26 "Be angry, but do not sin." Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry. 27 Do not give the devil a chance. 28 The one who steals must stop stealing. Instead, he should work hard with his hands to do good, so he can share with people in need. 29 Do not let any bad words come out of your mouths. Speak only what is good and helpful, so your words can help those who hear. 30 Do not make God's Holy Spirit sad. You were sealed by Him for the day when you will be set free. 31 Get rid of all bitterness, anger, yelling, and hurtful talk, along with every kind of evil. 32 Be kind and tender to each other. Forgive each other, just as God forgave you through Christ.
