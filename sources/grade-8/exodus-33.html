The LORD Sends Israel Toward the Promised Land

1 The LORD spoke to Moses, "Leave this place, you and the people you led out of Egypt. Go up to the land I promised with an oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.' 2 I will send an angel before you and drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 3 Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go up among you, because you are a stubborn people. Otherwise, I might destroy you on the way."

4 When the people heard this bad news, they mourned. No one put on his jewelry.

5 The LORD said to Moses, "Tell the Israelites, 'You are a stubborn people. If I went with you even for a moment, I would destroy you. Now take off your jewelry, and I will decide what to do with you.'"

6 So the Israelites took off their jewelry from Mount Horeb onward.

Moses Sets Up the Tent of Meeting

7 Moses used to take the tent and set it up outside the camp, far away from the camp. He called it the tent of meeting. Anyone who wanted to seek the the LORD would go out to the tent of meeting outside the camp. 8 Whenever Moses went out to the tent, all the people rose and stood at the entrances to their tents, watching Moses until he entered the tent.

9 As Moses went into the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance, while the the LORD spoke with Moses. 10 Whenever the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance to the tent, they all stood and worshiped, each at the entrance to his tent. 11 The the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as one person speaks to a friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his young aide Joshua son of Nun did not leave the tent.

Moses Pleads for the LORD's Presence

12 Moses said to the the LORD, "You tell me to lead these people, but You have not told me whom You will send with me. You have said, 'I know you by name, and you have found favor with Me.' 13 Now, if I have found favor in Your sight, please show me Your ways so I can know You and keep finding favor with You. Remember, too, that this nation is Your people."

14 The the LORD replied, "My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."

15 Then Moses said to Him, "If Your Presence does not go with us, do not send us up from here. 16 How will anyone know that You are pleased with me and Your people unless You go with us? What else will make us different from all the other people on the earth?"

17 The the LORD said to Moses, "I will do the very thing you have asked, because you have found favor with Me and I know you by name."

Moses Asks to See the LORD's Glory

18 Then Moses said, "Please show me Your glory." 19 The the LORD said, "I will let all My goodness pass in front of you, and I will proclaim My name, the LORD, before you. I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion. 20 But He said, "You cannot see My face, because no one can see Me and live. 21 There is a place near Me where you can stand on a rock. 22 When My glory passes by, I will put you in a cleft in the rock and cover you with My hand until I have passed by. 23 Then I will take away My hand, and you will see My back. But My face will not be seen."
