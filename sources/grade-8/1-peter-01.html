Greetings to God's Chosen People

1 Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes to God's chosen people who live like strangers in the world. You are scattered through Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. 2 You were chosen because God the Father knew you ahead of time. The Spirit makes you holy so you can obey Jesus Christ and be cleansed by His blood. May grace and peace be given to you more and more.

A New Birth and Living Hope

3 Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In His great mercy He has given us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead. 4 He has given us an inheritance that can never perish, spoil, or fade. This inheritance is kept in heaven for you. 5 Through faith you are shielded by God's power until the salvation that is ready to be shown in the last time.

6 In all this you greatly rejoice, even though now for a little while you may have to suffer grief in all kinds of trials. 7 These troubles test your faith. Your faith is worth more than gold, which can be destroyed even though fire tests it. When Jesus Christ is revealed, your faith will bring praise, glory, and honor. 8 You have not seen Him, but you love Him. You do not see Him now, but you believe in Him. You are filled with a joy too great for words and full of glory. 9 You are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

10 The prophets who spoke about the grace that was coming to you searched and studied carefully. 11 They tried to learn what time or situation the Spirit of Christ in them was pointing to. He spoke ahead of time about Christ's sufferings and the glory that would follow. 12 It was shown to them that they were not serving themselves but you. They spoke about things that have now been told to you by those who preached the good news to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to look into these things.

Called to Live Holy Lives

13 So get ready for action. Keep your minds clear, and set your hope fully on the grace that will be given to you when Jesus Christ is revealed. 14 As obedient children, do not let your lives be shaped by the evil desires you had when you did not know better. 15 Instead, be holy in all you do, just as the One who called you is holy. 16 For it is written, "Be holy, because I am holy."

17 Since you call on a Father who judges each person's work fairly, live with reverent fear during your time as strangers in the world. 18 You know you were not set free from your empty way of life, handed down by your ancestors, by things that can perish, like silver or gold. 19 Instead, you were bought with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without fault or stain. 20 Christ was known before the world was made, but He was shown to you in these last times for your sake. 21 Through Him you believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory. So your faith and hope are in God.

Born Again Through God's Word

22 Now that you have made your souls pure by obeying the truth, love one another deeply from a clean heart. 23 You have been born again, not from seed that can die, but from seed that cannot die, through the living and lasting word of God. 24 For, "All people are like grass, and all their glory is like the flowers of the field. The grass dries up, and the flowers fall." 25 "But the word of the Lord lasts forever." And this is the word that was preached to you.
