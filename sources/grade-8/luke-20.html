Jesus' Authority Questioned

1 One day, while Jesus was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the good news, the chief priests, teachers of the law, and elders came up to Him. 2 "Tell us," they said, "by what authority are You doing these things? Or who gave You this authority?"

3 Jesus replied, "I will ask you a question too. Tell Me: 4 Was John's baptism from heaven or from men?"

5 They discussed it among themselves: "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will ask, 'Why didn't you believe him?' 6 But if we say, 'From men,' all the people will stone us, because they are sure John was a prophet." 7 So they answered that they didn't know where it was from.

8 Jesus said, "Then I won't tell you by what authority I do these things either."

The Parable of the Tenants

9 He began to tell the people this parable: "A man planted a vineyard and rented it to some farmers. Then he went away for a long time. 10 At harvest time he sent a servant to the farmers to collect some of the fruit from the vineyard, but they beat him and sent him away with nothing. 11 He sent another servant, but they beat him too, treated him badly, and sent him away with nothing. 12 He sent a third, and they wounded him and threw him out.

13 "Then the owner of the vineyard said, 'What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. Maybe they will respect him.'

14 "But when the farmers saw him, they said to one another, 'This is the heir. Let's kill him, so the inheritance will be ours.'

15 "So they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. 16 What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them? He will come and kill those farmers and give the vineyard to others."

When the people heard this, they said, "May that never happen!"

17 Jesus looked at them and said, "What does this Scripture mean: 'The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone'? 18 Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken, but the one it falls on will be crushed."

19 The teachers of the law and the chief priests knew He had spoken this parable against them. They wanted to arrest Him right then, but they were afraid of the people.

Questions from the Religious Leaders

20 They watched Him closely and sent spies who pretended to be sincere. They hoped to catch Him in something He said, so they could hand Him over to the governor's power and authority. 21 They came to Him and said, "Teacher, we know that You speak and teach what is right. You do not show favoritism, but You teach the way of God truthfully. 22 Is it right for us to pay taxes to Caesar or not?"

23 Jesus saw through their trickery and said to them, 24 "Show Me a denarius. Whose picture and inscription are on it?"

"Caesar's," they replied.

25 He said, "Then give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and to God what is God's."

26 They could not trap Him in what He said before the people. Amazed by His answer, they became silent.

27 Some Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Him. 28 "Teacher," they said, "Moses wrote for us that if a man's brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, the man must marry the widow and raise up children for his brother. 29 Now there were seven brothers. The first married a woman and died childless. 30 The second married her and died childless, and the third did the same. 31 In fact, all seven married her and died without leaving any children. 32 Finally, the woman died too. 33 Now then, at the resurrection, whose wife will she be? For all seven were married to her."

34 Jesus said to them, "The people of this age marry and are given in marriage. 35 But those who are worthy to share in that age and in the resurrection from the dead will neither marry nor be given in marriage. 36 In fact, they can no longer die, because they are like the angels. They are God's children, since they are children of the resurrection.

37 "Even Moses showed that the dead are raised, in the passage about the burning bush, when he called the Lord 'the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' 38 He is not the God of the dead, but of the living, because to Him all are alive."

39 Some of the teachers of the law answered, "Well said, Teacher!" 40 From then on, no one dared to ask Him any more questions.

41 Then Jesus said to them, "How can people say that the Christ is David's son? 42 David himself says in the Book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand 43 until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet."' 44 David calls Him 'Lord.' So how can He be his son?"

Warning Against the Teachers of the Law

45 While all the people were listening, Jesus said to His disciples, 46 "Beware of the teachers of the law. They like to walk around in flowing robes, and they love greetings in the marketplaces, the most important seats in the synagogues, and the places of honor at banquets. 47 They devour widows' houses and, for show, make long prayers. These men will be punished most severely."
