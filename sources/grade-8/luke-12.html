Jesus Warns His Disciples

1 While a huge crowd gathered—so many people that they stepped on each other—Jesus began to speak first to His disciples. "Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees. It means pretending. 2 Nothing is covered up that won't be uncovered, and nothing is hidden that won't be known. 3 So what you have said in the dark will be heard in the light, and what you whispered in a room will be shouted from the rooftops."

4 "I tell you, My friends, do not be afraid of those who kill the body and can do nothing more after that. 5 But I will show you whom to fear: Fear the One who, after killing the body, has power to throw you into hell. Yes, I tell you, fear Him. 6 Aren't five sparrows sold for two pennies? Yet not one of them is forgotten by God. 7 Even the hairs on your head are all counted. Don't be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows."

8 "I tell you, everyone who confesses Me before people, the Son of Man will also confess before God's angels. 9 But whoever denies Me before people will be denied before God's angels. 10 Everyone who speaks against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but anyone who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven. 11 When you are brought before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, don't worry about how to defend yourselves or what to say. 12 For the Holy Spirit will teach you at that time what you should say."

Guard Against Greed

13 Someone in the crowd said to Him, "Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me."

14 Jesus replied, "Man, who appointed Me judge or divider over you?" 15 "Watch out! Guard yourselves against every kind of greed. Life is not about having many things."

16 Then He told them this parable: "A rich man's land produced a big crop. 17 He thought to himself, 'What should I do? I have no place to store my crops.' 18 Then he said, 'I know what I'll do. I'll tear down my barns and build bigger ones, and there I'll store all my grain and goods.' 19 And I'll say to myself, 'You have plenty of good things saved up for many years. Take it easy; eat, drink, and be merry.'"

20 But God said to him, "You fool! This very night your life will be demanded from you. Then who will get what you have prepared?"

21 "That's how it is for anyone who piles up wealth for himself but isn't rich toward God."

Do Not Worry

22 Then Jesus said to His disciples, "So I tell you, don't worry about your life, about what you will eat, or about your body, about what you will wear. 23 Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothes."

24 "Look at the ravens. They don't plant or harvest. They have no storeroom or barn, yet God feeds them. How much more valuable you are than birds! 25 Who of you by worrying can add a single hour to your life? 26 Since you can't do that small thing, why do you worry about the rest?"

27 "Think about how the wildflowers grow. They don't work or spin thread. Yet I tell you, not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of them. 28 If God dresses the grass of the field like that—grass that is here today and thrown into the fire tomorrow—how much more will He clothe you, you who have little faith!"

29 "And don't keep worrying about what you will eat or drink. 30 The people of the world run after all these things, but your Father knows you need them. 31 Instead, seek His kingdom, and these things will be given to you as well."

32 "Don't be afraid, little flock, because your Father is pleased to give you the kingdom. 33 Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Make purses for yourselves that won't wear out—a treasure in heaven that never runs out, where no thief comes near and no moth destroys. 34 For where your treasure is, there your heart will be too."

Be Ready and Judge the Times

35 "Stay dressed for work and keep your lamps burning. 36 Be like servants waiting for their master to return from a wedding feast, so that when he comes and knocks, they can open the door for him right away. 37 Those servants are blessed when the master finds them awake at his coming. I tell you the truth: He will dress himself to serve, have them sit at the table, and wait on them. 38 They will be blessed whether he comes in the second or third watch of the night, if he finds them ready."

39 "But understand this: If the owner of the house had known when the thief was coming, he would not have let his house be broken into. 40 You also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour you don't expect."

41 Peter said, "Lord, are you telling this parable to us, or to everyone?"

42 The Lord answered, "Who then is the faithful and wise manager? The master will put him in charge of the servants to give them their food at the right time. 43 That servant is blessed if the master finds him doing this when he returns. 44 I tell you the truth, he will put him in charge of all he owns."

45 "But suppose that servant says to himself, 'My master is taking a long time to come,' and then starts beating the other servants, men and women, and eating and drinking and getting drunk. 46 The master of that servant will come on a day he doesn't expect and at an hour he doesn't know. He will punish him severely and put him with the unbelievers."

47 "The servant who knows his master's will but doesn't get ready or do what the master wants will be beaten with many blows. 48 But the one who doesn't know and does things deserving punishment will be beaten with few blows. From everyone who has been given much, much will be demanded. And from the one who has been trusted with much, much more will be asked."

49 "I have come to bring fire on the earth, and how I wish it were already burning! 50 But I have a baptism to go through, and I am troubled until it is finished. 51 Do you think I came to bring peace on earth? No, I tell you, but division. 52 From now on, five in one home will be divided, three against two and two against three. 53 They will be divided: father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law."

54 He said to the crowd, "When you see a cloud rising in the west, you say right away, 'It's going to rain,' and it does. 55 And when the south wind blows, you say, 'It's going to be hot,' and it is. 56 You hypocrites! You know how to read the signs of the earth and sky. How is it that you don't know how to read this time?"

57 "Why don't you judge for yourselves what is right? 58 As you go with your opponent to the ruler, try hard to settle with him on the way. Otherwise he may drag you before the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison. 59 I tell you, you won't get out until you have paid the very last penny."
