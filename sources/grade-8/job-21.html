Job Answers His Friends

1 Then Job answered: 2 "Listen carefully to my words. Let this be your comfort. 3 Stay with me while I speak. After I finish, you may mock me. 4 Am I complaining to another person? Why shouldn't I be impatient? 5 Look at me and be amazed. Put your hand over your mouth. 6 When I think about it, I am terrified, and trembling takes hold of my body.

The Wicked Often Prosper

"7 Why do wicked people keep living, grow old, and gain power? 8 Their descendants are settled around them, and their children are right in front of them. 9 Their homes are safe from fear, and God's punishment does not touch them. 10 Their bulls breed without fail, and their cows have healthy calves. 11 Their little ones run out like a flock of sheep, and their children dance and play. 12 They sing with tambourines and harps, and they celebrate to the sound of the flute. 13 They spend their days in comfort, and they go peacefully to the grave. 14 But they say to God, 'Leave us alone! We don't want to know Your ways. 15 Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him? What good would it do us to pray to Him?' 16 Yet their success is not really in their own hands, so I keep away from the advice of wicked people.

God Will Judge the Wicked

"17 How often does the light of wicked people go out? Does trouble come on them? Does God, in His anger, hand out destruction? 18 Are they like straw before the wind, or like chaff a storm blows away? 19 God saves up a person's wrongdoing for his children. He pays him back, and he knows it. 20 Let his own eyes see his ruin. Let him drink the anger of the Almighty. 21 What does he care about his family after he is gone, when his months are cut short? 22 Can anyone teach God anything? He judges even the powerful. 23 One person dies at the height of his strength, feeling safe and at ease. 24 His body is well fed, and his bones are full of marrow. 25 Another dies with a bitter heart, never having known good times. 26 Both lie down in the dust, and worms cover them.

Job Rejects Empty Comfort

"27 Look, I know your thoughts and the plans you make against me. 28 For you say, 'Where is the house of the rich man? Where is the tent where wicked people live?' 29 Haven't you asked travelers on the road? Don't you recognize the signs they see? 30 Wicked people are spared on the day of disaster and rescued on the day of anger. 31 Who dares to tell him he is wrong? Who pays him back for what he has done? 32 He is carried to the grave, and someone watches over his tomb. 33 The soil of the valley is sweet to him. Crowds follow him, and more go ahead of him than can be counted. 34 So how can you comfort me with empty words? Your answers are still misleading."
