God Asks About Mountain Goats and Deer

1 “Do you know when the mountain goats give birth? Do you watch when the deer has her fawn? 2 Do you count the months until they are ready to give birth? Do you know when that time comes? 3 They crouch down and bring forth their young. They deliver their little ones. 4 Their young grow strong in the wild. They leave and do not return.

The Wild Donkey and the Wild Ox

5 “Who set the wild donkey free? Who untied the ropes of the fast donkey? 6 I gave him the wasteland for his home and the salt flats for his place to live. 7 He ignores the noise of the city. He does not hear the driver’s shouts. 8 He roams the hills for food and looks for any green plant.

9 “Will the wild ox be willing to serve you? Will it stay by your feeding trough at night? 10 Can you tie the wild ox to a plow with a rope? Will it plow the valleys behind you? 11 Will you trust it because it is so strong? Will you leave your hard work to it? 12 Can you trust it to bring home your grain and gather it to your threshing floor?

The Ostrich, the Horse, and Birds of Prey

13 “The ostrich’s wings flap proudly, but are her wings and feathers like a stork’s? 14 She leaves her eggs on the ground and lets the dust warm them. 15 She forgets that a foot could crush them, or that a wild animal could trample them. 16 She is hard on her young, as if they were not hers. Her work is wasted, and she feels no fear. 17 God took wisdom from her and gave her no understanding. 18 Yet when she runs, she leaves the horse and its rider far behind.

19 “Do you give the horse its strength? Do you clothe its neck with a mane? 20 Do you make it jump like a locust? Its proud snorting scares people. 21 It paws the ground in the valley and is happy in its strength. It charges into battle. 22 It laughs at danger and is not afraid. It does not turn back from the sword. 23 The quiver rattles against it, along with the flashing spear and javelin. 24 It charges so hard that it seems to swallow the ground. It cannot stand still when the trumpet sounds. 25 When the trumpet blows, it says, ‘Aha!’ It smells the battle from far away and hears the thunder of leaders and the shouting.

26 “Does the hawk fly by your wisdom and spread its wings toward the south? 27 Does the eagle soar at your command and build its nest on high? 28 It lives on a cliff and stays there at night. A rocky peak is its safe place. 29 From there it looks for food. Its eyes see it from far away. 30 Its young feed on blood, and where the dead are, there it is.”
