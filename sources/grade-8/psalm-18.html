David's Song of Deliverance

1 I love You, the LORD, my strength. 2 The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my Savior. My God is my rock. I hide in Him. He is my shield, the strength that saves me, and my safe place. 3 I will call on the LORD, who deserves praise. Then I will be saved from my enemies.

4 Death's ropes wrapped around me. Floods of trouble scared me. 5 The grave's ropes held me. Death's traps came in front of me. 6 When I was in trouble, I called to the LORD. I cried to my God for help. He heard my voice from His temple. My cry reached His ears.

The LORD Answers with Power

7 The earth shook and quaked. The foundations of the mountains trembled. They shook because He was angry. 8 Smoke rose from His nostrils. Fire came from His mouth and burned. Coals blazed from it. 9 He opened the sky and came down. Dark clouds were under His feet. 10 He rode on a cherub and flew. He soared on the wind's wings. 11 He made darkness His hiding place. Rain clouds surrounded Him. 12 From the brightness before Him, His clouds passed by with hail and burning coals. 13 The LORD thundered from heaven. The Most High sent hail and burning coals. 14 He shot His arrows and scattered His enemies. He flashed lightning and threw them into panic. 15 The sea beds showed. The world's foundations were laid bare. This happened at Your rebuke, the LORD, at the blast of Your angry breath.

16 He reached down from heaven and took hold of me. He pulled me out of deep water. 17 He saved me from my strong enemy and from foes too strong for me. 18 They attacked me when I was in trouble, but the LORD supported me. 19 He brought me to a wide open place. He rescued me because He was pleased with me.

God Strengthens and Rewards

20 The LORD has rewarded me for doing right. He paid me back for my clean hands. 21 I have kept the LORD's ways. I have not turned from my God to do wrong. 22 All His rules are in front of me. I have not ignored His laws. 23 I was innocent before Him. I kept myself from sin. 24 So the LORD paid me back for my right living. He saw that my hands were clean.

25 You are faithful to faithful people. You are perfect to those who are perfect. 26 You are pure to those who are pure. But You are clever with those who twist things. 27 You save suffering people, but You bring down those with proud eyes. 28 You, the LORD, light my lamp. My God turns my darkness into light. 29 With You I can charge through an army. With my God I can climb over a wall. 30 God's way is perfect. The LORD's word is pure. He is a shield to everyone who trusts in Him.

31 Who is God besides the LORD? Who is a rock except our God? 32 God gives me strength and makes my path clear. 33 He makes my feet like a deer's feet. He sets me on high places. 34 He trains my hands for war. My arms can bend a bronze bow. 35 You have given me Your saving shield. Your right hand holds me up. Your gentleness makes me great. 36 You make my steps wide under me, so my feet do not slip. 37 I chase my enemies and catch them. I do not turn back until they are destroyed. 38 I crush them so they cannot get up. They fall under my feet. 39 You give me strength for the battle. You make my enemies bow under me. 40 You make my enemies run away. I destroy those who hate me. 41 They cry for help, but no one saves them. They even cry to the LORD, but He does not answer. 42 I grind them like dust in the wind. I trample them like mud in the streets. 43 You save me from fighting with my people. You make me the leader of nations. People I did not know serve me. 44 As soon as they hear me, they obey me. Foreigners submit to me. 45 Foreigners lose their strength. They come trembling out of their forts.

Praise the Living God

46 The LORD lives! Blessed be my Rock! May the God who saves me be praised. 47 He is the God who pays back my enemies and brings nations under me. 48 He saves me from my enemies. You lift me above my foes. You rescue me from violent men. 49 So I will praise You, the LORD, among the nations. I will sing to Your name. 50 He gives His king great victories. He shows faithful love to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.
