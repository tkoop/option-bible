God's Past Victories

1 We heard with our own ears, O God. Our fathers told us about the great things You did long ago. 2 With Your hand You drove out the nations and planted Your people. You crushed other peoples and sent them away. 3 They did not win the land with their swords, and their own strength did not save them. It was Your right hand, Your arm, and the light of Your face, because You were pleased with them.

4 You are my King, O God. Command victories for Jacob. 5 Through You we push back our enemies. In Your name we trample those who rise against us. 6 I do not trust in my bow, and my sword does not save me.

7 For You have saved us from our enemies and shamed those who hate us. 8 In God we boast all day long, and we will praise Your name forever. Selah

But Now We Are in Trouble

9 Yet You have rejected and humbled us. You no longer go out with our armies. 10 You made us turn back from the enemy, and those who hate us took our goods. 11 You gave us up to be eaten like sheep and scattered us among the nations.

12 You sell Your people for almost nothing, and You gain no profit from the sale. 13 You make us a shame to our neighbors, a mockery and an insult to those around us. 14 You make us a warning story among the nations, a thing people shake their heads at among all peoples.

15 All day long my shame is before me, and shame covers my face. 16 It comes from the voice of those who taunt and insult, because of the enemy and the one who wants revenge.

We Have Stayed True to God

17 All this has happened to us, yet we have not forgotten You or broken Your covenant. 18 Our hearts have not turned back, and our steps have not left Your path. 19 Yet You have crushed us in the place of wild dogs and covered us with deep darkness.

20 If we had forgotten the name of our God or spread out our hands to a foreign god, 21 would not God have found it out? For He knows the secrets of the heart. 22 Yet for Your sake we are killed all day long. We are treated like sheep to be slaughtered.

A Prayer for Help

23 Wake up, O Lord! Why do You sleep? Rise up! Do not reject us forever. 24 Why do You hide Your face and forget our trouble and our hardship?

25 Our soul has sunk to the dust, and our bodies cling to the ground. 26 Rise up and help us! Redeem us because of Your faithful love.
