A Man's Deep Suffering

1 I am the man who has suffered under the the LORD's angry punishment. 2 He led me and brought me into darkness instead of light. 3 He turns His hand against me again and again, all day long.

4 He has worn out my body and skin and broken my bones. 5 He has trapped me with bitterness and hardship. 6 He has made me live in dark places, like people who died long ago.

7 He has walled me in so I cannot get out; He has made my chains heavy. 8 Even when I cry out and ask for help, He shuts out my prayer. 9 He has blocked my path with cut stones and made my roads crooked.

10 He is like a bear waiting to attack me, like a lion hiding in the bushes. 11 He has led me off my path and torn me apart; He has left me alone and ruined. 12 He bent His bow and set me up as a target for His arrows.

13 He shot His arrows deep into me. 14 I have become a joke to all my people; they sing about me all day long. 15 He has filled me with bitterness and made me drink a bitter drink.

16 He has broken my teeth with gravel and covered me with ashes. 17 I have lost all peace; I have forgotten what good times feel like. 18 I said, "My strength is gone, and my hope in the the LORD is gone."

Hope in the LORD's Mercy

19 Remember my suffering and my wandering, the bitterness and the poison. 20 My soul keeps thinking about it and sinks inside me. 21 But I bring this to mind, and because of that I still have hope.

22 The LORD's faithful love never ends; His compassion never stops. 23 They are new every morning. Great is Your faithfulness. 24 "The the LORD is all I need," says my soul, "that is why I will hope in Him."

25 The LORD is good to those who wait for Him and seek Him. 26 It is good to wait quietly for the the LORD to save us. 27 It is good for a man to carry a heavy load while he is young.

28 Let him sit alone and be quiet, because the the LORD has put this on him. 29 Let him put his mouth in the dust—maybe there is still hope. 30 Let him turn his cheek to the one who hits him and be filled with shame.

31 The Lord will not reject us forever. 32 Even when He brings grief, He will show compassion because His faithful love is so great. 33 He does not enjoy hurting people or making them suffer.

34 The Lord does not approve of crushing prisoners underfoot, 35 denying a man justice before the Most High, 36 or cheating a man in court.

37 Who can speak and make something happen unless the Lord commands it? 38 Good and bad both come from the mouth of the Most High. 39 So why should a living person complain when he is punished for his sins?

A Call to Repent

40 Let us look closely at our ways and turn back to the the LORD. 41 Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven. 42 We have sinned and rebelled, and You have not forgiven us.

43 You have wrapped Yourself in anger and pursued us; You have killed without pity. 44 You have covered Yourself with a cloud so our prayers cannot get through. 45 You have made us like garbage and trash among the nations.

46 All our enemies open their mouths against us. 47 We have faced panic and traps, ruin and destruction. 48 My eyes pour out rivers of tears because of the ruin of my people.

49 My eyes will not stop crying; they will not rest 50 until the the LORD looks down and sees from heaven. 51 What I see breaks my heart for all the young women of my city.

A Cry for Help and Justice

52 My enemies hunted me like a bird for no reason. 53 They shut me up in a pit and threw stones at me. 54 Water closed over my head, and I said, "I am lost."

55 I called on Your name, the the LORD, from the bottom of the pit. 56 You heard my voice; do not hide Your ear from my cry for help. 57 You came close when I called on You and said, "Do not be afraid!"

58 You have stood up for me, O Lord; You have saved my life. 59 You have seen, O the the LORD, the wrong done to me; judge my case. 60 You have seen all their revenge and all their plans against me. 61 You have heard, O the the LORD, their insults and all their plans against me.

62 My enemies whisper and think about me all day long. 63 Watch them when they sit and when they stand; I am the one they mock.

64 Pay them back, O the the LORD, for what their hands have done. 65 Give them a sorrowful heart; may Your curse fall on them. 66 Chase them down in anger and destroy them from under the heavens of the the LORD.
