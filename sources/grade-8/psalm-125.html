God Protects His People

1Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion. It cannot be shaken, and it lasts forever. 2Just as the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds His people now and forever.

3The power of wicked people will not stay over the land given to good people, so good people won't be pushed to do wrong. 4Do good, O the LORD, to those who are good and to those whose hearts are right. 5But those who turn to crooked paths, the LORD will lead away with those who do evil. Peace be on Israel.
