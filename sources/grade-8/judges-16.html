Samson Visits Gaza

1 Samson went to Gaza. There he saw a prostitute and spent the night with her. 2 The people of Gaza were told, “Samson has come here!” They surrounded the city and hid to ambush him all night at the gate. They stayed quiet all night, saying, “We will kill him at dawn.” 3 Samson lay there until midnight. Then he got up at midnight, took hold of the doors of the city gate and the two posts, pulled them out along with the bar, put them on his shoulders, and carried them to the top of the hill facing Hebron.

Samson and Delilah

4 After that, he loved a woman in the Valley of Sorek. Her name was Delilah. 5 The Philistine rulers came to her and said, “Trick him. Find out what makes his strength so great and how we can overpower him. Then we can tie him up and hurt him. Each of us will give you 1,100 pieces of silver.”

6 Delilah said to Samson, “Tell me, please, what makes your strength so great and how you can be tied up and hurt.”

7 Samson said, “If they tie me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not dried, I will become weak and be like any other man.”

8 Then the Philistine rulers brought her seven fresh bowstrings that had not dried, and she tied him up with them. 9 Men were hiding in the room. She said, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” He snapped the bowstrings like a thread snaps in a flame. So no one found out the secret of his strength.

10 Delilah said to Samson, “You have tricked me and lied to me. Now tell me the truth: how can you be tied up?”

11 He said, “If they tie me tightly with new ropes that have never been used, I will become weak and be like any other man.”

12 So Delilah took new ropes and tied him up with them. Then she said, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” Men were hiding in the room. He snapped the ropes off his arms like a thread.

13 Delilah said to Samson, “You have tricked me and lied to me. Tell me now how you can be tied up.”

He said, “If you weave the seven braids of my hair into the fabric on the loom...”

14 She fastened it with a pin and called out, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” He woke up and pulled out the pin and the fabric.

15 Then she said to him, “How can you say you love me when your heart isn't with me? You have tricked me three times now and haven't told me what makes your strength so great.”

16 She kept nagging him every day with her words until he was worn out and felt he could die.

17 So he told her his whole heart. He said, “No razor has ever touched my head, because I have been set apart to God since I was in my mother's womb. If my hair is cut, my strength will leave me, and I will become weak and be like any other man.”

Samson Is Captured

18 When Delilah saw that he had told her his whole heart, she sent for the Philistine rulers and said, “Come back this time, because he has told me his whole heart.” So the Philistine rulers came, bringing the money with them.

19 She got him to sleep on her knees. Then she called a man and had him shave off the seven braids of Samson's head. She began to weaken him, and his strength left him.

20 She said, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”

He woke up and said, “I will go out like before and shake myself free.” But he did not know that the LORD had left him.

21 The Philistines seized him, gouged out his eyes, and brought him down to Gaza. They bound him with bronze shackles, and he ground grain in the prison.

22 But his hair began to grow back after it had been shaved.

Samson's Final Victory

23 The Philistine rulers gathered to offer a great sacrifice to their god Dagon and to celebrate. They said, “Our god has handed Samson, our enemy, over to us.”

24 When the people saw him, they praised their god, saying, “Our god has handed over to us our enemy, the one who ruined our land and killed so many of us.”

25 When they were in a good mood, they said, “Call Samson so he can entertain us.” So they called Samson from the prison, and he performed for them. Then they stood him between the pillars.

26 Samson said to the young man holding his hand, “Let me feel the pillars that hold up the building, so I can lean on them.”

27 The building was full of men and women. All the Philistine rulers were there, and about three thousand men and women were on the roof watching Samson perform.

28 Then Samson called to the LORD and said, “O Lord the LORD, please remember me. Give me strength just this once, O God, so I may get revenge on the Philistines for my two eyes.”

29 Samson grabbed the two middle pillars that held up the building and leaned against them, one with his right hand and one with his left.

30 Samson said, “Let me die with the Philistines.” Then he pushed with all his strength, and the building fell on the rulers and all the people in it. So he killed more people when he died than he had killed during his life.

31 Then his brothers and his father's whole family went down, brought him back, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of his father Manoah. He had judged Israel for twenty years.
