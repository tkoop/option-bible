John Sees an Open Door in Heaven

1 After this, I looked and saw an open door in heaven. The voice I had heard before sounded like a trumpet and spoke to me. It said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must happen after this."

2 At once I was in the Spirit, and I saw a throne in heaven with someone sitting on it. 3 The One sitting there looked like jasper and carnelian stone. A rainbow surrounded the throne and looked like an emerald. 4 Around the throne were twenty-four other thrones, and on them sat twenty-four elders. They wore white clothes and had gold crowns on their heads.

Lightning, Thunder, and the Seven Spirits

5 Lightning, sounds, and thunder came from the throne. In front of the throne, seven burning lamps blazed. These are the seven Spirits of God. 6 In front of the throne was something like a sea of glass, clear as crystal. In the center, around the throne, were four living creatures covered with eyes in front and behind. 7 The first looked like a lion, the second like a young bull, the third had a face like a man, and the fourth looked like a flying eagle.

The Four Living Creatures Worship

8 Each of the four creatures had six wings and was covered with eyes all around and inside. Day and night they never stopped saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, and is, and is to come!"

The Elders Worship the One on the Throne

9 Whenever the living creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to the One seated on the throne, the One who lives forever and ever, 10 the twenty-four elders fall down before the One seated on the throne. They worship the One who lives forever and ever, and they throw their crowns down before the throne, saying, 11 "Worthy are You, our Lord and God, to receive glory, honor, and power, because You created all things. By Your will they existed and were created."
