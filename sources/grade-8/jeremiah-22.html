A Warning to Judah's King

1 This is what the LORD says: "Go down to the palace of the king of Judah and proclaim this message there: 2 Hear the word of the LORD, O king of Judah, you who sit on David's throne—your officials and your people who come through these gates."

3 This is what the LORD says: "Do justice and righteousness. Rescue the robbed from the hand of the oppressor. Do not mistreat or rob the foreigner, the orphan, or the widow. Do not shed innocent blood in this place. 4 For if you really do this, then kings who sit on David's throne will come through the gates of this house, riding in chariots and on horses—they, their officials, and their people. 5 But if you do not listen to these words, I swear by Myself, declares the LORD, that this house will become a ruin."

Judgment on the Royal House

6 For this is what the LORD says about the house of the king of Judah: "You are like Gilead to Me, like the top of Lebanon. But I will surely make you a desert, cities no one lives in. 7 I will send destroyers against you, each with his weapons. They will cut down your best cedars and throw them into the fire. 8 Many nations will pass by this city and ask one another, 'Why has the LORD done such a thing to this great city?' 9 And they will answer, 'Because they left the covenant of the LORD their God and worshiped and served other gods.'"

10 Do not weep for the dead or mourn for them. Weep bitterly for the one who is going away, because he will never return or see his homeland again. 11 For this is what the LORD says about Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who ruled in place of his father Josiah and has gone out from this place: "He will never return here. 12 He will die in the place where they took him; he will never see this land again."

A Warning Against Unjust Rule

13 "Woe to him who builds his house by wrongdoing and his upper rooms by unfairness, who makes his neighbor work for nothing and does not pay him his wages, 14 who says, 'I will build myself a spacious house with large upper rooms. I will cut out windows and panel it with cedar and paint it with red paint.'

15 "Do you think you are a king because you excel in cedar? Did not your father eat and drink and do justice and righteousness? Then it went well with him. 16 He judged the case of the poor and needy; then it went well. Is that not what it means to know Me?" declares the LORD. 17 "But your eyes and your heart are set on nothing but dishonest gain, on shedding innocent blood, on taking money by force and on violence."

18 Therefore this is what the LORD says about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: "They will not mourn for him, saying, 'Alas, my brother!' or 'Alas, my sister!' They will not mourn for him, saying, 'Alas, my lord!' or 'Alas, his glory!' 19 He will be buried like a donkey, dragged off and thrown outside the gates of Jerusalem."

20 "Go up to Lebanon and cry out; raise your voice in Bashan; cry out from Abarim, for all your lovers have been crushed. 21 I spoke to you in your prosperity, but you said, 'I will not listen.' This has been your way from your youth, that you have not obeyed My voice. 22 The wind will drive away all your shepherds, and your lovers will go into captivity. Then you will be ashamed and humiliated because of all your wickedness. 23 O inhabitant of Lebanon, nested in the cedars, how you will groan when pains come upon you, pain like a woman in labor."

Judgment on Coniah

24 "As surely as I live," declares the LORD, "even if Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were a signet ring on My right hand, I would still tear you off. 25 I will hand you over to those you fear, who seek your life, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to the Chaldeans. 26 I will hurl you and the mother who bore you into another land, where neither of you was born, and there you will both die. 27 But as for the land they long to return to, they will never return there."

28 Is this man Coniah a despised, broken pot, a vessel no one wants? Why are he and his descendants hurled out and cast into a land they do not know? 29 O land, land, land, hear the word of the LORD! 30 This is what the LORD says: "Write this man down as childless, a man who will not prosper in his days. For none of his descendants will prosper, sitting on David's throne or ruling again in Judah."
