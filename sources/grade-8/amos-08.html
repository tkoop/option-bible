Amos Sees a Basket of Summer Fruit

1 This is what the Lord the LORD showed me: a basket of summer fruit.

2 The LORD asked, "Amos, what do you see?"

I said, "A basket of summer fruit."

Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come for My people Israel. I will not spare them anymore."

3 "In that day," declares the Lord the LORD, "the temple songs will turn into crying. There will be many dead bodies thrown everywhere! Hush!"

A Warning to Unfair Merchants

4 Listen to this, you who trample the needy and crush the poor of the land.

5 You say, "When will the New Moon be over so we can sell grain? When will the Sabbath end so we can sell wheat? We will make the measure smaller, raise the price, and cheat with dishonest scales. 6 That way we can buy the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, and sell even the chaff of the wheat."

7 The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob: "Surely I will never forget any of their deeds."

8 Will not the land shake because of this, and everyone living in it mourn? The whole land will rise like the Nile, surge and settle like the Nile of Egypt.

A Day of Darkness and Grief

9 "On that day," declares the Lord the LORD, "I will make the sun go down at noon and darken the earth in broad daylight.

10 "I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into songs of grief. I will make everyone wear sackcloth and shave their heads. I will make it like mourning for an only child, and its end will be like a bitter day."

A Famine of Hearing the LORD's Word

11 "Look, the days are coming," declares the Lord the LORD, "when I will send a famine on the land—not a famine of bread or a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the words of the LORD."

12 People will stagger from sea to sea and wander from north to east, looking for the word of the LORD, but they will not find it.

13 In that day the beautiful young women and young men will faint from thirst.

14 Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria and say, "As your god lives, O Dan," or, "As the way of Beersheba lives"—they will fall and never rise again.
