Kings Defeated East of the Jordan

1 These are the kings of the land the Israelites defeated. They took the land east of the Jordan, from the Arnon River to Mount Hermon, including all the eastern plain. 2 Sihon, king of the Amorites, lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon River, through the middle of that valley, up to the Jabbok River, the border of the Ammonites, and over half of Gilead. 3 His land also included the eastern plain up to the Sea of Chinnereth and down to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, east of Beth Jeshimoth, and south below the slopes of Pisgah. 4 Og, king of Bashan, was one of the last Rephaim. He lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei. 5 He ruled Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all of Bashan up to the borders of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and over half of Gilead up to the border of Sihon, king of Heshbon. 6 Moses, servant of the LORD, and the Israelites defeated them. Moses, servant of the LORD, gave their land to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

Kings Defeated West of the Jordan

7 These are the kings of the land Joshua and the Israelites defeated west of the Jordan. Their land ran from Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir. Joshua gave this land to the tribes of Israel as their share. 8 It included the hill country, the lowlands, the Arabah, the slopes, the wilderness, and the Negev. The people living there were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

The Thirty-One Kings

9 the king of Jericho and the king of Ai near Bethel; 10 the king of Jerusalem and the king of Hebron; 11 the king of Jarmuth and the king of Lachish; 12 the king of Eglon and the king of Gezer; 13 the king of Debir and the king of Geder; 14 the king of Hormah and the king of Arad; 15 the king of Libnah and the king of Adullam; 16 the king of Makkedah and the king of Bethel; 17 the king of Tappuah and the king of Hepher; 18 the king of Aphek and the king of Sharon; 19 the king of Madon and the king of Hazor; 20 the king of Shimron-meron and the king of Achshaph; 21 the king of Taanach and the king of Megiddo; 22 the king of Kedesh and the king of Jokneam in Carmel; 23 the king of Dor in Naphath-dor and the king of Goiim in Gilgal; 24 and the king of Tirzah. That made thirty-one kings in all.
