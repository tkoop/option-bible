Don't Judge Others

1 Don't judge others, or you will be judged too. 2 The same standard you use to judge others will be used to judge you, and the same measure you give will be measured back to you.

3 Why do you notice the small fault in your brother's eye but ignore the big fault in your own eye? 4 How can you say to your brother, "Let me take the small fault out of your eye," when there's a big fault in your own eye? 5 You hypocrite! First take the big fault out of your own eye. Then you will see clearly enough to take the small fault out of your brother's eye.

6 Don't give sacred things to people who won't value them, and don't waste your best gifts on those who will only trample them and then turn on you.

Ask, Seek, and Knock

7 Ask, and it will be given to you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened to you. 8 Everyone who asks receives, everyone who seeks finds, and the door is opened to everyone who knocks.

9 Which of you, if your son asks for bread, would give him a stone? 10 Or if he asks for a fish, would give him a snake?

11 So if you, though sinful, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will Your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask Him!

12 So do for others what you want them to do for you. That sums up the Law and the Prophets.

The Narrow Gate and False Prophets

13 Enter through the narrow gate. The wide gate and broad road lead to destruction, and many people go through it. 14 But the narrow gate and hard road lead to life, and only a few find it.

15 Watch out for false prophets. They come to you looking gentle, but inside they are dangerous and greedy.

16 You will know them by what they do. Do people pick grapes from thorn bushes or figs from thistles?

17 A good tree produces good fruit, and a bad tree produces bad fruit. 18 A good tree can't produce bad fruit, and a bad tree can't produce good fruit. 19 Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.

20 So you will know them by what they do.

21 Not everyone who says to Me, "Lord, Lord," will enter the kingdom of heaven, but only the one who does the will of My Father in heaven.

22 Many will say to Me on that day, "Lord, Lord, didn't we prophesy in Your name? Didn't we drive out demons in Your name? Didn't we do many miracles in Your name?"

23 Then I will tell them plainly, "I never knew you. Get away from Me, you who do evil!"

Build on the Rock

24 Everyone who hears these words of Mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock. 25 The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house, but it didn't fall, because it was built on the rock.

26 But everyone who hears these words of Mine and doesn't do them is like a foolish man who built his house on sand. 27 The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it fell with a great crash.

28 When Jesus finished saying these things, the crowds were amazed at His teaching. 29 He taught them as one who had authority, not like their teachers of the law.
