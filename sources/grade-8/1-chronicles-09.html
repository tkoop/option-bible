People Who Returned to Jerusalem

1 All Israel was listed by family history. These records were written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. Judah was taken to Babylon because they were not faithful to God. 2 The first people to live again in their own towns were Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants. 3 Some people from Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem.

4 Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, was from the family line of Perez son of Judah. 5 From the Shilonites came Asaiah the firstborn and his sons. 6 From the Zerahites came Jeuel and 690 of their relatives.

7 From the Benjamites came Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah. 8 Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah. 9 Their family records showed 956 relatives. All these men were leaders of their family groups.

Priests, Levites, and Temple Workers

10 From the priests came Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin. 11 Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, was the chief official of God's house. 12 Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah, and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer, were also there. 13 They and their relatives were 1,760 strong, capable men. They were leaders of their family groups and did the work of serving in God's house.

14 From the Levites came Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, from the Merarites. 15 Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph. 16 Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun, and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of Netophah.

17 The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives. Shallum was the leader. 18 They served at the king's east gate. They were gatekeepers for the Levite camps. 19 Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his relatives from his father's house, the Korahites, were in charge of guarding the entrances to the tent. Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the camp of the the LORD. 20 Phinehas son of Eleazar had been their leader in the past, and the the LORD was with him. 21 Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

22 All these chosen gatekeepers numbered 212. They were listed by family history in their villages. David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to their trusted positions. 23 They and their sons guarded the gates of the the LORD's house, the tent. 24 The gatekeepers stood on four sides: east, west, north, and south. 25 Their relatives in their villages came to serve with them for seven days at a time.

26 The four chief gatekeepers were Levites. They were trusted with the rooms and storerooms of God's house. 27 They stayed around God's house because they were in charge of guarding it. They opened it every morning. 28 Some of them were in charge of the utensils used in worship. They counted them when they were brought in and when they were taken out.

29 Others were put in charge of the furniture and all the holy utensils, as well as the fine flour, wine, oil, incense, and spices. 30 Some of the priests mixed the spices. 31 Mattithiah, a Levite and the firstborn son of Shallum the Korahite, was trusted with baking the flat cakes. 32 Some of their relatives from the Kohathites were in charge of setting out the bread that was replaced every Sabbath.

Musicians and Levite Leaders

33 The musicians were family leaders of the Levites. They stayed in the rooms and were free from other work, because they served day and night. 34 These were the family leaders of the Levites. They lived in Jerusalem.

The Family of Saul

35 Jeiel, the father of Gibeon, lived in Gibeon. His wife was named Maacah. 36 His firstborn son was Abdon. His other sons were Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab, 37 Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth. 38 Mikloth became the father of Shimeam. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.

39 Ner became the father of Kish. Kish became the father of Saul. Saul became the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 40 Jonathan's son was Merib-baal. Merib-baal became the father of Micah. 41 Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, and Tahrea.

42 Ahaz became the father of Jarah. Jarah became the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri became the father of Moza. 43 Moza became the father of Binea. Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah was his son, and Azel was his son. 44 Azel had six sons. Their names were Azrikam, Bokeru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were Azel's sons.
