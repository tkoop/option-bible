Israel Warned Not to Be Proud

1 Hear, Israel! Today you are crossing the Jordan to go in and take over nations greater and stronger than you—cities with walls reaching up to the sky. 2 The people there are tall and strong. They are the Anakites. You know about them, and you have heard people ask, "Who can stand up to the Anakites?" 3 But know today that the LORD your God goes ahead of you. He is like a fire that destroys. He will destroy them and put them under your power. You will drive them out and wipe them out quickly, just as the LORD has promised you. 4 After the LORD your God has driven them out ahead of you, do not say in your heart, "The LORD brought me in to take this land because I am right with Him." No, it is because of the evil of these nations that the LORD is driving them out ahead of you. 5 You are not going in to take their land because you are good or because your heart is right. The LORD your God is driving them out before you because of their evil, and to keep the promise the LORD made to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 6 Know that the LORD your God is not giving you this good land to take over because you are good. You are a stubborn people.

Remembering Israel's Rebellion

7 Remember this, and do not forget: you made the LORD your God angry in the desert. From the day you left Egypt until you came to this place, you have rebelled against the LORD. 8 At Mount Horeb you made the LORD angry. He was so angry with you that He wanted to destroy you. 9 When I went up the mountain to receive the stone tablets—the tablets of the covenant the LORD made with you—I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water. 10 The LORD gave me the two stone tablets written with the finger of God. On them were all the words the LORD spoke to you from the fire on the mountain on the day you gathered there. 11 At the end of the forty days and forty nights, the LORD gave me the two stone tablets of the covenant.

The Golden Calf

12 Then the LORD said to me, "Get up and go down quickly from here, because your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have ruined themselves. They have quickly turned away from what I commanded them and made a metal idol for themselves." 13 The LORD also said, "I have seen these people, and they are stubborn." 14 "Leave Me alone so I can destroy them and wipe out their name from under heaven. Then I will make you into a nation stronger and greater than they are."

15 So I turned and went down the mountain while it was on fire. I had the two tablets of the covenant in my hands. 16 I looked, and there you were—you had sinned against the LORD your God. You had made a molded calf for yourselves. You had quickly turned away from the path the LORD had commanded you to follow. 17 So I took the two tablets and threw them from my hands, breaking them in front of you. 18 Then I fell down before the LORD for forty days and forty nights, just as I had done before. I ate no bread and drank no water. I did this because of all the sin you had committed by doing evil in the LORD's sight and making Him angry. 19 I was afraid of the LORD's anger and fury, because He was angry enough with you to destroy you. But the LORD listened to me that time too. 20 The LORD was very angry with Aaron and would have destroyed him, so I prayed for Aaron too at that time. 21 I took the sinful thing—the calf you had made—and burned it in the fire. I crushed it and ground it into powder, as fine as dust. Then I threw the dust into the stream that flowed down the mountain. 22 You also made the LORD angry at Taberah, at Massah, and at Kibroth Hattaavah. 23 When the LORD sent you from Kadesh Barnea and said, "Go up and take the land I have given you," you rebelled against the LORD your God. You did not believe Him or listen to His voice. 24 You have been rebelling against the LORD ever since I have known you.

Moses Prays for the People

25 So I fell down before the LORD for the forty days and forty nights I had already fallen down, because the LORD said He would destroy you. 26 I prayed to the LORD and said, "O Lord the LORD, do not destroy Your people and Your inheritance, whom You redeemed by Your great power and brought out of Egypt with a strong hand. 27 Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Do not look at how stubborn this people is, or at their evil and sin, 28 so that the land You brought us from will not say,

"'The LORD was not able to bring them into the land He promised them, and because He hated them, He brought them out to die in the desert.'

29 Yet they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out with Your great power and Your strong arm."
