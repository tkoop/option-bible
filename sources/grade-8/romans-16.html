Paul Recommends Phoebe

1 I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a servant of the church in Cenchrea. 2 Welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of God's people, and help her with anything she may need from you. She has helped many people, and she has helped me too.

Greetings to Fellow Believers

3 Greet Prisca and Aquila, my coworkers in Christ Jesus. 4 They risked their lives for me. I am thankful to them, and so are all the churches of the Gentiles. 5 Greet the church that meets in their home. Greet my dear friend Epenetus, who was the first person in Asia to trust in Christ. 6 Greet Mary, who has worked hard for you. 7 Greet Andronicus and Junia, my relatives and fellow prisoners. They are well known among the apostles, and they were in Christ before I was. 8 Greet Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord. 9 Greet Urbanus, our coworker in Christ, and my dear friend Stachys. 10 Greet Apelles, who has proved himself in Christ. Greet those who belong to Aristobulus's household. 11 Greet Herodion, my relative. Greet those in Narcissus's household who are in the Lord. 12 Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, women who have worked hard in the Lord. Greet my dear friend Persis, who has worked very hard in the Lord. 13 Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who has been like a mother to me too. 14 Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them. 15 Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them. 16 Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.

Warning Against Divisions

17 Brothers, I urge you to watch out for people who cause divisions and create obstacles that go against the teaching you learned. Stay away from them. 18 Such people do not serve our Lord Christ. They serve their own desires. By smooth talk and flattery they fool innocent people. 19 Everyone has heard about your obedience, so I rejoice over you. But I want you to be wise about what is good and innocent about what is evil. 20 The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

Final Greetings and Praise

21 Timothy, my coworker, sends greetings to you, and so do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my relatives. 22 I, Tertius, who wrote this letter, greet you in the Lord. 23 Gaius, who hosts me and the whole church, sends greetings to you. Erastus, the city treasurer, and our brother Quartus send greetings to you. 25 Now to Him who can strengthen you according to my gospel and the message of Jesus Christ, and according to the revealing of the mystery that was hidden for long ages, 26 but is now shown and made known through the prophetic writings, by the command of the eternal God, to bring about the obedience of faith among all the nations— 27 to the only wise God be glory forever through Jesus Christ. Amen.
