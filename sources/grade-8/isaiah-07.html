Judah Faces an Enemy Alliance

1 In the days of Ahaz son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up to fight against Jerusalem. But they could not overpower it.

2 When the house of David was told, "Aram has joined forces with Ephraim," the hearts of Ahaz and his people shook like trees in a forest blown by the wind.

The LORD Sends Isaiah to Ahaz

3 Then the the LORD said to Isaiah, "Take your son Shear-jashub and go meet Ahaz at the end of the upper pool, on the road to the washer's field. 4 Say to him, 'Be careful, keep calm, and do not be afraid. Do not lose heart because of these two dying embers—Rezin of Aram and the son of Remaliah. 5 For Aram, Ephraim, and Remaliah's son have plotted evil against you, 6 saying, "Let us go up against Judah and terrify it. Let us break through its walls and set up the son of Tabeel as king over it."'

7 This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: "It will not stand; it will not happen. 8 For the capital of Aram is Damascus, and the capital of Damascus is Rezin. Within sixty-five years Ephraim will be broken and will no longer be a people. 9 The capital of Ephraim is Samaria, and the capital of Samaria is Remaliah's son. If you do not stand firm in faith, you will not stand at all."

A Sign for the House of David

10 The the LORD spoke to Ahaz again: 11 "Ask the the LORD your God for a sign. Ask for something as deep as the grave or as high as the heavens." 12 But Ahaz said, "I will not ask; I will not test the the LORD."

13 Then Isaiah said, "Listen, house of David! Is it too small a thing for you to wear out people, that you also wear out my God? 14 Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign: The virgin will become pregnant and give birth to a son, and she will call Him Immanuel. 15 He will eat curds and honey by the time He knows how to refuse evil and choose good. 16 But before the boy knows how to refuse evil and choose good, the land of the two kings you dread will be deserted."

Trouble Coming from Assyria

17 The the LORD will bring on you and on your people and on your father's house days that have not come since the day Ephraim turned away from Judah. He will bring this about through the king of Assyria.

18 On that day the the LORD will whistle for the fly from the distant streams of Egypt and for the bee from the land of Assyria. 19 They will all come and settle in the steep ravines, in the cracks of the rocks, on all the thornbushes, and in all the pastures.

20 On that day the Lord will use a hired razor—the king of Assyria—shaving off the hair of the head, the legs, and the beard. 21 On that day a man will keep alive a young cow and two sheep. 22 Because there will be so much milk, he will eat curds. Yes, everyone left in the land will eat curds and honey.

23 On that day, in every place where a thousand vines worth a thousand silver coins once grew, there will be only briers and thorns. 24 People will go there with bows and arrows, because the whole land will be briers and thorns. 25 As for the hills that were once hoed with a hoe, you will not go there for fear of briers and thorns. They will become places where oxen are turned loose and sheep trample.
