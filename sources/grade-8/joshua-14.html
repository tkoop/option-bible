The Land Is Divided Among the Tribes

1 These are the portions of land the people of Israel received in Canaan. Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the Israelite tribes gave them out. 2 The land was given by casting lots to the nine and a half tribes, just as the LORD had commanded through Moses. 3 Moses had already given land to the two and a half tribes east of the Jordan. But he gave no land among them to the Levites. 4 The sons of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites did not get a share of the land, except cities to live in, with fields around them for their animals and other property. 5 So the Israelites did what the LORD had commanded Moses, and they divided the land.

Caleb Asks for His Share

6 The sons of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb son of Jephunneh, the Kenizzite, said to him, “You know what the LORD said to Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea about you and me. 7 I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land. I brought back an honest report.

“8 My brothers who went with me made the people afraid, but I followed the LORD my God with all my heart. 9 On that day Moses swore to me, ‘The land you walked on will be yours and your children’s forever, because you followed the LORD my God with all your heart.’

“10 Now the LORD has kept me alive, just as He promised, for forty-five years since He spoke those words to Moses, while Israel traveled in the desert. Today I am eighty-five years old. 11 I am still as strong today as I was the day Moses sent me out. I have the same strength now for war and for going out and coming in. 12 So give me this hill country that the LORD promised me that day. You heard then that the Anakim were there, with large, well-protected cities. Maybe the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, as the LORD has said.”

Caleb Receives Hebron

13 Then Joshua blessed Caleb son of Jephunneh and gave him Hebron as his share. 14 So Hebron has belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he followed the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. 15 Hebron used to be called Kiriath-arba. Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. Then the land had rest from war.
