David's Last Words

1 These are David's last words. David son of Jesse speaks. God raised him up. The God of Jacob anointed him. He was Israel's sweet singer.

2 The Spirit of the LORD spoke through him. God's word was on his tongue.

3 The God of Israel spoke. The Rock of Israel told him that a ruler over people must be fair and rule with respect for God.

4 Such a ruler is like the light of morning when the sun rises on a clear morning. He is like the brightness after rain that makes grass grow from the ground.

5 My family is not perfect before God, but He made an everlasting covenant with me. It is complete and secure in every way. He will bring my salvation and every desire to fulfillment.

6 But worthless people are all like thorns thrown away, because no one can pick them up by hand.

7 Anyone who touches them must use iron and a spear shaft. They are burned up completely in the fire.

David's Mighty Men

8 These are the names of David's mighty warriors: Josheb-Basshebeth, a Tahkemonite, was chief of the Three. He killed eight hundred men at one time with his spear.

9 Next was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite. He was one of the three mighty men. He stood with David against the Philistines when they gathered for battle at Pas-dammim, while Israel's soldiers ran away.

10 But Eleazar stayed and fought the Philistines until his hand was so tired it stuck to his sword. The LORD gave a great victory that day. The soldiers came back to Eleazar, but only to take things from the dead.

11 Next was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered at Lehi, where a field was full of lentils. The people ran away from the Philistines.

12 But Shammah stood in the middle of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. The LORD gave a great victory.

13 During harvest time, three of the thirty leaders went down to David at the cave of Adullam. A group of Philistines was camped in the Valley of Rephaim.

14 At that time David was in the stronghold, and the Philistine camp was in Bethlehem.

15 David was thirsty and said, "I wish someone would bring me water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem!"

16 So the three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem, and brought it to David. But he would not drink it. Instead, he poured it out to the LORD.

17 He said, "I could never do that, the LORD. This is like the blood of men who risked their lives." So he would not drink it. These were the brave deeds of the three mighty men.

18 Abishai, Joab's brother and Zeruiah's son, was chief of the Three. He killed three hundred men with his spear and became famous among the Three.

19 He was honored more than the Three and became their commander, though he was not one of the Three.

20 Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a brave warrior from Kabzeel. He did great deeds. He killed two of Moab's best fighters. On a snowy day he went down into a pit and killed a lion.

21 He also killed a huge Egyptian. The Egyptian had a spear, but Benaiah went at him with a club. He took the spear from the Egyptian's hand and killed him with it.

22 These were the brave deeds of Benaiah son of Jehoiada. He became famous with the three mighty men.

23 He was honored more than the Thirty, but he was not one of the Three. David put him in charge of his bodyguard.

The Thirty

24 Among the Thirty were Asahel, Joab's brother; Elhanan son of Dodo from Bethlehem; 25 Shammah the Harodite; Elika the Harodite; 26 Helez the Paltite; Ira son of Ikkesh from Tekoa; 27 Abiezer from Anathoth; Mebunnai the Hushathite; 28 Zalmon the Ahohite; Maharai the Netophathite; 29 Heleb son of Baanah the Netophathite; Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of Benjamin; 30 Benaiah of Pirathon; Hiddai from the brooks of Gaash; 31 Abi-albon the Arbathite; Azmaveth the Bahurimite; 32 Eliahba the Shaalbonite; the sons of Jashen; Jonathan; 33 Shammah the Hararite; Ahiam son of Sharar the Hararite; 34 Eliphelet son of Ahasbai the Maacathite; Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite; 35 Hezro the Carmelite; Paarai the Arbite; 36 Igal son of Nathan from Zobah; Bani the Gadite; 37 Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai the Beerothite, Joab son of Zeruiah's armor-bearer; 38 Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite; 39 and Uriah the Hittite. There were thirty-seven in all.
