Mordecai Mourns for the Jews

1 When Mordecai learned all that had been done, he tore his clothes and put on sackcloth and ashes. He went out into the city and cried out with a loud, bitter cry. 2 He went as far as the king's gate, because no one dressed in sackcloth was allowed to enter it. 3 In every province, wherever the king's command and order reached, there was great mourning among the Jews. They fasted, wept, and cried out in sorrow, and many lay in sackcloth and ashes.

Esther Learns of the Threat

4 When Esther's young women and her eunuchs came and told her, the queen was very upset. She sent clothes for Mordecai to wear so he could take off his sackcloth, but he would not accept them. 5 Then Esther called for Hathach, one of the king's eunuchs who had been appointed to serve her. She ordered him to go to Mordecai and find out what was happening and why.

6 Hathach went out to Mordecai in the city square in front of the king's gate. 7 Mordecai told him everything that had happened to him, including the exact amount of money Haman had promised to pay into the king's treasury to destroy the Jews. 8 Mordecai also gave him a copy of the written order that had been issued in Susa to destroy them. He told Hathach to show it to Esther, explain it to her, and order her to go to the king to beg for mercy and plead for her people. 9 Hathach went back and told Esther what Mordecai had said.

10 Then Esther spoke to Hathach and told him to go to Mordecai and say:

11 “All the king's servants and the people in the king's provinces know that anyone, man or woman, who goes to the king in the inner court without being called must be put to death. The only exception is if the king holds out his golden scepter, and then that person lives. But I have not been called to go to the king for thirty days.”

12 They told Mordecai what Esther had said.

Mordecai Urges Esther to Help

13 Then Mordecai told them to reply to Esther: “Do not think to yourself that you will be safe in the king's palace while the rest of the Jews are not.

14 “If you stay silent now, help and rescue will come to the Jews from somewhere else, but you and your family will be destroyed. Who knows? Maybe you were made queen for a time like this.”

15 Then Esther told them to reply to Mordecai:

16 “Go and gather all the Jews in Susa. Fast for me. Do not eat or drink for three days, night or day. My young women and I will fast too, as you do. Then I will go to the king, even though it is against the law. If I die, I die.”

17 Mordecai went away and did everything Esther had commanded.
