A Prayer for the King

1 Give your justice to the king, O God, and your righteousness to the king's son. 2 He will judge your people with righteousness, and your poor with justice.

3 The mountains will bring peace to the people, and the hills will bring fairness. 4 He will defend the poor of the people, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.

A Lasting and Peaceful Rule

5 People will respect you as long as the sun shines, and as long as the moon lasts, through every generation. 6 He will be like rain falling on a newly mowed field, like showers that water the earth. 7 In his days good people will thrive, and peace will be plentiful until the moon is no more. 8 He will rule from sea to sea, from the river to the ends of the earth.

Nations Honor the King

9 Desert tribes will bow before him, and his enemies will be defeated. 10 The kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands will bring tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba will bring gifts. 11 All kings will bow down to him, and all nations will serve him.

12 For he will rescue the needy who cry out, and the poor who have no helper. 13 He will have pity on the poor and needy; he will save the lives of the needy. 14 He will redeem their lives from oppression and violence, and their lives will be precious to him.

15 Long may he live! May gold from Sheba be given to him. May people pray for him always; may they bless him all day long. 16 May the land produce plenty of grain, waving even on the mountain tops. May its harvest be as rich as Lebanon's; may people in the cities grow like grass in a field. 17 May his name last forever; may his fame continue as long as the sun. May all nations be blessed through him; may they call him blessed.

Praise to the LORD

18 Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonders. 19 Blessed be His glorious name forever; may all the earth be filled with His glory. Amen and Amen. 20 This ends the prayers of David son of Jesse.
