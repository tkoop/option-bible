Hoshea Rules Israel and Assyria Attacks

1 In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled for nine years. 2 He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, but not like the kings of Israel who had ruled before him.

3 Shalmaneser king of Assyria attacked him. Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute. 4 But the king of Assyria found out that Hoshea was plotting against him. Hoshea had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and he had not sent the yearly tribute to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria arrested Hoshea and put him in prison.

5 Then the king of Assyria marched through the whole land. He came to Samaria and surrounded it for three years. 6 In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria. He carried the Israelites away to Assyria and settled them in Halah, Gozan, the Habor River, and the cities of Media.

Why Israel Was Carried Away

7 This happened because the Israelites had sinned against the LORD their God. He had brought them up out of Egypt, away from Pharaoh king of Egypt. But they worshiped other gods. 8 They followed the customs of the nations that the LORD had driven out ahead of the Israelites. They also followed customs that the kings of Israel had made.

9 The Israelites secretly did things that were not right against the LORD their God. They built high places in all their cities, from small guard towers to fortified cities. 10 They set up sacred stone pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.

11 They burned incense on all the high places, just as the nations had done that the LORD drove out ahead of them. They did evil things that made the LORD angry. 12 They worshiped idols, even though the LORD had told them not to do that.

13 The LORD warned Israel and Judah through all His prophets and seers. He said, "Turn from your evil ways. Keep My commands and rules. Follow all the Law I gave your ancestors. I sent it to you through My servants the prophets."

14 But they would not listen. They were stubborn, just like their ancestors, who did not trust the LORD their God.

15 They rejected His rules and the agreement He made with their ancestors. They rejected the warnings He gave them. They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves. They copied the nations around them, even though the LORD had told them not to act like those nations. 16 They ignored all the commands of the LORD their God. They made two metal calves for themselves. They made an Asherah pole. They bowed down to all the stars in the sky. They served Baal.

17 They sacrificed their sons and daughters in fire. They practiced fortune-telling and sorcery. They gave themselves up to doing evil in the LORD’s eyes, and they made Him angry. 18 So the LORD was very angry with Israel. He removed them from His presence. Only the tribe of Judah was left.

19 But even Judah did not keep the the LORD’s commands. They followed the customs that Israel had made. 20 So the LORD rejected all the people of Israel. He punished them and handed them over to raiders, until He had thrown them out of His presence.

21 When the LORD tore Israel away from David’s family, the people made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. Jeroboam led Israel away from following the LORD. He caused them to commit a terrible sin. 22 The Israelites followed all the sins Jeroboam had done. They did not stop.

23 Finally the LORD removed Israel from His presence, just as He had said through all His servants the prophets. He carried the Israelites away from their own land to Assyria, where they are to this day.

New Peoples Settle in Samaria

24 The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim. He settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the Israelites. They took over Samaria and lived in its cities. 25 At first, when they lived there, they did not worship the LORD. So the LORD sent lions among them, and the lions killed some of them.

26 They told the king of Assyria, "The nations you moved and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the law of the God of this land. So He has sent lions among them. The lions are killing them, because they do not know the law of the God of this land."

27 The king of Assyria ordered, "Send one of the priests you took from there. Let him go live there and teach them the law of the God of this land."

28 So one of the priests who had been carried away from Samaria came and lived in Bethel. He taught them how to worship the LORD.

Mixed Worship in Samaria

29 But each nation made its own gods. They put them in the high places the people of Samaria had built. Each nation did this in the cities where it lived. 30 The men of Babylon made Succoth Benoth. The men of Cuth made Nergal. The men of Hamath made Ashima. 31 The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32 They worshiped the LORD, but they also chose priests from among themselves for the high places. These priests sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places. 33 They worshiped the LORD, but they also served their own gods, following the customs of the nations they had come from. 34 Even today they still follow their old customs. They do not worship the LORD. They do not follow the rules, laws, or commands that the LORD gave the descendants of Jacob. He named them Israel.

35 The LORD had made an agreement with them. He commanded them, "Do not worship other gods. Do not bow down to them. Do not serve them. Do not sacrifice to them. 36 The LORD is the One who brought you up from Egypt with great power and a strong arm. Worship Him. Bow down to Him. Sacrifice to Him. 37 Keep the rules, laws, and commands He wrote for you. You must always do them. Do not worship other gods. 38 Do not forget the agreement I made with you. Do not worship other gods. 39 Instead, worship the the LORD your God. He will save you from all your enemies."

40 But they did not listen. They kept following their old ways. 41 So these nations worshiped the LORD, but they also served their idols. Their children and grandchildren did the same thing their ancestors did, and they still do it today.
