Nahash Threatens Jabesh-gilead

1 Nahash the Ammonite marched against Jabesh-gilead and set up camp there. All the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Make an agreement with us, and we will serve you.”

2 But Nahash the Ammonite answered, “I will make an agreement with you on one condition: I will cut out the right eye of each of you, and I will bring shame on all Israel.”

3 The elders of Jabesh said to him, “Give us seven days so we can send messengers through all Israel. If no one comes to rescue us, we will give ourselves up to you.”

4 When the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and told the people these words, all the people cried loudly.

Saul Rises to Rescue

5 Just then Saul was coming in from the field with his oxen. Saul asked, “What is wrong with the people? Why are they weeping?” They told him what the men of Jabesh had said.

6 When Saul heard these words, the Spirit of God came powerfully upon him, and he became very angry.

7 He took a pair of oxen, cut them into pieces, and sent them by messengers through all Israel. He said, “This is what will be done to the oxen of anyone who does not follow Saul and Samuel.” Then the fear of the LORD fell on the people, and all of them came out together.

8 When Saul counted them at Bezek, there were 300,000 men of Israel and 30,000 men of Judah.

Jabesh-gilead Is Saved

9 They said to the messengers who had come, “Tell the men of Jabesh-gilead, ‘Rescue will come to you tomorrow by the time the sun is hot.’” So the messengers went and told the men of Jabesh, and they rejoiced.

10 Then the men of Jabesh said to the Ammonites, “Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you can do to us whatever you want.”

11 The next day Saul divided the people into three groups. During the early morning they entered the Ammonite camp and killed them until the heat of the day. Those who were left scattered, so that no two of them were left together.

12 Then the people said to Samuel, “Who said that Saul should not rule over us? Bring those men, and we will kill them.”

13 But Saul said, “No one will be killed today, because today the LORD has rescued Israel.”

Saul’s Kingship Renewed

14 Then Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingship there.”

15 So all the people went to Gilgal. There they made Saul king before the LORD. They offered peace offerings before the LORD, and Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.
