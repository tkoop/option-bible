Habakkuk's Prayer

1 This is a prayer of Habakkuk the prophet. 2 The LORD, I have heard what people say about You, and I am afraid. the LORD, bring Your work back to life in our time. Make it known in our time. Even when You are angry, remember mercy.

The LORD Comes in Power

3 God came from Teman, and the Holy One came from Mount Paran. Selah. His glory covered the heavens, and His praise filled the earth. 4 His brightness was like the light. Rays flashed from His hand, and His power was hidden there. 5 Sickness went before Him, and burning trouble followed at His feet.

6 He stood and measured the earth. He looked and made the nations tremble. The old mountains broke apart, and the lasting hills bowed down. His ways last forever. 7 I saw the tents of Cushan in trouble, and the tent curtains of Midian shook.

8 Were You angry with the rivers, O the LORD? Was Your wrath against the rivers? Did You ride on Your horses, Your chariots of salvation? 9 You uncovered Your bow and called for many arrows. Selah. You split the earth with rivers. 10 The mountains saw You and shook. A flood of water rushed by. The deep sea roared and raised its waves high. 11 The sun and moon stood still in the sky. They moved at the light of Your arrows, at the flash of Your shining spear. 12 You marched through the earth in anger. You crushed the nations in fury.

13 You came to save Your people, to save Your chosen one. You crushed the leader of the wicked house, laying him bare from top to bottom. Selah. 14 You struck their warriors with their own arrows. They rushed like a whirlwind to scatter me, and they rejoiced as if they were secretly swallowing up the poor. 15 You trampled the sea with Your horses, stirring up many waters.

Habakkuk's Fear and Faith

16 I heard, and my body shook. My lips trembled at the sound. Weakness entered my bones, and I shook all over. Yet I will wait quietly for the day of trouble, when the enemy comes against my people.

Joy in the LORD

17 Even if the fig tree does not bloom and the vines have no grapes; even if the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food; even if the sheep are taken from the pen and there are no cattle in the stalls. 18 Yet I will rejoice in the the LORD. I will be glad in the God who saves me. 19 The LORD, the Lord, is my strength. He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and lets me walk on the high places. For the music leader, with stringed instruments.
