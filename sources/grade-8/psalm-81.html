Praise God, Our Strength

1 For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. Of Asaph. Sing for joy to God our strength; shout aloud to the God of Jacob. 2 Lift up a song and beat the tambourine, the pleasant harp and lyre. 3 Sound the ram’s horn at the new moon, and at the full moon on the day of our feast. 4 For this is a law for Israel, a ruling from the God of Jacob.

God Remembers Israel’s Rescue

5 He set this as a testimony for Joseph when he went out over the land of Egypt, where I heard a language I did not know.

6 “I took his shoulder off the burden; his hands were freed from the basket. 7 In your distress you called, and I rescued you; I answered you from the thundercloud; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah

God Calls His People to Listen

8 “Hear, O My people, and I will testify against you, O Israel, if you would only listen to Me! 9 There must be no foreign god among you; you must not bow down to a foreign god. 10 I am the the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it.

11 “But My people did not listen to My voice; Israel refused to obey Me. 12 So I gave them up to their stubborn hearts to follow their own plans.

God Promises Good to Those Who Listen

13 “If only My people would listen to Me, if only Israel would walk in My ways, 14 I would quickly subdue their enemies and turn My hand against their foes. 15 Those who hate the the LORD would pretend to obey Him, and their doom would last forever. 16 But I would feed you with the finest wheat; with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.”
