A Good Name and God's Order

1 A good name is better than great wealth, and being well liked is better than silver and gold. 2 Rich and poor meet together; the LORD makes them all. 3 A prudent person sees trouble and hides, but simple people keep going and are punished. 4 The reward of humility and fear of the LORD is wealth, honor, and life.

5 Thorns and traps lie on the path of the crooked; whoever guards his life stays far from them. 6 Train up a child in the way he should go, and even when he is old he will not turn from it. 7 The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is a slave to the lender. 8 Whoever sows injustice will reap trouble, and the rod of his anger will fail. 9 A generous person will be blessed, because he shares his bread with the poor. 10 Drive out the mocker, and fighting will leave; yes, arguing and shame will stop.

11 Whoever loves a pure heart and speaks with grace will have the king as a friend. 12 The LORD's eyes watch over knowledge, but He overturns the words of the treacherous.

13 The lazy person says, "There is a lion outside! I will be killed in the streets!"

14 The mouth of an adulteress is a deep pit; a person cursed by the LORD falls into it. 15 Foolishness is tied to a child's heart, but the rod of discipline drives it far from him. 16 Whoever oppresses the poor to get richer, or gives to the rich, will surely come to poverty.

Listen to the Wise

17 Turn your ear and hear the words of the wise, and set your heart on my knowledge. 18 For it is pleasant if you keep them inside you; let them be fixed together on your lips. 19 So that your trust may be in the LORD, I have made them known to you today, yes, even to you. 20 Have I not written to you excellent sayings of counsel and knowledge? 21 I did this to make you know the truth of true words, so that you may bring back true words to those who sent you.

Do Not Wrong the Poor

22 Do not rob the poor because he is poor, and do not crush the needy at the city gate. 23 For the LORD will defend their case and take the life of those who rob them.

24 Do not be friends with a hot-tempered man, and do not go with a man who is easily angered. 25 You may learn his ways and get a trap for your own life.

26 Do not be one of those who shake hands in pledge or put up security for debts. 27 If you have nothing to pay with, why should they take even your bed from under you?

28 Do not move an old boundary stone that your fathers set up. 29 Do you see a man skilled in his work? He will stand before kings; he will not stand before lowly men.
