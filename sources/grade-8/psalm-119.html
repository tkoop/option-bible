A Prayer About God's Word

1 Happy are those who live without blame and follow the LORD's teachings. 2 Happy are those who obey His rules and seek Him with all their heart. 3 They do no wrong; they walk in His ways. 4 You have commanded us to obey Your rules carefully.

5 I wish my ways were steady so I could keep Your rules. 6 Then I would not be ashamed when I look at all Your commands. 7 I will praise You with an honest heart when I learn Your right decisions. 8 I will keep Your rules; do not leave me completely.

9 How can a young person keep his life pure? By living according to Your word. 10 I seek You with all my heart; do not let me stray from Your commands. 11 I have stored Your word in my heart so I will not sin against You. 12 Blessed are You, the LORD; teach me Your rules.

13 I tell with my lips all the decisions You have spoken. 14 I find joy in following Your rules as much as in great wealth. 15 I will think about Your commands and watch Your ways. 16 I will enjoy Your rules; I will not forget Your word.

17 Be good to Your servant so I can live and keep Your word. 18 Open my eyes so I can see wonderful things in Your teaching. 19 I am only a visitor on earth; do not hide Your commands from me. 20 I long for Your rules with all my heart all the time.

21 You correct proud people who are cursed because they turn from Your commands. 22 Remove scorn and shame from me, for I have obeyed Your rules. 23 Even when leaders plot against me, I, Your servant, will think about Your rules. 24 Your rules bring me joy; they guide me like wise friends.

25 I am crushed to the dust; give me life as Your word promises. 26 I told You my ways, and You answered me; teach me Your rules. 27 Help me understand Your commands, and I will think about Your wonderful works. 28 I am weak with sorrow; make me strong as Your word says.

29 Keep me from false ways; be gracious and teach me Your law. 30 I have chosen the path of faith; I keep Your rules in front of me. 31 I hold tightly to Your rules; the LORD, do not let me be ashamed. 32 I will run to obey Your commands when You give me a willing heart.

33 Teach me, the LORD, to follow Your rules, and I will keep them to the end. 34 Give me understanding so I can keep Your law with all my heart. 35 Lead me in the path of Your commands, because I enjoy it. 36 Turn my heart toward Your rules, not toward selfish gain.

37 Turn my eyes away from worthless things; give me life in Your ways. 38 Keep Your promise to Your servant so people may honor You. 39 Take away the shame I fear, for Your rules are good. 40 See, I long for Your commands; give me life through Your goodness.

41 Let Your faithful love come to me, the LORD; save me as You promised. 42 Then I can answer those who mock me, because I trust Your word. 43 Do not take the word of truth from my mouth, because I hope in Your rules. 44 I will keep Your law always, forever and ever.

45 I will walk freely because I have sought Your commands. 46 I will speak of Your rules before kings and not be ashamed. 47 I find joy in Your commands, which I love. 48 I lift my hands to Your commands, which I love, and I think about Your rules.

49 Remember Your word to Your servant; You gave me hope through it. 50 This comforts me in my trouble: Your promise gives me life. 51 Proud people mock me badly, but I do not turn from Your law. 52 When I think of Your old decisions, the LORD, I find comfort.

53 I burn with anger because wicked people abandon Your law. 54 Your rules are my songs in my temporary home. 55 I remember Your name at night, the LORD, and keep Your law. 56 This has happened to me because I kept Your commands.

57 The LORD is my share; I promise to keep Your words. 58 I ask for Your favor with all my heart; be kind to me as You promised. 59 When I think about my ways, I turn my steps toward Your rules. 60 I hurry and do not delay to keep Your commands.

61 The wicked may trap me, but I do not forget Your law. 62 At midnight I get up to praise You for Your right decisions. 63 I am a friend to all who honor You and keep Your commands. 64 The earth is full of Your faithful love, the LORD; teach me Your rules.

65 You have been good to Your servant, the LORD, as Your word says. 66 Teach me good sense and knowledge, because I trust Your commands. 67 Before I suffered, I went astray, but now I keep Your word. 68 You are good and do good; teach me Your rules.

69 Proud people spread lies about me, but I keep Your commands with all my heart. 70 Their hearts are dull, but I enjoy Your law. 71 It was good for me to suffer so I could learn Your rules. 72 Your teaching from Your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver.

73 Your hands made me and shaped me; give me understanding to learn Your commands. 74 Those who honor You will see me and be glad, because I hoped in Your word. 75 I know Your decisions are right, the LORD, and You have disciplined me in faithfulness. 76 Let Your faithful love comfort me, as You promised Your servant.

77 Let Your mercy come to me so I can live, for Your law is my joy. 78 Let proud people be ashamed because they lied about me; I will think about Your commands. 79 Let those who honor You turn to me so they can know Your rules. 80 Let my heart be whole in Your rules so I will not be ashamed.

81 I long for Your rescue; I hope in Your word. 82 My eyes grow tired waiting for Your promise; I ask, "When will You comfort me?" 83 I am like a dried wineskin in smoke, but I have not forgotten Your rules. 84 How long must Your servant wait? When will You judge those who chase me?

85 Proud people have dug traps for me; they ignore Your law. 86 All Your commands are faithful; they attack me with lies; help me! 87 They have almost destroyed me on earth, but I have not left Your commands. 88 Give me life because of Your faithful love, so I can keep the rules from Your mouth.

89 Forever, the LORD, Your word stands firm in heaven. 90 Your faithfulness lasts for every generation; You made the earth, and it stays. 91 All things serve You, and they stand today by Your decisions. 92 If Your law had not been my joy, I would have died in my trouble.

93 I will never forget Your commands, because through them You gave me life. 94 I am Yours; save me, because I have sought Your commands. 95 Wicked people wait to destroy me, but I think about Your rules. 96 I have seen limits to everything perfect, but Your command is very wide.

97 Oh, how I love Your law! I think about it all day. 98 Your command makes me wiser than my enemies because it is always with me. 99 I understand more than my teachers because I think about Your rules. 100 I understand more than older people because I keep Your commands.

101 I keep my feet from every evil path so I can keep Your word. 102 I do not turn from Your rules, because You have taught me. 103 Your words are sweet to my taste, sweeter than honey in my mouth. 104 Through Your commands I gain understanding, so I hate every false way.

105 Your word is a lamp for my feet and a light for my path. 106 I have promised and will keep it: I will follow Your right rules. 107 I suffer greatly; the LORD, give me life as Your word says. 108 Accept my willing praise, the LORD, and teach me Your rules.

109 My life is always in danger, but I do not forget Your law. 110 Wicked people set traps for me, but I do not stray from Your commands. 111 Your rules are my lasting treasure because they bring joy to my heart. 112 I turn my heart to keep Your rules forever, to the end.

113 I hate divided hearts, but I love Your law. 114 You are my hiding place and my shield; I hope in Your word. 115 Leave me alone, you evildoers, so I can keep the commands of my God. 116 Support me as You promised so I can live; do not let me be ashamed of my hope.

117 Hold me up, and I will be safe and keep Your rules always. 118 You reject all who stray from Your rules, because their tricks are false. 119 You remove all the wicked from the earth like waste metal, so I love Your rules. 120 My body trembles with fear of You, and I am afraid of Your decisions.

121 I have done what is right and fair; do not leave me to those who oppress me. 122 Promise good to Your servant; do not let proud people oppress me. 123 My eyes long for Your rescue and for Your right promise to come true. 124 Treat Your servant with faithful love and teach me Your rules.

125 I am Your servant; give me understanding so I can know Your rules. 126 It is time for the LORD to act, because people have broken Your law. 127 So I love Your commands more than gold, even the finest gold. 128 So I count all Your commands as right; I hate every false way.

129 Your rules are wonderful; so I keep them with all my heart. 130 When Your words are explained, they give light and understanding to simple people. 131 I open my mouth and pant because I long for Your commands. 132 Turn to me and be kind to me, as You do for those who love Your name.

133 Make my steps steady by Your word, and do not let sin rule over me. 134 Rescue me from being oppressed by people so I can keep Your commands. 135 Let Your face shine on Your servant and teach me Your rules. 136 Streams of tears flow from my eyes because people do not keep Your law.

137 You are right, the LORD, and Your rules are right. 138 You have commanded Your rules with perfect goodness and faithfulness. 139 My passion eats me up because my enemies forget Your words. 140 Your promise has been tested and proven, and Your servant loves it.

141 I am small and looked down on, yet I do not forget Your commands. 142 Your goodness is right forever, and Your law is true. 143 Trouble and distress have found me, but Your commands are my joy. 144 Your rules are right forever; give me understanding so I can live.

145 I cry to You with all my heart; answer me, the LORD! I will keep Your rules. 146 I call to You; save me so I can obey Your rules. 147 I get up before dawn and cry for help; I hope in Your words. 148 My eyes stay awake through the night so I can think about Your promise.

149 Hear my voice, the LORD, in Your faithful love; give me life by Your justice. 150 People who chase me with evil plans come near; they are far from Your law. 151 But You are near, the LORD, and all Your commands are true. 152 Long ago I learned from Your rules that You made them to last forever.

153 Look at my trouble and rescue me, for I do not forget Your law. 154 Defend my case and redeem me; give me life as You promised. 155 Rescue is far from wicked people because they do not seek Your rules. 156 Your mercy is great, the LORD; give me life by Your rules.

157 Many chase me and oppose me, but I do not turn from Your rules. 158 I look at faithless people with disgust because they do not keep Your commands. 159 See how I love Your commands; give me life by Your faithful love. 160 The whole of Your word is true, and all Your right rules last forever.

161 Rulers chase me for no reason, but my heart trembles at Your words. 162 I rejoice in Your word like someone who finds great treasure. 163 I hate and despise lies, but I love Your law. 164 Seven times a day I praise You for Your right decisions.

165 Those who love Your law have great peace, and nothing makes them stumble. 166 I hope for Your rescue, the LORD, and I keep Your commands. 167 My whole being keeps Your rules; I love them very much. 168 I keep Your commands and rules because all my ways are before You.

169 Let my cry reach You, the LORD; give me understanding by Your word. 170 Let my prayer reach You; rescue me as You promised. 171 My lips pour out praise because You teach me Your rules. 172 My tongue sings about Your word because all Your commands are right.

173 Let Your hand be ready to help me, because I have chosen Your commands. 174 I long for Your rescue, the LORD, and Your law is my joy. 175 Let me live and praise You, and let Your rules help me. 176 I have wandered like a lost sheep; seek Your servant, for I do not forget Your commands.
