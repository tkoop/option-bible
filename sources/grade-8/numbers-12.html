Miriam and Aaron Speak Against Moses

1 Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married, for he had married a Cushite woman. 2 They said, "Has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Hasn't He also spoken through us?" And the LORD heard this.

3 Now Moses was a very humble man, more humble than anyone else on earth.

The LORD Calls Them to the Tent of Meeting

4 At once the LORD said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, "All three of you come out to the tent of meeting." So the three of them went out. 5 Then the LORD came down in a pillar of cloud. He stood at the entrance to the tent and called Aaron and Miriam. The two of them came forward.

6 He said, "Listen to My words: When there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, reveal Myself to them in visions. I speak to them in dreams. 7 But this is not true of My servant Moses. He is faithful in all My house. 8 I speak with him face to face—clearly, not in riddles. He sees the form of the LORD. Why weren't you afraid to speak against My servant Moses?"

9 The LORD's anger burned against them, and He left.

Miriam Is Struck with Leprosy

10 When the cloud lifted from above the tent, Miriam had leprosy, white as snow. Aaron turned toward her and saw that she had leprosy. 11 Then Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not hold this sin against us. We acted foolishly, and we have sinned. 12 Please don't let her be like a dead baby whose body is half eaten away when it comes from its mother's womb." 13 So Moses cried out to the LORD, "Please, God, heal her!"

14 The LORD said to Moses, "If her father had spit in her face, wouldn't she be disgraced for seven days? Let her be shut out of the camp for seven days. After that, she can be brought back." 15 So Miriam was shut out of the camp for seven days, and the people did not move on until she was brought back.

Israel Moves On

16 After that, the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the Wilderness of Paran.
