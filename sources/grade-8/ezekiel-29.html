God's Message Against Pharaoh

1 In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me: 2 "Son of man, turn your face toward Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt."

"3 Speak and say that this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Pharaoh, king of Egypt, you great crocodile lying among your rivers. You said the Nile was yours and that you made it yourself."

"4 I will put hooks in your jaws and make the fish in your rivers stick to your scales. I will pull you up out of your rivers, and all the fish will stick to your scales. 5 I will leave you in the desert, you and all the fish from your rivers. You will fall on the open field. You will not be gathered or picked up. I will give you as food to the wild animals and the birds of the sky. 6 Then all who live in Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because they were like a weak reed staff to the people of Israel. 7 When they took hold of you by the hand, you splintered and tore open their shoulders. When they leaned on you, you broke and made their hips shake."

Egypt Will Be Ruined and Later Restored

"8 So this is what the Lord GOD says: I will bring a sword against you and cut off both people and animals from you. 9 The land of Egypt will become an empty wasteland. Then they will know that I am the LORD, because you claimed the Nile was yours and that you made it. 10 So I am against you and your rivers. I will make the land of Egypt a ruined, empty wasteland from Migdol to Syene, all the way to the border of Cush. 11 No person or animal will travel through it, and no one will live there for forty years. 12 I will make Egypt the most ruined land among ruined lands, and its cities will lie waste for forty years among ruined cities. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and spread them among the countries.

"13 For this is what the Lord GOD says: After forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples where they were scattered. 14 I will bring back the captives of Egypt and return them to the land of Pathros, their homeland. There they will be a lowly kingdom. 15 It will be the lowliest of kingdoms and will never again be greater than other nations. I will make it so small that it will not rule over other nations. 16 It will never again be a source of hope for the people of Israel. It will only remind them of their sin when they turn to Egypt for help. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD."

Egypt Will Reward Babylon

17 In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me.

"18 Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made his army work very hard against Tyre. Every soldier's head became bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare. But he and his army got no pay from Tyre for all that work.

"19 So this is what the Lord GOD says: I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. He will carry off its wealth, take its plunder, and seize its goods. That will be pay for his army. 20 I gave him Egypt as his pay for the work he did for Me, declares the Lord GOD. 21 On that day I will make Israel strong again, and I will open your mouth among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
