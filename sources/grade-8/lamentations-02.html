God's Anger Against Zion

1 How the Lord has covered the Daughter of Zion with a cloud in His anger! He has thrown down from heaven to earth the splendor of Israel. He did not remember His temple, where He rested His feet, in the day of His anger. 2 The Lord has shown no mercy. He has swallowed up all of Jacob's homes. In His wrath He has torn down Judah's strongholds and brought them crashing to the ground. He has dishonored the kingdom and its leaders. 3 In His fierce anger He has cut off all of Israel's strength. He has pulled back His right hand from the enemy. He has burned against Jacob like a fire that consumes everything around it. 4 He has bent His bow like an enemy; His right hand is ready like a foe. He has killed all our loved ones. In the tent of the Daughter of Zion He has poured out His wrath like fire. 5 The Lord has become like an enemy. He has swallowed up Israel. He has swallowed up all her palaces and destroyed her strongholds. He has increased sorrow and mourning in the Daughter of Judah. 6 He has torn down His shelter like a garden and ruined His meeting place. the LORD has made Zion forget her sacred festivals and Sabbaths. In His fierce anger He has rejected king and priest. 7 The Lord has rejected His altar and abandoned His sanctuary. He has handed over to the enemy the walls of her palaces. They shouted in the house of the LORD as on a festival day. 8 The LORD planned to destroy the wall of the Daughter of Zion. He stretched out a measuring line and did not hold back His hand from destroying. He made the city's defenses and its wall mourn, and together they wasted away. 9 Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has destroyed and broken her bars. Her king and princes are among the nations. There is no law, and her prophets find no vision from the LORD.

Mourning in Jerusalem

10 The elders of the Daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have thrown dust on their heads and put on sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground. 11 My eyes are worn out from tears; my stomach churns; my heart is poured out on the ground because of the destruction of my people. Children and infants faint in the streets of the city. 12 They say to their mothers, "Where is bread and wine?" as they faint in the streets like dying men, and their lives are poured out on their mothers' laps.

Enemies Mock Jerusalem

13 What can I say for you? To what can I compare you, Daughter of Jerusalem? To what can I liken you, that I may comfort you, Virgin Daughter of Zion? For your wound is as great as the sea. Who can heal you? 14 Your prophets have seen false and empty visions for you. They did not expose your sin to bring you back from captivity. Instead, they spoke lies that led you astray.

15 All who pass by clap their hands in scorn at you. They hiss and shake their heads at the Daughter of Jerusalem: "Is this the city that was called the perfection of beauty, the joy of all the earth?"

16 All your enemies open their mouths against you. They hiss and grind their teeth and say, "We have destroyed her! This is the day we longed for. We have finally seen it!"

17 The LORD has done what He planned. He has carried out His word, which He commanded long ago. He has thrown down without mercy. He has made the enemy rejoice over you and made your enemies strong.

A Call to Cry Out to God

18 Their hearts cried out to the Lord, "O wall of Zion! Let your tears flow like a river day and night. Give yourself no rest, and do not let your eyes stop crying."

19 Arise, cry out in the night, at the beginning of the night watches. Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord. Lift up your hands to Him for the life of your little ones, who faint from hunger on every corner. 20 Look, O the LORD, and consider: to whom have You done this? Should women eat their own children, the babies they have nurtured? Should priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

21 Young and old lie on the ground in the streets; my young women and my young men have fallen by the sword. You have slain them in the day of Your anger; You have slaughtered them without mercy. 22 You have brought terror from every side, as on a festival day. On the day of the LORD's anger no one escaped or survived; those I raised and brought up, my enemy has destroyed.
