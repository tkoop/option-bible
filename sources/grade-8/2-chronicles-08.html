Solomon’s Building Projects

1 At the end of twenty years, after Solomon had built the temple of the LORD and his own palace, 2 he rebuilt the towns that Hiram had given him and settled Israelites in them. 3 Solomon went to Hamath Zobah and captured it. 4 He also built Tadmor in the desert and all the store cities he had built in Hamath. 5 He rebuilt Upper Beth Horon and Lower Beth Horon as fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars. 6 He did the same with Baalath and all his store cities, and with all the cities for his chariots and horses—whatever he wanted to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout his whole kingdom.

Solomon’s Workforce and Officials

7 There were still people left from the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. They were not Israelites. 8 Solomon made the descendants of these survivors do forced labor, and they still do today. 9 But Solomon did not make the Israelites slaves for his work. They were his fighting men, commanders of his chariots, and charioteers. 10 They were also King Solomon’s chief officials—two hundred fifty officials who supervised the men.

Solomon’s Palace and Temple Worship

11 Solomon brought Pharaoh’s daughter up from the City of David to the palace he had built for her, because he said, “My wife must not live in the palace of David king of Israel, because the places where the ark of the LORD has entered are holy.” 12 On the altar of the LORD that he had built in front of the portico, Solomon sacrificed burnt offerings to the LORD, 13 according to the daily requirement for offerings commanded by Moses—for the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the three annual festivals: the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the Festival of Weeks, and the Festival of Tabernacles. 14 In keeping with the ordinance of his father David, he appointed the divisions of the priests for their duties, and the Levites to lead the praise and assist the priests according to each day’s requirement. He also appointed the gatekeepers by their divisions for each gate, because this was what David the man of God had ordered. 15 The priests and Levites did not turn from the king’s commands in any matter, including the treasuries. 16 All Solomon’s work was carried out, from the day the foundation of the temple of the LORD was laid until it was finished. So the temple of the LORD was completed.

Solomon’s Voyage to Ophir

17 Then Solomon went to Ezion Geber and Elath on the coast of Edom. 18 Hiram sent him ships commanded by his own men, sailors who knew the sea. These joined Solomon’s men and sailed to Ophir and brought back four hundred fifty talents of gold, which they delivered to King Solomon.
