The Lampstand

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 "Speak to Aaron and tell him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps should shine in front of the lampstand.'"

3 Aaron did so. He set up the lamps in front of the lampstand, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 4 The lampstand was hammered from gold. From its base to its flowers, it was hammered work. Aaron made it following the pattern the LORD had shown Moses.

Cleansing and Dedicating the Levites

5 The LORD said to Moses, 6 "Take the Levites from among the people of Israel and cleanse them. 7 This is how you should make them clean: sprinkle the water of cleansing on them, have them shave their whole body, wash their clothes, and then they will be clean. 8 Then they should take a young bull from the herd, along with its grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil. You should also take a second young bull from the herd as a sin offering. 9 Bring the Levites before the tent of meeting and gather the whole community of Israel. 10 Bring the Levites before the LORD, and have the people of Israel lay their hands on them. 11 Aaron will wave the Levites as a wave offering before the LORD for the people of Israel, so they can serve the LORD. 12 The Levites will lay their hands on the heads of the bulls. You should offer one bull as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD to make atonement for the Levites. 13 Set the Levites before Aaron and his sons, and wave them as a wave offering to the LORD.

"14 Thus you shall separate the Levites from among the people of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine. 15 After that the Levites shall go in to serve at the tent of meeting, when you have cleansed them and waved them as a wave offering. 16 For they are completely given to Me from among the people of Israel. In place of all the firstborn of the people of Israel, who open the womb, I have taken them for Myself. 17 For all the firstborn among the people of Israel are Mine, both people and animals. On the day I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart for Myself. 18 I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the people of Israel. 19 I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the people of Israel, to serve the people of Israel at the tent of meeting and to make atonement for them, so that no plague may come on the people of Israel when they come near the sanctuary."

20 So Moses, Aaron, and all the community of Israel did for the Levites everything the LORD had commanded Moses about them. 21 The Levites purified themselves from sin and washed their clothes. Aaron waved them as a wave offering before the LORD, and he made atonement for them to cleanse them. 22 After that, the Levites began their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and his sons, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

Age Limits for Levite Service

23 The LORD said to Moses, 24 "This is the rule for the Levites: from twenty-five years old and up, they shall come to serve in the tent of meeting. 25 From the age of fifty, they shall retire from regular service and serve no more. 26 They may help their fellow Levites by keeping guard in the tent of meeting, but they shall do no regular service. Thus you shall deal with the Levites in their duties."
