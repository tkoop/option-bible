Moses Is Told to Ordain Aaron

1 The LORD spoke to Moses: 2 "Take Aaron and his sons with you, and the garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread. 3 Bring the whole community together at the entrance to the tent of meeting." 4 Moses did as the LORD commanded him, and the community gathered at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 5 Moses said to the community, "This is what the LORD has commanded to be done."

Aaron Is Washed, Dressed, and Anointed

6 Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water. 7 He put the tunic on Aaron, tied the sash around him, clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him. He tied the woven band of the ephod around him and fastened it. 8 He put the breastpiece on him and placed the Urim and Thummim in it. 9 He set the turban on Aaron's head, and on the front of the turban he placed the gold plate, the holy crown, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

10 Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, and he made them holy. 11 He sprinkled some of the oil on the altar seven times. He anointed the altar and all its tools, the basin, and its stand, to make them holy. 12 He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him to make him holy.

13 Moses brought Aaron's sons forward, clothed them with tunics, tied sashes around them, and put caps on them, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Sin and Burnt Offerings

14 Then Moses brought the bull for the sin offering. Aaron and his sons placed their hands on the bull's head. 15 Moses killed it. He took the blood and, with his finger, put it on the horns of the altar all around. He purified the altar. He poured the rest of the blood at the base of the altar and made it holy, to purify it. 16 He took all the fat around the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and burned them on the altar. 17 He burned the bull's hide, flesh, and dung outside the camp, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

18 Then Moses brought the ram for the burnt offering. Aaron and his sons placed their hands on its head. 19 Moses killed it and sprinkled the blood all around the altar. 20 He cut the ram into pieces and burned the head, the pieces, and the fat. 21 He washed the inner parts and the legs with water and burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Ordination Ram and Final Consecration

22 Then Moses brought the second ram, the ram used for ordination. Aaron and his sons placed their hands on its head. 23 Moses killed it. He took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 24 He brought Aaron's sons forward and put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Then Moses sprinkled the blood all around the altar.

25 He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat around the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh. 26 From the basket of unleavened bread that was before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one loaf mixed with oil, and one wafer. He put them on the fat and on the right thigh. 27 He placed all of this in the hands of Aaron and his sons, and they waved it before the LORD as a wave offering. 28 Then Moses took it from their hands and burned it on the altar on top of the burnt offering. It was an ordination offering, a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

29 Moses took the breast and waved it before the LORD as a wave offering. It was Moses' share from the ram of ordination, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 30 Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood on the altar and sprinkled them on Aaron, his clothes, his sons, and their clothes. In this way he made Aaron, his clothes, his sons, and their clothes holy.

31 Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance to the tent of meeting and eat it there with the bread in the basket of ordination, as I commanded when I said, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat it.' 32 You shall burn whatever is left of the meat and the bread.

33 "You shall not leave the entrance to the tent of meeting for seven days, until your time of ordination is complete, because it takes seven days to ordain you. 34 The LORD has commanded this, as was done today, to make atonement for you.

35 "You shall stay at the entrance to the tent of meeting day and night for seven days, and keep the LORD's charge, so that you will not die, for this is what I was commanded."

36 Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD had commanded through Moses.
