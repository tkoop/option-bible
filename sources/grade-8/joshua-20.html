God Commands Cities of Refuge

1 Then the LORD said to Joshua, 2 “Tell the Israelites to set apart cities of refuge, just as I told you through Moses. 3 That way, anyone who kills a person by accident, without meaning to, can run there. Those cities will protect him from the one who wants revenge for the death.

4 “When someone runs to one of those cities, he must stand at the city gate and explain his case to the city elders. Then they must let him in and give him a place to live with them. 5 If the one who wants revenge follows him, the city must not hand over the killer. He killed his neighbor by accident, not on purpose, and he did not hate him before. 6 He must stay in that city until he has had a trial before the community and until the high priest who is serving then dies. After that, he may go home to the city he ran from.”

The Six Cities of Refuge

7 So the Israelites set apart Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali; Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim; and Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron, in the hill country of Judah. 8 East of the Jordan, near Jericho, they set apart Bezer in the wilderness plateau from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan from the tribe of Manasseh.

Who Could Use the Cities

9 These cities were chosen for all the Israelites and for any foreigner living among them. Anyone who killed a person by accident could run there and not be killed by the one seeking revenge until he had stood trial before the community.
