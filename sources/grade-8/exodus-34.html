Moses Prepares New Stone Tablets

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Chisel out two stone tablets like the first ones. I will write on them the words that were on the first tablets, the ones you broke. 2 Be ready in the morning, and come up on Mount Sinai. Present yourself to Me there on top of the mountain. 3 No one may come up with you. No one may be seen anywhere on the mountain, and no flocks or herds may graze in front of it."

4 So Moses chiseled out two stone tablets like the first ones. He got up early in the morning and went up Mount Sinai, as the LORD had commanded him. He carried the two stone tablets in his hands. 5 Then the LORD came down in the cloud, stood with him there, and proclaimed His name, the LORD.

6 Then the LORD passed in front of him and proclaimed: "The LORD, the LORD, the compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, full of faithful love and truth, 7 keeping faithful love for thousands, forgiving wrongdoing, rebellion, and sin. But He will not leave the guilty unpunished. He will bring the fathers' sin on the children and grandchildren, even to the third and fourth generations."

8 Moses quickly bowed to the ground and worshiped.

9 "O Lord, if I have found favor in Your sight," he said, "please go with us. Even though this is a stubborn people, forgive our wrongdoing and sin, and take us as Your own."

God's Covenant and Commands

10 Then the LORD said, "I am making a covenant. Before all your people I will do wonders that have never been done in any nation in all the earth. Everyone among whom you live will see the LORD's work, for it is an awesome thing that I am doing with you.

11 "Observe what I command you today. I will drive out before you the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 12 Be careful not to make a covenant with the people who live in the land you are entering. If you do, they may become a trap among you. 13 Instead, tear down their altars, smash their sacred stones, and cut down their Asherah poles. 14 You must not worship any other god, because the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God."

15 "Be careful not to make a treaty with the people of that land. When they worship their gods and sacrifice to them, they will invite you, and you will eat their sacrifices. 16 If you take some of their daughters as wives for your sons, those daughters will worship their gods and lead your sons to do the same."

17 "You must not make any metal gods for yourselves."

18 "Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. For seven days eat bread without yeast, as I commanded you, at the set time in the month of Abib, because in Abib you came out of Egypt. 19 Every firstborn male is Mine, including the firstborn of all your livestock, whether ox or sheep. 20 You must redeem the firstborn of a donkey with a lamb. If you do not redeem it, break its neck. You must redeem all the firstborn of your sons. No one may come before Me without bringing an offering."

21 "Work six days, but rest on the seventh day. Even during plowing and harvest, you must rest. 22 Celebrate the Feast of Weeks with the first grain from your wheat harvest, and the Feast of Ingathering at the end of the year."

23 "Three times a year all your males must appear before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel. 24 I will drive out nations before you and enlarge your territory, so no one will want your land when you go up three times a year to appear before the LORD your God."

25 "Do not offer the blood of My sacrifice with anything that has yeast. Do not let the Passover sacrifice remain until morning. 26 Bring the best of the firstfruits from your soil to the house of the LORD your God. Do not boil a young goat in its mother's milk."

27 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Write down these words, because by these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel."

28 Moses was there with the LORD forty days and forty nights. He ate no bread and drank no water. The LORD wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant—the Ten Commandments.

Moses' Face Shines

29 When Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the covenant in his hands, he did not know that his face was shining because he had spoken with the LORD. 30 When Aaron and all the Israelites saw Moses, they saw that his face was shining. They were afraid to come near him.

31 But Moses called to them, so Aaron and all the leaders of the community came back to him, and Moses spoke to them. 32 Afterward all the Israelites came near, and Moses commanded them to do everything the LORD had told him on Mount Sinai.

33 When Moses finished speaking with them, he put a veil over his face. 34 Whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with Him, he took off the veil until he came out. When he came out, he told the Israelites what he had been commanded. 35 The Israelites would see that Moses' face was glowing. Then Moses would put the veil back over his face until he went in to speak with the LORD.
