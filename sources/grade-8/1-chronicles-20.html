David Defeats the Ammonites

1 In the spring, when kings go out to war, Joab led the army. He destroyed the land of the Ammonites and went to Rabbah and besieged it. David stayed in Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah and destroyed it. 2 David took the crown from the head of their king. It weighed a talent of gold, and it had a precious stone. It was placed on David's head. He also brought out a great amount of plunder from the city. 3 He brought out the people who were in it and made them work with saws, iron picks, and axes. David did this to all the Ammonite towns. Then David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.

David's Men Defeat Philistine Giants

4 Later, war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Sippai, one of the descendants of the Rephaim, and they were subdued. 5 There was another battle with the Philistines. Elhanan son of Jair struck down Lahmi, the brother of Goliath the Gittite. His spear shaft was like a weaver's beam. 6 There was another battle at Gath. There was a huge man who had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He also was descended from the Rephaim. 7 He taunted Israel, and Jonathan son of Shimea, David's brother, struck him down. 8 These were descendants of the Rephaim in Gath. They fell by the hand of David and his men.
