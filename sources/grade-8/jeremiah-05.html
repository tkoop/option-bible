A Search for Justice in Jerusalem

1 Roam through the streets of Jerusalem and look. Search its squares. If you can find one person who does what is right and seeks truth, then I will forgive the city. 2 Even if they say, "I swear by the living the LORD," they are lying.

3 The LORD, don't Your eyes look for truth? You struck them, but they didn't give in. You wore them down, but they refused to learn. They set their faces like stone and refused to turn back. 4 Then I said, "They're just poor people. They're foolish. They don't know the the LORD's way or what their God expects." 5 "I'll go to the leaders and speak to them, because they know the the LORD's way and what their God expects." But they too have broken the yoke and snapped the straps.

6 So a lion from the forest will kill them, a wolf from the desert will destroy them, and a leopard will prowl around their cities. Anyone who leaves will be torn apart, because their sins are many and their turning away is great. 7 "Why should I forgive you? Your children have left Me and sworn by gods that aren't real. I fed them well, but they committed adultery and crowded into the prostitute's house."

8 They were well-fed, lusty horses, each one chasing after his neighbor's wife. 9 "Shouldn't I punish people like this?" declares the the LORD. "Shouldn't I take revenge on a nation like this?"

The Coming Judgment

10 Go up through her vine rows and destroy, but don't destroy everything. Strip away her branches, because they don't belong to the the LORD. 11 "The house of Israel and the house of Judah have been very unfaithful to Me," declares the the LORD.

12 They have lied about the the LORD and said, "He won't harm us. Disaster won't come on us. We won't see war or starvation." 13 The prophets are like wind, and the word isn't in them. So that is what will happen to them.

14 So this is what the the LORD, the God of Armies, says: "Because you have said this, I am making My words like fire in your mouth. These people are like wood, and the fire will burn them up." 15 "Look, I am bringing a nation against you from far away, house of Israel," declares the the LORD. "It is an old nation, a nation from long ago. You won't know its language, and you won't understand what they say."

16 Their quiver is like an open grave. All of them are warriors. 17 They will eat your harvest and food, your sons and daughters, your flocks and herds, and your vines and fig trees. They will use the sword to destroy your fortified cities, the ones you trust in. 18 "Yet even in those days," declares the the LORD, "I won't destroy you completely."

19 When they ask, "Why has the the LORD our God done all this to us?"

You will answer, "Just as you left Me and served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land that isn't yours."

A Stubborn and Rebellious People

20 Declare this in the house of Jacob and proclaim it in Judah: 21 "Hear this, you foolish and senseless people. You have eyes but don't see. You have ears but don't hear." 22 "Don't you fear Me?" declares the the LORD. "Don't you tremble before Me? I made the sand a boundary for the sea, an everlasting rule it can't cross. The waves may toss, but they can't win. They may roar, but they can't cross it."

23 But these people have stubborn and rebellious hearts. They have turned away and left. 24 They don't say in their hearts, "Let us fear the the LORD our God, who gives rain in its season, both the early and late rain, and keeps for us the appointed weeks of harvest." 25 Your wrongdoing has turned these blessings away, and your sins have kept good from you.

Corrupt Leaders and False Prophets

26 Wicked men are found among My people. They watch like bird catchers lying in wait. They set a trap and catch people. 27 Like a cage full of birds, their houses are full of deceit. That's why they've become great and rich. 28 They are fat and sleek. They also do evil in matters of justice. They don't defend the orphan's case or judge the poor fairly.

29 "Shouldn't I punish them for this?" declares the the LORD. "Shouldn't I take revenge on a nation like this?" 30 A horrible and shocking thing has happened in the land: 31 The prophets prophesy lies, and the priests rule by their own power. My people love it that way! But what will you do when it ends?
