Dead in Sin, Alive in Christ

1 You were dead in your sins and wrong choices. 2 You used to live that way, following the ways of this world and the ruler of the evil spirits in the air, the spirit that is now working in people who refuse to obey. 3 We all lived like that too at one time. We followed our selfish desires and did what our bodies and minds wanted. Like everyone else, we were under God's anger by nature.

4 But God is rich in mercy. Because He loved us so much, 5 He made us alive with Christ even when we were dead in our sins. You are saved by grace. 6 God raised us up with Christ and seated us with Him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus. 7 He did this so that in the coming ages He might show the amazing riches of His grace through His kindness to us in Christ Jesus.

Saved by Grace Through Faith

8 You are saved by grace through faith. This is not from your own doing; it is God's gift. 9 It is not from works, so no one can brag. 10 For we are God's work, created in Christ Jesus for good works. God prepared these works ahead of time so that we would walk in them.

Christ Breaks Down the Wall

11 So remember that you Gentiles by birth were called "uncircumcised" by those called "circumcised" in the flesh by human hands. 12 At that time you were without Christ. You were cut off from Israel's community, strangers to God's promises, with no hope and without God in the world. 13 But now in Christ Jesus, you who were far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

14 For He Himself is our peace. He made the two groups one. He broke down the dividing wall of hostility in His flesh. 15 He set aside the law with its commands and rules in order to create in Himself one new people out of the two, making peace. 16 He reconciled both to God in one body through the cross, killing the hostility there. 17 He came and preached peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near. 18 For through Him we both have access to the Father by one Spirit.

God's Household and Temple

19 So you are no longer strangers and aliens. You are fellow citizens with the saints and members of God's household. 20 You are built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus Himself as the cornerstone. 21 In Him the whole building is joined together and grows into a holy temple in the Lord. 22 In Him you too are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit.
