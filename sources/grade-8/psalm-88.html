A Prayer from Deep Darkness

1 The LORD, God of my salvation, I cry out before You day and night. 2 Let my prayer come before You; turn Your ear to my cry. 3 For my soul is full of troubles, and my life is close to the grave.

4 I am counted among those who go down to the pit; I am like a man with no strength. 5 I am abandoned among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom You remember no more, and who are cut off from Your help.

Cast into the Depths

6 You have put me in the lowest pit, in dark places, in the depths. 7 Your wrath lies heavy on me; You have overwhelmed me with all Your waves. Selah. 8 You have taken my friends far from me; You have made me a horror to them. I am shut in and cannot escape.

9 My eyes grow weak from sorrow. I call on You every day, O the LORD; I spread out my hands to You.

Questions from the Darkness

10 Do You work wonders for the dead? Do the dead rise to praise You? Selah. 11 Is Your loving devotion declared in the grave, Your faithfulness in destruction? 12 Are Your wonders known in the darkness, or Your righteousness in the land where people are forgotten?

13 But I cry out to You, O the LORD; in the morning my prayer comes before You. 14 Why, O the LORD, do You reject my soul? Why do You hide Your face from me?

Left Alone in the Dark

15 Since I was young, I have suffered and been near death. I have endured Your terrors and am worn out. 16 Your wrath has swept over me; Your terrors have destroyed me. 17 They surround me all day like water; they close in on me from every side. 18 You have taken my loved ones and friends far from me; darkness is my only companion.
