The LORD's Message to Joel

1 This is the word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel.

A Plague of Locusts

2 Hear this, you elders, and listen, all who live in the land. Has anything like this happened in your days or in the days of your fathers? 3 Tell it to your children, and let your children tell their children, and their children tell the next generation. 4 What the first locusts left behind, the second locusts ate; what the second left, the third ate; and what the third left, the fourth ate.

Wake Up and Weep

5 Wake up, you drunkards, and weep! Wail, all you who drink wine, because the sweet wine has been taken from your mouths. 6 For a mighty nation has come up against my land. It is too strong to count. Its teeth are like a lion's teeth, and it has the fangs of a lioness. 7 It has ruined my vine and broken my fig tree. It has stripped off the bark and thrown it away, so the branches have turned white.

8 Mourn like a young bride in sackcloth, grieving for the husband she married when she was young. 9 Grain offerings and drink offerings have been cut off from the house of the LORD. The priests who serve the LORD mourn. 10 The fields are ruined, and the land mourns. The grain is destroyed, the new wine is dried up, and the olive oil is gone.

11 Be ashamed, you farmers! Wail, you grape growers, over the wheat and barley, because the harvest in the field has been lost. 12 The vine is dried up, and the fig tree is withered. The pomegranate, the palm, and the apple tree—with all the trees of the field—have dried up. Surely the joy of people has faded away.

Call the Priests to Mourn

13 Put on sackcloth and mourn, you priests! Wail, you who serve at the altar. Come and spend the night in sackcloth, you servants of my God, because grain offerings and drink offerings are no longer brought to the house of your God. 14 Set apart a day of fasting and call a special meeting. Gather the elders and everyone who lives in the land to the house of the LORD your God, and cry out to the LORD.

The Day of the LORD Is Near

15 Oh, what a terrible day! The day of the LORD is near. It comes like destruction sent by the Almighty. 16 Hasn't our food been cut off right before our eyes? Joy and gladness have left the house of our God. 17 The seeds rot under the soil. The storehouses are empty. The barns are torn down because the grain has dried up.

18 How the animals groan! The herds of cattle are confused because they have no pasture. Even the flocks of sheep have been ruined. 19 To You, the LORD, I cry out, for fire has eaten the pastures of the wilderness, and flames have burned all the trees of the field. 20 Even the wild animals cry out to You, because the streams have dried up, and fire has eaten the pastures of the wilderness.
