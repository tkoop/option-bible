The Famine and the Gibeonites

1 During David's reign, a famine lasted three years in a row. David asked the LORD for help. The LORD said, "It is because of the blood Saul and his family spilled. He killed the Gibeonites."

2 So the king called the Gibeonites and spoke to them. The Gibeonites were not Israelites; they were a small group left from the Amorites. The Israelites had promised not to harm them, but Saul had tried to wipe them out because he was eager to help Israel and Judah.

3 David asked the Gibeonites, "What should I do for you? How can I make this right so you will bless the LORD's land?"

4 The Gibeonites said, "We do not want silver or gold from Saul or his family. We also do not want any Israelite put to death."

David said, "Tell me what you want, and I will do it."

5 They answered the king, "The man who destroyed us and tried to wipe us out of Israel's land..."

6 "Let seven of his male descendants be handed over to us. We will hang them before the LORD at Gibeah, Saul's hometown, the place the LORD chose."

The king said, "I will give them to you."

7 The king spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, Saul's son, because of the promise David and Jonathan had made before the LORD.

David Hands Over Saul's Descendants

8 But the king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the two sons Rizpah daughter of Aiah had borne to Saul. He also took the five sons of Michal, Saul's daughter, whom she had borne to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.

9 He gave them to the Gibeonites, and they hanged them on a hill before the LORD. All seven died together. They were put to death early in the harvest, when the barley harvest began.

10 Rizpah daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and spread it on a rock for herself. She stayed there from the start of the harvest until rain fell from the sky. She kept the birds of the air from landing on the bodies during the day, and she kept the wild animals away at night.

11 David was told what Rizpah, Saul's concubine, had done.

David Buries Saul and Jonathan

12 So David went and took the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan from the men of Jabesh Gilead. They had stolen them from the public square in Beth Shan, where the Philistines had hung them on the day they killed Saul at Gilboa.

13 David brought up the bones of Saul and Jonathan. They also gathered the bones of those who had been hanged.

14 They buried the bones of Saul and Jonathan in Zela, in Benjamin's land, in the tomb of Saul's father Kish. After they did everything the king commanded, God answered prayer for the land.

More Battles with the Philistines

15 Later, war broke out again between the Philistines and Israel. David and his men went down to fight. David became very tired.

16 Ishbi-Benob, one of the descendants of Rapha, had a bronze spear that weighed three hundred shekels. He carried a new sword and planned to kill David.

17 But Abishai son of Zeruiah came to help David. He struck the Philistine and killed him. Then David's men made a promise to him: "Never again will you go out with us to battle, so that Israel will not lose its leader."

18 After this, another battle took place with the Philistines at Gob. Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph, who was also one of Rapha's descendants.

19 In another battle with the Philistines at Gob, Elhanan son of Jaare the weaver killed Goliath the Gittite. Goliath's spear shaft was as thick as a weaver's beam.

20 There was still another battle at Gath. A huge man was there with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, twenty-four in all. He also came from Rapha's family.

21 When he made fun of Israel, Jonathan, son of David's brother Shimea, killed him.

22 These four men were Rapha's descendants in Gath. They died at the hands of David and his men.
