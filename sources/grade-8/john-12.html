Jesus Anointed at Bethany

1 Six days before Passover, Jesus went to Bethany, where Lazarus lived—the man Jesus had raised from the dead. 2 They gave a dinner for Him there. Martha served, and Lazarus was one of the people eating with Him. 3 Mary took about a pound of very expensive perfume made of pure nard and poured it on Jesus' feet. Then she wiped His feet with her hair. The whole house was filled with the smell of the perfume.

4 But Judas Iscariot, one of His disciples—the one who would later hand Him over—said, 5 "Why wasn't this perfume sold for three hundred silver coins and given to the poor?" 6 He didn't say this because he cared about the poor. He said it because he was a thief. He kept the money box and stole from what was put into it.

7 Jesus said, "Leave her alone. She saved this perfume for the day I'm buried. 8 You will always have poor people with you, but you won't always have Me."

9 A large crowd of Jews heard that Jesus was there, so they came. They came not only to see Jesus but also to see Lazarus, whom He had raised from the dead. 10 So the chief priests made plans to kill Lazarus too, 11 because many Jews were leaving them and putting their faith in Jesus because of him.

The Triumphal Entry

12 The next day, the large crowd that had come for the feast heard that Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem. 13 They took palm branches and went out to meet Him. They shouted, "Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord—the King of Israel!"

14 Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it. This happened just as it is written: 15 "Do not be afraid, daughter of Zion. Look, your King is coming, sitting on a donkey's colt."

16 At first, His disciples didn't understand this. But after Jesus was glorified, they remembered that these things had been written about Him and that they had been done for Him.

17 The crowd that had been with Him when He called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead kept telling others about it. 18 That's why the crowd went out to meet Him—because they heard He had done this sign.

19 So the Pharisees said to each other, "You see that you're getting nowhere. Look, the whole world has gone after Him!"

Jesus Speaks About His Death

20 Some Greeks were among the people who went up to worship at the feast. 21 They came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee, and said, "Sir, we want to see Jesus." 22 Philip went and told Andrew. Then Andrew and Philip went and told Jesus.

23 Jesus answered them, "The time has come for the Son of Man to be glorified. 24 I tell you the truth: unless a grain of wheat falls to the ground and dies, it stays alone. But if it dies, it produces a lot of grain."

25 "Anyone who loves their own life will lose it. But anyone who hates their life in this world will keep it for eternal life. 26 Whoever serves Me must follow Me. Where I am, My servant will be too. The Father will honor anyone who serves Me."

27 Jesus said, "Now My soul is troubled. What should I say—'Father, save Me from this hour'? No, this is why I came to this hour. 28 Father, glorify Your name."

Then a voice came from heaven: "I have glorified it, and I will glorify it again."

29 The crowd standing there heard it. Some said it was thunder. Others said, "An angel has spoken to Him."

30 Jesus answered, "This voice came for your sake, not for Mine. 31 Now this world is being judged. Now the ruler of this world will be thrown out. 32 And when I am lifted up from the earth, I will draw all people to Myself."

33 He said this to show what kind of death He was going to die.

34 The crowd answered Him, "We have heard from the Law that the Christ will remain forever. So how can you say the Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?"

35 Jesus told them, "The light will be with you for a little while longer. Walk while you have the light, so the darkness won't overtake you. Anyone who walks in the dark doesn't know where they're going. 36 While you have the light, believe in the light, so you can become children of light."

After Jesus said this, He left and hid from them.

Unbelief and Jesus' Final Teaching

37 Even though He had done so many signs right in front of them, they still didn't believe in Him. 38 This happened to fulfill what the prophet Isaiah said: "Lord, who has believed our message? And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?"

39 That's why they couldn't believe. Isaiah also said, 40 "He has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts, so they won't see with their eyes or understand with their hearts and turn to Me—and I would heal them."

41 Isaiah said this because he saw His glory and spoke about Him.

42 Still, many of the leaders believed in Him. But because of the Pharisees, they didn't admit it, because they didn't want to be thrown out of the synagogue. 43 They loved praise from people more than praise from God.

44 Jesus called out, "Whoever believes in Me doesn't really believe in Me alone, but in the One who sent Me. 45 Whoever sees Me sees the One who sent Me. 46 I have come into the world as light, so that everyone who believes in Me won't stay in darkness."

47 "If anyone hears My words and doesn't keep them, I don't judge them, because I didn't come to judge the world but to save it. 48 The person who rejects Me and doesn't accept My words already has a judge: the word I spoke will judge them on the last day."

49 "I didn't speak on My own. The Father who sent Me gave Me a command about what to say and how to say it. 50 I know His command brings eternal life. So I say exactly what the Father has told Me to say."
