Moving On to Maturity

1 So let us leave the basic teaching about Christ and move on toward maturity. We should not start over by laying again the foundation of turning away from actions that bring death and of faith in God, 2 or of teaching about baptisms, the laying on of hands, the rising of the dead, and eternal judgment. 3 And we will do this if God allows.

The Danger of Falling Away

4 It is impossible to bring back to repentance those who were once enlightened, who tasted the heavenly gift, who shared in the Holy Spirit, 5 who tasted God's good message and the powers of the coming age, and then fell away. 6 They are crucifying the Son of God again for themselves and putting Him to public shame.

7 Land that soaks up the rain that often falls on it and produces useful crops for the farmers receives a blessing from God. 8 But land that grows thorns and weeds is worthless and close to being cursed. In the end, it will be burned.

9 Dear friends, even though we speak like this, we are sure that you have better things—things that go with salvation. 10 God is not unfair. He will not forget the work you have done and the love you have shown for His name by serving God's people and by continuing to serve them. 11 We want each of you to show this same eagerness to the very end, so that you may be fully sure of your hope. 12 Then you will not be lazy. Instead, you will follow the example of those who receive what God has promised because of their faith and patience.

God's Promise and Our Hope

13 When God made His promise to Abraham, He could not swear by anyone greater, so He swore by Himself. 14 He said, "I will surely bless you and multiply you." 15 And so, after waiting patiently, Abraham received what was promised.

16 People swear by something greater than themselves, and an oath settles every argument for them. 17 God wanted to make it very clear to those who would receive the promise that His plan would never change. So He added an oath. 18 God cannot lie. So by these two unchangeable things, we who have run to Him for safety can hold on to the hope set before us with strong encouragement. 19 We have this hope as an anchor for the soul. It is safe and sure, and it goes into the inner place behind the curtain. 20 Jesus has gone there ahead of us. He has become a high priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek.
