Joab Warns David

1 Someone told Joab, "The king is crying and mourning for Absalom." 2 So the victory that day turned into sadness for all the people, because they heard the king was grieving for his son. 3 The people slipped into the city that day, like people who are ashamed when they run away from a battle. 4 The king covered his face and cried out loudly, "O my son Absalom! Absalom, my son, my son!"

5 Joab went into the king's house and said, "Today you have shamed the faces of all your servants who saved your life today, and the lives of your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your concubines. 6 You love those who hate you and hate those who love you. You have shown today that the commanders and servants mean nothing to you. Today I see that if Absalom were alive and all of us were dead, it would please you. 7 Now get up, go out, and speak kindly to your servants. I swear by the LORD that if you do not go out, not one man will stay with you tonight. That would be worse for you than all the trouble you have had since you were young."

8 Then the king got up and sat at the gate. The people were told, "The king is sitting at the gate." All the people came before the king, but Israel had fled, each man to his tent. 9 All the people in all the tribes of Israel were arguing, "The king saved us from our enemies and from the Philistines. Now he has fled from the land because of Absalom. 10 Absalom, whom we anointed over us, died in battle. So why are you silent about bringing the king back?"

David Returns to Jerusalem

11 King David sent word to Zadok and Abiathar the priests: "Speak to the elders of Judah and say, 'Why are you the last to bring the king back to his house? The word of all Israel has reached the king, asking for him to come home. 12 You are my brothers, my own flesh and bone. Why are you the last to bring the king back?' 13 And say to Amasa, 'Are you not my own flesh and bone? May God do to me, and more, if you are not commander of the army before me always in place of Joab.'"

14 He won the hearts of all the men of Judah as one man, so they sent to the king, "Return, you and all your servants." 15 So the king returned and came as far as the Jordan. Judah came to Gilgal to meet the king and help bring him across the Jordan.

Shimei and Mephibosheth Meet the King

16 Shimei son of Gera, a Benjaminite from Bahurim, hurried down with the men of Judah to meet King David. 17 A thousand men from Benjamin were with him, along with Ziba, the servant of Saul's house, his fifteen sons, and twenty servants. They rushed across the Jordan ahead of the king. 18 The ferry crossed to bring the king's household over and to do what seemed good to him. Shimei son of Gera fell before the king as he crossed the Jordan. 19 He said to the king, "My lord, do not count me guilty. Do not remember what your servant did wrong on the day my lord the king left Jerusalem. Do not take it to heart. 20 Your servant knows he sinned. Today I am the first of Joseph's house to come down to meet my lord the king."

21 Abishai son of Zeruiah answered, "Shouldn't Shimei be put to death for this? He cursed the LORD's anointed."

22 David said, "What do I have to do with you, sons of Zeruiah, that you are against me today? Should any man be put to death in Israel today? Don't I know today that I am king over Israel?" 23 The king said to Shimei, "You will not die." The king swore to him.

24 Mephibosheth, Saul's son, came down to meet the king. He had not cared for his feet, trimmed his beard, or washed his clothes from the day the king left until he returned safely. 25 When he came to Jerusalem to meet the king, the king said, "Why didn't you go with me, Mephibosheth?"

26 He answered, "My lord the king, my servant tricked me. I said, 'I will saddle my donkey and ride with the king,' because your servant is lame. 27 He slandered your servant to my lord the king. But my lord the king is like an angel of God. Do what seems good to you. 28 All my father's family were only dead men before my lord the king, yet you set your servant among those who eat at your table. What right do I have to ask for more from the king?"

29 The king said, "Why say more? I have decided that you and Ziba will divide the field."

30 Mephibosheth said, "Let him take it all, since my lord the king has come home in peace."

Barzillai's Farewell and a Dispute

31 Barzillai the Gileadite came down from Rogelim and crossed the Jordan with the king to send him on his way. 32 Barzillai was very old, eighty years old. He had provided for the king while he stayed at Mahanaim, because he was a very rich man. 33 The king said to Barzillai, "Cross with me, and I will take care of you with me in Jerusalem."

34 Barzillai said to the king, "How many days are left in my life, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem? 35 I am eighty years old today. Can I tell good from bad? Can your servant taste what he eats or drinks? Can I still hear singers? Why should your servant be a burden to my lord the king? 36 Your servant will go a little way across the Jordan with the king. Why should the king repay me with such a reward? 37 Please let your servant go back and die in my own city, near my parents' tomb. But here is your servant Chimham. Let him cross with my lord the king, and do for him what seems good to you."

38 The king said, "Chimham will cross with me. I will do for him what seems good to you, and whatever you choose for me I will do for you."

39 All the people crossed the Jordan, and the king crossed too. He kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and Barzillai went back home.

40 The king went on to Gilgal, and Chimham went with him. All the people of Judah and half the people of Israel brought the king across.

41 Then all the men of Israel came to the king and said, "Why have our brothers, the men of Judah, stolen you away? They brought the king and his household across the Jordan, and all of David's men with him."

42 All the men of Judah answered the men of Israel, "Because the king is our close relative. Why are you angry about this? Have we eaten at the king's expense? Has he given us any gift?"

43 The men of Israel answered the men of Judah, "We have ten shares in the king, and we have more right to David than you. Why did you despise us? Weren't we the first to talk about bringing our king back?" But the words of the men of Judah were harsher than the words of the men of Israel.
