Who May Live with the LORD?

1 A psalm of David. The LORD, who may stay in Your tent? Who may live on Your holy mountain?

2 The one who walks with honesty and does what is right, speaking truth in his heart. 3 He does not gossip about others, does no harm to his neighbor, and does not bring shame on his friend. 4 He has no respect for a worthless person, but he honors those who fear the LORD. He keeps his promise even when it hurts. 5 He does not charge interest when he lends his money, and he does not take a bribe to hurt an innocent person. The one who does these things will never be shaken.
