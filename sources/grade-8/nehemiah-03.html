Rebuilding the Sheep and Fish Gates

1 Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests rebuilt the Sheep Gate. They dedicated it and set its doors in place. They worked as far as the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel, and they dedicated that part too. 2 The men of Jericho rebuilt the next section. Zakkur son of Imri rebuilt next to them.

3 The sons of Hassenaah rebuilt the Fish Gate. They put its beams, doors, bolts, and bars in place. 4 Next to them, Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired the wall. Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, repaired next to him. Zadok son of Baana also repaired next to him. 5 The men of Tekoa repaired the next section. But their leaders did not help with the work their supervisors gave them.

Repairing the Old Gate and Nearby Wall

6 Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate. They put its beams, doors, bolts, and bars in place. 7 Men from Gibeon and Mizpah repaired next to them. These included Melatiah of Gibeon and Jadon of Meronoth. Those towns were under the governor of the province west of the Euphrates River. 8 Uzziel son of Harhaiah, a goldsmith, repaired next to them. Hananiah, a perfume maker, repaired next to him. They rebuilt Jerusalem’s wall as far as the Broad Wall.

9 Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half of Jerusalem’s district, repaired next to them. 10 Jedaiah son of Harumaph repaired next to them, across from his house. Hattush son of Hashabneiah repaired next to him. 11 Malkijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-Moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens. 12 Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of the other half of Jerusalem’s district, repaired next to them. He worked with his daughters.

The Valley, Dung, and Fountain Gates

13 Hanun and the people of Zanoah rebuilt the Valley Gate. They put its doors, bolts, and bars in place. They also repaired a thousand cubits of wall as far as the Dung Gate. 14 Malkijah son of Recab, ruler of the district of Beth Hakkerem, repaired the Dung Gate. He rebuilt it and put its doors, bolts, and bars in place. 15 Shallun son of Kol-Hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate. He rebuilt it, put a roof over it, and set its doors, bolts, and bars in place. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Siloam by the King’s Garden, as far as the steps going down from the City of David.

Repairs Around the Rest of the Wall

16 Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth Zur, repaired next to him. He worked up to the place across from David’s tombs, the artificial pool, and the House of the Heroes. 17 The Levites repaired next to him under Rehum son of Bani. Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, repaired next to him for his district. 18 Their fellow Levites repaired next to him under Binnui son of Henadad, ruler of the other half of the district of Keilah.

19 Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section next to him. He worked from the place across from the steps to the armory to the corner. 20 Baruch son of Zabbai eagerly repaired another section next to him. He worked from the corner to the entrance to the house of Eliashib the high priest. 21 Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired another section next to him. He worked from the entrance to Eliashib’s house to the end of it.

22 The priests from the surrounding region repaired next to him. 23 Benjamin and Hasshub repaired in front of their own house. Next to them, Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, repaired beside his house. 24 Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section next to him. He worked from Azariah’s house to the corner.

25 Palal son of Uzai worked across from the corner and the tower that sticks out from the upper palace near the courtyard of the guard. Pedaiah son of Parosh worked next to him. 26 The temple servants who lived on the hill of Ophel made repairs next to them. They worked up to the place across from the Water Gate on the east and the tower that sticks out. 27 The men of Tekoa repaired another section next to them. They worked from the large tower that sticks out to the wall of Ophel.

28 Above the Horse Gate, the priests repaired, each in front of his own house. 29 Zadok son of Immer repaired next to them in front of his house. Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the guard at the East Gate, repaired next to him. 30 Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section next to him. Meshullam son of Berechiah repaired next to them in front of his living quarters.

31 Malkijah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired next to him as far as the house of the temple servants and the merchants. This was across from the Inspection Gate and as far as the room above the corner. 32 Between the room above the corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and merchants repaired.
