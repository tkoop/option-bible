A Song for the Sabbath

1 A psalm. A song for the Sabbath day. It is good to praise the the LORD, and to sing to Your name, O Most High. 2 It is good to tell of Your love in the morning and Your faithfulness at night. 3 Praise Him with a ten-stringed harp and the sound of a lyre. 4 For You, O the LORD, have made me glad by Your deeds; I sing for joy at the work of Your hands.

5 How great are Your works, O the LORD! Your plans are very deep. 6 A foolish person does not know this, and a person without sense does not understand it. 7 Even when the wicked grow like grass and all who do evil flourish, they will be destroyed forever. 8 But You, O the LORD, are exalted forever. 9 For look, Your enemies, O the LORD—yes, Your enemies will perish, and all who do evil will be scattered.

10 You have made me strong like a wild ox; You have poured fresh oil on me. 11 My eyes have seen my enemies fall, and my ears have heard the ruin of the wicked who rise against me.

12 Those who do right will flourish like a palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. 13 They are planted in the house of the the LORD and grow strong in the courtyards of our God. 14 Even in old age they will still bear fruit; they will stay fresh and green. 15 They will say, "The the LORD is just. He is my Rock, and there is no wrong in Him."
