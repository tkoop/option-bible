Babylon Takes Jerusalem

1 In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem and surrounded it. 2 The Lord gave Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into his hand, along with some of the objects from the house of God. Nebuchadnezzar brought them to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and put the objects in his god's treasury.

Young Men Are Chosen

3 Then the king ordered Ashpenaz, his chief official, to bring in some of the Israelites from the royal family and the nobles. 4 They were to be young men with no physical flaws, good-looking, and wise. They had to know a lot, understand what they learned, and be able to serve in the king's palace. They also had to learn the language and writings of the Babylonians. 5 The king gave them a daily amount of his own food and wine. They were to be trained for three years, and after that they would stand before the king. 6 Among them were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah from the tribe of Judah. 7 The chief official gave them new names: Daniel he called Belteshazzar, Hananiah he called Shadrach, Mishael he called Meshach, and Azariah he called Abednego.

Daniel Refuses the King's Food

8 But Daniel decided he would not make himself unclean with the king's food or wine. So he asked the chief official for permission not to defile himself. 9 God caused the chief official to show Daniel kindness and mercy. 10 The chief official said to Daniel, "I am afraid of my lord the king, who chose your food and your drink. Why should he see you looking worse than the other young men your age? Then you would put my life in danger with the king."

11 Then Daniel said to the guard the chief official had put in charge of Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, 12 "Please test your servants for ten days. Give us only vegetables to eat and water to drink. 13 Then compare our looks with the looks of the young men who eat the king's food, and treat your servants based on what you see."

14 So the guard listened to them and tested them for ten days. 15 After ten days, they looked better and healthier than all the young men who ate the king's food. 16 So the guard took away their regular food and wine and gave them vegetables instead.

God Gives Wisdom

17 God gave these four young men knowledge and skill in every kind of writing and wisdom. Daniel also understood all visions and dreams. 18 When the time was up, the chief official brought them to Nebuchadnezzar. 19 The king talked with them, and none of them was found to be like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. So they were allowed to serve before the king. 20 In every matter of wisdom and understanding that the king asked them about, he found them ten times better than all his magicians and enchanters in his whole kingdom. 21 Daniel stayed there until the first year of King Cyrus.
