A Message for Baruch

1 This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah. Baruch wrote these words on a scroll from Jeremiah’s mouth in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

God Answers Baruch

2 “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch: 3 ‘You said, “How terrible for me! For the LORD has added sorrow to my pain. I am worn out with groaning, and I find no rest.”’

Do Not Seek Great Things

4 “So this is what you must say to him: ‘This is what the LORD says: I am tearing down what I built, and I am pulling up what I planted. This applies to the whole earth.’ 5 Should you then seek great things for yourself? Do not seek them. For I am bringing disaster on all people, declares the LORD. But wherever you go, I will let you keep your life.’”
