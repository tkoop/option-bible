Teach What Fits Sound Doctrine

1But you should speak what fits sound teaching. 2Older men should be sober, worthy of respect, self-controlled, and sound in faith, love, and endurance. 3Older women should be reverent in how they live. They shouldn't be slanderers or slaves to much wine. They should teach what is good. 4Then they can train the young women to love their husbands and children, 5to be self-controlled and pure, to work at home, to be kind, and to submit to their own husbands. That way, no one will speak badly of God's word.

6Likewise, urge the young men to be self-controlled. 7In everything, set yourself as an example of good works. In your teaching, show integrity and seriousness. 8Use sound speech that no one can condemn. Then anyone who opposes you will be ashamed, because they'll have nothing bad to say about us.

9Slaves should obey their own masters in everything. They should try to please them and not talk back. 10They shouldn't steal, but should show complete honesty, so that in every way they make the teaching about God our Savior attractive.

God's Grace Trains Us

11For God's grace has appeared, bringing salvation to all people. 12It trains us to say no to ungodliness and worldly desires, and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in this present age. 13We wait for the blessed hope and the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ. 14He gave Himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for Himself a people who are His own, eager to do good works.

15Speak these things, encourage, and correct with all authority. Don't let anyone look down on you.
