Jacob Meets Rachel at the Well

1 Jacob set out again and traveled to the land of the people of the east. 2 He looked and saw a well in the field. Three flocks of sheep were lying beside it, because the flocks drank from that well. The stone on the mouth of the well was large. 3 When all the flocks gathered there, the shepherds rolled the stone away from the mouth of the well, watered the sheep, and put the stone back in its place.

4 Jacob said to them, "My brothers, where are you from?" 5 He asked, "Do you know Laban, Nahor's son?" 6 He asked, "Is he well?" 7 He said, "Look, the day is still long. It is not time to gather the livestock. Water the sheep and go feed them."

They answered, "We are from Haran." They said, "We know him." They said, "He is well. Look, his daughter Rachel is coming with the sheep." 8 They said, "We cannot until all the flocks are gathered and they roll the stone away from the mouth of the well. Then we will water the sheep."

9 While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with her father's sheep, because she watched her father's sheep. 10 When Jacob saw Rachel, his uncle Laban's daughter, and Laban's sheep, Jacob went over, rolled the stone away from the mouth of the well, and watered Laban's sheep. 11 Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud. 12 Jacob told Rachel that he was her father's relative and Rebekah's son. She ran and told her father.

Jacob Stays with Laban

13 When Laban heard the news about his nephew Jacob, he ran to meet him. He hugged him and kissed him and brought him to his house. Jacob told Laban everything. 14 Laban said to him, "Surely you are family." Jacob stayed with him a month.

15 Then Laban said to Jacob, "Should you work for me for nothing just because you are my relative? Tell me what your wages should be."

16 Laban had two daughters. The older was Leah, and the younger was Rachel. 17 Leah had weak eyes, but Rachel was beautiful in form and appearance.

18 Jacob loved Rachel, so he said, "I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel."

19 Laban said, "It is better that I give her to you than to another man. Stay with me."

20 So Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed like only a few days to him because he loved her.

21 Then Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife. My time is up, and I want to be with her as my wife."

22 Laban gathered all the people of that place and made a feast.

Laban Tricks Jacob

23 In the evening he took his daughter Leah and brought her to Jacob, and he spent the night with her. 24 Laban gave his servant Zilpah to his daughter Leah as her servant.

25 In the morning, Jacob saw that it was Leah. He said to Laban, "What have you done to me? Did I not serve you for Rachel? Why have you tricked me?"

26 Laban said, "That is not how we do it in our place. We do not give the younger daughter before the firstborn."

27 Laban said, "Finish this week, and we will give you this one too, if you will serve me another seven years."

28 Jacob did so. He finished the week, and Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife. 29 Laban gave his servant Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as her servant. 30 Jacob also spent the night with Rachel. He loved Rachel more than Leah, and he served Laban another seven years.

Leah and Rachel Have Children

31 The LORD saw that Leah was unloved, so He made it possible for her to have children, but Rachel could not have children.

32 Leah became pregnant and had a son. She named him Reuben, because she said, "The LORD has seen my suffering. Surely now my husband will love me."

33 She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, "The LORD heard that I was unloved, so He has given me this son too." She named him Simeon.

34 She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, "Now at last my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons." So she named him Levi.

35 She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, "This time I will praise the LORD." So she named him Judah. Then she stopped having children.
