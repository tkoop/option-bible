A Prayer for Help and Peace

1 Answer me when I call, O God who makes me right! You gave me room when I was in trouble; have mercy on me and hear my prayer.

2 How long will you people turn my honor into shame? How long will you love empty things and chase lies? Selah.

3 But know that the LORD has chosen those who are faithful for Himself; the LORD hears when I call to Him.

4 Tremble, and do not sin. Think about these things in your heart while you lie in bed, and be quiet. Selah.

5 Offer right sacrifices, and put your trust in the LORD.

6 Many are saying, "Who will show us any good?" Let the light of Your face shine on us, O the LORD.

7 You have filled my heart with more joy than they have when their grain and new wine are plentiful.

8 I will lie down and sleep in peace, because You alone, O the LORD, keep me safe.
