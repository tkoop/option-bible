Israel Camps at the Jordan

1 Early in the morning Joshua got up. He and all the Israelites left Shittim and went to the Jordan. They camped there before crossing. 2 After three days the officers went through the camp. 3 They gave this command to the people: "When you see the ark of the covenant of the the LORD your God being carried by the Levite priests, leave your place and follow it. 4 Keep about 3,000 feet between you and the ark. Do not go near it, so you will know which way to go, because you have never traveled this way before."

Joshua Prepares the People and Priests

5 Joshua told the people, "Make yourselves holy, because tomorrow the the LORD will do amazing things among you. 6 Then Joshua said to the priests, "Pick up the ark of the covenant and go ahead of the people." So they picked up the ark and went ahead of them.

7 The the LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel. They will know that I am with you just as I was with Moses. 8 Tell the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, 'When you reach the edge of the Jordan's water, stand still in the Jordan.'"

9 Joshua said to the Israelites, "Come here and listen to the words of the the LORD your God. 10 By this you will know that the living God is among you. He will certainly drive out before you the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites. 11 Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing over ahead of you into the Jordan. 12 Now choose for yourselves twelve men from Israel, one from each tribe. 13 As soon as the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, touch the water of the Jordan, the water flowing down from upstream will be cut off and stand up in one heap."

The Jordan River Stops Flowing

14 The people left their tents to cross the Jordan. The priests carrying the ark went ahead of them. 15 The Jordan was at flood stage, overflowing all its banks during harvest time. When the priests carrying the ark reached the Jordan and their feet touched the water's edge, 16 the water flowing from upstream stopped. It piled up in a heap far away at Adam, a town near Zarethan. The water flowing toward the Sea of the Arabah—the Salt Sea—was completely cut off. Then the people crossed over opposite Jericho. 17 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the the LORD stood on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole nation had crossed the Jordan.
