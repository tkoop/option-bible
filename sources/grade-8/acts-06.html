Choosing Seven Helpers

1 In those days, as the number of believers grew, the Greek-speaking believers complained to the Hebrew-speaking believers that their widows were being left out of the daily food distribution.

2 So the Twelve called all the believers together and said, “It wouldn’t be right for us to stop preaching God’s word to serve food. 3 Brothers, choose seven men from among you who are known for their good reputation and are full of the Spirit and wisdom. We will put them in charge of this work. 4 Then we can give our full attention to prayer and to preaching God’s word.”

5 This plan pleased everyone. They chose Stephen, a man full of faith and the Holy Spirit, and Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolaus, who was from Antioch and had become a Jewish convert. 6 They brought these men to the apostles, who prayed and laid their hands on them.

7 So God’s word spread, and the number of believers in Jerusalem grew quickly. A large number of priests also came to believe.

Stephen’s Ministry

8 Stephen was full of grace and power. He did great miracles and signs among the people.

9 But some men from the synagogue of the Freedmen, and from the synagogues of Cyrene, Alexandria, Cilicia, and Asia, began to argue with Stephen. 10 But they could not stand against his wisdom and the Spirit by which he spoke.

False Charges Against Stephen

11 So they secretly got some men to say, “We heard him speak against Moses and against God.” 12 They stirred up the people, the elders, and the teachers of the law. They seized Stephen and brought him before the council.

13 They also set up false witnesses, who said, “This man never stops speaking against this holy place and the law. 14 We have heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place and change the customs Moses gave us.”

15 Everyone in the council looked at him closely and saw that his face looked like an angel’s face.
