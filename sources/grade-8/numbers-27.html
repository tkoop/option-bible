Zelophehad's Daughters Ask for an Inheritance

1 The daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph, came forward. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 2 They stood before Moses, before Eleazar the priest, before the leaders, and before the whole community at the entrance to the tent of meeting and said, 3 "Our father died in the wilderness. He was not one of Korah's followers who gathered against the LORD, but he died because of his own sin, and he had no sons. 4 Why should our father's name disappear from his clan just because he had no son? Give us land among our father's brothers."

The LORD Answers and Gives a Law

5 So Moses brought their case before the LORD. 6 The The LORD said to Moses,

7 "The daughters of Zelophehad are right. You must certainly give them an inheritance among their father's brothers and pass their father's share on to them. 8 Tell the Israelites: If a man dies and has no son, then you must transfer his inheritance to his daughter. 9 If he has no daughter, give his inheritance to his brothers. 10 If he has no brothers, give his inheritance to his father's brothers. 11 If his father has no brothers, then give his inheritance to the closest relative in his clan, and that relative will take possession of it. This will be a lasting rule for the Israelites, as the LORD has commanded Moses."

Moses Is Told to View the Promised Land

12 The The LORD said to Moses, "Go up into the Abarim mountains and look at the land I have given to the Israelites. 13 After you have seen it, you will be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron was gathered. 14 For in the wilderness of Zin, when the people argued with you at the waters of Meribah Kadesh, you rebelled against My command and did not honor Me as holy before their eyes."

Moses Asks for a New Leader

15 Moses said to the LORD,

16 "Let the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation. 17 He must go out before them and come in before them, lead them out and bring them in, so the congregation of the LORD will not be like sheep without a shepherd."

18 The The LORD said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man who has the Spirit in him, and lay your hand on him. 19 Stand him before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation, and commission him in their sight. 20 Give him some of your authority, so the whole congregation of Israel will obey him. 21 He will stand before Eleazar the priest, who will seek the LORD's will for him through the Urim. At Joshua's command the people will go out, and at his command they will come in—he and all the Israelites with him, even the whole congregation."

22 Moses did as the LORD commanded. He took Joshua and stood him before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation. 23 He laid his hands on Joshua and commissioned him, just as the LORD had spoken through Moses.
