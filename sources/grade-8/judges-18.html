The Danites Look for Land

1 In those days there was no king in Israel. The tribe of Dan was looking for land to settle in, because they had not yet received their share of land among the tribes of Israel. 2 So the Danites sent five strong men from their clans, from Zorah and Eshtaol, to scout the land and explore it. They said to them, "Go and explore the land." The men came to the hill country of Ephraim, to Micah's house, and stayed there. 3 While they were at Micah's house, they recognized the young Levite's voice. They turned aside and asked him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing here? What do you have here?" 4 He told them, "Micah has done all of this for me. He hired me, and I am his priest." 5 They said to him, "Please ask God if our trip will succeed." 6 The priest answered, "Go in peace. The LORD is watching over your journey."

The Spies Find Laish

7 Then the five men left and came to Laish. They saw that the people there lived safely, like the Sidonians—quiet and secure. The land had everything they needed, and there was no ruler to oppress them. They were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone. 8 When they returned to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, their brothers asked, "How did it go?" 9 They said, "Come on, let's go up and attack them. We have seen the land, and it is very good. Don't hold back! Don't be slow to go and take the land. 10 When you get there, you will find a peaceful people and a spacious land, because God has given it into your hands. It is a place where nothing is lacking."

The Danites March Out and Take Micah's Priest

11 So six hundred Danites set out from Zorah and Eshtaol, armed for battle. 12 They went up and camped at Kiriath Jearim in Judah. That is why the place west of Kiriath Jearim is called Mahaneh Dan to this day. 13 From there they passed through the hill country of Ephraim and came to Micah's house. 14 Then the five men who had scouted Laish said to their brothers, "Did you know that in these houses there are an ephod, household idols, a carved image, and a cast idol? Now think about what you should do." 15 They turned aside and went to the house of the young Levite at Micah's house and greeted him. 16 The six hundred Danites stood at the entrance of the gate, armed for battle.

17 The five men who had scouted the land went in and took the carved image, the ephod, the household idols, and the cast idol, while the priest stood at the gate with the six hundred armed men. 18 When these men entered Micah's house and took the carved image, the ephod, the household idols, and the cast idol, the priest asked them, "What are you doing?" 19 They said to him, "Be quiet. Come with us and be a father and priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to one man's house, or to be priest to a tribe and family in Israel?" 20 The priest was glad. He took the ephod, the household idols, and the carved image, and went among the people. 21 They put the children, the livestock, and their belongings in front of them and turned to leave.

22 When they had gone a good distance from Micah's house, the men who lived in the houses near Micah gathered and chased the Danites. 23 They called out to the Danites, who turned around and said to Micah, "What is wrong with you? Why have you gathered these men?" 24 He said, "You took the gods I made, and my priest, and went away. What do I have left? How can you ask me, 'What is wrong with you?'" 25 The Danites said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, or angry men will attack you, and you and your household will lose your lives." 26 So the Danites went on their way, and Micah went back to his house, because he saw that they were stronger than he was.

Dan Captures Laish and Sets Up an Idol

27 The Danites took what Micah had made, and his priest, and went to Laish, to a peaceful and secure people. They attacked them with the sword and burned the city. 28 No one came to rescue them, because the city was far from Sidon, and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley that belongs to Beth Rehob. The Danites rebuilt the city and lived there. 29 They named the city Dan, after their ancestor Dan, who was born to Israel. The city had been called Laish before. 30 The Danites set up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, and his sons were priests for the tribe of Dan until the land was taken into captivity. 31 They kept Micah's carved image the whole time the house of God was at Shiloh.
