A Psalm of David

1 The LORD said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet."

2 the LORD will send out your strong scepter from Zion. Rule in the middle of your enemies.

3 Your people will freely step forward on the day you show your power. They will shine in holy beauty, and your young people will be like the morning dew.

4 The LORD has sworn an oath and will not change His mind: "You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek."

5 The Lord stands at your right hand. He will crush kings on the day He is angry.

6 He will judge the nations. He will fill the land with dead bodies. He will crush the head of the enemy across the wide earth.

7 He will drink from a stream beside the road, and then He will hold His head high.
