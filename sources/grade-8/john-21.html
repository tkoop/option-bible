Jesus Appears to His Disciples Again

1 After these things, Jesus showed Himself to His disciples again by the Sea of Tiberias. This is how He showed Himself: 2 Simon Peter, Thomas (called Didymus), Nathanael from Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two other disciples were together.

3 Simon Peter said to them, "I'm going fishing." They said to him, "We'll go with you." They went out and got into the boat, but that night they caught nothing.

A Miracle on the Shore

4 When morning came, Jesus stood on the shore, but the disciples didn't know it was Jesus.

5 Jesus said to them, "Children, do you have any fish?"

They answered Him, "No."

6 He said to them, "Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some." So they cast it, and then they couldn't pull it in because of the large number of fish.

7 The disciple Jesus loved said to Peter, "It is the Lord!" When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer garment, because he was naked, and jumped into the sea.

8 The other disciples came in the boat, dragging the net full of fish, because they weren't far from shore—only about a hundred yards away.

9 When they landed, they saw a charcoal fire there with fish on it, and some bread.

10 Jesus said to them, "Bring some of the fish you have just caught."

11 Simon Peter climbed aboard and dragged the net ashore. It was full of large fish—153 of them. Even with so many, the net didn't tear.

12 Jesus said to them, "Come and have breakfast." None of the disciples dared ask Him, "Who are You?" They knew it was the Lord.

13 Jesus came, took the bread, and gave it to them. He did the same with the fish.

14 This was now the third time Jesus appeared to His disciples after He was raised from the dead.

Jesus Asks Peter About Love

15 When they had finished eating, Jesus said to Simon Peter, "Simon son of John, do you love Me more than these?"

"Yes, Lord," he said, "You know that I love You."

Jesus said to him, "Feed My lambs."

16 He said to him again a second time, "Simon son of John, do you love Me?"

"Yes, Lord," he said, "You know that I love You."

Jesus said to him, "Take care of My sheep."

17 The third time, He said to him, "Simon son of John, do you love Me?"

Peter was sad that Jesus asked him three times if he loved Him. He said to Him, "Lord, You know all things. You know that I love You."

Jesus said to him, "Feed My sheep."

18 "Truly, truly, I tell you, when you were young, you dressed yourself and went where you wanted. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and someone else will dress you and take you where you don't want to go."

19 Jesus said this to show how Peter would die and bring glory to God. After saying this, He said to him, "Follow Me."

What About the Other Disciple?

20 Peter turned and saw the disciple Jesus loved following them. This was the one who had leaned against Jesus' chest at the supper and asked, "Lord, who is going to betray You?"

21 When Peter saw him, he said to Jesus, "Lord, what about him?"

22 Jesus said to him, "If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you? You follow Me."

23 So the rumor spread among the brothers that this disciple would not die. But Jesus didn't say he wouldn't die. He only said, "If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you?"

24 This is the disciple who gives this witness and wrote these things. We know his witness is true.

25 Jesus did many other things too. If every one of them were written down, I suppose the whole world could not hold the books that would be written.
