The Philistines Gather for Battle

1 The Philistines brought all their armies together at Aphek, while Israel camped by the spring in Jezreel. 2 The Philistine leaders marched out by hundreds and thousands, and David and his men marched at the rear with Achish.

Philistine Leaders Question David

3 The Philistine leaders asked, "What are these Hebrews doing here?"

Achish answered, "Isn't this David, servant of Saul, king of Israel? He has been with me for days or even years, and I have found nothing wrong with him since the day he came to me."

4 But the Philistine leaders were angry with Achish and said, "Send the man back to the place you put him. He must not fight with us, or he might turn against us during the battle. What better way could he win back his master's favor than by bringing him our heads? 5 Isn't this the David people sang about in their dances: 'Saul has killed his thousands, and David his tens of thousands'?"

Achish Sends David Away

6 So Achish called David and said, "As surely as the the LORD lives, you have been honest and good in my eyes. I would be glad for you to go out and come in with me in the army, because I have found no wrong in you since you came to me. But the leaders are not pleased with you. 7 So go back now and leave in peace, so you won't displease the Philistine leaders."

8 David said, "What have I done? What fault have you found in me since I have been with you, that I can't go and fight the enemies of my lord the king?"

9 Achish answered, "I know you are as good as an angel of God in my eyes. But the Philistine leaders have said, 'He must not go into battle with us.' 10 So get up early in the morning, you and your men who came with you. Go back to the place I gave you. Leave at dawn, as soon as it is light."

11 So David and his men got up early and went back to Philistine territory, while the Philistines went up to Jezreel.
