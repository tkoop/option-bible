Job Answers His Friends

"1 Look, my eye has seen all this, and my ear has heard and understood it. 2 What you know, I know too; I am not less than you. 3 But I want to speak to the Almighty; I desire to argue with God. 4 Yet you smear people with lies. All of you are worthless doctors. 5 If only you would be quiet, that would be wisdom for you. 6 Hear my argument now, and listen to the pleading of my lips. 7 Will you speak wrongly for God and speak deceitfully for Him? 8 Will you take His side? Will you argue God's case? 9 Will it go well when He examines you? Can you deceive Him as you deceive a person? 10 He will surely rebuke you if you secretly show favoritism. 11 Will not His majesty terrify you, and His dread fall on you? 12 Your sayings are as worthless as ashes; your defenses are like clay."

Job Speaks to God

"13 Be quiet and let me speak; then I will accept whatever comes. 14 Why should I risk my life? 15 Even if He kills me, I will still hope in Him. I must still defend my ways to His face. 16 He will be my salvation, because no godless person comes before Him. 17 Listen carefully to my words; let what I say reach your ears. 18 Look, I have prepared my case; I know I will be vindicated. 19 Who will contend with me? For then I would be silent and die."

Job Asks God for Answers

"20 Only grant me two things, and then I will not hide from Your face: 21 withdraw Your hand from me, and do not let Your terror make me afraid. 22 Then call, and I will answer; or let me speak, and You reply to me. 23 How many wrongs and sins have I done? Show me my rebellion and sin. 24 Why do You hide Your face and consider me Your enemy? 25 Will You frighten a windblown leaf? Will You pursue dry chaff? 26 For You write bitter things against me and make me inherit the sins of my youth. 27 You put my feet in stocks and watch all my paths; You set a limit around the soles of my feet. 28 So a person wastes away like rotting wood, like a garment eaten by moths."
