Our Heavenly Home

1 For we know that if the tent we live in on earth is destroyed, we have a building from God. It is an eternal house in heaven, not made by human hands. 2 In this tent we groan, longing to be clothed with our heavenly dwelling. 3 If we are clothed with it, we will not be found naked. 4 While we are in this tent, we groan and feel weighed down. We do not want to be unclothed, but clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life. 5 God made us for this very purpose, and He gave us the Spirit as a guarantee.

Living to Please the Lord

6 So we are always confident. We know that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord. 7 For we walk by faith, not by sight. 8 We are confident, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord. 9 So we make it our goal to please Him, whether we are at home or away. 10 For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each of us may receive what is due for what we did in the body, whether good or bad. 11 Since we know the fear of the Lord, we persuade people. But we are well known to God, and I hope we are also well known in your consciences. 12 We are not trying to praise ourselves to you again. Instead, we are giving you a reason to be proud of us, so that you can answer those who boast about outward things and not about what is in the heart. 13 If we are out of our minds, it is for God; if we are in our right minds, it is for you.

Christ’s Love Compels Us

14 For the love of Christ controls us, because we have decided this: One died for all, so all died. 15 He died for all so that those who live would no longer live for themselves, but for Him who died for them and was raised. 16 So from now on we do not view anyone from a human point of view. Even though we once knew Christ from a human point of view, we do not know Him that way anymore. 17 So if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away; look, new things have come.

The Ministry of Reconciliation

18 All this comes from God. He reconciled us to Himself through Christ, and He gave us the ministry of reconciliation. 19 In Christ, God was reconciling the world to Himself. He did not count people’s sins against them, and He has given us the message of reconciliation. 20 So we are Christ’s ambassadors, as though God were making His appeal through us. We beg you on Christ’s behalf: “Be reconciled to God.” 21 For our sake, God made Him who knew no sin to be sin, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.
