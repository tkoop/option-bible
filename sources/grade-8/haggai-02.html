The LORD Promises Greater Glory for the Temple

1 On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet. 2 The LORD told him to speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah; to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest; and to the rest of the people. He said, 3 "Who is left among you who saw this temple in its first glory? How does it look to you now? Does it not seem like nothing to you? 4 Yet now be strong, Zerubbabel, says the LORD. Be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. Be strong, all you people of the land, says the LORD, and work, for I am with you, says the LORD of Hosts. 5 I made a promise to you when you came out of Egypt, and My Spirit stays among you, so do not be afraid."

6 "For the LORD of Hosts says: In a little while I will shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry land. 7 I will shake all the nations, and the wealth of all the nations will come. I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of Hosts. 8 The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine, says the LORD of Hosts. 9 The glory of this later house will be greater than the glory of the first one, says the LORD of Hosts. In this place I will give peace, says the LORD of Hosts."

The LORD Calls His People to Be Clean

10 On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet. 11 The LORD of Hosts said, "Ask the priests about the law. 12 If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and that fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any food, will that food become holy?"

The priests answered, "No."

13 Then Haggai said, "If someone is unclean because of a dead body and touches any of these things, will they become unclean?"

The priests answered, "Yes, they will become unclean."

14 Then Haggai said, "So it is with this people and this nation before Me, says the LORD. So is every work of their hands, and what they offer there is unclean."

15 Then the LORD said, "Now think carefully about this from today on, remembering the time before a stone was laid on a stone in the LORD's temple. 16 When you came to a heap of grain expecting twenty measures, there were only ten. When you came to the winepress to draw fifty measures, there were only twenty. 17 I struck you with blight, mildew, and hail in all the work of your hands, yet you did not turn to Me, says the LORD. 18 Think carefully from this day on—from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the LORD's temple foundation was laid. Think carefully. 19 Is the seed still in the barn? Have the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree not yet produced fruit? From this day on I will bless you."

The LORD Promises to Honor Zerubbabel

20 A second time, on the twenty-fourth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to Haggai. 21 The LORD told Haggai to speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, and say, "I will shake the heavens and the earth. 22 I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the power of the nations' kingdoms. I will overthrow chariots and their riders; horses and their riders will fall, each by his brother's sword. 23 On that day, says the LORD of Hosts, I will take you, My servant Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, says the LORD, and make you like My signet ring, for I have chosen you, says the LORD of Hosts."
