A Levite Takes a Concubine

1 In those days Israel had no king. A Levite who lived in the far side of the hill country of Ephraim took a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah. 2 His concubine was unfaithful to him. She left him and went to her father's house in Bethlehem in Judah, where she stayed for four months.

3 Her husband got up and went after her to speak kindly to her and bring her back. His servant and two donkeys went with him. Her father welcomed him into the house, and when the young woman's father saw him, he was glad to meet him. 4 Her father insisted that he stay, so he stayed with him three days. They ate and drank and spent the nights there.

5 On the fourth day they got up early in the morning, and he got ready to leave. The young woman's father said to his son-in-law, "Eat something to give you strength, and then you can go." 6 So the two of them sat down and ate and drank together. The young woman's father said, "Please stay another night and enjoy yourself." 7 The man got up to leave, but his father-in-law urged him, so he stayed there that night.

8 On the fifth day he got up early to leave. The young woman's father said, "Please eat something to strengthen you. Wait until the day is almost over." So the two of them ate. 9 When the man got up to leave with his concubine and servant, his father-in-law, the young woman's father, said to him, "Look, the day is almost over. Please stay tonight. The day is almost gone. Stay here and enjoy yourself. Tomorrow you can get up early and go on your way."

The Trip to Gibeah

10 But the man would not stay the night. He got up and left. He came to Jebus, that is, Jerusalem, with his two saddled donkeys and his concubine. 11 When they were near Jebus, the day was very late. The servant said to his master, "Come, let's turn aside to this Jebusite city and spend the night there."

12 His master said to him, "No, we won't go into a foreign city whose people are not Israelites. We'll go on to Gibeah." 13 He said to his servant, "Come, let's go to one of these places and spend the night in Gibeah or Ramah."

14 So they went on. The sun went down as they came near Gibeah in Benjamin. 15 They turned aside there to spend the night in Gibeah. He went in and sat down in the city square, but no one invited them into a house to spend the night.

An Old Man Offers Hospitality

16 That evening an old man came in from his work in the fields. He was from the hill country of Ephraim and was living in Gibeah. The people of that place were Benjamites.

17 He looked up and saw the traveler in the city square. The old man asked, "Where are you going, and where do you come from?"

18 He answered, "We are traveling from Bethlehem in Judah to the far side of the hill country of Ephraim. I am from there. I went to Bethlehem in Judah, and now I am going to the house of the the LORD. But no one has taken me into his home. 19 We have straw and feed for our donkeys, and bread and wine for me, your servant, the young woman, and the servant who is with me. We don't need anything."

20 The old man said, "Peace be with you. Let me take care of all your needs. Just don't spend the night in the square."

21 So he brought him into his house and fed the donkeys. They washed their feet and ate and drank.

The Attack at Gibeah

22 While they were enjoying themselves, some wicked men of the city surrounded the house and beat on the door. They shouted to the old man, the owner of the house, "Bring out the man who came to your house so we can have sex with him."

23 The owner of the house went out to them and said, "No, my brothers, don't do this evil thing. This man has come into my house. Don't do such a disgraceful thing. 24 Look, here is my virgin daughter and his concubine. I will bring them out. You can have your way with them and do what you want with them. But don't do this disgraceful thing to this man."

25 But the men would not listen to him. So he took his concubine and brought her out to them. They raped her and abused her all night until morning. At dawn they let her go.

26 At dawn the woman came back and fell at the door of the man's house where her master was, and lay there until daylight. 27 In the morning her master got up, opened the doors of the house, and went out to continue his journey. There was his concubine, fallen at the doorway of the house, with her hands on the threshold.

28 He said to her, "Get up; let's go." But she did not answer. Then he put her on his donkey and went home.

29 When he reached home, he took a knife, grabbed his concubine, and cut her into twelve pieces, limb by limb. Then he sent the pieces throughout all Israel. 30 Everyone who saw it said, "Nothing like this has ever happened or been seen since the day the Israelites came up out of Egypt until now. Think about it, take advice, and speak."
