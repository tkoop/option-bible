Rehoboam Goes to Shechem

1 Rehoboam went to Shechem, because all Israel had come there to make him king. 2 When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard about it, he came back from Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon.

The People Ask for Relief

3 They sent for Jeroboam and brought him back. Then Jeroboam and all Israel went to Rehoboam and said, 4 “Your father made our load heavy. Now make his hard work lighter and remove the heavy load he put on us, and we will serve you.”

5 Rehoboam said, “Come back to me in three days.” So the people went away.

Rehoboam Gets Advice

6 King Rehoboam talked with the elders who had served his father Solomon while Solomon was alive. He asked, “What do you advise? How should I answer these people?”

7 They said, “If you are kind to these people and please them, and speak good words to them, they will be your servants forever.”

8 But Rehoboam ignored the elders’ advice. Instead, he talked with the young men who had grown up with him and served him.

9 He asked them, “What do you advise? How should we answer the people who said to me, ‘Lighten the load your father put on us’?”

10 The young men who had grown up with him said, “Tell the people who said to you, ‘Your father made our load heavy, but you make it lighter for us,’ ‘I am much tougher than my father.’ 11 My father burdened you with a heavy load; I will make it even heavier. My father punished you with whips; I will punish you with sharp, stinging lashes.”

12 On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came back to Rehoboam, just as the king had said.

13 The king answered them harshly. King Rehoboam rejected the elders’ advice.

14 He spoke to them as the young men had advised: “My father made your load heavy, but I will add to it. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you with sharp, stinging lashes.”

The Kingdom Splits

15 The king did not listen to the people, because this was from God. He did this to keep the word the LORD had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

16 When all Israel saw that the king refused to listen to them, they said to the king, “What part do we have in David? We have no share in Jesse’s son. Go to your tents, Israel! Look after your own house, David!” Then all Israel went home.

17 But Rehoboam still ruled over the Israelites living in the cities of Judah.

18 King Rehoboam sent Hadoram, who was in charge of forced labor. But the Israelites stoned him to death. King Rehoboam quickly got into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

19 So Israel has rebelled against the house of David to this day.
