Reuben and Gad Ask for Land East of the Jordan

1 The Reubenites and Gadites had very many animals. 2 They saw the land of Jazer and Gilead, and they saw that it was good for animals. So the Gadites and Reubenites came to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the people. 3 They said, "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon— 4 the land the the LORD defeated for the people of Israel is land for animals, and we, your servants, have animals. 5 If we have found favor in your eyes, let this land be given to your servants to own. Do not make us cross the Jordan."

Moses Warns Against Repeating the Past

6 Moses said to the Gadites and Reubenites, "Will your brothers go to war while you sit here? 7 Why do you discourage the people of Israel from crossing into the land the the LORD has given them? 8 Your fathers did the same thing when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to look at the land. 9 They went up to the Valley of Eshcol, saw the land, and discouraged the people of Israel from entering the land the the LORD had given them. 10 The the LORD was angry that day, and He swore an oath: 11 'Surely none of the men who came up from Egypt, twenty years old or older, will see the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, because they did not follow Me fully— 12 except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, because they followed the the LORD fully.' 13 So the the LORD was angry with Israel and made them wander in the desert for forty years, until the whole generation that had done evil in the the LORD's sight was gone. 14 And now you have taken your fathers' place, a group of sinful people, ready to make the the LORD even angrier with Israel. 15 If you turn away from following Him, He will leave them in the desert again, and you will destroy all these people."

The Tribes Promise to Help Fight

16 They came closer and said, "We will build sheep pens here for our animals and towns for our children. 17 But we will arm ourselves and go quickly ahead of the people of Israel until we bring them to their place. Our children will stay in the fortified towns, safe from the people who live in the land. 18 We will not go home until every person among the people of Israel has received his land. 19 We will not share in their land west of the Jordan, because our share of land has come to us east of the Jordan."

20 Moses said to them, "If you do this—if you arm yourselves before the the LORD for war, 21 and all your armed men cross the Jordan before the the LORD until He has driven out His enemies before Him, 22 and the land is brought under control before the the LORD—then afterward you may return, and you will be blameless before the the LORD and Israel, and this land will be your land before the the LORD. 23 But if you do not do this, you have sinned against the the LORD. Know that your sin will find you. 24 Build towns for your children and pens for your sheep, and do what you have promised."

25 The Gadites and Reubenites said to Moses, "Your servants will do as my lord commands. 26 Our children, wives, animals, and all our livestock will stay in the towns of Gilead. 27 But your servants will cross over, every man armed for war, before the the LORD to fight, as my lord says."

Moses Gives the Land to the Tribes

28 Moses commanded them to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the families of the tribes of Israel. 29 Moses said to them, "If the Gadites and Reubenites cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the the LORD, and the land is brought under control before you, then you will give them the land of Gilead to own. 30 But if they do not cross armed with you, they will receive land among you in Canaan." 31 The Gadites and Reubenites answered, "We will do what the the LORD has said to your servants. 32 We will cross over armed before the the LORD into Canaan, and our share of land will stay with us east of the Jordan."

33 So Moses gave to the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan—the land with its cities and the land around them. 34 The Gadites built Dibon, Ataroth, and Aroer. 35 They also built Atroth-shophan, Jazer, and Jogbehah. 36 They built Beth-nimrah and Beth-haran as fortified cities, and they made sheep pens. 37 The Reubenites built Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kiriathaim. 38 They built Nebo, Baal-meon, and Sibmah, and they gave new names to the cities they built. 39 The sons of Makir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it, and drove out the Amorites who were there. 40 Moses gave Gilead to Makir son of Manasseh, and he settled there. 41 Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their villages and called them Havvoth-jair. 42 Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and named it Nobah after himself.
