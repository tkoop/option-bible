A Call to Praise the LORD

1 Praise the LORD! Praise God in His sanctuary; praise Him in His mighty heavens. 2 Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him for His excellent greatness.

3 Praise Him with the sound of the trumpet; praise Him with the harp and lyre. 4 Praise Him with the tambourine and dancing; praise Him with strings and flute. 5 Praise Him with sounding cymbals; praise Him with loud cymbals.

6 Let everything that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
