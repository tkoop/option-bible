God Speaks Through His Son

1 Long ago, God spoke to our ancestors in many ways through the prophets. 2 But in these last days, He has spoken to us through His Son. God made His Son the heir of all things, and He created the world through Him. 3 The Son shines with God's glory and shows exactly what God is like. He holds everything together by His powerful word. After He had made us clean from sin, He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty in heaven. 4 So He became far greater than the angels, because the name He received is much better than theirs.

The Son Is Greater Than Angels

5 For God never said to any angel, "You are My Son; today I have become your Father." Or again, "I will be his Father, and he will be My Son." 6 And again, when He brings His firstborn Son into the world, He says, "Let all of God's angels worship Him." 7 He also says about the angels, "He makes His angels spirits, and His servants a flame of fire."

8 But about the Son He says, "Your throne, O God, lasts forever and ever, and the scepter of Your kingdom is a scepter of justice. 9 You have loved what is right and hated what is wrong. So God, Your God, has anointed You with the oil of joy more than Your companions."

10 And, "You, Lord, laid the foundation of the earth in the beginning, and the heavens are the work of Your hands. 11 They will pass away, but You remain. They will all wear out like a piece of clothing. 12 You will roll them up like a robe, and they will be changed like clothes. But You are the same, and Your years will never end."

13 But God never said to any angel, "Sit at My right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet."

Angels Serve God's People

14 Are not all angels serving spirits sent to help those who will receive salvation?
