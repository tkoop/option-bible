David Calls on the LORD

1 I call to You, the LORD. You are my Rock. Don't stay silent when I need You. If You stay silent, I'll be like those who go down to the grave. 2 Hear my prayers when I cry out to You. I lift my hands toward Your holy place.

Don't Drag Me Away with the Wicked

3 Don't drag me away with evil people or with those who do wrong. They act friendly to their neighbors, but they plan evil in their hearts. 4 Pay them back for what they've done. Give them what their evil actions deserve. 5 They don't understand what the LORD does or the work of His hands. So He will tear them down and not build them up.

The LORD Is My Strength

6 Praise the LORD, because He heard my prayers. 7 The LORD is my strength and shield. My heart trusted in Him, and He helped me. My heart is glad, and I will thank Him with my songs. 8 The LORD gives strength to His people. He is the safe place that saves His chosen king.

Save Your People

9 Save Your people and bless the people You chose. Lead them like a shepherd and carry them forever.
