Greeting and God's Promises

1 Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, writes to those who received a faith as precious as ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ. 2 May grace and peace be given to you in growing measure as you know God and Jesus our Lord.

3 His divine power has given us everything we need for a godly life through knowing Him who called us by His own glory and goodness. 4 Through these, He has given us His great and precious promises. Because of them, you can share in God's nature, since you have escaped the corruption in the world caused by evil desires.

Adding to Your Faith

5 For this reason, make every effort to add goodness to your faith. Then add knowledge to goodness. 6 Add self-control to knowledge, perseverance to self-control, and godliness to perseverance. 7 Add brotherly love to godliness, and love to brotherly love.

8 If you have these qualities and they keep growing, they will keep you from being useless or unproductive in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ. 9 But anyone who lacks them is nearsighted and blind. He has forgotten that he was cleansed from his past sins.

10 So, brothers and sisters, be all the more eager to make your calling and election sure. If you do these things, you will never fall. 11 You will be richly welcomed into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

A Final Reminder

12 So I will always remind you of these things, even though you know them and are firmly grounded in the truth you have. 13 I think it is right to refresh your memory as long as I am in this body. 14 I know I will soon leave this life, just as our Lord Jesus Christ has shown me. 15 I will also work hard so that after I am gone, you will always be able to remember these things.

Eyewitnesses and the Prophetic Word

16 We did not follow made-up stories when we told you about the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. We were eyewitnesses of His greatness. 17 He received honor and glory from God the Father when a voice came to Him from the Majestic Glory. It said, "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased." 18 We heard this voice from heaven when we were with Him on the holy mountain.

19 We also have the prophetic word, and it is very reliable. You will do well to pay attention to it, like a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts. 20 Above all, know this: no prophecy in Scripture comes from a person's own explanation. 21 Prophecy never came from human will, but people spoke from God as they were moved by the Holy Spirit.
