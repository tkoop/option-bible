Hezekiah Opens the Temple

1Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-nine years. His mother's name was Abijah, the daughter of Zechariah. 2He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father David had done.

3In the first month of the first year of his rule, Hezekiah opened the doors of the LORD's house and repaired them. 4He brought in the priests and Levites and gathered them in the square on the east side.

5He said to them, "Listen to me, Levites. Set yourselves apart now, and set apart the house of the LORD, the God of your fathers. Take all the unclean things out of the holy place. 6Our fathers were unfaithful. They did evil in the eyes of the LORD our God. They left Him and turned their faces away from the LORD's dwelling place. They turned away from Him. 7They also shut the doors of the porch and put out the lamps. They did not burn incense or offer burnt sacrifices in the holy place to the God of Israel. 8So the LORD's anger has fallen on Judah and Jerusalem. He has made them an object of terror, ruin, and scorn, as you can see with your own eyes. 9That is why our fathers fell by the sword, and our sons, daughters, and wives are in captivity. 10Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, so that His fierce anger will turn away from us. 11Now, my sons, do not be careless, for the LORD has chosen you to stand before Him, to serve Him, to assist Him, and to burn incense."

The Levites Cleanse the Temple

12The Levites who went to work were Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah from the Kohathites; Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel from the Merarites; Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah from the Gershonites; 13from the descendants of Elizaphan, Shimri and Jeiel; from the descendants of Asaph, Zechariah and Mattaniah; 14from the descendants of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and from the descendants of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.

15They gathered their fellow Levites, set themselves apart, and went in to clean the house of the LORD, as the king had commanded and as the LORD had said. 16The priests went into the inner part of the LORD's house to clean it. They carried all the unclean things they found in the temple of the LORD out to the courtyard of the LORD's house. The Levites took them and carried them out to the Kidron Valley. 17They began to set the temple apart on the first day of the first month. By the eighth day they had reached the porch of the LORD. They spent eight more days setting apart the LORD's house, finishing on the sixteenth day of the first month.

Sacrifices and Worship Are Restored

18Then they went in to King Hezekiah and reported, "We have cleaned the whole house of the LORD, the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the table of the showbread with all its utensils. 19We have also prepared and set apart all the articles that King Ahaz removed when he was unfaithful. They are now in front of the altar of the LORD."

20Early the next morning King Hezekiah gathered the city leaders and went up to the house of the LORD. 21They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats as a sin offering for the kingdom, the sanctuary, and Judah. The king told the priests, the descendants of Aaron, to offer them on the altar of the LORD. 22So the priests slaughtered the bulls and sprinkled their blood on the altar. They slaughtered the rams and sprinkled their blood on the altar. They also slaughtered the lambs and sprinkled their blood on the altar. 23Then they brought the goats for the sin offering before the king and the people, and they laid their hands on them. 24The priests slaughtered them and sprinkled their blood on the altar as a sin offering to make atonement for all Israel, because the king had said, "The burnt offering and the sin offering are for all Israel."

25Hezekiah placed the Levites in the house of the LORD with cymbals, harps, and lyres, as David, Gad the king's seer, and Nathan the prophet had commanded. The command came from the LORD through His prophets. 26The Levites stood with David's instruments, and the priests stood with the trumpets. 27Hezekiah gave the order to offer the burnt offering on the altar. When the burnt offering began, the song to the LORD began too, along with the trumpets and the instruments of David, king of Israel. 28The whole crowd bowed in worship, while the singers sang and the trumpeters played. All this continued until the burnt offering was finished. 29When the offerings were finished, the king and everyone with him knelt and worshiped. 30King Hezekiah and his officials told the Levites to praise the LORD with the words of David and Asaph the seer. So they praised Him with joy, bowed their heads, and worshiped.

31Then Hezekiah said, "Now that you have set yourselves apart to the LORD, come near and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the house of the LORD." So the people brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and everyone whose heart was willing brought burnt offerings. 32The burnt offerings the people brought were seventy bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs. All of these were for a burnt offering to the LORD. 33The dedicated animals were six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep. 34But there were not enough priests to skin all the burnt offerings, so their fellow Levites helped them until the work was done and until more priests had been set apart. The Levites had been more eager to set themselves apart than the priests. 35There were also many burnt offerings, along with the fat of the fellowship offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings. So the service of the house of the LORD was restored. 36Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had done for the people, because it had happened so suddenly.
