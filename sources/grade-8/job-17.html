Job's Spirit Is Broken

1 My spirit is broken; my days are running out; the grave waits for me. 2 Mockers surround me, and my eyes must stay on their insults.

Job Asks God for Help

3 Please put up a pledge for me with Yourself. Who else will shake hands with me? 4 You have hidden their hearts from understanding, so You will not lift them up. 5 If someone betrays friends for a share of the reward, his children's eyes will grow weak.

Job's Suffering and Others' Reactions

6 He has made me a joke to people; I have become someone they laugh at. 7 My eyes have grown weak with sorrow; my whole body is only a shadow. 8 Good people are shocked by this; innocent people are stirred up against the wicked. 9 But good people will stay on their path; those with clean hands will grow stronger. 10 But come back, all of you; I won't find a wise person among you.

Job Looks Toward the Grave

11 My days have passed; my plans are broken; the desires of my heart are gone. 12 They turn night into day and say, "Light is near," even though it is dark.

13 If I hope for the grave as my home and make my bed in darkness, 14 I have said to decay, "You are my father," and to the worm, "My mother and my sister."

15 So where is my hope? Who can see any hope for me? 16 Will my hope go down to the gates of the grave? Will we rest together in the dust?
