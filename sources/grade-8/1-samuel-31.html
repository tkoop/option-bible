The Philistines Defeat Israel

1 The Philistines fought against Israel. The Israelite men ran away from them, and many fell dead on Mount Gilboa. 2 The Philistines chased Saul and his sons. They killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, who were Saul's sons.

Saul Dies on the Battlefield

3 The fighting grew fierce around Saul. Archers found him and shot him, and he was badly wounded. 4 Saul said to the man carrying his armor, "Draw your sword and run me through with it. Otherwise these uncircumcised men will come and run me through and mistreat me." But the armor-bearer was afraid to do it. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it. 5 When the armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he fell on his sword too and died with him. 6 So Saul, his three sons, his armor-bearer, and all his men died together that day.

The Philistines Take Over Towns and Dishonor Saul

7 When the Israelites across the valley and beyond the Jordan saw that their army had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they left their towns and ran away. Then the Philistines moved in and lived there. 8 The next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead. They found Saul and his three sons lying on Mount Gilboa. 9 They cut off his head, took his armor, and sent messengers all over Philistia to announce the news to their idols' temples and to the people. 10 They put his armor in the temple of Ashtoreth and hung his body on the wall of Beth-shan.

Men of Jabesh-gilead Recover the Bodies

11 When the people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, 12 all the brave men got up and traveled all night. They took Saul's body and his sons' bodies down from the wall of Beth-shan, brought them to Jabesh, and burned them there. 13 Then they took the bones and buried them under a tamarisk tree in Jabesh. They fasted seven days.
