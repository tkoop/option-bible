Some Will Turn Away from the Faith

1 The Spirit clearly says that in later times some people will turn away from the faith. They will pay attention to spirits that trick them and to teachings that come from demons. 2 These teachings spread through hypocrites who lie. Their consciences have been burned so they no longer feel guilt. 3 They forbid people to marry and tell them to stay away from certain foods. But God made those foods to be received with thanks by people who believe and know the truth. 4 Everything God created is good. Nothing should be rejected if it is received with thanks, 5 because God's word and prayer make it holy.

A Good Servant of Christ Jesus

6 If you point these things out to the brothers, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourished by the words of faith and of the good teaching you have followed. 7 But turn away from godless and silly stories. Instead, train yourself to live a godly life. 8 Physical training helps for a little while, but godliness helps in every way. It carries a promise for this life and for the life to come. 9 This is a true saying, and it deserves full acceptance. 10 That is why we work hard and struggle. We have put our hope in the living God, who saves all people, and especially those who believe.

Timothy's Duties

11 Command and teach these things. 12 Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young. Instead, be an example to believers in what you say, how you live, in love, in faith, and in purity. 13 Until I come, pay attention to reading aloud, to encouraging others, and to teaching. 14 Don't neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy when the elders laid their hands on you. 15 Think about these things. Live by them, so that your progress will be clear to everyone. 16 Watch yourself and your teaching. Stay with them, because if you do this, you will save both yourself and the people who listen to you.
