A Father's Warning

1 My son, keep my words, and store my commands with you. 2 Keep my commands and live; let my teaching be as precious to you as your own eyesight. 3 Tie them on your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart. 4 Say to wisdom, "You are my sister," and call understanding your close friend. 5 They will keep you from the forbidden woman, from the stranger who flatters with her words.

A Foolish Young Man

6 For at the window of my house I looked out through my lattice, 7 and I saw among the naive. I noticed among the youths a young man without sense. 8 He was passing through the street near her corner, taking the road to her house 9 in the twilight, in the evening, in the dark of night.

The Trap Is Set

10 And there was a woman coming to meet him, dressed like a prostitute, with a cunning heart. 11 She is loud and stubborn; her feet do not stay at home. 12 Now she is in the street, now in the squares; she waits at every corner.

13 She grabbed him and kissed him; with a bold face she said to him:

14 "I have peace offerings; today I paid my vows. 15 So I came out to meet you, to look for you, and I found you. 16 I have spread my bed with coverings, colored linens from Egypt. 17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. 18 Come, let us drink our fill of love until morning; let us delight ourselves with love. 19 For the man is not at home; he has gone on a long journey. 20 He took a bag of money with him; he will be home on the day of the full moon."

21 With her many words she persuades him; with her smooth talk she leads him astray. 22 He follows her at once, like an ox going to slaughter, like a deer stepping into a trap. 23 An arrow pierces his liver. He is like a bird rushing into a snare, not knowing it will cost him his life.

A Final Warning

24 Now then, my sons, listen to me, and pay attention to my words. 25 Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. 26 For many are the victims she has brought down, and strong men are among her slain. 27 Her house is a road to the grave, leading down to the rooms of death.
