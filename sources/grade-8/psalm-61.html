For the choir director. With stringed instruments. A Psalm of David.

A Prayer for Safety

1 Hear my cry, God; listen to my prayer. 2 From far away I call to You when I feel weak. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I am. 3 For You have been my refuge, a strong tower against the enemy. 4 I will dwell in Your tent forever; I will take refuge in the shelter of Your wings. Selah

5 For You, God, have heard my vows; You have given me the inheritance of those who honor Your name.

A Prayer for the King

6 Add days to the king’s life; may his years last through many generations. 7 May he rule in God’s presence forever; let Your faithful love and truth protect him. 8 Then I will always sing praise to Your name, keeping my vows day by day.
