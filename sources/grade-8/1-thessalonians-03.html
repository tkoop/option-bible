Paul Sends Timothy to Encourage the Church

1 At last we could not stand it any longer. We decided to stay in Athens by ourselves. 2 We sent Timothy, our brother and God's helper in sharing the good news about Christ. He came to strengthen you and encourage you in your faith. 3 We did not want anyone to give up because of these troubles. You know that we are meant to face hard times. 4 When we were with you, we told you ahead of time that we would suffer. That is what happened, as you know. 5 That is why, when I could not wait any longer, I sent Timothy to find out about your faith. I was afraid the tempter might have tested you, and our work would be wasted.

Timothy Returns with Good News

6 But now Timothy has come back from you with good news. He told us about your faith and love. He said you always remember us kindly and long to see us, just as we long to see you. 7 Because of this, brothers and sisters, we were comforted in all our trouble and distress by your faith. 8 Now we feel alive again if you are standing firm in the Lord. 9 How can we thank God enough for you? We have so much joy because of you as we pray to our God. 10 Night and day we pray with all our heart that we may see you face to face. We also pray that we can fill in what is missing in your faith.

Paul Prays for the Thessalonians

11 May our God and Father Himself, and our Lord Jesus, make our way to you. 12 May the Lord make your love grow and overflow for each other and for everyone, just as ours does for you. 13 May He strengthen your hearts so you will be blameless and holy before our God and Father when our Lord Jesus comes with all His holy people. Amen.
