Judah's Borders

1 The lot for the family groups of Judah's tribe went to the border of Edom, the Wilderness of Zin, and the far south. 2 Their southern border started at the bay of the Salt Sea, from the southern end of it. 3 It went south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, passed through Zin, went up south of Kadesh-barnea, crossed Hezron, went up to Addar, and turned at Karka. 4 It passed along to Azmon, went out by the Brook of Egypt, and ended at the sea. This was your southern border.

5 The eastern border was the Salt Sea up to the mouth of the Jordan. The northern border began at the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan. 6 The border went up to Beth-hoglah and passed north of Beth-arabah. Then it went up to the stone of Bohan, Reuben's son. 7 It went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor, turned north toward Gilgal, across from the Ascent of Adummim south of the valley. It passed to the waters of En-shemesh and ended at En-rogel. 8 It went up by the Valley of Ben-hinnom to the southern slope of the Jebusite city, Jerusalem. It went up to the top of the mountain west of the Valley of Hinnom, at the north end of the Valley of Rephaim. 9 From the top of the mountain, the border curved to the spring of Nephtoah and went out to the cities of Mount Ephron. Then it curved to Baalah, which is Kiriath-jearim. 10 It turned west from Baalah to Mount Seir, passed along the north slope of Mount Jearim, which is Chesalon, went down to Beth-shemesh, and passed through Timnah. 11 It went out to the north shoulder of Ekron, curved to Shikkeron, passed Mount Baalah, went out to Jabneel, and ended at the sea. 12 The western border was the Great Sea and its coast. This was the border all around the family groups of Judah's sons.

Caleb's Portion

13 As the the LORD had said, Joshua gave Caleb son of Jephunneh a portion among Judah's sons. It was Kiriath-arba, which is Hebron. Arba was the father of Anak. 14 Caleb drove out the three sons of Anak from there: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak. 15 He went up from there against the people of Debir. Debir used to be called Kiriath-sepher. 16 Caleb said, "Whoever attacks Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as a wife." 17 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, captured it, and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

18 When she came, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got down from the donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you want?" 19 She said, "Give me a blessing. You have given me land in the Negev. Give me springs of water too." So he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs. 20 This was the land given to the tribe of Judah's sons by their family groups.

Cities of Judah

21 The cities at the far south of Judah's tribe, toward Edom's border, were Kabzeel, Eder, Jagur, 22 Kinah, Dimonah, Adadah, 23 Kedesh, Hazor, Ithnan, 24 Ziph, Telem, Bealoth, 25 Hazor-hadattah, Kerioth-hezron (that is Hazor), 26 Amam, Shema, Moladah, 27 Hazar-gaddah, Heshmon, Beth-pelet, 28 Hazar-shual, Beersheba, Biziothiah, 29 Baalah, Iim, Ezem, 30 Eltolad, Chesil, Hormah, 31 Ziklag, Madmannah, Sansannah, 32 Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon—twenty-nine cities with their villages.

33 In the lowland were Eshtaol, Zorah, Ashnah, 34 Zanoah, En-gannim, Tappuah, Enam, 35 Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh, Azekah, 36 Shaaraim, Adithaim, Gederah, and Gederothaim—fourteen cities with their villages. 37 Zenan, Hadashah, Migdal-gad, 38 Dilean, Mizpeh, Joktheel, 39 Lachish, Bozkath, Eglon, 40 Cabbon, Lahmas, Chitlish, 41 Gederoth, Beth-dagon, Naamah, and Makkedah—sixteen cities with their villages. 42 Libnah, Ether, Ashan, 43 Iphtah, Ashnah, Nezib, 44 Keilah, Achzib, and Mareshah—nine cities with their villages. 45 Ekron, with its daughters and villages, 46 and all the places from Ekron to the sea, beside Ashdod, with their villages, 47 Ashdod, with its daughters and villages, Gaza, with its daughters and villages, as far as the Brook of Egypt, and the Great Sea border.

48 In the hill country were Shamir, Jattir, Socoh, 49 Dannah, Kiriath-sannah (that is Debir), 50 Anab, Eshtemoh, Anim, 51 Goshen, Holon, and Giloh—eleven cities with their villages. 52 Arab, Rumah, Eshan, 53 Janim, Beth-tappuah, Aphekah, 54 Humtah, Kiriath-arba (that is Hebron), and Zior—nine cities with their villages.

55 Maon, Carmel, Ziph, Juttah, 56 Jezreel, Jokdeam, Zanoah, 57 Cain, Gibeah, and Timnah—ten cities with their villages. 58 Halhul, Beth-zur, Gedor, 59 Maarath, Beth-anoth, Eltekon—six cities with their villages. 60 Kiriath-baal (that is Kiriath-jearim) and Rabbah—two cities with their villages. 61 In the wilderness were Beth-arabah, Middin, Secacah, 62 Nibshan, the City of Salt, and En-gedi—six cities with their villages.

63 But Judah's sons could not drive out the Jebusites who lived in Jerusalem. So the Jebusites live there with Judah's sons to this day.
