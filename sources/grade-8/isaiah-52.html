Wake Up, Jerusalem

1 Awake, awake! Put on your strength, O Zion. Put on your beautiful clothes, O Jerusalem, the holy city. The uncircumcised and the unclean will not enter you anymore. 2 Shake off the dust. Rise up and sit enthroned, O Jerusalem. Loosen the chains from your neck, O captive Daughter of Zion.

God Promises Freedom

3 For this is what the LORD says: "You were sold for nothing, and you will be bought back without money." 4 For this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: "At first My people went down to Egypt to live there. Then Assyria treated them badly for no reason."

5 "And now what do I have here?" declares the LORD. "My people have been taken away for nothing. Their rulers cry out," declares the LORD, "and My name is insulted all day long." 6 So My people will know My name. In that day they will know that I am the One who says, 'Here I am.'

Good News for Zion

7 How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of the one who brings good news, who announces peace, who brings good news of happiness, who announces salvation, and who says to Zion, "Your God reigns!" 8 Listen! Your watchmen lift up their voices. Together they shout for joy, because they will see the LORD face to face when He returns to Zion.

9 Break into song together, you ruins of Jerusalem, for the LORD has comforted His people. He has set Jerusalem free. 10 The LORD has shown His holy power to all the nations, and all the ends of the earth will see the salvation of our God.

11 Leave, leave! Get out of there! Do not touch anything unclean. Get out from the middle of her. Be pure, you who carry the vessels of the LORD. 12 You will not leave in a hurry, and you will not run away, because the LORD will go before you, and the God of Israel will guard your back.

The Servant of God

13 Look, My Servant will succeed. He will be raised up and lifted high and greatly honored. 14 Just as many people were shocked at Him—His face was so damaged that He did not look like a man, and His form was not like that of other people— 15 so He will sprinkle many nations. Kings will close their mouths because of Him. They will see what they were not told about, and they will understand what they had not heard.
