A Song of Praise and Prayer for Help

1My heart is steady, O God. I will sing and make music with my whole self. 2Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake the dawn. 3I will praise you, the LORD, among the nations. I will sing praises to you among the peoples. 4For your love is great above the heavens, and your faithfulness reaches to the skies. 5Be lifted up, O God, above the heavens. Let your glory cover all the earth. 6Save your loved ones with your right hand. Answer me, so they may be rescued.

7God has spoken in His holiness: "I will triumph! I will divide Shechem and measure the Valley of Succoth. 8Gilead is Mine; Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is My helmet, and Judah is My scepter. 9Moab is My washbasin. Upon Edom I throw My sandal. Over Philistia I shout in triumph."

10Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? 11Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You not march out with our armies? 12Give us help against the enemy, for human help is useless. 13With God we will act with strength, and He will trample our enemies.
