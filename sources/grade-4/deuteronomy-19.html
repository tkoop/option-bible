Cities of Safety for People Who Kill by Accident

1 When the LORD your God destroys the nations whose land the LORD your God gives you, you will take their place and live in their cities and houses. 2 Set apart three cities for yourself in the middle of the land the LORD your God gives you to own. 3 Make the road ready for yourself, and divide the land the LORD your God gives you into three parts, so anyone who kills a person can run there.

4 This is the rule for the person who kills someone and runs there to stay alive: He killed his neighbor by accident, and he did not hate him before. 5 For example, a man goes into the woods with his neighbor to cut wood. He swings his ax to cut a tree. The iron part flies off the handle and hits his neighbor, and his neighbor dies. That man may run to one of these cities and live. 6 If he does not, the one who takes revenge for the killing may chase the killer. He may be angry and catch him, because the road is long. Then he may kill him, even though the killer did not deserve to die, because he did not hate his neighbor before. 7 That is why I command you: Set apart three cities for yourself.

8 If the LORD your God makes your land bigger, as He promised your fathers, and gives you all the land He said He would give your fathers, 9 and if you keep all these commands I give you today—to love the LORD your God and walk in His ways all your days—then add three more cities to these three. 10 Do this so no innocent person is killed in the land the LORD your God gives you as your share. Then you will not be guilty of that death.

Murderers Are Not Safe in These Cities

11 But if a man hates his neighbor, hides and waits for him, attacks and kills him, and then runs to one of these cities, 12 the leaders of his city must send men to bring him back from there. They must hand him over to the one who takes revenge for the killing, and he must die. 13 Do not feel sorry for him. Remove the guilt of an innocent person's death from Israel, so it will go well with you.

Do Not Move Your Neighbor's Land Boundary

14 Do not move your neighbor's boundary marker. It was set in the land you will receive as your share in the land the LORD your God gives you to own.

Witnesses and False Witnesses

15 One witness is not enough to prove a man guilty of any wrong or sin. A matter must be settled by two or three witnesses. 16 If a false witness stands up against a man and says he did wrong, 17 both men who have the argument must stand before the LORD, before the priests and judges who are serving then.

18 The judges must look into it carefully. If the witness is lying, and he lied about his brother, 19 do to him what he planned to do to his brother. Remove the evil from among you. 20 Then the rest will hear and be afraid. They will not do such an evil thing among you again. 21 Do not feel sorry. Life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
