John Sees God's Throne in Heaven

1 After these things, I saw a door open in heaven. The first voice I heard sounded like a trumpet. It spoke to me and said, "Come up here. I will show you what must happen after this."

2 Right away I was in the Spirit. I saw a throne in heaven, and One was sitting on it. 3 The One on the throne looked like jasper and a red stone. A rainbow shone around the throne. It looked like an emerald. 4 Around the throne were twenty-four seats. On them sat twenty-four elders. They wore white clothes. They had gold crowns on their heads.

5 Lightning, thunder, and voices came from the throne. Seven lamps of fire burned in front of the throne. They are the seven Spirits of God. 6 In front of the throne was a sea of glass like crystal. In the middle and around the throne were four living creatures. They were full of eyes in front and behind. 7 The first creature looked like a lion. The second looked like a calf. The third had a face like a man. The fourth looked like a flying eagle.

8 Each of the four creatures had six wings. They were full of eyes all around and inside. Day and night they do not stop saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, and is, and is to come."

The Elders Worship God

9 When the creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to the One sitting on the throne, who lives forever and ever, 10 the twenty-four elders fall down before Him. They worship the One who lives forever and ever. They throw their crowns in front of the throne. They say, 11 "You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory, honor, and power. You made all things. They are here because You wanted them to be."
