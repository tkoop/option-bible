Sing a New Song to the the LORD

1 Sing a new song to the LORD. All the earth, sing to the LORD. 2 Sing to the LORD. Praise His name. Tell everyone about His saving power each day. 3 Tell the nations about His glory. Tell all people about His amazing works.

The LORD Is Great

4 The LORD is very great. People should praise Him. He is to be feared more than all other gods. 5 All the gods of other people are just pretend gods. But the LORD made the heavens. 6 He is full of honor and greatness. His holy place has strength and beauty.

Give the the LORD Glory

7 All families of the nations, give the LORD glory and strength. 8 Give the LORD the honor His name deserves. Bring a gift and come to His temple. 9 Worship the LORD in holy beauty. All the earth, shake with fear before Him.

The LORD Rules the World

10 Tell the nations, "The LORD rules! The world is set in place. It will not be moved. He will judge all people fairly." 11 Let the sky be glad. Let the earth rejoice. Let the sea roar, and everything in it.

All Creation Praises Him

12 Let the fields be happy, and all that is in them. Then all the trees in the forest will sing for joy 13 before the LORD, because He is coming. He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with what is right. He will judge the people with His faithfulness.
