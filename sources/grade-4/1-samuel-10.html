Samuel Anoints Saul

1 Samuel took a jar of oil and poured it on Saul's head. He kissed him. Then he said, "The LORD has chosen you to lead His people.

2 When you leave me today, you will meet two men near Rachel's grave, at Zelzah in Benjamin. They will tell you the donkeys you went to find are found. They will say your father stopped worrying about them. Now he worries about you. He asks what he should do about his son.

3 Go on from there. You will come to the oak tree at Tabor. There you will meet three men going up to God at Bethel. One will carry three young goats. One will carry three loaves of bread. One will carry a jar of wine.

4 They will say hello to you. They will give you two loaves of bread. Take the bread from them.

5 After that, go to Gibeah, where God is worshiped. A Philistine camp is there. When you go into the town, you will meet a group of prophets coming down from the hill. They will have harps, tambourines, flutes, and lyres. They will be speaking for God.

6 The Spirit of the LORD will come on you in a strong way. You will speak for God with them. You will become like a new person.

7 When these things happen, do what you need to do. God is with you.

8 Go down ahead of me to Gilgal. I will come down to you. I will give offerings to God. Wait seven days until I come to you. Then I will tell you what to do.

9 As Saul turned to leave Samuel, God changed his heart. All these signs happened that day.

Saul Meets the Prophets

10 When Saul and his helper came to Gibeah, a group of prophets met him. The Spirit of God came on him in a strong way. He spoke for God with them.

11 People who knew Saul before saw him speaking for God with the prophets. They said to each other, "What has happened to Kish's son? Is Saul a prophet too?"

12 A man who lived there said, "Who is the father of these prophets?"

So people began to say, "Is Saul a prophet too?"

13 When Saul finished speaking for God, he went up to the hill.

Saul Tells His Uncle

14 Saul's uncle asked him and his helper, "Where have you been?"

Saul said, "We went to look for the donkeys. But we did not find them. So we went to see Samuel."

15 His uncle said, "Tell me, what did Samuel say to you?"

16 Saul said, "He told us the donkeys were found." But Saul did not tell his uncle what Samuel said about being king.

The People Choose a King

17 Then Samuel called all the people to come to the LORD at Mizpah.

18 He said to the people of Israel, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I brought you out of Egypt. I saved you from the Egyptians and from all the kings who hurt you."

19 "But today you turned away from your God. He saves you from all your troubles. You told Him you did not want Him. You asked for a king to rule you. Now stand before the LORD by your tribes and your family groups."

20 So Samuel brought all the tribes near. The tribe of Benjamin was chosen.

21 Then he brought the family groups of Benjamin near. The family group of Matri was chosen. Then Saul son of Kish was chosen. But when they looked for him, they could not find him.

22 So they asked the LORD, "Has the man come here?"

The LORD said, "Yes. He is hiding among the supplies."

23 They ran and brought him out. When he stood among the people, he was taller than everyone else.

24 Then Samuel said to all the people, "Do you see the man the LORD has chosen? There is no one like him among all the people."

Then all the people shouted, "Long live the king!"

25 Then Samuel told the people what a king should do. He wrote it in a book. He put the book before the LORD. Then he sent all the people home.

Saul Goes Home

26 Saul went home to Gibeah too. Some brave men went with him. God had touched their hearts.

Some People Do Not Like Saul

27 But some bad men said, "How can this man save us?" They did not like him. They did not bring him a gift. But Saul did not say anything.
