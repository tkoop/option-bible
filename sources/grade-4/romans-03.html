What Good Is It to Be a Jew?

1 What good is it to be a Jew? What good is being circumcised? 2 Much in every way! First, God gave the Jews His own words.

God Is Always True

3 What if some Jews did not believe God? Does that make God not keep His promise? 4 No! God is always true, even if every person lies. The Bible says, “You are right when You speak. You win when You are judged.”

5 But if our bad ways show that God is good, what can we say? Is God wrong to be angry and punish? I am talking like a person. 6 No! If that were true, how could God judge the world?

7 But someone may say, “If my lie makes God's truth look great and brings Him praise, why am I still judged as a sinner?” 8 Why not say, as some people wrongly say we say, “Let us do bad so good can come”? They are wrong, and they deserve to be judged.

No One Is Right with God

9 So what? Are we better than others? No! We already said that all people, Jews and non-Jews, are trapped by sin. 10 The Bible says, “No one is right with God, not even one. 11 No one understands. No one looks for God. 12 All have turned away. They have all become useless. No one does good, not even one. 13 Their throats are like open graves. Their tongues tell lies. Snake poison is on their lips. 14 Their mouths are full of curses and bitter words. 15 Their feet run fast to hurt people. 16 They leave ruin and pain wherever they go. 17 They do not know the way of peace. 18 They do not fear God.”

19 We know that God's law speaks to those under the law. It stops every mouth. It shows that the whole world is guilty before God. 20 So no one will be made right with God by doing what the law says. The law shows us our sin.

Made Right with God by Faith

21 But now God's way to be right with Him is shown without the law. The Law and the Prophets tell about it. 22 God makes people right with Him when they trust Jesus Christ. This is for everyone who believes. There is no difference between people. 23 For all people have sinned. All fall short of God's greatness. 24 But God makes them right with Him for free. He does this by His kindness. He sets them free through Christ Jesus.

25 God sent Jesus as the way to take away our sins. His blood paid for them. We receive this by faith. God did this to show He is right. In the past, God was patient and did not punish sins. 26 He did this to show He is right now. He is fair, and He makes right anyone who trusts Jesus.

27 So can anyone brag? No. Not because of a rule about doing good works. It is because of faith. 28 We believe that a person is made right with God by faith, not by doing what the law says.

29 Is God only the God of the Jews? Is He not also the God of other people? Yes, He is the God of other people too. 30 There is only one God. He will make Jews right with Him by faith. He will also make non-Jews right with Him by faith. 31 So do we throw away God's law because of faith? No! Instead, we show that the law matters.
