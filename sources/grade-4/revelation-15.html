Seven Angels with the Last Troubles

1 I saw another big and amazing sign in heaven. Seven angels had the seven last troubles. God's anger was finished in them.

The Sea Made of Glass

2 I saw something like a sea made of glass mixed with fire. People who had won against the beast, his statue, and the number of his name stood on the glassy sea. They had God's harps.

The Song of Moses and the Lamb

3 They sang the song of Moses, God's servant, and the song of the Lamb. They said, "Your works are great and amazing, Lord God Almighty. Your ways are right and true, King of all nations. 4 Who will not be afraid of You, Lord, and praise Your name? You alone are holy. All nations will come and worship You, because Your right acts have been shown."

The Temple in Heaven Opens

5 After this I looked, and the temple of the tent of witness in heaven opened. 6 Seven angels came out of the temple. They had the seven troubles. They wore clean, bright linen and gold belts around their chests.

7 One of the four living beings gave the seven angels seven gold bowls. The bowls were full of God's anger. God lives forever and ever. 8 The temple filled with smoke from God's glory and His power. No one could go in until the seven troubles of the seven angels were done.
