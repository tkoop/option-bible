God's Message About Ammon

1 This is what the LORD says about the Ammonites: "Does Israel have no children? Does Israel have no one to take his place? Then why did Milcom take Gad? Why do his people live in Gad's towns? 2 So listen. The days are coming. I will make a loud battle cry against Rabbah, the main city of Ammon. It will become a pile of ruins. Its small towns will burn. Then Israel will take back what was taken from him. 3 Cry out, Heshbon, because Ai is ruined! Cry out, women of Rabbah! Put on rough cloth and be sad. Run along the walls. Milcom will be carried away, and his priests and leaders will go with him."

4 "Why do you brag about your valleys? You turn away, but you trust your stored treasures. You say, 'Who can come against me?' 5 I will bring fear on you. Fear will come from all around you. You will be pushed out. Each person will run straight ahead. No one will gather the ones who run away," says the Lord the LORD of Armies. "But later I will bring the Ammonites back from being captives." 6

God's Message About Edom

7 This is what the LORD of Armies says about Edom: "Is there no wisdom in Teman? Has good advice died? Has their wisdom gone? 8 Turn and run! Live deep in the caves, people of Dedan. I am bringing trouble on Esau. It is time to punish him. 9 If grape pickers came, would they leave a few grapes? If thieves came at night, would they take only what they need? 10 But I will strip Esau bare. I will uncover his hiding places. He cannot hide. His children, his brothers, and his neighbors will be ruined. Then he will be no more. 11 Leave your orphans. I will keep them alive. Let your widows trust in Me."

12 "This is what the LORD says: Even those who should not have to drink the cup must drink it. You will not go free. You will drink it. 13 I have sworn by Myself that Bozrah will become a ruined place. People will laugh at it. It will be empty and cursed. All its towns will be ruins forever. 14 I have heard a message from the LORD. A messenger is sent to the nations: Gather together and attack Edom! Get ready for war! 15 I will make you small among the nations. People will look down on you. 16 Your pride has tricked you. You live in the cracks of the rocks. You hold the high hill. But even if you build your nest high like an eagle, I will bring you down."

17 "Edom will be a ruined place. Everyone who passes by will be shocked. They will whistle at all its wounds. 18 Edom will be like Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns near them. No one will live there. No person will stay there. 19 Like a lion coming from the bushes by the Jordan to a green field, I will chase Edom away fast. Then I will choose a young leader for it. Who is like Me? Who can stand against Me? What shepherd can stand before Me? 20 So listen to the LORD's plan for Edom. Listen to what He plans for the people of Teman. Even the little sheep will be dragged away. Their home will be destroyed. 21 At the sound of their fall the earth will shake. Their cry will be heard at the Red Sea. 22 Look! Someone will fly up like an eagle and swoop down. He will spread his wings over Bozrah. On that day, the hearts of Edom's warriors will be like the heart of a woman in pain."

God's Message About Damascus

23 "Hamath and Arpad are ashamed. They heard bad news and melted with fear. They are worried like the sea that cannot be quiet. 24 Damascus has become weak. It turned to run. Fear has taken hold. Pain and sorrow have grabbed it, like a woman having a baby. 25 How can the city that was praised be left alone? It was the city I loved. 26 So its young men will fall in its streets. All the soldiers will be silent on that day. 27 I will set fire to the wall of Damascus. It will burn the palaces of Ben-Hadad," says the LORD of Armies.

God's Message About Kedar and Hazor

28 This is what the LORD says: "Get up and attack Kedar. Destroy the people of the east. 29 They will take their tents and sheep. They will take their curtains and all their things. They will take their camels. They will shout, 'Fear is all around!' 30 Run away! Get far away! Live deep in the caves, people of Hazor," says the LORD. "The king of Babylon has made a plan against you. He has thought of a plan. 31 Get up and attack a nation that feels safe. It lives alone. It has no gates and no bars. It lives by itself. 32 Their camels will be taken. Their many animals will be taken. I will scatter them to the wind. I will bring trouble from every side. 33 Hazor will be a home for wild dogs. It will be empty forever. No one will live there. No person will stay there."

God's Message About Elam

34 This is the LORD's message to Jeremiah the prophet about Elam. It came at the start of Zedekiah's rule over Judah. 35 This is what the LORD of Armies says: "I will break the bow of Elam. It is the best of their power. 36 I will bring the four winds from the four corners of heaven against Elam. I will scatter them to all these winds. People from Elam will go to every nation. 37 I will make Elam afraid before their enemies and before those who want to kill them. I will bring trouble on them. My anger will burn. I will send the sword after them until I have destroyed them. 38 I will set up My throne in Elam. I will destroy the king and leaders there. 39 But in the last days, I will bring Elam back from being captives," says the LORD.
