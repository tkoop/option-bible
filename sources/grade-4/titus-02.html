Teach What Is Right

1 But you should talk about things that fit good teaching.

How Older People Should Live

2 Older men should be sober, respected, sensible, and strong in faith, love, and patience.

3 Older women should act in a holy way. They should not say bad things about others. They should not drink too much wine. They should teach what is good.

How Young People Should Live

4 Then they can teach younger women to love their husbands and children. 5 They should be sensible, pure, and good at home. They should obey their own husbands. Then people will not say bad things about God's message.

6 In the same way, tell young men to control themselves.

7 Show yourself as a good example in everything. Teach with honesty and respect. 8 Use words that are healthy and cannot be blamed. Then people who are against us will be ashamed. They will have nothing bad to say about us.

How Servants Should Live

9 Slaves should obey their own masters in everything. They should try to please them. They should not talk back. 10 They should not steal. They should show they can be trusted. Then they make God's teaching look good. God is our Savior.

God's Grace Helps Us

11 God's grace has come to save all people. 12 It teaches us to say no to sin and to want things of this world. It teaches us to live with self-control, to do right, and to honor God now. 13 We wait for the happy hope and for our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, to come in glory. 14 He gave Himself for us. He did this to set us free from all sin. He made us clean. He made us His own people. He wants us to be eager to do good.

Keep Teaching These Things

15 Talk about these things. Encourage people. Correct people with full authority. Do not let anyone look down on you.
