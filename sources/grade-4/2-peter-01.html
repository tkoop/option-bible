Peter Writes to God's People

1 Simon Peter, a servant and messenger of Jesus Christ. To those who got a faith as special as ours. It came through the right way of our God and Savior, Jesus Christ. 2 May God give you more and more grace and peace as you know God and Jesus our Lord.

God Gives Us All We Need

3 His power has given us everything we need for life and living for God. We get it through knowing Him who called us by His own glory and goodness. 4 Through these, He gave us great and very special promises. Through them, you can share in God's own life. You can also get away from the bad wants in the world that ruin people.

Grow in Good Things

5 So do your best to add goodness to your faith. Then add knowing to your goodness. 6 Add self-control to your knowing. Add patience to your self-control. Add living for God to your patience. 7 Add love for brothers and sisters to your living for God. Add love to that love. 8 If you have these things and they grow, you will not be lazy or useless in knowing our Lord Jesus Christ. 9 But anyone who does not have them is blind. He can see only what is close. He forgot that God washed away his old sins.

Make Your Calling Sure

10 So, brothers and sisters, work hard to make sure God called you and chose you. If you do these things, you will never fall. 11 Then God will richly welcome you into the kingdom that lasts forever. That is the kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Peter's Reminder

12 So I will always remind you about these things. You already know them. You are strong in the truth you have. 13 I think it is right to wake you up with this reminder while I am still in this body. 14 I know I will soon leave this body behind. Our Lord Jesus Christ showed me this. 15 I will work hard so that after I leave, you will always remember these things.

We Saw Jesus' Glory

16 We did not follow made-up stories when we told you about the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. We saw His glory with our own eyes. 17 God the Father gave Him honor and glory. A voice came to Him from the great and glorious God.

"This is My Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with Him."

18 We heard this voice from heaven. We were with Him on the holy mountain.

Trust the Prophets' Words

19 We also have the prophets' words. They are even more sure. You are right to pay attention to them. They are like a lamp shining in a dark place. They shine until the day comes and light shines in your hearts. 20 First, know this: no message from God in the Bible came from a person's own idea. 21 For no message from God ever came by a person's want. Instead, people spoke from God as they were moved by the Holy Spirit.
