Pashhur Punishes Jeremiah

1Pashhur was a priest. He was Immer's son. He was in charge of the LORD's house. He heard Jeremiah say these things. 2He had Jeremiah the prophet beaten. Then he put him in the stocks. This was at the Upper Gate of Benjamin, at the LORD's house.

Jeremiah Speaks to Pashhur

3The next day, Pashhur let Jeremiah out of the stocks. Jeremiah said to him, "The LORD does not call you Pashhur. He calls you Fear All Around. 4This is what the LORD says: 'I will make you a fear to yourself and to all your friends. With your own eyes, you will see them fall by the sword of their enemies. I will give all of Judah to the king of Babylon. He will take them to Babylon or kill them with the sword. 5I will give all the wealth of this city to its enemies. I will give its goods and its valuable things. I will give all the treasures of the kings of Judah. The enemies will take them and carry them to Babylon. 6And you, Pashhur, and all who live in your house, will go away as captives. You will go to Babylon. There you will die and be buried. So will all your friends. You told them lies.'"

Jeremiah Complains to the LORD

7O the LORD, You tricked me, and I was tricked. You are stronger than me, and You won. All day long, people laugh at me. Everyone makes fun of me. 8Every time I speak, I cry out. I say, "There is trouble and ruin!" So the LORD's word brings me shame and mean words all day long. 9But if I say, "I will not say His name or speak for Him anymore," His word is like a fire burning in my heart. It is shut up in my bones. I am tired of holding it in. I cannot keep it in.

10I hear many people whisper, "Fear All Around! Tell on him! Let's tell on him!" All my close friends watch for me to fall.

They say, "Maybe he will be fooled. Then we can beat him and pay him back."

11But the LORD is with me like a strong soldier. So the people who chase me will trip and not win. They will fail and be very ashamed. People will never forget their shame. 12The LORD of All, You test good people. You look at hearts and minds. Let me see You pay them back. I have given my case to You. 13Sing to the LORD! Praise the LORD! He saves the life of a person in need from bad people.

Jeremiah Curses His Birth

14I curse the day I was born! May no one bless the day my mother had me! 15I curse the man who told my father, "A boy is born to you!" He made my father very happy. 16May that man be like the towns the LORD destroyed without pity. May he hear crying in the morning and a war cry at noon. 17I was not killed before I was born. So my mother's womb became my grave, and it stayed full forever. 18Why was I born? I only see trouble and sadness. My days end in shame.
