A Sad Song for Israel's Leaders

1 You, sing a sad song for the leaders of Israel.

The Mother Lion

2 Say, "What was your mother like? She was like a lioness among lions. She lay down with young lions and raised her cubs."

The First Young Lion

3 One of her cubs grew into a young lion. He learned to hunt. He attacked people. 4 Other countries heard about him. They caught him in a trap. They dragged him with hooks to Egypt.

The Second Young Lion

5 When she saw her hope was gone, she took another cub and raised him to be a young lion. 6 He walked among lions. He became a young lion. He learned to hunt. He attacked people. 7 He broke their strong places and ruined their cities. The land and everything in it shook at his roar. 8 Countries from all around came against him. They spread a net over him. They caught him in a trap. 9 They put him in a cage with hooks and took him to the king of Babylon. They kept him in prison so he could never roar again in the hills of Israel.

The Vine

10 Your mother was like a grapevine planted by the water. It grew well and had many branches. 11 Its branches were strong enough to be rulers' staffs. It grew tall among the thick branches. People saw how tall and full it was. 12 Then it was pulled up in anger and thrown to the ground. A hot east wind dried its fruit. Its strong branch broke and dried up. Fire burned it up. 13 Now it is planted in a dry and empty land. 14 Fire came from its own branch and burned its fruit. Now there is no strong branch to be a ruler's staff. This is a sad song, and people will sing it as a sad song.
