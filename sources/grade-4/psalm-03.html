David Runs from His Son Absalom

1 The LORD, how many enemies I have! Many people are rising up against me.

2 Many say, "God won't save him."

David Trusts God

3 But you, the LORD, are a shield around me. You are my honor, and You lift me up.

4 I call to the LORD, and He answers me from His holy hill.

5 I lie down and sleep. I wake up again, because the LORD keeps me safe. 6 I will not be afraid of thousands of people all around me.

David Asks for Help

7 Rise up, the LORD! Save me, my God! You hit all my enemies on the cheek. You break the teeth of bad people.

8 The LORD saves people. Please bless Your people.
