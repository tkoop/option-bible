Job Talks Back to Eliphaz

1 Job answered and said, 2 "If only my sadness could be weighed, and all my trouble put on a scale together! 3 It would be heavier than the sand of the sea. That is why my words have been rough. 4 The arrows of the Almighty are in me. Their poison fills my spirit. God's terrors line up against me. 5 Does a wild donkey cry out when it has grass? Does an ox make noise when it has food? 6 Is tasteless food eaten without salt? Is there flavor in the white of an egg? 7 I do not want them. They make me sick."

Job Wants God to End His Pain

8 "I wish I could have what I ask. I wish God would give me what I hope for. 9 I wish God would be pleased to crush me, to let go His hand and cut me off. 10 Then I would still have comfort. I would be glad in pain without holding back, because I have not hidden the words of the Holy One. 11 What strength do I have to keep going? What is left for me to wait for? 12 Is my strength like stone? Is my body made of bronze? 13 I have no help in myself. Good sense has left me."

Job Says His Friends Are Like Dry Streams

14 "A hurting person should get kindness from a friend, or he may turn away from fearing the Almighty. 15 But my brothers have been untrue, like a stream that dries up. They pass by like rushing streams. 16 They are dark with ice. Snow hides on them. 17 When it gets warm, they melt away and disappear from their place. 18 Travelers turn off their path. They go into empty land and die. 19 The travelers from Tema look. The travelers from Sheba hope for them. 20 They are ashamed because they trusted. They come and are disappointed."

Job Asks His Friends to Listen

21 "Now you have become nothing. You see trouble and are afraid. 22 Did I ever say, 'Give me something'? Did I say, 'Pay for me with your money'? 23 Did I ask you to save me from an enemy? Did I ask you to buy me back from cruel men? 24 Teach me, and I will be quiet. Show me where I was wrong. 25 Honest words can hurt, but what does your arguing show? 26 Do you think you can fix words? The words of a sad man are like wind. 27 You would even throw dice for an orphan. You would sell your friend. 28 But now please look at me. I will not lie to your face. 29 Please turn back. Let there be no wrong. Turn again, and my good name is still there. 30 Is there wrong on my tongue? Can my mouth not tell trouble?"
