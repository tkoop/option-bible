God's Agreement with His People

1 This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 2 The LORD said, "Listen to the words of this agreement. Tell them to the men of Judah and to the people who live in Jerusalem. 3 Tell them that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Bad things will happen to the person who does not obey the words of this agreement. 4 I gave this command to your fathers when I brought them out of Egypt. That place was like a hot iron furnace. I said, "Obey Me. Do everything I tell you to do. Then you will be My people, and I will be your God." 5 I made this promise to your fathers. I promised to give them a land with plenty of milk and honey. That is still true today.'"

Then I answered, "Amen, the LORD."

6 Then the LORD said to me, "Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Say, 'Listen to the words of this agreement and obey them.' 7 From the time I brought your fathers out of Egypt until today, I have warned them again and again. I said, 'Obey My voice.' 8 But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. Each person followed his own stubborn and evil heart. So I brought on them all the curses of this agreement that I commanded them to keep, but they did not keep them."

The People Break the Agreement

9 The LORD said to me, "The men of Judah and the people who live in Jerusalem have made a secret plan against Me. 10 They have gone back to the sins of their fathers. Their fathers refused to listen to My words. These people followed other gods and served them. The family of Israel and the family of Judah have broken the agreement I made with their fathers."

11 So the LORD says, "I am going to bring trouble on them. They will not be able to escape it. They will cry out to Me, but I will not listen to them. 12 Then the cities of Judah and the people who live in Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods they burn spices to. But those gods will not save them when trouble comes. 13 Judah, you have as many gods as you have cities. You people have set up as many altars to burn spices to Baal as Jerusalem has streets."

14 The LORD said to me, "Do not pray for these people. Do not cry out or pray for them, because I will not listen when they cry out to Me in their time of trouble."

15 The LORD says, "Why should My dear people be in My house? They have done many evil things. Can holy meat keep trouble away from you? You do evil, and then you are glad."

16 The LORD once called you a green olive tree. It was beautiful and full of fruit. But now, with a loud roar, He will set fire to it. Its branches will burn up. 17 The LORD of Heaven's Armies planted you. But He has said He will bring trouble on you. This is because of the evil of the families of Israel and Judah. They made Him angry by burning spices to Baal.

18 The LORD told me what was happening, so I knew. Then He showed me what they were doing.

A Plot Against Jeremiah

"19 I was like a gentle lamb being led to be killed. I did not know they had made plans against me. They said they would destroy me like a tree with its fruit. They said they would cut me off from the land of the living, so no one would remember my name. 20 But the LORD of Heaven's Armies judges fairly. He tests people's hearts and minds. The LORD, let me see You punish them, because I have trusted You with my case."

21 So the LORD says this about the men of Anathoth who are trying to kill you: They say you must not prophesy in the LORD's name, or you will die by their hands. 22 So the LORD of Heaven's Armies says, "I will punish them. Their young men will die by the sword. Their sons and daughters will die from hunger. 23 No one will be left among them. I will bring trouble on the men of Anathoth in the year I punish them."
