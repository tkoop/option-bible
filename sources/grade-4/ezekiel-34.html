The LORD Gives Ezekiel a Message

1 The LORD gave me a message.

The LORD Speaks Against the Shepherds

2 "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy and say to them: This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: How terrible for the shepherds of Israel who only take care of themselves! Should not shepherds take care of the flock? 3 You eat the best food. You wear the wool. You kill the fat animals. But you do not feed the flock. 4 You have not helped the weak. You have not healed the sick. You have not tied up the hurt ones. You have not brought back the lost ones. You have not looked for the missing ones. You have ruled them with force and cruelty.

5 "So they were scattered because there was no shepherd. They became food for all the wild animals. 6 My sheep wandered on all the mountains and on every high hill. They were scattered all over the earth. No one looked for them. No one searched for them.

7 "So, you shepherds, listen to the word of the LORD: 8 As surely as I live, says the Lord, the LORD, My sheep became food for wild animals because no one led them. My shepherds did not look for them. They fed only themselves. They did not feed My sheep. 9 So, you shepherds, listen to the word of the LORD. 10 This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I am against the shepherds. I will take My sheep back from them. I will stop them from tending the flock, so they will not feed themselves anymore. I will rescue My sheep from their mouths. They will not be food for them anymore.

The LORD Will Care for His Sheep

11 "For this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I Myself will search for My sheep and look after them. 12 As a shepherd looks after his scattered flock when he is with them, so will I look after My sheep. I will rescue them from all the places where they were scattered on a cloudy and dark day. 13 I will bring them out from the nations. I will gather them from the countries. I will bring them into their own land. I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, in the valleys, and in all the places where people live. 14 I will feed them in a good pasture. The high mountains of Israel will be their grazing land. There they will lie down in good grazing land. They will eat rich food on the mountains of Israel. 15 I Myself will feed My sheep and make them lie down, says the Lord, the LORD. 16 I will look for the lost ones and bring back the strays. I will tie up the hurt ones and make the weak ones strong. But I will destroy the fat and strong ones. I will shepherd them with fairness.

The LORD Will Judge the Sheep

17 "As for you, My flock, this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I will judge between sheep and sheep, between rams and goats. 18 Is it not enough for you to eat the good grass? Must you also trample the rest of the grass with your feet? Is it not enough for you to drink clean water? Must you also muddy the rest with your feet? 19 My sheep must eat what you have trampled. They must drink what you have muddied with your feet.

The LORD Will Judge the Strong Sheep

20 "So this is what the Lord, the LORD, says to them: I Myself will judge between the fat sheep and the thin sheep. 21 Because you push with your side and shoulder. You hit the weak sheep with your horns until you scatter them away. 22 I will save My flock. They will no longer be prey. I will judge between one sheep and another.

The LORD Will Send One Shepherd

23 "I will place one shepherd over them. He will be My servant David. He will feed them. He will be their shepherd. 24 I, the LORD, will be their God. My servant David will be a leader among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.

The LORD Promises Peace and Blessing

25 "I will make an agreement of peace with them. I will remove dangerous animals from the land. Then they will live safely in the desert. They will sleep in the woods. 26 I will bless them and the places around My hill. I will send rain at the right time. The rains will be a blessing. 27 The trees will give fruit. The land will give crops. They will be safe in their land. They will know that I am the LORD when I break the heavy bars from their necks and save them from those who made them slaves. 28 They will no longer be prey for the nations. Wild animals will not eat them. They will live in safety, and no one will make them afraid. 29 I will give them a famous garden. They will not starve in the land. They will not be shamed by the nations anymore. 30 Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them. They are the people of Israel, My people, says the Lord, the LORD. 31 You are My sheep. You are the sheep I feed. You are My people, and I am your God, says the Lord, the LORD."
