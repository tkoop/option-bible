God Warns the City of Ariel

1 Oh no, Ariel! Ariel is the city where David stayed. Count year after year. Let the feasts come back. 2 But I will bring trouble to Ariel. She will cry and be sad. To Me, she will be like a fire on an altar.

3 I will camp all around you. I will put towers around you. I will build walls to trap you. 4 You will be brought low. You will speak from the ground. Your words will come out like a whisper from the dust. Your voice will sound like a spirit from the ground. Your words will chirp from the dust.

5 But your many enemies will be like fine dust. The cruel crowd will be like straw that blows away. It will happen fast, in a moment. 6 The LORD of Armies will visit you with thunder and a big shake of the ground. There will be loud noise, a storm, and a wind. Fire will burn everything.

7 Then the crowd of all the nations that fight Ariel will be like a dream at night. All who attack her, her fort, and trouble her will be like a night dream. 8 It will be like a hungry man who dreams he is eating, but wakes up and is still hungry. It will be like a thirsty man who dreams he is drinking, but wakes up and is tired, and his soul still wants water. So it will be for all the nations that fight Mount Zion.

God Makes the People Sleep

9 Be amazed and shocked. Make yourselves blind and unable to see. Be drunk, but not with wine. Stagger, but not with strong drink.

10 For the LORD has poured out on you a spirit of deep sleep. He has shut your eyes—the prophets. He has covered your heads—the prophets who see visions.

11 For you, this whole vision is like words in a sealed book. If someone who can read gets it, he says, "I cannot read it, because it is sealed."

12 If someone who cannot read gets it, he says, "I do not know how to read."

13 Then the LORD said, "Because these people come near Me with their mouths and honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far from Me. Their fear of Me is only rules that men taught. 14 So I will do something amazing again to these people. I will do one wonder after another. The wise men's wisdom will die. The smart men's understanding will be hidden."

People Try to Hide Their Plans

15 Oh no to those who go deep to hide their plans from the LORD. They do their work in the dark. They say, "Who sees us? Who knows us?"

16 You have turned things upside down. It is like the pot maker is counted as clay.

Can the pot say to the pot maker, "You did not make me"? Can the jar say to the pot maker, "He does not understand"?

God Will Make Things New

17 In a very short time, Lebanon will become good farm land. Good farm land will be like a forest. 18 On that day, deaf people will hear the words of the book. Blind people's eyes will see out of the dark and gloom. 19 The humble will have more joy in the LORD. The poor will be glad in the Holy One of Israel.

20 The cruel will be gone. The mockers will end. All who look for evil will be stopped. 21 They make a person sin with words. They trap the one who speaks for what is right at the gate. They take away justice from an innocent person with empty charges.

God Will Bless Jacob's Family

22 So the LORD, who saved Abraham, says, "Jacob will not be ashamed anymore. His face will not turn pale anymore. 23 For when he sees his children, the work of My hands among them, they will honor My name. They will honor the Holy One of Jacob. They will stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24 Then people who go the wrong way in spirit will learn. People who complain will learn the lesson."
