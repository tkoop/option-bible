Paul Speaks to the Crowd

1 "Brothers and fathers, please listen to me now. I want to speak for myself to you. 2 When the people heard him speak in Hebrew, they became very quiet. Then Paul said,

3 "I am a Jew. I was born in Tarsus in Cilicia. But I grew up here in this city. I learned from Gamaliel. I followed our fathers' law very carefully. I was very eager for God, just like all of you are today. 4 I hurt people who followed the Way. I had men and women put in jail. I kept doing it until some died. 5 The high priest and all the elders can tell you this is true. They gave me letters to take to our Jewish brothers in Damascus. I was going there to bring back the followers of Jesus who were in chains. I would take them to Jerusalem to be punished.

6 "About noon, I was on my way to Damascus. Suddenly a bright light from heaven shone all around me. 7 I fell to the ground. Then I heard a voice say, 'Saul, Saul, why are you hurting Me?' 8 I asked, 'Who are You, Lord?' He said, 'I am Jesus from Nazareth. You are hurting Me.' 9 The men with me saw the light. But they did not hear the voice of the One who spoke to me. 10 I said, 'Lord, what should I do?' The Lord said, 'Get up and go to Damascus. There you will be told all the things you must do.' 11 I could not see because the light was so bright. So the men with me led me by the hand into Damascus.

12 "A man named Ananias lived there. He obeyed God's law and was respected by all the Jews who lived there. 13 He came to me and stood by me. He said, 'Brother Saul, see again.' Right then I could see him. 14 Then he said, 'The God of our fathers chose you to know His will. He chose you to see the Righteous One and hear His voice.' 15 You will tell all people what you have seen and heard. 16 And now, why wait? Get up, be baptized, and wash away your sins. Call on His name.

17 "Later, I came back to Jerusalem. While I was praying in the temple, I had a vision. 18 I saw the Lord. He said, 'Hurry! Leave Jerusalem fast. The people here will not listen to your words about Me.' 19 I said, 'Lord, they know I used to put people in jail. I beat people in every synagogue who believed in You.' 20 When Stephen, who told the truth about You, was killed, I was there. I agreed with it. I watched the coats of the men who killed him. 21 Then the Lord said to me, 'Go! I will send you far away to the nations.'"

The Crowd Turns Angry

22 The people listened until Paul said this. Then they shouted, "Take this man away! He should not live!" 23 They yelled and threw off their coats. They threw dust into the air.

The Roman Commander Steps In

24 The commander ordered Paul to be brought into the fort. He said Paul should be whipped to find out why the people were shouting at him. 25 As they tied Paul up to whip him, Paul said to the officer standing there, "Is it legal for you to whip a Roman citizen who has not been found guilty?"

26 When the officer heard this, he went to the commander. He said, "What are you going to do? This man is a Roman citizen." 27 The commander came to Paul. He asked, "Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?" Paul said, "Yes." 28 The commander said, "I paid a lot of money for my citizenship." Paul said, "But I was born a citizen."

29 Right away, the men who were going to question Paul stepped back. The commander was afraid because he had learned Paul was a Roman citizen. He had tied him up.

Paul Before the Jewish Leaders

30 The next day, the commander wanted to know for sure why the Jews were accusing Paul. So he set Paul free. He called the chief priests and all the council to meet. Then he brought Paul down and stood him in front of them.
