Priests Are Split into Groups

1 These were the groups of Aaron's sons. Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 2 Nadab and Abihu died before their father. They had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests. 3 David split them into groups with Zadok from Eleazar's family and Ahimelech from Ithamar's family. They did set jobs. 4 More leaders came from Eleazar's family than from Ithamar's. Eleazar's family had sixteen leaders. Ithamar's family had eight leaders. 5 They picked the groups by a random draw. Both families had leaders for God's holy work. 6 Shemaiah son of Nethanel, a Levite and writer, wrote their names before the king, the leaders, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the family leaders of the priests and Levites. One family group was chosen for Eleazar, and one for Ithamar.

The 24 Priest Groups

7 The first group was Jehoiarib. The second was Jedaiah. 8 The third was Harim. The fourth was Seorim. 9 The fifth was Malchijah. The sixth was Mijamin. 10 The seventh was Hakkoz. The eighth was Abijah. 11 The ninth was Jeshua. The tenth was Shecaniah. 12 The eleventh was Eliashib. The twelfth was Jakim. 13 The thirteenth was Huppah. The fourteenth was Jeshebeab. 14 The fifteenth was Bilgah. The sixteenth was Immer. 15 The seventeenth was Hezir. The eighteenth was Happizzez. 16 The nineteenth was Pethahiah. The twentieth was Ezekiel. 17 The twenty-first was Jachin. The twenty-second was Gamul. 18 The twenty-third was Delaiah. The twenty-fourth was Maaziah.

19 These were their work times. They went to the house of the the LORD to serve, as the rules said. Aaron had set this. The The LORD God of Israel had told him to do it.

Other Levites Are Listed

20 The rest of Levi's family was listed too. From Amram's family came Shubael. From Shubael's family came Jehdeiah. 21 From Rehabiah's family, the leader was Isshiah. 22 From the Izhar family came Shelomoth. From Shelomoth's family came Jahath. 23 Hebron's sons were Jeriah first, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth. 24 From Uzziel's family came Micah. From Micah's family came Shamir. 25 Micah's brother was Isshiah. From Isshiah's family came Zechariah. 26 Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi. Jaaziah's son was Beno. 27 From Merari's family through Jaaziah came Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri. 28 Mahli had a son named Eleazar, but Eleazar had no sons. 29 Kish's son was Jerahmeel. 30 Mushi's sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth. These were the Levite families.

Levites Draw Lots Too

31 They drew lots too, just like Aaron's sons. King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the family leaders of the priests and Levites were there. The older family group and the younger group were treated the same.
