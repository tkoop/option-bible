Josiah Becomes King

1 Josiah was 8 years old when he became king. He ruled 31 years in Jerusalem. 2 He did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD. He followed the ways of his father David. He did not turn right or left.

Josiah Cleans the Land

3 In the eighth year of his rule, while he was still young, he began to seek the God of his father David. In the twelfth year, he began to clean Judah and Jerusalem. He removed hill altars, Asherah poles, carved idols, and metal idols. 4 He had the altars of Baal torn down. He cut down the incense altars above them. He broke the Asherah poles, carved idols, and metal idols. He ground them to dust and scattered the dust on the graves of those who had sacrificed to them. 5 He burned the bones of the priests on their altars. He cleaned Judah and Jerusalem. 6 He did the same in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, Simeon, and Naphtali, and in the ruins around them. 7 He tore down the altars and Asherah poles. He smashed the idols to dust. He cut down all the incense altars in all Israel. Then he went back to Jerusalem.

The Temple Is Fixed

8 In the eighteenth year of his rule, when he was cleaning the land and the temple, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the city leader, and Joah son of Joahaz the record keeper to fix the temple of the the LORD his God. 9 They came to Hilkiah the high priest. They gave him the money brought into God's house. The Levites who guarded the door had collected it from Manasseh, Ephraim, all the rest of Israel, all Judah and Benjamin, and the people of Jerusalem. 10 They gave it to the men in charge of the work on the the LORD's house. The workers used it to fix and make the temple strong. 11 They gave money to the carpenters and builders. They bought cut stone and wood to fix the buildings that the kings of Judah had let fall apart. 12 The men did the work well. The Levites in charge were Jahath and Obadiah from Merari's family, and Zechariah and Meshullam from Kohath's family. The Levites were skilled with music. 13 They led the carriers and all the workers. Some Levites were writers, officers, and gatekeepers.

The Book of the Law Is Found

14 When they brought out the money for the the LORD's house, Hilkiah the priest found the Book of the Law of the the LORD given by Moses. 15 Hilkiah said to Shaphan the writer, "I found the Book of the Law in the the LORD's house." Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan. 16 Shaphan brought the book to the king. He said, "Your servants are doing all they were told. They melted down the money found in the the LORD's house and gave it to the men in charge of the work." 17 They melted down the money found in the the LORD's house. They gave it to the men in charge and to the workers. 18 Shaphan the writer told the king, "Hilkiah the priest gave me a book." Then Shaphan read from it before the king. 19 When the king heard the words of the Law, he tore his clothes in sorrow.

20 The king told Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the writer, and Asaiah the king's servant, 21 "Go and ask the the LORD for me. Ask for the people still left in Israel and Judah. Ask about the words in this book that was found. The the LORD is very angry with us. He has poured out His anger on us because our fathers did not obey the the LORD's word. They did not do all that is written in this book."

The Prophet Huldah Speaks

22 Hilkiah and the men the king sent went to Huldah the woman prophet. She was the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath, son of Hasrah, who took care of the clothes. She lived in Jerusalem in the Second District. They spoke to her.

23 She said to them, "The The LORD, the God of Israel, says: Tell the man who sent you to me, 24 The The LORD says: I will bring trouble on this place and on the people who live here. I will bring all the curses written in the book they read to the king of Judah. 25 They left Me. They burned incense to other gods. They made Me angry with all they did. So My anger will pour out on this place. It will not stop.

26 "But to the king of Judah who sent you to ask the the LORD, say this: The The LORD, the God of Israel, says: You heard these words. 27 Your heart was soft. You humbled yourself before God when you heard His words against this place and its people. You tore your clothes and cried before Me, so I heard you, says the the LORD. 28 I will let you die in peace. You will be buried with your fathers. Your eyes will not see all the trouble I bring on this place and its people."

Josiah Makes a Promise

29 The king sent for all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem. He gathered them together. 30 The king went up to the the LORD's house. All the men of Judah, the people of Jerusalem, the priests, the Levites, and all the people, young and old, went with him. He read to them all the words of the Book of the Agreement found in the the LORD's house. 31 The king stood by his place. He made an agreement before the the LORD. He promised to follow the the LORD and keep His commands, rules, and laws with all his heart and all his soul. He promised to do the words of the agreement written in this book. 32 He made all the people in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand and agree. The people of Jerusalem did what the agreement with God, the God of their fathers, said. 33 Josiah took away all the hated idols from all the lands of the Israelites. He made all the people in Israel serve the the LORD their God. As long as he lived, they did not stop following the the LORD, the God of their fathers.
