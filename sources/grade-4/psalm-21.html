A Song for the King

1 The LORD, the king is happy because of Your strength. He is very glad because You save him. 2 You gave him what his heart wanted. You did not keep back the words he asked for.

God Gives the King Good Things

3 You go to meet him with good gifts. You put a gold crown on his head. 4 He asked You for life, and You gave it to him. He will live forever. 5 His honor is great because You save him. You give him glory and power. 6 You bless him forever. You make him happy with the joy of being near You.

The King Trusts the LORD

7 The king trusts in the LORD. Because the Most High loves him faithfully, he will not fall.

God Will Stop the Enemies

8 Your hand will find all Your enemies. Your right hand will find the people who hate You. 9 You will make them like a hot oven when You come. The LORD will destroy them in His anger, and fire will burn them up. 10 You will destroy their children from the earth. Their family will be gone from the people. 11 They plan evil against You and make bad plans, but they will not win. 12 You will make them run away. You will aim Your arrows at their faces.

We Praise the LORD

13 The LORD, show how great You are with Your strength! We will sing and praise Your power.
