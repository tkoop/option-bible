Jesus Warns His Followers

1 I have told you these things so you won't give up. 2 They will throw you out of your places of worship. In fact, a time is coming when anyone who kills you will think they are helping God. 3 They will do these things because they don't know the Father or Me. 4 I have told you this so that when the time comes, you will remember I told you. I didn't tell you at first because I was with you.

Jesus Says He Is Going Away

5 But now I'm going to the One who sent Me. None of you asks Me, "Where are You going?" 6 Instead, you are sad because I said these things. 7 But I tell you the truth: It is good for you that I go away. If I don't go, the Helper won't come to you. But if I go, I will send Him to you.

The Helper Will Show What Is Right

8 When He comes, He will show the world it is wrong about sin, about God's right way, and about judgment. 9 He will show it is wrong about sin, because people don't believe in Me. 10 He will show it is wrong about God's right way, because I'm going to the Father, and you won't see Me anymore. 11 He will show it is wrong about judgment, because the ruler of this world has already been judged.

Jesus Still Has More to Say

12 I still have many things to say to you, but you can't understand them now. 13 But when the Spirit of truth comes, He will guide you into all truth. He won't speak on His own. He will speak what He hears and tell you what is coming. 14 He will make Me known, because He will take what is Mine and tell it to you. 15 Everything the Father has is Mine. That's why I said He takes what is Mine and tells it to you.

Jesus Talks About Seeing Them Again

16 In a little while, you won't see Me. Then after a little while, you will see Me.

The Followers Do Not Understand

17 Some of His followers said to each other, "What does He mean by, 'In a little while, you won't see Me, and then after a little while, you will see Me'? And by, 'I'm going to the Father'?" 18 They kept asking, "What does 'a little while' mean? We don't know what He is saying."

Jesus Explains What Will Happen

19 Jesus knew they wanted to ask Him. So He said, "Are you asking each other about this? I said, 'In a little while, you won't see Me, and then after a little while, you will see Me.' 20 I tell you the truth: You will cry and be sad, but the world will be happy. You will be sad, but your sadness will turn into joy. 21 A woman has pain when she has a baby because her time has come. But when the baby is born, she forgets the pain because she is happy a person has come into the world. 22 So you have sadness now. But I will see you again, and your heart will be glad. No one will take your joy from you."

Jesus Teaches About Prayer

23 On that day, you won't ask Me anything. I tell you the truth: The Father will give you whatever you ask in My name. 24 Until now, you haven't asked for anything in My name. Ask, and you will get it, so your joy will be full. 25 I have told you these things in stories. A time is coming when I won't use stories anymore. I will speak clearly about the Father. 26 On that day, you will ask in My name. I'm not saying I will ask the Father for you. 27 The Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and believed I came from God. 28 I came from the Father into the world. Now I'm leaving the world and going to the Father.

The Followers Say They Believe

29 His followers said, "Look, now You speak plainly! You are not using stories now. 30 Now we know You know everything. You don't need anyone to ask You questions. We believe You came from God."

Jesus Gives Peace

31 Jesus answered them, "Do you believe now? 32 Look, a time is coming, and it is already here, when you will all run away. Each of you will go to your own home. You will leave Me alone. But I'm not alone, because the Father is with Me. 33 I told you these things so you can have peace in Me. In the world, you will have trouble. But be brave! I have beaten the world."
