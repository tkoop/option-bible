God Talks to Israel's Mountains

1 "Son of man, speak to the mountains of Israel and tell them to listen to the word of the LORD. 2 The Lord the LORD says this: The enemy said about you that the old hills were theirs now.

3 "So speak for Me and say this: They ruined you and hurt you from all sides. You became a thing other nations took. People talked about you and made fun of you. 4 So, mountains of Israel, listen to the word of the Lord the LORD. The Lord the LORD says this to the mountains and hills, to the valleys and dry riverbeds, to the empty ruins and left-behind cities. They became things for other nations around to steal and laugh at.

5 "So the Lord the LORD says this: I spoke against the other nations in My hot anger. I spoke against Edom too. They took My land and were glad and mean about it. They thought its grasslands were theirs to steal. 6 So speak about the land of Israel. Say to the mountains and hills, to the valleys and dry riverbeds, that the Lord the LORD says this: Look, I spoke in My hot anger. I was angry because you took insults from the nations. 7 So the Lord the LORD says this: I raised My hand and made a promise. The nations around you will be ashamed.

8 "But you, mountains of Israel, will grow branches and fruit for My people Israel. They will come home soon. 9 Look, I am on your side. I will turn to you. You will be planted and grown.

10 "I will put many people on you. All Israel will live there. The cities will have people again, and the ruins will be built again. 11 I will put many people and animals on you. They will grow and have young. I will make you lived-in like before. I will make you do better than before. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 12 I will let My people Israel walk on you. They will own you. You will be their land to keep. You will not take their children from them anymore.

13 "The Lord the LORD says this: People say to you that you destroy people and take children from your nation. 14 So you will not destroy people or take children from your nation anymore, says the Lord the LORD. 15 I will not let other nations make fun of you. You will not be ashamed anymore. You will not make your nation fall, says the Lord the LORD."

God Will Make Israel New

16 The word of the LORD came to me again. It said,

17 "Son of man, when Israel lived in their land, they made it dirty by how they acted. They were as unclean as a woman during her monthly time. 18 So I poured out My anger on them. They had spilled blood on the land. They had made it dirty with false gods. 19 I sent them to live among other nations. They spread out in many lands. I judged them for what they did.

20 "Wherever they went, they made My holy name look bad. People said that these are the LORD's people, but they had to leave His land. 21 But I cared about My holy name. Israel had made it look bad among the nations where they went.

22 "So tell Israel that the Lord the LORD says this: I am not doing this for you. I am doing it for My holy name. You made My name look bad among the nations where you went. 23 I will make My great name holy again. The nations made it look bad. You made it look bad too. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, says the Lord the LORD. They will see that I am holy because of you.

24 "I will take you out of the nations. I will gather you from all the lands. I will bring you back to your own land. 25 I will sprinkle clean water on you. You will be clean. I will wash you from all your dirt and all your false gods. 26 I will give you a new heart. I will put a new spirit in you. I will take out your hard heart of stone. I will give you a soft heart.

27 "I will put My Spirit in you. I will help you follow My rules and keep My laws. 28 Then you will live in the land I gave your fathers. You will be My people, and I will be your God.

29 "I will save you from all your dirt. I will call for the grain and make it grow. You will not go hungry again. 30 I will make the fruit trees and fields grow more. You will not be shamed by hunger in front of other nations. 31 Then you will remember your bad ways and your bad deeds. You will hate what you did because of your sins and wrong things.

32 "I am not doing this for you, says the Lord the LORD. Know this. Be ashamed and disgraced for your ways, Israel.

The Land Will Be Full Again

33 "The Lord the LORD says this: On the day I clean you from all your sins, I will let people live in the cities again. The ruins will be built again. 34 The empty land will be farmed. It will not stay empty for everyone to see.

35 "Then people will say that this empty land is now like the garden of Eden. The ruined, empty, broken cities are now strong and lived in.

36 "Then the nations around you that are left will know that I, the LORD, rebuilt what was broken. I planted again what was empty. I, the LORD, said it, and I will do it.

37 "The Lord the LORD says this: I will let Israel ask Me for this again. I will make their people grow like a flock of sheep. 38 Like the holy flock, like the flock in Jerusalem at its special feasts, the ruined cities will be full of people. Then you will know that I am the LORD."
