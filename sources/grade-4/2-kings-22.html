Josiah Becomes King

1 Josiah was eight years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for thirty-one years. His mother was Jedidah. She was the daughter of Adaiah from Bozkath. 2 Josiah did what pleased the LORD. He followed all the ways of David, his father. He never turned away from them.

The Temple Is Repaired

3 In the eighteenth year of King Josiah, the king sent Shaphan to the LORD's temple. Shaphan was the son of Azaliah and the grandson of Meshullam. He was the secretary. 4 The king said, "Go up to Hilkiah the high priest. Have him count the money brought into the LORD's temple. The guards at the door collected it from the people. 5 Give it to the men in charge of the work in the LORD's temple. They will pay the workers who fix the temple. 6 Give it to the woodworkers, the builders, and the stone workers. They will buy wood and stone to fix the temple. 7 Do not ask them to show how they spend it, because they are honest."

The Book of the Law Is Found

8 Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the secretary, "I found the Book of the Law in the LORD's temple." Then Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and Shaphan read it. 9 Shaphan went to the king and told him what happened. He said, "Your servants took the money found in the temple and gave it to the men in charge of the work in the LORD's temple. 10 Hilkiah the priest gave me a book." Then Shaphan read it to the king. 11 When the king heard the words of the Book of the Law, he tore his clothes.

The King Asks the LORD

12 The king gave orders to Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Achbor son of Micaiah, Shaphan the secretary, and Asaiah the king's servant. He said, 13 "Go and ask the LORD for me, for the people, and for all Judah. Ask about the words of this book that was found. The LORD is very angry with us, because our fathers did not obey the words of this book. They did not do all that is written for us."

Huldah the Prophet Speaks

14 Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophet. She was the wife of Shallum son of Tikvah, son of Harhas. He took care of the king's clothes. Huldah lived in Jerusalem, in the second part of the city. They spoke to her. 15 She said, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says. Tell the man who sent you to Me, 16 'I will bring trouble on this place and on the people who live here. I will do all the bad things written in the book the king of Judah read. 17 They left Me and burned incense to other gods. They made Me angry by all they did. So My anger will burn against this place, and it will not go out.'

18 "But tell the king of Judah who sent you to ask the LORD this: 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says. You heard My words. 19 Your heart was soft, and you were sorry before the LORD. You heard what I said about this place and its people. I said it would be ruined and cursed. You tore your clothes and cried before Me. So I have heard you, says the LORD. 20 I will let you die in peace. You will be buried with your fathers. Your eyes will not see all the trouble I will bring on this place.'" Then they brought the answer back to the king.
