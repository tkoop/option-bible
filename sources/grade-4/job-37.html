Elihu Talks About God's Power in the Storm

1 "My heart pounds and jumps from its place when I think about this. 2 Listen closely to the roar of His voice and the rumble that comes from His mouth. 3 He sends His lightning under the whole sky and to the ends of the earth. 4 After that, a voice roars. He thunders with His great voice. He does not hold back when people hear it. 5 God thunders in wonderful ways with His voice. He does great things we cannot understand.

6 "He tells the snow, 'Fall on the earth.' He tells the rain, 'Pour down hard.' 7 He stops people from working, so everyone can know what He is doing. 8 Then wild animals go into their dens and stay in their caves. 9 The storm comes from its room, and the cold comes with the strong winds. 10 God's breath makes ice, and the wide waters freeze. 11 He fills the clouds with water, and He scatters His lightning through them. 12 The clouds turn around to do what He tells them, all over the earth. 13 He may send it to punish people, to help the land, or to show His love.

Elihu Asks Job to Think

14 "Listen to this, Job. Stand up and think about God's wonders. 15 Do you know how God controls the clouds and makes His cloud shine with lightning? 16 Do you know how the clouds hang in the sky, the wonders of Him who knows everything? 17 Your clothes get hot when the land is still because of the south wind. 18 Can you, with Him, spread out the sky, strong like a bronze mirror?

19 "Teach us what we should say to Him. We cannot get our words ready because of the dark. 20 Should I tell Him that I want to speak? If a man speaks, will he be swallowed up? 21 Now people do not see the light. It is bright in the sky, but the wind blows by and clears the clouds.

God's Great Power and Justice

22 "Out of the north comes gold, and God is clothed in wonderful greatness. 23 The Almighty—we cannot find Him. He is great in power. He does not twist justice, and He does not stop doing what is right. 24 So people fear Him. He does not look at those who think they are wise in their own hearts."
