The Prophets Help the Builders

1 The prophets Haggai and Zechariah, the son of Iddo, spoke God's message to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem. They spoke in the name of the God of Israel, who watched over them. 2 Then Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua, the son of Jozadak, got up and started to rebuild God's house in Jerusalem. God's prophets were with them and helped them.

Officials Ask About the Building

3 At that time, Tattenai, the governor of the land across the river, and Shethar-bozenai and their friends came to them. They said, "Who told you to build this house and finish it? 4 What are the names of the men building this building?" 5 But God watched over the Jewish leaders, and they did not stop the work until a report went to Darius and an answer came back by letter.

A Letter to King Darius

6 This is a copy of the letter that Tattenai, the governor of the land across the river, and Shethar-bozenai and his friends, the officials there, sent to King Darius. 7 They sent a report to him. It said, "To King Darius: Peace to you. 8 Let the king know that we went to Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with big stones, and wood is put in the walls. The work is going fast and doing well in their hands. 9 Then we asked those leaders, 'Who told you to build this house and finish it?' 10 We also asked their names, so we could tell you and write down the names of their leaders.

11 "They answered us, 'We are servants of the God of heaven and earth. We are rebuilding the house that was built many years ago. A great king of Israel built it and finished it. 12 But our fathers made the God of heaven angry, so He let Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, the Chaldean, rule over them. Nebuchadnezzar destroyed this house and took the people to Babylon. 13 But in the first year of Cyrus, king of Babylon, King Cyrus gave an order to rebuild this house of God. 14 The gold and silver things from God's house were taken by Nebuchadnezzar from the temple in Jerusalem to the temple in Babylon. King Cyrus took them from the temple in Babylon and gave them to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he made governor. 15 King Cyrus told him, 'Take these things, go, and put them in the temple in Jerusalem. Let God's house be rebuilt in its place.' 16 Then Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of God's house in Jerusalem. From that time until now they have been building it, and it is not finished yet.'

"Now, if it pleases the king, let a search be made in the king's records in Babylon. See if King Cyrus gave an order to rebuild God's house in Jerusalem. Then let the king tell us what he wants to do about this."
