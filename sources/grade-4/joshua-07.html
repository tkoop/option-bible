Achan Breaks God's Rule

1 The Israelites did not obey God. Achan took some of the things that had to be destroyed. He was from the tribe of Judah. His family line was Zerah, then Zabdi, then Carmi, then Achan. The the LORD was very angry with the Israelites.

Joshua Sends Men to Spy on Ai

2 Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai. Ai was near Beth Aven, east of Bethel. He said to them, "Go up and look at the land." So the men went up and looked at Ai.

3 They came back to Joshua and said, "Do not send all the people. Send about two or three thousand men to attack Ai. Do not make all the people tired, because Ai has only a few men."

The First Fight at Ai

4 So about three thousand men went up. But they ran away from the men of Ai.

5 The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them. They chased them from the city gate to Shebarim. They killed them as they ran down the hill. Then the people of Israel were very afraid.

Joshua Prays to the LORD

6 Joshua tore his clothes. He fell to the ground in front of the ark of the the LORD. He stayed there until evening. The leaders of Israel did the same. They put dust on their heads.

7 Joshua said, "Oh, Lord the the LORD, why did You bring us across the Jordan? Did You bring us here so the Amorites could destroy us? I wish we had stayed on the other side of the Jordan! 8 O Lord, what can I say now? Israel has turned and run from its enemies. 9 The Canaanites and all the people who live in this land will hear about this. They will come against us and destroy us completely. Then what will You do for Your great name?"

God Tells Joshua What Happened

10 The the LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying on your face? 11 Israel has sinned. They broke My covenant, the one I gave them. They took some of the things that had to be destroyed. They stole. They lied. They put those things with their own things. 12 That is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They will run from their enemies, because they themselves are now set apart to be destroyed. I will not be with you anymore unless you destroy what is set apart from among you. 13 Get up and make the people holy. Tell them, 'Make yourselves holy for tomorrow. The the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: There are things set apart to be destroyed among you, Israel. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove those things from among you.' 14 In the morning, you must come near tribe by tribe. The tribe the the LORD chooses will come forward family group by family group. The family group the the LORD chooses will come forward family by family. The family the the LORD chooses will come forward man by man. 15 The one who is caught with the things set apart must be burned with fire. Everything he has must be burned too. He broke the the LORD's covenant and did a very bad thing in Israel."

Joshua Finds the Guilty Person

16 Joshua got up early in the morning. He brought Israel forward tribe by tribe. The tribe of Judah was chosen. 17 He brought the family groups of Judah forward. The family group of the Zerahites was chosen. He brought the Zerahites forward family by family. The family of Zabdi was chosen. 18 He brought the family of Zabdi forward man by man. Achan was chosen. He was the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah.

Achan Admits What He Did

19 Then Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give honor to the the LORD, the God of Israel, and make confession to Him. Tell me what you did. Do not hide it from me."

20 Achan answered, "It is true. I sinned against the the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did. 21 I saw a beautiful coat from Shinar among the things taken. I saw two hundred pieces of silver and a gold bar that weighed fifty pieces. I wanted them, so I took them. They are hidden in the ground in my tent. The silver is under them."

The Things Are Found

22 Joshua sent men. They ran to the tent. There they found the hidden things. The silver was under them. 23 They took the things out of the tent and brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites. They laid them out before the the LORD.

Achan Is Punished

24 Then Joshua and all Israel took Achan, son of Zerah. They took the silver, the coat, and the gold bar. They took his sons and daughters, his ox, his donkey, his sheep, his tent, and everything he owned. They brought them to the Valley of Achor.

25 Joshua said, "You have brought trouble on us. Today the the LORD will bring trouble on you." Then all Israel stoned him. They burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.

26 They piled a big heap of stones over him. It is still there today. Then the the LORD stopped being so angry. So that place is called the Valley of Achor to this day.
