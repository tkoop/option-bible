Cornelius Sees an Angel

1 In Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius. He was an army officer in the Italian group. 2 He and his whole family worshiped God and respected Him. He gave many gifts to the people. He prayed to God all the time.

3 One day, about three in the afternoon, he saw an angel from God in a vision. The angel came to him and said, "Cornelius."

4 Cornelius looked at the angel. He was scared. He said, "What is it, Lord?"

The angel said, "Your prayers and your gifts have come up to God. He remembers them.

5 "Now send men to Joppa. Bring back a man named Simon. People call him Peter.

6 "He is staying with another Simon. That Simon works with leather. His house is by the sea."

7 When the angel finished talking, Cornelius called two of his servants and one soldier who loved God. The soldier stayed close to him.

8 Cornelius told them everything. Then he sent them to Joppa.

Peter Sees a Vision

9 The next day, the men were near the city. Peter went up on the roof to pray.

10 He was hungry and wanted to eat. While they made food ready, he had a vision.

11 He saw heaven open. Something like a big sheet came down. It was held by four corners and lowered to the ground.

12 In it were all kinds of animals with four feet. There were also things that crawl on the ground and birds from the sky.

13 A voice said to him, "Get up, Peter. Kill and eat."

14 Peter said, "No, Lord! I have never eaten anything that is not allowed or unclean."

15 The voice said to him again, "Do not call unclean what God has made clean."

16 This happened three times. Then the sheet was taken back up into heaven.

Peter Meets Cornelius's Men

17 Peter was wondering what the vision meant. Just then, the men from Cornelius reached Simon's house. They stood at the gate.

18 They called out and asked if Simon, called Peter, was staying there.

19 Peter was still thinking about the vision. The Holy Spirit said to him, "Look, three men are looking for you.

20 "Get up, go down, and go with them. Do not doubt. I sent them."

21 Peter went down to the men. He said, "Look, I am the one you are looking for. Why are you here?"

22 They said, "Cornelius is an army officer. He does what is right and respects God. All the Jewish people speak well of him. A holy angel told him to send for you. He wants you to come to his house and hear your words."

23 Peter invited them in and let them stay. The next day he got up and went with them. Some brothers from Joppa went too.

24 The next day they came to Caesarea. Cornelius was waiting for them. He had called his family and close friends together.

Peter and Cornelius Meet

25 When Peter was about to go in, Cornelius met him. He fell at Peter's feet and worshiped him.

26 Peter lifted him up. He said, "Stand up. I am a man too."

27 They talked together. Then Peter went in. He saw many people gathered there.

28 He said to them, "You know it is against our law for a Jewish man to be close to a person from another nation. But God showed me that I should not call any person common or unclean.

29 "So when you sent for me, I came without arguing. Now tell me, why did you send for me?"

30 Cornelius said, "Four days ago I was in my house praying. It was about this same hour, the ninth hour. Then a man in bright clothes stood in front of me.

31 "He said, 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard. Your gifts are remembered before God.

32 "'Send to Joppa and call for Simon, called Peter. He is staying in the house of Simon the leather worker by the sea.'

33 "So I sent for you right away. You did well to come. Now we are all here before God. We want to hear everything the Lord has told you to say."

Peter Tells About Jesus

34 Then Peter spoke. He said, "I now see that God treats everyone the same.

35 "In every nation, the person who respects Him and does what is right is accepted by Him.

36 "God sent His word to the people of Israel. He told them the good news of peace through Jesus Christ. Jesus is Lord of all.

37 "You know the message that spread through all Judea. It started in Galilee after John preached about baptism.

38 "You know how God poured His Holy Spirit and power on Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus went around doing good. He healed all the people the devil had hurt. God was with Him.

39 "We are witnesses of everything He did in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem. They killed Him by hanging Him on a tree.

40 "God raised Him on the third day. God let Him be seen.

41 "He was not seen by all the people. He was seen by witnesses God chose ahead of time. We are those witnesses. We ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead.

42 "He told us to preach to the people. He told us to say that Jesus is the One God chose to judge the living and the dead.

43 "All the prophets say this about Him: Everyone who believes in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name."

The Holy Spirit Comes

44 While Peter was still speaking, the Holy Spirit came down on all who heard the message.

45 The Jewish believers who came with Peter were amazed. The gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out on people from other nations too.

46 They heard them speaking in different languages and praising God. Then Peter said,

47 "Can anyone stop these people from being baptized with water? They received the Holy Spirit just like we did."

48 Peter told them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay for a few days.
