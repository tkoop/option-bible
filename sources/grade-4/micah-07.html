Micah Is Sad About His People

1 How sad for me! I am like someone picking summer fruit, like grapes left after harvest. There is no bunch to eat. I am hungry for the first good fruit. 2 Good people are gone from the land. No one does right. They all wait to hurt others. They trap each other. 3 They are skilled at doing wrong. The ruler asks for gifts. The judge takes bribes. The powerful man says what he wants. They work together to do wrong. 4 Even the best of them is like a thorn bush. The most honest one is sharper than thorns. The day you set has come. Now they are confused.

People Turn Against Each Other

5 Do not trust a friend. Do not trust a close friend. Watch what you say to the one who lies in your arms. 6 A son shames his father. A daughter turns against her mother. A wife turns against her mother-in-law. A man's enemies are in his own home.

Micah Trusts God

7 But I will look to the the LORD. I will wait for God, who saves me. My God will hear me. 8 Do not laugh at me, my enemy. I may fall, but I will get up. I may sit in the dark, but the the LORD will be my light. 9 I will take the the LORD's anger because I sinned against Him. He will stand up for me and make things right. He will bring me into the light. I will see that He is right. 10 My enemy will see it and be ashamed. She said, "Where is the the LORD your God?" My eyes will see her. She will be stepped on like mud in the street.

God Will Bring People Home

11 The day will come when your walls are built again. On that day, your land will get bigger. 12 In that day, people will come to you from Assyria, from strong cities, from walled cities to the river, from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain. 13 But the land will be empty because of the people who live there. It will be because of what they have done.

God Will Care for His People

14 Take care of your people with your stick. They are your flock. They live alone in the woods, in Carmel. Let them eat in Bashan and Gilead, like in the old days. 15 Like in the days when you came out of Egypt, I will show them amazing things. 16 The nations will see and be ashamed of their strength. They will put their hands over their mouths. Their ears will be deaf. 17 They will lick the dust like a snake. They will crawl out of their holes like worms. They will shake with fear because of the the LORD our God. They will be afraid of You.

Praise God's Mercy

18 Who is a God like You? You forgive sin. You let go of the wrongs of the people who are left of Your own people. You do not stay angry forever, because You love to show mercy. 19 He will come back and have mercy on us. He will defeat our sins. You will throw all their sins into the deep sea. 20 You will keep Your promise to Jacob. You will show mercy to Abraham. You promised this to our fathers long ago.
