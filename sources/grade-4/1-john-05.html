Born of God

1 Everyone who believes Jesus is the Christ is born of God. 2 Everyone who loves the Father loves His child too. We know we love God's children when we love God and keep His commands. 3 Loving God means keeping His commands. And His commands are not hard. 4 Everything born of God wins over the world. Our faith is the win that wins over the world. 5 Who wins over the world? The one who believes Jesus is the Son of God.

Jesus Came with Water and Blood

6 Jesus Christ is the One who came by water and blood. He came not by water only, but by water and blood. The Spirit tells the truth, because the Spirit is the truth. 7 There are three who tell about it. 8 They are the Spirit, the water, and the blood. The three agree as one.

God's Word Is True

9 We take people's words. But God's word is greater. God has told about His Son. 10 The one who believes in the Son of God has this word in him. The one who does not believe God calls God a liar, because he did not believe God's word about His Son.

Life in the Son

11 This is God's word: God gave us life that lasts forever. This life is in His Son. 12 The one who has the Son has life. The one who does not have the Son of God does not have life.

Know You Have Life

13 I wrote these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God. I want you to know you have life that lasts forever.

Prayer and God's Will

14 We can come to God with courage. If we ask for anything that fits His will, He hears us. 15 If we know He hears us, we know we have what we asked from Him.

Sin That Leads to Death

16 If anyone sees his brother sin in a way that does not lead to death, he should ask God. God will give life to those who sin in a way that does not lead to death. There is sin that leads to death. I am not saying to pray about that. 17 All wrong is sin. But there is sin that does not lead to death.

We Belong to God

18 We know everyone born of God does not keep sinning. The One born of God keeps him safe. The evil one does not touch him. 19 We know we are from God. The whole world lies in the evil one.

The True God

20 We know the Son of God has come. He helped us understand so we may know Him who is true. We are in Him who is true, in His Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and life that lasts forever.

Stay Away from Idols

21 Dear children, keep yourselves away from false gods.
