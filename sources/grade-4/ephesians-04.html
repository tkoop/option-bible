Live the Way God Called You

1 I am in jail because I belong to the Lord. I ask you to live in a way that fits the call you got. 2 Be humble and gentle. Be patient. Put up with each other in love. 3 Work hard to keep the peace and stay together as one in the Spirit.

4 There is one body and one Spirit. God called you to share one hope. 5 There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism. 6 There is one God and Father of all. He is over all, through all, and in all.

Christ Gives Gifts to His People

7 But each of us got a gift of grace. Christ gave it in just the right amount. 8 It says, "When He went up high, He led captives away, and He gave gifts to people." 9 What does "He went up" mean? It means He also came down to the lower parts of the earth. 10 The One who came down is the same One who went up above all the heavens. He did this to fill everything.

11 He gave some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to tell the good news, and some to be pastors and teachers. 12 He did this to help God's people get ready to serve others and to build up Christ's body. 13 We keep going until we all believe the same and know God's Son. We want to grow up to be like Christ in every way.

14 Then we will not be little children anymore. We will not be tossed around like waves or blown by every new teaching. We will not be tricked by people who plan to fool us. 15 Instead, we speak the truth in love. We grow up in every way into Christ, who is the head. 16 The whole body is joined and held together by every part. Each part does its work. This makes the body grow and build itself up in love.

Live Like New People

17 So I say this with the Lord's help: Do not live the way people who do not know God live. Their thoughts are empty. 18 Their minds are dark. They are cut off from God's life because they do not know Him. Their hearts are hard. 19 They stopped caring. They gave themselves up to doing bad things. They do every kind of wrong thing, and they always want more.

20 But that is not how you learned about Christ. 21 You heard Him. You were taught in Him. The truth is in Jesus. 22 You were taught to take off your old self. That old self was going bad because of wrong desires that trick you. 23 Let your thinking be made new. 24 Put on the new self. God made it to be like Him. It is right and holy and true.

How to Treat Each Other

25 So each of you must stop lying. Speak the truth to your neighbor. We are parts of the same body. 26 You may get angry, but do not sin. Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry. 27 Do not give the devil a chance.

28 Anyone who steals must stop stealing. He should work with his hands and do good. Then he can share with people who need help. 29 Do not let any bad words come out of your mouth. Say only what is good and helpful. Then your words will help the people who hear them.

30 Do not make God's Holy Spirit sad. He sealed you for the day you will be set free. 31 Get rid of all bitterness, anger, yelling, and mean talk. Put away all bad feelings. 32 Be kind and tender to each other. Forgive each other, just as God in Christ forgave you.
