Three Years of Peace

1 For three years there was no war between Aram and Israel. 2 In the third year, Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went to see the king of Israel.

The King of Israel Asks for Help

3 The king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth-gilead is ours? We have not taken it back from the king of Aram."

4 Then he said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you go with me to fight at Ramoth-gilead?"

Jehoshaphat said, "I am like you. My people are like your people. My horses are like your horses."

Prophets Say to Go

5 Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please ask the the LORD what to do today."

6 The king of Israel gathered about four hundred prophets. He asked them, "Should I go to war at Ramoth-gilead, or should I wait?"

The prophets said, "Go up. The Lord will give it into the king's hand."

Micaiah Is Called

7 Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not another prophet of the the LORD here? Let us ask him too."

8 The king of Israel said, "There is still one man who can ask the the LORD for us. But I hate him, because he never says good things about me. He only says bad things. His name is Micaiah son of Imlah."

Jehoshaphat said, "Please do not say that."

9 The king of Israel called an officer and said, "Bring Micaiah son of Imlah quickly."

Prophets at the Gate

10 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, sat on their thrones. They wore royal clothes. They were at the threshing floor by the gate of Samaria. All the prophets were speaking in front of them.

11 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns. He said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'With these you will push Aram until they are destroyed.'"

12 All the prophets said the same thing: "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and win. The the LORD will give it into the king's hand."

13 The messenger who went to call Micaiah said to him, "Look, all the prophets are saying the same good thing to the king. Please say a good thing too."

14 Micaiah said, "As surely as the the LORD lives, I will say only what the the LORD tells me."

Micaiah Speaks to the King

15 Micaiah came to the king. The king said, "Micaiah, should we go to Ramoth-gilead to fight, or should we not?"

Micaiah said, "Go up and win. The The LORD will give it into the king's hand."

16 The king said, "How many times must I make you promise to tell me only the truth in the name of the the LORD?"

17 Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the hills like sheep with no shepherd. The The LORD said, 'They have no master. Let each one go home in peace.'"

18 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Didn't I tell you he would not say good things about me? He would only say bad things."

19 Micaiah said, "So listen to the the LORD's word. I saw the the LORD sitting on His throne. All the armies of heaven stood around Him, on His right and on His left. The The LORD said, 'Who will trick Ahab into going up and dying at Ramoth-gilead?' One said one thing, and one said another. Then a spirit came out and stood before the the LORD. It said, 'I will trick him.' The The LORD asked, 'How?'

The spirit said, 'I will go out and put false words in the mouths of all his prophets.' The The LORD said, 'You will trick him. Go and do it.' So now the the LORD has put false words in the mouths of all these prophets. The The LORD has said bad things will happen to you."

24 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came close and hit Micaiah on the cheek. He said, "Which way did the the LORD's Spirit leave me to speak to you?"

25 Micaiah said, "You will see on that day when you go into a back room to hide."

26 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah. Send him back to Amon, the city leader, and to Joash, the king's son."

27 He said, "Say, 'The king says: Put this man in prison. Give him only bread and water until I come home safe.'"

28 Micaiah said, "If you come home safe, then the the LORD did not speak through me." He also said, "Listen, everyone!"

Battle at Ramoth-gilead

29 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went up to Ramoth-gilead.

30 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will dress like someone else and go into battle. But you wear your royal clothes." Then the king of Israel dressed like someone else and went into battle.

31 The king of Aram ordered thirty-two chariot commanders, "Do not fight with anyone, big or small. Fight only with the king of Israel."

32 When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they thought, "That must be the king of Israel." They turned to fight him. Jehoshaphat cried out.

33 When they saw he was not the king of Israel, they stopped chasing him.

34 A man shot an arrow without aiming. It hit the king of Israel between parts of his armor. The king said to his chariot driver, "Turn around and get me out of the battle. I am hurt."

35 The battle got worse that day. The king stood in his chariot facing Aram. Blood ran from his wound into the bottom of the chariot. He died that evening.

36 At sunset, a shout went through the army: "Every man go to his city. Every man go to his land."

The King of Israel Dies

37 The king died. They brought him to Samaria and buried him there.

38 They washed his chariot at the pool of Samaria. Dogs licked his blood. Prostitutes washed there. This happened just as the the LORD had said.

39 The rest of Ahab's acts and all he did are written in the book of the history of the kings of Israel. This includes the ivory house he built and the cities he built. 40 Ahab died and was buried with his fathers. His son Ahaziah became king after him.

Jehoshaphat Rules Judah

41 Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king of Judah in the fourth year of Ahab, king of Israel.

42 Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king. He ruled twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

43 He followed the way of his father Asa. He did not turn away from it. He did what was right in the the LORD's eyes. But the high places were not removed. The people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

44 Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.

45 The rest of Jehoshaphat's acts, his power, and his wars are written in the book of the history of the kings of Judah.

46 He removed the rest of the male temple prostitutes who were left from his father Asa's time.

47 There was no king in Edom. A governor ruled there.

48 Jehoshaphat built ships to go to Ophir for gold. But they did not go, because the ships were broken at Ezion-geber.

49 Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants go with your servants." But Jehoshaphat would not.

50 Jehoshaphat died and was buried with his fathers in the city of David, his father. His son Jehoram became king after him.

Ahaziah Rules Israel

51 Ahaziah son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah. He ruled two years over Israel.

52 He did evil in the the LORD's eyes. He followed the way of his father and mother, and the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin.

53 He served Baal and worshiped him. He made the the LORD, the God of Israel, angry, just like his father had done.
