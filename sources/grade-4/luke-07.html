Jesus Heals a Soldier's Servant

1 When Jesus finished saying all these things to the people, He went into Capernaum. 2 An army leader had a servant he cared about very much. The servant was sick and about to die. 3 When the leader heard about Jesus, he sent some Jewish elders to Him. He asked Jesus to come and heal his servant. 4 They came to Jesus and begged Him hard. They said, "He deserves for you to do this for him, 5 because he loves our people and built our meeting place for us."

6 Jesus went with them. When He was not far from the house, the army leader sent friends to Him. They said, "Lord, please do not trouble Yourself. I am not good enough for You to come into my house. 7 That is why I did not think I should come to You myself. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed. 8 I am a man under orders too. I have soldiers under me. I tell one, 'Go,' and he goes. I tell another, 'Come,' and he comes. I tell my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

9 When Jesus heard this, He was amazed at the man. He turned to the crowd following Him and said, "I tell you, I have not found faith like this, even in Israel." 10 The men who were sent went back home. They found the servant well.

Jesus Brings a Widow's Son Back to Life

11 Soon after, Jesus went to a town called Nain. His disciples went with Him, and a big crowd too. 12 As He came near the town gate, a dead man was being carried out. He was the only son of his mother, and she was a widow. A big crowd from the town was with her.

13 The Lord saw her. He felt sorry for her. He said to her, "Do not cry." 14 He came up and touched the coffin. The men carrying it stopped. He said, "Young man, I say to you, get up." 15 The dead man sat up and began to talk. Jesus gave him back to his mother.

16 Everyone was afraid. They praised God. They said, "A great prophet has come among us," and "God has come to help His people." 17 News about Jesus spread all over Judea and the nearby places.

John Asks About Jesus

18 John's disciples told him about all these things. 19 John called two of his disciples. He sent them to the Lord to ask, "Are You the One who is to come, or should we look for someone else?"

20 The men came to Jesus. They said that John the Baptist had sent them to ask if You are the One who is to come, or if they should look for someone else.

21 At that time, Jesus healed many people of sickness and pain. He also forced out bad spirits. He made many blind people see. 22 He answered them, "Go and tell John what you have seen and heard. Blind people see. Lame people walk. People with skin disease are made clean. Deaf people hear. Dead people are raised. Poor people hear good news." 23 "Happy is the person who does not lose faith in Me."

Jesus Talks About John

24 After John's messengers left, Jesus began to speak to the crowds about John. He said, "What did you go out into the desert to see? A weak person blown about by the wind? 25 What did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft clothes? People in fancy clothes who live in comfort are in kings' houses. 26 What did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes. I tell you, he is more than a prophet. 27 This is the one the Bible talks about. It says that God is sending His messenger ahead of You to get Your way ready. 28 I tell you, no one born of a woman is greater than John. But the least person in God's kingdom is greater than he."

29 All the people, even tax collectors, heard this. They agreed that God was right, because they had been baptized by John. 30 But the strict Jewish leaders and the teachers of the law did not accept God's plan for themselves. They were not baptized by John.

People Who Will Not Listen

31 Jesus said, "What are the people of this time like? What can I compare them to? 32 They are like children sitting in the market. They call to each other that they played a happy song but the others did not dance, and that they sang a sad song but the others did not cry. 33 John the Baptist came. He did not eat bread or drink wine. You say he has a demon. 34 The Son of Man came. He eats and drinks. You say He eats too much and drinks too much and is a friend of tax collectors and sinners! 35 But wisdom is shown to be right by all she does."

A Sinful Woman Shows Love

36 One of the strict Jewish leaders asked Jesus to eat with him. Jesus went into his house and sat down to eat. 37 A woman in the town who had sinned much heard that Jesus was eating there. She brought a small bottle of perfume. 38 She stood behind Him at His feet. She cried. She began to wet His feet with her tears. She dried them with her hair. She kissed His feet. She put perfume on them.

39 The strict Jewish leader who invited Jesus saw this. He said to himself, "If this man were a prophet, he would know who this woman is and what kind of woman she is. She is a sinner, and she is touching him."

40 Jesus answered him, "Simon, I have something to say to you."

"Say it, Teacher," he said.

41 "A man who lent money had two people who owed him money. One owed 500 silver coins. The other owed 50. 42 Neither could pay. So the man forgave both. Which one will love him more?"

43 Simon answered, "I think the one who owed more."

Jesus said, "You are right."

44 Then Jesus turned to the woman. He said to Simon, "Do you see this woman? I came into your house. You did not give Me water for My feet. But she wet My feet with her tears and dried them with her hair. 45 You did not kiss Me. But she has not stopped kissing My feet since I came in. 46 You did not put oil on My head. But she put perfume on My feet. 47 So I tell you, her many sins are forgiven. She loved much. But a person who is forgiven little loves little."

48 He said to her, "Your sins are forgiven."

49 The other people eating with Him began to say to themselves, "Who is this man who even forgives sins?"

50 He said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you. Go in peace."
