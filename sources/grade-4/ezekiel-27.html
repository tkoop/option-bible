A Sad Song for Tyre

1 The word of the LORD came to me.

2 "Son of man, sing a sad song about Tyre.

Tyre Was Proud and Beautiful

"3 Say to Tyre, who lives by the sea and trades with many lands. This is what the Lord the LORD says: You, Tyre, have said, 'I am perfect in beauty.' 4 Your borders are in the heart of the sea. Your builders made you very beautiful.

"5 They built your ship boards from pine trees of Senir. They took cedar from Lebanon to make your mast. 6 They made your oars from oak trees of Bashan. They made your deck from ivory and wood from the coasts of Cyprus. 7 Your sail was fine linen from Egypt. It was your flag. Your cover was blue and purple cloth from the coasts of Elishah.

Tyre's Helpers and Soldiers

"8 Men from Sidon and Arvad rowed for you. The wise men of Tyre were your sailors and ship guides. 9 Old men from Gebal and their skilled workers fixed your leaks. Ships from all over the sea and their sailors came to trade with you. 10 Men from Persia, Lud, and Put served in your army. They hung shields and helmets on your walls. They made you look great. 11 Men from Arvad and your army stood on your walls all around. Men from Gammad stood in your towers. They hung their shields on your walls all around. They made you beautiful.

Many Nations Traded with Tyre

"12 Tarshish traded with you because you had so much wealth. They gave silver, iron, tin, and lead for your goods. 13 Greece, Tubal, and Meshech traded with you. They brought people and bronze tools to trade. 14 From Beth-togarmah they gave horses, war horses, and mules for your goods. 15 The men of Dedan traded with you. You traded with many lands. They brought ivory and dark wood to pay you. 16 Aram traded with you because you made so many things. They gave blue-green stone, purple cloth, fine sewing, linen, coral, and red stones for your goods.

"17 Judah and Israel traded with you. They gave wheat, cakes, honey, oil, and sweet oil for your trade. 18 Damascus traded with you because you had so much wealth and so many goods. They gave wine from Helbon and white wool. 19 Dan and Greece brought iron, sweet spice, and cane for your trade. 20 Dedan traded with you for saddle blankets. 21 Arabia and all the princes of Kedar traded with you. They traded in lambs, rams, and goats.

"22 Traders of Sheba and Raamah traded with you. They gave gold, the best spices, and precious stones for your goods. 23 Haran, Canneh, Eden, and traders from Sheba, Asshur, and Kilmad traded with you. 24 They traded with you in beautiful clothes, blue and purple cloth, colorful rugs, and strong ropes.

Tyre Will Sink

"25 Ships of Tarshish carried your goods. You were full and very heavy on the sea. 26 Your rowers took you into deep water. The east wind broke your ship in the middle of the sea. 27 Your money, goods, trade, sailors, ship guides, repairers, traders, soldiers, and everyone with you will sink in the sea on the day you fall.

People Will Cry for Tyre

"28 The lands by the sea will shake when your ship guides cry out. 29 All who row, all sailors, and all ship guides of the sea will come down from their ships and stand on the land. 30 They will cry loudly over you and cry with pain. They will throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes. 31 They will shave their heads for you and put on rough cloth. They will cry for you with bitter hearts and deep sadness. 32 They will sing a sad song for you and cry over you: 'Who was like Tyre, now quiet in the sea?'

"33 When your goods went out over the seas, you made many people rich. Your wealth and trade made the kings of the earth rich. 34 Now you are broken by the seas in deep water. Your trade and all your people have fallen with you. 35 All who live by the sea are shocked at you. Their kings are afraid and shake. 36 The traders among the nations hiss at you. You have become a terrible thing, and you will be no more forever."
