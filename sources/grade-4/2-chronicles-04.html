The Bronze Altar and the Sea

1 Solomon made a bronze altar. It was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high. 2 He also made the Sea out of cast metal. It was round. It was ten cubits from one side to the other. It was five cubits high. A line thirty cubits long went all the way around it.

3 Below the rim, there were bulls all around it. There were ten bulls in each cubit. The bulls were in two rows. They were cast as part of the Sea. 4 The Sea stood on twelve bulls. Three bulls faced north. Three faced west. Three faced south. Three faced east. The Sea was on top of them. Their backs faced the middle. 5 The Sea was as thick as a hand is wide. Its rim was like the rim of a cup. It looked like a lily flower. It held three thousand baths.

6 Solomon made ten basins for washing. He put five on the right side and five on the left side. The priests washed things for burnt offerings in them. The priests washed in the Sea.

Lampstands, Tables, and Bowls

7 He made ten gold lampstands. They were made the way they should be. He put them in the temple. Five were on the right side and five on the left side. 8 He made ten tables. He put them in the temple. Five were on the right side and five on the left side. He also made one hundred gold bowls for sprinkling.

The Courtyard

9 He made the priests' courtyard. He also made the big courtyard and its doors. He covered the doors with bronze. 10 He put the Sea on the southeast side. It was on the south side toward the east.

Huram's Work

11 Huram made the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. Huram finished the work he had started for King Solomon in the house of God. 12 He made two pillars. He made two bowl-shaped tops for the pillars. He made two sets of net-like work to cover the two bowl-shaped tops on the pillars. 13 He made four hundred pomegranates for the two sets of net-like work. There were two rows of pomegranates on each net. They covered the two bowl-shaped tops on the pillars. 14 He made the stands and the basins that went on them. 15 He made the Sea and the twelve bulls under it. 16 Huram-abi made the pots, shovels, forks, and all their tools for King Solomon for the house of the LORD. They were made of polished bronze. 17 The king had them cast in clay molds in the plain of Jordan. This was between Succoth and Zeredah. 18 Solomon made so many of these things that no one could weigh all the bronze.

Gold Items for the Temple

19 Solomon made all the things for the house of God. He made the gold altar. He made the tables for the Bread of the Presence. 20 He made the lampstands of pure gold. They had lamps to burn in front of the Most Holy Place. They were made the way they should be. 21 He made the gold flowers, lamps, and tongs. They were made of the purest gold. 22 He made the gold wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, small bowls, and incense holders. He made the gold doors for the inner room, the Most Holy Place. He also made the gold doors for the main hall of the temple.
