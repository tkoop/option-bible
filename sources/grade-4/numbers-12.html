Miriam and Aaron Complain About Moses

1 Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of his wife from Cush. He had married her. 2 They said, "Has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Hasn't He also spoken through us?" And the LORD heard it. 3 Moses was a very humble man. He was more humble than anyone else on earth.

The LORD Calls All Three

4 Right away the LORD said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, "Come out to the meeting tent, all three of you." So the three of them went out. 5 Then the LORD came down in a pillar of cloud. He stood at the door of the tent and called Aaron and Miriam. The two of them came forward.

The LORD Speaks to Aaron and Miriam

6 He said, "Listen to My words. When there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, show Myself to them in visions. I speak to them in dreams. 7 But it is not like that with My servant Moses. He is faithful in all My house. 8 I speak with him directly. I speak clearly, not in puzzles. He sees the form of the LORD. Why then were you not afraid to speak against My servant Moses?" 9 The LORD was very angry with them, and He left.

Miriam Becomes Sick

10 When the cloud moved away from the tent, Miriam had a bad skin disease. Her skin was as white as snow. Aaron looked at her and saw that she had the skin disease.

Aaron Asks Moses for Help

11 Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not blame us for the sin we did so foolishly. 12 Do not let her be like a baby born dead, with half its body gone."

13 So Moses cried out to the LORD, "Please, God, heal her!"

Miriam Stays Outside the Camp

14 The LORD replied to Moses, "If her father had shamed her, she would be ashamed for seven days. Keep her outside the camp for seven days. After that she can come back." 15 So Miriam was kept outside the camp for seven days. The people did not move on until she was brought back. 16 After that, the people left Hazeroth and camped in the Desert of Paran.
