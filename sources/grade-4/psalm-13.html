David Feels Forgotten

1 For the music leader. A song by David. The LORD, how long will You forget me? Will it be forever? How long will You look away from me? 2 How long must I worry alone? My heart is sad all day. How long will my enemy win over me?

David Asks God for Help

3 Look at me and answer me, the LORD my God. Give me strength, or I will die.

4 Then my enemy will say, "I won!" The people who hurt me will be glad because I failed.

David Trusts God

5 But I trust in Your kind love. My heart will be glad because You save me. 6 I will sing to the LORD, because He has been good to me.
