Stand Free in Christ

1 Christ set you free so you could be free. So stand strong. Do not let anyone put the heavy chain of slavery on you again. 2 Listen, I, Paul, tell you this: If you get circumcised, Christ will not help you at all. 3 I say again to every man who gets circumcised: You must obey the whole law. 4 You who try to be right with God by following the law have been cut off from Christ. You have fallen away from His grace. 5 But we wait with hope for God to make us right. We wait by faith and with the help of the Spirit. 6 In Christ Jesus, being circumcised or not does not matter. What matters is faith that works through love.

7 You were doing well. Who stopped you from obeying the truth? 8 That kind of talk does not come from God, who called you. 9 A little bad teaching can spread to many people. 10 I trust the Lord that you will not think differently. The person who is troubling you will be punished, no matter who it is. 11 Brothers and sisters, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still being hurt for my faith? Then no one would be upset by the message of the cross. 12 I wish those troublemakers would leave you alone forever.

Use Freedom to Love

13 You were called to be free. But do not use your freedom to do wrong. Instead, serve each other with love. 14 The whole law is summed up in one command: "Love your neighbor as you love yourself." 15 If you fight and hurt each other, watch out, or you will destroy each other.

Live by the Spirit

16 So I say, live by the Spirit, and you will not do what your sinful nature wants. 17 Your sinful nature wants what the Spirit does not want. The Spirit wants what your sinful nature does not want. They fight against each other, so you do not do what you want. 18 But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.

Wrong Ways and Good Ways

19 The wrong things people do are clear: sexual sins, impure acts, and wild living. 20 They include worshiping false gods, witchcraft, hatred, fighting, jealousy, anger, selfish ambition, divisions, and groups that split people. 21 They also include envy, getting drunk, wild parties, and other things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that people who do these things will not be part of God's kingdom.

The Good Fruit of the Spirit

22 But the Spirit gives these good things: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faithfulness. 23 He also gives gentleness and self-control. There is no law against these things. 24 People who belong to Christ Jesus have put their sinful nature to death, along with its wrong wants and feelings. 25 If we live by the Spirit, let us also follow the Spirit. 26 Let us not become proud. Let us not provoke each other or envy each other.
