God's Message About the Nations

1 This is the message from the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet about the nations. 2 It is about Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt. His army was by the Euphrates River at Carchemish. Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, beat them in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, king of Judah.

Egypt Gets Ready to Fight

3 Get your shields ready. Get your armor ready. Get ready to fight. 4 Tie up the horses. Ride, horsemen! Stand in your helmets. Shine your spears. Put on your armor.

Egypt Runs Away

5 Why do I see them scared? They run away. Their strong men are beaten. They run fast and do not look back. Fear is all around them, says the LORD. 6 Do not let the fast runner get away. Do not let the strong man escape. In the north, by the Euphrates River, they trip and fall.

Egypt Rises Like a River

7 Who is rising like the Nile, with water like rushing rivers? 8 Egypt rises like the Nile. Its water rushes like rivers. Egypt says, "I will rise. I will cover the earth. I will destroy cities and the people in them." 9 Horses, go forward! Chariots, rush in! Strong men, go out to fight. Soldiers from Cush and Put carry shields. Soldiers from Lud use bows.

God Will Punish Egypt

10 That day belongs to the Lord, the LORD of heaven's armies. It is a day when He punishes His enemies. The sword will eat and drink its fill of their blood. The Lord, the LORD of heaven's armies, has a sacrifice in the north by the Euphrates River.

Egypt Cannot Be Healed

11 Go up to Gilead and get medicine, young woman of Egypt. You used many medicines for nothing. There is no healing for you. 12 The nations heard about your shame. Your cry fills the earth. Strong men trip over each other. They both fall.

Babylon Will Attack Egypt

13 This is the message the LORD gave Jeremiah the prophet. It is about Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, coming to attack Egypt. 14 Tell it in Egypt. Announce it in Migdol. Announce it in Memphis and Tahpanhes. Say, "Get ready. Be prepared. The sword will destroy all around you."

15 Why are your brave men swept away? They did not stand, because the LORD pushed them down. 16 He made many trip and fall on each other. They said, "Get up. Let us go back to our people and our home land. Let us get away from the sword of the cruel enemy."

17 People called Pharaoh, king of Egypt, Noise. He let his chance pass. 18 "As surely as I live, says the King. His name is the LORD of heaven's armies. Someone will come like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel by the sea." 19 Get your things ready to leave, people of Egypt. Memphis will become empty and burned. No one will live there.

Egypt Will Fall

20 Egypt is like a pretty young cow. But a biting fly from the north is coming, coming. 21 Her hired soldiers are like fat calves. They also turn and run. They do not stand, because their bad day has come. It is time for them to be punished. 22 She hisses like a snake. They march with an army and come with axes, like men cutting wood. 23 They will cut down her thick forest, says the LORD. It is too thick to search. They are more than grasshoppers. They cannot be counted. 24 The people of Egypt will be put to shame. They will be given to the people of the north.

God Will Punish Egypt's False Gods

25 The LORD of heaven's armies, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I will punish Amon of No, Pharaoh, Egypt, her gods, her kings, and those who trust in Pharaoh. 26 I will give them to those who want to kill them. I will give them to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and his men. After that, Egypt will be lived in again like before, says the LORD."

God Will Save His People

27 "But you, Jacob My servant, do not be afraid. Do not be scared, Israel. Look, I will save you from far away. I will save your children from the land where they are captives. Jacob will come back. He will be quiet and safe. No one will scare him. 28 You, My servant Jacob, do not be afraid, says the LORD. I am with you. I will completely destroy all the nations where I sent you. But I will not completely destroy you. I will punish you fairly. I will not leave you without punishment."
