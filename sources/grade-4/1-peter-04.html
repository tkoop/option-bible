Christ Suffered for Us

1 Christ suffered in His body. So you should think the same way. Anyone who suffers in the body has stopped living for sin. 2 Live the rest of your time in the body not for what people want, but for what God wants. 3 You already had enough time to do what people who do not know God want. You lived in sexual sin, bad desires, drinking too much, wild parties, and worship of false gods.

4 Now they are shocked that you do not join them in the same wild life of sin. So they say bad things about you. 5 But they will have to answer to God. He is ready to judge the living and the dead. 6 This is why the good news was told even to people who have died. They were judged in the body like all people. But they can live in the spirit like God.

Love and Help Each Other

7 The end of all things is near. So be wise and stay awake so you can pray. 8 Most of all, love each other deeply. Love covers many sins. 9 Welcome guests with love. Do not complain.

10 Each of you has received a gift. Use it to help each other. Be good workers with God's many gifts. 11 If you speak, speak God's words. If you serve, serve with the strength God gives. Then God will be praised in everything through Jesus Christ. He has glory and power forever and ever. Amen.

Hard Times and God's Plan

12 Dear friends, do not be surprised by the hard test that comes to you. Do not think it is strange, as if something strange is happening to you. 13 But be glad that you share in Christ's sufferings. Then you will be very glad and happy when His glory is shown. 14 If you are made fun of for Christ's name, you are blessed. The Spirit of glory and God rests on you.

15 But none of you should suffer because you are a murderer, a thief, a bad person, or a busybody. 16 But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed. Praise God because of that name.

17 It is time for God to judge. It starts with God's family. If it starts with us, what will happen to people who do not obey God's good news? 18 And, "If it is hard for a good person to be saved, what will happen to people who do not know God and who sin?" 19 So those who suffer because it is God's will should give their souls to God, who made everything. He is faithful. Keep doing good.
