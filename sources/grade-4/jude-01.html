Jude Greets God's People

1 Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, writes to the people God called. God the Father loves you. Jesus Christ keeps you safe. 2 May you have lots of mercy, peace, and love.

Fight for the Faith

3 Dear friends, I wanted to write to you about how God saves us all. But I felt I had to write to you. I want you to fight for the faith God gave His holy people. 4 Some bad men have slipped in among you. Long ago God said they would be judged. They change God's grace into an excuse to do wrong. They also say no to our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

God Judged Sin Before

5 You already know this, but I want to remind you. Jesus saved His people out of Egypt. Later He destroyed those who did not believe. 6 Some angels did not stay in their high place. They left their own home. He keeps them in dark chains forever. They will be judged on the great day. 7 Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns around them did the same kind of wrong. They are an example of people who suffer forever in fire.

False Teachers Are Dangerous

8 These men also have dreams. They make their bodies dirty. They do not obey leaders. They speak bad words against glorious beings. 9 But even Michael, the top angel, did not dare to say a bad judgment when he argued with the devil about Moses' body. He said, "The The LORD rebuke you!" 10 But these men say bad things about what they do not know. They act like animals. They know some things by instinct, but those things destroy them. 11 How sad for them! They follow Cain's bad way. They run after money like Balaam did wrong. They are destroyed like Korah's people. 12 These men are spots in your love feasts. They eat with you and do not care. They only feed themselves. They are clouds with no rain, blown by wind. They are fall trees with no fruit, pulled up by roots. They died twice. 13 They are wild sea waves that foam up their shame. They are wandering stars. Dark, dark night is kept for them forever.

Enoch Warned About Them

14 Enoch was the seventh man after Adam. He said this about them: 15 "See, the the LORD is coming with thousands and thousands of His holy ones. He will judge all people. He will show all ungodly people they are wrong for all the bad things they did. He will show them they are wrong for all the hard words they said against Him."

Keep Away from Their Bad Ways

16 These men complain and find fault. They follow their own wrong wants. They brag with big words. They praise people only to get something. 17 But, dear friends, remember the words the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ said before. 18 They told you, "In the last days, people will make fun of God. They will follow their own bad wants." 19 These men split you up. They follow only what feels natural. They do not have the Spirit.

Stay Strong in God's Love

20 But you, dear friends, build each other up in your most holy faith. Pray with the help of the Holy Spirit. 21 Keep in God's love. Wait for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that gives life forever. 22 Be kind to people who doubt. 23 Save some people by pulling them out of the fire. Be kind to others, but be afraid. Hate even clothes made dirty by sin.

Praise God

24 God can keep you from falling. He can bring you to His glory with no blame and with great joy. 25 To the only God who saves us, give glory, greatness, power, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord. This was true before time, now, and forever. Amen.
