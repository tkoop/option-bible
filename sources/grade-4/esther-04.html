Mordecai Is Sad

1 When Mordecai learned what had happened, he tore his clothes. He put on rough cloth and ashes. He went into the city. He cried out with a loud and bitter cry. 2 He went up to the king's gate. But he could not go in, because no one in rough cloth could enter the king's gate. 3 In every part of the land, the king's order made the Jews very sad. They fasted, cried, and wailed. Many lay in rough cloth and ashes.

Esther Hears About Mordecai

4 Esther's helpers and servants came and told her about Mordecai. The queen was very upset. She sent clothes for Mordecai to wear instead of his rough cloth. But he would not take them. 5 Then Esther called Hathach. He was one of the king's helpers who served her. She told him to find out why Mordecai was so upset. 6 So Hathach went out to Mordecai. Mordecai was in the city square in front of the king's gate. 7 Mordecai told him all that had happened. He also told how much money Haman had promised to pay into the king's money room to destroy the Jews. 8 Mordecai gave Hathach a copy of the king's order. It said the Jews would be destroyed. He told Hathach to show it to Esther and explain it. He also told Esther to go to the king. She should beg for mercy and ask for her people.

Hathach Brings the Message

9 Hathach went back and told Esther what Mordecai had said. 10 Then Esther told him to give this answer to Mordecai: 11 "All the king's officials and the people in his lands know the rule. Anyone who goes to the king in the inner room without being called must die. Only if the king holds out his gold scepter will that person live. But I have not been called to go to the king for thirty days."

Mordecai's Answer

12 Esther's words were told to Mordecai. 13 He sent back this answer: "Do not think you will escape in the king's house while all the other Jews die. 14 If you stay quiet now, help and rescue will come to the Jews from somewhere else. But you and your family will die. Who knows? Maybe you became queen for a time like this."

Esther's Brave Plan

15 Then Esther sent this reply to Mordecai: 16 "Go and gather all the Jews in Susa. Fast for me. Do not eat or drink for three days, night or day. I and my helpers will fast too. Then I will go to the king, even though it is against the rule. If I die, I die." 17 So Mordecai went away and did everything Esther had told him.
