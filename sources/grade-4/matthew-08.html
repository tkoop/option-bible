Jesus Heals a Man with a Skin Disease

1 When Jesus came down from the mountain, many people followed Him. 2 A man with a skin disease came to Him. He knelt down and said, "Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean."

3 Jesus reached out His hand and touched the man. He said, "I am willing. Be clean!" Right away the man's skin disease was gone.

4 Jesus said to him, "Do not tell anyone. But go and show yourself to the priest. Offer the gift that Moses told you to bring. This will be proof for them."

A Roman Officer Trusts Jesus

5 When Jesus went into Capernaum, a Roman army officer came to Him. He asked Jesus to help. 6 He said, "Lord, my servant is at home in bed. He cannot move, and he is in great pain."

7 Jesus said, "I will come and heal him."

8 The officer answered, "Lord, I am not good enough for You to come into my house. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed."

9 He said, "I am a man under orders too. I have soldiers under me. I tell one, 'Go,' and he goes. I tell another, 'Come,' and he comes. I tell my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

10 Jesus was amazed when He heard this. He said to the people following Him, "I tell you the truth. I have not found faith like this in anyone in Israel. 11 I tell you, many people will come from east and west. They will sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in God's kingdom in heaven. 12 But some people who should be in the kingdom will be thrown out into the dark. There they will cry and grind their teeth."

13 Jesus said to the officer, "Go home. It will happen just as you believed." And his servant was healed that same hour.

Jesus Heals Peter's Mother-in-Law and Others

14 Jesus went to Peter's house. He saw Peter's mother-in-law in bed with a fever. 15 Jesus touched her hand. The fever left her. She got up and served Him.

16 That evening, people brought many who had evil spirits. Jesus spoke, and the spirits left. He healed all who were sick.

17 This happened to make true what the prophet Isaiah said: "He took our weaknesses and carried our sicknesses."

Following Jesus

18 Jesus saw the crowd around Him. He told His followers to go to the other side of the sea. 19 A teacher of the law came to Him. He said, "Teacher, I will follow You wherever You go."

20 Jesus said, "Foxes have holes to live in. Birds have nests. But the Son of Man has no place to rest His head."

21 Another follower said to Him, "Lord, first let me go and bury my father."

22 Jesus said, "Follow Me. Let others take care of burying the dead."

Jesus Stops a Storm

23 Jesus got into a boat. His followers went with Him. 24 A big storm came up on the sea. The boat was covered by waves. But Jesus was sleeping.

25 The followers came to Him and woke Him up. They said, "Lord, save us! We are dying!"

26 Jesus said, "Why are you afraid? You have so little faith!" Then He got up and told the wind and the sea to stop. The sea became very calm.

27 The men were amazed. They said, "What kind of man is this? Even the wind and the sea obey Him!"

Jesus Heals Two Men with Evil Spirits

28 Jesus came to the other side of the sea, to the land of the Gadarenes. Two men with evil spirits came out of the tombs to meet Him. They were so wild that no one could pass that way.

29 They shouted, "What do You want with us, Son of God? Have You come here to hurt us before it is time?"

30 Far away, a large herd of pigs was eating. 31 The evil spirits begged Him, "If You make us leave, send us into the herd of pigs."

32 Jesus said, "Go!" The spirits left and went into the pigs. The whole herd ran down the hill into the sea and died in the water.

33 The men who were watching the pigs ran away. They went to the town and told everything. They told about the men with evil spirits too.

34 The whole town came out to meet Jesus. When they saw Him, they begged Him to leave their area.
