Joahaz Becomes King

1 The people of the land took Josiah’s son Jehoahaz. They made him king in Jerusalem after his father died. 2 Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for three months. 3 The king of Egypt took him off the throne. He made the land pay a big tax: one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold. 4 Then Neco, king of Egypt, made Jehoahaz’s brother Eliakim king over Judah and Jerusalem. He changed his name to Jehoiakim. Neco took Jehoahaz to Egypt.

Jehoiakim Rules in Judah

5 Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for eleven years. He did bad things that the LORD his God did not like. 6 Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, attacked him. He tied him up with bronze chains and took him to Babylon. 7 Nebuchadnezzar took some special things from the LORD’s house. He brought them to Babylon and put them in his own temple. 8 The rest of what Jehoiakim did is written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. This includes the bad things he did and what was found against him. His son Jehoiachin became king after him.

Jehoiachin and Zedekiah Rule

9 Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for three months and ten days. He did bad things that the LORD did not like. 10 Later, King Nebuchadnezzar sent for him. He brought him to Babylon with the valuable things from the LORD’s house. He made Zedekiah, Jehoiachin’s brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem. 11 Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for eleven years. 12 He did bad things that the LORD his God did not like. He would not show respect to Jeremiah the prophet. Jeremiah spoke the LORD’s words. 13 He also turned against King Nebuchadnezzar. Nebuchadnezzar had made him promise by God to be faithful. But Zedekiah was stubborn. He made his heart hard. He would not turn back to the LORD, the God of Israel.

Jerusalem Is Destroyed

14 All the leaders of the priests and the people also did more and more wrong. They followed the bad things other nations did. They made the LORD’s house dirty. The LORD had set it apart in Jerusalem. 15 The LORD, the God of their fathers, kept sending them messages through His messengers. He did this because He cared about His people and His home. 16 But they made fun of God’s messengers. They hated His words. They laughed at His prophets. The LORD’s anger grew against His people. At last there was no way to fix it.

17 So He brought the king of Babylon against them. The king killed their young men with swords in the holy house. He did not spare young men or young women. He did not spare old people or very old people. God gave all of them to him. 18 He took to Babylon all the things, big and small, from God’s house. He took the treasures of the LORD’s house. He took the king’s treasures and the treasures of his leaders. 19 They burned down God’s house. They broke down Jerusalem’s wall. They burned all its palaces. They destroyed everything of value. 20 The people who did not die by the sword were taken to Babylon. Nebuchadnezzar made them his servants. They served him and his sons until the kingdom of Persia became strong. 21 The land finally got its rest. All the time it was empty, it rested. This lasted until seventy years were done. It happened just as the LORD had said through Jeremiah.

Cyrus Lets the People Go Home

22 In the first year of Cyrus, king of Persia, the LORD’s word spoken by Jeremiah came true. The LORD moved Cyrus, king of Persia, to act. Cyrus sent a message through all his kingdom. He also wrote it down. 23 “This is what Cyrus, king of Persia, says: ‘The LORD, the God of heaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth. He has chosen me to build Him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever among you belongs to His people, may the LORD his God be with him. Let him go to Jerusalem.’”
