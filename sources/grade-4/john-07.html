Jesus Walks in Galilee

1 After this, Jesus walked in Galilee. He did not want to walk in Judea, because the Jewish leaders wanted to kill Him. 2 Now the Jewish Festival of Shelters was near. 3 So His brothers said to Him, "Leave here and go to Judea. Then your followers can see the things you do. 4 No one does a thing in secret if he wants people to know him. If you do these things, show yourself to the world." 5 His brothers did not believe in Him. 6 Jesus said to them, "My time has not come yet. But your time is always ready. 7 The world cannot hate you, but it hates Me. It hates Me because I tell it that its works are bad. 8 You go up to the festival. I am not going up to this festival yet, because My time is not full." 9 After He said this, He stayed in Galilee.

Jesus Goes to the Festival

10 But when His brothers went up to the festival, He went up too. He did not go openly. He went in secret. 11 The Jewish leaders looked for Him at the festival. They said, "Where is He?"

12 The crowd talked a lot about Him. Some said, "He is good."

Others said, "No, He leads the crowd the wrong way." 13 But no one spoke openly about Him. They were afraid of the Jewish leaders.

Jesus Teaches in the Temple

14 When the festival was about half over, Jesus went up to the temple. He taught there. 15 The Jewish leaders were amazed. They said, "How does this man know so much? He has not studied."

16 Jesus answered them, "My teaching is not Mine. It comes from the One who sent Me. 17 If anyone wants to do God's will, he will know if My teaching comes from God or if I speak on My own. 18 A person who speaks on his own wants his own praise. But a person who wants praise for the One who sent him is true. There is no wrong in him."

19 "Did not Moses give you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why do you want to kill Me?"

20 The crowd answered, "You have a demon! Who wants to kill you?"

21 Jesus answered them, "I did one thing, and all of you are amazed. 22 That is why Moses gave you circumcision. Circumcision did not start with Moses. It came from the fathers. And you circumcise a man on the Sabbath. 23 If a man is circumcised on the Sabbath so the law of Moses is not broken, why are you angry at Me? I made a whole man well on the Sabbath. 24 Do not judge by how things look. Judge with right judgment."

Is Jesus the Christ?

25 Some people from Jerusalem said, "Is this not the man they want to kill? 26 Look! He speaks openly, and they say nothing to Him. Have the rulers really decided that this is the Christ? 27 But we know where this man is from. When the Christ comes, no one will know where He is from."

28 Jesus cried out as He taught in the temple, "Yes, you know Me, and you know where I am from. But I have not come on My own. The One who sent Me is true. You do not know Him. 29 I know Him, because I am from Him, and He sent Me."

30 They tried to seize Him, but no one laid a hand on Him. His time had not come yet. 31 Many in the crowd believed in Him. They said, "When the Christ comes, will He do more signs than this man has done?"

32 The Pharisees heard the crowd talking about Him. The Pharisees and chief priests sent guards to seize Him.

Jesus Talks About Leaving

33 Jesus said, "I will be with you a little longer. Then I go to the One who sent Me. 34 You will look for Me and not find Me. Where I am, you cannot come."

35 The Jewish leaders said to each other, "Where will this man go? We will not find Him. Will He go to the Jews scattered among the Greeks and teach the Greeks? 36 What does He mean by saying, 'You will look for Me and not find Me. Where I am, you cannot come'?"

Jesus Offers Living Water

37 On the last and greatest day of the festival, Jesus stood up. He cried out, "If anyone is thirsty, come to Me and drink. 38 If anyone believes in Me, rivers of living water will flow from inside him. This is what the Bible says."

39 Jesus said this about the Spirit. People who believed in Him were going to receive the Spirit. The Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been shown His glory.

People Argue About Jesus

40 When some in the crowd heard this, they said, "This is truly the Prophet."

41 Others said, "This is the Christ."

But some said, "Does the Christ come from Galilee?" 42 "Does not the Bible say the Christ will come from David's family and from Bethlehem, the town where David was?"

43 So the crowd was split because of Him. 44 Some of them wanted to seize Him. But no one laid hands on Him.

The Guards Report Back

45 The guards went to the chief priests and Pharisees. They said, "Why did you not bring Him?"

46 The guards answered, "No man has ever spoken like this man."

47 The Pharisees answered them, "Have you been fooled too? 48 Has any ruler or Pharisee believed in Him? 49 But this crowd does not know the law. They are under a curse."

50 Nicodemus was one of them. He had gone to Jesus before. He said to them, 51 "Does our law judge a man before it hears him and knows what he does?"

52 They answered, "Are you from Galilee too? Look into it. You will see that no prophet comes from Galilee."

53 Then each person went home.
