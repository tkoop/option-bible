The LORD's Warrior from Edom

1 Who is this coming from Edom, from Bozrah, with red-stained clothes? Who is this dressed in glory, walking in His great strength? “It is I. I speak what is right. I can save.”

2 Why are Your clothes red? Why do Your robes look like someone who stomped grapes in a wine press?

3 “I stomped the grapes alone. No one from the nations helped Me. I trampled them in My anger. I crushed them in My fierce anger. Their blood splashed on My clothes. I stained all My robes. 4 I had a day of punishment in My heart. The time to rescue had come.

5 I looked, and no one helped. I was shocked that no one stood by Me. So My own power saved Me. My anger held Me up. 6 I trampled the nations in My anger. In My wrath I made them stumble, and I spilled their blood on the ground.”

Remembering the LORD's Love

7 I will remember the LORD's faithful love. I will praise Him for all He has done for us. He has done many good things for Israel's family. He did them because of His mercy and His great love.

8 He said, “They are truly My people. They are children who will not lie.” So He became their Savior.

9 In all their trouble, He was troubled too. The Angel of His Presence saved them. Because of His love and mercy, He rescued them. He lifted them up and carried them through all the old days.

10 But they rebelled. They made His Holy Spirit sad. So He turned against them and became their enemy. He fought against them Himself.

The People Remember Moses

11 Then His people remembered the old days, the days of Moses. They asked, “Where is the One who brought them up out of the sea with the shepherds of His flock? Where is the One who put His Holy Spirit among them, who sent His glorious power to lead them by Moses' right hand, who split the waters before them to make a name for Himself forever, who led them through the deep like a horse in the desert, so they did not stumble?”

12 13 14 Like cattle going down into a valley, the Spirit of the LORD gave them rest. You led Your people this way to make a glorious name for Yourself.

A Prayer for Mercy

15 Look down from heaven and see. Look from Your holy home, high and glorious. Where is Your strong love? Where is Your power? Why do You hold back Your mercy and kindness from me?

16 But You are our Father. Abraham does not know us. Israel does not recognize us. You, O the LORD, are our Father. Our Redeemer from long ago is Your name.

17 Why, O the LORD, do You let us wander from Your ways? Why do You make our hearts hard so we do not fear You? Come back for Your servants, the tribes You chose to be Yours.

18 For a little while Your people had Your holy place. But our enemies trampled Your holy place.

19 We have become like people You never ruled. We are like people not called by Your name.
