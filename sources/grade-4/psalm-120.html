A Prayer for Help

1 A song for going up. I was in trouble. I called to the LORD. He answered me. 2 The LORD, save me from lying lips and from a tricky tongue. 3 What will He give you, and what more will He do to you, you tricky tongue? 4 Sharp arrows from a strong soldier, with hot coals from a bush!

Living Far Away

5 Oh no! I live far away in Meshech. I stay with the tents of Kedar. 6 I have stayed too long with people who hate peace. 7 I want peace, but when I speak, they want war.
