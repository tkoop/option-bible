Ezekiel Sees a Vision

1 In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, I was among the people taken away by the Kebar River. The sky opened, and I saw visions from God. 2 It was the fifth day of the month. That was the fifth year after King Jehoiachin was taken away. 3 The LORD spoke to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the land of Babylon by the Kebar River. The LORD's power was on him.

Four Living Creatures

4 I looked, and I saw a strong wind coming from the north. There was a big cloud with fire flashing in it. Bright light shone all around it. In the middle of the fire, it looked like glowing metal. 5 In the middle of the cloud, I saw four living creatures. They looked like people. 6 Each one had four faces and four wings. 7 Their legs were straight. Their feet were like a calf's feet. They shone like shiny metal. 8 They had human hands under their wings on all four sides. All four of them had faces and wings. 9 Their wings touched each other. They did not turn when they moved. Each one went straight ahead. 10 Each one had a human face. On the right was a lion's face. On the left was an ox's face. Each one also had an eagle's face. 11 Their wings spread out above. Two wings touched the one next to it. Two wings covered their bodies. 12 Each one went straight ahead. They went where the Spirit wanted them to go. They did not turn as they moved. 13 The living creatures looked like burning coals of fire. They moved back and forth like torches. Bright lightning came out of the fire. 14 The creatures moved quickly back and forth, like flashes of lightning.

Wheels Beside the Creatures

15 As I looked at the creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground beside each one. Each wheel faced all four ways. 16 The wheels looked like shiny stone. All four wheels looked the same. It was like a wheel inside a wheel. 17 They could move in any of the four directions. They did not turn as they moved. 18 The edges of the wheels were tall and frightening. They were full of eyes all around. 19 When the creatures moved, the wheels moved with them. When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose too. 20 The creatures went where the Spirit wanted to go. The wheels went with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels. 21 When the creatures moved, the wheels moved. When they stopped, the wheels stopped. When they rose from the ground, the wheels rose too, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

The Sky and the Throne

22 Over the heads of the creatures was something like a great, scary sky. It shone like ice. 23 Under the sky, their wings were straight. Each one had two wings touching the one next to it, and two wings covering its body. 24 When they moved, I heard the sound of their wings. It sounded like rushing water. It sounded like the voice of God. It was a loud noise, like an army camp. When they stopped, they lowered their wings. 25 A voice came from above the sky over their heads. When they stopped, they lowered their wings. 26 Above the sky over their heads was something that looked like a throne. It looked like a blue stone. On the throne was a figure that looked like a man. 27 From His waist up, He looked like glowing metal, like fire with light all around. From His waist down, He looked like fire with light all around. 28 He looked like a rainbow in the clouds on a rainy day. This was the glory of the LORD. I saw it, and I bowed down to the ground. Then I heard a voice speaking.
