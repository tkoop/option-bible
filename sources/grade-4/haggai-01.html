The LORD Speaks to the Leaders

1 On the first day of the sixth month, in the second year that Darius was king, the the LORD spoke through the prophet Haggai. He spoke to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, the leader of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

2 The LORD All-Powerful says, "These people say, 'Now is not the time to build the house of the the LORD.'"

3 Then the the LORD spoke again through Haggai the prophet.

Are You Living in Fancy Houses?

4 The LORD asked, "Is it time for you to live in nice houses while My house is still in ruins? 5 Think about how you are living. 6 You planted a lot, but you picked a little. You eat, but you are still hungry. You drink, but you are still thirsty. You wear clothes, but you are still cold. You earn money, but it goes into a bag with holes. 7 Think about how you are living. 8 Go up the mountain, bring wood, and build My house. Then I will be happy with it, and I will be honored, says the the LORD. 9 You hoped for a lot, but you got a little. I blew away what you brought home. Why? Because My house is still in ruins, while each of you runs to his own house. 10 So, because of you, I stopped the dew from the sky, and the ground stopped giving crops. 11 I called for dry weather on the land and the hills, on the grain, the new wine, the olive oil, and everything the ground grows. I called it on people, animals, and all your hard work."

The People Obey the LORD

12 Then Zerubbabel, Joshua, and all the rest of the people listened to the the LORD their God. They also listened to Haggai the prophet, because the the LORD their God had sent him. And the people respected the the LORD.

The LORD Is With You

13 Then Haggai, the the LORD's messenger, gave the the LORD's message to the people: "I am with you," says the the LORD.

The People Start to Build

14 So the the LORD gave Zerubbabel, Joshua, and all the rest of the people new energy. They came and worked on the house of the the LORD All-Powerful, their God. 15 They started on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year that Darius was king.
