God Shows Who Should Be Priest

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

2 "Speak to the people of Israel. Ask each family leader to give you a stick. There will be twelve sticks, one for each tribe. Write each man's name on his stick. 3 Write Aaron's name on the stick for the family of Levi. Each family leader gets one stick. 4 Put the sticks in the special tent where I meet with you. 5 The stick of the man I choose will grow. Then I will stop the people of Israel from complaining about you. They complain about you and Aaron."

6 Moses told the people of Israel. Each leader gave a stick. There were twelve sticks. Aaron's stick was with them. 7 Moses put the sticks in front of the LORD in the tent.

8 The next day, Moses went into the tent. Aaron's stick for the family of Levi had grown. It had buds, flowers, and ripe almonds. 9 Moses brought all the sticks out to the people. They looked at them. Each man took his own stick.

10 The LORD said to Moses, "Put Aaron's stick back in the tent. Keep it there as a sign for the rebels. Then their complaining against Me will stop. You can end their complaining, and they will not die."

11 Moses did what the LORD said.

The People Are Afraid

12 The people of Israel said to Moses, "We are dying! We are lost! All of us are lost!" 13 "Anyone who comes near the tent of the LORD will die. Will all of us die?"
