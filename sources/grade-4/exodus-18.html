Jethro Comes to Moses

1 Jethro was a priest in Midian. He was Moses' father-in-law. He heard all that God had done for Moses and for His people, Israel. He heard that the LORD had brought Israel out of Egypt. 2 Moses had sent his wife, Zipporah, away. Now Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, took her back. 3 He also took her two sons. One son was named Gershom. Moses had said, "I have lived as a stranger in a foreign land." 4 The other son was named Eliezer. Moses had said, "The God of my father helped me. He saved me from Pharaoh's sword." 5 Jethro came to Moses in the desert. He brought Moses' wife and sons. Moses was camped at God's mountain. 6 Jethro sent a message to Moses. He said, "I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you. Your wife and her two sons are with me." 7 Moses went out to meet his father-in-law. He bowed down and kissed him. They greeted each other. Then they went into the tent.

Jethro Praises God

8 Moses told his father-in-law all that the LORD had done to Pharaoh and Egypt for Israel. He told about the hard times they had on the way. He told how the LORD saved them. 9 Jethro was glad for all the good things the LORD had done for Israel. The LORD had saved them from Egypt. 10 Jethro said, "Praise be to the LORD. He saved you from Egypt and from Pharaoh. He saved the people from Egypt. 11 Now I know that the LORD is greater than all other gods. He did this when they were proud and mean to Israel." 12 Then Jethro brought a burnt offering and other gifts to God. Aaron and all the leaders of Israel came. They ate a meal with Moses' father-in-law in front of God.

Moses Helps the People

13 The next day, Moses sat to settle problems for the people. They stood around him from morning until night. 14 His father-in-law saw all that Moses was doing. He asked, "What are you doing for the people? Why do you sit alone? All the people stand around you from morning until night." 15 Moses said to his father-in-law, "The people come to me to find out what God wants. 16 When they have a problem, they come to me. I decide who is right. I tell them God's rules and laws."

Jethro Gives Advice

17 Moses' father-in-law said, "What you are doing is not good. 18 You and the people who come to you will get too tired. The work is too hard for you. You cannot do it alone. 19 Listen to me. I will give you advice. God will be with you. You should stand for the people before God. Bring their problems to Him. 20 Teach them God's rules and laws. Show them how to live and what work to do. 21 Choose good men from all the people. Choose men who fear God. Choose men who tell the truth and hate taking money to do wrong. Put them over the people. Make them leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 22 Let them settle problems for the people all the time. Let them bring the big problems to you. Let them settle the small problems themselves. This will make your work lighter. They will help carry the load. 23 If you do this, and if God tells you to, you can stand strong. All the people can go home in peace."

Moses Follows the Advice

24 Moses listened to his father-in-law. He did everything Jethro said. 25 Moses chose good men from all Israel. He made them leaders over the people. They were leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 26 They settled problems for the people all the time. They brought the hard problems to Moses. They settled the small problems themselves. 27 Then Moses sent his father-in-law away. Jethro went back to his own land.
