The People Listed in Israel

1 All the people of Israel were listed by family. Their names were written in the book of the kings of Israel. Judah was taken to Babylon because they did wrong. 2 The first people to live again in their own towns were Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants. 3 Some people from Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem.

Families from Judah and Benjamin

4 Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, came from Perez, who was a son of Judah. 5 From the people of Shiloh came Asaiah, the firstborn son, and his sons. 6 From the family of Zerah came Jeuel and his relatives. There were 690 of them. 7 From Benjamin came Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah. 8 Also Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah. 9 These men were family leaders. They and their relatives numbered 956.

The Priests

10 From the priests came Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin. 11 Also Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub. He was the leader in God's house. 12 Also Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer. 13 They and their relatives were family leaders. There were 1,760 of them. They were strong men who did the work in God's house.

The Levites

14 From the Levites came Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah. He was from the family of Merari. 15 Also Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph. 16 Also Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah. He lived in the villages of Netophah.

The Gatekeepers

17 The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives. Shallum was the leader. 18 Until then they were at the king's gate on the east. They were gatekeepers for the Levite camps. 19 Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his relatives from the family of Korah, were in charge of the work. They guarded the entrances to the tent. Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the camp of the LORD. 20 Long ago Phinehas son of Eleazar had been their leader, and the LORD was with him. 21 Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 22 All these chosen men were gatekeepers at the doorways. There were 212 of them. They were listed by family in their villages. David and Samuel the prophet had chosen them because they could be trusted. 23 They and their sons guarded the gates of the LORD's house, the tent. 24 The gatekeepers were on the four sides: east, west, north, and south. 25 Their relatives came from their villages at set times to help them for seven days. 26 The four chief gatekeepers were Levites. They were trusted to be over the rooms and treasures of God's house. 27 They stayed around God's house because they had to guard it. They had the key and opened it every morning.

Workers in God's House

28 Some of them were in charge of the tools used in worship. They counted them when they came in and when they went out. 29 Others were in charge of the holy things, and of the flour, wine, oil, incense, and spices. 30 Some priests' sons mixed the spices. 31 Mattithiah, a Levite and firstborn son of Shallum the Korahite, was trusted to make the flat bread. 32 Some people from the family of Kohath, their relatives, were in charge of the bread set out each Sabbath.

The Singers

33 The singers, leaders of the Levites, stayed in the rooms and were free from other work, because they worked day and night. 34 These men were leaders of the Levites by their families. They lived in Jerusalem.

Saul's Family in Gibeon

35 In Gibeon lived Jeiel, father of Gibeon. His wife's name was Maacah. 36 His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, and Nadab. 37 Also Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth. 38 Mikloth was the father of Shimeam. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem. 39 Ner was the father of Kish, Kish the father of Saul, and Saul the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 40 Jonathan's son was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal was the father of Micah. 41 Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, and Tahrea. 42 Ahaz was the father of Jarah; Jarah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri was the father of Moza. 43 Moza was the father of Binea; Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son. 44 Azel had six sons. Their names were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were Azel's sons.
