Paul Writes to Titus

1 I am Paul. I serve God. Jesus Christ sent me to tell His message. I help God’s chosen people believe. I help them know the truth. This truth helps us live for God. 2 We hope to live forever. God never lies. He promised this life long ago. 3 Now, at the right time, He has shown His word. I preach it because God our Savior told me to. 4 To Titus, my true child in our shared faith: May God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior give you grace and peace.

Rules for Church Leaders

5 I left you in Crete for a reason. You need to fix what is missing. You also need to choose church leaders in each town. Do it the way I told you. 6 A church leader must be a good man. No one should be able to blame him. He should have only one wife. His children should believe. They should not be known for wild living. They should not refuse to obey. 7 A leader cares for God’s people. So he must be blameless. He must not be proud. He must not get angry fast. He must not drink too much. He must not hit people. He must not love money in a wrong way. 8 He must welcome guests. He must love good things. He must control himself. He must do what is right. He must live for God. He must stay in control. 9 He must hold tight to the true message we teach. Then he can encourage people with good teaching. He can also show why some people are wrong.

False Teachers in Crete

10 Many people do not obey. They say empty things. They trick people. This is true of many who follow Jewish rules. 11 You must stop them from talking. They ruin whole families. They teach things they should not teach. They do it to get money in a bad way. 12 One of their own writers said, “People from Crete always lie. They act like wild animals. They are lazy and only want food.” 13 This is true. So correct them firmly. Help them have a strong faith. 14 They should not listen to Jewish stories. They should not follow rules made by people. Those rules turn people away from the truth. 15 To people who are pure, everything is pure. But to people who are dirty inside and do not believe, nothing is pure. Even their minds and hearts are dirty. 16 They say they know God. But their actions say no. They are awful. They do not obey. They are useless for any good work.
