Joseph Is Sold to Potiphar

1 Joseph was taken down to Egypt. An Egyptian named Potiphar bought him. Potiphar was an officer of Pharaoh and the captain of the guard. He bought Joseph from the Ishmaelites who had brought him there. 2 The LORD was with Joseph. Joseph became a successful man. He served in the house of his Egyptian master.

Joseph Serves in Potiphar's House

3 His master saw that the LORD was with Joseph. He also saw that the LORD made everything Joseph did turn out well. 4 Joseph found favor with his master. So his master made him his helper. Then he put Joseph in charge of his whole house. He gave Joseph control of everything he owned. 5 From the time Joseph was put in charge of the house and everything in it, the LORD blessed the Egyptian's house because of Joseph. The LORD's blessing was on everything the Egyptian owned, both inside the house and out in the fields. 6 So the master left everything he owned in Joseph's care. He did not worry about anything except the food he ate. Joseph was very handsome.

Potiphar's Wife Wants Joseph

7 After this, his master's wife looked at Joseph. She said, "Come to bed with me." 8 But Joseph refused. He said to his master's wife, "See, my master does not know what is in the house. He has put everything he has in my care. 9 No one in this house is more important than I am. He has not kept anything from me except you, because you are his wife. How could I do such an evil thing? It would be a great sin against God." 10 She kept asking Joseph day after day. But he would not listen to her. He would not sleep with her or even stay near her.

Potiphar's Wife Lies About Joseph

11 One day Joseph went into the house to do his work. None of the other men were in the house. 12 She grabbed his coat and said, "Come to bed with me." But Joseph left his coat in her hand. He ran away and went outside. 13 When she saw that he had left his coat in her hand and run outside, 14 she called the men of the house and said, "Look! My husband brought this Hebrew man here to make fun of us. He came in to sleep with me, but I screamed loudly. 15 When he heard me scream, he left his coat beside me and ran outside." 16 She kept his coat with her until his master came home.

17 Then she told her husband the same story. She said, "The Hebrew servant you brought us came in to make fun of me. 18 When I screamed, he left his coat beside me and ran outside." 19 When her husband heard what she said, "Your servant did this to me," he became angry.

Joseph Is Put in Prison

20 So his master took Joseph and put him in prison. It was the place where the king's prisoners were kept. Joseph stayed there. 21 But the LORD was with Joseph. He showed Joseph kindness. He also caused the prison guard to like Joseph. 22 The prison guard put Joseph in charge of all the prisoners. Everything they did there was done under Joseph's care. 23 The prison guard did not check on anything Joseph was in charge of. This was because the LORD was with Joseph. The LORD made everything Joseph did turn out well.
