A song about God's love and promise

1 I will sing about the LORD's love forever. With my mouth I will tell all people how You keep Your promises. 2 I have said, "Love lasts forever. You show in the sky that You keep Your promises."

3 You said, "I made a promise to My chosen one. I swore to David, My servant. 4 I will make your family last forever. I will build your throne for all time." Selah

God is great and good

5 The sky praises Your wonders, O the LORD. It praises how You keep Your promises where holy ones meet. 6 Who in the sky can be like the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD? 7 God is feared where holy ones meet. He is awesome to all around Him. 8 O the LORD God of armies, who is like You? The LORD is mighty, and how You keep Your promises is all around You.

9 You rule the wild sea. When its waves rise, You calm them. 10 You crushed the sea monster Rahab like a dead body. You scattered Your enemies with Your strong arm. 11 The sky is Yours. The earth is Yours too. You made the world and all that is in it. 12 You made the north and the south. Tabor and Hermon shout for joy at Your name. 13 Your arm is strong. Your hand is mighty. Your right hand is lifted high.

14 Doing right and being fair hold up Your throne. Love and truth go before You. 15 Happy are the people who know the joyful shout. They walk, O the LORD, in the light of Your face. 16 They are happy in Your name all day. Your goodness lifts them up. 17 You are their strength and glory. Your favor makes us strong. 18 Our shield belongs to the LORD. Our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel.

God's promise to David

19 Long ago You spoke in a vision to Your faithful people. You said, "I gave help to a strong man. I lifted up a young man from the people. 20 I found My servant David. I poured holy oil on him. 21 My hand will hold him. My arm will make him strong. 22 No enemy will trick him. No bad man will hurt him. 23 I will beat down his enemies before him. I will strike those who hate him. 24 I will keep My promises and love him. His power will grow because of My name. 25 I will put his hand over the sea. His right hand will rule the rivers."

26 "He will call to Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the Rock who saves me.' 27 I will make him My firstborn son. He will be the greatest king on earth. 28 I will keep My love for him forever. My promise to him will stand. 29 I will make his family last forever. His throne will last as long as the sky."

30 "If his sons leave My law and do not follow My rules, 31 if they break My laws and do not keep My commands, 32 then I will punish their wrong with a rod. I will hit them for their sins. 33 But I will not take My love from him. I will not stop being true. 34 I will not break My promise. I will not change what I said. 35 Once I swore by My holy name, I will not lie to David. 36 His family will last forever. His throne will stand before Me like the sun. 37 It will last like the moon, a faithful witness in the sky." Selah

But God seems to have turned away

38 But You have turned away and hated. You are angry with Your chosen king. 39 You have broken Your promise to Your servant. You threw his crown in the dust. 40 You broke down all his walls. You made his forts fall. 41 All who pass by rob him. He is a shame to his neighbors. 42 You lifted up his enemies' hands. You made all his foes glad. 43 You turned his sword back. You did not help him in war. 44 You ended his glory. You threw his throne to the ground. 45 You cut short his young days. You covered him with shame. Selah

46 How long, O the LORD? Will You hide forever? Will Your anger burn like fire? 47 Remember how short my life is. Why did You make all people for nothing? 48 What man can live and not see death? Can he save his life from the grave? Selah

49 O Lord, where is Your old love? You swore to David that You would be true. 50 Remember, O Lord, how Your servants are shamed. I carry in my heart the shame from all nations. 51 Your enemies have mocked You, O the LORD. They mocked Your chosen king.

52 Blessed be the LORD forever! Amen and Amen.
