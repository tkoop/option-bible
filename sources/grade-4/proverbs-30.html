Agur's Message

1 These are the words of Agur son of Jakeh. This is his message. The man says to Ithiel, to Ithiel and Ucal:

2 I am not as smart as other people. I do not understand like a person. 3 I did not learn wisdom. I do not know the Holy One.

4 Who went up to heaven and came back down? Who held the wind in His hands? Who wrapped the water in a cloth? Who made the whole earth? What is His name? What is His Son's name? You know this is true.

5 Every word of God is pure. He is a shield for those who trust in Him. 6 Do not add to His words. If you do, He will correct you, and you will be shown to be a liar.

Agur's Prayer

7 I ask You for two things. Please do not say no before I die. 8 Keep lies and false words far from me. Do not give me poverty or riches. Give me just enough food for each day. 9 If I have too much, I might forget You and say, "Who is the LORD?" If I am poor, I might steal and bring shame to God's name.

Be Careful with Words

10 Do not tell lies about a servant to his master. If you do, he may curse you, and you will be guilty.

Four Kinds of People

11 Some people curse their fathers and do not bless their mothers. 12 Some people think they are pure, but they are still dirty. 13 Some people look down on others with proud eyes. 14 Some people have teeth like swords and jaws like knives. They eat up poor and needy people.

Things That Are Never Full

15 The leech has two daughters. They say, "Give! Give!" Three things are never full. Four things never say, "Enough!" 16 They are the grave, a woman who cannot have a baby, dry ground that wants more water, and fire that never says, "Enough!"

A Warning for Children

17 An eye that makes fun of a father or hates to obey a mother will be picked out by ravens in the valley and eaten by young eagles.

Amazing Things

18 Three things are too amazing for me. Four things I do not understand: 19 how an eagle flies in the sky, how a snake moves on a rock, how a ship sails on the sea, and how a man loves a young woman.

A Woman Who Cheats

20 This is how a woman who cheats acts: She eats, wipes her mouth, and says, "I did nothing wrong."

Things That Shake the Earth

21 The earth shakes under three things. It cannot stand four: 22 a servant who becomes king, a foolish person who has too much food, 23 a woman who was not loved but gets married, and a servant girl who takes her boss's place.

Small but Wise

24 Four things on earth are small, but very wise. 25 Ants are not strong, but they save food for summer. 26 Small animals that live in rocks are not powerful, but they make safe homes there. 27 Grasshoppers have no king, but they march together. 28 A lizard can be caught by hand, but it lives in kings' palaces.

Proud Walkers

29 Three things walk with pride. Four things look proud when they walk: 30 a lion, the strongest animal, who does not back away from anything; 31 a rooster that walks proudly, a male goat, and a king with his army.

Stop and Think

32 If you have been foolish and proud, or if you planned to do wrong, stop talking. 33 Just as shaking milk makes butter, and twisting a nose makes it bleed, stirring up anger causes fights.
