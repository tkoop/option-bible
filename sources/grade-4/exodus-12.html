God Gives Rules for the Passover

1 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron in Egypt, 2 "This month will be the first month for you. It will be the first month of your year.

"3 Tell all the people of Israel: On the tenth day of this month, each man must take a lamb for his family. One lamb for one home. 4 If a home is too small to eat a whole lamb, the man and his nearest neighbor should share one. Count the people. Give each person enough to eat. 5 The lamb for you must be a one-year-old male with no defects. It can be a sheep or a goat.

"6 Keep it until the fourteenth day of the month. Then all the people of Israel must kill it in the evening. 7 Take some blood and put it on the two side posts and the top of the door of the house where you eat the lamb. 8 That night eat the meat roasted over fire. Eat it with bitter herbs and bread with no yeast.

"9 Do not eat it raw or boiled in water. Roast it over fire. Cook the head, legs, and inside parts too. 10 Do not leave any of it until morning. If some is left, burn it in the fire. 11 Eat it like this: Have your cloak tucked in your belt. Wear your sandals. Hold your walking stick in your hand. Eat it fast. This is the the LORD's Passover.

"12 That night I will pass through Egypt. I will kill every firstborn son and animal. I will punish all the gods of Egypt. I am the the LORD. 13 The blood will be a sign for you on the houses where you are. When I see the blood, I will pass over you. No deadly sickness will hurt you when I punish Egypt.

"14 This day will be a day for you to remember. Celebrate it as a feast to the the LORD. Celebrate it for all time, for all your families to come. 15 For seven days, eat bread with no yeast. On the first day, take all yeast out of your houses. Anyone who eats anything with yeast from the first day to the seventh day will be cut off from Israel. 16 On the first day, have a holy meeting. On the seventh day, have a holy meeting too. Do no work on those days. You may only make the food that everyone will eat.

"17 Keep the Feast of Bread with No Yeast. I brought your people out of Egypt on this very day. Keep this day forever, for all your families to come. 18 In the first month, you must eat bread with no yeast. Start on the evening of the fourteenth day. Stop on the evening of the twenty-first day.

"19 For seven days, no yeast should be found in your houses. Anyone who eats anything with yeast, whether a visitor or born in the land, will be cut off from Israel. 20 Do not eat anything with yeast in any of your homes. Eat only bread with no yeast."

Moses Tells the Leaders

21 Then Moses called the leaders of Israel and said, "Go right now. Pick lambs for your families. Kill the Passover lamb.

"22 Take a bunch of hyssop. Dip it in the blood in the bowl. Put some blood on the top and both sides of the doorframe. No one may go out of the house door until morning. 23 When the the LORD goes through the land to kill the Egyptians, He will see the blood on the top and sides of the doorframe. He will pass over that doorway. He will not let the destroyer come into your houses to hurt you. 24 Obey these rules forever. They are for you and your children.

"25 When you enter the land the the LORD will give you, as He promised, keep this special meal. 26 When your children ask, 'What does this special meal mean to you?' 27 tell them, 'This is the Passover sacrifice to the the LORD. He passed over the houses of the Israelites in Egypt. He spared our homes when He killed the Egyptians.' " Then the people bowed down and worshiped.

28 The Israelites did just what the the LORD told Moses and Aaron.

The First Passover Night

29 At midnight, the the LORD killed all the firstborn in Egypt. He killed the firstborn of Pharaoh, who sat on his throne. He killed the firstborn of the prisoner in the dungeon. He killed the firstborn of all the animals. 30 Pharaoh and all his servants and all the Egyptians got up during the night. There was loud crying in Egypt. There was not one house without someone dead.

31 During the night, Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron. He said, "Get up! Leave my people, you and the Israelites! Go worship the the LORD, just as you asked. 32 Take your sheep and cattle, as you said, and go. And bless me too."

33 The Egyptians pushed the people to leave fast. They said, "If you do not go, we will all die!" 34 So the people took dough before yeast was added. They carried their bread bowls on their shoulders. The bowls were wrapped in their clothes.

35 The Israelites did what Moses told them. They asked the Egyptians for silver things, gold things, and clothes. 36 The the LORD made the Egyptians kind to the people. They gave them what they asked for. So the Israelites took the wealth of Egypt.

37 The Israelites traveled from Rameses to Succoth. About six hundred thousand men walked, not counting women and children. 38 Many other people went with them. They also took many sheep, goats, and cattle.

39 They baked flat bread with no yeast from the dough they brought from Egypt. The dough had no yeast because they were forced to leave Egypt fast. They did not have time to make food for themselves. 40 The Israelites lived in Egypt for 430 years. 41 At the end of 430 years, on that very day, all the the LORD's armies left Egypt. 42 The the LORD watched over them that night to bring them out of Egypt. So this night is for the the LORD. Keep it for all your families to come.

Rules for Who May Eat the Passover

43 The The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Here are the rules for Passover. No foreigner may eat it. 44 Any slave you bought may eat it after you circumcise him. 45 A visitor or hired worker may not eat it. 46 It must be eaten inside one house. Do not take any meat outside the house. Do not break any of its bones.

"47 All the people of Israel must celebrate it. 48 If a foreigner living with you wants to celebrate the the LORD's Passover, all the males in his home must be circumcised. Then he may join in like a person born in the land. No one who is not circumcised may eat it. 49 The same law is for the person born in the land and for the foreigner living with you."

50 All the Israelites did just what the the LORD told Moses and Aaron. 51 On that very day, the the LORD brought the Israelites out of Egypt by their armies.
