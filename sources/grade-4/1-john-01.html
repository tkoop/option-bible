The Word That Gives Life

1 We tell about the Word that gives life. He was there from the start. We heard Him. We saw Him with our own eyes. We looked at Him, and our hands touched Him. 2 This life was shown to us. We saw it, and we tell you about it. We tell you about life that lasts forever. It was with the Father, and it was shown to us.

3 We tell you what we saw and heard. We do this so you can share life with us. We share life with the Father and with His Son, Jesus Christ. 4 We write these things so our joy will be full.

God Is Light

5 Here is the message we heard from Him and tell you: God is light. There is no darkness in Him at all.

6 If we say, "We share life with Him," but we live in darkness, we lie. We do not do what is true.

7 But if we live in the light, like He is in the light, we share life with each other. The blood of Jesus, His Son, makes us clean from all sin.

8 If we say, "We have no sin," we fool ourselves. The truth is not in us.

9 If we tell God our sins, He is faithful and fair. He will forgive our sins and make us clean from all wrong.

10 If we say, "We have not sinned," we make Him a liar. His word is not in us.
