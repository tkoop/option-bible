Safe with God

1The person who stays in the safe place of the Most High rests in the shadow of the Almighty.

2I will say to the LORD, "You are my safe place and my strong fort. You are my God, and I trust in You."

God Keeps You Safe

3He will save you from the trap of a bird catcher and from terrible sickness. 4He will cover you with His feathers. You will be safe under His wings. His truth is your shield and your armor.

5You will not be afraid of the fear of the night or of the arrow that flies by day. 6You will not fear sickness that walks in the dark or ruin that strikes at noon. 7A thousand may fall beside you, and ten thousand at your right side, but it will not come near you. 8You will only look with your eyes and see what happens to bad people.

9This is because you made the LORD your safe home. You made the Most High your safe place. 10No bad thing will happen to you. No sickness will come near your tent.

11He will tell His angels to watch over you in all your ways. 12They will lift you up in their hands so your foot does not hit a stone. 13You will walk on lions and snakes. You will step on young lions and serpents.

God's Promise

14"Because he loves Me, I will save him. Because he knows My name, I will keep him safe. 15When he calls to Me, I will answer him. I will be with him in trouble. I will rescue him and honor him. 16I will give him a long life and make him happy. I will show him how I save."
