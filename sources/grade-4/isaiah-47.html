Babylon Will Fall

"1 Go down and sit in the dust, young woman of Babylon. Sit on the ground with no throne, daughter of the Chaldeans. People will not call you gentle and soft anymore. 2 Get the millstones and grind flour. Take off your veil. Lift your skirt. Show your legs. Walk through the rivers. 3 People will see your naked body and your shame. I will punish you. I will not spare anyone."

The LORD Saves His People

4 Our Redeemer—the the LORD of Hosts is His name—the Holy One of Israel.

Babylon Will Be Punished

"5 Sit quietly and go into the dark, daughter of the Chaldeans. People will not call you the queen of kingdoms anymore. 6 I was angry with My people. I let them be hurt. I gave them to you. You showed them no mercy. You put a very heavy load on the old people. 7 You said you would be queen forever. But you did not think about these things. You did not remember what would happen to you. 8 Now listen to this, you who love comfort and sit safely. You say in your heart that you are the only one. You say you will not be a widow and will not lose your children. 9 These two things will come to you in one moment, in one day. You will lose your children, and you will become a widow. They will come on you fully, even with all your magic and strong spells. 10 You trusted in your bad ways. You said no one sees you. Your wisdom and knowledge led you the wrong way. You said in your heart that you are the only one. 11 But trouble will come on you. You will not know how to make it go away. Disaster will fall on you. You cannot pay for it. Ruin will come on you suddenly, and you will not know it. 12 Stand by your spells and all your magic. You worked hard at them since you were young. Maybe they will help you. Maybe they will scare people. 13 You are tired from all your plans. Let those people stand up and save you. They study the sky and the stars. They tell what will happen each new moon. 14 Look, they are like dry grass. Fire burns them up. They cannot save themselves from the fire. There is no coal to warm them, no fire to sit by. 15 That is what they are to you. You worked with them. They traded with you since you were young. Each one goes his own way. No one can save you."
