Israel Sins with the Moabite Women

1 While Israel stayed in Shittim, the men started to sin with Moabite women. 2 The Moabite women invited the people to eat food offered to their gods. The people ate it and bowed down to those gods. 3 So Israel started worshiping Baal Peor. The LORD became very angry with Israel.

The LORD Tells Moses to Punish the Leaders

4 The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and put them to death in the sun before the LORD. Then the LORD's anger will turn away from Israel." 5 Moses said to Israel's judges, "Each judge kill the men in his group who joined Baal Peor."

Phinehas Stops the Sickness

6 Then an Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his fellow Israelites. He did this while Moses and all of Israel watched. They were crying at the door of the meeting tent. 7 Phinehas, son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron the priest, saw this. He got up from among the people and took a spear in his hand. 8 He followed the Israelite man into the tent and stabbed both of them. He stabbed the Israelite man and the woman. Then the sickness stopped spreading among the Israelites. 9 But 24,000 people had already died from the sickness.

The LORD Praises Phinehas

10 The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 11 "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned My anger away from Israel. He was very jealous for Me, so I did not destroy all Israel in My jealousy. 12 So I am giving him My peace promise. 13 He and his children after him will always be priests, because he was jealous for his God and made things right for the Israelites."

The Names of the Two Who Died

14 The Israelite who was killed was Zimri son of Salu. He was a leader of a Simeonite family. 15 The Midianite woman who was killed was Cozbi daughter of Zur. Her father was the head of a Midianite family.

The LORD Tells Israel to Fight Midian

16 The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 17 "Treat the Midianites as enemies and attack them. They treated you like enemies. They tricked you at Peor and through Cozbi, a Midianite woman. 18 She was killed on the day of the sickness because of Peor."
