The LORD Will Punish Babylon

1 The LORD says, "I am stirring up a destroying wind against Babylon and against the people of Leb-kamai. 2 I will send strangers to Babylon. They will scatter her and empty her land. They will be all around her on the day of trouble. 3 Let the archer bend his bow. Let the soldier put on his armor. Do not spare her young men. Destroy all her army. 4 Her dead will fall in the land of the Chaldeans. Her wounded will fall in her streets. 5 Israel and Judah have not been left by their God, the LORD of Hosts. Their land is full of guilt against the Holy One of Israel."

6 Run away from Babylon! Save your lives! Do not be destroyed when she is punished. This is the time for the LORD's payback. He will pay her back.

Babylon Falls Like a Cup

7 Babylon was a gold cup in the LORD's hand. It made all the earth drunk. The nations drank its wine. So the nations went wild. 8 Babylon fell suddenly and broke. Cry for her! Bring medicine for her pain. Maybe she can be healed. 9 We tried to heal Babylon, but she could not be healed. Leave her. Let us go home. Her punishment has reached the sky. It is as high as the clouds. 10 The LORD has shown that we are right. Come, let us tell in Zion what the LORD our God has done. 11 Sharpen the arrows! Fill the arrow bags! The LORD has stirred up the kings of Media. He wants to destroy Babylon. This is the LORD's payback. It is payback for His temple. 12 Hold up a flag against Babylon's walls. Keep watch strong. Set guards. Get ready to ambush. The LORD planned this and did what He said to Babylon's people.

13 You who live by many waters and have many treasures, your time is up. Your greedy gain is measured. 14 The LORD of Hosts has made a strong promise. He said, "I will fill you with people like locusts. They will shout for victory over you."

The LORD Made Everything

15 God made the earth with His power. He set the world in place with His wisdom. He stretched out the heavens with His understanding. 16 When He thunders, water pours in the sky. He brings clouds from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain. He brings wind from His storehouses. 17 Every person is foolish and knows nothing. Every gold worker is ashamed of his idol. His metal god is a lie. It has no breath. 18 They are worthless. They are tricks. When God comes to punish, they will be destroyed. 19 Jacob's God is not like them. He made everything. Israel is His own people. His name is the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD's Hammer

20 "You are My hammer and weapon of war. With you I smash nations. With you I destroy kingdoms. 21 With you I smash horse and rider. With you I smash war cart and driver. 22 With you I smash man and woman. With you I smash old and young. With you I smash young man and young woman. 23 With you I smash shepherd and flock. With you I smash farmer and oxen. With you I smash leaders and officials. 24 Before your eyes I will pay back Babylon and all the people of Chaldea for all the bad things they did in Zion," declares the LORD.

Babylon Will Burn

25 "I am against you, you destroying mountain," declares the LORD. "You destroy the whole earth. I will stretch out My hand against you and roll you down from the rocks. I will make you a burned-up mountain. 26 No one will take a stone from you for a corner or for a foundation. You will be empty forever," declares the LORD.

Nations Will Attack Babylon

27 Hold up a flag in the land! Blow the trumpet among the nations! Get the nations ready to fight her. Call the kingdoms against her: Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz. Pick a leader against her. Bring up horses like locusts. 28 Get the nations ready to fight her, the kings of Media, their leaders and all their officials, and every land they rule. 29 The land shakes and twists. The LORD's plans against Babylon stand. He will make Babylon an empty land with no one living in it. 30 Babylon's strong men stop fighting. They stay in their safe places. Their strength is gone. They are like women. Her houses are set on fire. Her gate bars are broken. 31 One runner runs to meet another. One messenger meets another. They tell the king of Babylon that his city is taken from every side. 32 The river crossings are seized. The wet lands are burned. The soldiers are scared.

33 "For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: The daughter of Babylon is like a threshing floor when it is trampled. In a little while, her harvest time will come."

Babylon Hurt God's People

34 Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, ate us up. He crushed us. He made us like an empty jar. He swallowed us like a sea monster. He filled his stomach with our good food. He drove us out.

35 "Let the violence done to me and my body come back on Babylon," says the person who lives in Zion.

"My blood come back on the people of Chaldea," says Jerusalem.

The LORD Fights for His People

36 "So this is what the LORD says: I will plead your case. I will pay you back. I will dry up her sea and make her spring dry. 37 Babylon will become a pile of broken stones. Wild dogs will live there. People will be scared of her and mock her. No one will live there. 38 They will all roar like lions. They will growl like young lions. 39 While they are hot with wine, I will set out their feast. I will make them drunk so they are happy. Then they will sleep a sleep that never ends. They will not wake up. 40 I will bring them down like lambs to be killed, like rams with goats," declares the LORD.

Babylon Is Captured

41 Sheshach is taken! The pride of the whole earth is seized! Babylon has become a scary place to all nations. 42 The sea has come up over Babylon. She is covered by many waves. 43 Her cities are empty. They are dry and bare. No one lives in them. No person walks through them.

44 "I will punish Bel in Babylon. I will make him spit out what he swallowed. The nations will not flow to him anymore. Yes, Babylon's wall has fallen."

God's People Should Leave

45 "Come out of her, My people! Save your lives from the LORD's hot anger. 46 Do not let your heart get weak. Do not be scared by the news you hear in the land. News will come one year. More news will come the next year. There will be violence in the land. One ruler will fight another."

Babylon's False Gods Will Be Punished

47 "So days are coming when I will punish Babylon's false gods. The whole land will be put to shame. All her dead will fall inside her. 48 Then heaven and earth and everything in them will sing for joy over Babylon. Attackers will come from the north to hurt her," declares the LORD.

49 Babylon must fall because of Israel's dead. Babylon's dead have fallen because of all the earth.

Remember Jerusalem

50 You who escaped the sword, go! Do not stand still! Remember the LORD from far away. Let Jerusalem come into your mind. 51 We are ashamed because we heard insults. Shame covered our faces because strangers came into the holy places of the LORD's house.

Babylon's High Walls Will Fall

52 "So days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will punish her false gods. Hurt people will cry out in all her land. 53 Even if Babylon climbs to the sky and makes her strong place higher, attackers from Me will come to her," declares the LORD.

A Cry from Babylon

54 Listen! A cry from Babylon! Great ruin from the land of the Chaldeans! 55 The LORD is laying Babylon waste. He is destroying her loud voice. Her waves roar like many waters. The sound of their voice is loud. 56 An attacker has come against her, against Babylon. Her strong men are taken. Their bows are broken. The LORD is the God of payback. He will surely pay back.

57 "I will make her leaders and wise men drunk. I will make her helpers and strong men drunk. They will sleep a sleep that never ends and not wake up," declares the LORD, whose name is the LORD of Hosts.

58 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Babylon's wide walls will be knocked down. Her high gates will be burned with fire. People work for nothing. Nations tire themselves out for the fire."

Jeremiah Sends Seraiah to Babylon

59 This is the message Jeremiah the prophet gave to Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah. Seraiah was in charge of the resting place. He went to Babylon with Zedekiah king of Judah in the fourth year of his rule. 60 Jeremiah wrote in one book all the bad things that would come to Babylon. He wrote all these words about Babylon.

61 Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you come to Babylon, see that you read all these words. 62 Say, 'O the LORD, You have spoken about this place. You said You would cut it off, so that no person or animal will live in it. It will be empty forever.'

"When you finish reading this book, tie a stone to it and throw it into the Euphrates River. 63 64 Say, 'This is how Babylon will sink. It will not rise again, because of the trouble I am bringing on it. Its people will be worn out.' These are the words of Jeremiah."
