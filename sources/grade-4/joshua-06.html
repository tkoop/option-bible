Jericho Is Locked Up

1 Jericho was locked tight because of the Israelites. No one went out, and no one came in.

The LORD Gives a Plan

2 The LORD said to Joshua, "Look, I have given Jericho to you. I have also given you its king and its strong soldiers. 3 You must walk around the city. All the fighting men will go around the city one time. Do this for six days. 4 Seven priests will carry seven trumpets made from ram horns. They will walk in front of the ark of the the LORD. On the seventh day, you will go around the city seven times. The priests will blow the trumpets. 5 When the priests blow the trumpets for a long time, and you hear the sound, all the people will shout loudly. Then the city wall will fall down. After that, the people will go straight into the city."

Joshua Gives Orders

6 Joshua called the priests. He said, "Pick up the ark of the the LORD. Seven priests will carry seven trumpets in front of the ark of the the LORD." 7 Then he said to the people, "Go! March around the city. The armed men will go in front of the ark of the the LORD." 8 After Joshua spoke, the seven priests carried the seven trumpets. They walked ahead and blew them. The ark of the the LORD followed them. 9 The armed men walked in front of the priests who blew the trumpets. The men at the back walked behind the ark. The priests kept blowing the trumpets.

10 Joshua told the people, "Do not shout. Do not make any noise. Do not say a word until I tell you to shout. Then shout!"

The First Six Days

11 They carried the ark of the the LORD around the city one time. Then they went back to camp and stayed there for the night. 12 Joshua got up early in the morning. The priests carried the ark of the the LORD. 13 The seven priests carried the seven trumpets. They walked in front of the ark of the the LORD and blew them. The armed men walked in front of the priests. The men at the back walked behind the ark of the the LORD. The priests kept blowing the trumpets. 14 On the second day, they went around the city one time. Then they went back to camp. They did this for six days.

The Seventh Day

15 On the seventh day, they got up early at sunrise. They went around the city seven times, just as before. That day, they went around the city seven times. 16 The seventh time, the priests blew the trumpets. Joshua said to the people, "Shout! The LORD has given you the city."

Joshua's Warning

17 "The city and all in it must be destroyed completely for the the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute and all who are with her in her house will live. She hid the men we sent. 18 But you must stay away from the things that are to be destroyed. If you take any of them, you will bring trouble to all of Israel. 19 All the silver, gold, bronze, and iron things are holy to the the LORD. They will go into the treasure room of the the LORD's house."

The Walls Fall

20 The people shouted when the priests blew the trumpets. When the people heard the trumpet sound, they shouted loudly. The wall fell down. Then the people went straight into the city and took it. 21 They destroyed everything in the city. They killed men and women, young and old. They killed oxen, sheep, and donkeys with swords.

Rahab Is Saved

22 Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, "Go into the prostitute's house. Bring out the woman and all who belong to her, just as you promised her." 23 The young spies went in and brought out Rahab. They brought out her father, mother, brothers, and all who belonged to her. They brought out her whole family and left them outside Israel's camp. 24 They burned the city and everything in it. But they put the silver, gold, bronze, and iron into the treasure room of the the LORD's house. 25 Joshua let Rahab the prostitute live. He also let her father's family and all who belonged to her live. She still lives among the Israelites today. She hid the men Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

Joshua Curses Rebuilding Jericho

26 At that time Joshua made them promise. He said, "Anyone who tries to rebuild Jericho will be cursed by the the LORD. He will lose his oldest son when he lays the foundation. He will lose his youngest son when he sets up the gates." 27 The the LORD was with Joshua. People all over the land heard about him.
