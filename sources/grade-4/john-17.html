Jesus Prays for Himself

1 Jesus said these things. He looked up to heaven and said, “Father, the time has come. Make Your Son great, so Your Son can make You great. 2 You gave Him power over all people. He gives them life that lasts forever. He gives this life to all the people You gave Him. 3 This is the life that lasts forever: that they know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, the One You sent. 4 I made You great on earth. I finished the work You gave Me to do. 5 Now, Father, make Me great beside You. Give Me the greatness I had with You before the world began.

Jesus Prays for His Followers

“6 I showed Your name to the people You gave Me from the world. They were Yours. You gave them to Me. They have kept Your word. 7 Now they know that everything You gave Me comes from You. 8 I gave them the words You gave Me. They took them and knew they were true. They knew I came from You. They believed You sent Me.

“9 I pray for them. I do not pray for the world. I pray for the ones You gave Me, because they are Yours. 10 All I have is Yours, and all You have is Mine. I am made great in them. 11 I am not in the world anymore, but they are in the world. I am coming to You, Holy Father. Keep them safe by Your name, the name You gave Me. Keep them together as one, just like We are one. 12 While I was with them, I kept them and watched over them by Your name, the name You gave Me. None of them was lost, except the one who was set to be lost. This happened so God’s Word would come true. 13 Now I am coming to You. I say these things in the world so they can have My joy. I want their joy to be full.

“14 I gave them Your word. The world hated them, because they do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world. 15 I do not ask You to take them out of the world. I ask You to keep them safe from the evil one. 16 They do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world.

“17 Make them holy by Your truth. Your word is truth. 18 You sent Me into the world. I also sent them into the world. 19 I set Myself apart for them, so they can be set apart by the truth.

Jesus Prays for All Who Will Believe

“20 I do not pray for these alone. I also pray for those who will believe in Me through their words. 21 I pray that all of them may be one. Father, just as You are in Me, and I am in You, I pray that they may be in Us. Then the world will believe that You sent Me. 22 I gave them the greatness You gave Me. I did this so they may be one, just as We are one. 23 I am in them, and You are in Me. I pray they may be made complete as one. Then the world will know that You sent Me, and that You love them, just as You love Me.

“24 Father, I want the people You gave Me to be with Me where I am. I want them to see My greatness, the greatness You gave Me. You loved Me before the world began. 25 Good Father, the world did not know You. But I knew You, and these knew that You sent Me. 26 I made Your name known to them, and I will keep making it known. I do this so the love You have for Me may be in them, and I may be in them.”
