Paul Visits Jerusalem Again

1 Fourteen years later, I went up to Jerusalem again. Barnabas came with me. I took Titus too. 2 I went because God showed me to go. I told the leaders the good news I preach to non-Jews. I told it to the important men alone. I did not want my work to be for nothing. 3 Titus was with me. He was Greek. But no one made him get the Jewish mark. 4 Some false brothers sneaked in. They came to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus. They wanted to make us slaves. 5 We did not give in to them, not even for a minute. We did this so the true good news would stay with you.

The Leaders Agree with Paul

6 The men who seemed important did not add anything to my message. It does not matter who they were. God does not play favorites. 7 Instead, they saw that God gave me the job of telling the good news to non-Jews. Peter had the same job for the Jews. 8 The same God who worked through Peter for the Jews also worked through me for other nations. 9 James, Peter, and John seemed like strong leaders. They saw the gift God gave me. They shook hands with Barnabas and me. They agreed we would go to non-Jews. They would go to the Jews. 10 They only asked us to remember poor people. I was already glad to do that.

Paul Corrects Peter

11 Later, Peter came to Antioch. I stood up to him face to face. He was wrong. 12 Before some men came from James, Peter ate with non-Jews. But when those men came, he pulled back. He stayed away from them. He was afraid of the Jewish believers who wanted the Jewish mark. 13 The other Jewish believers followed him. Even Barnabas was led away by their fake acting. 14 I saw they were not living by the true good news. So I said to Peter in front of everyone, "If you are a Jew but live like a non-Jew, how can you make non-Jews live like Jews?"

We Are Made Right by Faith

15 We are Jews by birth. We are not sinners from other nations. 16 But we know that no one is made right with God by doing what the law says. We are made right by faith in Jesus Christ. So we believed in Christ Jesus. We did this so we could be made right by faith in Christ, not by doing what the law says. No one will be made right by doing what the law says. 17 We try to be made right in Christ. What if we still look like sinners? Does that mean Christ helps sin? No way! 18 If I put back what I took down, I show I break the rules. 19 The law killed my old way. Now I live for God. 20 I was nailed to the cross with Christ. I do not live anymore. Christ lives in me. The life I live now in my body, I live by faith in the Son of God. He loved me and gave Himself for me. 21 I do not throw away God's grace. If people could be made right by the law, then Christ died for nothing.
