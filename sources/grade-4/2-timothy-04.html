Paul's Charge to Timothy

1 I speak strongly before God and Christ Jesus. He will judge people who are alive and people who have died. I also speak by His coming and His kingdom. 2 Preach God's word. Be ready to speak at any time. Correct people. Warn them. Ask them to do right. Do this with patience and teaching.

3 A time will come when people will not listen to true teaching. Instead, they will want teachers who say what they like to hear. They will gather many teachers to please themselves. 4 They will stop listening to the truth. They will turn to made-up stories.

5 But you must stay calm and clear in all things. You must face hard times. Do the work of one who tells the good news. Finish the work God gave you.

Paul's Life Is Almost Over

6 I am already being poured out like an offering to God. The time for me to leave is here. 7 I have fought well. I have finished the race. I have kept believing. 8 Now a prize for doing right is waiting for me. The Lord, who judges fairly, will give it to me on that day. He will give it not only to me, but also to all who have loved His coming.

Paul Asks Timothy to Come

9 Try hard to come to me soon. 10 Demas left me because he loved this world. He went to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia. Titus went to Dalmatia. 11 Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you. He helps me a lot in my work. 12 I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

Paul's Things and His Enemies

13 When you come, bring the coat I left with Carpus at Troas. Also bring the books, and especially the papers. 14 Alexander the metal worker hurt me very much. The Lord will pay him back for what he did. 15 You should also watch out for him. He fought hard against our words.

The Lord Stood by Paul

16 The first time I spoke for myself, no one came to help me. Everyone left me. May God not blame them. 17 But the Lord stood by me and made me strong. He did this so I could tell the good news fully. All people could hear it. I was saved from great danger. 18 The Lord will save me from every evil work. He will bring me safely into His kingdom in heaven. Glory belongs to Him forever and ever. Amen.

Final Greetings

19 Say hello to Prisca and Aquila and the family of Onesiphorus. 20 Erastus stayed in Corinth. I left Trophimus sick in Miletus. 21 Try hard to come before winter. Eubulus says hello to you. Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers say hello too. 22 The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.
