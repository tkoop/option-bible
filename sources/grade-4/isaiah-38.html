Hezekiah Gets Very Sick

1 In those days, Hezekiah got very sick. The prophet Isaiah, son of Amoz, came to him and said, "This is what the the LORD says: Get your things in order, because you are going to die and will not get well." 2 Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the the LORD. 3 He said, "Please, the LORD, remember how I have lived for You with a true heart. I have done what is good in Your eyes." Then Hezekiah cried very hard.

The LORD Gives Hezekiah More Years

4 Then the word of the the LORD came to Isaiah. 5 He said, "Go and tell Hezekiah that the the LORD, the God of your father David, says: I heard your prayer. I saw your tears. I will give you fifteen more years to live. 6 I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will keep this city safe. 7 This will be a sign for you from the the LORD that the the LORD will do what He said. I will make the shadow go back ten steps on the stairway of Ahaz." 8 So the sun moved back ten steps on the stairway where it had gone down.

Hezekiah's Song After He Gets Well

9 This is what Hezekiah, king of Judah, wrote after he was sick and got well again:

10 "I said, 'In the middle of my life, I must go to the gates of the grave. I am losing the rest of my years.' 11 I said, 'I will not see the the LORD in the land of the living. I will not look at people in this world anymore.' 12 My home is taken from me, like a tent is taken down. My life is rolled up like cloth by a weaver. He cuts me off from the loom. From day to night, You bring me to an end.

13 "I waited until morning. Like a lion, He breaks all my bones. From day to night, You bring me to an end. 14 I cried out like a bird. I moaned like a dove. My eyes grew weak as I looked up. Lord, I am in trouble. Please help me. 15 What can I say? He spoke to me and did this. I will live quietly all my years because my heart is bitter.

16 "Lord, people live by these things. All these things give life to my spirit. You will make me well and let me live. 17 Look, I had bitter pain, but it brought me peace. You loved me and saved my soul from the deep pit. You threw all my sins behind Your back. 18 The grave cannot praise You. Death cannot sing to You. Those who go down to the pit cannot hope in Your truth.

19 "The living, the living, they praise You, as I do today. Fathers tell their children about Your truth. 20 The LORD saved me, so we will sing and play music all our lives in the house of the the LORD."

Isaiah Tells How to Help Hezekiah

21 Isaiah said, "Take a lump of figs and put it on the sore, and he will get well."

22 Hezekiah asked, "What sign will show that I will go up to the house of the the LORD?"
