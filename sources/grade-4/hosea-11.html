God Loved Israel Like a Child

1 "When Israel was a little child, I loved him. I called My son out of Egypt. 2 The more I called them, the more they walked away. They gave gifts to Baal. They burned smoke to idols.

God Taught Israel to Walk

3 "I taught Ephraim to walk. I held him in My arms. But he did not know I made him well. 4 I led them with kind ropes and love. I was like one who lifts a heavy load off their jaw. I bent down and fed them.

Israel Would Be Punished

5 "They will not go back to Egypt. Assyria will be their king, because they would not come back to Me. 6 A sword will sweep through their cities. It will break their gates. It will destroy them because of their bad plans. 7 My people want to turn away from Me. They call to the Most High, but He will not lift them up.

God's Heart Was Full of Love

8 "How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart turns inside Me. My love grows warm. 9 I will not pour out My hot anger. I will not destroy Ephraim again. I am God, not a man. I am the Holy One among you. I will not come in anger.

God Would Bring His People Home

10 "They will follow the the LORD. He will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come shaking from the west. 11 They will come shaking like birds from Egypt and like doves from Assyria. I will settle them in their homes. 12 Ephraim has surrounded Me with lies. Israel's people have surrounded Me with tricks. But Judah still walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One," says the the LORD.
