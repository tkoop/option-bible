God Will Make Zion Shine

1 Get up and shine, because your light has come. The LORD's glory rises on you. 2 Darkness covers the earth, and thick darkness covers the nations. But the LORD will rise on you, and His glory will shine over you. 3 Nations will come to your light. Kings will come to the bright light of your new day.

People Will Come to God's People

4 Look up and see. People gather all around and come to you. Your sons come from far away, and your daughters are carried home. 5 Then you will see and shine with joy. Your heart will pound and grow big with happiness. The riches of the sea will come to you. The wealth of the nations will come to you. 6 Camels in long lines will cover your land. Young camels from Midian and Ephah will come. All of them come from Sheba. They carry gold and sweet incense. They tell of the LORD's praise.

God Will Be Honored in His House

7 All the sheep of Kedar will gather to you. The rams of Nebaioth will serve you. They will go up on My altar and be accepted. I will honor My beautiful house. 8 Who are these who fly like clouds, like doves flying home? 9 The far islands wait for Me. The ships of Tarshish come first. They bring your sons from far away. They bring silver and gold. They come for the name of the LORD your God and for the Holy One of Israel, because He has honored you.

God Will Restore the City

10 People from other lands will build your walls. Their kings will serve you. I hit you in anger, but now I show you kindness. 11 Your gates will always be open. They will not close day or night. The wealth of the nations will come in. Their kings will be led to you. 12 Any nation or kingdom that will not serve you will be lost. It will be completely destroyed.

13 The glory of Lebanon will come to you. The cypress, elm, and boxwood trees will come together. They will make My holy place beautiful. I will honor the place where I rest My feet. 14 The sons of the people who hurt you will come and bow to you. All who looked down on you will fall facedown at your feet. They will call you the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel. 15 Even though you were left alone and hated, with no one passing by, I will make you a lasting pride. You will be a joy for every generation.

God Will Give Peace and Light

16 You will be fed by the nations and their kings. Then you will know that I am the LORD your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob. 17 Instead of bronze, I will bring gold. Instead of iron, I will bring silver. Instead of wood, I will bring bronze. Instead of stones, I will bring iron. I will make your leaders bring peace. I will make your rulers do right. 18 No more will people hear about fighting or breaking in your land. No more will they hear about ruin inside your borders. You will call your walls Salvation. You will call your gates Praise.

19 The sun will not be your light by day. The moon will not shine on you by night. The LORD will be your light forever. Your God will be your beauty. 20 Your sun will not go down. Your moon will not fade away. The LORD will be your light forever. The days of your sadness will end. 21 Then all your people will do right. They will own the land forever. They are the branch I planted. They are the work of My hands, to show My glory. 22 The smallest one will become a thousand. The little one will become a strong nation. I am the LORD. I will make it happen fast when the time is right.
