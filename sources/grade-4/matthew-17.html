Jesus Shines on the Mountain

1 Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John, the brother of James. He led them up a high mountain by themselves. 2 There Jesus changed. His face shone like the sun, and His clothes became white as light. 3 Then Moses and Elijah appeared. They were talking with Jesus.

4 Peter said to Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. If You want, I will make three tents here. One for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

5 While Peter was still talking, a bright cloud came over them. A voice from the cloud said, "This is My Son, whom I love. I am pleased with Him. Listen to Him!" 6 The disciples heard this and fell on their faces. They were very afraid.

7 Jesus came and touched them. "Get up," He said. "Do not be afraid." 8 When they looked up, they saw no one but Jesus.

9 As they went down the mountain, Jesus told them, "Do not tell anyone what you saw until the Son of Man rises from the dead."

10 The disciples asked Him, "Then why do the teachers of the law say Elijah must come first?"

11 Jesus answered, "Elijah does come. He will make all things right again. 12 But I tell you, Elijah has already come. They did not know him. They did to him what they wanted. In the same way, the Son of Man will suffer from them."

13 Then the disciples understood He was talking about John the Baptist.

Jesus Heals a Boy

14 When they came to the crowd, a man came to Jesus. He got on his knees before Him.

15 He said, "Lord, have mercy on my son. He has fits. He gets hurt a lot. He often falls into fire or into water. 16 I brought him to Your disciples, but they could not heal him."

17 Jesus answered, "You people do not believe! You are turned around. How long must I stay with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy to Me."

18 Jesus spoke sternly to the demon. It left the boy. The boy was healed right then.

19 The disciples came to Jesus alone. They asked, "Why could we not drive it out?"

20 Jesus said, "Because you have so little faith. I tell you the truth. If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you."

Jesus Tells About His Death

22 While they were together in Galilee, Jesus said to them, "The Son of Man will be handed over to people. 23 They will kill Him, and on the third day He will rise again." The disciples were very sad.

Jesus Pays the Temple Tax

24 When they came to Capernaum, the men who collected the temple tax came to Peter. They asked, "Does your Teacher pay the temple tax?"

25 "Yes," Peter said.

When Peter came into the house, Jesus spoke first. "What do you think, Simon? Do kings of the earth collect taxes from their own sons or from other people?"

26 "From other people," Peter answered.

Jesus said to him, "Then the sons are free."

27 "But we do not want to upset them. Go to the lake and throw in a hook. Take the first fish you catch. Open its mouth, and you will find a coin. Take it and give it to them for Me and for you."
