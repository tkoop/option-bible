Paul Changed His Plans

1 I decided not to come back to you in sadness. 2 If I make you sad, who can make me happy? Only the people I made sad. 3 I wrote so that when I came, I would not be sad because of the people who should make me happy. I trust all of you. I know my joy is your joy too. 4 I wrote with many tears. I was very sad and worried. I did not write to make you sad. I wrote so you would know how much I love you.

Forgive the One Who Did Wrong

5 If someone made you sad, he did not make only me sad. He made all of you sad, at least a little. I am not saying this to be too hard on you. 6 The punishment that most of you gave him is enough. 7 Now you should forgive him and comfort him. Do not let too much sadness crush him. 8 So I ask you to show him love. 9 I wrote to see if you would obey in everything. 10 If you forgive someone, I forgive him too. What I forgive, I forgive for you, with Christ watching. 11 We do this so Satan will not trick us. We know his plans.

God Opens a Door

12 When I came to Troas to tell the good news about Christ, the Lord opened a door for me. 13 But I had no peace in my spirit. I did not find Titus, my brother. So I said goodbye and went to Macedonia.

God Leads Us to Win

14 Thanks be to God! He always leads us in victory in Christ. He uses us to spread the smell of knowing Him everywhere. 15 We are like a sweet smell of Christ to God. We are that smell to people who are being saved and to people who are lost. 16 To some, it smells like death leading to death. To others, it smells like life leading to life. Who is able to do this? 17 We are not like many who sell God's word for money. We speak in Christ. We speak honestly before God. We speak as people sent by God.
