The Good News About Jesus

1 This is the start of the good news about Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

John Gets People Ready

2 The prophet Isaiah wrote, “I am sending My messenger ahead of You. He will get Your road ready. 3 A voice calls in the desert: ‘Get the Lord’s road ready. Make a straight path for Him.’”

4 John the Baptist came. He was in the desert. He told people to be baptized. This showed they were sorry for their sins. Then God would forgive them. 5 People from Jerusalem and all over Judea came to him. They said they were sorry for their sins. John baptized them in the Jordan River. 6 John wore clothes made of camel hair. He had a leather belt. He ate locusts and wild honey.

7 John told people, “Someone is coming after me. He is stronger than I am. I am not good enough to even untie His shoes. 8 I baptize you with water. But He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”

Jesus Is Baptized

9 In those days, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee. John baptized Him in the Jordan River. 10 As Jesus came up out of the water, He saw the sky open. The Holy Spirit came down on Him like a dove. 11 A voice came from heaven. It said, “You are My Son, whom I love. I am very happy with You.”

Jesus Is Tempted

12 Right away, the Holy Spirit sent Jesus into the desert. 13 He stayed there 40 days. Satan tempted Him. Wild animals were there too. Angels took care of Him.

Jesus Starts His Work

14 After John was put in jail, Jesus went to Galilee. He told people the good news from God. 15 He said, “The time has come. God’s kingdom is near. Be sorry for your sins and believe the good news.”

Jesus Calls His First Followers

16 Jesus walked by the Sea of Galilee. He saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were fishing with nets. 17 Jesus said, “Come follow Me. I will teach you to fish for people.” 18 Right away, they left their nets and followed Him. 19 Jesus walked a little farther. He saw James and his brother John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in a boat fixing their nets. 20 Jesus called them. They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men. Then they followed Jesus.

Jesus Teaches in Capernaum

21 Jesus and His friends went to Capernaum. On the Sabbath, Jesus went into the meeting place and taught. 22 The people were amazed. He taught like someone with power. He did not teach like the teachers of the law.

Jesus Forces Out an Evil Spirit

23 In the meeting place, a man with an evil spirit shouted, 24 “What do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Did you come to destroy us? I know who You are. You are the Holy One of God!”

25 Jesus said, “Be quiet! Come out of him!” 26 The evil spirit shook the man. It shouted loudly. Then it came out of him. 27 Everyone was amazed. They asked each other, “What is this? He teaches with power! He even tells evil spirits what to do, and they obey Him!” 28 Right away, news about Jesus spread all over Galilee.

Jesus Heals Simon’s Mother-in-Law

29 Jesus left the meeting place. He went with James and John to the home of Simon and Andrew. 30 Simon’s mother-in-law was in bed with a fever. They told Jesus about her. 31 He went to her. He took her hand and helped her up. The fever left her. Then she began to serve them.

Jesus Heals Many People

32 That evening, after the sun went down, people brought Jesus everyone who was sick or had evil spirits. 33 The whole town came to the door. 34 Jesus healed many sick people. He also forced out many evil spirits. He did not let the spirits talk, because they knew who He was.

Jesus Prays and Preaches

35 Very early in the morning, before daylight, Jesus got up. He went to a quiet place and prayed. 36 Simon and his friends went to look for Him. 37 They found Him and said, “Everyone is looking for You.”

38 Jesus said, “Let us go to the nearby towns. I want to preach there too. That is why I came.” 39 So He traveled all over Galilee. He preached in the meeting places and drove out evil spirits.

Jesus Heals a Man with a Skin Disease

40 A man with a skin disease came to Jesus. He begged Him. He knelt down and said, “If You want to, You can make me clean.”

41 Jesus felt sorry for him. He reached out His hand and touched him. He said, “I want to. Be clean!” 42 Right away, the skin disease left the man. 43 Then Jesus sent him away with a strong warning. 44 He said, “Do not tell anyone about this. Go show yourself to the priest. Give the offering Moses told people to give when they are made clean. This will show everyone what happened.” 45 But the man went out and told everyone. He talked about it so much that Jesus could not go into a town openly. He stayed outside in quiet places. But people still came to Him from everywhere.
