Hezekiah Gets Sick

1 In those days Hezekiah got sick and was near death. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, "Put your house in order. You are going to die, and you will not live."

2 Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD. 3 He said, "Please, the LORD, remember how I have walked before You in truth and with a whole heart. I have done what is good in Your eyes." Then Hezekiah cried and wept bitterly.

God Promises to Heal Hezekiah

4 Before Isaiah left the middle of the city, the LORD spoke to him. 5 He said, "Go back and tell Hezekiah, the leader of My people, 'This is what the LORD, the God of your father David, says: I have heard your prayer. I have seen your tears. I will heal you. On the third day you will go up to the house of the LORD. 6 I will add fifteen years to your life. I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will protect this city for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David.'"

7 Isaiah said, "Take a lump of figs." They took it and put it on the boil, and Hezekiah got well.

A Sign for Hezekiah

8 Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "What will be the sign that the LORD will heal me, and that I will go up to the house of the LORD on the third day?"

9 Isaiah said, "This is the sign from the LORD that the LORD will do what He said: The shadow will move ten steps forward, or it will go back ten steps."

10 Hezekiah said, "It is easy for the shadow to go down ten steps. Let it go back ten steps instead."

11 Isaiah the prophet prayed to the LORD, and the LORD made the shadow go back ten steps on the steps of Ahaz.

Visitors from Babylon

12 At that time, Berodach-baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, because he heard that Hezekiah had been sick.

13 Hezekiah welcomed them and showed them all his treasures: silver, gold, spices, fine oil, his whole storehouse, and everything in his kingdom. There was nothing in his house or in all his rule that he did not show them.

14 Then Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and asked, "What did these men say? Where did they come from?"

Hezekiah said, "They came from a far country, from Babylon."

15 Isaiah asked, "What did they see in your house?"

Hezekiah said, "They saw everything in my house. There was nothing in my storehouses that I did not show them."

Isaiah Warns Hezekiah

16 Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Listen to the word of the LORD. 17 The days are coming when everything in your house, and everything your fathers saved up until now, will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left, says the LORD. 18 Some of your own sons, who will be born to you, will be taken away. They will become servants in the palace of the king of Babylon."

19 Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "The word of the LORD you spoke is good. Will there not be peace and truth in my days?"

The Rest of Hezekiah's Story

20 The rest of Hezekiah's deeds, and all his power, and how he made the pool and the tunnel and brought water into the city, are written in the book of the kings of Judah.

Hezekiah Dies

21 Hezekiah died and was buried with his fathers. His son Manasseh became king after him.
