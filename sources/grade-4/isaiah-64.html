A Prayer for God to Come Down

1If only You would tear open the sky and come down! Then the mountains would shake before You. 2It would be like fire that burns dry wood. It would be like fire that makes water boil. You would make Your name known to Your enemies. People in other countries would be scared before You. 3You did amazing things we did not expect. You came down, and the mountains shook before You.

No Other God Is Like You

4No one has ever heard this. No ear has ever heard it. No eye has ever seen a God like You. You help those who wait for You.

We Have Sinned

5You welcome people who are happy to do what is right. You welcome people who remember Your ways. You were angry, and we sinned. We have kept on sinning. Can we be saved? 6We are all sinful. Even the good things we do are like dirty clothes. We all fade like a leaf. Our sins carry us away like the wind. 7No one calls out to You. No one tries to hold on to You. You turned away from us. Our sins have worn us out.

God Is Our Father

8But now, the LORD, You are our Father. We are the clay. You made us. We are all the work of Your hands. 9Do not be too angry, the LORD. Do not remember our sins forever. Look at us. We are all Your people.

God's Holy Cities Are Ruined

10Your holy cities have become empty land. Zion is empty land. Jerusalem is ruined. 11Our holy and lovely house has been burned by fire. Our fathers praised You there. All our nice things are ruined. 12After all this, the LORD, will You hold back? Will You stay quiet and punish us so much?
