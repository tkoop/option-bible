The Fool Says There Is No God

1 The foolish person says in his heart, "There is no God." They have done wrong. They do evil things. No one does good.

The LORD Looks Down from Heaven

2 The LORD looks down from heaven at people. He wants to see if any understand and seek God. 3 Everyone has turned away. Everyone has become evil. No one does good, not even one. 4 Will the people who do wrong never learn? They hurt my people without care. They do not call on the the LORD.

God Is with Those Who Do Right

5 Then they will be very afraid, because God is with those who do right. 6 You ruin the plans of poor people, but the the LORD keeps them safe. 7 Oh, I wish someone would save Israel from Zion! When the the LORD brings His people back, Jacob will be happy, and Israel will be glad.
