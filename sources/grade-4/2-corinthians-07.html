Live Clean and Holy Lives

1 So, dear friends, since we have these promises, let us clean ourselves from every bad thing that hurts our bodies and spirits. Let us live holy lives because we respect God.

Paul's Care for the Corinthians

2 Open your hearts to us. We have not hurt anyone. We have not ruined anyone. We have not cheated anyone. 3 I do not say this to blame you. I already told you that you are in our hearts. We are ready to live and die with you. 4 I speak very freely to you. I am very proud of you. I am full of comfort. I am very happy even when we have troubles.

God Brings Comfort

5 When we came to Macedonia, our bodies had no rest. We had troubles all around. People fought us from outside, and we were afraid inside. 6 But God, who comforts sad people, comforted us when Titus came. 7 God comforted us not just when Titus came. Titus also told us how you comforted him. He told us that you miss us, that you were sad, and that you care about me. This made me very happy.

Sadness That Leads to Good Change

8 Even if my letter made you sad, I do not regret it. For a little while I was sorry because my letter made you sad. But it was only for a short time. 9 Now I am happy. I am not happy because you were sad. I am happy because your sadness led you to change your hearts. You were sad in a way that pleases God. So you did not lose anything because of us. 10 Sadness that comes from God leads to a change of heart that helps you be saved. You will not be sorry for that. But sadness that comes from the world leads to death. 11 Look at what this sadness from God has done in you. It gave you a strong desire to do right. It made you want to clear your name. It made you angry at the wrong. It made you afraid. It made you miss us. It made you care about me. It made you want the wrong to be punished. In every way, you showed that you are clean in this matter. 12 So when I wrote to you, I did not write just for the person who did wrong. I did not write just for the person who was hurt. I wrote so that you could see how much you care about us, and God could see it too.

Paul's Trust in the Corinthians

13 So we are comforted. We were also very happy to see Titus so happy, because all of you made his heart feel better. 14 I told Titus good things about you, and I was not ashamed. Everything we told you was true. So what we said about you to Titus was true too. 15 He loves you even more now. He remembers how all of you obeyed. You welcomed him with respect and fear. 16 I am happy that I can trust you completely.
