Israel's Hidden Sins

1 When I wanted to heal Israel, Ephraim's sin showed. Samaria did bad things too. They told lies. A thief came in, and robbers took things outside. 2 They do not think about all their wrongs. Now their bad deeds surround them. They are right in front of Me. 3 They make the king happy with their bad deeds. They make the leaders happy with their lies. 4 They all burn with sin, like a hot oven. The baker stops tending the fire while the dough rises.

5 On our king's day, the leaders got sick from wine. The king shook hands with men who mock. 6 They get ready in their hearts like a hot oven. They lie in wait all night. In the morning, it burns like a blazing fire. 7 They are all hot like an oven. They eat up their judges. All their kings have fallen. None of them calls to Me.

8 Ephraim mixes with other nations. Ephraim is like bread cooked on only one side. 9 Strangers eat his strength, and he does not know it. Gray hair shows on him, and he does not know it. 10 Israel's pride speaks against him. Yet they do not turn back to the LORD their God. They do not look for Him, even after all this.

Foolish Help from Egypt and Assyria

11 Ephraim is like a silly dove with no sense. It calls to Egypt and goes to Assyria. 12 When they go, I will spread My net over them. I will pull them down like birds in the sky. I will punish them as their people hear.

God Warns Israel

13 Trouble for them, because they ran from Me! Ruin for them, because they turned against Me! I wanted to save them, but they tell lies about Me. 14 They do not cry to Me from their hearts. They only wail on their beds. They gather for grain and new wine, and they turn away from Me. 15 I trained and strengthened their arms, but they plan bad things against Me. 16 They turn, but not to God above. They are like a bad bow. Their leaders will fall by the sword because of their proud words. This will make them a joke in Egypt.
