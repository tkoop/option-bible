Do Not Follow False Prophets

1 If a prophet or dreamer rises among you and gives you a sign or wonder, 2 and that sign or wonder happens, he may say, "Let us go after other gods you do not know and serve them." 3 Do not listen to that prophet or dreamer. the LORD your God is testing you to see if you love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul. 4 You must follow the LORD your God and fear Him. Keep His commands and obey His voice. Serve Him and stay close to Him. 5 That prophet or dreamer must be put to death, because he spoke against the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt and saved you from slavery. He tried to lead you away from the path the LORD your God commanded you to walk. Remove the evil from among you.

Do Not Follow Family or Friends Who Lead You Away

6 If your brother, your son or daughter, your wife, or your close friend secretly tries to lead you away, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods you and your fathers do not know," 7 gods of people who live around you, from one end of the earth to the other, 8 do not give in to him or listen to him. Do not feel sorry for him. Do not spare him. Do not hide him. 9 You must kill him. You must be the first to put him to death. Then all the people must help. 10 Stone him to death, because he tried to pull you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt and out of slavery. 11 Then all Israel will hear and be afraid. They will not do such a bad thing again among you.

What to Do with a City That Turns Away

12 If you hear that in one of your cities the LORD your God gives you to live in, 13 bad men have gone out from among you and led the people of their city away, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods you do not know," 14 then check it well. Ask and search carefully. If it is true that this terrible thing happened among you, 15 you must strike the people of that city with the sword. Destroy it completely, and all that is in it, even its animals. 16 Gather all its goods in the street and burn the city and all its goods with fire as a whole offering to the LORD your God. It must be a heap of ruins forever. It must not be rebuilt. 17 Do not keep anything that was set apart to be destroyed. Then the LORD may turn from His great anger and be kind to you. He will have mercy on you and make you many, as He promised your fathers. 18 Do this if you obey the LORD your God and keep all His commands that I give you today, doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.
