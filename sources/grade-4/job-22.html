Eliphaz Speaks to Job

1 Then Eliphaz from Teman answered Job. 2 “Can a person help God? A wise person helps only himself. 3 Does the Almighty care if you are good? Does He gain if you do right? 4 Does He correct you because you fear Him? Does He take you to court for that?”

5 “Your wrongs are big. Your sins never stop. 6 You took things from your brothers for no reason. You took clothes from people who had none. 7 You gave no water to tired people. You kept bread from hungry people. 8 The strong man took the land. The important man lived there. 9 You sent widows away with nothing. You hurt children who had no father.”

10 “So traps are all around you. Fear comes fast. 11 It is dark, so you cannot see. A flood of water covers you.”

God Is High in Heaven

12 “God is high in heaven. Look at the stars. They are very high. 13 But you say, ‘What does God know? Can He judge through the dark clouds? 14 Thick clouds hide Him. He cannot see. He walks on the sky.’”

Bad Men Walked This Way Before

15 “Will you keep the old road that bad men walked? 16 They were taken away before their time. Their foundation was washed away like a river. 17 They said to God, ‘Leave us alone! What can the Almighty do to us?’ 18 Yet He filled their houses with good things. But I stay far from the plans of bad men.”

Good People Are Glad When Bad Men Fall

19 “Good people see it and are glad. Clean people laugh at them. 20 They say, ‘Our enemies are gone. Fire has eaten what was left.’”

Make Peace with God

21 “Make peace with God now. Then good things will come to you. 22 Take His teaching from His mouth. Keep His words in your heart. 23 If you come back to the Almighty, you will be built up again. Push wrong away from your tent. 24 Put gold in the dust. Put Ophir gold with the stones in the stream. 25 Then the Almighty will be your gold and your silver. 26 Then you will enjoy the Almighty. You will lift up your face to God. 27 You will pray to Him, and He will hear you. You will keep your promises. 28 You will decide something, and it will happen. Light will shine on your road. 29 When people are brought low, you will say, ‘There is a way up.’ God will save the humble person. 30 He will even save a guilty person. Yes, that person will be saved because your hands are clean.”
