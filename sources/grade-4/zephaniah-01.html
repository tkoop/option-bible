Zephaniah's Message

1 This is the message from the LORD that came to Zephaniah. He was the son of Cushi, who was the son of Gedaliah, who was the son of Amariah, who was the son of Hezekiah. This happened when Josiah, son of Amon, was king of Judah.

God Will Judge the World

2 3 The the LORD says, "I will take away everything from the earth. I will take away people and animals. I will take away the birds in the sky and the fish in the sea. I will remove the things that make people trip into sin, and I will remove bad people. I will cut people off from the earth," says the the LORD.

God Will Judge Judah

4 5 6 The the LORD says, "I will punish Judah and all who live in Jerusalem. I will remove every sign of Baal worship from this place. I will remove the names of the false priests and the other priests. I will remove people who bow on roofs to worship the stars in the sky. I will remove people who bow down and make promises to the the LORD, but also make promises to their false king. I will remove people who turn away from following the the LORD. They do not look for the the LORD or ask Him for help."

7 Be quiet before the Lord the LORD, because the Day of the the LORD is near. The the LORD has made a special meal ready. He has set it apart for His guests.

Punishment on Jerusalem

8 9 The the LORD says, "On the Day of the the LORD's sacrifice, I will punish the leaders and the king's sons. I will punish everyone who wears foreign clothes. On that day, I will punish everyone who jumps over the doorstep. They fill their master's house with hurt and lies."

10 The the LORD says, "On that day, there will be a loud cry from the Fish Gate. There will be a sad wail from the second part of the city. There will be a loud crash from the hills."

11 Cry out, people who live in the Hollow! All the traders are gone. Everyone who counted silver is cut off.

12 13 The the LORD says, "At that time, I will search Jerusalem with lamps. I will punish the men who sit still and do not care. They say in their hearts, 'The the LORD will not do anything, good or bad.' Their money will be taken. Their houses will be destroyed. They will build houses but not live in them. They will plant grape fields but not drink the wine."

The Day of the LORD Is Near

14 The great Day of the the LORD is near. It is very near and coming fast. Listen! On the Day of the the LORD, there will be a bitter cry. There, the warrior will shout.

15 16 That day is a day of anger. It is a day of trouble and pain. It is a day of ruin and empty places. It is a day of darkness and gloom. It is a day of clouds and blackness. It is a day of trumpets and battle cries. It will come against strong cities and high towers.

No One Can Escape

17 18 The the LORD says, "I will bring so much trouble on people that they will walk like blind people, because they sinned against the the LORD. Their blood will be poured out like dust. Their bodies will be like trash. Their silver and gold will not save them on the Day of the the LORD's anger. The whole earth will be burned up by the fire of His jealousy. He will quickly end the lives of everyone on earth."
