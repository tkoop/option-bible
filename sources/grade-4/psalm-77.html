A Prayer When I Am Sad

1 I cried out to God. I cried to God, and He heard me. 2 When I was in trouble, I looked for the Lord. At night I reached out my hand, and it did not get tired. My heart would not be comforted. 3 I thought about God and groaned. I was sad, and my spirit grew weak. Selah.

I Could Not Sleep

4 You kept my eyes open. I was too upset to speak. 5 I thought about the old days, the years long ago. 6 At night I remembered my song. I thought in my heart, and my spirit searched.

Did God Leave Me?

7 Will the Lord leave me forever? Will He never be kind again? 8 Has His kind love gone away forever? Has His promise ended for all time? 9 Has God forgotten to be kind? Has He stopped caring for me because He is angry? Selah.

I Will Remember God's Power

10 Then I said, "This is my sadness: the Most High God's power has changed." 11 I will remember what the LORD has done. Yes, I will remember Your wonderful works from long ago. 12 I will think about all Your work. I will talk about what You have done.

God Saved His People

13 Your way, God, is holy. Who is a great God like our God? 14 You are the God who does amazing things. You showed Your power to people everywhere. 15 You rescued Your people with Your power—the children of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

God Came in the Storm

16 The water saw You, God. The water saw You and shook. Even the deep water trembled. Selah. 17 The clouds poured down water. The sky thundered. Your lightning flashed everywhere. 18 Your thunder rolled in the storm. Your lightning lit up the world. The earth shook and trembled.

God Led His People

19 Your way was in the sea. Your path was through the deep water, but no one saw Your footprints. 20 You led Your people like sheep. You used Moses and Aaron to guide them.
