A Bad Thing in the Church

1 People say there is sexual sin among you. A man is living with his father's wife. Even people who do not know God do not do that. 2 And you are proud! You should be sad instead. You should remove the man who did this wrong thing from among you.

Paul Judges the Man

3 Even though I am not with you in person, I am with you in spirit. I have already decided what should happen to the man who did this. It is as if I were there. 4 When you meet together in the name of our Lord Jesus, and my spirit is with you, and the power of our Lord Jesus is with you, 5 give this man to Satan, so his sinful self is destroyed, but his spirit is saved on the day of the Lord.

Yeast and the Passover

6 You are proud, and that is not good. Don't you know that a little yeast makes all the dough rise? 7 Get rid of the old yeast, so you can be new dough without yeast. Christ, our Passover lamb, was sacrificed. 8 So let us celebrate, not with old yeast or yeast of hate and evil, but with bread without yeast, with truth and honesty.

Who Not to Spend Time With

9 In my letter I told you not to spend time with people who sin sexually. 10 I did not mean all sinful people in this world, or greedy people, or thieves, or people who worship false gods. If you stayed away from all of them, you would have to leave the world. 11 But now I tell you: do not spend time with anyone who says he is a believer but is sexually sinful, greedy, worships false gods, mean, drunk, or a thief. Do not even eat with that person.

Judging Inside and Outside

12 It is not my job to judge people outside the church. You should judge the people inside. 13 God judges people outside. Remove the evil person from among you.
