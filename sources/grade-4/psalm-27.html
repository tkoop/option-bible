The LORD Is My Light and My Safety

1 The LORD is my light and the One who saves me. Who should I be afraid of? The LORD is the safe place in my life. Who can scare me? 2 Bad people came close to hurt me. My enemies and foes tripped and fell. 3 Even if an army camps against me, I will not be afraid. Even if war comes against me, I will still trust in the LORD.

4 One thing I ask of the LORD. This is what I want: to live in the LORD's home all my days. I want to see how good the LORD is. I want to look around in His holy place. 5 On a bad day, He will hide me in His safe place. He will keep me safe under His tent. He will lift me up on a rock. 6 Now I will stand tall above my enemies all around. In His tent I will give happy sacrifices. I will sing and make music to the LORD.

A Prayer When I Am Afraid

7 Hear me, the LORD, when I call. Be kind to me and answer me. 8 My heart says, "Look for the face of the LORD." I will look for Your face, the LORD. 9 Do not hide Your face from me. Do not push away Your servant in anger. You have helped me. Do not leave me or forget me, God who saves me.

10 Even if my father and mother leave me, The LORD will take me in. 11 Teach me Your way, the LORD. Lead me on a safe, straight path because of my enemies. 12 Do not let my enemies take me. Liars have stood up against me. They breathe out harm.

Wait for the LORD

13 I believe I will see the goodness of the LORD in this life. 14 Wait for the LORD. Be strong and take heart. Wait for the LORD.
