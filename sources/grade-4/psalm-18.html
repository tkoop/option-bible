1 For the music leader. A song of David, who served the LORD. David sang these words to the LORD on the day the LORD saved him from all his enemies and from Saul. I love You, the LORD. You are my strength. 2 The LORD is my rock, my safe place, and the One who saves me. My God is my rock. I hide in Him. He is my shield and my strength. He saves me and keeps me safe. 3 I will call to the LORD. He is worth praising. Then He will save me from my enemies.

4 Death tied me up like ropes. Bad floods scared me. 5 The grave closed in on me. Death set traps for me. 6 When I was in trouble, I called to the LORD. I cried to my God for help. He heard my voice from His holy house. My cry reached His ears.

God Comes to Help

7 The earth shook and quaked. The bottoms of the mountains trembled. They shook because He was angry. 8 Smoke came from His nose. Fire came from His mouth and burned. Hot coals came from it. 9 He pushed the sky apart and came down. Dark clouds were under His feet. 10 He rode on a winged creature and flew. He flew on the wings of the wind. 11 He hid in the dark. Dark clouds covered Him. 12 Bright light shone before Him. His clouds passed by. There was hail and hot coals of fire. 13 The LORD thundered in the sky. The Most High spoke. There was hail and hot coals. 14 He shot His arrows and scattered His enemies. He sent lightning and confused them. 15 The sea pulled back. The bottom of the world showed. You, the LORD, spoke sharply. The wind from Your nose blew.

God Pulls David Out

16 He reached down from high up and took me. He pulled me out of deep water. 17 He saved me from my strong enemy. My enemies were too strong for me. 18 They came after me when I was in trouble. But the LORD helped me. 19 He brought me to a safe, wide place. He saved me because He was happy with me.

David Did Right

20 The LORD paid me back for doing right. He rewarded me for keeping my hands clean. 21 I kept the LORD's ways. I did not turn away from my God to do wrong. 22 I kept all His rules in front of me. I did not ignore His laws. 23 I was honest with Him. I stayed away from sin. 24 So the LORD paid me back for doing right. He saw that my hands were clean.

God Treats People Fairly

25 You are loyal to loyal people. You are good to people who do right. 26 You are pure to pure people. You are clever with crooked people. 27 You save people who are hurting. You bring down people who are proud. 28 You, the LORD, light my lamp. My God makes my dark place bright. 29 With You I can run through an army. With my God I can jump over a wall. 30 God's way is perfect. the LORD's words are pure. He is a shield to all who hide in Him.

God Gives David Strength

31 Who is God besides the LORD? Who is the Rock except our God? 32 God gives me strength. He makes my way safe. 33 He makes my feet like a deer's feet. He helps me stand on high places. 34 He teaches my hands to fight. My arms can bend a bow made of hard metal. 35 You gave me the shield of Your help. Your right hand holds me up. Your kindness lifts me up. 36 You made my path wide under me. My feet did not slip.

37 I chased my enemies and caught them. I did not stop until they were gone. 38 I hit them so they could not get up. They fell under my feet. 39 You gave me strength for the fight. You made my enemies fall under me. 40 You made my enemies run away. I destroyed my foes. 41 They cried for help, but no one saved them. They cried to the LORD, but He did not answer them. 42 I crushed them like dust in the wind. I threw them out like mud in the street. 43 You saved me from fighting with my own people. You made me the leader of nations. People I did not know serve me. 44 As soon as they hear me, they obey me. People from other lands obey me. 45 People from other lands give up. They come out of their forts shaking.

David Praises the LORD

46 The LORD is alive! Praise my Rock! May the God who saves me be praised! 47 He is the God who pays back my enemies. He puts nations under me. 48 He saves me from my enemies. You lift me up above my foes. You save me from cruel men. 49 So I will thank You, the LORD, among the nations. I will sing to Your name. 50 He gives great help to His king. He shows love to His chosen one, to David and his children forever.
