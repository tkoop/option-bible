Why Do People Fight God?

1 Why do the nations get so mad? Why do people make empty plans? 2 The kings of the earth stand ready, and the rulers join together. They stand against the the LORD and against His Chosen King. 3 They say, "Let us break their chains and throw off their ropes."

4 The One who sits in heaven laughs. The Lord makes fun of them. 5 Then He speaks to them in anger and scares them with His great anger.

6 He says, "I have put My King on Zion, My holy mountain."

God's Promise to His Son

7 I will tell what God decided. The the LORD said to Me, "You are My Son. Today I have become Your Father. 8 Ask Me, and I will give You the nations to own. You will own the ends of the earth. 9 You will break them with an iron rod. You will smash them like a clay pot."

A Warning to the Kings

10 So be wise, kings. Be warned, judges of the earth. 11 Serve the the LORD with fear, and be glad while trembling. 12 Honor the Son, or He may get angry and you may die on the way. His anger can burn in a moment. Happy are all who trust in Him.
