Issachar's Family

1 Issachar had four sons: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron. 2 Tola's sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel. They were leaders of their families. In David's time, Tola's family had 22,600 strong soldiers.

3 Uzzi's son was Izrahiah. Izrahiah's sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah. These five men were leaders. 4 Their families had 36,000 soldiers ready for war. They had many wives and children.

5 The other families of Issachar had 87,000 strong soldiers. All were listed by family records.

Benjamin's Family

6 Benjamin had three sons: Bela, Becher, and Jediael. 7 Bela's sons were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri. These five were family leaders. By family records, Bela's family had 22,034 strong soldiers.

8 Becher's sons were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jerimoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. 9 By family records, their family leaders had 20,200 strong soldiers.

10 Jediael's son was Bilhan. Bilhan's sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar. 11 These men were family leaders and strong soldiers. They had 17,200 men ready to fight in war.

12 Shuppim and Huppim were sons of Ir. Hushim was the son of Aher.

Naphtali's Family

13 Naphtali's sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum. They were the sons of Bilhah.

Manasseh's Family

14 Manasseh's sons were Asriel, whom his Aramean second wife bore. She also bore Machir, the father of Gilead. 15 Machir took a wife for Huppim and Shuppim. His sister's name was Maacah. Another descendant was Zelophehad, who had daughters.

16 Machir's wife Maacah had a son named Peresh. His brother was Sheresh. Sheresh's sons were Ulam and Rakem. 17 Ulam's son was Bedan. These men were from Gilead, the son of Machir and grandson of Manasseh.

18 Machir's sister Hammoleketh had sons named Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. 19 Shemida's sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

Ephraim's Family

20 Ephraim's line was Shuthelah, Bered, Tahath, Eleadah, and Tahath. 21 Zabad was his son, then Shuthelah. Ezer and Elead were also sons. Men from Gath, born in that land, killed them because they came to take their animals. 22 Ephraim was very sad for a long time. His brothers came to comfort him.

23 Later, Ephraim went to his wife. She became pregnant and had a son. He named the boy Beriah because trouble came to his home. 24 His daughter Sheerah built Lower Beth-horon, Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

25 Rephah was his son. Resheph was Rephah's son. Telah was Resheph's son. Tahan was Telah's son. 26 Ladan was Tahan's son. Ammihud was Ladan's son. Elishama was Ammihud's son. 27 Nun was Elishama's son. Joshua was Nun's son.

28 Ephraim's family lived in Bethel and its towns. To the east was Naaran. To the west were Gezer and its towns, Shechem and its towns, and Ayyah and its towns. 29 Near Manasseh's border were Beth-shean and its towns, Taanach and its towns, Megiddo and its towns, and Dor and its towns. The sons of Joseph, son of Israel, lived in these places.

Asher's Family

30 Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. Their sister was Serah. 31 Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel. Malchiel was the father of Birzaith.

32 Heber had sons named Japhlet, Shomer, and Hotham. Their sister was Shua. 33 Japhlet's sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. 34 Shemer's sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram.

35 Shemer's brother Helem had sons named Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. 36 Zophah's sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah. 37 Also Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera. 38 Jether's sons were Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. 39 Ulla's sons were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.

40 All these were sons of Asher. They were family leaders and brave soldiers. They were chief leaders. By family records, 26,000 men were ready for war.
