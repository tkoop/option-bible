A Prayer for Help

1 For the music leader. A song of David. In you, the LORD, I run for safety. Please don't let me be ashamed forever. Save me because You do what is right. 2 Listen to me quickly. Come rescue me soon. Be my strong rock. Be my safe fort. Save me. 3 You are my rock and my fort. Lead me and guide me because of who You are. 4 Keep me out of the trap they hid for me. You are my safe place. 5 I give You my life. You saved me, the LORD. You are the true God.

6 I hate people who trust in empty lies. But I trust in the LORD. 7 I will be glad and happy because of Your love. You saw my trouble. You knew I was hurting. 8 You did not let my enemy catch me. You put my feet in a big, open place.

David Is in Trouble

9 Be kind to me, the LORD. I am in trouble. My eyes hurt from crying. I am sad in my heart and body. 10 My life is full of pain. My years are full of groaning. My strength is gone because of my sin. My bones are weak. 11 All my enemies make fun of me. My neighbors are scared of me. My friends run away when they see me. People on the street avoid me. 12 People forgot me like I am dead. I feel like a broken pot.

13 I hear many people whispering, "Danger is all around!" They plan together against me. They want to kill me.

I Trust in the LORD

14 But I trust in you, the LORD. I say, "You are my God." 15 My whole life is in Your hands. Save me from my enemies. Save me from those who chase me. 16 Smile on me, Your servant. Save me because of Your love.

Stop the Wicked

17 The LORD, do not let me be ashamed. I called to You. Let the bad people be ashamed. Let them be quiet in the grave. 18 Stop their lying mouths. They speak proud and mean words against good people.

God's Goodness

19 How great is Your goodness! You keep it for people who respect You. You give it to them where everyone can see, to those who hide in You. 20 You hide them in Your safe place. You keep them safe from people's evil plans. You keep them safe in Your home from mean words.

21 Praise the LORD! He did a great thing for me. He showed me His love when I was in a city under attack. 22 I was scared and said, "You have forgotten me!" But You heard my cry for help. You listened when I called.

Be Strong

23 Love the LORD, all His faithful people! The LORD keeps those who are true. But He pays back proud people in full. 24 Be strong. Take heart, all you who hope in the LORD.
