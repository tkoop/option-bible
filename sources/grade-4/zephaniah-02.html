Gather Before God's Anger

1 Gather together. Gather, people who feel no shame. 2 Do this before the command takes effect. Do this before the day blows away like straw. Do this before the LORD's hot anger comes on you. Do this before the day of the LORD comes on you. 3 Look for the LORD, all you humble people of the land who do what He says is right. Look for what is right. Try to be humble. Maybe you will be safe on the day of the LORD.

God Will Judge the Philistines

4 Gaza will be empty. Ashkelon will be ruins. Ashdod will be pushed out at noon. Ekron will be pulled up. 5 Oh no, people who live by the sea, nation of the Cherethites! The LORD's word is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines: "I will destroy you, and no one will stay." 6 The land by the sea will become fields. Shepherds will have wells. Sheep will have pens. 7 The coast will belong to the people left from Judah's family. They will feed their sheep there. In the evening they will lie down in Ashkelon's houses. For the LORD their God will care for them and bring back the ones taken away.

God Will Judge Moab and Ammon

8 "I have heard Moab make fun of My people. I have heard the Ammonites say mean things. They made fun of My people and bragged about their land." 9 "So, as sure as I live," says the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, "Moab will be like Sodom. The Ammonites will be like Gomorrah. They will be full of weeds and salt pits. They will be empty forever. My people who are left will take their things. The rest of My nation will own their land." 10 "This will happen to them because they were proud. They made fun of and bragged against the people of the LORD of Armies."

All Nations Will Worship the LORD

11 The LORD will make them afraid. He will make all the gods of the earth weak. Then every nation will bow to Him. Each person will worship in their own place, even by the far seas. 12 "You too, people of Cush, will be killed by My sword."

God Will Destroy Assyria

13 He will reach out His hand against the north. He will destroy Assyria. He will make Nineveh an empty, dry place like a desert. 14 Herds will lie down in the middle of it. Animals from many nations will be there. Owls and screech owls will sleep on its pillars. Their calls will sound from the windows. Broken stones will be in the doorways. He will show the cedar beams. 15 This is the happy city that lived without fear. It said in its heart, "I am the only one. No one else is like me." Now it is a ruin. Wild animals live there. Everyone who passes by hisses and shakes his fist.
