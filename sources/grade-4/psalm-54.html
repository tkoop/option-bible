David Asks God to Save Him

1 This song is for the music leader. Use stringed instruments. David wrote it to teach. The Ziphites went to Saul and said, "Is David not hiding with us?" Save me, O God, by Your name. Judge me by Your power.

2 Hear my prayer, O God. Listen to the words of my mouth.

3 Strangers have turned against me. Mean men want to kill me. They do not care about God.

4 God is my helper. The Lord keeps me alive.

5 He will send trouble back to my enemies. In Your truth, destroy them.

6 I will freely give You an offering. I will praise Your name, O the LORD, because it is good.

7 He has saved me from all trouble. My eyes have seen my enemies fall.
