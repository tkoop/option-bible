David Asks God for Help

1 Please listen to me, O the LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and in need. 2 Protect my life, for I am faithful to You. Save me, Your servant, who trusts in You, my God.

3 Be kind to me, O Lord, for I call to You all day long. 4 Make my heart happy, for to You, O Lord, I give my whole heart. 5 For You, O Lord, are good and ready to forgive. You are full of faithful love for all who call on You.

Listen to My Prayer

6 Listen to my prayer, O the LORD, and hear my cry for help. 7 When I am in trouble, I call on You, because You answer me.

No One Is Like God

8 No god is like You, O Lord, and no one does what You do. 9 All the nations You made will come and bow down to You, O Lord, and they will honor Your name. 10 For You are great and do amazing things. You alone are God.

Teach Me to Follow God

11 Teach me how to live, O the LORD, so I can follow Your truth. Give me a heart that is fully Yours, so I may respect Your name. 12 I will praise You, O Lord my God, with all my heart, and I will honor Your name forever. 13 For Your faithful love for me is great. You have saved me from the grave.

Help Me Against My Enemies

14 Proud people attack me, O God. A group of mean people wants to kill me. They do not care about You. 15 But You, O Lord, are kind and caring. You are not quick to get angry. You are full of faithful love and truth. 16 Please look at me and be kind to me. Give Your strength to me, Your servant, and save the son of Your servant woman. 17 Show me a sign of Your goodness, so my enemies see it and feel ashamed. This is because You, O the LORD, have helped me and comforted me.
