Praise the LORD

1 Praise the the LORD! It is good to sing praise to our God. It feels nice and right to praise Him. 2 The LORD builds Jerusalem. He brings Israel's people home. 3 He heals people with broken hearts. He bandages their hurts.

God Is Great and Good

4 He counts all the stars. He calls each one by name. 5 Our Lord is great. He is very strong. No one can measure how smart He is. 6 The LORD helps humble people. He brings down people who do wrong.

Sing and See God's Care

7 Sing to the LORD and thank Him. Play music to our God with a harp. 8 He covers the sky with clouds. He sends rain to the earth. He makes grass grow on the hills. 9 He feeds the animals. He feeds young ravens when they call for food.

What God Likes

10 He is not pleased by a horse's strength or a person's speed. 11 The LORD is happy with people who respect Him and wait for His kind love.

God Blesses Jerusalem

12 Praise the LORD, Jerusalem! Praise your God, Zion! 13 He makes the bars on your gates strong. He blesses your children inside you. 14 He gives peace inside your land. He fills you with the best wheat.

God's Word Works Fast

15 He sends His command to the earth. His word moves fast. 16 He gives snow like wool. He scatters frost like ashes. 17 He throws down hail like small rocks. Who can stand up to His cold? 18 He sends His word and melts the ice. He sends His wind, and the water flows.

God's Word to Israel

19 He tells His word to Jacob. He gives His rules and decisions to Israel. 20 He has not done this for any other nation. They do not know His rules. Praise the LORD!
