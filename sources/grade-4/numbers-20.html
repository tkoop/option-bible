The People Complain About Water

1 In the first month, all the people of Israel came to the desert of Zin. They stayed in Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried there. 2 There was no water for the people. So they came together against Moses and Aaron. 3 The people argued with Moses. They said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before the LORD! 4 Why did you bring the people of the LORD into this desert? Did you bring us here to die? We and our animals will die here. 5 Why did you bring us up from Egypt? Why did you bring us to this bad place? It is not a place with grain, figs, grapes, or pomegranates. There is no water to drink."

Moses and Aaron Ask the LORD for Help

6 Moses and Aaron left the people and went to the door of the meeting tent. They fell on their faces. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to them. 7 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 8 "Take the staff. Bring the people together. You and your brother Aaron must speak to the rock while they watch. Water will come from the rock. Give water to the people and their animals."

9 Moses took the staff from in front of the LORD, just as He had told him.

Moses Hits the Rock

10 Moses and Aaron brought the people together in front of the rock. Moses said to them, "Listen, you rebels! Must we bring water out of this rock for you?" 11 Moses lifted his hand. He hit the rock two times with his staff. A lot of water came out. The people and their animals drank.

The LORD Punishes Moses and Aaron

12 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "You did not trust Me enough to honor Me before the people of Israel. So you will not lead this people into the land I gave them." 13 These waters are called Meribah. There the people of Israel argued with the LORD. There the LORD showed how holy He is.

Israel Asks to Pass Through Edom

14 Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom. He said, "Your brother Israel says this: You know all the trouble we have had. 15 Our fathers went down to Egypt. We lived there a long time. The Egyptians were bad to us and to our fathers. 16 We cried to the LORD. He heard us. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now we are in Kadesh, a city at the edge of your land. 17 Please let us pass through your land. We will not walk through fields or vineyards. We will not drink water from wells. We will stay on the king's road. We will not turn right or left until we pass through your land."

Edom Refuses

18 But Edom said to him, "You may not pass through my land. If you do, I will come out to fight you with a sword."

19 The people of Israel said to him, "We will go up on the road. If we or our animals drink your water, we will pay for it. Just let us pass through on foot. We want nothing else."

20 But Edom said, "You may not pass through." Then Edom came out to meet them with many people and a strong army.

21 So Edom would not let Israel pass through his land. Israel turned away from him.

Aaron Dies on Mount Hor

22 All the people of Israel traveled from Kadesh. They came to Mount Hor. 23 At Mount Hor, near the border of Edom, the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said, 24 "Aaron will die and join his ancestors. He will not enter the land I gave to the people of Israel. This is because you both went against My word at the waters of Meribah. 25 Take Aaron and his son Eleazar. Bring them up Mount Hor. 26 Take Aaron's clothes off. Put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will die there."

27 Moses did what the LORD commanded. He and Aaron and Eleazar went up Mount Hor while all the people watched. 28 Moses took Aaron's clothes off. He put them on Eleazar. Aaron died there on top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down. 29 All the people saw that Aaron had died. All Israel cried for Aaron for thirty days.
