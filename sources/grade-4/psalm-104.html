Praise the LORD

1 Bless the the LORD, O my soul. O the LORD my God, You are very great. You wear glory and honor.

God Made the World

2 He wraps Himself in light like a cloak. He spreads out the sky like a tent. 3 He builds His rooms above the water. He makes the clouds His chariot. He walks on the wind. 4 He makes the wind His helpers. He makes fire His workers. 5 He set the earth on strong supports. It will never be moved.

God Set the Waters in Place

6 You covered the earth with water like a blanket. The water stood over the mountains. 7 When You spoke sharply, the water ran away. When You thundered, it hurried off. 8 The mountains rose up. The valleys sank down to the place You made for them. 9 You set a line they cannot pass. They will never cover the earth again.

God Gives Water and Food

10 He sends springs into the valleys. They flow between the mountains. 11 They give water to all wild animals. Wild donkeys drink and are not thirsty. 12 Birds build nests near them. They sing in the trees. 13 He waters the mountains from above. The earth is full of good things You make. 14 He makes grass grow for the cows. He makes plants for people to grow. Then people can get food from the earth. 15 Wine makes people happy. Oil makes faces shine. Bread keeps people strong.

God Made Homes for Animals

16 The LORD's trees get plenty of water. He planted the tall cedar trees in Lebanon. 17 Birds build nests in them. The stork makes her home in the pine trees. 18 High mountains are for wild goats. Cliffs are safe places for small rock animals.

God Made Day and Night

19 He made the moon to show the times. The sun knows when to go down. 20 You bring darkness, and night comes. Then all the forest animals come out. 21 Young lions roar for food. They look to God for what they eat. 22 The sun rises, and they go back. They lie down in their caves. 23 People go out to work. They work until evening.

God's Great Works

24 O the LORD, You have made so many things! You made them all with wisdom. The earth is full of what You make. 25 The sea is big and wide. It is full of life, more than we can count. There are big and small animals. 26 Ships sail there. You made the big sea creature to play in it.

God Feeds All Life

27 All living things look to You. You give them food at the right time. 28 When You give it, they pick it up. When You open Your hand, they have all the good they need. 29 When You hide Your face, they are scared. When You take away their breath, they die and go back to dust. 30 When You send Your Spirit, they are made. You make the earth new again.

Praise the LORD Forever

31 May the the LORD's glory last forever. May the the LORD be happy with what He made. 32 He looks at the earth, and it shakes. He touches the mountains, and they smoke. 33 I will sing to the the LORD all my life. I will praise my God as long as I live. 34 May my thoughts please Him. I will be glad in the the LORD. 35 May sinners be gone from the earth. May bad people be no more. Bless the the LORD, O my soul. Praise the the LORD!
