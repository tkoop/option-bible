God Has a Case

1 The LORD says, "Get up and speak to the mountains. Let the hills hear you." 2 Mountains, listen to the LORD's case. Strong parts of the earth, listen. The LORD has a case against His people. He will talk with Israel.

God Remembers His People

3 "My people, what did I do to you? How did I wear you out? Tell Me if I did wrong. 4 I brought you up from Egypt. I set you free from being slaves. I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam to lead you. 5 My people, remember what Balak, the king of Moab, planned. Remember what Balaam son of Beor said back to him. Remember the trip from Shittim to Gilgal. Then you will know the good things the LORD has done."

What Should I Bring?

6 "What should I bring to the LORD? How should I bow to the God of heaven? Should I bring animals burned on the altar? Should I bring calves one year old? 7 Will the LORD be happy with thousands of rams? Will He be happy with lots of oil? Should I give my first child for my wrongs? Should I give my own child for my sin?"

What God Wants

8 God has shown you, people, what is good. What does the LORD want from you? He wants you to do what is right. He wants you to love kindness. He wants you to walk with your God in a humble way.

God Speaks to the City

9 The LORD calls to the city. Wisdom respects Your name. He says, "Listen to the punishment and to the One who sent it." 10 Is there still stolen money in a bad person's house? Are there short measures that are cursed? 11 Can I say a person is good if he uses bad scales? Can I say he is good if he uses false weights? 12 The rich people in the city hurt others. The people who live there tell lies. Their words are tricky.

God Will Punish

13 "So I will punish you. I will make you empty because of your sins. 14 You will eat but not be full. You will still be hungry inside. You will try to save things, but you will lose them. What you save, I will give to the sword. 15 You will plant but not pick. You will press olives but not use the oil. You will press grapes but not drink the wine. 16 You have kept Omri's rules. You have done all the bad ways of Ahab's house. You have followed their plans. So I will make you empty. People will laugh at the people who live there. You will carry the shame of My people."
