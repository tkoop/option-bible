The LORD's Message to Israel

1 This is the heavy message the LORD gave to Israel through Malachi.

2 The LORD says, "I have loved you." But you ask, "How have You loved us?" The LORD says, "Was not Esau Jacob's brother? Yet I loved Jacob, and I hated Esau. 3 I made his mountains a wasteland, and I gave his land to wild animals."

4 Edom may say, "We have been crushed, but we will rebuild the ruins."

But the LORD Almighty says, "They may build, but I will tear it down. People will call them the Wicked Land. They are the people the LORD is angry with forever."

5 You will see this with your own eyes and say, "Great is the LORD, even beyond Israel's borders!"

The Priests Do Not Honor the LORD

6 The LORD Almighty says, "A son honors his father, and a worker honors his master. If I am a father, where is My honor? If I am a master, where is My respect?"

He says to the priests, "You show no respect for My name."

But you ask, "How have we shown no respect for Your name?"

7 They did this by offering unclean food on My altar.

But you ask, "How have we made You unclean?"

The LORD says, "You did it by saying the LORD's table is not important."

8 The LORD Almighty says, "When you offer blind animals, is that not wrong? When you offer lame or sick animals, is that not wrong? Try giving them to your governor! Would he be happy with you? Would he accept you?"

9 "Now ask God to be kind to us. You caused this. Will He accept you?" says the LORD Almighty.

10 "I wish one of you would close the doors, so you would not light useless fires on My altar. I am not happy with you," says the LORD Almighty. "I will not accept any offering from your hands."

The LORD's Name Will Be Great

11 "My name will be great among the nations. From where the sun rises to where it sets, pure incense and clean offerings will be given to My name. My name will be great among the nations," says the LORD Almighty.

12 "But you treat it as unclean. You say, 'The Lord's table is unclean, and its food is not good.'"

13 "You also say, 'What a bother!' and you look down on it," says the LORD Almighty. "When you bring stolen, lame, or sick animals and offer them, should I accept them from your hands?" says the LORD.

14 "Cursed is the person who cheats. He has a good male animal in his flock and promises to give it, but then gives a sick animal to the Lord. I am a great King," says the LORD Almighty. "My name is feared among the nations."
