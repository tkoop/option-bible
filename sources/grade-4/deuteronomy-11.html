Love and Obey the LORD

1 Love the LORD your God every day. Keep His rules and commands. 2 You need to know today that your children did not see what I am talking about. They did not see the LORD your God teach you. They did not see His great power, His strong hand, and His arm that He stretched out. 3 They did not see His signs and what He did in Egypt to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and to all his land. 4 They did not see what He did to Egypt's army, its horses, and its chariots. He made the Red Sea cover them when they chased you. The LORD destroyed them, and they are still gone today. 5 They did not see what He did for you in the desert until you came to this place. 6 They did not see what He did to Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, son of Reuben. The ground opened its mouth and swallowed them, their homes, their tents, and everything that was with them. This happened right in front of all Israel. 7 But your eyes saw all the great things the LORD did.

Keep God's Commands

8 Keep all the command I give you today. Then you will be strong. You will go in and take the land you are crossing over to take. 9 Then you will live a long time on the land. The LORD promised it to your fathers and to their children after them. It is a land with plenty of milk and honey.

The Good Land

10 The land you are going to take is not like Egypt. You came out of Egypt. There you planted seeds and watered them by foot, like a garden. 11 But the land you are crossing into is a land of hills and valleys. It drinks rain from the sky. 12 It is a land the LORD your God cares for. His eyes are on it all the time, from the start of the year to the end.

Obey and Be Blessed

13 If you listen carefully to my commands that I give you today, and love the LORD your God and serve Him with all your heart and all your soul, 14 then I will send rain on your land at the right time. I will send the early rain and the late rain. You will gather your grain, your wine, and your oil. 15 I will give grass in your field for your animals. You will eat and be full. 16 Be careful. Do not let your heart be tricked. Do not turn away and serve other gods. Do not bow down to them. 17 If you do, the LORD's anger will burn against you. He will close the sky so there is no rain. The land will not give its crops. You will soon die off the good land the LORD is giving you.

Remember God's Words

18 Keep these words of mine in your heart and in your soul. Tie them on your hand as a sign. Let them be like bands between your eyes. 19 Teach them to your children. Talk about them when you sit at home, when you walk on the road, when you lie down, and when you get up. 20 Write them on the door frames of your house and on your gates. 21 Then your days and your children's days will be many on the land. The LORD promised to give it to your fathers. They will be as long as the sky is above the earth.

God Will Help His People

22 If you are careful to keep all these things I command you to do, and love the LORD your God, walk in all His ways, and stay close to Him, 23 then the LORD will drive out all these nations in front of you. You will take over nations that are bigger and stronger than you. 24 Every place where the bottom of your foot steps will be yours. Your border will stretch from the desert to Lebanon, and from the Euphrates River to the western sea. 25 No one will be able to stand against you. The LORD your God will make all the people in the land you walk on afraid of you. This is what He promised you.

A Blessing and a Curse

26 Look, today I set before you a blessing and a curse. 27 You get the blessing if you obey the commands of the LORD your God that I give you today. 28 You get the curse if you do not obey the commands of the LORD your God. Do not turn from the way I command you today. Do not follow other gods you have not known. 29 When the LORD your God brings you into the land you are going to take, put the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal. 30 Those mountains are across the Jordan. They are west of the road, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the valley. They are near Gilgal, by the big trees of Moreh. 31 You are crossing the Jordan to go in and take the land the LORD your God is giving you. Take it and live in it. 32 Be careful to do all the rules and decisions I set before you today.
