Ananias and Sapphira

1 Now a man named Ananias and his wife Sapphira sold some land. 2 His wife knew about it. He kept back part of the money. He brought only part of it and put it at the apostles' feet.

3 Peter said, "Ananias, why did Satan fill your heart? You lied to the Holy Spirit. You kept back part of the money from the land. 4 Before it was sold, was it not yours? After it was sold, was the money not yours to use? Why did you plan this in your heart? You did not lie to people. You lied to God."

5 When Ananias heard this, he fell down and died. Great fear came on everyone who heard it. 6 The young men got up, wrapped him, carried him out, and buried him.

7 About three hours later, his wife came in. She did not know what had happened.

8 Peter asked her, "Tell me, did you sell the land for this much?"

She said, "Yes, for that much."

9 Peter said to her, "Why did you two agree to test the Spirit of the Lord? Look! The men who buried your husband are at the door. They will carry you out too."

10 Right away she fell at his feet and died. The young men came in and found her dead. They carried her out and buried her beside her husband.

11 Great fear came on the whole church and on everyone who heard these things.

The Apostles Heal Many People

12 The apostles did many amazing miracles among the people. All the believers were together in Solomon's Porch. 13 The people praised them, but no one else dared to join them.

14 More and more men and women believed and joined the Lord. 15 People brought sick people into the streets. They laid them on beds and mats. They hoped even Peter's shadow would fall on some of them as he walked by. 16 Crowds came from the towns around Jerusalem. They brought sick people and people bothered by evil spirits. All of them were healed.

The Apostles Are Put in Jail

17 The high priest and all his friends, who were Sadducees, became very jealous. They got up 18 and grabbed the apostles. They put them in the public jail.

19 But at night an angel of the Lord opened the jail doors and led them out. 20 The angel said, "Go and stand in the temple. Tell the people all the words of this life."

21 At daybreak they went into the temple and taught. The high priest and his friends came. They called the council and all the elders of Israel. They sent to the jail to bring the apostles.

22 The officers went to the jail, but they did not find them. They went back and reported, 23 "We found the jail locked tight. The guards were standing at the doors. But when we opened it, no one was inside."

24 The temple guard and the chief priests heard this. They did not know what to think. 25 Someone came and told them, "Look! The men you put in jail are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

26 Then the temple guard went with the officers. They brought the apostles without force. They were afraid the people might throw stones at them.

The Apostles Stand Before the Leaders

27 They brought them and stood them before the council. The high priest asked them, 28 "We told you not to teach in this name. But you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching. You want to blame us for this man's death."

29 Peter and the apostles answered, "We must obey God instead of people. 30 The God of our fathers raised Jesus. You killed Him by hanging Him on a tree. 31 God lifted Him up to His right hand. He made Him Leader and Savior. He gives people a chance to turn from sin and have their sins forgiven. He gives this to Israel. 32 We tell what we have seen and heard. The Holy Spirit does too. God gave Him to people who obey Him."

33 When they heard this, they were very angry. They wanted to kill the apostles.

Gamaliel Gives Advice

34 But a Pharisee named Gamaliel stood up in the council. He was a teacher of the law. All the people respected him. He told the men to go outside for a little while. 35 He said to them, "Men of Israel, be careful. Think about what you are about to do to these men. 36 Before now, Theudas rose up. He said he was someone important. About four hundred men joined him. He was killed. All who followed him scattered. They did not amount to anything. 37 After him, Judas the Galilean rose up. This was when the people were counted. He led people away after him. He died too. All who followed him scattered. 38 So now I tell you: Leave these men alone. Let them go. If this plan or work comes from people, it will fall apart. 39 But if it comes from God, you cannot stop it. You might even be found fighting against God."

40 They listened to him. They called the apostles in. They beat them. They told them not to speak in the name of Jesus. Then they let them go.

41 The apostles left the council. They were happy to suffer shame for the Name. 42 Every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not stop. They taught and preached that Jesus is the Christ.
