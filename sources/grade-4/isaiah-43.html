God Promises to Help Israel

1 But now the the LORD says this: "I made you, Jacob. I shaped you, Israel. Do not be afraid. I have saved you. I called you by your name. You are Mine.

2 "When you go through water, I will be with you. When you cross rivers, they will not sweep you away. When you walk through fire, you will not burn. The flames will not hurt you.

3 "I am the LORD, your God. I am the Holy One of Israel. I am the One who saves you. I gave Egypt to pay for you. I gave Cush and Seba in your place.

4 "You are special to Me. You are honored. I love you. I will give people in your place. I will give nations to save your life."

God Will Bring His People Home

5 "Do not be afraid. I am with you. I will bring your children from the east. I will gather you from the west.

6 "I will say to the north, 'Give them up!' I will say to the south, 'Do not hold them back!' Bring My sons from far away. Bring My daughters from the ends of the earth.

7 "I made everyone who is called by My name. I made them for My glory. I shaped them. I made them."

The LORD Is the Only God

8 "Bring out the people who are blind, even though they have eyes. Bring out the deaf, even though they have ears.

9 "All the nations come together. The peoples gather. Who among them can tell us this? Who can make us hear what will happen next? Let them bring people who saw it to prove they are right. Or let them listen and say, 'It is true.'

10 "You are My witnesses," says the LORD, "and My servant, whom I chose. I chose you so you can know Me and trust Me. Then you can understand that I am the One. No god was made before Me. No god will come after Me.

11 "I am the LORD. I am the only One who saves. There is no one else.

12 "I told you what would happen. I saved you. I made you hear it. No strange god was among you. So you are My witnesses," says the LORD, "and I am God.

13 "Even from the start, I am the One. No one can take you out of My hand. I act, and no one can stop it."

The LORD Will Save His People

14 The The LORD says this: "I am the One who saves you. I am the Holy One of Israel. For your sake, I will send to Babylon. I will bring them down. They will run away, even the Chaldeans, in their ships.

15 "I am the LORD. I am your Holy One. I made Israel. I am your King.

16 "I make a way in the sea. I make a path in deep water.

17 "I bring out chariots and horses. I bring out armies and strong men. They lie down together. They will not get up. They are put out, like a lamp."

God Makes a New Way

18 "Do not remember the old things. Do not think about things from long ago.

19 "Look, I am doing something new. It is starting now. Can you see it? I will make a road in the desert. I will make rivers in dry land.

20 "The wild animals will praise Me. Even the jackals and ostriches will praise Me. I give water in the desert. I make rivers in dry land. I do this to give drink to My people, the ones I chose.

21 "The people I made for Myself will tell about My praise."

Israel Did Not Honor God

22 "But you did not call to Me, Jacob. You grew tired of Me, Israel.

23 "You did not bring Me sheep to burn for Me. You did not honor Me with your gifts. I did not make you work hard with grain offerings. I did not tire you out with incense.

24 "You did not buy sweet cane for Me with money. You did not make Me happy with the fat from your gifts. Instead, you made Me work hard with your sins. You tired Me out with your wrongs.

25 "I am the One who wipes out your wrongs. I do it for My own sake. I will not remember your sins.

26 "Remind Me. Let us talk it over. Tell your side, so you can be right.

27 "Your first father sinned. The people who spoke for you turned against Me.

28 "So I will let the holy leaders be put to shame. I will let Jacob be destroyed. I will let Israel be shamed."
