John the Baptist Preaches

1 In those days, John the Baptist came to preach in the desert of Judea. 2 He said, "Turn from your sins, for God's kingdom in heaven is near." 3 This was the man Isaiah the prophet talked about when he said, "A voice calls out in the desert, 'Get the LORD's road ready. Make His paths straight.'"

4 John wore clothes made from camel hair. He had a leather belt around his waist. He ate bugs called locusts and wild honey.

5 People from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and the land around the Jordan River came to see him. 6 They told God they were sorry for their sins, and John baptized them in the Jordan River.

John Speaks to the Leaders

7 John saw many Pharisees and Sadducees coming to be baptized. He said to them, "You snakes! Who told you to run away from God's coming anger? 8 Do things that show you have changed your hearts. 9 Do not think you can say, 'We have Abraham as our father.' I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones. 10 The axe is already at the roots of the trees. Every tree that does not make good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire. 11 I baptize you with water to show you are sorry. But the One coming after me is stronger than I am. I am not good enough to carry His shoes. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire. 12 He holds a tool to separate grain. He will clean the place where grain is separated and gather His wheat into the barn. But He will burn the husks with fire that never goes out."

Jesus Is Baptized

13 Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River to be baptized by John. 14 John tried to stop Him. He said, "I need to be baptized by You, and do You come to me?" 15 Jesus answered, "Let it be this way now. It is right for us to do all that God wants." Then John let Him be baptized. 16 As soon as Jesus was baptized, He came up out of the water. Then the sky opened, and He saw God's Spirit coming down like a dove and landing on Him. 17 A voice from heaven said, "This is My beloved Son. I am very pleased with Him."
