About the Lord's Day

1 Now, brothers, we do not need to write to you about the times and seasons. 2 You know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night. 3 When people say, "Peace and safety," then sudden ruin will come on them, like pain comes on a woman having a baby. They will not escape.

Children of Light

4 But you, brothers, are not in the dark, so that day will not surprise you like a thief. 5 You are all children of light and children of the day. We are not children of the night or the dark. 6 So let's not sleep like the others. Let's stay awake and think clearly. 7 People who sleep, sleep at night. People who get drunk, get drunk at night. 8 But we belong to the day, so let's think clearly. Let's put on faith and love like a chest plate, and hope of being saved like a helmet.

God's Plan to Save Us

9 God did not choose us to be punished. He chose us to be saved through our Lord Jesus Christ. 10 He died for us, so whether we are awake or asleep, we will live with Him. 11 So encourage one another and help each other grow, just as you are doing.

Respect Your Leaders

12 Brothers, we ask you to respect those who work hard among you, who lead you in the Lord and teach you. 13 Think very highly of them because of their work. Live in peace with each other.

Live at Peace

14 We urge you, brothers, to warn those who are lazy, comfort those who feel sad, help those who are weak, and be patient with everyone. 15 Make sure no one pays back wrong for wrong. Always try to do good to each other and to all people.

Joy, Prayer, and Thanks

16 Be joyful always. 17 Pray all the time. 18 Give thanks for everything, because this is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus.

Test What Is Good

19 Don't put out the Spirit. 20 Don't look down on God's messages. 21 Test everything. Hold on to what is good. 22 Stay away from every kind of evil.

Final Prayer and Hello

23 May the God of peace make you completely holy. May your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept safe and without blame when our Lord Jesus Christ comes. 24 The One who called you is faithful. He will do it. 25 Brothers, pray for us. 26 Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss. 27 I ask you by the Lord to read this letter to all the brothers. 28 May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
