To the Church in Ephesus

“1 To the angel of the church in Ephesus, write: These are the words of the One who holds the seven stars in His right hand and walks among the seven gold lampstands. 2 I know your deeds, your hard work, and your staying strong. I know you cannot stand bad people. You tested those who say they are apostles but are not, and you found them to be liars. 3 You have kept going and have endured hard things for My name, and you have not grown tired. 4 But I have this against you: You have left the love you had at first.

“5 Think about how far you have fallen! Turn from your sin and do the things you did at first. If you do not turn, I will come to you and take your lampstand out of its place. 6 But you have this good thing: You hate what the Nicolaitans do, and I hate it too.

“7 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who wins, I will give the right to eat from the tree of life in God's paradise.”

To the Church in Smyrna

“8 To the angel of the church in Smyrna, write: These are the words of the One who is the First and the Last, who died and came back to life. 9 I know your trouble and your poverty, but you are rich! I know the bad things said by those who say they are Jews but are not. They are really a meeting place of Satan.

“10 Do not be afraid of what you are about to suffer. The devil will put some of you in jail to test you, and you will suffer for ten days. Be faithful even if it costs you your life, and I will give you the crown of life. 11 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who wins will not be hurt by the second death.”

To the Church in Pergamum

“12 To the angel of the church in Pergamum, write: These are the words of the One who has the sharp sword with two edges. 13 I know where you live, where Satan has his throne. Yet you hold on to My name. You did not turn away from your faith in Me, even when Antipas, My faithful witness, was killed among you, where Satan lives.

“14 But I have a few things against you: Some of you follow the teaching of Balaam. He taught Balak to lead the people of Israel into sin, so they ate food offered to idols and did sexual wrong. 15 In the same way, some of you follow the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

“16 So turn from your sin! If you do not, I will come to you soon and fight against them with the sword from My mouth. 17 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who wins, I will give some of the hidden manna. I will also give that person a white stone with a new name written on it, a name only the one who gets it knows.”

To the Church in Thyatira

“18 To the angel of the church in Thyatira, write: These are the words of the Son of God. His eyes are like fire, and His feet are like shiny bronze. 19 I know your deeds, your love, your faith, your service, and your staying strong. Your last deeds are more than your first.

“20 But I have this against you: You let that woman Jezebel stay. She calls herself a prophet, and she teaches and leads My servants into sexual wrong and eating food offered to idols. 21 I gave her time to turn from her sexual wrong, but she does not want to. 22 So I will throw her on a bed of suffering. I will make those who sin with her suffer very much, unless they turn from what she does.

“23 I will kill her children. Then all the churches will know that I am the One who looks into hearts and minds. I will pay each of you back for what you have done. 24 Now I say to the rest of you in Thyatira, to those who do not follow her teaching and have not learned Satan's so-called deep secrets: I will not put any other heavy load on you. 25 Just hold on to what you have until I come.

“26 To the one who wins and does My work to the end, I will give power over the nations. 27 That person will rule them with an iron rod and break them like clay pots, just as I received power from My Father. 28 I will also give that person the morning star. 29 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.”
