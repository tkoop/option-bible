God Rests on the Seventh Day

1 So the sky and the land and everything in them were finished. 2 By the seventh day, God had finished the work He had done. So on the seventh day He rested from all His work. 3 Then God blessed the seventh day and made it special. He did this because on that day He rested from all the work He had done to make everything.

The Story of the Garden

4 This is the story of the sky and the land when they were made. It tells how the LORD God made the earth and the sky. 5 No bushes had grown on the earth yet. No plants had come up in the fields yet. This was because the LORD God had not sent rain on the earth, and there was no man to work the ground. 6 But water came up from the earth and watered all the ground. 7 Then the LORD God made the man from dust from the ground. He breathed the breath of life into the man's nose, and the man became a living person.

8 The LORD God planted a garden in Eden, in the east. He put the man He had made there. 9 The LORD God made every tree grow from the ground. These trees were nice to look at and good to eat. In the middle of the garden were the tree of life and the tree of knowing good and bad.

10 A river flowed out of Eden to water the garden. From there it split into four rivers. 11 The first river is called Pishon. It goes through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold. 12 The gold of that land is pure. Bdellium and onyx stone are found there. 13 The second river is called Gihon. It goes through the whole land of Cush. 14 The third river is called Tigris. It flows east of Asshur. The fourth river is the Euphrates.

15 Then the LORD God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden. The man was to work it and take care of it. 16 The LORD God told the man, "You may eat from any tree in the garden. 17 But you must not eat from the tree of knowing good and bad. If you eat from it, you will die."

God Makes a Helper

18 The LORD God said, "It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper who is right for him." 19 The LORD God had made every wild animal and every bird from the ground. He brought them to the man to see what names he would give them. Whatever the man called each living thing became its name. 20 The man gave names to all the farm animals, to the birds, and to all the wild animals. But no helper was found who was right for the man.

The First Woman

21 So the LORD God made the man fall into a deep sleep. While he slept, He took one of the man's ribs and closed the place with flesh. 22 From the rib that the LORD God took from the man, He made a woman. He brought her to the man. 23 The man said, "This is someone from my own body. She will be called woman, because she was taken from man." 24 That is why a man leaves his father and mother and joins his wife. The two become one. 25 The man and his wife were both naked, and they were not ashamed.
