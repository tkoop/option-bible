Come Back to the LORD

1 "Come, let us go back to the LORD. He hurt us, but He will heal us. He hit us, but He will fix our hurts. 2 In two days, He will make us live again. On the third day, He will lift us up. Then we will live with Him. 3 Let us learn about the LORD. Let us keep trying to know Him. He will come like the sunrise. He will come to us like rain, like spring rain that wets the ground."

God Speaks to Ephraim and Judah

4 "What can I do with you, Ephraim? What can I do with you, Judah? Your love is like morning mist. It is like early dew that goes away. 5 So I spoke through My prophets. My words struck you down. My decisions came out like light.

God Wants Kindness, Not Gifts

6 "I want kindness, not sacrifices. I want people to know God, not just burnt gifts. 7 But they broke My agreement, like Adam did. They were not faithful to Me.

Bad Deeds in Israel

8 "Gilead is a town full of bad people. It is stained with blood. 9 Robbers wait to attack a man. Priests do the same. They kill people on the road to Shechem. They carry out evil plans.

Trouble for Israel and Judah

10 "I have seen a terrible thing in Israel. Ephraim acts like a prostitute. Israel is made dirty. 11 Judah, you also have a harvest coming. I will bring My people back."
