The LORD Speaks to Moses

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. 2 He said, "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When anyone sins without meaning to and breaks one of My commands, here is what must be done."

Offering for a Priest's Sin

3 If the anointed priest sins, he brings guilt on the people. He must bring a young bull to the LORD. The bull must have no defect. It is a sin offering. 4 He must bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting, before the LORD. He must lay his hand on the bull's head. Then he must kill the bull before the LORD. 5 The anointed priest must take some of the bull's blood and bring it into the tent of meeting. 6 He must dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD. He must do this in front of the curtain of the holy place. 7 He must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of sweet-smelling incense before the LORD. This altar is in the tent of meeting. He must pour the rest of the bull's blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings. That altar is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

8 He must take all the fat from the bull of the sin offering. This is the fat that covers the insides and all the fat around the insides. 9 He must also take the two kidneys and the fat on them near the lower back. He must take the fatty part over the liver, with the kidneys. He must remove these. 10 The priest must burn them on the altar of burnt offerings, just as the fat is removed from an ox in a peace offering. 11 He must take the bull's skin, all its meat, its head, its legs, its insides, and its waste. 12 He must take the whole bull outside the camp. He must take it to a clean place where the ashes are poured out. He must burn it there on a wood fire. It must be burned where the ashes are poured out.

Offering for the Whole People's Sin

13 If all the people of Israel sin without meaning to, and they do not know they sinned, and they break one of the LORD's commands, they are guilty. 14 When they learn about their sin, the people must bring a young bull as a sin offering. They must bring it to the front of the tent of meeting. 15 The leaders of the people must lay their hands on the bull's head. They must kill the bull before the LORD. 16 The anointed priest must bring some of the bull's blood into the tent of meeting. 17 He must dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD. He must do this in front of the curtain. 18 He must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar before the LORD. This altar is in the tent of meeting. He must pour the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings. That altar is at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 19 He must take all the fat from the bull and burn it on the altar.

20 He must do the same thing with this bull that he did with the sin offering bull. In this way the priest makes things right for them, and they are forgiven. 21 He must take the bull outside the camp and burn it. He must burn it the same way he burned the first bull. This is the sin offering for the whole people.

Offering for a Leader's Sin

22 If a leader sins without meaning to and breaks one of the commands of the LORD his God, he is guilty. 23 When he learns about his sin, he must bring a male goat. The goat must have no defect. 24 He must lay his hand on the goat's head. He must kill the goat where the burnt offerings are killed, before the LORD. It is a sin offering. 25 The priest must take some of the blood with his finger. He must put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings. He must pour the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offerings. 26 He must burn all the fat on the altar. He must do it the same way the fat is burned in a peace offering. In this way the priest makes things right for him, and he is forgiven.

Offering for One Person's Sin

27 If one person from the people sins without meaning to and breaks one of the LORD's commands, he is guilty. 28 When he learns about his sin, he must bring a female goat. The goat must have no defect. 29 He must lay his hand on the goat's head. He must kill the goat where the burnt offerings are killed. 30 The priest must take some of the blood with his finger. He must put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings. He must pour the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. 31 He must take all the fat away, just as the fat is taken away from a peace offering. The priest must burn it on the altar. It is a sweet smell to the LORD. In this way the priest makes things right for him, and he is forgiven.

Offering for a Lamb's Sin

32 If he brings a lamb for his sin offering, he must bring a female lamb. The lamb must have no defect. 33 He must lay his hand on the lamb's head. He must kill the lamb for a sin offering where the burnt offerings are killed. 34 The priest must take some of the blood with his finger. He must put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offerings. He must pour the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. 35 He must take all the fat away. He must do it the same way the fat is taken from a lamb in a peace offering. The priest must burn the fat on the altar with the offerings burned for the LORD. In this way the priest makes things right for him, and he is forgiven.
