Paul Did Not Use Big Words

1 Brothers, when I came to you, I did not come with fancy words or human wisdom. I told you about God's truth. 2 I decided to know only one thing among you: Jesus Christ and His death on the cross. 3 I came to you weak, scared, and shaking a lot. 4 My words and my preaching were not with smart talk. They showed the Spirit's power. 5 I did this so your faith would not rest on people's wisdom, but on God's power.

God's Secret Wisdom

6 We do speak wisdom to strong believers. But it is not the wisdom of this world. It is not the wisdom of the leaders of this world, who are fading away. 7 We speak God's secret wisdom. God hid it, but He planned it long ago for our honor. 8 None of the leaders of this world knew it. If they had known it, they would not have killed the Lord of glory. 9 But the Bible says, "No eye has seen it. No ear has heard it. No heart has dreamed it. God has made it ready for people who love Him."

The Spirit Shows Us God's Gifts

10 But God showed these things to us through the Spirit. The Spirit looks into all things, even the deep things of God. 11 Who knows what a person thinks except that person's own spirit? In the same way, no one knows God's thoughts except God's Spirit. 12 We did not get the spirit of the world. We got the Spirit from God. So we can know the good things God gave us. 13 We talk about these things with words the Spirit teaches, not words from human wisdom. We match spiritual things with spiritual things.

Spiritual People Understand

14 A person without the Spirit does not accept the things of God's Spirit. To him they seem foolish. He cannot understand them, because they are understood in a spiritual way. 15 But a spiritual person can judge all things. Yet no one judges him. 16 The Bible asks, "Who has known the Lord's mind? Who can teach Him?" But we have the mind of Christ.
