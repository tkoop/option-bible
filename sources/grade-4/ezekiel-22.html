The City of Blood

1 The word of the LORD came to me.

2 He said, “Son of man, will you judge? Will you judge the city of blood? Then show her all her terrible sins.”

3 He said, “Say this to the city:”

“This is what the Lord the LORD says: O city, you shed blood in your own city. You make idols that make you dirty.

4 You are guilty because of the blood you spilled. You are dirty because of the idols you made. Your time is up. Your day has come. So I will make you a shame to the nations and a joke to all lands.

5 Near and far lands will laugh at you. You are dirty and full of trouble.

6 Look, the leaders of Israel in you use their power to spill blood.

7 In you, people look down on fathers and mothers. They cheat strangers. They hurt orphans and widows.

8 You hate My holy things. You make My Sabbath days dirty.

9 In you, people tell lies to spill blood. In you, people eat on the hills and do bad things.

10 In you, men slept with their father's wife. In you, they slept with women during their monthly time.

11 In you, one man sleeps with his neighbor's wife. Another sleeps with his daughter-in-law. Another sleeps with his sister.

12 In you, people take money to spill blood. You charge interest and make unfair profit. You cheat your neighbors. You have forgotten Me, says the Lord the LORD.

13 Look, I am very angry about the money you made by cheating. I am angry about the blood in you.

14 Can your heart stay strong? Can your hands stay strong when I punish you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.

15 I will scatter you among the nations. I will spread you through the lands. I will take away your dirt from you.

16 You will be shamed in front of the nations. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

The Metal Furnace

17 The word of the LORD came to me.

18 He said, “Son of man, the people of Israel have become like useless metal to Me. They are all bronze, tin, iron, and lead in a furnace. They are like silver waste.”

19 “So this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because all of you became like junk, I will gather you into Jerusalem.

20 “I will gather you like silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin into a furnace. I will blow on it with fire to melt you. In My anger and wrath I will put you there and melt you.

21 “I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of My anger. You will melt inside it.

22 “You will melt like silver in a furnace. Then you will know that I, the LORD, poured out My anger on you.”

The Land Is Not Clean

23 The word of the LORD came to me.

24 He said, “Son of man, say this to the land:”

“You are a land that is not clean. No rain falls on you in your day of anger.

25 “Her prophets in her are like a roaring lion tearing its food. They eat people. They take treasure and good things. They make many widows in her.

26 “Her priests break My law and make My holy things dirty. They do not show the difference between holy and common. They do not teach clean and dirty. They forget My Sabbath days, and I am treated badly among them.

27 “Her leaders in her are like wolves tearing their food. They spill blood and kill people to make unfair money.

28 “Her prophets cover them with lies. They see false visions. They tell lies. They say, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says,’ but the LORD did not speak.

29 “The people of the land cheat others and steal. They hurt the poor and needy. They treat strangers unfairly.

30 “I looked for one man among them who would build a wall and step in for Me, to keep the land from being destroyed. But I found none.

31 “So I poured out My anger on them. I destroyed them with the fire of My wrath. I made them pay for what they did, says the Lord the LORD.”
