Job Remembers His Good Days

1 Job spoke again and said:

2 “I wish I could go back to the months before, like the days when God watched over me. 3 His lamp shone over my head, and by His light I walked through the dark. 4 I was like I was in my best days, when God was my close friend and blessed my home. 5 The Almighty was still with me, and my children were around me. 6 My path was like it was washed in butter, and rocks gave me streams of oil.

Job Was Honored by Everyone

7 “When I went out to the city gate and sat down in the square, 8 young men saw me and hid, and old men stood up and stayed standing. 9 Leaders stopped talking and put their hand over their mouth. 10 Important people stayed quiet, and their tongues stuck to the roof of their mouth. 11 When ears heard me, they called me happy. When eyes saw me, they spoke well of me.

Job Helped People in Need

12 “I saved poor people who cried for help. I helped children with no father and no helper. 13 The dying person blessed me. I made the sad widow's heart sing. 14 I put on right living like clothes. Fair choices were my robe and turban. 15 I was eyes for blind people and feet for people who could not walk. 16 I was like a father to poor people. I looked into their problems. 17 I stopped bad men and took back what they stole.

Job Thought His Good Life Would Last

18 “I thought I would die old, with my family, after many days. 19 My roots reached the water, and dew stayed on my branches all night. 20 People still honored me, and my bow was strong in my hand.

People Listened to Job

21 “People listened to me and waited. They were quiet for my advice. 22 After I spoke, they did not speak again. My words fell on them like rain. 23 They waited for me like rain. They opened their mouths wide for spring rain. 24 I laughed with them, and they could not believe it. They did not make my face sad. 25 I chose their way and sat as leader. I lived like a king among soldiers, like one who comforts sad people.”
