God Led His People Out

1 When Israel left Egypt, Jacob's family left people who spoke a different language. 2 Judah became His holy place. Israel became the land He ruled.

The Sea and Mountains Moved

3 The sea saw them and ran away. The Jordan River turned back. 4 The mountains jumped like rams. The hills jumped like lambs.

5 Sea, why do you run away? Jordan, why do you turn back? 6 Mountains, why do you jump like rams? Hills, why do you jump like lambs?

The Earth Shook Before God

7 Earth, shake with fear before the Lord! Shake before the God of Jacob! 8 He turned the rock into a pool of water. He turned the hard stone into a spring.
