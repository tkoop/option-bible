A Warning About the Desert by the Sea

1 Like strong winds in the dry south, an enemy comes from the desert. It comes from a scary land. 2 A hard message was shown to me. The traitor betrays, and the destroyer destroys. Go up, Elam! Attack Media! I will end all her crying.

3 So my body is full of pain. Pain grabs me like a woman having a baby. I am mixed up when I hear it. I am scared when I see it. 4 My heart is weak. Fear shakes me. The evening I wanted has become a fear to me.

5 Set the table. Watch the tower. Eat and drink. Rise up, leaders! Get your shields ready.

6 For this is what the Lord says to me: “Go, stand a lookout. Have him report what he sees. 7 When he sees chariots with two horses, riders on donkeys, and riders on camels, let him watch very closely.”

8 The lookout shouted, “My lord, I stand on the watchtower every day. I stay at my post every night.”

9 Then the lookout said, “Look! A chariot with men is coming, with two horses.”

Someone answered, “Babylon has fallen! It has fallen! All the statues of her gods are broken on the ground.”

10 My people, you are like grain being crushed. I tell you what I heard from the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel.

A Warning About Dumah

11 A message about Dumah: Someone calls to me from Seir, “Watchman, what is left of the night? Watchman, what is left of the night?”

12 The watchman said, “Morning is coming, but night is coming too. If you want to ask, ask. Come back again.”

A Warning About Arabia

13 A message about Arabia: In the woods of Arabia you will sleep, travelers from Dedan.

14 Bring water for the thirsty. People of Tema, give bread to the runaway. 15 They run from swords, from a sword pulled out, from a bow ready to shoot, and from the heavy trouble of war.

16 For this is what the Lord says to me: “In one year, like a worker counts his time, all the greatness of Kedar will end.” 17 Only a few of Kedar’s brave men with bows will be left. For the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.
