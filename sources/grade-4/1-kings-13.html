A Man of God Warns the King

1 Jeroboam stood by the altar to burn incense. A man of God came from Judah to Bethel because the the LORD sent him. 2 He cried out against the altar by the word of the the LORD. He said, "Altar, altar! The the LORD says this: A boy named Josiah will be born in David's family. He will burn on you the priests of the high places who burn incense on you. He will burn human bones on you." 3 That day the man of God gave a sign. He said, "This is the sign the the LORD has spoken: The altar will split apart. The ashes on it will spill out."

4 When King Jeroboam heard what the man of God said about the altar, he reached out his hand from the altar. He said, "Grab him!" But his hand dried up. He could not pull it back. 5 The altar split apart. The ashes spilled out, just as the man of God had said by the word of the the LORD.

6 The king said to the man of God, "Please ask the the LORD your God to pray for me. Ask Him to make my hand well again." So the man of God prayed to the the LORD. The king's hand became well again, like before. 7 The king said to the man of God, "Come home with me. Eat with me. I will give you a gift."

8 The man of God said to the king, "Even if you gave me half your house, I would not go with you. I will not eat bread or drink water here." 9 "The the LORD told me not to eat bread or drink water here. He told me not to go back the same way I came."

10 So he went another way. He did not go back the way he came to Bethel.

An Old Prophet Finds the Man of God

11 An old prophet lived in Bethel. His sons came and told him everything the man of God had done that day in Bethel. They told him the words he had said to the king. 12 Their father asked, "Which way did he go?" His sons showed him the way the man of God from Judah had gone.

13 The old prophet said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." They saddled it. He rode on it.

14 He went after the man of God. He found him sitting under an oak tree. He asked, "Are you the man of God who came from Judah?"

The man said, "I am."

15 The old prophet said, "Come home with me and eat bread."

16 The man of God said, "I cannot go back with you or come in with you. I will not eat bread or drink water with you here." 17 "The the LORD told me not to eat bread or drink water there. He told me not to go back by the way I came."

18 The old prophet said, "I am a prophet too, like you. An angel spoke to me by the word of the the LORD and said, 'Bring him back to your house. Let him eat bread and drink water.'" But he lied to him.

19 So the man of God went back with him. He ate bread in his house. He drank water. 20 While they sat at the table, the word of the the LORD came to the prophet who had brought him back.

21 He cried out to the man of God from Judah, "The the LORD says this: You did not obey the the LORD. You did not keep the command the the LORD your God gave you. 22 You came back. You ate bread and drank water in the place where He said, 'Do not eat bread or drink water.' So your body will not be buried with your fathers."

23 After he ate bread and drank water, the old prophet saddled the donkey for the man of God.

The Man of God Is Killed

24 The man of God left. A lion found him on the road and killed him. His body lay on the road. The donkey stood by it. The lion stood by the body too.

25 Some men passed by. They saw the body on the road and the lion standing by it. They went and told the city where the old prophet lived.

26 When the prophet who brought him back heard this, he said, "He is the man of God who did not obey the the LORD. The the LORD gave him to the lion. It tore him and killed him, just as the the LORD had said." 27 He said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." They saddled it.

28 He went and found the body on the road. The donkey and the lion were standing by it. The lion had not eaten the body. It had not hurt the donkey.

29 The prophet picked up the body of the man of God. He put it on the donkey. He brought it back. The old prophet came to the city to cry for him and bury him.

30 He laid the body in his own tomb. They cried over him, "Oh, my brother!"

31 After he buried him, he said to his sons, "When I die, bury me in the same tomb where the man of God is buried. Put my bones next to his bones." 32 The words he spoke by the word of the the LORD will come true. He spoke them against the altar in Bethel and against all the high places in the cities of Samaria.

Jeroboam Still Does Wrong

33 After this, Jeroboam still did not turn from his evil way. He made priests from the people. Anyone who wanted to be a priest could become one. 34 This became a sin for Jeroboam's family. It led to them being wiped out from the earth.
