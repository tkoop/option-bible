Jehoiada Plans for a New King

1 In the seventh year, Jehoiada grew strong and made a promise with the captains of hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri. 2 They went all through Judah. They brought the Levites from every city in Judah and the family leaders of Israel to Jerusalem. 3 The whole group made a promise with the king at God’s house. Jehoiada said, “Look, the king’s son will rule. The LORD said this would happen for David’s family.”

4 “This is what you must do. One third of you priests and Levites who come to work on the Sabbath will guard the doors. 5 One third will be at the king’s house. One third will be at the Foundation Gate. All the people will be in the yards of the LORD’s house. 6 No one may go into the LORD’s house except the priests and Levites who are working. They may go in because they are holy. All the people must follow the LORD’s rules. 7 The Levites must stand around the king. Each man must hold his weapon. Anyone who goes into the temple must die. You must stay close to the king wherever he goes.”

8 The Levites and all Judah did everything Jehoiada the priest told them. Each man brought his group. Some were just starting work for the Sabbath, and some were ending. Jehoiada did not send any group away. 9 Jehoiada the priest gave the captains of hundreds the spears, shields, and small shields that had belonged to King David. They were in God’s house. 10 He put all the people in place. Each man held his weapon. They stood all around the king, from the south side of God’s house to the north side, near the altar and the temple.

Joash Is Crowned King

11 They brought out the king’s son. They put the crown on him. They gave him the law book. They made him king. Jehoiada and his sons poured oil on him. They said, “Long live the king!”

Athaliah Is Caught

12 Athaliah heard the noise of people running and praising the king. She came to the people at the LORD’s house. 13 She looked and saw the king standing by his pillar at the door. The leaders and trumpet players stood by the king. All the people were happy. They blew trumpets. The singers sang and played music and led the praise. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and yelled, “You are turning against the king! You are turning against the king!”

14 Jehoiada the priest brought out the captains of hundreds who led the army. He said, “Take her out between the rows. Anyone who follows her must die by the sword.” He had already said, “Do not kill her in the LORD’s house.” 15 They grabbed her. She went to the entrance of the Horse Gate at the king’s house. They killed her there.

The People Promise to Follow the LORD

16 Jehoiada made a promise between himself, all the people, and the king. They would be the LORD’s people. 17 All the people went to Baal’s temple and tore it down. They smashed its altars and statues. They killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars.

Worship Starts Again

18 Jehoiada put the priests and Levites in charge of the LORD’s house. They followed David’s groups. They gave burnt offerings to the LORD, as Moses’ law says. They did it with joy and singing, as David had ordered. 19 He put gatekeepers at the gates of the LORD’s house. No one who was unclean in any way could go in.

The King Sits on His Throne

20 He took the captains of hundreds, the leaders, the rulers of the people, and all the people. They brought the king down from the LORD’s house. They went through the Upper Gate to the king’s house. They sat the king on the throne. 21 All the people were happy. The city was quiet, because Athaliah had been killed with a sword.
