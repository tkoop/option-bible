David Prays for Forgiveness

1 For the music leader. A psalm of David. Nathan the prophet came to David after David had sinned with Bathsheba. Have mercy on me, God. Be kind to me because of Your faithful love. You have so much mercy. Wipe away my wrongs. 2 Wash me clean from my wrongs. Make me pure from my sin. 3 I know my wrongs. My sin is always in front of me. 4 I sinned against You alone. I did what is bad in Your eyes. So You are right when You speak. You are fair when You judge.

A Clean Heart

5 I was born sinful. I have done wrong since my mother was pregnant with me. 6 You want truth deep inside me. You teach me wisdom in my secret heart. 7 Clean me with hyssop, and I will be clean. Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow. 8 Let me hear joy and gladness again. Let the bones You broke be happy again.

9 Turn Your face away from my sins. Wipe away all my wrongs. 10 Make a clean heart in me, God. Give me a new and steady spirit. 11 Do not throw me away from Your presence. Do not take Your Holy Spirit from me. 12 Give me back the joy of Your saving me. Give me a willing spirit to help me stand.

Teaching Others

13 Then I will teach wrongdoers Your ways. Sinners will come back to You. 14 Save me from the guilt of killing, God. You are the God who saves me. Then I will sing about how good and right You are. 15 Lord, open my lips. My mouth will tell Your praise.

What God Really Wants

16 You do not want sacrifices, or I would give them. You do not want burnt offerings. 17 The sacrifice God wants is a broken spirit. A broken and sorry heart, God, You will not turn away.

Blessing Jerusalem

18 Be kind to Zion. Build up the walls of Jerusalem. 19 Then You will be pleased with good sacrifices and whole burnt offerings. Then bulls will be offered on Your altar.
