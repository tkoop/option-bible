God Makes a Promise to Abram

1 When Abram was 99 years old, the LORD came to him and said, "I am God All-Powerful. Live for Me and do what is right. 2 I will make a special promise between Me and you. I will make your family very big."

3 Abram fell face down. 4 Then God said, "Here is My promise with you. You will be the father of many nations. 5 Your name will not be Abram anymore. Your name will be Abraham, because I have made you the father of many nations. 6 I will give you many children. I will make you the father of many nations, and kings will come from you. 7 I will keep My promise with you and with your children and their children forever. I will be your God, and I will be their God too. 8 I will give you and your children after you the land where you are living now. I will give you all the land of Canaan to keep forever. I will be their God."

The Sign of God's Promise

9 God said to Abraham, "You must keep My promise. You and your children and their children must keep it. 10 This is My promise that you must keep. It is between Me and you and your children after you: Every male among you must be circumcised. 11 You must be circumcised in your bodies. This will be the sign of My promise between Me and you. 12 Every baby boy must be circumcised when he is 8 days old. This rule lasts forever. It is for boys born in your homes and for boys bought with your money, even if they are not from your family. 13 Boys born in your home and boys bought with your money must be circumcised. My promise will be in your bodies forever. 14 Any male who is not circumcised has broken My promise. He will be sent away from his people."

New Names

15 God said to Abraham, "Do not call your wife Sarai anymore. Her name will be Sarah. 16 I will bless her. I will give you a son through her. I will bless her, and she will be the mother of many nations. Kings will come from her."

17 Abraham fell face down and laughed. He said to himself, "Can a 100-year-old man have a baby? Can Sarah have a baby when she is 90?" 18 Abraham said to God, "I wish Ishmael could live and be blessed by You."

19 God said, "Your wife Sarah will have a son. You will name him Isaac. I will keep My promise with him forever. It will be for him and his children after him. 20 As for Ishmael, I have heard you. I have blessed him. I will give him many children, and his family will grow very big. He will be the father of 12 leaders, and I will make him a great nation. 21 But I will keep My promise with Isaac. Sarah will have him for you next year at this time."

22 When God finished talking with Abraham, God left him.

Abraham Obeys God

23 That same day, Abraham took his son Ishmael and all the males in his home. He took the boys born there and the men he had bought. He circumcised them, just as God had told him. 24 Abraham was 99 years old when he was circumcised. 25 Ishmael was 13 years old when he was circumcised. 26 Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised on that same day. 27 All the men in his home were circumcised too. This included the men born there and the men bought with money.
