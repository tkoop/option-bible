Saul Looks for the Lost Donkeys

1 There was a man named Kish. He was a strong man from the family of Benjamin. Kish was the son of Abiel. Abiel was the son of Zeror. Zeror was the son of Becorath. Becorath was the son of Aphiah. They were Benjamites. 2 Kish had a son named Saul. Saul was young and handsome. No one in Israel was better looking than he was. He was taller than everyone else. 3 Some donkeys that belonged to Kish were lost. Kish said to Saul, "Take one helper and go look for the donkeys."

4 Saul and his helper went through the hill country of Ephraim. They went through Shalishah. They went through Shaalim. They went through the land of Benjamin. They could not find the donkeys. 5 When they came to Zuph, Saul said to his helper, "Let's go home. my father may stop worrying about the donkeys. He may start worrying about us."

They Go to See the Man of God

6 The helper said, "Look, there is a man of God in this town. He is an important man. Everything he says comes true. Let's go there. Maybe he can tell us which way to go."

7 Saul said to his helper, "If we go, what will we bring the man? We have no bread left. We have no gift for the man of God. What do we have?"

8 The helper answered, "I have a little silver. I can give it to the man of God. He can tell us which way to go."

9 In the past, people in Israel called a prophet a seer. When they wanted to ask God something, they said, "Let's go to the seer."

10 Saul said, "That is a good idea. Let's go." So they went to the town where the man of God was.

The Girls at the Well

11 As they went up the hill to the town, they met some young women. The women were getting water. Saul and his helper asked, "Is the seer here?"

12 The women said, "Yes, he is. He is right here. Hurry, because he came to town today. The people have a sacrifice at the high place."

13 They said, "As soon as you go into the town, you will find him before he goes up to the high place to eat. The people will not eat until he comes. He blesses the sacrifice first. Then the invited guests eat. Go now. You will find him at this time of day."

14 They went up to the town. As they came into the middle of the town, Samuel came out. He was going to meet them. He was going up to the high place.

The LORD Speaks to Samuel

15 The day before Saul came, the LORD told Samuel, 16 "Tomorrow at this time I will send you a man from Benjamin. Anoint him as leader over My people Israel. He will save My people from the Philistines. I have seen My people. Their cry has come to Me." 17 When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said, "Look, this is the man I told you about. He will rule over My people."

Samuel Meets Saul

18 Saul went up to Samuel at the gate. He said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."

19 Samuel said, "I am the seer. Go up to the high place ahead of me. Eat with me today. In the morning I will send you on your way. I will tell you everything in your heart. 20 Do not worry about the donkeys you lost three days ago. They have been found. Also, who is all Israel looking for? Is it not you and your father's family?"

21 Saul answered, "I am from the smallest tribe in Israel, Benjamin. my family is the least important family in Benjamin. Why are you talking to me like this?"

Saul Eats with Samuel

22 Samuel took Saul and his helper. He brought them into the big room. He gave them the best seats with the invited guests. There were about thirty men.

23 Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the special portion I gave you to keep."

24 The cook brought the thigh and what was on it. He set it in front of Saul. Samuel said, "Look, this is the part saved for you. Eat it. It was kept for you for this special time. I invited the people." So Saul ate with Samuel that day.

Samuel Sends Saul Home

25 They went down from the high place to the town. Samuel talked with Saul on the roof.

26 They got up early. At dawn, Samuel called Saul on the roof. He said, "Get up. I will send you away." Saul got up. Both of them went outside.

27 As they went down to the edge of the town, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell your helper to go ahead of us. You stand here today, so I can tell you God's word." The helper went on ahead.
