Who May Join the LORD's Meeting

1 A man whose private parts are crushed or cut off may not come into the the LORD's meeting place. 2 No child born from a wrong marriage may come into the the LORD's meeting place. Even after ten generations, that family may not come in. 3 No Ammonite or Moabite may come into the the LORD's meeting place. Even after ten generations, they may not come in forever.

4 This is because they did not meet you with bread and water on the road when you came out of Egypt. They also paid Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram Naharaim to curse you. 5 But the the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam. The the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the the LORD your God loves you. 6 Do not seek their peace or good for all your days. Never seek it.

7 Do not hate an Edomite, because he is your brother. Do not hate an Egyptian, because you lived as a stranger in his land. 8 Children born to them in the third generation may come into the the LORD's meeting place.

Rules for the Army Camp

9 When you go out to fight your enemies, be careful to stay away from everything bad. 10 If a man among you is not clean at night, he must go outside the camp. He may not come into the camp. 11 When evening comes, he must wash with water. When the sun goes down, he may come back into the camp.

12 You must have a place outside the camp. Go there. 13 You must have a stick with your things. When you sit outside, dig with it. Then cover your waste. 14 For the the LORD your God walks in the middle of your camp. He is there to save you and to give your enemies to you. So your camp must be holy. He must not see anything shameful in you, or He will turn away from you.

Do Not Send Back a Runaway Slave

15 Do not hand over a slave to his master if he runs away to you. 16 Let him live with you where he wants, in one of your towns. Do not treat him badly.

Rules About Worship and Money

17 No Israelite man or woman may be a temple prostitute. 18 Do not bring money earned by a prostitute or by a dog into the the LORD's house to keep a promise to God. The the LORD your God hates both.

19 Do not charge interest to your brother on money, food, or anything else you lend. 20 You may charge interest to a person from another nation. But do not charge interest to your brother. Then the the LORD your God will bless you in everything you do in the land you are going to take.

Keep Your Promises to the LORD

21 If you make a promise to the the LORD your God, do not wait to keep it. The the LORD your God will ask you for it. If you do not keep it, it is a sin. 22 But if you do not make a promise, you will not sin. 23 Be careful to do what your mouth says, because you promised it to the the LORD your God.

Eating from a Neighbor's Field

24 If you go into your neighbor's grape field, you may eat grapes until you are full. But do not put any in your basket. 25 If you go into your neighbor's grain field, you may pick grain with your hand. But do not use a sickle on your neighbor's grain.
