Good People Are Taken Away

1 Good people die, and no one cares. Good people are taken away, and no one knows this keeps them safe from bad times. 2 People who live right will have peace. They will rest when they lie down.

God Talks to the Sinners

3 But you, who use magic, come here. You children of people who cheat in marriage and sell their bodies. 4 Who are you making fun of? Who do you open your mouth at and stick out your tongue at? Are you not children who do wrong? Are you not from a family of lies? 5 You burn with desire among the oak trees and under every green tree. You kill children in the valleys and in the cracks of the rocks.

6 Your share is with the smooth stones in the stream. They are your lot. You poured out drink offerings to them. You brought grain offerings to them. Should this make Me feel better? 7 You set up your bed on a high and tall mountain. You went up there to make sacrifices. 8 Behind your door and door frame you put your sign. You showed your bed to Me. You went up and made your bed wide. You made a deal with them. You loved their bed. You looked at their naked bodies.

9 You went to the king with oil. You made more perfumes. You sent your messengers far away. You lowered yourself down to the grave. 10 You got tired from going your many ways, but you did not say, "I give up." You found new strength, so you did not get weak. 11 Who did you worry about and fear, so you lied? You did not remember Me. You did not take it to heart. Have I not been quiet for a long time? And you do not fear Me.

12 I will tell about your right ways and your works, but they will not help you. 13 When you cry for help, let the things you gathered save you. The wind will carry them all away. A breath will take them. But the one who trusts in Me will get the land. He will own My holy mountain.

God Makes a Clear Road

14 And it will be said, "Build up, build up. Make the road clear. Take the blocks out of the way of My people."

God Lives with Humble People

15 For this is what the One who is high and lifted up says. He lives forever. His name is Holy. He says, "I live in a high and holy place. I also live with the one who is sorry and humble in spirit. I give new life to humble people. I give new life to sorry people."

16 For I will not stay mad forever. I will not be angry all the time. A person's spirit would grow weak before Me. The breath of life I made would fail. 17 I was angry because of his sinful greed. I hit him and hid My face. I was angry, but he kept going his own way.

God Promises to Heal

18 I have seen his ways, but I will heal him. I will guide him and bring comfort to him and to those who cry for him. 19 I will put praise on their lips: "Peace, peace to those far away and to those near," says the the LORD. "And I will heal them."

Bad People Have No Peace

20 But bad people are like the sea that cannot rest. Its water keeps tossing. It throws up mud and dirt. 21 "There is no peace," says My God, "for bad people."
