Solomon Talks to the People

1 Then Solomon said, “The LORD has said He will live in the dark cloud. 2 But I have built You a big house. It is a place for You to live forever.”

Solomon Blesses the People

3 Then the king turned around. He blessed all the people of Israel. All the people were standing there.

Solomon Speaks About God's Promise

“4 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel. He kept His word. He said it with His mouth to my father David. Now He has done it with His hands. 5 He said, ‘From the day I brought My people out of Egypt, I did not choose any city from the tribes of Israel. I did not choose a city to build a house for My name. I did not choose a man to rule My people Israel.’ 6 ‘But I chose Jerusalem for My name to be there. I chose David to rule My people Israel.’

“7 My father David wanted to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 8 But the LORD said to my father David, ‘You wanted to build a house for My name. That was good. You had that in your heart.’ 9 ‘But you will not build the house. Your son, your own child, will build the house for My name.’

“10 The LORD has done what He said. I took my father David’s place. I sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised. I built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 11 I put the ark there. In it is the LORD’s promise. He made it with the people of Israel.”

Solomon Kneels to Pray

12 Then Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar. He was in front of all the people of Israel. He spread out his hands. 13 Solomon had made a bronze stand. It was about seven and a half feet long and wide. It was about four and a half feet high. He put it in the middle of the yard. He stood on it. He knelt down before all the people of Israel. He spread his hands toward heaven.

Solomon's Prayer

“14 O the LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven or on earth. You keep Your promise. You show love to Your servants who follow You with all their hearts. 15 You kept what You promised to Your servant David, my father. You said it with Your mouth. You did it with Your hand today. 16 Now, O the LORD, God of Israel, keep for Your servant David, my father, what You promised him. You said, ‘you will never fail to have a man to sit before Me on the throne of Israel, if your sons guard their ways and walk in My law as you have walked before Me.’ 17 Now, O the LORD, God of Israel, let Your word to Your servant David come true.”

“18 But will God really live with people on earth? Heaven, even the highest heaven, cannot hold You. How much less this house I built! 19 Yet listen to the prayer of Your servant. Listen to his plea, O the LORD my God. Hear the cry and the prayer he prays to You. 20 May Your eyes be open to this house day and night. You said You would put Your name here. Hear the prayer Your servant prays toward this place. 21 Hear the plea of Your servant and of Your people Israel. They pray toward this place. Hear from heaven, where You live. Hear and forgive.”

“22 When a man sins against his neighbor and must take an oath, and he comes to take that oath before Your altar in this house, 23 then hear from heaven. Do what is right. Judge Your servants. Punish the bad man for his bad ways. Show the good man is right by giving him a good reward.”

“24 When Your people Israel lose to an enemy because they sinned against You, and they come back to You and praise Your name, and pray to You in this house, 25 then hear from heaven. Forgive the sin of Your people Israel. Bring them back to the land You gave them and their fathers.”

“26 When the sky is shut and there is no rain because they sinned against You, and they pray toward this place and praise Your name, and turn from their sin because You punished them, 27 then hear from heaven. Forgive the sin of Your servants and Your people Israel. Teach them the good way to walk. Send rain on Your land, which You gave Your people to own.”

“28 When there is no food in the land, or sickness, or plant disease, or bugs, or enemies attack their cities, or any other trouble or sickness comes, 29 then any man or all Your people Israel may pray. Each one knows his own hurt and pain. He spreads out his hands toward this house. 30 Then hear from heaven, where You live. Forgive. Give each man what he deserves. You alone know the hearts of all people. 31 Then they will fear You. They will walk in Your ways all their lives in the land You gave our fathers.”

“32 And for the stranger who is not Your people Israel but comes from a far land because of Your great name and Your strong hand and Your power—when he comes and prays toward this house, 33 then hear from heaven, where You live. Do what he asks. Then all people on earth will know Your name and fear You, like Your people Israel do. They will know that this house I built is called by Your name.”

“34 When Your people go to war against their enemies, wherever You send them, and they pray to You toward this city You chose and the house I built for Your name, 35 then hear from heaven their prayer and their plea. Help them.”

“36 When they sin against You—no one is without sin—and You are angry with them and let an enemy take them away to a far or near land, 37 and when they think about it in the land where they were taken, and they are sorry and pray to You there, saying, ‘We sinned. We did wrong. We were bad,’ 38 and when they come back to You with all their heart and soul in that land, and pray toward the land You gave their fathers, toward the city You chose, and toward the house I built for Your name, 39 then hear from heaven, where You live, their prayer and their plea. Help them. Forgive Your people who sinned against You.”

“40 Now, my God, may Your eyes be open and Your ears listen to the prayer made in this place. 41 Now get up, O the LORD God. Come to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your power. May Your priests, O the LORD God, be saved. May Your faithful people be happy with what is good. 42 O the LORD God, do not turn away the king You chose. Remember Your love for Your servant David.”
