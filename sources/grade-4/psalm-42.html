A Song by Korah’s Family: Longing for God

1 A deer longs for streams of water. Just like that, my soul longs for You, God. 2 My soul is thirsty for God, for the living God. When can I go and see God? 3 I have cried so much day and night. People ask me all day, “Where is your God?”

Remembering Happy Days

4 I remember these things, and I tell God how I feel. I used to walk with the crowd to God’s house. We sang happy songs and gave thanks. Many people were there for the feast.

Hope in God

5 Why are you sad, my soul? Why are you upset inside me? Hope in God. I will praise Him again. He saves me. He is my God. 6 My God, I am sad deep inside. So I remember You, even here by the Jordan River and Mount Hermon, near Mount Mizar.

Deep Trouble

7 One trouble calls to another. I hear Your waterfalls roar. Your waves wash over me again and again. 8 By day the the LORD shows His kind love. At night His song is with me. I pray to the God who gives me life.

Asking God Why

9 I say to God, my Rock, “Why have You forgotten me? Why must I walk around sad while my enemy hurts me?”

10 My enemies make fun of me. They hurt me deep inside. They say all day, “Where is your God?”

Still Hoping in God

11 Why are you sad, my soul? Why are you upset inside me? Hope in God. I will praise Him again. He saves me. He is my God.
