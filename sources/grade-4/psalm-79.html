Enemies Ruin God's City

1 A song by Asaph. God, other nations came into Your land. They made Your holy place dirty. They turned Jerusalem into a pile of rocks. 2 They gave the dead bodies of Your servants to the birds. They gave the flesh of Your faithful people to wild animals. 3 They poured out blood like water all around Jerusalem. No one was there to bury them. 4 We became a shame to our neighbors. People all around us laughed at us and made fun of us.

How Long Will God Be Angry?

5 How long, the LORD? Will You be angry forever? Will Your jealousy burn like fire? 6 Pour out Your anger on the nations that do not know You. Pour it on the kingdoms that do not call on Your name. 7 For they destroyed Your people Israel and ruined their homes.

God's People Ask for Help

8 Do not blame us for sins of long ago. Let Your kindness come to us soon, because we are very weak. 9 Help us, God who saves us, so Your name will be honored. Save us and forgive our sins to show how great You are.

10 Why do the nations ask, 'Where is their God?' Let the nations see You punish those who killed Your servants, right before our eyes. 11 Let the cries of people in prison come to You. By Your great power, keep alive those who are about to die. 12 Pay back our neighbors seven times for the insults they threw at You, Lord.

We Will Praise God

13 Then we, Your people, the sheep You care for, will thank You forever. We will tell of Your praise from one family to the next.
