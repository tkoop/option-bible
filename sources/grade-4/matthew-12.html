Jesus and the Sabbath

1 At that time, Jesus walked through the grain fields on a Sabbath day. His disciples were hungry. They began to pick heads of grain and eat them. 2 The Pharisees saw this. They said to Him, "Look! Your disciples are doing what is not allowed on the Sabbath."

3 He said to them, "Have you not read what David did when he and his men were hungry? 4 He went into God's house and ate the bread set apart for God. It was not allowed for him or his men to eat it. Only the priests could eat it. 5 Or have you not read in the Law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple work and are not guilty? 6 I tell you, something greater than the temple is here. 7 If you had known what this means, 'I want mercy, not sacrifice,' you would not have blamed innocent people. 8 For the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath."

Healing on the Sabbath

9 Then Jesus left there and went into their synagogue. 10 A man was there whose hand did not work. The Pharisees wanted to accuse Jesus. They asked Him, "Is it allowed to heal on the Sabbath?"

11 He said to them, "If one of you has a sheep and it falls into a hole on the Sabbath, won't he grab it and pull it out? 12 So a man is worth much more than a sheep. It is allowed to do good on the Sabbath." 13 Then He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." The man stretched it out. It became well, like the other hand.

The Pharisees Make Plans

14 The Pharisees went out and planned how to kill Him. 15 Jesus knew this. He left there. Many crowds followed Him. He healed them all. 16 He told them not to tell others who He was.

God's Chosen Servant

17 This was to fulfill what the prophet Isaiah said: 18 "Here is My Servant whom I chose. He is My Beloved. I am pleased with Him. I will put My Spirit on Him. He will tell the nations what is right. 19 He will not argue or shout. No one will hear His voice in the streets. 20 He will not break a bent reed. He will not put out a weak flame. He will keep going until He brings justice to victory. 21 The nations will put their hope in His name."

Jesus and Beelzebul

22 Then a man who was demon-possessed, blind, and unable to speak was brought to Jesus. Jesus healed him. The man could speak and see. 23 All the crowds were amazed. They said, "Could this be the Son of David?"

24 The Pharisees heard this. They said, "This man does not drive out demons except by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

25 Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said to them, "Every kingdom that fights against itself is ruined. Every city or house that fights against itself will not stand. 26 If Satan drives out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his kingdom stand? 27 If I drive out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons drive them out? So they will be your judges. 28 But if I drive out demons by the Spirit of God, then God's kingdom has come to you.

"29 How can anyone go into a strong man's house and take his things, unless he first ties up the strong man? Then he can rob his house. 30 Whoever is not with Me is against Me. Whoever does not gather with Me scatters.

"31 So I tell you, people will be forgiven for every sin and blasphemy. But blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven. 32 Anyone who speaks against the Son of Man will be forgiven. But anyone who speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, not in this age or in the age to come. 33 Make the tree good, and its fruit will be good. Or make the tree bad, and its fruit will be bad. A tree is known by its fruit. 34 You snakes! How can you speak good things when you are evil? The mouth speaks what the heart is full of. 35 A good man brings good things out of his good treasure. A bad man brings bad things out of his bad treasure. 36 I tell you, people will give an account for every careless word they speak on the day of judgment. 37 For by your words you will be found right, and by your words you will be found wrong."

The Sign of Jonah

38 Then some of the teachers of the law and Pharisees answered Him. They said, "Teacher, we want to see a sign from You."

39 He answered them, "This is an evil and unfaithful generation. It asks for a sign. But no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah. 40 For just as Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of the big fish, so the Son of Man will be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth. 41 The men of Nineveh will stand up at the judgment with this generation. They will judge it. For they turned from their sin when Jonah preached to them. And something greater than Jonah is here. 42 The queen of the South will rise at the judgment with this generation. She will judge it. For she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom. And something greater than Solomon is here. 43 When an evil spirit leaves a person, it goes through dry places looking for rest. It does not find any. 44 Then it says, 'I will go back to my house, the place I left.' When it goes back, it finds the house empty, swept, and put in order. 45 Then it goes and takes with it seven other spirits worse than itself. They go in and live there. So the last state of that person is worse than the first. That is how it will be with this evil generation."

Jesus' True Family

46 While Jesus was still speaking to the crowds, His mother and brothers stood outside. They wanted to speak to Him. 47 Someone told Him, "Look! Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside. They want to speak to You."

48 He answered the one speaking, "Who is My mother? Who are My brothers?" 49 He stretched out His hand toward His disciples. He said, "Here are My mother and My brothers. 50 For whoever does the will of My Father in heaven is My brother and sister and mother."
