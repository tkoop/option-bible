David Prays in a Cave

1 I cry out loud to the the LORD. I beg the the LORD to help me. 2 I tell the the LORD all my worries. I tell Him how much trouble I am in.

3 I feel weak inside, but You know where I go. People hide traps where I walk. 4 Look to my right and see. No one knows me. I have nowhere to run. No one cares about my life.

5 I cry to You, the LORD. I say, "You are my safe place. You are my hope in this life." 6 Listen to my cry, because I am very weak. Save me from the people chasing me. They are stronger than I am.

7 Get me out of this prison, so I can thank You. Good people will be around me, because You will be good to me.
