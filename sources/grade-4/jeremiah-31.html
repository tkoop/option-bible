God Will Bring His People Back

1 "At that time," declares the LORD, "I will be the God of all the families of Israel, and they will be My people."

2 This is what the LORD says: "The people who lived through war found help in the desert. Israel, when I go to give him rest, 3 the LORD showed Himself to me from far away. He said, 'I have loved you with a love that lasts forever. So I have pulled you close with kind love.'"

4 "I will build you again, and you will be built again, O young woman Israel. You will again play your tambourines and dance with joy. 5 You will plant grape fields again on the hills of Samaria. The farmers will plant and eat the fruit."

6 "For a day will come when watchmen on Ephraim's hills call out, 'Get up! Let us go up to Zion, to the LORD our God!'"

7 For this is what the LORD says: "Sing with joy for Jacob. Shout among the leading nations. Let your praise be heard. Say, 'O the LORD, save Your people, the rest of Israel!'"

8 "Look, I will bring them from the north land and gather them from the ends of the earth. The blind and the lame will be with them. So will women who are expecting and women in labor. A big group will come back!"

9 "They will come crying. I will lead them with prayer. I will make them walk by streams of water. They will walk on a straight path and not trip. For I am Israel's Father. Ephraim is My Firstborn."

God Will Gather His People

10 Listen to the LORD's word, O nations. Tell it far away by the sea: "The One who scattered Israel will gather him. He will keep him like a shepherd keeps his sheep." 11 For the LORD has paid to free Jacob. He has saved him from the hand that was too strong for him.

12 They will come and shout for joy on Zion's high places. They will shine because of the LORD's good gifts—grain, new wine, oil, and young sheep and cows. Their life will be like a watered garden. They will not be weak again.

13 Then the young women will dance with joy. Young men and old men will too. I will turn their sadness into joy. I will comfort them and make them glad instead of sad.

14 "I will fill the priests with plenty. My people will be full with My good things," says the LORD.

Rachel Cries for Her Children

15 This is what the LORD says: "A voice is heard in Ramah. It is crying and loud weeping. Rachel cries for her children. She will not be comforted, because they are gone."

16 This is what the LORD says: "Stop your crying. Stop your tears. You will be paid for your work," says the LORD. "Your children will come back from the enemy's land. 17 There is hope for your future," says the LORD. "Your children will come back to their own land."

18 "I have heard Ephraim crying."

"You corrected me, and I was corrected like a young cow that is not trained. Bring me back, and I will come back, for You are the LORD my God. 19 After I came back, I was sorry. After I learned, I hit my leg in grief. I was ashamed and embarrassed, because I carried the shame of my youth."

20 "Is Ephraim not My dear son? Is he not My joy? I speak against him, but I still remember him. So My heart wants him. I have deep pity for him," says the LORD.

21 "Put up road signs for yourself. Make signposts. Remember the road you traveled. Come back, O young woman Israel. Come back to your cities. 22 How long will you wander, you stubborn daughter? For the LORD has made something new on earth: a woman will surround a man."

23 This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "When I bring them back, they will say this again in Judah and its cities:"

"The LORD bless you, O good home, O holy mountain."

24 "Judah and all its cities will live together in the land. So will farmers and people who move with their sheep. 25 For I have given rest to the tired soul. I have filled every sad soul."

26 Then I woke up and looked around. My sleep was very sweet to me.

A New Promise from God

27 "Look, the days are coming," says the LORD, "when I will fill Israel and Judah with people and animals. 28 Just as I watched over them to pull up and tear down, to destroy and ruin, and to bring trouble, so I will watch over them to build and plant," says the LORD.

29 "In those days, people will not say, 'Fathers ate sour grapes, and children's teeth hurt.' 30 Instead, each person will die for his own sin. The person who eats sour grapes will have his own teeth hurt."

31 "Look, the days are coming," says the LORD, "when I will make a new promise with Israel and Judah. 32 It will not be like the promise I made with their fathers when I took them by the hand and led them out of Egypt. They broke that promise, even though I was like a husband to them," says the LORD.

33 "But this is the promise I will make with Israel after those days," says the LORD. "I will put My law in their minds and write it on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people. 34 No one will need to teach his neighbor or brother, saying, 'Know the LORD,' because all of them will know Me, from the small to the great," says the LORD. "For I will forgive their wrongs and not remember their sins anymore."

35 This is what the LORD says: "He gives the sun for light in the day. He sets the moon and stars for light at night. He stirs the sea so its waves roar. The LORD of Armies is His name."

36 "Only if these rules stop before My eyes," says the LORD, "will Israel's children stop being a nation before Me."

37 This is what the LORD says: "Only if the sky above can be measured and the earth's base below can be found out will I turn away from all of Israel's children for all they have done," says the LORD.

Jerusalem Will Be Built Again

38 "Look, the days are coming," says the LORD, "when the city will be rebuilt for the LORD from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate. 39 The measuring line will stretch out straight to Gareb hill and then turn toward Goah. 40 The whole valley of dead bodies and ashes, and all the fields to the Kidron Valley, to the corner of the Horse Gate in the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will never be pulled up or torn down again."
