Some Leaders Question Jesus

1 Then some Pharisees and teachers of the law came to Jesus from Jerusalem. 2 They said, "Why do your followers not wash their hands before they eat? They do not follow what our older leaders taught."

3 Jesus answered, "Why do you break God's command? 4 God said, 'Honor your father and your mother.' He also said, 'Anyone who curses his father or mother must die.' 5 But you say a person can tell his father or mother, "I already gave to God what I would have used to help you," 6 and then he does not have to take care of them. In this way, you cancel God's word to keep your own rules."

7 Jesus said, "You pretenders! Isaiah said true words about you: 8 'These people honor me with their words, but their hearts are far from me. 9 They worship me, but it means nothing. They teach man-made rules as if they were God's commands.'"

What Really Makes a Person Unclean

10 Then Jesus called the crowd to come closer. He said, "Listen and understand. 11 What goes into a person's mouth does not make him unclean. What comes out of his mouth makes him unclean."

12 Later, His followers came to Him. They said, "Do you know the Pharisees were upset when they heard what you said?"

13 Jesus answered, "Every plant my Father in heaven did not plant will be pulled up. 14 Leave them alone. They are blind leaders of blind people. If a blind person leads another blind person, both will fall into a hole."

15 Peter said to Him, "Please explain this story to us."

16 Jesus said, "Are you still not able to understand? 17 Do you not know that everything that goes into the mouth goes to the stomach and then leaves the body? 18 But what comes out of the mouth comes from the heart. That is what makes a person unclean. 19 From the heart come bad thoughts, murder, cheating in marriage, sexual sins, stealing, lying about others, and cursing God. 20 These things make a person unclean. But eating with unwashed hands does not make a person unclean."

A Woman from Canaan Asks for Help

21 Then Jesus left that place and went to the area near Tyre and Sidon.

22 A woman from Canaan came out. She cried out, "Lord, Son of David, please help me! My daughter is very troubled by a demon."

23 Jesus did not say a word to her. His followers came to Him and asked, "Please send her away. She keeps shouting behind us."

24 Jesus answered, "I was sent only to the lost sheep of the family of Israel."

25 The woman came and knelt before Him. She said, "Lord, help me."

26 Jesus answered, "It is not right to take the children's bread and give it to the dogs."

27 She said, "Yes, Lord. But even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their owners' table."

28 Then Jesus answered her, "Woman, you have great faith! Let it happen for you just as you want." And her daughter was healed at that same time.

Jesus Heals Many People

29 Jesus left there and went beside the Sea of Galilee. Then He went up on a hill and sat down.

30 Many people came to Him. They brought people who could not walk, could not see, had twisted hands, could not speak, and many others. They laid them at His feet. He healed them.

31 The crowd was amazed when they saw people who could not speak now speaking, people with twisted hands now well, people who could not walk now walking, and people who could not see now seeing. They praised the God of Israel.

Jesus Feeds a Large Crowd

32 Jesus called His followers to Him. He said, "I feel sorry for the crowd. They have been with me three days, and they have nothing to eat. I do not want to send them away hungry. They might get too weak on the way."

33 His followers said to Him, "Where can we get enough bread in this empty place to feed all these people?"

34 Jesus asked them, "How many loaves of bread do you have?"

They said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

35 Jesus told the crowd to sit down on the ground.

36 He took the seven loaves and the fish. He gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to His followers. His followers gave them to the crowd.

37 Everyone ate and was full. They picked up seven baskets full of leftover pieces.

38 About four thousand men ate, not counting women and children.

39 Then Jesus sent the crowd away. He got into a boat and went to the area of Magadan.
