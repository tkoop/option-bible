False Teachers Will Come

1 But in the past there were also false prophets among the people. In the same way, there will be false teachers among you. They will secretly bring in wrong ideas that destroy people. They will even turn their backs on the Master who bought them. They will bring quick ruin on themselves. 2 Many people will follow their bad ways. Because of them, people will say bad things about the true way. 3 They will cheat you with fake words because they want money. Their punishment was decided long ago. It is real, and their ruin is coming.

God Has Punished Bad People Before

4 God did not spare the angels who sinned. He sent them to a dark place and tied them with chains. They are kept there until they are judged. 5 God did not spare the old world either. He brought a flood on the world of people who did not obey Him. But He kept Noah safe. Noah told people to do right. Noah and seven others were saved. 6 God also punished the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah. He burned them to ashes. He made them a warning for people who will not obey God later. 7 But God saved Lot. Lot was a good man. He was upset by the dirty lives of the lawless people around him. 8 That good man lived among them day after day. What he saw and heard hurt his good heart. 9 So the Lord knows how to save good people from trouble. He also knows how to keep bad people for punishment until the day they are judged.

What These Bad Teachers Are Like

10 This is very true for people who follow their bad body wants and hate anyone telling them what to do. They are bold and stubborn. They are not afraid to say mean things about great beings. 11 Even angels are stronger and have more power than they do. But angels do not say mean things about them to the Lord. 12 But these people are like animals that do not think. They live by instinct. They are born to be caught and destroyed. They say mean things about things they do not understand. They will be destroyed like animals. 13 They will be paid back with hurt for the hurt they caused. They think having fun means partying in the daytime. They are spots and stains. They enjoy fooling people while they eat with you. 14 Their eyes are full of wrong desire for other people's wives. They never stop sinning. They trick people who are not strong in faith. They have hearts that practice wanting more and more. They are children under a curse. 15 They left the straight road and went the wrong way. They followed the way of Balaam son of Beor. He loved getting paid to do wrong. 16 But a donkey scolded him for his wrong. The donkey could not talk, but it spoke with a man's voice. It stopped the prophet from acting crazy. 17 These people are like wells with no water. They are like clouds blown by a storm. The darkest dark is saved for them. 18 They say proud and empty things. They use body wants to trick people who are just getting away from those who live in error.

They Promise Freedom But Are Slaves

19 They promise freedom, but they are slaves to ruin. A person is a slave to whatever controls them. 20 If people escape the bad things of the world by knowing our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and then get trapped in them again and lose, their last state is worse than the first. 21 It would have been better for them not to know the right way than to know it and turn away from the holy rule given to them. 22 This old saying is true for them: "A dog goes back to its own throw-up, and a washed pig goes back to roll in the mud."
