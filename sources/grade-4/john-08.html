Jesus Goes to the Mount of Olives

1 Jesus went to the Mount of Olives. 2 At dawn He came back to the temple. All the people came to Him, and He sat down and taught them.

A Woman Caught in Adultery

3 The teachers of the law and the Pharisees brought a woman to Him. She had been caught cheating on her husband. They made her stand in the middle. 4 They said to Him, "Teacher, this woman was caught cheating on her husband. 5 Moses told us in the Law to stone women like this. What do You say?"

6 They said this to test Him. They wanted a reason to accuse Him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with His finger. 7 They kept asking Him. He stood up and said, "Let the one among you who has no sin throw the first stone at her." 8 Then He bent down again and wrote on the ground.

9 When they heard this, they went out one by one. The older men left first. Jesus was left alone with the woman. She was still standing there. 10 Jesus stood up and said to her, "Woman, where are they? Did no one say you are guilty?"

11 She said, "No one, Lord."

Jesus said, "I do not say you are guilty either. Go, and do not sin again."

Jesus Is the Light of the World

12 Jesus spoke to them again. He said, "I am the light of the world. Anyone who follows Me will not walk in the dark. He will have the light that gives life."

The Pharisees Question Jesus

13 The Pharisees said to Him, "You speak for Yourself. Your words are not true."

14 Jesus answered, "Even if I speak for Myself, My words are true. I know where I came from and where I am going. But you do not know where I come from or where I am going. 15 You judge by human ways. I do not judge anyone. 16 But if I do judge, My judgment is true. I am not alone. The Father who sent Me is with Me. 17 It is written in your Law that the words of two people are true. 18 I speak for Myself, and the Father who sent Me speaks for Me too."

19 They asked Him, "Where is Your Father?"

Jesus said, "You do not know Me or My Father. If you knew Me, you would know My Father."

20 He said these things while He taught in the temple, near the money box. No one arrested Him, because His time had not yet come.

Jesus Warns About Sin

21 He said to them again, "I am going away. You will look for Me, but you will die in your sin. Where I go, you cannot come."

22 The Jews said, "Will He kill Himself? He says, 'Where I go, you cannot come.'"

23 He said to them, "You are from below. I am from above. You are of this world. I am not of this world. 24 That is why I told you that you will die in your sins. If you do not believe that I am He, you will die in your sins."

25 They asked Him, "Who are You?"

Jesus said, "I am who I have been telling you from the start. 26 I have much to say and judge about you. But the One who sent Me is true. I say to the world what I heard from Him."

27 They did not understand that He was talking about the Father.

28 Jesus said to them, "When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am He. I do nothing on My own. I speak just as the Father taught Me. 29 The One who sent Me is with Me. He has not left Me alone, because I always do what pleases Him."

30 As He said these things, many people believed in Him.

The Truth Will Set You Free

31 Jesus said to the Jews who believed in Him, "If you stay in My word, you are truly My followers. 32 You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

33 They answered Him, "We are Abraham's children. We have never been slaves to anyone. How can You say we will be free?"

34 Jesus answered them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who sins is a slave of sin. 35 A slave does not stay in the house forever. A son stays forever. 36 So if the Son sets you free, you are free indeed. 37 I know you are Abraham's children. But you want to kill Me, because My word has no room in you. 38 I speak what I saw with My Father. And you do what you heard from your father."

39 They said to Him, "Abraham is our father."

Jesus said to them, "If you were Abraham's children, you would do Abraham's works. 40 But now you want to kill Me, a man who told you the truth I heard from God. Abraham did not do this. 41 You do the works of your father."

They said, "We were not born from sexual sin. We have one Father, God."

42 Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love Me. I came from God, and I am here. I did not come on My own. He sent Me. 43 Why do you not understand My words? You cannot hear My word. 44 You are from your father the devil. You want to do what your father wants. He was a murderer from the start. He does not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he lies, he speaks his own words, because he is a liar and the father of lies. 45 Because I tell the truth, you do not believe Me. 46 Which of you can show that I have sinned? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe Me? 47 The person who is from God hears God's words. You do not hear them, because you are not from God."

48 The Jews answered Him, "Are we not right to say You are a Samaritan and have a demon?"

49 Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon. I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me. 50 I do not look for My own glory. There is One who looks for it and judges. 51 Truly, truly, I tell you, if anyone keeps My word, he will never see death."

52 The Jews said to Him, "Now we know You have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets died. Yet You say if anyone keeps Your word, he will never taste death. 53 Are You greater than our father Abraham, who died? The prophets died too. Who do You make Yourself?"

54 Jesus answered, "If I bring glory to Myself, My glory is nothing. My Father brings glory to Me. You say He is your God. 55 You do not know Him. But I know Him. If I said I did not know Him, I would be a liar like you. But I know Him, and I keep His word. 56 Your father Abraham was glad to see My day. He saw it and was glad."

57 The Jews said to Him, "You are not even fifty years old. Have You seen Abraham?"

58 Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, before Abraham was, I am."

They Try to Stone Jesus

59 They picked up stones to throw at Him. But Jesus hid and left the temple.
