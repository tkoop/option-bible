A Picture of the Siege

1 “You, son of man, take a brick. Put it in front of you. Draw a picture of Jerusalem on it. 2 Build a wall around it to show a siege. Pile up dirt against it. Put army camps around it. Put big poles all around it to break the wall. 3 Take an iron pan. Put it between you and the city like a wall. Turn your face toward the city. It will be under attack. Attack it. This is a sign for the people of Israel.”

Ezekiel Lies on His Side

4 “Lie on your left side. Put the sin of Israel on it. You will carry their sin. 5 I give you 390 days. That is the same number as the years of their sin. You will carry the sin of Israel. 6 When you finish those days, lie on your right side. Carry the sin of Judah. I give you 40 days. One day stands for one year. 7 Turn your face toward the siege of Jerusalem. Show your bare arm. Speak against the city. 8 I will tie you with ropes. You will not turn from one side to the other until your days of attack are done.”

Ezekiel's Food and Water

9 “Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt. Put them in one jar. Make bread from them. Eat it for the 390 days you lie on your side. 10 Eat only a small piece of bread each day. 11 Measure the water you drink. Drink it at set times. 12 Eat the bread like barley cakes. Bake it over human waste while people watch.”

13 “The LORD says, ‘This is how the people of Israel will eat unclean bread among the nations where I send them.’”

Ezekiel Asks for Help

14 “Oh, Lord the LORD! I have never made myself unclean. From my youth until now, I have not eaten meat that died on its own or was torn by animals. No unclean meat has ever entered my mouth.”

The LORD Answers Ezekiel

15 “Look, I will let you use cow dung instead of human dung. Bake your bread over that.”

16 “Look, I will take away the bread in Jerusalem. The people will eat bread by weight and worry. They will drink water by measure and be afraid. 17 They will not have enough bread or water. They will waste away because of their sin.”
