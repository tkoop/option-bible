Wise Words and Foolish Talk

1 A person who stays by himself wants only his own way. He fights against good advice. 2 A fool does not like to learn. He only wants to tell what he thinks. 3 When bad things come, shame comes too. With shame comes hurtful words. 4 A person's words are like deep water. Wise words flow like a small river. 5 It is not good to favor a bad person. It is not good to keep a fair person from justice.

6 A fool's lips start fights. His mouth asks for trouble. 7 A fool's mouth can ruin him. His lips can trap his own life.

8 Gossip sounds like tasty food. It sinks deep inside a person. 9 A lazy worker is like a person who destroys things.

A Safe Place

10 The name of the LORD is a strong tower. Good people run to it and are safe. 11 Rich people think their money is a strong city. They think it is a wall no one can climb.

12 Before a person falls, his heart is proud. But being humble comes before honor. 13 Answering before listening is foolish and shameful. 14 A person's spirit can help him through sickness. But who can help a broken heart? 15 A wise heart learns new things. A wise ear looks for knowledge.

16 A gift can open the way. It can bring a person to important people. 17 The first person in a case may seem right. Then another person comes and asks questions. 18 Drawing lots can stop fights. It can separate strong enemies.

19 A hurt brother is harder to win than a strong city. Fights are like the bars of a strong house.

Words Can Help or Hurt

20 A person's words can fill his stomach. The words from his lips can satisfy him. 21 Words have power over life and death. People who love to talk will see the results.

A Good Wife and True Friends

22 A man who finds a good wife finds something good. He gets favor from the LORD. 23 A poor person asks for help kindly. A rich person answers in a rough way. 24 A person with many friends may be ruined. But there is a friend who stays closer than a brother.
