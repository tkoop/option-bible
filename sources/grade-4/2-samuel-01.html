David Hears That Saul and Jonathan Died

1 After Saul died, David came back from fighting the Amalekites. He stayed in Ziklag for two days.

2 On the third day, a man came from Saul's camp. His clothes were torn, and dirt was on his head. He came to David, fell to the ground, and bowed down.

3 David asked him, "Where did you come from?"

The man said, "I ran away from Israel's camp."

4 David said, "What happened? Tell me."

The man said, "Our people ran from the battle. Many died. Saul and his son Jonathan are dead too."

5 David asked the young man, "How do you know Saul and Jonathan are dead?"

6 "I happened to be on Mount Gilboa. Saul was leaning on his spear. The chariots and horsemen were close to him. 7 He turned and saw me. He called to me. I said, 'Here I am.' 8 He asked, 'Who are you?' I said, 'I am an Amalekite.'

9 "He said, 'Stand over me and kill me. I am in great pain, but I am still alive.' 10 So I stood over him and killed him. I knew he could not live after he fell. I took the crown from his head and the band from his arm. I brought them to you, my lord."

11 David grabbed his clothes and tore them. All the men with him did the same.

12 They cried and did not eat until evening. They were sad for Saul, Jonathan, the the LORD's people, and Israel. They died by the sword.

13 David asked the young man, "Where are you from?"

He said, "I am an Amalekite. My father was a foreigner."

14 David said, "Why were you not afraid to hurt the the LORD's chosen king?"

15 David called one of the young men and said, "Go and strike him." The young man struck him, and he died.

16 David said, "Your blood is on your own head. Your mouth spoke against you. You said, 'I killed the the LORD's chosen king.'"

17 David sang a sad song for Saul and Jonathan.

David's Sad Song for Saul and Jonathan

18 David said, "Teach the people of Judah the bow song. It is written in the Book of Jashar."

19 "Israel, your pride lies dead on your hills. How the mighty have fallen! 20 Do not tell this in Gath. Do not say it in the streets of Ashkelon. The Philistine girls might be glad. Girls from other lands might cheer. 21 Mountains of Gilboa, may no dew or rain fall on you. May your fields not grow grain. For there the shield of the mighty was thrown down. Saul's shield was left without oil."

22 "From the blood of the dead and the fat of the mighty, Jonathan's bow did not turn back. Saul's sword did not come back empty. 23 Saul and Jonathan were loved and pleasant in life. In death they were not parted. They were faster than eagles. They were stronger than lions. 24 Daughters of Israel, cry for Saul. He clothed you in red with nice things. He put gold jewelry on your clothes."

25 "How the mighty have fallen in battle! Jonathan is dead on your hills. 26 I am sad for you, Jonathan my brother. You were very kind to me. Your love for me was wonderful. It was more than the love of women. 27 How the mighty have fallen! The weapons of war are gone."
