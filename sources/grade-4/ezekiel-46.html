The East Gate Rules

1 The Lord the LORD says, "The inner gate that faces east must stay shut on the six work days. But it must be open on the Sabbath day and on the day of the New Moon."

The Prince Comes to Worship

2 The prince comes in from outside through the porch of the gate. He stands by the gatepost. The priests get his burnt offering and his peace offerings ready. He bows down at the doorstep of the gate. Then he goes out. The gate stays open until evening.

The People Worship Too

3 On the Sabbath days and on the New Moons, the people of the land bow down before the LORD at the door of that gate.

Sabbath Offerings

4 On the Sabbath day, the prince gives the LORD a burnt offering. It is six lambs without a mark and one ram without a mark. 5 He gives a grain offering with it. He gives an ephah of grain for the ram. For the lambs, he gives as much as he can. He also gives a hin of oil for each ephah.

New Moon Offerings

6 On the day of the New Moon, he gives a young bull without a mark, six lambs, and a ram. They must have no mark. 7 He gives a grain offering with them. He gives an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram. For the lambs, he gives as much as he can. He also gives a hin of oil for each ephah.

How People Come and Go

8 When the prince comes in, he comes through the porch of the gate. He goes out the same way. 9 When the people come before the LORD at the special times, the one who comes in by the north gate goes out by the south gate. The one who comes in by the south gate goes out by the north gate. No one goes back out the same gate. They go out by the other gate. 10 The prince is with them. When they come in, he comes in. When they go out, he goes out.

Offerings at Feasts

11 At the feasts and special times, the grain offering is an ephah for a bull, an ephah for a ram, and as much as he can for the lambs. He also gives a hin of oil for each ephah.

The Prince's Free Gift

12 When the prince gives a free gift to the LORD, a burnt offering or peace offerings, the east gate is opened for him. He gives his burnt offering and his peace offerings the same way he does on the Sabbath day. Then he goes out. After he goes out, the gate is shut.

The Daily Offering

13 You shall give a lamb one year old without a mark as a burnt offering to the LORD every morning. You shall give it every morning.

14 And you shall get a grain offering ready with it every morning. It is one sixth of an ephah of fine flour. It is one third of a hin of oil to wet the fine flour. It is a grain offering to the LORD. This rule lasts forever.

15 They get the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil ready every morning. It is a regular burnt offering.

Gifts of Land

16 The Lord the LORD says, "If the prince gives a gift of land to one of his sons, it is his land. It belongs to his sons. It is their land to keep. 17 But if he gives a gift of land from his family land to one of his servants, it is his until the year of freedom. Then it goes back to the prince. But the family land belongs to his sons. 18 The prince must not take any of the people's land to push them out. He must give his sons land from his own land, so that none of My people are scattered from their land."

Rooms for the Priests

19 He brought me through the entrance by the side of the gate to the holy rooms for the priests. They faced north. There was a place at the far west end. 20 He said to me, "This is the place where the priests boil the guilt offering and the sin offering. They bake the grain offering here. They do not take them out to the outer court, so the people are not made holy."

The Outer Courts

21 He brought me out to the outer court. He led me past the four corners of the court. There was a small court in each corner. 22 In the four corners of the outer court were closed-in courts. They were 40 cubits long and 30 cubits wide. All four were the same size. 23 A wall went around all four. Cooking places were built under the walls all around.

Kitchens for the People

24 He said to me, "These are the kitchens. The temple workers boil the people's sacrifices here."
