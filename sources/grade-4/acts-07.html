The High Priest Asks Stephen a Question

1 The high priest asked Stephen, "Are these things true?"

Stephen Talks About Abraham

2 Stephen said, "Men, brothers and fathers, listen. The God of glory showed Himself to our father Abraham. This was in Mesopotamia, before Abraham lived in Haran."

3 God said, "Leave your country and your family. Go to the land I will show you."

4 Stephen said, "So Abraham left the land of Chaldea and lived in Haran. After his father died, God moved him to this land, where you live now."

5 God did not give him any land here. He did not even get a small piece of ground. But God promised to give the land to him and to his children after him. Abraham had no child yet.

6 God said, "Your children will live in a foreign land. They will be slaves and be treated badly for 400 years."

7 God said, "I will judge the nation they serve. After that, they will come out and worship Me in this place."

8 Stephen said, "God gave Abraham the agreement of circumcision. Then Abraham had Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day. Isaac had Jacob. Jacob had the twelve family leaders."

Stephen Talks About Joseph

9 Stephen said, "The family leaders were jealous of Joseph. They sold him to Egypt. But God was with Joseph."

10 God saved him from all his troubles. God gave him grace and wisdom before Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. Pharaoh made Joseph ruler over Egypt and over his whole house.

11 A big famine and much trouble came over Egypt and Canaan. Our fathers could not find food.

12 Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt. He sent our fathers there first.

13 The second time, Joseph made himself known to his brothers. Pharaoh learned about Joseph's family.

14 Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his family. There were 75 people.

15 Jacob went down to Egypt. He and our fathers died there.

16 Their bodies were moved to Shechem. They were buried in the tomb that Abraham bought from the sons of Hamor in Shechem. He paid silver for it.

17 The time was getting close for God's promise to Abraham. The people in Egypt grew and became many.

18 Then a new king came to power in Egypt. He did not know Joseph.

19 He tricked our people. He was cruel to our fathers. He made them leave their baby boys outside so they would die.

Stephen Talks About Moses

20 Stephen said, "At that time Moses was born. He was a beautiful child to God. He was cared for in his father's house for three months."

21 When he was put outside, Pharaoh's daughter took him. She raised him as her own son.

22 Moses learned all the wisdom of the Egyptians. He was strong in what he said and did.

23 When he was 40 years old, he decided to visit his brothers, the people of Israel.

24 He saw an Israelite being wronged. Moses defended him and punished the Egyptian by hitting him.

25 Moses thought his brothers would understand that God was giving them freedom through him. But they did not understand.

26 The next day he saw two of them fighting. He tried to make peace. He said, "Men, you are brothers. Why are you hurting each other?"

27 The man who was wronging his neighbor pushed Moses away. He said, "Who made you our ruler and judge? 28 Do you want to kill me the way you killed the Egyptian yesterday?"

29 Stephen said, "When Moses heard this, he ran away to Midian. He lived there as a stranger. He had two sons."

30 After 40 years, an angel appeared to him in a burning bush. This was in the wilderness near Mount Sinai.

31 Moses saw it and was amazed. When he went to look, the voice of the LORD came to him.

32 God said, "I am the God of your fathers. I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob." Moses was so afraid that he did not dare to look.

33 The LORD said, "Take off your sandals. The place where you are standing is holy ground." 34 He said, "I have seen how My people suffer in Egypt. I have heard their groaning. I have come down to set them free. Now come, I am sending you to Egypt."

35 Stephen said, "This is the Moses they refused. They said, 'Who made you our ruler and judge?' But God sent him as ruler and rescuer, with the angel who appeared to him in the bush."

36 He led them out. He did wonders and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and for 40 years in the wilderness.

37 Stephen said, "This is Moses, who said to the people of Israel, 'God will raise up a prophet for you like me. He will come from your own brothers.'"

38 This is the man who was with our people in the desert. He was with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai. He received living words to give to us.

39 Our fathers did not want to obey him. They pushed him away. In their hearts they turned back to Egypt.

40 Stephen said, "They said to Aaron, 'Make us gods to go before us. We do not know what happened to Moses, who led us out of Egypt.'"

41 So in those days they made a calf. They offered a sacrifice to the idol. They were happy with what their hands had made.

42 Stephen said, "But God turned away from them. He let them worship the stars in the sky. This is what the prophets wrote: 'Did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings for 40 years in the wilderness, O people of Israel? 43 You took up the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Rephan. You made these images to worship them. I will send you beyond Babylon.'"

Stephen Talks About the Tent and the Temple

44 Stephen said, "Our fathers had the tent of witness in the wilderness. They made it just as God told Moses. They followed the pattern Moses had seen. 45 Our fathers brought it with Joshua when they took the land from the nations God drove out before them. They used it until the time of David. 46 David found favor with God. He asked to find a home for the God of Jacob. 47 But Solomon built a house for God."

48 Stephen said, "But the Most High does not live in houses made by hands. The prophet says, 'Heaven is My throne. The earth is My footstool. What kind of house will you build for Me? says the LORD. Where is the place where I can rest? 50 Did not My hand make all these things?'"

Stephen Speaks to the Council

51 Stephen said, "You are stubborn people. Your hearts and ears are not right with God. You always fight against the Holy Spirit, just like your fathers did. 52 Which prophet did your fathers not hurt? They killed the men who told about the coming of the Righteous One. Now you have become traitors and murderers. 53 You received God's law, which angels gave, but you did not obey it."

The Council Gets Angry

54 When they heard this, they were very angry. They ground their teeth at him.

55 Stephen was full of the Holy Spirit. He looked up to heaven and saw God's glory. He saw Jesus standing at the right hand of God.

56 Stephen said, "Look! I see heaven open. I see the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God."

57 They covered their ears and shouted loudly. They all rushed at him.

58 They dragged him out of the city and threw stones at him. The witnesses laid their coats at the feet of a young man named Saul.

Stephen Is Killed

59 They stoned Stephen as he called out, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

60 Stephen knelt down and cried out loudly, "Lord, do not hold this sin against them." Then he died.
