The Famine Gets Worse

1 The famine was very bad in the land. 2 When the grain they had brought from Egypt was gone, their father said to them, "Go back and buy us a little more food."

3 Judah said to him, "The man warned us that we could not see his face unless our brother came with us. 4 If you send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you. 5 But if you will not send him, we will not go down, because the man said we could not see his face unless our brother came with us."

6 Israel said, "Why did you treat me so badly by telling the man you have another brother?"

7 They said, "The man asked us about ourselves and our family. He asked if our father was still alive and if we had another brother. We told him what he asked. How could we know he would tell us to bring our brother down?"

Judah Promises to Keep Benjamin Safe

8 Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the boy with me. We will go now. Then we will live and not die. We, you, and our children will all live. 9 I will be responsible for him. You can hold me to it. If I do not bring him back to you and show him to you, I will be to blame as long as I live. 10 If we had not waited, we could have gone and come back twice by now."

Jacob Sends Gifts to Egypt

11 Their father Israel said to them, "If it must be so, then do this: Take some of the best things of the land in your bags. Carry down a gift to the man: a little balm, a little honey, spices, myrrh, pistachio nuts, and almonds. 12 Take twice as much money with you. Carry back the money that was put back in your sacks. Maybe it was a mistake. 13 Take your brother too. Go back to the man. 14 May God Almighty show you mercy before the man, so he sends your other brother and Benjamin back. As for me, if I lose them, I lose them."

15 So the men took the gift and twice as much money. They took Benjamin too. They went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.

Joseph Sees Benjamin

16 When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the man over his house, "Bring the men into the house. Kill an animal and make it ready, because the men will eat with me at noon." 17 The man did as Joseph said. He brought the men to Joseph's house.

18 The men were afraid because they were brought into Joseph's house. They said, "We are brought in because of the money that was put back in our sacks the first time. He wants to blame us, attack us, and make us slaves, along with our donkeys."

The Brothers Explain the Money

19 They went up to the man who was over Joseph's house and spoke to him at the door of the house. 20 They said, "Please, sir, we came down the first time to buy food. 21 When we reached the place where we stayed, we opened our sacks. Each man's money was in his sack, all of it. We brought it back with us. 22 We also brought more money to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks."

23 He said, "Peace to you. Do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you treasure in your sacks. I got your money." Then he brought Simeon out to them.

24 The man brought the men into Joseph's house. He gave them water to wash their feet and gave food to their donkeys. 25 They got the gift ready for Joseph to come at noon, because they heard they would eat there.

Joseph Eats with His Brothers

26 When Joseph came home, they brought him the gift in the house and bowed down to him on the ground.

27 He asked how they were. He said, "Is your old father well, the one you told me about? Is he still alive?"

28 They said, "Our father, your servant, is well. He is still alive." Then they bowed down.

29 He looked up and saw Benjamin, his mother's son. He said, "Is this your youngest brother, the one you told me about?" Then he said, "May God be kind to you, my son."

30 Joseph hurried away because he felt deep love for his brother. He looked for a place to cry. He went into his room and cried there. 31 Then he washed his face and came out. He got control of himself and said, "Serve the food."

32 They served Joseph by himself. They served his brothers by themselves. The Egyptians who ate with him sat by themselves. Egyptians will not eat with Hebrews. They think it is wrong. 33 They sat before him, the oldest first and the youngest last. The men were surprised. 34 He sent food to them from his table. But Benjamin got five times as much as any of them. They ate and drank and were happy with him.
