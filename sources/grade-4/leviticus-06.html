Rules for Guilt Offerings

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. 2 "If a person sins against the LORD by lying to a neighbor about something given to him to keep, or something left with him, or something stolen, or by cheating a neighbor, 3 or if he finds something lost and lies about it, or swears a false promise about any of these sins, 4 then he has sinned and is guilty. He must give back what he stole or took by force, or the thing left with him, or the lost thing he found. 5 He must pay for whatever he lied about. He must pay the full amount plus one-fifth more. He must give it to the owner on the day he says he is guilty. 6 Then he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD through the priest: a ram from the flock with no flaw, worth the right amount. 7 The priest will make things right for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for any of these things he has done."

Rules for Burnt and Grain Offerings

8 The LORD spoke to Moses. 9 "Command Aaron and his sons: This is the rule for the burnt offering. The burnt offering must stay on the fire on the altar all night until morning. The fire on the altar must keep burning. 10 The priest must put on his linen clothes and linen underclothes. He must take the ashes from the burnt offering off the fire and put them beside the altar. 11 Then he must take off those clothes and put on other clothes. He must carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. 12 The fire on the altar must keep burning. It must not go out. Each morning the priest must put wood on the fire. He must place the burnt offering on it. He must burn the fat parts of the peace offerings on it. 13 The fire must keep burning on the altar all the time. It must not go out.

"14 This is the rule for the grain offering. Aaron's sons must bring it before the LORD, in front of the altar. 15 The priest must take a handful of fine flour and oil from it, and all the sweet-smelling resin on it. He must burn this part on the altar. It is a sweet smell to the LORD. 16 Aaron and his sons must eat what is left. They must eat it as bread without yeast in a holy place. They must eat it in the yard of the meeting tent. 17 It must not be baked with yeast. I have given it as their share of My gifts burned by fire. It is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering. 18 Any male from Aaron's family may eat it. It is their share forever from the gifts burned by fire to the LORD for all time. Anything that touches these gifts will become holy."

Rules for the Priest's Grain Offering

19 The LORD spoke to Moses. 20 "This is the offering Aaron and his sons must bring to the LORD on the day he becomes priest: a small amount of fine flour as a regular grain offering. They must bring half in the morning and half in the evening. 21 It must be made with oil on a flat pan. Bring it well mixed. Bring it as baked pieces of grain offering, a sweet smell to the LORD. 22 The priest who takes his place must make it. It is a rule forever for the LORD. It must be burned completely. 23 Every grain offering from a priest must be burned completely. It must not be eaten."

Rules for Sin Offerings

24 The LORD spoke to Moses. 25 "Tell Aaron and his sons: This is the rule for the sin offering. The sin offering must be killed where the burnt offering is killed, before the LORD. It is most holy. 26 The priest who offers it must eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place, in the yard of the meeting tent. 27 Anything that touches its meat will become holy. If any of its blood gets on a piece of clothing, you must wash that clothing in a holy place.

"28 The clay pot used to boil it must be broken. If it is boiled in a bronze pot, the pot must be scrubbed and washed with water. 29 Any male among the priests may eat it. It is most holy. 30 But no sin offering may be eaten if its blood was brought into the meeting tent to pay for sin in the holy place. It must be burned."
