Paul Greets the Church

1 Paul, a worker sent by Christ Jesus because God wanted it, and Timothy, our brother. 2 To God's holy and faithful people in Christ at Colossae: May God our Father give you grace and peace.

Paul Thanks God for Them

3 We always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. We do this when we pray for you. 4 We heard about your faith in Christ Jesus. We heard about your love for all God's people. 5 You have this hope. It is saved for you in heaven. You heard about it before in the true message, the good news. 6 This good news is now with you. It is growing and bearing fruit all over the world. It is doing the same with you. It has done this since the day you heard it. It has done this since you truly knew God's grace. 7 You learned it from Epaphras, our dear fellow worker. He is a faithful helper of Christ for you. 8 He also told us about your love in the Spirit.

Paul Prays for the Church

9 Because of this, since the day we heard, we have not stopped praying for you. We ask that you may be filled with the knowledge of His will. We ask that you may have spiritual wisdom and understanding. 10 Then you can live in a way that honors the Lord. You can please Him in every way. You can bear fruit in every good work. You can grow in knowing God. 11 You will be made strong with all power, by His glorious might. Then you can have great patience. You will not give up. Do this with joy. 12 Give thanks to the Father. He has made you able to share in the good things God gives His holy people in the light. 13 He has saved us from the power of darkness. He has brought us into the kingdom of His dear Son. 14 In Him we have freedom. Our sins are forgiven.

Who Christ Is

15 The Son is the image of the God we cannot see. He is first over all creation. 16 All things were made through Him and for Him. This includes things in heaven and on earth. It includes seen and unseen things. It includes thrones, powers, rulers, and authorities. 17 He is before all things. All things hold together in Him. 18 He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning. He is first to rise from the dead. So He is first in everything. 19 God was pleased to have all of Himself live in Him.

God Made Peace Through Christ

20 Through Him God made peace. He brought all things back to Himself. This includes things on earth and in heaven. He did this by the blood of His cross. 21 Once you were far from God. You were enemies in your minds. This was because of your evil deeds. 22 But now God has brought you back to Himself. He did this through Christ's body and death. He wants you to be holy, without blame, and without fault before Him. 23 But you must keep going in your faith. Be strong and firm. Do not move away from the hope of the good news you heard. This good news was preached to all creation under heaven. I, Paul, became a helper of this good news.

Paul's Work for the Church

24 Now I am glad to suffer for you. In my body, I am filling up what is still needed of Christ's sufferings. I do this for His body, the church. 25 I became a helper of this by God's plan. God gave me this work. I fully share God's word with you. 26 This secret was hidden for ages and generations. But now it has been shown to God's holy people. 27 God wanted to make known to the nations how great and rich this secret is. The secret is Christ in you. He is your hope of great honor. 28 We tell about Him. We warn every person. We teach every person with all wisdom. We want to present every person complete in Christ. 29 For this I work hard. I struggle with all the strength Christ gives me.
