Israel Broke God's Agreement

1 Blow the trumpet! Put the trumpet to your lips! An eagle flies over the the LORD's house. Israel broke My agreement and turned away from My rules.

2 Israel cries out to Me, "Our God, we know You!"

3 But Israel turned away from what is good. An enemy will chase him.

Israel Made Idols

4 They chose kings, but not by Me. They made leaders, but I did not pick them. They used silver and gold to make idols for themselves. That will destroy them.

5 I rejected your calf, Samaria. I am angry with them. How long until they are clean?

6 It came from Israel. A worker made it. It is not God. Samaria's calf will be broken into pieces.

Israel Will Get What It Plants

7 They plant wind and get a storm. The grain does not stand. What sprouts makes no flour. Even if it does, strangers eat it.

8 Israel is swallowed up. Now they are among the nations. No one wants them, like a broken pot.

Israel Looked for Help in the Wrong Place

9 They went to Assyria. They are like a wild donkey alone. Ephraim paid other nations to be on their side.

10 Even if they hire nations to help them, I will gather them now. They will start to grow weak under the heavy rule of the great king.

11 Ephraim made many altars for sin, but the altars became places for more sin.

God Will Punish Israel

12 I wrote ten thousand of My rules for him, but he thought they were strange.

13 They offer gifts to Me and eat the meat, but the the LORD does not accept them. Now He will remember their wrongs and punish their sins. They will go back to Egypt.

14 Israel forgot his Maker and built palaces. Judah built many strong cities. But I will send fire on their cities, and it will burn their strong buildings.
