Solomon Rules All Israel

1 King Solomon ruled all Israel. 2 These were his leaders. Azariah son of Zadok was the priest. 3 Elihoreph and Ahijah, sons of Shisha, were writers. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud wrote down what happened. 4 Benaiah son of Jehoiada led the army. Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 5 Azariah son of Nathan led the officers. Zabud son of Nathan was a priest and the king's friend. 6 Ahishar ran the palace. Adoniram son of Abda was over the workers who had to work for the king.

Solomon's Twelve Helpers

7 Solomon had twelve helpers over all Israel. They fed the king and his house. Each helper did this one month each year. 8 Their names were these. Ben-Hur was in the hills of Ephraim. 9 Ben-Deker was in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth Shemesh, Aijalon, and Beth-hanan. 10 Ben-Hesed was in Arubboth. He also had Socoh and all the land of Hepher.

11 Ben-Abinadab was in all of Naphath-dor. Taphath, Solomon's daughter, was his wife. 12 Baana son of Ahilud was in Taanach, Megiddo, and all Beth-shean near Zarethan, below Jezreel. His land went from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and past Jokneam. 13 Ben-Geber was in Ramoth Gilead. He also had the towns of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead. He had Gilead and the land of Argob in Bashan. There were sixty big cities there with strong walls and bronze gates.

14 Ahinadab son of Iddo was in Mahanaim. 15 Ahimaaz was in Naphtali. He also married Basemath, Solomon's daughter. 16 Baana son of Hushai was in Asher and Bealoth. 17 Jehoshaphat son of Paruah was in Issachar. 18 Shimei son of Ela was in Benjamin. 19 Geber son of Uri was in the land of Gilead. This was the land of Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan. One helper was in charge of that land.

Israel Grows and Is Happy

20 Judah and Israel were many. They were like sand by the sea. They ate, drank, and were happy. 21 Solomon ruled all the kingdoms from the River to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt. They brought gifts and served Solomon all his life.

Food for Solomon's House

22 Solomon's food for one day was thirty large baskets of fine flour and sixty large baskets of meal. 23 It also had ten fat oxen, twenty oxen from the field, and one hundred sheep. This was besides deer, gazelle, and fattened birds. 24 He ruled all the land west of the River. He ruled all the kings west of the River, from Tiphsah to Gaza. He had peace all around. 25 All the days of Solomon, Judah and Israel lived safely. They lived from Dan to Beersheba. Each man sat under his vine and under his fig tree. 26 Solomon had forty thousand stalls for chariot horses. He had twelve thousand horsemen.

27 The helpers fed King Solomon and all who came to the king's table. Each helper did this one month. They made sure nothing was missing. 28 They brought barley and straw for the horses and chariot horses. They brought it to the place where it was needed. Each helper did this as needed.

Solomon's Great Wisdom

29 God gave Solomon wisdom. It was very great. His understanding was wide like the sand on the seashore. 30 Solomon's wisdom was greater than the wisdom of all the sons of the east. It was greater than all the wisdom of Egypt. 31 He was wiser than all men. He was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, sons of Mahol. His name was known in all the nations around. 32 He spoke three thousand proverbs. He had one thousand and five songs. 33 He spoke about trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop on the wall. He spoke about animals, birds, crawling things, and fish. 34 People came from all nations to hear Solomon's wisdom. All the kings of the earth who heard of his wisdom came too.
