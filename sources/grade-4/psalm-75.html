A Song of Thanks

For the music leader. To the tune "Do Not Destroy." A song by Asaph. 1We give thanks to You, God; we give thanks, for You are near to us. People tell about Your amazing works.

God Speaks

2"When I choose the right time, I will judge fairly. 3The earth and everyone in it may shake, but I keep its pillars steady. 4I told proud people, 'Stop bragging,' and I told bad people, 'Do not show off your power.' 5Do not act proud. Do not speak like you are better than others."

6Being lifted up does not come from the east or the west. It does not come from the desert either. 7God is the judge. He brings one person down and lifts another up. 8The LORD holds a cup. It is full of wine mixed with spices. He pours it out, and all the bad people of the earth drink it to the last drop.

9But I will tell of this forever. I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

God Will Judge the Wicked

10"I will take away all the power of bad people, but good people will be lifted up."
