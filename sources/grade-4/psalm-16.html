A Prayer of David

1 Keep me safe, God, because I trust in You.

2 I said to the LORD, "You are my Lord. Without You, nothing is good for me."

3 As for God's holy people in the land, they are the best ones. I like them very much.

4 People who run after other gods will have more and more trouble. I will not pour their blood offerings. I will not say their names.

God Gives Me Good Things

5 The LORD is my share and my cup. You keep my place safe.

6 My land is in a good place. My home is nice to me.

7 I will praise the LORD, because He gives me advice. Even at night, my heart teaches me.

8 I keep the LORD in front of me always. Because He is beside me, I will not fall.

9 So my heart is glad, and I praise Him with my tongue. My body will live in peace.

God Shows Me Life

10 For You will not leave me in the grave. You will not let Your Holy One rot.

11 You will show me the way to live. In Your presence, there is so much joy. At Your right hand, happy things last forever.
