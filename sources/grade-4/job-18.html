Bildad Answers Job

1 Then Bildad, a man from Shuh, spoke.

Bildad Tells Job About Bad People

2 "How long will you keep talking? Think first, then we can talk. 3 Why do you treat us like animals? Why do you think we are not smart? 4 You are tearing yourself apart in anger. Should the whole world be left empty just for you? Should a big rock be moved from where it sits?

Bad People Lose Their Light

5 "The light of bad people goes out. The fire they make does not shine. 6 The light in his tent gets dark. The lamp above him goes out.

Bad Plans Catch Him

7 "His strong steps get shorter. His own bad plans make him fall. 8 His feet get caught in a net. He walks right into its ropes. 9 A trap grabs his heel. A snare holds him tight. 10 A rope is hidden in the ground for him. A trap waits on his path.

Fears and Trouble Come

11 "Fears scare him all around. They chase him with every step. 12 He has no strength left. Trouble is waiting right beside him. 13 Trouble eats pieces of his skin. Death eats the rest of him. 14 He is pulled from his safe tent. He is led away to death.

His Home Is Ruined

15 "Fire stays in his tent. Burning sulfur is thrown over his home. 16 His roots dry up below. His branches die above. 17 He is forgotten on earth. He has no name in the land. 18 He is pushed from light into darkness. He is chased out of the world.

No One Is Left

19 "He has no children or grandchildren among his people. No one is left in his home. 20 People in the west are shocked at what happened to him. People in the east shake with fear. 21 This is how bad people end up. This is the home of one who does not know God."
