Be One in Christ

1 If being in Christ helps you, if love comforts you, if you share the Spirit, if you have kind hearts and care, 2 then fill me with joy. Think the same way. Have the same love. Be one in heart and mind. 3 Do not do things just to win or to look good. Be humble. Think others are more important than you. 4 Do not only look at your own needs. Look at other people's needs too. 5 Think the way Christ Jesus thought.

6 He was God, but He did not hold on to being equal with God. 7 Instead, He made Himself nothing. He became a servant. He became like a man. 8 He was found as a man. He humbled Himself. He obeyed even to death, even death on a cross. 9 So God lifted Him up very high. God gave Him the name above every name. 10 At the name of Jesus, every knee will bow. Knees in heaven, on earth, and under the earth will bow. 11 Every tongue will say that Jesus Christ is Lord. This brings glory to God the Father.

Work Out Your Salvation

12 So, my dear friends, you obeyed when I was with you. Now obey even more when I am away. Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. 13 For God is at work in you. He gives you the want to do good and the power to do it. This pleases Him. 14 Do everything without complaining or arguing. 15 Then you will be pure and good. You will be God's children without blame. You live among bad and crooked people. Shine like lights in the world. 16 Hold fast to the word of life. Then on the day of Christ, I can be proud. I will know I did not run for nothing. I did not work for nothing.

17 Even if my life is poured out like a drink offering on your faith, I am glad. I share joy with all of you. 18 You should be glad too. Share joy with me.

Timothy

19 I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon. Then I can be cheered by news about you. 20 I have no one like him. He truly cares about you. 21 All the others look out for themselves. They do not care about Jesus Christ. 22 But you know Timothy. He served with me like a son with a father. He helped me share the good news. 23 So I hope to send him as soon as I see how things go with me. 24 I trust in the Lord that I will come soon too.

Epaphroditus

25 I think I need to send Epaphroditus to you. He is my brother, my helper, and my fellow soldier. He is also your messenger. He helped me when I needed it. 26 He longs to see all of you. He was upset because you heard he was sick. 27 He was sick, almost to death. But God had mercy on him. God had mercy on me too. I did not want more sorrow on top of sorrow. 28 So I am sending him even more. I want you to be glad to see him. I want to be less worried. 29 Welcome him in the Lord with all joy. Honor people like him. 30 He almost died for the work of Christ. He risked his life to make up for the help you could not give me.
