Listen to God's Word

1 Listen to the word the the LORD speaks to you, people of Israel.

2 This is what the the LORD says: "Do not learn the ways of other nations. Do not be scared by signs in the sky. Other nations are scared by them. 3 The customs of the nations are worthless. They cut down a tree from the forest. A worker shapes it with his tool. 4 They decorate it with silver and gold. They nail it down with a hammer and nails so it will not fall over. 5 They are like a scarecrow in a field. They cannot talk. They must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not be afraid of them. They cannot hurt anyone, and they cannot help anyone."

The LORD Is the True God

6 No one is like You, the LORD. You are great, and Your name is strong and powerful. 7 Everyone should fear You, King of the nations. You deserve it. Among all the wise men of the nations and in all their kingdoms, no one is like You. 8 They are all foolish. They learn from worthless idols made of wood. 9 People bring silver from Tarshish and gold from Uphaz. A worker and a goldsmith make the idols. They cover them with blue and purple cloth. Skilled men make all of this. 10 But the the LORD is the true God. He is the living God and the King forever. The earth shakes when He is angry. The nations cannot stand up to His anger.

Tell the Nations

11 So you must tell them this: "The gods who did not make the sky and the earth will disappear from the earth and from under the sky."

God Made Everything

12 God made the earth with His power. He set the world in place with His wisdom. He stretched out the sky with His understanding. 13 When He thunders, the waters in the sky roar. He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning with the rain. He brings the wind out of His storehouses.

Idols Are Useless

14 Every person is foolish and knows nothing. Every goldsmith is ashamed of his idols. His metal images are a lie. They have no breath. 15 They are worthless. They are made to trick people. When the time comes for them to be punished, they will be destroyed. 16 The Portion of Jacob is not like them. He made everything. Israel is the people He chose to be His own. The LORD of Hosts is His name.

Judgment Is Coming

17 Gather your things, people of Zion. You live under attack. 18 For this is what the the LORD says: "I will throw out the people who live in this land. I will bring trouble on them so they can be caught."

Jeremiah Is Sad

19 I am so sad because I am broken. My wound hurts. But I said, "This is my sickness, and I must carry it." 20 My tent is destroyed. All its ropes are cut. My children have left me and are gone. No one is left to set up my tent or hang my curtains. 21 The leaders are foolish. They do not look for the the LORD. So they have not done well, and all their sheep are scattered. 22 Listen! News is coming. There is a loud noise from the north. It will make the cities of Judah empty. They will become homes for wild animals.

A Prayer to the LORD

23 I know, the LORD, that people cannot choose their own way. No one who walks can direct his own steps. 24 Correct me, the LORD, but be fair. Do not correct me in Your anger, or You will destroy me. 25 Pour out Your anger on the nations that do not know You. Pour it on the families that do not pray to You. They have destroyed Jacob. They have wiped him out. They have ruined his home.
