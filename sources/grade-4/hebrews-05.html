Jesus Is Our High Priest

1 Every high priest is chosen from among people. He is chosen to work for people with God. He offers gifts and sacrifices for sins. 2 He can be gentle with people who do not know and who go wrong, because he is weak too. 3 Because of this, he must offer a gift for his own sins, just like he does for the people. 4 No one takes this honor for himself. He must be called by God, like Aaron was.

5 In the same way, Christ did not make Himself high priest. God said to Him, "You are My Son. Today I have become Your Father." 6 And in another place He says, "You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek."

7 While He lived on earth, Jesus prayed to God with a loud voice and tears. God could save Him from death. God heard Him because Jesus respected God. 8 Even though He was the Son, He learned to obey by what He suffered. 9 After He was made perfect, He became the way for all who obey Him to be saved forever. 10 God called Him high priest in the order of Melchizedek.

Grow in God's Word

11 We have much to say about this. It is hard to explain because you are slow to listen. 12 By now you should be teachers. But you need someone to teach you again the first lessons of God's word. You need milk, not solid food. 13 Anyone who lives on milk is still a baby. He does not know much about right living. 14 Solid food is for grown people. They have learned to tell good from bad.
