God Tells Jacob to Go to Bethel

1 God said to Jacob, "Get up. Go up to Bethel and stay there. Build an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you ran away from your brother Esau."

Jacob's Family Gets Ready

2 Then Jacob said to his family and to all who were with him, "Get rid of the false gods you have with you. Make yourselves clean. Change your clothes."

3 He said, "Let us get up and go up to Bethel. I will build an altar there to God. He is the God who answered me when I was in trouble. He has been with me everywhere I went."

4 So they gave Jacob all the false gods they had and the rings in their ears. Jacob hid them under the oak tree near Shechem.

5 They left there. God made the people in the nearby towns afraid, so no one chased Jacob's sons.

Jacob Builds an Altar at Bethel

6 Jacob and all the people with him came to Luz, which is Bethel, in the land of Canaan.

7 There he built an altar. He called the place El Bethel, because God had shown Himself to him there when he ran away from his brother.

Deborah Dies

8 Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died. She was buried under the oak tree below Bethel. Jacob called the tree Allon Bacuth, which means "oak of weeping."

God Blesses Jacob Again

9 When Jacob came back from Paddan Aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.

God Changes Jacob's Name

10 God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. You will not be called Jacob anymore. Your name will be Israel." So God called him Israel.

God Promises Jacob

11 God said to him, "I am God Almighty. Have many children and grow in number. Nations and groups of nations will come from you. Kings will be born from your family. 12 The land I gave to Abraham and Isaac, I will give to you. I will give this land to you and to your children after you."

Jacob Honors God

13 Then God went up from Jacob in the place where He had spoken with him.

14 Jacob set up a stone pillar where God had spoken with him. He poured a drink offering and oil on it.

15 Jacob named the place Bethel, because God had spoken to him there.

Rachel Dies

16 They left Bethel. While they were still a long way from Ephrath, Rachel began to have her baby. She had a hard time giving birth.

17 While Rachel was having a hard time, the woman helping her said, "Do not be afraid. You will have another son."

18 As Rachel was dying, she named her son Ben-Oni. But his father named him Benjamin.

19 Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem.

20 Jacob set up a pillar over her grave. It is still called Rachel's tomb today.

Jacob's Family Grows

21 Israel moved on and put his tent past Migdal Eder.

22 While Israel lived there, Reuben went and slept with Bilhah, his father's servant wife. Israel heard about it. Jacob had twelve sons.

23 The sons of Leah were Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 24 The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin. 25 The sons of Bilhah, Rachel's servant, were Dan and Naphtali. 26 The sons of Zilpah, Leah's servant, were Gad and Asher. These were Jacob's sons who were born to him in Paddan Aram.

Isaac Dies

27 Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath Arba, which is Hebron. Abraham and Isaac had lived there.

28 Isaac lived to be 180 years old.

29 Isaac was very old when he died. Esau and Jacob buried him.
