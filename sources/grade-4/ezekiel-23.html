Two Sisters Who Were Not Faithful

1 The word of the LORD came to me. He said, "2 Son of man, two women were born to one mother. 3 They cheated in Egypt. They cheated when they were young. There men touched their chests, and there they lost their purity. 4 Their names were Oholah, the older sister, and Oholibah. They became Mine. They had sons and daughters. Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem."

"5 Oholah cheated while she was Mine. She wanted her lovers, the Assyrians, who were near. 6 They wore blue clothes. They were leaders and officials. They were all handsome young men. They rode horses. 7 She gave herself to them. They were the best men of Assyria. She made herself dirty with all their idols. She wanted them. 8 She did not stop cheating from Egypt. When she was young, men slept with her. They took her purity. They poured their cheating on her. 9 So I gave her to her lovers. I gave her to the Assyrians she wanted. 10 They showed her nakedness. They took her sons and daughters. They killed them with swords. She became a bad name among women. They punished her."

Oholibah Is Worse

"11 Her sister Oholibah saw this. She cheated even more. Her cheating was worse than her sister's. 12 She wanted the men of Assyria: leaders and officials who were near. They wore beautiful clothes. They were horsemen on horses. They were all handsome young men. 13 I saw that both sisters were dirty. They went the same bad way."

"14 She cheated even more. She saw men carved on a wall. They were pictures of Chaldeans carved in red. 15 They wore belts around their waists. They had long hats on their heads. They all looked like officers. They looked like Babylonians, the Chaldeans, from their home land. 16 She wanted them when she saw them. She sent messengers to them in Chaldea. 17 The sons of Babylon came to her bed of love. They made her dirty by cheating. She was dirty because of them. Then she turned away from them in disgust. 18 She showed her cheating. She showed her nakedness. Then I turned away from her, like I turned away from her sister. 19 Still she cheated more. She remembered her young days when she cheated in Egypt. 20 She wanted men whose bodies were like donkeys and whose strength was like horses. 21 You remembered your cheating from when you were young. The Egyptians touched your chest when you were young."

God Will Punish Oholibah

"22 So, Oholibah, this is what the Lord, the LORD says: 'I will stir up your lovers against you. You are sick of them. I will bring them against you from all sides. 23 They are Babylonians and all the Chaldeans: Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them. They are handsome young men, leaders and officials, officers and famous men. They all ride horses. 24 They will come against you with weapons, chariots, and wagons, and with many people. They will put shields and helmets around you. I will let them judge you by their own rules. 25 I will be jealous for you. They will hurt you in anger. They will cut off your nose and ears. Your people left alive will fall by swords. They will take your sons and daughters. Your people left alive will burn in fire. 26 They will strip off your clothes and take your pretty things. 27 So I will stop your cheating and your Egypt cheating. You will not look at them again. You will not remember Egypt anymore."

"28 This is what the Lord, the LORD says: 'I will give you to people you hate. I will give you to people you are sick of. 29 They will hurt you with hate. They will take all you worked for. They will leave you naked and bare. Your cheating will be seen. Your bad ways and cheating will be shown. 30 This will happen because you cheated after other nations. You made yourself dirty with their idols. 31 You walked your sister's way. So I will put her cup in your hand. 32 This is what the Lord, the LORD says: 'You will drink your sister's cup. It is deep and wide. It holds a lot. People will laugh at you and make fun of you. 33 You will be full of drunkenness and sadness. It is a cup of horror and ruin. It is the cup of your sister Samaria. 34 You will drink it and empty it. You will bite its broken pieces and tear your chest. I have spoken, says the Lord, the LORD.' 35 So this is what the Lord, the LORD says: 'Because you forgot Me and turned your back on Me, you must carry your bad ways and your cheating.'"

The Sisters' Terrible Sins

36 The LORD said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then tell them their terrible sins. 37 They cheated on their husband. Blood is on their hands. They cheated with their idols. They even passed their sons, whom they had for Me, through fire as food for their idols. 38 They did this to Me too: They made My holy place dirty. They treated My Sabbath days as not holy. 39 On the day they killed their children for their idols, they came into My holy place to make it dirty. See, they did this in My house. 40 They even sent for men from far away. A messenger was sent to them. They came. For those men you bathed, painted your eyes, and put on jewelry. 41 You sat on a fancy bed with a table set before it. On it you put My incense and My oil. 42 A noisy crowd was with her. Men from the desert came, drunk men from many people. They put bracelets on the women's wrists and pretty crowns on their heads. 43 Then I said that the woman worn out by cheating would now cheat with men, and they with her. 44 They went to her like men go to a prostitute. So they went to Oholah and Oholibah, those wicked women. 45 But good men will judge them. They will judge them like cheating wives and like women who kill. They are cheating wives, and blood is on their hands."

The Crowd Will Judge Them

"46 This is what the Lord, the LORD says: 'Bring a crowd against them. Give them to fear and to being robbed. 47 The crowd will stone them and cut them down with swords. They will kill their sons and daughters. They will burn their houses with fire. 48 So I will make cheating stop in the land. All women will learn a lesson and not cheat like yours. 49 They will pay you back for your cheating. You will carry the sin of your idols. Then you will know that I am the Lord, the LORD.'"
