Be Good Citizens

1 Remind them to obey leaders and people in charge, and to be ready to do every good thing. 2 They should not say bad things about anyone. They should be peaceful and kind, and show true gentleness to everyone.

3 We were once like that too. We were foolish. We did not obey. We were tricked. We served many wrong wants and pleasures. We lived in badness and jealousy. People hated us, and we hated each other.

God Saved Us

4 But then God our Savior showed His kindness and love to people. 5 He saved us. He did not save us because of good things we did. He saved us because of His mercy. He washed us and made us new through the Holy Spirit. 6 He poured out the Holy Spirit on us in a big way through Jesus Christ our Savior. 7 So now, by His grace, we are right with God. We can hope to live forever.

8 This is a true saying. I want you to be sure about these things. Then people who believe in God will be careful to do good works. These things are good and help people.

Stay Away From Foolish Talk

9 But stay away from foolish questions, family histories, fights, and arguments about the law. They do not help. They are useless. 10 Warn a person who causes trouble once or twice. Then stay away from him. 11 Know that this kind of person is turned away from truth and keeps sinning. He brings judgment on himself.

Paul's Final Words

12 When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, try hard to come to me at Nicopolis. I have decided to stay there for the winter. 13 Help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their trip. Make sure they have what they need. 14 Our people should also learn to do good works and help people in real need. Then they will not be useless. 15 Everyone with me says hello to you. Say hello to those who love us because of faith. May grace be with all of you.
