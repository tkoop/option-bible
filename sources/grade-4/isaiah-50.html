The LORD Speaks to His People

1 This is what the LORD says: "Where is the divorce paper for your mother? I did not send her away without a reason. Or did I sell you to pay a debt? No. You were sold because of your sins. Your mother was sent away because of your wrongs. 2 Why was no one here when I came? Why did no one answer when I called? Am I not strong enough to save you? Do I have no power to help? Look, I can dry up the sea with a word. I can turn rivers into dry land. The fish die and rot because there is no water. 3 I can cover the sky with darkness. I can put black cloth over it."

The Servant Speaks

4 The Lord the LORD gave Me words to teach. He taught Me how to help tired people with a word. Each morning He wakes Me. He opens My ear to listen like a student. 5 The Lord the LORD opened My ear. I did not say no. I did not turn back. 6 I let people hit My back. I let them pull hair from My face. I did not hide My face from shame and spit. 7 But the Lord the LORD helps Me. So I am not ashamed. So I set My face like a hard rock. I know I will not be put to shame. 8 The One who says I am right is near. Who can argue with Me? Let us stand together. Who can take Me to court? Let him come near. 9 Look, the Lord the LORD helps Me. Who can say I am guilty? Look, all My enemies will wear out like old clothes. Moths will eat them.

A Warning and a Call

10 Who among you fears the LORD and listens to His servant? If you walk in the dark with no light, trust in the name of the LORD. Lean on your God. 11 Look, all of you start fires and light torches. Walk in the light of your fire. Walk by the torches you made. But this is what you get from Me: you will lie down in pain.
