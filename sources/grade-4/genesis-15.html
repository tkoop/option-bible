God Talks to Abram

1 After all this, the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision. The LORD said, "Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your shield. Your reward will be very great."

2 But Abram said, "Lord the LORD, what will You give me? I have no children. The one who will get my house is Eliezer from Damascus." 3 Abram said, "Look, You have given me no child. So a servant in my house will get everything I own."

4 Then the word of the LORD came to him. He said, "This man will not get your things. A son of your own will get them."

5 The LORD took him outside and said, "Look up at the sky and count the stars, if you can. So big will your family be."

6 Abram believed the LORD. The LORD said that Abram was right with Him.

7 The LORD also said to him, "I am the LORD. I brought you out of Ur in Chaldea to give you this land to own."

Abram Asks How He Will Know

8 But Abram asked, "Lord the LORD, how will I know I will own it?"

Abram Gets the Animals Ready

9 The LORD said to him, "Bring Me a young cow, a goat, and a male sheep. Each should be three years old. Also bring a dove and a young pigeon."

10 So Abram brought all these animals to the LORD. He cut them in two and put the halves across from each other. But he did not cut the birds in half.

11 Birds that eat dead things came down on the pieces. Abram chased them away.

12 As the sun was going down, Abram fell into a deep sleep. A deep, scary darkness fell on him.

God Tells About the Future

13 Then the LORD said to Abram, "Know for sure that your children will live in a land that is not theirs. They will be slaves and be treated badly for four hundred years. 14 But I will punish the nation that makes them slaves. After that, they will leave with many good things.

15 "You, however, will go to your fathers in peace. You will be buried at a good old age. 16 After four generations, your children will come back here. The Amorites have not yet done all their wrong."

17 When the sun had set and it was dark, a smoking pot and a burning torch passed between the pieces.

God Makes His Promise Sure

18 On that day the LORD made a promise with Abram. He said, "I have given this land to your children. It goes from the river in Egypt to the great Euphrates River. 19 It is the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, 20 Hittites, Perizzites, and Rephaim, 21 Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites."
