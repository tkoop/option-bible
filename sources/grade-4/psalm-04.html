David Prays for Help

1 Answer me when I call, God who does what is right. You helped me when I was in trouble. Be kind to me and hear my prayer.

David Talks to His Enemies

2 How long will you people turn my good name into shame? How long will you love empty things and look for lies? Selah

God Keeps His People Safe

3 Know that the the LORD has chosen good people for Himself. The the LORD will hear me when I call to Him. 4 Be angry, but do not sin. On your bed, think in your heart and be still. Selah 5 Offer right sacrifices, and trust in the the LORD.

David Asks for God's Blessing

6 Many are asking, "Who can show us the good?"

Shine the light of Your face on us, O the LORD.

David Rests in Peace

7 You have filled my heart with more joy than when they have lots of food and drink. 8 In peace I will lie down and sleep, for You alone, O the LORD, keep me safe.
