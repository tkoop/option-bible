A New King from Jesse's Family

1 A new branch will grow from Jesse's family. From his roots, it will bear fruit.

2 The Spirit of the LORD will rest on Him. He will have wisdom and understanding. He will have good plans and strength. He will know things and honor the the LORD.

3 He will not judge by what He sees. He will not decide by what He hears. 4 He will judge poor people fairly. He will help humble people. He will strike the earth with words from His mouth. He will kill evil people with just a breath. 5 He will wear right living like a belt. He will wear faithfulness like a band around His waist.

Peace on Earth

6 Wolves will live with lambs. Leopards will lie down with young goats. Calves, lions, and young farm animals will be together. A little child will lead them. 7 Cows and bears will eat together. Their babies will lie down together. Lions will eat straw like cows. 8 Babies will play near snake holes. Young children will put their hands into snake nests. 9 They will not hurt or destroy on the the LORD's holy mountain. Everyone on earth will know the the LORD, like the sea is full of water.

People from All Nations Come to Him

10 On that day, Jesse's root will stand like a flag for all people. Nations will come to Him. His resting place will be wonderful.

11 On that day, the Lord will reach out His hand a second time. He will bring back the rest of His people from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Babylonia, Hamath, and the islands of the sea. 12 He will raise a flag for the nations. He will gather Israel's people who were sent away. He will bring together Judah's scattered people from all over the earth.

God Brings His People Home

13 Ephraim will no longer be jealous. Judah's enemies will be destroyed. Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah, and Judah will not attack Ephraim. 14 They will fly down on Philistia in the west. Together they will take things from people in the east. They will rule Edom and Moab. The Ammonites will obey them.

15 The LORD will dry up the sea of Egypt. With a hot wind, He will wave His hand over the Euphrates River. He will split it into seven streams. People will walk across in sandals. 16 There will be a road for the rest of His people coming from Assyria. It will be like the road Israel had when they came up from Egypt.
