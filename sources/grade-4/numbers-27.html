Zelophehad's Daughters Ask for Land

1 The daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the family groups of Manasseh son of Joseph, came forward. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 2 They stood in front of Moses, in front of Eleazar the priest, in front of the leaders, and in front of all the people, at the door of the tent of meeting. They said, 3 "Our father died in the desert. He was not with Korah's group that turned against the the LORD. He died for his own sin. He had no sons. 4 Why should our father's name be taken away from his family because he has no son? Give us land with our father's relatives."

5 So Moses took their problem to the the LORD.

6 The the LORD said to Moses, 7 "The daughters of Zelophehad are right. Give them land to own with their father's relatives. Give them their father's land. 8 Tell the Israelites: If a man dies and has no son, give his land to his daughter. 9 If he has no daughter, give his land to his brothers. 10 If he has no brothers, give his land to his father's brothers. 11 If his father has no brothers, give his land to the closest relative in his family. He will own it. This is a rule for the Israelites, just as the the LORD told Moses."

God Shows Moses the Land

12 Then the the LORD said to Moses, "Go up this mountain of Abarim and look at the land I gave to the Israelites. 13 After you see it, you will die and be with your people, just as your brother Aaron died. 14 For when the people turned against Me in the desert of Zin, you did not obey My command to show how holy I am to them at the water. These are the waters of Meribah at Kadesh in the desert of Zin."

Joshua Is Chosen to Lead

15 Then Moses spoke to the the LORD: 16 "The the LORD, God of the spirits of all people, please choose a man to lead the people. 17 He should lead them when they leave and when they return. He should lead them out and bring them in, so the the LORD's people will not be like sheep with no shepherd."

18 The the LORD said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun. He is a man with the Spirit in him. Put your hand on him. 19 Stand him in front of Eleazar the priest and in front of all the people. Give him his job while they watch. 20 Put some of your honor on him, so all the Israelites will listen to him. 21 He will stand in front of Eleazar the priest. Eleazar will ask the the LORD for him by the Urim. At his word they will go out, and at his word they will come in—Joshua and all the Israelites with him, even all the people."

22 So Moses did what the the LORD commanded. He took Joshua and stood him before Eleazar the priest and before all the people. 23 He put his hands on him and gave him his job, just as the the LORD had said through Moses.
