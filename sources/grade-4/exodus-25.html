God Asks for Gifts

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 "Tell the Israelites to bring Me an offering. Take it from every person whose heart wants to give. 3 This is the offering you shall take from them: gold, silver, and bronze. 4 blue, purple, and red thread; fine white cloth; and goat hair. 5 red sheepskins, other animal skins, and acacia wood. 6 oil for the lamps; spices for the special oil and for the sweet-smelling incense. 7 onyx stones and other stones to set in the special vest and the chest piece. 8 Make a holy place for Me. Then I will live among them. 9 Make the tent and everything in it just like the pattern I show you.

The Holy Box

10 "Make a box of acacia wood. It shall be about 3 feet 9 inches long, 2 feet 3 inches wide, and 2 feet 3 inches high. 11 Cover it with pure gold inside and out. Put a gold rim around the top. 12 Cast four gold rings for it. Put them on its four corners. Put two rings on one side and two on the other side. 13 Make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold. 14 Put the poles through the rings on the sides of the box to carry the box. 15 The poles must stay in the rings. Do not take them out. 16 Put the stone tablets with My rules in the box.

17 "Make a lid of pure gold. It shall be about 3 feet 9 inches long and 2 feet 3 inches wide. 18 Make two winged angels of hammered gold. Put them at the two ends of the lid. 19 Make one angel at one end and one at the other end. Make them from the lid. 20 The angels shall spread their wings up. Their wings shall cover the lid. They shall face each other, looking at the lid. 21 Put the lid on top of the box. Put the stone tablets inside the box. 22 I will meet you there, above the lid, between the two angels on the box of the law. I will tell you all the commands I have for the Israelites.

The Table

23 "Make a table of acacia wood. It shall be about 3 feet long, 1 foot 6 inches wide, and 2 feet 3 inches high. 24 Cover it with pure gold. Put a gold rim around it. 25 Make a rim around it about 4 inches wide. Put a gold edge around that rim. 26 Make four gold rings for it. Put them on the four corners near its legs. 27 Put the rings close to the rim. They are places for the poles to carry the table. 28 Make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold. Carry the table with them. 29 Make its plates, dishes, bowls, and jars of pure gold. Use them for pouring out offerings. 30 Always put the special bread on the table before Me.

The Lampstand

31 "Make a lampstand of pure gold. Hammer it out. Its base, stem, cups, buds, and flowers shall be one piece. 32 Six branches shall come out from its sides. Three branches from one side and three from the other side. 33 Each branch shall have three cups shaped like almond flowers, with a bud and a flower. Do the same for all six branches. 34 The lampstand shall have four cups shaped like almond flowers, with buds and flowers. 35 Put a bud under each pair of branches that come out of the lampstand. 36 The buds and branches shall be one piece with it. All of it shall be one piece of pure gold, hammered out. 37 Make seven lamps for it. Light them so they shine in front of it. 38 Make its wick trimmers and trays of pure gold. 39 Make the lampstand and all these parts from about 75 pounds of pure gold. 40 Be careful to make everything just like the pattern I showed you on the mountain."
