The Sons of Israel

1 These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, 2 Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

Judah's Family

3 The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, and Shelah. These three were born to him by the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite woman. Er, Judah's oldest son, was bad in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD killed him. 4 Tamar, Judah's daughter-in-law, had Perez and Zerah by him. So Judah had five sons. 5 The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul. 6 The sons of Zerah were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara—five in all. 7 The son of Carmi was Achar, who brought trouble on Israel by taking things set apart for God. 8 The son of Ethan was Azariah.

From Hezron to David

9 The sons born to Hezron were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb. 10 Ram was the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, the leader of the people of Judah. 11 Nahshon was the father of Salmon, and Salmon was the father of Boaz. 12 Boaz was the father of Obed, and Obed was the father of Jesse. 13 Jesse was the father of Eliab, his oldest son; Abinadab, the second; and Shimea, the third. 14 He was also the father of Nathanel, the fourth; Raddai, the fifth. 15 Ozem was the sixth, and David was the seventh. 16 Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah had three sons: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel. 17 Abigail had Amasa. Amasa's father was Jether the Ishmaelite.

Caleb's Family

18 Caleb, Hezron's son, had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth. Her sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. 19 When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath. She had Hur. 20 Hur was the father of Uri, and Uri was the father of Bezalel. 21 Later, Hezron married the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead. He was sixty years old, and she had Segub. 22 Segub was the father of Jair, who had twenty-three towns in the land of Gilead. 23 But Geshur and Aram took the towns of Jair, along with Kenath and its sixty villages. All these men came from Machir, the father of Gilead. 24 After Hezron died in Caleb Ephrathah, his wife Abijah had Ashhur, the father of Tekoa.

Jerahmeel's Family

25 The sons of Jerahmeel, Hezron's oldest son, were Ram, the oldest; Bunah; Oren; Ozem; and Ahijah. 26 Jerahmeel had another wife named Atarah. She was the mother of Onam. 27 The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel's oldest son, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. 28 The sons of Onam were Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai were Nadab and Abishur. 29 Abishur's wife was Abihail, and she had Ahban and Molid. 30 The sons of Nadab were Seled and Appaim. Seled died without children. 31 The son of Appaim was Ishi. The son of Ishi was Sheshan. The son of Sheshan was Ahlai. 32 The sons of Jada, Shammai's brother, were Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without children. 33 The sons of Jonathan were Peleth and Zaza. These came from Jerahmeel. 34 Sheshan had no sons, only daughters. He had an Egyptian servant named Jarha. 35 Sheshan gave his daughter to his servant Jarha. She had Attai. 36 Attai was the father of Nathan. Nathan was the father of Zabad. 37 Zabad was the father of Ephlal. Ephlal was the father of Obed. 38 Obed was the father of Jehu. Jehu was the father of Azariah. 39 Azariah was the father of Helez. Helez was the father of Eleasah. 40 Eleasah was the father of Sismai. Sismai was the father of Shallum. 41 Shallum was the father of Jekamiah. Jekamiah was the father of Elishama.

More of Caleb's Family

42 The sons of Caleb, Jerahmeel's brother, were Mesha, his oldest son, who was the father of Ziph, and the sons of Mareshah, the father of Hebron. 43 The sons of Hebron were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. 44 Shema was the father of Raham, the father of Jorkeam. Rekem was the father of Shammai. 45 Shammai's son was Maon. Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

46 Caleb's servant wife Ephah had Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran was the father of Gazez. 47 The sons of Jahdai were Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. 48 Caleb's servant wife Maacah had Sheber and Tirhanah. 49 She also had Shaaph, the father of Madmannah, and Sheva, the father of Machbenah and Gibea. Caleb's daughter was Achsah.

The Rest of Caleb's Family

50 These were the sons of Caleb. The sons of Hur, Ephrathah's oldest son, were Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim, 51 Salma, the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph, the father of Beth-gader. 52 Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim, had sons: Haroeh, and half of the Manahathites. 53 The families of Kiriath-jearim were the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. From these came the Zorathites and Eshtaolites. 54 The sons of Salma were Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, and half of the Manahathites, the Zorites. 55 The families of writers who lived at Jabez were the Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites. These were the Kenites who came from Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab.
