Saul Meets Jesus

1 Saul was still very angry. He wanted to hurt the Lord's followers. He went to the high priest. 2 He asked for letters to the Jewish meeting houses in Damascus. The letters would let him take any men or women who followed the Way. He would tie them up and bring them to Jerusalem.

3 On the way to Damascus, a bright light from heaven suddenly shone around him. 4 He fell to the ground. He heard a voice say, "Saul, Saul, why do you hurt Me?"

5 Saul asked, "Who are You, Lord?"

The voice said, "I am Jesus. You are hurting Me. 6 Get up and go into the city. Someone will tell you what to do."

7 The men with Saul stood still. They heard the voice, but they saw no one. 8 Saul got up. His eyes were open, but he could not see. They led him by the hand into Damascus. 9 For three days he could not see. He did not eat or drink.

Ananias Helps Saul

10 In Damascus there was a follower named Ananias. The Lord spoke to him in a vision. The Lord said, "Ananias!"

Ananias said, "Here I am, Lord."

11 The Lord said, "Go to the house of Judas on Straight Street. Ask for a man from Tarsus named Saul. He is praying. 12 In a vision he saw a man named Ananias come in and put his hands on him. Then he could see again."

13 Ananias said, "Lord, I have heard many bad things about this man. He hurt Your holy people in Jerusalem. 14 He came here with power from the chief priests. He wants to tie up everyone who calls on Your name."

15 The Lord said, "Go. This man is My chosen tool. He will tell My name to people of other nations, to kings, and to the people of Israel. 16 I will show him how much he must suffer for My name."

17 Ananias went to the house. He went in. He put his hands on Saul. He said, "Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus appeared to you on the road. He sent me so you can see again and be filled with the Holy Spirit."

18 Right away something like scales fell from Saul's eyes. He could see again. He got up and was baptized.

19 After he ate some food, Saul got strong again. He stayed with the followers in Damascus for a few days. 20 Then he began to preach in the Jewish meeting houses. He said that Jesus is the Son of God.

21 The people who heard him were surprised. They said, "Isn't this the man who hurt people in Jerusalem who call on this name? Didn't he come here to take them to the chief priests?"

22 But Saul became stronger and stronger. He confused the Jews who lived in Damascus. He proved that Jesus is the Christ.

Saul Escapes from Damascus

23 After many days, the Jews made a plan to kill Saul. 24 Saul learned about the plan. They watched the city gates day and night to kill him. 25 But at night his followers put him in a basket. They lowered him through a hole in the wall.

Saul Goes to Jerusalem

26 Saul went to Jerusalem. He tried to join the followers. But they were all afraid of him. They did not believe he was a follower. 27 Barnabas took him to the apostles. He told them how Saul saw the Lord on the road. He told how the Lord spoke to him. He told how Saul spoke bravely in Damascus in the name of Jesus.

28 Saul stayed with them. He went in and out of Jerusalem. He spoke boldly in the name of the Lord. 29 He talked and argued with the Greek-speaking Jews. They tried to kill him. 30 When the believers found out, they took him to Caesarea. Then they sent him to Tarsus.

31 The church in Judea, Galilee, and Samaria had peace. It was built up. It grew in number. The people lived in fear of the Lord. The Holy Spirit gave them comfort.

Peter Heals Aeneas

32 Peter traveled around. He went to visit the Lord's people in Lydda. 33 There he found a man named Aeneas. Aeneas could not walk. He had stayed in bed for eight years.

34 Peter said to him, "Aeneas, Jesus Christ heals you. Get up and make your bed." Right away Aeneas got up. 35 All the people in Lydda and Sharon saw him. They turned to the Lord.

Tabitha Is Raised to Life

36 In Joppa there was a follower named Tabitha. In Greek her name was Dorcas. She did many good things and helped poor people. 37 About that time she got sick and died. The people washed her body. They put her in an upstairs room.

38 Lydda was near Joppa. The followers heard that Peter was there. They sent two men to him. They asked him, "Please come to us right away." 39 Peter went with them. When he arrived, they took him upstairs. All the widows stood around him. They were crying. They showed him the coats and clothes Dorcas had made.

40 Peter sent everyone out of the room. He knelt down and prayed. Then he turned to the body and said, "Tabitha, get up." She opened her eyes. She saw Peter. She sat up. 41 He took her hand and helped her stand. Then he called the believers and the widows. He showed them that she was alive.

42 News of this spread all over Joppa. Many people believed in the Lord. 43 Peter stayed in Joppa for many days. He stayed with Simon, a man who worked with animal skins.
