All Things in Heaven Praise the LORD

1 Praise the LORD from heaven! Praise Him in the highest places! 2 Praise Him, all His angels! Praise Him, all His armies in heaven! 3 Praise Him, sun and moon! Praise Him, all shining stars! 4 Praise Him, highest heavens! Praise Him, waters above the sky! 5 Let them praise the name of the LORD, because He spoke, and they were made. 6 He put them in place to last forever. He made a rule that will not be broken.

All Things on Earth Praise the LORD

7 Praise the LORD from the earth! Praise Him, big sea animals and deep ocean places! 8 Praise Him, fire and hail, snow and clouds, and strong winds that do what He says! 9 Praise Him, mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedar trees! 10 Praise Him, wild animals and all farm animals, small things that crawl and birds that fly! 11 Praise Him, kings of the earth and all nations, leaders and all judges of the earth! 12 Praise Him, young men and young women, old people and young people too!

13 Let them praise the name of the LORD, because only His name is great. His greatness is above earth and heaven. 14 He gave His people strength. He is praised by all His faithful people. They are the children of Israel, people close to Him. Praise the LORD!
