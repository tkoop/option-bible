God Stands Among the Leaders

1 God stands in the meeting of gods. He judges them.

2 “How long will you judge in a wrong way and be nice to bad people? Selah. 3 Help the weak and kids with no dad. Do right for the poor and those who hurt. 4 Save the weak and needy. Get them away from bad people.”

They Do Not Understand

5 They know nothing. They do not understand. They walk in the dark. The whole earth shakes.

God Speaks to the Leaders

6 I said, “You are gods. You are all sons of the Most High.” 7 But you will die like people. You will fall like leaders.”

God, Judge the Earth

8 Get up, O God. Judge the earth, because all the nations are Your.
