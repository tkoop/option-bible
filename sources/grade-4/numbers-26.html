The LORD Tells Moses to Count the People

1 After the sickness ended, the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar, Aaron's son, the priest. 2 Count all the people of Israel by their families. Count every man who is 20 years old or older and can go to war. 3 Moses and Eleazar the priest told them this on the flat land of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho. 4 They counted the men who were 20 or older, just as the LORD had told Moses to do for the Israelites who came out of Egypt.

Reuben's Family

5 Reuben was the oldest son of Israel. His families were the Hanochites from Hanoch, the Palluites from Pallu, 6 the Hezronites from Hezron, and the Carmites from Carmi. 7 These Reuben families had 43,730 men. 8 Pallu's son was Eliab. 9 Eliab's sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram were the men who turned against Moses and Aaron in Korah's group. They turned against the LORD. 10 The ground opened and swallowed them and Korah. Fire killed 250 men. This was a warning sign. 11 But Korah's sons did not die.

Simeon's Family

12 Simeon's families were the Nemuelites from Nemuel, the Jaminites from Jamin, and the Jachinites from Jachin. 13 They were also the Zerahites from Zerah and the Shaulites from Shaul. 14 These Simeon families had 22,200 men.

Gad's Family

15 Gad's families were the Zephonites from Zephon, the Haggites from Haggi, and the Shunites from Shuni. 16 They were also the Oznites from Ozni and the Erites from Eri. 17 They were also the Arodites from Arod and the Arelites from Areli. 18 These Gad families had 40,500 men.

Judah's Family

19 Judah's sons Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. 20 Judah's families were the Shelanites from Shelah, the Perezites from Perez, and the Zerahites from Zerah. 21 Perez's families were the Hezronites from Hezron and the Hamulites from Hamul. 22 These Judah families had 76,500 men.

Issachar's Family

23 Issachar's families were the Tolaites from Tola and the Punites from Puvah. 24 They were also the Jashubites from Jashub and the Shimronites from Shimron. 25 These Issachar families had 64,300 men.

Zebulun's Family

26 Zebulun's families were the Sardites from Sered, the Elonites from Elon, and the Jahleelites from Jahleel. 27 These Zebulun families had 60,500 men.

Joseph's Sons

28 Joseph's sons were Manasseh and Ephraim. 29 Manasseh's families were the Machirites from Machir. Machir was the father of Gilead, and the Gileadites came from Gilead. 30 Gilead's families were the Iezerites from Iezer and the Helekites from Helek. 31 They were also the Asrielites from Asriel and the Shechemites from Shechem. 32 They were also the Shemidaites from Shemida and the Hepherites from Hepher. 33 Zelophehad, Hepher's son, had no sons. He had only daughters. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 34 These Manasseh families had 52,700 men.

Ephraim's Family

35 Ephraim's families were the Shuthalhites from Shuthelah, the Bachrites from Becher, and the Tahanites from Tahan. 36 Shuthelah's son Eran had the Eranite family. 37 These Ephraim families had 32,500 men. These were Joseph's sons by their families.

Benjamin's Family

38 Benjamin's families were the Belaites from Bela, the Ashbelites from Ashbel, and the Ahiramites from Ahiram. 39 They were also the Shuphamites from Shupham and the Huphamites from Hupham. 40 Bela's sons were Ard and Naaman. Their families were the Ardites and the Naamites. 41 These Benjamin families had 45,600 men.

Dan's Family

42 Dan's family was the Shuhamites from Shuham. These were Dan's families. 43 The Shuhamites had 64,400 men.

Asher's Family

44 Asher's families were the Imnites from Imnah, the Ishvites from Ishvi, and the Beriites from Beriah. 45 Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel. Their families were the Heberites and the Malchielites. 46 Asher had a daughter named Serah. 47 These Asher families had 53,400 men.

Naphtali's Family

48 Naphtali's families were the Jahzeelites from Jahzeel and the Gunites from Guni. 49 They were also the Jezerites from Jezer and the Shillemites from Shillem. 50 These Naphtali families had 45,400 men.

51 So the Israelites who were counted were 601,730 men.

Land for the People

52 The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 53 "Share the land among these people as their own land. Share it by the number of names. 54 Give more land to a big group and less land to a small group. Each group gets land based on how many people were counted. 55 But share the land by drawing lots. Share it by the names of the family groups of their fathers. 56 Share the land by drawing lots. Share it between big groups and small groups."

Levi's Family

57 The Levites were counted by their families. They were the Gershonites from Gershon, the Kohathites from Kohath, and the Merarites from Merari. 58 These Levite families were the Libnites, the Hebronites, the Mahlites, the Mushites, and the Korahites. Kohath was the father of Amram. 59 Amram's wife was Jochebed. She was a daughter of Levi, born to Levi in Egypt. She had Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam. 60 Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 61 Nadab and Abihu died when they brought wrong fire before the LORD. 62 The Levites who were counted were 23,000 males who were one month old or older. They were not counted with the other Israelites because they did not get land among the Israelites. 63 These are the people Moses and Eleazar the priest counted. They counted the Israelites on the flat land of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho. 64 None of these men were in the count that Moses and Aaron the priest made in the Sinai desert. 65 The LORD had said they would die in the desert. No one was left except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
