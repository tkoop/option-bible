Names of the People Who Signed

1 These people signed the agreement: Nehemiah the governor, son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah. 2 Seraiah, Azariah, and Jeremiah. 3 Pashhur, Amariah, and Malchijah. 4 Hattush, Shebaniah, and Malluch. 5 Harim, Meremoth, and Obadiah. 6 Daniel, Ginnethon, and Baruch. 7 Meshullam, Abijah, and Mijamin. 8 Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah. These were the priests.

9 The Levites were Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui from the family of Henadad, and Kadmiel. 10 Their brothers were Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, and Hanan. 11 Mica, Rehob, and Hashabiah. 12 Zaccur, Sherebiah, and Shebaniah. 13 Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

14 The leaders of the people were Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, and Bani. 15 Bunni, Azgad, and Bebai. 16 Adonijah, Bigvai, and Adin. 17 Ater, Hezekiah, and Azzur. 18 Hodiah, Hashum, and Bezai. 19 Hariph, Anathoth, and Nobai. 20 Magpiash, Meshullam, and Hezir. 21 Meshezabel, Zadok, and Jaddua. 22 Pelatiah, Hanan, and Anaiah. 23 Hoshea, Hananiah, and Hasshub. 24 Halohesh, Pilha, and Shobek. 25 Rehum, Hashabnah, and Maaseiah. 26 Ahijah, Hanan, and Anan. 27 Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

The Rest of the People Join the Promise

28 The rest of the people joined too. This included the priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and all who had turned away from the peoples of the lands to obey God's Law. Their wives, sons, and daughters joined as well, if they were old enough to understand. 29 They joined their brothers and leaders. They promised with a curse and an oath to follow God's Law, which God gave through Moses, His servant. They also promised to obey carefully all the commands, rules, and laws of the LORD our God.

30 We will not give our daughters to marry people from other lands. We will not take their daughters to marry our sons. 31 If people from other lands bring things to sell on the Sabbath or on a holy day, we will not buy from them. We will let the land rest every seventh year, and we will cancel all debts. 32 We will give one-third of a shekel each year to help with the work in the house of our God. 33 This money is for the holy bread, the regular grain offering, the regular burnt offering, the Sabbath offerings, the new moon offerings, the feast day offerings, the holy gifts, and the sin offerings that make things right for Israel. It is also for all the work in the house of our God. 34 We used lots to choose who will bring wood to God's house. Each family will bring wood at set times every year. It will be burned on the altar of the LORD our God, as the Law says. 35 We will bring the first crops from our land and the first fruit from every tree to the house of the LORD every year. 36 We will bring our firstborn sons and our firstborn animals to the house of our God. We will bring the firstborn of our herds and flocks to the priests who serve in God's house, as the Law says. 37 We will bring the first part of our dough, our gifts, the fruit of every tree, new wine, and olive oil to the priests in the rooms of God's house. We will bring a tenth of our crops to the Levites, because they collect the tenth in all the towns where we work. 38 A priest from Aaron's family must be with the Levites when they collect the tenth. Then the Levites must bring one-tenth of that tenth to the rooms of the storehouse in God's house. 39 The people of Israel and the Levites will bring the grain, new wine, and olive oil to the rooms where the holy tools are kept. The priests who serve, the gatekeepers, and the singers are there. We will not forget the house of our God.
