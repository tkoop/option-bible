Rehoboam Goes to Shechem

1 Rehoboam went to Shechem. All Israel had come there to make him king.

2 When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard this, he came back from Egypt. He had run away there from King Solomon.

The People Ask for Help

3 The people sent for Jeroboam. He and all Israel came to Rehoboam and said, 4 “Your father made our work very hard. Now make his hard work and heavy load lighter, and we will serve you.”

Rehoboam Asks the Older Men

5 Rehoboam said, “Come back to me in three days.” So the people left.

6 King Rehoboam asked the older men who had served his father Solomon while he was alive, “How do you say I should answer these people?”

7 They said, “If you are kind to these people and please them and speak good words to them, they will be your servants forever.”

Rehoboam Asks the Young Men

8 But Rehoboam did not listen to the older men’s advice. He asked the young men who had grown up with him and served him.

9 He asked them, “What do you say? How should I answer these people who say, ‘Make the hard work your father gave us easier’?”

Rehoboam Answers the People

10 The young men who had grown up with him said, “Tell the people who said to you, ‘Your father made our load heavy, but you make it lighter,’ ‘I am even stronger than my father.’ 11 Say, ‘My father put a heavy load on you, but I will make your load even heavier. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you much worse.’”

12 On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, as the king had said, “Come back to me on the third day.”

13 The king answered them in a harsh way. He did not listen to the older men’s advice.

14 He spoke to them the way the young men told him. He said, “My father made your load heavy, but I will make it heavier. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you much worse.”

Israel Turns Away

15 The king did not listen to the people. This happened because God planned it. The LORD wanted to keep His word, which He had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

16 When all Israel saw that the king would not listen to them, they said, “We have no share in David! We have no part in Jesse’s son! Israel, go home! David, take care of your own kingdom!” Then all Israel went home.

Rehoboam Rules Judah

17 But Rehoboam still ruled over the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah.

18 King Rehoboam sent Hadoram, who was in charge of forced work. The Israelites threw stones at him, and he died. King Rehoboam got into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

19 Israel has been against David’s family to this day.
