Light for People in the Dark

1 People in trouble will not stay sad forever. Long ago, God made the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali sad. But later, He will bring honor to the land by the sea, across the Jordan, and to Galilee, where many nations live. 2 The people who walked in the dark have seen a great light. A light has shone on those who live in a very dark land.

God Gives Joy and Freedom

3 You made the nation bigger and gave them more joy. They are happy in front of You, like people happy at harvest time, like soldiers happy when they share things they won in war. 4 For, just like when Midian was beaten, You broke the heavy load on them. You broke the bar on their shoulders and the stick of the one who pushed them down. 5 Every soldier's boot from battle and every coat covered in blood will be burned. They will be food for the fire.

A Child Who Will Bring Peace

6 A child is born to us. A son is given to us. He will be the ruler, and the job of ruling will be on His shoulders. People will call Him Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. 7 His rule and peace will never end. He will sit on David's throne and rule over His kingdom. He will make it strong and fair, and He will keep it right, from now on and forever. The LORD who rules over all will make this happen because He cares so much.

God Speaks Against Israel

8 The Lord sent a word against Jacob. It will come down on Israel. 9 All the people will know it—Ephraim and the people who live in Samaria. They speak with pride and a proud heart. They say,

10 "The bricks fell down, but we will build with cut stone. The fig trees were cut down, but we will put cedars in their place."

11 But the LORD made Rezin's enemies strong against them. He stirred up their enemies. 12 People from Aram in the east and Philistines in the west have eaten up Israel. But even after all this, His anger is not gone. His hand is still raised.

God Punishes His People

13 But the people did not turn back to God, who punished them. They did not look for the LORD who rules over all. 14 So the LORD will take away from Israel the head and the tail, the palm branch and the reed, all in one day. 15 The leaders and important people are the head. The prophets who tell lies are the tail. 16 The ones who lead these people lead them the wrong way. The ones who follow are lost.

17 So the Lord will not be happy with the young men. He will not feel sorry for the children without fathers or the women whose husbands have died. Everyone is evil and does wrong. Everyone says foolish things. But even after all this, His anger is not gone. His hand is still raised.

18 Evil burns like a fire. It eats up thorns and weeds. It sets the thick woods on fire, and smoke rises up. 19 Because of the LORD's anger, the land will be burned. The people will be like wood for the fire. They will not be kind to each other. 20 They will grab food on the right, but still be hungry. They will eat on the left, but not be full. Each person will attack his own people. 21 Manasseh will attack Ephraim, and Ephraim will attack Manasseh. Together they will turn against Judah. But even after all this, His anger is not gone. His hand is still raised.
