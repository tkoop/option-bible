The Old Tent and Its Rules

1 The first agreement had rules for worship. It also had a holy place on earth. 2 A tent was set up. The first room had a lamp, a table, and special bread. People called this room the Holy Place.

3 Behind the second curtain was another room. People called it the Most Holy Place. 4 It had a gold altar for incense. It also had the wooden box of the agreement, covered with gold on every side. Inside were a gold jar with manna, Aaron's stick that grew buds, and the stone tablets of the agreement. 5 Above it stood angels of glory. They covered the lid of mercy. We will not talk about all these things now.

6 The priests went into the first room again and again. They did their work there. 7 But only the high priest went into the second room. He went only once a year. He always brought blood. He gave it for his own sins and for the sins the people did without knowing.

What the Holy Spirit Teaches

8 The Holy Spirit shows this: the way into the Most Holy Place was not open yet. The first tent was still standing. 9 This is a picture for now. The gifts and sacrifices they bring cannot make a worshiper feel fully clean inside. 10 They are only about food, drink, washings, and body rules. They last until God makes a better way.

Christ Is Our High Priest

11 But Christ came. He is the high priest of the good things that have come. He went through a bigger and better tent. Hands did not make it. It is not part of this world. 12 He did not enter by the blood of goats and calves. He entered the Most Holy Place once and for all time. He used His own blood. He bought freedom that lasts forever.

13 The blood of goats and bulls was sprinkled on people who were not clean. The ashes of a young cow also helped. These things made the body clean. 14 So Christ's blood can do much more. He offered Himself to God without fault. He did this through the Spirit that never ends. His blood will clean our hearts from bad deeds. Then we can serve the living God.

A New Agreement

15 So Christ is the helper for a new agreement. He did this so the people God called can receive the promised gift that lasts forever. His death set them free from the wrongs they did under the first agreement.

16 When there is an agreement, the one who made it must die. 17 An agreement starts to work only after the person dies. It means nothing while that person is alive.

Blood Makes Things Clean

18 So the first agreement was not started without blood. 19 Moses told the people every command in the law. Then he took the blood of calves and goats. He mixed it with water, red wool, and a plant called hyssop. He sprinkled the book and all the people. 20 He said, “This is the blood of the agreement God commanded you to keep.” 21 He also sprinkled the tent and all the things used to serve God.

22 The law says almost everything is made clean with blood. Without blood being poured out, there is no forgiveness.

Better Gifts for Heaven

23 So the copies of things in heaven had to be cleaned with these gifts. But the real things in heaven need better gifts. 24 Christ did not go into a man-made copy of the true holy place. He went into heaven itself. Now He stands before God for us.

Christ's One Sacrifice

25 He does not offer Himself again and again, like the high priest who goes into the holy place every year with someone else's blood. 26 If He did, He would have suffered many times since the world began. But now He has come once. It is near the end of time. He came to take away sin by giving Himself.

27 People die only once. After that, they are judged. 28 In the same way, Christ was offered once to take away the sins of many. He will come a second time. He will not come to deal with sin. He will come to save the people who are waiting for Him.
