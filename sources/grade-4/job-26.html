Job Talks About How Powerful God Is

1 Then Job answered and said,

2 "How you helped the weak! You saved a person with no strength. 3 How you advised someone with no wisdom! You shared much good advice. 4 Who did you speak to? Whose breath came from you?

5 The dead shake under the waters, with all who live there. 6 The grave is open before Him. The place of ruin has no cover.

7 He stretches out the north over empty space. He hangs the earth on nothing. 8 He wraps water in His clouds. The clouds do not break under the water. 9 He covers the face of the full moon. He spreads His cloud over it. 10 He drew a circle on the waters, where light and dark meet.

11 The pillars of heaven shake. They are amazed at His warning. 12 By His power He calmed the sea. By His wisdom He broke the sea monster Rahab. 13 By His breath the sky became clear. His hand struck the fleeing snake.

14 These are only the edges of His ways. We hear only a faint whisper of Him. Who can understand His mighty thunder?"
