1Moses answered, "But they will not believe me. They will not listen to me. They will say, 'The LORD did not appear to you.'"

2The LORD said to him, "What is in your hand?"

Moses said, "A staff."

3The LORD said, "Throw it on the ground." Moses threw it on the ground, and it became a snake. Moses ran away from it.

4The LORD said to Moses, "Reach out your hand and hold its tail." Moses reached out his hand and held it, and it became a staff in his hand.

5The LORD said, "Do this so they may believe that the LORD, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, has appeared to you."

6Again the LORD said to him, "Put your hand inside your coat." He put his hand inside his coat. When he took it out, his hand was white with a bad skin disease, like snow.

7The LORD said, "Put your hand back inside your coat." He put it back, then took it out again. His hand was well again, like the rest of his skin.

8The LORD said, "If they do not believe you or listen to the first sign, they may believe the second sign."

9The LORD said, "If they do not believe these two signs or listen to you, take water from the Nile River and pour it on the dry ground. The water you take from the river will become blood on the ground."

10Moses said to the LORD, "Please, Lord, I am not a good speaker. I was not good yesterday or the day before. I am not good even now, after You have spoken to Your servant. I am slow to speak and slow with my tongue."

11The LORD said to him, "Who made man's mouth? Who makes a person unable to talk, or deaf, or able to see, or blind? Is it not I, the LORD?"

12The LORD said, "Now go. I will be with your mouth. I will teach you what to say."

13Moses said, "Please, Lord, send someone else."

14The LORD became angry with Moses. He said, "What about Aaron, your brother, the Levite? I know he can speak well. He is coming to meet you. He will be glad in his heart when he sees you."

15The LORD said, "You will speak to him. Put the words in his mouth. I will be with your mouth and with his mouth. I will teach you both what to do."

16The LORD said, "He will speak to the people for you. He will be your mouth. You will be like God to him."

17The LORD said, "Take this staff in your hand. You will do the signs with it."

Moses Goes Back to Egypt

18Moses went back to Jethro, his father-in-law. He said, "Let me go back to my brothers in Egypt. I want to see if they are still alive."

Jethro said, "Go in peace."

19The LORD said to Moses in Midian, "Go back to Egypt. All the men who wanted to kill you are dead."

20Moses took his wife and sons and put them on a donkey. He went back to Egypt. Moses took God's staff in his hand.

21The LORD said to Moses, "When you go back to Egypt, do all the wonders I have given you before Pharaoh. But I will make him stubborn, so he will not let the people go."

22The LORD said, "Say to Pharaoh, 'This is what the LORD says: Israel is My firstborn son.' 23'Let My son go so he can serve Me. If you refuse to let him go, I will kill your firstborn son.'"

The LORD Meets Moses on the Way

24On the way, at a place to sleep, the LORD met Moses and tried to kill him.

25Zipporah took a sharp stone and cut off the skin from her son. She touched Moses' feet with it and said, "You are a husband of blood to me."

26So the LORD let Moses go. Then Zipporah said, "You are a husband of blood because of the cutting."

Aaron Meets Moses

28Moses told Aaron all the words the LORD had sent him to say. He also told him about all the signs the LORD had commanded him to do.

29Moses and Aaron went and gathered all the elders of the people of Israel.

30Aaron spoke all the words the LORD had spoken to Moses. He did the signs in front of the people.

31The people believed. They heard that the LORD had visited the people of Israel and had seen their trouble. They bowed down and worshiped.
