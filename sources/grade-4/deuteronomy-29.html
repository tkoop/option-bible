Moses Makes a New Promise in Moab

1 These are the words of the promise that the the LORD told Moses to make with the people of Israel in the land of Moab. This was besides the promise He made with them at Horeb.

2 Moses called all Israel together and said to them, “You have seen all that the the LORD did in front of your eyes in Egypt. He did it to Pharaoh, to all his helpers, and to all his land. 3 Your eyes saw the hard times, signs, and amazing things. 4 But even today, the the LORD has not given you a heart to know Him, or eyes to see, or ears to hear. 5 I led you for forty years in the desert. Your clothes did not wear out, and your sandals did not wear out on your feet. 6 You did not eat bread, and you did not drink wine or strong drink, so you would know that I am the the LORD your God.”

7 “When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to fight us, and we beat them. 8 We took their land and gave it to the families of Reuben, Gad, and half of the family group of Manasseh. 9 So obey the words of this promise. Do them, so you will do well in all you do.”

Everyone Stands Before the LORD

10 “All of you are standing today before the the LORD your God: your leaders, your family groups, your older men, and your officials—every man of Israel. 11 Your little ones, your wives, and the outsider living in your camp, from the one who cuts your wood to the one who carries your water. 12 You are here to join the promise of the the LORD your God, and His vow, which the the LORD your God is making with you today. 13 He will make you His people today, and He will be your God, just as He said to you and promised your fathers—Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 14 I am not making this promise and this vow only with you. 15 I am making it with those standing here with us today before the the LORD our God, and also with those who are not here today.”

16 “You know how we lived in Egypt, and how we traveled through the nations you passed through. 17 You saw their hated things and their idols made of wood and stone, silver and gold, that were among them.”

A Warning About Turning Away

18 “Be careful. Do not let any man or woman, family, or family group among you turn away today from the the LORD our God to serve the gods of those nations. Do not let a bitter root grow among you that makes poisonous, bitter fruit. 19 When a person hears these words of the vow, he may feel safe in his heart and say, ‘I will be okay, even if I keep doing what I want.’ This will bring ruin to both wet and dry land. 20 The the LORD will not want to forgive him. Instead, the the LORD’s anger and jealousy will burn against that man. All the curses written in this book will stay on him. The the LORD will wipe out his name from under heaven. 21 The the LORD will set him apart from all the family groups of Israel for harm, just like all the curses of the promise written in this Book of the Law.”

What People Will Say Later

22 “The next generation, your children after you, and the outsider who comes from a far land, will say when they see the sickness of that land and the diseases the the LORD brought on it, 23 that the whole land is sulfur and salt, an empty, burned land. Nothing is planted, nothing grows, and no grass grows there. It is like Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the the LORD destroyed in His fierce anger. 24 All nations will ask, ‘Why did the the LORD do this to this land? What does this great anger mean?’ 25 Then people will say, ‘It is because they left the promise of the the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He made with them when He brought them out of Egypt. 26 They went and served other gods and worshiped them—gods they did not know and that He did not give to them. 27 So the the LORD’s anger burned against that land, to bring all the curses written in this book on it. 28 The the LORD pulled them up from their land in anger and great fury, and threw them into another land, as it is today.’”

Secret Things Belong to God

29 “The secret things belong to the the LORD our God. But the things He has shown belong to us and to our children forever, so we can do all the words of this law.”
