A Song for Judah

1 On that day, people will sing this song in the land of Judah: "We have a strong city. He makes walls and safe places to keep us safe." 2 Open the gates. Let the good nation come in. They keep their promises.

God Keeps Us Safe

3 You will keep in full peace the person whose mind stays on You, because he trusts You. 4 Trust in the LORD forever, because GOD the LORD is the Rock that lasts forever.

God Brings Down the Proud

5 He brought down the people who live high up. He made the proud city low. He brought it down to the ground. He pushed it into the dust. 6 Feet walked on it. The feet of poor people. The steps of needy people.

God Makes the Way Straight

7 The road of good people is straight. You make the path smooth for good people.

We Wait for the LORD

8 Yes, the LORD, we wait for You on the road of Your fair rules. Your name and Your fame are what our souls want. 9 At night my soul wants You. My spirit in me looks for You early. When Your fair rules come to the earth, people learn to do right.

The Wicked Do Not Learn

10 Even if bad people are treated kindly, they do not learn to do right. In a land where people do right, they still do wrong. They do not see the great power of the LORD. 11 The LORD, Your hand is high up, but they do not see. Let them see Your strong care for Your people, and let them be ashamed. Let fire eat up Your enemies.

God Gives Us Peace

12 The LORD, You will give us peace. You have done all our work for us. 13 The LORD our God, other rulers ruled us. But we remember only Your name.

The Dead Will Not Rise

14 Dead people will not live again. Spirits will not rise. So You punished them and wiped them out. You erased all memory of them.

God Made the Nation Big

15 The LORD, You made the nation bigger. You made the nation bigger. You are honored. You made all the borders of the land far. 16 The LORD, they looked for You when they were in trouble. When You punished them, they whispered a quiet prayer.

Like a Woman in Pain

17 Like a woman about to have a baby twists and cries in pain, so we were before You, the LORD. 18 We were like a woman with a baby, but nothing came of it. We did not bring safety to the earth. No new people were born.

The Dead Will Live

19 Your dead people will live. Their bodies will rise. Wake up and sing, you who live in the dust! For Your dew is like morning dew. The earth will bring out her dead.

Hide for a Little While

20 "Go, My people. Go into your rooms and close your doors behind you. Hide for a short time until the anger passes."

21 Look, the LORD is coming out of His place to punish the people of the earth for their wrong. The earth will show her killed people. She will not hide her dead people anymore.
