Saul Says Yes to Stephen's Death

1 Saul was glad Stephen died. That day, people hurt the church in Jerusalem very much. All the believers, except the apostles, ran away to towns in Judea and Samaria. 2 Good men carried Stephen and cried loudly for him. 3 Saul tried to ruin the church. He went into homes. He dragged out men and women. He put them in jail.

The Good News Spreads

4 The believers who ran away told people the good news as they went. 5 Philip went down to a city in Samaria. He told the people about Christ. 6 The crowds listened to Philip. They heard his words and saw the signs he did. 7 Bad spirits cried out loud and left many people. Many who could not walk or move were healed. 8 The whole city was very happy.

Simon the Magician

9 A man named Simon had done magic in that city. He amazed the people of Samaria. He said he was a great man. 10 Everyone, from small to big, listened to him. They said, "This man is God's power. People call it Great." 11 They listened to him because his magic had amazed them for a long time.

12 But when the people believed Philip's good news about God's kingdom and the name of Jesus Christ, men and women were baptized. 13 Simon believed too. He was baptized. He stayed close to Philip. He was amazed when he saw the big signs and powerful things Philip did.

Peter and John Pray for the Holy Spirit

14 The apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted God's word. They sent Peter and John to them. 15 Peter and John came down and prayed for the people. They prayed that the people would receive the Holy Spirit. 16 The Holy Spirit had not yet come on any of them. They had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. 17 Then Peter and John put their hands on them. The people received the Holy Spirit.

18 Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given when the apostles put their hands on people. He offered them money. 19 He said, "Give me this power too. Then anyone I put my hands on will receive the Holy Spirit."

20 Peter said to him, "May your money be lost with you! You thought you could buy God's gift with money. 21 You have no part in this. Your heart is not right with God. 22 So turn away from your bad ways. Pray to the Lord. Maybe He will forgive the bad thoughts in your heart. 23 I see that you are full of bitter anger. You are trapped in wrong."

24 Simon answered, "Pray to the Lord for me. Ask Him that none of the bad things you said will happen to me."

25 After Peter and John told people about the Lord and spoke God's word, they went back to Jerusalem. On the way, they told the good news in many Samaritan towns.

Philip and the Man from Ethiopia

26 An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip. He said, "Get up and go south. Take the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza. It is a desert road."

27 So Philip got up and went. He saw a man from Ethiopia. The man was an important worker. He worked for Candace, the queen of Ethiopia. He was in charge of all her money. He had gone to Jerusalem to worship. 28 Now he was going home. He sat in his chariot and read from the book of Isaiah the prophet.

29 The Holy Spirit said to Philip, "Go to that chariot and stay near it."

30 Philip ran to the chariot. He heard the man reading from Isaiah the prophet. He asked, "Do you understand what you are reading?"

31 The man said, "How can I understand unless someone shows me the way?" Then he asked Philip to get in and sit with him.

32 The part he was reading said, "He was led like a sheep to be killed. He was quiet like a lamb before the one who cuts its wool. He did not open His mouth. 33 When He was hurt, no one was fair to Him. Who can tell about His family? His life was taken from the earth."

34 The official asked Philip, "Who is the prophet talking about? Is it about himself or someone else?"

35 Then Philip began with that part of the Bible. He told the man the good news about Jesus.

36 As they traveled down the road, they came to some water. The official said, "Look! There is water. What stops me from being baptized?"

38 He told the chariot to stop. Both Philip and the official got down into the water. Philip baptized him.

39 When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord took Philip away. The official did not see him again. The official went on his way, happy. 40 Philip was found in the city of Azotus. He traveled through and told the good news in all the towns until he came to Caesarea.
