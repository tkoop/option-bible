The Family Leaders Who Came Back

1 These are the family leaders who came back with me from Babylon. This was when King Artaxerxes was king. 2 From Phinehas' family came Gershom. From Ithamar's family came Daniel. From David's family came Hattush. 3 From Shecaniah's family, from Parosh's family, came Zechariah. 150 men came with him. 4 From Pahath-moab's family came Elihoenai son of Zerahiah. 200 men came with him. 5 From Shecaniah's family came the son of Jahaziel. 300 men came with him. 6 From Adin's family came Ebed son of Jonathan. 50 men came with him. 7 From Elam's family came Isaiah son of Athaliah. 70 men came with him.

8 From Shephatiah's family came Zebadiah son of Michael. 80 men came with him. 9 From Joab's family came Obadiah son of Jehiel. 218 men came with him. 10 From Shelomith's family came the son of Josiphiah. 160 men came with him. 11 From Bebai's family came Zechariah son of Bebai. 28 men came with him. 12 From Azgad's family came Johanan son of Hakkatan. 110 men came with him. 13 From the last of Adonikam's family came Eliphelet, Jeuel, and Shemaiah. 60 men came with them. 14 From Bigvai's family came Uthai and Zabbud. 70 men came with them.

Ezra Looks for Levites

15 I gathered the people by the river that goes to Ahava. We camped there three days. I looked at the people and the priests. I did not find any Levites there. 16 Then I sent leaders: Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam. They could understand God's law. I also sent Joiarib and Elnathan. 17 I sent them to Iddo, the leader, at Casiphia. I told them what to say to Iddo and his brothers, the temple helpers at Casiphia. They were to bring us helpers for God's house. 18 God's hand was good to us. They brought us Sherebiah. He was a wise man from Mahli's family. Mahli was from Levi, who was from Israel. Sherebiah came with his sons and brothers. There were 18 men. 19 They also brought Hashabiah and Jeshaiah from Merari's family. With them came their brothers and sons. There were 20 men. 20 They also brought 220 temple helpers. David and the leaders had given them to help the Levites. All of them were named.

A Fast for Safe Travel

21 There by the Ahava River, I called for a fast. We were to be humble before our God. We asked Him for a safe trip for us, our children, and all our things. 22 I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to help us on the road. We had told the king, "The hand of our God is good to all who seek Him, and His power and anger are against all who leave Him." 23 So we fasted and asked our God for help. He heard us.

Ezra Gives the Gifts to the Priests

24 Then I set apart twelve of the priest leaders. I chose Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers. 25 I weighed out the silver, gold, and things for God's house. The king, his advisors, his leaders, and the Israelites who were there had given these gifts. 26 I weighed out 650 talents of silver, silver things worth 100 talents, and 100 talents of gold. 27 I also weighed out 20 gold bowls worth 1,000 coins. I weighed two fine bronze things that were as precious as gold. 28 I said to them, "You are holy to the LORD. These things are holy too. The silver and gold are a free gift to the LORD, the God of your ancestors." 29 Watch these things and keep them safe. Do not lose them until you weigh them in the rooms of the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. Weigh them before the leaders of the priests, the Levites, and the family leaders of Israel. 30 So the priests and Levites took the weighed silver, gold, and things. They were to bring them to God's house in Jerusalem.

The Trip to Jerusalem

31 On the twelfth day of the first month, we left the Ahava River. We went to Jerusalem. God's hand was on us. He kept us safe from enemies and robbers on the way. 32 We got to Jerusalem. We rested there three days. 33 On the fourth day, we weighed the silver, gold, and things in God's house. We gave them to Meremoth son of Uriah the priest. Eleazar son of Phinehas was there. Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui, who were Levites, were also there. 34 Everything was counted and weighed. The total weight was written down.

The People Worship God

35 The people who came back from being captives gave burnt offerings to the God of Israel. They gave 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, 77 lambs, and 12 male goats for sin. All of these were burnt offerings to the LORD. 36 They gave the king's orders to the governors west of the river. The governors helped the people and God's house.
