Paul Defends His Work

1 I, Paul, ask you to be gentle and kind, like Christ. I am not proud when I am with you, but I am strong when I am away. 2 I pray that when I am with you, I will not have to be tough. I plan to be tough with people who think we live only for this world.

3 We live in human bodies, but we do not fight the way the world fights. 4 Our weapons are not human weapons. God's power makes them strong. They can break down strong walls. 5 We tear down wrong ideas and proud thoughts that stand against knowing God. We make every thought obey Christ. 6 We are ready to punish anyone who does not obey, once you fully obey.

Paul Talks About His Power

7 You look at what is on the outside. If someone thinks he belongs to Christ, he should think again. We belong to Christ just as much. 8 Even if I talk more about the power the Lord gave us to build you up, not to tear you down, I will not be embarrassed. 9 I do not want you to think my letters are meant to scare you.

10 Some people say, "His letters are strong, but when he is with us, he is weak, and his words are not worth much."

11 People who say that should know this: when we are away, our words in letters are strong, and when we are with you, our actions are strong too.

Paul Does Not Brag Too Much

12 We do not dare to put ourselves with people who praise themselves. They compare themselves only to themselves, and that shows they do not understand. 13 But we will not brag about things too big for us. We will stay in the work God gave us, and that work reaches to you.

14 We are not doing more than our work, as if we had not come to you. We were the first to bring the good news about Christ to you. 15 We do not brag about other people's work. We hope that as you trust God more, our work with you will grow because of you. 16 Then we can tell the good news in lands beyond you. We will not brag about work someone else already did.

17 But, "If someone brags, he should brag about the Lord." 18 It is not the person who praises himself who is accepted. It is the person the Lord praises.
