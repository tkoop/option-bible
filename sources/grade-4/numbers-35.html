God Talks to Moses

1 The LORD spoke to Moses on the flat land of Moab. This was by the Jordan River near Jericho. He said,

Cities for the Levites

2 "Tell the people of Israel to give the Levites cities to live in. The cities must come from the land you own. You must also give the Levites open land around the cities.

3 "The cities are for the Levites to live in. The open land is for their animals, their things, and all their livestock.

4 "The open land around the cities you give to the Levites must go out 1,000 cubits from the city wall all around.

5 "You must measure 2,000 cubits outside the city on the east side, 2,000 on the south side, 2,000 on the west side, and 2,000 on the north side. Put the city in the middle. This land will be pasture for the cities.

6 "You will give six cities to the Levites. These are cities of safety. A person who kills someone by mistake can run to one of them. You will also give the Levites 42 more cities.

7 "In all, you will give the Levites 48 cities, along with their open land.

8 "The cities you give must come from the land the people of Israel own. A big family group gives more cities. A small family group gives fewer. Give cities to match the land each group got."

God Speaks Again

9 The LORD spoke to Moses again. He said,

Cities of Safety

10 "Tell the people of Israel that you are about to cross the Jordan River into Canaan.

11 "Choose cities of safety for yourselves. A person who kills someone by mistake can run to one of them.

12 "These cities will be safe places from the person who wants to punish the killer. The killer will not die until he stands trial before the whole community.

13 "You will give six cities of safety.

14 "You must give three cities on the east side of the Jordan River. Give three cities in the land of Canaan. All six will be cities of safety.

15 "These six cities will be safe places for the people of Israel, for strangers, and for people living among you. Anyone who kills a person by mistake can run to one of them.

Rules About Murder

16 "But if a person hits someone with an iron tool and the person dies, the hitter is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

17 "If someone throws a stone and kills a person, the thrower is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

18 "If someone hits a person with a wooden tool and kills them, the hitter is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

19 "The person who punishes the killer must put the murderer to death. When he finds the murderer, he must kill him.

20 "If a person pushes someone because of hate, or throws something at them on purpose, and the person dies,

21 "or if he hits the person with his hand in hate and the person dies, the hitter is a murderer. The person who punishes the killer must put him to death when he finds him.

22 "But if a person pushes someone suddenly, without hate, or throws something without meaning to hurt,

23 "or drops a stone without seeing, and the stone kills someone, and he did not hate the person or try to hurt them,

24 "then the whole community must judge between the killer and the person who wants to punish him. They must use these rules.

25 "The community must save the killer from the person who wants to punish him. They must send the killer back to the city of safety he ran to. He must stay there until the high priest dies. The high priest was anointed with holy oil.

26 "But if the killer leaves the city of safety where he ran,

27 "and the person who punishes him finds him outside the city, that person may kill the killer. The killer's death is not murder.

28 "The killer must stay in his city of safety until the high priest dies. After that, he may go back to his own land.

29 "These rules are for you and your children forever, wherever you live.

30 "If someone kills a person, the killer may be put to death only if witnesses say so. One witness is not enough to put a person to death.

31 "Do not take money to spare the life of a murderer. The murderer is guilty and must be put to death.

32 "Do not take money to let a killer leave his city of safety early. He may not go home before the high priest dies.

Keep the Land Clean

33 "Do not make the land unclean where you live. Murder makes the land unclean. The land cannot be made clean again except by the blood of the murderer.

34 "Do not make the land unclean where you live, because I, the LORD, live among the people of Israel."
