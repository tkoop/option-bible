God Wants Real Fasting

1 Shout out loud. Don't hold back. Lift your voice like a horn. Tell My people they have turned away. Tell Jacob's family they have done wrong. 2 They look for Me every day. They say they want to know My ways. They act like people who do what is right and do not leave God's rules. They ask Me for fair choices. They want to be close to God.

3 They say, "Why did we skip meals and You didn't see? Why did we go hungry and You didn't care?" But look! On your fast day, you do what you like. You make your workers work too hard.

4 Look, you skip meals so you can argue and fight. You even hit people with angry fists. That is not the kind of fasting that makes God hear you.

5 Is this the fast I want? A day when a person hurts himself? Should he bow his head like a bent reed and lie on rough cloth and ashes? Do you call that a fast, a day the LORD likes?

6 No, this is the fast I want: open the chains of wrong. Untie the heavy ropes. Set free people who are pushed down. Break every heavy load. 7 Share your bread with hungry people. Bring poor people with no home into your house. Give clothes to people with none. Don't turn away from your own family.

8 Then your light will shine like morning. You will get well fast. Doing right will lead you. The glory of the LORD will guard you. 9 Then you will call, and the LORD will answer. You will cry for help, and He will say, "Here I am." But you must stop putting heavy loads on people. Stop pointing fingers. Stop saying empty, mean things.

10 If you give food to hungry people and help those who are hurting, then your light will shine in the dark. Your darkest times will be like noon. 11 The LORD will always lead you. He will fill you up in dry places. He will make your bones strong. You will be like a garden with water. You will be like a spring whose water never stops.

12 Your people will rebuild old ruins. You will raise up old foundations. People will call you the one who fixes broken walls and the one who makes paths good to live on.

Keep the Sabbath Special

13 If you stop doing your own thing on the Sabbath, call the Sabbath a joy, honor the holy day of the LORD, and honor Him by not going your own way, not doing what you want, and not just talking your own words. 14 Then you will find joy in the LORD. I will make you ride high on the earth. I will feed you with the land your father Jacob was given. The LORD has spoken.
