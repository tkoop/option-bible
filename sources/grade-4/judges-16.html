Samson Goes to Gaza

1 Samson went to Gaza. There he saw a woman who sold herself, and he went in to her. 2 The people of Gaza heard, "Samson is here!" So they surrounded the city and hid to catch him. They waited all night at the city gate. They were quiet all night and said, "We will kill him when morning comes."

3 Samson slept until the middle of the night. Then he got up. He took hold of the city gate doors and the two posts. He pulled them up with the bar. He put them on his shoulders and carried them to the top of the hill facing Hebron.

Samson and Delilah

4 Later, Samson loved a woman in the Valley of Sorek. Her name was Delilah. 5 The Philistine rulers came to her and said, "Trick him. Find out where his great strength comes from. Find out how we can beat him. Then we can tie him up and hurt him. Each of us will give you 1,100 pieces of silver."

6 Delilah said to Samson, "Please tell me where your great strength comes from. Tell me how someone can tie you up and hurt you."

7 Samson said to her, "If they tie me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not dried, I will be weak like any other man."

8 The Philistine rulers brought her seven fresh bowstrings that had not dried. She tied him with them. 9 Men were hiding in the inner room. She said, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" He broke the strings like a thread of flax burns in a fire. No one knew where his strength came from.

10 Delilah said to Samson, "You tricked me. You lied to me. Now tell me how someone can tie you up."

11 He said, "If they tie me with new ropes that have never been used, I will be weak like any other man."

12 Delilah took new ropes and tied him. She said, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" Men were hiding in the inner room. He broke the ropes off his arms like a thread.

13 Delilah said to Samson, "You have tricked me again. You lied to me. Tell me now how someone can tie you up."

He said, "If you weave the seven braids of my hair into the cloth on the loom."

14 She fastened it with a pin and said, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!" He woke up and pulled out the pin, the loom, and the cloth.

15 She said to him, "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? This is the third time you have tricked me. You have not told me where your great strength comes from." 16 She kept asking him every day. She pushed him so hard that he felt like he would die.

17 So he told her all his heart. He said, "No one has ever cut my hair. I have been set apart for God since I was in my mother's womb. If my head is shaved, my strength will leave me. I will be weak like any other man."

18 Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart. She sent for the Philistine rulers and said, "Come back this time. He has told me all his heart." The Philistine rulers came to her. They brought the money with them.

19 She made him sleep on her knees. She called a man. He shaved off the seven braids of Samson's head. She began to hurt him, and his strength left him.

20 She said, "Samson, the Philistines are attacking you!"

He woke up and said, "I will go out like before and shake myself free." But he did not know that the LORD had left him.

Samson Is Caught

21 The Philistines grabbed him. They took out his eyes. They brought him down to Gaza. They tied him with bronze chains. He ground grain in the prison. 22 But the hair on his head began to grow again.

The Philistines Praise Dagon

23 The Philistine rulers gathered to offer a big sacrifice to their god Dagon. They were happy. They said, "Our god has given our enemy Samson into our hands."

24 When the people saw him, they praised their god. They said, "Our god has given our enemy into our hands. He destroyed our land. He killed many of us."

Samson Makes the Philistines Laugh

25 When they were happy, they said, "Call Samson so he can make us laugh." They called Samson from the prison. He made them laugh. They stood him between the pillars.

26 Samson said to the boy who held his hand, "Let me feel the pillars that hold up the house. I want to lean on them."

27 The house was full of men and women. All the Philistine rulers were there. About 3,000 men and women were on the roof watching Samson make them laugh.

Samson's Last Prayer

28 Samson called to the LORD and said, "Lord, the LORD, remember me. Make me strong just this once, O God. Let me get back at the Philistines for my two eyes."

29 Samson took hold of the two middle pillars that held up the house. He leaned on them. He put one hand on one pillar and the other hand on the other pillar.

30 Samson said, "Let me die with the Philistines." He pushed with all his strength. The house fell on the rulers and on all the people. More people died when he died than he had killed while he lived.

31 His brothers and all his father's family came down. They carried him up and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of his father Manoah. He had judged Israel for twenty years.
