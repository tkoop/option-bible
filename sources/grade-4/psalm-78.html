A lesson to remember

1 Listen, my people. Hear my teaching. Turn your ears to my words. 2 I will open my mouth and tell a story. I will share old secrets. 3 We heard these things. We know them. Our fathers told them to us.

4 We will not hide them from their children. We will tell the next group of kids about the great things the LORD has done. We will tell about His power and His wonders. 5 He made a rule for Jacob's family. He gave a law to Israel. He told our fathers to teach it to their children. 6 Then the next group would know it. Even kids not born yet would learn it. They would grow up and tell their own children. 7 Then they could trust God. They would not forget what God did. They would keep His commands. 8 They would not be like their fathers. Their fathers were stubborn and would not obey. Their hearts were not true to God. Their spirits were not faithful to Him.

9 The men of Ephraim had bows, but they turned back on the day of battle. 10 They did not keep God's agreement. They would not follow His law. 11 They forgot what He had done. They forgot the wonders He showed them.

God's wonders in Egypt

12 God did amazing things where their fathers could see. He did them in Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. 13 He split the sea and led them through it. He made the water stand like a wall. 14 He led them with a cloud in the day. He led them with fire all night. 15 He broke rocks in the desert. He gave them plenty to drink, like water from the deep sea. 16 He made streams come from the rock. The water flowed down like rivers.

The people complain in the desert

17 But they sinned even more against God. They fought against the Most High in the dry land. 18 They tested God in their hearts. They asked for food they wanted. 19 They spoke against God. They said, "Can God set a table in the desert? 20 He hit the rock, and water poured out. Streams rushed by. Can He also give bread? Can He get meat for His people?"

21 So the LORD heard them. He was very angry. Fire burned against Jacob. His anger rose against Israel. 22 They did not believe in God. They did not trust His power to save.

God sends food from heaven

23 But He told the clouds above to open. He opened the doors of heaven. 24 He rained bread on them to eat. He gave them grain from heaven. 25 People ate the food of angels. He sent them food until they were full. 26 He made the east wind blow in the sky. By His power He led the south wind. 27 He rained meat on them like dust. He rained birds like sand by the sea. 28 He let them fall in the middle of the camp. They fell all around their tents. 29 The people ate and were very full. He gave them what they wanted. 30 But they were not done wanting. The food was still in their mouths. 31 Then God's anger rose against them. He killed their strongest men. He brought down the young men of Israel.

32 Even after all this, they still sinned. They did not believe Him, even with His wonders. 33 So He made their days end like a breath. Their years ended in fear.

34 When He killed some of them, the others looked for Him. They turned back and searched for God. 35 They remembered that God was their rock. The Most High God was the one who saved them. 36 But they only praised Him with their mouths. With their tongues they lied to Him. 37 Their hearts were not true to Him. They were not faithful to His agreement.

God is kind, but they forget again

38 But God was kind. He forgave their sin. He did not destroy them. He held back His anger many times. He did not let all His wrath come out. 39 He remembered that they were only people. They were like wind that passes and does not come back.

40 How often they fought against Him in the desert! How often they made Him sad in the dry land! 41 They tested God again and again. They made the Holy One of Israel angry. 42 They did not remember His power. They did not remember the day He saved them from their enemy. 43 They did not remember the signs He did in Egypt. They did not remember His wonders in the fields of Zoan.

God's plagues in Egypt

44 He turned their rivers into blood. They could not drink from their streams. 45 He sent flies that ate them. He sent frogs that ruined them. 46 He gave their crops to the locust. He gave the fruit of their work to the grasshopper. 47 He killed their vines with hail. He killed their fig trees with frost. 48 He gave their animals to the hail. He gave their flocks to lightning. 49 He sent His burning anger on them. He sent wrath, trouble, and distress. He sent angels to hurt them. 50 He made a path for His anger. He did not spare them from death. He gave their lives to sickness. 51 He struck all the firstborn in Egypt. He struck the first sons in the tents of Ham.

52 Then He led His people out like sheep. He guided them in the desert like a flock. 53 He led them safely. They were not afraid. The sea covered their enemies. 54 He brought them to His holy place. He brought them to the mountain His right hand won. 55 He drove out nations in front of them. He gave them land to live in. He settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

56 But they tested God the Most High. They fought against Him. They did not keep His rules. 57 They turned back and were not faithful, like their fathers. They were like a bow that does not shoot straight. 58 They made Him angry with their hilltop altars. They made Him jealous with their idols. 59 God heard this and was very angry. He turned away from Israel. 60 He left His home at Shiloh. That was the tent where He lived among people. 61 He let His power be taken away. He let His glory fall into the enemy's hand. 62 He gave His people to the sword. He let His anger fall on His own people. 63 Fire ate their young men. Their young women did not sing happy songs. 64 Their priests fell by the sword. Their widows did not cry.

65 Then the Lord woke up, like a man waking from sleep. He was like a strong man shouting after wine. 66 He beat back His enemies. He put them to shame forever.

God chooses David

67 He turned away from the tent of Joseph. He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim. 68 He chose the tribe of Judah. He chose Mount Zion, which He loves. 69 He built His holy place high like the heavens. He made it strong like the earth He set forever. 70 He chose David, His servant. He took him from the sheep pens. 71 He brought him from caring for mother sheep. He brought him to lead Jacob, His people. He brought him to lead Israel, His own land. 72 David led them with a true heart. He guided them with skillful hands.
