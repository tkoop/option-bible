Jesus Comes Back to Life

1 After the Sabbath day, at dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb. 2 Suddenly the ground shook hard. An angel of the Lord came down from heaven. He went to the tomb and rolled the stone away. Then he sat on it. 3 He looked like lightning, and his clothes were white as snow. 4 The guards shook with fear. They became like dead men.

5 The angel said to the women, "Do not be afraid. I know you are looking for Jesus, who was put on a cross. 6 He is not here. He is alive again, just like He said. Come and see the place where He lay. 7 Then go quickly and tell His followers that He is alive again. He is going ahead of you to Galilee. You will see Him there. I have told you."

8 The women hurried away from the tomb. They were afraid, but they were also very happy. They ran to tell His followers. 9 Suddenly Jesus met them. He said, "Hello." The women came to Him. They held His feet and worshiped Him. 10 Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go and tell My brothers to go to Galilee. They will see Me there."

The Soldiers Tell a Lie

11 While the women were going, some guards went into the city. They told the chief priests everything that had happened. 12 The chief priests met with the elders. They made a plan. They gave the soldiers a lot of money. 13 They said, "Say that His followers came at night and stole Him while we were sleeping. 14 If the governor hears this, we will explain it to him. We will keep you out of trouble." 15 The soldiers took the money and did what they were told. This story spread among the Jews. It is still told today.

Jesus Gives His Last Words

16 The eleven followers went to Galilee. They went to the mountain Jesus had told them about. 17 When they saw Him, they worshiped Him. But some doubted. 18 Jesus came to them and said, "All power in heaven and on earth has been given to Me. 19 So go and make followers of all nations. Baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. 20 Teach them to obey everything I have told you. And I am with you always, even to the end of time."
