Jesus Is Tested in the Desert

1 Jesus was full of the Holy Spirit. He left the Jordan River, and the Spirit led Him into the desert. 2 There the devil tested Him for forty days. Jesus ate nothing during those days. When the days ended, He was hungry.

3 The devil said to Him, "If You are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread."

4 Jesus answered, "It is written, 'A person does not live by bread alone.'"

5 The devil led Him up high and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment. 6 The devil said to Him, "I will give You all this power and glory. It has been given to me, and I give it to anyone I want. 7 So if You bow down and worship me, it will all be Yours."

8 Jesus answered, "It is written, 'Worship the LORD your God and serve Him only.'"

9 The devil led Him to Jerusalem and set Him on the highest part of the temple. He said, "If You are the Son of God, jump down from here. 10 For it is written, 'He will command His angels to take care of You. 11 They will lift You up in their hands so You will not hit Your foot on a stone.'"

12 Jesus answered, "It is also written, 'Do not test the LORD your God.'"

13 When the devil finished all his testing, he left Him for a while. 14 Jesus went back to Galilee in the power of the Spirit. News about Him spread through all the nearby land. 15 He taught in their meeting places, and everyone praised Him.

Jesus Teaches in Nazareth

16 He went to Nazareth, where He grew up. On the day of rest He went into the meeting place, as He usually did, and stood up to read. 17 Someone gave Him the book of the prophet Isaiah. He opened the book and found the place where these words were written: 18 "The Spirit of the LORD is on Me. He has chosen Me to tell good news to the poor. He has sent Me to tell prisoners they are free. He has sent Me to help blind people see again. He has sent Me to set free people who are hurt. 19 He has sent Me to tell about the year when the LORD will show His kindness."

20 Then He closed the book, gave it back to the helper, and sat down. Everyone in the meeting place stared at Him. 21 He began to say to them, "Today these words from God have come true while you listen."

22 Everyone spoke well of Him. They were amazed at the kind words from His mouth. They said, "Isn't this Joseph's son?"

23 Jesus said to them, "Surely you will say this to Me: 'Doctor, heal yourself!' And you will say, 'Do here in your hometown what we heard You did in Capernaum.'"

24 He said, "I tell you the truth, no prophet is accepted in his hometown. 25 I tell you the truth, there were many widows in Israel in the days of Elijah. It did not rain for three and a half years, and there was a great hunger over all the land. 26 Yet Elijah was not sent to any of them. He was sent to a widow in Zarephath in Sidon. 27 And there were many people with skin disease in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet. None of them was healed except Naaman the Syrian."

28 When all the people in the meeting place heard this, they were very angry. 29 They got up, threw Him out of the town, and took Him to the edge of the hill where the town was built. They wanted to throw Him down the cliff. 30 But He walked right through the crowd and went on His way.

Jesus Teaches and Heals in Capernaum

31 He went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee, and taught them on the day of rest. 32 They were amazed at His teaching, because He taught with power.

33 In the meeting place there was a man with an evil spirit. He cried out in a loud voice, 34 "Go away! What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!"

35 Jesus said to him, "Be quiet! Come out of him!" The evil spirit threw the man down in the middle and came out of him without hurting him.

36 Everyone was amazed. They said to each other, "What is this teaching? He gives orders to evil spirits with power, and they come out!" 37 News about Him spread everywhere in the area.

Jesus Heals Many People

38 Jesus left the meeting place and went to Simon's house. Simon's mother-in-law was sick with a high fever, and they asked Him about her. 39 He stood over her and told the fever to go away. It left her right away, and she got up and served them.

40 When the sun went down, all who had sick people with different diseases brought them to Him. He put His hands on each one and healed them. 41 Demons also came out of many people. They shouted, "You are the Son of God!" But He told them to be quiet because they knew He was the Christ.

42 When day came, He went to a quiet place. The crowds looked for Him and came to Him. They tried to keep Him from leaving them. 43 But He said to them, "I must tell the good news of God's kingdom to other towns too. That is why I was sent." 44 And He kept preaching in the meeting places of Judea.
