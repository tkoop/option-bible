Peter Writes Again

1 Dear friends, this is the second letter I write to you. I write to wake up your true thinking. I want you to remember. 2 Remember the words the holy prophets said. Remember the command from our Lord and Savior. His apostles gave that command to you.

People Who Make Fun Will Come

3 First, know this. In the last days, people who make fun will come. They will follow their own bad wants. 4 They will say, "Where is the promise that He will come? Our fathers died long ago. But everything is the same as it was from the start of the world."

God Made the World

5 They choose not to see this. Long ago, God's word made the heavens. He made the earth too. The earth came from water and was made with water. 6 Water also destroyed the world back then. It was a great flood. 7 God's same word keeps the heavens and earth we have now. They are kept for fire. They are kept for the day God judges people. That day will destroy people who do not follow God.

God Is Patient

8 Dear friends, do not forget this. To the Lord, one day is like a thousand years. A thousand years are like one day. 9 The Lord is not slow to keep His promise. Some people think He is slow. But He is patient with you. He does not want anyone to die in sin. He wants everyone to turn from sin.

The Day of the Lord

10 But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. The heavens will pass away with a loud noise. The parts of the world will burn up and be destroyed. The earth and what people have done on it will not be found. 11 Since all these things will be destroyed, what kind of people should you be? You should live holy lives. You should live for God. 12 Look for the day of God. Want it to come soon. On that day, the heavens will burn and be destroyed. The parts of the world will melt in the heat. 13 But God promised something new. We look for new heavens and a new earth. Only right living will be there.

Live Good Lives

14 So, dear friends, since you look for these things, try hard. Be found by Him in peace. Be clean from sin. Be without blame. 15 Think of our Lord's patience as a chance to be saved. Our dear brother Paul wrote to you about this. God gave him wisdom to write it. 16 He writes about these things in all his letters. Some parts are hard to understand. People who do not know much and people who are not strong twist those parts. They also twist the other holy writings. They do this to destroy themselves. 17 Dear friends, you already know this. Be careful. Do not be led away by the wrong teaching of people who break God's rules. Do not fall from your strong faith. 18 Grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. May He have glory now and forever. Amen.
