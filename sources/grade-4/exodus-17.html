Water from the Rock

1 All the people of Israel left the desert of Sin. They traveled the way the the LORD told them. They camped at Rephidim. There was no water to drink.

2 The people argued with Moses. They said, "Give us water to drink." Moses said to them, "Why do you argue with me? Why do you test the the LORD?"

3 The people were thirsty. They complained to Moses. They said, "Why did you bring us out of Egypt? Did you bring us here to die of thirst, us and our children and our animals?"

4 Moses cried out to the the LORD. He said, "What should I do with these people? They are almost ready to throw stones at me."

5 The The LORD said to Moses, "Go ahead of the people. Take some leaders of Israel with you. Take the stick in your hand. It is the one you used to hit the Nile. Then go. 6 I will stand in front of you there on the rock at Horeb. Hit the rock. Water will come out of it. The people will drink." Moses did this while the leaders watched.

7 He named the place Massah and Meribah. That was because the people of Israel argued. It was also because they tested the the LORD. They said, "Is the the LORD with us or not?"

Amalek Attacks Israel

8 The Amalekites came and fought Israel at Rephidim. 9 Moses said to Joshua, "Pick some men for us. Go out and fight Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on top of the hill. I will hold God's stick in my hand."

10 Joshua did what Moses said. He fought Amalek. Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill. 11 When Moses held up his hand, Israel was stronger. When he put his hand down, Amalek was stronger.

12 Moses' hands got tired. So they put a stone under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur held up his hands. One stood on one side, and one on the other side. His hands stayed up until the sun went down. 13 Joshua beat Amalek and his people with the sword.

14 The The LORD said to Moses, "Write this in a book to remember it. Read it to Joshua. I will wipe out Amalek's name from under the sky."

15 Moses built an altar. He named it "The The LORD Is My Banner." 16 He said, "A hand was raised to the the LORD's throne! The The LORD will fight Amalek forever."
