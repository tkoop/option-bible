A Prayer for the King

1 May the LORD answer you when you have trouble. May the God of Jacob keep you safe. 2 May He send you help from His holy place. May He hold you up from Zion. 3 May He remember all your gifts. May He be pleased with the gifts you burn. Selah. 4 May He give you what your heart wants. May He make all your plans work. 5 We will shout with joy when you win. We will raise our flags for our God. May the LORD give you all you ask for.

God Saves His King

6 Now I know that the LORD saves His chosen king. He answers him from His holy heaven. He saves him with the power of His right hand. 7 Some trust in war carts. Some trust in horses. But we trust in the LORD our God. 8 They fall down and lose. But we stand up and stay strong. 9 The LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call!
