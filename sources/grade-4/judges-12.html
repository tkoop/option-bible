Ephraim Gets Angry at Jephthah

1 The men of Ephraim came together. They went north and said to Jephthah, "Why did you go fight the Ammonites and not call us to go with you? We will burn your house down!"

2 Jephthah said to them, "I and my people had a big fight with the Ammonites. I called you, but you did not save me from them. 3 I saw that you would not save me. So I risked my life and went to fight the Ammonites. The LORD gave them to me. Why are you coming to fight me today?"

4 Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead. They fought Ephraim. The Gileadites beat them, because the men of Ephraim had said, "You Gileadites are just runaway Ephraim people living among Ephraim and Manasseh."

5 The Gileadites took the crossing places of the Jordan River that led to Ephraim. When an Ephraim man who had escaped tried to cross, the Gilead men asked him, "Are you from Ephraim?" If he said no, they told him, "Say 'Shibboleth.'"

6 He said, "Sibboleth," because he could not say it right. Then they grabbed him and killed him at the Jordan crossing. At that time, 42,000 men from Ephraim died.

7 Jephthah led Israel for six years. Then he died and was buried in a town in Gilead.

More Leaders of Israel

8 After him, Ibzan from Bethlehem led Israel. 9 He had 30 sons and 30 daughters. He sent his daughters away to marry, and he brought in 30 young women from outside for his sons. He led Israel for seven years. 10 Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

11 After him, Elon from Zebulun led Israel. He led Israel for ten years. 12 Then Elon from Zebulun died and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

13 After him, Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon led Israel. 14 He had 40 sons and 30 grandsons. They rode on 70 donkeys. He led Israel for eight years. 15 Then Abdon son of Hillel from Pirathon died and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, on the hill country of the Amalekites.
