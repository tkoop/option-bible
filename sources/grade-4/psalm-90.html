A Prayer by Moses

1Lord, You have been our home in every time. 2Before the mountains were born, before You made the earth and the world, You are God from forever to forever.

Our Lives Are Short

3You turn people back to dust and say, "Return, people." 4For in Your eyes, a thousand years are like yesterday that is gone, or like a few hours in the night.

5You sweep people away in their sleep. They are like grass that grows in the morning. 6In the morning it blooms and grows. By evening it dries up and dies.

Our Sins and Our Days

7We are used up by Your anger. We are scared by Your great anger. 8You put our sins in front of You. Even our secret sins are in Your sight. 9All our days pass under Your anger. Our years end like a sigh.

10Most people live to be seventy. Some live to eighty if they are strong. But even those years are full of trouble and sadness. Life passes quickly, and we are gone.

Ask God for Help

11Who knows how strong Your anger is? It is as great as the honor You deserve. 12So teach us to count our days, so we can have wise hearts.

13Return, O the LORD! How long? Have pity on Your servants.

14Fill us with Your love in the morning, so we can sing and be glad all our days. 15Make us glad for as many days as You made us sad, for as many years as we saw trouble.

16Let Your work be seen by Your servants, and Your glory by their children. 17May the Lord our God show us favor. Make the work of our hands last. Yes, make our work last.
