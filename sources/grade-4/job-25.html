Bildad Says No One Is Clean Before God

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered. He said,

2 "God has power, and people fear Him. He makes peace in heaven. 3 No one can count all His armies. His light shines on everyone. 4 So how can a person be right with God? How can someone born from a woman be clean? 5 Even the moon is not bright enough for Him. The stars are not clean in His eyes. 6 How much less a person, who is only a worm!"
