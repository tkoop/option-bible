Jesus Rides Into Jerusalem

1 As Jesus and His disciples got close to Jerusalem, they came to Bethphage. This town is on the Mount of Olives. Jesus sent two disciples. 2 He said, "Go into the village ahead of you. You will find a donkey tied up. A young donkey will be with her. Untie them and bring them to Me." 3 If anyone says anything to you, say, "The Lord needs them." He will send them right away. 4 This happened to make true what the prophet said.

5 "Tell the people of Zion, 'Look! Your King is coming to you. He is gentle. He rides on a donkey. He rides on a young donkey.'"

6 The disciples went and did what Jesus told them. 7 They brought the donkey and the young donkey. They put their coats on the animals. Jesus sat on the coats. 8 A big crowd spread their coats on the road. Other people cut branches from trees and spread them on the road.

9 The people in front of Jesus and the people behind Him shouted, "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!"

10 When Jesus came into Jerusalem, the whole city was shaken. The people asked, "Who is this?"

11 The crowds said, "This is Jesus. He is the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee."

Jesus Cleans the Temple

12 Jesus went into the temple. He threw out all the people who were buying and selling there. He turned over the tables of the money changers. He turned over the chairs of the people selling doves.

13 He said to them, "It is written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer.' But you make it a den for robbers."

14 Blind people and lame people came to Him in the temple. He healed them.

15 The chief priests and teachers saw the wonderful things He did. They saw the children shouting in the temple, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" They were angry.

16 They said to Him, "Do you hear what these children are saying?"

Jesus said, "Yes. Have you never read, 'From the mouths of little children and babies, You have made praise perfect'?"

17 Jesus left them. He went out of the city to Bethany. He stayed there.

Jesus Makes a Fig Tree Dry Up

18 In the morning, Jesus was going back to the city. He was hungry.

19 He saw a fig tree by the road. He went to it. But He found only leaves. He said to it, "May no fruit ever grow on you again." Right away the fig tree dried up.

20 The disciples saw it. They were amazed. They said, "How did the fig tree dry up so fast?"

21 Jesus answered, "I tell you the truth. If you have faith and do not doubt, you will do more than this to the fig tree. You can even say to this mountain, 'Move and throw yourself into the sea,' and it will happen. 22 If you believe, you will get everything you ask for in prayer."

Who Gave Jesus His Power?

23 Jesus went back to the temple. He was teaching. The chief priests and the elders came to Him. They said, "By what power do you do these things? Who gave you this power?"

24 Jesus answered, "I will ask you one question too. If you answer Me, I will tell you by what power I do these things. 25 Where did John's baptism come from? From heaven or from people?"

The chief priests and elders thought to themselves, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will say, 'Then why did you not believe him?'" 26 "But if we say, 'From people,' we are afraid of the crowd. All the people think John is a prophet."

27 So they answered Jesus, "We do not know."

Jesus said, "Then I will not tell you by what power I do these things."

The Story of Two Sons

28 Jesus said, "What do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first son and said, 'Son, go work in the vineyard today.' 29 The son answered, 'I will not.' But later he changed his mind and went. 30 The man went to the second son and said the same thing. The second son said, 'I will, sir.' But he did not go."

31 "Which son did what his father wanted?"

The chief priests and elders said, "The first."

Jesus said, "I tell you the truth. Tax collectors and prostitutes will go into God's kingdom before you."

32 "John came to show you the right way. You did not believe him. But tax collectors and prostitutes did believe him. Even after you saw that, you did not change your minds and believe him."

The Story of the Vineyard

33 Jesus said, "Listen to another story. A man owned a vineyard. He planted it. He put a wall around it. He dug a place for a wine press. He built a tower. Then he rented it to some farmers. He went away. 34 When it was time for the fruit, he sent his servants to the farmers to get his share. 35 The farmers took his servants. They beat one. They killed one. They threw stones at another. 36 He sent more servants than before. The farmers did the same thing to them. 37 Last, he sent his son. He thought, 'They will respect my son.' 38 But the farmers saw the son. They said to each other, 'This is the one who will get everything. Let's kill him and take his land.' 39 They took him. They threw him out of the vineyard. They killed him."

40 "So when the owner comes, what will he do to those farmers?"

41 The chief priests and elders said, "He will destroy those bad men. He will rent the vineyard to other farmers. They will give him the fruit at the right time."

42 Jesus said, "Have you never read this in the Bible? 'The stone the builders did not want has become the most important stone. The Lord did this. It is wonderful to us.' 43 So I tell you, God's kingdom will be taken from you. It will be given to people who make good fruit. 44 Anyone who falls on this stone will be broken. Anyone it falls on will be crushed."

45 The chief priests and the Pharisees heard Jesus' stories. They knew He was talking about them. 46 They wanted to arrest Him. But they were afraid of the crowds. The crowds thought Jesus was a prophet.
