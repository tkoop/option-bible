Praise the LORD

1Praise the LORD! I will thank the LORD with all my heart. I will do this with good people who follow God, in our meeting.

God's Great Works

2The LORD does great things. People who love His works study them. 3His work is beautiful and great. He always does what is right, and that lasts forever.

God Is Kind and Caring

4He made people remember His miracles. The LORD is kind and caring. 5He gives food to those who respect Him. He remembers His promise forever.

God Helps His People

6He showed His people how strong His works are. He gave them the lands of other nations. 7His hands made things that are true and fair. All His rules can be trusted. 8They stand strong forever. They are made with truth and what is right. 9He sent help to His people. He made His promise last forever. His name is holy and wonderful.

Wisdom Starts with God

10Respecting the LORD is the start of wisdom. Everyone who follows His rules learns good sense. His praise lasts forever!
