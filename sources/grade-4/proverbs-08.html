Wisdom Calls Out

1Does wisdom call out? Does understanding raise her voice? 2She stands on high places, by the road, where paths cross. 3She stands by the city gates and calls out at the doors.

Wisdom Speaks to People

4“I call to you, people. I speak to everyone. 5People who are simple, learn to be smart. People who are foolish, learn to think. 6Listen, for I speak good and wise things. My words are right. 7My mouth speaks truth. I hate wrong words. 8All my words are good. None are crooked or twisted. 9They are clear to those who understand. They are right to those who seek knowledge.

Wisdom Is Better Than Money

10“Take my teaching, not silver. Choose knowledge over gold. 11Wisdom is better than jewels. Nothing anyone wants can match it. 12I am Wisdom. I live with good sense. I find knowledge and wise plans.

Wisdom Hates Evil

13“To fear the the LORD is to hate evil. I hate pride, bragging, bad ways, and twisted words. 14I have good advice and wise plans. I have understanding and strength.

Wisdom Helps Leaders

15“Kings rule by me. Leaders make fair laws by me. 16Princes rule by me. All leaders judge fairly by me. 17I love those who love me. Those who look for me find me.

Wisdom Brings Wealth

18“Riches and honor are with me. So are lasting wealth and right living. 19My fruit is better than gold. What I give is better than silver. 20I walk on the path of right living. I stay on the road of fairness. 21I give wealth to those who love me. I fill their storage rooms.

Wisdom Was There at Creation

22“The LORD made me first, before He began His work long ago. 23I was set up from long ago, before the earth began. 24When there were no deep oceans, I was born. When there were no springs of water, 25before the mountains were set in place, before the hills were born, 26before He made the earth and its fields, or the dust of the world.

27“When He set the sky in place, I was there. He drew a circle on the deep sea. 28He made the clouds strong above. He made the deep springs strong. 29He set a limit for the sea, so the waters would not cross it. He laid the earth's foundations. 30I was beside Him like a skilled worker. I was His joy day by day. I rejoiced before Him always. 31I rejoiced in His world where people live. My delight was with people.

Listen to Wisdom

32“Now, children, listen to me. Happy are those who keep my ways. 33Listen to teaching and be wise. Do not ignore it. 34Happy is the one who listens to me, who waits at my doors every day, who keeps my doorways.

35“For whoever finds me finds life. He gets favor from the the LORD. 36But whoever sins against me hurts himself. All who hate me love death.”
