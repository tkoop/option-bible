Azariah Becomes King of Judah

1 In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam, king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah became king of Judah. 2 He was sixteen years old when he became king. He was king in Jerusalem for fifty-two years. His mother was Jecoliah, from Jerusalem. 3 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done. 4 But the hilltop worship places were not taken away. The people still gave gifts and burned incense there.

5 The LORD made the king sick with leprosy. He had it until he died. He lived in a separate house. His son Jotham was in charge of the palace and judged the people of the land. 6 The other things Azariah did are written in the book of the kings of Judah. 7 Azariah died and was buried with his family in the City of David. His son Jotham became king after him.

Zechariah Becomes King of Israel

8 In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam became king of Israel in Samaria. He was king for six months. 9 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his fathers had done. He did not stop the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. 10 Shallum son of Jabesh made a secret plan against him. He struck him down and killed him in front of the people. Then he became king in his place. 11 The other things Zechariah did are written in the book of the kings of Israel. 12 This was the word the LORD had spoken to Jehu: “Your sons will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.” And it happened.

Shallum Rules Israel

13 In the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah, king of Judah, Shallum son of Jabesh became king. He was king in Samaria for one month. 14 Menahem son of Gad went up from Tirzah to Samaria. He attacked Shallum son of Jabesh and killed him. Then he became king in his place. 15 The other things Shallum did, and the plan he made, are written in the book of the kings of Israel.

Menahem Rules Israel

16 Then Menahem attacked Tiphsah and everyone in it and its borders. They would not open the city gates, so he attacked it. He killed everyone in the city, even women who were going to have babies. 17 In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Menahem son of Gad became king of Israel in Samaria. He was king for ten years. 18 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not stop the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. 19 Pul, the king of Assyria, came into the land. Menahem gave Pul one thousand talents of silver so that Pul would help him keep his kingdom. 20 Menahem got the silver from all the rich men of Israel. He made each man give fifty pieces of silver to the king of Assyria. Then the king of Assyria went back and did not stay in the land. 21 The other things Menahem did are written in the book of the kings of Israel. 22 Menahem died and was buried with his family. His son Pekahiah became king after him.

Pekahiah Rules Israel

23 In the fiftieth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king of Israel in Samaria. He was king for two years. 24 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not stop the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. 25 Pekah son of Remaliah, one of his officers, made a secret plan against him. With Argob, Arieh, and fifty men from Gilead, he struck him down and killed him in the palace in Samaria. Then he became king in his place. 26 The other things Pekahiah did are written in the book of the kings of Israel.

Pekah Rules Israel

27 In the fifty-second year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king of Israel in Samaria. He was king for twenty years. 28 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not stop the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. 29 In the days of Pekah, king of Israel, Tiglath-Pileser, king of Assyria, came. He took Ijon, Abel Beth Maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, Galilee, and all the land of Naphtali. He carried the people away to Assyria. 30 Hoshea son of Elah made a secret plan against Pekah son of Remaliah. He struck him down and killed him. Then he became king in his place. This was in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah. 31 The other things Pekah did are written in the book of the kings of Israel.

Jotham Rules Judah

32 In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah became king of Judah. 33 He was twenty-five years old when he became king. He was king in Jerusalem for sixteen years. His mother was Jerusha, the daughter of Zadok. 34 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Uzziah had done. 35 But the hilltop worship places were not taken away. The people still gave gifts and burned incense there. He built the upper gate of the house of the LORD. 36 The other things Jotham did are written in the book of the kings of Judah. 37 In those days the LORD began to send Rezin, king of Aram, and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 38 Jotham died and was buried with his family in the City of David, his father's city. His son Ahaz became king after him.
