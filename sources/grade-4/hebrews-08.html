Jesus Is Our Great High Priest

1 The main thing is this: We have a high priest like that. He sat down on the right side of the throne of the Majesty in heaven. 2 He serves in the holy place and in the true tent. The Lord set up that tent, not people.

3 Every high priest is chosen to give gifts and offerings. So this Priest must have something to give too. 4 If He were on earth, He would not be a priest, because other priests already give gifts the way the law says.

The First Tent Was Only a Copy

5 They serve in a place that is only a copy and shadow of the things in heaven. That is why God told Moses, when he was about to build the tent, "Be careful to make everything just like the pattern I showed you on the mountain."

6 But now Jesus has a much better work. He is the go-between for a better agreement. That agreement is based on better promises. 7 If the first agreement had been perfect, there would be no need for a second one.

God Promises a New Agreement

8 But God found fault with the people. He said, "Look, the days are coming, says the Lord, when I will make a new agreement with the people of Israel and with the people of Judah. 9 It will not be like the agreement I made with their fathers when I took them by the hand to lead them out of Egypt. They did not keep My agreement, so I did not care for them, says the Lord.

10 "This is the agreement I will make with the people of Israel after those days, says the Lord: I will put My laws in their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people.

11 "No one will need to teach his neighbor or his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' because everyone will know Me, from the least to the greatest.

12 "For I will forgive their wrongs and not remember their sins anymore."

The Old Agreement Is Ending

13 By calling this one "new," God has made the first agreement old. And what is old and wearing out will soon be gone.
