David asks God to fight for him

1 The LORD, fight the people who fight me. Stand against the people who attack me. 2 Pick up your shield and small shield. Get up and help me. 3 Lift your spear and throw it at the people chasing me. Say to me, "I will save you."

He prays that his enemies will lose

4 Make the people who want to kill me feel ashamed. Make the people who plan bad things for me turn back and feel confused. 5 Make them like dust in the wind. Let the angel of the the LORD push them away. 6 Make their road dark and slippery. Let the angel of the the LORD chase them.

7 They hid a net for me for no reason. They dug a hole for me for no reason. 8 Let trouble come on them by surprise. Let the net they hid catch them. Let them fall into the hole and be ruined.

David praises God for saving him

9 Then my soul will be happy in the the LORD. I will be glad because He saves me. 10 All my bones will say, "the the LORD, who is like You? You save poor people from those who are too strong for them. You save poor and needy people from those who rob them."

His enemies lie about him

11 Mean people stand up and lie about me. They ask me things I do not know. 12 They pay me back with bad for good. They hurt my heart.

13 But when they were sick, I wore rough clothes. I made myself humble with not eating. My prayers were not answered. 14 I walked sadly like I did for a friend or brother. I bowed down in sadness like I lost my mother.

15 But when I fell, they were happy and came together. People I did not know attacked me. They said bad things about me all the time. 16 Like mean people at a party, they grit their teeth at me.

David asks God to rescue him

17 How long, O Lord, will You look on? Save my soul from their attacks. Save my life from these lions. 18 Then I will thank You in the big group. I will praise You among many people.

19 Do not let my enemies brag about me for no reason. Do not let those who hate me wink for no reason. 20 They do not talk about peace. They make tricks against quiet people in the land. 21 They open their mouths wide against me and say, "Aha, aha! We saw it!"

22 O the the LORD, You have seen. Do not be quiet. O Lord, do not stay far from me. 23 Wake up and stand up for my side. Stand up for my case, my God and my Lord. 24 Judge me by Your right ways, O the the LORD my God. Do not let them brag about me.

25 Do not let them say in their hearts, "Aha, we have swallowed him up!" Do not let them say, "We have eaten him up!" 26 Let the people who are happy about my trouble feel ashamed together. Let the people who put themselves above me be covered with shame.

David hopes good people will be glad

27 Let the people who want what is right shout for joy and be glad. Let them always say, "Great is the the LORD, who is happy with His servant." 28 Then my tongue will say Your right ways and Your praise all day long.
