God, Please Stop the Wicked

1 The LORD, God who pays back wrong, show yourself! 2 Stand up, Judge of the whole world. Give proud people what they deserve. 3 How long, the LORD, will bad people keep winning? How long will they cheer? 4 They say proud and mean things. All who do wrong brag. 5 They hurt your people, the LORD. They trouble the ones who belong to you. 6 They kill widows and strangers. They murder children with no parents. 7 They say, "The LORD does not see us. The God of Jacob does not notice."

God Sees and Knows

8 Listen, you silly people! You fools, when will you get smart? 9 The One who made ears, can't He hear? The One who made eyes, can't He see? 10 The One who trains nations, won't He correct them? The One who teaches people what to know—the LORD—knows what people think. 11 The LORD knows what people think. Their thoughts are like a puff of air.

God Helps His People

12 Happy is the person the LORD corrects and teaches from His law. 13 He gets rest from hard days until the wicked are caught. 14 The LORD will not leave His people. He will not give up the ones who belong to Him. 15 Fairness will come back to good people. Everyone with a right heart will follow it.

God Helps Me

16 Who will stand up for me against bad people? Who will help me against those who do wrong? 17 If the LORD had not helped me, I would soon be in the quiet grave. 18 When I thought, "I am falling," your faithful love, the LORD, held me up. 19 When I have many worries, your comfort cheers me up.

Bad Rulers Cannot Stand with God

20 Can bad rulers be your friends? They make unfair laws. 21 They gang up against good people. They say innocent people should die. 22 But the LORD is my safe place. My God is the rock I hide behind. 23 He will make their wrong come back on them. He will wipe them out for their evil. The LORD our God will destroy them.
