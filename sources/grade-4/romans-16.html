Phoebe, a Helper in the Church

1 I tell you about our sister Phoebe. She serves the church in Cenchreae. 2 Welcome her in the Lord in a way that is right for God's holy people. Help her with anything she needs from you, because she has helped many people, and me too.

Greetings to Friends and Workers

3 Greet Priscilla and Aquila. They work with me for Christ Jesus. 4 They risked their lives for me. I am thankful for them, and so are all the churches of the people who are not Jews. 5 Greet the church that meets in their home. Greet my dear friend Epaenetus. He was the first person in Asia to follow Christ. 6 Greet Mary. She worked very hard for you. 7 Greet Andronicus and Junia. They are my relatives and were in prison with me. They are well known among the apostles, and they followed Christ before I did. 8 Greet Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord. 9 Greet Urbanus, who works with us for Christ, and my dear friend Stachys. 10 Greet Apelles, who has shown he is true in Christ. Greet the people from Aristobulus's home. 11 Greet Herodion, my relative. Greet the people from Narcissus's home who belong to the Lord. 12 Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa. They work hard for the Lord. Greet my dear friend Persis. She worked very hard for the Lord. 13 Greet Rufus, who is chosen in the Lord, and his mother. She has been like a mother to me too. 14 Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them. 15 Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, Olympas, and all God's holy people with them. 16 Greet each other with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.

Watch Out for People Who Cause Trouble

17 I beg you, brothers and sisters, to watch out for people who cause fights and lead others to sin. They go against the teaching you learned. Stay away from them. 18 These people do not serve our Lord Christ. They serve their own stomachs. They use smooth talk and kind words to trick the hearts of innocent people. 19 Everyone has heard that you obey. So I am glad about you. But I want you to be wise about what is good, and innocent about what is bad. 20 The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

Greetings from Paul's Friends

21 Timothy, who works with me, greets you. So do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my relatives. 22 I am Tertius. I wrote this letter. I greet you in the Lord. 23 Gaius greets you. He has given me a place to stay, and he does the same for the whole church. Erastus, who keeps the city's money, greets you. So does Quartus, a brother.

Praise to God

25 Now to God, who can make you strong. He does this through my good news and through the message about Jesus Christ. This shows a secret plan that was hidden for a very long time. 26 But now it is shown. It is made known through the writings of the prophets. The God who lives forever gave the command. It is for all nations, so they will obey and have faith. 27 To the only wise God be glory forever through Jesus Christ. Amen.
