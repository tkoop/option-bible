The People Refuse to Listen to the LORD

1Jeremiah finished telling all the people all the words the LORD their God had sent him. These were all words from the LORD for them. 2Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the proud men said to Jeremiah, "You are telling a lie. the LORD our God did not send you to say, 'Do not go to Egypt to live there.' 3But Baruch son of Neriah is turning you against us. He wants the Chaldeans to kill us and take us to Babylon." 4So Johanan son of Kareah, all the army leaders, and all the people did not obey the LORD. They did not stay in the land of Judah.

They Take Everyone to Egypt

5Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the army leaders took all the people left in Judah. These people had come back from many nations to live in Judah. 6They took the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, and every other person. Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, had left these people with Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan. They also took Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah. 7They went to Egypt because they did not obey the LORD. They came to Tahpanhes.

Jeremiah Gives a Sign in Egypt

8Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes. 9He said, "Take some big stones in your hand. Hide them in the cement in the brick floor at the entrance to Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes. Do this while the men of Judah watch. 10Say to them, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am going to send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. I will set his throne over these stones I hid. He will spread his royal tent over them. 11He will come and attack Egypt. Some will die. Some will be taken captive. Some will be killed with swords. 12I will set fire to the houses of Egypt's gods. He will burn them and carry the people away. He will wrap himself in Egypt like a shepherd wraps his coat. Then he will leave in peace. 13He will break the stone pillars at Beth-shemesh in Egypt. He will burn the houses of Egypt's gods.'"
