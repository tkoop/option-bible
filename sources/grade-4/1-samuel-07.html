The Ark Comes to Kiriath-jearim

1 The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took the ark of the the LORD. They brought it to Abinadab's house on the hill. They set apart his son Eleazar to watch over the ark of the the LORD. 2 The ark stayed in Kiriath-jearim for a long time—twenty years. All the people of Israel were sad and wanted the the LORD.

Israel Turns Back to God

3 Then Samuel said to all Israel, "If you want to come back to the the LORD with all your heart, then put away the foreign gods and the Ashtoreth statues from among you. Get your hearts ready for the the LORD. Serve Him only. Then He will save you from the Philistines."

4 So the Israelites put away the Baals and the Ashtoreths. They served the the LORD only.

Samuel Calls Israel Together

5 Then Samuel said, "Gather all Israel at Mizpah. I will pray to the the LORD for you."

6 They gathered at Mizpah. They drew water and poured it out before the the LORD. That day they did not eat. They said, "We have sinned against the the LORD." Samuel judged the Israelites at Mizpah.

The Philistines Attack

7 The Philistines heard that Israel had gathered at Mizpah. The Philistine leaders went up to fight Israel. The Israelites heard about it and were afraid.

8 The Israelites said to Samuel, "Do not stop praying to the the LORD our God for us. Ask Him to save us from the Philistines."

God Helps Israel Win

9 Samuel took a baby lamb and burned it all as an offering to the the LORD. He prayed to the the LORD for Israel. The the LORD answered him. 10 While Samuel was offering the burnt offering, the Philistines came near to fight Israel. But that day the the LORD thundered with a loud voice against the Philistines. He confused them. They were beaten by Israel.

11 The men of Israel marched out of Mizpah. They chased the Philistines. They beat them all the way to a place below Beth-car.

12 Then Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen. He named it Ebenezer. He said, "So far the the LORD has helped us."

Israel Gets Its Cities Back

13 The Philistines were beaten down. They did not come into Israel's land again. The the LORD worked against the Philistines all the days of Samuel. 14 The cities the Philistines had taken from Israel, from Ekron to Gath, were given back. Israel took their land back from the Philistines. There was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

Samuel Judges Israel

15 Samuel judged Israel all his life. 16 Every year he traveled from Bethel to Gilgal to Mizpah. He judged Israel in all these places. 17 Then he went back to Ramah. His home was there. He judged Israel there. He built an altar to the the LORD there.
