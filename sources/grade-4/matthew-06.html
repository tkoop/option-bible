Giving to People in Need

1 Be careful not to do your good deeds in front of people just to be seen by them. If you do, your Father in heaven will not give you a reward.

2 So when you give to people in need, do not blow a trumpet before you, like the show-offs do in places of worship and on the streets. They want people to praise them. I tell you the truth, they already have their reward.

3 But when you give to someone in need, keep it a secret. 4 Then your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

How to Pray

5 And when you pray, do not be like the show-offs. They love to stand and pray in places of worship and on street corners so people can see them. I tell you the truth, they already have their reward.

6 But when you pray, go into your room, close your door, and pray to your Father, who cannot be seen. Then your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

7 When you pray, do not keep saying the same words over and over, like people who do not know God do. They think they will be heard because they use many words. 8 Do not be like them. Your Father knows what you need before you ask Him.

The Lord's Prayer

9 So pray like this: "Our Father in heaven, may Your name be kept holy. 10 May Your kingdom come. May what You want be done on earth, just as it is in heaven. 11 Give us today the food we need. 12 Forgive us for the wrong things we have done, just as we forgive people who have wronged us. 13 And do not let us be tempted to do wrong. But save us from the evil one."

Forgiving Others

14 If you forgive other people when they do wrong to you, your Father in heaven will forgive you too. 15 But if you do not forgive them, your Father will not forgive your wrongs.

Fasting

16 When you fast, do not look sad like the show-offs do. They make their faces look sad so people will see they are fasting. I tell you the truth, they already have their reward.

17 But when you fast, wash your face and put oil on your head. 18 Then no one will know you are fasting, except your Father, who cannot be seen. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

Treasures in Heaven

19 Do not store up treasures for yourselves on earth. Moths and rust can destroy them, and thieves can break in and steal them. 20 Instead, store up treasures for yourselves in heaven. Moths and rust cannot destroy them there, and thieves cannot break in and steal them. 21 For where your treasure is, there your heart will be too.

22 The eye is like a lamp for the body. So if your eye is healthy, your whole body will be full of light. 23 But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If the light in you is really darkness, how dark that is!

24 No one can serve two masters. He will hate one and love the other, or he will be loyal to one and look down on the other. You cannot serve both God and money.

Do Not Worry

25 So I tell you, do not worry about your life. Do not worry about what you will eat or drink. Do not worry about what you will wear. Is life not more than food? Is the body not more than clothes?

26 Look at the birds in the sky. They do not plant seeds or gather crops or store food in barns. But your Father in heaven feeds them. Are you not worth much more than they are?

27 Can any of you add even one hour to his life by worrying? 28 And why do you worry about clothes? Look at how the wild flowers grow. They do not work or make clothes. 29 But I tell you, even King Solomon in all his glory was not dressed as well as one of these flowers. 30 If God dresses the grass of the field so well, and it is here today and gone tomorrow, will He not dress you even better? You have so little faith!

31 So do not worry. Do not say, "What will we eat?" or "What will we drink?" or "What will we wear?" 32 People who do not know God chase after all these things. But your Father in heaven knows you need them. 33 But put God's kingdom and His right way first. Then all these things will be given to you too. 34 So do not worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will have its own worries. Each day has enough trouble of its own.
