A Baby Boy Is Born

1 A man from the family of Levi married a woman from the family of Levi. 2 She became pregnant and had a baby boy. She saw that he was a good baby, so she hid him for three months.

3 When she could not hide him any more, she got a basket made from plant stems. She covered it with tar and sticky stuff. She put the baby in the basket. Then she put the basket in the tall grass by the river. 4 His sister stood far away to see what would happen to him.

5 Pharaoh's daughter came down to wash in the river. Her helpers walked by the river. She saw the basket in the tall grass. She sent her helper to get it. 6 She opened it and saw the baby. The baby boy was crying. She felt sorry for him. She said, "This is one of the Hebrew children."

7 Then his sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, "Should I go and find a Hebrew woman to feed the baby for you?"

8 "Go," Pharaoh's daughter said. So the girl went and got the baby's mother. 9 Pharaoh's daughter said to her, "Take this baby and feed him for me. I will pay you." The woman took the baby and fed him.

10 The baby grew up. She brought him to Pharaoh's daughter. He became her son. She named him Moses. She said, "I pulled him out of the water."

Moses Grows Up

11 Years later, Moses was grown. He went out to see his own people. He saw how hard they worked. He saw an Egyptian man hit a Hebrew man, one of his people. 12 He looked this way and that. He saw no one. He hit the Egyptian man and hid him in the sand.

13 The next day, he went out again. He saw two Hebrew men fighting. He said to the one who started it, "Why are you hitting your friend?"

14 The man said, "Who made you our leader and judge? Are you going to kill me like you killed the Egyptian?"

Moses was afraid. He thought, "Now people know what I did!"

Moses Runs Away

15 Pharaoh heard about this. He tried to kill Moses. Moses ran away from Pharaoh. He went to live in the land of Midian. He sat down by a well.

Moses Helps at the Well

16 The priest of Midian had seven daughters. They came to get water. They filled the water tubs to give water to their father's sheep. 17 Some shepherds came and chased them away. Moses stood up and helped the girls. He gave water to their sheep.

18 The girls went home to their father Reuel. He asked, "Why did you come home so early today?"

19 They said, "An Egyptian man saved us from the shepherds. He even got water for us and gave water to the sheep."

20 He asked, "Where is he? Why did you leave him? Ask him to come eat with us."

21 Moses agreed to stay with the man. The man gave his daughter Zipporah to Moses to be his wife. 22 She had a baby boy. Moses named him Gershom. He said, "I have been a stranger in a new land."

God Hears His People

23 After a long time, the king of Egypt died. The people of Israel cried out because of their hard work. Their cry for help went up to God. 24 God heard their crying. God remembered His promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 25 God saw the people of Israel, and God knew.
