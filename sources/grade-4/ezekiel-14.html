People with Idols Ask God

1 Some leaders of Israel came to me and sat down in front of me.

2 Then the word of the LORD came to me. He said,

3 "Son of man, these men have brought their idols into their hearts. They have put their sin right in front of them. Should I let them ask Me anything?

4 "So speak to them. Tell them, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: When any man of Israel brings his idols into his heart and puts his sin and the thing that makes him fall right in front of him, and then he comes to a prophet, I, the LORD, will answer him. I will answer him because of his many idols.' 5 I will do this to grab the hearts of the people of Israel. They have turned away from Me because of their idols.

6 "So tell the people of Israel, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: Turn back! Turn away from your idols and from all your bad things.' 7 For any man of Israel, or any foreigner living in Israel, who turns away from Me and brings his idols into his heart and puts his sin and the thing that makes him fall right in front of him, and then comes to a prophet to ask Me something, I, the LORD, will answer him Myself.

8 "I will turn against that man. I will make him a warning sign to others. People will point at him and talk about him. I will cut him off from My people. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 9 If a prophet is tricked and says something, I, the LORD, have tricked that prophet. I will reach out My hand against him and destroy him from among My people Israel.

10 "They will carry their guilt. The person who asked will be guilty just like the prophet. 11 I will do this so the people of Israel will stop wandering away from Me. They will stop making themselves dirty with all their sins. Then they will be My people, and I will be their God, says the Lord the LORD."

God Will Punish a Sinful Land

12 The word of the LORD came to me again. He said,

13 "Son of man, if a land sins against Me by not being faithful, I will reach out My hand against it. I will break its food supply. I will send hunger into it. I will cut off people and animals from it.

14 "Even if these three men—Noah, Daniel, and Job—were in that land, they could save only themselves because they did right, says the Lord the LORD.

15 "Or if I send wild animals through the land, and they kill the children, and the land becomes empty so no one walks through it because of the animals, 16 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, even if those three men were in it, they could not save their sons or daughters. They would save only themselves, and the land would be empty.

17 "Or if I bring a sword against that land and say, 'Sword, go through the land,' and I cut off people and animals from it, 18 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, those three men could not save their sons or daughters. They would save only themselves.

19 "Or if I send sickness into that land and pour out My anger on it with blood, to cut off people and animals from it, 20 as surely as I live, says the Lord the LORD, even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, they could not save a son or a daughter. They would save only themselves because they did right.

21 "For this is what the Lord the LORD says: How much worse it will be when I send My four terrible punishments against Jerusalem—sword, hunger, wild animals, and sickness—to cut off people and animals from it!

22 "But look, some people will be left in it. Sons and daughters will be brought out to you. When you see how they live and what they do, you will feel better about the trouble I brought on Jerusalem. You will see that I brought all of it on the city.

23 "They will comfort you when you see how they live and what they do. Then you will know that I did not do all this for no reason, says the Lord the LORD."
