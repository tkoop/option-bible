The Flying Scroll

1 Then I looked up and saw a scroll flying through the air.

2 The angel asked me, "What do you see?"

I said, "I see a flying scroll. It is twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide."

3 Then he said, "This is the curse that is going out over the whole earth. On one side it says every thief will be taken away. On the other side it says everyone who lies when they promise to tell the truth will be taken away."

4 "I will send it out," says the LORD of Hosts, "and it will go into the house of the thief and the house of the one who lies when they promise to tell the truth using My name. It will stay in that house and destroy it, along with its wood and stones."

The Woman in the Basket

5 Then the angel who was talking with me came forward and said, "Lift up your eyes and see what is coming."

6 I asked, "What is it?"

He said, "It is a measuring basket." Then he said, "This is the wrong done all over the land."

7 Then a lead cover was lifted, and a woman was sitting inside the basket.

8 He said, "This is Evil." Then he pushed her down into the basket and put the lead cover over the opening.

9 Then I looked up and saw two women coming out. The wind was in their wings. They had wings like a stork's wings, and they lifted the basket up between heaven and earth.

10 I asked the angel who was talking with me, "Where are they taking the basket?"

11 He said, "To build a house for it in the land of Shinar. When it is ready, it will be set there on its base."
