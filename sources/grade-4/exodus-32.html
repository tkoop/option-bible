The People Make a Gold Calf

1 The people saw that Moses was taking a long time to come down from the mountain. So they gathered around Aaron and said, "Come, make us gods who will go ahead of us. As for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of Egypt, we don't know what has happened to him."

2 Aaron said to them, "Take off the gold earrings from your wives, your sons, and your daughters, and bring them to me."

3 All the people took off their gold earrings and brought them to Aaron.

4 He took the gold from their hands. He shaped it with a tool and made a gold calf. Then they said, "These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up out of Egypt."

5 When Aaron saw this, he built an altar in front of the calf. Aaron called out, "Tomorrow is a feast to the LORD."

6 The next day the people got up early. They offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings. Then the people sat down to eat and drink. They got up to have fun and dance.

God Talks to Moses

7 The LORD said to Moses, "Go down. Your people, whom you brought up out of Egypt, have done very wrong. 8 They have quickly turned away from what I told them to do. They have made a gold calf for themselves. They have bowed down to it and sacrificed to it. They said, 'These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up out of Egypt.'"

9 The LORD said to Moses, "I have seen these people. They are very stubborn. 10 Now leave Me alone. My anger will burn against them and destroy them. Then I will make you into a great nation."

11 Moses begged the LORD his God. He said, "The LORD, why are You so angry with Your people? You brought them out of Egypt with great power and strength. 12 Why should the Egyptians say, 'He brought them out to hurt them, to kill them in the mountains, and to wipe them off the earth'? Turn from Your fierce anger. Change Your mind and do not bring disaster on Your people. 13 Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Israel. You swore to them by Yourself. You said, 'I will make your children and grandchildren as many as the stars in the sky. I will give all this land to your children and grandchildren, and they will have it forever.'"

14 So the LORD changed His mind about the disaster He had said He would bring on His people.

Moses Comes Down the Mountain

15 Moses turned and went down the mountain. He had the two stone tablets with God's law in his hands. They were written on both sides, front and back. 16 The tablets were made by God. The writing was God's writing, cut into the stones.

17 Joshua heard the people shouting. He said to Moses, "There is a sound of war in the camp."

18 Moses said, "That is not the sound of winners shouting. It is not the sound of losers crying. I hear singing."

19 When Moses got close to the camp, he saw the calf and the dancing. Moses was very angry. He threw the tablets from his hands and broke them at the foot of the mountain.

20 He took the calf they had made. He burned it in the fire. He ground it into powder. He scattered it on the water. Then he made the Israelites drink it.

Moses Questions Aaron

21 Moses said to Aaron, "What did these people do to you, that you brought such a big sin on them?"

22 Aaron said, "Don't let my lord's anger burn. You know these people. They are set on doing wrong. 23 They said to me, 'Make us gods who will go ahead of us. As for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of Egypt, we don't know what has happened to him.' 24 So I said to them, 'Whoever has gold, take it off.' They gave it to me. I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf."

Moses Punishes the People

25 Moses saw that the people were out of control, because Aaron had let them get out of control. This brought shame on them among their enemies.

26 Moses stood at the gate of the camp and said, "Whoever is for the LORD, come to me." All the sons of Levi gathered around him. 27 He said to them, "This is what the LORD God of Israel says: 'Each man put his sword on his side. Go back and forth through the camp from gate to gate. Kill your brother, your friend, and your neighbor.'"

28 The sons of Levi did what Moses said. About three thousand men died that day.

29 Moses said, "Today you have given yourselves to the LORD. Each of you fought against his son and brother. So He will bless you today."

Moses Prays for the People

30 The next day Moses said to the people, "You have sinned a great sin. Now I will go up to the LORD. Maybe I can make up for your sin."

31 Moses went back to the LORD and said, "Oh, these people have sinned a great sin. They have made gods of gold for themselves. 32 But now, if You will forgive their sin, forgive them. If You won't, please erase me from Your book that You have written."

33 The LORD said to Moses, "Whoever has sinned against Me, I will erase from My book. 34 But now go. Lead the people to the place I told you. My angel will go ahead of you. But on the day I punish, I will punish them for their sin."

35 The LORD struck the people with a sickness because of what they did with the calf Aaron made.
