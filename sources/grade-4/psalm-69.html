David Asks God to Save Him

1 Save me, God! The water is up to my neck. 2 I sank in deep mud. I can't find a place to stand. I went into deep water, and the flood swept over me. 3 I am tired from calling out. My throat is dry. My eyes are tired as I wait for my God. 4 My enemies hate me for no reason. They are more than the hairs on my head. The people who want to destroy me are strong. My enemies tell lies. They make me pay for things I did not take. 5 God, You know my foolish ways. My sins are not hidden from You.

David Prays for God's People

6 Lord, the LORD of all armies, do not let the people who hope in You be ashamed because of me. God of Israel, do not let those who look for You be put to shame because of me. 7 I carry shame for You. Shame covers my face. 8 I am like a stranger to my brothers. I am like a foreigner to my mother's sons. 9 My love for Your house eats me up inside. The mean words of people who hate You fall on me. 10 I cried and did not eat. People made fun of me for it. 11 I wore rough sackcloth. People used me as a joke. 12 People at the city gate talk about me. Drunk people sing about me.

David Asks God to Rescue Him

13 But my prayer is to You, the LORD. At the right time, answer me with Your great love. God, answer me with Your true help. 14 Save me from the mud. Do not let me sink. Save me from my enemies and from deep water. 15 Do not let the flood sweep over me. Do not let the deep water swallow me. Do not let the well close its mouth over me.

16 Answer me, the LORD, because Your love is good. Turn to me because You are very kind. 17 Do not hide Your face from me, Your servant. Answer me fast, because I am in trouble. 18 Come close and save my life. Buy me back because of my enemies. 19 You know my shame and my disgrace. All my enemies are in front of You. 20 Shame broke my heart. I am sick. I looked for someone to feel sorry for me, but no one did. I looked for someone to comfort me, but I found none. 21 They gave me bitter food. They gave me vinegar to drink when I was thirsty.

David Prays About His Enemies

22 Let their table be a trap. Let their peace be a snare. 23 Let their eyes grow dark so they cannot see. Make their legs shake all the time. 24 Pour out Your anger on them. Let Your hot anger catch them. 25 Let their homes be empty. Let no one live in their tents. 26 They hurt the one You already hit. They talk about the pain of the ones You wounded. 27 Add their guilt to their guilt. Do not let them share in Your right way. 28 Wipe their names from the book of life. Do not write their names with good people. 29 But I am poor and in pain. God, lift me up with Your help.

David Praises God

30 I will praise God's name with a song. I will make Him great with thanks. 31 This will please the LORD more than a big bull with horns. 32 The humble will see and be glad. You who look for God, let your hearts live. 33 The LORD hears those in need. He does not turn away from His people in chains. 34 Let heaven and earth praise Him. Let the seas and all that moves in them praise Him. 35 God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah. People will live there and own it. 36 The children of His servants will get it. People who love His name will live there.
