A Message Isaiah Saw

1 This is the message Isaiah son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem.

Peace in the Days to Come

2 In the days to come, the mountain where the the LORD's house stands will be the most important mountain. It will stand higher than all the hills. People from all countries will come to it. 3 Many people will come and say, "Let's go up to the mountain of the the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways, so we can walk in His paths." For teaching will go out from Zion, and the word of the the LORD from Jerusalem.

4 Then He will judge between the countries. He will settle fights for many people. They will turn their swords into farm tools and their spears into garden knives. Countries will not fight each other anymore. They will not learn how to fight wars anymore.

Walk in the Light of the the LORD

5 Come, family of Jacob. Let's walk in the light of the the LORD.

Why the the LORD Left His People

6 For You have left Your people, the family of Jacob. They are full of ways from the east. They follow fortune-tellers like the Philistines. They make deals with people from other lands. 7 Their land is full of silver and gold. There is no end to their treasure. Their land is full of horses. There is no end to their war carts. 8 Their land is full of false gods. They bow down to things they made with their own hands. 9 So people are brought down low. Do not forgive them.

The Day of the the LORD

10 Go into the rocks. Hide in the dust. Get away from the fear of the the LORD and from His great glory. 11 The proud look on people's faces will be brought down. The proud ways of people will be made low. On that day, the the LORD alone will be lifted high.

12 The Day of the the LORD of Heaven's Armies will come against all proud and lifted-up people. Everything that is raised high will be brought low. 13 It will come against the tall cedar trees of Lebanon and against the oak trees of Bashan. 14 It will come against all the high mountains and all the raised hills. 15 It will come against every high tower and every strong wall. 16 It will come against every ship from Tarshish and every beautiful boat.

17 So the pride of people will be brought down. The proud ways of people will be made low. On that day, the the LORD alone will be lifted high. 18 The false gods will disappear completely.

19 People will run into caves in the rocks and holes in the ground. They will run away from the fear of the the LORD and from His great glory, when He stands up to shake the earth.

20 On that day, people will throw away their silver and gold idols. They made them to worship them. They will throw them to the moles and bats.

21 They will run into caves in the rocks and cracks in the cliffs. They will run away from the fear of the the LORD and from His great glory, when He stands up to shake the earth.

22 Stop trusting people. They only have a breath in their noses. They are worth nothing.
