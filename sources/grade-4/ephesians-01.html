Paul Writes to God’s People

1 I am Paul. God made me an apostle of Christ Jesus. I write to God’s holy people in Ephesus. You believe in Christ Jesus. 2 May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

God Chose Us in Christ

3 Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! He has blessed us with every good gift from heaven through Christ. 4 Before God made the world, He chose us in Christ. He chose us to be holy and without blame before Him. He did this because He loves us. 5 He planned ahead of time to make us His children through Jesus Christ. This was what He wanted to do. 6 So we praise His glorious grace. He gave us this grace freely in His loved Son.

7 Through Christ’s blood, we are set free. Our sins are forgiven. This is because God is so rich in grace. 8 He gave us so much grace, along with wisdom and understanding. 9 He let us know His secret plan. He was pleased to do this through Christ. 10 When the time is right, God will bring everything together under Christ. This includes things in heaven and things on earth.

11 In Christ, we were chosen. God planned this ahead of time. He works everything by His own plan. 12 We were the first to hope in Christ. We live to praise His glory. 13 You also heard the true message. It is the good news that saves you. You believed in Christ. Then God marked you with the Holy Spirit He promised. 14 The Holy Spirit is God’s promise that we will receive what He has saved for us. He will finish setting us free. This brings praise to His glory.

Paul Prays for the Church

15 I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus. I heard about your love for all God’s people. 16 So I never stop thanking God for you. I always remember you when I pray. 17 I pray that God, the Father of glory, will give you wisdom. May He show you more about Himself. He is the God of our Lord Jesus Christ.

18 I pray that your hearts will be filled with light. Then you will know the hope He calls you to. You will know how rich and glorious His gift is to His people. 19 I pray you will know His great power for us who believe. It is the same power that works in us.

Christ Rules Over All

20 God used this power in Christ. He raised Christ from the dead. He seated Him at His right hand in heaven. 21 Christ is above all rulers and leaders. He is above every name. This is true now and in the future. 22 God put everything under Christ’s feet. He made Him head of the church. 23 The church is His body. It is filled with Him who fills everything.
