Baasha Builds a Town to Stop Asa

1 In the thirty-sixth year of King Asa's rule, Baasha, king of Israel, went up against Judah. He built the town of Ramah. He did this so no one could go in or out to King Asa of Judah.

Asa Asks King Ben-Hadad for Help

2 King Asa took silver and gold from the LORD's house and from his own house. He sent it to Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, who lived in Damascus. He said, 3 "There is a deal between me and you, and between my father and your father. Look, I have sent you silver and gold. Go and break your deal with Baasha, king of Israel. Then he will leave me alone."

4 Ben-Hadad listened to King Asa. He sent his army leaders to the towns of Israel. They attacked Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the storage places in the towns of Naphtali.

5 When Baasha heard this, he stopped building Ramah. He ended his work.

6 King Asa took all of Judah. They carried the stones and wood from Ramah that Baasha had built. They used them to build Geba and Mizpah.

A Prophet Warns Asa

7 At that time, Hanani the seer came to King Asa of Judah. He said to him, "You trusted the king of Aram. You did not trust the LORD your God. So the army of the king of Aram got away from you.

8 "Were not the Cushites and Libyans a very big army with many chariots and horsemen? Yet when you trusted the LORD, He gave them into your hand.

9 "The LORD's eyes look all over the earth. He wants to help people whose hearts are fully His. You did a foolish thing. So from now on, you will have wars."

10 Asa got angry with the seer. He put him in jail, because he was very angry at him for this. At that time, Asa was mean to some of the people.

11 The other things Asa did, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

Asa Gets Sick and Dies

12 In the thirty-ninth year of his rule, Asa got sick in his feet. His sickness was very bad. But even then, he did not ask the LORD for help. He asked doctors instead.

13 In the forty-first year of his rule, Asa died. He was buried with his family.

14 They buried him in the tombs he had dug for himself in the city of David. They laid him on a bed full of spices and many kinds of perfume oil. Skilled workers made the perfume. They made a very big fire for him.
