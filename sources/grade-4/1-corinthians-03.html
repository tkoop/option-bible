Paul Talks to Baby Christians

1 Brothers, I could not talk to you like people who follow God's Spirit. I had to talk to you like people who follow their own wants, like babies in Christ. 2 I gave you milk to drink, not solid food. You were not ready for it yet. Even now, you are still not ready. 3 You are still living by your own wants. When you are jealous and argue with each other, you are acting like people who do not know God. You are living like everyone else.

4 When one of you says, "I follow Paul," and another says, "I follow Apollos," you are acting like everyone else.

God Makes Things Grow

5 What is Apollos? What is Paul? They are servants. Through them, you believed. The Lord gave each one his job. 6 I planted the seed. Apollos watered it. But God made it grow. 7 So the one who plants is not important. The one who waters is not important. God is the one who makes things grow. 8 The one who plants and the one who waters work together. Each one will get his own pay for his own work. 9 We work together for God. You are God's field. You are God's house.

Building on Jesus

10 God was kind to me. He gave me this work. I laid the foundation like a wise builder. Now someone else builds on it. Each person must be careful how he builds. 11 No one can lay any other foundation. Jesus Christ is the only foundation. 12 If someone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, pretty stones, wood, grass, or straw, 13 his work will be shown for what it is. The day will show it. It will be shown in fire. The fire will test what kind of work each person did. 14 If the work stays, the person will get a reward. 15 If the work burns up, the person will lose it. But the person will be saved. He will be saved like someone going through fire.

God's Temple

16 Don't you know that you are God's temple? God's Spirit lives in you. 17 If anyone ruins God's temple, God will ruin that person. God's temple is holy. You are that temple.

Do Not Be Fooled

18 Do not fool yourself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this world, he should become foolish. Then he can become wise. 19 The wisdom of this world is foolish to God. It is written, "God catches wise people in their own tricks."

20 And again, "The Lord knows that the thoughts of wise people are useless."

Do Not Brag

21 So do not brag about people. All things are yours. 22 Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, things now, and things to come—all are yours. 23 You belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.
