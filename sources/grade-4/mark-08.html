Jesus Feeds a Big Crowd

1 In those days, a big crowd came again. They had nothing to eat. Jesus called His disciples to Him.

2 Jesus felt sorry for them. He said, "They have been with Me for three days now. They have nothing to eat. 3 If I send them home hungry, they may get too weak on the way. Some of them came from far away."

4 His disciples answered, "Where can anyone find enough bread to feed all these people out here in this empty place?"

5 Jesus asked, "How many loaves of bread do you have?"

"Seven," they said.

6 Jesus told the crowd to sit down on the ground. He took the seven loaves. He thanked God for them. Then He broke them. He gave them to His disciples to pass out. 7 The disciples also had a few small fish. Jesus blessed them and told His disciples to pass them out too.

8 Everyone ate and was full. The disciples picked up seven baskets of leftover pieces.

9 About four thousand people were there. Then Jesus sent them home.

The Pharisees Ask for a Miracle

10 Right away, Jesus got into a boat with His disciples. They went to the area of Dalmanutha.

11 The Pharisees came out. They began to argue with Jesus. They tested Him. They wanted Him to show a miracle from heaven.

12 Jesus sighed deeply. He said, "Why does this group of people ask for a miracle? I tell you the truth. No miracle will be given to them."

13 Then Jesus left them. He got back into the boat. He went to the other side of the lake.

Watch Out for Bad Teaching

14 The disciples forgot to bring bread. They had only one loaf with them in the boat.

15 Jesus warned them. He said, "Be careful. Watch out for the bad teaching of the Pharisees and of Herod."

16 The disciples talked among themselves. They said, "We do not have any bread."

17 Jesus knew what they were saying. He said, "Why are you talking about having no bread? Do you still not understand? Are your hearts hard? 18 You have eyes. Can you not see? You have ears. Can you not hear? Do you not remember?"

19 Jesus said, "When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets of leftover pieces did you pick up?"

"Twelve," they said.

20 Jesus said, "And when I broke the seven loaves for the four thousand, how many baskets did you pick up?"

"Seven," they said.

21 Jesus said, "Do you still not understand?"

Jesus Heals a Blind Man

22 They came to Bethsaida. People brought a blind man to Jesus. They begged Him to touch the man.

23 Jesus took the blind man by the hand. He led him out of the town. He spit on the man's eyes. He put His hands on him. Then He asked, "Do you see anything?"

24 The man looked up. He said, "I see people. They look like trees walking around."

25 Then Jesus put His hands on the man's eyes again. The man looked hard. His sight came back. He could see everything clearly.

26 Jesus sent him home. He said, "Do not go into the town."

Peter Says Jesus Is the Christ

27 Jesus and His disciples went to the villages near Caesarea Philippi. On the way, He asked His disciples, "Who do people say I am?"

28 They answered, "Some say John the Baptist. Some say Elijah. Others say one of the prophets."

29 Jesus asked them, "Who do you say I am?"

Peter answered, "You are the Christ."

30 Jesus told them not to tell anyone about Him.

Jesus Talks About His Death

31 Then Jesus began to teach them. He said the Son of Man must suffer many things. The leaders, the top priests, and the teachers of the law would not accept Him. They would kill Him. But after three days, He would rise again.

32 He said this plainly. Peter took Jesus aside and began to scold Him.

33 Jesus turned around. He saw His disciples. He scolded Peter. He said, "Get away from Me, Satan! You are thinking like people, not like God."

What It Means to Follow Jesus

34 Jesus called the crowd and His disciples to Him. He said, "If anyone wants to follow Me, he must say no to himself. He must carry his cross. Then he must follow Me."

35 Jesus said, "Anyone who wants to save his life will lose it. But anyone who loses his life for Me and for the good news will save it."

36 Jesus said, "What good is it if a man gains the whole world but loses his soul?"

37 Jesus said, "What can a man give to get his soul back?"

38 Jesus said, "If anyone is ashamed of Me and My words in this sinful and unfaithful group, the Son of Man will be ashamed of him. This will happen when He comes with His Father's glory and with the holy angels."
