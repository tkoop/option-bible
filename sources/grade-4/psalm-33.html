Praise the LORD with Music

1 Sing with joy to the LORD, you good people. It is right for honest people to praise Him. 2 Praise the LORD with a harp. Sing to Him with a harp that has ten strings. 3 Sing a new song to Him. Play well and shout with joy.

The LORD's Words Are Right

4 The the LORD's words are right. He does all His work with care. 5 He loves what is right and fair. The earth is full of the LORD's kind love.

God Made the Heavens and Sea

6 The the LORD spoke, and the heavens were made. All the stars were made by the breath of His mouth. 7 He gathers the sea water into a big pile. He stores the deep water.

All People Should Fear the LORD

8 Let all the earth fear the the LORD. Let all the people in the world be amazed by Him. 9 He spoke, and it happened. He gave a command, and it stood firm.

The LORD Stops the Nations' Plans

10 The the LORD stops the plans of countries. He ruins the plans of people. 11 The the LORD's plan lasts forever. The plans in His heart last for all time.

Happy Is the Nation That Chooses God

12 Happy is the nation whose God is the the LORD. Happy are the people He chose to be His own.

The LORD Sees Everyone

13 The the LORD looks down from heaven. He sees all people. 14 From where He lives, He looks at all who live on earth. 15 He made their hearts. He sees all the things they do.

No Army Can Save a King

16 No king is saved by a big army. No strong fighter escapes by his great strength. 17 A horse cannot save anyone. Even with its great strength, it cannot save.

The LORD Watches Those Who Trust Him

18 The the LORD watches over those who fear Him. He watches those who trust His kind love. 19 He saves them from death. He keeps them alive when there is no food.

We Wait for the LORD

20 We wait for the the LORD. He is our help and our protector. 21 Our hearts are glad in Him, because we trust His special name. 22 May Your kind love rest on us, O the LORD, as we put our hope in You.
