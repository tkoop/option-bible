Jesus Is Greater Than Moses

1 So, holy brothers, you share in a call from heaven. Think about Jesus. He is the One God sent, and He is our High Priest. 2 He was faithful to God, who made Him. Moses was faithful too, in all of God's house.

3 Jesus has more honor than Moses. The one who builds a house has more honor than the house. 4 Every house is built by someone. But God made everything.

5 Moses was faithful like a servant in all of God's house. He told about things God would say later. 6 But Christ is faithful like a Son over God's house. We are His house if we keep our hope strong and do not give up.

A Warning from the Holy Spirit

7 So the Holy Spirit says, "Today, if you hear His voice, 8 do not make your hearts hard. Do not be like the people who turned against God in the desert. 9 There, your fathers tested Me. They saw My works for forty years. 10 So I was angry with that group of people. I said, 'They always go the wrong way in their hearts. They do not know My ways.' 11 I said with anger, 'They will not enter My rest.'"

Do Not Turn Away from God

12 Brothers, be careful. Do not let any of you have an evil heart that does not trust God. Do not turn away from the living God.

13 But help each other every day, while it is still called Today. Then none of you will get a hard heart because sin tricks you.

Hold On to Hope

14 We share in Christ if we keep our hope strong from the start to the end.

15 It says, "Today, if you hear His voice, do not make your hearts hard like the people who turned against God."

They Did Not Trust God

16 Who heard God and still turned against Him? Was it not all the people who left Egypt with Moses? 17 Who made God angry for forty years? Was it not the people who sinned? Their bodies fell in the desert. 18 Who did God say would not enter His rest? Was it not the people who did not obey? 19 So we see they could not go in because they did not trust God.
