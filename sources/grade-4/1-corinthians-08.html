Food Given to Idols

1 Now about food given to idols: We know that we all have knowledge. But knowledge makes people proud, while love helps people grow. 2 If anyone thinks he knows a lot, he still does not know as he should. 3 But if anyone loves God, God knows him.

Only One True God

4 So about eating food offered to idols: We know an idol is not really anything in the world. And there is only one God. 5 Even if people call many things gods in heaven or on earth, there are many so-called gods and lords. 6 But for us there is one God, the Father. All things come from Him, and we live for Him. And there is one Lord, Jesus Christ. All things came through Him, and we live through Him.

7 But not everyone knows this. Some people are still used to idols. When they eat this food, they think it was offered to an idol. Their weak sense of right and wrong is made dirty. 8 Food does not bring us to God. We are not worse if we do not eat. We are not better if we do eat.

Do Not Make Others Fall

9 Be careful so that your freedom does not make weak people fall into sin. 10 If a person with a weak sense of right and wrong sees you eating in a temple for idols, won't he feel okay to eat food offered to idols? 11 So the weak brother, for whom Christ died, is ruined by your knowledge.

12 By sinning against your brothers and hurting their weak sense of right and wrong, you sin against Christ. 13 So if my food makes my brother fall into sin, I will never eat meat again. I do not want to make him fall.
