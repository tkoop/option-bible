The LORD Is in Charge

1 The king’s heart is like a stream of water in the the LORD’s hand. He turns it where He wants. 2 A man may think his own way is right. But the the LORD looks at his heart.

Doing What Is Right

3 Doing what is right and fair pleases the the LORD more than a sacrifice. 4 Proud eyes and a proud heart are the lamp of bad people. They are sin.

Careful Plans and Hard Work

5 Careful plans bring plenty. But rushing leads to being poor. 6 Money made by lies is like a puff of smoke. It is a deadly trap. 7 The bad things bad people do sweep them away, because they refuse to do what is right.

Two Kinds of People

8 A guilty man’s way is crooked. But a pure man’s way is straight. 9 It is better to live on a corner of the roof than share a house with a fighting wife. 10 A bad man wants to do bad things. He shows no kindness to his neighbor.

Learning from Correction

11 When a mocker is punished, a simple person learns. When a wise person is taught, he gains knowledge. 12 A good man pays attention to the house of bad people. He brings bad people to ruin.

Helping the Poor

13 If a person shuts his ears to the poor when they cry, he will cry and not be answered. 14 A gift given in secret calms anger. A bribe in the pocket stops great anger. 15 Doing what is right brings joy to good people. It brings fear to those who do wrong.

The Path of Wisdom

16 A man who leaves the path of wisdom will rest among the dead. 17 A person who loves pleasure will become poor. A person who loves wine and fine oil will not get rich. 18 Bad people pay the price for good people. Traitors pay the price for those who do right.

Home and Family

19 It is better to live in the desert than with a fighting and angry wife. 20 A wise person’s house has good things and oil. But a foolish person eats them all up.

Seeking What Is Right

21 A person who seeks what is right and kind finds life, what is right, and honor. 22 A wise person goes up against a city of strong men. He brings down the strong place they trust. 23 A person who guards his mouth and tongue keeps himself from trouble.

Pride and Laziness

24 A mocker is a proud and rude person who acts with angry pride. 25 A lazy person’s wants kill him, because his hands will not work. 26 All day long he wants more. But a good person gives and does not hold back.

Worship and Truth

27 The LORD hates the sacrifice of bad people. It is even worse when they bring it with bad plans. 28 A false witness will die. But a person who listens will speak for a long time.

The End of the Chapter

29 A bad man makes his face hard. But a good man thinks about his ways. 30 No wisdom, no understanding, and no advice can stand against the the LORD. 31 A horse is made ready for the day of war. But winning comes from the the LORD.
