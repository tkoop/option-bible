Praise the LORD with a New Song

1Praise the LORD! Sing a new song to the LORD. Sing His praise with God's people. 2Let Israel be happy with its Maker. Let the people of Zion be glad with their King. 3Let them praise His name with dancing. Let them sing to Him with tambourines and harps.

The LORD Loves His People

4The LORD is happy with His people. He saves humble people. 5Let God's people be glad and proud. Let them sing for joy on their beds.

God's People Will Bring Justice

6Let God's people sing loud praises to God. Let them hold a sharp sword in their hands. 7They will punish the nations. They will punish all the peoples. 8They will tie up their kings with chains. They will bind their leaders with iron chains. 9They will do the punishment that was written down. This is an honor for all God's people. Praise the LORD!
