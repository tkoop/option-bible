God's City on Holy Mountains

1 This is a song by the sons of Korah. God's city rests on holy mountains. 2 The LORD loves the gates of Zion. He loves them more than all the homes of Jacob. 3 People say wonderful things about you, city of God. Selah

People from Every Nation

4 I will name Rahab and Babylon to those who know Me. Look! Philistia, Tyre, and Cush— "This one was born there."

5 People will say about Zion, "This one and that one were born in her." The Most High Himself will make her strong.

6 The LORD will write in the book of the nations, "This one was born there." Selah

All Joy Comes from God's City

7 Singers and dancers will say, "All my joy comes from you."
