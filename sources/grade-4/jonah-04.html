Jonah Gets Angry

1 Jonah was very angry. He was upset. 2 He prayed to the LORD: "The LORD, this is what I said before I left home. That is why I ran to Tarshish. I knew You are kind and caring. You are slow to get angry. You love people very much. You change Your mind and do not send trouble. 3 Now, the LORD, please take my life. It is better for me to die than to live."

4 The LORD said, "Do you have a good reason to be angry?"

Jonah Waits Outside the City

5 Jonah left the city and sat east of it. He made a small shelter and sat in its shade. He waited to see what would happen to the city.

6 The LORD God made a plant grow over Jonah. It shaded his head and helped him feel better. Jonah was very happy about the plant.

The Plant Dies

7 The next day at dawn, God sent a worm. The worm ate the plant, and the plant died.

8 When the sun came up, God sent a hot east wind. The sun beat down on Jonah's head. He felt very weak. He wanted to die. He said, "It is better for me to die than to live."

9 The LORD said, "Do you have a good reason to be angry about the plant?"

Jonah said, "I am angry enough to die."

The LORD Cares for Nineveh

10 The LORD said, "You cared about the plant. You did not work for it or grow it. It grew in one night and died in one night. 11 Should I not care about Nineveh, that big city? More than 120,000 people live there. They do not know right from left. There are many animals too."
