Eliphaz Speaks Again

1 Then Eliphaz, the man from Teman, answered and said:

Job's Words Are Empty

2 "Should a wise man answer with empty words and fill his belly with hot air? 3 Should he argue with words that help no one? 4 But you are pushing away the fear of God. You are stopping people from thinking about God. 5 Your sin teaches your mouth what to say. You choose the words of a sneaky person. 6 Your own mouth says you are wrong, not me. Your own lips speak against you.

Are You the First Man?

7 "Were you the first man ever born? Were you born before the hills? 8 Have you heard God's secret plans? Do you think you are the only wise one? 9 What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that we do not? 10 We have old and gray-haired men here. They are older than your father. 11 Are God's comfort words too small for you? Are His gentle words not enough for you? 12 Why does your heart pull you away? Why do your eyes flash with anger? 13 You turn your spirit against God. You bring these words from your mouth.

No One Can Be Pure

14 "What person can be pure? Who born of a woman can be right with God? 15 Look, God does not even trust His holy ones. The heavens are not pure to Him. 16 So how much less a person who is awful and dirty? He does wrong as easily as drinking water!

What Wise Men Say

17 "I will show you. Listen to me. I will tell what I have seen. 18 Wise men told this. They did not hide it from their fathers. 19 The land was given to them alone. No stranger lived among them.

What Happens to Wicked People

20 "Wicked people hurt all their days. The years meant for cruel people are full of pain. 21 Scary sounds fill his ears. Even when things go well, trouble comes to him. 22 He does not think he will get out of the dark. He is marked to die by the sword. 23 He walks around looking for bread and says, 'Where is it?' He knows a dark day is near. 24 Fear and pain scare him. They beat him down like a king ready for battle. 25 He raised his hand against God. He fights the Almighty. 26 He runs hard against Him with a strong shield. 27 He has let pride cover his face, and fat grow on his sides. 28 He lived in ruined towns, in houses no one should live in. They were ready to become piles of stones. 29 He will not stay rich. His money will not last. His things will not spread over the earth. 30 He will not get out of the dark. Fire will dry up his branches. God's breath will blow him away. 31 He should not trust in empty things. He fools himself. Empty things will be his pay. 32 It will be paid before his time. His branch will not stay green. 33 He will drop his unripe grapes like a vine. He will drop his flowers like an olive tree. 34 The group of people who do not care about God is empty. Fire burns the tents of those who take bribes. 35 They plan trouble and do wrong. They get ready to lie."
