A Prayer for Help

1 Save me from my enemies, my God. Keep me safe from people who stand against me. 2 Save me from people who do wrong. Save me from killers.

3 They hide and wait to hurt me. Mean men plan against me, but I did nothing wrong. I did not sin, the LORD. 4 They run to get ready, but I did nothing wrong. Wake up and help me. Look!

God Wakes Up to Judge

5 You are the LORD God of all armies. You are the God of Israel. Wake up and punish all people. Do not be kind to bad people who turn away from You. Selah

Enemies Growl Like Dogs

6 They come back in the evening. They growl like dogs. They walk around the city. 7 See what they say with their mouths. Their words cut like swords. They think no one hears them.

God Laughs at Them

8 But You, the LORD, laugh at them. You make fun of all people.

David Trusts God

9 You are my strength. I will watch for You, because God is my safe place. 10 My God is full of kind love. He will come to help me. God will let me see my enemies lose.

David Asks God to Stop His Enemies

11 Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Send them away with Your power, and make them fall, O Lord, our shield. 12 Their mouths sin, and their words are bad. Let them be caught in their pride. They speak curses and lies.

13 Be angry and wipe them out. Wipe them out, so everyone on earth will know that God rules over Jacob. Selah

14 They come back in the evening. They growl like dogs. They walk around the city. 15 They look for food. They growl if they are not full.

David Will Sing to God

16 But I will sing about Your strength. In the morning I will tell about Your kind love. You have been my safe place. You help me when I am in trouble. 17 You are my strength, so I will sing to You. God is my safe place. God is the one who loves me with kind love.
