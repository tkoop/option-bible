Moses and the Burning Bush

1 Moses was taking care of the sheep of Jethro, his wife’s dad. Jethro was a priest in Midian. Moses led the sheep to the far side of the desert. He came to Horeb, the mountain of God. 2 There the Angel of the LORD appeared to Moses in a fire that blazed from a bush. Moses looked and saw that the bush was on fire, but it was not burning up.

3 Moses said, “I will go see this great thing. Why does the bush not burn up?”

God Calls Moses

4 The LORD saw that Moses went to look. God called to him from the bush, “Moses! Moses!”

Moses said, “Here I am.”

5 God said, “Do not come closer. Take off your shoes. The place where you are standing is holy ground.”

6 God said, “I am the God of your dad. I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” Moses hid his face. He was afraid to look at God.

God Cares for His People

7 The LORD said, “I have seen how My people in Egypt are hurting. I have heard them cry because of the men who make them work hard. I know how they suffer.

8 “So I have come down to save them from the Egyptians. I will bring them to a good, big land. It is a land with milk and honey. It is the home of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

9 “And now the cry of the people of Israel has come to Me. I have seen how the Egyptians are being hard on them.

10 “So now, go. I am sending you to Pharaoh. Bring My people, the people of Israel, out of Egypt.”

Moses Asks God

11 But Moses said to God, “Who am I? Why should I go to Pharaoh? Why should I bring the people of Israel out of Egypt?”

12 God said, “I will be with you. This will be the sign for you that I sent you: When you bring the people out of Egypt, you will worship God on this mountain.”

13 Moses said to God, “What if I go to the people of Israel and say, ‘The God of your fathers sent me to you,’ and they ask, ‘What is His name?’ What should I tell them?”

God Tells His Name

14 God said to Moses, “I AM WHO I AM. This is what you are to say to the people of Israel: ‘I AM sent me to you.’”

15 God also said to Moses, “Say to the people of Israel, ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—has sent me to you.’ This is My name forever. This is how people will remember Me for all time.”

God’s Plan to Free His People

16 God said, “Go and gather the leaders of Israel. Say to them, ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—came to me. He said, “I have watched over you. I have seen what was done to you in Egypt.

17 “‘“And I have promised to bring you up out of your trouble in Egypt. I will bring you to the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—a land with milk and honey.”’

18 “The elders will listen to you. Then you and the elders will go to the king of Egypt. Say to him, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us. Let us go three days into the desert to offer sacrifices to the LORD our God.’

19 “But I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go unless someone forces him with a strong hand.

20 “So I will reach out My hand and strike Egypt with all the amazing things I will do there. After that, he will let you go.

21 “I will make the Egyptians kind to these people. So when you leave, you will not go empty-handed.

22 “Each woman will ask her neighbor and any woman living in her house for silver, gold, and clothes. You will put these things on your sons and your daughters. In this way, you will take things from the Egyptians.”
