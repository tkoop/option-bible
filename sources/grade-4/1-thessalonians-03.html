Paul Sends Timothy to Help

1 So, when we could not wait any longer, we decided to stay in Athens alone. 2 We sent Timothy, our brother and God's helper in sharing the good news about Christ. He came to make you strong and to cheer you up in your faith.

3 We did this so no one would be upset by these hard times. You know we are meant to have trouble. 4 When we were with you, we told you we would suffer. And it happened, just like you know.

5 So, when I could not wait any longer, I sent to learn about your faith. I did not want the devil to tempt you and make our work useless.

Good News from Timothy

6 But Timothy came to us from you. He brought good news about your faith and love. He said you always think of us kindly and want to see us, just like we want to see you.

7 So, brothers and sisters, in all our trouble and hard times, we were cheered up by your faith. 8 Now we feel alive again, if you stay strong in the Lord.

Paul Thanks God

9 How can we thank God enough for you? We are so happy about you before our God. 10 Night and day we pray hard that we can see you and help what is missing in your faith.

A Prayer for You

11 May God our Father and our Lord Jesus guide us to you. 12 May the Lord help you love each other more and more, and love everyone, just as we love you. 13 Then He can make your hearts strong and good, so you will be without blame in holy living before God our Father when our Lord Jesus comes with all His holy people. Amen.
