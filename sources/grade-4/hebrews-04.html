God's Rest Is Still Open

1 The promise to enter God's rest is still here. So we should be careful. None of you should miss it. 2 We heard the good news too, like those people long ago. But the message did not help them. They did not mix it with faith.

3 We who believe are going into that rest. God said, "I swore in My anger, 'They will never enter My rest.'" But God's work was finished when the world began.

4 In one place God said about the seventh day, "God rested on the seventh day from all His work." 5 And again He said, "They will never enter My rest."

6 So some people can still enter God's rest. The people who heard the good news first did not enter. They did not obey. 7 So God sets another day. He calls it "Today." Much later, He spoke through David. He said, "Today, if you hear His voice, do not be stubborn."

8 If Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken about another day. 9 So God's people still have a Sabbath rest.

10 Anyone who enters God's rest also rests from his own works. He rests just like God did from His works.

11 So let us try hard to enter that rest. Then no one will fall by following the same bad example of not obeying.

God's Word Sees Everything

12 God's word is alive and powerful. It is sharper than any sword. It cuts deep. It goes all the way to the soul and spirit. It cuts between joints and marrow. It judges what we think and want in our hearts.

13 Nothing God made is hidden from Him. Everything is open before His eyes. We must answer to Him.

We Have a Great High Priest

14 We have a great high priest. He went through the heavens. He is Jesus, the Son of God. So let us hold on to what we say we believe.

15 We do not have a high priest who cannot feel our weaknesses. He was tested in every way, just like us, but He did not sin.

16 So let us come to God's throne of grace with courage. Then we will receive mercy. We will find grace to help us when we need it.
