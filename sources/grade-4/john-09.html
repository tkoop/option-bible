Jesus Heals a Man Born Blind

1 As Jesus walked by, He saw a man who had been blind since birth. 2 His followers asked Him, "Teacher, who sinned? Did this man sin, or did his parents sin? Why was he born blind?"

3 Jesus answered, "This man did not sin, and his parents did not sin. He was born blind so that God's power could be seen in him. 4 We must do the work of the One who sent Me while it is still day. Night is coming, when no one can work. 5 While I am in the world, I am the light of the world."

6 After He said this, He spit on the ground. He made mud with the spit. Then He put the mud on the man's eyes. 7 He told him, "Go wash in the pool of Siloam." (Siloam means "Sent.") The man went and washed. He came back seeing.

People Ask the Man What Happened

8 The neighbors and people who had seen him beg before said, "Isn't this the man who used to sit and beg?"

9 Some said, "Yes, he is."

Others said, "No, but he looks like him."

The man said, "I am the one."

10 They asked him, "Then how were your eyes opened?"

11 He answered, "The man called Jesus made mud. He put it on my eyes. He told me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' So I went and washed. Then I could see."

12 They asked him, "Where is He?"

He said, "I don't know."

The Pharisees Question the Man

13 They brought the man who had been blind to the Pharisees. 14 It was a Sabbath day when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.

15 So the Pharisees asked him again how he could see. He said to them, "He put mud on my eyes. I washed. Now I see."

16 Some of the Pharisees said, "This man is not from God. He does not keep the Sabbath."

Others said, "How can a sinner do such signs?" They did not agree.

17 They asked the blind man again, "What do you say about Him? He opened your eyes."

He said, "He is a prophet."

The Man's Parents Are Asked

18 The Jews did not believe that he had been blind and now could see. They called his parents. 19 They asked them, "Is this your son? You say he was born blind. How does he see now?"

20 His parents answered, "We know this is our son. We know he was born blind. 21 But we do not know how he sees now. We do not know who opened his eyes. Ask him. He is old enough. He can speak for himself."

22 His parents said this because they were afraid of the Jews. The Jews had already agreed that anyone who said Jesus was the Christ would be thrown out of the place where Jews worship. 23 That is why his parents said, "He is old enough. Ask him."

The Man Speaks Up

24 So they called the man who had been blind a second time. They said to him, "Give glory to God. We know this man is a sinner."

25 He answered, "I don't know if He is a sinner. One thing I do know: I was blind, but now I see."

26 Then they said to him, "What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?"

27 He answered, "I already told you. You did not listen. Why do you want to hear it again? Do you want to be His disciples too?"

28 They yelled at him and said, "You are His disciple. We are disciples of Moses. 29 We know God spoke to Moses. But we do not know where this man comes from."

30 The man answered, "That is amazing! You don't know where He comes from, but He opened my eyes. 31 We know God does not listen to sinners. But God listens to anyone who worships Him and does what He wants. 32 No one has ever heard of anyone opening the eyes of a person born blind. 33 If this man were not from God, He could not do anything."

34 They answered him, "You were born in sin! Are you trying to teach us?" Then they threw him out.

Jesus Finds the Man

35 Jesus heard that they had thrown him out. He found him and said, "Do you believe in the Son of Man?"

36 He answered, "Who is He, Lord? Tell me so I can believe in Him."

37 Jesus said to him, "You have seen Him. He is the one talking with you."

38 He said, "Lord, I believe." And he worshiped Him.

39 Jesus said, "I came into this world to judge. I came so that people who do not see may see, and people who see may become blind."

40 Some Pharisees who were with Him heard this. They said to Him, "Are we blind too?"

41 Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would not have sin. But now you say, 'We see.' So your sin stays."
