Jesus Heals a Man with a Bad Hand

1 Another time Jesus went into the meeting house. A man there had a hand that was all dried up. 2 Some people watched Jesus. They wanted to find a reason to say He did wrong. They wanted to see if He would heal the man on the Sabbath. 3 Jesus said to the man with the bad hand, “Stand up in front of everyone.” 4 Then Jesus asked them, “Is it right on the Sabbath to do good or to do bad? Is it right to save a life or to kill?” But they did not say a word. 5 Jesus looked around at them. He was angry. He was sad because their hearts were hard. He said to the man, “Hold out your hand.” The man held it out. His hand was made well again. 6 The Pharisees left right away. They made a plan with the Herodians to kill Jesus.

Crowds Follow Jesus

7 Jesus went away with His followers to the lake. A big crowd from Galilee followed Him. 8 Many people came to Him from Judea, Jerusalem, Idumea, and the lands across the Jordan River. People also came from around Tyre and Sidon. They came because they heard what He was doing. 9 Jesus told His followers to have a small boat ready for Him. He did not want the crowd to push Him. 10 He had healed many people. So sick people pushed in to touch Him. 11 When bad spirits saw Jesus, they fell down in front of Him. They cried out, “You are the Son of God!” 12 But Jesus told them again and again not to tell who He was.

Jesus Chooses Twelve Helpers

13 Jesus went up on a hill. He called the people He wanted. They came to Him. 14 He chose twelve men to be with Him. He would send them out to tell the good news. 15 He gave them power to send bad spirits away. 16 These were the twelve: Simon, whom He named Peter; 17 James, the son of Zebedee, and his brother John, whom He named Boanerges, which means “sons of thunder”; 18 Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot; 19 and Judas Iscariot, who later turned Jesus over to His enemies.

Jesus Is Accused of Evil

20 Jesus went into a house. Again a crowd came. It was so big that Jesus and His followers could not even eat. 21 When His family heard this, they came to take Him home. They said, “He has lost His mind.” 22 Teachers of the law came down from Jerusalem. They said, “He has Beelzebul! He sends out bad spirits by the power of the ruler of bad spirits.” 23 Jesus called them to Him. He spoke to them in stories. He said, “How can Satan send out Satan? 24 If a kingdom fights against itself, that kingdom cannot last. 25 If a family fights against itself, that family cannot last. 26 If Satan fights against himself, he cannot last. His end has come. 27 No one can go into a strong man’s house and take his things unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can take what he wants.” 28 “Truly I tell you, people will be forgiven for all their sins and for every bad thing they say. 29 But anyone who says bad things about the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. They are guilty of a sin that lasts forever.” 30 Jesus said this because they were saying, “He has a bad spirit.”

Jesus’ True Family

31 Jesus’ mother and brothers came. They stood outside and sent someone to call Him. 32 A crowd was sitting around Jesus. They told Him, “Look, Your mother and brothers are outside. They are looking for You.” 33 “Who are My mother and My brothers?” Jesus asked. 34 Then He looked at the people sitting around Him. He said, “Here are My mother and My brothers! 35 Anyone who does what God wants is My brother, My sister, and My mother.”
