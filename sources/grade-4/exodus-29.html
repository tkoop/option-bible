How to Make the Priests Ready

1 This is what you must do to make Aaron and his sons ready to serve Me as priests. Take one young bull and two rams that have no flaws. 2 Make bread with no yeast. Make flat cakes mixed with oil. Make thin wafers with oil on them. Use fine wheat flour. 3 Put all these in one basket. Bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams. 4 Bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the meeting tent. Wash them with water.

5 Take the clothes and put them on Aaron. Put on the long shirt, the robe, the special vest, and the chest piece. Tie the vest on him with its belt. 6 Put the head cloth on his head. Put the holy crown on the head cloth. 7 Take the oil used to make a priest. Pour it on his head to set him apart. 8 Bring his sons and put shirts on them. 9 Tie belts around Aaron and his sons. Put hats on them. They will be priests forever. This is the rule you must follow to make Aaron and his sons priests.

The Bull That Pays for Sin

10 Bring the bull to the front of the meeting tent. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head. 11 Kill the bull in front of the LORD at the door of the meeting tent. 12 Take some of the bull's blood. Put it on the horns of the altar with your finger. Pour the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar. 13 Take all the fat that covers the inside parts. Take the best part of the liver and the two kidneys with their fat. Burn them on the altar. 14 Burn the bull's meat, its skin, and its waste outside the camp. This is an offering to pay for sin.

The First Ram

15 Take one of the rams. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head. 16 Kill the ram. Splash its blood on all sides of the altar. 17 Cut the ram into pieces. Wash its inside parts and its legs. Put them with its head and the other pieces. 18 Then burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD. It smells good to Him. It is a fire offering to the LORD.

The Second Ram

19 Take the second ram. Aaron and his sons must put their hands on its head. 20 Kill it. Take some of its blood and put it on the right ear of Aaron and his sons. Put it on the thumb of their right hand and the big toe of their right foot. Then splash the rest of the blood on all sides of the altar. 21 Take some blood from the altar and some oil used to make a priest. Sprinkle them on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes. Then he and his clothes will be made holy, and his sons and their clothes too.

22 Take the fat from the ram. Take the fat tail, the fat that covers the inside parts, the best part of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh. This ram is for making priests. 23 Take one loaf of bread, one cake made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of bread with no yeast that is before the LORD. 24 Put all these in the hands of Aaron and his sons. Wave them in front of the LORD. This is a wave offering. 25 Then take them from their hands and burn them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. This smells good to the LORD. It is a fire offering to the LORD.

26 Take the chest of the ram used to make priests. Wave it in front of the LORD. It will be your share. 27 Make holy for Aaron and his sons the chest that was waved and the thigh that was lifted from the ram used to make priests. 28 This will belong to Aaron and his sons forever. It is the part the people of Israel give to the LORD from their peace offerings. 29 Aaron's holy clothes will belong to his sons after him. They will wear them when they are made priests. 30 The son who becomes priest after him must wear them for seven days when he goes into the meeting tent to serve in the holy place.

Food for the Priests

31 You must take the ram used to make priests and boil its meat in a holy place. 32 At the door of the meeting tent, Aaron and his sons must eat the meat of the ram and the bread in the basket. 33 They must eat the food that was used to pay for their wrongs and make them priests. No one else may eat it, because it is holy. 34 If any meat or bread is left until morning, you must burn it up. It must not be eaten, because it is holy. 35 Do for Aaron and his sons everything I have told you. Make them priests for seven days.

36 Each day, kill a bull as an offering to pay for sin. Clean the altar by paying for its wrongs. Pour oil on it to make it holy. 37 For seven days, pay for the altar's wrongs and make it holy. Then the altar will be most holy. Anything that touches it will be holy.

Offerings Every Day

38 This is what you must offer on the altar all the time: two lambs one year old, each day, forever. 39 Offer one lamb in the morning and the other in the evening. 40 With the first lamb, offer a small amount of fine flour mixed with oil, and a small cup of wine. 41 Offer the second lamb the same way in the evening. Offer the same flour and drink offering. This smells good to the LORD. It is a fire offering.

42 For all time to come, offer this burnt offering at the door of the meeting tent in front of the LORD. There I will meet with you and speak with you. 43 There I will meet with the people of Israel. That place will be made holy by My glory. 44 So I will make the meeting tent and the altar holy. I will make Aaron and his sons holy so they can serve Me as priests. 45 Then I will live among the people of Israel. I will be their God. 46 They will know that I am the LORD their God. I brought them out of Egypt so I could live among them. I am the LORD their God.
