Moses Speaks to the People

1 Moses called all the people of Israel together. He said, "These are the things the LORD told us to do. 2 You may work for six days. But the seventh day must be holy. It is a day of rest for the LORD. Anyone who works on that day must die. 3 Do not light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day."

Gifts for the Tent of Meeting

4 Moses said to all the people, "This is what the LORD commanded. 5 Take an offering for the LORD from among you. Everyone who wants to give may bring the LORD's offering. Bring gold, silver, and bronze. 6 Bring blue, purple, and red yarn, fine linen, and goat hair. 7 Bring red ram skins, other animal skins, and acacia wood. 8 Bring oil for the light, spices for the special oil, and sweet spices for the incense. 9 Bring onyx stones and other stones to set in the special vest and the chest piece."

10 "Every skilled person among you must come and make all that the LORD commanded. 11 Make the tent, its cover, its hooks, its frames, its bars, its posts, and its bases. 12 Make the ark, its poles, its cover, and the curtain that hides it. 13 Make the table, its poles, all its tools, and the special bread. 14 Make the lamp holder, its tools, its lamps, and the oil for light. 15 Make the incense altar, its poles, the special oil, the sweet spices, and the curtain for the tent door. 16 Make the altar for burnt offerings, its bronze grate, its poles, all its tools, the wash bowl, and its stand. 17 Make the curtains for the yard, its posts, its bases, and the curtain for the yard gate. 18 Make the tent pegs, the yard pegs, and their ropes. 19 Make the special clothes for serving in the holy place. Make the holy clothes for Aaron the priest and for his sons to serve as priests."

The People Bring Gifts

20 Then all the people of Israel left Moses. 21 Everyone whose heart moved them came and brought a gift to the LORD. They brought things for the tent of meeting, for all its work, and for the holy clothes. 22 Men and women with willing hearts came. They brought pins, earrings, rings, and bracelets. They brought all kinds of gold. Each man brought gold to the LORD. 23 Everyone who had blue, purple, or red yarn, fine linen, goat hair, red ram skins, or other animal skins brought them. 24 Everyone who gave silver or bronze brought it to the LORD. Everyone who had acacia wood for the work brought it. 25 All the women who knew how to spin made yarn with their hands. They brought blue, purple, red, and fine linen yarn. 26 All the women whose hearts moved them in wisdom spun goat hair. 27 The leaders brought onyx stones and setting stones for the special vest and chest piece. 28 They also brought spices and oil for the light, for the special oil, and for the sweet incense. 29 The men and women of Israel whose hearts moved them brought a free gift to the LORD for all the work the LORD commanded through Moses.

Bezalel and Oholiab

30 Moses said to the people of Israel, "See, the LORD has called Bezalel by name. He is the son of Uri and the grandson of Hur, from the family of Judah. 31 The LORD filled him with the Spirit of God. He gave him wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and skill in all kinds of work. 32 He can make designs and work with gold, silver, and bronze. 33 He can cut stones to set and carve wood. He can make all kinds of beautiful work. 34 The LORD put it in his heart to teach. He will teach Bezalel and Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, from the family of Dan. 35 The LORD filled them with skill to do all kinds of work. They can cut, design, and sew with blue, purple, red, and fine linen. They can weave. They can make all kinds of work and designs."
