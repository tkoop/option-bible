The Holy Spirit Comes

1 On the day called Pentecost, they were all together in one place. 2 Suddenly a sound like a strong wind came from heaven. It filled the whole house where they were sitting. 3 They saw what looked like flames of fire. The flames separated and rested on each of them. 4 They were all filled with the Holy Spirit. They began to speak in other languages, as the Spirit helped them.

People from Many Lands Hear God's Works

5 Jews who worshiped God were living in Jerusalem. They came from every nation under heaven. 6 When this sound happened, a large crowd came together. The people were confused because each person heard them speaking in their own language.

7 They were amazed. They asked, "Aren't all these speakers from Galilee? 8 Then how is it that each of us hears them in our own language? 9 We are Parthians, Medes, and Elamites. Some of us live in Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia. 10 Some live in Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya near Cyrene. Some of us are visitors from Rome. 11 We are Jews and people who turned to the Jewish faith. We are Cretans and Arabs. We hear them telling the great things God has done, and they are using our own languages!"

12 They were all amazed and confused. They asked each other, "What does this mean?"

13 But others made fun of them. They said, "They have had too much new wine!"

Peter Speaks to the Crowd

14 Then Peter stood up with the eleven apostles. He raised his voice and spoke to them: "Men of Judea and all who live in Jerusalem, let this be known to you. Listen to my words. 15 These men are not drunk, as you think. It is only nine in the morning! 16 No, this is what the prophet Joel said:

"17 'In the last days, God says, I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will speak God's message. Your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams. 18 In those days I will pour out My Spirit on My servants, both men and women, and they will speak God's message. 19 I will show wonders in the sky above and signs on the earth below: blood, fire, and smoke. 20 The sun will turn dark, and the moon will turn to blood, before the great and wonderful day of the Lord comes. 21 And everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'

"22 People of Israel, listen to this: Jesus of Nazareth was a man God sent to you. God showed who He was by miracles, wonders, and signs. God did these things among you through Him, as you yourselves know. 23 God planned this and knew it ahead of time. Yet you handed Him over, and sinful men killed Him by nailing Him to a cross. 24 But God raised Him from the dead. He set Him free from the pain of death, because death could not hold Him.

"25 David said about Him: 'I saw the Lord always before me. Because He is at my right hand, I will not be shaken. 26 So my heart is glad, and my tongue rejoices. My body will also rest in hope. 27 You will not leave me in the grave. You will not let Your Holy One rot. 28 You have shown me the paths of life. You will fill me with joy in Your presence.'

"29 Brothers, I can tell you plainly that David died and was buried. His tomb is still here with us today. 30 He was a prophet. He knew God had promised him that a child from his family would sit on his throne. 31 He saw what was coming. He spoke about the Christ being raised from the dead. He said the Christ would not be left in the grave, and His body would not rot. 32 God raised this Jesus to life. We are all witnesses of this. 33 God lifted Him up to His right hand. He received the promised Holy Spirit from the Father. He has poured out what you now see and hear. 34 David did not go up to heaven. But he said, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "35 Sit at My right hand until I put Your enemies under Your control."' 36 So let all the people of Israel know for sure: God made this Jesus, whom you killed on a cross, both Lord and Christ."

The People Ask What to Do

37 When they heard this, they were deeply moved. They asked Peter and the other apostles, "Brothers, what should we do?"

Peter Tells Them What to Do

38 Peter said to them, "Turn from your sins and be baptized, each of you, in the name of Jesus Christ. Then your sins will be forgiven, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. 39 This promise is for you and your children. It is also for all who are far away, for everyone the Lord our God calls to Himself." 40 With many other words he warned them and begged them, "Save yourselves from these sinful people."

The Believers Share Life Together

41 Those who accepted his message were baptized. About three thousand people joined them that day. 42 They stayed faithful to the apostles' teaching. They spent time together, broke bread, and prayed. 43 Everyone felt awe. The apostles did many wonders and signs. 44 All the believers were together and shared everything they had. 45 They sold their land and belongings. They gave the money to anyone who needed it. 46 Every day they met together in the temple. They broke bread in their homes and ate with glad and honest hearts. 47 They praised God and were liked by all the people. The Lord added to their group every day those who were being saved.
