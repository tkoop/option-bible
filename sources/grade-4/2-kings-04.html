A Widow's Oil

1 A woman whose husband was one of the young prophets cried out to Elisha.

She said, "My husband, your servant, is dead. You know he feared the LORD. Now the man we owe money to has come to take my two sons as slaves."

2 Elisha said to her, "What can I do for you? Tell me what you have in your house."

She said, "I have nothing in my house but a little oil."

3 He said, "Go ask all your neighbors for empty jars. Don't ask for just a few."

4 He said, "Then go inside and shut the door behind you and your sons. Pour the oil into all those jars. When one jar is full, put it aside."

5 She left him and shut the door behind her and her sons. Her sons brought jars to her, and she poured the oil.

6 When the jars were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another jar."

He said, "There are no more jars."

Then the oil stopped.

7 She went to the man of God and told him.

He said, "Go sell the oil. Pay what you owe. Then you and your sons can live on what is left."

The Woman from Shunem

8 One day Elisha went to Shunem. An important woman lived there. She asked him to eat with her. So whenever he passed by, he stopped there to eat.

9 She said to her husband, "I know he is a holy man of God. He passes by us all the time."

10 She said, "Let's make a small room on the roof. Let's put a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp there for him. Then when he comes, he can stay there."

11 One day he came and went up to the room and lay down.

12 He said to his servant Gehazi, "Call the woman from Shunem." He called her, and she stood before him.

13 Elisha said, "Tell her, 'You have taken good care of us. What can we do for you? Should we speak to the king or the army leader for you?'"

She answered, "I live among my own people."

14 Elisha asked, "What can we do for her?"

Gehazi said, "She has no son, and her husband is old."

15 He said, "Call her." He called her, and she stood in the doorway.

16 He said, "At this time next year, you will hold a son in your arms."

She said, "No, my lord, man of God, please don't lie to me, your servant."

17 The woman became pregnant. At that time the next year, she had a son, just as Elisha had said.

The Boy Gets Sick

18 The boy grew up. One day he went out to his father, who was with the workers in the field.

19 The boy said to his father, "My head! My head!"

His father said to a servant, "Carry him to his mother."

20 The servant carried him to his mother. He sat on her lap until noon, then he died.

21 She went up and laid him on the man of God's bed. She shut the door and went out.

22 She called her husband and said, "Send me one servant and one donkey. I want to go to the man of God and come back."

23 He said, "Why go today? It is not a new moon or Sabbath."

She said, "It's all right."

24 She saddled the donkey. She told her servant, "Go fast. Don't slow down for me unless I tell you."

25 She went to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When he saw her far off, he said to Gehazi, "Look, the woman from Shunem is coming."

26 He said, "Run to meet her. Ask her, 'Are you well? Is your husband well? Is the child well?'"

She said, "We are well."

27 When she reached the man of God on the hill, she grabbed his feet. Gehazi came to push her away. But the man of God said, "Leave her alone. She is very sad. The LORD has kept this from me. He did not tell me."

28 She said, "Did I ask you for a son, my lord? Didn't I say, 'Don't trick me'?"

29 He said to Gehazi, "Tuck in your clothes, take my staff, and go. Don't stop to greet anyone. If someone greets you, don't answer. Put my staff on the boy's face."

30 The boy's mother said, "As surely as the LORD lives and you live, I will not leave you." So he got up and went with her.

31 Gehazi went ahead and put the staff on the boy's face. But there was no sound and no sign of life. He went back to meet Elisha and said, "The boy has not woken up."

32 Elisha went into the house. The boy was dead and lying on the bed.

33 He went in and shut the door behind them both. Then he prayed to the LORD.

34 He went up and lay on the child. He put his mouth on the boy's mouth, his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands. The boy's body became warm.

35 Then he got up and walked back and forth in the house once. Then he went up again and stretched himself over the boy. The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

36 He called Gehazi and said, "Call the woman from Shunem." He called her, and she came. Elisha said, "Take your son."

37 She came in and fell at his feet. She bowed to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.

The Pot of Stew

38 Elisha went back to Gilgal. There was a famine in the land. The young prophets sat before him. He told his servant, "Put on the big pot and make stew for the young prophets."

39 One man went out to the field to gather herbs. He found a wild vine and picked wild gourds. He filled his coat with them. He came back and cut them into the pot of stew. They did not know what they were.

40 They served the stew to the men. As they ate, they cried out, "The stew is poisoned, man of God!" They could not eat it.

41 He said, "Bring flour." He threw it into the pot. He said, "Serve it to the people so they can eat." There was nothing wrong with it.

Bread for Many

42 A man from Baal-shalishah brought bread. He had twenty loaves of barley bread and fresh grain in his sack. Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat."

43 His servant said, "How can I feed a hundred men with this?"

Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat. The LORD says they will eat and have some left."

44 He set it before them. They ate and had some left, just as the LORD had said.
