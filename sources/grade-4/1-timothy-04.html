Some People Will Leave the Faith

1 The Spirit says clearly that in later times some people will leave the faith. They will listen to lying spirits and demon teachings. 2 These teachers act like they are good, but their own hearts are hard. 3 They tell people not to marry. They tell people not to eat some foods. But God made those foods for people to eat with thanks. God made them for people who believe and know the truth.

4 Everything God made is good. Nothing should be thrown away if it is taken with thanks. 5 It is made clean by God's word and by prayer.

Be a Good Helper of Jesus

6 If you tell the brothers these things, you will be a good helper of Christ Jesus. You grow strong from the words of faith and good teaching that you have followed.

Train Yourself to Follow God

7 Stay away from bad, silly stories. Train yourself to follow God. 8 Body training helps a little. But following God helps in every way. It has a promise for this life and the life to come.

Hope in the Living God

9 This is a true word. It should be accepted by all. 10 We work hard for this. We hope in the living God. He is the Savior of all people. He is the Savior most of all for people who believe.

Teach and Be an Example

11 Tell people these things. Teach them. 12 Do not let anyone look down on you because of your youth. Be an example to believers in what you say, how you live, in love, in faith, and in clean living.

13 Until I come, give yourself to reading the Bible, to encouraging people, and to teaching. 14 Do not forget the gift given to you. It was given to you through prophecy when the leaders put their hands on you.

15 Think about these things. Live in them. Then everyone will see your progress. 16 Watch yourself and your teaching. Keep doing these things. If you do, you will save yourself and the people who hear you.
