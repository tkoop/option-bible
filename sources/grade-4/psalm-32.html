A Song About God's Forgiveness

1 Happy is the person whose wrongs are forgiven. Happy is the person whose sins are covered. 2 Happy is the person whose wrongdoing the the LORD does not count against him, and who has no lies in his heart.

3 When I kept quiet, my bones felt weak. I groaned all day. 4 Day and night, Your hand was heavy on me. My strength dried up like it does in summer heat.

5 Then I told You about my sin. I did not hide my wrongdoing. I said, "I will confess my wrongs to the the LORD." And You took away my guilt.

God Is My Safe Place

6 So let every faithful person pray to You while You can be found. When big floods come, they will not reach him. 7 You are my safe place. You keep me safe from trouble. You surround me with songs of rescue.

God Teaches Us

8 "I will teach you and show you the way to go. I will give you advice, and I will watch over you. 9 Do not be like a horse or a mule that has no sense. They must be led with a bit and bridle, or they will not come close to you."

Joy for God's People

10 Many troubles come to people who do wrong, but faithful love surrounds the person who trusts in the the LORD. 11 Be glad in the the LORD and rejoice, you who do right. Shout for joy, all you with honest hearts.
