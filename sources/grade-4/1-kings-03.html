Solomon Marries the King of Egypt's Daughter

1 Solomon married the daughter of Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He brought her to the City of David. She stayed there until he finished building his house, the house of the the LORD, and the wall around Jerusalem.

2 The people still burned offerings on the high hills. They did that because no house had been built for the the LORD yet.

3 Solomon loved the the LORD. He followed the rules of his father David. But he still burned offerings and incense on the high hills.

Solomon Asks God for Wisdom

4 The king went to Gibeon to give offerings there. It was the most important high place. Solomon gave one thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

5 That night at Gibeon, the the LORD came to Solomon in a dream. God said, "Ask for anything. I will give it to you."

6 Solomon said, "You showed great kindness to Your servant, my father David. He walked with You in truth and did what was right. His heart was right with You. You kept this great kindness for him. You gave him a son to sit on his throne, just like today. 7 And now, the LORD my God, You have made Your servant king in place of my father David. But I am only a little child. I do not know how to lead. 8 Your servant is among Your people, whom You chose. They are a great people. No one can count them all. 9 So give Your servant a heart that listens. Then he can judge Your people. Help him know right from wrong. Who can judge this great people of Yours?"

10 The Lord was pleased that Solomon asked for this.

11 God said to him, "Because you asked for this, and did not ask for a long life, or riches, or the death of your enemies, but asked for wisdom to make fair choices, I will give it to you. 12 I will do what you asked. I give you a wise and understanding heart. No one before you or after you will be like you. 13 I also give you what you did not ask for: riches and honor. No king will be like you all your life. 14 If you walk in My ways and keep My rules and commands, like your father David did, I will give you a long life."

15 Solomon woke up. He saw it was a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the the LORD. He gave burnt offerings and peace offerings. He also made a feast for all his servants.

Two Women Bring a Problem to the King

16 Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king. They stood before him.

17 One woman said, "Please, sir, this woman and I live in the same house. I had a baby while she was there. 18 Three days after my baby was born, she had a baby too. We were alone in the house. No one else was there, just the two of us. 19 During the night, this woman's baby died because she lay on it. 20 She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while I was sleeping. She put him in her arms and put her dead baby in my arms. 21 When I got up in the morning to feed my baby, he was dead. But when I looked closely, I saw he was not my baby."

22 The other woman said, "No! The living baby is mine, and the dead one is yours."

But the first woman said, "No! The dead baby is yours, and the living one is mine." They argued in front of the king.

23 The king said, "One woman says her baby is alive and the other woman's baby is dead. The other woman says the opposite."

24 The king said, "Bring me a sword." So they brought a sword to him.

25 The king said, "Cut the living baby in two. Give half to one woman and half to the other."

26 The woman whose baby was alive loved her son very much. She said to the king, "Please, sir, give her the living baby. Do not kill him."

But the other woman said, "He will not be mine or yours. Cut him in half."

27 The king answered, "Give the living baby to the first woman. Do not kill him. She is his mother."

28 All Israel heard about the king's decision. They respected the king, because they saw God's wisdom in him to make fair choices.
