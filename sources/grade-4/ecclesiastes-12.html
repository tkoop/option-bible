Remember Your Creator

1 Remember your Creator while you are young. Do it before bad days come. Do it before years come when you will say, “I do not enjoy them.” 2 Do it before the sun, the light, the moon, and the stars grow dark. Do it before clouds come back after rain.

3 When you are old, your hands may shake. Your strong legs may bend. You may stop chewing well, because there are few teeth. Your eyes may see dimly. 4 Your lips may close. You may not chew loudly. You may wake when a bird sings. Your voice may grow weak. 5 You may be afraid of high places. You may be scared on the road. Your hair may turn white. You may move slowly. Your desire may fade. Then you go to your forever home. People mourn in the streets.

6 Do this before your life ends. It is like a silver cord snapping. It is like a gold bowl breaking. It is like a jar breaking at a spring. It is like a wheel breaking at a well. 7 The dust goes back to the earth. The spirit goes back to God, who gave it.

8 The Teacher says, “Everything is like a breath. It all passes away.”

9 The Teacher was wise. He also taught people. He thought about things. He studied them. He put many wise sayings in order. 10 The Teacher tried to find helpful words. He wrote true and right words.

11 Wise words are like sharp sticks. They push you to do right. Collected sayings are like strong nails. One Shepherd gives them.

12 My son, be careful about more than this. People make many books. There is no end to them. Too much study makes the body tired.

The Main Point

13 Here is the end of it all. We have heard everything. Fear God. Keep His rules. This is what people should do. 14 God will bring every act into court. He will judge even secret things. He will judge good and bad.
