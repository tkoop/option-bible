Enemies Try to Trick Nehemiah

1 Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and our other enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall. There were no gaps left in it. But I had not put doors in the gates yet. 2 Sanballat and Geshem sent me a message. They said, "Come, let us meet in one of the villages on the plain of Ono." But they wanted to hurt me.

3 I sent messengers back to them. I said, "I am doing a big job. I cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and go down to you?" 4 Four times they sent me the same message. Each time I gave them the same answer.

5 The fifth time, Sanballat sent his helper to me. The helper had an open letter in his hand.

6 The letter said, "People everywhere are talking. Geshem says it is true. You and the Jews are planning to turn against the king. That is why you are building the wall. People also say you want to be their king. 7 You even set up prophets in Jerusalem to say about you, 'There is a king in Judah!' The king will hear this. So come, let us meet together."

8 I sent back this answer: "Nothing you are saying is true. You are making it up." 9 They all wanted to scare us. They thought, "Their hands will get weak, and the work will not be done."

But I prayed, "God, give me strength."

A False Prophet Tries to Scare Nehemiah

10 I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah son of Mehetabel. He could not leave his home. He said, "Let us meet at God's house, inside the temple. Let us close the temple doors. Men are coming to kill you. They are coming to kill you at night."

11 I said, "Should a man like me run away? Should someone like me go into the temple just to stay alive? No, I will not go in."

12 I knew God had not sent him. He had spoken against me because Tobiah and Sanballat paid him. 13 They paid him to scare me. They wanted me to sin by going into the temple. Then they could shame me.

14 My God, remember what Tobiah and Sanballat have done. Also remember Noadiah, a woman prophet, and the other prophets who tried to scare me.

The Wall Is Finished

15 We finished the wall on the twenty-fifth day of Elul. It took fifty-two days. 16 When all our enemies heard about it, the nations around us were afraid. They lost their courage. They knew God had helped us do this work.

Tobiah's Friends in Judah

17 In those days, the leaders of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah. Tobiah sent letters back to them. 18 Many people in Judah had promised to help him. Tobiah was connected to them by marriage. He was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah. His son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah. 19 They also told me about the good things Tobiah did. Then they told Tobiah what I said. Tobiah sent letters to scare me.
