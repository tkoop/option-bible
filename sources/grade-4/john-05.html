Jesus Heals a Sick Man

1 After this, there was a Jewish feast. Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 2 In Jerusalem, by the Sheep Gate, there is a pool called Bethesda in Hebrew. It has five porches. 3 Many sick people lay there. Some were blind. Some could not walk. Some could not move well.

5 One man had been sick for 38 years. 6 Jesus saw him lying there. He knew the man had been sick a long time. Jesus said, "Do you want to get well?"

7 The sick man said, "Sir, I have no one to put me in the pool when the water moves. By the time I get there, someone else goes in first."

8 Jesus said, "Get up. Pick up your mat and walk." 9 Right away the man was well. He picked up his mat and walked. That day was a Sabbath.

10 The Jewish leaders said to the healed man, "It is the Sabbath. You may not carry your mat."

11 He said, "The man who made me well told me, 'Pick up your mat and walk.'"

12 They asked him, "Who told you, 'Pick up and walk'?"

13 The healed man did not know who it was. Jesus had slipped away because many people were there.

14 Later Jesus found him in the temple. He said, "See, you are well. Stop sinning, so nothing worse happens to you."

15 The man went away. He told the Jewish leaders that Jesus had made him well.

The Leaders Turn Against Jesus

16 Because of this, the Jewish leaders were against Jesus. He had done these things on the Sabbath.

17 Jesus said to them, "My Father is working even now. I am working too."

18 This made the Jewish leaders want to kill Him even more. He did not just break the Sabbath. He also said God was His own Father. That made Him equal with God.

Jesus Explains His Work

19 Jesus said to them, "I tell you the truth. The Son cannot do anything by Himself. He only does what He sees the Father doing. What the Father does, the Son does too. 20 The Father loves the Son. He shows Him everything He does. He will show Him even greater things than these. Then you will be amazed."

"21 Just as the Father raises dead people and gives them life, the Son gives life to anyone He wants. 22 The Father does not judge anyone. He gave all judging to the Son. 23 He did this so everyone will honor the Son, just as they honor the Father. Anyone who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent Him."

"24 I tell you the truth. Anyone who hears My words and believes the One who sent Me has life that never ends. That person will not be judged. He has moved from death to life. 25 I tell you the truth. A time is coming, and it is here now, when dead people will hear the voice of the Son of God. Those who hear will live. 26 The Father has life in Himself. He gave the Son the power to have life in Himself too. 27 He gave the Son the right to judge, because He is the Son of Man."

"28 Do not be surprised at this. A time is coming when all people in the graves will hear His voice. 29 They will come out. People who did good will rise to life. People who did wrong will rise to be judged. 30 I cannot do anything by Myself. I judge as I hear. My judgment is fair. I do not do what I want. I do what the One who sent Me wants."

"31 If I say good things about Myself, that is not enough proof. 32 Someone else speaks for Me. I know His words about Me are true. 33 You sent people to John. He told the truth. 34 I do not need people to say good things about Me. I say this so you can be saved."

"35 John was like a lamp that burned and gave light. For a while, you were happy to enjoy his light. 36 But I have proof bigger than John's. The Father gave Me work to do. The work I am doing shows that the Father sent Me."

"37 The Father who sent Me has spoken for Me. You have never heard His voice. You have never seen what He looks like. 38 You do not keep His words in your hearts. You do not believe the One He sent."

"39 You study the Holy Books. You think they give you life that never ends. Those books tell about Me. 40 But you do not want to come to Me to have life."

"41 I do not look for praise from people. 42 But I know you. You do not have God's love in your hearts. 43 I came in My Father's name, and you do not receive Me. If someone else comes in his own name, you will receive him. 44 How can you believe? You accept praise from each other. You do not look for praise from the only God."

"45 Do not think I will accuse you before the Father. Moses is the one who accuses you. You put your hope in him. 46 If you believed Moses, you would believe Me, because he wrote about Me. 47 If you do not believe his writings, how will you believe My words?"
