Our Body Is Like a Tent

1 We know this: If our body, like a tent on earth, is taken down, we have a home from God. It is a house in heaven that lasts forever. God made it. Human hands did not make it. 2 In this tent we groan. We want to put on our home from heaven. 3 When we put it on, we will not be found with no clothes. 4 While we are in this tent, we groan and feel heavy. We do not want to take off our clothes. We want to put on new clothes. Then our dying body will be filled with life. 5 God made us for this. He gave us His Spirit as a promise.

We Want to Please the Lord

6 So we are always brave. We know that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord. 7 We live by faith, not by what we see. 8 We are brave and would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord. 9 So we try hard to please Him, whether we are at home or away. 10 We must all stand before Christ and be judged. Each of us will get what is right for what we did in the body, good or bad.

We Help People Believe

11 Because we know the Lord should be feared, we try to help people believe. God sees who we really are. I hope you see it in your hearts too. 12 We are not trying to make you praise us again. We are giving you a reason to be proud of us. Then you can answer people who brag about how they look, not about their hearts. 13 If we seem crazy, it is for God. If we are in our right mind, it is for you.

Christ's Love Makes Us New

14 Christ's love pushes us to do this. We believe this: One died for all, so all died. 15 He died for all so that people who live will not live for themselves. They will live for Him who died for them and rose again. 16 So from now on we do not look at people just by what we see on the outside. Even if we once knew Christ in a human way, we do not know Him that way now. 17 So if anyone is in Christ, he is a new person. The old things are gone. Look, new things have come! 18 All this comes from God. He made us right with Himself through Christ. He gave us the job of helping others be made right with Him. 19 God was making the whole world right with Himself in Christ. He did not count people's wrongs against them. He gave us the message that brings people back to Him. 20 So we speak for Christ. It is like God is asking you through us. We ask you for Christ: “Be made right with God.” 21 God made Him, who never sinned, to take our sin for us. He did this so that in Him we might be right with God.
