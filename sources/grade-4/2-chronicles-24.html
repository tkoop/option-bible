Joash Becomes King

1 Joash was seven years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for forty years. His mother's name was Zibiah. She was from Beersheba. 2 Joash did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD during all the days of Jehoiada the priest. 3 Jehoiada picked two wives for him, and he had sons and daughters.

Joash Fixes the Temple

4 Later, Joash wanted to fix the the LORD's house.

5 He gathered the priests and Levites. He said to them, "Go out to the cities of Judah. Collect money each year from all Israel to fix the house of your God. Hurry." But the Levites did not hurry.

6 The king called Jehoiada the chief priest. He asked, "Why didn't you make the Levites bring from Judah and Jerusalem the money that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, and the people of Israel gave for the tent of meeting?"

7 The sons of Athaliah, that bad woman, had broken into God's house. They had also used the holy things from the the LORD's temple for the Baals.

8 The king gave an order. They made a chest and put it outside, at the gate of the the LORD's temple. 9 They told the people of Judah and Jerusalem to bring to the the LORD the tax that Moses, the servant of God, had asked Israel to give in the desert. 10 All the leaders and all the people were happy. They brought money and put it in the chest until it was full. 11 When the Levites brought the chest to the king's men, they saw there was a lot of money. The king's secretary and the chief priest's officer would empty the chest. They would carry it back to its place. They did this every day. They gathered a lot of money.

12 The king and Jehoiada gave the money to the men who did the work on the the LORD's house. They hired stonecutters and carpenters to fix the the LORD's house. They also hired men who worked with iron and bronze to make the the LORD's house strong. 13 The workers did the work. The repair went well. They made God's house like it was before, and they made it strong. 14 When they finished, they brought the rest of the money to the king and Jehoiada. They made things for the the LORD's temple: serving things, things for burnt offerings, bowls, and gold and silver things. They offered burnt offerings in the the LORD's temple every day while Jehoiada lived.

Jehoiada Dies

15 Jehoiada grew very old. He died at one hundred thirty years old. 16 They buried him with the kings in the City of David. He had done good in Israel, and for God and His house.

Joash Turns Away from God

17 After Jehoiada died, the leaders of Judah came to the king and bowed down to him. The king listened to them. 18 They left the house of the the LORD, the God of their fathers. They served Asherah poles and idols. So God's anger came on Judah and Jerusalem because of their guilt. 19 God sent prophets to bring them back to the the LORD. The prophets warned them, but they would not listen.

Zechariah Warns the People

20 The Spirit of God came on Zechariah, the son of Jehoiada the priest. He stood in front of the people and said, "This is what God says: Why do you break the the LORD's commands? You will not do well. You have left the the LORD, so He has left you." 21 They made a plan against him at the king's command. They threw stones at him in the courtyard of the the LORD's temple. 22 King Joash did not remember the kindness that Jehoiada, his father, had shown him. He killed Jehoiada's son. As Zechariah died, he said, "May the the LORD see this and punish them."

Judgment Comes to Joash

23 At the end of the year, the army of Aram came against him. They came to Judah and Jerusalem. They destroyed all the leaders of the people. They sent all the things they took to the king of Damascus. 24 The army of Aram came with only a few men. But the the LORD gave a very large army into their hands, because Judah had left the the LORD, the God of their fathers. So Joash was punished.

Joash Is Killed

25 When they left, they left Joash badly hurt. His servants made a plan against him because of the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest. They killed him on his bed. They buried him in the City of David, but not in the tombs of the kings. 26 The men who made the plan against him were Zabad, son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad, son of Shimrith the Moabite woman. 27 Many things were said against Joash. The work on God's house was written in the book of the kings. His son Amaziah became king after him.
