The LORD Rules the World

1 The LORD rules. He wears great power. The world stands firm. It will not shake.

2 Your throne has been firm since long ago. You live forever.

God Is Stronger Than the Waves

3 The rivers rise up, O the LORD. They lift their voices. Their waves crash. 4 The LORD in heaven is stronger than loud waters and crashing waves.

God's House Is Holy

5 Your promises are very true. Your house is holy and beautiful, O the LORD. It will last forever.
