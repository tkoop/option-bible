Be Careful at a Ruler's Table

1 When you sit down to eat with a ruler, watch carefully what is in front of you. 2 If you like food too much, be careful not to eat too much. 3 Do not want his fancy food, because that food tricks you.

Do Not Chase Riches

4 Do not work too hard to get rich. Be smart enough to stop. 5 If you look at money for just a moment, it can be gone. It grows wings and flies away like a bird into the sky.

Do Not Eat with a Stingy Person

6 Do not eat the food of a person who is stingy, and do not want his fancy food. 7 What he really thinks is who he is. He says, "Eat and drink," but he does not really want you to. 8 You will throw up the food you ate, and your kind words will be wasted.

Do Not Talk to Fools

9 Do not talk to a foolish person, because he will make fun of your wise words.

Do Not Move Old Boundary Stones

10 Do not move an old boundary stone, and do not take land from orphans. 11 Their Redeemer is strong. He will stand up for them against you.

Listen to Wise Teaching

12 Give your heart to teaching, and listen with your ears to wise words.

Discipline Your Child

13 Do not refuse to discipline a child. If you punish him with a stick, he will not die. 14 Punish him, and you will save his life.

A Father's Joy

15 My son, if you are wise, I will be very happy. 16 I will be glad deep inside when you say what is right.

Do Not Envy Sinners

17 Do not be jealous of sinners. Always be eager to honor the the LORD. 18 You have a good future, and your hope will not be taken away.

Stay on the Right Path

19 Listen, my son, be wise, and keep your mind on the right path. 20 Do not be with people who drink too much wine or eat too much food. 21 People who drink too much and eat too much become poor, and sleepiness makes them wear old clothes.

Listen to Your Parents

22 Listen to your father, who gave you life, and do not look down on your mother when she is old. 23 Buy the truth and do not sell it. Get wisdom, discipline, and understanding. 24 The father of a good man is very happy. A man who has a wise son is glad. 25 May your father and mother be happy! May the woman who gave birth to you be joyful!

Stay Away from Bad Women

26 My son, give me your full attention, and keep your eyes on my ways. 27 A prostitute is a deep hole, and a woman who cheats is a narrow well. 28 She waits like a robber and leads many men to be unfaithful.

Warnings About Wine

29 Who has trouble? Who has sadness? Who has fights? Who has complaints? Who has bruises for no reason? Who has red eyes? 30 People who stay too long over wine, who go to taste mixed wine. 31 Do not stare at wine when it is red, when it shines in the cup, when it goes down easily!

32 In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a poison snake. 33 You will see strange things, and your mind will think confusing thoughts. 34 You will feel like someone sleeping on a stormy sea or lying on top of a ship's ropes. 35 "They hit me," you will say, "but I'm not hurt! They beat me, but I don't feel it! When will I wake up so I can find another drink?"
