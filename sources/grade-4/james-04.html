Why Do People Fight?

1 Where do fights and wars come from among you? Don't they come from the bad wants inside you? 2 You want things and don't have them. You kill and are jealous, but you still can't get them. You fight and make war. You don't have things because you don't ask God. 3 When you do ask, you don't get them, because you ask for bad reasons. You want to use them for your own fun.

Don't Love the World

4 You are not faithful to God! Don't you know that being friends with the world makes you an enemy of God? Anyone who wants to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God. 5 Do you think the Bible says this for no reason? The Spirit that God put in us wants us for Himself. He is jealous for us. 6 But God gives even more grace. That is why it says, "God stands against proud people, but He gives grace to humble people."

Come Close to God

7 So give yourselves to God. Stand against the devil, and he will run away from you. 8 Come close to God, and He will come close to you. Wash your hands, you sinners. Make your hearts clean, you people who can't decide. 9 Be sad and cry. Let your laughing turn to crying, and your joy turn to sadness. 10 Be humble before the Lord, and He will lift you up.

Don't Say Bad Things About Others

11 Brothers, don't say bad things about each other. Anyone who says bad things about a brother or judges that brother says bad things about God's law and judges it. But if you judge God's law, you are not following it. You are acting like its judge. 12 There is only One who made the law and who judges. He can save people or destroy them. So who are you to judge your neighbor?

Don't Brag About Tomorrow

13 Now listen, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go to this city. We will stay there a year and buy and sell and make money." 14 You don't even know what will happen tomorrow. What is your life? It is like a fog that shows up for a little while and then is gone. 15 Instead, you should say, "If the Lord wants us to, we will live and do this or that." 16 But now you brag with pride. All that kind of bragging is bad. 17 So if someone knows the right thing to do and doesn't do it, that is sin.
