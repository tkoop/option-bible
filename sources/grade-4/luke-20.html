Jesus Is Asked About His Power

1 One day, while Jesus was teaching the people in the temple and sharing the good news, the chief priests, teachers of the law, and elders came to Him. 2 They said, "Tell us, by what power are You doing these things? Who gave You this power?"

3 Jesus said, "I will ask you a question too. Tell Me. 4 Was John's baptism from heaven or from people?"

5 They thought to themselves, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will ask, 'Why didn't you believe him?' 6 But if we say, 'From people,' all the people will throw stones at us, because they believe John was a prophet."

7 They answered, "We don't know where it was from."

8 Jesus said, "Then I won't tell you by what power I do these things either."

A Story About a Grape Field

9 Then He began to tell the people this story: "A man planted a grape field. He rented it to some farmers and went away for a long time. 10 At harvest time, he sent a servant to the farmers to get some of the fruit. But the farmers beat him and sent him away with nothing.

11 "He sent another servant. They beat him too, treated him badly, and sent him away with nothing. 12 He sent a third servant. They hurt him and threw him out.

13 "The owner of the grape field said, 'What should I do? I will send my dear Son. Maybe they will respect Him.'

14 "But when the farmers saw Him, they said to each other, 'This is the one who will get everything. Let's kill Him so it will be ours.' 15 They threw Him out of the grape field and killed Him. What will the owner do to them?

16 "He will come and kill those farmers and give the grape field to others."

When the people heard this, they said, "No, that must not happen!"

17 Jesus looked at them and said, "Then what does this mean? 'The stone the builders did not want has become the most important stone.' 18 Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken. And the stone will crush anyone it falls on."

19 The teachers of the law and chief priests knew He was talking about them. So they wanted to arrest Him right then, but they were afraid of the people.

Paying Taxes to Caesar

20 They watched Him closely. They sent spies who pretended to be good. They wanted to catch Him in His words so they could hand Him over to the governor's power.

21 They said, "Teacher, we know You speak and teach what is right. You don't favor anyone. You truly teach God's way. 22 Is it right for us to pay taxes to Caesar or not?"

23 Jesus saw their trick. He said to them, 24 "Show Me a coin. Whose picture and name are on it?"

"Caesar's," they said.

25 He said, "Then give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and give to God what is God's."

26 They could not catch Him in His words in front of the people. They were amazed at His answer and said nothing.

A Question About Rising from the Dead

27 Some Sadducees came to Him. They say there is no rising from the dead. 28 They said, "Teacher, Moses wrote this for us: If a man's brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, the brother should marry her and have children for his brother. 29 There were seven brothers. The first married a woman and died without children. 30 The second married her. 31 The third married her too. All seven married her and died without children. 32 Last of all, the woman died. 33 So in the rising from the dead, whose wife will she be? All seven were married to her."

34 Jesus said, "People in this world get married. 35 But those who are chosen to take part in that world and in the rising from the dead do not get married. 36 They cannot die anymore. They are like angels. They are God's children, because they belong to the rising from the dead. 37 Even Moses showed that the dead rise. He said at the bush, 'The Lord is the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' 38 He is not the God of the dead, but of the living. To Him, all are alive."

39 Some teachers of the law said, "Teacher, You spoke well." 40 They did not dare ask Him anything else.

Jesus Asks About the Christ

41 Then Jesus said to them, "How can people say the Christ is David's Son? 42 David himself says in the Book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, 43 "Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet."' 44 David calls Him 'Lord.' So how can He be David's Son?"

A Warning About Proud Teachers

45 While all the people were listening, Jesus said to His disciples, "Watch out for the teachers of the law. 46 They like to walk around in long robes. They love greetings in the marketplaces. They love the best seats in the meeting places and the best places at feasts. 47 They take widows' houses and pray long prayers to look good. They will be punished even more."
