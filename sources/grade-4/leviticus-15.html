1 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

Rules for a Man with a Flow

2 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: If a man has a flow from his body, that flow is not clean. 3 This is how he is not clean from his flow: If the flow comes out or if it is stopped, he is not clean. 4 Any bed he lies on is not clean. Any seat he sits on is not clean. 5 Anyone who touches his bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 6 Anyone who sits on a seat the man sat on must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 7 Anyone who touches the man's skin must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 8 If the man spits on a clean person, that person must wash his clothes and bathe in water. He will not be clean until evening. 9 Any saddle he rides on is not clean. 10 Anyone who touches anything that was under him will not be clean until evening. Anyone who carries those things must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 11 If the man touches someone without first washing his hands in water, the person he touched must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 12 Any clay pot he touches must be broken. Any wood thing must be washed in water.

How a Man Becomes Clean

13 "When the man is clean from his flow, he must count seven days. Then he must wash his clothes and bathe his body in fresh water. Then he will be clean. 14 On the eighth day, he must take two doves or two young pigeons. He must come to the the LORD at the door of the meeting tent and give them to the priest. 15 The priest will make one bird a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make him right with the the LORD because of his flow.

Rules for a Man's Seed

16 "When a man has a flow of seed, he must bathe his whole body in water. He will not be clean until evening. 17 Any clothes or leather that have seed on them must be washed in water. They will not be clean until evening. 18 If a man lies with a woman and seed comes out, both must bathe in water. They will not be clean until evening.

Rules for a Woman's Period

19 "When a woman has a flow of blood from her body, she will not be clean for seven days because of her period. Anyone who touches her will not be clean until evening. 20 Anything she lies on during her period is not clean. Anything she sits on is not clean. 21 Anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 22 Anyone who touches any seat she sits on must wash his clothes and bathe in water. That person will not be clean until evening. 23 If something on her bed or seat is touched, it will not be clean until evening. 24 If a man lies with her, he becomes not clean like her. He will not be clean for seven days. Every bed he lies on will not be clean.

Rules for a Woman's Long Flow

25 "If a woman has blood flowing for many days outside her period, or if it flows past her regular period, she will not be clean all the days of her flow, just like during her period. 26 Any bed or seat she uses during her flow is not clean, just like during her period. 27 Anyone who touches these things will not be clean. He must wash his clothes and bathe in water. He will not be clean until evening.

How a Woman Becomes Clean

28 "When she is clean from her flow, she must count seven days. After that, she will be clean. 29 On the eighth day, she must take two doves or two young pigeons. She must bring them to the priest at the door of the meeting tent. 30 The priest will make one bird a sin offering and the other a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make her right with the the LORD because of her flow that made her not clean.

Keep God's Holy Tent Clean

31 "You must keep the Israelites away from things that make them not clean. Then they will not die by making My holy tent not clean. It is among them.

The Law in Short

32 "This is the law for a person with a flow. It is also for a man who has a flow of seed and becomes not clean. 33 It is also for a woman during her period, and for any man or woman who has a flow, or for a man who lies with a woman who is not clean."
