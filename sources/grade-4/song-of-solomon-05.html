The Groom Comes to His Garden

1 "I came into my garden, my sister, my bride. I picked my myrrh with my spice. I ate my honeycomb with my honey. I drank my wine with my milk. Eat, friends. Drink and be happy, loved ones."

2 "I am asleep, but my heart is awake. I hear my loved one knocking:"

"Open the door for me, my sister, my dear one, my dove, my perfect one. My head is wet with dew. My hair is wet with night rain."

3 "I took off my robe. How can I put it on again? I washed my feet. How can I get them dirty? 4 My loved one put his hand through the hole in the door. My heart beat fast for him. 5 I got up to open the door for my loved one. My hands dripped with myrrh. My fingers dripped with myrrh on the lock. 6 I opened the door for my loved one, but he had gone. My heart sank when he spoke. I looked for him, but I could not find him. I called him, but he did not answer. 7 The guards found me while they walked around the city. They hit me and hurt me. They took my veil from me. These were the guards on the walls."

The Bride Asks the Girls of Jerusalem

8 "Girls of Jerusalem, I beg you: If you find my loved one, tell him I am weak with love."

The Girls Ask About Her Loved One

9 "What makes your loved one better than others, most beautiful woman? Why do you ask us to promise?"

The Bride Praises Her Loved One

10 "My loved one is bright and red. He stands out among ten thousand. 11 His head is pure gold. His hair is wavy and black like a raven. 12 His eyes are like doves by streams of water. They are washed in milk and set in their sockets.

13 His cheeks are like beds of spice, like towers of perfume. His lips are like lilies that drip with myrrh. 14 His arms are like gold rods set with jewels. His body is like smooth ivory decorated with blue stones. 15 His legs are like marble pillars on gold bases. He looks like Lebanon, like its tall cedar trees. 16 His mouth is very sweet. He is all lovely. This is my loved one. This is my friend, girls of Jerusalem."
