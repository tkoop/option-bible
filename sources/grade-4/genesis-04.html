Cain and Abel

1 Adam slept with his wife Eve. She got pregnant and had a baby boy named Cain. She said, "With the the LORD's help, I have a baby boy." 2 Later she had another son. His name was Abel. Abel took care of sheep. Cain worked the ground.

3 After some time, Cain brought some food from the ground as a gift to the the LORD. 4 Abel brought the best parts from his first lambs. The The LORD was pleased with Abel and his gift. 5 But He was not pleased with Cain and his gift. Cain got very angry, and his face looked sad.

6 The The LORD said to Cain, "Why are you angry? Why does your face look sad?" 7 "If you do what is right, will you not be accepted? But if you do not do what is right, sin is waiting at your door. It wants to control you, but you must control it."

8 Cain spoke to his brother Abel. When they were in the field, Cain attacked Abel and killed him.

9 Then the the LORD said to Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?"

"I don't know," Cain said. "Am I supposed to watch over my brother?"

10 The The LORD said, "What did you do? Listen! Your brother's blood is crying to Me from the ground."

11 The The LORD said, "Now you are cursed and driven from the ground. It opened its mouth to drink your brother's blood from your hand. 12 When you work the ground, it will not give you good crops. You will wander around the earth."

13 Cain said to the the LORD, "My punishment is more than I can bear. 14 Today you are sending me away from the land. I will be hidden from you. I will wander on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me."

15 The The LORD said to him, "No! If anyone kills Cain, he will be punished seven times." Then the the LORD put a mark on Cain so that no one who found him would kill him.

16 So Cain went out from the the LORD's presence and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden.

Cain's Family

17 Cain slept with his wife. She got pregnant and had a son named Enoch. Cain was building a city, and he named the city after his son Enoch. 18 Enoch had a son named Irad. Irad had a son named Mehujael. Mehujael had a son named Methushael. Methushael had a son named Lamech.

19 Lamech married two women. One was named Adah, and the other was named Zillah. 20 Adah had a son named Jabal. He was the father of people who live in tents and keep animals. 21 His brother was Jubal. He was the father of people who play harps and flutes. 22 Zillah had a son named Tubal-cain. He made tools out of bronze and iron. Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah.

23 Lamech said to his wives, "Adah and Zillah, listen to me. Wives of Lamech, hear my words. I killed a man for hurting me. I killed a young man for hitting me."

24 If Cain is paid back seven times, then Lamech will be paid back seventy-seven times.

Seth and Enosh

25 Adam slept with his wife again. She had a son and named him Seth. She said, "God has given me another child in place of Abel, because Cain killed him." 26 Seth had a son named Enosh. Then people began to call on the name of the the LORD.
