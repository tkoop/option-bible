Let's Grow Up in Faith

1 So let's move past the first lessons about Christ. Let's grow up and be strong in faith. 2 We do not need to start over with the first steps: turning from the wrong things we do, trusting God, lessons about baptism, laying hands on people, rising from the dead, and God's final judgment. 3 We will do this if God allows.

A Strong Warning

4 It is impossible to make people turn back to God again if they once saw God's light, tasted His gift from heaven, shared in the Holy Spirit, 5 tasted God's good word, and felt the power of the future world, 6 and then turned away. They would be like putting the Son of God on a cross again and shaming Him.

7 Land that drinks the rain that falls on it and grows good plants for the people who work it gets God's blessing. 8 But land that grows thorns and weeds is no good. It is close to being cursed, and its end is to be burned.

Good Things About You

9 Dear friends, even when we talk like this, we are sure you will do better things, things that go with being saved. 10 God is not unfair. He will not forget your work and your love. You showed your love by helping God's people, and you still do. 11 We want each of you to keep working hard until the end, so you can be very sure of your hope. 12 Then you will not be lazy. You will copy those who trusted God and waited patiently, and they received what God promised.

God Keeps His Promise

13 When God made a promise to Abraham, He had no one greater to swear by, so He swore by Himself. 14 He said, "I will surely bless you, and I will make your family very large." 15 Abraham waited patiently, and then he received what God promised.

16 People swear by someone greater than them. An oath makes a promise strong and ends arguments. 17 God wanted to show the people who receive His promise that His plan will not change. So He used an oath. 18 God cannot lie. So we have two things that will not change. This gives strong comfort to us who run to God for safety. We can hold tight to the hope God gives us.

19 We have this hope like a strong anchor for our soul. It is safe and sure. It goes into the place behind the curtain. 20 Jesus went there first for us. He became our high priest forever, like Melchizedek.
