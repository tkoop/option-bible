Land for Manasseh

1 The family group of Manasseh got its land. Manasseh was Joseph's oldest son. Machir was Manasseh's oldest son and Gilead's father. Machir was a soldier, so he got Gilead and Bashan. 2 The rest of Manasseh's family groups also got land. They were Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These were the sons of Manasseh, son of Joseph.

3 Zelophehad was Hepher's son. Hepher was Gilead's son. Gilead was Machir's son. Machir was Manasseh's son. Zelophehad had no sons. He had only daughters. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 4 The daughters came to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders. They said, "The LORD told Moses to give us land with our brothers." So Joshua gave them land with their father's brothers, just as the LORD said. 5 So Manasseh got ten shares of land. This was besides Gilead and Bashan across the Jordan River. 6 Manasseh's daughters got land with his sons. The land of Gilead went to the rest of Manasseh's sons.

Manasseh's Border

7 Manasseh's border went from Asher to Michmethath, east of Shechem. Then it turned south to the people of En Tappuah. 8 The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh. But the town of Tappuah, on Manasseh's border, belonged to Ephraim. 9 The border went down to the Kanah Valley. The towns south of the valley, among Manasseh's towns, belonged to Ephraim. Manasseh's border was north of the valley and reached the sea. 10 Ephraim's land was to the south. Manasseh's land was to the north. The sea was their border. They touched Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

11 In Issachar and Asher, Manasseh also had Beth Shan, Ibleam, Dor, Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo, with their towns. 12 But Manasseh's people could not take these towns. The Canaanites wanted to stay in that land. 13 Later, when the Israelites got stronger, they made the Canaanites work for them. But they did not drive them out completely.

Joseph's Family Asks for More Land

14 The sons of Joseph spoke to Joshua. They said, "Why did you give us only one share of land? We are many people. The LORD has blessed us a lot." 15 Joshua said to them, "If you are many people, go up into the forest. Clear a place for yourself there in the land of the Perizzites and Rephaim. The hill country of Ephraim is too small for you."

16 The sons of Joseph said, "The hill country is not enough for us. All the Canaanites in the valley have iron chariots. They are in Beth Shan and its towns. They are also in the Jezreel Valley."

17 Joshua said to the family of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are many people. You are very strong. You will not have just one share. 18 The hill country will be yours. It is a forest. You will clear it. Its far borders will be yours. The Canaanites have iron chariots. But you will drive them out."
