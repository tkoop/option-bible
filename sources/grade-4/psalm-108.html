David Praises God

1 This is a song and psalm of David. My heart is ready, God. I will sing and make music with all my heart. 2 Wake up, my heart! Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake up early. 3 I will thank You among the countries, O the LORD. I will sing praises to You among the people.

4 For Your kind love is great above the sky. Your truth reaches to the clouds. 5 Be lifted up, God, above the sky. Let Your glory be above all the earth.

6 Save with Your right hand and answer me, so Your loved ones can be saved.

God Promises Victory

7 God has spoken from His holy place: “I will win. I will share Shechem and measure the Valley of Succoth. 8 Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is the helmet for My head. Judah is My ruler’s stick. 9 Moab is My washbowl. Edom is under My foot. Over Philistia I shout in victory.”

David Asks for Help

10 Who will take me to the strong city? Who will lead me to Edom? 11 Have You turned away from us, God? Will You not go out with our armies? 12 Give us help against the enemy, for help from people is no good. 13 With God we will do great things, and He will step on our enemies.
