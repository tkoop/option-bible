Moses Sings to the Sky and Earth

1 Listen, heavens! I will speak. Earth, hear the words from my mouth. 2 Let my teaching fall like rain. Let my words drop like dew. They are like soft rain on new grass. They are like showers on young plants. 3 I will say the name of the the LORD. Give our God great praise! 4 God is like a strong Rock. What He does is perfect. All His ways are fair. He is a faithful God. He does no wrong. He is good and right.

The People Did Wrong

5 But His people did wrong to Him. They are not His children anymore. They are a crooked and twisted group. 6 Is this how you pay back the LORD, you foolish people with no sense? Is He not your Father? Did He not make you and keep you?

Remember What God Did

7 Remember the old days. Think about the years long ago. Ask your father. He will tell you. Ask your older leaders. They will tell you. 8 When God Most High gave each nation its land, when He split people into groups, He set the borders for the nations. He did this for the people of Israel. 9 The LORD chose His people as His share. Jacob is His special gift.

10 God found His people in a dry desert. It was an empty, wild place. He took care of them. He taught them. He kept them safe like the center of His eye. 11 Like an eagle, He stirred up His nest and hovered over His young. He spread His wings and caught them. He carried them on His wings. 12 Only the LORD led His people. No false god was with them. 13 God let His people ride on the high places of the land. He fed them with food from the fields. He gave them honey from the rock. He gave them oil from hard stone. 14 He gave them butter from cows and milk from sheep. He gave them fat from lambs and rams from Bashan and goats. He gave them the best wheat. You drank the best wine from grapes.

Israel Turned Away from God

15 But Israel grew rich and rebelled. You grew fat, thick, and smooth. Then he left God who made him. He turned away from the Rock who saved him. 16 They made God jealous with false gods. They made Him angry with terrible sins. 17 They gave gifts to demons, not to God. They worshiped gods they did not know. These gods were new. Your fathers did not know them. 18 You forgot the Rock who gave you birth. You forgot the God who made you.

The LORD Is Angry

19 The LORD saw this. He was angry with His sons and daughters. He pushed them away.

God Speaks

"20 I will turn My face away from them. I will see what happens to them at the end. They are a crooked people. They are children who cannot be trusted. 21 They made Me jealous with things that are not God. They made Me angry with their empty idols. So I will make them jealous with a people who are not a nation. I will make them angry with a foolish nation. 22 My anger is like a fire. It burns down to the grave. It eats up the earth and its crops. It sets the bases of the mountains on fire. 23 I will bring many troubles on them. I will use up My arrows on them. 24 Hunger will waste them. Sickness and bitter disease will eat them up. I will send wild animals to bite them. I will send snakes in the dust to sting them. 25 Outside, swords will kill their children. Inside, fear will rule. Young men and young women will die. Babies and old men will die.

"26 I said I would scatter them and erase their name from people. 27 But I held back. I did not want their enemies to brag. I did not want them to say that they won by their own power and that the LORD did not do this."

The People Have No Sense

28 They are a people with no good plans. They have no sense. 29 If they were wise, they would understand this. They would see what will happen to them in the end. 30 How could one enemy chase a thousand of them? How could two chase ten thousand? Only because their Rock let their enemies have them. Only because the LORD gave them up. 31 Their rock is not like our Rock. Even our enemies agree. 32 Their vine comes from Sodom's vine. It comes from Gomorrah's fields. Their grapes are poison. Their clusters are bitter. 33 Their wine is like snake poison. It is like the deadly poison of cobras.

The LORD Will Pay Back

"34 I have kept this with Me. It is locked in My storehouse. 35 Payback is Mine. I will pay them back. Their time to fall is coming. Their day of trouble is near. What is coming for them will happen fast."

The LORD Will Help His People

36 The LORD will stand up for His people. He will show mercy to His servants. He will see that their strength is gone. No one is left, slave or free.

Where Are Their Gods?

"37 Where are their gods? Where is the rock they trusted? 38 Those gods ate the fat of their gifts. They drank the wine of their offerings. Let them rise up and help you. Let them be your protection. 39 See now that I am He. There is no God but Me. I bring death, and I give life. I wound, and I heal. No one can save people from My hand. 40 I lift My hand to heaven and say, as I live forever, 41 I will sharpen My flashing sword. My hand will take hold of justice. I will pay back My enemies. I will pay back those who hate Me. 42 I will make My arrows drunk with blood. My sword will eat flesh. It will eat the blood of the dead and the captives, even the heads of the enemy leaders."

All Nations Praise the LORD

43 Nations, be glad with His people! The LORD will pay back for the blood of His servants. He will take revenge on His enemies. He will make His land and His people clean.

Moses Teaches the People

44 Moses and Joshua son of Nun spoke all these words of this song to the people. 45 When Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel, 46 he said to them, "Put your heart into all these words. I tell you today to teach them to your children. Then they will keep and do all the words of this law. 47 These words are not empty for you. They are your life. By these words you will live a long time in the land you are crossing the Jordan to own."

The LORD Calls Moses Home

48 That same day the LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

"49 Go up to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, across from Jericho. Look at the land of Canaan. I am giving it to the people of Israel to own. 50 Die on the mountain you are going up. You will join your people, like your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and joined his people. 51 This is because you and Aaron broke faith with Me among the people of Israel at the waters of Meribah Kadesh in the desert of Zin. This is because you did not honor Me as holy among the people of Israel. 52 You will see the land from far away. But you will not go into the land I am giving to the people of Israel."
