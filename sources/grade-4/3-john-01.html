Greeting to Gaius

1 The older church leader writes to Gaius, whom he loves truly. 2 Dear friend, I pray that you do well in all things and stay healthy, just as your soul does well. 3 I was very happy when brothers came and said you live in the truth, just as you really do. 4 I have no greater joy than this: I hear that my children live in the truth.

Helping God’s Workers

5 Dear friend, you are faithful in all you do for the brothers, even for strangers. 6 They told the church about your love. You will do well to help them on their way in a way that honors God. 7 They went out to work for God’s Name. They took no help from people who are not believers. 8 So we should help people like them. Then we work together for the truth.

Warning About Diotrephes

9 I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who likes to be first, will not welcome us. 10 So if I come, I will talk about what he does. He says bad things about us. He is not satisfied with that. He will not welcome the brothers either. He stops anyone who wants to welcome them. He throws them out of the church.

Do Good, Not Bad

11 Dear friend, do not copy what is bad. Copy what is good. The person who does good belongs to God. The person who does bad has not seen God. 12 Everyone says good things about Demetrius. The truth itself says so too. We also say he is good. You know that what we say is true.

Final Words

13 I had many things to write to you. But I do not want to write with pen and ink. 14 I hope to see you soon. Then we can talk face to face. Peace to you. The friends say hello to you. Say hello to the friends by name.
