Keep God's Rules

1 Keep all the commands I give you today. Then you will live. You will grow. You will go in and take the land the the LORD promised to your fathers.

2 Remember how the the LORD your God led you through the desert for forty years. He did this to make you humble. He tested you. He wanted to know what was in your heart. He wanted to know if you would keep His commands or not.

3 He let you go hungry. Then He fed you with manna. You and your fathers had never seen this food before. He did this so you would learn that people do not live by bread alone. People live by every word that comes from the the LORD’s mouth.

4 Your clothes did not wear out. Your feet did not swell. This was true for forty years.

5 Know in your heart that the the LORD your God trains you, just like a man trains his son.

6 Keep the commands of the the LORD your God. Walk in His ways. Fear Him.

A Good Land

7 The The LORD your God is bringing you into a good land. It has streams and springs. Water comes from deep places in the valleys and hills.

8 It is a land with wheat and barley. It has vines, figs, and pomegranates. It has olive trees for oil and honey.

9 You will have plenty of bread there. You will not lack anything. The stones there are iron. You can dig copper from the hills.

10 When you have eaten and are full, praise the the LORD your God for the good land He has given you.

Do Not Forget God

11 Be careful. Do not forget the the LORD your God. Do not stop keeping His commands, His rules, and His laws that I give you today.

12 When you have eaten and are full, when you have built good houses and live in them, 13 and when your herds and flocks grow, and your silver and gold grow, and everything you have grows, 14 do not let your heart get proud. Do not forget the the LORD your God. He brought you out of Egypt. He brought you out of slavery.

15 He led you through the big and scary desert. There were snakes and scorpions. There was no water. But He gave you water from hard rock.

16 He fed you with manna in the desert. Your fathers had not known this food. He did this to make you humble and to test you. In the end, He wanted to do good for you.

Give God the Credit

17 Do not say in your heart, “My own power and strength made me rich.”

18 Remember the the LORD your God. He is the One who gives you the power to get wealth. He does this to keep the promise He made to your fathers, just as He does today.

A Serious Warning

19 If you forget the the LORD your God, and go after other gods, and serve them and bow down to them, I tell you today that you will be destroyed.

20 The the LORD will destroy other nations for you. In the same way, you will be destroyed if you do not obey the the LORD your God.
