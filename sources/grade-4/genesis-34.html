Dinah Is Hurt

1 Dinah was Jacob's daughter. Her mother was Leah. She went out to see the women who lived in that land. 2 Shechem was the son of Hamor the Hivite. He was the leader of that land. He saw Dinah. He took her and hurt her. 3 He cared for Dinah, Jacob's daughter. He loved the girl. He spoke kindly to her.

Shechem Wants to Marry Dinah

4 Shechem said to Hamor his father, “Get me this girl to be my wife.” 5 Jacob heard that Shechem had hurt his daughter Dinah. His sons were in the fields with the animals. Jacob waited quietly until they came home. 6 Hamor, Shechem's father, went to Jacob to talk with him.

Jacob's Sons Are Angry

7 Jacob's sons came home from the fields. They heard what had happened. They were very sad and angry. Shechem had done a terrible thing. He had hurt Jacob's daughter. Such a thing should not be done.

Hamor and Shechem Ask for Dinah

8 Hamor talked with them. He said, “My son Shechem loves your daughter. Please give her to him to be his wife. 9 Let our families join. Give us your daughters. Take our daughters for yourselves. 10 You can live with us. The land is open to you. Live here, trade here, and own land here.”

11 Shechem said to her father and brothers, “Please be kind to me. I will give you whatever you ask. 12 Ask for a big bride price and gifts. I will pay what you say. Just give me the girl to be my wife.”

Jacob's Sons Trick Them

13 Jacob's sons answered Shechem and Hamor with a trick. They did this because Shechem had hurt their sister Dinah. 14 They said, “We cannot give our sister to a man who is not circumcised. That would shame us. 15 We will agree only if you become like us. Every man among you must be circumcised. 16 Then we will give our daughters to you and take your daughters. We will live together and become one people. 17 But if you will not be circumcised, we will take our daughter and leave.”

The Men of the City Agree

18 Hamor and Shechem liked what they heard. 19 The young man did not wait. He did it right away because he liked Jacob's daughter. He was the most honored man in his father's house. 20 Hamor and Shechem went to the city gate. They spoke to the men of the city.

21 “These men are peaceful with us. Let them live and trade in our land. The land is big enough for all of us. Let us take their daughters as wives, and let us give our daughters to them. 22 But they will live with us and become one people only if every man among us is circumcised, like they are. 23 Their animals and things will be ours. Let us agree, and they will live with us.” 24 All the men at the city gate listened to Hamor and Shechem. Every man was circumcised.

Simeon and Levi Attack

25 On the third day, the men were sore. Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers and Jacob's sons, took their swords. They came into the city without fear. They killed every man. 26 They killed Hamor and Shechem with the sword. They took Dinah from Shechem's house and left. 27 Jacob's sons came to the dead men. They took things from the city because their sister had been hurt. 28 They took sheep, cows, and donkeys. They took what was in the city and in the fields. 29 They took all the wealth, the wives, and the children. They took everything in the houses.

Jacob Is Afraid

30 Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me. You made me look bad to the people who live here, the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I do not have many people. They may join together and attack me. Then I and my family will be destroyed.”

31 They said, “Should he treat our sister with no respect?”
