Jerusalem Is Lonely

1 How lonely is the city that once had many people! She was great among the nations, but now she is like a widow. She was a princess among the lands, but now she must work like a slave. 2 She cries hard at night. Tears run down her cheeks. None of the people she loved come to comfort her. All her friends turned against her and became her enemies.

3 Judah was taken away because she was poor and had to work hard. Now she lives far away among other nations. She can't find rest. Her enemies caught her in a tight place. 4 The roads to Zion are sad. No one comes for the feasts. Her gates are empty. Her priests groan. Her young women are sad. She is very bitter.

5 Her enemies won. They are in charge now, because the LORD punished her for many sins. Even her little children were taken away by the enemy. 6 The people of Zion lost all their beauty. Her leaders are like deer with no grass to eat. They are weak, and they run from the one chasing them.

7 In her trouble, Jerusalem remembered all the good things she once had. Her people fell into enemy hands, and no one helped her. Her enemies saw her and laughed at her fall. 8 Jerusalem sinned badly. So she became unclean. Everyone who honored her now looks down on her, because they saw her shame. She groans and turns away.

Jerusalem Cries to the LORD

9 Her sin is clear, but she did not think about what would happen. She fell in a terrible way. No one comforts her. "Look, the LORD, at how much I hurt! The enemy has become great." 10 The enemy reached out and took all her treasures. She saw foreign nations go into her holy place. You said they could not join Your people. 11 All her people groan as they look for bread. They gave their treasures for food to stay alive. "Look, the LORD, and see! I am hated."

Jerusalem Speaks

"12 Does my pain mean nothing to you, all you who pass by? Look and see if there is pain like mine. The LORD caused it on the day He was very angry. 13 From heaven He sent fire down into my bones. He spread a net for my feet. He turned me back. He left me empty and weak all day.

"14 My sins are like a heavy load around my neck. His hand tied them on. They weigh on my neck. The Lord has taken my strength. He put me in the hands of people I cannot fight. I cannot get up. 15 The Lord crushed all my strong men. He called a crowd to break my young men. The Lord crushed Judah's young women like grapes in a press. 16 I cry because of these things. Tears run from my eyes. No one is near to comfort me or give me new life. My children are left empty, because the enemy won."

17 Zion held out her hands, but no one comforted her. The LORD told Jacob's enemies to surround her. Jerusalem became unclean among them.

The LORD Is Right

"18 The LORD is right. I went against Him. Listen, all you people, and look at my pain. My young men and young women were taken away. 19 I called to my friends, but they tricked me. My priests and leaders died in the city while they looked for food to save their lives.

"20 Look, the LORD, I am in trouble! My stomach turns. My heart is upset inside me. I was very rebellious. Outside, the sword kills. Inside, it is like death. 21 They heard me groan, but no one comforted me. All my enemies heard about my trouble and were glad, because You did it. You brought the day You promised. May they become like me.

"22 Let all their evil come before You. Do to them what You did to me for all my sins. I groan so much, and my heart is weak."
