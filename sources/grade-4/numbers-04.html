The Kohathites' Work in the Tent

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

2"Count the Kohathite part of the Levites. Count them by their family groups. 3Count men from 30 to 50 years old. These men will work in the tent of meeting. 4This is the work of the Kohathites in the tent of meeting. They take care of the most holy things.

5"When the camp moves, Aaron and his sons will go in. They will take down the curtain in front of the ark. They will cover the ark with it. 6They will put a leather cover over it. Then they will spread a blue cloth over it. They will put the carrying poles in. 7They will spread a blue cloth over the table that holds the special bread. They will put the bowls, pans, jars, and drink cups on it. The bread that is always there will stay on it. 8They will spread a red cloth over these things. They will cover them with a leather cover. They will put the carrying poles in.

9"They will take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand. They will cover its lamps, tongs, trays, and oil jars. 10They will put the lampstand and all its tools in a leather cover. They will put it on a carrying frame. 11They will spread a blue cloth over the gold altar. They will cover it with a leather cover. They will put its carrying poles in. 12They will take all the tools used in the holy place. They will put them in a blue cloth. They will cover them with a leather cover. They will put them on a carrying frame.

13"They will take the ashes off the altar. They will spread a purple cloth over it. 14They will put all its tools on it: fire pans, forks, shovels, and bowls. They will cover it with a leather cover. They will put its carrying poles in. 15Aaron and his sons will finish covering the holy place and its tools. Then the Kohathites will come to carry them. But they must not touch the holy things. If they do, they will die. These are the loads the Kohathites carry in the tent of meeting. 16Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, will be in charge of the oil for the light, the sweet-smelling incense, the regular food gift, and the oil for special use. He will take care of the whole tent and everything in it, both the holy place and its tools."

Rules for the Kohathites

17The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

18"Do not let the Kohathite family groups be cut off from the Levites. 19Do this so they will live and not die when they come near the most holy place. Aaron and his sons will go in. They will give each man his work and his load. 20But they must not go in to look at the holy things, even for a moment. If they do, they will die."

The Gershonites' Work

21The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

22"Count the Gershonites too. Count them by their families and family groups. 23Count men from 30 to 50 years old. These men will work in the tent of meeting. 24This is the work of the Gershonite family groups. They will serve and carry things. 25They will carry the curtains of the tent, the tent of meeting, its covering, the leather covering on top, and the curtain at the door of the tent of meeting. 26They will carry the curtains of the courtyard, the curtain at the gate of the courtyard around the tent and altar, the ropes, and all their tools. They will do all the work for these things. 27Aaron and his sons will be in charge of all the work of the Gershonites. Moses and Aaron will tell them what to carry and what to do. They will put them in charge of their loads. 28This is the work of the Gershonites in the tent of meeting. They will work under Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest."

The Merarites' Work

29Count the Merarites by their clans and family groups. 30Count men from 30 to 50 years old. These men will work in the tent of meeting. 31They will carry the frames of the tent, its bars, posts, and bases. 32They will carry the posts around the courtyard, their bases, pegs, and ropes, and all their tools. Moses and Aaron will count these things by name and give them their loads. 33This is the work of the Merarites in the tent of meeting. They will work under Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.

Counting the Levites

34Moses, Aaron, and the leaders counted the Kohathites by their clans and family groups. 35They counted men from 30 to 50 years old who would work in the tent of meeting. 36There were 2,750 men. 37These were the Kohathites who were counted. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had told Moses.

38They also counted the Gershonites by their clans and family groups. 39They counted men from 30 to 50 years old who would work in the tent of meeting. 40There were 2,630 men. 41These were the Gershonites who were counted. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had said.

42They counted the Merarites by their clans and family groups. 43They counted men from 30 to 50 years old who would work in the tent of meeting. 44There were 3,200 men. 45These were the Merarites who were counted. Moses and Aaron counted them as the LORD had told Moses.

46Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel counted all the Levites by their clans and family groups. 47They counted men from 30 to 50 years old who would serve and carry loads in the tent of meeting. 48There were 8,580 men. 49Moses counted each man and gave him his work and load, as the LORD had told him.
