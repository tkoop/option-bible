God's Glory Comes Back to the Temple

1 He led me to the gate that faces east. 2 I saw the glory of the God of Israel coming from the east. His voice was like the sound of many waters. The earth shone with His glory. 3 It looked like the vision I saw when He came to destroy the city. It also looked like what I saw by the Kebar River. So I fell on my face. 4 The glory of the the LORD came into the temple through the east gate. 5 The Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court. The glory of the the LORD filled the temple. 6 A man stood beside me. I heard someone speak to me from the temple.

God Speaks to Ezekiel

7 He said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place where My feet rest. I will live here among the people of Israel forever. The house of Israel and their kings will no longer make My holy name unclean by their unfaithful ways or by the dead bodies of their kings on their hilltop altars. 8 When they put their doorstep next to My doorstep, and their door frame next to My door frame, with only a wall between us, they made My holy name unclean by the bad things they did. So I destroyed them in My anger. 9 Now let them put their unfaithful ways and the dead bodies of their kings far from Me. Then I will live among them forever.

10 "You, son of man, tell the people of Israel about this temple. Then they will be ashamed of their sins. Let them measure its plan. 11 If they are ashamed of everything they have done, then show them the design of the temple. Show them its exits and entrances. Show them all its shapes and rules. Write it down where they can see it. Then they will keep all its rules and do them. 12 This is the law of the temple: The whole area around it on top of the mountain is most holy. Yes, this is the law of the temple."

The Altar's Size

13 These are the measurements of the altar in cubits. One cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth. The gutter is one cubit deep and one cubit wide. It has a rim one span wide around its edge. This is the height of the altar. 14 From the gutter on the ground to the lower ledge is two cubits. The ledge is one cubit wide. From the lower ledge to the upper ledge is four cubits. That ledge is one cubit wide. 15 The top of the altar is four cubits high. Four horns stick up from it. 16 The top is square. It is twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide. 17 The ledge around it is also square. It is fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide. It has a rim half a cubit wide all around. Its gutter is one cubit wide all around. Its steps face east.

Getting the Altar Ready

18 He said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord the LORD says: These are the rules for the altar on the day it is built. Offer burnt offerings on it, and sprinkle blood on it. 19 Give a young bull as a sin offering to the Levite priests from Zadok's family who come near to serve Me, says the Lord the LORD. 20 Take some of the blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge, and all around the border. This will clean the altar and pay for its sin. 21 Take the bull for the sin offering and burn it in the place set aside for the temple, outside the holy place.

22 "On the second day, offer a male goat with no flaws as a sin offering. Clean the altar the same way it was cleaned with the bull. 23 When the cleaning is finished, offer a young bull and a ram from the flock. Both must have no flaws. 24 Bring them to the the LORD. The priests will throw salt on them and offer them as a burnt offering to the the LORD.

25 "For seven days, make a goat as a sin offering each day. Also offer a young bull and a ram from the flock. Both must have no flaws. 26 For seven days, the priests will pay for the altar's sin, clean it, and set it apart for God. 27 When the days are over, on the eighth day and after that, the priests may offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings on the altar. Then I will accept you, declares the Lord the LORD."
