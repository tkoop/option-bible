God Speaks to Ezekiel

1 The word of the the LORD came to me.

God Says the Old Saying Is Wrong

“2 What do you mean by using this saying in Israel: ‘Parents do wrong, and their children get hurt’? 3 As surely as I live, declares the Lord the LORD, you will not use this saying in Israel anymore.

Everyone Answers for Their Own Sins

“4 Look, every person belongs to Me. Fathers and sons both belong to Me. The person who sins is the one who will die.

A Good Man Lives

“5 Suppose a man does what is right and fair. 6 He does not eat at altars on the hills. He does not look to Israel's false gods. He does not sleep with another man's wife. He does not go near a woman during her period. 7 He does not hurt anyone. He gives back what he borrowed. He does not steal. He gives food to the hungry. He gives clothes to people with no clothes. 8 He does not lend money to make extra money from it. He stays away from wrong. He makes fair choices between people. 9 He follows My rules and keeps My laws. He is a good man. He will live, declares the Lord the LORD.

A Bad Son Dies

“10 But suppose he has a son who hurts people and kills. This son does one of these bad things. 11 The father did not do these things. But the son eats at altars on the hills. He sleeps with another man's wife. 12 He hurts poor and needy people. He steals. He does not give back what he took. He looks to false gods. He does very wrong things. 13 He lends money to make extra money. He will not live. He did all these bad things, so he will die. His death is his own fault.

A Good Grandson Lives

“14 Now suppose this son has a son. This grandson sees all the sins his father did. But he does not do the same things. 15 He does not eat at altars on the hills. He does not look to Israel's false gods. He does not sleep with another man's wife. 16 He does not hurt anyone. He does not keep what he borrowed. He does not steal. He gives food to the hungry. He gives clothes to people with no clothes. 17 He stays away from wrong. He does not lend money to make extra money. He follows My laws. He will not die for his father's wrong. He will live. 18 His father hurt people and stole from his brother. He did wrong among his people. So he will die for his wrong.

God Answers Their Question

“19 But you ask, ‘Why doesn't the son pay for the father's wrong?’ Since the son did what is right and fair, and kept My laws, he will live. 20 The person who sins will die. A son will not pay for his father's wrong. A father will not pay for his son's wrong. Good people get credit for their good. Bad people get blame for their bad.

Bad People Can Change

“21 But if a bad man stops doing all his sins and keeps My laws and does what is right and fair, he will live. He will not die. 22 None of his past wrongs will be remembered. Because he did right, he will live.

God Does Not Want Death

“23 Do I want bad people to die? declares the Lord the LORD. No. I want them to stop their bad ways and live.

“24 But if a good man stops doing right and does wrong, like the bad man does, will he live? No. None of his good will be remembered. He will die because he was not faithful and because he sinned.

God's Way Is Fair

“25 But you say, ‘The Lord's way is not fair.’ Listen, people of Israel: Is My way unfair? No. Your ways are unfair. 26 If a good man stops doing right and does wrong, he will die. He will die because of the wrong he did.

“27 But if a bad man stops his bad ways and does what is right and fair, he will save his life. 28 He thought about his wrongs and stopped. He will live. He will not die.

“29 But the people of Israel say, ‘The Lord's way is not fair.’ Are My ways unfair? No. Your ways are unfair.

Turn and Live

“30 So I will judge you, people of Israel. Each of you will be judged by what you do, declares the Lord the LORD. Turn back. Stop all your sins, so sin will not ruin you. 31 Throw away all the sins you have done. Get a new heart and a new spirit. Why should you die, people of Israel? 32 I do not want anyone to die, declares the Lord the LORD. So turn back and live!”
