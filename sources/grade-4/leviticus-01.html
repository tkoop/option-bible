Rules for Burnt Offerings

1 The LORD called to Moses. He spoke to him from the tent of meeting. He said, 2 "Tell the Israelites: When one of you brings an offering to the LORD, you must bring your offering from the animals. Use cattle or sheep.

Offerings from Cattle

3 "If his offering is a burnt offering from the cattle, he must bring a male animal without any flaw. He must bring it to the door of the tent of meeting. Then the LORD will accept him.

4 "He must put his hand on the head of the burnt offering. Then it will be accepted for him. It will make things right for him.

5 "He must kill the young bull in front of the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, will bring the blood. They will splash the blood all around the altar at the door of the tent of meeting.

6 "He must skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces.

7 "Aaron's sons, the priests, must put fire on the altar. They must stack wood on the fire.

8 "They must put the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood on the fire on the altar.

9 "He must wash the inside parts and the legs with water. The priest will burn all of it on the altar. It is a burnt offering. It is a gift by fire. It has a smell the LORD likes.

Offerings from Sheep or Goats

10 "If his offering is from the sheep or goats for a burnt offering, he must bring a male animal without any flaw.

11 "He must kill it on the north side of the altar in front of the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, will splash its blood all around the altar.

12 "The priest must cut it into pieces, with its head and its fat. He must put them on the wood on the fire on the altar.

13 "He must wash the inside parts and the legs with water. The priest will bring all of it and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering. It is a gift by fire. It has a smell the LORD likes.

Offerings from Birds

14 "If his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he must bring turtledoves or young pigeons.

15 "The priest will bring it to the altar. He will twist off its head and burn it on the altar. Its blood will be drained on the side of the altar.

16 "He must remove its food pouch with its feathers. He must throw it on the east side of the altar, where the ashes go.

17 "He must tear it open by the wings, but not tear it all the way apart. The priest will burn it on the altar on the wood on the fire. It is a burnt offering. It is a gift by fire. It has a smell the LORD likes."
