Joseph Hears Jacob Is Sick

1 Joseph was told, "Your father is sick." So Joseph took his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, and went to him.

2 Jacob was told, "Your son Joseph is coming to you." Israel sat up in bed and felt strong.

Jacob Remembers God's Promise

3 Jacob said to Joseph, "God Almighty showed Himself to me at Luz in Canaan and blessed me. 4 He said to me, 'I will give you many children. I will make you into a big group of people. I will give this land to your children after you to keep forever.'"

5 Jacob said, "Now your two sons born to you in Egypt before I came to you in Egypt are mine. Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, just like Reuben and Simeon are mine. 6 Any children you have after them will be yours. They will be called by their brothers' names when the land is shared. 7 When I came from Paddan, Rachel died on the way in Canaan. We were not far from Ephrath. I buried her there on the way to Ephrath. That place is Bethlehem."

Joseph Brings His Sons to Jacob

8 Israel saw Joseph's sons and said, "Who are these?"

9 Joseph said to his father, "They are my sons. God gave them to me here. Please bring them to me so I can bless them."

10 Israel's eyes were weak because of age. He could not see well. Joseph brought them close. Israel kissed them and hugged them.

Jacob Blesses Ephraim and Manasseh

11 Israel said to Joseph, "I did not think I would see you again. But now God has let me see your children too."

12 Joseph took them from Israel's knees. Then Joseph bowed down with his face to the ground.

13 Joseph took both boys. He put Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel's left. He put Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel's right. Then he brought them to Israel.

14 Israel reached out his right hand and put it on Ephraim's head. Ephraim was the younger. He put his left hand on Manasseh's head. He crossed his hands, even though Manasseh was born first.

15 He blessed Joseph and said, "God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac walked, God who has been my shepherd all my life to this day, 16 may the angel who saved me from all harm bless these boys. May my name be on them, and the names of my fathers Abraham and Isaac. May they grow into a big group of people in the land."

17 When Joseph saw that his father put his right hand on Ephraim's head, he was upset. He took his father's hand to move it from Ephraim's head to Manasseh's head.

18 Joseph said to his father, "No, my father. This one was born first. Put your right hand on his head."

19 But his father refused and said, "I know, my son, I know. He will also become a people. He will also be great. But his younger brother will be greater than he. His children will become many nations."

20 He blessed them that day and said, "People in Israel will use your name when they bless. They will say, 'May God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh.'" So he put Ephraim before Manasseh.

Jacob's Last Words to Joseph

21 Israel said to Joseph, "I am about to die. But God will be with you. He will bring you back to the land of your fathers. 22 Also, I give you one more share than your brothers. I took it from the Amorites with my sword and my bow."
