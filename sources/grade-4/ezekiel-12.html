Ezekiel Acts Out Being Sent Away

1 The word of the LORD came to me. 2 "Son of man, you are living among a stubborn people. They have eyes to see, but they do not see. They have ears to hear, but they do not hear. They are a stubborn people. 3 So you, son of man, pack your bags to go away. In their sight, go away by day. From your place, move to another place in their sight. Maybe they will see, even though they are a stubborn people. 4 Bring out your things packed to go away by day in their sight. Then in the evening, in their sight, go out like people who are sent away. 5 In their sight, dig through the wall and bring your things out through it. 6 In their sight, lift your bag on your shoulder and take it out in the dark. Cover your face so you cannot see the land. For I have made you a sign to the people of Israel.

7 So I did as I was told. I brought out my things packed to go away by day. Then in the evening I dug through the wall with my hands. I took out my things in the dark, carrying them on my shoulder, in their sight.

The Sign Explained

8 In the morning, the word of the LORD came to me. 9 "Son of man, did not the stubborn people of Israel ask you, 'What are you doing?'"

10 "Say to them, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: This message is about the prince in Jerusalem and all the people of Israel who are there. 11 I am a sign to you. As I have done, so it will be done to them. They will go away as prisoners. 12 The prince among them will carry his things on his shoulder in the dark and go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out. He will cover his face so that he cannot see the land. 13 I will catch him in My net, and he will be trapped. I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Babylonians, but he will not see it. There he will die. 14 I will scatter to every wind all who are around him. I will scatter his helpers and all his soldiers. I will chase them with swords. 15 Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I scatter them among the nations and spread them through the countries. 16 But I will spare a few of them from the sword, hunger, and sickness. They can tell of all their bad ways among the nations where they go. Then they will know that I am the LORD.'"

A Warning About Food and Water

17 The word of the LORD came to me. 18 "Son of man, eat your food with shaking. Drink your water with fear and worry. 19 Say to the people of the land, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says about those who live in Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread in worry and drink their water in fear. This is because their land will be empty of everything in it. That is because of the wrong done by all who live there. 20 The cities where people live will be destroyed, and the land will become empty. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

The Days Are Near

21 The word of the LORD came to me. 22 "Son of man, what is this saying you have in the land of Israel: 'The days go by, and the visions never come true'?"

23 "So say to them, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: I will put an end to this saying in Israel. They will not say it anymore. Instead, they will say, "The days are near, and every vision will come true." 24 For there will be no more false visions or sweet lies in Israel. 25 For I am the LORD. I will speak, and what I speak will happen. It will not be put off any longer. In your days, you stubborn people, I will speak a word and do it, says the Lord the LORD.'"

The Vision Will Soon Come True

26 The word of the LORD came to me. 27 "Look! The people of Israel say, 'The vision he sees is for far in the future.' 28 So say to them, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: None of My words will be put off any longer. The word I speak will be done, says the Lord the LORD.'"
