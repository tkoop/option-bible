Praise for God's City

1 A song. A psalm for the sons of Korah. The LORD is great. People should praise Him very much in the city of our God. It is His holy mountain. 2 It is beautiful and high. It makes all the earth happy. It is like the mountains in the far north. It is Mount Zion, the city of the great King. 3 God is in its strong towers. He has shown that He is a safe place.

God Stops the Kings

4 Look! The kings came together. They all marched by together. 5 They saw it and were amazed. They ran away in fear. 6 They shook with fear there. Pain hit them like a woman having a baby. 7 You broke the ships from Tarshish with an east wind. 8 We heard about these things, and now we have seen them in the city of the LORD of heaven's armies, in the city of our God. God will keep it strong forever. Selah.

We Think About God's Love

9 In Your temple, O God, we think about Your kind love. 10 Like Your name, O God, Your praise reaches all over the world. You always do what is right. 11 Mount Zion is glad. The people of Judah are happy because you judge fairly.

Tell Others About God's City

12 Walk around Zion. Count its towers. 13 Look closely at its walls. Look at its strong towers. Then you can tell the next group of people. 14 This God is our God forever. He will lead us even after we die.
