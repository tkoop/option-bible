Solomon Builds the Temple

1 It was 480 years after the Israelites left Egypt. In the fourth year of Solomon's rule over Israel, in the month of Ziv, the second month, Solomon began to build the the LORD's house. 2 The house King Solomon built for the the LORD was 60 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. 3 The front porch was 20 cubits long across the width of the house. It was 10 cubits wide in front of the house. 4 He made windows with narrow slats for the house.

5 Against the wall of the house, he built a side building around the walls. It went around the main hall and the holiest room. He made side rooms all around. 6 The lowest floor was 5 cubits wide. The middle floor was 6 cubits wide. The third floor was 7 cubits wide. He made ledges around the outside of the house so the beams would not be fastened into the walls. 7 The house was built with finished stones cut at the stone yard. No hammer, axe, or iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built. 8 The door to the middle floor was on the right side of the house. Winding stairs led up to the middle floor, and from the middle floor to the third floor. 9 So he built the house and finished it. He covered it with beams and cedar boards. 10 He built the side building all around the house. Each floor was 5 cubits high. He fastened it to the house with cedar beams.

God Speaks to Solomon

11 Then the word of the the LORD came to Solomon. He said, 12 "This house you are building—if you walk in My rules, do what is right, and keep all My commands by living by them, then I will do what I promised your father David. 13 I will live among the Israelites. I will not leave My people Israel."

14 So Solomon built the house and finished it.

Inside the Temple

15 He lined the inside walls of the house with cedar boards. He covered the walls from the floor to the ceiling with wood. He covered the floor with cypress boards. 16 He set apart 20 cubits at the back of the house with cedar boards from floor to ceiling. This made the inner room, the holiest room. 17 The main hall in front of the inner room was 40 cubits long. 18 The inside of the house was cedar. It was carved with gourds and open flowers. Everything was cedar. No stone could be seen. 19 He got the inner room ready inside the house. He put the ark of the the LORD's promise there. 20 The inner room was 20 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 20 cubits high. He covered it with pure gold. He also covered the cedar altar with gold. 21 Solomon covered the inside of the house with pure gold. He put gold chains across the front of the inner room and covered it with gold. 22 He covered the whole house with gold until it was finished. He also covered with gold the whole altar in the inner room.

The Winged Creatures

23 In the inner room, he made two winged creatures from olive wood. Each was 10 cubits high. 24 One wing of the first creature was 5 cubits. The other wing was 5 cubits. From the tip of one wing to the tip of the other was 10 cubits. 25 The second creature was also 10 cubits. Both creatures had the same size and shape. 26 The first creature was 10 cubits high, and so was the second. 27 He put the creatures in the inner room. Their wings were spread out. The wing of one touched one wall. The wing of the other touched the other wall. Their wings touched each other in the middle of the room. 28 He covered the creatures with gold.

Carvings and Floors

29 He carved all the walls of the house all around. He carved winged creatures, palm trees, and open flowers. He did this in the inner and outer rooms. 30 He covered the floor of the house with gold. He did this in the inner and outer rooms.

Doors and the Courtyard

31 For the door of the inner room, he made doors of olive wood. The doorframe was one-fifth of the wall. 32 On the two olive-wood doors, he carved winged creatures, palm trees, and open flowers. He covered them with gold. He spread gold over the creatures and palm trees. 33 He made olive-wood doorposts for the door of the main hall. They were one-fourth of the wall. 34 He made two doors of cypress wood. Each door had two folding parts. 35 He carved winged creatures, palm trees, and open flowers on them. He covered them with gold. The gold was spread evenly over the carved work. 36 He built the inner courtyard with three rows of cut stone and one row of cedar beams.

When the Temple Was Finished

37 The foundation of the the LORD's house was laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv. 38 In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, the eighth month, the house was finished. It was finished in all its parts and plans. He spent seven years building it.
