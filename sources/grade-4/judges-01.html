After Joshua Dies

1 After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the LORD, "Who will be the first to go up and fight for us against the Canaanites?"

2 The LORD said, "Judah will go first. I have given the land to him."

Judah and Simeon Fight Together

3 Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Come with me to fight the Canaanites in my part of the land. Then I will go with you to fight in your part." So Simeon went with him.

4 Judah went to fight. The LORD gave them victory over the Canaanites and Perizzites. They killed ten thousand men at Bezek. 5 They found Adoni-Bezek in Bezek. They fought him and beat the Canaanites and Perizzites. 6 Adoni-Bezek ran away, but they chased and caught him. They cut off his thumbs and big toes.

7 Adoni-Bezek said, "Seventy kings had their thumbs and big toes cut off. They picked up food scraps under my table. I did the same to others, and now God has paid me back." Then they took him to Jerusalem, where he died.

8 The men of Judah fought Jerusalem. They took the city, killed people with swords, and burned it.

9 Later, Judah's men went to fight Canaanites in the hills, the southern desert, and the lowlands. 10 Judah went to Hebron, which used to be called Kiriath-arba. They beat Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. 11 Then they went to Debir, which used to be called Kiriath-sepher.

Caleb and His Daughter

12 Caleb said, "I will give my daughter Achsah in marriage to the man who takes Kiriath-sepher." 13 Othniel, son of Kenaz and Caleb's younger brother, took the city. So Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah to marry.

14 When she came, she told her husband to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey, and Caleb asked her, "What do you want?"

15 She said, "Please bless me. You gave me dry land in the south, so give me springs of water too." Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs.

16 The Kenites, from Moses' father-in-law's family, went with Judah's men from the city of palm trees to the desert of Judah near Arad. They went to live among the people there.

17 Judah and his brother Simeon fought the Canaanites in Zephath. They destroyed the city completely and called it Hormah. 18 Judah also took Gaza and its land, Ashkelon and its land, and Ekron and its land.

19 The LORD was with Judah. They took the hills, but they could not push out the people in the valleys, because those people had iron chariots. 20 As Moses had said, they gave Hebron to Caleb. He drove out the three sons of Anak from there.

21 The people of Benjamin did not drive out the Jebusites in Jerusalem. So the Jebusites still live there with them today.

Joseph's Family Fights Bethel

22 The family of Joseph also went to fight Bethel, and the the LORD was with them. 23 They sent scouts to spy on Bethel, which used to be called Luz. 24 The scouts saw a man leaving the city. They said, "Please show us how to get into the city, and we will treat you well." 25 He showed them the way in. They killed the people of the city with swords, but they let the man and his whole family go free. 26 The man went to the land of the Hittites and built a city. He called it Luz, and it still has that name today.

Other Tribes Do Not Drive Out Enemies

27 Manasseh did not drive out the people of Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, and Megiddo, or their nearby towns, because the Canaanites were set on staying in that land. 28 When Israel grew strong, they made the Canaanites work as slaves, but they never drove them out completely.

29 Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanites in Gezer, so the Canaanites kept living among them there. 30 Zebulun did not drive out the people of Kitron and Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and were made to work.

31 Asher did not drive out the people of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob. 32 So the people of Asher lived among the Canaanites, the people already in the land, because they did not drive them out. 33 Naphtali did not drive out the people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath. They lived among the Canaanites, the people already in the land. The people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath were made to work.

34 The Amorites pushed the people of Dan into the hills and would not let them go down into the valley. 35 The Amorites were set on staying in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. But when Joseph's family grew strong, they made them work. 36 The Amorites' border ran from the pass of Akrabbim, from the rock, and upward.
