David Asks God for Fast Help

1 God, save me! The LORD, hurry to help me.

David's Enemies Will Be Stopped

2 Let the people who want to kill me feel ashamed and confused. Let those who want to hurt me turn back and feel ashamed. 3 Let the people who say, “Ha! Ha!” turn back because they feel ashamed.

People Who Trust God Will Praise Him

4 Let all who look for You be happy and glad in You. Let those who love Your saving help always say, “God is great!”

David Needs Help Now

5 But I am poor and need help. God, hurry to me! You are my help and the One who saves me. The LORD, do not wait.
