The King Makes a Gold Statue

1 King Nebuchadnezzar made a gold statue. It was about 90 feet tall and 9 feet wide. He set it up in the flat land of Dura in Babylon. 2 King Nebuchadnezzar sent for all the leaders in his land. He wanted the top leaders, bosses, governors, helpers, money keepers, judges, and all other officials. They were to come to the special day for the statue. 3 All these leaders came. They stood in front of the statue.

The Rule About the Statue

4 A man shouted, "Listen, all you people, nations, and languages! 5 When you hear horns, flutes, harps, and all kinds of music, you must fall down. You must worship the gold statue that the king set up. 6 Anyone who does not fall down and worship will be thrown right away into a hot, burning fire."

7 So when all the people heard the music, they fell down. They worshiped the gold statue.

Some Men Tell on the Three Friends

8 Then some Babylonian men came forward. They accused the Jewish men.

9 They said to King Nebuchadnezzar, "Long live the king! 10 You, O king, made a rule. You said that every person who hears the music must fall down and worship the gold statue. 11 You also said that anyone who does not fall down and worship will be thrown into the hot fire. 12 There are some Jewish men you put in charge of Babylon. Their names are Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. These men do not listen to you, O king. They do not serve your gods. They do not worship your gold statue."

The King Gets Angry

13 Then King Nebuchadnezzar became very angry. He ordered Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to be brought to him. They brought the men to the king.

14 Nebuchadnezzar said to them, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is it true that you do not serve my gods? Is it true that you do not worship the gold statue I set up? 15 Now, when you hear the music, be ready to fall down and worship the statue I made. If you do, all is well. But if you do not, you will be thrown into the hot fire. And what god can save you from my hand?"

The Three Friends Answer

16 Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered the king, "O King Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to answer you about this. 17 If our God can save us from the hot fire and from your hand, O king, He will save us. 18 But if He does not, you must know this, O king: We will not serve your gods. We will not worship your gold statue."

The Fire Is Made Hotter

19 Then Nebuchadnezzar was full of anger. His face changed. He ordered the fire made seven times hotter than usual. 20 He told some strong soldiers in his army to tie up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He told them to throw the men into the fire. 21 So the men were tied up. They still had on their coats, pants, hats, and other clothes. Then they were thrown into the fire. 22 The king's order was harsh, and the fire was very hot. The flames killed the soldiers who took Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to the fire. 23 The three men fell into the fire. They were still tied up.

Four Men in the Fire

24 Then King Nebuchadnezzar was shocked. He jumped up. He asked his helpers, "Did we not throw three tied-up men into the fire?"

"Yes, O king," they answered.

25 He said, "Look! I see four men. They are walking in the fire. They are not hurt. The fourth looks like a son of the gods."

26 Nebuchadnezzar went near the door of the fire. He said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out!" Then they came out of the fire.

27 The leaders, bosses, governors, and the king's helpers came together. They saw that the fire had not hurt the men. Their hair was not burned. Their clothes were not changed. They did not even smell like fire.

The King Praises Their God

28 Nebuchadnezzar said, "Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! He sent His angel. He saved His servants who trusted in Him. They did not obey the king's order. They gave up their bodies instead of serving or worshiping any god except their God. 29 So I make this rule: If any person, from any people, nation, or language, says anything bad against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that person will be cut to pieces. His house will be torn down. No other god can save like this."

30 Then the king gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego more honor in Babylon.
