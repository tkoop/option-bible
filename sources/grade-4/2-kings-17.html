Hoshea Rules Israel

1 Hoshea son of Elah became king of Israel in Samaria. This was in the twelfth year that Ahaz was king of Judah. Hoshea ruled for nine years. 2 He did what was wrong in the eyes of the the LORD. But he was not as bad as the kings of Israel before him.

3 Shalmaneser, king of Assyria, came against him. Hoshea served him and paid him a yearly payment. 4 But the king of Assyria found out that Hoshea had a secret plan. Hoshea had sent messengers to So, king of Egypt. He also stopped paying the yearly payment to Assyria. So the king of Assyria arrested him and put him in prison. 5 The king of Assyria attacked the whole land. He went up to Samaria and surrounded it for three years. 6 In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria took Samaria. He carried the people of Israel away to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

Why Israel Was Punished

7 This happened because the people of Israel sinned against the the LORD their God. He had brought them out of Egypt. He saved them from Pharaoh, king of Egypt. But they worshiped other gods. 8 They followed the ways of the nations that the the LORD had driven out ahead of them. They also followed the ways that the kings of Israel had made. 9 The people of Israel did things in secret that were not right against the the LORD their God. They built altars on high places in all their cities. They built them from the watchtower to the walled city. 10 They set up stone pillars and wooden poles for the goddess Asherah on every high hill and under every green tree. 11 They burned incense on all the high places. They did this like the nations the the LORD had sent away before them. They did evil things that made the the LORD angry. 12 They served idols, even though the the LORD had said, "Do not do this."

13 The LORD warned Israel and Judah. He used all His prophets. He said, "Turn from your evil ways. Keep My commands and rules. Obey all the law I gave your fathers. I sent it to you through My servants the prophets."

14 But they did not listen. They were stubborn, like their fathers. Their fathers had not believed in the the LORD their God. 15 They rejected His rules and the agreement He made with their fathers. They ignored His warnings. They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves. They copied the nations around them, even though the the LORD had told them not to do those things. 16 They left all the commands of the the LORD their God. They made two metal calves. They made an Asherah pole. They bowed down to all the stars in the sky. They served Baal. 17 They even made their sons and daughters pass through fire. They used magic and spells. They sold themselves to do evil in the eyes of the the LORD. They made Him angry.

18 So the the LORD was very angry with Israel. He took them away from His sight. Only the tribe of Judah was left. 19 But Judah also did not keep the commands of the the LORD their God. They followed the ways that Israel had made. 20 So the the LORD rejected all the people of Israel. He punished them. He handed them over to robbers. At last, He threw them out of His sight. 21 He had torn Israel away from the family of David. The people made Jeroboam son of Nebat their king. Jeroboam led Israel away from following the the LORD. He made them sin a great sin. 22 The people of Israel kept doing all the sins of Jeroboam. They did not stop. 23 At last, the the LORD removed Israel from His sight, just as He had said through all His servants the prophets. Israel was carried away from their own land to Assyria, and they are still there today.

New People Move to Samaria

24 The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Ava, Hamath, and Sepharvaim. He settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the people of Israel. They took over Samaria and lived in its cities. 25 At first, they did not fear the the LORD. So the the LORD sent lions among them. The lions killed some of them.

26 The settlers said to the king of Assyria, "The nations you moved and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the rules of the God of this land. That is why He sent lions among them. The lions are killing them, because they do not know the rules of the God of this land."

27 Then the king of Assyria gave this order: "Take one of the priests you carried away from there. Let him go back and live there. Let him teach them the rules of the God of this land."

28 So one of the priests who had been carried away from Samaria came back. He lived in Bethel. He taught them how to fear the the LORD.

They Worship Many Gods

29 But each nation still made its own gods. They put them in the high places that the people of Samaria had made. Each nation did this in the cities where they lived. 30 The men of Babylon made Succoth Benoth. The men of Cuth made Nergal. The men of Hamath made Ashima. 31 The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32 They also feared the the LORD. But they chose priests from among themselves. These priests offered sacrifices for them in the houses on the high places. 33 They feared the the LORD, but they also served their own gods. They followed the ways of the nations they had come from. 34 To this day, they still do as they did at first. They do not truly fear the the LORD. They do not follow the rules, laws, teachings, or commands that the the LORD gave to the children of Jacob. He named them Israel.

35 The LORD had made an agreement with them. He commanded them, "Do not fear other gods. Do not bow down to them. Do not serve them. Do not offer sacrifices to them." 36 Instead, fear the the LORD. He is the One who brought you up out of Egypt with great power and a strong arm. Bow down to Him. Offer sacrifices to Him. 37 Keep the rules, laws, teachings, and commands that He wrote for you. Do them always. Do not fear other gods. 38 Do not forget the agreement He made with you. Do not fear other gods. 39 Fear the the LORD your God. He will save you from the hand of all your enemies.

40 But they did not listen. They kept doing what they had done before. 41 So these nations feared the the LORD, but they also served their carved idols. Their children and grandchildren did the same. They still do it today, just as their fathers did.
