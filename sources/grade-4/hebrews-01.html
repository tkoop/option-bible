God Spoke Through His Son

1 Long ago, God spoke to our fathers in many ways through His prophets. 2 But in these last days, He has spoken to us through His Son. God made His Son the one who will get all things. He also made the world through His Son.

3 The Son shines with God's bright light. He is just like God. He holds everything together by His strong word. After He took away our sins, He sat down next to God in heaven.

4 So the Son became much greater than the angels. The name He received is much better than their names.

The Son Is Greater Than Angels

5 God never said to any angel, "You are My Son. Today I have become Your Father." He also said, "I will be His Father, and He will be My Son."

6 And when God brings His first Son into the world, He says, "Let all of God's angels bow down to Him."

7 About the angels, God says, "He makes His angels like the wind and His servants like fire."

The Son Rules Forever

8 But about the Son, God says, "Your throne, O God, will last forever. Your kingdom will be ruled the right way. 9 You have loved what is right and hated what is wrong. So God, Your God, has poured oil of joy on You more than on anyone else."

10 He also says, "Lord, in the beginning You made the earth. The heavens are the work of Your hands. 11 They will end, but You will stay. They will all grow old like clothes. 12 You will roll them up like a coat. They will be changed like clothes. But You are the same, and Your years will never end."

13 God never said to any angel, "Sit in the place of honor next to Me until I make Your enemies bow down to You."

Angels Help God's People

14 Angels are spirits who serve God. He sends them to help those who will be saved.
