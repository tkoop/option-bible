Kings in the North Join Together

1 When Jabin, king of Hazor, heard the news, he sent word to Jobab, king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph. 2 He also sent word to the kings in the north who lived in the hills, in the dry valley south of the Sea of Galilee, in the lowlands, and by the sea near Dor. 3 He sent word to the Canaanites in the east and west, to the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hills, and to the Hivites under Mount Hermon in the land of Mizpah. 4 They came out with all their armies. There were so many people that they were like sand on the beach. They had many horses and war carts. 5 All these kings joined together. They came and set up camp by the waters of Merom to fight Israel.

The LORD Helps Joshua Win

6 The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them. Tomorrow at this time, I will hand all of them over to Israel to be killed. You must make their horses unable to run and burn their war carts."

7 Joshua and all his soldiers came upon them suddenly by the waters of Merom and attacked them. 8 The LORD gave them into Israel's hands. Israel struck them and chased them to Greater Sidon, to Misrephoth-maim, and east to the Valley of Mizpah. They struck them until no one was left alive. 9 Joshua did what the LORD said. He made their horses unable to run and burned their war carts.

10 At that time Joshua turned back and took Hazor. He killed its king with a sword. Hazor had been the most important of all those kingdoms. 11 They killed everyone in Hazor with swords. They destroyed them completely. No one was left alive. Then Joshua burned Hazor.

12 Joshua took all the cities of those kings. He killed the people and kings with swords. He destroyed them completely, just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had told him to do. 13 But Israel did not burn the cities that were still standing on their old hills. Joshua burned only Hazor. 14 The Israelites took all the treasures and animals from those cities. But they killed every person with swords until no one was left alive. 15 Joshua did everything just as the LORD had told Moses, and Moses had told Joshua. He did not leave out anything the LORD had told Moses to do.

Joshua Takes the Land

16 So Joshua took all that land. He took the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowlands, the Arabah, the hill country of Israel, and its lowlands. 17 He took land from Mount Halak, which goes up toward Seir, all the way to Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings and killed them. 18 Joshua fought with all these kings for a long time. 19 No city made peace with Israel except the Hivites who lived in Gibeon. Israel took all the rest in battle. 20 The LORD had made them stubborn. He did this so they would fight Israel and be destroyed completely. He did not show them mercy. He wanted to destroy them, just as He had told Moses.

21 At that time Joshua went and destroyed the Anakites from the hill country, from Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and Israel. He destroyed them and their cities completely. 22 No Anakites were left in Israel except in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod. 23 Joshua took the whole land, just as the LORD had told Moses. He gave it to Israel to own, by their tribes. Then the land had peace from war.
