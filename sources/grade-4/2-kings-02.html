Elijah Is Taken to Heaven

1 When the LORD was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a storm, Elijah and Elisha left Gilgal.

2 Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here. The LORD has sent me to Bethel."

But Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you."

So they went down to Bethel.

3 The young prophets at Bethel came out to Elisha. They said, "Do you know that the LORD will take your master from you today?"

Elisha said, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

4 Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here. The LORD has sent me to Jericho."

Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you."

So they came to Jericho.

5 The young prophets at Jericho came to Elisha. They said, "Do you know that the LORD will take your master from you today?"

He said, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

6 Elijah said, "Please stay here. The LORD has sent me to the Jordan River."

Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you."

So the two of them walked on.

7 Fifty men from the young prophets went too. They stood far away and watched. Elijah and Elisha stood by the Jordan River.

8 Elijah took his cloak and rolled it up. He hit the water with it. The water split to the right and left. The two men walked across on dry ground.

9 After they crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I can do for you before I am taken from you."

Elisha said, "Please let me have a double share of your spirit."

10 Elijah said, "You have asked a hard thing. But if you see me when I am taken from you, it will happen. If not, it will not."

11 As they kept walking and talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire came between them. Elijah went up to heaven in a storm.

12 Elisha saw it and cried out, "My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!" Then he could not see Elijah anymore. He grabbed his clothes and tore them in two.

Elisha Becomes the Prophet

13 Elisha picked up the cloak that had fallen from Elijah. He went back and stood by the Jordan River.

14 He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen. He hit the water and said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" He hit the water again. The water split to the right and left. Elisha crossed over.

15 The young prophets from Jericho saw Elisha from far away. They said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed to the ground.

16 They said to him, "Look, we have fifty strong men. Please let them go look for your master. Maybe the Spirit of the LORD carried him away and threw him on a mountain or in a valley."

Elisha said, "Do not send them."

17 They kept asking until he gave in. Then he said, "Send them." So they sent fifty men. They looked for three days but did not find Elijah.

18 When they came back to him in Jericho, he said to them, "Didn't I tell you not to go?"

Elisha Makes the Water Good

19 The men of the city said to Elisha, "Look, sir, this city is a good place to live, as you can see. But the water is bad, and the land does not grow crops."

20 Elisha said, "Bring me a new bowl and put salt in it." They brought it to him.

21 He went out to the spring of water and threw salt into it. He said, "This is what the LORD says: I have healed this water. It will not cause death or bad land anymore."

22 The water was healed, just as Elisha had said. It is still good today.

Boys Tease Elisha

23 He left there and went up to Bethel. As he walked up the road, some young boys came out of the city. They made fun of him and said, "Go up, bald head! Go up, bald head!"

24 He turned around and looked at them. He cursed them in the name of the LORD. Then two female bears came out of the woods and attacked forty-two of the boys.

25 He went from there to Mount Carmel. Then he went back to Samaria.
