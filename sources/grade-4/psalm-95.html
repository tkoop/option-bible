Come and Praise the LORD

1 Come, let us sing to the LORD. Let us shout to the Rock who saves us. 2 Let us come to Him with thanks. Let us sing happy songs to Him. 3 The LORD is a great God. He is a great King over all gods. 4 He holds the deep places of the earth. The tall mountains are His too. 5 The sea is His, because He made it. His hands made the dry land. 6 Come, let us bow down and worship. Let us kneel before the LORD, our Maker.

Listen to God's Warning

7 He is our God. We are His people. We are like sheep He cares for. Today, if you hear His voice,

8 “Do not be stubborn like the people at Meribah. Do not be stubborn like that day at Massah in the desert. 9 Your fathers tested and tried Me there, even though they saw what I did.

10 “For forty years I was angry with those people. I said, ‘They are people whose hearts always go the wrong way. They do not know how I want them to live.’ 11 So I made a strong promise in My anger: ‘They will never come into the place of rest I promised.’”
