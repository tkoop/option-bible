Benjamin's Family

1 Benjamin had Bela as his first son. Ashbel was his second son. Aharah was his third son. 2 Nohah was his fourth son. Rapha was his fifth son.

3 Bela's sons were Addar, Gera, and Abihud. 4 He also had Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah. 5 He also had Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

6 These were Ehud's sons. They were leaders of families who lived in Geba. They were taken to Manahath.

7 Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera took them away. Gera was the father of Uzza and Ahihud.

More Benjamin Families

8 Shaharaim had children in Moab after he sent his wives Hushim and Baara away. 9 From Hushim he had Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcam. 10 He also had Jeuz, Sakiah, and Mirmah. These sons were leaders of families.

11 From Hushim he had Abitub and Elpaal. 12 Elpaal's sons were Eber, Misham, and Shemed. Elpaal built Ono and Lod and the nearby towns.

13 Beriah and Shema were leaders of families in Aijalon. They chased away the people of Gath.

14 Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth were leaders too. 15 Zebadiah, Arad, and Eder were leaders too.

16 Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were sons of Beriah. 17 Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, and Heber were also leaders.

18 Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were sons of Elpaal. 19 Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi were leaders. 20 Elienai, Zillethai, and Eliel were leaders too.

21 Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were sons of Shimei. 22 Ishpan, Eber, and Eliel were leaders. 23 Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan were leaders. 24 Hananiah, Elam, and Anthothijah were leaders.

25 Iphdeiah and Penuel were sons of Shashak. 26 Shamsherai, Shehariah, and Athaliah were leaders. 27 Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were sons of Jeroham.

28 All these men were leaders of families. They lived in Jerusalem with their families.

Families in Gibeon

29 Gibeon's father lived in Gibeon. His wife was Maacah. 30 His first son was Abdon. Then came Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab. 31 He also had Gedor, Ahio, and Zechar. 32 Mikloth was the father of Shimeah. They lived near their brothers in Jerusalem.

King Saul's Family

33 Ner was the father of Kish. Kish was the father of Saul. Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 34 Jonathan's son was Merib-baal. Merib-baal was the father of Micah.

35 Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. 36 Ahaz was the father of Jehoaddah. Jehoaddah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri was the father of Moza. 37 Moza was the father of Binea. Raphah was his son. Eleasah was his son. Azel was his son.

38 Azel had six sons. Their names were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were Azel's sons. 39 His brother Eshek had sons too. Ulam was the first son. Jeush was the second son. Eliphelet was the third son. 40 Ulam's sons were strong men who used bows. They had many sons and grandsons. There were 150 of them. All these were Benjamin's sons.
