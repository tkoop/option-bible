David Wants to Build a House for the LORD

1 When King David was living in his house, the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies around him. 2 The king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I live in a house made of cedar wood. But the ark of God stays in a tent." 3 Nathan said to the king, "Do everything you want. the LORD is with you."

The LORD's Promise to David

4 But that night, the word of the LORD came to Nathan. He said, 5 "Go and tell My servant David: This is what the LORD says: Will you build a house for Me to live in? 6 I have not lived in a house since the day I brought the people of Israel out of Egypt. I have moved about in a tent. 7 Everywhere I walked with all the people of Israel, I never asked any tribe I told to take care of My people Israel why they had not built Me a house of cedar."

8 "Now say this to My servant David: This is what the LORD of Armies says: I took you from the sheep pen, from following the sheep, to be ruler over My people Israel. 9 I was with you everywhere you went. I cut off all your enemies before you. I will make your name great, like the names of the great people on earth. 10 I will give a place to My people Israel. I will plant them there. They will live there and not be moved anymore. Wicked people will not hurt them again, like before. 11 From the day I put judges over My people Israel, I have given you rest from all your enemies. The LORD tells you that the LORD will make a house for you."

12 "When your days are full and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your child after you, who comes from your body. I will make his kingdom strong. 13 He will build a house for My name. I will set up his kingdom forever. 14 I will be his Father, and he will be My Son. If he sins, I will correct him with a stick and with blows from people. 15 But My faithful love will not leave him, like I took it from Saul, whom I took from before you. 16 Your house and your kingdom will last forever before you. Your throne will be set up forever."

17 Nathan told David all these words and all this vision.

David Prays to the LORD

18 Then King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, 19 "Who am I, Lord, the LORD, and what is my family, that You brought me this far? 20 This is still small in Your eyes, Lord, the LORD. You have spoken about Your servant's house for a long time. Is this how You usually treat people, Lord, the LORD? 21 You did this because of Your word and because of Your heart. You have shown Your servant this great thing."

22 "So You are great, Lord, the LORD. There is no one like You. There is no God besides You, from all we have heard. 23 Who is like Your people Israel? They are one nation on earth that God went to save for Himself. He did this to make a name for Himself. He did great and scary things for Your land, driving out nations and their gods before Your people, whom You saved from Egypt. 24 You made Your people Israel Your people forever. You, the LORD, became their God."

25 "Now, the LORD God, keep forever the word You spoke about Your servant and his house. Do what You said. 26 Let Your name be great forever. Then people will say that the LORD of Armies is God over Israel. The house of Your servant David will be set up before You. 27 You, the LORD of Armies, God of Israel, have told Your servant that You will build him a house. So Your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to You. 28 Now, Lord, the LORD, You are God. Your words are true. You have promised this good thing to Your servant. 29 Now please bless Your servant's house. Let it last forever before You. Lord, the LORD, You have spoken. Your servant's house will be blessed forever by Your blessing."
