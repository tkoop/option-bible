Balaam and Balak Make Offerings

1 Balaam said to Balak, “Build seven altars here. Get seven bulls and seven rams ready for me.” 2 Balak did what Balaam said. Balak and Balaam offered a bull and a ram on each altar.

Balaam Goes to Meet the LORD

3 Balaam said to Balak, “Stand by your burnt offering. I will go away. Maybe the LORD will come to meet me. I will tell you what He shows me.” Then Balaam went to a high, quiet place.

4 God met Balaam. Balaam said, “I have set up seven altars. I offered a bull and a ram on each one.”

5 The LORD put a message in Balaam's mouth. He said, “Go back to Balak and say this to him.”

6 Balaam went back to Balak. Balak was standing by his burnt offering. All the leaders of Moab were with him.

Balaam's First Message

7 “Balak brought me from Aram. The king of Moab brought me from the eastern hills. Balak wants me to curse Jacob and speak against Israel.

8 “How can I curse people God has not cursed? How can I speak against people the LORD has not spoken against?

9 “I see them from the top of the rocks. I look at them from the hills. Look, these people live alone. They are not counted with other nations.

10 “Who can count Jacob's dust? Who can count even a fourth of Israel? Let me die like good people. Let my end be like theirs.”

11 Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, but you have blessed them!”

12 Balaam answered, “I must say what the LORD puts in my mouth.”

13 Balak said, “Come with me to another place. You will see only part of them, not all. Curse them for me from there.”

14 Balak took Balaam to a field called Zophim, on top of Mount Pisgah. Balaam built seven altars there. He offered a bull and a ram on each altar.

15 Balaam said to Balak, “Stand here by your burnt offering. I will go over there to meet the LORD.”

16 The LORD met Balaam. He put a message in Balaam's mouth. He said, “Go back to Balak and say this to him.”

17 Balaam went back to Balak. Balak was standing by his burnt offering. The leaders of Moab were with him. Balak said, “What did the LORD say?”

Balaam's Second Message

18 “Get up, Balak, and listen. Hear me, son of Zippor.

19 “God is not a man, so He does not lie. He is not a human, so He does not change His mind. If He says something, He does it. If He speaks, He makes it happen.

20 “Look, I was told to bless. He has blessed them, and I cannot change it.

21 “God has not seen wrong in Jacob. He has not seen trouble in Israel. The LORD their God is with them. They shout like a king is among them.

22 “God brought them out of Egypt. He is strong like a wild ox.

23 “No magic will work against Jacob. No tricks will work against Israel. People will say about Jacob and Israel, ‘Look what God has done!’

24 “Look, these people rise up like a lioness. They are like a lion that does not rest until it eats its food and drinks the blood of those it killed.”

25 Balak said to Balaam, “Do not curse them at all. Do not bless them at all.”

26 Balaam answered, “I told you I must do everything the LORD says.”

27 Balak said, “Come, I will take you to another place. Maybe God will be happy if you curse them for me from there.”

28 Balak took Balaam to the top of Mount Peor. It looks out over the empty land.

29 Balaam said to Balak, “Build seven altars here. Get seven bulls and seven rams ready for me.”

30 Balak did what Balaam said. He offered a bull and a ram on each altar.
