God Calls the Whole Earth

1 A song by Asaph. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and calls the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets. 2 From Zion, so beautiful, God shines out. 3 Our God comes and will not stay quiet. Fire burns in front of Him. A big storm blows all around Him. 4 He calls to the sky above and to the earth. He will judge His people.

God Gathers His Faithful People

5 "Bring My faithful people to Me. They made a promise to Me with an offering."

6 The sky says He is right. God Himself is the judge. Selah.

God Speaks to His People

7 "Listen, My people, and I will speak. Israel, I will speak against you. I am God, your God. 8 I am not scolding you for your offerings. I always see your burnt offerings. 9 I do not need a bull from your house or goats from your pens. 10 Every wild animal in the forest is Mine. The cattle on a thousand hills are Mine. 11 I know every bird in the mountains. The animals in the fields are Mine. 12 If I were hungry, I would not tell you, because the whole world is Mine, and all that is in it. 13 Do I eat the meat of bulls? Do I drink the blood of goats? 14 Bring a thank offering to God. Keep your promises to the Most High. 15 Call on Me when you are in trouble. I will save you, and you will honor Me."

God Speaks to the Wicked

16 But God says to the wicked, "What right do you have to talk about My laws? Why do you take My promise with your lips? 17 You hate My teaching and throw My words behind you. 18 When you see a thief, you join him. You take part with people who cheat in marriage. 19 You use your mouth for bad things. You tie your tongue to lies. 20 You sit and speak against your brother. You tell lies about your own mother's son. 21 You did these things, and I kept quiet. You thought I was like you. But I will correct you and show you your wrongs."

A Warning and a Promise

22 "Now think about this, you who forget God, or I will tear you apart, and no one will save you. 23 The one who gives a thank offering honors Me. I will show how God saves to the one who lives right."
