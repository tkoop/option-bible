Paul Writes to Timothy

1 Paul is a worker sent by Christ Jesus. God our Savior and Christ Jesus our hope told him to do this. 2 To Timothy, my true child in faith: May you have grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

Stop False Teaching

3 When I went to Macedonia, I asked you to stay in Ephesus. There, you can tell some people not to teach wrong things. 4 They should not spend time on made-up stories or long family lists. Those things make people argue. They do not help God's work, which comes by faith. 5 The goal of this teaching is love. It comes from a clean heart, a good conscience, and real faith. 6 Some people have turned away from these things. They talk about empty things. 7 They want to be teachers of God's law. But they do not understand what they say or what they talk about.

God's Law Is Good

8 We know God's law is good when people use it the right way. 9 The law is not for good people. It is for people who break rules and do not obey. It is for people who do not honor God and who sin. It is for people who are not holy and who treat holy things badly. It is for people who hurt their father or mother. It is for murderers. 10 It is for people who do wrong sex acts, men who have sex with men, people who capture and sell others, liars, and people who lie under oath. It is also for anything else that goes against true teaching. 11 This true teaching agrees with the good news. The good news shows the greatness of the blessed God. He trusted me with this good news.

Paul Thanks God for Mercy

12 I thank Christ Jesus our Lord. He gave me strength. He saw that I could be trusted. He put me in His work. 13 Before, I said bad things about God. I hurt His people. I was rude and mean. But God was kind to me. I did those things because I did not know better. I did not believe. 14 Our Lord's grace poured over me. It came with faith and love in Christ Jesus. 15 This is a true saying. People can fully trust it: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners. I am the worst of them. 16 But God was kind to me for this reason. I was the worst sinner. Jesus Christ showed His great patience in me. He made me an example for people who would believe in Him and get life forever. 17 To the King who rules forever, who never dies, who cannot be seen, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.

Timothy's Work

18 Timothy, my child, I give you this job. Prophets spoke about you before. Use their words so you can fight the good fight. 19 Hold on to faith and a good conscience. Some people have thrown these away. They have wrecked their faith. 20 Hymenaeus and Alexander are two of them. I gave them to Satan so they would learn not to say bad things about God.
