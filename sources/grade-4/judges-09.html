Abimelech Plans to Rule

1 Abimelech was Jerubbaal's son. He went to Shechem to see his mother's brothers. He talked to them and to all his mother's family. 2 He said, "Please tell all the leaders of Shechem: Which is better for you? Should seventy men, all sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you? Or should one man rule over you? Remember, I am your own flesh and blood."

3 His mother's brothers told all these words to the leaders of Shechem. The leaders' hearts turned to Abimelech. They said, "He is our brother." 4 They gave him seventy pieces of silver from the house of Baal-berith. Abimelech used the money to hire worthless, reckless men. They followed him. 5 He went to his father's house in Ophrah. He killed his seventy brothers, Jerubbaal's sons, on one stone. But Jotham, Jerubbaal's youngest son, was left alive because he hid.

The People Make Abimelech King

6 All the leaders of Shechem and all the people of Beth-millo met together. They went to the oak tree at Shechem and made Abimelech king.

Jotham Warns the People

7 When Jotham heard this, he went and stood on top of Mount Gerizim. He raised his voice and cried out.

He said, "Hear me, leaders of Shechem, that God may hear you.

8 "The trees went to choose a king for themselves. They said to the olive tree, 'Be our king.' 9 But the olive tree said, 'Should I give up my oil that honors God and men, and go rule over the trees?' 10 The trees said to the fig tree, 'Come, be our king.' 11 The fig tree said, 'Should I give up my sweet fruit, and go rule over the trees?' 12 The trees said to the grapevine, 'Come, be our king.' 13 The grapevine said, 'Should I give up my wine that makes God and men glad, and go rule over the trees?' 14 Then all the trees said to the thornbush, 'Come, be our king.' 15 The thornbush said to the trees, 'If you truly make me king over you, come and rest in my shade. But if not, fire will come out of the thornbush and burn up the cedar trees of Lebanon.

16 "Now, if you have acted true and good in making Abimelech king, and if you have done good to Jerubbaal and his house, and have paid him back for what he did— 17 for my father fought for you. He risked his life and saved you from Midian. 18 But you rose up against my father's house today. You killed his seventy sons on one stone. You made Abimelech, the son of his slave woman, king over the leaders of Shechem, because he is your brother— 19 if you have acted true and good with Jerubbaal and his house today, then be glad with Abimelech, and let him be glad with you.

20 "But if not, let fire come out from Abimelech and burn up the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo. Let fire come out from the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo and burn up Abimelech."

21 Jotham ran away and fled. He went to Beer and lived there because of Abimelech, his brother.

22 Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.

23 God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem. The leaders of Shechem turned against Abimelech. 24 This happened so the wrong done to Jerubbaal's seventy sons would come back on them. Their blood would be on Abimelech, their brother, who killed them, and on the leaders of Shechem, who helped him kill his brothers.

25 The leaders of Shechem put men in hiding on the hills. They robbed everyone who passed by. Someone told Abimelech.

Gaal Boasts in Shechem

26 Gaal son of Ebed came with his brothers and moved to Shechem. The leaders of Shechem trusted him.

27 They went out to the fields and picked grapes. They made wine and had a party. They went into their god's house. They ate and drank. They cursed Abimelech.

28 Gaal son of Ebed said, "Who is Abimelech? Who is Shechem? Why should we serve him? Is he not Jerubbaal's son? Is Zebul not his helper? Serve the men of Hamor, Shechem's father. Why should we serve Abimelech?"

29 "If only I had these people under my hand! I would remove Abimelech." Then he said to Abimelech, "Build up your army and come out."

30 Zebul, the city ruler, heard Gaal's words. He was very angry.

31 He sent men secretly to Abimelech. He said, "Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers have come to Shechem. They are turning the city against you. 32 Come by night with your men. Hide in the fields. 33 In the morning, when the sun comes up, get up early and attack the city. When Gaal and his men come out to fight you, do what you can."

34 Abimelech and all his men got up at night. They hid around Shechem in four groups.

35 Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the city gate. Abimelech and his men got up from hiding.

36 Gaal saw the men and said to Zebul, "Look! Men are coming down from the hills."

Zebul said, "You are seeing the shadows of the hills. They look like men."

37 Gaal spoke again, "Look! Men are coming down from the middle of the land. One group is coming from the oak tree of the fortune tellers."

38 Zebul said to him, "Where is your big talk now? You said you should not serve Abimelech. Are these not the men you looked down on? Go out now and fight them."

39 Gaal went out in front of the leaders of Shechem and fought Abimelech.

40 Abimelech chased him. Gaal ran away. Many men fell dead all the way to the city gate.

41 Abimelech stayed in Arumah. Zebul drove Gaal and his brothers out of Shechem. They could not live there.

Abimelech Attacks Shechem

42 The next day the people went out to the fields. Someone told Abimelech.

43 He took his men and split them into three groups. They hid in the fields. He saw the people coming out of the city. He got up and attacked them.

44 Abimelech and his group rushed and stood at the city gate. The other two groups rushed at everyone in the fields and killed them.

45 Abimelech fought the city all day. He took the city. He killed the people in it. He tore down the city and spread salt on it.

The Tower Burns

46 All the leaders of the tower of Shechem heard this. They went into the strong room of their god's temple.

47 Someone told Abimelech that all the leaders of the tower of Shechem had gathered.

48 Abimelech and all his men went up Mount Zalmon. Abimelech took an ax. He cut a branch from a tree. He put it on his shoulder. He said to his men, "Hurry! Do what you see me do."

49 Each man cut a branch too. They followed Abimelech. They put the branches against the strong room. They set it on fire. All the people in the tower of Shechem died—about a thousand men and women.

Abimelech Dies

50 Then Abimelech went to Thebez. He camped there and took it.

51 There was a strong tower in the city. All the men, women, and leaders ran there. They shut themselves in. They went up to the roof of the tower.

52 Abimelech came to the tower and fought it. He went up to the door to burn it with fire.

53 A woman threw a piece of a millstone down on Abimelech's head. It broke his skull.

54 He quickly called his helper who carried his weapons. He said, "Draw your sword and kill me. I do not want people to say that a woman killed me." His helper stabbed him, and he died.

55 When the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, they went home.

God Pays Back

56 God paid back Abimelech for the evil he did to his father by killing his seventy brothers. 57 God also paid back the men of Shechem for their evil. The curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came true for them.
