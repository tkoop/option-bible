Trouble for leaders who hurt poor people

1 Trouble is coming for leaders who make bad laws! They write rules that hurt people. 2 They push poor people away from what is fair. They take what belongs to My poor people. They take things from widows. They rob children whose fathers have died.

3 What will you do when God punishes you? What will you do when trouble comes from far away? Who will help you? Where will you leave your money? 4 You will have no choice but to bow like prisoners. You will fall with people who are killed. Even after all this, the LORD is still angry. His hand is still raised.

The LORD uses Assyria to punish

5 Trouble is coming, Assyria! You are the stick I use when I am angry. The club in your hand shows My anger. 6 I will send you to a nation that does not follow God. I will send you against people I am angry with. You will take their things and rob them. You will walk on them like mud in the street.

7 But Assyria does not think like that. It does not plan like that. In its heart, it wants to destroy and wipe out many nations.

8 Assyria's leaders say, "Are not all my commanders like kings? 9 Isn't Calno like Carchemish? Isn't Hamath like Arpad? Isn't Samaria like Damascus? 10 my hand has taken kingdoms that worship false gods. Their carved statues were more than those in Jerusalem and Samaria. 11 I did this to Samaria and its false gods. I will do the same to Jerusalem and its idols."

The LORD will punish Assyria's pride

12 When the Lord finishes all His work on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will punish the king of Assyria for his proud words and proud look.

13 The king says, "I did this by my own power. I did this by my wisdom. I am smart. I moved the borders of nations. I took their treasures. I brought down their leaders like a strong bull. 14 my hand found the wealth of nations like a bird finds a nest. I gathered their things like someone gathers eggs. No bird flapped a wing or made a sound."

15 Can an axe brag about the person who swings it? Can a saw think it is better than the person who uses it? A stick cannot swing the person who holds it. A club cannot lift the person who uses it.

The LORD will burn away Assyria's glory

16 So the Lord GOD of Hosts will make His strong soldiers sick and weak. Under His glory, a fire will burn like a big fire. 17 The light of Israel will be a fire. His Holy One will be a flame. In one day it will burn and destroy Assyria's thorns and weeds. 18 It will destroy the beauty of Assyria's forest and good land. It will destroy body and soul. It will be like a sick person getting weaker and weaker. 19 So few trees will be left in Assyria's forest that a child could count them.

A small group will trust the LORD

20 In that day, the leftover people of Israel and the survivors of Jacob's family will not depend on the one who hit them anymore. They will depend on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, in truth. 21 A small group will come back. The rest of Jacob's people will come back to the Mighty God. 22 Even if Israel's people are as many as sand by the sea, only a small group will come back. God has decided to destroy them, but He will do what is right. 23 The Lord GOD of Hosts will finish the destruction He has planned for the whole earth.

Do not be afraid of Assyria

24 So the Lord GOD of Hosts says, "My people who live in Zion, do not be afraid of Assyria. Assyria will hit you with a rod and lift a club against you, like Egypt did." 25 In a very short time, My anger will end. Then My anger will be against Assyria and destroy them. 26 The LORD of Hosts will whip Assyria. He will punish them like He punished Midian at the rock of Oreb. He will raise His stick over the sea, like He did in Egypt. 27 In that day, Assyria's heavy load will leave your shoulders. Its yoke will leave your neck. The yoke will break because you are strong and well-fed.

Assyria marches toward Jerusalem

28 Assyria has come to Aiath. It has passed through Migron. At Michmash it stores its supplies. 29 They crossed the pass. They stayed at Geba for the night. Ramah is afraid. Gibeah of Saul ran away. 30 Cry out, people of Gallim! Listen, Laishah! Poor Anathoth, cry for help! 31 Madmenah runs away. The people of Gebim run for safety. 32 Today Assyria stops at Nob. It shakes its fist at the mountain of Zion, at the hill of Jerusalem.

The LORD will cut down Assyria

33 Look, the Lord GOD of Hosts will chop off the branches with a loud crash. The tall trees will be cut down. The proud ones will be brought low. 34 He will cut down the thick bushes with iron. Lebanon will fall by the power of the Mighty One.
