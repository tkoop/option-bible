God Tells Hosea to Love His Wife Again

1 The LORD said to me, "Go and love a woman who is loved by someone else and is not faithful in marriage. Love her the way the LORD loves the people of Israel. They turn to other gods and like cakes given to false gods."

2 So I bought her for fifteen pieces of silver. I also gave a big basket and a smaller basket of barley.

3 I said to her, "You will stay with me for many days. You must not be unfaithful, and you must not be with any other man. I will be faithful to you, too."

Israel Will Be Without a King for a Time

4 The people of Israel will live many days without a king, without a leader, without offerings, without stone pillars, without the priest's special vest, and without small idols.

5 Later, the people of Israel will come back. They will look for the LORD their God and for David their king. They will respect the LORD and His goodness in the last days.
