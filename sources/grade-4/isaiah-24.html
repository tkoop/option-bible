The LORD Ruins the Earth

1 Look! The LORD is making the earth empty. He is ruining it. He is twisting the ground. He is scattering the people who live there. 2 Everyone will be the same. People and priests will be the same. Workers and bosses will be the same. Buyers and sellers will be the same. Lenders and borrowers will be the same. 3 The earth will be completely emptied. It will be robbed clean. the LORD has said this.

Everyone Is Guilty

4 The earth is sad and dying. The whole world is weak and fading. Even the proud leaders of the earth are weak. 5 The earth is dirty because of the people who live on it. They broke the LORD's laws. They changed His rules. They broke His promise that lasts forever. 6 So a curse is eating up the earth. The people who live there are guilty. That is why earth people are burned up, and only a few are left.

Sadness and Empty Cities

7 The new wine is sad. The grapevines are weak. All happy people are groaning. 8 The happy sound of drums stops. The noise of party people stops. The joy of the harp stops. 9 They do not sing while they drink wine. Strong drink tastes bitter to those who drink it.

10 The broken city is in ruins. Every house is closed. No one can enter. 11 People cry in the streets because there is no wine. All joy has turned sad. Happiness has left the earth. 12 The city is left empty. Its gate is broken down.

A Few Are Left

13 So it will be in the middle of the earth among the nations. It will be like shaking an olive tree. It will be like picking the last grapes.

Praise from Far Away

14 They lift up their voices. They shout for joy. From the west they praise the LORD's greatness. 15 So praise the LORD in the east. Honor the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the faraway islands. 16 From the ends of the earth we heard songs of praise to the Good One. But I said, “I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Oh, no! Traitors have been bad! Traitors have been very bad!”

Trouble for Earth People

17 Terror, a pit, and a trap are waiting for you who live on the earth. 18 The person who runs from the sound of terror will fall into the pit. The person who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the trap. The windows of heaven are opened. The foundations of the earth shake. 19 The earth is broken into pieces. The earth is split apart. The earth shakes hard. 20 The earth wobbles like a drunk person. It sways like a small hut. Its sins are heavy on it. It falls and does not get up again.

The LORD Rules at Last

21 On that day the LORD will punish the armies in heaven above and the kings on earth below. 22 They will be gathered like prisoners in a pit. They will be shut in a cell. After many days they will be punished. 23 The moon will be ashamed. The sun will be embarrassed. This is because the LORD of Armies will rule on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem. His leaders will see His glory.
