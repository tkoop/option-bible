The Good News Paul Gave

1 Brothers and sisters, I want to tell you again about the good news I told you. You received it. You stay in it. 2 This good news saves you if you hold on to the words I told you. If not, you believed for nothing. 3 I gave you the most important thing first. Christ died for our sins, just like the Bible says. 4 He was buried. He rose again on the third day, just like the Bible says. 5 He appeared to Peter. Then He appeared to the twelve. 6 After that, He appeared to more than five hundred brothers and sisters at one time. Most of them are still alive. Some have died. 7 Then He appeared to James. Then He appeared to all the apostles. 8 Last of all, He appeared to me too, like a baby born too soon. 9 I am the least of the apostles. I am not even good enough to be called an apostle. I made God's people suffer. 10 But God's grace made me what I am. His grace to me was not wasted. I worked harder than all of them. But it was not really me. It was God's grace working with me. 11 So whether it was I or they, we preach the same thing. And that is what you believed.

If There Is No Rising From the Dead

12 We preach that Christ rose from the dead. So why do some of you say there is no rising from the dead? 13 If there is no rising from the dead, then Christ did not rise. 14 If Christ did not rise, then our preaching is empty. And your faith is empty. 15 We would even be lying about God. We said God raised Christ. But if dead people do not rise, then God did not raise Him. 16 If dead people do not rise, then Christ did not rise. 17 If Christ did not rise, your faith is worth nothing. You are still in your sins. 18 Then people who died believing in Christ are lost. 19 If we hope in Christ only for this life, we are the saddest people of all.

Christ Is the First to Rise

20 But Christ did rise from the dead. He is the first of those who died to rise. 21 Death came through one man. So rising from the dead comes through one man too. 22 All people die because of Adam. So all people will be made alive because of Christ. 23 But each person rises in his own turn. Christ is first. Then, when He comes again, those who belong to Christ will rise. 24 Then the end will come. Christ will give the kingdom to God the Father. He will end all rule, all power, and all might. 25 Christ must rule until He defeats all His enemies. 26 The last enemy to be ended is death. 27 The Bible says, “God gave Christ power over all things.” But when it says “all things,” it does not mean God. God is the One who gave Christ that power. 28 When Christ has power over all things, then the Son Himself will be under God. God gave Christ power over all things. Then God will be everything to everyone. 29 What will people do who are baptized for those who died? If dead people do not rise, why are they baptized for them? 30 And why do we put ourselves in danger every hour? 31 Brothers and sisters, I face death every day. I am proud of you because of Christ Jesus our Lord. 32 If I fought wild animals in Ephesus for human reasons, what good is it to me? If dead people do not rise, then, “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.” 33 Do not be fooled. Bad friends ruin good habits. 34 Think clearly and do what is right. Stop sinning. Some of you do not know God. I say this to shame you.

What Kind of Body Will We Have?

35 But someone will ask, “How are dead people raised? What kind of body will they have?” 36 You foolish person! What you plant does not live unless it dies first. 37 When you plant, you do not plant the full plant. You plant a bare seed. It may be wheat or something else. 38 But God gives it the body He wants. He gives each seed its own body. 39 Not all bodies are the same. People have one kind. Animals have another. Birds have another. Fish have another. 40 There are bodies in heaven and bodies on earth. The glory of the heavenly bodies is one kind. The glory of the earthly bodies is another kind. 41 The sun has one glory. The moon has another. The stars have another. Even stars are different in glory. 42 It is the same with rising from the dead. What is buried breaks down. What is raised will never break down. 43 It is planted in shame. It is raised in glory. It is planted in weakness. It is raised in power. 44 It is planted as a natural body. It is raised as a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body. 45 The Bible says, “The first man, Adam, became a living being.” The last Adam became a life-giving spirit. 46 The spiritual did not come first. The natural came first. Then the spiritual came. 47 The first man was made from dust. The second man came from heaven. 48 People made from dust are like the man made from dust. People from heaven are like the man from heaven. 49 We were like the man made from dust. We will be like the man from heaven. 50 I tell you this, brothers and sisters: human bodies cannot take God's kingdom. What wears out cannot take what lasts forever.

We Will Be Changed

51 Listen! I tell you a secret. Not all of us will die. But all of us will be changed. 52 It will happen in a flash. It will happen in the blink of an eye. The last trumpet will sound. The dead will rise and never die again. And we will be changed. 53 This dying body must receive life that never dies. This body that can die must receive life that lasts forever. 54 When this dying body receives life that never dies, then these words will come true: “Death has lost its power.” 55 “Death, where is your win? Death, where is your pain?” 56 The pain of death is sin. The power of sin is the law. 57 But thanks be to God! He gives us the win through our Lord Jesus Christ. 58 So, dear brothers and sisters, stand strong. Do not move away. Always do more and more in the Lord's work. You know that your work for the Lord is not wasted.
