Land for the Tribes

1 These are the names of the tribes. Dan will get one share. It will be on the far north side, by the road to Hethlon, up to the way into Hamath, and as far as Hazar Enan, near the border of Damascus and Hamath. It will run from the east side to the west side. 2 Asher will get one share next to Dan's border, from east to west. 3 Naphtali will get one share next to Asher's border, from east to west. 4 Manasseh will get one share next to Naphtali's border, from east to west. 5 Ephraim will get one share next to Manasseh's border, from east to west. 6 Reuben will get one share next to Ephraim's border, from east to west. 7 Judah will get one share next to Reuben's border, from east to west.

The Special Land

8 Next to Judah's border, from east to west, will be the special land you set apart. It will be 25,000 wide and as long as one share. It will run from the east side to the west side. The holy place will be in the middle. 9 The special land you set apart for the the LORD will be 25,000 long and 10,000 wide. 10 This holy land will be for the priests. It will be 25,000 long on the north side, 10,000 wide on the west side, 10,000 wide on the east side, and 25,000 long on the south side. The the LORD's holy place will be in the middle.

11 It will be for the priests who are set apart from Zadok's sons. They kept My rules. They did not go wrong like the Levites did when Israel went wrong. 12 This very holy land set apart for them will be next to the Levites' land. 13 Next to the priests' land, the Levites will get a share. It will be 25,000 long and 10,000 wide. The whole length will be 25,000. The width will be 10,000. 14 They must not sell or trade any of it. They must not give away this special part of the land, because it is holy to the the LORD.

15 The rest of the land will be 5,000 wide and 25,000 long. It will be common land for the city. It will be for homes and open fields. The city will be in the middle. 16 The city will measure 4,500 on the north side, 4,500 on the south side, 4,500 on the east side, and 4,500 on the west side. 17 The city's open fields will reach 250 to the north, 250 to the south, 250 to the east, and 250 to the west. 18 The rest of the land next to the holy land will run east and west. It will be 10,000 on each side. Its crops will give food to the city workers. 19 The city workers will farm it. They will come from all the tribes of Israel. 20 The whole piece will be a square. It will be 25,000 by 25,000. You are to set apart the holy land, along with the city's land. 21 The rest of the land on both sides of the holy land and the city's land will belong to the ruler. It will go east from the 25,000 of the holy land to the east border. It will go west from the 25,000 to the west border. The holy land and the temple will be in the middle. 22 So the Levites' land and the city's land will be in the middle of the ruler's land. The ruler's land will be between Judah's border and Benjamin's border.

Land for the Other Tribes

23 The rest of the tribes: Benjamin will get one share, from east to west. 24 Simeon will get one share next to Benjamin's border, from east to west. 25 Issachar will get one share next to Simeon's border, from east to west. 26 Zebulun will get one share next to Issachar's border, from east to west. 27 Gad will get one share next to Zebulun's border, from east to west. 28 Along Gad's border, from east to west, will be the south part. It will reach from Tamar to the waters of Meribah Kadesh, then to the Brook of Egypt and the Great Sea.

29 This is the land you will give to the tribes of Israel as their share. These are their parts, says the Lord the LORD.

The City Gates

30 These are the city's exits. On the north side, it will be 4,500 long. 31 There will be three gates on the north side. They are the gate of Reuben, the gate of Judah, and the gate of Levi. 32 On the east side, it will be 4,500 long. There will be three gates. They are the gate of Joseph, the gate of Benjamin, and the gate of Dan. 33 On the south side, it will be 4,500 long. There will be three gates. They are the gate of Simeon, the gate of Issachar, and the gate of Zebulun. 34 On the west side, it will be 4,500 long. There will be three gates. They are the gate of Gad, the gate of Asher, and the gate of Naphtali.

The City's New Name

35 The city will measure 18,000 all around. From that day on, the name of the city will be: The LORD Is There.
