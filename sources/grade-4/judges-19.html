A Levite and His Woman

1 In those days, Israel had no king. A Levite lived in the far hills of Ephraim. He took a woman to live with him from Bethlehem in Judah. 2 His woman was not faithful to him. She left him and went to her father's house in Bethlehem in Judah. She stayed there four months.

3 Her husband went after her to speak kindly to her and bring her back. He had his servant and two donkeys with him. Her father saw him and was glad to meet him. 4 His father-in-law, the young woman's father, held on to him. He stayed three days. They ate and drank and slept there.

The Father-in-Law Delays Them

5 On the fourth day they got up early. He was ready to go. The young woman's father said to his son-in-law, "Eat a piece of bread to give you strength. Then you can go."

6 So the two men sat and ate and drank. The young woman's father said to the man, "Please stay another night. Let your heart be happy."

7 The man got up to go, but his father-in-law urged him. So he stayed there. 8 On the fifth day he got up early to go. The young woman's father said, "Eat something to give you strength. Wait until the day is late." So the two men ate together.

9 The man got up to go, with his woman and his servant. His father-in-law said, "Look, the day is almost over. Please stay tonight. Look, the day is ending. Stay here and let your heart be happy. Tomorrow get up early and go home."

They Travel Toward Gibeah

10 But the man did not want to stay. He got up and went. He came near Jebus, which is Jerusalem. He had two donkeys with loads on them, and his woman was with him.

11 The day was very late. The servant said to his master, "Come, let's turn aside to this city of the Jebusites and spend the night there."

12 His master said, "We will not turn aside into a city of people who are not Israelites. We will go on to Gibeah." 13 He said to his servant, "Come, let's go near one of these places. We will spend the night in Gibeah or Ramah."

14 So they went on. The sun set near Gibeah in Benjamin. 15 They turned aside to spend the night in Gibeah. He sat in the city square. No one took them into a house to spend the night.

An Old Man Helps Them

16 An old man was coming from his work in the field at evening. He was from the hills of Ephraim, but he lived in Gibeah. The people of that place were Benjaminites. He saw the traveler in the square.

17 The old man said, "Where are you going? Where did you come from?"

18 He said, "We are passing from Bethlehem in Judah to the far side of the hills of Ephraim. I am from there. I went to Bethlehem in Judah, and now I am going to the house of the the LORD. No one has taken me into his house. 19 We have straw and feed for our donkeys. We have bread and wine for me, your servant, and the young man with us. We do not lack anything."

20 The old man said, "Peace to you. Let me take care of all your needs. Only do not spend the night in the square." 21 He brought him to his house. He fed the donkeys. They washed their feet. They ate and drank.

Wicked Men Come to the Door

22 While they were having a good time, some bad men of the city surrounded the house. They beat on the door. They said to the old man, the owner of the house, "Bring out the man who came to your house. We want to know him."

23 The owner of the house went out to them. He said, "No, my brothers, do not do this evil thing. This man came into my house. Do not do this foolish thing. 24 Look, here is my unmarried daughter, and his woman. Let me bring them out. You can hurt them and do what you think is good. But do not do this thing to this man."

A Terrible Crime

25 But the men would not listen. The man took his woman and brought her out to them. They hurt her all night until morning. At dawn they let her go. 26 As the morning was breaking, the woman came to the door of the man's house where her lord was. She fell down there until it was light.

27 In the morning her lord got up. He opened the doors of the house. He went out to go on his way. He saw the woman, his woman, fallen at the door. Her hands were on the doorstep. 28 He said to her, "Get up. Let's go." But she did not answer. He put her on the donkey. The man got up and went to his home.

Israel Hears About It

29 He came to his house. He took a knife. He took hold of his woman. He cut her into twelve pieces, part by part. He sent her to all the land of Israel. 30 Everyone who saw it said, "Nothing like this has ever happened or been seen since the people of Israel came up from Egypt until today. Think about it. Talk it over. Speak."
