Wise Words About Being Poor and Rich

1 It is better to be poor and honest than to be a fool who speaks wrong. 2 Wanting to do good is not enough without knowing what is right. A person who rushes into things can sin. 3 A foolish person ruins his own life, but his heart gets angry at the LORD. 4 Money brings many friends, but a poor person loses his friend.

Truth and Friendship

5 A false witness will not get away free, and a person who tells lies will not escape. 6 Many people want a kind leader to like them. Everyone is a friend to a person who gives gifts. 7 All of a poor man's brothers hate him. His friends stay even farther away. He may beg for help, but they are gone.

Wisdom and Good Sense

8 A person who gets wisdom loves his own life. A person who keeps good sense will find good. 9 A false witness will not get away free, and a person who tells lies will be destroyed. 10 It is not good for a fool to live a fancy life. It is even worse for a servant to rule over leaders. 11 A person with good sense is slow to get angry. It is his honor to let a wrong go. 12 A king's anger is like a lion's roar, but his kindness is like dew on the grass.

Family and Home

13 A foolish son ruins his father. A wife who always argues is like a leak that never stops. 14 Houses and money come from parents, but a wise wife comes from the LORD. 15 Being lazy makes a person fall into a deep sleep, and a lazy person will go hungry. 16 A person who keeps the rules keeps his own life. A person who does not care how he lives will die. 17 A person who is kind to the poor lends to the LORD, and He will pay him back.

Teaching and Plans

18 Teach your son while there is still hope. Do not be willing to let him die. 19 A person with a very bad temper will be punished. If you save him from trouble, you will have to do it again. 20 Listen to advice and learn from being corrected, so you may be wise in the end. 21 People make many plans in their hearts, but the LORD's plan will stand. 22 What people want most is kindness. It is better to be poor than to be a liar. 23 Respecting the LORD brings life. A person who has it rests happy and is not hurt by trouble.

Lazy and Foolish People

24 A lazy person puts his hand in the dish, but he is too lazy to bring it to his mouth. 25 Punish a person who makes fun of others, and a simple person becomes wise. Correct a wise person, and he learns more. 26 A son who attacks his father and drives away his mother brings shame and disgrace. 27 Stop listening to teaching, my son, and you will stray from what is right. 28 A worthless witness laughs at justice, and wicked people swallow up wrong. 29 Punishments are ready for people who make fun of others, and beatings are for the backs of fools.
