Hezekiah Becomes King

1Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-nine years. His mother was Abijah. She was the daughter of Zechariah. 2He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. He did all the things David his father had done.

Hezekiah Opens the Temple

3In the first month of his first year as king, Hezekiah opened the doors of the LORD's house. He fixed them. 4He brought the priests and the Levites. He gathered them in the east side of the city.

Hezekiah Speaks to the Levites

5"Listen to me, Levites. Make yourselves clean now. Make the house of the LORD, the God of your fathers, clean. Take the dirty things out of the holy place. 6Our fathers did wrong. They did evil in the eyes of the LORD our God. They left Him. They turned away from the LORD's house. They turned their backs. 7They shut the doors of the porch. They put out the lamps. They did not burn incense. They did not give burnt offerings in the holy place to the God of Israel.

8"So the LORD's anger was on Judah and Jerusalem. He made them a terrible sight. He made them ruined and laughed at. You can see this with your own eyes. 9Our fathers died in war. Our sons, daughters, and wives were taken away.

10"Now I want to make a promise to the LORD, the God of Israel. I want His anger to leave us. 11Now, my sons, do not be lazy. The LORD chose you to stand before Him. He chose you to serve Him. He chose you to be His helpers and to burn incense.

The Levites Are Named

12The Levites stood up. They were Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah from the Kohathites. They were Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel from the Merarites. They were Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah from the Gershonites. 13They were Zechariah and Mattaniah from the Asaphites. They were Shimei and Jehiel from the Hemanites. They were Shemaiah and Uzziel from the Jeduthunites.

The Temple Is Cleaned

14They gathered their brothers. They made themselves clean. They went to clean the the LORD's house. They did what the king said by the the LORD's word. 15The priests went deep inside the the LORD's house to clean it. They brought out all the dirty things they found in the the LORD's temple. They brought them to the courtyard. The Levites took them out to the Kidron Valley. 16They began to make the holy place clean on the first day of the first month. On the eighth day they reached the porch. They made the the LORD's house clean for eight more days. They finished on the sixteenth day of the first month.

The Priests Report to the King

17Then they went to King Hezekiah. They said, "We have cleaned all of the the LORD's house. We cleaned the altar for burnt offerings and all its tools. We cleaned the table for the bread and all its tools. 18We also got ready all the tools King Ahaz had thrown away when he did wrong. 19We made them clean. See, they are in front of the the LORD's altar.

Hezekiah Offers Sacrifices

20King Hezekiah got up early. He gathered the city leaders. They went up to the the LORD's house. 21They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats. These were a sin offering for the kingdom, the holy place, and Judah. Hezekiah told the priests, the sons of Aaron, to offer them on the the LORD's altar. 22They killed the bulls. The priests took the blood and threw it on the altar. They killed the rams and threw the blood on the altar. They killed the lambs and threw the blood on the altar. 23They brought the male goats for the sin offering to the king and the people. They put their hands on them. 24The priests killed them. They used the blood to pay for sin on the altar. They did this for all Israel. The king said the burnt offering and the sin offering were for all Israel.

Worship and Music

25Hezekiah put the Levites in the the LORD's house. They had cymbals, harps, and lyres. They did this as David, Gad the king's seer, and Nathan the prophet had said. The the LORD gave this command through His prophets. 26The Levites stood with David's instruments. The priests stood with trumpets. 27Hezekiah said to offer the burnt offering on the altar. When the burnt offering began, the song to the the LORD began too. The trumpets played. They used David's instruments. David was king of Israel. 28All the people bowed down. The singers sang. The trumpeters played. They did this until the burnt offering was done. 29When the offering was done, the king and all the people there bowed down and worshiped.

Praise and More Offerings

30King Hezekiah and the leaders told the Levites to praise the the LORD. They used the words of David and Asaph the seer. The Levites praised with joy. They bowed down and worshiped. 31Hezekiah said, "Now you have given yourselves to the the LORD. Come near. Bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the the LORD's house." The people brought sacrifices and thank offerings. Everyone with a willing heart brought burnt offerings. 32The people brought seventy bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs. These were burnt offerings to the the LORD.

The Priests Need Help

33There were also six hundred oxen and three thousand sheep that were made holy. 34There were not enough priests to skin all the burnt offerings. So the Levites helped their brothers. They helped until the work was done and the priests were clean. The Levites were more ready in heart to make themselves clean than the priests. 35There were also many burnt offerings. There was fat from the peace offerings. There were drink offerings. The work of the the LORD's house was set in order. 36Hezekiah and all the people were glad. God had made the people ready so fast.
