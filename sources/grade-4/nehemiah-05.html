The People Cry Out for Help

1 The people and their wives cried out loudly against their Jewish brothers.

2 Some said, "We have many sons and daughters. We need grain to eat so we can live."

3 Some said, "We have given up our fields, vineyards, and houses to get grain during the famine."

4 Others said, "We borrowed money to pay the king's tax on our fields and vineyards. 5 We are the same kind of people as our brothers. Our children are as good as theirs. But we have to sell our sons and daughters as slaves. Some of our daughters are already slaves. We have no power to stop it. Our fields and vineyards belong to other people now."

Nehemiah Stops the Unfair Charges

6 I was very angry when I heard their cry and these words. 7 I thought about it. Then I argued with the leaders. I said to them, "You are charging your brothers too much interest!" I called a big meeting about them.

8 I said, "We have bought back our Jewish brothers who were sold to other nations. Now you are selling your brothers, and they may be sold to us!" They were quiet. They had nothing to say.

9 I also said, "What you are doing is not good. Shouldn't you live with respect for our God? Then our enemies will not make fun of us. 10 I, my brothers, and my servants are lending them money and grain. Please, let us stop charging this extra money. 11 Please give back to them today their fields, vineyards, olive trees, and houses. Also give back the extra money, grain, new wine, and oil you took from them."

12 They said, "We will give everything back. We will not ask them for anything. We will do what you say." Then I called the priests. I made the leaders swear to do this.

13 I also shook out my coat. I said, "May God shake out of his house and out of his work every man who does not keep this promise. May he be shaken out and left with nothing."

The whole group said, "Amen," and praised the LORD. The people did what they promised.

Nehemiah Works Hard for the People

14 From the time King Artaxerxes made me governor of Judah, from his twentieth year to his thirty-second year, I and my brothers did not eat the food given to the governor.

15 The governors before me made the people carry a heavy load. They took bread and wine from them, plus forty pieces of silver. Even their servants ruled over the people. But I did not do this, because I feared God.

16 I worked hard on this wall. All my servants were gathered there to work. We did not buy any land.

17 Also, one hundred fifty Jewish men and leaders ate at my table. Visitors from the nations around us came too. 18 Each day I prepared one ox, six good sheep, and some birds. Every ten days I had all kinds of wine in large amounts. But I did not ask for the governor's food, because the work was very hard for these people.

19 Remember me, my God, for good. Remember all I have done for these people.
