A Letter from King Nebuchadnezzar

1 King Nebuchadnezzar sends this to all nations and people who speak every language and live everywhere. May you have much peace! 2 I am glad to tell you about the great signs and wonders the Most High God did for me. 3 His signs are great. His wonders are strong. His kingdom lasts forever. His rule goes on and on.

Nebuchadnezzar's Dream

4 I, Nebuchadnezzar, was resting in my house. I was doing well in my palace. 5 I had a dream that scared me. In bed, thoughts and pictures in my head scared me.

6 So I sent for all the wise men of Babylon. I wanted them to tell me what the dream meant. 7 The magicians, wise men, star readers, and fortune tellers came in. I told them the dream, but they could not tell me what it meant.

8 At last, Daniel came to me. He is called Belteshazzar, after my god's name. God's Spirit is in him. I told him the dream.

9 I said, "Belteshazzar, you are the leader of the magicians. I know God's Spirit is in you. No secret is too hard for you. Tell me what I saw in my dream and what it means.

10 "In my dream, I was lying in bed. I saw a tree in the middle of the earth. It was very tall.

11 The tree grew big and strong. Its top reached the sky. People everywhere could see it.

12 Its leaves were pretty. It had lots of fruit. It gave food to all. Wild animals rested under it. Birds lived in its branches. Every living thing got food from it.

13 "While I was lying in bed, I saw a holy messenger come down from heaven.

14 "He shouted in a loud voice, 'Cut down the tree! Cut off its branches! Pull off its leaves! Scatter its fruit! Make the animals run away from under it! Make the birds leave its branches!

15 "'But leave the stump and its roots in the ground. Put a band of iron and bronze around it. Let it stay in the grass. Let the man be wet with dew from heaven. Let him eat grass with the wild animals.

16 "'Let his mind change from a man's mind to an animal's mind. Let seven years go by.

17 "'This order comes from the holy messengers. It is a command from the holy ones. It is so that all living people may know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives them to anyone He wants. He can put the lowest man in charge.'

18 "This is the dream I saw. Now you, Belteshazzar, tell me what it means. The wise men of my kingdom could not tell me. But you can, because God's Spirit is in you."

Daniel Explains the Dream

19 Then Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, was quiet for a while. His thoughts scared him. The king said, "Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or its meaning scare you."

Belteshazzar answered, "My lord, may the dream be for those who hate you, and its meaning for your enemies!

20 "The tree you saw grew big and strong. Its top reached the sky. It could be seen everywhere.

21 Its leaves were pretty. Its fruit was plenty. It gave food to all. Wild animals lived under it. Birds lived in its branches.

22 That tree is you, O king! You have grown great and strong. Your greatness has reached the sky. Your rule reaches the ends of the earth.

23 "You also saw a holy messenger come down from heaven. He said, 'Cut down the tree and destroy it. But leave the stump and roots in the ground. Put a band of iron and bronze around it. Let it stay in the grass. Let the man be wet with dew from heaven. Let him live with wild animals until seven years go by.'

24 "This is what it means, O king. This is the order from the Most High that has come to you, my lord the king.

25 "You will be sent away from people. You will live with wild animals. You will eat grass like an ox. You will be wet with dew from heaven. Seven years will go by. This will last until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives them to anyone He wants.

26 "The command said to leave the stump and roots. This means your kingdom will be given back to you. It will happen when you know that Heaven rules.

27 "So, O king, please listen to my advice. Stop sinning. Do what is right. Stop doing wrong. Be kind to poor people. Then maybe your good life will last longer."

The Dream Comes True

28 All of this happened to King Nebuchadnezzar.

29 Twelve months later, he was walking on the roof of his palace in Babylon.

30 He said, "Look at this great Babylon! I built it with my strong power. It shows my glory and greatness."

31 While the words were still in his mouth, a voice came from heaven. It said, "King Nebuchadnezzar, your kingdom has been taken from you.

32 "You will be sent away from people. You will live with wild animals. You will eat grass like an ox. Seven years will go by. This will last until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdoms of men. He gives them to anyone He wants."

33 Right away, the words came true. Nebuchadnezzar was sent away from people. He ate grass like an ox. Dew from heaven made his body wet. His hair grew like eagle feathers. His nails grew like bird claws.

Nebuchadnezzar Praises God

34 At the end of that time, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up to heaven. My mind came back to me. I praised the Most High. I honored Him and gave Him glory. He lives forever. His rule lasts forever. His kingdom lasts from one family line to the next.

35 All the people on earth are like nothing next to Him. He does what He wants with the armies of heaven and the people of earth. No one can stop Him. No one can say to Him, "What have You done?"

36 At that same time, my mind came back to me. My honor and beauty came back to me. They were for the glory of my kingdom. My leaders came looking for me. I was put back on my throne. I became even greater than before.

37 Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and lift up and honor the King of heaven. All His works are true. All His ways are fair. He can bring down proud people.
