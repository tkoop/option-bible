Judah's Sin Is Deep

1 Judah's sin is written with an iron pen. It is cut with a diamond point on the tablet of their heart. It is also on the horns of their altars. 2 Even their children remember their altars and the wooden poles for a false god. They stand by green trees and on high hills.

3 My mountain in the field, I will let enemies take your wealth and all your treasures. I will also let them take your high places, because of sin all through your land. 4 You will let go of the land I gave you. I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know. You made Me angry. My anger will burn forever.

Trust People or the LORD

5 This is what the the LORD says: "Cursed is the man who trusts in people. He relies on human power. He turns his heart away from the the LORD."

6 He will be like a bush in the desert. He will not see good come. He will live in dry places in the desert, in a salty land where no one lives.

7 But blessed is the man who trusts in the the LORD. The the LORD is his safe place. 8 He is like a tree planted by water. Its roots reach the stream. It does not fear when heat comes. Its leaves stay green. It does not worry in a dry year. It does not stop making fruit.

The Heart Is Sick

9 The heart is more tricky than anything else. It is very sick. Who can know it? 10 I, the the LORD, search the heart. I test the mind. I give each person what he has earned by his ways, and by the fruit of his deeds. 11 A bird that sits on eggs it did not lay is like a man who gets rich in a wrong way. In the middle of his life, his riches will leave him. In the end, he will look foolish.

The LORD Is Our Hope

12 A glorious throne, high from the beginning, is the place of our temple. 13 O the LORD, You are the hope of Israel. All who leave You will be put to shame. Those who turn away from You will be written in the dust, because they left the the LORD, the spring of living water.

Jeremiah's Prayer

14 Heal me, O the LORD, and I will be healed. Save me, and I will be saved. You are my praise.

15 Look, they keep saying to me, "Where is the word of the the LORD? Let it come now!"

16 But I did not run away from being a shepherd after You. I did not want the day of trouble. You know. What came from my lips was open before You. 17 Do not be a terror to me. You are my safe place in the day of trouble. 18 Let those who chase me be put to shame, but do not let me be put to shame. Let them be afraid, but do not let me be afraid. Bring the day of trouble on them. Break them with double trouble.

Keep the Sabbath Day Holy

19 This is what the the LORD said to me: "Go and stand at the gate where the people go. It is the gate the kings of Judah use to go in and out. Stand at all the gates of Jerusalem too."

20 Say to them, "Hear the word of the the LORD, you kings of Judah, all Judah, and all who live in Jerusalem and come in through these gates."

21 This is what the the LORD says: "Be careful. Do not carry a load on the Sabbath day. Do not bring it through the gates of Jerusalem. 22 Do not carry a load out of your houses on the Sabbath day. Do not do any work. Keep the Sabbath day holy, as I told your fathers."

23 But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. They were stubborn. They would not listen or learn.

24 "But if you listen carefully to Me," says the the LORD, "and do not bring a load through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day, and keep the Sabbath day holy by not doing any work on it, 25 then kings and princes who sit on David's throne will come through the gates of this city. They will ride in chariots and on horses. They and their princes, the men of Judah and the people of Jerusalem, will come. This city will be lived in forever. 26 People will come from the cities of Judah and from around Jerusalem. They will come from the land of Benjamin, from the lowlands, from the hill country, and from the south. They will bring burnt gifts and sacrifices, grain gifts and incense. They will bring thank-you gifts to the house of the the LORD. 27 But if you do not listen to Me and keep the Sabbath day holy, and if you carry a load as you go through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, then I will start a fire in its gates. It will burn up the palaces of Jerusalem. It will not be put out."
