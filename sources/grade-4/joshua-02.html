Joshua Sends Spies to Jericho

1 Then Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Shittim to spy on the land. He told them, "Go, look over the land, and especially Jericho." So they went and stayed at the house of a woman named Rahab.

2 Someone told the king of Jericho, "Men from Israel have come here tonight to spy on the land."

The King Sends for the Spies

3 So the king sent a message to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to your house. They came to spy on the whole land."

4 But Rahab had already hidden the two men. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from. 5 They left when it was getting dark, and the city gate was about to close. I do not know where they went. Chase them fast, and you may catch them."

6 But she had taken them up to the roof and hidden them under stalks of flax that she had laid out there.

7 The men chased them toward the road to the shallow places in the Jordan River. As soon as the chasers had left, the city gate was shut.

Rahab Talks to the Spies

8 Before the spies went to sleep, Rahab went up to the roof.

9 She said to them, "I know that the LORD has given you this land. We are very afraid of you. Everyone who lives here is scared and weak because of you.

10 "We heard how the LORD dried up the Red Sea for you when you came out of Egypt. We heard what you did to Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings across the Jordan. You destroyed them completely.

11 "When we heard that, we were very scared. Everyone lost courage because of you. The LORD your God is God in heaven above and on earth below.

12 "Now please promise me by the LORD that you will be kind to my father's family, just as I was kind to you. Give me a sure sign.

13 "Promise to save my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and all their people. Keep us alive and do not let us die."

The Spies Make a Promise

14 The men said to her, "We will protect you with our lives! If you do not tell about our plan, then when the LORD gives us this land, we will be kind and faithful to you."

Rahab Helps the Spies Escape

15 So Rahab let them down by a rope through the window. Her house was built into the city wall.

16 She said, "Go to the hills so the men chasing you will not find you. Hide there for three days until they come back. Then go on your way."

17 The men said to her, "We will keep the promise you made us swear. 18 When we come into the land, tie this red rope in the window you let us down from. Bring your father, mother, brothers, and all your father's family into your house. 19 If anyone goes out of your house into the street, he will be responsible for his own death, and we will not be blamed. But if anyone in your house is hurt, we will be blamed. 20 But if you tell about our plan, we will not have to keep our promise to you."

21 Rahab said, "I agree. Let it be as you say." Then she sent them on their way and tied the red rope in the window.

22 The spies went to the hills and stayed there three days, until the men chasing them came back. The chasers looked all along the road but did not find them.

The Spies Report to Joshua

23 Then the two spies came down from the hills, crossed the river, and went to Joshua son of Nun. They told him everything that had happened.

24 They said to Joshua, "The LORD has surely given the whole land to us. Everyone who lives there is very afraid of us."
