God Will Bring You to a New Land

1 When the LORD your God brings you into the land you are going to take, He will drive out many peoples before you. They are the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. These are seven peoples, and they are bigger and stronger than you. 2 The the LORD your God will give them to you. You must defeat them and destroy them completely. Do not make an agreement with them. Do not show them mercy. 3 Do not marry them. Do not give your daughter to their son. Do not take their daughter for your son. 4 They will turn your sons and daughters away from Me. They will serve other gods. Then the the LORD will be angry with you. He will quickly destroy you.

Destroy Their Altars and Idols

5 This is what you must do to them: tear down their altars, smash their sacred stones, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved idols in fire. 6 You are a holy people to the the LORD your God. The the LORD your God chose you to be His special people. He chose you from all the peoples on the earth. 7 The the LORD did not choose you because you were many. You were the fewest of all peoples. 8 The the LORD chose you because He loved you. He kept the promise He made to your fathers. He brought you out with a strong hand. He saved you from slavery in Egypt. He saved you from Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

God Keeps His Promises

9 So know this: The the LORD your God is God. He is the faithful God. He keeps His agreement and His faithful love for a thousand generations. He does this for those who love Him and keep His commands. 10 But He pays back those who hate Him. He destroys them. He will not wait. He will pay them back. 11 So keep the commands, laws, and rules I give you today. Do what they say. 12 If you listen to these rules and keep them and do them, then the the LORD your God will keep His agreement and faithful love with you, as He promised your fathers. 13 He will love you, bless you, and make you grow. He will bless your children and your crops. He will bless your grain, wine, and oil. He will bless your young cows and sheep in the land He promised your fathers. He promised to give this land to you. 14 You will be blessed more than all peoples. No man or woman among you will be unable to have children. None of your animals will be unable to have young. 15 The the LORD will take away all sickness from you. He will not put the terrible diseases you knew in Egypt on you. He will put them on your enemies.

Do Not Worship Other Gods

16 You will destroy all the peoples the the LORD your God gives you. Do not feel sorry for them. Do not serve their gods, because that would trap you. 17 You may think in your heart that these peoples are bigger than me. You may wonder how you can push them out. 18 Do not be afraid of them. Remember what the the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and all Egypt. 19 Remember the great troubles, signs, and wonders you saw with your own eyes. Remember the the LORD your God's strong hand and outstretched arm. He brought you out. The the LORD your God will do the same to all the peoples you fear.

Do Not Be Afraid

20 The the LORD your God will also send hornets among them. He will do this until those left and those hiding from you are destroyed. 21 Do not be afraid of them. The the LORD your God is with you. He is great and fearsome. 22 The the LORD your God will drive out these peoples little by little. You will not be able to destroy them all at once. If you did, wild animals would grow too many and hurt you. 23 The the LORD your God will give them to you. He will throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed. 24 He will let you capture their kings. You will wipe out their names from under heaven. No one will stand against you until you destroy them.

Stay Away from Idols

25 You must burn their carved gods in fire. Do not want their silver or gold. Do not take it for you, or it will trap you. It is hated by the the LORD your God. 26 Do not bring that hated thing into your house. You would become like it. Hate it completely. Hate it fully, because it is set apart to be destroyed.
