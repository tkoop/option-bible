God Helps Those Who Care for the Poor

1 For the music leader. A psalm of David. Happy is the person who cares for poor people. The LORD saves him when trouble comes. 2 The LORD watches over him and keeps him alive. He is happy in the land. Do not let his enemies get what they want. 3 The LORD helps him when he is sick in bed. You make him well again when he is sick.

David Asks God for Mercy

4 I said, “The LORD, have mercy on me. Heal me, because I have sinned against You.”

Enemies Wish David Harm

5 My enemies say bad things about me. They ask, “When will he die? When will people forget his name?”

6 When someone comes to see me, he says false things. He keeps bad thoughts in his heart. Then he goes out and spreads them. 7 All who hate me whisper together against me. They plan to hurt me.

8 They say, “A terrible sickness has hit him. He will never get up again.”

A Trusted Friend Turns Away

9 Even my close friend, whom I trusted, who ate with me, has turned against me.

David Trusts God to Lift Him Up

10 But You, the LORD, have mercy on me and raise me up, so I can pay them back. 11 This is how I know You are happy with me: my enemy does not win over me. 12 You hold me up because I have done right. You let me stand before You forever.

Praise to the LORD

13 Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, from now and forever. Amen and amen.
