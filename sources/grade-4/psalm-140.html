David Prays for Safety

1 The LORD, save me from bad people. Keep me safe from men who hurt others. 2 They think up bad things in their hearts. They stir up fights all day long. 3 They make their tongues sharp like snakes. Snake poison is under their lips.

4 The LORD, keep me safe from the hands of bad people. Protect me from men who hurt others. They plan to make me trip and fall. 5 Proud people hid a trap for me. They spread ropes like a net by my path. They set traps for me.

6 I said to the the LORD, "You are my God. The LORD, listen to my cry for help." 7 O the LORD, my Lord, You are strong. You save me. You keep my head safe in battle.

8 O the LORD, do not give bad people what they want. Do not let their plans work, or they will brag. 9 May trouble cover the heads of those around me. Their own words bring them trouble. 10 May hot coals fall on them. May they be thrown into fire, into deep mud, and never get up. 11 Do not let a person who tells lies be safe in the land. May trouble hunt down the man who hurts others.

12 I know the the LORD will help poor people get justice. He will stand up for those in need. 13 Surely good people will praise Your name. Honest people will live with You.
