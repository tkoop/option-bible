A Song of Going Up

1 The LORD, remember David and all his troubles. 2 He promised the LORD. He made a promise to the Mighty One of Jacob.

David Promises to Find a Home for the LORD

3 "I will not go into my house. I will not lie on my bed. 4 I will not let my eyes sleep. I will not let my eyelids close. 5 I will do this until I find a place for the LORD, a home for the Mighty One of Jacob."

6 We heard about it in Ephrathah. We found it in the fields of Jaar. 7 Let us go to His home. Let us worship at His footstool.

A Prayer for the LORD to Rest

8 Get up, the LORD, and go to Your resting place—You and the box of Your strength. 9 May Your priests wear what is right. May Your faithful people shout for joy. 10 For David, Your servant, do not turn away Your chosen king.

The LORD's Promise to David

11 The LORD made a strong promise to David. He will not take it back. He said, "I will put one of your children on your throne. 12 If your sons keep My agreement and follow the rules I teach them, then their sons will sit on your throne forever."

The LORD Chooses Zion

13 The LORD chose Zion. He wanted it for His home.

14 "This is My resting place forever. Here I will live, because I wanted it. 15 I will bless her food. I will give her poor people bread to eat. 16 I will dress her priests with saving help. Her faithful people will sing for joy. 17 There I will make David strong. I have prepared a light for My chosen king. 18 I will cover his enemies with shame. But his crown will shine."
