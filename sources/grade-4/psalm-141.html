David Prays for Help

1 O the LORD, I call to You. Hurry and help me! Listen to my voice when I call to You. 2 Let my prayer be like sweet smoke before You. Let my hands lifted up be like the evening offering.

3 O the LORD, put a guard on my mouth. Keep watch over my lips. 4 Do not let my heart want bad things. Do not let me do wrong with people who do wrong. Do not let me eat their tasty food.

David Asks God to Keep Him Good

5 Let a good person correct me. That is kind. Let him scold me. It will be like a blessing on my head. I will not say no. I still pray against their bad deeds. 6 Their leaders will fall off the cliff. They will hear my words, and they will like them.

7 Our bones are scattered at the grave like dirt that has been dug up.

David Trusts God

8 But my eyes look to You, O the LORD God. I trust in You. Do not take my life away. 9 Keep me from the traps they set for me. Keep me from the traps of people who do wrong. 10 Let bad people fall into their own nets while I get away safe.
