Israel Should Not Celebrate

1 Do not be happy, Israel. Do not cheer like other nations. You have turned away from your God. You love pay from selling yourself on every grain floor. 2 The grain floor and wine press will not feed them. The new wine will not help them.

They Will Not Stay in God's Land

3 They will not stay in the the LORD's land. Ephraim will go back to Egypt. They will eat food that is not clean in Assyria. 4 They will not pour wine for the the LORD. Their gifts will not make Him happy. Their gifts will be like food for people in mourning. Everyone who eats them will become unclean. Their bread will only fill their stomachs. It will not go into the the LORD's house.

What Will You Do?

5 He asked, "What will you do on the special day? What will you do on the the LORD's feast day?"

Hard Days Are Coming

6 Look, they are running from trouble. Egypt will gather them. Memphis will bury them. Thorns will take their silver. Weeds will grow in their tents. 7 The days to punish have come. The days to pay back have come. Israel will know. The prophet is a fool. The man with God's Spirit is crazy. This is because your sin is great, and your hate is great.

The Prophet Watches

8 The prophet watches over Ephraim for my God. But traps are on all his paths. There is hate in the house of his God. 9 They have sinned deeply, like in the days of Gibeah. God will remember their wrong. He will punish their sins.

God Found Israel

10 I found Israel like grapes in the desert. I saw your fathers like the first figs on a fig tree. But they went to Baal Peor. They gave themselves to Shame. They became as awful as the thing they loved.

Ephraim's Glory Will Go

11 Ephraim's honor will fly away like a bird. There will be no babies born, no one pregnant, and no baby started. 12 Even if they raise children, I will take their children away. How sad for them when I leave them! 13 I have seen Ephraim like Tyre, planted in a nice place. But Ephraim will bring his children out to be killed.

A Prayer to the LORD

14 Hosea prayed, "Give them, O the LORD—what will You give? Give them mothers who lose their babies and cannot feed them."

God Says No

15 All their wrong is in Gilgal. There I hated them. Because of their bad deeds, I will push them out of My house. I will not love them anymore. All their leaders are against Me. 16 Ephraim is hit hard. Their root is dry. They will make no fruit. Even if they have children, I will kill the babies they love. 17 My God will reject them because they did not obey Him. They will wander among the nations.
