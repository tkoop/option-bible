God Is Good

1 This song is by Asaph. For sure, God is good to Israel. He is good to people with clean hearts.

I Almost Slipped

2 But I almost slipped. My feet almost gave way. 3 I was jealous of proud people. I saw bad people doing well.

The Wicked Seem Happy

4 They do not hurt when they die. Their bodies are strong. 5 They do not have the troubles other people have. They are not hit with sickness like others. 6 So pride is like a necklace for them. They wear hurt and mean acts like clothes. 7 Their eyes bulge from fat. Their hearts dream up bad things.

8 They laugh and say mean things. From their high place they speak with pride. They plan to hurt people. 9 They talk like they own heaven. Their words go all over the earth. 10 So their people turn to them. They drink up all they can.

11 They say, "How would God know? Does the Most High know anything?"

12 Look, these are bad people. They are always at ease. They get more and more money.

I Felt I Lived for Nothing

13 I kept my heart clean for nothing. I washed my hands to show I did no wrong. 14 All day I was hurt. Every morning I was told I was wrong.

15 If I had talked like that, I would have turned against Your children. 16 I tried to understand this. It was too hard for me.

Then I Understood

17 Then I went into God's holy place. There I saw what would happen to them at the end. 18 You put them on slippery ground. You make them fall down.

19 In a moment they are destroyed. Fear sweeps them away. 20 They are like a dream when someone wakes up. When You get up, Lord, You will think they are nothing.

21 My heart was sad. My feelings were hurt. 22 I was foolish and did not know. I was like a wild animal with You.

God Holds My Hand

23 But I am always with You. You hold my right hand. 24 You lead me with Your advice. After that, You will take me to glory.

25 Who do I have in heaven but You? Nothing on earth makes me happy like You. 26 My body and my heart may fail. But God is the strength of my heart. He is my share forever.

27 People far from You will die. You destroy all who are not true to You. 28 But it is good for me to be near God. I have made the LORD my safe place. I will tell about all the things You have done.
