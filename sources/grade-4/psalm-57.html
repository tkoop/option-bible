David Asks God for Help

1 Be kind to me, God. Be kind to me. I run to You for safety. I will hide in Your care until the trouble is gone. 2 I cry out to God Most High, to God who helps me.

God Sends Help from Heaven

3 He sends help from heaven and saves me. He stops the people who chase me. God sends His love and His truth. 4 I am among dangerous men. They are like hungry beasts. Their teeth are like sharp spears and arrows. Their words cut like swords.

Praise God Above All

5 Be praised, O God, above the sky. Let Your greatness be seen over the whole earth. 6 They set a trap for my feet. I was weak and sad. They dug a hole in my path, but they fell into it themselves.

7 My heart is strong, O God. My heart is strong. I will sing and make music. 8 Wake up, my heart! Wake up, music! I will wake up at dawn to praise You.

Praise God Among the Nations

9 I will praise You, Lord, where all nations can hear. I will sing about You among all people. 10 Your love is so big it reaches the sky. Your truth reaches the clouds. 11 Be praised, O God, above the sky. Let Your greatness be seen over the whole earth.
