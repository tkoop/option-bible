Live to Please God

1 Finally, friends, we ask you and beg you in the Lord Jesus to live the way we taught you. We want you to please God, and we want you to do this even more. 2 You know the rules we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

Stay Away from Sexual Sin

3 God wants you to live a holy life. So stay away from sexual sin. 4 Each of you should know how to use his own body in a clean and respectful way. 5 Do not let strong wrong desires control you, like people who do not know God. 6 Do not cheat or take advantage of another Christian in this. The Lord punishes people who do these things. We told you this before, and we warned you. 7 God did not call us to live dirty lives. He called us to live holy lives. 8 So anyone who ignores this rule is not ignoring people. He is ignoring God, who gives you His Holy Spirit.

Love Each Other and Work Hard

9 You do not need us to write to you about loving each other like family. God has taught you how to love each other. 10 You already show love to all the Christians in Macedonia. But we ask you, friends, to love even more. 11 Try hard to live a quiet life. Take care of your own work. Work with your own hands, as we told you. 12 Then people outside the church will respect the way you live. You will not need to depend on anyone.

Hope for Those Who Have Died

13 Friends, we do not want you to be sad about people who have died, like people who have no hope. 14 We believe Jesus died and rose again. So God will bring back with Jesus those who died believing in Him. 15 We tell you this by the Lord's word: We who are still alive when the Lord comes will not go before those who have died. 16 The Lord Himself will come down from heaven. He will shout a command. The voice of a chief angel will be heard. God's trumpet will sound. First, the dead who belong to Christ will rise. 17 Then we who are still alive will be taken up with them into the clouds. We will meet the Lord in the air. Then we will be with the Lord forever. 18 So cheer each other up with these words.
