Daniel's Dream of Four Beasts

1 In the first year that Belshazzar was king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream. He saw pictures in his mind while he lay in bed. Then he wrote the dream down. He told the main parts.

2 Daniel said he looked in his dream at night. He saw the four winds of the sky stirring up the big sea. 3 Four big animals came up out of the sea. Each one was different. 4 The first animal was like a lion. It had eagle wings. Daniel watched until its wings were pulled off. It was lifted from the ground. It stood on two feet like a man. It was given a man's heart.

5 Another animal came next. It was like a bear. It rose up on one side. It had three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. Someone said to it, "Get up! Eat a lot of meat!" 6 Then Daniel looked and saw another animal. It was like a leopard. It had four bird wings on its back. It had four heads. It was given power to rule. 7 Then he saw a fourth animal in his night dream. It was scary and very strong. It had big iron teeth. It ate and crushed things. It stomped on what was left with its feet. It was different from all the other animals. It had ten horns. 8 Daniel looked at the horns. Then a small horn grew up among them. Three of the first horns were pulled out to make room for it. This horn had eyes like a person's eyes. It had a mouth that spoke proud words.

9 Daniel watched until thrones were set up. Then the Ancient of Days sat down. His clothes were white like snow. The hair on His head was like clean wool. His throne was like fire. Its wheels were burning fire. 10 A river of fire flowed out from in front of Him. Thousands and thousands served Him. Millions stood before Him. The court sat to judge, and the books were opened.

11 Daniel kept looking because of the loud proud words the horn was saying. He saw the animal killed. Its body was destroyed and thrown into the fire. 12 The other animals lost their power to rule. But they were allowed to live for a while.

The Son of Man

13 Daniel saw in his night dream. He saw someone who looked like a man coming with the clouds of heaven. He came to the Ancient of Days. He was brought before Him. 14 He was given power to rule, honor, and a kingdom. All people, all nations, and all languages will serve Him. His rule will last forever. It will never end. His kingdom will never be destroyed.

The Meaning of the Dream

15 I, Daniel, felt upset deep inside. The pictures in my head troubled me. 16 I went up to one of the people standing there. I asked him to tell me the truth about all this. He told me what it meant.

17 He said, "These four big animals are four kings. They will rise up from the earth. 18 But God's holy people who belong to the Most High will receive the kingdom. They will own it forever and ever."

19 Then I wanted to know the truth about the fourth animal. It was different from all the others. It was very scary. It had iron teeth and bronze claws. It ate, crushed, and stomped on what was left with its feet. 20 I also wanted to know about the ten horns on its head. I wanted to know about the other horn that grew up. Three horns fell before it. That horn had eyes and a mouth that spoke proud words. It looked stronger than the others. 21 I saw that horn fight against God's holy people. It won against them for a time. 22 This lasted until the Ancient of Days came. He gave justice to God's holy people who belong to the Most High. Then the time came for God's people to own the kingdom.

23 He said, "The fourth animal means a fourth kingdom on earth. It will be different from all other kingdoms. It will eat up the whole earth. It will stomp on it and crush it. 24 The ten horns mean ten kings. They will come from this kingdom. After them, another king will come. He will be different from the first ones. He will bring down three kings. 25 He will speak against the Most High. He will wear out God's holy people who belong to the Most High. He will try to change the set times and the law. God's people will be given into his hand for three and a half years. 26 But the court will sit to judge. His power will be taken away. It will be destroyed forever. 27 The kingdom, power, and greatness of all the kingdoms under heaven will be given to the people who are God's holy people of the Most High. Their kingdom will last forever. Every ruler will serve and obey them."

28 This is the end of the matter. I, Daniel, was very troubled in my thoughts. My face changed. But I kept these things in my heart.
