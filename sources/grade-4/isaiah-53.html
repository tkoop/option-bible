The Servant Who Suffers

1 Who believed our news? Who saw the LORD's power? 2 He grew up like a young plant. He was like a root in dry ground. He had no beauty to make us look at Him. We did not want Him. 3 People did not like Him. They turned away from Him. He was a man full of pain. He knew sickness. People hid their faces from Him. We thought He was not worth much.

He Took Our Pain

4 But He took our sickness. He carried our pain. We thought God was punishing Him. 5 He was hurt for our wrongs. He was crushed for our sins. He took our punishment so we could have peace. His cuts healed us. 6 We all went the wrong way, like sheep. Each of us went his own way. The LORD put all our sin on Him.

He Was Hurt for Us

7 He was treated badly and hurt, but He did not say a word. He was led like a sheep to be killed. He was like a sheep that is quiet when its wool is cut. He did not open His mouth. 8 He was taken away by force and judged unfairly. Who cared about His people? He was taken from the living. He was hurt for the sins of My people. 9 They put Him in a grave with bad people. But He was with a rich man when He died. He had not done wrong. He had not lied.

God's Plan Wins

10 The LORD wanted to crush Him and let Him suffer. If He gives His life for sin, He will see His children. He will live a long time. The LORD's plan will win. 11 He will see the hard work of His life and be satisfied. Because He knows what is right, My Servant will make many people right. He will carry their sins. 12 So I will give Him a great place with many people. He will share the good things He wins. He poured out His life to death. He was counted with bad people. He carried the sin of many. He asks God to forgive them.
