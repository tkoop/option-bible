David's Song About Doeg

For the music leader. A wise song of David. This was when Doeg the Edomite came to Saul and said, "David went to Ahimelech's house."

1 Why do you brag about doing bad, strong man? God's love lasts all day.

2 Your tongue plans trouble. It is like a sharp razor. You trick people. 3 You love what is bad more than what is good. You love lies more than truth. 4 You love words that hurt people. Your tongue tricks people.

5 But God will bring you down forever. He will snatch you and pull you from your tent. He will pull you up by the roots from the land of the living.

Good People See What Happens

6 Good people will see it and be afraid. They will laugh at him and say,

7 "Look at the man who did not make God his safe place. He trusted his many riches. He made himself strong with his bad ways."

David Trusts God

8 But I am like a green olive tree in God's house. I trust in God's love forever and ever.

9 I will praise You forever, because You did this. I will wait for Your name, because it is good, with Your faithful people.
