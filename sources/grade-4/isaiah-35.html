The Desert Will Bloom

1 The desert and dry land will be happy. The empty land will be happy and bloom like a flower. 2 It will bloom and be very happy. It will sing with joy. The beauty of Lebanon will be given to it. The beauty of Carmel and Sharon will be given to it. People will see the the LORD's great beauty, the beauty of our God.

God Will Help Weak People

3 Make weak hands strong. Make weak knees strong. 4 Say to people with scared hearts, "Be strong. Do not be afraid. Look, your God will come to punish. He will pay back what is owed, and He will save you."

God Will Heal People

5 Then blind people will see, and deaf people will hear. 6 Then people who cannot walk will jump like deer. People who cannot speak will sing with joy. Water will pour out in the desert. Streams will flow in the dry land. 7 Hot sand will become a pool. Dry ground will have springs of water. Where jackals live, grass and tall plants will grow.

The Road of Holiness

8 A road will be there. It will be called the Way of Holiness. People who are not clean will not walk on it. It is for people who walk that way. Even people who do not know much will not get lost. 9 No lion will be there. No wild animal will walk on it. They will not be found there. But saved people will walk there. 10 The people the the LORD has set free will come back. They will enter Zion singing. They will be crowned with joy that never ends. Joy and gladness will catch up with them. Sorrow and sighing will run away.
