Run the Race with Faith

1 So, since many people who believed in God are watching us like a big cloud, let's put away everything that slows us down. Let's also put away sin that holds on to us. Then let's run the race God set for us, and keep going even when it is hard. 2 Look at Jesus. He started our faith and makes it complete. He saw the joy ahead. So He stayed on the cross and did not care about the shame. Now He sits at God's right side. 3 Think about Jesus. Bad people fought against Him. He stayed strong. Think about Him so you do not get tired or give up in your hearts. 4 In your fight against sin, you have not bled yet.

God Trains His Children

5 Have you forgotten the words that speak to you like sons? They say, "My son, do not think lightly of the the LORD's training. Do not give up when He corrects you. 6 For the the LORD trains the one He loves. He punishes every son He accepts."

7 You must keep going through hard training. God is treating you like His sons. What father does not train his son? 8 If you get no training, then you are not true sons. All true sons get training.

9 We had fathers on earth who trained us, and we listened to them. So we should obey the Father of our spirits even more. Then we will live. 10 They trained us for a short time, the way they thought best. But God trains us for our good. He wants us to share His holy life. 11 Right now, training feels hard, not fun. But later it gives good fruit. It gives peace and right living to people who learn from it.

Be Strong and Holy

12 So lift up your tired hands. Make your weak knees strong. 13 Make straight paths for your feet. Then the weak part will not get worse. It will get better. 14 Try to live in peace with all people. Try to be holy. No one will see the the LORD without holiness. 15 Watch out. Do not let anyone miss God's grace. Do not let a bitter root grow. It can trouble people and make many dirty. 16 Do not let anyone be like Esau. He was not holy. He sold his right as firstborn son for one meal. 17 You know what happened later. He wanted the blessing, but he was turned away. He could not get it back, even when he cried.

A Better Mountain

18 You did not come to a mountain you can touch. It had fire, darkness, gloom, and a storm. 19 You did not hear a trumpet and a voice. The people who heard it begged for no more words.

20 They could not stand the command: "Even if an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned."

21 The sight was so scary that Moses said, "I am terrified and shaking."

22 But you have come to Mount Zion. You have come to the city of the living God. You have come to heavenly Jerusalem. You have come to thousands and thousands of angels. 23 You have come to God's people, the firstborn whose names are in heaven. You have come to God, who judges all. You have come to the spirits of good people made perfect. 24 You have come to Jesus. He makes peace in a new agreement. His blood speaks a better word than Abel's blood.

Do Not Refuse God

25 Be careful. Do not say no to the One who speaks. The people long ago did not escape when they said no to God's warning on earth. We will not escape if we turn away from God's warning from heaven. 26 Back then, His voice shook the earth. Now He has promised, "I will shake the earth one more time. I will shake heaven too."

27 The words "one more time" mean the things that can be shaken will be taken away. These are things God made. The things that cannot be shaken will stay.

28 So let's be thankful. We are getting a kingdom that cannot be shaken. Let's worship God in a way that pleases Him. Let's do it with respect and fear. 29 Our God is a fire that burns everything up.
