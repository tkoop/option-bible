I Trust in the LORD

1 I put my trust in You, the LORD. Please never let me be ashamed. 2 Because You do what is right, save me and rescue me. Listen to me and help me. 3 Be my safe rock that I can always go to. You have said You will save me. You are my rock and my strong safe place.

God Has Helped Me Since I Was Young

4 My God, save me from bad people. Save me from those who are mean and cruel. 5 You are my hope, Lord. You are the LORD I trust since I was young. 6 I have leaned on You since I was born. You brought me into the world. I always praise You. 7 Many people look at me in surprise. But You are my strong safe place. 8 My mouth is full of praise for You. I talk about Your greatness all day.

Do Not Leave Me When I Am Old

9 Do not throw me away when I am old. Do not leave me when my strength is gone.

My Enemies Want to Hurt Me

10 My enemies talk about me. The people who want to hurt me plan together.

11 They say, “God has left him. Chase him and grab him. No one can save him.”

12 God, do not stay far from me. My God, hurry to help me. 13 Let the people who accuse me be put to shame. Let them be ruined. Cover them with shame and disgrace, because they want to hurt me.

I Will Keep Hoping and Praising God

14 But I will always hope in You. I will praise You more and more. 15 My mouth will tell about Your right ways. It will tell about the ways You save people all day long. I cannot count them all. 16 I will come with the great things the Lord, the LORD, has done. I will remember that You alone do what is right. 17 God, You have taught me since I was young. I still tell about Your wonderful works. 18 Even when I am old and gray, God, do not leave me. Let me tell Your strength to the next generation. Let me tell Your power to everyone who comes after.

God's Great Power

19 Your goodness reaches up to the sky, God. You have done great things. No one is like You, God. 20 You have let me go through many hard times. But You will make me live again. You will lift me up from the grave. 21 You will make me great again. You will comfort me again.

I Will Sing Praises to God

22 I will praise You with a harp, my God, because You are faithful. I will sing to You with music. You are the Holy One of Israel. 23 My lips will shout for joy as I sing to You. My whole self will sing, because You saved me. 24 My tongue will talk about Your right help all day. The people who tried to hurt me are ashamed and confused.
