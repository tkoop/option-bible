The King's Dream

1 In the second year of Nebuchadnezzar's rule, he had dreams. His spirit was upset, and he could not sleep. 2 The king called for the wise men, the magicians, the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans. He wanted them to tell him his dreams. They came and stood before the king. 3 The king said, "I had a dream. My spirit is upset. I want to know what it means."

The Wise Men Cannot Explain the Dream

4 The Chaldeans answered the king, "O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will explain it."

5 The king answered the Chaldeans, "This is my decision. If you do not tell me the dream and what it means, I will have you cut into pieces. I will make your houses into piles of rubble. 6 But if you tell me the dream and what it means, I will give you gifts and rewards and great honor. So tell me the dream and what it means."

7 They answered again, "Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will explain it." 8 The king answered, "I know for sure that you are trying to get more time. You see that I have made my decision.

9 If you do not tell me the dream and what it means, there is only one law for you. You have agreed to tell me lies and bad things until things change. So tell me the dream and what it means. Then I will know you can explain it."

10 The Chaldeans answered the king, "No one on earth can do what the king asks. No great and powerful king has ever asked such a thing of any magician, enchanter, or Chaldean. 11 What the king asks is too hard. No one can show it to the king except the gods. But they do not live with people."

12 Because of this, the king became very angry. He ordered that all the wise men of Babylon be killed. 13 The law went out. The wise men were to be killed. Men looked for Daniel and his friends to kill them too.

Daniel Asks for Time

14 Then Daniel spoke wisely and carefully to Arioch, the king's captain. Arioch had gone out to kill the wise men of Babylon. 15 Daniel asked Arioch, "Why did the king make such a harsh law?" Then Arioch told Daniel what had happened. 16 Daniel went in and asked the king to give him time. Then he would tell the king the dream and what it means.

Daniel and His Friends Pray

17 Daniel went home. He told his friends Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah what had happened. 18 He asked them to pray for mercy from the God of heaven. They prayed so that Daniel and his friends would not die with the other wise men of Babylon. 19 During the night, the secret was shown to Daniel in a vision. Then Daniel praised the God of heaven.

20 Daniel said, "Praise be to God's name forever and ever! Wisdom and power belong to Him. 21 He changes times and seasons. He removes kings and sets up kings. He gives wisdom to wise people. He gives knowledge to people who understand.

22 "He shows deep and hidden things. He knows what is in the dark. Light lives with Him.

23 "I thank You and praise You, God of my fathers. You gave me wisdom and power. Now You have told me what we asked of You. You have shown us the king's problem."

Daniel Goes to the King

24 Then Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had chosen to kill the wise men of Babylon. Daniel said, "Do not kill the wise men of Babylon. Take me to the king. I will tell the king what the dream means."

25 Arioch quickly took Daniel to the king. He said, "I found a man from the captives of Judah. He can tell the king what the dream means."

26 The king said to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, "Can you tell me the dream I saw and what it means?"

Daniel Explains the Dream

27 Daniel answered the king, "No wise man, magician, or reader of dreams can tell the king this secret. 28 But there is a God in heaven who shows secrets. He has told King Nebuchadnezzar what will happen in the last days. This is your dream. These are the visions you saw while you were in bed.

29 "As you lay in bed, O king, your thoughts turned to what will happen after this. The One who shows secrets has told you what will happen.

30 "This secret was shown to me, but not because I am wiser than anyone else. It was shown so that you, O king, may know what it means. Then you will know the thoughts of your heart.

31 "You, O king, were looking. You saw a huge statue. It was very big and bright. It stood in front of you. It looked scary. 32 The head of the statue was pure gold. Its chest and arms were silver. Its belly and thighs were bronze. 33 Its legs were iron. Its feet were part iron and part clay. 34 While you watched, a stone was cut out, but not by human hands. It hit the statue on its feet of iron and clay. It broke them. 35 Then the iron, clay, bronze, silver, and gold were crushed together. They became like dust on a summer field. The wind blew them away. No one could find them again. But the stone that hit the statue became a huge mountain. It filled the whole earth.

36 "This is the dream. Now I will tell you what it means.

37 "You, O king, are the greatest king. The God of heaven gave you a kingdom, power, strength, and honor. 38 He gave you power over people, animals, and birds. He made you ruler over them all. You are the head of gold. 39 After you, another kingdom will rise. It will not be as strong as yours. Then a third kingdom, made of bronze, will rule over all the earth. 40 The fourth kingdom will be strong like iron. Iron breaks and crushes everything. Like iron, it will break and crush all these kingdoms.

41 "You saw the feet and toes. Some were clay and some were iron. This means the kingdom will be divided. It will have some of the strength of iron, because you saw iron mixed with clay. 42 The toes were part iron and part clay. So this kingdom will be partly strong and partly weak. 43 You saw iron mixed with clay. People will try to join together by marriage, but they will not stay together. Iron does not mix with clay.

44 "In the days of those kings, the God of heaven will set up a kingdom that will never be destroyed. No other people will take it. It will crush and end all these kingdoms. It will stand forever. 45 You saw a stone cut from a mountain, but not by human hands. It crushed the iron, bronze, clay, silver, and gold. The great God has shown the king what will happen after this. The dream is true, and its meaning can be trusted."

The King Honors Daniel

46 Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face. He worshiped Daniel. He ordered that an offering and incense be brought for him. 47 The king said to Daniel, "Truly, your God is the God of gods and the Lord of kings. He shows secrets. That is why you could show this secret." 48 Then the king gave Daniel a high position. He gave him many great gifts. He made him ruler over all of Babylon. He made him chief over all the wise men of Babylon. 49 Daniel asked the king, and the king put Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in charge of the work of Babylon. But Daniel stayed at the king's gate.
