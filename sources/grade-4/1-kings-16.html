God Warns Baasha

1 Then the word of the the LORD came to Jehu, the son of Hanani, against Baasha. It said, 2 "I lifted you up from the dust. I made you a leader over My people Israel. But you walked in the way of Jeroboam. You made My people Israel sin. You made Me angry with their sins. 3 So I will wipe out Baasha and his family. I will make your family like the family of Jeroboam son of Nebat. 4 Dogs will eat the ones from Baasha who die in the city. Birds in the sky will eat the ones who die in the fields."

5 The rest of the things Baasha did, and his power, are written in the book of the kings of Israel. 6 Baasha died and was buried with his fathers in Tirzah. His son Elah became king after him. 7 The word of the the LORD also came through the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and his family. It was because of all the evil he did in the eyes of the the LORD. He made the the LORD angry with what he did. He became like the family of Jeroboam. He also struck down Jeroboam's family.

Elah Rules Israel

8 Elah son of Baasha became king over Israel in Tirzah. This was in the 26th year of Asa, king of Judah. He ruled two years. 9 He was in Tirzah. He got drunk in the house of Arza. Arza was in charge of the palace in Tirzah. 10 Zimri was the leader of half the chariots. He made a plan against Elah. In the 27th year of Asa, king of Judah, Zimri came and struck Elah and killed him. Then Zimri became king.

11 As soon as Zimri sat on the throne, he killed all of Baasha's family. He did not leave one man. He did not even leave a relative or a friend. 12 Zimri wiped out all of Baasha's family. This happened just as the the LORD had said through the prophet Jehu. 13 This was for all the sins of Baasha and his son Elah. They sinned. They made Israel sin. They made the the LORD, the God of Israel, angry with their worthless idols. 14 The rest of what Elah did is written in the book of the kings of Israel.

Zimri Rules for a Short Time

15 Zimri became king in Tirzah. This was in the 27th year of Asa, king of Judah. He ruled seven days. The army was camped at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines. 16 The army in the camp heard that Zimri had made a plan and killed the king. So all Israel made Omri king that day. Omri was the army commander. 17 Omri and all Israel went up from Gibbethon. They surrounded Tirzah. 18 Zimri saw that the city was taken. He went into the strong part of the king's house. He set the king's house on fire around him. He died in the fire.

19 This happened because of the sins Zimri committed. He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He walked in the way of Jeroboam. He made Israel sin. 20 The rest of what Zimri did, and the plan he made, is written in the book of the kings of Israel.

The People Choose a King

21 Then the people of Israel split into two groups. Half followed Tibni son of Ginath and wanted him to be king. The other half followed Omri. 22 The people who followed Omri were stronger than the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath. Tibni died. Then Omri became king.

Omri Rules Israel

23 Omri became king over Israel in the 31st year of Asa, king of Judah. He ruled twelve years. He ruled six years in Tirzah. 24 He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver. He built on the hill. He named the city he built Samaria. He named it after Shemer, who owned the hill.

25 Omri did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He did worse than all who came before him. 26 He walked in all the ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat. He made Israel sin. He made the the LORD, the God of Israel, angry with their worthless idols. 27 The rest of what Omri did, and his power, is written in the book of the kings of Israel. 28 Omri died and was buried in Samaria. His son Ahab became king after him.

Ahab Rules Israel

29 Ahab son of Omri became king over Israel in the 38th year of Asa, king of Judah. Ahab son of Omri ruled over Israel in Samaria for twenty-two years. 30 Ahab son of Omri did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He did more evil than all who came before him.

31 It was not enough for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat. He married Jezebel, the daughter of Ethbaal, king of the Sidonians. He went and served Baal and worshiped him. 32 He set up an altar for Baal in the house of Baal that he built in Samaria. 33 Ahab made a wooden pole for Asherah. He did more to make the the LORD, the God of Israel, angry than all the kings of Israel before him.

34 In Ahab's days, Hiel from Bethel built Jericho again. He laid its foundation with his oldest son, Abiram. He set up its gates with his youngest son, Segub. This happened just as the the LORD had said through Joshua son of Nun.
