The Five Kings Attack Gibeon

1 Adoni-Zedek was the king of Jerusalem. He heard that Joshua had taken Ai and destroyed it. He heard that Joshua did to Ai and its king what he did to Jericho and its king. He also heard that the people of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and now lived with them. 2 He was very afraid. Gibeon was a big city, like a royal city. It was bigger than Ai, and all its men were strong fighters.

3 So Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, sent word to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish, and Debir king of Eglon. 4 He said, "Come up to me and help me. Let's attack Gibeon. It made peace with Joshua and Israel."

5 So these five Amorite kings, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon, gathered together. They went up with all their armies. They camped near Gibeon and fought against it.

Gibeon Asks for Help

6 The men of Gibeon sent a message to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal. They said, "Don't stop helping us, your servants. Come up to us fast and save us and help us. All the Amorite kings in the hills have gathered against us."

7 So Joshua went up from Gilgal. All his army went with him. They were strong fighters.

The LORD Promises Victory

8 The LORD said to Joshua, "Don't be afraid of them. I have given them to you. No one can stand against you." 9 Joshua marched all night from Gilgal and came to them by surprise.

The LORD Routes the Enemy

10 The LORD confused them before Israel. Israel killed many of them at Gibeon. Israel chased them down the road to Upper Beth Horon. Israel killed them all the way to Azekah and Makkedah. 11 As they ran from Israel down the hill from Beth Horon, the LORD threw big hailstones from the sky. More men died from the hail than from the sword.

The Sun Stands Still

12 On that day the LORD gave the Amorites to Israel. Joshua prayed to the LORD while Israel watched. He said, "Sun, stand still over Gibeon. Moon, stand still over the valley of Aijalon." 13 The sun stopped. The moon stood still until Israel beat its enemies. This is written in the Book of Jashar. The sun stayed in the middle of the sky. It did not go down for about a whole day. 14 There has never been a day like that before or since. The LORD listened to a man. The LORD fought for Israel.

15 Then Joshua and all Israel went back to the camp at Gilgal.

The Five Kings Are Caught

16 The five kings ran away. They hid in a cave at Makkedah.

17 Someone told Joshua, "The five kings are hiding in a cave at Makkedah."

18 Joshua said, "Roll big stones to the mouth of the cave. Put men there to guard them."

Chase the Enemy

19 Joshua said to his men, "Don't you stay here. Chase the enemy. Attack them from behind. Don't let them get to their cities. The LORD your God has given them to you."

20 Joshua and Israel killed almost all of them. The ones left ran to safe cities. 21 Then all the people went back safely to Joshua at the camp at Makkedah. No one said a bad word against the Israelites.

The Kings Are Brought Out

22 Joshua said, "Open the cave. Bring the five kings out to me."

23 So they did. They brought out the five kings: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon.

24 When they brought the kings to Joshua, he called all the men of Israel. He said to the army leaders who went with him, "Come here. Put your feet on the necks of these kings." So they came and put their feet on their necks. 25 Then Joshua said to them, "Don't be afraid. Don't give up. Be strong and brave. The LORD will do this to all your enemies you fight."

26 Then Joshua killed them. He hung them on five trees. They hung there until evening.

27 At sunset Joshua told them to take the bodies down. They threw them into the cave where they had hidden. They put big stones at the mouth of the cave. The stones are still there today.

Joshua Takes More Cities

28 That day Joshua took Makkedah. He killed everyone in it. He destroyed it, just like he did to Jericho. He did the same to its king. No one was left alive.

29 Then Joshua and all Israel went from Makkedah to Libnah. They fought against it. 30 The LORD gave Libnah and its king to Israel. Israel killed everyone there. No one was left alive. Joshua did to its king what he did to Jericho's king.

31 Then Joshua and all Israel went from Libnah to Lachish. They camped there and fought. 32 The LORD gave Lachish to Israel. Israel took it on the second day. They killed everyone there, just like they did to Libnah.

33 Then Horam, king of Gezer, came to help Lachish. Joshua killed him and his army. No one was left alive.

34 Then Joshua and all Israel went from Lachish to Eglon. They camped and fought. 35 They took it that day. They killed everyone there. They destroyed it, just like they did to Lachish.

36 Then Joshua and all Israel went up from Eglon to Hebron. They fought against it. 37 They took it. They killed the king, the nearby towns, and all the people. No one was left alive. Joshua destroyed it and all its people, just like he did to Eglon.

38 Then Joshua and all Israel went back to Debir. They fought against it. 39 They took it. They killed the king and the nearby towns. They killed everyone there. No one was left alive. Joshua treated Debir and its king the same way he treated Hebron and Libnah.

Joshua Wins the Land

40 Joshua took all the land: the hills, the south country, the lowlands, and the slopes. He killed all the kings. No one was left alive. He destroyed every living thing, just as the LORD God of Israel had told him to do. 41 Joshua fought from Kadesh Barnea to Gaza. He took all the land of Goshen up to Gibeon. 42 Joshua took all these kings and their land at one time, because the LORD God of Israel fought for Israel.

43 Then Joshua and all Israel went back to the camp at Gilgal.
