People Grow and Do Wrong

1 People began to grow in number on the earth. Daughters were born to them. 2 The sons of God saw that the daughters of people were pretty. They married any women they wanted.

3 Then the LORD said, "My Spirit will not stay with people forever. They are only human. They will live 120 years."

4 In those days, and later too, there were Nephilim on the earth. The sons of God went to the daughters of people, and they had children. These children became the strong men of long ago. They were famous men.

God Is Sad About People's Bad Choices

5 The LORD saw that people were very bad on the earth. Every thought in their hearts was only bad all the time. 6 The LORD was sorry He had made people on the earth. He was sad in His heart.

7 So the LORD said, "I will wipe people off the earth. I made them, but now I will wipe out people, animals, bugs, and birds. I am sad that I made them." 8 But Noah found favor with the LORD.

Noah Was a Good Man

9 This is the story of Noah's family. Noah was a good man. He did right in his time. He walked with God. 10 Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

The Earth Is Full of Fighting

11 The earth was ruined in God's eyes. It was full of fighting. 12 God saw that the earth was ruined. All people had ruined their lives on the earth.

God Tells Noah to Build an Ark

13 God said to Noah, "The time has come to end all living things. The earth is full of fighting because of them. I will destroy them and the earth.

"14 Make yourself an ark from gopher wood. Make rooms in it. Cover it inside and out with tar. 15 This is how you will make it: The ark will be 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. 16 Make a window near the top. Finish it one cubit from the top. Put the door on the side. Make lower, second, and third floors.

"17 I am bringing a flood of water on the earth. It will destroy all living things under the sky. Everything on earth will die. 18 But I will make My promise with you. You, your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives will go into the ark with you.

"19 Bring two of every living thing into the ark. Bring them to keep them alive with you. They will be male and female. 20 Birds, animals, and all small things on the ground will come to you in pairs. This will keep them alive. 21 Take all the food that can be eaten. Gather it for yourself and for them. It will be food for you and for them."

22 Noah did all this. He did just what God told him to do.
