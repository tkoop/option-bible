David's Sons Born in Hebron

1 These were David's sons born to him in Hebron. The first son was Amnon. His mother was Ahinoam from Jezreel. The second son was Daniel. His mother was Abigail from Carmel. 2 The third son was Absalom. His mother was Maacah, daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur. The fourth son was Adonijah. His mother was Haggith. 3 The fifth son was Shephatiah. His mother was Abital. The sixth son was Ithream. His mother was David's wife Eglah. 4 Six sons were born to David in Hebron. He ruled there seven years and six months. He also ruled in Jerusalem thirty-three years.

David's Sons Born in Jerusalem

5 These sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. Their mother was Bathshua, daughter of Ammiel. 6 Ibhar, Elishama, and Eliphelet were born there too. 7 Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia were also born there. 8 Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet were born there as well. Those nine sons were in addition to the first four. 9 All these were David's sons. He also had sons by his other wives. Tamar was their sister.

Kings in David's Family Line

10 Solomon's son was Rehoboam. Rehoboam's son was Abijah. Abijah's son was Asa. Asa's son was Jehoshaphat. 11 Jehoshaphat's son was Joram. Joram's son was Ahaziah. Ahaziah's son was Joash. 12 Joash's son was Amaziah. Amaziah's son was Azariah. Azariah's son was Jotham. 13 Jotham's son was Ahaz. Ahaz's son was Hezekiah. Hezekiah's son was Manasseh. 14 Manasseh's son was Amon. Amon's son was Josiah.

Josiah's Sons

15 Josiah's sons were Johanan, the firstborn; Jehoiakim, the second; Zedekiah, the third; and Shallum, the fourth. 16 Jehoiakim's sons were Jeconiah and Zedekiah. 17 Jeconiah, the captive, had a son named Shealtiel. 18 Jeconiah's other sons were Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

More of David's Family Line

19 Pedaiah's sons were Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel's sons were Meshullam and Hananiah. Shelomith was their sister. 20 Five more of his sons were Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed. 21 Hananiah's sons were Pelatiah and Jeshaiah. The sons of Rephaiah, Arnan, Obadiah, and Shecaniah are also named. 22 Shecaniah's son was Shemaiah. Shemaiah's sons were Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat. That was six in all. 23 Neariah's sons were Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam. That was three in all. 24 Elioenai's sons were Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani. That was seven in all.
