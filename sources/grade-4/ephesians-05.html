Live Like God's Dear Children

1 So be like God. You are His dear children. 2 Live with love, just as Christ loved us. He gave Himself for us. He was like a sweet-smelling gift and sacrifice to God.

3 Do not let sexual sin, dirty wrongs, or wanting too much even be talked about among you. God's holy people should not do these things. 4 Do not say dirty words. Do not talk in a silly or rude way. These things do not fit God's people. Say thanks to God instead. 5 Be sure of this: no one who does sexual sin, dirty wrongs, or wants too much will get a share in Christ's and God's kingdom. A greedy person worships things instead of God.

6 Do not let anyone trick you with words that mean nothing. God is angry at people who do not obey Him. These things bring His anger. 7 So do not join them in doing wrong.

Walk in the Light

8 You used to be in the dark. Now you are light in the Lord. Live like children of light. 9 Light brings all goodness, right living, and truth. 10 Find out what makes the Lord happy.

11 Do not join in the useless works of darkness. Instead, show that they are wrong. 12 It is shameful even to talk about what some people do in secret. 13 When light shines, it shows what is really there. Anything that shows the truth is like light.

14 That is why it says, "Wake up, you who are sleeping! Get up from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."

Live Wisely

15 So be very careful how you live. Do not live like people who are not wise. Live like wise people. 16 Use your time well, because these days are bad. 17 So do not be foolish. Understand what the Lord wants.

18 Do not get drunk with wine. That leads to wild living. Instead, be filled with the Spirit. 19 Speak to each other with psalms and songs of praise. Sing and make music in your heart to the Lord. 20 Always give thanks to God the Father for everything, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 21 Be ready to serve each other out of respect for Christ.

Wives and Husbands

22 Wives, obey your own husbands as you would obey the Lord. 23 The husband is the head of the wife, just as Christ is the head of the church. He is the Savior of the body. 24 Just as the church obeys Christ, wives should obey their husbands in everything.

25 Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave Himself for her. 26 He did this to make her holy. He washed her with water and His word. 27 He did this so He could bring her to Himself as a beautiful church. She would have no spot or wrinkle or any other flaw. She would be holy and without blame.

28 In the same way, husbands should love their own wives as they love their own bodies. A man who loves his wife loves himself. 29 No one ever hated his own body. Instead, he feeds it and takes care of it. Christ does the same for the church. 30 We are parts of His body.

31 That is why it says, "A man leaves his father and mother and joins his wife. The two become one body."

32 This is a deep truth. I am talking about Christ and the church. 33 So each of you should love his wife as he loves himself. And the wife should respect her husband.
