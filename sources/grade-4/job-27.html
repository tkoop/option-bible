Job Speaks Again

1 Job kept talking. He said:

"2 God is alive, but He took away my rights. The Almighty made my heart sad. 3 While I still breathe, and God's breath is in my nose, 4 my lips will not say wrong things, and my tongue will not tell lies.

"5 I will never say you are right. I will never give up my good name. 6 I will hold on to my right ways. I will not let them go. My heart will not blame me as long as I live. 7 Let my enemy be like bad men. Let the one who fights me be like a wrongdoer.

"8 What hope does a man who does not care about God have when he is cut off, when God takes his life? 9 Will God hear his cry when trouble comes on him? 10 Will he be happy with the Almighty? Will he call on God all the time?

"11 I will teach you about God's power. I will not hide what the Almighty has planned. 12 You have all seen this. Why do you talk nonsense?

What Happens to Bad Men

"13 This is what bad men get from God. This is what cruel men get from the Almighty: 14 If he has many sons, they will die by the sword. His children will not have enough food. 15 The ones left after him will be buried. Their widows will not cry.

"16 He may pile up silver like dust. He may save clothes like clay. 17 But good people will wear what he got ready. Innocent people will share his silver. 18 The house he builds is weak, like a moth's nest. It is like a small tent a watchman makes. 19 He goes to bed rich, but he will not stay that way. He opens his eyes, and he is gone.

"20 Fear comes on him like a flood. A storm carries him away at night. 21 The east wind picks him up and takes him away. It blows him from his place. 22 It hits him hard with no mercy. He runs from its power in fear. 23 It claps its hands at him and hisses at him from where it is."
