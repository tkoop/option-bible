God Was Good to His Land

1 The LORD, You were good to Your land. You made things good again for Jacob's family. 2 You took away Your people's sin. You covered all their wrongs. Selah.

3 You let go of Your anger. You stopped being mad.

Please Help Us Again

4 God, our Savior, please bring us back again. Please stop being mad at us. 5 Will You be mad at us forever? Will Your anger last for all time? 6 Won't You make us live again? Then Your people will be happy with You. 7 The LORD, show us Your kind love. Give us Your help.

God Promises Peace

8 I will listen to what God the the LORD says. He promises peace to His people and to those who follow Him. They must not go back to doing foolish things. 9 His help is near to those who respect Him. Then His glory will stay in our land.

Good Things Will Come

10 Love and truth come together. Right and peace become friends. 11 Truth will grow from the ground. Right will come from heaven. 12 The LORD will give good things. Our land will grow good food. 13 Right will walk ahead of Him. It will make a path for His feet.
