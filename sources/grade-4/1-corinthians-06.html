Don't Settle Problems in Court

1 If one of you has a problem with another person, do you dare take it to court before people who do wrong, instead of before God's holy people? 2 Don't you know that God's holy people will judge the world? If you will judge the world, aren't you good enough to judge small problems? 3 Don't you know we will judge angels? So we can surely judge everyday problems.

4 So if you have everyday problems to settle, why do you pick people the church looks down on to judge them? 5 I say this to make you feel ashamed. Isn't there even one wise person among you who can settle a problem between two believers? 6 Instead, one believer takes another believer to court, and people who do not believe in God watch.

7 So the fact that you have court cases against each other is already a loss for you. Why not let someone wrong you? Why not let someone cheat you? 8 But you are the ones who cheat and hurt people, even your own brothers in faith.

Who Will Be in God's Kingdom?

9 Don't you know that people who do wrong will not get God's kingdom? Don't be fooled. People who live in sexual sin, worship fake gods, cheat on their spouse, or have sex with people of the same gender will not get it. 10 Thieves, greedy people, drunkards, people who say mean things, and cheaters will not get God's kingdom either. 11 Some of you used to do these things. But you were washed clean. You were made holy. You were made right with God in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by the Spirit of our God.

Use Your Body to Honor God

12 I am allowed to do all things, but not all things help me. I am allowed to do all things, but I will not be controlled by anything. 13 Food is for the stomach, and the stomach is for food. But God will do away with both. The body is not for sexual sin. It is for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body. 14 God raised the Lord by His power, and He will raise us too.

15 Don't you know your bodies are parts of Christ? Should I take what belongs to Christ and join it to a prostitute? No! 16 Don't you know that a man who joins himself to a prostitute becomes one body with her? The Bible says, "The two will become one flesh."

17 But anyone who joins himself to the Lord becomes one spirit with Him. 18 Run away from sexual sin. Every other sin a person does is outside the body. But sexual sin is a sin against his own body. 19 Don't you know your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, who lives in you? You have Him from God, and you do not belong to yourselves. 20 God paid a high price to buy you. So honor God with your body.
