A Song by David

1 For the music leader. A song by David. David served the LORD. A thought came to my heart about bad people's sin: "They do not fear God."

2 He thinks he is too good. He does not see his sin or hate it. 3 His words are mean and full of lies. He stopped being wise and good. 4 Even in bed he plans bad things. He walks a bad road. He does not say no to evil.

God's Great Love

5 The LORD, Your kind love reaches the sky. Your truth reaches the clouds. 6 Your goodness is like God's tall mountains. Your fair choices are like the deep ocean. The LORD, You keep people and animals safe.

7 How special Your kind love is, God! People find safety in the shadow of Your wings. 8 They eat the good things in Your house. You give them water from Your river of joy. 9 You are the source of life. In Your light, we see clearly.

A Prayer for Help

10 Keep showing Your kind love to people who know You. Show Your right way to people with honest hearts. 11 Do not let proud people step on me. Do not let bad people push me away. 12 There the bad people fall. They are thrown down and cannot get up.
