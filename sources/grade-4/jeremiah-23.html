God Will Punish Bad Shepherds

1 How sad for the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep I care for! says the the LORD.

2 So the the LORD, the God of Israel, says to the shepherds who lead My people: You scattered My flock. You drove them away. You did not take care of them. So now I will punish you for the bad things you did, says the the LORD.

3 I will gather the rest of My flock from all the lands where I sent them. I will bring them back to their own land. They will have many children and grow.

4 I will give them new shepherds. The new shepherds will take care of them. Then My people will not be afraid or upset. Not one will be missing, says the the LORD.

A Good King Is Coming

5 Look, the days are coming, says the the LORD. I will give David a good and fair king. He will be like a new branch. He will rule well. He will do what is right and fair in the land.

6 In his time, Judah will be safe, and Israel will live in peace. His name will be “The LORD Is Our Righteousness.”

God Will Bring His People Home

7 So look, the days are coming, says the the LORD. People will not say anymore, “As sure as the the LORD is alive, who brought Israel up from Egypt.”

8 Instead they will say, “The The LORD is alive! He brought Israel home from the north and from all the lands where He sent them.” Then they will live in their own land.

Bad Prophets Hurt the Land

9 My heart is broken inside me. All my bones shake. I am like a drunk man, like a man full of wine. This is because of the the LORD and His holiness.

10 The land is full of people who are not faithful in marriage. The The LORD’s curse makes the land sad. The fields are dry. The people do wrong, and their strength is not used for good.

11 The prophets and priests are not living for God. I found their evil even in My house, says the the LORD.

12 So their path will be slippery. They will be pushed into the dark and fall. I will bring trouble on them in the year I punish them, says the the LORD.

False Prophets in Samaria and Jerusalem

13 I saw something foolish in the prophets of Samaria. They prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel the wrong way.

14 I saw a terrible thing in the prophets of Jerusalem. They are not faithful in marriage, and they live by lies. They make bad people stronger, so no one stops doing wrong. To me they are all like Sodom, and the people there are like Gomorrah.

15 So the the LORD All-Powerful says about these prophets: I will make them eat bitter food and drink poisoned water, because ungodly ways came from Jerusalem’s prophets and spread through the whole land.

Do Not Listen to False Prophets

16 The The LORD All-Powerful says: “Do not listen to the prophets who speak to you. They are filling your heads with empty things. They tell you their own ideas, not words from the the LORD.”

17 They keep telling people who do not like the the LORD, “The The LORD says you will have peace.” They tell everyone who follows their own stubborn heart, “No bad thing will come to you.”

18 But who has been close to the the LORD? Who has seen and heard His words? Who has listened to His words and obeyed?

The LORD’s Storm

19 Look, the the LORD’s storm is coming. His anger is like a strong wind. It will hit the heads of bad people.

20 The The LORD’s anger will not turn back until He has done what He planned in His heart. In the days to come, you will understand this clearly.

The LORD Did Not Send These Prophets

21 I did not send these prophets, but they ran to speak. I did not speak to them, but they prophesied.

22 If they had stood with me, they would have told My people My words. Then My people would have turned from their bad ways and bad actions.

God Is Everywhere

23 Am I a God who is only near? says the the LORD. Am I not also a God far away?

24 Can anyone hide in a secret place so I cannot see him? says the the LORD. Do I not fill heaven and earth? says the the LORD.

Dreams Are Not God’s Word

25 I have heard what the prophets say. They prophesy lies in My name. They say, “I had a dream! I had a dream!”

26 How long will these lying prophets keep false ideas in their hearts?

27 They think their dreams can make My people forget My name, just as their fathers forgot My name and served Baal.

28 A prophet who has a dream may tell the dream. A prophet who has My word must speak My word faithfully. Empty talk is not the same as God’s true word, says the the LORD.

29 My word is like fire, says the the LORD. It is like a hammer that breaks rock.

The LORD Is Against These Prophets

30 So I am against the prophets, says the the LORD, who steal My words from each other.

31 I am against the prophets, says the the LORD, who use their tongues and say, “The The LORD says this.”

32 I am against prophets who tell false dreams, says the the LORD. They tell them and lead My people the wrong way with lies and foolish talk. I did not send them or command them. They do not help My people at all, says the the LORD.

The LORD’s Heavy Load

33 When these people, or a prophet, or a priest asks you, “What is the the LORD’s heavy load?”

You must say to them, “You are the heavy load! I will throw you away, says the the LORD.”

34 I will punish any prophet, priest, or person who says, “This is the the LORD’s heavy load.” I will punish him and his family.

35 This is what each person says to his neighbor and to his brother: “What did the the LORD answer?” and “What did the the LORD say?”

36 Do not say “the the LORD’s heavy load” anymore. That phrase is just a person’s own words. You are changing the words of the living God, the the LORD All-Powerful, our God.

37 This is what you say to the prophet: “What did the the LORD answer you?” and “What did the the LORD say?”

38 If you say, “This is the the LORD’s heavy load,” the the LORD says this: Because you said, “The the LORD’s heavy load,” even though I told you not to say that,

39 I will make you forget completely. I will throw you away from Me. I will throw away the city I gave to you and your fathers.

40 I will bring lasting shame on you. People will never forget it.
