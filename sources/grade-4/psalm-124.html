God Saved Us from Danger

1 If the the LORD had not been on our side, let Israel now say. 2 If the the LORD had not been on our side when people rose up against us, 3 they would have wiped us out. They were very angry with us. 4 The waters would have rushed over us. The flood would have swept over our lives. 5 Yes, the wild waters would have swept over us.

Our Help Comes from God

6 Praise the the LORD, who did not let us become food for their teeth. 7 We got away like a bird from a trap. The trap broke, and we got free. 8 Our help is in the name of the the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
