A Message About Egypt

1 This is a message about Egypt. Look! The LORD rides on a fast cloud and comes to Egypt. Egypt's false gods shake with fear. The Egyptians' hearts melt inside them.

2 The LORD says, "I will make Egyptians fight each other. They will fight brother against brother and friend against friend. City will fight city, and kingdom will fight kingdom."

3 The Egyptians will lose their courage. Their plans will come to nothing. They will ask their false gods, fortune tellers, and people who talk to spirits for help.

4 The LORD says, "I will hand Egypt over to a hard master. A strong, mean king will rule them." This is what the Lord, the LORD of Armies, says.

The Nile Will Dry Up

5 The water in the sea will dry up. The river will become dry and cracked.

6 The canals will smell bad. The streams of Egypt will get low and dry up. The reeds and tall grass will die.

7 Grass along the Nile will dry up. All the seeds planted by the Nile will dry up and blow away. They will be gone.

8 The fishermen will be sad. Everyone who throws a hook in the Nile will cry. The people who spread nets on the water will feel weak.

9 The people who work with flax will be upset. The people who weave fine cloth will be upset too.

10 The people who hold up the land will be crushed. Everyone who works for pay will be sad.

Egypt's Leaders Are Foolish

11 The leaders of Zoan are very foolish. Pharaoh's wise men give bad advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, "I am the son of wise men. I come from old kings"?

12 Where are your wise men? Let them tell you what the the LORD of Armies has planned against Egypt. They should know.

13 The leaders of Zoan are fools. The leaders of Memphis are tricked. The leaders who should guide Egypt's people have led Egypt the wrong way.

14 The The LORD put a spirit of confusion in Egypt. Egypt staggers in all it does, like a drunk man staggering in his own mess.

15 No one in Egypt can help—not the rich or the poor, not the strong or the weak.

Egypt Will Be Afraid

16 In that day, the Egyptians will be weak and afraid. They will tremble at the the LORD of Armies' hand when He shakes it over them.

17 The land of Judah will scare Egypt. Everyone who hears about it will be afraid because of what the the LORD of Armies planned against Egypt.

18 In that day, five cities in Egypt will speak the language of Canaan. They will promise to follow the the LORD of Armies. One city will be called the City of Destruction.

19 In that day, there will be an altar to the the LORD in the middle of Egypt. There will be a stone pillar to the the LORD near its border.

20 It will be a sign and a witness for the the LORD of Armies in Egypt. When the Egyptians cry out to the the LORD because of people who hurt them, He will send a savior to rescue them.

21 The the LORD will show Himself to the Egyptians. They will know the the LORD that day. They will worship Him with gifts and offerings. They will make promises to the the LORD and keep them.

22 The the LORD will hit Egypt and then heal it. The Egyptians will come back to the the LORD. He will listen to their prayers and heal them.

A Road Between Egypt and Assyria

23 In that day, there will be a road from Egypt to Assyria. Assyrians will go to Egypt, and Egyptians will go to Assyria. Egyptians and Assyrians will worship the the LORD together.

24 In that day, Israel will be the third nation with Egypt and Assyria. They will be a blessing in the middle of the earth.

25 The the LORD of Armies will bless them. He will say, "Egypt is My people. Assyria is the work of My hands. Israel is My own land."
