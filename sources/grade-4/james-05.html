Warning to Rich People

1 Come now, you rich people. Cry and wail, because hard times are coming to you. 2 Your money has gone bad. Moths have eaten your clothes. 3 Your gold and silver have rusted. Their rust will show you are wrong. It will eat your bodies like fire. You saved up money for the last days.

4 Look, the pay you kept from the workers who cut your fields is crying out against you. The harvesters' cries have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts. 5 You lived for fun and comfort. You fed your hearts for the day of slaughter. 6 You judged and killed the good man. He does not fight back against you.

Be Patient Until the Lord Comes

7 So be patient, brothers. Wait until the Lord comes. Look at the farmer. He waits for the good crop from the land. He is patient until it gets the early and late rain. 8 You be patient too. Make your hearts strong, because the Lord is coming soon. 9 Do not groan against each other, brothers, so you will not be judged. Look, the Judge stands at the door.

Learn from the Prophets

10 Brothers, look at the prophets. They suffered and were patient. They spoke in the name of the Lord. Take them as an example. 11 We think people who keep going are blessed. You have heard about Job. He kept going. You saw what the Lord did at the end. The Lord is full of kindness and mercy.

Do Not Swear

12 Above all, my brothers, do not swear. Do not swear by heaven, earth, or anything else. Let your yes be yes, and your no be no. Then you will not fall under judgment.

Pray for Those Who Are Sick

13 Is anyone among you suffering? He should pray. Is anyone happy? He should sing. 14 Is anyone among you sick? He should call the church leaders. They should pray over him and rub oil on him in the name of the Lord. 15 The prayer of faith will save the sick person. The Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven. 16 So tell each other your sins. Pray for each other, so you may be healed. The prayer of a good person is powerful and it works.

Elijah Prayed

17 Elijah was a man like us. He prayed hard that it would not rain. It did not rain on the land for three and a half years. 18 He prayed again. The sky gave rain. The land grew its crop.

Help Those Who Wander

19 My brothers, if one of you wanders from the truth, and someone brings him back, 20 know this: The one who turns a sinner from his wrong way will save his soul from death. He will cover a lot of sins.
