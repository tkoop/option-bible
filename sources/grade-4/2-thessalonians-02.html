Do Not Be Fooled About the Lord's Coming

1 Now, about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered to Him: we ask you, friends, 2 not to be quickly shaken in your mind or scared. Do not be fooled by a spirit, a word, or a letter that seems to come from us. It might say the day of the Lord has already come.

3 Do not let anyone trick you in any way. That day will not come until people turn away from God first. Then the man of lawlessness will be shown. He is the son of destruction. 4 He fights against God and puts himself above every god or thing people worship. He will even sit in God's temple and say he is God.

5 Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?

6 And you know what is holding him back now. He will be shown when the time is right. 7 The secret power of lawlessness is already working. But the one who holds it back will keep doing so until he is taken out of the way.

8 Then the lawless one will be shown. The Lord Jesus will kill him with the breath of His mouth. He will destroy him when He comes. 9 The lawless one will come with Satan's power. He will do all kinds of power, signs, and false wonders. 10 He will trick people who are lost. They did not love the truth that could save them.

11 So God sends them a strong lie. They believe what is false. 12 Then all who did not believe the truth but liked doing wrong will be judged.

God Chose You

13 But we must always thank God for you, friends loved by the Lord. God chose you from the start to be saved. He makes you holy by the Spirit and helps you believe the truth. 14 He called you through the good news we told you. He did this so you can share in the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Stand Firm

15 So, friends, stand firm. Hold on to the teachings we gave you, whether by word or by letter.

16 May our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father comfort you. They loved us and gave us lasting comfort and good hope by His grace. 17 May He make your hearts strong. May He help you do and say every good thing.
