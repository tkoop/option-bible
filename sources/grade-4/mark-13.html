Jesus Leaves the Temple

1As Jesus was leaving the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, “Teacher, look at these big stones and buildings!”

2Jesus said, “Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left on another. They will all be torn down.”

Jesus Talks on the Mount of Olives

3Jesus was sitting on the Mount of Olives, across from the temple. Peter, James, John, and Andrew came to Him alone.

4They said, “Tell us, when will these things happen? What sign will show that all these things are about to be finished?”

Be Careful

5Jesus began to tell them, “Watch out! Don't let anyone trick you. 6Many will come using My name. They will say, ‘I am He,’ and they will trick many people.

7“When you hear about wars and news of wars, don't be scared. These things must happen, but the end is not yet. 8Nation will rise against nation. Kingdom will rise against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in many places. There will be famines. These are just the start of the hard times.

Hard Times for Jesus' Friends

9“Watch out for yourselves. People will hand you over to courts. You will be beaten in meeting places. Because of Me, you will stand before rulers and kings. You will tell them about Me. 10The good news must first be told to all nations. 11When they arrest you and hand you over, don't worry ahead of time about what to say. Say what is given to you at that time. You will not be the ones speaking. The Holy Spirit will speak.

12“Brother will hand over brother to die. A father will hand over his child. Children will turn against their parents and kill them. 13Everyone will hate you because of My name. But the one who stays strong to the end will be saved.

Run Away When Trouble Comes

14“When you see the terrible thing that destroys standing where it should not be—let the reader understand—then people in Judea must run to the mountains. 15If someone is on the roof, don't go down into the house to get anything. 16If someone is in the field, don't go back to get a coat. 17It will be very hard for pregnant women and nursing mothers in those days. 18Pray that it won't happen in winter.

The Worst Trouble Ever

19“Those days will have worse trouble than any time since God made the world until now. Nothing like it will ever happen again. 20If the Lord had not made those days shorter, no one would live. But He made them shorter for the sake of His chosen people.

False Christs Will Come

21“If someone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or ‘There He is!’ don't believe it. 22False Christs and false prophets will come. They will do signs and wonders to trick God's chosen people. If it were possible, they would trick even them. 23So watch out. I have told you everything ahead of time.

The Son of Man Will Come

24“But in those days, after that trouble, the sun will go dark. The moon will not give its light. 25The stars will fall from the sky. The powers in heaven will shake. 26Then people will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory. 27Then He will send His angels. He will gather His chosen people from all directions, from one end of the earth to the other.

Learn from the Fig Tree

28“Learn this lesson from the fig tree. When its branch gets soft and leaves come out, you know summer is near. 29So when you see these things happening, you know He is near, right at the door. 30I tell you the truth: the people living now will not all die before all these things happen. 31Heaven and earth will end, but My words will never end.

No One Knows the Day

32“No one knows that day or hour. The angels in heaven don't know. The Son doesn't know. Only the Father knows. 33Watch out and stay awake, because you don't know when the time will come.

Stay Awake

34“It is like a man who went on a trip. He left his house and gave each servant a job. He told the doorkeeper to stay awake. 35So stay awake. You don't know when the owner of the house will come. It could be evening, midnight, when the rooster crows, or morning. 36Don't let him come suddenly and find you sleeping. 37What I say to you, I say to everyone: Stay awake!”
