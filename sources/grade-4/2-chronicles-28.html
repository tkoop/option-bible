Ahaz Becomes King

1 Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for sixteen years. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, like his father David. 2 He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel. He also made metal statues for the Baals. 3 He burned incense in the Valley of Ben Hinnom. He burned his sons in the fire. This was like the terrible things the nations did. The LORD had driven those nations out before the people of Israel. 4 He offered sacrifices and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree.

Judah Is Punished

5 So the LORD his God let him fall into the hands of the king of Aram. The Arameans beat him. They took many people away to Damascus. He was also given into the hands of the king of Israel, who hit him hard. 6 Pekah son of Remaliah killed one hundred twenty thousand people in Judah in one day. They were all strong soldiers. This happened because they had left the LORD, the God of their fathers. 7 Zichri, a strong man from Ephraim, killed Maaseiah, the king's son. He killed Azrikam, the leader of the palace. He killed Elkanah, who was second to the king. 8 The Israelites took two hundred thousand of their brothers as prisoners. They took women, sons, and daughters. They also took many things. They brought the things to Samaria.

A Prophet Speaks

9 But a prophet of the LORD named Oded was there. He went out to meet the army that was coming to Samaria. He said to them, "Look, the LORD God of your fathers was angry with Judah. So He gave them into your hand. You killed them in a rage that reached up to heaven. 10 And now you plan to make the people of Judah and Jerusalem your male and female slaves. Are you not also guilty before the LORD your God? 11 Now hear me. Send back the captives you took from your brothers. The LORD's fierce anger is on you."

Leaders Stop the Captives

12 Then some leaders of Ephraim stood against the men coming back from war. They were Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai. 13 They said to them, "You must not bring the captives here. You are trying to bring guilt on us from the LORD. That would add to our sins and guilt. Our guilt is already great, and the the LORD's fierce anger is on Israel." 14 So the armed men left the captives and the things they had taken in front of the leaders and the whole group. 15 The men named above stood up. They took the captives. From the things taken, they clothed all who had no clothes. They gave them clothes and sandals. They gave them food and drink. They put oil on them. They put all the weak ones on donkeys. They brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, near their brothers. Then they went back to Samaria.

More Enemies Attack

16 At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria for help. 17 The Edomites had come again. They struck Judah and carried away captives. 18 The Philistines raided the cities in the lowlands and in the south of Judah. They captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco and its nearby towns, Timnah and its nearby towns, and Gimzo and its nearby towns. Then they settled there. 19 The LORD had brought Judah low because of Ahaz, king of Israel. He led Judah to do wrong, and he had been very unfaithful to the LORD.

Assyria Does Not Help

20 Tilgath-pilneser, king of Assyria, came against him. He troubled him instead of helping him. 21 Ahaz took part of the the LORD's house, part of the king's house, and part from the leaders. He gave it to the king of Assyria. But it did not help him. 22 In the time of trouble, King Ahaz became even more unfaithful to the LORD.

Ahaz Worships Other Gods

23 He offered sacrifices to the gods of Damascus, the ones that had beaten him. He said, "The gods of the kings of Aram help them. I will sacrifice to them so they will help me." But they caused his ruin, and the ruin of all Israel. 24 Ahaz gathered the things from God's house. He cut up the things from God's house. He shut the doors of the the LORD's house. He made altars in every corner of Jerusalem. 25 In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods. This made the LORD, the God of his fathers, angry.

Ahaz Dies

26 The rest of his acts and his ways, from first to last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel. 27 Ahaz died and was buried with his fathers in the city in Jerusalem. But they did not bury him in the tombs of the kings of Israel. His son Hezekiah became king after him.
