God Will Take Away Help

1 Look! The Lord, the LORD of hosts, is taking away help from Jerusalem and Judah. He is taking away all the food and water they need. 2 He is taking away strong men, soldiers, judges, prophets, people who tell the future, and old leaders. 3 He is taking away captains of fifty, important men, advisors, smart builders, and people who know secret charms.

Young Leaders and Hard Times

4 I will make boys their leaders. Little children will rule over them. 5 People will be hard on each other. One person will fight with another. Young people will be rude to old people. People no one respects will boss around people who are respected.

6 A man will grab his brother in his father's house and say, "You have a coat. Be our leader. Take care of this ruined place."

7 But that day he will say, "I will not fix things. I have no food and no clothes at home. Do not make me the leader of the people."

Judah Has Turned Away

8 Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen. They speak and act against the LORD. They go against His glory. 9 The look on their faces shows they are guilty. They brag about their sin like the people of Sodom did. They do not hide it. How sad for them! They have brought trouble on themselves.

Good and Bad People

10 Tell good people that it will go well for them. They will enjoy the good they have done. 11 How sad for bad people! Things will go badly for them. They will be paid back for what they did.

My People Are Misled

12 My people are ruled by children. Women lead them. My people, your leaders lead you the wrong way. They have ruined your path.

The LORD Judges His People

13 The LORD stands to argue His case. He stands to judge the nations. 14 The LORD will come to judge the old leaders and rulers of His people. He will say, "You have ruined the vineyard. The things you took from poor people are in your houses." 15 "Why do you crush My people? Why do you hurt poor people?" says the Lord, the LORD of hosts.

A Warning to Proud Women

16 The LORD said, "Because the women of Zion are proud. They walk with their necks held high. They flirt with their eyes. They take small steps as they walk. They make noise with their feet. 17 So the Lord will put sores on the heads of Zion's women. The LORD will show their private parts."

Fancy Things Will Be Taken Away

18 On that day the Lord will take away their fancy ankle chains, headbands, and moon-shaped necklaces. 19 He will take away their dangling earrings, bracelets, and veils. 20 He will take away their pretty hats, arm bands, belts, perfume boxes, and charms. 21 He will take away their rings and nose rings. 22 He will take away their party dresses, capes, coats, and purses. 23 He will take away their mirrors, fine linen clothes, head wraps, and shawls.

Sad Changes and Loss

24 Instead of perfume, there will be a bad smell. Instead of a belt, a rope. Instead of nice hair, baldness. Instead of a fine dress, a rough cloth dress. Instead of beauty, a burn mark. 25 Your men will fall by the sword. Your strong men will die in battle. 26 Her gates will cry out and be sad. She will be empty and sit on the ground.
