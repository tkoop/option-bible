The LORD Tells Joshua to Take Stones

1 After all the people crossed the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua, 2 "Take twelve men for yourselves from the people. Pick one man from each tribe. 3 Tell them, 'Take twelve stones for yourselves from the middle of the Jordan. Take them from where the priests stood. Carry them with you. Put them down where you camp tonight.'"

Joshua Chooses the Men

4 Joshua called the twelve men he had chosen from the Israelites. He picked one man from each tribe. 5 Joshua said to them, "Cross over in front of the ark of the LORD your God. Go into the middle of the Jordan. Each of you lift one stone for yourselves onto his shoulder. There should be one stone for each tribe of Israel. 6 This will be a sign for you. Tomorrow your children may ask, 'What do these stones mean to you?' 7 Tell them, 'The Jordan's water stopped flowing. It stopped in front of the ark of the LORD's promise when the ark crossed the Jordan. These stones will remind the people of Israel forever.'"

The People Cross the River

8 The people of Israel did what Joshua told them. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan. They took one stone for each tribe, just as the LORD had told Joshua. They carried the stones to their camp and put them there. 9 Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan. He put them where the priests stood while carrying the ark of the promise. The stones are still there today. 10 The priests stood in the middle of the Jordan. They stayed there until Joshua finished telling the people all that the LORD had told him to say. This was what Moses had told Joshua to say. Then the people hurried across. 11 After all the people had crossed, the ark of the LORD crossed too. The priests went in front of the people. 12 The men from Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh crossed first. They were ready for battle. About 40,000 men went in front of the other Israelites, just as Moses had told them. 13 They crossed in front of the LORD to the plains of Jericho. 14 That day the LORD made Joshua a great leader for all Israel. The people respected him all his life, just as they had respected Moses.

The Priests Leave the Jordan

15 Then the LORD said to Joshua, 16 "Tell the priests who carry the ark of the promise to come up from the Jordan."

17 Joshua told the priests, "Come up from the Jordan."

18 When the priests came up from the middle of the Jordan, the water returned. As soon as the priests' feet touched dry ground, the Jordan's water went back to its place and overflowed its banks, just like before.

Stones Set Up at Gilgal

19 The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month. They camped at Gilgal, on the east side of Jericho. 20 Joshua set up the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan at Gilgal. 21 He said to the Israelites, "In the future, your children will ask their fathers, 'What do these stones mean?' 22 Tell them, 'The people of Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground.' 23 For the LORD your God dried up the Jordan in front of you until you crossed over. He did the same thing at the Red Sea. He dried it up in front of us until we crossed over. 24 He did this so all the people on earth would know the LORD is very strong. He did this so you would respect the LORD your God forever."
