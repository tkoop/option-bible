The Lamb Opens the Scroll

1 Then I saw a scroll in the right hand of Him who sat on the throne. It had writing inside and out. It was sealed with seven seals. 2 I also saw a strong angel shouting loudly, "Who is good enough to open the scroll and break its seals?" 3 But no one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside it. 4 I cried and cried because no one was good enough to open the scroll or look inside it.

5 Then one of the elders said to me, "Do not cry. Look! The Lion from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has won. He can open the scroll and its seven seals."

6 Then I saw a Lamb that looked like it had been killed. It stood in the middle of the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders. It had seven horns and seven eyes. The eyes are the seven Spirits of God sent out to all the earth. 7 The Lamb came and took the scroll from the right hand of Him who sat on the throne. 8 When He took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each one had a harp and gold bowls full of sweet smoke. The smoke means the prayers of God's holy people.

Heaven Sings a New Song

9 They sang a new song. They said, "You are good enough to take the scroll and open its seals. You were killed, and with Your blood You bought people for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation. 10 You made them a kingdom and priests to serve our God. They will rule on the earth."

Praise for the Lamb

11 Then I looked and heard the voices of many angels around the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. Their number was millions and millions, and thousands and thousands. 12 They said in a loud voice, "The Lamb that was killed is good enough to receive power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise!"

13 Then I heard every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, and on the sea. Everything in them said, "Praise, honor, glory, and power belong forever and ever to Him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb!"

14 The four living creatures said, "Amen!" Then the elders fell down and worshiped.
