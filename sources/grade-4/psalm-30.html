David Thanks God

1 I will praise You, the LORD, because You lifted me up. You did not let my enemies laugh at me. 2 the LORD my God, I called to You, and You healed me. 3 the LORD, You brought me back from death. You kept me alive so I would not go down to the grave.

Sing to God

4 Sing to the LORD, all you who love Him. Give thanks to His holy name. 5 For His anger lasts only a short time, but His kindness lasts all our lives. We may cry at night, but joy comes in the morning.

I Thought I Was Safe

6 When things were going well, I said, "I will never fall." 7 the LORD, by Your kindness You made me strong like a mountain. Then You looked away, and I was scared.

David Asks for Help

8 I called to You, the LORD. I begged You for mercy. 9 What good would it do if I died? Would dust praise You? Would it tell Your truth? 10 Hear me, the LORD, and be kind to me. the LORD, please help me.

Sadness Turns to Joy

11 You turned my sadness into dancing. You took off my sad clothes and dressed me with joy. 12 Now I will sing and praise You. I will not be quiet. the LORD my God, I will thank You forever.
