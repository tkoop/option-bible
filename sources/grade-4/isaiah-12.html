A Song of Thanks to the LORD

1 On that day, you will say, “I will praise You, the LORD. You were angry with me, but You stopped being angry, and You make me feel better.” 2 See, God saves me. I will trust and not be afraid. The LORD God is my strength and my song. He saved me.

3 With joy, you will get water from the wells that save.

Tell the Nations About the LORD

4 On that day, you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD. Call on His name. Tell the nations what He has done. Say that His name is high.” 5 Sing to the LORD, because He has done great things. Let everyone on earth know it. 6 Shout and sing with joy, people of Zion, because the Holy One of Israel is great among you.
