A Vision About Judah and Jerusalem

1 This is the vision Isaiah son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem. It happened in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.

The LORD Speaks to His People

2 Hear, heavens! Listen, earth! The LORD has spoken: "I raised children and brought them up, but they turned against Me. 3 The ox knows its owner, and the donkey knows its master's food bowl, but Israel does not know. My people do not understand."

A Warning to a Sinful Nation

4 Oh, what a sinful nation! You are a people heavy with sin. You come from people who do wrong. You are children who ruin things. You left the LORD. You looked down on the Holy One of Israel. You turned your backs on Him.

5 Why do you want more beatings? Will you keep turning away? Your whole head is sick. Your whole heart is weak. 6 From the bottom of your foot to the top of your head, nothing is healthy. You have cuts, bruises, and fresh wounds. No one has cleaned them, bandaged them, or softened them with oil.

7 Your land is empty. Your cities are burned. Strangers eat your food right in front of you. Your land is ruined, like a land turned upside down by strangers. 8 The city of Zion is left like a little hut in a vineyard, like a small shelter in a field, like a city surrounded by enemies.

A Few People Are Left Safe

9 If the LORD of Armies had not left us a few survivors, we would be like Sodom. We would be like Gomorrah.

10 Hear the LORD's word, you rulers of Sodom! Listen to our God's teaching, you people of Gomorrah!

The LORD Does Not Want Empty Worship

11 The LORD says, "What are all your sacrifices to Me? I have had enough of your burnt rams and the fat of your fat cows. I do not want the blood of bulls, lambs, or goats. 12 When you come to stand before Me, who asked you to trample My courtyards?

13 "Stop bringing worthless gifts. Your incense makes Me sick. I cannot stand your new moon meetings, your Sabbath meetings, and your special meetings. They are full of sin. 14 I hate your new moons and your special days. They are a heavy load to Me. I am tired of carrying them. 15 When you raise your hands to pray, I will look away. Even if you pray many times, I will not listen. Your hands are full of blood.

16 "Wash yourselves and make yourselves clean. Take your bad deeds away from My eyes. Stop doing wrong. 17 Learn to do good. Look for what is fair. Help people who are hurt. Stand up for children with no parents. Speak for women whose husbands have died.

18 The LORD says, "Come now, let us talk this out. Even if your sins are bright red, they will become white like snow. Even if they are deep red, they will become like clean wool. 19 If you are willing and listen, you will eat the good food of the land. 20 But if you refuse and turn against Me, you will be killed by the sword." The LORD has spoken.

The City Has Changed

21 How did the faithful city become like a woman who sells herself? It was full of fair choices. Right living stayed there. But now it is full of murderers. 22 Your silver has become junk. Your wine is mixed with water. 23 Your leaders do not obey. They are friends of thieves. Everyone loves bribes and chases gifts. They do not stand up for children with no parents. They do not listen to a woman whose husband died.

The LORD Will Clean and Fix Zion

24 So the Lord, the LORD of Armies, the Mighty One of Israel, says, "Ah! I will get relief from My enemies. I will pay back those who hate Me. 25 I will turn My hand against you. I will clean away your junk metal. I will remove all your tin. 26 I will bring back your judges like before. I will bring back your helpers like at the start. After that, you will be called the City of Fairness, the City That Can Be Trusted."

27 Zion will be saved by fair choices. The people who come back to her will be saved by doing right. 28 But people who turn against God and people who sin will be broken together. People who leave the LORD will be destroyed.

A Warning About False Worship

29 You will be ashamed of the special oak trees you wanted. You will be embarrassed by the gardens you chose. 30 You will be like an oak tree whose leaves dry up, and like a garden with no water. 31 Strong people will be like dry grass, and their work will be like a spark. Both will burn together, and no one will put out the fire.
