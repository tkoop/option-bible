The LORD Rules the Earth

1 The LORD rules. Let the earth be happy. Let the faraway islands be glad. 2 Clouds and thick darkness are around Him. Right and fair hold up His throne. 3 Fire goes before Him. It burns His enemies all around. 4 His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees it and shakes. 5 The mountains melt like wax before the the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth. 6 The sky shows His right ways. All people see His glory. 7 People who worship statues are put to shame. Those who brag about fake gods are put to shame. All gods, worship Him.

God's People Are Glad

8 Zion hears and is glad. The people of Judah are glad because of Your fair rules, O the LORD. 9 For You, the the LORD, are highest over all the earth. You are lifted up far above all gods. 10 You who love the the LORD, hate what is bad. He keeps His people safe. He saves them from bad people. 11 Light is planted for good people. Joy is planted for those with honest hearts. 12 You who do right, be glad in the the LORD. Thank His holy name.
