Offerings in the New Land

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land I am giving you to live in, 3 and you give a food gift to the the LORD from your cows or sheep, as a smell the the LORD likes, it may be a burnt gift or a gift to keep a promise, a free gift, or a gift at a special time. 4 The person who gives it must give to the the LORD a grain gift: a tenth of a basket of good flour mixed with a fourth of a jar of oil. 5 With each lamb that is burned or given, you are to get ready a fourth of a jar of wine as a drink gift. 6 With a ram, give a grain gift of two-tenths of a basket of good flour mixed with a third of a jar of oil. 7 Give a third of a jar of wine as a drink gift, a smell the the LORD likes. 8 When you get ready a young bull as a burnt gift or gift to keep a promise, or a peace gift to the the LORD, 9 give with the bull a grain gift of three-tenths of a basket of good flour mixed with half a jar of oil. 10 Give half a jar of wine as a drink gift, a food gift with a smell the the LORD likes. 11 Do this for each bull, ram, lamb, or goat. 12 You may make as many as you give.

The Same Rules for Everyone

13 "Every person born in Israel must do these things this way when he gives a food gift with a smell the the LORD likes. 14 If a visitor living with you gives a food gift to the the LORD, he must do it the way you do. 15 The whole group must have the same rule for you and the visitor living among you. It is a rule that lasts forever. You and the visitor are the same before the the LORD. 16 There is one law and one rule for you and the visitor living with you."

First Bread Offering

17 Then the the LORD said to Moses, 18 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land I am bringing you to, 19 and you eat food from that land, you must give a gift to the the LORD. 20 From the first of your dough, give a cake as a gift. Give it like a gift from the grain you gather. 21 For all time, give the the LORD a gift from the first of your dough.

Forgiving Mistakes

22 "Now if you sin without meaning to and do not keep all these commands the the LORD has spoken to Moses, 23 all that the the LORD has commanded you through Moses from the day the the LORD gave them and on through the years to come, 24 and if it is done without the whole group knowing, then the whole group must get ready one young bull as a burnt gift, a smell the the LORD likes, with its grain gift and drink gift the right way, and one male goat as a sin gift. 25 The priest must make things right for the whole group of Israel, so they can be forgiven, because it was a mistake, and they brought their food gift to the the LORD and their sin gift before the the LORD for their mistake. 26 Then the whole group of Israel and the visitor living among them will be forgiven, because all the people acted without meaning to. 27 Also, if one person sins without meaning to, he must bring a one-year-old female goat as a sin gift. 28 The priest must make things right before the the LORD for the person who sinned without meaning to, to make things right for him, and he will be forgiven. 29 You must have the same law for the person born in Israel and for the visitor living among you.

Choosing to Sin

30 "But the person who sins on purpose, whether born in Israel or a visitor, speaks against the the LORD. That person must be cut off from his people. 31 Because he hated the the LORD's word and broke His command, that person must be cut off. His wrong will stay on him."

A Man Gathers Wood on the Sabbath

32 While the Israelites were in the desert, a man was found gathering wood on the Sabbath day. 33 The people who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses, Aaron, and all the people. 34 They put him in a safe place because it had not been said what should be done to him. 35 Then the the LORD said to Moses, "The man must surely be put to death. All the people must stone him outside the camp." 36 So all the people took him outside the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, just as the the LORD had told Moses.

Tassels to Help Remember

37 Then the the LORD said to Moses, 38 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: For all time to come, they are to make tassels on the corners of their clothes, with a blue cord on each tassel. 39 It will be a tassel for you to see and remember all the the LORD's commands, so you will not follow your own wishes and what you see, which can lead you away from God. 40 Then you will remember and obey all My commands and be holy to your God. 41 I am the the LORD your God who brought you out of Egypt to be your God. I am the the LORD your God."
