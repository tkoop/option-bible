Daniel Has a Vision

1 In the third year that King Belshazzar ruled, I, Daniel, saw another vision. It came after the first one I had seen. 2 In the vision, I saw myself in the strong city of Susa, in the land of Elam. I was standing by the Ulai River.

The Ram and the Goat

3 I looked up and saw a ram with two horns. It stood by the river. Both horns were high, but one was higher than the other. The higher one grew up last. 4 I saw the ram push to the west, north, and south. No animal could stand against it. No one could save them from its power. It did what it wanted and became very strong.

5 While I watched, a male goat came from the west. It moved so fast across the whole earth that its feet did not touch the ground. It had one big horn between its eyes. 6 It came to the ram with two horns that I had seen by the river. It charged at the ram in great anger. 7 I saw it reach the ram. It was furious. It hit the ram and broke its two horns. The ram was too weak to fight back. The goat threw the ram to the ground and trampled it. No one could save the ram. 8 The goat became very powerful. But when it was at its strongest, its big horn broke. Four horns grew in its place. They pointed toward the four winds of heaven.

The Little Horn

9 From one of those horns, a small horn grew. It grew very strong toward the south, the east, and the beautiful land. 10 It grew so much that it reached the army of heaven. It threw some of the army and some of the stars to the ground. Then it trampled them. 11 It even grew up to the Prince of the army of heaven. It stopped the daily offering. It threw down the holy place where God's people worshiped. 12 Because people sinned, the army and the daily offering were given to it. Truth was thrown to the ground. It did all this and succeeded.

How Long Will This Last?

13 Then I heard one holy being speak. Another holy being asked him, “How long will this vision last? How long will the daily offering be stopped? How long will the holy place be ruined? How long will the army be trampled?” 14 He said, “It will last for 2,300 evenings and mornings. Then the holy place will be made clean.”

Gabriel Explains the Vision

15 I, Daniel, saw the vision and wanted to understand it. Then I saw someone who looked like a man standing in front of me. 16 I heard a person’s voice from the Ulai River. It said, “Gabriel, explain the vision to this man.” 17 Gabriel came near to where I stood. I was afraid and fell on my face. He said to me, “Son of man, understand this. The vision is about the time of the end.” 18 While he was speaking, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground. Then he touched me and helped me stand up.

19 He said, “I will tell you what will happen near the end of God’s anger. The vision is for the time that has been set. 20 The ram with two horns stands for the kings of Media and Persia. 21 The male goat stands for the king of Greece. The big horn between its eyes is the first king. 22 The horn that broke stands for four kingdoms that will come from his nation. But they will not be as strong as he was.

23 When those kingdoms are almost finished, and the people have sinned very much, a fierce king will rise. He will be very clever. 24 His power will be great, but not from his own strength. He will destroy in shocking ways. He will succeed in what he does. He will destroy strong people and God’s holy people. 25 He will use his clever plans to make lies succeed. He will think he is very great. While people feel safe, he will destroy many. He will even stand against the Prince of princes. But he will be broken, and no human hand will do it.

26 The vision about the evenings and mornings is true. But keep the vision secret for now, because it is for many days from now.”

27 I, Daniel, was weak and sick for several days. Then I got up and did the king’s work. I was amazed by the vision, but no one could explain it to me.
