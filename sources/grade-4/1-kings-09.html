God Answers Solomon's Prayer

1 Solomon finished building the house of the the LORD, the king's house, and everything he wanted to do. 2 Then the the LORD appeared to Solomon again, like He did at Gibeon.

3 The The LORD said, "I heard your prayer. I made this house you built holy. My name will be here forever. My eyes and My heart will be here always.

4 If you live for Me like your father David did, with a true heart and doing right, and do all I tell you, and keep My rules, 5 then I will keep your family on Israel's throne forever. I promised your father David that his family would always rule Israel.

6 But if you or your sons stop following Me and do not keep My commands and rules I gave you, and if you serve and worship other gods, 7 then I will remove Israel from the land I gave them. I will also send away this house I made holy for My name. Israel will become a joke and a shame to all nations.

8 This house will become a pile of stones. Everyone who passes by will be shocked and hiss. They will ask why the the LORD did such a thing to this land and this house. 9 Others will answer that they left the the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of Egypt. They held to other gods, worshiped them, and served them. That is why the the LORD brought all this trouble on them."

Solomon's Building Work

10 Solomon took twenty years to build the two houses: the house of the the LORD and his own house. 11 King Solomon gave twenty towns in Galilee to Hiram, king of Tyre. Hiram had given him cedar wood, cypress wood, and gold for all his building plans.

12 Hiram left Tyre to see the towns Solomon gave him, but he did not like them. 13 He asked, "What are these towns you gave me, my brother?" He called them the land of Cabul. They still call them that today.

14 Hiram sent the king one hundred twenty talents of gold.

15 This is the list of forced workers King Solomon used to build the house of the the LORD, his own house, the terraces, Jerusalem's wall, and the towns of Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.

16 Pharaoh, king of Egypt, had attacked Gezer and burned it. He killed the Canaanites who lived there. Then he gave the city to his daughter, Solomon's wife, as a wedding gift.

17 Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Lower Beth-horon. 18 He also built Baalath and Tamar in the desert. 19 He built store cities, cities for his chariots and horses, and everything else he wanted to build in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and all his land.

20 Solomon forced the people still left in the land to work. They were Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, not Israelites. 21 They still work as forced labor today.

22 Solomon did not force Israelites to work as slaves. They were his soldiers, helpers, leaders, captains, chariot leaders, and horsemen. 23 Solomon had five hundred fifty bosses over his workers.

24 After Pharaoh's daughter moved from the City of David to the house Solomon built for her, Solomon built the terraces. 25 Three times each year Solomon gave burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar he built for the the LORD. He burned incense to the the LORD. In this way he finished the temple.

Solomon's Ships

26 King Solomon built ships at Ezion-geber, near Eloth in Edom. 27 Hiram sent sailors who knew the sea to work with Solomon's men. 28 They sailed to Ophir and brought back four hundred twenty talents of gold for King Solomon.
