Joseph Brings His Family to Pharaoh

1 Joseph went to Pharaoh and said, "My father and brothers came from Canaan with their sheep and cows and everything they own. They are now in Goshen."

2 He picked five of his brothers and brought them to Pharaoh.

3 Pharaoh asked his brothers, "What work do you do?"

They said, "Your servants take care of sheep. We and our fathers have always done this."

4 They also said to Pharaoh, "We came to live here because there is no grass for your servants' sheep in Canaan. The famine is very bad there. Please let your servants live in Goshen."

5 Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Your father and brothers have come to you. 6 The land of Egypt is yours to use. Settle your father and brothers in the best land. Let them live in Goshen. If you know any good men among them, put them in charge of my animals."

7 Then Joseph brought his father Jacob to Pharaoh. Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

8 Pharaoh asked Jacob, "How old are you?"

9 Jacob said to Pharaoh, "I have lived 130 years. My years have been few and hard. They are not as many as my fathers' years."

10 Jacob blessed Pharaoh and left.

11 So Joseph settled his father and brothers in Egypt. He gave them land in the best part of the country, in the area of Rameses, just as Pharaoh had told him.

12 Joseph gave food to his father, his brothers, and all his father's family. He gave enough for their little ones too.

The Famine Gets Worse

13 There was no food in the whole land. The famine was very bad. Egypt and Canaan were worn out by it.

14 Joseph gathered all the money in Egypt and Canaan that people paid for grain. He brought it to Pharaoh's house.

15 When the money in Egypt and Canaan was gone, all the Egyptians came to Joseph. They said, "Give us food. Why should we die in front of you? Our money is gone!"

16 Joseph said, "If your money is gone, give me your animals. I will sell you food for your animals."

17 So they brought their animals to Joseph. He gave them food for their horses, sheep, cows, and donkeys. All that year he fed them for their animals.

18 When that year ended, they came to him the next year. They said, "We will not hide from our master that our money is gone and our animals belong to our master. We have nothing left for our master except our bodies and our land. 19 Why should we die in front of you, we and our land? Buy us and our land for food. Then we and our land will be Pharaoh's slaves. Give us seed so we can live and not die, and the land will not be empty."

Joseph Buys the Land for Pharaoh

20 So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh. Every man sold his field because the famine was so bad. So the land became Pharaoh's.

21 Joseph moved the people into the cities, from one end of Egypt to the other.

22 But he did not buy the priests' land. The priests got food from Pharaoh, so they did not need to sell their land.

23 Then Joseph said to the people, "I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you to plant in the ground. 24 When you gather the crops, give one fifth to Pharaoh. Keep four fifths for yourselves to plant fields, feed yourselves and your families, and feed your children."

25 They said, "You saved our lives. Let us find favor with our master. We will be Pharaoh's slaves."

26 So Joseph made a rule that one fifth of the crops in Egypt belongs to Pharaoh. This rule is still in place today. Only the priests' land does not belong to Pharaoh.

Israel's Family Grows in Egypt

27 Israel lived in Egypt, in Goshen. They got land there and grew very much.

28 Jacob lived in Egypt seventeen years. He was 147 years old when he died.

Jacob Asks Joseph for a Promise

29 When it was almost time for Israel to die, he called his son Joseph. He said, "If I have found favor with you, put your hand under my leg and promise to be kind and true to me. Do not bury me in Egypt. 30 When I lie down with my fathers, carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their grave."

31 Joseph said, "I will do what you ask." Then Israel bowed at the head of his bed.
