The Old Law Could Not Fix Sin

1 The law is only a shadow of the good things coming. It is not the real thing. So it can never make people perfect by offering the same sacrifices every year. 2 If it could, they would have stopped. The people who worship would have had no more guilt from sin. 3 But those sacrifices remind people of their sins every year. 4 It is not possible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.

Christ Came to Do God's Will

5 So when He came into the world, He said, “You did not want sacrifices and offerings. But You made a body for Me. 6 You did not like burnt offerings and sin offerings. 7 Then I said, ‘Look, I have come. It is written about Me in the book. I come to do Your will, O God.’”

8 He said this first: “You did not want sacrifices and offerings, burnt offerings and sin offerings. You were not pleased with them, even though people offer them by the law.”

9 Then He said, “Look, I have come to do Your will.” He takes away the first way to set up the second way.

10 And by that will, we are made holy through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

Christ Offered One Sacrifice

11 Priests stand and serve every day. They offer the same sacrifices again and again. But those sacrifices can never take away sins. 12 But Christ offered one sacrifice for sins for all time. Then He sat down at the right hand of God. 13 Now He waits until His enemies are put under His feet. 14 By one offering He made perfect forever the people God is making holy.

God's New Promise

15 The Holy Spirit also tells us this. He said before, 16 “This is the promise I will make with them after those days, says the Lord. I will put My laws in their hearts. I will write them on their minds. 17 I will not remember their sins and wrongs anymore.”

18 Where there is forgiveness of these things, there is no more offering for sin.

We Can Come Near to God

19 So, brothers and sisters, we have courage to go into the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus. 20 He made a new and living way for us through the curtain. That curtain is His body. 21 We have a great priest over God's house.

22 So let us come near to God with a true heart and full trust. Our hearts are sprinkled clean from guilt. Our bodies are washed with clean water. 23 Let us hold tightly to the hope we say we have. We must not turn from it. The One who promised is faithful. 24 Let us think about how to help each other love and do good. 25 Do not stay away from meeting together, as some do. But cheer each other up. Do this even more as you see the Day coming.

A Warning About Sin

26 If we choose to keep sinning after we know the truth, no sacrifice for sins is left. 27 There is only a scary waiting for judgment and fire that will burn God's enemies. 28 Anyone who broke the law of Moses died without mercy when two or three people saw it. 29 How much worse do you think it will be for someone who walks on the Son of God, treats the blood of the promise like it is nothing, and insults the Spirit of grace? 30 We know the One who said, “I will punish. I will pay them back.” And again, “The Lord will judge His people.” 31 It is a scary thing to fall into the hands of the living God.

Remember Your Hard Times

32 Remember the early days. After you received the light, you stayed strong through many hard times. 33 Sometimes you were laughed at in front of others and had hard times. Sometimes you stood by people who were treated the same way. 34 You felt sorry for people in jail. You were happy even when people took your things. You knew you had something better in heaven that lasts. 35 So do not throw away your courage. It will bring a big reward. 36 You need to be patient. Then, after you do God's will, you will receive what He promised. 37 In a very short time, the One who is coming will come. He will not be late. 38 But the one who is right with God will live by faith. If anyone turns back, I will not be pleased with them. 39 But we are not people who turn back and are lost. We are people who believe and are saved.
