Paul Says Hello

1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy send this letter to the church in Thessalonica. You belong to God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. Grace to you and peace.

Paul Thanks God

2 We always thank God for all of you. We talk about you when we pray. 3 We remember, before God our Father, your work that shows your faith. We remember your hard work that shows your love. We remember how you do not give up, because you hope in our Lord Jesus Christ. 4 Dear friends, God loves you. We know He chose you.

The Good News Came with Power

5 Our good news came to you not just with words. It came with power, with the Holy Spirit, and with strong belief. You know how we lived among you to help you. 6 You followed our example and the Lord's example. You received the word with joy from the Holy Spirit, even when you had much trouble. 7 So you became an example to all believers in Macedonia and Achaia.

Your Faith Is Known Everywhere

8 The Lord's message spread from you in Macedonia and Achaia. Also, your faith in God has spread everywhere. So we do not need to say anything. 9 People tell us how you welcomed us. They tell how you turned to God from idols. Now you serve the living and true God. 10 They tell how you wait for His Son to come from heaven. God raised Him from the dead. His name is Jesus. He saves us from the punishment that is coming.
