Hiram Helps David Build a House

1 Hiram was king of Tyre. He sent messengers to David. He also sent cedar logs, stone workers, and wood workers to build a house for David.

2 David knew that the the LORD had made him king over Israel. The the LORD had made his kingdom great for His people Israel.

David's Children in Jerusalem

3 In Jerusalem, David took more wives. He had more sons and daughters.

4 These are the names of his children born in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon, 5 Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet, 6 Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, 7 Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.

David Fights the Philistines

8 The Philistines heard that David had been made king over all Israel. They all went up to find him. But David heard about it and went out to meet them.

9 The Philistines came and attacked the Valley of Rephaim.

10 So David asked God, "Should I go up against the Philistines? Will You give them to me?"

The the LORD said, "Go. I will give them to you."

11 So David went up to Baal-perazim. He beat them there. He said, "Like water breaking through, God has broken through my enemies for me." So that place was called Baal-perazim.

12 There the Philistines left their idols. David had them burned in the fire.

David Wins Again

13 The Philistines came again and attacked the valley.

14 David asked God again. God said, "Do not go straight after them. Go around them. Attack them in front of the balsam trees."

15 God said, "When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, go out to fight. Then God will have gone out before you to beat the Philistine camp."

16 David did what God told him. They beat the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gezer.

David Becomes Famous

17 David became famous in all the lands. The the LORD made all the nations afraid of him.
