A Prayer for Help

1 Please hear my honest prayer, the LORD. Listen to my cry. Please hear my prayer from lips that do not lie. 2 Let my good name come from You. Let Your eyes see what is fair. 3 You tested my heart. You came to me at night. You tried me and found no wrong. I promised not to sin with my mouth.

4 People do many things, but by Your words I stayed away from cruel people. 5 My steps stayed on Your paths. My feet did not slip.

Asking for Protection

6 I call on You, God, because You will answer me. Please listen to me and hear my words. 7 Show Your great love. You save those who trust in You. Keep them safe from those who fight them. 8 Keep me like the most precious part of Your eye. Hide me under the safe cover of Your wings.

Enemies All Around

9 The wicked hurt me. My enemies surround me. 10 They have hard hearts. Their mouths speak proud words. 11 They follow me and surround me now. Their eyes look down to throw me to the ground. 12 They are like a hungry lion. They are like a young lion hiding and waiting.

Asking God to Act

13 Rise up, the LORD. Face them and make them fall. Save me from the wicked with Your sword. 14 Use Your hand, the LORD, to save me from these people. They belong to this world, and this world is all they get. You fill their stomachs with good things. Their children have plenty, and they leave extra to their little ones.

Hope in God

15 But I will see Your face because I am right with You. When I wake up, I will be happy to be with You.
