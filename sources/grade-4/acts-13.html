Leaders in the Church at Antioch

1 In the church at Antioch, there were prophets and teachers. They were Barnabas, Simeon (called Niger), Lucius from Cyrene, Manaen (who grew up with Herod the ruler), and Saul.

The Holy Spirit Sends Barnabas and Saul

2 While they worshiped the Lord and went without food, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart Barnabas and Saul for Me. I have called them to do My work.” 3 Then they went without food and prayed. They put their hands on Barnabas and Saul and sent them out.

On the Island of Cyprus

4 So the Holy Spirit sent them out. They went down to Seleucia. Then they sailed to Cyprus. 5 When they came to Salamis, they told God's message in the Jewish meeting places. John helped them. 6 They went all over the island to Paphos. There they met a Jewish man who used magic. He was a false prophet named Bar-Jesus. 7 He was with Sergius Paulus, the Roman leader. Sergius was a wise man. He sent for Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear God's message. 8 But Elymas the magician (that is what his name means) tried to stop them. He wanted to turn the Roman leader away from believing. 9 But Saul, also called Paul, was full of the Holy Spirit. He looked hard at Elymas. 10 Paul said, “You son of the devil! You hate what is right. You are full of tricks and bad plans. Will you never stop twisting the Lord's right ways?” 11 “Now the Lord's hand is on you. For a while, you will be blind and not see the sun.” Right then, a dark mist came over Elymas. He walked around looking for someone to lead him by the hand. 12 The Roman leader believed when he saw what happened. He was amazed at the Lord's teaching.

Paul and Barnabas Go to Antioch in Pisidia

13 Paul and the men with him sailed from Paphos and came to Perga in Pamphylia. John left them and went back to Jerusalem. 14 They traveled from Perga and came to Antioch in Pisidia. On the Sabbath, they went into the meeting place and sat down.

Paul Is Asked to Speak

15 After the Law and the Prophets were read, the leaders of the meeting sent word to them. They said, “Brothers, if you have a helpful message for the people, please speak.”

Paul Speaks to the People

16 Paul stood up and motioned with his hand. He said, “Men of Israel and you who respect God, listen. 17 The God of Israel chose our fathers. He made His people strong while they lived in Egypt. With great power, He led them out. 18 He was patient with them for about forty years in the desert. 19 He destroyed seven nations in Canaan and gave their land to His people. 20 All this took about 450 years. After that, He gave them leaders until Samuel the prophet.

“21 Then they asked for a king. God gave them Saul, son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin. He ruled for forty years. 22 Then God removed Saul and made David their king. God said about David, ‘I have found David, son of Jesse. He is a man who wants what I want. He will do all I ask.’ 23 From David's family, God brought a Savior to Israel. It is Jesus, just as He promised. 24 Before Jesus came, John told all the people of Israel to be baptized and turn from sin. 25 As John finished his work, he said, ‘Do you think I am the one you are waiting for? I am not. But someone is coming after me. I am not even good enough to untie His shoes.’ 26 Brothers, children of Abraham and all of you who respect God, this message of salvation was sent to us.

“27 The people in Jerusalem and their leaders did not know Jesus. They did not understand the prophets' words that are read every Sabbath. But by killing Him, they made those words come true. 28 They found no reason to kill Him. But they asked Pilate to have Him put to death. 29 When they had done all that was written about Him, they took Him down from the cross and put Him in a tomb. 30 But God raised Him from the dead. 31 For many days, He appeared to the people who came with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem. They are now His witnesses to the people.

“32 We bring you good news. God made a promise to our fathers. 33 He has kept that promise for us, their children, by raising Jesus. It is written in Psalm 2: ‘You are My Son; today I have become Your Father.’ 34 God raised Jesus from the dead so He would never die again. God said, ‘I will give you the holy and sure blessings promised to David.’ 35 So God says in another psalm, ‘You will not let Your Holy One rot in the grave.’ 36 David served God in his time. Then he died and was buried with his fathers. His body decayed. 37 But Jesus, whom God raised, did not decay.

“38 So I want you to know, brothers, that through Jesus, forgiveness of sins is announced to you. 39 Everyone who believes in Him is made right with God. The law of Moses could not make you right. 40 Be careful, then, so the prophets' words do not happen to you. 41 ‘Look, you who make fun of God! Be amazed and be destroyed. I am doing something in your time that you will not believe, even if someone tells it to you.’”

Some Believe, but Others Reject the Message

42 As Paul and Barnabas left, the people asked them to say these things again the next Sabbath. 43 When the meeting ended, many Jews and people who worshiped God followed Paul and Barnabas. Paul and Barnabas talked with them and urged them to keep trusting God's grace. 44 The next Sabbath, almost the whole city came to hear the Lord's message. 45 But when the Jews saw the crowds, they became jealous. They argued against Paul and said bad things about him.

46 Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly: “God's message had to be told to you first. But you push it away. You show you are not worthy of eternal life. So we are going to the people who are not Jews. 47 The Lord commanded us, ‘I have made you a light for the nations, so you can bring salvation to the ends of the earth.’”

48 When the non-Jewish people heard this, they were glad. They praised the Lord's message. All who were chosen for eternal life believed. 49 The Lord's message spread through the whole area. 50 But the Jews stirred up the respected women and the city leaders. They started trouble against Paul and Barnabas and made them leave the area. 51 Paul and Barnabas shook the dust off their feet to show they were done with that place. Then they went to Iconium. 52 The believers were full of joy and the Holy Spirit.
