The LORD Heard My Cry

1 I love the LORD because He hears my voice. He listens when I beg for help. 2 Because He listened to me, I will pray to Him all my life. 3 Death wrapped around me like ropes. The grave's pain caught me. I felt trouble and sadness.

4 So I called on the LORD's name. I said, “The LORD, save my life!” 5 The the LORD is kind and good. Our God is full of mercy. 6 The the LORD protects simple people. I was weak, but He saved me. 7 My soul, go back to your rest, for the the LORD has been good to you.

8 You saved my life from death. You kept my eyes from crying. You kept my feet from falling. 9 I will walk with the the LORD while I am alive. 10 I believed even when I said, “I am in great pain.” 11 When I was afraid, I said, “Everyone lies.”

I Will Thank the LORD

12 What can I give the the LORD for all the good things He has done for me? 13 I will thank the the LORD for saving me. I will call on His name. 14 I will keep my promises to the the LORD where all His people can see. 15 The the LORD cares deeply when His faithful people die.

16 The LORD, I am truly Your servant. I am Your servant, the son of Your servant. You set me free from my chains. 17 I will give You a thank offering. I will call on the the LORD's name. 18 I will keep my promises to the the LORD where all His people can see. 19 I will do this in the courtyards of the the LORD's house, right in your midst, O Jerusalem. Praise the the LORD!
