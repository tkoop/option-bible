The LORD Has a Case Against Israel

1 Children of Israel, hear the word of the the LORD, for the the LORD has a case against the people who live in the land. He says, "There is no truth, no kindness, and no knowing God in the land. 2 People curse, lie, kill, steal, and are not faithful in marriage. One killing comes right after another."

The Land Suffers

3 So the land is sad. Everyone who lives there grows weak. The wild animals, the birds, and even the fish are dying.

The LORD Speaks to Israel

4 "Do not let anyone argue. Do not let anyone blame. Your people are like people who fight with the priest. 5 You will trip and fall today. The prophet will trip and fall with you at night. I will destroy your mother.

6 "My people are destroyed because they do not know Me. Because you did not want to know Me, I will not let you be My priest. You forgot the law of your God, so I will forget your children. 7 The more they grew, the more they sinned against Me. I will turn their honor into shame.

8 "They feed on the sin of My people. They set their hearts on doing wrong. 9 The people and the priest will be the same. I will punish them for what they do. I will pay them back for their deeds.

10 "They will eat but not be full. They will be unfaithful but not have more children, because they left the the LORD to be unfaithful. 11 Wine and new wine take away good sense.

12 "My people ask a piece of wood for answers. Their stick tells them what to do. A spirit of unfaithfulness has led them the wrong way. They are unfaithful to their God.

13 "They sacrifice on the tops of mountains. They burn incense on hills. They do this under oak, poplar, and other trees, because the shade is good. So your daughters will be unfaithful, and your brides will cheat on their husbands. 14 I will not punish your daughters when they are unfaithful. I will not punish your brides when they cheat. This is because the men go off with prostitutes and offer sacrifices with temple prostitutes. People who do not understand will be ruined.

15 "Israel, even if you are unfaithful, let Judah not be guilty. Do not go to Gilgal. Do not go up to Beth Aven. Do not swear by the life of the the LORD. 16 Israel is stubborn, like a stubborn young cow. Now the the LORD will feed them like a lamb in a wide field. 17 Ephraim is joined to idols. Leave him alone.

18 "Their wine is gone. They keep being unfaithful. Their leaders love shame. 19 A wind has wrapped her in its wings. They will be ashamed because of their sacrifices."
