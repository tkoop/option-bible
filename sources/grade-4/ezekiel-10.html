The Throne Above the Cherubim

1 I looked up. I saw above the sky over the cherubim's heads something like a throne. It looked like a blue stone.

2 The LORD said to the man in white clothes, "Go in among the wheels, between the cherubim. Fill your hands with hot coals from among the cherubim. Throw them over the city." He went in while I watched.

3 The cherubim stood on the south side of the temple. When the man went in, a cloud filled the inner yard. 4 Then the glory of the LORD rose from above the cherub to the door of the temple. The temple filled with the cloud. The yard filled with the bright light of the glory of the LORD. 5 The sound of the cherubim's wings could be heard as far as the outer yard. It sounded like the voice of God Almighty when He speaks.

The Man Takes Fire

6 When the LORD told the man in white clothes, "Take fire from between the wheels, from between the cherubim," the man went in and stood by a wheel. 7 Then one cherub reached out his hand to the fire among them. He took some and put it into the hands of the man in white clothes. The man took it and went out. 8 The cherubim looked like they had a person's hand under their wings.

The Wheels Beside the Cherubim

9 Then I looked and saw four wheels by the cherubim. One wheel was by each cherub. The wheels shone like a bright stone. 10 They all looked the same. Each one looked like a wheel inside a wheel. 11 When they moved, they could go in any of four directions. They did not turn as they went. They followed where the head faced. 12 Their whole bodies, backs, hands, and wings were full of eyes all around. Their four wheels were full of eyes too. 13 I heard the wheels being called "the spinning wheels." 14 Each one had four faces. The first face was a cherub's face. The second was a person's face. The third was a lion's face. The fourth was an eagle's face.

The Cherubim Rise and Leave

15 Then the cherubim rose up. These were the living creatures I had seen by the Kebar River. 16 When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved with them. When the cherubim lifted their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not leave their side. 17 When they stood still, the wheels stood still. When they rose, the wheels rose with them. The spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

18 Then the glory of the LORD left the door of the temple and stood above the cherubim. 19 The cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the ground. The wheels were beside them. They stopped at the east gate of the LORD's house. The glory of the God of Israel was above them. 20 These were the living creatures I had seen under the God of Israel by the Kebar River. I knew they were cherubim. 21 Each had four faces and four wings. They had what looked like a person's hands under their wings. 22 Their faces looked like the faces I had seen by the Kebar River. Each creature moved straight ahead.
