Tax Collectors and Sinners Come to Jesus

1 Now all the tax collectors and sinners came close to listen to Jesus.

2 But the Pharisees and teachers of the law grumbled. They said, "This man welcomes sinners and eats with them."

The Lost Sheep

3 So Jesus told them this story:

4 "Suppose one of you has one hundred sheep and loses one. Won't he leave the ninety-nine sheep in the field and go look for the lost one until he finds it?

5 "When he finds it, he puts it on his shoulders and is happy.

6 "Then he goes home and calls his friends and neighbors together. He says, 'Be happy with me! I found my lost sheep.'

7 "I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who turns from sin than over ninety-nine good people who do not need to turn.

The Lost Coin

8 "Or suppose a woman has ten silver coins and loses one. Won't she light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it?

9 "When she finds it, she calls her friends and neighbors together and says, 'Be happy with me! I found the coin I lost.'

10 "In the same way, I tell you, there is joy before God's angels over one sinner who turns from sin.

The Lost Son

11 Jesus also said, "A man had two sons. 12 The younger son said to his father, 'Father, give me my share of what you own.' So the father divided his things between them.

13 "After a few days, the younger son packed up all he had and went to a far country. There he wasted his money on wild living. 14 When he had spent everything, a terrible food shortage hit that country, and he began to go hungry.

15 "So he went and worked for a man from that country. The man sent him to the fields to feed pigs. 16 He wanted to eat the bean pods the pigs ate, but no one gave him any.

17 "Then he thought clearly. He said, 'How many of my father's workers have plenty of food, but here I am starving to death! 18 I will go back to my father and say to him, "Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. 19 I am not worthy to be called your son. Make me like one of your workers."'

20 "So he got up and went to his father. While he was still far away, his father saw him and felt sorry for him. He ran to him, hugged him, and kissed him. 21 The son said to him, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. I am not worthy to be called your son.'

22 "But the father said to his servants, 'Quick! Bring the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet. 23 Bring the fat calf and kill it. Let us eat and celebrate. 24 For this son of mine was dead and is alive again. He was lost and is found.' So they began to celebrate.

25 "Now the older son was in the field. As he came near the house, he heard music and dancing. 26 He called one of the servants and asked what was going on.

27 "The servant said, 'Your brother has come home. Your father killed the fat calf because your brother is back and well.'

28 "The older son got angry and would not go in. So his father went out and begged him to come in.

29 "But he answered his father, 'Look! All these years I have worked for you. I never disobeyed you. But you never gave me even a young goat so I could have a party with my friends. 30 But when this son of yours came back, who wasted your money on bad women, you killed the fat calf for him!'

31 "The father said, 'My son, you are always with me, and everything I have is yours. 32 But we had to celebrate and be glad, because this brother of yours was dead and is alive again. He was lost and is found.'"
