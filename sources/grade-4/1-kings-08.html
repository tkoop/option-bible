Solomon Brings the Ark to the Temple

1 Then Solomon gathered the elders of Israel and all the leaders of the tribes. These were the family leaders of the Israelites. They came to King Solomon in Jerusalem. They were to bring the ark of the LORD's covenant from the City of David, which is Zion. 2 All the men of Israel came to King Solomon for the feast. It was the seventh month, the month called Ethanim. 3 All the elders of Israel came, and the priests carried the ark. 4 They brought up the ark of the LORD, the tent of meeting, and all the holy things in the tent. The priests and Levites carried them. 5 King Solomon and all the people of Israel were there in front of the ark. They sacrificed so many sheep and oxen that no one could count them. 6 The priests brought the ark of the LORD's covenant to its place. They put it in the inner room of the temple, the Most Holy Place. They set it under the wings of the cherubim. 7 The cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark. They covered the ark and its poles. 8 The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the holy place in front of the inner room. But they could not be seen from outside. They are still there today. 9 There was nothing in the ark except the two stone tablets. Moses had put them there at Horeb. That is where the LORD made a covenant with the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

The Cloud Fills the Temple

10 When the priests came out of the Holy Place, a cloud filled the LORD's temple. 11 The priests could not stand and serve because of the cloud. The glory of the LORD filled the temple.

Solomon Speaks to the People

12 Then Solomon said, “13 The LORD said He would live in thick darkness. I have built a house for You. It is a place where You can live forever.” 14 Then the king turned around and blessed all the people of Israel while they stood. He said, “15 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel. He spoke with His mouth to my father David. He did what He promised with His hand. 16 He said, ‘Since the day I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from any tribe of Israel to build a house for My name. But I chose David to rule over My people Israel.’ 17 My father David wanted to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 18 But the LORD said to my father David, ‘You wanted to build a house for My name. You did well to want that. 19 But you will not build the house. Your son, who comes from your own body, will build the house for My name.’ 20 Now the LORD has done what He said. I have taken my father David's place. I sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised. I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 21 I have put the ark there. It holds the LORD's covenant. He made it with our fathers when He brought them out of Egypt.”

Solomon Prays

22 Then Solomon stood before the altar of the LORD in front of all the people. He spread his hands toward heaven. 23 He prayed, “O the LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven above or on earth below. You keep Your covenant and show love to Your servants. They walk before You with all their heart. 24 You have kept what You promised to Your servant my father David. You spoke with Your mouth, and You did it with Your hand. It is as it is today. 25 Now, O the LORD, God of Israel, keep the promise You made to Your servant my father David. You said, ‘You will never fail to have a man sit before Me on the throne of Israel. But your sons must guard their way and walk before Me, as you have walked before Me.’ 26 And now, O God of Israel, let Your words come true. You spoke them to Your servant my father David. 27 But will God really live on earth? Look, the heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot hold You. How much less this house I have built!

“28 Yet please listen to the prayer of Your servant and to his plea, O the LORD my God. Hear the cry and the prayer that Your servant prays before You today. 29 May Your eyes be open toward this house night and day. This is the place You said would have Your name. Hear the prayer Your servant prays toward this place. 30 Hear the plea of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Hear from heaven, where You live. Hear and forgive. 31 When a man sins against his neighbor and must take an oath, and he comes to swear before Your altar in this house, 32 then hear from heaven and act. Judge Your servants. Show the wicked person he is wrong by bringing his guilt on his own head. Show the good person he is right by giving him what is right.

“33 When Your people Israel lose to an enemy because they sinned against You, and they come back to You and praise Your name and pray and plead with You in this house, 34 then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your people Israel. Bring them back to the land You gave their fathers. 35 When the sky is shut and there is no rain because they sinned against You, and they pray toward this place and praise Your name and turn from their sin because You punished them, 36 then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your servants, Your people Israel. Teach them the good way to live. Send rain on Your land that You gave Your people to own.

“37 When there is hunger or sickness in the land, or plant disease, or locusts, or enemies attacking their cities, or any trouble or sickness, 38 then hear every prayer and plea that any man or all Your people Israel may bring. Each one knows his own pain. He spreads out his hands toward this house. 39 Then hear from heaven, where You live, and forgive and act. Give each man what he deserves, for You know his heart. You alone know the hearts of all people. 40 Do this so they will fear You all the days they live in the land You gave our fathers.

“41 Also, for the foreigner who is not one of Your people Israel but comes from far away because of Your name— 42 they will hear of Your great name and strong hand and outstretched arm. When he comes to pray toward this house, 43 then hear from heaven, where You live, and do what the foreigner asks. Do this so all the peoples of the earth may know Your name and fear You, just as Your people Israel do. Then they will know that this house I built is called by Your name.

“44 When Your people go out to fight their enemies, wherever You send them, and they pray to the LORD toward the city You chose and the house I built for Your name, 45 then hear from heaven their prayer and their plea, and help them. 46 When they sin against You—for no one does not sin—and You are angry with them and let an enemy take them captive to a far or near land,

“47 and when they think about it in the land where they are held, and they turn back and plead with You, saying, ‘We have sinned. We have done wrong. We have been wicked,’ 48 and when they return to You with all their heart and soul in the land of their enemies who took them captive, and they pray to You toward the land You gave their fathers, the city You chose, and the house I built for Your name, 49 then hear from heaven, where You live, their prayer and plea, and help them. 50 Forgive Your people who sinned against You. Forgive all the wrongs they did against You. Make their captors show them mercy.

“51 For they are Your people and Your own special people. You brought them out of Egypt, out of the iron furnace. 52 May Your eyes be open to the plea of Your servant and of Your people Israel. Listen to them whenever they call on You. 53 For You set them apart as Your own people from all the peoples of the earth. You spoke this through Your servant Moses when You brought our fathers out of Egypt, O Lord, the LORD.”

54 When Solomon finished praying, he got up from before the altar of the LORD. He had been kneeling with his hands spread toward heaven.

Solomon Blesses the People

55 He stood and blessed all the people of Israel in a loud voice. He said, “56 Blessed be the LORD. He has given rest to His people Israel, just as He promised. Not one of all His good promises has failed. He spoke them through His servant Moses. 57 May the LORD our God be with us, as He was with our fathers. May He not leave us or give up on us. 58 May He turn our hearts to Him. Then we can walk in all His ways and keep His commands, rules, and laws that He gave our fathers. 59 May these words of mine, which I have prayed before the LORD, be near to the LORD our God day and night. May He help His servant and His people Israel, as each day needs. 60 Then all the peoples of the earth will know that the LORD is God. There is no other. 61 And may your hearts be fully given to the LORD our God. Walk in His rules and keep His commands, as you do today.”

Sacrifices and the Feast

62 Then the king and all Israel offered sacrifices to the LORD. 63 Solomon offered peace offerings to the LORD. He offered 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep. The king and all the people dedicated the temple of the LORD. 64 That same day the king made the middle of the courtyard holy. It was in front of the temple. He did this because the bronze altar before the LORD was too small. It could not hold all the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and fat of the peace offerings. 65 Solomon and all Israel with him made a large crowd. They came from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt. They celebrated the feast before the LORD our God for seven days. Then they celebrated seven more days. That was fourteen days in all. 66 On the eighth day he sent the people home. They blessed the king. They went to their tents. They were happy and glad in heart. They were glad because of all the good things the LORD had done for His servant David and for His people Israel.
