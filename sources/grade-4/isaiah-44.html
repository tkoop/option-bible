The LORD Helps His People

1 The LORD says, “Now listen, Jacob, My servant. Israel, I chose you. 2 I made you and formed you in your mother’s womb. I will help you. Do not be afraid, Jacob, My servant. Jeshurun, I chose you. 3 I will pour water on thirsty land. I will pour streams on dry ground. I will pour My Spirit on your children. I will send My blessing on your family. 4 They will grow like grass. They will grow like willow trees by streams. 5 One will say, ‘I belong to the the LORD.’ Another will call himself Jacob. Another will write on his hand, ‘I belong to the the LORD,’ and take the name Israel.”

Only the LORD Is God

6 The LORD, the King and Savior of Israel, the the LORD who rules everything, says, “I am the first. I am the last. There is no God but Me. 7 Who is like Me? Let him speak and show it. Let him tell Me, since I chose the people long ago. Let them tell what will happen next. 8 Do not be afraid. Do not be scared. Have I not told you since long ago? I told you. You are My witnesses. Is there any God but Me? There is no Rock. I know of none.”

Idols Are Worthless

9 All who make a carved idol make something empty. Their favorite things do not help. Their witnesses do not see or know. So they will be ashamed. 10 Who made a god? Who poured a metal idol that helps no one? 11 Look, all who join them will be ashamed. The workers are only people. They all gather and stand together. They are afraid. They are ashamed together.

12 The blacksmith works with tongs in the coals. He shapes it with hammers. He works hard with his strong arm. He gets hungry and weak. He drinks no water. He gets tired. 13 The woodworker stretches a line. He marks it with a pencil. He shapes it with planes. He marks it with a compass. He makes it look like a man. He makes it look nice, to put in a house. 14 He cuts down cedar trees. He takes cypress and oak. He picks strong trees from the forest. He plants a pine tree. The rain makes it grow.

15 The wood is used to burn. A man takes some and warms himself. He lights a fire and bakes bread. He also makes a god and worships it. He makes a carved idol and bows to it. 16 He burns half of it in the fire. He cooks meat on the other half. He eats and is full. He warms himself and says, ‘Ah, I am warm. I see the fire.’ 17 He makes the rest into a god, his carved idol. He bows to it and worships it. He prays to it and says, ‘Save me, for you are my god.’

18 They do not know or understand. Their eyes are covered so they cannot see. Their hearts are covered so they cannot understand. 19 No one thinks about it. No one has knowledge or sense to say, ‘I burned half of it in the fire. I baked bread on its coals. I roasted meat and ate it. Should I make the rest into something hated? Should I bow to a piece of wood?’ 20 He feeds on ashes. A tricked heart leads him the wrong way. He cannot save himself. He cannot say, ‘There is a lie in my right hand.’

The LORD Forgives Israel

21 “Remember these things, Jacob. You are My servant, Israel. I made you. You are My servant. Israel, I will not forget you. 22 I wiped out your wrongs like a cloud. I wiped out your sins like fog. Come back to Me, for I saved you.”

23 Sing, heavens, because the the LORD has done it. Shout, deep parts of the earth. Break into song, mountains, forests, and every tree. The The LORD saved Jacob and shows His glory in Israel.

The LORD Will Rebuild Jerusalem

24 The LORD, your Savior, who formed you in your mother’s womb, says, “I am the the LORD. I made everything. I alone stretched out the heavens. Who spread out the earth with Me? 25 I stop the signs of false prophets. I make fortune-tellers look foolish. I turn wise men back. I make their knowledge look silly.

“I keep the word of My servant. I do what My messengers say. I say to Jerusalem, ‘You will be lived in.’ I say to the cities of Judah, ‘You will be rebuilt.’ I will raise up her broken places. 27 I say to the deep sea, ‘Be dry.’ I will dry up your rivers. 28 I say to Cyrus, ‘You are My shepherd.’ He will do all I want. I say to Jerusalem, ‘You will be rebuilt.’ I say to the temple, ‘You will be started again.’”
