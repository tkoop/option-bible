Nehemiah Hears Sad News About Jerusalem

1 These are the words of Nehemiah, son of Hachaliah. In the month of Kislev, in the twentieth year, I was in the city of Susa. 2 My brother Hanani came with some men from Judah. I asked them about the Jews who were left from the people taken away, and about Jerusalem. 3 They said to me, "The people left in that land are in big trouble and shame. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates are burned with fire." 4 When I heard these words, I sat down and cried. I was sad for days. I did not eat, and I prayed to the God of heaven.

Nehemiah Prays to God

5 I said, "Please, the LORD God of heaven, great and mighty God, who keeps His promise and is kind to those who love Him and keep His commands. 6 Please let Your eyes be open and Your ears listen to the prayer of Your servant today. I pray day and night for Your servants, the people of Israel. I say sorry for the sins of the people of Israel. We sinned against You. I and my family have sinned. 7 We did very wrong against You. We did not keep Your commands, Your rules, and Your laws that You gave to Your servant Moses.

8 Please remember the word You told Your servant Moses: 'If you are not faithful, I will spread you among the nations.' 9 But if you come back to Me and keep My commands and do them, even if you are pushed far away, I will bring you together from there. I will bring you to the place I chose for My name. 10 They are Your servants and Your people. You saved them with great power and a strong hand. 11 Please, the LORD, let Your ear listen to the prayer of Your servant and of Your servants who want to honor Your name. Help Your servant today, and let this man show him kindness. I served drinks to the king."
