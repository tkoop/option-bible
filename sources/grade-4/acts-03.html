Peter and John Heal a Man Who Could Not Walk

1 One day Peter and John went to the temple to pray at three in the afternoon. 2 A man who could not walk since he was born was carried to the temple gate called Beautiful. People put him there every day to beg from those going into the temple.

3 When he saw Peter and John about to go in, he asked them for money. 4 Peter and John looked at him and said, "Look at us!" 5 So the man looked at them. He thought he would get something from them.

6 But Peter said, "I don't have silver or gold. But what I have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ from Nazareth, get up and walk!"

7 Peter took him by the right hand and helped him up. Right away his feet and ankles became strong. 8 He jumped up and stood. Then he began to walk. He went into the temple with them, walking and jumping and praising God.

9 All the people saw him walking and praising God. 10 They knew he was the man who used to sit and beg at the Beautiful Gate. They were full of wonder and surprise at what had happened to him.

Peter Talks to the People

11 The man who was healed held on to Peter and John. All the people were amazed. They ran to them in the part of the temple called Solomon's Porch.

"12 Men of Israel, why are you amazed at this? Why do you stare at us? It was not by our own power or goodness that this man walks. 13 The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has honored His Servant Jesus. You handed Him over and said no to Him in front of Pilate, even though Pilate wanted to let Him go.

"14 You said no to the Holy and Good One. You asked for a killer to be set free for you. 15 You killed the One who gives life. But God raised Him from the dead. We saw it happen.

"16 This man you see and know was made strong by faith in His name. It is the name of Jesus and faith that comes through Him that gave him this full healing in front of your eyes.

"17 Now, brothers, I know you did this without knowing. Your leaders did too. 18 But this is how God kept His word. He said long ago through all the prophets that His Christ would suffer.

"19 So change your hearts and turn to God. Then your sins will be wiped away. 20 Times of rest will come from the Lord. He will send Jesus, the Christ He chose for you. 21 Jesus must stay in heaven until the time comes for God to make all things new. God promised this long ago through His holy prophets.

"22 Moses said that the Lord your God will raise up for you a Prophet like Moses from among your own people. You must listen to Him in everything He tells you. 23 Anyone who does not listen to this Prophet will be cut off from his people.

"24 All the prophets from Samuel on spoke about these days. 25 You are children of the prophets. You share in the promise God made with your fathers. God said to Abraham that through his family all the families of the earth will be blessed.

"26 God raised up His Servant and sent Him first to you. He sent Him to bless you by turning each of you from your evil ways."
