A Song to Thank God

1 Shout with joy to the the LORD, all the earth. 2 Serve the the LORD with joy. Come before Him with happy songs.

3 Know that the the LORD is God. He made us. We did not make ourselves. We are His people. We are sheep in His care.

4 Come into His gates with thanks. Come into His yards with praise. Thank Him. Bless His name. 5 For the the LORD is good. His love never ends. He keeps His promises forever.
