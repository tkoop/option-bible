The LORD Rules the World

1 The LORD rules. Let the nations shake. He sits above the angels. Let the earth shake. 2 The LORD is great in Zion. He is high above all nations.

Praise God's Holy Name

3 Let them praise Your great and awesome name. He is holy. 4 The King is strong. He loves justice. You made things fair. You did what is right and fair for Israel. 5 Lift up the LORD our God. Bow down at His footstool. He is holy.

God Answers His People

6 Moses and Aaron were His priests. Samuel was one of those who called on His name. They called to the LORD, and He answered them. 7 He spoke to them in a cloud pillar. They kept His rules and the law He gave them. 8 The LORD our God, You answered them. You forgave them, but You punished their wrongs. 9 Lift up the LORD our God and bow down at His holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.
