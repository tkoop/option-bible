The LORD Is My Shepherd

A song by David. 1 The LORD is my shepherd. I will not need anything. 2 He makes me lie down in green fields. He leads me by quiet water. 3 He gives me new strength. He leads me on the right path to show His goodness.

4 Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, because You are with me. Your rod and Your staff comfort me.

5 You set a table for me where my enemies can see. You pour oil on my head. My cup is full and running over. 6 Goodness and love will follow me all my life. I will live in the LORD's house forever.
