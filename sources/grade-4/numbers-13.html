The LORD Sends Spies

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2 "Send some men to look at the land of Canaan. I am giving this land to the people of Israel. Send one leader from each tribe."

3 So Moses sent them from the desert of Paran, just as the LORD said. The men were leaders of the Israelites.

The Men's Names

4 From Reuben: Shammua son of Zaccur. 5 From Simeon: Shaphat son of Hori. 6 From Judah: Caleb son of Jephunneh. 7 From Issachar: Igal son of Joseph. 8 From Ephraim: Hoshea son of Nun. 9 From Benjamin: Palti son of Raphu. 10 From Zebulun: Gaddiel son of Sodi. 11 From Manasseh: Gaddi son of Susi. 12 From Dan: Ammiel son of Gemalli. 13 From Asher: Sethur son of Michael. 14 From Naphtali: Nahbi son of Vophsi. 15 From Gad: Geuel son of Machi.

16 These were the men Moses sent to look at the land. Moses gave Hoshea son of Nun a new name. He called him Joshua.

Moses Sends the Spies

17 Moses sent them to look at the land of Canaan. He said, "Go up through the Negev. Then go up into the hills. 18 See what the land is like. See if the people there are strong or weak. See if there are many people or few. 19 See if the land is good or bad. See if the towns have walls or not. 20 See if the land has good soil. See if there are trees. Be brave. Bring back some of the fruit of the land." It was the time when the first grapes were ready.

The Spies Look at the Land

21 So the men went up and looked at the land. They went from the desert of Zin to Rehob near Hamath. 22 They went south and came to Hebron. Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the people of Anak, lived there. Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt. 23 They came to the Valley of Eshcol. There they cut a branch with one big bunch of grapes. Two men carried it on a pole. They also took some pomegranates and figs. 24 That place was called the Valley of Eshcol because of the grape bunch the Israelites cut there. 25 They came back after forty days.

The Spies Return

26 They went to Moses and Aaron and all the Israelites at Kadesh in the desert of Paran. They told Moses and the people what they had seen. They showed them the fruit of the land.

The Spies' Report

27 They said, "We went to the land you sent us to. It is a very rich land. Here is some of its fruit. 28 But the people there are strong. The cities are big and have walls. We also saw Anak's people there. 29 The Amalekites live in the Negev. The Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the hills. The Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan."

30 Caleb quieted the people before Moses. He said, "Let's go now and take the land. We can do it."

31 But the men who went with him said, "We cannot go up against those people. They are stronger than we are."

32 They spread a bad report about the land to the Israelites. They said, "The land we looked at is dangerous for the people who live there. All the people we saw there are very tall. 33 We saw giants there. Anak's people are giants too. We felt like tiny bugs next to them."
