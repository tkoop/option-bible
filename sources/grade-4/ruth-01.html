A Hard Time in the Land

1 Long ago, when Israel had judges, there was no food in the land. A man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live in the fields of Moab. He went with his wife and his two sons. 2 The man's name was Elimelech. His wife was Naomi. His sons were Mahlon and Chilion. They were from Bethlehem in Judah. They came to Moab and lived there. 3 Then Elimelech died. Naomi was left with her two sons. 4 The sons married women from Moab. One was named Orpah, and the other was named Ruth. They lived there for about ten years. 5 Then Mahlon and Chilion died too. Naomi was left without her husband and without her two sons.

Naomi and Her Sons' Wives Start Home

6 In Moab, Naomi heard that the LORD had helped His people. He gave them food. So Naomi and her sons' wives got up to go back from Moab. 7 Naomi and her two sons' wives left the place where they had been. They started down the road to Judah. 8 Naomi said to her two sons' wives, "Go back, each of you, to your mother's house. May the LORD be kind to you, as you were kind to the men who died and to me. 9 May the LORD give you rest in the home of a new husband." Then she kissed them. They cried.

10 They said, "No, we will go with you to your people."

Naomi Tells Them to Stay

11 Naomi said, "Go back, my daughters. Why come with me? Do I have more sons who could be your husbands? 12 Go back, my daughters. I am too old to have a husband. Even if I had hope, and even if I had a husband tonight and had sons, 13 would you wait for them to grow up? Would you stay single for them? No, my daughters. It is much harder for me than for you. The LORD has turned against me."

14 They cried again. Orpah kissed Naomi and left. But Ruth held on to Naomi. 15 Naomi said, "Look, your sister-in-law is going back to her people and her gods. Go back with her."

Ruth Chooses to Stay

16 But Ruth said, "Do not make me leave you. Do not make me turn back. Where you go, I will go. Where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people. Your God will be my God. 17 Where you die, I will die. There I will be buried. May the LORD punish me if anything but death keeps us apart."

Naomi and Ruth Reach Bethlehem

18 Naomi saw that Ruth would not change her mind. So she stopped talking to her. 19 The two women walked until they came to Bethlehem. When they got there, the whole town was stirred up. The women said, "Is this Naomi?"

20 Naomi said, "Do not call me Naomi. Call me Mara. The Almighty has made my life very bitter. 21 I went away full, but the LORD brought me back empty. Why call me Naomi? The LORD has spoken against me. The Almighty has hurt me."

22 So Naomi came back from Moab. Her son's wife Ruth, the woman from Moab, came with her. They got to Bethlehem at the start of the barley harvest.
