Jesus Is Whipped and Made Fun Of

1 Then Pilate took Jesus and had Him whipped. 2 The soldiers twisted thorns into a crown. They put it on His head. They put a purple robe on Him. 3 They came to Him again and again. They said, "Hello, King of the Jews!" They hit Him in the face.

Pilate Shows Jesus to the People

4 Pilate went out again. He said to the Jews, "Look, I bring Him out to you. I want you to know I find no fault in Him."

5 Jesus came out wearing the thorn crown and purple robe. Pilate said, "Here is the man!"

6 When the chief priests and helpers saw Him, they shouted, "Crucify Him! Crucify Him!"

Pilate said, "Take Him and crucify Him yourselves. I find no fault in Him."

7 The Jews answered, "We have a law. By that law He must die. He said He is the Son of God."

Pilate Gets Scared

8 When Pilate heard this, he was even more afraid. 9 He went back into his office. He asked Jesus, "Where are You from?" Jesus did not answer.

10 Pilate said, "Won't You talk to me? Don't You know I have power to free You and power to kill You on a cross?"

11 Jesus answered, "You would have no power over Me if it were not given to you from above. So the one who handed Me over to you is guilty of a worse sin."

12 From then on, Pilate tried to free Him. But the Jews shouted, "If you free this man, you are no friend of Caesar. Anyone who says he is a king fights against Caesar."

Pilate Hands Jesus Over

13 When Pilate heard this, he brought Jesus out. He sat on the judge's seat. The place was called Stone Pavement. In Hebrew it is Gabbatha. 14 It was the day to get ready for Passover. It was about noon. Pilate said to the Jews, "Here is your King!"

15 They shouted, "Take Him away! Take Him away! Crucify Him!"

Pilate said, "Should I crucify your King?"

The chief priests answered, "We have no king but Caesar."

16 Then Pilate handed Him over to be killed on a cross. The soldiers took Jesus away. 17 Jesus carried His own cross. He went out to the Place of the Skull. In Hebrew it is called Golgotha. 18 There they nailed Him to a cross. Two other men were with Him. One was on each side. Jesus was in the middle.

The Sign on the Cross

19 Pilate wrote a sign. He put it on the cross. It said, "Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews."

20 Many Jews read the sign. The place where Jesus was killed was near the city. The sign was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.

21 The chief priests said to Pilate, "Do not write, 'The King of the Jews.' Write, 'He said, I am the King of the Jews.'"

22 Pilate answered, "What I have written, I have written."

The Soldiers Take Jesus' Clothes

23 When the soldiers nailed Jesus to the cross, they took His clothes. They made four parts. Each soldier got one part. They also took His long shirt. It had no seams. It was woven all in one piece.

24 They said to each other, "Let's not tear it. Let's throw dice to see who gets it." This happened to make God's Word come true.

It says, "They split My clothes among them. They threw dice for My shirt." So the soldiers did this.

Jesus Cares for His Mother

25 Near Jesus' cross stood His mother. His mother's sister stood there too. Mary, the wife of Clopas, stood there. Mary Magdalene stood there.

26 Jesus saw His mother. He saw the follower He loved standing near. He said to His mother, "Woman, here is your son."

27 Then He said to the follower, "Here is your mother." From that time, the follower took her into his home.

Jesus Dies

28 After this, Jesus knew everything was done. He said this to make God's Word come true: "I am thirsty."

29 A jar of sour wine was there. They soaked a sponge in the wine. They put it on a plant branch. They held it to His mouth.

30 When Jesus drank the sour wine, He said, "It is finished." Then He bowed His head. He gave up His spirit.

A Soldier Pokes Jesus' Side

31 It was the day to get ready. The next day was a special Sabbath. The Jews did not want the bodies to stay on the crosses on the Sabbath. So they asked Pilate to break the men's legs and take them down. 32 The soldiers came. They broke the legs of the first man. They broke the legs of the other man too. Both were killed with Jesus. 33 But when they came to Jesus, they saw He was already dead. They did not break His legs. 34 Instead, one soldier poked His side with a spear. Blood and water came out right away.

35 The one who saw this has told about it. His story is true. He knows he tells the truth. He tells it so you may believe.

36 This happened to make God's Word come true: "None of His bones will be broken."

37 Another writing says, "They will look at the One they poked."

Jesus Is Buried

38 After this, Joseph from Arimathea asked Pilate for Jesus' body. Joseph was a follower of Jesus, but he kept it secret because he was afraid of the Jews. Pilate said yes. So Joseph came and took His body.

39 Nicodemus came too. He was the man who came to Jesus at night before. He brought about 75 pounds of sweet-smelling spices.

40 So they took Jesus' body. They wrapped it in cloth strips with spices. This is how the Jews bury their dead. 41 There was a garden where Jesus was killed. In the garden was a new tomb. No one had been put there yet. 42 Because it was the Jewish day to get ready, and the tomb was close, they put Jesus there.
