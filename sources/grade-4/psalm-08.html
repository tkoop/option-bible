God Is Great

1 The LORD, our Lord, how great is Your name in all the world! You put Your glory above the sky.

2 From the mouths of babies and little children, You made strong praise. This stops Your enemies and those who want revenge.

God Made the Sky

3 When I look at Your sky, the work of Your hands, I see the moon and stars You put there.

What Are People?

4 What is a person that You think about him? What is a human being that You care for him?

5 You made people a little lower than God. You crowned them with glory and honor.

People Rule the Animals

6 You put people in charge of all You made. You put everything under their feet: 7 all sheep and cows, and the wild animals too; 8 birds in the sky, fish in the sea, and all that travels through the ocean.

Praise God Again

9 The LORD, our Lord, how great is Your name in all the world!
