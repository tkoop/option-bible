Sarai Gives Hagar to Abram

1 Abram's wife Sarai had no children. She had a servant girl from Egypt. Her name was Hagar.

2 Sarai said to Abram, "Look, the LORD has stopped me from having children. Please go to my servant girl. Maybe I can have a family through her." Abram listened to Sarai.

3 Abram had lived in the land of Canaan for ten years. Then Sarai took Hagar, her Egyptian servant girl, and gave her to Abram to be his wife.

4 Abram went to Hagar, and she became pregnant. When Hagar saw she was pregnant, she looked down on Sarai.

5 Sarai said to Abram, "This is your fault! I gave my servant to you. Now she is pregnant, and she treats me badly. May the LORD decide between you and me."

6 Abram said, "Your servant is in your hands. Do what you think is best." Then Sarai was mean to Hagar, and Hagar ran away.

The Angel Meets Hagar

7 The angel of the LORD found Hagar by a spring in the desert. The spring was on the way to Shur.

8 The angel said, "Hagar, servant of Sarai, where did you come from? Where are you going?"

Hagar said, "I am running away from Sarai, my boss."

9 The angel said, "Go back to your boss and obey her."

10 The angel also said, "I will give you so many children that no one can count them."

11 The angel said, "Look, you are pregnant. You will have a son. You will name him Ishmael, because the LORD heard your trouble."

12 He will be wild like a donkey. He will fight with everyone, and everyone will fight with him. He will not get along with his brothers.

Hagar Talks to God

13 Hagar called the the LORD who spoke to her, "You are the God who sees me." She said this because she thought she had seen the God who sees her.

14 So the well was called Beer-lahai-roi. It is between Kadesh and Bered.

Ishmael Is Born

15 Hagar had a son for Abram. Abram named him Ishmael.

16 Abram was 86 years old when Hagar had Ishmael.
