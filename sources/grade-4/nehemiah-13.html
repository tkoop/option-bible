People Hear God's Law

1 That day the people read from the Book of Moses. They heard that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever join God's people. 2 This was because they did not help Israel with food and water. They paid Balaam to curse Israel. But our God turned the curse into a blessing. 3 When the people heard the law, they sent away everyone who was not from Israel.

Tobiah Gets a Room

4 Before this, Eliashib the priest was close to Tobiah. 5 He gave Tobiah a big room in God's house. Before that, people kept grain gifts, incense, temple things, and the tenth part of grain, new wine, and oil there. These were for the Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and gifts for the priests.

6 All this time I was not in Jerusalem. I had gone to the king of Babylon in the thirty-second year of his rule. Later I asked to leave and came back.

Nehemiah Cleans God's House

7 I came to Jerusalem. I saw the bad thing Eliashib did for Tobiah. He made a room for him in the yards of God's house. 8 This made me very angry. I threw all of Tobiah's things out of the room. 9 I told them to clean the rooms. They cleaned God's house. I brought back the grain gifts and incense.

10 I also learned that the Levites had not been given their share. The Levites and singers who did the work had gone back to their fields. 11 I argued with the leaders. I said, "Why has God's house been left empty?" I brought them together and put them back in their places. 12 Then all Judah brought the tenth part of grain, new wine, and oil into the store rooms. 13 I put Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the writer, Pedaiah the Levite, and Hanan son of Zaccur son of Mattaniah in charge of the store rooms. They were trusted men. They were to share the gifts with their brothers.

14 "Remember me, my God, for this. Do not wipe out the good things I did for Your house and its work."

People Break the Sabbath

15 In those days I saw people in Judah pressing grapes on the Sabbath. They brought big piles of grain and loaded donkeys. They also brought wine, grapes, figs, and all kinds of loads into Jerusalem on the Sabbath. I warned them not to sell food on that day. 16 Men from Tyre who lived there brought fish and all kinds of goods. They sold them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah in Jerusalem.

17 I argued with the leaders of Judah. I said to them, "What bad thing are you doing? You are ruining the Sabbath. 18 Didn't your fathers do this? Our God brought all this trouble on us and on this city. Now you bring more anger on Israel by ruining the Sabbath."

19 When the gates of Jerusalem began to get dark before the Sabbath, I told them to shut the doors. I said they should not open them until after the Sabbath. I put some of my men at the gates so no load could come in on the Sabbath.

20 Sellers of all kinds of goods stayed outside Jerusalem once or twice. 21 I warned them. I said, "Why do you stay outside the wall? If you do it again, I will use force against you." After that, they did not come on the Sabbath.

22 I told the Levites to make themselves clean and come guard the gates. They were to keep the Sabbath holy. "Remember me, my God, for this. Spare me because of Your great love."

Mixed Marriages

23 In those days I also saw Jews who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab. 24 Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod. They could not speak the language of Judah. They spoke the language of many peoples.

25 I argued with them. I cursed them. I hit some of them. I pulled out their hair. I made them promise by God, "You will not give your daughters to their sons. You will not take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves."

26 "Didn't King Solomon of Israel sin because of this? There was no king like him among many nations. He was loved by his God. God made him king over all Israel. Yet foreign women made him sin. 27 Should we listen to you and do all this great evil? Should we be unfaithful to our God by marrying foreign women?"

28 One son of Joiada, son of Eliashib the high priest, was married to a daughter of Sanballat the Horonite. I chased him away from me.

29 "Remember them, my God, because they have made the priesthood and the promise of the priests and Levites unclean."

30 So I cleaned them from everything foreign. I set the priests and Levites in their jobs. Each man had his work. 31 I also set times for wood for the altar and for the first crops. "Remember me, my God, for good."
