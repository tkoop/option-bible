Praise the LORD

1 Praise the LORD! Give thanks to the LORD, because He is good. His love lasts forever. 2 Who can tell all the great things the LORD has done? Who can say all the good things about Him? 3 Happy are those who do what is right and fair all the time.

Remember Me, LORD

4 Remember me, the LORD, when You show kindness to Your people. Help me when You save them. 5 Let me see the good things You give to the people You chose. Let me be glad with the joy of Your nation. Let me praise You with Your people.

We Sinned Like Our Fathers

6 We sinned like our fathers did. We did wrong and did bad things. 7 Our fathers in Egypt did not understand Your great works. They did not remember how much You loved them. They turned against You at the Red Sea.

God Saved Them at the Red Sea

8 But He saved them so people would know His name. He showed His great power. 9 He spoke to the Red Sea, and it dried up. He led them through the deep water like it was dry ground. 10 He saved them from their enemies. He set them free from those who hated them. 11 The water covered their enemies. Not one of them was left. 12 Then they believed His words. They sang His praise.

They Soon Forgot God

13 But they soon forgot what He had done. They did not wait for His plan. 14 They wanted bad things in the desert. They tested God in the dry land. 15 He gave them what they asked for. But He sent a sickness that made them weak. 16 In the camp they were jealous of Moses and of Aaron, the holy man of the LORD.

The Earth Swallowed the Rebels

17 The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan. It covered the group of Abiram. 18 Fire burned among their group. Flames burned up the wicked people.

They Made a Golden Calf

19 At Horeb they made a calf. They bowed down to a metal statue. 20 They traded their glorious God for a statue of an ox that eats grass. 21 They forgot God, their Savior, who did great things in Egypt. 22 He did wonderful things in Egypt. He did scary things at the Red Sea. 23 So He said He would destroy them. But Moses, His chosen servant, stood before Him and turned His anger away, so He would not destroy them.

They Did Not Trust God

24 They did not want the good land. They did not trust His word. 25 They complained in their tents. They did not listen to the LORD's voice. 26 So He raised His hand against them. He said He would make them fall in the desert. 27 He would scatter their children among the nations. He would spread them out in other lands.

They Served Other Gods

28 They joined themselves to Baal of Peor. They ate food offered to dead idols. 29 They made Him angry with what they did. So a sickness broke out among them. 30 Then Phinehas stood up and did what was right. The sickness stopped. 31 God counted this as something good. People will remember it forever.

They Upset Moses

32 They made Him angry at the waters of Meribah. Moses got in trouble because of them. 33 They upset him so much that he spoke without thinking.

They Did Not Obey God

34 They did not destroy the nations, like the LORD told them to. 35 They mixed with the nations and learned their ways. 36 They served their idols, and the idols trapped them. 37 They sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons. 38 They spilled the blood of innocent children. They sacrificed them to the idols of Canaan. The land was made dirty by all that blood. 39 They became unclean by what they did. They were unfaithful in what they did.

God Was Angry but Kind

40 So the LORD was angry with His people. He turned away from His own people. 41 He let other nations rule over them. Their enemies were in charge of them. 42 Their enemies crushed them. They were beaten down under their power. 43 He saved them many times. But they kept turning against Him. They became weak because of their sin. 44 He saw their trouble. He heard their cry. 45 He remembered His promise to them. He felt sorry for them because His love is so great. 46 He made the people who took them away feel sorry for them.

A Prayer for Help

47 Save us, the LORD our God. Bring us back from the nations. Then we will thank You for Your holy name. We will praise You. 48 Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, forever and ever. Let all the people say, 'Amen!' Praise the LORD!
