Hard Times in the Last Days

1 But know this: In the last days, hard times will come. 2 People will love only themselves and money. They will brag and be proud. They will say mean things and not obey their parents. They will be unthankful and not holy. 3 They will not love others or forgive. They will tell lies and not control themselves. They will be mean and hate what is good. 4 They will turn on friends, act without thinking, and be full of pride. They will love fun more than God. 5 They will look like they love God, but they will say no to God's power. Stay away from people like that.

False Teachers Will Come

6 Some of them sneak into homes and lead weak women away. These women are full of sin. They follow many wrong wants. 7 They always learn, but they never learn the truth. 8 Just like Jannes and Jambres stood against Moses, these people stand against the truth. Their minds are ruined. They fail the test of faith. 9 But they will not get far. Everyone will see how foolish they are, just like people saw those men's foolishness.

Follow Paul's Example

10 But you followed my teaching, my way of life, my plan, my faith, my patience, my love, and my staying power. 11 You also saw my troubles and pain. You saw what happened to me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. You saw how I was treated. The Lord rescued me from all of them. 12 Yes, everyone who wants to live for God in Christ Jesus will be treated badly. 13 But bad people and fakes will get worse and worse. They will trick others, and they will be tricked too.

Stay True to God's Word

14 But you must stay in what you learned and trusted. You know who taught you. 15 You have known the holy writings since you were a baby. They can make you wise so you can be saved. This comes through faith in Christ Jesus. 16 All Scripture is from God. It is useful for teaching, for showing what is wrong, for fixing mistakes, and for training people to do right. 17 This helps God's person be ready and able to do every good work.
