Philistines Fight Israel

1 The Philistines fought against Israel. The men of Israel ran away from the Philistines. Many fell dead on Mount Gilboa. 2 The Philistines went after Saul and his sons. They killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, who were Saul's sons. 3 The battle got hard for Saul. The archers caught up to him. They shot at him, and he was badly hurt.

Saul Dies

4 Saul said to the man who carried his armor, "Draw your sword and kill me with it. I do not want those men who do not follow God to come and hurt me." But the man was very afraid, so he would not do it. Then Saul took his own sword and fell on it. 5 When the man who carried his armor saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died. 6 So Saul died. His three sons died too. All of his family died with him. 7 All the men of Israel in the valley saw that Israel ran away. They saw that Saul and his sons were dead. So they left their towns and ran away too. The Philistines came and lived in those towns.

Philistines Take Saul's Things

8 The next day, the Philistines came to take things from the dead men. They found Saul and his sons lying dead on Mount Gilboa. 9 They took his armor and his head. They sent them all around the land of the Philistines. They wanted to tell their idols and their people. 10 They put his armor in the house of their gods. They put his head in the house of Dagon.

Men of Jabesh Bury Saul

11 The people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul. 12 All the brave men got up. They took the bodies of Saul and his sons. They brought them to Jabesh. They buried their bones under a big tree in Jabesh. Then they did not eat for seven days.

Why Saul Died

13 Saul died because he turned away from the the LORD. He did not obey the the LORD's word. He also asked a person who talked to spirits for help. 14 Saul did not ask the the LORD for help. So the the LORD put him to death. The the LORD gave the kingdom to David, the son of Jesse.
