Paul's Deep Love for His People

1 I tell the truth in Christ. I am not lying. My heart and the Holy Spirit agree that this is true. 2 I am very sad. My heart hurts all the time. 3 I would even wish to be away from Christ for my brothers and sisters. They are my own people. 4 They are Israelites. God gave them many good things. He made them His children. He showed them His glory. He made agreements with them. He gave them His law. He told them how to worship Him. And He gave them His promises. 5 Their ancestors are great. Christ came from them as a man. He is God over all. Praise Him forever! Amen.

God Keeps His Promises

6 But God's word has not failed. Not everyone born in Israel is truly Israel. 7 Not all of Abraham's children are God's children. God said, "Your family will be named through Isaac." 8 This means God's children are not just children born in the usual way. God's children are the children He promised. They count as Abraham's family. 9 Here is God's promise: "At the right time I will come back, and Sarah will have a son." 10 Rebekah also had children. They had the same father, our father Isaac. 11 Before the twins were born, before they did good or bad, God chose. He did this so His plan would stand. God chooses by His own plan, not by what people do. 12 God told Rebekah, "The older son will serve the younger son." 13 The Bible says, "I loved Jacob, but I did not love Esau."

Is God Fair?

14 So what should we say? Is God unfair? No, never! 15 God said to Moses, "I will be kind to anyone I choose. I will care for anyone I choose." 16 So it does not depend on what we want or how hard we try. It depends on God, who is kind. 17 The Bible says to Pharaoh, "I made you important for this reason: I wanted to show My power in you. I wanted My name to be known all over the world." 18 So God is kind to anyone He wants. He also makes some people stubborn.

God Is the Potter

19 You might say to me, "Then why does God still blame people? No one can stop His plan."

20 But who are you, a person, to talk back to God? A pot does not say to the one who made it, "Why did You make me like this?" 21 The potter has the right to make pots from the same clay. He can make one pot for a special use and another for a plain use. 22 What if God wanted to show His anger and power? He waited with great patience. He let people who are ready for punishment stay for a while. 23 He did this to show how great His glory is. He is kind to people He made ready for glory. 24 He called us. He called us not only from the Jews but also from the other nations.

God Calls People from All Nations

25 God said in Hosea, "I will call people who were not My people, 'My people.' I will call a woman who was not loved, 'My loved one.'" 26 "In the place where people said, 'You are not My people,' they will be called 'children of the living God.'"

27 Isaiah cried out about Israel: "Even if there are as many Israelites as sand by the sea, only a few will be saved." 28 The Lord will do what He said on the earth. He will finish it and cut it short. 29 Isaiah also said, "If the Lord of all had not left us some children, we would have been like Sodom. We would have been like Gomorrah."

Right with God by Faith

30 So what should we say? People who are not Jews did not try to be right with God. But they became right with God by faith. 31 But Israel tried to follow a law to be right with God. They did not reach it. 32 Why? Because they tried by doing things, not by faith. They tripped over the stone that makes people trip. 33 The Bible says, "Look, I put a stone in Zion that makes people trip. It is a rock that makes people fall. But anyone who trusts in Him will not be put to shame."
