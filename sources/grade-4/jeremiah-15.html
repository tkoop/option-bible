The LORD Will Punish the People

1 Then the The LORD said to me, "Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me, I would not feel sorry for this people. Send them away from Me, and let them go. 2 If they ask you, 'Where should we go?' tell them, 'This is what the the LORD says: Those meant for death will die; those meant for the sword will be killed by the sword; those meant for hunger will starve; and those meant to be taken away will be taken away.'"

3 The LORD says, "I will send four kinds of trouble on them. The sword will kill some. Dogs will drag away some. Birds in the sky and animals on the ground will eat and destroy the rest. 4 I will make them a scary sight to all the kingdoms of the earth. This is because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem."

No One Will Feel Sorry for Jerusalem

5 The LORD says, "Who will feel sorry for you, Jerusalem? Who will be sad for you? Who will stop and ask how you are? 6 You left Me. You turned your back on Me. So I reached out My hand against you and destroyed you. I am tired of being kind."

7 "I will scatter them at the gates of the land. I will take away their children and destroy My people, because they did not turn from their bad ways. 8 Their widows will be more than the sand by the sea. I will bring an enemy at noon to hurt them. I will suddenly bring fear and trouble on mothers and their children. 9 The mother with seven children will be weak and die. Her life will end before the day is over. She will be shamed and disgraced. The rest of them I will kill with the sword in front of their enemies," says the the LORD.

Jeremiah Is Sad

10 "Oh, how sad I am, my mother, that you gave birth to me! I am a man who argues with everyone in the land. I have not loaned or borrowed money, but everyone curses me."

The LORD Promises to Help Jeremiah

11 The LORD said, "I will help you and do good for you. I will make your enemies beg you for help when trouble comes. 12 Can iron break iron from the north? Can bronze break it? 13 I will let your wealth and treasures be taken for free, because of all your sins, all over your land. 14 I will make you go with your enemies to a land you do not know. My anger is like a fire that burns against you."

Jeremiah Prays to the the LORD

15 "You know me, the LORD. Remember me and take care of me. Pay back those who hurt me. Do not stay angry with me for long. Know that I suffer shame because of You. 16 I found Your words and took them to heart. They made me happy and glad, because I am called by Your name, the LORD God of all armies. 17 I did not join people who party and laugh, and I did not have fun. I stayed alone, because Your hand was on me. You made me angry at wrong. 18 Why does my pain never stop? Why is my hurt so bad it will not heal? Will You let me down like a stream that dries up? Will Your help fail like water that is not there?"

The LORD Answers Jeremiah

19 The LORD says, "If you come back to Me, I will bring you back. You will stand before Me. If you choose good words over bad words, you will speak for Me. People will come to you, but you will not go to them. 20 I will make you a strong wall of bronze for these people. They will fight you, but they will not win. I am with you to save you and rescue you," says the the LORD. 21 "I will save you from bad people and set you free from mean people."
