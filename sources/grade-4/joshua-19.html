Land for Simeon

1 The second lot was for the families of Simeon. Their land was inside Judah's land. 2 They got Beersheba, Sheba, and Moladah. 3 They got Hazar-shual, Balah, and Ezem. 4 They got Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah. 5 They got Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah. 6 They got Beth-lebaoth and Sharuhen. That was thirteen towns and their villages. 7 They got Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan. That was four towns and their villages. 8 They also got all the villages around these towns, all the way to Baalath-beer, which is Ramah in the south. This was Simeon's land. 9 Simeon's land came from Judah's part, because Judah had too much land. So Simeon lived inside Judah's land.

Land for Zebulun

10 The third lot was for the families of Zebulun. Their border reached to Sarid. 11 It went west to Maralah and touched Dabbesheth. It met the stream east of Jokneam. 12 From Sarid it turned east, toward the sunrise, to the border of Chisloth-tabor. Then it went to Daberath and up to Japhia. 13 From there it went east to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin. It reached Rimmon and turned toward Neah. 14 Then the border went north around Hannathon. It ended at the Valley of Iphtah-el. 15 It had Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem. That was twelve towns and their villages. 16 This was Zebulun's land, with these towns and villages.

Land for Issachar

17 The fourth lot was for the families of Issachar. 18 Their land had Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem. 19 It had Hapharaim, Shion, and Anaharath. 20 It had Rabbith, Kishion, and Abez. 21 It had Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 22 The border touched Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh. It ended at the Jordan River. That was sixteen towns and their villages. 23 This was Issachar's land, with these towns and villages.

Land for Asher

24 The fifth lot was for the families of Asher. 25 Their land had Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph. 26 It had Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal. On the west it touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath. 27 Then it turned east to Beth-dagon. It touched Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el. It went north to Beth-emek and Neiel. It reached Cabul on the left. 28 It went to Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah. It reached as far as big Sidon. 29 Then the border turned to Ramah. It went to the strong city of Tyre. It turned to Hosah. It ended at the sea near Achzib. 30 It had Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob. That was twenty-two towns and their villages. 31 This was Asher's land, with these towns and villages.

Land for Naphtali

32 The sixth lot was for the families of Naphtali. 33 Their border started at Heleph and the big tree at Zaanannim. It passed Adami-nekeb and Jabneel. It went to Lakkum and ended at the Jordan River. 34 Then the border turned west to Aznoth-tabor. It went to Hukkok. It touched Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and the Jordan River on the east. 35 The strong towns were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth. 36 It had Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor. 37 It had Kedesh, Edrei, and En-hazor. 38 It had Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh. That was nineteen towns and their villages. 39 This was Naphtali's land, with these towns and villages.

Land for Dan

40 The seventh lot was for the families of Dan. 41 Their land had Zorah, Eshtaol, and Ir-shemesh. 42 It had Shaalabbin, Aijalon, and Ithlah. 43 It had Elon, Timnah, and Ekron. 44 It had Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath. 45 It had Jehud, Bene-berak, and Gath-rimmon. 46 It had Me-jarkon and Rakkon, near Joppa. 47 The Danites could not take all their land, so they went up and fought Leshem. They took it and killed its people. They lived there and named it Dan, after their father Dan. 48 This was Dan's land, with these towns and villages.

Joshua's City

49 When they finished dividing the land, the people of Israel gave Joshua son of Nun a share among them. 50 As the LORD had said, they gave him the city he asked for: Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim. He built the city and lived there. 51 These are the lands that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the family leaders gave out by lot to the tribes of Israel at Shiloh, in front of the LORD, at the door of the Tent of Meeting. So they finished dividing the land.
