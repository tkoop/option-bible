A Message About Israel

1 This is a message from the LORD about Israel. The LORD, who spreads out the sky, lays the earth's base, and makes the human spirit inside a person, says:

God's Plan for Jerusalem

"2 I will make Jerusalem like a cup that makes all the countries around it stumble. Judah will also be attacked. Enemies will surround Jerusalem.

"3 On that day, all the countries in the world will gather against her. I will make Jerusalem like a heavy stone. Everyone who tries to lift it will hurt themselves.

"4 On that day," declares the LORD, "I will make every horse panic and its rider go crazy. I will watch over Judah, but I will blind all the horses of the other countries."

5 Then Judah's leaders will say in their hearts, "The people of Jerusalem are strong. The LORD of Armies is their God."

"6 On that day I will make Judah's leaders like a fire in a pile of wood and like a torch in dry grain. They will burn up all the countries around them, on every side. But Jerusalem will stay safe in her place."

The Lord Will Save His People

7 The LORD will save Judah's homes first. He does this so the honor of David's family and of Jerusalem's people will not be greater than Judah's.

8 On that day the LORD will protect the people who live in Jerusalem. The weakest among them will be like David. David's family will be like God, like the LORD's angel going before them.

God Will Pour Out His Spirit

"9 On that day I will destroy all the countries that come against Jerusalem.

"10 Then I will pour out on David's family and on the people of Jerusalem a spirit of kindness and prayer. They will look at Me, the One they hurt. They will cry for Him like people cry for an only child. They will be very sad for Him like people are sad for a firstborn son."

A Time of Deep Sadness

11 On that day the crying in Jerusalem will be as great as the crying at Hadad Rimmon in the valley of Megiddo.

12 The land will be sad. Each family will be by itself, and their wives by themselves. David's family and their wives will be by themselves. Nathan's family and their wives will be by themselves.

13 Levi's family and their wives will be by themselves. Shimei's family and their wives will be by themselves.

14 All the other families will be by themselves, and their wives by themselves.
