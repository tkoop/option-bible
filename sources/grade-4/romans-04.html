Abraham Was Made Right by Faith

1 So what can we say about Abraham, our father long ago? What did he find? 2 If Abraham was made right by the good things he did, he could brag. But he could not brag before God. 3 What does God's book say? It says, "Abraham trusted God. God counted that as being right with Him." 4 A worker gets pay. That pay is not a gift. It is owed. 5 But a person who does not work, and trusts God who makes bad people right, has faith. God counts that faith as being right with Him.

David Says the Same Thing

6 David says the same thing. He says God counts a person right without that person doing good works. 7 He said, "Happy are the people whose wrong ways are forgiven. Happy are the people whose sins are covered. 8 Happy is the man whose sin the LORD will never count against him."

This Gift Is for All People

9 Is this happy gift only for people who are circumcised? Or is it also for people who are not? We have been saying that Abraham's faith was counted as being right with God. 10 When was it counted? Was it after he was circumcised, or before? It was before he was circumcised, not after. 11 Abraham got the mark of circumcision. It was a sign that he was already right with God because of his faith. So he is the father of all who believe, even if they are not circumcised. God counts them right too. 12 He is also the father of people who are circumcised. But they must not only be circumcised. They must also follow the same faith our father Abraham had before he was circumcised.

The Promise Comes by Faith

13 God promised Abraham and his family that they would receive the world. This promise did not come through the law. It came through being right with God by faith. 14 If people who follow the law are the ones who get the promise, then faith means nothing. The promise would be empty. 15 The law brings God's anger. Where there is no law, there is no breaking of the law. 16 So the promise comes by faith. Then it is a free gift. It is sure for all of Abraham's family. It is not only for people who follow the law. It is also for people who have Abraham's faith. He is the father of us all.

God Gives Life to the Dead

17 It is written, "I have made you a father of many nations." Abraham is our father. He believed in God. God gives life to dead people. He calls things that are not here yet as if they are. 18 Abraham hoped when there was no hope. He believed God's words: "So shall your children be." 19 Abraham's faith did not get weak. He knew his body was almost dead. He was about one hundred years old. He knew Sarah could not have a baby. 20 But he did not doubt God's promise. His faith grew strong. He gave glory to God. 21 He was sure God could do what He promised. 22 That is why it was counted to him as being right with God.

Written for Us Too

23 The words "it was counted to him" were not written only for Abraham. 24 They were also written for us. God will count us right too, if we believe in Him. He raised Jesus our Lord from the dead. 25 Jesus was handed over to die because of our sins. He was raised to life to make us right with God.
