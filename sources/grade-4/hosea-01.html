Hosea's Story Begins

1 This is the word of the LORD that came to Hosea. Hosea was the son of Beeri. It came in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah. They were kings of Judah. It also came in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash. He was king of Israel.

2 When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, the LORD said to him, "Go, marry a woman who is not faithful. Have children with her. The people in this land have turned away from the LORD. They are not faithful to Him."

3 So Hosea went and married Gomer. She was the daughter of Diblaim. She became pregnant and had a son.

Hosea's Children

4 Then the LORD said to Hosea, "Name him Jezreel. In a little while I will punish Jehu's family. This is because of the blood spilled at Jezreel. I will end the kingdom of Israel. 5 On that day I will break Israel's bow in the Valley of Jezreel."

6 Gomer became pregnant again. She had a daughter. The LORD said to Hosea, "Name her 'No Mercy.' I will not show mercy to Israel anymore. I will not forgive them at all. 7 But I will be kind to Judah. I will save them. I will not do it with bows, swords, or war. I will not do it with horses or horsemen. I will save them by the LORD their God."

8 After Gomer stopped nursing 'No Mercy,' she became pregnant again. She had a son.

9 The LORD said, "Name him 'Not My People.' You are not My people. I am not your God."

Hope for God's People

10 But the people of Israel will be many. They will be like the sand by the sea. No one can count them. In the place where people said, "You are not My people," they will say, "You are children of the living God." 11 The people of Judah and Israel will come together. They will choose one leader. They will go up from the land. The day of Jezreel will be great.
