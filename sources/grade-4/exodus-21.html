Rules for Servants

1 These are the rules you must put before the people. 2 If you buy a Hebrew servant, he will work for six years. In the seventh year, he will go free. He will not pay anything. 3 If he came in alone, he will leave alone. If he had a wife, his wife will leave with him. 4 If his master gives him a wife, and she has sons or daughters for him, the wife and her children will belong to the master. The man will leave alone.

5 But if the servant says, “I love my master, my wife, and my children. I do not want to go free,” 6 then his master will take him to God. He will take him to the door or the door frame. Then his master will make a hole in his ear with a sharp tool. He will serve his master for life.

7 If a man sells his daughter to be a servant girl, she will not go free the same way the male servants go free. 8 If she does not please the master who chose her for himself, he must let someone buy her back. He must not sell her to people from another country, because he was not fair to her. 9 If he gives her to his son, he must treat her like a daughter. 10 If he marries another wife, he must still give her food, clothes, and time with him. 11 If he does not do these three things for her, she may go free. She will not pay anything.

Rules About Hurting People

12 Anyone who hits a person and kills him must be put to death. 13 But if he did not plan to kill him, and God let it happen, then I will give you a place where he can run for safety. 14 But if a man plans to kill his neighbor on purpose, you must take him away from My altar. He must die. 15 Anyone who hits his father or his mother must be put to death. 16 Anyone who kidnaps a person and sells him must be put to death. If the person is found with him, he must be put to death. 17 Anyone who curses his father or his mother must be put to death.

18 If two men fight and one hits the other with a stone or his fist, and the man does not die but must stay in bed, 19 and if the hurt man gets up and walks outside with a cane, the one who hit him is not guilty. But he must pay for the time the man could not work, and he must pay for the healing.

20 If a man hits his male or female servant with a stick and the servant dies, the man must be punished. 21 But if the servant lives for a day or two, the man will not be punished, because the servant belongs to him.

22 If men fight and hit a pregnant woman, and her baby is born early, but no one is badly hurt, the man who hit her must pay a fine. The woman's husband will decide the fine, and judges will set the amount. 23 But if someone is badly hurt, you must give life for life. 24 Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot. 25 Burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

26 If a man hits his male or female servant's eye and ruins it, he must let the servant go free because of the eye. 27 If he knocks out his male or female servant's tooth, he must let the servant go free because of the tooth.

Rules About Animals and Pits

28 If an ox kills a man or woman, the ox must be stoned to death. Its meat must not be eaten. The owner is not guilty. 29 But if the ox had hurt people before, and the owner knew but did not keep it tied up, and it kills a man or woman, the ox must be stoned. The owner must also be put to death. 30 If a price is put on his life, he must pay that price to save his life. 31 This same rule applies if the ox hurts a boy or a girl.

32 If the ox hurts a male or female servant, the owner must give the servant's master thirty pieces of silver. The ox must also be stoned. 33 If a man opens a pit, or digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or donkey falls into it, 34 the man who owns the pit must pay for the animal. He must give money to the animal's owner, and he keeps the dead animal. 35 If one man's ox hurts another man's ox and it dies, they will sell the live ox and share the money. They will also share the dead ox. 36 But if people knew the ox had hurt animals before, and the owner did not keep it tied up, he must give an ox for the ox that died. He keeps the dead ox.
