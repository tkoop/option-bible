Help Each Other

1 We who are strong should help carry the weak. We should not just please ourselves. 2 Each of us should try to please the person next to us. We should do good and help them grow strong.

3 For even Christ did not please Himself. The Bible says,

"The mean words of people who insult You fell on Me."

4 Everything written long ago was written to teach us. The Bible helps us keep going and gives us hope.

Live in Peace Together

5 May God, who helps us keep going and comforts us, help you agree with each other, like Christ Jesus wants. 6 Then you can all praise God together. He is the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

7 So welcome each other, just as Christ welcomed you. This brings praise to God.

Christ Came for All People

8 I tell you, Christ came to serve the Jewish people. He showed God's truth. He made God's promises to their fathers come true.

9 He did this so people who are not Jews may praise God for His mercy. The Bible says, "So I will praise You among the nations. I will sing to Your name."

10 Again it says, "Be happy, you nations, with His people."

11 And again, "Praise the LORD, all you nations. Let all people praise Him."

12 And again, Isaiah says, "The family line of Jesse will grow. One will come to rule the nations. People from all nations will hope in Him."

13 May God, who gives hope, fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in Him. Then you will overflow with hope through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Paul's Work for God

14 My brothers and sisters, I am sure you are full of goodness. You know a lot, and you can help each other learn. 15 I wrote to you boldly about some things. I did this to remind you. God gave me grace to do this.

16 I serve Christ Jesus by telling the good news to people who are not Jews. It is like a holy job. I want their lives to be a gift God accepts. The Holy Spirit makes them holy.

17 So I am proud of my work for God through Christ Jesus. 18 I will only talk about what Christ did through me. He helped people who are not Jews obey God. He did this by what I said and did.

19 God's Spirit gave me power to do signs and wonders. I told the good news about Christ everywhere from Jerusalem to Illyricum.

20 I always wanted to tell the good news where people did not know Christ. I did not want to build on another person's work. 21 The Bible says, "People who were not told about Him will see. People who have not heard will understand."

Paul's Plans to Visit

22 That is why I could not come to you sooner. 23 But now I have no more work here. I have wanted to visit you for many years.

24 I plan to visit you on my way to Spain. I hope to see you and enjoy time with you. Then you can help me on my trip.

25 But now I am going to Jerusalem. I am taking help to God's people there. 26 The churches in Macedonia and Achaia wanted to help. They gave money for poor believers in Jerusalem.

27 They were glad to do it. They owe them this help. If people who are not Jews received spiritual gifts from the Jews, they should help them with money and food. 28 So after I finish this job and make sure they get the gift, I will go to Spain. I will visit you on the way.

29 I know that when I come to you, Christ will bless us fully.

Paul Asks for Prayer

30 Brothers and sisters, I ask you to pray for me. Pray by the power of our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit. Help me in my struggle. 31 Pray that people in Judea who do not believe will not hurt me. Pray that God's people in Jerusalem will accept the gift.

32 Then I can come to you with joy, if God wants. We can rest together. 33 May the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.
