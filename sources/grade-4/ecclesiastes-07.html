A Good Name Is Better

1 A good name is better than perfume. 2 The day a person dies is better than the day a person is born. It is better to go to a house where people are sad than to go to a party. Death comes to every person. The living should think about that.

3 Being sad is better than laughing, because a sad face can make the heart better. 4 Wise people think about sad things. Foolish people think about fun.

Listen to Wise People

5 It is better to listen to a wise person correct you than to listen to fools sing. 6 A fool's laugh is like thorns crackling under a pot. It means nothing. 7 Being treated badly can make a wise person act like a fool. A bribe can ruin the heart.

8 The end of a thing is better than the start. Being patient is better than being proud. 9 Do not be quick to get angry. Anger stays in the heart of a fool.

10 Do not say, "Why were the old days better than now?" That is not a wise question.

Wisdom and Money

11 Wisdom is as good as money you get from your family. It is even better for people who are alive. 12 Wisdom keeps you safe, like money does. But knowing things is better, because wisdom helps you live.

Look at God's Work

13 Look at what God does. No one can fix what He bends. 14 On a good day, be happy. On a bad day, think about it. God made both days. People cannot know what will happen next.

15 I have seen strange things in my life. A good person can die even while doing right. A bad person can live a long time while doing wrong.

Do Not Go Too Far

16 Do not try to be too good. Do not try to be too wise. Why would you ruin yourself? 17 Do not do too much wrong. Do not be a fool. Why would you die too soon? 18 Hold on to both. Do not let go of either one. People who fear God stay away from both extremes.

19 Wisdom makes a wise person stronger than ten leaders in a city. 20 No one on earth always does good and never sins.

21 Do not listen to every word people say. You might hear your worker curse you. 22 You know in your heart that you have cursed other people many times.

Looking for Wisdom

23 I tested all this with wisdom. I said, 'I will be wise.' But it was too hard for me. 24 What is real is far away and very deep. Who can find it? 25 I turned my mind to learn and search for wisdom and how things fit together. I wanted to know why being wicked is foolish and why being foolish is crazy.

A Warning

26 I found something more bitter than death. It is a woman whose heart is like traps and nets. Her hands are like chains. A man who pleases God will escape her. A sinner will get caught by her.

27 The Teacher says, "Look, I found this. I put one thing with another to find an answer. 28 I looked for it with all my heart, but I did not find it. I found one good man among a thousand. But I did not find one good woman among them all. 29 Look, I found only this: God made people good, but they have looked for many tricks."
