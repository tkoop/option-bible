Give Glory to the LORD

1 A song of David. Give to the LORD, you mighty beings. Give to the LORD glory and strength. 2 Give to the LORD the glory His name deserves. Worship the LORD in His holy beauty.

3 The LORD's voice is over the waters. The God of glory thunders. The LORD is over many waters. 4 The LORD's voice is strong. The LORD's voice is grand.

5 The LORD's voice breaks the cedar trees. Yes, the LORD breaks the cedar trees of Lebanon. 6 He makes Lebanon jump like a calf. He makes Sirion jump like a young wild ox.

7 The LORD's voice flashes fire. 8 The LORD's voice shakes the desert. The LORD shakes the desert of Kadesh.

9 The LORD's voice twists the oak trees. It strips the woods bare. In His temple all cry, "Glory!"

10 The LORD sits as King over the flood. The LORD sits as King forever. 11 The LORD gives strength to His people. The LORD blesses His people with peace.
