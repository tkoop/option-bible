A Mighty Angel

1 I saw another strong angel coming down from heaven. He was wrapped in a cloud. A rainbow was on his head. His face was like the sun. His feet were like pillars of fire.

2 He had a small open scroll in his hand. He put his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land.

3 He cried out with a loud voice, like a lion roars. When he cried out, the seven thunders spoke with their own voices.

4 When the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write. But I heard a voice from heaven. It said, "Seal what the seven thunders said. Do not write them down."

The Angel's Promise

5 The angel I saw was standing on the sea and on the land. He raised his right hand to heaven.

6 The angel swore by the One who lives forever and ever. He made heaven and everything in it. He made the earth and everything in it. He made the sea and everything in it. He said, "There will be no more waiting!"

7 But in the days when the seventh angel is about to blow his trumpet, God's secret plan will be finished. God told this to His servants, the prophets.

John Eats the Scroll

8 I heard the voice from heaven again. It spoke to me. It said, "Go and take the open scroll from the hand of the angel who stands on the sea and on the land."

9 So I went to the angel. I said, "Give me the little scroll."

The angel said, "Take it and eat it. It will make your stomach bitter. But in your mouth it will be sweet like honey."

10 I took the little scroll from the angel's hand. I ate it. It was sweet like honey in my mouth. But after I ate it, my stomach became bitter.

11 Then they said to me, "You must tell God's message again to many peoples, nations, languages, and kings."
