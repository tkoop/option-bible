A Song for Everyone

1 Hear this, all people. Listen, everyone in the world. 2 Rich and poor, high and low, all listen together. 3 My mouth will speak wise things. My heart will share good thoughts. 4 I will listen to a wise saying. I will play my harp and explain my puzzle.

Money Cannot Save a Life

5 Why should I be afraid on bad days, when my sins catch up with me? 6 Some people trust in their money. They brag about how rich they are. 7 No one can buy back a brother's life. No one can pay God to save him. 8 It costs too much to save a life. No one can pay it. 9 So he cannot live forever. He cannot stay out of the grave.

Rich People Still Die

10 We see that wise people die. Foolish people die too. They leave their money to others. 11 They think their houses will last forever. They name lands after themselves. 12 But even a respected person will not last. He is like an animal that dies. 13 This is how foolish people live. But later people agree with their words. 14 They are like sheep led to the grave. Death is their shepherd. Good people will rule over them in the morning. Their bodies will rot in the grave, far from their fancy homes.

God Saves His People

15 But God will buy back my life from the grave. He will take me with Him.

Do Not Fear the Rich

16 Do not be afraid when a person gets rich. Do not worry when his home gets nicer. 17 When he dies, he takes nothing with him. His wealth does not follow him. 18 While he lives, he praises himself. People praise you when you do well for yourself. 19 He will go where his fathers went. He will never see light again. 20 A person may be honored, but if he does not understand, he is like an animal that dies.
