This song is for the music leader. It uses the tune "The Lily of the Promise." David wrote it to teach. It is about when he fought Aram Naharaim and Aram Zobah. Joab came back and beat twelve thousand men from Edom in the Valley of Salt.

God Has Turned Away from Us

1 You turned away from us, God. You broke us. You were angry. Please bring us back. 2 You shook the land and tore it open. Fix its cracks, because it is shaking.

3 You made Your people see hard times. You gave us wine that makes us wobble. 4 You gave a flag to people who fear You, so they can lift it up because of truth. Selah. 5 Answer us and save us by Your right hand, so the people You love may be saved.

God Promises to Win

6 God has spoken from His holy place: "I will win. I will divide Shechem. I will measure the Valley of Succoth.

7 "Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine. Ephraim is the helmet on My head. Judah is My ruler's stick.

8 "Moab is My washbasin. I will throw My sandal on Edom. I will shout with joy over Philistia."

We Ask God for Help

9 Who will take me to the strong city? Who will lead me to Edom? 10 Have You not turned away from us, God? Will You not go with our armies?

11 Give us help against our enemies, because help from people is worth nothing. 12 With God we will be brave, and He will crush our enemies.
