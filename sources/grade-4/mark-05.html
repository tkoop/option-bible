Jesus Goes Across the Sea

1 Jesus and His followers went across the sea to the land of the Gerasenes. 2 As soon as Jesus got out of the boat, a man with a bad spirit came from the graves to meet Him. 3 The man lived among the graves. No one could tie him up, not even with chains. 4 People had often tied him with chains and foot chains. He broke the chains and smashed the foot chains. No one was strong enough to control him. 5 Night and day he was in the graves and in the hills. He cried out and hurt himself with stones.

Jesus Helps a Man with Bad Spirits

6 The man saw Jesus from far away. He ran to Jesus and bowed down to Him. 7 He shouted, "What do You want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg You by God, don't hurt me!"

8 Jesus had said to the spirit, "Come out of this man, you bad spirit."

9 Jesus asked him, "What is your name?"

The spirit answered, "My name is Legion, because we are many."

10 The spirits begged Jesus not to send them out of that land.

11 A big herd of pigs was eating on the hillside. 12 The spirits begged, "Send us into the pigs, so we can go into them." 13 Jesus let them. The bad spirits came out of the man and went into the pigs. The herd, about two thousand pigs, ran down the hill into the sea and drowned.

14 The men who fed the pigs ran away. They told the people in the town and in the fields. The people came to see what had happened.

15 They came to Jesus and saw the man who had been controlled by demons. He was sitting down, wearing clothes, and thinking clearly. The people were afraid. 16 The people who had seen it told them what happened to the man and to the pigs.

17 The people began to beg Jesus to leave their land.

18 As Jesus got into the boat, the man who had been controlled by demons begged to go with Him. 19 Jesus did not let him. He said, "Go home to your people. Tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and how He has been kind to you." 20 The man went away. He began to tell people in the Ten Towns what Jesus had done for him. Everyone was amazed.

Jesus Heals a Woman and a Girl

21 Jesus crossed back over the sea in the boat. A big crowd gathered around Him by the sea. 22 A man named Jairus came. He was a leader of the synagogue. He saw Jesus and fell at His feet. 23 He begged Jesus, "My little daughter is very sick. Please come and put Your hands on her. Then she will be healed and live." 24 Jesus went with him. A big crowd followed and pressed around Jesus.

25 A woman had been bleeding for twelve years. 26 She had suffered much from many doctors. She had spent all her money. She was not better. She was worse. 27 She heard about Jesus. She came through the crowd from behind and touched His clothes.

28 She thought, "If I just touch His clothes, I will be healed." 29 Right away her bleeding stopped. She felt in her body that she was healed.

30 Jesus knew that power had gone out of Him. He turned around in the crowd and said, "Who touched My clothes?"

31 His followers said to Him, "You see the crowd pressing You. Why do You ask who touched You?"

32 Jesus looked around to see who had done it.

33 The woman knew what had happened to her. She came and fell down before Jesus. She was shaking with fear. She told Him the whole truth.

34 Jesus said to her, "Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace. Be well and free from your sickness."

35 While Jesus was still speaking, some people came from the house of the synagogue leader. They said, "Your daughter is dead. Why bother the Teacher anymore?"

36 Jesus paid no attention to what they said. He told the synagogue leader, "Don't be afraid. Just believe."

37 He let no one go with Him except Peter, James, and John, the brother of James.

38 They came to the house of the synagogue leader. Jesus saw a lot of noise. People were crying loudly. 39 He went in and said to them, "Why are you making so much noise and crying? The child is not dead. She is only sleeping."

40 They laughed at Him. Jesus made them all leave. He took the child's father and mother and the followers who were with Him. They went into the room where the child was.

41 Jesus took the child by the hand. He said to her, "Talitha koum." This means, "Little girl, I say to you, get up!"

42 Right away the girl stood up and walked around. She was twelve years old. The people were completely amazed.

43 Jesus told them strongly not to let anyone know about this. He also said to give the girl something to eat.
