Uzziah Becomes King

1 All the people of Judah picked Uzziah. He was sixteen years old. They made him king after his father Amaziah. 2 He built the town of Eloth. He brought it back to Judah. This was after the king died and was laid to rest with his family. 3 Uzziah was sixteen when he became king. He ruled 52 years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

Uzziah Does What Is Right

4 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. He did what his father Amaziah had done. 5 He asked God for help while Zechariah was alive. Zechariah understood God's messages. As long as Uzziah looked for the LORD, God helped him do well.

Uzziah Wins Battles

6 He went out and fought the Philistines. He broke down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. He built towns in Ashdod and in Philistine land. 7 God helped him fight the Philistines, the Arabs who lived in Gur-baal, and the Meunites. 8 The Ammonites paid Uzziah money. His fame spread all the way to Egypt. He became very strong.

Uzziah Builds Up Jerusalem

9 Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem. They were at the Corner Gate, the Valley Gate, and the Angle. He made them strong. 10 He built towers in the desert. He dug many wells. He had much livestock in the low hills and the flat land. He had farmers and grape growers in the mountains and in Carmel. He loved the land.

Uzziah's Army

11 Uzziah had an army ready for war. They went out in groups. Jeiel the writer and Maaseiah the officer counted them. Hananiah, one of the king's leaders, was over them. 12 The number of family leaders, the strong men, was 2,600. 13 Under them was an army of 307,500 men. They fought with great power. They helped the king against his enemies. 14 Uzziah gave all the army shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and stones for slings. 15 In Jerusalem he made machines. Skilled men made them. They were put on the towers and corners. They shot arrows and big stones. His name spread far away. God helped him in a wonderful way until he became strong.

Uzziah Becomes Proud

16 But when he became strong, his heart became proud. He did wrong against the LORD his God. He went into the temple of the LORD. He wanted to burn incense on the incense altar. 17 Azariah the priest went in after him. Eighty brave priests of the LORD went with him.

The Priests Stop Uzziah

18 They said, “Uzziah, it is not for you to burn incense to the LORD. Only the priests, the sons of Aaron, may do that. They are set apart to burn incense. Get out of the holy place. You have done wrong. The LORD God will not give you honor for this.”

Uzziah Gets Sick

19 Uzziah was angry. He had a pan of incense in his hand. While he was angry with the priests, in front of the priests in the house of the LORD, by the incense altar, a skin disease broke out on his forehead. 20 Azariah the chief priest and all the priests looked at him. They saw that he had a skin disease on his forehead. They hurried him out. He hurried to leave too, because the LORD had struck him. 21 King Uzziah had the skin disease until the day he died. He lived in a house by himself. He was kept away from the house of the LORD. His son Jotham ruled the people.

The End of Uzziah's Rule

22 The other things Uzziah did, from first to last, were written by the prophet Isaiah, the son of Amoz. 23 Uzziah died and was buried with his family. They buried him in the field where the kings were buried. People said he had a skin disease. His son Jotham became king after him.
