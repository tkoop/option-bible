From Adam to Noah

1 Adam was the father of Seth. Seth was the father of Enosh. 2 Enosh was the father of Kenan. Kenan was the father of Mahalalel. Mahalalel was the father of Jared. 3 Jared was the father of Enoch. Enoch was the father of Methuselah. Methuselah was the father of Lamech. 4 Lamech was the father of Noah. Noah had three sons. Their names were Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

The Sons of Japheth

5 Japheth had these sons: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 6 Gomer had these sons: Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah. 7 Javan had these sons: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim.

The Sons of Ham

8 Ham had these sons: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 9 Cush had these sons: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. Raamah had these sons: Sheba and Dedan. 10 Cush was the father of Nimrod. Nimrod became a mighty hero on earth. 11 Mizraim was the father of the Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim. 12 He was also the father of the Pathrusim, Casluhim, and Caphtorim. The Philistines came from the Casluhim. 13 Canaan's first son was Sidon. He also had Heth. 14 Canaan had these people groups: the Jebusites, Amorites, and Girgashites. 15 He had the Hivites, Arkites, and Sinites. 16 He had the Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites.

The Sons of Shem

17 Shem had these sons: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. 18 Arphaxad was the father of Shelah. Shelah was the father of Eber. 19 Eber had two sons. One was Peleg, because the earth was split in his time. The other was Joktan. 20 Joktan had these sons: Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah. 21 He had Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah. 22 He had Ebal, Abimael, and Sheba. 23 He had Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were Joktan's sons.

From Shem to Abraham

24 Shem was the father of Arphaxad. Arphaxad was the father of Shelah. 25 Shelah was the father of Eber. Eber was the father of Peleg. Peleg was the father of Reu. 26 Reu was the father of Serug. Serug was the father of Nahor. Nahor was the father of Terah. 27 Terah was the father of Abram. Abram is Abraham.

Abraham's Family

28 Abraham had two sons. They were Isaac and Ishmael. 29 Here are Ishmael's sons: Nebaioth was the first. Then Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam. 30 Then Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema. 31 Then Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were Ishmael's sons. 32 Abraham also had sons by Keturah, another wife. Their sons were Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan had Sheba and Dedan. 33 Midian had Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these came from Keturah. 34 Abraham was the father of Isaac. Isaac had two sons. They were Esau and Israel. 35 Esau had these sons: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 36 Eliphaz had these sons: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek. 37 Reuel had these sons: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

The Sons of Seir

38 Seir had these sons: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. 39 Lotan had Hori, Homam, and Timna. Lotan's sister was Timna. 40 Shobal had Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. Zibeon had Aiah and Anah. 41 Anah had Dishon. Dishon had Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 42 Ezer had Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. Dishan had Uz and Aran.

Kings of Edom

43 These kings ruled in Edom before Israel had a king. Bela was the son of Beor. His city was Dinhabah. 44 Bela died. Then Jobab, the son of Zerah from Bozrah, ruled. 45 Jobab died. Then Husham from the land of Teman ruled. 46 Husham died. Then Hadad, the son of Bedad, ruled. He beat Midian in the field of Moab. His city was Avith. 47 Hadad died. Then Samlah from Masrekah ruled. 48 Samlah died. Then Shaul from Rehoboth by the river ruled. 49 Shaul died. Then Baal-Hanan, the son of Achbor, ruled. 50 Baal-Hanan died. Then Hadad ruled. His city was Pau. His wife was Mehetabel. She was the daughter of Matred, and Matred was the daughter of Mezahab.

Leaders of Edom

51 Hadad died. These were the leaders of Edom: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, 52 Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 53 Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 54 Magdiel, and Iram. These were the leaders of Edom.
