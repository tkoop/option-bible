The LORD Talks to Cyrus

1 The LORD says this to Cyrus, the man He chose to lead: "I hold your right hand. I will help you beat nations. I will take power from kings. I will open doors for you. Gates will not stay shut. 2 I will go in front of you. I will make rough places flat. I will break bronze doors. I will cut iron bars."

3 "I will give you treasures hidden in dark places. I will give you secret riches. Then you will know I am the LORD, the God of Israel. I call you by name. 4 I do this for Jacob, who serves Me, and for Israel, My chosen people. I call you by name. I give you a special name. But you do not know Me."

5 "I am the LORD. There is no other God. I will give you strength, even though you do not know Me. 6 Then people from sunrise to sunset will know there is no one but Me. I am the LORD. There is no other. 7 I make light. I make darkness. I bring peace. I bring trouble. I, the LORD, do all these things."

8 "Rain down, heavens. Let the skies pour down what is right. Let the earth open up. Let saving help grow. Let what is right grow too. I, the LORD, made this."

Do Not Fight Your Maker

9 It is bad for a man who fights his Maker. He is like a clay pot among other pots. Will clay ask the potter what he is making? Will the thing made say he has no hands? 10 It is bad for one who says to his father, "What did you make?" or to his mother, "What did you bring into the world?"

The LORD Made Everything

11 The LORD, the Holy One of Israel, who made Israel, says this: "Ask Me about what will happen. Will you tell Me what to do with My children? Will you tell Me what to do with the work of My hands? 12 I made the earth. I made people on it. My hands spread out the skies. I told all the stars to be there. 13 I will raise Cyrus to do what is right. I will make his path straight. He will build My city again. He will let My people go free. He will not ask for money or pay. This is what the LORD of Armies says."

Other Nations Will Bow to You

14 The LORD says this: "Egypt's goods and Cush's goods, and the tall Sabeans, will come to you. They will be yours. They will walk behind you. They will come in chains. They will bow down to you. They will say that God is with you and there is no other God."

15 Truly, You are a God who hides Himself. You are the God of Israel. You save people.

16 They will all be ashamed. The men who make false gods will leave together in shame. 17 But Israel will be saved by the LORD forever. You will never be ashamed.

The LORD Made the World

18 The LORD says this: "He made the heavens. He is God. He made the earth. He set it in place. He did not make it empty. He made it for people to live in. I am the LORD. There is no other. 19 I did not speak in secret in a dark land. I did not tell Jacob's people, 'Look for Me for nothing.' I, the LORD, speak truth. I say what is right.

20 "Come together. Come near, you who ran away from the nations. They do not know anything. They carry wooden gods. They pray to a god that cannot save. 21 Speak up. Make your case. Let them talk together. Who said this long ago? Who told it from the start? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no other God but Me. I am a good God who saves. There is no one else.

Turn to the LORD and Be Saved

22 "Turn to Me and be saved, all the world. I am God. There is no other. 23 I made a promise by Myself. Truth came from My mouth. My word will not change. Every knee will bow to Me. Every tongue will promise to Me. 24 They will say that only in the LORD are right ways and strength. All who get angry at Him will come to Him and be ashamed. 25 All Israel's children will be made right in the LORD. They will praise Him."
