Trouble in Israel

1 Now, city of soldiers, gather your troops. We are under attack. They hit Israel's leader on the cheek with a stick.

A Ruler from Bethlehem

2 But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, you are small among Judah's family groups. Yet from you will come for Me a ruler for Israel. His beginnings are from long ago, from days of old. 3 So He will give them up until she who is in labor gives birth. Then the rest of His brothers will return to the people of Israel. 4 He will stand and care for His people with the strength of the LORD, and with the glory of the name of the LORD His God. They will live safely, because now He will be great to the ends of the earth.

Peace and Rescue

5 This One will bring peace. When the Assyrian comes into our land and walks in our palaces, we will raise up seven shepherds and eight leaders against him. 6 They will rule Assyria with the sword, and they will enter Nimrod's land through its gates. He will save us from the Assyrian when he comes into our land and steps over our border.

Jacob's People among the Nations

7 The rest of Jacob's people will be among many nations like dew from the LORD, like rain on the grass. It does not wait for people, and people cannot control it. 8 Jacob's people left will be among the nations, among many peoples, like a lion among forest animals, like a young lion among sheep. It will crush and tear, and no one will save them. 9 Your hand will be raised over your enemies, and all your foes will be destroyed.

God Takes Away Wrong Things

10 On that day, says the LORD, I will take away your horses and destroy your chariots. 11 I will destroy the cities of your land and tear down all your forts. 12 I will take away your magic, and you will have no more fortune tellers. 13 I will destroy your carved idols and your stone pillars, and you will not worship what your hands made anymore. 14 I will pull up your idols from among you and destroy your cities. 15 I will punish the nations that did not listen, in anger and fury.
