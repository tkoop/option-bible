John the Baptist Gets People Ready

1 In the fifteenth year of Tiberius Caesar's rule, Pontius Pilate was the ruler of Judea. Herod ruled Galilee. His brother Philip ruled Iturea and Trachonitis. Lysanias ruled Abilene. 2 Annas and Caiaphas were high priests. God's word came to John, Zechariah's son, in the desert.

3 John went all around the land near the Jordan River. He told people to be baptized. This showed they were sorry for their sins. God would forgive them.

4 This is what the prophet Isaiah wrote: "A voice calls out in the desert, 'Get the Lord's road ready. Make straight paths for Him.' 5 Every valley will be filled. Every mountain and hill will be made low. Crooked roads will become straight. Rough roads will become smooth. 6 And all people will see God's saving power."

John Warns the Crowds

7 John said to the crowds coming to be baptized, "You snakes! Who warned you to run away from God's coming anger? 8 Do things that show you are truly sorry. Do not start saying to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones. 9 The ax is already at the root of the trees. Every tree that does not make good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire."

John Tells People What to Do

10 The crowds asked him, "What should we do?"

11 John answered, "If you have two shirts, give one to someone who has none. If you have food, do the same."

12 Tax collectors also came to be baptized. They asked, "Teacher, what should we do?"

13 John said, "Do not collect more money than you are told to."

14 Some soldiers asked him, "What should we do?"

He said, "Do not rob anyone. Do not lie about people. Be happy with your pay."

15 The people were waiting. They wondered in their hearts if John might be the Christ.

John Says Jesus Is the One

16 John answered everyone, "I baptize you with water. But someone stronger than me is coming. I am not good enough to untie His sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

17 "He has a tool in His hand to separate grain. He will clean the place where grain is threshed. He will gather the good grain into His barn. But He will burn the worthless husks with fire that cannot be put out."

18 John said many other things too. He told the people the good news.

Herod Puts John in Prison

19 But John spoke against Herod the ruler. Herod had married his brother's wife, Herodias. John also spoke against all the other bad things Herod did. 20 So Herod added one more wrong. He locked John in prison.

Jesus Is Baptized

21 When all the people were being baptized, Jesus was baptized too. As He prayed, heaven opened. 22 The Holy Spirit came down on Him in a body like a dove. A voice from heaven said, "You are My Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with You."

Jesus' Family Line

23 Jesus was about thirty years old when He began His work. People thought He was Joseph's son. Joseph was Heli's son.

24 Heli was the son of Matthat. Matthat was the son of Levi. Levi was the son of Melchi. Melchi was the son of Jannai. Jannai was the son of Joseph. 25 Joseph was the son of Mattathias. Mattathias was the son of Amos. Amos was the son of Nahum. Nahum was the son of Esli. Esli was the son of Naggai. 26 Naggai was the son of Maath. Maath was the son of Mattathias. Mattathias was the son of Semein. Semein was the son of Josech. Josech was the son of Joda. 27 Joda was the son of Joanan. Joanan was the son of Rhesa. Rhesa was the son of Zerubbabel. Zerubbabel was the son of Shealtiel. Shealtiel was the son of Neri. 28 Neri was the son of Melchi. Melchi was the son of Addi. Addi was the son of Cosam. Cosam was the son of Elmadam. Elmadam was the son of Er.

29 Er was the son of Joshua. Joshua was the son of Eliezer. Eliezer was the son of Jorim. Jorim was the son of Matthat. Matthat was the son of Levi. 30 Levi was the son of Simeon. Simeon was the son of Judah. Judah was the son of Joseph. Joseph was the son of Jonam. Jonam was the son of Eliakim. 31 Eliakim was the son of Melea. Melea was the son of Menna. Menna was the son of Mattatha. Mattatha was the son of Nathan. Nathan was the son of David. 32 David was the son of Jesse. Jesse was the son of Obed. Obed was the son of Boaz. Boaz was the son of Salmon. Salmon was the son of Nahshon. 33 Nahshon was the son of Amminadab. Amminadab was the son of Admin. Admin was the son of Arni. Arni was the son of Hezron. Hezron was the son of Perez. Perez was the son of Judah.

34 Judah was the son of Jacob. Jacob was the son of Isaac. Isaac was the son of Abraham. Abraham was the son of Terah. Terah was the son of Nahor. 35 Nahor was the son of Serug. Serug was the son of Reu. Reu was the son of Peleg. Peleg was the son of Eber. Eber was the son of Shelah. 36 Shelah was the son of Cainan. Cainan was the son of Arphaxad. Arphaxad was the son of Shem. Shem was the son of Noah. Noah was the son of Lamech. 37 Lamech was the son of Methuselah. Methuselah was the son of Enoch. Enoch was the son of Jared. Jared was the son of Mahalalel. Mahalalel was the son of Cainan. 38 Cainan was the son of Enosh. Enosh was the son of Seth. Seth was the son of Adam. Adam was the son of God.
