Four Chariots

1 I looked up again and saw four chariots coming from between two bronze mountains. 2 The first chariot had red horses. The second had black horses. 3 The third had white horses. The fourth had spotted horses. All the horses were strong.

4 I asked the angel who was talking to me, "What are these, my lord?"

5 The angel said, "These are the four spirits of heaven. They are going out from where they stood before the Lord of all the earth."

6 The chariot with black horses goes to the north country. The white horses follow them. The spotted horses go to the south country.

7 The strong horses went out. They wanted to go and walk all over the earth. The LORD said, "Go and walk all over the earth." So they went.

8 Then He called to me and said, "Look, the ones going to the north country have brought peace to My Spirit there."

The Crown for the High Priest

9 The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 10 "Take a gift from the people who came back from Babylon. Get it from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah. Go that same day to the house of Josiah, son of Zephaniah. 11 Take silver and gold and make a crown. Put it on the head of Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest. 12 Tell him, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Here is a man whose name is the Branch. He will grow where He is and build the temple of the LORD. 13 Yes, He will build the temple of the LORD. He will be dressed in honor and will sit and rule on His throne. He will be a priest on His throne, and there will be peace between the two.' 14 The crown will be a reminder in the temple of the LORD. It will be for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen, son of Zephaniah. 15 People far away will come and build the temple of the LORD. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts sent me to you. This will happen if you listen carefully to the voice of the LORD your God."
