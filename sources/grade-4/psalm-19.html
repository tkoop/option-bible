The Sky Tells About God

1 This song is for the music leader. David wrote it. The sky tells how great God is. The wide space above shows what His hands made. 2 Each day speaks to the next day. Each night shows what God knows. 3 There are no words. We cannot hear their voice. 4 But their message goes all over the earth. Their words reach the ends of the world. God put a tent for the sun in the sky. 5 The sun is like a groom leaving his room. It is like a strong man who is happy to run. 6 It rises at one end of the sky. It travels to the other end. Nothing can hide from its heat.

God's Law Is Good

7 The law of the LORD is perfect. It gives new life to people. The the LORD's words can be trusted. They make a simple person wise. 8 The the LORD's rules are right. They make the heart happy. The the LORD's command is pure. It makes the eyes see clearly. 9 Respect for the LORD is pure. It lasts forever. The the LORD's decisions are true and right. 10 They are worth more than lots of gold. They are sweeter than honey from the honeycomb.

A Prayer to God

11 They warn Your servant. Keeping them brings a big reward. 12 Who can know his own mistakes? Wash me clean from hidden sins. 13 Keep Your servant from sins I do on purpose. Do not let them rule me. Then I will be blameless. I will be free from big sin. 14 Let the words of my mouth and the thoughts of my heart be pleasing to You. O the LORD, You are my Rock and my Savior.
