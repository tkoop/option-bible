Who Is Wise?

1 Who is like a wise person? Who knows what things mean? Wisdom makes a person's face shine. It also softens a hard face.

Obey the King

2 Obey the king's command. I say this because you made a promise to God. 3 Do not hurry to leave the king. Do not join a bad plan. He does what he wants. 4 A king's word has power. No one can ask him, "What are you doing?"

Right Times

5 Whoever obeys his command will not get hurt. A wise heart knows the right time and way. 6 There is a right time and way for every thing. But people have much trouble.

No One Knows the Future

7 No one knows what will happen next. Who can tell someone what is coming? 8 No one can hold back the wind. No one can stop the day they die. No one can leave a war early. Doing wrong will not save the people who do it.

I Saw All This

9 I saw all this. I thought about everything done on earth. Sometimes one person rules another and hurts them. 10 I also saw bad people buried. They used to come and go from the holy place. People forgot them in the city where they did this. This too is empty.

Justice Is Slow

11 When a bad act is not punished fast, people's hearts fill with plans to do wrong.

Fear God

12 A bad person may do many wrong things and live a long time. But I know it will go better for people who fear God and respect Him. 13 Bad people do not fear God, so things will not go well for them. Their days will not last long like a shadow. 14 There is something empty that happens on earth. Good people get what bad people deserve. Bad people get what good people deserve. I say this too is empty.

Enjoy Life

15 So I praise joy. There is nothing better for a person on earth than to eat, drink, and be glad. Joy will stay with them in their work all the days of life that God gives them on earth.

God's Work Is Too Big

16 I decided to learn wisdom and to see the work done on earth. Some people cannot sleep day or night. 17 Then I saw all that God has done. No one can understand what happens on earth. People work hard to find it, but they cannot. Even if a wise person says they know, they cannot really understand.
