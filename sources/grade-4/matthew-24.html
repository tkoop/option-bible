Jesus Leaves the Temple

1 Jesus left the temple. His followers came to Him and showed Him the temple buildings. 2 He said, "Do you see all these things? I tell you the truth. Not one stone here will be left on another. Every stone will be thrown down."

The Disciples Ask About the End

3 Jesus was sitting on the Mount of Olives. His followers came to Him alone. They said, "Tell us, when will this happen? What will be the sign of Your coming and of the end of the world?"

Be Careful Not to Be Tricked

4 Jesus answered, "Be careful. Do not let anyone trick you. 5 Many people will come using My name. They will say, 'I am the Christ.' They will trick many people. 6 You will hear about wars and stories of wars. Do not be afraid. These things must happen. But the end is not yet. 7 One nation will fight another nation. One kingdom will fight another kingdom. There will be times with no food. There will be earthquakes in many places. 8 All these things are just the start of the hard times."

Hard Times for Believers

9 Then people will hand you over to be hurt. They will kill you. All people will hate you because of My name. 10 Then many people will turn away from their faith. They will turn against each other. They will hate each other. 11 Many fake prophets will come. They will trick many people. 12 Because bad things will grow, the love of many people will grow cold. 13 But the one who keeps going to the end will be saved. 14 This good news about God's kingdom will be told in all the world. It will be a witness to all nations. Then the end will come.

The Awful Thing in the Holy Place

15 So when you see the terrible thing that ruins standing in the holy place, the one spoken of by the prophet Daniel, the reader should understand. 16 Then people in Judea should run to the hills. 17 A person on the roof should not go down to get things from the house. 18 A person in the field should not go back to get his coat. 19 It will be very hard in those days for women who are pregnant or feeding babies. 20 Pray that you will not have to run away in winter or on the Sabbath day. 21 For then there will be great trouble. It will be worse than any trouble since the world began. It will never be that bad again. 22 If those days were not made shorter, no one would live. But for the sake of God's chosen people, those days will be made shorter.

Do Not Believe Fake Christs

23 At that time, if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'There He is!' do not believe it. 24 Fake Christs and fake prophets will come. They will do big miracles and amazing things. They will try to trick even God's chosen people if they can. 25 See, I have told you before it happens. 26 So if anyone tells you, 'He is out in the desert,' do not go out. If they say, 'He is in the back rooms,' do not believe it. 27 The coming of the Son of Man will be like lightning. It flashes from the east and shines to the west. 28 Where there is a dead body, vultures gather.

The Son of Man Comes

29 Right after the trouble of those days, the sun will become dark. The moon will not give its light. The stars will fall from the sky. The powers in the sky will be shaken. 30 Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky. All the people of the earth will cry. They will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. 31 He will send His angels with a loud trumpet sound. They will gather His chosen people from every direction, from one end of the sky to the other.

Learn from the Fig Tree

32 Learn this lesson from the fig tree. When its branches get soft and leaves grow, you know that summer is near. 33 In the same way, when you see all these things, you know that it is very close. 34 I tell you the truth. The people living then will not all die before all these things happen. 35 Heaven and earth will pass away. But My words will never pass away.

No One Knows the Day

36 But no one knows about that day or hour. The angels in heaven do not know. The Son does not know. Only the Father knows. 37 It will be like in Noah's time when the Son of Man comes. 38 In those days before the flood, people were eating and drinking. They were getting married. This went on until Noah went into the ark. 39 They did not know what was coming. The flood came and took them all away. It will be like that when the Son of Man comes. 40 Two men will be in the field. One will be taken. The other will be left. 41 Two women will be grinding grain with a hand mill. One will be taken. The other will be left. 42 So stay awake. You do not know the day your Lord will come. 43 But know this. If the owner of a house had known what time at night the thief was coming, he would have stayed awake. He would not have let the thief break in. 44 So you too must be ready. The Son of Man will come at a time you do not expect.

The Good and Bad Servants

45 Who is the faithful and wise servant? His master put him in charge of the other servants. He gives them food at the right time. 46 That servant is blessed if his master comes back and finds him doing this. 47 I tell you the truth. The master will put him in charge of everything he owns. 48 But if that servant is bad, he may think, 'my master is taking a long time.' 49 He may start to beat the other servants. He may eat and drink with drunk people. 50 His master will come on a day he does not expect. He will come at a time he does not know. 51 He will cut him to pieces. He will put him with the pretenders. There, people will cry and grind their teeth.
