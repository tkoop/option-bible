The LORD Talks to Jonah

1 The word of the the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai. 2 The The LORD said, “Get up and go to the big city of Nineveh. Tell the people there that I see their bad ways.”

3 But Jonah ran away from the the LORD. He went to Tarshish. He went to Joppa and found a ship going to Tarshish. He paid the fare and got on. He sailed with them to Tarshish to get away from the the LORD.

The Storm on the Sea

4 Then the the LORD sent a big wind on the sea. A big storm came up. The ship was about to break apart. 5 The sailors were afraid. Each man cried to his own god. They threw the things on the ship into the sea to make the ship lighter. But Jonah had gone down into the ship. He lay down and fell into a deep sleep.

6 The captain came to him and said, “How can you sleep? Get up and call to your god! Maybe he will care about us and not let us die.”

7 The sailors said to each other, “Come, let us draw lots. Then we will know who caused this trouble.” They drew lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

8 They asked him, “Tell us, who caused this trouble for us? What is your job? Where do you come from? What country are you from? What people are you from?”

9 He said, “I am a Hebrew. I worship the the LORD, the God of heaven. He made the sea and the dry land.”

10 The men were very afraid. They said, “What have you done?” They knew he was running away from the the LORD, because he had told them.

11 The sea was getting rougher and rougher. They asked him, “What should we do to you so the sea will be calm for us?”

12 He said, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea. Then it will be calm. I know this big storm came on you because of me.”

13 The men tried hard to row back to land. But they could not. The sea got even wilder.

14 They cried to the the LORD, “Please, the the LORD, do not let us die because of this man. Do not blame us for killing a good man. You, the the LORD, have done what You wanted.”

Jonah Is Thrown into the Sea

15 Then they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea. The sea became calm. 16 The men were very afraid of the the LORD. They gave a gift to the the LORD. They made promises to Him.

The Big Fish

17 The The LORD sent a big fish to swallow Jonah. Jonah was in the fish’s belly for three days and three nights.
