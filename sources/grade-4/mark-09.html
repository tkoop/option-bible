Some Will See God's Kingdom

1 Truly, I tell you, some people standing here will not die before they see God's kingdom come with power.

Jesus Shines on the Mountain

2 Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John with Him. He led them up a high mountain. They were alone. 3 There Jesus changed. His clothes became very white. They were whiter than any person on earth could make them. 4 Elijah and Moses appeared and talked with Jesus.

5 Peter said, "Teacher, it is good for us to be here. Let us make three shelters: one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

6 They were very scared. Peter did not know what to say. 7 Then a cloud came over them. A voice came from the cloud: "This is My Son, whom I love. Listen to Him!"

8 Suddenly they looked around. They saw no one but Jesus.

9 As they came down the mountain, Jesus told them, "Do not tell anyone what you saw until the Son of Man rises from the dead." 10 They kept this to themselves. They asked each other what "rising from the dead" meant.

11 They asked Jesus, "Why do the teachers of the law say Elijah must come first?"

12 Jesus said, "Elijah does come first. He makes all things ready. It is also written that the Son of Man will suffer many things and be treated like nothing. 13 But I tell you, Elijah has already come. People did to him what they wanted, just as it is written about him."

Jesus Heals a Boy with an Evil Spirit

14 When they came to the other disciples, they saw a big crowd around them. Teachers of the law were arguing with them. 15 As soon as the crowd saw Jesus, they were amazed. They ran to greet Him.

16 Jesus asked, "What are you arguing about with them?"

17 A man from the crowd said, "Teacher, I brought my son to You. He has a spirit that makes him unable to speak. 18 Wherever it takes him, it throws him down. He foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and his body gets stiff. I asked Your disciples to drive it out, but they could not."

19 Jesus said, "You people do not believe! How long must I stay with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy to Me."

20 They brought the boy. When the spirit saw Jesus, it shook him. he fell on the ground and rolled around, foaming at the mouth.

21 Jesus asked the father, "How long has this been happening to him?"

22 The father said, "Since he was a little child. It has often thrown him into fire and water to kill him. If You can do anything, have pity on us and help us."

23 Jesus said, "If You can? All things are possible for the one who believes."

24 Right away the father cried out, "I believe! Help me not to doubt!"

25 Jesus saw the crowd running together. He spoke firmly to the evil spirit. "You spirit that makes him unable to hear or speak, I command you to come out of him and never go back in."

26 The spirit screamed and shook him hard. Then it came out. The boy looked like he was dead. Many people said, "He is dead."

27 But Jesus took his hand and helped him up. he stood up.

28 Later, when Jesus went into a house, His disciples asked Him privately, "Why could we not drive it out?"

29 Jesus said, "This kind can come out only by prayer."

Jesus Talks About His Death

30 They left there and traveled through Galilee. Jesus did not want anyone to know. 31 He was teaching His disciples. He said, "The Son of Man will be handed over to men. They will kill Him. After three days He will rise again." 32 But they did not understand what He meant. They were afraid to ask Him.

Who Is the Greatest?

33 They came to Capernaum. When Jesus was in the house, He asked them, "What were you arguing about on the road?" 34 They said nothing. On the road they had argued about who was the greatest.

35 Jesus sat down and called the Twelve. He said, "If anyone wants to be first, he must be last of all and servant of all." 36 He took a little child and stood the child in the middle of them. He hugged the child and said, 37 "Whoever welcomes one of these little children in My name welcomes Me. Whoever welcomes Me welcomes not only Me, but the One who sent Me."

38 John said to Him, "Teacher, we saw a man driving out demons in Your name. He does not follow us. We tried to stop him."

39 Jesus said, "Do not stop him. No one who does a miracle in My name can soon speak badly about Me. 40 Whoever is not against us is for us. 41 Truly I tell you, anyone who gives you a cup of water because you belong to Christ will not lose his reward."

Warnings About Sin

42 Jesus said, "If anyone leads one of these little ones who believe in Me to sin, it would be better for him to have a heavy stone tied around his neck and be thrown into the sea."

43 Jesus said, "If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life with one hand than to go into hell with two hands, into the fire that never goes out."

45 "If your foot causes you to sin, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life lame than to be thrown into hell with two feet."

47 "If your eye causes you to sin, throw it out. It is better for you to enter God's kingdom with one eye than to be thrown into hell with two eyes. 48 There the worms do not die, and the fire is never put out."

Salt and Peace

49 For everyone will be tested by fire.

50 Salt is good. But if salt loses its taste, how can you make it salty again? Keep your salt, and live in peace with each other.
