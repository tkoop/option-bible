The LORD Speaks to Ezekiel

1 The word of the LORD came to me. 2 "Son of man, what makes vine wood better than other wood from branches in the forest?

3 "Can people use its wood to make useful things? Can they take a peg from it to hang things on? 4 Look, it is thrown into the fire for fuel. The fire burns both ends and blackens the middle. Can it be used for anything? 5 Even before it was burned, it could not be used for work. How much less now that the fire has burned and blackened it!

Jerusalem Is Like Useless Vine Wood

6 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: As I have given vine wood among forest trees to be burned, I will do the same to the people living in Jerusalem. 7 I will turn against them. They got out of the fire, but the fire will still burn them up. When I turn against them, you will know that I am the LORD. 8 I will make the land empty because they were not faithful to Me, declares the Lord the LORD."
