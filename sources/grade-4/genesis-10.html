Noah's Sons and Their Families

1 These are the families of Noah's sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Sons were born to them after the flood.

Japheth's Family

2 Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 3 Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 4 Javan's sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. 5 From these men, the people of the nations spread into their lands. Each group had its own language and family.

Ham's Family

6 Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 7 Cush's sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. Raamah's sons were Sheba and Dedan. 8 Cush was the father of Nimrod. Nimrod was the first great hero on earth. 9 He was a mighty hunter before the LORD. That is why people say, "Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the LORD." 10 His kingdom began in Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh. These were in the land of Shinar. 11 From that land he went to Assyria. There he built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, and Calah. 12 He also built Resen. It sits between Nineveh and Calah. Calah is the great city.

13 Mizraim was the father of Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim. 14 He was also the father of Pathrusim, Casluhim, and Caphtorim. The Philistines came from Casluhim.

15 Canaan was the father of Sidon, his first son, and Heth. 16 He was also the father of the Jebusites, the Amorites, and the Girgashites. 17 He was also the father of the Hivites, the Arkites, and the Sinites. 18 He was also the father of the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. Later the Canaanite families spread out. 19 The edge of the Canaanite land went from Sidon toward Gerar, all the way to Gaza. It went toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, all the way to Lasha. 20 These were Ham's sons. They are listed by their families, languages, lands, and nations.

Shem's Family

21 Shem also had sons. He was Japheth's older brother. He was the father of all the sons of Eber. 22 Shem's sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. 23 Aram's sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. 24 Arphaxad was the father of Shelah. Shelah was the father of Eber.

25 Eber had two sons. One was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided. His brother was named Joktan. 26 Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah. 27 He was also the father of Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah. 28 He was also the father of Obal, Abimael, and Sheba. 29 He was also the father of Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were Joktan's sons. 30 They lived in the land from Mesha toward Sephar, in the eastern hills. 31 These were Shem's sons. They are listed by their families, languages, lands, and nations. 32 These are the families of Noah's sons, listed by their family lines and nations. From these, the nations spread out over the earth after the flood.
