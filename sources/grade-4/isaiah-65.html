God Reaches Out to People

1 I showed Myself to people who did not ask for Me. People who did not look for Me found Me. I said to a nation that did not call My name, "Here I am! Here I am!" 2 All day I held out My hands to stubborn people. They walked in bad ways. They followed their own bad plans.

3 These people always made Me angry right in front of Me. They killed animals in gardens. They burned sweet smoke on brick altars. 4 They sat among graves. They stayed in secret places at night. They ate pig meat. Their pots held soup made from bad meat.

5 They said, "Stay away! Do not come near me, because I am too holy for you!" These people are like smoke in My nose. They are like a fire that burns all day. 6 Look, it is written in front of Me. I will not be quiet. I will pay them back. I will pay them back in full.

God Will Punish Sin

7 You and your fathers have sinned together," says the the LORD. "They burned sweet smoke on mountains. They insulted Me on hills. So I will pay them back in full for their old bad deeds.

God Will Save His Servants

8 This is what the the LORD says: "When juice is still found in a bunch of grapes, people say not to destroy it, because there is still some good in it. In the same way, I will help My servants. I will not destroy them all." 9 I will bring children from Jacob. From Judah, I will bring people to take My mountains. My chosen people will take them. My servants will live there. 10 Sharon will become a place for sheep. The Valley of Achor will be a place for cows to rest. This is for My people who looked for Me.

People Who Turn Away from God

11 But you who leave the the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who set a table for the god called Fortune and fill cups with wine for the god called Destiny, 12 I will let the sword get you. All of you will bow down to be killed. I called, but you did not answer. I spoke, but you did not listen. You did bad things in My eyes. You chose what I did not like.

God's Servants Will Be Happy

13 So the Lord the LORD says: "My servants will eat, but you will be hungry. My servants will drink, but you will be thirsty. My servants will be happy, but you will be ashamed." 14 My servants will sing for joy from a glad heart. But you will cry from a sad heart. You will wail with a broken spirit. 15 You will leave your name behind as a curse for My chosen people. The Lord the LORD will put you to death. But He will give His servants a new name.

16 Anyone who asks for a blessing in the land will bless by the God of truth. Anyone who makes a promise in the land will promise by the God of truth. This is because the old troubles will be forgotten. They will be hidden from My eyes.

God Will Make a New World

17 Look, I will make new heavens and a new earth. The old things will not be remembered. They will not come to mind. 18 But be glad and rejoice forever in what I make. I will make Jerusalem a joy. Its people will be a delight. 19 I will be glad over Jerusalem. I will rejoice over My people. No one will hear crying or loud sobbing there anymore.

Long Life and Peace

20 No baby will live only a few days. No old person will die before a full life. A young person who dies will be one hundred years old. A bad person who lives to one hundred will be cursed.

21 They will build houses and live in them. They will plant grape gardens and eat their fruit. 22 They will not build for others to live in. They will not plant for others to eat. My people will live as long as a tree. My chosen people will fully enjoy the work of their hands.

23 They will not work for nothing. They will not have children doomed to trouble. They will be people blessed by the the LORD. Their children will be with them. 24 Before they call, I will answer. While they are still talking, I will hear.

25 "The wolf and the lamb will eat together. The lion will eat straw like the cow. The snake will eat dust. They will not hurt or destroy on all My holy mountain," says the the LORD.
