Give Thanks to God

1 Give thanks to the LORD. Call on His name. Tell people in other lands what He has done. 2 Sing to Him. Sing His praise. Tell about all His wonderful works. 3 Praise His holy name. Let those who look for the LORD be glad. 4 Look for the LORD and His strength. Look for His face always. 5 Remember the wonderful things He did. Remember His miracles and the words He spoke.

God's Promise to Abraham

6 You are the children of Abraham, God's servant. You are the sons of Jacob, His chosen people. 7 He is the LORD our God. He rules all the earth. 8 He remembers His promise forever. He gave His word for a thousand families. 9 He made a promise to Abraham. He made a promise to Isaac. 10 He made it strong for Jacob. He made it last for Israel forever. 11 He said, "I will give you the land of Canaan. It will be your own land."

12 They were few in number. They were strangers in that land. 13 They traveled from nation to nation. They went from one kingdom to another. 14 He did not let anyone hurt them. He told kings to leave them alone. 15 He said, "Do not touch My chosen ones. Do not hurt My prophets."

Joseph in Egypt

16 He called for hunger in the land. He took away their food. 17 He sent a man ahead of them. It was Joseph, sold as a slave. 18 They hurt his feet with chains. They put iron chains on him. 19 He stayed there until the right time. The LORD's word tested him. 20 The king sent for him and set him free. The ruler of the people let him go. 21 He made Joseph the boss of his house. He put him over all he owned. 22 Joseph could teach the leaders what to do. He could teach the old men wisdom.

God Brings His People Out of Egypt

23 Then Israel came to Egypt. Jacob lived in Egypt. 24 The LORD made His people grow in number. He made them stronger than their enemies. 25 Their enemies' hearts turned to hate His people. They made secret plans against His servants. 26 He sent Moses, His servant. He sent Aaron, whom He chose. 27 They did His miracles among them. They did wonders in Egypt.

28 He sent darkness, and it got dark. They had not obeyed His words. 29 He turned their water into blood. He killed their fish. 30 Frogs filled their land. They were even in the kings' rooms. 31 He spoke, and flies came. Tiny bugs came all over their land. 32 He gave them hail instead of rain. Lightning flashed through their land. 33 He hit their grape plants and fig trees. He broke the trees in their land. 34 He spoke, and locusts came. Young locusts came without number. 35 They ate every plant in the land. They ate the food from the soil. 36 He hit the oldest child in every home. He took the first and best of their strength.

37 He brought Israel out with silver and gold. None of His people fell. 38 Egypt was glad when they left. The people of Israel had scared them.

God Takes Care of His People

39 He spread a cloud to cover them. He gave fire to light the night. 40 They asked, and He brought quail. He fed them with bread from heaven. 41 He opened a rock, and water poured out. It flowed like a river in the dry land. 42 He remembered His holy promise. He remembered His promise to Abraham, His servant. 43 He brought out His people with joy. He brought out His chosen ones with songs. 44 He gave them the lands of other nations. They took what other people had worked for. 45 He did this so they would keep His rules and obey His laws. Praise the LORD!
