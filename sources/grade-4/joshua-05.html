The Enemy Kings Are Afraid

1 All the Amorite kings west of the Jordan heard the news. All the Canaanite kings by the sea heard it too. They heard that the LORD had dried up the Jordan for the Israelites. The Israelites crossed over. The kings were very afraid. They lost all courage because of the Israelites.

The Men Are Circumcised

2 At that time, the LORD said to Joshua, "Make knives from sharp stone. Circumcise the sons of Israel again." 3 So Joshua made knives from sharp stone. He circumcised the sons of Israel at Gibeath-haaraloth.

4 This is why Joshua circumcised them. All the men who came out of Egypt were soldiers. They died on the way in the desert after they left Egypt. 5 All the people who came out had been circumcised. But the people born in the desert on the trip from Egypt had not been circumcised. 6 The people of Israel walked in the desert for forty years. All the soldiers who came out of Egypt died. They did not obey the LORD. The LORD had sworn that He would not let them see the land. He had promised their fathers He would give us this land. It is a rich land with plenty of good food. 7 So He raised up their sons in their place. Joshua circumcised them. They had not been circumcised on the way. 8 After all the men were circumcised, they stayed in the camp until they were healed.

God Takes Away Their Shame

9 Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Today I have taken away the shame of Egypt from you." So that place was called Gilgal. It is still called that today.

The People Keep the Passover

10 On the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, the Israelites camped at Gilgal. They were on the plains of Jericho. They kept the Passover. 11 The day after the Passover, on that very day, they ate bread without yeast. They also ate roasted grain from the food of the land. 12 The day after they ate from the food of the land, the manna stopped. There was no more manna for the Israelites. That year they ate from the harvest of the land of Canaan.

Joshua Meets God's Commander

13 When Joshua was near Jericho, he looked up. He saw a man standing in front of him. The man had a sword in his hand. It was pulled out. Joshua went to him. He asked, "Are you for us or for our enemies?"

14 "Neither," he replied. "I have now come as commander of the the LORD's army."

Then Joshua fell facedown on the ground. He worshiped. He asked, "What does my Lord have to say to His servant?"

15 The commander of the the LORD's army replied, "Take off your sandals. The place where you are standing is holy." And Joshua did so.
