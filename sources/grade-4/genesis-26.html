A Time of Hunger

1 There was a famine in the land. It was not the first one. There had been a famine when Abraham lived. Isaac went to Abimelech. Abimelech was king of the Philistines in Gerar.

2 The LORD appeared to Isaac. He said, "Do not go down to Egypt. Live in the land I tell you about. 3 Stay in this land. I will be with you and bless you. I will give all these lands to you and your children and grandchildren. I will keep the promise I made to your father Abraham. 4 I will make your children and grandchildren as many as the stars in the sky. I will give all these lands to them. All nations on earth will be blessed through them. 5 I will do this because Abraham obeyed Me. He kept My rules and My laws."

6 So Isaac stayed in Gerar.

Isaac and Rebekah in Gerar

7 The men there asked about his wife. Isaac said, "She is my sister." He was afraid to say, "She is my wife." He thought, "The men here might kill me because of Rebekah." She was very pretty.

8 Isaac stayed there a long time. Abimelech, king of the Philistines, looked out a window. He saw Isaac holding his wife Rebekah close.

9 Abimelech called Isaac. He said, "She is your wife! Why did you say, 'She is my sister'?" Isaac said, "I thought I might die because of her."

10 Abimelech said, "What have you done to us? One of our men might have slept with your wife. Then we would be guilty." 11 Abimelech told all the people, "Anyone who hurts this man or his wife must be put to death."

12 Isaac planted crops in that land. That same year he got one hundred times as much. the LORD blessed him.

13 Isaac became rich. He got richer and richer until he was very rich.

14 He had many sheep, cows, and workers. The Philistines were jealous of him.

15 The Philistines had filled with dirt all the wells that Abraham's workers had dug.

16 Abimelech said to Isaac, "Go away from us. You are much stronger than we are."

17 So Isaac left. He camped in the Gerar Valley. He settled there.

Digging Wells Again

18 Isaac dug again the wells from Abraham's time. The Philistines had filled them after Abraham died. Isaac gave them the same names Abraham had given them.

19 Isaac's workers dug in the valley. They found a well with fresh water.

20 The men of Gerar argued with Isaac's men. They said, "This water is ours." So Isaac named the well Esek because they argued with him.

21 They dug another well. They argued over that one too. Isaac named it Sitnah.

22 Isaac moved from there. He dug another well. No one argued over it. He named it Rehoboth. He said, "Now the LORD has made room for us. We will do well in this land."

23 Isaac went up to Beersheba from there.

God Promises to Be With Isaac

24 The LORD appeared to Isaac that night. He said, "I am the God of your father Abraham. Do not be afraid. I am with you. I will bless you. I will make your children and grandchildren many. I will do this because of Abraham, My servant."

25 Isaac built an altar there. He called on the name of the LORD. He put up his tent there. Isaac's workers dug a well there.

Isaac and Abimelech Make Peace

26 Abimelech came to Isaac from Gerar. Ahuzzath, his friend, came too. Phicol, the army leader, came too.

27 Isaac said to them, "Why did you come to me? You hated me. You sent me away from you."

28 They said, "We see that the LORD has been with you. So we said, 'Let us make a promise between us and you.' 29 You will not hurt us. We did not touch you, and we only did good to you. We sent you away in peace. Now the LORD has blessed you."

30 Isaac made a feast for them. They ate and drank.

31 They got up early in the morning. They made a promise to each other. Isaac sent them away. They left in peace.

32 That same day, Isaac's workers came to him. They told him about the well they had dug. They said, "We found water." 33 Isaac named the well Shibah. That is why the city is called Beersheba to this day.

Esau's Wives

34 Esau was forty years old. He married Judith. She was the daughter of Beeri the Hittite. He also married Basemath. She was the daughter of Elon the Hittite.

35 These wives made life hard for Isaac and Rebekah.
