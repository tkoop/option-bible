God Gives Ezekiel a Message

1 The LORD gave me a message.

God Tells Ezekiel to Speak Against Gog

2 "Man, turn your face toward Gog. He is from the land of Magog. He is the top leader of Meshech and Tubal. Speak against him. 3 Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: I am against you, Gog. You are the top leader of Meshech and Tubal. 4 I will turn you around. I will put hooks in your jaws. I will bring you out with all your army. They will ride horses. They will wear full armor. They will carry shields and swords. 5 Persia, Cush, and Put will be with them. They will carry shields and wear helmets. 6 Gomer and all his troops will come. Beth Togarmah will come from the far north with all his troops. Many nations will be with you. 7 Get ready. Stay ready. You and all your troops must be ready. Be a guard for them. 8 After many days, you will be gathered. In the last years, you will go to a land that is safe again after war. Its people came from many nations. They live on the mountains of Israel. That land was empty for a long time. Now those people live there in safety. 9 You will come like a storm. You will cover the land like a cloud. Your troops and many nations will come with you.'"

God Says Gog Will Plan an Attack

10 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: On that day, you will think bad thoughts. You will plan something evil. 11 You will say, 'I will attack a land of open towns. I will attack quiet people who live in safety. They have no walls or gates.' 12 You will want to take their stuff and steal their things. You will attack places that were empty but now have people. Those people came from many nations. They have animals and goods. They live in the middle of the world.

13 "Sheba, Dedan, and the traders from Tarshish, and all their leaders, will say to you, 'Did you come to steal? Did you bring your troops to take things? Are you taking silver and gold, animals and goods? Are you taking a big prize?'"

God Speaks to Gog Again

14 "So, man, speak My words. Tell Gog, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: On that day, My people Israel will live in safety. Won't you know it? 15 You will come from your home in the far north. Many nations will come with you. They will ride horses. It will be a big, strong army. 16 You will come against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. In the last days, I will bring you to My land. Then the nations will know Me. Through you, Gog, I will show them that I am holy.'"

God Says He Will Judge Gog

17 "This is what the Lord the LORD says: Are you the one I told about long ago? My servants, the prophets of Israel, said for many years that I would bring you against them. 18 But on that day, when Gog comes against Israel, the Lord the LORD says My anger will rise. 19 Because I am jealous and very angry, I say there will be a big earthquake in Israel. 20 Fish, birds, wild animals, and all small animals on the ground will shake because of Me. All people on earth will shake too. Mountains will fall. High rocks will fall. Every wall will fall to the ground. 21 I will bring war against Gog on all My mountains, says the Lord the LORD. Each man will fight his brother with a sword. 22 I will judge him with sickness and bloodshed. I will rain on him, his troops, and the nations with him. I will send heavy rain, hail, fire, and burning rock. 23 So I will show how great and holy I am. Many nations will know Me. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
