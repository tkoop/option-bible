God's Message About Tyre

1 This is a sad message about Tyre. Cry out, ships of Tarshish! Tyre is destroyed. Its houses are broken, and no one can go in. People from Kittim heard the news. 2 Be quiet, people who live by the sea. Merchants from Sidon crossed the sea and filled your land. 3 By many waters came grain from Shihor and crops from the Nile. Tyre became a market for the nations.

4 Be ashamed, Sidon, you strong place by the sea. The sea says, "I have not had labor pains. I have not had a baby. I have not raised young men or brought up young women." 5 When Egypt hears this news, they will hurt, just as Tyre did.

6 Go across to Tarshish. Cry out, people who live by the sea. 7 Is this the city you used to cheer for? It is very old. Its people traveled far to live there.

God Plans Tyre's Fall

8 Who planned this against Tyre? Tyre was a city that wore a crown. Its sellers were like rulers. Its traders were honored on earth. 9 The LORD of Hosts planned it. He will bring down all proud beauty. He will put all honored people to shame.

10 Daughter of Tarshish, cross your land like the Nile. There is no more harbor. 11 He stretched out His hand over the sea. He shook the kingdoms. The LORD gave a command about Canaan: destroy its strong places.

12 He said, "You will not be happy anymore, crushed young woman of Sidon. Get up and go to Kittim. Even there you will find no rest."

Tyre and the Chaldeans

13 Look at the land of the Chaldeans. They are now a weak people. Assyria made it a home for wild animals. They built war towers. They tore down its palaces. They made it a ruin. 14 Cry out, ships of Tarshish, for your strong place is destroyed.

Tyre Will Be Forgotten

15 In that day, Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years. It will be like one king's whole life. After seventy years, Tyre will be like a song about a woman who sells herself. 16 Take a harp. Walk around the city, like a forgotten woman. Play well and sing many songs, so you will be remembered.

Tyre Will Return

17 After seventy years, the LORD will visit Tyre. She will go back to her pay. She will sell herself to all the kingdoms of the earth. 18 Her trade and her pay will be set apart for the LORD. It will not be saved or stored. Her trade will be for the people who live with the LORD. It will give them food to eat and good clothes to wear.
