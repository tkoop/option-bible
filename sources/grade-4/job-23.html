Job Wants to Talk to God

1 Then Job answered and said, 2 "Even today my complaint is bitter. My hand feels heavy with my groaning. 3 Oh, if I only knew where to find Him! If I could come to His place! 4 I would lay my case before Him. I would fill my mouth with my reasons. 5 I would know what words He would answer me. I would understand what He would say to me.

Job Says God Would Listen

6 "Would He argue with me with His great power? No, He would listen to me. 7 There a good person could talk with Him. I would be saved forever from my Judge.

Job Cannot Find God

8 "Look, I go forward, but He is not there. I go back, but I cannot see Him. 9 He works on the left, but I do not find Him. He turns to the right, but I do not see Him.

Job Has Followed God

10 "But He knows the way I take. When He has tested me, I will come out like gold. 11 My foot has followed His steps. I have kept His way and have not turned away. 12 I have not left the words He told me to keep. I have kept His words like treasure, more than my daily bread.

God Does What He Wants

13 "But He never changes. Who can make Him change? He does what He wants. 14 He will finish what He has planned for me. He has many plans like these.

Job Is Afraid of God

15 "So I am afraid of Him. When I think about Him, I am scared. 16 God has made my heart weak. The Almighty has scared me. 17 But the dark has not silenced me. The thick dark has not covered my face."
