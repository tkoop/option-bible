Deborah and Barak Sing a Song

1 On that day, Deborah and Barak, son of Abinoam, sang this song.

2 “The leaders in Israel led the way. The people gave themselves freely. Praise the the LORD!” 3 “Hear this, kings! Listen, rulers! I will sing to the the LORD. I will sing. I will make music to the the LORD, the God of Israel.”

God Fought for Israel

4 “The LORD, when You went out from Seir, when You marched from Edom, the earth shook. The sky poured. The clouds poured water. 5 The mountains shook before the the LORD. Even Mount Sinai shook before the the LORD, the God of Israel.”

Hard Times in Israel

6 “In the days of Shamgar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the roads were empty. Travelers took crooked paths. 7 Life in the villages stopped. It stopped until I, Deborah, stood up. I stood up to care for Israel like a mother. 8 They chose new gods. Then war came to the city gates. Not one shield or spear was seen among forty thousand in Israel. 9 My heart is with the leaders of Israel. It is with the people who gave themselves freely. Praise the the LORD!”

People Come to Fight

10 “Those who ride white donkeys, sitting on saddle blankets, and those who walk on the road, listen. 11 Listen to the singers by the wells. They sing about the the LORD’s good deeds. They sing about His good deeds for His fighters in Israel. Then the the LORD’s people went down to the city gates.”

Wake Up, Deborah and Barak

12 “Wake up, wake up, Deborah! Wake up, wake up, sing a song! Get up, Barak! Son of Abinoam, take your prisoners! 13 Then the rest of the people came down to the leaders. The the LORD’s people came down to me to fight the strong enemy.”

Who Came to Help

14 “Some came from Ephraim, from Amalek’s land. Benjamin came with his people after you. Leaders came down from Makir. From Zebulun came men who carry the leader’s staff. 15 The leaders of Issachar were with Deborah. Issachar was with Barak. They were sent into the valley under his lead. The men of Reuben thought hard about what to do.”

16 “Why did you stay among the sheep pens? Why did you listen to the whistles for the flocks? The men of Reuben thought hard about what to do. 17 Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan. Dan, why did you stay by the ships? Asher stayed by the sea. He stayed in his bays. 18 The people of Zebulun risked their lives. Naphtali did too on the high places of the field.”

The Battle

19 “Kings came and fought. The kings of Canaan fought at Taanach by the waters of Megiddo. But they took no silver. 20 The stars in the sky fought. From their paths they fought against Sisera. 21 The Kishon River swept them away. The old river, the Kishon River. March on, my soul, with strength! 22 Then the horses’ hooves pounded. They pounded from the running of his strong horses.”

23 “Curse Meroz,” said the angel of the the LORD. “Curse its people hard. They did not come to help the the LORD. They did not help the the LORD against the strong enemy.”

Jael Is Blessed

24 “Jael is most blessed among women. She is the wife of Heber the Kenite. She is most blessed of women in the tent. 25 He asked for water. She gave him milk. She brought him butter in a bowl fit for leaders. 26 Her hand reached for the tent peg. Her right hand reached for the worker’s hammer. She hit Sisera. She crushed his head. She broke and pierced his temple. 27 Between her feet he bent down. He fell. He lay still. Between her feet he bent down. He fell. Where he bent down, there he fell, dead.”

Sisera’s Mother Waits

28 Sisera’s mother looked through the window. She cried from the lattice. “Why is his chariot so late? Why do his chariot wheels wait?”

29 Her wise ladies answered her. She kept saying to herself, 30 “They must be finding and sharing the things they took. A girl or two for each man. Pretty clothes for Sisera. Pretty clothes with fancy work. Fancy clothes for the winner’s neck.”

Peace for the Land

31 “So may all your enemies die, the the LORD! But may those who love You be like the sun when it rises in its strength.” Then the land had peace for forty years.
