God Warns Israel's Leaders

1 Hear this, priests! Pay attention, people of Israel! Listen, king! For judgment is on you, because you have been a trap at Mizpah and a net spread on Tabor. 2 The rebels have gone deep into killing, but I will punish them all.

Israel Turns Away from God

3 I know Ephraim. Israel is not hidden from Me. Now, Ephraim, you have been unfaithful. Israel is made unclean. 4 Their bad actions keep them from coming back to their God. A spirit of unfaithfulness is in them, and they do not know the LORD.

5 Israel's pride shows they are guilty. Israel and Ephraim trip because of their sin. Even Judah trips with them. 6 They will go with their sheep and cows to look for the LORD, but they will not find Him. He has left them.

A Warning of War

7 They have not been true to the LORD. They have had children who are not their own. Now one month will destroy them and their land. 8 Blow the horn in Gibeah! Blow the trumpet in Ramah! Shout the war cry in Beth-aven! "Look behind you, Benjamin!"

Judah and Ephraim Will Be Punished

9 Ephraim will be ruined on the day I correct them. Among Israel's tribes, I tell what is sure to happen. 10 Judah's leaders are like people who move boundary stones. I will pour out My anger on them like water. 11 Ephraim is crushed and treated unfairly, because he chose to follow worthless idols.

12 So I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like rot to Judah. 13 When Ephraim saw he was sick and Judah saw his wound, Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to the great king. But he cannot heal you or cure your wound.

God Will Go Away and Come Back

14 I am like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to Judah. I will tear them apart and go away. I will carry them off, and no one will save them. 15 Then I will go back to My place until they admit they are guilty and seek Me. In their trouble they will look for Me.
