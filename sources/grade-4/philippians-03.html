Be Glad in the Lord

1 Finally, my brothers and sisters, be glad in the Lord. It is not hard for me to write the same things to you again. It keeps you safe. 2 Watch out for those bad people. They are like dogs. They do evil work. They cut the body in a wrong way. 3 We are the true people who worship God by His Spirit. We are proud of Christ Jesus. We do not trust in our own bodies.

Paul's Old Life

4 I could trust in my body too. If anyone thinks they can trust in their body, I can do it even more. 5 I was circumcised when I was eight days old. I am from Israel. I am from the tribe of Benjamin. I am a Hebrew, born from Hebrews. I was a Pharisee, a strict follower of the law. 6 I wanted so much to serve God that I hurt the church. I kept the law so well that no one could blame me.

Paul Gives Up All for Christ

7 But I used to think those things helped me. Now I see they hurt me, because of Christ. 8 Yes, I think everything is a loss. It is better to know Christ Jesus my Lord. Because of Him, I lost everything. I think of it like trash. I want to gain Christ. 9 I want to be found in Him. I do not want my own right way from the law. I want the right way that comes from God through faith in Christ.

Knowing Christ

10 I want to know Him. I want to know the power that raised Him from the dead. I want to share in His suffering. I want to be like Him in His death. 11 Then maybe I can rise from the dead too.

Press On to the Goal

12 I have not gotten all of this yet. I am not perfect yet. But I keep going. I want to take hold of it, because Christ Jesus took hold of me. 13 Brothers and sisters, I do not think I have it all yet. But I do one thing. I forget what is behind me. I reach out to what is ahead. 14 I run toward the goal. The prize is God calling me up to heaven in Christ Jesus.

Live by What We Know

15 All of us who are grown up in faith should think this way. If you think something else, God will show you the truth. 16 But we must keep living by the truth we already have.

Follow Good Examples

17 Brothers and sisters, follow my example. Keep your eyes on those who live like we do. 18 I told you many times, and now I say it with tears: many people live as enemies of the cross of Christ. 19 Their end is ruin. Their stomach is their god. They are proud of things they should be ashamed of. They think only about things on earth.

We Belong to Heaven

20 But we belong to heaven. We wait for a Savior from there. He is the Lord Jesus Christ. 21 He will change our weak bodies. They will be like His glorious body. He can do this by His power. He can put everything under His control.
