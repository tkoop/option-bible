God Speaks to Ezekiel

1 The word of the the LORD came to me.

God Warns the Ammonites

2 “Son of man, turn your face toward the Ammonites and speak against them. 3 Say to the Ammonites, ‘Hear the word of the Lord, the the LORD. This is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because you said, “Aha!” when My holy place was made unclean, when the land of Israel was empty, and when the people of Judah were taken away, 4 I will give you to the people of the East. They will camp among you and make homes there. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk. 5 I will make Rabbah a place for camels, and the Ammonites a place for sheep to rest. Then you will know that I am the the LORD. 6 For this is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your feet and were glad with all your heart to see Israel hurt, 7 I have reached out My hand against you. I will give you to other nations as things they take in war. I will cut you off from the peoples, and you will die in other lands. I will destroy you. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.’”

God Warns Moab and Seir

8 “This is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because Moab and Seir said, ‘Look, the family of Judah is like all the other nations,’ 9 I will open Moab to attack, starting from its border towns. I will take away its best land, including Beth Jeshimoth, Baal Meon, and Kiriathaim. 10 I will give that land to the Ammonites, and the Ammonites will not be remembered among the nations. 11 I will punish Moab. Then they will know that I am the the LORD.”

God Warns Edom

12 “This is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because Edom took revenge on the family of Judah and did wrong by paying them back, 13 I will reach out My hand against Edom. I will cut off people and animals from it. I will make it empty from Teman, and Dedan will fall by the sword. 14 I will let My people Israel punish Edom. They will deal with Edom with My anger and My fierce anger. Then they will know My punishment, says the Lord, the the LORD.”

God Warns the Philistines

15 “This is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because the Philistines took revenge with hate in their hearts and tried to destroy Israel forever, 16 I will reach out My hand against the Philistines. I will cut off the Cherethites and destroy those left on the coast. 17 I will punish them greatly in My anger and correct them. Then they will know that I am the the LORD when I punish them.”
