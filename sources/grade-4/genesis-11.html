The Tower of Babel

1 Everyone on earth spoke the same language and used the same words. 2 As people traveled east, they found a flat valley in Shinar and settled there. 3 They said to one another, "Come, let's make bricks and bake them well." They used bricks instead of stones, and tar for mortar. 4 Then they said, "Come, let's build a city and a tower with its top in the sky. Let's become famous, so we will not be scattered over the whole earth."

5 The LORD came down to see the city and the tower that people had built. 6 the LORD said, "Look, they are one people, and they all speak one language. This is only the start of what they will do. Soon nothing they plan will be too hard for them. 7 Come, let's go down and mix up their language. Then they will not understand one another." 8 So the LORD scattered them from there over the whole earth, and they stopped building the city. 9 That is why the city was called Babel. There the LORD mixed up the language of the whole earth, and from there the LORD scattered the people over the whole earth.

Shem's Family Line

10 This is the family line of Shem. Two years after the flood, when Shem was 100 years old, he had a son named Arphaxad. 11 After Arphaxad was born, Shem lived 500 more years and had other sons and daughters.

12 When Arphaxad was 35 years old, he had a son named Shelah. 13 After Shelah was born, Arphaxad lived 403 more years and had other sons and daughters.

14 When Shelah was 30 years old, he had a son named Eber. 15 After Eber was born, Shelah lived 403 more years and had other sons and daughters.

16 When Eber was 34 years old, he had a son named Peleg. 17 After Peleg was born, Eber lived 430 more years and had other sons and daughters.

18 When Peleg was 30 years old, he had a son named Reu. 19 After Reu was born, Peleg lived 209 more years and had other sons and daughters.

20 When Reu was 32 years old, he had a son named Serug. 21 After Serug was born, Reu lived 207 more years and had other sons and daughters.

22 When Serug was 30 years old, he had a son named Nahor. 23 After Nahor was born, Serug lived 200 more years and had other sons and daughters.

24 When Nahor was 29 years old, he had a son named Terah. 25 After Terah was born, Nahor lived 119 more years and had other sons and daughters.

26 When Terah was 70 years old, he had sons named Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

Terah's Family

27 This is the family line of Terah. Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran was the father of Lot. 28 Haran died while his father Terah was still alive, in the land where he was born, Ur of the Chaldeans.

29 Abram and Nahor both took wives. Abram's wife was Sarai. Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran. Haran was the father of both Milcah and Iscah. 30 But Sarai could not have children.

31 Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot (the son of Haran), and his daughter-in-law Sarai (the wife of Abram). They left Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. They came to Haran and stayed there. 32 Terah lived 205 years and died in Haran.
