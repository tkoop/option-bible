A Song Asking God to Bless Us

1 For the music leader. With stringed instruments. A psalm. A song. May God be kind to us and bless us. May His face shine on us. Selah. 2 Then people everywhere will know Your way. They will know how You save people among all nations.

All Nations Praise God

3 May all people praise You, O God. May all people praise You. 4 Let the nations be glad and sing for joy. You judge people fairly. You lead the nations on earth. Selah.

5 May all people praise You, O God. May all people praise You.

God Gives a Good Harvest

6 The earth has given its crops. God, our God, will bless us. 7 God will bless us. May people all over the world respect Him.
