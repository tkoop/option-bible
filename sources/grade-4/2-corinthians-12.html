Paul Tells About Visions From the Lord

1 I must keep bragging, even though it does not help. I will tell about visions and things the Lord showed me. 2 I know a man who belongs to Christ. Fourteen years ago, he was taken up to the third heaven. I do not know if he was in his body or not. God knows. 3 I know that this man was taken up. I do not know if it was in his body or not. God knows. 4 He was taken to Paradise. He heard things that no one can say. People are not allowed to talk about them.

5 I will brag about that man. But I will not brag about myself, except about my weak times. 6 If I wanted to brag, I would not be foolish, because I would tell the truth. But I hold back, so no one thinks I am more than what they see in me or hear from me.

A Thorn in Paul's Body

7 I saw very special things. So I was given a thorn in my body. It is like a messenger from Satan. It bothers me so I will not get proud. 8 Three times I asked the Lord to take it away. 9 But He said to me, "My grace is enough for you. My power works best when you are weak." So I will brag even more about my weak times. Then Christ's power can rest on me. 10 For Christ's sake, I am okay with weak times, insults, hard times, people chasing me, and trouble. When I am weak, then I am strong.

Paul and the Church

11 I have been a foolish man! You made me do it. You should have said good things about me. I was not less than those who say they are great apostles, even though I am nothing. 12 The signs of a true apostle were done among you. There were signs and wonders and strong works. I did them with great patience. 13 What did you have less than the other churches? Only this: I did not cost you money. Forgive me for that wrong.

14 Look, I am ready to come to you a third time. I will not be a trouble to you. I do not want what you have. I want you. Children do not save up for their parents. Parents save up for their children. 15 I will gladly spend my money and myself for your souls. If I love you more, will you love me less?

16 But even so, I did not cost you money. You say, "I was tricky, so I caught you by a trick." 17 Did I take advantage of you through anyone I sent to you? 18 I asked Titus to go, and I sent our brother with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit? Did we not follow the same steps?

Paul's Worries

19 Have you thought all this time that we are defending ourselves to you? We speak in Christ, in front of God, and all of it is to help you grow. 20 I am afraid that when I come, I may not find you as I want. You may not find me as you want. I am afraid there may be fighting, jealousy, anger, selfish groups, mean talk, gossip, pride, and mess.

21 I am afraid that when I come again, my God may humble me in front of you. I may have to be sad for many who sinned before and did not say sorry. They did not turn away from their dirty sins, sexual sins, and wild living.
