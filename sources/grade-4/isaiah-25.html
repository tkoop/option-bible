Isaiah Praises God

1 O the LORD, You are my God. I will lift You up. I will praise Your name. You have done amazing things. You planned them long ago. They are true and sure. 2 You turned the city into a pile of rocks. You made the strong city a ruin. The palace of strangers is gone. It will never be built again. 3 So strong people will honor You. Cities of cruel nations will fear You.

4 You have been a safe place for poor people. You have been a safe place for needy people in trouble. You are like a shelter from the storm. You are like shade from the hot sun. The breath of cruel people is like a storm hitting a wall. 5 You quiet the noise of strangers like heat in a dry place. You stop the song of cruel people like heat under a cloud.

God Will Make a Big Feast

6 On this mountain, the LORD of Hosts will make a big feast for all people. It will have rich food and fine wine. The food will be full of good meat. The wine will be old and clear. 7 On this mountain, He will take away the cover over all people. He will remove the blanket over all nations. 8 He will end death forever. The Lord, the LORD, will wipe away tears from every face. He will take away the shame of His people from all the earth. The LORD has spoken.

People Will Praise God

9 On that day people will say, “Look! This is our God. We waited for Him, and He saved us. This is the LORD. We waited for Him. Let us be glad and happy because He saved us.”

God Will Judge Moab

10 The hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain. Moab will be crushed like straw in a trash pile. 11 Moab will stretch out its arms in the middle of it, like a swimmer stretches out his arms to swim. But the LORD will bring down its pride and stop what its hands can do. 12 He will bring down your high walls and strong towers. He will knock them to the ground, down to the dust.
