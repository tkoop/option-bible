Moses Sees the Land

1 Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo. He stood on top of Pisgah, across from Jericho. 2 The LORD showed him the whole land. He saw from Gilead to Dan. He saw all of Naphtali. He saw the land of Ephraim and Manasseh. He saw all of Judah to the sea in the west. 3 He saw the south land and the valley near Jericho, the city of palm trees. He saw as far as Zoar.

4 The LORD said to him, "This is the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I said I would give it to your family. I have let you see it with your eyes. But you will not cross over into it."

Moses Dies

5 Moses, the servant of the LORD, died there in Moab. This was just as the LORD had said. 6 The LORD buried him in a valley in Moab, across from Beth-peor. No one knows where his grave is, even now. 7 Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eyes were still good. He was still strong.

The People Are Sad

8 The Israelites were very sad for Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days. Then their time of sadness was over.

Joshua Leads the People

9 Joshua, the son of Nun, was full of wisdom. Moses had put his hands on him. So the Israelites listened to him. They did what the LORD had told Moses.

Moses Was a Great Prophet

10 No prophet like Moses has come in Israel since then. The LORD knew him in a very close way. 11 Moses did all the signs and wonders the LORD sent him to do in Egypt. He did them for Pharaoh, for all his helpers, and for all his country. 12 He did all the great and powerful things that all Israel saw.
