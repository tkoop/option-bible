David Wins Many Battles

1 After this, David attacked the Philistines. He beat them and took Gath and its nearby towns from them. 2 David beat Moab. The Moabites became David's servants and paid him gifts.

3 David beat King Hadadezer of Zobah near Hamath. Hadadezer was on his way to set up his power at the Euphrates River. 4 David captured 1,000 chariots, 7,000 horsemen, and 20,000 foot soldiers. He hurt all the horses that pulled the chariots so they could not run, but he kept 100.

5 When the Arameans from Damascus came to help Hadadezer, David killed 22,000 of them. 6 David put soldiers in Damascus in Aram. The Arameans became his servants and paid him gifts. The LORD helped David win everywhere he went.

7 David took the gold shields from Hadadezer's servants and brought them to Jerusalem. 8 David took a lot of bronze from Tibhath and Cun, cities of Hadadezer. Solomon later used it to make the bronze sea, the pillars, and other bronze things.

9 King Tou of Hamath heard that David had beaten all the army of King Hadadezer of Zobah. 10 King Tou sent his son Hadoram to King David. He asked David for peace and blessed him, because David had fought and beaten Hadadezer. Hadadezer had often fought Tou. Hadoram brought all kinds of gold, silver, and bronze things.

11 King David gave these things to the LORD. He also gave silver and gold he had taken from Edom, Moab, Ammon, Philistia, and Amalek.

12 Abishai son of Zeruiah killed 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. 13 David put soldiers in Edom. All of Edom became his servants. The LORD helped David win everywhere he went.

David's Rule and Helpers

14 David ruled over all Israel. He was fair and did right for all his people.

15 Joab son of Zeruiah led the army. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the record keeper. 16 Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests. Shavsha was the writer. 17 Benaiah son of Jehoiada led the Cherethites and Pelethites. David's sons were his top helpers.
