A Peaceful Home Is Better

1 It is better to eat dry bread in peace than to have a house full of big meals and fighting. 2 A wise servant will rule over a son who brings shame. He will share the family property with the brothers.

The LORD Tests Our Hearts

3 People use a pot to melt silver and gold. In the same way, the LORD tests what is in our hearts. 4 A bad person listens to evil words. A liar pays attention to hurtful talk.

Do Not Mock Poor People

5 Whoever makes fun of a poor person insults his Maker. Whoever is glad when trouble comes will not go free. 6 Grandchildren are like a crown for old people. A son makes his father proud.

Words Should Fit the Person

7 Fancy talk does not fit a fool. How much worse are lying words for a good leader! 8 A bribe seems like a lucky charm to the one who gives it. He thinks it helps him everywhere he goes.

Forgive and Stay Friends

9 A person who forgives a wrong shows love. But a person who keeps talking about it breaks friends apart. 10 A wise person learns more from a strong warning than a fool learns from a hundred hits. 11 A bad person wants only to fight. A harsh messenger will be sent to him.

Stay Away from Fools

12 It is safer to meet a bear whose babies were taken than a foolish person doing foolish things. 13 If someone pays back good with evil, trouble will stay in his home.

Stop Fights Early

14 Starting a fight is like breaking open a dam. Stop the argument before it gets worse. 15 It is very wrong to the LORD to let a guilty person go free or to blame an innocent person.

Money and Wisdom

16 Why should a fool have money if he does not want to buy wisdom? 17 A friend loves at all times. A brother is there to help in trouble. 18 A person without good sense shakes hands to make a promise. He agrees to pay someone else's debt.

Loving Wrong Brings Trouble

19 A person who loves to do wrong loves to fight. A person who acts proud invites trouble. 20 A crooked mind will not find good. A person with a twisted tongue will fall into trouble.

Foolish Children Bring Grief

21 A man who has a foolish son has grief. The father of a fool has no joy. 22 A happy heart is good medicine. But a sad spirit dries up the bones.

Justice and Wisdom

23 A bad person takes a secret bribe to twist justice. 24 A smart person keeps wisdom in mind. But a fool's eyes look everywhere.

A Foolish Son

25 A foolish son makes his father sad and his mother bitter. 26 It is not good to punish a good person. It is not good to hit leaders for doing right.

Think Before You Speak

27 A person who knows a lot holds back his words. A person with good sense stays calm. 28 Even a fool seems wise when he is quiet. He seems smart when he keeps his mouth shut.
