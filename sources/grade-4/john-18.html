Jesus Goes to a Garden

1 After Jesus said these things, He went out with His followers. They crossed the Kidron Valley. There was a garden there. Jesus and His followers went into it. 2 Judas, who betrayed Him, knew the place. Jesus often met there with His followers.

Judas Comes with Soldiers

3 Judas came there. He brought soldiers and guards from the top priests and religious leaders. They had lanterns, torches, and weapons. 4 Jesus knew all that was going to happen. He went out and said to them, "Who are you looking for?"

5 They answered, "Jesus of Nazareth."

Jesus said, "I am He." Judas, who betrayed Him, was standing with them.

6 When Jesus said, "I am He," they stepped back. They fell to the ground.

7 He asked them again, "Who are you looking for?"

They said, "Jesus of Nazareth."

8 Jesus answered, "I told you that I am He. If you are looking for Me, let these men go."

9 This happened so Jesus' words would come true. He had said, "I have not lost one of those You gave Me."

10 Simon Peter had a sword. He pulled it out. He hit the top priest's servant. He cut off the servant's right ear. The servant's name was Malchus.

11 Jesus said to Peter, "Put your sword away! Should I not accept the suffering the Father has given Me?"

12 Then the soldiers, the commander, and the Jewish guards arrested Jesus. They tied Him up.

Jesus Is Taken to Annas

13 They led Him first to Annas. Annas was the father-in-law of Caiaphas. Caiaphas was the top priest that year. 14 Caiaphas was the one who told the Jewish leaders this. He said it was better for one man to die for the people.

Peter Follows Jesus

15 Simon Peter and another follower went with Jesus. That follower knew the top priest. So he went with Jesus into the top priest's courtyard. 16 Peter stood outside by the door. The other follower knew the top priest. He came out. He spoke to the girl who watched the door. He brought Peter in.

17 The girl at the door said to Peter, "Aren't you one of this man's followers?"

He said, "No, I am not."

18 It was cold. The servants and guards stood around a fire of coals. They warmed themselves. Peter also stood with them. He warmed himself.

The High Priest Questions Jesus

19 The top priest asked Jesus about His followers and His teaching.

20 Jesus answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world. I always taught in the meeting place and in the temple. All the Jews meet there. I said nothing in secret. 21 Why do you ask Me? Ask the people who heard Me. They know what I said."

22 When Jesus said this, one of the guards standing nearby hit Jesus. He said, "Is that how You answer the top priest?"

23 Jesus answered him, "If I said something wrong, tell what was wrong. But if I said what is right, why did You hit Me?"

24 Then Annas sent Him, still tied up, to Caiaphas the top priest.

25 Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said to him, "Aren't you one of His followers?"

He denied it. He said, "No, I am not."

26 One of the top priest's servants was there. He was a relative of the man whose ear Peter had cut off. He said, "Didn't I see you with Him in the garden?"

27 Peter denied it again. Right away a rooster crowed.

Jesus Is Taken to Pilate

28 They led Jesus from Caiaphas to the ruler's house. It was early morning. The Jewish leaders did not go into the house. They did not want to become unclean. They wanted to eat the Passover meal.

29 Pilate went out to them. He said, "What charge do you bring against this man?"

30 They answered, "If He were not a bad man, we would not have handed Him over to you."

31 Pilate said to them, "Take Him yourselves and judge Him by your law."

The Jewish leaders said, "We are not allowed to put anyone to death."

32 This happened so Jesus' words would come true. It showed what kind of death He would die.

Pilate Talks with Jesus

33 Pilate went back into the house. He called for Jesus. He asked Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?"

34 Jesus answered, "Are you asking this on your own? Or did others tell you about Me?"

35 Pilate answered, "Am I a Jew? Your own people and top priests handed You over to me. What have You done?"

36 Jesus answered, "My kingdom is not of this world. If My kingdom were of this world, My servants would fight. They would keep Me from being handed over to the Jewish leaders. But My kingdom is not from here."

37 Pilate said to Him, "So you are a king?"

Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. I was born for this. I came into the world to tell the truth. Everyone who is from the truth listens to My voice."

38 Pilate said to Him, "What is truth?" Then he went out again to the Jewish leaders. He said, "I find no fault in Him."

39 Pilate said to them, "You have a custom. I release one prisoner for you at the Passover. Do you want me to release the King of the Jews for you?"

40 They shouted, "Not Him! Give us Barabbas!" Now Barabbas was a robber.
