Isaac Is Old and Blind

1 Isaac was old, and his eyes were weak. He could not see. He called his older son, Esau. He said, "My son."

Esau said, "Here I am."

2 Isaac said, "I am old now. I do not know when I will die."

3 "Now get your weapons. Take your bow and arrows. Go out to the field. Hunt some wild animal for me. 4 Cook me a tasty meal, the kind I like. Bring it to me. I will eat it. Then I will bless you before I die."

Rebekah Helps Jacob

5 Rebekah was listening while Isaac talked to his son Esau. Esau went to the field to hunt. He would bring back the food.

6 Rebekah said to her son Jacob, "I heard your father say to your brother Esau, 7 'Bring me some wild animal and cook me a tasty meal. I will eat it. Then I will bless you before the LORD before I die.' 8 Now, my son, listen to me. Do what I tell you. 9 Go to the flock. Bring me two good young goats. I will cook them into a tasty meal for your father, the way he likes it. 10 Take it to your father. He will eat it. Then he may bless you before he dies."

11 Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "My brother Esau is a hairy man. I have smooth skin."

12 "What if my father touches me? He will think I am tricking him. Then I will get a curse, not a blessing."

13 His mother said, "My son, let the curse fall on me. Just listen to me. Go get the goats for me."

14 So Jacob went and got the goats. He brought them to his mother. She cooked a tasty meal, just the way his father liked it. 15 Rebekah took the best clothes of Esau, her older son. She had them in the house. She put them on Jacob, her younger son. 16 She put goat skins on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck. 17 Then she put the tasty meal and the bread she had made into Jacob's hands.

Jacob Tricks Isaac

18 Jacob went to his father. He said, "My father."

Isaac said, "Yes, my son. Who is it?"

19 Jacob said, "I am Esau, your first son. I did what you told me. Please sit up. Eat some of the food I hunted. Then you can bless me."

20 Isaac said, "How did you find it so fast, my son?"

Jacob said, "The LORD your God helped me find it."

21 Isaac said, "Come near, my son. Let me touch you. I want to know if you are really my son Esau."

22 Jacob went close to his father Isaac. Isaac touched him. Isaac said, "The voice is Jacob's voice. But the hands are Esau's hands."

23 Isaac did not know him, because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau's hands. So Isaac blessed him.

24 Isaac said, "Are you really my son Esau?"

Jacob said, "I am."

25 Isaac said, "My son, bring me some of the food you hunted to eat. Then I will bless you." Jacob brought it to him, and Isaac ate. Jacob also brought him wine, and Isaac drank.

26 Then Isaac said, "Come here, my son, and kiss me."

27 Jacob went to him and kissed him. Isaac smelled his clothes. Then Isaac blessed him. He said, "Ah, my son smells like a field that the LORD has blessed."

28 "May God give you dew from heaven and good things from the earth. May you have lots of grain and new wine."

29 "May countries serve you. May people bow down to you. Be the boss over your brothers. May your mother's sons bow down to you. May those who curse you be cursed. May those who bless you be blessed."

Esau Finds Out

30 After Isaac finished blessing Jacob, Jacob had just left. Then his brother Esau came in from hunting.

31 Esau also cooked a tasty meal. He brought it to his father. He said, "My father, please sit up. Eat some of the food I hunted. Then you can bless me."

32 Isaac said, "Who are you?"

Esau said, "I am your son. I am your first son, Esau."

33 Isaac shook hard. He was very scared. He said, "Then who hunted the animal and brought it to me? I ate it before you came. I blessed him. He will stay blessed!"

34 When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out loud. It was a big, bitter cry. He said to his father, "Bless me too, my father!"

35 Isaac said, "Your brother came by tricking me. He took your blessing."

36 Esau said, "He is named Jacob for a reason. He has tricked me two times. He took my rights as the first son. Now he has taken my blessing." Then Esau said, "Have you not saved any blessing for me?"

37 Isaac said to Esau, "I made him boss over you. I gave all his relatives to him as servants. I gave him grain and new wine. So what can I do for you, my son?"

38 Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, my father!" Then Esau cried loudly.

39 Isaac said to him, "Your home will be away from the good things of the earth. Your home will be away from the dew of heaven above. 40 You will live by your sword. You will serve your brother. But when you get restless, you will break free from his control."

41 Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing his father gave him. Esau said to himself, "My father will die soon. Then I will kill my brother Jacob."

Rebekah Sends Jacob Away

42 Someone told Rebekah what her older son Esau had said. She sent for her younger son Jacob. She said to him, "Your brother Esau plans to kill you. He wants to get even with you. 43 Now, my son, do what I say. Run away fast to my brother Laban in Haran. 44 Stay with him for a while. Stay until your brother's anger goes away. 45 When your brother is not angry with you anymore, and he forgets what you did to him, I will send for you. I will bring you back. Why should I lose both of you in one day?"

Rebekah Talks to Isaac

46 Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am tired of living because of these Hittite women. If Jacob takes a wife from these women here, from these Hittite women, my life will not be worth living."
