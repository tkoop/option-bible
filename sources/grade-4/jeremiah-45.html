A Message for Baruch

1 This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah. Baruch wrote these words on a scroll as Jeremiah said them. This was in the fourth year of King Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. He said:

2 "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch: 3 You said, 'I am so sad! The LORD has added sorrow to my pain. I am tired from crying, and I have found no rest.' 4 So you must tell him, 'This is what the LORD says: I will tear down what I built. I will pull up what I planted. This is for the whole earth.' 5 And you? Do you look for big things for yourself? Do not look for them. I will bring trouble on all people, says the LORD. But I will give you your life wherever you go."
