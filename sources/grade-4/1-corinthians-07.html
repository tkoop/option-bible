About Marriage and Single Life

1 Now about the things you wrote: "It is good for a man not to have sexual relations with a woman." 2 But because there is so much sexual sin, each man should have his own wife, and each woman should have her own husband.

3 A husband should give his wife what she needs. A wife should do the same for her husband. 4 A wife does not have full power over her own body. Her husband does. A husband does not have full power over his own body. His wife does. 5 Do not keep away from each other. You may stop for a short time if you both agree, so you can pray. Then come together again. This way Satan will not tempt you, because you cannot control yourselves. 6 I say this as kind advice, not as a command. 7 I wish everyone were like me. But each person has his own gift from God. One has this gift, and another has that gift.

Advice for Single People and Widows

8 To people who are not married and to widows I say this: It is good for them to stay as I am. 9 But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry. It is better to marry than to burn with desire.

Rules for Married People

10 To married people I give this command. It is not from me, but from the Lord: A wife should not leave her husband. 11 If she does leave, she should stay unmarried, or she should go back to her husband. And a husband should not leave his wife.

When One Spouse Does Not Believe

12 To the rest I say this. I say it, not the Lord: If a believer has a wife who does not believe, and she is willing to live with him, he should not leave her. 13 If a woman has a husband who does not believe, and he is willing to live with her, she should not leave her husband. 14 The husband who does not believe is made holy through his wife. The wife who does not believe is made holy through her husband. If not, your children would be unclean. But now they are holy. 15 If the one who does not believe wants to leave, let him go. The Christian brother or sister is not tied to them in such a case. God has called you to live in peace. 16 Wife, how do you know if you will save your husband? Husband, how do you know if you will save your wife?

Live the Life God Gave You

17 Each person should live the life the Lord has given. This is what God called each person to do. I teach this in all the churches. 18 If a man was already circumcised when God called him, he should not try to undo it. If he was not circumcised, he should not get circumcised. 19 Circumcision means nothing. Not being circumcised means nothing. What matters is obeying God's commands. 20 Each person should stay in the situation he was in when God called him. 21 Were you a slave when God called you? Do not worry about it. But if you can become free, do that. 22 The slave who was called by the Lord is the Lord's free person. The free person who was called is Christ's slave. 23 You were bought with a price. Do not become slaves of people. 24 Brothers and sisters, each person should stay with God in the situation he was in when God called him.

Advice for People Not Married

25 About people who have never married, I do not have a command from the Lord. But I give my opinion, as one who has received the Lord's mercy and can be trusted. 26 Because of the hard times now, I think it is good for a person to stay as he is. 27 Are you married? Do not try to get free. Are you not married? Do not look for a wife. 28 If you marry, you have not sinned. If a virgin marries, she has not sinned. But those who marry will have trouble in this life. I want to spare you that trouble.

29 Brothers and sisters, time is short. From now on, those who have wives should live as if they did not. 30 Those who cry should live as if they did not cry. Those who are happy should live as if they were not happy. Those who buy things should live as if they did not own them. 31 Those who use the things of this world should not use them too much. This world's shape is passing away.

32 I want you to be free from worry. A man who is not married cares about the Lord's things, how to please the Lord. 33 A married man cares about the things of this world, how to please his wife. 34 A woman who is not married, and a virgin, cares about the Lord's things, so she can be holy in body and spirit. A married woman cares about the things of this world, how to please her husband. 35 I say this to help you, not to trap you. I say it so you can live in a good and proper way, and serve the Lord without being pulled away.

About Engaged Couples

36 If a man thinks he is not doing right by the virgin he is engaged to, and she is past the usual age to marry, and he feels he should marry her, he should do what he wants. He does not sin. Let them marry. 37 But a man who has decided firmly in his heart, and is not forced, but has control over his own will, and has decided not to marry her, does well. 38 So the man who marries her does well, and the man who does not marry her does even better.

39 A wife is tied to her husband as long as he lives. If her husband dies, she is free to marry anyone she wants, but only in the Lord. 40 In my opinion, she is happier if she stays as she is. And I think I too have God's Spirit.
