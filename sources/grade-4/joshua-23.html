Joshua Talks to Israel

1 A long time passed. The LORD had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them. Joshua was very old.

2 Joshua called all Israel together. He called the elders, leaders, judges, and officers. He said, "I am old. I am very old.

3 "You have seen all that the LORD your God did to these nations for you. The LORD your God fought for you.

4 "Look, I gave your tribes these nations that are still left. I also gave you all the nations I destroyed. This land goes from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west.

5 "The LORD your God will push them out before you. He will drive them out before you. You will take their land. The LORD your God promised this to you.

6 "Be very strong. Keep and do all that is written in the Book of the Law of Moses. Do not turn left or right from it.

7 "Do not mix with these nations still among you. Do not say the names of their gods. Do not swear by them. Do not serve them. Do not bow down to them.

8 "Hold close to the LORD your God, like you have done until today.

9 "The LORD drove out big and strong nations before you. No one has stood against you to this day.

10 "One of you can chase a thousand. The LORD your God fights for you, just as He promised.

11 "So be very careful to love the LORD your God.

Joshua Warns Them

12 "But if you turn away and hold to the rest of these nations still among you, and if you marry them and join them,

13 "then know for sure that the LORD your God will not drive these nations out before you anymore. They will be traps and snares for you. They will be like whips on your sides and thorns in your eyes. You will die and lose this good land the LORD your God gave you.

Joshua Says Goodbye

14 "Now I am going to die, like all people do. You know in your heart and soul that not one good thing the LORD your God promised you has failed. Everything has come true.

15 "Just as every good thing the LORD your God promised you has come true, so the LORD will bring every bad thing on you. He will destroy you from this good land the LORD your God gave you.

16 "If you break the agreement the LORD your God made with you, and go serve other gods and bow to them, then the LORD will be angry with you. You will quickly lose the good land He gave you."
