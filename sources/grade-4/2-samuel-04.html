Saul's Son Loses Courage

1 When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, he lost courage. All of Israel was afraid. 2 Saul's son had two men who were leaders of soldiers. One was named Baanah, and the other was named Rechab. They were sons of Rimmon from Beeroth, and they were from the family of Benjamin. Beeroth was counted as part of Benjamin. 3 The people of Beeroth ran away to Gittaim. They lived there as strangers until this day.

4 Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who could not walk well. He was five years old when news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up and ran. As she hurried, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

Ish-bosheth Is Killed

5 Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon from Beeroth, went to Ish-bosheth's house. It was the hot part of the day, at noon. Ish-bosheth was lying on his bed. 6 They went into the middle of the house as if to get some wheat. Then they stabbed him in the stomach. Rechab and his brother Baanah got away. 7 They went into the house while he was lying on his bed in his bedroom. They struck him and killed him. Then they cut off his head and took it with them. They traveled all night along the road through the Arabah.

8 They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron. They said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy. He tried to kill you. Today the the LORD has paid back Saul and his family for what they did to you."

David Answers the Killers

9 David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, sons of Rimmon from Beeroth. He said, "As surely as the the LORD lives, who saved me from all trouble, 10 when someone told me, 'Saul is dead,' thinking he was bringing good news, I grabbed him and killed him at Ziklag. That was his reward for bringing that news. 11 How much more should I now make you pay for killing a good man in his own house while he lay on his bed? I will remove you from the earth."

David Punishes the Killers

12 David gave an order to his young men. They killed Rechab and Baanah. They cut off their hands and feet. Then they hung them by the pool in Hebron. They also took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in Abner's grave in Hebron.
