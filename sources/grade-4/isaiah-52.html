God Tells Zion to Wake Up

1 Wake up, wake up! Put on your strength, O Zion! Put on your beautiful clothes, O Jerusalem, the holy city. People who are not part of God's people and are not clean will not come in to you anymore.

2 Shake off your dust. Get up and sit on your throne, O Jerusalem. Take off the chains around your neck, O captive people of Zion.

The LORD Will Set His People Free

3 For this is what the LORD says: "You were sold for nothing, and without money you will be set free."

4 For this is what the Lord the LORD says: "At first My people went down to Egypt to live; then Assyria hurt them."

5 "And now what do I have here?" says the LORD. "My people were taken away for nothing, and those who rule them laugh at Me," says the LORD. "And all day long people say bad things about My name.

6 So My people will know My name. So on that day they will know that I am the one who speaks. Here I am."

Good News for Zion

7 How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those who bring good news, who tell of peace, who bring good news of good things, who tell of being saved! They say to Zion, "Your God is king!"

8 Listen! Your watchmen lift up their voices. Together they sing for joy. For everyone will see with their own eyes when the LORD comes back to Zion.

9 Burst out in joy and sing together, O ruins of Jerusalem, for the LORD has comforted His people; He has set Jerusalem free.

10 The LORD has shown His holy power to all the nations, and all the ends of the earth will see how our God saves.

Come Out and Be Clean

11 Go away, go away! Come out from there! Do not touch anything unclean! Come out from her! Clean yourselves, you who carry the things of the LORD.

12 For you will not leave in a hurry, and you will not run away. For the LORD will go in front of you, and the God of Israel will guard your back.

The LORD's Servant

13 Look! My Servant will do well. He will be raised up and lifted high. He will be very great.

14 Just as many people were shocked at You, His face was hurt more than any man's, and His looks were worse than other people.

15 So He will make many nations clean, and kings will close their mouths because of Him. For they will see what they were not told, and they will understand what they did not hear.
