No Punishment Now

1 So now there is no punishment for people who are in Christ Jesus. 2 The power of the Spirit, who gives life in Christ Jesus, has set you free from the power of sin and death. 3 The law could not save us because our human nature was too weak. So God sent His own Son in a body like ours. He came to take away sin, and God punished sin in His Son's body. 4 Now the good things the law wanted can happen in us. We do not live to please our sinful selves. We live to please the Spirit.

Two Ways to Live

5 People who live to please their sinful nature think about sinful things. But people who live to please the Spirit think about what the Spirit wants. 6 Thinking only about sinful things leads to death. Thinking about what the Spirit wants gives life and peace. 7 A mind that thinks only about sin is against God. It does not obey God's law. It cannot obey it. 8 So people who live only for sinful things cannot please God.

God's Spirit Lives in You

9 But you are not like that. You live by the Spirit, if God's Spirit lives in you. If anyone does not have Christ's Spirit, that person does not belong to Him. 10 If Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, but your spirit is alive because God made you right. 11 If the Spirit of God, who raised Jesus from the dead, lives in you, then God, who raised Christ from the dead, will also give life to your bodies that die through His Spirit who lives in you.

We Are God's Children

12 So, brothers and sisters, we do not owe anything to our sinful nature. We do not have to live to please it. 13 If you live to please your sinful nature, you will die. But if you use the Spirit's power to stop doing wrong things with your body, you will live. 14 All who are led by God's Spirit are God's children. 15 You did not get a spirit that makes you a slave again and makes you afraid. You got the Spirit who makes you God's child. With that Spirit, we cry, "Abba, Father." 16 The Spirit Himself tells our spirit that we are God's children. 17 If we are God's children, then we will receive what God has promised. We will share it with Christ. But we must suffer with Him first, so we can share His glory.

Future Glory

18 I think the hard things we suffer now are nothing compared to the glory God will show us later. 19 All creation is waiting. It wants to see God's children shown for who they are. 20 God made the world broken and empty. The world did not choose it. But God did it with hope. 21 One day creation itself will be set free from decay and death. It will share the freedom and glory of God's children. 22 We know that all creation has been groaning like it is in pain giving birth until now. 23 We who have God's Spirit also groan inside. We wait for God to make us fully His children. We wait for our bodies to be set free. 24 We were saved because of this hope. But if we can see something, it is not hope. Who hopes for what they already see? 25 If we hope for what we cannot see, we wait for it with patience.

The Spirit Helps Us Pray

26 The Spirit also helps us when we are weak. We do not know how to pray the way we should. But the Spirit Himself prays for us with deep groans that words cannot say. 27 God, who looks at our hearts, knows what the Spirit is thinking. The Spirit prays for God's people the way God wants.

God Works for Good

28 We know that God makes everything work for good for people who love Him. He does this for those He has called to follow His plan. 29 God knew His people before they were born. He chose them to become like His Son. Then His Son would be the first and most important among many brothers and sisters. 30 Those He chose, He also called. Those He called, He made right with Him. Those He made right, He also gave glory.

Nothing Can Separate Us

31 What can we say about all this? If God is for us, no one can stand against us. 32 God did not keep back His own Son. He gave Him up for all of us. So He will surely give us everything else we need, along with His Son. 33 Who can accuse the people God has chosen? God is the one who makes them right. 34 Who can condemn us? Christ Jesus died. He was raised to life. He sits at God's right side. He prays for us. 35 Who can separate us from Christ's love? Can trouble, or pain, or being chased for our faith, or hunger, or having no clothes, or danger, or a sword? 36 It is written, "For Your sake we face death all day. We are counted like sheep to be killed." 37 But in all these things we win completely through Him who loved us. 38 I am sure that nothing can separate us from God's love. Not death or life. Not angels or rulers or powers. Not things now or things to come. 39 Not high or low. Not anything else in all creation. Nothing can separate us from God's love that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
