Esther Tells the King Her Request

1 The king and Haman went to eat with Queen Esther. 2 While they drank wine at the second dinner, the king asked Esther again, "What do you want, Queen Esther? It will be given to you. What are you asking for? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be done."

3 Queen Esther answered, "If you are pleased with me, King, and if it pleases you, please save my life. That is what I ask. And save my people. That is what I want. 4 I and my people have been sold to be destroyed, killed, and wiped out. If we had only been sold as slaves, I would have kept quiet, because the enemy is not worth the harm he brings to the king."

The King Finds Out Who Hurt Esther's People

5 King Xerxes asked Queen Esther, "Who is he, and where is he, who has dared to do such a thing?"

6 Esther replied, "The man who hates us and is our enemy is this bad Haman!" Haman was scared in front of the king and queen.

7 The king was so angry that he got up from the dinner and went into the palace garden. Haman stayed to beg Queen Esther to spare his life. He saw the king had decided to destroy him.

8 When the king came back from the garden to the dinner hall, Haman was falling on the bed where Esther was. The king said, "Would he dare attack the queen while I am in the house?" As soon as the king said this, they covered Haman's face.

Haman Is Punished

9 Then Harbonah, one of the king's servants, said, "Look, there is a very tall pole for hanging at Haman's house. He had it built for Mordecai, who helped save the king's life. Hang him on it!"

The king said, "Hang him on it!"

10 So they hanged Haman on the pole he had made for Mordecai. Then the king's anger went away.
