Moab Sends a Lamb

1 Send a lamb to the ruler of the land. Send it from Sela through the desert to Mount Zion.

2 The women of Moab are like birds driven from their nests. They wait at the river crossings of Arnon.

Hide the People Who Run Away

3 Give good advice. Do what is right. Be like cool shade at noon. Hide the people who are chased away. Do not tell where they are. 4 Let Moab's people who were chased away stay with you. Be a safe place for them from the one who destroys. The one who takes by force is gone. The ruin is over. The one who hurts people is gone from the land.

A Good Ruler Will Come

5 Then a king's seat will be set up in loving care. One will sit on it and be true. He will be in David's tent. He will judge fairly and look for what is right. He will be quick to do right.

Moab Is Too Proud

6 We heard how proud Moab is. He is very proud. We heard his anger and his proud words. But his words are not true. 7 So Moab will cry for Moab. Everyone will cry. You will be sad for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth. You will be very sad.

Moab's Fields and Vines Are Ruined

8 The fields of Heshbon are weak. The grapevines of Sibmah are weak. The rulers of other nations hit their branches. Those branches reached to Jazer. They spread into the desert. Its new growth spread far, even over the sea. 9 So I will cry like Jazer cries for Sibmah's grapevines. I will cry tears for Heshbon and Elealeh. The happy shouts have stopped for your summer fruit and your harvest.

10 Joy is taken from the good fields. In the vineyards no one sings or shouts. No one steps on grapes to make wine. I stopped the happy shout at the wine presses.

11 So my heart aches for Moab like a sad song. My deep feelings ache for Kir-hareseth.

12 When people see Moab is tired on the high place, he will go to his holy place to pray. But he will not be able.

The LORD's Word About Moab

13 This is the word the LORD spoke about Moab long ago.

14 But now the LORD says, "In three years, like a worker's years, Moab's glory will be brought down. Its large crowd will be small. The few left will be very weak."
