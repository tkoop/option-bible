God Is King Over the Whole World

1 Clap your hands, all you people. Shout to God with a happy shout. 2 The LORD Most High is awesome. He is the great King over all the earth.

3 He makes other nations obey us. He puts them under our feet. 4 He chose the land we get. It is the best part of Jacob's land, and He loves it.

5 God went up with a shout. The LORD went up with the sound of a trumpet. 6 Sing to God. Sing to God. Sing to our King. Sing to God.

7 God is King of all the earth. Sing a wise song to Him. 8 God rules over the nations. God sits on His holy throne.

9 The leaders of the nations came together. They are the people of the God of Abraham. The rulers of the earth belong to God. He is raised up very high.
