Jacob's Family in Egypt

1 These are the names of Israel's sons who went to Egypt with Jacob. Each man came with his family. 2 Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah. 3 Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin. 4 Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 5 Jacob's family had seventy people. Joseph was already in Egypt. 6 Joseph died. His brothers died too. So did all the people from that time.

The Israelites Grow Strong

7 The Israelites had many children. They grew fast and became very strong. The land was full of them.

A New King Makes Them Slaves

8 A new king ruled Egypt. He did not know Joseph. 9 He said to his people, "Look! The Israelites are more and stronger than we are. 10 Let us be smart with them. If we do not, they will grow more. If war comes, they may join our enemies and fight us. Then they might leave the land."

11 So the Egyptians put bosses over them. The bosses made them work hard. The Israelites built Pithom and Raamses for Pharaoh. These were cities to store things. 12 The more the Egyptians hurt them, the more the Israelites grew. The Egyptians were afraid of them. 13 The Egyptians made the Israelites work very hard. 14 They made life bitter. They worked with clay and bricks. They did all kinds of hard work in the fields. Every job was hard.

The King Talks to the Midwives

15 The king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives. One was named Shiphrah. The other was named Puah. 16 He said, "When you help the Hebrew women have babies, watch them as they give birth. If the baby is a boy, kill him. If it is a girl, let her live."

The Midwives Fear God

17 But the midwives feared God. They did not do what the king said. They let the boys live.

18 The king called the midwives and said, "Why did you do this? Why did you let the boys live?"

19 The midwives said to Pharaoh, "Hebrew women are not like Egyptian women. They are strong. They have their babies before we get there."

God Helps the Midwives

20 God was kind to the midwives. The Israelites grew very much and became very strong. 21 Because the midwives feared God, He gave them families.

Pharaoh's New Order

22 Pharaoh told all his people, "Every Hebrew boy who is born, throw him into the Nile. But let every girl live."
