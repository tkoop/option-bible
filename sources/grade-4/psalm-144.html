A Song by David

1 Praise the LORD my Rock. He trains my hands for war. He trains my fingers for battle. 2 He is kind to me. He is my safe place and my strong tower. He saves me. He is my shield, and I trust in Him. He puts nations under me.

3 The LORD, what is a person that You know him? What is a human being that You think about him? 4 People are like a breath. Their days pass like a shadow.

David Asks for Help

5 The LORD, bend Your sky and come down. Touch the mountains so they smoke. 6 Send lightning and scatter them. Shoot Your arrows and make them run away. 7 Reach down from heaven and save me. Pull me from deep water. Save me from strangers. 8 They say things that are not true. They do not keep their promises.

David Sings a New Song

9 God, I will sing a new song to You. I will play music to You on a harp with ten strings. 10 He gives kings the win. He saves His servant David from the sword that kills. 11 Save me and pull me from strangers. They say things that are not true. They do not keep their promises.

Blessings for the People

12 May our sons grow strong like plants when they are young. May our daughters be like beautiful pillars in a palace. 13 May our barns be full with all kinds of food. May our sheep have thousands and thousands of babies in our fields. 14 May our oxen carry heavy loads. May our walls stay strong. May no one be taken away. May our streets be quiet. 15 Happy are the people who have these things. Happy are the people whose God is the LORD.
