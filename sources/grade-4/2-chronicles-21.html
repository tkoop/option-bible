Jehoram Becomes King

1 Jehoshaphat died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Jehoram became king after him. 2 Jehoram had brothers. They were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. They were all sons of Jehoshaphat, king of Israel. 3 Their father gave them many gifts of silver, gold, and other valuable things. He also gave them strong cities in Judah. But he gave the kingdom to Jehoram, because Jehoram was the firstborn son. 4 When Jehoram took power, he made himself strong. He killed all his brothers with a sword. He also killed some leaders of Israel. 5 Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for eight years.

Jehoram Does Evil

6 He followed the ways of the kings of Israel, just as Ahab’s family had done. He married Ahab’s daughter. He did what was evil in the eyes of the the LORD. 7 But the LORD did not want to destroy David’s family. He had made a promise to David. He had promised to keep David’s family line alive forever.

Edom and Libnah Revolt

8 In Jehoram’s time, Edom broke away from Judah’s rule. They chose their own king. 9 Jehoram went with his officers and all his chariots. The Edomites surrounded him and his chariot leaders. But he got up and attacked them at night. 10 Edom is still free from Judah today. At that time, Libnah also broke away from his rule. This happened because Jehoram had left the LORD, the God of his fathers. 11 He also made high places on the hills of Judah. He led the people of Jerusalem away from the LORD. He led Judah down the wrong path.

Elijah's Letter

12 Then a letter came to Jehoram from Elijah the prophet. It said, "This is what the LORD, the God of your father David, says: You have not followed the ways of your father Jehoshaphat. You have not followed the ways of Asa, king of Judah. 13 Instead, you have followed the ways of the kings of Israel. You have led Judah and the people of Jerusalem away from the LORD, just like Ahab’s family did. You have also killed your brothers. They were your father’s family, and they were better than you. 14 So the LORD will bring a terrible sickness on your people, your sons, your wives, and all your things. 15 You yourself will be very sick in your stomach. Your sickness will get worse day after day, until your insides come out."

Judah Is Attacked

16 Then the LORD made the Philistines and the Arabs angry at Jehoram. These Arabs lived near the Cushites. 17 They came into Judah and broke in. They took all the things they found in the king’s house. They also took his sons and his wives. The only son left was Jehoahaz, his youngest son.

Jehoram Dies

18 After all this, the LORD made Jehoram very sick in his stomach. The sickness could not be cured. 19 After two years, his sickness got so bad that his insides came out. He died in great pain. His people did not burn spices for him, as they had done for his fathers. 20 He was thirty-two years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for eight years. No one was sad when he died. They buried him in the City of David, but not with the other kings.
