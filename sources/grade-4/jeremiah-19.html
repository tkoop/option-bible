The LORD Tells Jeremiah to Buy a Clay Jar

1 The LORD said, "Go and buy a clay jar from a man who makes pots. Take some of the leaders of the people and some of the leaders of the priests." 2 "Go out to the Valley of Ben Hinnom, near the Gate of Broken Pots. Say there the words I tell you."

God Warns the People of Judah

3 "Listen to the word of the the LORD, kings of Judah and people of Jerusalem. The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says He is bringing trouble on this place. Anyone who hears about it will be shocked. 4 They turned away from Me and made this place unholy. They burned sweet-smelling smoke to other gods that they, their fathers, and their kings did not know. They filled this place with the blood of people who did nothing wrong. 5 They built places to worship Baal. They burned their sons in the fire as gifts to Baal. I did not tell them to do this. It never came to My mind."

The Valley Gets a New Name

6 "The LORD says that soon this place will not be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben Hinnom. It will be called the Valley of Killing. 7 Here I will ruin the plans of Judah and Jerusalem. I will let their enemies kill them with swords. I will give their dead bodies to the birds and wild animals to eat. 8 I will make this city a ruin. People will hiss when they see it. Everyone who walks by will be shocked and hiss because of all its hurts. 9 I will make them eat their own sons and daughters. They will eat each other when their enemies surround them and make life hard."

Jeremiah Breaks the Jar

10 The LORD said, "Then break the jar in front of the men who came with you."

11 Jeremiah told the people, "The LORD of Armies says He will break this people and this city like a jar maker breaks a jar. It cannot be put back together. They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no room left. 12 The LORD says He will do this to this place and to the people who live here. He will make this city like Topheth. 13 The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be unclean like Topheth. This is because on their roofs they burned sweet-smelling smoke to all the stars and poured out drinks to other gods."

Jeremiah Speaks at the House of the the LORD

14 Jeremiah came back from Topheth, where the the LORD had sent him to speak His message. He stood in the courtyard of the house of the the LORD. He said to all the people, 15 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says He is bringing on this city and all its nearby towns all the trouble He said He would. The people were stubborn and would not listen to His words."
