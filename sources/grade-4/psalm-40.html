David Waits for the LORD

1 I waited and waited for the LORD. He leaned down to me and heard my cry for help. 2 He pulled me out of a deep, muddy pit. He set my feet on a rock and made my steps firm. 3 He gave me a new song to sing. It praises our God. Many people will see it and respect God, and they will trust the LORD.

4 Happy is the person who trusts the LORD. That person does not follow proud people or believe lies. 5 The LORD, my God, You have done many wonderful things. You have great plans for us. No one is like You. If I tried to tell all of them, there would be too many to count.

Doing God's Will

6 You did not want gifts or offerings. Instead, You made me able to listen. You did not ask for burned gifts or gifts for sin. 7 Then I said, "Here I am. I have come. It is written about me in the book. 8 I want to do what You want, my God. Your rules are in my heart."

9 I tell people what is right when many people are together. I do not keep my mouth shut. You, the LORD, know this. 10 I have not kept Your goodness a secret in my heart. I have told people about Your faithfulness and Your help. I have not hidden Your love and Your truth from the crowd. 11 The LORD, do not keep Your mercy from me. Let Your love and truth always protect me.

David Asks for Help

12 Troubles are all around me. I cannot count them. My sins have caught up with me, and I cannot see. They are more than the hairs on my head. My heart feels weak. 13 The LORD, please save me. Hurry and help me, the LORD.

14 Let the people who try to kill me be ashamed and confused. Let the people who want to hurt me turn back and feel ashamed. 15 Let the people who say, "Aha! Aha!" be ruined because of their shame.

Praise the LORD

16 Let everyone who looks for You be happy and glad in You. Let those who love Your saving help always say, "The LORD is great!" 17 But I am poor and need help. Please think of me, Lord. You are my helper and my rescuer. My God, do not wait.
