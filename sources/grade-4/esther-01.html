King Ahasuerus Throws a Big Party

1 This happened in the days of King Ahasuerus. He ruled 127 lands, from India to Cush. 2 At that time, King Ahasuerus sat on his royal seat in the strong city of Susa. 3 In his third year as king, he made a big dinner for all his leaders and helpers. The army leaders of Persia and Media, and the important men from the lands, stood before him. 4 For 180 days, he showed how rich and great his kingdom was. 5 After those days, the king made a seven-day party in the garden of the king's palace. It was for all the people in the city of Susa, from the least important to the most important. 6 White and blue cloth hung there. Fine linen and purple cloth were tied with cords to silver rings on stone pillars. Gold and silver couches sat on a floor made of pretty, expensive stones. 7 They served drinks in gold cups. The king gave plenty of wine, because he was very generous. 8 No one had to drink. The king told the men who ran his house to let each man choose for himself. 9 Queen Vashti also made a dinner for the women in the king's palace.

The King Calls for Queen Vashti

10 On the seventh day, the king was happy from wine. He called seven men who helped him. Their names were Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carkas. 11 He told them to bring Queen Vashti to him. She was to wear the royal crown. He wanted to show the people and leaders how beautiful she was. 12 But Queen Vashti would not come. The king had sent the men with his message. The king became very angry, and his anger burned inside him.

The King Asks His Wise Men

13 Then the king talked to the wise men. They knew the right times to do things. He usually asked men who knew the law and what was fair. 14 The men closest to him were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan. They were seven leaders of Persia and Media. They saw the king often and were very important in the kingdom. 15 The king asked, "What does the law say we should do to Queen Vashti? She did not obey the king's command that came through the helpers."

Memucan Gives Advice

16 Memucan answered in front of the king and the leaders. He said, "Queen Vashti did wrong. She hurt not only the king, but all the leaders and all the people in the lands of King Ahasuerus. 17 All the women will hear what the queen did. Then they will look down on their husbands. They will say that King Ahasuerus told Queen Vashti to come, but she did not come. 18 Today the important women of Persia and Media will hear about this. They will tell all the king's leaders. Then there will be no end to disrespect and anger.

19 "If it pleases the king, send out a royal rule. Write it in the laws of Persia and Media, so it cannot be changed. Queen Vashti must never come before King Ahasuerus again. Give her royal place to a woman better than she is. 20 Then the king's rule will be heard in all his big kingdom. All women, from the least to the most important, will honor their husbands."

21 The king and the leaders liked this advice. The king did what Memucan said.

The King Sends Letters

22 He sent letters to all the lands in his kingdom. Each land got the letter in its own writing, and each people got it in its own language. The letter said that every man should be the boss in his own home.
