Rooms Around the Temple

1He took me out through the north way to the outer yard. He brought me to a room. It was across from the special place and across from the building on the north side. 2The front of the north door was 100 cubits long. It was 50 cubits wide. 3Rooms faced rooms on three levels. They were across from the twenty cubits of the inner yard. They were across from the stone floor of the outer yard. There were covered walks, one above another. 4In front of the rooms was a walk. It was ten cubits wide. It went to the inner yard. Their doors faced north. 5The top rooms were shorter. The covered walks took space from them. They took space from the middle and bottom rooms too. 6The rooms were on three levels, like pillars in the yards. But there were no pillars. So the top rooms were smaller than the lower and middle rooms. 7Outside, a wall stood in front of the rooms. It was fifty cubits long. It ran beside the rooms toward the outer yard. 8The rooms on the outer yard side were fifty cubits long. The building was one hundred cubits long in front of them. 9Below these rooms was a door. It was on the east side. People used it to enter from the outer yard. 10On the east side, the wall of the yard had rooms. They were across from the special place and across from the building. 11There was a walk in front of them. It looked like the rooms on the north side. They were as long and as wide. Their doors and rules were the same. 12The south rooms had doors like the north rooms. There was a door at the start of the walk. The walk was in front of the wall. People entered from the east.

Holy Rooms for the Priests

13He said to me, "The north and south rooms across from the special place are holy rooms. The priests who are close to the LORD will eat the holy food there. They will put the holiest offerings there. They are the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. This place is holy."

14When the priests go in, they must not go out to the outer yard in their holy clothes. They must leave those clothes there, because they are holy. They must put on other clothes before they go near the people.

The Wall Around the Temple

15He finished measuring the inner house. Then he brought me out through the east gate. He measured all around. 16He measured the east side with a measuring reed. It was 500 reeds long. 17He measured the north side. It was 500 reeds long. 18He measured the south side. It was 500 reeds long. 19He measured the west side. It was 500 reeds long. 20He measured all four sides. There was a wall around it. It was 500 reeds long and 500 reeds wide. It separated the holy place from the common place.
