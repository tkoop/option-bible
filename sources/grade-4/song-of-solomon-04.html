The Man Praises the Woman He Loves

1 "You are beautiful, my darling. Yes, you are beautiful. Your eyes are like doves behind your veil. Your hair is like a flock of goats coming down Mount Gilead. 2 Your teeth are like a flock of sheep that just got a haircut and a bath. They all match in pairs, and none is missing. 3 Your lips are like a red thread. Your mouth is lovely. Your cheeks are like a piece of pomegranate behind your veil.

4 "Your neck is like David's tower, built in rows of stone. A thousand shields hang there, shields of brave soldiers. 5 Your breasts are like two young deer, twins of a gazelle, eating among lilies.

6 "I will stay until day breaks and the shadows run away. Then I will go to the mountain of myrrh and the hill of frankincense. 7 You are beautiful in every way, my darling. There is no flaw in you.

8 "Come away with me from Lebanon, my bride. Come away from Lebanon. Look down from the top of Amana, from the top of Senir and Hermon, from the lions' dens and the leopards' mountains. 9 You have won my heart, my sister, my bride. You won my heart with one look from your eyes, with one bead from your necklace.

10 "How beautiful is your love, my sister, my bride! Your love is better than wine, and the smell of your oils is better than any spice. 11 Your lips, my bride, drip honey. Honey and milk are under your tongue. Your clothes smell like Lebanon.

12 "You are like a locked garden, my sister, my bride. You are like a locked spring, a sealed fountain. 13 Your garden grows pomegranates and all kinds of good fruit, with henna and nard. 14 There is nard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon, with all kinds of incense trees, myrrh and aloes, and all the best spices. 15 You are a garden fountain, a well of fresh water, streams flowing from Lebanon."

The Woman Invites Her Love

16 "Wake up, north wind, and come, south wind! Blow on my garden so its spices spread out. Let my love come into his garden and eat its best fruit."
