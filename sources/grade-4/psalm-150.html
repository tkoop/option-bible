A Song of Praise

1 Praise the LORD. Praise God in His holy place. Praise Him in the strong sky. 2 Praise Him for His mighty acts. Praise Him for how great He is.

3 Praise Him with a trumpet. Praise Him with a harp and a lyre. 4 Praise Him with a tambourine and dancing. Praise Him with strings and a flute.

5 Praise Him with loud cymbals. Praise Him with crashing cymbals. 6 Let everything that breathes praise the LORD. Praise the LORD.
