The Women Go to the Tomb

1 After the Sabbath was over, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices. They wanted to put the spices on Jesus' body. 2 Very early on Sunday, when the sun came up, they went to the tomb. 3 They asked each other, “Who will roll the stone away from the door of the tomb for us?” 4 But when they looked up, they saw that the stone was already rolled away. It was very big. 5 They went into the tomb. There they saw a young man. He wore a white robe. He sat on the right side. The women were amazed.

6 The young man said to them, “Do not be amazed. You are looking for Jesus from Nazareth. He was put on a cross. But He is alive again. He is not here. Look at the place where they put Him. 7 Go and tell His followers and Peter that He is going ahead of you to Galilee. You will see Him there, just as He told you.”

8 The women left the tomb and ran away. They were shaking and shocked. They were afraid. They did not say anything to anyone.

Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene

9 Early on Sunday, after Jesus rose from the dead, He appeared first to Mary Magdalene. He had sent seven demons out of her before. 10 She went and told the people who had been with Him. They were sad and crying. 11 They heard that Jesus was alive. They heard that Mary had seen Him. But they did not believe it.

Jesus Appears to Two Followers

12 Later, Jesus appeared in a different form to two of His followers. They were walking to the country. 13 Those two went and told the rest. But the rest did not believe them.

Jesus Appears to the Eleven

14 Later, Jesus appeared to the eleven while they were eating. He scolded them for not believing. They had stubborn hearts. They did not believe the people who saw Him alive again.

Jesus Sends His Followers Out

15 Jesus said to them, “Go into all the world. Tell the good news to everything God made. 16 Anyone who believes and is baptized will be saved. But anyone who does not believe will be punished. 17 These signs will follow those who believe. In My name, they will send demons out. They will speak in new languages. 18 They will pick up snakes with their hands. If they drink anything deadly, it will not hurt them. They will put their hands on sick people, and the sick people will get well.”

Jesus Goes Up to Heaven

19 After the Lord Jesus finished speaking to them, He was taken up into heaven. He sat in the place of honor next to God. 20 The followers went out. They preached everywhere. The Lord worked with them. He made their words sure by the miracles that followed.
