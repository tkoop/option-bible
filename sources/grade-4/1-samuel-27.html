David Goes to the Philistines

1 David said to himself, "One day Saul will catch me. It is best for me to run to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will stop looking for me all over Israel, and I will get away from him." 2 So David and his six hundred men went to Achish son of Maoc, king of Gath. 3 David and his men lived in Gath with Achish. Each man brought his family. David had two wives: Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail from Carmel, who had been Nabal's wife. 4 When Saul heard that David had run to Gath, he stopped looking for him.

David Asks for a Town

5 David said to Achish, "If you are happy with me, give me a place in one of the far towns, so I can live there. Why should your servant live with you in your city?" 6 So that day Achish gave him Ziklag. Even now it belongs to the kings of Judah.

7 David lived in the land of the Philistines for one year and four months.

David Raids Nearby Peoples

8 David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites. These people had lived in that land for a long time, from Shur all the way to Egypt. 9 When David attacked the land, he did not leave a man or woman alive. He took sheep, cows, donkeys, camels, and clothes. Then he went back to Achish.

Achish Asks David

10 Achish would ask, "Where did you raid today?"

David would answer, "In the south of Judah," or "In the south of the Jerahmeelites," or "In the south of the Kenites."

11 David did not let anyone live to be taken to Gath. He said, "They might tell on us and say, 'Look what David did.'" He did this the whole time he lived in the land of the Philistines.

Achish Trusts David

12 Achish trusted David. He thought, "David has made his own people hate him. So he will be my servant forever."
