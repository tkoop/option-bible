Mary Puts Perfume on Jesus

1 Six days before Passover, Jesus went to Bethany. That was where Lazarus lived. Jesus had raised Lazarus from the dead. 2 There they made a dinner for Him. Martha served. Lazarus was one of the people at the table with Jesus. 3 Mary took a pound of very costly perfume. It was pure nard. She put it on Jesus' feet. She wiped His feet with her hair. The whole house smelled like the perfume.

4 One of His disciples spoke. It was Judas Iscariot. He was the one who would hand Jesus over. He said, 5 "Why wasn't this perfume sold? It could have brought a lot of money. We could have given it to poor people."

6 He said this, but not because he cared about poor people. He was a thief. He kept the money bag and stole from it.

7 Jesus said, "Leave her alone. She saved this perfume for the day I will be buried. 8 You will always have poor people with you. But you will not always have Me."

The Plot to Kill Lazarus

9 A big crowd of Jews found out Jesus was there. They came not only to see Jesus, but also to see Lazarus. Jesus had raised Lazarus from the dead. 10 The chief priests made a plan to kill Lazarus too. 11 Many Jews left and believed in Jesus because of Lazarus.

Jesus Comes to Jerusalem

12 The next day, a big crowd came to the feast. They heard Jesus was coming to Jerusalem. 13 They took palm branches and went out to meet Him. They shouted, "Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! He is the King of Israel!"

14 Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it. This happened just as it was written: 15 "Do not be afraid, daughter of Zion. Look! Your King is coming. He is sitting on a donkey's colt."

16 At first, His disciples did not understand this. But after Jesus was honored, they remembered these things. They remembered that these things were written about Him and that they had done them for Him. 17 The crowd that was with Him kept telling about Lazarus. Jesus had called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead. 18 That is why the crowd went to meet Him. They had heard He did this sign.

19 The Pharisees said to each other, "Look! You are not getting anywhere. See, the whole world has gone after Him."

Some Greeks Want to See Jesus

20 Some Greeks were there. They came up to worship at the feast. 21 They came to Philip. He was from Bethsaida in Galilee. They asked him, "Sir, we want to see Jesus."

22 Philip went and told Andrew. Then Andrew and Philip went and told Jesus.

Jesus Talks About His Death

23 Jesus answered them, "The time has come for the Son of Man to be honored. 24 I tell you the truth. Unless a grain of wheat falls into the ground and dies, it stays alone. But if it dies, it makes much fruit. 25 Anyone who loves his life will lose it. Anyone who hates his life in this world will keep it for life that lasts forever. 26 If anyone serves Me, he must follow Me. Where I am, My servant will be too. If anyone serves Me, the Father will honor him.

27 Now My soul is troubled. What should I say? 'Father, save Me from this hour'? But that is why I came to this hour. 28 Father, make Your name great."

Then a voice came from heaven. It said, "I have made it great, and I will make it great again."

29 The crowd standing there heard it. Some said it thundered. Others said an angel spoke to Him. 30 Jesus answered, "This voice came, not for Me, but for you. 31 Now this world is judged. Now the ruler of this world will be thrown out. 32 And I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to Myself." 33 He said this to show what kind of death He would die.

34 The crowd answered Him, "We heard from the Law that the Christ stays forever. So how can you say the Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?"

35 Jesus said to them, "You still have the light with you for a little while. Walk while you have the light. Then darkness will not catch you. The one who walks in darkness does not know where he is going. 36 Believe in the light while you have it. Then you will be children of light." After Jesus said this, He went away and hid from them.

Many People Do Not Believe

37 Even though He had done so many signs in front of them, they did not believe in Him. 38 This happened so the word of Isaiah the prophet would come true. He said, "Lord, who has believed our message? Who has seen the Lord's power?" 39 That is why they could not believe. Isaiah also said, 40 "He has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts. Then they will not see with their eyes or understand with their hearts. They will not turn, and I will not heal them." 41 Isaiah said this because he saw His glory and spoke about Him.

42 Even so, many leaders believed in Him. But they did not say so because of the Pharisees. They were afraid they would be put out of the synagogue. 43 They loved praise from people more than praise from God.

Jesus Speaks About Believing

44 Jesus cried out, "The one who believes in Me does not believe in Me only. He believes in the One who sent Me. 45 The one who sees Me sees the One who sent Me. 46 I came into the world as a light. Then everyone who believes in Me will not stay in darkness. 47 If anyone hears My words and does not keep them, I do not judge him. I did not come to judge the world, but to save the world. 48 The one who rejects Me and does not take My words has a judge. The word I spoke will judge him on the last day. 49 I did not speak on My own. The Father who sent Me told Me what to say and what to speak. 50 I know His command is life that lasts forever. So I speak just as the Father told Me."
