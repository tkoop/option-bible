Everyone Has the Same End

1 I thought about all these things. I saw that good people and wise people, and the things they do, are in God's hands. No one knows if love or hate will come to them. 2 Everyone has the same end. It does not matter if a person is good or bad, clean or unclean, or gives offerings or not. The person who does right and the person who sins end the same way. The person who makes a promise and the person who fears making one end the same way. 3 This is a bad thing in all that happens on earth. Everyone has the same end. Also, people's hearts are full of evil and wild thoughts while they live. Then they die.

4 Anyone who is alive still has hope. It is better to be a poor person who is alive than a great person who is dead. 5 Living people know they will die. Dead people know nothing. They have no more reward. People forget them. 6 Their love, their hate, and their jealousy are gone. They will never again share in anything done on earth.

Enjoy Life While You Can

7 So go and eat your bread with joy. Drink your wine with a happy heart, for God is already pleased with your work. 8 Always wear your bright clothes, and always have oil on your head, to show joy. 9 Enjoy life with the wife you love. Enjoy all the days of your short life, for God gave you this life on earth. This is your share in life and in the work you do on earth. 10 Do whatever your hand finds to do. Do it with all your strength. There is no work or planning or knowing or wisdom in the grave, where you are going.

Time and Chance Happen to All

11 I saw another thing on earth. The fastest runner does not always win the race. The strongest fighter does not always win the battle. Wise people do not always get bread. Smart people do not always get money. People who know a lot do not always get good things. Time and chance happen to all of them. 12 No one knows when his time will come. People are like fish caught in a net or birds caught in a trap. Trouble comes on them suddenly.

A Poor Wise Man Saves a City

13 I also saw this wisdom on earth. It seemed great to me. 14 There was a small city with few people in it. A great king came against it. He surrounded it and built big towers to attack it. 15 A poor wise man lived in the city. He saved the city with his wisdom. But no one remembered that poor man. 16 So I said, "Wisdom is better than strength." But people looked down on the poor man's wisdom, and no one listened to his words. 17 The quiet words of wise people are better heard than the shouts of a foolish ruler. 18 Wisdom is better than weapons of war. But one sinner can destroy much good.
