The Lamb and His People

1 Then I looked, and there was the Lamb standing on Mount Zion. With Him were 144,000 people. They had His name and His Father's name written on their foreheads. 2 I heard a sound from heaven. It was like the sound of many waters. It was like loud thunder. The sound I heard was like people playing harps. 3 They sang a new song before the throne, before the four living creatures, and before the elders. No one could learn the song except the 144,000 who were bought from the earth. 4 These people kept themselves clean. They did not sin with women. They are pure. They follow the Lamb wherever He goes. They were bought from among people. They are the first gift to God and to the Lamb. 5 No lie was found in their mouths. They are without blame.

The Three Angels

6 Then I saw another angel flying high in the sky. He had the good news that lasts forever. He was to share it with everyone on earth. He was to share it with every nation, tribe, language, and people. 7 He said in a loud voice, "Fear God and give Him praise. The time has come for Him to judge. Worship the One who made heaven, earth, the sea, and the springs of water." 8 Another angel followed and said, "Fallen! Fallen is great Babylon! She made all the nations drink the wine of her angry sin."

9 A third angel followed them. He said in a loud voice, "If anyone worships the beast and its image, and gets its mark on his forehead or hand, 10 he will drink the wine of God's anger. It is poured full strength into the cup of His wrath. He will be hurt in fire and burning sulfur. This will happen before the holy angels and before the Lamb. 11 The smoke of their pain goes up forever and ever. Those who worship the beast and its image have no rest day or night. The same is true for anyone who gets the mark of its name."

12 Here is where God's people need to be patient. They keep God's commands and trust in Jesus.

13 Then I heard a voice from heaven say, "Write this: Happy are those who die in the Lord from now on."

"Yes," says the Spirit. "They will rest from their hard work. Their good works will follow them."

The Harvest

14 Then I looked, and I saw a white cloud. Someone sat on the cloud. He looked like the Son of Man. He had a gold crown on His head. He had a sharp sickle in His hand. 15 Another angel came out of the temple. He called in a loud voice to the One on the cloud, "Send your sickle and reap. The time has come to reap. The harvest of the earth is ripe." 16 The One on the cloud swung His sickle over the earth. The earth was harvested.

17 Another angel came out of the temple in heaven. He also had a sharp sickle. 18 Another angel came out from the altar. He had power over fire. He called in a loud voice to the one with the sharp sickle, "Send your sharp sickle. Gather the clusters of grapes from the earth's vine. The grapes are ripe."

The Winepress of God's Anger

19 The angel swung his sickle on the earth. He gathered the grapes. He threw them into God's great winepress of anger. 20 The winepress was trampled outside the city. Blood came out of it. It reached up to the horses' mouths. It went for about 180 miles.
