Bringing the First Crops

1 When you enter the land the LORD your God is giving you to own, and you take it over and live there, 2 take some of the first crops from all that your land grows. Put them in a basket and go to the place the LORD your God will choose for His Name. 3 Go to the priest who serves at that time and say to him, “I tell the LORD your God today that I have come to the land the LORD promised our fathers He would give us.” 4 The priest will take the basket from your hand and set it in front of the altar of the LORD your God.

Remembering God’s Help

5 Then you will say before the LORD your God, “My father was a wandering man from Aram. He went down to Egypt with a few people. He lived there, and his family grew into a great and strong nation. 6 But the Egyptians were mean to us. They hurt us and made us do hard work. 7 Then we called to the LORD, the God of our fathers. The LORD heard us and saw our trouble, our hard work, and our pain. 8 So the LORD brought us out of Egypt with great power and amazing signs and miracles. 9 He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land with plenty of good things. 10 And now I bring the first crops from the ground that You, the LORD, have given me.” Place the basket before the LORD your God and bow down before Him.

11 Then you and the Levites and the people from other lands who live among you will be happy with all the good things the LORD your God has given you and your family.

Sharing the Tithe

12 When you finish giving a tenth of all your crops in the third year, the year for giving a tenth, give it to the Levites, the people from other lands, the children with no father, and the women whose husbands have died. Then they can eat in your towns and have enough.

13 Then say to the LORD your God, “I have taken the holy part out of my house. I have given it to the Levites, the people from other lands, the children with no father, and the women whose husbands have died. I did all You commanded. I have not gone away from Your commands or forgotten them. 14 I did not eat any of the holy part while I was mourning. I did not take any of it while I was unclean. I did not give any of it to dead people. I listened to the LORD my God. I did everything You told me to do. 15 Look down from Your holy home in heaven. Bless Your people Israel and the land You gave us, just as You promised our fathers. It is a land with plenty of good things.”

Promising to Obey God

16 The LORD your God commands you today to follow these rules and laws. Obey them carefully with all your heart and soul. 17 You have said today that the LORD is your God. You will live the way He wants. You will obey His rules, His commands, and His laws. You will listen to Him. 18 The LORD has said today that you are His people, His special treasure, just as He promised. You will obey all His commands. 19 He will make you the praise and glory and honor of all the nations He has made. You will be a holy people for the LORD your God, just as He promised.
