The Beast That Came from the Sea

1 Then I saw a beast come up from the sea. It had ten horns and seven heads. It had ten crowns on its horns. It had names that spoke against God on its heads. 2 The beast looked like a leopard. Its feet were like a bear's feet. Its mouth was like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave the beast its power, its throne, and great power to rule. 3 One of the beast's heads looked like it had been killed. But the deadly wound healed. The whole world was amazed and followed the beast. 4 People worshiped the dragon because he gave power to the beast. They also worshiped the beast. They said, "Who is like the beast? Who can fight against it?"

5 The beast was given a mouth to speak proud words against God. It was allowed to use its power for forty-two months. 6 It opened its mouth to speak against God. It spoke against God's name. It spoke against God's home and the people who live in heaven. 7 It was allowed to fight God's holy people and beat them. It was given power over every tribe, people, language, and nation. 8 All people on earth will worship it. But those whose names are in the Lamb's book of life will not. The Lamb was killed. Their names were written before the world began. 9 If anyone has ears, listen. 10 If someone is to be taken prisoner, they will be taken prisoner. If someone is to be killed with a sword, they will be killed with a sword. This is where God's people need patience and faith.

The Beast That Came from the Earth

11 Then I saw another beast come up from the earth. It had two horns like a lamb's horns. But it spoke like a dragon. 12 It used all the power of the first beast right in front of it. It made the earth and everyone living on it worship the first beast. That beast's deadly wound had healed. 13 It did great miracles. It even made fire come down from heaven to earth while people watched. 14 It tricked the people living on earth. It told them, "Make a statue of the beast that was hurt by a sword but lived." 15 It was allowed to give life to the statue. Then the statue could speak. It made people who did not worship the statue be killed.

The Mark of the Beast

16 It made everyone get a mark. Small and great people, rich and poor, free and slave, all got it. The mark was on the right hand or on the forehead. 17 No one could buy or sell unless they had the mark. The mark was the beast's name or the number of its name. 18 This needs wisdom. Anyone who understands should figure out the beast's number. It is a man's number. His number is 666.
