Darius Rules Babylon

1 In the first year that Darius ruled, he was king over Babylon. Darius was the son of Ahasuerus. He was from Media. 2 In that first year, I, Daniel, was reading. I learned from the books how many years the LORD had said through Jeremiah the prophet. Jerusalem would be empty for seventy years.

Daniel Prays for His People

3 I turned to the Lord God to pray and beg Him. I did not eat. I wore rough cloth and put ashes on myself.

4 I prayed to the LORD my God and said I was wrong. I said, "Lord, You are great and awesome. You keep Your promise. You show love to those who love You and obey Your commands. 5 We sinned. We did wrong. We did bad things. We turned against You. We did not follow Your commands and Your rules. 6 We did not listen to Your servants, the prophets. They spoke in Your name to our kings, our leaders, and our fathers. They spoke to all the people too. 7 Lord, You are right. We are ashamed today. This is true for the men of Judah, the people in Jerusalem, and all Israel. It is true for those near and far, in all the lands where You sent them. They were not faithful to You. 8 the LORD, we are ashamed. Our kings, leaders, and fathers are ashamed. We sinned against You. 9 Lord our God, You are kind and forgiving. We turned against You. 10 We did not listen to the LORD our God. We did not follow His laws. He gave those laws to us through His servants, the prophets. 11 All Israel broke Your law and turned away. They would not listen to You. So the trouble God promised in the Law of Moses, His servant, came on us. We sinned against Him. 12 You did what You said You would do to us and our rulers. You brought a big trouble on us. Nothing like this has happened anywhere under heaven. Nothing like what happened to Jerusalem. 13 All this trouble came on us, just like it is written in the Law of Moses. But we did not ask the LORD our God for help. We did not turn from our wrong ways. We did not learn Your truth. 14 So the LORD kept the trouble ready and brought it on us. the LORD our God is right in all He does. But we did not listen to Him. 15 Now, Lord our God, You brought Your people out of Egypt with strong power. You made Your name great, even today. We sinned. We did bad things. 16 Lord, You do what is right. Please turn Your anger away from Your city, Jerusalem. It is Your holy mountain. Our sins and our fathers' sins made Jerusalem and Your people a shame to everyone around us. 17 Now, our God, hear the prayer of Your servant. Hear his request. Lord, for Your own sake, shine on Your holy place that is now empty. 18 Listen, my God. Hear me. Open Your eyes and see. We are ruined. See the city that has Your name. We are not asking You because we are good. We ask because You are very kind. 19 Lord, hear me. Lord, forgive me. Lord, listen and act. My God, do not wait. Do this for Your own sake. Your city and Your people have Your name."

Gabriel Brings God's Answer

20 While I was still talking and praying, I told God I was wrong. I told Him my people Israel were wrong. I asked the LORD my God to help His holy mountain. 21 While I was still praying, Gabriel came. I had seen him before in a vision. He flew quickly to me at the time of the evening offering.

22 He taught me and talked with me. He said, "Daniel, I came now to help you understand. 23 When you first prayed, an answer came. I came to tell you, because you are very loved. So think about the message and understand the vision. 24 Seventy weeks are set for your people and your holy city. They will end sin. They will pay for wrong. They will bring lasting good. They will finish the vision and the prophet's words. They will make the Most Holy Place holy. 25 Know and understand this. From the time the order is given to bring the Jews back and rebuild Jerusalem, until the Chosen Ruler comes, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks. The city will be rebuilt with streets and a ditch, but in hard times. 26 After the sixty-two weeks, the Chosen Ruler will be killed. He will have nothing. Then the people of the ruler who is coming will destroy the city and the holy place. The end will come like a flood. Until the end there will be war. God has set the ruin. 27 He will make a strong promise with many for one week. In the middle of the week, he will stop the offerings and gifts. With horrible things, one who ruins will come. This will last until the set ruin is poured out on the one who ruins."
