David Stays Home

1 In the spring, when kings go to war, David sent Joab with his officers and all of Israel's army. They defeated the Ammonites and surrounded Rabbah. But David stayed in Jerusalem.

David Sees Bathsheba

2 One evening David got up from his bed and walked on the roof of the king's house. From the roof he saw a woman washing. She was very beautiful.

David Sends for Bathsheba

3 David sent someone to ask about her. The man said, "She is Bathsheba. She is the daughter of Eliam and the wife of Uriah the Hittite."

4 David sent messengers to bring her. She came to him, and he slept with her. She had just finished her monthly washing. Then she went home.

Bathsheba Is Pregnant

5 The woman became pregnant. She sent word to David and said, "I am pregnant."

Uriah Comes to David

6 Then David sent word to Joab: "Send me Uriah the Hittite." Joab sent Uriah to David.

7 When Uriah came, David asked him how Joab was, how the men were, and how the war was going.

David Tries to Send Uriah Home

8 David said to Uriah, "Go home and rest." Uriah left the king's house. A gift from the king was sent after him.

Uriah Will Not Go Home

9 But Uriah slept at the door of the king's house with all his master's servants. He did not go home.

10 David was told that Uriah did not go home. So David asked Uriah, "You just came from a trip. Why didn't you go home?"

11 Uriah said to David, "The ark of God and the people of Israel and Judah are staying in tents. My lord Joab and his men are camped in the fields. How could I go home to eat and drink and lie with my wife? I promise I will not do that!"

Uriah Stays in Jerusalem

12 David said to him, "Stay here one more day. Tomorrow I will send you back." So Uriah stayed in Jerusalem that day and the next.

David Makes Uriah Drunk

13 David invited him to eat and drink. David made him drunk. That evening Uriah went out to sleep on his mat with his master's servants. He did not go home.

David Writes a Letter

14 In the morning David wrote a letter to Joab and sent it with Uriah. 15 In the letter he wrote, "Put Uriah in the front where the fight is hardest. Then pull back from him so he will be hit and die."

Uriah Dies in Battle

16 While Joab was attacking the city, he put Uriah where he knew the strongest enemy soldiers were. 17 The men of the city came out and fought Joab. Some of David's men died. Uriah the Hittite died too.

Joab Sends a Message

18 Joab sent David a full report about the battle.

19 He told the messenger, "When you finish telling the king about the battle, 20 the king may get angry. He may ask, 'Why did you go so close to the city to fight? Didn't you know they shoot arrows from the wall? 21 Who killed Abimelech son of Jerub-Besheth? Didn't a woman throw a heavy stone on him from the wall, so he died in Thebez? Why did you go so near the wall?' Then tell him, 'Your servant Uriah the Hittite is dead too.'"

David Hears the News

22 The messenger went and told David everything Joab had sent him to say.

23 The messenger said to David, "The men were stronger than us. They came out against us in the open field. But we pushed them back to the city gate. 24 Then the archers shot down at your servants from the wall. Some of the king's men died. Your servant Uriah the Hittite is dead too."

25 David said to the messenger, "Tell Joab, 'Do not worry about this. War kills one man as well as another. Fight harder against the city and destroy it.' Tell him this to cheer him up."

David Marries Bathsheba

26 When Uriah's wife heard that her husband was dead, she mourned for him. 27 After her time of sadness was over, David brought her to his house. She became his wife and had a son. But what David had done did not please the LORD.
