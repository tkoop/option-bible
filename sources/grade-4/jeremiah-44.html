Jeremiah Speaks to the Jews in Egypt

1 This message came to Jeremiah for all the Jews living in Egypt—in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Noph, and Pathros. 2 The LORD said, "You have seen all the trouble I brought on Jerusalem and on all the cities of Judah. Look, they are empty today. No one lives there. 3 This happened because of the bad things they did. They made Me angry. They burned incense and served other gods. Those gods were not known by them or by their fathers. 4 I sent all My servants, the prophets. I sent them again and again. I said, 'Do not do this terrible thing that I hate.' 5 But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. They did not turn from their bad ways. They kept burning incense to other gods. 6 So My anger flared up. It burned in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem. They became empty ruins, as they are today."

God Warns Them Again

7 The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "Why are you doing such a bad thing to yourselves? Why are you cutting off the men and women, children and babies of Judah? Then no one will be left. 8 You make Me angry with what your hands do. You burn incense to other gods in Egypt, where you have come to live. You do this so you will be cut off. You will become a curse and a shame among all the nations. 9 Have you forgotten the bad things your fathers did? Have you forgotten the bad things the kings of Judah did? Have you forgotten the bad things their wives did? Have you forgotten your own bad things and your wives' bad things? They did these things in Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. 10 To this day they have not been sorry. They have not shown respect. They have not followed My law and My rules that I gave to you and your fathers."

God Promises Judgment

11 So the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "I am turning against you for trouble. I will cut off all of Judah. 12 I will take the rest of Judah who decided to go to Egypt to live there. They will all die in Egypt. They will die by the sword and by hunger. From the least to the greatest, they will die. They will become a curse, a horror, and a shame. 13 I will punish the people who live in Egypt, just as I punished Jerusalem. They will die by the sword, by hunger, and by disease. 14 None of the rest of Judah who came to Egypt to live there will escape or survive. No one will go back to Judah, except a few who escape."

The People Refuse to Listen

15 Then all the men who knew their wives burned incense to other gods, and all the women standing there in the large crowd, and all the people living in Egypt in Pathros, answered Jeremiah. They said,

16 "We will not listen to you or to the word you spoke to us in the name of the LORD. 17 We will do everything we said we would do. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven. We will pour out drink offerings to her. We and our fathers, our kings and our leaders, did this in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Then we had plenty of bread. We were well off, and we saw no trouble. 18 But since we stopped burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything. We have been killed by the sword and by hunger."

19 The women said, "When we burned incense to the Queen of Heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, did we make her cakes and pour out drink offerings without our husbands knowing?"

Jeremiah Answers the People

20 Then Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men and women who had answered him, 21 "The incense you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem—you and your fathers, your kings, your leaders, and all the people—did not the LORD remember it? Did it not come to His mind? 22 The LORD could not stand it anymore because of your evil deeds and the terrible things you did. So your land became empty ruins and a horror. It became a curse, with no one living there, as it is today. 23 This happened because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD. You did not obey the LORD. You did not follow His law, His rules, or His teachings. So this trouble came on you, as it has today."

God's Final Warning

24 Jeremiah said to all the people and to the women, "Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah in Egypt."

25 The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "You and your wives have said with your mouths and done with your hands what you promised. You said, 'We will keep our promises. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her.' So go ahead. Keep your promises. Do what you said. 26 But now hear the word of the LORD, all Judah living in Egypt. I have sworn by My great name, says the LORD, that no one in Judah living in Egypt will ever again use My name to make a promise. No one will say, 'As the Lord the LORD lives.' 27 I am watching over them for trouble, not for good. All the men of Judah in Egypt will die by the sword and by hunger until they are gone. 28 Only a few will escape the sword and return from Egypt to Judah. Then all the rest of Judah who came to Egypt to live will know whose word is true—Mine or theirs. 29 This will be a sign to you that I will punish you in this place. Then you will know that My words will come true and bring trouble. 30 I will give Pharaoh Hophra, king of Egypt, to his enemies and to those who want to kill him. I did the same thing to Zedekiah, king of Judah. I gave him to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, his enemy who wanted to kill him."
