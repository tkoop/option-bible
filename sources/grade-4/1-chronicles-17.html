David Wants to Build a House for God

1 David was living in his house. He said to Nathan the prophet, “Look, I live in a house made of cedar. But the ark of the LORD’s agreement is in a tent.”

2 Nathan said to David, “Do everything you want to do, because God is with you.”

3 That night God spoke to Nathan.

4 He said, “Go and tell My servant David what the LORD says: You will not build a house for Me to live in.

5 “I have not lived in a house since the day I brought Israel up. I have moved from tent to tent and from place to place. 6 Wherever I went with all Israel, I never asked one of Israel’s judges, whom I told to take care of My people, why he had not built Me a house of cedar.

7 “Now tell him what the LORD of Armies says: I took you from the sheep pen, from following the sheep, to rule over My people Israel. 8 I was with you wherever you went. I defeated all your enemies in front of you. I made your name great, like the names of the great people on earth. 9 I will give My people Israel a place to live. I will plant them there. They will live in their own place and not be afraid anymore. Wicked people will not hurt them again, as they did before.

10 “For the days when I gave judges over My people Israel, I brought down all your enemies. I tell you that the LORD will build you a house. 11 When your days are over and you go to be with your fathers, I will raise up your child after you, one of your sons. I will make his kingdom strong.

12 “He will build a house for Me. I will make his throne last forever. 13 I will be his Father, and he will be My son. I will not take My faithful love away from him, as I took it away from the one before you. 14 I will put him in My house and in My kingdom forever. His throne will be set up forever.”

15 Nathan told David all these words and this vision.

David Prays to God

16 King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, “Who am I, O the LORD God, and what is my family, that You have brought me this far?

17 “This was a small thing in Your eyes, God. You have spoken about Your servant’s family far into the future. You have treated me as a person of high rank, O the LORD God.

18 “What more can David say to You about Your servant? You know Your servant.

19 “The LORD, You did all this great thing for Your servant, following Your own heart, to make all these great things known.

20 “The LORD, there is no one like You. There is no God but You. We have heard it with our own ears.

21 “And who is like Your people Israel? They are the one nation on earth that God went to rescue as His own people. You made Your name great and wonderful by driving out nations before Your people, whom You rescued from Egypt.

22 “You made Your people Israel Your own people forever. And You, the LORD, became their God.

23 “Now, the LORD, let the word You spoke about Your servant and his family come true forever. Do as You have said.

24 “Let Your name be strong and great forever. People will say, ‘The LORD of Armies is the God of Israel. He is God to Israel.’ And the house of Your servant David will be set up before You.

25 “You, my God, have shown Your servant that You will build him a house. That is why Your servant has found courage to pray to You.

26 “Now, the LORD, You are God. You have promised this good thing to Your servant.

27 “Now You have been pleased to bless Your servant’s house, so it will last forever before You. For You, the LORD, have blessed it, and it will be blessed forever.”
