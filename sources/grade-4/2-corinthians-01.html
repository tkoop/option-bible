Paul Greets God's People

1 Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, sent by God's will, and Timothy our brother, write to God's church in Corinth. We also write to all God's people in Achaia. 2 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

God Gives Comfort

3 Praise God! He is the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. He is the Father who shows mercy. He is the God who gives comfort. 4 He comforts us when we have trouble. Then we can comfort others in any trouble. We use the comfort God gave us. 5 Christ suffered, and His suffering comes to us. But through Christ, God's comfort comes to us too, and it is even more. 6 If we are in trouble, it is to help you. It brings you comfort and saves you. If God comforts us, it is also to help you. It helps you stay strong when you suffer the same things we suffer. 7 Our hope for you is strong. We know this: as you share our suffering, you also share God's comfort.

Paul's Hard Time in Asia

8 Brothers, we want you to know about the trouble we had in Asia. It was very hard. It was more than we could handle. We even thought we might die. 9 We thought we were going to die. This taught us not to trust ourselves. We trust God instead. He raises the dead. 10 He saved us from great danger of death. He will save us again. We put our hope in Him. 11 You help us by praying for us. Then many people will thank God for the gift He gave us. They will thank Him because many prayed.

Paul's Good Conscience

12 We are proud of this. Our own hearts tell us it is true. We lived in this world in a holy and honest way before God. We did this even more with you. We did not use human wisdom. We used God's grace. 13 We write only what you can read and understand. I hope you will understand us fully. 14 You already understand us a little. We are proud of you, just as you will be proud of us on the day of our Lord Jesus.

Paul Changes His Travel Plans

15 Because I trusted this, I wanted to visit you first. Then you would have a second blessing. 16 I planned to go through you to Macedonia. Then I would come back to you from Macedonia. Then you would send me to Judea. 17 Did I make this plan lightly? Do I plan like people do in this world? Do I say yes and then no? 18 But God is faithful. Our message to you is not yes and no. 19 God's Son, Jesus Christ, was preached to you by us. Silvanus, Timothy, and I preached Him. He is not yes and no. In Him, the answer is always yes. 20 All of God's promises are yes in Him. So through Him we say Amen to God's glory. 21 God makes us strong with you in Christ. He chose us for His work. 22 He put His mark on us. He gave us the Holy Spirit in our hearts. The Spirit is our promise of more to come. 23 I ask God to be my witness. I did not come to Corinth again because I wanted to spare you. 24 We do not rule over your faith. We work with you for your joy. You stand strong by faith.
