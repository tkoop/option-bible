The Priests' Clothes

1 They used blue, purple, and red yarn to make clothes for serving in the holy place. They also made holy clothes for Aaron, just as the LORD had told Moses.

The Ephod

2 He made the ephod from gold, blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen. 3 They hammered gold into thin sheets and cut it into threads. They worked the gold into the blue, purple, and red yarn, and the fine linen. A skilled worker did this. 4 They made shoulder pieces for the ephod. The pieces joined at its two ends. 5 The waistband of the ephod was made the same way. It was one piece with the ephod. It had gold, blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses. 6 They put onyx stones in gold settings. They carved the stones like a seal with the names of the sons of Israel. 7 They fastened the stones to the shoulder pieces of the ephod. They were memory stones for the sons of Israel. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses.

The Breastpiece

8 He made the breastpiece the same way as the ephod. It had gold, blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen. 9 It was square when folded. It was one span long and one span wide. 10 They put four rows of stones in it. The first row had carnelian, chrysolite, and emerald. 11 The second row had turquoise, sapphire, and diamond. 12 The third row had jacinth, agate, and amethyst. 13 The fourth row had beryl, onyx, and jasper. The stones were set in gold. 14 The twelve stones stood for the twelve sons of Israel. Each stone had one name carved on it like a seal. There was one name for each tribe.

15 They made braided chains of pure gold for the breastpiece. They were like cords. 16 They made two gold settings and two gold rings. They fastened the rings to the two corners of the breastpiece. 17 They put the two gold chains on the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece. 18 They fastened the other ends of the chains to the two settings. They put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod in front. 19 They made two more gold rings. They put them on the other two corners of the breastpiece, on the side next to the ephod. 20 They made two more gold rings. They put them on the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front. They were near the seam, above the waistband of the ephod. 21 They tied the breastpiece to the ephod with a blue cord. The rings of the breastpiece joined the rings of the ephod. The breastpiece stayed above the waistband of the ephod. It did not swing away from the ephod. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses.

The Robe

22 They made the robe for the ephod from blue cloth. A weaver made it. 23 The neck opening was in the middle of the top. It had a woven edge around it so it would not tear. 24 They made pomegranates from blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen. They put them around the bottom edge of the robe. 25 They made bells of pure gold. They put the bells between the pomegranates around the bottom edge of the robe. 26 The bells and pomegranates went around the bottom edge of the robe. This robe was for serving. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses.

Other Priest Clothes

27 They made tunics of fine linen for Aaron and his sons. A weaver made them. 28 They made the turban of fine linen. They made the headbands of fine linen. They made the linen underclothes of fine linen. 29 They made the sash of fine linen with blue, purple, and red yarn. An embroiderer made it. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses.

The Holy Crown

30 They made the holy crown plate from pure gold. They carved on it like a seal: HOLY TO the LORD. 31 They put a blue cord on it. They used it to fasten the plate to the turban. They did this just as the LORD had told Moses.

All the Work Is Done

32 So all the work on the holy tent, the meeting tent, was done. The Israelites did all this just as the LORD had told Moses.

They Bring Everything to Moses

33 Then they brought the holy tent to Moses. They brought the tent and all its things. They brought its hooks, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases. 34 They brought the red ram skin covering, the fine leather covering, and the curtain. 35 They brought the ark of the covenant with its poles and the lid.

36 They brought the table with all its tools and the special bread. 37 They brought the pure gold lampstand with its lamps. They brought the lamps on the branches and all its tools. They brought the oil for the light. 38 They brought the gold altar, the anointing oil, the sweet-smelling incense, and the curtain for the tent door.

39 They brought the bronze altar with its bronze grate, its poles, and all its tools. They brought the washbasin with its stand. 40 They brought the curtains for the courtyard with its posts and bases. They brought the curtain for the courtyard gate. They brought its ropes and tent pegs. They brought all the tools for serving in the holy tent, the meeting tent. 41 They brought the woven clothes for serving in the holy place. They brought the holy clothes for Aaron the priest. They brought the clothes for his sons to serve as priests.

Moses Blesses the People

42 The Israelites had done all the work just as the LORD had told Moses. 43 Moses looked at all the work. He saw that they had done it just as the LORD had told him. So he blessed them.
