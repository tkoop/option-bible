The Fool Says There Is No God

1 The fool says to himself, “There is no God.” They are bad. They do bad things. No one does good.

God Looks at People

2 God looks down from heaven at people. He wants to see if anyone understands and looks for God. 3 They all turned away. Together, they became bad. No one does good. Not even one.

Those Who Do Wrong

4 Will those who do wrong never learn? They hurt my people as if it were nothing. They do not pray to God. 5 There they are, very afraid, even though there was nothing to fear. God scattered the bones of those who attacked His people. He put them to shame, because He rejected them.

Help Comes from Zion

6 I wish Israel’s help would come from Zion! When God brings His people home again, Jacob will be glad. Israel will be happy.
