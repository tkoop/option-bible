Jesus Turns Water into Wine

1 On the third day there was a wedding in Cana in Galilee. Jesus' mother was there. 2 Jesus and His disciples were also invited to the wedding. 3 When the wine ran out, Jesus' mother said to Him, "They have no more wine." 4 Jesus said to her, "Woman, what does that have to do with Me? My time has not come yet."

5 His mother said to the servants, "Do whatever He tells you." 6 There were six stone water jars there. The Jews used them to wash. Each jar held about 20 to 30 gallons. 7 Jesus said to them, "Fill the jars with water." So they filled them to the top. 8 Then He said, "Now draw some out and take it to the man in charge of the feast." So they took it.

9 The man in charge of the feast tasted the water. It had become wine. He did not know where it came from. But the servants who had drawn the water knew. He called the bridegroom. 10 He said, "Everyone serves the best wine first. Then they serve the cheaper wine after people have had enough to drink. But you have kept the best wine until now." 11 This was the first sign Jesus did in Cana in Galilee. It showed His glory. His disciples believed in Him.

12 After this, He went down to Capernaum. His mother, His brothers, and His disciples went with Him. They stayed there for a few days.

Jesus Clears the Temple

13 The Passover of the Jews was near. Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 14 In the temple He found people selling cows, sheep, and doves. He also found money changers sitting there. 15 He made a whip out of cords. He drove everyone out of the temple. He drove out the sheep and cows too. He poured out the money changers' coins. He turned over their tables.

16 He said to the dove sellers, "Take these things away! Do not make My Father's house a place for selling things." 17 His disciples remembered that it is written, "I care so much for Your house that it eats Me up."

18 The Jews answered Him, "What sign do You show us? Why do You do these things?"

19 Jesus answered them, "Destroy this temple. I will raise it up in three days."

20 The Jews said, "It took forty-six years to build this temple. Will You raise it up in three days?"

21 But He was talking about the temple of His body. 22 Later, when He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered what He had said. They believed the Scripture and His words.

23 While He was in Jerusalem for the Passover feast, many people believed in His name. They saw the signs He did. 24 But Jesus did not trust Himself to them. He knew all people. 25 He did not need anyone to tell Him about a person. He knew what was in a person's heart.
