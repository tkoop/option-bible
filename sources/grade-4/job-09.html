Job Answers His Friends

1 Then Job answered:

2 "I know this is true. But how can a person be right with God? 3 If someone wanted to argue with God, he could not answer even one question out of a thousand. 4 God is wise and strong. Who has stood against Him and won?

God Is Very Strong

5 "He moves mountains, and they do not even know it. He turns them over when He is angry. 6 He shakes the earth out of its place. What holds it up trembles. 7 He tells the sun not to shine, and He hides the stars. 8 He alone spreads out the sky. He walks on the waves of the sea. 9 He made the stars named the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the stars in the south. 10 He does great things we cannot understand. He does more wonders than we can count.

God Passes By

11 "If He passes by me, I do not see Him. If He moves on, I do not notice Him. 12 If He takes something away, who can stop Him? Who can ask Him, 'What are You doing?' 13 God does not hold back His anger. Even strong helpers bow down to Him.

Job Says He Cannot Answer God

14 "So how can I answer Him? How can I choose my words with Him? 15 Even if I were right, I could not answer Him. I could only beg my Judge for mercy. 16 If I called Him and He answered, I still would not believe He listened to me. 17 He crushes me in a storm. He gives me many wounds for no reason. 18 He will not let me catch my breath. He fills me with bitter pain. 19 If this is about strength, He is strong! If this is about justice, who can call Him into court? 20 Even if I were right, my own words would show me wrong. Even if I had no blame, He would say I am guilty. 21 I have done no wrong, but I do not know myself. I hate my life.

God Treats All People the Same

22 "It is all the same. So I say, 'He destroys both good people and bad people.' 23 When sudden trouble kills, He laughs at the pain of innocent people. 24 The earth is given to wicked people. He covers the eyes of its judges. If it is not Him, then who is it?

Job's Days Are Short

25 "My days are faster than a runner. They run by without any good. 26 They pass like boats made of reeds, like an eagle diving for food.

Job Is Afraid

27 "If I say, 'I will forget my trouble and smile again,' 28 I still fear all my pain. I know You will not clear me. 29 If I am guilty, why work so hard for nothing? It is useless. 30 Even if I wash with snow water and make my hands clean with soap, 31 You will throw me into a pit. Even my clothes will hate me.

No One Stands Between Job and God

32 "He is not a man like me, so I cannot answer Him. We cannot go to court together. 33 There is no one between us to put a hand on us both. 34 Let Him take away His punishment, so I am not afraid of Him. 35 Then I would speak without being afraid of Him. But now I cannot."
