Jesus Shows Himself by the Sea

1 After this, Jesus showed Himself again to His followers by the Sea of Tiberias. This is how He showed Himself:

2 Simon Peter, Thomas (called Didymus), Nathanael from Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two other followers were together.

3 Simon Peter said, “I am going fishing.”

They said, “We will go with you.” They went out and got into the boat. But they caught nothing that night.

4 Early in the morning, Jesus stood on the shore. But the followers did not know it was Jesus.

5 Jesus said to them, “Children, do you have any fish?”

They answered, “No.”

6 He said, “Throw your net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.”

So they threw it in. Now they could not pull the net in because there were so many fish.

7 The follower Jesus loved said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his coat, because he had taken it off. Then he jumped into the sea.

8 The other followers came in the boat. They pulled the net full of fish. They were not far from land, only about 100 yards away.

9 When they got to land, they saw a fire of hot coals. Fish was on it, and bread.

10 Jesus said, “Bring some of the fish you just caught.”

11 Simon Peter got into the boat and pulled the net to land. It was full of big fish, 153 of them. Even with so many, the net did not break.

12 Jesus said, “Come and eat breakfast.”

None of the followers dared ask Him, “Who are You?” They knew it was the Lord.

13 Jesus came and took the bread and gave it to them. He did the same with the fish.

14 This was the third time Jesus showed Himself to His followers after He was raised from the dead.

Jesus Talks with Peter

15 After they ate, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me more than these?”

Peter said, “Yes, Lord. You know I love You.”

Jesus said, “Feed My lambs.”

16 Jesus said to him again, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me?”

Peter said, “Yes, Lord. You know I love You.”

Jesus said, “Take care of My sheep.”

17 A third time Jesus said, “Simon, son of John, do you love Me?”

Peter was sad because Jesus asked him a third time, “Do you love Me?”

Peter said, “Lord, You know everything. You know I love You.”

Jesus said, “Feed My sheep.”

18 Jesus said, “I tell you the truth. When you were young, you put on your own clothes and went where you wanted. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands. Someone else will dress you and take you where you do not want to go.”

19 Jesus said this to show how Peter would die and honor God. Then He said, “Follow Me.”

The Follower Jesus Loved

20 Peter turned around. He saw the follower Jesus loved coming behind them. This was the one who had leaned on Jesus at dinner and asked, “Lord, who will turn against You?”

21 Peter saw him and said to Jesus, “Lord, what about him?”

22 Jesus said, “If I want him to stay until I come, what is that to you? You follow Me.”

23 So people said the follower would not die. But Jesus did not say he would not die. He said, “If I want him to stay until I come, what is that to you?”

24 This is the follower who tells about these things. He wrote them down. We know what he says is true.

25 Jesus did many other things too. If all of them were written down, one by one, I think the whole world would not have room for the books.
