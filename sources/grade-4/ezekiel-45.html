The Holy Land for God's House

1 When you divide the land for your families, you must give a special gift to the LORD. It will be a holy part of the land. It will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. It will be holy all around. 2 From this, a square piece 500 by 500 cubits will be for the holy place. There will be 50 cubits of open land around it. 3 From this measured land, measure 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. The holy place and the Most Holy Place will be there. 4 This holy part of the land will be for the priests who serve in the holy place and come near to serve the LORD. It will be a place for their homes and a holy space for the temple. 5 An area 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide will be for the Levites who serve in the temple. It will be their land, with twenty rooms. 6 You will give the city land 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, next to the holy part. It will be for all the people of Israel.

Land for the Prince

7 The prince will have land on both sides of the holy part and the city land. One part will reach the west edge, and the other will reach the east edge. Both will run the full length from west to east. 8 This land in Israel will be his. My princes will not treat My people badly anymore. They will share the land with the people of Israel by their tribes.

Fair Leaders and Fair Weights

9 This is what the Lord GOD says: “Enough, princes of Israel! Stop hurting people and stealing. Do what is right and fair. Stop taking land from My people, declares the Lord GOD.” 10 You must use fair scales, fair ephahs, and fair baths. 11 The ephah and the bath must be the same size. The bath is one tenth of a homer. The ephah is one tenth of a homer. Their size will follow the homer. 12 The shekel will be twenty gerahs. Your mina will be sixty shekels: twenty, twenty-five, and fifteen.

Gifts and Offerings

13 This is the gift you will give: one sixth of an ephah from a homer of wheat, and one sixth of an ephah from a homer of barley. 14 The rule for oil is this: give one tenth of a bath for each cor. A cor is ten baths. 15 Give one sheep from every two hundred sheep from Israel's good fields. This is for the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offering. It will help make things right for the people, says the Lord GOD. 16 All the people of the land will give this gift to the prince in Israel. 17 The prince will give the burnt offerings, the grain offerings, and the drink offerings at the feasts, the new moons, the Sabbath days, and all the special days of the people of Israel. He will give the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offering to help make things right for the people of Israel.

Cleaning the Temple

18 This is what the Lord GOD says: “On the first day of the first month, take a young bull with no bad spot. Use it to clean the holy place.” 19 The priest will take some blood from the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the temple, on the four corners of the altar court, and on the doorposts of the inner gate. 20 You will do the same on the seventh day of the month for anyone who sins by mistake or without knowing. This will help make things right for the temple.

Passover and Other Feasts

21 On the fourteenth day of the first month, you will have the Passover. It is a feast of seven days. People will eat bread with no yeast. 22 On that day, the prince will give a bull for himself and for all the people as a sin offering. 23 For the seven days of the feast, he will give seven bulls and seven rams with no bad spots each day as a burnt offering to the LORD. He will also give a male goat each day as a sin offering. 24 He will give a grain offering: one ephah for the bull, one ephah for the ram, and one hin of oil for each ephah. 25 On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, at the feast, he will do the same for seven days. He will give the sin offering, the burnt offering, the grain offering, and the oil.
