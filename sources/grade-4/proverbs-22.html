A Good Name

1 A good name is better than lots of money. Being liked is better than silver and gold.

Rich and Poor

2 Rich people and poor people are alike in one way: The LORD made them all. 3 Smart people see trouble and hide. Foolish people keep going and get hurt.

4 Being humble and fearing the LORD brings wealth, honor, and life. 5 Thorns and traps are on the path of a bad person. A person who guards his life stays far away from them. 6 Teach a child the right way to live. Even when he is old, he will not leave it. 7 Rich people rule over poor people. People who borrow money are slaves to those who lend.

8 A person who does wrong will get trouble. His angry power will be destroyed. 9 A kind person will be blessed, because he shares his food with poor people. 10 Send away the person who makes fun of others, and fighting will stop. Arguing and shame will end. 11 A person who loves a clean heart and kind words will have the king as a friend.

12 The eyes of the LORD watch over what is right. But He ruins the words of a person who cannot be trusted. 13 The lazy person says, "There is a lion outside! I will be killed in the streets!" 14 The mouth of a bad woman is a deep trap. A person under the LORD's anger will fall into it. 15 Foolishness is tied to a child's heart. But the rod of discipline will push it far away. 16 A person who hurts poor people to get rich, or gives to rich people, will end up poor.

Listen to Wise Words

17 Turn your ear to hear the words of wise people, and put your heart into learning what I know. 18 It is nice if you keep them inside you, and if they stay on your lips. 19 So that your trust will be in the LORD, I teach you today, even you. 20 Have I not written for you thirty sayings of advice and knowledge? 21 I wrote them to show you true and sure words, so you can give a true answer to those who sent you.

Warnings

22 Do not rob poor people because they are poor. Do not crush hurting people at the gate. 23 For the LORD will stand up for them. He will take from those who rob them. 24 Do not be friends with an angry person. Do not go around with a hot-tempered person. 25 Or you might learn his ways and get caught in a trap. 26 Do not promise to pay for someone else's debt. 27 If you have nothing to pay with, why should your bed be taken from under you? 28 Do not move an old boundary stone that your fathers set up. 29 Do you see a person who is good at his work? He will stand before kings. He will not stand before unknown people.
