Amos Sees Grasshoppers

1 This is what the Lord the LORD showed me. He was making grasshoppers. It was right after the king's workers cut the first grass. The new grass was just starting to grow.

2 When the grasshoppers had eaten all the grass, I said, "Please forgive Jacob, Lord the LORD! He is too small to survive this."

3 The LORD changed His mind. He said, "This will not happen."

Amos Sees Fire

4 This is what the Lord the LORD showed me. He was calling fire to judge. The fire burned the deep water and began to burn the land.

5 Then I said, "Please stop, Lord the LORD! Jacob is too small to survive this."

6 The LORD changed His mind again. He said, "This will not happen either."

Amos Sees a Weight on a String

7 This is what He showed me. The Lord stood by a wall. He held a weight on a string in His hand.

8 The LORD asked me, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A weight on a string." Then the Lord said, "I am putting this test in the middle of My people Israel. I will not spare them anymore. 9 The hilltop altars of Isaac will be ruined. Israel's temples will be destroyed. I will fight against Jeroboam's family with a sword."

Amaziah Sends a Message to the King

10 Then Amaziah, the priest at Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, king of Israel. He said, "Amos is plotting against you among the people. The country cannot stand all his words. 11 He said Amos told you, 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will be taken away from its land.'"

Amaziah Tells Amos to Leave

12 Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, go away! Run to Judah. Earn food there and prophesy there. 13 Do not prophesy at Bethel again. It is the king's holy place and the nation's temple."

Amos Answers Amaziah

14 Amos answered, "I was not a prophet. I was not from a prophet's family. I was a shepherd and a man who picked sycamore figs. 15 But the LORD took me from tending sheep. The LORD said to me, 'Go. Give My messages to My people Israel.' 16 Now listen to the LORD's word. You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel. Do not speak against Isaac's family.' 17 So the LORD says, 'Your wife will sell herself in the city. Your sons and daughters will die by the sword. Your land will be split up with a measuring rope. You will die in an unclean land. Israel will surely be taken from its land.'"
