The LORD Tells Moses What to Do

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. 2 He said, "Take Aaron and his sons with him. Bring the clothes, the special oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of bread without yeast. 3 Bring all the people together at the door of the meeting tent."

Moses Obeys the LORD

4 Moses did what the LORD told him to do. The people came together at the door of the meeting tent. 5 Moses said to them, "This is what the LORD said to do."

Aaron and His Sons Are Washed and Dressed

6 Moses brought Aaron and his sons near. He washed them with water. 7 He put a shirt on Aaron. He tied a belt around him. He put a robe on him. He put a special vest on him. He tied the vest's belt around him and fastened it. 8 He put the chest piece on him. He put the Urim and Thummim inside it. 9 He put the head wrap on Aaron's head. He put the gold plate, the holy crown, on the front of the head wrap. He did this as the LORD had told Moses.

The Meeting Tent and Altar Are Made Holy

10 Moses took the special oil. He poured it on the meeting tent and on everything in it. He made them holy for the LORD. 11 He sprinkled the oil on the altar seven times. He poured oil on the altar and on all its tools, and on the wash bowl and its stand, to make them holy. 12 He poured some of the oil on Aaron's head. He poured it on him to make him holy.

Aaron's Sons Are Dressed

13 Moses brought Aaron's sons near. He put shirts on them. He tied belts around them. He put caps on them. He did this as the LORD had told Moses.

A Bull Is Offered for Sin

14 Moses brought the bull for the sin offering. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the bull's head. 15 Moses killed the bull. He took the blood. He put it on the corners of the altar with his finger. He put blood all around. He poured the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar. This made the altar holy. It paid for sin. 16 Moses took all the fat from inside the bull, the best part of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat. He burned them on the altar. 17 He burned the bull, its skin, its meat, and its waste with fire outside the camp. He did this as the LORD had told Moses.

A Ram Is Burned as an Offering

18 Moses brought the ram for the burnt offering. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the ram's head. 19 Moses killed it. He threw the blood all around the altar. 20 He cut the ram into pieces. He burned the head, the pieces, and the fat. 21 He washed the inside parts and the legs with water. Then Moses burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering. Its smell pleased the LORD. It was a gift burned for the LORD. He did this as the LORD had told Moses.

A Second Ram Makes Them Priests

22 Moses brought the second ram. It was the ram used to make them priests. Aaron and his sons put their hands on the ram's head. 23 Moses killed it. He took some blood and put it on the tip of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 24 He brought Aaron's sons near. He put some blood on the tips of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Moses threw the blood all around the altar. 25 He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat from inside, the best part of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right leg. 26 From the basket of bread without yeast that was before the LORD, he took one loaf of bread without yeast, one loaf with oil, and one thin cake. He put them on the fat and on the right leg. 27 He put all of them on the hands of Aaron and on the hands of his sons. He waved them before the LORD. 28 Moses took them from their hands. He burned them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. This was an offering to make them priests. Its smell pleased the LORD. It was a gift burned for the LORD. 29 Moses took the chest part. He waved it before the LORD. This part was for Moses. It came from the ram used to make them priests. He did this as the LORD had told Moses.

Aaron and His Sons Are Made Holy

30 Moses took some of the special oil and some of the blood from the altar. He sprinkled them on Aaron and on his clothes. He sprinkled them on his sons and on their clothes. He made Aaron and his clothes holy. He made his sons and their clothes holy. 31 Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the door of the meeting tent. Eat it there. Eat the bread in the basket for making priests. I told you to do this. I said, 'Aaron and his sons must eat it.' 32 Burn what is left of the meat and the bread with fire. 33 You must not go out from the door of the meeting tent for seven days, until your time of being made priests is done. It takes seven days to finish. 34 Do this because the LORD told you to do it today. It pays for your sin. 35 Stay at the door of the meeting tent day and night for seven days. Keep the LORD's rule. Then you will not die. I was told to tell you this." 36 Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD told them to do through Moses.
