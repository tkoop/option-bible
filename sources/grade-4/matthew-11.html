Jesus Teaches in the Towns

1 After Jesus finished teaching His twelve disciples, He left there. He went to teach and tell the good news in their towns.

John Asks About Jesus

2 John heard in prison what Christ was doing. 3 He sent his disciples to ask Jesus, "Are You the One who is coming, or should we wait for someone else?"

Jesus Answers John

4 Jesus answered, "Go back and tell John what you hear and see. 5 Blind people can see. People who could not walk can walk. People with skin disease are made clean. Deaf people can hear. Dead people are raised to life. And the good news is told to poor people. 6 Happy is the person who does not turn away because of Me."

Jesus Talks About John

7 As John's disciples were leaving, Jesus began to talk to the crowd about John. "What did you go out into the desert to see? A tall plant shaking in the wind? 8 No? Did you go to see a man in soft clothes? People who wear soft clothes live in kings' houses. 9 Then why did you go? To see a prophet? Yes. I tell you, he is more than a prophet. 10 He is the one the Bible talks about. The Lord says He will send His helper ahead of You. He will make Your road ready before You.

"11 I tell you the truth. No one born from a woman is greater than John the Baptist. But even the smallest person in God's kingdom is greater than John. 12 From the time of John until now, God's kingdom has been pushed hard, and strong people try to take it.

"13 All the prophets and the Law told God's message until John. 14 If you are willing to accept it, he is Elijah who was to come. 15 Anyone who has ears should listen."

Jesus Talks About the People

16 "What can I compare this group of people to? They are like children sitting in the town square. They call to their friends. 17 They say they played a song for them, but the friends did not dance. They say they cried for them, but the friends did not cry with them. 18 John came. He did not eat or drink like others. People say he has a demon. 19 The Son of Man came eating and drinking. People say He eats too much and drinks too much. He is a friend of tax collectors and sinners. But wisdom is shown to be right by what it does."

Jesus Warns Some Towns

20 Then Jesus began to speak against the towns where He had done most of His powerful works, because they did not turn from sin. 21 "It will be bad for you, Chorazin! It will be bad for you, Bethsaida! If the powerful works done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have turned from sin long ago. They would have worn rough clothes and put ashes on themselves to show they were sorry.

"22 But I tell you, on the day God judges, it will be easier for Tyre and Sidon than for you. 23 And you, Capernaum, do you think you will be lifted up to heaven? No, you will go down to the place of the dead! If the powerful works done in you had been done in Sodom, that city would still be here today. 24 But I tell you, on the day God judges, it will be easier for Sodom than for you."

Jesus Thanks His Father

25 "I praise You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You hid these things from wise and smart people and showed them to little children. 26 Yes, Father, this is what You wanted. 27 My Father has given Me all things. No one knows the Son except the Father. No one knows the Father except the Son and anyone the Son chooses to tell.

"28 Come to Me, all you who are tired and carry heavy loads, and I will give you rest. 29 Take My yoke on you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. 30 For My yoke is easy and My load is light."
