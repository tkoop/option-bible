Jesus' Work and Promise

1Dear Theophilus, in my first book I wrote about all the things Jesus began to do and teach. 2He kept teaching until the day He was taken up to heaven. Before that, through the Holy Spirit, He told the apostles He had chosen what to do. 3After He suffered, He showed Himself alive to them in many ways. For forty days He appeared to them and talked about God's kingdom.

4While He was eating with them, He told them not to leave Jerusalem. He said, "Wait for what the Father promised. You heard Me talk about it." 5John baptized with water, but in a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.

6So they asked Him, "Lord, are you going to make Israel a kingdom again now?"

7He said to them, "It is not for you to know the times or dates the Father set by His own power. 8But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you. Then you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."

Jesus Goes Up to Heaven

9After He said this, He was lifted up while they watched. A cloud hid Him from their sight. 10They were looking up into the sky as He went. Suddenly two men in white clothes stood by them. 11They said, "Men of Galilee, why are you standing here looking into the sky? This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come back in the same way you saw Him go."

12Then they went back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives. It was near Jerusalem, not far away.

The Believers Pray Together

13When they arrived, they went upstairs to the room where they were staying. Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James the son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas the son of James were there. 14They all prayed together. The women were there too, and Mary the mother of Jesus, and His brothers.

Peter Speaks to the Believers

15In those days Peter stood up among the believers. There were about 120 people. He said, 16"Brothers, the Holy Spirit said through David long ago that this had to happen. David spoke about Judas, who led the men who arrested Jesus. 17He was one of us. He shared in this work. 18He used the money he got for his wrong to buy a field. He fell headfirst, his body broke open, and his insides spilled out. 19Everyone in Jerusalem heard about it. They called the field in their own language Akeldama, which means Field of Blood.

"20For it is written in the book of Psalms: 'Let his home be empty. Let no one live in it.' And, 'Let someone else take his job.' 21So we need to choose one of the men who were with us the whole time the Lord Jesus was with us. 22He was with us from John's baptism until the day He was taken up to heaven. This man must be a witness with us that Jesus rose from the dead."

Choosing a New Apostle

23They put forward two men: Joseph called Barsabbas (also called Justus) and Matthias. 24They prayed, "Lord, You know everyone's heart. Show us which of these two You have chosen 25to take this job as an apostle, since Judas left it to go where he belonged." 26They cast lots, and Matthias was chosen. So he was added to the eleven apostles.
