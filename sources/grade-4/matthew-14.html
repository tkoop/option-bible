Herod Has John Killed

1 At that time Herod, the ruler, heard about Jesus. 2 He said to his servants, "This is John the Baptist. He has risen from the dead! That is why these powers work in him."

3 Herod had arrested John. He tied him up and put him in prison because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.

4 John had told him, "It is not right for you to have her."

5 Herod wanted to kill John, but he was afraid of the crowd. They thought John was a man from God.

6 On Herod's birthday, Herodias's daughter danced for the guests. Herod was pleased. 7 So he promised to give her whatever she asked.

8 Her mother told her what to ask. She said, "Give me here on a plate the head of John the Baptist."

9 The king was sad. But because of his promises and the guests, he ordered it. 10 He sent men to cut off John's head in prison. 11 His head was brought on a plate and given to the girl. She brought it to her mother.

12 John's followers came. They took his body and buried it. Then they went and told Jesus.

Jesus Feeds a Large Crowd

13 When Jesus heard this, He left there in a boat to a quiet place alone. But the crowds heard and followed Him on foot from the towns.

14 When Jesus came out, He saw a large crowd. He felt sorry for them and healed their sick.

15 In the evening, the followers came to Him and said, "This is a lonely place, and the hour is already late. Send the crowds away so they can go to the villages and buy food."

16 Jesus said, "They do not need to go. You give them something to eat."

17 They said, "We have only five loaves of bread and two fish here."

18 He said, "Bring them here to Me."

19 He told the crowds to sit on the grass. He took the five loaves and two fish. He looked up to heaven and blessed them. He broke the loaves and gave them to the followers. The followers gave them to the crowds.

20 Everyone ate and was full. They picked up twelve baskets full of leftover pieces. 21 About five thousand men ate, not counting women and children.

22 Right away Jesus made the followers get into the boat. He told them to go ahead of Him to the other side while He sent the crowds away.

23 After sending the crowds away, He went up on a mountain by Himself to pray. When evening came, He was there alone.

24 The boat was already far from land. The waves were beating it, because the wind was against it.

25 In the last part of the night, Jesus came to them, walking on the sea.

26 The followers saw Him walking on the sea. They were scared. They said, "It is a ghost!" and cried out in fear.

27 But Jesus spoke to them right away: "Take heart! It is I. Do not be afraid."

28 Peter answered Him, "Lord, if it is You, tell me to come to You on the water."

29 He said, "Come." Peter got out of the boat. He walked on the water and came toward Jesus.

30 But when he saw the strong wind, he was afraid. He began to sink and cried out, "Lord, save me!"

31 Right away Jesus reached out His hand and caught him. He said, "You have little faith. Why did you doubt?"

32 When they got into the boat, the wind stopped. 33 The men in the boat worshiped Him and said, "Truly You are the Son of God."

34 They crossed over and came to land at Gennesaret.

35 The men of that place knew Him. They sent word all around that area and brought all who were sick to Him. 36 They begged Him to let them just touch the edge of His clothes. Everyone who touched it was healed.
