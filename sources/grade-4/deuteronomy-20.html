Going to War

1 When you go out to war against your enemies and see horses and chariots and people more than you, do not be afraid of them, for the LORD your God, who brought you up from the land of Egypt, is with you.

The Priest Speaks to the People

2 When you come near the battle, the priest will go up and speak to the people.

3 He will say to them, "Hear, Israel, today you are coming near to fight your enemies. Do not let your heart be weak. Do not be afraid. Do not panic. Do not be scared of them.

4 "The LORD your God goes with you to fight for you against your enemies and to save you."

Some Soldiers May Go Home

5 Then the officers will speak to the people. They will say, "Who is the man who built a new house but has not used it? Let him go home. He might die in battle, and another man might use it.

6 "Who is the man who planted a grape field but has not eaten from it? Let him go home. He might die in battle, and another man might eat from it.

7 "Who is the man who promised to marry a woman but has not married her? Let him go home. He might die in battle, and another man might marry her."

8 The officers will also speak to the people again. They will say, "Who is the man who is afraid and has a weak heart? Let him go home, so his brothers' hearts will not become weak like his."

9 When the officers finish speaking to the people, they will choose army leaders to lead the people.

Making Peace with a City

10 When you come near a city to fight it, first offer peace to it.

11 If it answers with peace and opens to you, then all the people in it will be your workers and serve you.

12 But if it does not make peace with you, but fights you, then you must surround it.

13 The LORD your God will give it into your hand, and you will strike down all its men with the sword.

14 But you may take the women, children, animals, and all the other things in the city. You may take all of it for you. You may eat the things you took from your enemies, which the LORD your God gave you.

Cities Far Away

15 You must do this to all cities that are very far from you, cities that are not from these nations.

Cities in the Land

16 But in the cities of these people that the LORD your God gives you as your land, do not let anything that breathes live.

17 You must completely destroy them: the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, as the LORD your God commanded you.

18 Do this so they will not teach you to do the bad things they did for their gods. If you do those things, you will sin against the LORD your God.

Do Not Hurt Fruit Trees

19 When you surround a city for many days to fight it and take it, do not destroy its trees by cutting them with an axe. You may eat from them. Do not cut them down. The trees in the field are not people, so you should not fight them while you surround the city.

20 But you may destroy a tree that you know is not a food tree. You may cut it down and build war works against the city that fights you, until it falls.
