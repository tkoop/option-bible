Jesus Leaves Galilee

1 When Jesus finished these words, He left Galilee and went to the area of Judea across the Jordan. 2 Many crowds followed Him, and He healed them there.

The Pharisees Ask About Divorce

3 The Pharisees came to Him to test Him. They asked, "Is it right for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?"

4 He answered, "Haven't you read that from the beginning the Creator made them male and female? 5 He said, 'For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one body.' 6 So they are no longer two. They are one body. So what God has joined together, no person should separate."

7 They said to Him, "Then why did Moses tell us to give a paper that ends a marriage and divorce her?"

8 He said to them, "Moses let you divorce your wives because you were stubborn. But it was not like that from the start. 9 I tell you, anyone who divorces his wife, unless she has been unfaithful, and marries another woman, is guilty of adultery."

10 His disciples said to Him, "If that is how it is between a man and his wife, it is better not to marry."

11 He said to them, "Not everyone can accept this teaching. Only those God gives it to can. 12 Some men are born unable to marry. Some are made that way by people. Some choose this for the kingdom of heaven. Anyone who can accept this should accept it."

Jesus Blesses the Children

13 Then little children were brought to Him so He could put His hands on them and pray. But the disciples told them to stop.

14 Jesus said, "Let the little children come to Me. Do not stop them. The kingdom of heaven belongs to people like them."

15 He put His hands on them and left there.

A Rich Young Man

16 Look, a man came to Him and said, "Teacher, what good thing must I do to have life that never ends?"

17 He said to him, "Why do you ask Me about what is good? Only One is good. But if you want to enter life, keep the commands."

18 He said to Him, "Which ones?"

Jesus said, "Do not murder. Do not commit adultery. Do not steal. Do not lie about others. 19 Honor your father and your mother, and love your neighbor as you love yourself."

20 The young man said to Him, "I have kept all these. What am I still missing?"

21 Jesus said to him, "If you want to be perfect, go sell your things and give to the poor. Then you will have treasure in heaven. Come and follow Me."

22 When the young man heard this, he went away sad, because he had many things.

Jesus Talks About Rich People

23 Jesus said to His disciples, "Truly I tell you, it is hard for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven. 24 Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God."

25 When the disciples heard this, they were very amazed. They said, "Who then can be saved?"

26 Jesus looked at them and said, "For people this is impossible. But with God all things are possible."

27 Then Peter answered Him, "Look, we have left everything and followed you. What will there be for us?"

28 Jesus said to them, "Truly I tell you, in the new world, when the Son of Man sits on His glorious throne, you who have followed Me will also sit on twelve thrones. You will judge the twelve tribes of Israel. 29 And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or wife or children or fields for My name will receive a hundred times as much and will receive life that never ends. 30 But many who are first will be last, and the last will be first."
