Jesus Is Taken to Pilate

1 The whole crowd stood up and led Jesus to Pilate. 2 They began to blame Him. They said, “We found this man turning our people against us. He stops us from paying taxes to Caesar. He says He is the Christ, the King.”

3 Pilate asked Him, “Are you the King of the Jews?”

Jesus answered, “You say so.”

4 Pilate said to the leading priests and the crowd, “I see no wrong in this man.”

5 But they kept saying, “He stirs up the people all over Judea. He started in Galilee and has come here.”

Jesus Is Sent to Herod

6 When Pilate heard this, he asked if the man was from Galilee. 7 He learned that Jesus was in Herod’s area. So he sent Jesus to Herod, who was in Jerusalem at that time.

8 Herod was very glad to see Jesus. He had wanted to see Him for a long time. He had heard about Him and hoped to see Him do a miracle. 9 He asked Jesus many questions, but Jesus did not answer.

10 The leading priests and teachers of the law stood there. They spoke against Him. 11 Herod and his soldiers made fun of Jesus. They put a bright robe on Him and sent Him back to Pilate. 12 That day Herod and Pilate became friends. Before, they had been enemies.

Pilate Tries to Let Jesus Go

13 Pilate called the leading priests, the rulers, and the people together. 14 He said to them, “You brought this man to me. You say he turns people away. I have questioned him in front of you. I see no wrong in him for the things you say he did. 15 Herod did not find wrong either. He sent him back to us. See, he has done nothing that should get him killed. 16 So I will punish him and let him go.”

The Crowd Chooses Barabbas

18 But they all shouted, “Take this man away! Let Barabbas go free!” 19 Barabbas was in prison for a riot and murder.

20 Pilate wanted to let Jesus go. He spoke to them again. 21 But they shouted, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!”

22 A third time he said to them, “What wrong has this man done? I see no reason to kill Him. So I will punish Him and let Him go.” 23 But they kept shouting loudly to crucify Him. Their shouting won.

24 Pilate gave in to what they wanted. 25 He let the man they asked for go free. That man was in prison for riot and murder. He handed Jesus over to them.

Jesus Carries His Cross

26 As they led Him away, they grabbed Simon from Cyrene. He was coming from the country. They put the cross on him to carry behind Jesus. 27 A large crowd followed Him. Women were there too. They cried and mourned for Him.

28 Jesus turned to them and said, “Women of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me. Weep for yourselves and your children. 29 Days are coming when people will say, ‘Happy are women who never had children, who never fed babies.’ 30 Then they will say to the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and to the hills, ‘Cover us!’ 31 If they hurt Me while I am innocent, what will happen to you?”

Jesus Is Crucified

32 Two other criminals were led out to be killed with Him. 33 When they came to the place called Skull, they nailed Him to a cross there. They also nailed the criminals to crosses, one on His right and one on His left.

34 Jesus said, “Father, forgive them. They do not know what they are doing.” They divided His clothes and drew straws.

35 The people stood and watched. The rulers laughed at Him. They said, “He saved others. Let Him save Himself, if He is God’s chosen Christ.”

36 The soldiers also made fun of Him. They came and offered Him sour wine. 37 They said, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!”

38 A sign above Him said, “This is the King of the Jews.”

39 One of the criminals hanging there insulted Him. He said, “Are you not the Christ? Save yourself and us!”

40 The other criminal answered and told him to stop. He said, “Don’t you fear God? We are under the same punishment. 41 We deserve it. We are getting what we deserve for what we did. But this man did nothing wrong.”

42 Then he said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.”

43 Jesus said to him, “I tell you the truth, today you will be with Me in Paradise.”

Jesus Dies on the Cross

44 It was about noon. Darkness came over the whole land until three in the afternoon. 45 The sun stopped shining. The curtain in the temple tore in two.

46 Jesus cried out in a loud voice, “Father, I put My spirit in your hands.” Then He breathed His last.

47 The army officer saw what happened. He praised God and said, “Surely this man was innocent.” 48 All the crowds who came to see this sight saw what happened. They went home beating their chests. 49 All His friends and the women who had followed Him from Galilee stood far away and watched.

Jesus Is Buried

50 There was a man named Joseph. He was a good and fair man. He was a member of the council. 51 He had not agreed with their plan and action. He was from the Jewish town of Arimathea. He was waiting for God’s kingdom.

52 He went to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body. 53 He took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid it in a tomb cut in rock. No one had ever been laid there.

54 It was the day before the Sabbath. The Sabbath was about to begin. 55 The women who had come with Him from Galilee followed. They saw the tomb and how His body was laid. 56 Then they went home and prepared spices and perfumes. They rested on the Sabbath, as the law said.
