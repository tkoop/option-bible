Should We Keep Sinning?

1 What should we say? Should we keep on sinning so God's grace can grow? 2 No! We died to sin. How can we still live in it?

Baptized into Christ's Death

3 Don't you know this? All of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His death. 4 So we were buried with Him through baptism into death. Christ was raised from the dead by the Father's glory. In the same way, we can live a new life. 5 If we have been joined to Him in His death, we will also be joined to Him when He rises.

6 We know our old self was nailed to the cross with Him. This was so the power of sin would be broken. Then we would not be slaves to sin. 7 Anyone who has died is free from sin. 8 If we died with Christ, we believe we will also live with Him.

9 We know Christ was raised from the dead. He will never die again. Death has no power over Him. 10 The death He died, He died to sin once for all. The life He lives, He lives to God.

Alive to God

11 In the same way, think of yourselves as dead to sin but alive to God in Christ Jesus. 12 So don't let sin rule in your body that will die. Don't obey its wants. 13 Don't offer your body parts to sin as tools for wrong. Instead, offer yourselves to God as people alive from the dead. Offer your body parts to God as tools for right.

14 Sin will not rule you, because you are not under law but under grace.

Slaves to What We Obey

15 What then? Should we sin because we are not under law but under grace? No! 16 Don't you know that when you offer yourselves to obey someone, you become slaves to the one you obey? You are slaves either to sin, which leads to death, or to obeying, which leads to being right with God.

17 Thank God! You used to be slaves to sin. But now you have obeyed from the heart the teaching you were given. 18 You were set free from sin and became slaves to doing right.

19 I am using human words because you are weak. Just as you once gave your body parts as slaves to dirty things and evil, now give your body parts as slaves to doing right. This leads to holy living. 20 When you were slaves to sin, you were free from doing right. 21 What good did you get then? You are now ashamed of those things, and they end in death. 22 But now you are free from sin and slaves to God. You get fruit that leads to holy living, and the end is life forever.

Sin Pays Death, God Gives Life

23 The pay of sin is death, but God's gift is life forever in Christ Jesus our Lord.
