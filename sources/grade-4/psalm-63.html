A Song of David in the Desert of Judah

1 God, You are my God. I look for You with all my heart. I am thirsty for You. My body is weak for You. It is like a dry, tired land with no water. 2 So I saw You in the holy place. I saw Your power and Your glory.

Praising God

3 Your kind love is better than life. My lips will praise You. 4 So I will bless You while I live. I will lift my hands in Your name. 5 I will be as full as after a big feast. My mouth will praise You with happy lips.

Thinking About God at Night

6 When I am in bed, I remember You. I think about You at night. 7 You have helped me. I will be happy in the shadow of Your wings. 8 I hold tight to You. Your right hand holds me up.

God Keeps David Safe

9 But the people who want to kill me will go deep into the ground. 10 They will be killed by swords. Wild animals will eat them. 11 But the king will be glad in God. Everyone who makes a promise in His name will praise Him. People who lie will stop talking.
