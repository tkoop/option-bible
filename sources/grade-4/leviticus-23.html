The LORD’s Special Times

1 The LORD said to Moses,

2 “Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘These are My special times. They are the the LORD’s special times, and you are to call them holy meetings. 3 You may work for six days. But the seventh day is a Sabbath. It is a day of complete rest and a holy meeting. You must not do any work. No matter where you live, it is a Sabbath to the the LORD. 4 These are the the LORD’s special times. They are holy meetings that you must call at the right time. 5 The the LORD’s Passover starts at sunset on the fourteenth day of the first month. 6 On the fifteenth day of that month, the Feast of Bread Without Yeast to the the LORD begins. For seven days you must eat bread without yeast. 7 On the first day, you are to have a holy meeting. You must not do any regular work. 8 For seven days, you are to bring a fire offering to the the LORD. On the seventh day, you are to have a holy meeting. You must not do any regular work.’”

First Crops

9 The the LORD also said to Moses,

10 “Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘When you come to the land I am giving you, and you gather its grain, you are to bring the first bundle of your grain to the priest. 11 The priest will lift up the bundle before the the LORD so He will accept it for you. He must lift it up on the day after the Sabbath. 12 On the day you lift up the bundle, you are to offer a one-year-old lamb that has no flaw. It is a burnt offering to the the LORD. 13 You must also bring a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. It is a fire offering to the the LORD. It has a smell that pleases Him. You must also bring a drink offering of one-fourth of a hin of wine. 14 You must not eat any bread, roasted grain, or fresh grain until the very day you bring this offering to your God. This is a rule for all time for your families, no matter where you live. 15 Starting on the day after the Sabbath, the day you brought the lifted bundle, you are to count seven full weeks. 16 You must count fifty days, until the day after the seventh Sabbath. Then you are to bring a new grain offering to the the LORD. 17 Bring two loaves of bread from where you live. They are a lifted offering. They are made from two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour and baked with yeast. They are first crops for the the LORD. 18 With the bread, you are to bring seven one-year-old male lambs with no flaw, one young bull, and two rams. They are a burnt offering to the the LORD. You must also bring their grain offerings and drink offerings. It is a fire offering with a smell that pleases the the LORD. 19 You are also to offer one male goat as a sin offering, and two one-year-old male lambs as a peace offering. 20 The priest will lift up the lambs before the the LORD with the first-crop bread. The bread and the two lambs are set apart for the the LORD for the priest. 21 On that same day, you are to call a holy meeting. You must not do any regular work. This is a rule for all time, no matter where you live, for your families. 22 When you gather the grain of your land, do not gather all the way to the edge of your field. Do not pick up what is left. Leave it for poor people and for outsiders. I am the the LORD your God.’”

A Day of Loud Horns

23 The the LORD also said to Moses,

24 “Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘On the first day of the seventh month, you are to have a day of rest. It is a holy meeting announced with loud horn blasts. 25 You must not do any regular work. You are to bring a fire offering to the the LORD.’”

The Day to Make Things Right

26 Then the the LORD said to Moses,

27 “The tenth day of this seventh month is the Day to Make Things Right. You are to have a holy meeting. You must make yourselves humble. You are to bring a fire offering to the the LORD. 28 On this day you must not do any work. It is the Day to Make Things Right. It makes things right for you before the the LORD your God. 29 If anyone does not make himself humble on this day, he must be cut off from his people. 30 I will wipe out anyone who does any work on this day. 31 You must not do any work at all. This is a rule for all time, no matter where you live, for your families. 32 It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you. You must make yourselves humble. You are to rest from the evening of the ninth day of the month until the next evening.”

The Feast of Tents

33 The the LORD also said to Moses,

34 “Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, the Feast of Tents to the the LORD begins. It lasts for seven days. 35 On the first day, you are to have a holy meeting. You must not do any regular work. 36 For seven days, you are to bring a fire offering to the the LORD. On the eighth day, you are to have a holy meeting. You are to bring a fire offering to the the LORD. It is a special meeting. You must not do any regular work. 37 These are the the LORD’s special times. You are to call them holy meetings. Bring fire offerings to the the LORD. Bring burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices, and drink offerings, each on its own day. 38 These offerings are in addition to the the LORD’s Sabbaths. They are also in addition to your gifts, your promises, and your free gifts that you give to the the LORD. 39 On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered the crops of your land, you are to celebrate a feast to the the LORD for seven days. On the first day, you are to rest completely. On the eighth day, you are to rest completely. 40 On the first day, you are to take fruit from good trees, palm branches, leafy branches, and willow branches from the stream. You are to be glad before the the LORD your God for seven days. 41 You are to celebrate this feast to the the LORD for seven days each year. This is a rule for all time. You are to celebrate it in the seventh month. 42 You are to live in tents for seven days. Every Israelite born in the land must live in tents. 43 Do this so that your families will know that I made the Israelites live in tents when I brought them out of Egypt. I am the the LORD your God.’”

44 So Moses told the Israelites about the the LORD’s special times.
