What Our Fathers Told Us

1 We heard it ourselves, God. Our fathers told us what You did long ago. 2 You pushed out other nations by Your power and planted Your people there. You crushed other groups and sent them away. 3 They did not take the land by their own power. Their own strength did not save them. It was Your power and Your strength and Your favor, because You were pleased with them. 4 You are my King, God. Please give help to Jacob. 5 With Your help we will push back our enemies. In Your name we will walk over those who fight us. 6 I will not trust my bow. My sword will not save me. 7 You saved us from our enemies. You made those who hate us feel ashamed. 8 We praise God all day long. We will thank Your name forever.

But Now We Feel Left Alone

9 But now You have pushed us away and put us to shame. You do not go out with our armies. 10 You make us turn back from our enemies. Those who hate us take our things. 11 You let us be like sheep for food. You scattered us among the nations. 12 You sold Your people for almost nothing. You did not get much money for us. 13 You made us a shame to our neighbors. People all around us laugh at us and make fun of us. 14 You made us a bad example to other nations. People everywhere shake their heads at us. 15 All day long I feel ashamed. Shame covers me. 16 I hear people tease and say bad things. I see the enemy and the one who wants to hurt us.

We Have Not Forgotten You

17 All this happened to us, but we did not forget You. We did not lie about Your promise. 18 Our hearts did not turn away. Our steps did not wander from Your path. 19 Yet You crushed us in a place of wild animals. You covered us with deep darkness. 20 If we forgot the name of our God, or prayed to another god, 21 would not God find out? He knows the secrets of our hearts. 22 But for Your sake we are killed all day long. People count us like sheep to be killed.

Please Wake Up and Help Us

23 Wake up, Lord! Why do You sleep? Get up! Do not leave us forever. 24 Why do You hide Your face? Why do You forget our trouble and our hard times? 25 We are pushed down to the dust. Our bodies stick to the ground. 26 Get up and help us. Save us because of Your love.
