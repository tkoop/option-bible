Israel Does Wrong Again

1 After Ehud died, the people of Israel did wrong again. They did what the LORD saw as bad.

2 So the LORD let Jabin, king of Canaan, rule over them. Jabin ruled in Hazor. His army boss was Sisera. Sisera lived in Harosheth Haggoyim.

3 The people of Israel cried out to the LORD. Jabin had 900 iron war carts. He was very mean to Israel for 20 years.

Deborah, a Woman Who Spoke for God

4 At that time, Deborah was a leader for Israel. She was a woman who spoke for God. She was the wife of Lappidoth.

5 She sat under Deborah's palm tree. It was between Ramah and Bethel, in the hills of Ephraim. The people of Israel came to her to settle their problems.

Deborah Calls Barak

6 She sent for Barak, the son of Abinoam. He was from Kedesh in Naphtali. She said to him, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has told you to go. Take 10,000 men from Naphtali and Zebulun. Go up to Mount Tabor. 7 I will bring Sisera, Jabin's army boss, with his war carts and his men to the Kishon River. I will give him to you."

8 Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go. If you do not go with me, I will not go."

9 "I will go with you," Deborah said. "But the way you are going, you will not get the praise. The LORD will let a woman beat Sisera." Then Deborah went with Barak to Kedesh.

10 There Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali. Ten thousand men went up with him. Deborah went up too.

11 Heber the Kenite had left the other Kenites. They came from Hobab, Moses' father-in-law. Heber put his tent by the big tree in Zaanannim, near Kedesh.

12 People told Sisera that Barak, son of Abinoam, had gone up to Mount Tabor.

13 So Sisera called all his war carts. There were 900 iron carts. He called all his men from Harosheth Haggoyim to the Kishon River.

Barak Goes to Battle

14 Deborah said to Barak, "Get up! Today the LORD has given Sisera to you. The LORD has gone ahead of you." So Barak went down Mount Tabor. Ten thousand men followed him.

15 The LORD threw Sisera's army into a panic. Barak's men beat them with swords. Sisera got down from his war cart and ran away on foot.

16 Barak chased the war carts and the army to Harosheth Haggoyim. All of Sisera's men fell by the sword. Not one was left.

Sisera Runs to Jael's Tent

17 But Sisera ran on foot to Jael's tent. She was Heber's wife. Jabin, king of Hazor, and Heber's family had a peace deal.

18 Jael went out to meet Sisera. She said, "Come in, my lord. Come in. Do not be afraid." He went into her tent. She covered him with a blanket.

19 He said, "I am thirsty. Please give me a little water." She opened a bottle of milk. She gave him a drink. Then she covered him again.

20 He told her, "Stand at the tent door. If a man comes and asks you, 'Is anyone here?' say, 'No.'"

21 But Jael, Heber's wife, took a tent peg and a hammer. She went to him quietly. He was fast asleep and very tired. She hit the peg into the side of his head. It went into the ground. He died.

22 Just then Barak came by. He was chasing Sisera. Jael went out to meet him. She said, "Come. I will show you the man you want." He went in with her. There was Sisera, dead. The tent peg was in the side of his head.

Israel Wins

23 That day God brought down Jabin, king of Canaan, for the people of Israel.

24 The people of Israel kept getting stronger against Jabin, king of Canaan. They destroyed him.
