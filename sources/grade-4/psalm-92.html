A Song for the Sabbath Day

1 It is good to thank the LORD. It is good to sing to Your name, O Most High. 2 It is good to tell of Your love in the morning. It is good to tell of Your care at night. 3 We praise You with a ten-string harp, a lyre, and soft music.

God Made Me Glad

4 For You, the LORD, made me happy by what You did. I will sing for joy at the work of Your hands. 5 How great are Your works, the LORD! Your thoughts are very deep.

Bad People Will Not Last

6 A foolish person does not know this. A silly person does not understand it. 7 Bad people grow fast like grass. All who do wrong seem strong for a time. But they will be destroyed forever. 8 But You, the LORD, are high forever. 9 See, Your enemies, the LORD. See, Your enemies will die. All who do wrong will be scattered.

10 You have made me strong like a powerful ox. You have poured fresh oil on me. 11 My eyes have seen my enemies fall. My ears have heard the cry of those who rise against me.

Good People Grow Strong

12 Good people will grow like a palm tree. They will grow strong like a cedar in Lebanon. 13 They are planted in the house of the LORD. They grow in the yards of our God. 14 They will still bear fruit in old age. They will stay fresh and green. 15 They will show that the LORD is fair. He is my rock, and He does no wrong.
