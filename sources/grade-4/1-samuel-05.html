The Philistines Take the Ark

1 The Philistines took God's ark. They carried it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 2 They brought the ark into Dagon's temple. They set it next to Dagon.

Dagon Falls Down

3 The people of Ashdod got up early the next day. They saw Dagon lying face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD. They picked up Dagon and put him back in his place. 4 The next morning, they got up early again. They saw Dagon lying face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD. Dagon's head and both hands were broken off at the doorstep. Only his body was left. 5 That is why Dagon's priests and everyone who comes into Dagon's house in Ashdod still do not step on Dagon's doorstep to this day.

God Punishes Ashdod

6 The the LORD's hand was heavy on the people of Ashdod. He ruined them. He struck them with painful lumps. This happened to Ashdod and the land around it. 7 When the men of Ashdod saw this, they said, "The ark of the God of Israel must not stay with us. His hand is too hard on us and on our god Dagon."

The Ark Goes to Gath

8 They sent for all the Philistine rulers. They asked, "What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?"

The rulers said, "Move the ark of the God of Israel to Gath." So they moved it there.

9 After they moved it, the the LORD's hand was against that city. There was a great fear. He struck the men of the city, young and old. Painful lumps broke out on them.

Trouble in Ekron

10 They sent the ark of God to Ekron. When it got there, the people of Ekron cried out, "They brought the ark of the God of Israel here to kill us and our people!"

11 They gathered all the Philistine rulers. They said, "Send away the ark of the God of Israel. Let it go back to its place. It must not kill us and our people." Death and fear filled the whole city. God's hand was very heavy there. 12 The men who did not die were struck with painful lumps. The cry of the city went up to heaven.
