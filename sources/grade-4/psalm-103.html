A Song to Praise God

1 Praise the LORD, my soul. With all my heart, praise His holy name. 2 Praise the LORD, my soul. Do not forget all the good things He has done.

3 He forgives all your sins. He heals all your sickness. 4 He saves your life from death. He crowns you with love and mercy. 5 He fills you with good things. Your youth comes back like an eagle's.

God Is Fair and Kind

6 The LORD does what is right and fair for all who are treated badly. 7 He showed His ways to Moses. He showed His great works to the people of Israel.

8 The LORD is kind and full of grace. He is slow to anger. He has much love. 9 He will not argue forever. He will not stay angry forever. 10 He has not treated us as our sins deserve. He has not paid us back for our wrongs.

God's Love Is Big

11 His love for those who respect Him is as high as the sky above the earth. 12 He has moved our sins far away from us, as far as east is from west.

13 A father is kind to his children. In the same way, the LORD is kind to those who respect Him. 14 He knows how we are made. He remembers that we are dust.

People Are Like Grass

15 People live a short time. They grow like grass. They bloom like a flower in a field. 16 The wind blows over it, and it is gone. Its place does not remember it.

17 But the LORD's love lasts forever and ever for those who respect Him. His goodness lasts for their children and grandchildren. 18 It is for those who keep His agreement and remember to obey His rules.

The LORD Rules All

19 The LORD set up His throne in heaven. His kingdom rules over all.

Everything Praises the LORD

20 Praise the LORD, all His angels. They are strong and do what He says. They listen to His words. 21 Praise the LORD, all His armies. His servants do what He wants. 22 Praise the LORD, all the things He has made, in every place He rules. Praise the LORD, my soul.
