God Tells Moses to Set Up the Tent

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

2 "On the first day of the first month, set up the tent where you meet with Me. 3 Put the box that holds the law inside. Cover it with the curtain. 4 Bring in the table and set out its bread. Bring in the lampstand and light its lamps. 5 Put the gold altar for sweet smoke in front of the box that holds the law. Put the curtain at the door of the tent."

6 "Put the altar for burnt gifts in front of the door of the tent. 7 Put the wash bowl between the tent and the altar. Put water in it. 8 Set up the yard all around. Put the curtain at the gate of the yard."

9 "Take the oil for pouring. Pour oil on the tent and everything in it. Make it holy. Make its things holy. It will be holy. 10 Pour oil on the altar for burnt gifts and all its things. Make the altar very holy. 11 Pour oil on the wash bowl and its base. Make it holy."

12 "Bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the tent. Wash them with water. 13 Dress Aaron in the holy clothes. Pour oil on him and make him holy. Then he can serve Me as priest. 14 Bring his sons near. Dress them in shirts. 15 Pour oil on them like you poured oil on their father. They will serve Me as priests. This pouring of oil will make them priests forever, for all their children after them."

Moses Sets Up the Tent

16 Moses did all this. He did just as the LORD told him. 17 The tent was set up on the first day of the first month of the second year. 18 Moses set up the tent. He put in its bases, boards, bars, and pillars. 19 He spread the tent over it. He put the cover on top, just as the LORD had told Moses.

20 He took the law and put it in the box. He put the poles on the box. He put the cover on top of the box. 21 He brought the box into the tent. He hung the curtain. It covered the box that holds the law, just as the LORD had told Moses.

22 He put the table in the tent. It was on the north side, outside the curtain. 23 He set bread on it in a row before the LORD, just as the LORD had told Moses. 24 He put the lampstand in the tent. It was across from the table, on the south side. 25 He lit the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had told Moses. 26 He put the gold altar in the tent, in front of the curtain. 27 He burned sweet smoke on it, just as the LORD had told Moses.

28 He put the curtain at the door of the tent. 29 He put the altar for burnt gifts at the door of the tent. He offered the burnt gift and the grain gift on it, just as the LORD had told Moses. 30 He put the wash bowl between the tent and the altar. He put water in it for washing. 31 Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet from it. 32 They washed when they went into the tent and when they came near the altar, just as the LORD had told Moses. 33 Moses set up the yard all around the tent and the altar. He hung the curtain at the gate of the yard. Then Moses finished the work.

The Cloud and God's Glory

34 Then the cloud covered the tent. The glory of the LORD filled the tent. 35 Moses could not go into the tent, because the cloud stayed on it. The glory of the LORD filled the tent.

36 When the cloud rose from the tent, the people of Israel moved on in all their trips. 37 If the cloud did not rise, they did not move until it rose. 38 The cloud of the LORD was over the tent by day. At night, fire was in it. All the people of Israel could see it on all their trips.
