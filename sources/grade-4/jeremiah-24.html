Two Baskets of Figs

1 After Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, took Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and the leaders of Judah, and the skilled workers and metalworkers from Jerusalem, and brought them to Babylon, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs. They were in front of the temple of the LORD.

2 One basket had very good figs, like figs that ripen early. The other basket had very bad figs, so bad they could not be eaten.

3 Then the LORD asked me, "Jeremiah, what do you see?"

I said, "Figs. The good ones are very good, but the bad ones are so bad they cannot be eaten."

4 Then the word of the LORD came to me. He said,

The Good Figs

5 "This is what The LORD, the God of Israel, says: Like these good figs, I will treat the people of Judah who were taken away to Babylon as good. I sent them there for their good. 6 I will watch over them for good. I will bring them back to this land. I will build them up, not tear them down. I will plant them, not pull them up. 7 I will give them a heart that knows Me. They will be My people, and I will be their God. They will come back to Me with all their heart."

The Bad Figs

8 "This is what the LORD says: I will treat Zedekiah, king of Judah, and his leaders, and the people left in Jerusalem, and the people living in Egypt, like the bad figs that are too bad to eat. 9 I will make them a terrible thing and a bad example to all the kingdoms of the earth. People will shame them, make fun of them, and curse them everywhere I send them. 10 I will send war, hunger, and sickness on them until they are gone from the land I gave to them and their fathers."
