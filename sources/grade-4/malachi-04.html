God's Day Will Come

1 The LORD of Armies says, "A day is coming that will burn like a hot oven. Proud people and people who do wrong will be like dry grass. The day that comes will burn them up. It will not leave a root or a branch. 2 But you who honor My name will see the sun of goodness rise. It will bring healing. You will go out and jump like young cows let out of a pen. 3 You will step on the bad people. They will be like ashes under the soles of your feet on the day I get ready," says the LORD of Armies.

Remember God's Law

4 "Remember the law that My servant Moses gave. I told him the rules and right ways for all Israel at Mount Horeb."

Elijah Will Come

5 "Look, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and scary day of the LORD comes. 6 He will help fathers and children love each other again. Then I will not come and put a curse on the land."
