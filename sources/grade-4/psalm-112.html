A Song About a Good Person

1 Praise the the LORD! Happy is the person who respects the the LORD and loves His rules. 2 His children will be strong in the land. Good people will be blessed. 3 His home has money and good things. His good deeds last forever. 4 Light shines in the dark for good people. They are kind, caring, and fair.

How a Good Person Lives

5 Good things come to the person who is kind and shares. He does his work in a fair way. 6 He will not be shaken. People will remember the good person forever. 7 He will not be afraid of bad news. His heart is strong because he trusts the the LORD. 8 His heart is safe. He will not be afraid until he sees his enemies fall. 9 He gives to poor people. His good deeds last forever. People will honor him.

Bad People Will Not Last

10 Bad people will see this and get mad. They will be very angry and fade away. What bad people want will not last.
