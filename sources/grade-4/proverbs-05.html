Listen to Wise Words

1 My son, listen to my wise words. Turn your ear to what I understand. 2 Then you can make good choices. Your lips can keep wise words.

The Danger of Another Man's Wife

3 A woman who is not your wife may speak sweet words. Her talk may be smooth like oil. 4 But in the end she is bitter like a bad herb. She is sharp like a sword with two edges. 5 Her feet go down to death. Her steps lead to the grave. 6 She does not think about the path of life. She does not know her ways are not steady.

Stay Away From Her

7 So now, my sons, listen to me. Do not turn away from what I say. 8 Keep your way far from her. Do not go near the door of her house. 9 Then you will not give your strength to others. You will not give your years to a cruel person. 10 Strangers will not eat up your wealth. Your hard work will not fill a stranger's house. 11 At the end of your life, when your body is worn out, you will groan.

12 You will say, "How I hated being taught! My heart did not like correction! 13 I did not listen to my teachers. I did not turn my ear to my guides. 14 I almost ruined my life in front of everyone."

Be Faithful in Marriage

15 Drink water from your own well. Drink fresh water from your own spring. 16 Why should your water flow in the streets? Why should your streams flow in the public squares? 17 They are for you alone. Do not share them with strangers. 18 May your spring be blessed. Be happy with the wife you married when you were young. 19 She is like a loving deer. She is like a graceful young deer. Let her love fill you always. Let her love hold you forever.

Why Turn to Another Woman?

20 My son, why would you be drawn to a woman who is not your wife? Why would you hug a stranger?

God Sees Everything

21 The LORD sees the way a man lives. He watches all his paths. 22 A bad man's wrong acts catch him. His sins hold him tight. 23 He will die because he would not learn. His great foolishness leads him the wrong way.
