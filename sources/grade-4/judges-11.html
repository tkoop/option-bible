Jephthah and His Family

1 Jephthah was a strong fighter from Gilead. His mother was a woman who sold her body. Gilead was his father. 2 Gilead had other sons by his wife. When they grew up, they sent Jephthah away. They said, "You will not get any of our father's land. You are the son of another woman."

3 Jephthah ran away from his brothers. He lived in the land of Tob. Bad men came to him. They joined him.

The Ammonites Fight Israel

4 After a while, the Ammonites fought against Israel. 5 When the Ammonites fought Israel, the leaders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah from Tob. 6 They said to him, "Come and be our leader. Then we will fight the Ammonites."

7 Jephthah said to the leaders of Gilead, "Didn't you hate me and send me away from my father's house? Why do you come to me now, when you are in trouble?"

8 The leaders said, "That is why we come back to you now. Come with us and fight the Ammonites. Then you will be the leader of all who live in Gilead."

9 Jephthah said, "If you bring me back to fight the Ammonites, and the the LORD gives them to me, then I will be your leader."

10 The leaders said, "The The LORD is listening to what we say. We will do what you say." 11 So Jephthah went with the leaders of Gilead. The people made him their leader and captain. Jephthah said all these words before the the LORD in Mizpah.

Jephthah Sends Messengers

12 Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites. He said, "What do you have against me? Why are you fighting in my land?"

13 The king of the Ammonites said to Jephthah's messengers, "Israel took my land when they came up from Egypt. It was from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River and to the Jordan River. Give it back to me in peace."

14 Jephthah sent messengers again to the king of the Ammonites. 15 He said, "Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the Ammonites. 16 When Israel came up from Egypt, they went through the desert to the Red Sea. Then they came to Kadesh. 17 Israel asked the king of Edom to let them pass through his land, but he would not listen. Israel also asked the king of Moab, but he would not agree. So Israel stayed at Kadesh."

18 "Then Israel went through the desert. They went around the land of Edom and the land of Moab. They came from the east to the land of Moab. They camped on the other side of the Arnon River. They did not go into Moab, because the Arnon River was the border of Moab. 19 Israel asked Sihon, king of the Amorites in Heshbon, to let them pass through his land to their home. 20 But Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his land. He gathered all his people. They camped at Jahaz. Then they fought against Israel."

21 "The The LORD, the God of Israel, gave Sihon and all his people into Israel's hands. Israel beat them. Israel took all the land of the Amorites who lived there. 22 They took all the land of the Amorites from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River. They took it from the desert to the Jordan River. 23 Now the the LORD, the God of Israel, has driven out the Amorites for His people Israel. Should you take it?"

24 "Aren't you to take what Chemosh, your god, gives you? We will take what the the LORD our God gives us. 25 Are you better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever fight against Israel? 26 Israel has lived in Heshbon and its towns, in Aroer and its towns, and in all the cities along the Arnon River for three hundred years. Why didn't you take them back then? 27 I have not sinned against you. But you are doing wrong to me by fighting me. Let the the LORD judge today between the people of Israel and the people of Ammon."

28 But the king of the Ammonites did not listen to Jephthah's words.

God's Spirit Helps Jephthah

29 The Spirit of the the LORD came on Jephthah. He passed through Gilead and Manasseh. He passed through Mizpah in Gilead. From Mizpah he crossed over to the Ammonites.

Jephthah Makes a Promise

30 Jephthah made a promise to the the LORD. He said, "If You give the Ammonites into my hands, 31 then whatever comes out of the doors of my house to meet me when I come home safe from the Ammonites will be the the LORD's. I will give it as a burnt offering."

32 Jephthah crossed over to the Ammonites to fight them. The The LORD gave them into his hands. 33 He beat them very badly. He took twenty cities from Aroer to Minnith and to Abel-keramim. The Ammonites were beaten by Israel.

Jephthah's Daughter

34 Jephthah came to his house in Mizpah. His daughter came out to meet him. She was playing a tambourine and dancing. She was his only child. He had no other son or daughter. 35 When he saw her, he tore his clothes. He said, "Oh no, my daughter! You have made me very sad. You have brought trouble on me. I made a promise to the the LORD, and I cannot take it back."

36 She said to him, "My father, you made a promise to the the LORD. Do to me what you said. The The LORD has punished your enemies, the Ammonites, for you. 37 Let me do this one thing. Leave me alone for two months. Let me go into the hills with my friends. I will cry because I will never marry."

38 He said, "Go." He sent her away for two months. She and her friends went into the hills and cried because she would never marry. 39 After two months, she came back to her father. He did what he had promised. She had never been with a man. This became a rule in Israel. 40 Every year, the young women of Israel went for four days to sing sad songs for the daughter of Jephthah from Gilead.
