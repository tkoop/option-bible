The Tabernacle Curtains

1 Make the tabernacle with ten curtains. Use fine linen. Use blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Make cherubim on them with skill. 2 Each curtain must be 28 cubits long and 4 cubits wide. All the curtains must be the same size. 3 Join five curtains together. Join the other five curtains together. 4 Put blue loops on the edge of the last curtain in the first set. Do the same on the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 5 Make 50 loops on one curtain. Make 50 loops on the edge of the last curtain in the second set. The loops must match each other. 6 Make 50 gold hooks. Join the curtains with the hooks. Then the tabernacle will be one piece.

The Goat Hair Tent

7 Make curtains of goat hair for the tent over the tabernacle. Make 11 curtains in all. 8 Each of the 11 curtains must be 30 cubits long and 4 cubits wide. 9 Join five curtains into one set. Join the other six into another set. Fold the sixth curtain over the front of the tent. 10 Make 50 loops along the edge of the last curtain in the first set. Make 50 loops along the edge of the matching curtain in the second set. 11 Make 50 bronze hooks. Put them through the loops. Join the tent together as one piece. 12 Let the extra half curtain hang over the back of the tabernacle. 13 The extra cubit on one side and the extra cubit on the other side must hang over the sides of the tabernacle. This will cover it. 14 Make a cover for the tent from red ram skins. Make another cover over that from fine leather.

The Frame Boards

15 Make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 16 Each frame must be 10 cubits long and 1.5 cubits wide. 17 Make two pegs on the side of each frame. Join them together. Make all the frames this way. 18 Make 20 frames for the south side of the tabernacle. 19 Make 40 silver bases for these frames. Put two bases under each frame. One base is for each peg. 20 Make 20 frames for the north side of the tabernacle. 21 Make 40 silver bases for these frames. Put two bases under each frame. 22 Make 6 frames for the back of the tabernacle, on the west side. 23 Make 2 frames for the two back corners of the tabernacle. 24 Join them from bottom to top. Fit them into one ring. Make both corner frames this way. 25 There must be 8 frames and 16 silver bases. Put two bases under each frame.

The Crossbars

26 Make 5 crossbars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle. 27 Make 5 crossbars for the frames on the other side. Make 5 crossbars for the frames on the back side, on the west. 28 The middle crossbar must go through the middle of the frames. It must run from one end to the other end. 29 Cover the frames with gold. Make gold rings to hold the crossbars. Cover the crossbars with gold too. 30 Set up the tabernacle the way you were shown on the mountain.

The Veil and the Holy Place

31 Make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Use fine linen. Make cherubim on it with skill. 32 Hang it on four posts of acacia wood. Cover the posts with gold. Use gold hooks. Put them on four silver bases. 33 Hang the veil from the hooks. Put the ark of the Testimony behind the veil. The veil will separate the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place. 34 Put the mercy seat on the ark in the Most Holy Place. 35 Put the table outside the veil, on the north side of the tabernacle. Put the lampstand across from the table, on the south side.

The Entrance Curtain

36 For the entrance to the tent, make a curtain of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Use fine linen. Make it with skill, like an embroiderer. 37 Make five posts of acacia wood for the curtain. Cover them with gold. Make gold hooks. Make five bronze bases for them.
