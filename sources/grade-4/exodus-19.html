Israel Comes to Sinai

1 Three months after the Israelites left Egypt, they came to the Sinai desert. 2 They left Rephidim and came to the Sinai desert. Israel camped there, near the mountain.

God's Special Promise

3 Moses went up to God. The LORD called to him from the mountain and said, "Say this to Jacob's family and to the people of Israel: 4 'You saw what I did to Egypt. I carried you safely to Myself, like an eagle lifting its young. 5 Now, if you listen to My voice and keep My agreement, you will be My special treasure among all nations. The whole earth is Mine. 6 You will be a kingdom of priests and a holy nation to Me. These are the words you must say to the people of Israel.'"

The People Promise to Obey

7 Moses came back. He called the leaders of the people and told them all the LORD's words. 8 All the people answered together, "We will do everything the LORD has said." Then Moses told the LORD what the people said.

God Gets Ready to Meet Them

9 The LORD said to Moses, "I will come to you in a thick cloud. Then the people will hear Me talk with you. They will always trust you." Moses told the LORD what the people had said.

Getting Ready for the Third Day

10 The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people. Make them clean today and tomorrow. Have them wash their clothes. 11 Be ready on the third day. On that day, the LORD will come down on Mount Sinai. All the people will see Him. 12 Put a fence around the mountain for the people. Tell them, 'Be careful. Do not go up the mountain. Do not touch its edge. Anyone who touches the mountain must die.' 13 No one may touch them. They must be killed with stones or arrows. No person or animal may live. They may come up the mountain only when the trumpet blows a long sound."

The People Wash and Wait

14 Moses went down the mountain to the people. He made them clean, and they washed their clothes. 15 He told the people, "Be ready for three days. Do not sleep with a woman."

God Comes Down on the Mountain

16 On the morning of the third day, there was thunder and lightning. A thick cloud covered the mountain. A trumpet blew very loud. All the people in the camp were afraid. 17 Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God. They stood at the foot of the mountain. 18 Mount Sinai was covered with smoke. The LORD came down on it in fire. Smoke rose like smoke from a big oven. The whole mountain shook hard. 19 The trumpet sound grew louder and louder. Moses spoke, and God answered him with a loud voice.

God Calls Moses Up

20 The LORD came down to the top of Mount Sinai. He called Moses to the top, and Moses went up.

21 The LORD said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people. Do not let them rush in to see the LORD. If they do, many will die. 22 The priests who come near the LORD must also make themselves clean. If they do not, the LORD may punish them."

23 Moses said to the LORD, "The people cannot come up Mount Sinai. You warned us. You said, 'Put a fence around the mountain and make it holy.'"

24 The LORD said, "Go down. Then come back up with Aaron. But do not let the priests and people break through to come up to the LORD. If they do, He may punish them."

25 So Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.
