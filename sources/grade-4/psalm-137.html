By the Rivers of Babylon

1 By the rivers of Babylon, we sat and cried when we thought of Zion. 2 We hung our harps on the willow trees there.

3 The people who took us prisoner made us sing. Our enemies wanted us to be happy. They said, “Sing us one of Zion’s songs.”

4 But how can we sing the the LORD’s song in a strange land?

5 If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand forget what to do. 6 May my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you. May I not be happy unless Jerusalem is my greatest joy.

A Prayer for Justice

7 The LORD, remember what Edom did when Jerusalem fell. They said, “Tear it down! Tear it down to the ground!”

8 Babylon, you will be destroyed. Happy is the one who pays you back for what you did to us. 9 Happy is the one who grabs your babies and throws them against the rocks.
