Jesus Teaches How to Pray

1 One day Jesus was praying in a place. When He finished, one of His followers said, "Lord, teach us to pray. John taught his followers too."

2 He said to them, "When you pray, say: Father, may Your name be holy. Let Your kingdom come. 3 Give us the bread we need today. 4 Forgive us our sins. We forgive everyone who does wrong to us. And do not lead us into trouble."

A Story About a Late-Night Friend

5 Then He said, "Suppose one of you goes to a friend at midnight and asks for three loaves of bread. 6 You say a friend is visiting and you have no food to give him. 7 The friend inside says the door is locked and his children are in bed, so he cannot get up to give you anything. 8 I tell you, even if he will not get up because you are friends, he will get up because you keep asking. He will give you all you need."

Ask, Seek, and Knock

9 "So I tell you: Ask, and it will be given to you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened to you. 10 Everyone who asks receives. The one who seeks finds. The door is opened to the one who knocks."

11 "Which of you fathers, if your son asks for a fish, will give him a snake? 12 Or if he asks for an egg, will give him a scorpion? 13 If you are not good, but you know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!"

Jesus Heals a Man Who Could Not Talk

14 One day Jesus was sending out an evil spirit that made a man unable to talk. When the spirit left, the man talked, and the crowd was amazed. 15 But some said, "He sends out demons by Beelzebul, the boss of demons." 16 Others tried to test Him. They asked for a sign from heaven.

Jesus Answers His Critics

17 Jesus knew their thoughts. He said, "Every kingdom that fights against itself is ruined. A home that fights against itself falls. 18 If Satan fights against himself, how can his kingdom last? You say I send out demons by Beelzebul. 19 If I send them out by Beelzebul, who do your people use to send them out? So they will judge you. 20 But if I send them out by God's power, then God's kingdom has come to you."

21 "When a strong man with weapons guards his house, his things are safe. 22 But when a stronger man attacks and beats him, he takes the strong man's weapons and shares his stuff. 23 Anyone who is not with Me is against Me. Anyone who does not gather with Me scatters."

A Warning About Evil Spirits

24 "When an evil spirit leaves a person, it goes through dry places looking for rest. It does not find any. Then it goes back to the house it left. 25 It finds the house clean and neat. 26 Then it brings seven other spirits worse than itself. They go in and live there. That person ends up worse than before."

The Best Way to Be Happy

27 As Jesus said these things, a woman in the crowd called out, "How happy is the mother who gave birth to You, and how happy are the breasts that fed You!"

28 He said, "Even more, happy are those who hear God's word and do what it says."

Jesus Talks About Jonah and the Queen

29 As the crowd grew, Jesus began to say, "This is a bad generation. It wants a sign, but no sign will be given except the sign of Jonah. 30 Just as Jonah was a sign to the people of Nineveh, so the Son of Man will be a sign to this generation."

31 "The Queen of the South will stand up on judgment day with the people of this generation and judge them. She came from far away to hear Solomon's wise words. And now someone greater than Solomon is here. 32 The men of Nineveh will stand up on judgment day with this generation and judge it. They turned from their sins when Jonah preached to them. And now someone greater than Jonah is here."

Light and Dark

33 "No one lights a lamp and hides it. No one puts it under a bowl. They put it on a stand, so people who come in can see the light. 34 Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eyes are good, your whole body is full of light. When they are bad, your body is full of darkness."

35 "So be careful that the light in you is not darkness. 36 If your whole body is full of light, with no dark part, then it will be all light. It will be like a lamp shining its light on you."

Jesus Warns the Pharisees

37 While Jesus was speaking, a Pharisee asked Him to eat with him. Jesus went in and sat down. 38 The Pharisee was surprised that Jesus did not wash before the meal.

39 The Lord said to him, "You Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and dish. But inside you are full of greed and evil. 40 You fools! Did not the one who made the outside also make the inside? 41 But give what you have to the poor, and everything will be clean for you."

42 "But shame on you Pharisees! You give a tenth of your mint, rue, and every herb. But you forget justice and the love of God. You should do these things and not forget the others. 43 Shame on you Pharisees! You love the best seats in the synagogues and greetings in the marketplaces. 44 Shame on you! You are like hidden graves. People walk over them and do not know it."

Jesus Warns the Teachers of the Law Too

45 One of the teachers of the law said, "Teacher, when you say these things, you insult us too."

46 Jesus said, "Shame on you teachers of the law too! You put heavy loads on people. But you do not help them with even one finger. 47 Shame on you! You build tombs for the prophets, but your fathers killed them. 48 So you show that you agree with what your fathers did. They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs."

49 "Because of this, God said He would send them prophets and apostles, and some of them would be killed and chased. 50 So the blood of all the prophets, spilled since the world began, will be blamed on this generation. 51 From Abel's blood to Zechariah's blood, who died between the altar and the temple. Yes, I tell you, it will be blamed on this generation. 52 Shame on you teachers of the law! You took away the key of knowledge. You did not go in yourselves, and you stopped those who were trying to go in."

The Leaders Turn Against Jesus

53 As Jesus left there, the teachers of the law and Pharisees became very angry with Him. They asked Him many hard questions. 54 They watched Him closely, hoping to catch Him in something He said.
