The LORD Speaks About a Dry Time

1 This is the message the LORD gave to Jeremiah about the dry time. 2 Judah is sad. Its gates are weak. People bow their heads to the ground. Jerusalem cries out. 3 The leaders send their helpers to get water. They go to the water holes, but find no water. They come back with empty jars. They are ashamed and upset. They cover their heads in shame. 4 The ground is cracked and dry because there is no rain. The farmers are ashamed. They cover their heads. 5 Even the mother deer in the field leaves her baby, because there is no grass to eat. 6 The wild donkeys stand on the bare hills. They gasp for air like wild dogs. Their eyes grow weak, because there is no grass.

Jeremiah Prays to the LORD

7 Our sins speak against us. the LORD, do something good to honor Your name. We have turned away from You many times. We have sinned against You. 8 You are Israel's hope. You save us in hard times. Why are You like a stranger in the land? Why are You like a traveler who stays for one night? 9 Why are You like a man who is confused? Why are You like a strong man who cannot save? But You are with us, the LORD. Your name is on us. Do not leave us!

The LORD Answers

10 This is what the LORD says about these people: "They love to wander. They do not hold back their feet. the LORD does not accept them. Now He will remember their wrongs and punish their sins."

11 the LORD said to me, "Do not pray for good for these people. 12 When they go without food, I will not listen to their cry. When they bring burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will end them with war, hunger, and sickness."

Jeremiah Talks About the Prophets

13 Then I said, "Oh, Lord, the LORD, the prophets say to them, 'You will not see war. You will not be hungry. I will give you true peace here.'"

The LORD Speaks About the Prophets

14 the LORD said to me, "The prophets lie and use My name. I did not send them. I did not command them. I did not speak to them. They give you false visions. They tell you lies that come from their own hearts." 15 So this is what the LORD says about the prophets who speak in His name but whom He did not send. They say, "There will be no war or hunger in this land." But those prophets will die by war and hunger. 16 The people they speak to will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem. They will die from hunger and war. No one will bury them—not them, their wives, their sons, or their daughters. I will punish them for their evil.

The LORD Tells Jeremiah What to Say

17 You will say this to them: "Let my eyes cry tears day and night. Do not let them stop. For the young women of my people are broken. They have a very bad hurt. 18 If I go out to the field, I see people killed by war. If I go into the city, I see people sick from hunger. Even the prophet and the priest wander in a land they do not know."

The People Pray to the LORD

19 Have You completely turned away from Judah? Do You hate Zion? Why did You hurt us so we cannot be healed? We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for healing, but there was only trouble. 20 We know our wrongs, the LORD. We know the sins of our fathers. We have sinned against You. 21 For Your name's sake, do not hate us. Do not bring shame to Your glorious throne. Remember Your promise to us. Do not break it. 22 Do any of the false gods of the nations bring rain? Do the skies give showers? Are You not the LORD our God? We hope in You, because You made all these things.
