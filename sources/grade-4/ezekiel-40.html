Ezekiel Sees a New Temple

1 It was twenty-five years after we were taken away. It was the start of the year, on the tenth day of the month. It was fourteen years after the city fell. On that day, the LORD's power came on me. He brought me there. 2 In visions from God, He brought me to Israel. He set me on a very high mountain in the south. There was something like a city there.

The Man at the Gate

3 He brought me there. I saw a man who looked like bronze. He stood at the gate. He had a linen cord and a measuring rod.

4 The man said to me, “Son of man, look with your eyes. Listen with your ears. Pay attention to all I show you. I brought you here so I can show you these things. Tell the people of Israel all that you see.”

Measuring the East Gate

5 I saw a wall around the house. The man had a measuring rod six cubits long. A cubit is about 18 inches. He measured the wall. It was one rod thick and one rod high.

6 He went to the east gate. He walked up its steps. He measured the doorstep. It was one rod wide. He measured the other doorstep. It was one rod wide too. 7 Each side room was one rod long and one rod wide. The space between side rooms was five cubits. The porch that faced inside had a doorstep one rod wide. 8 He measured the porch from inside. It was one rod. 9 The porch was eight cubits long. Its door posts were two cubits. The porch faced the house.

10 The east gate had three side rooms on each side. They were all the same size. The door posts on both sides were the same size too. 11 He measured the gate opening. It was ten cubits wide. The gate was thirteen cubits long. 12 In front of the side rooms was a low wall one cubit high. Each side room was six cubits across. The low wall on the other side was one cubit high too. 13 He measured the gate from roof to roof of the side rooms. It was twenty-five cubits wide. One door faced the other door.

14 The door posts were sixty cubits high. The yard reached the gate all around. 15 The porch at the inside gate was fifty cubits long. 16 The side rooms and door posts had windows all around. The porch had windows all around inside. Palm trees were on the door posts.

The Outer Yard

17 He brought me to the outer yard. I saw a stone floor all around. There were thirty rooms on the floor. 18 The floor was next to the gates. It was as long as the gates. It was the lower floor. 19 He measured from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner yard. It was one hundred cubits to the east and to the north.

The North and South Gates

20 There was a gate on the north side of the outer yard. He measured its length and width. 21 It had three side rooms on each side. Its door posts and porch were like the first gate. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 22 Its windows, porch, and palm trees were like the east gate. Seven steps led up to it. The porch was in front of them. 23 The inner yard had a gate facing the north gate and the east gate. It was one hundred cubits from gate to gate.

24 He led me south. I saw a south gate. He measured its door posts and porch. They were like the others. 25 It had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 26 Seven steps led up to it. Its porch was in front. Palm trees were on its door posts, one on each side. 27 The inner yard had a south gate. It was one hundred cubits from gate to gate.

The Inner Yard

28 He brought me to the inner yard through the south gate. He measured the south gate. It was like the others. 29 Its side rooms, door posts, and porch were like the others. It had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 30 There were porches all around. They were twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide. 31 Its porch faced the outer yard. Palm trees were on its door posts. Eight steps led up to it.

32 He brought me to the east gate of the inner yard. He measured it. It was like the others. 33 Its side rooms, door posts, and porch were like the others. It had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 34 Its porch faced the outer yard. Palm trees were on its door posts, one on each side. Eight steps led up to it.

35 He brought me to the north gate. He measured it. It was like the others. 36 Its side rooms, door posts, and porch were like the others. It had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 37 Its door posts faced the outer yard. Palm trees were on them, one on each side. Eight steps led up to it.

Rooms for the Priests

38 There was a room by the door posts of the gate. There they washed the burnt offering. 39 In the porch of the gate there were two tables on each side. On them they killed the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. 40 Outside the porch, on the north side, there were two tables. On the other side of the porch there were two more tables. 41 So there were four tables on one side and four on the other. That made eight tables. They killed the sacrifices on them.

42 There were four tables of cut stone for the burnt offering. Each was one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high. They put the tools on them. 43 Hooks about three inches long were fastened around. The meat of the offering was put on the tables. 44 Outside the inner gate were rooms for singers. One was on the north side, facing south. The other was on the south side, facing north.

45 The man said to me, “The room that faces south is for the priests who take care of the temple. 46 The room that faces north is for the priests who take care of the altar. These priests are sons of Zadok. They are Levites who come near the LORD to serve Him.”

The Altar and the House

47 He measured the yard. It was one hundred cubits square. The altar was in front of the house. 48 He brought me to the porch of the house. He measured the porch. It was five cubits on each side. The gate was three cubits wide on each side. 49 The porch was twenty cubits long and eleven cubits wide. Steps led up to it. There were pillars by the door posts, one on each side.
