Jehoshaphat Becomes King

1 Jehoshaphat, Asa's son, became king after him. He grew strong in Israel. 2 He put soldiers in all the strong cities of Judah. He also put leaders in charge of the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.

He Follows God

3 The LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the good ways of his father David. He did not worship the Baals. 4 He looked to the God of his father. He walked in God's commands. He did not act like Israel.

5 So the LORD made his kingdom strong. All the people of Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat. He had great riches and honor. 6 His heart was set on following the LORD. He also removed the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah.

He Sends Teachers

7 In the third year of his rule, he sent his leaders to teach in the cities of Judah. Their names were Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah. 8 The Levites with them were Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah. The priests with them were Elishama and Jehoram. 9 They taught in Judah. They had the book of the law of the LORD with them. They went through all the cities of Judah and taught the people.

Other Nations Fear God

10 Fear of the LORD fell on all the kingdoms around Judah, so they did not fight Jehoshaphat. 11 Some Philistines brought gifts and silver to Jehoshaphat. The Arabs brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats.

Jehoshaphat Grows Strong

12 Jehoshaphat kept growing greater. He built forts and store cities in Judah. 13 He had much work in the cities of Judah. Strong, brave soldiers were in Jerusalem.

His Army

14 Here is the count of them by their family groups in Judah. The leaders of thousands were Adnah the leader, with 300,000 mighty men. 15 Next was Jehohanan the leader, with 280,000 men. 16 Next was Amasiah son of Zichri, who gave himself to the LORD. He had 200,000 mighty men. 17 From Benjamin there was Eliada, a mighty man, with 200,000 men armed with bows and shields. 18 Next was Jehozabad, with 180,000 men ready for war. 19 These men served the king. This was in addition to the men the king had placed in the strong cities all over Judah.
