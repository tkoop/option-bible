Accept People Who Have Weak Faith

1 Welcome the person who is weak in faith. Do not argue about what people think is right. 2 One person believes they can eat anything. But a weak person eats only vegetables. 3 The person who eats should not look down on the person who does not eat. And the person who does not eat should not judge the person who eats. God accepted that person. 4 Who are you to judge someone else's servant? He stands or falls to his own master. And he will stand, because the Lord can make him stand.

5 One person thinks one day is more special than another. Another person thinks every day is the same. Each person should be sure in his own mind. 6 The person who honors the day does it for the Lord. The person who eats eats for the Lord and gives thanks to God. The person who does not eat does not eat for the Lord and gives thanks to God.

7 None of us lives only for himself. And none of us dies only for himself. 8 If we live, we live for the Lord. If we die, we die for the Lord. So whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord. 9 Christ died and came back to life for this reason. He is Lord of both the dead and the living.

Do Not Judge Each Other

10 Why do you judge your brother? Or why do you look down on your brother? We will all stand before God to be judged. 11 It is written: "As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to Me, and every tongue will admit that God is right." 12 So each of us will answer to God for himself.

Do Not Make Others Fall

13 So let us stop judging each other. Instead, decide not to put anything in a brother's way that makes him fall. 14 I know and am sure in the Lord Jesus that nothing is wrong by itself. But if someone thinks it is wrong, then it is wrong for him. 15 If your brother is hurt because of food, you are not walking in love. Do not destroy the person Christ died for just because of food.

16 Do not let people speak badly about your good. 17 God's kingdom is not about eating and drinking. It is about doing what is right, having peace, and having joy in the Holy Spirit. 18 The person who serves Christ in this way pleases God and is approved by people.

19 So let us try to live in peace and build each other up. 20 Do not tear down God's work because of food. All food is okay. But it is wrong for a person to eat if it makes someone else fall. 21 It is good not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything that makes your brother fall.

22 Keep the faith you have between you and God. Happy is the person who does not judge himself by what he chooses. 23 But the person who is not sure is guilty if he eats. He does not eat from faith. And everything that is not from faith is sin.
