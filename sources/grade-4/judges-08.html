Gideon Answers the Men of Ephraim

1 The men of Ephraim said to Gideon, "What have you done to us? Why didn't you call us when you went to fight Midian?" They argued with him very hard.

2 Gideon said, "What have I done now that is like what you did? Aren't the small grapes Ephraim picked better than all the grapes my family picked? 3 God gave you Oreb and Zeeb, the leaders of Midian. What could I do like you?" When he said this, their anger went away.

4 Gideon and his 300 men came to the Jordan River. They were tired, but they kept chasing.

Gideon Asks for Food

5 Gideon said to the men of Succoth, "Please give some bread to the men with me. They are tired. I am chasing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian."

6 The leaders of Succoth said, "Do you already have Zebah and Zalmunna in your hand? Why should we give bread to your soldiers?"

7 Gideon said, "When the LORD gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will tear your skin with thorns and briers from the desert."

8 Gideon went up to Penuel and spoke the same way. The men of Penuel answered him like the men of Succoth did. 9 He also said to the men of Penuel, "When I come back safely, I will tear down this tower."

Gideon Defeats the Last Midianites

10 Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their camp. About 15,000 men were left from all the people of the east. 120,000 swordsmen had died.

11 Gideon went up by the road of the tent dwellers, east of Nobah and Jogbehah. He attacked the camp while it felt safe. 12 Zebah and Zalmunna ran away. Gideon chased them and caught the two Midian kings, Zebah and Zalmunna. The whole camp panicked.

13 Gideon son of Joash came back from the battle by the road that goes up to Heres.

Gideon Punishes Succoth and Penuel

14 He caught a young man from Succoth and asked him questions. The young man wrote down the leaders and elders of Succoth for him. There were 77 men.

15 Gideon came to the men of Succoth and said, "Here are Zebah and Zalmunna. You made fun of me. You said, 'Do we already have Zebah and Zalmunna in your hand? Why should we give food to your tired men?'"

16 Gideon took the elders of the city and desert thorns and briers. He used them to punish the men of Succoth. 17 He tore down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of that city.

Gideon Kills the Two Kings

18 Gideon said to Zebah and Zalmunna, "Where are the men you killed at Tabor?"

They said, "They looked like you, like sons of a king."

19 Gideon said, "They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. I promise by the LORD, if you had kept them alive, I would not kill you."

20 Gideon said to Jether, his oldest son, "Get up and kill them." But the boy did not pull out his sword, because he was afraid. He was still young.

21 Zebah and Zalmunna said, "You do it. You are strong like a man."

So Gideon got up and killed Zebah and Zalmunna. He took the crescent ornaments from their camels' necks.

Israel Wants Gideon to Rule

22 The men of Israel said to Gideon, "Rule over us. You and your son and your grandson can rule, because you saved us from Midian."

23 Gideon said, "I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you. The LORD will rule over you."

Gideon Makes a Gold Ephod

24 Gideon said to them, "I ask one thing from you: each man give me an earring from what he took." They had gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites.

25 They said, "We will give them." They spread out a coat, and each man threw an earring from what he had taken onto it.

26 The gold earrings Gideon asked for weighed 1,700 shekels. This was besides the crescent ornaments, purple cloth, and clothes from the Midian kings, and besides the chains from the camels' necks.

27 Gideon made an ephod from it and put it in his town, Ophrah. All Israel worshiped it there. It became a trap for Gideon and his family.

28 Midian was beaten down before Israel. They did not become strong again. The land had peace for 40 years while Gideon lived.

Gideon's Family and Death

29 Jerubbaal son of Joash went home and lived there. 30 Gideon had 70 sons, because he had many wives. 31 His concubine in Shechem also had a son, and Gideon named him Abimelech. 32 Gideon son of Joash died at a good old age. He was buried in the tomb of his father Joash in Ophrah of Abiezer.

Israel Turns Away from God

33 After Gideon died, the people of Israel turned back and worshiped the Baals. They made Baal-Berith their god. 34 Israel did not remember the LORD their God, who had saved them from all their enemies around them. 35 They did not show kindness to Gideon's family for all the good he had done for Israel.
