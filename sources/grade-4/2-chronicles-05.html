Solomon Finishes the Temple

1 All the work Solomon did for the LORD’s house was finished. Solomon brought in the holy things his father David had set apart. He put the silver, gold, and all the vessels in the treasuries of God’s house. 2 Then Solomon called together the leaders of Israel in Jerusalem. He called the elders, the heads of the tribes, and the family leaders. They were to bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant from the city of David, which is Zion. 3 All the men of Israel met with the king during the feast in the seventh month.

4 All the elders of Israel came. The Levites carried the ark. 5 They also carried the tent of meeting and all the holy things that were in it. The priests and Levites brought them up. 6 King Solomon and all the people of Israel who were there stood before the ark. They sacrificed so many sheep and oxen that no one could count them.

The Ark Is Placed in the Most Holy Place

7 The priests brought the ark of the LORD’s covenant to its place. They put it in the inner room of the temple, the Most Holy Place. They put it under the wings of the cherubim. 8 The cherubim spread their wings over the ark and its carrying poles. They covered them from above. 9 The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the front of the inner room. But they could not be seen from outside. They are still there today. 10 There was nothing in the ark except the two stone tablets Moses had put there at Mount Horeb. That was where the LORD made His covenant with the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

The Singers Praise the LORD

11 All the priests who were there made themselves holy. They did not have to stay in their groups. When they came out of the holy place, 12 the Levites who sang stood east of the altar. They were Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and brothers. They wore fine linen. They had cymbals, harps, and lyres. One hundred twenty priests stood with them and blew trumpets.

God’s Glory Fills the Temple

13 The trumpeters and singers made one sound. They praised and thanked the LORD. They lifted their voices with trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments. They sang, “He is good! His faithful love lasts forever!” Then the house of the LORD was filled with a cloud. 14 The priests could not stand and serve because of the cloud. The glory of the LORD had filled God’s house.
