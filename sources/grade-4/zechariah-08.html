The LORD Cares for Zion

1 The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me. He said,

2 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: I am very jealous for Zion. Yes, I am jealous for her with great anger. 3 This is what the LORD says: I will come back to Zion and live in Jerusalem. Then Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth. The mountain of the LORD of Hosts will be called the Holy Mountain."

4 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Old men and old women will sit in the streets of Jerusalem again. Each one will have a walking stick in his hand because he is very old. 5 The streets of the city will be full of boys and girls playing in them. 6 This is what the LORD of Hosts says: If this seems amazing to the people left alive, should it also seem amazing to Me?" says the LORD of Hosts.

7 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Look, I will save My people from the land in the east and from the land in the west. 8 I will bring them back. They will live in Jerusalem. They will be My people, and I will be their God. I will be true and do what is right."

The LORD Tells People to Be Strong

9 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Let your hands be strong, you who hear these words now. These words came from the prophets who were there when the house of the LORD of Hosts was started. 10 Before those days, people and animals did not get paid for their work. There was no peace for anyone who went out or came in. I turned every person against his neighbor. 11 But now I will not treat the rest of this people the way I did before," says the LORD of Hosts.

12 "For the seed of peace will grow well. The grapevine will give its fruit. The land will give its food. The sky will give its dew. I will give all these things to the rest of this people. 13 You have been a curse among the nations, O people of Judah and Israel. In the same way, I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid. Let your hands be strong."

The LORD Will Do Good

14 "For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: Just as I planned to hurt you when your fathers made Me angry, and I did not change My mind, 15 so now I have planned to do good to Jerusalem and the house of Judah. Do not be afraid."

16 "These are the things you must do: Speak the truth to each other. Give true and fair judgment in your city gates. 17 Do not plan evil in your hearts against your neighbor. Do not love to make false promises. I hate all these things," says the LORD.

Sad Days Will Become Happy

18 The word of the LORD of Hosts came to me again. He said,

19 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: The fasts in the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months will become times of joy and gladness for the people of Judah. They will be happy feasts. So love truth and peace."

Many People Will Seek the LORD

20 "This is what the LORD of Hosts says: People from many cities will still come. 21 The people of one city will go to another city and say, 'Let us go at once to ask the LORD to be kind to us. Let us seek the LORD of Hosts. I will go too.' 22 Many strong nations and peoples will come to seek the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem. They will ask the LORD to be kind to them. 23 In those days, ten men from all kinds of nations and languages will hold the corner of a Jewish man's coat. They will say, 'Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.'"
