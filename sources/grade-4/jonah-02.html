Jonah Prays from the Fish

1 Jonah prayed to the the LORD his God from inside the fish.

2 Jonah said, "I was in trouble, so I called to the the LORD. He answered me. I cried out from the grave, and You heard my voice.

3 "You threw me deep into the sea. Water was all around me. Your waves rolled over me.

4 I said, 'I am thrown away from Your sight. But I will look again toward Your holy temple.'

5 "The water was all around me. It came up to my neck. The deep sea was around me. Seaweed wrapped around my head. 6 I went down to the bottom of the mountains. The earth closed around me forever. But You, the the LORD my God, brought me up from the grave. 7 When I was weak and ready to give up, I remembered the the LORD. My prayer came to You in Your holy temple.

8 "People who worship false gods turn away from Your faithful love. 9 But I will thank You with a loud voice. I will give You a gift. I will keep my promise to You. The LORD saves."

The Fish Spits Jonah Out

10 Then the the LORD spoke to the fish, and it spit Jonah out onto dry land.
