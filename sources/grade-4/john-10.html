Jesus Tells a Story About Sheep

1 "I tell you the truth. Anyone who does not go into the sheep pen through the door, but climbs in some other way, is a thief and a robber. 2 But the one who comes in through the door is the shepherd of the sheep. 3 The man at the gate opens it for Him. The sheep hear His voice. He calls His own sheep by name and leads them out. 4 When He has brought out all His own sheep, He walks ahead of them. The sheep follow Him because they know His voice. 5 They will not follow a stranger. They will run away from him because they do not know the voice of strangers."

6 Jesus told them this story, but they did not understand what He was saying.

Jesus Is the Door

7 Jesus said again, "I tell you the truth. I am the door for the sheep. 8 All who came before Me are thieves and robbers. But the sheep did not listen to them. 9 I am the door. If anyone comes in through Me, he will be saved. He will go in and out and find good grass. 10 The thief comes only to steal, kill, and destroy. I came so they can have life, and have it more than enough."

Jesus Is the Good Shepherd

11 "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives up His life for the sheep. 12 The hired man is not the shepherd, and the sheep are not his own. When he sees the wolf coming, he leaves the sheep and runs away. Then the wolf grabs them and scatters them. 13 he runs away because he is paid to work and does not care about the sheep.

14 "I am the good shepherd. I know My sheep, and My sheep know Me. 15 The Father knows Me, and I know the Father. I give up My life for the sheep. 16 I have other sheep that are not in this sheep pen. I must bring them too. They will listen to My voice. Then there will be one flock and one shepherd.

17 "That is why the Father loves Me. I give up My life so I can take it back again. 18 No one takes it from Me. I give it up by My own choice. I have the right to give it up, and I have the right to take it back. My Father gave Me this command."

People Are Divided

19 Again, the Jews were divided because of what He said.

20 Many of them said, "He has an evil spirit and is crazy! Why listen to Him?"

21 Others said, "These are not the words of a man with an evil spirit. Can an evil spirit open the eyes of a blind man?"

Jesus at the Temple in Winter

22 Then it was time for a special feast in Jerusalem. It was winter. 23 Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon's porch.

The Jewish Leaders Question Jesus

24 The Jewish leaders gathered around Him and said, "How long will You keep us waiting? If You are the Christ, tell us plainly."

25 Jesus answered, "I told you, and you do not believe. The works I do in My Father's name speak for Me. 26 But you do not believe because you are not My sheep. 27 My sheep listen to My voice. I know them, and they follow Me. 28 I give them life that never ends. They will never be lost. No one will snatch them out of My hand. 29 My Father gave them to Me. He is greater than all. No one can snatch them out of My Father's hand. 30 I and the Father are one."

31 Again, the Jewish leaders picked up stones to stone Him.

32 Jesus answered them, "I showed you many good works from the Father. For which one are you going to stone Me?"

33 The Jewish leaders answered, "We are not stoning You for a good work. We are stoning You because You speak against God. You are only a man, but You make Yourself God."

34 Jesus answered them, "It is written in your law, 'I said, you are gods.' 35 If He called them gods, and God's Word cannot be changed, 36 then why do you say I speak against God? I am the One the Father set apart and sent into the world. You say I speak against God because I said, 'I am the Son of God.' 37 If I do not do My Father's works, do not believe Me. 38 But if I do them, even if you do not believe Me, believe the works. Then you will know and understand that the Father is in Me, and I am in the Father."

39 They tried again to grab Him, but He escaped from them.

Many People Believe

40 Jesus went back across the Jordan River to the place where John first baptized. He stayed there. 41 Many people came to Him. They said, "John did no miracle, but everything John said about this Man was true." 42 Many people there believed in Him.
