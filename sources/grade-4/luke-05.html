Jesus Calls His First Followers

1 One day, Jesus stood by Lake Gennesaret. The crowd pushed in to hear God's word. 2 He saw two boats by the lake. The fishermen had gotten out and were washing their nets. 3 Jesus got into one of the boats. It was Simon's boat. He asked Simon to push out a little from land. Then Jesus sat in the boat and taught the crowd.

4 When He finished speaking, He said to Simon, "Put out into the deep water. Let down your nets to catch fish."

5 Simon answered, "Master, we worked all night and caught nothing. But at your word I will let down the nets."

6 They did this, and they caught a lot of fish. Their nets began to break. 7 They signaled to their friends in the other boat to come help. They came and filled both boats. The boats began to sink.

8 Simon Peter saw this. He fell at Jesus' knees and said, "Go away from me, Lord! I am a sinful man." 9 He and all his friends were amazed at the catch of fish.

10 Jesus said to Simon, "Don't be afraid. From now on you will catch people." James and John, the sons of Zebedee, were Simon's fishing friends. They were amazed too.

11 They pulled their boats onto the shore. They left everything and followed Him.

Jesus Heals a Man with Leprosy

12 In one town, a man was full of leprosy. He saw Jesus. He fell on his face and begged, "Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean."

13 Jesus reached out His hand and touched him. He said, "I am willing. Be clean!" Right away the leprosy left him.

14 Jesus told him, "Don't tell anyone. But go and show yourself to the priest. Offer what Moses told you to offer. This will be a sign to them."

15 News about Him spread even more. Big crowds came to hear Him and to be healed from sickness. 16 But Jesus often went to quiet places to pray.

Jesus Heals a Paralyzed Man

17 One day Jesus was teaching. Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there. They had come from Jerusalem and from every town in Galilee and Judea. The Lord's power was with Him to heal people.

18 Some men carried a paralyzed man on a mat. They tried to bring him in and lay him before Jesus. 19 They could not get through the crowd. So they went up on the roof. They lowered him through the roof tiles into the middle, right in front of Jesus.

20 Jesus saw their faith. He said, "Man, your sins are forgiven."

21 The teachers of the law and the Pharisees began to think, "Who is this man? He speaks against God! Who can forgive sins but God alone?"

22 Jesus knew what they were thinking. He answered them, "Why do you question this in your hearts? 23 Which is easier: to say, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Get up and walk'? 24 But so you may know the Son of Man has power on earth to forgive sins." Then He said to the paralyzed man, "I tell you, get up, take your mat, and go home."

25 Right away the man stood up in front of them. He picked up what he had been lying on and went home. He praised God. 26 Everyone was amazed. They praised God. They were filled with fear and said, "We saw amazing things today!"

Jesus Calls Levi

27 After this, Jesus went out and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax booth. Jesus said to him, "Follow Me." 28 Levi got up, left everything, and followed Him.

29 Levi gave a big dinner for Jesus at his house. Many tax collectors and other people were eating with them.

Jesus Eats with Sinners

30 The Pharisees and their teachers of the law complained to His disciples. They said, "Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?"

31 Jesus answered, "Healthy people don't need a doctor, but sick people do. 32 I did not come to call good people. I came to call sinners to turn to God."

Jesus Talks About Fasting

33 They said to Him, "John's disciples often fast and pray. So do the Pharisees' disciples. But your disciples eat and drink."

34 Jesus said to them, "Can you make the wedding guests fast while the groom is with them? 35 But the days will come when the groom is taken away from them. Then they will fast."

Jesus Tells a Story

36 He told them this story: "No one tears a patch from a new coat and puts it on an old coat. If he does, the new coat will tear. The patch from the new coat will not match the old coat. 37 No one puts new wine into old wineskins. If he does, the new wine will burst the skins. The wine will spill out, and the skins will be ruined. 38 New wine must be put into new wineskins. 39 No one who drinks old wine wants new wine. He says, 'The old is better.'"
