The Leaders Come to God

1 It was the seventh year. On the tenth day of the fifth month, some leaders of Israel came to ask the the LORD for help. They sat in front of me. 2 Then the the LORD spoke to me.

God Says He Will Not Answer

3 "Son of man, speak to the leaders of Israel. Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: Are you coming to ask Me for help? As I live, I will not answer you, says the Lord the LORD.' 4 Will you judge them? Will you judge them, son of man? Tell them about the bad things their fathers did."

God Remembers Israel's Past

5 "Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: On the day I chose Israel, I raised My hand to the family of Jacob. I made Myself known to them in Egypt. I raised My hand and said, "I am the the LORD your God." ' 6 On that day I raised My hand to bring them out of Egypt. I would take them to a land I found for them. It had milk and honey. It was the best land of all. 7 I said to them, "Each of you throw away the bad things you look at. Do not make yourselves dirty with Egypt's idols. I am the the LORD your God." 8 But they did not obey Me. They would not listen. They did not throw away their bad things. They did not leave Egypt's idols. So I said I would pour out My anger on them in Egypt. 9 But I did it for My name's sake. I did not let My name be made dirty in front of the nations. They saw Me bring Israel out. 10 So I brought them out of Egypt. I led them into the desert. 11 I gave them My rules and laws. A person who follows them will live. 12 I also gave them My Sabbaths. They were a sign between Me and them. They would know that I am the the LORD who makes them holy. 13 But the people of Israel did not obey Me in the desert. They did not follow My rules. They said no to My laws. They made My Sabbaths dirty. So I said I would pour out My anger to destroy them in the desert. 14 But I did it for My name's sake. I did not let My name be made dirty in front of the nations. They saw Me bring Israel out. 15 I also raised My hand to them in the desert. I said I would not bring them into the land I gave them. It had milk and honey and was the best land. 16 I did this because they said no to My rules. They did not follow My laws. They made My Sabbaths dirty. Their hearts went after their idols. 17 But I felt sorry for them. I did not destroy them all in the desert. 18 I said to their children in the desert, "Do not follow your fathers' rules. Do not keep their laws. Do not make yourselves dirty with their idols." 19 "I am the the LORD your God. Follow My rules. Keep My laws and do them." 20 "Keep My Sabbaths holy. They are a sign between Me and you. Then you will know that I am the the LORD your God." 21 But the children did not obey Me. They did not follow My rules. They did not keep My laws. They made My Sabbaths dirty. So I said I would pour out My anger on them in the desert. 22 But I held back My hand. I did it for My name's sake. I did not let My name be made dirty in front of the nations. They saw Me bring Israel out. 23 I also raised My hand to them in the desert. I said I would scatter them among the nations. I would send them to other lands. 24 I did this because they did not keep My laws. They said no to My rules. They made My Sabbaths dirty. Their eyes went after their fathers' idols. 25 So I gave them rules that were not good. I gave them laws they could not live by. 26 I let them become dirty by their gifts. They offered all their first babies. I did this to make them guilty. Then they would know that I am the the LORD.'"

God Judges the Fathers

27 "So, son of man, speak to the people of Israel. Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: Your fathers also spoke bad about Me. They were not faithful to Me. 28 I brought them to the land I promised. They saw high hills and green trees. They gave sacrifices there. They gave gifts that made Me angry. They burned sweet smells there. They poured out drink offerings. 29 Then I said, "What is this high place where you go?" So it is called High Place to this day.'"

God Says He Will Not Be Asked

30 "So say to Israel, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: Will you make yourselves dirty like your fathers? Will you chase after bad things? 31 When you bring gifts and burn your children, you make yourselves dirty with all your idols. And I will not be asked by you, people of Israel. As I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will not be asked by you. 32 You may think, "Let us be like other nations. Let us be like the families of the lands. Let us serve wood and stone." But that will not happen.'"

God Will Rule His People

33 "As I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will rule over you with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out anger. 34 I will bring you out from the nations. I will gather you from the lands where you are scattered. I will do it with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out anger. 35 I will bring you into the desert of the nations. There I will judge you face to face. 36 I will judge you like I judged your fathers in the desert of Egypt, says the Lord the LORD. 37 I will make you pass under the stick. I will bring you into the promise of the agreement. 38 I will take out the rebels and sinners among you. I will send them away from where they live. They will not come into the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the the LORD."

God Says to Serve Him

39 "As for you, people of Israel, this is what the Lord the LORD says: Go, serve your idols, each of you. But after that, if you will not listen to Me, do not make My holy name dirty again with your gifts and idols. 40 For on My holy mountain, on the high mountain of Israel, says the Lord the LORD, all the people of Israel will serve Me in the land. There I will accept them. There I will ask for your gifts and first crops. I will ask for all your holy things. 41 I will accept you like a sweet smell. I will do this when I bring you from the nations. I will gather you from the lands where you are scattered. I will show Myself holy through you in front of the nations. 42 Then you will know that I am the the LORD. I will bring you into the land of Israel. I raised My hand to give this land to your fathers. 43 There you will remember your ways and all the things you did. You became dirty by them. You will hate yourselves for all the bad things you did. 44 Then you will know that I am the the LORD. I will do this for My name's sake. I will not do it like your bad ways or your ruined acts, people of Israel, says the Lord the LORD."

45 Then the the LORD spoke to me.

Fire in the South

46 "Son of man, look toward the south. Speak to the south. Prophesy against the forest in the south. 47 Say to the forest in the south, 'Hear the word of the the LORD. This is what the Lord the LORD says: I am about to set you on fire. It will burn every green tree and every dry tree. The bright flame will not go out. Every face from south to north will be burned.' 48 All people will see that I, the the LORD, started the fire. It will not go out."

49 Then I said, "Oh, Lord the LORD! People say I just tell stories."
