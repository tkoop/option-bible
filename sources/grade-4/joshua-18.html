The Tent of Meeting at Shiloh

1 All the people of Israel came together at Shiloh. They set up the tent of meeting there. The land was now under their control.

2 Seven tribes of Israel still had no land of their own.

Joshua Tells Them to Stop Waiting

3 Joshua said to the Israelites, “How long will you wait to take the land the the LORD God of your fathers gave you? 4 Pick three men from each tribe. I will send them. They will go through the land and write it down. They will bring it back to me. 5 Then divide the land into seven parts. Judah will stay in the south. Joseph’s family will stay in the north. 6 You write down the seven parts of the land and bring them to me. I will cast lots for you here before the the LORD our God. 7 The Levites do not get a part of the land like the rest of you. Serving the the LORD is their share. Gad, Reuben, and half of Manasseh already got their land east of the Jordan River. Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave it to them.”

8 The men got up and went. Joshua told the men who went to write the land, “Go through the land and write it down. Then come back to me. I will cast lots for you here before the the LORD in Shiloh.”

9 The men went through the land. They wrote it in a book. They divided it into seven parts by cities. Then they came back to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh.

10 Joshua cast lots for them before the the LORD in Shiloh. There Joshua divided the land among the Israelites by their groups.

Benjamin’s Land

11 The lot came up for the tribe of Benjamin, by its families. Its land was between Judah and Joseph.

12 Their north border started at the Jordan River. It went up on the north side of Jericho. It went up west through the hill country. It ended at the desert of Beth-aven. 13 From there the border passed south of Luz, also called Bethel. It went down to Ataroth-addar on the mountain south of Lower Beth-horon. 14 The border turned west and south from the mountain before Beth-horon. It ended at Kiriath-baal (that is, Kiriath-jearim), a city of Judah. This was the west side.

15 The south side started at the end of Kiriath-jearim. It went west to the spring of Nephtoah. 16 It went down to the end of the mountain near the Valley of Ben Hinnom, north of the Valley of Rephaim. It went down the Valley of Hinnom south of the Jebusite city. It went down to En-rogel. 17 It turned north to En-shemesh. It went to Geliloth, across from the road to Adummim. It went down to the stone of Bohan, Reuben’s son.

18 It passed on the north side of the Arabah. It went down into the Arabah. 19 It passed north of Beth-hoglah. It ended at the north end of the Salt Sea, at the south end of the Jordan River. This was the south border. 20 The Jordan River was the east border. This was Benjamin’s land, by its families, with towns all around.

Cities of Benjamin

21 The cities of Benjamin, by families, were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, and Emek-keziz. 22 They also included Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, and Bethel. 23 They also included Avvim, Parah, and Ophrah. 24 They also included Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba. That is twelve cities with their villages.

25 They also included Gibeon, Ramah, and Beeroth. 26 They also included Mizpah, Chephirah, and Mozah. 27 They also included Rekem, Irpeel, and Taralah. 28 They also included Zelah, Eleph, the Jebusite city (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath. That is fourteen cities with their villages. This was Benjamin’s land, by families.
