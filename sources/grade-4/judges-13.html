Israel Does Wrong Again

1 The people of Israel did wrong again. The LORD saw it. So He let the Philistines rule them for forty years.

An Angel Visits Manoah's Wife

2 A man named Manoah lived in Zorah. He was from the family of Dan. His wife could not have children.

3 The angel of the LORD came to the woman. He said, “You cannot have children now, but you will have a baby boy.”

4 “Be careful. Do not drink wine or beer. Do not eat anything unclean.”

5 “You are going to have a baby. No one may cut his hair. The boy will be a Nazirite for God from birth. He will begin to save Israel from the Philistines.”

6 The woman went to her husband. She said, “A man of God came to me. He looked like an angel of God. He was very frightening. I did not ask where he came from. He did not tell me his name.”

7 “He told me, ‘You are going to have a baby boy. Do not drink wine or beer. Do not eat anything unclean. The boy will be a Nazirite for God from birth until he dies.’”

Manoah Prays for Help

8 Manoah prayed to the LORD. He said, “Please, Lord, send the man of God back to us. Teach us what to do for the boy who will be born.”

The Angel Comes Again

9 God heard Manoah. The angel of God came to the woman again. She was sitting in a field. Her husband was not with her.

10 The woman ran quickly to her husband. She said, “The man who came to me before has appeared to me again.”

11 Manoah got up and followed his wife. He came to the man. He said, “Are you the man who spoke to my wife?”

“I am,” the man said.

12 Manoah said, “When your words come true, what should the boy’s life be like? What should he do?”

13 The angel of the LORD said to Manoah, “Your wife must be careful to do all I told her.”

14 “She must not eat anything from a grapevine. She must not drink wine or beer. She must not eat anything unclean. She must obey all I commanded her.”

Manoah Offers a Gift

15 Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, “Please stay with us. We will make a young goat for you.”

16 The angel of the LORD said to Manoah, “If you keep me here, I will not eat your bread. If you make a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD.” Manoah did not know he was the angel of the LORD.

17 Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, “What is your name? We want to honor you when your words come true.”

18 The angel of the LORD said to him, “Why do you ask my name? It is too wonderful to tell.”

Manoah and His Wife Worship

19 Manoah took a young goat and a grain offering. He offered them on a rock to the LORD. The LORD did a wonderful thing while Manoah and his wife watched.

20 The flame went up from the altar toward heaven. The angel of the LORD went up in the flame. Manoah and his wife saw it. They fell with their faces to the ground.

21 The angel of the LORD did not appear to Manoah or his wife again. Then Manoah knew he was the angel of the LORD.

22 Manoah said to his wife, “We will die! We have seen God.”

23 His wife said to him, “If the LORD wanted to kill us, He would not have accepted our burnt offering and grain offering. He would not have shown us these things. He would not have told us this now.”

Samson Is Born

24 The woman had a baby boy. She named him Samson. The boy grew. The LORD blessed him.

25 The Spirit of the LORD began to move him. This was in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.
