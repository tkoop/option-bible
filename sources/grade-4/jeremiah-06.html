Run from Jerusalem

1 People of Benjamin, run for safety! Leave Jerusalem! Blow the trumpet in Tekoa! Raise a signal at Beth-haccerem! Great trouble is coming from the north. 2 I compare the daughter of Zion to a beautiful, gentle woman. 3 Shepherds and their flocks will come to her. They will set up tents all around her. Each man will feed in his own place.

The Enemy Plans an Attack

4 "Get ready for war against her! Get up! Let's attack at noon! Oh no, the day is fading! The evening shadows are stretching out." 5 "Get up! Let's attack at night! Let's destroy her big houses!"

6 The LORD of Armies says, "Cut down trees and pile up dirt against Jerusalem. This city must be punished. It is full of trouble inside." 7 Just as a well pours out fresh water, she pours out fresh evil. People hear about violence and stealing there. Sickness and wounds are always before Me.

8 The LORD says, "Jerusalem, let this be a warning to you. If not, I will turn away from you. I will make you an empty land where no one lives."

The LORD's Anger at Sin

9 The LORD of Armies says, "The enemy will pick Israel clean like a vine. Go back and pick the branches again, like someone picking grapes." 10 Who can I talk to and warn? They will not listen. Their ears are closed. They cannot hear. They make fun of the the LORD's word. They do not want it.

11 But I am full of the the LORD's anger. I am tired of holding it back. So I will pour it out on children in the street and on groups of young men. Husbands and wives will be caught too. Old people and those who have lived long will be caught. 12 Their houses, fields, and wives will go to other people. I will punish the people who live in this land, says the the LORD.

13 From the small to the great, all want money. From prophet to priest, all tell lies. 14 They have treated My people's hurt like it is no big deal. They say, "Peace, peace," but there is no peace.

15 Were they ashamed when they did terrible things? No, they were not ashamed at all. They did not even know how to blush. So they will fall with others who fall. When I punish them, they will trip, says the the LORD.

The Old Good Way

16 The LORD says, "Stand where roads meet and look. Ask for the old ways. Ask which way is good. Walk in it. Then your souls will find rest."

But they said, "We will not go."

17 The LORD says, "I put guards over you. Listen to the trumpet sound."

But they said, "We will not listen."

18 So listen, nations. Know, people, what will happen to them. 19 Listen, earth! I am bringing trouble on these people. It comes from their own plans. They did not listen to My words. They turned away from My law.

20 Why do you bring Me sweet smoke from Sheba and sweet cane from far away? Your burned gifts do not please Me. Your gifts do not make Me happy.

Things That Make People Trip

21 The LORD says, "I will put things that make these people trip. Fathers and sons will trip over them together. Neighbors and friends will die."

An Army from the North

22 The LORD says, "Look! People are coming from the north. A great nation is coming from far away. 23 They carry bows and spears. They are mean and show no mercy. Their sound is like the sea. They ride horses. They are ready for war against you, daughter of Zion."

24 We heard the news. Our hands are weak. Trouble has grabbed us. Pain has grabbed us like a woman giving birth.

25 Do not go out to the field. Do not walk on the road. The enemy has a sword. Fear is all around.

26 Daughter of My people, wear rough cloth. Roll in ashes. Mourn like you lost your only child. Cry loudly, because the destroyer will suddenly come against us.

27 I have made you a tester of metals among My people. You will know and test their ways.

28 They are all stubborn. They go around telling lies. They are like bronze and iron. They all destroy.

29 The air pump blows hard. The lead melts. The fire burns for nothing. The bad people are not taken away.

30 They are called rejected silver, because the the LORD has rejected them.
