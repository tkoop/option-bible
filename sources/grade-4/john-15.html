Jesus Is the True Vine

"1 I am the true vine. My Father takes care of the vine. 2 He cuts off every branch in Me that does not make fruit. He trims every branch that does make fruit, so it can make more fruit. 3 You are already clean because of the words I said to you. 4 Stay in Me, and I will stay in you. A branch cannot make fruit by itself. It must stay in the vine. You can't make fruit unless you stay in Me."

"5 I am the vine. You are the branches. The one who stays in Me and I in him makes lots of fruit. Without Me, you can't do anything. 6 If someone does not stay in Me, he is thrown out like a branch. He dries up. People pick up the dry branches and throw them in the fire. They burn. 7 If you stay in Me, and My words stay in you, ask for what you want. It will be done for you. 8 This makes My Father glad, because you make lots of fruit. Then you will be My followers."

Stay in Jesus' Love

"9 The Father loved Me. I loved you too. Stay in My love. 10 If you obey My rules, you will stay in My love. I obeyed My Father's rules, and I stay in His love. 11 I told you these things so My joy can be in you. Then your joy will be full. 12 This is My rule: Love each other like I loved you."

"13 No one has greater love than this. A person gives his life for his friends. 14 You are My friends if you do what I tell you. 15 I don't call you servants now. A servant doesn't know what his master is doing. I call you friends, because I told you everything I heard from My Father. 16 You didn't choose Me. I chose you. I put you here so you can go and make fruit. Your fruit will last. Then whatever you ask the Father in My name, He will give you. 17 I tell you these things so you love each other."

The World Hates Jesus' Friends

"18 If the world hates you, remember it hated Me first. 19 If you were from the world, the world would love you. But you are not from the world. I chose you out of the world. So the world hates you. 20 Remember what I told you: 'A worker is not greater than his master.' If they hurt Me, they will hurt you too. If they listened to My words, they will listen to yours too. 21 But they will do all these things to you because of My name. They don't know the One who sent Me."

"22 If I had not come and talked to them, they would not have sin. But now they have no excuse for their sin. 23 The one who hates Me hates My Father too. 24 If I had not done works among them that no one else did, they would not have sin. But now they have seen them, and they hate Me and My Father. 25 This happened so the words in their law would come true: 'They hated Me for no reason.'"

The Helper Will Come

"26 When the Helper comes, I will send Him to you from the Father. He is the Spirit of truth. He comes from the Father. He will tell about Me. 27 You will tell about Me too, because you have been with Me from the start."
