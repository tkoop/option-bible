Praise the LORD

1 Praise The LORD! Praise the name of the LORD! Praise Him, you servants of the LORD! 2 You stand in the house of the LORD, in the yards of the house of our God. 3 Praise The LORD, because the LORD is good. Sing to His name, because it is nice. 4 For The LORD chose Jacob for Himself. He chose Israel to be His special people.

The LORD Is Great

5 I know that the LORD is great. Our Lord is above all gods. 6 The LORD does whatever He wants in heaven and on earth, in the seas and in all the deep water. 7 He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning for rain. He brings the wind out of His storage rooms.

What the LORD Did

8 He struck down the oldest children in Egypt, both people and animals. 9 He sent signs and wonders in Egypt. He did them to Pharaoh and all his servants. 10 He struck down many nations. He killed mighty kings. 11 He killed Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan. 12 He gave their land to His people, Israel, as a gift. 13 The LORD, Your name lasts forever. People will remember You, the LORD, for all time. 14 For The LORD will judge His people. He will be kind to His servants.

Idols Are Not Real

15 The idols of the nations are silver and gold. They are made by human hands. 16 They have mouths, but they cannot speak. They have eyes, but they cannot see. 17 They have ears, but they cannot hear. There is no breath in their mouths. 18 Those who make them will be like them. So will everyone who trusts in them.

Praise the LORD

19 People of Israel, praise The LORD! Family of Aaron, praise The LORD! 20 Family of Levi, praise The LORD! All who fear the LORD, praise The LORD! 21 Praise The LORD in Zion! He lives in Jerusalem. Praise The LORD!
