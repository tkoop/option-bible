Nebuchadnezzar Attacks Jerusalem

1 Zedekiah had been king for nine years. On the tenth day of the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. He brought all his army. They set up camp around the city and built walls against it. 2 The city was under attack until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.

3 By the ninth day of the month, the famine in the city was bad. There was no bread for the people. 4 Then the city wall was broken. All the soldiers ran away at night through the gate between the two walls near the king's garden. The Chaldeans were all around the city. Zedekiah went toward the Arabah. 5 The Chaldean army chased the king. They caught him on the plains of Jericho. All his soldiers ran away from him.

Zedekiah Is Captured

6 They took the king to the king of Babylon at Riblah. There they judged him. 7 They killed Zedekiah's sons while he watched. Then they put out Zedekiah's eyes. They tied him with bronze chains and took him to Babylon.

Jerusalem Is Burned

8 In the nineteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, on the seventh day of the fifth month, Nebuzaradan came to Jerusalem. He was the captain of the guard and a servant of the king of Babylon. 9 He burned the house of the LORD, the king's house, and all the houses in Jerusalem. He burned every large house with fire. 10 All the Chaldean army with the captain of the guard broke down the walls around Jerusalem. 11 Nebuzaradan took the rest of the people who were left in the city. He also took the people who had joined the king of Babylon and the rest of the crowd. 12 But the captain of the guard left some of the poorest people in the land. They were to take care of the grape fields and farms.

Temple Things Are Taken

13 The Chaldeans broke the bronze pillars, the stands, and the bronze sea in the house of the LORD. They took the bronze to Babylon. 14 They took the pots, shovels, wick trimmers, spoons, and all the bronze tools used there. 15 The captain of the guard took the fire pans and bowls. Some were gold and some were silver. 16 The two pillars, the one sea, and the stands that Solomon made for the house of the LORD had more bronze than anyone could weigh. 17 One pillar was about 27 feet tall. Its bronze top was about 4.5 feet tall. It had a net of bronze and fruit shapes all around the top. The second pillar was like the first one, with a net.

Some Leaders Are Killed

18 The captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and three doorkeepers. 19 From the city he took one man who worked in the palace and was in charge of the soldiers. He also took five men who advised the king and were found in the city. He took the army writer who gathered the people. He took sixty men from the people who were found in the city. 20 Nebuzaradan took them to the king of Babylon at Riblah. 21 The king of Babylon killed them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Judah was taken away from its land.

Gedaliah Rules the Leftover People

22 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon chose Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan to rule over the people left in Judah. 23 When all the army leaders heard this, they and their men came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They were Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth from Netophah, Jaazaniah son of Maacath, and their men. 24 Gedaliah promised them and their men. He said, "Do not be afraid of the Chaldean servants. Stay in the land and serve the king of Babylon. Then it will go well with you."

Gedaliah Is Killed

25 In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah son of Elishama, from the king's family, came with ten men. They killed Gedaliah, the Jews, and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah. 26 Then all the people, small and great, and the army leaders got up and went to Egypt. They were afraid of the Chaldeans.

Jehoiachin Is Set Free

27 In the first year of Evil-Merodach king of Babylon, on the twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month, he let Jehoiachin king of Judah out of prison. This was in the thirty-seventh year after King Jehoiachin of Judah was taken away. 28 He spoke kindly to him. He gave him a seat above the seats of the other kings with him in Babylon. 29 Jehoiachin took off his prison clothes. He ate bread in front of the king all the days of his life. 30 The king gave him food every day, all the days of his life.
