God Warns Ephraim

1 How sad for the proud crown of Ephraim's people who get drunk! How sad for the fading flower that shows their beauty! It sits on the head of a rich valley. The people there are drunk with wine. 2 Look! The Lord has someone strong and mighty. He is like a hailstorm, a storm that destroys. He is like floodwaters that rush and overflow. He will throw it down to the ground with His hand. 3 The proud crown of Ephraim's people who get drunk will be trampled underfoot. 4 The fading flower of its beauty, which is on the head of a rich valley, will be like a ripe fig before summer. As soon as someone sees it, he eats it up.

The LORD Will Be a Crown for His People

5 In that day, the LORD of Armies will be a beautiful crown and a lovely headband for His people who are left. 6 He will give a spirit of fairness to the one who judges. He will give strength to those who fight back at the city gate.

Leaders Who Drink Too Much

7 These leaders also stagger from wine and stumble from strong drink. The priest and the prophet stumble from strong drink and are confused by wine. They stumble because of strong drink. They see things wrong, and they judge wrong. 8 All their tables are covered with vomit. There is no clean spot.

Who Will Listen?

9 Who will He teach? Who will understand His message? Babies who just stopped drinking milk? Little ones just taken from their mother's breast? 10 For they say, "Rule after rule, rule after rule. Line after line, line after line. A little here, a little there."

11 So He will speak to these people with strange, mocking words and a foreign tongue. 12 He said to them, "This is the place to rest. Let tired people rest here. This is a calm place." But they would not listen. 13 So the LORD's word to them will be, "Rule after rule, rule after rule. Line after line, line after line. A little here, a little there," so that they may go and fall backward. They will be broken. They will be trapped and caught.

A Warning to Jerusalem's Leaders

14 So listen to the LORD's word, you mockers who rule these people in Jerusalem. 15 You said, "We made a deal with death. We made an agreement with the grave. When the terrible trouble comes, it will not reach us. We made lies our safe place. We hid in false words."

The Cornerstone in Jerusalem

16 So the Lord, the LORD, says, "Look! I am putting a stone in Jerusalem. It is a tested stone. It is a precious cornerstone. It is a strong base. The one who trusts will not panic." 17 I will make fairness like a measuring line. I will make right living like a straight line. Hail will wash away the safe place made of lies. Water will flood the hiding place. 18 Your deal with death will be canceled. Your agreement with the grave will not last. When the terrible trouble passes through, it will trample you. 19 Each time it passes, it will grab you. It will come morning after morning, day and night. Understanding the news will bring only fear. 20 The bed is too short to lie down on. The blanket is too small to cover you.

God's Strange Work

21 The LORD will stand up like He did at Mount Perazim. He will be angry like He was in the Valley of Gibeon. He will do His work, a strange work. He will do His job, a strange job. 22 So now do not make fun. Your chains may get stronger. I heard the Lord, the LORD of Armies, say He will destroy the whole land.

God Teaches the Farmer

23 Listen to me. Hear my voice. Pay attention and hear my words. 24 Does a farmer plow all day just to plant? Does he keep turning the dirt? 25 When the ground is smooth, doesn't he plant dill and scatter cumin? Doesn't he plant wheat in rows, barley where it belongs, and rye along the edge? 26 His God teaches him and shows him the right way. 27 Dill is not beaten with a sharp farm tool. A cart wheel is not rolled over cumin. Dill is beaten with a stick. Cumin is beaten with a rod. 28 Grain is crushed, but not forever. The cart wheel rolls over it, but it does not turn it to dust. 29 This also comes from the the LORD of Armies. He gives wonderful advice and great wisdom.
