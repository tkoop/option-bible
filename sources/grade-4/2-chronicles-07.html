Fire from Heaven

1 When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven. It burned up the burnt offering and the sacrifices. The glory of the LORD filled the temple. 2 The priests could not go into the LORD’s temple, because the glory of the LORD filled it. 3 All the Israelites saw the fire come down. They saw the glory of the LORD on the temple. They knelt with their faces to the ground. They worshiped and praised the LORD. They said He is good. His loving kindness lasts forever.

Solomon Gives Offerings

4 Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices to the LORD. 5 King Solomon offered 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep. He and all the people dedicated God’s temple. 6 The priests stood at their stations. The Levites played instruments David had made to praise the LORD. They said His loving kindness lasts forever. The priests blew trumpets. All Israel stood there. 7 Solomon made the middle of the courtyard holy. It was in front of the LORD’s temple. He offered burnt offerings and fat from peace offerings there. The bronze altar he made was too small. It could not hold the food offering, the burnt offering, and the fat.

The Festival

8 At that time Solomon and all Israel with him kept the festival for seven days. A very large crowd came from Lebo-hamath to the stream of Egypt. 9 On the eighth day they held a special meeting. They had kept the altar dedication for seven days. They had kept the festival for seven days. 10 On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, he sent the people to their tents. They were happy and glad. They were glad because of the good things the LORD had done for David, Solomon, and Israel His people. 11 Solomon finished the LORD’s temple and the king’s house. He did well in all he planned to do in both.

The LORD Speaks to Solomon

12 The LORD appeared to Solomon at night. He said, “I have heard your prayer. I have chosen this place for Myself. It will be a house where people give sacrifices. 13 If I close the sky so no rain falls, or tell locusts to eat the land, or send sickness among My people, 14 and if My people who are called by My name are humble, pray, look for Me, and turn from their bad ways, then I will hear them from heaven. I will forgive their sin and heal their land.”

15 “Now My eyes will be open. My ears will listen to the prayer made in this place. 16 I have chosen and made holy this temple. My name will be here forever. My eyes and My heart will be here always.”

17 “As for you, if you walk before Me as David your father walked, and do all I commanded you to do, and keep My rules and My laws, 18 then I will make your kingdom last. I promised David your father that no one from your family would stop ruling over Israel.”

19 “But if you turn away and leave My rules and commands I gave you, and if you go serve and worship other gods, 20 then I will pull Israel out of the land I gave them. I will throw away this temple I made holy for My name. I will make it a warning and a joke to all nations.”

21 “This temple is great now, but all who pass by will be shocked. They will ask why the LORD did this to this land and temple. 22 People will answer that they left the LORD, the God of their fathers. He brought them out of Egypt. They held on to other gods, worshiped them, and served them. So He brought all this trouble on them.”
