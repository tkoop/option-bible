David Trusts the LORD

1 I trust the LORD to keep me safe. Why do you say to me, "Run away to safety"?

2 Look, bad people bend their bows. They put arrows on the string. They shoot from hiding at good people. 3 If everything falls apart, what can a good person do?

The LORD Rules from Heaven

4 The LORD is in His holy temple. His throne is in heaven. He sees everyone. He looks closely at people. 5 The LORD tests good people and bad people. He hates those who do wrong and love hurting others.

God Will Punish the Wicked

6 The LORD will rain fire and burning sulfur on bad people. A hot wind will be their share. 7 For the LORD is good. He loves good deeds. Good people will see Him.
