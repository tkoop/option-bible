God Will Help His People

1 The LORD will be kind to Jacob. He will choose Israel again. He will put them in their own land. People from other lands will join them. They will stay with Jacob’s family. 2 Other nations will take them and bring them home. Israel’s people will have them as servants. They will make slaves of the people who made them slaves. They will rule over the ones who hurt them.

God Gives His People Rest

3 On the day the LORD gives you rest, you will be free from your pain and fear. You will not do the hard work they forced on you.

A Song Against the King of Babylon

“4 On that day you will sing this song about the king of Babylon: “How the cruel ruler has stopped! How his angry power has stopped! 5 The LORD has broken the stick of bad people. He has broken the rod of rulers. 6 The cruel ruler hit nations in anger many times. He ruled people with angry power and did not stop. 7 Now all the earth is quiet and at rest. People sing for joy. 8 Even the cypress and cedar trees of Lebanon are glad because of you. They say, ‘Since you are down, no one comes to cut us down.’

“9 The grave below wakes up to meet you when you come. It stirs up dead leaders for you. It makes all kings of nations stand up from their thrones. 10 They all speak to you. They say, ‘You are weak like us now. You are just like us.’ 11 Your pride has gone down to the grave. The sound of your harps has stopped. Worms are spread under you. Bugs cover you.

“12 How you have fallen from heaven, O bright morning star, son of the dawn! You are cut down to the ground, you who hurt the nations.

“13 You said in your heart, ‘I will go up to heaven. I will set my throne above God’s stars. I will sit on the mountain where God meets, in the far north. 14 I will rise above the high clouds. I will be like the Most High.’

“15 But you are brought down to the grave, to the deep pit.

“16 People who see you stare at you. They think, ‘Is this the man who shook the earth and made kingdoms shake? 17 He made the world like a desert. He tore down its cities. He did not let his prisoners go home.’

“18 All kings of nations lie in honor, each in his own tomb. 19 But you are thrown out of your grave like a hated branch. You are covered with the dead. You are like a body that was walked on. 20 You will not be buried with them, because you ruined your land and killed your people. The children of bad people will not be named. 21 Get ready to kill his children for what their fathers did wrong. Then they will not rise and take the earth or fill cities with people.”

God’s Plan for Babylon and Assyria

22 The LORD of hosts says, “I will rise against them. I will cut off Babylon’s name and all who are left. I will cut off its children and grandchildren. 23 I will make Babylon a home for wild animals and pools of water. I will sweep it away with a broom of ruin.”

24 The LORD of hosts has sworn, “What I planned will happen. What I decided will stand. 25 I will break Assyria in My land. I will crush him on My mountains. His yoke will leave My people. His heavy load will leave their shoulders.”

26 This is the plan for the whole earth. This is the LORD’s hand stretched out over all nations. 27 The LORD of hosts has planned it. Who can stop it? His hand is stretched out. Who can turn it back?

A Warning for Philistia

28 In the year King Ahaz died, this heavy message came:

29 “Do not be glad, all Philistia, because the stick that hit you is broken. From the root of the snake will come a viper. Its fruit will be a flying, fiery snake. 30 The poor will have food. The needy will lie down in safety. I will kill your root with hunger. I will kill those who are left. 31 Cry out at your gates! Cry out in your city! Melt away, all Philistia! Smoke comes from the north. No one will be left alone in his place.”

32 What will people answer the messengers of the nations? “The LORD has built Zion. The poor of His people will be safe there.”
