A Prayer from Deep Trouble

1 the LORD, I called to You from deep trouble. 2 Lord, please hear my voice! Let Your ears listen to my cries for help. 3 If You, the LORD, kept track of wrongs, Lord, who could stand? 4 But with You there is forgiveness, so that people will respect You.

Waiting for the LORD

5 I wait for the LORD; my heart waits, and I hope in His word. 6 My heart waits for the Lord more than guards wait for the morning—more than guards wait for the morning.

Hope for Israel

7 Israel, put your hope in the LORD, for with the LORD is faithful love, and with Him is great rescue. 8 He will rescue Israel from all their wrongs.
