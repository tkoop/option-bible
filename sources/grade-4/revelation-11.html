The Two Witnesses

1 I was given a measuring stick like a rod. A voice said, "Get up and measure God's temple and the altar, and count the people worshiping there." 2 But do not measure the outer courtyard. Leave it out, because it was given to the nations. They will walk all over the holy city for 42 months.

3 I will give power to my two witnesses. They will speak God's message for 1,260 days. They will wear rough cloth. 4 These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.

5 If anyone tries to hurt them, fire comes from their mouths and eats up their enemies. Anyone who tries to hurt them must be killed. 6 They have power to shut the sky so rain does not fall while they speak God's message. They have power to turn water into blood. They can hit the earth with any plague whenever they want.

7 When they finish speaking, the beast that comes up from the deep pit will fight them. It will beat them and kill them. 8 Their bodies will lie in the street of the big city. That city is called Sodom and Egypt in a spiritual way. It is where their Lord was nailed to a cross. 9 People from many nations, tribes, languages, and groups will look at their bodies for three and a half days. They will not let the bodies be buried.

10 The people who live on earth will be happy about this. They will celebrate and send gifts to each other, because these two prophets made life hard for them. 11 But after three and a half days, God's breath of life went into them. They stood up. The people watching them were very afraid.

12 Then they heard a loud voice from heaven say, "Come up here!" They went up to heaven in a cloud. Their enemies watched them. 13 At that same hour there was a big earthquake. A tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed. The rest were afraid and gave praise to God in heaven. 14 The second disaster is over. Look, the third disaster is coming soon.

The Seventh Trumpet

15 The seventh angel blew his horn. Loud voices in heaven said, "The world's kingdom now belongs to our Lord and His Christ. He will rule forever and ever." 16 The 24 elders who sit on thrones before God fell on their faces and worshiped God.

17 They said, "We thank You, Lord God Almighty. You are the One who is and who was. You have taken Your great power and now You rule. 18 The nations were angry. Your anger came. It is time to judge the dead, to give Your reward to Your servants, the prophets and the holy people, and to all who fear Your name, small and great, and to destroy those who destroy the earth."

19 Then God's temple in heaven opened. People could see the box of His agreement in His temple. There were flashes of lightning, voices, thunder, an earthquake, and big hail.
