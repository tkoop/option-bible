Share What You Have

1 Share your food with others. After many days, you will find it again. 2 Give part to seven people, and even to eight. You do not know what trouble will come to the land. 3 If clouds get full, they pour rain on the earth. If a tree falls to the south or north, it stays where it fell. 4 If you wait for the wind, you will not plant. If you watch the clouds, you will not harvest.

Work Even When You Do Not Know

5 You do not know how the wind moves. You do not know how bones grow before a baby is born. In the same way, you do not know the work of God, who makes everything. 6 Plant your seeds in the morning. Do not stop working in the evening. You do not know which will do well—this one or that one—or if both will be good.

Enjoy the Good Days

7 Light is sweet. It is good to see the sun. 8 If a man lives many years, let him be happy in all of them. But let him remember the dark days. They will be many. Everything that comes is like a puff of smoke.

9 Young man, be happy in your youth. Let your heart cheer you in your young days. Walk in the way your heart wants and in what your eyes see. But know that God will judge you for all these things. 10 Take sorrow out of your heart. Put bad things away from your body. Youth and young days are like a puff of smoke.
