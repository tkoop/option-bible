Honesty and Fairness

1The LORD hates cheating scales. But fair weights make Him happy. 2When people are proud, shame comes. But humble people get wisdom. 3Good people are guided by honesty. But crooked people are ruined by their tricks.

4Money can't save you on the day of God's anger. But doing right can save you from death. 5Doing right keeps good people on the right path. But bad people fall because of their own bad choices. 6Doing right saves good people. But traitors get trapped by their own wants.

Good and Bad People

7When a bad person dies, their hope dies too. Their proud hopes are gone. 8Good people are saved from trouble. But bad people get into trouble. 9A person who doesn't respect God can ruin a neighbor with words. But good people are saved by knowing what's right.

10When good people do well, the whole town is happy. When bad people are gone, people cheer. 11Good people's blessings make a city great. But bad people's words destroy it.

Words and Friends

12Someone who looks down on a neighbor has no sense. A wise person stays quiet. 13A gossip tells secrets. But a loyal person keeps them. 14Without good advice, a nation falls. With many wise helpers, it is saved.

15A person who promises to pay a stranger's debt will get hurt. But a person who refuses to promise is safe. 16A kind woman keeps her good name. But harsh men keep their money. 17A kind person helps himself. But a cruel person hurts himself.

Doing Right Leads to Life

18Bad people earn pay that tricks them. But people who do right get a true reward. 19Doing right truly leads to life. But chasing evil leads to death. 20The LORD hates people with crooked hearts. But He is pleased with people who live right.

21Bad people will be punished. But good people's children will be saved. 22A pretty woman with no good sense is like a gold ring in a pig's nose. 23Good people want only good things. But bad people's hopes bring anger.

Giving and Money

24Some people give freely and get more. Others hold back what they should give and become poor. 25A generous person will do well. Someone who helps others will be helped too. 26People will curse the one who hides food. But blessings will go to the one who sells it.

27People who look for good find favor. But people who look for trouble find trouble. 28People who trust in money will fall. But good people will grow like green leaves. 29A person who troubles his family will get nothing. A foolish person will serve a wise person.

30What good people do is like a tree of life. A person who leads others to do right is wise. 31If good people are punished on earth, how much more will bad and sinful people be!
