Happy People Who Follow God's Law

1 Happy are people who live without blame and follow the LORD's law. 2 Happy are people who keep His rules and look for Him with all their heart. 3 They do no wrong. They walk in His ways. 4 You have told us to keep Your rules carefully.

5 I wish my ways were steady to keep Your rules. 6 Then I would not be ashamed when I look at all Your commands. 7 I will thank You with an honest heart when I learn Your right ways. 8 I will keep Your rules. Do not leave me alone.

Keeping Life Clean by God's Word

9 How can a young person keep his way clean? By living by Your word. 10 I look for You with all my heart. Do not let me stray from Your commands. 11 I have hidden Your word in my heart so I will not sin against You. 12 Praise be to You, the LORD. Teach me Your rules.

13 I have told with my lips all the laws You speak. 14 I am happy in the way of Your rules, like someone who finds great treasure. 15 I will think about Your commands and look at Your ways. 16 I delight in Your rules. I will not forget Your word.

God's Servant Asks for Help

17 Be good to Your servant, and I will live and keep Your word. 18 Open my eyes so I can see wonderful things in Your law. 19 I am a stranger on earth. Do not hide Your commands from me. 20 My soul is worn out from wanting Your laws all the time.

21 You scold the proud who are cursed, who stray from Your commands. 22 Take away shame and mocking from me, for I keep Your rules. 23 Even leaders sit and talk against me, but Your servant thinks about Your rules. 24 Your rules are my joy. They are my wise helpers.

Clinging to God's Word in Trouble

25 My soul clings to the dust. Give me life by Your word. 26 I told You my ways, and You answered me. Teach me Your rules. 27 Help me understand the way of Your commands, and I will think about Your wonders. 28 My soul is sad and tired. Make me strong by Your word.

29 Keep me from the false way. Be kind to me and teach me Your law. 30 I have chosen the way of truth. I have set Your laws before me. 31 I hold fast to Your rules. the LORD, do not let me be ashamed. 32 I run the way of Your commands, for You make my heart wide.

Asking God to Teach His Ways

33 Teach me, the LORD, the way of Your rules, and I will keep them to the end. 34 Give me understanding, and I will keep Your law and guard it with all my heart. 35 Lead me in the path of Your commands, for I delight in it. 36 Turn my heart to Your rules, and not to getting money.

37 Turn my eyes from worthless things. Give me life in Your ways. 38 Keep Your promise to Your servant, who fears You. 39 Take away my shame, which I fear, for Your laws are good. 40 I long for Your commands. Give me life in Your right ways.

God's Kindness and Salvation

41 Let Your loving-kindness come to me, the LORD, Your salvation as You promised. 42 Then I can answer those who make fun of me, for I trust in Your word. 43 Do not take the word of truth from my mouth, for I hope in Your laws. 44 I will keep Your law always, forever and ever.

45 I will walk in a wide place, for I seek Your commands. 46 I will speak of Your rules before kings and not be ashamed. 47 I delight in Your commands, which I love. 48 I lift my hands to Your commands, which I love, and I think about Your rules.

Comfort in God's Word

49 Remember Your word to Your servant, on which You made me hope. 50 This is my comfort in my trouble: Your word has given me life. 51 Proud people mock me, but I do not turn from Your law. 52 I remember Your laws from long ago, the LORD, and I am comforted.

53 Hot anger grabs me because wicked people leave Your law. 54 Your rules are my songs in the house where I stay. 55 The LORD, I remember Your name at night, and I keep Your law. 56 This has happened to me because I keep Your commands.

The LORD Is My Portion

57 The LORD is my share. I said I would keep Your words. 58 I ask for Your favor with all my heart. Be kind to me as You promised. 59 I thought about my ways and turned my feet to Your rules. 60 I hurried and did not wait to keep Your commands.

61 The cords of wicked people trap me, but I do not forget Your law. 62 At midnight I rise to thank You for Your right laws. 63 I am a friend to all who fear You and keep Your commands. 64 The earth is full of Your loving-kindness, the LORD. Teach me Your rules.

Learning from Hard Times

65 You have done good to Your servant, the LORD, as You promised. 66 Teach me good sense and knowledge, for I believe in Your commands. 67 Before I was in trouble I went astray, but now I keep Your word. 68 You are good and do good. Teach me Your rules.

69 Proud people tell lies about me, but I keep Your commands with all my heart. 70 Their heart is fat like grease, but I delight in Your law. 71 It was good for me to be in trouble, so I can learn Your rules. 72 Your law from Your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver.

Made by God's Hands

73 Your hands made me and shaped me. Give me understanding to learn Your commands. 74 Those who fear You see me and are glad, because I hope in Your word. 75 I know, the LORD, that Your laws are right, and that in faithfulness You troubled me. 76 Let Your loving-kindness comfort me, as You promised Your servant.

77 Let Your mercy come to me so I may live, for Your law is my delight. 78 Let the proud be ashamed, for they wronged me. I think about Your commands. 79 Let those who fear You turn to me, and they will know Your rules. 80 Let my heart be blameless in Your rules, so I will not be ashamed.

Waiting for God's Help

81 My soul faints for Your salvation. I hope in Your word. 82 My eyes are tired looking for Your promise. I ask when You will comfort me. 83 I am like a dry bag in smoke, but I do not forget Your rules. 84 How many are the days of Your servant? When will You judge those who chase me?

85 Proud people have dug pits for me. They do not follow Your law. 86 All Your commands are faithful. They chase me with lies. Help me. 87 They almost finished me on earth, but I did not leave Your commands. 88 Give me life by Your loving-kindness, and I will keep the rules You speak.

God's Word Lasts Forever

89 Your word, the LORD, stands forever in heaven. 90 Your faithfulness lasts for all time. You made the earth, and it stands. 91 Your laws stand today, for all things serve You. 92 If Your law had not been my delight, I would have died in my trouble.

93 I will never forget Your commands, for by them You gave me life. 94 I am Yours. Save me, for I seek Your commands. 95 Wicked people wait to destroy me, but I think about Your rules. 96 I have seen that everything has an end, but Your command is very wide.

Loving God's Law

97 Oh, how I love Your law! I think about it all day. 98 Your commands make me wiser than my enemies, for they are always with me. 99 I have more understanding than all my teachers, for Your rules are my thought. 100 I understand more than the old, for I keep Your commands.

101 I keep my feet from every evil path, so I can keep Your word. 102 I do not turn from Your laws, for You have taught me. 103 How sweet are Your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth! 104 I get understanding from Your commands. So I hate every false way.

God's Word Lights the Way

105 Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. 106 I have promised and I will keep it: I will keep Your right laws. 107 I am very troubled, the LORD. Give me life as You promised. 108 Please accept the free gifts of my mouth, the LORD. Teach me Your laws.

109 My life is always in danger, but I do not forget Your law. 110 Wicked people set a trap for me, but I do not stray from Your commands. 111 Your rules are my treasure forever, for they are the joy of my heart. 112 I turn my heart to do Your rules forever, to the end.

Choosing God's Law

113 I hate people with two minds, but I love Your law. 114 You are my hiding place and my shield. I hope in Your word. 115 Go away from me, you evildoers, so I can keep my God's commands. 116 Hold me up as You promised, and I will live. Do not let me be ashamed of my hope.

117 Hold me up, and I will be safe. I will always look at Your rules. 118 You throw away all who stray from Your rules, for their trickery is false. 119 You put away all the wicked of the earth like trash. So I love Your rules. 120 My flesh trembles in fear of You. I am afraid of Your laws.

Doing What Is Right

121 I have done what is right and fair. Do not leave me to those who hurt me. 122 Promise good for Your servant. Do not let proud people hurt me. 123 My eyes are tired looking for Your salvation and for Your right word. 124 Deal with Your servant by Your loving-kindness, and teach me Your rules.

125 I am Your servant. Give me understanding so I may know Your rules. 126 It is time for the LORD to act, for they have broken Your law. 127 So I love Your commands more than gold, more than fine gold. 128 So I follow all Your commands. I hate every false way.

Wonderful Rules

129 Your rules are wonderful. So my soul keeps them. 130 The opening of Your words gives light. It makes simple people wise. 131 I open my mouth and pant, for I long for Your commands. 132 Turn to me and be kind to me, as You do for those who love Your name.

133 Make my steps steady by Your word. Do not let sin rule over me. 134 Save me from people who hurt me, and I will keep Your commands. 135 Make Your face shine on Your servant, and teach me Your rules. 136 My eyes pour down streams of water because people do not keep Your law.

God Is Right

137 You are right, the LORD, and Your laws are right. 138 You have commanded Your rules, and they are right and faithful. 139 I am worn out because my enemies forget Your words. 140 Your word is very pure, and Your servant loves it.

141 I am small and looked down on, but I do not forget Your commands. 142 Your right way lasts forever, and Your law is true. 143 Trouble and sadness find me, but Your commands are my delight. 144 Your rules are right forever. Give me understanding and I will live.

Calling to God

145 I call with all my heart. Answer me, the LORD. I will keep Your rules. 146 I call to You. Save me, and I will keep Your rules. 147 I rise before dawn and cry for help. I hope in Your words. 148 My eyes open before the night watches to think about Your word.

149 Hear my voice, the LORD, by Your loving-kindness. Give me life by Your laws. 150 People who chase evil come near. They are far from Your law. 151 You are near, the LORD, and all Your commands are true. 152 Long ago I learned from Your rules that You made them to last forever.

Asking for Rescue

153 Look at my trouble and save me, for I do not forget Your law. 154 Fight my fight and save me. Give me life by Your word. 155 Salvation is far from wicked people, for they do not seek Your rules. 156 The LORD, Your mercies are many. Give me life by Your laws.

157 Many chase me and hurt me, but I do not turn from Your rules. 158 I see people who are not faithful and I am angry, because they do not keep Your word. 159 See how I love Your commands, the LORD. Give me life by Your loving-kindness. 160 All of Your word is true, and all Your right laws last forever.

Peace for Those Who Love God's Law

161 Leaders chase me for no reason, but my heart fears Your words. 162 I rejoice in Your word like one who finds great treasure. 163 I hate and turn away from lies, but I love Your law. 164 Seven times a day I praise You for Your right laws.

165 Great peace belongs to those who love Your law, and nothing makes them stumble. 166 I hope for Your salvation, the LORD. I do Your commands. 167 My soul keeps Your rules, and I love them very much. 168 I keep Your commands and
