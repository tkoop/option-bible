David Prays for Help

1 For the music leader. Use stringed instruments. This is a song of David. The LORD, do not scold me when You are angry. Do not punish me when You are very angry. 2 Be kind to me, the LORD, because I am weak. Heal me, the LORD, because my bones hurt.

3 I am very upset. How long, the LORD? How long? 4 Come back, the LORD, and save me. Save me because You are kind. 5 No one remembers You when they die. Who can praise You from the grave?

6 I am tired from crying. All night I cry in bed, and my bed is wet with tears. 7 My eyes are weak from sadness. They are tired because of all my enemies.

The LORD Hears David

8 Go away from me, all you who do wrong. The LORD has heard my crying. 9 The LORD has heard my cry for help. The LORD will listen to my prayer. 10 May all my enemies be ashamed and very afraid. May they turn and run away in shame.
