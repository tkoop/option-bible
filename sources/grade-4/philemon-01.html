Paul Writes to Philemon

1 Paul, a prisoner for Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, write to Philemon. Philemon is our dear friend and helper. 2 We also write to Apphia our sister, Archippus our fellow soldier, and the church that meets in your house. 3 I pray that God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ will give you grace and peace.

Paul Thanks God for Philemon

4 I thank my God all the time. I remember you when I pray. 5 I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus. I hear about your love for all God's people. 6 I pray that your sharing of faith will help you know all the good things we have in Christ. 7 Your love has given me much joy and comfort. You have made God's people feel rested.

Paul Asks Philemon to Welcome Onesimus

8 In Christ, I could tell you what to do. That would be the right thing. 9 But I would rather ask you because of love. I am Paul, an old man. Now I am also a prisoner for Christ Jesus. 10 I ask you to care for my child Onesimus. I became his father in the faith while I was in chains. 11 He was not useful to you before. But now he is useful to you and to me. 12 I am sending him back to you. He is very dear to me. 13 I wanted to keep him with me. Then he could help me while I am in chains for the good news. 14 But I did not want to do anything without your permission. I want your good deed to be by choice, not by force. 15 Maybe he was away from you for a little while so you could have him back forever. 16 He is not just a slave now. He is more than a slave. He is a dear brother. He is very dear to me. He should be even more dear to you, both in this life and in the Lord. 17 So if you think of me as a partner, welcome him as you would welcome me. 18 If he has wronged you in any way, or if he owes you anything, charge it to me. 19 I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will pay it back. I will not even mention that you owe me your own self. 20 Yes, brother, let me have joy from you in the Lord. Make my heart feel rested in Christ. 21 I write to you because I trust you will obey. I know you will do even more than I ask.

Paul Says Goodbye

22 Also, get a room ready for me. I hope that through your prayers I will be set free and come to you. 23 Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, says hello to you. 24 Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my helpers, say hello to you. 25 May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
