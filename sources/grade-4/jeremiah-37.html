Zedekiah Becomes King

1 Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah. Zedekiah ruled instead of Jehoiachin son of Jehoiakim. 2 But Zedekiah, his helpers, and the people did not listen to the words the the LORD had spoken through Jeremiah the prophet.

Jeremiah Is Asked to Pray

3 King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah the priest, son of Maaseiah, to Jeremiah the prophet. They said, "Please pray to the the LORD our God for us." 4 Jeremiah could walk freely among the people, because he had not been put in prison yet.

The Babylonians Leave for a Time

5 Pharaoh's army came out of Egypt. When the Babylonians who were attacking Jerusalem heard about them, they left Jerusalem.

God's Message About Egypt

6 Then the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet. 7 The the LORD, the God of Israel, said, "Tell the king of Judah, who sent you to ask Me, 'Pharaoh's army that came to help you will go back to Egypt.' 8 Then the Babylonians will come back and fight this city. They will take it and burn it down."

Do Not Be Fooled

9 The the LORD said, "Do not fool yourselves. You may say, 'The Babylonians will leave us.' But they will not! 10 Even if you beat the whole Babylonian army, so that only hurt men are left in their tents, they would still get up and burn this city down."

Jeremiah Is Arrested

11 After the Babylonian army left Jerusalem because of Pharaoh's army, 12 Jeremiah started to leave the city. He was going to the land of Benjamin to get his share of the land among the people there. 13 When he reached the Benjamin Gate, Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, the guard captain, arrested him. Irijah said, "You are running away to the Babylonians!"

14 Jeremiah said, "That is not true! I am not running away to the Babylonians." But Irijah would not listen to him. Instead, he arrested Jeremiah and brought him to the leaders.

15 The leaders were angry with Jeremiah. They beat him and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the secretary. They had made that house into a prison.

Jeremiah in Prison

16 Jeremiah was put in a deep cell in a dungeon. He stayed there many days.

The King's Private Question

17 King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah and had him brought to the palace. He asked him privately, "Is there any word from the the LORD?" Jeremiah answered, "Yes. You will be handed over to the king of Babylon."

Jeremiah Defends Himself

18 Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, "What wrong did I do to you, your helpers, or these people, that you put me in prison? 19 Where are your prophets who said to you, 'The king of Babylon will not come against you or this land'? 20 But now, my lord the king, please listen to me. Let my request come before you. Do not send me back to Jonathan's house, or I will die there."

Jeremiah Moved to the Courtyard

21 King Zedekiah ordered that Jeremiah be put in the courtyard of the guard. He was to get a loaf of bread each day from the bakers' street, until all the bread in the city was gone. So Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.
