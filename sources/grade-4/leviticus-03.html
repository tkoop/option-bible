Peace Offering from a Cow

1 If someone brings a peace offering from the cows, it can be a male or a female. It must have no flaws. Bring it before the LORD. 2 Put your hand on the head of the gift. Kill it at the door of the meeting tent. Aaron's sons, the priests, will splash the blood all around the altar. 3 From the peace offering, bring a burned gift to the LORD. Bring the fat that covers the inside parts. Bring all the fat on the inside parts. 4 Bring the two kidneys and the fat on them near the lower back. Also bring the covering on the liver. Take these off with the kidneys. 5 Aaron's sons will burn it on the altar. It goes on top of the burned gift on the wood fire. It is a sweet smell to the LORD.

Peace Offering from a Sheep

6 If the peace offering comes from the flock, it can be a male or a female. It must have no flaws. 7 If someone brings a lamb, bring it before the LORD. 8 Put your hand on its head. Kill it before the meeting tent. Aaron's sons will splash the blood all around the altar. 9 From the peace offering, bring a burned gift to the LORD. Bring the whole fat tail. Cut it off near the backbone. Bring the fat that covers the inside parts. Bring all the fat on the inside parts. 10 Bring the two kidneys and the fat on them near the lower back. Also bring the covering on the liver. Take these off with the kidneys. 11 The priest will burn it on the altar. It is a food gift to the LORD.

Peace Offering from a Goat

12 If the offering is a goat, bring it before the LORD. 13 Put your hand on its head. Kill it before the meeting tent. Aaron's sons will splash the blood all around the altar. 14 From it, bring a burned gift to the LORD. Bring the fat that covers the inside parts. Bring all the fat on the inside parts. 15 Bring the two kidneys and the fat on them near the lower back. Also bring the covering on the liver. Take these off with the kidneys. 16 The priest will burn them on the altar. It is a food gift. It smells sweet. All the fat belongs to the LORD.

A Rule for All Time

17 This is a rule forever for all your family lines, wherever you live. Do not eat any fat or any blood.
