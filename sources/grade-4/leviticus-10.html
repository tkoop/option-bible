Nadab and Abihu Offer Wrong Fire

1 Nadab and Abihu were Aaron's sons. Each man took his pan. They put fire in the pans and added incense. They brought fire to the LORD that He did not ask for. 2 Fire came from the LORD. It burned them up. They died in front of the LORD.

Moses Speaks to Aaron

3 Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the LORD said: 'I will show that I am holy among those close to Me. I will be honored by all the people.'" Aaron said nothing.

4 Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan. They were sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle. Moses said, "Come here. Carry your relatives away from the holy place. Take them outside the camp." 5 They came and carried them in their shirts. They took them outside the camp, just as Moses said.

6 Moses said to Aaron, Eleazar, and Ithamar, "Do not mess up your hair. Do not tear your clothes. If you do, you will die. The LORD will be angry with all the people. But all the Israelites may cry for the men the LORD burned. 7 Do not leave the door of the meeting tent. If you do, you will die. The LORD's special oil is on you." They did as Moses said.

God Gives Rules for the Priests

8 Then the LORD spoke to Aaron. 9 He said, "You and your sons must not drink wine or strong drink when you go into the meeting tent. If you do, you will die. This rule is for all time, for your family after you. 10 You must tell the difference between holy things and common things. You must tell the difference between clean things and unclean things. 11 You must teach the Israelites all the rules the LORD gave them through Moses."

Food for the Priests

12 Moses said to Aaron, Eleazar, and Ithamar, "Take the grain gift that is left from the food gifts given to the LORD. Eat it without yeast beside the altar. It is very holy. 13 Eat it in the holy place. It is your share and your sons' share from the LORD's food gifts. The LORD told me to do this. 14 But you, your sons, and your daughters may eat the breast meat that was waved and the thigh meat that was given. Eat it in a clean place. The LORD gave these to you and your sons. They are your share from the peace gifts of the Israelites. 15 The given thigh and the waved breast must be brought with the fat parts of the food gifts. Wave them before the LORD. This will be your share and your children's share forever. The LORD commanded this."

Moses Checks the Sin Offering

16 Moses asked about the goat for the sin gift. He found it had been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's other sons. He asked, "17 Why did you not eat the sin gift in the holy place? It is very holy. The LORD gave it to you to take away the people's wrong. You were to make things right for them before the LORD. 18 Its blood was not brought into the holy place. So you should have eaten the goat in the holy place, as I told you."

19 Aaron replied to Moses, "Today my sons gave their sin gift and burnt gift to the LORD. But bad things have happened to me. Would the LORD be happy if I ate the sin gift today?" 20 Moses heard this and was satisfied.
