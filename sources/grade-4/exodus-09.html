God Sends Sickness on Animals

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and say, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let My people go. They need to serve Me.' 2 But if you refuse to let them go and keep holding them back, 3 then the LORD will make a very bad sickness hit your animals in the field. It will hit your horses, donkeys, camels, cows, and sheep. 4 But the LORD will make a difference between Israel's animals and Egypt's animals. None of the animals that belong to the people of Israel will die."

5 The LORD set a time and said, "Tomorrow the LORD will do this in the land."

6 The next day the LORD did it. All the animals of the Egyptians died. But not one animal that belonged to the people of Israel died.

7 Pharaoh sent men to look. Not even one of Israel's animals had died. But Pharaoh was stubborn, and he would not let the people go.

Sores on People and Animals

8 Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Take handfuls of black dust from a fire. In front of Pharaoh, Moses will throw it up into the air."

9 It will turn into dust all over Egypt. Then bad sores will break out on people and animals all over Egypt.

10 So they took soot from the fire and stood in front of Pharaoh. Moses threw it into the air. Then sores broke out on people and animals.

11 The magic men could not stand in front of Moses, because the sores were on them and on all the Egyptians.

12 But the LORD made Pharaoh's heart hard, and he would not listen to them. This was just like the LORD had said to Moses.

Hail Is Coming

13 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Get up early in the morning. Stand in front of Pharaoh and say, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: Let My people go. They need to serve Me. 14 This time I will send all My plagues on you, your officials, and your people. Then you will know there is no one like Me in the whole earth. 15 By now I could have used My hand to strike you and your people with sickness. Then you would be gone from the earth. 16 But I let you live for this reason: I want to show you My power. I want people all over the earth to know My name. 17 But you still act proud against My people. You still will not let them go. 18 Look, tomorrow at this time I will make very heavy hail fall. There has never been hail like it in Egypt since Egypt began. 19 So now send and bring in your animals and everything you have in the field. Any person or animal left in the field and not brought into a house will die when the hail falls on them.'"

20 Some of Pharaoh's officials feared what the LORD said. They hurried to bring their servants and animals inside. 21 But others did not care about what the LORD said. They left their servants and animals in the field.

Hail Falls on Egypt

22 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Reach out your hand toward the sky. Then hail will fall all over Egypt. It will hit people, animals, and every plant in the field."

23 Moses reached out his stick toward the sky. The LORD sent thunder and hail. Lightning flashed to the ground. The LORD rained hail on Egypt. 24 The hail was very heavy. There had never been hail like it in Egypt since it became a nation.

25 All over Egypt, the hail hit everything in the fields. It hit people and animals. It beat down every plant and broke every tree.

26 Only in Goshen, where the people of Israel lived, did no hail fall.

Pharaoh Says He Was Wrong

27 Then Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron. He said, "This time I have sinned. The LORD is right, and I and my people are wrong. 28 Pray to the LORD. The thunder and hail are too much. I will let you go. You do not need to stay any longer."

29 Moses said to him, "As soon as I leave the city, I will lift my hands to the LORD. The thunder will stop. There will be no more hail. Then you will know that the earth belongs to the LORD. 30 But I know you and your officials do not yet fear the LORD God."

31 The flax and barley were ruined, because the barley was ripe and the flax was in bloom. 32 But the wheat and another grain were not ruined, because they were not ready yet.

33 Moses left Pharaoh and went out of the city. He lifted his hands to the LORD. The thunder and hail stopped. The rain did not pour down on the land anymore.

34 When Pharaoh saw that the rain, hail, and thunder had stopped, he sinned again. He and his officials became stubborn again.

35 Pharaoh's heart stayed hard. He would not let the people of Israel go. This was just like the LORD had said through Moses.
