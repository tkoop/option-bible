David Makes Solomon King

1 David was very old. He made his son Solomon king over Israel. 2 David gathered all the leaders of Israel, the priests, and the Levites.

Levites Are Counted

3 The Levites who were 30 years old or older were counted. There were 38,000 men. 4 Of these, 24,000 were to oversee the work of the house of the LORD. Six thousand were officers and judges. 5 Four thousand were gatekeepers. Four thousand praised the LORD with instruments David made for praise.

Levite Family Groups

6 David divided them into groups for the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 7 Gershon's sons were Laadan and Shimei. 8 Laadan's sons were Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel. There were three. 9 Shimei's sons were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran. There were three. These men were leaders of Laadan's family groups. 10 Shimei also had four sons: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. 11 Jahath was the chief, and Zizah was second. Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one family group.

Aaron and Moses

12 Kohath's sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. There were four. 13 Amram's sons were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart forever to be most holy. He was to burn incense before the LORD, serve Him, and bless in His name forever. 14 Moses was a man of God. His sons were counted with the tribe of Levi. 15 Moses' sons were Gershom and Eliezer. 16 Gershom's son was Shebuel the chief. 17 Eliezer's sons were Rehabiah the chief. Eliezer had no other sons. But Rehabiah had many sons. 18 Izhar's son was Shelomith the chief. 19 Hebron's sons were Jeriah the chief, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth. 20 Uzziel's sons were Micah the chief and Isshiah second. 21 Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli's sons were Eleazar and Kish. 22 Eleazar died and had no sons, only daughters. Their relatives, the sons of Kish, married them. 23 Mushi's sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth. There were three.

Jobs for the Levites

24 These were Levi's descendants, grouped by family. Their leaders were counted by name. All the men were 20 years old or older. They did the work for the house of the LORD.

25 David said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to His people. He lives in Jerusalem forever."

26 So the Levites did not need to carry the tent and all its things for service anymore. 27 For by David's last words, the Levites were counted from 20 years old and up. 28 Their job was to help Aaron's sons serve in the house of the LORD. They worked in the courtyards and rooms. They helped make all holy things clean. They did the work of the house of God. 29 They also cared for the holy bread, fine flour for grain offerings, bread without yeast, food cooked in pans, mixed dough, and all measuring tools. 30 They stood every morning to thank and praise the LORD. They did the same in the evening. 31 They helped with all burnt offerings given to the LORD on Sabbaths, new moons, and special days. They did this by number, as the rules said, always before the LORD. 32 They kept watch over the tent of meeting, the holy place, and their brothers, Aaron's sons. They served in the house of the LORD.
