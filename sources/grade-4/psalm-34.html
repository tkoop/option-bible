A Song by David, When He Acted Crazy Before Abimelech

David Praises the LORD

1 I will praise the LORD at all times. His praise will always be on my lips. 2 I am proud of the LORD. Let people who are hurting hear and be happy. 3 Praise the LORD with me. Let us tell how great His name is together.

The LORD Saves Those Who Trust Him

4 I looked for the LORD, and He answered me. He saved me from all my fears. 5 People who look to Him shine with joy. Their faces will not look ashamed. 6 This poor man called out, and the LORD heard him. He saved him from all his troubles.

The LORD Protects His People

7 The angel of the LORD stays near those who respect Him, and he saves them. 8 Taste and see that the LORD is good. The man who trusts in Him is happy. 9 Respect the LORD, you His people, for those who respect Him do not lack anything. 10 Even young lions may go hungry, but those who look for the LORD will not lack any good thing.

Teaching Children to Respect the LORD

11 "Come, my children, listen to me. I will teach you to respect the LORD. 12 Who wants to live and see good days? 13 Keep your tongue from evil. Keep your lips from lies. 14 Turn from evil and do good. Look for peace and go after it."

The LORD Watches Over People

15 The eyes of the LORD are on good people. His ears listen to their cries. 16 The LORD is against people who do evil. He will make people forget them on the earth.

The LORD Saves People in Trouble

17 Good people cry out, and the LORD hears them. He saves them from all their troubles. 18 The LORD is near to people who are sad and hurting. He saves those who are sorry and humble. 19 Good people may have many troubles, but the LORD saves them from all of them. 20 He guards all their bones. Not one of them is broken.

Evil People Will Be Punished

21 Evil will destroy bad people. Those who hate good people will be punished. 22 The LORD saves people who serve Him. No one who trusts in Him will be punished.
