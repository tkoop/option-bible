God Tells Moses Where to Camp

1 The LORD said to Moses, 2 "Tell the people of Israel to turn back. Have them camp in front of Pi Hahiroth. It is between Migdol and the sea. Camp by the sea, in front of Baal Zephon. 3 Pharaoh will think the people of Israel are lost. He will think the desert has trapped them. 4 I will make Pharaoh's heart hard. He will chase them. I will show My glory through Pharaoh and all his army. Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD." And the people did as they were told.

Pharaoh Changes His Mind

5 Someone told the king of Egypt that the people had run away. Then Pharaoh and his servants changed their minds. They said, "What have we done? We let Israel go. They do not have to work for us anymore." 6 Pharaoh got his chariot ready. He took his army with him. 7 He took 600 of his best chariots. He took all the chariots of Egypt. Officers rode in each one.

8 The LORD made Pharaoh's heart hard. The king of Egypt chased the people of Israel. The people of Israel left proudly. 9 The Egyptians chased them. They had horses, chariots, and men on horses. They had a big army. They caught up with the Israelites. The Israelites were camped by the sea near Pi Hahiroth, in front of Baal Zephon.

The People Are Afraid

10 Pharaoh came close. The people of Israel looked up. They saw the Egyptians coming after them. They were very afraid. They cried out to the LORD. 11 They said to Moses, "Are there no graves in Egypt? Did you bring us here to die in the desert? Why did you do this to us? Why did you bring us out of Egypt? 12 Didn't we tell you in Egypt to leave us alone so we could serve the Egyptians? It is better to serve the Egyptians than to die in the desert."

Moses Helps the People

13 Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid. Stand still. See how the LORD will save you today. Today you see the Egyptians. Never again will you see them. 14 The LORD will fight for you. Be quiet."

God Tells Moses What to Do

15 The LORD said to Moses, "Why are you crying to Me? Tell the people of Israel to move on. 16 Lift up your staff. Stretch out your hand over the sea. Split the sea open. Then the people of Israel can walk through the sea on dry ground. 17 I will make the hearts of the Egyptians hard. They will follow the people of Israel. I will show My glory through Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen. 18 Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD."

The Cloud Moves Behind the People

19 The angel of God had been going in front of the camp of Israel. Now he moved and went behind them. The pillar of cloud moved from in front of them and stood behind them. 20 It stood between the Egyptian camp and the Israelite camp. The cloud brought darkness to the Egyptians. It gave light to the Israelites. The two groups did not come near each other all night.

The Sea Opens Up

21 Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. The LORD pushed the sea back with a strong east wind all night. He made the sea dry land. The water split apart. 22 The people of Israel walked through the sea on dry ground. The water stood like a wall on their right and on their left.

The Egyptians Follow

23 All of Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and men on horses followed them into the sea. 24 Before dawn, the LORD looked down from the pillar of fire and cloud at the Egyptian camp. He made the camp panic. He took the wheels off their chariots. He made the chariots hard to drive. 25 The Egyptians said, "Let us run away from Israel! The LORD is fighting for them against us!"

The Sea Comes Back

26 The LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea. The water will come back over the Egyptians, their chariots, and their horsemen." 27 Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. At daybreak, the sea went back to its normal place. The Egyptians tried to run from it. But the LORD threw them into the sea. 28 The water came back. It covered the chariots, the horsemen, and all of Pharaoh's army that had followed them into the sea. Not one of them was left.

Israel Is Saved

29 The people of Israel had walked through the sea on dry ground. The water had stood like a wall on their right and on their left. 30 That day the LORD saved Israel from the Egyptians. Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the shore. 31 Israel saw the great thing the LORD had done in Egypt. The people feared the LORD. They believed in the LORD and in Moses, His servant.
