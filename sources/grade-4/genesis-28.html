Isaac Sends Jacob Away

1 Isaac called Jacob and blessed him. He said, "Do not marry a woman from Canaan. 2 Go to Paddan Aram. Go to your mother's father Bethuel's house. Marry one of Laban's daughters. Laban is your mother's brother. 3 May God Almighty bless you. May He make you grow and have many children. May you become a big group of people. 4 May He give you Abraham's blessing. May it be for you and your children. Then you can take the land where you live. God gave that land to Abraham."

5 Isaac sent Jacob away. Jacob went to Paddan Aram, to Laban. Laban was Bethuel's son. Bethuel was an Aramean. Laban was the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

Esau Marries Again

6 Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him to Paddan Aram to find a wife. Isaac had told Jacob not to marry a woman from Canaan. 7 Jacob listened to his father and mother. Then he went to Paddan Aram. 8 Esau saw that his father did not like the Canaanite women. 9 So Esau went to Ishmael. He married Mahalath. She was Ishmael's daughter. Ishmael was Abraham's son. Mahalath was the sister of Nebaioth. This was besides Esau's other wives.

Jacob's Dream

10 Jacob left Beersheba and went toward Haran. 11 He came to a place and stayed there for the night, because the sun had gone down. He took a stone from that place. He put it under his head. Then he lay down to sleep.

12 He dreamed. He saw a ladder standing on the ground. Its top reached up to heaven. God's angels were going up and down on it. 13 The LORD stood above the ladder. He said, "I am the LORD, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. I will give you and your children the land you are lying on. 14 Your children will be like the dust of the earth. You will spread out to the west, east, north, and south. All the families of the earth will be blessed through you and your children. 15 I am with you. I will keep you safe wherever you go. I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done what I promised you."

16 Jacob woke up from his sleep. He said, "The LORD is in this place, and I did not know it!" 17 He was afraid. He said, "This place is so great and scary! This is God's house. This is the gate of heaven."

18 Early in the morning, Jacob got up. He took the stone he had put under his head. He set it up like a pillar. He poured oil on top of it. 19 He called that place Bethel. Before that, the town was called Luz.

Jacob Makes a Promise

20 Jacob made a promise. He said, "If God will be with me and keep me safe on this trip, and give me food to eat and clothes to wear, 21 then I will come back safely to my father's house. The LORD will be my God. 22 This stone pillar will be God's house. I will give You a tenth of everything You give me."
