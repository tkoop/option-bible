Jesus Talks About His Death

1 When Jesus finished all these words, He said to His followers, 2 “You know that the Passover is in two days. Then the Son of Man will be handed over to be killed on a cross.”

The Leaders Plan to Kill Jesus

3 Then the chief priests and the elders of the people met in the yard of Caiaphas, the high priest. 4 They planned to catch Jesus by a trick and kill Him. 5 But they said, “Not during the feast, or the people may make trouble.”

A Woman Pours Perfume on Jesus

6 Jesus was in Bethany. He was at the house of Simon the leper. 7 A woman came to Him. She had a jar of costly perfume. She poured it on His head while He sat at the table.

8 The followers saw this. They were upset. They said, “Why waste this? 9 It could have been sold for a lot of money. The money could have been given to poor people.”

10 Jesus knew this. He said to them, “Why bother this woman? She did a good thing for Me. 11 You will always have poor people with you. But you will not always have Me. 12 She poured this perfume on Me to get My body ready to be buried. 13 I tell you the truth. Wherever this good news is told in the whole world, what she did will be told too. People will remember her.”

Judas Agrees to Betray Jesus

14 Then one of the twelve, Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests. 15 He said, “What will you give me if I hand Him over to you?” They paid him thirty silver coins. 16 From then on, Judas looked for a good time to hand Him over.

The Last Supper

17 On the first day of the bread with no yeast, the followers came to Jesus. They said, “Where do you want us to get ready for you to eat the Passover?”

18 He said, “Go into the city to a certain man. Tell him the Teacher says His time is near. He will eat the Passover with His followers at that man's house.”

19 The followers did what Jesus told them. They got the Passover ready. 20 When evening came, Jesus sat at the table with the twelve followers.

21 While they were eating, He said, “I tell you the truth. One of you will hand Me over.”

22 They were very sad. They began to say to Him, one after another, “Lord, is it I?”

23 He answered, “The one who dips his hand in the bowl with Me will hand Me over. 24 The Son of Man will go, just as it is written about Him. But it will be bad for the man who hands Him over. It would be better for that man if he had never been born.”

25 Judas, who would hand Him over, said, “Rabbi, is it I?”

Jesus said to him, “You have said so.”

26 While they were eating, Jesus took bread. He gave thanks and broke it. He gave it to His followers. He said, “Take and eat. This is My body.” 27 Then He took a cup. He gave thanks. He gave it to them. He said, “Drink from it, all of you. 28 For this is My blood of the agreement. It is poured out for many people. It brings forgiveness of sins. 29 I tell you, I will not drink wine again. I will not drink until that day. Then I will drink it new with you in My Father's kingdom.”

30 They sang a hymn. Then they went out to the Mount of Olives.

Jesus Says His Followers Will Leave Him

31 Then Jesus said to them, “This very night you will all turn away from Me. For it is written, ‘I will hit the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’ 32 But after I rise, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”

33 Peter answered, “Even if all the others turn away from You, I never will.”

34 Jesus said to him, “I tell you the truth. This very night, before the rooster crows, you will say three times that you do not know Me.”

35 Peter said to Him, “Even if I must die with You, I will never say I do not know You.” All the followers said the same thing.

Jesus Prays in the Garden

36 Then Jesus went with them to a place called Gethsemane. He said to His followers, “Sit here while I go over there and pray.”

37 He took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with Him. He began to feel very sad and troubled.

38 Then He said to them, “My soul is very sad, even to death. Stay here and keep watch with Me.”

39 He went a little farther. He fell with His face to the ground. He prayed, “My Father, if it is possible, let this suffering pass from Me. Yet not what I want, but what You want.”

40 He came back to His followers. He found them sleeping. He said to Peter, “Could you not keep watch with Me for one hour? 41 Keep watch and pray. Then you will not fall into temptation. The spirit is willing, but the body is weak.”

42 He went away again. He prayed, “My Father, if I must suffer, let Your will be done.”

43 He came back. He found them sleeping again. Their eyes were heavy.

44 He left them. He went away again. He prayed the same words a third time.

45 Then He came back to His followers. He said, “Are you still sleeping and resting? Look, the time is near. The Son of Man is handed over to sinners. 46 Get up. Let us go. Look, the one who hands Me over is near.”

Judas Hands Jesus Over

47 While He was still speaking, Judas came. He was one of the twelve. A big crowd came with him. They had swords and clubs. They came from the chief priests and elders of the people.

48 The man who would hand Him over had given them a sign. He said, “The one I kiss is the man. Grab him.”

49 Right away Judas went to Jesus. He said, “Greetings, Rabbi.” He kissed Him.

50 Jesus said to him, “Friend, do what you came to do.” Then the men came up. They put their hands on Jesus and took Him.

51 One of the men with Jesus reached for his sword. He pulled it out. He struck the servant of the high priest. He cut off his ear.

52 Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back in its place. All who take the sword will die by the sword. 53 Do you think I cannot ask My Father? He would send Me more than twelve armies of angels right now. 54 But how would the holy writings come true? They say it must happen this way.”

55 At that hour Jesus said to the crowds, “Did you come out with swords and clubs to arrest Me, as if I were a robber? Every day I sat in the temple teaching, and you did not arrest Me. 56 But all this has happened so the writings of the prophets would come true.” Then all the followers left Him and ran away.

57 Those who seized Jesus took Him to Caiaphas the high priest. The teachers of the law and elders were gathered there.

58 Peter followed Him far away. He went to the yard of the high priest. He went in and sat with the guards. He wanted to see what would happen.

Jesus Is Put on Trial

59 The chief priests and the whole council tried to find false witness against Jesus. They wanted to put Him to death. 60 They did not find any, even though many false witnesses came forward. At last two came forward.

61 They said, “This man said he could destroy God's temple and build it again in three days.”

62 The high priest stood up. He said to Jesus, “Do you have no answer? What are these men saying against you?”

63 But Jesus was quiet. The high priest said to Him, “I make you swear by the living God. Tell us if you are the Christ, the Son of God.”

64 Jesus said to him, “You have said so. But I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power. You will see Him coming on the clouds of heaven.”

65 Then the high priest tore his clothes. He said, “He has spoken against God! Why do we need more witnesses? Look, you have now heard the evil words.”

66 “What do you think?”

They answered, “He is guilty and should die.”

67 Then some spit in His face. They hit Him with their fists. Others slapped Him.

68 They said, “Christ, prophesy for us. Who hit you?”

Peter Says He Does Not Know Jesus

69 Peter was sitting outside in the yard. A servant girl came to him. She said, “You also were with Jesus, the man from Galilee.”

70 But he denied it in front of everyone. He said, “I do not know what you mean.”

71 He went out to the gate. Another servant girl saw him. She said to the people there, “This man was with Jesus of Nazareth.”

72 Again he denied it. He swore, “I do not know the man.”

73 After a little while, the people standing there came up to Peter. They said, “Surely you are one of them. Your accent gives you away.”

74 Then he began to curse and swear. He said, “I do not know the man!” Right away a rooster crowed.

75 Peter remembered what Jesus had said: “Before the rooster crows, you will say three times that you do not know Me.” He went outside and cried bitterly.
