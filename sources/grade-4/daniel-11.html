Daniel Hears About Future Kings

1 In the first year that Darius the Mede ruled, I stood by to help and strengthen him. 2 Now I will tell you the truth. Three more kings will rule Persia. The fourth king will be richer than all of them. He will use his money to make everyone fight the kingdom of Greece. 3 Then a strong king will rise up. He will rule with great power and do what he wants. 4 But when he becomes strong, his kingdom will break apart. It will be split into four parts, like the four winds. It will not go to his children. It will not be ruled the way he ruled. His kingdom will be pulled up and given to others.

The King of the South and the King of the North

5 The king of the south will grow strong. But one of his leaders will grow even stronger and rule a bigger kingdom. 6 After some years, they will join together. The daughter of the king of the south will go to the king of the north to make peace. But she will not keep her power. His power will not last. She will be given up, along with the people who brought her, her child, and the one who helped her at that time. 7 But one from her family will take her place. He will come with an army. He will go into the strong place of the king of the north. He will fight them and win. 8 He will also take their gods to Egypt, along with their statues and valuable things of silver and gold. For some years he will leave the king of the north alone. 9 Then the king of the south will go into the land of the king of the north. But he will go back to his own land.

10 His sons will start a war. They will gather many soldiers. An army will come, sweep through, and go back. It will keep fighting up to his strong place. 11 Then the king of the south will get very angry. He will go out and fight the king of the north. The king of the north will raise a big army, but it will be given to the king of the south. 12 When the army is taken away, he will become proud. He will kill many thousands, but he will not win.

13 The king of the north will raise another army. It will be bigger than the first. After some years, he will come with a big army and many supplies. 14 At that time many will rise up against the king of the south. Also, some violent men from your people will try to make the vision come true, but they will fail. 15 Then the king of the north will come. He will build a ramp to attack. He will capture a strong city. The armies of the south will not be able to stand. Even their best soldiers will not have the strength to stand. 16 The one who comes will do what he wants. No one will stand against him. He will stand in the Beautiful Land, and destruction will be in his hand.

17 He will decide to come with all the power of his kingdom. He will make peace with the king of the south. He will give him his daughter to marry, to destroy the kingdom. But she will not stand with him or help him. 18 Then he will turn to the coastlands and capture many. But a leader will stop his pride and turn his pride back on him. 19 Then he will turn to the strong places of his own land. But he will stumble and fall, and he will be no more.

20 After him, another king will rise. He will send a tax collector through the splendid kingdom. But in a few days he will be broken, not in anger or in war. 21 Then a worthless man will take his place. He will not be given royal honor. He will come when people feel safe. He will take the kingdom by trickery. 22 Then the strong armies will be swept away before him and broken. The leader of the agreement will also be broken. 23 After an agreement is made with him, he will act with lies. He will rise to power with only a few people. 24 When things are calm, he will invade the richest parts of the land. He will do things his fathers and grandfathers never did. He will give stolen goods, treasure, and wealth to his followers. He will plan attacks on strong cities, but only for a time.

More Wars Between South and North

25 He will use his power and courage against the king of the south with a big army. The king of the south will fight with a very big and strong army. But he will not stand, because they will make plans against him. 26 The people who eat at his table will destroy him. His army will be swept away. Many will be killed. 27 The two kings will sit at the same table. Both will have evil in their hearts. They will tell lies to each other. But it will not work, because the end is still to come at the set time.

28 Then he will go back to his land with much wealth. But his heart will be against the holy agreement. He will do something and then go back to his land. 29 At the set time he will come back and go south. But it will not be like before. 30 Ships from Kittim will come against him. He will be afraid and go back. Then he will be angry at the holy agreement and do something. He will go back and help the people who leave the holy agreement.

The Holy Place Is Ruined

31 His soldiers will rise up. They will make the holy place unclean. They will stop the daily offering. They will set up the terrible thing that causes ruin. 32 He will use smooth words to turn against him the people who break the agreement. But the people who know their God will be strong and do great things. 33 The wise people will help many understand. But they will fall by sword and fire. They will be captured and robbed for a time. 34 When they fall, they will get a little help. Many will join them with smooth words. 35 Some wise people will stumble. This will make them clean and pure until the end comes. The end will come at the set time.

The Proud King

36 Then the king will do what he wants. He will lift himself up above every god. He will say amazing things against the God of gods. He will succeed until God's anger is finished, because what is decided will be done. 37 He will not care about the gods of his fathers. He will not care about the one women desire. He will not care about any god. He will make himself greater than all of them. 38 Instead, he will honor a god of strong places. It is a god his fathers did not know. He will honor it with gold, silver, precious stones, and costly things. 39 He will attack the strongest forts with the help of a foreign god. He will give great honor to those who follow him. He will make them rulers over many. He will give out land for money.

The End of the King

40 At the time of the end, the king of the south will fight him. The king of the north will attack him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships. He will invade countries and sweep through them like a flood. 41 He will also enter the Beautiful Land. Many countries will fall. But Edom, Moab, and the leaders of Ammon will escape from his hand. 42 He will reach out his hand against the countries. Even the land of Egypt will not escape. 43 He will take control of the treasures of gold and silver and all the riches of Egypt. The Libyans and Cushites will follow him. 44 But rumors from the east and the north will alarm him. He will go out in a great rage to destroy many people. 45 He will set up his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain. But he will come to his end, and no one will help him.
