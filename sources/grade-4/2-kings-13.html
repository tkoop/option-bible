Jehoahaz Rules Israel

1 In the twenty-third year of Joash, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled for seventeen years. 2 He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD. He followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin. Jehoahaz did not turn away from those sins.

3 So The LORD was very angry with Israel. He let Hazael, king of Aram, and Ben-Hadad, Hazael’s son, hurt them for a long time. 4 Then Jehoahaz begged the LORD for help. The LORD listened to him, because He saw how hard the king of Aram was on Israel. 5 The LORD gave Israel a rescuer. The people got free from Aram’s power. They went back to their tents, like before.

6 But Israel still did not turn from the sins of Jeroboam’s family. They kept doing the same wrong things. A pole for a false god also stayed in Samaria. 7 Jehoahaz had only fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers left. The king of Aram had killed the rest and crushed them like dust. 8 The rest of Jehoahaz’s story, and his brave deeds, are written in the book of the kings of Israel. 9 Jehoahaz died and was buried in Samaria. His son Joash became king after him.

Joash Rules Israel

10 In the thirty-seventh year of Joash, king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled for sixteen years. 11 He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD. He did not turn from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel to sin.

12 The rest of Jehoash’s story, and his brave deeds, and his war with Amaziah, king of Judah, are written in the book of the kings of Israel. 13 Jehoash died and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel. Then his son Jeroboam became king.

Elisha Helps King Jehoash

14 Elisha was sick with the illness that would kill him. Jehoash, king of Israel, came down to see him. He cried over Elisha and said, "My father, my father! You are Israel’s strength and protection!" 15 Elisha said to him, "Get a bow and some arrows." So Jehoash got a bow and arrows.

16 Elisha said to the king of Israel, "Put your hand on the bow." The king put his hand on it. Elisha put his hands on the king’s hands. 17 Elisha said, "Open the east window." The king opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot!" The king shot. Then Elisha said, "This is the arrow of victory from the LORD. It is the arrow of victory over Aram. You will beat Aram at Aphek until it is finished."

18 Then Elisha said, "Take the arrows." The king took them. Elisha said, "Hit the ground." The king hit the ground three times and stopped. 19 The man of God was angry with him. He said, "You should have hit the ground five or six times. Then you would have beaten Aram until it was finished. But now you will beat Aram only three times."

20 Elisha died and was buried. At the start of a new year, groups of men from Moab came into the land. 21 Some people were burying a man. They saw a raiding group coming. They threw the man into Elisha’s tomb. When the man touched Elisha’s bones, he came back to life and stood up.

God Is Kind to Israel

22 Hazael, king of Aram, had been hard on Israel all during Jehoahaz’s life. 23 But the LORD was kind to Israel. He felt sorry for them. He turned back to them because of His promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He did not want to destroy them or push them away from Him. 24 Hazael died. His son Ben-Hadad became king after him. 25 Jehoash, son of Jehoahaz, took back the cities that Ben-Hadad had taken from his father Jehoahaz in the war. Jehoash beat Ben-Hadad three times and got Israel’s cities back.
