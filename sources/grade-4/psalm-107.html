Give Thanks to the LORD

1 Give thanks to the LORD, because He is good. His love never ends. 2 The people the LORD saved should say so. He saved them from their enemies. 3 He brought them back from many lands. He brought them from the east and the west, from the north and the south.

God Helps People Lost in the Desert

4 Some people wandered in the desert. They could not find a city to live in. 5 They were hungry and thirsty. They were very tired and weak. 6 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their problems. 7 He led them on a straight path. He took them to a city where they could live. 8 Let them thank the LORD for His love. Let them thank Him for the wonderful things He did for people. 9 He gives water to thirsty people. He fills hungry people with good food.

God Sets Prisoners Free

10 Some people sat in the dark. They were prisoners in chains. 11 They did not obey God's words. They did not listen to the Most High. 12 So God made them work hard. They fell down, and no one helped them. 13 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their problems. 14 He brought them out of the dark. He freed them from their chains. 15 Let them thank the LORD for His love. Let them thank Him for the wonderful things He did for people. 16 He broke the bronze gates. He cut the iron bars.

God Heals Sick People

17 Some people were foolish. Their sins made them sick. 18 They did not want to eat. They were close to death. 19 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their problems. 20 He sent His word and healed them. He saved them from dying. 21 Let them thank the LORD for His love. Let them thank Him for the wonderful things He did for people. 22 Let them bring gifts to thank the LORD. Let them tell others what He did, and be happy.

God Calms the Storm

23 Some people went to sea in boats. They worked on the big water. 24 They saw what the LORD did. They saw His wonders in the deep sea. 25 He told the wind to blow hard. The waves grew high. 26 The boats went up high and down low. The sailors were very scared. 27 They stumbled like drunk people. They forgot what to do.

28 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble. He saved them from their problems. 29 He made the storm quiet. The waves became still. 30 They were happy when the water was calm. He brought them to the place they wanted. 31 Let them thank the LORD for His love. Let them thank Him for the wonderful things He did for people. 32 Let them praise the LORD with the people. Let them praise Him with the leaders.

God Changes the Land

33 He can make rivers dry. He can make springs dry up. 34 He can make good land dry and salty, because the people there do bad things. 35 He can make a desert into a lake. He can make dry land into springs. 36 He lets hungry people live there. They make a city to live in. 37 They plant crops and grape vines. They get a big harvest. 38 He blesses them, and they grow in number. Their animals do not go away.

39 But later they become fewer and sad. Hard times and sorrow bring them down. 40 He puts shame on proud leaders. He makes them wander in empty places with no road. 41 But He lifts poor people out of trouble. He makes their families grow like flocks of sheep. 42 Good people see this and are glad. People who do wrong stop talking.

Think About God's Love

43 Who is wise? Think about these things. Then people will understand the LORD's love.
