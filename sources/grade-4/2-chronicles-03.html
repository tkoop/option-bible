Solomon Builds God's House

1 Then Solomon started to build the the LORD's house in Jerusalem. He built it on Mount Moriah. That was where the the LORD had shown Himself to his father David. David had picked that spot. It was the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 2 Solomon started building on the second day of the second month. This was in his fourth year as king.

The Size of the Temple

3 These are the sizes Solomon set for God's house. It was 60 cubits long. It was 20 cubits wide. 4 The porch in front was 20 cubits long across the width of the house. It was 120 cubits high. He covered the inside with pure gold.

5 He covered the main room with cypress wood. He covered it with fine gold. He put palm trees and chains on it for beauty. 6 He made the temple pretty with special stones. The gold was gold from Parvaim. 7 He covered the beams, door frames, walls, and doors with gold. He cut angel statues into the walls.

The Most Holy Room

8 He made the Most Holy Room. Its length matched the width of the house. It was 20 cubits. Its width was 20 cubits too. He covered it with 600 talents of fine gold. 9 The nails weighed 50 shekels of gold. He also covered the top rooms with gold.

10 In the Most Holy Room, he made two angel statues. They were carved work. He covered them with gold. 11 The angel statues had 20 cubits of wings in all. One wing of the first statue was 5 cubits long. It touched the wall of the house. Its other wing was 5 cubits long. It touched the wing of the other statue. 12 The other statue's wing was 5 cubits long. It touched the wall of the house. Its other wing was 5 cubits long. It touched the first statue's wing. 13 Their wings spread out 20 cubits. They stood on their feet. They faced the main room.

Curtain and Pillars

14 He made the curtain from blue, purple, and red yarn and fine linen. He wove angel statues into it. 15 In front of the house, he made two pillars. Each was 35 cubits high. Each had a top piece 5 cubits high. 16 He made chains and put them on top of the pillars. He made 100 pomegranates. He put them on the chains. 17 He set up the pillars in front of the temple. One was on the south. One was on the north. He named the south one Jachin. He named the north one Boaz.
