Nicodemus Visits Jesus at Night

1 There was a man named Nicodemus. He was one of the Pharisees, a religious group, and a leader of the Jews. 2 He came to Jesus at night and said, "Teacher, we know you are a teacher from God. No one can do these miracles unless God is with him."

3 Jesus answered, "I tell you the truth: No one can see God's kingdom unless they are born again."

4 Nicodemus asked, "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he go back into his mother's body and be born a second time?"

5 Jesus said, "I tell you the truth: No one can enter God's kingdom unless they are born of water and the Spirit. 6 What is born from people is people, and what is born from the Spirit is spirit. 7 Do not be amazed that I told you, 'All of you must be born again.' 8 The wind blows where it wants. You hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. It is the same with everyone born from the Spirit."

9 Nicodemus asked, "How can these things happen?"

10 Jesus answered, "Are you the teacher of Israel, and you do not understand these things? 11 I tell you the truth: We speak about what we know, and we tell about what we have seen. But you do not accept our words. 12 If I told you about things on earth and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you about things in heaven? 13 No one has gone up to heaven except the One who came down from heaven, the Son of Man. 14 Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the desert, so the Son of Man must be lifted up. 15 Then everyone who believes in Him will have life that lasts forever.

16 "For God loved the world so much that He gave His only Son, so that everyone who believes in Him will not die but will have life that lasts forever. 17 God did not send His Son into the world to judge the world. He sent Him so the world could be saved through Him. 18 The one who believes in Him is not judged. But the one who does not believe is already judged, because that person has not believed in the name of God's only Son. 19 This is the judgment: The light came into the world, but people loved the dark more than the light, because their deeds were evil. 20 Everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so their deeds will not be shown. 21 But the one who does what is true comes to the light, so it may be seen that their deeds are done in God."

Jesus and His Followers Baptize

22 After this, Jesus and His disciples went to the land of Judea. He stayed there with them and baptized people. 23 John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because there was much water there. People came and were baptized. 24 John had not yet been put in prison.

25 John's disciples and a Jewish man argued about washing to make people clean. 26 They came to John and said, "Teacher, the man who was with you across the Jordan, the one you told about, is baptizing now, and everyone is going to Him."

John Points to Jesus

27 John answered, "A man can receive only what is given to him from heaven. 28 You yourselves know I said, 'I am not the Christ. I was sent ahead of Him.' 29 The bride belongs to the groom. The friend of the groom stands and listens to him. He is very happy because of the groom's voice. So my joy is now full. 30 He must become greater, and I must become less."

31 The One who comes from above is above all. The one who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks like someone from the earth. The One who comes from heaven is above all. 32 He tells what He has seen and heard, but no one accepts His words. 33 The one who accepts His words shows that God is true. 34 The One God sent speaks God's words, because God gives the Spirit without limit. 35 The Father loves the Son and has put everything in His hand. 36 The one who believes in the Son has life that lasts forever. But the one who does not obey the Son will not see life. God's anger stays on that person.
