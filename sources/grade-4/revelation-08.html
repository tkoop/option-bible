The Seventh Seal Is Opened

1 When the Lamb opened the seventh seal, there was quiet in heaven for about half an hour. 2 I saw seven angels standing before God. They were given seven trumpets. 3 Another angel came and stood at the altar. He had a gold bowl for burning incense. He was given a lot of incense to offer with the prayers of all God's people. He offered it on the gold altar before the throne. 4 The smoke from the incense went up with the prayers of God's people. It went up to God from the angel's hand. 5 The angel took the bowl and filled it with fire from the altar. Then he threw it on the earth. There were thunder, loud noises, lightning, and an earthquake.

The Angels Blow Their Trumpets

6 The seven angels with the seven trumpets got ready to blow them. 7 The first angel blew his trumpet. Hail and fire mixed with blood came down on the earth. A third of the earth burned up. A third of the trees burned up. All the green grass burned up.

8 The second angel blew his trumpet. Something like a huge mountain on fire was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea turned into blood. 9 A third of the living things in the sea died. A third of the ships were destroyed.

10 The third angel blew his trumpet. A great star, burning like a lamp, fell from the sky. It fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water. 11 The star's name is Wormwood. A third of the water turned bitter. Many people died because the water was bitter.

12 The fourth angel blew his trumpet. A third of the sun, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars were hit. So a third of them became dark. A third of the day had no light, and a third of the night too.

13 I looked and heard an eagle flying high in the sky. It called out in a loud voice, "Woe! Woe! Woe to the people who live on the earth, because of the trumpet blasts that the last three angels are about to blow!"
