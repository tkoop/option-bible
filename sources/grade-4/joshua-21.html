Levites Ask for Cities

1 The leaders of the Levite families came to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the Israelite tribes. 2 They were at Shiloh in Canaan. They said, "The LORD told Moses to give us cities to live in and fields for our animals." 3 So the Israelites gave the Levites cities and fields from their own land, just as the LORD had said.

4 The Kohathite families got their share first. The sons of Aaron the priest got thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin. 5 The rest of Kohath's sons got ten cities by lot from Ephraim, Dan, and half of Manasseh. 6 The sons of Gershon got thirteen cities by lot from Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and half of Manasseh in Bashan. 7 The sons of Merari got twelve cities for their families from Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. 8 The Israelites gave these cities and fields to the Levites by lot, just as the LORD had told Moses.

Cities for Aaron's Family

9 From Judah and Simeon, they gave cities by name to Aaron's sons. 10 These were the Kohathites, and they got the first share. 11 They gave them Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron, in the hills of Judah, with its fields. Arba was the father of Anak. 12 But the fields and villages around the city were given to Caleb son of Jephunneh. 13 To Aaron's sons, the priests, they gave Hebron, a safe city for someone who killed by accident, along with Libnah and its fields. 14 They also gave Jattir, Eshtemoa, and their fields. 15 They gave Holon, Debir, and their fields. 16 They gave Ain, Juttah, and Beth-shemesh. That was nine cities from these two tribes, with their fields. 17 From Benjamin they gave Gibeon, Geba, 18 Anathoth, and Almon—four cities with their fields. 19 All the cities for Aaron's priest sons were thirteen, with their fields.

Cities for Other Kohathites

20 The rest of the Kohathite families got cities by lot from Ephraim. 21 They gave them Shechem, a safe city in the hills of Ephraim, along with Gezer and its fields. 22 They gave Kibzaim and Beth-horon, with their fields. 23 From Dan they gave Eltekeh and Gibbethon. 24 They gave Aijalon and Gath-rimmon. That was four cities from Dan, with their fields. 25 From half of Manasseh they gave Taanach and Gath-rimmon. That was two cities with their fields. 26 All together, the Kohath families got ten cities with their fields.

Cities for Gershonites

27 From half of Manasseh they gave the Gershon families Golan in Bashan, a safe city, and Beeshterah, with their fields. That was two cities. 28 From Issachar they gave Kishion and Daberath. 29 They gave Jarmuth and En-gannim. That was four cities with their fields. 30 From Asher they gave Mishal and Abdon. 31 They gave Helkath and Rehob. That was four cities with their fields. 32 From Naphtali they gave Kedesh in Galilee, a safe city, Hammoth-dor, and Kartan. That was three cities with their fields. 33 All together, the Gershon families got thirteen cities with their fields.

Cities for Merarites

34 From Zebulun they gave the Merari families Jokneam and Kartah. 35 They gave Dimnah and Nahalal. That was four cities with their fields. 36 From Reuben they gave Bezer and Jahzah. 37 They gave Kedemoth and Mephaath. That was four cities with their fields. 38 From Gad they gave Ramoth in Gilead, a safe city, and Mahanaim. 39 They gave Heshbon and Jazer. That was four cities with their fields. 40 All together, the Merari families got twelve cities with their fields.

All the Levite Cities

41 The Levites had forty-eight cities in all among the Israelites, with their fields. 42 Every one of these cities had fields around it.

The LORD Keeps His Promise

43 The LORD gave Israel all the land He had promised to their fathers. They took it and lived there. 44 The LORD gave them rest on every side, just as He had promised their fathers. None of their enemies could stand against them. the LORD gave all their enemies into their hands. 45 Not one of the good promises the LORD made to Israel failed. Everything came true.
