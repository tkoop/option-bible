David Prays to God for Help

1 O the LORD, hear my prayer. Listen to my cry for help. Answer me because You are faithful and right. 2 Do not judge Your servant, because no one is right before You.

3 My enemy chases me. He has crushed my life to the ground. He has made me live in dark places like people who died long ago. 4 My spirit is weak inside me. My heart is worried inside me.

5 I remember the days long ago. I think about all Your works. I think about what Your hands have made. 6 I hold out my hands to You. My soul is thirsty for You like dry land. Selah.

David Asks God to Answer Fast

7 Answer me fast, O the LORD. My spirit is weak. Do not hide Your face from me, or I will be like those who go down to the grave. 8 Let me hear Your kindness in the morning, because I trust in You. Show me the way I should go, because I give myself to You.

David Asks God to Lead Him

9 Save me from my enemies, O the LORD. I come to You for safety. 10 Teach me to do Your will, because You are my God. Let Your good Spirit lead me on flat ground.

David Trusts God to Help

11 Because of Your name, O the LORD, keep me alive. In Your right ways, bring me out of trouble. 12 In Your kindness, defeat my enemies. Destroy all who hurt me, because I am Your servant.
