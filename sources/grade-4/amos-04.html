God Speaks to the Women of Samaria

1 Listen to this word, you cows of Bashan, who are on the mountain of Samaria. You push down poor people, and you hurt needy people. You say to your masters, “Bring more drinks, and we will drink.”

The LORD Will Punish Them

2 The Lord, the LORD, has promised by His holy name, “Days are coming when people will drag you away with hooks. They will drag your children away with fishhooks. 3 You will go out through holes in the wall. Each woman will go straight ahead. You will be thrown out toward Harmon,” says the the LORD.

God Tells Them to Keep Doing Wrong

4 “Come to Bethel and sin. Sin more and more at Gilgal. Bring your sacrifices every morning. Bring your tithes every three days. 5 Burn a thank offering with yeast. Announce your gifts. Tell everyone about them. This is what you love to do, people of Israel,” says the Lord, the LORD.

God Sent Hard Times

6 “I let you go hungry in every city. You did not have enough bread in any place. Yet you did not come back to Me,” says the the LORD.

7 “I also kept rain from you when the harvest was still three months away. I made it rain on one town, but not on another. One field got rain, and another field dried up. 8 People from two or three towns walked to another town to get water, but they still were thirsty. Yet you did not come back to Me,” says the the LORD.

9 “I struck you with plant disease and mildew. Locusts ate your many gardens and vineyards. They ate your fig trees and olive trees. Yet you did not come back to Me,” says the the LORD.

10 “I sent sickness among you like Egypt had. I killed your young men with swords. I took your horses away. I made your camps smell bad. Yet you did not come back to Me,” says the the LORD.

11 “I destroyed some of you like I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. You were like a burning stick pulled from a fire. Yet you did not come back to Me,” says the the LORD.

Get Ready to Meet God

12 “So this is what I will do to you, Israel. Because I will do this, get ready to meet your God, Israel!”

13 He is the One who made the mountains and the wind. He tells people what He is thinking. He turns morning into darkness. He walks on the high places of the earth. The The LORD, the God of heaven's armies, is His name.
