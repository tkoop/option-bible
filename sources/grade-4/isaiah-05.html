A Song About a Grape Garden

1 I will sing for my dear friend a song about his grape garden. My dear friend had a grape garden on a very good hill. 2 He dug it up and took out the stones. He planted the best grape plants. He built a watchtower in it. He cut out a wine press in it. He hoped for good grapes, but it made bad grapes.

3 "Now, you people who live in Jerusalem and in Judah, please decide between Me and My grape garden. 4 What more could I have done for My grape garden? Why did I hope for good grapes, but it made bad grapes? 5 Now I will tell you what I will do to My grape garden. I will take away its fence. It will be burned. I will break down its wall. It will be walked on. 6 I will make it a waste place. It will not be trimmed or dug. Thorns and weeds will grow. I will tell the clouds not to rain on it."

7 The grape garden of the LORD of Hosts is the house of Israel. The men of Judah are the plant He loved. He looked for right and fair ways, but saw people getting hurt. He looked for people doing right, but heard cries for help.

Warnings About Greed

8 How sad for people who join house to house and field to field until no place is left. Then you will live alone in the land. 9 The LORD of Hosts has said in my ears, "Surely many houses will be empty. Great and good houses will have no one living in them. 10 Ten acres of grape garden will make only one small jar of wine. A big basket of seed will make only a little grain."

Warnings About Drinking

11 How sad for people who get up early to drink strong drink. They stay up late at night, and wine makes them wild. 12 Their parties have harps, lyres, drums, and flutes. They have wine too. But they do not look at the work of the LORD. They do not see what His hands have made.

Trouble Is Coming

13 So My people will be taken away because they do not know Me. Their great men will be hungry. Their crowds will be thirsty. 14 The grave has made its mouth big. It opens wide with no end. Their glory, their crowds, their noise, and their happy people will go down into it. 15 People will be brought low. Proud men will be brought down. The eyes of proud people will be brought down. 16 But the LORD of Hosts will be lifted up by right and fair ways. The Holy God will show He is holy by doing right. 17 Then lambs will eat grass like in a field. Strangers will eat the broken places of rich men.

More Warnings

18 How sad for people who drag sin along with lies. They pull wrong like heavy cart ropes. 19 They say, "Let Him hurry and do His work fast so we can see it. Let the plan of the Holy One of Israel come near and happen, so we can know it."

20 How sad for people who call bad good and good bad. They make dark into light and light into dark. They make bitter into sweet and sweet into bitter. 21 How sad for people who are wise in their own eyes and clever in their own sight.

22 How sad for people who are strong at drinking wine and brave at mixing strong drink. 23 They make the bad man look right for money. They take right away from good people.

The Fire of Judgment

24 So fire will burn them like dry grass and straw. Their roots will rot. Their flowers will blow away like dust. This is because they said no to the law of the LORD of Hosts. They made fun of the word of the Holy One of Israel. 25 So the LORD was very angry with His people. He raised His hand and hit them. The mountains shook. Their dead bodies were like trash in the streets. But even then, His anger did not stop. His hand was still raised.

An Army Is Coming

26 The LORD will lift up a flag for far-away nations. He will whistle for them from the ends of the earth. Look, they come fast. 27 None of them is tired. None of them falls. None sleeps or rests. Their belts are tight. Their shoe straps do not break. 28 Their arrows are sharp. Their bows are ready. Their horses' feet are like hard rock. Their wheels are like a windstorm. 29 They roar like lions. They growl like young lions. They grab their catch and carry it off. No one saves it. 30 In that day they will roar over them like the sea. Anyone who looks at the land sees dark and trouble. The sky is dark with clouds.
