Job Answers His Friends

1 Then Job answered:

2 "I have heard many things like these. You are all terrible comforters.

3 "Will your long speeches never end? Why do you keep talking?

4 "I could talk like you if you were in my place. I could say many things against you and shake my head at you.

5 "But I would cheer you up with my words. My kind words would help you feel better.

6 "Even if I talk, my pain does not go away. If I stay quiet, it still does not leave.

Job Talks to God

7 "Now He has worn me out. You have ruined everyone around me.

8 "You have made me thin and weak. My weak body shows my trouble. It speaks against me.

9 "God's anger has torn me apart. He hates me. He grinds His teeth at me. My enemy stares hard at me.

10 "They open their mouths to speak against me. They hit my face and shame me. They all join together against me.

11 "God has put me in the hands of bad people. He has given me to bad people.

12 "I was safe and calm, but God broke me. He grabbed me by the neck and crushed me. He made me His target.

13 "His arrows surround me. He cuts me deep with no mercy. He spills my pain on the ground.

14 "He breaks me again and again. He attacks me like a strong soldier.

Job's Sadness and Hope

15 "I put rough cloth on my skin to show my sadness. I have lost my strength.

16 "My face is red from crying. Deep darkness covers my eyes.

17 "My hands have done no wrong. My prayer is pure.

18 "Earth, do not hide my blood. Do not let my cry be quiet.

19 "Even now, my Witness is in heaven. My Helper is up above.

20 "My friends make fun of me. My eyes pour out tears to God.

21 "I wish someone could speak to God for me, like a person speaks for a friend.

22 "In a few years, I will go away and not come back."
