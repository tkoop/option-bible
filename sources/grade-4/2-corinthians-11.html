Paul Asks Them to Listen

1 I wish you would put up with a little of my foolish talk. You already do. 2 I care about you the way God cares. I promised to give you to Christ like a pure bride. 3 But I am afraid the snake tricked Eve, and your minds may be led away from true love for Christ. 4 If someone comes and tells about a different Jesus, or a different spirit, or a different good news, you put up with it too easily.

5 I do not think I am less than the best apostles. 6 I may not speak well, but I know a lot. We showed you this in every way.

Paul Did Not Charge Them

7 Did I do wrong by lowering myself so you could be lifted up? 8 I took money from other churches to serve you. 9 When I was with you and needed things, I did not bother anyone. The brothers from Macedonia filled my need. I kept myself from being a burden to you in every way, and I will keep doing that.

10 As Christ's truth is in me, no one in Achaia will stop me from boasting about this. 11 Do I not love you? God knows I do.

Paul Warns About Fake Teachers

12 I will keep doing what I do. I want to stop those who look for a chance to boast that they are like us. 13 They are false apostles. They trick people. They pretend to be Christ's apostles. 14 Even Satan pretends to be an angel of light. 15 So it is no surprise if his helpers pretend to be helpers of what is right. Their end will match their works.

Paul Boasts About His Weakness

16 I say again, do not think I am a fool. But if you do, accept me as a fool so I can boast a little. 17 What I say in this proud way is not how the Lord would speak, but like a fool. 18 Since many boast about human things, I will boast too.

19 You gladly put up with fools because you think you are wise! 20 You put up with anyone who makes you slaves, eats your food, takes your money, acts proud, or hits you in the face. 21 I say this to my shame: We were too weak to do that! But whatever anyone else dares to boast about, I dare to boast about too. (I am speaking like a fool.)

22 Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they from Abraham's family? So am I. 23 Are they Christ's servants? I am talking like a crazy person, but I am more. I have worked harder. I have been in jail more. I have been beaten more. I have faced death many times.

24 Five times I got 39 lashes from the Jews. 25 Three times I was beaten with rods. Once I was stoned. Three times I was shipwrecked. I spent a night and a day in the sea.

26 I have traveled often. I have been in danger from rivers, robbers, my own people, Gentiles, cities, empty places, the sea, and false brothers.

27 I have worked hard and toiled. I have often gone without sleep. I have been hungry and thirsty. I have often gone without food. I have been cold and without clothes. 28 Besides all this, every day I feel pressure and worry about all the churches.

29 Who is weak, and I do not feel weak? Who is led into sin, and I do not burn with anger? 30 If I must boast, I will boast about the things that show my weakness.

31 The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, who is praised forever, knows I am not lying. 32 In Damascus the governor under King Aretas guarded the city to catch me. 33 But I was let down in a basket through a window in the wall, and I escaped his hands.
