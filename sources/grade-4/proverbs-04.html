Listen to Your Father

1 Listen, my sons, to a father's teaching. Pay attention and gain understanding. 2 I give you good teaching. Don't leave my law.

3 For I was a son to my father. I was young and the only child my mother had. 4 He taught me. He said, "Hold my words close to your heart. Keep my commands and live."

Get Wisdom

5 Get wisdom. Get understanding. Don't forget my words. Don't turn away from what I say. 6 Don't leave wisdom, and she will guard you. Love her, and she will keep you. 7 Wisdom is the first thing to get. Get wisdom, and with all you get, get understanding. 8 Value her, and she will lift you up. Hold her close, and she will honor you. 9 She will put a pretty crown on your head. She will give you a crown of beauty.

Wisdom Helps You Live

10 Listen, my son, and take my words. Then you will live many years. 11 I taught you the way of wisdom. I led you on straight paths. 12 When you walk, nothing will get in your way. If you run, you won't fall. 13 Hold on to teaching. Don't let go. Keep it, for it is your life.

Stay Away from Bad People

14 Don't go on the path of bad people. Don't walk in the way of evil people. 15 Stay away from it. Don't pass by it. Turn away from it and keep going. 16 They can't sleep unless they do bad things. They lose sleep unless they make someone fall. 17 They eat the bread of badness and drink the wine of hurting others. 18 But the path of good people is like the light of morning. It gets brighter and brighter until full day. 19 The way of bad people is like deep darkness. They don't know what makes them fall.

Guard Your Heart

20 My son, listen to my words. Turn your ear to what I say. 21 Don't let them leave your sight. Keep them in your heart. 22 They are life to those who find them. They bring health to the whole body. 23 Guard your heart more than anything else, for life comes from it. 24 Take bad talk out of your mouth. Stay far from lies and bad words. 25 Let your eyes look straight ahead. Look right at what is in front of you. 26 Make the path for your feet level. Then all your ways will be firm. 27 Don't turn right or left. Keep your foot away from bad.
