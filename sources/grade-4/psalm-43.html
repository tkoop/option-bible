A Prayer for Help

1 Stand up for me, God. Fight my case against people who do not follow You. Save me from liars and people who do wrong. 2 You are my strong God. Why did You turn away from me? Why must I walk sad, pushed down by my enemy?

God's Light and Truth

3 Send Your light and Your truth. Let them lead me. Let them bring me to Your holy mountain and Your home. 4 Then I will go to God's altar. I will go to God, my greatest joy. I will praise You with a harp, O God, my God.

Hope in God

5 Why are you so sad, my soul? Why are you upset inside me? Put your hope in God. I will still praise Him. He is the One who saves me and my God.
