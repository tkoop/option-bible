Jehu Sends Letters

1 Ahab had 70 sons in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters. He sent them to Samaria to the leaders of Jezreel, the old men, and the men who cared for Ahab's sons. The letter said:

2 When this letter comes to you, your master's sons are with you. You also have chariots, horses, a strong city, and weapons. 3 Pick the best and right son of your master. Put him on his father's throne. Then fight for your master's family.

The Leaders Answer Jehu

4 But the leaders were very afraid. They said, "Two kings could not stand against Jehu. How can we stand?"

5 The palace boss, the city leaders, the old men, and the caregivers sent a message to Jehu. They said, "We are your servants. We will do all you say. We will not make anyone king. Do what you think is good."

6 Jehu wrote again: "If you are on my side, and if you listen to me, take the heads of your master's men. Bring them to me in Jezreel tomorrow at this time." The king's sons were 70 men. The great men of the city had raised them.

Jehu Kills Ahab's Sons

7 When the letter came, they took the king's sons and killed them. They put the heads in baskets. They sent them to Jehu in Jezreel.

8 A messenger came and told Jehu, "They brought the heads of the king's sons."

Jehu said, "Put them in two piles at the gate until morning."

Jehu Speaks to the People

9 In the morning, Jehu went out. He stood before all the people. He said, "You are not guilty. I made a plan against my master and killed him. But who killed all these men? 10 Know this: None of the the LORD's words against Ahab's family will fail. The The LORD did what He said through His servant Elijah."

Jehu Kills the Rest of Ahab's Family

11 Jehu killed all who were left of Ahab's family in Jezreel. He killed his great men, his close friends, and his priests. He did not leave one.

12 Then Jehu went to Samaria. On the way he came to Beth-eked of the shepherds.

13 There Jehu met the brothers of Ahaziah, king of Judah. He asked, "Who are you?"

They said, "We are brothers of Ahaziah. We are going down to greet the king's sons and the queen mother's sons."

14 Jehu said, "Take them alive!" They took them alive. Then they killed them at the pit of Beth-eked. There were 42 men. They did not leave one.

Jehu Meets Jehonadab

15 Jehu left there and found Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. Jehu blessed him. He asked, "Is your heart right with me, as my heart is with you?"

Jehonadab said, "Yes."

Jehu said, "Give me your hand." He gave his hand. Jehu helped him up into the chariot.

16 Jehu said, "Come with me. See how I care for the the LORD." They rode in his chariot.

17 Jehu came to Samaria. He killed all who were left of Ahab's family in Samaria. He destroyed them, just as the the LORD had said through Elijah.

Jehu Ends Baal Worship

18 Then Jehu gathered all the people. He said, "Ahab served Baal a little. Jehu will serve him much. 19 Now call all prophets of Baal, all his servants, and all his priests. Do not miss anyone. I have a big offering for Baal. Anyone who is missing will not live." But Jehu was tricking them. He wanted to destroy the people who served Baal.

20 Jehu said, "Set apart a special meeting for Baal." So they called it. 21 Jehu sent word through all Israel. All who served Baal came. Not one was missing. They came into the house of Baal. The house was full from one end to the other.

22 Jehu said to the man in charge of the clothes room, "Bring out clothes for all who serve Baal." He brought out the clothes.

23 Jehu and Jehonadab went into the house of Baal. Jehu said to the servants of Baal, "Look around. Make sure no servant of the the LORD is here with you. Only people who serve Baal should be here."

24 They went in to make offerings. Jehu had placed 80 men outside. He said, "If any man lets one of these men escape, he will die." 25 When Jehu finished the burnt offering, he said to the guards and officers, "Go in and kill them. Do not let anyone escape." They killed them with swords. They threw the bodies out. Then they went into the inner room of the house of Baal.

26 They brought out the sacred stones from the house of Baal and burned them. 27 They tore down the sacred stone of Baal. They tore down the house of Baal. They made it a toilet. It is still that way today. 28 So Jehu got rid of Baal worship in Israel.

Jehu's Later Years

29 But Jehu did not stop following the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat. Jeroboam had led Israel to sin. Jehu kept the golden calves in Bethel and Dan. 30 The The LORD said to Jehu, "You have done well. You did what is right in My eyes. You did to Ahab's family all that was in My heart. So your sons will be kings of Israel for four generations." 31 But Jehu did not follow the law of the the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. He did not stop the sins that Jeroboam led Israel into.

32 In those days the the LORD began to take land from Israel. Hazael hurt Israel in all its land. 33 He took the land east of the Jordan. He took all the land of Gilead, the land of Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh. He took it from Aroer by the Arnon Valley through Gilead and Bashan.

34 The rest of the things Jehu did, and all his power, are written in the book of the history of the kings of Israel. 35 Jehu died and was buried with his fathers in Samaria. his son Jehoahaz became king after him. 36 Jehu ruled over Israel in Samaria for 28 years.
