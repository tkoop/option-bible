Habakkuk's Complaint

1 This is the hard message that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

2 "How long, the LORD, must I call and You not hear? I cry to You, 'So much violence!' but You do not save. 3 Why do You make me see bad things? Why do You look at trouble? Robbery and violence are before me. People fight and argue. 4 So God's law is weak. Justice does not last. Bad people trap good people. So justice comes out wrong."

God's Answer

5 "Look at the nations. Watch them. Be very amazed! I will do something in your days that you will not believe, even if someone tells you. 6 I am bringing the Chaldeans. They are mean and fast. They march across the land to take homes that are not theirs. 7 They are scary and frightening. They make their own rules and honor. 8 Their horses run faster than leopards. They are fiercer than wolves at night. Their horse riders spread out. They come from far away. They rush like an eagle that wants to eat. 9 They all come ready to hurt people. They sweep in like a hot east wind. They gather prisoners like sand. 10 They laugh at kings and leaders. They make fun of every strong city. They pile up dirt and take the city. 11 Then they move on. They are guilty. They worship their own strength as a god."

Habakkuk's Second Complaint

12 "Have You not been here since the beginning, the LORD? My God, my Holy One, we will not die. You chose them to judge, the LORD. Mighty God, You set them to correct people. 13 Your eyes are too pure to look at evil. You cannot look at wrong. Why do You look at cheaters? Why are You quiet when bad people destroy people better than them? 14 You make people like fish in the sea, like crawling things with no leader. 15 They pull them all up with a hook. They catch them in a net. They gather them in a fishing net. So they are happy and glad. 16 So they offer gifts to their net. They burn incense to their fishing net. Their net gives them plenty. Their food is rich. 17 Will they keep emptying their net? Will they keep killing nations and not show mercy?"
