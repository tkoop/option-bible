Jesus Tells a Story About a Farmer

1 That same day, Jesus left the house and sat by the sea. 2 Big crowds came to Him. He got into a boat and sat down. All the people stood on the shore.

3 He told them many stories. He said, "Look! A farmer went out to plant seeds. 4 As he planted, some seeds fell on the path. Birds came and ate them. 5 Some fell on rocky ground with little dirt. They sprang up fast because the dirt was not deep. 6 But when the sun came up, they burned and dried up because they had no roots. 7 Some fell among thorns. The thorns grew and choked them. 8 Some fell on good dirt. They grew and gave fruit—some one hundred, some sixty, some thirty. 9 Whoever has ears, let him listen."

Why Jesus Used Stories

10 His followers came to Him and asked, "Why do you tell them stories?"

11 He answered, "You can learn the secrets of God's kingdom. That gift is for you, not for them. 12 Whoever has something will get more, and have plenty. Whoever has nothing will lose even what he has. 13 That is why I speak to them in stories. They look, but they do not see. They hear, but they do not hear or understand."

14 He said, "So the words of the prophet Isaiah come true for them."

"You will hear, but you will not understand. You will look, but you will not see. 15 These people's hearts are hard. Their ears are dull. They have closed their eyes. If they had not, they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, and understand with their hearts. Then they would turn to Me, and I would heal them."

16 "But your eyes are blessed because they see. Your ears are blessed because they hear. 17 I tell you for sure: Many prophets and good people wanted to see what you see, but they did not. They wanted to hear what you hear, but they did not."

18 "So listen to the story of the farmer."

What the Story Means

19 "When anyone hears the word about God's kingdom and does not understand it, the evil one comes. He takes away the seed that was planted in that person's heart. This is the seed that fell on the path. 20 The seed on rocky ground is the person who hears the word and takes it with joy. 21 But he has no root in himself. He lasts only a short time. When trouble or people make fun of him because of the word, he quickly gives up. 22 The seed among thorns is the person who hears the word. But the worries of this life and the trick of money choke the word. It does not grow fruit. 23 The seed on good dirt is the person who hears the word and understands it. He gives fruit—some one hundred, some sixty, some thirty."

The Story of the Weeds

24 Jesus told them another story. He said, "God's kingdom is like a man who planted good seed in his field. 25 While people slept, his enemy came. He planted weeds among the wheat and left. 26 When the plants grew and made grain, the weeds showed up too."

27 The man's servants came to him and said, "Sir, didn't you plant good seed in your field? Where did the weeds come from?"

28 "An enemy did this," he said.

The servants asked, "Do you want us to go pull them up?"

29 "No," he said. "If you pull the weeds, you might pull the wheat too. 30 Let both grow until harvest time. Then I will tell the workers: First pull the weeds and tie them into bundles to burn. Then gather the wheat into my barn."

More Stories About God's Kingdom

31 Jesus told them another story. He said, "God's kingdom is like a mustard seed that a man took and planted in his field. 32 It is the smallest of all seeds. But when it grows, it is bigger than garden plants. It becomes a tree. Birds come and make nests in its branches."

33 He told them another story. "God's kingdom is like yeast that a woman mixed into a big bowl of flour. She mixed it until all the dough rose."

34 Jesus told the crowds all these things in stories. He did not tell them anything without a story. 35 This happened to make true what the prophet said: "I will open my mouth and tell stories. I will speak things hidden since the world began."

Jesus Explains the Weed Story

36 Then Jesus left the crowds and went into the house. His followers came to Him and said, "Explain to us the story about the weeds in the field."

37 He answered, "The one who planted the good seed is the Son of Man. 38 The field is the world. The good seeds are the people of the kingdom. The weeds are the people of the evil one. 39 The enemy who planted them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age. The harvesters are angels. 40 Just as weeds are pulled up and burned, so it will be at the end of the age. 41 The Son of Man will send His angels. They will take out of His kingdom everything that causes sin and all who do wrong. 42 They will throw them into a hot fire. There they will cry and grind their teeth. 43 Then God's good people will shine like the sun in their Father's kingdom. Whoever has ears, let him listen."

Stories About Treasure, Pearls, and a Net

44 "God's kingdom is like treasure hidden in a field. A man found it and hid it again. He was so happy that he sold everything he had and bought that field."

45 "Again, God's kingdom is like a man who buys and sells things. He looked for fine pearls. 46 When he found one pearl worth a lot, he sold everything he had and bought it."

47 "Again, God's kingdom is like a net that was thrown into the sea. It caught fish of every kind. 48 When it was full, the fishermen pulled it to shore. They sat down and put the good fish in baskets. They threw the bad fish away."

49 "So it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come and separate the bad people from the good people. 50 They will throw the bad people into a hot fire. There they will cry and grind their teeth."

51 Jesus asked, "Do you understand all this?"

"Yes," they said.

52 He said, "So every teacher of God's Law who becomes a follower of God's kingdom is like a homeowner. He brings out new things and old things from his treasure room."

Jesus Goes to His Hometown

53 When Jesus finished these stories, He left that place. 54 He came to His hometown and taught the people in their meeting place. They were amazed and said, "Where did this man get such wisdom and power?"

55 "Isn't this the carpenter's son? Isn't His mother Mary? Aren't His brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas? 56 Aren't all His sisters here with us? Where did He get all this?"

57 They were upset with Him. Jesus said, "A prophet is respected everywhere except in his hometown and in his own home."

58 He did not do many miracles there because the people did not believe.
