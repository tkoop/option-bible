Praise the LORD

1 Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD, O my soul! 2 I will praise the LORD as long as I live. I will sing to my God while I have life.

Do Not Trust in People

3 Do not put your trust in leaders. Do not trust in people who cannot save you. 4 When his breath leaves him, he goes back to the dust. On that day, his plans are gone.

Happy in the LORD

5 Happy is the person whose help is the God of Jacob. His hope is in the LORD his God. 6 He made heaven and earth. He made the sea and everything in it. He is faithful forever.

The LORD Helps People

7 He does what is right for people who are treated badly. He gives food to hungry people. The LORD sets prisoners free. 8 The LORD helps blind people see. The LORD lifts up people who are bent down. The LORD loves people who do right. 9 The LORD watches over people from other lands. He helps children with no father and women with no husband. He turns the plans of bad people upside down.

The LORD Rules Forever

10 The LORD will rule forever. Your God, O Zion, will rule for all time. Praise the LORD!
