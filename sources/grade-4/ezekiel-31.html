A Warning to Egypt

1 In the eleventh year, on the first day of the third month, the the LORD spoke to me. 2 He said, "Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his people: Who are you like in your greatness?

The Great Tree and Its Fall

"3 Look, Assyria was like a cedar tree in Lebanon. It had beautiful branches. It gave shade like a forest. It grew very tall. Its top rose high among the thick branches. 4 Water made it grow. The deep water made it tall. Rivers ran around the place where it was planted. Channels sent water to all the trees in the field. 5 So it grew taller than all the trees in the field. Its branches grew many and long because it had lots of water. 6 All the birds in the sky made nests in its branches. All the wild animals had babies under its branches. Many nations lived in its shade. 7 It was beautiful because it was big. Its branches were long. Its roots reached down to lots of water. 8 The cedar trees in God's garden could not hide it. The cypress trees were not like its branches. The plane trees were not like its branches. No tree in God's garden was as pretty as it. 9 I made it pretty with many branches. All the trees in Eden, in God's garden, were jealous of it.

"10 So the Lord the LORD says, Because you grew so tall, and your top reached into the thick branches, and you became proud because of your height, 11 I will hand you over to a strong ruler of the nations. He will treat you the way your wrongs deserve. I have thrown you out. 12 Strangers, mean nations, cut it down and left it. Its branches fell on the mountains and in all the valleys. Its branches broke in every part of the land. All the people of the earth went down from its shade and left it. 13 All the birds in the sky settled on its fallen tree. All the wild animals were on its branches. 14 This is so no tree with lots of water will grow proud and tall. No tree will put its top among the thick branches. No tree that drinks water will stand tall in pride. They all must die and go down to the earth below, to be with people who go down to the grave. 15 The Lord the LORD says, On the day it went down to the grave, I made people sad. I covered the deep water over it. I stopped its rivers. I held back the great waters. I made Lebanon dark because of it. All the trees in the field were weak for it. 16 I made nations shake when they heard it fall. I brought it down to the grave with those who go down to the grave. All the trees of Eden, the best and finest of Lebanon, all that drink water, were comforted in the earth below. 17 They also went down to the grave with it, to join those killed by the sword. The people who lived in its shade among the nations also went down. 18 Who are you like in glory and greatness among the trees of Eden? You will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the earth below. You will lie among people who are not circumcised, with those killed by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his people, says the Lord the LORD."
