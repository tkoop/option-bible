Elisha Shares God's News

1 Elisha said, "Listen to the word of the the LORD. The The LORD says, 'Tomorrow at this time, a basket of fine flour will cost one coin. Two baskets of barley will cost one coin. This will happen at the gate of Samaria.'"

2 The officer who helped the king said to the man of God, "Look! Even if the the LORD opened windows in heaven, could this happen?"

Elisha said, "You will see it with your eyes. But you will not eat it."

Four Sick Men Make a Plan

3 Four men had a bad skin disease. They sat at the city gate. They said to each other, "Why are we sitting here until we die? 4 If we go into the city, we will die because there is no food. If we stay here, we will die too. Let's go to the Syrian camp. If they let us live, we live. If they kill us, we die anyway."

God Scares the Syrians

5 They got up when it was dark. They went to the Syrian camp. When they got to the edge of the camp, no one was there.

6 The The LORD had made the Syrian army hear a loud noise. It sounded like chariots, horses, and a big army. They said to each other, "The king of Israel hired the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to attack us!" 7 So they got up and ran away in the dark. They left their tents, horses, and donkeys. The camp was just as it was. They ran to save their lives.

The Men Find Treasure

8 The sick men came to the edge of the camp. They went into one tent. They ate and drank. They took silver, gold, and clothes. They hid them. Then they went into another tent. They took things and hid them too.

They Tell the King

9 They said to each other, "We are not doing right. Today is a good news day. We should not keep quiet. If we wait until morning, trouble will come to us. Let's go and tell the king's family." 10 They came to the city gate. They called to the gatekeeper. They said, "We went to the Syrian camp. No one is there. We did not hear a person. The horses and donkeys are tied up. The tents are still there."

11 The gatekeeper called out. He told the king inside.

The King Checks the Camp

12 The king got up at night. He said to his servants, "I will tell you what the Syrians did to us. They know we are hungry. So they left the camp to hide in the field. They think, 'When the people come out of the city, we will catch them alive. Then we will get into the city.'"

13 One servant said, "Please let us take five of the horses still in the city. They are few, like the people left. Let us send them to look."

14 So they took two chariot horses. The king sent men after the Syrian camp. He said, "Go and look."

15 They went after them to the Jordan River. The whole road was full of clothes and things. The Syrians had thrown them away when they ran fast. The messengers came back and told the king.

God's Word Comes True

16 The people went out. They took things from the Syrian camp. Then a basket of fine flour cost one coin. Two baskets of barley cost one coin. This happened just as the the LORD had said.

The Officer Dies at the Gate

17 The king had put the officer who leaned on his arm in charge of the gate. The people walked on him at the gate, and he died. This happened just as the man of God had said when the king came to him.

18 The man of God had said to the king, "Tomorrow at this time, two baskets of barley will cost one coin. A basket of fine flour will cost one coin. This will be at the gate of Samaria."

19 The officer had answered the man of God. He said, "Look! Even if the the LORD opened windows in heaven, could this happen?"

The man of God said, "You will see it with your eyes. But you will not eat it."

20 That is what happened to him. The people walked on him at the gate, and he died.
