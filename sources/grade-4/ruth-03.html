Naomi Makes a Plan

1 Naomi said to Ruth, "My daughter, I want to find a safe home for you. I want things to go well for you."

2 "Boaz is a relative of ours. He is the man whose workers you have been with. Tonight he will clean barley at the grain floor."

3 "Wash up and put on perfume. Put on your nice clothes. Then go down to the grain floor. But do not let him know you are there until he is done eating and drinking."

4 "Watch where he lies down. Then go and uncover his feet and lie down. He will tell you what to do."

5 Ruth said, "I will do everything you say."

Ruth Follows the Plan

6 So Ruth went down to the grain floor. She did everything Naomi told her to do.

7 Boaz ate and drank. He felt good. He went to lie down at the end of the grain pile. Ruth came quietly. She uncovered his feet and lay down.

Boaz Finds Ruth

8 In the middle of the night, the man woke up with a start. He turned over. He saw a woman lying at his feet.

9 "Who are you?" Boaz asked.

"I am your servant Ruth," she said. "Please spread your coat over me. You are a family helper."

10 "The LORD bless you, my daughter," Boaz said. "This kindness is greater than the first one. You did not run after young men, rich or poor."

11 "Now, my daughter, do not be afraid. I will do for you all you ask. All the people in my town know you are a good woman."

12 "It is true that I am a family helper. But there is another man who is a closer relative than I am."

13 "Stay here tonight. In the morning, if he wants to help you, good. Let him help you. But if he does not want to, as surely as the the LORD lives, I will help you. Lie here until morning."

14 So Ruth lay at his feet until morning. She got up before anyone could recognize her. Boaz said, "No one must know that a woman came to the grain floor."

15 He also said, "Bring me the shawl you are wearing. Hold it out." She held it out. He measured six measures of barley and put them on her. Then he went back to town.

Ruth Tells Naomi

16 When Ruth came to Naomi, Naomi asked, "How did it go, my daughter?" Then Ruth told her everything Boaz had done for her.

17 Ruth said, "He gave me these six measures of barley. He said, 'Do not go back to Naomi with nothing.'"

18 Naomi said, "Wait, my daughter, until you find out what happens. The man will not rest until this is settled today."
