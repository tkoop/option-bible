A Song About God's House

1 For the music leader. A song for Korah's sons. How lovely is Your home, the LORD of Heaven's Armies! 2 I want to be in the LORD's special place so much that I feel weak. My heart and body cry out for the living God.

3 Even a small bird has found a home. A swallow has a nest for her babies near the place where people worship You, the LORD of Heaven's Armies. You are my King and my God. 4 Happy are the people who live in Your house. They always praise You.

The Road to God's House

5 Happy is the person who gets strength from You. That person wants to travel to Your house. 6 When people walk through the dry Valley of Tears, they make it a place with springs. The first rain fills it with pools. 7 They get stronger and stronger until they meet God in Zion.

A Prayer to God

8 The LORD God of Heaven's Armies, hear my prayer. Listen, God of Jacob. 9 Look at our protector, O God. Look kindly at Your chosen king.

One Day With God

10 One day in Your special place is better than a thousand days anywhere else. I would rather stand at the door of my God's house than live in the tents of bad people. 11 The LORD God is like the sun and a shield. The LORD gives help and honor. He does not keep any good thing from people who do right. 12 The LORD of Heaven's Armies, happy is the person who trusts in You.
