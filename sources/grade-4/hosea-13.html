Israel Turns Away from God

1 When Ephraim spoke, people shook with fear. He became great in Israel. But he sinned by worshiping Baal, and he died. 2 Now they sin more and more. They make metal idols for themselves. They make them just the way they want, all from silver. Skilled workers make them. They say, "Men who kill people kiss the calf idols." 3 So they will be like a morning cloud. They will be like dew that goes away. They will be like straw blown away from grain. They will be like smoke from a chimney.

God Is the Only Savior

4 "I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt. You must know that I am the only God. No one else can save you. 5 I knew you in the desert, in the dry land."

6 They ate until they were full. Their hearts became proud. So they forgot Me.

God Will Punish Israel

7 "So I will be like a lion to them. I will watch for them like a leopard on the road. 8 I will meet them like a bear that lost her cubs. I will tear open their hearts. I will eat them there like a lion. Wild animals will tear them apart. 9 You are destroyed, Israel, because you are against Me. You are against the One who helps you."

No King Can Save Them

10 Where is your king now? Can he save you in all your cities? Where are your judges? You said, "Give me a king and leaders."

11 "I gave you a king when I was angry. I took him away when I was full of wrath."

Sin Brings Pain

12 Ephraim's guilt is saved up. His sin is not forgotten. 13 Pain like a woman in labor will come to him. He is not a wise son, because he does not come when it is time to be born.

God Will Not Show Pity

14 "I will save them from the grave. I will buy them back from death. Death, where are your hurts? Grave, where is your sting? I will not show pity."

An East Wind Will Come

15 He grows well among his brothers. But an east wind will come. The wind of the LORD will rise from the desert. His spring will dry up. His well will dry up. His storehouse will be robbed of every treasure. 16 Samaria will be punished because she turned against her God. They will fall by the sword. Their babies will be smashed to pieces. Their pregnant women will be cut open.
