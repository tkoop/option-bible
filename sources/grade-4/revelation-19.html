Praise in Heaven

1 After these things I heard a loud voice like a great crowd in heaven. They said, "Praise the Lord! Salvation and glory and power belong to our God. 2 His decisions are true and fair. He judged the great evil woman who ruined the earth with her sexual sin. He punished her for killing His servants."

3 Again they said, "Praise the Lord! The smoke from her rises forever and ever."

4 The 24 leaders and the four living beings fell down and worshiped God, who sits on the throne. They said, "Amen. Praise the Lord!"

5 A voice came from the throne. It said, "Praise our God, all you His servants, and you who respect Him, both small and great."

The Wedding of the Lamb

6 I heard something like a great crowd, like the roar of many waters, and like strong thunder. They said, "Praise the Lord! The Lord our God, the All-Powerful One, is King. 7 Let us be glad and rejoice. Let us give Him glory. The wedding of the Lamb has come, and His bride has gotten ready."

8 She was given fine white clothes, bright and clean. The fine clothes mean the good things God's holy people have done.

9 The angel said to me, "Write this: Happy are those invited to the wedding dinner of the Lamb." Then he said, "These are the true words from God."

10 I fell down at his feet to worship him. But he said, "Don't worship me! I am a servant like you and your brothers who tell about Jesus. Worship God. The message about Jesus is what prophecy is all about."

The Rider on the White Horse

11 I saw heaven open. There was a white horse. Its rider is called Faithful and True. He judges and fights fairly. 12 His eyes were like flames of fire. He wore many crowns on His head. He has a name written that only He knows. 13 He wore a robe dipped in blood. His name is called the Word of God.

14 Heaven's armies followed Him. They rode white horses and wore clean white clothes. 15 A sharp sword came from His mouth. With it He will hit the nations. He will rule them with an iron stick. He will carry out God's fierce anger, the anger of the All-Powerful God. 16 On His robe and on His thigh He has a name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.

God's Great Dinner

17 I saw an angel standing in the sun. He cried in a loud voice to all the birds flying in the sky, "Come! Gather for God's great dinner. 18 Eat the flesh of kings, army leaders, strong men, horses and riders, and all people, slaves and free, small and great."

The Beast and the False Prophet

19 I saw the beast and the kings of the earth with their armies gathered to fight the rider on the horse and His army. 20 The beast was caught. The false prophet was with him. He had done amazing signs that tricked the people who got the beast's mark and worshiped his statue. The two were thrown alive into the lake of fire burning with sulfur. 21 The rest were killed by the sword from the rider's mouth. All the birds ate until they were full.
