A Song for the King

1 My heart is full of a good thought. I say my words to the king. My tongue is like the pen of a good writer.

The King's Beauty and Power

2 You are the best looking of men. Kind words pour from your lips. So God has blessed you forever. 3 Strap your sword to your side, mighty hero. Show your glory and greatness. 4 Ride out in victory with your greatness. Fight for truth, kindness, and what is right. Let your right hand do great things. 5 Your arrows hit the hearts of the king's enemies. The nations fall under you.

6 Your throne, God, lasts forever and ever. Justice is the power of Your kingdom. 7 You have loved what is right and hated what is wrong. So God, your God, has poured oil of joy on you more than on your friends. 8 All your clothes smell sweet with myrrh, aloes, and cassia. Music from the palace makes you glad. 9 Princesses stand among your honored women. The queen stands at your right hand in gold from Ophir.

The Bride's New Life

10 Listen, daughter. Think and listen well. Forget your people and your father's house. 11 The king will want your beauty. Bow down to him, because he is your lord. 12 The daughter of Tyre will come with a gift. Rich men will ask for your favor. 13 The princess is very beautiful in her room. Her dress is sewn with gold. 14 She is led to the king in colorful clothes. Her young friends are brought to you. 15 They are led in with joy and gladness. They go into the king's palace.

A Promise for the Future

16 Your sons will take the place of your fathers. You will make them leaders in all the land. 17 I will keep your name alive in every generation. So the nations will praise you forever and ever.
