Samuel Gives Saul a Command

1 Samuel said to Saul, "The LORD sent me to pour oil on you and make you king over His people Israel. Now listen to what the LORD says. 2 This is what the LORD of Hosts says: 'I will punish the Amalekites for what they did to Israel. They attacked Israel on the way up from Egypt.' 3 Now go and attack the Amalekites. Destroy everything they have. Do not spare them. Kill men, women, children, and babies. Kill their oxen, sheep, camels, and donkeys."

Saul Gets Ready to Fight

4 So Saul called the people together. He counted them at Telaim. There were 200,000 foot soldiers and 10,000 men from Judah. 5 Saul came to the city of Amalek. He waited in the valley.

6 Saul said to the Kenites, "Leave now. Get away from the Amalekites. If you stay, I will destroy you with them. You were kind to Israel when they came up from Egypt." So the Kenites left.

Saul Spares Agag and the Best Animals

7 Saul defeated the Amalekites from Havilah to Shur, near Egypt. 8 He caught Agag, king of the Amalekites, alive. He killed everyone else with swords. 9 But Saul and the people spared Agag. They also spared the best sheep, oxen, calves, and lambs. They spared everything good. They did not want to destroy those things. But they destroyed everything weak and worthless.

The LORD Is Sorry He Made Saul King

10 The LORD spoke to Samuel: 11 "I am sorry I made Saul king. He stopped following Me. He did not do what I told him." Samuel was very angry. He cried to the LORD all night.

Samuel Goes to Find Saul

12 Early in the morning, Samuel went to meet Saul. Someone told him, "Saul went to Carmel. He set up a monument there. Then he turned and went down to Gilgal."

13 Samuel came to Saul. Saul said, "The LORD bless you! I have done what the LORD told me."

14 Samuel said, "Then what is this sound of sheep and cows I hear?"

15 Saul said, "The people brought them from the Amalekites. They spared the best sheep and oxen to sacrifice to the LORD your God. We destroyed the rest."

16 Samuel said, "Stop! Let me tell you what the LORD said to me last night."

"Speak," they said.

Samuel Tells Saul He Failed

17 Samuel said, "You may think you are not important. But you are the leader of Israel's tribes. The LORD made you king over Israel. 18 The LORD sent you on a job. He said, 'Go and destroy the sinful Amalekites. Fight them until they are gone.' 19 Why did you not obey the LORD? Why did you take the things they had and do wrong in the eyes of the LORD?"

20 Saul said to Samuel, "But I did obey the LORD. I went where He sent me. I brought back Agag, king of Amalek. I destroyed the Amalekites. 21 But the people took some sheep and oxen from the things we were to destroy. They took the best ones to sacrifice to the LORD your God at Gilgal."

22 Samuel said, "Does The LORD want burnt offerings and sacrifices more than He wants you to obey Him? No. Obeying is better than sacrifice. Listening to Him is better than offering fat rams. 23 Rebelling is like the sin of witchcraft. Being stubborn is like worshiping false gods. You rejected the word of the LORD. So He rejected you as king."

24 Saul said to Samuel, "I sinned. I broke the command of the LORD and your words. I did it because I was afraid of the people. I listened to them instead. 25 Please forgive my sin now. Come back with me so I can worship the LORD."

26 Samuel said to Saul, "I will not go back with you. You rejected the word of the LORD. So the LORD rejected you as king over Israel."

27 As Samuel turned to leave, Saul grabbed the edge of his robe. It tore.

28 Samuel said to him, "The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel away from you today. He has given it to someone better than you. 29 The LORD, the Glory of Israel, does not lie. He does not change His mind. He is not a man."

30 Then Saul said, "I sinned. But please honor me in front of my people's leaders and in front of Israel. Come back with me so I can worship the LORD your God."

31 So Samuel went back with Saul. Saul worshiped the LORD.

Samuel Kills Agag

32 Samuel said, "Bring Agag, king of the Amalekites, to me." Agag came to him, looking cheerful.

"Surely the worst of death is over."

33 Samuel said, "Your sword made many women lose their children. So your mother will lose her child." Then Samuel cut Agag into pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.

34 Samuel went to Ramah. Saul went up to his home in Gibeah. 35 Samuel never saw Saul again before Saul died. Samuel was very sad about Saul. The LORD was sorry He had made Saul king over Israel.
