God Speaks to Jeremiah in Prison

1 Jeremiah was still in the prison yard. 2 The LORD said, "I made the world. I shaped it and set it firm. 3 The LORD is His name. Call to Me. I will answer you. I will tell you great things you do not know."

The LORD Talks About the Broken Houses

4 The LORD, the God of Israel, said, "This is about the houses in this city and the kings' houses in Judah. They were torn down to help the enemy. 5 The enemy is coming to fight. The houses will be full of dead people. I struck those people down because I was angry. I turned away from this city because of all its evil."

God Will Heal His People

6 The LORD said, "But I will bring health and healing to this city. I will heal My people. I will give them much peace and truth. 7 I will bring Judah and Israel back from being taken away. I will build them up like before. 8 I will make them clean from all the wrong they did against Me. I will forgive all their sins. 9 Then this city will be a reason for joy and praise and honor to all nations on earth. They will hear about all the good I do for My people. They will be amazed and afraid because of all the good and peace I give."

Joy Will Come Back to the City

10 The LORD said, "In this place you say is empty, with no people or animals, in Judah's towns and Jerusalem's streets that are empty, with no one living there and no animals, people will hear sounds again. 11 People will hear joy and gladness. People will hear the bride and groom. People will hear others thank the LORD in His house. They will say,

'Give thanks to the LORD of all armies. The LORD is good. His faithful love lasts forever.'

"For I will make this land good again like before," says the LORD.

12 The LORD of all armies said, "This empty place, with no people or animals, and all its towns, will have shepherds again. Their sheep will rest there. 13 In the hill towns, the lowland towns, the south towns, Benjamin's land, around Jerusalem, and Judah's towns, sheep will walk past the one who counts them," says the LORD.

God's Promise to David

14 The LORD said, "Days are coming when I will keep My good promise to Israel and Judah. 15 In those days, I will make a good and fair king grow from David's family. He will do what is right in the land. 16 In those days, Judah will be saved. Jerusalem will be safe. People will call her, 'The LORD is the One who makes us right.'"

17 The LORD said, "David will never run out of sons to sit on Israel's throne. 18 The priests from Levi's family will never run out of men to bring offerings to Me," says the LORD.

God Will Keep His Promise

19 The LORD spoke to Jeremiah again. 20 He said, "If you can break My promise of day and night, so day and night do not come at the right time, 21 then you can break My promise to David and the priests. That would mean David has no son to rule. 22 But just as no one can count the stars or measure the sand by the sea, I will make David's family and the Levites who serve Me very many," says the LORD.

God Will Not Reject His People

23 The LORD spoke to Jeremiah again. 24 He said, "Have you seen what these people say? They say,

'The LORD chose two families, but He threw them away.'

"They look down on My people. They say My people are not a nation anymore. 25 But if I did not set day and night and the rules for sky and earth, 26 then I would also throw away Jacob's children and David My servant. I would not let David's children rule over Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob's children. But I will bring My people back and show them mercy," says the LORD.
