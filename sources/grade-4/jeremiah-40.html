Jeremiah Is Set Free

1 This is the message the the LORD gave Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan, the guard captain, set him free at Ramah. He had found Jeremiah in chains with the people from Jerusalem and Judah who were being taken to Babylon. 2 The guard captain found Jeremiah and said, “The The LORD your God said this trouble would come to this place. 3 And now the the LORD has done it, just like He said. This happened because you sinned against the the LORD and did not listen to His voice. That is why this trouble came to you. 4 But now I am setting you free today from the chains on your hands. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will take care of you. But if it seems wrong to you to come with me to Babylon, then don't come. Look, the whole land is in front of you. Go wherever seems good and right to you.”

5 But before Jeremiah could turn to leave, Nebuzaradan added, “Go back to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. The king of Babylon put him in charge of the cities of Judah. Stay with him among the people. Or go anywhere else that seems right to you.” Then the guard captain gave him food and a gift, and let him go. 6 So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. He stayed with him among the people left in the land.

Gedaliah Is Warned

7 All the army leaders in the fields and their men heard that the king of Babylon had put Gedaliah son of Ahikam in charge of the land. He was also in charge of the men, women, and children who were the poorest in the land. They had not been taken to Babylon. 8 So they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They were Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai from Netophah, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, along with their men.

9 Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, took an oath before them and their men. He said, “Do not be afraid to serve the Babylonians. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and things will go well for you. 10 As for me, I will stay in Mizpah to speak for you to the Babylonians who come to us. You gather wine, summer fruit, and oil. Put them in your jars. Live in the cities you have taken.”

11 All the Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and other lands heard that the king of Babylon had left some people in Judah. They heard he had put Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over them. 12 So they came back from all the places where they had been scattered. They came to Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They gathered a lot of wine and summer fruit.

13 Then Johanan son of Kareah and all the army leaders in the fields came to Gedaliah at Mizpah. 14 They said to him, “Do you know that Baalis, king of the Ammonites, sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to kill you?” But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.

15 Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke to Gedaliah in private at Mizpah. He said, “Let me go kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah. No one will know. Why should he kill you and scatter all the Jews who are gathered to you? Then the rest of the people of Judah would die.”

16 But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, “Do not do this. You are telling lies about Ishmael.”
