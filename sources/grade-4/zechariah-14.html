The Day of the LORD Is Coming

1 Look, a day for the LORD is coming. Your things will be taken and split up among you. 2 For I will bring all the nations to fight against Jerusalem. The city will be taken. Homes will be robbed. Women will be hurt. Half the people will be taken away. But the rest will still be in the city.

3 Then the LORD will go out and fight those nations. He will fight like He does on a day of war. 4 On that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The mountain will split in two from east to west. A very big valley will open. Half the mountain will move north, and half will move south. 5 You will run to the valley between My mountains. That valley will reach to Azal. You will run like you ran from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all His holy ones will come with Him.

A Strange Day and Living Water

6 On that day there will be no light. There will be no cold or frost. 7 It will be a special day that only the LORD knows. It will not be day or night. But in the evening there will be light. 8 On that day living water will flow out of Jerusalem. Half will go to the east sea, and half to the west sea. It will flow in summer and in winter. 9 The LORD will be King over the whole earth. On that day the LORD will be one, and His name will be one.

Jerusalem Will Be Safe

10 All the land will become flat from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem. But Jerusalem will stay high where it is. It will stretch from the Benjamin Gate to the old gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's wine presses. 11 People will live there. There will never again be an order to wipe it out completely. Jerusalem will be safe.

The LORD Punishes the Nations

12 This is the sickness the LORD will send on all the people who fight Jerusalem: Their bodies will rot while they stand. Their eyes will rot in their heads. Their tongues will rot in their mouths. 13 On that day the LORD will make them very afraid. Each person will grab another person's hand. One person's hand will be raised against another's hand. 14 Even Judah will fight in Jerusalem. The wealth of all the nations around will be gathered: gold, silver, and clothes in great amounts. 15 The same sickness will hit the horses, mules, camels, donkeys, and all the animals in those camps.

All Nations Will Worship the LORD

16 Then all the people left from the nations that fought Jerusalem will go up every year to worship the King, the LORD of Armies, and to celebrate the Festival of Shelters. 17 If any family on earth does not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of Armies, there will be no rain for them. 18 If the family of Egypt does not go up, the LORD will send on them the same sickness He sends on nations that do not go to celebrate the Festival of Shelters. 19 This will be the punishment for Egypt and for all nations that do not go to celebrate the Festival of Shelters.

Everything Will Be Holy

20 On that day the bells on the horses will say, ‘Holy to the LORD.’ 21 Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Armies. People who bring sacrifices can take them and cook their offerings in them. There will no longer be any sellers in the LORD’s house on that day.
