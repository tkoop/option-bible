The Word and the Light

1 In the beginning was the Word. The Word was with God, and the Word was God. 2 He was with God in the beginning. 3 All things were made through Him. Without Him, nothing was made. 4 In Him was life, and that life was the light of people. 5 The light shines in the dark, and the dark did not overcome it.

6 A man named John was sent from God. 7 He came to tell about the light. He came so all people could believe through him. 8 He was not the light. He came to tell about the light.

9 The true light was coming into the world. It gives light to everyone. 10 He was in the world. The world was made through Him, but the world did not know Him. 11 He came to His own people, but they did not welcome Him. 12 Yet to all who did welcome Him, who believed in His name, He gave the right to become children of God. 13 They were born not from blood, not from a man's desire, and not from a man's will, but from God.

14 The Word became a man and lived among us. We saw His glory. It was the glory of the only Son from the Father. He was full of grace and truth. 15 John told about Him. He cried out, "This is the One I meant when I said, 'The One coming after me is greater than me, because He was before me.'" 16 From all He has, we all received grace. It was grace on top of grace. 17 The law was given through Moses. Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ. 18 No one has ever seen God. The only Son, who is God and is at the Father's side, has made Him known.

John Tells About Jesus

19 This is John's story. The Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to ask him, "Who are you?"

20 He did not say no. He said clearly, "I am not the Christ."

21 They asked, "Are you Elijah?"

He said, "No."

They asked, "Are you the Prophet?"

He said, "No."

22 They said, "Who are you? We need an answer for those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?"

23 John said what Isaiah the prophet said: "I am a voice calling in the desert, 'Make a straight road for the Lord.'"

24 Some Pharisees had sent them. 25 They asked him, "Why do you baptize if you are not the Christ, not Elijah, and not the Prophet?"

26 John answered, "I baptize with water. But among you stands One you do not know. 27 He is coming after me. I am not worthy to untie His sandal strap." 28 This happened in Bethany, across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

John Points to Jesus

29 The next day John saw Jesus coming to him. He said, "Look! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world! 30 This is the One I meant when I said, 'A man who comes after me is greater than me, because He was before me.' 31 I did not know Him. But I came baptizing with water so He would be shown to Israel."

32 John also said, "I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove. It stayed on Him. 33 I did not know Him. But the One who sent me to baptize with water told me that the One I see the Spirit come down and stay on is the One who baptizes with the Holy Spirit. 34 I have seen this. I tell you, He is the Son of God."

Jesus' First Followers

35 The next day John was there again with two of his followers. 36 He looked at Jesus walking by and said, "Look! The Lamb of God!"

37 The two followers heard him and followed Jesus.

38 Jesus turned and saw them following. He said, "What do you want?"

They said, "Rabbi, where are you staying?" Rabbi means Teacher.

39 Jesus said, "Come and see." They came and saw where He stayed. They stayed with Him that day. It was about four in the afternoon.

40 Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, was one of the two who heard John and followed Jesus. 41 First he found his brother Simon and said, "We found the Messiah!" Messiah means Christ.

42 He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, "You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas." Cephas means Peter.

Jesus Calls More Followers

43 The next day Jesus wanted to go to Galilee. He found Philip and said, "Follow Me." 44 Philip was from Bethsaida, the town of Andrew and Peter.

45 Philip found Nathanael and said, "We found the One Moses wrote about in the Law. The prophets wrote about Him too. He is Jesus from Nazareth, the son of Joseph."

46 Nathanael said, "Can anything good come from Nazareth?"

Philip said, "Come and see."

47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming to Him. He said, "Here is a true Israelite. There is no trick in him."

48 Nathanael said, "How do you know me?"

Jesus said, "Before Philip called you, I saw you under the fig tree."

49 Nathanael said, "Rabbi, You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!"

50 Jesus said, "Do you believe because I said I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than these. 51 I tell you the truth, you will see heaven open. You will see God's angels going up and coming down on the Son of Man."
