A Prayer for Help

1 Hear my prayer, the LORD. Let my cry reach You. 2 Do not hide Your face from me when I am in trouble. Turn Your ear to me. Answer me fast when I call.

3 My days pass like smoke. My bones feel like they burn. 4 My heart feels struck down like grass that dries up. I forget to eat my bread. 5 Because I groan so loud, my bones cling to my skin.

6 I am like a bird alone in the desert, like an owl in empty places. 7 I cannot sleep. I am like a lonely bird on a roof.

8 My enemies make fun of me all day. People who tease me use my name to curse. 9 I eat ashes like bread. My tears mix with my drink. 10 This is because of Your anger. You lifted me up and threw me down. 11 My days are like a shadow at evening. I dry up like grass.

God Rules Forever

12 But You, the LORD, sit on Your throne forever. People will remember You for all time. 13 You will get up and show love to Zion. It is time to be kind to her. The set time has come. 14 Your servants love her stones. They feel sorry for her dust.

15 Nations will fear the LORD's name. All kings on earth will fear Your glory. 16 The LORD builds up Zion. He shows His glory there. 17 He listens to the prayer of the poor. He does not turn away their prayer.

18 Write this for people who come later. Then people not yet born can praise the LORD. 19 The LORD looked down from His holy place in heaven. He looked at the earth. 20 He heard the groans of prisoners. He set free those who were about to die. 21 Then they will tell the LORD's name in Zion. They will praise Him in Jerusalem. 22 Peoples and kingdoms will come together to serve the LORD.

Please Do Not Take Me Away Too Soon

23 He made me weak along the way. He cut my days short. 24 I say, "My God, do not take me away in the middle of my life. Your years last for all time."

25 Long ago You made the earth. The heavens are the work of Your hands. 26 They will pass away, but You will stay. They will wear out like clothes. You will change them like a robe, and they will pass away. 27 But You are the same. Your years never end. 28 The children of Your servants will live there. Their children will be safe with You.
