David Prays to the LORD

1 I call to You, the LORD. Do not be quiet to me, my Rock. If You do not answer, I will be like people who go down to the grave. 2 Hear my cry for help when I lift my hands to Your holy place.

Keep Me Away from Bad People

3 Do not drag me away with bad people, with people who do wrong. They say nice things to their friends, but their hearts are full of bad plans. 4 Give them what they deserve for their bad deeds. Pay them back for the wrong they have done. 5 They do not care about what the LORD does or about His great works. So He will tear them down, and they will not be built again.

The LORD Heard My Prayer

6 Praise the LORD! He heard my cry for help. 7 The LORD is my strength and my shield. My heart trusts in Him, and He helps me. So my heart is glad, and I will sing to thank Him.

The LORD Helps His People

8 The LORD gives strength to His people. He keeps His chosen one safe. 9 Save Your people and bless the people You chose. Be their shepherd and carry them forever.
