God Sets Apart the Firstborn

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2 "Set apart for Me every firstborn male. The first baby born to every mother among the Israelites belongs to Me. This is true for people and for animals."

Moses Reminds the People

3 So Moses said to the people, "Remember this day. It is the day you came out of Egypt, out of the place where you were slaves. The LORD brought you out of there by His great power. Nothing with yeast may be eaten. 4 Today, in the month of Abib, you are going out.

The Feast Without Yeast

"5 The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites, and Jebusites. He promised your fathers He would give you this land. It is a land full of good things. So keep this special service in this month. 6 For seven days, eat bread without yeast. On the seventh day, have a feast for the LORD. 7 Eat bread without yeast for all seven days. Do not let bread with yeast be seen among you. Do not let yeast be seen anywhere in your land.

A Sign to Help You Remember

"8 On that day, tell your son, 'It is because of what the LORD did for me when I came out of Egypt.' 9 It will be a sign for you on your hand and a reminder between your eyes. This is so the LORD's teaching will be in your mouth. By His great power, the LORD brought you out of Egypt. 10 So keep this rule at the right time, year after year.

Give the Firstborn to God

"11 The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites. He promised you and your fathers this land, and He will give it to you. 12 You must give the LORD the first baby born to every mother. Every firstborn male among your animals belongs to the LORD. 13 You must buy back every firstborn donkey with a lamb. If you do not buy it back, break its neck. You must also buy back your firstborn sons.

Answering Your Child's Question

"14 In the future, when your son asks you, 'What does this mean?' you must tell him, 'The LORD brought us out of Egypt by His great power. He brought us out of the place where we were slaves.' 15 When Pharaoh refused to let us go, the LORD killed every firstborn in Egypt. He killed the firstborn of people and of animals. So I sacrifice to the LORD the first male born to every mother. But I buy back my firstborn sons. 16 This will be a sign on your hand and a reminder between your eyes. The LORD brought us out of Egypt by His great power."

God Leads the People Another Way

17 When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them on the road through the land of the Philistines. That road was shorter. God said, "If the people face war, they might change their minds and go back to Egypt." 18 So God led the people around, through the desert toward the Red Sea. The Israelites left Egypt ready to fight. 19 Moses took Joseph's bones with him. Joseph had made the Israelites promise, "God will surely take care of you. Carry my bones with you when you leave this place."

God Goes Before Them

20 The people left Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the desert. 21 The LORD went ahead of them. During the day, He was in a pillar of cloud to show them the way. During the night, He was in a pillar of fire to give them light. So they could travel day and night. 22 The pillar of cloud did not leave them during the day. The pillar of fire did not leave them during the night.
