Josiah Keeps the Passover

1 Josiah kept the Passover for the LORD in Jerusalem. They killed the Passover lamb on the fourteenth day of the first month. 2 He put the priests in their work groups and helped them serve in the house of the LORD. 3 He said to the Levites, who taught all Israel and were holy to the LORD, "Put the holy ark in the house Solomon built. You do not have to carry it on your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and His people Israel."

4 Get ready by your family groups, by your divisions. Follow what King David wrote and what his son Solomon wrote. 5 Stand in the holy place with the groups of your brothers, the sons of the people, and with the family groups of the Levites. 6 Kill the Passover lamb. Clean yourselves. Get ready to help your brothers do what the LORD said through Moses."

Gifts for the Passover

7 Josiah gave the people thirty thousand lambs and young goats, and three thousand bulls. These were for the Passover for all the people who were there. 8 His leaders also gave gifts to the people, the priests, and the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the leaders of God's house, gave the priests two thousand six hundred lambs for the Passover and three hundred bulls. 9 Conaniah and his brothers Shemaiah, Nethanel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad, the leaders of the Levites, gave the Levites five thousand lambs for the Passover and five hundred bulls.

The Passover Is Served

10 The work was ready. The priests stood where they should stand, and the Levites stood in their groups, just as the king had said. 11 They killed the Passover lamb. The priests sprinkled the blood. The Levites skinned the animals. 12 They took away the burnt offering parts to give to each family group of the people. They offered them to the LORD, as Moses' book says. They did the same with the bulls. 13 They roasted the Passover lamb over the fire, as the rule says. They cooked the holy parts in pots, pans, and bowls. Then they hurried and gave them to all the people.

14 After that, they got things ready for themselves and the priests. The priests, Aaron's sons, were busy offering burnt offerings and fat until night. The Levites got things ready for themselves and for the priests, Aaron's sons. 15 The singers, Asaph's sons, stood where they should stand, as King David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, the king's prophet, had said. The gatekeepers stood at each gate. They did not have to leave their work, because their brothers the Levites got things ready for them. 16 So all the work of the LORD was ready that day. They kept the Passover and offered burnt offerings on the altar of the LORD, just as King Josiah had said.

17 The Israelites who were there kept the Passover then. They also kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days. 18 No Passover like it had been kept in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. None of Israel's kings had kept a Passover like the one Josiah kept, with the priests, the Levites, all Judah and Israel who were there, and the people of Jerusalem. 19 This Passover was kept in the eighteenth year of Josiah's rule.

Josiah Fights Neco

20 After all this, Josiah had fixed up the temple. Neco, the king of Egypt, went up to fight at Carchemish by the Euphrates River. Josiah went out to meet him. 21 Neco sent men to Josiah with this message: "King of Judah, this is not your fight. I am not coming against you today. I am going to fight another group. God told me to hurry. Stop fighting against God, who is with me, or He will destroy you." 22 But Josiah did not turn away. He went out in disguise to fight. He did not listen to Neco's words, which came from God. He went to fight in the valley of Megiddo.

Josiah Is Wounded and Dies

23 Archers shot King Josiah. He said to his servants, "Carry me away. I am badly hurt." 24 His servants took him out of his chariot. They put him in his second chariot. They brought him to Jerusalem. He died and was buried with his fathers. All Judah and Jerusalem were sad for Josiah.

People Mourn Josiah

25 Jeremiah was sad for Josiah. All the men and women who sang made sad songs about Josiah. They made these songs a rule in Israel. They are written in the book of Laments. 26 The rest of what Josiah did, and his good deeds, are written in the law of the LORD. 27 His first and last acts are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.
