The Philistines Get Ready to Fight

1 At that time, the Philistines gathered their armies to fight Israel.

Achish said to David, "You know for sure that you and your men will go out with me in the camp."

2 David said to Achish, "Then you will know what your servant can do."

Achish said to David, "Then I will make you my bodyguard forever."

Samuel Dies

3 Samuel had died. All Israel was sad and buried him in Ramah, his town. Saul had sent away all the people who talk to spirits.

The Two Armies Camp

4 The Philistines came and camped at Shunem. Saul gathered all Israel and camped at Gilboa.

Saul Is Afraid

5 Saul saw the Philistine camp. He was afraid, and his heart shook very much.

6 Saul asked the the LORD for help, but the the LORD did not answer him. He did not answer by dreams, by the Urim stones, or by prophets.

Saul Looks for a Woman Who Talks to Spirits

7 Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who talks to spirits. I will go to her and ask her for help."

His servants said, "There is a woman who talks to spirits at En Dor."

Saul Goes to the Woman at Night

8 Saul put on other clothes to hide who he was. He and two men went to the woman at night. He said, "Please tell my future by a spirit. Bring up for me the person I name."

9 The woman said, "You know what Saul did. He took the people who talk to spirits out of the land. Why are you trying to trap me and kill me?"

10 Saul promised her by the the LORD, "As the the LORD lives, no harm will come to you for this."

11 The woman said, "Who should I bring up for you?"

Saul said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

12 When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out loudly. The woman said to Saul, "Why did you trick me? You are Saul!"

13 The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?"

The woman said to Saul, "I see a spirit coming up from the ground."

14 Saul said, "What does he look like?"

The woman said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wearing a robe." Saul knew it was Samuel. He bowed his face to the ground.

Samuel Speaks to Saul

15 Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you bothered me by bringing me up?"

Saul said, "I am in great trouble. The Philistines are fighting against me, and God has left me. He does not answer me anymore, not by prophets or by dreams. So I called you to tell me what to do."

16 Samuel said, "Why do you ask me? The the LORD has left you and become your enemy."

17 Samuel said, "The The LORD has done what He said through me. The The LORD has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbor David."

18 Samuel said, "Because you did not obey the the LORD and did not carry out His fierce anger against Amalek, the the LORD has done this to you today."

19 Samuel said, "The The LORD will also give Israel with you into the hands of the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The The LORD will also give the army of Israel into the hands of the Philistines."

Saul Falls Down

20 Saul quickly fell full length on the ground. He was very afraid of Samuel's words. He had no strength, because he had not eaten all day or all night.

The Woman Feeds Saul

21 The woman came to Saul. She saw that he was very upset. She said, "Look, your servant listened to you. I risked my life and did what you said. 22 Now please listen to me too. Let me put a piece of bread in front of you. Eat, so you will have strength for your trip."

23 He refused and said, "I will not eat." But his servants and the woman urged him. He listened to them. He got up from the ground and sat on the bed.

24 The woman had a fat calf in the house. She hurried and killed it. She took flour, kneaded it, and baked bread without yeast.

25 She set the food in front of Saul and his servants. They ate. Then they got up and left that night.
