Dead in Sin

1 And you were dead in your wrongs and sins. 2 You used to live in them, like the world around you. You followed the evil ruler of the air. His spirit works now in people who do not obey God. 3 We all lived like that once. We did what our bodies and minds wanted. Like everyone else, we were born under God's anger.

Saved by Grace

4 But God is full of mercy. He loved us with great love. 5 Even when we were dead in our sins, He made us alive with Christ. You are saved by grace. 6 He raised us up. He sat us with Christ in heaven. 7 He did this so that in the times to come, He could show how very rich His grace is. He shows kindness to us through Christ Jesus.

8 You are saved by grace through faith. This is not from yourselves. It is God's gift. 9 It is not from things you do. No one can brag. 10 We are God's work. He made us new in Christ Jesus to do good things. God planned these good things long ago for us to do.

One New Family

11 So remember this: You were not Jews. People who are called "circumcised" called you "uncircumcised." That mark was made by hands. 12 At that time, you were without Christ. You were cut off from Israel. You did not share in God's promises. You had no hope. You were without God in the world. 13 But now, in Christ Jesus, you who were far away have been brought near by Christ's blood.

14 Christ Himself is our peace. He made both groups one. He broke down the wall that kept them apart. 15 In His body, He ended the law with its commands and rules. He did this to make one new person out of the two groups. He brought peace. 16 He joined both groups to God in one body through the cross. On the cross, He put their hate to death. 17 He came and told good news of peace to you who were far away and to those who were near. 18 Through Him, both of us can come to the Father by one Spirit.

God's House

19 So you are no longer strangers or guests. You are citizens with God's people. You belong to God's family. 20 You are built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets. Christ Jesus Himself is the most important stone. 21 In Him, the whole building fits together. It grows into a holy temple for the Lord. 22 In Him, you are being built together into a home for God by the Spirit.
