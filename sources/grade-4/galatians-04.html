A Child Who Will Own Everything

1 I say this: While a child who will get everything is still young, he is like a slave. He owns it all, but he must obey the adults who care for him until his father says it is time. 2 He is under those adults until the time his father set. 3 In the same way, when we were young, we were slaves to the basic things of this world. 4 But when the right time came, God sent His Son. He was born from a woman. He was born under the law. 5 He did this to set free the people under the law. Then we could become God's children. 6 Because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of His Son into your hearts. The Spirit cries, "Abba, Father." 7 So you are not a slave anymore. You are a son. And if you are a son, you are also one who will get what God gives.

Do Not Go Back

8 Before, you did not know God. You served things that are not really gods. 9 But now you know God. Even more, God knows you. So why do you turn back to weak and poor things? Do you want to be slaves to them again? 10 You watch days, months, times, and years. 11 I am afraid for you. I hope my work for you was not for nothing.

Paul's Love for Them

12 Brothers, I beg you, become like me. I became like you. You did not hurt me. 13 You know I first told you the good news because I was weak in my body. 14 You did not look down on my body's trouble. You did not spit at me. Instead, you welcomed me like an angel of God, like Christ Jesus. 15 Where is that happy feeling you had? I can say this: If you could, you would have taken out your own eyes and given them to me. 16 Have I become your enemy because I tell you the truth? 17 Those people want you, but not for a good reason. They want to shut you out so you will want them. 18 It is good to want good things always, not only when I am with you. 19 My children, I feel pain for you again. I will keep feeling pain until Christ shows in you. 20 I wish I could be with you now. I would talk in a different way. I do not know what to do about you.

Two Mothers

21 Tell me, you who want to be under the law, do you not hear the law? 22 It is written that Abraham had two sons. One was from a slave woman. One was from a free woman. 23 The son from the slave woman was born in the normal way. The son from the free woman was born because of God's promise. 24 These things are a picture. The two women are like two special promises. One promise is from Mount Sinai. It gives birth to slaves. This is Hagar. 25 Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia. She is like the city of Jerusalem now. She and her children are slaves. 26 But the Jerusalem in heaven is free. She is our mother. 27 It is written, "Be happy, woman who has no children. Shout and cry out, woman who has never had pain. The children of the desert will be more than the children of the woman who has a husband." 28 Brothers, you are children of the promise, like Isaac. 29 Back then, the son born in the normal way hurt the son born by the Spirit. It is the same now. 30 But what does the writing say? "Send away the slave woman and her son. The slave woman's son will not share the family gift with the free woman's son." 31 So, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman. We are children of the free woman.
