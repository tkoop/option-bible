A Prayer for Mercy

1 I look up to You, who live in heaven. 2 Look! Like a servant looks to his master, and like a maid looks to her mistress, so we look to the LORD our God until He is kind to us.

3 Be kind to us, the LORD, be kind to us, because we have had too much scorn. 4 We have had too much mocking from people who have an easy life, and too much scorn from proud people.
