The LORD Will Judge the Nations

1 For look, in those days and at that time, I will bring back the people of Judah and Jerusalem. 2 I will bring all the nations down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will judge them for what they did to My people Israel. They scattered My people among the nations and split up My land. 3 They threw lots for My people. They sold boys to bad women. They sold girls for wine and drank it.

4 Tyre, Sidon, and all the lands of Philistia, what are you to Me? Are you paying Me back? If you are paying Me back, I will quickly pay you back. You will get what you deserve. 5 You took My silver and gold. You carried My best things into your temples. 6 You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks. You sent them far from their home. 7 But I will bring them back from where you sold them. I will pay you back for what you did.

8 I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah. They will sell them to faraway people. The LORD has spoken.

Get Ready for War

9 Tell this to the nations: Get ready for war! Wake up the strong men. Let all the fighters come near.

10 Make your plows into swords. Make your garden tools into spears. Let even a weak man say, "I am a strong fighter."

11 Hurry and come, all nations around. Gather there. The LORD, bring down Your warriors.

12 Let the nations get ready and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will sit to judge all the nations around. 13 Cut the grain, because the harvest is ready. Come and crush the grapes, because the winepress is full. The vats spill over. Their wrong is great.

The Day of the LORD Is Near

14 Huge crowds, huge crowds in the Valley of Decision! The day of the LORD is near in the Valley of Decision. 15 The sun and moon will grow dark. The stars will stop shining.

The LORD Protects His People

16 The LORD will roar from Zion. He will shout from Jerusalem. Heaven and earth will shake. But the LORD will be a safe place for His people. He will be a strong fort for the people of Israel. 17 Then you will know that I am the LORD your God. I live in Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy. Strangers will not walk through it again.

Good Things for God's People

18 On that day, the mountains will drip with sweet wine. The hills will flow with milk. All the streams of Judah will flow with water. A spring will come from the house of the LORD and water the valley of Shittim. 19 Egypt will become empty land. Edom will become a dry desert. This is because they hurt the people of Judah. They killed innocent people in their land. 20 But Judah will live forever. Jerusalem will last for all time. 21 I will pay them back for the blood they spilled. I did not pay them back before. The LORD lives in Zion.
