Paul Greets Timothy

1 Paul is an apostle of Christ Jesus. God chose this by His will. It is about the promise of life in Christ Jesus. 2 To Timothy, my dear child: grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

Paul Thanks God for Timothy

3 I thank God. I serve Him with a clean heart, like my family before me. I remember you day and night in my prayers. 4 I remember your tears. I want to see you. Then I will be full of joy. 5 I remember your true faith. It lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice. I am sure it lives in you too.

Use God's Gift

6 That is why I remind you to stir up God's gift in you. God gave it to you when I put my hands on you. 7 God did not give us a spirit of fear. He gave us power, love, and self-control.

Do Not Be Ashamed

8 So do not be ashamed of the story of our Lord. Do not be ashamed of me, His prisoner. Share the hard times for the good news. God gives you power to do it. 9 God saved us. He called us with a holy call. He did not do it because of our works. He did it because of His own plan and grace. He gave this grace to us in Christ Jesus before time began. 10 Now it is shown to us. Jesus Christ our Savior came and made it clear. He ended death. He brought life and life that never ends. He did this through the good news. 11 I was chosen to tell this good news. I am a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher.

Hold the True Teaching

12 That is why I suffer these things. But I am not ashamed. I know whom I have trusted. I am sure He can keep what I trusted to Him until that day. 13 Hold on to the true words you heard from me. Hold them with faith and love in Christ Jesus. 14 Guard the good gift. The Holy Spirit lives in us. He will help you.

Friends Who Helped Paul

15 You know that everyone in Asia turned away from me. Phygelus and Hermogenes are two of them. 16 May the Lord show mercy to Onesiphorus's family. He cheered me up many times. He was not ashamed of my chains. 17 When he was in Rome, he looked hard for me. He found me. 18 May the Lord let him find mercy from the Lord on that day. You know better than I do how much he helped in Ephesus.
