Masters and Slaves

1 Masters, give your slaves what is right and fair. You know you also have a Master in heaven.

Pray and Live Wisely

2 Keep praying. Stay awake and be thankful. 3 Pray for us too. Ask God to open a door for our message, so we can tell the secret plan of Christ. That is why I am in chains. 4 Pray that I can make it clear, as I should speak.

5 Walk with wisdom toward people outside the church. Make the most of every chance. 6 Let your words always be kind and helpful, so you know how to answer each person.

Paul's Friends and Helpers

7 Tychicus will tell you everything about me. He is a loved brother, a faithful helper, and a fellow servant in the Lord. 8 I sent him to you for this: so you may know how we are, and so he may comfort your hearts. 9 With him is Onesimus, the faithful and loved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you everything here.

10 Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you. Mark, Barnabas's cousin, greets you too. You got instructions about him. If he comes to you, welcome him. 11 Jesus, called Justus, also greets you. These are the only Jewish helpers I have for God's kingdom. They have been a comfort to me.

12 Epaphras, who is one of you, greets you. He is a servant of Christ Jesus. He always works hard in prayer for you, so you may stand mature and fully sure in all God's will. 13 I can say he works very hard for you and for the people in Laodicea and Hierapolis.

14 Luke, the loved doctor, and Demas greet you.

Final Words

15 Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and Nympha and the church in her house. 16 When this letter is read among you, make sure it is read in the church of the Laodiceans too. Also read the letter from Laodicea. 17 Tell Archippus, "See that you finish the work you got in the Lord."

18 I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
