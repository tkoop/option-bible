Kings East of the Jordan River

1 These are the kings of the land that the people of Israel beat. They took the land east of the Jordan River. It went from the Arnon River valley to Mount Hermon. It also went east to the dry plain. 2 Sihon was king of the Amorites. He lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer by the Arnon River, through the middle of that river, up to the Jabbok River. That was the border of the Ammonites. He also ruled half of Gilead. 3 He ruled the dry plain east to the Sea of Galilee and the Salt Sea. He ruled the road to Beth-jeshimoth and the south under the slopes of Pisgah. 4 Og was king of Bashan. He was one of the last of a giant people called the Rephaim. He lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei. 5 He ruled Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all of Bashan. His land went to the borders of the Geshurites and Maacathites. It also reached half of Gilead, next to Sihon, king of Heshbon. 6 Moses, the servant of the LORD, and the Israelites beat these kings. Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave their land to the people of Reuben, Gad, and half of the family group of Manasseh to live on.

Kings West of the Jordan River

7 These are the kings of the land Joshua and the Israelites beat west of the Jordan River. Their land went from Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which goes up toward Seir. Joshua gave this land to the people of Israel to share. 8 It included the hill country, the lowlands, the dry plain, the slopes, the desert, and the south country. The people there were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

The Thirty-One Kings

9 The king of Jericho, one. The king of Ai, near Bethel, one. 10 The king of Jerusalem, one. The king of Hebron, one. 11 The king of Jarmuth, one. The king of Lachish, one. 12 The king of Eglon, one. The king of Gezer, one. 13 The king of Debir, one. The king of Geder, one. 14 The king of Hormah, one. The king of Arad, one. 15 The king of Libnah, one. The king of Adullam, one. 16 The king of Makkedah, one. The king of Bethel, one. 17 The king of Tappuah, one. The king of Hepher, one. 18 The king of Aphek, one. The king of Lasharon, one. 19 The king of Madon, one. The king of Hazor, one. 20 The king of Shimron-meron, one. The king of Achshaph, one. 21 The king of Taanach, one. The king of Megiddo, one. 22 The king of Kedesh, one. The king of Jokneam in Carmel, one. 23 The king of Dor in Naphath-dor, one. The king of Goiim in Gilgal, one. 24 The king of Tirzah, one. All together there were thirty-one kings.
