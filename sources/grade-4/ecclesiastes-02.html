I Tried to Find Joy

1 I said to myself, "Let me try being happy. Let me enjoy good things." But that was useless too. 2 I said, "Laughing is silly. What good is fun?"

3 I tried to cheer myself with wine. My mind still used wisdom. I tried foolish things. I wanted to see what good people can do on earth in their short lives.

4 I made my work bigger. I built houses for myself. I planted vineyards. 5 I made gardens and parks. I planted all kinds of fruit trees in them. 6 I made pools of water to water a forest of growing trees.

7 I bought slaves, men and women. I had servants born in my house. I had more cows, sheep, and goats than anyone before me in Jerusalem. 8 I gathered silver and gold. I gathered treasures from kings and lands. I got singers, men and women. I got many wives, which men enjoy.

9 I became great. I had more than anyone in Jerusalem before me. My wisdom stayed with me. 10 I did not keep my eyes from anything they wanted. I did not keep my heart from any joy. My heart was glad from all my work. That was my share from all my work.

Everything Was Useless

11 Then I looked at all the work my hands had done. I looked at the hard work I had done. But all of it was useless. There was no real gain on earth.

Wisdom and Folly

12 Then I turned to look at wisdom, madness, and foolishness. What can the next king do? Only what has already been done. 13 I saw that wisdom is better than foolishness, like light is better than dark.

14 The wise man has eyes in his head, but the fool walks in the dark. Yet I saw that the same thing happens to both. 15 I said to myself, "What happens to the fool will happen to me too. So why was I so wise?" I said to myself, "That is useless too."

16 No one remembers the wise man or the fool forever. In the days to come, all will be forgotten. The wise man dies just like the fool.

I Hated My Work

17 So I hated life. The work done on earth was bad to me. All was useless. 18 I hated all the work I did on earth. I must leave it to the person who comes after me.

19 Who knows if that person will be wise or foolish? Yet he will rule over all my work. I worked and used wisdom on earth. That is useless too. 20 So I gave up. My heart was sad about all the work I did on earth.

21 A person may work with wisdom, knowledge, and skill. But he must give his share to someone who did not work for it. That is useless. It is a great wrong. 22 What does a person get from all his work and worry on earth?

23 All his days are pain. His work is sad. Even at night his heart does not rest. That is useless too.

God Gives Joy

24 There is nothing better for a person than to eat and drink and find joy in his work. I saw that this too comes from God. 25 For who can eat or feel joy without Him? 26 God gives wisdom, knowledge, and joy to the person who pleases Him. But to the sinner He gives the work of gathering and storing up, to give to the one who pleases God. That is useless.
