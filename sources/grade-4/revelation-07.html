Four Angels Hold Back the Wind

1 After this, I saw four angels. They stood at the four corners of the earth. They held back the four winds. No wind blew on the earth, the sea, or any tree. 2 Then I saw another angel. He came from the east. He had the seal of the living God. He called out in a loud voice to the four angels. God had given them power to hurt the earth and the sea. 3 He said, "Do not hurt the earth, the sea, or the trees. Wait until we put God's mark on the foreheads of His servants."

God's Servants Are Counted

4 Then I heard how many people were marked. There were 144,000. They came from all the tribes of Israel. 5 From Judah: 12,000. From Reuben: 12,000. From Gad: 12,000. 6 From Asher: 12,000. From Naphtali: 12,000. From Manasseh: 12,000. 7 From Simeon: 12,000. From Levi: 12,000. From Issachar: 12,000. 8 From Zebulun: 12,000. From Joseph: 12,000. From Benjamin: 12,000.

A Huge Crowd in White Robes

9 After this, I saw a huge crowd. No one could count them. They came from every nation, tribe, people, and language. They stood in front of the throne and in front of the Lamb. They wore white robes. They held palm branches in their hands. 10 They shouted in a loud voice, "Our God saves us! He sits on the throne. The Lamb saves us too!"

11 All the angels stood around the throne. The leaders and the four living beings were there too. They fell on their faces before the throne. They worshiped God. 12 They said, "Amen! Praise, glory, wisdom, thanks, honor, power, and strength belong to our God forever and ever. Amen!"

Who Are These People?

13 One of the leaders spoke to me. He said, "Who are these people in white robes? Where did they come from?"

14 I said, "Sir, you know."

Then he said, "These are the people who came out of the great trouble. They washed their robes. They made them white in the blood of the Lamb."

God Takes Care of Them

15 So they stand before God's throne. They serve Him day and night in His temple. The One who sits on the throne will live with them. 16 They will never be hungry again. They will never be thirsty again. The sun will not burn them. No heat will hurt them. 17 The Lamb is in the middle of the throne. He will be their shepherd. He will lead them to springs of living water. God will wipe every tear from their eyes.
