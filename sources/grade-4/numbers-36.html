A Question About Land

1 The leaders of Gilead's family group came to Moses. Gilead was the son of Machir, and Machir was the son of Manasseh. They were from Joseph's family. They spoke to Moses and to the leaders of Israel's family groups. 2 They said, "The LORD told you, our leader, to give the land to Israel's people by drawing lots. The LORD also told you to give our brother Zelophehad's land to his daughters. 3 But if his daughters marry men from another tribe of Israel, their land will be taken from our tribe. It will be added to the tribe they marry into. Then our tribe will lose that land. 4 When the special freedom year comes for Israel's people, their land will be added to the tribe they married into. It will be taken away from our tribe."

God's Answer

5 Moses told the people of Israel what the LORD said. He said, "The tribe of Joseph is right. 6 This is what the LORD commands about Zelophehad's daughters: They may marry anyone they want. But they must marry a man from their father's tribe. 7 Land in Israel must not move from one tribe to another. Each person must keep the land of his father's tribe. 8 Every girl who gets land in Israel must marry a man from her father's tribe. Then Israel's people can keep the land of their fathers. 9 So no land will move from one tribe to another. Each tribe of Israel must keep its own land."

The Daughters Obey

10 Zelophehad's daughters did what the LORD told Moses. 11 Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married their cousins on their father's side. 12 They married men from the family groups of Manasseh, who was Joseph's son. Their land stayed with their father's tribe.

The End of the Commands

13 These are the rules and laws the LORD gave Israel through Moses. He gave them on the flat land of Moab, by the Jordan River, across from Jericho.
