Wise Words for Life

1 Bad people run away even when no one chases them. But good people are very brave. 2 When a country does wrong, it has many rulers. But a wise leader helps it stay strong longer. 3 A poor person who is mean to other poor people is like a hard rain that ruins the crops. 4 People who leave God's law praise bad people. But people who keep God's law stand up to them. 5 Bad people do not understand what is fair. But people who follow the the LORD understand it well.

Rich and Poor

6 It is better to be poor and honest than rich and crooked. 7 A son who keeps the law is smart. But a boy who hangs around with greedy eaters brings shame to his dad. 8 A person who gets rich by charging high interest will end up giving it to someone kind to poor people. 9 If a person will not listen to God's law, God hates even his prayer. 10 A person who leads good people into a bad path will be caught in his own trap. But honest people will get good things. 11 A rich person thinks he is wise. But a poor person with good sense knows he is not truly wise.

Rulers and Leaders

12 When good people win, there is great joy. When bad people take charge, people hide. 13 A person who hides his sins will not do well. But a person who says he is sorry and stops doing wrong will be forgiven. 14 Happy is the person who always shows respect for the the LORD. But a person with a hard heart falls into trouble. 15 A bad ruler over poor people is fierce and scary. 16 A leader with no good sense hurts many people. But a leader who hates cheating will live a long time. 17 A person who has killed someone will run away for life. Do not help him. 18 A person who lives an honest life will be safe. But a person who lives a crooked life will fall fast.

Work and Money

19 A person who works his land will have plenty of food. But a person who chases empty dreams will be very poor. 20 A person who keeps his word will be blessed a lot. But a person who rushes to get rich will not escape trouble. 21 It is not good to treat people unfairly. But some people will do wrong for a piece of bread. 22 A greedy person rushes after money and does not know that poverty is coming. 23 A person who corrects someone will be liked more later than a person who only says sweet things. 24 A person who steals from his mom or dad and says, "I did nothing wrong," is a friend to a person who destroys things.

Trust in the LORD

25 A greedy person starts fights. But a person who trusts in the the LORD will be blessed. 26 A person who trusts his own heart is a fool. But a person who lives with wisdom will be saved. 27 A person who gives to poor people will not be in need. But a person who looks away will receive many curses. 28 When bad people take power, people hide. But when bad people die, good people will do well.
