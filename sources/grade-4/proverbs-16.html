Plans and the LORD

1 People make plans in their hearts, but the answer on their tongues comes from the LORD. 2 People think all their ways are right, but the LORD checks their hearts. 3 Give your work to the LORD, and your plans will come true. 4 The LORD made everything for His plan, even bad people for a day of trouble.

Pride and Humility

5 The LORD hates a proud heart. Know that a proud person will be punished. 6 Love and truth can cover wrongs, and fearing the LORD helps people turn from evil. 7 When a person pleases the LORD, He makes even enemies be at peace with that person. 8 It is better to have a little with what is right than a lot gained by wrong. 9 People plan their path, but the LORD sets their steps.

Kings and Fairness

10 A king's words are like a fair decision; his mouth should not be unfair. 11 Fair scales and weights come from the LORD; He cares about all the weights. 12 Kings hate bad actions, because a throne stands strong by doing right. 13 A king likes honest words, and he loves people who speak truth. 14 A king's anger can bring death, but a wise person can calm it. 15 When a king smiles, there is life; his favor is like a spring rain cloud.

Wisdom and Words

16 It is much better to get wisdom than gold, and understanding is better than silver. 17 Good people stay away from evil; the one who watches his path keeps himself safe. 18 Pride comes before ruin, and a proud spirit before a fall. 19 It is better to be humble with poor people than to share stolen things with proud people. 20 The one who listens to teaching will do well, and happy is the one who trusts the LORD. 21 People with wise hearts are called smart, and kind words help people learn. 22 Understanding is like a life-giving spring for its owner, but teaching a fool is foolish. 23 A wise heart teaches his mouth, and adds knowledge to his lips. 24 Kind words are like honey, sweet to the soul and healing to the body.

Bad Choices and Bad People

25 There is a way that looks right to a person, but it ends in death. 26 A worker's hunger helps him work, because his need pushes him on. 27 A bad person digs up trouble, and his words burn like fire. 28 A crooked person spreads trouble, and a gossip splits close friends. 29 A violent person tricks his neighbor and leads him down a bad path. 30 A person who winks plans bad things; a person who presses his lips has done evil. 31 Gray hair is a crown of glory, found on the path of doing right. 32 It is better to be patient than a warrior, and to control yourself than to take a city. 33 People throw lots, but the LORD decides the result.
