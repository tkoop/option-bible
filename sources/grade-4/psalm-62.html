A Song by David

1 I wait quietly for God alone. He saves me. 2 He alone is my rock. He saves me. He is my safe place. I will not be shaken.

People Who Hurt Me

3 How long will you attack one man? Will all of you knock him down? He is like a wall about to fall, like a fence ready to break. 4 They plan to bring him down from his high place. They love lies. They say good things with their mouths, but they curse him in their hearts. Selah

I Will Trust in God

5 I tell myself to rest in God alone. I hope in Him. 6 He alone is my rock. He saves me. He is my safe place. I will not be shaken. 7 God saves me and gives me honor. He is my strong rock. I am safe in Him. 8 Trust in Him at all times, people. Pour out your hearts to Him. God is our safe place. Selah

People Are Not Strong

9 People are like a puff of air. Even important people are a lie. On a scale, they are too light. They weigh less than a puff of air. 10 Do not trust in money you took by force. Do not hope in stolen things. Even if your money grows, do not set your heart on it.

God Is Strong and Kind

11 God has said one thing. I have heard two things. Power belongs to God. 12 And that You, Lord, are kind. You will give each person what he deserves.
