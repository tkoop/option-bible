Wise Words and Family

1 A wise child listens when a parent corrects him. A person who laughs at good advice will not listen to a warning. 2 A person's own words can bring good things. But a person who betrays others wants to hurt them. 3 A person who guards his mouth keeps his life safe. A person who talks too much brings trouble on himself. 4 A lazy person wants things but gets nothing. A hard worker gets plenty. 5 A good person hates lies. A bad person brings shame and disgrace. 6 Doing right keeps a good person on the right path. Doing wrong leads a bad person to fall.

Money and Hard Work

7 Some people act rich but have nothing. Some act poor but are very rich. 8 A rich person can use money to save his life. A poor person does not hear threats. 9 Good people shine like a bright light. A bad person's lamp goes out. 10 Pride brings fights. Wise people listen to advice. 11 Money from tricks does not last. Money earned by hard work grows.

Hope and Good Advice

12 Waiting too long makes the heart sick. Getting what a person wants is like a tree that gives life. 13 A person who laughs at good advice will be punished. A person who respects a command is rewarded. 14 A wise person's teaching is like water that gives life. It helps a person stay away from deadly traps. 15 Good sense wins favor. The path of a person who betrays others is hard. 16 A careful person acts with knowledge. A fool shows his foolishness.

Friends and Choices

17 A bad messenger gets into trouble. A faithful messenger brings healing. 18 A person who ignores correction becomes poor and ashamed. A person who listens to correction is honored. 19 Getting what a person wants feels good. But fools hate turning away from evil. 20 A person who walks with wise people becomes wise. A person who walks with fools is ruined. 21 Trouble follows people who do wrong. Good things come to good people.

Family and Food

22 A good person leaves wealth for his grandchildren. A bad person's wealth is saved for good people. 23 Poor people's fields can grow a lot of food. But without fairness, it is swept away. 24 A parent who does not correct his child does not love him. A parent who loves his child corrects him. 25 Good people eat until they are full. Bad people go hungry.
