The LORD Speaks

1 The LORD says this: Heaven is My throne. The earth is where I rest My feet. What house will you build for Me? Where will I rest? 2 My hand made all these things. So they came to be, says the the LORD. But I look to the poor, to the one who is sad in spirit, and who shakes at My word.

Wrong Worship

3 A person who kills a bull is like one who kills a man. A person who gives a lamb is like one who breaks a dog's neck. A person who brings a grain gift is like one who brings pig's blood. A person who burns sweet smoke is like one who blesses a false god. They chose their own ways. Their hearts enjoy their bad things. 4 So I will choose their punishment. I will bring the things they fear. I called, but no one answered. I spoke, but they did not listen. They did evil in My eyes. They chose what I did not want.

God's People Are Mocked

5 Listen to the word of the the LORD, you who shake at His word. Your brothers hate you and push you away because of My name. They say,

"Let the the LORD be honored, so we can see your joy."

But they will be put to shame. 6 A loud noise comes from the city! A sound comes from the temple! It is the the LORD paying back His enemies.

A New Nation Is Born

7 Before she was in pain, she had a baby. Before the pain came, she gave birth to a boy. 8 Who has heard of such a thing? Who has seen such things? Can a land be born in one day? Can a whole nation be born in one moment? Yet Zion had pain and gave birth to her children. 9 Would I bring pain and not let the baby come? says the the LORD. Would I, who helps birth, close the womb? says your God.

Joy for Jerusalem

10 Be happy with Jerusalem and be glad for her, all who love her. Be very happy with her, all who cry for her. 11 So you may drink milk and be full from her comfort. So you may drink deeply and enjoy her glory.

12 For the the LORD says this: I will give her peace like a river. I will give her the glory of the nations like a flooding stream. You will drink milk. You will be carried on her hip and bounced on her knees. 13 As a man is comforted by his mother, so I will comfort you. You will be comforted in Jerusalem. 14 You will see this. Your heart will be glad. Your bones will grow strong like grass. The hand of the the LORD will be known to His servants. He will be angry with His enemies.

The LORD Comes to Judge

15 For the the LORD will come in fire. His war carts will come like a windstorm. He will pay back His anger with fury. He will speak strong words with flames of fire. 16 For the the LORD will judge all people with fire and with His sword. Many will be killed by the the LORD. 17 Those who make themselves holy and clean themselves to go into gardens, following one in the middle, eating pig meat, bad things, and mice, will end together, says the the LORD.

All Nations Will See God's Glory

18 I know their works and their thoughts. It is time to gather all nations and all languages. They will come and see My glory. 19 I will put a sign among them. I will send some who are left to the nations: Tarshish, Pul, Lud (those who pull the bow), Tubal, Javan, and the far islands that have not heard of Me or seen My glory. They will tell My glory among the nations.

20 They will bring all your brothers from all nations as a gift to the the LORD. They will come on horses, in carts, in litters, on mules, and on camels, to My holy hill in Jerusalem, says the the LORD. It will be like when the people of Israel bring a clean gift to the the LORD's house. 21 And I will take some of them to be priests and Levites, says the the LORD.

God's People Will Last Forever

22 For as the new heavens and the new earth that I make will stay before Me, says the the LORD, so your children and your name will stay. 23 From new moon to new moon, and from Sabbath to Sabbath, all people will come to bow down before Me, says the the LORD. 24 They will go out and see the dead bodies of the men who turned against Me. Their worms will not die. Their fire will not go out. They will be hated by all people.
