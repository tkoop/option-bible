David Asks God for Help

1 The LORD my God, I run to You for safety. Save me and keep me safe from all who chase me. 2 If You do not, they may tear me apart like a lion, and no one can save me.

David Says He Did Nothing Wrong

3 The LORD my God, if I did this wrong, if my hands are guilty, 4 if I hurt a friend for no reason or took things from someone who did nothing wrong, 5 then let my enemy chase me and catch me. Let him step on me and push me into the dust.

David Asks God to Judge

6 Get up, the LORD, in Your anger. Rise up against those who hate me. Wake up and help me, for You said You would judge. 7 Let people from all nations gather around You. Rule over them from heaven.

God Judges Fairly

8 The LORD judges all people. Judge me, the LORD, by my right doing and by my honest heart. 9 Stop the bad things bad people do. Help good people stand strong. You test what is in our hearts. 10 God is my shield. He saves people whose hearts are good. 11 God is a fair judge. He is angry with bad people every day.

The Wicked Hurt Themselves

12 If a bad person does not change, God will sharpen His sword. He will get His bow ready. 13 He has made deadly weapons. He sets His arrows on fire. 14 Look, bad people make trouble. They carry sin and bring lies into the world. 15 They dig a hole, but they fall into it themselves. 16 The trouble they make comes back on them. The harm they do falls on their own heads.

David Thanks God

17 I will thank the LORD because He is right. I will sing to the name of the LORD, the Most High.
