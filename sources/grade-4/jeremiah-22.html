The LORD Tells the King to Do Right

1 The LORD said, "Go to the king of Judah's house. Speak this word there. 2 Hear the word of the LORD, king of Judah. You sit on David's throne. You and your servants and your people come through these gates.

3 "Do what is fair and right. Help the person who was robbed. Save him from the one who hurts him. Do not be mean. Do not crush people. Do not kill innocent people. Do not hurt the stranger, the child with no father, or the widow in this place.

4 "If you do these things, then kings will sit on David's throne. They will come through the gates of this house. They will ride in chariots and on horses. The king, his servants, and his people will come too.

5 "But if you do not listen to these words, I swear by Myself, says the LORD, this house will become a ruin."

The LORD Will Judge the King's House

6 The LORD says about the house of the king of Judah, "You are very special to Me, like the best land. But I will make you a desert. Your cities will be empty.

7 "I will send men to destroy you. Each man will have his weapons. They will cut down your best cedar trees. They will throw them into the fire.

8 "Many nations will pass by this city. Each person will say to his friend, 'Why did the LORD do this to this great city?' 9 People will answer, 'Because they left the LORD their God. They bowed down to other gods and served them.'"

Do Not Weep for the King Taken Away

10 Do not weep for the dead. Do not feel sorry for him. Weep hard for the one who is taken away. He will not come back. He will not see his home land again.

11 The LORD says about Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah. He became king after his father Josiah. He left this place. He will not come back here. 12 He will die where they took him. He will not see this land again.

The LORD Warns King Jehoiakim

13 It is bad for the one who builds his house by doing wrong. He builds upper rooms by being unfair. He makes his neighbor work for free. He does not pay him.

14 He says, "I will build a big house for myself. It will have large rooms. I will cut windows in it. I will cover it with cedar wood. I will paint it red."

15 The LORD says, "Do you think you are a king because you use cedar wood? Did your father not eat and drink? He did what was fair and right. Then things went well for him. 16 He helped poor and needy people. Then things went well. Is that not what it means to know Me? says the LORD. 17 But your eyes and your heart look only for money. You want to hurt innocent people. You want to push people down and be mean."

18 So the LORD says about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, "No one will cry for him. No one will say, 'Oh, my brother!' or 'Oh, my sister!' No one will cry, 'Oh, my lord!' or 'Oh, his glory!'

19 "He will be buried like a donkey. He will be dragged away and thrown outside the gates of Jerusalem."

The LORD Speaks About King Coniah

20 "Go up to Lebanon and cry. Lift your voice in Bashan. Cry from Abarim. All your friends are broken.

21 "I spoke to you when you were safe. But you said, 'I will not listen.' You have been like this since you were young. You did not listen to My voice. 22 The wind will blow away your leaders. Your friends will go into captivity. Then you will be ashamed. You will feel bad because of all your wrong.

23 "You live in Lebanon. You live among cedar trees. How you will groan when pain comes. It will be like pain for a woman having a baby.

24 "As I live, says the LORD, even if Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were a ring on My right hand, I would pull you off. 25 I will give you to those who want to kill you. I will give you to those you fear. I will give you to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to the Chaldeans. 26 I will throw you and your mother, who gave birth to you, into another land. You were not born there. You will die there. 27 They will not come back to the land they want so much."

28 Is this man Coniah like a broken pot that no one wants? Why are he and his children thrown into a land they do not know? 29 Earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the LORD. 30 The LORD says, "Write this man down as having no children. He will not do well in his life. None of his children will sit on David's throne or rule in Judah."
