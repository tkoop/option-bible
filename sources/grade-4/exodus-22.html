Rules About Stealing

1 If a man steals an ox or a sheep and kills it or sells it, he must pay back five oxen for one ox, and four sheep for one sheep. 2 If a thief is caught breaking in and is hit so he dies, no one is guilty for his death. 3 But if the sun has come up, he is guilty. The thief must pay back what he stole. If he has nothing, he must be sold to pay for his theft. 4 If the stolen animal is found alive with him, whether it is an ox, a donkey, or a sheep, he must pay back two for one.

Rules About Fields and Fire

5 If a man lets his animals eat in another man’s field or vineyard, or lets them loose so they eat there, he must pay from the best of his own field or vineyard. 6 If a fire starts and burns thorns, and it burns up grain that is stacked or still growing, or the whole field, the person who started the fire must pay for all of it.

Rules About Keeping Things Safe

7 If a man gives his neighbor money or things to keep safe, and they are stolen from his house, the thief, if caught, must pay back two for one. 8 If the thief is not found, the owner of the house must come before God. He must show that he did not take his neighbor’s things. 9 For any wrong about an ox, a donkey, a sheep, clothes, or any lost thing that someone says, “This is mine,” both sides must come before God. The one God says is wrong must pay back two for one to his neighbor.

Rules About Animals Kept Safe or Borrowed

10 If a man gives his neighbor a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any animal to keep safe, and it dies, is hurt, or is taken while no one is watching, 11 the two men must make a promise before the LORD. They must find out if the keeper took his neighbor’s animal. The owner must accept the promise, and the keeper does not have to pay. 12 But if the animal was stolen, the keeper must pay the owner. 13 If a wild animal tore it up, he must bring the torn animal as proof. He does not have to pay for it.

14 If a man borrows an animal from his neighbor and it is hurt or dies while the owner is not there, he must pay for it. 15 If the owner was there, he does not have to pay. If the animal was hired, the pay covers the loss.

Rules About Marriage Payments

16 If a man gets a young woman who is not promised to anyone to sleep with him, he must pay the price for a bride and marry her. 17 If her father refuses to give her to him, he must pay money equal to the price for a bride.

Rules About Bad Acts

18 You must not let a woman who uses magic live. 19 Anyone who sleeps with an animal must be put to death. 20 Anyone who gives an offering to any god but the LORD alone must be destroyed.

Rules About Caring for People

21 You must not be mean to a foreigner or push him around. You were foreigners in the land of Egypt. 22 You must not be mean to any widow or child with no father. 23 If you are mean to them and they cry out to Me, I will surely hear their cry. 24 My anger will burn, and I will kill you with a sword. Then your wives will become widows, and your children will have no father.

Rules About Lending Money

25 If you lend money to one of My people who is poor, you must not act like a money lender to him. You must not charge him interest. 26 If you take your neighbor’s coat as a promise to pay, you must give it back before the sun goes down. 27 That coat is his only cover. It is what he sleeps in. What else can he sleep in? If he cries out to Me, I will hear him, because I am kind.

Rules About Giving to God

28 You must not speak badly about God, and you must not curse a leader of your people. 29 You must not wait to give from your harvest and your wine. You must give Me your first son. 30 Do the same with your oxen and sheep. A new baby animal stays with its mother for seven days. On the eighth day, give it to Me. 31 You must be My holy people, because I am the LORD who makes you holy. You must not eat meat from an animal torn by wild animals in the field. Throw it to the dogs.
