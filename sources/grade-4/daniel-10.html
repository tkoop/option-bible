Daniel Gets a True Message

1 In the third year that Cyrus was king of Persia, a message was shown to Daniel. People called him Belteshazzar. The message was true. It was about a big war. Daniel understood the message and what he saw in the vision. 2 At that time, I, Daniel, was sad for three whole weeks. 3 I did not eat tasty food. I did not eat meat or drink wine. I did not put oil on myself. I did this for three weeks.

4 On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I stood by the great river, the Tigris.

Daniel Sees a Bright Man

5 I looked up and saw a man. He wore linen clothes. A belt of pure gold from Uphaz was around his waist. 6 His body was like a bright gem. His face was bright like lightning. His eyes were like fire. His arms and feet shone like polished brass. His voice sounded like a big crowd.

7 I, Daniel, was the only one who saw the vision. The men with me did not see it. They were very afraid and ran to hide. 8 So I was alone. I saw this great vision. I had no strength left. My face looked weak and sick. I had no power. 9 Then I heard his voice. When I heard it, I fell into a deep sleep with my face on the ground.

10 A hand touched me. It made me shake on my hands and knees.

The Man Talks to Daniel

11 He said, "Daniel, you are greatly loved. Understand what I say to you. Stand up. I was sent to you." When he said this, I stood up shaking.

12 He said, "Do not be afraid, Daniel. From the first day you decided to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard. I came because of your words."

A Fight in Heaven

13 But the prince of Persia stopped me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the top leaders, came to help me. I stayed there with the kings of Persia. 14 Now I came to help you understand what will happen to your people in the last days. The vision is for many days.

15 When he said these words, I looked down and could not speak.

Daniel Feels Weak

16 Then someone who looked like a man touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke to the one standing in front of me. I said, "My lord, this vision has made me very sad. I have no strength left. 17 How can I, your servant, talk with you, my lord? I can hardly breathe."

18 Then someone who looked like a man touched me again and gave me strength.

19 He said, "Peace to you. Do not be afraid. Be strong. Yes, be strong." When he spoke to me, I felt strong again.

I said, "Speak, my lord, because you have made me strong."

20 He said, "Do you know why I came to you? Now I will go back to fight the prince of Persia. After I go, the prince of Greece will come. 21 But first I will tell you what is written in the book of truth. No one helps me with these things except Michael, your prince."
