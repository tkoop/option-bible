Help Each Other

1 Brothers, if someone is caught in a sin, you who follow the Spirit should help that person. Be gentle. Watch yourself so you won't be tempted too. 2 Carry each other's heavy loads. In this way you obey the law of Christ. 3 If someone thinks he is important but he is not, he tricks himself. 4 Each person should check his own work. Then he can be proud of himself. He should not compare himself to others. 5 Each person will carry his own load.

6 The one who learns God's word should share good things with the one who teaches.

Plant Good, Get Good

7 Don't be fooled. God is not fooled. A person gets back what he plants. 8 If he plants for his sinful self, he will get ruin. If he plants for the Spirit, he will get life that lasts forever. 9 Let us not get tired of doing good. At the right time we will get a good crop if we don't give up. 10 So while we have time, let us do good to everyone. Let us do extra good to those who believe.

Paul's Final Words

11 See how big my letters are. I write to you with my own hand. 12 Some people want to look good to others. They push you to be circumcised. They do this so they won't be hurt because of the cross of Christ. 13 Even those who are circumcised don't keep the law. They want you circumcised so they can brag about your body. 14 I don't want to brag about anything but the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. Because of that cross, the world no longer matters to me, and I no longer matter to the world. 15 Being circumcised or not being circumcised doesn't matter. What matters is being made new. 16 Peace and mercy be on all who follow this rule. Peace and mercy be on God's people Israel too. 17 From now on, don't trouble me. I carry the marks of Jesus on my body. 18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
