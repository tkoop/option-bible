Elihu Says God Is Above Us

1 Then Elihu answered and said:

2 "Do you think this is fair? You say, 'I am more right than God.' 3 But you ask, 'What good is it for me? What do I gain if I do not sin?' 4 I will answer you, and your friends too. 5 Look up at the sky and see. Look at the clouds high above you.

6 "If you sin, does it hurt God? If you sin many times, what does it do to Him? 7 If you do right, what do you give Him? What does He take from your hand? 8 Your sin hurts people like you. Your good helps people like you.

9 "People cry out when they are pushed down. They call for help when strong people hurt them. 10 But no one says, 'Where is God, my Maker, who gives songs at night?' 11 He teaches us more than the animals. He makes us wiser than the birds.

12 "There they cry out, but He does not answer. This is because bad men are proud. 13 Surely God does not listen to empty cries. The Almighty does not pay attention to them. 14 How much less will He hear you when you say you cannot see Him! Your case is before Him, and you must wait for Him.

15 "Now, because He does not punish in anger, and He does not pay much mind to foolish talk, 16 Job opens his mouth for nothing. He says many words with no knowledge."
