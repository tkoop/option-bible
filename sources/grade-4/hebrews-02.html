Pay Close Attention to God's Word

1 So we must listen very closely to what we have heard. We must not lose it. 2 The message that angels gave was true. Every sin and every time people did not listen got a fair punishment. 3 How can we escape if we do not care about this great rescue? The Lord first told about it. People who heard it told us it was true. 4 God also showed it was true. He used signs and wonders. He used many kinds of power. He gave gifts from the Holy Spirit, just as He wanted.

Jesus Was Made Lower Than Angels

5 God did not put the coming world under angels. That is the world we are talking about. 6 Someone said, "What is a man? Why do You remember him? What is a son of man? Why do You care for him? 7 You made him a little lower than angels for a short time. You gave him glory and honor. 8 You put everything under his feet." When God put everything under him, nothing was left out. But now we do not yet see everything under his rule.

Jesus Brings Many Sons to Glory

9 But we see Jesus. He was made a little lower than angels for a short time. Now He is crowned with glory and honor because He suffered death. By God's kindness, He died for all people. 10 All things are for Him, and all things come from Him. He is bringing many sons to glory. So it was right for Him to make Jesus, the leader of their rescue, complete through suffering. 11 Jesus makes people holy. He and the people He makes holy come from one family. So He is not ashamed to call them His brothers. 12 He says, "I will tell Your name to My brothers. I will sing praise to You among the people." 13 He also says, "I will trust in Him." And again, "Here I am, with the children God gave Me."

Jesus Shared Our Life to Save Us

14 The children have bodies and blood. So Jesus became like them. He did this so that by dying He could destroy the devil, who had the power of death. 15 He did this to free all people who were slaves their whole lives because they feared death. 16 He does not help angels. He helps the family of Abraham. 17 So He had to be like His brothers in every way. Then He could be a kind and faithful high priest for God. He could pay for the sins of the people. 18 He suffered when He was tested. So He can help people who are tested.
