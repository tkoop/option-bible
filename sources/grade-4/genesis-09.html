God Blesses Noah

1 God blessed Noah and his sons. He said, "Have many children. Fill the whole earth. 2 All animals will be afraid of you. Every bird, every small animal, and all fish in the sea will fear you. I have put them in your hands. 3 You may eat any living thing that moves. I give you all of them, just like I gave you the green plants. 4 But do not eat meat that still has its lifeblood in it. 5 I will hold you responsible for your lives. I will demand an answer from every animal. I will also demand an answer from every person. If one person kills another, I will demand an answer. 6 If a person kills someone, people must put him to death. God made people like Himself. 7 Now you must have many children. Spread out over the earth and have many more."

God's Promise to Noah

8 God said to Noah and his sons, "I am making My promise with you and your children after you. 9 I am also making it with every living thing that is with you: the birds, the farm animals, and all the wild animals. Every animal that came out of the boat is included. 10 I promise that never again will all life be wiped out by a flood. Never again will a flood destroy the earth. 11 I promise that never again will all life be wiped out by a flood. Never again will a flood destroy the earth.

12 "This is the sign of My promise between Me and you and every living thing with you, for all time. 13 I have put My rainbow in the clouds. It will be the sign of My promise between Me and the earth. 14 When I bring clouds over the earth and the rainbow shows in the clouds, 15 I will remember My promise between Me and you and all living things. The waters will never again become a flood to destroy all life. 16 Whenever the rainbow is in the clouds, I will see it. I will remember the promise that lasts forever between God and all living things on the earth. 17 This is the sign of the promise I have made between Me and all life on the earth."

Noah's Sons

18 Noah's sons who came out of the boat were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham became the father of Canaan. 19 These three sons became the fathers of all people on earth.

Noah Plants a Garden

20 Noah was a farmer. He planted a grape garden. 21 He drank some of the wine. He got drunk. He lay without clothes in his tent. 22 Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father without clothes. He told his two brothers outside. 23 Shem and Japheth took a coat. They put it on their shoulders. They walked backward. They covered their father. They did not look at him. 24 Noah woke up. He knew what his youngest son had done. 25 He said, "May Canaan be cursed. He will be the lowest servant to his brothers. 26 Praise the the LORD, the God of Shem. May Canaan be his servant. 27 May God make Japheth great. May he live in the tents of Shem. May Canaan be his servant."

28 After the flood, Noah lived 350 more years. 29 Noah lived 950 years in all. Then he died.
