A Song of David to Remember

1 The LORD, do not punish me in Your anger. Do not correct me when You are very angry. 2 Your arrows hit me deep. Your hand presses down on me.

3 My body is not well because of Your anger. My bones hurt because of my sin. 4 My sins are too many to count. They are a heavy load I cannot carry. 5 My sores smell bad and are rotting because of my foolish choices.

6 I am bent over and brought low. I am sad all day long. 7 My back burns with pain. My body is not well. 8 I feel numb and crushed. I groan because my heart is hurting.

David Talks to God

9 Lord, You know all I want. You hear my groans. 10 My heart beats fast. My strength is gone. Even the light in my eyes is fading.

Friends and Family Stay Away

11 My friends stand far away because I am sick. My family stands far away too. 12 People who want to kill me set traps. People who want to hurt me say mean things. They lie all day long.

David Stays Quiet

13 But I am like a deaf man. I do not hear. I am like a man who cannot speak. I do not open my mouth. 14 I am like a man who cannot hear. My mouth has no answer.

David Trusts God

15 I put my hope in You, the LORD. You will answer me, Lord my God. 16 I said, "Do not let them be happy over me. They make fun of me when I fall." 17 I am ready to fall. My pain is always with me. 18 Yes, I admit my sin. I am worried because of my sin.

David's Enemies

19 Many enemies are strong. Many people hate me for no reason. 20 They do evil back when I do good. They speak against me because I try to do good.

David Asks for Help

21 Do not leave me, the LORD. Do not stay far from me, my God. 22 Hurry and help me, Lord. You are my Savior.
