Jacob's Last Words to His Sons

1 Jacob called his sons and said, "Come here, and I will tell you what will happen to you later. 2 Come together and listen, sons of Jacob. Listen to Israel, your father.

"3 Reuben, you are my first son. You are my strength and the first sign of my power. You were to be the best in honor and power. 4 But you are as unsteady as water, so you will not lead. You slept with my wife and shamed me.

"5 Simeon and Levi are brothers. Their swords are tools of hurt. 6 I will not join their plans. I will not let my honor mix with their group. They killed people in anger, and they hurt oxen for no good reason. 7 Their anger is fierce, and their hate is cruel. I will split them up among Jacob's family and scatter them in Israel.

"8 Judah, your brothers will praise you. You will hold your enemies by the neck. Your father's sons will bow down to you. 9 Judah is like a young lion. He eats and then rests. He lies down like a lion, and no one dares wake him. 10 The ruler's stick will not leave Judah. The leader's staff will stay with him until the promised king comes. Then people will obey him. 11 He ties his young donkey to the grapevine and his baby donkey to the best vine. He washes his clothes in wine and his coat in grape juice. 12 His eyes are red from wine, and his teeth are white from milk.

"13 Zebulun will live by the sea. He will be a safe place for ships, and his land will reach to Sidon.

"14 Issachar is like a strong donkey lying down between its packs. 15 He saw that rest was good and that the land was nice. So he bent his shoulder to carry heavy loads and became a servant.

"16 Dan will judge his people like one of Israel's tribes. 17 Dan will be like a snake on the road and a viper on the path. It bites the horse's heels, and the rider falls back. 18 I wait for Your help, the LORD.

"19 Raiders will attack Gad, but he will attack them back.

"20 Asher's food will be rich, and he will give good food to kings.

"21 Naphtali is like a free deer that speaks beautiful words.

"22 Joseph is like a fruitful branch by a spring. His branches grow over the wall. 23 His enemies shot at him and hurt him badly. 24 But his bow stayed strong. His arms were made strong by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, by the Shepherd, the Rock of Israel. 25 This help comes from the God of your father, who will help you. It comes from the Almighty, who will bless you with blessings from heaven above, from the deep below, and with children and family. 26 Your father's blessings are stronger than the blessings of my parents. They reach the highest hills. May they rest on Joseph's head and on the crown of the one set apart among his brothers.

"27 Benjamin is like a hungry wolf. In the morning he eats the food, and in the evening he shares the prize."

28 These are the twelve tribes of Israel. This is what their father said to them when he blessed them. He blessed each one with the right blessing.

Jacob Tells Where to Bury Him

29 Then he told them, "I am about to join my people. Bury me with my fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite. 30 Bury me in the cave in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre in Canaan. Abraham bought that field from Ephron the Hittite to use as a burial place. 31 Abraham and his wife Sarah are buried there. Isaac and his wife Rebekah are buried there. I buried Leah there. 32 The field and the cave in it were bought from the sons of Heth."

33 When Jacob finished telling his sons what to do, he pulled his feet onto the bed, took his last breath, and joined his people.
