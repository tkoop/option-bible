People Ask About Fasting

1 In the fourth year that King Darius ruled, the LORD gave a message to Zechariah. It was on the fourth day of the ninth month, called Kislev. 2 People from the town of Bethel sent Sharezer, Regem-Melech, and their men. They wanted to ask the LORD to be kind to them. 3 They asked the priests in the LORD's house and the prophets, “Should I be sad and not eat in the fifth month, like I have done for many years?”

God Asks About Their Fasting

4 Then the LORD of Hosts gave me this message: 5 “Ask all the people and the priests: When you did not eat and were sad in the fifth and seventh months for seventy years, was it really for Me that you did it? 6 When you ate and drank, were you not doing it for yourselves?”

7 Are these not the words the LORD said through the old prophets? Back then, Jerusalem and the towns around it had people living in them. They were safe. The south country and the low hills also had people.

God Tells Them to Be Fair and Kind

8 The LORD gave another message to Zechariah. He said, 9 “This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Be fair when you judge. Show love and kindness to each other. 10 Do not hurt widows or children with no parents. Do not hurt strangers or poor people. Do not think bad things about each other in your hearts.”

The People Would Not Listen

11 But they would not listen. They turned away and would not obey. They stopped their ears so they could not hear. 12 They made their hearts as hard as rock. They would not listen to God's law or to the words the LORD of Hosts sent by His Spirit through the old prophets. So the LORD of Hosts was very angry.

13 The LORD of Hosts says, “When I called, they did not listen. So when they call, I will not listen.” 14 “I sent them to live among nations they did not know. The land became empty after them. No one could travel through it or come back. They turned the good land into a waste.”
