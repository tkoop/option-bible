Athaliah Takes Over

1 Athaliah was the mother of King Ahaziah. She saw that her son had died. Then she got up and killed all the king’s family.

Joash Is Saved

2 But Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash, Ahaziah’s son. She stole him from the king’s sons who were being killed. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom. They hid him from Athaliah, and he was not killed. 3 Joash was hidden with Jehosheba in the the LORD’s house for six years. Athaliah ruled the land.

Jehoiada Makes a Plan

4 In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent for the captains of the hundreds. They were Carites and guards. He brought them to him at the the LORD’s house. There he made an agreement with them. He made them promise. Then he showed them the king’s son.

5 He told them, “Here is what you must do. One third of you who come on the Sabbath will guard the king. 6 One third will be at the Sur Gate. One third will be at the gate behind the guards. You will guard the house and keep it safe. 7 The rest of you will guard the the LORD’s house around the king. 8 Stay around the king. Each man must have his weapon in his hand. Anyone who comes near your group must be killed. Stay with the king when he goes out and when he comes in.”

9 The captains did all that Jehoiada the priest told them. Each man took his own men. Some were coming on the Sabbath, and some were leaving the Sabbath. They came to Jehoiada the priest. 10 The priest gave the captains the spears and shields that had belonged to King David. They were in the the LORD’s house. 11 The guards stood with weapons in hand around the king. They stood from the right side of the house to the left side, by the altar and the house.

Joash Is Crowned

12 Jehoiada brought out the king’s son. He put the crown on him. He gave him the law. They made him king and anointed him. They clapped their hands and said, “Long live the king!”

Athaliah Sees the New King

13 Athaliah heard the noise of the guards and the people. She went to the people in the the LORD’s house. 14 She saw the king standing by the pillar, as was the custom. The captains and trumpeters were by the king. All the people were happy and blew trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, “Treason! Treason!”

15 Jehoiada the priest gave an order to the captains who were in charge of the army. He said, “Take her out past the soldiers. Kill anyone who follows her. Do not kill her in the the LORD’s house.” 16 They grabbed her. She went by the way the horses go into the king’s house. There they killed her.

The People Follow the LORD

17 Jehoiada made an agreement between the the LORD and the king and the people. They would be the the LORD’s people. He also made an agreement between the king and the people. 18 All the people went to the house of Baal. They tore it down. They broke his altars and idols into pieces. They killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. Then the priest set guards over the the LORD’s house.

Joash Sits on the Throne

19 Jehoiada took the captains, the Carites, the guards, and all the people. They brought the king down from the the LORD’s house. They went by the guards’ gate to the king’s house. Joash sat on the king’s throne. 20 All the people were happy. The city was quiet. They killed Athaliah with a sword at the king’s house. 21 Joash was seven years old when he became king.
