Gideon and His Army

1 Early in the morning, Gideon (also called Jerubbaal) and all his men got up. They camped by the spring of Harod. The Midian camp was north of them in the valley, near Moreh hill.

Gideon's Army Gets Smaller

2 The LORD said to Gideon, "There are too many men with you. I will not give Midian into their hands, or Israel may brag and say, 'I saved myself.' 3 So call out to the men, 'Anyone who is afraid may go home from Mount Gilead.'" Then 22,000 men went home, and 10,000 stayed.

4 The LORD said to Gideon, "There are still too many men. Take them to the water. I will test them for you there. The men I say can go with you will go. The men I say cannot go will not go." 5 So Gideon took the men to the water. The LORD said, "Separate the men who lap water with their tongues, like a dog. Also separate the men who kneel down to drink."

6 300 men lapped water with their hands to their mouths. The rest knelt down to drink.

7 The LORD said to Gideon, "I will save you with the 300 men who lapped. I will give Midian into your hand. Let everyone else go home." 8 So the men took food and trumpets. Gideon sent everyone else home. The 300 men stayed. The Midian camp was below them in the valley.

Gideon Spies on the Enemy Camp

9 That night the LORD said to Gideon, "Get up and go down to the camp. I have given it into your hand. 10 But if you are afraid to go down, take your helper Purah with you. 11 Listen to what they say. Then you will feel strong and brave, and you can go down to the camp." So Gideon and Purah went to the edge of the enemy camp.

12 Midian, Amalek, and the people from the east were spread out in the valley. There were so many of them, like locusts. Their camels were too many to count, like sand by the sea.

13 Gideon came close. A man was telling his friend a dream. He said, "I had a dream. A loaf of barley bread rolled into the Midian camp. It hit a tent. The tent fell down, turned upside down, and fell flat."

14 His friend answered, "This can only be the sword of Gideon son of Joash, an Israelite. God has given Midian and the whole camp into his hand."

15 When Gideon heard the dream and what it meant, he bowed down to worship. Then he went back to the Israel camp and said, "Get up! The LORD has given the Midian camp into your hand."

The Battle Plan

16 He divided the 300 men into three groups. He gave each man a trumpet and an empty jar. Inside each jar was a torch.

17 Gideon said to them, "Watch me and do what I do. When I get to the edge of the camp, do just as I do. 18 I and everyone with me will blow our trumpets. Then you blow your trumpets all around the camp. Shout, 'For the LORD and for Gideon!'"

The Battle

19 Gideon and 100 men with him came to the edge of the camp late at night, just as the guards were changing. They blew their trumpets and broke the jars in their hands.

20 All three groups blew their trumpets and broke their jars. They held torches in their left hands and trumpets in their right hands. They shouted, "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!"

21 Each man stood in his place around the camp. The whole enemy camp ran away, shouting and crying out.

22 The 300 men kept blowing their trumpets. The LORD made the enemy soldiers turn their swords on each other all through the camp. The army ran away to Beth Shittah, toward Zererah, and to the border of Abel Meholah by Tabbath.

Israel Chases Midian

23 Men from Israel, Naphtali, Asher, and Manasseh were called out. They chased the Midianites.

24 Gideon sent messengers all through the hill country of Ephraim. He said, "Come down and fight Midian. Take control of the Jordan River all the way to Beth Barah." So the men of Ephraim were called out. They took control of the Jordan River all the way to Beth Barah. 25 They caught two Midian leaders, Oreb and Zeeb. They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb, and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb. They kept chasing Midian. They brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon on the other side of the Jordan.
