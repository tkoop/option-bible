Jesus Talks to the Crowd

1 Then Jesus talked to the crowds and to His disciples. 2 He said, "The teachers and Pharisees sit in Moses' seat. 3 So do what they tell you to do, and obey their words. But do not live the way they live, because they say things and do not do them. 4 They tie heavy loads that are hard to carry and put them on people's shoulders. But they will not even lift a finger to help. 5 They do everything so people will see them. They make their prayer boxes big and their robe tassels long. 6 They like the best seats at dinners and the best seats in meeting places. 7 They like greetings in the market and being called 'Teacher' by people.

8 "But you must not be called 'Teacher,' because you have one Teacher, and you are all brothers. 9 Do not call anyone on earth 'Father,' because you have one Father in heaven. 10 Do not be called leaders, because you have one Leader, the Christ. 11 The greatest among you will be your servant. 12 Anyone who lifts himself up will be brought low, and anyone who brings himself low will be lifted up.

Jesus Speaks Woes to the Pharisees

13 "How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You shut the door to heaven's kingdom in front of people. You do not go in yourselves, and you stop those who are trying to go in. 15 How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You travel over land and sea to make one new follower. Then, when he follows, you make him twice as much a child of hell as yourselves.

16 "How terrible for you, blind guides! You say that if anyone swears by the temple, it means nothing, but if anyone swears by the gold in the temple, he must keep his promise. 17 You blind fools! Which is greater, the gold or the temple that makes the gold holy? 18 You also say that if anyone swears by the altar, it means nothing, but if anyone swears by the gift on it, he must keep his promise. 19 You blind! Which is greater, the gift or the altar that makes the gift holy? 20 So anyone who swears by the altar swears by it and by everything on it. 21 Anyone who swears by the temple swears by it and by the One who lives in it. 22 Anyone who swears by heaven swears by God's throne and by the One sitting on it.

23 "How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You give a tenth of your mint, dill, and cumin. But you forget the bigger parts of God's law: being fair, showing mercy, and being faithful. You should do these things and not forget the small ones. 24 You blind guides! You pay attention to tiny things but ignore big things. 25 How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You wash the outside of the cup and dish, but inside they are full of greed and not controlling yourselves. 26 You blind Pharisee! First wash the inside of the cup and dish, so the outside can be clean too. 27 How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You are like graves painted white. They look nice outside, but inside they are full of dead bones and everything unclean. 28 In the same way, you look good to people outside, but inside you are full of pretending and doing wrong.

29 "How terrible for you, teachers and Pharisees, you pretenders! You build tombs for the prophets and decorate the graves of good people. 30 And you say that if you had lived back then, you would not have helped kill the prophets. 31 So you show that you are children of the people who killed the prophets. 32 Go ahead and finish the bad things your fathers started! 33 You snakes, you baby snakes! How will you escape being punished in hell? 34 So I am sending you prophets, wise people, and teachers. Some of them you will kill and put on a cross. Some of them you will beat in your meeting places and chase from town to town. 35 This is so all the blood of good people spilled on earth will come on you, from the blood of good Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the temple and the altar. 36 I tell you the truth, all these things will happen to people living now.

Jesus Cries Over Jerusalem

37 "Jerusalem, Jerusalem! You kill the prophets and stone the people sent to you. How often I wanted to gather your children like a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you would not let Me. 38 Look, your house is left empty. 39 I tell you, you will not see Me again until you say that the One who comes in the name of the Lord is blessed."
