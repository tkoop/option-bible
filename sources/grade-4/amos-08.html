Amos Sees a Basket of Summer Fruit

1 The Lord the LORD showed me a basket of summer fruit. 2 He said, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the Lord the LORD said to me, "The end has come for My people Israel. I will not spare them anymore."

3 "In that day," says the Lord the LORD, "the songs in the temple will turn to sad cries. Dead bodies will be everywhere. Be quiet!"

God Warns the Greedy

4 Listen to this, you who walk on the poor and wipe out the needy in the land. 5 You say, "When will the new moon be over so we can sell grain? When will the Sabbath be over so we can sell wheat? We will make the measure small and the price big. We will cheat with false scales." 6 We will buy poor people with silver and needy people for a pair of shoes. We will even sell the sweepings with the wheat.

7 The LORD has made a promise by the pride of Jacob: "I will never forget what they have done." 8 "Will the land not shake because of this? Will not everyone who lives in it cry? The whole land will rise like the Nile. It will be stirred up and then go down again, like the Nile in Egypt."

A Dark Day Is Coming

9 "In that day," says the Lord the LORD, "I will make the sun go down at noon. I will make the earth dark in the middle of the day. 10 I will turn your celebrations into sad times. I will turn all your songs into crying. I will make everyone wear rough cloth and shave their heads. I will make it like crying for an only child. The end will be a bitter day."

A Hunger for God's Word

11 "The days are coming," says the Lord the LORD, "when I will send hunger to the land. It will not be hunger for bread. It will not be thirst for water. It will be hunger to hear the words of the LORD. 12 People will wander from sea to sea. They will go from north to east. They will look for the word of the LORD, but they will not find it."

The End of the Wicked

13 "In that day, the pretty young women and strong young men will faint from thirst. 14 Those who make promises by the sin of Samaria will say, 'As surely as your god lives, Dan,' or, 'As surely as the way to Beersheba lives.' They will fall and never get up again."
