Jesus Warns About Making Others Sin

1 Then Jesus said to His disciples, "Things that make people sin will happen. But it will be very bad for the person who makes them happen. 2 It would be better for him to have a big stone tied to his neck and be thrown into the sea than to make one of these little ones sin."

Forgive Others

3 "Watch out for yourselves. If your brother sins, tell him he is wrong. If he says sorry, forgive him. 4 Even if he sins against you seven times in one day, and seven times comes back to you and says, 'I am sorry,' you must forgive him."

More Faith

5 The apostles said to the Lord, "Give us more faith!"

6 The Lord replied, "If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, 'Be pulled up and planted in the sea,' and it would listen to you."

Serving Without Pride

7 "Which of you has a servant who comes in from plowing or watching sheep in the field? Would you tell him, 'Come and sit down to eat right now'?" 8 "No. He will tell him, 'Get my dinner ready. Put on your work clothes and serve me while I eat and drink. After that, you can eat and drink.'" 9 "Does he thank the servant for doing what he was told?" 10 "So you also, when you have done everything you were told to do, should say, 'We are not good servants. We only did our job.'"

Ten Sick Men

11 While Jesus was going to Jerusalem, He passed between Samaria and Galilee. 12 As He entered a village, ten men with a bad skin disease met Him. They stood far away. 13 They called out, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"

14 When He saw them, He said, "Go, show yourselves to the priests." As they went, they were healed.

15 One of them, when he saw he was healed, came back. He praised God with a loud voice. 16 He fell on his face at Jesus' feet and thanked Him. He was a Samaritan.

One Man Comes Back

17 Jesus asked, "Were not all ten healed? Where are the other nine? 18 Was no one found to come back and give glory to God except this foreigner?" 19 He said to the man, "Get up and go. your faith has made you well."

The Kingdom of God

20 The Pharisees asked Him when the kingdom of God would come. He replied, "The kingdom of God will not come in a way you can see. 21 People will not say, 'Look, here it is!' or 'There it is!' For the kingdom of God is among you."

The Day the Son of Man Comes

22 Then He said to His disciples, "Days are coming when you will want to see one of the days of the Son of Man, but you will not see it. 23 People will say to you, 'Look, there He is!' or 'Look, here He is!' Do not go out or run after them. 24 For as lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one side to the other, so will the Son of Man be in His day. 25 But first He must suffer many things and be turned away by the people of this time.

26 "Just as it was in the days of Noah, so it will be in the days of the Son of Man. 27 People were eating and drinking, getting married, up to the day Noah went into the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all. 28 It was the same in the days of Lot. People were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building. 29 But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and burning sulfur rained down from heaven and destroyed them all. 30 It will be the same on the day the Son of Man is shown.

31 "On that day, no one on the roof should go down to get things from inside the house. No one in the field should go back. 32 Remember Lot's wife! 33 Whoever tries to keep his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life will keep it. 34 I tell you, on that night two men will be in one bed. One will be taken, and the other will be left. 35 Two women will be grinding grain together. One will be taken, and the other will be left."

Where Will This Happen?

37 They asked, "Where, Lord?"

He replied, "Where the body is, there the birds of prey will gather."
