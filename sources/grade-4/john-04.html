Jesus Goes to Galilee

1 The Pharisees heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more followers than John. 2 But Jesus did not baptize people Himself. His followers did. 3 So He left Judea and went back to Galilee. 4 He had to go through Samaria.

Jesus Meets a Woman at the Well

5 He came to a town in Samaria called Sychar. It was near the field Jacob gave to his son Joseph. 6 Jacob's well was there. Jesus was tired from the trip. He sat by the well. It was about noon. 7 A woman from Samaria came to get water. Jesus said to her, "Give Me a drink." 8 His followers had gone to town to buy food.

9 The Samaritan woman said to Him, "Why do You ask me for a drink? I am a Samaritan woman. Jews do not share things with Samaritans."

10 Jesus answered her, "If you knew God's gift and who is asking you for a drink, you would ask Him. He would give you living water."

11 "Sir," the woman said, "You have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. Where do You get this living water? 12 Are You greater than our father Jacob? He gave us this well. He drank from it. His sons and his animals drank from it too."

13 Jesus said, "Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again. 14 But whoever drinks the water I give will never be thirsty. The water I give will become a spring in him. It will give life that never ends."

15 The woman said, "Sir, give me this water. Then I will not be thirsty. I will not have to come here to draw water."

16 Jesus said, "Go call your husband and come back."

17 She answered, "I have no husband."

Jesus said, "You are right to say, 'I have no husband.' 18 You have had five husbands. The man you have now is not your husband. You spoke the truth."

19 The woman said, "Sir, I see that You are a prophet. 20 Our fathers worshiped on this mountain. But you Jews say the place to worship is in Jerusalem."

21 Jesus said, "Believe Me, woman. The time is coming when you will not worship the Father on this mountain or in Jerusalem. 22 You worship what you do not know. We worship what we know. The way to be saved comes from the Jews. 23 But the time is coming, and it is now here, when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth. The Father wants people like that to worship Him. 24 God is Spirit. His worshipers must worship Him in spirit and truth."

25 The woman said, "I know that the Messiah is coming. He is called the Christ. When He comes, He will tell us everything."

26 Jesus said, "I am He. I am talking to you."

27 Just then His followers came back. They were surprised that He was talking to a woman. But no one asked, "What do You want?" or "Why are You talking to her?"

The Woman Tells Her Town

28 The woman left her water jar. She went into town and said to the people, 29 "Come see a man who told me everything I did. Could this be the Christ?" 30 Many people came out of town and went to Him.

Jesus Talks About the Harvest

31 Meanwhile His followers said, "Rabbi, eat something."

32 Jesus said, "I have food to eat that you do not know about."

33 The followers asked each other, "Did someone bring Him food?"

34 Jesus said, "My food is to do what God sent Me to do. I want to finish His work. 35 You say, 'There are four more months until harvest.' But I tell you, look up. Look at the fields. They are already ready for harvest. 36 The worker who harvests gets paid. He gathers fruit for life that never ends. Then the one who plants and the one who harvests can be glad together. 37 This saying is true: 'One person plants, and another person harvests.' 38 I sent you to harvest what you did not work for. Others worked hard, and you get to share in their work."

Many Samaritans Believe

39 Many Samaritans in that town believed in Him. They believed because of what the woman said. She said, "He told me everything I did."

40 The Samaritans asked Him to stay with them. He stayed there two days. 41 Many more people believed because of His words.

42 They said to the woman, "We believe now. We do not believe just because of what you said. We have heard Him for ourselves. We know that He is truly the Savior of the world."

Jesus Returns to Galilee

43 After two days, He left for Galilee. 44 Jesus had said, "A prophet is not honored in his own hometown." 45 When He got to Galilee, the people there welcomed Him. They had seen all He did in Jerusalem at the feast. They had gone to the feast too.

Jesus Heals a Sick Boy

46 He went to Cana in Galilee again. That was where He had turned water into wine. A royal official was there. His son was sick in Capernaum. 47 The official heard that Jesus had come from Judea to Galilee. He went to Jesus and asked Him to come down and heal his son. The boy was about to die.

48 Jesus said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you will not believe."

49 The official said, "Sir, come down before my child dies."

50 Jesus said, "Go. Your son lives." The man believed what Jesus said and went.

51 While he was going down, his servants met him. They said his son was alive.

52 He asked what time the boy got better. They said, "The fever left him yesterday at one o'clock."

53 The father knew that was the same hour Jesus said, "Your son lives." He and his whole family believed. 54 This was the second sign Jesus did after coming from Judea to Galilee.
