Oil for the Lamp

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2 "Command the Israelites to bring you clean oil from crushed olives for the lamp. This oil will keep the lamps burning all the time.

3 "Aaron must place it outside the curtain in the meeting tent. He must keep it burning before the LORD from evening to morning. This rule lasts for all time for your families.

4 "Aaron must keep the lamps burning on the clean lampstand before the LORD all the time."

Bread for the Holy Table

5 "Take fine flour and bake twelve loaves of bread. Use two-tenths of a basket of flour for each loaf.

6 "Put the loaves in two rows. Put six loaves in each row on the clean table before the LORD.

7 "Put pure incense on the row of loaves. It will be a reminder gift for the bread. It is a gift burned for the LORD.

8 "Every Sabbath day, Aaron must set the bread before the LORD. This is a lasting promise from the Israelites.

9 "The bread belongs to Aaron and his sons. They must eat it in a special place, because it is very holy. It comes from the gifts burned for the LORD. This rule lasts forever."

A Man Who Cursed God's Name

10 A man whose mother was an Israelite and whose father was an Egyptian went out among the Israelites. He and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

11 The son of the Israelite woman spoke against God's name and cursed. The people brought him to Moses. His mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, from the tribe of Dan.

12 They put him in jail until the LORD told them what to do.

God Answers About the Curser

13 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 14 "Take the man who cursed outside the camp. Everyone who heard him must put their hands on his head. Then all the people must throw stones at him.

15 "Speak to the Israelites. Tell them: If anyone curses his God, he will be guilty.

16 "Anyone who speaks against the LORD's name must be put to death. All the people must throw stones at him. This is true for a foreigner or for someone born in the land. If he speaks against the LORD's name, he must die.

17 "If anyone kills a person, he must be put to death.

18 "If anyone kills an animal, he must pay for it. One life for another life.

19 "If a man hurts his neighbor, he must be hurt the same way.

20 "A broken bone for a broken bone, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth. If he hurts a person, he must be hurt the same way.

21 "Whoever kills an animal must pay for it. Whoever kills a person must be put to death.

22 "You must have the same law for the foreigner and for the person born among you. I am the LORD your God."

23 Moses told the Israelites. They took the man who cursed outside the camp and threw stones at him. The Israelites did what the LORD had commanded Moses.
