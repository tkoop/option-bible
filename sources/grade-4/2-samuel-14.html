Joab Sends a Wise Woman to the King

1 Joab son of Zeruiah knew the king missed Absalom very much. 2 Joab sent to Tekoa and brought a wise woman from there. He said to her, "Please act sad. Wear sad clothes. Do not put oil on yourself. Be like a woman who has been sad about someone who died for a long time."

3 "Go to the king and say these words to him." Then Joab told her exactly what to say.

The Woman Asks the King for Help

4 The woman from Tekoa went to the king. She fell face down on the ground and bowed. She said, "Help me, please, King!"

5 The king said to her, "What is wrong?"

She said, "I am a widow. My husband has died. 6 I had two sons. They fought in a field. No one was there to stop them. One hit the other and killed him. 7 Now all my family has turned against me. They say, 'Give us the one who killed his brother, so we can kill him for the brother he killed. Then we will destroy the one who gets the family land.' They want to take away my last hope. Then my husband will have no name left on earth."

The King Promises to Protect Her

8 The king said to the woman, "Go home. I will take care of this for you."

9 The woman from Tekoa said to the king, "My lord the king, the blame is on me and my family. But the king and his throne are not guilty."

10 The king said, "If anyone bothers you, bring him to me. He will not hurt you again."

11 She said, "Please, let the king remember the LORD your God. Then the one who wants revenge will not kill my son."

He said, "As sure as The LORD lives, not one hair of your son's head will fall to the ground."

The Woman Speaks to the King

12 Then the woman said, "Please let me, your servant, say one more thing to my lord the king."

He said, "Say it."

13 The woman said, "Why did you plan something like this against God's people? By saying this, the king is like someone who is guilty. He does not bring back the one he sent away. 14 We all must die someday. We are like water spilled on the ground that cannot be picked up again. God does not take life away. He makes a way so the one who was sent away can come back to Him. 15 Now I came to say this to my lord the king, because the people made me afraid. I thought, 'I will speak to the king. Maybe the king will do what I ask.' 16 The king will listen. He will save me from the man who wants to destroy me and my son. He wants to take away our family's land that God gave us. 17 I thought, 'The word of my lord the king will bring me peace. My lord the king is like an angel of God. he knows what is good and what is bad. May the LORD your God be with you.'"

The King Learns Joab's Plan

18 The king said to the woman, "Please do not hide anything from me when I ask you."

She said, "Let my lord the king speak."

19 The king said, "Is Joab behind all this with you?"

She answered, "As sure as you live, my lord the king, no one can change even a little bit of what my lord the king says. 20 Your servant Joab did this to fix this problem. My lord is wise, like an angel of God. he knows everything that happens on earth."

Absalom Comes Home

21 Then the king said to Joab, "I have done this. Go bring back the young man Absalom."

22 Joab fell face down on the ground and bowed. He blessed the king. Joab said, "Today your servant knows he has found favor in your eyes, my lord the king. The king has done what his servant asked."

23 So Joab went to Geshur and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.

24 The king said, "Let him go to his own house. He must not see my face." So Absalom went to his own house and did not see the king's face.

Absalom's Good Looks

25 In all Israel, no one was as handsome as Absalom. From the bottom of his foot to the top of his head, he had no flaw. 26 When he cut his hair, he cut it at the end of every year because it was heavy on him. He weighed the hair of his head. It was two hundred shekels by the king's scale. 27 Absalom had three sons and one daughter. His daughter's name was Tamar. She was a beautiful woman.

Absalom Wants to See the King

28 Absalom lived in Jerusalem two full years. He did not see the king's face.

29 Then Absalom sent for Joab to send him to the king. But Joab would not come. Absalom sent again a second time. But Joab would not come.

30 So Absalom said to his servants, "Look, Joab's field is next to mine. He has barley there. Go set it on fire." Absalom's servants set the field on fire.

31 Then Joab got up and came to Absalom's house. He said to him, "Why did your servants set my field on fire?"

32 Absalom said to Joab, "Look, I sent for you. I said, 'Come here. I want to send you to the king. I want to ask, "Why did I come from Geshur? It would have been better for me to stay there." Now let me see the king's face. If I did wrong, let him kill me.'"

33 So Joab came to the king and told him. When the king called for Absalom, he came. He bowed face down to the ground before the king. The king kissed Absalom.
