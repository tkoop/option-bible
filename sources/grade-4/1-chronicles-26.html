Gatekeepers and Their Families

1 These were the groups of gate guards. The Korahites had Meshelemiah. He was the son of Kore. He came from Asaph's family. 2 Meshelemiah's sons were Zechariah, the firstborn, Jediael, Zebadiah, Jathniel, 3 Elam, Johanan, and Elioenai. 4 Obed-edom's sons were Shemaiah, the firstborn, Jehozabad, Joah, Sacar, Nethanel, 5 Ammiel, Issachar, and Peulthai. God had blessed Obed-edom. 6 Shemaiah had sons who led their family. They were strong men. 7 Shemaiah's sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His brothers were Elihu and Semachiah. 8 All these men came from Obed-edom. They and their sons and brothers were strong men. They could work hard. There were sixty-two from Obed-edom. 9 Meshelemiah had sons and brothers. They were strong men. There were eighteen of them.

10 Hosah was from Merari's family. His sons were Shimri, the chief. Shimri was not the firstborn. But his father made him chief. 11 Hilkiah was second, Tebaliah was third, and Zechariah was fourth. Hosah had thirteen sons and brothers. 12 These groups of gate guards had jobs. The leaders of the men served in the house of the LORD. They served like their brothers.

Gate Assignments

13 They drew lots for every gate. Both small and large families took part. 14 The east gate lot fell to Shelemiah. They drew lots for Zechariah, his son. He was a wise helper. His lot fell to the north. 15 Obed-edom got the south gate. His sons got the storehouse. 16 Shuppim and Hosah got the west gate. They were at the Shalleketh gate on the road. One group watched across from the other.

17 Each day, six Levites were at the east gate. Four were at the north gate. Four were at the south gate. Two at a time were at the storehouse. 18 At Parbar on the west, four were on the road. Two were at Parbar. 19 These were the groups of gate guards for the Korahites and Merarites.

Temple Treasurers

20 The Levites were in charge of the treasures of God's house. They were also in charge of the treasures of the holy things. 21 Ladan came from Gershon's family. The family leaders from Ladan were Jehieli. 22 Jehieli's sons were Zetham and Joel, his brother. They were in charge of the treasures of the LORD's house. 23 The Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites also served. 24 Shebuel was the son of Gershom. Gershom was the son of Moses. Shebuel was the leader over the treasures. 25 Eliezer's family line was Rehabiah, Jeshaiah, Joram, Zichri, and Shelomoth.

26 This Shelomoth and his relatives were in charge of all the treasures of the holy things. King David and the family leaders had given these things. The leaders were army leaders over thousands and hundreds. 27 They gave things taken in war to make the house of the LORD strong. 28 Samuel the prophet, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah also gave things. All these gifts were under the care of Shelomith and his relatives.

Officials and Judges

29 Chenaniah and his sons from the Izharites worked outside. They were officers and judges over Israel. 30 Hashabiah and his relatives from the Hebronites were 1,700 strong men. They served west of the Jordan River. They did all the work for the LORD and the king. 31 Jerijah was the leader of the Hebronites. Family records showed their family lines. In David's fortieth year as king, they looked for strong men. They found them at Jazer in Gilead. 32 Jerijah's relatives were 2,700 strong men and family leaders. King David put them in charge of the Reubenites, Gadites, and half of Manasseh. They handled all matters for God and the king.
