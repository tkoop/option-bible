Ziba Brings Food to David

1 David had gone a little past the top of the hill. Then Ziba, the servant of Mephibosheth, met him. Ziba had two donkeys with saddles. They carried 200 loaves of bread, 100 bunches of raisins, 100 pieces of summer fruit, and a bag of wine.

2 The king asked, “What are these for?”

Ziba said, “The donkeys are for the king’s people to ride. The bread and fruit are for the young men to eat. The wine is for tired people in the desert to drink.”

3 The king asked, “Where is your master?”

Ziba said, “He is staying in Jerusalem. He said, ‘Today the people of Israel will give my father’s kingdom back to me.’”

4 Then the king said to Ziba, “All that belonged to Mephibosheth is yours now.”

Ziba bowed and said, “I hope I have found favor in your eyes, my lord the king.”

Shimei Curses David

5 David came to Bahurim. A man from Saul’s family came out. His name was Shimei son of Gera. He came out cursing.

6 He threw stones at David and at all the king’s servants. All the people and the soldiers were on David’s right and left.

7 Shimei cursed and said, “Get out! Get out! You are a murderer! You are a worthless man! 8 The LORD has brought back on you all the blood of Saul’s family. You ruled after him, and the LORD has given the kingdom to your son Absalom. Look! You are caught in your own evil. You are a murderer!”

9 Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, “Why should this worthless man curse my lord the king? Let me go cut off his head.”

10 The king said, “What do I have to do with you, sons of Zeruiah? If he curses because the LORD said to him, ‘Curse David,’ who can say, ‘Why did you do that?’”

11 David said to Abishai and all his servants, “Look! My own son is trying to kill me. So this man from Benjamin is even worse. Leave him alone. Let him curse, for the LORD told him to. 12 Maybe the LORD will see my trouble and pay me back with good for his curse today.”

13 David and his men went along the road. Shimei walked along the hillside across from him. He kept cursing and throwing stones and dust.

14 The king and all the people with him came in tired. They rested there.

Absalom Reaches Jerusalem

15 Absalom and all the people of Israel came to Jerusalem. Ahithophel was with him.

Hushai Comes to Absalom

16 When David’s friend Hushai the Archite came to Absalom, Hushai said, “Long live the king! Long live the king!”

17 Absalom said to Hushai, “Is this your kindness to your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?”

18 Hushai said to Absalom, “No. I will belong to the one the LORD chose. I will stay with him and with these people and all Israel. 19 Also, who should I serve? Should I not serve your father’s son? I served your father before. I will serve you now.”

20 Absalom said to Ahithophel, “Give your advice. What should we do?”

Ahithophel Gives Advice

21 Ahithophel said to Absalom, “Go and sleep with your father’s slave wives he left to watch the house. Then all Israel will hear that you have made yourself hated by your father. The hands of everyone with you will be strong.”

22 So they set up a tent for Absalom on the roof. There, in front of all Israel, he slept with his father’s slave wives.

23 In those days, people thought Ahithophel’s advice was like asking God. Both David and Absalom listened to him.
