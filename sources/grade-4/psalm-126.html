A Happy Song for God's People

1 A song for going up. When the the LORD brought His people back to Zion, we felt like we were dreaming. 2 Then our mouths were full of laughs, and our tongues sang happy songs. Then the other nations said, "The The LORD did big things for them."

3 The The LORD did big things for us, and we are glad. 4 the the LORD, bring back our people who were taken away, like streams in the dry land.

5 People who plant with tears will gather crops with joy. 6 A man who goes out crying and carries seed to plant will come back with joy, carrying his bundles of grain.
