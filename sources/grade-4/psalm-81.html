Sing to God with Joy

1 Sing out loud to God. He is our strength. Shout to the God of Jacob. 2 Start the song. Play the tambourine. Play the sweet harp. 3 Blow the trumpet on the new moon. Blow it on the full moon. Blow it on our feast day. 4 This is a rule for Israel. It is a law from the God of Jacob.

God Helped His People

5 He made this rule for Joseph. Joseph went out from Egypt. I heard a voice I did not know:

6 "I took the heavy load from his shoulders. His hands were free from the baskets. 7 You called to Me when you were in trouble. I saved you. I answered you from a hidden thundercloud. I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

8 "Hear Me, My people. I will warn you. Israel, if you would listen to Me! 9 Do not have any strange god among you. Do not bow down to a god from another land. 10 I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt. Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it. 11 But My people did not listen to My voice. Israel did not want Me. 12 So I let them go their own way. They followed their own plans. 13 If only My people would listen to Me! Israel would walk in My ways. 14 I would quickly beat down their enemies. I would turn My hand against their enemies. 15 Those who hate the LORD would bow low before Him. Their trouble would last forever. 16 I would feed them the best wheat. I would fill you with honey from the rock."
