Amos's Message

1 Amos was a shepherd from Tekoa. He saw these words about Israel. This was in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel. He saw them two years before the earthquake.

A Loud Warning from God

2 The LORD roars from Zion. He shouts from Jerusalem. The green fields where shepherds feed sheep become sad. The top of Mount Carmel dries up.

Damascus Will Be Punished

3 This is what the LORD says: "Damascus has sinned again and again. I will not take back My punishment. They crushed the people of Gilead with iron tools. 4 I will send fire on Hazael's house. It will burn Ben-hadad's palaces. 5 I will break the gates of Damascus. I will remove the people from the Valley of Aven. I will take away the ruler from Beth-eden. The people of Aram will be taken away to Kir," says the LORD.

Gaza Will Be Punished

6 This is what the LORD says: "Gaza has sinned again and again. I will not take back My punishment. They took whole towns of people away and handed them over to Edom. 7 I will send fire on Gaza's wall. It will burn its palaces. 8 I will remove the people from Ashdod. I will take away the ruler from Ashkelon. I will strike Ekron with My hand. The rest of the Philistine people will die," says the Lord, the LORD.

Tyre Will Be Punished

9 This is what the LORD says: "Tyre has sinned again and again. I will not take back My punishment. They sent whole towns of people away to Edom. They forgot their promise to be like brothers. 10 I will send fire on Tyre's wall. It will burn its palaces," says the LORD.

Edom Will Be Punished

11 This is what the LORD says: "Edom has sinned again and again. I will not take back My punishment. Edom chased his brother with a sword. He showed no mercy. His anger never stopped. He kept being angry all the time. 12 I will send fire on Teman. It will burn Bozrah's palaces," says the LORD.

Ammon Will Be Punished

13 This is what the LORD says: "The Ammonites have sinned again and again. I will not take back My punishment. They cut open pregnant women in Gilead. They did this to take more land. 14 I will set fire to Rabbah's wall. It will burn its palaces. There will be shouting in battle and a big storm. 15 Their king will be taken away. His leaders will go with him," says the LORD.
