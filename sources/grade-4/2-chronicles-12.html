Rehoboam Stops Following God

1 After Rehoboam became strong as king, he and all the people of Israel with him left the law of the LORD. 2 In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, they were not faithful to the LORD. So Shishak, king of Egypt, attacked Jerusalem.

3 He had 1,200 war carts, 60,000 men on horses, and many people with him from Egypt. They were Libyans, Sukkites, and Cushites. 4 He took the strong cities of Judah and came to Jerusalem.

God Sends a Warning

5 Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and the leaders of Judah. They had gathered in Jerusalem because they were afraid of Shishak. He said to them, "This is what the LORD says: 'You have left Me, so I will leave you to Shishak.'"

The Leaders Say Sorry

6 The leaders of Israel and the king made themselves humble. They said, "The LORD is right."

God Shows Mercy

7 When the LORD saw that they made themselves humble, He spoke to Shemaiah. He said, "They made themselves humble, so I will not destroy them. I will soon save them. My anger will not pour out on Jerusalem through Shishak. 8 But they will serve him, so they can learn the difference between serving Me and serving the kings of other lands."

Shishak Takes the Treasures

9 Shishak, king of Egypt, attacked Jerusalem. He took the treasures of the LORD's house and the treasures of the king's house. He took everything. He even took the gold shields that Solomon had made. 10 King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place. He gave them to the leaders of the guards who watched the door of the king's house.

11 When the king went to the LORD's house, the guards went with him. They carried the shields. Then they took them back to the guard room.

Rehoboam Becomes Strong Again

12 Because Rehoboam made himself humble, the LORD's anger turned from him. He was not destroyed all the way. Also, some good things were in Judah.

13 King Rehoboam became strong in Jerusalem and stayed king. He was 41 years old when he became king. He ruled 17 years in Jerusalem. This was the city the LORD chose from all the tribes of Israel. He put His Name there. Rehoboam's mother was Naamah. She was an Ammonite.

14 He did wrong because he had not set his heart to look for the LORD.

Rehoboam Dies

15 The things Rehoboam did first and last are written in the books of Shemaiah the prophet and Iddo the man who saw visions. They also wrote family lists. There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the time.

16 Rehoboam died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Abijah became king after him.
