Trouble for a Faraway Land

1 Trouble is coming to the land with buzzing wings, far past the rivers of Cush! 2 That land sends messengers by sea in boats made of reeds. Go, fast messengers, to a tall people with smooth skin. They are feared far and wide. They are a strong nation with a strange language. Rivers split their land.

Everyone Will See the Sign

3 All you people of the world, you who live on the earth, when a flag is raised on the mountains, you will see it. When a horn blows, you will hear it.

The LORD Speaks

4 This is what the LORD says to me: "I will stay quiet and watch from My home. I will be like heat waves in the sun, and like a cloud of dew in the hot harvest time."

The Vine Is Cut Back

5 Before the harvest, when the flowers are gone and the grapes are growing, He will cut the new shoots with pruning knives and cut away the spreading branches. 6 They will be left for the mountain birds and the wild animals. The birds will eat them all summer. The wild animals will eat them all winter.

A Gift to the LORD

7 At that time, gifts will be brought to the LORD Almighty from that tall people with smooth skin. They are feared far and wide. They are a strong nation with a strange language. Rivers split their land. The gifts will go to Mount Zion, the place of the Name of the LORD Almighty.
