The Followers Choose Seven Helpers

1 In those days, more and more people became followers. The Greek-speaking followers were upset with the Hebrew-speaking followers. They said the widows were left out when food was given out each day.

2 The twelve called all the followers together. They said, "It is not right for us to stop teaching God's word so we can serve food. 3 Brothers, pick seven men from among you. They should be known as men full of the Spirit and wisdom. We will put them in charge of this work. 4 We will keep praying and teaching God's word."

5 This pleased everyone. They chose Stephen. He was full of faith and the Holy Spirit. They also chose Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolas. Nicolas was from Antioch and had become a follower.

6 They brought these men to the apostles. The apostles prayed and put their hands on them.

7 God's word grew. Many more people in Jerusalem became followers. Many priests also believed.

Stephen Does Great Miracles

8 Stephen was full of God's kindness and power. He did great miracles among the people.

Some Men Argue with Stephen

9 Some men from the meeting place of the freed slaves stood up. Other men from Cyrene, Alexandria, Cilicia, and Asia stood up too. They argued with Stephen. 10 But they could not beat his wisdom and the Spirit that helped him speak.

11 Then they secretly found men to say, "We heard him speak bad words against Moses and God."

12 They made the people, the elders, and the teachers of the law angry. They rushed up, grabbed Stephen, and took him to the council.

13 They brought false witnesses. The witnesses said, "This man never stops speaking against this holy place and the law. 14 We heard him say that Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place and change the customs Moses gave us."

15 Everyone in the council looked at Stephen. They saw his face looked like an angel's face.
