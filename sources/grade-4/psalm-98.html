Praise God for His Great Victory

1 Sing a new song to the the LORD, because He has done amazing things. His strong and holy power won the victory. 2 The LORD has shown His saving power. He has shown His right and good ways to all the nations. 3 He remembered His kind love and His faithfulness to Israel's family. Everyone on earth has seen how our God saves.

All the Earth Should Praise the LORD

4 Shout to the the LORD, all the earth! Break out in song and praise Him. 5 Praise the the LORD with the harp. Sing with music and songs. 6 Play trumpets and the ram's horn. Shout for joy before the the LORD, the King.

All Creation Praises the LORD

7 Let the sea roar, and everything in it. Let the world and all who live in it shout. 8 Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the mountains sing together for joy. 9 They sing before the the LORD, because He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world fairly, and He will judge all people with what is right.
