Ezra Hears Bad News

1 After this, the leaders came to me. They said, "The people of Israel, the priests, and the Levites have not kept away from the people of the lands. They have copied the bad things those people do. They have mixed with the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites." 2 They have taken some of their daughters to be wives for themselves and their sons. So the holy family line has mixed with the people of the land. The leaders and officials led the way in this wrong.

Ezra Is Very Sad

3 When I heard this, I tore my clothes. I pulled hair from my head and beard. I sat down, stunned. 4 Then everyone who was afraid of the words of the God of Israel came to me. They came because of the wrong the people had done. I sat there, stunned, until the evening sacrifice.

Ezra Prays to God

5 At the time of the evening sacrifice, I got up from where I was sitting. My clothes were still torn. I fell on my knees. I spread out my hands to the LORD my God.

6 I said, "O my God, I am ashamed. I feel disgraced. I cannot even look up to You, my God. Our wrongs are more than we can count. Our guilt has reached up to heaven.

7 "Since the time of our fathers until today, our guilt has been great. Because of our wrongs, we, our kings, and our priests have been handed over to other kings. We have been killed by the sword. We have been taken away. We have been robbed. We are ashamed, just like today.

8 "But now, for a short time, the LORD our God has been kind to us. He left us a few people. He gave us a safe place in His holy place. Even while we were slaves, our God gave us new life. He gave light to our eyes.

9 "Even though we are slaves, our God has not left us. He has shown us His kind love before the kings of Persia. He gave us new life to build God's house again. He let us fix its broken parts. He gave us safety in Judah and Jerusalem.

10 "And now, our God, what can we say after this? We have turned away from Your commands. 11 You gave these commands through Your servants the prophets. They said,

'The land you are going to take is made dirty by the people who live there. They have filled it with bad things from one end to the other. 12 So do not give your daughters to their sons. Do not take their daughters for your sons. Do not look for their peace or good. Then you will be strong. You will eat the good things of the land. You will leave it to your sons forever.'

13 "After all the bad things that happened to us because of our wrongs and great guilt, You, our God, have not punished us as much as we deserve. You have left us a few people like this.

14 "Should we break Your commands again? Should we marry these people who do bad things? Would You not be so angry with us that You would destroy us? Then no one would be left.

15 "The LORD, God of Israel, You are right. That is why a few of us are still here today. Here we are before You in our guilt. Because of this, no one can stand before You."
