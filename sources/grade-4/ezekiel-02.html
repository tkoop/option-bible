God Calls Ezekiel

1 He said to me, “Son of man, stand up on your feet. I will speak to you.” 2 As He spoke, the Spirit came into me and set me on my feet. Then I heard Him speaking to me.

3 He said, “Son of man, I am sending you to the people of Israel. They are a nation that keeps turning away from Me. They and their fathers have turned away from Me to this very day. 4 They are stubborn and mean. I am sending you to them, and you must say to them, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says.’ 5 They may listen, or they may not. They are a stubborn family. But they will know that a prophet was with them.”

6 “And you, Son of man, do not be afraid of them or their words. Do not be afraid. They are like thorns and sharp weeds around you, and you live among scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words. Do not be scared by how they look. They are a stubborn family. 7 But speak My words to them. They may listen, or they may not. They are stubborn.”

8 “But you, Son of man, listen to what I say. Do not be stubborn like that stubborn family. Open your mouth and eat what I give you.”

Ezekiel Sees a Scroll

9 Then I looked. I saw a hand reaching out to me. In it was a scroll. 10 He opened it in front of me. It was written on the front and the back. It had words of sadness, crying, and trouble.
