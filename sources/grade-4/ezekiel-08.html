Ezekiel Sees a Strange Vision

1 In the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day, I sat in my house. The leaders of Judah sat in front of me. Then the hand of the Lord GOD came on me there. 2 I looked and saw a shape like a man. From His waist down, He looked like fire. From His waist up, He shone like bright metal. 3 He reached out what looked like a hand and took me by the hair of my head. The Spirit lifted me between the ground and the sky. He brought me in a vision to Jerusalem. He brought me to the north gate of the inner yard. There was a false god there that makes God jealous. 4 There I saw the glory of the God of Israel. It looked like the vision I saw in the valley.

The False God at the Gate

5 He said to me, “Son of Man, look toward the north.” I looked north. By the altar gate, at the entrance, stood that false god that makes God jealous. 6 He said to me, “Son of Man, do you see what they are doing? The people of Israel are doing terrible sins here. They drive Me away from My holy place. But you will see even worse sins than these.”

A Hole in the Wall

7 Then He brought me to the entrance of the yard. I looked and saw a hole in the wall. 8 He said to me, “Son of Man, dig through the wall.” I dug through the wall and found a door.

A Hidden Room Full of Idols

9 He said, “Go in and see the terrible things they do here.” 10 I went in and looked. I saw all kinds of crawling things and gross animals. I also saw all the false gods of Israel carved all around the wall. 11 Seventy leaders of Israel stood in front of them. Jaazaniah son of Shaphan stood among them. Each man held a small pan. Sweet-smelling smoke rose up.

12 He said to me, “Son of Man, do you see what the leaders of Israel do in the dark? Each man is in his own private room. They say, ‘The The LORD does not see us. The the LORD has left the land.’ 13 You will see them do even worse sins.”

Women Cry for Tammuz

14 Then He brought me to the north gate of the the LORD’s house. I saw women sitting there, crying for Tammuz, a false god. 15 He said to me, “Son of Man, do you see this? You will see even worse sins than these.”

Men Worship the Sun

16 Then He brought me into the inner yard of the the LORD’s house. At the entrance to the the LORD’s temple, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men. Their backs were toward the the LORD’s temple. Their faces looked east. They bowed down to the sun. 17 He said to me, “Son of Man, have you seen this? Is it a small thing for Judah to do these terrible sins here? They fill the land with hurt and keep making Me angry. Look! They are insulting Me. 18 So I will act in anger. I will not feel sorry for them. I will not spare them. Even if they shout loudly to Me, I will not listen.”
