The Land for Judah

1 The land share for the family groups of Judah went down to the land of Edom and to the Desert of Zin in the far south. 2 Their south border started at the far end of the Dead Sea. 3 It crossed south of Scorpion Pass, went to Zin, and went up to Kadesh Barnea. Then it passed Hezron, went up to Addar, and curved around to Karka. 4 Then it passed Azmon and joined the stream of Egypt. It ended at the sea. This was their south border.

5 The east border was the Dead Sea all the way to where the Jordan River ends. The north border started at the bay of the sea where the Jordan River ends. 6 It went up to Beth Hoglah and passed north of Beth Arabah. Then it went up to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 7 The border went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor. It turned north to Gilgal, across from the Pass of Adummim south of the stream. It passed the waters of En Shemesh and ended at En Rogel.

8 Then it ran up the Valley of Ben Hinnom on the south side of the Jebusite city. That city is Jerusalem. Then it climbed to the top of the hill west of the Hinnom Valley, at the north end of the Valley of Rephaim. 9 From the top of the hill, the border went toward the spring of Nephtoah. It came out at the towns of Mount Ephron and went down toward Baalah. Baalah is Kiriath Jearim. 10 Then it curved west from Baalah to Mount Seir. It ran along the north side of Mount Jearim. That is Kesalon. It went down to Beth Shemesh and crossed to Timnah. 11 It went to the north side of Ekron. It turned toward Shikkeron, passed Mount Baalah, and reached Jabneel. The border ended at the sea. 12 The west border was the sea. These were the borders around the family groups of Judah.

Caleb Gets Hebron

13 The LORD told Joshua to give Caleb son of Jephunneh a share in Judah. So Joshua gave him Kiriath Arba. That is Hebron. Arba was the father of Anak. 14 Caleb drove out three sons of Anak from Hebron: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. 15 From there he went up against the people living in Debir. Before, it was called Kiriath Sepher.

16 Caleb said, "I will let the man who attacks and takes Kiriath Sepher marry my daughter Achsah."

17 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, took it. So Caleb gave his daughter Achsah to him to marry. 18 When Achsah came to Othniel, she told him to ask her father for a field. When she got off her donkey, Caleb asked her, "What do you want?"

19 She said, "Please give me a blessing. You gave me land in the Negev. Please give me springs of water too." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

Towns of Judah

20 This was the land share for the tribe of Judah, by family groups.

Towns in the South

21 The towns in the far south were Kabzeel, Eder, and Jagur. 22 Kinah, Dimonah, and Adadah. 23 Kedesh, Hazor, and Ithnan. 24 Ziph, Telem, and Bealoth. 25 Hazor Hadattah and Kerioth Hezron (that is, Hazor). 26 Amam, Shema, and Moladah.

27 Hazar Gaddah, Heshmon, and Beth Pelet. 28 Hazar Shual, Beersheba, and Biziothiah. 29 Baalah, Iim, and Azem. 30 Eltolad, Chesil, and Hormah. 31 Ziklag, Madmannah, and Sansannah. 32 Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon. That is twenty-nine towns and their villages.

Towns in the Lowlands

33 Eshtaol, Zorah, and Ashnah. 34 Zanoah, En Gannim, Tappuah, and Enam. 35 Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh, and Azekah. 36 Shaaraim, Adithaim, Gederah, and Gederothaim. That is fourteen towns and their villages.

37 Zenan, Hadashah, and Migdal Gad. 38 Dilean, Mizpeh, and Joktheel. 39 Lachish, Bozkath, and Eglon. 40 Cabbon, Lahmam, and Kithlish. 41 Gederoth, Beth Dagon, Naamah, and Makkedah. That is sixteen towns and their villages.

42 Libnah, Ether, and Ashan. 43 Jiphtah, Ashnah, and Nezib. 44 Keilah, Achzib, and Mareshah. That is nine towns and their villages.

Towns near the Sea

45 Ekron and its nearby towns and villages. 46 West of Ekron, all the towns near Ashdod and their villages. 47 Ashdod and its nearby towns and villages. Gaza and its nearby towns and villages, as far as the stream of Egypt and the sea.

Towns in the Hill Country

48 Shamir, Jattir, and Socoh. 49 Dannah and Kiriath Sannah (that is, Debir). 50 Anab, Eshtemoh, and Anim. 51 Goshen, Holon, and Giloh. That is eleven towns and their villages.

52 Arab, Dumah, and Eshan. 53 Janim, Beth Tappuah, and Aphekah. 54 Humtah, Kiriath Arba (that is, Hebron), and Zior. That is nine towns and their villages.

55 Maon, Carmel, Ziph, and Juttah. 56 Jezreel, Jokdeam, and Zanoah. 57 Kain, Gibeah, and Timnah. That is ten towns and their villages.

58 Halhul, Beth Zur, and Gedor. 59 Maarath, Beth Anoth, and Eltekon. That is six towns and their villages. 60 Kiriath Baal (that is, Kiriath Jearim) and Rabbah. That is two towns and their villages.

Towns in the Desert

61 Beth Arabah, Middin, and Secacah. 62 Nibshan, the City of Salt, and En Gedi. That is six towns and their villages.

Jerusalem Not Taken

63 But the people of Judah could not drive out the Jebusites, who lived in Jerusalem. So the Jebusites live there with the people of Judah to this day.
