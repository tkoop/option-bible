The Leaders Plan to Kill Jesus

1 Now the Feast of Bread Without Yeast, called Passover, was near. 2 The chief priests and teachers of the law wanted to kill Jesus. They were afraid of the people.

3 Satan entered Judas, called Iscariot. He was one of the Twelve. 4 Judas went and talked with the chief priests and leaders. He told them how he could hand Jesus over to them. 5 They were glad. They agreed to give him money. 6 He said yes. He looked for a good time to hand Jesus over when no crowd was there.

Getting Ready for the Passover

7 The day of Bread Without Yeast came. On that day the Passover lamb had to be killed.

8 Jesus sent Peter and John. He said, "Go and get the Passover meal ready for us."

9 They asked, "Where do You want us to get it ready?"

10 He said, "When you go into the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him to the house he goes into. 11 Tell the owner of the house that the Teacher asks where the room is where He can eat the Passover with His friends. 12 He will show you a big room upstairs. It will be ready. Get the meal ready there."

13 They went and found it just as He said. They got the Passover ready.

The Last Supper

14 When the time came, Jesus sat down. His followers sat with Him.

15 He said to them, "I have really wanted to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. 16 I tell you, I will not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

17 He took a cup, gave thanks, and said, "Take this and share it among yourselves. 18 I tell you, I will not drink wine until God's kingdom comes."

19 He took bread, gave thanks, and broke it. He gave it to them and said, "This is My body. It is given for you. Do this to remember Me."

20 In the same way, after the meal, He took the cup. He said, "This cup is the new promise. It is sealed with My blood, which is poured out for you."

21 He said, "But look, the hand of the one who is handing Me over is with Me at the table. 22 The Son of Man will go as it has been set. But it will be terrible for the man who hands Him over."

23 They began to ask each other who would do this.

Who Is the Greatest?

24 A fight started among them. They argued about who seemed to be the greatest.

25 Jesus said to them, "The kings of the nations rule over them. Their leaders are called helpers. 26 But you must not be like that. The greatest among you should be like the youngest. The one who leads should be like the one who serves. 27 Who is greater? The one who sits at the table, or the one who serves? Is it not the one who sits? But I am among you as one who serves."

28 He said, "You are the ones who stayed with Me in My hard times. 29 I am giving you a kingdom, just as My Father gave one to Me. 30 You will eat and drink at My table in My kingdom. You will sit on thrones and judge the twelve tribes of Israel."

Jesus Warns Peter

31 He said, "Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to test you all. 32 But I have prayed for you. I pray that your faith will not fail. When you turn back, help your brothers."

33 Peter said, "Lord, I am ready to go with You to jail and to death."

34 Jesus said, "Peter, I tell you, the rooster will not crow today until you say three times that you do not know Me."

Get Ready for Hard Times

35 Jesus asked them, "When I sent you out without a money bag, a bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?"

They said, "Nothing."

36 He said, "But now, whoever has a money bag should take it. Take a bag too. Whoever does not have a sword should sell his coat and buy one."

37 He said, "I tell you, this must come true in Me: 'He was counted with those who broke the law.' What is written about Me is coming to an end."

38 They said, "Lord, here are two swords."

Jesus said, "That is enough."

Jesus Prays on the Mount of Olives

39 Jesus went out and went to the Mount of Olives, as He usually did. His followers followed Him.

40 When He got there, He said, "Pray so you will not be tested."

41 He went a short way from them. He knelt down and prayed.

42 He said, "Father, if You are willing, take this cup from Me. But not My will. Let Your will be done."

43 An angel from heaven appeared to Him and made Him strong.

44 He was in great pain. He prayed harder. His sweat fell like drops of blood to the ground.

45 He got up from praying and went to His followers. He found them sleeping because they were sad.

46 He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so you will not be tested."

Jesus Is Arrested

47 While He was still speaking, a crowd came. Judas, one of the Twelve, led them. He came near Jesus to kiss Him.

48 Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you handing over the Son of Man with a kiss?"

49 Those with Jesus saw what was going to happen. They said, "Lord, should we strike with a sword?"

50 One of them hit the servant of the high priest. He cut off the servant's right ear.

51 Jesus said, "Stop! That is enough." He touched the ear and healed it.

52 Jesus said to the chief priests, temple guards, and elders who came for Him, "Did you come out with swords and clubs as if I were a robber?"

53 He said, "Every day I was with you in the temple. You did not grab Me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness is here."

54 They seized Him and led Him away. They took Him to the high priest's house. Peter followed far behind.

55 They lit a fire in the middle of the yard. Peter sat among them.

56 A servant girl saw him sitting by the fire. She looked at him and said, "This man was with Him too."

57 Peter said, "Woman, I don't know Him."

58 A little later, someone else saw him and said, "You are one of them too."

Peter said, "Man, I am not."

59 About an hour later, another man said strongly, "This man was with Him. He is from Galilee too."

60 Peter said, "Man, I don't know what you are talking about." While he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

61 The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Peter remembered what the Lord had said: 'Before the rooster crows today, you will say three times that you do not know Me.'

62 Peter went outside and cried hard.

Jesus Is Mocked

63 The men holding Jesus made fun of Him. They hit Him.

64 They covered His eyes and said, "Tell us who hit You."

65 They said many bad things about Him.

Jesus Before the Council

66 When day came, the elders of the people, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law met together. They took Him to their council. They said,

67 "If You are the Christ, tell us."

Jesus said, "If I tell you, you will not believe. 68 If I ask you, you will not answer. 69 From now on, the Son of Man will sit at the right hand of God's power."

70 They all said, "Are You the Son of God then?"

Jesus said, "You say that I am."

71 They said, "What more do we need? We heard it from His own mouth."
