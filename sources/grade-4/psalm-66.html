A Song of Praise to God

1 Shout with joy to God, all you people of the earth! 2 Sing about the glory of His name. Make His praise wonderful. 3 Say to God, "Your works are amazing! Because You are so strong, Your enemies will bow to You." 4 All the earth will bow down to You and sing to You. They will sing about Your name. Selah.

See What God Has Done

5 Come and see what God has done. He does amazing things for people. 6 He turned the sea into dry land. They walked across the river. We were happy in Him there. 7 He rules the world forever with His power. His eyes watch the nations. Don't let stubborn people lift themselves up. Selah.

God Tests His People

8 Praise our God, you nations. Let people hear His praise. 9 He keeps us alive. He does not let our feet slip. 10 For You, God, have tested us. You made us pure like silver. 11 You brought us into a trap. You put a heavy load on our backs. 12 You let enemies walk all over us. We went through fire and water, but You brought us to a place with plenty.

I Will Keep My Promises

13 I will come to Your house with burnt offerings. I will keep the promises I made to You. 14 My lips promised. My mouth spoke when I was in trouble. 15 I will bring You burnt offerings of fat animals, with the smoke of rams. I will offer bulls and goats. Selah.

Tell Others What God Did

16 Come and listen, all you who fear God. I will tell you what He did for me. 17 I cried out to Him with my mouth. Praise was on my tongue. 18 If I had sin in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. 19 But God has listened. He has heard my prayer. 20 Praise God, who has not turned away my prayer or His faithful love from me.
