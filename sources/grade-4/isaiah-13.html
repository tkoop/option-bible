A Message About Babylon

1 This is a message about Babylon. Isaiah, the son of Amoz, saw it.

God Calls His Army

2 Raise a flag on a bare hill. Call out to them. Wave your hand so they can go through the gates of the leaders.

3 I have told My holy ones to come. I called My strong fighters to show My anger. They are happy to win for Me.

4 Listen! There is a loud noise on the mountains, like a big crowd. Listen! There is a loud roar among the kingdoms, like nations gathered together. the LORD of Armies is getting an army ready for war. 5 They come from far away, from the ends of the sky. the LORD and His angry weapons come to destroy the whole earth.

The Day of the LORD

6 Cry out! The Day of the LORD is near. It will come like ruin from the Almighty. 7 So all hands will go weak, and every heart will melt. 8 Fear, panic, and pain will grab them. They will twist and turn like a woman having a baby. They will look at each other, and their faces will turn red with fear.

9 Look! The Day of the LORD is coming. It will be cruel. It will bring fury and burning anger. It will make the earth empty, and it will destroy the sinners in it. 10 The stars and their groups in the sky will not shine. The sun will be dark when it rises, and the moon will not give its light.

God Will Judge the World

11 I will punish the world for its bad ways. I will punish the wicked for their wrongs. I will end the pride of proud people, and I will bring down the pride of cruel rulers. 12 I will make people harder to find than pure gold. People will be rarer than the gold of Ophir. 13 I will shake the sky, and the earth will shake out of its place. This will happen because of the anger of the LORD of Armies and His burning anger on that day.

The People Will Run Away

14 They will be like a scared deer. They will be like sheep with no shepherd. Each person will turn to his own people. Each will run to his own land. 15 Anyone who is caught will be stabbed. Anyone who is caught will die by the sword. 16 Their babies will be killed in front of them. Their houses will be robbed. Their wives will be attacked.

The Medes Will Attack

17 Look! I will stir up the Medes against them. The Medes do not care about silver. They do not want gold. 18 Their bows will kill the young men. They will not feel sorry for the babies. They will not spare the children.

Babylon Will Be Destroyed

19 Babylon is the jewel of kingdoms. It is the pride of the Chaldeans. But God will destroy it like Sodom and Gomorrah. 20 No one will live there ever again, for generation after generation. No Arab will put up a tent there. No shepherd will make his sheep lie down there. 21 Wild animals of the desert will lie there. Its houses will be full of owls. Ostriches will live there. Wild goats will jump around. 22 Hyenas will howl in its strong forts. Jackals will howl in its fine palaces. Babylon's time is near. Its days will not last long.
