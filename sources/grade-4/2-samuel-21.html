A Long Time Without Food

1 During David’s rule, there was no food for three years in a row. David asked the the LORD for help. The The LORD said, “This is because of Saul and his family. They killed the Gibeonites.” 2 The king called the Gibeonites and talked with them. The Gibeonites were not Israelites. They were part of the Amorites who were left. Israel had promised to let them live. But Saul tried to kill them to help Israel and Judah.

David Asks How to Make Things Right

3 David said to the Gibeonites, “What can I do for you? How can I make this right so you will bless the LORD’s land?”

4 The Gibeonites said, “We do not want silver or gold from Saul or his family. We also cannot kill anyone in Israel.”

Then David asked, “What do you want me to do for you?”

5 They said, “The man who hurt us and tried to wipe us out kept us from living in Israel.”

Seven Men From Saul’s Family

6 “Give us seven men from his family. We will hang them before the the LORD in Gibeah, Saul’s town. Saul was the man the LORD chose.”

The king said, “I will give them to you.”

7 But the king saved Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul. He kept him safe because of the promise before the the LORD between David and Jonathan, son of Saul. 8 Instead, the king took Armoni and Mephibosheth. These were the two sons Rizpah daughter of Aiah had for Saul. He also took the five sons of Merab daughter of Saul. Merab had these sons with Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite. 9 He gave them to the Gibeonites. They hanged them on a hill before the the LORD. All seven died together. They were killed at the start of the harvest, when the barley was ready.

Rizpah Watches Over the Bodies

10 Rizpah daughter of Aiah took rough cloth and spread it on a rock for herself. She stayed there from the start of the harvest until rain fell from the sky. She did not let birds land on the bodies during the day. She did not let wild animals come near at night.

11 David heard what Rizpah, a wife of Saul, had done.

12 David went and took the bones of Saul and Jonathan from the men of Jabesh Gilead. These men had taken the bones from the town square in Beth Shan. The Philistines had hung the bodies there when they killed Saul at Gilboa. 13 David brought the bones of Saul and Jonathan from there. The people also gathered the bones of the men who had been hanged. 14 They buried the bones of Saul and Jonathan in Zela, in Benjamin’s land. They put them in the tomb of Saul’s father, Kish. After they did all the king said, God heard their prayers and helped the land.

More Battles With the Philistines

15 Later, the Philistines fought Israel again. David and his men went to fight. David got very tired. 16 Ishbi-Benob was from Rapha’s family. He had a heavy bronze spear and a new sword. He tried to kill David.

17 But Abishai son of Zeruiah helped David. He hit the Philistine and killed him.

Then David’s men made a promise. They said, “You must not go out to battle with us again. We do not want Israel’s light to go out.”

18 Later, there was another battle with the Philistines at Gob. Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph, who was from Rapha’s family.

19 There was another battle with the Philistines at Gob. Elhanan son of Jaare-Oregim from Bethlehem killed Goliath the Gittite. Goliath’s spear was as big as a heavy wooden beam.

20 There was another battle at Gath. There was a very tall man there. He had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot. That is 24 in all. He was from Rapha’s family.

21 When he made fun of Israel, Jonathan, the son of David’s brother Shimei, killed him.

22 These four men from Rapha’s family in Gath were killed by David and his men.
