Jesus Is Taken to Pilate

1 Early in the morning, the chief priests, older leaders, and teachers of the law met together. The whole Jewish council agreed. They tied Jesus up. They led Him away and handed Him to Pilate. 2 Pilate asked Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?" Jesus answered, "That's what you say." 3 The chief priests said many bad things against Him. 4 Pilate asked Him again, "Won't you answer? Look how many things they say against you." 5 But Jesus did not answer anymore. Pilate was amazed.

Barabbas or Jesus?

6 At the feast, Pilate used to let one prisoner go free. The people could choose. 7 A man named Barabbas was in jail with rebels. They had killed someone during a riot. 8 The crowd came and asked Pilate to do what he always did. 9 Pilate asked them, "Do you want me to let the King of the Jews go free for you?" 10 He knew the chief priests had handed Jesus over because they were jealous. 11 But the chief priests stirred up the crowd. They asked for Barabbas instead. 12 Pilate asked them again, "What do you want me to do with the man you call the King of the Jews?" 13 They shouted, "Crucify Him!" 14 Pilate said, "Why? What bad thing did He do?" But they shouted even louder, "Crucify Him!" 15 Pilate wanted to make the crowd happy. So he let Barabbas go free. He had Jesus whipped. Then he handed Jesus over to be crucified.

Soldiers Make Fun of Jesus

16 The soldiers led Jesus into the courtyard. This place was called the Praetorium. They called all the soldiers together. 17 They put a purple robe on Him. They made a crown of thorns and put it on His head. 18 They began to say to Him, "Greetings, King of the Jews!" 19 They hit His head with a stick. They spit on Him. They got down on their knees and pretended to worship Him. 20 After they made fun of Him, they took the purple robe off Him. They put His own clothes back on Him. Then they led Him out to be crucified.

Simon Carries Jesus' Cross

21 A man named Simon was passing by. He was from Cyrene. He was the father of Alexander and Rufus. He was coming from the country. The soldiers forced him to carry Jesus' cross. 22 They brought Jesus to a place called Golgotha. That means "Place of the Skull." 23 They offered Him wine mixed with a spice called myrrh. But He did not take it. 24 They hung Him on the cross. They divided His clothes among themselves. They threw dice to see who would get what. 25 It was nine o'clock in the morning when they crucified Him. 26 A sign said why He was being killed. It said, "THE KING OF THE JEWS." 27 They also crucified two robbers with Him. One was on His right side, and one was on His left.

People Make Fun of Jesus on the Cross

29 People walking by made fun of Him. They shook their heads and said, "Ha! You said You could tear down the temple and build it again in three days. 30 Come down from the cross and save Yourself!" 31 The chief priests and teachers of the law also made fun of Him. They said to each other, "He saved other people, but He can't save Himself. 32 Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down from the cross now. Then we will see and believe." The men crucified with Him also insulted Him.

Jesus Dies

33 At noon, darkness came over the whole land. It lasted until three in the afternoon. 34 At three o'clock, Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" That means, "My God, My God, why have You left Me?" 35 Some people standing there heard it. They said, "Listen! He is calling Elijah." 36 Someone ran and soaked a sponge in sour wine. He put it on a stick and gave Jesus a drink. He said, "Wait! Let's see if Elijah comes to take Him down." 37 Jesus cried out with a loud voice and died. 38 The curtain in the temple tore in two from top to bottom. 39 A Roman officer was standing in front of Him. He saw how Jesus died. He said, "This man really was the Son of God."

Women Watch from Far Away

40 Some women were watching from far away. They included Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and Joses, and Salome. 41 They had followed Jesus and helped Him when He was in Galilee. Many other women had also come with Him to Jerusalem.

Jesus Is Buried

42 It was already evening. It was the day before the Sabbath. 43 Joseph came from the town of Arimathea. He was an important man in the Jewish council. He was also waiting for God's kingdom. He was brave. He went to Pilate and asked for Jesus' body. 44 Pilate was surprised that Jesus was already dead. He called the Roman officer and asked if Jesus had been dead for a while. 45 When he learned from the officer, he gave the body to Joseph. 46 Joseph bought a linen cloth. He took Jesus down from the cross. He wrapped Him in the cloth. He put Him in a tomb cut out of rock. He rolled a stone in front of the tomb door. 47 Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses watched where Jesus was laid.
