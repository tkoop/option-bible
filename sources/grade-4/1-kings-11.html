Solomon’s Many Wives

1 King Solomon loved many foreign women. He loved Pharaoh’s daughter. He also loved women from Moab, Ammon, Edom, Sidon, and the Hittites.

2 They were from nations the LORD had told Israel about: “Do not marry them. Do not let them marry you. They will turn your hearts to their gods.” But Solomon stayed close to them because he loved them.

3 He had seven hundred wives who were princesses. He had three hundred other wives. His wives turned his heart away.

Solomon Turns to Other Gods

4 When Solomon was old, his wives turned his heart to other gods. His heart was not fully with the LORD his God, like his father David’s heart.

5 Solomon followed Ashtoreth, the goddess of the Sidonians. He also followed Milcom, the hated god of the Ammonites. 6 Solomon did what was bad in the eyes of the LORD. He did not follow the LORD fully, like his father David did.

7 Then Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, the hated god of Moab. He built it on the hill east of Jerusalem. He also built one for Molech, the hated god of the Ammonites. 8 He did the same for all his foreign wives. They burned incense and offered sacrifices to their gods.

The LORD Is Angry with Solomon

9 The LORD was angry with Solomon. His heart had turned away from the LORD, the God of Israel. The LORD had appeared to him two times. 10 The LORD had commanded him not to follow other gods. But Solomon did not keep what the LORD commanded.

The LORD Speaks to Solomon

11 So the LORD said to Solomon, “Because you did this, you did not keep My covenant and My rules that I gave you. I will tear the kingdom away from you. I will give it to your servant. 12 But I will not do it while you are alive. I will tear it away from your son. 13 Yet I will not tear away the whole kingdom. I will give one tribe to your son. I do this for My servant David and for Jerusalem, the city I chose.”

Hadad Becomes an Enemy

14 Then the LORD raised up an enemy against Solomon. It was Hadad the Edomite. He was from the royal family of Edom.

Hadad Flees to Egypt

15 This happened when David was king. Joab, the leader of the army, went to bury the dead. He killed every male in Edom. 16 Joab and all Israel stayed there six months. They killed every male in Edom.

17 But Hadad ran away. He was still a young boy. Some of his father’s Edomite servants went with him to Egypt. 18 They left Midian and came to Paran. They took men from Paran with them. Then they came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt. Pharaoh gave Hadad a house, land, and food.

19 Hadad found great favor with Pharaoh. Pharaoh gave him a wife. She was the sister of his own wife, Queen Tahpenes. 20 The sister of Tahpenes had a son named Genubath. Tahpenes raised him in Pharaoh’s house. Genubath grew up among Pharaoh’s children.

21 In Egypt, Hadad heard that David had died. He also heard that Joab, the army leader, had died. Then Hadad said to Pharaoh, “Let me go back to my own country.”

22 Pharaoh said to him, “What do you lack here with me? Why do you want to go to your country?”

Hadad said, “Just let me go.”

Rezon Becomes an Enemy

23 God also raised up another enemy. It was Rezon son of Eliada. He had run away from his master, Hadadezer king of Zobah. 24 David had killed some of Hadadezer’s men. Rezon gathered men around him. He became the leader of a group of fighters. They went to Damascus and lived there. Rezon ruled in Damascus. 25 Rezon was Israel’s enemy all the days of Solomon. He caused trouble, along with Hadad. Rezon ruled over Aram.

Jeroboam Rebels Against Solomon

26 Jeroboam son of Nebat rebelled against the king. He was from Zeredah in Ephraim. His mother was Zeruah, a widow. Jeroboam was Solomon’s servant. 27 This is why he rebelled: Solomon was building the Millo. He was closing a gap in the wall of his father David’s city. 28 Jeroboam was a strong and able man. Solomon saw that the young man worked hard. So Solomon put him in charge of all the forced labor of the house of Joseph.

Ahijah Meets Jeroboam

29 At that time, Jeroboam left Jerusalem. The prophet Ahijah the Shilonite met him on the road. Ahijah was wearing a new cloak. The two men were alone in the field. 30 Ahijah took the new cloak he was wearing. He tore it into twelve pieces.

Ahijah’s Message from the LORD

31 Ahijah said to Jeroboam, “Take ten pieces for yourself. The LORD, the God of Israel, says: I am tearing the kingdom from Solomon’s hand. I will give you ten tribes. 32 One tribe will stay with Solomon’s family. This is for My servant David and for Jerusalem, the city I chose from all the tribes of Israel. 33 They left Me. They bowed down to Ashtoreth, the goddess of the Sidonians. They bowed to Chemosh, the god of Moab. They bowed to Milcom, the god of the Ammonites. They did not walk in My ways. They did not do what is right in My eyes. They did not keep My rules and My laws, like David, Solomon’s father, did.”

34 “But I will not take the whole kingdom from Solomon’s hand. I will make him ruler all his life. I do this for My servant David, whom I chose. David kept My commands and My rules. 35 I will take the kingdom from his son’s hand. I will give you ten tribes. 36 I will give one tribe to his son. Then My servant David will always have a lamp before Me in Jerusalem. That is the city I chose for My name.”

37 “I will take you, and you will rule over all you want, and you will be king over Israel. 38 If you listen to all I command you, and walk in My ways, and do what is right in My eyes, and keep My rules and commands like My servant David did, then I will be with you. I will build you a lasting house, like I built for David. I will give Israel to you. 39 I will make David’s family suffer because of this. But not forever.”

40 Solomon tried to kill Jeroboam. But Jeroboam ran away to Egypt. He went to Shishak king of Egypt. He stayed in Egypt until Solomon died.

Solomon Dies

41 The rest of Solomon’s deeds and his wisdom are written in the book of Solomon’s deeds. 42 Solomon ruled in Jerusalem over all Israel for forty years. 43 Then Solomon died and was buried in the city of his father David. His son Rehoboam became king after him.
