Amnon Loves Tamar

1 After this, David's son Absalom had a beautiful sister named Tamar. David's son Amnon loved her. 2 Amnon was so upset about Tamar that he felt sick. She was not married yet, and he thought he could not be with her. 3 Amnon had a friend named Jonadab. Jonadab was the son of Shimeah, David's brother. Jonadab was very clever.

4 Jonadab said to Amnon, "Why do you look so weak every morning? Won't you tell me?"

Amnon said, "I love Tamar. She is my brother Absalom's sister."

Amnon's Trick

5 Jonadab said, "Lie down on your bed and act sick. When your father comes to see you, say, 'Please let my sister Tamar come and make me some bread. Let her make the food where I can see it, so I can eat what she makes.'"

6 So Amnon lay down and acted sick. The king came to see him. Amnon said, "Please let my sister Tamar come and make two cakes where I can see them, so I can eat from her hand."

7 David sent word to Tamar at home. He said, "Go to your brother Amnon's house and make food for him."

8 Tamar went to her brother Amnon's house. He was lying down. She took dough and worked it. She made cakes in front of him and cooked them. 9 She took the pan and set the food before him, but he would not eat. Amnon said, "Send everyone out." So everyone left.

10 Amnon said to Tamar, "Bring the food into the bedroom, and I will eat from your hand." Tamar took the cakes she had made and brought them to her brother Amnon in the bedroom.

Amnon Hurts Tamar

11 She brought them close to him to eat. He grabbed her and said, "Come, lie with me, my sister."

12 She said, "No, my brother! Don't force me. This should not be done in Israel. Don't do this terrible thing. 13 Where would I take my shame? And you would be like a wicked man in Israel. Please talk to the king. He would not keep me from you."

14 But he would not listen to her. He was stronger than she was, so he forced her and lay with her.

15 Then Amnon hated her very much. His hate was greater than the love he had felt for her. He said, "Get up and go!"

16 She said, "No! Sending me away is worse than the terrible thing you just did to me." But he would not listen to her.

17 He called his servant and said, "Send this woman out of here, and lock the door after her."

18 The servant put her out and locked the door. She was wearing a long robe with sleeves, because the king's unmarried daughters wore robes like that.

19 Tamar put ashes on her head and tore the robe she was wearing. She put her hand on her head and went away, crying loudly.

20 Her brother Absalom said to her, "Has Amnon, your brother, been with you? Be quiet now, my sister. He is your brother. Don't let this break your heart." So Tamar stayed alone and sad in her brother Absalom's house.

21 King David heard about all these things, and he was very angry.

22 Absalom did not speak to Amnon at all, because he hated Amnon for what he had done to his sister Tamar.

Absalom Makes a Plan

23 After two full years, Absalom had men who cut the wool from sheep at Baal-hazor near Ephraim. Absalom invited all the king's sons.

24 Absalom came to the king and said, "Your servant has sheep shearers. Please let the king and his servants come with your servant."

25 The king said to Absalom, "No, my son. We should not all go, or we would be too much for you." Absalom kept asking him to go, but the king would not go. Still, the king wished him well.

26 Then Absalom said, "If not, please let my brother Amnon go with us."

The king said, "Why should he go with you?"

27 But Absalom kept asking until the king sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him.

28 Absalom told his servants, "Watch Amnon. When he is happy from wine, I will say to you, 'Strike Amnon and kill him.' Do not be afraid. I have told you to do this. Be strong and brave."

29 Absalom's servants did to Amnon what Absalom had told them. Then all the king's sons got up. Each one rode on his mule and ran away.

30 While they were on the way, news came to David. It said, "Absalom has killed all the king's sons. Not one is left."

31 The king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground. All his servants stood by with their clothes torn.

32 But Jonadab, son of Shimeah, David's brother, answered, "My lord, do not think all the young men, the king's sons, are dead. Only Amnon is dead. Absalom has planned this since the day Amnon hurt his sister Tamar. 33 So now, my lord the king, do not take it to heart and think all the king's sons are dead. Only Amnon is dead."

Absalom Runs Away

34 But Absalom ran away. The watchman looked up and saw many people coming from the road behind him, from the side of the hill.

35 Jonadab said to the king, "Look, the king's sons are coming. Your servant was right."

36 When he finished speaking, the king's sons came in. They cried loudly, and the king and all his servants cried very much too.

37 Absalom ran away and went to Talmai son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. David mourned for his son every day. 38 Absalom stayed in Geshur for three years.

39 King David wanted to see Absalom, because he was no longer so sad about Amnon, who was dead.
