The LORD Gives Hope for the New House

1 On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the LORD's message came to Haggai the prophet. 2 The LORD told him to say to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, the leader of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the rest of the people, 3 "Who is left among you who saw this house in its first glory? How does it look to you now? Doesn't it seem like nothing to you? 4 But now be strong, Zerubbabel, says the LORD. Be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. Be strong, all you people of the land, says the LORD. Get to work, for I am with you, says the LORD of Hosts. 5 I made this promise to you when you left Egypt. My Spirit is still with you. Do not be afraid."

6 The LORD of Hosts says, "In a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry land. 7 I will shake all the nations. The treasure all nations want will come. I will fill this house with glory. 8 The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine. 9 The glory of this house will be greater in the end than it was at first. In this place I will give peace," says the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD Teaches About Clean and Unclean

10 On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the LORD's message came to Haggai the prophet. 11 The LORD of Hosts says, "Ask the priests about the law. 12 If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his coat, and that fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any food, will that food become holy?"

The priests answered, "No."

13 Then Haggai said, "If someone who is unclean from touching a dead body touches any of these foods, will it become unclean?"

The priests answered, "It will become unclean."

14 Then Haggai answered, "So it is with these people and this nation before Me, says the LORD. So it is with every work of their hands. What they offer there is unclean."

15 "Now think about this from today back to before any stone was laid on another stone in the LORD's house. 16 Before that, when someone came to a pile of grain that should have been twenty baskets, there were only ten. When someone came to the wine vat to get fifty jugs, there were only twenty. 17 I struck you with disease, mold, and hail in all your work. But you still did not turn to Me, says the LORD."

18 "Think about this from today on, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the LORD's house was started. Think about it. 19 Is the seed still in the barn? The vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not given fruit yet. But from today on, I will bless you."

The LORD Promises to Shake the Nations

20 Again on the twenty-fourth day of the month, the LORD's message came to Haggai. He said, 21 "Tell Zerubbabel, the leader of Judah, 'I will shake the heavens and the earth.'"

22 "I will turn kingdoms upside down. I will destroy the power of the kingdoms of the nations. I will turn over the chariots and their riders. The horses and their riders will fall, each by his brother's sword."

23 "On that day, says the LORD of Hosts, I will take you, My servant Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, says the LORD. I will make you like My special ring, for I have chosen you, says the LORD of Hosts."
