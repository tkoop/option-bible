Honor Does Not Fit a Fool

1 Honor does not fit a fool. It is as odd as snow in summer or rain at harvest time. 2 A curse with no reason will not come true. It is like a bird that flies by and does not land. 3 A whip is for a horse, and a bit is for a donkey. A rod is for the back of a fool.

4 Do not answer a fool in his foolish way, or you will be like him. 5 Answer a fool in his foolish way, or he will think he is wise.

6 Sending a message with a fool is like hurting your own feet and drinking trouble. 7 A wise saying in the mouth of a fool is like the legs of a lame person that hang weak and useless. 8 Giving honor to a fool is like putting a stone in a sling. It does not help and can only cause harm. 9 A wise saying in the mouth of a fool is like a thorn in the hand of a drunk person.

Hiring a Fool

10 Hiring a fool or just anyone is like a person shooting arrows with no aim. It can hurt people.

Fools Repeat Mistakes

11 A dog goes back to what it threw up. In the same way, a fool goes back to his foolish ways. 12 Do you see a person who thinks he is wise? There is more hope for a fool than for him.

Lazy People

13 A lazy person says, "There is a lion in the street! There is a lion in the town square!" 14 A door swings back and forth. A lazy person just turns in his bed. 15 A lazy person puts his hand in the dish, but he is too tired to bring it back to his mouth. 16 A lazy person thinks he is wiser than seven people who give good answers.

Stay Out of Other People's Fights

17 Grabbing a dog by the ears is dangerous. So is jumping into someone else's fight.

Joking Can Hurt People

18 A crazy person who shoots fire and causes death is like 19 a person who tricks his neighbor and says, "I was only joking!"

Gossip Starts Fights

20 Without wood, a fire goes out. Without a gossip, a fight stops. 21 Charcoal keeps coals hot, and wood keeps a fire burning. In the same way, a person who likes to argue keeps fights going. 22 The words of a gossip are like tasty food. They go deep inside a person.

Hidden Hate

23 Sweet words with an evil heart are like shiny silver painted on a clay pot. 24 A person who hates hides it with his words and keeps lies hidden inside. 25 When he speaks kindly, do not believe him, because seven terrible things are in his heart. 26 His hate may be covered by lies, but his evil will be shown to everyone.

Traps You Set Can Hurt You

27 A person who digs a hole may fall into it. A person who rolls a stone may have it roll back on him. 28 A lying tongue crushes people, and the liar hates them. Smooth words bring trouble.
