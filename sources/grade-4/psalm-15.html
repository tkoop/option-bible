Who Can Live Near the LORD?

1 The LORD, who can stay in Your tent? Who can live on Your holy hill?

2 He does what is right. He lives the right way. He tells the truth from his heart.

3 He does not talk bad about people. He does not hurt his neighbor. He does not say mean things about his friends.

4 He does not respect bad people. He honors people who fear the LORD. He keeps his promise, even when it hurts.

5 He does not lend money to make extra money. He does not take money to hurt an innocent person. A person who does these things will never fall.
