The LORD Wants Right and Fair Living

1 This is what the LORD says: "Keep what is right and do what is fair. My saving help is coming soon. My right way will soon be seen." 2 Happy is the person who does this. Happy is the one who holds on to it. He keeps the Sabbath day and does not ruin it. He keeps his hands from doing bad.

The LORD Welcomes All People

3 A person from another land who joins the LORD must not say, "The LORD will keep me out of His people."

A man who cannot have children must not say, "I can never have children."

4 This is what the LORD says to the men who cannot have children: "If you keep My Sabbath days, choose what pleases Me, and hold on to My agreement, 5 I will give you a home in My house and a place inside My walls. I will give you a name better than sons and daughters. Your name will last forever. 6 Foreigners who join the LORD to serve Him, to love His name, and to be His servants must keep the Sabbath and not ruin it. They must hold on to My agreement. 7 I will bring them to My holy hill. I will make them happy in My house of prayer. The gifts they burn on the altar and their other gifts will be accepted. My house will be called a house of prayer for all people."

8 The Lord, the LORD, says this. He gathers the people of Israel who were sent away. He says, "I will gather even more people to them."

Bad Leaders Are Warned

9 Come, all you wild animals of the field. Come and eat. Come, all you wild animals of the forest. Come and eat. 10 Israel's guards are blind. They know nothing. They are like dogs that cannot bark. They lie down and dream. They like to sleep. 11 They are dogs with big appetites. They never have enough. They are shepherds who do not understand. They all go their own way. Each one wants more for himself. 12 They say, "Come, let us get wine. Let us drink strong drink. Tomorrow will be just like today, but even better."
