Greetings from James

1 James is a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ. He writes to the twelve tribes scattered among the nations. Greetings.

Joy in Hard Times

2 My brothers, be very happy when you face all kinds of troubles. 3 You know that when your faith is tested, it helps you learn to keep going. 4 Let that help you keep going until it is finished. Then you will be grown up and whole. You will not lack anything.

Ask God for Wisdom

5 If any of you needs wisdom, ask God. He gives to everyone freely and does not scold. He will give it to him. 6 But ask with faith. Do not doubt. A person who doubts is like a wave of the sea blown and tossed by the wind. 7 That person should not think he will get anything from the Lord. 8 He cannot make up his mind. He is not steady in anything he does.

Poor and Rich

9 A brother who is poor should be glad that God lifts him up. 10 A rich person should be glad that God humbles him. He will pass away like a wildflower. 11 The sun rises hot and dries up the grass. The flower falls off. Its beauty is gone. In the same way, rich people will fade away while they are busy.

Blessing for Those Who Keep Going

12 Happy is the person who keeps going when tested. After he passes the test, he will get the crown of life. God promised it to those who love Him.

Where Temptation Comes From

13 No one should say when tempted, "God is tempting me." God is not tempted by evil. He does not tempt anyone. 14 Each person is tempted when his own desire pulls him away and traps him. 15 Then desire grows and gives birth to sin. When sin is finished, it brings death.

Good Gifts from God

16 Do not be fooled, my dear brothers. 17 Every good gift and every perfect gift comes from above. It comes from the Father who made the lights. He does not change like shifting shadows. 18 He chose to give us new life through the true word. He made us the first and best of all He made.

Listen, Speak, and Get Angry Slowly

19 My dear brothers, know this: Everyone should listen quickly, speak slowly, and be slow to get angry. 20 A person's anger does not make things right with God.

Do What the Word Says

21 So throw away all dirty and evil things. Humbly accept the word planted in you. It can save your souls. 22 Do what the word says. Do not just listen. If you only listen, you fool yourselves.

Look in the Mirror

23 If someone listens to the word but does not do it, he is like a man looking at his face in a mirror. 24 He looks at himself, goes away, and right away forgets what he looked like.

The Perfect Law That Gives Freedom

25 But the person who looks into the perfect law that gives freedom and keeps at it is not a forgetful listener. He does what it says. He will be blessed in what he does.

True Religion

26 If someone thinks he is religious but cannot control his tongue, he tricks his own heart. His religion is useless. 27 Pure and clean religion before God the Father is this: Help orphans and widows in their trouble. Keep yourselves clean from the world.
