Jesus Is Tempted in the Desert

1 Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the desert to be tested by the devil. 2 He did not eat for forty days and forty nights. Then He was hungry.

3 The devil came to Him and said, "If You are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread."

4 Jesus answered, "It is written: 'People do not live on bread alone. They live on every word that comes from God's mouth.'"

5 Then the devil took Him to the holy city. He put Him on the highest part of the temple. 6 "If You are the Son of God," the devil said, "jump down. The Bible says, 'God will tell His angels to watch over You. They will hold You up with their hands, so You will not hit Your foot on a stone.'"

7 Jesus said, "It is also written, 'Do not test the Lord your God.'"

8 Again, the devil took Him to a very high mountain. He showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory. 9 "I will give all this to You," the devil said, "if You bow down and worship me."

10 Jesus said, "Go away, Satan! It is written, 'Worship the Lord your God, and serve Him only.'" 11 Then the devil left Him. Angels came and helped Him.

Jesus Begins His Work in Galilee

12 When Jesus heard that John had been put in jail, He went to Galilee. 13 He left Nazareth and went to live in Capernaum. That town is by the lake, in the area of Zebulun and Naphtali.

14 This happened to make true what the prophet Isaiah said: 15 "Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, by the sea, across the Jordan, Galilee where the nations live. 16 The people who sat in darkness have seen a great light. Light has come to those who sat in the land of the shadow of death."

17 From then on, Jesus began to preach. He said, "Turn from your sins, because God's kingdom is near."

Jesus Calls His First Helpers

18 Jesus was walking by the Sea of Galilee. He saw two brothers, Simon (called Peter) and his brother Andrew. They were throwing a net into the lake, because they were fishermen.

19 Jesus said to them, "Come, follow Me, and I will send you to fish for people."

20 Right away they left their nets and followed Him. 21 Jesus went on and saw two other brothers, James son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat with their father Zebedee, fixing their nets. Jesus called them. 22 They left the boat and their father right away and followed Him.

Jesus Teaches and Heals Many People

23 Jesus traveled all over Galilee. He taught in their meeting places. He told the good news about God's kingdom. He healed all kinds of sickness and disease among the people. 24 People all over Syria heard about Him. They brought to Him everyone who was sick. Some had many kinds of diseases. Some were in great pain. Some had demons. Some had seizures. Some could not walk. He healed them all. 25 Big crowds followed Him from Galilee, the Ten Towns, Jerusalem, Judea, and the land across the Jordan.
