Rules for Vows

1 The LORD said to Moses. 2 He said, "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: If you make a special promise to the LORD about a person's value, use these values."

Value of a Person

3 A man twenty to sixty years old is worth fifty silver coins. 4 A woman that age is worth thirty. 5 A boy five to twenty years old is worth twenty. A girl that age is worth ten. 6 A child from one month to five years old is worth five silver coins for a boy and three for a girl. 7 A man sixty or older is worth fifteen. A woman that age is worth ten. 8 If you are too poor to pay the set value, bring the person to the priest. The priest will set a value you can afford.

Animals That Are Vowed

9 If the promise is an animal that can be given to the LORD, the animal becomes holy. 10 Do not trade it for another animal. Do not swap a good one for a bad one, or a bad one for a good one. If you do swap, both animals become holy. 11 If the animal is not clean and cannot be given to the LORD, bring it to the priest. 12 The priest will decide if it is good or bad. The value he sets will stand. 13 If you want it back, pay the value plus one fifth more.

Houses

14 If a person gives a house to the LORD, the priest will decide its value. The value he sets will stand. 15 If you want the house back, pay the value plus one fifth more. Then it is yours again.

Fields

16 If you give part of your family land to the LORD, its value is based on how much seed it takes. It is worth fifty silver coins for each large basket of barley seed. 17 If you give the field in the Jubilee year, the value stays the same. 18 If you give it after the Jubilee year, the priest counts the years left until the next Jubilee year. The value goes down for those years.

19 If you want the field back, pay the value plus one fifth more. Then it is yours again. 20 If you do not buy it back, or if you sell it to someone else, it can never be bought back. 21 When the field is set free in the Jubilee year, it becomes holy, like a field given fully to the LORD. It becomes the priests' land.

22 If you give a field you bought, not family land, to the LORD, 23 the priest counts its value up to the Jubilee year, and you pay that value that day as holy to the LORD. 24 In the Jubilee year, the field goes back to the person it was bought from, the first owner of the land. 25 Every value uses the holy coin weight: twenty small coins make one silver coin.

Firstborn Animals

26 No one may give the firstborn of an animal, because the firstborn already belongs to the LORD. An ox or a sheep belongs to the LORD. 27 If the animal is not clean, it can be bought back at its set value plus one fifth. If you do not buy it back, it is sold at its set value.

Things Fully Given to God

28 But nothing a person owns and fully gives to the LORD can be sold or bought back. This includes a person, an animal, or family land. Everything fully given is most holy to the LORD. 29 No person who is fully given to be destroyed can be bought back. That person must be put to death.

Tithes

30 A tenth of everything from the land belongs to the LORD. This includes grain from the ground and fruit from the trees. It is holy to the LORD. 31 If you want to buy back a tenth, pay its value plus one fifth more. 32 Every tenth animal from the herd or flock is holy to the LORD. It is every tenth animal that passes under the rod. 33 Do not pick out the good from the bad, and do not swap animals. If you swap, both the animal and the one swapped for it become holy. They cannot be bought back.

All These Commands

34 These are the commands the LORD gave Moses for the Israelites at Mount Sinai.
