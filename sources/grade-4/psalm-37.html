Do Not Worry About Bad People

1 Do not get mad at bad people. Do not wish you were like those who do wrong. 2 They fade fast, like grass. They dry up like green plants.

Trust the LORD and Do Good

3 Trust in the LORD and do good. Live in the land and be faithful. 4 Enjoy the LORD, and He will give you what your heart wants. 5 Give your way to the LORD. Trust Him, and He will do it. 6 He will make your good shine like the morning sun. He will make what is right shine like noon.

Wait for the LORD

7 Be still before the LORD and wait for Him. Do not get upset when bad people do well. Do not worry about people who make bad plans. 8 Stop being angry. Let go of rage. Do not get upset. It leads to evil.

Bad People Will Fade Away

9 Bad people will be cut off. But people who hope in the LORD will get the land. 10 In a little while, the bad person will be gone. You may look for him, but he will not be there. 11 But gentle people will get the land. They will enjoy peace and plenty.

12 Bad people plan harm against good people. They grind their teeth at them. 13 But the Lord laughs at them, because He sees their day is coming. 14 Bad people pull out swords and bend bows. They want to hurt poor and needy people. They want to kill people who do right. 15 But their swords will stab their own hearts. Their bows will break.

Good People Are Better Off

16 A little that a good person has is better than the riches of many bad people. 17 The arms of bad people will be broken. But the LORD holds up good people.

The LORD Cares for Good People

18 The LORD knows the days of good people. Their land will last forever. 19 In hard times they will not be ashamed. In days with no food, they will have enough. 20 Bad people will die. The LORD's enemies will fade like grass. They will be gone like smoke.

21 Bad people borrow and do not pay back. Good people are kind and give. 22 People the LORD blesses will get the land. People He curses will be cut off.

The LORD Guides Good People

23 The LORD makes a person's steps steady. He is glad in the way that person goes. 24 If he falls, he will not be thrown down. The LORD holds his hand. 25 I was young, and now I am old. I have never seen good people left alone. I have never seen their children begging for bread. 26 They are always kind and lending. Their children are a blessing.

Live the Right Way

27 Turn from evil and do good. Then you will live in the land forever. 28 The LORD loves what is right. He will not leave His faithful people. They are kept safe forever. But the children of bad people will be cut off. 29 Good people will get the land. They will live there forever.

30 A good person's mouth speaks wisdom. his tongue speaks what is right. 31 God's law is in his heart. his steps will not slip.

Bad People Cannot Win

32 Bad people watch good people. They want to kill them. 33 But the LORD will not leave good people in their power. He will not let them be found guilty when they are judged. 34 Wait for the LORD. Keep His way. He will lift you up to get the land. You will see bad people cut off.

35 I saw a bad, scary person. he grew strong like a tree with deep roots. 36 But he passed away and was gone. I looked for him, but I could not find him.

Look at Good People

37 Look at good people. See people who do right. People of peace have a good future. 38 But rebels will be destroyed together. The future of bad people will be cut off.

The LORD Saves His People

39 Good people are saved by the LORD. He is their strong place in trouble. 40 The LORD helps them and frees them. He saves them from bad people, because they trust in Him.
