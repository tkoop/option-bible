The LORD Speaks Against Israel's Mountains

1 The word of the LORD came to me. 2 He said, "Son of man, look toward the mountains of Israel and speak against them. 3 Say, 'Mountains of Israel, listen to the word of the Lord the LORD. This is what the Lord the LORD says to the mountains and hills, to the valleys and low places: I will bring war against you, and I will destroy the places where you worship false gods. 4 Your altars will become empty ruins. Your places for burning incense will be broken. I will throw your dead bodies down in front of your false gods. 5 I will put the dead bodies of Israel's people in front of their false gods. I will scatter your bones around your altars. 6 Wherever you live, your cities will be destroyed and your hilltop worship places ruined. Your altars will be destroyed and ruined. Your false gods will be broken and gone. Your places for burning incense will be cut down. The things you made will be wiped out. 7 Dead people will fall among you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'

8 "But I will let some of you live. Some of you will escape being killed in war and live among other nations. You will be scattered through many countries. 9 Then the ones who escape will remember Me among the nations where they are taken. They will remember how I was hurt because their hearts left Me and followed false gods with their eyes. They will feel sick about the bad things they did and all their terrible sins. 10 Then they will know that I am the LORD. I did not say this for nothing. I really will bring this trouble on them."

The LORD Warns of More Trouble

11 This is what the Lord the LORD says: "Clap your hands and stamp your foot and say, 'Oh no! Because of all the evil, terrible sins of the people of Israel, they will fall by war, by hunger, and by sickness.' 12 People far away will die from sickness. People close by will be killed in war. People who are left and trapped will die from hunger. In this way, I will use up My anger on them. 13 Then you will know that I am the LORD. You will see their dead bodies lying among their false gods and around their altars. This will happen on every high hill, on every mountaintop, under every green tree, and under every leafy oak. It will happen wherever they burned sweet-smelling offerings to their false gods. 14 I will reach out My hand against them. I will make the land empty and ruined, from the desert to Diblah. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
