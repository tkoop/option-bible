David Praises God

1 I will lift You up, my God and King. I will praise Your name forever and ever. 2 Every day I will praise You. I will praise Your name forever and ever. 3 The LORD is great. People should praise Him very much. No one can understand how great He is.

4 One group of people will tell the next group about Your works. They will tell about Your strong acts. 5 They will talk about how great and beautiful You are. I will think about Your amazing works. 6 They will tell how strong Your amazing deeds are. I will tell about Your greatness. 7 They will share the memory of Your great kindness. They will sing with joy about Your good ways.

The LORD Is Kind and Good

8 The LORD is kind and caring. He is slow to get angry. His love is very big. 9 The LORD is good to everyone. He is kind to everything He made.

People Praise God's Kingdom

10 Everything You made will thank You, the LORD. Your faithful people will praise You. 11 They will tell about how great Your kingdom is. They will talk about Your power. 12 They will help people know Your strong acts and how great Your kingdom is. 13 Your kingdom lasts forever. Your rule lasts for all time.

God Helps and Feeds People

14 The LORD holds up everyone who falls. He lifts up everyone who is bent down. 15 All eyes look to You. You give them food at the right time. 16 You open Your hand. You give every living thing all it needs.

The LORD Is Fair and Near

17 The LORD is right in everything He does. He is kind in all His work. 18 The LORD is close to everyone who calls on Him. He is close to all who call on Him and mean it. 19 He gives those who respect Him what they need. He hears their cry and saves them. 20 The LORD keeps safe all who love Him. But He will destroy all evil people. 21 My mouth will speak praise to the LORD. Let all people bless His holy name forever and ever.
