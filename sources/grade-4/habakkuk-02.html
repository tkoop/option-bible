Habakkuk Waits to Hear from God

1 I will stand at my watch. I will stay on the wall. I will look to see what He will say to me. I will see how I should answer my complaint.

The Lord Answers Habakkuk

2 The LORD answered me. He said, “Write down the vision. Make it clear on tablets. Then a runner can read it as he runs. 3 The vision will wait for its time. It tells about the end. It will not lie. If it is slow, wait for it. It will come for sure. It will not be late. 4 See, he is proud. He is not right inside. But a good person will live by faith.

The Proud Ruler Will Be Judged

5 “Wine tricks him. He is proud and never rests. He is as greedy as the grave. Death is never full. He gathers all nations to himself. He collects all peoples.

Trouble for the Greedy

6 “Will not all these people make fun of him with mocking words? They will say that trouble is coming to the one who takes things that are not his. They will ask how long he will pile them up. They will say he loads himself with debts. 7 Will not the people you owe money to rise up suddenly? Then you will be afraid. They will take your things. 8 You took things from many nations. So the people left will take things from you. This is because you killed people and hurt the land, the city, and the people who live there.

Trouble for the Violent

9 “Trouble is coming to the one who builds his house with stolen gain and puts his home high up to be safe from trouble. 10 You planned shame for your house when you hurt many peoples. You have sinned against your own life. 11 The stones in the wall will cry out. The wooden beams will answer them.

Trouble for Builders of Bloodshed

12 “Trouble is coming to the one who builds a city with blood and starts a town with wrong. 13 Look, the the LORD of Armies has said this: people work only to feed the fire. Nations tire themselves out for nothing. 14 The earth will be full of people knowing the the LORD's glory. It will be like water covering the sea.

Trouble for Those Who Make Others Drunk

15 “Trouble is coming to the one who makes his neighbor drink. He pours out his anger and gets them drunk. Then he looks at their shame. 16 You will be full of shame, not glory. You will drink too, and show your shame. The LORD's cup of punishment will come to you. Your glory will be covered with shame. 17 The harm you did to Lebanon will cover you. The hurt to the animals will scare you. This is because you killed people and hurt the land, the city, and the people who live there.

Idols Are Useless

18 “What good is an idol? The one who made it shaped it. It is a metal statue that teaches lies. The maker trusts in what he made. But the idol cannot speak. 19 Trouble is coming to the one who tells wood to wake up and a silent stone to get up. Can it teach? Look, it is covered with gold and silver. But it has no breath in it.

The Lord Is in His Temple

20 “But the the LORD is in His holy temple. Let all the earth be quiet before Him.”
