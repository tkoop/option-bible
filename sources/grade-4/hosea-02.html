God Tells His Children to Call Each Other His People

1 "Tell your brothers, 'You are My people.' Tell your sisters, 'You are shown mercy.'"

Israel Was Not Faithful

2 "Argue with your mother. Argue with her. She is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Tell her to stop looking and acting like a prostitute. Tell her to stop cheating. 3 If she does not stop, I will take off her clothes. I will leave her bare, like the day she was born. I will make her like an empty desert. I will make her like dry land. I will let her die of thirst. 4 I will not show mercy to her children, because they are children born from cheating. 5 Their mother was not faithful. The one who had them acted in a shameful way. She said, 'I will follow my lovers. They give me my bread and water. They give me my wool and cloth. They give me my oil and drinks.'"

6 "So I will block her path with thorns. I will build a wall so she cannot find her way. 7 She will chase her lovers, but she will not catch them. She will look for them, but she will not find them. Then she will say, 'I will go back to my first husband. Things were better for me then.'"

God Will Take Back His Gifts

8 "She did not know that I gave her grain, new wine, and oil. I gave her much silver and gold. But she used them to honor Baal. 9 So I will take back My grain when it is time. I will take back My new wine when it is ready. I will take away the wool and cloth that cover her. 10 Now I will show her shame to her lovers. No one will save her from My hand. 11 I will stop all her joy. I will stop her feasts, her new moon parties, and her Sabbath days. I will stop all her special days."

God Will Punish Israel

12 "I will ruin her grapevines and fig trees. She said, 'These are gifts my lovers gave me.' I will turn them into a forest. Wild animals will eat them. 13 I will punish her for the days she worshiped Baal. She burned incense to them. She put on rings and jewels. She went after her lovers and forgot Me, says the the LORD."

God Will Bring Israel Back

14 "So I will win her back. I will lead her into the desert. I will speak kindly to her. 15 There I will give her vineyards. I will make the Valley of Trouble a door of hope. There she will answer Me like when she was young. It will be like the day she left Egypt. 16 In that day, says the the LORD, you will call Me 'my Husband.' You will not call Me 'my Baal' anymore. 17 I will take the names of the Baals out of her mouth. No one will remember those names again."

God Will Make Peace

18 "On that day I will make a promise with the wild animals, the birds, and the small animals that crawl. I will take away bows, swords, and war from the land. I will let them rest safely."

God Will Marry Israel Again

19 "I will promise to marry you forever. I will promise to marry you with what is right and fair. I will do it with love and mercy. 20 I will promise to marry you with faithfulness. Then you will know the the LORD."

God Will Bless the Land

21 "In that day, says the the LORD, I will answer. I will answer the sky, and the sky will answer the earth. 22 The earth will give grain, wine, and oil. They will give them to Jezreel. 23 I will plant her for Myself in the land. I will show mercy to the one called 'No Mercy.' I will say to the one called 'Not My People,' 'You are My people.' And he will say, 'You are my God.'"
