Wise and Foolish People

1 A man who is stubborn and will not take advice will suddenly be destroyed, and no one can help him. 2 When good people do well, everyone is happy. When bad people rule, people are sad and complain. 3 A man who loves wisdom makes his father glad. But a man who spends time with bad women wastes his money. 4 A fair king makes the land strong and steady. But a man who takes extra money from people tears it down. 5 A man who flatters his neighbor sets a trap for him. 6 A bad man gets caught in his own sin. But a good man can sing and be happy. 7 A good man thinks about what poor people need. A bad man does not understand. 8 People who make fun of others stir up a city. Wise people calm anger. 9 If a wise man goes to court with a fool, the fool gets mad and laughs, and there is no peace. 10 Men who want to hurt others hate an honest man. They try to kill good people. 11 A fool lets out all his anger. A wise man holds it back. 12 If a ruler listens to lies, all his helpers are bad.

Poor People and Rulers

13 Poor people and mean people have one thing in common: the LORD gives life to both. 14 A king who judges poor people fairly will have a throne that lasts forever. 15 Discipline and correction give wisdom. But a child who is left alone shames his mother. 16 When bad people grow strong, sin grows too. But good people will see them fall.

Teach Your Children

17 Discipline your son, and he will give you rest. He will make you happy. 18 Without God's guidance, people run wild. But people who keep His law are happy. 19 A servant cannot be corrected by words alone. Even if he understands, he will not answer. 20 Do you see a man who speaks too fast? There is more hope for a fool than for him. 21 A servant who is spoiled from youth will cause trouble later.

Trust in the LORD

22 An angry man stirs up fights. A hot-tempered man does many wrong things. 23 A man's pride will bring him down. But a humble person will be honored. 24 A man who helps a thief puts his own life in danger. He hears the curse but does not tell. 25 Being afraid of people is a trap. But a person who trusts in the LORD is safe. 26 Many people want a leader to like them. But true justice for a person comes from the LORD. 27 Good people cannot stand a bad man. Bad people cannot stand a good man.
