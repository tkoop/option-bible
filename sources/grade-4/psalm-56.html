David Prays for Help

For the music leader. To the tune of "The Dove on Distant Trees." A song by David. This was when the Philistines caught him in Gath.

1 God, be kind to me. People hunt me down. They attack me all day. 2 My enemies hunt me all day. Many proud people attack me.

3 When I am afraid, I will trust in You. 4 I praise God's word. I trust in God. I will not be afraid. What can people do to me?

5 All day they twist my words. They plan to hurt me. 6 They team up and hide. They watch my steps. They wait to take my life.

7 Will they get away with their sin? God, in Your anger, bring down the nations.

8 You count my steps as I wander. Put my tears in Your bottle. Are they not in Your book?

9 Then my enemies will turn back when I call. I know this: God is for me. 10 I praise God's word. I praise the LORD's word. 11 I trust in God. I will not be afraid. What can people do to me?

12 God, I have promised things to You. I will give You thanks. 13 You saved my life from death. You kept my feet from falling. Now I can walk before God in the light of life.
