David Becomes King

1 All Israel came together to David at Hebron. They said, "Look, we are your own family. 2 Even when Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. The the LORD your God said to you, 'You will take care of My people Israel. You will be the leader over My people Israel.'"

3 All the leaders of Israel came to King David at Hebron. David made an agreement with them before the the LORD. They poured oil on David to make him king over Israel. This happened just as the the LORD had said through Samuel.

David Takes Jerusalem

4 David and all Israel went to Jerusalem. It was called Jebus then. The Jebusites lived there. 5 The people living there said to David, "You will not come in here." But David took the strong place of Zion. That place is called the City of David.

6 David said, "The first man to hit the Jebusites will be the leader and commander." Joab, son of Zeruiah, went up first. So he became the leader. 7 David lived in the strong place. That is why people call it the City of David.

8 David built the city all around. He built from the Millo to the wall around it. Joab fixed the rest of the city.

9 David became greater and greater. The LORD of armies was with him.

David's Brave Men

10 These were the leaders of David's brave men. They helped make him strong in his kingdom. All Israel helped make him king. This happened as the the LORD had said about Israel.

11 This is how many brave men David had. Jashobeam, son of Hachmoni, was leader of the thirty. He raised his spear and killed three hundred men at one time.

12 After him came Eleazar, son of Dodo. He was from Ahoah. He was one of the three brave men.

13 He was with David at Pas-dammim. The Philistines gathered there for war. The field was full of barley. The people ran away from the Philistines. 14 But they stood in the middle of the field. They saved it. They hit the Philistines. The LORD helped them win a great battle.

15 Three of the thirty leaders went down to David. He was at the rock by the cave of Adullam. The Philistine army was camped in the Valley of Rephaim. 16 David was in the strong place. The Philistines had soldiers in Bethlehem.

David Wants Water from Bethlehem

17 David wanted water. He said, "I wish someone would give me water to drink from the well in Bethlehem by the gate!"

18 So the three men broke through the Philistine camp. They got water from the well in Bethlehem by the gate. They carried it to David. But David would not drink it. He poured it out to the the LORD.

19 David said, "No! I will not do that. My God, it would be wrong. This water is like the blood of these men. They risked their lives to bring it." So he would not drink it. These three brave men did this.

20 Abishai, brother of Joab, was leader of the three. He raised his spear and killed three hundred men. He was famous among the three.

21 He was honored more than the thirty. He became their leader. But he was not one of the three.

22 Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, was a brave man from Kabzeel. He did great things. He killed two sons of Ariel from Moab. He also went down into a pit on a snowy day and killed a lion.

23 He killed a big Egyptian man. The Egyptian was about seven and a half feet tall. He had a spear like a weaver's beam. Benaiah went down to him with a stick. He took the spear from the Egyptian's hand. Then he killed the Egyptian with his own spear.

24 Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, did these things. He was famous among the three brave men. 25 He was honored more than the thirty. But he was not one of the three. David put him in charge of his guards.

More Brave Men

26 Here are other brave soldiers: Asahel, brother of Joab; Elhanan, son of Dodo, from Bethlehem; 27 Shammoth the Harorite; Helez the Pelonite; 28 Ira, son of Ikkesh, from Tekoa; Abiezer from Anathoth; 29 Sibbecai the Hushathite; Ilai the Ahohite; 30 Maharai from Netophah; Heled, son of Baanah, from Netophah; 31 Ithai, son of Ribai, from Gibeah of the Benjaminites; Benaiah from Pirathon.

32 Hurai from the valleys of Gaash; Abiel the Arbathite; 33 Azmaveth from Bahurim; Eliahba from Shaalbon; 34 the sons of Hashem from Gizon; Jonathan, son of Shagee, the Hararite; 35 Ahiam, son of Sacar, the Hararite; Eliphal, son of Ur; 36 Hepher from Mecherah; Ahijah the Pelonite; 37 Hezro from Carmel; Naarai, son of Ezbai.

38 Joel, brother of Nathan; Mibhar, son of Hagri; 39 Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai from Beeroth, who carried Joab's armor; 40 Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite; 41 Uriah the Hittite; Zabad, son of Ahlai; 42 Adina, son of Shiza, from Reuben, leader of the Reubenites, with thirty men with him; 43 Hanan, son of Maacah; Joshaphat the Mithnite.

44 Uzzia from Ashterath; Shama and Jeiel, sons of Hotham from Aroer; 45 Jediael, son of Shimri; his brother Joha, the Tizite; 46 Eliel from Mahavah; Jeribai and Joshaviah, sons of Elnaam; Ithmah the Moabite; 47 Eliel; Obed; and Jaasiel from Mezobah.
