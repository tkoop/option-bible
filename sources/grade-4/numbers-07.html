Moses Sets Up the Meeting Tent

1 On the day Moses finished setting up the meeting tent, he poured oil on it and made it holy. He did the same to all its things, the altar, and all the altar's things.

Gifts from the Leaders

2 The leaders of Israel brought gifts. They were the heads of their families. They were the leaders of the tribes who counted the people. 3 They brought their gifts to the LORD. They brought six covered carts and twelve oxen. Two leaders shared one cart. Each leader gave one ox. They brought them to the front of the meeting tent.

The LORD Speaks to Moses

4 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 5 "Take these gifts from them. Use them to work at the tent of meeting. Give them to the Levites. Give each man what his work needs."

6 Moses took the carts and oxen. He gave them to the Levites. 7 He gave two carts and four oxen to the sons of Gershon. He gave them what their work needed. 8 He gave four carts and eight oxen to the sons of Merari. Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, was in charge of them. He gave them what their work needed. 9 He gave none to the sons of Kohath. Their work was holy. They carried the holy things on their shoulders.

Gifts for the Altar

10 The leaders gave gifts for the altar on the day it was anointed. They brought their gifts before the altar. 11 The LORD said to Moses, "One leader will bring his gift each day. They will give gifts for the altar's special use."

Day One: Judah

12 On the first day, Nahshon son of Amminadab brought his gift. He was the leader of Judah. 13 His gift was one silver plate. It weighed 130 shekels. He also gave one silver bowl. It weighed 70 shekels. Both were by the holy shekel. They were full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain gift. 14 He gave one gold dish. It weighed 10 shekels. It was full of incense. 15 He gave one young bull, one ram, and one male lamb one year old. These were for a burnt gift. 16 He gave one male goat for a sin gift. 17 For a peace gift, he gave two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs one year old. This was the gift of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

All the Gifts Together

84 This is what the leaders of Israel gave when the altar was set apart. On the day it was anointed, twelve leaders gave gifts. They gave twelve silver plates, twelve silver bowls, and twelve gold dishes. 85 Each silver plate weighed 130 shekels. Each silver bowl weighed 70 shekels. All the silver things weighed 2,400 shekels by the holy shekel. 86 Each gold dish weighed 10 shekels. All the gold dishes weighed 120 shekels. 87 For the burnt gift, they gave twelve young bulls, twelve rams, and twelve male lambs one year old. They also gave grain gifts for them. They gave twelve male goats for sin gifts. 88 For peace gifts, they gave twenty-four oxen, sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty male lambs one year old. This was the gift for the altar after it was anointed.

The LORD Speaks from the Meeting Tent

89 When Moses went into the meeting tent to speak with the LORD, he heard the voice speaking to him. The voice came from above the cover, between the two winged angels, on the ark of the law. The LORD spoke to him.
