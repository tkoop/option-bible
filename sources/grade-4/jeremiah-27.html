Jeremiah Wears a Yoke

1 At the start of King Jehoiakim's rule, this message came to Jeremiah from the LORD. He said, 2 "Make a wooden yoke with straps, and put it on your neck."

A Message for the Kings

3 Send these yokes to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon. Their messengers are coming to Jerusalem to see King Zedekiah of Judah. 4 Tell them, "The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Give this message to your masters: 5 I made the earth, the people, and the animals on it. I did this with My great power and strong arm. I give it to anyone I choose. 6 Now I have given all these lands to My servant, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon. I have even given him the wild animals to serve him. 7 All nations will serve him, and then his son, and then his grandson. This will last until the time for his own land comes. Then many nations and great kings will make him serve them. 8 Any nation or kingdom that will not serve King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon and will not put its neck under his yoke, I will punish with war, hunger, and sickness, says the LORD. I will destroy them by his power."

Do Not Listen to False Prophets

9 So do not listen to your prophets, fortune-tellers, dreamers, magic workers, or sorcerers. They tell you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon." 10 They are telling you lies. They want to take you far from your land. I will drive you out, and you will be destroyed. 11 But any nation that puts its neck under the king of Babylon's yoke and serves him, I will let stay in its own land, says the LORD. They can farm it and live there.

12 I also spoke to Zedekiah, king of Judah. I said, "Put your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon. Serve him and his people, and you will live." 13 Why should you and your people die by war, hunger, and sickness? The LORD has said this will happen to any nation that will not serve the king of Babylon. 14 Do not listen to the prophets who tell you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon." They are lying to you. 15 I did not send them, says the LORD. They are telling lies in My name. They want to drive you out, and you will be destroyed. You and the prophets who speak to you will be destroyed.

16 I also spoke to the priests and all the people. I said, "The LORD says: Do not listen to your prophets. They tell you that soon the things from the LORD's temple will be brought back from Babylon. They are lying to you. 17 Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon, and you will live. Why should this city be destroyed?"

A Test for the Prophets

18 If they are prophets, and if they really have the LORD's word, let them ask the LORD of Hosts not to let the rest of the holy things in the LORD's temple, the king's house, and Jerusalem be taken to Babylon.

What Will Happen to the Temple Things

19 The LORD of Hosts says this about the pillars, the big bowl, the stands, and the rest of the things left in this city. 20 King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon did not take them when he took King Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim of Judah from Jerusalem to Babylon, along with all the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem. 21 The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says this about the things left in the LORD's temple and in the king's house in Jerusalem: 22 They will be carried to Babylon and stay there until I come for them, says the LORD. Then I will bring them back to this place.
