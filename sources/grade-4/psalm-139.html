God Knows Everything About Me

1 The LORD, You looked deep into me. You know me. 2 You know when I sit down and when I get up. You know what I am thinking, even from far away. 3 You see where I go and where I rest. You know all the things I do. 4 The LORD, before I say a word, You know it all. 5 You go before me and behind me. You put Your hand on me. 6 That knowledge is too wonderful for me. I cannot reach it.

God Is Everywhere

7 Where can I go to hide from Your Spirit? Where can I run from You? 8 If I go up to heaven, You are there. If I lie down in the deepest place, You are there. 9 If I travel with the sunrise, or live far across the sea, 10 even there Your hand will lead me. Your strong hand will hold me. 11 If I say, "The dark will hide me, and the light will turn to night around me," 12 even the dark is not dark to You. The night shines like day, because dark and light are the same to You.

God Made Me

13 You made my inner self. You put me together in my mother's womb. 14 I praise You because You made me in an amazing way. Your works are amazing, and I know it well. 15 My body was not hidden from You when I was made in secret. You made me deep in the earth. 16 Your eyes saw me before I was formed. All my days were written in Your book before even one of them happened.

God's Thoughts Are Special

17 God, Your thoughts are so special to me! There are so many of them! 18 If I tried to count them, they would be more than the sand. When I wake up, I am still with You.

I Hate Evil

19 God, I wish You would kill the bad people! Get away from me, you killers! 20 They say bad things about You. Your enemies use Your name in a wrong way. 21 The LORD, I hate those who hate You. I hate those who fight against You. 22 I hate them completely. I call them my enemies.

Search My Heart

23 Search me, God, and know my heart. Test me and know what worries me. 24 See if I am doing wrong, and lead me on the right path forever.
