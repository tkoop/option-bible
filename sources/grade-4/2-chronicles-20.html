Enemies Come to Fight Jehoshaphat

1 After this, the people of Moab and Ammon came, along with some Meunites, to make war against Jehoshaphat. 2 Some men came and told Jehoshaphat, "A big army is coming against you from Aram, from across the sea. They are already at Hazazon-tamar, which is En-gedi."

Jehoshaphat Prays for Help

3 Jehoshaphat was afraid. He decided to ask the LORD for help. He told all Judah to go without food. 4 People from all the towns of Judah came together to ask the LORD for help.

5 Jehoshaphat stood up in the crowd of Judah and Jerusalem in the LORD's house. He was in the new courtyard. 6 He said, "LORD, God of our fathers, are You not God in heaven? You rule all the kingdoms of the nations. Power and strength are in Your hand. No one can stand against You. 7 Our God, did You not drive out the people who lived in this land before Your people Israel? You gave it to the children of Abraham, Your friend, for all time. 8 They lived there and built a holy place for Your name. They said, 9 'If trouble comes on us—sword, punishment, sickness, or hunger—we will stand before this house and before You, because Your name is in this house. We will cry to You in our trouble, and You will hear and save us.' 10 Now look! The men of Ammon and Moab and Mount Seir are here. You did not let Israel enter their land when Israel came from Egypt. Israel turned away from them and did not destroy them. 11 See how they pay us back. They come to drive us out of the land You gave us. 12 Our God, will You not judge them? We have no strength against this big army that comes against us. We do not know what to do. But our eyes are on You."

13 All Judah stood before the LORD, with their wives and children and little ones.

God Answers Through Jahaziel

14 Then the Spirit of the LORD came on Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah. He was a Levite from Asaph's family. He was in the middle of the crowd. 15 He said, "Listen, all Judah and people of Jerusalem and King Jehoshaphat! The LORD says to you, 'Do not be afraid. Do not be scared of this big army. The battle is not yours. It is God's.' 16 Tomorrow go down against them. They are coming up by the hill of Ziz. You will find them at the end of the valley, before the desert of Jeruel. 17 You do not need to fight in this battle. Stand still and see how the LORD saves you. People of Judah and Jerusalem, do not be afraid. Do not be scared. Go out against them tomorrow, and the LORD will be with you."

18 Jehoshaphat bowed low with his face to the ground. All Judah and the people of Jerusalem fell before the LORD to worship Him. 19 Then Levites from the Kohathites and Korahites stood up to praise the LORD, the God of Israel, with a very loud voice.

Judah Goes Out to Battle

20 Early in the morning they got up and went out to the desert of Tekoa. As they left, Jehoshaphat stood and said, "Listen to me, people of Judah and Jerusalem! Trust in the LORD your God, and you will be safe. Trust His prophets, and you will do well."

21 After asking the people, he chose singers for the LORD. They praised the LORD for His holy beauty. They went out ahead of the army and said, "Give thanks to the LORD, for His love lasts forever."

22 When they began to sing and praise, the LORD set ambushes against Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, who had come against Judah. They were defeated. 23 The men of Ammon and Moab turned against the people of Mount Seir. They destroyed them completely. When they finished with Seir, they turned on each other and destroyed themselves. 24 When Judah came to the watchtower in the desert, they looked at the army. They saw dead bodies on the ground. No one had escaped.

25 Jehoshaphat and his people came to take the goods. They found many bodies and valuable things. They took so much that they could not carry it all. They spent three days taking the goods, because there was so much. 26 On the fourth day they met in the Valley of Blessing. There they praised the LORD. That is why the place is called the Valley of Blessing to this day.

27 Then all the men of Judah and Jerusalem went back. Jehoshaphat led them. They were happy as they returned to Jerusalem, because the LORD had made them glad over their enemies. 28 They came to Jerusalem, to the house of the LORD, with harps, lyres, and trumpets.

29 Fear of God came on all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against Israel's enemies. 30 Then Jehoshaphat's kingdom was at peace. His God gave him rest on every side.

Jehoshaphat's Later Years

31 Jehoshaphat ruled over Judah. He was 35 years old when he became king. He ruled 25 years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. 32 He followed the ways of his father Asa. He did not turn from them. He did what was right in the LORD's eyes. 33 But the high places were not taken away. The people still had not set their hearts on the God of their fathers. 34 The rest of Jehoshaphat's deeds, first and last, are written in the book of Jehu son of Hanani. That book is mentioned in the book of the kings of Israel.

35 Later, Jehoshaphat king of Judah joined with Ahaziah king of Israel. Ahaziah did many bad things. 36 They agreed to build ships to go to Tarshish. They built the ships in Ezion-geber. 37 Eliezer son of Dodavahu from Mareshah spoke against Jehoshaphat. He said, "Because you joined with Ahaziah, the LORD will break up what you have made." The ships were wrecked and could not sail to Tarshish.
