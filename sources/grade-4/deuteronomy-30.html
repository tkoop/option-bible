God Will Bring His People Back

1 When all these things happen to you—the good and the bad that I put before you—and you think about them in all the nations where the LORD your God sends you, 2 and you and your children turn back to the LORD your God and listen to His voice with all your heart and all your soul, doing all I tell you today, 3 then the LORD your God will bring you back from being held captive. He will be kind to you. He will gather you from all the nations where the LORD your God sent you. 4 Even if you are sent to the far edge of the sky, the LORD your God will gather you and bring you back from there. 5 The LORD your God will bring you to the land your fathers owned. You will own it. He will make you do well and make you many more than your fathers.

God Will Change Hearts

6 The LORD your God will change your heart and your children's hearts. Then you can love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, so you can live. 7 The LORD your God will put all these bad things on your enemies, on those who hate you and chase you. 8 You will turn back and listen to the LORD's voice. You will do all His commands that I give you today. 9 The LORD your God will help you do well in all your work. He will bless the children you have, your animals, and your crops. The LORD will be happy to do good to you again, like He was happy to do good to your fathers. 10 This will happen if you listen to the LORD your God and keep His commands and rules written in this Book of the Law. It will happen if you turn back to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.

God's Word Is Near

11 The command I give you today is not too hard for you. It is not far away. 12 It is not up in heaven. You should not say, "Who will go up to heaven to get it for us and tell it to us, so we can do it?" 13 It is not across the sea. You should not say, "Who will cross the sea to get it for us and tell it to us, so we can do it?" 14 The word is very close to you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so you can do it.

Choose Life

15 Look, today I put before you life and good, and death and bad. 16 I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commands, rules, and laws. Then you will live and grow. The LORD your God will bless you in the land you are going to take. 17 But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are pulled away to bow down to other gods and serve them, 18 I tell you today that you will be destroyed. You will not live a long time in the land you are crossing the Jordan to take. 19 I call heaven and earth to speak against you today. I have put before you life and death, good and bad. So choose life, so you and your children can live. 20 Love the LORD your God. Listen to His voice. Stay close to Him. He is your life. He will give you many years in the land the LORD your God promised to give your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
