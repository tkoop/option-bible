Saul Wants David Dead

1 Saul told his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David. But Jonathan loved David very much.

Jonathan Helps David

2 Jonathan told David, "My father Saul wants to kill you. Be careful tomorrow morning. Stay in a secret place and hide." 3 "I will go out and stand by my father in the field where you are. I will talk to my father about you. Then I will see what he says and tell you."

Jonathan Speaks to Saul

4 Jonathan spoke well of David to Saul his father. He said, "King, do not do wrong to your servant David. He has not done wrong to you. His work has been very good for you. 5 He risked his life and killed the Philistine. The LORD gave a great win to all Israel. You saw it and were glad. Why do wrong and kill a man who did no wrong? Why kill David for no reason?"

Saul Makes a Promise

6 Saul listened to Jonathan and made a promise. He said, "As surely as the LORD lives, David will not be killed." 7 Jonathan called David and told him all this. Then Jonathan brought David to Saul. David was with Saul like before.

David Fights Again

8 Later, war came again. David went out and fought the Philistines. He hit them hard, and they ran away.

Saul Tries to Hurt David

9 Saul was sitting in his house with his spear in his hand. An evil spirit from the LORD came on Saul. David was playing music. 10 Saul tried to pin David to the wall with his spear. David moved away, and the spear hit the wall. David ran away and escaped that night.

Michal Helps David Run

11 Saul sent men to watch David's house. They were to kill him in the morning. Michal told David, "If you do not get away tonight, you will be killed tomorrow." 12 Michal helped David down through a window. He left and ran away. He got away. 13 Michal took a statue and put it in the bed. She put goat hair at the head. She covered it with clothes.

14 Saul sent men to take David. But Michal said, "He is sick."

15 Saul sent men to see David. He said, "Bring him up to me in the bed, so I can kill him." 16 The men went in. They saw the statue in the bed. It had goat hair at the head.

17 Saul said to Michal, "Why did you trick me? You let my enemy go, and he got away." Michal said to Saul, "He told me, 'Let me go. Why should I kill you?'"

David Goes to Samuel

18 David ran away and got away. He went to Samuel at Ramah. He told Samuel all that Saul had done. Then David and Samuel went and stayed at Naioth.

19 Someone told Saul, "David is at Naioth in Ramah."

Saul's Men Speak God's Words

20 Saul sent men to take David. They saw a group of men who spoke for God. Samuel was standing with them. God's Spirit came on Saul's men, and they also spoke God's words. 21 People told Saul. He sent more men. They also spoke God's words. Saul sent men a third time. They also spoke God's words.

22 Then Saul went to Ramah himself. He came to a big well at Secu. He asked, "Where are Samuel and David?"

Someone said, "They are at Naioth in Ramah."

23 Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. God's Spirit came on him too. He walked and spoke God's words until he got to Naioth.

24 He took off his clothes. He spoke God's words in front of Samuel. He lay with no clothes all day and all night. That is why people say, "Is Saul one of the men who speak for God?"
