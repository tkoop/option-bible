Herod Hurts the Church

1 About that time, King Herod began to hurt some people in the church. 2 He killed James, John's brother, with a sword. 3 When Herod saw that this pleased the Jews, he also arrested Peter. This was during the feast when people ate bread with no yeast. 4 After he caught Peter, he put him in prison. He gave him to four groups of four soldiers to guard him. He planned to bring Peter out to the people after the Passover feast.

5 So Peter was kept in prison. But the church prayed hard to God for him.

An Angel Frees Peter

6 The night before Herod was going to bring him out, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers. He was tied with two chains. Guards watched the door. 7 Suddenly an angel of the Lord stood there. A light shone in the room. The angel hit Peter on the side and woke him up. He said, "Get up fast!" The chains fell off Peter's hands.

8 "Put on your belt and sandals," the angel said. Peter did. "Put on your coat and follow me," he said. 9 Peter followed him out. He did not know it was really happening. He thought he was seeing a vision. 10 They passed the first and second guards. They came to the iron gate that led to the city. It opened by itself. They went out and walked down one street. Then the angel left him.

11 Then Peter knew it was real. He said, "Now I know for sure that the Lord sent His angel. He saved me from Herod's hand and from what the Jewish people hoped would happen."

Peter Goes to Mary's House

12 When he understood this, he went to Mary's house. She was the mother of John, also called Mark. Many people were gathered there and were praying. 13 He knocked at the gate. A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer. 14 She knew Peter's voice. She was so happy that she did not open the gate. Instead, she ran in and said Peter was standing at the gate.

15 "You are crazy!" they said to her. But she kept saying it was true. They said, "It must be his angel." 16 But Peter kept knocking. When they opened the door, they saw him and were amazed. 17 Peter motioned with his hand for them to be quiet. He told them how the Lord had brought him out of prison. "Tell these things to James and the brothers," he said. Then he left and went to another place.

Herod Dies

18 When morning came, the soldiers were very upset. They did not know what had happened to Peter. 19 Herod looked for him but could not find him. He questioned the guards and had them killed. Then he went down from Judea to Caesarea and stayed there.

20 Herod was angry with the people of Tyre and Sidon. They came to him together. They won over Blastus, the king's trusted helper. They asked for peace, because their land got food from the king's country. 21 On the set day, Herod put on royal clothes, sat on his throne, and gave a speech to them. 22 The people shouted, "This is the voice of a god, not a man!" 23 Right away an angel of the Lord struck him, because he did not give God the glory. He was eaten by worms and died.

God's Word Grows

24 But the word of God kept growing and spreading. 25 Barnabas and Saul finished their work and went back to Jerusalem. They took John, called Mark, with them.
