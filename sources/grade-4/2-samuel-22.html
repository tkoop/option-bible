David's Song

1 David sang to the LORD the words of this song when the LORD had saved him from all his enemies and from Saul.

David Praises the LORD

2 "The LORD is my rock, my safe place, and my rescuer. 3 My God is my rock. I hide in Him. He is my shield and the power that saves me. He is my strong place and my safe place. He saves me from being hurt. 4 I will call on the LORD. He should be praised. He saves me from my enemies.

God Saves David from Trouble

5 "The waves of death came over me. Floods of trouble scared me. 6 The ropes of the grave held me. The traps of death came to get me. 7 When I was in trouble, I called to the LORD. I cried to my God. He heard my voice from His temple. My cry for help reached His ears.

The LORD Comes with Power

8 "The earth shook and moved. The base of the sky shook. They shook because He was angry. 9 Smoke came from His nose. Fire came from His mouth and burned. Hot coals blazed. 10 He pushed the sky aside and came down. Dark clouds were under His feet. 11 He rode on a winged angel and flew. He soared on the wings of the wind. 12 He made darkness His tent. Dark clouds and water gathered around Him. 13 From His bright light, burning coals blazed. 14 The LORD thundered from heaven. The Most High sent out His voice. 15 He shot arrows and scattered them. He sent lightning and chased them away. 16 The sea's deep places showed. The world's base was seen. This happened when the LORD spoke strongly and blew with angry breath.

God Rescues David

17 "He reached down from high up and took me. He pulled me out of deep water. 18 He saved me from my strong enemy and from enemies too strong for me. 19 They came at me on my bad day, but the LORD held me up. 20 He brought me to a wide open place. He saved me because He was pleased with me.

David Did Right

21 "The LORD paid me back for doing right. He gave me what my clean hands earned. 22 I followed the ways of the LORD. I did not do wrong against my God. 23 All His rules were in front of me. I did not ignore His laws. 24 I was without blame before Him. I kept myself from sin. 25 So the LORD paid me back for doing right, for being clean before His eyes.

God Is Fair

26 "To the faithful, You are faithful. To the one without blame, You are without blame. 27 To the pure, You are pure. To the crooked, You are clever. 28 You save people who are hurting, but You bring down proud people.

God Gives Strength

29 "For You are my lamp, the LORD. You make my darkness bright. 30 With You I can run through an army. With my God I can jump over a wall. 31 God's way is perfect. The word of the LORD is pure. He is a shield to all who hide in Him. 32 Who is God besides the LORD? Who is a rock except our God? 33 God is my strong fort. He makes my way clear. 34 He makes my feet like a deer's feet. He sets me on high places. 35 He trains my hands for war. My arms can bend a bronze bow. 36 You gave me Your saving shield. Your right hand holds me up. Your kindness lifts me high. 37 You make a wide path for me, so my feet do not slip.

David Wins Battles

38 "I chased my enemies and destroyed them. I did not turn back until they were gone. 39 I crushed them. They could not get up. They fell under my feet. 40 You gave me strength for the fight. You made my enemies fall under me. 41 You made my enemies turn and run. I destroyed those who hated me. 42 They looked, but no one saved them. They called to the LORD, but He did not answer them. 43 I ground them like dust. I crushed and stomped them like mud in the streets.

David Rules Many Nations

44 "You saved me from my people's fights. You kept me as head of nations. People I did not know serve me. 45 People from other lands give in to me. When they hear me, they obey me. 46 People from other lands lose courage. They come shaking out of their strong places.

Praise the LORD

47 "The LORD lives! Praise my Rock! May the God who saves me be lifted high! 48 He is the God who pays back my enemies and brings nations under me. 49 He brings me out from my enemies. You lift me above my enemies. You save me from violent men. 50 So I will praise You, the LORD, among the nations. I will sing to Your name. 51 He gives great saving to His king. He shows kindness to His chosen one, to David and his family forever."
