Ahab Wants Naboth's Grape Field

1 After these things, Naboth the man from Jezreel had a grape field in Jezreel. It was next to the palace of Ahab, king of Samaria.

2 Ahab said to Naboth, "Give me your grape field. I want to make it a garden for vegetables. It is close to my house. I will give you a better grape field for it. Or, if you like, I will pay you money for it."

3 Naboth said to Ahab, "The LORD forbid that I give you the land my fathers left me."

4 Ahab went home. He was sad and angry because of what Naboth said. He lay on his bed, turned his face to the wall, and would not eat.

5 His wife Jezebel came in and said, "Why are you so sad? Why won't you eat?"

6 He said, "I asked Naboth for his grape field. I offered money or another grape field. He said no."

7 Jezebel said, "Are you not the king of Israel? Get up, eat, and be happy. I will get you Naboth's grape field."

Jezebel's Bad Plan

8 She wrote letters in Ahab's name. She used his seal. She sent them to the leaders and important men who lived in Naboth's city.

9 In the letters she wrote, "Call a fast. Put Naboth in a high place among the people. 10 Bring two bad men to sit across from him. Have them say that he cursed God and the king. Then take him out and kill him with stones."

Naboth Is Killed

11 The men of his city, the leaders and important men, did what Jezebel sent. They did what the letters said.

12 They called a fast. They put Naboth at the head of the people.

13 Two bad men came in and sat across from him. They accused Naboth in front of the people. They said, "Naboth cursed God and the king." Then they took him outside the city and killed him with stones.

14 They sent word to Jezebel: "Naboth has been killed with stones and is dead."

15 When Jezebel heard that Naboth was killed with stones and was dead, she said to Ahab, "Get up and take Naboth's grape field. He would not sell it to you, but now he is dead."

16 When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he got up to go down and take Naboth's grape field.

Elijah Speaks for the LORD

17 Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the man from Tishbe. 18 It said, "Go meet Ahab king of Israel in Samaria. He is in Naboth's grape field, where he went down to take it."

19 "Say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Did you kill a man and take his land? In the place where dogs licked Naboth's blood, dogs will lick your blood too.'"

20 Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, my enemy?"

Elijah said, "I have found you, because you sold yourself to do evil in the LORD's eyes. 21 I will bring trouble on you. I will destroy your family. I will remove every male in Ahab's family from Israel, whether slave or free. 22 I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah. You made Israel sin, and you made Me angry. 23 The LORD also says about Jezebel: Dogs will eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel. 24 Anyone of Ahab's family who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs. Anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by birds of the sky."

25 No one was like Ahab. He sold himself to do evil in the LORD's eyes, because his wife Jezebel led him to do it.

26 He did very bad things by going after fake gods, like the Amorites did. The LORD had driven them out before the people of Israel.

Ahab Is Sorry

27 When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes. He put rough cloth on his skin. He did not eat. He lay in rough cloth and walked slowly.

28 Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the man from Tishbe. 29 It said, "Have you seen how Ahab is sorry before Me? Because he is sorry before Me, I will not bring this trouble in his days. I will bring it on his house in his son's days."
