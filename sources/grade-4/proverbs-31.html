King Lemuel's Mother Teaches Him

1 These are the words of King Lemuel. His mother taught him this message.

2 "What, my son? What, child of my body? What, child I prayed for? 3 Do not give your strength to women. Do not give your ways to those who ruin kings.

4 "It is not right for kings, Lemuel, to drink wine. It is not right for leaders to want strong drink. 5 They may drink and forget the law. They may take away the rights of poor people.

6 "Give strong drink to someone who is dying. Give wine to someone who is very sad. 7 Let them drink and forget they are poor. Let them not remember their hard times.

8 "Speak up for people who cannot speak. Speak up for those who have no one to help. 9 Speak up and judge fairly. Stand up for poor and needy people."

A Good Wife

10 Who can find a very good wife? She is worth more than jewels.

11 Her husband trusts her. He will have all he needs.

12 She does him good, not harm, all her life.

13 She looks for wool and flax. She works with willing hands.

14 She is like trading ships. She brings food from far away.

15 She gets up while it is still night. She gives food to her family and work to her servants.

16 She looks at a field and buys it. She plants a vineyard with what her hands earn.

17 She works with strength. Her arms are strong.

18 She knows her work is good. Her lamp does not go out at night.

19 She spins thread with her hands. She holds the spinning tool.

20 She opens her hand to poor people. She reaches out to needy people.

21 She is not afraid of snow for her family. All her family wear warm clothes.

22 She makes blankets for herself. Her clothes are fine linen and purple.

23 Her husband is known at the city gate. He sits with the leaders of the land.

24 She makes linen cloth and sells it. She gives belts to traders.

25 She wears strength and honor. She smiles at the future.

26 She speaks with wisdom. Kind words are on her tongue.

27 She watches over her home. She does not waste time being lazy.

28 Her children stand up and call her blessed. Her husband praises her:

29 "Many women have done well, but you are better than all of them."

30 Charm can fool people. Beauty fades. But a woman who honors the LORD should be praised.

31 Give her the reward of her work. Let her deeds praise her in the city.
