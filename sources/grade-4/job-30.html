Younger Men Make Fun of Job

“1 But now young men laugh at me. They are younger than I am. I would not even let their fathers work with my sheep dogs. 2 Their strong hands were no help to me. Their strength was gone. 3 They are thin from hunger. They wander in dry, empty land. They look for food in ruined places. 4 They pick salty weeds among the bushes. They eat roots to stay alive. 5 People chase them away. They shout at them as if they were thieves. 6 They live in holes in the ground and in rocks. They cry out among the rocks. 7 They make loud noises among the bushes. They gather under thorn plants. 8 They are foolish people. They have no good name. They were chased out of the land.

Job Loses His Honor

“9 Now I am the song they sing. I am the joke they tell. 10 They hate me. They stay away from me. They even spit in my face. 11 He let them loose and hurt me. They act wild in front of me. 12 On my right side, the crowd rises up. They trip my feet. They bring trouble to me. 13 They block my path. They help my trouble. No one stops them. 14 They rush in through a big break. They roll around in the ruins. 15 Fear comes over me. My honor is blown away like the wind. My help is gone like a cloud.

Job Cries Out to God

“16 Now my life feels poured out inside me. Days of pain have taken hold of me. 17 Night cuts into my bones. My pain never rests. 18 My clothes are changed by His great power. They wrap around me like a collar. 19 He has thrown me into the mud. I am like dust and ashes. 20 I cry to You, but You do not answer me. I stand up, and You just look at me. 21 You have become cruel to me. You fight me with Your strong hand. 22 You lift me into the wind. You toss me around. You melt me in the storm. 23 I know You will bring me to death. That is the home for all who live.

Job Remembers His Kindness

“24 Yet doesn't He reach out His hand against a broken man, even when he cries for help in his trouble? 25 Did I not cry for people in hard times? Did I not feel sad for the poor? 26 I hoped for good, but evil came. I waited for light, but darkness came. 27 My stomach is upset and does not rest. Days of pain come to me. 28 I walk in black without the sun. I stand in the crowd and cry for help. 29 I am like a brother to wild dogs. I am a friend to ostriches. 30 My skin turns black and falls off. My bones burn with heat. 31 My harp is used for sad songs. My flute is for sounds of crying.”
