A Song by David

1 The LORD said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your power.”

2 The LORD will send your strong power from Zion. Rule even when your enemies are around you.

The King's Helpers

3 Your people will gladly help you when you are strong. They will be holy and bright like the morning dew. You have young helpers like dew drops.

A Promise That Lasts

4 The LORD made a promise and will not change His mind: “You are a priest forever, like Melchizedek.”

The King Wins

5 The Lord is at your right hand. He will crush kings when He is angry.

6 He will judge the nations. He will pile up the dead. He will crush the leaders of the whole earth.

7 He will drink from a stream beside the road. Then He will lift His head in victory.
