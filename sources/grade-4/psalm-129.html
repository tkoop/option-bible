A Song for Going Up

1 Let Israel say, "They have hurt me a lot since I was young. 2 They have hurt me a lot since I was young, but they did not beat me.

3 Farmers plowed on my back. They made long cuts there. 4 The LORD is good. He cut the ropes of the bad people.

Enemies of Zion

5 May everyone who hates Zion turn back and feel ashamed. 6 May they be like grass on a roof. It dries up before it can grow. 7 No one can fill his hand with it. No one can fill his arms with it.

8 People who walk by do not say, "May the LORD bless you. We bless you in the name of the LORD."
