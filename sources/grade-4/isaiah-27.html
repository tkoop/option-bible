The Lord Will Punish the Sea Monster

1 In that day, the LORD will punish Leviathan, the snake that runs away, with His strong sword. He will kill the dragon in the sea.

A Song About God's Vineyard

2 “In that day, sing about a vineyard full of fruit. 3 I am the LORD. I take care of it. I water it all the time. I keep it safe day and night, so no one can hurt it. 4 I am not angry. I wish thorns and weeds were here for Me to fight! I would go against them. I would burn them all up. 5 Or let them hold on to My strength. Then they can make peace with Me. Yes, let them make peace with Me.”

Israel Will Grow and Fill the World

6 Later, Jacob will grow roots. Israel will bloom and bud. It will fill the whole world with fruit.

The Lord Corrected Israel

7 Did the LORD punish Israel like He punished her enemies? Was she killed like those who killed her? 8 By war and being sent away, You fought with her. You took her away with a strong wind on a hot day. 9 So Jacob's sin will be forgiven. This is the full fruit of taking away his sin: he will crush all the altar stones into small pieces. No poles for Asherah and no incense altars will be left.

The Empty City

10 The strong city is empty. It is like a deserted home in the desert. A calf eats grass there. It lies down there and eats the branches. 11 When the branches dry up, they break off. Women come and burn them for firewood. This is because the people do not understand. So their Maker will not have pity on them. Their Creator will not be kind to them.

God Will Bring His People Home

12 In that day, the LORD will gather you, children of Israel, one by one. He will gather you from the Euphrates River to the stream in Egypt. 13 In that day, a loud trumpet will sound. The people lost in Assyria will come. The people scattered in Egypt will come. They will worship the LORD on His holy mountain in Jerusalem.
