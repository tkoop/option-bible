Israel Makes a Promise

1 The men of Israel made a promise at Mizpah. They said, "None of us will let our daughters marry the men of Benjamin."

2 So the people went to Bethel. They sat there before God until evening. They cried loudly and sadly.

3 They said, "Why, O the LORD, God of Israel, did this happen in Israel? Today one tribe is gone from Israel."

The People Worship God

4 The next day the people got up early. They built an altar there. They gave burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Finding the Missing Tribe

5 Then the Israelites asked, "Which tribe did not come to the meeting before the LORD?" They had made a big promise. Anyone who did not come to the LORD at Mizpah would be put to death.

6 The Israelites were sad for their brothers in Benjamin. They said, "Today one tribe is gone from Israel."

7 They asked, "What will we do for wives for the men who are left? We swore to the LORD that we would not let our daughters marry them."

8 So they asked, "Which tribe of Israel did not come to the LORD at Mizpah?" They found that no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the meeting.

9 When they counted the people, no one from Jabesh-gilead was there.

Jabesh-gilead Is Punished

10 So the people sent 12,000 brave men. They told them, "Go and kill the people of Jabesh-gilead with swords. Kill the women and children too. 11 This is what you must do: Kill every man, and kill every woman who has slept with a man."

12 They found 400 young women in Jabesh-gilead who had not slept with a man. They brought them to the camp at Shiloh in the land of Canaan.

Peace with Benjamin

13 Then the whole group sent a peaceful message to the men of Benjamin at the rock of Rimmon.

14 At that time the men of Benjamin came back. The Israelites gave them the women they had saved from Jabesh-gilead. But there were not enough women for all of them.

15 The people were sad for Benjamin. The LORD had made a break among the tribes of Israel.

The Leaders Plan for Wives

16 Then the elders of the group said, "What will we do for wives for the men who are left? The women of Benjamin have been destroyed."

17 They said, "The men left in Benjamin must have their own land, so one tribe is not lost from Israel."

18 But we cannot give them our daughters as wives. The Israelites had sworn, "Anyone who gives a wife to a man from Benjamin is cursed."

The Plan at Shiloh

19 So they said, "Look, every year there is a feast to the LORD in Shiloh. It is north of Bethel, east of the road from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah."

20 So they told the men of Benjamin, "Go and wait in the grape fields."

21 They said, "Watch. When you see the young women of Shiloh come out to dance, each man must come out of the grape fields. Grab a wife for himself from the young women of Shiloh. Then go to the land of Benjamin."

22 They said, "If their fathers or brothers come to us to complain, we will say, 'Be kind to them. We did not take a wife for each man in the war. You did not give them wives, so you are not guilty.'"

Benjamin Is Restored

23 So the men of Benjamin did this. They took the number of wives they needed from the women who were dancing. Then they went back to their own land. They rebuilt the cities and lived in them.

24 At that time the Israelites went home, each to his own tribe and family. They left and went to their own land.

No King in Israel

25 In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did what he thought was right.
