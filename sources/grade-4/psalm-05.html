A Morning Prayer

1 Listen to my words, the LORD. Understand my thoughts. 2 Listen to my cry, my King and my God. I pray to You. 3 In the morning, the LORD, You hear my voice. In the morning I bring my prayer to You and wait.

God Does Not Love Evil

4 You are not a God who likes bad things. Evil cannot stay with You. 5 Proud people cannot stand before You. You hate everyone who does wrong. 6 You destroy people who tell lies. The LORD hates people who kill and cheat.

Coming to God's House

7 But I will come into Your house because of Your great love. I will bow toward Your holy temple with respect. 8 Lead me, the LORD, on the right path because of my enemies. Make Your way straight in front of me.

The Wicked Are in Trouble

9 They do not speak the truth. Their hearts are full of evil. Their throats are like open graves. They use smooth words to trick people. 10 Punish them, God. Let them fall because of their own plans. Push them away because they have sinned so much. They have turned against You.

God Protects His People

11 But let everyone who trusts in You be happy. Let them sing with joy forever. Keep them safe, because they love Your name. Let them be glad in You. 12 You bless those who do right, the LORD. You cover them with Your kindness like a shield.
