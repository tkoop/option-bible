The Pharisees and Sadducees Ask for a Sign

1 The Pharisees and Sadducees came to Jesus. They wanted to test Him. They asked Him to show them a sign from heaven. 2 Jesus answered, "When evening comes, you say, 'The sky is red, so it will be fair weather.' 3 And in the morning you say, 'The sky is red and dark, so it will rain.' You can read the sky, but you cannot read the signs of the times. 4 Bad and unfaithful people ask for a sign. But no sign will be given to them except the sign of Jonah." Then He left them and went away.

Watch Out for the Yeast

5 The disciples went across the lake. They forgot to bring bread. 6 Jesus said to them, "Watch out! Be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

7 The disciples talked among themselves. They said, "We did not bring any bread."

8 Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said, "You have so little faith! Why are you talking about having no bread? 9 Do you not remember the five loaves for the five thousand? How many baskets did you pick up? 10 And do you not remember the seven loaves for the four thousand? How many baskets did you pick up? 11 How can you not understand? I was not talking about bread. I said, 'Be careful of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees.'"

12 Then they understood. He was not warning them about yeast in bread. He was warning them about the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

Who Is the Son of Man?

13 Jesus went to the area of Caesarea Philippi. He asked His disciples, "Who do people say the Son of Man is?"

14 They answered, "Some say John the Baptist. Others say Elijah. Others say Jeremiah or one of the prophets."

15 Jesus asked them, "But who do you say I am?"

16 Simon Peter answered, "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God."

17 Jesus answered him, "You are blessed, Simon son of Jonah. No person told you this. My Father in heaven showed it to you. 18 I tell you, you are Peter. On this rock I will build My church. The power of death will not beat it. 19 I will give you the keys to the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you tie up on earth will be tied up in heaven. Whatever you set free on earth will be set free in heaven."

20 Then Jesus told His disciples not to tell anyone that He was the Christ.

Jesus Tells About His Death and Rising

21 From that time on, Jesus began to show His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem. He would suffer many things from the older leaders, the chief priests, and the teachers of the law. He would be killed, and He would rise again on the third day.

22 Peter took Jesus aside and began to scold Him. He said, "Never, Lord! This will never happen to You!"

23 Jesus turned and said to Peter, "Get behind Me, Satan! You are in My way. You are thinking like a person, not like God."

Follow Jesus

24 Then Jesus said to His disciples, "If anyone wants to follow Me, he must say no to himself. He must pick up his cross and follow Me. 25 Anyone who tries to save his life will lose it. But anyone who gives up his life for Me will find it. 26 What good is it if a person gains the whole world but loses his life? What can a person give to buy back his life? 27 The Son of Man will come in His Father's glory with His angels. Then He will pay each person for what he has done. 28 I tell you the truth: some people standing here will not die before they see the Son of Man coming in His kingdom."
