Micah Speaks to the Leaders

1 Then I said, "Listen, leaders of Jacob! You rulers of Israel's people, should you not know what is right? 2 You hate good and love what is bad. You tear the skin off My people and the meat from their bones. 3 You eat My people's meat. You pull their skin off. You break their bones and chop them up like meat for a pot, like meat in a big pot."

4 Then they will cry to the the LORD, but He will not answer them. At that time He will turn away from them, because they did such bad things.

False Prophets Will Be Shamed

5 This is what the the LORD says about the prophets who lead My people the wrong way. They say, "Peace," but they bite with their teeth. They start a fight with anyone who does not feed them.

6 So night will come for you with no visions. It will be dark for you, and you will not be able to tell the future. The sun will go down on the prophets, and the day will turn black over them.

7 The seers will be ashamed. The people who tell the future will be put to shame. They will all cover their mouths, because God will not answer them.

Micah Is Filled with God's Spirit

8 But I am filled with power by the Spirit of the the LORD. I have justice and strength. I can tell Jacob about his wrongs and Israel about his sin.

Leaders Who Hate Justice

9 Now listen to this, leaders of Jacob's family and rulers of Israel's people. You hate what is right. You twist everything that is good. 10 You build Zion with blood. You build Jerusalem with sin.

11 Her leaders decide cases for money. Her priests teach for pay. Her prophets tell the future for silver. Yet they lean on the the LORD and say, "Isn't the the LORD with us? No bad thing will come on us."

The City Will Be Ruined

12 So because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field. Jerusalem will become a pile of rocks. The hill of the the LORD's house will become a forest.
