Hezekiah Becomes King of Judah

1 In the third year that Hoshea son of Elah was king of Israel, Hezekiah son of Ahaz became king of Judah. 2 He was 25 years old when he became king. He ruled 29 years in Jerusalem. His mother was Abi, the daughter of Zechariah.

Hezekiah Does What Is Right

3 He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. He did what King David did. 4 He took away the high places. He broke the stone pillars. He cut down the Asherah poles. He smashed the bronze snake Moses had made. He called it Nehushtan. Up to that time, the people of Israel had burned incense to it.

Hezekiah Trusts the LORD

5 He trusted in the LORD, the God of Israel. No king of Judah before him or after him was like him. 6 He stayed close to the LORD. He did not turn away from Him. He kept the commands the LORD gave through Moses. 7 The LORD was with him. He did well in everything he did. He said no to the king of Assyria and did not serve him. 8 He fought the Philistines and won, all the way to Gaza. He took their land from the watchtower to the walled city.

Assyria Takes Israel Away

9 In Hezekiah's fourth year, which was Hoshea's seventh year, Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against Samaria and surrounded it. 10 After three years, they took the city. This was Hezekiah's sixth year and Hoshea's ninth year. 11 The king of Assyria took the people of Israel to Assyria. He put them in Halah, by the Habor River, and in the cities of the Medes. 12 This happened because they did not listen to the LORD their God. They broke His agreement. They did not do what Moses, the LORD's servant, told them. They did not listen or obey.

Sennacherib Attacks Judah

13 In Hezekiah's fourteenth year, Sennacherib king of Assyria attacked all the strong cities of Judah and took them. 14 Hezekiah king of Judah sent a message to the king of Assyria at Lachish. He said, "I did wrong. Please go away from me. I will pay whatever you ask." The king of Assyria asked Hezekiah to pay 300 large pieces of silver and 30 large pieces of gold. 15 Hezekiah gave all the silver found in the LORD's house and in the king's treasure rooms. 16 At that time, Hezekiah stripped the gold from the doors and doorposts of the LORD's house. He gave it to the king of Assyria.

Rabshakeh Comes to Jerusalem

17 The king of Assyria sent Tartan, Rabsaris, and Rabshakeh from Lachish to King Hezekiah in Jerusalem. They came with a large army. They went up to Jerusalem and stood by the upper pool, on the road to the washer's field. 18 They called for the king. Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the house, Shebna the writer, and Joah son of Asaph, who kept the records, came out to them.

19 Rabshakeh said, "Tell Hezekiah that the great king of Assyria asks what you are trusting in. 20 He says you say you have plans and power for war, but those are only words. Now who are you trusting in, since you turned against me? 21 Look, you are trusting in Egypt. Egypt is like a broken stick. If you lean on it, it breaks and cuts your hand. That is what Pharaoh king of Egypt is like to all who trust in him. 22 If you say, 'We trust in the LORD our God,' isn't He the one whose high places and altars Hezekiah took away? Hezekiah told Judah and Jerusalem, 'Bow down only at this altar in Jerusalem.' 23 Make a deal with my master, the king of Assyria. I will give you two thousand horses if you can put riders on them. 24 How can you turn back even one small officer of my master? You trust in Egypt for chariots and horsemen. 25 Did I come up to destroy this place without the LORD? The LORD said to me, 'Go up and destroy this land.'"

26 Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebna, and Joah said to Rabshakeh, "Please speak to your servants in Aramaic. We understand it. Do not speak to us in Hebrew where the people on the wall can hear."

27 Rabshakeh said, "Did my master send me to speak these words to your king and to you leaders, and not to the men on the wall? They will suffer and starve with you."

Rabshakeh Speaks to the People

28 Rabshakeh stood and called out loudly in Hebrew, "Listen to the word of the great king of Assyria! 29 Do not let Hezekiah fool you. He cannot save you from my hand. 30 Do not let Hezekiah make you trust in the LORD. He says, 'The LORD will save us. This city will not be given to the king of Assyria.' 31 Do not listen to Hezekiah. The king of Assyria says, 'Make peace with me. Come out to me. Then each of you will eat from your own vine and fig tree and drink from your own well.' 32 I will come and take you to a land like your own land. It is a land of grain and new wine, bread and vineyards, olive oil and honey. You will live and not die. Do not listen to Hezekiah when he tells you, 'The LORD will save us.' 33 Did the gods of the nations save their lands from the king of Assyria? 34 Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? Did they save Samaria from me? 35 Which of all the gods of these lands saved their land from me? So how can the LORD save Jerusalem from me?"

The People Stay Quiet

36 The people were quiet. They did not answer him at all. This was because the king had said, "Do not answer him." 37 Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the house, Shebna the writer, and Joah son of Asaph, who kept the records, came to Hezekiah. Their clothes were torn. They told him what Rabshakeh had said.
