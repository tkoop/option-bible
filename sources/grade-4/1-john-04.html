Check the Spirits

1 Dear friends, do not believe every spirit. Test the spirits to see if they come from God. Many fake prophets have gone out into the world. 2 This is how you know the Spirit of God: Every spirit that says Jesus Christ came in the flesh is from God. 3 Every spirit that does not say this about Jesus is not from God. That is the spirit of the antichrist. You have heard it is coming, and it is already in the world. 4 You are from God, dear children. You have won over them, because the One in you is greater than the one in the world. 5 They are from the world. So they speak like the world, and the world listens to them. 6 We are from God. The person who knows God listens to us. The person who is not from God does not listen to us. This is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of lies.

God Is Love

7 Dear friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 8 The person who does not love does not know God, because God is love. 9 God showed His love to us this way: He sent His only Son into the world so that we may live through Him. 10 This is love: not that we loved God, but that He loved us. He sent His Son to take away our sins. 11 Dear friends, since God loved us so much, we should love one another. 12 No one has ever seen God. But if we love one another, God lives in us, and His love is made complete in us.

13 This is how we know we live in Him and He lives in us: He has given us His Spirit. 14 We have seen and tell others that the Father sent His Son to save the world. 15 If anyone says Jesus is the Son of God, God lives in him, and he lives in God. 16 We have known and believed the love God has for us. God is love. The person who lives in love lives in God, and God lives in him.

Love Has No Fear

17 Love is made complete in us this way: We can be brave on judgment day. We are like Him in this world. 18 There is no fear in love. Perfect love pushes fear away. Fear brings punishment. A person who is afraid has not been made perfect in love. 19 We love because He first loved us. 20 If someone says, "I love God," but hates his brother, he is a liar. A person who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, cannot love God, whom he has not seen. 21 We have this command from Him: The person who loves God must also love his brother.
