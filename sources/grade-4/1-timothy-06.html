Slaves and Masters

1 Slaves should think their own masters are worth full respect. This is so God's name and teaching won't be spoken against. 2 Slaves who have masters who believe should not look down on them, because they are brothers. They should serve them even more, because the masters who get the help are believers and loved. Teach and urge these things.

False Teachers

3 If anyone teaches something different and does not agree with the true words of our Lord Jesus Christ and the teaching that goes with living for God, 4 he is proud and knows nothing. He is sick with questions and fights about words. These bring envy, fighting, bad words, and evil guesses. 5 People with ruined minds who have lost the truth keep causing trouble. They think living for God is a way to get money.

Being Happy with Enough

6 But living for God and being happy with what we have is great gain. 7 We brought nothing into the world. We can take nothing out. 8 If we have food and clothes, we should be happy with that.

The Danger of Money

9 People who want to be rich fall into a trap. They fall into many silly and harmful wants. These wants pull people down into ruin and loss. 10 Loving money is the root of all kinds of evil. Some people who wanted it left the faith. They hurt themselves with many sorrows.

Timothy's Fight

11 But you, man of God, run from these things. Chase after doing right, living for God, faith, love, patience, and a gentle spirit. 12 Keep fighting the good fight of faith. Hold on to life that lasts forever. God called you to it. You said the good words about it in front of many people.

A Big Charge

13 I charge you before God, who gives life to all, and before Christ Jesus, who spoke the good words before Pontius Pilate. 14 Keep this command clean and without fault until our Lord Jesus Christ shows Himself. 15 God will show it at the right time. He is the happy and only Ruler. He is King of kings and Lord of lords. 16 He alone never dies. He lives in light no one can go near. No person has seen Him. No person can see Him. Honor and power belong to Him forever. Amen.

Rich People

17 Tell rich people in this world not to be proud. They should not trust in money that can go away. They should trust in God, who richly gives us all things to enjoy. 18 They should do good. They should be rich in good works. They should be ready to give and share. 19 They will save up a good base for the future. Then they can hold on to real life.

Keep the Truth

20 Timothy, guard what was given to you. Stay away from empty talk that is not holy. Stay away from false ideas that are called knowledge. 21 Some people say they have it. They missed the faith. Grace be with you.
