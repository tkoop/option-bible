The LORD Speaks to Moses

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2 "Command the Israelites. Tell them to be careful to bring Me My offering—My food for My burnt offerings and My pleasing smell—at the right time."

Daily Offerings

3 "Tell them this is the burnt offering to give the LORD: two perfect male lambs, one year old, each day. 4 Offer one lamb in the morning. Offer the other in the evening."

5 "With each lamb, give a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil. 6 This regular burnt offering was set up at Mount Sinai. It is a fire offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD."

7 "For each lamb, pour out a drink offering of one-fourth of a hin of wine. Pour it in the holy place as an offering to the LORD. 8 Offer the second lamb in the evening. Make its grain and drink offerings like the morning ones. It is a burnt offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD."

Sabbath Offerings

9 "On the Sabbath, offer two perfect male lambs, one year old. Also offer a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, with its drink offering. 10 This is the burnt offering for each Sabbath. It is in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering."

Monthly Offerings

11 "At the start of each month, bring a burnt offering to the LORD. Bring two young bulls, one ram, and seven perfect male lambs one year old. 12 For each bull, offer a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. For the ram, offer two-tenths of an ephah. 13 For each lamb, offer one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. This burnt offering has a pleasing smell to the LORD."

14 "Their drink offerings are half a hin of wine for a bull, one-third of a hin for a ram, and one-fourth of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering for each month, all year long. 15 Also offer one male goat as a sin offering to the LORD to pay for your sins. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering."

Passover and Unleavened Bread

16 "The Passover of the LORD is on day 14 of the first month. 17 On day 15 of that month is a feast. For seven days, eat bread made without yeast. 18 On the first day, have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work."

19 "Bring a burnt offering to the LORD. Bring two young bulls, one ram, and seven perfect male lambs one year old. 20 Their grain offering is three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for each bull, and two-tenths for the ram. 21 Offer one-tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 22 Also offer one male goat as a sin offering to pay for your sins."

23 "Offer these in addition to the morning burnt offering, which is the regular burnt offering. 24 Do this every day for seven days. It is a fire offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD. It is in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. 25 On the seventh day, have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work."

Offerings for the Feast of Weeks

26 "On the day you bring the first grain from your harvest to the LORD, at your Feast of Weeks, have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work. 27 Bring a burnt offering with a pleasing smell to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs one year old."

28 "Their grain offering is three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for each bull, and two-tenths for the ram. 29 Offer one-tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 30 Also offer one male goat to pay for your sins. 31 Do this in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering. The animals must be perfect for you, with their drink offerings."
