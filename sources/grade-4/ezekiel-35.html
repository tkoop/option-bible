God Speaks Against Mount Seir

1 The LORD gave me a message. 2 He said, "Son of man, turn your face toward Mount Seir and speak against it."

3 Then He said to Mount Seir, "This is what the Lord the LORD says: Look, I am against you, Mount Seir. I will reach out My hand against you. I will make you a wasteland. 4 I will wreck your cities. You will become a wasteland. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

"5 You have hated for a long time. You let Israel's people be killed by the sword when they were in trouble, at their final punishment. 6 So, as sure as I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will let you be killed. Killing will chase you. Because you did not hate killing, killing will chase you.

"7 I will make Mount Seir a wasteland, a total wasteland. I will stop anyone from passing through it or coming back. 8 I will fill its mountains with dead people. Those killed by the sword will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your low places. 9 I will make you a lasting wasteland. Your cities will not be lived in. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

"10 Because you said these two nations and two lands would be yours, and that you would take them. But the LORD was there. 11 So, as sure as I live, says the Lord the LORD, I will deal with you the way your anger and jealousy led you to deal with them. I will make Myself known among them when I judge you.

"12 Then you will know that I, the LORD, heard all the mean things you said against the mountains of Israel. You said they were empty and given to you to eat. 13 You bragged against Me with your mouth. You said many things against Me. I heard it all.

"14 This is what the Lord the LORD says: While the whole earth is happy, I will make you a wasteland. 15 You were happy when Israel's land was empty. So I will do the same to you. You will become a wasteland, Mount Seir and all Edom, yes, all of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
