God Calls Israel Back

1 "If you come back, Israel, come back to Me," says the LORD. "If you take away your bad idols from My eyes and do not turn away. 2 If you promise that the LORD lives, with truth, fair choices, and right living, then other nations will be blessed by Him. They will praise Him."

Break Up Hard Ground

3 This is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem: "Break up your hard ground. Do not plant seeds among thorns. 4 Give yourselves to the LORD. Take away the stubborn part of your hearts, men of Judah and people of Jerusalem. If not, My anger will burst out like fire. It will burn, and no one will put it out. This is because of your bad deeds."

Warning from the North

5 Tell Judah. Tell Jerusalem. Say, "Blow the horn in the land! Shout loud. Tell people to come together and run to the strong cities. 6 Lift a flag toward Zion. Find a safe place. Do not wait! I am bringing trouble from the north. It will be big trouble."

The Enemy Comes

7 A lion has come up from his thick bushes. A destroyer of nations has left his home. He has left to ruin your land. Your cities will be broken. No one will live in them. 8 So put on rough cloth. Cry and wail. the LORD's hot anger has not turned away from us.

God Speaks About That Day

9 "On that day," says the LORD, "the king and leaders will lose heart. The priests will be shocked. The prophets will be amazed."

Jeremiah's Sad Prayer

10 Then I said, "Oh, Lord, the LORD! You have tricked this people and Jerusalem. You said we would have peace, but we are in great danger."

God's Wind of Trouble

11 At that time people will tell this people and Jerusalem that a hot wind from the bare hills in the desert blows toward My people. It is not to clean grain or make it ready. 12 A stronger wind than that comes from Me. Now I will also say how I will judge them.

Enemies Come Fast

13 Look! He comes up like clouds. His war carts are like a storm. His horses are faster than eagles.

"Oh no! We are ruined!"

14 Jerusalem, wash the bad from your heart, so you may be saved. How long will you keep bad thoughts?

News from Dan

15 A voice tells from Dan. It tells of trouble from Mount Ephraim. 16 Tell the nations. Tell Jerusalem that enemies are coming from a far land. They shout against the cities of Judah. 17 They surround her like men who guard a field, because she turned against Me, says the LORD. 18 "Your ways and your deeds brought this on you. This is your punishment. It is bitter. It has reached your heart!"

Jeremiah's Pain

19 My pain! My pain! I hurt inside. My heart pounds. I cannot be quiet. I heard the horn. I heard the war alarm. 20 One trouble comes after another. The whole land is ruined. My tents are broken. My curtains are torn in a moment. 21 How long must I see the flag of war and hear the horn?

God's People Do Not Know Him

22 "My people are foolish. They do not know Me. They are silly children. They do not understand. They are smart at doing bad. They do not know how to do good."

The Land Becomes Empty

23 I looked at the earth. It was empty and bare. I looked at the sky. Its light was gone. 24 I looked at the mountains. They were shaking. All the hills moved. 25 I looked. There was no person. All the birds of the sky had flown away. 26 I looked. The good land was a desert. All its cities were broken before the LORD, before His fierce anger.

God Will Not End All

27 This is what the LORD says: "The whole land will be empty. But I will not destroy it all. 28 So the earth will be sad. The sky above will grow dark. I have spoken, and I will not change My mind. I have decided, and I will not turn back."

Cities Are Left Empty

29 At the sound of the horseman and the bowman, all the people run away. They hide in thick bushes and climb on rocks. Every city is left empty. No one lives in them.

Jerusalem Is Alone

30 And you, ruined one, what will you do? You may wear red. You may wear gold. You may paint your eyes. But it is for nothing. Your lovers hate you. They want to kill you. 31 I hear a cry like a woman having a baby. It is a cry of pain like a woman having her first baby. It is the cry of Zion's daughter. She gasps for breath. She holds out her hands.

"Oh no! I am weak. Murderers are killing me!"
