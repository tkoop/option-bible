To the Church in Sardis

1 "To the angel of the church in Sardis, write this: These are the words of the One who has the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars. I know what you have done. People think you are alive, but you are really dead. 2 Wake up! Make strong what is left. It is about to die. I have not found your good works finished before My God.

3 "Remember what you received and heard. Keep it, and turn back to God. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief. You will not know what time I will come to you.

4 "But a few people in Sardis have not made their clothes dirty. They are worthy, and they will walk with Me in white clothes. 5 The one who stays strong will wear white clothes. I will not erase his name from the book of life. I will say his name before My Father and before His angels. 6 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

To the Church in Philadelphia

7 "To the angel of the church in Philadelphia, write this: These are the words of the Holy One, the True One. He has the key of David. He opens, and no one shuts. He shuts, and no one opens. 8 I know what you have done. Look, I have put an open door before you. No one can shut it. You have a little strength. You kept My word. You did not deny My name.

9 "Look, some people say they are Jews, but they are not. They belong to Satan's meeting place. I will make them come and bow down at your feet. Then they will know that I loved you. 10 You kept My word and stayed strong. So I will keep you safe from the time of trouble that is coming to the whole world. It will test the people who live on earth.

11 "I am coming soon. Hold on to what you have, so no one takes your crown. 12 The one who stays strong, I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God. He will never leave again. I will write on him the name of My God. I will write the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem. It comes down from heaven from My God. I will also write My new name. 13 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."

To the Church in Laodicea

14 "To the angel of the church in Laodicea, write this: These are the words of the Amen, the faithful and true witness. He is the beginning of God's creation. 15 I know what you have done. You are not cold, and you are not hot. I wish you were one or the other.

16 "So, because you are lukewarm—not hot and not cold—I am about to spit you out of My mouth. 17 You say, 'I am rich. I have gotten rich. I need nothing.' But you do not know that you are in trouble, poor, blind, and naked.

18 "I tell you to buy from Me gold that fire has cleaned, so you can be rich. Buy white clothes, so you can cover yourself. Then your shame will not show. Buy eye medicine, so you can put it on your eyes and see. 19 I correct and train the people I love. So be eager, and turn back to God.

20 "Look, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in. I will eat with him, and he will eat with Me. 21 The one who stays strong, I will let him sit with Me on My throne. I won the victory too, and I sat down with My Father on His throne. 22 Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
