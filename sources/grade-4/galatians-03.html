Paul Talks to the Galatians

1 You silly Galatians! Who tricked you? You saw Jesus Christ shown as nailed to a cross right before your eyes. 2 I want to ask you one thing. Did you get God's Spirit by doing the law, or by hearing and trusting? 3 Are you that silly? You started with God's Spirit. Now are you trying to finish by your own strength? 4 Did you go through all that for nothing? I hope it was not for nothing. 5 Does God give you His Spirit and do great works among you because you do the law, or because you hear and trust?

Abraham and Faith

6 Abraham trusted God. God counted that as right with Him. 7 So know this: people who trust are Abraham's children. 8 Long ago, God's Book showed that God would make all nations right with Him by trust. So it told Abraham the good news: "All nations will be blessed through you." 9 So people who trust are blessed with Abraham, the man who trusted.

10 But people who try to be right by doing the law are under a curse. God's Book says, "Cursed is everyone who does not keep doing all that is written in the law book." 11 It is clear that no one becomes right with God by the law. God's Book says, "The one who is right will live by trust." 12 The law is not based on trust. It says, "The one who does these things will live by them."

13 Christ set us free from the law's curse. He became a curse for us. God's Book says, "Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree." 14 He did this so Abraham's blessing could come to all nations through Christ Jesus. Then we could get the promised Spirit by trusting.

God's Promise and the Law

15 Brothers, here is a human example. Once a person's agreement is made strong, no one throws it out or adds to it. 16 God's promises were given to Abraham and to his family line. It does not say "and to families," like many. It says "and to your family line," like one. That one is Christ. 17 Here is what I mean. The law came 430 years later. It does not cancel the agreement God made before. So it cannot take away the promise. 18 If we get what God promised by the law, then it is not by promise anymore. But God gave it to Abraham as a gift through a promise. 19 Then why was the law given? It was added because people broke God's rules. It lasted until the family line came, the one the promise was about. The law was given through angels and put into effect by a middle man. 20 A middle man stands between two sides. But God is one.

21 So is the law against God's promises? No, not at all! If a law had been given that could give life, then we would become right with God by the law. 22 But God's Book put everyone under sin's power. It did this so the promise could be given to those who trust in Jesus Christ. 23 Before trust came, we were locked up by the law. We were held there until trust was shown. 24 So the law was our guide until Christ came. It helped us become right with God by trusting. 25 Now that trust has come, we are not under that guide anymore.

26 You are all God's children through trust in Christ Jesus. 27 All of you who were baptized into Christ have put on Christ. 28 There is no Jew or Greek. There is no slave or free person. There is no male or female. You are all one in Christ Jesus. 29 If you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's family line. You will get the promise.
