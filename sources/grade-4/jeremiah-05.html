God Looks for One Good Person

1 Run back and forth through the streets of Jerusalem. Look and learn. Search the town squares. If you find even one man, if one person does what is right and wants to be faithful, then I will forgive the city. 2 Even when they say, "I swear by the LORD," they are lying.

The People Will Not Change

3 O the LORD, don't Your eyes look for truth? You struck them, but they did not feel pain. You wore them out, but they would not take correction. They made their faces harder than rock. They would not turn back. 4 I said, "Surely they are only poor. They have acted foolishly, for they do not know the way of the LORD, the law of their God."

5 I will go to the great ones and speak to them, for they know the way of the LORD, the law of their God. But these people have thrown off God's rule together. They have broken free from His control. 6 So a lion from the forest will attack them. A wolf from the desert will destroy them. A leopard will wait near their cities. Everyone who goes out will be torn apart, because their sins are many and they keep turning away from God.

They Were Not Faithful

7 "How can I forgive you? Your children have left Me and sworn by gods that are not real. I gave them what they needed, but they were not faithful to Me. They crowded into a prostitute's house. 8 They were like well-fed horses. Each one wanted his neighbor's wife.

9 "Should I not punish them for this? Should I not take revenge on a nation like this?" says the LORD. 10 Go up through her vineyards and destroy them, but do not destroy everything. Cut off her branches, for they do not belong to the LORD.

11 "For the family of Israel and the family of Judah have been very unfaithful to Me," says the LORD.

12 They have lied about the LORD and said, "He will not do anything. No trouble will come to us. We will not see war or hunger." 13 The prophets are just wind. God's word is not in them. So may what they say happen to them.

God's Word Is Like Fire

14 So this is what the LORD God of Armies says: "Because you have spoken this word, look, I will make My words in your mouth like fire. This people will be like wood, and the fire will burn them up. 15 Look, I am bringing a nation from far away against you, people of Israel," says the LORD. "It is a very old nation. You do not know its language, and you will not understand what it says." 16 Their arrows are like an open grave. All their soldiers are strong.

17 They will eat your harvest and your food. They will eat your sons and daughters. They will eat your flocks and herds. They will eat your vines and fig trees. With the sword they will destroy the strong cities you trust in. 18 "Yet even in those days," says the LORD, "I will not destroy you completely."

19 "When you ask why the the LORD our God did all this to us, you are to tell them that just as you left Me and served other gods in your land, you will serve foreign people in a land that is not yours."

Foolish People

20 Tell this in the family of Jacob. Announce it in Judah. 21 "Listen to this, foolish people, who have eyes but do not see, who have ears but do not hear."

22 "Do you not fear Me?" says the LORD. "Do you not shake before Me? I made the sand a wall for the sea. It is a limit that lasts forever, and the sea cannot cross it. Its waves may crash, but they cannot win. They may roar, but they cannot cross it. 23 But these people have stubborn and rebellious hearts. They have turned away and gone. 24 They do not say in their hearts that they should fear the the LORD our God, who gives rain at the right time, both fall and spring rain, and keeps the harvest time for us.

25 Your sins have turned these good things away. Your sins have kept good from you. 26 For wicked men are found among My people. They hide like bird catchers. They set traps and catch people. 27 Like a cage full of birds, their houses are full of tricks. That is why they became great and rich. 28 They are fat and smooth. They do very bad things. They do not help orphans, and they do not stand up for poor people.

A Terrible Thing

29 "Should I not punish them for this? Should I not take revenge on a nation like this?" says the LORD. 30 A terrible and shocking thing has happened in the land. 31 The prophets say false things. The priests rule by their own power. My people love it that way. But what will you do in the end?
