A Warning for Jerusalem

1 What is wrong with you? Why did you all go up to the roofs? 2 The city is full of noise. The town is in an uproar. It used to be a happy city. Your dead did not die by the sword. They did not die in battle. 3 All your leaders ran away together. They were caught without a fight. Everyone who was found was caught too. They ran far away.

The Lord's Day of Trouble

4 So I said, "Leave me alone so I can cry hard. Do not rush to comfort me about the trouble of my people." 5 The Lord, the LORD of Hosts, has a day of noise, trouble, and confusion in the Valley of Vision. Walls are broken down. People cry to the mountain. 6 Elam took up its quiver. It came with chariots and horsemen. Kir got ready with shields. 7 Your best valleys were full of chariots. Horsemen stood at the gate.

8 The LORD took away Judah's protection. That day, you looked at the weapons in the Forest House. 9 You saw the broken walls of David's city. You gathered water from the lower pool. 10 You counted the houses in Jerusalem. You tore down houses to make the wall stronger. 11 You made a pool between the walls for the old water. But you did not look to its Maker. You did not see its Creator from far away.

No Sorrow, No Forgiveness

12 That day, the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, called for crying, sadness, shaved heads, and rough clothes. 13 But instead, there was joy and gladness. People killed cows and sheep. They ate meat and drank wine. They said, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die." 14 The LORD of Hosts spoke in my ears: "This wrong will not be forgiven. You will die for it," says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

A Message for Shebna

15 This is what the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, says: "Go to Shebna, the man in charge of the palace. 16 What are you doing here? Who gave you the right to dig a grave here? You cut a grave high up. You carved a resting place for yourself in the rock. 17 The LORD will throw you away with force. He will wrap you up. 18 He will roll you up like a ball and throw you into a wide land. There you will die. There your fine chariots will stay. They will shame your master's house. 19 I will push you out of your job. He will pull you down from your place."

Eliakim Will Take His Place

20 That day, I will call my servant Eliakim, son of Hilkiah. 21 I will give him your robe. I will tie your belt on him. I will put your power in his hand. He will be like a father to the people of Jerusalem and Judah. 22 I will put the key of David's house on his shoulder. He will open, and no one will shut. He will shut, and no one will open. 23 I will make him like a strong peg in a safe place. He will be a seat of honor for his family. 24 All the honor of his family will hang on him. His children and relatives will depend on him. Even small things, from bowls to jars, will hang on him. 25 That day, says the LORD of Hosts, the peg in the safe place will be pulled out. It will be cut down and fall. The load on it will be cut off. The LORD has spoken.
