Jesus Is Born

1 At that time, Caesar Augustus sent out an order. Everyone in the world had to be counted. 2 This was the first time people were counted while Quirinius was governor of Syria. 3 Everyone went to his own town to be counted.

4 Joseph went up from the town of Nazareth in Galilee to Judea. He went to Bethlehem, the town of David. He went because he was from David's family. 5 He went to be counted with Mary. She was promised to marry him, and she was going to have a baby.

6 While they were there, the time came for her baby to be born. 7 She had her first son. She wrapped Him in cloths and laid Him in a feeding box. There was no room for them in the place to stay.

Shepherds Hear the Good News

8 There were shepherds in the fields nearby. They were watching their sheep at night. 9 An angel of the Lord stood by them. The glory of the Lord shone around them. They were very afraid.

10 "Do not be afraid. I bring you good news. It will bring great joy to all people. 11 Today in David's town a Savior is born for you. He is Christ the Lord. 12 This is how you will know Him. You will find a baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a feeding box."

13 Suddenly a great crowd of angels from heaven was with the angel. They praised God and said, 14 "Glory to God in the highest. Peace on earth to people He is pleased with."

15 When the angels went back to heaven, the shepherds said to each other, "Let's go to Bethlehem. Let's see this thing the Lord has told us about."

16 They hurried and found Mary, Joseph, and the baby. The baby was lying in the feeding box. 17 When they saw Him, they told what had been said about this child. 18 Everyone who heard it was amazed at what the shepherds said. 19 Mary kept all these things in her heart. She thought about them. 20 The shepherds went back. They praised and thanked God for all they had heard and seen. It was just as they had been told.

Jesus Is Brought to the Temple

21 When the baby was eight days old, He was circumcised. He was named Jesus. That was the name the angel gave before Mary was pregnant with Him. 22 When the time came for Mary and Joseph to be made clean, they brought Him to Jerusalem. They brought Him to give Him to the Lord.

23 It is written in the law of the Lord, "Every first baby boy belongs to the Lord." 24 They also gave an offering, as the law of the Lord says. They gave two doves or two young pigeons.

Simeon and Anna Praise God

25 In Jerusalem there was a man named Simeon. He was good and loved God. He was waiting for God to comfort Israel. The Holy Spirit was on him. 26 The Holy Spirit had told him he would not die before he saw the Lord's Christ. 27 The Spirit led him to the temple. When the parents brought in the child Jesus, Simeon came too. They were doing what the law said. 28 Simeon took the baby in His arms. He praised God and said,

29 "Now, Lord, You can let Your servant go in peace. 30 I have seen Your salvation with my own eyes. 31 You made it ready for all people. 32 It is a light to show the way to the nations. It brings glory to Your people Israel."

33 Jesus' father and mother were amazed at what was said about Him. 34 Simeon blessed them. Then he said to Mary, "This child will cause many in Israel to fall and to rise. He will be a sign people speak against. 35 Then the secret thoughts of many hearts will be shown. And a sword will go through your own soul too."

36 There was also a prophet named Anna. She was the daughter of Phanuel, from the tribe of Asher. She was very old. She had lived with her husband seven years after she got married. 37 Then she was a widow until she was 84. She stayed at the temple. She worshiped God night and day. She fasted and prayed. 38 At that time she came up to them. She thanked God. She talked about the baby to all who were waiting for Jerusalem to be set free.

Jesus Grows in Nazareth

39 When Joseph and Mary had done everything the law of the Lord said, they went back to Galilee. They went to their own town, Nazareth. 40 The child grew strong. He was filled with wisdom. God's grace was on Him.

Jesus Talks with Teachers

41 Every year Jesus' parents went to Jerusalem for the Passover feast. 42 When He was twelve years old, they went up as usual for the feast. 43 After the feast days were over, they started home. But the boy Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. His parents did not know it. 44 They thought He was with the group. They traveled one day. Then they looked for Him among family and friends. 45 They did not find Him. So they went back to Jerusalem to look for Him.

46 After three days they found Him in the temple. He was sitting with the teachers. He was listening to them and asking them questions. 47 Everyone who heard Him was amazed at His understanding and His answers.

48 When His parents saw Him, they were surprised. His mother said, "Child, why did You do this to us? Your father and I have been looking for You. We were very worried." 49 Jesus said, "Why were you looking for Me? Did you not know I had to be in My Father's house?" 50 They did not understand what He said.

51 He went down to Nazareth with them. He obeyed them. His mother kept all these things in her heart. 52 Jesus grew in wisdom and in years. He grew in favor with God and with people.
