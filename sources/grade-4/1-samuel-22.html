David Hides in a Cave

1 David left that place and ran away to a cave at Adullam. His brothers and his father's family heard about it. They came down to him there.

2 Many men came to him. Some were in trouble. Some owed money. Some were sad and angry. David became their leader. There were about four hundred men.

David Takes His Parents to Moab

3 David left there and went to Mizpeh in Moab. He said to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and mother stay with you until I know what God will do for me.”

4 David brought them to the king of Moab. They stayed with him while David was in the safe place.

Gad Tells David to Go to Judah

5 The prophet Gad said to David, “Do not stay in the safe place. Go into the land of Judah.” So David went and came to the forest of Hereth.

Saul Speaks to His Men

6 Saul heard that people knew where David and his men were. Saul was sitting under a tree in Ramah. He had his spear in his hand. His servants stood around him.

7 Saul said to his servants standing around him, “Listen, men of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse give fields and grape farms to all of you? Will he make you leaders of thousands and hundreds? 8 You have all turned against me. No one tells me when my son makes a promise with the son of Jesse. No one feels sorry for me. No one tells me that my son has stirred up my servant to hide and wait to hurt me, as he does today.”

Doeg Tells on David

9 Doeg the Edomite was there with Saul's men. He said, “I saw the son of Jesse come to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob. 10 Ahimelech asked the LORD for him. He gave him food. He also gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”

Saul Calls the Priests

11 Then the king sent for Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and all his father's family, the priests at Nob. They all came to the king.

12 Saul said, “Listen now, son of Ahitub.”

Ahimelech said, “Here I am, my lord.”

Saul Blames Ahimelech

13 Saul said, “Why have you and the son of Jesse turned against me? You gave him bread and a sword. You asked God for him. Now he rises up to hide and wait to hurt me, as he does today.”

14 Ahimelech answered the king, “Who among all your servants is as loyal as David? He is your son-in-law. He leads your guards. He is honored in your house. 15 Did I just now begin to ask God for him? I would never do that! Do not blame me or my father's family. I did not know about this, big or small.”

Saul Orders the Priests Killed

16 The king said, “You will surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's family.”

17 The king said to the guards standing around him, “Turn and kill the priests of the LORD. Their hands are with David. They knew he was running away, and they did not tell me. The king's servants would not even raise a hand to strike the priests of the LORD.”

18 The king said to Doeg, “You turn and strike the priests.” Doeg the Edomite turned and struck the priests. That day he killed eighty-five men who wore priest clothes.

Doeg Kills the People of Nob

19 He also killed the people of Nob, the city of the priests. He killed men and women, children and babies. He killed oxen, donkeys, and sheep with his sword.

20 But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped. His name was Abiathar. He ran to David.

21 Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the LORD.

David Speaks to Abiathar

22 David said to Abiathar, “I knew that day when Doeg the Edomite was there. I knew he would tell Saul. I have caused the death of all your father's family. 23 Stay with me. Do not be afraid. The man who wants to kill you wants to kill me too. You will be safe with me.”
