God Keeps His People Safe

1 People who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion. It will not move, and it stands forever. 2 Just as mountains stand all around Jerusalem, the LORD stands all around His people now and forever.

3 The bad rule of evil people will not stay on the land given to good people. Then good people won't reach out their hands to do wrong. 4 The LORD, do good to those who are good, and to those with right hearts.

5 But people who turn to crooked ways, the LORD will lead them away with those who do bad things. May Israel have peace.
