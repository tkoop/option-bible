The River of Life

1 Then the angel showed me a river of water that gives life. It was clear like glass. It flowed from the throne of God and the Lamb. 2 The river ran down the middle of the city's main street. On each side of the river grew the tree of life. It had twelve kinds of fruit. It gave fruit every month. Its leaves healed the countries.

3 Nothing will be cursed anymore. The throne of God and the Lamb will be there. His servants will worship Him. 4 They will see His face. His name will be on their foreheads. 5 There will be no more night. They will not need light from a lamp or from the sun. The Lord God will give them light. They will rule forever and ever.

The Angel Speaks

6 Then the angel said to me, "These words are true and can be trusted. The Lord God, who is over the spirits of the prophets, sent His angel to show His servants what must soon happen."

Jesus Is Coming Soon

7 "Look! I am coming soon. Blessed is the one who obeys the words of this prophecy."

8 I, John, heard and saw these things. When I heard and saw them, I fell down. I worshiped at the feet of the angel who showed them to me.

9 But he said to me, "Don't do that! I am a servant just like you, your brothers who are prophets, and everyone who obeys this book. Worship God!"

10 Then he said to me, "Do not keep these words secret. The time is near. 11 Let the one who does wrong keep doing wrong. Let the one who is dirty keep being dirty. Let the one who does right keep doing right. Let the one who is holy keep being holy."

12 "Look! I am coming soon. I will bring My reward with Me. I will pay each person for what they have done. 13 I am the Alpha and the Omega. I am the First and the Last. I am the Beginning and the End."

14 "Blessed are those who wash their robes. They will have the right to eat from the tree of life. They will go through the gates into the city. 15 But outside are unclean people, those who use magic, those who are immoral, murderers, idol worshipers, and everyone who loves and tells lies."

16 "I, Jesus, sent My angel to tell you these things for the churches. I am the Root and the descendant of David. I am the bright Morning Star."

Come and Drink

17 The Spirit and the bride say, "Come!"

Let the one who hears say, "Come!"

Let the thirsty one come. Let anyone who wants it take the water of life for free.

Final Words

18 I warn everyone who hears the words of this prophecy: If anyone adds to them, God will add to that person the troubles written in this book.

19 If anyone takes away from the words of this prophecy, God will take away that person's share in the tree of life and in the holy city. This is written in this book.

20 The One who says these things says, "Yes, I am coming soon."

Amen. Come, Lord Jesus!

21 May the kindness of the Lord Jesus be with all God's holy people. Amen.
