A Story About Not Giving Up on Prayer

1 Jesus told His followers a story. He wanted them to pray all the time and not give up.

2 He said, "In one town there was a judge. He did not fear God. He did not care what people thought. 3 A widow lived there. She kept coming to him and asking for fair help against the man who hurt her. 4 For a while he said no. 5 But then he said to himself that he did not fear God and did not care what people thought. Still, the widow kept bothering him. So he would help her, so she would stop bothering him."

6 The Lord said, "Listen to what the bad judge said. 7 Will not God help His chosen people? They cry to Him day and night. Will He wait too long to help them? 8 I tell you, He will help them soon. But when the Son of Man comes, will He find people who trust Him?"

Two Men Pray at the Temple

9 Jesus told this story to some people who thought they were good and looked down on others.

10 Two men went up to the temple to pray. One was a Pharisee. The other was a tax collector.

11 The Pharisee stood alone and prayed, "God, I thank You that I am not like other people. I am not a thief. I am not unfair. I am not a cheater in marriage. I am not even like this tax collector. 12 I go without food two days each week. I give one tenth of all I get."

13 But the tax collector stood far away. He would not even look up to heaven. He hit his chest and said, "God, be kind to me. I am a sinner."

14 Jesus said, "I tell you, this man went home right with God. The other man did not. Everyone who lifts himself up will be brought down. Everyone who puts himself down will be lifted up."

Jesus Welcomes Little Children

15 People brought babies to Jesus. They wanted Him to touch them. The followers saw this. They told the people to stop.

16 But Jesus called the children to Him. He said, "Let the little children come to Me. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like them. 17 I tell you the truth. Anyone who does not accept God's kingdom like a little child will never enter it."

A Rich Leader Asks About Life That Never Ends

18 A leader asked Him, "Good Teacher, what must I do to have life that never ends?"

19 Jesus said, "Why do you call Me good? No one is good except God alone. 20 You know the rules. Do not cheat in marriage. Do not kill. Do not steal. Do not lie about others. Honor your father and mother."

21 The man said, "I have kept all these since I was young."

22 When Jesus heard this, He said to him, "You still need one thing. Sell all you have. Give it to poor people. Then you will have treasure in heaven. Come, follow Me."

23 When the man heard this, he became very sad. He was very rich.

24 Jesus looked at him and said, "It is hard for rich people to enter God's kingdom. 25 It is easier for a camel to go through the tiny hole in a needle than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom."

26 The people who heard this asked, "Then who can be saved?"

27 Jesus said, "What people cannot do, God can do."

28 Peter said, "Look, we left everything to follow You!"

29 Jesus said, "I tell you the truth. No one who leaves home, wife, brothers, sisters, parents, or children for God's kingdom 30 will get much more in this life. In the life to come, they will get life that never ends."

Jesus Talks About What Will Happen to Him

31 Jesus took the twelve followers aside. He said, "Look, we are going up to Jerusalem. Everything God's helpers wrote about the Son of Man will come true. 32 He will be handed over to people who are not Jews. They will make fun of Him. They will say mean things to Him. They will spit on Him. 33 They will beat Him. They will kill Him. On the third day, He will rise again."

34 The followers did not understand any of this. Its meaning was hidden from them. They did not know what He was talking about.

Jesus Heals a Blind Man

35 As Jesus came near Jericho, a blind man sat by the road. He was begging.

36 He heard the crowd going by. He asked what was happening. 37 People told him, "Jesus of Nazareth is passing by."

38 He called out, "Jesus, Son of David, be kind to me!"

39 The people in front told him to be quiet. But he shouted even more, "Son of David, be kind to me!"

40 Jesus stopped. He told them to bring the man to Him. When the man came near, Jesus asked him, 41 "What do you want Me to do for you?" The man said he wanted to see.

42 Jesus said, "Now you can see. Your faith has healed you."

43 Right away the man could see. He followed Jesus. He praised God. All the people saw it. They praised God too.
