The Story of the Vineyard

1 Jesus began to speak to them in stories. A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it. He dug a wine press. He built a tower. He rented the vineyard to farmers. Then he went away. 2 At harvest time, he sent a servant to the farmers to get some fruit from the vineyard.

3 The farmers took the servant, beat him, and sent him away with nothing.

4 Then the man sent another servant. They hit him on the head and treated him badly.

5 He sent another one, and they killed him. He sent many more. Some they beat. Some they killed.

6 The man had one son left. He loved his son. He sent him last. He thought, “They will respect my son.”

7 But the farmers said to each other, “This is the one who will get everything. Come, let’s kill him. Then the vineyard will be ours.”

8 So they took him, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.

9 What will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and kill those farmers. Then he will give the vineyard to others. 10 Haven’t you read this part of the Bible? “The stone the builders said no to has become the most important stone. 11 The Lord did this, and it is wonderful to us.”

12 They wanted to arrest Jesus. They knew He told the story against them. But they were afraid of the crowd. So they left Him and went away.

A Question About Taxes

13 Some Pharisees and some Herodians came to Jesus. They wanted to trap Him with His words.

14 They said, “Teacher, we know You are honest. You do not care what people think. You teach God’s way truly. Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar, or not? Should we pay, or not?”

15 Jesus knew they were pretending. He said, “Why are you testing Me? Bring Me a coin. I want to see it.”

16 They brought a coin.

Jesus asked, “Whose picture is this? Whose words are on it?”

They said, “Caesar’s.”

17 Jesus said, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s. Give to God what is God’s.” They were amazed at Him.

A Question About Life After Death

18 Some Sadducees came to Jesus. They do not believe people rise from the dead. They asked Him,

19 “Teacher, Moses wrote this for us: If a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife but no child, the brother should marry her. Then they can have children for the dead brother. 20 There were seven brothers. The first brother married her. He died and left no child. 21 The second brother married her and died. He left no child. The third brother did the same. 22 All seven brothers married her and left no children. Last of all, the woman died. 23 When people rise from the dead, whose wife will she be? All seven had married her.”

24 Jesus said, “You are wrong. You do not know the Bible or God’s power. 25 When people rise from the dead, they do not marry. They are not given in marriage. They are like angels in heaven. 26 Now, about dead people rising: Have you not read in Moses’ book, at the bush? God said to Moses, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’ 27 He is not the God of dead people. He is the God of living people. You are very wrong.”

The Most Important Command

28 One of the teachers of the law came. He heard them arguing. He saw that Jesus answered well. He asked Jesus, “Which command is first of all?”

29 Jesus answered, “The first is this: ‘Hear, Israel. The Lord our God is one Lord. 30 Love the Lord your God with all your heart, all your soul, all your mind, and all your strength.’ 31 The second is this: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ No other command is bigger than these.”

32 The teacher said to Jesus, “Teacher, You are right. God is one. There is no other God but Him. 33 To love God with all your heart, all your understanding, and all your strength, and to love your neighbor as yourself, is more than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.”

34 Jesus saw that he answered wisely. He said, “You are not far from God’s kingdom.” After that, no one dared ask Him anything.

Jesus Asks About the Christ

35 While Jesus was teaching in the temple, He asked, “How can the teachers of the law say that the Christ is David’s son?”

36 David himself said by the Holy Spirit, “The Lord said to my Lord, ‘Sit at My right hand until I put Your enemies under Your feet.’”

37 David calls Him Lord. So how can He be David’s son? The crowd listened to Him with joy.

Watch Out for Proud Teachers

38 Jesus said in His teaching, “Watch out for the teachers of the law. They like to walk in long robes. They like greetings in the marketplaces. They like the best seats in the places of worship. They like the best places at dinners. 39 They eat up widows’ houses. 40 They pray long prayers to show off. These men will be punished even more.”

The Widow’s Gift

41 Jesus sat across from the temple money box. He watched how the crowd put money into it. Many rich people put in a lot.

42 Then a poor widow came. She put in two small coins. Together they were worth very little.

43 Jesus called His disciples. He said, “I tell you the truth. This poor widow put in more than all the others who put money into the box.”

44 They all gave from what they had extra. But she gave from her need. She put in all she had. It was her whole life.
