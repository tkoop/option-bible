Elihu Answers Job

1 Then Elihu answered and said:

Listen to My Words

2 "Hear my words, you wise men. Give ear to me, you who know things. 3 The ear tests words, like the mouth tastes food. 4 Let us choose what is right. Let us know among us what is good.

Job Said He Was Right

5 "Job said, 'I am right. God took away my fair chance. 6 People call me a liar, but I have not sinned. I am hurt bad, but I did no wrong.' 7 What man is like Job? He takes in mean words like water. 8 He goes with bad men and walks with people who do wrong. 9 He said, 'It does not help a man to be good to God.'

God Is Always Fair

10 "So listen to me, you men with good hearts. God would never do wrong. The Almighty would never do bad. 11 He pays a man for his work. He lets a man find what his road brings. 12 Truly, God will not do wrong. The Almighty will not twist what is right. 13 Who told Him how to run the earth? Who put the whole world in place? 14 If He set His heart on it, He would take back His spirit and breath. 15 All people would die together, and man would go back to dust.

16 "If you understand, hear this. Listen to my words. 17 Can someone who hates what is right rule? Would you say the good and mighty God is bad? 18 Is it right to call a king worthless, or leaders bad? 19 How much less to God. He does not favor princes. He does not treat rich people better than poor people. They are all made by His hands. 20 They can die in a moment. At night, people shake and pass away. Strong men are taken away without anyone's hand.

God Sees Everything

21 "His eyes are on a man's ways. He sees all his steps. 22 There is no dark place or deep shadow where bad men can hide. 23 God does not need to watch a man longer before bringing him to court. 24 He breaks strong men without asking questions. He puts other men in their place. 25 So He knows what they do. He turns night on them, and they are crushed. 26 He hits them like bad men where people can see. 27 This is because they turned away from Him and did not think about His ways. 28 So the cry of poor people comes to Him. He hears the cry of people who hurt.

God Is Fair and Just

29 "When He is quiet, who can blame Him? When He hides His face, who can see Him? It is the same for a nation or a man. 30 This keeps bad men from ruling and trapping the people. 31 Has anyone said to God, 'I have paid for my wrong. I will not do wrong again; 32 teach me what I do not see. If I did wrong, I will not do it again'? 33 Should He pay you back your way, just because you do not like it? You choose, not I. So say what you know.

Job Should Be Tested

34 "Men who understand will say to me, and a wise man who hears me will say: 35 'Job speaks without knowing. His words show no sense.' 36 Job should be tested to the end, because he answers like bad men. 37 He adds sin to his sin. He mocks us, and says many words against God."
