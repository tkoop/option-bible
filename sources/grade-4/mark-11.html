Jesus Rides into Jerusalem

1 As Jesus and His friends came near Jerusalem, near Bethphage and Bethany by the Mount of Olives, He sent two of His followers. 2 He said to them, "Go into the village across from you. As soon as you go in, you will find a young donkey tied up. No one has ever sat on it. Untie it and bring it here. 3 If anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord needs it. He will send it back soon.'"

4 They went and found the young donkey outside by the road, tied at a door. They untied it. 5 Some people standing there asked, "What are you doing? Why are you untying the donkey?" 6 They told them what Jesus had said, and the people let them go.

7 They brought the young donkey to Jesus. They put their coats on it, and Jesus sat on it. 8 Many people spread their coats on the road. Others cut branches from the fields and spread them on the road.

9 The people walking ahead and behind shouted, "Save us! God bless the One who comes in the name of the Lord! 10 God bless the kingdom of our father David that is coming! Save us, God in heaven!"

11 Jesus went into Jerusalem and into the temple. He looked all around. It was already late, so He went out to Bethany with the twelve.

Jesus Curses a Fig Tree

12 The next day, as they left Bethany, Jesus was hungry. 13 He saw a fig tree far away with leaves on it. He went to see if it had fruit. But when He got there, He found only leaves. It was not the time for figs. 14 Jesus said to the tree, "No one will ever eat fruit from you again." His followers heard Him say this.

Jesus Clears the Temple

15 They came to Jerusalem again. Jesus went into the temple. He began to throw out the people who were buying and selling there. He turned over the tables of the money changers and the chairs of those selling doves. 16 He did not let anyone carry things through the temple.

17 He taught them and said, "It is written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.' But you have made it a hideout for robbers."

18 The leading priests and teachers of the law heard this. They looked for a way to kill Him. They were afraid of Him, because all the people were amazed at His teaching. 19 When evening came, they went out of the city.

The Dried-Up Fig Tree

20 The next morning, as they passed by, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots. 21 Peter remembered and said to Him, "Teacher, look! The fig tree you cursed has dried up."

22 Jesus said to them, "Have faith in God. 23 I tell you the truth: If anyone says to this mountain, 'Move and throw yourself into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will happen, it will happen for him. 24 So I tell you, when you pray and ask for anything, believe that you have received it, and you will have it. 25 When you stand and pray, if you have anything against anyone, forgive them. Then your Father in heaven will forgive you for your sins."

Leaders Question Jesus

27 They came to Jerusalem again. As Jesus was walking in the temple, the leading priests, teachers of the law, and elders came to Him.

28 They asked Him, "By what right do you do these things? Who gave you this right?"

29 Jesus said to them, "I will ask you one thing. Answer Me, and I will tell you by what right I do these things. 30 Was John's baptism from heaven or from people? Answer Me."

31 They talked among themselves. They said, "If we say it came from heaven, He will ask why you did not believe John. 32 But if we say it came from people, we are afraid of the crowd. Everyone thinks John was a true prophet."

33 So they answered Jesus, "We do not know."

Jesus said to them, "Then I will not tell you by what right I do these things."
