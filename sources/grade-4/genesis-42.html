Jacob Sends His Sons to Egypt

1 Jacob saw that there was grain in Egypt. He said to his sons, "Why are you staring at each other? 2 I heard there is grain in Egypt. Go down there and buy some for us. Then we can live and not die." 3 So ten of Joseph's brothers went down to buy grain in Egypt. 4 But Jacob did not send Joseph's brother Benjamin with them. He was afraid something bad might happen to him. 5 Israel's sons came with the others to buy grain, because there was no food in Canaan.

Joseph Recognizes His Brothers

6 Joseph was the boss of the land. He sold grain to all the people. Joseph's brothers came and bowed down to him. Their faces touched the ground.

7 Joseph saw his brothers and knew them. But he acted like a stranger to them. He spoke harshly to them. He asked, "Where are you from?" They said, "From Canaan. We came to buy food." 8 Joseph knew his brothers, but they did not know him.

9 Joseph remembered the dreams he had about them. He said to them, "You are spies. You came to see the weak parts of our land."

10 They said, "No, my lord. Your servants came to buy food. 11 We are all sons of one man. Your servants are honest men. We are not spies."

12 He said, "No. You came to see the weak parts of our land."

13 They said, "Your servants are twelve brothers. We are sons of one man in Canaan. The youngest is with our father today. One brother is gone."

Joseph Tests His Brothers

14 Joseph said, "I told you. You are spies. 15 I will test you this way. As Pharaoh lives, you will not leave here unless your youngest brother comes here. 16 Send one of you to get your brother. The rest will stay locked up. Then I will test your words. If they are not true, then, as Pharaoh lives, you are spies." 17 He put them in prison for three days.

Joseph Shows Kindness

18 On the third day, Joseph said to them, "Do this and live. I fear God. 19 If you are honest, let one brother stay in prison. The rest can go take grain home for your hungry families. 20 Bring your youngest brother to me. Then your words will be proven true, and you will not die." They did what he said.

The Brothers Feel Guilty

21 They said to each other, "We are guilty about our brother. We saw how sad he was when he begged us, but we did not listen. That is why this trouble came to us."

22 Reuben said, "Did I not tell you not to hurt the boy? You did not listen. Now we must answer for his blood."

23 They did not know Joseph understood them. A man was translating for them. 24 Joseph turned away and cried. Then he came back and spoke to them. He took Simeon from them and tied him up while they watched.

Joseph Sends Them Home

25 Joseph told his men to fill their bags with grain. He also told them to put each man's money back in his sack. And he told them to give them food for the trip. They did so. 26 The brothers loaded the grain on their donkeys and left. 27 At the place where they stopped for the night, one brother opened his sack to feed his donkey. He saw his money at the top of the sack.

28 He said to his brothers, "My money has been put back! Look, it is in my sack." Their hearts sank. They were afraid.

They said to each other, "What has God done to us?"

The Brothers Return to Jacob

29 They came to their father Jacob in Canaan. They told him everything that happened. 30 They said, "The man who is boss of that land spoke harshly to us. He treated us like spies. 31 We said, 'We are honest men, not spies.' 32 We are twelve brothers, sons of our father. One brother is gone. The youngest is with our father in Canaan."

33 The man who is boss of that land said, "This is how I will know you are honest. Leave one brother with me. Take grain for your homes and go. 34 Bring your youngest brother to me. Then I will know you are not spies but honest. I will give your brother back to you, and you can trade in that land."

35 As they emptied their sacks, each man found his bundle of money in his sack. They and their father saw the money bundles, and they were afraid. 36 Their father Jacob said, "You have taken my children from me. Joseph is gone. Simeon is gone. Now you want Benjamin too. Everything is against me."

37 Reuben said to his father, "You may kill my two sons if I do not bring him back. Put him in my care. I will bring him back to you."

38 Jacob said, "My son will not go down with you. His brother is dead, and he is the only one left. If something bad happens on the trip you take, you will bring my gray head down to the grave in sorrow."
