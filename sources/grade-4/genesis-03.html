The Snake Tricks the Woman

1 The snake was trickier than any other wild animal the LORD God had made. He asked the woman, "Did God really say you must not eat fruit from any tree in the garden?"

2 The woman said to the snake, "We can eat fruit from the trees in the garden. 3 But God said we must not eat fruit from the tree in the middle of the garden. We must not even touch it, or we will die."

4 The snake said to the woman, "You will not die. 5 God knows that when you eat it, you will understand. You will be like God and know good from bad."

6 The woman saw that the tree was good for food. It was pretty to look at, and it could make a person wise. So she took some fruit and ate it. She gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate it too.

7 Then they understood, and they knew they were naked. So they sewed fig leaves together and made clothes for themselves.

Adam and Eve Hide from God

8 The man and his wife heard the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool part of the day. They hid from the LORD God among the trees.

9 The LORD God called to the man, "Where are you?"

10 The man said, "I heard Your voice in the garden. I was afraid because I was naked, so I hid."

11 The LORD God said, "Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree I told you not to eat from?"

12 The man said, "The woman You gave to be with me gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate it."

13 The LORD God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?"

The woman said, "The snake tricked me, and I ate."

God Speaks to the Snake, the Woman, and the Man

14 The LORD God said to the snake, "Because you did this, you are cursed more than all farm animals and wild animals. You will crawl on your belly and eat dust all your life. 15 I will make you and the woman hate each other. Your children and her children will be enemies. He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel."

16 To the woman He said, "I will make your pain much worse when you have children. You will give birth in pain. You will want your husband, and he will be in charge of you."

17 To Adam He said, "Because you listened to your wife and ate from the tree I told you not to eat from, the ground is cursed because of you. You will have to work hard to eat from it all your life. 18 It will grow thorns and weeds for you, and you will eat plants from the field. 19 You will sweat to eat your bread until you go back to the ground. You were made from dust, and you will return to dust."

Adam Names Eve

20 Adam named his wife Eve, because she would be the mother of all living people.

God Sends Them Out

21 The LORD God made clothes from animal skins for Adam and his wife, and He dressed them.

22 The LORD God said, "Look! The man has become like one of Us. He knows good and bad. Now he might reach out his hand, take fruit from the tree of life, eat it, and live forever."

23 So the LORD God sent him out of the garden of Eden to work the ground he came from.

24 He drove the man out. Then He put angels on the east side of the garden of Eden. He also put a flaming sword that turned every way to guard the way to the tree of life.
