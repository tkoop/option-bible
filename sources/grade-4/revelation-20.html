An Angel Locks Up Satan

1 I saw an angel come down from heaven. He had the key to the deep pit. He also had a big chain in his hand. 2 He grabbed the dragon. The dragon is the old snake. He is the Devil and Satan. The angel tied him up for a thousand years. 3 He threw him into the deep pit. He shut it and locked it. This was so Satan could not trick the nations anymore. It would last until the thousand years were over. After that, he must be set free for a short time.

People Who Rule With Christ

4 I saw thrones. People sat on them. They were given the power to judge. I also saw the souls of people who were killed. They died because they told about Jesus. They died because they obeyed God's word. They had not bowed down to the beast. They had not bowed down to its statue. They had not taken its mark on their forehead or hand. They came to life. They ruled with Christ for a thousand years.

5 The rest of the dead did not come to life. This was until the thousand years were over. This is the first rising from the dead. 6 Happy and holy are the people who take part in the first rising from the dead. The second death has no power over them. They will be priests for God and for Christ. They will rule with Him for a thousand years.

Satan Is Let Loose

7 When the thousand years are over, Satan will be set free from his prison. 8 He will go out and trick the nations all over the world. These are Gog and Magog. He will gather them for war. There will be as many of them as grains of sand by the sea. 9 They marched across the wide land. They surrounded the camp of God's holy people. They surrounded the city He loves. But fire came down from heaven. It burned them up. 10 The devil who tricked them was thrown into the lake of fire and burning sulfur. The beast and the false prophet are there too. They will suffer day and night forever and ever.

The Last Judgment

11 I saw a great white throne. I saw the One sitting on it. Earth and sky ran away from His face. There was no place left for them. 12 I saw the dead. Some were great. Some were small. They stood before the throne. Books were opened. Another book was opened too. It is the book of life. The dead were judged by what they had done. It was written in the books. 13 The sea gave up the dead who were in it. Death and the grave gave up the dead who were in them. Each person was judged by what they had done. 14 Then death and the grave were thrown into the lake of fire. This lake of fire is the second death. 15 Anyone whose name was not in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire.
