A Spring to Wash Away Sin

1 On that day, a spring will open for David's family and for the people of Jerusalem. It will wash away their sin and make them clean. 2 On that day, says the LORD of Armies, "I will take away the names of the false gods from the land. People will not remember them anymore. I will also remove the false prophets and the bad spirit from the land."

False Prophets Will Be Stopped

3 If a man still says he is a prophet, his father and mother who had him will say, "You will not live, because you lied and said it was from the LORD." Then they will stab him. 4 On that day, people who say they are prophets will be ashamed of their visions. They will not wear a rough hair coat to trick people.

5 He will say, "I am not a prophet. I am a farmer. Someone made me a slave when I was young."

6 If someone asks him, "What are these cuts on your body?"

He will answer, "I got them at my friends' house."

The Shepherd Is Struck

7 "Wake up, sword, against My Shepherd, against the man who is My friend," says the LORD of Armies. "Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will run away. I will punish the little ones too."

A Third Will Remain

8 In all the land, says the LORD, two-thirds of the people will die, and one-third will be left. 9 I will bring that third part through the fire. I will make them pure like silver is made pure. I will test them like gold is tested. They will call on My name, and I will answer them. I will say, "They are My people."

They will say, "The LORD is my God."
