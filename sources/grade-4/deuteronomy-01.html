Moses Speaks to Israel

1 These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel. They were in the desert, east of the Jordan River. They were in the Arabah, near Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab. 2 It takes eleven days to walk from Horeb to Kadesh Barnea by way of Mount Seir.

3 In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to the Israelites. He told them everything the the LORD had commanded him to say. 4 This was after he defeated Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og, king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei. 5 East of the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law. He said:

God Tells Israel to Move

6 The the LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb. He said, "You have stayed at this mountain long enough. 7 Turn and travel on. Go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all their neighbors. Go to the Arabah, the hills, the lowlands, the Negev, the coast, the land of the Canaanites, and Lebanon. Go as far as the great river, the Euphrates. 8 See, I have given you this land. Go in and take it. It is the land the the LORD promised to give to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and to their children after them."

Moses Says He Cannot Lead Alone

9 At that time I said to you, "I cannot carry you by myself. 10 The the LORD your God has made you grow. Today you are as many as the stars in the sky. 11 May the the LORD, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times more. May He bless you, just as He promised you. 12 How can I carry your troubles, your loads, and your arguments by myself? 13 Choose wise, understanding, and known men from your tribes. I will make them your leaders. They will be leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. They will be officers for your tribes."

14 You answered me and said, "What you said is good. We will do it."

Choosing Leaders and Judges

15 So I took the wise and known men from your tribes and made them leaders over you. They were leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. They were officers for your tribes. 16 At that time I told your judges, "Listen to your brothers. Judge fairly between a man and his brother or a stranger. 17 Do not favor one person over another in court. Listen to the small and the great alike. Do not be afraid of anyone, because judgment belongs to God. Bring me any case that is too hard for you, and I will hear it." 18 At that time I told you everything you should do.

They Travel to Kadesh

19 Then we left Horeb, just as the the LORD our God commanded us. We traveled through the big and scary desert you saw. We came to Kadesh Barnea. 20 I said to you, "You have come to the hill country of the Amorites. The the LORD our God is giving it to us. 21 See, the the LORD your God has given you this land. Go up and take it. Do not be afraid or discouraged. The the LORD your God spoke to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He said He would give it to you."

Spies Are Sent

22 Then all of you came to me and said, "Let us send men ahead of us. They can explore the land for us. They can bring back a report about the way we should go and the towns we will enter."

23 That seemed good to me. So I chose twelve men, one from each tribe. 24 They turned and went up into the hill country. They came to the Valley of Eshcol and spied it out.

25 They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us. They reported, "The land the the LORD our God is giving us is good."

The People Refuse to Go

26 But you did not want to go up. You turned against the the LORD your God. 27 You grumbled in your tents and said, "The the LORD hated us. That is why He brought us out of Egypt. He wanted to hand us over to the Amorites to destroy us. 28 Where can we go? Our brothers have scared us. They say, 'The people there are bigger and taller than we are. Their cities are big and have walls up to the sky. We even saw giants there.'"

Moses Encourages the People

29 Then I said to you, "Do not be afraid of them. 30 The the LORD your God goes ahead of you. He will fight for you. You saw everything He did for you in Egypt. 31 You saw how the the LORD your God carried you in the desert, like a man carries his son. He did this all along the way until you came to this place."

32 But even after this, you did not believe the the LORD your God. 33 He went ahead of you on the journey. At night He was in the fire, and during the day He was in the cloud. He looked for places for you to camp. He showed you the way you should go.

God Is Angry

34 The the LORD heard your words. He was angry and took an oath. He said, "35 Not one of these men from this evil generation will see the good land I promised to give to your fathers. 36 Only Caleb son of Jephunneh will see it. I will give him and his children the land he walked on, because he followed the the LORD fully."

37 The the LORD was angry with me too because of you. He said, "You will not go in there either."

38 But Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, will go in. Encourage him, because he will give Israel the land as their share. 39 Your little ones, whom you said would be taken, and your children who do not yet know right from wrong, will go in. I will give them the land, and they will own it. 40 But as for you, turn around and go into the desert by way of the Red Sea.

Israel Tries to Fight

41 Then you answered me, "We have sinned against the the LORD. We will go up and fight, just as the the LORD our God commanded us." So each of you grabbed your weapons and thought it was easy to go up the hill.

42 But the the LORD said to me, "Tell them, 'Do not go up or fight. I am not with you. You will be beaten by your enemies.'"

43 I told you this, but you did not listen. You turned against the the LORD's command and went up the hill anyway.

They Are Defeated

44 The Amorites who lived in those hills came out to meet you. They chased you like bees. They beat you in Seir all the way to Hormah. 45 You came back and cried to the the LORD, but the the LORD did not listen to your voice or pay attention to you. 46 So you stayed in Kadesh for many days, as long as you had stayed before.
