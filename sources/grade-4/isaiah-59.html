The Lord Can Still Save

1 The LORD is strong enough to save us. He can hear us.

Sin Keeps Us Apart from God

2 But your sins have put a wall between you and your God. Your wrongs have made Him turn away, so He will not hear you.

People Do Wrong

3 Your hands are stained with blood. Your fingers are stained with sin. Your lips tell lies. Your tongue speaks bad things.

No One Does Right

4 No one asks for what is right. No one is fair and true. People trust in empty things and tell lies. They plan trouble and cause harm.

Bad Plans Do Not Help

5 They hatch snake eggs and weave spider webs. If someone eats one of their eggs, he dies. If an egg is crushed, a snake comes out. 6 Their webs will not become clothes. They cannot cover themselves with what they do. What they do is wrong. They use their hands to hurt people.

They Hurt Others

7 Their feet run to do bad things. They hurry to kill innocent people. Their thoughts are full of wrong. They leave ruin and trouble behind them. 8 They do not know how to live in peace. What they do is not fair. They make twisted paths. No one who walks on them finds peace.

We Are in the Dark

9 So what is fair is far from us. Right living does not reach us. We wait for light, but we see only dark. We look for bright light, but we walk in deep dark. 10 We feel along the wall like blind people. We feel around like we have no eyes. We trip at noon like it is night. We are like dead people in empty places.

11 We all growl like bears. We moan like doves. We look for what is fair, but there is none. We look for God to save us, but He is far away.

We Admit Our Sins

12 Our wrongs are many before you. Our sins speak against us. We know our wrongs. Our sins stay with us. 13 We turned against the LORD and lied. We turned away from our God. We talk about hurting people and turning away. We plan lies in our hearts and speak them.

Nothing Is Fair

14 So what is fair is pushed back. Right living stands far away. Truth has fallen in the street. What is right cannot get in. 15 Truth is gone. Anyone who stops doing wrong gets robbed. The LORD saw this. It was bad in His eyes, because nothing was fair.

The Lord Steps In

16 He saw there was no one to help. He was amazed that no one stepped in. So His own power saved Him. His right living held Him up. 17 He put on right living like armor. He put a helmet of saving power on His head. He wore clothes of payback. He wrapped Himself in strong love for what is right.

He Will Pay Back His Enemies

18 He will pay back each person for what they do. He will be angry with those who fight Him. He will pay back His enemies, even faraway lands. 19 People in the west and east will fear the LORD's name. They will see His glory. When the enemy comes like a flood, the Spirit of the LORD will stand against him.

The Lord Keeps His Promise

20 The one who sets people free will come to Zion. He will come to those in Jacob who turn from sin, says the LORD. 21 The LORD says, "This is My promise to them. My Spirit is on you. My words are in your mouth. They will not leave your mouth, or your children's mouths, or your grandchildren's mouths, from now on and forever."
