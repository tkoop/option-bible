Rules for a Person with a Skin Disease

1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

2 "These are the rules for a person with a skin disease when he is made clean. He must be brought to the priest. 3 The priest must go outside the camp and look at him. If the skin disease is healed, 4 the priest must order two clean birds, cedar wood, red yarn, and hyssop for the person who is being made clean.

5 "The priest must order one bird to be killed over fresh water in a clay pot. 6 He must take the live bird, the cedar wood, the red yarn, and the hyssop. He must dip them and the live bird in the blood of the bird that was killed. The blood must be mixed with the water. 7 He must sprinkle the person who is being made clean seven times. Then the priest must say the person is clean. He must let the live bird go in the open field.

8 "The person must wash his clothes. He must shave off all his hair. He must bathe in water. Then he is clean. After that, he may come into the camp. But he must stay outside his tent for seven days. 9 On the seventh day, he must shave all his hair again. He must shave his head, his beard, and his eyebrows. He must wash his clothes. He must bathe in water. Then he is clean.

Offerings for a Person Who Is Made Clean

10 "On the eighth day, he must take two male lambs with nothing wrong with them. He must take one young female sheep one year old with nothing wrong with it. He must take three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and one log of oil. 11 The priest must bring the person who is being made clean and these offerings before the LORD at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 12 The priest must take one of the male lambs and offer it as a guilt offering. He must take the log of oil. He must wave them before the LORD. 13 He must kill the lamb in the holy place where the sin offering and the burnt offering are killed. The guilt offering belongs to the priest, like the sin offering. It is most holy.

14 "The priest must take some of the blood of the guilt offering. He must put it on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 15 The priest must take some of the oil from the log. He must pour it into the palm of his own left hand. 16 He must dip the finger of his right hand in the oil. He must sprinkle some of the oil before the LORD seven times. 17 He must put some of the oil left in his palm on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. He must put it on top of the blood of the guilt offering. 18 The rest of the oil in his palm must be put on the head of the person being made clean. The priest must make atonement for him before the LORD.

19 "Then the priest must offer the sin offering. He must make atonement for the person being made clean from his uncleanness. After that, he must kill the burnt offering. 20 The priest must burn the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar. He must make atonement for the person. Then he is clean.

Offerings for a Poor Person

21 "If the person is poor and cannot afford these offerings, he must take one male lamb for a guilt offering. It must be waved to make atonement for him. He must take one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and one log of oil. 22 He must take two doves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford. One must be a sin offering, and the other must be a burnt offering. 23 On the eighth day, he must bring them to the priest at the entrance to the tent of meeting, before the LORD, to be made clean.

24 "The priest must take the lamb for the guilt offering and the log of oil. He must wave them before the LORD. 25 He must kill the lamb. He must take some of its blood and put it on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 26 The priest must pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand. 27 He must dip the finger of his right hand in the oil. He must sprinkle some of the oil before the LORD seven times. 28 He must put some of the oil on the right earlobe of the person being made clean, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. He must put it on top of the blood of the guilt offering. 29 The rest of the oil must be put on the head of the person being made clean. The priest must make atonement for him before the LORD.

30 "He must offer one of the doves or pigeons, whichever the person can afford. One must be a sin offering, and the other a burnt offering, along with the grain offering. The priest must make atonement for the person before the LORD. Then he is clean. 31 These are the rules for a person with a skin disease who cannot afford the usual offerings for being made clean. 32 This is the law for a person who has a skin disease.

Rules for a House with Mold

33 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,

34 "When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving to you, I will put a disease like mold in a house in the land you own.

35 "The owner of the house must come and tell the priest. He must say,

"I see something like a disease in my house."

36 "Then the priest must order the house to be emptied before he comes to look at the disease. Then everything in the house will not become unclean. After that, the priest must come in to look at the house.

37 "He must look at the disease. If the disease is in the walls of the house, with green or red marks that look sunk in, 38 the priest must go out to the door of the house. He must shut up the house for seven days.

39 "On the seventh day, the priest must come back and look. If the disease has spread in the walls of the house, 40 the priest must order the stones with the disease to be taken out. They must be thrown outside the city in an unclean place. 41 He must have the inside of the house scraped all around. The dust that is scraped off must be thrown outside the city in an unclean place. 42 They must take other stones and put them in place of the old stones. They must take new clay and plaster the house.

43 "If the disease comes back and breaks out in the house after the stones are taken out and the house is scraped and plastered, 44 the priest must come and look. If the disease has spread, it is a bad mold in the house. It is unclean. 45 He must tear down the house. He must carry its stones, its wood, and all the broken pieces of the house outside the city to an unclean place.

46 "Anyone who goes into the house while it is closed up will be unclean until evening. 47 Anyone who sleeps in the house or eats in the house must wash his clothes.

48 "If the priest comes back and sees that the disease has not spread after the house was plastered, he must say the house is clean. The disease is healed.

49 "To make the house clean, he must take two birds, cedar wood, red yarn, and hyssop. 50 He must kill one bird over fresh water in a clay pot.

51 "He must take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the red yarn, and the live bird. He must dip them in the blood of the bird that was killed and in the water. He must sprinkle the house seven times. 52 He must make the house clean with the blood of the bird, the water, the live bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop, and the red yarn. 53 He must let the live bird go in the open field outside the city. He must make atonement for the house. Then it is clean.

54 "These are the rules for every kind of skin disease, for a scab, 55 for mold in clothing or in a house, 56 and for a swelling, a rash, or a bright spot. 57 They teach when something is unclean and when it is clean. This is the law for a skin disease."
