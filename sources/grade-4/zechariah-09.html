God's Message Against Nearby Lands

1 This is a message from the the LORD. His word is against the land of Hadrach. It will rest on Damascus. People everywhere and all the tribes of Israel look to the the LORD. 2 Hamath is next to it. Tyre and Sidon are very wise, but that will not help them. 3 Tyre built a strong fort for herself. She piled up silver like dust. She piled up gold like mud in the streets. 4 Look, the Lord will take her away. He will throw her riches into the sea. Fire will burn her up. 5 Ashkelon will see it and be afraid. Gaza will shake with pain. Ekron will lose hope. The king of Gaza will die. Ashkelon will be empty.

6 "People from many nations will live in Ashdod. I will end the pride of the Philistines. 7 I will take away the blood they eat and the bad things between their teeth. Then they too will belong to our God. They will be like a family group in Judah. Ekron will be like the Jebusites. 8 I will set up camp around My house to guard it from armies passing by. No one will trouble them again. I have seen this with My own eyes."

The King Comes to Zion

9 Be very happy, daughter of Zion! Shout, daughter of Jerusalem! Look, your King is coming to you. He does what is right and saves people. He is gentle. He rides a donkey, a young donkey.

10 "I will take away war carts from Ephraim and horses from Jerusalem. I will break the war bow. I will speak peace to the nations. His rule will reach from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth. 11 As for you, because of the blood of your promise, I will set your prisoners free from a pit with no water. 12 Come back to your safe place, prisoners who still have hope. Today I say I will give you twice as much. 13 I will bend Judah like a bow. I will fill it with Ephraim. I will stir up your sons, Zion, to fight your sons, Greece. I will make you like a strong man's sword."

God Fights for His People

14 The The LORD will appear above them. His arrow will shoot out like lightning. The Lord, the the LORD, will blow the trumpet. He will march in the storms from the south. 15 The The LORD of Hosts will guard them. They will destroy and conquer with sling stones. They will drink and shout like with wine. They will be as full as a bowl, like the corners of the altar. 16 On that day the the LORD their God will save them like a flock of sheep. They will be like shiny jewels in a crown over His land. 17 How good and beautiful they will be! Grain will make the young men grow strong. New wine will make the young women grow strong.
