David Praises God

1 I will thank the LORD with all my heart. I will tell of all Your wonderful works. 2 I will be glad and happy in You. I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High.

3 When my enemies turn and run, they trip and die before You. 4 For You did what was fair for me. You sat on Your throne and judge fairly.

God Stops the Wicked

5 You spoke sharply to the nations. You destroyed the wicked. You wiped out their name forever and ever. 6 The enemy is gone forever. Their cities lie in ruins. People no longer remember them.

7 But the LORD sits on His throne forever. He has set up His throne to judge. 8 He will judge the world fairly. He will rule the nations with what is right.

God Is a Safe Place

9 The LORD is a safe place for people who are hurt. He is a strong tower in times of trouble. 10 Those who know Your name trust in You. For You, the LORD, have not left the people who look for You.

11 Sing praise to the LORD, who lives in Zion. Tell the nations what He has done. 12 He remembers people who were killed. He does not forget their cries for help.

David Asks for Help

13 Be kind to me, the LORD. See how my enemies hurt me. Save me from death. 14 Then I can tell everyone in Zion how great You are. I will be glad because of Your help.

The Wicked Fall into Their Own Traps

15 The nations fell into the hole they dug. Their feet got caught in the net they hid. 16 The LORD showed that He is fair. The wicked are caught in their own traps. Think about this.

17 Wicked people will go down to the grave. So will all nations that forget God. 18 But God will not forget poor people forever. The hope of hurt people will not die.

19 Rise up, the LORD. Do not let people win. Let the nations be judged in front of You. 20 Put fear in them, the LORD. Let the nations know they are only people. Think about this.
