Jesus Shows John the Future

1 This book is about Jesus Christ. God gave it to Him to show His servants what must happen soon. He sent His angel to His servant John to make it known. 2 John told about everything he saw. He saw God's word and the truth about Jesus Christ. 3 God blesses the person who reads these words out loud. God blesses the people who hear them and obey them. The time is near.

John Writes to Seven Churches

4 I, John, write to the seven churches in Asia. May grace and peace be with you. They come from the One who is, who was, and who is coming. They also come from the seven Spirits in front of His throne. 5 They come from Jesus Christ too. He is the true witness. He is the first to rise from the dead. He rules over the kings of the earth. Jesus loves us. He set us free from our sins with His blood. 6 He made us a kingdom of priests for His God and Father. Glory and power belong to Him forever and ever. Amen.

Jesus Is Coming

7 "Look! He is coming with the clouds. Everyone will see Him. Even the people who hurt Him will see Him. All the people on earth will cry because of Him." Yes, it will happen. Amen. 8 The Lord God says, "I am the Alpha and the Omega. I am the One who is, who was, and who is coming. I am the All-Powerful One."

John's Vision

9 I, John, am your brother. I share with you in the hard times, the kingdom, and the patient waiting that we have in Jesus. I was on the island of Patmos. I was there because of God's word and the truth about Jesus. 10 On the Lord's Day, the Spirit came on me. I heard a loud voice behind me. It sounded like a trumpet. 11 The voice said, "Write in a book what you see. Send it to the seven churches. Send it to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea."

12 I turned to see who was talking to me. When I turned, I saw seven gold lampstands. 13 In the middle of them stood someone like the Son of Man. He wore a long robe down to His feet. He had a gold belt around His chest. 14 His head and hair were white like wool, as white as snow. His eyes were like fire. 15 His feet were like shiny bronze in a hot fire. His voice was like the sound of rushing water. 16 He held seven stars in His right hand. A sharp sword with two edges came out of His mouth. His face shone like the bright sun.

Jesus Talks to John

17 When I saw Him, I fell at His feet like a dead man. He put His right hand on me. He said, "Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last. 18 I am the One who lives. I was dead, but now I am alive forever and ever. I hold the keys to death and the place of the dead.

19 "Write what you have seen, what is happening now, and what will happen later. 20 Here is the secret meaning of the seven stars in My right hand and the seven gold lampstands. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches. The seven lampstands are the seven churches."
