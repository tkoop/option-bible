Korah and His Friends Turn Against Moses

1 Korah was the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi. Dathan and Abiram were sons of Eliab. On was the son of Peleth. They were from Reuben's family. They brought men with them. 2 They stood up against Moses. With them were 250 men from Israel. They were leaders the people knew well. 3 They came together against Moses and Aaron. They said, “You have gone too far! All the people are holy. the LORD is with them. Why do you put yourselves above the LORD's people?”

4 Moses heard this. He fell with his face to the ground.

God Will Show Who Is Holy

5 Moses said to Korah and all his group, “In the morning the LORD will show who belongs to Him. He will show who is holy. He will bring that person near to Him. The one He chooses, He will bring near. 6 Do this. Take pans for burning sweet smoke. Korah and all his group, take them. 7 Tomorrow put fire and sweet smoke in them. Bring them before the LORD. The man the LORD chooses will be holy. You Levites have gone too far!”

8 Moses said to Korah, “Listen, you Levites! 9 Isn't it enough for you that the God of Israel set you apart from the people of Israel? He brought you near to serve in the meeting tent. He set you to stand before the people and help them. 10 He brought you and all your fellow Levites near Him. But now you want the priest work too? 11 You and all your followers have joined together against the LORD. Who is Aaron that you complain about him?”

Dathan and Abiram Refuse to Come

12 Moses sent for Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab. But they said, “We will not come!” 13 They said, “Isn't it enough that you brought us up from a rich land to kill us in the desert? And now you want to boss us around! 14 You have not brought us into a rich land. You have not given us fields and grape farms. Do you want to treat us like we cannot see? We will not come!”

Moses Prays to the LORD

15 Moses was very angry. He said to the LORD, “Do not accept their offering. I have not taken even one donkey from them. I have not hurt any of them.”

The Men Bring Their Pans

16 Moses said to Korah, “You and all your followers must come before the LORD tomorrow. You and they and Aaron must come. 17 Each man must take his pan and put sweet smoke in it. Bring all 250 pans before the LORD. You and Aaron must each bring a pan.”

18 So each man took his pan. He put fire and sweet smoke in it. The men stood with Moses and Aaron at the door of the meeting tent. 19 Korah brought all the people together against them at the door. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people.

God's Glory Appears

20 The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 21 “Separate yourselves from this group. Then I will destroy them in a moment.”

Moses and Aaron Ask God to Spare the People

22 But Moses and Aaron fell with their faces to the ground. They cried out, “O God, God who gives breath to all living things, will You be angry with the whole group when only one man sins?”

God Tells Moses to Move the People

23 The LORD said to Moses, 24 “Tell the group, ‘Move away from the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.’”

25 Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram. The leaders of Israel followed him. 26 He warned the people, “Move back from the tents of these bad men! Do not touch anything that belongs to them. If you do, you will be swept away because of all their sins.”

27 So the people moved away from the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram came out. They stood at the doors of their tents. Their wives, children, and little ones stood with them.

Moses Says How They Will Know

28 Then Moses said, “By this you will know that the LORD sent me to do all these things. I did not plan them myself. 29 If these men die a normal death, and have only what all people have, then the LORD has not sent me. 30 But if the LORD makes something new happen, and the ground opens and swallows them and all that belongs to them, and they go alive into the grave, then you will know that these men did not respect the LORD.”

The Ground Swallows the Rebels

31 As soon as he finished speaking, the ground under them split open. 32 The earth opened its mouth. It swallowed them and their houses. It swallowed all the people with Korah and all their things. 33 They went alive into the grave. Everything they owned went with them. The earth closed over them. They were gone from the group. 34 All the Israelites around them ran away. They shouted, “The ground is going to swallow us too!”

Fire Burns the 250 Men

35 Fire came out from the LORD. It burned up the 250 men who offered the sweet smoke.

The Pans Are Made into a Cover

36 The LORD said to Moses, 37 “Tell Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, to take the pans out of the fire. Scatter the coals away. The pans are holy. 38 Make these pans, which belonged to men who sinned, into thin metal sheets to cover the altar. They brought them before the LORD, so they are holy. Let them be a sign to the people of Israel.”

39 So Eleazar the priest took the bronze pans. The men who were burned had brought them. He had them hammered into a cover for the altar. 40 This was what the LORD had told Moses. It was to remind the people of Israel that no one except a child of Aaron's family should burn sweet smoke before the LORD. If he does, he will be like Korah and his followers.

The People Complain Again

41 The next day all the people of Israel complained against Moses and Aaron. They said, “You have killed the LORD's people!”

42 The people gathered against Moses and Aaron. They looked toward the meeting tent. They saw the cloud cover it. The glory of the LORD appeared. 43 Moses and Aaron went to the front of the meeting tent.

44 The LORD spoke to Moses: 45 “Get away from this group. I will destroy them in a moment.” Then Moses and Aaron fell with their faces to the ground.

Aaron Stops the Sickness

46 Then Moses said to Aaron, “Take your pan. Put fire from the altar in it. Add sweet smoke. Hurry to the group. Pay for their wrong. The LORD's anger has come out. The sickness has started.”

47 Aaron did what Moses said. He ran into the middle of the group. The sickness had already started among the people. He offered the sweet smoke and paid for their wrong. 48 He stood between the dead and the living. The sickness stopped. 49 The number of dead from the sickness was 14,700. This was besides those who died because of Korah. 50 Then Aaron went back to Moses at the door of the meeting tent. The sickness had stopped.
