Put Away Bad Things and Grow

1 So, put away all badness, tricking, pretending, being jealous, and talking bad about people. 2 Like new babies, want pure milk from God's word. Then you can grow up and be saved. 3 You have tasted that the Lord is good.

Jesus Is the Living Stone

4 Come to Him. He is the living stone. People did not want Him. But God chose Him. He is very special to God. 5 You are like living stones too. You are being built into a spiritual house. You are a holy group of priests. You offer spiritual gifts that God accepts through Jesus Christ.

6 It says in the Bible, "Look! I put a stone in Zion. It is chosen and very special. It is the most important stone. Anyone who trusts in Him will not be put to shame."

7 So to you who believe, He is very special. But to those who do not believe, "The stone the builders did not want became the most important stone."

8 And, "A stone that makes people trip, and a rock that makes them fall." They trip because they do not obey the word. That is what they were made for.

You Are God's Special People

9 But you are a chosen family. You are royal priests. You are a holy nation. You are God's own people. You tell about the good things of the One who called you out of darkness into His amazing light.

10 Once you were not a people. Now you are God's people. Once you had not received mercy. Now you have received mercy.

Live Good Lives Among Other People

11 Dear friends, I ask you, as strangers and visitors in this world, to stay away from wrong wants of the body. They fight against your soul.

12 Live good lives among other people. Then, when they say bad things about you as if you were bad people, they will see your good works. They will praise God on the day He comes to judge.

Obey Human Leaders

13 For the Lord's sake, obey every human rule. Obey the king, because he is the top ruler.

14 Obey the leaders, because they are sent by him to punish bad people and praise good people.

15 God wants you to do good. Then you will stop foolish people from saying bad things, because they do not know.

16 Live like free people. But do not use your freedom to cover up bad things. Live like servants of God.

17 Honor all people. Love the family of believers. Fear God. Honor the king.

Servants and Hard Times

18 Servants, obey your masters with all respect. Obey not only the good and kind ones, but also the mean ones.

19 It is a good thing if someone suffers pain for doing right, because he wants to please God.

20 What good is it if you are hit for doing wrong and you take it? But if you suffer for doing good and take it, that is a good thing in God's eyes.

How Christ Showed Us the Way

21 You were called to this. Christ suffered for you. He left you an example to follow in His steps.

22 He did no wrong. No lie was found in His mouth.

23 When people said bad things to Him, He did not say bad things back. When He suffered, He did not threaten. He gave Himself to the One who judges fairly.

24 He carried our sins in His body on the cross. He did this so we could stop living for sin and live for what is right. By His wounds, you are healed.

25 You were like sheep that went the wrong way. But now you have turned back to the Shepherd and Guardian of your souls.
