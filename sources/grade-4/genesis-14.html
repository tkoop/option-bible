Four Kings Fight Five Kings

1 In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim, 2 these kings made war with Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar. 3 All these kings came together in the Valley of Siddim, which is the Salt Sea. 4 For twelve years they served Chedorlaomer. In the thirteenth year they turned against him.

5 In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings with him came. They beat the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim. 6 They also beat the Horites in the hill country of Seir, all the way to El-paran near the desert. 7 Then they turned back and came to En-mishpat, which is Kadesh. They beat all the country of the Amalekites and the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.

8 Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out. They set up for battle in the Valley of Siddim. 9 They fought against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar. It was four kings against five. 10 The Valley of Siddim had many tar pits. The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah ran away and fell into them. The rest ran to the hills.

11 The winners took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food. Then they left. 12 They also took Lot, Abram's nephew, and his things. Lot was living in Sodom. Then they left.

Abram Rescues Lot

13 One man who got away came and told Abram the Hebrew. Abram was living near the big trees of Mamre the Amorite. Mamre's brothers were Eshcol and Aner. They had made a promise to help Abram. 14 Abram heard that his relative had been taken. He led out his trained men, 318 men born in his house. He chased the enemy as far as Dan. 15 At night Abram split his men into groups. He and his servants beat the enemy. They chased them to Hobah, north of Damascus. 16 Abram brought back all the goods. He brought back his relative Lot and Lot's things. He also brought back the women and the people.

Melchizedek Blesses Abram

17 After Abram came back from beating Chedorlaomer and the kings with him, the king of Sodom came out to meet him in the Valley of Shaveh, which is the King's Valley. 18 Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought out bread and wine. He was a priest of God Most High. 19 He blessed Abram and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Maker of heaven and earth. 20 And blessed be God Most High, who gave your enemies into your hand." Abram gave him one tenth of everything.

Abram Refuses the King of Sodom's Gifts

21 The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people. You can keep the goods." 22 Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I promised the LORD, God Most High, Maker of heaven and earth. 23 I will not take a thread or a sandal strap or anything that is yours. You will not say, 'I made Abram rich.' 24 I will take nothing except what the young men ate and the share for the men who went with me: Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them take their share."
