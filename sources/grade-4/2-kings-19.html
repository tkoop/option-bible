Hezekiah Asks Isaiah for Help

1 When King Hezekiah heard this, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and went into the house of the LORD. 2 He sent Eliakim, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the writer, and the leading priests. They all wore sackcloth. They went to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.

3 They said to him, "King Hezekiah says this: Today is a day of trouble, correction, and shame. Children are ready to be born, but there is no strength to bring them out. 4 Maybe the LORD your God heard all the words of the Rabshakeh. His master, the king of Assyria, sent him to make fun of the living God. Maybe the LORD your God will correct him for the words He heard. So pray for the people who are left."

5 So King Hezekiah's servants went to Isaiah.

6 Isaiah said to them, "Tell your master this: The LORD says, 'Do not be afraid of the words you heard. The servants of the king of Assyria insulted Me. 7 Look, I will put a spirit in him. He will hear a report and go back to his own land. There I will have him killed by the sword.'"

8 When the Rabshakeh heard that the king of Assyria had left Lachish, he went back and found the king fighting against Libnah.

Assyria Sends Another Threat

9 Now Sennacherib heard a report that Tirhakah, king of Cush, was coming to fight him. So he sent messengers to Hezekiah again. He said:

10 "Tell Hezekiah king of Judah this: 'Do not let your God, whom you trust, trick you. Do not believe Him when He says Jerusalem will not be given to the king of Assyria. 11 You have heard what the kings of Assyria did to all the lands. They destroyed them completely. Will you be saved? 12 Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed save them? Did they save Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar? 13 Where are the kings of Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?'"

14 Hezekiah took the letters from the messengers and read them. Then he went up to the house of the LORD and spread them out before the LORD.

Hezekiah Prays to the LORD

15 Hezekiah prayed before the LORD: "O the LORD, God of Israel, who sits above the cherubim, You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth. 16 Please listen, O the LORD, and hear. Please open Your eyes, O the LORD, and see. Listen to the words Sennacherib sent to insult the living God. 17 It is true, O the LORD. The kings of Assyria destroyed the nations and their lands.

18 "They threw their gods into the fire and destroyed them. Those gods were not really gods. They were only wood and stone, made by human hands. 19 And now, O the LORD our God, please save us from his hand. Then all the kingdoms of the earth will know that You alone, O the LORD, are God."

God Answers Through Isaiah

20 Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah: "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Because you prayed to Me about Sennacherib king of Assyria, 21 this is what the LORD has said against him: The people of Zion hate you and laugh at you. The people of Jerusalem shake their heads at you. 22 Whom have you insulted and spoken bad things about? Who have you shouted at? You proudly raised your eyes against the Holy One of Israel!

23 "Through your messengers you insulted the Lord. You said, 'With my many chariots I went up to the high mountains, to the far parts of Lebanon. I cut down its tallest cedar trees and its best cypress trees. I went to its farthest places, to its thickest forest. 24 I dug wells and drank foreign water. With the bottoms of my feet I dried up all the rivers of Egypt.'

25 "Have you not heard? Long ago I made this plan. In the old days I planned it. Now I have carried it out. I let you crush strong cities into piles of stones. 26 So their people, with no strength, are scared and ashamed. They are like plants in a field, like fresh green grass, like grass on a roof that dries up before it grows. 27 But I know when you sit and when you go out and when you come in. I know how angry you are at Me.

28 "Because you are angry at Me, and because I have heard your proud talk, I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your mouth. I will send you back the way you came.

29 "This will be a sign for you: This year you will eat what grows by itself. Next year you will eat what grows from that. But in the third year you will plant and harvest. You will plant vineyards and eat their fruit. 30 The people of Judah who are left will grow roots below and bear fruit above. 31 For out of Jerusalem will come people who are left. Out of Mount Zion will come people who escape. The strong care of the LORD will do this.

32 "So this is what the LORD says about the king of Assyria: He will not come into this city. He will not shoot an arrow here. He will not come against it with a shield. He will not build a ramp to attack it. 33 He will go back the way he came. He will not come into this city. The LORD says this. 34 I will protect this city and save it. I will do it for My own sake and for the sake of My servant David.'"

God Saves Jerusalem

35 That night the angel of the LORD went out and killed 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. In the morning, people saw that they were all dead. 36 So Sennacherib king of Assyria left. He went back to Nineveh and stayed there. 37 One day, while he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with a sword. They ran away to the land of Ararat. Then his son Esarhaddon became king after him.
