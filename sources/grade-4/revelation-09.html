The Fifth Trumpet

1 The fifth angel blew his trumpet. I saw a star that had fallen from the sky to the earth. He was given the key to the deep hole. 2 He opened the deep hole. Smoke rose up like smoke from a big oven. The sun and the air became dark because of the smoke. 3 From the smoke came locusts on the earth. They were given power like scorpions on the earth. 4 They were told not to hurt the grass, the green plants, or the trees. They could hurt only people who did not have God's mark on their foreheads. 5 They were not to kill people. They were to hurt them for five months. The hurt felt like a scorpion sting. 6 In those days people looked for death but could not find it. They wanted to die, but death ran away from them.

7 The locusts looked like horses ready for war. They had crowns like gold on their heads. Their faces looked like people's faces. 8 They had hair like a woman's hair. Their teeth were like lions' teeth. 9 They had chest plates like iron. The sound of their wings sounded like many horses and wagons running to war. 10 They had tails and stings like scorpions. Their tails had power to hurt people for five months. 11 Their king was the angel of the deep hole. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon. In Greek it is Apollyon. 12 The first terrible thing was over. Two more terrible things were coming.

The Sixth Trumpet

13 The sixth angel blew his trumpet. I heard a voice from the four horns of the gold altar in front of God. 14 The voice said to the sixth angel with the trumpet, "Let the four angels go. They are tied up at the big river Euphrates." 15 The four angels were ready for that hour, day, month, and year. They were set free to kill a third of the people. 16 I heard the number of the horse soldiers. It was two hundred million.

17 In the vision, I saw the horses and the riders. They had chest plates of fire, blue, and sulfur. The horses' heads were like lions' heads. Fire, smoke, and sulfur came out of their mouths. 18 A third of the people died from these three plagues: fire, smoke, and sulfur that came from their mouths. 19 The horses' power was in their mouths and in their tails. Their tails were like snakes with heads. They hurt people with them.

People Still Did Not Turn to God

20 The rest of the people did not die from these plagues. They still did not turn from their sins. They kept worshiping evil spirits and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood. Those idols cannot see, hear, or walk. 21 They did not turn from their murders, evil magic, sexual sins, or stealing.
