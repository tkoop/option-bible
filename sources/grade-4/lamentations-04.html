The City Is Ruined

1 How the gold has lost its shine! The best gold is changed. The holy stones are thrown in every street. 2 The sons of Zion were very valuable. Now people treat them like clay pots made by a potter. 3 Even wild animals feed their babies. But my people have become cruel, like birds in the desert that do not care for their young.

Children Are Hungry

4 Babies' tongues stick to their mouths because they are thirsty. Little children ask for bread, but no one gives them any. 5 People who once ate rich food are now poor in the streets. People raised in fine clothes now sit in piles of ashes. 6 My people have been punished more than Sodom. Sodom was destroyed in a moment, and no one helped her.

Leaders and People

7 Her leaders were brighter than snow and whiter than milk. Their bodies were redder than red jewels. They looked like blue jewels. 8 Now they look blacker than dirt. No one knows them in the streets. Their skin sticks to their bones. It is dry like a stick. 9 People killed by a sword are better off than people who die of hunger. Hungry people waste away, hurt by pain, because the fields have no food. 10 Kind women have cooked their own children. The children became food for them when my people were destroyed.

The LORD's Anger

11 The LORD used up all His anger. He poured out His fierce anger. He set fire to Zion. It burned the city to the ground. 12 The kings of the earth did not believe it. No one in the world believed it. They thought no enemy could get into Jerusalem's gates. 13 But it happened because of the sins of the prophets and the wrongs of the priests. They killed good people in the city. 14 They wandered blind in the streets. They were covered with blood. No one could touch their clothes.

15 People shouted to them, "Go away! You are unclean! Go away! Go away! Do not touch us!"

So they ran and wandered. People in other lands said, "They cannot stay here."

16 The LORD Himself scattered them. He does not look at them with favor anymore. The priests get no honor. The leaders get no kindness. 17 Our eyes grew tired from watching for help that never came. We watched for a nation that could not save us.

The Enemy Wins

18 They followed our steps, so we could not walk in our streets. Our end was near. Our days were counted, because our end had come. 19 Our enemies chased us faster than eagles in the sky. They chased us over the mountains. They waited to trap us in the desert.

20 The king the the LORD chose was caught in their traps. He was our very life. We had said, "We will live among the nations under his protection."

Hope for Zion

21 People of Edom, be happy now. You live in the land of Uz. But the cup of trouble will come to you too. You will get drunk and be shamed.

22 People of Zion, your punishment is finished. He will not make you stay away any longer. People of Edom, He will punish you for your wrongs. He will show your sins.
