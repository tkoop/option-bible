God Chooses David

1 The LORD said to Samuel, "How long will you be sad about Saul? I have said no to him being king over Israel. Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse in Bethlehem, for I have chosen one of his sons to be king."

2 Samuel said, "How can I go? Saul will hear about it and kill me."

The LORD said, "Take a young cow with you. Say, 'I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.'"

3 The LORD said, "Then invite Jesse to the sacrifice. I will show you what to do. You are to put oil on the one I show you for Me."

4 Samuel did what the LORD said and went to Bethlehem. The city leaders met him. They were scared and asked, "Do you come in peace?"

5 "I have come in peace," Samuel replied. "I have come to sacrifice to the LORD. Make yourselves clean and come with me to the sacrifice." Then he made Jesse and his sons clean and invited them to the sacrifice.

6 When they arrived, Samuel saw Eliab and said, "Surely here before the LORD is His anointed."

7 But the LORD said to Samuel, "Do not look at how he looks or how tall he is. I have said no to him. People look at what is on the outside, but the LORD looks at the heart."

8 Then Jesse showed Abinadab to Samuel. Samuel said, "The LORD has not chosen this one either."

9 Next Jesse showed Shammah to Samuel. Samuel said, "The LORD has not chosen this one either."

10 Jesse showed seven of his sons to Samuel. Samuel said, "The LORD has not chosen any of these."

11 Samuel asked Jesse, "Are these all your sons?"

Jesse replied, "There is still the youngest. He is taking care of the sheep."

Samuel said, "Send for him. We will not sit down to eat until he comes."

12 So Jesse sent for him and brought him in. He was reddish, with beautiful eyes and a handsome face. The LORD said, "Rise and put oil on him, for he is the one."

13 Samuel took the horn of oil and put oil on David in front of his brothers. The Spirit of the LORD came on David with power from that day on. Then Samuel left and went to Ramah.

David Plays for Saul

14 The Spirit of the LORD had left Saul. An evil spirit from the LORD began to trouble him.

15 Saul's servants said to him, "Surely an evil spirit from God is troubling you. 16 Let our master tell his servants here to find a man who can play the harp. He will play when the evil spirit from God comes on you, and you will feel better."

17 Saul said to his servants, "Find me someone who plays well, and bring him to me."

18 One of the servants answered, "I have seen a son of Jesse in Bethlehem who knows how to play the harp. He is brave and strong, a fighter, speaks well, and is handsome. The LORD is with him."

19 Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep."

20 Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat. He sent them to Saul with his son David.

21 David came to Saul and stood before him. Saul loved him very much. David became the one who carried Saul's armor.

22 Then Saul sent word to Jesse: "Let David stay with me, because I like him."

23 Whenever the spirit from God came on Saul, David took the harp and played. Saul felt better, and the evil spirit left him.
