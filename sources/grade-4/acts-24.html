Paul Is Accused Before Felix

1 After five days, the high priest Ananias came down with some elders and a lawyer named Tertullus. They told the governor about Paul. 2 When Paul was called, Tertullus began to accuse him. He said, "Because of you, we have much peace. Because of your care, good things have happened for this nation. 3 In every way and everywhere, most excellent Felix, we are very thankful. 4 But I do not want to take too much of your time. I ask you to listen to us for a short time, because you are kind. 5 We found this man to be a troublemaker. He stirs up fights among all the Jews all over the world. He is a leader of the group called the Nazarenes. 6 He also tried to make the temple unclean. We caught him. 8 If you ask him, you can learn about all these things we say against him."

9 The Jews agreed. They said, "Yes, that is true."

Paul Defends Himself

10 The governor nodded for Paul to speak. Paul answered, "I know you have been a judge for this nation for many years. So I speak for myself with confidence. 11 You can learn that I went to Jerusalem to worship less than twelve days ago. 12 They did not find me arguing with anyone in the temple. I did not stir up a crowd in the synagogues or in the city. 13 They cannot prove to you the things they say against me now. 14 But I tell you this: I worship the God of my fathers by following the Way, which they call a group. I believe everything in the Law and the Prophets. 15 I have hope in God, just like they do. I hope that good people and bad people will rise from the dead. 16 So I always try to keep my heart clear toward God and people. 17 After many years, I came to bring gifts to help my people and to offer sacrifices. 18 They found me in the temple. I had made myself clean. I was not with a crowd, and I was not making noise. Some Jews from Asia 19 should be here before you to accuse me if they have anything against me. 20 Or let these men say what wrong they found when I stood before the council. 21 Or let them tell about the one thing I shouted while I stood with them: 'I am judged before you today because of the hope of rising from the dead.'"

Felix Puts Off the Case

22 Felix knew a lot about the Way. He delayed their case. He said, "When Lysias the commander comes down, I will decide your case." 23 He told the captain to keep Paul safe. He said Paul could have some freedom, and no one should stop his friends from helping him.

Paul Talks with Felix and Drusilla

24 Some days later, Felix came with his wife Drusilla, who was a Jew. He sent for Paul and heard him talk about faith in Christ Jesus. 25 As Paul talked about doing right, self-control, and the judgment to come, Felix became afraid. He said, "Go away for now. When I have time, I will call for you." 26 At the same time, Felix hoped Paul would give him money. So he sent for Paul often and talked with him. 27 After two years, Porcius Festus took Felix's place. Felix wanted to do the Jews a favor, so he left Paul in chains.
