The LORD Visits Abraham

1 The LORD appeared to Abraham by the big trees at Mamre. Abraham was sitting at the door of his tent in the hot part of the day. 2 He looked up and saw three men standing near him. He ran from the tent door to meet them and bowed down to the ground.

3 Abraham said, "My lord, if you are pleased with me, please do not go past me, your servant." 4 "Please let me bring a little water. Wash your feet and rest under the tree." 5 "I will get you some bread to eat. Then you can go on your way, because you came to me, your servant."

"Do as you have said," they said.

6 Abraham hurried into the tent and said to Sarah, "Quick! Take three large bowls of fine flour, mix it, and make bread."

7 Abraham ran to the herd and took a young calf that was soft and good. He gave it to a servant, who hurried to cook it. 8 Abraham took cheese and milk and the calf he had cooked. He set them before the men. He stood by them under the tree while they ate.

9 They said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?"

"She is in the tent," he said.

10 The LORD said, "I will come back to you at this time next year. Your wife Sarah will have a son." Sarah was listening at the tent door behind him.

11 Abraham and Sarah were very old. Sarah could not have children anymore.

12 Sarah laughed to herself. She said, "I am too old to have a baby, and my husband is old too."

13 The LORD said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh? She said, 'Can I really have a baby when I am old?'"

14 The LORD said, "Is anything too hard for the LORD? I will come back at the right time next year, and Sarah will have a son."

15 Sarah was afraid, so she said, "I did not laugh."

But the LORD said, "Yes, you did laugh."

16 Then the men got up to leave and looked toward Sodom. Abraham walked with them to send them off.

The LORD Tells Abraham His Plan

17 The LORD said, "Should I keep from Abraham what I am going to do? 18 Abraham will become a great and strong nation. All nations on earth will be blessed through him. 19 I chose him so he will teach his children and family to follow the LORD and do what is right and fair. Then the LORD can do for Abraham what He promised."

20 The LORD said, "The people of Sodom and Gomorrah have done so much wrong. Their sin is very bad. 21 I will go down and see if they have really done all the bad things people say. If not, I will know."

22 The men turned and went toward Sodom. Abraham stayed standing before the LORD.

Abraham Asks the LORD to Spare Sodom

23 Abraham came closer and said, "Will You really destroy good people along with bad people? 24 What if there are fifty good people in the city? Will You still destroy the place and not spare it for those fifty good people? 25 You would never do such a thing. You would never kill good people with bad people, as if they were the same. The Judge of all the earth will do what is right."

26 The LORD said, "If I find fifty good people in Sodom, I will spare the whole place for them."

27 Abraham said, "I am only dust and ashes, but I dare to speak to the Lord."

28 Abraham said, "What if there are five less than fifty good people? Will You destroy the whole city for that?"

The LORD said, "If I find forty-five good people there, I will not destroy it."

29 Abraham said, "What if forty good people are found there?"

The LORD said, "I will not destroy it for forty."

30 Abraham said, "Please do not be angry, Lord. What if thirty good people are found there?"

The LORD said, "I will not destroy it if I find thirty."

31 Abraham said, "I dare to speak to the Lord. What if twenty good people are found there?"

The LORD said, "I will not destroy it for twenty."

32 Abraham said, "Please do not be angry, Lord. I will speak just this once. What if ten good people are found there?"

The LORD said, "I will not destroy it for ten."

33 When the LORD finished speaking with Abraham, He left. Abraham went back to his home.
