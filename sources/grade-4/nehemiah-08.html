The People Want God's Law

1 All the people came together as one in the open place in front of the Water Gate. They asked Ezra the teacher to bring out the Book of the Law of Moses. The LORD had told Moses to give this law to Israel. 2 On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the Law to the people. Men, women, and all who could listen and understand were there.

3 He read from it from early morning until noon. He stood in the open place in front of the Water Gate. Men, women, and those who could understand listened. Everyone paid attention to the Book of the Law.

4 Ezra the teacher stood on a wood platform made for this. Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah stood on his right. Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam stood on his left.

5 Ezra opened the book where everyone could see. He was above all the people. When he opened it, all the people stood up. 6 Ezra praised the LORD, the great God. All the people lifted their hands and said, "Amen, Amen!" Then they bowed down and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.

7 The Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the other Levites—helped the people understand the Law. The people stayed where they stood.

8 They read from the Book of the Law of God. They explained it and helped people understand. So the people understood what was read.

9 Nehemiah the leader, Ezra the priest and teacher, and the Levites who helped the people spoke to all the people. They said, "This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not be sad. Do not cry." All the people were crying when they heard the words of the Law.

10 Then he said to them, "Go and eat good food. Drink sweet drinks. Send food to people who have nothing ready. This day is holy to our Lord. Do not be sad. The joy of the LORD is your strength."

11 The Levites quieted all the people. They said, "Be quiet. This day is holy. Do not be sad."

12 Then all the people went to eat and drink. They sent food to others. They had a big, happy party. They did this because they understood the words that were taught to them.

The People Make Booths

13 On the second day, the leaders of the families, the priests, and the Levites came to Ezra the teacher. They wanted to learn more about the Law. 14 They found a rule in the Law. The LORD had told Moses that the people of Israel should live in booths during the seventh month feast.

15 They told everyone in all their towns and in Jerusalem, "Go out to the hills and bring back branches from olive trees, wild olive trees, myrtle trees, palm trees, and other leafy trees. Make booths with them, as the Law says."

16 So the people went out and brought branches back. They made booths on their roofs, in their yards, in the yards of God's house, and in the open places near the Water Gate and the Gate of Ephraim.

17 All the people who came back from being taken away made booths and lived in them. Since the time of Joshua son of Nun, the people of Israel had not celebrated like this. Everyone was very happy.

18 Day by day, from the first day to the last, Ezra read from the Book of the Law of God. They celebrated the feast for seven days. On the eighth day, they had a special meeting, as the rules said.
