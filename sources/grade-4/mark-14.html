Leaders Plan to Kill Jesus

1 It was two days before Passover and the Feast of Bread Without Yeast. The chief priests and teachers of the law wanted to trick Jesus and kill Him. 2 They said, "We must not do it during the feast. The people might start a riot."

A Woman Pours Perfume on Jesus

3 Jesus was in Bethany at the house of Simon, who had leprosy. A woman came with a stone jar of very costly perfume made from pure nard. She broke the jar and poured the perfume on Jesus' head.

4 Some people were angry. They said to each other, "Why waste this perfume? 5 It could have been sold for more than three hundred silver coins and given to poor people." They scolded her.

6 But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why are you bothering her? She has done a good thing for Me. 7 You will always have poor people with you. You can help them anytime you want. But you will not always have Me. 8 She did what she could. She poured perfume on My body to get Me ready for burial. 9 I tell you the truth: Wherever the good news is told in all the world, people will also tell what she did. They will remember her."

Judas Agrees to Betray Jesus

10 Judas Iscariot was one of the Twelve. He went to the chief priests to hand Jesus over to them. 11 They were glad to hear it. They promised to give him money. Judas looked for a good time to turn Jesus in.

The Last Supper

12 On the first day of the Feast of Bread Without Yeast, when people killed the Passover lamb, Jesus' followers asked Him, "Where do you want us to go and get ready for you to eat the Passover?"

13 He sent two of His followers. He said, "Go into the city. You will meet a man carrying a jar of water. Follow him. 14 Tell the owner of the house he goes into, 'The Teacher asks where His guest room is. He wants to eat the Passover there with His followers.' 15 He will show you a big room upstairs. It will be ready and furnished. Get things ready for us there."

16 The followers went into the city. They found everything just as He said. They got the Passover ready. 17 That evening, Jesus came with the Twelve.

18 While they were sitting at the table eating, Jesus said, "I tell you the truth: One of you who eats with Me will hand Me over."

19 They were sad. They asked Him one by one, "It is not me, is it?"

20 He said, "It is one of the Twelve. It is the one who dips bread in the bowl with Me. 21 The Son of Man will go just as the Bible says about Him. But it will be very bad for the man who hands the Son of Man over. It would be better for him if he had never been born."

22 While they ate, Jesus took bread. He thanked God and broke it. He gave it to them and said, "Take this. It is My body." 23 Then He took a cup. He gave thanks and gave it to them. They all drank from it. 24 He said, "This is My blood of the new agreement. It is poured out for many people. 25 I tell you the truth: I will not drink grape juice again until the day I drink it new in God's kingdom."

26 They sang a song. Then they went out to the Mount of Olives.

Jesus Says His Followers Will Leave Him

27 Jesus said to them, "All of you will turn away from Me. The Bible says, 'I will hit the shepherd, and the sheep will run away.' 28 But after I rise from death, I will go ahead of you to Galilee."

29 Peter said, "Even if everyone else turns away, I will not."

30 Jesus said to him, "I tell you the truth: Tonight, before the rooster crows two times, you will say three times that you do not know Me."

31 Peter said even more strongly, "Even if I have to die with You, I will never say I do not know You." All the others said the same thing.

Jesus Prays in the Garden

32 They came to a place called Gethsemane. Jesus said to His followers, "Sit here while I pray."

33 He took Peter, James, and John with Him. He began to feel very troubled and upset.

34 He said to them, "My heart is so sad I could die. Stay here and keep watch."

35 He went a little farther. He fell to the ground and prayed that, if possible, this hard time would pass.

36 He said, "Abba, Father, You can do anything. Take this cup away from Me. But do not do what I want. Do what You want."

37 He came back and found them sleeping. He said to Peter, "Simon, are you asleep? Could you not stay awake one hour? 38 Stay awake and pray. Then you will not fall into trouble. The spirit wants to do right, but the body is weak."

39 He went away again and prayed the same words.

40 He came back and found them sleeping again. Their eyes were heavy. They did not know what to say to Him.

41 He came the third time and said, "Are you still sleeping and resting? Enough! The time has come. Look! The Son of Man is being handed over to sinful people. 42 Get up! Let us go! The one who will hand Me over is here."

Jesus Is Arrested

43 While Jesus was still talking, Judas came. He was one of the Twelve. A crowd came with him. They had swords and clubs. The chief priests, teachers of the law, and elders had sent them.

44 Judas had given them a sign. He said, "The one I kiss is the man. Grab Him and take Him away safely." 45 Judas went straight to Jesus. He said, "Rabbi!" and kissed Him.

46 They grabbed Jesus and arrested Him.

47 One of the men standing there pulled out a sword. He hit the servant of the high priest and cut off his ear.

48 Jesus said to them, "Did you come out with swords and clubs to arrest Me like I am a robber? 49 I was with you every day in the temple teaching, and you did not arrest Me. But this happened so the Bible would come true."

50 Everyone left Him and ran away.

51 A young man was following Jesus. He was wearing only a linen cloth. The men grabbed him. 52 He ran away naked and left the cloth behind.

Jesus Is Taken to the High Priest

53 They led Jesus to the high priest. All the chief priests, elders, and teachers of the law met together.

54 Peter followed far behind. He went into the courtyard of the high priest. He sat with the guards and warmed himself by the fire.

55 The chief priests and the whole council wanted proof against Jesus so they could kill Him. But they could not find any.

56 Many people lied about Him. Their stories did not match.

57 Some stood up and lied about Him. They said, 58 "We heard Him say, 'I will tear down this temple made by people. In three days I will build another one not made by people.' " 59 But even their stories did not match.

60 Then the high priest stood up in front of everyone. He asked Jesus, "Do you have no answer? What do these men say against you?" 61 But Jesus stayed quiet. He did not answer. The high priest asked again, "Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?"

62 Jesus said, "I am. And you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power. He will come with the clouds of heaven."

63 The high priest tore his clothes. He said, "Why do we need more witnesses? 64 You have heard the bad words against God. What do you think?" They all said He was guilty and should die.

65 Some began to spit on Him. They covered His face. They hit Him with their fists. They said, "Tell us what will happen!" The guards took Him and hit Him.

Peter Denies Jesus

66 Peter was still in the courtyard below. One of the servant girls of the high priest came by.

67 She saw Peter warming himself. She looked at him and said, "You were with Jesus from Nazareth too."

68 But he said no. "I do not know Him. I do not understand what you are talking about." Then he went out to the front yard, and a rooster crowed.

69 The servant girl saw him again. She began to say to the people standing there, "This man is one of them."

70 But he said no again. After a little while, the people standing there said to Peter, "You are one of them. You are from Galilee."

71 Then he began to curse and swear. He said, "I do not know this man you are talking about."

72 Right away the rooster crowed a second time. Peter remembered what Jesus had said: "Before the rooster crows two times, you will say three times that you do not know Me." Then Peter cried.
