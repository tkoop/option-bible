Isaiah Sees the Lord

1 In the year King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord. He was sitting on a high throne. His robe filled the temple.

2 Seraphs stood above Him. Each had six wings. With two wings they covered their faces. With two they covered their feet. With two they flew.

3 They called to each other and said, "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Armies! The whole earth is full of His glory."

4 The doorways shook when they spoke. The temple filled with smoke.

5 I said, "Oh no! I am ruined! I am a man who says bad things. I live with people who say bad things. I have seen the King, the LORD of Armies!"

Isaiah Is Made Clean

6 One of the seraphs flew to me. He had a hot coal in his hand. He took it with tongs from the altar.

7 He touched my mouth with it. He said, "See! This has touched your lips. Your sin is taken away. Your wrong is forgiven."

The Lord Sends Isaiah

8 I heard the voice of the Lord. He said, "Who should I send? Who will go for Us?"

I said, "Here I am. Send me!"

9 He said, "Go and tell these people, 'You will hear, but you will not understand. You will see, but you will not know.'"

10 "Make their hearts hard. Make their ears dull. Make their eyes blind. Then they will not see with their eyes. They will not hear with their ears. They will not understand with their hearts. They will not turn back to Me. I will not heal them."

11 I said, "Lord, how long?"

He said, "Until cities are empty. No one lives in them. Houses are empty. No people are in them. The land is empty and destroyed."

12 The LORD will send people far away. The land will be very empty.

13 But one tenth will be left. Even that will be burned again. But like a tree stump, God's holy people will stay.
