God's Message to Jeremiah

1 This is the message the LORD gave to Jeremiah. 2 The LORD, the God of Israel, says, "Write in a book all the words I have spoken to you. 3 A time is coming when I will bring My people Israel and Judah back home. I will give them the land I gave their fathers. They will live there again."

A Time of Trouble

4 These are the words the LORD spoke about Israel and Judah. 5 The LORD says, "We have heard cries of fear. There is no peace. 6 Ask and see: Can a man have a baby? Why do I see every man with his hands on his stomach, like a woman in labor? Why is every face white with fear? 7 That day will be terrible. Nothing will be like it. It will be a hard time for Jacob, but he will be saved."

God Will Free His People

8 The LORD of heaven's armies says, "I will break the heavy load off your neck. I will set you free. No one from another land will make you a slave again. 9 They will serve the LORD their God and a king from David's family. I will raise up that king for them."

Do Not Be Afraid

10 The LORD says, "Do not be afraid, Jacob, My servant. Do not be scared, Israel. I will save you from far away. I will save your children from the land where they are held. Jacob will come home and rest. No one will scare him. 11 I am with you to save you. I will destroy the nations where I scattered you. But I will not destroy you. I will punish you the right amount. I will not let you go without punishment."

A Deep Hurt

12 The LORD says, "Your hurt is too deep to heal. Your wound is very bad. 13 No one will stand up for you. There is no medicine for your wound. You cannot be healed. 14 All your friends have forgotten you. They do not care about you. I punished you like a cruel enemy would, because your guilt is great. Your sins are many. 15 Why do you cry about your wound? Your pain cannot be healed. I did this to you because your guilt is great. Your sins are many."

Enemies Will Be Judged

16 The LORD says, "So all who hurt you will be hurt. All your enemies will become prisoners. Those who rob you will be robbed. I will punish those who attack you. 17 I will make you well again. I will heal your wounds. People called you someone no one wants. They said no one cares about Zion."

God Will Bring His People Home

18 The LORD says, "I will bring Jacob's homes back. I will be kind to his tents. The city will be built again on its hill. The palace will stand where it was. 19 Songs of thanks will come from them. People will celebrate. I will make them many. They will not be few. I will make them great. They will not be small. 20 Their children will be like before. Their group will be set up before Me. I will punish all who hurt them. 21 Their leader will be one of them. Their ruler will come from among them. I will bring him near to Me. He will come to Me. No one would dare come to Me on his own. 22 You will be My people, and I will be your God."

God's Storm

23 The LORD's storm goes out in anger. It is a strong storm. It will hit the heads of bad people. 24 The LORD's great anger will not stop until He does what He plans. In the last days, you will understand this.
