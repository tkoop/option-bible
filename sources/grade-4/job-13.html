Job Answers His Friends

1 “My eyes have seen all this. My ears have heard and understood it. 2 What you know, I know too. I am not less than you. 3 But I want to speak to the Almighty. I want to argue my case with God. 4 But you cover me with lies. You are all bad doctors. 5 I wish you would be quiet. That would be wise for you. 6 Listen to my side. Hear my words.

7 Will you say wrong things for God? Will you lie for Him? 8 Will you take His side? Will you fight for God? 9 Will it go well when He checks you? Can you trick Him like you trick people? 10 He will surely scold you if you secretly take His side. 11 His greatness would scare you. Fear of Him would fall on you. 12 Your sayings are like ashes. Your defenses are like clay.

Job Wants to Speak to God

13 Be quiet and let me speak. Then let happen what will happen. 14 Why do I risk my life? Why do I hold my life in my hands? 15 Even if He kills me, I will still hope in Him. But I will argue my ways to His face. 16 This will save me. A bad man cannot come to Him.

Job Says He Is Right

17 Listen closely to my words. Let your ears hear what I say. 18 Look, I have made my case ready. I know I am right. 19 Who can argue with me? If someone could, I would be quiet and die.

Job Prays to God

20 God, give me only these two things. Then I will not hide from You: 21 Take Your hand far from me. Do not let Your fear scare me. 22 Then call me, and I will answer. Or let me speak, and You answer me. 23 How many wrong things and sins do I have? Show me my wrong and my sin. 24 Why do You hide Your face? Why do You treat me like an enemy? 25 Will You scare a leaf blown by wind? Will You chase dry straw? 26 You write bitter things against me. You make me pay for the sins of my youth. 27 You put my feet in chains. You watch all my ways. You mark the bottoms of my feet. 28 So people waste away like rotten cloth, like clothes eaten by moths.”
