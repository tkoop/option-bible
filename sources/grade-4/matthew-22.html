The Wedding Feast

1 Jesus told them another story. 2 "God's kingdom in heaven is like a king who made a wedding feast for his son. 3 He sent his servants to call the people he had invited, but they did not want to come. 4 He sent other servants and said, 'Tell the invited people, "Look, I have made my dinner ready. My bulls and fat calves are killed, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast."' 5 But they did not care. One man went to his farm, and another went to his work. 6 The rest grabbed his servants, hurt them, and killed them. 7 The king was very angry. He sent his army and killed those murderers. He burned their city. 8 Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding feast is ready, but the people I invited were not good enough. 9 Go to the roads and invite everyone you find to the wedding.' 10 The servants went out into the streets. They brought in everyone they found, both bad and good. The wedding hall was full of guests. 11 The king came in to see the guests. He saw a man who was not wearing wedding clothes. 12 'Friend,' he asked, 'how did you get in here without wedding clothes?' The man said nothing. 13 Then the king said to his servants, 'Tie his hands and feet. Throw him into the dark place outside. There people will cry and grind their teeth.' 14 Many are invited, but few are chosen."

Paying Taxes to Caesar

15 The Pharisees went out. They planned to trap Jesus with His words. 16 They sent their followers to Him with the Herodians. They said, "Teacher, we know You are true. You teach God's way truly. You do not care what people think, because You do not look at how people look. 17 So tell us, what do You think? Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar or not?"

18 Jesus knew their bad plan. He said, "You pretenders! Why are you testing Me? 19 Show Me the coin for the tax." They brought Him a coin. 20 He asked, "Whose picture is on this? Whose words are on it?" 21 They said, "Caesar's." Then Jesus said, "Give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and give to God what is God's." 22 They heard this and were amazed. They left Him and went away.

A Question About Life After Death

23 That same day, the Sadducees came to Him. They say people do not rise from the dead. They asked Him a question. 24 "Teacher," they said, "Moses told us that if a man dies with no children, his brother must marry his wife and have children for him. 25 There were seven brothers among us. The first one married and died. He had no children, so he left his wife to his brother. 26 The same thing happened to the second and third brothers, all the way to the seventh. 27 Last of all, the woman died. 28 So when people rise from the dead, whose wife will she be? All seven had married her."

29 Jesus answered, "You are wrong. You do not know God's writings or God's power. 30 When people rise from the dead, they do not marry or get married. They are like angels in heaven. 31 But about the dead rising, have you not read what God said to you? 32 'I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' He is not the God of the dead, but of the living." 33 The crowds heard this. They were amazed at His teaching.

The Greatest Command

34 When the Pharisees heard that Jesus had made the Sadducees be quiet, they came together. 35 One of them, a teacher of the law, tested Him with a question. 36 "Teacher, which command in the law is the greatest?"

37 Jesus said to him, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind. 38 This is the first and greatest command. 39 The second is like it: Love your neighbor as yourself. 40 All the Law and the Prophets depend on these two commands."

41 While the Pharisees were together, Jesus asked them, "What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is He?"

42 They said, "David's."

43 Jesus said to them, "Then how does David, with the Spirit's help, call Him Lord? David says, 44 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand, until I put Your enemies under Your feet."' 45 If David calls Him Lord, how can He be David's son?"

46 No one could answer Him. From that day on, no one dared to ask Him anything else.
