The LORD Tells Moses to Go

1 Then the LORD said to Moses, "Leave this place. You and the people you brought up out of Egypt must go up to the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I said I would give it to their children. 2 I will send an angel ahead of you. I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 3 Go up to a rich land with plenty of good things to eat. But I will not go with you, because you are stubborn. If I go with you, I might destroy you on the way."

4 The people heard this bad news. They were sad. No one wore jewelry.

5 The LORD said to Moses, "Tell the Israelites, 'You are stubborn people. If I stayed with you even for a moment, I would destroy you. Now take off your jewelry. Then I will decide what to do with you.' 6 So the Israelites took off their jewelry at Mount Horeb.

Moses Sets Up the Tent of Meeting

7 Moses used to take the tent and set it up outside the camp, far from the camp. He called it the tent of meeting. Anyone who wanted to ask the LORD something would go to the tent of meeting outside the camp. 8 When Moses went out to the tent, all the people got up. Each person stood at the door of his tent and watched Moses until he went into the tent. 9 When Moses went into the tent, the pillar of cloud came down and stood at the door of the tent. The LORD would speak with Moses. 10 All the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the door of the tent. Then all the people got up and bowed down. Each person stood at the door of his tent. 11 The LORD would speak to Moses face to face, like a man talks to his friend. Then Moses would go back to the camp. But his helper, Joshua son of Nun, stayed in the tent.

Moses Asks the LORD to Stay

12 Moses said to the LORD, "Look, You told me, 'Lead these people up,' but You have not told me whom You will send with me. Yet You said, 'I know you by name, and I am pleased with you.' 13 Now if You are pleased with me, please teach me Your ways, so I may know You and please You. Remember that this nation is Your people."

14 The LORD replied, "I will go with you, and I will give you rest."

15 Then Moses said to Him, "If You do not go with us, do not lead us up from here. 16 For how will anyone know that You are pleased with me and Your people? It is only if You go with us. Then we will be different from all the other people on earth."

17 The LORD said to Moses, "I will do what you ask. You have pleased Me, and I know you by name."

Moses Asks to See God's Glory

18 Then Moses said, "Please let me see Your glory."

19 The LORD replied, "I will let all My goodness pass in front of you. I will say My name, the LORD, as I pass by. I will be kind to anyone I want. I will feel sorry for anyone I want." 20 But He added, "You cannot see My face. No one can see Me and live." 21 Then the LORD said, "Look, there is a place near Me where you may stand on a rock. 22 When My glory passes by, I will put you in a crack in the rock. I will cover you with My hand until I have passed by. 23 Then I will take away My hand. You will see My back, but My face must not be seen."
