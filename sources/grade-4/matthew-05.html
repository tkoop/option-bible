Jesus Teaches on a Mountain

1 When Jesus saw the crowds, He went up on a mountain. He sat down, and His followers came to Him. 2 He opened His mouth and taught them. He said:

The Happy People

3 "Happy are people who know they need God, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs. 4 Happy are people who cry, because they will be comforted. 5 Happy are people who are gentle, because they will receive the earth. 6 Happy are people who are hungry and thirsty for what is right, because they will be filled. 7 Happy are people who show mercy, because they will get mercy. 8 Happy are people with clean hearts, because they will see God. 9 Happy are people who make peace, because they will be called God's children. 10 Happy are people who are hurt for doing right, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.

11 "You are happy when people make fun of you, chase you, and say lies about you because of Me. 12 Be glad and jump for joy, because your reward in heaven is big. People did the same thing to the prophets before you."

Salt and Light

13 "You are the salt of the earth. But if salt loses its taste, how can it be made salty again? It is not good for anything. It is thrown out and stepped on by people. 14 You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden. 15 People do not light a lamp and put it under a bowl. They put it on a stand, and it gives light to everyone in the house. 16 In the same way, let your light shine before people, so they can see your good works and praise your Father in heaven."

Jesus and God's Law

17 "Do not think that I came to destroy the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to destroy them. I came to make them complete. 18 I tell you the truth: until heaven and earth pass away, not one small letter or part of a letter will pass from the Law until everything happens. 19 So anyone who breaks one of the least of these commands and teaches people to do that will be called least in the kingdom of heaven. But anyone who does them and teaches them will be called great in the kingdom of heaven. 20 I tell you that unless your goodness is more than the goodness of the teachers of the law and the Pharisees, you will not enter the kingdom of heaven."

Anger

21 "You have heard that it was said to the people long ago, 'Do not murder.' Anyone who murders will be guilty in court. 22 But I tell you that anyone who is angry with his brother will be guilty in court. Anyone who calls his brother 'good-for-nothing' will be guilty before the council. Anyone who calls him 'fool' will be guilty and may be thrown into the fire of hell. 23 So if you are bringing your gift to the altar and there you remember that your brother has something against you, 24 leave your gift there in front of the altar. First go and make peace with your brother. Then come back and offer your gift. 25 Make peace quickly with the one who is against you while you are on the way. If not, he may hand you over to the judge. The judge may hand you over to the guard, and you may be thrown into prison. 26 I tell you the truth: you will not get out until you have paid the last small coin."

Looking and Marriage

27 "You have heard that it was said, 'Do not commit adultery.' 28 But I tell you that anyone who looks at a woman to want her has already committed adultery with her in his heart. 29 If your right eye makes you sin, take it out and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than to have your whole body thrown into hell. 30 If your right hand makes you sin, cut it off and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than to have your whole body go into hell. 31 It was also said, 'Anyone who divorces his wife must give her a paper of divorce.' 32 But I tell you that anyone who divorces his wife, except for sexual sin, causes her to commit adultery. And anyone who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."

Oaths and Promises

33 "You have heard that it was said to the people long ago, 'Do not break your promises to the Lord, but keep your oaths to Him.' 34 But I tell you not to swear at all. Do not swear by heaven, because it is God's throne. 35 Do not swear by the earth, because it is His footstool. Do not swear by Jerusalem, because it is the city of the great King. 36 Do not swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black. 37 Let your 'Yes' be 'Yes,' and your 'No' be 'No.' Anything more than this comes from the evil one."

Do Not Get Even

38 "You have heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.' 39 But I tell you not to fight back against an evil person. If someone slaps you on the right cheek, turn the other cheek to him. 40 If someone wants to sue you and take your shirt, let him have your coat too. 41 If someone makes you go one mile, go with him two miles. 42 Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who wants to borrow from you."

Love Your Enemies

43 "You have heard that it was said, 'Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.' 44 But I tell you, love your enemies and pray for those who hurt you. 45 Then you will be children of your Father in heaven. He makes His sun rise on bad people and good people. He sends rain on those who do right and those who do wrong. 46 If you love only those who love you, what reward do you get? Even tax collectors do that. 47 If you greet only your brothers, what are you doing more than others? Even people who do not know God do that. 48 So be perfect, just as your Father in heaven is perfect."
