Give Thanks to the LORD

1 Give thanks to the the LORD, because He is good. His faithful love lasts forever. 2 Give thanks to the God of gods. His faithful love lasts forever. 3 Give thanks to the Lord of lords. His faithful love lasts forever.

The LORD Made Everything

4 Give thanks to Him, the only One who does great wonders. His faithful love lasts forever. 5 He made the sky with wisdom. His faithful love lasts forever. 6 He spread the land over the water. His faithful love lasts forever. 7 He made the big lights. His faithful love lasts forever. 8 He made the sun to rule the day. His faithful love lasts forever. 9 He made the moon and stars to rule the night. His faithful love lasts forever.

The LORD Saved His People

10 He struck down Egypt's firstborn sons. His faithful love lasts forever. 11 He brought Israel out of Egypt. His faithful love lasts forever. 12 He saved them with His great power. His faithful love lasts forever. 13 He split the Red Sea in two. His faithful love lasts forever. 14 He led Israel through the sea. His faithful love lasts forever. 15 He sank Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea. His faithful love lasts forever. 16 He led His people through the desert. His faithful love lasts forever.

The LORD Gave His People a Home

17 He struck down great kings. His faithful love lasts forever. 18 He killed mighty kings. His faithful love lasts forever. 19 He struck down Sihon, king of the Amorites. His faithful love lasts forever. 20 He struck down Og, king of Bashan. His faithful love lasts forever. 21 He gave their land to His people. His faithful love lasts forever. 22 He gave it to Israel, His servant. His faithful love lasts forever.

The LORD Cares for Us

23 He remembered us when we were weak. His faithful love lasts forever. 24 He saved us from our enemies. His faithful love lasts forever. 25 He gives food to every living thing. His faithful love lasts forever. 26 Give thanks to the God of heaven. His faithful love lasts forever.
