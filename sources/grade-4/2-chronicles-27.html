Jotham Becomes King

1 Jotham was 25 years old when he became king. He was king in Jerusalem for 16 years. His mother was Jerusha. She was the daughter of Zadok. 2 He did what was right in the the LORD's eyes, just like his father Uzziah. But he did not go into the the LORD's temple. The people kept doing wrong.

Jotham Builds in Judah

3 Jotham built the Upper Gate of the the LORD's temple. He also did a lot of work on the wall at the Hill of Ophel. 4 He built towns in the hill country of Judah. He built forts and towers in the woods.

Jotham Fights the Ammonites

5 Jotham fought the king of the Ammonites and beat them. That year, the Ammonites paid him 100 talents of silver, 10,000 baskets of wheat, and 10,000 baskets of barley. They paid the same in the second and third years. 6 Jotham became strong because he walked the right way before the the LORD his God.

7 The rest of what Jotham did, and his wars, are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. 8 He was 25 years old when he became king. He was king in Jerusalem for 16 years. 9 Jotham died and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His son Ahaz became king after him.
