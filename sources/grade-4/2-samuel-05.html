David Becomes King of All Israel

1 All the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron. They said, "We are your own family. 2 In the past, when Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. The LORD said to you, 'You will take care of My people Israel. You will be their leader.'"

3 All the leaders of Israel came to King David at Hebron. They made an agreement with him before the LORD. They poured oil on David and made him king over Israel.

David's Years as King

4 David was 30 years old when he became king. He ruled for 40 years. 5 He ruled Judah from Hebron for 7 and a half years. Then he ruled all Israel and Judah from Jerusalem for 33 years.

David Takes the City of Jerusalem

6 The king and his men went to Jerusalem to fight the Jebusites who lived there. The Jebusites said to David, "You cannot come in here. Even blind and lame people can stop you." They thought David would never get in.

7 But David captured the fort of Zion. It became the City of David. 8 That day David said, "Whoever attacks the Jebusites must go up the water tunnel. He must reach the blind and lame people David hates." So people say, "Blind and lame people cannot come into the house."

9 David lived in the fort. He called it the City of David. He built all around it, from the wall inward. 10 David became stronger and stronger. The LORD God of All Armies was with him.

Hiram Helps David Build a House

11 Hiram, king of Tyre, sent men to David. They brought cedar wood, wood workers, and stone workers. They built a house for David. 12 David knew the LORD had made him king over Israel. He knew the LORD had made his kingdom strong for His people Israel.

More Children for David

13 After David came from Hebron, he took more wives and servant wives in Jerusalem. More sons and daughters were born to him. 14 Their names were Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. 15 Then came Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia. 16 Then came Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

David Beats the Philistines Twice

17 The Philistines heard David had been made king over Israel. They came to look for him. David heard about it and went down to his fort. 18 The Philistines came and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

19 David asked the LORD, "Should I go and fight the Philistines? Will You give them to me?"

The LORD said, "Go. I will give the Philistines to you."

20 David went to Baal-perazim and beat them there. He said, "The LORD has broken through my enemies in front of me like a flood of water." So he named that place Baal-perazim. 21 The Philistines left their false gods there. David and his men carried them away.

22 The Philistines came again and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

23 David asked the LORD what to do. 24 The LORD said, "Do not go straight up. Go around behind them. Come at them from in front of the trees. When you hear a sound like marching on top of the trees, hurry. Then I will go out ahead of you to strike the Philistine camp."

25 David did what the LORD told him. He struck the Philistines from Geba to Gezer.
