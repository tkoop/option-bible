Two Kinds of People

1 Happy is the man who does not go with bad people. He does not stand where sinners stand. He does not sit with people who make fun of others. 2 But he loves the the LORD's rules. He thinks about them day and night. 3 He is like a tree by a stream. It gives fruit at the right time. Its leaves do not dry up. Everything he does goes well.

4 Bad people are not like that. They are like dry bits of wheat that the wind blows away. 5 So bad people will not stand when God judges. Sinners will not stand with good people. 6 The LORD knows how good people live. But bad people will be lost forever.
