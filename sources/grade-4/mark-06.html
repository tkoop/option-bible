Jesus Goes Back to His Hometown

1 Jesus left that place and went to His hometown. His disciples went with Him. 2 On the Sabbath, He began to teach in the synagogue. Many people heard Him and were amazed. They asked, "Where did this man get these things? What wisdom was given to Him? How can He do such big miracles?"

3 "Isn't this the carpenter? Isn't He Mary's son? Aren't James, Joses, Judas, and Simon His brothers? Aren't His sisters here with us?" They were upset with Him.

4 Jesus said, "A prophet is honored everywhere except in his hometown, with his relatives, and in his own home." 5 He could not do any big miracle there. He only put His hands on a few sick people and healed them. 6 He was amazed because they did not believe. Then He went around the villages and taught.

Jesus Sends Out the Twelve

7 Jesus called the twelve disciples. He began to send them out two by two. He gave them power over evil spirits. 8 He told them to take nothing for the trip but a walking stick. They could not take bread, a bag, or money in their belts. 9 They could wear sandals, but they could not wear two shirts.

10 He said, "When you go into a house, stay there until you leave that place. 11 If a place will not welcome you or listen to you, shake the dust off your feet when you leave. That will be a warning to them." 12 So they went out and told people to turn from sin. 13 They forced out many evil spirits. They put oil on many sick people and healed them.

Herod Hears About Jesus

14 King Herod heard about Jesus. His name was known everywhere. Some people said, "John the Baptist has come back to life! That is why these powers work in Him."

15 Others said, "He is Elijah."

Others said, "He is a prophet, like one of the prophets."

16 But Herod said, "John, the man I killed, has come back to life!"

Herod Puts John in Jail

17 Herod had sent men to arrest John. They tied him up and put him in jail. Herod did this because of Herodias. She was the wife of his brother Philip, but Herod had married her. 18 John had told Herod, "It is not right for you to have your brother's wife." 19 Herodias hated John and wanted to kill him, but she could not. 20 Herod was afraid of John, because he knew John was a good and holy man. He liked to listen to John, even though John's words confused him, and he listened with joy.

21 On Herod's birthday, he gave a dinner for his leaders, army officers, and the important people of Galilee. 22 His wife's daughter came in and danced. She pleased Herod and his guests. The king said to the girl, "Ask me for anything you want, and I will give it to you." 23 He promised her, "I will give you whatever you ask, up to half of my kingdom."

24 She went out and asked her mother, "What should I ask for?"

Her mother said, "Ask for the head of John the Baptist."

25 Right away, the girl hurried to the king and asked, "I want you to give me John the Baptist's head on a plate right now."

26 The king was very sad. But because of his promise and his guests, he did not want to say no. 27 Right away, he sent a soldier to bring John's head. The soldier went and cut off John's head in the jail. 28 He brought the head on a plate and gave it to the girl. Then the girl gave it to her mother. 29 When John's disciples heard about it, they came and took his body and put it in a tomb.

Jesus Feeds Five Thousand People

30 The disciples came back to Jesus. They told Him everything they had done and taught. 31 Jesus said, "Come with me to a quiet place and rest for a while." Many people were coming and going, and they did not even have time to eat. 32 So they went away in a boat to a quiet place by themselves.

33 But many people saw them leave. They knew who they were. People ran there on foot from all the towns and got there first. 34 When Jesus got out of the boat, He saw a big crowd. He felt sorry for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd. So He began to teach them many things.

35 It was getting late. The disciples came to Jesus and said, "This is a lonely place, and it is already late. 36 Send the people away so they can go to the nearby farms and villages and buy food for themselves."

37 Jesus answered, "You give them something to eat."

The disciples said, "Should we go buy bread for two hundred coins and feed them?"

38 Jesus said, "How many loaves do you have? Go and see."

They found out and said, "Five loaves and two fish."

39 Jesus told them to make everyone sit down in groups on the green grass. 40 They sat down in rows of hundreds and fifties. 41 Jesus took the five loaves and two fish. He looked up to heaven and blessed them. He broke the loaves and gave them to His disciples to pass out. He also divided the two fish among everyone. 42 Everyone ate and was full. 43 The disciples picked up twelve baskets of leftover bread and fish. 44 Five thousand men ate the bread.

Jesus Walks on Water

45 Right away, Jesus made His disciples get into the boat. He told them to go ahead to the other side, to Bethsaida. He would send the crowd away. 46 After He said goodbye, He went up on a mountain to pray.

47 That evening, the boat was in the middle of the sea. Jesus was alone on land. 48 He saw His disciples working hard to row. The wind was against them. Late at night, Jesus came to them. He was walking on the sea. He was going to pass by them.

49 When they saw Him walking on the sea, they cried out. They thought He was a ghost. 50 They all saw Him and were scared. Right away, Jesus spoke to them: "Be brave! It is I. Do not be afraid." 51 He got into the boat with them, and the wind stopped. They were very amazed. 52 They had not understood about the loaves. Their hearts were hard.

53 They crossed over and came to land at Gennesaret. They tied up the boat. 54 As soon as they got out, people recognized Jesus. 55 People ran through that whole area. They carried sick people on beds to wherever they heard Jesus was. 56 Wherever Jesus went, in villages, cities, or farms, people put sick people in the town squares. They begged Him to let them touch the edge of His coat. Everyone who touched it was healed.
