God Speaks Against Gog

1 You, son of man, tell what will happen to Gog. Say, “This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Look, I am against you, Gog, leader of Meshech and Tubal.

2 I will turn you around. I will lead you. I will bring you up from the far north. I will bring you against the mountains of Israel. 3 Then I will knock the bow out of your left hand. I will knock the arrows out of your right hand.

4 You will fall on the mountains of Israel. You and all your troops and the people with you will fall. I will give you to the birds and wild animals to eat. 5 You will fall in the open field. I have said it, says the Lord, the LORD.

6 I will send fire on Magog and on people who live safely on the islands. Then they will know that I am the LORD. 7 I will make My holy name known among My people Israel. I will not let My holy name be shamed again. The nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel. 8 It is coming. It will happen, says the Lord, the LORD. This is the day I spoke about.

Israel Will Burn the Weapons

9 Then people who live in the cities of Israel will go out. They will gather the weapons and burn them. They will burn shields, small shields, bows, arrows, clubs, and spears. They will burn them for seven years. 10 They will not gather wood from the fields or cut wood from the forests. They will burn the weapons. They will take back what was taken from them. They will rob the robbers, says the Lord, the LORD.

Gog Will Be Buried

11 On that day I will give Gog a grave in Israel. It will be in the valley where travelers pass, east of the sea. It will block the path. There they will bury Gog and all his army. They will call that place the Valley of Gog's Army. 12 The people of Israel will bury them. It will take seven months to make the land clean. 13 All the people of the land will help bury them. That day will bring Me honor, says the Lord, the LORD.

14 Men will be chosen to walk through the land all the time. They will bury the bodies left on the ground to make the land clean. After seven months, they will search for more. 15 When they walk through the land, if someone sees a bone, he will put a marker next to it. Then the buriers will bury it in the Valley of Gog's Army. 16 There will also be a city called Hamonah. In this way they will make the land clean.

A Big Feast for Birds and Animals

17 You, son of man, this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Tell every bird and wild animal, “Gather around for the big sacrifice I am making for you on the mountains of Israel. You will eat meat and drink blood.” 18 You will eat the meat of strong men. You will drink the blood of the leaders of the land. You will eat rams, lambs, goats, and bulls. All of them are fat animals from Bashan.

19 You will eat fat until you are full. You will drink blood until you are drunk. This is from My sacrifice that I made for you. 20 You will be full at My table with horses and chariots, with strong men and all kinds of soldiers, says the Lord, the LORD.

God Shows His Power to the Nations

21 I will show My glory among the nations. All the nations will see My punishment that I gave. They will see My hand that I put on them. 22 From that day on, the people of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God. 23 The nations will know that the people of Israel were taken away because of their sin. They turned against Me. So I hid My face from them. I gave them to their enemies, and they all died by the sword. 24 I did this because of their unclean ways and their sins. I hid My face from them.

God Will Bring Israel Home

25 So this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Now I will bring back Israel's people. I will be kind to all the people of Israel. I will care about My holy name. 26 They will forget their shame and all the wrong they did against Me. They will live safely in their own land. No one will scare them. 27 I will bring them back from the nations. I will gather them from the lands of their enemies. I will show Myself holy through them in front of many nations. 28 Then they will know that I am the LORD their God. I brought them to their own land. I did not leave any of them among the nations. 29 I will not hide My face from them anymore. I will pour out My Spirit on the people of Israel, says the Lord, the LORD.
