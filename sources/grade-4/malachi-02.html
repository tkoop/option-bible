God Warns the Priests

1 "Now this command is for you priests. 2 If you do not listen, and if you do not care to honor My name," says the LORD of Hosts, "I will send a curse on you, and I will curse your blessings. Yes, I have already cursed them, because you do not take it to heart. 3 Look, I will punish your children. I will smear dung on your faces. It is the dung from your feast offerings. Then you will be thrown away with it. 4 Then you will know that I sent you this command. I did this so My agreement with Levi will last," says the LORD of Hosts.

5 "My agreement with him was for life and peace. I gave those to him. He had to respect Me. He did respect Me and honor My name. 6 He told the truth. He did not lie. He walked with Me in peace and did what was right. He helped many people stop doing wrong. 7 A priest's lips should keep knowledge. People should learn from him. He is a messenger from the LORD of Hosts."

8 "But you turned away from the right way. You led many people to do wrong. You ruined the agreement with Levi," says the LORD of Hosts. 9 "So I made people look down on you. I made you feel small in front of everyone. You did not follow My ways. You were unfair when you taught the law."

Judah Breaks Faith

10 "Don't we all have one Father? Didn't one God make us? Why do we break promises to each other and ruin the agreement our fathers made? 11 Judah has broken promises. A terrible thing happened in Israel and Jerusalem. Judah has ruined the holy place the the LORD loves. Judah married a woman who worships a false god. 12 The The LORD will remove any man who does this from Jacob's people. Even if he brings an offering to the the LORD of Hosts, he will be cut off."

God Hates Divorce

13 "Here is another thing you do. You cover the altar of the the LORD with tears. You cry and groan. He does not look at your offerings anymore. He does not accept them from your hands. 14 You ask, 'Why?' It is because the the LORD has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth. You broke your promise to her. She is your partner and the wife of your agreement. 15 Didn't the the LORD make them one? He gave them a share of His Spirit. What does God want? He wants children who follow Him. So guard yourselves in your spirit. Do not break your promise to the wife you married when you were young."

16 "For I hate divorce," says the the LORD, the God of Israel, "and I hate it when a man hurts his wife," says the the LORD of Hosts. "So guard yourselves in your spirit. Do not break your promise."

People Question God

17 "You made the the LORD tired with your words. You ask, 'How did we make Him tired?' You say, 'Everyone who does bad things is good to the the LORD. He likes them.' Or you say, 'Where is the God who is fair?'"
