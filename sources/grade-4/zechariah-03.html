Joshua Gets Clean Clothes

1 The LORD showed me Joshua. He was the high priest. He stood before the angel of the LORD. Satan stood at Joshua's right side to blame him.

2 The LORD said to Satan, "The LORD says no to you, Satan! The LORD chose Jerusalem, so He says no to you! Isn't Joshua like a stick pulled from a fire?"

3 Joshua was wearing dirty clothes. He stood before the angel.

4 The angel said to the men standing there, "Take off his dirty clothes." Then he said to Joshua, "Look, I have taken away your sin. I will dress you in clean, beautiful clothes."

5 I said, "Put a clean turban on his head." So they put a clean turban on his head. They dressed him in clean clothes. The angel of the LORD stood there.

A Promise from the LORD

6 The angel of the LORD gave Joshua this message from the LORD: 7 "If you follow My ways and do what I ask, then you will be in charge of My house and My yards. I will let you walk with these who stand here."

"8 Listen, Joshua the high priest. You and your friends who sit before you are men who show what is coming. I am bringing My Servant, the Branch.

"9 Look at the stone I put before Joshua. One stone has seven eyes. I will carve writing on it, says the LORD of Hosts. I will take away the sin of this land in one day. 10 In that day, says the LORD of Hosts, each of you will invite your neighbor to sit under a vine and a fig tree."
