The Dan Tribe Looks for Land

1 In those days there was no king in Israel. The tribe of Dan was looking for land to live in. Up to that day, they had not received land among the tribes of Israel.

2 So the Danites sent five men from their families. They were strong men from Zorah and Eshtaol. They went to spy out the land and look it over. They told them, "Go and explore the land." The men came to the hill country of Ephraim, to Micah's house, and stayed there.

The Spies Meet Micah's Priest

3 While they were near Micah's house, they heard the young Levite's voice. They turned aside and asked him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing here?"

4 He said, "Micah did this and that for me. He hired me, and I became his priest."

5 They said to him, "Please ask God if our trip will succeed."

6 The priest said, "Go in peace. The LORD is with you on your way."

The Spies Find Laish

7 Then the five men went on. They came to Laish. They saw the people living there safely, like the people of Sidon. They were quiet and safe. No one took their land. They were far from Sidon, and they had no help from anyone.

8 They came back to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol. Their brothers asked, "What did you see?"

The Spy Report

9 They said, "Get up! Let us go up and attack them. We saw the land, and it is very good. Do not be slow. Go and take the land. 10 When you get there, you will find a safe people. The land is large. God has given it to you. There is nothing you lack there."

The Danites Set Out

11 So 600 men from the Dan tribe left Zorah and Eshtaol. They carried weapons. 12 They went up and camped at Kiriath Jearim in Judah. That is why people call that place Mahaneh Dan even today. It is near Kiriath Jearim.

13 From there they passed through the hill country of Ephraim and came to Micah's house.

They Take Micah's Things

14 The five men who had spied out the land of Laish said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are a priest's vest, family idols, a carved image, and a metal image? Think about what you should do."

15 They turned aside and went to the young Levite's house at Micah's place. They greeted him.

16 The 600 Danite men stood at the gate, wearing their weapons.

17 The five men went in and took the carved image, the priest's vest, the family idols, and the metal image. The priest stood at the gate with the 600 armed men.

18 When those men went into Micah's house and took the carved image, the priest's vest, the family idols, and the metal image, the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

19 They said to him, "Be quiet. Come with us. Be a father and priest to us. Is it better to be priest for one man's house, or priest for a whole tribe and family in Israel?"

20 The priest was glad. He took the priest's vest, the family idols, and the carved image, and went with the people.

21 They put the children, the animals, and their goods in front. Then they turned and left.

Micah Chases Them

22 They had gone far from Micah's house. The men who lived near Micah gathered and chased the Danites.

23 They called out to the Danites. The Danites turned around and said to Micah, "Why are you shouting?"

24 Micah said, "You took the gods I made and my priest. What do I have left? How can you ask me what is wrong?"

25 The Danites said to him, "Do not let us hear your voice. Angry men might attack you, and you and your household could die."

26 Then the Danites went on their way. Micah saw they were too strong for him, so he turned back and went home.

Dan Conquers Laish

27 The Danites took what Micah had made and the priest who had been his. They came to Laish, to a quiet and safe people. They killed them with swords and burned the city.

28 No one came to help them. The city was far from Sidon. They had no ties with anyone. It was in the valley near Beth Rehob. The Danites rebuilt the city and lived there.

29 They named the city Dan, after their father Dan, who was born to Israel. Before that, the city was called Laish.

Dan Sets Up an Idol

30 The Danites set up the carved image for themselves. Jonathan, son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, and his sons were priests for the Dan tribe until the land was taken into captivity.

31 They kept Micah's carved image the whole time God's house was at Shiloh.
