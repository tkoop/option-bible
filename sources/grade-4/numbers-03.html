Aaron's Sons

1 These are the family lines of Aaron and Moses. The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. 2 Aaron's sons were Nadab, the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 3 These sons were priests. They were chosen and set apart to serve. 4 Nadab and Abihu died in front of the LORD. They offered fire that was not allowed. This happened in the Sinai desert. They had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests while their father Aaron was alive.

The Levites Help the Priests

5 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "6 Bring the Levites to Aaron the priest. They will help him. 7 They will care for his work and the work of all the people at the meeting tent. They will work in the holy tent. 8 They will care for all the things in the meeting tent. They will care for the duties of the Israelites. They will work in the holy tent. 9 Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. The Levites are given to him from the Israelites. 10 Appoint Aaron and his sons. They must do their priest work. Anyone else who comes near must die."

The Levites Belong to the LORD

11 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "12 Look, I have taken the Levites from the Israelites. They take the place of all the firstborn sons. So the Levites belong to Me. 13 This is because all firstborn sons belong to Me. On the day I killed the firstborn in Egypt, I set apart all the firstborn in Israel for Me. This includes people and animals. They are Mine. I am the LORD."

Counting the Levites

14 The LORD spoke to Moses in the Sinai desert. He said, "15 Count the Levites by their family groups. Count every male who is one month old or older." 16 Moses counted them as the LORD had told him.

17 Levi had three sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 18 Gershon's sons were Libni and Shimei. These were the Gershonite families. 19 Kohath's sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. These were the Kohathite families. 20 Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi. These were the Levite families. 21 The Gershonites had two family groups: the Libnites and the Shimites. 22 They counted 7,500 males who were one month old or older. 23 The Gershonites camped behind the holy tent, on the west side. 24 Their leader was Eliasaph son of Lael. 25 The Gershonites took care of the holy tent. They took care of its cover and the curtain at the door of the meeting tent. 26 They also took care of the curtains around the courtyard, the curtain at the courtyard gate near the holy tent and altar, and the ropes used for all this work.

27 The Kohathites had four family groups: the Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, and Uzzielites. 28 They counted 8,600 males who were one month old or older. They took care of the holy things. 29 The Kohathites camped on the south side of the holy tent. 30 Their leader was Elizaphan son of Uzziel. 31 They took care of the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and the holy tools used with them. They also took care of the curtain and all the work for these things. 32 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest was the leader over the Levite leaders. He watched over those who took care of the holy things.

33 The Merarites had two family groups: the Mahlites and the Mushites. 34 They counted 6,200 males who were one month old or older. 35 Their leader was Zuriel son of Abihail. They camped on the north side of the holy tent. 36 They took care of the frames, bars, posts, bases, and all the tools of the holy tent, and all the work for it. 37 They also took care of the posts around the courtyard, their bases, tent pegs, and ropes. 38 Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons camped on the east side, in front of the holy tent. They took care of the holy place for the Israelites. Anyone else who came near had to die. 39 All the Levites that Moses and Aaron counted were 22,000 males who were one month old or older.

Counting the Firstborn

40 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "Count all the firstborn males among the Israelites who are one month old or older. Write down their names and count them. 41 Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn Israelites. Take the Levites' animals instead of all the firstborn animals of the Israelites. The Levites belong to Me. I am the LORD." 42 Moses counted all the firstborn Israelites, just as the LORD had told him. 43 There were 22,273 firstborn males who were one month old or older.

Buying Back the Extra Firstborn

44 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "45 Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn Israelites. Take the Levites' animals instead of the Israelites' animals. The Levites belong to Me. I am the LORD. 46 The Levites were 22,000, but there were 22,273 firstborn Israelites. So 273 more firstborn sons must be bought back. 47 Take five silver coins for each of the 273. Use the holy weight. One silver coin equals twenty small coins. 48 Give the silver to Aaron and his sons for the ones who are bought back." 49 Moses took the buy-back money for the extra firstborn sons. 50 He took 1,365 silver coins, using the holy weight. 51 Moses gave the silver to Aaron and his sons, just as the LORD had told him.
