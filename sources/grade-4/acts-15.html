A Big Question About the Law

1 Some men came from Judea. They taught the believers, "You must be circumcised the way Moses taught, or you cannot be saved." 2 This caused a big argument between Paul and Barnabas and them. So the church chose Paul, Barnabas, and some others to go to Jerusalem. They would ask the apostles and elders about this question.

Paul and Barnabas Go to Jerusalem

3 The church sent them on their way. They went through Phoenicia and Samaria. They told how people who are not Jews were turning to God. This made all the believers very happy. 4 When they reached Jerusalem, the church, the apostles, and the elders welcomed them. Paul and Barnabas told them all that God had done through them.

Some Believers Want the Law Followed

5 But some believers who were Pharisees stood up. They said, "People who are not Jews must be circumcised. They must also be told to obey Moses' law." 6 The apostles and elders met to talk about this.

Peter Speaks

7 After much talk, Peter stood up. He said, "Brothers, you know that long ago God chose me to tell the Good News to people who are not Jews. They heard it and believed. 8 God knows what is in hearts. He showed He accepted them by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as He did for us. 9 He made no difference between us and them. He made their hearts clean by faith. 10 So why are you testing God? Why put a heavy load on the disciples' necks? We and our fathers could not carry that load. 11 We believe we are saved by the grace of the Lord Jesus. They are saved the same way."

Barnabas and Paul Tell What God Did

12 Everyone became quiet. They listened as Barnabas and Paul told about the signs and wonders God had done among people who are not Jews through them.

James Gives His Answer

13 When they finished, James spoke. "Brothers, listen to me. 14 Simon has told how God first helped people who are not Jews. He chose some of them to be His people. 15 The prophets' words agree with this. It is written: 16 'After this I will come back. I will rebuild David's fallen tent. I will fix its broken parts. I will make it stand again. 17 So the rest of the people may seek the LORD. All the nations I have called by My name may seek Him, says the LORD, who does these things.' 18 He has known these things from long ago. 19 So I think we should not make it hard for people who are not Jews who turn to God. 20 But we should write to them. They should stay away from things offered to idols, from sexual sin, from meat of animals killed by strangling, and from blood. 21 For Moses has people in every city who read his law in the synagogues every Sabbath."

The Church Sends a Letter

22 Then the apostles and elders, with the whole church, thought it was good to choose men from among them. They would send them to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas, called Barsabbas, and Silas. These men were leaders among the brothers.

23 They sent this letter by them: "The apostles and elders, your brothers, to the brothers among people who are not Jews in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia: Hello. 24 We heard that some men from us, whom we did not send, upset you with their words. They troubled your minds. 25 We all agreed to choose men and send them to you with our dear friends Barnabas and Paul. 26 These men risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 27 So we send Judas and Silas. They will tell you the same things in person. 28 The Holy Spirit and we think you should not have any heavy load, except these needed things: 29 Stay away from food offered to idols, from blood, from meat of strangled animals, and from sexual sin. If you keep away from these, you will do well. Good-bye."

The Letter Makes People Glad

30 So the men were sent off. They went down to Antioch. They gathered the church and gave them the letter. 31 The people read it and were glad because of the comfort. 32 Judas and Silas, who were also prophets, spoke a lot. They encouraged and strengthened the brothers. 33 After spending some time, they were sent off in peace by the brothers to those who had sent them.

Paul and Barnabas Stay in Antioch

35 Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch. They taught and preached the word of the Lord with many others.

Paul and Barnabas Travel Again

36 After some days, Paul said to Barnabas, "Let's go back and visit the brothers in every city where we preached the word of the Lord. Let's see how they are doing." 37 Barnabas wanted to take John, called Mark, with them. 38 But Paul did not think it was good to take him, because he had left them in Pamphylia and did not go with them to the work. 39 They had a sharp disagreement. So they parted. Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus. 40 Paul chose Silas. The brothers sent him off, trusting him to the Lord's grace. 41 He went through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.
