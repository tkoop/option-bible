The Servant Speaks

1 Listen to you islands. Pay attention, you people far away. The LORD called Me before I was born. He named Me while I was still in My mother’s womb. 2 He made My mouth like a sharp sword. He hid Me in the shadow of His hand. He made Me like a polished arrow. He hid Me in His quiver.

God’s Chosen Servant

3 Then He said to Me, “You are My servant, Israel. I will show My glory through You.” 4 But I said, “I worked for nothing. I used up My strength for no good. But the LORD will make things right for Me. My God will reward Me.”

A Light for All Nations

5 Now the LORD speaks. He made Me in My mother’s womb to be His servant. He sent Me to bring Jacob back to Him. He sent Me to gather Israel to Him. I am honored by the LORD. My God makes Me strong. 6 He says, “It is not enough for You to be My servant. You will not just bring back the tribes of Jacob. You will not just bring back the people of Israel who are kept safe. I will make You a light for the nations. My saving help will reach the ends of the earth.”

The Lord Will Honor His Servant

7 This is what the LORD says. He sets His people free. He is the Holy One of Israel. He speaks to the one people hated. He speaks to the one people turned away. He speaks to the servant of rulers. Kings will see You and stand up. Leaders will bow down to You. This will happen because of the LORD. He is faithful. He is the Holy One of Israel. He chose You.

Freedom for God’s People

8 This is what the LORD says: “I will answer You when the time is right. I will help You on the day I save You. I will keep You safe. I will make You a promise for the people. You will build up the land again. You will give back the empty places. 9 You will tell the prisoners, ‘Come out.’ You will tell those in the dark, ‘Be free.’ They will eat along the roads. They will find food on every hill. 10 They will not be hungry. They will not be thirsty. The hot sun will not hurt them. The one who loves them will lead them. He will guide them to springs of water. 11 I will make all My mountains into roads. My highways will be lifted up. 12 Look! Some will come from far away. Some will come from the north. Some will come from the west. Some will come from the land of Sinim.”

Heaven and Earth Praise God

13 Sing, you heavens! Be glad, you earth! Burst into song, you mountains! The LORD comforts His people. He is kind to those who hurt.

Zion Feels Forgotten

14 But Zion said, “The LORD left me. The Lord forgot me.”

God Never Forgets

15 The LORD says, “Can a mother forget her baby? Can she stop loving the child she bore? Even if she forgets, I will not forget you. 16 Look, I wrote your name on My hands. Your walls are always before Me. 17 Your children hurry back. The people who broke you and ruined you will leave you.

“18 Look up and see. All your children gather and come to you. As surely as I live,” says the LORD, “you will wear them like pretty jewels. You will put them on like a bride. 19 You were ruined and empty. Your land was destroyed. But now your land is too small for your people. The people who ate you up will be far away. 20 The children born while you were sad will say, ‘This place is too small for us. Give us more room.’ 21 Then you will say in your heart, ‘Who had these children for me? I lost my children. I was alone. I was sent away. Who raised these children? I was left by myself. Where did these children come from?’”

Nations Will Help God’s People

22 This is what the Lord the LORD says: “I will lift My hand to the nations. I will raise My flag for the peoples. They will bring your sons in their arms. They will carry your daughters on their shoulders. 23 Kings will take care of you. Their queens will feed you like mothers. They will bow down to you with their faces to the ground. They will lick dust from your feet. Then you will know that I am the LORD. Those who wait for Me will not be ashamed.”

God Will Rescue His People

24 Can a strong man’s stolen things be taken back? Can prisoners be saved from a cruel man? 25 But this is what the LORD says: “Yes, prisoners will be taken from strong men. Stolen things will be saved from cruel men. I will fight those who fight you. I will save your children. 26 I will make your enemies eat their own flesh. They will be drunk on their own blood, like wine. Then everyone will know that I, the LORD, save you. I set you free. I am the strong God of Jacob.”
