Eliphaz Speaks to Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite said: 2“If someone tries to talk to you, will you get tired? But who can keep from speaking? 3In the past, you taught many people. You made weak hands strong. 4Your words held up people who were falling. You made weak knees strong. 5But now trouble comes to you, and you are tired. It touches you, and you are scared. 6Isn’t your fear of God your safety? Isn’t living right your hope?

7“Think now. Who has ever died while innocent? Where were good people wiped out? 8As I have seen, people who do wrong and cause trouble get back what they gave. 9God’s breath destroys them. His angry wind ends them. 10The lion may roar. The fierce lion may growl. But the young lions’ teeth are broken. 11The old lion dies with no food. The young lions scatter.

A Vision at Night

12“A secret word came to me. My ear caught a whisper of it. 13It came in scary thoughts at night, when deep sleep falls on people. 14Fear and shaking came over me. They made all my bones shake. 15A spirit passed by my face, and the hair on my body stood up. 16It stood still, but I could not see its shape. A form was before my eyes. Then I heard a quiet voice:

17“‘Can a person be right before God? Can a man be clean before his Maker? 18Look, He does not trust His servants. He finds fault with His angels. 19How much more people who live in clay houses, whose base is dust! They are crushed more easily than a moth. 20From morning to night they are beaten down. Without anyone noticing, they are gone forever. 21Their life is pulled up like a tent. They die without wisdom.’”
