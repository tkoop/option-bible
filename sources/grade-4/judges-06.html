The Israelites Do Wrong

1 The Israelites did wrong in the the LORD's eyes. So the the LORD let Midian rule them for seven years. 2 Midian was stronger than Israel. The Israelites hid in caves, dens, and safe places in the mountains to get away from Midian.

3 When the Israelites planted crops, the Midianites, the Amalekites, and people from the east came to fight them. 4 They camped against Israel. They ruined the crops. They left no food for Israel. They took sheep, oxen, and donkeys. 5 They came with many animals and tents. They were like a swarm of locusts. Their camels could not be counted. They came to ruin the land.

6 Israel became very poor because of Midian. The Israelites cried out to the the LORD.

The LORD Sends a Prophet

7 When the Israelites cried out to the the LORD because of Midian, 8 the the LORD sent a man to speak to the Israelites. He said, "This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I brought you up from Egypt. I brought you out of the land where you were slaves. 9 I saved you from the Egyptians. I saved you from all who hurt you. I drove them out before you. I gave you their land. 10 I said, I am the the LORD your God. Do not be afraid of the gods of the Amorites, whose land you live in. But you did not listen to Me."

The Angel Visits Gideon

11 The angel of the the LORD came and sat under the oak tree in Ophrah. It belonged to Joash, who was from the family of Abiezer. His son Gideon was beating out wheat in a winepress to hide it from Midian. 12 The angel of the the LORD appeared to him and said, "The LORD is with you, strong and brave man."

13 Gideon said, "Please, my lord, if the the LORD is with us, why has all this happened? Where are all the wonderful things our fathers told us about? They said the the LORD brought us up from Egypt. But now the the LORD has left us. He has let Midian rule us."

14 The LORD turned to him and said, "Go with the strength you have. Save Israel from Midian. Have I not sent you?"

15 Gideon said, "Please, Lord, how can I save Israel? My family is the weakest in Manasseh. I am the youngest in my father's house."

16 The LORD said, "I will be with you. You will beat Midian as if they were one man."

17 Gideon said, "If I have found favor with you, give me a sign that it is really you speaking to me."

18 "Please do not go away until I come back with my offering and set it before you."

The The LORD said, "I will stay until you return."

19 Gideon went in and cooked a young goat. He made bread without yeast. He put the meat in a basket. He put the broth in a pot. He brought them out to the angel under the oak tree and gave them to him.

20 The angel of God said, "Take the meat and the bread. Put them on this rock. Pour out the broth." Gideon did so.

21 The angel of the the LORD touched the meat and bread with the end of his staff. Fire came up from the rock and burned up the meat and bread. Then the angel of the the LORD went away.

22 Gideon saw that it was the angel of the the LORD. He cried out, "Oh no, Lord, the LORD! I have seen the angel of the the LORD face to face!"

23 The LORD said to him, "Peace be with you. Do not be afraid. You will not die."

24 Gideon built an altar to the the LORD there. He called it "The LORD Is Peace." It is still in Ophrah, which belongs to the family of Abiezer.

Gideon Tears Down the Altar

25 That night the the LORD said to Gideon, "Take your father's young bull. Take the second bull, the one that is seven years old. Pull down your father's altar to Baal. Cut down the Asherah pole beside it. 26 Build an altar to the the LORD your God on top of this strong place. Use the wood from the Asherah pole you cut down. Take the second bull and burn it as an offering to the the LORD."

27 Gideon took ten of his servants. He did what the the LORD said. But he was afraid of his father's family and the men of the town. So he did it at night.

28 In the morning the men of the town got up. They saw that the altar to Baal was broken down. The Asherah pole was cut down. The second bull was offered on the new altar.

29 They asked each other, "Who did this?" They looked into it and found out. They said, "Gideon son of Joash did this."

30 The men of the town said to Joash, "Bring out your son. He must die. He broke down the altar to Baal. He cut down the Asherah pole."

31 Joash said to all who stood against him, "Will you fight for Baal? If Baal is a god, let him fight for himself. If anyone fights for Baal, that person must die before morning. If Baal is a god, let him fight, because his altar was broken down."

32 So that day they called Gideon "Jerubbaal." They said, "Let Baal fight against him, because he broke down Baal's altar."

The Enemy Gathers

33 The Midianites, the Amalekites, and people from the east gathered together. They crossed over and camped in the Jezreel Valley.

34 The Spirit of the the LORD came upon Gideon. He blew a trumpet. The people of Abiezer followed him.

35 He sent messengers to all of Manasseh. He sent messengers to Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali too. They came to join him.

Gideon Asks for a Sign

36 Gideon said to God, "If You will save Israel by my hand, as You said, 37 I will put a piece of wool on the ground where grain is beaten. If there is dew only on the wool, and all the ground is dry, then I will know that You will save Israel by my hand, as You said."

38 That is what happened. The next morning Gideon got up early. He squeezed the wool. He got a bowl full of water from it.

39 Gideon said to God, "Do not be angry with me. Let me ask one more time. Let the wool be dry this time, and let all the ground have dew."

40 That night God did it. The wool was dry, but there was dew on all the ground.
