The Family of Judah

1 The sons of Judah were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. 2 Reaiah was Shobal's son. Reaiah's son was Jahath. Jahath's sons were Ahumai and Lahad. These were the Zorathite family groups. 3 Etam's sons were Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. Their sister was Hazzelelponi. 4 Penuel was Gedor's father. Ezer was Hushah's father. These were Hur's sons. Hur was Ephrathah's oldest son and Bethlehem's father. 5 Ashhur was Tekoa's father. He had two wives, Helah and Naarah. 6 Naarah's sons were Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. 7 Helah's sons were Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan. 8 Koz was Anub's father and Zobebah's father. He was also father of the family groups of Aharhel. Aharhel was Harum's son.

Jabez Prays to God

9 Jabez was more honored than his brothers. His mother named him Jabez. She said, "I gave birth to him in pain."

10 Jabez prayed to the God of Israel. He said, "If only You would bless me and make my land bigger! Let Your hand be with me. Keep me from harm, so I will not be in pain." God answered his prayer.

More of Judah's Family

11 Chelub was Shuhah's brother. Chelub was Mehir's father. Mehir was Eshton's father. 12 Eshton was father of Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah. Tehinnah was father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah. 13 Kenaz's sons were Othniel and Seraiah. Othniel's son was Hathath. 14 Meonothai was Ophrah's father. Seraiah was Joab's father. Joab was father of Ge-harashim. They were craftsmen.

15 Caleb was Jephunneh's son. Caleb's sons were Iru, Elah, and Naam. Elah's son was Kenaz. 16 Jehallelel's sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel. 17 Ezra's sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. Mered's wife Bithiah had Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah. Ishbah was father of Eshtemoa. 18 These were the sons of Pharaoh's daughter Bithiah. Mered married her. His wife from Judah had Jered, Gedor's father; Heber, Soco's father; and Jekuthiel, Zanoah's father.

19 Hodiah's wife was Naham's sister. Her sons were the father of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite. 20 Shimon's sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. Ishi's sons were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

21 Shelah was Judah's son. Shelah's sons were Er, Lecah's father; Laadah, Mareshah's father; and the family groups of linen workers at Beth-ashbea. 22 Jokim, the men of Cozeba, Joash, and Saraph ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem. These names come from old records. 23 They were potters. They lived in Netaim and Gederah. They stayed there and worked for the king.

Simeon's Family and Towns

24 Simeon's sons were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul. 25 Shaul's son was Shallum. Shallum's son was Mibsam. Mibsam's son was Mishma. 26 Mishma's sons were Hammuel, Zaccur, and Shimei. 27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters. His brothers did not have many children. So their whole family group was not as big as Judah's family group.

28 They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazar-shual. 29 They also lived in Bilhah, Ezem, and Tolad. 30 They lived in Bethuel, Hormah, and Ziklag. 31 They lived in Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their towns until David was king. 32 Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan. That is five towns. 33 They also had all the villages around them, as far as Baal. These were their homes. They kept their family records.

Simeon's Leaders

34 Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah, 35 Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah son of Seraiah son of Asiel, 36 Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah, 37 Ziza son of Shiphi son of Allon son of Jedaiah son of Shimri son of Shemaiah. 38 These men were leaders in their family groups. Their families grew very big.

Simeon's Men Take New Land

39 They went to the entrance of Gedor, on the east side of the valley. They went to find grass for their sheep. 40 They found rich, good grass. The land was wide, quiet, and peaceful. Hamites had lived there before. 41 These men were listed by name. They came in the days of Hezekiah, king of Judah. They attacked the Hamites' tents and the Meunites who were there. They destroyed them completely, and they are still gone today. Then they lived there, because there was grass for their sheep.

42 Five hundred of these Simeonites went to Mount Seir. Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel led them. These four were sons of Ishi. 43 They defeated the rest of the Amalekites who had escaped. They still live there today.
