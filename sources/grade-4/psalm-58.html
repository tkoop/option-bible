David's Prayer About Bad Leaders

1 For the music leader. To the tune "Do Not Destroy." A song of David. Do you really say what is right, you leaders? Do you judge people fairly, you people?

2 No, in your hearts you plan bad things. Your hands are full of violence on the earth.

3 Bad people turn away from God from the time they are born. They have always told lies.

4 Their poison is like a snake's poison. They are like a deaf cobra that will not listen. 5 It will not listen to snake charmers, even if the snake charmer is very good.

God Will Stop the Wicked

6 O God, break the teeth in their mouths. O the LORD, pull out the fangs of those young lions.

7 May they melt away like water that runs off. When He shoots His arrows, may they be useless.

8 They are like a snail that melts away. They are like a baby born dead that never sees the sun.

9 Before your pots feel the heat of thorn bushes, whether green or dry, He will sweep them away like a storm.

Good People Will Be Glad

10 Good people will be glad when they see God punish bad people. They will wash their feet in the blood of bad people.

11 Then people will say, "Surely good people get a reward. Surely God judges the earth."
