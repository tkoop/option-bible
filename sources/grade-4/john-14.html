Jesus Comforts His Friends

1 Jesus said, "Don't let your hearts be worried. Trust in God. Trust in Me too. 2 My Father's house has many rooms. If that were not true, I would have told you. I am going there to get a place ready for you. 3 After I go and get a place ready for you, I will come back. I will take you to be with Me. Then you can be where I am. 4 You know the way to where I am going."

5 Thomas said to Him, "Lord, we don't know where You are going. How can we know the way?"

The Way to the Father

6 Jesus said, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one goes to the Father except through Me. 7 If you knew Me, you would know My Father too. Now you know Him and have seen Him."

8 Philip said to Him, "Lord, show us the Father. That is all we need."

9 Jesus said, "Philip, I have been with you a long time. Don't you know Me yet? Anyone who has seen Me has seen the Father. How can you say, 'Show us the Father'? 10 Don't you believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me? The words I say to you are not My own. The Father who lives in Me does His work. 11 Believe Me when I say I am in the Father and the Father is in Me. Or at least believe because of the things I do.

12 "I tell you the truth: Anyone who believes in Me will do the same things I do. He will do even greater things than these, because I am going to the Father. 13 Whatever you ask in My name, I will do it. Then the Father will be honored through the Son. 14 If you ask Me for anything in My name, I will do it.

Jesus Promises a Helper

15 "If you love Me, you will obey My commands. 16 I will ask the Father. He will give you another Helper. This Helper will be with you forever. 17 He is the Spirit of truth. The world cannot accept Him. The world does not see Him or know Him. But you know Him. He lives with you and will be in you.

18 "I will not leave you alone. I will come to you. 19 In a little while, the world will not see Me anymore. But you will see Me. Because I live, you will live too. 20 On that day, you will know that I am in My Father. You are in Me, and I am in you.

21 "Whoever has My commands and obeys them is the one who loves Me. The one who loves Me will be loved by My Father. I will love him too. I will show Myself to him."

22 Judas, not Judas Iscariot, said to Him, "Lord, why will You show Yourself to us and not to the world?"

23 Jesus answered, "If anyone loves Me, he will obey My word. My Father will love him. We will come to him and make our home with him. 24 Anyone who does not love Me does not obey My words. The word you hear is not Mine. It is from the Father who sent Me.

25 "I have told you these things while I am still with you. 26 But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, will come. The Father will send Him in My name. He will teach you everything. He will remind you of everything I said to you.

Jesus Gives Peace

27 "I give you peace. It is My peace I give you. I do not give it the way the world gives. Don't let your hearts be worried or afraid. 28 You heard Me say, 'I am going away, and I will come back to you.' If you loved Me, you would be glad I am going to the Father. That is because the Father is greater than I am. 29 I have told you this now, before it happens. Then when it happens, you will believe.

30 "I will not talk with you much longer. The ruler of this world is coming. He has no power over Me. 31 But I do what the Father told Me. Then the world will know I love the Father. Get up. Let's go from here."
