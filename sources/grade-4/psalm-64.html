David Asks God for Help

1 God, please hear my voice when I pray to You. Keep me safe from my enemies. 2 Hide me from the secret plans of bad people. Hide me from the noisy crowd of people who do wrong.

3 They make their tongues sharp like swords. They aim bitter words like arrows. 4 From hiding places, they shoot at good people. They shoot fast and do not fear.

5 They help each other do bad things. They plan traps in secret. They say, “Who will see us?” 6 They look for ways to do wrong. They search hard. No one can see their hidden thoughts.

God Will Judge the Wicked

7 But God will shoot an arrow at them. They will be hurt all at once. 8 Their own words will turn against them. Everyone who sees them will run away.

9 Then all people will be afraid. They will tell what God did. They will think carefully about His work.

Good People Praise God

10 Good people will be happy in the the LORD. They will trust in Him. All honest people will praise Him.
