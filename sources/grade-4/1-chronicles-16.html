David Brings the Ark to Jerusalem

1 They brought the Ark of God and put it in the tent David had set up. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to God. 2 David finished giving the burnt and peace offerings. Then he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. 3 He gave every man and woman in Israel a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake.

4 David chose some Levites to serve before the Ark of the LORD. They were to remember, thank, and praise the LORD, the God of Israel. 5 Asaph was the leader. Zechariah was second. Then came Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel. They played harps and lyres. Asaph played cymbals. 6 The priests Benaiah and Jahaziel blew trumpets all the time before the Ark of the Covenant of God.

7 That day David first gave this song of thanks to Asaph and his brothers to sing to the LORD.

A Song of Thanks

8 “Give thanks to the LORD. Call on His name. Tell the nations what He has done. 9 Sing to Him. Make music to Him. Talk about all His wonderful works. 10 Be proud of His holy name. Let the hearts of those who seek the LORD be glad. 11 Look to the LORD and His strength. Seek His face always. 12 Remember the wonderful things He did. Remember His miracles and His fair decisions. 13 You are from Israel, His servant. You are Jacob’s children, the ones He chose.

14 “He is the LORD our God. His rules are for the whole world. 15 Remember His agreement forever. Remember the word He gave for a thousand generations. 16 It is the agreement He made with Abraham, and His promise to Isaac. 17 He made it a law for Jacob. It lasts forever for Israel. 18 He said, ‘I will give you the land of Canaan. It will be your share of land.’ 19 You were only a few people then. You lived there as strangers.

20 “They walked from nation to nation. They went from one kingdom to another. 21 He did not let anyone hurt them. He warned kings about them. 22 He said, ‘Do not touch My chosen people. Do not hurt My prophets.’ 23 Sing to the LORD, all the earth. Tell His saving power every day. 24 Tell all nations about His glory. Tell all people about His wonderful works. 25 The LORD is very great. People praise Him. He is feared more than all gods. 26 All the gods of the nations are false. But the LORD made the heavens.

27 “Honor and beauty are before Him. Strength and joy are in His place. 28 Families of nations, give the LORD glory and strength. 29 Give the LORD the glory His name deserves. Bring an offering and come to Him. Worship the LORD in holy beauty. 30 All the earth, shake before Him. The world is set and will not move. 31 Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice. Let people say among the nations, ‘The LORD rules.’

32 “Let the sea roar and everything in it. Let the fields be glad and everything in them. 33 Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD, because He is coming to judge the earth. 34 Give thanks to the LORD, because He is good. His love lasts forever. 35 Say, ‘Save us, God of our salvation. Gather us and rescue us from the nations. Then we will thank Your holy name and praise You.’”

36 Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Then all the people said, ‘Amen,’ and praised the LORD.

Daily Worship Continues

37 David left Asaph and his brothers there before the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD. They served before the Ark every day. 38 Obed-Edom and his 68 relatives served as gatekeepers. Obed-Edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah were gatekeepers too. 39 Zadok the priest and his brothers served before the tent of the LORD at the high place in Gibeon.

40 They offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering every morning and evening. They did everything written in the Law of the LORD that He gave Israel. 41 Heman and Jeduthun were with them, and the rest of those chosen and named. They thanked the LORD because His love lasts forever. 42 Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments for sacred songs. The sons of Jeduthun were gatekeepers. 43 Then all the people went home. David went home to bless his family.
