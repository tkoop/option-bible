Jesus Tells About a Bad Worker

1 "There was a rich man. He had a worker who took care of his things. Someone said the worker was wasting the rich man's stuff.

2 So the rich man called the worker in. He said, 'Tell me how you used my things. You cannot be my worker anymore.'

3 The worker said to himself, 'What will I do now? My boss is taking my job from me. I am not strong enough to dig, and I am too proud to beg. 4 I know what I will do. Then, when I lose my job, people will let me stay in their homes.'

5 So he called in each person who owed money to his boss. He asked the first one, 'How much do you owe my boss?'

6 The man said, 'A hundred jars of olive oil.'

"The worker said, 'Take your bill. Sit down fast, and write fifty.'

7 Then he asked another man, 'And you, how much do you owe?'

"The man said, 'A hundred baskets of wheat.'

"The worker said, 'Take your bill and write eighty.'

8 The boss praised the dishonest worker because he had acted smartly. People in this world are smarter with their own people than people who follow the light.

Jesus Teaches About Money

9 "I tell you, use money in this world to make friends for yourselves. Then, when the money is gone, they will welcome you into homes that last forever.

10 "A person who is faithful with a little is faithful with a lot. A person who is dishonest with a little is dishonest with a lot.

11 "So if you have not been faithful with money in this world, who will trust you with true riches?

12 "And if you have not been faithful with what belongs to someone else, who will give you what is your own?

13 "No servant can serve two bosses. He will hate one and love the other, or he will stay with one and look down on the other. You cannot serve both God and money."

The Pharisees Make Fun of Jesus

14 The Pharisees loved money. They heard all this and made fun of Jesus.

15 "You say you are good in front of people. But God knows your hearts. What people think is great is very bad to God.

16 "God's Law and the Prophets were taught until John. Since then, the good news about God's kingdom is being told. Everyone is trying hard to get into it.

17 "It is easier for the sky and the earth to pass away than for one little mark of God's Law to be lost.

18 "Anyone who sends away his wife and marries another sins. And anyone who marries a woman who was sent away sins."

The Rich Man and Lazarus

19 "There was a rich man. He wore fine purple clothes and soft linen. He lived in great comfort every day.

20 "A poor man named Lazarus lay at the rich man's gate. His body was full of sores. 21 He wanted to eat the crumbs that fell from the rich man's table. Even dogs came and licked his sores.

22 "The poor man died. Angels carried him to be with Abraham. The rich man also died and was buried.

23 "In the place of the dead, the rich man was in pain. He looked up and saw Abraham far away, with Lazarus by his side.

24 "He called out, 'Father Abraham, have mercy on me. Send Lazarus to dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue. I hurt in this fire.'

25 "Abraham said, 'Child, remember that in your life you got your good things. Lazarus got bad things. Now he is comforted here, and you are in pain. 26 And besides all this, a big gap is fixed between us and you. People cannot cross from here to you, and no one can cross from there to us.'

27 "The rich man said, 'Then I beg you, father, send him to my father's house. 28 I have five brothers. Warn them so they will not come to this place of pain.'

29 "Abraham said, 'They have Moses and the Prophets. Let them listen to them.'

30 "The rich man said, 'No, father Abraham. But if someone goes to them from the dead, they will change their hearts.'

31 "Abraham said, 'If they do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, they will not believe even if someone comes back to life.'"
