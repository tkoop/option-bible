Amos Is Sad for Israel

1 Listen to this message, people of Israel. I am sad for you. 2 "Israel has fallen like a young woman. She will not get up again. She lies on her land, and no one helps her up."

God Warns Israel

3 The Lord GOD says this: "A city that sends out 1,000 soldiers will have only 100 left. A city that sends out 100 will have only 10 left for Israel."

4 The LORD says to Israel: "Look for Me, and you will live. 5 Do not look for help at Bethel. Do not go to Gilgal. Do not travel to Beersheba. Gilgal will be carried away, and Bethel will become a place of trouble. 6 Look for the LORD, and you will live. If not, He will come like fire through Joseph's family. It will burn, and no one in Bethel can put it out.

7 "You turn fair choices into something bitter. You throw goodness down to the ground!"

8 He made the stars called Pleiades and Orion. He turns dark into morning. He makes day dark like night. He calls sea water and pours it on the land. His name is the LORD. 9 He brings ruin on strong people. He brings trouble to their strong towers. 10 They hate the person who corrects them at the city gate. They hate the person who tells the truth.

11 So, because you step on poor people and take grain from them, you built stone houses, but you will not live in them. You planted nice grape fields, but you will not drink their wine. 12 I know you do many wrong things. You have so many sins. You hurt good people. You take money to be unfair. You turn poor people away at the city gate. 13 So wise people stay quiet then, because those days are bad.

God Wants Fairness

14 Look for good, not bad, so you can live. Then the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, like you say. 15 Hate bad and love good. Make fair choices at the city gate. Maybe the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be kind to the people left from Joseph's family.

16 So the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says: "There will be crying in all the streets. People will say, 'Oh no! Oh no!' in all the open places. Farmers will be called to cry. People who know sad songs will be called to wail. 17 In all the grape fields, people will cry, because I will walk through your land," says the LORD.

The Day of the LORD

18 How sad for people who want the Day of the LORD to come! Why do you want it? That day will be dark, not light. 19 It will be like a man who runs from a lion and meets a bear. Or he goes home, leans on the wall, and a snake bites him. 20 Will not the Day of the LORD be dark, not light? It will be gloomy, with no bright light.

God Does Not Want Empty Worship

21 "I hate your celebrations! I cannot stand the smell of your special meetings. 22 Even if you bring Me burnt gifts and grain gifts, I will not accept them. I will not even look at your fat animals you give for peace. 23 Take away your loud songs from Me! I will not listen to your harp music. 24 But let fair choices roll on like a river, and right living flow like a strong stream."

25 "Did you bring Me gifts and offerings in the desert for forty years, people of Israel? 26 You carried Sikkuth, your king, and Kaiwan, your star god, the idols you made for yourselves. 27 So I will send you away to a land past Damascus," says the LORD. His name is the God of Hosts.
