God's Love Makes Us His Children

1 Look at how much love the Father has given us. He calls us God's children, and we are. That is why the world does not know us. It did not know Him. 2 Dear friends, we are God's children now. We do not yet know what we will be. But we know this: when He shows Himself, we will be like Him. We will see Him just as He is. 3 Everyone who has this hope in Him keeps himself pure, just as He is pure.

Keep Away from Sin

4 Everyone who sins breaks God's law. Sin is breaking God's law. 5 And you know that He came to take away sins. There is no sin in Him. 6 No one who stays in Him keeps sinning. No one who keeps sinning has seen Him or known Him. 7 Dear children, do not let anyone trick you. The person who does what is right is right, just as He is right. 8 The person who keeps sinning belongs to the devil. The devil has been sinning since the beginning. The Son of God came to destroy the devil's work. 9 No one born of God keeps sinning. God's seed stays in him. He cannot keep sinning because he is born of God. 10 This shows who God's children are and who the devil's children are. Anyone who does not do what is right is not from God. Neither is anyone who does not love his brother.

Love One Another

11 This is the message you heard from the start: we should love one another. 12 Do not be like Cain. He belonged to the evil one and killed his brother. Why did he kill him? Because his own deeds were evil, but his brother's deeds were good. 13 Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you. 14 We know we have moved from death to life because we love our brothers. Anyone who does not love stays in death. 15 Anyone who hates his brother is a murderer. And you know that no murderer has life that lasts forever living in him. 16 We know what love is by this: He gave His life for us. So we should give our lives for our brothers. 17 If someone has things of this world and sees his brother in need, but closes his heart to him, how can God's love stay in him? 18 Dear children, let us not just say we love with words. Let us love by what we do and by being true.

God Hears Us

19 By this we will know we belong to the truth. We will feel sure in our hearts before Him. 20 If our hearts say we are wrong, God is greater than our hearts. He knows everything. 21 Dear friends, if our hearts do not say we are wrong, we can be bold with God. 22 We get what we ask from Him because we obey His commands and do what pleases Him. 23 His command is this: believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as He told us. 24 The person who obeys His commands stays in Him, and He stays in that person. By this we know He stays in us: He gave us His Spirit.
