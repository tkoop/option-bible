Abraham Goes to Gerar

1 Abraham left that place. He went to the south country. He lived between Kadesh and Shur. He stayed in Gerar for a while. 2 Abraham said to his wife Sarah, "She is my sister." Abimelech, the king of Gerar, sent men, and they took Sarah.

God Warns Abimelech

3 That night God came to Abimelech in a dream. He said to him, "You are as good as dead. You took a married woman."

4 Abimelech had not gone near her. He said, "Lord, will You kill even people who did nothing wrong? 5 Did he not say to me, 'She is my sister'? And she herself said, 'He is my brother.' I did this with an honest heart. I did not mean to do wrong."

6 God said to him in the dream, "I know you did this with an honest heart. I also kept you from sinning against Me. That is why I did not let you touch her. 7 Now give the man back his wife. He is a prophet. He will pray for you, and you will live. If you do not give her back, know this: you and all who belong to you will surely die."

Abimelech Tells His Servants

8 Early in the morning, Abimelech got up. He called all his servants. He told them everything. They were very afraid.

Abimelech Talks with Abraham

9 Then Abimelech called Abraham. He said to him, "What have you done to us? How did I sin against you, that you brought such a big sin on me and my kingdom? You have done things to me that should not be done." 10 Abimelech said to Abraham, "What did you see? Why did you do this?"

11 Abraham said, "I thought, 'There is no fear of God in this place. They will kill me because of my wife.' 12 Besides, she really is my sister. She is my father's daughter, but not my mother's daughter. She became my wife. 13 When God made me leave my father's house, I said to her, 'Please be kind to me. Everywhere we go, say, "He is my brother."'"

Abimelech Gives Gifts

14 Then Abimelech took sheep, cows, male and female servants. He gave them to Abraham. He gave Sarah back to him. 15 Abimelech said, "Look, my land is before you. Live where you like." 16 He said to Sarah, "Look, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver. This proves to everyone with you that you did nothing wrong. You are cleared before everyone."

God Heals Abimelech's Family

17 Abraham prayed to God. God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants. Then they had children. 18 For the LORD had closed every womb in Abimelech's house because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
