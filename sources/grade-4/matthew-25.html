The Story of the Ten Young Women

1 "The kingdom of heaven is like ten young women. They took their lamps and went out to meet the groom. 2 Five of them were silly, and five were wise. 3 The silly ones took their lamps but did not take extra oil. 4 The wise ones took oil in jars with their lamps. 5 The groom was late. So all the young women got sleepy and fell asleep.

6 "At midnight someone shouted, 'Look! The groom is coming! Go out to meet him!' 7 Then all the young women woke up and fixed their lamps. 8 The silly ones said to the wise ones, 'Give us some of your oil. Our lamps are going out.' 9 But the wise ones answered, 'No. There may not be enough for us and you. Go buy some for yourselves.' 10 While they were gone to buy oil, the groom came. The ready ones went in with him to the wedding. Then the door was shut.

11 "Later the other young women came and said, 'Lord, Lord, open the door for us!' But he answered, 'I do not know you.' 12 So stay awake. You do not know the day or the hour.

The Story of the Talents

14 "It is like a man who went on a trip. He called his servants and gave them his money to use. 15 He gave five bags of money to one, two bags to another, and one bag to another. He gave each one what he could handle. Then he left.

16 "The servant who got five bags went right away and worked with the money. He made five more bags. 17 The one with two bags did the same. He made two more bags. 18 But the servant who got one bag went and dug a hole in the ground. He hid his master's money there.

19 "After a long time, the master came back. He settled accounts with his servants. 20 The servant who had five bags came and brought five more. He said, 'Sir, you gave me five bags of money. Look, I made five more.' 21 His master said, 'Well done, good and faithful servant! You were faithful with a little. I will put you in charge of much. Come and share your master's joy.'

22 "The servant with two bags also came. He said, 'Sir, you gave me two bags of money. Look, I made two more.' 23 His master said, 'Well done, good and faithful servant! You were faithful with a little. I will put you in charge of much. Come and share your master's joy.'

24 "Then the servant who had one bag came. He said, 'Sir, I knew you are a hard man. You harvest where you did not plant, and you gather where you did not scatter. 25 I was afraid. So I hid your money in the ground. Here is your money.'

26 "His master answered, 'You wicked, lazy servant! You knew that I harvest where I did not plant and gather where I did not scatter. 27 Then you should have put my money with the bankers. When I came back, I would have gotten my money with interest.' 28 'Take the money from him and give it to the servant who has ten bags. 29 For everyone who has will be given more, and he will have plenty. But the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away. 30 Throw that useless servant out into the dark. There people will cry and grind their teeth.'

The Sheep and the Goats

31 "When the Son of Man comes in His glory, and all the angels with Him, then He will sit on His glorious throne. 32 All the nations will be gathered before Him. He will separate the people, like a shepherd separates sheep from goats. 33 He will put the sheep on His right and the goats on His left.

34 "Then the King will say to those on His right, 'Come, you who are blessed by My Father. Take your place in the kingdom that was made ready for you since the world began. 35 For I was hungry, and you gave Me food. I was thirsty, and you gave Me drink. I was a stranger, and you welcomed Me. 36 I was naked, and you clothed Me. I was sick, and you visited Me. I was in prison, and you came to Me.'

37 "Then the good people will answer Him, 'Lord, when did we see You hungry and feed You? Or thirsty and give You drink? 38 When did we see You as a stranger and welcome You? Or naked and clothe You? 39 When did we see You sick or in prison and come to You?' 40 The King will answer them, 'Truly I tell you, when you did it for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did it for Me.'

41 "Then He will say to those on His left, 'Go away from Me, you who are cursed, into the fire that lasts forever. It was made ready for the devil and his angels. 42 For I was hungry, and you did not give Me food. I was thirsty, and you did not give Me drink. 43 I was a stranger, and you did not welcome Me. I was naked, and you did not clothe Me. I was sick and in prison, and you did not visit Me.' 44 Then they will answer, 'Lord, when did we see You hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and not help You?' 45 He will answer them, 'Truly I tell you, when you did not do it for one of the least of these, you did not do it for Me.' 46 And these will go away to punishment that lasts forever, but the good people will go to life that lasts forever."
