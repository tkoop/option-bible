Israel Goes to War

1 Samuel's words came to all Israel. The Israelites went out to fight the Philistines. They camped at Ebenezer. The Philistines camped at Aphek.

2 The Philistines lined up to fight Israel. Israel lost and ran away. The Philistines killed about four thousand men in the field.

Israel Brings the Ark

3 When the people came back to camp, the leaders of Israel said, "Why did the the LORD let us lose today to the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the the LORD's promise from Shiloh. It can be with us and save us from our enemies."

4 So the people sent men to Shiloh. They brought back the ark of the promise of the the LORD of heaven's armies. He sits between the angels. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of God.

5 When the ark of the the LORD's promise came into camp, all Israel shouted so loud that the ground shook.

6 The Philistines heard the shout. They asked, "What is this loud shout in the Hebrew camp?" Then they learned that the ark of the the LORD had come into camp.

7 The Philistines were afraid. They said, "God has come into camp! Oh no! This has never happened before. 8 Oh no! Who will save us from these mighty gods? These are the gods who struck Egypt with every plague in the desert. 9 Be strong and act like men, Philistines! Do not let the Hebrews rule you as you ruled them. Be men and fight!"

10 The Philistines fought. Israel lost again. Every man ran home. The loss was very great. Thirty thousand soldiers from Israel fell. 11 The ark of God was taken. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

Eli Hears the Bad News

12 A man from Benjamin ran from the battle line. He came to Shiloh that same day. His clothes were torn, and dirt was on his head.

13 When he came, Eli was sitting on a seat by the road. He was watching, because his heart was afraid for the ark of God. The man came into the city to tell the news. The whole city cried out. 14 Eli heard the cry and asked, "What is this noise?" The man hurried and told Eli. 15 Eli was ninety-eight years old. His eyes were weak, and he could not see.

16 The man said to Eli, "I am the one who came from the battle. I ran from the battle today."

Eli asked, "What happened, my son?"

17 The messenger said, "Israel ran from the Philistines. Many people died. Your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead. The ark of God has been taken."

18 When the man spoke about the ark of God, Eli fell backward off his chair by the gate. His neck broke, and he died. He was old and heavy. He had led Israel for forty years.

Ichabod Is Born

19 His daughter-in-law, Phinehas's wife, was about to have a baby. When she heard that the ark of God was taken and that her father-in-law and husband were dead, she knelt down and gave birth. Her pain came fast.

20 As she was dying, the women beside her said, "Do not be afraid. You have a son." But she did not answer. She did not pay attention.

21 She named the baby Ichabod. She said, "Glory has left Israel," because the ark of God was taken and because of her father-in-law and husband. 22 She said, "Glory has left Israel, for the ark of God has been taken."
