Grain Offerings

1 When someone brings a grain offering to the the LORD, it must be fine flour. He must pour oil on it and put frankincense on it. 2 He must bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests. One priest will take a full handful of the fine flour and oil, with all the frankincense, and burn it on the altar. This burned part is a reminder. It is a fire gift with a smell that pleases the the LORD. 3 The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy, from the fire gifts of the the LORD.

Baked and Cooked Grain Offerings

4 If you bring a grain offering baked in an oven, it must be fine flour. It can be cakes without yeast mixed with oil, or thin cakes without yeast brushed with oil. 5 If your offering is a grain offering cooked on a pan, it must be fine flour mixed with oil, without yeast. 6 Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering. 7 If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan, it must be made of fine flour with oil.

Giving the Offering to the Priest

8 Bring the grain offering made from these things to the the LORD. Present it to the priest, and he will bring it to the altar. 9 The priest will take the reminder part from the grain offering and burn it on the altar. It is a fire gift with a smell that pleases the the LORD. 10 The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy, from the fire gifts of the the LORD.

Rules for Grain Offerings

11 Every grain offering you bring to the the LORD must have no yeast. You must not burn any yeast or honey as a fire gift to the the LORD. 12 You may bring them to the the LORD as a gift of first things, but they must not go up on the altar as a smell that pleases Him. 13 Season every grain offering with salt. Do not leave the salt of God's agreement out of your grain offering. Add salt to all your offerings.

First Crops Offering

14 If you bring a grain offering of first crops to the the LORD, bring fresh grain, crushed and roasted in fire, as your first-crop grain offering. 15 Put oil on it and add frankincense. It is a grain offering. 16 The priest will burn the reminder part from its crushed grain and oil, with all its frankincense. It is a fire gift to the the LORD.
