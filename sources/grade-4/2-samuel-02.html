David Asks the LORD

1 After that, David asked the LORD, "Should I go up to one of the towns of Judah?"

The LORD said, "Go up."

David asked, "Where should I go up?"

The LORD said, "To Hebron."

2 David went up with his two wives. One was Ahinoam from Jezreel. The other was Abigail, who had been Nabal's wife from Carmel.

3 David also brought the men who were with him. Each man brought his family. They lived in the towns near Hebron.

David Is Made King Over Judah

4 The men of Judah came to David. They made him king over the people of Judah. They told David, "The men of Jabesh-gilead buried Saul."

David Thanks the Men of Jabesh-gilead

5 David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead. He said, "May you be blessed by the LORD. You were kind to Saul, your lord. You buried him.

6 "Now may the LORD be kind and faithful to you. I will do the same good thing for you, because you did this for Saul.

7 "Now let your hands be strong. Be brave men. Your lord Saul is dead. The people of Judah have made me king over them."

Ish-bosheth Is Made King Over Israel

8 But Abner, the son of Ner, was the leader of Saul's army. He took Ish-bosheth, Saul's son, and brought him to Mahanaim.

9 Abner made Ish-bosheth king over Gilead, Ashur, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all Israel.

10 Ish-bosheth was forty years old when he became king over Israel. He ruled for two years. But the people of Judah followed David.

11 David was king in Hebron over the people of Judah for seven years and six months.

The Two Armies Meet at Gibeon

12 Abner and the servants of Ish-bosheth went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.

13 Joab, the son of Zeruiah, and David's servants went out too. They met by the pool of Gibeon. One group sat on one side of the pool, and the other group sat on the other side.

14 Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men get up and fight before us."

Joab said, "Let them get up."

15 So twelve men from Benjamin and Ish-bosheth stood up, and twelve of David's men.

16 Each man grabbed the man across from him by the head and stabbed him in the side. They all fell down together. That place in Gibeon was called Helkath-hazzurim.

17 The battle was very hard that day. Abner and the men of Israel lost to David's men.

Asahel Chases Abner

18 The three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel could run as fast as a deer in a field.

19 Asahel chased Abner. He did not turn to the right or to the left.

20 Abner looked behind him and said, "Is that you, Asahel?"

Asahel said, "Yes, it is me."

21 Abner said, "Turn to your right or your left. Grab one of the young men and take his armor for yourself." But Asahel would not stop chasing him.

22 Abner said again, "Stop chasing me. Why should I hit you and knock you to the ground? How could I face your brother Joab?"

23 But Asahel would not turn. So Abner stabbed him in the stomach with the back end of his spear. The spear came out his back. Asahel fell and died there. Everyone who came to the place where Asahel fell and died stopped.

24 Joab and Abishai chased Abner. The sun was going down when they reached the hill of Ammah near Giah, on the way to the desert of Gibeon.

25 The men of Benjamin gathered behind Abner. They stood together on top of a hill.

Abner and Joab Talk

26 Abner called to Joab, "Must the sword kill forever? Don't you know this will end in sorrow? How long before you tell the people to stop chasing their brothers?"

27 Joab said, "As surely as God lives, if you had not spoken, the people would have chased their brothers until morning."

28 Joab blew the trumpet. All the people stopped. They did not chase Israel anymore, and they did not fight anymore.

29 Abner and his men walked all night through the Arabah. They crossed the Jordan River and went to Mahanaim.

The People Count Their Losses

30 Joab came back from chasing Abner. He gathered all the people. David's men were missing nineteen men and Asahel.

31 But David's men had killed 360 men from Benjamin and Abner's men.

32 They carried Asahel and buried him in his father's tomb at Bethlehem. Joab and his men walked all night and reached Hebron at sunrise.
