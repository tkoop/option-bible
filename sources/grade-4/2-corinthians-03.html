We Do Not Need Letters

1 Are we starting to talk about how good we are again? Do we need letters that say we are good, like some people do? Do we need those letters to come to you or from you? 2 You are our letter. It is written in our hearts. Everyone knows it and reads it. 3 You show that you are a letter from Christ. We helped write it. It is not written with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God. It is not written on stone tablets, but on the soft hearts of people.

4 We have this kind of trust in God through Christ. 5 We are not able to do this by ourselves. We cannot take the credit. God gives us the power to do it. 6 God made us able to be helpers of a new agreement. It is not about written rules. It is about the Spirit. The written rules bring death, but the Spirit gives life.

The Old Way and the New Way

7 The old way brought death. It was written on stone. It came with glory. The people of Israel could not look at Moses' face because it was so bright. That bright glory was fading away. 8 So the Spirit's way will have even greater glory. 9 If the old way that brought punishment had glory, then the new way that makes people right with God has much greater glory. 10 What had glory then does not seem great now. The new glory is so much greater. 11 If the old way that was fading had glory, then the new way that lasts has much greater glory.

We Have Hope and Courage

12 We have this hope. So we speak with great courage. 13 We are not like Moses. He put a veil over his face. He did this so the people of Israel would not see the glory fade.

The Veil Is Taken Away

14 But their minds were closed. Even today, the same veil is there when people read the old agreement. It has not been taken away. It is taken away only in Christ. 15 Even today, when Moses is read, a veil covers their hearts. 16 But when someone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away. 17 The Lord is the Spirit. Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom. 18 We all have unveiled faces. We see the Lord's glory. We are being changed to look like Him. We go from glory to glory. This comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.
