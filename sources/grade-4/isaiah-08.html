A Big Sign Is Written

1 The LORD said to me, "Take a big piece of paper and write on it with a common pen: 'Quick to take things, fast to steal.'" 2 I will have two trusted men watch this. They are Uriah the priest and Zechariah, the son of Jeberechiah. 3 Then I went to the woman who spoke for God. She became pregnant and had a son. The LORD said to me, "Name him 'Quick to take things, fast to steal.'" 4 Before the boy can say "Dad" or "Mom," the good things of Damascus and the things taken from Samaria will be carried away by the king of Assyria.

The Assyrian Army Comes Like a Flood

5 The LORD spoke to me again. 6 "These people do not want the calm waters of Shiloah. They are happy with Rezin and the son of Remaliah. 7 So the Lord will bring a strong flood against them. It is the king of Assyria and all his power. It will rise over every river and every bank. 8 It will move through Judah and cover it. The water will reach up to the neck. Its wings will spread over the whole land. God is with us."

The Nations Will Be Broken

9 "Be broken, you nations! Listen, you far countries! Get ready, and you will be broken. Get ready, and you will be broken. 10 Make your plans, but they will come to nothing. Speak your words, but they will not stand. God is with us."

Do Not Fear What Others Fear

11 The LORD spoke to me with a strong hand. He warned me not to walk like these people. He said, 12 "Do not say, 'It is a trap,' when these people say, 'It is a trap.' Do not be afraid of what they fear. Do not be scared of them. 13 Honor the LORD of All as holy. Let Him be the one you fear. Let Him be the one you dread. 14 He will be a safe place. But He will also be a stone that makes people trip and a rock that makes them fall. This will happen to both parts of Israel. He will be a trap and a snare to the people of Jerusalem. 15 Many will trip over Him. They will fall and be broken. They will be caught and taken away."

Save God's Teaching and Wait for Him

16 "Tie up the words of God's truth. Keep His teaching safe for my followers." 17 I will wait for the LORD. He turns away from Jacob's family. I will put my hope in Him. 18 Look! I and the children the LORD gave me are special signs in Israel. They come from the LORD of All, who lives on Mount Zion.

Look to God's Word, Not to Spirits

19 People may say to you, "Ask the people who talk to spirits. Ask the ones who whisper and mumble." But should a people ask their God? Should they ask dead people for the living? 20 Go to God's teaching and His words of truth. If they do not speak like this, they have no light. 21 They will walk through the land. They will be in pain and hungry. When they are hungry, they will be angry. They will speak bad words about their king and their God. They will look up. 22 They will look at the earth. But they will see trouble and darkness. They will be pushed into thick darkness.
