The Temple Is Measured

1 Then the man brought me to the outer room. He measured the door posts. The door was six cubits wide on each side. 2 The door was ten cubits wide. The sides of the door were five cubits wide on each side. He measured the outer room. It was forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide.

3 Then he went into the inner room. He measured the door posts. They were two cubits wide. The door was six cubits wide. The opening was seven cubits wide. 4 He measured the inner room. It was twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. It was across from the outer room. He said to me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”

5 He measured the wall of the temple. It was six cubits thick. The side rooms around the temple were four cubits wide. 6 The side rooms were in three floors. There were thirty rooms on each floor. There were ledges around the temple wall. They held up the side rooms. They were not fastened into the temple wall.

7 The side rooms got wider on each higher floor. The building around the temple got wider as it went up. A stairway went from the lowest floor to the top floor through the middle floor. 8 I saw that the temple had a raised base all around it. This base held up the side rooms. It was six cubits high.

9 The outer wall of the side rooms was five cubits thick. The open space between the side rooms of the temple and the outer rooms was twenty cubits wide all around the temple. 10 This open space was all around the temple. 11 The side rooms had a door on the north and a door on the south. The open space was five cubits wide all around.

12 The building facing the separate area on the west was seventy cubits wide. Its wall was five cubits thick all around. It was ninety cubits long. 13 He measured the temple. It was one hundred cubits long. The separate area, the building, and its walls were also one hundred cubits long. 14 The front of the temple and the separate area on the east were one hundred cubits wide.

15 He measured the building facing the separate area on the west. It was one hundred cubits long, with porches on each side. The inner room and the porches of the court were measured too. 16 The doorways, the windows, and the porches around all three were covered with wood. The floor was wood from the doorway to the windows. The windows were covered. 17 Above the door, all the way to the inner room, and on the outside and on all the walls, inside and outside, there were measurements.

Carvings in the Temple

18 It was carved with cherubim and palm trees. A palm tree was between each cherub. Each cherub had two faces. 19 One face was a man’s face. It looked toward the palm tree on one side. The other face was a lion’s face. It looked toward the palm tree on the other side. This carving went all around the temple. 20 From the floor to above the door, cherubim and palm trees were carved on the temple wall. 21 The outer room had a square doorframe. The front of the Most Holy Place looked the same.

The Altar and Doors

22 The altar was made of wood. It was three cubits high and two cubits square. It had corners, a base, and sides made of wood. He said to me, “This is the table that is before the the LORD.” 23 The outer room and the Most Holy Place had double doors. 24 Each door had two parts. There were two swinging doors for each. There were two doors for one side and two for the other.

25 Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the doors of the outer room, like those on the walls. There was a wooden cover over the front of the porch outside. 26 There were windows and palm trees on both sides of the porch. The side rooms of the temple had overhangs.
