Moses Tells Israel to Obey God's Laws

1 Listen, Israel. I am teaching you the rules and laws to do. Then you may live and take the land the the LORD God of your fathers gives you. 2 Do not add to what I tell you. Do not take away from it. Keep the commands of the the LORD your God that I give you.

What Happened at Baal Peor

3 Your eyes saw what the the LORD did at Baal Peor. The LORD your God destroyed every man among you who followed Baal Peor. 4 But you who stayed close to the the LORD your God are alive today.

God's Laws Make Israel Wise

5 See, I taught you rules and laws, just as the the LORD my God told me. Do them in the land you are going to take. 6 Keep them and do them. They will be your wisdom and understanding before the nations. When they hear all these rules, they will say, “This great nation is wise and understanding.” 7 What great nation has a god so near as the the LORD our God whenever we call to Him? 8 What great nation has rules and laws as fair as all this law I set before you today?

Remember the Day at Horeb

9 Be very careful. Guard yourself, lest you forget the things your eyes saw, and lest they leave your heart all the days of your life. Teach them to your children and your grandchildren.

10 Remember the day you stood before the the LORD your God at Horeb, when the the LORD said to me, “Gather the people to Me. I will let them hear My words. They will learn to fear Me all the days they live on the earth. They will teach their children.”

11 You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain. The mountain burned with fire up to the sky. There was darkness, cloud, and thick darkness. 12 The LORD spoke to you from the fire. You heard the sound of words, but you did not see a form. You heard only a voice.

13 He told you His covenant. He commanded you to do the ten commands. He wrote them on two stone tablets. 14 At that time the the LORD commanded me to teach you rules and laws, so you can do them in the land you are crossing to take.

Do Not Make Idols

15 Guard yourselves carefully. You saw no form on the day the the LORD spoke to you from the fire at Horeb. 16 So do not corrupt yourselves. Do not make a carved idol. Do not make a picture of a man or a woman. 17 Do not make a picture of any animal on the earth, any bird that flies in the sky, 18 any thing that creeps on the ground, or any fish in the water under the earth.

19 Do not look up to the sky and see the sun, the moon, and the stars, all the armies of the sky, and be pulled away. Do not bow down to them or serve them. The LORD your God has given them to all the peoples under the sky.

God Brought Israel Out of Egypt

20 The LORD took you and brought you out of the hard slavery in Egypt. He made you His own people, as you are today.

Moses Will Not Enter the Land

21 The LORD was angry with me because of your words. He swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land the the LORD your God gives you as your own. 22 I will not cross the Jordan. I will die in this land. But you will cross and take this good land.

Warning About Idols

23 Be careful not to forget the covenant the the LORD your God made with you. Do not make a carved idol of anything the the LORD your God has forbidden you to make. 24 The LORD your God is a fire that burns up. He is a jealous God.

Israel Will Be Scattered

25 When you have children and grandchildren, and you grow old in the land, do not become corrupt. Do not make a carved idol of anything. Do not do evil in the eyes of the the LORD your God. Do not make Him angry. 26 Today I call heaven and earth to speak against you. You will quickly be destroyed from the land you are crossing the Jordan to take. You will not live there long. You will be completely destroyed. 27 The LORD will scatter you among the nations. Only a few of you will be left where the the LORD sends you. 28 There you will serve gods made by human hands, wood and stone. They cannot see, hear, eat, or smell.

God Is Merciful

29 But from there you will seek the the LORD your God, and you will find Him. You will search with all your heart and all your soul. 30 When all these things happen to you, in the last days, you will return to the the LORD your God and obey His voice. 31 The LORD your God is kind. He will not leave you or destroy you. He will not forget the covenant with your fathers that He promised them.

No God Like the LORD

32 Ask about the days that were before you, since God made man on the earth. Ask from one end of the sky to the other. Has anything so great happened? Has anything like it been heard? 33 Has any people heard the voice of God speaking from the fire, as you heard, and lived? 34 Has any god tried to take a nation for himself from another nation, with tests, signs, wonders, war, great power, and terrible acts? That is what the the LORD your God did for you in Egypt, and you saw it. 35 You were shown this so you would know that the the LORD is God. There is no other.

36 From heaven He let you hear His voice to teach you. On earth He let you see His great fire. You heard His words from the fire. 37 Because He loved your fathers and chose their children after them, He brought you out of Egypt by His great power. 38 He drove out nations greater and stronger than you, so He could bring you in and give you their land as your own today. 39 Know today, and keep it in mind, that the the LORD is God in heaven above and on earth below. There is no other. 40 Keep His rules and commands that I give you today, so it may go well with you and your children after you. Then you will live long on the land the the LORD your God gives you for all time.

Cities of Safety

41 Then Moses set apart three cities east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise. 42 A person who killed his neighbor by accident, without hating him before, could run to one of these cities and live. 43 Bezer in the wilderness plateau was for the Reubenites. Ramoth in Gilead was for the Gadites. Golan in Bashan was for the Manassites.

Moses Gives the Law

44 This is the law Moses set before the people of Israel. 45 These are the rules, laws, and decisions Moses spoke to the people of Israel when they came out of Egypt.

46 This was east of the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon. Moses and the people of Israel defeated him when they came out of Egypt. 47 They took his land and the land of Og king of Bashan. These were the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan. 48 Their land went from Aroer by the Arnon Valley to Mount Sirion, that is, Hermon. 49 It also included all the Arabah east of the Jordan, to the Sea of the Arabah, under the slopes of Pisgah.
