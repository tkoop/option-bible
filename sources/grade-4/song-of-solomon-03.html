The Bride Looks for the One She Loves

1 On my bed at night, I looked for the one I love. I looked for him, but I did not find him. 2 I will get up now and walk around the city. I will look in the streets and squares. I will look for the one I love. I looked for him, but I did not find him.

3 The watchmen who walk around the city found me. "Have you seen the one I love?" 4 I had just passed them when I found the one I love. I held him and did not let him go. I brought him to my mother's house, to the room where I was born.

5 "Daughters of Jerusalem, I make you promise by the deer and does of the field: do not wake love until it is ready."

Solomon's Wedding Carriage

6 "Who is this coming up from the desert like smoke? She smells like myrrh and frankincense, and like all the spices a seller has."

7 Look, it is Solomon's carriage. Sixty strong men stand around it. They are the strong men of Israel. 8 They all hold swords. They know how to fight. Each man has his sword on his thigh because they are afraid at night.

9 King Solomon made a carriage for himself from the wood of Lebanon. 10 He made its posts silver. He made its base gold. He made its seat purple. Its inside was made with love by the daughters of Jerusalem.

11 "Daughters of Zion, go out and see King Solomon. He wears the crown his mother put on him on his wedding day, on the day his heart was glad."
