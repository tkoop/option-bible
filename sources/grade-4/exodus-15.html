The Song of Moses

1 Then Moses and the people of Israel sang this song to the LORD. They said, "I will sing to the LORD, because He is great and strong. He threw the horse and rider into the sea. 2 The LORD is my strength and my song. He saved me. This is my God, and I will praise Him. He is my father's God, and I will lift Him up. 3 The LORD fights in war. The LORD is His name.

"4 Pharaoh's war carts and his army, He threw into the sea. His best captains sank in the Red Sea. 5 Deep water covered them. They sank to the bottom like a stone. 6 Your right hand, the LORD, is strong and great. Your right hand, the LORD, breaks the enemy. 7 In Your great power, You knock down those who fight You. You send out Your anger, and it eats them up like dry grass. 8 With a strong breath from Your nose, the water piled up. The floods stood like a wall. The deep sea became solid in the middle of the sea.

"9 The enemy said, 'I will chase them. I will catch them. I will take their things. I will get what I want. I will pull out my sword. My hand will destroy them.' 10 You blew with Your wind. The sea covered them. They sank like lead in the deep water. 11 the LORD, which god is like You? Who is like You, great and holy? You are wonderful, and You do amazing things. 12 You reached out Your right hand. The ground swallowed them.

"13 In Your kind love, You led the people You saved. In Your strength, You guided them to Your holy home. 14 The nations heard and shook. Fear grabbed the people who live in Philistia. 15 The leaders of Edom were scared. The leaders of Moab shook with fear. All the people in Canaan melted away. 16 Great fear fell on them. By Your strong arm, they became as still as stone until Your people passed by, the LORD, until the people You bought passed by. 17 You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain You gave them. The LORD, You made a place for Your home, a holy place, the LORD, that Your hands made. 18 The LORD will rule forever and ever."

Pharaoh's Army Drowned

19 Pharaoh's horses, war carts, and men on horses went into the sea. The LORD brought the sea water back over them. But the people of Israel walked on dry ground in the middle of the sea.

Miriam Sings

20 Miriam, a woman prophet and Aaron's sister, took a drum in her hand. All the women went out after her with drums and dancing. 21 Miriam sang to them, "Sing to the LORD, because He is great and strong. He threw the horse and rider into the sea."

No Water in the Desert

22 Moses led Israel away from the Red Sea. They went into the desert of Shur. They walked three days in the desert and found no water. 23 They came to Marah, but they could not drink the water there because it was bitter. That is why the place was called Marah. 24 The people complained to Moses. They said, "What will we drink?"

God Makes Bitter Water Sweet

25 Moses cried out to the LORD. The LORD showed him a piece of wood. He threw it into the water, and the water became sweet. There the LORD made a rule and a law for them. There He tested them. 26 He said, "If you listen carefully to the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is right in His eyes, and listen to His commands, and keep all His rules, I will not put on you any of the sicknesses I put on the Egyptians. I am the LORD who heals you."

Rest at Elim

27 They came to Elim. There were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees. They camped there by the water.
