Rules for the Red Cow and Touching the Dead

1 Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron.

2 "This is the rule of the law that the LORD gives. Tell the Israelites to bring a red cow to Moses. It must have no spots or bad marks. It must never have been made to work. 3 Give the cow to Eleazar the priest. He must take it outside the camp and kill it while Eleazar watches. 4 Eleazar must take some blood on his finger. He must sprinkle it seven times toward the front of the tent where the LORD met them. 5 Then burn the cow while Eleazar watches. Burn its skin, meat, blood, and dung.

6 The priest must take cedar wood, a plant called hyssop, and red string. He must throw them into the fire where the cow is burning. 7 Then the priest must wash his clothes and wash his body in water. After that he may go back into the camp. But he will be not clean until evening. 8 The man who burns the cow must wash his clothes and wash his body in water. He will also be not clean until evening.

9 A clean man must gather the ashes of the cow. He must put them in a clean place outside the camp. The people of Israel must keep them. They use them in water that makes people clean. This water takes away sin. 10 The man who gathers the ashes must wash his clothes. He will be not clean until evening. This rule lasts forever for the Israelites and for the stranger who lives with them.

11 Anyone who touches a dead body will be not clean for seven days. 12 He must make himself clean with the water on the third day and on the seventh day. Then he will be clean. But if he does not make himself clean on those days, he will not be clean. 13 Anyone who touches a dead body and does not make himself clean makes the LORD's tent dirty. That person must be sent away from Israel. He is still not clean, because the cleaning water was not sprinkled on him. His not-clean state is still on him.

14 This is the rule when a man dies in a tent. Everyone who goes into the tent, and everyone already in the tent, will be not clean for seven days. 15 Any open jar with no lid tied on it is not clean. 16 Anyone in an open field who touches a person killed by a sword, or a dead body, or a human bone, or a grave, will be not clean for seven days.

17 For the not-clean person, they must take some ashes from the burned gift for sin. They must put them in a jar. Then they must pour fresh water over them. 18 Then a clean person must take hyssop. He must dip it in the water. He must sprinkle it on the tent, on all the things in it, and on the people who were there. He must also sprinkle anyone who touched a bone, a dead person, or a grave. 19 The clean person must sprinkle the not-clean person on the third day and on the seventh day. After the not-clean person is clean on the seventh day, he must wash his clothes and wash in water. He will be clean by evening.

20 But if a person who is not clean does not make himself clean, he must be sent away from the group. He made the LORD's holy place dirty. The cleaning water was not sprinkled on him. He is not clean. 21 This rule lasts forever. The person who sprinkles the cleaning water must wash his clothes. Anyone who touches the cleaning water will be not clean until evening. 22 Anything a not-clean person touches will be not clean. Anyone who touches it will be not clean until evening."
