The Leaders Plan to Kill Jesus

1 Early in the morning, all the top priests and the leaders of the people met. They planned how to kill Jesus. 2 They tied Jesus up and took Him to Pilate, the governor.

Judas Feels Sorry

3 Judas was the one who had handed Jesus over. When he saw that Jesus was found guilty, he felt sorry. He took the thirty silver coins back to the top priests and leaders. 4 He said, "I sinned. I handed over an innocent man."

The leaders answered, "That is not our problem. You deal with it."

5 Judas threw the money into the temple and left. Then he went and hanged himself.

6 The top priests picked up the coins. They said, "We cannot put this money into the temple treasury. It is money paid for a man's death."

7 They talked about it and used the money to buy a field from a potter. It was for burying strangers. 8 So that field has been called the Field of Blood ever since.

9 Then what the prophet Jeremiah said came true. He said, "They took the thirty silver coins, the price for the One the people of Israel set a price on. 10 They used them to buy the potter's field, just as the LORD told me."

Jesus Stands Before Pilate

11 Jesus stood in front of the governor. The governor asked Him, "Are you the King of the Jews?"

Jesus said, "You said it."

12 While the top priests and leaders accused Him, Jesus said nothing.

13 Pilate said to Him, "Don't you hear all the things they are saying against you?"

14 Jesus did not answer even one word. This surprised the governor.

The Crowd Picks Barabbas

15 At the feast, the governor was used to letting one prisoner go free. The crowd could choose. 16 At that time they had a well-known prisoner named Barabbas.

17 When the people gathered, Pilate asked them, "Which one do you want me to set free: Barabbas or Jesus, who is called the Christ?"

18 Pilate knew they had handed Jesus over because they were jealous.

19 While Pilate was sitting on the judge's seat, his wife sent him a message. She said, "Stay away from that good man. I had a bad dream today because of Him, and it troubled me a lot."

20 The top priests and leaders talked the crowd into asking for Barabbas and having Jesus killed.

21 Pilate asked, "Which of the two do you want me to set free?"

The people said, "Barabbas."

22 Pilate said, "Then what should I do with Jesus, who is called the Christ?"

They all said, "Nail Him to a cross!"

23 Pilate asked, "Why? What bad thing did He do?"

But they shouted even louder, "Nail Him to a cross!"

24 Pilate saw that it was no use. The crowd was getting louder. So he took water and washed his hands in front of them. He said, "I am not guilty of this man's death. You must take the blame."

25 All the people answered, "Let His death be on us and on our children!"

26 Then Pilate set Barabbas free. He had Jesus whipped and handed Him over to be nailed to a cross.

Soldiers Make Fun of Jesus

27 The governor's soldiers took Jesus into the governor's house. They called all the soldiers together around Him. 28 They took off His clothes and put a red robe on Him.

29 They made a crown from thorns and put it on His head. They put a stick in His right hand. Then they knelt in front of Him and made fun of Him. They said, "Hello, King of the Jews!"

30 They spit on Him. They took the stick and hit Him on the head with it. 31 After they made fun of Him, they took off the robe and put His own clothes back on Him. Then they led Him away to nail Him to a cross.

Jesus Is Nailed to a Cross

32 On the way out, they found a man from Cyrene named Simon. They made him carry Jesus' cross. 33 They came to a place called Golgotha, which means Place of the Skull. 34 They gave Him wine mixed with something bitter. He tasted it but did not want to drink it.

35 After they nailed Him to the cross, they divided His clothes by throwing lots. 36 Then they sat down and watched Him there. 37 They put a sign above His head. It said, "This is Jesus, the King of the Jews."

38 Two robbers were nailed to crosses with Him. One was on His right and one was on His left.

39 People walking by insulted Him. They shook their heads. 40 They said, "You said you could tear down the temple and build it again in three days. Save yourself! If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross!"

41 The top priests, teachers of the law, and leaders also made fun of Him. 42 They said, "He saved other people, but He cannot save Himself! He is the King of Israel. Let Him come down from the cross now, and we will believe in Him." 43 They said, "He trusts in God. Let God save Him now if He wants Him. After all, He said, 'I am the Son of God.'"

44 The robbers who were nailed to crosses with Him also insulted Him.

Jesus Dies

45 From noon until three in the afternoon, darkness covered the whole land. 46 About three in the afternoon, Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?" That means, "My God, My God, why have You left Me?"

47 Some people standing there heard it and said, "He is calling for Elijah."

48 One of them ran and got a sponge. He filled it with sour wine, put it on a stick, and gave it to Jesus to drink.

49 The others said, "Leave Him alone. Let's see if Elijah comes to save Him."

50 Jesus cried out again in a loud voice and then gave up His spirit.

51 The curtain in the temple tore in two from top to bottom. The earth shook, and the rocks split. 52 The tombs opened, and many holy people who had died were raised to life. 53 After Jesus rose, they came out of the tombs and went into the holy city. Many people saw them.

54 The army officer and the soldiers with him were guarding Jesus. When they saw the earthquake and everything that happened, they were very afraid. They said, "This man really was the Son of God!"

55 Many women were there, watching from far away. They had followed Jesus from Galilee and helped Him. 56 Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee's sons.

Jesus Is Buried

57 In the evening, a rich man named Joseph came. He was from Arimathea, and he was a follower of Jesus. 58 He went to Pilate and asked for Jesus' body. Pilate ordered that it be given to him.

59 Joseph took the body and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth. 60 He put it in his own new tomb, which he had cut out of the rock. He rolled a big stone in front of the tomb door and went away.

61 Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were sitting there across from the tomb.

Guards Watch the Tomb

62 The next day was the day after the Preparation Day. The top priests and the Pharisees went to Pilate.

63 They said, "Sir, we remember that liar said, 'After three days I will rise again.' 64 So please order the tomb to be guarded until the third day. His followers might come and steal the body and tell the people, 'He has risen from the dead.' Then the last lie would be worse than the first."

65 Pilate said to them, "You have a guard. Go and make it as safe as you can."

66 They went and made the tomb safe. They sealed the stone and put guards there.
