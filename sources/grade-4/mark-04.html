Jesus Teaches by the Sea

1 Again Jesus began to teach by the sea. A very big crowd came to Him. So He got into a boat and sat on the water. The whole crowd stood on the shore. 2 He taught them many things with stories. In His teaching He said to them:

The Story of the Farmer

3 "Listen! A farmer went out to plant seeds. 4 As he planted, some seeds fell by the path. Birds came and ate them. 5 Other seeds fell on rocky ground with little soil. They sprang up fast because the soil was not deep. 6 When the sun came up, they burned and dried up because they had no roots. 7 Other seeds fell among thorns. The thorns grew up and choked them, and they gave no fruit. 8 Other seeds fell on good soil. They grew up and got bigger and gave fruit: one gave thirty, one gave sixty, and one gave one hundred. 9 Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

10 When He was alone, the people around Him, with the twelve, asked Him about the stories.

Jesus Explains the Story

11 He said to them, "The secret of God's kingdom is given to you. But to people outside, everything is in stories. 12 They look, but they do not really see. They listen, but they do not understand. Then they will not turn and be forgiven."

13 He said to them, "Do you not know this story? How will you know all the stories?"

14 "The farmer plants God's message. 15 These people are like the seed by the path. The message is planted, but as soon as they hear it, Satan comes and takes the message away from them. 16 These people are like the seed on rocky ground. When they hear the message, they take it with joy. 17 But they have no root in themselves. They do not last long. When trouble or people hurt them because of the message, they give up. 18 Others are like the seed among thorns. They hear the message. 19 But the worries of this life, the lies of money, and wanting other things come in and choke the message. It does not grow. 20 These people are like the seed on good soil. They hear the message, take it in, and grow fruit: thirty, sixty, or one hundred."

A Lamp and a Warning

21 He said to them, "Does a lamp come to be put under a basket or under a bed? No, it is put on a lamp stand. 22 Nothing is hidden that will not be shown. Nothing is kept secret that will not come into the light. 23 Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

24 He said to them, "Pay attention to what you hear. The way you measure will be measured to you, and more will be added to you. 25 Whoever has will be given more. Whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away."

God's Kingdom Grows

26 He said, "God's kingdom is like this: a man throws seed on the ground. 27 Night and day he sleeps and wakes. The seed sprouts and grows, but he does not know how. 28 The ground makes fruit on its own: first a green stem, then a head of grain, then full grain in the head. 29 As soon as the fruit is ready, he cuts it down, because harvest time has come."

God's Kingdom Is Like a Mustard Seed

30 He said, "How can we show God's kingdom? What story can we use? 31 It is like a mustard seed. When it is planted in the ground, it is the smallest seed on earth. 32 But when it is planted, it grows up and becomes the biggest plant in the garden. It makes big branches, so the birds of the sky can rest in its shade."

33 He spoke the message to them with many stories like these, as much as they could understand. 34 He did not speak to them without a story. But when He was alone, He explained everything to His own followers.

Jesus Stops the Storm

35 That day, when evening came, He said to them, "Let us go to the other side." 36 They left the crowd and took Him in the boat, just as He was. Other boats were with Him.

37 A big storm with wind came up. The waves splashed into the boat, so the boat was already filling with water. 38 Jesus was in the back of the boat, sleeping on a pillow. They woke Him and said, "Teacher, don't you care that we are dying?"

39 He woke up and told the wind and the sea, "Quiet! Be still!" The wind stopped, and it became very calm. 40 He said to them, "Why are you afraid? Do you still not trust Me?"

41 They were very afraid and said to each other, "Who is this? Even the wind and the sea obey Him!"
