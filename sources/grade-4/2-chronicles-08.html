Solomon Builds Cities

1 After twenty years, Solomon finished building the the LORD’s house and his own house. 2 Solomon built again the cities Hiram gave him. He moved Israel people to live in them.

3 Then Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and took it over. 4 He built Tadmor in the desert. He also built cities to store things in Hamath. 5 He built Upper and Lower Beth-horon again. He made them strong cities with walls, gates, and bars. 6 He built Baalath too. He built cities to store things, cities for his chariots and horses, and all he wanted to build in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and his whole land.

Workers for Solomon

7 Some people were left from the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. They were not Israel people. 8 Their children stayed in the land after them. Israel had not destroyed them. Solomon made them work hard. They still do this today.

9 But Solomon did not make Israel people work like that. They were his soldiers, leaders, and chariot and horse captains. 10 King Solomon had 250 leaders. They watched over the people.

Solomon’s Wife and Worship

11 Solomon brought Pharaoh’s daughter from David’s City to the house he built for her. He said, “My wife will not live in David’s house, king of Israel. The places where the the LORD’s holy box came are holy.”

12 At that time, Solomon gave burnt gifts to the the LORD on the the LORD’s altar. He had built it in front of the porch. 13 He gave them each day, as Moses said. He did this on rest days, new moon days, and three special times each year: the Feast of Bread Without Yeast, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tents.

14 Like his father David’s plan, Solomon put priests in groups to serve. He put Levites in groups to sing praise and help the priests each day. He also put gate guards in groups at each gate. David, the man of God, had told him to do this. 15 They did not turn away from the king’s words about the priests, Levites, or treasure rooms. 16 So all Solomon’s work was done. It started when they laid the base of the the LORD’s house. It ended when the the LORD’s house was finished. The The LORD’s house was done.

Gold from Ophir

17 Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and Eloth on the coast of Edom. 18 There, Hiram sent him ships with his servants. Sailors who knew the sea went too. They went with Solomon’s servants to Ophir. They brought back 450 talents of gold to King Solomon.
