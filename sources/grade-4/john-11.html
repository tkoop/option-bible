Lazarus Is Sick

1 A man named Lazarus was sick. He was from Bethany, the town of Mary and her sister Martha. 2 Mary was the one who put perfume on the Lord and wiped His feet with her hair. Lazarus was her brother.

3 So the sisters sent word to Jesus. They said, "Lord, the one You love is sick."

4 Jesus heard this. He said, "This sickness will not end in death. It is for God's glory. The Son of God will get glory through it."

5 Jesus loved Martha, her sister, and Lazarus. 6 When He heard Lazarus was sick, He stayed where He was for two more days.

Jesus Goes to Bethany

7 Then He said to His followers, "Let us go back to Judea."

8 "Teacher," they said, "the Jews just tried to stone You. Are You going there again?"

9 Jesus answered, "There are twelve hours of light in a day. If someone walks in the day, he does not trip. He sees the light of this world. 10 But if someone walks at night, he trips. There is no light."

11 After this, He said, "Our friend Lazarus is asleep. But I am going to wake him up."

12 His followers said, "Lord, if he is asleep, he will get better."

13 They thought He meant real sleep. But Jesus meant Lazarus had died.

14 Then Jesus said plainly, "Lazarus is dead. 15 I am glad I was not there, so you may believe. But let us go to him."

16 Thomas, called the Twin, said to the other followers, "Let us go too, so we can die with Him."

17 When Jesus arrived, He found Lazarus had been in the tomb four days. 18 Bethany was near Jerusalem, about two miles away. 19 Many Jews had come to Martha and Mary. They came to comfort them about their brother.

20 Martha heard Jesus was coming. She went to meet Him. Mary stayed home.

Jesus Talks with Martha

21 Martha said to Jesus, "Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died. 22 But I know God will give You whatever You ask Him."

23 Jesus said, "Your brother will rise again."

24 Martha said, "I know he will rise again in the last day."

25 Jesus said, "I am the one who brings people back to life and gives life. The one who believes in Me will live, even if he dies. 26 Everyone who lives and believes in Me will never die. Do you believe this?"

27 "Yes, Lord," she said. "I believe You are the Christ, the Son of God, the One who comes into the world."

28 After she said this, she went back. She called her sister Mary quietly. She said, "The Teacher is here. He is calling you."

29 Mary got up quickly and went to Him. 30 Jesus had not yet come into the town. He was still where Martha met Him. 31 The Jews in the house were comforting Mary. They saw her get up fast and leave. They followed her. They thought she was going to the tomb to cry there.

32 Mary came to where Jesus was. She saw Him and fell at His feet. She said, "Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died."

Jesus Weeps

33 Jesus saw her crying. He saw the Jews with her crying too. He was very upset in spirit. He was troubled.

34 He asked, "Where have you laid him?"

They said, "Lord, come and see."

35 Jesus wept.

36 The Jews said, "See how He loved him!"

37 But some said, "He opened the eyes of the blind man. Could He not have kept this man from dying?"

38 Jesus was still very upset. He came to the tomb. It was a cave. A stone was over it.

Lazarus Is Raised

39 Jesus said, "Take away the stone."

Martha said, "Lord, he smells bad now. He has been dead four days."

40 Jesus said, "Did I not tell you that if you believe, you will see God's glory?"

41 So they took away the stone. Jesus looked up and said, "Father, I thank You that You heard Me. 42 I know You always hear Me. But I said this for the crowd standing here. I said it so they will believe You sent Me."

43 After He said this, He called in a loud voice, "Lazarus, come out!"

44 The dead man came out. His hands and feet were wrapped in strips of cloth. His face was wrapped in a cloth. Jesus said, "Take off the grave clothes and let him go."

45 Many Jews who had come to Mary saw what Jesus did. They believed in Him.

Leaders Plan to Kill Jesus

46 But some went to the Pharisees. They told them what Jesus had done.

47 The chief priests and Pharisees called a meeting. They said, "What are we doing? This man does many miracles. 48 If we let Him go on, everyone will believe in Him. Then the Romans will come and take away our place and our nation."

49 Caiaphas, the high priest that year, said, "You do not know anything. You do not see that it is better for you that one man die for the people. 50 Then the whole nation will not die."

51 He did not say this on his own. As high priest that year, he spoke a message from God. He said Jesus would die for the nation. 52 He also said Jesus would die to bring together God's scattered children into one family.

53 From that day on, they planned to kill Him.

54 So Jesus did not walk openly among the Jews anymore. He left there. He went to a town called Ephraim, near the desert. He stayed there with His followers.

People Look for Jesus

55 The Jewish Passover feast was near. Many people went up from the country to Jerusalem. They went to make themselves clean before Passover.

56 They looked for Jesus. They talked with each other in the temple. They said, "What do you think? Will He not come to the feast?"

57 The chief priests and Pharisees had given orders. If anyone knew where Jesus was, he must tell them. They wanted to arrest Him.
