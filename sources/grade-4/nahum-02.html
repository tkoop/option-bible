An Enemy Attacks Nineveh

1 An enemy is coming to attack you. Guard your walls. Watch the road. Get ready. Gather all your strength.

The LORD Will Restore Israel

2 For the LORD will make Jacob great again, like Israel. Enemies broke them and ruined their grapevines.

The Army Marches

3 His strong soldiers' shields are red. His brave men wear red. The chariots shine like fire when they are ready. The spears are raised. 4 The chariots race through the streets. They rush around the squares. They look like torches. They flash like lightning. 5 He calls his leaders. They trip as they run. They hurry to the wall. A shield is set up.

The City Is Taken

6 The river gates open. The palace falls down. 7 It is decided that the city will be taken away. Its young women cry like doves. They beat their chests. 8 Nineveh has been like a pool of water for a long time. Now its people run away. "Stop! Stop!" they shout, but no one stops. 9 Take the silver! Take the gold! There is no end to the riches. There are many precious things. 10 Empty! Empty and ruined! Hearts melt. Knees shake. Bodies tremble. Every face turns pale.

Where Are the Lions?

11 Where is the lions' den? Where did the young lions eat? The lion and lioness walked there with their cubs. Nothing scared them. 12 The lion killed enough for his cubs. He caught food for his lionesses. He filled his caves with food. His dens were full of torn meat.

The LORD Is Against Nineveh

13 Look, I am against you, says the LORD of Armies. I will burn your chariots in smoke. A sword will eat your young lions. I will cut off your food from the land. No one will hear your messengers anymore.
