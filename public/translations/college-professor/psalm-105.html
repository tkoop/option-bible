A Summons to Celebrate the LORD's Covenant Faithfulness

1Give thanks to the LORD, invoke HishisHis name, and proclaim HishisHis mighty deeds among the peoples. 2Sing to HimhimHim, chant psalms to HimhimHim, and meditate upon all HishisHis wondrous works. 3Exult in HishisHis holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

4Seek the LORD and HishisHis strength; pursue HishisHis presence without ceasing. 5Remember the marvels HeheHe has wrought, HishisHis portents and the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments uttered by HishisHis mouth, 6O offspring of Abraham HishisHis servant, children of Jacob HishisHis chosen. 7He is the LORD our God; HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments prevail throughout the whole EarthEarthearth.

The Everlasting Covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob

8He remembers HishisHis covenant forever, the promise HeheHe ordained for a thousand generations— 9the covenant HeheHe struck with Abraham and the oath HeheHe swore to Isaac. 10He ratified it for Jacob as an ordinance, for Israel as a covenant in perpetuity, 11saying, "I will give you the land of Canaan as the measured allotment of your inheritance."

12When they were but a handful, few in number and sojourners within it, 13they wandered from nation to nation, from one realm to another people. 14He allowed no mortal to oppress them; HeheHe rebuked kings on their behalf.

15"Do not touch MymyMy anointed ones, and do MymyMy prophets no harm."

Joseph's Exaltation and Israel's Sojourn in Egypt

16He summoned famine upon the land and shattered every staff of bread. 17He sent a man before them—Joseph—sold into slavery. 18They afflicted his feet with shackles; his soul was confined in iron. 19Until the appointed time, when his word came to pass, the word of the LORD tested him. 20The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free. 21He appointed him master of his household and steward of all his possessions, 22to bind his princes at his discretion and to impart wisdom to his elders.

23Then Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. 24The LORD made HishisHis people exceedingly fruitful and stronger than their adversaries. 25He turned their hearts to hate HishisHis people, to deal treacherously with HishisHis servants.

The Plagues, the Exodus, and Wilderness Provision

26He sent Moses HishisHis servant, and Aaron whom HeheHe had chosen. 27They performed HishisHis signs among them and wonders in the land of Ham. 28He sent darkness and made it dark, and they did not defy HishisHis words. 29He turned their waters into blood and slew their fish. 30Their land teemed with frogs, even in the royal chambers. 31He spoke, and swarms of flies came, and gnats throughout all their territory. 32He gave them hail for rain and blazing fire upon their land. 33He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their borders. 34He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts beyond number— 35and they devoured every herb in their land and consumed the fruit of their soil.

36He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their strength. 37He brought HishisHis people out with silver and gold, and none among HishisHis tribes stumbled. 38Egypt rejoiced at their departure, for terror of them had fallen upon it. 39He spread a cloud for a covering and fire to illumine the night. 40They asked, and HeheHe brought quail and satisfied them with bread from Heavenheavenheaven. 41He opened the rock, and waters gushed forth; they ran in the arid wastes like a river. 42For HeheHe remembered HishisHis holy promise to Abraham HishisHis servant.

43He brought out HishisHis people with gladness, HishisHis chosen with shouts of joy. 44He gave them the lands of the nations, and they inherited the toil of the peoples, 45that they might keep HishisHis statutes and observe HishisHis laws. Praise the LORD!
