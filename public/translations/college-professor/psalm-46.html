God as Refuge Amid Cosmic Cataclysm

1God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of distress. 2Therefore we shall not fear, though the earth be convulsed and the mountains be cast into the heart of the seas, 3though its waters roar and foam and the mountains quake in its surge. Selah.

The Holy City and the Tumult of Nations

4There is a river whose streams gladden the city of God, the holy habitation of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 5God is within her; she shall not be moved. God will succor her when morning dawns. 6The nations roar, the kingdoms falter; HeheHe utters HishisHis voice, and the earth melts. 7The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.

The Desolations of the LORD and the Summons to Stillness

8Come, behold the works of the LORD, how HeheHe has wrought desolation upon the earth. 9He makes wars cease unto the ends of the earth; HeheHe breaks the bow and shatters the spear; HeheHe burns the chariots with fire.

10"Cease your striving, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations; I will be exalted in the earth."

11The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.
