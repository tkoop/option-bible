Exhortations for the Congregation’s Various Households

1But you must articulate what is consonant with salubrious doctrine: 2older men are to be temperate, venerable, discreet, and robust in faith, in love, and in endurance; 3older women, in like manner, are to be reverent in deportment, not calumnious, not enslaved to copious wine, but instructors of what is noble; 4so that they may admonish the young women to be affectionate toward their husbands and children, 5discreet, chaste, domestic, beneficent, and submissive to their own husbands, lest the word of God be blasphemed.

6Likewise, exhort younger men to be self-controlled. 7In all things, present yourself as a paradigm of noble works; in teaching, exhibit incorruptibility and gravity, 8together with sound speech beyond reproach, so that any adversary may be put to shame, having no malignancy to allege concerning us.
