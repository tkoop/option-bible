From Death in Sin to Life in Christ

1And as for you, who were dead in your trespasses and sins, 2in which you formerly conducted yourselves according to the course of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit that is now at work among the sons of disobedience, 3among whom we all also formerly conducted ourselves in the cravings of our flesh, indulging the inclinations of the flesh and of the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, as the rest of mankind.

4But God, being rich in mercy, because of the great love with which HeheHe loved us, 5even when we were dead in our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved— 6and raised us up together and seated us together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus, 7so that in the coming ages HeheHe might display the surpassing riches of HishisHis grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

8For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God, 9not from works, so that no one may boast. 10For we are HishisHis workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we might conduct ourselves in them.

The Reconciliation of Gentile and Jew in Christ

11Therefore remember that formerly you, the Gentiles in the flesh, who are called “the uncircumcision” by those called “the circumcision” in the flesh, made by hands, 12were at that time without Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world. 13But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far off have become near by the blood of Christ.

14For HeheHe HimselfhimselfHimself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the dividing wall of hostility, 15by abolishing in HishisHis flesh the law of commandments expressed in ordinances, so that HeheHe might create in HimselfhimselfHimself one new man from the two, thus making peace, 16and might reconcile both to God in one body through the cross, thereby putting to death the hostility in HimselfhimselfHimself. 17And HeheHe came and preached peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near. 18For through HimhimHim we both have access in one SpiritspiritSpirit to the FatherfatherFather.

The Household and Temple of God

19So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God, 20built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus HimselfhimselfHimself being the cornerstone, 21in whom the whole building, being fitted together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord, 22in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God in the SpiritspiritSpirit.
