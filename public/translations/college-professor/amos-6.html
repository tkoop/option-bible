Woe to the Complacent in Zion and Samaria

1Woe to those who are at ease in Zion and to those who feel secure on Mount Samaria—the distinguished of the foremost nation, to whom the house of Israel comes. 2Cross over to Calneh and see; go from there to great Hamath; then descend to Gath of the Philistines. Are those realms superior to these, or is their territory more expansive than your own? 3You defer the day of disaster and draw near a seat of violence.

Luxury in the Face of Ruin

4You who recline on ivory couches and sprawl upon your divans, eating lambs from the flock and fattened calves from the stall, 5who strum idle melodies on the harp and devise songs for yourselves as David did, 6who drink wine from bowls and anoint yourselves with the choicest oils, yet feel no anguish over the ruin of Joseph.

Exile of the Self-Indulgent

7Therefore they shall now be led away at the head of the exiles, and the carousing of the self-indulgent shall pass away.

The LORD's Oath Against Jacob

8The Lord the LORD has sworn by HimselfhimselfHimself—the the LORD, the God of Hosts, declares: "I abhor the arrogance of Jacob and hate his palaces; I will surrender the city and all that fills it."

9If ten men remain in a single house, they shall die. 10When a relative or the one who burns him carries the bones out of the house and says to the one in the inner chamber, "IsisIs anyone still with you?"

When he answers, "No," the relative will say, "Hush! For the name of the the LORD is not to be invoked."

11For behold, the the LORD commands, and HeheHe will strike the great house into fragments and the small house into cracks.

12Do horses run on rock? Does one plow there with oxen? Yet you have turned justice into venom and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.

13You who exult in emptiness, boasting, "Have we not gained power by our own strength?"

14For behold, I am raising up a nation against you, O house of Israel, declares the the LORD, the God of Hosts, and they shall oppress you from the entrance of Hamath to the BrookbrookBrook of the Arabah.
