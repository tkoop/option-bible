Praise for the Wondrous JudgmentsJudgementsJudgments of the LORD

1O the LORD, YouyouYou are my God; I will exalt YouyouYou, and I will praise YouryourYour name, for YouyouYou have wrought wonders—designs ordained from of old in perfect faithfulness. 2For YouyouYou have consigned the city to a heap and the fortified city to ruin; the palace of aliens is a city no more, and it shall never be rebuilt. 3Therefore a mighty people will glorify YouyouYou, and the cities of ruthless nations will revere YouyouYou.

4For YouyouYou have been a fortress to the poor, a fortress to the needy in his distress, a refuge from the storm and a shade from the heat; for the breath of the ruthless is like a storm driving against a wall. 5Like heat in a desiccated land, YouyouYou will subdue the tumult of foreigners; like heat stilled beneath a cloud, the song of the ruthless will be silenced.

The Eschatological Banquet and the Defeat of Death

6On this mountain the LORD of Hosts will prepare for all peoples a feast of rich food, a banquet of aged wines—rich food full of marrow, aged wine well refined. 7On this mountain HeheHe will destroy the shroud that is cast over all peoples and the veil that is spread over all nations. 8He will swallow up death forever; and the Lord the LORD will wipe away tears from every face; HeheHe will remove the reproach of HishisHis people from all the earth, for the LORD has spoken.

9In that day it will be said, "Behold, this is our God; we have waited for HimhimHim, and HeheHe will save us. This is the LORD; we have waited for HimhimHim. Let us rejoice and be glad in HishisHis salvation."

Moab's Humiliation and the Triumph of Zion

10For the hand of the LORD will rest upon this mountain, while Moab is trampled in its place, as straw is trampled in the waters of a dunghill. 11He will spread out his hands in its midst, as a swimmer spreads his hands to swim; yet his pride will be brought low together with the craftiness of his hands. 12He will bring down the high fortifications of your walls, lay them low, and cast them to the ground, even to the dust.
