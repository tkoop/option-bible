The Patrimony Allotted to Simeon and Zebulun

1The second lot issued for the clans of Simeon, and their inheritance fell within the territory already assigned to Judah. 2Their allotment comprised Beersheba (also called Sheba), Moladah, 3Hazar-shual, Balah, and Ezem; 4Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah; 5Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah; 6Beth-lebaoth and Sharuhen—thirteen cities, together with their villages. 7It further included Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan—four cities with their villages— 8along with all the surrounding villages as far as Baalath-beer (Ramath of the Negev). Such was the inheritance of the Simeonite clans. 9Because Judah's portion proved disproportionately extensive, Simeon's inheritance was taken from within Judah's territory; thus the Simeonites received their portion in the midst of Judah's land.

10The third lot came up for the Zebulunites according to their clans, and the boundary of their inheritance extended to Sarid. 11From there it ascended to the sea, reaching Maralah; it touched Dabbasheth and extended to the brook east of Jokneam. 12Turning east from Sarid toward the sunrise, it reached the border of Chisloth-tabor, extended to Daberath, and ascended to Japhia. 13Thence it crossed eastward to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, came out at Rimmon, and turned toward Neah. 14The border then curved northward around Hannathon, and its termini were the ValleyvalleyValley of Iphtah-el. 15The inheritance further included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem—twelve cities with their villages. 16These cities and villages constituted the inheritance of the Zebulunites according to their clans.

The Inheritances of Issachar and Asher

17The fourth lot issued for Issachar, for its clans. 18Their territory embraced Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem, 19Hapharaim, Shion, and Anaharath; 20Rabbith, Kishion, and Abez; 21Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 22The boundary touched Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, and its terminus was the Jordan—sixteen cities with their villages. 23These cities and villages were the inheritance of the Issacharites according to their clans.

24The fifth lot came out for the tribe of Asher according to its clans. 25Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph; 26Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal. On the west it touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath. 27Turning eastward toward Beth-dagon, it touched Zebulun and the ValleyvalleyValley of Iphtah-el on the north, then Beth-emek and Neiel, and extended to Cabul on the left. 28It further reached Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as GreatergreaterGreater Sidon. 29The boundary then turned to Ramah and the fortified city of Tyre; turning again to Hosah, it terminated at the sea in the region of Achzib, 30together with Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob—twenty-two cities with their villages. 31These cities and villages were the inheritance of the Asherites according to their clans.

The Inheritances of Naphtali and Dan

32The sixth lot issued for the Naphtalites according to their clans. 33Their boundary ran from Heleph, from the oak at Zaanannim, through Adami-nekeb and Jabneel, to Lakkum, and its terminus was the Jordan. 34The boundary then turned westward to Aznoth-tabor and went out to Hukkok; it touched Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan on the east. 35The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth, 36Adamah, Ramah, Hazor, 37Kedesh, Edrei, and En-hazor, 38Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh—nineteen cities with their villages. 39These cities and villages were the inheritance of the Naphtalites according to their clans.

40The seventh lot came out for the tribe of Dan according to its clans. 41The territory of their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh, 42Shaalabbin, Aijalon, and Ithlah; 43Elon, Timnah, and Ekron; 44Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath; 45Jehud, Bene-berak, and Gath-rimmon; 46Me-jarkon and Rakkon, with the territory facing Joppa. 47When the territory of the Danites proved too small for them, they went up and fought against Leshem, captured it, struck it with the sword, took possession of it, settled in it, and called Leshem Dan, after the name of their ancestor Dan. 48These cities and villages were the inheritance of the Danites according to their clans.

Joshua's Inheritance and the Completion of the Allotment

49When the Israelites had finished allotting the land according to its boundaries, they gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them. 50By the command of the LORD, they gave him the city he requested—Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim. He rebuilt the city and settled in it. 51These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelite tribes allotted by lot at Shiloh before the LORD, at the entrance to the tent of meeting. Thus they finished dividing the land.
