Prohibition of Defective Sacrifices

1You shall not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that bears a blemish or any other defect, for such an offering is an abomination to the LORD your God.

Judicial Sanctions for Idolatry

2If, within one of the towns that the LORD your God gives you, there is found a man or a woman who does what is evil in the sight of the LORD your God by transgressing HishisHis covenant, 3and if that person has gone and served other gods and worshiped them, or the sun, or the moon, or any of the host of Heavenheavenheaven—which I have not commanded— 4and if the matter is reported to you and you hear of it, then you must investigate it diligently; and if the report proves true and well established—that this abomination has indeed been perpetrated in Israel—

5then you shall bring out to your gates that man or that woman who has committed this wicked deed, and you shall stone them with stones so that they die. 6On the testimony of two or three witnesses the condemned person shall be put to death, but no one shall be put to death on the testimony of a single witness. 7The hands of the witnesses shall be the first to put the offender to death, and afterward the hands of all the people; thus you shall purge the evil from your midst.

Adjudication of Difficult Cases

8If a case is too difficult for you to adjudicate—matters of bloodshed, of legal claims, or of bodily injury—within your towns, then you shall arise and go up to the place that the LORD your God will choose. 9You shall come to the Levitical priests and to the judge who is in office in those days, inquire of them, and they shall declare to you the verdict of the case. 10You shall act according to the decision that they render for you from that place which the LORD will choose, and you must be careful to do all that they instruct you. 11According to the law that they teach you and according to the judgmentjudgementjudgment that they pronounce for you, you shall act; you shall not deviate to the right or to the left from the decision that they declare to you.

12And the person who acts presumptuously, refusing to heed the priest who stands there to minister before the LORD your God or the judge—that person shall die, and you shall purge the evil from Israel. 13Then all the people shall hear and fear, and they shall not act presumptuously again.

Provisions Concerning a Future Monarch

14When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, take possession of it, and settle in it, and you say, “I will set a king over me, like all the nations around me,”

15you must surely set over you a king whom the LORD your God chooses. From among your brothers you shall set a king over you; you may not appoint over you a foreigner who is not your brother.

16He must not acquire many horses for himself, nor lead the people back to Egypt in order to acquire more horses, for the LORD has declared to you, “You shall never return that way again.”

17He must not take many wives, lest his heart be led astray, nor may he amass for himself an excessive store of silver and gold.

18When he is seated upon the throne of his kingdom, he shall write for himself a transcript of this law in a scroll, in the presence of the Levitical priests. 19It shall remain with him, and he shall read from it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the LORD his God, to observe all the words of this law and these statutes, and to carry them out, 20so that his heart does not become haughty toward his brothers and he does not turn aside from the commandment to the right or to the left, in order that he and his sons may prolong their days over his kingdom in the midst of Israel.
