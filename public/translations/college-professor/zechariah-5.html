The Vision of the Flying Scroll

1I raised my eyes once more and beheld a scroll in flight.

2He said to me, "What do you see?"

I answered, "I see a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits, and its width is ten cubits."

3He said to me, "This is the curse that is going out over the face of the whole land: for in accordance with it every thief shall be purged from the land, and in accordance with it every perjurer shall be purged from the land."

4"I will send it out," declares the LORD of Hosts, "and it shall enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by MymyMy name; it shall lodge in his house and consume it, timber and stones together."

The Vision of the Woman in the Ephah

5The angel who was speaking with me came forward and said, "Lift up your eyes and see what this is that is going out."

6I asked, "What is it?"

He answered, "It is an ephah that is going out. This is their iniquity throughout the land."

7And behold, a lead cover was lifted, and a woman was sitting in the midst of the ephah.

8He said, "This is Wickedness." Then he thrust her into the ephah and cast the lead stone upon its mouth.

The Removal of Wickedness to Shinar

9Then I lifted my eyes and saw two women coming out, with wind in their wings; they had wings like a stork's wings, and they lifted the ephah between Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.

10I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "Where are they carrying the ephah?"

11He replied, "They are going to build a house for it in the land of Shinar; when it is ready, it will be set there upon its base."
