The Wise Son and the Scoffer's Refusal

1A sagacious son heeds the discipline of his father, while the scoffer remains obdurate to rebuke. 2By the fruit of his own speech a man nourishes himself well, whereas the very appetite of the treacherous is inflamed with violence. 3The one who guards his mouth preserves his life, while he who gives free rein to his lips courts his own destruction.

4The sluggard's soul craves and receives nothing, but the soul of the diligent is abundantly sated. 5The righteous abhor falsehood, while the wicked comport themselves basely and heap disgrace upon themselves. 6Righteousness stands sentinel over the way of the blameless, but wickedness subverts the path of the sinner.

The Paradoxes of Wealth and the Fruit of Diligence

7One man amasses apparent riches yet possesses nothing, while another affects poverty yet commands great wealth. 8A man's riches may serve as the ransom of his life, whereas the poor man remains unrebuked. 9The light of the righteous shines with gladness, while the lamp of the wicked is extinguished.

10Arrogance begets nothing but strife, yet wisdom resides with those who accept counsel. 11Wealth amassed in haste dwindles away, but he who gathers little by little amplifies his store. 12Hope deferred sickens the heart, whereas desire fulfilled is a tree of life.

Instruction, Discernment, and the Company One Keeps

13He who contemns the word brings ruin upon himself, while he who reveres the commandment shall be recompensed. 14The instruction of the wise is a fountain of life, diverting one from the snares of death. 15Sound discernment wins favourfavourfavor, whereas the path of the treacherous is arduous.

16Every prudent man acts with discernment, while the fool disseminates his folly. 17A wicked envoy stumbles into adversity, while a faithful messenger brings healing. 18Poverty and disgrace befall him who disregards discipline, but he who heeds reproof is honouredhonouredhonored.

Righteousness, Inheritance, and the Satisfaction of the Soul

19A desire fulfilled is sweet to the soul, yet fools abhor turning away from evil. 20He who consorts with the wise grows wise, while the companion of fools suffers harm. 21Calamity pursues sinners, whereas prosperity rewards the righteous.

22A good man bequeaths an inheritance to his children's children, but the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the righteous. 23The fallow ground of the poor yields abundant food, yet without justice it is swept away. 24He who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves him disciplines him with diligence. 25The righteous eat to the satisfaction of the soul, while the belly of the wicked goes hungry.
