Establishing Cities of Refuge for the Unintentional Manslayer

1When the LORD your God cuts off the nations whose land the LORD your God gives you, and you dispossess them and dwell in their cities and houses, 2set apart for yourself three cities in the midst of your land that the LORD your God gives you to possess. 3Prepare the way for yourself, and divide into three parts the territory of your land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, so that any manslayer may flee there.

4This is the matter concerning the manslayer who flees there and lives: whoever strikes his neighbourneighbourneighbor without prior knowledge, not having hated him before. 5For instance, if a man goes into the forest with his neighbourneighbourneighbor to cut wood, and as his hand swings the axe to fell a tree, the iron head slips from the handle and strikes his neighbourneighbourneighbor so that he dies, he may flee to one of these cities and live. 6Otherwise, the avenger of blood might pursue the manslayer while his heart is hot, overtake him because the distance is great, and strike him dead, though he does not deserve death, since he did not hate his neighbourneighbourneighbor previously.

7Therefore I command you, saying, “Set apart for yourself three cities.”

Enlarging the Land and the Cities of Refuge

8And if the LORD your God enlarges your border, as HeheHe swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land that HeheHe promised to give your fathers, 9provided that you keep all this commandment that I command you today—to love the LORD your God and to walk in HishisHis ways all your days—then you shall add three more cities for yourself besides these three, 10so that innocent blood will not be shed in the midst of your land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance, and bloodguilt will not be upon you.

Punishing Premeditated Murder

11But if a man hates his neighbourneighbourneighbor and lies in wait for him, rises against him, and strikes him so that he dies, and he flees to one of these cities, 12then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there and deliver him into the hand of the avenger of blood, that he may die. 13Your eye shall not pity him, but you shall purge the innocent blood from Israel, that it may go well with you.

Safeguarding Boundaries and Testimony

14You shall not move your neighbourneighbourneighbor's landmark, which the ancestors have set in your inheritance that you will inherit in the land that the LORD your God gives you to possess. 15A single witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity or sin in any offenceoffenceoffense he may commit; a matter shall be established only by the testimony of two or three witnesses. 16If a malicious witness rises up against a man to accuse him of wrongdoing, 17then both men who have the dispute shall stand before the LORD, before the priests and judges who are in those days. 18The judges shall inquire diligently, and if the witness proves to be a false witness who has testified falsely against his brother, 19then you shall do to him as he had intended to do to his brother, and you shall purge the evil from your midst. 20The rest shall hear and fear, and they shall never again do such an evil thing among you. 21Your eye shall not pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
