A Supplication for Expedient Deliverance

For the director of music. A Psalm of David, for remembrance. 1Hasten, O God, to rescue me; make haste, O the LORD, to my aid. 2May those who seek my life be shamed and confounded; may those who delight in my ruin turn back and be dishonoureddishonoureddishonored. 3May those who cry, “Aha! Aha!” recoil in their own disgrace. 4Let all who seek YouyouYou rejoice and be glad in YouyouYou; let those who love YouryourYour salvation say continually, “God is great!” 5But I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; O the LORD, do not delay.
