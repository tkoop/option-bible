Naomi’s Counsel to Ruth

1Naomi, her mother-in-law, addressed her: “My daughter, is it not incumbent upon me to secure for you a tranquil domestic settlement, so that your welfare may be advanced? 2Moreover, is not Boaz our kinsman, alongside whose maidens you have been laboring? Observe: this very night he is winnowing barley upon the threshing floor. 3Therefore bathe, anoint yourself, array yourself in your garments, and descend to the threshing floor; yet do not disclose your presence to the man until he has concluded his eating and drinking. 4And when he reclines, mark precisely the place of his repose; then approach, uncover his feet, and lie down; whereupon he will apprise you of what you ought to do.”

5She replied, “Everything you have enjoined, I shall perform.”

Ruth’s Nocturnal Descent to the Threshing Floor

6Thus she descended to the threshing floor and executed all that her mother-in-law had prescribed. 7Boaz, having eaten and drunk and finding his heart gladdened, went to lie down at the extremity of the grain heap; thereupon Ruth approached with circumspection, uncovered his feet, and reclined. 8At midnight the man was startled and turned; and, to his astonishment, a woman lay at his feet.

9He said, “Who are you?”

She said, “I am Ruth, your maidservant; take me under your marital protection, for you are my kinsman-redeemer.”

Boaz’s Pledge of Redemption

10He replied, “May the LORD bless you, my daughter. Your present act of devotedness surpasses your former kindness, inasmuch as you have not pursued younger men, whether affluent or impoverished. 11And now, my daughter, be not apprehensive; I will accomplish for you all that you request, for the entire populace of my city recognizesrecognisesrecognizes you as a woman of exemplary virtue. 12It is indeed true that I am a kinsman-redeemer; nevertheless, there exists a redeemer nearer of kin than I. 13Remain here this night; and in the morning, if he is willing to perform the office of redeemer for you, well and good—let him redeem you. Yet should he decline, then, as the LORD lives, I myself will redeem you. Recline here until morning.”

14So she reclined at his feet until morning, then arose before anyone could discern another; and he cautioned, “Let it not be reported that a woman has visited the threshing floor.” 15He said, “Bring the mantle that is upon you and hold it forth.” She held it, and he measured six measures of barley into it and laid it upon her; then he repaired to the city.

Ruth’s Report to Naomi

16When Ruth returned to her mother-in-law, Naomi inquired, “How fares it with you, my daughter?”

Ruth then recounted all that the man had done for her. 17“He bestowed upon me these six measures of barley, saying, ‘You must not return to your mother-in-law empty-handed.’”

18Naomi replied, “Be patient, my daughter, until you learn how the matter will eventuate; for the man will not be at rest until he has brought the affair to completion this very day.”
