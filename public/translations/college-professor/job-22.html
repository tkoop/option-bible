Eliphaz Charges Job with Concealed Iniquity

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

“2Can a mortal man be of service to God? Surely the discerning man profits himself. 3Is it any pleasure to the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty that you are righteous, or any gain to HimhimHim that you make your ways blameless? 4Is it on account of your piety that HeheHe reproves you, and brings you into judgmentjudgementjudgment?

“5Is not your wickedness great, and is there no end to your iniquities? 6For you have exacted sureties from your brothers without cause, and stripped the naked of their garments. 7You have not given water to the faint to drink, and you have withheld bread from the famished. 8The EarthEarthearth was the domain of the powerful, and the man of distinction dwelt in it. 9You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.

“10Therefore snares encircle you, and sudden dread terrifies you; 11or darkness, so that you cannot see, and a deluge of waters covers you.

The Folly of Imagining That God Is Distant

“12Is not God in the loftiness of Heavenheavenheaven? Behold the summit of the stars, how exalted they are! 13Yet you say, ‘What does God know? Can HeheHe judge through the dark cloud?’ 14Thick clouds are a hiding place for HimhimHim, so that HeheHe does not see; and HeheHe walks upon the vault of Heavenheavenheaven. 15Will you keep to the ancient way that wicked men have trodden— 16those who were snatched away before their time, whose foundation was swept away like a river? 17They said to God, ‘Depart from us,’ and, ‘What can the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty do to us?’ 18Yet HeheHe filled their houses with good; but the counsel of the wicked is far from me. 19The righteous see it and rejoice, and the innocent mock them. 20Surely our adversaries are cut off, and fire consumes their remnant.

The Promise of Restoration through Repentance

“21Acquaint yourself with HimhimHim, and be at peace; thereby good shall come to you. 22Receive instruction from HishisHis mouth, and lay up HishisHis words in your heart. 23If you return to the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, you will be restored; if you remove iniquity far from your tent, 24and lay your gold in the dust, and the gold of Ophir among the stones of the brooks, 25then the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty will be your gold, and you will have abundant silver. 26For then you will delight yourself in the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, and lift up your face to God. 27You will pray to HimhimHim, and HeheHe will hear you; and you will pay your vows. 28You will also decree a thing, and it will be established for you; and light will shine upon your ways. 29When men are brought low, you will say, ‘There is exaltation’; and HeheHe will save the one with downcast eyes. 30He will deliver even the guilty one; and he will be delivered by the purity of your hands.”
