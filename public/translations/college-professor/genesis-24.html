Abraham’s Commission to His Servant for Isaac’s Marriage

1Now Abraham had grown venerable and advanced in years, and the LORD had blessed him in every respect. 2Abraham said to the eldest servant of his household, the steward who governed all that he possessed, “Place your hand beneath my thigh, 3and I will make you swear by the LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven and the God of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites among whom I dwell; 4but you shall go to my country and my kindred, and take a wife for my son Isaac.”

5The servant said to him, “What if the woman is unwilling to follow me to this land? Should I then take your son back to the land from which you came?” 6Abraham said to him, “Take heed that you do not take my son back there. 7The LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, who took me from my father’s house and from the land of my birth, and who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, ‘To your seed I will give this land’—HeheHe will send HishisHis angel before you, and you shall take a wife for my son from there. 8If the woman is unwilling to follow you, then you shall be released from this oath; only do not take my son back there.” 9So the servant placed his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and swore to him concerning this matter.

The Servant’s Journey to Nahor’s City and His Prayer at the Well

10Then the servant took ten camels from his master’s camels and departed, carrying all manner of his master’s goods in his hand; he arose and journeyed to Aram-naharaim, to the city of Nahor. 11At evening, the time when the women go out to draw water, he made the camels kneel outside the city by the well. 12He said, “O the LORD, God of my master Abraham, please grant me success this day, and show steadfast love to my master Abraham.”

13“Behold, I stand by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water. 14Let it be that the young woman to whom I say, ‘Please tip your jar that I may drink,’ and who says, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels also’—let her be the one YouyouYou have appointed for YouryourYour servant Isaac. By this I shall know that YouyouYou have shown steadfast love to my master.”

15Before he had finished speaking, behold, Rebekah came out—the daughter of Bethuel, son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham’s brother Nahor—with her jar upon her shoulder. 16She was exceedingly beautiful in appearance, a virgin whom no man had known; she went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came up. 17The servant ran to meet her and said, “Please give me a little water from your jar.” 18She said, “Drink, my lord,” and she hurried and lowered her jar upon her hand and gave him a drink. 19When she had finished giving him a drink, she said, “I will draw water for your camels also, until they have finished drinking.” 20She hurried, emptied her jar into the trough, ran again to the well to draw, and drew for all his camels. 21Meanwhile the man gazed upon her in silence, seeking to discern whether the LORD had prospered his journey or not.

22When the camels had finished drinking, the man took a gold nose ring weighing a beka, and two gold bracelets weighing ten shekels, and affixed them to her. 23He said, “Whose daughter are you? Tell me, please. Is there room in your father’s house for us to lodge?” 24She said to him, “I am the daughter of Bethuel, son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor.” 25She also said to him, “We have both straw and fodder in abundance, and room to lodge.” 26Then the man bowed and worshiped the LORD. 27He said, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not withdrawn HishisHis steadfast love and HishisHis faithfulness from my master. As for me, the LORD has led me in the way to the house of my master’s kinsmen.”

Rebekah’s Report and the Servant’s Audience with Her Household

28The young woman ran and told her mother’s household these things. 29Now Rebekah had a brother named Laban, and Laban ran outside to the man at the spring. 30When he saw the ring and the bracelets on his sister’s hands, and heard the words of Rebekah his sister, saying, “Thus spoke the man to me,” he came to the man; and behold, he was standing by the camels at the spring. 31He said, “Come in, you who are blessed of the LORD. Why do you stand outside? I have prepared the house, and there is a place for the camels.” 32So the man came into the house; he unloaded the camels and gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water to wash the feet of him and the men who were with him. 33Food was set before him, but he said, “I will not eat until I have spoken my words.” They said, “Speak.”

34He said, “I am Abraham’s servant. 35The LORD has greatly blessed my master, and he has become great; HeheHe has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, servants and maidservants, camels and donkeys. 36Sarah my master’s wife bore a son to my master in her old age, and he has given him all that he has. 37My master made me swear, saying, ‘You shall not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites in whose land I dwell; 38but you shall go to my father’s house and to my kindred, and take a wife for my son.’

39“I said to my master, ‘What if the woman will not follow me?’ 40He said to me, ‘The LORD, before whom I have walked, will send HishisHis angel with you and prosper your way; you shall take a wife for my son from my kindred, from my father’s house. 41Then you will be free from my oath: if, when you come to my kindred, they do not give her to you, you will be free from my oath.’

42“So I came today to the spring, and I said, ‘O the LORD, God of my master Abraham, if now YouyouYou are prospering my way on which I am going— 43behold, I stand by the spring of water—let it be that the young woman who comes out to draw, to whom I say, “Please give me a little water from your jar,” 44and who says to me, “Drink, and I will draw for your camels also”—let her be the woman whom the LORD has appointed for my master’s son.’ 45Before I had finished speaking in my heart, behold, Rebekah came out with her jar upon her shoulder; she went down to the spring and drew. I said to her, ‘Please give me a drink.’ 46She hurried and lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels also.’ So I drank, and she watered the camels also. 47Then I asked her, ‘Whose daughter are you?’ She said, ‘The daughter of Bethuel, son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.’ So I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her arms. 48Then I bowed and worshiped the LORD, and blessed the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who led me in the way of truth to take the daughter of my master’s brother for his son. 49Now if you will deal kindly and truly with my master, tell me; and if not, tell me, that I may turn to the right hand or to the left.”

50Laban and Bethuel answered, “The thing has come from the LORD; we cannot speak to you either evil or good. 51Behold, Rebekah is before you; take her and go, and let her be the wife of your master’s son, as the LORD has spoken.” 52When Abraham’s servant heard their words, he bowed to the ground before the LORD. 53The servant brought out silver and gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry, and garments, and gave them to Rebekah; he also gave precious things to her brother and to her mother.

Rebekah’s Departure and Isaac’s Reception of His Bride

54He and the men who were with him ate and drank, and lodged there. They rose in the morning, and he said, “Send me away to my master.” 55Her brother and her mother said, “Let the young woman stay with us a few days, at least ten; after that she may go.” 56He said to them, “Do not delay me, since the LORD has prospered my way; send me away, that I may go to my master.” 57They said, “We will call the young woman and ask her.” 58They called Rebekah and said to her, “Will you go with this man?” She said, “I will go.”

59So they sent away Rebekah their sister, and her nurse, together with Abraham’s servant and his men. 60They blessed Rebekah and said to her, “Our sister, may you become thousands of ten thousands, and may your seed possess the gate of those who hate them.” 61Then Rebekah and her young women arose, mounted the camels, and followed the man. The servant took Rebekah and departed.

62Now Isaac had come from the way to Beer-lahai-roi, and he was dwelling in the land of the Negev. 63Isaac went out to meditate in the field toward evening; he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold, camels were coming. 64Rebekah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Isaac, she dismounted from the camel. 65She said to the servant, “Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?” The servant said, “He is my master.” So she took her veil and covered herself. 66The servant told Isaac all the things that he had done. 67Then Isaac brought her into the tent of Sarah his mother; he took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. Thus Isaac was comforted after his mother’s death.
