The Floating Axehead and the Prophetic Community

1The sons of the prophets said to Elisha, "ThetheThe quarters in which we reside under your tutelage have grown excessively cramped."

2"Let us go, I pray, to the Jordan; there let each man take a beam, that we may construct for ourselves a place in which to dwell."

And Elisha said, "Go."

3Then one of them said, "Please consent to accompany your servants."

And he said, "I will go."

4So he went with them. They came to the Jordan and felled trees.

5As one of them was felling a beam, the iron axehead fell into the water, and he cried out, "Alas, my lord! It was borrowed."

6The man of God asked, "Where did it fall?" When he showed him the place, he hewed a stick, cast it there, and the iron floated.

7Then he said, "Lift it out for yourself." So he reached out his hand and took it.

Elisha and the Aramean Intrigues

8Now the king of Aram was at war with Israel. He took counsel with his servants, saying, "MymyMy encampment shall be at such-and-such a place."

9But the man of God sent word to the king of Israel, "Beware lest you pass by this place, for the Arameans are descending there."

10The king of Israel therefore sent to the place of which the man of God had warned him; having been forewarned, he kept vigilant there, not merely once or twice.

11The king of Aram was enraged over this, and he summoned his servants and demanded, "WillwillWill you not disclose to me which among us is in league with the king of Israel?"

12One of his servants replied, "No, my lord the king; but Elisha, the prophet who is in Israel, tells the king of Israel the very words you speak in your bedchamber."

13He said, "Go and see where he is, that I may send and seize him."

And they reported, "Behold, he is in Dothan."

14So he sent horses and chariots and a substantial force. They came by night and surrounded the city.

15When the servant of the man of God rose early and went out, behold, an army with horses and chariots was surrounding the city. His servant said, "Alas, my master! What shall we do?"

16He said, "Do not be afraid, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them."

17Then Elisha prayed, "ThetheThe LORD, I pray, open his eyes that he may see." And the LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw; and behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

18As they came down to him, Elisha prayed to the LORD, "Strike this people with blindness, I pray." And the LORD struck them with blindness according to the word of Elisha.

19Then Elisha said to them, "This is not the way, and this is not the city; follow me, and I will lead you to the man whom you seek." And he led them to Samaria.

20When they had entered Samaria, Elisha said, "ThetheThe LORD, open the eyes of these, that they may see." And the LORD opened their eyes, and they saw; and behold, they were in the midst of Samaria.

21When the king of Israel saw them, he said to Elisha, "MymyMy father, shall I strike them down? Shall I strike them down?"

22He answered, "YouyouYou shall not strike them. Would you strike those whom you have taken captive with your sword and your bow? Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink and return to their master."

23So he prepared a great feast for them; and after they had eaten and drunk, he sent them away, and they went to their master. And the raiding bands of Aram no longer came into the land of Israel.

The Siege of Samaria and the King's Despair

24Afterward Ben-hadad king of Aram gathered all his army and went up and besieged Samaria.

25There was a great famine in Samaria; and behold, they besieged it until a donkey's head was sold for eighty shekels of silver, and a quarter of a kab of dove's dung for five shekels.

26As the king of Israel was passing by upon the wall, a woman cried out to him, "Save me, my lord the king!"

27He said, "If the LORD does not save you, how can I save you? From the threshing floor or from the winepress?"

28And the king said to her, "What troubles you?" She answered, "This woman said to me, 'Give your son, that we may eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.

29"So we boiled my son and ate him. And I said to her the next day, 'Give your son, that we may eat him,' but she hid her son."

30When the king heard the woman's words, he tore his garments as he passed along upon the wall; and the people saw that he wore sackcloth beneath upon his flesh.

31Then he said, "May God do so to me, and more also, if the head of Elisha the son of Shaphat remains on him today."

32Now Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him. He sent a man from before him, but before the messenger came to him, he said to the elders, "Do you see how this son of a murderer has sent to take away my head? Look, when the messenger comes, shut the door and hold him fast at the door. Is not the sound of his master's feet behind him?"

33While he was still speaking with them, behold, the messenger came down to him, and he said, "Behold, this evil is from the LORD; why should I wait for the LORD any longer?"
