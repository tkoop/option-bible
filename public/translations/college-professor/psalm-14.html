The Folly of the Godless and the LORD’s Refuge

1The benighted fool declares within his heart, “There is no God.” They have corrupted themselves and perpetrated abominations; not one among them practices goodness. 2From Heavenheavenheaven the LORD surveys the children of Adam to discern whether any possess understanding, whether any seek after God.

3They have all turned aside and become uniformly corrupt; there is none who does good—not even one. 4Do all these workers of iniquity lack understanding? They devour my people as if consuming bread, and they do not invoke the LORD.

5There they are, seized with overwhelming dread, for God dwells among the generation of the righteous. 6You evildoers would put the counsel of the poor to shame, yet the LORD is their refuge. 7O that Israel’s salvation might issue forth from Zion! When the LORD restores the fortunes of HishisHis people, let Jacob rejoice and Israel be glad.
