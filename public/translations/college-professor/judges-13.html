Israel's Sin and Subjugation to the Philistines

1The Israelites once more perpetrated evil in the sight of the the LORD, so the the LORD delivered them into the Philistines' power for forty years.

The Angelic Announcement of Samson's Birth

2A certain man of Zorah, belonging to the Danite clan and named Manoah, had a wife who was barren and had borne no children. 3The angel of the the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, “Behold, you are barren and have borne no children; yet you shall conceive and bear a son. 4Therefore, please guard yourself: do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean. 5For behold, you shall conceive and bear a son, and no razor shall come upon his head, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb; and he shall begin to deliver Israel from the hand of the Philistines.”

6The woman went and told her husband, “A man of God came to me; his appearance was like the appearance of an angel of God—remarkably awe-inspiring. I did not ask where he came from, and he did not tell me his name. 7He said to me, ‘Behold, you shall conceive and bear a son. Now drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb until the day of his death.’”

Manoah's Prayer and the Angel's Return

8Then Manoah prayed to the the LORD and said, “O Lord, please let the man of God whom YouyouYou sent come to us again and teach us what we are to do for the child who is to be born.”

9And God listened to Manoah's voice, and the angel of God came again to the woman while she sat in the field; but her husband Manoah was not with her. 10The woman hurried and ran and told her husband, “Behold, the man who came to me the other day has appeared to me.”

11Manoah rose and followed his wife, came to the man, and said to him, “Are you the man who spoke to the woman?”

He answered, “I am.”

12Manoah said, “Now may your words be fulfilled. How shall the boy be reared, and what shall be his vocation?”

13The angel of the the LORD said to Manoah, “Of all that I said to the woman, let her heed it. 14She must not eat anything that comes from the vine, neither drink wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean; she must observe all that I commanded her.”

The Offering, the Angel's Ascent, and Samson's Birth

15Manoah said to the angel of the the LORD, “Please let us detain you, and we will prepare a young goat for you.”

16The angel of the the LORD said to Manoah, “Even if you detain me, I will not eat your bread; but if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the the LORD.” For Manoah did not know that he was the angel of the the LORD.

17Manoah said to the angel of the the LORD, “What is your name, so that when your word is fulfilled we may honourhonourhonor you?”

18The angel of the the LORD said to him, “Why do you ask my name, seeing that it is wonderful?”

19So Manoah took a young goat with a grain offering and offered it on the rock to the the LORD, and the angel acted wondrously while Manoah and his wife looked on. 20As the flame rose heavenward from the altar, the angel of the the LORD ascended in the altar's flame. Manoah and his wife looked on and fell prostrate upon the ground.

21The angel of the the LORD did not appear again to Manoah or his wife. Then Manoah knew that he had been the angel of the the LORD.

22Manoah said to his wife, “We shall surely die, for we have seen God.”

23His wife said to him, “If the the LORD had desired to kill us, HeheHe would not have accepted a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hands, nor would HeheHe have shown us all these things, nor would HeheHe have told us such things at this time.”

24The woman bore a son and named him Samson; and the child grew, and the the LORD blessed him. 25The SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD began to stir him in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.
