A Paternal Exhortation to Acquire Wisdom and Understanding

1Hear, O sons, a father’s instruction, and attend that you may know understanding; 2for I have given you sound doctrine—do not forsake my teaching. 3For I was a son to my father, tender and uniquely cherished before my mother; 4and he taught me and said to me, “Let your heart retain my words; keep my commandments, and live.” 5Acquire wisdom; acquire understanding. Do not forget, nor turn aside from the words of my mouth. 6Do not forsake her, and she will preserve you; love her, and she will keep you. 7Wisdom is the principal thing: acquire wisdom, and with all your acquisitions, acquire understanding. 8Prize her, and she will exalt you; she will honourhonourhonor you when you embrace her. 9She will give to your head a garland of grace; a crown of glory she will deliver to you.

The Divergent Trajectories of the Righteous and the Wicked

10Hear, my son, and receive my sayings, and the years of your life will be multiplied to you. 11I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have led you in the paths of uprightness. 12When you walk, your steps will not be constrained, and if you run, you will not stumble. 13Hold fast to instruction; do not let it go; keep it, for it is your life. 14Do not enter the path of the wicked, and do not go in the way of evil men. 15Avoid it; do not pass by it; turn from it and pass on. 16For they cannot sleep unless they do evil, and their sleep is taken from them unless they cause someone to stumble. 17For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence. 18But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, shining ever brighter unto the perfect day. 19The way of the wicked is like darkness; they do not know at what they stumble.

The Custody of the Heart and the Discipline of Conduct

20My son, attend to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. 21Let them not depart from your eyes; keep them in the midst of your heart, 22for they are life to those who find them, and healing to all their flesh. 23Keep your heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. 24Put away from you perverseness of mouth, and put perverse lips far from you. 25Let your eyes look straight ahead, and let your eyelids look directly before you. 26Ponder the path of your feet, and all your ways will be established. 27Do not turn to the right or to the left; remove your foot from evil.
