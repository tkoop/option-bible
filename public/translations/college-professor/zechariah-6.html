The Vision of the Four Chariots

1I turned again, lifted my eyes, and saw: behold, four chariots were issuing forth from between two mountains, and those mountains were mountains of bronze. 2The first chariot was drawn by red horses, the second by black horses; 3the third by white horses, and the fourth by dappled, vigorous horses. 4And I answered and said to the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”

5And the angel answered and said to me, “These are the four spirits of Heavenheavenheaven, going out from their station before the Lord of all the earth. 6The black horses in it go out to the land of the north, and the white go out after them, and the dappled go out to the land of the south. 7And the vigorous horses went out and sought to go and patrol the earth; and he said, ‘Go, patrol the earth.’ So they patrolled the earth. 8And he cried out to me and spoke, saying, ‘Behold, those who go out to the land of the north have set MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit at rest in the land of the north.’”

The Crowning of Joshua the High Priest

9The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 10“Take silver and gold from the exiles—from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon—and you yourself shall go, on that very day, into the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. 11And you shall take the silver and gold, make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.
