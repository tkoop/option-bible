Paul's Astonishment at the Galatians' Bewitchment

1O unreflective Galatians, who has bewitched you, before whose very eyes Jesus Christ was publicly placarded as crucified? 2I would learn this alone from you: did you receive the SpiritspiritSpirit from works of the LawlawLaw, or from hearing with faith? 3Are you so devoid of understanding? Having commenced in the SpiritspiritSpirit, are you now being brought to completion in the flesh? 4Have you suffered so much to no purpose—if, indeed, it was to no purpose? 5Does HeheHe, then, who lavishly furnishes the SpiritspiritSpirit to you and effects mighty works among you, do so from works of the LawlawLaw, or from hearing with faith?

Abraham's Faith and the Blessing of the Nations

6Just as Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness, 7know, then, that those who are of faith are sons of Abraham. 8And the ScripturescriptureScripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, proclaimed the gospel in advance to Abraham: "In you shall all the nations be blessed." 9So then those who are of faith are blessed together with Abraham, the man of faith.

The Curse of the Law and Redemption in Christ

10For as many as are of works of the LawlawLaw are under a curse; for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all things written in the book of the LawlawLaw, so as to perform them." 11Now it is evident that no one is justified before God by the LawlawLaw, for "ThetheThe righteous shall live by faith." 12The LawlawLaw is not of faith; rather, "ThetheThe one who does them shall live by them." 13Christ redeemed us from the curse of the LawlawLaw, having become a curse for us—for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree"— 14so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promised SpiritspiritSpirit through faith.

The Law's Place in the Divine Economy of Promise

15Brothers, I put this in human terms: even a covenant ratified by a man no one voids or amends. 16Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his Seed. He does not say, "And to seeds," as referring to many, but "And to your Seed," as referring to one, who is Christ. 17What I am saying is this: the LawlawLaw, which came four hundred and thirty years later, does not invalidate a covenant previously ratified by God, so as to nullify the promise. 18For if the inheritance is by the LawlawLaw, it is no longer by promise; but God graciously granted it to Abraham by promise.

The Law as Guardian until Faith's Revelation

19Why then the LawlawLaw? It was added because of transgressions, until the Seed should come to whom the promise had been made; and it was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator. 20Now a mediator is not for a single party, but God is one. 21Is the LawlawLaw, then, contrary to the promises of God? By no means! For if a law had been given that could impart life, then righteousness would indeed be by the LawlawLaw. 22But the ScripturescriptureScripture imprisoned all things under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe. 23Now before faith came, we were held in custody under the LawlawLaw, being shut up unto the faith that was to be revealed. 24So then the LawlawLaw has become our guardian to lead us to Christ, so that we might be justified by faith. 25But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.

26For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus. 27For as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ. 28There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus. 29And if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs according to promise.
