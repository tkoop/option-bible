A Summons to Seek Rain from the LORD

1Petition the LORD for rain in the season of the latter rain; the LORD, who fashions lightning flashes, will grant them showers, and to each man grass in the field.

The LORD's Indignation Against False Shepherds

2For the idols have uttered iniquity, and the diviners have envisioned falsehood; they have spoken empty words, and their dreams proffer only vain consolation; therefore the people wander like sheep, oppressed because there is no shepherd.

3My wrath burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the he-goats; for the LORD of Hosts has visited HishisHis flock, the house of Judah, and will render them like HishisHis royal steed in battle.

4From Judah shall come the cornerstone, the tent peg, the battle bow, and every ruler together.

5They shall be like warriors trampling the mire of the streets in battle; they shall fight because the LORD is with them, and they shall put the horsemen to shame.

The Restoration of Judah and Ephraim

6I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph; I will restore them, for I have pitied them, and they shall be as though I had never rejected them, for I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

7Ephraim shall be like a mighty man, and their heart shall rejoice as with wine; their children shall see and rejoice, and their heart shall exult in the LORD.

8I will whistle for them and gather them, for I have redeemed them; and they shall increase as they once increased.

9I will sow them among the peoples, yet they will remember MemeMe in distant lands; they and their children shall live and return.

10I will bring them back from the land of Egypt and gather them from Assyria; I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon, until no more room can be found for them.

The LORD's Triumph Over Oppressing Nations

11They will pass through the sea of distress and strike the waves of the sea, so that all the depths of the Nile will dry up; the pride of Assyria will be brought down, and the sceptresceptrescepter of Egypt will depart.

12I will strengthen them in the the LORD, and in HishisHis name they will walk, declares the the LORD.
