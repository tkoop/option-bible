Job Affirms God's Incomparable Power and Wisdom

2"Indeed, I know that it is so; yet how can a mortal be vindicated before God? 3If he should wish to contend with HimhimHim, he could not answer HimhimHim once in a thousand times. 4Wise in heart and mighty in strength is HeheHe; who has hardened himself against HimhimHim and prospered?

Job's Anguish Over Divine Justice and Human Helplessness

22"It is all one; therefore I say, 'He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.' 23When the scourge strikes suddenly, HeheHe mocks the plight of the innocent. 24The EarthEarthearth is given into the hand of the wicked; HeheHe covers the faces of its judges. If it is not HeheHe, then who is it?

Job's Plea for an Arbiter and Relief from Terror
