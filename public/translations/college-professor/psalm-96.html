A New Song of Universal Praise

1Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. 2Sing to the LORD, bless HishisHis name; proclaim HishisHis salvation from day to day. 3Declare HishisHis glory among the nations, HishisHis wonders among all peoples.

The LORD's Incomparable Greatness

4For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; HeheHe is to be feared above all gods. 5For all the gods of the peoples are but idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 6SplendourSplendourSplendor and majesty are before HimhimHim; strength and beauty are in HishisHis sanctuary.

Homage from All the Peoples

7Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. 8Ascribe to the LORD the glory due HishisHis name; bring an offering and come into HishisHis courts. 9Worship the LORD in the splendoursplendoursplendor of holiness; tremble before HimhimHim, all the earth.

The LORD's Righteous Reign and Coming JudgmentJudgementJudgment

10Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns. The world is firmly established; it will not be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity.” 11Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad; let the sea resound, and all that fills it. 12Let the field exult, and all that is in it; then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy 13before the LORD, for HeheHe comes, for HeheHe comes to judge the earth.
