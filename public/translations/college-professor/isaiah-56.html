A Summons to Justice and Righteousness

1Thus says the the LORD: “Uphold justice and execute righteousness, for MymyMy salvation is imminent, and MymyMy righteousness is on the verge of disclosure.” 2Blessed is the man who performs this, and the son of man who cleaves to it—who safeguards the Sabbath from profanation and restrains his hand from perpetrating any evil.

The Incorporation of Foreigners and Eunuchs into the Covenant

3Let not the foreigner who has affiliated himself with the the LORD say, “The the LORD will assuredly alienate me from HishisHis people”; nor let the eunuch say, “Behold, I am barren and bereft of posterity.”

4For thus says the the LORD: “To the eunuchs who preserve MymyMy Sabbaths, who elect what pleases MemeMe, and who tenaciously uphold MymyMy covenant— 5I shall bestow upon them, within MymyMy house and within MymyMy walls, a memorial and a name surpassing sons and daughters; I shall confer upon them an everlasting name that shall never be excised.

“And to the foreigners who cleave to the the LORD in order to minister to HimhimHim, to love the name of the the LORD, and to become HishisHis servants—everyone who guards the Sabbath from desecration and clings to MymyMy covenant— 67I shall usher them into MymyMy holy mountain and gladden them in MymyMy house of prayer; their burnt offerings and sacrifices shall find acceptance upon MymyMy altar, for MymyMy house shall be denominated a house of prayer for all peoples.”

The Lord GOD’s Further Gathering of Israel

8Declares the Lord GOD, who gathers the dispersed of Israel: “I shall yet gather to him others besides those already assembled.”

A Summons to Devour and a Condemnation of Blind Shepherds

9Come, all you beasts of the field; come, all you beasts of the forest, to devour.

10Israel’s sentinels are blind; they are all devoid of knowledge. They are all mute hounds, incapable of barking; they are dreamers reclining, enamored of slumber. 11Moreover, the hounds are ravenous; they know no satiety. They are shepherds devoid of discernment; they have all veered to their own course, each in pursuit of his own lucre.

12They cry, “Come, let us procure wine; let us intoxicate ourselves with strong drink! Tomorrow will resemble today, only in far greater measure.”
