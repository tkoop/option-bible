A Vision of Joshua the High Priest before the Angel of the LORD

1Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the LORD, with Satan stationed at his right hand to indict him. 2The LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan! Indeed, the LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you. Is this not a brand snatched from the fire?”

The Soiled Garments and the Cleansing of the Priest

3Now Joshua was arrayed in soiled garments while standing before the Angel.

4So HeheHe answered and said to those standing before HimhimHim, “Remove the soiled garments from him.” Then HeheHe said to him, “See, I have taken away your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you in sumptuous apparel.”

5Then I said, “Let them place a pure turban upon his head.” So they placed a pure turban upon his head and clothed him, while the Angel of the LORD stood by.
