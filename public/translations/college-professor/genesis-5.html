The Generations of Adam

1This is the book of the generations of Adam: on the day when God created humankind, HeheHe fashioned him in the likeness of God; 2male and female HeheHe created them, HeheHe blessed them, and HeheHe called their name Adam in the day of their creation.

The Patriarchal Succession Before the Deluge

3Adam lived one hundred and thirty years, and he begot a son in his own likeness, after his image, and he called his name Seth. 4After he begot Seth, the days of Adam were eight hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters. 5Thus all the days of Adam amounted to nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.

6Seth lived one hundred and five years, and he begot Enosh. 7After he begot Enosh, Seth lived eight hundred and seven years, and he begot sons and daughters. 8Thus all the days of Seth amounted to nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.

9Enosh lived ninety years, and he begot Kenan. 10After he begot Kenan, Enosh lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and he begot sons and daughters. 11Thus all the days of Enosh amounted to nine hundred and five years, and he died.

12Kenan lived seventy years, and he begot Mahalalel. 13After he begot Mahalalel, Kenan lived eight hundred and forty years, and he begot sons and daughters. 14Thus all the days of Kenan amounted to nine hundred and ten years, and he died.

15Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, and he begot Jared. 16After he begot Jared, Mahalalel lived eight hundred and thirty years, and he begot sons and daughters. 17Thus all the days of Mahalalel amounted to eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.

18Jared lived one hundred and sixty-two years, and he begot Enoch. 19After he begot Enoch, Jared lived eight hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters. 20Thus all the days of Jared amounted to nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.

The Translation of Enoch

21Enoch lived sixty-five years, and he begot Methuselah. 22After he begot Methuselah, Enoch walked with God three hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters. 23Thus all the days of Enoch amounted to three hundred and sixty-five years. 24Enoch walked with God, and he was no more, for God took him.

25Methuselah lived one hundred and eighty-seven years, and he begot Lamech. 26After he begot Lamech, Methuselah lived seven hundred and eighty-two years, and he begot sons and daughters. 27Thus all the days of Methuselah amounted to nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.

The Birth of Noah

28Lamech lived one hundred and eighty-two years, and he begot a son. 29He called his name Noah, saying, “This one shall comfort us concerning our work and the toil of our hands, because of the ground which the LORD has cursed.”

30After he begot Noah, Lamech lived five hundred and ninety-five years, and he begot sons and daughters. 31Thus all the days of Lamech amounted to seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died.

32Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah begot Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
