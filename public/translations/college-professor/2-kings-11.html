Athaliah's Usurpation and the Preservation of the Royal Heir

1When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, perceived that her son had perished, she arose and annihilated every scion of the royal house. 2Yet Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash, the son of Ahaziah, and surreptitiously removed him from among the king's sons who were being put to death; with his nurse she hid him in the bedchamber, concealing him from Athaliah so that he was not slain. 3He remained hidden with her in the house of the LORD for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

Jehoiada's Conspiracy and the Anointing of the Boy King

4In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent and took the captains of the hundreds of the Carites and the runners; he brought them to himself in the house of the LORD, and there, within the house of the LORD, he made a covenant with them, bound them by oath, and showed them the king. 5He commanded them, saying, "This is the thing that you shall do: one third of you, those coming on the Sabbath, shall keep watch over the king's house; 6another third shall be at the gate of Sur, and another third at the gate behind the runners; you shall keep watch over the house. 7Your two divisions that go out on the Sabbath shall keep watch over the house of the LORD for the king. 8You shall surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand; whoever approaches the ranks shall be put to death. Be with the king in his going out and in his coming in."

9The captains of the hundreds did all that Jehoiada the priest commanded; each took his men, those coming on the Sabbath together with those going off on the Sabbath, and they came to Jehoiada the priest. 10The priest gave to the captains of the hundreds the spears and shields of King David that were in the house of the LORD. 11The runners stood, each man with his weapons in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and by the house, surrounding the king. 12Then he brought out the king's son, set the crown upon him, and gave him the testimony; they made him king, anointed him, clapped their hands, and said, "Long live the king!"

Athaliah's Downfall and the Priest's Command

13When Athaliah heard the sound of the runners and of the people, she came to the people in the house of the LORD. 14She looked, and there stood the king by the pillar according to custom, with the captains and the trumpeters beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets; then Athaliah tore her garments and cried, "Treason! Treason!" 15Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of the hundreds, the commanders of the host, and said to them, "BringbringBring her out between the ranks; whoever follows her, kill with the sword," for the priest had said, "She shall not be put to death in the house of the LORD." 16So they laid hands on her, and she went by the way of the horses' entrance to the king's house, and there she was put to death.

Renewal of the Covenant and the Purge of Baal Worship

17Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they should be the LORD's people; and between the king and the people. 18All the people of the land went to the house of Baal and tore it down; they thoroughly broke its altars and its images in pieces; they killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. The priest appointed officers over the house of the LORD. 19He took the captains of the hundreds, the Carites, the runners, and all the people of the land; they brought the king down from the house of the LORD and came by the way of the gate of the runners to the king's house. He sat on the throne of the kings. 20All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; Athaliah they put to death with the sword at the king's house. 21Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.
