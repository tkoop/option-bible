Laws Concerning Theft and Restitution

1Should a man purloin an ox or a sheep and thereafter either slaughter or vend it, he must recompense five head of cattle for the ox and four sheep for the sheep. 2If a thief is apprehended in the act of breaking in and is struck down so that he dies, no bloodguilt attaches to the one who struck him. 3Yet if the sun has risen upon him, bloodguilt attaches to the slayer; the thief must make full restitution, and should he possess nothing, he himself shall be sold to answer for his theft. 4If the stolen property is discovered alive in his possession—whether ox, donkey, or sheep—he shall restore twofold.

Laws on Property Damage and Safekeeping

5If a man permits his livestock to graze a field or vineyard and then lets them stray so that they feed in another's field, he must make restitution from the choicest produce of his own field and vineyard. 6If a fire breaks out and spreads among thorns, consuming sheaves, standing grain, or an entire field, the one who kindled it must make full restitution.

7If a man entrusts silver or goods to his neighbourneighbourneighbor for safekeeping, and they are stolen from that neighbourneighbourneighbor's house, the thief, if apprehended, shall repay twofold. 8If the thief is not found, the owner of the house shall be brought before the judges, that it may be determined whether he has laid hands upon his neighbourneighbourneighbor's goods. 9In every case of breach of trust—whether concerning an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or any lost article about which someone asserts, "This is it"—both parties shall bring their dispute before the judges; the one whom the judges condemn must pay double to his neighbourneighbourneighbor.

Laws on Borrowed and Entrusted Animals

10If a man entrusts to his neighbourneighbourneighbor a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any animal for safekeeping, and it dies, is injured, or is carried off, with no witness to the event, 11then an oath by the LORD shall stand between them both, attesting that he has not misappropriated his neighbourneighbourneighbor's property; the owner shall accept that oath, and no restitution shall be exacted. 12But if it was stolen from his keeping, he must make restitution to the owner. 13If it was torn by beasts, he shall produce the carcass as evidence and shall not make restitution for the torn animal.

14If a man borrows from his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and the thing is injured or dies while its owner is not present, he must make full restitution. 15If the owner is present, no restitution is required; and if the animal was hired, its hire covers the loss.

Moral, Social, and Ceremonial Laws

16If a man seduces a virgin who is not betrothed and lies with her, he must pay her bride-price and take her as his wife. 17If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, he shall pay silver equal to the bride-price of virgins. 18You shall not permit a sorceress to live. 19Whoever has sexual relations with an animal must surely be put to death. 20Whoever sacrifices to any god other than the LORD alone shall be devoted to destruction.

21You shall not wrong a sojourner, nor shall you oppress him, for you yourselves were sojourners in the land of Egypt. 22You shall not afflict any widow or orphan. 23If you do afflict them, and they cry out to MemeMe, I will surely hear their cry. 24Then MymyMy wrath will burn, and I will slay you with the sword, so that your wives become widows and your children fatherless.

25If you lend money to any of MymyMy people among you who is poor, you shall not be to him as a creditor, nor shall you charge him interest. 26If you ever take your neighbourneighbourneighbor's garment in pledge, you shall return it to him before the sun goes down. 27For that is his only covering; it is his cloak for his body. In what, then, shall he sleep? And if he cries out to MemeMe, I will hear, for I am gracious.

28You shall not revile God, nor curse a ruler of your people. 29You shall not delay to offer from the fullness of your harvest and from the outflow of your presses; the firstborn of your sons you shall give to MemeMe. 30Likewise you shall do with your oxen and with your sheep: seven days it shall be with its mother; on the eighth day you shall give it to MemeMe. 31You shall be holy men to MemeMe; therefore you shall not eat any flesh torn by beasts in the field; you shall cast it to the dogs.
