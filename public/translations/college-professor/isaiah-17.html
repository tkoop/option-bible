Oracle Against Damascus and the Humbling of Jacob

1An oracle concerning Damascus: Behold, Damascus shall be divested of its civic standing and reduced to a heap of ruins. 2The cities of Aroer shall be abandoned, becoming sheepfolds where flocks repose untroubled by any terror. 3The fortress shall vanish from Ephraim, and sovereignty from Damascus, together with the remnant of Aram; they shall become like the glory of the children of Israel, declares the LORD of Hosts. 4In that day the glory of Jacob shall be brought low, and the fatness of his flesh shall waste away. 5It shall be as when a reaper gathers the standing grain and his arm harvests the ears; yes, as when one gleans ears in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim.

A Remnant and Renewed Regard for the Holy One

6Yet gleanings shall remain in it, as when an olive tree is beaten—two or three berries upon the summit of the uppermost bough, four or five upon its fruitful branches—declares the LORD, the God of Israel. 7In that day a person shall look to his MakermakerMaker, and his eyes shall regard the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel.

Abandoned Idolatry and Desolate Strongholds

8He shall not look to the altars, the work of his hands, nor regard what his fingers have fashioned—neither the Asherah poles nor the incense altars. 9In that day his fortified cities shall resemble the abandoned thickets and lofty summits that were forsaken before the children of Israel, and desolation shall prevail.

The Futility of Forsaking God and the Rebuke of Nations

10For you have forgotten the God of your salvation and have not remembered the RockrockRock of your stronghold; therefore you plant pleasant plants and sow the shoot of a stranger. 11On the day you plant it, it shall flourish, and in the morning your seed shall blossom; yet the harvest shall be a heap of ruin in the day of sickness and incurable pain. 12Woe to the multitude of many peoples, who roar like the roaring of the seas, and to the rushing of nations, which rush like the rushing of mighty waters! 13The nations shall rush like the rushing of many waters; but HeheHe shall rebuke them, and they shall flee far away, chased like chaff upon the mountains before the wind, and like a rolling thistle before a gale. 14At evening, behold, sudden terror; before morning, they are no more. This is the portion of our plunderers and the lot of our robbers.
