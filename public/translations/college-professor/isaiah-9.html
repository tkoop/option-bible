A Great Light for the Northern Provinces

1For there shall be no gloom for her who was in anguish: as in the former time HeheHe brought into contempt the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, so in the latter time HeheHe has glorified the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations. 2The people who walk in darkness have seen a great light; upon those dwelling in the land of deep darkness, a light has dawned.

3You have multiplied the nation, yet YouyouYou have not magnified its joy; they rejoice before YouyouYou as with the joy of harvest, as men exult when dividing plunder. 4For, as in the day of Midian’s defeat, YouyouYou have shattered the yoke that burdened them, the bar upon their shoulders, and the rod of their oppressor. 5For every boot that tramples in the tumult of battle, and every garment rolled in blood, shall be consigned to the fire as fuel.

The Royal Child and His Enduring Kingdom

6For to us a child is born, to us a son is given; and the government will be upon HishisHis shoulder, and HishisHis name shall be called Wonderful CounsellorCounsellorCounselor, MightymightyMighty God, EverlastingeverlastingEverlasting FatherfatherFather, Prince of Peace. 7Of the increase of HishisHis government and of peace there shall be no end; upon the throne of David and over his kingdom HeheHe shall reign, to establish and uphold it with justice and with righteousness from this time forth and forevermore. The zeal of the LORD of Hosts will accomplish this.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment upon Israel’s Pride

8The Lord has dispatched a word against Jacob, and it has fallen upon Israel.

9All the people shall know it—Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria—who in pride and in the arrogance of heart say:

10“The bricks have fallen, yet we shall rebuild with dressed stone; the sycamores have been felled, yet we shall replace them with cedars.”

11Therefore the LORD has raised up the adversaries of Rezin against him and incited his enemies. 12Arameans from the east and Philistines from the west have devoured Israel with open mouth. Yet for all this HishisHis anger is not turned away, and HishisHis hand is still upraised.

13But the people did not turn back to HimhimHim who struck them, nor did they seek the LORD of Hosts. 14Therefore the LORD will cut off from Israel head and tail, palm branch and reed, in a single day. 15The head is the elder and the honouredhonouredhonored man; the tail is the prophet who teaches falsehood. 16For the leaders of this people lead them astray, and those who are led by them are swallowed up.

17Therefore the Lord will take no delight in their young men, and will show no compassion to their fatherless and widows; for everyone is profane and an evildoer, and every mouth speaks folly. Yet for all this HishisHis anger is not turned away, and HishisHis hand is still upraised.

The Consuming Fire of Divine Wrath

18For wickedness burns like a fire consuming thorns and briers; it kindles the thickets of the forest, so that they roll upward in a column of smoke. 19By the wrath of the LORD of Hosts the land is scorched, and the people become fuel for the fire; no one spares his brother. 20They snatch on the right, yet are still hungry; they devour on the left, yet are not satisfied; each devours his own kindred. 21Manasseh devours Ephraim, and Ephraim Manasseh; together they turn against Judah. Yet for all this HishisHis anger is not turned away, and HishisHis hand is still upraised.
