An Imprecatory Psalm Against the Confederated Hostility of the Nations

1A song; a psalm of Asaph. O God, do not remain silent; do not be quiet, O God; do not be still. 2For behold, YouryourYour enemies are in tumult, and those who hate YouyouYou have grown arrogantly defiant. 3They scheme with cunning against YouryourYour people, and they conspire against YouryourYour treasured ones.

4They say, “Come, let us annihilate them as a nation, so that the name of Israel may be remembered no more.”

5For they have conspired with one accord; they have made a covenant against YouyouYou— 6the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, 7Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre. 8Assyria also has joined them; they have become the arm of the children of Lot. Selah.

9Do to them as to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin at the River Kishon— 10those who were destroyed at Endor, who became manure for the soil. 11Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, 12who said, “Let us seize for ourselves the pastures of God.”

13Make them like whirling dust, O my God, like chaff before the wind. 14As fire consumes a forest, as a flame sets the mountains ablaze, 15so pursue them with YouryourYour tempest and terrify them with YouryourYour storm.

16Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek YouryourYour name, O the LORD. 17Let them be put to shame and dismayed forever; let them perish in disgrace. 18Let them know that YouyouYou alone are the LORD, the MostmostMost HighhighHigh over all the earth.
