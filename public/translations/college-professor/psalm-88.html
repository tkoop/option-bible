A Profound Lament in the Shadow of Death

1A song, a psalm of the sons of Korah, for the director of music, according to Mahalath Leannoth; a Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. The LORD, God of my salvation, day and night I have cried out before YouyouYou. 2May my prayer come before YouyouYou; incline YouryourYour ear to my supplication.

3For my soul is overwhelmed with troubles, and my life has drawn near to Sheol. 4I am reckoned among those who descend into the pit; I have become as a man bereft of strength. 5I am forsaken among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom YouyouYou remember no longer, cut off from YouryourYour care. 6You have laid me in the lowest pit, in dark places, in the depths. 7Your wrath presses hard upon me, and YouyouYou have afflicted me with all YouryourYour waves. Selah.

8You have removed my companions far from me; YouyouYou have made me an object of loathing to them; I am imprisoned and cannot go forth. 9My eye wastes away from affliction; every day I call upon YouyouYou, the LORD; I spread out my hands to YouyouYou. 10Will YouyouYou work wonders for the dead? Will the shades arise to praise YouyouYou? Selah. 11Is YouryourYour steadfast love recounted in the grave, YouryourYour faithfulness in Abaddon? 12Are YouryourYour wonders known in the darkness, YouryourYour righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

13But I cry out to YouyouYou, the LORD; in the morning my prayer comes before YouyouYou. 14Why, the LORD, do YouyouYou reject my soul? Why do YouyouYou hide YouryourYour face from me? 15From my youth I have been afflicted and near death; I bear YouryourYour terrors; I am overwhelmed. 16Your burning anger has swept over me; YouryourYour terrors have destroyed me. 17They surround me like water all day long; they encompass me on every side. 18You have removed friend and companion far from me; darkness is my only companion.
