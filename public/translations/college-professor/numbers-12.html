Miriam and Aaron's Insurrection Against Moses' Authority

1Miriam and Aaron began to speak against Moses on account of the Cushite woman whom he had married—for he had indeed taken a Cushite wife. 3Now Moses was preeminently meek, surpassing every other human being upon the face of the earth.

2They said, "Has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Has HeheHe not spoken through us as well?" And the LORD heard it.

The LORD's Theophany and Rebuke of the Siblings

4Suddenly the LORD said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, "YouyouYou three come out to the tent of meeting." So the three of them went out. 5Then the LORD descended in a pillar of cloud, stood at the entrance of the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam; and the two of them came forward.

6He said, "Listen now to MymyMy words: If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, make MyselfmyselfMyself known to him in a vision; I speak with him in a dream. 7Not so with MymyMy servant Moses; he is faithful in all MymyMy house. 8With him I speak directly and plainly, not in riddles, and he beholds the form of the LORD. Why then were you not afraid to speak against MymyMy servant Moses?"

9The anger of the LORD was kindled against them, and HeheHe departed.

Miriam's Leprosy and Moses' Intercession

10When the cloud withdrew from above the tent, behold, Miriam was leprous, white as snow; and Aaron turned toward Miriam, and behold, she was leprous. 11Then Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not lay upon us the sin we have so foolishly committed. 12Do not let her be like a stillborn infant whose flesh is half consumed when it emerges from its mother's womb."

13So Moses cried out to the LORD, "O God, please heal her!"

14The LORD replied to Moses, "If her father had but spat in her face, would she not be disgraced for seven days? Let her be confined outside the camp for seven days, and afterward she may be brought back in."

Restoration and the Resumption of the Journey

15So Miriam was confined outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was brought back in. 16Afterward the people set out from Hazeroth and encamped in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Paran.
