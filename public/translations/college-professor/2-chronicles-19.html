Jehoshaphat’s Return and a Prophet’s Rebuke

1Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, returned in peace to his house in Jerusalem. 2Jehu son of Hanani, the seer, went out to meet him and said to King Jehoshaphat, “Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, the LORD’s wrath is upon you. 3Nevertheless, good things are found in you, for you have removed the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart to seek God.”

4Jehoshaphat dwelt in Jerusalem; then he went out again among the people from Beersheba to Mount Ephraim and brought them back to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

Judicial Appointments Throughout Judah

5He appointed judges in the land, in all the fortified cities of Judah.

6He said to the judges, “Consider what you are doing, for you do not judge for man but for the LORD, and HeheHe is with you in the matter of judgmentjudgementjudgment. 7Now let the fear of the LORD be upon you; be careful and do it, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice, no partiality, and no taking of bribes.”

The Jerusalem Tribunal and Its Charge

8In Jerusalem Jehoshaphat also appointed some of the Levites, priests, and heads of Israelite families to render the LORD’s judgmentjudgementjudgment and to decide disputes, and they returned to Jerusalem.

9He commanded them, “You shall do this in faithfulness and with a whole heart, in the fear of the LORD. 10In every case that comes to you from your brothers who dwell in their cities—whether it concerns bloodshed, law and commandment, statutes and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments—you shall warn them, so that they do not become guilty before the LORD and wrath come upon you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not be guilty. 11Behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in every matter of the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in every matter of the king; and the Levites are officers before you. Act with courage, and may the LORD be with those who do good.”
