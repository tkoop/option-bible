The LORD Commands Jeremiah to Proclaim JudgmentJudgementJudgment

1At the commencement of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this oracle issued from the LORD: 2“Thus says the LORD: Stand in the courtyard of the LORD’s house and speak to all the cities of Judah that come to worship there all the words that I have commanded you to speak; do not abridge a single word. 3Perhaps they will listen and turn—each from his evil way—so that I may relent of the calamity I intend to inflict upon them because of the wickedness of their deeds. 4And you shall declare to them, ‘Thus says the LORD: If you do not listen to MemeMe and walk in MymyMy law, which I have set before you, 5and if you do not heed the words of MymyMy servants the prophets, whom I send to you again and again—though you have not listened— 6then I will make this house like Shiloh and this city a byword of execration among all the nations of the earth.’”

Jeremiah Is Seized in the Temple Court

7Now the priests, prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah uttering these words in the house of the LORD.

8And as soon as Jeremiah had finished speaking all that the LORD had commanded him to speak to all the people, the priests, prophets, and all the people seized him, crying out, “You must surely die!” 9“Why have you prophesied in the name of the LORD, saying, ‘This house will become like Shiloh, and this city will be desolate, without inhabitant’?” And all the people thronged about Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.

10When the officials of Judah heard of these things, they ascended from the royal palace to the house of the LORD and took their seats at the entrance of the NewnewNew GategateGate of the LORD’s house.

11Then the priests and prophets addressed the officials and all the people: “This man deserves death, for he has prophesied against this city, as you yourselves have heard with your own ears!”

Jeremiah's DefenceDefenceDefense and the Elders' Appeal

12Then Jeremiah said to all the officials and all the people, “The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words you have heard. 13Therefore, amend your ways and your deeds, and obey the voice of the LORD your God, so that HeheHe may relent of the disaster HeheHe has pronounced against you. 14As for me, behold, I am in your hands; deal with me as seems good and right to you. 15But be assured that if you put me to death, you will bring innocent blood upon yourselves, upon this city, and upon its inhabitants; for in truth the LORD has sent me to speak all these words in your hearing.”

16Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and prophets, “This man is not deserving of death, for he has spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God!”

17Then certain of the elders of the land rose and addressed the entire assembly: 18“Micah of Moresheth prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah and declared to all the people of Judah, ‘This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Zion shall be plowed like a field, Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins, and the temple mount a forested ridge.’ 19Did Hezekiah king of Judah, or anyone in Judah, put him to death? The Fate of Uriah and Jeremiah's Deliverance

20There was also a man, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim, who prophesied in the name of the LORD and prophesied against this city and this land in terms identical to all the words of Jeremiah.

21When King Jehoiakim, together with all his warriors and officials, heard his words, the king sought to put him to death; but Uriah, upon hearing of it, was seized with fear and fled to Egypt. 22Then King Jehoiakim dispatched men to Egypt—Elnathan son of Achbor, along with certain other men— 23and they brought Uriah back from Egypt and delivered him to King Jehoiakim, who had him executed by the sword and cast his corpse into the burial ground of the common people.

24Nevertheless, Ahikam son of Shaphan supported Jeremiah, so that he was not surrendered to the people to be put to death.
