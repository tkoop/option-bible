The Siege and Fall of Nineveh

1The scatterer has pressed his assault against you; guard the rampart, watch the road, brace yourself for exertion, and summon every reserve of power. 2For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob as the majesty of Israel, though destroyers have laid them waste and ruined their vines. 3The shields of his mighty men are crimson; his valiant warriors are clad in scarlet. The chariots flash like fire on the day he arrays them, and the cypress spears are brandished. 4The chariots race through the streets and dash about the squares; they appear like torches and dart like lightning. 5He summons his nobles, and they stumble as they advance; they rush to its wall, and the protective shield is set in place. 6The river gates are thrown open, and the palace collapses. 7It is decreed: she is exiled and carried away, while her maidservants moan like doves, beating their breasts. 8Nineveh has been like a pool of water from her earliest days, but now they flee. “Stop! Stop!” they cry, yet no one turns back. 9Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! There is no end to the treasure, an abundance of every precious thing.

The Desolation of Nineveh

10She is emptied—yes, desolated and laid waste! Hearts melt, knees knock, bodies tremble, and every face grows pale. 11Where is the lions’ den, the feeding ground of the young lions, where lion and lioness prowled with their cubs, with nothing to make them afraid? 12The lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled prey for his lionesses; he filled his dens with prey and his lairs with torn carcasses.

The LORD’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment against Nineveh

13Behold, I am against you, declares the LORD of Hosts. I will burn your chariots in smoke; the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voices of your messengers will be heard no more.
