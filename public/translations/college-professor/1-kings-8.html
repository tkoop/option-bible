The Ark Brought into the Temple

1Then Solomon convoked the elders of Israel, together with all the heads of the tribes and the leaders of the ancestral houses of the Israelites, so that they might bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the CitycityCity of David, that is, Zion. 2All the men of Israel assembled to King Solomon at the festival in the month Ethanim—that is, the seventh month.

3When all the elders of Israel had arrived, the priests lifted the ark. 4They carried up the ark of the LORD, along with the tent of meeting and all the sacred utensils that were in the tent; the priests and the Levites brought them up. 5King Solomon and the entire congregation of Israel that had assembled before him stood with him before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen in such prodigious numbers that they could neither be counted nor enumerated.

6Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its appointed place, into the inner sanctuary of the house—to the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace—beneath the wings of the cherubim. 7For the cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark, so that the cherubim overshadowed the ark and its poles from above. 8The poles were of such length that their ends could be seen from the holy place before the inner sanctuary, though they were not visible from outside; and there they remain to this day. 9There was nothing in the ark save the two tablets of stone that Moses had placed there at Horeb, where the LORD covenanted with the Israelites when they departed from the land of Egypt.

10When the priests emerged from the holy place, the cloud filled the house of the LORD, 11so that the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud; for the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD.

Solomon's Blessing and Temple Dedication

12Then Solomon said, “The LORD has declared that HeheHe would dwell in thick darkness. 13I have indeed built YouyouYou an exalted house, a fixed abode for YouryourYour habitation forever.”

14Then the king turned about and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel stood. 15He said, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with HishisHis mouth to David my father and has fulfilled it with HishisHis hand, saying, 16‘Since the day that I brought MymyMy people Israel out of Egypt, I have not chosen any city among all the tribes of Israel in which to build a house for MymyMy name to be there; rather, I have chosen David to be over MymyMy people Israel.’

17“Now it was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 18But the LORD said to David my father, ‘Because it was in your heart to build a house for MymyMy name, you did well that it was in your heart.

19“Nevertheless, you shall not build the house; but your son, who shall come forth from your loins, he shall build the house for MymyMy name.’ 20Now the LORD has established HishisHis word that HeheHe spoke; for I have risen in place of David my father and sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. 21And there I have set a place for the ark, in which is the covenant of the LORD that HeheHe made with our fathers when HeheHe brought them out of the land of Egypt.”

Benediction, Sacrifices, and Dismissal

54Now when Solomon had finished offering all this prayer and supplication to the LORD, he arose from before the altar of the LORD, from kneeling on his knees with his hands spread out toward Heavenheavenheaven. 55He stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying, 56“Blessed be the LORD, who has given rest to HishisHis people Israel according to all that HeheHe promised; not one word has failed of all HishisHis good promise, which HeheHe spoke by the hand of Moses HishisHis servant.

57“The LORD our God be with us, as HeheHe was with our fathers; let HimhimHim not leave us nor forsake us, 58that HeheHe may incline our hearts to HimhimHim, to walk in all HishisHis ways and keep HishisHis commandments, HishisHis statutes, and HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments, which HeheHe commanded our fathers.

59“And may these words of mine, which I have made supplication before the LORD, be near to the LORD our God day and night, that HeheHe may maintain the cause of HishisHis servant and the cause of HishisHis people Israel, as each day requires, 60that all the peoples of the earth may know that the LORD, HeheHe is God; there is no other. 61Let your hearts therefore be wholly devoted to the LORD our God, to walk in HishisHis statutes and keep HishisHis commandments, as at this day.”

62Then the king and all Israel with him offered sacrifice before the LORD. 63Solomon offered as peace offerings to the LORD twenty-two thousand cattle and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep. So the king and all the children of Israel dedicated the house of the LORD. 64On that day the king consecrated the middle of the court that was before the house of the LORD, because there the bronze altar before the LORD was too small to contain the burnt offering and the grain offering and the fat portions of the peace offerings.

65At that time Solomon held the feast, and all Israel with him—a great assembly from Lebo-hamath to the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt—before the LORD our God, seven days and seven days, fourteen days in all. 66On the eighth day he sent the people away; and they blessed the king and went to their tents, joyful and glad of heart for all the goodness that the LORD had shown to HishisHis servant David and to HishisHis people Israel.
