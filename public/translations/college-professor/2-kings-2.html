Elijah’s Ascent and Elisha’s Commissioning

1When the the LORD was about to bear Elijah heavenward in a tempest, Elijah and Elisha departed from Gilgal.

2Elijah said to Elisha, “Remain here, I pray, for the the LORD has commissioned me as far as Bethel.”

But Elisha replied, “As the the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I shall not abandon you.” Thus they descended to Bethel.

3The sons of the prophets at Bethel came forth to Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that the the LORD will remove your master from above your head this day?”

He answered, “Indeed, I know; be still.”

4Elijah said to him, “Elisha, abide here, I pray, for the the LORD has sent me to Jericho.”

But he said, “As the the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not forsake you.” So they came to Jericho.

5The sons of the prophets at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that the the LORD will remove your master from above your head this day?”

He replied, “Yes, I know; be silent.”

6Elijah said to him, “Remain here, I pray, for the the LORD has dispatched me to the Jordan.”

But he answered, “As the the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you.” So the two of them continued onward.

7Fifty men from the company of prophets went and stood at a distance, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

8Elijah took his mantle, rolled it up, and struck the waters, so that they parted to this side and that, and the two crossed over on dry ground.

9When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, “Ask what I should do for you before I am taken from you.”

And Elisha said, “Please let a twofold portion of your spirit be upon me.”

10He said, “You have asked a hard thing. Nevertheless, if you see me when I am taken from you, it shall be so for you; but if not, it shall not be.”

11As they walked and conversed, behold, a fiery chariot and fiery horses separated them, and Elijah ascended in the whirlwind to Heavenheavenheaven.

12Elisha saw it and cried out, “My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!”

He saw him no more; then he seized his garments and tore them in two.

13He also took up Elijah’s mantle that had fallen from him, returned, and stood by the bank of the Jordan.

14He took the mantle of Elijah that had fallen from him and struck the waters, saying, “Where is the the LORD, the God of Elijah?”

When he had struck the waters, they parted to this side and that, and Elisha crossed over.

Elisha’s Vindication and the Healing of Jericho’s Waters

15When the prophetic disciples at Jericho saw him from afar, they said, “The spirit of Elijah rests upon Elisha.”

They came to meet him and prostrated themselves to the ground before him.

16They said to him, “Behold, your servants have fifty able men; let them go, we pray, and search for your master, lest the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD has carried him away and cast him upon some mountain or into some valley.”

He said, “You shall not send.”

17But they pressed him until he was ashamed, and he said, “Send them.”

So they sent fifty men, who searched for three days but did not find him.

18When they returned to him while he was residing at Jericho, he said to them, “Did I not say to you, ‘Do not go’?”

19Then the men of the city said to Elisha, “Behold, the site of this city is pleasant, as my lord sees; but the water is bad, and the land is barren.”

20He said, “Bring me a new flask and put salt in it.” So they brought it to him.

21Then he went out to the spring of the waters and threw salt there, and said, “Thus says the the LORD, ‘I have healed these waters; from there shall be no more death or barrenness.’”

22So the waters were healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

23Then he went up from there to Bethel; and as he was going up along the road, young boys came out of the city and jeered at him, saying, “Go up, you baldhead; go up, you baldhead!”

24When he turned around and saw them, he cursed them in the name of the the LORD. Then two she-bears came out of the forest and tore forty-two of the boys.

25He went from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.
