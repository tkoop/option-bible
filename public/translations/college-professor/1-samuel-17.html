The Philistines Defy Israel

1Now the Philistines gathered their forces for war and assembled at Socoh in Judah; they encamped between Socoh and Azekah, at Ephes-dammim. 2Saul and the men of Israel gathered and encamped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah, where they drew up their battle lines against the Philistines. 3The Philistines occupied the hillside on one side, Israel occupied the hillside on the other, and the valley lay between them.

4A champion emerged from the Philistine camp—Goliath of Gath—whose height was six cubits and a span. 5He wore a bronze helmet upon his head and was clad in a coat of mailpostmail; the coat weighed five thousand shekels of bronze. 6He had bronze greaves upon his legs and a bronze javelin slung between his shoulders. 7The shaft of his spear resembled a weaver's beam, and the iron point of his spear weighed six hundred shekels; moreover, his shield-bearer advanced before him.

8He took his stand and cried out to Israel's battle lines, "Why do you come out to array yourselves for war? Am I not a Philistine, and are you not Saul's servants? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him descend to me." 9"If he can fight with me and strike me down, then we shall become your servants; but if I prevail against him and strike him down, then you shall become our servants and serve us." 10The Philistine continued, "I have defied the ranks of Israel this day. Give me a man, that we may fight together." 11When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they were dismayed and filled with great dread.

David Is Sent to the Camp

12Now David was the son of an Ephrathite of Bethlehem in Judah, whose name was Jesse; he had eight sons. In Saul's days the man was old, advanced in years among men. 13The three eldest sons of Jesse had followed Saul into the war: the firstborn Eliab, the second Abinadab, and the third Shammah. 14David was the youngest, while the three eldest followed Saul. 15David went back and forth from Saul to tend his father's sheep at Bethlehem. 16For forty days the Philistine approached morning and evening and took his stand.

17Jesse said to David his son, "Take for your brothers an ephah of this roasted grain and these ten loaves, and carry them quickly to the camp to your brothers." 18"Take these ten cheeses to the commander of their thousand, and see how your brothers fare, and bring back their token."

19Now Saul and all the men of Israel were in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah, fighting with the Philistines. 20David rose early in the morning, left the sheep with a keeper, took the provisions, and went as Jesse had commanded him. He reached the encampment as the army was marching out to the battle line, shouting the war cry. 21Israel and the Philistines drew up in battle array, army against army. 22David left his baggage with the keeper of supplies, ran to the ranks, and went and greeted his brothers. 23As he was speaking with them, behold, the champion—the Philistine of Gath named Goliath—came up from the Philistine ranks and uttered the same words as before; and David heard them. 24All the men of Israel, upon seeing the man, fled from him and were seized with great terror.

25The men of Israel said, "Have you seen this man who has come up? Surely he has come up to defy Israel. The king will enrich the man who kills him with great wealth, give him his daughter, and make his father's house free in Israel."

David Enters the Conflict

26David said to the men standing by him, "What shall be done for the man who kills this Philistine and removes the reproach from Israel? For who is this uncircumcised Philistine, that he should defy the armies of the living God?"

27The people answered him in the same manner, "So it shall be done for the man who kills him."

28His eldest brother Eliab heard him speaking to the men, and Eliab's anger burned against David. He said, "Why have you come down? With whom have you left those few sheep in the wilderness? I know your presumption and the evil of your heart, for you have come down to see the battle."

29David replied, "What have I done now? Was it not merely a word?" 30He turned away from him toward another and spoke in the same way, and the people answered him as before. 31When the words that David had spoken were heard, they were reported to Saul, and he sent for him.

32David said to Saul, "Let not my lord's heart fail on account of this Philistine; your servant will go and fight with this Philistine."

33Saul said to David, "YouyouYou cannot go against this Philistine to fight with him, for you are only a youth, and he has been a warrior from his youth."

34David said to Saul, "YouryourYour servant was tending sheep for his father. When a lion or a bear came and carried off a lamb from the flock, 35I went out after it, struck it down, and rescued the lamb from its mouth; and when it rose against me, I seized it by its beard, struck it down, and killed it. 36Your servant has struck down both lion and bear; and this uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them, since he has defied the armies of the living God."

37David added, "ThetheThe LORD, who delivered me from the paw of the lion and from the paw of the bear, HeheHe will deliver me from the hand of this Philistine."

Saul said to David, "Go, and the the LORD be with you."

38Saul clothed David with his own armourarmourarmor; he placed a bronze helmet on his head and clothed him with a coat of mailpostmail. 39David girded his sword over the armourarmourarmor and attempted to walk, but he could not, for he had not tested them. David said to Saul, "I cannot go in these, for I have not tested them." So David took them off.

40He took his staff in his hand and chose five smooth stones from the brook, placing them in his shepherd's bag; his sling was in his hand. Then he drew near to the Philistine. 41The Philistine advanced and drew near to David, with his shield-bearer before him. 42When the Philistine looked and saw David, he disdained him, for he was but a youth—ruddy and handsome in appearance. 43The Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with sticks?" And the Philistine cursed David by his gods. 44The Philistine said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the air and to the beasts of the field."

45David said to the Philistine, "YouyouYou come to me with sword and spear and javelin, but I come to you in the name of the the LORD of hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you have defied. 46This day the the LORD will deliver you into my hand, and I will strike you down and cut off your head. I will give the carcasses of the Philistine host this day to the birds of the air and to the beasts of the earth, so that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel. 47And all this assembly shall know that the the LORD does not save by sword or spear; for the battle is the the LORD's, and HeheHe will give you into our hands."

David's Victory and Saul's Inquiry

48When the Philistine arose and came and drew near to meet David, David ran quickly toward the battle line to meet the Philistine. 49David put his hand into his bag and took out a stone and slung it; he struck the Philistine on the forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground. 50Thus David prevailed over the Philistine with sling and stone: he struck the Philistine and killed him, though there was no sword in David's hand. 51David ran and stood over the Philistine, took his sword, drew it from its sheath, and killed him, cutting off his head with it. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled.

52The men of Israel and Judah rose and shouted and pursued the Philistines as far as the entrance of the valley and the gates of Ekron. The slain of the Philistines fell along the way from Shaaraim to Gath and Ekron. 53The children of Israel returned from pursuing the Philistines and plundered their camp. 54David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, but he put his armourarmourarmor in his tent.

55When Saul saw David going out against the Philistine, he said to Abner, the commander of the army, "Abner, whose son is this youth?"

Abner said, "As surely as you live, O king, I do not know."

56The king said, "Inquire whose son this young man is."

57As David returned from striking down the Philistine, Abner took him and brought him before Saul, with the head of the Philistine in his hand.

58Saul said to him, "Whose son are you, young man?"

David said, "I am the son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite."
