Jeremiah’s Epistle to the Exiles in Babylon

1These are the words of the epistle that Jeremiah the prophet dispatched from Jerusalem to the surviving elders among the exiles, together with the priests, the prophets, and all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had deported from Jerusalem into Babylon. 2This dispatchdispatch, despatchdispatch followed the departure from Jerusalem of King Jeconiah, the queen mother, the court officials, the princes of Judah and Jerusalem, and the artisans and smiths. 3The letter was entrusted to Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom King Zedekiah of Judah had commissioned as envoys to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, in Babylon, with this message:

The LORD’s Directives for Life in Babylon

4“Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, to the whole company of exiles whom I have caused to be carried away from Jerusalem into Babylon: 5Erect dwellings and inhabit them; cultivate gardens and consume their produce. 6Take spouses and beget sons and daughters; take spouses for your sons and give your daughters to husbands, so that they may bear sons and daughters; proliferate there, and do not let your numbers dwindle. 7Moreover, pursue the welfare of the city into which I have exiled you, and intercede with the LORD on its behalf, for in its prosperity your own tranquillitytranquillitytranquility shall be found.

8“For thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Let neither your prophets nor your diviners in your midst beguile you, and give no heed to the dreams you yourselves dream. 9For they prophesy deceitfully to you in MymyMy name; I have not commissioned them, declares the LORD.”

The Seventy-Year Promise and the Condemnation of the Recalcitrant Remnant

10“For thus says the LORD: When Babylon’s seventy years have run their course, I shall attend to you and fulfillfulfilfulfill MymyMy gracious promise by restoring you to this place. 11For I know the designs I entertain concerning you, declares the LORD—designs of welfare and not of calamity, intended to bestow upon you a future and a hope. 12Then you shall invoke MemeMe, and you shall approach MemeMe in prayer, and I shall heed you. 13And you shall seek MemeMe and find MemeMe, provided you search for MemeMe with your whole heart. 14And I shall be found by you, declares the LORD, and I shall restore your fortunes, and I shall gather you from all the nations and from every region to which I have banished you, declares the LORD, and I shall return you to the place whence I exiled you.”

15“Because you have asserted that the LORD has raised up prophets for you in Babylon. 16Thus says the LORD concerning the king who occupies David’s throne, and concerning all the inhabitants of this city—your kinsmen who did not accompany you into exile: 17Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Behold, I am unleashing upon them sword, famine, and plague, and I shall render them like vile figs, so putrid as to be inedible. 18I shall hound them with sword, famine, and plague; I shall make them an object of terror to all the kingdoms of the earth, a curse, a spectacle of horror, a byword of derision, and a reproach among all the nations to which I have banished them. 19Because they did not heed MymyMy words, declares the LORD, which I dispatched to them through MymyMy servants the prophets, assiduously and repeatedly; yet you refused to listen, declares the LORD.”

20“Therefore, hear the word of the LORD, all you exiles whom I have dispatched from Jerusalem to Babylon. 21Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, concerning Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who utter falsehood to you in MymyMy name: Behold, I shall deliver them into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon, and he shall put them to death before your eyes. 22And from them a byword of cursing shall be taken up by all the exiles of Judah in Babylon, praying that the LORD make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon consigned to the flames. 23Because they perpetrated outrage in Israel, committing adultery with their neighbors’ wives and uttering falsehood in MymyMy name—something I never commanded—and I MyselfmyselfMyself know it and bear witness, declares the LORD.”

The Rebuke of Shemaiah’s Presumptuous Correspondence

24“You shall likewise declare to Shemaiah the Nehelamite: 25‘Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Because you dispatched letters in your own name to all the people in Jerusalem, to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, 26claiming that the LORD has made Zephaniah priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to serve as an officer in the house of the LORD over every madman who prophesies, so that you might confine him in the stocks and in the collar. 27Why, then, have you not rebuked Jeremiah of Anathoth, who prophesies to you? 28For he has written to us in Babylon that the captivity is long and that we should build houses, dwell in them, plant gardens, and eat their fruit.’”

29And Zephaniah the priest read this epistle in the hearing of Jeremiah the prophet.

30“Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 31Send to all the exiles, saying, ‘Thus says the LORD concerning Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you, though I did not send him, and has caused you to trust in a lie, 32therefore thus says the LORD: Behold, I will punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his offspring; he shall not have a man to dwell among this people, neither shall he behold the good that I will do for MymyMy people, declares the LORD, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD.’”
