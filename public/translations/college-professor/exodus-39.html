Fashioning the Priestly Vestments

1From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the artisans fashioned woven vestments for ministration in the sanctuary, and they likewise fashioned the holy garments for Aaron, precisely as the LORD had commanded Moses. 2He fashioned the ephod of gold, together with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and of finely twisted linen. 3They hammered the gold into thin sheets and cut threads from them, weaving them into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and the fine linen, the labourlabourlabor of a master craftsman. 4They made shoulder pieces for the ephod, joining them at its two extremities. 5The skillfully woven waistband of the ephod was of one piece with it, wrought in the same manner—gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen—as the LORD had commanded Moses. 6They set the onyx stones in gold filigree settings, engraved like a seal with the names of the sons of Israel. 7Then they fastened them upon the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Breastpiece and Its Mountings

8He made the breastpiece with the same craftsmanship as the ephod—gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen. 9It was square and doubled, a span in length and a span in breadth. 10They filled it with four rows of stones: the first row was carnelian, chrysolite, and emerald; 11the second row was turquoise, sapphire, and diamond; 12the third row was jacinth, agate, and amethyst; 13and the fourth row was beryl, onyx, and jasper. These stones were set in gold filigree settings. 14The stones corresponded to the names of the twelve sons of Israel, each engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes. 15For the breastpiece they made braided chains of pure gold, like cords. 16They made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and they fastened the rings to the two corners of the breastpiece. 17Then they attached the two gold chains to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece, 18and they fastened the other ends of the chains to the two filigree settings, attaching them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front. 19They made two gold rings and attached them to the other two corners of the breastpiece, on the inner edge adjacent to the ephod. 20They made two more gold rings and attached them to the lower part of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front, near the seam just above the woven waistband of the ephod. 21Then they tied the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue yarn, so that the breastpiece rested above the waistband of the ephod and would not swing away from the ephod, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Robe, Tunics, and Holy Crown

22They made the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth, the work of a weaver, 23with an opening in its centrecentrecenter like the opening of a collar, and with a band around the opening so that it would not tear. 24They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen around the lower hem of the robe. 25They also made bells of pure gold and set them around the hem between the pomegranates. 26A bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate—all around the lower hem of the robe for ministration, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 27They made tunics of fine linen, woven work, for Aaron and his sons, 28and the turban of fine linen, the ornate headbands, and the linen undergarments of finely twisted linen, 29and the sash of finely twisted linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 30They made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold and engraved upon it, like the engraving of a seal: HOLY TO the LORD. 31They attached to it a blue cord to mount it upon the turban above, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

Presentation, Inspection, and Blessing

32Thus all the work on the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, was completed; the Israelites had done everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 33Then they brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent with all its furnishings, its clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases; 34the covering of ram skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather, and the veil of the screen; 35the ark of the testimony with its poles and the mercy seat; 36the table with all its utensils and the bread of the PresencepresencePresence; 37the pure gold lampstand with its lamps, the arranged lamps, and all its utensils, together with the oil for the light; 38the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the curtain for the entrance to the tent; 39the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils; the basin with its stand; 40the curtains of the courtyard with its posts and bases; the curtain for the gate of the courtyard; its ropes and tent pegs; and all the equipment for the service of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting; 41the woven garments for ministration in the sanctuary; the holy garments for Aaron the priest; and the garments for his sons to serve as priests. 42The Israelites had done all the work just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 43And Moses inspected all the work and saw that they had done it just as the LORD had commanded. So Moses blessed them.
