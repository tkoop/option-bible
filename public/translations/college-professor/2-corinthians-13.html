Paul’s Third Visit and the Standard of Witness

1This marks the third occasion on which I am coming to you: every matter must be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses. 2I forewarned you before, and I do so a second time, as though present though now absent, that if I return, I shall show no leniency to those who sinned previously, nor to any of the rest.

3Since you seek proof that Christ is speaking through me—HeheHe who is not weak toward you, but powerful among you— 4for HeheHe was indeed crucified in weakness, yet HeheHe lives by the power of God. For we too are weak in HimhimHim, yet we shall live with HimhimHim by the power of God for your benefit.

Self-Examination, Truth, and Apostolic Integrity

5Examine yourselves, to determine whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Or do you not recognizerecogniserecognize that Jesus Christ is in you?—unless, of course, you are disqualified. 6Yet I trust that you will come to recognizerecogniserecognize that we are not disqualified.

7Now we pray to God that you may do no evil—not so that we may appear approved, but so that you may do what is good, even if we should seem disqualified. 8For we can do nothing against the truth, but only for the truth. 9For we rejoice whenever we are weak while you are strong; indeed, this is what we pray for—your complete restoration. 10This is why I write these things while absent, so that when I am present I need not act with severity in accordance with the authority the Lord has given me for edification, and not for destruction.

Final Exhortations and the Apostolic Benediction

11Finally, brothers, rejoice; be restored; be encouraged; be of one mind; live in peace—and the God of love and peace will be with you. 12Greet one another with a holy kiss. 13All the saints send you their greetings. 14The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit be with all of you.
