Admonition Against Suretyship and Sloth

1My son, if you have stood surety for your neighbourneighbourneighbor and pledged your hand on behalf of a stranger, 2you are snared by the words of your mouth and taken captive by the words of your mouth. 3Do this now, my son, and deliver yourself, for you have come into the hand of your neighbourneighbourneighbor; go, humble yourself, and importune your neighbourneighbourneighbor. 4Give no sleep to your eyes, nor slumber to your eyelids. 5Extricate yourself as a gazelle from the hunter’s grasp, as a bird from the fowler’s hand.

6Go to the ant, O sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise. 7She has no chief, overseer, or ruler; 8she prepares her bread in summer and gathers her food in harvest. 9How long will you lie there, O sluggard? When will you arise from your sleep? 10A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest—and 11your poverty will come upon you like a robber, and your want like an armed man.

12A scoundrel, a man of iniquity, walks with a perverse mouth; 13he winks with his eyes, shuffles with his feet, and gestures with his fingers; 14with perversity in his heart he devises evil continually, sowing discord; 15therefore his calamity will come upon him suddenly, and in a moment he will be shattered beyond recovery.

The Wicked Man and the LORD’s Abominations

16There are six things the LORD hates, seven that are abominations to HishisHis soul: 17haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, 18a heart devising wicked schemes, feet hastening to run to evil, 19a false witness who utters lies, and one who sows discord among brothers.

Parental Wisdom and the Adulteress’s Snare

20My son, keep your father’s commandment, and do not forsake your mother’s teaching. 21Bind them continually upon your heart; fasten them about your neck. 22When you walk, they will guide you; when you lie down, they will watch over you; and when you awake, they will converse with you. 23For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching is a light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way of life.

24They will preserve you from the evil woman, from the smooth tongue of the adulteress. 25Do not covet her beauty in your heart, nor let her eyelids captivate you. 26For a prostitute can be purchased for no more than a loaf of bread, but an adulteress stalks a man’s precious life. 27Can a man carry fire in his bosom and his clothes not be burned? 28Or can a man walk upon hot coals and his feet not be scorched? 29So is he who goes in to his neighbourneighbourneighbor’s wife; no one who touches her will go unpunished.

The Adulterer’s Ruin and the Jealous Husband’s Vengeance

30Men do not despise a thief if he steals to satisfy his hunger; 31yet if he is caught, he must repay sevenfold and surrender all the wealth of his house. 32He who commits adultery lacks understanding; he who does so ruins his own life. 33He will incur wounds and dishonourdishonourdishonor, and his reproach will not be blotted out. 34For jealousy enrages a husband, and he will show no mercy in the day of vengeance. 35He will accept no ransom; he will not be appeased, though you multiply bribes.
