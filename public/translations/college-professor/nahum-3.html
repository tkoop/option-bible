Woe to the Blood-Soaked City

1Woe to the blood-drenched city, teeming with falsehood and rapine, from which the prey never departs. 2The crack of the whip and the rumble of wheels, the gallop of horses and the bounding of chariots; 3the charging horseman, the flashing blade, the gleaming spear; heaps of the slain, masses of corpses, bodies beyond counting—they stumble over their own dead. 4Because of the countless harlotries of the seductive harlot, mistress of sorceries, who sells nations by her seductions and families by her spells.

The LORD's Verdict Against Nineveh

5“Behold, I am against you, declares the LORD of Hosts; I will lift your skirts over your face and expose your nakedness to the nations and your shame to the kingdoms. 6I will cast abominations upon you, treat you with contempt, and make a spectacle of you.”

7And everyone who sees you will shrink from you and say, “Nineveh is laid waste—who will mourn for her?” Where shall I seek comforters for you?

Nineveh and the Fate of Thebes

8Are you better than Thebes, seated among the rivers, with water surrounding her, whose rampart was the sea and whose wall was the water? 9Cush and Egypt were her strength, beyond number; Put and Libya were her allies. 10Yet she too went into exile, into captivity; her little ones were dashed to pieces at the head of every street; lots were cast for her nobles, and all her great men were bound in chains.

The Collapse of Assyria

11You too will be drunk; you will be hidden; you too will seek a stronghold against the enemy. 12All your fortresses are like fig trees with first-ripe figs: if shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater. 13Behold, the people in your midst are like women; the gates of your land stand wide open to your enemies; fire has consumed your bars. 14Draw water for the siege, strengthen your fortresses; go into the clay and tread the mortar, reinforce the brickkiln. 15There fire will devour you; the sword will cut you off; it will consume you as the young locust consumes; multiply yourself like the young locust, multiply yourself like the locust. 16You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of Heavenheavenheaven; the young locust strips the land and flies away. 17Your crowned ones are like locusts, your marshals like swarms of grasshoppers that settle in the hedges on a cold day; when the sun rises they fly away, and no one knows where they have gone.

18Your shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria; your nobles dwell in the dust; your people are scattered upon the mountains, and there is none to gather them. 19There is no healing for your bruise; your wound is grievous. All who hear the report of you clap their hands over you, for upon whom has your wickedness not passed continually?
