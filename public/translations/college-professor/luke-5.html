The Miraculous Catch and the Summons of the First Disciples

1On one occasion, while HeheHe was standing beside the Lake of Gennesaret and the crowd was pressing in upon HimhimHim so as to hear the word of God, 2HeheHe observed two boats moored at the water’s edge, and the fishermen—having disembarked from them—were washing their nets. 3Stepping into one of the boats, namely Simon’s, HeheHe asked him to put out a short distance from the shore; and, having sat down, HeheHe taught the crowds from the boat.

4When HeheHe had finished speaking, HeheHe said to Simon, “Put out into the deep water and let down your nets for a catch.”

5Simon answered, “Master, we have toiled through the whole night and caught nothing; yet, at YouryourYour word, I will let down the nets.”

6When they had done so, they enclosed a vast multitude of fish, and their nets began to tear. 7They therefore signalledsignalledsignaled to their partners in the other boat to come and help them; and they came and filled both boats, so that they began to sink.

8Seeing this, Simon Peter fell at Jesus’ knees, saying, “Depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinful man.”

9For astonishment had seized him and all who were with him because of the catch of fish that they had taken, 10and likewise James and John, the sons of Zebedee, who were Simon’s partners. Then Jesus said to Simon, “Do not be afraid; from now on you will be catching men.”

11When they had brought the boats to land, they left everything and followed HimhimHim.

A Leper Is Cleansed and the Report Spreads Abroad

12While HeheHe was in one of the cities, behold, a man full of leprosy; and when he saw Jesus, he fell on his face and implored HimhimHim, “Lord, if YouyouYou are willing, YouyouYou can make me clean.”

Authority to Forgive Sins Displayed in the Paralytic’s Healing

17On one of those days, while HeheHe was teaching, Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting by—men who had come from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem—and the power of the Lord was with HimhimHim to heal.

18And behold, men were carrying a man who was paralyzed, lying on a bed, and they were seeking to bring him in and to lay him before HimhimHim. 19But, not finding by what way they might bring him in because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and lowered him with his pallet through the tiles into the middle, before Jesus.

20Seeing their faith, HeheHe said, “Man, your sins are forgiven you.”

21The scribes and Pharisees began to reason, saying, “Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who can forgive sins except God alone?”

22Jesus, knowing their reasonings, answered and said to them, “Why do you reason in your hearts? 23Which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven you,’ or to say, ‘Rise and walk’? 24But that you may know that the SonsonSon of ManmanMan has authority on EarthEarthearth to forgive sins.” He said to the paralyzed man, “I say to you, rise, take up your pallet, and go to your house.”

25And immediately, having risen before them, he took up that on which he had lain and departed to his house, glorifying God. 26And amazement seized them all, and they glorified God; they were filled with fear, saying, “We have seen extraordinary things today.”

The Calling of Levi and Disputations Concerning Fasting

27After these things HeheHe went out and observed a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax booth, and HeheHe said to him, “Follow MemeMe.” 28And he, having risen, left everything and followed HimhimHim.

29Levi made a great feast for HimhimHim in his house; and there was a large crowd of tax collectors and others reclining at table with them.

30The Pharisees and their scribes were grumbling at HishisHis disciples, saying, “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”

31Jesus answered them, “It is not the healthy who need a physician, but those who are ill. 32I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”

33They said to HimhimHim, “John’s disciples fast often and make petitions, and likewise those of the Pharisees, but yours eat and drink.”

34Jesus said to them, “Can you make the attendants of the bridegroom fast while the bridegroom is with them? 35But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them; then, in those days, they will fast.”

36He also set forth a parable to them: “No one tears a patch from a new garment and puts it on an old garment; otherwise he will tear the new, and the patch from the new will not agree with the old. 37And no one puts new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the new wine will burst the skins, and it will be spilled, and the skins will be destroyed. 38Rather, new wine must be put into new wineskins. 39And no one, having drunk old wine, desires new; for he says, ‘The old is better.’”
