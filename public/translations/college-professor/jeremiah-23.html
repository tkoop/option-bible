The Righteous Branch and the New Exodus

5“Behold, the days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when I will raise up for David a righteous BranchbranchBranch, and HeheHe shall reign as KingkingKing and act wisely, and HeheHe shall execute justice and righteousness in the land. 6In HishisHis days Judah will be saved, and Israel will dwell securely; and this is the name by which HeheHe will be called: ‘The LORD is our righteousness.’” 7“Therefore, behold, the days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when they shall no longer say, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up the people of Israel out of the land of Egypt,’ 8but, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up and led the descendants of the house of Israel out of the north country and out of all the lands where I had driven them.’ Then they shall dwell in their own land.”

The Corruption of Prophets and Priests

9Concerning the prophets: my heart is broken within me; all my bones shake; I am like a drunken man, like a man overcome by wine, because of the the LORD and because of HishisHis holy words. 10For the land is full of adulterers; because of the curse the land mourns, and the pastures of the wilderness are dried up. Their course is evil, and their might is not right. 11“Both prophet and priest are profane; even in MymyMy house I have found their wickedness,” declares the the LORD. 12“Therefore their way shall be to them like slippery paths in the darkness; they shall be driven and fall in it, for I will bring disaster upon them in the year of their punishment,” declares the the LORD.

13“In the prophets of Samaria I saw an offenciveoffenciveoffensive thing: they prophesied by Baal and led MymyMy people Israel astray. 14But in the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen a horrible thing: they commit adultery and walk in falsehood; they strengthen the hands of evildoers, so that no one turns from his evil; all of them have become like Sodom to MemeMe, and its inhabitants like Gomorrah. 15Therefore thus says the the LORD of Hosts concerning the prophets: Behold, I will feed them with wormwood and give them poisonous water to drink, for from the prophets of Jerusalem ungodliness has gone out into all the land,” declares the the LORD of Hosts.

16Thus says the the LORD of Hosts: “Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you; they delude you. They speak a vision of their own heart, not from the mouth of the the LORD. 17They say continually to those who despise MemeMe, ‘The the LORD has said, “You shall have peace,”’ and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his own heart, ‘No disaster shall come upon you.’ 18For who among them has stood in the council of the the LORD to see and to hear HishisHis word, or who has paid attention to HishisHis word and listened? 19Behold, the storm of the the LORD! Wrath has gone forth, a whirling tempest; it will burst upon the head of the wicked. 20The anger of the the LORD will not turn back until HeheHe has executed and accomplished the designs of HishisHis heart. In the latter days you will understand it clearly.”

The Burden of the LORD and the Perversion of the Word

33“When this people or a prophet or a priest asks you, ‘What is the burden of the the LORD?’ you shall say to them, ‘You are the burden, and I will cast you off,’ declares the the LORD. 34And as for the prophet or priest or any of the people who says, ‘The burden of the the LORD,’ I will punish that man and his house.”

35“Thus you shall say every one to his neighbourneighbourneighbor and every one to his brother, ‘What has the the LORD answered?’ or ‘What has the the LORD spoken?’ 36But the burden of the the LORD you shall mention no more, for the burden is every man’s own word, and you pervert the words of the living God, the the LORD of Hosts, our God. 37Thus you shall say to the prophet, ‘What has the the LORD answered you?’ or ‘What has the the LORD spoken?’ 38But if you say, ‘The burden of the the LORD,’ thus says the the LORD, ‘Because you have said this word, “The burden of the the LORD,” when I sent to you, saying, “You shall not say, ‘The burden of the the LORD,’” 39therefore, behold, I will surely lift you up and cast you away from MymyMy presence, and the city that I gave to you and your fathers. 40And I will bring upon you everlasting reproach and perpetual shame, which shall not be forgotten.’”
