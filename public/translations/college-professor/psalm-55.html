David's Anguish and Appeal for God's Attention

For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments; a didactic poem of David. 1Hearken to my prayer, O God, and do not conceal YourselfyourselfYourself from my supplication. 2Give heed to me and answer me; I am distraught in my meditation and I groan. 3Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked—for they cast iniquity upon me and in anger they bear a grudge against me. 4My heart is in anguish within me, and the terrors of death have fallen upon me. 5Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror has overwhelmed me.

The Desire to Flee from Turmoil

6And I said, "If only I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and find rest. 7Behold, I would wander far away; I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah. 8I would hasten to my refuge from the rushing wind and the storm."

9O Lord, confound their speech, for I see violence and strife in the city. 10Day and night they surround it upon its walls, and iniquity and toil are within it. 11Destruction is in its midst; oppression and deceit do not depart from its streets.

12For it is not an enemy who taunts me—then I could endure it; nor is it one who hates me who has risen up against me—then I could hide from him. 13But it is you, a man my equal, my companion, my familiar friend— 14we who shared sweet counsel together and walked in the house of God among the throng. 15Let death seize them by surprise; let them go down alive to Sheol, for evil is in their midst, in their dwellings.

Trust in God's Deliverance and JudgmentJudgementJudgment

16As for me, I call upon God, and the LORD delivers me. 17Evening, morning, and noon I lament and moan, and HeheHe hears my voice. 18He redeems my soul in peace from the battle waged against me, for many are arrayed against me. 19God will hear and answer them—HeheHe who sits enthroned from of old—Selah—because they do not change and do not fear God.

20He has stretched out his hands against his allies; he has profaned his covenant. 21His speech was flattery itself, smoother than butter, yet war lurked in his heart; his words were softer than oil, but they were unsheathed blades. 22Cast your burden upon the LORD, and HeheHe will sustain you; HeheHe will never permit the righteous to be shaken. 23But YouyouYou, O God, will bring them down to the pit of destruction; bloodthirsty and deceitful men will not live out half their days.
