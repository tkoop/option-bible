The Allotment of Joseph's Descendants

1The lot for the sons of Joseph extended from the Jordan at Jericho to the waters of Jericho on the east, traversing the wilderness that ascends from Jericho into the hill country of Bethel. 2From Bethel it passed to Luz, skirted the border of the Archites at Ataroth, 3and descended westward to the border of the Japhletites, reaching as far as the border of LowerlowerLower Beth-horon and as far as Gezer, with its termini toward the sea. 4Thus Manasseh and Ephraim, the sons of Joseph, received their inheritance.

The Boundary of Ephraim

5The boundary of the Ephraimites, according to their clans, extended eastward from Ataroth-addar to Upper Beth-horon. 6It proceeded toward the sea to Michmethah on the north, then curved eastward to Taanath-shiloh, passing it on the east to Janohah. 7From Janohah it descended to Ataroth and Naarath, reached Jericho, and terminated at the Jordan. 8The boundary went out from Tappuah westward to the Wadi Kanah, with its outlets at the sea; this constituted the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim according to its families.

Ephraim's Enclaves and the Unsubdued Canaanites

9The Ephraimites also possessed distinct cities situated within the inheritance of the Manassites, together with their villages. 10They did not dispossess the Canaanites dwelling in Gezer; rather, the Canaanites continued to reside among the Ephraimites to this day, though they were subjected to forced labourlabourlabor.
