The LORD Commands the House of David to Do Justice

1Thus says the LORD: “Go down to the house of the king of Judah and speak this word there, 2saying, ‘Hear the word of the LORD, O king of Judah, you who sit on the throne of David—you and your servants and your people who enter these gates. 3Thus says the LORD: Execute judgmentjudgementjudgment and righteousness, and deliver the plundered from the hand of the oppressor. Do not mistreat or do violence to the sojourner, the fatherless, or the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place. 4For if you indeed do this, kings sitting on David’s throne will enter through the gates of this house, riding in chariots and on horses—he, his servants, and his people. 5But if you will not hear these words, I swear by MyselfmyselfMyself, declares the LORD, that this house shall become a desolation.’”

10“Do not weep for the dead, nor mourn for him; weep bitterly for him who goes away, for he shall return no more, nor see his native land. 11For thus says the LORD concerning Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who reigned in place of his father Josiah, who went out from this place: ‘He shall not return here anymore, 12but he shall die in the place to which they have carried him captive, and he shall see this land no more.’”

Woe to the Oppressive King and His Dynasty

Did not your father eat and drink and do justice and righteousness? Then it was well with him. 16He judged the cause of the poor and needy; then it was well. The LORD Declares the Exile of Coniah

18“Therefore thus says the LORD concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: ‘They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, my brother!” or “Alas, my sister!” They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, my lord!” or “Alas, his glory!”’ 19He shall be buried with the burial of a donkey—dragged and cast out beyond the gates of Jerusalem.”

20“Go up to Lebanon and cry out; lift up your voice in Bashan; cry out from Abarim, for all your allies are shattered. 21I spoke to you in your time of security, but you said, ‘I will not listen.’ This has been your way from your youth, for you have not obeyed MymyMy voice. 22The wind will drive away all your shepherds, and your allies shall go into captivity; then you shall be ashamed and confounded because of all your wickedness. 23O inhabitant of Lebanon, you who nest in the cedars, how you will wail when pangs come upon you, pain like that of a woman in labourlabourlabor!”
