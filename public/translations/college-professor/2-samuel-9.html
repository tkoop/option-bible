David's Search for a Survivor of Saul's House

1David inquired, "IsisIs there yet any survivor of the house of Saul to whom I might extend steadfast love for Jonathan's sake?"

2There was a servant of Saul's house named Ziba, and he was summoned before David.

The king said to him, "Are you Ziba?"

He replied, "I am your servant."

3The king asked, "IsisIs there no one still remaining of the house of Saul to whom I may manifest the steadfast love of God?"

Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, lame in both feet."

4The king said to him, "Where might he be?"

Ziba answered, "HeheHe resides in the house of Machir son of Ammiel, in Lo Debar."

5King David therefore dispatched messengers and brought him from the house of Machir son of Ammiel in Lo Debar.

Mephibosheth Presented Before the King

6When Mephibosheth, son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came into David's presence, he prostrated himself with his face to the ground.

David said, "Mephibosheth?"

He answered, "Here is your servant."

7David said to him, "FearfearFear not, for I shall surely show you steadfast love for the sake of your father Jonathan, and I shall restore to you the whole patrimony of your father Saul; moreover, you shall take your meals at my table in perpetuity."

8He bowed and said, "What is your servant, that you should look with favourfavourfavor upon one so utterly inconsequential as I?"

The Restoration of Saul's Estate and Household

9The king then summoned Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "I have consigned to your master's son everything that belonged to Saul and to his entire household.

10You, therefore, together with your sons and your servants, shall cultivate the land for him, and you shall gather in the harvest, so that your master's son may have sustenance; yet Mephibosheth, your master's son, shall always dine at my table." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

11Ziba said to the king, "YouryourYour servant shall do precisely as my lord the king commands."

The king said, "As for Mephibosheth, he shall dine at my table like one of the king's sons."

12Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica, and everyone residing in Ziba's household was in Mephibosheth's service.

13Thus Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem, for he continually ate at the king's table, though he was lame in both feet.
