Exhortation to Purify the Soul and Yearn for Spiritual Nourishment

1Having therefore laid aside every species of malice, all deceit, hypocrisy, envy, and every form of slander, 2as newborn infants, yearn for the unadulterated and rational milk, that by it you may mature unto salvation— 3if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.

Living as God's People amid a Hostile World

11Beloved, I entreat you, as sojourners and exiles, to abstain from fleshly desires, which campaign against your soul. 12Maintain an excellent mode of life among the Gentiles, so that, in the very matter in which they malign you as malefactors, they may, by beholding your good works, glorify God in the day of visitation.

13For the Lord's sake, submit yourselves to every human institution—whether to the king as supreme 14or to governors as those sent by him to punish evildoers and to praise those who do good.

15For this is the will of God: that by doing good you may muzzle the ignorance of foolish men. 16Live as free people, not using your freedom as a cloak for malice, but as slaves of God. 17HonourHonourHonor everyone; love the brotherhood; fear God; honourhonourhonor the king.

Submission to Authority and the Suffering Exemplar

18Household servants, be subject to your masters with all reverence—not only to the good and gentle, but also to the perverse.

19For this is grace: if, for conscience toward God, anyone endures sorrows while suffering unjustly. 20For what glory is it if, when you sin and are beaten for it, you endure? But if, when you do good and suffer for it, you endure, this is grace in the sight of God.

21For to this you have been called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in HishisHis steps.
