David Organizes the Temple Musicians

1David, together with the military commanders, set apart for service the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, who prophesied with harps, lyres, and cymbals; the number of those assigned to the work of their service was established. 2Among the sons of Asaph were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah, under Asaph's direction, prophesying under the king's supervision. 3The sons of Jeduthun were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah—six in all—under their father Jeduthun's direction, prophesying with the harp in thanksgiving and praise to the LORD. 4The sons of Heman were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. 5All these were Heman's sons, Heman being the king's seer in the words of God, appointed to exalt his power; and God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters. 6All of them served under their father's direction in the song of the house of the LORD, with cymbals, harps, and lyres, for the service of the house of God, Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman being under the king's supervision. 7Together with their brothers, all trained in singing to the LORD—skilfulskilfulskillful men—they numbered two hundred eighty-eight. 8They cast lots for their duties, without distinction between the lesser and the greater, teacher and pupil alike.

The Casting of Lots for the Singers

9The first lot, designated for Asaph, fell to Joseph; the second to Gedaliah, together with his sons and brothers, twelve in number. 10The third fell to Zaccur, his sons and brothers, twelve. 11The fourth to Izri, his sons and brothers, twelve. 12The fifth to Nethaniah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 13The sixth to Bukkiah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 14The seventh to Jesarelah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 15The eighth to Jeshaiah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 16The ninth to Mattaniah, his sons and brothers, twelve.

The Remaining Courses of the Temple Musicians

17The tenth fell to Shimei, his sons and brothers, twelve. 18The eleventh to Azarel, his sons and brothers, twelve. 19The twelfth to Hashabiah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 20The thirteenth to Shubael, his sons and brothers, twelve. 21The fourteenth to Mattithiah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 22The fifteenth to Jeremoth, his sons and brothers, twelve. 23The sixteenth to Hananiah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 24The seventeenth to Joshbekashah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 25The eighteenth fell to Hanani, his sons and brothers, twelve. 26The nineteenth to Mallothi, his sons and brothers, twelve. 27The twentieth to Eliathah, his sons and brothers, twelve. 28The twenty-first to Hothir, his sons and brothers, twelve. 29The twenty-second to Giddalti, his sons and brothers, twelve. 30The twenty-third to Mahazioth, his sons and brothers, twelve. 31The twenty-fourth to Romamti-ezer, his sons and brothers, twelve.
