Prohibition and Punishment of Child Sacrifice to Molech

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Say to the Israelites: Any Israelite, or any sojourner dwelling among the Israelites, who offers any of his offspring to Molech shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stones.

3“I MyselfmyselfMyself will set MymyMy face against that man and cut him off from among his people, because he has given of his offspring to Molech, thereby defiling MymyMy sanctuary and profaning MymyMy holy name. 4If the people of the land deliberately close their eyes to that man when he gives of his offspring to Molech and do not put him to death, 5then I will set MymyMy face against that man and against his family, and I will cut him off—together with all who prostitute themselves by following him into Molech's service—from among their people.

Condemnation of Mediums and the Imperative of Holiness

6“Any person who turns to mediums or to spiritists in order to prostitute himself with them—I will set MymyMy face against that person and cut him off from among his people.

7“Sanctify yourselves, therefore, and be holy, for I am the LORD your God. 8Keep MymyMy statutes and practice them; I am the LORD, who sanctifies you.

Penalties for Sexual Transgressions and Related Abominations

9“Anyone who curses his father or his mother must be put to death; because he has cursed his father or his mother, his blood is upon him. 10If a man commits adultery with another man's wife—with the wife of his neighbourneighbourneighbor—both the adulterer and the adulteress must be put to death.

11“If a man lies with his father's wife, he has uncovered his father's nakedness; both of them must be put to death, and their blood is upon them. 12If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both must be put to death; they have committed a perversion, and their blood is upon them. 13If a man lies with a male as with a woman, both have committed an abomination; they must be put to death, and their blood is upon them. 14If a man marries a woman and her mother, that is depravity; he and they shall be burned with fire, so that there may be no depravity among you.

15“If a man has sexual relations with an animal, he must be put to death, and you shall kill the animal. 16If a woman approaches any animal to mate with it, you shall kill the woman and the animal; they must be put to death, and their blood is upon them.

17“If a man takes his sister, whether his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness, and she sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace; they shall be cut off in the sight of their people. He has uncovered his sister's nakedness and shall bear his iniquity. 18If a man lies with a woman during her menstrual flow and uncovers her nakedness—having exposed the source of her flow—and she uncovers the source of her blood, both of them shall be cut off from among their people. 19You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister or of your father's sister, for one who does so has uncovered his near kin; they shall bear their iniquity. 20If a man lies with his aunt—that is, his uncle's wife—he has uncovered his uncle's nakedness; they shall bear their sin and die childless. 21If a man takes his brother's wife, it is impurity; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness, and they shall be childless.

Separation, Holiness, and Divine Election

22“You shall keep all MymyMy statutes and all MymyMy ordinances and do them, so that the land to which I am bringing you to dwell in it will not vomit you out. 23You shall not follow the statutes of the nations I am driving out before you, for they did all these things, and I abhorred them. 24I have said to you that you shall inherit their land, and I will give it to you to possess—a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God, who has separated you from the peoples.

25“You shall distinguish between the clean and the unclean among beasts, and between the unclean and the clean among birds; and you shall not make yourselves detestable by beast, by bird, or by anything that creeps on the ground, which I have set apart as unclean for you. 26You shall be holy to MemeMe, for I am the LORD, holy, and I have separated you from the peoples to be MinemineMine. 27Any man or woman who is a medium or a spiritist must be put to death; they shall be stoned with stones, and their blood is upon them.”
