Oracle Against Mount Seir

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, direct your hostility against Mount Seir, and prophesy against it. 3And say to it: Thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am set against you, Mount Seir, and I will reach out against you in judgmentjudgementjudgment and reduce you to utter desolation and ruin. 4I will reduce your cities to rubble, and you yourself shall become a desolation, and you shall know that I am the LORD.

The Charge of Perpetual Enmity

5“Because you have nursed an age-old enmity and have surrendered the people of Israel to the sword in the hour of their calamity, at the time of their final reckoning, 6therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will consign you to bloodshed, and bloodshed shall hound you; because you did not abhor bloodshed, bloodshed shall hound you. 7I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a waste, and I will extirpate from it all who pass through and all who return. 8I will glut its mountains with its slain; upon your hills, in your valleys, and in all your ravines, those felled by the sword shall lie. 9I will make you an everlasting desolation, and your cities shall remain uninhabited; then you shall know that I am the LORD.

Divine Retribution for Edom's Boasting

10“Because you said, ‘These two nations and these two lands shall be mine, and we will possess them’—although the LORD was there— 11therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will requite you according to the wrath and jealousy you manifested in your hatred of them, and I will disclose MyselfmyselfMyself among them when I judge you. 12Then you shall know that I, the LORD, have heard all the blasphemies that you have uttered against the mountains of Israel, saying, ‘They are laid waste; they are delivered to us to be devoured.’ 13You have magnified yourselves against MemeMe with your mouths and have multiplied your words against MemeMe; I have heard it.

Requital for Rejoicing over Israel's Desolation

14“Thus says the Lord GOD: When the whole EarthEarthearth rejoices, I will make you desolate. 15As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it lay desolate, so I will requite you; you shall be a desolation, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it. Then they shall know that I am the LORD.”
