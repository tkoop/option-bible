The Divine Mandate to Annihilate Amalek

1Samuel said to Saul, “The LORD sent me to anoint you king over HishisHis people, over Israel. Now therefore attend to the voice of the words of the LORD.” 2“This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘I have taken note of what Amalek did to Israel—how he opposed them on their way up from Egypt. 3Now go and strike Amalek and devote to destruction all that belongs to him; show him no mercy; put to death men and women, children and infants, oxen and sheep, camels and donkeys.’”

Saul’s Mobilization and the Kenite Exemption

4Saul summoned the troops and numbered them at Telaim: two hundred thousand foot soldiers and ten thousand men of Judah. 5Saul came to the city of Amalek and laid an ambush in the valley. 6Saul said to the Kenites, “You have shown kindness to all the Israelites when they came up from Egypt. Depart now; withdraw from among the Amalekites, lest I destroy you along with them.” So the Kenites withdrew from among the Amalekites.

7Saul defeated Amalek from Havilah to Shur, which lies east of Egypt. 8He captured Agag, king of Amalek, alive, but devoted all the other people to destruction with the sword. 9Saul and his troops spared Agag and the best of the sheep and cattle, the fatlings and lambs, and all that was good; they were unwilling to devote them to destruction, but everything despised and worthless they devoted to destruction.

The LORD’s Rejection of Saul’s Kingship

10Then the word of the LORD came to Samuel, saying, 11“I regret that I have made Saul king, for he has turned back from following MemeMe and has not carried out MymyMy commands.” Samuel was angry, and he cried out to the LORD all night.

12Early in the morning Samuel rose to meet Saul, but he was told, “Saul has gone to Carmel, and behold, he has erected a monument for himself; then he turned and passed on and went down to Gilgal.”

13Samuel came to Saul, and Saul said to him, “Blessed are you of the LORD! I have carried out the command of the LORD.”

14Samuel said, “What then is this bleating of sheep in my ears and the lowing of cattle that I hear?”

15Saul said, “They have brought them from the Amalekites; for the people spared the best of the sheep and cattle to sacrifice to the LORD your God, but the rest we devoted to destruction.”

16“Enough!” Samuel said to Saul. “Let me tell you what the LORD said to me last night.”

“Tell me,” Saul replied.

17Samuel said, “Although you were once small in your own eyes, did you not become the head of the tribes of Israel? The LORD anointed you king over Israel. 18And HeheHe sent you on a mission and said, ‘Go and devote to destruction the sinners, the Amalekites; wage war against them until you have wiped them out.’ 19Why then did you not obey the LORD? Why did you rush upon the plunder and do evil in the sight of the LORD?”

20Saul said to Samuel, “I did obey the LORD. I went on the mission the LORD assigned me. I brought back Agag king of Amalek and devoted the Amalekites to destruction. 21But the troops took sheep and cattle from the plunder—the best of the things devoted to destruction—to sacrifice to the LORD your God at Gilgal.”

22Samuel said, “Does the LORD delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices as much as in obedience to HishisHis voice? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to heed is better than the fat of rams. 23For rebellion is as the sin of divination, and arrogance is as the evil of idolatry. Because you have rejected the word of the LORD, HeheHe has rejected you as king.”

Saul’s Confession and Samuel’s Final JudgmentJudgementJudgment

24Saul said to Samuel, “I have sinned; I have transgressed the LORD’s command and your words, because I feared the people and obeyed their voice. 25Now, I beg you, pardon my sin and return with me, so that I may worship the LORD.”

26Samuel said to Saul, “I will not return with you, for you have rejected the word of the LORD, and the LORD has rejected you from being king over Israel.”

27As Samuel turned to go, Saul seized the hem of his robe, and it tore.

28Samuel said to him, “The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to your neighbourneighbourneighbor who is better than you. 29Moreover, the GlorygloryGlory of Israel will not lie or change HishisHis mind, for HeheHe is not a man, that HeheHe should change HishisHis mind.”

30Saul said, “I have sinned; but please honourhonourhonor me now before the elders of my people and before Israel, and return with me, that I may bow in worship to the LORD your God.”

31So Samuel went back with Saul, and Saul worshiped the LORD.

32Samuel said, “Bring me Agag king of the Amalekites.”

So Agag came to him cheerfully, for Agag thought, “Surely the bitterness of death is past.”

33Samuel said, “As your sword has made women childless, so shall your mother be childless among women.” And Samuel hacked Agag to pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.

34Then Samuel went to Ramah, and Saul went up to his house at Gibeah of Saul.

35Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death, though Samuel grieved over Saul. And the LORD regretted that HeheHe had made Saul king over Israel.
