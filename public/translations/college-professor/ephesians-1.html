Apostolic Salutation and Benediction

1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus and the faithful in Christ Jesus: 2Grace and peace to you from God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ.

The Spiritual Blessings Bestowed in Christ

3Blessed be the God and FatherfatherFather of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places, 4just as HeheHe chose us in HimhimHim before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before HimhimHim in love, 5having predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of HishisHis will, 6to the praise of the glory of HishisHis grace, which HeheHe lavished upon us in the BelovedbelovedBeloved.

7In HimhimHim we have redemption through HishisHis blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of HishisHis grace, 8which HeheHe lavished upon us in all wisdom and insight, 9making known to us the mystery of HishisHis will, according to HishisHis good pleasure, which HeheHe purposed in HimhimHim, 10as an economy for the fullness of the times, to recapitulate all things in Christ—things in Heavenheavenheaven and things on EarthEarthearth—in HimhimHim.

Inheritance, Sealing, and the Spirit's Guarantee

11In HimhimHim we have also obtained an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of HimhimHim who accomplishes all things according to the counsel of HishisHis will, 12so that we who were the first to hope in Christ might be to the praise of HishisHis glory. 13In HimhimHim you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in it, were sealed with the promised HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 14who is the pledge of our inheritance until the redemption of the acquired possession, to the praise of HishisHis glory.

Thanksgiving and Intercession for Spiritual Illumination

15For this reason I too, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love toward all the saints, 16do not cease giving thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers, 17that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the FatherfatherFather of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of HimhimHim, 18having the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of HishisHis calling, what are the riches of the glory of HishisHis inheritance among the saints, 19and what is the surpassing greatness of HishisHis power toward us who believe, according to the working of HishisHis mighty strength, 20which HeheHe worked in Christ when HeheHe raised HimhimHim from the dead and seated HimhimHim at HishisHis right hand in the heavenly places, 21far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come. 22And HeheHe put all things under HishisHis feet and gave HimhimHim as head over all things to the church, 23which is HishisHis body, the fullness of HimhimHim who fills all in all.
