The East Gate and Sabbath Worship

1Thus says the Lord, the LORD: The gate of the inner court that faces east shall remain closed during the six working days, yet it shall be opened on the Sabbath and on the day of the NewnewNew MoonmoonMoon. 2The prince shall enter from without through the vestibule of the gate and stand beside the gatepost; the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall worship at the threshold of the gate. Then he shall depart, but the gate shall not be closed until evening. 3On the Sabbaths and on the NewnewNew MoonsmoonsMoons, the people of the land shall worship at the entrance of that gate before the LORD.

Sacrifices for Sabbaths and New Moons

4The burnt offering that the prince is to present to the LORD on the Sabbath shall be six unblemished lambs and an unblemished ram. 5The grain offering accompanying the ram shall be an ephah, and the grain offering with the lambs shall be as much as he can afford, together with a hin of oil per ephah. 6On the day of the NewnewNew MoonmoonMoon, the offering shall be a bull from the herd without blemish, six lambs, and a ram, all of them without blemish. 7He shall provide a grain offering of an ephah with the bull and an ephah with the ram, and with the lambs as much as he is able, together with a hin of oil per ephah.

Processions, Festal Assemblies, and the Daily Offering

8When the prince enters, he shall come in by way of the vestibule of the gate, and he shall go out by the same way. 9But when the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed festivals, whoever enters by the north gate to worship shall leave by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate shall leave by the north gate; no one shall turn back by the gate through which he entered, but each shall go out straight ahead. 10When they enter, the prince shall enter among them, and when they depart, he shall depart. 11At the festivals and appointed seasons, the grain offering shall be an ephah with a bull, an ephah with a ram, and with the lambs as much as one is able, together with a hin of oil per ephah. 12When the prince makes a freewill offering, whether a burnt offering or peace offerings, as a freewill offering to the LORD, the gate that faces east shall be opened for him; he shall present his burnt offering and his peace offerings as he does on the Sabbath, and then he shall go out, after which the gate shall be closed.

13You shall provide a year-old lamb without blemish as a burnt offering to the LORD each day; morning by morning you shall provide it. 14And you shall provide a grain offering with it each morning: one-sixth of an ephah, with one-third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour, as a grain offering to the LORD. This is a perpetual statute. 15Thus they shall provide the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil every morning as a regular burnt offering.

Inheritance, Chambers, and Temple Kitchens

16Thus says the Lord, the LORD: If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance; it shall belong to his sons and remain their hereditary possession. 17But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his only until the year of emancipation, when it shall revert to the prince; only his sons may retain a permanent inheritance. 18The prince shall not take any of the people's inheritance by dispossessing them of their property; he shall provide an inheritance for his sons from his own property, so that none of MymyMy people may be driven from his holding.

19Then he brought me through the entrance at the side of the gate into the holy chambers for the priests, which face north, and behold, there was a place at the far western end. 20And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests are to boil the guilt offering and the sin offering and to bake the grain offering, so that they need not bring them out into the outer court and sanctify the people."

21Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me around to the four corners of the court; and behold, in each corner of the court there was a court. 22In the four corners of the court were enclosed courts, forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide; the four were of the same dimensions. 23A row of masonry ran all around them, around the four of them, and cooking hearths were fashioned beneath the rows all around. 24And he said to me, "These are the kitchens where the ministers of the temple shall boil the sacrifices of the people."
