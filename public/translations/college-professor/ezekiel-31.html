The Word of the LORD to Ezekiel Concerning Pharaoh

1In the eleventh year, on the first day of the third month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying:

2“Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude: To whom are you like in your greatness? 3Behold, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, beautiful of branch, shading and forested, and lofty in stature; its top was among the thick boughs.

“4The waters made him grow; the deep exalted him; its rivers ran round about his planting, and it sent its channels to all the trees of the field. 5Therefore his height was raised above all the trees of the field; his boughs multiplied, and his branches grew long as they spread, because of abundant water. 6All the birds of Heavenheavenheaven nested in its boughs; all the beasts of the field brought forth beneath its branches; and in its shade all the great nations dwelt.

“7Thus it was beautiful in its greatness, in the length of its branches, for its root reached to abundant waters. 8The cedars in the garden of God could not overshadow it; the cypresses were not like its boughs, and the plane trees were not like its branches; no tree in the garden of God resembled it in beauty. 9I made it beautiful in the abundance of its branches, so that all the trees of Eden in the garden of God envied it.”

The JudgmentJudgementJudgment of the Proud Cedar

10“Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Because you were exalted in stature, and it set its top among the thick boughs, and its heart was lifted up in its height, 11I will deliver it into the hand of the ruler of the nations, who shall deal with it according to its wickedness; I have banished it.

“12Foreigners, the most ruthless of the nations, cut it down and abandoned it; its branches fell upon the mountains and in all the valleys; its boughs were broken in all the ravines of the land; and all the peoples of the earth departed from its shadow and left it. 13Upon its ruin all the birds of Heavenheavenheaven dwell, and all the beasts of the field are among its branches.

“14So that none of the trees by the waters may exalt themselves in their height, nor set their tops among the thick boughs, nor any that drink water stand in their height; for all are consigned to death, to the lower parts of the earth, among the sons of men, to those who descend into the pit.”

Lamentation and Descent into Sheol

15“Thus says the Lord the LORD: On the day of its descent to Sheol, I caused mourning; I covered the deep over it; I restrained its rivers, and the abundant waters were held back; I darkened Lebanon over it, and all the trees of the field fainted for it.

“16I made the nations tremble at the sound of its fall, when I cast it down to Sheol with those who descend to the pit; and all the trees of Eden, the choicest and best of Lebanon, all that drink water, were comforted in the lower EarthEarthearth. 17They too descended with it to Sheol, to those slain by the sword, and those who were its arm dwelt in its shadow among the nations.

18“To whom are you like in glory and greatness among the trees of Eden? You shall be brought down with the trees of Eden to the lower parts of the earth; you shall lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude,” declares the Lord the LORD.
