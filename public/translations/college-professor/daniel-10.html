A Revelation Granted to Daniel

1In the third year of King Cyrus of Persia, a revelation was granted to Daniel, who was called Belteshazzar. The message was true and concerned a great conflict, and understanding of the message was given to him in a vision. 2In those days I, Daniel, was in mourning for three full weeks. 3I ate no delicacies; no meat or wine passed my lips; I did not anoint myself with oil until the three weeks had run their course. 4On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was standing beside the great river, the Tigris.

The Heavenly Messenger by the Tigris

5I lifted my eyes and looked, and there stood a man clothed in linen, belted about the waist with gold from Uphaz. 6His body was like beryl, his face like lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and feet like burnished bronze, and the sound of his words like the roar of a multitude. 7I, Daniel, alone saw the vision, for the men who were with me did not see it; nevertheless, a great dread fell upon them, and they fled to hide themselves. 8So I was left alone and saw this great vision. No strength remained in me; my vigourvigourvigor was turned to ruin within me, and I retained no power. 9Then I heard the sound of his words; and as I heard it, I fell into a deep sleep, my face to the ground. 10Then a hand touched me and set me trembling upon my knees and the palms of my hands.

The Messenger’s Commission and the Heavenly Conflict

11He said to me, “O Daniel, man greatly beloved, understand the words that I speak to you, and stand upright, for now I am sent to you.” When he had spoken this word to me, I stood trembling.

12He said to me, “Do not be afraid, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard, and I have come because of your words. 13The prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me for twenty-one days, but Michael, one of the chief princes, came to my aid, for I had been left there with the kings of Persia. 14Now I have come to make you understand what will befall your people in the latter days, for the vision concerns those days.”

15While he was speaking these words to me, I turned my face to the ground and was speechless.

16Then one resembling a man touched my lips, and I opened my mouth and spoke; I said to the one standing before me, “O my lord, because of the vision my anguish has overwhelmed me, and I have no strength. 17How, then, can this servant of my lord speak with my lord? For now no strength remains in me, and no breath is left in me.”

18Again one with the appearance of a man touched me and strengthened me.

19He said, “Do not be afraid, O man greatly beloved. Peace be with you; be strong, yes, be very strong.” When he spoke to me, I was strengthened.

And I said, “Speak, my lord, for you have strengthened me.”

20Then he said, “Do you know why I have come to you? Now I will return to fight the prince of Persia, and when I leave, the prince of Greece will come. 21But I will tell you what is inscribed in the book of truth, and no one strengthens me against these except Michael, your prince.”
