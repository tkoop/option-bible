David Anointed King over All Israel

1All Israel mustered to David at Hebron and declared, “Behold, we are your own kin.”

2Even in former days, while Saul still reigned, you were the one who led Israel forth and brought them in; and the LORD your God said to you, “You shall shepherd MymyMy people Israel, and you shall be ruler over MymyMy people Israel.”

3So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and David covenanted with them there before the LORD; and they anointed David king over Israel, in accordance with the word of the LORD given through Samuel.

David Captures Jerusalem

4Then David and all Israel marched on Jerusalem—that is, Jebus—where the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land, dwelt.

5The inhabitants of Jebus said to David, “You shall not come in here.” Yet David captured the stronghold of Zion, that is, the CitycityCity of David.

6David declared, “Whoever first strikes down the Jebusites shall be chief and commander.” Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, and thereby became chief.

7David took up residence in the stronghold; hence they called it the CitycityCity of David.

8He rebuilt the city all around, from the Millo to the surrounding wall, and Joab restored the remainder of the city.

9David grew ever greater, for the LORD of Hosts was with him.

David’s Mighty Men

10Now these are the heads of the mighty men who stood with David and lent him strong support in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel.

11This is the roster of David’s mighty men: Jashobeam son of Hachmoni, chief of the thirty, who brandished his spear against three hundred men slain in a single encounter.

12After him stood Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men.

13He was with David at Pas-dammim, where the Philistines had gathered for battle on a field full of barley; the people fled before the Philistines.

14But they took their stand in the midst of the field, defended it, struck down the Philistines, and the LORD wrought a great deliverance.

15Three of the thirty chief men went down to David at the rock, to the cave of Adullam, while the Philistine host was encamped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim.

16David was then in the stronghold, and a Philistine garrison held Bethlehem.

17David longed and said, “Who will give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem by the gate?”

18So the three broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well of Bethlehem by the gate, carried it, and brought it to David; but David refused to drink it and poured it out to the LORD.

19He said, “Far be it from me before my God to do this. Shall I drink the blood of these men, who brought it at the risk of their lives?” Therefore he would not drink it. These are the deeds the three mighty men performed.

20Abishai, Joab’s brother, was chief of the three; he wielded his spear against three hundred and slew them, winning a name among the three.

21He was doubly honouredhonouredhonored among the three and became their commander, though he did not attain to the first three.

22Benaiah son of Jehoiada, a valiant man from Kabzeel, renowned for great deeds, struck down two sons of Ariel of Moab; he also descended into a pit on a snowy day and killed a lion.

23He slew an Egyptian of imposing stature, five cubits tall; the Egyptian bore a spear like a weaver’s beam, yet Benaiah went down to him with a staff, seized the spear from the Egyptian’s hand, and killed him with his own spear.

24These were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he won a name among the three mighty men.

25He was honouredhonouredhonored among the thirty, though he did not reach the first three; David appointed him over his bodyguard.
