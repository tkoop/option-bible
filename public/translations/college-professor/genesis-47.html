Joseph Presents His Family before Pharaoh

1Joseph entered and reported to Pharaoh, saying, “My father and my brothers, together with their flocks, herds, and all their possessions, have come from the land of Canaan, and they are now in the land of Goshen.” 2From among his brothers he selected five men and presented them before Pharaoh.

3Pharaoh asked his brothers, “What is your occupation?”

They replied to Pharaoh, “Your servants are shepherds of flocks, as were our fathers.”

4They further said to Pharaoh, “We have come to reside in the land as sojourners, because there is no pasture for your servants’ flocks, for the famine is severe in the land of Canaan; now, please, let your servants dwell in the land of Goshen.”

5Pharaoh then spoke to Joseph, saying, “Your father and your brothers have come to you. 6The land of Egypt lies open before you; settle your father and your brothers in the best of the land. Let them dwell in the land of Goshen, and if you know any capable men among them, entrust them with charge over my livestock.”

Jacob Blesses Pharaoh and Settles in Goshen

7Joseph brought his father Jacob and presented him before Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh. 8Pharaoh said to Jacob, “How many are the days of the years of your life?”

9Jacob said to Pharaoh, “The days of the years of my sojournings are one hundred thirty years. Few and difficult have been the days of the years of my life, and they have not reached the days of the years of the lives of my fathers in the days of their sojournings.”

10Jacob blessed Pharaoh and went out from his presence. 11Joseph settled his father and his brothers and gave them a holding in the land of Egypt, in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded. 12Joseph provided his father, his brothers, and all his father’s household with bread, according to the number of their dependents.

Joseph Administers Egypt during the Famine

13There was no bread in all the land, for the famine was exceedingly severe; the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan languished because of the famine. 14Joseph collected all the money that was found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan in exchange for the grain they were buying, and Joseph brought the money into Pharaoh’s house.

15When the money was spent in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan, all Egypt came to Joseph, saying, “Give us bread. Why should we die before you? For the money is gone.”

16Joseph said, “Give your livestock, and I will give you bread for your livestock, since the money is gone.”

17They brought their livestock to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for horses, flocks of sheep, herds of cattle, and donkeys; he sustained them with bread in return for all their livestock during that year.

18When that year had ended, they came to him in the second year and said, “We will not conceal from my lord that our money is exhausted and the livestock of the cattle belongs to my lord; nothing remains before my lord except our bodies and our land. 19Why should we die before your eyes, we and our land together? Buy us and our land for bread, and we and our land will become servants to Pharaoh; and give us seed, that we may live and not die, and that the land may not become desolate.”

20So Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh, because every Egyptian sold his field, for the famine pressed heavily upon them; thus the land became Pharaoh’s. 21As for the people, he relocated them to the cities from one end of Egypt’s border to the other. 22Only the land of the priests he did not buy, for the priests received an allotment from Pharaoh, and they ate the allotment that Pharaoh gave them; therefore they did not sell their land.

23Joseph said to the people, “Behold, I have bought you and your land today for Pharaoh; here is seed for you, and sow the land. 24At the harvests you shall give a fifth to Pharaoh, and four-fifths shall be yours for seed for the field, for your food, for those in your households, and for the nourishment of your little ones.”

25They said, “You have preserved our lives; may we find favourfavourfavor in my lord’s eyes, and we will be servants to Pharaoh.”

26Joseph established this as a statute concerning the land of Egypt to this day: a fifth belongs to Pharaoh, and only the priests’ land did not become Pharaoh’s.

Israel Prospers and Jacob Prepares for Death

27Israel dwelt in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen; they acquired holdings there and were exceedingly fruitful and multiplied. 28Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years, so the days of Jacob’s life were one hundred forty-seven years.

29When the time drew near for Israel to die, he called his son Joseph and said, “If I have now found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, swear to me a solemn oath—do not bury me in Egypt. 30I will lie with my fathers; carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their burial place.”

He said, “I will do as your word.”

31He said, “Swear to me.” So he swore to him, and Israel bowed in worship at the head of the bed.
