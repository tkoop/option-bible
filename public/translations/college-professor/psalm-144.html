A Royal Hymn of Thanksgiving, Supplication, and Prosperity

1Blessed be the LORD, my RockrockRock, who disciplines my hands for martial combat and my fingers for the fray; 2HeheHe is my steadfast love and my Fortress, my Stronghold and my Deliverer, my Shield, in whom I take refuge, who subjugates my people beneath me. 3O the LORD, what is mortal man that YouyouYou should take cognizance of him, or a human being that YouyouYou should esteem him? 4Man is but a breath; his days are like a shadow that passes away.

5Rend the heavens, O the LORD, and descend; touch the mountains, that they may smoke. 6Flash forth lightning and scatter them; launch YouryourYour arrows and put them to rout. 7Reach down from on high; rescue me and deliver me from the surging waters, from the grasp of foreigners, 8whose mouths utter deceit and whose right hands are pledged to falsehood.

9I will sing to YouyouYou a new song, O God; upon a ten-stringed harp I will make music to YouyouYou. 10He grants victory to kings; HeheHe delivers HishisHis servant David from the perilous sword. 11Rescue me and deliver me from the grasp of foreigners, whose mouths utter deceit and whose right hands are pledged to falsehood.

12Then our sons will be like well-nurtured saplings, and our daughters like pillars sculpted for the adornment of a palace. 13Our granaries will be filled, furnishing produce of every sort; our flocks will multiply by thousands, by tens of thousands in our pastures. 14Our oxen will be heavily laden; there will be no breach, no banishment, and no outcry in our thoroughfares. 15Blessed are the people for whom this is so; blessed are the people whose God is the LORD.
