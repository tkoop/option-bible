Job's Shattered Spirit and the Mockery of His Companions

1My spirit is shattered; my days have been extinguished; the grave stands ready for me. 2Surely mockers surround me, while my eyes remain fixed upon their defiance.

A Plea for Divine Surety and the Exposure of the Godless

3Please grant me a pledge with YouyouYou, for who else will stand surety for me? 4For YouyouYou have concealed their hearts from discernment; therefore YouyouYou will not exalt them. 5Whoever denounces his friends for a share of the spoils will see his children's eyes fail. 6He has made me a byword among the peoples, so that I have become one at whom they spit. 7My eyes have dimmed with grief, and all my frame has dwindled to a shadow. 8The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent rouses himself against the godless. 9Yet the righteous will hold fast to his course, and the one whose hands are clean will grow ever stronger.

Job's Rebuke of His Companions' Folly

10But come now, all of you; I shall not find a wise man among you. 11My days have passed; my plans have been severed, even the desires of my heart. 12They turn night into day, saying, "LightlightLight is near at hand, notwithstanding the darkness."

Descent into Sheol and the Extinction of Hope

13If I look to Sheol as my home, and spread my couch in the darkness, 14I have said to corruption, "YouyouYou are my father," and to the worm, "YouyouYou are my mother and my sister." 15Where, then, is my hope? Who could possibly discern it? 16Will it descend to the bars of Sheol, when together we sink into the dust?
