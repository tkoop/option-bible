The Scandal of Incestuous Immorality and the Call to Discipline

1It is reported, indeed, that sexual immorality obtains among you, and of a kind not even named among the Gentiles—namely, that someone is cohabiting with his father's wife. 2And you are inflated with pride, rather than having mourned, so that the one who perpetrated this deed might be expelled from your midst. 3For I, though absent in body, am present in spirit, and have already rendered judgmentjudgementjudgment, as though present, upon the one who has so wrought this; 4when you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus, my spirit being present with the power of our Lord Jesus, 5to deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that his spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.

Purging the Old Leaven for the Passover Feast

6Your vaunting is not seemly. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the entire lump? 7Purge out the old leaven, that you may become a new unleavened batch, even as you are unleavened; for Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed. 8Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, nor with leaven of malice and wickedness, but with unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

Discerning Fellowship: the World and the Professing Brother

9I wrote to you in my epistle not to associate with the sexually immoral—not at all meaning the immoral persons of this world, or the covetous and extortioners, or idolaters, for then you would have to depart from the world. 10But now I write to you not to associate with anyone who is called a brother if he is sexually immoral, or covetous, or an idolater, or a reviler, or a drunkard, or an extortioner; with such a one do not even eat. 11For what have I to do with judging those outside? Do you not judge those within? 12But God judges those outside. 13Expel the wicked man from among you.
