The Exodus and the Trembling of Creation

1When Israel departed from Egypt, and the house of Jacob from a nation whose speech was alien, 2Judah became HishisHis sanctuary, and Israel HishisHis dominion. 3The sea beheld it and fled; the Jordan turned back. 4The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.

5What disquiets you, O sea, that you flee? What turns you back, O Jordan? 6What moves you, O mountains, to skip like rams, and you, O hills, like lambs?

7Tremble, O EarthEarthearth, before the Lord, before the God of Jacob, 8who turns the rock into a pool of water and the flint into a fountain of waters.
