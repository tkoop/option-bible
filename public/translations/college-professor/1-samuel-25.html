The Death of Samuel and David's Retreat into the Wilderness

1When Samuel died, all Israel assembled, mourned him, and interred him at his home in Ramah; thereupon David arose and descended into the wilderness of Paran.

The Affluence of Nabal and the Contumacy of a Churl

2There was a man in Maon whose enterprise was in Carmel, and he was surpassingly affluent, possessing three thousand sheep and a thousand goats; he was shearing his sheep in Carmel. 3The man's name was Nabal, and his wife's name was Abigail; the woman was sagacious and comely, whereas the man was harsh and depraved in his dealings. 4While David was in the wilderness, he learned that Nabal was shearing his sheep.

5David therefore sent ten young men and said to them, "Go up to Carmel, present yourselves before Nabal, and greet him in my name. 6Thus you shall say: 'Peace be to you, to your house, and to all that is yours.' 7I have heard that you have shearers; now your shepherds have been with us, and we neither insulted them nor missed anything of theirs throughout their sojourn in Carmel. 8Ask your young men, and they will tell you. Therefore let these young men find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, for we have come on a festive day; please bestow whatever you can afford upon your servants and upon your son David."

9David's men went, delivered all these words to Nabal in David's name, and waited.

10Nabal answered David's servants, "Who is David, and who is the son of Jesse? There are many servants nowadays who are breaking away from their masters. 11Shall I take my bread, my water, and my meat, which I have prepared for my shearers, and give it to men whom I do not know?"

12So David's men turned back and reported to him every one of these words.

13David said to his men, "Gird on your swords!" So David girded on his own sword, and about four hundred men followed David, while two hundred remained with the baggage.

14But one of the young men told Abigail, Nabal's wife, "David sent messengers from the wilderness to greet our master, but he reviled them. 15Yet the men were very good to us; we were not insulted, and we missed nothing all the days we went about with them in the fields. 16They were a wall to us both by day and by night throughout the time we were with them tending the sheep. 17Now consider what you should do, for calamity is irrevocably decreed against our master and all his household; he is such a scoundrel that no one can speak to him."

Abigail's Swift and Sagacious Intercession

18Then Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves, two skins of wine, five sheep already prepared, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred clusters of raisins, and two hundred cakes of figs, and loaded them on donkeys. 19She said to her servants, "Go on ahead of me; I am coming after you." But she did not tell her husband Nabal. 20As she was riding on the donkey and descending the mountain under cover, behold, David and his men were coming down toward her, and she met them.

21Now David had said, "Surely in vain I guarded all that belongs to this man in the wilderness, so that nothing of his was missing; yet he has repaid me evil for good. 22May God do so to the enemies of David, and more also, if by morning I leave so much as one male of all who belong to him."

23When Abigail saw David, she hurried, dismounted from the donkey, fell before David with her face to the ground, and bowed to the ground. 24She fell at his feet and said, "On me, my lord, be the guilt; please let your maidservant speak in your ears and hear the words of your maidservant. 25Let not my lord regard this worthless man, Nabal, for as his name is, so is he: Nabal is his name, and folly is with him; but I, your maidservant, did not see the young men whom you sent. 26Now, my lord, as the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, since the LORD has restrained you from bloodguilt and from avenging yourself with your own hand, now let your enemies and those who seek harm for my lord be as Nabal. 27And now let this gift, which your maidservant has brought to my lord, be given to the young men who follow my lord. 28Please forgive the transgression of your maidservant, for the LORD will certainly make for my lord an enduring dynasty, because my lord fights the battles of the LORD, and evil will not be found in you throughout your days. 29If a man rises to pursue you and to seek your life, the life of my lord will be bound in the bundle of life with the LORD your God, while the lives of your enemies HeheHe will sling out as from the pouch of a sling. 30And when the LORD has done for my lord according to all the good that HeheHe has spoken concerning you and has appointed you ruler over Israel, 31this shall not be a source of pangs to you or a snare to your conscience, namely, that my lord has shed blood without cause or that my lord has avenged himself; and when the LORD has dealt well with my lord, then remember your maidservant."

32David said to Abigail, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who sent you this day to meet me. 33Blessed be your discernment, and blessed be you, who this day kept me from bloodshed and from avenging myself. 34Nevertheless, as the LORD, the God of Israel, lives, who restrained me from harming you, if you had not hurried to meet me, surely by morning not one male would have been left to Nabal." 35Then David received from her hand what she had brought him, and he said to her, "Go up to your house in peace; see, I have heard your voice and have granted your request."

Nabal's Demise and David's Marriages

36Abigail came to Nabal, and behold, he was holding a feast in his house, like the feast of a king; Nabal's heart was merry, and he was very drunk, so she told him nothing, small or great, until the morning light. 37In the morning, when the wine had gone out of Nabal, his wife told him these things, and his heart died within him, and he became as a stone. 38About ten days later, the LORD struck Nabal, and he died.

39When David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD, who has pleaded the cause of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and has kept HishisHis servant from evil; the LORD has returned the wickedness of Nabal upon his own head." Then David sent and spoke to Abigail, to take her as his wife.

40When David's servants came to Abigail at Carmel, they spoke to her, saying, "David has sent us to you to take you as his wife."

41She arose, bowed with her face to the ground, and said, "Behold, your maidservant is here to be a servant, to wash the feet of my lord's servants."

42Then Abigail hurried and arose, rode on a donkey, with her five maidservants following her, and she went after David and became his wife. 43David had also taken Ahinoam of Jezreel, and both of them became his wives. 44Saul had given Michal, his daughter and David's wife, to Palti the son of Laish, who was from Gallim.
