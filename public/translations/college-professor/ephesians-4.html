Walking Worthy of the Vocation to Which One Has Been Summoned

1As a prisoner in the Lord, therefore, I exhort you to conduct your lives in a manner worthy of the vocation to which you have been called, 2with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love, 3striving to preserve the unity of the SpiritspiritSpirit in the bond of peace. 4There is one body and one SpiritspiritSpirit, just as you were called to one hope in your calling; 5one Lord, one faith, one baptism; 6one God and FatherfatherFather of all, who is over all, through all, and in all.

Christ’s Gifts and the Church’s Maturation

7Yet to each of us grace has been apportioned according to the measure of Christ’s gift. 8Hence it is said, “When HeheHe ascended on high, HeheHe led captives in HishisHis train and gave gifts to humanity.” 9Now what is the significance of “He ascended,” if not that HeheHe first descended into the lower regions of the earth? 10The OneoneOne who descended is HimselfhimselfHimself also the OneoneOne who ascended far above all the heavens, so that HeheHe might fill all things. 11And HeheHe HimselfhimselfHimself appointed some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as pastors and teachers, 12for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, toward the edification of the body of Christ, 13until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the SonsonSon of God, to mature adulthood, to the measure of the stature of Christ’s fullness. 14Thus we are no longer to be infants, tossed to and fro and carried about by every wind of teaching, by the cunning and craftiness of people in their deceitful scheming. 15Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in all respects into HimhimHim who is the head—Christ HimselfhimselfHimself. 16From HimhimHim the whole body, being fitted and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love as each part does its work according to its proper measure.

Renouncing the Former Way and Embracing the New Self

17So I declare and testify in the Lord that you must no longer live as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their thinking. 18Their understanding is darkened; they are alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance within them, stemming from the hardness of their hearts. 19Having lost all sensitivity, they have surrendered themselves to sensuality so as to indulge in every kind of impurity with insatiable greed. 20But you did not so learn Christ, 21assuming that you have heard HimhimHim and been taught in HimhimHim, as truth resides in Jesus. 22You were instructed to put off the old self, which belongs to your former way of life and is being corrupted by deceitful desires, 23and to be renewed in the spirit of your minds, 24and to put on the new self, created in God’s likeness in true righteousness and holiness.

The Ethical Imperatives of the Renewed Life

25Therefore, having laid aside falsehood, let each of you speak truth with his neighbourneighbourneighbor, for we are members of one another. 26“Be angry, yet do not sin; do not let the sun set upon your wrath.” 27And give no opportunity to the devil. 28Let the thief steal no longer; rather, let him toil, doing honest work with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with those in need. 29Let no corrupt word proceed from your mouth, but only what is good for edification according to the need, that it may impart grace to those who hear. 30And do not grieve the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption. 31Let all bitterness, rage, anger, clamourclamourclamor, and slander be removed from you, together with all malice. 32Rather, be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as God in Christ forgave you.
