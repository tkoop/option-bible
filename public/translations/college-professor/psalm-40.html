A Psalm of David: Waiting, Deliverance, and a New Song

1I waited with unwavering patience for the LORD, and HeheHe inclined toward me and heard my cry for help. 2He drew me up from the pit of tumult, from the miry clay, and HeheHe set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure. 3He placed a new song in my mouth—a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and stand in awe, and they will place their confidence in the LORD.

Trust, Wonders, and the Incomparability of God

4Blessed is the person who makes the LORD his refuge, who does not turn toward the arrogant or to those who stray into falsehood. 5Many are the wonders YouyouYou have wrought, O the LORD my God, and YouryourYour purposes toward us are beyond reckoning; none can be compared to YouyouYou. Were I to proclaim and recount them, they would elude any complete enumeration.

Obedience, Testimony, and Supplication

6You have not delighted in sacrifice and offering; YouyouYou have opened my ears to hear. You have not demanded burnt offerings and sin offerings. 7Then I said, “Here I am; I have come, as it is written concerning me in the scroll of the book. 8I take delight in doing YouryourYour will, O my God; YouryourYour law resides in the depths of my being.” 9I have proclaimed abundant righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I have not restrained my lips—O the LORD, YouyouYou know it well. 10I have not hidden YouryourYour righteousness within my heart; I have declared YouryourYour faithfulness and YouryourYour deliverance. I have not withheld YouryourYour steadfast love and YouryourYour truth from the great assembly. 11Do not withhold YouryourYour mercy from me, O the LORD; may YouryourYour steadfast love and faithfulness continually guard me. 12For innumerable calamities have encircled me; my iniquities have overtaken me, and I can no longer lift up my eyes. They outnumber the hairs of my head, and my heart fails within me.

Deliverance, Enemies, and the Hope of the Needy

13Be pleased, O the LORD, to deliver me; hasten, O the LORD, to my aid. 14Let those who seek to destroy my life be altogether ashamed and confounded; let those who delight in my harm be turned back and disgraced.

15Let them be appalled because of their disgrace—those who say to me, “Aha, aha!”

16May all who seek YouyouYou rejoice and be glad in YouyouYou; may those who love YouryourYour salvation continually declare, “The LORD be magnified!”

17As for me, I am poor and needy, but the Lord takes thought for me. You are my help and my deliverer; do not delay, O my God.
