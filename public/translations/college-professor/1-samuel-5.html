The Seizure and Installation of the Ark at Ashdod

1The Philistines seized the ark of God and conveyed it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 2Having taken the ark of God, they brought it into the temple of Dagon and installed it beside Dagon.

The Humiliation of Dagon Before the Ark

3The next morning, the Ashdodites discovered Dagon prostrate on the ground before the ark of the LORD, so they raised Dagon and restored him to his place. 4When they rose early the following morning, Dagon was again prostrate before the ark of the LORD, with his head and both hands severed at the threshold; only the torso of Dagon remained. 5Therefore, to this day, the priests of Dagon and all who enter the temple of Dagon in Ashdod do not tread upon the threshold of Dagon.

The Crushing JudgmentJudgementJudgment upon Ashdod

6The hand of the LORD grew heavy against the Ashdodites, and HeheHe devastated them, striking Ashdod and its surrounding territory with tumors. 7When the men of Ashdod perceived this, they said, “The ark of the God of Israel must not dwell among us, for HishisHis hand is harsh against us and against our god Dagon.”

8They sent for and assembled all the lords of the Philistines, asking, “What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?”

The lords replied, “Let the ark of the God of Israel be taken around to Gath.” So they took the ark of the God of Israel there. 9But after they had taken it there, the hand of the LORD was against the city, producing an exceedingly great panic; HeheHe struck the men of the city, from the least to the greatest, and tumors broke out among them.

The Ark at Ekron and the Philistines’ Capitulation

10They then sent the ark of God to Ekron, and as the ark of God arrived, the Ekronites cried out, “They have brought the ark of the God of Israel around to me in order to kill me and my people.”

11So they sent and gathered all the lords of the Philistines and said, “Send away the ark of the God of Israel; let it return to its own place, so that it does not kill me and my people.” For a deadly panic had seized the whole city, and the hand of God was exceedingly heavy there. 12The men who did not die were struck with tumors, and the outcry of the city ascended to Heavenheavenheaven.
