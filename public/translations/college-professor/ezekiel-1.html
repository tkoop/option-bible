Ezekiel's Prophetic Inauguration in Exile

1In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, while I was dwelling among the exiles by the Kebar River, the heavens were opened and I beheld visions of God. 2On the fifth day of the month—it was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin— 3the word of the the LORD came to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans by the Kebar River, and there the hand of the the LORD was upon him.

The Four Living Creatures and the Divine Chariot

4As I looked, behold, a storm wind came from the north: a great cloud, with flashing fire and a radiance encircling it; and from its midst there issued something like the gleam of burnished metal from the heart of the fire. 5Out of its midst came the likeness of four living creatures, and this was their form: they had a human likeness. 6Each had four faces and four wings. 7Their legs were straight, and the soles of their feet were like the soles of a calf's foot; they sparkled like burnished bronze. 8Beneath their wings, on their four sides, they had human hands; and all four possessed faces and wings.

9Their wings touched one another; they did not turn as they went, but each moved straight ahead. 10As for the likeness of their faces, each had a human face; the four had a lion's face on the right, an ox's face on the left, and an eagle's face. 11Their wings were spread upward; two of each were joined to one another, and two covered their bodies. 12Each moved straight ahead; wherever the spirit was to go, they went, without turning in their course.

13As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like torches moving to and fro among the living creatures; the fire was bright, and lightning flashed out from the fire. 14The living creatures darted to and fro like flashes of lightning.

The Wheels and the Spirit of the Living Creatures

15As I looked upon the living creatures, behold, a wheel rested on the earth beside each one, corresponding to its four faces. 16The appearance of the wheels and their workmanship was like the gleam of beryl; the four had one likeness; their appearance and workmanship were as though a wheel were within a wheel. 17When they went, they went on their four sides; they did not turn as they went. 18As for their rims, they were high and awesome, and their rims were full of eyes all around the four of them.

19When the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up. 20Wherever the spirit was to go, they went; there the spirit went; and the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels. 21When those went, these went; when those stood still, these stood still; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

The Throne, the Expanse, and the Glory of the LORD

22Over the heads of the living creatures there was the likeness of an expanse, like the awesome gleam of crystal, stretched out above their heads. 23Under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward another; each had two wings covering its body, and two covering the other side. 24When they went, I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, a tumultuous sound like the sound of an army; when they stood still, they let down their wings. 25And there was a voice from above the expanse that was over their heads; when they stood still, they let down their wings.

26Above the expanse over their heads was the likeness of a throne, in appearance like a sapphire stone; and upon the likeness of the throne was a likeness with the appearance of a man above it. 27From the appearance of HishisHis waist upward I saw something like gleaming metal, like the appearance of fire enclosed all around; and from the appearance of HishisHis waist downward I saw something like the appearance of fire, and there was brightness around HimhimHim. 28Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the the LORD. And when I saw it, I fell upon my face, and I heard a voice speaking.
