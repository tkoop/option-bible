The LORD’s Indictment Against Israel

1Hear the word of the LORD, O children of Israel, for the LORD has a case against the inhabitants of the land: “There is no truth, no steadfast love, and no knowledge of God in the land.” 2Cursing, deception, murder, theft, and adultery proliferate, and bloodshed follows upon bloodshed. 3Therefore the land mourns, and all who dwell in it languish; even the beasts of the field, the birds of the heavens, and the fish of the sea are swept away.

The Priesthood’s Failure and the People’s Ruin

4Yet let no one accuse, and let no one rebuke, for your people are like those who strive with the priest. 5You will stumble in daylight, and the prophet will stumble with you by night; so I will destroy your mother. 6My people are destroyed for want of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I reject you from serving as MymyMy priest; and because you have forgotten the law of your God, I will forget your children.

7The more they multiplied, the more they sinned against MemeMe; they exchanged their GlorygloryGlory for disgrace. 8They feed upon the sin of MymyMy people and set their hearts upon iniquity. 9So it shall be—like people, like priest: I will punish both alike for their ways and repay them according to their deeds.

Idolatry and Its Corrupting Fruit

10They shall eat yet not be satisfied; they shall be promiscuous yet not multiply, because they have forsaken the LORD to devote themselves 11to prostitution, to wine, and to new wine, which steal away understanding. 12My people seek counsel from their wooden idols, and their divining rods answer them; for a spirit of prostitution has led them astray, and they have prostituted themselves against their God.

13They offer sacrifices on the mountaintops and burn incense on the hills, beneath oak, poplar, and terebinth, because their shade is delightful; therefore your daughters prostitute themselves and your brides commit adultery. 14I will not punish your daughters for their prostitution, nor your brides for their adultery, because the men themselves go aside with prostitutes and sacrifice with shrine prostitutes; thus a people without understanding is ruined. 15Though you prostitute yourself, O Israel, let not Judah incur guilt. Do not go to Gilgal, do not ascend to Beth Aven, and do not swear, “As the LORD lives!”

16For Israel is as stubborn as a refractory heifer; how, then, can the LORD shepherd them like a lamb in an open pasture? 17Ephraim is bound to idols; let him alone. 18Their drink is spent, then they turn to prostitution; their rulers dearly love disgrace. 19The wind will wrap them in its wings, and their sacrifices will bring them to shame.
