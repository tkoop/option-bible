1The LORD my God, in YouyouYou I have taken refuge; save me and deliver me from all my pursuers. 2Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it apart, with no one to deliver.

3The LORD my God, if I have done this, if there is iniquity in my hands, 4if I have repaid my ally with evil, or plundered my adversary without cause, 5then let the enemy pursue and overtake my soul, trample my life to the ground, and lay my glory in the dust. Selah.

6Arise, the LORD, in YouryourYour anger; lift YourselfyourselfYourself up against the fury of my adversaries, and awake for me the judgmentjudgementjudgment YouyouYou have commanded. 7Let the congregation of the peoples surround YouyouYou; return, then, and take YouryourYour seat above them. 8The LORD judges the peoples; judge me, the LORD, according to my righteousness and according to my integrity.

9Let the evil of the wicked come to an end, and establish the righteous—O righteous God, who tests hearts and minds. 10My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart. 11God is a righteous judge, a God who is indignant every day.

12If he does not repent, HeheHe will whet HishisHis sword; HeheHe has bent and strung HishisHis bow. 13He has prepared for him the instruments of death; HeheHe fashions HishisHis arrows against the pursuers.

14Behold, he travails with iniquity, conceives mischief, and gives birth to falsehood. 15He has dug a pit and hollowed it out, and has fallen into the hole he made. 16His toil shall return upon his own head, and his violence shall descend upon his crown.

17I will give thanks to the LORD according to HishisHis righteousness, and I will sing praise to the name of the LORD MostmostMost HighhighHigh.
