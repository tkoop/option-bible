16"No one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel or places it beneath a bed; rather, he sets it upon a lampstand, so that those entering may see the light. 17For nothing is concealed that shall not be revealed, nor anything hidden that shall not be known and brought to light. 18Take heed, therefore, how you hear; for to him who has, more shall be given, and from him who does not have, even that which he supposes he has shall be taken away."
