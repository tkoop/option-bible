1The word of the LORD came to me:

The End of the Land Decreed

2“Son of man, this is what the Lord the LORD says to the land of Israel: ‘The end! The end has come upon the four corners of the earth.’ 3The end is now upon you, and I will send MymyMy anger against you; I will judge you according to your ways and bring upon you all your abominations. 4My eye will not spare you, nor will I have pity; for I will bring your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.’”

Calamity Upon Calamity

5“Thus says the Lord the LORD: ‘Disaster upon disaster! Behold, it is coming. 6The end has come! The end has come! It has awakened against you. Behold, it is coming. 7Doom has come upon you, O inhabitant of the land. The time has come; the day is near—panic in the mountains, not a joyful shout. 8Now, very soon, I will pour out MymyMy wrath upon you and spend MymyMy anger against you; I will judge you according to your ways and bring upon you all your abominations. 9My eye will not spare, nor will I have pity. I will repay you according to your ways, and your abominations will be in your midst. Then you will know that I am the the LORD who strikes.’”

The Day of Reckoning

10Behold, the day! Behold, it is coming! The dawn has broken; the rod has budded; arrogance has blossomed. 11Violence has grown into a rod of wickedness. None of them, none of their multitude, none of their wealth, nor anything eminent among them will remain.

12The time has come; the day has arrived. Let not the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn, for wrath is upon all their multitude. 13For the seller will not return to what he sold, even though they are still alive; for the vision concerning all their multitude will not be revoked, and because of their iniquity, no one will preserve his life. 14They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready, but no one goes to battle, for MymyMy wrath is upon all their multitude.

Sword, Famine, and Plague

15The sword is outside, and plague and famine are within. Whoever is in the field will die by the sword, and famine and plague will devour those in the city. 16Their fugitives who escape will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning, each for his own iniquity.

17Every hand will go limp, and every knee will turn to water. 18They will put on sackcloth; horror will cover them; shame will be on every face, and baldness on every head. 19They will throw their silver into the streets, and their gold will be treated as unclean. Their silver and gold will not be able to save them in the day of the the LORD's wrath. They will not satisfy their souls or fill their stomachs, for their wealth was the stumbling block of their iniquity.

The Profanation of the Sanctuary

20They transformed the beauty of HishisHis ornaments into pride and made images of their abominations and detestable things with it; therefore I will make it an unclean thing to them. 21I will give it into the hands of foreigners as plunder, and to the wicked of the earth as spoil, and they will profane it. 22I will turn MymyMy face away from them, and they will profane MymyMy hidden place; robbers will enter it and profane it.

23Forge the chain, for the land is full of bloodshed, and the city is full of violence. 24So I will bring the worst of the nations, and they will possess their houses; I will put an end to the pride of the strong, and their holy places will be profaned.

25Anguish is coming; they will seek peace, but there will be none. 26Disaster upon disaster will come, and rumourrumourrumor upon rumourrumourrumor. They will seek a vision from a prophet, but the law will perish from the priest and counsel from the elders. 27The king will mourn, the prince will be clothed with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land will tremble. I will deal with them according to their conduct, and I will judge them by their own standards; then they will know that I am the the LORD.
