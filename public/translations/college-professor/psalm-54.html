A Prayer for Deliverance from Treacherous Adversaries

1For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments—a Maskil of David—composed when the Ziphites approached Saul and declared, “Is David not concealed among us?” Deliver me, O God, by YouryourYour name, and vindicate me by YouryourYour might.

2Hear my prayer, O God; attend to the utterances of my mouth. 3For strangers have risen against me, and ruthless men seek my life, inasmuch as they have no regard for God. Selah.

4Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul. 5He will repay my enemies with evil; in YouryourYour faithfulness, bring them to ruin.

6I will freely sacrifice to YouyouYou; I will praise YouryourYour name, O the LORD, for it is good. 7For HeheHe has delivered me from every distress, and my eyes have looked in triumph upon my foes.
