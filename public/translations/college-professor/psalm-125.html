A Pilgrimage Hymn of Confidence

1A SongsongSong of Ascents. Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved but abides forever.

2As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds HishisHis people, from this time forth and forevermore.

The Protection of the Righteous

3For the rod of wickedness shall not rest upon the allotment of the righteous, lest the righteous extend their hands unto iniquity.

A Prayer for the Upright and Peace for Israel

4Do good, O the LORD, to the good, and to those upright in their hearts.

5But those who turn aside unto their crooked ways, the LORD will lead away with the workers of iniquity. Peace be upon Israel.
