Flight from the Impending Northern Onslaught

1Flee, sons of Benjamin, from the midst of Jerusalem! Blow the trumpet in Tekoa; raise a signal over Beth-haccerem, for evil looks down from the north, even great destruction. 2Though she is beautiful and delicate, I will destroy the DaughterdaughterDaughter of Zion. 3Shepherds and their flocks will come against her; they will pitch their tents all around her, and each will claim his own portion.

4Consecrate war against her! Arise, let us go up at noon. Woe to us, for the day turns, and the shadows of evening lengthen. 5Arise, let us go up by night and destroy her palaces.

The LORD’s Indictment and the Coming Siege

6For thus says the LORD of hosts: Cut down trees and cast up a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This is the city to be punished; she is full of oppression within her. 7As a well keeps its water cold, so she keeps her wickedness cold; violence and destruction are heard in her; sickness and wounds are continually before MemeMe.

8Be corrected, O Jerusalem, lest MymyMy soul be estranged from you; lest I make you a desolation, a land uninhabited. 9Thus says the LORD of hosts: They shall thoroughly glean the remnant of Israel like a vine; pass your hand again over the branches like a grape gatherer.

10To whom shall I speak and give warning, that they may hear? Behold, their ears are closed, and they cannot listen. Behold, the word of the LORD has become a reproach to them; they have no delight in it. 11I am full of the wrath of the LORD; I am weary of holding it in. Pour it out upon the children in the street and upon the young men gathered together, for both husband and wife shall be taken, the old with those full of days. 12Their houses shall be turned over to others, their fields and wives together, for I will stretch out MymyMy hand against the inhabitants of the land, declares the LORD. 13For from the least to the greatest, everyone is greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, everyone deals falsely.

14They have healed the wound of MymyMy people lightly, saying, “Peace, peace,” when there is no peace. 15Were they ashamed when they committed abomination? No, they were not at all ashamed; they did not know how to blush. Therefore they shall fall among those who fall; at the time I punish them, they shall stumble, says the LORD.

16Thus says the LORD: Stand by the roads and look, and ask for the ancient paths, where the good way is, and walk in it, and find rest for your souls.

But they said, “We will not walk in it.”

17I set watchmen over you, saying, “Pay attention to the sound of the trumpet.”

But they said, “We will not pay attention.”

18Therefore hear, O nations, and know, O congregation, what shall befall them. 19Hear, O EarthEarthearth: behold, I am bringing disaster upon this people, the fruit of their devices, because they have not paid attention to MymyMy words; and as for MymyMy law, they have rejected it. 20What is this to MemeMe, frankincense from Sheba, and sweet cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices do not please MemeMe. 21Therefore thus says the LORD: Behold, I will lay stumbling blocks before this people, and fathers and sons together shall stumble over them; neighbourneighbourneighbor and friend shall perish.

The Northern Host and the Daughter of Zion

22Thus says the LORD: Behold, a people is coming from the north country, a great nation is stirring from the farthest parts of the earth. 23They lay hold of bow and spear; they are cruel and have no mercy; their voice roars like the sea; they ride on horses, set in array as a man for war, against you, O DaughterdaughterDaughter of Zion.

24We have heard the report of it; our hands drop down; anguish has seized us, pain as of a woman in labourlabourlabor.

25Go not out into the field, nor walk on the road, for the sword of the enemy and terror are on every side. 26O DaughterdaughterDaughter of MymyMy people, put on sackcloth and roll in ashes; make mourning as for an only son, most bitter lamentation, for suddenly the destroyer will come upon us.

The Refiner’s Test and the Rejected Silver

27I have made you a tester of metals among MymyMy people, a fortress, that you may know and test their ways. 28They are all stubborn rebels, going about with slanders; they are bronze and iron, all of them corrupt. 29The bellows are scorched; the lead is consumed in the fire; the refining is in vain, and the wicked are not separated. 30They are called rejected silver, for the LORD has rejected them.
