Ezra's Sacerdotal Genealogy and Postexilic Repatriation

1Subsequent to these occurrences, during the reign of Artaxerxes, king of Persia, Ezra—son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah, 2son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub, 3son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth, 4son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki, 5son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest—ascended from Babylon.

6This Ezra undertook the ascent from Babylon as a scribe preeminently proficient in the law of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given; and the king granted him every petition, in accordance with the hand of the LORD his God upon him. 7Moreover, certain of the Israelites, together with priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants, ascended to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

Scribal Erudition and the Zionward Pilgrimage

8He reached Jerusalem in the fifth month, in the seventh year of the king. 9For he had commenced the ascent from Babylon on the first day of the first month, and he arrived in Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month, by the gracious hand of his God upon him. 10For Ezra had devoted his heart to studying the law of the LORD, to practisingpractisingpracticing it, and to teaching in Israel statutes and ordinances.

The Imperial Decree and Cultic Provisions

11This is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe—a scribe of the words of the commandments of the LORD and of HishisHis statutes for Israel:

12“Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven: Greetings. 13I hereby decree that anyone of the people of Israel within my realm, including their priests and Levites, who freely volunteers to accompany you to Jerusalem, may go.

14“For you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to investigate Judah and Jerusalem according to the LawlawLaw of your God that is in your hand, 15and to convey the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem, 16together with all the silver and gold that you may find throughout the province of Babylon, along with the freewill offerings of the people and of the priests who freely offer for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

17“With this money, therefore, you shall diligently purchase bulls, rams, and lambs, together with their grain offerings and drink offerings, and you shall offer them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. 18Whatever seems good to you and to your brothers to do with the remaining silver and gold, you may do according to the will of your God.

19“The vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in full before the God of Jerusalem. 20Whatever else is needed for the house of your God that you may need to provide, provide it from the royal treasury.

21“And I, King Artaxerxes, issue a decree to all the treasurers in the province Beyond the River: Whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, asks of you, let it be done diligently, 22up to one hundred talents of silver, one hundred cors of wheat, one hundred baths of wine, one hundred baths of oil, and salt without prescribed limit.

23“Whatever is commanded by the God of Heavenheavenheaven, let it be done with diligence for the house of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, lest wrath fall upon the realm of the king and his sons. 24We also inform you that it shall not be lawful to impose tribute, custom, or toll upon any of the priests, Levites, singers, doorkeepers, temple servants, or other servants of this house of God.

25“And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges to judge all the people in the province Beyond the River—all who know the laws of your God—and instruct those who do not know them. 26Whoever will not obey the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgmentjudgementjudgment be executed upon him diligently, whether by death, banishment, confiscation of goods, or imprisonment.”

Doxological Thanksgiving and Communal Mobilization

Thus I was strengthened according to the hand of the LORD my God upon me, and I gathered the principal men of Israel to go up with me.
