The Devastation Wrought by the LORD

1Behold, the LORD desolates the earth and renders it a waste; HeheHe distorts its countenance and disperses its inhabitants. 2It shall be as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maidservant, so with her mistress; as with the purchaser, so with the vendor; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the creditor, so with the debtor. 3The EarthEarthearth shall be utterly emptied and utterly despoiled, for the LORD has uttered this word.

4The EarthEarthearth mourns and withers; the world languishes and fades; the exalted among the people of the earth languish. 5The EarthEarthearth is defiled beneath its inhabitants, because they have transgressed the laws, violated the statute, and shattered the everlasting covenant. 6Therefore a curse devours the earth, and its inhabitants are adjudged guilty; hence the inhabitants of the earth are scorched, and few mortals remain.

7The new wine mourns, the vine languishes, and all the merry-hearted sigh. 8The mirth of tambourines ceases, the clamourclamourclamor of the revelers is stilled, and the joy of the lyre ceases. 9They drink no wine with song; strong drink is bitter to its drinkers. 10The city of chaos is shattered; every house is shut up, so that none may enter. 11There is an outcry in the streets over wine; all joy has grown dark; the gladness of the earth is exiled. 12Desolation remains in the city, and the gate is smitten with ruin.

Praise amid Universal Ruin

13For thus it shall be in the midst of the earth among the peoples: as the shaking of an olive tree, as the gleanings when the vintage is ended. 14They lift up their voices and sing; from the west they acclaim the majesty of the LORD. 15Therefore glorify the LORD in the east; extol the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the islands of the sea.

16From the end of the earth we have heard songs: “Glory to the RighteousrighteousRighteous OneoneOne.” But I said, “I waste away, I waste away! Woe to me! The treacherous deal treacherously; indeed, the treacherous deal most treacherously.”

Terror, Pit, and Snare for the Inhabitants of Earth

17Terror and pit and snare are upon you, O inhabitant of the earth. 18It shall be that he who flees from the sound of terror shall fall into the pit, and he who ascends from the pit shall be ensnared; for the windows of Heavenheavenheaven are opened, and the foundations of the earth tremble. 19The EarthEarthearth is utterly broken; the earth is split asunder; the earth is shaken violently. 20The EarthEarthearth reels like a drunkard and sways like a hut; its transgression lies heavy upon it, and it falls, never to rise again.

The Final Overthrow and the Reign of the LORD

21It shall be in that day that the LORD will punish the host of Heavenheavenheaven in Heavenheavenheaven and the kings of the earth on the earth. 22They shall be gathered together as prisoners are gathered in a pit and shut up in prison; and after many days they shall be punished. 23Then the moon will be confounded and the sun ashamed, for the LORD of Hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before HishisHis elders is glory.
