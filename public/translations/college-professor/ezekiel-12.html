Ezekiel Enacts the Coming Exile

1The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, you dwell in the midst of a rebellious house; they have eyes to see, yet they do not see, and ears to hear, yet they do not hear, for they are a rebellious house.

3“And you, son of man, prepare an exile’s baggage for yourself, and go into exile by day before their eyes; from your place you shall remove to another place before their eyes. Perhaps they will perceive, though they are a rebellious house. 4Before their eyes you shall bring out your baggage by day, as baggage for exile, and in the evening you shall go out before their eyes, as those who go out into exile. 5Before their eyes, dig through the wall for yourself, and carry your baggage out through it. 6Before their eyes you shall lift it upon your shoulder and carry it out in the dark; you shall veil your face so that you do not see the land, for I have appointed you a portent to the house of Israel.”

7So I did as I was commanded: I brought out my baggage by day, as baggage for exile, and in the evening I dug through the wall with my hand; I brought it out in the dark, and I bore it upon my shoulder before their eyes.

The Oracle Concerning the Prince

8In the morning the word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 9“Son of man, has not the rebellious house, the house of Israel, said to you, ‘What are you doing?’”

10“Say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: This oracle concerns the prince in Jerusalem and all the house of Israel who are in it.’ 11Say, ‘I am a sign for you: as I have done, so shall it be done to them; they shall go into exile, into captivity.’ 12And the prince who is among them shall bear his baggage upon his shoulder in the dark and go out; they shall dig through the wall to bring him out through it; he shall cover his face, so that he may not see the land with his eyes. 13I will spread MymyMy net over him, and he shall be caught in MymyMy snare; I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, yet he shall not see it, and there he shall die. 14I will scatter to every wind all who are around him, his helpers and all his bands, and I will draw the sword after them. 15Then they shall know that I am the the LORD, when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them through the countries. 16But I will spare a few of them from the sword, from famine, and from pestilence, so that they may recount all their abominations among the nations to which they go, and know that I am the the LORD.”

Famine and Desolation for Jerusalem

17The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 18“Son of man, eat your bread with trembling, and drink your water with quaking and anxiety. 19Say to the people of the land, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They shall eat their bread with anxiety and drink their water in dismay, because the land shall be stripped of its fullness on account of the violence of all who dwell in it. 20The inhabited cities shall be laid waste, and the land shall become a desolation; then you shall know that I am the the LORD.’”

Prophecy No Longer Delayed

21The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 22“Son of man, what is this proverb that you have in the land of Israel, saying, ‘The days are prolonged, and every vision fails’? 23Therefore say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: I will put an end to this proverb, and they shall no longer use it as a proverb in Israel. But say to them, “The days are at hand, and every vision will be fulfilled.”’ 24For there shall no longer be any false vision or flattering divination among the house of Israel. 25For I am the the LORD; I will speak the word that I will speak, and it will come to pass; it will no longer be delayed. For in your days, O rebellious house, I will speak the word and fulfillfulfilfulfill it, declares the Lord the LORD.”

26The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 27“Son of man, behold, the house of Israel says, ‘The vision that he sees is for many days ahead, and he prophesies of distant times.’ 28Therefore say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: None of MymyMy words will be delayed any longer, but the word that I speak will be performed, declares the Lord the LORD.’”
