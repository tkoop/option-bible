The Sign-Child and the Assyrian Flood

1The LORD said to me, “Take a large scroll and write on it with an ordinary stylus, ‘Swift to the spoil, speedy to the plunder.’” 2I had reliable witnesses attest for me—Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah. 3Then I approached the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. The LORD said to me, “Name him Swift to the Spoil, Speedy to the Plunder.” 4For before the boy can say “My father” or “My mother,” the wealth of Damascus and the plunder of Samaria will be carried off to the king of Assyria.

5The LORD spoke to me again, saying: 6“Because this people has rejected the gently flowing waters of Shiloah and exults in Rezin and the son of Remaliah, 7therefore the Lord is about to bring against them the mighty floodwaters of the Euphrates—the king of Assyria with all his glory. It will overflow its channels and run over all its banks. 8It will sweep through Judah, flooding and passing over until it reaches the neck; its outstretched wings will cover the full breadth of your land, O Immanuel.”

The Nations Shattered before Immanuel

9Be shattered, O peoples, and be dismayed; give ear, all you distant lands. Arm yourselves and be dismayed; arm yourselves and be dismayed. 10Devise a plan, but it will be thwarted; propose a word, but it will not stand, for God is with us.

The Lord as Sanctuary, Stumbling Stone, and Sealed Hope

11For the the LORD spoke to me with a strong hand and warned me not to follow the way of this people, saying, 12“Do not say, ‘A conspiracy,’ concerning everything this people calls a conspiracy, and do not fear what they fear, nor be in dread of it. 13The LORD of Hosts—HimhimHim you shall sanctify; HeheHe shall be your fear, and HeheHe shall be your dread. 14He will be a sanctuary, but to both houses of Israel a stone to stumble over and a rock to trip upon, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem a trap and a snare. 15Many will stumble over them; they will fall and be broken; they will be ensnared and captured.” 16Bind up the testimony and seal the law among my disciples. 17I will wait for the the LORD, who hides HishisHis face from the house of Jacob, and I will hope in HimhimHim. 18Here I am, and the children the the LORD has given me, as signs and wonders in Israel from the the LORD of Hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

The Darkness of Rejecting Divine Counsel

19When they say to you, “Inquire of the mediums and the spiritists who whisper and mutter,” should not a people inquire of their God? Should they consult the dead on behalf of the living? 20To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak in accordance with this word, there is no dawn for them. 21They will pass through the land distressed and hungry; and when they are hungry, they will be enraged and will curse their king and their God and look upward. 22Then they will look to the earth and see only distress and darkness, the gloom of anguish; and they will be thrust into thick darkness.
