Standing Firm in the Liberty Christ Has Secured

1For freedom Christ has emancipated us; stand firm, therefore, and do not permit yourselves to be entangled again in a yoke of servitude. 2Mark well: I, Paul, declare to you that if you undergo circumcision, Christ will avail you nothing. 3I solemnly attest once more to every man who submits to circumcision that he becomes obligated to observe the entire LawlawLaw. 4As for you who would be justified by the LawlawLaw, you have been severed from Christ; you have fallen from grace. 5For through the SpiritspiritSpirit, by faith, we eagerly await the hope of righteousness. 6For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision possesses any efficacy whatsoever; rather, faith energized through love is what counts.

7You were progressing admirably; who impeded you from obeying the truth? 8Such persuasion is not from the OneoneOne who calls you. 9A mere modicum of leaven leavens the entire mass. 10I am confident in the Lord concerning you, that you will entertain no contrary conviction; yet whoever is agitating you shall bear his condemnation, whoever he may be. 11And I, brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? In that case the scandal of the cross has been nullified. 12Would that those who are unsettling you would go so far as to emasculate themselves!

Freedom Fulfilled Through Mutual Love

13For you, brothers, were called to freedom; only do not convert that freedom into an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another. 14For the whole LawlawLaw is fulfilled in one word, namely: “You shall love your neighbourneighbourneighbor as yourself.” 15But if you bite and devour one another, beware lest you be consumed by one another.

Walking by the Spirit and the Antithesis of Fleshly Works

16I say, then, walk by the SpiritspiritSpirit, and you will not gratify the desire of the flesh. 17For the flesh craves what is contrary to the SpiritspiritSpirit, and the SpiritspiritSpirit what is contrary to the flesh; these are antagonistic to each other, so that you do not do whatever you wish. 18But if you are led by the SpiritspiritSpirit, you are not under the LawlawLaw.

19Now the works of the flesh are evident: sexual immorality, impurity, and debauchery; 20idolatry, sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalries, dissensions, factions; 21envy, drunkenness, carousing, and the like. I forewarn you, just as I have forewarned you, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

22But the fruit of the SpiritspiritSpirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, 23gentleness, and self-control; against such things there is no law. 24And those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh, together with its passions and desires. 25If we live by the SpiritspiritSpirit, let us also keep in step with the SpiritspiritSpirit. 26Let us not become conceited, provoking one another and envying one another.
