Jesus Heals a Paralytic and Forgives Sins

1A few days later, when Jesus again entered Capernaum, the people heard that HeheHe had come home. 2They gathered in such overwhelming numbers that no space remained, not even at the doorway, and HeheHe proclaimed the word to them. 3Then some men arrived, bearing a paralytic carried by four of them. 4Unable to bring him to Jesus because of the crowd, they uncovered the roof above HimhimHim, and after digging through it they lowered the pallet on which the paralytic lay. 5Seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”

6Now some scribes were sitting there, reasoning in their hearts, 7“Why does this man speak in this manner? He is blaspheming! Who can forgive sins except God alone?”

8Immediately Jesus, perceiving in HishisHis spirit that they were reasoning thus within themselves, said to them, “Why do you reason about these things in your hearts? 9Which is easier: to say to this paralytic, ‘your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Rise, take up your pallet, and walk’? 10But so that you may know that the SonsonSon of ManmanMan has authority on EarthEarthearth to forgive sins”—HeheHe said to the paralytic— 11“I tell you, rise, take up your pallet, and go home.”

12And he rose immediately, took up the pallet, and went out before them all, so that everyone was astonished and glorified God, saying, “We have never seen anything like this!”

Teaching by the Sea and the Call of Levi

13And again HeheHe went out beside the sea; the whole crowd came to HimhimHim, and HeheHe taught them. 14As HeheHe passed by, HeheHe saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax booth, and HeheHe said to him, “Follow MemeMe.” And he rose and followed HimhimHim. 15And as HeheHe reclined at table in his house, many tax collectors and sinners were reclining with Jesus and HishisHis disciples, for there were many, and they followed HimhimHim. 16When the scribes of the Pharisees saw that HeheHe was eating with sinners and tax collectors, they said to HishisHis disciples, “Why does HeheHe eat with tax collectors and sinners?”

17And Jesus, hearing it, said to them, “Those who are well have no need of a physician, but those who are sick. I came not to call the righteous, but sinners.”

Fasting and the Newness of the Kingdom

18Now the disciples of John and the Pharisees were fasting. And they came and said to HimhimHim, “Why do the disciples of John and the disciples of the Pharisees fast, but YouryourYour disciples do not fast?” 19And Jesus said to them, “Can the wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast. 20But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast on that day.

21“No one sews a piece of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; otherwise the patch tears away from it, the new from the old, and a worse tear is made. 22And no one puts new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wine bursts the skins, and the wine is destroyed, and so are the skins. But new wine is for fresh wineskins.”

The Son of Man and the Sabbath

23And it happened that on the Sabbath HeheHe was passing through the grainfields, and HishisHis disciples began to make their way, plucking heads of grain. 24And the Pharisees said to HimhimHim, “Look, why are they doing what is not lawful on the Sabbath?” 25And HeheHe said to them, “Have you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry, he and those with him? 26How he entered the house of God in the days of Abiathar the high priest and ate the bread of the PresencepresencePresence, which it is not lawful for any but the priests to eat, and also gave it to those who were with him?” 27And HeheHe said to them, “The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath. 28So the SonsonSon of ManmanMan is Lord even of the Sabbath.”
