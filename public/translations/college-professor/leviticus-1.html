The Divine Summons from the Tent of Meeting

1The LORD summoned Moses and addressed him from the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, saying,

Burnt Offerings from the Herd

2“Speak to the Israelites and declare to them: Whenever any individual among you presents an offering to the LORD, you shall bring your offering from the domestic livestock—whether from the herd or from the flock. 3If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall present an unblemished male; he shall bring it to the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, so that it may be accepted on his behalf before the LORD. 4He shall impose his hand upon the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be accepted on his behalf, to effect atonement for him.

5“He shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall present the blood and dash it against the altar on every side, at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 6He shall flay the burnt offering and dissect it into its several portions. 7The sons of Aaron the priest shall lay fire upon the altar and arrange wood upon the fire. 8Then Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall arrange the portions—the head and the suet—upon the fire that rests upon the wood that rests upon the altar. 9Its entrails and its shanks he shall wash with water, and the priest shall incinerate the whole of it upon the altar: a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a fragrance pleasing to the LORD.

Burnt Offerings from the Flock

10“If his offering is a burnt offering from the flock—whether from the sheep or from the goats—he shall present an unblemished male. 11He shall slaughter it on the northern side of the altar before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall dash its blood against the altar on every side. 12The priest shall dissect it into its portions and arrange them, together with its head and its fat, upon the fire that is upon the wood that is upon the altar. 13The entrails and the shanks he shall wash with water, and the priest shall present the whole and incinerate it upon the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a fragrance pleasing to the LORD.

Burnt Offerings of Birds

14“If his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he shall bring his offering of turtledoves or young pigeons. 15The priest shall bring it to the altar, wring off its head, and incinerate it upon the altar; its blood shall be drained against the side of the altar. 16He shall remove its crop with its plumage and cast it beside the altar on the east side, at the place of the ashes. 17He shall cleave it open by its wings without severing it, and the priest shall incinerate it upon the altar, upon the wood that is upon the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a fragrance pleasing to the LORD.”
