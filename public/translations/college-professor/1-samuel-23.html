David Delivers Keilah from the Philistines

1David was informed: “The Philistines are fighting against Keilah and plundering the threshing floors.”

2David inquired of the LORD, “Shall I go and attack these Philistines?”

The LORD answered, “Go and attack the Philistines and deliver Keilah.”

3David’s men said to him, “We are already afraid here in Judah; how much more, then, if we go to Keilah to face the Philistine battle lines?”

4David inquired of the LORD again.

The LORD answered him, “Arise, go down to Keilah, for I will deliver the Philistines into your power.”

5So David and his men went to Keilah, fought the Philistines, drove off their livestock, and struck them with a great slaughter; thus David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.

6When Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, he came down with an ephod in his hand.

Saul Learns of David's Refuge at Keilah

7Saul was told that David had come to Keilah, and Saul said, “God has delivered him into my power, for he has shut himself in by entering a town with gates and bars.”

8Saul mobilizedmobilisedmobilized all the people for war, to go down to Keilah and besiege David and his men.

9David perceived that Saul was contriving evil against him, and he said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod here.”

10David said, “O the LORD, God of Israel, YouryourYour servant has certainly heard that Saul seeks to come to Keilah to destroy the city on my account.”

11“Will the citizens of Keilah surrender me to him? Will Saul come down, as YouryourYour servant has heard? O the LORD, God of Israel, please tell YouryourYour servant.”

The LORD said, “He will come down.”

12David asked, “Will the citizens of Keilah surrender me and my men to Saul?”

The LORD said, “They will surrender you.”

13Then David and his men—about six hundred—rose and departed from Keilah, wandering wherever they could. When Saul was told that David had escaped from Keilah, he desisted from marching forth.

David's Sojourn in the Wilderness of Ziph

14David stayed in the wilderness, in the strongholds, and remained in the hill country in the wilderness of Ziph. Saul sought him every day, but God did not deliver him to Saul.

15David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh, and he saw that Saul had come out to seek his life.

16Jonathan, Saul’s son, came to David at Horesh and encouraged him in God.

17He said to him, “Do not be afraid, for my father Saul will not capture you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be second to you; even my father Saul knows this.”

18The two of them made a covenant before the LORD. David remained at Horesh, while Jonathan went home.

The Ziphites' Betrayal and Saul's Pursuit

19The Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah and said, “Is David not hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah, south of Jeshimon? 20Now, O king, come down whenever you wish; our part is to surrender him to the king.”

21Saul said, “May you be blessed by the LORD, for you have had compassion on me.”

22“Go, please, and prepare further, and find out exactly where he is and who has seen him there, for I am told he is very shrewd. 23See and know all the hiding places where he hides, and return to me with reliable information; then I will go with you. If he is in the land, I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah.”

24They set out and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah, south of Jeshimon.

25Saul and his men went to seek David. David was informed, and he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. When Saul heard of it, he pursued David in the wilderness of Maon.

26Saul went along one side of the mountain, and David and his men along the other. David hurried to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were encircling David and his men in order to seize them.

27A messenger came to Saul, saying, “Hurry and come, for the Philistines have raided the land.”

28Saul turned back from pursuing David and went to meet the Philistines. Therefore that place was called the RockrockRock of Escape.

29David went up from there and lived in the strongholds of En-gedi.
