Elihu's Indignation at the Silence of Job's Friends

1When these three men desisted from answering Job, because he was righteous in his own estimation. 2Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the clan of Ram, became incandescent with rage; his wrath blazed against Job because he vindicated himself rather than God. 3His anger also burned against his three friends, because they had found no answer, yet had condemned Job. 4Now Elihu had waited to address Job, because they were older than he in days. 5Yet when Elihu saw that these three men had no answer in their mouths, his anger burned again.

Elihu's Deferential Challenge to the Elders

6“I am young in days, and you are aged; therefore I held back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you. 7I said, ‘Days should speak, and a multitude of years should teach wisdom.’ 8But truly it is spirit in man, and the breath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty gives them understanding. 9It is not the many who are wise, nor do elders understand justice. 10Therefore I say: Hear me; I too will declare my knowledge.

11“Behold, I waited for your words; I listened to your reasonings while you searched for words. 12I paid close attention to you, and behold, none of you convicted Job; none of you answered his words. 13Lest you say, ‘We have found wisdom; God, not man, will thrust him down.’ 14He has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your words.

Elihu's Compulsion to Speak

15“They are dismayed; they answer no more, for words have departed from them. 16I have waited, because they do not speak, because they stand still and answer no more. 17I too will answer my part; I too will declare my knowledge. 18For I am full of words; the spirit within my belly constrains me. 19Behold, my belly is like wine without a vent; like new wineskins, it is ready to burst. 20I will speak, that I may be relieved; I will open my lips and answer. 21I will not show partiality to any man, nor flatter any person. 22For I do not know how to flatter; my MakermakerMaker would soon take me away.”
