The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

1And HeheHe began to speak to them in parables: “A certain proprietor planted a vineyard, enclosed it with a hedge, dug a winepress, built a watchtower, leased it to husbandmen, and journeyed abroad.”

2“At the appointed season he dispatched a servant to the tenants to receive from them a share of the vineyard’s produce. 3They seized him, scourged him, and sent him away empty-handed. 4He sent another servant; they struck him on the head and treated him ignominiously. 5He sent yet another, whom they slew; and of many others, some they beat and some they put to death.”

6“He still had one beloved son, and as a last resort he sent him to them, saying, ‘They will reverence my son.’ 7But those tenants said among themselves, ‘This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and the inheritance shall be ours.’ 8So they seized him, slew him, and cast him outside the vineyard.”
