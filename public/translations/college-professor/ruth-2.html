Naomi's Kinsman and Ruth's Resolve to Glean

1Now Naomi had a kinsman of her husband, a man of eminent standing and martial valourvalourvalor from the clan of Elimelech, whose name was Boaz. 2Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Please allow me to go into the fields and glean among the ears of grain behind anyone in whose eyes I may find favourfavourfavor."

Naomi replied, "Go, my daughter."

3So she went out and entered a field, gleaning behind the reapers; and, as providence would have it, the portion of field she worked belonged to Boaz, who was of the clan of Elimelech.

Boaz's Greeting, Inquiry, and Protection

4And behold, Boaz came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, "ThetheThe LORD be with you!"

They answered him, "ThetheThe LORD bless you!"

5Boaz said to his servant who was set over the reapers, "To whom does this young woman belong?"

6The servant who was set over the reapers answered, "She is the Moabite woman who returned with Naomi from the country of Moab. 7She requested permission to glean and gather among the sheaves behind the reapers; she arrived and has remained here from morning until now, apart from a brief respite in the shelter."

8Then Boaz said to Ruth, "Listen, my daughter: do not go to glean in another field, neither pass beyond this one; instead, cleave to my young women. 9Keep your eyes on the field they are reaping, and follow after them. Have I not charged the young men not to touch you? And when you are thirsty, go to the vessels and drink what the young men have drawn."

10Then she fell on her face and bowed to the ground, and said to him, "Why have I found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, that you should take notice of me, though I am a foreigner?"

11Boaz answered and said to her, "It has been fully reported to me all that you have done for your mother-in-law after the death of your husband: how you left your father and your mother and the land of your birth, and came to a people you did not know before. 12May the LORD repay your work, and may your reward be complete from the LORD, the God of Israel, under whose wings you have come to take refuge."

13Then she said, "May I find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and spoken kindly to your servant, though I am not like one of your servants."

14At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come here and eat some bread, and dip your piece in the vinegar." So she sat beside the reapers, and he offered her roasted grain; she ate until she was satisfied and had some left over.

15When she rose to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, saying, "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not reproach her. 16Also pull out some handfuls for her from the bundles and leave them for her to gather, and do not rebuke her."

The Harvest's Yield and Naomi's Recognition

17So Ruth gleaned in the field until evening; then she beat out what she had gathered, and it was about an ephah of barley.

18She carried it into the city, and her mother-in-law saw how much she had gleaned. Ruth also brought out and gave her what she had left over after she had eaten her fill.

19Her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today, and where did you work? Blessed be the man who took notice of you!"

Then Ruth told her mother-in-law, "ThetheThe name of the man with whom I worked today is Boaz."

20Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "May he be blessed by the LORD, who has not withdrawn HishisHis kindness from the living and the dead. That man is a close relative of ours; he is one of our kinsman-redeemers."

21Ruth the Moabite said that he had also told her to cling to his young men until they had finished all his harvest.

22Naomi said to Ruth, "It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his young women, so that you are not molested in another field."

23So Ruth stayed close to the young women of Boaz to glean until the barley harvest and the wheat harvest were completed; and she dwelt with her mother-in-law.
