The Miraculous Feeding of the Four Thousand

1In those days, when a vast multitude had once more assembled and were destitute of anything to eat, HeheHe summoned HishisHis disciples and addressed them, 2“I am seized with profound compassion for this multitude, because they have already remained in MymyMy company three days and have nothing to eat. 3If I dismiss them to their homes fasting, they will faint upon the journey, and some of them have come from a great distance.”

4His disciples answered HimhimHim, “Whence, in this wilderness, could anyone procure sufficient bread to satiate so great a multitude?”

5He asked them, “How many loaves do you have?” They replied, “Seven.”

6He then directed the multitude to recline upon the earth, and, taking the seven loaves, HeheHe gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to HishisHis disciples to distribute among the people; and they did so. 7They also had a few small fish, and after blessing them HeheHe instructed them to set these before the multitude as well. 8So they ate and were fully satisfied, and they gathered seven baskets of surplus fragments. 9There were about four thousand people, and HeheHe dismissed them.

10Immediately HeheHe embarked in the boat with HishisHis disciples and went to the region of Dalmanutha.

Confrontation with the Pharisees and the Warning against Leaven

14Now they had forgotten to take bread, and except for one loaf they had none with them in the boat. 15He cautioned them, saying, “Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.”

16They began to deliberate among themselves that they had no bread. 17Knowing this, HeheHe said to them, “Why do you deliberate that you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or comprehend? Is your heart obdurate? 18Having eyes, do you not see? Having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember? 19When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of fragments did you take up?” They said to HimhimHim, “Twelve.” 20“And when I broke the seven for the four thousand, how many basketfuls of fragments did you take up?” They said to HimhimHim, “Seven.” 21He said to them, “Do you not yet understand?”

The Healing of the Blind Man at Bethsaida

22They came to Bethsaida, and some people brought a blind man to HimhimHim and begged HimhimHim to touch him. 23Taking the blind man by the hand, HeheHe led him outside the village, and, after spitting on his eyes and laying HishisHis hands on him, HeheHe asked him, “Do you see anything?” 24He looked up and said, “I see people, but they look like trees walking.” 25Then HeheHe laid HishisHis hands on his eyes again, and the man gazed intently and was restored, and he saw everything clearly. 26Jesus sent him to his home, saying, “Do not even enter the village.”
