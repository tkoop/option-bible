Joshua's Farewell Charge to Israel's Leadership

1After a long time had passed since the LORD had granted Israel repose from all their surrounding enemies, Joshua had grown old and advanced in years.

2Joshua summoned all Israel—its elders, leaders, judges, and officers—and addressed them: “I have grown old and advanced in years.

3“You have witnessed everything the LORD your God has wrought upon all these nations for your sake; indeed, it is the LORD your God who has waged war on your behalf.

4“Behold, I have apportioned to you, by your tribes, the surviving nations together with all the peoples I have extirpated from the Jordan to the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea in the west.

5“The LORD your God HimselfhimselfHimself will drive them out before you and dispossess them; you shall inherit their land, precisely as the LORD your God has pledged to you.

Exhortations to Steadfast Obedience and Separation

6“Be exceedingly steadfast, then, in observing and performing all that is inscribed in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses, so that you do not turn aside from it either to the right or to the left.

7“Do not commingle with these surviving nations that dwell among you; do not invoke the names of their deities, nor swear by them, nor render them service, nor bow down to them.

8“Rather, cleave to the LORD your God, as you have done hitherto.

9“For the LORD has dispossessed before you nations great and formidable; to this day no human being has withstood you.

10“A single one of you shall put a thousand to flight, because the LORD your God HimselfhimselfHimself is the OneoneOne who wages war on your behalf, just as HeheHe has declared to you.

11“Be exceedingly vigilant over your souls, then, in loving the LORD your God.

The Peril of Covenant Infidelity and the Certainty of JudgmentJudgementJudgment

12“For if you turn back and attach yourselves to the remnant of these nations that still dwell among you, and contract marriages with them, entering into their midst as they enter into yours,

13“then know with certainty that the LORD your God will no longer dispossess these nations before you; instead, they shall become a snare and a trap to you, a scourge at your sides and barbs in your eyes, until you vanish from this good land that the LORD your God has bestowed upon you.

14“Behold, I am this day proceeding along the way of all the earth; and you know, with all your heart and all your soul, that not a single word among all the good promises the LORD your God spoke concerning you has failed—every one of them has been fulfilled for you; not one has fallen.

15“And just as every good thing the LORD your God promised you has come to pass, so too will the LORD bring upon you every calamity HeheHe has threatened, until HeheHe has eradicated you from this good land that the LORD your God has granted you.

16“If you violate the covenant of the LORD your God that HeheHe enjoined upon you, and go to serve other gods and bow down to them, then the LORD's wrath will blaze against you, and you will swiftly perish from the good land HeheHe has given you.”
