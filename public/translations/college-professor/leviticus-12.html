Purification after Childbirth

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

2“Speak to the Israelites and say: ‘When a woman conceives and gives birth to a son, she shall be ceremonially unclean for seven days, as during the days of her menstrual impurity. 3On the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised. 4Then she shall continue in the blood of her purification for thirty-three days; she shall not touch anything holy, nor shall she enter the sanctuary, until the days of her purification have been fulfilled. 5But if she gives birth to a daughter, she shall be unclean for two weeks, as during her menstruation, and she shall continue in the blood of her purification for sixty-six days.’”

Offerings for Purification

6When the days of her purification have been fulfilled, whether for a son or for a daughter, she shall bring to the priest at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting a year-old lamb for a burnt offering, and a young pigeon or a turtledove for a sin offering. 7He shall present it before the LORD and make atonement for her, so that she may be cleansed from the flow of her blood. This is the law for the woman who gives birth, whether to a male or to a female child.

Alternative Offerings for the Indigent

8If she cannot afford a lamb, then she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for a sin offering, and the priest shall make atonement for her, and she shall be clean.
