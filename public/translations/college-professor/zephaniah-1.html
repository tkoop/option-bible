The Word of the LORD to Zephaniah

1The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

The Universal Extirpation of the Earth

2“I shall utterly extirpate all things from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD. 3“I shall extirpate man and beast alike; I shall extirpate the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, together with the stumbling blocks and the wicked. I shall cut off humankind from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD.

The Imminence and Terror of the Day of the LORD

14The great day of the LORD is near—near and hastening swiftly. The sound of the day of the LORD is bitter; there the mighty warrior cries out. 15That day is a day of wrath, a day of distress and anguish, a day of ruin and devastation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness, 16a day of trumpet blast and battle cry against the fortified cities and against the lofty battlements.

17I shall bring distress upon mankind, so that they shall walk as the blind, because they have sinned against the LORD; their blood shall be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung. 18Neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them on the day of the wrath of the LORD. In the fire of HishisHis jealousy all the earth shall be consumed, for HeheHe will make a full and sudden end of all who dwell upon the earth.
