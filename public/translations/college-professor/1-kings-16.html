Prophetic JudgmentJudgementJudgment against Baasha

1The word of the LORD came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha, saying, 2“Because I exalted you from the dust and made you leader over MymyMy people Israel, yet you have walked in the way of Jeroboam and have caused MymyMy people Israel to sin, provoking MemeMe to anger with their sins, 3behold, I will burn up Baasha and his house, and I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat. 4Whoever of Baasha dies in the city, the dogs will eat; and whoever of him dies in the field, the birds of Heavenheavenheaven will eat.”

5Now the rest of the acts of Baasha, what he did, and his might—are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 6Baasha slept with his fathers and was buried in Tirzah, and his son Elah reigned in his place. 7Moreover, the word of the LORD came by the hand of the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and his house, both because of all the evil he did in the sight of the LORD, provoking HimhimHim to anger by the work of his hands, so that he became like the house of Jeroboam, and because he struck it down.

Elah, Zimri, and the Struggle for the Throne

8In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah son of Baasha became king over Israel in Tirzah, and he reigned two years. 9While he was in Tirzah, drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, who was in charge of the palace at Tirzah, his servant Zimri, commander of half the chariots, conspired against him. 10In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri went in, struck him down, and killed him, and reigned in his place.

11As soon as he began to reign, as soon as he sat on his throne, he struck down all the house of Baasha; he left him not one male, neither his kinsmen nor his friends. 12Thus Zimri destroyed all the house of Baasha, according to the word of the LORD which HeheHe spoke against Baasha by Jehu the prophet, 13for all the sins of Baasha and the sins of Elah his son, which they sinned and by which they made Israel sin, provoking the LORD God of Israel to anger with their worthless idols. 14Now the rest of the acts of Elah, and all that he did, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

15In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri reigned in Tirzah seven days; and the people were encamped against Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines. 16The people in the camp heard that Zimri had conspired and had also struck down the king; therefore all Israel made Omri, the commander of the army, king over Israel that day in the camp.

17Then Omri went up from Gibbethon, and all Israel with him, and they besieged Tirzah. 18When Zimri saw that the city was taken, he went into the citadel of the king's house and burned the king's house over himself with fire, and died, 19because of his sins which he sinned in doing evil in the sight of the LORD, in walking in the way of Jeroboam, and in his sin which he did, making Israel sin. 20Now the rest of the acts of Zimri, and his conspiracy which he made, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

The Division of Israel and Omri's Triumph

21Then the people of Israel were divided into two parts: half the people followed Tibni son of Ginath, to make him king, and half followed Omri. 22But the people who followed Omri prevailed over the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath; so Tibni died, and Omri reigned.

23In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri began to reign over Israel, and he reigned twelve years; he reigned six years in Tirzah. 24He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver, and he built on the hill, and called the name of the city which he built Samaria, after the name of Shemer, owner of the hill.

25Omri did evil in the sight of the LORD, and did worse than all who were before him. 26For he walked in all the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and in his sin by which he made Israel sin, provoking the LORD God of Israel to anger with their worthless idols. 27Now the rest of the acts of Omri that he did, and his might that he showed, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 28Omri slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria, and his son Ahab reigned in his place.

Ahab, Jezebel, and the Apostasy of Israel

29In the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah, Ahab son of Omri began to reign over Israel, and Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years. 30Ahab son of Omri did evil in the sight of the LORD more than all who were before him. 31As though it were a trivial matter for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, he took as wife Jezebel the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians, and went and served Baal and worshiped him.

32He erected an altar for Baal in the house of Baal, which he built in Samaria. 33Ahab also made the Asherah; and Ahab did more to provoke the LORD God of Israel to anger than all the kings of Israel who were before him. 34In his days Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho; he laid its foundation at the cost of Abiram his firstborn, and set up its gates at the cost of Segub his youngest, according to the word of the LORD, which HeheHe spoke by Joshua son of Nun.
