The Altar of Incense

1You shall make an altar for burning incense; you shall make it of acacia wood. 2It shall be square—a cubit long and a cubit wide—and two cubits high; its horns shall be of one piece with it. 3Overlay it with pure gold—its top, its sides all around, its walls, and its horns—and make a gold molding around it.

4Make two gold rings beneath its molding, on its two opposite sides, to serve as housings for the poles by which it shall be borne. 5Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold. 6Place the altar before the veil that is over the ark of the testimony, before the atonement cover that is over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

7Aaron shall burn fragrant incense upon it; every morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it. 8And when Aaron sets up the lamps at twilight, he shall burn it—a perpetual incense before the LORD throughout your generations. 9You shall not offer upon it unauthorized incense, nor burnt offering, nor grain offering; and you shall not pour a drink offering upon it. 10Once a year Aaron shall make atonement on its horns; throughout your generations he shall make atonement upon it once a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonement. It is most holy to the LORD.

The Census Ransom

11The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 12"When you take a census of the Israelites to number them, each man shall give a ransom for his life to the LORD when you number them, so that no plague may come upon them in the numbering. 13This is what everyone who is counted shall give: half a shekel, by the sanctuary shekel—twenty gerahs to the shekel—half a shekel as an offering to the LORD.

14"Everyone who is counted, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the offering of the LORD. 15The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less, than the half shekel, when giving the offering of the LORD to make atonement for your souls. 16You shall take the atonement money from the Israelites and devote it to the service of the tent of meeting; it shall be for the Israelites a memorial before the LORD, to make atonement for your souls."

The Bronze Basin

17The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 18"YouyouYou shall make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing; place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it. 19Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it. 20Whenever they enter the tent of meeting or approach the altar to minister, to burn an offering by fire to the LORD, they shall wash with water, lest they die. 21They shall wash their hands and their feet, so that they will not die; this shall be a perpetual statute for them, for him and his descendants throughout their generations."

The Holy Anointing Oil and Sacred Incense

22The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 23"Take for yourself the finest spices: five hundred shekels of liquid myrrh, half as much—two hundred fifty shekels—of fragrant cinnamon, two hundred fifty shekels of fragrant cane, 24and five hundred shekels of cassia, all by the sanctuary shekel, together with a hin of olive oil. 25Make of these a holy anointing oil, a fragrant compound, the work of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil.

26"With you shall anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony, 27the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, and the altar of incense, 28the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand. 29Sanctify them, and they shall be most holy; whatever touches them shall be holy.

30"You shall anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them to serve as priests to MemeMe. 31And you shall say to the Israelites, 'This shall be a holy anointing oil to MemeMe throughout your generations. 32It shall not be poured upon ordinary flesh, nor shall you make any other like it in composition; it is holy, and it shall be holy to you. 33Whoever compounds any like it, or whoever puts any of it on a stranger, shall be cut off from his people.'"

34ThetheThe LORD said to Moses, "Take for yourself fragrant spices—stacte, onycha, and galbanum—and pure frankincense, all in equal measures. 35Prepare them into incense, a fragrant blend, the work of a perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and holy. 36Beat some of it very fine and place it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you; it shall be most holy to you.

37"You are not to make this incense for yourselves using the same formula. It is to be regarded as holy to the LORD. 38Whoever makes any like it, to smell it, shall be cut off from his people."
