God Has Not Rejected His People

1I inquire, then, whether God has repudiated HishisHis people? Far from it! For I myself am an Israelite, of Abraham's seed and of Benjamin's tribe. 2God has not repudiated HishisHis people, whom HeheHe foreknew. Or do you not know what the ScripturescriptureScripture says in the passage concerning Elijah—how he pleads with God against Israel? 3"Lord, they have killed YouryourYour prophets and torn down YouryourYour altars, and I alone have been left, and they seek my life." 4But what does the divine response say to him? "I have left for MyselfmyselfMyself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal." 5So too, in the present epoch, a remnant has emerged according to the election of grace. 6But if it is by grace, it is no longer on the basis of works; otherwise grace would cease to be grace. 7What, then, is the conclusion? Israel did not attain what it sought, but the elect attained it, while the remainder were hardened. 8As it is written: "God gave them a spirit of stupor—eyes that do not see and ears that do not hear—unto this very day." 9And David declares: "Let their table become a snare and a trap, a stumbling block and a retribution to them; 10let their eyes be darkened so that they do not see, and bend their backs forever."

Israel's Stumbling and the Salvation of the Gentiles

11I ask, then, did they stumble so as to fall irrevocably? Far from it! Rather, through their trespass salvation has come to the Gentiles, so as to provoke them to jealousy. 12Now if their trespass constitutes riches for the world, and their diminution riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their fullness be! 13For I am addressing you Gentiles. Inasmuch as I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I glorify my ministry, 14if perchance I may provoke to jealousy those of my own flesh and save some among them. 15For if their rejection brings about the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?

The Olive Tree, the Mystery, and God's Irrevocable Purpose

16If the firstfruits are holy, the whole lump is holy as well; and if the root is holy, the branches are likewise. 17But if some branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive, were grafted in among them and became a fellow partaker of the root's richness in the olive tree, 18do not vaunt yourself against the branches. But if you do vaunt yourself, you do not bear the root, but the root bears you. 19You will say, then, "Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in." 20Granted: they were broken off through unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be haughty, but fear. 21For if God did not spare the natural branches, neither will HeheHe spare you. 22Behold, therefore, the goodness and the severity of God: severity toward those who have fallen, but the goodness of God toward you, if you abide in HishisHis goodness; otherwise you too will be severed. 23And they, if they do not persist in unbelief, will be grafted in again, for God is able to graft them in. 24For if you were hewn out of the olive tree that is wild by nature and were grafted, contrary to nature, into a cultivated olive, how much more will these natural branches be grafted into their own olive! 25For I do not wish you, brothers, to be uninformed of this mystery, lest you be wise in your own conceits: a partial hardening has befallen Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has entered. 26And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written: "The Deliverer will come out of Zion; HeheHe will turn away ungodliness from Jacob. 27And this is MymyMy covenant with them, when I remove their sins." 28As to the gospel, they are enemies for your sake; but as to election, they are beloved for the fathers' sake. 29For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable. 30For just as you once were disobedient to God but now have been shown mercy through their disobedience, 31so too they have now become disobedient, in order that, through the mercy extended to you, they also may be shown mercy. 32For God has shut up all in disobedience, so that HeheHe might show mercy to all.

The Doxology of God's Unsearchable Wisdom

33O, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments and how inscrutable HishisHis ways! 34"For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been HishisHis counsellorcounsellorcounselor?" 35"Or who has first given to HimhimHim, that he should be recompensed?" 36For from HimhimHim and through HimhimHim and to HimhimHim are all things. To HimhimHim be glory forever. Amen.
