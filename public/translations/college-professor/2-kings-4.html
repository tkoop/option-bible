The Widow’s Oil Is Multiplied

1Now a wife from among the company of the prophets appealed to Elisha, saying, “My husband, your servant, has perished, and you know that I stood in awe of the LORD. Moreover, the creditor has arrived to confiscate my two sons and reduce them to servitude.”

2Elisha said to her, “How am I to assist you? Tell me, what possessions remain in your household?”

She answered, “I have nothing in the house save a flask of oil.”

3He said, “Go and procure empty containers from all your neighbors; do not limit yourself to a scant few.”

4He added, “Then enter, shut the door behind you and your sons, pour the oil into all these vessels, and remove each one once it is filled.”

5So she departed from him and shut the door behind her and her sons. They brought the vessels to her, and she poured.

6When the vessels were full, she said to her son, “Bring me another vessel.”

He said, “No further vessel remains.” Then the oil ceased to flow.

7She came and reported to the man of God. He said, “Go, sell the oil and satisfy your debt, and you and your sons shall subsist upon the surplus.”

Elisha and the Shunammite Woman

8One day Elisha went to Shunem, where a woman of distinction resided, and she importuned him to dine. So whenever he travelledtravelledtraveled that route, he would break his journey there for a meal.

9She said to her husband, “Behold, I perceive that this man who continually passes by us is a holy man of God. 10Let us make a modest chamber on the roof and furnish it for him with a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp, so that whenever he comes to us he may lodge there.”

11One day he came there, withdrew to the upper chamber, and lay down there.

12He said to Gehazi his servant, “Summon this Shunammite.” So he called her, and she stood before him.

13He said to him, “Say now to her, ‘You have extended to us such meticulous solicitude; what is to be done for you? Would you have me speak on your behalf to the king or to the commander of the army?’”

She answered, “I dwell among my own people.”

14He said, “What further benefaction might be rendered to her?”

Gehazi answered, “She has no son, and her husband is aged.”

15He said, “Call her.” So he called her, and she stood at the door.

16He said, “By this time next year you shall be cradling a son.”

She said, “No, my lord, man of God, do not delude me.”

17But the woman conceived and bore a son at that season the next year, exactly as Elisha had spoken to her.

The Death and Restoration of the Shunammite’s Son

18When the child had grown, one day he went out to his father among the harvesters.

19He said to his father, “My head, my head!”

His father said to a servant, “Convey him to his mother.”

20The servant carried him to his mother; he rested upon her knees until noon, and then he died.

21She went up and placed him upon the prophet’s couch, shut the door upon him, and went out.

22She called to her husband and said, “Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, that I may hasten to the man of God and return.”

23He said, “Why do you repair to him this day? It is neither the new moon nor the Sabbath.”

She said, “It is well.”

24Then she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, “Drive on; do not slacken the pace for me unless I tell you.”

25So she set out and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her from a distance, he said to Gehazi his servant, “Behold, the Shunammite approaches.”

26He said, “Run now to meet her, and say to her, ‘Is all well with you? Is all well with your husband? Is all well with the child?’”

She answered, “It is well.”

27When she reached the man of God on the mountain, she seized his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, “Leave her alone, for her spirit is embittered, and the LORD has concealed the matter from me and has not disclosed it to me.”

28She said, “Did I ask my lord for a son? Did I not say, ‘Do not delude me’?”

29He said to Gehazi, “Brace yourself, take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet anyone, do not greet him; if anyone greets you, do not answer; and lay my staff on the child’s face.”

30The mother of the child said, “As the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you.” So he arose and followed her.

31Gehazi went on before them and laid the staff on the child’s face, but there was no sound and no response. So he returned to meet him and reported, “The child has not awakened.”

32When Elisha entered the house, the child lay dead upon his bed.

33He went in and shut the door behind the two of them and prayed to the LORD.

34He went up and lay upon the child, placing his mouth to his mouth, his eyes to his eyes, and his hands to his hands; and as he stretched himself over him, the flesh of the child grew warm.

35Then he returned and paced back and forth in the house; then he went up and stretched himself over him again. The child sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

36He called Gehazi and said, “Summon this Shunammite.” So he called her, and she came to him. Then he said, “Take up your son.”

37She came in, fell at his feet, bowed to the ground, and then took up her son and went out.

Elisha Purifies a Deadly Stew and Feeds a Hundred Men

38Elisha returned to Gilgal, and there was a famine in the land. The sons of the prophets were sitting before him. He said to his servant, “Set the large pot on the fire, and prepare a pottage for the sons of the prophets.”

39One of them went out into the field to gather herbs. He found a wild vine and gathered from it as many wild gourds as his cloak could hold; then he came and sliced them into the pot of stew, not knowing what they were.

40They served it to the men to eat. But as they were eating the stew, they cried out, “There is poison in the pot, man of God!” And they could not eat it.

41He said, “Then fetch meal.” He threw it into the pot and said, “Serve it to the people, that they may eat.” And there was nothing noxious in the pot.

42A man came from Baal-shalishah, bringing to the man of God twenty loaves of barley bread from the firstfruits, and fresh grain in his bag. Elisha said, “Give it to the people, that they may eat.”

43His servant said, “How am I to present this before a hundred men?”

But Elisha said, “Give it to the people, that they may eat, for thus says the LORD, ‘They shall eat and have a surplus.’”

44So he set it before them, and they ate and had a surplus, in accordance with the word of the LORD.
