The Earthly Tent and the Heavenly Edifice

1For we know that should the earthly tabernacle of our sojourn be dismantled, we possess an edifice from God—an eternal house in the heavens, not fashioned by human hands. 2Indeed, within this tent we groan, yearning to be invested with our heavenly habitation, 3provided that, once clothed, we shall not be found naked. 4For those of us dwelling in this tent groan under our burden, inasmuch as we do not desire to be stripped bare, but to be further clothed, so that what is mortal may be engulfed by life. 5Moreover, HeheHe who has fashioned us for this very end is God, who has granted us the SpiritspiritSpirit as an earnest.

Walking by Faith and Appearing Before Christ

6Hence we are ever confident, knowing that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord; 7for we walk by faith, not by sight. 8We are well pleased, indeed, to be absent from the body and present with the Lord. 9Therefore, whether at home or away, we make it our ambition to be well-pleasing to HimhimHim. 10For we must all be manifested before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat of Christ, so that each may receive the recompense for the things done through the body, whether good or evil.

11Knowing, therefore, the fear of the Lord, we persuade men; yet we are manifest to God, and I trust that we are manifest also in your consciences. 12We are not again commending ourselves to you, but we are furnishing you with an occasion for boasting concerning us, so that you may have something to say to those who boast in outward show and not in the heart. 13For if we are out of our minds, it is unto God; if we are sober-minded, it is for your sake.

14For the love of Christ constrains us, because we have concluded that OneoneOne died for all, and therefore all died. 15And HeheHe died for all, so that those who live should no longer live for themselves, but for HimhimHim who died and was raised on their behalf. 16Consequently, from now on we know no one according to the flesh; even if we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we know HimhimHim no longer in that manner.

The New Creation and the Ministry of Reconciliation

17Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation: the old things have passed away; behold, new things have come. 18Now all things are from God, who reconciled us to HimselfhimselfHimself through Christ and gave to us the ministry of reconciliation. 19Namely, God was in Christ reconciling the world to HimselfhimselfHimself, not reckoning their transgressions to them, and having committed to us the word of reconciliation.

20We are therefore ambassadors for Christ, as though God were entreating through us; we implore you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God. 21For HeheHe made HimhimHim who knew no sin to be sin on our behalf, so that we might become the righteousness of God in HimhimHim.
