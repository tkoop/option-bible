The Tongue, Divine Omniscience, and the Heart

1A gentle rejoinder pacifies wrath, whereas a harsh utterance provokes anger. 2The tongue of the wise commends knowledge, but the mouth of fools overflows with folly. 3The eyes of the LORD pervade every place, scrutinizing the wicked and the good alike. 4A healing tongue is a tree of life, yet perversity lodged therein shatters the spirit.

5A fool despises his father’s discipline, but whoever heeds reproof acquires prudence. 6The house of the righteous contains great treasure, whereas the income of the wicked is fraught with trouble. 7The lips of the wise disseminate knowledge; the hearts of fools do not.

Worship, Correction, and the Hidden Heart

8The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is HishisHis delight. 9The LORD abhors the way of the wicked, yet HeheHe loves those who pursue righteousness. 10Grievous discipline befalls him who forsakes the way, and he who hates reproof shall perish. 11Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD; how much more the hearts of human beings!

12A scoffer does not love to be reproved; he will not go to the wise. 13A glad heart brightens the face, but sorrow of heart crushes the spirit. 14The discerning heart seeks knowledge, while the mouth of fools feeds on folly. 15All the days of the afflicted are wretched, but a cheerful heart is a continual feast.

Contentment, Anger, and the Paths of Wisdom

16Better is a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure accompanied by turmoil. 17Better a dish of vegetables where love is than a fattened ox with hatred. 18A wrathful man stirs up strife, but one slow to anger pacifies contention. 19The sluggard’s way is like a hedge of thorns, but the path of the upright is a highway.

20A wise son gladdens his father, but a foolish man despises his mother. 21Folly is joy to one lacking sense, but a man of understanding walks a straight path. 22Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed. 23A man rejoices in an apt reply, and a timely word is exceedingly good. 24The path of life leads upward for the wise, that he may turn away from Sheol below.

Divine Justice, Prayer, and the Fear of the LORD

25The LORD uproots the house of the proud, but HeheHe establishes the widow’s boundary. 26The LORD abominates the thoughts of the wicked, but the words of the pure are pleasing. 27He who is greedy for gain troubles his household, but he who hates bribes shall live. 28The heart of the righteous ponders an answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours forth evil.

29The LORD is far from the wicked, but HeheHe hears the prayer of the righteous. 30The light of the eyes gladdens the heart, and good news nourishes the bones. 31Whoever heeds life-giving reproof will dwell among the wise. 32He who rejects discipline despises himself, but whoever heeds reproof acquires understanding. 33The fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom, and humility comes before honourhonourhonor.
