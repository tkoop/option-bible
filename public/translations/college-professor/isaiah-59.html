The LORD's Omnipotence and the Obstacle of Iniquity

1Behold, the hand of the LORD is not so shortened that it cannot deliver, nor is HishisHis ear so dull that it cannot hear. 2Rather, it is your iniquities that have severed you from your God, and your sins have concealed HishisHis face from you, so that HeheHe does not hear. 3For your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken falsehood, and your tongue mutters injustice. 4No one calls in righteousness, and none pleads in faithfulness; they trust in emptiness and speak vanity; they conceive trouble and beget iniquity. 5They hatch viper's eggs and weave a spider's web; whoever consumes their eggs dies, and a crushed egg hatches forth a viper. 6Their webs shall not become garments, nor shall they cover themselves with their works; their works are works of iniquity, and deeds of violence are in their hands. 7Their feet run to evil, and they hasten to shed innocent blood; their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity; devastation and ruin lie along their paths. 8The way of peace they do not know, and there is no justice in their tracks; they have made crooked paths for themselves, and no one who walks in them knows peace.

The Collective Confession of Transgression

9Therefore justice is far from us, and righteousness does not overtake us; we hope for light, but behold, darkness; for brightness, but we walk in gloom. 10Like the blind we grope along a wall, and as though we had no eyes we grope; we stumble at noon as in twilight; among the vigorous we are as the dead. 11We all growl like bears and moan bitterly like doves; we look for justice, but there is none, for salvation, but it is far from us. 12For our transgressions are multiplied before YouyouYou, and our sins testify against us; for our transgressions are with us, and our iniquities—we know them. 13We have been rebelling and denying the LORD, turning away from our God, speaking oppression and apostasy, conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood. 14So justice is driven back, and righteousness stands at a distance; for truth has stumbled in the street, and uprightness cannot enter. 15Truth is lacking, and whoever turns from evil is plundered. The LORD saw it, and it was evil in HishisHis eyes that there was no justice.

The LORD's Righteous Intervention and Vengeance

16He saw that there was no man, and was appalled that there was no intercessor; so HishisHis own arm brought salvation to HimhimHim, and HishisHis righteousness upheld HimhimHim. 17He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation on HishisHis head; HeheHe put on garments of vengeance for clothing, and wrapped HimselfhimselfHimself in zeal as in a cloak. 18According to their deeds, so will HeheHe repay: wrath to HishisHis adversaries, recompense to HishisHis enemies; to the coastlands HeheHe will render recompense. 19So they shall fear the name of the LORD from the west, and HishisHis glory from the rising of the sun; for HeheHe will come like a rushing torrent, driven by the breath of the LORD.

The Redeemer and the Everlasting Covenant

20"The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from transgression," declares the LORD. 21"And as for MemeMe, this is MymyMy covenant with them," says the LORD: "My SpiritspiritSpirit that is upon you, and MymyMy words that I have put in your mouth, shall not depart from your mouth, nor from the mouth of your offspring, nor from the mouth of your offspring's offspring, from now and forever," says the LORD.
