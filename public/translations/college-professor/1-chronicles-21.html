Satan Provokes David to Number Israel

1Satan stood against Israel and incited David to number Israel. 2David said to Joab and to the commanders of the people, “Go, number Israel from Beersheba to Dan, and bring me their count, that I may know it.”

3Joab said, “May the LORD multiply HishisHis people a hundredfold. Are they not all, my lord the king, your servants? Why then does my lord require this? Why should it become a guilt for Israel?”

4Nevertheless, the king’s word prevailed against Joab; and Joab departed and went throughout all Israel, and came to Jerusalem.

5Joab gave David the number of the muster of the people: all Israel had 1,100,000 men who drew the sword, and Judah had 470,000 men who drew the sword. 6But Levi and Benjamin he did not number among them, for the king’s command was abhorrent to Joab. 7And this thing was evil in the sight of God, and HeheHe struck Israel.

8David said to God, “I have sinned greatly in that I have done this thing; but now, I pray YouyouYou, take away the iniquity of YouryourYour servant, for I have acted very foolishly.”

9The LORD spoke to Gad, David’s seer, saying, 10“Go and speak to David, saying, ‘Thus says the LORD: I am offering you three things; choose one of them for yourself, that I may do it to you.’”

11Gad came to David and said to him, “Thus says the LORD: ‘Choose for yourself. 12If three years of famine; or three months to be swept away before your foes, while the sword of your enemies overtakes you; or three days of the sword of the LORD, even pestilence in the land, with the angel of the LORD destroying throughout all the territory of Israel—now consider what answer I shall return to HimhimHim who sent me.’”

13David said to Gad, “I am in great distress. Let me, I pray, fall into the hand of the LORD, for HishisHis mercies are very great; but let me not fall into the hand of man.”

The Pestilence and the Angel at Jerusalem

14So the LORD sent a pestilence upon Israel, and seventy thousand men of Israel fell. 15God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem; and as he was destroying, the LORD saw, and HeheHe relented of the calamity, and said to the destroying angel, “It is enough; now withdraw your hand.” The angel of the LORD was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

16David lifted up his eyes and saw the angel of the LORD standing between EarthEarthearth and Heavenheavenheaven, with his drawn sword in his hand stretched out over Jerusalem. David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell upon their faces. 17David said to God, “Was it not I who commanded the people to be numbered? I indeed have sinned and done wickedly; but these sheep, what have they done? O the LORD my God, let YouryourYour hand, I pray, be against me and against my father’s house, but not against YouryourYour people, that there be a plague.”

The Threshing Floor of Ornan and the Altar

18Then the angel of the LORD commanded Gad to say to David that David should go up and raise an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 19So David went up at the word of Gad, which he had spoken in the name of the LORD.

20Now Ornan was threshing wheat; and Ornan turned back and saw the angel, and his four sons with him hid themselves. 21As David came to Ornan, Ornan looked and saw David, and went out from the threshing floor and bowed down to David with his face to the ground.

22David said to Ornan, “Give me the site of the threshing floor, that I may build an altar to the LORD on it; give it to me at the full price, that the plague may be restrained from the people.”

23Ornan said to David, “Take it, my lord the king, and do what is good in your eyes. See, I give the oxen for burnt offerings, and the threshing sledges for wood, and the wheat for the grain offering; I give it all.”

24King David said to Ornan, “No, but I will surely buy it for the full price; for I will not take what is yours for the LORD, nor offer a burnt offering without cost.”

25So David gave Ornan six hundred shekels of gold by weight for the site. 26David built an altar to the LORD there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and called on the LORD; and HeheHe answered him with fire from Heavenheavenheaven upon the altar of burnt offering. 27The LORD spoke to the angel, and he put his sword back into its sheath.

28At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he sacrificed there. 29Now the tabernacle of the LORD, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offering, were at that time at the high place in Gibeon. 30But David could not go before it to inquire of God, for he was afraid because of the sword of the angel of the LORD.
