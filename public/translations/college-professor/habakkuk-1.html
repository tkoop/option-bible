The Prophet’s Burden and the Erosion of Justice

1The prophetic oracle that Habakkuk the prophet beheld.

2How long, O the LORD, must I cry for help, but YouyouYou do not listen? I cry out to YouyouYou, “Violence!” but YouyouYou do not rescue? 3Why do YouyouYou cause me to behold iniquity and compel me to gaze upon misery? Ruin and violence confront me; strife and contention arise. 4Therefore the law is enfeebled, and justice never emerges; for the wicked hem in the righteous, so judgmentjudgementjudgment issues forth distorted.

The LORD’s Staggering Reply and the Ascendancy of the Chaldeans

5“Look among the nations and observe; be confounded, utterly bewildered. For I am effecting a work in your days that you would not believe even if it were recounted.”

6For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and impetuous nation, who traverse the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own. 7They are dreaded and fearsome; their justice and sovereignty proceed from themselves. 8Their horses are swifter than leopards, fiercer than wolves of the evening; their horsemen charge ahead, and their riders come from afar, flying like an eagle swooping to devour. 9They all come for violence; their faces press eastward, and they gather captives like sand. 10They scoff at kings, and rulers are a derision to them; they laugh at every fortress, for they heap up EarthEarthearth and capture it. 11Then they surge onward like the wind and pass by—guilty men whose might is their god.

The Prophet’s Second Plea Against the Triumph of the Wicked

12Are YouyouYou not from everlasting, O the LORD my God, my HolyholyHoly OneoneOne? We shall not die. O the LORD, YouyouYou have appointed them for judgmentjudgementjudgment; O RockrockRock, YouyouYou have established them for chastisement. 13Your eyes are too pure to behold evil, and YouyouYou cannot gaze upon iniquity. Why do YouyouYou regard the treacherous and remain silent while the wicked devours one more righteous than himself? 14You make humankind like the fish of the sea, like crawling creatures without a ruler.

15He draws them all up with a hook; he catches them in his net and gathers them in his dragnet; therefore he rejoices and exults. 16Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his dragnet, for by them his portion is fat and his food is rich. 17Will he then empty his net and continue slaughtering nations without pity?
