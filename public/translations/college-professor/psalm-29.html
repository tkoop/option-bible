A Psalm of David: The Thunderous Voice of the LORD

1Attribute to the LORD, O sons of God, attribute to the LORD glory and might. 2Attribute to the LORD the glory due HishisHis name; bow before the LORD in the splendoursplendoursplendor of holiness.

3The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is over many waters. 4The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic.

5The voice of the LORD shatters the cedars; indeed, the LORD splinters the cedars of Lebanon. 6He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox.

7The voice of the LORD hews forth flames of fire. 8The voice of the LORD convulses the wilderness; the LORD convulses the wilderness of Kadesh.

9The voice of the LORD brings the deer to labourlabourlabor and lays the forests bare; and in HishisHis temple all proclaim, "GlorygloryGlory!"

The LORD Enthroned over the Flood and Blessing His People

10The LORD sat enthroned over the flood; the LORD sits as KingkingKing forever. 11The LORD imparts strength to HishisHis people; the LORD blesses HishisHis people with peace.
