No Condemnation and the Liberating Law of the Spirit

1There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. 2For the law of the SpiritspiritSpirit of life in Christ Jesus has liberated you from the law of sin and death. 3For what the law, enfeebled as it was by the flesh, could not accomplish, God accomplished by sending HishisHis own SonsonSon in the likeness of sinful flesh and as a sin offering, thereby condemning sin in the flesh. 4He did so in order that the righteous requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us, who do not conduct ourselves according to the flesh but according to the SpiritspiritSpirit.

5For those who live according to the flesh orient their minds toward the things of the flesh, whereas those who live according to the SpiritspiritSpirit orient their minds toward the things of the SpiritspiritSpirit. 6For the mind set upon the flesh is death, but the mind set upon the SpiritspiritSpirit is life and peace. 7Because the mind set upon the flesh is inimical to God: it does not submit to God's law, nor indeed can it. 8Those who are in the flesh are incapable of pleasing God.

9But you are not in the flesh but in the SpiritspiritSpirit, if indeed the SpiritspiritSpirit of God resides in you. And if anyone does not possess the SpiritspiritSpirit of Christ, that person is not HishisHis. 10But if Christ is in you, although the body is dead because of sin, the SpiritspiritSpirit is life because of righteousness. 11And if the SpiritspiritSpirit of HimhimHim who raised Jesus from the dead resides in you, HeheHe who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also impart life to your mortal frames through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit who resides in you.

Divine Sonship and the Obligations of the Spirit-Led Life

12So then, brothers, we are debtors—not to the flesh, so as to live according to the flesh. 13For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the SpiritspiritSpirit you mortify the deeds of the body, you will live. 14For all who are led by the SpiritspiritSpirit of God are sons of God. 15For you did not receive a spirit of servitude leading again to fear, but you received the SpiritspiritSpirit of adoption, by whom we cry, "Abba, FatherfatherFather." 16The SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God. 17And if children, then heirs—heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ—if indeed we suffer with HimhimHim, so that we may also be glorified with HimhimHim.

The Groaning of Creation and the Spirit's Intercession

18For I consider that the afflictions of the present epoch are not worthy to be compared with the glory that is to be revealed to us. 19For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the sons of God. 20For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but because of HimhimHim who subjected it, in hope 21that the creation itself will be set free from its bondage to decay into the glorious liberty of the children of God.

22For we know that the whole creation groans and travails in birth pangs together until now. 23And not only that, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the SpiritspiritSpirit, groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for adoption as sons, the redemption of our body. 24For in hope we were saved; but hope that is seen is not hope, for who hopes for what he sees? 25But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with patient endurance.

26Likewise the SpiritspiritSpirit also comes to the aid of our weakness; for we do not know what to pray for as we ought, but the SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself intercedes with inarticulate groanings. 27And HeheHe who searches the hearts knows what the mind of the SpiritspiritSpirit is, because HeheHe intercedes for the saints in accordance with God's will.

28And we know that all things cooperate for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to HishisHis purpose. 29For those whom HeheHe foreknew, HeheHe also predestined to be conformed to the image of HishisHis SonsonSon, so that HeheHe might be the firstborn among many brothers. 30And those whom HeheHe predestined, HeheHe also called; and those whom HeheHe called, HeheHe also justified; and those whom HeheHe justified, HeheHe also glorified.

The Invincible Love of God in Christ

31What, then, are we to say to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us? 32He who did not spare HishisHis own SonsonSon, but handed HimhimHim over for us all, how will HeheHe not also, together with HimhimHim, graciously bestow upon us all things? 33Who will lodge an accusation against God's elect? God is the OneoneOne who justifies. 34Who is the one who condemns? Christ Jesus is the OneoneOne who died, and, more than that, was raised; HeheHe is at the right hand of God, and HeheHe also intercedes for us.

35Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword? 36As it is written, "For YouryourYour sake we are being put to death all day long; we were considered as sheep for the slaughter." 37But in all these things we are more than conquerors through HimhimHim who loved us.

38For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers, 39nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
