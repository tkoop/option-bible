The Oracle of Nahum Concerning Nineveh

1An oracle concerning Nineveh: the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

The LORD's Jealous Wrath and Steadfast Goodness

2The LORD is a jealous and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and fierce in wrath. The LORD takes vengeance upon HishisHis adversaries, and HeheHe reserves indignation for HishisHis enemies. 3The LORD is long-suffering and possessed of great might; the LORD will not at all acquit the guilty. His way is in the whirlwind and in the tempest, and the clouds are the dust of HishisHis feet.

4He rebukes the sea and desiccates it, and HeheHe dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel languish, and the blossom of Lebanon withers. 5The mountains tremble before HimhimHim, and the hills dissolve; the earth heaves before HimhimHim, together with the world and all its inhabitants.

6Who can stand before HishisHis indignation? Who can rise up amid the fierceness of HishisHis anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before HimhimHim.

7The LORD is good—a stronghold in the day of distress—and HeheHe knows those who seek refuge in HimhimHim. 8With an overflowing flood HeheHe will bring its place to an utter end, and darkness will pursue HishisHis enemies.

The Oracle Against Nineveh

9What do you devise against the LORD? He is effecting an utter end; affliction shall not rise up a second time. 10For though they are entangled like thorns, and though they are drunk as drunkards, they shall be consumed like stubble fully dried. 11From you has gone forth one who devises evil against the LORD, a counsellorcounsellorcounselor of worthlessness.

12Thus says the LORD: “Though they stand intact and in great numbers, yet they shall be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more. 13And now I will break his yoke from upon you, and I will tear your bonds asunder.”

14The LORD has commanded concerning you: “You shall have no descendants to bear your name. From the house of your gods I will cut off the carved image and the molten image. I will prepare your grave, for you are contemptible.”

Deliverance and Peace for Judah

15Behold, upon the mountains, the feet of a messenger bringing good tidings, announcing peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah; fulfillfulfilfulfill your vows. For the wicked one shall no more pass through you; he has been utterly cut off.
