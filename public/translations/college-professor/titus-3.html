Exhortations to Civic Deference and Universal Benevolence

1Admonish them to be subject to magistrates and authorities, to be obedient, and to remain prepared for every beneficent undertaking; 2to calumniate no one, to be pacific and equitable, exhibiting complete forbearance toward all humanity.

3For we ourselves were formerly insensate, recalcitrant, and led astray, enslaved to multifarious cravings and pleasures, conducting our lives in malice and envy, detestable and mutually abhorrent.
