1But now, Job, please hear my words; listen to all my speech. 2Behold, I have now opened my mouth, and my tongue speaks. 3My words declare the uprightness of my heart, and my lips utter knowledge sincerely. 4The SpiritspiritSpirit of God has made me, and the breath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty gives me life.

5If you can, answer me; take your stand before me; stand up. 6Behold, I am, like you, a mortal before God; I too was formed from clay. 7Behold, my terror shall not make you afraid, nor shall my hand be heavy upon you.

Job’s Protest of Innocence Rehearsed

8Surely you have spoken in my hearing, and I have heard the voice of your words: 9“I am pure, without transgression; I am innocent, and there is no iniquity in me. 10Behold, HeheHe finds pretexts against me; HeheHe counts me as HishisHis enemy. 11He puts my feet in the stocks; HeheHe watches all my paths.”

12Behold, in this you are not right; I will answer you, for God is greater than man. 13Why do you contend against HimhimHim? For HeheHe need not answer for any of HishisHis words.

God’s Warnings, Discipline, and Redemption

14For God speaks once, and again, yet man does not perceive it. 15In a dream, a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls upon men, in slumberings upon the bed, 16then HeheHe opens the ears of men and seals HishisHis instruction upon them through their chastening, 17to turn man from his deed and to hide pride from man. 18He keeps back his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing by the sword.

19He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and with unceasing contention in his bones, 20so that his life loathes bread, and his soul dainty food. 21His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; and his bones, which were not seen, stick out. 22Yea, his soul draws near to the pit, and his life to the destroyers.

23If there be with him an angel, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to declare to man what is right for him, 24then HeheHe is gracious to him and says, “Deliver him from going down to the pit; I have found a ransom.”

25His flesh shall be fresher than a child’s; he shall return to the days of his youth. 26He shall pray to God, and HeheHe will be favourablefavourablefavorable to him; and he shall see HishisHis face with joy, and HeheHe will render to man his righteousness. 27He looks upon men, and if any say, “I have sinned, and perverted that which was right, and it availed me nothing,” 28HeheHe will redeem his soul from going into the pit, and his life shall see the light.

29Behold, all these things God works twice, three times with a man, 30to bring back his soul from the pit, to be enlightened with the light of life.

Elihu’s Final Summons and Offer of Instruction

31Pay attention, O Job, listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. 32If you have anything to say, answer me; speak, for I desire to justify you. 33If not, listen to me; be silent, and I shall teach you wisdom.
