A Song of Ascents; of David

1If the LORD had not been our ally—let Israel now declare— 2if the LORD had not been our ally when mortals rose up against us, 3then, when their wrath flared against us, they would have swallowed us alive.

4Then the waters would have overwhelmed us, and the torrent would have swept over our soul; 5then the arrogant waters would have passed over our soul.

Praise for the LORD's Deliverance

6Blessed be the LORD, who has not consigned us to be prey to their teeth. 7Our soul has escaped like a bird from the fowler's snare; the snare is shattered, and we have been delivered.
