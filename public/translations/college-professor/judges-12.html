The Ephraimite Grievance and Jephthah’s Vindication

1The Ephraimites mustered their forces, crossed northward, and said to Jephthah, “Why did you cross over to wage war against the Ammonites without summoning us to accompany you? We shall burn your house down upon you.”

2Jephthah answered them, “My people and I were embroiled in a grave conflict with the Ammonites, and I did summon you, yet you did not deliver me from their power. 3When I perceived that no deliverance would be forthcoming from you, I staked my life and crossed over against the Ammonites, and the LORD delivered them into my hand. Why, then, have you ascended against me this day to wage war upon me?”

The Gileadite Reprisal and the Shibboleth Ordeal

4Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim; the Gileadites struck them down because the Ephraimites had said, “You Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim—scattered among Ephraim and among Manasseh.”

5The Gileadites seized the fords of the Jordan to intercept the Ephraimites. Whenever an Ephraimite fugitive pleaded, “Let me cross over,” the Gileadites would question him.

They would ask, “Are you an Ephraimite?”

If he answered, “No,” they would proceed to the phonetic test.

6They would say, “Pronounce ‘Shibboleth.’”

He would answer, “Sibboleth,” because he could not articulate the word correctly. Then they seized him and slaughtered him at the fords of the Jordan; at that time forty-two thousand Ephraimites fell.

Jephthah’s Demise and the Succession of Minor Judges

7Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was interred among the cities of Gilead.

8After him, Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel. 9He had thirty sons and thirty daughters; he gave his thirty daughters in marriage outside his household and brought in thirty young women from outside for his sons. He judged Israel seven years. 10Then Ibzan died and was buried at Bethlehem.

11After him, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel; he judged Israel ten years. 12Then Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried at Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

13After him, Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel. 14He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode upon seventy donkeys; he judged Israel eight years. 15Then Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried at Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.
