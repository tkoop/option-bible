Josiah’s Accession and the Extirpation of Idolatry

1Josiah ascended the throne at eight years of age, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. 2He comported himself with rectitude before the LORD, walking in the ways of his father David and deviating neither to the right nor to the left. 3In the eighth year of his reign, though still a youth, he began to seek the God of his father David; and in the twelfth year he initiated the purification of Judah and Jerusalem from the high places, the Asherim, the graven images, and the molten idols.

4Before him they demolished the altars of the Baals, and he hewed down the incense altars that stood above them; he shattered the Asherim, the graven images, and the molten idols, pulverized them, and scattered the dust upon the graves of those who had sacrificed to them. 5He likewise incinerated the bones of the priests upon their altars, thereby purifying Judah and Jerusalem.

6He executed the same reforms in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, Simeon, and as far as Naphtali, even amid their surrounding ruins. 7Throughout the land of Israel he demolished the altars, beat the Asherim and graven images to pieces, ground the idols to powder, and cut down every incense altar; thereafter he returned to Jerusalem.

The Restoration of the Temple and the Discovery of the Law

8In the eighteenth year of his reign, having purified both the land and the temple, he dispatched Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city, and Joah son of Joahaz the recorder, to restore the house of the LORD his God. 9They came to Hilkiah the high priest and delivered the funds that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites who guarded the threshold had amassed from Manasseh, Ephraim, and all the remnant of Israel, as well as from all Judah and Benjamin and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 10They committed these resources to the overseers of the work on the house of the LORD, who in turn allocated them to the artisans laboring in the house of the LORD, both to repair and to fortify it. 11They also gave them to the carpenters and builders for the purchase of quarried stone and timber for joinery and roofing, in order to restore the buildings that the kings of Judah had reduced to ruin.

12The men discharged their labourlabourlabor with fidelity. Over them were appointed as supervisors Jahath and Obadiah, Levites of the sons of Merari, together with Zechariah and Meshullam, Levites of the sons of Kohath; all the Levites were proficient in instrumental music. 13They were likewise placed in charge of the porters and of all who supervised every species of work, while some of the Levites served as scribes, officials, and gatekeepers. 14As they extracted the money that had been brought into the house of the LORD, Hilkiah the priest discovered the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD given through Moses.

15Hilkiah said to Shaphan the scribe, “I have found the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw in the house of the LORD.” And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

16Shaphan conveyed the book to the king and rendered an additional report, saying, “Your servants are executing all that has been committed to their charge. 17They have melted down the money that was found in the house of the LORD and have consigned it to the custody of the supervisors and the workmen.”

18Shaphan the scribe informed the king, “Hilkiah the priest has entrusted me with a book.” Then Shaphan read it aloud in the king’s presence.

19When the king heard the words of the LawlawLaw, he rentletrent his garments.

20The king charged Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king’s servant, 21“Proceed and consult the LORD on my behalf and on behalf of the surviving remnant in Israel and Judah concerning the words of the book that has been found, for formidable is the wrath of the LORD that has been unleashed upon us, inasmuch as our ancestors failed to observe the word of the LORD so as to do all that is prescribed in this book.”

The Oracle Delivered through Huldah the Prophetess

22So Hilkiah and those whom the king had commissioned repaired to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath, son of Hasrah, keeper of the wardrobe—she resided in Jerusalem in the SecondsecondSecond Quarter—and they communicated to her the king’s charge.

23She said to them, “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Convey to the man who dispatched you to MemeMe, 24‘Thus says the LORD: Behold, I am inflicting calamity upon this place and upon its inhabitants—all the imprecations inscribed in the book that they read before the king of Judah. 25Because they have abandoned MemeMe and offered incense to alien deities, provoking MemeMe to anger with all the works of their hands, MymyMy wrath shall be effused upon this place and shall not be extinguished.’”

26“But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, you shall respond thus: Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning the words that you have heard: 27Because your heart was pliant and you abased yourself before MemeMe, rentletrent your garments, and you wept before MemeMe, I too have hearkened to you, declares the LORD. 28Behold, I will gather you to your ancestors, and you shall be gathered to your sepulcher in peace; your eyes shall not behold all the evil that I am bringing upon this place and upon its inhabitants.” And they relayed the response to the king.

The Covenant Renewal and Josiah’s Final Reforms

29Thereupon the king sent and convened all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem. 30The king ascended to the house of the LORD, accompanied by all the men of Judah, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests, the Levites, and all the people, from the most eminent to the least significant; and he read aloud in their presence all the words of the BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.

31The king took his station and ratified a covenant before the LORD to walk in the LORD’s ways, to observe HishisHis commandments, HishisHis testimonies, and HishisHis statutes with entire heart and soul, and to execute the terms of the covenant written in this book. 32He also obliged all who were present in Jerusalem and Benjamin to stand in assent; and the inhabitants of Jerusalem conducted themselves in conformity with the covenant of God, the God of their fathers.

33Josiah eradicated every abomination from all the territories of the Israelites and constrained all who were found in Israel to serve the LORD their God. Throughout his days they did not deviate from following the LORD, the God of their fathers.
