Darius Organizes the Realm and Elevates Daniel

1It pleased Darius to appoint one hundred twenty satraps over the kingdom, stationed throughout the entire realm, 2and over them three chief administrators, among whom Daniel was one, so that these satraps might render account to them and the king suffer no loss. 3Then this Daniel distinguished himself above the administrators and satraps, because an extraordinary spirit was in him, and the king contemplated establishing him over the whole kingdom.

4Thereupon the administrators and satraps sought to discover a pretext against Daniel in the affairs of the kingdom, yet they could find neither corruption nor negligence, since he was faithful and no fault or corruption was found in him. 5Consequently these men said, "We shall find no pretext against this Daniel unless we find it in the law of his God."

The Conspirators' Irrevocable Ordinance

6Then these administrators and satraps thronged to the king and addressed him thus: "O King Darius, live forever! 7All the administrators of the kingdom, the prefects and satraps, the counselors and governors, have conferred together to establish a royal statute and to enforce an interdict: for thirty days, whoever petitions any god or human being except you, O KingkingKing, shall be cast into the den of lions. 8Now, O KingkingKing, establish the interdict and sign the document, so that it cannot be altered, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which does not pass away." 9Therefore King Darius signed the document and the interdict.

Daniel's Defiance and the King's Agony

10When Daniel learned that the document had been signed, he went to his house, where, in his upper chamber, with windows opened toward Jerusalem, he knelt three times each day, prayed, and gave thanks before his God, precisely as he had done before. 11Then these men assembled and found Daniel petitioning and supplicating before his God.

12They approached the king and said concerning the interdict, "Did you not sign an interdict, O KingkingKing, that for thirty days any person who petitions any god or human being except you, O KingkingKing, shall be cast into the den of lions?"

The king answered, "ThetheThe matter is established, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which does not pass away."

13Then they answered before the king, "Daniel, one of the exiles from Judah, pays no heed to you, O KingkingKing, nor to the interdict that you signed, but petitions his God three times a day."

14When the king heard the matter, he was deeply distressed concerning Daniel, and he resolved to deliver him; he labouredlabouredlabored until sunset to rescue him. 15Then these men thronged to the king and said, "Know, O KingkingKing, that it is a law of the Medes and Persians that no interdict or statute which the king establishes may be changed."

Daniel Cast into the Lions' Den

16Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel and cast him into the den of lions. The king said to Daniel, "May your God, whom you serve continually, deliver you!" 17A stone was brought and placed over the mouth of the den, and the king sealed it with his own signet and with the signets of his nobles, so that nothing might be changed concerning Daniel. 18Then the king went to his palace and spent the night fasting; no entertainments were brought before him, and sleep fled from him.

Deliverance at Dawn and the Accusers' Fate

19Then at daybreak, as soon as it was light, the king arose and went in haste to the den of lions. 20As he drew near to the den, he cried out to Daniel with an anguished voice; the king spoke and said, "Daniel, servant of the LivinglivingLiving God, has your God, whom you serve continually, been able to deliver you from the lions?"

21Then Daniel spoke with the king, "O KingkingKing, live forever! 22My God sent HishisHis angel and shut the mouths of the lions, so that they have not harmed me, because I was found innocent before HimhimHim; and also before you, O King, I have done no wrong."

23Then the king was exceedingly glad concerning him, and commanded that Daniel be taken up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no injury was found on him, because he had trusted in his God. 24The king then commanded, and those men who had maliciously accused Daniel were brought and cast into the den of lions—they, their children, and their wives; and before they reached the bottom of the den, the lions overpowered them and crushed all their bones.

Darius's Proclamation and Daniel's Prosperity

25Then King Darius wrote to all peoples, nations, and languages dwelling in all the earth: "May your peace be multiplied. 26I issue a decree that in every dominion of my kingdom people shall fear and tremble before the God of Daniel, for HeheHe is the LivinglivingLiving God and endures forever; HishisHis kingdom shall not be destroyed, and HishisHis dominion shall be to the end. 27He delivers and rescues; HeheHe performs signs and wonders in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth; HeheHe has delivered Daniel from the power of the lions." 28So this Daniel prospered during the reign of Darius and during the reign of Cyrus the Persian.
