The Healing River Flowing from the Sanctuary

1He brought me back to the entrance of the temple, and behold, water was issuing from beneath the threshold of the temple toward the east, for the temple faced east; and the water was descending from beneath the south side of the temple, south of the altar. 2Then he brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around on the outside to the outer gate facing east, and behold, water was trickling from the south side.

3The man went out eastward with a measuring line in his hand, measured a thousand cubits, and led me through the water, which was ankle-deep. 4He measured another thousand cubits and led me through the water, which was knee-deep; then he measured another thousand and led me through, and the water reached my waist. 5He measured yet another thousand, but the river had become an impassable torrent, for the waters had risen into a current deep enough for swimming, a river that could not be forded.

6He said to me, "SonsonSon of man, have you seen this?" Then he led me back to the bank of the river. 7When I returned, behold, on the bank of the river there were very many trees on this side and on that.

8He said to me, "These waters go out toward the eastern region and descend into the Arabah; they enter the sea, and when they flow into the sea, the waters are healed. 9And wherever the river goes, every living creature that swarms shall live, and there shall be very many fish, because these waters go there and are healed; and everything shall live wherever the river goes."

10Fishermen shall stand beside it from En-gedi to En-eglaim; it shall be a place for spreading nets, and its fish shall be of many kinds, like the fish of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea, exceedingly many. 11But its swamps and marshes shall not be healed; they are given over to salt. 12And on the bank of the river, on this side and on that, shall grow every tree for food; its leaf shall not wither, nor shall its fruit fail; it shall bear fresh fruit every month, because its waters flow from the sanctuary; and its fruit shall be for food, and its leaf for healing.

The Boundaries of the Inherited Land

13Thus says the Lord the LORD: "This is the border by which you shall inherit the land according to the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall have two portions. 14You shall inherit it, each one equally with his brother, concerning which I lifted up MymyMy hand to give it to your fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

15"And this is the border of the land: on the north side, from the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea by way of Hethlon to the entrance of Zedad. 16Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim (which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath), and Hazer-hatticon (which is by the border of Hauran). 17The border from the sea shall be Hazar-enan at the north border of Damascus, with the border of Hamath to the north; this is the north side.

18"On the east side, from between Hauran and Damascus, along the Jordan, between Gilead and the land of Israel, to the eastern sea, to Tamar; this is the east side. 19On the south side, from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, along the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt to the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea; this is the south side toward the south. 20And the west side shall be the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea from the border as far as opposite the entrance of Hamath; this is the west side."

The Division of the Land among the Tribes and the Sojourners

21"So you shall divide this land among yourselves according to the tribes of Israel.

22You shall allot it as an inheritance to yourselves and to the sojourners who reside among you and who have begotten children among you; they shall be to you as native-born among the children of Israel, and with you they shall fall by lot for an inheritance among the tribes of Israel. 23In whatever tribe the sojourner resides, there you shall give him his inheritance, declares the Lord the LORD."
