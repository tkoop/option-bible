The Collapse of Egyptian Wisdom

11The princes of Zoan are fools, and Pharaoh's wise counselors proffer witless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, "I am a son of the wise, a son of ancient kings"? 12Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and disclose what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.

13The princes of Zoan have become fools; the princes of Memphis are deceived, and the leaders of her tribes have led Egypt astray. 14The LORD has poured out upon her a spirit of confusion, so that they have made Egypt stagger in all her undertakings, as a drunkard staggers in his own vomit. 15There will be nothing Egypt can accomplish—neither by its leaders nor by its common people, neither by the strong nor by the weak.
