The Sower by the Sea

1Again HeheHe began to teach beside the sea; and an inordinately vast multitude congregated unto HimhimHim, so that HeheHe embarked in a boat and sat upon the sea, while the entire multitude stood upon the land beside the sea. 2And HeheHe instructed them in many things through parables, and in HishisHis teaching HeheHe said to them:

3"Listen! Behold, the sower went forth to sow. 4And it came to pass, in the act of sowing, that some fell beside the road, and the birds came and devoured it. 5And other seed fell upon rocky ground, where it possessed scant soil; and immediately it sprang up, because it lacked depth of soil. 6But when the sun arose, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered. 7And other seed fell among thorns; and the thorns ascended and choked it, and it yielded no fruit. 8And others fell upon good soil, and, springing up and growing, yielded fruit—thirtyfold, sixtyfold, and a hundredfold. 9And HeheHe said, 'He who has ears to hear, let him hear.'"

The Mystery of the Kingdom Explained

10And when HeheHe was alone, those about HimhimHim, together with the TwelvetwelveTwelve, inquired of HimhimHim concerning the parables.

11And HeheHe said to them, "To you has been given the mystery of the kingdom of God; but to those outside, all things occur in parables; 12so that seeing they may see, and yet not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and yet not understand; lest they should turn, and be forgiven."

13"Do you not know this parable? And how will you comprehend all the parables? 14The sower sows the word. 15And these are the ones beside the road where the word is sown: when they hear, Satan immediately comes and snatches away the word sown in them.

16"And these, likewise, are the ones sown on rocky ground, who, when they hear the word, immediately receive it with joy. 17Yet they have no root in themselves, but are ephemeral; then, when tribulation or persecution arises on account of the word, immediately they stumble. 18And others are the ones sown among thorns: these are those who hear the word, 19but the cares of the age, the deceitfulness of riches, and the desires for other things, entering in, choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. 20And these are the ones sown on good soil: those who hear the word, receive it, and bear fruit—thirtyfold, sixtyfold, and a hundredfold."

Further Parables of the Kingdom

21"Does a lamp come in to be put under a basket or under a bed? Is it not to be put on a lampstand? 22For there is nothing hidden except to be revealed, nor has anything become secret except that it should come into the open. 23If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear."

24"Take heed what you hear: with the measure you measure, it shall be measured to you, and more shall be added to you. 25For whoever has, to him shall be given; and whoever does not have, even what he has shall be taken from him."

26"The kingdom of God is like this: as a man casts seed upon the earth, 27and sleeps and rises night and day, while the seed sprouts and grows—he knows not how. 28The EarthEarthearth bears fruit of itself: first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear. 29But when the fruit ripens, immediately he sends the sickle, because the harvest has come."

30"How shall we liken the kingdom of God, or in what parable shall we set it? 31It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when sown upon the earth, is smaller than all the seeds on the earth; 32yet when sown it grows and becomes greater than all the garden plants, and makes large branches, so that the birds of Heavenheavenheaven can nest under its shade."

33And with many such parables HeheHe spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear. 34And without a parable HeheHe did not speak to them; but privately HeheHe expounded all things to HishisHis own disciples.

Jesus Calms the Storm

35And on that day, when evening had come, HeheHe says to them, "Let us pass over to the other side."

36And leaving the crowd, they take HimhimHim, just as HeheHe was, in the boat; and other boats were with HimhimHim.

37And a great storm of wind arises, and the waves break into the boat, so that the boat is already filling. 38And HeheHe HimselfhimselfHimself was in the stern, sleeping on the cushion; and they wake HimhimHim and say to HimhimHim, "TeacherteacherTeacher, do YouyouYou not care that we are perishing?"

39And being awakened, HeheHe rebuked the wind and the sea: "Be silent! Be muzzled!" And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.

40And HeheHe said to them, "Why are you cowardly? Do you not yet have faith?"

41And they feared with great fear, and said to one another, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey HimhimHim?"
