The Prophet's Trembling and Resolute Faith

16I heard, and my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the sound; decay entered my bones; I trembled where I stood. Yet I will wait in quietness for the day of distress to come upon the nation that invades us.

17Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls, 18yet I will rejoice in the LORD; I will exult in the God of my salvation. 19The LORD God is my strength; HeheHe makes my feet like those of a deer and enables me to walk on the heights.
