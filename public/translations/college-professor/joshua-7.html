Israel's Unfaithfulness and the Defeat at Ai

1But the Israelites acted unfaithfully concerning the devoted things: Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what had been devoted, and the LORD's wrath ignited against the Israelites. 2Joshua, meanwhile, dispatched men from Jericho to Ai, which lies near Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and instructed them, "Ascend and reconnoiter the land." The men ascended and reconnoitered Ai. 3Upon their return to Joshua, they reported, "Let not the whole people ascend; rather, let about two or three thousand men go up and strike Ai, so that you do not weary the entire people, for the men of Ai are few." 4So roughly three thousand men went up, yet they fled before the men of Ai. 5The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them and pursued them from the gate as far as Shebarim, striking them down on the descent; and the courage of the people melted and became as water.

Joshua's Lament and the LORD's Rebuke

6Then Joshua tore his garments and fell on his face to the earth before the ark of the LORD until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they put dust upon their heads. 7Joshua said, "Ah, the LORD, why did YouyouYou ever bring this people across the Jordan, only to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites for destruction? Would that we had been content to remain beyond the Jordan! 8O the LORD, what shall I say, now that Israel has turned its back before its enemies? 9When the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land hear of it, they will surround us and cut off our name from the earth; and what will YouyouYou do for YouryourYour great name?"

10The LORD said to Joshua, "Rise! Why have you fallen on your face? 11Israel has sinned; they have transgressed MymyMy covenant that I commanded them; they have taken some of the devoted things; they have stolen, acted deceitfully, and placed them among their own baggage. 12Therefore the Israelites cannot stand before their enemies; they turn their backs before their enemies, because they have become devoted to destruction. I will no longer be with you unless you destroy the devoted things from among you. 13Rise, consecrate the people, and say, 'Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for thus says the LORD God of Israel: There is a devoted thing in your midst, O Israel; you cannot stand before your enemies until you remove the devoted thing from your midst.' 14In the morning you shall come near by your tribes; and the tribe that the LORD takes shall come near by families; and the family that the LORD takes shall come near by households; and the household that the LORD takes shall come near man by man. 15And he who is taken with the devoted thing shall be burned with fire, he and all that belongs to him, because he has transgressed the covenant of the LORD and committed a disgraceful thing in Israel."

The Sacred Lot and Achan's Confession

16So Joshua rose early in the morning and brought Israel near by tribes, and the tribe of Judah was taken. 17He brought near the clan of Judah, and the clan of the Zerahites was taken; then he brought near the clan of the Zerahites man by man, and Zabdi was taken. 18He brought near his household man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken. 19Joshua said to Achan, "MymyMy son, give glory to the LORD God of Israel and give HimhimHim praise. Tell me now what you have done; do not hide it from me." 20Achan answered Joshua, "Truly I have sinned against the LORD God of Israel, and this is what I did: 21I saw among the spoil a sumptuous Shinar cloak, two hundred shekels of silver, and a gold bar weighing fifty shekels; I coveted them and took them. Behold, they are hidden in the earth in the midst of my tent, with the silver beneath it." 22Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent; and behold, it was hidden in his tent, with the silver beneath it. 23They took them from the tent and brought them to Joshua and all Israel, and laid them out before the LORD.

24Joshua and all Israel with him took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, the gold bar, his sons, his daughters, his ox, his donkey, his sheep, his tent, and all that he had, and brought them up to the ValleyvalleyValley of Achor. 25Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble upon us? The LORD will trouble you this day." And all Israel stoned him with stones and burned them with fire. 26They raised over him a great heap of stones, which remains to this day. Then the LORD turned from the fierceness of HishisHis anger. Therefore that place has been called the ValleyvalleyValley of Achor to this day.
