The Accession of Abijah and the Outbreak of War

1In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, Abijah became king over Judah. 2He reigned in Jerusalem for three years; his mother was Micaiah, daughter of Uriel, from Gibeah, and war persisted between Abijah and Jeroboam. 3Abijah marshaled for battle four hundred thousand chosen men, valiant warriors, whereas Jeroboam arrayed against him eight hundred thousand selected men, mighty warriors of valourvalourvalor.

Abijah's Remonstrance from Mount Zemaraim

4Then Abijah ascended Mount Zemaraim in the hill country of Ephraim and declared, "Hear me, Jeroboam and all Israel! 5Do you not know that the LORD, the God of Israel, conferred the kingdom upon David and his sons forever by an enduring covenant? 6Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, a servant of Solomon son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master. 7Worthless and wicked men gathered about him, strengthening themselves against Rehoboam son of Solomon, who was then young and tender-hearted and could not withstand them. 8And now you imagine that you can withstand the kingdom of the LORD, which rests in the hands of David's sons; indeed, you are a vast multitude, and you have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam fashioned for you as gods. 9Have you not driven out the priests of the LORD, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and made priests for yourselves after the manner of the peoples of other lands? Anyone who comes to consecrate himself with a young bull and seven rams becomes a priest of things that are not gods. 10But as for us, the LORD is our God, and we have not forsaken HimhimHim; the priests who minister to the LORD are the sons of Aaron, and the Levites attend to their duties. 11Morning by morning and evening by evening they offer burnt offerings and fragrant incense to the LORD, arrange the rows of bread upon the ceremonially clean table, and light the lamps of the golden lampstand in the evening; for we are keeping the charge of the LORD our God, while you have forsaken HimhimHim. 12See, God is with us at our head, and HishisHis priests with the trumpets are to sound the alarm against you. O children of Israel, do not wage war against the LORD, the God of your fathers, for you will not prosper."

The Battle Turns and Jeroboam's Army Is Routed

13Meanwhile, Jeroboam had sent an ambush around behind them, so that while his forces were before Judah, the ambush lay in their rear. 14When Judah turned and beheld battle on both front and rear, they cried out to the LORD, and the priests sounded the trumpets. 15Then the men of Judah raised the battle cry, and as they shouted, God struck down Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah. 16The Israelites fled before Judah, and God delivered them into Judah's hand. 17Abijah and his people inflicted a devastating slaughter upon them, and five hundred thousand chosen men of Israel fell slain. 18Thus were the Israelites humbled at that time, while the men of Judah prevailed, because they relied upon the LORD, the God of their fathers.

Abijah's Triumphs, Family, and Recorded Acts

19Abijah pursued Jeroboam and captured cities from him—Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron, together with their villages. 20Jeroboam never recovered his strength during Abijah's days; the LORD struck him, and he died. 21Abijah, however, grew mighty: he took fourteen wives and begot twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters. 22The remainder of Abijah's acts, his ways and his words, are recorded in the TreatisetreatiseTreatise of the ProphetprophetProphet Iddo.
