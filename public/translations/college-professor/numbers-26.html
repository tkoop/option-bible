Census Commanded on the Plains of Moab

1After the plague had run its course, the LORD spoke to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, saying, 2“Take a census of the whole congregation of the Israelites according to their ancestral houses, counting every male twenty years of age and older who is able to bear arms in Israel.” 3This took place on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan near Jericho, where Moses and Eleazar the priest addressed the people, saying, 4“Count those twenty years old and upward, just as the LORD commanded Moses concerning the Israelites who came out of Egypt.”

The Enrollment of Israel’s Tribes

5Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, comprised four clans: the Hanochites, from Hanoch; the Palluites, from Pallu; 6the Hezronites, from Hezron; and the Carmiites, from Carmi. 7The Reubenite enrollment was 43,730. 8Pallu’s son was Eliab, 9whose sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram—the same Dathan and Abiram summoned by the assembly, who contended against Moses and Aaron in Korah’s company when they rebelled against the LORD. 10The EarthEarthearth opened its mouth and swallowed them along with Korah, while the company perished as fire consumed 250 men, so that they became a warning. 11The sons of Korah, however, did not die.

12The Simeonites by their clans were the Nemuelites, from Nemuel; the Jaminites, from Jamin; the Jachinites, from Jachin; 13the Zerahites, from Zerah; and the Shaulites, from Shaul. 14Their enrollment was 22,200.

15The Gadites by their clans were the Zephonites, from Zephon; the Haggites, from Haggi; the Shunites, from Shuni; 16the Oznites, from Ozni; the Erites, from Eri; 17the Arodites, from Arod; and the Areelites, from Areli. 18Their enrollment was 40,500.

19Judah’s sons Er and Onan died in Canaan. 20The Judahite clans were the Shelanites, from Shelah; the Perezites, from Perez; and the Zerahites, from Zerah. 21The Perezites comprised the Hezronites, from Hezron, and the Hamulites, from Hamul. 22Their enrollment was 76,500.

23The Issacharites by their clans were the Tolaites, from Tola; the Punites, from Puvah; 24the Jashubites, from Jashub; and the Shimronites, from Shimron. 25Their enrollment was 64,300.

26The Zebulunites by their clans were the Seredites, from Sered; the Elonites, from Elon; and the Jahleelites, from Jahleel. 27Their enrollment was 60,500.

28Joseph’s descendants were Manasseh and Ephraim. 29The Manassites by their clans were the Machirites, from Machir; Machir fathered Gilead, and the Gileadites descended from Gilead. 30Gilead’s sons included the Iezerites, from Iezer; the Helekites, from Helek; 31the Asrielites, from Asriel; the Shechemites, from Shechem; 32the Shemidaites, from Shemida; and the Hepherites, from Hepher. 33Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters—Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 34The Manassite enrollment was 52,700.

35The Ephraimites by their clans were the Shuthelahites, from Shuthelah; the Becherites, from Becher; and the Tahanites, from Tahan. 36Shuthelah’s son was Eran, ancestor of the Eranites. 37These were Joseph’s descendants by their clans; the Ephraimite enrollment was 32,500.

38The Benjaminites by their clans were the Belaites, from Bela; the Ashbelites, from Ashbel; and the Ahiramites, from Ahiram. 39They also included the Shuphamites, from Shupham, and the Huphamites, from Hupham. 40Bela’s sons were Ard and Naaman, ancestors of the Ardites and the Naamites. 41The Benjaminite enrollment was 45,600.

42The Danites by their clans were the Shuhamites, from Shuham. 43Their enrollment was 64,400.

44The Asherites by their clans were the Imnites, from Imnah; the Ishvites, from Ishvi; and the Beriites, from Beriah. 45Beriah’s descendants were the Heberites, from Heber, and the Malchielites, from Malchiel. 46Asher had a daughter named Serah. 47The Asherite enrollment was 53,400.

48The Naphtali clans by their families were the Jahzeelites, from Jahzeel; the Gunites, from Guni; 49the Jezerites, from Jezer; and the Shillemites, from Shillem. 50Their enrollment was 45,400. 51Thus the total enrollment of the Israelites was 601,730.

Inheritance Apportioned by Population and Lot

52Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 53“This land is to be divided among them as an inheritance, in proportion to the number of names. 54To the more numerous you shall give a larger inheritance, and to the fewer a smaller one; each tribe’s inheritance shall correspond to its enrollment. 55Nevertheless, the land must be divided by lot; they shall inherit according to the names of their ancestral tribes. 56Whether the group is large or small, its inheritance shall be determined by lot.”

Levites Counted Apart and the Passing of the Sinai Generation

57The Levites were counted by their clans: the Gershonites, from Gershon; the Kohathites, from Kohath; and the Merarites, from Merari. 58The Levite families included the Libnites, the Hebronites, the Mahlites, the Mushites, and the Korahites. Kohath fathered Amram, 59whose wife was Jochebed, a daughter of Levi who had been born to Levi in Egypt; she bore Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam. 60To Aaron were born Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 61Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before the LORD. 62The Levite enrollment was 23,000, counting every male one month old and older; they were not included among the Israelites, for no inheritance was allotted to them among the Israelites.

63These are the enrollments that Moses and Eleazar the priest recorded when they counted the Israelites on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan at Jericho. 64Among them there was not one man from the earlier enrollment that Moses and Aaron the priest had made in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Sinai, 65for the LORD had said that they would surely die in the wilderness. Not one of them survived except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
