The Prophetic Word in the Days of the Kings

1The word of the LORD that came to Hosea, son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

The Command to Take a Wife of Harlotry

2At the commencement of the LORD’s speaking through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a wife devoted to harlotry, together with children of harlotry, for the land itself has practisedpractisedpracticed egregious whoredom, forsaking the LORD.” 3Accordingly, he went and took Gomer, daughter of Diblaim; and she conceived and bore him a son.

4Then the LORD said to him, “Name him Jezreel, for in but a little while I will requite the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and I will terminate the kingdom of the house of Israel. 5And on that day I will shatter the bow of Israel in the ValleyvalleyValley of Jezreel.”
