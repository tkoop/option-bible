A Prayer for Deliverance from Violent and Deceitful Enemies

1Rescue me, O the LORD, from evil; guard me from men of violence, 2who contrive iniquity within their hearts and foment strife without ceasing. 3They whet their tongues like a serpent's; the venom of asps lies beneath their lips. Selah.

4Keep me, O the LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from men of violence, who plot to make my steps falter. 5The arrogant have concealed a snare for me; they have spread cords as a net; they have laid traps beside my path. Selah.

The Psalmist's Appeal for Divine Protection

6I have said to the LORD, "YouyouYou are my God." Hear, O the LORD, the voice of my supplications. 7O the LORD, my Lord, the strength of my salvation, YouyouYou shield my head in the day of battle.

Imprecation against the Wicked

8Do not grant, O the LORD, the cravings of the wicked; do not let their schemes prosper, lest they exalt themselves. Selah. 9May the heads of those who surround me be enveloped in the trouble their lips have wrought. 10May burning coals be heaped upon them; may they be cast into the fire, into deep mire, from which they cannot arise. 11Let not the slanderer be established in the land; let evil hunt down the violent man without relenting.

Confidence in Divine Justice and Praise

12I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted and execute justice for the poor. 13Surely the righteous will give thanks to your name; the upright shall dwell before your presence.
