The Human High Priesthood and Its Limitations

1Indeed, every high priest is appointed from among human beings and acts on their behalf in matters relating to God, so that he may present both gifts and sacrifices for sins. 2He is able to deal gently with those who are ignorant and astray, since he himself is likewise encompassed by weakness. 3Consequently, he is obligated to offer sacrifices for sins on his own behalf as well as on behalf of the people. 4Moreover, no one arrogates this honourhonourhonor to himself; rather, one receives it only when summoned by God, just as Aaron was.

The Call to Spiritual Maturity

11Concerning HimhimHim we have a great deal to say, and it is difficult to articulate, since you have become sluggish in hearing. 12For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you have need again for someone to teach you the elementary principles of the oracles of God, and you have come to need milk and not solid food. 13For everyone who partakes of milk is unskilled in the word of righteousness, for he is an infant. 14But solid food belongs to the mature, whose faculties, through habitual exercise, have been trained to distinguish good from evil.
