A Psalm of David: The Elevation of the Soul unto the the LORD

1Unto YouyouYou, O the LORD, do I lift up my very soul. 2In YouyouYou, O my God, have I reposed my trust; let me not be brought to shame, neither let my adversaries exult triumphantly over me. 3Yea, none who wait upon YouyouYou shall suffer disgrace; but those who practice perfidy without provocation shall be put to shame.

4Make known to me YouryourYour ways, O the LORD, and instruct me in YouryourYour paths. 5Lead me in YouryourYour truth and teach me, for YouyouYou are the God of my salvation; the whole day long I wait upon YouyouYou. 6Remember, O the LORD, YouryourYour tender mercies and YouryourYour steadfast love, for they are from everlasting. 7Remember not the transgressions of my youth, nor my rebellious deeds; according to YouryourYour steadfast love remember me, for the sake of YouryourYour goodness, O the LORD.

8The ThetheThe LORD is good and upright; therefore HeheHe instructs sinners in the way. 9He guides the meek in judgmentjudgementjudgment and teaches the meek HishisHis way. 10All the paths of the the LORD are steadfast love and faithfulness to those who keep HishisHis covenant and HishisHis testimonies. 11For YouryourYour name’s sake, O the LORD, pardon my iniquity, for it is great.

12Who, then, is the man that fears the the LORD? Him will HeheHe instruct in the way he should choose. 13His soul shall dwell in prosperity, and his offspring shall inherit the land. 14The ThetheThe LORD takes into HishisHis confidence those who fear HimhimHim, and makes known to them HishisHis covenant. 15My eyes are ever toward the the LORD, for HeheHe shall pluck my feet out of the net.

16Turn to me and be gracious to me, for I am solitary and afflicted. 17The distresses of my heart are enlarged; bring me forth out of my straits. 18Consider my affliction and my toil, and forgive all my sins. 19Behold how numerous are my enemies, and with cruel hatred they detest me. 20Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I have taken refuge in YouyouYou. 21Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for YouyouYou. 22Redeem Israel, O God, from all its troubles.
