The Vision of the Restored Temple

1In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city had been struck down—on that very day—the hand of the LORD was upon me, and HeheHe brought me there. 2In visions of God HeheHe brought me to the land of Israel and set me down upon a very high mountain, on whose southern side was something like the structure of a city. 3He brought me there, and behold, a man whose appearance was like bronze, standing in the gate, with a linen cord and a measuring rod in his hand. 4The man said to me, “Son of man, see with your eyes, hear with your ears, and set your heart upon all that I show you, for you have been brought here so that I may show it to you; declare to the house of Israel all that you see.”

Measuring the Outer Wall and the Eastern Gate

5And behold, a wall all around outside the temple; the measuring rod in the man’s hand was six cubits long, each cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth. He measured the thickness of the building, one rod, and its height, one rod. 6Then he came to the gate facing east; he ascended its steps and measured the threshold of the gate, one rod wide, and the other threshold, one rod wide. 7Each guardroom was one rod long and one rod wide, and between the guardrooms was five cubits; the threshold of the gate beside the portico of the gate toward the house was one rod. 8He also measured the portico of the gate toward the house, one rod. 9Then he measured the portico of the gate, eight cubits, and its jambs, two cubits; and the portico of the gate was toward the house. 10The gate facing east had three guardrooms on either side; the three had one measurement, and the jambs on either side had one measurement. 11He measured the width of the gate’s entrance, ten cubits, and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits. 12There was a ledge of one cubit before the guardrooms, and a ledge of one cubit on the other side; and each guardroom was six cubits on either side. 13He measured the gate from the roof of one guardroom to the roof of the other, a width of twenty-five cubits, door opposite door. 14He made the jambs sixty cubits high, and the courtyard extended to the jamb on every side of the gate. 15From the front of the entrance gate to the front of the inner portico of the gate was fifty cubits. 16There were latticed windows to the guardrooms and to their jambs within the gate all around, and likewise to the porticos; windows all around on the inside, and palm trees on each jamb.

The Outer Court and the Remaining Gates

17Then he brought me into the outer courtyard, and behold, chambers and a pavementroadwaypavement made all around the courtyard; thirty chambers were upon the pavementroadwaypavement. 18The pavementroadwaypavement ran along the side of the gates, corresponding to the length of the gates—the lower pavementroadwaypavement. 19He measured the width from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner courtyard outside, one hundred cubits, eastward and northward. 20As for the gate of the outer courtyard facing north, he measured its length and its width. 21Its guardrooms—three on this side and three on that—its jambs, and its portico were according to the first measurement: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 22Its windows, its portico, and its palm trees were according to the measurement of the gate facing east; and seven steps went up to it, with its portico before them. 23There was a gate to the inner courtyard opposite the north gate and the east gate; he measured from gate to gate, one hundred cubits. 24Then he led me southward, and behold, a gate toward the south; he measured its jambs and its portico according to these measurements. 25There were windows in it and in its portico all around, like those windows; fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 26There were seven steps going up to it, and its portico was before them; it had palm trees on its jambs, one on this side and one on that. 27There was a gate to the inner courtyard toward the south; he measured from gate to gate toward the south, one hundred cubits. 28Then he brought me to the inner courtyard through the south gate; he measured the south gate according to these measurements. 29Its guardrooms, its jambs, and its portico were according to these measurements; and its portico had windows all around; fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 30There were porticos all around, twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide. 31Its portico faced the outer courtyard; palm trees were on its jambs; and eight steps led up to it. 32He brought me to the inner courtyard toward the east; he measured the gate according to these measurements. 33Its guardrooms, its jambs, and its portico were according to these measurements; and its portico had windows all around; fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 34Its portico faced the outer courtyard; palm trees were on its jambs on this side and on that; and eight steps led up to it. 35He brought me to the north gate; he measured it according to these measurements. 36Its guardrooms, its jambs, and its portico; and it had windows all around; fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 37Its jambs faced the outer courtyard; palm trees were on its jambs on this side and on that; and eight steps led up to it.

Priestly Chambers, the Altar, and the Temple Vestibule

38There was a chamber with its entrance by the jambs of the gates; there they rinsed the burnt offering. 39In the portico of the gate were two tables on this side and two tables on that, on which to slaughter the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. 40Outside, at the side of the entrance to the north gate, were two tables; and on the other side of the portico of the gate were two tables. 41Four tables on this side and four tables on that side of the gate—eight tables—on which they slaughtered. 42There were four tables of hewn stone for the burnt offering, a cubit and a half long, a cubit and a half wide, and one cubit high; upon them they laid the instruments with which they slaughtered the burnt offering and the sacrifice. 43Hooks, a handbreadth long, were fastened all around within; and upon the tables was the flesh of the offering. 44Outside the inner gate were chambers for the singers in the inner courtyard, which was at the side of the north gate, and their faces were toward the south; one was at the side of the east gate, facing north. 45He said to me, “This chamber whose face is toward the south is for the priests who keep charge of the temple. 46And the chamber whose face is toward the north is for the priests who keep charge of the altar; these are the sons of Zadok, who, among the sons of Levi, draw near to the LORD to minister to HimhimHim.” 47He measured the courtyard, a square, one hundred cubits long and one hundred cubits wide; and the altar was before the house. 48He brought me to the portico of the house and measured the jambs of the portico, five cubits on this side and five cubits on that; and the width of the gate was three cubits on this side and three cubits on that. 49The length of the portico was twenty cubits, and the width eleven cubits; and by the steps by which they went up to it, there were pillars by the jambs, one on this side and one on that.
