Exhortation to Ministerial Fortitude

1You, my child, therefore, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus. 2Moreover, the things you heard from me in the presence of many witnesses, commit to faithful men who will be qualified to instruct others. 3Join in hardship as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 4No one engaged in military service entangles himself in the affairs of everyday life, so as to please the one who enlisted him. 5Likewise, if anyone competes as an athlete, he is not crowned unless he has competed according to the rules. 6The farmer who toils must be the first to partake of the fruits. 7Reflect upon what I am saying, for the Lord will grant you discernment in all matters.

The Risen Christ and the Unbound Word

8Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David, in accordance with my gospel, 9for which I endure suffering, even to the point of imprisonment, as though I were a malefactor; yet the word of God is not shackled. 10For this reason I endure everything for the sake of the elect, that they too may attain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, together with eternal glory.

Approved Workmanship and the Household of God

14Remind them of these things, solemnly charging them before God not to engage in verbal disputes, which serve no useful purpose and tend only to the ruin of those who listen. 15Strive to present yourself to God as one approved, a workman who has no cause for shame, rightly handling the word of truth. 16But avoid profane and empty chatter, for it will lead to further progress in ungodliness, 17and their teaching will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus, 18men who have deviated from the truth by asserting that the resurrection has already occurred, thereby overturning the faith of some.

19Nevertheless, God’s firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: “The Lord knows those who are HishisHis,” and, “Let everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord depart from wickedness.” 20Now in a large household there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and earthenware, some for honourablehonourablehonorable use and some for dishonourabledishonourabledishonorable. 21If, then, anyone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honourhonourhonor, sanctified and useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.

The Gentle Servant and the Recovery of Opponents

22Flee youthful passions, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who invoke the Lord from a pure heart. 23Reject foolish and uneducated speculations, knowing that they beget quarrels. 24And the Lord’s servant must not be quarrelsome, but must be gentle toward all, able to teach, and forbearing, 25with gentleness correcting those who oppose him, in the hope that God may grant them repentance leading to a full knowledge of the truth, 26and that they may come to their senses and escape from the snare of the devil, having been captured by him to do his will.
