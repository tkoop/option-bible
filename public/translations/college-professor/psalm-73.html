The Vexation of the Righteous Amid the Flourishing of the Wicked, and the Consolation of Divine Justice

1A psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. 2But as for me, my feet had almost slipped; my steps had nearly given way. 3For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

4They have no pangs in their death, and their bodies remain well nourished. 5They are free from the burdens common to mortals, nor are they afflicted as others are. 6Therefore pride is their necklace, and violence envelops them like a garment. 7Their eyes protrude from sheer abundance, while the conceits of their hearts overflow unchecked. 8They scoff and utter evil; from their lofty station they threaten oppression. 9They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongues strut across the earth. 10Therefore their people turn to them and drink in their overflowing words.

11They say, “How can God know? Does the MostmostMost HighhighHigh have knowledge?”

12Behold, these are the wicked—always at ease, they amass wealth.

13Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure and washed my hands in innocence. 14All day long I am afflicted, and every morning brings new chastening. 15If I had said, “I will speak this way,” I would have betrayed the generation of YouryourYour children. 16When I sought to comprehend this, it proved wearisome to my sight.

17Until I entered the sanctuary of God, then I discerned their end. 18Surely YouyouYou set them on slippery ground; YouyouYou cast them down to ruin. 19How suddenly they are laid waste, utterly consumed by terrors! 20Like a dream when one awakes, O Lord, when YouyouYou arise, YouyouYou despise their form.

21When my heart was grieved and my inmost being was pierced, 22I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before YouyouYou.

23Yet I am always with YouyouYou; YouyouYou have held my right hand. 24You guide me with YouryourYour counsel, and afterward YouyouYou will take me into glory. 25Whom have I in Heavenheavenheaven but YouyouYou? And on EarthEarthearth I desire no one besides YouyouYou. 26My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.

27Those far from YouyouYou will perish; YouyouYou destroy all who are unfaithful to YouyouYou. 28But as for me, it is good to draw near to God. I have made the Lord the LORD my refuge, that I may declare all YouryourYour works.
