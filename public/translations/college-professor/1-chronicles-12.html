The Gathering of David's Early Adherents at Ziklag

1These are the men who rallied to David at Ziklag while he was still constrained on account of Saul son of Kish; they ranked among the mighty men, auxiliaries in war. 2They were archers, adept with either hand at hurling stones and shooting arrows from the bow—kinsmen of Saul from Benjamin.

3Ahiezer the chief, and Joash, sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Beracah and Jehu the Anathothite; 4Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the thirty and over the thirty; Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite; 5Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite; 6Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam the Korahites; 7Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham of Gedor.

8From Gad there defected to David at the stronghold in the wilderness mighty men of valourvalourvalor, men of the army, bearing shield and spear, whose faces were like the faces of lions and who were swift as gazelles upon the mountains:

9Ezer the chief, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third; 10Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth; 11Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh; 12Johanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth; 13Jeremiah the tenth, Machbannai the eleventh.

14These were of the sons of Gad, captains of the host: the least of them could confront a hundred, and the greatest a thousand. 15These are they who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it overflowed all its banks, and put to flight all the inhabitants of the valleys, both eastward and westward.

The Compact with Benjamin and Judah

16Moreover, men of Benjamin and Judah came to David at the stronghold. 17David went out before them and answered, "If you have come to me peaceably to help me, my heart shall be knit to you; but if you have come to betray me to my adversaries, though no violence is in my hands, may the God of our fathers see and judge."

18Then the SpiritspiritSpirit clothed Amasai, chief of the thirty, and he said, "We are yours, David, and with you, son of Jesse: peace, peace to you, and peace to your helpers, for your God helps you." David received them and appointed them captains of the troop.

The Manassite Accession and the Growing Host

19Some of Manasseh defected to David when he came with the Philistines against Saul to battle, yet they did not assist them, for the Philistine lords, upon deliberation, sent him away, saying, "At the peril of our heads he will fall to his master Saul." 20As he journeyed to Ziklag, there joined him of Manasseh Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zilthai, captains of thousands in Manasseh. 21They helped David against the band, for all were mighty men of valourvalourvalor and captains in the army. 22For day by day men came to David to help him, until the camp had become a great host, like the host of God.

The Hebron Assembly and the Transfer of the Kingdom

23These are the numbers of the heads of men armed for war who came to David at Hebron, to transfer the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the word of the LORD. 24The sons of Judah: 6,800, equipped with shield and spear.

25From the sons of Simeon: 7,100 mighty men of valourvalourvalor for the war. 26From the sons of Levi: 4,600. 27Jehoiada, leader of Aaron, with 3,700. 28Zadok, a young mighty man of valourvalourvalor, with twenty-two commanders from his father's house.

29From the sons of Benjamin, Saul's kinsmen: 3,000, the majority of whom until that time had kept charge of the house of Saul. 30From the sons of Ephraim: 20,800 mighty men of valourvalourvalor, famous men in their fathers' houses. 31From the half-tribe of Manasseh: 18,000, designated by name to come and make David king.

32From the sons of Issachar: 200 chiefs, men endowed with discernment of the times, knowing what Israel ought to do, and all their brethren at their command. 33From Zebulun: 50,000 who went out to the army, arrayed for battle with all the implements of war, single-minded in their support. 34From Naphtali: 1,000 commanders, with 37,000 men bearing shield and spear.

35From Dan: 28,600, arrayed for battle. 36From Asher: 40,000 who went out to the army, to set the battle in array. 37From beyond the Jordan—Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh: 120,000, with all the implements of war.

38All these men of war, keeping ranks, came to Hebron with a perfect heart to make David king over all Israel; and all the rest of Israel were of one heart to make David king. 39They remained there with David three days, eating and drinking, for their brethren had prepared for them. 40Moreover, those near them, as far as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, brought bread on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen, together with food, flour, fig cakes, raisin cakes, wine, oil, oxen, and sheep in abundance, for there was joy in Israel.
