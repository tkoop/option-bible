Elihu Extols the Thunder and Sovereignty of God

"1At this my heart likewise trembles and leaps from its place. 2Listen attentively to the thunder of HishisHis voice and to the rumbling that issues from HishisHis mouth. 3He sends it forth beneath the whole Heavenheavenheaven, and HishisHis lightning to the very ends of the earth. 4Afterward a voice roars; HeheHe thunders with the majesty of HishisHis voice, and HeheHe does not restrain the lightning when HishisHis voice is heard. 5God thunders marvelously with HishisHis voice; HeheHe does great things that we do not know.

"6For HeheHe commands the snow, 'Fall upon the earth,' and the rain shower, 'Be a mighty downpour.' 7HeheHe seals the hand of every person, so that all mankind may recognizerecogniserecognize HishisHis work. 8Then the beasts retreat into their lairs and remain in their dens. 9From its chamber comes the tempest, and cold from the scattering winds. 10By the breath of God ice is formed, and the broad waters are frozen.

"11He also burdens the thick cloud with moisture and scatters HishisHis lightning through the clouds. 12And it turns about at HishisHis direction, that they may do whatever HeheHe commands them upon the face of the inhabited EarthEarthearth. 13Whether for correction, for HishisHis land, or for mercy, HeheHe brings it to pass.

Job Is Summoned to Contemplate Divine Wonders

"14Hear this, O Job; stand still and consider the wondrous works of God. 15Do you know how God ordains them, and causes the light of HishisHis cloud to shine? 16Do you know the balancing of the clouds, the wondrous works of HimhimHim who is perfect in knowledge? 17Your garments grow warm when the earth is stilled by the south wind.

"18Can you, with HimhimHim, spread out the skies, strong as a cast metal mirror? 19Teach us what we should say to HimhimHim; we cannot arrange our case because of darkness. 20Shall it be reported to HimhimHim that I would speak? If a man speaks, surely he will be swallowed up.

The Unsearchable Glory of the Almighty

"21And now they do not see the light, though it is bright in the skies; but the wind passes and clears them. 22From the north comes golden splendoursplendoursplendor; God is clothed with awesome majesty. 23The AlmightyalmightyAlmighty is beyond our reach; HeheHe is exalted in power; and in justice and abundant righteousness HeheHe does not oppress. 24Therefore men fear HimhimHim; HeheHe does not regard any who are wise of heart."
