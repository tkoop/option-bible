The Sign of the Serpent Before Pharaoh

8The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 9“When Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, ‘Give yourselves a wonder,’ then you shall say to Aaron, ‘Take your staff and hurl it down before Pharaoh, that it may become a serpent.’”

10So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and did as the LORD had commanded; Aaron cast down his staff before Pharaoh and before his servants, and it became a serpent. 11Then Pharaoh summoned the wise men and the sorcerers, and the magicians of Egypt likewise did the same by their secret arts. 12Each man cast down his staff, and they became serpents; yet Aaron’s staff swallowed up their staffs. 13But Pharaoh’s heart hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

The First Plague: The Nile Turned to Blood

14The LORD said to Moses, “Pharaoh’s heart is obdurate; he refuses to release the people. 15Go to Pharaoh in the morning, when he goes out to the water, and station yourself upon the bank of the Nile to meet him; take in your hand the staff that was transformed into a serpent. 16And you shall say to him, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying, “Let MymyMy people go, that they may serve MemeMe in the wilderness”; but behold, until now you have not listened. 17Thus says the LORD: “By this you shall know that I am the LORD: behold, with the staff in MymyMy hand I am about to strike the water that is in the Nile, and it shall be turned to blood. 18The fish that are in the Nile shall die, the Nile shall stink, and the Egyptians shall be unable to drink water from the Nile.”’”

19The LORD said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt—over their rivers, canals, ponds, and all their reservoirs of water—that they may become blood; and there shall be blood throughout all the land of Egypt, even in vessels of wood and stone.’” 20Moses and Aaron did so, just as the LORD had commanded; in the sight of Pharaoh and his servants, Aaron lifted up the staff and struck the water that was in the Nile, and all the water in the Nile was turned to blood. 21The fish in the Nile died, the river stank, and the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile; and the blood was throughout all the land of Egypt.

22But the magicians of Egypt did the same by their secret arts; and Pharaoh’s heart hardened, and he did not listen to them, as the LORD had spoken. 23Pharaoh turned and went into his house, nor did he take even this to heart. 24All the Egyptians dug all around the Nile for water to drink, for they could not drink the water of the Nile. 25Seven days passed after the LORD had struck the Nile.
