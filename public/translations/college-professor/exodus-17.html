The Journey to Rephidim and the Crisis of Thirst

1The entire congregation of the Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sin, journeying by stages in accordance with the command of the LORD, and encamped at Rephidim, where no water was available for the people to drink.

2Consequently, the people entered into contention with Moses, demanding, “Provide us with water, that we may drink.”

Moses answered them, “Why do you contend with me? Why do you put the LORD to the test?”

3The people, parched with thirst in that place, murmured against Moses and said, “Why have you brought us up from Egypt, only to put me, my children, and my livestock to death by thirst?”

4Moses cried out to the LORD, “What shall I do with this people? They are scarcely a step from stoning me.”

5The LORD said to Moses, “Go on ahead of the people, taking with you some of the elders of Israel, and carry in your hand the staff with which you struck the Nile; then proceed.”

6The LORD said, “Behold, I will stand before you there on the rock at Horeb; strike the rock, and water shall flow from it, that the people may drink.” Moses did so in the presence of the elders of Israel.

7He named that place Massah and Meribah, on account of the contention of the Israelites and their testing of the LORD, when they said, “Is the LORD among us, or not?”

Amalek's Assault and Israel's Triumph

8Then Amalek came and waged war against Israel at Rephidim.

9Moses said to Joshua, “Select men for us and go out to engage Amalek in battle. Tomorrow I will take my stand on the hilltop, the staff of God in my hand.”

10Joshua did as Moses had instructed him and fought against Amalek, while Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the summit of the hill.

11So it was that whenever Moses lifted his hand, Israel prevailed, and whenever he lowered his hand, Amalek prevailed.

12But when Moses' hands became weary, they took a stone and set it under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur upheld his hands, one on either side, so that his hands remained firm until sunset.

13Joshua overwhelmed Amalek and his people by the sword.

The Memorial Decree and the Altar of the LORD

14The LORD said to Moses, “Inscribe this in a book as a memorial, and recite it in Joshua's hearing, for I will utterly efface the memory of Amalek from beneath the heavens.”

15Moses then built an altar and called its name, “The LORD is my banner.”

16Moses said, “Because a hand has been raised against the throne of the LORD, the LORD will make war upon Amalek from generation to generation.”
