Oracle Against the Ammonites

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, direct your attention against the Ammonites and prophesy against them.

3“And say to the Ammonites, ‘Hear the word of the Lord GOD: Thus says the Lord GOD: Because you said, “Aha!” concerning MymyMy sanctuary, that it was profaned, and concerning the land of Israel, that it was desolate, and concerning the house of Judah, that they went into exile,

4“‘therefore I am consigning you to the eastern peoples as a possession. They shall pitch their camps in your midst and erect their dwellings within you; they shall consume your produce and drink your milk. 5I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels and the Ammonites a resting place for flocks, and you shall know that I am the LORD.’

6“For thus says the Lord GOD: Because you clapped your hands, stamped your feet, and rejoiced with all the contempt of your soul against the land of Israel, 7therefore, behold, I have stretched out MymyMy hand against you and will give you as plunder to the nations. I will cut you off from the peoples and make you perish from the countries; I will destroy you, and you shall know that I am the LORD.”

8“Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Moab and Seir said that the house of Judah was like all the nations, 9therefore, behold, I am laying open the flank of Moab, from its cities to its frontier cities, the splendoursplendoursplendor of the land—Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim,

10“and I will give it to the Ammonites as a possession, together with the sons of the East, so that the Ammonites may not be remembered among the nations. 11I will execute judgmentsjudgementsjudgments upon Moab, and they shall know that I am the LORD.”

Oracle Against Edom

12“Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Edom acted vengefully against the house of Judah and incurred grave guilt by avenging itself upon them, 13therefore thus says the Lord GOD: I will stretch out MymyMy hand against Edom and cut off from it both man and beast; I will make it desolate from Teman, and those of Dedan shall fall by the sword.

14“I will take vengeance on Edom by the hand of MymyMy people Israel, and they shall deal with Edom according to MymyMy anger and MymyMy wrath; and they shall know MymyMy vengeance, declares the Lord GOD.”

Oracle Against the Philistines

15“Thus says the Lord GOD: Because the Philistines acted vengefully and took revenge with malice in their souls, seeking to destroy with everlasting enmity, 16therefore, behold, I am stretching out MymyMy hand against the Philistines; I will cut off the Cherethites and destroy the remnant of the seacoast,

17“and I will execute great vengeance upon them in wrathful rebukes; and they shall know that I am the LORD, when I lay MymyMy vengeance upon them.”
