Paul's Anguish for Israel and Its Covenant Prerogatives

1I speak the truth in Christ—I do not prevaricate—while my conscience corroborates my testimony in the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 2that I am afflicted by profound sorrow and incessant anguish of heart. 3For I could wish myself anathematized and severed from Christ on behalf of my brothers, my kinsmen according to the flesh— 4Israelites, to whom pertain adoption, glory, covenants, legislation, liturgy, and promises; 5whose are the patriarchs, and from whom, according to the flesh, comes the Christ, who is God over all, eternally blessed. Amen.

The Integrity of God's Word and the True Israel

6Yet it is not as though the word of God has miscarried. For not all who descend from Israel constitute Israel, 7nor are all Abraham's seed automatically his children; rather, “In Isaac shall your seed be called.” 8That is, the children of the flesh are not ipso facto children of God; rather, the children of the promise are reckoned as offspring. 9For this is the word of promise: “At this appointed time I will come, and Sarah shall have a son.”

10Moreover, Rebekah conceived by one man, our father Isaac, 11before the twins had been born or had perpetrated any good or evil, so that God's elective purpose might remain inviolable, 12not on the basis of works but on the basis of HimhimHim who calls—she was told, “The elder shall serve the younger.” 13As it is written, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”

Divine Righteousness, Mercy, and the Potter's Authority

14What then shall we conclude? Is there unrighteousness with God? By no means! 15For HeheHe says to Moses, “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.” 16Consequently, it depends not on the one who wills or the one who runs, but on God who shows mercy. 17For ScripturescriptureScripture says to Pharaoh, “For this very purpose I have raised you up, that I might exhibit MymyMy power in you, and that MymyMy name might be proclaimed throughout all the earth.” 18Thus HeheHe has mercy on whomever HeheHe wills, and HeheHe hardens whomever HeheHe wills.
