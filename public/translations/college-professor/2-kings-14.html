The Reign of Amaziah in Judah

1In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash became king of Judah. 2He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother was Jehoaddan, from Jerusalem. 3He did what was upright in the eyes of the LORD, though not with the wholehearted fidelity of his ancestor David; rather, he conducted himself in every respect as his father Joash had done. 4Yet the high places were not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and to burn incense upon them.

5When the kingdom was firmly established in his hand, he put to death the servants who had assassinated his father the king. 6But he did not execute the children of the murderers, in accordance with what is written in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses, wherein the LORD commanded, “Fathers shall not be put to death because of their children, nor shall children be put to death because of their fathers; each shall die for his own sin.” 7He struck down ten thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt, seized Sela in battle, and renamed it Joktheel, a name it bears to this day.

Amaziah's Defeat by Israel

8Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come, let us confront one another face to face.”

9Jehoash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, “The thistle that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son for a wife’; but a wild beast that was in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle. 10You have indeed struck Edom, and your heart has lifted you up. Glory in it and stay at home; why should you stir up trouble so that you fall, you and Judah with you?”

11But Amaziah would not listen, so Jehoash king of Israel advanced; he and Amaziah king of Judah confronted one another at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 12Judah was routed before Israel, and every man fled to his tent. 13Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Jehoash son of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh; then he came to Jerusalem and tore down the wall of Jerusalem from the GategateGate of Ephraim to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate—a breach of four hundred cubits. 14He carried off all the gold and silver and all the vessels found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the royal palace, together with hostages, and returned to Samaria.

15The rest of the acts of Jehoash—what he did, his might, and his war with Amaziah king of Judah—are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 16Jehoash slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; his son Jeroboam reigned in his place.

The Death of Amaziah and the Rise of Azariah

17Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 18The rest of the acts of Amaziah—are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 19A conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but they sent men after him to Lachish and put him to death there. 20They brought him back on horses and buried him in Jerusalem with his fathers in the CitycityCity of David. 21Then all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. 22He rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah, after the king had been laid to rest with his fathers.

Jeroboam II and the Restoration of Israel

23In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash became king of Israel in Samaria, and he reigned forty-one years. 24He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had made Israel sin. 25He restored the border of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the SeaseaSea of the Arabah, in accordance with the word that the LORD, the God of Israel, had spoken through HishisHis servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher. 26For the LORD saw the very bitter affliction of Israel: there was no one, whether confined or at large, and no helper for Israel. 27Yet the LORD had not decreed that HeheHe would blot out the name of Israel from under Heavenheavenheaven, so HeheHe saved them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash. 28The rest of the acts of Jeroboam—all that he did, his might, how he waged war, and how he recovered Damascus and Hamath, which had belonged to Judah, for Israel—are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 29Jeroboam slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel; his son Zechariah reigned in his place.
