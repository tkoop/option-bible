A Communal Hymn of Thanksgiving, Confession, and Supplication

1Hallelujah. Render thanksgiving unto the LORD, for HeheHe is benevolent; indeed, HishisHis covenantal lovingkindness persists in perpetuity. 2Who possesses the eloquence to articulate the LORD’s mighty deeds, or to proclaim the entirety of HishisHis praise? 3Blessed are they who uphold judicial equity and practice righteousness in every season. 4Remember me, O the LORD, in YouryourYour favourfavourfavor toward YouryourYour people; visit me with YouryourYour salvation, 5that I might behold the felicity of YouryourYour elect, exult in the jubilation of YouryourYour nation, and triumph alongside YouryourYour heritage.

6We have sinned in concert with our forebears; we have wrought iniquity; we have acted with consummate wickedness. 7Our forebears in Egypt did not apprehend YouryourYour marvellousmarvellousmarvelous deeds; they failed to remember the plenitude of YouryourYour mercy, and instead rebelled at the Red SeaseaSea. 8Nevertheless, HeheHe delivered them for the sake of HishisHis name, to make manifest HishisHis omnipotence. 9He rebuked the Red SeaseaSea, so that it desiccated, and HeheHe conducted them through the abyss as though through a desert. 10Thus HeheHe rescued them from the grasp of the adversary and ransomed them from the power of the enemy. 11The waters overwhelmed their oppressors, so that not a single one survived. 12Then they gave credence to HishisHis utterances and intoned HishisHis praise.

13Yet they swiftly forgot HishisHis deeds and refused to await HishisHis counsel. 14Rather, they indulged a licentious craving in the wilderness and subjected God to trial in the wasteland. 15He granted their petition, yet dispatched a wasting sickness into their souls. 16When envy arose in the encampment against Moses and against Aaron, the holy one of the LORD, 17the earth yawned open and engulfed Dathan, then concealed the assembly of Abiram. 18Moreover, a conflagration erupted among their company, and the blaze devoured the wicked. 19At Horeb they fashioned a calf and prostrated themselves before a molten image. 20They bartered away their glory for the likeness of a grass-consuming ox. 21They forgot God, their SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, who had wrought stupendous deeds in Egypt, 22marvellousmarvellousmarvelous works in the land of Ham, and terrifying wonders at the Red SeaseaSea. 23Consequently, HeheHe declared that HeheHe would annihilate them—had not Moses, HishisHis elect, stationed himself in the breach before HimhimHim to avert HishisHis wrath from their destruction. 24Thereafter they disdained the desirable land, placing no confidence in HishisHis promise. 25They grumbled within their tents and refused to heed the voice of the LORD. 26Therefore HeheHe lifted HishisHis hand and swore that HeheHe would cause them to perish in the wilderness, 27and that HeheHe would cast their seed among the nations, dispersing them throughout foreign territories. 28Then they yoked themselves to Baal of Peor and partook of sacrifices consecrated to the dead. 29By their practices they provoked the LORD to wrath, and a pestilence erupted in their midst. 30Then Phinehas arose and interposed, and the plague was arrested. 31And this was reckoned unto him as righteousness for all generations in perpetuity. 32They provoked HimhimHim at the waters of Meribah, and Moses suffered affliction on their account, 33because they embittered his spirit, so that he uttered precipitous words with his lips.

34They failed to exterminate the peoples, as the LORD had commanded them, 35but instead intermingled with the nations and assimilated their practices. 36They venerated their idols, which devolved into a snare for them. 37They immolated their sons and daughters unto demons. 38They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters—whom they offered to the idols of Canaan, so that the land was defiled by bloodshed. 39Thus they were polluted by their conduct and prostituted themselves through their practices. 40Then the wrath of the LORD was inflamed against HishisHis people, and HeheHe abominated HishisHis inheritance; 41HeheHe delivered them into the hand of the nations, so that their adversaries exercised dominion over them. 42Their enemies subjugated and afflicted them, reducing them to submission beneath their power. 43On numerous occasions HeheHe rescued them, yet they persisted in rebellious counsel and were debased by their iniquity. 44Nevertheless, HeheHe regarded their affliction when HeheHe heard their supplication. 45For their sake HeheHe remembered HishisHis covenant and relented in accordance with the magnitude of HishisHis mercy. 46He also caused them to find compassion in the sight of all their captors.

47Deliver us, O the LORD our God, and assemble us from among the nations, that we may render thanksgiving to YouryourYour holy name and exult in YouryourYour praise. 48Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from eternity unto eternity! And let all the people declare, “Amen!” Hallelujah!
