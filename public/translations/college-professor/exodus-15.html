20Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took a tambourine in her hand, and all the women went out after her with tambourines and dancing. 21And Miriam answered them, "Sing to the LORD, for HeheHe has triumphed gloriously; horse and rider HeheHe has hurled into the sea."
