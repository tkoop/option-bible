The Priestly and Levitical Burden of the Sanctuary

1The LORD said to Aaron, "YouyouYou and your sons, together with your ancestral house, shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary; and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.

"2Bring near to you also your brothers—the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father—that they may join you and minister to you while you and your sons stand before the tent of testimony. 3They shall keep your charge and the charge of the whole tent, yet they shall not approach the holy vessels or the altar, lest they die—both they and you. 4They shall be joined to you and shall keep the charge of the tent of meeting for all its service, and no outsider shall come near you. 5You shall keep the charge of the sanctuary and of the altar, so that wrath may not again fall upon the children of Israel.

"6Behold, I have taken your brothers the Levites from among the children of Israel; they are a gift to you, given to the LORD to perform the service of the tent of meeting. 7You and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for everything concerning the altar and within the veil, and you shall serve. I give the priesthood as a gift, and the outsider who approaches shall be put to death."

The Priestly Portion of Israel’s Holy Offerings

"8Behold, I have given you the charge of MymyMy contributions; all the consecrated things of the children of Israel I have given to you and to your sons as a portion, as a perpetual statute. 9This shall be yours from the most holy things, reserved from the fire: every offering of theirs, every grain offering, sin offering, and guilt offering that they render to MemeMe shall be most holy for you and your sons. 10In a most holy place you shall eat it; every male may eat it; it shall be holy to you. 11This also is yours: the contribution of their gift, all the wave offerings of the children of Israel; I have given them to you, to your sons, and to your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute. Everyone who is clean in your house may eat it. 12All the best of the oil, all the best of the new wine and the grain—their firstfruits, which they give to the LORD—I have given to you. 13The firstfruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

"14Every devoted thing in Israel shall be yours. 15Everything that opens the womb of all flesh, which they offer to the LORD—whether of man or beast—shall be yours. Nevertheless, you shall surely redeem the firstborn of man, and you shall redeem the firstborn of an unclean beast. 16And their redemption, from a month old, you shall redeem, according to your valuation: five shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs. 17But the firstborn of an ox, a sheep, or a goat you shall not redeem; they are holy. You shall sprinkle their blood upon the altar and burn their fat as an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 18Their flesh shall be yours, as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours. 19All the contributions of holy things that the children of Israel present to the LORD I have given to you, to your sons, and to your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute. It is a covenant of salt forever before the LORD for you and your offspring with you."

Levitical Inheritance and the Prohibition of Common Approach

20The LORD said to Aaron, "YouyouYou shall have no inheritance in their land, neither shall you have a portion among them. I am your portion and your inheritance among the children of Israel. 21To the sons of Levi, behold, I have given every tithe in Israel for an inheritance, in return for the service that they render, the service of the tent of meeting. 22The children of Israel shall no longer come near the tent of meeting, lest they bear sin and die. 23The Levites shall perform the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their iniquity. It shall be a statute forever throughout your generations, and among the children of Israel they shall have no inheritance. 24For the tithe of the children of Israel, which they present to the LORD as a contribution, I have given to the Levites for an inheritance. Therefore I have said to them that among the children of Israel they shall have no inheritance."

The Levites’ Obligation to Offer a Tithe of the Tithe

25The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, "26Moreover, you shall speak to the Levites and say to them, 'When you take from the children of Israel the tithe that I have given you from them for your inheritance, you shall present a contribution from it to the LORD, a tithe of the tithe. 27Your contribution shall be reckoned to you as though it were the grain of the threshing floor and as the produce of the winepress. 28So you shall present a contribution to the LORD from all your tithes, which you take from the children of Israel; and from it you shall give the LORD’s contribution to Aaron the priest. 29Out of all your gifts you shall present every contribution to the LORD, from all the best thereof, the hallowed part of it.' 30Therefore say to them, 'When you have set apart the best of it, then the rest shall be reckoned to the Levites as the produce of the threshing floor and as the produce of the winepress. 31You may eat it in any place, you and your households, because it is your reward in return for your service in the tent of meeting. 32You shall bear no sin by reason of it, when you have set apart the best of it. Neither shall you profane the holy things of the children of Israel, lest you die.'"
