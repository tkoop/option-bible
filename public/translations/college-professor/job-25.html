Bildad’s Final Rejoinder: The Overwhelming Majesty of the Divine

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

The Innumerable Hosts and Universal Light of God

2“Dominion and dread attend HimhimHim; HeheHe establishes tranquillitytranquillitytranquility in HishisHis exalted realms. 3His hosts are beyond numbering, and HishisHis light shines upon all

Human Frailty and the Impossibility of Righteousness

4“How, then, can mortal man be justified before God? How can one born of woman attain purity? 5Behold, even the moon lacks luster, and the stars are not spotless in HishisHis sight. 6How much less, then, is man, who is but a maggot, and the son of man, who is but a worm!”
