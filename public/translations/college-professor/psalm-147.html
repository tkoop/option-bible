A Hymn of Praise to the LORD for Jerusalem’s Restoration and His Sovereign Providence

1Praise the LORD, for it is good; sing praises to our God, for it is pleasant, and praise is befitting. 2The LORD builds up Jerusalem; HeheHe gathers the exiles of Israel. 3He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

4He counts the number of the stars; HeheHe calls them all by name. 5Great is our Lord and mighty in power; HishisHis understanding is beyond measure. 6The LORD lifts up the humble; HeheHe casts the wicked to the ground.

7Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the lyre. 8He covers the sky with clouds; HeheHe provides rain for the earth and makes grass grow on the hills. 9He gives food to the beasts and to the young ravens when they cry.

10He does not delight in the strength of the horse, nor take pleasure in the legs of a man. 11The LORD takes pleasure in those who fear HimhimHim, in those who hope in HishisHis steadfast love.

12Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion! 13For HeheHe strengthens the bars of your gates; HeheHe blesses your children within you. 14He makes peace within your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat.

15He sends forth HishisHis command to the earth; HishisHis word runs swiftly. 16He gives snow like wool; HeheHe scatters frost like ashes; HeheHe casts forth HishisHis hail like crumbs. 17Who can stand before HishisHis cold? 18He sends out HishisHis word and melts them; HeheHe makes HishisHis wind blow, and the waters flow.

19He declares HishisHis word to Jacob, HishisHis statutes and ordinances to Israel. 20He has not dealt thus with any nation; they do not know HishisHis ordinances. Praise the LORD!
