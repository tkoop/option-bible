Ahaziah’s Accession and Character

1The inhabitants of Jerusalem enthroned Ahaziah, Jehoram’s youngest son, in his stead, because the marauding contingent that had accompanied the Arabians into the encampment had slain all the elder heirs; thus Ahaziah, son of Jehoram, king of Judah, assumed the throne. 2Ahaziah was forty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem; his mother was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri. 3He too pursued the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother counselledcounselledcounseled him toward wickedness. 4He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, for after his father’s death they became his counselors unto his ruin.

Alliance with Joram of Israel

5He likewise followed their counsel and marched with Joram, son of Ahab, king of Israel, against Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth-gilead; and the Arameans wounded Joram. 6Therefore he returned to Jezreel to recuperate from the wounds inflicted upon him at Ramah during his campaign against Hazael, king of Aram. Meanwhile, Ahaziah, son of Jehoram, king of Judah, descended to Jezreel to visit Joram, son of Ahab, because the latter was ill.

Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment and the Fall of Ahaziah

7Ahaziah’s downfall was ordained by God when he went to Joram; for upon his arrival he went out with Joram to meet Jehu, son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to exterminate the house of Ahab. 8While Jehu was executing judgmentjudgementjudgment upon the house of Ahab, he discovered the princes of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah’s brothers who were serving Ahaziah, and he put them to death. 9He sought Ahaziah, and his men captured him while he was hiding in Samaria; they brought him to Jehu and slew him. They buried him, because they said, “He is the grandson of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart.” Thus the house of Ahaziah had no one possessing sufficient strength to retain the kingdom.

Athaliah’s Usurpation and the Preservation of Joash

10When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, perceived that her son was dead, she arose and extirpated all the royal progeny of the house of Judah. 11But Jehoshabeath, the king’s daughter, took Joash, son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king’s sons who were being slain, placing him and his nurse in a bedchamber. Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram and wife of Jehoiada the priest—for she was Ahaziah’s sister—hid him from Athaliah, so that she did not kill him. 12He remained concealed with them in the house of God for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.
