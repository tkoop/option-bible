The Day Jerusalem Is Besieged

1In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

2"SonsonSon of man, inscribe for yourself the name of this day, this very day; for on this day the king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem. 3And set forth a parable for the rebellious house, and say to them, 'Thus says the Lord GOD: Set the pot on the fire; set it on, and pour water into it. 4Gather its pieces into it, every choice piece—the thigh and the shoulder; fill it with the select bones. 5Take the choicest of the flock, and also heap the bones beneath it; boil its pieces thoroughly, and cook its bones in the midst of it.

6"'Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Woe to the city of bloodshed, to the pot whose corrosion is in it, and whose corrosion has not gone out of it! Empty it piece by piece; no lot has fallen upon it. 7For her blood was in the midst of her; she set it on the bare rock; she did not pour it out on the ground to cover it with dust. 8To rouse wrath, to exact vengeance, I have set her blood on the bare rock, that it may not be covered. 9Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Woe to the city of bloodshed! I too will make the pyre great. 10Heap on the logs and kindle the fire; cook the meat thoroughly and mix in the spices; let the bones be scorched. 11Then set the empty pot on its coals, that it may be heated and its bronze may glow, and its uncleanness may be melted in it, and its corrosion consumed. 12She has wearied herself with toil, yet her thick corrosion has not gone out of her; her corrosion shall not go out by fire. 13In your uncleanness lies indecency. Because I cleansed you, and you were not cleansed, you shall not be cleansed from your uncleanness anymore until I have satisfied MymyMy wrath upon you. 14I, the LORD, have spoken; it shall come to pass, and I will do it. I will not recede, nor will I spare, nor will I relent; according to your ways and according to your deeds they will judge you, declares the Lord GOD.'

The Fugitive's Report and the Prophet's Restored Speech

25"And you, son of man, on the day when I take from them their stronghold, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and the yearning of their soul—their sons and their daughters— 26on that day a fugitive will come to you to report it to you. 27On that day your mouth will be opened to the fugitive, and you shall speak and be mute no longer. So you will be a sign to them, and they shall know that I am the LORD."
