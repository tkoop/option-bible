An Exaltation of the LORD’s Universal Sovereignty and Condescending Grace

1Praise the the LORD! Praise HimhimHim, O servants of the the LORD; praise the name of the the LORD. 2Blessed be the name of the the LORD from this present hour even unto the ages of ages. 3From the sun’s rising unto its setting, the name of the the LORD is to be extolled.

4The ThetheThe LORD is exalted above all nations, and HishisHis glory transcends the heavens. 5Who is comparable unto the the LORD our God, enthroned on high, 6who deigns to gaze upon the heavens and the earth?

7He raises the indigent from the dust and lifts the destitute from the ash heap, 8to seat them among princes, among the princes of HishisHis people. 9He grants the barren woman a dwelling, making her a joyful mother of children. Praise the the LORD!
