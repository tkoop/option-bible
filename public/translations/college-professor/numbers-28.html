Divine Ordinances for Israel's Appointed Offerings

"3And you shall say to them, 'This is the fire offering that you shall offer to the LORD: two unblemished, year-old male lambs, each day as a perpetual burnt offering. 4You shall offer one lamb in the morning and the other between the two evenings. 5A tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter of a hin of beaten oil, shall accompany it. 6It is a perpetual burnt offering, ordained at Mount Sinai, a pleasing aroma—an offering by fire to the LORD. 7Its libation shall be a quarter of a hin for each lamb; pour out a libation of fermented drink to the LORD in the sanctuary. 8And you shall offer the second lamb between the two evenings; like the morning grain offering and its libation, you shall offer it as an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.'

Sabbath and New-Moon Offerings

"9On the Sabbath day, two unblemished year-old male lambs, together with two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and its libation. 10This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the perpetual burnt offering and its libation. 11On the first day of each month, you shall present to the LORD a burnt offering: two young bulls, one ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs. 12With each bull, three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering; with the ram, two-tenths of an ephah; 13and with each lamb, a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil. It is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma—an offering by fire to the LORD. 14Their libations shall be half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for a ram, and a quarter of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering for each month throughout the months of the year. 15And one male goat shall be offered as a sin offering to the LORD, in addition to the perpetual burnt offering and its libation.

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

"16On the fourteenth day of the first month is the LORD’s Passover. 17On the fifteenth day of this month there is a feast; for seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten. 18On the first day there shall be a holy convocation; you shall not do any ordinary work. 19You shall present to the LORD an offering by fire, a burnt offering: two young bulls, one ram, and seven year-old male lambs; they shall be unblemished for you. 20Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for a bull, and two-tenths for a ram. 21A tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs. 22And one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you. 23You shall offer these in addition to the morning burnt offering, which is the perpetual burnt offering. 24In the same way you shall offer daily, for seven days, the food of an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It shall be offered in addition to the perpetual burnt offering and its libation. 25On the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not do any ordinary work.

The Offering of Firstfruits at the Feast of Weeks
