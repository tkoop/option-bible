The Assembly's Revolt and the Spies' Encouragement

1That very night the whole assembly raised a clamorous cry, and the people wept bitterly. 2All the Israelites murmured against Moses and Aaron, and the entire assembly said to them, “Would that we had perished in Egypt, or that we had met our end in this wilderness! 3Why has the LORD brought us into this land only to fall by the sword, so that our wives and little ones become plunder? Would it not be better for us to return to Egypt?” 4And they said to one another, “Let us appoint a captain and go back to Egypt.”

5Then Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces before the whole assembly of the congregation of the Israelites.

6Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had spied out the land, tore their garments, 7and they said to the whole assembly of the Israelites, “The land through which we passed to spy it out is an exceedingly good land. 8If the LORD takes delight in us, HeheHe will bring us into this land and give it to us—a land flowing with milk and honey. 9Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not fear the people of the land, for they are bread for us; their protection has departed from them, and the LORD is with us. Do not fear them.”

10But the whole assembly was about to stone them, when the glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the Israelites.

Moses Intercedes and the LORD Responds

11And the LORD said to Moses, “How long will this people despise MemeMe, and how long will they refuse to believe in MemeMe, notwithstanding all the signs that I have performed in their midst? 12I will strike them with pestilence and disinherit them, and I will make of you a nation greater and mightier than they.”

13But Moses said to the LORD, “Then the Egyptians will hear of it, for by YouryourYour might YouyouYou brought up this people from among them, 14and they will tell the inhabitants of this land. They have heard that YouyouYou, the LORD, are in the midst of this people, whom YouyouYou have let be seen face to face, and that YouryourYour cloud stands over them, and that YouyouYou go before them in a pillar of cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night. 15Now if YouyouYou put this people to death all at once, then the nations who have heard the report of YouyouYou will say, 16‘Because the LORD was not able to bring this people into the land that HeheHe swore to give them, HeheHe has slaughtered them in the wilderness.’

“17And now, I pray, let the power of my Lord be great, even as YouyouYou have spoken, saying, 18‘The LORD is slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love, forgiving iniquity and transgression, yet by no means clearing the guilty, but visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and fourth generation.’ 19Pardon, I pray, the iniquity of this people according to the greatness of YouryourYour steadfast love, just as YouyouYou have forgiven this people from Egypt until now.”

20Then the LORD said, “I have pardoned, according to your word; 21but truly, as I live, and as all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the LORD,

“22none of the men who have seen MymyMy glory and MymyMy signs, which I wrought in Egypt and in the wilderness, and yet have put MemeMe to the test these ten times and have not obeyed MymyMy voice, 23shall see the land that I swore to give to their fathers; none of those who despised MemeMe shall see it. 24But MymyMy servant Caleb, because he had a different spirit with him and has followed MemeMe fully, I will bring him into the land into which he went, and his descendants shall possess it. 25Now, since the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwell in the valley, turn back tomorrow and set out into the wilderness by the way to the Red SeaseaSea.”

The Divine Decree of Wilderness Wandering

26And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying, “How long shall this wicked congregation murmur against MemeMe? 27I have heard the murmurings of the Israelites, which they murmur against MemeMe.

“28Say to them, ‘As I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you just as you have spoken in MymyMy hearing: 29your corpses shall fall in this wilderness, and all of you who were numbered, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, who have murmured against MemeMe, 30shall by no means enter the land concerning which I swore to make you dwell in it, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun. 31But your little ones, whom you said would become a prey, I will bring in, and they shall know the land that you have despised.

“‘32But as for you, your corpses shall fall in this wilderness; 33and your children shall be shepherds in the wilderness forty years, and shall bear your unfaithfulness, until your corpses are consumed in the wilderness. 34According to the number of the days in which you spied out the land, forty days, a year for each day, you shall bear your iniquity forty years, and you shall know MymyMy displeasure. 35I, the LORD, have spoken; surely thus will I do to all this wicked congregation that is gathered together against MemeMe: in this wilderness they shall be consumed, and there they shall die.’”

36And the men whom Moses had sent to spy out the land, who returned and made the whole congregation murmur against him by bringing up an evil report about the land— 37those men who brought up the evil report of the land died by the plague before the LORD. 38But Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh remained alive, of those men who went to spy out the land.

The Presumptuous Ascent and Defeat at Hormah

39When Moses spoke these words to all the Israelites, the people mourned greatly.

40And they rose early in the morning and went up to the top of the mountain, saying, “Behold, we are here, and we will go up to the place that the LORD has promised, for we have sinned.”

41But Moses said, “Why now are you transgressing the commandment of the LORD? It will not prosper. 42Do not go up, lest you be struck down before your enemies, for the LORD is not among you. 43For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there before you, and you shall fall by the sword; because you have turned back from following the LORD, the LORD will not be with you.”

44But they presumed to go up to the top of the mountain, although neither the ark of the covenant of the LORD nor Moses departed from the midst of the camp. 45Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who dwelt in that mountain came down and defeated them and pursued them as far as Hormah.
