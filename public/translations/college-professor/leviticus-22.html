Regulations Governing Priestly Purity and the Sacred Offerings

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Speak to Aaron and his sons, that they separate themselves from the sacred offerings of the sons of Israel, which they consecrate to MemeMe, and that they not desecrate MymyMy holy name: I am the LORD.

3“Say to them, ‘Throughout your generations, any man among all your descendants who, having an uncleanness upon him, approaches the sacred offerings that the sons of Israel consecrate to the LORD—that person shall be cut off from MymyMy presence: I am the LORD. 4None of the descendants of Aaron who has a leprous disease or a discharge may partake of the sacred offerings until he is purified. Whoever touches anything unclean through contact with the dead, or a man who has had an emission of semen, 5and whoever touches any swarming thing by which he may be made unclean, or a person from whom he may contract uncleanness, according to all his uncleanness— 6the person who touches such a thing shall remain unclean until the evening and shall not partake of the sacred offerings unless he has bathed his flesh in water. 7At sunset he shall be clean, and thereafter he may partake of the sacred offerings, for it is his food. 8He shall not eat that which dies of itself or is torn by beasts, lest he defile himself therewith: I am the LORD. 9They shall therefore keep MymyMy charge, lest they incur guilt for it and die thereby when they profane it: I am the LORD who sanctifies them.’

Restrictions on the Consumption of Sacred Food by Laypersons

10“No outsider shall eat of a sacred offering; neither a guest of the priest nor a hired labourerlabourerlaborer shall eat of a sacred offering. 11But if a priest acquires a person with his silver, that person may eat of it, and those born in his house may eat of his food. 12If a priest’s daughter is given to a layman, she shall not eat of the sacred contribution. 13But if a priest’s daughter is widowed or divorced, having no offspring, and returns to her father’s house as in her youth, she may eat of her father’s food; yet no outsider shall eat of it.

14“And if anyone eats of a sacred offering inadvertently, he shall add a fifth of its valuation to it and give the sacred offering to the priest. 15They shall not desecrate the sacred offerings of the sons of Israel, which they offer to the LORD, 16and so make them bear the iniquity of trespass in their eating of their sacred things: for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.”

Standards for Acceptable Sacrifices

17The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 18“Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the sons of Israel, and say to them, ‘When any man of the house of Israel or of the sojourners in Israel presents his offering to the LORD, whether for any of their vows or for any of their freewill offerings that they offer to the LORD as a burnt offering,

19“‘it must be a male without blemish, from the cattle, from the sheep, or from the goats, to be accepted for you. 20You shall not offer anything that has a blemish, for it will not be acceptable for you. 21And when anyone offers a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD, to fulfillfulfilfulfill a vow or as a freewill offering, from the cattle or from the flock, to be accepted it must be perfect; there shall be no blemish in it. 22Beasts that are blind, or fractured, or maimed, or afflicted with a running issue, or scurvy, or scabbed—you shall not offer these to the LORD, nor make of them a fire offering upon the altar to the LORD.

23“‘YouyouYou may offer a bull or a lamb that has an elongated or truncated member as a freewill offering, but for a vow it shall not be accepted. 24You shall not offer to the LORD any animal whose testicles are bruised, crushed, torn, or severed; neither shall you do so in your land. 25Nor shall you accept from the hand of a foreigner any of these to offer as the bread of your God, because their corruption is in them and a blemish is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.’”

Further Ordinances on Sacrificial Animals and Covenant Fidelity

26The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 27“When an ox or sheep or goat is born, it shall remain seven days with its mother, and from the eighth day onward it shall be accepted as a fire offering to the LORD. 28You shall not slaughter an ox or a sheep and its young in one day.

29“And when you offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving to the LORD, you shall offer it so that you may be accepted. 30It shall be eaten on that day; you shall leave none of it until morning: I am the LORD. 31You shall therefore keep MymyMy commandments and do them: I am the LORD. 32And you shall not desecrate MymyMy holy name, that I may be sanctified among the sons of Israel. I am the LORD who sanctifies you, 33who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God: I am the LORD.”
