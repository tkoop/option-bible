For the choirmaster; to the tune of “Aijeleth Hashachar”; a psalm of David.

A Cry of Dereliction and the Memory of Covenant Faithfulness

1My God, my God, why have YouyouYou forsaken me? Why are YouyouYou so remote from my deliverance, and from heeding the words of my anguished cry? 2O my God, I cry out by day, yet YouyouYou do not answer; by night, I find no repose. 3Yet YouyouYou are holy, enthroned upon the praises of Israel. 4In YouyouYou our fathers trusted; they trusted, and YouyouYou delivered them. 5They cried to YouyouYou and were saved; they trusted in YouyouYou and were not put to shame.

The Mockery of the Righteous Sufferer and His Agonizing Descent into Death

6But I am a worm and not a man, scorned by men and despised by the people. 7All who behold me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads.

8“He trusts in the the LORD; let the the LORD deliver him; let the the LORD rescue him, since HeheHe delights in him.”

9Yet YouyouYou brought me forth from the womb; YouyouYou made me trust in YouyouYou, even at my mother’s breast. 10From birth I was cast upon YouyouYou; from my mother’s womb YouyouYou have been my God. 11Do not be far from me, for trouble is near, and there is no one to help.

12Many bulls surround me; strong bulls of Bashan encircle me. 13They open their mouths against me like roaring, mauling lions. 14I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint; my heart has turned to wax and melted within me. 15My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue cleaves to the roof of my mouth; YouyouYou lay me in the dust of death.

16For dogs surround me; a company of evildoers encircles me; they have pierced my hands and my feet. 17I can count all my bones; they stare and gloat over me. 18They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.

19But YouyouYou, O the the LORD, be not far off; O my StrengthstrengthStrength, come quickly to help me. 20Deliver my soul from the sword, my precious life from the power of the dog. 21Save me from the mouth of the lion; at the horns of the wild oxen YouyouYou have answered me.

From Deliverance to the Universal Proclamation of the LORD’s Righteousness

22I will declare YouryourYour name to my brothers; in the midst of the assembly I will praise YouyouYou. 23You who fear the the LORD, praise HimhimHim! All you descendants of Jacob, honourhonourhonor HimhimHim! 27All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the the LORD; all the families of the nations will bow down before YouyouYou. 28For dominion belongs to the the LORD, and HeheHe rules over the nations. 29All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who go down to the dust will kneel before HimhimHim—those who cannot preserve their lives. 30Posterity will serve HimhimHim; they will declare the Lord to the next generation. 31They will come and proclaim HishisHis righteousness to a people yet unborn—all that HeheHe has done.
