The Appointment of the Seven

1During those days, as the disciples were multiplying, a murmuring arose on the part of the Hellenistic Jews against the Hebraic Jews, because their widows were being overlooked in the daily distribution of food. 2The twelve summoned the full body of disciples and declared, “It is not fitting for us to abandon the word of God in order to serve tables. 3But, brothers, choose from among yourselves seven men of good repute, full of the SpiritspiritSpirit and wisdom, whom we may appoint to this duty. 4But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word.”

5The proposal found favourfavourfavor with the whole assembly, and they selected Stephen, a man full of faith and the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, together with Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolaus, a proselyte from Antioch. 6These men were presented to the apostles, who, after praying, laid their hands upon them. 7And the word of God continued to increase, and the number of disciples in Jerusalem multiplied greatly; indeed, a large number of priests became obedient to the faith.

Stephen's Miracles and the Synagogue's Opposition

8Stephen, full of grace and power, performed great wonders and signs among the people. 9Then certain men from the synagogue known as that of the FreedmenfreedmenFreedmen, along with Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and others from Cilicia and Asia, rose up and disputed with Stephen. 10Yet they were unable to withstand the wisdom and the SpiritspiritSpirit with which he spoke.

False Accusations and Stephen's Arrest

11Then they secretly instigated men to say, “We have heard him utter blasphemous words against Moses and God.”

12And they stirred up the people, the elders, and the scribes; coming upon him, they seized him and brought him before the Sanhedrin.

13Moreover, they set up false witnesses, who said, “This man never ceases to speak words against this holy place and the law; 14for we have heard him say that this Jesus the Nazarene will destroy this place and change the customs that Moses handed down to us.”

15All those seated in the Sanhedrin, fixing their gaze upon him, saw his face as the face of an angel.
