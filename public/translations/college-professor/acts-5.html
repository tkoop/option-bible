The Deceitful Sale and Its Lethal Repercussions

1A certain man named Ananias, in concert with his wife Sapphira, alienated a piece of property; 2yet, with his wife's complicity, he sequestered a portion of the proceeds, conveying only a fraction and depositing it at the apostles' feet.

3Then Peter said, "Ananias, why has Satan so fully possessed your heart that you have deceived the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit and embezzled part of the price of the field? 4While it remained unsold, was it not your own? And after its sale, was not the money at your disposal? What prompted you to contrive this deed? You have not lied to human beings, but to God."

5Upon hearing these words, Ananias fell and expired; and a profound dread seized all who heard of the matter. 6The younger men then arose, shrouded him, bore him forth, and interred him.

7After an interval of about three hours, his wife entered, ignorant of what had transpired.

8Peter addressed her, "Tell me, did you and Ananias sell the land for this price?"

"Yes, for that price."

9Peter rejoined, "How could you and your husband have colluded to test the SpiritspiritSpirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of those who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out as well."

10Instantly she collapsed at his feet and expired; and when the young men entered and found her lifeless, they carried her out and buried her beside her husband.

11And great fear seized the whole church and all who heard of these events.

Apostolic Wonders, Communal Awe, and the Church's Augmentation

12Now through the apostles' hands many signs and wonders were performed among the people, and they were all gathered in unanimous concord in Solomon's Colonnade.

13Although the populace held them in high esteem, no one else dared to affiliate with them.

14Yet increasing multitudes of men and women, coming to faith, were continually added to the Lord.

15Consequently, they even carried the sick into the streets and laid them on cots and pallets, so that as Peter passed by, at least his shadow might fall upon some of them. 16Moreover, crowds converged from the cities surrounding Jerusalem, bringing the sick and those tormented by unclean spirits, and all of them were healed.

Arrest, Incarceration, and Angelic Emancipation

17Then the high priest and all his associates, who constituted the Sadducean sect, were filled with jealousy. 18They laid hands on the apostles and committed them to the public prison.

19But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the prison doors, led them out, and said, 20"Go and, standing in the temple, speak to the people all the words of this life."

21At daybreak they entered the temple and taught. When the high priest and his associates arrived, they convened the Sanhedrin and the entire council of the elders of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.

22But when the officers reached the prison, they did not find them there; so they returned and reported, 23"We found the prison securely locked and the guards standing at the doors, but upon opening them we found no one inside."

24Upon hearing this, the captain of the temple guard and the chief priests were perplexed, wondering what this might portend. 25Then someone arrived and announced, "Behold, the men you placed in prison are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

26Then the captain went with the officers and brought them, without resorting to force, for they feared the people might stone them.

The Sanhedrin's Interrogation and Gamaliel's Prudential Counsel

27They brought them in and set them before the Sanhedrin, and the high priest interrogated them.

28"We gave you strict orders not to teach in this name," he said, "yet you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching and intend to bring this man's blood upon us."

29Peter and the apostles answered, "We must obey God rather than human beings. 30The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you put to death by hanging HimhimHim on a tree. 31God exalted HimhimHim to HishisHis right hand as Leader and SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, to grant repentance and forgiveness of sins to Israel. 32We are witnesses of these things, and so is the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, whom God has given to those who obey HimhimHim."

33When they heard this, they were enraged and resolved to kill them.

34But a Pharisee named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law held in honourhonourhonor by all the people, rose in the Sanhedrin and ordered the men to be put outside for a short time.

35Then he said to them, "Men of Israel, take heed to yourselves regarding what you intend to do to these men. 36For before these days Theudas rose up, claiming to be someone, and a number of about four hundred men joined him; he was slain, and all who followed him were dispersed and came to nothing. 37After him Judas the Galilean rose up in the days of the census and drew away people after him; he too perished, and all who followed him were scattered.

38"So now I tell you, keep away from these men and leave them alone; for if this plan or this undertaking is of human origin, it will be overthrown. 39But if it is from God, you will not be able to overthrow them, lest you even be found fighting against God."

40They were persuaded by him; and after calling the apostles in, they flogged them, ordered them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and released them.
