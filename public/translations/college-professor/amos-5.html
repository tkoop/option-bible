A Lament over Fallen Israel

1Hear this word, O house of Israel—this dirge that I raise over you: 2“Fallen is Virgin Israel; she shall rise no more. She lies forsaken upon her own land, and there is no one to raise her up.”

3For thus says the Lord, the LORD: “The city that marches out a thousand strong shall have a hundred left, and the one that marches out a hundred strong shall have ten left to the house of Israel.”

A Call to Seek the LORD and Live

4For thus says the LORD to the house of Israel: “Seek MemeMe, and you shall live. 5Do not seek Bethel, nor enter Gilgal, nor cross over to Beersheba; for Gilgal shall surely go into exile, and Bethel shall come to nothing.” 6Seek the LORD and live, lest HeheHe break out like fire in the house of Joseph and devour it, with no one to quench it for Bethel.

7You who turn justice into wormwood and cast righteousness to the ground! 8He who made the Pleiades and Orion, who turns deep darkness into morning and darkens day into night, who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out upon the face of the earth—the LORD is HishisHis name. 9He flashes forth destruction against the strong, so that devastation comes upon the fortress.

10You hate the one who reproves in the gate and abhor the one who speaks with integrity. 11Therefore, because you trample on the poor and exact a tax of grain from him, though you have built houses of hewn stone, you shall not dwell in them; though you have planted pleasant vineyards, you shall not drink their wine. 12For I know how many are your transgressions and how great are your sins—you who oppress the righteous, take bribes, and thrust aside the needy in the gate. 13Therefore the prudent keep silent in such a time, for it is an evil time.

14Seek good and not evil, that you may live; and so the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, just as you say. 15Hate evil and love good, and establish justice in the gate; perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.

Mourning and the Day of the LORD

16Therefore thus says the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord: “There shall be wailing in all the squares, and in all the streets they shall cry, ‘Alas! Alas!’ They shall call the farmers to mourning and those skilled in lamentation to wailing. 17In all the vineyards there shall be wailing, for I will pass through your midst,” says the LORD.

18Woe to you who long for the DaydayDay of the LORD! Why do you long for the DaydayDay of the LORD? It is darkness and not light. 19It will be as when a man flees from a lion and a bear meets him, or when he goes into the house and leans his hand against the wall and a serpent bites him. 20Is not the DaydayDay of the LORD darkness and not light—gloom without any brightness?

The LORD’s Rejection of Hollow Worship

21“I hate, I despise your feasts, and I take no delight in your solemn assemblies. 22Even though you offer MemeMe burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them; and the peace offerings of your fattened cattle I will not regard. 23Take away from MemeMe the noise of your songs; to the melody of your harps I will not listen. 24But let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream. 25Did you bring MemeMe sacrifices and offerings in the wilderness for forty years, O house of Israel? 26You have lifted up Sikkuth, your king, and Kiyyun, your star god—images that you made for yourselves. 27Therefore I will exile you beyond Damascus,” says the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts.
