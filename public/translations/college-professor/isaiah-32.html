The Righteous Reign and the Reordering of Society

1Behold, a king shall reign in righteousness, and princes shall govern with justice. 2Each shall be like a refuge from the wind and a shelter from the storm, like rivulets of water in an arid land, like the shadow of a massive rock in a land of weariness. 3Then the eyes of those who see shall not be dimmed, and the ears of those who hear shall hearken. 4The heart of the rash shall attain understanding, and the tongue of the stammerer shall be quick to utter clear speech.

5No longer shall the fool be called noble, nor shall the knave be styled a magnate. 6For the fool utters folly, and his heart contrives iniquity: to practiseto practiseto practice hypocrisy, to speak perversity against the the LORD, to empty the soul of the hungry, and to withhold drink from the thirsty. 7His implements are malign; he devises stratagems to ruin the meek with false words, even when the needy plead for justice. 8But the nobleman devises noble purposes, and he shall stand firm in them.

A Summons to the Complacent Daughters of Zion

9Rise up, you women who are at ease; hear my voice, you complacent daughters; give ear to my speech. 10In little more than a year you complacent women shall tremble, for the vintage shall be consumed and the harvest shall not arrive. 11Tremble, you women at ease; strip yourselves bare and gird sackcloth about your loins. 12Beat your breasts over the pleasant fields, over the fruitful vine.

13Upon the soil of my people shall spring up thorns and briars, even upon all the houses of rejoicing in the exultant city. 14For the citadel shall be abandoned, the throng of the city deserted; the mound and the watchtower shall become caves forever, a delight for wild asses, a pasture for flocks.

The Outpouring of the Spirit and the Renewal of the Land

15Until the SpiritspiritSpirit is poured upon us from on high, and the wilderness becomes a garden-land, and the garden-land is reckoned a forest. 16Then justice shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness shall abide in the fertile field. 17And the effect of righteousness shall be peace, and the labourlabourlabor of righteousness, quietness and trust forever. 18My people shall dwell in a habitation of peace, and in dwellings of security, and in quiet resting places.

19When hail descends upon the forest, the city shall be humbled in the lowland. 20Blessed are you who sow beside all waters, sending forth the ox and the donkey to roam.
