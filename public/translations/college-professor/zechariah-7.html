An Embassy from Bethel and a Question About Fasting

1In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, that is, Kislev. 2Now Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech, along with his men, to entreat the favourfavourfavor of the LORD.

3"Should we mourn and fast in the fifth month, consecrating ourselves as we have done these many years?" they asked the priests of the house of the LORD of Hosts and the prophets.

The Divine Rebuke of Empty Ritual

4Then the word of the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying:

5"Say to all the people of the land and to the priests: When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months during these seventy years, was it for MemeMe that you fasted? 6And when you eat and when you drink, do you not eat and drink for yourselves? 7Are these not the very words that the LORD proclaimed through the former prophets, when Jerusalem and its surrounding cities were inhabited and tranquil, and the Negev and the Shephelah were populated?"

The Demand for Justice and the Consequences of Hardness

8And the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying:

9"Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Execute true justice, and show lovingkindness and compassion toward one another. 10Do not oppress the widow, the orphan, the sojourner, or the poor, and do not devise evil in your hearts against one another. 11But they refused to heed and turned a stubborn shoulder; they made their ears too dull to hear. 12They hardened their hearts like adamant, so as not to hear the law and the words that the LORD of Hosts had sent by HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit through the former prophets; therefore great wrath came from the LORD of Hosts. 13And as I called and they would not hear, so they will call and I will not hear, says the LORD of Hosts. 14I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations they had not known; the land was desolated behind them, so that no one passed through or returned. They turned the pleasant land into a desolation."
