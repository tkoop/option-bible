A Lament over the Erosion of Piety and the Ascendancy of Deceitful Speech

1Deliver us, O the LORD, for the pious have vanished; indeed, the faithful have disappeared from among the sons of men. 2They speak emptiness to one another; with flattering lips and divided hearts they converse.

3May the LORD cut off all flattering lips and every tongue that utters grandiose claims. 4These are the ones who declare, “By our tongues we shall prevail; our lips are our own—who can be lord over us?”

5“Because of the devastation of the poor and the groaning of the needy, now I will arise,” says the LORD. “I will establish in safety the one who yearns for it.”

6The utterances of the LORD are altogether pure—silver refined in an earthen crucible and purified sevenfold. 7You, O the LORD, will keep them; YouyouYou will guard him forever from this generation.

8The wicked strut about on every side while depravity is exalted among the sons of men.
