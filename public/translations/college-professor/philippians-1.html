Salutation and Thanksgiving

1Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are in Philippi, together with the overseers and deacons: 2grace to you and peace from God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ. 3I give thanks to my God for every remembrance of you, 4always in every prayer of mine making my petition for all of you with joy, 5on account of your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until the present. 6Being convinced of this very proposition, that the OneoneOne who inaugurated a good work in you will bring it to consummation until the day of Christ Jesus. 7It is altogether just for me to entertain this conviction concerning all of you, because I cherish you in my heart, inasmuch as, both in my bonds and in the defencedefencedefense and vindication of the gospel, you are all co-participants with me in grace. 8For God is my witness how I yearn for all of you with the tender mercies of Christ Jesus. 9And this is my prayer: that your love may overflow yet more and more in full knowledge and in all discernment, 10so that you may test and approve what is superior, in order that you may be pure and blameless until the day of Christ, 11having been filled with the fruit of righteousness that is through Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God.

The Progress of the Gospel amid Adversity

12Now I desire you to know, brothers, that my circumstances have rather conduced to the advancement of the gospel, 13so that my bonds have become evident throughout the whole praetorium and to all others, as being in Christ; 14and the majority of the brothers, having gained confidence in the Lord because of my bonds, are far more emboldened to proclaim the word without fear. 15Some, to be sure, proclaim Christ from envy and contention, but others from goodwill. 16The latter do so out of love, knowing that I am set for the defencedefencedefense of the gospel; 17the former announce Christ out of selfish ambition, not with pure motives, imagining that they are adding affliction to my bonds. 18What then? Only that in every manner, whether in pretencepretencepretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed; and in this I rejoice—indeed, I will continue to rejoice.

Christ Magnified in Life and in Death

19For I know that this will result in my deliverance through your supplication and the provision of the SpiritspiritSpirit of Jesus Christ, 20in accordance with my earnest expectation and hope, that in nothing shall I be ashamed, but that with all boldness, as always, so now also Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether by life or by death. 21For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain. 22Yet if to live in the flesh means fruitful labourlabourlabor for me, then what I shall choose I know not. 23For I am constrained between the two, having the longing to depart and be with Christ, for that is far better; 24yet to abide in the flesh is more needful for your sake. 25And being persuaded of this, I know that I shall remain and continue with all of you for your advancement and joy in the faith, 26so that your glorying in Christ Jesus may overflow toward me through my coming to you again.

A Summons to Gospel-Worthy Conduct

27Only let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or remain absent, I may hear of your circumstances, that you stand firm in one spirit, contending together with one soul for the faith of the gospel, 28and in nothing terrified by the adversaries; which is to them an evident token of destruction, but to you of salvation, and that from God. 29For to you it has been graciously granted on behalf of Christ, not only to believe in HimhimHim, but also to suffer for HimhimHim, 30having the same struggle which you saw in me and now hear in me.
