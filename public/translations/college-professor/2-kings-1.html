Ahaziah's Downfall and the Prophetic Denunciation

1After the death of Ahab, Moab rebelled against Israel. 2Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upper chamber in Samaria and was grievously injured; whereupon he dispatched messengers with the instruction: “Go, inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I shall recover from this malady.”

3Yet the angel of the LORD spoke to Elijah the Tishbite: “Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and speak to them, ‘Is it because there is no God in Israel that you go to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?’ Therefore thus says the LORD: ‘You shall not descend from the bed to which you have ascended, but you shall surely die.’” And Elijah went.

5When the messengers returned to him, he said to them, “Why have you returned?” 6They replied, “A man came up to meet us and said to us, ‘Go, return to the king who sent you, and speak to him, “Thus says the LORD: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you send to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore you shall not descend from the bed to which you have ascended, but you shall surely die.”’”

7He asked them, “What was the appearance of the man who came up to meet you and uttered these words to you?” 8They answered, “He was a hirsute man, girded with a leather belt about his loins.” And he said, “It is Elijah the Tishbite.”

The Incineration of the Royal Detachments

9Thereupon the king sent to him a captain of fifty with his fifty men, and he ascended to him; and behold, Elijah was seated upon the summit of the hill. And he said to him, “O man of God, the king says, ‘Come down.’” 10Elijah answered the captain of fifty, “If I am indeed a man of God, let fire descend from Heavenheavenheaven and consume you and your fifty.” Then fire descended from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed him and his fifty.

11Again he sent another captain of fifty with his fifty. He approached and said to him, “O man of God, thus says the king: ‘Come down quickly.’” 12Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, let fire descend from Heavenheavenheaven and consume you and your fifty.” Then the fire of God descended from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed him and his fifty.

13So he sent yet a third captain of fifty with his fifty. The third captain of fifty ascended, came, and fell upon his knees before Elijah, and supplicated him, saying, “O man of God, pray let my life, and the life of these fifty servants of your, be precious in your eyes. 14Behold, fire descended from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed the two former captains and their fifties; but now let my life be precious in your eyes.”

Elijah's Descent and the Demise of Ahaziah

15Then the angel of the LORD said to Elijah, “Go down with him; do not fear him.” So he arose and went down with him to the king. 16And he said to him, “Thus says the LORD: ‘Because you have sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron—is it because there is no God in Israel?—therefore you shall not descend from the bed to which you have ascended, but you shall surely die.’”

17So he died in accordance with the word of the LORD that Elijah had spoken. Because he had no son, Jehoram reigned in his stead, in the second year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah. 18Now the rest of the acts of Ahaziah that he performed, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?
