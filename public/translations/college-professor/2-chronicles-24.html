Joash’s Accession and the Renewal of the Temple

1Joash was seven years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem; his mother was Zibiah of Beersheba. 2Throughout all the days of Jehoiada the priest, Joash executed what was righteous in the sight of the LORD. 3Jehoiada secured two wives for him, and he begot sons and daughters.

4Afterward, Joash resolved in his heart to restore the house of the LORD. 5He assembled the priests and the Levites and charged them, “Proceed into the cities of Judah and collect, year by year, the monetary offering from all Israel for the repair of the house of your God; and you must expedite this affair.” Yet the Levites did not hasten.

6The king summoned Jehoiada the chief priest and demanded, “Why have you not required the Levites to bring from Judah and Jerusalem the tax imposed by Moses, the servant of the LORD, and by the congregation of Israel, for the tent of testimony?”

7For the sons of the wicked Athaliah had breached the house of God and had even devoted all the consecrated things of the house of the LORD to the Baals. 8At the king’s command, they fashioned a chest and placed it outside, at the gate of the house of the LORD. 9Then a proclamation was issued throughout Judah and Jerusalem, calling upon the people to bring to the LORD the tax that Moses, the servant of God, had levied upon Israel in the wilderness.

10All the officials and all the people rejoiced; they brought their contributions and cast them into the chest until it was full. 11Whenever the chest was brought by the Levites to the king’s overseers, and whenever they perceived that the silver was abundant, the king’s scribe and the chief priest’s officer would come, empty the chest, and carry it back to its place. Thus they proceeded day by day, amassing silver in profusion. 12The king and Jehoiada entrusted it to those executing the work of the service of the house of the LORD; they hired masons and carpenters to restore the house of the LORD, and likewise smiths of iron and bronze to reinforce the house of the LORD.

13The artisans labouredlabouredlabored, and the restoration progressed; they reestablished the house of God according to its original plan and strengthened it. 14When they had completed the work, they brought the remaining silver before the king and Jehoiada, and from it they fashioned vessels for the house of the LORD—vessels for service, for burnt offerings, and spoons, as well as vessels of gold and silver. Throughout all the days of Jehoiada, burnt offerings were continually presented in the house of the LORD.

Jehoiada’s Death and the Apostasy of Judah

15Jehoiada grew aged and full of days; he died at one hundred thirty years of age. 16He was interred among the kings in the city of David, because he had wrought good in Israel, and toward God, and toward HishisHis house.

17After Jehoiada’s death, the princes of Judah came and paid homage to the king; then the king heeded them. 18They abandoned the house of the LORD, the God of their fathers, and served the Asherim and the idols; consequently, wrath descended upon Judah and Jerusalem for this guilt. 19He sent prophets to them to turn them back to the LORD, and they bore witness against them, yet they would not listen.

The Martyrdom of Zechariah

20Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of God clothed Zechariah, the son of Jehoiada the priest, and he stood above the people and proclaimed, “Thus says God: Why do you transgress the commandments of the LORD, so that you cannot prosper? Because you have forsaken the LORD, HeheHe has forsaken you.”

21They conspired against him at the king’s behest and stoned him with stones in the court of the house of the LORD. 22King Joash did not remember the kindness that Jehoiada his father had shown him, but slew his son. As he was dying, he cried, “May the LORD see and require it.”

Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment and the Assassination of Joash

23At the turn of the year, the army of Aram marched against him; they invaded Judah and Jerusalem and destroyed all the princes of the people, and they sent all their spoil to the king of Damascus. 24Although the Aramean force came with few men, the LORD delivered a very great army into their hand, because they had forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers; thus judgmentjudgementjudgment was executed upon Joash.

25When they withdrew, they left him in severe afflictions; his servants conspired against him on account of the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest, and they assassinated him upon his bed, and he died. He was buried in the city of David, but not in the tombs of the kings. 26These were the conspirators against him: Zabad, the son of Shimeath the Ammonitess, and Jehozabad, the son of Shimrith the Moabitess. 27As for his sons, and the magnitude of the burdens imposed upon him, and the founding of the house of God, behold, they are recorded in the commentary on the book of the kings. His son Amaziah reigned in his stead.
