A Lament Beside the Rivers of Babylon

1By the watercourses of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion. 2Upon the willows within it we hung our lyres.

3For there our captors demanded words of song from us, and our tormentors demanded mirth, saying, “Sing for us one of the songs of Zion.”

4How, then, could we sing the song of the LORD upon alien soil?

5If I forget you, O Jerusalem, let my right hand forget its skill. 6Let my tongue cleave to the roof of my mouth, if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem above my chief joy.

7Remember, O the LORD, against the sons of Edom the day of Jerusalem, when they said, “Raze it, raze it, even to its foundation!”

8O daughter of Babylon, you who are doomed to devastation, blessed is the one who repays you the recompense you have dealt to us. 9Blessed is the one who seizes your little ones and dashes them against the rock.
