Paul’s Obedience to the Vision

19“Therefore, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision. 20Rather, I first preached to those in Damascus and Jerusalem, throughout all the region of Judea, and to the Gentiles, that they should repent and turn to God, performing deeds worthy of repentance. 21For this reason the Jews seized me in the temple and tried to kill me. 22But having received help from God, I have stood firm to this day, testifying to both small and great, saying nothing beyond what the prophets and Moses said would happen: 23that the Christ must suffer and that, as the first to rise from the dead, HeheHe would proclaim light to both our people and the Gentiles.”

28Agrippa said to Paul, “In a short time you would persuade me to become a Christian.”

29Paul replied, “I would pray to God that, whether in a short time or a long one, not only you but all who hear me today might become such as I am, except for these chains.”

30Then the king rose, and the governor, and Bernice, and those seated with them. 31When they had withdrawn, they spoke with one another, saying, “This man has done nothing deserving of death or imprisonment.”
