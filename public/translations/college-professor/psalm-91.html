A Psalm of Refuge in the Almighty

1He who inhabits the covert of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh shall lodge in the shadow of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 2I will say of the LORD, "HeheHe is my refuge and my fortress, my God, in Whom I will trust."

Deliverance from Perils Seen and Unseen

3Surely HeheHe will deliver you from the trapper's snare and from the deadly plague. 4He will cover you with HishisHis feathers, and under HishisHis wings you shall take refuge; HishisHis truth shall be your shield and bulwark.

5You shall not fear the terror of the night, nor the arrow that flies by day, 6nor the pestilence that stalks in darkness, nor the destruction that lays waste at noon. 7A thousand may fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand, but it shall not approach you. 8You shall only look with your eyes and see the recompense of the wicked.

The LORD as Dwelling Place and Guardian

9Because you have made the LORD—my refuge, the MostmostMost HighhighHigh—your dwelling place, 10no evil shall befall you, nor shall any plague come near your tent.

11For HeheHe will command HishisHis angels concerning you, to guard you in all your ways. 12They will bear you up in their hands, lest you strike your foot against a stone. 13You shall tread upon the lion and the cobra; you shall trample the young lion and the serpent.

The LORD's Promise to the Devoted

14"Because he has devoted himself to MemeMe, I will deliver him; I will set him on high, because he has known MymyMy name. 15He will call upon MemeMe, and I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him and honourhonourhonor him. 16With long life I will satisfy him, and I will show him MymyMy salvation."
