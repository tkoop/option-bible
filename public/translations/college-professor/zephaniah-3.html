The Condemnation of the Oppressing City

1Woe to the oppressing city, rebellious and defiled! 2She has not heeded the voice; she has not accepted correction. She has not trusted in the LORD; she has not drawn near to her God. 3Her officials within her are roaring lions; her judges are wolves that prowl at dusk and devour everything before dawn. 4Her prophets are reckless, faithless men; her priests profane the sanctuary and do violence to the law. 5The LORD within her is righteous; HeheHe does no wrong. Morning by morning HeheHe dispenses justice; at dawn HeheHe does not fail; yet the unjust know no shame.

6“I have cut off nations; their corner towers are desolate. I have laid waste their streets, so that no one passes through. Their cities are destroyed, without inhabitant. 7I said, ‘Surely you will fear MemeMe and accept correction.’ Then her dwelling would not be cut off, nor all that I have appointed against her. Yet they were eager to corrupt all their deeds. 8Therefore wait for MemeMe,” declares the LORD, “until the day I rise up to plunder. For MymyMy determination is to gather nations, to assemble kingdoms, to pour out upon them MymyMy indignation—all MymyMy burning anger. For in the fire of MymyMy jealousy all the earth will be consumed. 9For then I will restore to the peoples a pure language, that all of them may call upon the name of the LORD and serve HimhimHim with one accord. 10From beyond the rivers of Cush MymyMy worshipers, MymyMy dispersed ones, will bring MemeMe an offering. 11On that day you will not be put to shame for any of the deeds by which you have rebelled against MemeMe. For then I will remove from among you those who rejoice in their pride, and you will never again be haughty on MymyMy holy mountain. 12And I will leave in your midst a people humble and lowly, and they will trust in the name of the LORD. 13The remnant of Israel will not do wrong or speak lies, nor will a deceitful tongue be found in their mouths. For they will feed and lie down, and no one will make them afraid.”
