The LORD's Summons and the Rechabites' Testing

1The oracle that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying: 2"Go to the house of the Rechabites, speak to them, bring them into one of the chambers of the house of the LORD, and give them wine to drink."

3So Jeremiah took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah son of Habaziniah, his brothers, all his sons, and the entire house of the Rechabites. 4He brought them into the house of the LORD, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which lay beside the chamber of the officials, above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum, keeper of the threshold. 5Then he set before the Rechabites goblets full of wine and cups, and said to them, "Drink wine."

The Rechabites' Ancestral Vow and Their Flight to Jerusalem

6They replied, "We will not drink wine, for our father Jonadab son of Rechab commanded us that we and our sons should never drink wine. 7He also commanded that we should not build houses, sow seed, or plant vineyards for ourselves, that no vineyard should belong to us, and that we should dwell in tents all our days, so that we might live many days upon the face of the land where we sojourn. 8So we have obeyed the voice of our father Jonadab son of Rechab in all that he commanded us: we, our wives, our sons, and our daughters have never drunk wine. 9We have not built houses in which to dwell, and we have possessed no vineyard, field, or seed. 10We have dwelt in tents and have done everything our father Jonadab commanded us. 11But when Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came up against the land, we determined to go to Jerusalem, away from the armies of the Chaldeans and the Arameans, and so we have dwelt in Jerusalem."

Judah's Disobedience Measured Against Rechabite Fidelity

12Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 13"Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Go and say to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, 'Will you not receive instruction and listen to MymyMy words?' declares the LORD. 14The words of Jonadab son of Rechab, which he commanded his sons not to drink wine, have been upheld; they have not drunk wine to this day, because they obeyed their father's command. But I have spoken to you repeatedly and earnestly, and you have not listened to MemeMe. 15I have sent to you all MymyMy servants the prophets, repeatedly and earnestly, saying, 'Turn now, each of you, from his evil way, amend your conduct, and do not go after other gods to serve them; then you shall dwell in the land that I gave to you and to your fathers.' Yet you have not inclined your ear or listened to MemeMe. 16Because the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab have kept the commandment of their father that he commanded them, while this people has not listened to MemeMe. 17Therefore thus says the LORD, the God of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I am bringing upon Judah and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the calamity that I have spoken against them, because I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer."

The Reward of Rechabite Obedience

18And to the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, "Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Because you obeyed the commandment of your father Jonadab and kept all his commandments and did everything he commanded you, 19therefore thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Jonadab son of Rechab shall never lack a man to stand before MemeMe all the days."
