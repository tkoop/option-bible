The Indictment of Jerusalem's Bloodshed

1The word of the the LORD came to me, saying:

2“You, son of man, will you judge—will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then declare to her all her abominations. 3And say: Thus says the Lord the the LORD: O city that sheds blood in her midst, so that her day of reckoning may come, and that fashions idols for herself to defile herself! 4You have become guilty by the blood you have shed, and you have been defiled by the idols you have made; you have hastened your days and brought on your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all the lands. 5Those near and those far from you will mock you—you whose name is defiled and whose turmoil is great. 6Behold, the princes of Israel within you have each turned his strength to the shedding of blood. 7Father and mother they have treated with contempt in you; they have oppressed the sojourner in your midst; orphan and widow they have wronged in you.

8“My holy things you have despised, and MymyMy Sabbaths you have profaned. 9In you are slanderers who shed blood; in you they feast upon the mountains; in your midst they commit lewdness. 10In you they have uncovered a father’s nakedness; in you they have violated a woman during her menstrual impurity. 11One man commits an abomination with his neighbourneighbourneighbor’s wife; another lewdly defiles his daughter-in-law; another humbles his sister, his father’s daughter, in you. 12In you they take bribes to shed blood; you take interest and usury, and you extort your neighbors; and you have forgotten MemeMe, declares the Lord the the LORD.

13“And behold, I have struck MymyMy hand at your dishonest gain that you have made, and at the blood that has been in your midst. 14Can your heart endure, or can your hands remain strong, in the days when I deal with you? I the the LORD have spoken, and I will do it. 15I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the countries, and I will purge your filthiness from you. 16You will be profaned in your own sight before the nations, and you will know that I am the the LORD.”
