Philistine Mobilization and David’s Commission

1Now it came to pass in those days that the Philistines mustered their hosts for war against Israel; and Achish said to David, “You must understand assuredly that you and your men shall march out with me in the campaign.”

2David replied, “Therefore you know what your servant will do.” Achish then said to David, “Therefore I will appoint you the perpetual guardian of my person.”

Samuel’s Death and the Gathering of Armies

3Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned him and buried him in Ramah, his own city; and Saul had expelled from the land the mediums and the spiritists.

4The Philistines assembled and advanced, encamping at Shunem; Saul likewise gathered all Israel, and they encamped at Gilboa.

5When Saul beheld the Philistine encampment, he was seized with fear, and his heart trembled exceedingly.

6When Saul consulted the LORD, the LORD gave him no answer—neither by dreams, nor by the Urim, nor by prophets.

Saul’s Desperate Resort to the Medium at En-dor

7Saul then said to his servants, “Seek for me a woman who is a medium, that I may go to her and inquire through her.”

His servants replied, “Behold, there is such a woman at En-dor.”

8Saul disguised himself, putting on other garments, and set out with two men; they came to the woman by night. He said, “Please perform divination for me, and bring up for me whomever I name to you.”

9The woman said to him, “You surely know what Saul has done—how he has extirpated mediums and spiritists from the land. Why then do you imperil my life to bring about my death?”

10Saul swore to her by the LORD, saying, “As the LORD lives, no guilt shall be imputed to you in this matter.”

11The woman asked, “Whom shall I bring up for you?”

He answered, “Bring up Samuel for me.”

The Apparition of Samuel and Saul’s Doom

12When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice and said to Saul, “Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!”

13The king said to her, “Do not be afraid; but tell me what you see.”

The woman said to Saul, “I see a divine being ascending from the earth.”

14He asked her, “What is his appearance?”

She replied, “An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe.” Saul then recognizedrecogniserecognized that it was Samuel; he bowed with his face to the ground and prostrated himself.

15Samuel said to Saul, “Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?”

Saul answered, “I am in great distress: the Philistines are waging war against me, God has withdrawn from me, and HeheHe no longer answers me—neither through prophets nor through dreams. Therefore I have summoned you, that you may disclose to me what I ought to do.”

16Samuel said, “Why do you inquire of me, seeing that the LORD has departed from you and has become your adversary? 17The LORD has done what HeheHe foretold through me: the LORD has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbourneighbourneighbor—to David. 18Because you did not obey the LORD and did not execute HishisHis fierce wrath against Amalek, the LORD has brought this upon you today. 19Moreover, the LORD will deliver Israel, together with you, into the hand of the Philistines; tomorrow you and your sons shall be with me. Indeed, the LORD will give the camp of Israel into the hand of the Philistines.”

20Saul immediately fell full length to the ground, overcome with great fear at Samuel’s words; moreover, no strength remained in him, for he had eaten no bread all that day and all that night.

21The woman came to Saul and, seeing that he was utterly terrified, said to him, “Behold, your maidservant has hearkened to your voice; I have risked my life and have obeyed the words that you spoke to me. 22Now, therefore, please let your maidservant set a morsel of bread before you, that you may eat and recover your strength for the journey.”

23He refused, saying, “I will not eat.” Yet his servants, together with the woman, pressed him; he yielded to their voice, rose from the ground, and sat upon the bed.

24The woman had a fattened calf in the house; she quickly slaughtered it, took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread from it.

25She brought it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they arose and departed that night.
