The Ark's Sojourn in Philistine Territory

1The ark of the LORD remained in the territory of the Philistines for seven months.

The Philistines Consult Their Priests and Diviners

2The Philistines summoned their priests and diviners and inquired, "What are we to do with the ark of the LORD? Instruct us how we might restore it to its rightful place."

3They replied, "If you are sending away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it away empty; rather, you must surely return a guilt offering to HimhimHim. Then you will be healed, and it will become clear to you why HishisHis hand has not withdrawn from you."

4They asked, "What guilt offering ought we to return to HimhimHim?"

They answered, "Five golden tumors and five golden mice, corresponding to the number of Philistine lords, since the same plague has afflicted you all and your lords. 5You must fashion replicas of your tumors and of the mice that are ravaging the land, and thereby honourhonourhonor the God of Israel; perhaps HeheHe will relent and withdraw HishisHis hand from you, from your gods, and from your land. 6Why do you harden your hearts as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened theirs? When HeheHe had dealt harshly with them, did they not send the people away, so that they departed? 7Therefore, prepare a new cart, take two milk cows that have never been yoked, harness them to the cart, and send their calves away from them. 8Place the ark of the LORD on the cart, put the golden objects you are returning as a guilt offering in a chest beside it, and send it on its way. 9Then watch: if it travels the road toward Beth-shemesh, along its own border, then HeheHe has inflicted this great calamity upon us; but if not, we shall know that HishisHis hand did not strike us and that what befell us was mere chance."

The Ark Is Sent Away and Returns to Beth-shemesh

10The men did as instructed: they took two milk cows, yoked them to the cart, and penned their calves at home. 11They placed the ark of the LORD on the cart, along with the chest containing the golden mice and the replicas of their tumors. 12The cows went straight along the road to Beth-shemesh, keeping to the highway, lowing as they went and turning neither right nor left, while the Philistine lords followed them as far as the border of Beth-shemesh. 13Now the people of Beth-shemesh were harvesting wheat in the valley; when they looked up and saw the ark, they rejoiced at the sight. 14The cart entered the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh and stopped beside a large stone. They split the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD. 15The Levites lowered the ark of the LORD and the chest beside it, which held the golden objects, and set them upon the large stone. That day the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and presented sacrifices to the LORD. 16When the five Philistine lords saw this, they returned to Ekron that same day. 17These golden tumors, which the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to the LORD, were one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, and one for Ekron. 18The golden mice corresponded in number to all the Philistine cities under the five lords—both fortified towns and rural settlements—right up to the great stone of Abel, where they set down the ark of the LORD, a stone that remains to this day in the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment at Beth-shemesh

19Yet HeheHe struck down fifty thousand and seventy of the people because they had looked into the ark of the LORD, and the people mourned because the LORD had struck the people with a great blow.

20The men of Beth-shemesh exclaimed, "Who can stand before the LORD, this holy God? And to whom can we send it away from us?"
