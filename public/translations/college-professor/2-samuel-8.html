David’s Victories and the Subjection of the Nations

1After these events, David defeated the Philistines and subdued them; he also wrested Metheg-ammah from their grasp. 2He likewise defeated Moab, forcing them to lie prostrate upon the ground and measuring them with a cord: two cords’ worth he consigned to death, while a full cord’s worth he spared. Thus Moab became tributary to David, rendering tribute.

3David also defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as the latter marched to reassert his dominion at the Euphrates River. 4From him David captured seventeen hundred horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers; he hamstrung all the chariot horses, though he retained enough for a hundred chariots.

5When the Arameans of Damascus came to Hadadezer’s aid, David struck down twenty-two thousand of them. 6David then stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became tributary to David, bringing tribute. Moreover, the LORD granted David victory in all his campaigns.

7David seized the golden shields carried by Hadadezer’s officers and conveyed them to Jerusalem. 8From Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took an exceedingly great quantity of bronze.

Tribute from Hamath and the Consecration of the Spoils

9When Toi, king of Hamath, heard that David had defeated Hadadezer’s entire host, 10Toi sent his son Joram to King David to greet him and to congratulate him on his triumph over Hadadezer—for Hadadezer had been Toi’s adversary—and Joram brought vessels of silver, gold, and bronze.

11King David consecrated these to the LORD, together with the silver and gold he had dedicated from all the nations he had subdued: 12from Aram, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, Amalek, and the plunder of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

13David thereby won renown when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand Arameans in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 14He stationed garrisons throughout Edom—throughout all Edom—and all Edom became subject to David. The LORD also granted David victory in every campaign.

David’s Reign and Royal Administration

15So David reigned over all Israel, administering justice and righteousness to all his people.

16Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud served as recorder. 17Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Seraiah was scribe. 18Benaiah son of Jehoiada oversaw the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and David’s sons were priests.
