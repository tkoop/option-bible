Praise for the LORD’s Enduring Works

1Hallelujah! I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, in the council of the upright and in the assembly. 2The works of the LORD are great, diligently studied by all who delight in them. 3SplendourSplendourSplendor and majesty are HishisHis work, and HishisHis righteousness endures forever.

4He has made HishisHis wondrous works to be remembered; the LORD is gracious and merciful. 5He gives food to those who fear HimhimHim; HeheHe remembers HishisHis covenant forever. 6He has shown HishisHis people the power of HishisHis works, to give them the inheritance of the nations.

7The works of HishisHis hands are truth and justice; all HishisHis precepts are trustworthy, 8established forever and ever, accomplished in truth and uprightness. 9He has sent redemption to HishisHis people; HeheHe has ordained HishisHis covenant forever; holy and awesome is HishisHis name. 10The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all who practice HishisHis precepts have good understanding. His praise endures forever.
