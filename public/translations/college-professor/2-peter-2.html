The Imminent Peril of False Teachers and Their Destructive Factions

1But just as false prophets arose among the people, so too false teachers will arise among you, who will surreptitiously introduce destructive factions, even repudiating the Master who ransomed them, thereby precipitating swift ruin upon themselves. 2Many will emulate their licentiousness, on account of whom the way of truth will be traduced; 3and in their avarice they will exploit you with fabricated discourses, whose judgmentjudgementjudgment from antiquity is not inert, nor does their destruction slumber.

4For if God did not spare angels who sinned, but consigned them to Tartarus, delivering them to chains of gloom to be reserved for judgmentjudgementjudgment; 5and did not spare the antediluvian cosmos, but preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness, together with seven others, when HeheHe brought a deluge upon a world of the impious; 6and condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to devastation, reducing them to ashes, and set them forth as an exemplar for the impious who are to come; 7and rescued righteous Lot, who was vexed by the wanton conduct of the lawless— 8for that righteous man, dwelling among them, tormented his righteous soul day by day through the lawless deeds he observed and heard— 9then the Lord knows how to deliver the devout from trial and to reserve the unrighteous for punishment until the day of judgmentjudgementjudgment.

10And especially those who proceed in the defiling desire of the flesh and disdain lordship: audacious and self-willed, they do not tremble as they blaspheme glorious beings, 11whereas angels, though superior in might and power, do not bring against them a reviling judgmentjudgementjudgment before the Lord. 12But these men, like irrational beasts, born as natural creatures for capture and corruption, reviling what they do not comprehend, shall likewise be corrupted in their own corruption, 13receiving the wages of unrighteousness as the recompense of wrongdoing. They account revelry in broad daylight a pleasure; they are spots and blemishes, carousing in their deceptions while they feast with you, 14having eyes brimming with adulterous desire and insatiable for sin, enticing unstable souls, accursed offspring, their hearts exercised in covetousness.

The Way of Balaam and the Doom of the Ungodly

15Abandoning the straight path, they have wandered, following the way of Balaam son of Beor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness, 16but received a rebuke for his own transgression: a mute beast of burden, speaking with a human voice, restrained the prophet's madness. 17These are waterless springs and mists driven by a tempest; the blackness of darkness has been reserved for them. 18For uttering bombastic vanities, they entice, through the lusts of the flesh, those who have only barely escaped from those who live in error, 19promising them liberty while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by whatever one is overcome, by that he is enslaved. 20For if, after escaping the defilements of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior Jesus Christ, they are again entangled therein and overcome, their last condition has become worse than the first. 21For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than, having known it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them. 22The true proverb has befallen them: "A dog returns to its own vomit," and "a sow, after washing, returns to wallowing in the mire."
