A Dirge over the Princes of Israel

1You are to intone a dirge over the princes of Israel. 2And declare, “What was your mother? A lioness who crouched among lions and reared her cubs amid young lions.”

The First Young Lion

3She nurtured one of her cubs until he became a young lion; he learned to rend prey and devoured human beings. 4The nations heard of him; he was ensnared in their pit and dragged with hooks to the land of Egypt.

The Second Young Lion

5When she perceived that her hope had vanished, she took another cub and raised him to be a young lion. 6He prowled among lions; he became a young lion; he learned to rend prey and devoured human beings. 7He ravaged their widows and devastated their cities; the land and all its fullness were aghast at the sound of his roaring. 8Nations from the provinces round about arrayed themselves against him; they spread their net over him, and he was taken in their pit. 9They confined him in a cage with hooks and conveyed him to the king of Babylon; they incarcerated him in strongholds, so that his voice would no longer resound upon the mountains of Israel.

The Uprooted Vine

10Your mother was like a vine of your own bloodline, planted beside the waters; it was fruitful and luxuriantly branched because of abundant water. 11She had stalwart boughs, fit to be scepters of rulers; her stature towered above the dense foliage, and she was conspicuous in her height amid the profusion of her branches. 12Yet she was uprooted in fury and cast to the ground; the east wind withered her fruit; her stout boughs were shattered and dried, and fire consumed them. 13Now she is planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty land. 14Fire has issued from the rod of her branches and consumed her fruit, so that no sturdy bough remains in her to serve as a sceptresceptrescepter for ruling. This is a dirge, and it shall remain a dirge.
