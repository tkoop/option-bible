Let MymyMy people go, that they may serve MemeMe.’ 4For if you refuse to let MymyMy people go, behold, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your territory. 5They shall cover the surface of the land, so that no one can see the land; and they shall devour the rest that the hail has left to you, and they shall devour every tree that grows for you in the field. 6They shall fill your houses and the houses of all your servants and the houses of all Egypt—such as neither your fathers nor your fathers’ fathers have seen from the day they were upon the earth until this day.’ Then he turned and went out from Pharaoh.

The Courtiers’ Counsel and the Dispute over Departure

7Pharaoh’s servants said to him, “How long shall this man be a snare to us? Let the men go, that they may serve the LORD their God. Do you not yet know that Egypt is ruined?”

8So Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, “Go, serve the LORD your God. But who exactly are those who are going?”

9Moses said, “We will go with our young and our old; we will go with our sons and daughters and with our flocks and herds, for we must hold a festival to the LORD.”

10But he said to them, “The LORD be with you—if I ever let you and your little ones go! See, you are bent upon evil. 11No! Go, you men, and serve the LORD, for that is what you are seeking.” And he drove them out from Pharaoh’s presence.

The Plague of Locusts and Pharaoh’s Feigned Repentance

12Then the LORD said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts, that they may come upon the land of Egypt and devour every herb of the land, all that the hail has left.”

13So Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and the LORD brought an east wind upon the land all that day and all that night; and when morning came, the east wind had brought the locusts. 14The locusts came up over all the land of Egypt and settled throughout the whole territory of Egypt; never before had there been so many locusts, nor shall there ever be again. 15They covered the surface of the whole land, so that the land was darkened, and they devoured every herb of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left; no green thing remained, neither tree nor herb of the field, throughout all the land of Egypt.

16Then Pharaoh hastily summoned Moses and Aaron and said, “I have sinned against the LORD your God and against you. 17Now, please, forgive my sin just this once, and entreat the LORD your God, that HeheHe may remove from me this death.”

18So he went out from Pharaoh and entreated the LORD. 19And the LORD turned the wind into a very strong west wind, which lifted the locusts and drove them into the Red SeaseaSea; not one locust remained in all the territory of Egypt. 20But the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he did not let the Israelites go.

The Plague of Darkness and the Final Ultimatum

21Then the LORD said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward Heavenheavenheaven, that there may be darkness over the land of Egypt—a darkness that can be felt.”

22So Moses stretched out his hand toward Heavenheavenheaven, and there was thick darkness in all the land of Egypt for three days. 23They did not see one another, nor did anyone rise from his place for three days; but all the Israelites had light in their dwellings.

24Then Pharaoh called Moses and said, “Go, serve the LORD; let your little ones also go with you; only let your flocks and your herds remain behind.”

25But Moses said, “You must also give into our hand sacrifices and burnt offerings, that we may sacrifice to the LORD our God. 26Our livestock also shall go with us; not a hoof shall be left behind, for we must take some of them to serve the LORD our God, and we do not know with what we must serve the LORD until we arrive there.”

27But the LORD hardened Pharaoh’s heart, and he would not let them go.

28Then Pharaoh said to him, “Get away from me! Beware, lest you see my face again, for on the day you see my face you shall die.”

29Moses said, “As you say! I will not see your face again.”
