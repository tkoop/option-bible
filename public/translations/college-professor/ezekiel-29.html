An Oracle Against Pharaoh and All Egypt

1In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt. 3Speak, and say: Thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great monster reclining among his rivers, who has said, ‘My Nile is mine; I made it for MyselfmyselfMyself.’

“4I will put hooks in your jaws, and make the fish of your rivers adhere to your scales, and I will draw you up out of the midst of your rivers, together with all the fish of your rivers that cling to your scales. 5I will abandon you in the wilderness, you and all the fish of your rivers; you shall fall upon the open field, and not be gathered or buried. I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the heavens.

“6All the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am the the LORD, because they proved a fragile reed staff to the house of Israel. 7When they grasped you with the hand, you broke and tore all their shoulders; and when they leaned on you, you broke and made all their loins quake.

The Desolation and Humiliation of Egypt

“8Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I will bring a sword against you, and cut off from you both man and beast. 9The land of Egypt shall become a desolation and a waste, and they shall know that I am the the LORD, because he said, ‘The Nile is mine, and I made it.’ 10Behold, I am against you and against your rivers, and I will reduce the land of Egypt to utter waste and desolation, from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush.

“11No human foot shall pass through it, nor shall the foot of beast pass through it; it shall remain uninhabited for forty years. 12I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolated lands, and her cities shall be a desolation for forty years among ruined cities; and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them throughout the countries.

“13For thus says the Lord the LORD: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they were scattered. 14I will restore the fortunes of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros, their native land, and there they shall constitute a lowly kingdom. 15It shall be the lowliest of kingdoms, never again exalting itself above the nations; I will diminish them so that they never again rule over the nations.

“16It shall never again serve as a source of confidence for the house of Israel, but will remind them of their iniquity whenever they turn toward Egypt. Then they will know that I am the Lord the LORD.”

Nebuchadnezzar’s Compensation and the Vindication of Israel

17In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 18“Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made his army perform arduous labourlabourlabor against Tyre; every head was made bald and every shoulder rubbed bare, yet neither he nor his army received any wages from Tyre for the labourlabourlabor they expended against it.

“19Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he shall carry off its wealth, seize its spoil, and plunder its goods; and it shall serve as wages for his army. 20I have given him the land of Egypt as recompense for the labourlabourlabor he performed, because they worked for MemeMe, declares the Lord the LORD.

A Horn Raised for the House of Israel

“21On that day I will cause a horn to spring up for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth in their midst; and they shall know that I am the the LORD.”
