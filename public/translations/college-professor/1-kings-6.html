The Commencement of the Temple's Construction

1In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites had departed from Egypt—in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv, which is the second month—Solomon began to build the house of the LORD. 2The house that King Solomon built for the LORD measured sixty cubits in length, twenty cubits in width, and thirty cubits in height. 3The vestibule before the nave of the house was twenty cubits long, corresponding to the breadth of the house, and ten cubits wide in front of the house. 4He also made for the house windows with beveled frames.

5Against the wall of the house he built an annex encircling the walls of the house on every side, both about the nave and the inner sanctuary, and he made side chambers all around. 6The lowest storeylowest storeylowest story was five cubits wide, the middle six cubits, and the third seven cubits, for he had provided offsets in the wall of the house all around the outside, so that the beams would not be fastened into the walls of the house. 7While the house was being built, it was constructed of finished stone quarried at the site, so that no hammer, axe, or iron tool was heard in the house during its construction. 8The entrance to the middle story was on the right side of the house; and by winding stairs they went up to the middle story, and from the middle to the third. 9He built the house and finished it, and covered the house with beams and planks of cedar. 10He built the annex against the whole house, five cubits high, and fastened the house with cedar timbers.

The LORD's Covenant Promise to Solomon

11And the word of the LORD came to Solomon, saying, 12“Concerning this house that you are building, if you walk in MymyMy statutes and observe MymyMy ordinances and keep all MymyMy commandments by walking in them, then I will establish MymyMy word with you, which I spoke to your father David. 13And I will dwell among the children of Israel and will not forsake MymyMy people Israel.”

The Interior Embellishment of the Sanctuary

14So Solomon built the house and completed it. 15He lined the interior walls of the house with cedar boards, paneling from the floor of the house to the ceiling, and he overlaid the floor of the house with cypress boards. 16He built twenty cubits of the rear of the house with cedar boards, from the floor to the walls, and he constructed it within as an inner sanctuary, the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. 17The house—that is, the nave in front of it—was forty cubits long. 18The cedar within the house was carved with gourds and open flowers; all was cedar, and no stone was visible. 19He prepared the inner sanctuary within the house to set there the ark of the covenant of the LORD. 20The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in height; he overlaid it with pure gold, and he overlaid the altar, which was of cedar. 21Solomon overlaid the interior of the house with pure gold; he passed chains of gold across the front of the inner sanctuary, and he overlaid it with gold. 22He overlaid the whole house with gold until the whole house was finished; he also overlaid with gold the whole altar that was before the inner sanctuary.

23In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits high. 24One wing of the first cherub was five cubits, and the other wing of the first cherub was five cubits; the span from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other was ten cubits. 25The second cherub was also ten cubits; the two cherubim were of one measure and one form. 26The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so was the height of the other cherub. 27He placed the cherubim in the innermost room; their wings were spread, so that the wing of one touched one wall and the wing of the other touched the opposite wall, while their wings touched one another in the middle of the room. 28He overlaid the cherubim with gold.

29He carved all the walls of the house around, both inner and outer chambers, with carved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. 30He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, both in the inner and outer chambers. 31For the entrance to the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel and doorposts occupied one-fifth of the wall. 32The two doors were of olive wood; he carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold, spreading the gold over the cherubim and palm trees. 33So also he made for the entrance to the nave doorposts of olive wood, occupying one-fourth of the wall. 34He made two doors of cypress wood; each door had two leaves that folded. 35He carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold, evenly applied over the carved work. 36He built the inner court with three courses of hewn stone and a course of cedar beams.

The Completion of the Temple

37The foundation of the house of the LORD was laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv. 38In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, the eighth month, the house was completed in all its details and according to all its plans; thus he built it in seven years.
