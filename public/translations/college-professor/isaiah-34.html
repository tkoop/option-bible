A Summons to the Nations and the Cosmic Reckoning

1Draw near, you nations, to hear, and give heed, you peoples! Let the earth hear, together with all that fills it, the world and all its offspring. 2For the LORD has indignation against all the nations and fury against all their host; HeheHe has devoted them to destruction and delivered them to the slaughter.

3Their slain shall be cast out, and the stench of their corpses shall ascend; the mountains shall be drenched with their blood. 4All the host of Heavenheavenheaven shall waste away, and the skies shall be rolled up like a scroll; all their host shall wither, as a leaf withers from the vine and as one withers from the fig tree.

The Sword Sated in Heaven and Unsheathed upon Edom

5For MymyMy sword has drunk its fill in the heavens; behold, it descends in judgmentjudgementjudgment upon Edom, upon the people whom I have devoted to destruction. 6The sword of the LORD is gorged with blood and fattened with fat—from the blood of lambs and goats, from the fat of the kidneys of rams—for the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah and a great slaughter in the land of Edom. 7Wild oxen shall fall with them, and young bulls with the mighty; their land shall be saturated with blood, and their dust shall be made fat with fat. 8For the LORD has a day of vengeance, a year of recompense for the cause of Zion.

The Perpetual Desolation and the Haunting of the Wilderness

9Her streams shall be turned into pitch, and her dust into sulphursulphursulfur; her land shall become burning pitch. 10It shall not be quenched night or day; its smoke shall ascend forever. From generation to generation she shall lie desolate; no travellertravellertraveler shall pass through her forever and ever.

11The desert owl and the screech owl shall possess her, and the great owl and the raven shall dwell in her. He shall stretch over her the line of desolation and the plumb line of emptiness. 12Her nobles—there is no one there to proclaim a kingdom; all her princes shall be nothing.

13Thorns shall spring up in her palaces, nettles and thistles in her fortresses; she shall become a haunt for jackals, a courtyard for ostriches. 14Desert creatures shall meet with hyenas, and wild goats shall cry out to one another; there too Lilith shall settle and find rest. 15There the owl shall nest and lay eggs; she shall hatch them and gather them under her shadow. Indeed, the kites shall gather there, each with its mate.

16Search the book of the LORD and read: not one of these shall be missing; none shall lack its mate. For HishisHis mouth has commanded it, and HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit has gathered them. 17He has cast the lot for them, and HishisHis hand has divided it among them by line. They shall possess it forever; from generation to generation they shall dwell in it.
