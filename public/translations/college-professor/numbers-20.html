The Congregation's Grievance and Miriam's Demise at Kadesh

1In the first month, the entire congregation of the Israelites entered the wilderness of Zin and encamped at Kadesh. There Miriam died and was buried. 2Now the congregation lacked water, whereupon they gathered in opposition to Moses and Aaron.

3"Would that we had expired when our brethren expired before the the LORD! 4Why have you brought the congregation of the the LORD into this wilderness, that we and our beasts should perish here? 5Why have you raised us out of Egypt only to bring us to this execrable place? It affords no grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates, nor water to drink."

Moses and Aaron Seek Divine Direction at the Tent of Meeting

6Moses and Aaron withdrew from the presence of the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and fell upon their faces; then the glory of the the LORD appeared to them.

7The LORD then addressed Moses, saying, 8"Take the staff, and assemble the congregation—you and your brother Aaron—and speak to the rock before their eyes, that it may give forth its water. Thus you shall bring water out of the rock for them and give drink to the congregation and their livestock."

9So Moses took the staff from before the the LORD, as HeheHe had commanded him. 10Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly before the rock, and he said to them, "Listen, you rebels: shall we bring water for you out of this rock?" 11Then Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff; water gushed out abundantly, and the congregation and their livestock drank.

12The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not believe in MemeMe, so as to sanctify MemeMe before the eyes of the Israelites, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them." 13These are the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites contended with the the LORD, and HeheHe manifested HishisHis holiness among them.

Israel's Diplomatic Petition to Edom and Its Refusal

14From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom: "Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the hardship that has befallen us. 15Our ancestors descended into Egypt, and we sojourned there for many days; the Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers.

"16We cried to the the LORD, and HeheHe heard our voice and sent an angel, and brought us out of Egypt. And behold, we are at Kadesh, a city at the extremity of your territory. 17Please let us pass through your land. We will not pass through field or vineyard, nor drink water from a well; we will travel the king's highway, and we will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your territory."

18But Edom said to him, "YouyouYou shall not pass through me, lest I come out against you with the sword."

19The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the highway; and if we drink your water, I and my livestock, then I will pay its price. Only let me pass through on foot—nothing more."

20But he said, "YouyouYou shall not pass through." And Edom came out against them with a massive people and a strong hand.

21Thus Edom refused to let Israel pass through his territory; so Israel turned away from him.

Aaron's Translation of Office and Death upon Mount Hor

22They set out from Kadesh, and the Israelites, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor. 23The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron at Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, saying,

24"Let Aaron be gathered to his people, for he shall not enter the land that I have given to the Israelites, because you rebelled against MymyMy command at the waters of Meribah. 25Take Aaron and his son Eleazar, and bring them up Mount Hor. 26Strip Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar; and Aaron shall be gathered to his people and die there."

27Moses did as the the LORD commanded; and they went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole congregation. 28Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar; and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain. 29When the whole congregation saw that Aaron had breathed his last, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.
