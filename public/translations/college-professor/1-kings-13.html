The Prophet from Judah Confronts Jeroboam's Illicit Altar

1Now behold, Jeroboam was standing beside the altar to offer incense when a man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the word of the LORD. 2He cried out against the altar by the word of the LORD and said, "Altar, altar, thus says the LORD: 'Behold, a son shall be born to the house of David, Josiah by name; upon you he shall sacrifice the priests of the high places who burn incense upon you, and men's bones shall be burned upon you.'" 3And he gave a sign that same day, saying, "This is the sign that the LORD has spoken: Behold, the altar shall be torn apart, and the ashes that are on it shall be poured out."

4When the king heard the word of the man of God, which he had cried against the altar at Bethel, Jeroboam stretched out his hand from the altar and said, "Seize him!" But the hand that he stretched out against him withered, so that he could not draw it back to himself. 5The altar was also torn apart, and the ashes were poured out from the altar, according to the sign that the man of God had given by the word of the LORD.

6The king said to the man of God, "Entreat now the favourfavourfavor of the LORD your God, and pray for me, that my hand may be restored to me." So the man of God entreated the LORD, and the king's hand was restored to him as it had been before. 7Then the king said to him, "Come home with me and refresh yourself, and I will give you a present."

8But the man of God said to the king, "If you were to give me half of your house, I would not go with you, nor would I eat bread or drink water in this place. 9For so I was commanded by the word of the LORD, saying, 'YouyouYou shall not eat bread, nor shall you drink water, nor shall you return by the way that you came.' 10So he went another way and did not return by the way by which he had come to Bethel."

The Old Prophet of Bethel and His Deceitful Summons

11Now an old prophet was dwelling in Bethel, and his sons came and told him all that the man of God had done that day in Bethel, and the words that he had spoken to the king; and they reported them to their father. 12Their father said to them, "Which way did he go?" For his sons had seen the way that the man of God from Judah had taken. 13He said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." So they saddled the donkey for him, and he mounted it.

14He went after the man of God and found him sitting under an oak, and said to him, "Are you the man of God who came from Judah?"

He said, "I am."

15He said to him, "Come home with me and eat bread."

16He said, "I cannot return with you or go with you, nor will I eat bread or drink water with you in this place. 17For a word came to me by the word of the LORD, saying, 'YouyouYou shall not eat bread, nor shall you drink water there, nor shall you return by the way by which you came.'"

18HeheHe said to him, "I also am a prophet like you, and an angel spoke to me by the word of the LORD, saying, 'Bring him back with you to your house, that he may eat bread and drink water.'" But he lied to him.

19So he went back with him and ate bread in his house and drank water. 20And while they were sitting at the table, the word of the LORD came to the prophet who had brought him back.

21He cried out to the man of God who had come from Judah, saying, "Thus says the LORD: 'Because you have rebelled against the mouth of the LORD and have not kept the commandment that the LORD your God commanded you, 22but have returned and have eaten bread and drunk water in the place of which HeheHe said to you, "YouyouYou shall not eat bread and you shall not drink water," your corpse shall not come to the grave of your fathers.' 23And after he had eaten bread and after he had drunk, he saddled the donkey for him—that is, for the prophet whom he had brought back.

The Lion's Vengeance and the Burial of the Man of God

24And as he went, a lion met him on the road and killed him. His corpse was thrown in the road, and the donkey stood beside it, and the lion stood beside the corpse. 25And behold, men were passing by and saw the corpse thrown in the road, and the lion standing beside the corpse. They came and spoke in the city where the old prophet dwelt.

26When the prophet who had brought him back from the road heard of it, he said, "HeheHe is the man of God who rebelled against the mouth of the LORD; therefore the LORD has given him to the lion, which has torn him and killed him, according to the word of the LORD that HeheHe spoke to him."

27He said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." So they saddled it. 28He went and found his corpse thrown in the road, with the donkey and the lion standing beside the corpse. The lion had not eaten the corpse nor torn the donkey. 29The prophet took up the corpse of the man of God, laid it on the donkey, and brought it back; and the old prophet came to the city to mourn and to bury him.

30He laid his corpse in his own grave, and they mourned over him, saying, "Alas, my brother!"

31After he had buried him, he said to his sons, "When I die, bury me in the grave in which the man of God is buried; lay my bones beside his bones. 32For the saying that he cried by the word of the LORD against the altar in Bethel and against all the houses of the high places that are in the cities of Samaria shall surely come to pass."

Jeroboam's Obdurate Apostasy

33After this, Jeroboam did not turn from his evil way, but again made priests for the high places from among all the people. Whoever desired it, he consecrated, and they became priests of the high places. 34And this thing became sin to the house of Jeroboam, even to cut it off and to destroy it from the face of the earth.
