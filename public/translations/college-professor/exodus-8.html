The Plague of Frogs

1Then the LORD said to Moses, “Go in to Pharaoh and declare to him, ‘Thus says the LORD: “Release MymyMy people, that they may serve MemeMe. 2Yet should you refuse to release them, behold, I will plague your entire realm with frogs. 3The Nile shall teem with frogs, which shall ascend into your house, your bedchamber, and upon your bed, into the dwellings of your servants and your people, and into your ovens and your kneading bowls. 4The frogs shall come up upon you, your people, and all your servants.”’”

5And the LORD said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Extend your hand with your staff over the rivers, canals, and pools, and bring frogs up onto the land of Egypt.’” 6So Aaron extended his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land. 7But the magicians replicated the feat by their secret arts and brought frogs up onto the land of Egypt.

8Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, “Implore the LORD to remove the frogs from me and from my people, and I will let the people go to sacrifice to the LORD.”

9Moses said to Pharaoh, “Deign to tell me when I should intercede for you, your servants, and your people, so that the frogs may be cut off from you and your houses and remain only in the Nile.”

10Pharaoh replied, “Tomorrow.”

Moses said, “Let it be as you have said, that you may know that there is none like the LORD our God. 11The frogs shall depart from you and your houses, your servants, and your people; they shall remain only in the Nile.”

12So Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried to the LORD concerning the frogs that HeheHe had brought upon Pharaoh.

13And the LORD did according to the word of Moses: the frogs perished in the houses, the courtyards, and the fields. 14They gathered them into heaps, and the land reeked.

15But when Pharaoh saw that there was respite, he hardened his heart and refused to listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

The Plague of Gnats

16Then the LORD said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Extend your staff and strike the dust of the earth, so that it may become gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.’” 17They did so: Aaron extended his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the earth, and there were gnats on man and beast; all the dust of the earth became gnats throughout all the land of Egypt. 18The magicians attempted by their secret arts to generate gnats, but they could not; so there were gnats on man and beast.

19The magicians said to Pharaoh, “This is the finger of God.” But Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he refused to listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

The Plague of Flies

20Then the LORD said to Moses, “Rise early in the morning and present yourself before Pharaoh as he goes out to the water, and say to him, ‘Thus says the LORD: “Release MymyMy people, that they may serve MemeMe. 21For if you do not release them, behold, I will send swarms of flies upon you, your servants, your people, and your houses; the houses of the Egyptians shall be filled with swarms of flies, and even the ground on which they stand. 22But on that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, where MymyMy people dwell, so that no swarms of flies shall be there, that you may know that I am the LORD in the midst of the earth. 23I will put a division between MymyMy people and your people. This sign shall come to pass tomorrow.”’”

24And the LORD did so: a grievous swarm of flies invaded the house of Pharaoh and the houses of his servants and all the land of Egypt, and the land was devastated by the swarms of flies.

25Then Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said, “Go, sacrifice to your God in the land.”

26But Moses said, “It would not be right to do so, for the sacrifices we offer to the LORD our God are an abomination to the Egyptians. If we sacrifice what is abominable to the Egyptians before their eyes, will they not stone us? 27We must go a three days’ journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God, as HeheHe commands us.”

28Pharaoh said, “I will let you go to sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness; only you must not go very far away. Entreat for me.”

29Moses said, “Behold, I am going out from you, and I will plead with the LORD, and tomorrow the swarms of flies shall depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people. Only let Pharaoh not deal deceitfully again by refusing to release the people to sacrifice to the LORD.”

30So Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD. 31And the LORD did as Moses asked, removing the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; not one remained. 32But Pharaoh hardened his heart this time also, and did not let the people go.
