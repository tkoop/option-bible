An Oracle Against the Valley of Vision

1An oracle concerning the ValleyvalleyValley of Vision: What ails you now, that you have all gone up to the rooftops? 2O city full of shouting, tumultuous city, exultant town! Your slain are not slain by the sword, nor did they die in battle. 3All your leaders fled together; they were captured without a bow being drawn; all who were found in you were bound together, though they had fled from afar.

4Therefore I said, "Turn away from me, that I may weep bitterly; do not press me to be comforted over the ruin of the daughter of my people."

The LORD's Day of Tumult and Jerusalem's Futile Preparations

5For the Lord, the LORD of Hosts has a day of tumult and trampling and confusion in the ValleyvalleyValley of Vision—a breaking down of walls and a crying out to the mountain. 6Elam took up the quiver, with chariots of men and horsemen, and Kir laid bare the shield. 7Your choicest valleys were filled with chariots, and the horsemen stationed themselves at the gate.

8He uncovered the defenses of Judah; and you looked that day to the weapons of the House of the ForestforestForest. 9You have seen the breaches of the CitycityCity of David, that they are many; and you gathered the waters of the lower pool. 10You counted the houses of Jerusalem and tore down houses to fortify the wall. 11You made a reservoir between the two walls for the waters of the old pool, but you did not look to its MakermakerMaker, nor did you regard HimhimHim who fashioned it long ago.

12In that day the Lord, the LORD of Hosts called for weeping and mourning, for baldness and for putting on sackcloth.

13But behold, joy and gladness, slaughtering cattle and killing sheep, eating meat and drinking wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."

14And it was revealed in my ears by the LORD of Hosts: "Surely this iniquity will not be atoned for you until you die," says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.

Shebna's Downfall and Eliakim's Elevation

15Thus says the Lord, the LORD of Hosts: "Go to this steward, to Shebna, who is in charge of the household, and say,

16"What have you here, and whom have you here, that you have hewn out a tomb for yourself here, hewing your tomb on high and carving a dwelling for yourself in the rock?

17"Behold, the LORD will hurl you away violently, and will surely cover you.

18"He will surely roll you up like a ball and throw you into a vast land; there you shall die, and there shall be the chariots of your glory, the shame of your lord's house.

19"I will thrust you from your post, and from your office he will pull you down.

20"And it shall come to pass on that day that I will call MymyMy servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah.

21"I will clothe him with your robe and bind him with your sash; I will commit your authority into his hand. He will be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

22"I will place on his shoulder the key of the house of David; what he opens, no one will shut, and what he shuts, no one will open.

23"I will fasten him like a peg in a secure place, and he will be a throne of honourhonourhonor to his father's house.

24"They will hang on him all the glory of his father's house—offspring and issue, all the small vessels, from bowls to every kind of jar.

25"On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, the peg fastened in the secure place will be removed and cut down and fall, and the burden hanging on it will be cut off; for the LORD has spoken."
