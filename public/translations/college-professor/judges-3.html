Nations Left to Test Israel

1These are the nations that the the LORD left in place to test Israel through them—namely, all who had not known the entirety of Canaan's wars. 2He did so only that the successive generations of Israel's sons might acquire martial knowledge, instructing those who had not previously known such conflicts. 3These included the five Philistine lords, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites inhabiting Mount Lebanon from Mount Baal-hermon to the approach to Hamath. 4They remained to test Israel, to ascertain whether Israel would heed the commandments of the the LORD, which HeheHe had enjoined upon their ancestors through Moses. 5The Israelites settled among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 6They married the daughters of those peoples and gave their own daughters to the sons of those peoples, and thereby served their gods.

Othniel Delivers Israel

7The Israelites perpetrated evil in the the LORD's estimation: they forgot the the LORD their God and served the Baals and the Asherahs. 8Consequently the the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and HeheHe sold them into the power of Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram-naharaim; the Israelites served him for eight years. 9When the Israelites cried out to the the LORD, HeheHe raised up a deliverer for them—Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother—who saved them. 10The SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD came upon him; he judged Israel and went out to battle. The the LORD gave Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram, into his hand, and his hand overpowered Cushan-rishathaim. 11The land then enjoyed rest for forty years, until Othniel son of Kenaz died.

Ehud and Shamgar Deliver Israel

12The Israelites once more perpetrated evil in the the LORD's estimation; therefore the the LORD emboldened Eglon, king of Moab, against Israel, because they had done evil in the the LORD's estimation. 13He mustered the Ammonites and Amalekites, marched against Israel, defeated them, and occupied the CitycityCity of Palms. 14Thus the Israelites served Eglon, king of Moab, for eighteen years.

15When the Israelites cried out to the the LORD, HeheHe raised up a deliverer for them—Ehud son of Gera, a Benjamite, a left-handed man—and through him the Israelites sent tribute to Eglon, king of Moab. 16Ehud fashioned for himself a double-edged dagger roughly a cubit in length and secured it beneath his clothing upon his right thigh. 17He presented the tribute to Eglon, king of Moab, who was an exceedingly corpulent man. 18When he had finished presenting the tribute, he dismissed the men who had carried it.

19He, however, turned back from the carved images near Gilgal and said, “I have a confidential message for you, O king.”

The king replied, “Silence,” and all his attendants withdrew.

20Ehud approached him while he was sitting alone in his private, cool upper chamber, and said, “I have a message from God for you.” The king rose from his seat.

21Ehud extended his left hand, drew the dagger from his right thigh, and plunged it into Eglon's abdomen.

22Even the hilt followed the blade into the wound, and the fat closed over it, so that he could not extract the weapon from his belly; and Eglon's bowels discharged.

23Ehud then exited through the vestibule, closed the doors of the upper chamber behind him, and locked them.

24After he had gone out, his servants arrived and found the doors of the chamber locked; they surmised that he was relieving himself in the cool room.

25They waited until embarrassment overcame them, but he did not open the chamber doors; so they took the key, opened them, and discovered their lord fallen dead upon the floor.

26Ehud escaped while they delayed; he passed beyond the carved images and fled to Seirah.

27When he arrived, he sounded the trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim; the Israelites descended with him from the hill, with him leading them.

28He said to them, “Follow me, for the the LORD has delivered your enemies, the Moabites, into your hand.” They descended after him, seized the fords of the Jordan against Moab, and allowed no one to cross.

29At that time they struck down about ten thousand Moabites, all robust and valiant men; not one escaped.

30Thus Moab was subdued under Israel's hand that day, and the land enjoyed rest for eighty years.

31After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who struck down six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad and likewise delivered Israel.
