Job's Longing to Present His Case Before God

1Then Job answered and said: 2“Even today my complaint is bitter; my affliction outweighs my groaning. 3If only I knew where to find HimhimHim, that I might come to HishisHis seat. 4I would set my case in order before HimhimHim and fill my mouth with arguments. 5I would know the words with which HeheHe would answer me, and discern what HeheHe would say to me. 6Would HeheHe contend with me in HishisHis great power? No—HeheHe would pay heed to me. 7There the upright could reason with HimhimHim, and I should be delivered forever from my JudgejudgeJudge.

Job's Integrity amid God's Elusiveness

8“If I go east, HeheHe is not there; if I go west, I cannot discern HimhimHim. 9When HeheHe works in the north, I cannot behold HimhimHim; when HeheHe turns to the south, I cannot see HimhimHim. 10Yet HeheHe knows the way I have taken; when HeheHe has tested me, I shall come forth as gold. 11My feet have held fast to HishisHis steps; I have kept HishisHis way and have not turned aside. 12I have not departed from the command of HishisHis lips; I have treasured the words of HishisHis mouth more than my daily bread.

The Terror of God's Inscrutable Decrees

13“But HeheHe is unchangeable, and who can turn HimhimHim back? He does whatever HishisHis soul desires. 14For HeheHe carries out what HeheHe has decreed for me, and HeheHe has many such designs. 15Therefore I am dismayed in HishisHis presence; when I ponder this, I dread HimhimHim. 16God has made my heart faint; the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty has terrified me. 17For I am not silenced by the darkness, nor by the thick darkness that covers my face.”
