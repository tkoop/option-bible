The Proliferation of Humanity and the Transgression of the Sons of God

1When the human race began to proliferate across the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them, 2the sons of God beheld that the daughters of men were beautiful, and they took wives for themselves from among all whom they selected.

3The LORD said, “My SpiritspiritSpirit shall not contend with humankind in perpetuity, for he is but flesh; nevertheless, his days shall be limited to one hundred and twenty years.” 4The Nephilim were upon the earth in those days—and also afterward—when the sons of God consorted with the daughters of men, who bore children unto them. These were the mighty men of antiquity, men of renown.

Divine Regret and the Decree of Destruction

5The LORD saw that the wickedness of humankind was great upon the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was solely evil all the day long. 6And the LORD regretted that HeheHe had made humankind upon the earth, and HeheHe was grieved in HishisHis heart.

7The LORD said, “I will blot out humankind, whom I have created, from the face of the ground—both man and beast, creeping thing and bird of the heavens—for I regret that I have made them.” 8But Noah found favourfavourfavor in the eyes of the LORD.

Noah’s Righteousness Amidst Pervasive Corruption

9These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless among his contemporaries; Noah walked with God. 10And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

11Now the earth was corrupted before God, and the earth was filled with violence. 12And God saw the earth, and behold, it was corrupted, for all flesh had corrupted its conduct upon the earth.

The Covenant, the Ark, and the Impending Deluge

13God said to Noah, “The end of all flesh has come before MemeMe, for the earth is filled with violence by their doing; and behold, I am about to destroy them together with the earth. 14Construct for yourself an ark of gopher wood; make compartments within the ark, and seal it inside and out with pitch. 15And this is how you shall make it: the length of the ark shall be three hundred cubits, its breadth fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits. 16You shall make a roof for the ark, finishing it to a cubit above; and you shall set the door of the ark in its side; with lower, second, and third decks you shall make it.

17“And I, behold, I am bringing a deluge of waters upon the earth, to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life from under Heavenheavenheaven; everything that is on the earth shall perish. 18But I will establish MymyMy covenant with you; and you shall enter the ark—you, your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives with you. 19And of every living thing of all flesh, two of every sort you shall bring into the ark, to keep them alive with you; they shall be male and female. 20Of the birds after their kind, and of the beasts after their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of every sort shall come to you, to keep them alive. 21And you shall take for yourself of every food that is eaten, and you shall gather it, and it shall be food for you and for them.”

22And Noah did according to all that God had commanded him; thus he acted.
