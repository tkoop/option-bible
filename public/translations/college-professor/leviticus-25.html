The Sabbath Year and the Jubilee

1The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying, 2"Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you enter the land that I am giving to you, the land itself shall observe a Sabbath to the LORD. 3For six years you shall sow your field, and for six years you shall prune your vineyard and gather its produce; 4but in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of complete rest for the land—a Sabbath to the LORD. You shall not sow your field or prune your vineyard. 5You shall not reap the aftergrowth of your harvest, nor shall you gather the grapes of your unpruned vines; it shall be a year of solemn rest for the land. 6Whatever the land yields during its Sabbath shall be food for you—for yourself, your male and female servants, your hired labourerlabourerlaborer, and the resident alien who dwells with you—as well as for your livestock and the wild animals in your land; all its yield shall be for food. 7All its produce shall be for food.

The Year of Jubilee

8You shall count seven weeks of years, seven times seven years, so that the seven weeks of years amount to forty-nine years. 9Then you shall sound a trumpet blast on the tenth day of the seventh month; on the DaydayDay of Atonement you shall sound the trumpet throughout all your land. 10You shall consecrate the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty throughout the land to all its inhabitants. It shall be a jubilee for you, when each of you shall return to his property and each of you shall return to his clan. 11That fiftieth year shall be a jubilee for you; in it you shall neither sow nor reap the aftergrowth, nor gather the grapes from the unpruned vines. 12For it is a jubilee; it shall be holy to you, and you may eat the produce of the field. 13In this year of jubilee each of you shall return to his property.

Redemption of Land and Houses

23The land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is MinemineMine; for you are strangers and sojourners with MemeMe. 24In all the land of your possession, you shall provide for the redemption of the land. 25If your brother becomes poor and sells part of his property, then his nearest redeemer shall come and redeem what his brother has sold. 26If a man has no one to redeem it, yet his hand attains enough and he finds sufficient for its redemption, he shall calculate the years since its sale and repay the balance to the man to whom he sold it, and so return to his property. 27He shall calculate the years of its sale and restore the surplus to the man to whom he sold it, and then he shall return to his property. 28But if his hand has not attained enough to recover it, then what he sold shall remain in the hand of the buyer until the year of jubilee; in the jubilee it shall be released, and he shall return to his property. 29If a man sells a dwelling house in a walled city, he may redeem it until the end of the year of its sale; for a full year he shall have the right of redemption. 30If it is not redeemed within a full year, then the house in the walled city shall belong permanently to the buyer and his descendants; it shall not be released in the jubilee. 31But the houses in villages that have no wall around them shall be reckoned with the fields of the land; they may be redeemed, and they shall be released in the jubilee. 32As for the cities of the Levites, the houses in the cities of their possession may be redeemed by the Levites at any time. 33If one of the Levites redeems a house, then in the jubilee the sold house shall be released and he shall return to his possession, because the houses in the Levites' cities are their possession among the Israelites. 34But the fields of pastureland belonging to their cities may not be sold, for that is their perpetual possession.

Care for the Impoverished and the Enslaved

35If your brother becomes poor and cannot maintain himself with you, you shall support him as a stranger and a sojourner, and he shall live with you. 36You shall take no interest or profit from him, but you shall fear your God, so that your brother may live with you. 37You shall not lend him your money at interest, nor give him your food for profit. 38I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God. 39If your brother becomes poor beside you and sells himself to you, you shall not make him serve as a slave; 40he shall be with you as a hired worker and as a sojourner. He shall serve with you until the year of jubilee. 41Then he and his children with him shall go out from you and return to his clan and to the possession of his fathers. 42For they are MymyMy servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt; they shall not be sold as slaves. 43You shall not rule over him ruthlessly, but you shall fear your God. 44As for your male and female slaves whom you may have, you may buy male and female slaves from the nations around you. 45You may also buy them from the strangers who sojourn with you and from their clans born in your land, and they may be your property. 46You may bequeath them to your children after you to inherit as property. You may make them serve you forever. But as for your brothers, the Israelites, no one shall rule over his brother ruthlessly. 47If a stranger or sojourner with you becomes rich, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to the stranger or sojourner with you, or to a member of the stranger's clan, 48then after he is sold he may be redeemed. One of his brothers may redeem him, 49or his uncle or his cousin may redeem him, or a close relative from his clan may redeem him; or if he grows rich, he may redeem himself. 50He shall calculate with his buyer from the year he sold himself to him until the year of jubilee, and the price of his sale shall be according to the number of years; he shall be counted with him as a hired worker. 51If there are still many years left, he shall pay for his redemption in proportion to them from the money of his sale. 52If there are few years left until the year of jubilee, he shall calculate and pay for his redemption in proportion to the years. 53He shall be with him as a worker hired year by year; he shall not be ruled over ruthlessly in your sight. 54And if he is not redeemed by these means, then he and his children with him shall go out in the year of jubilee. 55For to MemeMe the Israelites are servants; they are MymyMy servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God."
