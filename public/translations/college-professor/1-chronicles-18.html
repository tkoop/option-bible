David’s Campaigns and the Subjugation of Surrounding Peoples

1Subsequent to these events, David vanquished the Philistines, reduced them to submission, and wrested from their control Gath together with its outlying villages. 2He likewise overcame the Moabites, who thereupon entered into vassalage to David and rendered him tribute. 3Furthermore, David defeated Hadadezer, king of Zobah, as far as Hamath, while the latter was advancing to establish his dominion at the Euphrates River. 4From him David seized a thousand chariots, seven thousand mounted warriors, and twenty thousand infantrymen; he hamstrung all the chariot horses, preserving only a hundred. 5When the Arameans of Damascus advanced to succor Hadadezer, king of Zobah, David slew twenty-two thousand of their men. 6David then stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus; the Arameans became subject to him and paid him tribute. Thus the LORD granted David victory in every campaign he undertook. 7David also seized the golden shields borne by Hadadezer’s officers and conveyed them to Jerusalem. 8From Tibhath and Cun, cities belonging to Hadadezer, David carried off an immense quantity of bronze, which Solomon subsequently employed in fashioning the bronze SeaseaSea, the pillars, and sundry bronze vessels.

9When Tou, king of Hamath, learned that David had annihilated the whole host of Hadadezer, king of Zobah, 10he dispatched his son Hadoram to King David to offer salutations and benedictions, for David had waged war against Hadadezer and vanquished him; indeed, Hadadezer had been hostile to Tou. Hadoram brought articles of every kind in gold, silver, and bronze. 11King David consecrated these, together with the silver and gold he had despoiled from all the nations—Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and Amalek—to the LORD. 12Moreover, Abishai, son of Zeruiah, struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 13He installed garrisons throughout Edom, and all the Edomites were brought under David’s sway. Thus the LORD granted David victory in every campaign.

David’s Governance and the Ordering of His Court

14Thus David reigned over all Israel, dispensing justice and righteousness to all his people. 15Joab, son of Zeruiah, commanded the army; Jehoshaphat, son of Ahilud, served as recorder; 16Zadok, son of Ahitub, and Abimelech, son of Abiathar, were priests; Shavsha was scribe; 17and Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, was over the Cherethites and Pelethites; David’s sons were chief officials in attendance upon the king.
