Ariel Besieged and Humbled

1Woe unto Ariel—Ariel, the city of David! Pile year upon year; let the festivals complete their circuits. 2Yet I will distress Ariel, and there shall be mourning and lamentation, and she shall be unto MemeMe as an altar hearth. 3I shall encamp about you in a ring; I shall besiege you with a siege mound and raise fortifications against you. 4You shall be brought low; you shall speak from the earth, and your speech shall issue from the dust. Your voice shall be faint, and your speech shall be like a spirit’s muttering from the ground, chirping from the dust.

The Dissolution of the Nations’ Siege

5The throng of your enemies shall be reduced to fine dust, and the horde of the ruthless shall pass like chaff—suddenly, in an instant. 6You shall be visited by the LORD of Hosts with thunder and earthquake and great noise, with whirlwind and tempest and the flame of devouring fire. 7It shall be as a dream, a night vision: the whole horde of the nations warring against Ariel—all her besiegers, her stronghold, and her oppressors. 8As when a hungry man dreams that he is eating, but awakens and his soul is empty; or as when a thirsty man dreams that he is drinking, but awakens and is faint, his soul still craving—so shall be the whole horde of the nations warring against Mount Zion.

Sealed Revelation and Spiritual Slumber

9Pause and be astonished; blind yourselves and be blind. They are drunk, but not with wine; they reel, but not with strong drink. 10For the LORD has poured out upon you a spirit of deep sleep; HeheHe has shut your eyes—the prophets—and covered your heads—the seers.

11The whole vision has become to you like the words of a sealed book. If it is given to one who can read, saying, “Please read this,”

HeheHe says, “I cannot, for it is sealed.”

12Or if the book is given to one who cannot read, saying, “Please read this,”

HeheHe says, “I do not know how to read.”

13Then the Lord said: “Because this people draws near to MemeMe with its mouth and honourshonourshonors MemeMe with its lips, while its heart is far from MemeMe, and its reverence for MemeMe is but a human commandment learned by rote— 14therefore, behold, I shall once more work wonders among this people—wonder upon wonder—and the wisdom of their wise shall perish, and the discernment of their discerning shall be hidden.”

Concealment, Reversal, and Jacob’s Vindication

15Woe to those who dig deep to hide their counsel from the LORD, whose works are in darkness, and who say, “Who sees us? Who knows us?”

16You turn things upside down! Shall the potter be counted as clay, that the thing made should say of its maker, “He did not make me,”

Or the thing formed say of its former, “He has no understanding”?

17In but a very little while, shall not Lebanon be turned into a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted as forest? 18In that day the deaf shall hear the words of a book, and the eyes of the blind shall see out of obscurity and out of darkness. 19The humble shall increase their joy in the LORD, and the needy among mankind shall rejoice in the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 20For the ruthless shall cease, and the scorner shall be consumed, and all who watch for iniquity shall be cut off— 21those who make a man guilty for a word, and snare him who rebukes in the gate, and turn aside the righteous with empty charges.

22Therefore thus says the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, concerning the house of Jacob: “Jacob shall not now be ashamed, neither shall his face now grow pale. 23For when he sees his children, the work of MymyMy hands, in his midst, they will sanctify MymyMy name; they will sanctify the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Jacob and stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24Those who err in spirit shall come to understanding, and those who murmur shall learn instruction.”
