The Women at the Tomb and the Angelic Annunciation

1Once the Sabbath had elapsed, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome procured aromatic spices, intending to come and anoint HimhimHim. 2Very early on the first day of the week, at sunrise, they came to the tomb.

3And they were saying among themselves, “Who will roll the stone away for us from the entrance of the tomb?” 4And looking up, they observed that the stone had been rolled away—for it was exceedingly large.

5And entering the tomb, they saw a young man enveloped in a white robe, seated on the right side, and they were overwhelmed with astonishment. 6And he said to them, “Do not be alarmed; you seek Jesus the Nazarene, the Crucified OneoneOne. He has been raised; HeheHe is not here. Behold the place where they laid HimhimHim. 7But go, report to HishisHis disciples and to Peter that HeheHe goes before you into Galilee; there you will see HimhimHim, just as HeheHe said to you.”

8And they went out and fled from the tomb, for trembling and bewilderment had seized them; and they said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid.

Post-Resurrection Appearances of the Lord

9Now after HeheHe had risen, early on the first day of the week, HeheHe appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom HeheHe had expelled seven demons. 10She went and announced it to those who had been with HimhimHim, who were mourning and weeping. 11And when they heard that HeheHe was alive and had been seen by her, they refused to believe.

12Afterward HeheHe appeared in another form to two of them as they were walking on their way into the country. 13And they went and reported it to the rest, but they did not believe them either. 14Later HeheHe appeared to the eleven as they reclined at table, and HeheHe reproached them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they had not believed those who had seen HimhimHim after HeheHe had risen.

The Commissioning, Ascension, and Apostolic Proclamation

15And HeheHe said to them, “Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to all creation. 16Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. 17And these signs will accompany those who believe: in MymyMy name they will cast out demons; they will speak with new tongues. 18They will take up serpents with their hands; and if they drink anything deadly, it will not harm them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover.”

19So then, after the Lord had spoken to them, HeheHe was taken up into Heavenheavenheaven and sat down at the right hand of God. 20And they went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed.
