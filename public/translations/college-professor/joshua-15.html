The Allotment and Boundaries of Judah

1The allotment for the clans of the tribe of Judah reached the territory of Edom, to the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Zin toward the south, at the farthest extremity of the south. 2Their southern boundary began at the tongue of the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea, at the inlet facing southward. 3It issued southward toward the Ascent of Akrabbim, crossed Zin, ascended south of Kadesh-barnea, passed Hezron, ascended to Addar, and bent toward Karka. 4It passed Azmon, reached the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt, and discharged toward the sea; this formed their southern frontier.

5The eastern frontier was the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea to the mouth of the Jordan; the northern frontier began at the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan. 6It ascended to Beth-hoglah, passed north of Beth-arabah, and ascended to the StonestoneStone of Bohan son of Reuben. 7Then the boundary ascended to Debir from the ValleyvalleyValley of Achor, turning north toward Gilgal, which lies opposite the Ascent of Adummim south of the valley; it continued to the waters of En-shemesh and ended at En-rogel. 8It ascended the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben-hinnom to the southern shoulder of the Jebusite city—that is, Jerusalem—then ascended to the summit of the mountain west of the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom, at the northern extremity of the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim.

9From the summit the boundary curved to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah and issued to the cities of Mount Ephron; then it curved to Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim. 10From Baalah it turned westward to Mount Seir, passed along the northern shoulder of Mount Jearim—that is, Chesalon—descended to Beth-shemesh, and crossed to Timnah. 11Then it went out to the northern shoulder of Ekron, curved to Shikkeron, passed along to Mount Baalah, and issued to Jabneel; the boundary ended at the sea. 12The western frontier was the coast of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea. These were the boundaries encircling the clans of the sons of Judah.

Caleb’s Portion and the Conquest of Debir

13By the LORD’s command to Joshua, he gave Caleb son of Jephunneh a portion among the sons of Judah—Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, the city of Arba, father of Anak. 14Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak—Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, descendants of Anak. 15From there he went up against the inhabitants of Debir, formerly Kiriath-sepher.

16And Caleb said, “Whoever attacks Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as a wife.”

17So Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s brother, captured it, and Caleb gave his daughter Achsah to him as a wife.

18When she came, she urged him to request a field from her father; and as she alighted from the donkey, Caleb asked her, “What do you want?”

19“Give me a blessing,” she answered. “Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me also springs of water.” So Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs.

20This was the inheritance of the clans of the tribe of Judah.

The Jebusites Remaining in Jerusalem

63But the descendants of Judah could not drive out the Jebusites, inhabitants of Jerusalem; so the Jebusites dwell with the descendants of Judah in Jerusalem to this day.
