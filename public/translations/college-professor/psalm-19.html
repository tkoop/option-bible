For the choirmaster. A psalm of David.

The Heavens Recount the Glory of God

1The heavens recount the glory of God, and the expanse proclaims the work of HishisHis hands. 2Day after day they pour forth speech, and night after night they disclose knowledge. 3There is no speech, there are no words; their voice is unheard. 4Yet their sound has gone out through all the earth, and their utterances to the end of the world. In them HeheHe has pitched a tent for the sun.

5And it is like a bridegroom coming forth from his chamber; it rejoices like a mighty man to run its course. 6Its rising is from one end of the heavens, and its circuit reaches the other; and nothing is hidden from its heat.

The Perfection of the LORD's Torah

7The law of the LORD is perfect, restoring the soul; the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple. 8The precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes. 9The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring forever; the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments of the LORD are true and altogether righteous. 10More to be desired are they than gold, even than much fine gold; sweeter also than honey and the drippings of the honeycomb.

A Servant's Prayer for Integrity

11Moreover, by them YouryourYour servant is warned; in keeping them there is great reward. 12Who can discern his own errors? Acquit me of hidden faults. 13Restrain YouryourYour servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me. Then I shall be blameless and innocent of great transgression. 14Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in YouryourYour sight, O the LORD, my RockrockRock and my Redeemer.
