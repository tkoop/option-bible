Counsel for the Son and the Rewards of Obedience

1My son, do not forget my instruction, but let your heart safeguard my commandments; 2for they shall add to you length of days, years of life, and peace. 3Let not steadfast love and faithfulness abandon you; keep them ever near to your heart, and engrave them upon the tablet of your inner being. 4Then you shall find favourfavourfavor and sound discernment in the sight of God and of humanity.

5Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not repose confidence in your own understanding; 6in every path acknowledge HimhimHim, and HeheHe will render your ways straight. 7Do not deem yourself wise in your own judgmentjudgementjudgment; revere the LORD and shun evil. 8This shall be healing to your flesh and refreshment to your bones.

9HonourHonourHonor the LORD with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your produce; 10then your barns shall be filled with plenty, and your vats shall overflow with new wine.

11My son, do not despise the discipline of the LORD, nor grow weary of HishisHis reproof; 12for whom the LORD loves HeheHe disciplines, just as a father delights in the son whom he has chosen.

The Incomparable Worth of Wisdom

13Blessed is the one who finds wisdom, and the one who acquires understanding; 14for her gain surpasses the gain of silver, and her yield exceeds fine gold. 15She is more precious than rubies, and nothing you desire can be set beside her. 16Long life is in her right hand; in her left are riches and honourhonourhonor. 17Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. 18She is a tree of life to those who seize her, and those who hold her fast are blessed.

Wisdom in Creation and the Security of the Upright

19The LORD founded the earth by wisdom; HeheHe established the heavens by understanding. 20By HishisHis knowledge the deeps were cleft, and the skies distill dew. 21My son, do not let them slip from your sight; keep prudence and discretion, 22for they shall be life to your soul and favourfavourfavor to your life. 23Then you shall walk securely, and you shall not falter. 24When you lie down, you shall not be afraid; indeed, your sleep shall be sweet. 25Do not fear sudden terror, nor the calamity that befalls the wicked when it comes; 26for the LORD shall be your confidence, and HeheHe will keep you from the snare.

Ethical Obligations and Divine Retribution

27Do not withhold good from its rightful claimants when it is in your power to do it. 28Do not say to your neighbourneighbourneighbor, "Go, and come back, and tomorrow I will give it," when you already have it with you. 29Do not devise evil against your neighbourneighbourneighbor while he dwells beside you in security. 30Do not contend with anyone without cause, if he has done you no harm. 31Do not envy a man of violence, and choose none of his ways.

32For the perverse are an abomination to the LORD, but HishisHis intimate counsel is with the upright. 33The curse of the LORD is upon the house of the wicked, but HeheHe blesses the dwelling of the righteous. 34He scorns the scornful, but gives grace to the humble. 35The wise shall inherit glory, but fools exalt their own disgrace.
