The Kinsman-Redeemer at the Gate

1Boaz had ascended to the city gate and seated himself there when, behold, the kinsman-redeemer of whom Boaz had spoken passed by; whereupon Boaz called out, "Turn aside, my friend, and sit down here." The man turned aside and sat down. 2Boaz then selected ten of the city's elders and said, "Sit down here," and they sat down.

The Field, the Moabite Widow, and the Sandal

3He said to the redeemer, "Naomi, who has returned from the territory of Moab, is selling the parcel of land that belonged to our kinsman Elimelech." 4"I determined to apprise you of this," he added, "and to say, 'Purchase it before those seated here and before the elders of my people. If you will redeem it, redeem it; but if you will not, tell me, so that I may know, for there is no one besides you to redeem it, and I come after you.'"

And he replied, "I will redeem it."

5Then Boaz said, "On the day you acquire the field from Naomi, you also acquire Ruth the Moabitess, the widow of the deceased, in order to raise up the name of the dead upon his inheritance." 6The redeemer said, "I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I impair my own inheritance. Redeem it for yourself, for I cannot redeem it."

7Now this had been the custom in Israel concerning redemption and exchange: to ratify any matter, a man would remove his sandal and give it to his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and this served as attestation in Israel. 8So when the redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself," he removed his sandal.

The Witnesses and the Blessing

9Boaz said to the elders and all the people, "YouyouYou are witnesses today that I have purchased from Naomi's hand all that belonged to Elimelech, and all that belonged to Chilion and Mahlon. 10I have also acquired Ruth the Moabitess, Mahlon's widow, as my wife, to raise up the name of the deceased upon his inheritance, so that the name of the deceased may not be cut off from among his brothers and from the gate of his place. You are witnesses today."

11All the people at the gate and the elders said, "We are witnesses. May the LORD make the woman entering your house like Rachel and Leah, who together built up the house of Israel. May you prosper in Ephrathah and gain renown in Bethlehem, 12and may your house be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, because of the offspring the LORD will give you by this young woman."

The Birth of Obed and the Davidic Lineage

13So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife; and he went in to her, and the LORD granted her conception, and she bore a son.

14The women said to Naomi, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD, who has not left you this day without a redeemer, and may his name be proclaimed in Israel! 15He shall be to you a restorer of life and a sustainer of your old age, for your daughter-in-law, who loves you, is better to you than seven sons, and she has borne him."

16Naomi took the child, laid him in her bosom, and became his nurse.

17The neighboring women gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi." They named him Obed; he was the father of Jesse, the father of David.

18These are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron, 19Hezron fathered Ram, Ram fathered Amminadab, 20Amminadab fathered Nahshon, Nahshon fathered Salmon, 21Salmon fathered Boaz, Boaz fathered Obed, 22Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.
