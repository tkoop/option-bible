Exhortation to Universal Intercession and Thanksgiving

1First of all, then, I exhort that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and eucharistic thanksgivings be offered on behalf of all humanity; 2on behalf of sovereigns and all who occupy positions of eminence, so that we may pass our days in an untroubled and tranquil existence, marked by comprehensive piety and dignified decorum. 3This conduct is noble and acceptable in the sight of God our SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, 4who desires all people to be delivered and to arrive at a full epistemic apprehension of the truth.

The Sole Mediator and Apostolic Testimony

5For there is one God and one mediator between God and humankind—the man Christ Jesus— 6who surrendered HimselfhimselfHimself as a ransom for all, a testimony rendered in its own appointed seasons. 7For this purpose I was ordained a herald and apostle—I speak the truth and do not lie—a teacher of the nations in faith and truth.

Orderly Conduct and Reverent Prayer in the Assembly

8I desire, therefore, that men in every locality pray, elevating holy hands, apart from wrath and contentious disputation. 9Likewise, women ought to adorn themselves in modest apparel, with propriety and sobriety, not with elaborate braids, gold, pearls, or sumptuous garments, 10but with the good works befitting women who profess reverence for God.

11Let a woman learn in quietude, in full submission. 12I do not, however, permit a woman to teach, nor to exercise authority over a man, but to remain in quietude. 13For Adam was fashioned first, and then Eve; 14and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, having been thoroughly beguiled, fell into transgression. 15Yet she shall be saved through childbearing, provided that they abide in faith, love, and sanctification, accompanied by sobriety.
