The Bronze Altar of Burnt Offering

1He fashioned the altar of burnt offering from acacia wood: it was square, five cubits in length and five cubits in breadth, and three cubits in height. 2He made its horns upon its four corners, the horns being of one piece with it, and he overlaid it with bronze. 3He made all the altar’s utensils of bronze—the pots, the shovels, the basins, the forks, and the firepans—and he made all its implements of the same material. 4He made for the altar a grating of bronze, a netlike work beneath its ledge, extending halfway down. 5He cast four rings at the four corners of the bronze grating, as holders for the poles. 6He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze, 7and he inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, so that it might be carried by them; he made it hollow, of boards.

The Bronze Basin

8He made the basin of bronze, and its stand of bronze, from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

The Courtyard and Its Hangings

9He made the court. On the south side, toward the south, the hangings of the court were of fine twisted linen, one hundred cubits in length; 10its twenty pillars had twenty bronze bases, while the hooks and bands of the pillars were silver. 11On the north side the arrangement was the same: one hundred cubits of hangings, twenty pillars, twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands for the pillars. 12On the west side the hangings were fifty cubits, with ten pillars, ten bases, and silver hooks and bands.

13On the east side the width was fifty cubits. 14The hangings on one shoulder were fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three bases; 15on the other shoulder, on either side of the gate of the court, the hangings were likewise fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three bases. 16All the hangings around the court were of fine twisted linen. 17The bases of the pillars were bronze, but their hooks, bands, and overlaid capitals were silver; indeed, all the pillars of the court were connected with silver bands. 18The screen for the gate of the court was embroidered work in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen; it was twenty cubits long and five cubits high, corresponding to the hangings of the court. 19Its four pillars were bronze, with bronze bases, silver hooks and bands, and silver-overlaid capitals. 20All the pegs for the tabernacle and for the court round about were bronze.

An Accounting of the Tabernacle Materials

21These are the records of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony, as they were recorded at the command of Moses for the service of the Levites under the hand of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest. 22Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that the the LORD had commanded Moses; 23and with him was Oholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver, designer, and embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and in fine linen.

24All the gold that was offered as a wave offering for the work, for all the work of the sanctuary, amounted to twenty-nine talents and seven hundred thirty shekels, by the sanctuary shekel. 25The silver from the census of the congregation was one hundred talents and one thousand seven hundred seventy-five shekels, by the sanctuary shekel— 26a beka per head, that is, half a shekel by the sanctuary shekel, for everyone twenty years old and upward who was numbered, totaling six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty men. 27From the one hundred talents of silver were cast the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the veil: one hundred bases from one hundred talents, a talent for each base. 28From the one thousand seven hundred seventy-five shekels he made hooks for the pillars, overlaid their capitals, and joined them with bands. 29The bronze offered amounted to seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels. 30With it he made the bases for the entrance of the tent of meeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grating, all the utensils of the altar, 31the bases around the court, the bases of the gate of the court, and all the pegs of the tabernacle and of the court round about.
