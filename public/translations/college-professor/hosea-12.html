Ephraim's Futile Pursuits and the LORD's Indictment

1Ephraim feeds on the wind and pursues the east wind; all day long he multiplies lies and violence. They make a covenant with Assyria, and oil is carried down to Egypt. 2The LORD has an indictment against Judah, and HeheHe will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his deeds HeheHe will repay him.

Jacob's Striving and the Call to Return

3In the womb he seized his brother's heel, and in his vigourvigourvigor he contended with God. 4He wrestled with the angel and prevailed; he wept and pleaded with HimhimHim. He found HimhimHim at Bethel, and there HeheHe spoke with us. 5And the LORD God of Hosts—the the LORD is HishisHis memorial name. 6So you, return to your God; keep steadfast love and justice, and wait continually for your God.

Canaanite Deceit and Ephraim's Self-Deception

7Canaan is a merchant; fraudulent balances are in his hand, and he loves to oppress. 8Ephraim says, “Surely I have grown rich; I have amassed wealth for myself. In all my toil, no one will find in me any iniquity that constitutes sin.” 9But I am the LORD your God from the land of Egypt; I will again make you dwell in tents, as in the days of the appointed feast.

Prophetic Revelation and Israel's Reckoning

10I have spoken to the prophets, and I have multiplied visions; and through the prophets I have set forth parables. 11Is there iniquity in Gilead? They are indeed worthless. In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls, and their altars are like heaps of stones in the furrows of a field. 12Jacob fled to the country of Aram; Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he tended sheep. 13By a prophet the LORD brought Israel up out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was preserved. 14Ephraim has provoked HimhimHim to bitter anger; therefore his bloodguilt shall remain upon him, and his Lord will repay him his reproach.
