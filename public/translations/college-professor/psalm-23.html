A Psalm of David: The LORD as Shepherd and Host

1A psalm of David. The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. 2He makes me lie down in verdant pastures; HeheHe leads me beside tranquil waters. 3He restores my soul; HeheHe guides me along righteous paths for HishisHis name's sake.

4Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; YouryourYour rod and YouryourYour staff, they comfort me.

5You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; YouyouYou anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. 6Surely goodness and mercy shall pursue me all the days of my life, and I shall dwell in the house of the LORD forever.
