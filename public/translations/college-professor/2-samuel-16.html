Ziba Brings Provisions to David

1David had scarcely passed a little beyond the summit when Ziba, the servant of Mephibosheth, met him with a pair of saddled donkeys, upon which were two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred cakes of raisins, a hundred cakes of summer fruit, and a skin of wine. 2The king asked, "What are these?"

Ziba replied, "ThetheThe donkeys are for the king's household to ride, the bread and summer fruit are for the young men to eat, and the wine is for those who grow weary in the wilderness to drink."

3The king asked, "Where is your master?"

Ziba answered, "HeheHe is staying in Jerusalem, for he said, 'Today the house of Israel will restore my father's kingdom to me.'"

4ThetheThe king said to Ziba, "Behold, all that belonged to Mephibosheth is yours."

Ziba bowed and said, "May I find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, my lord the king."

Shimei Curses David

5When King David reached Bahurim, a man from the family of the house of Saul came out from there; his name was Shimei son of Gera, and as he came he kept cursing. 6He pelted David and all the king's servants, all the people, and all the mighty men with stones, and the people and the mighty men were on David's right and on his left.

7As he cursed, Shimei said, "Get out, get out, you bloodstained man, you worthless scoundrel! 8The LORD has brought back upon you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you reigned, and the LORD has given the kingdom into the hand of your son Absalom. Behold, you are caught in your own evil, for you are a man of blood!"

9Then Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, "Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me cross over and remove his head."

10The king said, "What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah? If he curses, it is because the LORD has said to him, 'Curse David'; who then can say, 'Why have you done so?'"

11David said to Abishai and to all his servants, "Behold, my own son, who came forth from my body, seeks my life; how much more now this Benjaminite! Leave him alone and let him curse, for the LORD has told him to. 12Perhaps ThetheThe LORD will look upon my affliction and the LORD will repay me with good for his cursing this day."

13So David and his men went on their way, while Shimei went along the hillside opposite him, cursing as he went and throwing stones at him and flinging dust. 14The king and all the people who were with him arrived weary, and there they refreshed themselves.

Absalom Enters Jerusalem

15Absalom and all the people, the men of Israel, came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel was with him. 16When David's friend Hushai the Archite came to Absalom, Hushai said, "May the king live! May the king live!"

17Absalom said to Hushai, "IsisIs this your loyalty to your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?"

18Hushai said to Absalom, "No; rather, whom the LORD has chosen, and this people, and all the men of Israel—to him I will belong, and with him I will remain. 19And again, whom should I serve? Should I not serve his son? As I served your father, so I will serve you."

Ahithophel's Counsel to Absalom

20Absalom said to Ahithophel, "Give your advice. What shall we do?"

21Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Go in to your father's concubines, whom he left to keep the house, and all Israel will hear that you have made yourself abhorrent to your father, and the hands of all who are with you will be strengthened."

22So they pitched a tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.

23In those days the counsel that Ahithophel gave was as though one had consulted the word of God; such was all the counsel of Ahithophel, whether to David or to Absalom.
