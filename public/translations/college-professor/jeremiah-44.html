The Word to the Judeans in Egypt

1The message that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Judeans dwelling in the land of Egypt—those in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and the land of Pathros—ran as follows:

2“Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: You have witnessed the entire calamity that I brought upon Jerusalem and upon all the cities of Judah; behold, this day they lie waste, bereft of inhabitants, 3because of the wickedness they committed, provoking MemeMe to anger by going to burn incense and serve other gods—gods that neither they, nor you, nor your fathers ever knew. 4Yet I sent to you all MymyMy servants the prophets, early and persistently, saying, ‘Do not perpetrate this abominable thing that I abhor.’ 5But they would not listen or incline their ear; they did not repent of their wickedness or cease burning incense to other gods. 6Therefore MymyMy wrath and indignation were poured out and kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, so that they became a desolate waste, as they are this day.”

The LORD's Warning to the Remnant

7“Now therefore, thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Why do you inflict so great an evil upon your own souls, cutting off from the midst of Judah man and woman, infant and nursing child, so that no remnant is left to you? 8You provoke MemeMe to anger by the work of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt, to which you have gone to sojourn, thereby ensuring your own extermination and making yourselves a curse and a byword among all the nations of the earth. 9Have you forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, the wickedness of the kings of Judah, the wickedness of their wives, your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives—all that was perpetrated in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem? 10To this very day they have not been humbled, nor have they feared, nor have they walked in MymyMy law or in MymyMy statutes, which I set before you and before your fathers. 11Therefore, thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I am setting MymyMy face against you for calamity, to destroy all Judah. 12I will take the remnant of Judah—those who have resolved to enter Egypt to sojourn there—and they shall be utterly consumed; in the land of Egypt they shall fall, and by sword and famine they shall be consumed; from the least to the greatest, they shall die by sword and famine; and they shall become a horror, a desolation, a curse, and a reproach. 13I will punish those dwelling in the land of Egypt just as I punished Jerusalem—by sword, famine, and plague. 14Thus none of the remnant of Judah who have gone to sojourn in Egypt shall escape or survive to return to the land of Judah, for which they long to return and dwell; none shall return except a handful of fugitives.”

The People's Defiant Response

15Then all the men who knew that their wives had been burning incense to other gods, together with all the women standing there—a great assembly—and all the people dwelling in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying:

16“As for the word you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you! 17Rather, we will surely do everything that has proceeded from our own mouths: we will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out libations to her, just as we, our fathers, our kings, and our officials did in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. For then we had abundance of food, we prospered, and saw no calamity. 18But ever since we ceased burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out libations to her, we have lacked everything and have been consumed by sword and famine.”

19And the women added, “When we burned incense to the Queen of Heaven and poured out libations to her, did we make cakes bearing her image and pour out libations to her without our husbands' consent?”

Jeremiah's Concluding Oracle

20Then Jeremiah addressed all the people—men and women alike—and all those who had given him that answer, saying: 21“The incense that you, your fathers, your kings, your officials, and the people of the land burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem—did not the LORD remember it, and did it not come into HishisHis mind? 22So the LORD could no longer endure the evil of your deeds and the abominations you committed; therefore your land has become a desolation, a horror, and a curse, without inhabitant, as it is this day. 23Because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD, and did not obey the voice of the LORD or walk in HishisHis law, HishisHis statutes, or HishisHis testimonies, this calamity has befallen you, as it is this day.”

24Jeremiah further said to all the people, and to all the women, “Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who are in the land of Egypt. 25Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: ‘You and your wives have declared with your own mouths, “We will surely perform our vows that we have made to burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and to pour out libations to her.” So keep your vows and carry them out!’ 26But hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah dwelling in the land of Egypt: ‘Behold, I have sworn by MymyMy great name, says the LORD, that MymyMy name shall no longer be invoked by any man of Judah throughout the land of Egypt, saying, “As the Lord the LORD lives.”’ 27‘Behold, I am watching over them for harm and not for good; all the men of Judah in the land of Egypt shall be consumed by sword and famine until they are gone. 28Yet a small number who escape the sword shall return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah; and then the whole remnant of Judah that went to sojourn in Egypt shall know whose word stands—MinemineMine or theirs. 29And this shall be a sign to you, declares the LORD, that I will punish you in this place, so that you may know that MymyMy words will surely stand against you for harm.’ 30Thus says the LORD: ‘Behold, I will deliver Pharaoh Hophra, king of Egypt, into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those who seek his life, just as I delivered Zedekiah, king of Judah, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, his enemy who sought his life.’”
