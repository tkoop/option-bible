Job's Rebuttal to His Friends' Futile Consolation

1Then Job answered: 2"I have heard many such utterances; you are all grievous comforters. 3Is there no terminus to these blustering words? What provokes you, that you should retort? 4I, too, could discourse as you do, were your soul in my soul's stead; I could marshal words against you and wag my head at you. 5I would encourage you with my mouth, and the consolation of my lips would bring you relief. 6If I speak, my anguish is not diminished; if I refrain, what is lifted from me?

The Divine Onslaught and Job's Undeserved Ruin

7"Yet now HeheHe has worn me out; you have made desolate all my company. 8You have shrivelledshrivelledshriveled me, and it has become a witness; my gauntness rises up against me and testifies to my face. 9His wrath has torn me, and HeheHe has hated me; HeheHe gnashes HishisHis teeth against me; my adversary sharpens his eyes upon me. 10They gape at me with their mouths; they strike my cheek with derision; they are filled with contempt together against me.

11"God delivers me to the perverse, and casts me into the hands of the wicked. 12I was at ease, and HeheHe shattered me; HeheHe seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces; HeheHe set me up as HishisHis target. 13His archers surround me; HeheHe pierces my kidneys without mercy and pours out my gall upon the ground. 14He breaks me with breach upon breach; HeheHe rushes upon me like a warrior.

Job's Lament, Innocence, and Appeal to Heaven
