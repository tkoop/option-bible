Provision for the Levitical Priesthood

1The Levites—the entire tribe of Levi—shall possess no allotment or inheritance among the Israelites; rather, they shall subsist upon the offerings made by fire to the LORD, for that constitutes their inheritance. 2They shall have no inheritance among their kinsmen, since the LORD HimselfhimselfHimself is their inheritance, exactly as HeheHe has promised them.

3This, moreover, shall be the priests' due from the people, from those who present a sacrifice, whether ox or sheep: they shall give to the priest the shoulder, the jowls, and the stomach. 4You shall give him the firstfruits of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, together with the first shearing of your flock, 5for the LORD your God has chosen him and his descendants from all your tribes to stand and minister in the name of the LORD for all time.

6If a Levite comes from one of your towns anywhere in Israel where he has been residing, and comes, whenever he desires, to the place the LORD will choose, 7he may minister in the name of the LORD his God, like all his fellow Levites who stand there before the LORD. 8He shall receive an equal portion of food, apart from what he obtains by selling his ancestral estate.

Prohibition of Abominable Canaanite Practices

9When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, you must not learn to practiseto practiseto practice the abominations of those nations. 10No one among you shall be found who passes his son or daughter through the fire, or who practiseswho practiseswho practices divination, soothsaying, augury, or sorcery, 11or who casts spells, consults a medium or spiritist, or seeks guidance from the dead. 12For everyone who does these things is an abomination to the LORD, and on account of these abominations the LORD your God will drive out those nations before you. 13You must be blameless before the LORD your God.

14For those nations that you will dispossess listen to soothsayers and diviners, but the LORD your God has not permitted you to do so.

The Promised Prophet

15The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your brothers; you shall listen to him. 16This accords with everything you requested of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when you said, "Let us not hear the voice of the LORD our God, nor see this great fire any longer, lest we die."

17And the LORD said to me, "They have spoken well. 18I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers, and I will put MymyMy words in his mouth, and he will speak to them everything that I command him. 19And if anyone does not listen to MymyMy words that he speaks in MymyMy name, I will require it of him. 20But if a prophet presumes to speak in MymyMy name a word that I have not commanded him to speak, or speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die."

Discerning True and False Prophecy

21You may say in your heart, "How shall we know that the word has not been spoken by the LORD?" 22If the prophet speaks in the name of the LORD and the word does not come to pass, then the LORD has not spoken that word; the prophet has spoken presumptuously. You need not fear him.
