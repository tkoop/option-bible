The Philistine Host Assembles at Aphek

1The Philistines mustered all their forces at Aphek, and Israel encamped by the spring in Jezreel. 2As the Philistine rulers advanced with their companies of hundreds and thousands, David and his men marched in the rear with Achish.

The Philistine Commanders Challenge David’s Presence

3The Philistine commanders asked, “What are these Hebrews doing here?”

Achish answered, “Is this not David, servant of Saul king of Israel? He has been with me for a year or two, and from the day of his defection until now I have found no fault in him.”

4But the Philistine commanders were incensed with Achish and said, “Send the man back! Let him return to the place you assigned him. He must not descend with us into battle, lest he become an adversary to us in the fighting; for how else might he regain his master’s favourfavourfavor, if not by presenting the heads of our men? 5Is this not the David of whom they sing in their dances, ‘Saul has slain his thousands, and David his tens of thousands’?”

Achish Dismisses David in Peace

6So Achish summoned David and said to him, “As the LORD lives, you are upright, and your coming and going with me in the camp is good in my sight, because I have found no fault in you from the day you came to me until now. But you are not acceptable to the rulers. 7Now then, turn back and go in peace, so that you do not displease the Philistine rulers.”

8“But what have I done?” David asked. “What have you found in your servant from the day I came to you until now, that I should not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?”

9Achish replied, “I know that you are pleasing to me, like an angel of God. But the Philistine commanders have said, ‘He must not go up with us into battle.’ 10So get up early in the morning, along with your master’s servants who came with you, and leave at daybreak.”

11So David and his men rose early in the morning to return to the land of the Philistines, and the Philistines went up to Jezreel.
