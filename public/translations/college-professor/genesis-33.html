Jacob Prepares to Meet Esau

1Jacob lifted his gaze and saw Esau approaching with four hundred men; thereupon he apportioned the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two maidservants. 2He set the maidservants and their children first, Leah and her children after them, and Rachel with Joseph last. 3He himself passed on before them, prostrating himself to the ground seven times until he drew near his brother.

Jacob and Esau Reconcile

4Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, fell upon his neck, kissed him, and they wept.

5Esau lifted his eyes, saw the women and the children, and said, "Who are these with you?"

Jacob replied, "ThetheThe children whom God has graciously bestowed upon your servant."

6The maidservants drew near with their children and bowed down. 7Leah and her children likewise drew near and bowed; afterward Joseph and Rachel drew near and bowed.

8Esau asked, "What do you mean by all this company that I met?"

Jacob answered, "To find favourfavourfavor in the eyes of my lord."

9Esau said, "I have abundance, my brother; keep what is yours."

10Jacob said, "Please, no; if I have found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, accept my gift from my hand, for I have seen your face as though it were the face of God, and you have been gracious to me. 11Please take my blessing, which has been brought to you, because God has dealt graciously with me, and I have everything." He urged him, and he accepted it.

12Esau said, "Let us set out and travel; I will go before you."

13Jacob said to him, "MymyMy lord knows that the children are tender and that the flocks and herds are nursing; if they are driven hard for one day, all the flock will die. 14Let my lord go on ahead of his servant, while I move along slowly at the pace of the livestock before me and at the pace of the children, until I come to my lord at Seir."

15Esau said, "Let me leave with you some of the people who are with me."

Jacob said, "Why that? Let me find favourfavourfavor in my lord's eyes."

16So Esau returned that day to Seir.

Journey to Succoth and Shechem

17Jacob journeyed to Succoth and built a house for himself; for his livestock he made shelters; therefore the place was called Succoth. 18Jacob came safely to the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan, on his return from Paddan-aram, and he encamped before the city. 19From the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, he bought the parcel of ground where he had pitched his tent, for a hundred pieces of money. 20There he erected an altar and named it El Elohe Israel.
