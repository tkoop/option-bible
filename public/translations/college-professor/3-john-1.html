Opening Salutation and Prayer

1The elder to the beloved Gaius, whom I cherish in the truth. 2Beloved, I pray that in every respect you may flourish and enjoy bodily health, even as your soul flourishes.

Praise for Gaius's Faithful Hospitality

3For I rejoiced exceedingly when brethren came and bore witness to your truth, just as you walk in the truth. 4I have no greater joy than this: to hear that my children are walking in the truth. 5Beloved, you are acting faithfully in whatever you accomplish for the brethren—and that even for strangers— 6who have testified before the church to your love. You will do well to send them onward in a manner befitting God. 7For they have gone forth for the sake of the NamenameName, accepting nothing from the Gentiles. 8Therefore we ought to receive such men, so that we may become fellow workers for the truth.

Diotrephes and the Threat to Fellowship

9I wrote something to the church, but Diotrephes, who covets primacy among them, refuses to receive us. 10Therefore, if I come, I will call to mind his deeds, which he perpetrates, prating against us with malicious words; and not content with these, he neither receives the brethren, nor suffers those who would, and casts them out of the church. 11Beloved, do not imitate evil, but good. Whoever does good is from God; whoever does evil has not seen God.

Commendation of Demetrius and Final Greetings

12Demetrius has received testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself; we too testify, and you know that our testimony is true. 13I had much to write to you, but I am unwilling to write to you with pen and ink. 14I hope to see you soon, and we shall speak face to face. Peace to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
