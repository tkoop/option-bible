Habakkuk's Watch and the LORD's Command

1I will take my stand upon my watchtower and station myself upon the rampart, watching to see what HeheHe will say to me and what answer I shall return concerning my complaint.

2The LORD answered me and said, "Write down the vision and inscribe it plainly upon the tablets, so that whoever reads it may run with it."

The Vision's Sure FulfillmentFulfilmentFulfillment

3For the vision is yet for an appointed time; it testifies to the end and does not deceive. Though it tarry, wait for it, for it will surely come and will not be late. 4Behold, his soul is puffed up within him and is not upright; but the righteous shall live by his faith.

The Arrogance and Oppression of Babylon

5Moreover, wine is treacherous; the arrogant man does not abide. He enlarges his appetite like Sheol, and like death he is never satisfied. He gathers all nations to himself and collects all peoples to himself.

6Will not all these take up a taunt against him, and a mocking riddle, and say, "Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own—how long?—and burdens himself with pledges!"?

7Will not your creditors suddenly arise, and those who make you tremble awake? Then you will become spoil for them. 8Because you have plundered many nations, all the remnant of the peoples will plunder you, because of the blood of man and the violence done to the land, to the city, and to all its inhabitants.

Woes Against the Wicked and the LORD's Glory

9Woe to him who gains evil profit for his house, setting his nest on high to be delivered from the hand of calamity! 10You have devised shame for your house by cutting off many peoples, and you have sinned against your own soul. 11For the stone will cry out from the wall, and the beam will answer it from the timber.

12Woe to him who builds a city with blood and establishes a town by iniquity! 13Behold, is it not from the the LORD of Hosts that peoples labourlabourlabor for fire, and nations weary themselves for nothing? 14For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.

15Woe to him who makes his neighbourneighbourneighbor drink, pouring out your venom and making them drunk, so that you may gaze upon their nakedness! 16You are filled with shame rather than glory. Drink also, and be exposed! The cup in the the LORD's right hand will come around to you, and disgrace will cover your glory. 17For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you, and the destruction of beasts will terrify you, because of the blood of man and the violence to the land, to the city, and to all its inhabitants.

18What profit is an idol, when its maker has carved it—a metal image, a teacher of lies? For the craftsman trusts in his own handiwork when he makes mute idols. 19Woe to him who says to wood, "Awake!" and to silent stone, "Arise!" Can this teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all within it. 20But the the LORD is in HishisHis holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before HimhimHim.
