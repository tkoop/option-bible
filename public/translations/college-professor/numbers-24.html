Balaam’s First Oracle and Israel’s Blessed Encampment

1When Balaam perceived that it pleased the the LORD to bless Israel, he did not resort to sorcery as he had at other times, but turned his gaze toward the wilderness. 2When Balaam raised his eyes and saw Israel encamped tribe by tribe, the SpiritspiritSpirit of God came upon him, 3and he uttered his oracle: “The oracle of Balaam son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eye has been opened— 4the oracle of one who hears the words of God, who beholds the vision of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, who falls down with eyes uncovered: 5‘How lovely are your tents, O Jacob, your dwellings, O Israel! 6They spread like valleys, like gardens beside a river, like aloes the the LORD has planted, like cedars beside the waters. 7Water shall overflow from his buckets, and his seed shall have abundant water; his king shall be exalted above Agag, and his kingdom shall be lifted high. 8God brought him out of Egypt; he has the strength of a wild ox. He shall devour hostile nations and crush their bones; with his arrows he shall pierce them. 9He crouches and lies down like a lion, like a lioness—who dares rouse him? Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you!’”

Balak’s Fury and Balaam’s Refusal to Curse Israel

10Then Balak’s anger blazed against Balaam; he clapped his hands in fury and said to Balaam, “I summoned you to curse my enemies, yet behold, you have blessed them three times over. 11Now flee to your own place! I said I would reward you richly, but the the LORD has withheld honourhonourhonor from you.”

12Balaam replied to Balak, “Did I not tell the messengers you sent to me, 13‘If Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not transgress the command of the the LORD by doing anything of my own accord, good or evil; I must speak only what the the LORD speaks’? 14And now I am returning to my people; but come, let me advise you what this people will do to your people in the latter days.”

Balaam’s Messianic Oracle of Jacob’s Star and SceptreSceptreScepter

15Then he uttered his oracle: “The oracle of Balaam son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eye has been opened— 16the oracle of one who hears the words of God and knows the knowledge of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh, who beholds the vision of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, who falls down with eyes uncovered: 17‘I see him, but not now; I behold him, but not near. A star shall proceed from Jacob, and a sceptresceptrescepter shall rise from Israel; he shall crush the temples of Moab and lay waste all the sons of Sheth. 18Edom shall become a possession, and Seir—his enemy—shall become a possession, while Israel performs valiantly. 19A ruler shall arise from Jacob and destroy the remnant of the city.’”

Oracles Against Amalek, the Kenites, and Distant Peoples

20Then Balaam saw Amalek and uttered his oracle: “Amalek is the first of the nations, but its end shall be utter destruction.”

21Then he saw the Kenites and uttered his oracle: “Your dwelling is secure, and your nest is set in the rock; 22yet the Kenite shall be consumed until Asshur takes you captive.”

23Then he uttered his oracle: “Alas, who shall live when God ordains this? 24Ships shall come from Kittim and subdue Asshur and subdue Eber, but he too shall come to ruin.” 25Then Balaam arose and departed to his own place, and Balak also went his way.
