Job's Answer: The Weight of Divine and Human Abandonment

1Then Job answered and said: 2"How long will you vex my soul and crush me with words? 3You have now reproached me ten times over, and you are not ashamed to assail me. 4If I have truly gone astray, my error remains my own concern. 5If you truly magnify yourselves against me and adduce my disgrace as testimony against me, 6then know that God has wronged me and ensnared me in HishisHis net.

The Siege of God and the Collapse of Job's World

Job's Appeal for Compassion

21"Have compassion on me, my friends, have compassion, for the hand of God has struck me. 22Why do you pursue me as God does? Will you never be satisfied with my flesh?

Job's Hope in a Living Redeemer and Final Warning

23"Oh, that my words were recorded, that they were inscribed in a book, 24that with an iron stylus and lead they were engraved in rock forever! 25But I know that my Redeemer lives, and that in the end HeheHe will stand upon the dust. 26Even after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God. 27I will see HimhimHim for myself; my eyes will behold HimhimHim, and not as a stranger.
