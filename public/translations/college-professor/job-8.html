Bildad's First Speech to Job

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

“2How long will you persist in this torrent of verbiage, while the words of your mouth remain a blustering wind? 3Does God pervert justice, or does the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty distort righteousness? 4If your children sinned against HimhimHim, HeheHe consigned them to the power of their transgression.

“5If you seek God with earnest diligence and supplicate the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, 6if you are pure and upright, surely now HeheHe will rouse HimselfhimselfHimself on your behalf and bring to completion the dwelling of your righteousness. 7Though your beginning was diminutive, your latter end will increase immeasurably.

The Testimony of the Former Generation

“8I pray you, inquire of the former generation, and attend to the investigations of their fathers. 9For we are but of yesterday and know nothing, for our days upon the earth are a shadow. 10Will they not teach you and say to you, and bring forth words from their inmost being?

The Fragility of the Hypocrite's Prosperity

“11Can the papyrus thrust itself upward without a marsh? Can reeds flourish in the absence of water? 12While it is still verdant and uncut, it withers before every other plant. 13So are the paths of all who forget God, and the hope of the hypocrite perishes. 14Whose confidence is severed, and whose trust is a spider's web. 15He leans upon his house, yet it does not stand; he clings to it, yet it does not endure. 16He is luxuriant before the sun, and his shoots spread forth over his garden. 17His roots entwine the heap of stones; he seeks a dwelling among the rocks. 18If he is swept away from his place, it disowns him, saying, 'I have not seen you.' 19Behold, this is the joy of his way; and from the dust others will arise.

The Vindication of the Blameless

“20Behold, God does not reject a blameless man, nor does HeheHe strengthen the hand of evildoers. 21He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouts of joy. 22Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.”
