Moses Proclaims the Statute on Vows

1Then Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the Israelites, saying, “This is the thing that the LORD has commanded:” 2When a man vows a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind himself by a pledge, he shall not violate his word; he shall perform all that proceeds from his mouth.

The Vows of Daughters in Their Father's House

3If a woman, while in her father's house in her youth, vows a vow to the LORD and binds herself by a pledge, 4and her father hears her vow and her pledge by which she bound herself, and her father remains silent toward her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she bound herself shall stand. 5But if her father prohibits her on the day he hears, none of her vows or pledges by which she bound herself shall stand; and the LORD will forgive her, because her father prohibited her.

The Vows of Married Women and the Authority of Husbands

6If she is married to a husband while her vows are upon her, or while a rash utterance of her lips by which she bound herself is upon her, 7and her husband hears and remains silent toward her on the day he hears, then her vows shall stand, and her pledges by which she bound herself shall stand. 8But if her husband prohibits her on the day he hears, he nullifies her vow that is upon her and the rash utterance of her lips by which she bound herself; and the LORD will forgive her.

9Every vow of a widow or a divorced woman, by which she has bound herself, shall stand against her. 10If she vowed in her husband's house, or bound herself by an oath, 11and her husband heard and remained silent toward her and did not prohibit her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she bound herself shall stand. 12But if her husband indeed nullifies them on the day he hears, then whatever proceeded from her lips concerning her vows or concerning the binding of her soul shall not stand; her husband has nullified them, and the LORD will forgive her.

13Every vow and every binding oath to afflict her soul—her husband may confirm it, or her husband may nullify it. 14But if her husband is wholly silent toward her from day to day, then he confirms all her vows or all her pledges that are upon her; he has confirmed them, because he was silent toward her on the day he heard. 15But if he nullifies them after he has heard, then he shall bear her guilt.

The Concluding Summary of the Statute

16These are the statutes that the LORD commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter in her youth in her father's house.
