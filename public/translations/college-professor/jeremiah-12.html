Jeremiah’s Perplexity over the Prosperity of the Wicked

1Righteous are YouyouYou, O the LORD, when I plead my cause before YouyouYou; yet I would reason with YouyouYou concerning justice: why does the way of the wicked prosper, and why do all the treacherous live at ease? 2You have planted them, and they have struck root; they have grown and even borne fruit. You are ever upon their lips, yet remote from their inmost being.

3But YouyouYou know me, O the LORD; YouyouYou see me and test my heart toward YouyouYou. Drag them away like sheep to the slaughter, and set them apart for the day of slaughter. 4How long must the land mourn and the grass of every field wither? Because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it, beasts and birds are swept away; for they say, “He will not see our final end.”

5If you have run with footmen and they have wearied you, how then will you compete with horses? If you have stumbled in a land of peace, what will you do in the thickets of the Jordan? 6For even your brothers and your father’s house—even they have betrayed you; even they have cried out after you. Do not trust them, though they speak kindly to you.

The LORD’s Lament over His Inheritance

“7I have forsaken MymyMy house; I have abandoned MymyMy inheritance. I have given the beloved of MymyMy soul into the hands of her enemies. 8My inheritance has become to MemeMe like a lion in the forest; she has raised her voice against MemeMe; therefore I have hated her.

“9Is not MymyMy inheritance to MemeMe like a speckled bird of prey, with other birds of prey circling against her? Go, gather all the beasts of the field; bring them to devour her. 10Many shepherds have destroyed MymyMy vineyard; they have trampled MymyMy plot of land. They have turned MymyMy pleasant field into a desolate wasteland. 11They have made it a desolation; desolate, it mourns to MemeMe. The whole land is laid waste, because no one takes it to heart.

“12On all the barren heights in the wilderness destroyers have come, for the sword of the the LORD devours from one end of the earth to the other. No one has peace. 13They sowed wheat but harvested thorns; they exhausted themselves to no avail. Be ashamed of your harvests because of the fierce anger of the the LORD.”

Oracle Against the Neighboring Nations

“14Thus says the the LORD concerning all MymyMy wicked neighbors who touch the inheritance that I gave MymyMy people Israel: Behold, I will uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them. 15And after I have uprooted them, I will again have compassion on them and bring them back, each to his inheritance and each to his land.

“16And if they will diligently learn the ways of MymyMy people—to swear by MymyMy name, ‘As the the LORD lives,’ just as they taught MymyMy people to swear by Baal—then they will be established among MymyMy people. 17But if they will not obey, then I will uproot that nation and destroy it, declares the the LORD.”
