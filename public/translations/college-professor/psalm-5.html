A Morning Supplication for Righteous Guidance and Deliverance from the Wicked

1Listen to my words, the LORD; consider my inward meditation. 2Attend to the voice of my cry, my KingkingKing and my God, for to YouyouYou I pray. 3In the morning, the LORD, YouyouYou hear my voice; at dawn I lay my plea before YouyouYou and wait in expectation.

11But let all who take refuge in YouyouYou rejoice; let them ever shout for joy, because YouyouYou shelter them; and let those who love YouryourYour name exult in YouyouYou. 12For YouyouYou, the LORD, bless the righteous; YouyouYou surround him with favourfavourfavor as with a shield.
