Woe to Those Who Trust in Egypt

1Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help, who rely on horses, who trust in the abundance of chariots and in the multitude of horsemen. They do not look to the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel; they do not seek the LORD.

2Yet HeheHe too is wise and brings disaster; HeheHe does not retract HishisHis words. He will rise up against the house of the wicked and against the helpers of evildoers.

3But the Egyptians are mere mortals, not God; their horses are flesh, not spirit. When the LORD stretches out HishisHis hand, the helper will stumble, and the one helped will fall; both will perish together.

The LORD's DefenceDefenceDefense of Zion

4For thus the LORD has said to me: “As a lion growls, a great lion over its prey—and though a whole band of shepherds is called together against it, it is not terrified by their shouting or daunted by their clamourclamourclamor—so the LORD of Hosts will come down to do battle on Mount Zion and on its heights.”

5Like birds hovering overhead, so the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem. He will protect and deliver it; HeheHe will pass over and preserve it.

A Call to Repentance and Assyria's Downfall

6Return to HimhimHim from whom you have so deeply revolted, O children of Israel.

7For on that day, every one of you will reject the idols of silver and gold that your own hands have sinfully made.

8Then Assyria shall fall, but not by the sword of man; a sword not of mortals will devour him. He will flee from the sword, and his young men will be put to forced labourlabourlabor.

9His rock will pass away in terror, and his officers will panic at the battle standard,” declares the LORD, “whose fire is in Zion and whose furnace is in Jerusalem.”
