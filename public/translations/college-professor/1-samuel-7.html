The Ark’s Sojourn at Kiriath-jearim and Israel’s Penitential Renewal

1The inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim ascended and brought up the ark of the LORD, transporting it into the house of Abinadab upon the hill; there they consecrated his son Eleazar to guard the ark of the LORD. 2From the day the ark tarried at Kiriath-jearim, the days were multiplied—twenty years in all—and all the house of Israel mourned and sought after the LORD.

3Samuel then addressed the entirety of Israel, declaring, “If you return to the LORD with all your heart, expunge the foreign gods and the Ashtaroth from your midst, prepare your heart toward the LORD, and serve HimhimHim alone, HeheHe will rescue you from the Philistines’ hand.”

4Accordingly, the Israelites removed the Baals and the Ashtaroth and served the LORD alone.

5Samuel commanded, “Assemble all Israel at Mizpah, and I will intercede with the LORD on your behalf.”

6They convened at Mizpah, drew water, and poured it out before the LORD; on that day they fasted and confessed, “We have sinned against the LORD.” Samuel judged Israel at Mizpah.

The Philistine Advance and Israel’s Deliverance

7When the Philistines learned that Israel had assembled at Mizpah, the Philistine lords advanced against Israel; upon hearing of it, the Israelites were seized with fear.

8The Israelites entreated Samuel, “Do not desist from crying out to the LORD our God for us, that HeheHe may deliver us from the Philistines’ hand.”

9Samuel took a milk-fed lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to the LORD; he cried out to the LORD on Israel’s behalf, and the LORD answered him.

10As the Philistines approached to wage war against Israel, Samuel was presenting the burnt offering; the LORD thundered with a mighty voice over the Philistines, throwing them into disarray, and they were struck down before Israel.

11The men of Israel sallied forth from Mizpah, pursued the Philistines, and struck them down as far as below Beth-car.

12Samuel took a stone and placed it between Mizpah and Shen, naming it Ebenezer, and said, “Thus far has the LORD helped us.”

13The Philistines were humbled and no longer entered Israel’s territory; the hand of the LORD was against the Philistines throughout all Samuel’s days.

14The cities from Ekron to Gath that the Philistines had seized from Israel were restored to Israel; Israel reclaimed its territory from the Philistines’ hand, and peace obtained between Israel and the Amorites.

Samuel’s Judgeship and Circuit

15Samuel judged Israel throughout his life.

16Annually he made a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, judging Israel in all these places.

17His circuit returned to Ramah, for his household was there; there he judged Israel and erected an altar to the LORD.
