Agur’s Oracle and the Limits of Human Wisdom

1The words of Agur son of Jakeh constitute the oracle; the man declares unto Ithiel, unto Ithiel and Ucal. 2I am indeed more brutish than any man, and I lack the discernment proper to humankind. 3I have not learned wisdom, nor do I possess knowledge of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne. 4Who has ascended to Heavenheavenheaven and descended? Who has gathered the wind in HishisHis fists? Who has bound the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is HishisHis name, and what is the name of HishisHis son? Surely you know!

5Every utterance of God is flawless; HeheHe is a shield to those who take refuge in HimhimHim. 6Do not append anything to HishisHis words, lest HeheHe reprove you and you be exposed as a liar.

A Prayer for Integrity, Contentment, and Reverence

7Two things I ask of YouyouYou; do not refuse me before I die: 8keep falsehood and deceitful words far from me; grant me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the bread of my allotted portion. 9Lest I become sated and deny YouyouYou, saying, “Who is the LORD?” or lest I become impoverished and steal, thereby profaning the name of my God.

10Do not slander a servant to his master, lest he curse you, and you be found guilty.

Generational Vices and Insatiable Appetites

11There exists a generation that curses its father and withholds blessing from its mother. 12There is a generation pure in its own eyes, yet unwashed of its filth. 13There is a generation—how haughty are its eyes, and how pretentious its glances! 14There is a generation whose teeth are swords and whose molars are knives, to devour the afflicted from the earth and the destitute from among humankind.

15The leech has two daughters—“Give, give!” There are three things never satisfied, four that never say, “Enough!” 16Sheol, the barren womb, land that is never satisfied with water, and fire that never says, “Enough!” 17The eye that mocks a father and scorns obedience to a mother will be pecked out by the ravens of the valley and consumed by the vultures.

Numerical Proverbs and the Wisdom of Creation

18There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot comprehend: 19the way of an eagle through the sky, the way of a serpent upon a rock, the way of a ship upon the heart of the sea, and the way of a man with a maiden. 20Such is the way of an adulteress: she eats, wipes her mouth, and says, “I have done no wrong.”

21Under three things the earth trembles, under four it cannot bear up: 22a servant who becomes king, a fool glutted with food, 23an unloved woman when she marries, and a maidservant who supplants her mistress.

24Four things on EarthEarthearth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise: 25the ants are a people without strength, yet they store up their food in the summer; 26the rock badgers are a people without power, yet they make their home in the cliffs; 27the locusts have no king, yet they all advance in formation; 28the lizard can be caught in the hand, yet it is found in kings’ palaces.

29There are three things majestic in their stride, four that are majestic in their walk: 30the lion, mightiest of beasts, who does not retreat before anything; 31the strutting rooster; the he-goat; and a king secure against insurrection.

32If you have foolishly exalted yourself, or if you have plotted evil, put your hand over your mouth. 33For as the churning of milk yields butter, and the twisting of the nose draws blood, so the stirring of anger brings forth strife.
