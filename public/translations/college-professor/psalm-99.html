The Sovereign Reign of the LORD and the Trembling of the Nations

1The LORD reigns; let the peoples tremble. He is enthroned between the cherubim; let the earth quake. 2Great is the LORD in Zion; HeheHe is exalted above all the peoples. 3Let them praise YouryourYour great and awesome name—HeheHe is holy.

The Justice-Loving King and the Equity Established in Jacob

4The KingkingKing is mighty; HeheHe loves justice. You have established equity; YouyouYou have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob. 5Exalt the LORD our God and worship at HishisHis footstool; HeheHe is holy.

The Holy God Who Answers Moses, Aaron, and Samuel

6Moses and Aaron were among HishisHis priests, and Samuel among those who called upon HishisHis name. They called to the LORD, and HeheHe answered them. 7He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud; they kept HishisHis testimonies and the statute HeheHe gave them. 8O the LORD our God, YouyouYou answered them. You were a forgiving God to them, yet YouyouYou avenged their misdeeds. 9Exalt the LORD our God and worship at HishisHis holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.
