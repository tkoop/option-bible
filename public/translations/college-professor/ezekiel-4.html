Ezekiel Enacts the Siege of Jerusalem

1You, SonsonSon of man, procure a brick for yourself and set it before you, then incise upon its surface a representation of the city—that is, Jerusalem. 2Lay siege against it; erect a siege rampart against it; pour out an earthen ramp against it; pitch camps against it; and station battering rams around it. 3Take an iron plate for yourself and place it as an iron wall between you and the city; fix your gaze upon it, and let it be besieged while you besiege it. It shall serve as a sign to the house of Israel.

The Prophet Bears the Guilt of Israel and Judah

4You shall recline upon your left side and place upon it the iniquity of the house of Israel; you shall bear their iniquity for the number of days that you recline upon it. 5For I have allotted you three hundred ninety days, corresponding to the number of years of their iniquity, and you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel. 6When you have completed these, you shall recline a second time, upon your right side, and bear the iniquity of the house of Judah; I have assigned it to you: forty days, a day for a year, a day for a year. 7You shall direct your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm bared, and prophesy against it. 8Behold, I am placing fetters upon you, so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.

Rationed Provisions, Defiled Bread, and Impending Famine

9Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt for yourself, put them into a single vessel, and make bread for yourself from them; you shall eat it during the three hundred ninety days that you recline upon your side. 10Your food, which you eat, shall be by weight—twenty shekels a day—and you shall eat it at appointed times. 11Water you shall drink by measure—the sixth part of a hin—and you shall drink it at appointed times. 12You shall eat it in the form of barley cakes, baking it before their eyes over cakes of human excrement.

13Then the LORD said, “Thus shall the children of Israel eat their bread unclean among the nations to which I will drive them.”

14Then I said, “Ah, Lord the LORD! Behold, my soul has never been defiled; from my youth until now I have not eaten what died of itself or was torn by beasts, nor has any abominable flesh entered my mouth.”

15And HeheHe said to me, “See, I have given you cow dung in place of human dung, and you shall prepare your bread upon it.”

16And HeheHe said to me, “Son of man, behold, I am breaking the supply of bread in Jerusalem, so that they shall eat bread by weight and in anxious distress, and drink water by measure and in desolation.”

17Thus they shall lack bread and water, and each man shall waste away with his brother, and they shall rot away in their iniquity.
