A Summons to Return to the LORD

1“Come, let us return to the LORD, for HeheHe has torn us, yet HeheHe will heal us; HeheHe has struck us, but HeheHe will bind us up. 2After two days HeheHe will revive us; on the third day HeheHe will raise us up, that we may live in HishisHis presence. 3Let us press on to know the LORD; HishisHis appearing is as certain as the dawn. He will come to us like the rains, like the spring showers that water the earth.”

The LORD's Reproach of Ephemeral Devotion

4“What shall I do with you, Ephraim? What shall I do with you, Judah? For your steadfast love is like a morning cloud, like the dew that vanishes at daybreak. 5Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of MymyMy mouth; and your judgmentsjudgementsjudgments go forth like the light. 6For I desire steadfast love rather than sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings. 7But they, like Adam, transgressed the covenant; there they betrayed MemeMe.”

Corruption in Israel and Judah

8Gilead is a city of evildoers, tracked with blood. 9Like bandits lying in ambush for a man, so a company of priests murders along the road to Shechem, for they have committed an outrage.

A Harvest Appointed and a People Restored

10“In the house of Israel I have seen an abomination: Ephraim's harlotry is there, and Israel is defiled. 11And you too, Judah, have a harvest appointed for you, when I restore MymyMy people.”
