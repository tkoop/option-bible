The Song of Moses and Israel’s Rebellion

1Give ear, O heavens, and I shall speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth. 2My instruction shall drip like rain, my discourse shall distill like dew, like gentle rain upon the tender grass and like showers upon the herb. 3For I will proclaim the name of the LORD; ascribe greatness to our God. 4The RockrockRock—HishisHis work is perfect, for all HishisHis ways are justice; a God of faithfulness, without iniquity, righteous and upright is HeheHe.

5They have dealt corruptly toward HimhimHim; to their shame, they are no longer HishisHis children—a perverse and crooked generation. 6Do you thus repay the LORD, you foolish and senseless people? Is HeheHe not your FatherfatherFather who created you, who made you and established you? 7Remember the days of old; consider the years of many generations; ask your father, and he will show you; your elders, and they will tell you. 8When the MostmostMost HighhighHigh gave the nations their inheritance, when HeheHe separated the sons of men, HeheHe set the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel.

9For the LORD’s portion is HishisHis people; Jacob is the lot of HishisHis inheritance. 10He found him in a desert land, in the howling waste of the wilderness; HeheHe encircled him, HeheHe instructed him, HeheHe kept him as the apple of HishisHis eye. 11Like an eagle that stirs up its nest, that flutters over its young, spreading out its wings, catching them and bearing them on its pinions— 12the LORD alone led him, and no foreign god was with him.

13He made him ride on the high places of the earth, and he ate the produce of the fields; HeheHe nursed him with honey from the rock and oil from the flinty crag. 14Curds from the herd and milk from the flock, with fat of lambs, rams of Bashan and goats, with the finest of the wheat—and you drank wine from the blood of the grape. 15But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked; you grew fat, you grew thick, and you became sleek; then he forsook God who made him and scorned the RockrockRock of his salvation.

16They provoked HimhimHim to jealousy with strange gods; with abominations they provoked HimhimHim to anger. 17They sacrificed to demons, not God—to gods they had not known, to new gods that had come recently, which your fathers had not dreaded. 18You forgot the RockrockRock who begot you, and forgot God who formed you.

Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment upon a Faithless People

19The LORD saw it and spurned them, because of the provocation of HishisHis sons and daughters.

20And HeheHe said, “I will hide MymyMy face from them; I will see what their end will be, for they are a perverse generation, children in whom is no faithfulness. 21They have made MemeMe jealous with that which is not God; they have provoked MemeMe to anger with their idols. So I will make them jealous with a people that is not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation. 22For a fire is kindled in MymyMy anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol, devours the earth and its increase, and sets on fire the foundations of the mountains. 23I will heap disasters upon them; I will spend MymyMy arrows on them.

24“They shall be wasted with hunger, and devoured by plague and bitter destruction; I will send the teeth of beasts against them, with the venom of things that crawl in the dust. 25Outdoors the sword shall bereave, and indoors terror, for young man and virgin, the nursing child with the man of greygreygray hairs. 26I would have said, ‘I will scatter them afar; I will make the memory of them cease from among mankind’— 27had I not feared the provocation of the enemy, lest their adversaries misunderstand, lest they say, ‘Our hand is exalted, and the LORD has not done all this.’”

28For they are a nation void of counsel, and there is no understanding in them. 29If they were wise, they would understand this; they would discern their latter end. 30How could one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their RockrockRock had sold them, and the LORD had given them up?

31For their rock is not as our RockrockRock, even our enemies themselves being judges. 32For their vine comes from the vine of Sodom and from the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of gall, their clusters are bitter. 33Their wine is the venom of serpents, and the cruel poison of asps.

The LORD’s Vengeance and Sovereign Redemption

34“Is not this laid up in store with MemeMe, sealed in MymyMy treasuries? 35Vengeance is MinemineMine, and recompense, for the time when their foot shall slip; for the day of their calamity is at hand, and their doom hastens.”

36For the LORD will judge HishisHis people and have compassion on HishisHis servants, when HeheHe sees that their power is gone and there is none remaining, bond or free.

37“And HeheHe will say, ‘Where are their gods, the rock in which they took refuge, 38who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offering? Let them rise up and help you; let them be your protection!’”

39“See now that I am HeheHe, and there is no god besides MemeMe; I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; and there is none that can deliver out of MymyMy hand. 40For I lift up MymyMy hand to Heavenheavenheaven and swear, As I live forever, 41if I sharpen MymyMy flashing sword and MymyMy hand takes hold on judgmentjudgementjudgment, I will render vengeance to MymyMy adversaries and repay those who hate MemeMe. 42I will make MymyMy arrows drunk with blood, and MymyMy sword shall devour flesh, with the blood of the slain and the captives, from the heads of the leaders of the enemy.”

43Rejoice, O nations, with HishisHis people, for HeheHe avenges the blood of HishisHis servants and renders vengeance to HishisHis adversaries, and makes atonement for HishisHis land, for HishisHis people.

Moses’ Final Charge and Ascent to Nebo

44So Moses, together with Joshua the son of Nun, came and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people. 45And when Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel,

46he said to them, “Set your hearts to all the words that I am testifying against you today, that you may command your sons to observe and to do all the words of this law. 47For it is not an empty word for you, but it is your life, and by this word you shall prolong your days in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess.”

48The LORD spoke to Moses that very day, saying,

49“Go up this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel for a possession. 50And die on the mountain that you are ascending, and be gathered to your people, as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people.”

51“Because you broke faith with MemeMe in the midst of the sons of Israel at the waters of Meribah-kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin, and because you did not sanctify MemeMe in the midst of the sons of Israel. 52For you shall see the land from a distance, but you shall not enter there, into the land that I am giving to the sons of Israel.”
