1Then Jesus told them a parable to demonstrate that they must pray at all times and never succumb to discouragement. 2He said, “In a certain city there was a judge who neither feared God nor respected any human being.

4For a while he refused; but afterward he said to himself, ‘Although I neither fear God nor respect any human being, 5yet because this widow persists in troubling me, I will grant her justice, lest she ultimately wear me down by her relentless visits.’
