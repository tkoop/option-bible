Jericho Closed and the LORD’s Promise

1Now Jericho was shut up and closely barred because of the Israelites: no one went out, and no one came in. 2the LORD said to Joshua, “See, I have given Jericho into your hand, together with its king and its mighty men of valourvalourvalor. 3You shall march around the city, all the men of war circling it once; thus you shall do for six days. 4Seven priests shall carry before the ark seven trumpets of rams’ horns; on the seventh day you shall encircle the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets. 5When they make a long blast with the ram’s horn, and when you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout; then the wall of the city shall fall down flat, and the people shall go up, every man straight ahead.”

Joshua Commands the March

6Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark of the LORD.”

7He said to the people, “Pass on, and march around the city, and let the armed men pass on before the ark of the LORD.”

8So, when Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the LORD passed on and blew the trumpets; the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them. 9The armed men went before the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard came after the ark, while the priests kept blowing the trumpets as they went.

10Joshua commanded the people, saying, “You shall not shout, nor make your voice heard, and no word shall come out of your mouth, until the day I tell you to shout; then you shall shout.”

11Thus he made the ark of the LORD go around the city, circling it once; then they returned to the camp and lodged there.

12Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD. 13The seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams’ horns went on continually before the ark of the LORD, blowing the trumpets; the armed men went before them, and the rear guard followed the ark of the LORD, while the priests blew the trumpets. 14On the second day they marched around the city once and returned to the camp; they did so for six days. 15On the seventh day they rose early at dawn and marched around the city in the same manner seven times; on that day only they encircled the city seven times. 16At the seventh time, when the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, “Shout, for the LORD has given you the city. 17The city and everything in it shall be devoted to the LORD for destruction; only Rahab the prostitute, and all who are with her in the house, shall live, because she hid the messengers we sent. 18But you, keep yourselves from the devoted thing, lest you make yourselves accursed by taking from it and so make the camp of Israel accursed and bring trouble upon it. 19Yet all the silver and gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD; they shall go into the treasury of the LORD.”

The Devotion of Jericho and Rahab’s Deliverance

20So the people shouted, and the priests blew the trumpets; and when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, they shouted with a great shout, and the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight ahead, and they captured the city. 21They devoted to destruction by the edge of the sword all that was in the city, both men and women, young and old, oxen, sheep, and donkeys.

22Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, “Go into the prostitute’s house and bring out the woman and all that she has, as you swore to her.”

23So the young men who had been spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father, her mother, her brothers, and all that she had; all her relatives they brought out and set them outside the camp of Israel. 24They burned the city with fire, and all that was in it; only the silver, the gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron they put into the treasury of the house of the LORD. 25Thus Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father’s household, and all that she had; and she has lived among Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

Joshua’s Curse and Growing Renown

26At that time Joshua charged them with an oath, saying, “Cursed be the man before the LORD who rises up and rebuilds this city Jericho: he shall lay its foundation at the cost of his firstborn, and at the cost of his youngest son shall he set up its gates.” 27So the LORD was with Joshua, and his fame spread throughout all the land.
