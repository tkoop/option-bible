The Qualifications for Dwelling in the LORD’s Sanctuary

1A psalm of David. The LORD, who may sojourn in YouryourYour tent, and who may abide upon YouryourYour holy mountain? 2The one who walks in integrity and practices righteousness, and who speaks truth from the heart.

3He does not slander with his tongue, nor does he do evil to his neighbourneighbourneighbor, nor does he take up a reproach against his friend. 4In his eyes a vile person is despised, yet he honourshonourshonors those who fear the LORD; he keeps his oath even when it costs him dearly. 5He does not lend his money at interest, nor does he accept a bribe against the innocent. The one who does these things shall never be shaken.
