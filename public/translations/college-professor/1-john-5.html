Faith, Love, and the Conquest of the World

1Every person who believes that Jesus is the MessiahmessiahMessiah has been begotten of God; and everyone who cherishes the OneoneOne who begat also cherishes the one begotten from HimhimHim. 2By this we discern that we cherish the offspring of God: whenever we cherish God and execute HishisHis precepts. 3For this constitutes the love of God—that we preserve HishisHis commandments; and HishisHis commandments are by no means onerous. 4For every offspring begotten of God vanquishes the world; and this is the triumph that has subdued the world—our faith. 5And who is the conqueror of the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the SonsonSon of God?

The Divine Testimony and the Gift of Eternal Life

6This is HeheHe who came through water and blood—Jesus Christ—not through water alone, but through water and through blood; and the SpiritspiritSpirit is the witness, inasmuch as the SpiritspiritSpirit is truth itself. 7For there are three witnesses— 8the SpiritspiritSpirit, the water, and the blood—and these three concur as one. 9If we accept human testimony, the testimony of God is greater still, because this is God's testimony: He has borne witness concerning HishisHis SonsonSon. 10Whoever believes in the SonsonSon of God possesses that testimony within himself; whoever does not believe God has rendered HimhimHim a liar, because he has not trusted the testimony that God bore concerning HishisHis SonsonSon. 11Moreover, this is the testimony: God has conferred upon us life everlasting, and that life resides in HishisHis SonsonSon. 12Whoever possesses the SonsonSon possesses life; whoever lacks the SonsonSon of God is devoid of life. 13I have inscribed these words for you who trust in the name of the SonsonSon of God, that you may be assured you possess life everlasting.

Confidence in Prayer and Final Admonitions

14And this is the boldness we possess before HimhimHim: that if we petition HimhimHim for anything consonant with HishisHis will, HeheHe hearkens to us. 15And if we know that HeheHe hears us in whatever we petition, we know that we possess the petitions we have requested from HimhimHim. 16Should anyone perceive his brother committing a transgression not incurring death, let him petition, and God will grant life to that brother—not, however, for transgressions incurring death. There is a transgression incurring death; concerning that, I do not enjoin him to inquire. 17All unrighteousness is sin, yet there is sin that does not incur death. 18We know that everyone begotten of God does not persist in sin; rather, the OneoneOne begotten of God guards him, and the evil one cannot lay hold of him. 19We know that we derive from God, whereas the whole world is enthralled to the evil one. 20Moreover, we know that the SonsonSon of God has come and has imparted to us discernment, so that we may know the TruetrueTrue OneoneOne; and we abide in the TruetrueTrue OneoneOne, in HishisHis SonsonSon Jesus Christ. This OneoneOne is the true God and life everlasting. 21Little children, keep yourselves unblemished from idols.
