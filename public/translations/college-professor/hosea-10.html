Israel’s Luxuriant Vine and the Proliferation of Altars

1Israel is a luxuriant vine that bears fruit for itself; as its harvest multiplied, it multiplied altars in like measure, and as its land flourished, it made its sacred pillars ever more splendid. 2Their hearts are divided; now they must bear their guilt. He will break down their altars and shatter their sacred pillars.

3For now they will say, “We have no king, because we did not fear the LORD. What, then, could a king do for us?”

4They utter empty words and make covenants by false oaths, so justice springs up like hemlock in the furrows of a field.

The Impending Ruin of Beth-aven

5The inhabitants of Samaria will dread the calves of Beth-aven, for their people mourn over the idol, while its priests exult over its glory, since it has been carried away from them. 6It too will be carried off to Assyria as tribute to the great king. Ephraim will be seized with shame, and Israel will be put to shame by its own counsel.

7Samaria’s king will be cut off like foam upon the water.

8The high places of Aven will be destroyed—the very sin of Israel. Thorns and thistles will overrun their altars, and they will cry to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall upon us!”

The Reckoning for Israel’s Enduring Sin

9Since the days of Gibeah, O Israel, you have sinned, and there you have remained. Will not war at Gibeah overtake the sons of iniquity?

10I will discipline them at MymyMy pleasure; nations will be gathered against them to bind them for their double transgression. 11Ephraim is a trained heifer that loves to thresh; so I will put a yoke on her fair neck. I will harness Ephraim; Judah will plow, and Jacob will break up his fallow ground.

A Summons to Sow Righteousness

12Sow righteousness for yourselves, reap according to steadfast love; break up your fallow ground, for it is time to seek the LORD, until HeheHe comes and rains righteousness upon you.

13You have plowed wickedness and reaped injustice; you have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you have trusted in your own way and in your many warriors, 14a tumult will rise among your people, and all your fortresses will be devastated—as Shalman devastated Beth-arbel on the day of battle, when mothers were dashed to pieces along with their children.

15Thus it will be done to you, O Bethel, because of your wickedness. At dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.
