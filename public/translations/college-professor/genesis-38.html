Judah’s Separation from His Brothers and His Canaanite Marriage

1At that juncture, Judah withdrew from his brothers and descended to reside with an Adullamite named Hirah. 2There Judah beheld the daughter of a Canaanite called Shua; he took her in marriage and cohabited with her. 3She conceived and bore a son, whom he named Er. 4She conceived once more and bore a son, naming him Onan. 5She bore yet another son and named him Shelah; his birth occurred at Kezib.

The Deaths of Er and Onan and Tamar’s Widowhood

6Judah procured a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name was Tamar. 7But Er, Judah’s firstborn, proved wicked in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD slew him. 8Then Judah said to Onan, “Go in to your brother’s wife, fulfillfulfilfulfill the levirate obligation for her, and raise up offspring for your brother.”

9Yet Onan knew that the offspring would not be his own; consequently, whenever he lay with his brother’s wife, he spilled his semen upon the ground, lest he provide offspring for his brother. 10What he did displeased the LORD, so the LORD put him to death as well. 11Thereupon Judah said to Tamar, his daughter-in-law, “Remain a widow in your father’s house until my son Shelah matures.” For he reasoned, “Perhaps he too will die, like his brothers.” So Tamar departed and dwelt in her father’s house.

Tamar’s Veiled Encounter at Enaim

12After many days, Judah’s wife—the daughter of Shua—died. Once Judah was consoled, he ascended to Timnah with his friend Hirah the Adullamite, to the shearers of his flock. 13Tamar was informed, “Your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.”

14She therefore removed her widow’s garments, veiled herself, and sat at the entrance of Enaim, which lies on the road to Timnah; for she perceived that Shelah had grown up, yet she had not been given to him as wife. 15When Judah saw her, he took her for a prostitute, because she had covered her face.

16Unaware that she was his daughter-in-law, he turned aside to her by the roadside and said, “Come, pray, let me lie with you.”

She replied, “What will you give me, that you may lie with me?”

17“I will send you a young goat from the flock,” he said.

“Will you give me a pledge until you send it?” she asked.

18“What pledge shall I give you?” he asked.

“Your seal and its cord, and the staff in your hand,” she answered. So he gave them to her, lay with her, and she conceived by him.

19Then she arose and departed, removed her veil, and resumed her widow’s garments.

20Judah sent the young goat by his Adullamite friend to retrieve the pledge from the woman, but he could not find her.

21He inquired of the men of that place, “Where is the shrine prostitute who was beside the road at Enaim?”

They replied, “No shrine prostitute has been here.”

22So he returned to Judah and said, “I did not find her; moreover, the men of the place declared, ‘No shrine prostitute has been here.’”

23Judah responded, “Let her keep them, lest we become a byword; after all, I sent this goat, yet you did not find her.”

24About three months later, Judah was told, “Your daughter-in-law Tamar has engaged in prostitution, and behold, she is pregnant by her harlotry.”

Judah said, “Bring her out and let her be burned.”

25As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law: “I am pregnant by the man to whom these belong,” she said; “pray see whether you recognizerecogniserecognize whose seal, cords, and staff these are.”

26Judah recognizedrecogniserecognized them and said, “She is more righteous than I, for I did not give her to my son Shelah.” And he never again lay with her.

27When the time of her delivery arrived, behold, twins were in her womb.

28As she was giving birth, one child put forth his hand, and the midwife took a scarlet thread and bound it upon his wrist, saying, “This one came out first.”

29But as he drew back his hand, his brother emerged, and she said, “How you have burst forth!” So he was named Perez.

30Afterward his brother came out, the one with the scarlet thread upon his wrist, and he was named Zerah.
