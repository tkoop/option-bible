The LORD’s Summons to Covenant Fidelity

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘I am the LORD your God. 3You shall not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you dwelt, nor as they do in the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you; you shall not walk in their statutes. 4You shall follow MymyMy ordinances and keep MymyMy statutes, walking in them; I am the LORD your God. 5You shall keep MymyMy statutes and MymyMy ordinances, which a person shall do and live by them; I am the LORD.

Prohibitions Against Unlawful Sexual Unions

6“None of you shall approach a close relative by blood for sexual relations; I am the LORD.

7“You shall not have sexual relations with your mother; she is your mother, and you shall not have sexual relations with her. 8You shall not have sexual relations with your father’s wife, for that would expose your father’s nakedness. 9You shall not have sexual relations with your sister, whether she is your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter, whether she was raised in the same household or elsewhere. 10You shall not have sexual relations with your granddaughter, whether your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter, for their nakedness is your own. 11You shall not have sexual relations with the daughter of your father’s wife if she was raised in your father’s household, for she is your sister.

12“You shall not have sexual relations with your father’s sister, for she is your father’s close relative. 13You shall not have sexual relations with your mother’s sister, for she is your mother’s close relative. 14You shall not have sexual relations with your father’s brother—that is, with his wife—for she is your aunt. 15You shall not have sexual relations with your daughter-in-law, for she is your son’s wife. 16You shall not have sexual relations with your brother’s wife, for she belongs to your brother.

17“You shall not marry a woman and her daughter, or her granddaughter through her son or daughter, in order to have sexual relations with them; they are her close relatives, and such a union is depravity. 18You shall not marry a woman and then her sister as a rival, having sexual relations with the second while the first is still alive. 19You shall not approach a woman to have sexual relations with her during her menstrual uncleanness. 20You shall not have sexual relations with your neighbourneighbourneighbor’s wife, thereby defiling yourself. 21You shall not give any of your children to be sacrificed to Molech, nor profane the name of your God; I am the LORD. 22You shall not have sexual relations with a man as with a woman; that is detestable. 23You shall not have sexual relations with any animal, defiling yourself with it; and no woman shall present herself to an animal for mating, for that is a perversion.

The Defilement of the Land and Its Consequences

24“Do not defile yourselves in any of these ways, for by all these things the nations that I am driving out before you have defiled themselves. 25The land became defiled, so I punished its iniquity, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.
