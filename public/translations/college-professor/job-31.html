Job’s Covenant with His Eyes and Integrity in Conduct

1I have entered into a covenant with my eyes; wherefore should I fix my gaze upon a maiden? 2What allotment does God possess from above, or what heritage does the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty hold from the heights? 3Is not calamity the portion of the unrighteous, and disaster the lot of workers of iniquity? 4Does HeheHe not observe my ways and enumerate all my steps? 5If I have walked in falsehood, and my foot has hastened unto deceit, 6let HimhimHim weigh me in the scales of justice, and let God acknowledge my integrity. 7If my step has deviated from the path, and my heart has followed my eyes, and if any stain has adhered to my hands, 8then let another consume what I have sown, and let my descendants be rooted out. 9If my heart has been seduced by a woman, or if I have lain in ambush at my neighbourneighbourneighbor’s doorway, 10then let my wife grind for another, and let strangers bow down upon her. 11For that would constitute a heinous crime, an iniquity adjudicable by judges. 12For it is a fire consuming unto Abaddon, and it would extirpate all my increase.

Compassion for the Vulnerable and Reverence for God

13If I have spurned the cause of my male or female servant when they contended with me, 14what then shall I do when God arises? When HeheHe scrutinizes, what answer shall I return to HimhimHim? 15Did not HeheHe who fashioned me in the womb fashion him? And did not OneoneOne form us in the womb? 16If I have withheld from the poor their desire, or caused the widow’s eyes to fail, 17or eaten my morsel alone while the orphan did not partake of it— 18for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and from my mother’s womb I have guided her— 19if I have seen anyone perishing without raiment, or the needy without covering, 20if his loins have not blessed me, and if he was not warmed by the fleece of my sheep; 21if I have lifted my hand against the fatherless because I perceived my succor in the gate, 22then let my shoulder blade fall from my shoulder, and let my arm be broken from its socket. 23For calamity from God was a dread to me, and because of HishisHis majesty I could not endure.

Freedom from Idolatry, Vengeance, and Concealed Sin

24If I have made gold my confidence, or said to fine gold, “You are my trust”; 25if I have rejoiced because my wealth was abundant, and because my hand had acquired much; 26if I have beheld the sun when it shone, or the moon progressing in splendoursplendoursplendor, 27and my heart has been secretly enticed, and my mouth has kissed my hand, 28this too would be an iniquity for judges, for I would have denied God above. 29If I have rejoiced at the destruction of my enemy, or exulted when evil overtook him— 30I have not even permitted my mouth to sin by requesting his life under a curse— 31if the men of my tent have not said, “Who has not been satisfied with his meat?”— 32the stranger did not lodge outside; I opened my doors to the wayfarer— 33if I have concealed my transgressions like Adam, hiding my iniquity in my bosom, 34because I stood in great fear of the multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I remained silent and did not go out of the door—

Job’s Appeal for Vindication

35Oh, that I had OneoneOne to hear me! Here is my signature; let the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty answer me; and let my accuser compose an indictment. 36Surely I would bear it upon my shoulder; I would bind it upon me like a crown. 37I would recount to HimhimHim the number of my steps; like a prince I would draw near to HimhimHim. 38If my land cries out against me, and its furrows weep in unison; 39if I have consumed its produce without remuneration, and caused the life of its possessors to expire, 40let thorns spring up instead of wheat, and weeds instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.
