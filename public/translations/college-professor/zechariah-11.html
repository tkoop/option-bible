1Open your gates, O Lebanon, that fire may devour your cedars. 2Wail, O cypress, for the cedar has fallen, because the majestic trees have been devastated; wail, O oaks of Bashan, for the impenetrable forest has been brought low. 3Listen to the wailing of the shepherds, for their glory has been ruined; listen to the roaring of the lions, for the pride of the Jordan has been ruined.

The Flock Doomed to Slaughter

4Thus says the LORD my God: “Shepherd the flock destined for slaughter—the very flock whose buyers slaughter them with impunity, whose sellers proclaim, ‘Blessed be the LORD, for I have grown wealthy,’ and whose shepherds show them no compassion. 5For I will no longer have compassion on the inhabitants of the land,” declares the LORD. “Behold, I am about to deliver mankind, each into the hand of his neighbourneighbourneighbor and into the hand of his king; they shall crush the land, and I will not deliver anyone from their power.”

The Worthless Shepherd

15Then the LORD said to me, “Take once more the implements of a foolish shepherd. 16For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not attend to the perishing, will not seek the young, will not heal the broken, and will not sustain the standing; instead, he will devour the flesh of the fat and tear off their hooves.”

17Woe to the worthless shepherd who abandons the flock! A sword shall be upon his arm and upon his right eye; his arm shall wither utterly, and his right eye shall be completely darkened.
