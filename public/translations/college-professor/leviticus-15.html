Divine Ordinances Governing Male Discharges

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2“Speak to the Israelites and declare to them: If any man suffers a morbid discharge from his flesh, that very discharge renders him ritually unclean. 3This constitutes his uncleanness in the matter of his discharge: whether his flesh emits the flow or retains it, the condition itself is his uncleanness.

4“Every couch upon which the afflicted man lies shall be unclean, and every article upon which he sits shall be unclean. 5Anyone who touches his couch shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 6Anyone who sits upon an article on which the afflicted man has sat shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 7Anyone who touches the flesh of the afflicted man shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 8If the afflicted man expectorates upon a ritually clean person, the latter shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening.

9“Every saddle upon which the afflicted man rides shall be unclean. 10Everything beneath him, whatsoever he sits upon, shall impart uncleanness until evening to anyone who touches it; and whoever transports such objects shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 11Anyone touched by the afflicted man, if he has not rinsed his hands in water, shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 12Any earthen vessel touched by the afflicted man shall be shattered, whereas any wooden vessel shall be rinsed with water.

13“When the afflicted man is cleansed of his discharge, he shall reckon seven days for his purification, launder his garments, bathe his flesh in running water, and thereafter be clean. 14On the eighth day he shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, present himself before the the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and deliver them to the priest. 15The priest shall render one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering, and shall make atonement for him before the the LORD on account of his discharge.

Seminal Emission and Its Consequences

16“If semen is emitted by a man, he shall bathe his entire body in water and remain unclean until evening. 17Every garment and every skin upon which semen falls shall be washed in water and remain unclean until evening. 18If a woman lies with a man and copulative emission occurs, both shall bathe in water and remain unclean until evening.

Menstrual and Prolonged Female Discharges

19“When a woman has a discharge, and her discharge in her flesh is blood, she shall be in her menstrual impurity for seven days; everyone who touches her shall be unclean until evening. 20Everything upon which she lies during her menstrual impurity shall be unclean, and everything upon which she sits shall be unclean. 21Anyone who touches her bed shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 22Anyone who touches any object upon which she sits shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 23Whether it is the bed or the object upon which she sits, whoever touches it shall be unclean until evening. 24If a man lies with her, her menstrual impurity shall be upon him; he shall be unclean for seven days, and every bed upon which he lies shall be unclean.

25“If a woman has a flow of blood for many days outside the time of her menstruation, or if she has a flow beyond her menstrual period, she shall be unclean throughout all the days of her discharge, as in the days of her menstruation. 26Every bed upon which she lies during all the days of her discharge shall be to her as the bed of her menstruation, and every object upon which she sits shall be unclean, as in the uncleanness of her menstruation. 27Anyone who touches them shall be unclean; he shall launder his garments, bathe in water, and remain unclean until evening. 28If she is cleansed from her discharge, she shall count seven days for herself, and afterward she shall be clean. 29On the eighth day she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 30The priest shall make one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for her before the the LORD from her discharge.

Communal Separation and the Summation of the Law

31“Thus you shall separate the Israelites from their uncleanness, lest they die in their uncleanness by defiling MymyMy tabernacle that is among them. 32This is the law for the one who has a discharge, for the man from whom semen is emitted so that he becomes unclean thereby, 33and for the woman in her menstrual impurity, and for the one, male or female, who has a discharge, and for the man who lies with an unclean woman.”
