A Litany of Thanksgiving for the LORD’s Enduring Covenant Love

1Offer thanksgiving to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 2Offer thanksgiving to the God of gods, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 3Offer thanksgiving to the Lord of lords, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever.

4To HimhimHim who alone does great wonders, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 5To HimhimHim who by understanding made the heavens, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 6To HimhimHim who spread out the earth upon the waters, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 7To HimhimHim who made the great luminaries, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 8The sun to govern the day, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 9The moon and stars to govern the night, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever.

10To HimhimHim who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 11And brought Israel out from among them, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 12With a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 13To HimhimHim who divided the Red SeaseaSea, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 14And led Israel through its midst, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 15But swept Pharaoh and his host into the Red SeaseaSea, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever.

16To HimhimHim who led HishisHis people through the wilderness, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 17To HimhimHim who struck down great kings, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 18And slew mighty kings, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 19Sihon king of the Amorites, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 20And Og king of Bashan, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 21And gave their land as an inheritance, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 22A heritage to Israel HishisHis servant, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever.

23To HimhimHim who remembered us in our low estate, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 24And delivered us from our enemies, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 25Who gives food to all flesh, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 26Offer thanksgiving to the God of Heavenheavenheaven, for HishisHis loving devotion endures forever.
