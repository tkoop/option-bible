Cities Allotted to the Levites

1The LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying, “2Command the Israelites to give the Levites, out of the inheritance of their holdings, cities in which to dwell, together with pasturelands surrounding them, and you shall give the pasturelands to the Levites. 3The cities shall be theirs to dwell in, and their pasturelands shall be for their cattle, their property, and all their animals. 4The pasturelands of the cities that you give to the Levites shall extend a thousand cubits outward from the wall of each city in every direction. 5You shall measure outside the city: on the east side two thousand cubits, on the south side two thousand cubits, on the west side two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits, with the city in the centrecentrecenter; this shall constitute the pasturelands of their cities. 6The cities that you give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall designate so that the manslayer may flee to them, and besides these you shall give forty-two cities. 7All the cities that you give to the Levites shall be forty-eight, together with their pasturelands. 8As for the cities that you give from the possession of the Israelites, from the larger tribe you shall give many, and from the smaller tribe you shall give few; each tribe shall give cities to the Levites in proportion to the inheritance it receives.”

Cities of Refuge for the Manslayer

9The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, “10Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan, 11you shall designate cities to be cities of refuge for you, so that the manslayer who kills a person unintentionally may flee to them. 12The cities shall be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood, so that the manslayer does not die until he stands before the congregation for judgmentjudgementjudgment. 13The cities that you give shall be six cities of refuge. 14You shall provide three cities beyond the Jordan and three cities in the land of Canaan; they shall be cities of refuge. 15These six cities shall be a refuge for the Israelites, for the sojourner, and for the temporary resident among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally may flee there.

16“If he strikes him with an iron implement and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall be put to death. 17If he strikes him with a stone in his hand that can kill, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall be put to death. 18If he strikes him with a wooden implement in his hand that can kill, and he dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall be put to death. 19The avenger of blood himself shall put the murderer to death; when he encounters him, he shall put him to death. 20If he pushes him in hatred, or throws something at him with intent, and he dies, 21or if he strikes him in enmity with his hand so that he dies, the striker shall be put to death; he is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death when he encounters him. 22But if he pushes him suddenly without enmity, or throws any object at him without intent, 23or if, without seeing him, he drops a stone on him that could kill, and he dies, though he was not his enemy and did not seek his harm, 24then the congregation shall judge between the striker and the avenger of blood according to these ordinances. 25The congregation shall rescue the manslayer from the hand of the avenger of blood and return him to his city of refuge, where he had fled, and he shall dwell there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil. 26But if the manslayer goes beyond the boundary of his city of refuge to which he fled, 27and the avenger of blood finds him outside the boundary of his city of refuge and kills the manslayer, the avenger shall incur no bloodguilt. 28For he must remain in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest; after the death of the high priest, the manslayer may return to the land of his possession.

29“These things shall be a statute of judgmentjudgementjudgment for you throughout your generations in all your dwellings. 30If anyone kills a person, the murderer shall be put to death on the evidence of witnesses; but no one shall be put to death on the testimony of a single witness. 31Moreover, you shall not accept a ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death, but he shall surely be put to death. 32Nor shall you accept a ransom for one who has fled to his city of refuge, so that he may return to dwell in the land before the death of the high priest. 33You shall not pollute the land in which you are, for blood pollutes the land, and no expiation can be made for the land for the blood that is shed in it except by the blood of the one who shed it. 34You shall not defile the land in which you dwell, in which I, the LORD, dwell among you, for I, the LORD, dwell among the Israelites.”
