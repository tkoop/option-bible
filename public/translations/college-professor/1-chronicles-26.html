The Organization of the Gatekeeping Divisions

1The gatekeeping corps was divided as follows: among the Korahites, Meshelemiah son of Kore—one of the sons of Asaph—presided over a division; and Meshelemiah's sons were, in birth order, Zechariah, Jediael, Zebadiah, Jathniel, Elam, Jehohanan, and Elioenai. 23

4Obed-edom's sons were, likewise in birth order, Shemaiah, Jehozabad, Joah, Sacar, Nethanel, Ammiel, Issachar, and Peullethai, for God had blessed him. 5

6To Shemaiah were born sons who wielded authority within their father's household, being mighty men of valourvalourvalor. 7Shemaiah's sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad, while his brothers Elihu and Semachiah were likewise valorous men. 8All of these derived from the sons of Obed-edom—themselves, their sons, and their brothers—men of strength and valourvalourvalor, sixty-two in all, for the service. 9Meshelemiah also had sons and brothers, valorous men, eighteen in all.

10From the sons of Merari, Hosah had sons: Shimri the chief—for although he was not the firstborn, his father designated him chief—followed by Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. 11Hosah had thirteen sons and brothers in total.

12These divisions of the gatekeepers, corresponding to their chief men, were charged with ministering in the house of the LORD, even as their brothers were; 13and they cast lots, the lesser and the greater alike, according to their ancestral houses, for every gate.

14The lot for the east fell to Shelemiah; and for his son Zechariah, a counsellorcounsellorcounselor of discernment, they cast lots, and the north fell to him. 15To Obed-edom fell the south, and to his sons the storehouse. 16To Shuppim and Hosah fell the west, by the Shallecheth GategateGate on the ascending road, guard corresponding to guard.

17On any given day six Levites stood guard on the east, four on the north, four on the south, and two by two at the storehouse; on the west, at the Parbar, four stood on the road and two at the Parbar. 1819These were the divisions of the gatekeepers among the sons of Korah and the sons of Merari.

The Levitical Treasuries and Their Custodians

20Among the Levites, Ahijah presided over the treasuries of the house of God and over the treasuries of the dedicated things. 21The sons of Ladan—the Gershonite descendants of Ladan, and the heads of the ancestral houses of Ladan the Gershonite—were Jehieli; 22and the sons of Jehieli, Zetham and his brother Joel, presided over the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

23From the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites came Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, who was chief over the treasuries. 2425His relatives through Eliezer were Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.

26This Shelomith and his brothers were charged with all the treasuries of the dedicated things that King David and the heads of the fathers' houses—the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the commanders of the army—had dedicated. 27From the spoil of their battles they dedicated these things to maintain the house of the LORD. 28All that Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated—everything so dedicated—was placed in the custody of Shelomith and his brothers.

Administrative and Judicial Appointments Beyond the Jordan

29Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were assigned to outside duties for Israel, serving as officers and judges. 30Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers—1,700 able men—were in charge of Israel's affairs west of the Jordan, for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king. 31Of the Hebronites, Jerijah was chief according to the genealogies of his fathers; in the fortieth year of David's reign they were sought out, and men of valourvalourvalor were found among them at Jazer in Gilead. 32His brothers were 2,700 men of valourvalourvalor, heads of fathers' houses, whom King David appointed over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every matter pertaining to God and for the affairs of the king.
