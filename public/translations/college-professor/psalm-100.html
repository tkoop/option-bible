A Psalm of Thanksgiving

1Shout joyfully unto the the LORD, all the earth. 2Serve the the LORD with gladness; come into HishisHis presence with exultant song. 3Know that the the LORD—HeheHe is God; it is HeheHe who fashioned us, and not we ourselves; we are HishisHis people, and the sheep of HishisHis pasture. 4Enter HishisHis gates with thanksgiving and HishisHis courts with praise; give thanks unto HimhimHim, bless HishisHis NamenameName. 5For the the LORD is good; HishisHis steadfast love endures forever, and HishisHis faithfulness unto all generations.
