The Birth and Preservation of Moses

1A man of the house of Levi went forth and took a daughter of Levi to wife. 2The woman conceived and bore a son; and when she perceived that he was a comely child, she concealed him for three months. 3When she could no longer hide him, she fashioned for him a basket of bulrushes, daubed it with bitumen and pitch, placed the child within it, and laid it among the reeds upon the riverbank. 4His sister stationed herself at a distance to discern what would befall him.

5The daughter of Pharaoh descended to bathe in the river, while her maidens walked along the bank; and seeing the basket amidst the reeds, she sent her handmaid to retrieve it. 6When she opened it, she beheld the child, and lo, the infant wept. Moved with compassion, she declared, “This is one of the Hebrews’ children.”

7Then his sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, “Shall I go and summon a nurse from among the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for you?”

8Pharaoh's daughter replied, “Go.” So the girl went and called the child's mother. 9Pharaoh's daughter said to her, “Take this child away and nurse him for me, and I will give you your wages.” The woman took the child and nursed him.

10The child grew, and she brought him to Pharaoh's daughter, and he became her son. She named him Moses, saying, “Because I drew him out of the water.”

Moses Intervenes and Flees to Midian

11In those days, when Moses had grown to manhood, he went out to his brethren and observed their burdens; and he saw an Egyptian beating a Hebrew, one of his brethren. 12He looked this way and that, and perceiving no one, he struck the Egyptian and concealed him in the sand.

13On the following day he went out and saw two Hebrew men quarreling; and he said to the wrongdoer, “Why do you strike your companion?”

14The man retorted, “Who made you a prince and judge over us? Do you intend to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian?”

Moses was seized with fear and said, “Surely the matter is known.”

15When Pharaoh heard of the matter, he sought to kill Moses. But Moses fled from Pharaoh's presence and settled in the land of Midian, where he sat down beside a well.

Moses at the Well in Midian

16The priest of Midian had seven daughters; they came and drew water and filled the troughs to water their father's flock. 17Then shepherds arrived and drove them away; but Moses rose to their defencedefencedefense, watered their flock.

18When they returned to Reuel their father, he asked, “Why have you come so early today?”

19They answered, “An Egyptian rescued us from the shepherds, and he even drew water for us and watered the flock.”

20He said to his daughters, “Where is he? Why have you left the man behind? Invite him to share a meal.”

21Moses consented to dwell with the man, and he gave Moses his daughter Zipporah. 22She bore a son, and he named him Gershom, saying, “I have been a sojourner in a foreign land.”

God Hears Israel's Cry

23In the course of those many days the king of Egypt died, and the Israelites groaned under their bondage and cried out; and their cry for deliverance from servitude ascended to God. 24God heard their groaning, and God remembered HishisHis covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. 25God looked upon the Israelites, and God took cognizance of them.
