Aram and Israel’s Threat against Jerusalem

1In the days of Ahaz son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram marched up to wage war against Jerusalem, yet neither he nor Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, could prevail against it. 2When the house of David was told, “Aram has allied itself with Ephraim,” the hearts of Ahaz and his people trembled like forest trees before the wind.

The LORD’s Message to Ahaz

3Then the LORD said to Isaiah, “Go out now, you and your son Shear-jashub, to meet Ahaz at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, on the road to the Fuller’s FieldfieldField, 4and say to him, ‘Be careful, keep calm, do not be afraid, and do not let your heart grow faint because of these two smoldering stubs of firebrands—in the fierce anger of Rezin and Aram and the son of Remaliah. 5For Aram, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah have plotted evil against you, saying, 6“Let us march up against Judah, throw it into panic, divide it among ourselves, and install the son of Tabeel as king in its midst.” 7Thus says the Lord the LORD: “It shall not stand, nor shall it come to pass. 8For the head of Aram is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin; within sixty-five years Ephraim will be shattered so that it is no longer a people. 9The head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah. If you do not believe, you will not be established.”’”

The Sign of Immanuel

10The LORD spoke again to Ahaz, saying, 11“Ask a sign for yourself from the LORD your God; make it as deep as Sheol or as high as the heavens above.”

12But Ahaz said, “I will not ask, and I will not put the LORD to the test.”

13Then Isaiah said, “Hear now, O house of David: Is it too little for you to weary people, that you should weary my God as well? 14Therefore the Lord HimselfhimselfHimself will give you a sign: Behold, the virgin will conceive and bear a son, and she will call HishisHis name Immanuel. 15He will eat curds and honey until HeheHe knows to refuse the evil and choose the good. 16For before the boy knows to refuse the evil and choose the good, the land of the two kings you dread will be abandoned.”

The Assyrian Invasion and Its Aftermath

17The LORD will bring upon you and upon your people and upon your father’s house days that have not come since the day Ephraim departed from Judah—the king of Assyria. 18In that day the LORD will whistle for the fly that is at the end of the rivers of Egypt and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria. 19And they will all come and settle in the steep ravines, in the clefts of the rocks, and on all thorn bushes and in all pastures. 20In that day the Lord will shave with a razor hired from beyond the River—with the king of Assyria—the head, the hair of the legs, and the beard.

21In that day a man will keep alive a young cow and two sheep. 22And because of the abundance of milk they give, he will eat curds; for all who remain in the land will eat curds and honey. 23In that day every place where there were a thousand vines worth a thousand shekels of silver will become briers and thorns. 24Men will go there with bow and arrow, because all the land will be briers and thorns. 25And as for all the hills that were hoed with a hoe, you will not go there for fear of briers and thorns; but they will become a place where oxen are turned loose and sheep trample.
