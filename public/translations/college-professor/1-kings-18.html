Elijah's Commission

1After the lapse of many days—indeed, in the third year—the word of the the LORD came to Elijah, saying, "Go, present yourself to Ahab, and I shall grant rain upon the face of the earth." 2So Elijah went to present himself to Ahab. Now the famine bore down with severity upon Samaria.

3Meanwhile Ahab summoned Obadiah, who was in charge of the palace. (Now Obadiah feared the the LORD exceedingly.) 4For when Jezebel was exterminating the prophets of the the LORD, Obadiah had taken a hundred prophets and concealed them, fifty in a cave, and had sustained them with bread and water.

5Then Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the wadis; perhaps we may find forage and keep alive the horses and mules, so that we do not have to slaughter some of the livestock." 6So they divided the land between themselves to traverse it: Ahab went one route alone, and Obadiah went another route alone.

7As Obadiah was on the road, behold, Elijah encountered him; and he recognizedrecogniserecognized him and fell upon his face and said, "Are you indeed my lord Elijah?"

8He replied, "I am. Go, say to your lord, 'Behold, Elijah is here.'"

9HeheHe said, "What sin have I committed, that you should deliver your servant into the hand of Ahab to be put to death? 10As the the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom to which my lord has not sent to seek you; and when they said, 'He is not here,' he made the kingdom and the nation swear that they had not found you. 11And now you say, 'Go, tell your lord, "Behold, Elijah is here."'"

12"And it shall come to pass, as soon as I depart from you, that the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD will carry you off—I know not whither—and when I come and tell Ahab, and he cannot find you, he will kill me, although I, your servant, have feared the the LORD from my youth. 13Has it not been told to my lord what I did when Jezebel slew the prophets of the the LORD, how I hid a hundred men of the the LORD's prophets, fifty in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water? 14And now you say, 'Go, tell your lord, "Behold, Elijah is here,"' and he will kill me."

15Elijah said, "As the the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, I will certainly show myself to him today." 16So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and reported to him; and Ahab went to meet Elijah.

Elijah Confronts Ahab

17When Ahab saw Elijah, Ahab said to him, "Are you he who troubles Israel?"

18He answered, "I have not troubled Israel, but you and your father's house have, in that you have forsaken the commandments of the the LORD and have followed the Baals."

19"Now therefore send and gather all Israel to me at Mount Carmel, and the prophets of Baal four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of Asherah four hundred, who eat at Jezebel's table." 20So Ahab sent to all the Israelites and gathered the prophets together at Mount Carmel.

The Contest on Mount Carmel

21Elijah drew near to all the people and said, "How long will you limp between two opinions? If the the LORD is God, follow HimhimHim; but if Baal, follow him." And the people gave him no answer.

22Then Elijah said to the people, "I alone am left a prophet of the the LORD, but Baal's prophets are four hundred and fifty men. 23Let them therefore give us two bulls; and let them choose one bull for themselves and cut it in pieces and lay it on the wood, but put no fire to it; and I will prepare the other bull and lay it on the wood, and put no fire to it. 24Then you shall call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of the the LORD; and the God who answers by fire, HeheHe is God."

And all the people answered and said, "ThetheThe proposal is sound."

25Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose for yourselves one bull and prepare it first, for you are many; and call on the name of your god, but put no fire to it." 26So they took the bull that was given them and prepared it, and called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, "O Baal, answer us!" But there was no voice, and no one answered. And they cavorted about the altar they had made.

27At noon Elijah mocked them and said, "Cry aloud, for he is a god; perhaps he is meditating, or he is attending to his needs, or he is on a journey, or perhaps he is asleep and must be awakened." 28So they cried with a loud voice and gashed themselves according to their custom with swords and lances, until the blood gushed out upon them. 29When midday had passed, they prophesied until the time of the offering of the evening sacrifice, but there was no voice, no one answered, and no one paid attention.

30Then Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me." So all the people came near to him. And he repaired the altar of the the LORD that had been thrown down. 31Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of the the LORD had come, saying, "Israel shall be your name." 32And with the stones he built an altar in the name of the the LORD; and he made a trench around the altar, large enough to hold two seahs of seed.

33And he arranged the wood and cut the bull in pieces and laid it on the wood. 34He said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it on the burnt offering and on the wood." Then he said, "Do it a second time." And they did it a second time. Then he said, "Do it a third time." And they did it a third time. 35The water ran around the altar, and he also filled the trench with water.

36At the time of the offering of the evening sacrifice, Elijah the prophet came near and said, "O the LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known this day that YouyouYou are God in Israel, and that I am YouryourYour servant, and that I have done all these things at YouryourYour word. 37Answer me, O the LORD, answer me, that this people may know that YouyouYou are the the LORD God, and that YouyouYou have turned their heart back again."

38Then the fire of the the LORD fell and consumed the burnt offering and the wood and the stones and the dust, and licked up the water that was in the trench. 39When all the people saw it, they fell on their faces and said, "ThetheThe the LORD, HeheHe is God! The the LORD, HeheHe is God!"

40Elijah said to them, "Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape." And they seized them; and Elijah brought them down to the brook Kishon and slaughtered them there.

The Return of Rain

41Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink, for there is a sound of abundance of rain." 42So Ahab went up to eat and to drink. And Elijah went up to the top of Carmel; and he bowed himself down upon the earth and put his face between his knees.

43He said to his servant, "Go up now, look toward the sea."

And he went up and looked and said, "There is nothing."

And he said, "Go again seven times."

44At the seventh time he said, "Behold, a little cloud like a man's hand is rising out of the sea."

And he said, "Go up, say to Ahab, 'Prepare your chariot and go down, lest the rain stop you.'"

45In a little while the heavens grew black with clouds and wind, and there was a great rain. And Ahab rode and went to Jezreel. 46The hand of the the LORD was on Elijah; and he girded up his loins and ran before Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.
