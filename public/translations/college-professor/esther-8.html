Haman's Estate Transferred and Esther's Supplication

1On that very day King Ahasuerus bestowed upon Queen Esther the estate of Haman, the adversary of the Jews; and Mordecai presented himself before the king, because Esther had disclosed to him how Mordecai was related to her. 2The king, moreover, removed the signet ring he had reclaimed from Haman and conferred it upon Mordecai, while Esther installed Mordecai over Haman's household.

3Esther spoke once more before the king; she prostrated herself at his feet, wept, and implored him to avert the calamity devised by Haman the Agagite and to nullify the machination he had contrived against the Jews. 4The king extended the golden sceptresceptrescepter toward Esther, whereupon she rose and stood before the king.

5Esther said, “If it seems good to the king, and if I have found favourfavourfavor before him, and if the matter appears proper before the king, and if I am pleasing in his eyes, let it be written to rescind the dispatches devised by Haman son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, which he wrote to annihilate the Jews throughout all the king's provinces. 6For how could I endure to witness the disaster befalling my people, or how could I endure to witness the extermination of my kindred?”

The King Authorizes a Counter-Decree

7King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther and to Mordecai the Jew, “Behold, I have given Esther the house of Haman, and they have hanged him on the gallows because he stretched out his hand against the Jews. 8Now you yourselves write in the king's name concerning the Jews whatever seems good in your eyes, and seal it with the king's ring; for a document written in the king's name and sealed with the king's ring is irrevocable.”

The Royal Edict Dispatched Throughout the Empire

9So the king's scribes were summoned at that time, in the third month, that is, the month of Sivan, on the twenty-third day; and in accordance with all that Mordecai commanded, it was written to the Jews, to the satraps, to the governors, and to the officials of the one hundred twenty-seven provinces from India to Cush—province by province, people by people, in their own script and language, and to the Jews in their script and language. 10He wrote in the name of King Ahasuerus, sealed the letters with the king's ring, and dispatched them by mounted couriers riding swift horses bred from the royal stock.

11The king granted the Jews in every city permission to assemble and to stand for their lives—to destroy, to kill, and to annihilate every armed force of any people or province that assailed them, together with their little ones and their women, and to plunder their possessions. 12This was to occur on one day in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus, namely the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, the month of Adar. 13A copy of the writing was to be issued as law in every province and published to all peoples, so that the Jews might be prepared for that day to take vengeance upon their enemies. 14The couriers, mounted on royal steeds, rode out in haste, urged on by the king's command; and the decree was promulgated in the citadel of Susa.

Jewish Deliverance and Public Rejoicing

15Mordecai departed from the king's presence in royal raiment of blue and white, with a great crown of gold and a robe of purple fine linen; and the city of Susa shouted and rejoiced. 16For the Jews there was light and gladness, joy and honourhonourhonor. 17In every province and every city, wherever the king's command and decree arrived, there was for the Jews gladness and joy, feasting and a holiday; and many among the peoples of the land became Jews, for the fear of the Jews had fallen upon them.
