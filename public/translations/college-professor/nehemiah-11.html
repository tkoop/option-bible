Resettlement of Jerusalem

1The leaders of the people settled in Jerusalem, while the remainder of the populace cast lots to bring one of every ten inhabitants into the holy city of Jerusalem, leaving the other nine shares to the towns. 2The people blessed all the men who volunteered to reside in Jerusalem.

Provincial Leaders and Their Lineages

3These are the provincial leaders who dwelt in Jerusalem; yet in the towns of Judah each man occupied his own hereditary holding—the Israelites, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants, and the descendants of Solomon’s servants. 4Among the descendants of Judah and Benjamin who settled in Jerusalem, the Judahite line comprised Ataiah son of Uzziah, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel, of the descendants of Perez. 5Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, the Shilonite. 6All the descendants of Perez who dwelt in Jerusalem numbered four hundred sixty-eight valiant men.

7These are the Benjaminites: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah. 8After him came Gabbai and Sallai, and their company numbered nine hundred twenty-eight. 9Joel son of Zichri served as overseer over them, while Judah son of Hassenuah served as second in charge of the city.

Priestly and Levitical Rosters

10Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib and Jachin. 11Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, was leader over the house of God. 12Their kinsmen who performed the temple service numbered eight hundred twenty-two; and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah. 13His kinsmen, heads of fathers’ houses, numbered two hundred forty-two; and Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahasai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer. 14Their kinsmen, mighty men of valourvalourvalor, numbered one hundred twenty-eight; and their overseer was Zabdiel son of Haggedolim.

15Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hashub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni. 16Shabbethai and Jozabad, among the chief Levites, had oversight of the exterior work of the house of God. 17Mattaniah son of Micha, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, was chief in beginning the thanksgiving in prayer; Bakbukiah was second among his kinsmen; and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun. 18All the Levites in the holy city numbered two hundred eighty-four. 19The gatekeepers—Akkub, Talmon, and their kinsmen who guarded the gates—numbered one hundred seventy-two.

20The rest of Israel, the priests, and the Levites were distributed throughout all the towns of Judah, each in his inheritance. 21The temple servants dwelt in Ophel, and Ziha and Gishpa were over the temple servants. 22The overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Micha; of the sons of Asaph, the singers were in charge of the work of the house of God. 23For the king’s commandment governed them, providing a daily allotment for the singers. 24Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, of the descendants of Zerah son of Judah, stood at the king’s hand in all matters concerning the people.

Provincial Settlements and Townships

25As for the villages in their fields, some of the descendants of Judah dwelt in Kiriath-arba and its daughter settlements, in Dibon and its daughter settlements, and in Jekabzeel and its villages. 26They also dwelt in Jeshua, Moladah, and Beth-pelet, 27in Hazar-shual and Beersheba with its daughter settlements, 28in Ziklag and Meconah with its daughter settlements, 29in En-rimmon, Zorah, and Jarmuth, 30in Zanoah, Adullam and its villages, Lachish and its fields, and Azekah with its daughter settlements; and they encamped from Beersheba as far as the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom. 31The Benjaminites dwelt in Geba, Michmash, Aija, and Bethel with its daughter settlements, 32in Anathoth, Nob, and Ananiah, 33in Hazor, Ramah, and Gittaim, 34in Hadid, Zeboim, and Neballat, 35and in Lod, Ono, and the ValleyvalleyValley of Craftsmen. 36Certain divisions of the Levites in Judah were assigned to Benjamin.
