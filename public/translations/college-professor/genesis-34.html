Dinah's Violation and Jacob's Restrained Response

1Now Dinah, the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to observe the young women of the land. 2Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite and a prince of that country, beheld her; seizing her, he lay with her and violated her.

3Yet his soul clung to Dinah, Jacob's daughter; he loved the maiden and spoke tenderly to her. 4Shechem said to his father Hamor, "Obtain this maiden for me as a wife."

5When Jacob learned that his daughter Dinah had been defiled, his sons were in the field with his livestock; Jacob remained silent until their return.

Shechem's Petition and the Deceitful Circumcision Bargain

6Hamor, Shechem's father, went out to Jacob to confer with him. 7When Jacob's sons returned from the field and heard of the matter, the men were grieved and exceedingly incensed, because Shechem had committed an outrage in Israel by lying with Jacob's daughter—a deed that should never be done.

8Hamor spoke with them: "My son Shechem's soul longs for your daughter; I entreat you to give her to him as a wife. 9Intermarry with us: give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for yourselves. 10Dwell with us; the land shall be before you; dwell and trade in it, and acquire property there."

11Shechem said to her father and her brothers, "Let me find favourfavourfavor in your eyes; whatever you say to me I will give. 12Name an exorbitant bride-price and gift, and I will pay as you specify; only give me the maiden as a wife."

13Jacob's sons answered Shechem and his father Hamor deceitfully, because he had defiled their sister Dinah. 14They said to them, "We cannot do this thing—giving our sister to an uncircumcised man—for it would be a disgrace to us. 15But on this condition we will consent to you: if you will be circumcised like us, every male among you. 16Then we will give our daughters to you, take your daughters for ourselves, dwell with you, and become one people. 17But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter and depart."

The Slaughter at Shechem and Jacob's Rebuke

18Their proposal pleased Hamor and his son Shechem. 19The young man did not delay in carrying out the matter, for he delighted in Jacob's daughter; indeed, he was the most honouredhonouredhonored in his father's household.

20Hamor and his son Shechem went to the gate of their city and spoke to its men: 21"These men are at peace with us; therefore let them dwell in the land and trade in it, for the land is spacious enough for them. Let us take their daughters as wives and give them our daughters. 22Only on this condition will these men consent to dwell with us and become one people: every male among us must be circumcised, as they are circumcised. 23Will not their livestock, their property, and all their animals become ours? Let us only agree to this, and they will dwell with us."

24All who went out at the city gate listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every male was circumcised. 25On the third day, when they were still in pain, two of Jacob's sons—Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers—each took his sword, came upon the city unawares, and killed every male. 26They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the sword, took Dinah from Shechem's house, and departed. 27Jacob's sons came upon the slain and plundered the city, because its men had defiled their sister. 28They seized their flocks, herds, donkeys, and everything in the city and in the field. 29They took captive all their wealth, their children, and their wives, plundering everything in the houses.

30Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "YouyouYou have brought trouble upon me, making me odious among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. Since I am few in number, they will gather against me and strike me down, and I and my household will be destroyed." 31They replied, "Should he have treated our sister as though she were a prostitute?"
