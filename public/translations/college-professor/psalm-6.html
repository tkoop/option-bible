A Penitential Supplication for Healing and Deliverance

1To the choirmaster, with stringed instruments, upon the octave—a psalm of David. the LORD, do not reprove me in YouryourYour indignation, nor chasten me in YouryourYour fury. 2Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am languishing; heal me, the LORD, for my bones are convulsed with anguish. 3My soul is likewise seized with profound terror; but YouyouYou, the LORD—how long? 4Return, the LORD, and rescue my life; deliver me for the sake of YouryourYour loving devotion.

5For in death there is no remembrance of YouyouYou; who can give YouyouYou thanks from Sheol? 6I have grown weary with my groaning; every night I flood my bed with tears, and I drench my couch with weeping. 7My eye has grown dim with sorrow; it has wasted away because of all my adversaries.

8Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity, for the LORD has heard the voice of my weeping. 9The LORD has heard my supplication; the LORD receives my prayer. 10All my enemies shall be put to shame and sorely dismayed; they shall turn back and be confounded in a moment.
