A Psalm of David: The LORD's Compassionate Mercy and Universal Sovereignty

1Bless the LORD, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless HishisHis holy name. 2Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget any of HishisHis benefits— 3HeheHe who pardons all your iniquity and heals all your diseases, 4who redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with steadfast love and mercy, 5who satiates your desires with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's.

6The LORD executes righteousness and justice for all who are oppressed. 7He disclosed HishisHis ways to Moses, HishisHis deeds to the children of Israel. 8The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love. 9He will not perpetually contend, nor will HeheHe retain HishisHis anger forever. 10He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our iniquities.

11For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is HishisHis steadfast love toward those who fear HimhimHim; 12as far as the east is from the west, so far has HeheHe removed our transgressions from us. 13As a father shows compassion to his children, so the LORD shows compassion to those who fear HimhimHim. 14For HeheHe knows our frame; HeheHe remembers that we are but dust.

15As for mortals, their days are like grass; they flourish like a flower of the field; 16the wind passes over it, and it is gone, and its place acknowledges it no more. 17But the steadfast love of the LORD endures from everlasting to everlasting upon those who fear HimhimHim, and HishisHis righteousness unto children's children, 18to those who keep HishisHis covenant and remember to fulfillfulfilfulfill HishisHis commandments.

19The LORD has established HishisHis throne in the heavens, and HishisHis kingdom governs all. 20Bless the LORD, you HishisHis angels, mighty in strength, who do HishisHis word, obeying the voice of HishisHis word. 21Bless the LORD, all HishisHis hosts, HishisHis ministers who do HishisHis will. 22Bless the LORD, all HishisHis works, in all places of HishisHis dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul!
