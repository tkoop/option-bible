Paul’s Proclamation in Corinth

1When I came to you, brothers, I did not come proclaiming to you the testimony of God with any rhetorical superiority or wisdom, 2for I had resolved to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and HimhimHim crucified. 3I was present with you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling, 4and my discourse and my proclamation were not in persuasive words of wisdom, but in a demonstration of the SpiritspiritSpirit and of power, 5so that your faith might not rest upon human wisdom but upon the power of God.

The Hidden Wisdom of God

6Yet among the mature we do speak wisdom—not the wisdom of this age, nor that of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing— 7but we speak, in a mystery, the hidden wisdom of God, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory. 8None of the rulers of this age has known it; for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. 9But, as it is written, “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor has entered into the heart of man—what God has prepared for those who love HimhimHim.”

Revelation Through the Spirit

10For to us God has revealed them through the SpiritspiritSpirit, for the SpiritspiritSpirit searches all things, even the depths of God. 11For who among men knows the things of a man except the spirit of that man which is in him? Even so no one knows the things of God except the SpiritspiritSpirit of God. 12Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the SpiritspiritSpirit that is from God, that we might know the things freely given to us by God. 13And the things we speak we do not utter in words taught by human wisdom, but in words taught by the SpiritspiritSpirit, comparing spiritual realities with spiritual discernment.

Spiritual Discernment

14But the natural man does not accept the things of the SpiritspiritSpirit of God, for to him they are folly; neither can he know them, because they are spiritually appraised. 15But the spiritual person appraises all things, while he himself is appraised by no one. 16For “Who has known the mind of the Lord, that he should instruct HimhimHim?” But we have the mind of Christ.
