The Consecration of Aaron and His Sons

1The LORD said to Moses, 2“Take Aaron and his sons, together with their garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread, 3and assemble the entire congregation at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting.” 4Moses did as the LORD commanded, and the congregation was assembled at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 5Moses said to them, “This is what the LORD has commanded to be done.” 6Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water. 7He clothed Aaron with the tunic, girded him with the sash, invested him with the robe, and placed the ephod upon him; then he girded him with the ephod’s skillfully woven waistband and fastened it. 8He set the breastpiece upon him and inserted the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece. 9He placed the turban on Aaron’s head and affixed the golden plate—the holy crown—on the front of the turban, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 10Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and all that was in it, thereby consecrating them. 11He sprinkled some of the oil on the altar seven times, anointing the altar and all its utensils, together with the basin and its stand, to sanctify them. 12He poured anointing oil upon Aaron’s head and anointed him, setting him apart as holy. 13Moses then brought forward Aaron’s sons, clothed them with tunics, girded them with sashes, and bound headbands upon them, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Sin Offering and Burnt Offering

14He brought forward the bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 15Moses slaughtered it, took some of the blood, and with his finger applied it to the horns of the altar on every side, purifying the altar; he poured the remaining blood at the base of the altar and consecrated it, making atonement for it. 16Moses also took all the fat that enveloped the entrails, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and burned them on the altar. 17The bull—its hide, flesh, and offal—he burned outside the camp, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 18He presented the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 19Moses slaughtered the ram and dashed the blood against the altar on every side. 20He dismembered the ram and burned the head, the pieces, and the fat. 21After washing the entrails and the legs with water, he burned the entire ram on the altar as a burnt offering—a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD—just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Ordination Offering and Priestly Charge

22He presented the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 23Moses slaughtered it, took some of its blood, and put it on the lobe of Aaron’s right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot. 24He brought forward Aaron’s sons as well and put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the great toes of their right feet; then he dashed the rest of the blood against the altar on every side. 25He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat covering the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh. 26From the basket of unleavened bread that was before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer, and he placed them upon the fat portions and the right thigh. 27He put all these into the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved them as a wave offering before the LORD. 28Moses then took them from their hands and burned them on the altar atop the burnt offering; they were ordination offerings, a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD. 29He also took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD. It became Moses’ portion from the ram of ordination, as the LORD had commanded Moses. 30Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood from the altar and sprinkled them on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and their garments; thus he consecrated Aaron and his garments, together with his sons and their garments. 31Moses said to Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and eat it there with the bread that is in the basket of ordination offerings, as I commanded when I said, ‘Aaron and his sons shall eat it.’ 32Then burn the remainder of the meat and the bread. 33You must not leave the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting for seven days, until the days of your ordination are completed; for it will take seven days to ordain you. 34What has been done today was commanded by the LORD to make atonement for you. 35You must remain at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting day and night for seven days and keep the LORD’s charge, so that you will not die; for this is what I have been commanded.” 36So Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD had commanded through Moses.
