David’s Song of Deliverance

1David spoke these words to the LORD in song on the day the LORD delivered him from the grasp of all his enemies and from Saul’s hand. 2“The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. 3God is my rock, in whom I take refuge; HeheHe is my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold and my refuge, my SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, who saves me from violence. 4I call upon the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

Delivered from the Waters of Death

5“For the breakers of death surrounded me; the torrents of destruction terrified me. 6The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me. 7In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried out to my God, and from HishisHis temple HeheHe heard my voice; my cry came into HishisHis ears.

8“Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of the heavens quaked and were shaken, because HeheHe was angry. 9Smoke ascended from HishisHis nostrils, and devouring fire came from HishisHis mouth; coals were kindled by it. 10He bowed the heavens and descended, with thick darkness beneath HishisHis feet. 11He rode upon a cherub and flew; HeheHe was seen upon the wings of the wind. 12He made darkness HishisHis canopy around HimhimHim—dark waters and thick clouds of the skies. 13From the brightness before HimhimHim coals of fire burned. 14The LORD thundered from Heavenheavenheaven; the MostmostMost HighhighHigh uttered HishisHis voice. 15He shot forth arrows and scattered them; HeheHe flashed lightning and routed them. 16Then the channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of HishisHis nostrils.

17“He sent from on high and took me; HeheHe drew me out of many waters. 18He delivered me from my strong enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me. 19They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the LORD was my support. 20He brought me out into a broad place; HeheHe rescued me because HeheHe delighted in me.

Righteousness, Recompense, and Strength

21“The LORD has dealt with me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands HeheHe has repaid me. 22For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. 23For all HishisHis ordinances are before me, and from HishisHis statutes I have not turned aside. 24I have been blameless before HimhimHim, and I have kept myself from my iniquity. 25Therefore the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in HishisHis sight.

32“For who is God besides the LORD? And who is a rock besides our God? 33God is my strength and fortress, and HeheHe makes my way perfect. 34He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me upon my high places. 35He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 36You have also given me the shield of YouryourYour salvation, and YouryourYour gentleness has made me great. 37You enlarge my steps beneath me, so that my feet do not slip.

Triumph, Dominion, and Everlasting Praise

44“You have delivered me from the strivings of my people; YouyouYou have kept me as head of the nations; a people I did not know serves me. 45Foreigners cringe before me; as soon as they hear of me, they obey me. 46Foreigners fade away and come trembling from their strongholds.

47“The LORD lives, and blessed be my rock; and exalted be God, the rock of my salvation— 48the God who gives me vengeance and brings down peoples under me, 49who brings me out from my enemies. You exalt me above those who rise against me; YouyouYou deliver me from the violent man. 50Therefore I will give thanks to YouyouYou, O the LORD, among the nations, and I will sing praises to YouryourYour name. 51He is a tower of salvation to HishisHis king, and shows steadfast love to HishisHis anointed, to David and to his offspring forever.”
