Amnon's Infatuation with Tamar

1After these events, David's son Amnon conceived an ardent infatuation with Tamar, the daughter of David and sister of Absalom; she was exceedingly beautiful. 2Amnon became so distressed and physically debilitated on account of his sister Tamar that he fell ill, for she was a virgin, and it seemed to him an impossibility that he should have his way with her.

3Now Amnon had a friend, Jonadab son of Shimeah, David's brother; Jonadab was an exceedingly astute man. 4He said to him, “Why do you, the king's son, grow so gaunt morning after morning? Will you not confide in me?” Amnon replied, “I am in love with Tamar, the sister of my brother Absalom.”

5Jonadab said to him, “Lie down upon your bed and feign illness. When your father comes to see you, say to him, ‘Please let my sister Tamar come and prepare food before me, that I may watch and eat from her hand.’” 6So Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill. When the king came to see him, Amnon said to the king, “Please let my sister Tamar come and make two cakes in my sight, that I may eat from her hand.”

The Violation of Tamar

7David sent to Tamar at the house, saying, “Go now to your brother Amnon's house and prepare food for him.” 8Tamar went to the house of her brother Amnon, who was lying down. She took dough, kneaded it, made cakes in his sight, and cooked them. 9She took the pan and poured them out before him, but he refused to eat. Amnon said, “Send everyone away from me.” So they all went out. 10Amnon said to Tamar, “Bring the food into the chamber, that I may eat from your hand.” Tamar took the cakes she had made and brought them into the chamber to Amnon. 11As she brought them near for him to eat, he seized her and said, “Come, lie with me, my sister.”

12She said to him, “No, my brother; do not violate me, for such a thing is not done in Israel. Do not commit this outrage.” 13“Where should I carry my shame? And you would be counted among the fools in Israel. Now, I pray you, speak to the king, for he will not deny me to you.”

14But he would not listen to her. He was stronger than she was, and he violated her and lay with her. 15Then Amnon hated her with a very great hatred; indeed, the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her. Amnon said to her, “Get up and be gone.”

16She said to him, “No, for sending me away would be a greater evil than the other you did to me.” But he would not listen to her.

17He called the young man who served him and said, “Send this woman away from me and bolt the door after her.” 18His servant brought her out and locked the door after her. She was wearing a robe of many colourscolourscolors, for such were the garments of the king's virgin daughters. 19Tamar put ashes on her head, tore the robe she was wearing, laid her hand upon her head, and went away crying aloud as she went.

20Her brother Absalom said to her, “Has Amnon your brother been with you? Now be silent, my sister; he is your brother. Do not take this to heart.” So Tamar remained desolate in her brother Absalom's house. 21When King David heard of all these things, he was very angry. 22Absalom spoke to Amnon neither good nor bad, for Absalom hated Amnon because he had violated his sister Tamar.

Absalom's Retribution and Flight

23After two full years, Absalom had sheepshearers at Baal-hazor, which is near Ephraim, and Absalom invited all the king's sons. 24Absalom came to the king and said, “See now, your servant is shearing sheep; I pray that the king and his servants may go with your servant.”

25The king said to Absalom, “No, my son, let us not all go, lest we be a burden to you.” He urged him, but he would not go; instead, he blessed him.

Absalom said, “If not, then please let my brother Amnon go with us.”

The king said to him, “Why should he go with you?” 27But Absalom pressed him, and he sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him.

28Absalom commanded his servants, saying, “Watch now: when Amnon's heart is merry with wine and I say to you, ‘Strike Amnon and kill him,’ do not be afraid. Have I not commanded you? Be strong and valiant.” 29The servants of Absalom did to Amnon as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king's sons arose, and each mounted his mule and fled.

30While they were on the way, the report came to David, saying, “Absalom has struck down all the king's sons, and not one of them is left.” 31The king arose, tore his garments, and lay on the ground; all his servants stood by with their garments torn. 32Jonadab son of Shimeah, David's brother, answered and said, “Let not my lord say, ‘They have killed all the young men, the king's sons,’ for only Amnon is dead. This has been determined by Absalom from the day he violated his sister Tamar.” 33“Now therefore let not my lord the king take this to heart, to say, ‘All the king's sons are dead,’ for only Amnon is dead.”

34Absalom fled. The young man, the watchman, lifted his eyes and saw, and behold, many people were coming along the road behind him on the side of the hill. 35Jonadab said to the king, “Behold, the king's sons are coming; as your servant said, so it is.” 36As soon as he finished speaking, behold, the king's sons came and lifted up their voices and wept; the king and all his servants wept exceedingly. 37Absalom fled and went to Talmai son of Ammihur, king of Geshur. David mourned for his son all his days. 38Absalom fled and went to Geshur, and remained there three years. 39King David longed to go to Absalom, for he had been consoled over Amnon's death.
