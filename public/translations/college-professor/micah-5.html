The Siege and the Ruler from Bethlehem

1Now marshal your troops, O daughter of troops, for a siege has been laid against us; with a rod they will strike the cheek of Israel's judge. 2But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are small among the thousands of Judah, out of you shall come forth for MemeMe OneoneOne to be ruler in Israel, whose origins are from of old, from days of eternity.

3Therefore HeheHe will abandon them until the woman in labourlabourlabor has given birth; then the rest of HishisHis brothers will return to the children of Israel. 4And HeheHe will stand and shepherd HishisHis flock in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD HishisHis God; and they will dwell, for then HishisHis greatness will reach the ends of the earth.

5And HeheHe will be our peace. When Assyria comes into our land and treads upon our palaces, we will raise up against him seven shepherds and eight princes of men. 6They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod at its gates; and HeheHe will deliver us when Assyria comes into our land and treads within our borders.

The Remnant among the Nations

7The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers upon the grass, which neither waits for man nor tarries for the sons of men. 8The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, which tramples and tears as it passes through, with no one to rescue. 9your hand will be lifted up against your adversaries, and all your enemies will be cut off.

The LORD's Purification of His People

10And it shall be in that day, declares the LORD, that I will cut off your horses from your midst and destroy your chariots. 11And I will cut off the cities of your land and tear down all your fortresses. 12And I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you will have no soothsayers.

13And I will cut off your carved images and your pillars from your midst, and you shall no longer bow down to the work of your hands. 14And I will uproot your Asherah poles from your midst and destroy your cities. 15And I will execute vengeance in anger and wrath upon the nations that did not listen.
