A Professorial Meditation upon the Supremacy of Divine Instruction

1How blessed are those whose path remains immaculate, who walk in the instruction of the LORD! 2How blessed are those who preserve HishisHis testimonies and seek HimhimHim with an undivided heart, 3who perpetrate no iniquity but walk in HishisHis ways. 4You have ordained YouryourYour precepts for scrupulous observance. 5O that my ways might be established so as to keep YouryourYour statutes! 6Then shall I not be confounded when I regard all YouryourYour commandments. 7I will praise YouyouYou with an upright heart, as I learn YouryourYour righteous ordinances. 8I will keep YouryourYour statutes; abandon me not utterly.

9By what means may a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to YouryourYour word. 10With my whole heart I seek YouyouYou; let me not wander from YouryourYour commandments. 11I have treasured YouryourYour word in my heart, that I might not sin against YouyouYou. 12Blessed are YouyouYou, O the LORD; teach me YouryourYour statutes. 13With my lips I have declared all the ordinances of YouryourYour mouth. 14In the way of YouryourYour testimonies I delight as much as in all riches. 15I will meditate upon YouryourYour precepts and fix my eyes upon YouryourYour ways. 16I will delight in YouryourYour statutes; I will not forget YouryourYour word.

17Deal bountifully with YouryourYour servant, that I may live and keep YouryourYour word. 18Open my eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of YouryourYour law. 19I am a sojourner upon the earth; hide not YouryourYour commandments from me. 20My soul is consumed with longing for YouryourYour ordinances at all times. 21You rebuke the insolent, the accursed, who wander from YouryourYour commandments. 22Remove from me scorn and contempt, for I have kept YouryourYour testimonies. 23Even though princes sit and conspire against me, YouryourYour servant will meditate upon YouryourYour statutes. 24Your testimonies are my delight; they are my counselors.

25My soul cleaves to the dust; give me life according to YouryourYour word. 26When I recounted my ways, YouyouYou answered me; teach me YouryourYour statutes. 27Make me understand the way of YouryourYour precepts, and I will meditate upon YouryourYour wondrous works. 28My soul melts away for sorrow; strengthen me according to YouryourYour word. 29Put false ways far from me and graciously teach me YouryourYour law. 30I have chosen the way of faithfulness; I set YouryourYour ordinances before me. 31I cling to YouryourYour testimonies, O the LORD; let me not be put to shame. 32I will run in the way of YouryourYour commandments when YouyouYou enlarge my heart.

33Teach me, O the LORD, the way of YouryourYour statutes, and I will keep it to the end. 34Give me understanding, that I may keep YouryourYour law and observe it with my whole heart. 35Lead me in the path of YouryourYour commandments, for I delight in it. 36Incline my heart to YouryourYour testimonies, and not to selfish gain. 37Turn my eyes from looking at worthless things, and give me life in YouryourYour ways. 38Confirm to YouryourYour servant YouryourYour promise, that YouyouYou may be feared. 39Turn away the reproach that I dread, for YouryourYour ordinances are good. 40Behold, I long for YouryourYour precepts; in YouryourYour righteousness give me life.

41Let YouryourYour steadfast love come to me, O the LORD, YouryourYour salvation according to YouryourYour promise. 42Then shall I have an answer for him who taunts me, for I trust in YouryourYour word. 43And take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth, for my hope is in YouryourYour ordinances. 44I will keep YouryourYour law continually, forever and ever. 45And I shall walk in a wide place, for I have sought YouryourYour precepts. 46I will also speak of YouryourYour testimonies before kings and shall not be put to shame. 47For I find my delight in YouryourYour commandments, which I love. 48I will lift up my hands toward YouryourYour commandments, which I love, and I will meditate upon YouryourYour statutes.

49Remember YouryourYour word to YouryourYour servant, in which YouyouYou have made me hope. 50This is my comfort in my affliction, that YouryourYour promise gives me life. 51The insolent utterly deride me, but I do not turn away from YouryourYour law. 52When I think of YouryourYour ordinances from of old, I take comfort, O the LORD. 53Hot indignation seizes me because of the wicked, who forsake YouryourYour law. 54Your statutes have been my songs in the house of my sojourning. 55I remember YouryourYour name in the night, O the LORD, and keep YouryourYour law. 56This blessing has fallen to me, that I have kept YouryourYour precepts.

57The LORD is my portion; I promise to keep YouryourYour words. 58I entreat YouryourYour favourfavourfavor with all my heart; be gracious to me according to YouryourYour promise. 59When I think on my ways, I turn my feet to YouryourYour testimonies. 60I hasten and do not delay to keep YouryourYour commandments. 61Though the cords of the wicked ensnare me, I do not forget YouryourYour law. 62At midnight I rise to praise YouyouYou, because of YouryourYour righteous ordinances. 63I am a companion of all who fear YouyouYou, of those who keep YouryourYour precepts. 64The EarthEarthearth, O the LORD, is full of YouryourYour steadfast love; teach me YouryourYour statutes.

65You have dealt well with YouryourYour servant, O the LORD, according to YouryourYour word. 66Teach me good judgmentjudgementjudgment and knowledge, for I believe in YouryourYour commandments. 67Before I was afflicted I went astray, but now I keep YouryourYour word. 68You are good and do good; teach me YouryourYour statutes. 69The insolent smear me with lies, but with my whole heart I keep YouryourYour precepts. 70Their heart is unfeeling like fat, but I delight in YouryourYour law. 71It is good for me that I was afflicted, that I might learn YouryourYour statutes. 72The law of YouryourYour mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver pieces.

73Your hands have made and fashioned me; give me understanding that I may learn YouryourYour commandments. 74Those who fear YouyouYou shall see me and rejoice, because I have hoped in YouryourYour word. 75I know, O the LORD, that YouryourYour ordinances are righteous, and that in faithfulness YouyouYou have afflicted me. 76Let YouryourYour steadfast love comfort me according to YouryourYour promise to YouryourYour servant. 77Let YouryourYour mercy come to me, that I may live; for YouryourYour law is my delight. 78Let the insolent be put to shame, because they have wronged me with falsehood; as for me, I will meditate upon YouryourYour precepts. 79Let those who fear YouyouYou turn to me, that they may know YouryourYour testimonies. 80May my heart be blameless in YouryourYour statutes, that I may not be put to shame.

81My soul longs for YouryourYour salvation; I hope in YouryourYour word. 82My eyes long for YouryourYour promise; I ask, "When will YouyouYou comfort me?" 83For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke, yet I have not forgotten YouryourYour statutes. 84How many days must YouryourYour servant endure? When will YouyouYou judge those who persecute me? 85The insolent have dug pitfalls for me; they do not live according to YouryourYour law. 86All YouryourYour commandments are sure; they persecute me with falsehood; help me! 87They have almost made an end of me on EarthEarthearth, but I have not forsaken YouryourYour precepts. 88In YouryourYour steadfast love give me life, that I may keep the testimonies of YouryourYour mouth.

89Forever, O the LORD, YouryourYour word is firmly fixed in the heavens. 90Your faithfulness endures to all generations; YouyouYou have established the earth, and it stands fast. 91By YouryourYour ordinances they stand this day, for all things are YouryourYour servants. 92If YouryourYour law had not been my delight, I would have perished in my affliction. 93I will never forget YouryourYour precepts, for by them YouyouYou have given me life. 94I am YoursyoursYours; save me, for I have sought YouryourYour precepts. 95The wicked lie in wait to destroy me, but I consider YouryourYour testimonies. 96I have seen a limit to all perfection, but YouryourYour commandment is exceedingly broad.

97Oh how I love YouryourYour law! It is my meditation all the day. 98Your commandment makes me wiser than my enemies, for it is ever with me. 99I have more understanding than all my teachers, for YouryourYour testimonies are my meditation. 100I understand more than the aged, for I keep YouryourYour precepts. 101I hold back my feet from every evil way, in order to keep YouryourYour word. 102I do not turn aside from YouryourYour ordinances, for YouyouYou have taught me. 103How sweet are YouryourYour words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth! 104Through YouryourYour precepts I get understanding; therefore I hate every false way.

105Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. 106I have sworn and confirmed that I will keep YouryourYour righteous ordinances. 107I am severely afflicted; give me life, O the LORD, according to YouryourYour word. 108Accept my freewill offerings of praise, O the LORD, and teach me YouryourYour ordinances. 109I hold my life in my hand continually, but I do not forget YouryourYour law. 110The wicked have laid a snare for me, but I do not stray from YouryourYour precepts. 111Your testimonies are my heritage forever, for they are the joy of my heart. 112I incline my heart to perform YouryourYour statutes forever, to the end.

113I hate the double-minded, but I love YouryourYour law. 114You are my hiding place and my shield; I hope in YouryourYour word. 115Depart from me, you evildoers, that I may keep the commandments of my God. 116Uphold me according to YouryourYour promise, that I may live, and let me not be put to shame in my hope. 117Hold me up, that I may be safe and have regard for YouryourYour statutes continually. 118You spurn all who go astray from YouryourYour statutes, for their cunning is in vain. 119All the wicked of the earth YouyouYou discard like dross, therefore I love YouryourYour testimonies. 120My flesh trembles for fear of YouyouYou, and I am afraid of YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments.

121I have done what is just and right; do not leave me to my oppressors. 122Give YouryourYour servant a pledge of good; let not the insolent oppress me. 123My eyes long for YouryourYour salvation and for the fulfillmentfulfilmentfulfillment of YouryourYour righteous promise. 124Deal with YouryourYour servant according to YouryourYour steadfast love, and teach me YouryourYour statutes. 125I am YouryourYour servant; give me understanding, that I may know YouryourYour testimonies. 126It is time for the LORD to act, for YouryourYour law has been broken. 127Therefore I love YouryourYour commandments more than gold, even fine gold. 128Therefore I consider all YouryourYour precepts to be right; I hate every false way.

129Your testimonies are wonderful; therefore my soul keeps them. 130The unfolding of YouryourYour words gives light; it imparts understanding to the simple. 131I open my mouth and pant, because I long for YouryourYour commandments. 132Turn to me and be gracious to me, as is YouryourYour way with those who love YouryourYour name. 133Keep steady my steps according to YouryourYour promise, and let no iniquity get dominion over me. 134Redeem me from man's oppression, that I may keep YouryourYour precepts. 135Make YouryourYour face shine upon YouryourYour servant, and teach me YouryourYour statutes. 136My eyes shed streams of tears, because people do not keep YouryourYour law.
