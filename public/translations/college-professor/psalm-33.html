An Exhortation to Exultant Praise

1Exult in the LORD, O righteous; praise is comely for the upright. 2Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre; make melody to HimhimHim with the ten-stringed harp. 3Sing to HimhimHim a new song; play skillfully with a shout of triumph.

The Creative Efficacy of the Divine Word

4For the word of the LORD is upright, and all HishisHis work is done in faithfulness. 5He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the LORD's steadfast love. 6By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and by the breath of HishisHis mouth all their host. 7He gathers the waters of the sea as a heap; HeheHe puts the deeps in storehouses. 8Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of HimhimHim. 9For HeheHe spoke, and it came to be; HeheHe commanded, and it stood firm.

The Futility of Human Counsel and the Permanence of Divine Purpose

10The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nothing; HeheHe frustrates the thoughts of the peoples. 11The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the thoughts of HishisHis heart to all generations. 12Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people HeheHe has chosen as HishisHis heritage.

The LORD's Omniscient Regard and the Hope of the Faithful

13The LORD looks down from Heavenheavenheaven; HeheHe sees all the children of man. 14From HishisHis dwelling place HeheHe looks out on all the inhabitants of the earth— 15HeheHe who fashions the hearts of them all and observes all their deeds. 16The king is not saved by his great army; a warrior is not delivered by his great strength. 17The horse is a false hope for safety, and by its great might it cannot rescue. 18Behold, the eye of the LORD is on those who fear HimhimHim, on those who hope in HishisHis steadfast love, 19that HeheHe may deliver their souls from death and keep them alive in famine. 20Our souls wait for the LORD; HeheHe is our help and our shield. 21For our hearts rejoice in HimhimHim, because we trust in HishisHis holy name. 22Let YouryourYour steadfast love, O the LORD, be upon us, as we hope in YouyouYou.
