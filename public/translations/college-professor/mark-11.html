The Triumphal Entry into Jerusalem

1When they drew near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives, HeheHe sent two of HishisHis disciples, 2and said to them, “Go into the village opposite you, and immediately, as you enter it, you will find a colt tied, on which no one has yet sat; untie it and bring it. 3And if anyone says to you, ‘Why are you doing this?’ say, ‘The Lord has need of it and sends it back here immediately.’”

4And they went away and found a colt tied outside at the door, by the street, and they untied it. 5And some of those standing there were saying to them, “Why are you untying the colt?” 6And they told them just as Jesus had said, and they let them go.

7And they brought the colt to Jesus, and they threw their garments upon it, and HeheHe sat upon it. 8And many spread their garments on the road, while others were cutting leafy branches from the fields.

9And those going before and those following were crying out, “Hosanna! Blessed is HeheHe who comes in the name of the Lord! 10Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David! Hosanna in the highest!”

11And HeheHe entered Jerusalem and the temple; and after looking around at everything, since the hour was already late, HeheHe went out to Bethany with the TwelvetwelveTwelve.

The Withered Fig Tree and the Cleansed Temple

12And the next day, when they had gone out from Bethany, HeheHe was hungry. 13And seeing from afar a fig tree in leaf, HeheHe went to see whether HeheHe might find anything on it; and when HeheHe came to it, HeheHe found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs. 14And HeheHe answered and said to it, “May no one ever eat fruit from you again.” And HishisHis disciples were listening.

15And they came to Jerusalem. And having entered the temple, HeheHe began to cast out those who were buying and selling in the temple, and HeheHe overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those who sold doves. 16And HeheHe would not allow anyone to carry a vessel through the temple. 17And HeheHe was teaching them and saying, “Is it not written, ‘My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations’? But you have made it a den of robbers.”

18And the chief priests and the scribes heard it and were seeking how they might destroy HimhimHim; for they were afraid of HimhimHim, because all the crowd was astonished at HishisHis teaching. 19And whenever evening came, they went out of the city.

20And as they passed by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots. 21And Peter, remembering, said to HimhimHim, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree that you cursed has withered.”

Teaching on Faith, Prayer, and Forgiveness

22“Have faith in God.” 23And Jesus, answering, said to them, “Truly I say to you, whoever says to this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ and does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will come to pass, it will be granted to him. 24Therefore I say to you, all things for which you pray and ask, believe that you have received them, and they will be yours. 25And whenever you stand praying, if you have anything against anyone, forgive it, so that your FatherfatherFather who is in Heavenheavenheaven may also forgive you your trespasses.”

The Challenge to Jesus’ Authority

27And they came again to Jerusalem. And as HeheHe was walking in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders came to HimhimHim, 28and they were saying to HimhimHim, “By what authority are you doing these things, or who gave you this authority, that you should do these things?”

29And Jesus said to them, “I will ask you one question. Answer MemeMe, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things. 30The baptism of John—was it from Heavenheavenheaven or from men? Answer MemeMe.”

31And they reasoned among themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From Heavenheavenheaven,’ HeheHe will say, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’ 32But if we say, ‘From men’—they were afraid of the crowd, for everyone held John to be truly a prophet.

33And answering Jesus, they say, “We do not know.” And Jesus says to them, “Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.”
