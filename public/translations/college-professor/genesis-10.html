The Genealogical Record of Noah and His Descendants

1These, then, constitute the genealogical record of Noah—namely, Shem, Ham, and Japheth—and sons were born to them after the deluge. 2The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 3The sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 4The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. 5From these the maritime peoples dispersed into their territories, each according to its language, by their clans, within their nations.

The Descendants of Ham and the Rise of Nimrod’s Kingdoms

6The sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 7The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca; the sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan. 8Cush fathered Nimrod, who first became a mighty man upon the earth. 9He was a mighty hunter before the LORD; therefore the proverb arose, "Like Nimrod—a mighty hunter before the LORD." 10The inception of his dominion comprised Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, situated in the land of Shinar. 11From that land he went forth into Assyria and built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, and Calah, 12and Resen, which lies between Nineveh and the great city of Calah.

13Mizraim fathered the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, and the Naphtuhim, 14the Pathrusim, the Casluhim—from whom the Philistines emerged—and the Caphtorim. 15Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth, 16and the Jebusites, the Amorites, and the Girgashites, 17the Hivites, the Arkites, and the Sinites, 18the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites; and afterward the clans of the Canaanites were dispersed. 19The Canaanite border extended from Sidon toward Gerar, as far as Gaza, and toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha. 20These are the sons of Ham, according to their clans, by their languages, within their lands and nations.

The Descendants of Shem and the Dispersion of Peoples

21Sons were likewise born to Shem, the brother of Japheth the elder; Shem was the father of all the descendants of Eber. 22The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram. 23The sons of Aram were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. 24Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber. 25To Eber were born two sons: one was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided; and his brother was named Joktan. 26Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah, 27Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah, 28Obal, Abimael, and Sheba, 29Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab; all these were the sons of Joktan. 30Their settlement extended from Mesha toward Sephar, into the eastern hill country. 31These are the sons of Shem, according to their clans, by their languages, within their lands and nations. 32These are the clans of the sons of Noah, according to their generations, in their nations; and from these the nations were dispersed over the earth after the flood.
