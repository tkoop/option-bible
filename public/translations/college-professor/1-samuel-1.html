Elkanah and His Household

1Now there was a certain man from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim, whose name was Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Elihu, son of Tohu, son of Zuph, an Ephraimite. 2He had two wives: one was named Hannah, and the other Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none. 3This man went up from his city year by year to worship and to sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts in Shiloh, where Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests to the LORD.

4On the day when Elkanah sacrificed, he would give portions to Peninnah his wife and to all her sons and daughters; 5but to Hannah he gave a double portion, because he loved Hannah, although the LORD had closed her womb. 6Her rival, moreover, provoked her bitterly, seeking to vex her, because the LORD had closed her womb. 7And so it was year after year: whenever Hannah went up to the house of the LORD, Peninnah provoked her, so that she wept and would not eat.

8Then Elkanah her husband said to her, “Hannah, why do you weep? Why do you not eat? Why is your heart so downcast? Am I not better to you than ten sons?”

Hannah’s Prayer and Vow at Shiloh

9After they had eaten and drunk in Shiloh, Hannah rose up. Now Eli the priest was sitting on a seat by the doorpost of the temple of the LORD. 10In bitterness of soul she prayed to the LORD and wept bitterly.

11And she vowed a vow, saying, “O the LORD of Hosts, if YouyouYou will indeed look upon the affliction of YouryourYour maidservant and remember me, and not forget YouryourYour maidservant, but give to YouryourYour maidservant a male offspring, then I will give him to the LORD all the days of his life, and no razor shall come upon his head.”

12As she continued praying before the LORD, Eli watched her mouth. 13Hannah was speaking in her heart; only her lips moved, but her voice was not heard, so Eli supposed she was drunk.

14Then Eli said to her, “How long will you be drunk? Put away your wine from you.”

15Hannah answered, “No, my lord; I am a woman troubled in spirit. I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but I have poured out my soul before the LORD. 16Do not regard your maidservant as a worthless woman, for I have been speaking out of the abundance of my complaint and my vexation.”

17Then Eli answered, “Go in peace, and may the God of Israel grant your petition that you have asked of HimhimHim.”

18She said, “Let your maidservant find favourfavourfavor in your eyes.” Then the woman went her way and ate, and her countenance was no longer downcast.

The Birth and Dedication of Samuel

19They rose early in the morning and worshiped before the LORD, then returned and came to their house at Ramah. Elkanah lay with his wife Hannah, and the LORD remembered her. 20In the course of time Hannah conceived and bore a son, and she called his name Samuel, saying, “Because I have asked him of the LORD.” 21The man Elkanah went up with all his household to offer to the LORD the annual sacrifice and his vow.

22But Hannah did not go up, for she said to her husband, “Not until the child is weaned will I bring him, that he may appear before the LORD and remain there forever.”

23Elkanah her husband said to her, “Do what seems good to you; stay until you have weaned him; only may the LORD establish HishisHis word.” So the woman stayed and nursed her son until she weaned him.

24When she had weaned him, she took him up with her, along with three bulls, an ephah of flour, and a skin of wine, and brought him to the house of the LORD in Shiloh. The child was young. 25They slaughtered the bull and brought the child to Eli.

26She said, “Oh, my lord, as surely as you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here by you, praying to the LORD. 27For this child I prayed, and the LORD has granted me my petition that I asked of HimhimHim. 28Therefore I have dedicated him to the LORD; as long as he lives, he is dedicated to the LORD.” And he worshiped the LORD there.
