The Sapphire Throne and the Command to Scatter Fire

1Then I looked, and behold, above the expanse over the heads of the cherubim there appeared something like a sapphire stone, in appearance resembling a throne above them. 2And HeheHe said to the man clothed in linen, “Go in among the wheels beneath the cherub, and fill your hands with burning coals from among the cherubim, and scatter them over the city.” And he went in as I watched.

3Now the cherubim were standing on the south side of the temple when the man entered, and a cloud filled the inner court. 4Then the glory of the the LORD rose from above the cherub to the threshold of the temple. The temple was filled with the cloud, and the court was filled with the radiance of the glory of the the LORD. 5The sound of the cherubim’s wings could be heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty when HeheHe speaks.

6When HeheHe commanded the man clothed in linen, saying, “Take fire from between the wheels, from between the cherubim,” the man went in and stood beside a wheel. 7Then the cherub stretched out his hand from among the cherubim to the fire that was among them. He took some and placed it in the hands of the man clothed in linen, who took it and went out.

The Cherubim, the Wheels, and the Departing Glory

8The cherubim appeared to have the form of a human hand beneath their wings. 9Then I looked and saw four wheels beside the cherubim, one wheel beside each cherub, and the wheels gleamed like beryl. 10In appearance, all four were alike, as though a wheel were within a wheel. 11As they moved, they travelledtravelledtraveled in any of the four directions without pivoting; they followed the direction in which the heads faced, without turning as they moved. 12Their entire bodies—their backs, hands, and wings—were full of eyes all around, as were their four wheels. 13I heard the wheels being called “the whirling wheels.” 14Each of the cherubim had four faces: one face was that of a cherub, the second that of a man, the third that of a lion, and the fourth that of an eagle.

15Then the cherubim rose upward. These were the living creatures I had seen by the Kebar River. 16When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them; and when the cherubim raised their wings to rise from the earth, the wheels did not leave their side. 17When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still; when they rose, the wheels rose with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in them.

The Glory of the God of Israel Leaves the Temple

18Then the glory of the the LORD departed from the threshold of the temple and stood above the cherubim. 19As I watched, the cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the earth, and the wheels accompanied them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the the LORD, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them. 20These were the living creatures I had seen beneath the God of Israel by the Kebar River, and I knew that they were cherubim. 21Each had four faces and four wings, and beneath their wings was the form of human hands. 22Their faces were like the faces I had seen by the Kebar River. Each moved straight ahead.
