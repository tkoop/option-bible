David's Inquiry and Anointing at Hebron

1Subsequent to these occurrences, David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I ascend to one of the cities of Judah?" And the LORD said to him, "Ascend." David then asked, "To which city shall I ascend?" and HeheHe answered, "To Hebron." 2Accordingly, David went up there, taking with him his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail, the widow of Nabal the Carmelite. 3David also brought up the men who accompanied him, each with his household, and they settled in the towns of Hebron. 4Then the men of Judah came, and there they anointed David king over the house of Judah. David's Embassy to Jabesh-gilead

5David dispatched messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead and said to them, "May you be blessed by the LORD, because you have shown this kindness to Saul your lord and have buried him. 6Now may the LORD show steadfast love and faithfulness to you; and I myself will likewise requite you with good, because you have done this deed. 7Therefore let your hands be strengthened, and be valiant, for Saul your lord is dead, and the house of Judah has anointed me king over them."

Abner's Rival Monarchy and the Clash at Gibeon

8Meanwhile, Abner son of Ner, commander of Saul's army, took Ish-bosheth son of Saul and conveyed him across to Mahanaim, 9where he installed him as king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all Israel. 10Ish-bosheth, Saul's son, was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned two years; but the house of Judah followed David. 11The period during which David reigned in Hebron over the house of Judah amounted to seven years and six months. 12Abner son of Ner, together with the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, set out from Mahanaim for Gibeon. 13Joab son of Zeruiah, along with David's servants, likewise went forth and encountered them at the pool of Gibeon; there they sat, one party on one side of the pool and the other on the opposite side. 14Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men arise and hold a martial contest before us." Joab answered, "Let them arise." 15So they arose and were counted off, twelve for Benjamin and for Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve of David's servants. 16Each seized his opponent by the head and thrust his sword into his opponent's side, so that they fell together; therefore that place was called Helkath-hazzurim, which is at Gibeon. 17The battle that day was exceedingly fierce, and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated before David's servants. 18The three sons of Zeruiah were present there—Joab, Abishai, and Asahel—and Asahel was swift of foot as a gazelle in the open field. 19Asahel pursued Abner without deviating to the right or to the left. 20Abner turned and asked, "Are you Asahel?" He replied, "I am." 21Abner said to him, "Turn aside to your right or to your left, seize one of the young men, and take his equipment." But Asahel would not turn aside from pursuing him. 22Abner spoke again to Asahel: "Turn aside from pursuing me. Why should I strike you to the ground? How could I then lift my face before Joab your brother?" 23Yet he refused to turn aside; therefore Abner struck him in the abdomen with the butt of his spear, so that the spear emerged from his back, and he fell and died on the spot. Everyone who came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died stood still.

The Cessation of Hostilities and Return to Hebron

24Joab and Abishai pursued Abner; and as the sun was setting they reached the hill of Ammah, which lies before Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon. 25The Benjaminites rallied behind Abner, formed a single company, and took their stand on the top of a hill. 26Abner called to Joab, "Shall the sword devour forever? Do you not know that the end will be bitter? How long before you tell the people to turn back from pursuing their brothers?" 27Joab replied, "As God lives, if you had not spoken, the men would not have withdrawn from pursuing their brothers until morning." 28Then Joab blew the trumpet, and all the people halted; they pursued Israel no further and fought no more. 29Abner and his men marched through the Arabah all that night, crossed the Jordan, and came to Mahanaim. 30Joab returned from pursuing Abner and mustered all the people; among David's servants nineteen men were missing besides Asahel. 31But David's servants had struck down three hundred and sixty men among the Benjaminites and Abner's men. 32They took up Asahel and buried him in his father's tomb at Bethlehem. Joab and his men marched all night and reached Hebron at daybreak.
