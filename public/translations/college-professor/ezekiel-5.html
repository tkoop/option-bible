The Prophet’s Symbolic Shaving and the Apportionment of His Hair

1“And you, son of man, take unto yourself a keen sword; use it as a barber’s razor, and pass it over your head and over your beard; then take scales for weighing, and apportion the hair. 2During the days of the siege, when their term is fulfilled, you shall consign a third to the flames in the midst of the city; you shall take a third and smite it with the sword round about the city; and a third you shall disperse to the wind, while I unsheathe a sword in pursuit of them.

3“But take a few strands from there, and bind them in the folds of your garment. 4Again, take some of these and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them in the fire; from thence fire shall issue forth to the whole house of Israel.”
