Joseph’s Descent into Egyptian Servitude

1Joseph had been conveyed down to Egypt, where an Egyptian named Potiphar—an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard—purchased him from the Ishmaelites who had brought him thither.

Divine FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor within Potiphar’s Household

2The LORD was with Joseph, so that he prospered; he resided in the house of his Egyptian master. 3His master perceived that the LORD was with him and that the LORD granted success to all his undertakings. 4Joseph thus found favourfavourfavor in his sight and attended him; Potiphar appointed him over his household and entrusted all his possessions into his hands.

5From the moment Potiphar placed him in charge of his household and of all that he possessed, the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s house on account of Joseph; the blessing of the LORD rested upon everything Potiphar had, both in the house and in the field. 6So Potiphar relinquished all his affairs into Joseph’s care; apart from the food he ate, he concerned himself with nothing. Now Joseph was strikingly handsome in form and countenance.

Temptation, Integrity, and False Accusation

7After these events, his master’s wife cast her eyes upon Joseph and said, “Lie with me.”

8But he refused, saying to his master’s wife, “Behold, with me in charge, my master does not concern himself with anything in the house, and everything he owns he has entrusted to my care. 9No one in this house is greater than I, and he has withheld nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How then could I perpetrate this great wickedness and sin against God?”

10Although she importuned Joseph day after day, he would not lie with her, nor even remain in her presence.

11One day, when he entered the house to perform his duties, and no one of the household servants was within, 12she seized him by his garment, saying, “Lie with me,” but he left his garment in her hand and fled, escaping outside.

13When she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and had fled outside, 14she summoned the men of her household and said to them, “Look! This Hebrew has been brought to us to toy with us. He came to me to lie with me, but I cried out with a loud voice. 15When he heard me cry aloud, he left his garment beside me and fled outside.”

16She kept his garment beside her until his master came home.

17Then she addressed him, saying, “That Hebrew slave whom you brought to us came to me to toy with me. 18But as soon as I cried out with a loud voice, he left his garment beside me and fled outside.”

19When his master heard the words his wife spoke to him—namely, “This is how your slave treated me”—his anger burned.

20Joseph’s master took him and put him into the prison, the place where the king’s prisoners were confined; and he remained there in prison.

Divine Sustaining Presence in Prison

21But the LORD was with Joseph and extended kindness to him; HeheHe granted him favourfavourfavor in the eyes of the prison warden. 22The warden committed to Joseph’s charge all the prisoners in the prison, and he became responsible for all that was done there. 23The warden did not supervise anything that was in Joseph’s hand, because the LORD was with him, and the LORD made all that he did prosper.
