Reuben and Gad Seek an Inheritance East of the Jordan

1The Reubenites and Gadites possessed an exceedingly great abundance of livestock, and when they surveyed the lands of Jazer and Gilead, they perceived that the region was eminently suited to pastoral husbandry. 2Accordingly, the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben approached Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the congregation, and said, 3"Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon— 4the land that the LORD subdued before the congregation of Israel is a land for livestock, and your servants possess livestock in abundance. 5If we have found favourfavourfavor in your sight, let this land be granted to your servants as a possession, and do not compel us to cross the Jordan."

Moses Warns Against Repeating the Sin of Kadesh-barnea

6Moses said to the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben, "Shall your brothers go to war while you remain here? 7Why would you dishearten the Israelites from crossing into the land that the LORD has given them? 8This is precisely what your fathers did when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to survey the land. 9They ascended to the ValleyvalleyValley of Eshcol, saw the land, and then discouraged the Israelites so that they would not enter the land that the LORD had given them. 10On that day the LORD's anger was kindled, and HeheHe swore an oath, saying, 11'Surely none of the men who came up from Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall see the land I swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, because they have not wholly followed MemeMe, 12except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, for they have wholly followed the LORD. 13The LORD's anger burned against Israel, and HeheHe made them wander in the wilderness for forty years, until the entire generation that had done evil in the LORD's sight was consumed. 14And now you have arisen in your fathers' place, a brood of sinful men, only to add still more to the LORD's fierce anger against Israel. 15For if you turn away from following HimhimHim, HeheHe will again abandon this people to the wilderness, and you will bring ruin upon all this people.'"

The Tribes Pledge Armed Support for Israel

16Then they came near to him and said, "We will build sheepfolds here for our livestock and cities for our children. 17But we ourselves will go armed and march in the vanguard before the Israelites until we have brought them to their place, while our children remain in fortified cities, safe from the inhabitants of the land. 18We will not return to our homes until every Israelite has received his inheritance. 19For we shall not share in their inheritance west of the Jordan and beyond; our inheritance has fallen to us east of the Jordan."

20Moses said to them, "If you do this—if you arm yourselves before the LORD for war, 21and every armed man among you crosses the Jordan before the LORD until HeheHe has driven out HishisHis enemies from before HimhimHim, 22and the land is subdued before the LORD—then afterward you may return, and you will be blameless before the LORD and before Israel, and this land shall be your possession before the LORD. 23But if you do not do so, behold, you have sinned against the LORD; and be assured that your sin will find you out. 24Build cities for your children and folds for your flocks, and do as you have promised."

25The sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben said to Moses, "We, your servants, will do as my lord commands. 26Our children, our wives, our livestock, and all our beasts will remain in the cities of Gilead. 27But your servants will cross over, every man armed for battle, before the LORD to wage war, as my lord has spoken."

28Moses commanded them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of Israel. 29Moses said to them, "If the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead as a possession. 30But if they do not cross over armed with you, then they shall receive possessions among you in the land of Canaan." 31The sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben answered, "What the LORD has spoken to your servants, so we will do. 32We will cross over armed before the LORD into Canaan, and our inherited possession shall remain with us on this side of the Jordan."

The Transjordanian Territory Is Allotted and Settled

33Moses gave to them—to the sons of Gad, the sons of Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph—the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan: the land with its cities and the surrounding territories. 34The sons of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 35Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 36Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran—fortified cities and sheepfolds. 37The Reubenites built Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kiriathaim, 38along with Nebo, Baal-meon (whose names were changed), and Sibmah; and they gave names to the cities they built. 39Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it, and dispossessed the Amorites who were in it. 40Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he settled there. 41Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their villages, calling them Havvoth-jair. 42Nobah went and captured Kenath with its villages, and called it Nobah after his own name.
