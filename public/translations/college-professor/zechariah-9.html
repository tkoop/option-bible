The Prophetic Burden against Hadrach, Damascus, and the Maritime Strongholds

1The burden of the word of the LORD is in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is its place of repose, for the eyes of humankind—and indeed of all the tribes of Israel—are turned toward the LORD. 2Hamath, too, which borders upon it, and Tyre and Sidon, though they esteem themselves exceedingly wise, shall be brought low.

3Tyre has fortified herself with a stronghold; she has amassed silver like dust and refined gold like the mire of the streets. 4Behold, the Lord will dispossess her, strike down her wealth upon the sea, and she shall be consumed by fire.

5Ashkelon shall see and be seized with fear; Gaza shall writhe in great anguish; Ekron, because her expectation is confounded, shall be put to shame. The king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall no longer be inhabited. 6A mixed people shall settle in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

7I will remove the blood from their mouths and their abominations from between their teeth; then they, too, shall become a remnant for our God, and they shall be like a clan in Judah, while Ekron shall be like the Jebusites. 8I will encamp about MymyMy house as a bulwark against those who pass to and fro, and no oppressor shall again overrun them, for now I have seen with MymyMy own eyes.

The Advent of Zion’s Righteous and Humble King

9Rejoice exceedingly, O daughter of Zion; shout aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem. Behold, your KingkingKing is coming to you—righteous and victorious, humble, and riding upon a donkey, upon a colt, the foal of a donkey. 10I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem; the bow of war shall be shattered, and HeheHe shall proclaim peace to the nations. His dominion shall extend from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.

Covenantal Liberation and the LORD’s Warrior Triumph

11As for you, because of the blood of MymyMy covenant with you, I will release your prisoners from the waterless pit. 12Return to your stronghold, O prisoners of hope; even today I declare that I will restore to you double.

13For I have bent Judah as MymyMy bow and filled it with Ephraim; I will rouse your sons, O Zion, against your sons, O Greece, and I will make you like the sword of a warrior. 14Then the LORD will appear over them, and HishisHis arrow will flash forth like lightning; the Lord the LORD will sound the trumpet and go forth in the whirlwinds of the south.

15The LORD of Hosts will shield them; they shall devour and trample the slingstones. They shall drink and roar as if with wine; they shall be filled like a basin, like the corners of the altar. 16On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of HishisHis people, for they are like jewels in a crown, sparkling over HishisHis land. 17For how great is HishisHis goodness and HishisHis beauty! Grain shall make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.
