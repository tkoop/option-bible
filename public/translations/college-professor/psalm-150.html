A Final Doxology of Universal Praise

1Praise the LORD! Praise God in HishisHis sanctuary; praise HimhimHim in the firmament of HishisHis power. 2Praise HimhimHim for HishisHis mighty deeds; praise HimhimHim according to HishisHis surpassing greatness.

3Praise HimhimHim with the blast of the shofar; praise HimhimHim with lute and harp. 4Praise HimhimHim with timbrel and dance; praise HimhimHim with strings and flute.

5Praise HimhimHim with clashing cymbals; praise HimhimHim with resounding cymbals. 6Let every being that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
