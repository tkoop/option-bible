Exclusionary Criteria for Admission to the Congregation

1No man whose testicles have been crushed or whose genital member has been severed may be admitted to the assembly of the the LORD. 2No child of an illicit union shall enter the assembly of the the LORD; not even in the tenth generation shall any descendant of such a one be admitted to the assembly of the the LORD.

3No Ammonite or Moabite shall enter the assembly of the the LORD; even to the tenth generation, none of them shall ever be admitted to the assembly of the the LORD. 4This is because they did not meet you with bread and water on the road when you departed from Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram-naharaim to curse you. 5Yet the the LORD your God refused to heed Balaam; instead, the the LORD your God transformed the curse into a blessing for you, because the the LORD your God loved you. 6You shall never seek their peace or prosperity for all your days, forever.

7You must not despise an Edomite, for he is your brother; nor shall you despise an Egyptian, because you were an alien in his land. 8The third generation of children born to them may enter the assembly of the the LORD.

Sanitary and Moral Discipline within the Encampment

9When you march out with your host against your enemies, you must keep yourself from every evil. 10If there is among you a man who is not clean because of a nocturnal emission, he shall go outside the camp and shall not enter the midst of the camp. 11Toward evening he shall bathe in water, and at sunset he may come into the midst of the camp.

12You shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there. 13You shall have a trowel among your equipment, and when you sit down outside, you shall dig with it and then cover your excrement. 14For the the LORD your God walks in the midst of your camp, to deliver you and to give your enemies before you, so that your camp may be holy; and HeheHe shall see no indecent thing in you and turn away from following you.

Protections for Fugitive Servants and Prohibitions against Cultic Prostitution

15You shall not hand over to his master a slave who has escaped from his master to you. 16He shall dwell with you, in your midst, in the place that he chooses within one of your gates, wherever it seems best to him; you shall not oppress him. 17There shall be no cult prostitute among the daughters of Israel, and no cult prostitute among the sons of Israel. 18You shall not bring the hire of a harlot or the wages of a dog into the house of the the LORD your God for any vow, for both are an abomination to the the LORD your God.

Commercial Regulations, Votive Obligations, and Harvest Privileges

19You shall not charge interest to your brother—interest on money, interest on food, interest on any thing that is lent. 20You may charge interest to a foreigner, but to your brother you shall not charge interest, so that the the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hand in the land that you are entering to possess.

21When you make a vow to the the LORD your God, you shall not delay to pay it, for the the LORD your God will surely require it of you, and it would be sin in you. 22But if you refrain from vowing, it is no sin in you. 23You shall be careful to perform what goes out of your lips, just as you have freely vowed to the the LORD your God, promising with your mouth.
