Jezebel's Threat and Elijah's Flight

1Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah had done and how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. 2Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, "I swear by the gods that by this time tomorrow I will have made your life like the life of one of them."

3When Elijah saw this, he arose and fled for his life and came to Beersheba, which belongs to Judah, and left his servant there.

Elijah's Despair and Divine Sustenance

4He himself went a day's journey into the wilderness and came and sat down under a broom tree; and he asked that he might die, saying, "I have had enough; now, O the LORD, take my life, for I am no better than my fathers."

5Then he lay down and slept under the broom tree; and behold, an angel touched him and said to him, "Arise and eat." 6And he looked, and behold, there was at his head a cake baked on hot stones and a jar of water. And he ate and drank and lay down again.

7The angel of the LORD came again a second time and touched him and said, "Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you." 8And he arose and ate and drank, and went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mountain of God.

The LORD's Revelation at Horeb

9There he came to a cave and lodged there; and behold, the word of the LORD came to him, saying, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

10He said, "I have been very jealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts; for the children of Israel have forsaken YouryourYour covenant, torn down YouryourYour altars, and slain YouryourYour prophets with the sword. I alone am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

11He said, "Go out and stand on the mountain before the LORD." And behold, the LORD passed by, and a great and strong wind tore the mountains and shattered the rocks before the LORD, but the LORD was not in the wind. 12After the wind came an earthquake, but the LORD was not in the earthquake. After the earthquake came a fire, but the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire came a gentle whisper.

13When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his mantle and went out and stood at the entrance of the cave. And behold, a voice came to him and said, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

14He said, "I have been very jealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts; for the children of Israel have forsaken YouryourYour covenant, torn down YouryourYour altars, and slain YouryourYour prophets with the sword. I alone am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

Commissioning of Hazael, Jehu, and Elisha

15The LORD said to him, "Go, return on your way to the wilderness of Damascus; and when you arrive, anoint Hazael king over Aram. 16And you shall anoint Jehu son of Nimshi king over Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah you shall anoint as prophet in your place. 17And whoever escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall put to death; and whoever escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall put to death. 18Yet I will preserve seven thousand in Israel, all who have not bowed the knee to Baal, and every mouth that has not kissed him."

19Elijah departed from there and found Elisha son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he himself was with the twelfth. Elijah passed by him and cast his mantle upon him.

20He left the oxen and ran after Elijah and said, "Let me, I pray, kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow you."

Elijah said to him, "Go back again, for what have I done to you?"

21He turned back from following him and took the yoke of oxen and slaughtered them and boiled their flesh with the implements of the oxen and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he arose and went after Elijah and ministered to him.
