The Divine Commission of Joshua

1After the death of Moses, the servant of the LORD, the LORD addressed Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ attendant, with these words:

2“Moses MymyMy servant is dead. Now, therefore, arise—you together with all this people—and cross this Jordan into the land that I am granting to the children of Israel. 3Every place where the sole of your foot treads I have given to you, just as I promised Moses. 4Your border shall extend from the wilderness and this Lebanon to the great river, the Euphrates—that is, all the land of the Hittites—and as far as the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea toward the setting of the sun.

5“No one shall be able to withstand you throughout all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I shall be with you; I will neither abandon you nor forsake you. 6Be strong and courageous, for you shall apportion to this people the land that I swore to their ancestors to give them. 7Only be very strong and courageous, taking care to observe the whole law that Moses MymyMy servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from it to the right or to the left, so that you may prosper wherever you go.

8“This BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw must not depart from your mouth; meditate upon it day and night, so that you may be careful to do all that is written in it. Then you will prosper and succeed in all that you do. 9Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be terrified or dismayed, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”

Joshua's Directives to the People

10Joshua then commanded the officers of the people, 11“Go throughout the camp and instruct the people: ‘Prepare your provisions, for within three days you will cross this Jordan to enter and take possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.’”

Instructions to the Transjordan Tribes

12Moreover, Joshua addressed the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh:

13“Remember the word that Moses, the servant of the LORD, commanded you when he said, ‘The LORD your God is giving you rest and has given you this land.’ 14Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock shall remain in the land that Moses gave you beyond the Jordan, but all your valiant men, armed, shall cross over before your brothers and assist them,

15“until the LORD has given your brothers rest, as HeheHe has given you, and they also have taken possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving them. Then you may return to your own land and possess it—the land that Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you beyond the Jordan, toward the sunrise.”

The People's Pledge of Fidelity

16They answered Joshua, “All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

17“Just as we obeyed Moses in all things, so we will obey you. May the LORD your God be with you, as HeheHe was with Moses. 18Whoever rebels against your command and does not obey your words in all that you command him shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous!”
