The Dragon's Pursuit of the Woman and Her Seed

13When the dragon perceived that he had been cast to the earth, he pursued the woman who had brought forth the male child. 14The woman was given the two wings of the great eagle, so that she might fly from the presence of the serpent into her place in the wilderness, where she is nourished for a time, and times, and half a time. 15From his mouth the serpent spewed water like a river after the woman, to sweep her away with the flood. 16But the earth came to the woman's aid and opened its mouth, swallowing the river that the dragon had disgorged from his mouth. 17Enraged at the woman, the dragon departed to make war against the rest of her offspring, those who keep the commandments of God and hold the testimony of Jesus. And he took his stand upon the sand of the sea.
