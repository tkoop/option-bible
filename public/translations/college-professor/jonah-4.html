Jonah’s Anger and the LORD’s Compassion

1But Jonah was greatly displeased and became angry.

2So he prayed to the LORD and said, “Please, O the LORD, was this not what I said while I was still in my own country? That is why I fled beforehand to Tarshish, for I knew that YouyouYou are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love, and that YouyouYou relent from bringing calamity. 3And now, O the LORD, please take my life from me, for it is better for me to die than to live.”

4And the LORD said, “Is it right for you to be angry?”

Jonah Waits Outside Nineveh

5Then Jonah went out of the city and sat to the east of it. There he made himself a booth and sat under it in the shade, waiting to see what would become of the city.

6the LORD God appointed a plant, and it grew up over Jonah to provide shade over his head and to deliver him from his discomfort; and Jonah was exceedingly glad because of the plant. 7But when dawn came the next day, God appointed a worm, which attacked the plant so that it withered.

8When the sun rose, God appointed a scorching east wind; the sun beat upon Jonah’s head, so that he grew faint and begged to die, saying, “It is better for me to die than to live.”

The LORD’s Rebuke and Compassion

9And God said to Jonah, “Is it right for you to be angry about the plant?”

He said, “I am angry enough to die.”

10And the LORD said, “You have been concerned about the plant, for which you did not labourlabourlabor and which you did not grow; it sprang up in a night and perished in a night. 11And should I not have compassion on Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand people utterly bereft of even the most elementary discernment, and also many animals?”
