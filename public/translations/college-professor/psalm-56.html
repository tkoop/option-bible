A Miktam of David: Supplication amid Relentless Oppression

1For the choirmaster, to the melody “The DovedoveDove on DistantdistantDistant Terebinths,” a Miktam of David, composed when the Philistines seized him in Gath. Be gracious to me, O God, for mortals trample me; all day long they assail and crush me. 2My adversaries pursue me without respite; indeed, many in their arrogance contend against me.

3On the day I am afraid, I will put my trust in YouyouYou. 4In God—whose word I extol—in God I have trusted; I shall not fear. What can mere flesh accomplish against me?

5All day long they distort my words; every one of their schemes is bent upon my ruin. 6They conspire, they lurk, they watch my steps, while they wait to take my life. 7Shall they escape despite their iniquity? In YouryourYour wrath, O God, cast down the peoples.

8You have reckoned my wanderings; place my tears in YouryourYour flask. Are they not recorded in YouryourYour ledger? 9Then my foes will turn back on the day I call. This I know: God is on my side.

10In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise, 11in God I have trusted; I will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me?

12Your vows are upon me, O God; I will render thank offerings to YouyouYou. 13For YouyouYou have delivered my soul from death, and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.
