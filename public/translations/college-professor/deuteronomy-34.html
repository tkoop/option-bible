Moses Beholds the Promised Land

1Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah, which faces Jericho. And the LORD showed him the whole land—from Gilead to Dan— 2all of Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the Western SeaseaSea, 3the Negev, and the region of the Jordan ValleyvalleyValley, with Jericho, the CitycityCity of Palms, as far as Zoar. 4And the LORD said to him, “This is the land that I swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob when I said, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’ I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you shall not cross over into it.”

The Death and Burial of Moses

5So Moses, the servant of the LORD, died there in the land of Moab, as the LORD had said. 6And HeheHe buried him in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor, but to this day no one knows the location of his grave. 7Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died, yet his eyes were not dim, nor had his vigourvigourvigor faded.

Israel Mourns and Joshua Is Empowered

8The Israelites lamented Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days, until the period of weeping and mourning for Moses had come to an end. 9Now Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom, because Moses had laid his hands on him; so the Israelites obeyed him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Unparalleled Greatness of Moses

10Since then, no prophet has arisen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face, 11in all the signs and wonders that the LORD sent him to perform in the land of Egypt against Pharaoh and all his officials and all his land, 12and in all the mighty acts and all the great and terrifying deeds that Moses performed in the sight of all Israel.
