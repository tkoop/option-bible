Jacob's Journey Eastward

They answered, "We are from Haran."

Rachel at the Well

9While he was still conversing with them, Rachel arrived with her father's sheep, for she tended them. 10When Jacob saw Rachel, daughter of his mother's brother Laban, together with Laban's sheep, he approached, rolled the stone from the mouth of the well, and watered the flock belonging to his mother's brother Laban.

11Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud.

12Jacob informed Rachel that he was her father's kinsman and Rebekah's son; whereupon she ran and reported it to her father.

13Upon hearing the tidings concerning Jacob, his sister's son, Laban ran to meet him, embraced him, kissed him, and conducted him to his house. Jacob then recounted all these matters to Laban.

14Laban said to him, "Surely you are my own flesh and blood." And he remained with him for a month.

Jacob's Bargain with Laban

15Laban said to Jacob, "Because you are my kinsman, should you serve me gratuitously? Tell me, what shall your wages be?"

16Now Laban had two daughters: the elder was named Leah, and the younger Rachel. 17Leah's eyes were delicate, whereas Rachel was beautiful in form and beautiful in countenance. 18Jacob loved Rachel and said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel your younger daughter."

19Laban replied, "It is better that I give her to you than to another man; stay with me."

20Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed to him but a few days because of his love for her. 21Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days are completed, that I may go in to her."

22Laban gathered all the men of the place and prepared a feast. 23In the evening he took his daughter Leah and brought her to him, and he went in to her. 24Laban gave his daughter Leah his maidservant Zilpah as her attendant.

25In the morning, he discovered that it was Leah. He said to Laban, "What is this you have done to me? Did I not serve you for Rachel? Why then have you deceived me?"

26Laban said, "Such is not done in our place, to give the younger before the elder. 27FulfillFulfilFulfill the bridal week of this one, and we will give you that one also, for the service that you shall render me yet seven other years."

28Jacob did so and fulfilled her week; then he gave him Rachel his daughter as a wife. 29Laban gave Rachel his daughter his maidservant Bilhah as her attendant. 30He went in also to Rachel, and he loved Rachel more than Leah; and he served with him yet seven other years.

Leah and Her Sons

31When the the LORD saw that Leah was unloved, HeheHe opened her womb, but Rachel was barren. 32Leah conceived and bore a son, and she named him Reuben, for she said, "Because the the LORD has looked upon my affliction; surely now my husband will love me."

33She conceived again and bore a son, and said, "Because the the LORD has heard that I am unloved, HeheHe has given me this son also." And she named him Simeon.

34She conceived again and bore a son, and said, "Now this time my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons." Therefore she named him Levi.

35She conceived again and bore a son, and said, "This time I will praise the the LORD." Therefore she named him Judah; then she ceased bearing.
