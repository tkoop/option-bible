David's Final Charge to Solomon

1As David's days drew toward their end, he summoned his son Solomon and enjoined him, saying, 2"I am about to die, as every mortal must; therefore be strong, and comport yourself as a man. 3Moreover, keep the charge of the the LORD your God, walking in HishisHis ways and observing HishisHis statutes, HishisHis commandments, HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments, and HishisHis testimonies, as it is inscribed in the LawlawLaw of Moses, so that you may act wisely in all your endeavoursendeavoursendeavors and prosper wherever you turn, 4so that the the LORD may establish HishisHis word that HeheHe spoke concerning me, saying, 'If your sons attend to their way, to walk before MemeMe in truth with all their heart and all their soul, then you shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel.'"

5"Furthermore, you yourself know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me—how he dealt with the two commanders of Israel's armies, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether—how he murdered them in a season of peace, staining with the blood of war the belt upon his loins and the sandals upon his feet. 6Therefore deal with him according to your discernment, and do not allow his hoary head to descend to Sheol in peace."

7"But requite with lovingkindness the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them eat at your table, for they came to my aid when I fled from your brother Absalom. 8Moreover, Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim, remains with you; he uttered a bitter curse against me on the day I fled to Mahanaim, yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the the LORD, saying, 'I will not slay you with the sword.' 9Now do not acquit him, for you are a discerning man and will know what is fitting for him; bring his greygreygray head down to Sheol in blood."

10Then David rested with his ancestors and was buried in the CitycityCity of David. 11The days that David reigned over Israel were forty years: he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. 12Solomon sat upon the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was firmly established.

Adonijah's Petition and Solomon's Reckoning

13Now Adonijah son of Haggith approached Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, and she asked, "Do you come peaceably?"

He replied, "Peaceably."

14Then he said, "I have a matter to set before you."

She said, "Speak."

15He said, "YouyouYou know that the kingship was mine and that all Israel had set their hopes upon me to reign; yet the kingship has turned and passed to my brother, for it was his by the the LORD's decree."

16"Now I ask one request of you; do not refuse me."

She said, "Speak."

17He said, "Please speak to King Solomon—for he will not refuse you—that he give me Abishag the Shunammite as wife."

18She said, "Very well; I will intercede with the king for you."

19So Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him on Adonijah's behalf. The king rose to meet her, bowed before her, resumed his throne, and caused a throne to be set for the king's mother; and she sat at his right hand.

20Then she said, "I have one small petition to ask of you; do not refuse me."

The king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not refuse you."

21She said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as wife."

22King Solomon answered his mother, "Why do you ask Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? You might as well ask the kingdom for him—for he is my elder brother—and for Abiathar the priest and for Joab son of Zeruiah."

23Then King Solomon swore by the the LORD, saying, "May God do so to me, and still more, if Adonijah has not forfeited his life by this very request. 24Now therefore, as the the LORD lives, who has established me and set me upon my father David's throne, and who has built me a house, as HeheHe promised, Adonijah shall surely be put to death today."

25So King Solomon dispatched Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who struck him down, and he died.

The Purging of Joab and Abiathar

26To Abiathar the priest the king said, "Withdraw to your fields at Anathoth, for you deserve death; yet I will not put you to death this day, because you bore the ark of the Lord, the the LORD, before David my father, and because you shared in all my father's afflictions."

27So Solomon removed Abiathar from the priesthood of the the LORD, thereby fulfilling the word of the the LORD that HeheHe had spoken concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

28When the report reached Joab—for Joab had sided with Adonijah, though he had not sided with Absalom—Joab fled to the tent of the the LORD and seized the horns of the altar.

29King Solomon was told that Joab had fled to the tent of the the LORD and was beside the altar. Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him down."

30Benaiah came to the tent of the the LORD and said to him, "Thus says the king: 'Come out.'"

But he replied, "No; I will die here."

31Benaiah reported to the king, "Thus Joab spoke, and thus he answered me."

The king said, "Do as he has said; strike him down and bury him, and remove from me and from my father's house the innocent blood that Joab shed. 32The the LORD will bring his blood back upon his own head, because he fell upon two men more righteous and better than himself and slew them with the sword—Abner son of Ner, commander of Israel's army, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of Judah's army—without my father David's knowledge. 33So their blood shall return upon Joab's head and upon his descendants' heads forever; but upon David, his descendants, his house, and his throne, there shall be peace forever from the the LORD."

34Then Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up, struck him down, and killed him; and he was buried at his own house in the wilderness.

35The king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army in his stead, and Zadok the priest in Abiathar's stead.

Shimei's Transgression and Doom

36The king sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem and dwell there; do not leave for any other place. 37For on the day you leave and cross the BrookbrookBrook Kidron, know with certainty that you shall surely die; your blood will be on your own head."

38Shimei said to the king, "What you say is good; as my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." So Shimei dwelt in Jerusalem for many days.

39After three years, however, two of Shimei's servants fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath; and Shimei was told, "Behold, your servants are in Gath."

40Shimei arose, saddled his donkey, and went to Gath, to Achish, to seek his servants; and Shimei went and brought his servants back from Gath.

41It was told to Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had returned.

42The king sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the the LORD and warn you, saying, 'On the day you leave and go anywhere, know for certain that you shall surely die'? And you said to me, 'The word I have heard is good.' 43Why then have you not kept the oath of the the LORD and the commandment that I gave you? 44You know in your own heart all the evil that you did to David my father; the the LORD will return your wickedness upon your own head. 45But King Solomon shall be blessed, and David's throne shall be established before the the LORD forever."

46The king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who went out and struck him down, and he died. Thus the kingdom was secured in Solomon's hand.
