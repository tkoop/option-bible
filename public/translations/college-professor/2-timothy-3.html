The Character of the Last Days

1But know this: in the last days grievous times will come. 2For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, blasphemous, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, and unholy; 3unloving, unforgiving, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, and averse to goodness; 4treacherous, rash, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God. 5They will maintain an outward semblance of piety while repudiating its power; and from such persons you must turn away. 6For among them are those who insinuate themselves into households and make captives of susceptible women burdened with sins, swayed by manifold cravings, 7perpetually learning yet never arriving at a knowledge of the truth. 8Just as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so these men likewise withstand the truth; their minds are corrupted, and they are reprobate concerning the faith. 9But they will not progress much further, for their folly will be manifest to everyone, as the folly of those men was.

Paul’s Exhortation and Example

10You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my way of life, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, and my steadfastness, 11together with my persecutions and sufferings, such as befell me at Antioch, at Iconium, and at Lystra—persecutions I endured, yet the Lord delivered me from all of them. 12Indeed, everyone who wishes to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted. 13But wicked people and charlatans will progress from bad to worse, deceiving others and being deceived themselves.

14But as for you, abide in what you have learned and of which you have been assured, knowing from whom you learned it, 15and that from infancy you have known the sacred ScripturesscripturesScriptures, which are able to impart wisdom unto you for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.
