Moses Summons Israel to Covenantal Fidelity

1And now, O Israel, give heed to the statutes and the ordinances that I am instructing you to observe, that you may live and enter to possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, is bestowing upon you. 2You shall not add to the word that I command you, nor diminish from it, that you may keep the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you. 3Your eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal-peor; for the LORD your God destroyed from your midst all the men who followed Baal-peor. 4But you who cleaved to the LORD your God are all alive this day.

The Distinctive Wisdom and Nearness of Israel's God

5Behold, I have taught you statutes and ordinances, as the LORD my God commanded me, to perform them in the midst of the land into which you are entering to possess it. 6Therefore observe and perform them, for this shall be your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the peoples, who, when they hear all these things, shall say, "Surely this great nation is a wise and discerning people." 7For what great nation is there that has a god so near to it as the LORD our God is to us, whenever we call upon HimhimHim? 8And what great nation is there that has statutes and ordinances so righteous as all this law that I am setting before you today?

Warning Against Apostasy and the Promise of Restoration

21Moreover, the LORD was incensed against me on account of your words, and HeheHe swore that I should not cross the Jordan, nor enter the good land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance. 22For I am to die in this land; I shall not cross the Jordan; but you shall cross over and possess that good land. 23Take heed to yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of the LORD your God, which HeheHe made with you, and make for yourselves a graven image in the likeness of anything that the LORD your God has forbidden you. 24For the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

25When you beget children and children's children, and have grown old in the land, if you act corruptly and make a graven image in the likeness of anything, and do evil in the sight of the LORD your God, so as to provoke HimhimHim to anger, 26I call Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth to witness against you this day, that you shall quickly perish from the land into which you are crossing the Jordan to possess; you shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall be utterly destroyed. 27And the LORD will scatter you among the peoples, and you shall be left few in number among the nations whither the LORD will lead you. 28And there you shall serve gods of wood and stone, the work of men's hands, which neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.
