Oracle of JudgmentJudgementJudgment against the Prince of Tyre

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying: 2“Son of man, say to the prince of Tyre, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: Because your heart has been exalted, and you have said that you are a god dwelling in the seat of the gods in the heart of the seas, though you are only a man and not a god, and you have set your heart as though it were the heart of a god—

‘3Behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you. 4By your wisdom and understanding you have amassed wealth for yourself and gathered gold and silver into your treasuries. 5By the greatness of your wisdom in trade you have multiplied your wealth, and your heart has been lifted up because of your wealth.

‘6Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Because you have set your heart like the heart of a god, 7behold, I am bringing foreigners upon you, the most ruthless of nations; they will draw their swords against the beauty born of your wisdom and profane your splendoursplendoursplendor. 8They will bring you down to the pit, and you will die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas. 9Will you still say, “I am a god,” before those who kill you? You are a man, not a god, in the hands of those who profane you. 10You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners, for I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.’”

Lamentation over the King of Tyre

11The word of the LORD came to me, saying: 12“Son of man, raise a lamentation over the king of Tyre and say to him, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: You were the very epitome of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

‘13You were in Eden, the garden of God; every precious stone was your covering: ruby, topaz, and diamond, beryl, onyx, and jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald; and your settings and mountings were fashioned in gold. On the day you were created they were prepared.

‘14You were the anointed cherub who covers; I placed you. You were on the holy mountain of God; you walked among the stones of fire. 15You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created until iniquity was found in you.

‘16In the abundance of your trade your inner being was filled with violence, and you sinned; therefore I profaned you and expelled you from the mountain of God, and I destroyed you, O covering cherub, from the midst of the stones of fire. 17Your heart was lifted up because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendoursplendoursplendor. I cast you to the ground and made you a spectacle before kings.

‘18By the multitude of your iniquities and by the unrighteousness of your trade you profaned your sanctuaries; so I brought fire out from your midst, and it consumed you, and I reduced you to ashes on the earth in the sight of all who saw you. 19All who knew you among the peoples are appalled at you; you have become a horror, and you will be no more forever.’”

Oracle against Sidon and the Restoration of Israel

20The word of the LORD came to me, saying: 21“Son of man, set your face toward Sidon and prophesy against her. 22Say, ‘Thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I am against you, O Sidon, and I will be glorified in your midst; and they will know that I am the LORD when I execute judgmentsjudgementsjudgments in her and manifest MymyMy holiness in her.

‘23For I will send pestilence into her and blood into her streets; the slain will fall in her midst by the sword against her from all around; and they will know that I am the LORD. 24For the house of Israel there will no longer be a pricking brier or a painful thorn from all around those who scorn them; and they will know that I am the Lord the LORD.

‘25Thus says the Lord the LORD: When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they are scattered, I will manifest MymyMy holiness among them in the sight of the nations; and they will dwell in their own land, which I gave to MymyMy servant Jacob. 26They will dwell in it securely, build houses, and plant vineyards; they will dwell securely when I execute judgmentsjudgementsjudgments upon all around them who despise them. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.’”
