Hezekiah's Distress and Isaiah's Reassurance

3They said to him, “Thus says Hezekiah: ‘This day is a day of anguish, chastisement, and blasphemy; for the children have arrived at the birthstool, yet there is no vigourvigourvigor to bring them forth.’ 4Perchance the LORD your God has heeded all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master, the king of Assyria, dispatched to revile the living God, and will rebuke the words that the LORD your God has heard. 5So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

Sennacherib's Renewed Threat

8When the Rabshakeh learned that the king of Assyria had withdrawn from Lachish, he returned and found him campaigning against Libnah. 9Moreover, he heard concerning Tirhakah king of Cush, “Behold, he has marched forth to make war against you,” so he sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying, 10“Thus you shall say to Hezekiah king of Judah: ‘Do not let your God, in whom you trust, delude you by asserting, “Jerusalem shall not be delivered into the hand of the king of Assyria.” 11Behold, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all lands, devoting them to utter destruction; and shall you be delivered? 12Did the gods of the nations deliver those whom my fathers destroyed—Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the sons of Eden who were in Telassar? 13Where is the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’”

Hezekiah's Prayer and the Divine Oracle

14Hezekiah received the letters from the hand of the messengers and read them; then he ascended to the house of the LORD and spread them out before the LORD. 15Hezekiah prayed before the LORD and said, “O LORD, God of Israel, who are enthroned upon the cherubim, YouyouYou alone are God of all the kingdoms of the earth; YouyouYou have made Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth. 16Incline YouryourYour ear, O LORD, and hear; open YouryourYour eyes, O LORD, and see; and hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to revile the living God. 17Truly, O LORD, the kings of Assyria have laid waste the nations and their lands. 18And they have cast their gods into the fire; for they were no gods, but the work of men's hands, wood and stone, so they destroyed them. 19Now, therefore, O LORD our God, deliver us from his hand, that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that YouyouYou alone are the LORD God.”

20Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Because you have prayed to MemeMe concerning Sennacherib king of Assyria, I have heard.”

21“This is the word that the LORD has spoken concerning him: The virgin daughter of Zion despises you and mocks you; the daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head in derision at you. 22Whom have you reproached and blasphemed? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes on high? Against the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel! 23By your messengers you have reproached the Lord, and you have said, ‘With my many chariots I have ascended the heights of the mountains, the farthest reaches of Lebanon; I have felled its tall cedars and its choicest cypresses; I have penetrated its farthest lodging, the forest of its Carmel.’ 24I have dug and drunk strange waters, and with the soles of my feet I have dried up all the streams of Egypt.”

25“Have you not heard? 32“Therefore thus says the LORD concerning the king of Assyria: He shall not enter this city, nor shoot an arrow there, nor advance against it with a shield, nor raise a siege rampart against it. 33By the way that he came, by the same shall he return, and he shall not enter this city, says the LORD. 34For I will defend this city to deliver it, for MymyMy own sake and for the sake of MymyMy servant David.”

The Fall of the Assyrian Host

35And that night the angel of the LORD went out and struck down 185,000 in the camp of the Assyrians. When people arose early in the morning, behold, all were corpses. 36So Sennacherib king of Assyria decamped and went and returned and dwelt in Nineveh. 37And as he was worshipping in the house of Nisroch his god, Adrammelech and Sharezer, his sons, struck him down with the sword; and they fled into the land of Ararat. And Esarhaddon his son reigned in his stead.
