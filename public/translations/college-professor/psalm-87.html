A psalm of the sons of Korah—a song.

A Hymn of Zion’s Exalted Foundations and Cosmopolitan Destiny

1His foundation is established upon the holy mountains. 2The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. 3Glorious things are spoken of you, O city of God. Selah.

The Nations Enrolled Among Zion’s Children

4“I will recount Egypt and Babylon among those who acknowledge MemeMe—along with Philistia, Tyre, and Cush—each one born there.”

5And concerning Zion it shall be said, “This one and that one were born in her,” and the MostmostMost HighhighHigh HimselfhimselfHimself will establish her.

6The LORD will record in the register of the nations, “This one was born there.” Selah.

7Singers and dancers will declare, “All my springs are in you.”
