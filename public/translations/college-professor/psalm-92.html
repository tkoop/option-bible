6A senseless man does not know, nor does a fool understand this.

The Transience of the Wicked and the Exaltation of the LORD

7When the wicked spring up like grass and all evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever. 8But YouyouYou, O LORD, are exalted forevermore. 9For behold, YouryourYour enemies, O LORD—for behold, YouryourYour enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered.

The Anointing and Empowerment of the Psalmist

10But YouyouYou have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox; I have been anointed with fresh oil. 11My eyes have looked upon the downfall of my foes; my ears have heard the ruin of my wicked assailants.

The Flourishing of the Righteous in the Courts of God

12The righteous will flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. 13Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God. 14In old age they will still bear fruit; they will remain vigorous and verdant, 15to declare that the LORD is upright; HeheHe is my RockrockRock, and in HimhimHim there is no unrighteousness.
