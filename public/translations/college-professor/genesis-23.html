Sarah’s Death and Abraham’s Mourning

1Sarah’s years numbered one hundred twenty-seven; such were the days of Sarah’s life. 2Sarah died in Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—in the land of Canaan, and Abraham came to mourn for Sarah and to weep over her.

Abraham’s Petition for a Burial Site

3Abraham arose from before his dead and spoke to the Hittites, saying, 4“I am a stranger and sojourner among you; grant me a burial holding among you, that I may bury my dead out of my sight.”

5The Hittites answered Abraham, saying to him, 6“Hear us, my lord; you are a prince of God among us. Bury your dead in the choicest of our tombs; none of us will withhold from you a tomb for burying your dead.”

7Abraham rose and bowed to the people of the land, to the Hittites. 8He spoke with them, saying, “If it is your will that I bury my dead out of my sight, hear me and entreat Ephron son of Zohar on my behalf, 9that he may give me the cave of Machpelah, which is his, at the end of his field; for the full price let him give it to me in your presence as a burial holding.”

The Purchase of Machpelah and Sarah’s Burial

10Now Ephron was sitting among the Hittites, and Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the hearing of all the Hittites, of all who entered the gate of his city, saying, 11“No, my lord, hear me: I give you the field, and I give you the cave that is in it; in the sight of the sons of my people I give it to you. Bury your dead.”

12Abraham bowed before the people of the land. 13He spoke to Ephron in the hearing of the people of the land, saying, “No, but if you are willing, hear me: I will give the price of the field; take it from me, that I may bury my dead there.”

14Ephron answered Abraham, saying to him, 15“My lord, listen to me: a piece of land worth four hundred shekels of silver—what is that between me and you? Bury your dead.”

16Abraham listened to Ephron, and Abraham weighed out for Ephron the silver he had named in the hearing of the Hittites—four hundred shekels of silver, current among the merchants. 17Thus the field of Ephron in Machpelah, which lay before Mamre—the field, the cave in it, and all the trees in the field throughout its surrounding boundary—was transferred to Abraham as a possession, 18in the presence of the Hittites, of all who entered the gate of his city. 19Afterward Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field of Machpelah before Mamre—that is, Hebron—in the land of Canaan. 20The field and the cave in it were made over to Abraham as a burial holding by the Hittites.
