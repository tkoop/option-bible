The Solemn Convocation and Public Reading of the Law

1All the people coalesced into a single body in the open plaza before the Water GategateGate, and they urged Ezra the scribe to bring forth the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses, which the LORD had commanded for Israel. 2On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the LawlawLaw before the congregation—men and women alike, together with everyone possessing the discernment to comprehend what was heard. 3He read from it in the plaza before the Water GategateGate from daybreak until midday, in the presence of men, women, and those capable of understanding; and the ears of all the people were attentively fixed upon the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw.

Exposition, Instruction, and Festal Rejoicing

4Ezra the scribe stood upon a raised wooden rostrum erected for the occasion, with Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah at his right hand, and Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam at his left. 5Ezra opened the book before the eyes of all the people, for he was positioned above them all, and when he opened it, all the people stood. 6Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God, and all the people responded, “Amen, AmenamenAmen,” raising their hands; then they bowed their heads and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.

7Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the other Levites explicated the LawlawLaw for the people while they remained in their places. 8They read from the book, from the LawlawLaw of God, with clarity, rendering its meaning so that the people understood the reading.

9Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who instructed the people said to all the people, “This day is holy to the LORD your God; do not mourn or weep.” For all the people wept when they heard the words of the LawlawLaw.

10Then he said to them, “Go on your way; eat rich fare and drink sweet wine, and send portions to those for whom nothing has been prepared, for this day is holy to our Lord. Do not be grieved, for the joy of the LORD is your strength.”

11So the Levites quieted all the people, saying, “Be still, for this day is holy; do not be grieved.”

12All the people then went to eat and drink, to send portions, and to celebrate with great joy, because they had understood the words declared to them.

The Feast of Booths and Its Renewal

13On the second day, the heads of the ancestral houses of all the people, together with the priests and Levites, gathered to Ezra the scribe to gain insight into the words of the LawlawLaw. 14They found written in the LawlawLaw that the LORD had commanded through Moses that the Israelites should dwell in booths during the feast of the seventh month.

15They also proclaimed throughout all their cities and in Jerusalem, saying, “Go out to the hills and bring branches of olive, wild olive, myrtle, palm, and other leafy trees to make booths, as it is written.”

16So the people went out and brought the branches and made booths for themselves—each on his own roof, and in their courts, and in the courts of the house of God, and in the plaza at the Water GategateGate, and in the plaza at the GategateGate of Ephraim.

17All the assembly of those who had returned from exile made booths and dwelt in them; for from the days of Jeshua son of Nun until that day the Israelites had not done so. And there was very great rejoicing. 18Day by day, from the first day to the last, he read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of God. They observed the feast for seven days, and on the eighth day there was a solemn assembly, according to the ordinance.
