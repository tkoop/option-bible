Admonition Against the Allurements of the Seductress

1My son, attend to my wisdom; incline your ear to my understanding. 2So that you may preserve prudence, and your lips may safeguard knowledge.

3For the lips of a wanton woman drip honey, and her palate is smoother than oil. 4Yet her end is bitter as wormwood, and sharp as a two-edged sword. 5Her feet descend into death, and her steps lay hold on Sheol. 6She does not contemplate the path of life; her courses wander, and she is oblivious to it.

The Calamitous Consequences of Indulgence

7Now, therefore, my sons, hearken unto me, and do not depart from the words of my mouth. 8Keep your way far from her, and do not approach the door of her house.

9Lest you give your honourhonourhonor to others, and your years to the cruel. 10Lest strangers be sated with your strength, and your labourslabourslabors enrich a foreigner's house. 11And you groan at your latter end, when your flesh and body are consumed.

12And you say, “How I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof! 13I did not obey the voice of my teachers, nor incline my ear to my instructors. 14I had nearly been consumed by every evil in the midst of the assembly and congregation.”

The Sanctity and Blessing of Conjugal Love

15Drink water from your own cistern, and running water from your own well. 16Should your springs be scattered abroad, your streams in the streets? 17Let them be for you alone, and not for strangers with you.

18Let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice in the wife of your youth. 19A lovely deer, a graceful doe; let her breasts satisfy you at all times; be intoxicated always in her love. 20Why, my son, should you be intoxicated with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a foreigner?

Divine Omniscience and the Sinner's Self-Destruction

21For the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, and HeheHe makes level all his paths. 22His own iniquities shall capture the wicked, and he shall be held with the cords of his sin. 23He shall die for lack of discipline, and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.
