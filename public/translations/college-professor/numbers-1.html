Divine Mandate for a National Enumeration

1On the first day of the second month, in the second year after Israel's departure from Egypt, the LORD addressed Moses within the tent of meeting in the wilderness of Sinai, declaring, 2“Take a census of the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, organized by their clans and patriarchal houses, reckoning every male by name and by head. 3You and Aaron shall enumerate those twenty years of age and older, every man fit to march in Israel's host, mustering them according to their military divisions. 4And with you there shall be one man from each tribe, each the head of his patriarchal house.

5“These are the names of the men who shall stand with you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur; 6from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai; 7from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab; 8from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar; 9from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; 10from the sons of Joseph—from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur; 11from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni; 12from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; 13from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran; 14from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel; 15from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.”

16These were the men summoned from the congregation—leaders of their ancestral tribes and heads of Israel's thousands. 17Moses and Aaron took these men, who had been designated by name, 18and on the first day of the second month they convened the whole congregation; the people registered themselves by clans and patriarchal houses, by the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, head by head. 19Thus he enumerated them in the wilderness of Sinai, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Muster of the Tribal Hosts

20The descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 21The enumeration of the tribe of Reuben amounted to forty-six thousand five hundred. 22The descendants of Simeon were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 23The enumeration of the tribe of Simeon amounted to fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

24The descendants of Gad were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 25The enumeration of the tribe of Gad amounted to forty-five thousand six hundred fifty. 26The descendants of Judah were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 27The enumeration of the tribe of Judah amounted to seventy-four thousand six hundred.

28The descendants of Issachar were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 29The enumeration of the tribe of Issachar amounted to fifty-four thousand four hundred. 30The descendants of Zebulun were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 31The enumeration of the tribe of Zebulun amounted to fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

32The descendants of Joseph—namely, the descendants of Ephraim—were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 33The enumeration of the tribe of Ephraim amounted to forty thousand five hundred. 34The descendants of Manasseh were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 35The enumeration of the tribe of Manasseh amounted to thirty-two thousand two hundred.

36The descendants of Benjamin were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 37The enumeration of the tribe of Benjamin amounted to thirty-five thousand four hundred. 38The descendants of Dan were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 39The enumeration of the tribe of Dan amounted to sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

40The descendants of Asher were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 41The enumeration of the tribe of Asher amounted to forty-one thousand five hundred. 42The descendants of Naphtali were recorded by their generations, clans, and patriarchal houses, every male from twenty years old and upward who could go out to war. 43The enumeration of the tribe of Naphtali amounted to fifty-three thousand four hundred.

44These were the men who were enumerated—whom Moses and Aaron enumerated together with the leaders of Israel, twelve men, each representing his patriarchal house. 45All the enumerated among the sons of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, every man able to go to war in Israel— 46their total was six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty. 47But the Levites were not enumerated among them by their fathers' tribe.

Levitical Exemption and Tabernacle Stewardship

48Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 49“Only the tribe of Levi you shall not enumerate, nor take their sum among the sons of Israel. 50Instead, appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, over all its furnishings, and over everything belonging to it; they shall carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings, minister to it, and encamp around the tabernacle. 51When the tabernacle sets out, the Levites shall take it down; when it is to be pitched, they shall erect it; and any unauthorized person who approaches shall be put to death.

52“The sons of Israel shall encamp by their armies, each man by his own camp and each man by his own standard. 53But the Levites shall encamp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that wrath may not fall upon the congregation of the sons of Israel; and the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of the testimony.”

54Thus the sons of Israel did according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses; so they did.
