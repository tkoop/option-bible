A Festal Call to Worship and Divine Remembrance

1Sing exultantly to God, our strength; shout triumphantly to the God of Jacob. 2Lift up a song and beat the tambourine, the melodious lyre together with the harp. 3Blow the trumpet at the new moon, at the full moon, on the day of our festival. 4For this is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob.

The LORD's Deliverance from Egypt and Covenant Demand

5He established it as a testimony in Joseph when HeheHe marched out against the land of Egypt; I heard a tongue I had not known. 6I relieved his shoulder of its burden; his hands were freed from the basket. 7You called in distress, and I delivered you; I answered you in the secret place of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

8Hear, O MymyMy people, and I will testify against you; O Israel, if you would but listen to MemeMe! 9There shall be no strange god among you, nor shall you bow down to a foreign god. 10I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

Israel's Stubbornness and the Blessings Forfeited

11My people did not listen to MymyMy voice, and Israel refused to obey MemeMe. 12So I gave them up to the stubbornness of their hearts; they walked in their own counsels. 13If only MymyMy people would listen to MemeMe, that Israel might walk in MymyMy ways! 14I would soon subdue their enemies and turn MymyMy hand against their foes. 15Those who hate the LORD would cringe before HimhimHim, and their doom would last forever. 16He would feed you with the finest wheat; with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.
