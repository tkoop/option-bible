The Deluge Abates and the Ark Settles

1When God remembered Noah, and every living creature, and all the livestock that were with him in the ark, HeheHe caused a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters abated. 2The fountains of the deep and the windows of Heavenheavenheaven were closed, and the rain from Heavenheavenheaven was withheld. 3The waters steadily retreated from the earth in a continual ebb and flow, and at the end of one hundred fifty days they had markedly diminished. 4The ark came to rest upon the mountains of Ararat on the seventeenth day of the seventh month. 5The waters continued to abate until the tenth month, and on the first day of that month the summits of the mountains became visible.

The Raven and the Dove Are Dispatched

6At the end of forty days Noah opened the window he had made in the ark. 7He sent forth the raven, and it went out and returned repeatedly until the waters had dried from the earth. 8Then he sent out the dove to ascertain whether the waters had abated from the face of the ground. 9But the dove found no resting place for the sole of its foot, and it returned to him at the ark, because the waters still covered the whole EarthEarthearth; so he extended his hand, took it, and brought it back into the ark beside him. 10He waited yet another seven days and again sent the dove out of the ark. 11In the evening it returned to him, and there in its beak was a freshly plucked olive leaf; then Noah knew that the waters had receded from the earth. 12He waited another seven days and sent out the dove, but it did not return to him again.

The Ground Dries and the Ark Is Vacated

13In the six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the waters had dried from the earth. Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and behold, the surface of the ground was dry. 14In the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.

15Then God spoke to Noah, saying, 16"Come out of the ark—you and your wife, your sons and your sons' wives with you. 17Bring out with you every living creature of all flesh that is with you—birds, beasts, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth—so that they may swarm upon the earth and be fruitful and multiply upon it."

18So Noah came out, together with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives. 19Every animal, every creeping thing, and every bird—everything that moves on the earth—went out of the ark according to their families.

Noah's Sacrifice and the Divine Promise of Stability

20Then Noah built an altar to the LORD and took from every clean animal and every clean bird, and offered burnt offerings on the altar. 21The LORD smelled the soothing aroma, and the LORD said in HishisHis heart, "I will not again curse the ground on account of man, for the inclination of man's heart is evil from his youth; nor will I again strike down every living thing, as I have done. 22As long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night shall never cease."
