The Canaanite Kings Forge a Unified Front

1When all the kings who held sway on the western bank of the Jordan—in the hill country, in the lowlands, and along the entire littoral of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea opposite Lebanon, namely the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—learned of these events, 2they coalesced into a single coalition to wage war against Joshua and against Israel.

The Gibeonites Contrive an Elaborate Deception

3But when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had wrought against Jericho and Ai, they too resorted to artifice. 4They set out bearing provisions, loading their donkeys with frayed sacks and with wineskins that were antiquated, fissured, and patched. 5Their sandals were worn and patched upon their feet, their garments were threadbare, and all their victuals consisted of dry bread that had become crumbled and moldy.

6They repaired to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have journeyed from a far country; therefore now ratify a covenant with us."

7The Israelites said to the Hivite, "Perhaps you dwell in our midst; how then could we ratify a covenant with you?"

8"We are your servants," they replied to Joshua.

Joshua said to them, "Who are you, and from what land have you come?"

9Your servants have come from an exceedingly distant land, on account of the name of the LORD your God, for we have heard the report of HimhimHim and all that HeheHe did in Egypt, 10and all that HeheHe did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan—to Sihon king of Heshbon and to Og king of Bashan, who reigned at Ashtaroth. 11Therefore our elders and all the inhabitants of our land said to us, "Take provisions in your hand for the journey, go to meet them, and say to them, 'We are your servants; now make a covenant with us.'" 12This bread of ours was warm when we took it from our houses on the day we set out to come to you; but now, behold, it is dry and crumbled. 13And these wineskins were new when we filled them; behold, they have burst; and these garments and sandals of ours have become exceedingly worn because of the very long journey."

The Treaty Is Ratified Without Divine Counsel

14The men took some of their provisions, yet they did not seek counsel from the LORD. 15Joshua made peace with them and entered into a covenant with them to preserve their lives; and the leaders of the congregation swore an oath to them.

16At the end of three days, after they had entered into a covenant with them, it was discovered that they were near and were dwelling in their midst. 17The Israelites set out and reached their cities on the third day—Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim.

The Oath Is HonouredHonouredHonored and the Gibeonites Consigned to Servitude

18Yet the Israelites did not smite them, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel; and the whole congregation murmured against the leaders. 19All the leaders said to the whole congregation, "We have sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them. 20This is what we will do to them: we will let them live, lest wrath come upon us because of the oath that we swore to them." 21The leaders said to them, "Let them live." So they became hewers of wood and drawers of water for the whole congregation, just as the leaders had spoken to them.

22Joshua summoned them and said to them, "Why have you deceived us, saying, 'We are very far from you,' when in fact you dwell among us? Now therefore you are accursed, and servitude shall never be lifted from you: you shall be hewers of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God."

24They answered Joshua, "Because it was surely told to your servants that the LORD your God had commanded HishisHis servant Moses to give you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land before you; therefore we were exceedingly afraid for our lives because of you, and we have done this thing. 25And now, behold, we are in your hand; deal with us as seems good and right in your eyes."

26So he did to them and delivered them out of the hand of the Israelites, so that they did not put them to death. 27That day Joshua appointed them hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and for the altar of the LORD, in the place that HeheHe would choose, to this day.
