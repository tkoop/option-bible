Saul’s Renewed Pursuit and David’s Refuge at En Gedi

1Upon Saul’s return from pursuing the Philistines, he was informed that David was in the wilderness of En Gedi. 2Saul therefore selected three thousand chosen men from all Israel and went to seek David and his men upon the RocksrocksRocks of the Wild GoatsgoatsGoats. 3He came to the sheepfolds beside the road, where there was a cave; Saul entered it to relieve himself, while David and his men were sitting in the innermost recesses of the cave.

4David’s men said to him, “Behold, this is the day of which the LORD spoke to you: ‘Behold, I am giving your enemy into your hand, and you shall do to him whatever seems good to you.’” Then David arose and stealthily cut off the corner of Saul’s robe. 5Afterward David’s conscience smote him because he had cut off the corner of Saul’s robe.

6He said to his men, “The LORD forbid that I should do such a thing to my lord, the LORD’s anointed, by stretching out my hand against him; for he is the LORD’s anointed.”

7So David restrained his men with these words and did not permit them to rise against Saul. Then Saul rose from the cave and went on his way.

David’s Public Vindication Before Saul

8Afterward David arose, went out of the cave, and called after Saul, “My lord the king!” When Saul looked behind him, David bowed with his face to the ground and prostrated himself.

9David said to Saul, “Why do you give credence to the words of men who say, ‘David seeks your harm’? 10Behold, this day your eyes have seen how the LORD gave you into my hand today in the cave, and some urged me to kill you, but I spared you; for I said, ‘I will not stretch out my hand against my lord, for he is the LORD’s anointed.’ 11See, my father, see the corner of your robe in my hand; for in that I cut off the corner of your robe and did not kill you, know and see that there is no evil or rebellion in my hand, and I have not sinned against you, though you hunt my life to take it.”

12“May the LORD judge between me and you, and may the LORD avenge me upon you; but my hand shall not be against you. 13As the ancient proverb declares, ‘Wickedness proceeds from the wicked’; but my hand shall not be against you. 14After whom has the king of Israel come out? After whom do you pursue? After a dead dog? After a single flea? 15Therefore may the LORD be judge and adjudicate between me and you, and may HeheHe see and contend for my cause and deliver me out of your hand.”

Saul’s Repentant Acknowledgment

16When David had finished speaking these words to Saul, Saul said, “Is this your voice, my son David?” And Saul lifted up his voice and wept.

17“You are more righteous than I, for you have repaid me with good, whereas I have repaid you with evil. 18You have declared this day how well you have dealt with me, in that when the LORD gave me into your hand, you did not kill me.”

19“For if a man finds his enemy, will he let him go away unharmed? So may the LORD reward you with good for what you have done to me this day. 20Now I know that you shall surely be king, and that the kingdom of Israel shall be established in your hand. 21Swear to me therefore by the LORD that you will not cut off my offspring after me and that you will not destroy my name from my father’s house.”

22David swore to Saul. Then Saul went to his home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.
