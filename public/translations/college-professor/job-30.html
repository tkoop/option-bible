The Contempt of the Degenerate

1Yet now they deride me—young men whose days are fewer than mine, and whose fathers I should have scorned to rank even with the dogs that guarded my flock. 2Of what avail to me was the strength of their hands, seeing that their vigourvigourvigor had already decayed? 3Gaunt with deprivation and famine, they gnawed the parched EarthEarthearth in a desolate and ruinous wasteland; 4they gathered mallow from the scrub and made the roots of the broom shrub their sustenance.

5They were expelled from human society, and men shouted after them as though pursuing a thief. 6They took refuge in the clefts of ravines, in subterranean cavities and among the rocks; 7they brayed among the scrub and huddled together beneath the nettles. 8A brood of fools and of men without name, they were driven from the land.

Public Contempt and Unrestrained Hostility

9And now I have become their taunt; I am a byword among them. 10They hold me in abomination and keep aloof; they do not scruple to spit in my face. 11Because HeheHe has unstrung my bow and afflicted me, they have cast off restraint in my presence. 12On my right the rabble rise up; they thrust aside my feet and cast up against me their paths of destruction.

13They destroy my path; they further my ruin, and they need no accomplice. 14They advance as through a wide breach; amid the ruin they surge forward. 15Terrors are turned upon me; my dignity is driven away like the wind; my deliverance has vanished like a cloud.

Divine Affliction and the Collapse of Hope

16And now my life is poured out within me; days of affliction have seized me. 17Night pierces my bones, and my gnawing pains know no rest. 18By HishisHis great power HeheHe seizes my garment; HeheHe chokes me with the collar of my tunic. 19He has cast me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.

20I cry out to YouyouYou, but YouyouYou do not answer; I stand, and YouyouYou merely look at me. 21You have turned cruel toward me; with the might of YouryourYour hand YouyouYou persecute me. 22You lift me to the wind and drive me before it; YouyouYou dissolve me in the storm. 23For I know that YouyouYou will bring me to death, to the house appointed for all the living.

A Plea from the Depths of Suffering

24Yet would HeheHe not stretch out HishisHis hand against a heap of ruins, when one cries for help in his destruction? 25Did I not weep for those in trouble? Was I not grieved for the needy? 26Yet when I hoped for good, evil came; when I looked for light, darkness fell.

27My insides churn and find no rest; days of affliction confront me. 28I go about darkened, though not by the sun; I rise in the assembly and cry for help. 29I have become a brother to jackals and a companion to ostriches. 30My skin grows black and peels; my bones burn with fever. 31My lyre is tuned to mourning, and my pipe to the sound of weeping.
