Asa’s Alliance with Ben-hadad of Aram

1In the thirty-sixth year of Asa’s reign, Baasha, king of Israel, advanced against Judah and fortified Ramah, intending to prevent anyone from going out or coming in to Asa, king of Judah. 2Asa then brought out silver and gold from the treasuries of the house of the the LORD and from the royal palace, and sent them to Ben-hadad, king of Aram, who was residing in Damascus, with this message:

3“Let there be a covenant between me and you, as there was between my father and your father. See, I have sent you silver and gold; go, break your covenant with Baasha, king of Israel, so that he may withdraw from me.”

4Ben-hadad heeded King Asa and dispatched the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel; they struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the store cities of Naphtali. 5When Baasha heard of it, he ceased fortifying Ramah and abandoned his work. 6Then King Asa took all Judah, and they carried away the stones and timber of Ramah that Baasha had been using; with them Asa built Geba and Mizpah.

Hanani’s Prophetic Rebuke of Asa

7At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa, king of Judah, and said to him, “Because you relied on the king of Aram and did not rely on the the LORD your God, the army of the king of Aram has escaped your hand. 8Were not the Cushites and Libyans a vast army with very many chariots and horsemen? Yet when you relied on the the LORD, HeheHe delivered them into your hand. 9For the eyes of the the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole EarthEarthearth, to strengthen those whose heart is fully devoted to HimhimHim. You have acted foolishly in this; therefore, from now on you will have wars.”

10Asa was enraged at the seer and imprisoned him, for he was furious with him over this matter; at that time Asa also oppressed some of the people.

The Record of Asa’s Reign and His Death

11Now the acts of Asa, from first to last, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 12In the thirty-ninth year of his reign, Asa developed a severe disease in his feet; yet even in his illness he did not seek the the LORD, but consulted physicians. 13Asa then rested with his fathers and died in the forty-first year of his reign. 14They buried him in the tomb he had hewn for himself in the CitycityCity of David, and laid him on a bier filled with spices and various kinds of incense—a blend prepared by the perfumers. They also burned a very great fire for him.
