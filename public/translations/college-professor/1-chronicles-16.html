The Ark Is Brought to Jerusalem

1They brought the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 2When David had finished offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. 3He distributed to every Israelite, man and woman alike, a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake.

Levites Appointed for the Ark's Service

4He appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the LORD, to invoke, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel. 5Asaph was the chief, then Zechariah, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel, with harps and lyres; and Asaph sounded the cymbals. 6Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were to blow trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God. 7On that day David first committed to Asaph and his brothers the task of thanking the LORD.

A Psalm of Thanksgiving

13O offspring of Israel, HishisHis servant, children of Jacob, HishisHis chosen ones, 14HeheHe is the LORD our God; HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are in all the earth. 15Remember HishisHis covenant forever, the word HeheHe commanded for a thousand generations, 16the covenant HeheHe made with Abraham, and HishisHis oath to Isaac, 17which HeheHe established for Jacob as a statute, and for Israel as an everlasting covenant, saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance," 18when you were few in number, indeed very few, and sojourners in it, 19wandering from nation to nation and from one kingdom to another people. 20He allowed no one to oppress them; HeheHe rebuked kings on their behalf, 21saying, "Do not touch MymyMy anointed ones; do MymyMy prophets no harm."

Worship Maintained in Jerusalem and Gibeon

37So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark continually, as the daily service required. 38Obed-edom and his brothers, sixty-eight, together with Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah, served as gatekeepers. 39Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests served before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place in Gibeon, 40to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, and to do all that is written in the LawlawLaw of the LORD, which HeheHe commanded Israel. 41With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest who were chosen and expressly named to give thanks to the LORD, because HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 42Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets and cymbals and the other instruments for the songs of God, and the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate. 43Then all the people departed, each to his own home, and David went home to bless his household.
