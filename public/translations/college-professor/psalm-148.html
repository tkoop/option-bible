Hallelujah: A Cosmic Summons to Extol the Creator

1Hallelujah! Praise the LORD from the heavens; extol HimhimHim in the loftiest heights. 2Praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis angels; praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis celestial hosts. 3Praise HimhimHim, sun and moon; praise HimhimHim, every shining star. 4Praise HimhimHim, highest heavens, and the waters beyond the heavens.

5Let them extol the name of the LORD, for HeheHe issued the command, and they were brought into being. 6He established them for all eternity; HeheHe promulgated a decree that shall never be revoked.

Terrestrial and Human Acclamation of the Divine Sovereign

7Praise the LORD from the earth—great sea creatures and all oceanic depths, 8fire and hail, snow and vapourvapourvapor, and the storm wind executing HishisHis word; 9mountains and every hill, fruit trees and all cedars; 10wild beasts and all livestock, creeping things and winged birds.

11Kings of the earth and all nations, princes and every judge of the earth; 12young men and maidens together, the aged alongside children.

The Exaltation of the LORD's Name and the Elevation of His People

13Let them extol the name of the LORD, for HishisHis name alone is exalted; HishisHis splendoursplendoursplendor surpasses EarthEarthearth and Heavenheavenheaven. 14He has exalted a horn for HishisHis people—a renown for all HishisHis faithful, for the children of Israel, a people intimate with HimhimHim. Hallelujah!
