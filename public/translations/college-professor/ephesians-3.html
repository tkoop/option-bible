Paul’s Stewardship of the Revealed Mystery

1For this cause I, Paul, am held as a prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles— 2if, indeed, you have heard of the stewardship of God’s grace entrusted to me for your benefit, 3namely, that the mystery was disclosed to me through revelation, as I have cursorily noted before, 4by means of which, upon your reading, you may apprehend my insight into the mystery of Christ, 5a mystery that in earlier generations was not revealed to the sons of men, but has now been unveiled in the SpiritspiritSpirit to HishisHis holy apostles and prophets: 6that the Gentiles are co-heirs, co-members of the body, and co-participants in the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel, 7of which I became a minister in accordance with the gift of God’s grace bestowed upon me by the operative efficacy of HishisHis power.

The Cosmic Manifestation of Divine Wisdom

8To me, the least of all the saints, this grace was granted: to proclaim to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ, 9and to illuminate for all people what is the administration of the mystery concealed for ages in God, who created all things, 10so that now, through the church, the manifold wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms, 11in accordance with the eternal purpose that HeheHe accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord, 12in whom we possess boldness and confident access through faith in HimhimHim. 13Therefore I entreat you not to be disheartened by my tribulations on your behalf, for they constitute your glory.

Intercessory Prayer for Spiritual Empowerment

14For this reason I bow my knees before the FatherfatherFather, 15from whom every family in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth derives its name, 16that HeheHe would grant you, according to the riches of HishisHis glory, to be strengthened with power through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit in the inner man, 17so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, you being rooted and grounded in love, 18and that you may possess the strength to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth, 19and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

Doxology of Divine Power and Glory

20Now to HimhimHim who is able to do exceedingly abundantly beyond all that we ask or conceive, according to the power at work within us, 21to HimhimHim be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
