The Command to Record the Word

1The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying, 2“Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: ‘Write for yourself in a book all the words that I have spoken to you.’ 3For behold, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will restore the fortunes of MymyMy people Israel and Judah, says the LORD, and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it.”

The Great Day of Jacob’s Distress and Deliverance

4These are the words that the LORD spoke concerning Israel and Judah. 5For thus says the LORD: “We have heard a voice of trembling, of dread, and there is no peace. 6Ask now, and see: can a male give birth? Why then do I see every man with his hands upon his loins like a woman in labourlabourlabor, and every face turned ashen? 7Alas, for that day is great, without equal; it is a time of distress for Jacob, yet he shall be saved out of it.”

8“And it shall come to pass in that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, that I will break his yoke from off your neck, and I will burst your bonds, and strangers shall no longer subject him to servitude. 9They shall serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them.”

10“Therefore do not fear, O Jacob MymyMy servant, declares the LORD, nor be dismayed, O Israel; for behold, I will save you from afar, and your offspring from the land of their captivity. Jacob shall return, dwell in quiet, and be at ease, with none to terrify him. 11For I am with you to save you, declares the LORD; for I will bring to a full end all the nations among whom I have scattered you, but I will not bring you to a full end; yet I will chastise you with justice, and I will not leave you unpunished.”

The Incurable Wound and Its Healing

12“For thus says the LORD: ‘Your wound is incurable, and your bruise is grievous. 13There is no one to plead your cause, no remedy for your wound, no healing for you. 14All your lovers have forgotten you; they do not seek you. For I have struck you with the stroke of an enemy, with the chastisement of a ruthless one, because of the multitude of your iniquity, because your sins have multiplied. 15Why do you cry out over your wound? Your pain is incurable. Because of the multitude of your iniquity, because your sins have multiplied, I have done these things to you.’”

16“Therefore all who devour you shall be devoured, and all your adversaries shall go into captivity; those who plunder you shall be plundered, and all who prey upon you I will make a prey. 17For I will restore health to you, and I will heal you of your wounds, declares the LORD, because they have called you an outcast: ‘Zion, for whom no one inquires.’”

The Restoration of Jacob and the Coming Ruler

18Thus says the LORD: “Behold, I will restore the fortunes of Jacob’s tents, and I will have compassion on his dwellings; and the city shall be rebuilt upon its mound, and the palace shall stand in its proper place. 19Out of them shall come thanksgiving and the voice of merrymakers; and I will multiply them, and they shall not be diminished; and I will glorify them, and they shall not be small. 20His children shall be as in former days, and his assembly shall be established before MemeMe; and I will punish all who oppress him.”

21“And his leader shall be one of them, and his ruler shall come forth from their midst; and I will bring him near, and he shall approach MemeMe; for who is he who would dare devote his heart to approach MemeMe? declares the LORD. 22And you shall be MymyMy people, and I will be your God.”

23“Behold, the whirlwind of the LORD has gone forth in fury, a sweeping tempest; it will burst upon the head of the wicked. 24The fierce anger of the LORD will not turn back until HeheHe has fully accomplished the intents of HishisHis heart. In the latter days you will understand this.”
