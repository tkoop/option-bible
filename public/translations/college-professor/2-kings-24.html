Jehoiakim's Vassalage and the LORD's Wrath

1During Jehoiakim's reign, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, advanced against the land; Jehoiakim served as his vassal for three years, after which he defected and rebelled. 2The LORD, in HishisHis wrath, dispatched against him bands of Chaldeans, Arameans, Moabites, and Ammonites, and sent them against Judah to destroy it, in fulfillmentfulfilmentfulfillment of the word the LORD had spoken through HishisHis servants the prophets. 3This calamity befell Judah solely by the LORD's decree, so that HeheHe might expel them from HishisHis presence, on account of the sins of Manasseh and all that he had wrought. 4Moreover, there was the innocent blood he had spilled—Jerusalem itself had been saturated with it—and the LORD was unwilling to forgive.

5As for the remaining deeds of Jehoiakim and all that he accomplished, are they not recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 6Jehoiakim then died and was gathered to his ancestors, and his son Jehoiachin succeeded him upon the throne. 7Nor did the king of Egypt venture forth from his own territory again, for the king of Babylon had seized everything from the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt to the Euphrates that had once belonged to Egypt.

Jehoiachin's Surrender and the First Babylonian Exile

8Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned in Jerusalem for three months. His mother was Nehushta, daughter of Elnathan, from Jerusalem. 9He practisedpractisedpracticed what was evil in the sight of the LORD, emulating in every respect the conduct of his father. 10It was in those days that the officers of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, advanced against Jerusalem, and the city was subjected to siege. 11Nebuchadnezzar himself then marched against the city while his forces maintained the siege. 12Whereupon Jehoiachin, king of Judah, surrendered to the king of Babylon—together with his mother, his courtiers, his officials, and his eunuchs—and the king of Babylon took him captive in the eighth year of that monarch's reign.

13He stripped from that place all the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the royal palace, and he hacked to pieces all the gold vessels that Solomon, king of Israel, had fashioned for the temple of the LORD, just as the LORD had foretold. 14He exiled the entire populace of Jerusalem—its princes, its warriors of valourvalourvalor, its artisans and smiths, some ten thousand captives in all—leaving behind only the most destitute of the land. 15He carried Jehoiachin off to Babylon, along with the queen mother, the king’s wives, his officials, and the leading men of the land; these he led into captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon. 16There were seven thousand fighting men, together with a thousand craftsmen and smiths—all of them valiant men trained for war—and the king of Babylon brought them all captive to Babylon.

Zedekiah's Installation and Final Rebellion

17The king of Babylon then installed Mattaniah, Jehoiachin’s uncle, as king in his place, and changed his name to Zedekiah. 18Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for eleven years. His mother was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah. 19He, too, did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, following in all respects the pattern set by Jehoiakim. 20For it was through the LORD’s anger that events unfolded in Jerusalem and Judah until HeheHe cast them from HishisHis presence; and Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.
