The Futility of Human EndeavourEndeavourEndeavor Apart from the LORD

1Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labourlabourlabor in vain; unless the LORD watches over the city, the watchman keeps vigil in vain. 2It is futile for you to rise early and sit up late, toiling for bread; for HeheHe gives sleep to those HeheHe loves.

Children as the LORD's Heritage and Reward

3Behold, children are a heritage from the LORD, and the fruit of the womb is HishisHis reward. 4Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are the children of one's youth. 5Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them; they shall not be put to shame when they contend with their enemies at the gate.
