Israel's Security in the Eternal God

26"There is none like God, O Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to your help, and in HishisHis majesty through the skies. 27The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He will drive out the enemy before you and say, 'Destroy!' 28So Israel shall dwell in safety, the fountain of Jacob alone, in a land of grain and new wine; yes, his heavens shall drop down dew. 29Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help and the sword of your majesty! Your enemies shall cower before you, and you shall tread upon their high places."
