Psalm 69: A Davidic Lament amid the Surging Waters of Affliction

For the choirmaster, to the melody of “Lilies”; a psalm of David.

1Deliver me, O God, for the waters have risen to my very throat. 2I am sinking in the fathomless mire, where no footing may be found; I have plunged into the abyssal waters, and the torrent has swept over me. 3I have worn myself out with crying; my throat is hoarse, and my eyes grow dim while I wait for my God. 4My foes who hate me without cause are more than the hairs of my head; those who would destroy me have grown powerful, and they hate me with lies; though I stole nothing, I must now make restitution.

5O God, YouyouYou know my folly; my trespasses are not concealed from YouyouYou. 6May those who hope in YouyouYou not be ashamed because of me, O Lord, the LORD of Hosts; may those who seek YouyouYou not be dishonoureddishonoureddishonored because of me, O God of Israel. 7For I have borne reproach on YouryourYour account; shame has veiled my face. 8I have become a stranger to my brothers, an alien to my mother's sons.

Zeal for the House and the Scorn of the Mockers

9For zeal for YouryourYour house has devoured me, and the insults of those who insult YouyouYou have fallen upon me. 10When I wept and afflicted my soul with fasting, my very devotion became a reproach to me. 11I made sackcloth my garment, and I became a byword to them. 12Those who sit in the gate mock me, and I am the song of the drunkards.

13But as for me, my prayer is to YouyouYou, O the LORD, at a time of favourfavourfavor; in the greatness of YouryourYour steadfast love, O God, answer me in the truth of YouryourYour salvation. 14Rescue me from the mire, and do not let me sink; deliver me from my enemies and from the depths of the waters. 15Let not the torrent of waters overwhelm me, nor the abyss swallow me, nor the pit close its mouth over me. 16Answer me, O the LORD, for YouryourYour steadfast love is good; in the greatness of YouryourYour compassion, turn to me. 17Do not hide YouryourYour face from YouryourYour servant, for I am in anguish; answer me speedily. 18Draw near to my soul and redeem it; ransom me on account of my foes.

Reproach, Gall, and the Malice of Enemies

19You know my reproach, my shame, and my disgrace; all my oppressors are before YouyouYou. 20Reproach has shattered my heart, and I am sick; I waited for pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none. 21They gave me gall for my food, and in my thirst they made me drink vinegar.

Imprecations against the Wicked

22Let their table become a snare before them, and their well-being a trap. 23Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and make their loins shake unceasingly. 24Pour out YouryourYour wrath upon them, and let YouryourYour fierce anger pursue them. 25Let their encampment be desolate; let no one inhabit their tents. 26For they persecute the one YouyouYou have struck, and they rehearse the anguish of those YouyouYou have wounded. 27Lay guilt upon their guilt, and let them not enter into YouryourYour righteousness. 28Let them be erased from the book of life, and let them not be inscribed among the righteous.

Praise, Salvation, and the Restoration of Zion

29But I am afflicted and in pain; may YouryourYour salvation, O God, exalt me. 30I will praise the name of God with song, and I will magnify HimhimHim with thanksgiving. 31This will please the the LORD more than an ox or a bull with horns and hooves. 32The humble have seen it and rejoice; you who seek God, let your hearts live. 33For the the LORD hears the poor, and does not despise HishisHis prisoners. 34Let Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth praise HimhimHim, the seas and everything that moves within them. 35For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah, so that they may dwell there and inherit it. 36The offspring of HishisHis servants shall inherit it, and those who love HishisHis name shall dwell therein.
