1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, raise a lamentation over Tyre, 3and say to Tyre, who dwells at the entrances of the sea and traffics as a merchant among the peoples to many coastlands: Thus says the Lord, the LORD: You, Tyre, have said, ‘I am perfect in beauty.’”

The City’s Magnificent Construction

4Your borders are in the heart of the seas; your builders have perfected your beauty. 5They built all your planks of fir from Senir; they took cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you. 6They made your oars of oaks from Bashan; your deck they fashioned of ivory inlaid with boxwood from the isles of the Kittim. 7Fine embroidered linen from Egypt was your sail, to be your banner; blue and purple from the isles of Elishah was your awning.

Those Who Manned and Fortified Her

8The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers; your wise men, Tyre, were within you; they were your pilots. 9The elders of Gebal and its wise men were in you, repairing your seams; all the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you, to barter for your merchandise. 10Men of Persia, Lud, and Put were in your army, your men of war; they hung shield and helmet in you; they gave you splendoursplendoursplendor. 11Men of Arvad and your army were upon your walls all around, and the Gammadim were in your towers; they hung their shields all around your walls; they perfected your beauty.

The Vast Commerce of Tyre

12Tarshish was your merchant because of the abundance of all wealth; they gave silver, iron, tin, and lead for your wares. 13Javan, Tubal, and Meshech were your traders; they gave human beings and bronze vessels for your merchandise. 14From Beth-togarmah they gave horses, war horses, and mules for your wares.

15The sons of Dedan were your traders; many coastlands were the market of your hand; they brought back your payment in ivory tusks and ebony. 16Aram was your merchant because of the abundance of your works; they gave turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and rubies in your wares. 17Judah and the land of Israel were your traders; they gave wheat of Minnith, millet, honey, oil, and balm for your merchandise.

18Damascus was your merchant because of the abundance of your works, because of the abundance of all wealth, with wine of Helbon and wool of Zahar. 19Dan and Javan from Uzal traded in your wares; wrought iron, cassia, and calamus were given in your merchandise. 20Dedan traded with you in saddlecloths for riding. 21Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were merchants of your hand; in lambs, rams, and goats they traded with you.

22The traders of Sheba and Raamah were your traders; they gave for your wares the best of all spices, all precious stones, and gold. 23Haran, Canneh, and Eden, the traders of Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad traded with you. 24These traded with you in choice garments, in blue garments and embroidered work, and in carpets of colouredcolouredcolored stuff bound with cords and made secure, in your marketplace.

Tyre’s Ruin and Universal Lamentation

25The ships of Tarshish were the carriers of your merchandise. You were filled and heavily laden in the heart of the seas. 26Your rowers have brought you into great waters; the east wind has shattered you in the heart of the seas. 27Your wealth, your wares, your merchandise, your mariners, your pilots, your caulkers, your traders, all your men of war, and your whole assembly within you will sink into the heart of the sea on the day of your ruin.

28The surrounding countryside will quake at the sound of the cry of your pilots. 29All who handle the oar, the mariners, and all the pilots of the sea will come down from their ships; they will stand on the land. 30They will raise their voices over you and cry out bitterly; they will cast dust on their heads and roll in ashes. 31They will make themselves bald for you and gird on sackcloth; they will weep over you in bitterness of soul, with bitter lamentation.

32In their wailing they will take up a lamentation for you and lament over you: “Who is like Tyre, like one destroyed in the midst of the sea?”

33When your wares went out from the seas, you satisfied many peoples; with the abundance of your wealth and your merchandise you enriched the kings of the earth. 34Now you are shattered by the seas in the depths of the waters; your merchandise and all your company have fallen in your midst.

35All the inhabitants of the coastlands are appalled at you; their kings shudder with horror; their faces are convulsed. 36The merchants among the peoples hiss at you; you have become a horror, and you shall be no more forever.
