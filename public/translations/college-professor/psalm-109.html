A Psalm of David: An Appeal to God Amidst Malicious Accusation

1For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, do not be silent, 2for the mouths of the wicked and the deceitful have opened against me; they have spoken against me with lying tongues. 3They encircle me with words of hatred and assail me without cause. 4In return for my love they accuse me, though I remain devoted to prayer. 5They repay me evil for good, and hatred for my love.

Imprecations Against the Unrighteous Adversary

6Appoint a wicked man over him, and let an accuser stand at his right hand. 7When he is brought to judgmentjudgementjudgment, let him emerge condemned, and let his prayer be reckoned as sin. 8May his days be few, and may another take his office. 9May his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow. 10May his children wander as beggars and implore alms; may they seek sustenance from their ruined homes. 11May the creditor seize all that he possesses, and may strangers plunder the fruit of his toil. 12May no one extend mercy to him, nor show favourfavourfavor to his fatherless children. 13May his posterity be cut off, and may their name be blotted out in the next generation.

14May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and may the sin of his mother never be blotted out. 15May they stand perpetually before the LORD, that HeheHe may cut off their memory from the earth. 16For he did not remember to show kindness, but pursued the poor and needy and the brokenhearted, seeking to put them to death. 17Because he loved cursing, may it come upon him; because he took no delight in blessing, may it remain far from him. 18He clothed himself in cursing as in a garment; may it enter his inward parts like water and penetrate his bones like oil. 19May it be to him like the cloak he wears and like the belt he constantly fastens around himself. 20May this be the LORD’s retribution to my accusers, to those who speak evil against my life.

A Plea for Divine Mercy and Vindication

21But YouyouYou, O the LORD, the Lord, deal graciously with me for the sake of YouryourYour name; because YouryourYour steadfast love is good, deliver me. 22For I am poor and needy, and my heart is stricken within me. 23I vanish like a shadow at its lengthening; I am shaken off like a locust. 24My knees grow weak from fasting, and my flesh has grown lean for lack of oil. 25I have become a reproach to them; when they see me, they shake their heads. 26Help me, O the LORD my God; save me according to YouryourYour lovingkindness. 27Let them know that this is YouryourYour hand, that YouyouYou, O the LORD, have done it. 28They may curse, but YouyouYou will bless; when they rise up, they will be put to shame, while YouryourYour servant rejoices. 29May my accusers be clothed with disgrace and wrapped in their own shame as in a robe.

Praise for the LORD’s Faithful Deliverance

30With my mouth I will give great thanks to the LORD; I will praise HimhimHim in the midst of the multitude. 31For HeheHe stands at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those who condemn his soul.
