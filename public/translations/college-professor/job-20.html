Zophar’s Second Discourse: The Fleeting Triumph of the Wicked

1Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

2“Therefore my reflections impel me to reply, because of the agitation within me. 3I hear a reproof that dishonoursdishonoursdishonors me, and my understanding spirit compels my answer. 4Do you not know this from antiquity, from the time humanity was established upon the earth, 5that the triumph of the wicked is brief and the joy of the godless but momentary? 6Though his exaltation ascend to the heavens and his head touch the clouds, 7he perishes forever like his own refuse; those who beheld him ask, ‘Where is he?’ 8He flies away like a dream and is not found; he is driven off like a vision of the night. 9The eye that saw him sees him no more, and his place no longer beholds him.

The Reversal of Fraudulent Prosperity

10“His children conciliate the impoverished, and his hands restore his ill-gotten wealth. 11His bones, though full of youthful vigourvigourvigor, lie down with him in the dust. 12Though evil is sweet in his mouth and he hides it beneath his tongue, 13though he savors it and will not relinquish it, but keeps it in the roof of his mouth, 14his food turns in his entrails and becomes the venom of asps within him. 15He swallows riches and vomits them up; God expels them from his belly. 16He suckles the poison of asps; the viper’s tongue slays him. 17He does not behold the streams, the rivers flowing with honey and curds. 18He returns the fruit of his labourlabourlabor without consuming it, and he cannot exult in the wealth of his exchange.

Divine Retribution and the Wicked Man’s Portion

19“For he has crushed and abandoned the poor; he has seized a house he did not build. 20Because he knew no quietude in his belly, he retains nothing he desires. 21Nothing remains for him to devour; therefore his prosperity does not endure. 22In the fullness of his sufficiency he is in straits, and every hand of misery comes against him. 23When he fills his belly, God sends HishisHis fierce anger upon him and rains it upon his flesh. 24He may flee from the iron weapon, but the bronze bow pierces him. 25He draws it forth, and it comes out of his back; the glittering point emerges from his gall; terrors come upon him. 26All darkness is reserved for his treasures; an unfanned fire consumes him and devours the remnant in his tent. 27The heavens reveal his iniquity, and the earth rises up against him. 28The increase of his house departs; it flows away in the day of HishisHis anger. 29This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage decreed to him by God.”
