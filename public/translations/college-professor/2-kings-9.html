Elisha Commissions a Prophet to Anoint Jehu

1Elisha the prophet summoned one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Prepare for action, take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-gilead. 2When you arrive there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi; then bring him away from his brothers and take him into an inner room. 3Take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and say, 'Thus says the LORD: I have anointed you king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee; do not wait."

4So the young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead.

5When he arrived, the commanders of the army were sitting together, and he said, "I have a message for you, commander."

Jehu said, "For which of us?"

He answered, "For you, commander."

6He arose, entered the house, poured the oil on his head, and said, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: I have anointed you king over the people of the LORD, over Israel. 7You shall strike down the house of Ahab your lord, and I will avenge the blood of MymyMy servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of the LORD at the hand of Jezebel. 8The whole house of Ahab shall perish; I will cut off from Ahab every male, both bond and free, in Israel. 9I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah. 10The dogs shall eat Jezebel in the plot of Jezreel, and no one shall bury her." Then he opened the door and fled.

Jehu Is Proclaimed King

11Jehu went out to the servants of his lord, and they said to him, "IsisIs all well? Why did this madman come to you?"

He said, "YouyouYou know the man and his rant."

12They said, "That is a lie; tell us now."

He said, "Thus and thus he said to me: 'Thus says the LORD: I have anointed you king over Israel.'"

13They hurried, each man took his garment and placed it beneath him on the bare steps; they blew the trumpet and said, "Jehu is king."

14Thus Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi conspired against Joram. Now Joram with all Israel had been guarding Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram.

15But King Joram had returned to Jezreel to be healed of the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him while he fought Hazael king of Aram. Jehu said, "If you are willing, let no fugitive leave the city to go and report in Jezreel."

16Then Jehu rode and went to Jezreel, for Joram was lying there; and Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to visit Joram.

Jehu Confronts the Kings at Jezreel

17The watchman standing on the tower in Jezreel saw the company of Jehu as he came and said, "I see a company."

Joram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them and say, 'Is it peace?'"

18So the horseman went to meet him and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'"

Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Turn behind me."

The watchman reported that the messenger had reached them but did not return.

19Then he sent a second horseman, who came to them and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'"

Jehu answered, "What have you to do with peace? Turn behind me."

20The watchman reported, "HeheHe reached them, but he is not coming back; and the driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives furiously."

21Joram said, "Harness." So they harnessed his chariot. Then Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his chariot, and they went to meet Jehu and found him in the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite.

22When Joram saw Jehu, he said, "IsisIs it peace, Jehu?"

He answered, "What peace, so long as the whoredoms of your mother Jezebel and her sorceries are so many?"

23Joram turned his horses and fled, and said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

24Jehu drew his bow and struck Joram between his shoulders; the arrow pierced his heart, and he sank down in his chariot.

25Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, "Take him up and throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember how you and I rode side by side behind Ahab his father, and the LORD laid this burden on him: 26'Surely I have seen the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons,' says the LORD, 'and I will repay you in this very plot,' says the LORD. Now take him up and throw him into the plot, according to the word of the LORD."

27When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by the way of Beth-haggan. Jehu pursued him and said, "Strike him down too," in the chariot. At the ascent of Gur, which is by Ibleam, he fled to Megiddo and died there.

28His servants carried him to Jerusalem and buried him with his fathers in his tomb in the city of David.

29In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah had begun to reign over Judah.

The Death of Jezebel at Jezreel

30Jehu came to Jezreel, and Jezebel heard of it. She painted her eyes, adorned her head, and looked out of the window.

31As Jehu entered the gate, she said, "IsisIs it peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?"

32He lifted his face toward the window and said, "Who is with me? Who?" Then two or three eunuchs looked out at him.

33He said, "Throw her down." So they threw her down, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and they trampled her underfoot.

34He went in, ate and drank, and said, "Attend to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

35When they went to bury her, they found no more of her than the skull, the feet, and the palms of her hands.

36They returned and told him, and he said, "This is the word of the LORD that HeheHe spoke by HishisHis servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'In the plot of Jezreel the dogs shall eat the flesh of Jezebel, 37and her corpse shall be as dung on the surface of the field in the plot of Jezreel, so that no one will say, "This is Jezebel."'"
