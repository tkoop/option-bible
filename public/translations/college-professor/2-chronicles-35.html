Josiah Restores the Passover

1In Jerusalem, Josiah observed the Passover to the LORD, and the paschal victim was slaughtered on the fourteenth day of the first month. 2He stationed the priests at their posts and strengthened them for the service of the house of the LORD.

3To the Levites, who instructed all Israel and were consecrated to the LORD, he said, “Place the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David, king of Israel, built; you shall no longer carry it on your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and HishisHis people Israel.”

4Prepare yourselves according to your ancestral houses, in your divisions, according to the written ordinance of David, king of Israel, and the written ordinance of Solomon his son. 5Stand in the holy place according to the divisions of the ancestral houses of your brothers, the sons of the people, and according to the division of the ancestral house of the Levites. 6Slaughter the Passover, consecrate yourselves, and prepare it for your brothers, that you may carry out the word of the LORD given through Moses.

Provisions for the Passover Celebration

7From his own flock Josiah contributed thirty thousand lambs and kids and three thousand cattle; all of these were for the Passover offerings for the people who were present. 8His officials likewise contributed willingly: Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the rulers of the house of God, gave the priests two thousand six hundred Passover offerings and three hundred cattle. 9Conaniah, together with his brothers Shemaiah and Nethanel, and Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad, the leaders of the Levites, contributed to the Levites five thousand Passover offerings and five hundred cattle.

10The service was thus prepared: the priests stood in their places and the Levites in their divisions, according to the king's command. 11They slaughtered the Passover; the priests sprinkled the blood, while the Levites flayed the victims. 12They set aside the burnt offerings to distribute them by divisions of the ancestral houses of the people, so that they might be offered to the LORD, as it is written in the BookbookBook of Moses; and they did the same with the cattle. 13They roasted the Passover over the fire according to the ordinance, boiled the holy portions in pots, cauldrons, and pans, and then quickly distributed them to all the people.

14Afterward they prepared for themselves and for the priests, because the priests, the sons of Aaron, were occupied until nightfall in offering the burnt offerings and the fat; so the Levites prepared for themselves and for the priests, the sons of Aaron. 15The singers, the sons of Asaph, stood at their stations according to the command of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, the king's seer; the gatekeepers were at each gate. They did not need to leave their service, because their brothers the Levites prepared everything for them. 16Thus all the service of the LORD was prepared that day: the Passover was kept and the burnt offerings were offered on the altar of the LORD, according to the command of King Josiah.

17The Israelites who were present kept the Passover at that time and then celebrated the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread for seven days. 18No Passover like it had been kept in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet; none of the kings of Israel had kept a Passover such as Josiah kept, with the priests, the Levites, all Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 19In the eighteenth year of Josiah's reign this Passover was observed.

Josiah's Fatal Encounter with Pharaoh Neco

20After all this, when Josiah had prepared the temple, Neco, king of Egypt, came up to fight at Carchemish on the Euphrates, and Josiah went out to meet him. 21Neco sent messengers to him, saying, “What have you to do with me, king of Judah? I am not coming against you today, but against the house with which I am at war; God has commanded me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me, so that HeheHe does not destroy you.”

22But Josiah would not turn away from him; instead, he disguised himself in order to fight, and he did not heed the words of Neco, which came from the mouth of God. So he came to fight in the valley of Megiddo. 23The archers shot King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, “Carry me away, for I am severely wounded.”

Mourning for Josiah

24His servants carried him out of the chariot, placed him in the second chariot that he had, and brought him to Jerusalem, where he died and was buried in the tombs of his fathers. All Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah. 25Jeremiah also lamented for Josiah, and all the male and female singers speak of Josiah in their laments to this day; they established them as an ordinance in Israel, and they are written in the Laments. 26The rest of the acts of Josiah, and his good deeds in accordance with the LawlawLaw of the LORD, 27and his earlier and later deeds, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Israel and Judah.
