The Righteous Are Gathered Away

1The righteous perish, yet none takes it to heart; the godly are gathered away, while no one discerns that the righteous are gathered away from evil. 2The upright one enters into peace and rests upon his bed.

The Wicked Summoned to JudgmentJudgementJudgment

3“Draw near here, you sons of the sorceress, seed of the adulterer and the prostitute. 4At whom do you make sport? At whom do you widen your mouth and lengthen your tongue? Are you not children of transgression, seed of falsehood? 5You who inflame yourselves among the oaks, beneath every verdant tree, slaughtering children in the valleys, beneath the clefts of the rocks. 6Your portion is among the smooth stones of the valley; they, they are your lot. You have poured out a drink offering to them; you have offered a grain offering. Shall I relent concerning these things? 7On a high and lofty mountain you have set your bed; there too you went up to sacrifice. 8Behind the door and the doorpost you set your memorial, for, apart from MemeMe, you uncovered your bed and ascended; you widened it and cut off their bed for yourself from them; you loved; you gazed upon a hand. 9You went to Molech with oil and multiplied your perfumes; you sent your envoys far away and debased yourself even to Sheol.

10“You wearied yourself in the abundance of your way; you did not say, ‘It is hopeless’; you found strength in your own hand; therefore you were not sick. 11And of whom have you been anxious and afraid, that you should lie? And MemeMe you did not remember; you did not set MemeMe upon your heart. Have I not been silent from of old, and yet you do not fear MemeMe? 12I will declare your righteousness and your works, and they will not profit you. 13When you cry out, let your gathered ones save you; but the wind will carry them all away, and a breath will take them. But he who takes refuge in MemeMe will inherit the land and possess MymyMy holy mountain.”

The High and Holy One Restores the Contrite

14And HeheHe will say, “Build up, build up, prepare the way, remove the stumbling block from the way of MymyMy people.”

15For thus says the HighhighHigh and Lofty OneoneOne who inhabits eternity, whose name is HolyholyHoly: “I dwell in the high and holy place, and with the contrite and lowly in spirit, to revive the spirit of the lowly and to revive the heart of the contrite. 16For I will not contend forever, nor will I always be angry, for the spirit would grow faint before MemeMe, and the breaths that I made. 17Because of the iniquity of his covetous gain I was angry and struck him; I hid MymyMy face and was angry, yet he went on in the way of his heart. 18I have seen his ways, and I will heal him; I will lead him and restore comforts to him and to his mourners. 19I create the fruit of the lips: Peace, peace to the far and to the near,” says the the LORD, “and I will heal him.”

No Peace for the Wicked

20But the wicked are like the tossing sea, for it cannot be quiet, and its waters toss up mire and mud. 21“There is no peace,” says MymyMy God, “for the wicked.”
