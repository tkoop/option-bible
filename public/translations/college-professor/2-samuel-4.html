The Enfeeblement of Saul’s House after Abner’s Demise

1When Saul’s son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands grew slack, and all Israel was thrown into dismay. 2Now Saul had a son, Ish-bosheth, and two men who commanded raiding bands: Baanah and Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the Benjaminites—for Beeroth was reckoned among Benjamin. 3The Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have sojourned there as aliens down to this very day.

4Jonathan, Saul’s son, had a son who was lame in both feet; he was five years old when the report concerning Saul and Jonathan reached Jezreel. His nurse seized him and fled, and in her precipitous flight he fell and was lamed; his name was Mephibosheth.

The Treacherous Assassination of Ish-bosheth

5Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, set out and arrived at the house of Ish-bosheth at the oppressive heat of noon, while he was taking his midday rest. 6They penetrated into the interior of the house under the pretencepretencepretense of fetching wheat, struck him in the abdomen, and then Rechab and his brother Baanah made their escape. 7They entered the house where he lay upon his bed in his sleeping chamber, struck him down and killed him, beheaded him, carried off his head, and travelledtravelledtraveled all night along the Arabah road.

David’s Juridical Condemnation of the Regicides

8They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, “Behold, the head of Ish-bosheth, son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life; and the the LORD has this day granted vengeance to my lord the king against Saul and his offspring.”

9David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, “As the the LORD lives, who has redeemed my soul from every tribulation, 10when one came to tell me, ‘Behold, Saul is dead,’ supposing himself the bearer of glad tidings, I seized him and put him to death at Ziklag—that was the recompense I gave him for his news. 11How much more, then, when wicked men have slain a righteous man in his own house upon his bed? Shall I not now demand his blood from your hand and eradicate you from the earth?”

12David then commanded the young men, and they put them to death, cut off their hands and their feet, and hanged them beside the pool in Hebron. As for the head of Ish-bosheth, they took it and buried it in Abner’s tomb at Hebron.
