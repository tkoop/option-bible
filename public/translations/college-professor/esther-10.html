The Royal Levy and Mordecai's Exaltation

1King Ahasuerus imposed a tribute upon the mainland and upon the isles of the sea. 2As for the complete catalogue of his puissance and might, together with the narrative of Mordecai's preeminence—whereby the king elevated him—are not these matters inscribed in the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia? 3For Mordecai the Jew was second in rank to King Ahasuerus; he was illustrious among the Jews, held in favourfavourfavor by the great multitude of his brethren, one who sought the prosperity of his people and spoke peace to all his posterity.
