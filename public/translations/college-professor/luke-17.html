Stumbling Blocks, Forgiveness, and the Nature of Faith

1He said to HishisHis disciples, “It is an inescapable reality that stumbling blocks must come, yet woe to the one through whom they come. 2It would be more expedient for him if a millstone were hung around his neck and he were cast into the sea than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble. 3Be vigilant over yourselves; if your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him. 4And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times returns to you saying, ‘I repent,’ you shall forgive him.”

Faith and the Obligation of Servants

5The apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith.”

6And the Lord said, “If you have faith as a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, ‘Be uprooted and planted in the sea,’ and it would obey you.”

7“Which of you, having a servant plowing or keeping sheep, will say to him when he comes in from the field, ‘Go at once and recline to eat’? 8Will he not rather say to him, ‘Prepare my supper, gird yourself, and serve me while I eat and drink; afterward you shall eat and drink’? 9Does he thank the servant because he did what was commanded? 10So also you, when you have done all that was commanded you, say, ‘We are unprofitable servants; we have done what we ought to have done.’”

Ten Lepers Cleansed on the Journey to Jerusalem

11Now it happened that as HeheHe was journeying to Jerusalem, HeheHe was passing through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.

12And as HeheHe entered a certain village, ten leprous men met HimhimHim; they stood at a distance. 13And they raised their voices, saying, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us.”

14And seeing them, HeheHe said, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.” And it happened that as they went, they were cleansed.

15But one of them, seeing that he had been healed, returned, glorifying God with a loud voice. 16And he fell on his face at HishisHis feet, giving thanks to HimhimHim; and he was a Samaritan.

17Jesus answered and said, “Were not ten cleansed? But where are the nine? 18Were there not found any returning to give glory to God except this foreigner?” 19And HeheHe said to him, “Rise and go; your faith has saved you.”

The Kingdom in Their Midst and the Day of the Son of Man

20Being questioned by the Pharisees as to when the kingdom of God would come, HeheHe answered them and said, “The kingdom of God does not come with observation. 21Nor will they say, ‘Look, here it is!’ or ‘There it is!’ For behold, the kingdom of God is in your midst.”

22He said to HishisHis disciples, “Days will come when you will long to see one of the days of the SonsonSon of ManmanMan, and you will not see it. 23And they will say to you, ‘Look there!’ or ‘Look here!’ Do not go out, nor follow them. 24For as the lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one end to the other, so will the SonsonSon of ManmanMan be in HishisHis day. 25But first HeheHe must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

26“And just as it happened in the days of Noah, so it will be in the days of the SonsonSon of ManmanMan. 27They were eating, drinking, marrying, and being given in marriage until the day Noah entered the ark, and the flood came and destroyed them all. 28Likewise, just as it happened in the days of Lot—they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, and building— 29but on the day Lot went out from Sodom, fire and sulphursulphursulfur rained from Heavenheavenheaven and destroyed them all. 30So it will be on the day the SonsonSon of ManmanMan is revealed.

31“On that day, let the one who is on the housetop, with his goods in the house, not come down to take them away; and likewise let the one in the field not turn back. 32Remember Lot’s wife. 33Whoever seeks to preserve his life will lose it, but whoever loses it will keep it alive.”

34“I tell you, in that night two will be in one bed: one will be taken, and the other will be left. 35Two women will be grinding together: one will be taken, and the other will be left.”

37And they said to HimhimHim, “Where, Lord?”

And HeheHe said to them, “Where the corpse is, there the vultures will gather.”
