The Feast of Trumpets: A Solemn Convocation

1On the first day of the seventh month, you are to observe a sacred convocation; you must perform no laborious work, for it shall be a day of trumpet blasts for you. 2You shall present to the LORD a burnt offering—one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished—as a fragrant and pleasing aroma. 3Their grain offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 4and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 5You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you. 6These are in addition to the monthly burnt offering and its grain offering, the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and their libations, as ordained—a fire offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

The Day of Atonement: Humiliation and Expiation

7On the tenth day of this seventh month, you shall have a sacred convocation; you must humble yourselves and do no work. 8You shall present to the LORD a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 9Their grain offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 10and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 11You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the sin offering of atonement and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and their libations.

The Feast of Tabernacles: Seven Days of Ascending Offerings

12On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you shall have a sacred convocation; you must perform no laborious work, and you shall celebrate a feast to the LORD for seven days. 13You shall present a fire offering, a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 14Their grain offering shall be fine flour mingled with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths for each of the two rams, 15and one-tenth for each of the fourteen lambs. 16You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

17On the second day, you shall present twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 18Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 19You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libations.

20On the third day, you shall present eleven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 21Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 22You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

23On the fourth day, you shall present ten young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 24Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 25You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

26On the fifth day, you shall present nine young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 27Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 28You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

29On the sixth day, you shall present eight young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 30Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 31You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libations.

32On the seventh day, you shall present seven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 33Their grain offering and their libations for the bulls, rams, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 34You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

35On the eighth day, you shall have a solemn assembly; you must perform no laborious work. 36You shall present a fire offering, a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished. 37Their grain offering and their libations for the bull, ram, and lambs shall be according to their number, as prescribed. 38You shall also offer one male goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its libation.

Concluding Ordinance and Moses’ Report

39You shall present these to the LORD at your appointed feasts, besides your vow offerings and your freewill offerings—for your burnt offerings, your grain offerings, your libations, and your peace offerings. 40Thus Moses spoke to the Israelites according to all that the LORD had commanded him.
