The Lord's Oracle Against Tyre

1In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, because Tyre has said concerning Jerusalem, ‘Aha! The gates of the peoples are shattered; she has turned to me; I shall be filled, now that she is laid waste.’ 3Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I am against you, O Tyre, and I will bring up against you many nations, as the sea brings up its waves. 4They will destroy the walls of Tyre and demolish her towers; I will scrape her soil from her and make her a bare rock. 5She shall be in the midst of the sea a place for spreading nets, for I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD; and she shall become plunder for the nations. 6Her villages in the countryside shall be slain by the sword, and they shall know that I am the LORD.”

Nebuchadnezzar's Siege and Devastation of Tyre

7“For thus says the Lord the LORD: Behold, I will bring against Tyre from the north Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, king of kings, with horses, chariots, horsemen, and a great company of people. 8He will slay your villages in the countryside with the sword; he will set up a siege wall against you, pour out a siege ramp against you, and raise his shields against you. 9He will direct the blow of his battering rams against your walls, and with his axes he will break down your towers. 10Because of the multitude of his horses, their dust will cover you; your walls will tremble at the noise of horsemen, wheels, and chariots when he enters your gates as men enter a city already breached. 11With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets; he will slay your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground. 12They will plunder your wealth and loot your merchandise; they will break down your walls and destroy your pleasant houses; and they will cast your stones, your timber, and your dust into the midst of the water. 13I will put an end to the sound of your songs, and the sound of your harps will be heard no more. 14I will make you a bare rock; you shall be a place for spreading nets; you shall never be rebuilt, for I the LORD have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.”

The Lamentation of the Princes of the Sea

15“Thus says the Lord the LORD to Tyre: ‘Will not the coastlands tremble at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan and slaughter is made in your midst?’ 16Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, remove their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments; they will clothe themselves with trembling, sit on the ground, tremble every moment, and be appalled at you. 17They will raise a lamentation over you and say to you, ‘How you have perished, O renowned city, inhabited by seafarers! She was mighty on the sea, she and her inhabitants, who spread their terror to all who dwelt around them.’ 18Now the coastlands tremble on the day of your fall; the coastlands that are in the sea are dismayed at your departure.”

Tyre Consigned to the Pit

19“For thus says the Lord the LORD: When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep over you and the great waters cover you, 20then I will bring you down with those who go down to the pit, to the people of old, and I will make you dwell in the lowest parts of the earth, like the ancient ruins, with those who go down to the pit, so that you will not be inhabited; and I will bestow glory upon the land of the living. 21I will make you a horror, and you shall be no more; though you are sought, you will never be found again, declares the Lord the LORD.”
