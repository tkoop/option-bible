Israel's Renewed Apostasy and Canaanite Oppression

1After Ehud died, the Israelites again acted wickedly in the sight of the LORD. 2So the LORD delivered them into the power of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; the commander of his army was Sisera, who dwelt in Harosheth-hagoyim. 3Then the Israelites cried out to the LORD, for Jabin possessed nine hundred iron chariots and had cruelly oppressed the Israelites for twenty years.

Deborah's Prophetic Summons to Barak

4At that time Deborah, a prophetess and the wife of Lappidoth, was judging Israel. 5She used to sit beneath the palm of Deborah, between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites would come up to her for judgmentjudgementjudgment.

6She sent for Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali and said to him, “Has not the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded you: ‘Go, advance toward Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun’?” 7“I will draw Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, with his chariots and his multitude, to you at the BrookbrookBrook Kishon, and I will deliver him into your hand.”

8Barak said to her, “If you go with me, I will go; but if you do not go with me, I will not go.”

9She replied, “I will surely go with you; nevertheless, the honourhonourhonor of the journey you are undertaking will not be yours, for the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman.” Then Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh. 10Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali at Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up after him; Deborah went up with him.

The Armies Converge and Barak's Victory

11Now Heber the Kenite had separated from the Kenites, the descendants of Hobab, Moses' father-in-law, and had pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, near Kedesh. 12When Sisera was told that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor, 13he summoned all his chariots—nine hundred iron chariots—and all the people with him, from Harosheth-hagoyim to the BrookbrookBrook Kishon. 14Deborah said to Barak, “Arise, for this is the day the LORD has given Sisera into your hand. Has not the LORD gone out before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him. 15The LORD routed Sisera and all his chariots and army before Barak by the sword, and Sisera alighted from his chariot and fled on foot. 16Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Harosheth-hagoyim, and all of Sisera's army fell by the sword; not one survived.

Jael's Lethal Hospitality and Sisera's Demise

17Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite. 18Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, “Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not be afraid.” So he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

19He said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty.” So she opened a skin of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him. 20He said to her, “Stand at the entrance of the tent, and if anyone comes and asks you, ‘Is there a man here?’ you shall say, ‘No.’”

21He was sound asleep and exhausted. Then Jael, Heber's wife, took a tent peg and a hammer in her hand, went stealthily to him, drove the peg into his temple until it sank into the ground, and he died.

22As Barak pursued Sisera, Jael came out to meet him and said, “Come, and I will show you the man you are seeking.” He went in to her, and there lay Sisera dead, with the tent peg in his temple.

Israel's Final Triumph over Jabin

23Thus on that day God subdued Jabin king of Canaan before the Israelites. 24The hand of the Israelites pressed ever harder against Jabin king of Canaan until they had destroyed Jabin king of Canaan.
