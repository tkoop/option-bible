The Prophet’s Anguish and the People’s Perfidy

3“They bend their tongue like a bow; falsehood prevails over truth in the land. For they proceed from one evil to another, and they do not know MemeMe,” declares the LORD. 4“Let everyone beware of his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and put no trust in any brother, for every brother is a deceiver and every friend is a slanderer. 5They deceive one another and do not speak the truth; they have trained their tongues to lie and wear themselves out in iniquity. 6You dwell in the midst of deception; in their deceit they refuse to know MemeMe,” declares the LORD.

The LORD’s Refining and the Devastation of the Land

With his mouth a man speaks peace to his neighbourneighbourneighbor, but in his heart he sets an ambush. 9Should I not punish them for these things?” declares the LORD. “Should I not avenge MyselfmyselfMyself on a nation such as this?”

10“I will take up a weeping and wailing for the mountains, and a lamentation for the pastures of the wilderness, because they are desolate, with no one passing through; the sound of cattle is not heard; both the birds of the air and the beasts have fled and are gone. 11I will make Jerusalem a heap of ruins, a haunt of jackals, and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant.”

12Who is the man wise enough to understand this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he may declare it? Why has the land been ruined and laid waste like a desert, so that no one passes through?

13The LORD said, “It is because they have forsaken MymyMy law, which I set before them; they have not obeyed MymyMy voice or walked according to it, 14but have followed the stubbornness of their own hearts and gone after the Baals, as their fathers taught them.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment, Exile, and the Summons to Lamentation

15Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I will feed this people wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink. 16I will scatter them among the nations that neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send the sword after them until I have finished them off.”

17This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Consider, and call for the mourning women; send for the skilled women. 18Let them come quickly and raise a wail for us, that our eyes may overflow with tears and our eyelids gush with water. 19For the sound of wailing is heard from Zion: ‘How we are ruined! We are greatly ashamed, for we have left the land, because they have thrown down our dwellings.’”

20Now hear the word of the LORD, O women, and let your ears receive the word of HishisHis mouth. Teach your daughters to wail, and let each woman teach her neighbourneighbourneighbor a lament. 21For death has climbed through our windows and entered our palaces, to cut off the children from the streets and the young men from the squares.

22Speak: “This is what the LORD declares: ‘The corpses of men will fall like dung in the open field, and like sheaves behind the reaper, with no one to gather them.’”

The Vanity of Earthly Boasting and the Coming Reckoning

23This is what the LORD says: “Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, nor the strong man in his strength, nor the rich man in his riches. 24But let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows MemeMe, that I am the LORD, who exercises loving devotion, justice, and righteousness on the earth. For in these things I delight,” declares the LORD.

25“Behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will punish all who are circumcised yet uncircumcised: 26Egypt, Judah, Edom, the Ammonites, the Moabites, and all the inhabitants of the desert who clip the corners of their hair. For all these nations are uncircumcised, and the whole house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.”
