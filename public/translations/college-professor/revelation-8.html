The Seventh Seal and the Golden Censer

1When the LamblambLamb opened the seventh seal, silence fell upon Heavenheavenheaven for about half an hour. 2I beheld the seven angels standing before God, to whom seven trumpets were entrusted. 3Another angel, bearing a golden censer, approached and took his place at the altar; abundant incense was given to him, that he might present it with the prayers of all the saints upon the golden altar before the throne. 4The smoke of the incense, ascending with the prayers of the saints, rose before God from the angel's hand. 5The angel then took the censer, filled it with fire from the altar, and cast it upon the earth; whereupon there followed thunderclaps, rumblings, flashes of lightning, and an earthquake. 6The seven angels who held the seven trumpets then made ready to sound them.

The First Four Trumpet JudgmentsJudgementsJudgments

7The first angel sounded, and there came hail and fire mingled with blood, and it was cast upon the earth; a third of the earth was consumed by fire, as were a third of the trees and all the green grass. 8The second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain blazing with fire was hurled into the sea, so that a third of the sea became blood. 9A third of the living creatures in the sea perished, and a third of the ships were destroyed. 10The third angel sounded, and a great star, blazing like a torch, fell from Heavenheavenheaven upon a third of the rivers and upon the springs of water. 11The star's name is Wormwood, and a third of the waters became wormwood; many people died from the waters, because they had been made bitter. 12The fourth angel sounded, and a third of the sun, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars were struck, so that a third of them were darkened; a third of the day was deprived of light, and the night as well.

The Eagle's Threefold Woe

13Then I looked, and I heard an eagle flying in midheaven, crying out in a loud voice, "Woe, woe, woe to the inhabitants of the earth, because of the remaining trumpet blasts that the three angels are about to sound!"
