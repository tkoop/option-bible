The Vanity of Heathen Customs and the Supremacy of the LORD

1Hear the word that the LORD has spoken to you, O house of Israel. 2Thus says the LORD: “Do not learn the way of the nations, nor be dismayed by the signs of the heavens, for the nations are dismayed by them. 3For the customs of the peoples are vanity: one cuts a tree from the forest, the work of a craftsman's hands with an axe. 4They embellish it with silver and gold; they secure it with nails and hammers, lest it topple. 5They are like a scarecrow in a cucumber patch: they cannot speak, and they must be borne, for they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no harm, nor are they capable of any good.”

The Incomparable Creator and the Folly of Idols

6There is none like YouyouYou, O the LORD; YouyouYou are great, and YouryourYour name is great in might. 7Who would not fear YouyouYou, O KingkingKing of the nations? For it is YouryourYour due; for among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is none like YouyouYou. 8Yet in one respect they are uniformly brutish and foolish: the instruction drawn from these vanities is mere timber. 9Silver, beaten into plates, is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz—the work of a craftsman and the hands of a goldsmith. Their vestments are blue and purple; all are the work of skilled men. 10But the LORD is the true God; HeheHe is the living God and eternal King. The EarthEarthearth trembles at HishisHis wrath, and the nations cannot endure HishisHis indignation.

The True God’s Creative Sovereignty

11Thus you shall say to them: “The gods that did not make Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth shall perish from the earth and from under these heavens.” 12He made the earth by HishisHis power; HeheHe established the world by HishisHis wisdom, and by HishisHis understanding HeheHe stretched out the heavens. 13When HeheHe thunders, the waters in the heavens roar; HeheHe causes vapors to rise from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain and brings forth the wind from HishisHis storehouses. 14Every man is brutish in his knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idol, for his molten image is falsehood, and there is no breath in them. 15They are vanity, a work of delusion; in the time of their punishment they shall perish. 16The Portion of Jacob is not like them; for HeheHe is the MakermakerMaker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of HishisHis inheritance—the LORD of Hosts is HishisHis name.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment, Lament, and Intercession

17Gather up your belongings from the land, you who dwell under siege. 18For thus says the LORD: “Behold, I am about to hurl the inhabitants of the land away; I will distress them, that they may be captured.” 19Woe is me for my hurt! My wound is grievous; yet I said, “This is my sickness, and I must bear it.” 20My tent is destroyed, and all its cords are snapped; my children have departed from me, and are no more. There is no one left to pitch my tent or to raise its curtains. 21For the shepherds are brutish and have not sought the LORD; therefore they have not prospered, and all their flock is scattered. 22Behold, a report is coming—a great commotion from the north country—to make the cities of Judah a desolation, a lair of jackals.

23O the LORD, I know that a person's course is not in his own power; it is not for a man who walks to direct his own steps. 24Correct me, O the LORD, but with justice; not in YouryourYour anger, lest YouyouYou reduce me to nothing. 25Pour out YouryourYour wrath upon the nations that do not know YouyouYou, and upon the families that do not call on YouryourYour name; for they have devoured Jacob, consumed him, and laid waste his homeland.
