The Oracle Entrusted to Jeremiah

1The word that came to Jeremiah from the the LORD was this:

The Summons to Repentance and the False Security of the Temple

2the LORD said to Jeremiah, “Stand at the gate of the house of the the LORD and proclaim this word there, saying, ‘Hear the word of the the LORD, all you people of Judah who enter these gates to worship the the LORD.’ 3Thus says the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Amend your ways and your deeds, and I will let you dwell in this place. 4Do not trust in deceptive words that say, ‘The temple of the the LORD, the temple of the the LORD, the temple of the the LORD—these are our refuge.’ 5For if you truly amend your ways and your deeds, if you truly execute justice between one person and another, 6if you do not oppress the sojourner, the fatherless, or the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not walk after other gods to your own detriment, 7then I will let you dwell in this place, in the land I gave to your fathers, forever and ever.”

The Vanity of False Confidence

8“Behold, you are trusting in deceptive words to no avail. 9Do you suppose you may steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, burn incense to Baal, and walk after other gods you do not know, 10and then come and stand before MemeMe in this house that bears MymyMy name, saying, ‘We are delivered!’—all so that you may continue in these abominations? 11Has this house, which is called by MymyMy name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? Behold, even I have seen it, declares the the LORD.”

The Warning of Shiloh and the Coming Exile

12“But go now to the place I formerly established in Shiloh, where I first caused MymyMy name to dwell, and observe what I did to it on account of the wickedness of MymyMy people Israel. 13And now, because you persist in all these practices, declares the the LORD, and because I spoke to you earnestly and repeatedly, yet you did not listen, and I called to you, but you did not answer, 14I will therefore deal with this house that bears MymyMy name—the very house in which you place your trust—and with the place I gave to you and to your fathers, just as I dealt with Shiloh. 15I will cast you out of MymyMy presence, just as I cast out all your kindred, the entire seed of Ephraim. 16As for you, do not pray on behalf of this people, nor raise a cry or petition for them, nor intercede with MemeMe, because I will not hear you. 17Do you not perceive what they are doing in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem? 18The children collect the wood, the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead dough, in order to make cakes for the queen of Heavenheavenheaven and to pour out libations to other gods, thereby provoking MemeMe to anger. 19Is it I whom they provoke to anger? declares the the LORD. Is it not themselves, to their own disgrace?”

The Rejection of Sacrifice and the Valley of Slaughter

20“Therefore, thus says the Lord, the the LORD: Behold, MymyMy anger and MymyMy wrath shall be poured out upon this place—upon human beings and animals, upon the trees of the field and the fruit of the ground—and it shall burn without being quenched. 21Thus says the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Heap your burnt offerings upon your sacrifices and eat the flesh. 22For when I brought your ancestors out of the land of Egypt, I did not speak to them or command them about burnt offerings or sacrifices. 23But this is the word I commanded them: ‘Obey MymyMy voice, and I will be your God, and you shall be MymyMy people. Walk in all the way I command you, so that it may go well with you.’ 24Yet they did not listen or incline their ear; instead, they walked in the counsels and in the stubbornness of their evil heart, moving backward rather than forward. 25From the day your ancestors left the land of Egypt until this day, I have sent to you all MymyMy servants the prophets, rising early each day and sending them. 26Yet they did not listen to MemeMe or incline their ear; rather, they stiffened their necks and acted more wickedly than their fathers. 27Therefore you are to speak all these words to them, but they will not listen to you; you are to call to them, but they will not answer you. 28And you are to tell them, ‘This is the nation that has not obeyed the voice of the the LORD its God or accepted discipline. Truth has vanished; it has been cut off from their mouths.’ 29Cut off your hair and cast it away; raise a lamentation on the barren heights, for the the LORD has rejected and abandoned the generation of HishisHis wrath. 30For the people of Judah have done what is evil in MymyMy sight, declares the the LORD. They have set their abominations in the house that bears MymyMy name, thereby defiling it. 31They have built the high places of Topheth in the ValleyvalleyValley of the SonsonSon of Hinnom, to burn their sons and daughters in the fire—something I never commanded, nor did it ever enter MymyMy mind. 32Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares the the LORD, when it will no longer be called Topheth or the ValleyvalleyValley of the SonsonSon of Hinnom, but the ValleyvalleyValley of SlaughterslaughterSlaughter; for they shall bury in Topheth until no space remains. 33The corpses of this people shall become food for the birds of the heavens and the beasts of the earth, and no one shall drive them away. 34I will bring to an end in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, for the land shall become a desolation.”
