The LORD’s Sword Against the Land

1The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, turn your gaze toward Jerusalem, speak prophetically against the sanctuaries, and prophesy against the land of Israel. 3Say to the land of Israel, ‘Thus says the the LORD: Behold, I am against you, and I will draw MymyMy sword from its sheath and cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked. 4Because I cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked, MymyMy sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh from south to north. 5Then all flesh will know that I, the the LORD, have drawn MymyMy sword from its sheath; it shall not return again.’”

6And as for you, son of man, groan in their sight with a breaking of your loins and with bitterness. 7When they ask you, “Why are you groaning?” say,

“At the report that is coming, every heart will melt, all hands will grow feeble, every spirit will faint, and all knees will become as water. Behold, it is coming, and it shall be, declares the Lord, the the LORD.”

The Sharpened and Polished Sword

8Again the word of the the LORD came to me: 9“Son of man, prophesy and say, ‘Thus says the Lord, the the LORD: A sword, a sword is sharpened and also polished. 10It is sharpened for slaughter, polished to flash like lightning. Should we then rejoice in the sceptresceptrescepter of MymyMy son? The blade treats every staff with contempt. 11It has been given to be polished, to be seized in the hand; it is sharpened and polished to be placed in the hand of the slayer. 12Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is against MymyMy people; it is against all the princes of Israel. They are delivered to the sword along with MymyMy people; therefore strike your thigh. 13For it is a test; and what if even the sceptresceptrescepter that holds all in contempt should be no more? declares the Lord, the the LORD.’”

The Sword at the Crossroads

14“And you, son of man, prophesy and clap your hands; let the sword strike twice, even three times—the sword of the slain, the sword of the great one to be slain, penetrating them. 15I have set the point of the sword against all their gates, to melt the heart and multiply their stumbling blocks. Ah, it is fashioned like lightning, honed for slaughter. 16Slash to the right; set your blade to the left—wherever your face is turned. 17I too will clap MymyMy hands, and I will satisfy MymyMy wrath. I, the the LORD, have spoken.”

18The word of the the LORD came to me again: 19“And you, son of man, set before yourself two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to travel; both shall come from a single land. Make a signpost; make it at the head of the road to the city. 20Appoint a road for the sword to come to Rabbah of the Ammonites, and to Judah, into fortified Jerusalem.

21“For the king of Babylon stands at the parting of the ways, at the head of the two roads, to practiseto practiseto practice divination: he shakes arrows, inquires of household idols, and examines the liver. 22In his right hand is the divination for Jerusalem—to set battering rams, to open the mouth in slaughter, to lift up a shout, to set battering rams against the gates, to cast up a siege ramp, and to build a siege wall. 23To them it will seem like a vain divination, for sworn oaths are theirs; but he brings their iniquity to remembrance, that they may be seized.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Israel’s Prince and on Ammon

24“Therefore thus says the Lord, the the LORD: Because you have made your iniquity to be remembered, in that your transgressions are uncovered and your sins revealed in all your deeds—because you have been remembered, you shall be seized by the hand. 25And you, O profane, wicked prince of Israel, whose day has come in the time of final punishment, 26thus says the Lord, the the LORD: Remove the turban and take off the crown; things shall not remain as they are. Exalt the lowly and abase the lofty. 27A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it; this also shall not stand until the one whose right it is comes, and I will give it to HimhimHim.”

28“And you, son of man, prophesy and say, ‘Thus says the Lord, the the LORD concerning the Ammonites and concerning their reproach: A sword, a sword is drawn for slaughter, polished to consume, to flash like lightning— 29while they see false visions for you and divine lies for you, to lay you upon the necks of the wicked slain, whose day has come in the time of final punishment. 30Return the sword to its sheath. In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you. 31I will pour out MymyMy indignation upon you; I will blow upon you with the fire of MymyMy wrath, and I will deliver you into the hands of brutal men, craftsmen of destruction. 32You shall be fuel for the fire; your blood shall be in the midst of the land. You shall not be remembered, for I, the the LORD, have spoken.’”
