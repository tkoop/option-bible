The Priestly and Levitical Lineages in the Restoration

1These are the priests and Levites who ascended with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and with Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra, Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah, Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah, Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. These were the heads of the priests, together with their associates, in the days of Jeshua. 2Amariah, Malluch, Hattush; 3Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth; 4Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah; 5Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah; 6Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah; 7Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah. These were the heads of the priests and their brethren in the days of Jeshua.

8The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who, with his brothers, directed the thanksgiving; 9and Bakbukiah and Unni, their brothers, stood opposite them in their appointed watches.

The High-Priestly Succession and the Heads of the Priestly Houses

10Jeshua begot Joiakim; Joiakim begot Eliashib; and Eliashib begot Joiada. 11Joiada begot Jonathan, and Jonathan begot Jaddua.

12In the days of Joiakim, the heads of the ancestral houses among the priests were as follows: for Seraiah, Meraiah; for Jeremiah, Hananiah; 13for Ezra, Meshullam; for Amariah, Jehohanan; 14for Malluchi, Jonathan; for Shebaniah, Joseph; 15for Harim, Adna; for Meraioth, Helkai; 16for Iddo, Zechariah; for Ginnethon, Meshullam; 17for Abijah, Zichri; for Miniamin and Moadiah, Piltai; 18for Bilgah, Shammua; for Shemaiah, Jehonathan; 19for Joiarib, Mattenai; for Jedaiah, Uzzi; 20for Sallai, Kallai; for Amok, Eber; 21for Hilkiah, Hashabiah; and for Jedaiah, Nethanel.

22In the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, the Levites were recorded as heads of ancestral houses, as were the priests, under the reign of Darius the Persian. 23The sons of Levi, the heads of ancestral houses, were written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles down to the days of Johanan son of Eliashib. 24The chiefs of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua son of Kadmiel, and their brothers, who stood opposite them to praise and give thanks, watch corresponding to watch, by the command of David, the man of God. 25Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub, gatekeepers, kept watch at the storehouses of the gates. 26These served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and scribe.

The Dedication of Jerusalem’s Walls

27At the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem, they sought out the Levites from all their places to bring them to Jerusalem, in order to celebrate the dedication with gladness, thanksgivings, song, cymbals, harps, and lyres. 28The sons of the singers gathered from the district around Jerusalem, from the villages of the Netophathites, 29from Beth-gilgal, and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth, for the singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem. 30The priests and the Levites purified themselves, and they purified the people, the gates, and the wall.

31Then I brought the leaders of Judah up onto the wall and stationed two great choirs of thanksgiving, together with their processions, upon the wall, one proceeding to the right toward the DungdungDung GategateGate. 32Hoshaiah and half of the leaders of Judah followed them, 33along with Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam, 34Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, Jeremiah, 35and certain of the priests' sons with trumpets—Zechariah son of Jonathan son of Shemaiah son of Mattaniah son of Micaiah son of Zaccur son of Asaph— 36and his brothers Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani, with the musical instruments of David, the man of God; and Ezra the scribe went before them. 37At the FountainfountainFountain GategateGate, directly opposite them, they ascended by the stairs of the CitycityCity of David, at the ascent to the wall, above the house of David, as far as the Water GategateGate on the east.

38The other choir of thanksgiving went in the opposite direction, and I followed it, with half of the people on the wall, above the TowertowerTower of the OvensovensOvens, as far as the BroadbroadBroad Wall, 39and above the GategateGate of Ephraim, over the Old GategateGate, over the FishfishFish GategateGate, and the TowertowerTower of Hananel and the TowertowerTower of the Hundred, to the SheepsheepSheep GategateGate; and they halted at the GategateGate of the Guard.

40Then the two choirs of thanksgiving stood in the house of God, and I and half of the officials stood with me, 41together with the priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah with trumpets, 42and Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer, while the singers made themselves heard under Jezrahiah their overseer. 43They offered great sacrifices that day and rejoiced, because God had made them rejoice with great joy; the women and the children rejoiced as well, so that the joy of Jerusalem was heard from afar.

Provision for the Priestly and Levitical Orders

44On that day men were appointed over the chambers for the stores, the contributions, the firstfruits, and the tithes, to gather into them from the fields of the cities the portions required by the LawlawLaw for the priests and the Levites; for Judah rejoiced over the priests and Levites who stood in their service. 45They kept the charge of their God and the charge of purification, as did the singers and the gatekeepers, according to the command of David and of Solomon his son. 46For in the days of David and Asaph of old there were chiefs of the singers, and songs of praise and thanksgiving to God. 47In the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel gave the daily portions to the singers and the gatekeepers, and they set apart a portion for the Levites, while the Levites set apart a portion for the sons of Aaron.
