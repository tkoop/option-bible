Joseph Discloses His Identity to His Brothers

1Joseph could no longer contain himself in the presence of all who attended him, and he cried out, “Let everyone withdraw from me!” Thus no one remained with him when Joseph disclosed himself to his brothers. 2He wept so vociferously that the Egyptians heard, as did Pharaoh’s household. 3Joseph said to his brothers, “I am Joseph. Is my father still alive?” Yet his brothers were unable to answer him, for they were seized with consternation at his presence. 4Joseph said to his brothers, “Draw near to me, I pray you.” When they drew near, he said, “I am Joseph, your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.”

Joseph Interprets His Suffering as Divine Providence

5“And now, be not grieved, nor let anger burn within yourselves, because you sold me here; for God sent me before you to preserve life. 6For the famine has pervaded the land these two years, and five years yet remain in which there shall be neither plowing nor harvest. 7God sent me before you to preserve for you a remnant on EarthEarthearth, and to sustain your lives through a great deliverance. 8So now it was not you who sent me here, but God; and HeheHe has established me as a father to Pharaoh, and as lord of all his house, and as ruler over all the land of Egypt.

9“Hasten and go up to my father, and say to him, ‘Thus says your son Joseph: God has appointed me lord of all Egypt. Come down to me; do not tarry.’ 10You shall dwell in the land of Goshen, and you shall be near me—you, your children, your children’s children, your flocks, your herds, and all that you possess. 11There I will sustain you, for five years of famine remain, lest you and your household and all that you possess be reduced to penury. 12And behold, your eyes and the eyes of my brother Benjamin perceive that it is my own mouth that speaks to you. 13You must report to my father all my glory in Egypt, and all that you have seen, and you must hasten and bring my father down here.”

14Then he fell upon his brother Benjamin’s neck and wept, and Benjamin wept upon his neck. 15He kissed all his brothers and wept over them; and afterward his brothers conversed with him.

Pharaoh’s Regal Provision for the Family

16The news was heard in Pharaoh’s house: “Joseph’s brothers have arrived!” And it was pleasing in the sight of Pharaoh and his servants. 17Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Say to your brothers, ‘Do this: load your beasts and proceed to the land of Canaan; 18take your father and your households and come to me, and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you shall eat the fat of the land.’ 19You are also instructed to tell them, ‘Take wagons from the land of Egypt for your little ones and your wives, and bring your father and come. 20Do not be anxious about your possessions, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.’”

21The sons of Israel complied; Joseph gave them wagons in accordance with Pharaoh’s command, and he furnished them with provisions for the journey. 22To each of them he gave a change of garments, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of garments. 23To his father he sent ten donkeys bearing the bounty of Egypt, and ten female donkeys bearing grain, bread, and provisions for his father for the journey. 24He sent his brothers away, and they departed; and he said to them, “Do not quarrel on the road.”

Jacob’s Spirit Revived by Tidings of Joseph

25They ascended from Egypt and came to the land of Canaan, to Jacob their father. 26They reported to him, “Joseph is yet alive, and he rules over all the land of Egypt.” But his heart failed within him, for he gave no credence to them. 27They recounted to him all the words Joseph had spoken to them; and when he beheld the wagons Joseph had sent to convey him, the spirit of their father Jacob revived. 28Israel said, “It suffices; my son Joseph is yet alive. I will go and behold him before I die.”
