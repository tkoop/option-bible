The Spirit's Warning Concerning Apostasy in the Latter Times

1The SpiritspiritSpirit states with unmistakable clarity that in the latter times some will apostatize from the faith, devoting themselves to deceitful spirits and to demonic doctrines—disseminated through the hypocrisy of mendacious men whose consciences have been cauterized—2while forbidding marriage and enjoining abstinence from foods that God created to be received with gratitude by the faithful and by those who have come to know the truth. 3For every creation of God is good, and nothing received with thanksgiving is to be rejected, 4since it is consecrated by the word of God and by prayer. 5

The Good Servant of Christ Jesus

6If you set these matters before the brothers, you will prove yourself a good servant of Christ Jesus, being nourished by the words of the faith and by the sound doctrine that you have followed. 7But shun profane and superstitious fables; instead, discipline yourself for godliness. 8For physical training yields only limited benefit, whereas godliness is beneficial in every respect, since it carries the promise of both the present life and the life to come. 9The saying is trustworthy and deserving of full acceptance. 10For it is to this end that we labourlabourlabor and contend, because we have set our hope on the living God, who is the SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior of all humanity, and especially of those who believe.

Timothy's Pastoral Charge and Example

11Command and teach these things. 12Let no one treat your youth with contempt; rather, become a model for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, and in purity. 13Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading, to exhortation, and to teaching. 14Do not neglect the gift that resides in you, which was conferred upon you through prophecy together with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery. 15Ponder these matters; be absorbed in them, so that your advancement may be manifest to everyone. 16Keep close watch over yourself and over the teaching; remain in them, for by doing so you will save both yourself and those who listen to you.
