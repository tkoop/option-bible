The Consummation of Creation and the Sanctification of the Seventh Day

1Thus were the heavens and the earth brought to their consummation, together with the totality of their celestial and terrestrial hosts. 2By the seventh day God had brought to completion the labourlabourlabor that HeheHe had undertaken; and on that seventh day HeheHe desisted from every species of work that HeheHe had performed. 3God then blessed the seventh day and consecrated it, for on it HeheHe rested from all the creative labourlabourlabor that HeheHe had undertaken to perform.

The Account of Humanity and the Planting of Eden

4Such is the genealogy of the heavens and the earth at the time of their creation: when the LORD God fashioned the earth and the heavens, 5no shrub of the field had yet manifested itself upon the earth, nor had any herb of the field yet burgeoned, because the LORD God had not caused rain to fall upon the earth, and there was no human being to cultivate the soil; 6yet a mist ascended from the earth and irrigated the entire face of the ground. 7Then the LORD God fashioned the human being from the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, so that the human being became a living being. 8Moreover, the LORD God had planted a garden in Eden, toward the east, and there HeheHe placed the human being whom HeheHe had formed. 9Out of the ground the LORD God caused to spring every tree that is delightful to behold and salutary for nourishment; and in the midst of the garden were the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

The Rivers of Eden and the Divine Command

10A river issued forth from Eden to irrigate the garden, and from thence it divided and became four headstreams. 11The first is called Pishon; it circumnavigates the whole land of Havilah, where gold is found. 12The gold of that land is excellent; bdellium and onyx stone are also there. 13The second river is called Gihon; it circumnavigates the whole land of Cush. 14The third river is called Tigris; it flows eastward of Assyria. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.

15The LORD God took the man and settled him in the garden of Eden, to cultivate it and to keep it. 16And the LORD God issued a command to the man, saying, “You may freely eat from every tree of the garden; 17but from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat from it you shall surely die.”

The Creation of Woman and the Institution of Marriage

18Then the LORD God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone; I will make for him a helper commensurate with him.” 19Now the LORD God had formed out of the ground every beast of the field and every bird of the heavens, and HeheHe brought them to the man to see what he would call them; and whatever the man called each living creature, that became its name. 20The man therefore bestowed names upon all the cattle, upon the birds of the heavens, and upon every beast of the field; yet for the man no helper was found who was commensurate with him.

21So the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall upon the man, and while he slept HeheHe took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh in its place. 22And from the rib that the LORD God had taken from the man HeheHe fashioned a woman, and HeheHe brought her to the man.

23Then the man said, “This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of ManmanMan.” 24Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and cleave to his wife, and they shall become one flesh. 25And the man and his wife were both naked, yet they were not ashamed.
