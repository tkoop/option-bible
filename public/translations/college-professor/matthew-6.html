On the Cultivation of Righteousness before God

“1Take heed that you do not perform your righteousness before men in order to be gazed upon by them; for otherwise you shall have no recompense from your FatherfatherFather who is in Heavenheavenheaven. 2Thus, whenever you bestow alms, do not trumpet them before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the thoroughfares, that they may be glorified by men. Truly, I say to you, they have already received their reward in full. 3But when you give alms, let not your left hand know what your right hand is accomplishing, 4so that your almsgiving may remain in secrecy; and your FatherfatherFather, who beholds what is done in secret, will render recompense unto you.

On Prayer, Forgiveness, and the Lord’s Prayer

“5And whenever you pray, you shall not be as the hypocrites are, for they delight in praying while standing in the synagogues and at the corners of the broad streets, so that they may be conspicuous to men. Truly, I say to you, they have already collected their reward. 6But when you pray, enter into your inner chamber, and having shut your door, pray to your FatherfatherFather who is in secret; and your FatherfatherFather, who sees in secret, will recompense you. 7Moreover, when you pray, do not babble vain repetitions, as the pagans do, for they suppose that by their verbosity they will be heard. 8Do not, therefore, resemble them, for your FatherfatherFather knows of what things you have need before you ask HimhimHim.

“9Pray, then, in this manner: ‘Our FatherfatherFather in Heavenheavenheaven, hallowed be YouryourYour name. 10Your kingdom come; YouryourYour will be done, on EarthEarthearth as it is in Heavenheavenheaven. 11Give us this day our daily bread. 12And forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. 13And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.’ 14For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly FatherfatherFather will likewise forgive you; 15but if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your FatherfatherFather forgive your trespasses.

On Fasting and Secret Devotion

“16Furthermore, when you fast, do not be of a dismal countenance, as the hypocrites are, for they disfigure their faces in order that they may appear unto men to be fasting. Truly, I say to you, they have already received their reward. 17But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face, 18so that your fasting may not be manifest unto men, but only unto your FatherfatherFather, who is in secret; and your FatherfatherFather, who sees in secret, will recompense you.

On Treasures, Singleness of Vision, and Freedom from Anxiety

“19Do not amass for yourselves treasures upon EarthEarthearth, where moth and rust corrupt, and where thieves tunnel through and steal; 20but amass for yourselves treasures in Heavenheavenheaven, where neither moth nor rust corrupts, and where thieves neither tunnel through nor steal. 21For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

“22The eye is the lamp of the body. If your eye be healthy, your whole body shall be luminous; 23but if your eye be bad, your whole body shall be darkened. If, then, the light that is in you is darkness, how profound is that darkness! 24No one can be enslaved to two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will cleave to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money.

“25Therefore I say to you, do not be anxious concerning your life—what you shall eat or what you shall drink—nor concerning your body, what you shall wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing? 26Behold the birds of the Heavenheavenheaven: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and yet your heavenly FatherfatherFather nourishes them. Are you not of much more worth than they? 27And which of you, by being anxious, can add a single hour to his life? 28And why are you anxious about clothing? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin. 29Yet I say to you that not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these. 30Now if God so clothes the grass of the field, which today exists and tomorrow is cast into the oven, will HeheHe not much more clothe you, O you of little faith?

“31Therefore do not be anxious, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’ 32For the pagans seek after all these things; and your heavenly FatherfatherFather knows that you need them. 33But seek first the kingdom of God and HishisHis righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you. 34Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself; today has sufficient trouble of its own.”
