A Psalm of David Composed in the Wilderness of Judah

1O God, YouyouYou are my God; I seek YouyouYou with ardent devotion. My soul thirsts for YouyouYou; my flesh languishes for YouyouYou in a parched and exhausted land, devoid of water. 2I have beheld YouyouYou in the sanctuary, to gaze upon YouryourYour power and YouryourYour glory. 3Because YouryourYour steadfast love is better than life itself, my lips will extol YouyouYou. 4Thus I will bless YouyouYou throughout my days; in YouryourYour name I will lift up my hands.

5My soul shall be sated as with the richest fare; my mouth will praise YouyouYou with exultant lips. 6When I remember YouyouYou upon my bed, I meditate upon YouyouYou through the watches of the night. 7For YouyouYou have been my help; and in the protective shadow of YouryourYour wings I will sing for joy. 8My soul clings to YouyouYou; YouryourYour right hand sustains me.

9But those who seek my life in order to destroy it will descend into the lowest regions of the earth. 10They will be delivered to the sword; they will become carrion for jackals. 11Yet the king will rejoice in God; everyone who swears by HimhimHim will exult, for the mouths of those who speak falsehood will be stopped.
