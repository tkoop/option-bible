The Exiles Returning from Babylon

Those of Uncertain Lineage and the Assembly's Tally

59These also went up from Tel Melah, Tel Harsha, Cherub, Addan, and Immer, though they could not prove their ancestral houses or their lineage, whether they were of Israel: 60the descendants of Delaiah, Tobiah, and Nekoda: 652. 61And among the priests: the descendants of Habaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai, who had married one of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite and was called by their name. 62These sought their registration among those enrolled by genealogy, but they were not found; therefore they were excluded from the priesthood. 63The governor said to them, "YouyouYou shall not eat of the most holy things until a priest arises with Urim and Thummim."

64The whole assembly, as one body, numbered 42,360, 65besides 7,337 male and female servants; they also had 200 male and female singers. 66Their horses numbered 736 and their mules 245. 67Their camels numbered 435 and their donkeys 6,720. 68When they came to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, some of the heads of the ancestral houses gave freewill offerings to erect the house of God upon its foundation. 69According to their ability, they gave to the treasury of the work 61,000 darics of gold, 5,000 minas of silver, and 100 priestly garments. 70The priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, and some of the people settled in their towns, and all Israel in their towns.
