The Opening of the First Four Seals

1And I beheld when the LamblambLamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures cry out, in a voice like thunder, “Come!” 2And I looked, and behold, a white horse; and its rider carried a bow, and a crown was given to him, and he went forth conquering, and bent upon further conquest.

3And when HeheHe had opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come!” 4And another horse came forth, fiery red; and to its rider it was granted to take peace from the earth, that men should slaughter one another; and a great sword was given to him.

5And when HeheHe had opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come!” And I looked, and behold, a black horse; and its rider held a pair of scales in his hand.

6And I heard, as it were, a voice in the midst of the four living creatures, saying, “A choenix of wheat for a denarius, and three choenixes of barley for a denarius; and do not harm the oil and the wine.”

7And when HeheHe had opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, “Come!” 8And I looked, and behold, a pale horse; and the name of its rider was Death, and Hades followed with him. And authority was given to them over a fourth of the earth, to kill with sword, and with famine, and with pestilence, and by the beasts of the earth.

The Fifth Seal and the Cry of the Martyrs

9And when HeheHe had opened the fifth seal, I saw beneath the altar the souls of those who had been slain because of the word of God and because of the testimony which they maintained. 10And they cried out with a loud voice, saying, “How long, O Master, holy and true, until YouyouYou judge and avenge our blood upon those who dwell on the earth?” 11And to each of them was given a white robe; and it was said to them that they should rest a little while longer, until their fellow servants and their brothers, who were destined to be killed even as they had been, should be brought to their full number.

The Sixth Seal and the Great Day of Wrath

12And I saw when HeheHe opened the sixth seal, and there came a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became like blood. 13And the stars of Heavenheavenheaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree casts its unripe figs when it is shaken by a great wind. 14And the sky was parted like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved out of its place. 15And the kings of the earth, and the great ones, and the commanders, and the rich, and the strong, and every slave and free man, hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains.

16And they said to the mountains and to the rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of HimhimHim who sits on the throne, and from the wrath of the LamblambLamb, 17for the great day of TheirtheirTheir wrath has come, and who is able to stand?”
