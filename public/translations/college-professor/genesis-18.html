The LORD Appears to Abraham at Mamre

1The LORD appeared to Abraham by the oaks of Mamre while he was sitting at the entrance of his tent in the heat of the day. 2He lifted his eyes and saw three men standing near him. When he saw them, he ran from the tent door to meet them and bowed to the ground.

3He said, "MymyMy Lord, if I have found favourfavourfavor in YouryourYour eyes, please do not pass by YouryourYour servant. 4Let a little water be brought, that you may wash your feet and rest under the tree. 5I will bring a morsel of bread, that you may refresh your hearts—for you have come to your servant."

They said, "Do as you have said."

6So Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Hurry! Take three seahs of fine flour, knead it, and make cakes." 7Then Abraham ran to the herd, took a tender and choice calf, and gave it to a servant, who hurried to prepare it. 8He took curds and milk and the calf that had been prepared, set them before them, and stood by them under the tree while they ate.

A Divine Promise of a Son

9They said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?"

He said, "She is in the tent."

10And the LORD said, "I will surely return to you at this time next year, and behold, Sarah your wife shall have a son." Now Sarah was listening at the tent door behind him.

11Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years; the way of women had ceased for Sarah.

12So Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "After I am worn out and my lord is old, shall I have such pleasure?"

13The LORD said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh, saying, 'Shall I indeed bear a child, now that I am old?'"

14"IsisIs anything too hard for the LORD? At the appointed time I will return to you, about this time next year, and Sarah shall have a son."

15But Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid.

He said, "No, but you did laugh."

The LORD Discloses His Intentions Concerning Sodom

16Then the men rose up from there and looked toward Sodom, and Abraham went with them to send them on their way.

17And the LORD said, "Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do, 18seeing that Abraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him? 19For I have known him, that he may command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the LORD, to do righteousness and justice, so that the LORD may bring to Abraham what HeheHe has promised him."

20Then the LORD said, "Because the outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great and their sin is exceedingly grave, 21I will go down and see whether they have done altogether according to the outcry that has come to MemeMe; and if not, I will know."

Abraham Intercedes on Behalf of Sodom

26The LORD said, "If I find at Sodom fifty righteous within the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake."

27Abraham said, "Behold, I have undertaken to speak to the Lord, though I am but dust and ashes. 28Suppose five of the fifty righteous are lacking: will YouyouYou destroy the whole city for lack of five?"

The LORD said, "I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there."

29Abraham said, "Suppose forty are found there."

The LORD said, "For the sake of forty I will not do it."

30Abraham said, "Oh, let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak. Suppose thirty are found there."

The LORD said, "I will not do it if I find thirty there."

31Abraham said, "Behold, I have undertaken to speak to the Lord. Suppose twenty are found there."

The LORD said, "For the sake of twenty I will not destroy it."

32Abraham said, "Oh, let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak once more. Suppose ten are found there."
