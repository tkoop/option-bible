Exhortation to Concord and Humility

1Therefore, if there is any encouragement in Christ, if any solace of love, if any communion of the SpiritspiritSpirit, if any tender affections and mercies, 2complete my joy by being of one mind, possessing the same love, being united in spirit and intent upon one purpose. 3Do nothing from selfish ambition or vainglory, but in humility regard one another as surpassing yourselves. 4Let each of you look not only to your own interests, but also to the interests of others.

The Kenosis and Exaltation of Christ

5Have this mind among you which was also in Christ Jesus: 6Who, though existing in the form of God, did not regard equality with God as something to be seized, 7but emptied HimselfhimselfHimself, taking the form of a servant, being born in the likeness of men. 8And being found in appearance as a man, HeheHe humbled HimselfhimselfHimself and became obedient unto death—even death on a cross.

9Therefore God exalted HimhimHim to the highest place and bestowed upon HimhimHim the name above every name, 10so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of those in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth and under the earth, 11and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the FatherfatherFather.

Working Out Salvation with Fear and Trembling

12Therefore, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed, not only in my presence but now much more in my absence, work out your salvation with fear and trembling; 13for it is God who works in you, both to will and to work, for HishisHis good pleasure. 14Do all things without grumbling or disputing, 15that you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as luminaries in the world, 16holding fast the word of life, so that I may boast on the day of Christ that I did not run in vain nor labourlabourlabor in vain.

17But even if I am being poured out as a libation upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and share joy with all of you. 18Likewise you also should rejoice and share joy with me.

Commendation of Timothy and Epaphroditus

19Now I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I too may be encouraged when I learn of your circumstances. 20For I have no one like-minded who will genuinely care for your concerns; 21for all seek their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. 22But you know his proven worth, that as a child serves a father, so he served with me in the gospel. 23Therefore I hope to send him immediately, as soon as I see how things go with me. 24And I am confident in the Lord that I myself will come soon.

25But I deemed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier, who is also your messenger and minister to my need, 26since he was longing for all of you and was distressed because you heard that he was ill. 27For indeed he was ill, near to death; but God had mercy on him, and not only on him but also on me, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow. 28Therefore I am the more eager to send him, so that when you see him again you may rejoice, and I may be less sorrowful. 29Receive him, then, in the Lord with all joy, and hold such men in honourhonourhonor, 30because for the work of Christ he drew near to death, risking his life to make up for your deficiency of service to me.
