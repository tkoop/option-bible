The Prophet's Lamentation over a Devastated Land

1Alas for me, for I have become like one who gathers the summer harvest, like a gleaner after the vintage; no cluster remains to be eaten, nor any first-ripe fig to gratify my longing. 2The devout have vanished from the land, and among humankind no upright person remains; all lie in ambush for bloodshed, each hunting his brother with a net.

3Their hands are consummately skilled in mischief: the prince and the judge solicit a bribe; the magnate proclaims the craving of his soul, and they concertedly pervert the matter. 4The best of them is like a brier, and the most upright more noxious than a thorn hedge. The day foretold by your watchmen has arrived; now their perplexity shall overtake them.

5Place no trust in a neighbourneighbourneighbor; repose no confidence in an intimate friend; guard the portals of your mouth from the woman who reclines in your embrace. 6For a son treats his father with contempt, a daughter rises against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's adversaries are the members of his own household.

Confidence in the LORD Amid Collapse

7But as for me, I shall watch for the the LORD; I shall wait for the God of my salvation; my God will hear me.

8Do not exult over me, my enemy; though I have fallen, I shall rise again; though I sit in darkness, the the LORD shall be my light. 9Because I have sinned against HimhimHim, I shall bear the indignation of the the LORD until HeheHe pleads my cause and executes judgmentjudgementjudgment on my behalf; HeheHe will bring me forth into the light, and I shall behold HishisHis righteousness.

10Then my enemy shall see it and be clothed with shame—she who said to me, “Where is the the LORD your God?” My eyes shall behold her; now she shall be trampled as mire in the streets.

11A day shall come for rebuilding your walls; on that day the boundary shall be extended afar. 12In that day they shall come to you from Assyria and from the cities of Egypt, from Egypt to the River, from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain. 13Yet the earth shall become desolate because of its inhabitants, on account of the fruit of their deeds.

Shepherding, Restoration, and the Triumph of Mercy

14Shepherd YouryourYour people with YouryourYour staff, the flock of YouryourYour inheritance, who dwell alone in the forest, in the midst of Carmel; let them graze in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old.

15“As in the days when you came out of Egypt, I will show them MymyMy wonders.”

16The nations shall see and be put to shame, despite all their might; they shall lay their hand upon their mouth, and their ears shall become deaf. 17They shall lick the dust like a serpent, like crawling things of the earth; they shall come trembling out of their strongholds; they shall turn in dread to the the LORD our God, and they shall be afraid of you.

18Who is a God like YouyouYou, pardoning iniquity and passing over the transgression of the remnant of HishisHis inheritance? He does not retain HishisHis anger forever, because HeheHe delights in steadfast love. 19He will again have compassion on us; HeheHe will tread our iniquities underfoot and cast all their sins into the depths of the sea. 20You will show faithfulness to Jacob and steadfast love to Abraham, as YouyouYou swore to our fathers from days of old.
