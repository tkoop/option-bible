The Silver Trumpets and Their Use

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Make for yourself two silver trumpets of hammered work; they shall be for you for summoning the congregation and for breaking camp. 3When they are blown, the whole congregation shall assemble before you at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 4If only one is blown, then the leaders, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall assemble before you. 5When you blow an alarm, the camps encamped on the east shall set out. 6When you blow an alarm a second time, the camps encamped on the south shall set out; an alarm shall be blown for their departures. 7But when you assemble the assembly, you shall blow without sounding an alarm. 8The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets; this shall be a perpetual statute for you throughout your generations. 9When you go to war in your land against an enemy who oppresses you, you shall sound an alarm on the trumpets, and you shall be remembered before the LORD your God and be saved from your enemies. 10On the day of your gladness, at your appointed festivals, and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and your peace offerings; they shall serve as a memorial for you before your God. I am the LORD your God.”

Israel Sets Out from Sinai

11On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year, the cloud rose from above the tabernacle of the testimony. 12Then the Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sinai according to their journeys, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran. 13They set out for the first time at the command of the LORD through Moses.

14In the first place, according to their companies, the standard of the camp of Judah set out, with Nahshon son of Amminadab over its host; 15Nethanel son of Zuar over the host of Issachar; 16and Eliab son of Helon over the host of Zebulun. 17Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set out, carrying the tabernacle. 18Next the standard of the camp of Reuben set out, according to its companies, with Elizur son of Shedeur over its host; 19Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai over the host of Simeon; 20and Eliasaph son of Deuel over the host of Gad. 21Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the holy things, and the tabernacle was erected before their arrival. 22After them the standard of the camp of Ephraim set out, according to its companies, with Elishama son of Ammihud over its host; 23Gamaliel son of Pedahzur over the host of Manasseh; 24and Abidan son of Gideoni over the host of Benjamin. 25Finally, the standard of the camp of Dan set out, as the rear guard of all the camps, with Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai over its host; 26Pagiel son of Ochran over the host of Asher; 27and Ahira son of Enan over the host of Naphtali. 28These were the journeys of the Israelites according to their companies, and so they set out.

Moses Entreats Hobab

29Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ father-in-law, “We are journeying to the place of which the LORD said, ‘I will give it to you.’ Come with us, and we will do you good, for the LORD has spoken good concerning Israel.”

30Hobab said to him, “I will not go; I will return to my own land and to my kindred.”

31Moses replied, “Please do not forsake us, for you know how we camp in the wilderness, and you shall be eyes for us. 32If you go with us, then the good that the LORD does for us, we will do for you.”

The Ark and the Cloud Lead the People

33They set out from the mountain of the LORD on a three days’ journey, with the ark of the covenant of the LORD going before them for three days to seek out a resting place for them. 34The cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp. 35Whenever the ark set out, Moses said, “Rise up, O LORD! Let YouryourYour enemies be scattered, and let those who hate YouyouYou flee before YouyouYou.” 36And when it came to rest, he said, “Return, O LORD, to the countless thousands of Israel.”
