The Statute Governing the Purification of a Leper

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“This is the statute governing the leper for the day of his cleansing: he must be conducted to the priest. 3The priest shall repair outside the camp and inspect him; and if the leprous malady has been healed in the leper,

4“then the priest shall direct that two living, ritually clean birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop be taken for the party undergoing purification. 5The priest shall also direct that one of the birds be slain over fresh water in an earthenware vessel. 6As for the living bird, he shall take it, together with the cedar wood, the scarlet yarn, and the hyssop, and dip them, along with the living bird, into the blood of the bird that was slain, over the fresh water. 7He shall asperse seven times the one being purified from the leprous disease, and shall declare him clean; then he shall set the living bird free over the open country.

8“The one being purified shall launder his garments, depilate all his hair, and bathe in water, and he shall be clean; thereafter he may enter the camp, though he shall remain outside his tent for seven days. 9On the seventh day he shall remove all his hair—his head, his beard, his eyebrows, and every other hair; he shall wash his garments and bathe his flesh in water, and he shall be clean.

The Eighth-Day Offerings of the Cleansed Leper

10“On the eighth day he shall take two unblemished male lambs, one yearling ewe, three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour commingled with oil as a grain offering, and a log of oil. 11The officiating priest shall present the man, together with these offerings, before the the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting. 12The priest shall take one of the lambs and present it as a guilt offering, with the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the the LORD. 13He shall slay the lamb at the site where the sin offering and the burnt offering are slaughtered, within the holy place; for the guilt offering, like the sin offering, pertains to the priest and is most holy.

14“The priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering and place it on the right earlobe of the one being purified, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 15The priest shall take some of the log of oil and pour it into the hollow of his own left palm. 16The priest shall dip his right finger into the oil in his left palm and sprinkle of that oil with his finger seven times before the the LORD. 17From the remainder of the oil in his palm, the priest shall place some upon the right earlobe of the one being purified, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot, over the blood of the guilt offering. 18The leftover oil in the priest's palm he shall apply to the head of the one being purified; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the the LORD.

19“Then the priest shall perform the sin offering and effect atonement for the one being purified from his impurity; thereafter he shall slaughter the burnt offering. 20The priest shall offer the burnt offering and the grain offering upon the altar; thus the priest shall effect atonement for him, and he shall be clean.

Provision for the Impoverished Leper

21“If, however, he is indigent and his means do not suffice for these provisions, then he shall take one male lamb as a guilt offering, to be waved for his atonement, and one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mingled with oil for a grain offering, together with a log of oil. 22And two turtledoves or two young pigeons, according to what he can afford, one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering. 23On the eighth day he shall bring them for his purification to the priest, at the entrance of the tent of meeting, before the the LORD.

24“The priest shall take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the the LORD. 25He shall slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering, and the priest shall take some of its blood and place it on the right earlobe of the one being purified, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot. 26The priest shall pour some of the oil into the hollow of his own left palm. 27The priest shall, with his right finger, sprinkle some of the oil in his left palm seven times before the the LORD.

28“The priest shall put some of the oil that is in his palm on the right earlobe of the one being purified, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot, on the site of the blood of the guilt offering. 29The rest of the oil in the priest's palm he shall put on the head of the one being purified, to make atonement for him before the the LORD. 30He shall offer one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever his means allow, 31one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, together with the grain offering; and the priest shall make atonement before the the LORD for the one being purified. 32This is the statute for the leper afflicted with a leprous disease, whose means do not suffice for the offering customary to his cleansing.”

Leprosy in a Dwelling and Its Remediation

33The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 34“When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving to you for a possession, and I put a leprous disease in a house in the land of your possession, 35then the owner of the house shall come and report to the priest, saying, ‘It appears to me that there is a plague in the house.’ 36The priest shall direct that the house be emptied before he comes to inspect the plague, lest everything in the house become unclean; then the priest shall come to inspect the house.

37“He shall inspect the plague; and if the plague is in the walls of the house as greenish or reddish depressions that appear to sink deeper than the surface of the wall, 38then the priest shall withdraw from the house to its doorway and quarantine the house for seven days. 39On the seventh day the priest shall return and inspect it; and if the plague has spread in the walls of the house, 40then the priest shall order that the stones containing the plague be removed and cast into an unclean place outside the city.

41“The interior of the house shall be scraped all around, and the dust scraped off shall be poured out outside the city in an unclean place. 42They shall insert other stones in place of the removed stones, and he shall take other mortar and replaster the house. 43If the plague breaks out again in the house after he has taken out the stones and after the house has been scraped and plastered, 44then the priest shall come and inspect it; and if the plague has spread in the house, it is a malignant leprosy in the house—it is unclean.

45“He shall demolish the house—its stones, its timber, and all its mortar—and remove them outside the city to an unclean place. 46Furthermore, anyone who enters the house during the whole period of its confinement shall be unclean until evening. 47Whoever lies down in the house shall wash his garments, and whoever eats in the house shall wash his garments. 48If, however, the priest comes and inspects it and the plague has not spread in the house after it was plastered, then the priest shall declare the house clean, because the plague has healed.

49“To purify the house, he shall take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop. 50He shall slaughter one of the birds over fresh water in an earthenware vessel. 51He shall take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn, and the living bird, dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times. 52He shall purify the house with the blood of the bird, the fresh water, the living bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop, and the scarlet yarn. 53He shall set the living bird free outside the city over the open country; thus he shall make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

54“This is the statute for every sort of leprous disease and scall, 55for the leprosy of a garment and of a house, 56and for a swelling, a scab, and a bright spot, 57to instruct concerning uncleanness and cleanness. This is the law of leprosy.”
