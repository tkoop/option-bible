A Solemn Assembly of Fasting and Confession

1On the twenty-fourth day of this month, the Israelites assembled in fasting, clad in sackcloth, with dust upon them. 2The seed of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners and stood, confessing their own sins and the iniquities of their fathers. 3They stood in their place and read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD their God for a quarter of the day, and for another quarter they confessed and worshiped the LORD their God. 4Upon the stairs stood the Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani—and they cried out with a loud voice to the LORD their God.

A Confession of Present Distress and a Plea for Mercy

32"Now therefore, O our God, the great, the mighty, and the awesome God, who keeps covenant and steadfast love, let not all the hardship that has come upon us—upon our kings, our princes, our priests, our prophets, our fathers, and all YouryourYour people—from the days of the kings of Assyria to this day, seem insignificant before YouyouYou. 33You are righteous in all that has come upon us, for YouyouYou have dealt faithfully while we have acted wickedly. 34Our kings, our princes, our priests, and our fathers have not kept YouryourYour law or listened to YouryourYour commandments and YouryourYour testimonies with which YouyouYou testified against them. 35Even in their kingdom, and amid the great goodness that YouyouYou gave them, and in the broad and rich land that YouyouYou set before them, they did not serve YouyouYou or turn from their wicked deeds.

36"Behold, we are slaves this day; and the land that YouyouYou gave to our fathers, to eat its fruit and its goodness—behold, we are slaves upon it. 37Its abundant produce is for the kings whom YouyouYou have set over us because of our sins; they rule over our bodies and over our livestock as they please, and we are in great distress. 38Because of all this, we are making a firm covenant and writing it down; and upon the sealed document are our princes, our Levites, and our priests."
