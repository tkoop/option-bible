Nebuchadnezzar’s Disturbing Dream and Royal Decree

1In the second year of Nebuchadnezzar’s reign, Nebuchadnezzar had dreams; his spirit was deeply agitated, and sleep fled from him. 2The king commanded that the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and Chaldeans be summoned to declare to the king his dreams. They came and stood before the king. 3The king said to them, “I have had a dream, and my spirit is disquieted to know the dream.”

4The Chaldeans then addressed the king in Aramaic: “Long live the king! Tell your servants the dream, and we will declare its interpretation.”

5The king answered the Chaldeans, “My decree is final: if you do not make known to me the dream and its interpretation, you shall be dismembered, and your houses reduced to rubble. 6But if you show the dream and its interpretation, you shall receive gifts, lavish rewards, and great honourhonourhonor. Therefore show me the dream and its interpretation.”

7They answered a second time, “Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will show its interpretation.”

8The king answered, “I know with certainty that you are stalling for time, because you perceive that my decree is irrevocable. 9If you do not make known the dream to me, there is one uniform sentence for you all; for you have agreed to speak lying and corrupt words before me until circumstances change. Therefore tell me the dream, and I shall know that you can show the interpretation.”

10The Chaldeans answered the king, “There is no man on EarthEarthearth who can show the king’s matter, for no great and powerful king has ever asked such a thing of any magician, enchanter, or Chaldean. 11The thing the king asks is arduous, and no one can show it except the gods, whose dwelling is not among mortals.”

12Because of this the king was incensed and exceedingly wrathful, and he commanded that all the wise men of Babylon be destroyed. 13The decree went out, and the wise men were being killed; and they sought Daniel and his companions, that they too might be put to death.

Daniel Intervenes Amid the Death Decree

14Then Daniel replied with prudence and tact to Arioch, the captain of the king’s guard, who had gone out to execute the wise men of Babylon. 15He answered Arioch, the king’s officer, “Why is the king’s decree so urgent?” Then Arioch made the matter known to Daniel. 16Daniel went in and requested of the king that he would grant him time, and that he would show the king the interpretation.

17Then Daniel went to his house and made the matter known to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, his companions, 18that they might seek mercy from the God of Heavenheavenheaven concerning this mystery, so that Daniel and his companions might not perish with the rest of the wise men of Babylon. 19Then the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a night vision. Then Daniel blessed the God of Heavenheavenheaven.

20Daniel answered and said, “Blessed be the name of God from everlasting to everlasting, for wisdom and might are HishisHis. 21He changes times and seasons; HeheHe removes kings and establishes kings; HeheHe gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who have understanding. 22He reveals deep and hidden things; HeheHe knows what is in darkness, and light dwells with HimhimHim. 23To YouyouYou, O God of my fathers, I give thanks and praise, for YouyouYou have given me wisdom and might, and have now made known to me what we asked of YouyouYou, for YouyouYou have made known to us the king’s matter.”

The Dream Revealed and Its Interpretation Declared

24Therefore Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon; he went and said, “Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon; bring me before the king, and I will show the king the interpretation.”

25Then Arioch brought Daniel before the king in haste and said, “I have found a man among the exiles of Judah who will make known to the king the interpretation.”

26The king answered Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, “Are you able to make known to me the dream I saw and its interpretation?”

27Daniel answered before the king, “No wise men, enchanters, magicians, or astrologers can show the king the mystery that the king asks. 28But there is a God in Heavenheavenheaven who reveals mysteries, and HeheHe has made known to King Nebuchadnezzar what will be in the latter days. Your dream and the visions of your head upon your bed are these:”

29“As for you, O king, your thoughts arose upon your bed as to what would come after this; and HeheHe who reveals mysteries made known to you what will be. 30But as for me, this mystery has been revealed to me, not because I have more wisdom than all the living, but so that the interpretation may be made known to the king, and that you may know the thoughts of your heart.”

31“You, O king, were looking, and behold, a great image. This image, mighty and of surpassing brightness, stood before you, and its appearance was terrifying. 32The head of the image was fine gold; its breast and arms were silver; its belly and thighs were bronze; 33its legs were iron; its feet were partly iron and partly clay.”

34“You watched until a stone was cut out without hands, and it struck the image upon its feet of iron and clay and shattered them. 35Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold were crushed together and became like chaff from the summer threshing floors; the wind carried them away, and they vanished without a trace. But the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled the whole EarthEarthearth. 36This is the dream; now we will tell its interpretation before the king.”

37“You, O king, are king of kings, to whom the God of Heavenheavenheaven has given the kingdom, the power, the strength, and the glory. 38Wherever the sons of men dwell, the beasts of the field and the birds of the Heavenheavenheaven HeheHe has given into your hand, and has made you ruler over them all. You are the head of gold.”

39“After you another kingdom will arise, inferior to yours, and then a third kingdom of bronze, which shall rule over all the earth. 40A fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron, because iron breaks in pieces and shatters all things; and like iron that crushes, it shall break in pieces and crush.”

41“Whereas you saw the feet and toes, partly of baked clay and partly of iron, the kingdom shall be divided; yet the strength of iron shall be in it, just as you saw iron mixed with miry clay. 42The toes of the feet, partly iron and partly clay: the kingdom shall be partly strong and partly brittle. 43Whereas you saw iron mixed with miry clay, they shall mingle with the seed of men, but they shall not adhere one to another, just as iron does not mix with clay.”

44“In the days of those kings, the God of Heavenheavenheaven will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed; nor shall its sovereignty be left to another people; it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever. 45Inasmuch as you saw that a stone was cut from the mountain without hands, and that it broke in pieces the iron, the bronze, the clay, the silver, and the gold—the great God has made known to the king what shall come after this. The dream is certain, and its interpretation is trustworthy.”

Daniel Exalted and His Companions Advanced

46Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell upon his face and paid homage to Daniel, and commanded that an offering and incense be offered to him. 47The king answered Daniel, “Truly your God is God of gods and Lord of kings and Revealer of mysteries, since you were able to reveal this mystery.”

48Then the king made Daniel great, and gave him many great gifts, and made him ruler over the whole province of Babylon and chief prefect over all the wise men of Babylon. 49Daniel requested of the king, and he appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of the province of Babylon; but Daniel was at the gate of the king.
