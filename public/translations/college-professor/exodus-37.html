The Fashioning of the Ark of the Covenant

1Bezalel fabricated the ark from acacia timber, its dimensions being two and a half cubits in length, a cubit and a half in breadth, and a cubit and a half in height. 2He sheathed it with pure gold, both within and without, and fashioned a gold molding encircling it. 3He cast four gold rings for it and affixed them to its four feet, placing two rings upon one side and two upon the other. 4He then fashioned poles of acacia timber and sheathed them with gold. 5He inserted the poles into the rings along the ark's sides, thereby equipping it for transport.

6He fashioned the mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits in length and a cubit and a half in breadth. 7He made two cherubim of hammered gold at the mercy seat's extremities, 8one cherub at either end, wrought as one continuous piece with the mercy seat itself. 9The cherubim spread their wings upward, overshadowing the mercy seat with their pinions; they faced one another, their gaze directed toward the mercy seat.

The Table of the Presence

10He fashioned the table from acacia timber, two cubits in length, a cubit in breadth, and a cubit and a half in height. 11He sheathed it with pure gold and fashioned a gold molding encircling it. 12He fashioned a rim about it, a handbreadth in width, and set a gold molding upon that rim.

13He cast four gold rings for it and fastened them to the four corners at its four legs. 14The rings lay adjacent to the rim, serving as housings for the poles by which the table was carried. 15He fashioned the poles from acacia timber and sheathed them with gold, so that the table might be borne. 16He fashioned the table's utensils—its plates, dishes, bowls, and libation pitchers—out of pure gold.

The Golden Lampstand

17He fashioned the lampstand of pure gold, hammered work throughout; its base, shaft, cups, buds, and petals were all of one piece. 18Six branches extended from its sides—three from one side and three from the other. 19On one branch were three cups fashioned like almond blossoms, each with buds and petals, and likewise on the next branch, and so for all six branches extending from the lampstand.

20Upon the lampstand itself were four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals. 21A bud was situated beneath the first pair of branches, a bud beneath the second pair, and a bud beneath the third pair, for the six branches in all. 22Their buds and branches were all of one piece with the lampstand, hammered out of pure gold. 23He also fashioned seven lamps for it, together with wick trimmers and trays, of pure gold. 24He fashioned the lampstand and all its utensils from a talent of pure gold.

The Altar of Incense and the Sacred Compounds

25He fashioned the altar of incense from acacia timber. It was square, a cubit in length and a cubit in breadth, and two cubits in height; its horns were of one piece with it. 26He sheathed it with pure gold—its top, its sides, and its horns—and fashioned a gold molding encircling it. 27He made two gold rings below the molding on opposite sides, to hold the poles by which it was carried. 28He fashioned the poles from acacia timber and sheathed them with gold.

29He also prepared the holy anointing oil and the pure, fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.
