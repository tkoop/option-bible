Solomon’s Alliance with Pharaoh and Worship at the High Places

1Solomon contracted a marital alliance with Pharaoh, king of Egypt; he took Pharaoh’s daughter and brought her into the CitycityCity of David, where she remained until he had completed his own residence, the house of the LORD, and the wall surrounding Jerusalem. 2Meanwhile, the people continued to sacrifice at the high places, for no house had yet been built for the name of the LORD. 3Solomon loved the LORD, walking in the statutes of David his father; nevertheless, he himself sacrificed and burned incense at the high places. 4The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for it was the preeminent high place; upon that altar Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings.

The LORD Grants Solomon Wisdom

15Then Solomon awoke, and behold, it had been a dream. He came to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and made a feast for all his servants.

Solomon’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment in the Dispute of the Two Mothers

16Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him. 17The one woman said, “O my lord, this woman and I live in the same house, and I gave birth while she was in the house. 18On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were alone together; no stranger was with us in the house, only we two. 19And this woman’s son died in the night, because she lay on him. 20She arose at midnight and took my son from beside me, while I, your maidservant, slept, and laid him at her breast, and laid her dead son at my breast. 21When I rose in the morning to nurse my son, behold, he was dead. But when I examined him carefully in the morning, behold, he was not the son I had borne.”

22The other woman said, “No; the living child is mine, and the dead child is yours.”

But the first said, “No; the dead child is yours, and the living child is mine.” Thus they argued before the king.

23The king said, “The one says, ‘This is my living son, and your son is dead’; and the other says, ‘No, your son is dead, and my son is the living one.’”

24The king said, “Bring me a sword.” So they brought a sword before the king.

25The king said, “Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one and half to the other.”

26Then the woman whose son was alive—because her compassion yearned for her son—said to the king, “O my lord, give her the living child, and by no means put him to death.”

But the other said, “Let him be neither mine nor yours; divide him.”

27The king answered, “Give the living child to the first woman, and do not kill him; she is his mother.”

28When all Israel heard the judgmentjudgementjudgment that the king had rendered, they stood in awe of the king, because they perceived that the wisdom of God was in him to execute justice.
