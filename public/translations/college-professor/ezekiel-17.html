The Riddle of the Two Eagles and the Vine

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2"SonsonSon of man, propound a riddle and speak a parable to the house of Israel. 3And you shall say, Thus says the Lord the LORD: A great eagle with great wings, long pinions, and full plumage of variegated feathers came to Lebanon and took the top of the cedar. 4He cropped off the topmost of its young twigs and carried it to a land of Canaan; in a city of merchants he set it. 5He also took some of the seed of the land and planted it in a fertile field; he placed it beside abundant waters; he set it like a willow. 6It sprouted and became a spreading vine of low stature, its branches turning toward him, while its roots remained beneath him; so it became a vine and produced branches and sent out boughs. 7There was another great eagle with great wings and much plumage; and behold, this vine bent its roots toward him and sent out its branches toward him from the bed where it was planted, that he might water it. 8It was planted in good soil beside abundant waters, that it might produce branches and bear fruit and become a splendid vine. 9Say, Thus says the Lord the LORD: Will it thrive? Will he not pull up its roots and cut off its fruit, so that it withers—all its fresh sprouting leaves withering? It will not take a mighty arm or a multitude of people to pluck it up by its roots. 10Behold, though planted, will it thrive? When the east wind strikes it, will it not wither? On the beds where it grew it will wither."

The Interpretation of the Riddle

11The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 12"SonsonSon of man, say now to the rebellious house, 'Do you not know what these things mean?' Say, 'Behold, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took its king and its princes and brought them to him in Babylon.' 13HeheHe took one of the royal seed and made a covenant with him, binding him by oath; he also took away the chief men of the land, 14that the kingdom might be humble, not exalting itself, but keeping his covenant, that it might stand. 15But he rebelled against him by sending his envoys to Egypt to obtain horses and many people. Will he prosper? Will he escape who does such things? Can he break the covenant and still escape? 16As I live, declares the Lord the LORD, surely in the place of the king who made him king—whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke—there in Babylon he shall die. 17Pharaoh with his mighty army and great company will not avail for him in war, when siege ramps are thrown up and siege walls built to cut off many lives. 18He despised the oath by breaking the covenant; though he gave his hand, having done all these things, he shall not escape. 19Therefore thus says the Lord the LORD: As I live, surely MymyMy oath, which he despised, and MymyMy covenant, which he broke, I will bring upon his own head. 20I will spread MymyMy net over him, and he shall be caught in MymyMy snare; I will bring him to Babylon and enter into judgmentjudgementjudgment with him there for his trespass committed against MemeMe. 21All his fugitives in all his bands shall fall by the sword, and the survivors shall be scattered to every wind; and you shall know that I am the LORD; I have spoken."

The Promise of a Restored Cedar

22"Thus says the Lord the LORD: I will also take from the top of the tall cedar a tender sprig from the topmost of its young twigs, and I will plant it on a high and lofty mountain. 23On the mountain height of Israel I will plant it; it shall bear branches and produce fruit and become a noble cedar. Birds of every kind will nest under it; in the shade of its branches they will dwell. 24All the trees of the field shall know that I am the LORD; I bring low the high tree, exalt the low tree, dry up the green tree, and make the dry tree flourish. I, the LORD, have spoken and done it."
