A Psalm of David, for the Choirmaster, for Jeduthun

1I said, “I will guard my ways so that I do not sin with my tongue; I will restrain my mouth as with a muzzle while the wicked stand before me.” 2I was utterly silent; I held back even from speaking what was good, and my grief was inflamed. 3My heart burned within me; as I mused, the fire kindled, and then I spoke with my tongue.

The Measure of Human Frailty

4The LORD, make me to know my end, and the measure of my days, that I may know how transient I am. 5Behold, YouyouYou have measured out my days in handbreadths, and my whole span is as nothing before YouyouYou; surely all mankind at its best is but a breath. Selah. 6Surely a man walks as a mere shadow; surely they bustle about in vain; he heaps up wealth and does not know who will gather it.

A Plea for Deliverance from Divine Discipline

7And now, O Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in YouyouYou. 8Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me an object of scorn to the fool. 9I was mute; I did not open my mouth, for YouyouYou have done it. 10Remove YouryourYour affliction from me; I am consumed by the stroke of YouryourYour hand. 11When YouyouYou discipline a man with rebukes for iniquity, YouyouYou consume his prized possessions like a moth; surely all mankind is but a breath. Selah. 12Hear my prayer, O the LORD; give ear to my cry; do not be deaf to my tears; for I am a stranger with YouyouYou, a sojourner, as all my fathers were. 13Turn YouryourYour gaze from me, that I may find relief, before I depart and am no more.
