Solomon's Building Program and Settlements

1At the conclusion of the twenty years during which Solomon had erected the house of the LORD and his own palace, 2he rebuilt the cities that Huram had given him and settled the Israelites in them. 3Solomon then marched against Hamath-zobah and subjugated it. 4He built Tadmor in the wilderness, together with all the store-cities he had constructed in Hamath. 5He also fortified Upper Beth-horon and LowerlowerLower Beth-horon, transforming them into walled cities with gates and bars. 6Moreover, he built Baalath and all the store-cities belonging to Solomon, along with the chariot cities, the cavalry cities, and every other project that Solomon desired to undertake in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout the whole domain of his sovereignty.

Conscription of the Remaining Canaanites

7All the people who remained from the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—those not belonging to Israel— 8namely, their descendants who had survived in the land after them, whom the Israelites had not annihilated, Solomon conscripted into forced labourlabourlabor, and they remain subject to it to this day. 9Yet Solomon did not consign any Israelite to servitude for his labourlabourlabor, because they were warriors, chief officers, commanders of his chariots, and cavalrymen. 10These were the two hundred fifty chief officers who supervised the people on the king's behalf.
