The Shadow of the Law and the Inadequacy of Its Sacrifices

1For the law, though it possesses merely a shadow of the good things to come rather than the very image of those realities, can never, by the same sacrifices that are offered year after year, render perfect those who draw near. 2Otherwise, would they not have ceased to be offered, since the worshipers, once cleansed, would no longer have any consciousness of sins? 3But in these sacrifices there is an annual remembrance of sins. 4For it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to remove sins.
