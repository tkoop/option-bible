The Oracular Vision of Obadiah and the Divine Indictment of Edom

1The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord the LORD concerning Edom: We have heard a report from the the LORD, and an envoy has been dispatched among the nations, saying, “Arise, and let us rise up against her in battle.”

2Behold, I will reduce you to insignificance among the nations; you will be held in utter contempt. 3The arrogance of your heart has beguiled you, O inhabitant of the clefts of the rock, whose dwelling is in the heights, who says in his heart, “Who shall bring me down to the earth?” 4Though you exalt yourself like the eagle, and though you set your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down, declares the the LORD.

5If thieves were to come to you, if marauders by night—how utterly you would be ruined!—would they not steal only as much as they needed? If grape gatherers were to come to you, would they not leave gleanings? 6How thoroughly Esau will be pillaged, his concealed treasures plundered!

7All your allies have driven you to the border; your confederates have deceived and overpowered you; those who eat your bread have laid a snare beneath you; there is no discernment in him. 8On that day, declares the the LORD, will I not destroy the wise out of Edom and discernment from Mount Esau? 9Then your mighty men, O Teman, will be dismayed, and every man on Mount Esau will be cut down in the slaughter.

Edom's Violence against Jacob and the Day of Reckoning

10Because of the violence against your brother Jacob, shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever. 11On the day you stood aloof, on the day strangers carried off his wealth and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots for Jerusalem, you were like one of them.

12But do not gloat over your brother in the day of his calamity; do not rejoice over the people of Judah in the day of their ruin; do not boast in the day of distress. 13Do not enter the gate of MymyMy people in the day of their calamity; do not gloat over his disaster in the day of his calamity; do not loot his wealth in the day of his calamity. 14Do not stand at the crossroads to cut off his fugitives; do not surrender his survivors in the day of distress.

15For the day of the the LORD is near for all the nations. As you have done, it will be done to you; your recompense will return upon your own head. 16For as you have drunk on MymyMy holy mountain, so all the nations will drink continually; they will drink and swallow, and they will be as though they had never been.

The Restoration of Jacob and the Establishment of the Divine Kingdom

17But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance, and it will be holy, and the house of Jacob will take possession of its inheritance. 18The house of Jacob will be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble; they will burn and consume them, and there will be no survivor for the house of Esau, for the the LORD has spoken.

19Those of the Negev will possess Mount Esau, and those of the lowland will possess the land of the Philistines; they will possess the fields of Ephraim and Samaria, and Benjamin will possess Gilead. 20The exiles of the Israelites in Canaan will possess the land as far as Zarephath, and the exiles of Jerusalem in Sepharad will possess the cities of the Negev.

21Deliverers will ascend Mount Zion to judge Mount Esau, and the kingdom will belong to the the LORD.
