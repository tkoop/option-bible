A Petition for Divine Refuge and Deliverance

1In YouyouYou, the LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame; deliver me in YouryourYour righteousness. 2Incline YouryourYour ear to me; rescue me swiftly. Be my rock of refuge, a fortified stronghold to save me. 3For YouyouYou are my rock and my fortress; for the sake of YouryourYour name, lead and guide me. 4Free me from the snare they have hidden for me, for YouyouYou are my refuge.

5Into YouryourYour hands I commit my spirit; YouyouYou have redeemed me, the LORD, God of truth. 6I abhor those who cling to worthless idols, but as for me, I trust in the LORD. 7I will exult and rejoice in YouryourYour loving devotion, for YouyouYou have seen my affliction and YouyouYou have known the anguish of my soul. 8You have not surrendered me to the enemy; YouyouYou have set my feet in a spacious place.

9Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am in distress; my eyes grow dim with sorrow, and my soul and body with grief. 10For my life is consumed with anguish and my years with groaning; my strength falters because of my iniquity, and my bones waste away. 11Because of all my adversaries I have become a reproach—especially to my neighbors—and an object of dread to my acquaintances; those who see me in the street flee from me. 12I am forgotten as though dead; I have become like a shattered vessel. 13For I hear the whispering of many—“Terror on every side!”—as they conspire together against me and plot to take my life.

14But I trust in YouyouYou, the LORD; I say, “You are my God.” 15My times are in YouryourYour hands; deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from my pursuers. 16Let YouryourYour face shine upon YouryourYour servant; save me in YouryourYour steadfast love.

17The LORD, let me not be put to shame, for I call upon YouyouYou; let the wicked be put to shame and be silenced in Sheol. 18Let lying lips be silenced—those that speak arrogantly against the righteous with pride and contempt.

19How abundant is YouryourYour goodness, which YouyouYou have treasured up for those who fear YouyouYou and lavished before the children of men upon those who take refuge in YouyouYou! 20You hide them in the shelter of YouryourYour presence from the plots of man; YouyouYou shelter them in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.

21Blessed be the LORD, for HeheHe has shown me the wonders of HishisHis steadfast love in a besieged city. 22In my alarm I said, “I am cut off from YouryourYour sight.” Yet YouyouYou heard the voice of my supplications when I cried to YouyouYou for help.

23Love the LORD, all HishisHis faithful ones! The LORD preserves the faithful, but to the proud HeheHe repays in full. 24Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all you who hope in the LORD.
