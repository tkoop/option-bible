Oracle Concerning Babylon

1An oracle concerning Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz beheld. 2Raise a banner upon the barren height; call aloud to them; wave your hand, that they may enter the gates of the nobles.

3I have commanded MymyMy consecrated ones; I have likewise summoned MymyMy warriors to execute MymyMy wrath—those who exult in MymyMy majesty. 4A tumult resounds upon the mountains, like that of a great multitude; an uproar among the kingdoms, like nations assembled together. The LORD of Hosts is mustering a host for war. 5They come from a distant land, from the ends of Heavenheavenheaven—the the LORD and the instruments of HishisHis wrath—to devastate the whole EarthEarthearth.

The Day of the LORD

6Wail, for the day of the the LORD is near; it comes as devastation from the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 7Therefore every hand will grow feeble, and every human heart will melt away. 8They will be seized with terror; pangs and anguish will grip them. They will writhe like a woman in labourlabourlabor, staring at one another in astonishment, their faces aflame.

9Behold, the day of the the LORD comes—cruel, with wrath and fierce anger—to render the earth a desolation and to exterminate its sinners from it. 10For the stars of Heavenheavenheaven and their constellations will not give their light; the sun will be darkened as it rises, and the moon will not shed its light.

11I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity; I will bring to an end the arrogance of the proud and abase the haughtiness of the ruthless. 12I will make mortals rarer than pure gold, and humankind rarer than the gold of Ophir. 13Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth will be displaced from its place, because of the wrath of the the LORD of Hosts and the day of HishisHis fierce anger.

The Flight and Slaughter of the Nations

14And it will be as with a hunted gazelle, or as sheep without a shepherd: each will turn to his own people, and each will flee to his own land. 15Everyone who is found will be pierced; everyone who is captured will fall by the sword. 16Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses will be plundered, and their wives will be ravished.

The Medes and the Desolation of Babylon

17Behold, I am stirring up the Medes against them—men who have no regard for silver and no delight in gold. 18Their bows will dash the young men to pieces; they will show no mercy to the fruit of the womb, and their eyes will not pity the children.

19Babylon, the jewel of kingdoms, the glory and pride of the Chaldeans, will be overthrown by God like Sodom and Gomorrah. 20It will never be inhabited or settled from generation to generation; no Arab will pitch his tent there, and no shepherd will make his flock lie down there. 21But desert creatures will lie down there, and their houses will be full of owls; ostriches will dwell there, and wild goats will frolic there. 22Hyenas will howl in their citadels, and jackals in their luxurious palaces. Her time is near, and her days will not be prolonged.
