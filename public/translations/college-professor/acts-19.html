Paul's Arrival in Ephesus and the Gift of the Holy Spirit

1While Apollos remained in Corinth, Paul traversed the interior highlands and arrived at Ephesus, where he encountered certain disciples.

2Paul asked them, "Did you receive the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit when you came to believe?"

"No, we have not so much as heard that there is a HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit."

3So Paul asked, "Into what baptism, then, were you baptized?"

"Into John's baptism," they answered.

4Paul said, "John baptized with a baptism of repentance, enjoining the people to believe in the OneoneOne who would come after him—that is, in Jesus."

5Upon hearing this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. 6And when Paul laid his hands upon them, the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit came upon them, and they began to speak in tongues and to prophesy. 7There were in all about twelve men.

Reasoning in the Synagogue and the School of Tyrannus

8Paul entered the synagogue and spoke boldly for three months, reasoning persuasively concerning the kingdom of God. 9But when some hardened themselves and refused to believe, maligning the Way before the assembly, he withdrew from them, taking the disciples with him, and conducted daily discussions in the lecture hall of Tyrannus. 10This persisted for two years, so that all the inhabitants of Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the word of the Lord.

Extraordinary Miracles and the Vindication of Jesus' Name

11God wrought extraordinary miracles through Paul's hands, 12so that even handkerchiefs or aprons that had touched his skin were carried to the sick, and diseases departed from them, and evil spirits were expelled.

13Some itinerant Jewish exorcists also attempted to invoke the name of the Lord Jesus over those possessed by evil spirits, saying, "I adjure you by the Jesus whom Paul proclaims." 14Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, were engaged in this practice.

15But the evil spirit answered them, "Jesus I acknowledge, and Paul I know, but who are you?"

16Then the man in whom the evil spirit dwelt sprang upon them, overpowered them, and prevailed so completely that they fled from that house naked and wounded.

17This became known to all the Jews and Greeks dwelling in Ephesus, and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified. 18Many of those who had believed came forward, confessing and divulging their practices. 19And a considerable number of those who had practisedpractisedpracticed magic arts brought their books together and burned them before everyone; and when they reckoned their value, it amounted to fifty thousand pieces of silver. 20Thus the word of the Lord increased and prevailed mightily.

Paul's Plans and the Riot at Ephesus

21After these events had run their course, Paul resolved in the SpiritspiritSpirit to pass through Macedonia and Achaia and then go to Jerusalem, saying, "After I have been there, I must also see Rome."

22Sending two of his assistants, Timothy and Erastus, into Macedonia, he himself lingered in Asia for a while.

23About that time there arose no small disturbance concerning the Way. 24For a certain silversmith named Demetrius, who fashioned silver shrines of Artemis, provided no small livelihood to the craftsmen.

25He assembled them, together with workers in related trades, and said, "Men, you know that our prosperity depends upon this business. 26And you see and hear that not only in Ephesus but throughout almost all of Asia this Paul has persuaded and turned away a great multitude, declaring that gods made by human hands are no gods at all.

27"Moreover, there is danger not only that our trade may fall into disrepute but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis may be counted as nothing, and that her magnificence—she whom all Asia and the world worship—may be deposed."

28When they heard this, they were filled with rage and cried out, "GreatgreatGreat is Artemis of the Ephesians!" 29The city was filled with confusion, and they rushed with one accord into the theater, dragging along Gaius and Aristarchus, Paul's Macedonian companions.

30Paul wished to go in to the people, but the disciples would not let him. 31Even some of the Asiarchs, who were his friends, sent to him and urged him not to venture into the theater.

32Now the assembly was in confusion: some cried one thing and some another, and the majority did not know why they had convened. 33Some of the crowd brought Alexander forward, the Jews having pushed him ahead; and Alexander, motioning with his hand, wished to make a defencedefencedefense to the people. 34But when they recognizedrecogniserecognized that he was a Jew, they all with one voice cried out for about two hours, "GreatgreatGreat is Artemis of the Ephesians!"

35The city clerk quieted the crowd and said, "Men of Ephesus, what person is there who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is guardian of the temple of the great Artemis and of the image that fell from Heavenheavenheaven? 36Since these things are undeniable, you ought to be quiet and do nothing rash.

37"For you have brought these men here who are neither temple robbers nor blasphemers of our goddess. 38If, then, Demetrius and the craftsmen with him have a grievance against anyone, the courts are open and proconsuls are available; let them accuse one another.

39"But if you are seeking anything further, it shall be settled in the lawful assembly. 40For we are in danger of being charged with rioting today, there being no cause for which we can account concerning this commotion."

41And having said these things, he dismissed the assembly.
