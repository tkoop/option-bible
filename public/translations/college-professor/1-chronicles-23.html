David's Succession and the Assembly of Israel's Leaders

1When David had grown old and reached the fullness of his days, he installed his son Solomon as king over Israel. 2He gathered all the leaders of Israel, together with the priests and the Levites.

The Levites Enumerated and Assigned to Temple Service

3The Levites—those thirty years of age and older—were enumerated, and their number, reckoned by individual heads, came to thirty-eight thousand men. 4Of these, twenty-four thousand were appointed to oversee the work of the house of the LORD, while six thousand served as officers and judges. 5Four thousand were gatekeepers, and four thousand praised the LORD with the instruments that David had fashioned for praise. 6David then organized them into divisions corresponding to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

Genealogical Registers of the Levitical Clans

7The Gershonites comprised Laadan and Shimei. 8The sons of Laadan were Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel—three in all. 9The sons of Shimei were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran—three—these being the heads of the fathers' houses belonging to Laadan. 10The sons of Shimei were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah—four. 11Jahath was the chief and Zizah the second; Jeush and Beriah did not produce many sons, so they were reckoned as one father's house.

12The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four. 13The sons of Amram were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to consecrate the most holy things forever: to burn incense before the LORD, to minister to HimhimHim, and to bless in HishisHis name forever. 14Moses, the man of God, had his sons counted among the tribe of Levi. 15The sons of Moses were Gershom and Eliezer. 16The son of Gershom was Shebuel the chief. 17The son of Eliezer was Rehabiah the chief; Eliezer had no other sons, but the descendants of Rehabiah were exceedingly numerous. 18The son of Izhar was Shelomith the chief. 19The sons of Hebron were Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. 20The sons of Uzziel were Micah the chief and Isshiah the second.

21The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi; the sons of Mahli were Eleazar and Kish. 22Eleazar died without sons, having only daughters; their kinsmen, the sons of Kish, married them. 23The sons of Mushi were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three.

24These were the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses—the heads of the fathers' houses—as they were registered by name, from twenty years old and upward, those who performed the work for the service of the house of the LORD.

The Levites' Standing Duties in the Sanctuary

25For David had declared, "ThetheThe LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to HishisHis people, and HeheHe dwells in Jerusalem forever." 26Therefore the Levites no longer needed to carry the tabernacle or any of its vessels for its service. 27For by David's final instructions, the Levites were numbered from twenty years old and upward. 28Their station was beside the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD—over the courts, the chambers, the purification of all holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God— 29namely, the arrangement of the showbread, the fine flour for the grain offering, the unleavened wafers, the pan, the mixed offering, and every measure and quantity, 30and to stand every morning to give thanks and to praise the LORD, and likewise at evening, 31and for all the burnt offerings offered to the LORD on Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed feasts, according to the prescribed number, continually before the LORD. 32Thus they kept the charge of the tent of meeting, the charge of the holy place, and the charge of their brothers, the sons of Aaron, for the service of the house of the LORD.
