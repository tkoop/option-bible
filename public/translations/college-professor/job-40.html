The LORD’s Initial Challenge to Job

1Then the LORD answered Job, saying: 2"Shall one who contends with the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty presume to correct HimhimHim? Let him who reproves God answer."

3Then Job answered the LORD and said: 4"Behold, I am of no account; what reply can I make to YouyouYou? I have laid my hand upon my mouth. 5I have spoken once, and I will not answer again; twice, yet I will add nothing further."

Behemoth, the Preeminent of God’s Works

15"Behold now Behemoth, whom I made along with you; he feeds on grass like an ox. 16Behold, his vigourvigourvigor resides in his loins, and his power in the muscles of his belly. 17He makes his tail rigid like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are interwoven. 18His bones are like tubes of bronze; his limbs resemble bars of iron. 19He is the preeminent work of God; only his MakermakerMaker can draw near him with a sword. 20The mountains yield him their produce, and all the beasts of the field disport themselves there. 21He reclines under the lotus plants, in the covert of the reeds and in the marsh. 22The lotus plants shade him with their shadow; the willows of the brook encircle him. 23Though the river be turbulent, he is not dismayed; he remains secure, even should the Jordan surge against his mouth. 24Can anyone seize him while he keeps watch, or pierce his nose with a snare?"
