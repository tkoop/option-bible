Bildad’s Second Discourse: The Inevitable Downfall of the Wicked

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

2“How long will you continue to set bounds to words? Attain understanding, and then we shall speak. 3Why are we reckoned as beasts? Why are we esteemed unclean in your eyes? 4He tears himself in his anger; shall the earth be forsaken for your sake, or the rock be removed from its place?

The Collapse of the Wicked Man’s Prosperity

5“Indeed, the light of the wicked is extinguished, and the spark of his fire does not shine. 6The light in his tent is darkened, and his lamp above him is extinguished. 7His vigorous steps are shortened, and his own counsel casts him down. 8For he is thrust into a net by his own feet, and he walks upon a snare. 9A trap seizes him by the heel, and a snare holds him fast. 10A noose is hidden for him in the ground, and a trap lies in his path. 11Terrors frighten him on every side and harass him at every step. 12His strength is famished, and calamity stands ready at his side. 13It devours portions of his skin; the firstborn of death devours his limbs.

The Utter Eradication of His Name and Posterity

14“He is torn from the security of his tent and marched to the king of terrors. 15Nothing of his remains in his tent; brimstone dwells therein, and sulphursulphursulfur is scattered over his dwelling. 16His roots dry up below, and his branches wither above. 17His memory perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the street. 18He is driven from light into darkness and banished from the world. 19He has no offspring or posterity among his people, and no survivor in his dwellings. 20Those who come after are appalled at his fate, and those who went before are seized with horror. 21Surely such are the dwellings of the wicked, and this is the place of one who does not know God.”
