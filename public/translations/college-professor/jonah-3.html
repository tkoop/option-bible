The Renewed Commission and the Prophet's Obedience

1The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time, saying,

2“Arise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim to it the proclamation that I speak to you.”

3So Jonah arose and went to Nineveh, according to the word of the LORD. Now Nineveh was an exceedingly great city, a journey of three days.

The Proclamation of Impending Overthrow

4Jonah began to enter the city, a day's journey, and he cried out, saying, “Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown.”

Communal and Royal Penitence

5The people of Nineveh believed in God; they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest among them to the least. 6When the report reached the king of Nineveh, he arose from his throne, removed his royal robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes.

7“Let neither man nor beast, neither herd nor flock, taste anything; let them not feed, nor drink water; 8but let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and let them cry mightily to God; let everyone turn from his evil way and from the violence that is in their hands. 9Who knows? God may turn and relent, and turn away from HishisHis fierce anger, so that we do not perish.”
