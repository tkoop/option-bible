The Angelic Proclamation of Babylon's Fall

1After these things, I beheld another angel descending from Heavenheavenheaven, endowed with prodigious authority, and the earth was illumined by the radiance of his glory. 2And he cried out with a mighty voice, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a habitation of demons, a dungeon for every unclean spirit, a cage for every unclean bird, and a prison for every unclean and abhorred beast. 3For all the nations have imbibed the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth prostituted themselves with her, and the merchants of the earth amassed wealth through the potency of her luxury.”

The Heavenly Summons to Flee Babylon

4And I heard another voice from Heavenheavenheaven, saying, “Come out of her, MymyMy people, lest you participate in her sins and lest you receive of her plagues. 5For her sins have been heaped up even to Heavenheavenheaven, and God has remembered her iniquities. 6Render to her as she rendered, and double to her a twofold recompense according to her works; in the cup in which she mixed, mix for her a double portion. 7In proportion to the glory and luxury with which she glorified herself, give to her torment and mourning; for in her heart she says, ‘I sit as queen, and I am no widow, and mourning I shall never see.’ 8Therefore her plagues shall arrive in a single day—death, mourning, and famine—and she shall be consumed by fire, because mighty is the Lord God who judged her.”

The Lamentations of Kings and Merchants

9And the kings of the earth, who committed fornication and lived in luxury with her, will weep and lament over her when they behold the smoke of her burning, 10standing afar off because of the fear of her torment, saying, “Woe, woe, the great city, Babylon the mighty city! For in one hour your judgmentjudgementjudgment has come.”

11And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo any longer: 12cargo of gold and silver and precious stones and pearls and fine linen and purple and silk and scarlet, and every kind of citron wood and every ivory vessel and every vessel of most precious wood and bronze and iron and marble, 13and cinnamon and spice and incense and perfume and frankincense and wine and oil and fine flour and wheat and cattle and sheep and horses and chariots and slaves and human souls. 14And the fruit of your soul’s desire has departed from you, and all the opulent and resplendent things have perished from you, and they shall not find them anymore.

15The merchants of these things, who grew rich from her, will stand afar off because of the fear of her torment, weeping and mourning, 16saying, “Woe, woe, the great city, clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet, and gilded with gold and precious stone and pearl! 17For in one hour such great wealth was laid waste.”

18And every shipmaster and every voyager bound for any port, and sailors, and all who toil upon the sea, stood afar off, and, beholding the smoke of her burning, cried out, saying, “Who is like the great city?” 19And they threw dust on their heads, weeping and mourning, crying out and saying, “Woe, woe, the great city, in which all who have ships in the sea grew rich from her wealth! For in one hour she was laid waste.”

The Final Dirge and JudgmentJudgementJudgment of Babylon

20Rejoice over her, O Heavenheavenheaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, because God has adjudicated your cause against her. 21And a strong angel took up a stone like a great millstone and cast it into the sea, saying, “Thus with such violence Babylon the great city will be thrown down, and she will not be found anymore. 22And the voice of harpers and musicians and flute players and trumpeters will not be heard in you anymore, and every craftsman of every craft will not be found in you anymore, and the sound of a mill will not be heard in you anymore. 23And the light of a lamp will not shine in you anymore, and the voice of bride and bridegroom will not be heard in you anymore; because your merchants were the magnates of the earth, because all nations were led astray by your sorcery.” 24And in her was found the blood of prophets and saints and of all who have been slain on the earth.
