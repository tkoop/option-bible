A Liturgical Petition for the King's Deliverance

1May the LORD respond to you in the day of affliction; may the renown of the God of Jacob establish you securely. 2May HeheHe dispatchdispatch, despatchdispatch succor to you from the sanctuary, and uphold you from Zion. 3May HeheHe remember all your grain offerings, and regard your burnt offerings with favourfavourfavor. Selah.

4May HeheHe bestow upon you the longing of your heart, and bring all your plans to fruition. 5May we exult in your deliverance, and raise our banners in the name of our God. May the LORD satisfy all your petitions.

Confidence in the LORD's Anointed and His Saving Might

6Now I am assured that the LORD delivers HishisHis anointed; HeheHe answers him from HishisHis holy Heavenheavenheaven by the triumphant might of HishisHis right hand.

7Some place their confidence in chariots and others in horses, but we invoke the name of the LORD our God. 8They stumble and are laid low, whereas we arise and remain steadfast.

9The LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call.
