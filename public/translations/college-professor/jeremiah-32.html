Jeremiah Imprisoned and the Fall of Jerusalem Foretold

1The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar. 2At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the courtyard of the guard in the royal palace of Judah. 3For Zedekiah, king of Judah, had imprisoned him, saying, “Why do you prophesy as you do, saying, ‘Thus says the LORD: Behold, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it’?” 4Zedekiah, king of Judah, will not escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, but will surely be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon; he will speak with him mouth to mouth and see him eye to eye. 5He will take Zedekiah to Babylon, and there he will remain until I attend to him, declares the LORD. Though you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not succeed.

6Jeremiah said, “The word of the LORD came to me:”

The Purchase of the Field at Anathoth

7“Behold, Hanamel, the son of Shallum your uncle, is coming to you, saying, ‘Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth, for the right of redemption is yours to buy it.’”

8Then, just as the LORD had said, my cousin Hanamel came to me in the courtyard of the guard and said, “Buy my field that is in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin, for the right of inheritance and redemption is yours; buy it for yourself.” Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.

9So I bought the field in Anathoth from my cousin Hanamel, and I weighed out to him seventeen shekels of silver. 10I signed and sealed the deed, called witnesses, and weighed the silver on the scales. 11Then I took the sealed deed of purchase, containing the terms and conditions, together with the unsealed copy, 12and I gave the deed to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, in the presence of my cousin Hanamel, in the presence of the witnesses who signed the deed of purchase, and in the presence of all the Judeans who were sitting in the courtyard of the guard.

13In their presence I gave Baruch these instructions:

14“Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Take these documents, both the sealed deed of purchase and this unsealed copy, and put them in an earthen vessel, that they may endure for many days. 15For thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Houses and fields and vineyards shall again be bought in this land.”

Jeremiah's Prayer Before the LORD

16After I had given the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD:

17“Ah, Lord the LORD, YouyouYou have made the heavens and the earth by YouryourYour great power and outstretched arm; nothing is too hard for YouyouYou. 18You show steadfast love to thousands, yet bring the iniquity of the fathers into the bosom of their children after them. O great and mighty God, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, 19great in counsel and mighty in deed, whose eyes are open to all the ways of humankind, to give to each according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds.

20“You performed signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, as YouyouYou do to this day, both in Israel and among humankind, and YouyouYou have made a name for YourselfyourselfYourself, as it is this day. 21You brought YouryourYour people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terror. 22You gave them this land, which YouyouYou had sworn to give to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.

23“They came in and took possession of it, but they did not obey YouryourYour voice or walk in YouryourYour law; they did not do all that YouyouYou commanded them to do. Therefore YouyouYou have brought all this calamity upon them. 24Behold, the siege ramps have come up to the city to capture it, and the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans who fight against it, because of the sword, famine, and plague; and what YouyouYou have spoken has come to pass, and behold, YouyouYou see it. 25Yet YouyouYou have said to me, ‘Buy the field for silver and call witnesses,’ although the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.”

The LORD's Promise of Restoration

26Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

27“I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything too hard for MemeMe? 28Therefore thus says the LORD: Behold, I am giving this city into the hand of the Chaldeans and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and he will capture it. 29The Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will come in and set it on fire, and they will burn it down, along with the houses on whose roofs they burned incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke MemeMe to anger. 30For the children of Israel and the children of Judah have done nothing but evil in MymyMy sight from their youth; indeed, the children of Israel have done nothing but provoke MemeMe to anger by the work of their hands, declares the LORD. 31For this city has been to MemeMe a cause of MymyMy anger and MymyMy wrath from the day they built it until this day, so that I must remove it from MymyMy sight,

“32because of all the evil of the children of Israel and the children of Judah, which they have done to provoke MemeMe to anger—they, their kings, their officials, their priests, their prophets, the men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 33They turned their backs to MemeMe and not their faces; though I taught them again and again, they would not listen to receive instruction. 34They set their abominations in the house that is called by MymyMy name, to defile it. 35They built the high places of Baal in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom, to make their sons and daughters pass through the fire to Molech, which I did not command them, nor did it enter MymyMy mind that they should do this abomination, to make Judah sin.

“36You are saying about this city, ‘By sword, famine, and plague it will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon’; but thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 37Behold, I will gather them from all the lands to which I have driven them in MymyMy anger, in MymyMy great wrath, and in MymyMy fury, and I will bring them back to this place, and I will make them dwell in safety. 38They shall be MymyMy people, and I will be their God. 39I will give them one heart and one way, that they may fear MemeMe always, for their good and for the good of their children after them. 40I will make with them an everlasting covenant, that I will not turn away from doing good to them; and I will put MymyMy fear in their hearts, so that they will not depart from MemeMe. 41I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in truth, with all MymyMy heart and with all MymyMy soul.

“42For thus says the LORD: Just as I have brought all this great calamity upon this people, so I will bring upon them all the good that I have promised them. 43Fields shall be bought in this land of which you say, ‘It is a desolate waste, without man or beast; it has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.’ 44They shall buy fields for silver, and sign and seal deeds, and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, in the outskirts of Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, in the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland, and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their fortunes, declares the LORD.”
