Job’s Indignant Rejoinder to His Companions

1Then Job answered and said, 2“Truly you are the people, and wisdom will perish with you! 3But I too possess understanding, no less than you; I am not inferior to you. Who, after all, does not know such things? 4I have become a byword of derision among my companions, though I invoked God and received answer—a righteous and blameless man reduced to mockery. 5Ease breeds contempt for calamity, yet calamity stands poised for those whose step falters. 6The dwellings of marauders enjoy repose, and those who defy God remain secure, as though their deity were but an artifact borne in their own hand.

The Testimony of Creation and the Limits of Human Pretension

7“But inquire now of the beasts, and they will instruct you; of the birds of Heavenheavenheaven, and they will declare to you. 8Or commune with the earth, and it will instruct you; and the fish of the sea will recount to you. 9Which of all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has accomplished this? 10In HishisHis hand rests the life of every living thing and the breath of all humankind. 11Does not the ear assay words, as the palate discerns food? 12With the aged dwells wisdom, and understanding with length of days.

The Inscrutable Sovereignty of the Almighty

13“With HimhimHim reside wisdom and might; counsel and understanding are HishisHis. 14What HeheHe demolishes shall not be rebuilt; whom HeheHe confines shall not be released. 15If HeheHe withholds the waters, they languish; if HeheHe releases them, they overwhelm the earth. 16To HimhimHim belong strength and sound wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are HishisHis alike. 17He leads counselors away despoiled and renders judges witless. 18He unbinds the authority of kings and girds their loins with a loincloth. 19He leads priests away despoiled and subverts the established. 20He deprives the trustworthy of speech and takes discernment from elders. 21He pours contempt upon nobles and loosens the belt of the mighty. 22He unveils profundities hidden in darkness and brings the shadow of death into light. 23He magnifies nations, then annihilates them; HeheHe enlarges nations, then disperses them. 24He strips the leaders of the earth of their discernment and causes them to wander in a pathless waste. 25They grope in darkness without light, and HeheHe makes them stagger as drunkards.”
