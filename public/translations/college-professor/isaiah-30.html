Woe to the Rebellious Who Seek Egypt’s Refuge

1Woe to the rebellious children, says the the LORD, who execute a counsel not MinemineMine, and who ratify an alliance not by MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit, so as to heap sin upon sin. 2They descend to Egypt without consulting MymyMy mouth, to take refuge in Pharaoh’s stronghold and to shelter in Egypt’s shadow. 3Therefore Pharaoh’s stronghold shall become your ignominy, and the shelter of Egypt’s shadow your disgrace. 4For though his princes are at Zoan and his envoys reach Hanes, 5all shall be shamed by a people that cannot profit them—bringing neither help nor profit, but shame and reproach.

The Burden of the Negeb and Egypt’s Worthless Help

6An oracle concerning the beasts of the Negeb. Through a land of distress and anguish—whence issue the lioness and the lion, the viper and the flying serpent—they bear their wealth upon donkeys’ backs and their treasures upon camels’ humps, to a people that cannot profit them. 7Egypt’s help is a vanity and an emptiness; therefore I have called her “Rahab the Do-Nothing.” 8And now, go, write it before them on a tablet and inscribe it in a book, that it may stand as a witness for the last day, forevermore.

Rebellion Against the Prophets and the Holy One’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment

9For they are a rebellious people, mendacious children, children who refuse to hear the instruction of the the LORD. 10They say to the seers, “Do not see,” and to the prophets, “Do not prophesy to us what is right; speak to us smooth things, prophesy illusions. 11Turn aside from the way, depart from the path; let us hear no more of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel.”

12Therefore thus says the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel: “Because you despise this word and trust in oppression and rely upon perversity, 13this iniquity shall be to you like a breach in a high wall, bulging out and collapsing suddenly, in an instant. 14Its shattering shall be like the shattering of a potter’s vessel, smashed so ruthlessly that among its fragments no shard is found wherewith to take fire from the hearth or to draw water from the cistern.”

15For thus says the Lord, the the LORD, the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel: “In returning and rest you shall be saved; in quietness and in trust shall be your strength. But you refused. 16You said, ‘No! We will flee upon horses’—therefore you shall flee! ‘We will ride upon swift steeds’—therefore your pursuers shall be swift. 17A thousand shall flee at the rebuke of one; at the rebuke of five you shall flee, until you are left like a mast upon the summit of a mountain and like a signal upon a hill.”

18Therefore the the LORD waits to be gracious to you; therefore HeheHe will arise to show mercy to you. For the the LORD is a God of justice; blessed are all who wait for HimhimHim. 19O people in Zion, who dwell in Jerusalem, you will weep no more. He will surely be gracious to you at the sound of your cry; when HeheHe hears, HeheHe will answer you. 20Though the Lord gives you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, your TeacherteacherTeacher will no longer hide HimselfhimselfHimself, and your eyes will see your TeacherteacherTeacher. 21When you turn to the right and when you turn to the left, your ears will hear a word behind you, saying, “This is the way; walk in it.”

22Then you will defile the plating of your silver and the carved images of your gold; you will scatter them like a menstrual cloth, and you will say to them, “Be gone!” 23He will give rain for the seed you sow in the ground, and bread from the produce of the ground, and it shall be rich and fat. In that day your cattle will graze in broad pastures. 24The oxen and the donkeys that work the soil will eat salted fodder, winnowed with shovel and fork. 25On every high mountain and on every high hill there will be brooks and streams of water in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall. 26Moreover, the light of the moon will be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be sevenfold, like the light of seven days, on the day when the the LORD binds up the brokenness of HishisHis people and heals the wounds inflicted by HishisHis blow.

The Name of the LORD and the Defeat of Assyria

27Behold, the name of the the LORD comes from afar, burning with HishisHis anger, and in thick smoke; HishisHis lips are full of indignation, and HishisHis tongue is like a devouring fire. 28His breath is like an overflowing torrent that reaches to the neck—to sift the nations with the sieve of destruction, and to put a misleading bridle upon the jaws of the peoples. 29You shall have a song as in the night when a holy feast is kept, and gladness of heart, as when one goes with a flute to come to the mountain of the the LORD, to the RockrockRock of Israel. 30The LORD will cause HishisHis majestic voice to be heard and the descending blow of HishisHis arm to be seen, in furious anger and a flame of devouring fire, with a cloudburst and tempest and hailstones. 31The Assyrians will be terrified at the voice of the the LORD, when HeheHe strikes with HishisHis rod. 32And every stroke of the rod of punishment that the the LORD lays upon them will be accompanied by timbrels and lyres; with battles of brandishing HeheHe will fight against them. 33For Topheth has long been prepared; indeed, for the king it is made ready, its pyre made deep and wide, with fire and wood in abundance; the breath of the the LORD, like a stream of sulphursulphursulfur, kindles it.
