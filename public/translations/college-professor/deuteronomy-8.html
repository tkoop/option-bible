Exhortation to Obedience and the Meaning of Wilderness Discipline

1Be scrupulously observant of the entire commandment that I am enjoining upon you this day, so that you may live, multiply, enter, and possess the land that the LORD swore to your fathers. 2Remember the whole trajectory through which the LORD your God guided you during these forty years in the wilderness, in order to humble you and to assay you, discerning what lay within your heart—whether you would keep HishisHis commandments or not. 3He humbled you and caused you to hunger, then nourished you with manna—something neither you nor your ancestors had ever known—in order to teach you that humankind does not subsist on bread alone, but that humankind lives by every utterance that proceeds from the mouth of the LORD. 4Throughout these forty years, your garments did not wear out upon you, nor did your feet become swollen. 5Know, then, in your heart, that as a father disciplines his son, so the LORD your God disciplines you. 6Therefore keep the commandments of the LORD your God, walking in HishisHis ways and revering HimhimHim.

The Promise of a Fertile and Resource-Rich Land

7For the LORD your God is conducting you into a bountiful land—a land of watercourses, springs, and subterranean depths issuing forth in valleys and mountains— 8a land of wheat and barley, of vines, fig trees, and pomegranates, of olive oil and honey, 9a land where you will eat bread without privation, where you will want for nothing—a land whose stones are iron and whose mountains yield copper to be mined. 10When you have eaten your fill and are satisfied, you shall bless the LORD your God for the good land that HeheHe has bestowed upon you.

Warning Against Pride, Forgetfulness, and Self-Exaltation

11Take heed, lest you forget the LORD your God, by failing to observe HishisHis commandments, ordinances, and statutes, which I am enjoining upon you this day. 12Otherwise, after you have eaten and are sated, and have built stately houses and settled in them, 13and when your cattle and flocks have proliferated, and your silver and gold have increased, and everything in your possession has multiplied, 14then your heart will become exalted, and you will forget the LORD your God, who delivered you from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage. 15He guided you through that vast and terrifying wilderness, with its venomous serpents and scorpions and its parched, waterless terrain, and brought forth water for you from the flinty rock. 16He sustained you in the wilderness with manna unknown to your ancestors, in order to humble and test you, so that HeheHe might ultimately do you good. 17You may say in your heart, “My own power and the vigourvigourvigor of my hand have secured this prosperity for me.” 18But you must remember the LORD your God, for it is HeheHe who grants you the capacity to acquire wealth, thereby establishing HishisHis covenant, which HeheHe swore to your fathers, as is evident this day.

The Consequences of Idolatry and Disobedience

19If you forget the LORD your God and pursue other gods, serving and prostrating yourself before them, I solemnly testify against you today that you will utterly perish. 20Just as the nations that the LORD is destroying before you, so you too will perish, because you would not heed the voice of the LORD your God.
