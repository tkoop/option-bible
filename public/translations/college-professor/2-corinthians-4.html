The Integrity of Apostolic Proclamation

1Consequently, having been entrusted with this ministry in accordance with the mercy we have received, we do not succumb to discouragement. 2On the contrary, we have renounced the clandestine practices born of shame; we do not conduct ourselves with cunning, nor do we distort the word of God, but, through the open proclamation of truth, we commend ourselves to the conscience of every person before God. 3And if our gospel remains veiled, it is veiled only to those who are perishing. 4In their case, the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelieving, lest the radiance of the gospel of the glory of Christ—who is the very image of God—should illumine them.

Christ Proclaimed as Lord

5For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for the sake of Jesus. 6For God, who said, "LightlightLight shall shine out of darkness," has HimselfhimselfHimself shone in our hearts to bring to light the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.

Treasure in Fragile Clay Jars

7Yet we possess this treasure in fragile clay jars, so that the transcendent power may be seen to belong to God and not to originate in us. 8We are hard-pressed on every side, yet not crushed; bewildered, yet not driven to despair; 9persecuted, yet not forsaken; struck down, yet not destroyed. 10We always carry in our bodies the death of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our bodies. 11For we who are alive are perpetually being handed over to death for the sake of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may be manifested in our mortal flesh. 12Thus death is at work in us, but life in you.

Resurrection Hope and Eternal Perspective

13Yet, possessing the same spirit of faith, in accordance with what is written, "I believed, and therefore I spoke," we too believe, and therefore we speak. 14We know that HeheHe who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and present us together with you. 15For everything is for your sake, so that, as grace reaches more and more people, thanksgiving may overflow to the glory of God. 16Therefore we do not lose heart; though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day. 17For our light and momentary affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all measure, 18as we fix our gaze not on what is seen, but on what is unseen; for what is seen is transient, whereas what is unseen is eternal.
