The Branch from Jesse and the Anointing Spirit

1A shoot shall spring from the stump of Jesse, and a BranchbranchBranch from his roots shall bear fruit. 2The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD shall rest upon HimhimHim: the SpiritspiritSpirit of wisdom and understanding, the SpiritspiritSpirit of counsel and might, the SpiritspiritSpirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD.

3His delight shall be in the fear of the LORD; HeheHe shall not judge by the sight of HishisHis eyes, nor reprove by the hearing of HishisHis ears. 4But with righteousness HeheHe shall judge the poor, and with equity HeheHe shall decide for the meek of the earth; HeheHe shall smite the earth with the rod of HishisHis mouth, and with the breath of HishisHis lips HeheHe shall slay the wicked. 5Righteousness shall be the belt about HishisHis hips, and faithfulness the sash about HishisHis waist.

The Peaceable Kingdom

6The wolf shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the young goat; the calf, the young lion, and the fatling shall be together, and a little child shall lead them. 7The cow and the bear shall graze; their young shall lie down together; and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.

8The nursing child shall play over the hole of the cobra, and the weaned child shall put his hand upon the adder’s den. 9They shall not hurt nor destroy in all MymyMy holy mountain, for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.

The Gathering of the Remnant

10In that day the RootrootRoot of Jesse shall stand as a banner for the peoples; to HimhimHim the nations shall seek, and HishisHis resting place shall be glorious. 11In that day the Lord shall extend HishisHis hand a second time to recover the remnant of HishisHis people from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the coastlands of the sea.

12He shall raise a banner for the nations, and shall gather the outcasts of Israel, and assemble the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth. 13The jealousy of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off; Ephraim shall not be jealous of Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.

14They shall swoop down upon the slopes of the Philistines in the west, and together they shall plunder the sons of the east; they shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites shall obey them. 15The LORD shall devote to destruction the gulf of the Egyptian SeaseaSea, and shall wave HishisHis hand over the River with HishisHis scorching wind, and strike it into seven streams, and make men traverse it dry-shod. 16And there shall be a highway for the remnant of HishisHis people who remain from Assyria, as there was for Israel when he came up from the land of Egypt.
