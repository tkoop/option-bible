The Fall of Saul and His Sons in Battle

1The Philistines arrayed themselves for battle against Israel, whereupon the Israelites fled before them and fell slain upon Mount Gilboa. 2The Philistines pressed hard after Saul and his sons, striking down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul. 3The fighting pressed sorely upon Saul, and the archers overtook him and wounded him.

4Saul said to his armourarmourarmor-bearer, “Draw your sword and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised men come and abuse me.” Yet his armourarmourarmor-bearer refused, being seized with great fear; so Saul took up the sword and fell upon it.

5When his armourarmourarmor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he likewise fell upon his sword and died. 6Thus Saul died, together with his three sons and his entire household. 7When all the Israelites in the valley saw that their army had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities, and the Philistines came and occupied them.

The Desecration of Saul's Remains

8On the following day, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa. 9They stripped him, took his head and his armourarmourarmor, and sent messengers throughout the land of the Philistines to proclaim the tidings of victory to their idols and to the people. 10They placed his armourarmourarmor in the temple of their gods and fastened his head in the temple of Dagon.

The Men of Jabesh-Gilead Recover the Bodies

11When all the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, 12all the valiant men rose up, took away the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons, brought them to Jabesh, buried their bones beneath the oak in Jabesh, and observed a seven-day fast.

The Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment upon Saul's Unfaithfulness

13Thus Saul died because of his unfaithfulness, which he committed against the LORD in that he did not keep the word of the LORD, and because he consulted a medium, seeking guidance from her. 14He did not inquire of the LORD; therefore the LORD put him to death and transferred the kingdom to David, the son of Jesse.
