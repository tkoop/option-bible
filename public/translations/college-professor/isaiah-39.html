Babylonian Envoys and Hezekiah’s Ostentatious Display of Royal Wealth

1At that time, Merodach-baladan, son of Baladan and king of Babylon, dispatched envoys bearing letters and a tribute to Hezekiah, having learned that Hezekiah had been afflicted by illness and had subsequently convalesced. 2Hezekiah rejoiced at their arrival and exhibited to them his treasury—the silver, the gold, the spices, the precious oil, his entire armouryarmouryarmory, and every treasure found within his storehouses—so that there was not a single article in his palace or throughout his dominion that Hezekiah did not disclose to them.

Isaiah’s Interrogation and Hezekiah’s Candid Confession

3Then Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and inquired, “What did these men say, and whence have they come to you?”

Hezekiah replied, “They have come to me from a distant land, from Babylon.”

4Isaiah asked further, “What have they seen in your house?”

Hezekiah answered, “They have seen everything that is in my house; there was nothing in my treasuries that I did not show them.”

Prophetic Oracle of Impending Babylonian Captivity

5Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of the LORD of Hosts: 6Behold, days are coming when all that is in your house, together with all that your fathers have stored up until this day, shall be carried away to Babylon; nothing shall be left, says the LORD. 7And among your sons who shall issue from you, whom you shall beget, some shall be taken away and shall serve as eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.”

Hezekiah’s Acquiescence to the Divine Decree

8Then Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The word of the LORD that you have spoken is good.” For he thought, “There will be peace and truth in my days.”
