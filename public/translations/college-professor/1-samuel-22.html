David’s Refuge at Adullam

1David departed from that place and escaped to the cave of Adullam; when his brothers and all his father’s household heard of it, they went down to him there. 2Every man in distress, every man who was indebted, and every man who was discontented gathered to him, and he became their commander; about four hundred men were with him.

David Entrusts His Parents to Moab

3From there David went to Mizpeh of Moab and said to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and mother come out to you until I know what God will do for me.” 4So he left them with the king of Moab, and they remained with him all the days David was in the stronghold. 5Then the prophet Gad said to David, “Do not remain in the stronghold; depart and go into the land of Judah.” So David departed and came to the forest of Hereth.

Saul’s Accusation and the Slaughter at Nob

6Saul heard that David and the men with him had been discovered. Now Saul was sitting in Gibeah under the tamarisk tree on the hill, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him. 7Saul said to his servants standing around him, “Hear now, Benjamites! Will the son of Jesse give all of you fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds? 8For all of you have conspired against me, and no one informs me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you is concerned about me or informs me that my son has stirred up my servant against me to lie in wait, as at this day.”

9Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing by Saul’s servants, answered, “I saw the son of Jesse come to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob. 10He inquired of the the LORD for him, gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”

11So the king sent to summon Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and all his father’s household, the priests in Nob; they all came to the king.

12Saul said, “Listen now, son of Ahitub.”

He answered, “Here I am, my lord.”

13Saul said to him, “Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you gave him bread and a sword, and inquired of God for him, so that he has risen against me to lie in wait, as at this day?”

14Ahimelech answered the king, “Who among all your servants is as faithful as David, the king’s son-in-law, captain of your guard, and highly esteemed in your household? 15Have I today begun to inquire of God for him? May that never be! Do not charge your servant, or any of my father’s household, with anything; for your servant knew nothing of all this, small or great.”

16The king said, “You shall surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house.”

17The king said to the guards standing around him, “Turn and kill the priests of the the LORD, because they too have sided with David, and because they knew he was fleeing and did not disclose it to me.” But the king’s servants were unwilling to raise their hands to strike the priests of the the LORD.

18The king said to Doeg, “You turn and strike the priests.” Doeg the Edomite turned and struck the priests, and that day he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.

19He also struck Nob, the city of the priests, with the edge of the sword—men and women, children and infants, oxen, donkeys, and sheep—with the edge of the sword.

Abiathar’s Escape and David’s Responsibility

20But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, named Abiathar, escaped and fled after David. 21Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the the LORD. 22David said to Abiathar, “I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I have brought about the deaths of everyone in your father’s household. 23Stay with me; do not be afraid, for the one who seeks your life seeks mine as well; you will be safe with me.”
