A Psalm of David, the Servant of the LORD: The Corruption of the Wicked

1An oracle resounds within my heart concerning the transgression of the wicked: there is no fear of God before his eyes. 2For in his own estimation he flatters himself so thoroughly that he cannot discover his iniquity, much less abhor it. 3The utterances of his mouth are mischief and deceit; he has abandoned wisdom and the practice of good. 4Upon his bed he devises iniquity; he stations himself upon a path that is not good; he does not abhor evil.

A Prayer for Preservation from the Wicked

10Extend YouryourYour loving devotion to those who know YouyouYou, and YouryourYour righteousness to the upright in heart. 11Let not the foot of pride come against me, nor the hand of the wicked displace me. 12There the evildoers have fallen; they are thrown down and cannot rise.
