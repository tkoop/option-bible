Exhortation to Purification and Mutual Affection

1Therefore, beloved, since you hold these promises, let us purify ourselves from every defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God. 2Open your hearts to us: we have wronged no one, corrupted no one, and exploited no one. 3I do not utter this to condemn you; for I have already declared that you dwell so deeply in our hearts that we are bound to live and die with you. 4Great is my confidence in you; great is my boasting on your behalf. I am filled with consolation, and amid all our affliction I overflow with joy.

Affliction in Macedonia and the Comfort of Titus

5For even when we came into Macedonia, our bodies had no respite; rather, we were pressed on every side—conflicts without, fears within. 6Yet God, who consoles the downcast, consoled us through the arrival of Titus, 7not only by his coming but also by the consolation he had received from you, as he reported to us your longing, your lamentation, and your zeal on my behalf, so that I rejoiced all the more.

Godly Grief and Its Transformative Fruits

8For even if I grieved you by that letter, I do not regret it; though I did for a moment regret it, I perceive that the letter grieved you, yet only for a season. 9Now I rejoice—not because you were grieved, but because your grief issued in repentance; for you were grieved in accordance with God, so that you suffered no loss from us. 10For godly grief produces a repentance unto salvation, a repentance not to be regretted; but the grief of the world produces death. 11Observe what this godly grief has wrought in you: what earnestness, what defencedefencedefense, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what vindication! In every respect you have demonstrated yourselves to be pure in this matter. 12Therefore, although I wrote to you, it was not for the sake of the wrongdoer, nor for the sake of the one wronged, but in order that your earnest concern for us might be made manifest to you before God.

Titus’s Refreshment and Paul’s Apostolic Confidence

13For this reason we have been consoled; and beyond our own consolation, we rejoiced exceedingly over the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by all of you. 14For if I boasted to him about you, I was not put to shame; but as everything we spoke to you was true, so also our boasting to Titus proved true. 15And his affections toward you are all the more abundant as he recalls that all of you were obedient, and that you received him with fear and trembling. 16I rejoice that in everything I may be confident in you.
