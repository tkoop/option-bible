Ahaz's Apostasy and the LORD's Retribution

1Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; yet he did not do what was right in the sight of the LORD, as David his father had done. 2Instead, he walked in the ways of the kings of Israel and even cast molten images for the Baals. 3He burned incense in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben-Hinnom and consigned his sons to the fire, emulating the abominations of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed before the Israelites. 4He sacrificed and burned incense upon the high places, upon the hills, and beneath every verdant tree.

5Consequently, the LORD his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Aram; the Arameans struck him and carried away a great multitude of captives to Damascus. He was likewise delivered into the hand of the king of Israel, who inflicted upon him a devastating defeat. 6For in a single day Pekah son of Remaliah slew one hundred twenty thousand valiant men of Judah, because they had forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers. 7Moreover, Zichri, a mighty warrior of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the palace steward, and Elkanah the king's second-in-command. 8The Israelites also took captive two hundred thousand of their kinsmen—women, sons, and daughters—and plundered from them an immense spoil, which they conveyed to Samaria.

Oded's Prophetic Rebuke and the Restoration of Captives

9Now there was a prophet of the LORD named Oded, and he went out before the army as it came to Samaria and said to them, "Because the LORD, the God of your fathers, was wrathful against Judah, HeheHe delivered them into your hand, and you have slain them in a fury of unparalleled ferocity. 10And now you purpose to subjugate the people of Judah and Jerusalem, reducing them to male and female slaves; yet are you not yourselves laden with transgressions against the LORD your God? 11Heed me, therefore, and restore the captives whom you have taken from your brethren, for the fierce wrath of the LORD rests upon you."

12Then certain men among the chiefs of Ephraim—Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai—rose up against those returning from the campaign. 13They said to them, "YouyouYou must not bring the captives here, lest you heap further guilt upon us from the LORD, adding to our sins and our trespasses; for our guilt is already great, and fierce wrath is upon Israel."

14So the armed men relinquished the captives and the plunder before the princes and the whole assembly. 15Then the men who were designated by name arose, took charge of the captives, and from the spoil clothed all who were naked; they clothed them, shod them, fed them, gave them drink, anointed them, and conveyed every enfeebled person upon donkeys, bringing them to Jericho, the city of palms, near their kinsmen; then they returned to Samaria.

Ahaz's Futile Alliances and Deepening Apostasy

16At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to solicit their aid. 17For the Edomites had again invaded and struck Judah, carrying away captives. 18The Philistines likewise made raids upon the cities of the Shephelah and the Negev of Judah; they seized Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco and its villages, Timnah and its villages, and Gimzo and its villages, and settled there.

19For the LORD humbled Judah on account of Ahaz king of Israel, because he had cast off restraint in Judah and had been exceedingly unfaithful to the LORD. 20Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria came against him and oppressed him rather than strengthening him. 21For Ahaz had despoiled the house of the LORD and the house of the king and of the princes, and had given the proceeds to the king of Assyria, but it did not avail him.

22In the time of his distress, King Ahaz trespassed yet more against the LORD. 23He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus who had defeated him, reasoning that since the gods of the kings of Aram helped them, he would sacrifice to them that they might help him; but they proved to be the downfall of him and of all Israel. 24Ahaz gathered the vessels of the house of God, cut the vessels of the house of God in pieces, shut the doors of the house of the LORD, and made altars for himself in every corner of Jerusalem. 25In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, provoking the LORD, the God of his fathers.

26Now the rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 27Ahaz died and was buried in the city, in Jerusalem, but they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel; and Hezekiah his son succeeded him.
