Equity, Mercy, and Judicial Integrity

1“You must not propagate a false report, nor place your hand alongside the wicked to serve as a malicious witness. 2You shall not follow a multitude into wrongdoing; and when you testify in a dispute, you must not turn aside so as to incline after the crowd. 3Neither shall you show partiality to a poor person in his lawsuit.

4“If you encounter your enemy’s ox or donkey wandering astray, you must surely return it to him. 5If you see the donkey of one who hates you collapsed beneath its load, you must not abandon it to him; rather, you shall surely help him with it. 6You shall not pervert the justice due to your poor in his lawsuit.

7“Keep far from a false charge, and do not put the innocent and the righteous to death, for I will not acquit the guilty. 8Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and subverts the words of the righteous. 9Do not oppress a sojourner, for you know the soul of a sojourner, because you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

Sabbatical Years, Sabbath Rest, and Sacred Festivals
