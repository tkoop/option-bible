Judicial Punishment and the Dignity of the Condemned

1If a dispute arises between men and they come before the court for judgmentjudgementjudgment, the judges shall justify the righteous and condemn the wicked. 2If the wicked man deserves to be beaten, the judge shall cause him to lie down and be beaten in his presence, with the number of stripes proportioned to his wickedness. 3He may give him forty stripes, but no more, lest, in your sight, your brother be degraded should he receive an excessive beating beyond these. 4You shall not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain.

Levirate Marriage and Familial Obligation

5If brothers dwell together and one of them dies without a son, the wife of the deceased shall not be given in marriage to a stranger outside; her husband's brother shall go in to her, take her to himself as wife, and perform the duty of a brother-in-law to her. 6The first son she bears shall rise up in the name of his dead brother, so that his name may not be blotted out from Israel.

7But if the man does not desire to take his brother's widow, then his brother's widow shall go up to the gate, to the elders, and say, “My husband's brother refuses to raise up a name for his brother in Israel; he will not perform the duty of a brother-in-law to me.” 8Then the elders of his city shall call him and speak to him, and he shall stand and say, “I do not desire to take her.” 9Then his brother's widow shall come near to him in the sight of the elders, remove his sandal from his foot, spit in his face, and answer and say, “Thus shall it be done to the man who does not build up his brother's house.” 10And his name shall be called in Israel, “The house of the man whose sandal was removed.”

Violence Between Brothers and Honest Commerce

11When men strive together, a man and his brother, and the wife of one draws near to rescue her husband from the hand of the one who strikes him, and she puts out her hand and seizes him by his genitals, 12then you shall cut off her hand; your eye shall not pity.

13You shall not have in your bag two different stones, a large and a small. 14You shall not have in your house two different ephahs, a large and a small. 15A perfect and just stone you shall have; a perfect and just ephah you shall have, that your days may be long in the land that the LORD your God gives you. 16For all who do such things, all who act unjustly, are an abomination to the LORD your God.

The Memory of Amalek

17Remember what Amalek did to you on the way when you came out of Egypt, 18how he met you on the way and attacked you when you were faint and weary, cutting off all the stragglers behind you, and he did not fear God. 19When the LORD your God gives you rest from all your enemies around you in the land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance to possess, you shall blot out the memory of Amalek from under Heavenheavenheaven; you shall not forget.
