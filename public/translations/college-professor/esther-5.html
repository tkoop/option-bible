Esther’s Royal Audience

1On the third day, Esther arrayed herself in royal raiment and took her station in the inner court of the palace, directly opposite the king’s apartments; meanwhile, the king was seated upon his royal throne within the royal audience chamber, facing the palace entrance. 2When the king beheld Queen Esther standing in the court, she won his approval; he extended toward her the golden sceptresceptrescepter he held, and Esther drew near and touched the summit of the sceptresceptrescepter.

3The king inquired of her, “What is your request, Queen Esther? What petition do you bring? Even to half my kingdom, it shall be granted to you.”

The Queen’s Deferred Petition

4Esther replied, “If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come this day to the banquet I have prepared for him.”

5The king commanded, “DispatchDispatch, despatchDispatch Haman at once, that Esther’s bidding may be executed.” So the king and Haman repaired to the banquet Esther had prepared.

6At the wine banquet, the king said to Esther, “What is your petition? It shall be granted to you. And what is your request? Even to half the kingdom, it shall be fulfilled.”

7Esther answered, “My petition and my request are these: 8If I have won the king’s approval, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and fulfillfulfilfulfill my request, let the king and Haman come tomorrow to the banquet I shall prepare for them; then I will act in accordance with the king’s word.”

Haman’s Rage and Vengeful Counsel

9Haman departed that day rejoicing and in buoyant spirits; yet when he saw Mordecai in the king’s gate, and Mordecai neither rose nor trembled before him, Haman was consumed with wrath against Mordecai.

10Nevertheless, Haman mastered his indignation and went to his house; then he sent for and convened his friends together with his wife Zeresh.

11Haman rehearsed to them the splendoursplendoursplendor of his wealth, the multitude of his sons, every distinction the king had conferred upon him, and his elevation above the princes and courtiers of the king.

12Haman added, “Moreover, Queen Esther invited no one besides me to accompany the king to the banquet she prepared; and tomorrow, too, I am summoned by her along with the king. 13Yet all this is of no avail to me so long as I behold Mordecai the Jew seated at the king’s gate.”

14His wife Zeresh and all his friends counselledcounselledcounseled him, “Have a gallows erected, fifty cubits high, and in the morning entreat the king that Mordecai be hanged upon it; then you may go joyfully with the king to the banquet.” The proposal delighted Haman, and he had the gallows constructed.
