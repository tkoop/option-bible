Micah Condemns the Corrupt Rulers of Jacob

1And I said: Hear now, heads of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel: Is it not for you to know justice? 2Haters of good and lovers of evil, who strip the skin from them and the flesh from their bones— 3who eat the flesh of MymyMy people, flay them and strip their skin from them, break their bones and chop them like meat in a pot, like flesh in a cauldron. 4Then they will cry out to the LORD, but HeheHe will not answer them; at that time HeheHe will hide HishisHis face from them, because they have made their deeds evil.

False Prophets Denounced

5Thus says the LORD concerning the prophets who lead MymyMy people astray, who proclaim peace so long as they have something to bite with their teeth, but declare war against anyone who puts nothing in their mouths. 6Therefore night will come upon you, without vision, and darkness without divination; the sun will set on the prophets, and the day will blacken over them. 7The seers will be ashamed, the diviners disgraced, and they will all cover their mouths in shame, for there is no answer from God.

Micah's Spirit-Empowered Indictment and Zion's Doom

8But as for me, I am filled with power, with the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD, and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his transgression and to Israel his sin. 9Hear this, you leaders of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, who abhor justice and pervert all that is straight. 10They build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with iniquity. 11Her leaders judge for a bribe, her priests teach for a price, and her prophets divine for money; yet they lean on the LORD, saying, "IsisIs not the LORD among us? No disaster will come upon us." 12Therefore, on account of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the mount of the LORD's house a wooded height.
