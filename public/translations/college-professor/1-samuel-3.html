Samuel's Early Ministry and the Rarity of Divine Revelation

1Now the boy Samuel was ministering to the LORD before Eli; and in those days the word of the LORD was rare, and vision was not widespread. 2On that day Eli was lying in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow dim so that he could not see. 3Before the lamp of God went out, Samuel was lying in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was.

The LORD Calls Samuel in the Night

4Then the LORD called Samuel, and he said, “Here I am.”

5He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am, for you called me.”

But Eli said, “I did not call; go back and lie down.” So he went and lay down.

6The LORD called again, “Samuel!”

Samuel rose and went to Eli and said, “Here I am, for you called me.”

But Eli said, “I did not call, my son; lie down again.”

7Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, and the word of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him.

8The LORD called Samuel a third time. He got up, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am, for you called me.” Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy.

9So Eli said to Samuel, “Go and lie down, and if HeheHe calls you, you shall say, ‘Speak, the LORD, for YouryourYour servant hears.’”

10The LORD came and stood there, calling as before, “Samuel! Samuel!”

Then Samuel said, “Speak, for YouryourYour servant hears.”

The Oracle of JudgmentJudgementJudgment Against Eli's House

11The LORD said to Samuel, “Behold, I am about to do a thing in Israel at which both ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle. 12On that day I will carry out against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house, from beginning to end. 13For I have told him that I will judge his house forever for the iniquity that he knew, because his sons were blaspheming God, and he did not restrain them. 14Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the iniquity of the house of Eli shall never be atoned for by sacrifice or offering.”

Samuel Delivers the Oracle and Grows in Prophetic Authority

15Samuel lay until morning; then he opened the doors of the house of the LORD. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli the vision.

16Eli called Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son.”

He answered, “Here I am.”

17Eli said, “What is the word that HeheHe spoke to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God punish you, and worse, if you hide from me any of the words that HeheHe spoke to you.”

18So Samuel told him everything and hid nothing from him. Eli said, “It is the LORD. Let HimhimHim do what seems good to HimhimHim.”

19Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and not one of his words failed to come to pass. 20All Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established as a prophet of the LORD. 21The LORD continued to appear at Shiloh, for the LORD revealed HimselfhimselfHimself to Samuel at Shiloh by the word of the LORD.
