Azariah’s Reign over Judah

1In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam’s dominion over Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah assumed the kingship of Judah. 2He was sixteen years old at his accession and occupied the throne for fifty-two years in Jerusalem; his mother was Jecoliah of Jerusalem. 3He acted uprightly in the sight of the LORD, conforming entirely to the conduct of his father Amaziah.

4Only the high places remained unabolished; the populace continued to slaughter sacrificial victims and burn incense upon them. 5The LORD afflicted the king with leprosy, which persisted until his death, so that he dwelt in secluded quarters, while Jotham, the king’s son, administered the palace and governed the people of the land. 6As for the remaining exploits of Azariah and the totality of his accomplishments, are they not inscribed in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 7Azariah died and was buried with his ancestors in the CitycityCity of David, and his son Jotham succeeded him as king.

Israel’s Succession Crisis after Zechariah

8In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and his reign endured but six months. 9He acted wickedly in the sight of the LORD, as his fathers had done; he did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin. 10Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him, struck him down before the people, put him to death, and seized the throne in his stead. 11The remaining acts of Zechariah are indeed recorded—behold, they are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 12This fulfilled the word of the LORD, which HeheHe spoke to Jehu: “Your sons shall occupy the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.” And so it happened.

Israel’s Rapid Succession from Shallum to Pekah

13In the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah, king of Judah, Shallum son of Jabesh began to reign, and he reigned one month in Samaria. 14Menahem son of Gad went up from Tirzah, entered Samaria, struck down Shallum son of Jabesh there, killed him, and reigned in his place. 15The remaining acts of Shallum, together with the conspiracy he formed, are recorded—behold, they are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 16Then Menahem attacked Tiphsah and all within it and its surrounding territory, extending from Tirzah; because its inhabitants refused to open to him, he devastated the city and ripped open all its pregnant women.

17In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Menahem son of Gad began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and his reign lasted ten years. 18He acted wickedly in the sight of the LORD; throughout his days he never turned from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin. 19Pul, king of Assyria, invaded the land, and Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver so that Pul would support him and secure his hold on the kingdom. 20Menahem raised the silver from Israel—levying it upon all the men of substance, fifty shekels from each—to give to the king of Assyria; consequently, the king of Assyria withdrew and did not remain in the land. 21The remaining acts of Menahem, and all that he did, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 22Menahem died, and his son Pekahiah succeeded him as king.

23In the fiftieth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned two years. 24He acted wickedly in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin. 25Pekah son of Remaliah, his commander, conspired against him; with Argob and Arieh and fifty men of the Gileadites, he struck Pekahiah down in the citadel of the royal palace in Samaria, killed him, and reigned in his place. 26The remaining acts of Pekahiah, and all that he did, are recorded—behold, they are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

27In the fifty-second year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned twenty years. 28He acted wickedly in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin. 29During Pekah’s reign over Israel, Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, invaded and captured Ijon, Abel-beth-maachah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, Galilee, and the whole land of Naphtali; he deported the inhabitants to Assyria. 30Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah, struck him down and killed him, and reigned in his place in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah. 31The remaining acts of Pekah, and all that he did, are recorded—behold, they are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

Jotham’s Reign over Judah

32In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah began to reign as king of Judah. 33He was twenty-five years old at his accession and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; his mother was Jerusha, daughter of Zadok. 34He acted uprightly in the sight of the LORD, in accordance with all that his father Uzziah had done. 35Only the high places were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on them. He also built the Upper GategateGate of the house of the LORD. 36The remaining acts of Jotham, and all that he did, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 37In those days the LORD began to send Rezin, king of Aram, and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 38Jotham died and was buried with his ancestors in the CitycityCity of David, his father, and his son Ahaz succeeded him as king.
