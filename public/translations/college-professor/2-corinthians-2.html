Paul’s Pastoral Restraint and Affection

1For I resolved within myself that I would not return to you in grief. 2For if I grieve you, who is there to gladden me save the one whom I have grieved? 3And I wrote this very thing so that, upon my arrival, I might not be distressed by those who ought to have made me rejoice, since I had confidence in all of you that my joy would be the joy of you all. 4For I wrote to you out of great tribulation and anguish of heart, and with many tears—not to grieve you, but to make known to you the surpassing love that I bear toward you.

Restoring the Penitent Offender

5If anyone has caused grief, he has not grieved me alone, but, in some measure—not to overstate the case—all of you. 6The rebuke inflicted by the majority is sufficient for such a person. 7So, on the contrary, you ought rather to forgive and comfort him, lest he be swallowed up by excessive sorrow. 8Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love for him. 9For to this end I also wrote, that I might test you and ascertain whether you are obedient in all things. 10If you forgive anyone, I also forgive; and what I have forgiven—if indeed there was anything to forgive—I have forgiven in the presence of Christ for your sake. 11So that we may not be outwitted by Satan—for we are not ignorant of his designs.

An Open Door in Troas

12When I came to Troas to proclaim the gospel of Christ, and a door was opened to me in the Lord, 13I had no rest in my spirit, because I did not find my brother Titus; so, taking leave of them, I departed for Macedonia.

Triumph and Fragrance in Christ

14But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumphal procession and, through us, spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of HimhimHim in every place. 15For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing, 16to the one a fragrance from death to death, and to the other a fragrance from life to life. And who is sufficient for these things? 17For we are not, like so many, peddlers of the word of God; but, as men of sincerity, as commissioned by God, we speak in Christ in the sight of God.
