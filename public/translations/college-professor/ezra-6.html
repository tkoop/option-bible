Darius's Decree and the Recovery of Cyrus's Edict

1Then King Darius promulgated a decree, whereupon a search was conducted in the archival repository in which the treasures were deposited at Babylon. 2A scroll was discovered at Ecbatana, the citadel of the province of Media, bearing a memorandum inscribed thus: 3"In the first year of King Cyrus, Cyrus the king issued a decree concerning the house of God at Jerusalem: let the house be rebuilt as a locus of sacrifice, and let its foundations be firmly established; its height and its breadth shall each be sixty cubits, 4with three courses of massive stones and one course of fresh timber, the expense being defrayed from the royal treasury. 5Moreover, the gold and silver vessels of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar carried away from the temple in Jerusalem and transported to Babylon, shall be restored and brought back to the temple in Jerusalem, to their proper place, and deposited in the house of God."

Darius's Injunctions to the Officials of the Province Beyond the River

6"Now then, Tattenai, governor of Beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and your associates, the Apharsachites who are in Beyond the River—withdraw yourselves utterly from that place. 7Desist from interfering with the work on this house of God; let the governor and elders of the Jews erect it upon its original site. 8I further decree that the full expense for these elders of the Jews in building this house of God be disbursed to these men without delay from the royal revenue, from the tribute of Beyond the River, so that the labourlabourlabor is not interrupted. 9And whatever is requisite—young bulls, rams, and lambs for burnt offerings to the God of Heavenheavenheaven, together with wheat, salt, wine, and oil, according to the specification of the priests in Jerusalem—let it be furnished to them day by day without remission, 10that they may present acceptable sacrifices to the God of Heavenheavenheaven and intercede for the life of the king and his sons. 11I also decree that if any man shall tamper with this edict, a beam shall be extracted from his dwelling and he shall be impaled thereupon, while his house shall be reduced to a dunghill on account of this. 12May the God who has caused HishisHis name to dwell there overthrow every king or people who stretches forth a hand to alter or destroy this house of God in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have issued the decree; let it be executed with all diligence."

The Temple Completed, Dedicated, and Served

13Then Tattenai, governor of Beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates executed the decree with all diligence, precisely as King Darius had dispatched it. 14The elders of the Jews built and prospered through the prophetic ministrations of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo; they finished and built it in accordance with the commandment of the God of Israel and the decrees of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia. 15This house was completed on the third day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of the reign of King Darius.

16The Israelites—the priests, the Levites, and the rest of the exiles—celebrated the dedication of this house of God with exultation. 17For the dedication of this house of God they offered one hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and, as a sin offering for all Israel, twelve male goats according to the number of the tribes of Israel. 18They appointed the priests to their divisions and the Levites to their courses for the liturgical service of God in Jerusalem, as it is written in the BookbookBook of Moses.

The Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

19On the fourteenth day of the first month, the exiles celebrated the Passover. 20The priests and the Levites had purified themselves in unison, and all were ceremonially clean; they slaughtered the Passover lamb for all the exiles, for their priestly brethren, and for themselves. 21The Israelites who had returned from exile ate it, together with everyone who had segregated himself from the impurity of the peoples of the land in order to seek the the LORD God of Israel. 22They kept the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread for seven days in joy, because the the LORD had gladdened them and had turned the heart of the king of Assyria toward them, to fortify their hands in the labourlabourlabor of the house of God, the God of Israel.
