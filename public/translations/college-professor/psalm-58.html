A Miktam of David for the Choirmaster, to the Melody “Do Not Destroy”

1Do you indeed speak righteousness, O mighty ones? Do you judge with equity, O sons of men? 2No, in your hearts you devise injustice; your hands mete out violence upon the earth. 3The wicked are estranged from the womb; they go astray from birth, speaking lies.

4Their venom is like the venom of a snake, like a cobra that shuts its ears, 5that it cannot hear the voice of the charmers who skillfully weave spells.

6O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O the LORD, tear out the fangs of the lions. 7May they vanish like water that runs off; when they draw the bow, may their arrows be blunted. 8Like a snail that dissolves in its slime, like a stillborn child, may they never see the sun. 9Before your pots can feel the heat of the thorns—whether green or dry—HeheHe will sweep them away.

10The righteous will rejoice when they see vengeance; they will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked. 11Then men will say, “Surely there is a reward for the righteous; surely there is a God who judges in the earth.”
