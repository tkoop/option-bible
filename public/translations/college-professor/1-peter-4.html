Arming Oneself with Christ’s Resolve

1Consequently, since Christ suffered in the flesh, you yourselves must likewise arm yourselves with the identical disposition, for anyone who has suffered in the flesh has definitively ceased from sin, 2so that you no longer live the remainder of your mortal sojourn according to human cravings, but according to the will of God. 3For the time already elapsed has been more than adequate for accomplishing the purpose of the Gentiles, inasmuch as you formerly conducted yourselves in licentiousness, passions, inebriation, revelries, carousals, and execrable idolatries. 4In regard to which they are astonished that you do not rush with them into the same deluge of profligacy, and they heap abuse upon you. 5They will give account to HimhimHim who stands ready to judge the living and the dead. 6For this is why the gospel was proclaimed even to those who are now dead, so that, though judged in the flesh according to human standards, they might live in the spirit according to God.

Stewardship of Grace and Mutual Love

7Now the consummation of all things has drawn near; therefore exercise sound judgmentjudgementjudgment and be sober-minded, so that you may devote yourselves to prayer. 8Above all, maintain an earnest love for one another, because love covers a multitude of sins. 9Show hospitality to one another without murmuring. 10As good stewards of God’s varied grace, let each of you employ whatever gift he has received to serve one another. 11If anyone speaks, let him do so as one uttering the oracles of God; if anyone serves, let him do so from the strength that God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.

Suffering for the Name of Christ

12Beloved, do not be astonished at the fiery ordeal that has come upon you to test you, as though some alien calamity were befalling you. 13Rather, rejoice to the extent that you participate in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may also be overjoyed and exult when HishisHis glory is revealed. 14If you are reviled for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the SpiritspiritSpirit of glory, even the SpiritspiritSpirit of God, rests upon you. 15For let no one among you suffer as a murderer, a thief, a malefactor, or a busybody in others’ affairs. 16But if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed; instead, let him glorify God in that name. 17For the time has come for judgmentjudgementjudgment to commence at the household of God; and if it begins first with us, what will be the outcome for those who do not obey the gospel of God? 18And if the righteous is saved only with difficulty, where will the ungodly and the sinner stand? 19Therefore let those who suffer according to the will of God entrust their souls to a faithful CreatorcreatorCreator while continuing to do good.
