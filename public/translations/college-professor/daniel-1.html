The Babylonian Siege and the Exile of Judah's Elite

1In the third year of Jehoiakim's reign over Judah, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, advanced upon Jerusalem and besieged it. 2And the Lord delivered Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into his power, together with a portion of the vessels of the house of God; he conveyed them to the land of Shinar, to the temple of his deity, and deposited the vessels in the treasury of his god.

The Selection and Indoctrination of Royal Youths

3Then the king commanded Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to select from among the Israelites—of royal lineage and of noble extraction— 4youths in whom no physical defect was found, comely in appearance, sagacious in every branch of wisdom, conversant with knowledge, discerning in understanding, and endowed with the vigourvigourvigor requisite to stand in the king's palace, that he might instruct them in the literature and language of the Chaldeans. 5The king allotted to them a daily ration from the royal viands and from the wine of his own table; they were to be nurtured for three years, and thereafter they were to present themselves before the king. 6Among them were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, of the tribe of Judah. 7The chief of the eunuchs assigned them appellations: Daniel he styled Belteshazzar; Hananiah, Shadrach; Mishael, Meshach; and Azariah, Abednego.

Daniel's Determination to Preserve Ritual Purity

8But Daniel resolved in his heart not to defile himself with the king's delicacies or with the wine of his banquets; therefore he petitioned the chief of the eunuchs to be exempted from defilement. 9Now God granted Daniel favourfavourfavor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.

10And the chief said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has prescribed your food and your drink; for why should he behold your countenances more haggard than those of the youths of your own cohort? You would thereby render me culpable to the king.”

11Then Daniel said to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, 12“I entreat you to test your servants for ten days; let us be furnished with vegetables to eat and water to drink. 13Then compare our appearance with that of the youths who consume the king's delicacies, and deal with your servants in accordance with what you observe.”

14So he hearkened to them in this matter and tested them for ten days. 15At the end of ten days their appearance proved superior and their flesh more robust than that of all the youths who partook of the king's delicacies. 16Thereupon the steward withdrew their prescribed food and the wine appointed for their drink, and furnished them with vegetables.

Divine Endowment and Royal Acclamation

17As for these four youths, God conferred upon them knowledge and proficiency in every branch of literature and wisdom; moreover, Daniel possessed discernment in all visions and dreams. 18At the expiration of the days designated by the king for their presentation, the chief of the eunuchs escorted them into the presence of Nebuchadnezzar. 19The king conversed with them, and among them all none was found comparable to Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; consequently they were stationed before the king. 20In every matter of wisdom and discernment upon which the king interrogated them, he discovered them tenfold superior to all the magicians and enchanters in his entire realm. 21Daniel remained until the first year of King Cyrus.
