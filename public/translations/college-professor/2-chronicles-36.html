The Final Kings of Judah and the First Exile

1The people of the land took Jehoahaz, son of Josiah, and enthroned him in Jerusalem in his father's stead. 2Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. 3The king of Egypt deposed him in Jerusalem and levied upon the land a tribute of one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold. 4Then the king of Egypt made Eliakim, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem, and changed his name to Jehoiakim; he also took Jehoahaz, his brother, and carried him away to Egypt.

Jehoiakim's Reign and Nebuchadnezzar's Invasion

5Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD his God. 6Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came up against him, bound him in bronze fetters, and carried him to Babylon. 7Nebuchadnezzar also brought some of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon and placed them in his temple. 8The remaining acts of Jehoiakim, together with the abominations he committed and what was found against him, are recorded in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah; Jehoiachin, his son, reigned in his stead. 9Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem; he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD. 10At the turn of the year, King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babylon, along with the precious vessels of the house of the LORD, and made Zedekiah, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem.

Zedekiah's Rebellion and Jerusalem's Destruction

11Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. 12He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD his God and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke from the mouth of the LORD. 13He also rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God; he stiffened his neck and hardened his heart against turning to the LORD, the God of Israel. 14Moreover, all the chief priests and the people grew increasingly unfaithful after all the abominations of the nations, and they defiled the house of the LORD that HeheHe had consecrated in Jerusalem. 15The LORD, the God of their fathers, persistently sent word to them through HishisHis messengers, because HeheHe had compassion on HishisHis people and on HishisHis dwelling place. 16But they mocked the messengers of God, despised HishisHis words, and scoffed at HishisHis prophets, until the wrath of the LORD rose against HishisHis people, until there was no remedy.

17Therefore HeheHe brought against them the king of the Chaldeans, who slew their young men with the sword even in the sanctuary and showed no compassion to young man or maiden, to the old or the aged; HeheHe gave all into his hand. 18All the articles of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king and his officials, he brought to Babylon. 19They burned the house of God, broke down the wall of Jerusalem, burned all its palaces with fire, and destroyed all its precious vessels. 20Those who escaped the sword he carried away to Babylon, where they became servants to him and to his sons until the reign of Persia. 21Thus the land enjoyed its Sabbaths; throughout all the days of its desolation it kept Sabbath, to fulfillfulfilfulfill the word of the LORD spoken by Jeremiah, until seventy years were completed.

Cyrus's Edict and the Restoration

22In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, in order that the word of the LORD spoken by Jeremiah might be fulfilled, the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, so that he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom and also committed it to writing: 23"Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: 'The LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and HeheHe has charged me to build HimhimHim a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever among you of all HishisHis people—the LORD his God be with him—let him go up.'"
