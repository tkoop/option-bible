Israel Oppressed by Midian

1Again the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD, so the LORD gave them into the hand of Midian for seven years. 2The hand of Midian prevailed against Israel; and because of the Midianites the Israelites made for themselves the dens that are in the mountains, and the caves and the strongholds. 3Whenever Israel sowed, the Midianites, the Amalekites, and the sons of the East would come up against them. 4They would encamp against them as far as Gaza and destroy the produce of the land, leaving Israel no sustenance—neither sheep, nor ox, nor donkey. 5For they came up with their livestock and their tents in such multitudes as locusts, and both they and their camels were without number; and they entered the land to despoil it. 6Thus Israel was brought very low because of Midian, and the Israelites cried out to the LORD.

The LORD Sends a Prophet

Gideon Overthrows the Altar of Baal

25That night the LORD said to him, "Take your father's bull, the second bull seven years old, and pull down the altar of Baal that your father has, and cut down the Asherah that is beside it. 26Then build an altar to the LORD your God on the top of this stronghold in due order, and take the second bull and offer it as a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah that you cut down."

27So Gideon took ten men from among his servants and did as the LORD had told him; but because he feared his father's household and the men of the city, he could not do it by day, so he did it by night.

28When the men of the city arose early in the morning, behold, the altar of Baal was torn down, the Asherah beside it was cut down, and the second bull was offered on the altar that had been built. 29They said to one another, "Who has done this thing?" And when they inquired and searched, they said, "Gideon the son of Joash has done this thing." 30Then the men of the city said to Joash, "BringbringBring out your son, that he may die, because he has broken down the altar of Baal and because he has cut down the Asherah that was beside it."
