Woe to the Destroyer and a Plea for Grace

1Woe to you, destroyer, though you have not been destroyed; and you, betrayer, though none has betrayed you! When you have finished destroying, you will be destroyed; when you have ceased betraying, they will betray you. 2O the LORD, be gracious to us; we have waited for YouyouYou. Be our arm every morning, our salvation in time of distress. 3At the sound of the tumult, peoples fled; at YouryourYour exaltation, nations were scattered. 4Your spoil is gathered as the caterpillar gathers; as locusts swarm, so they swarm over it. 5The LORD is exalted, for HeheHe dwells on high; HeheHe has filled Zion with justice and righteousness. 6He will be the stability of your times, a wealth of salvation, wisdom and knowledge; the fear of the LORD is HishisHis treasure.

Desolation, Divine Intervention, and the Futility of the Wicked

7Behold, their valiant ones cry aloud in the streets; the envoys of peace weep bitterly. 8The highways are desolate; travelers have ceased. The covenant is broken; cities are despised; no regard is shown for man. 9The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is ashamed and withers; Sharon has become like a wilderness; Bashan and Carmel shed their foliage. 10“Now I will arise,” says the LORD; “now I will be exalted; now I will be lifted up.” 11You conceive chaff; you give birth to stubble; your breath is a fire that consumes you. 12Peoples will be burned to lime; thorns cut down will be set ablaze.

The Righteous, the Vision of Zion, and the LORD's Salvation

13Hear, you who are far away, what I have done; and you who are near, acknowledge MymyMy might. 14Sinners in Zion are terrified; trembling grips the godless: “Who among us can dwell with devouring fire? Who among us can dwell with everlasting burnings?”

15He who walks righteously and speaks uprightly, who despises gain from extortion, who shakes his hands free of bribes, who stops his ears from hearing of bloodshed and closes his eyes so as not to see evil— 16he will dwell on the heights; his refuge will be the fortresses of rocks; his bread will be given, his water assured. 17Your eyes will see the KingkingKing in HishisHis beauty; they will behold a land far away. 18Your heart will meditate on terror: “Where is the scribe? Where is the weigher? Where is he who counted the towers?”

19You will see no more the insolent people, a people of obscure speech that you cannot comprehend, of a stammering tongue that you cannot understand. 20Look upon Zion, the city of our appointed feasts; your eyes will see Jerusalem, a quiet habitation, a tent that will not be removed, whose stakes will never be pulled up, nor will any of its cords be broken. 21But there the majestic the LORD will be for us a place of broad rivers and streams, where no galley with oars will go, and no mighty ship will pass. 22For the LORD is our JudgejudgeJudge, the LORD is our LawgiverlawgiverLawgiver, the LORD is our King; HeheHe will save us. 23Your cords are slack; they cannot strengthen the mast; they cannot spread the sail. Then abundant spoil will be divided; even the lame will plunder. 24And no inhabitant will say, “I am sick”; the people who dwell in it will be forgiven their iniquity.
