A Summons to the Righteous Remnant

1"Listen to MemeMe, you who pursue righteousness, you who seek the the LORD: look to the rock from which you were hewn, and to the quarry from which you were dug. 2Look to Abraham your father and to Sarah who bore you; for when he was but one I called him, and I blessed him and multiplied him."

3For the the LORD will comfort Zion; HeheHe will comfort all her waste places, and HeheHe will make her wilderness like Eden, and her desert like the garden of the the LORD; joy and gladness will be found in her, together with thanksgiving and the voice of song.

God's Everlasting Salvation

4"Pay attention to MemeMe, O MymyMy people, and give ear to MemeMe, O MymyMy nation; for a law will go forth from MemeMe, and I will establish MymyMy justice as a light for the peoples. 5My righteousness is near; MymyMy salvation has gone forth; and MymyMy arms will judge the peoples; the coastlands will wait for MemeMe, and for MymyMy arm they will hope. 6Lift up your eyes to the heavens, and look upon the earth beneath; for the heavens will vanish like smoke, and the earth will wear out like a garment, and its inhabitants will perish in like manner; but MymyMy salvation will be forever, and MymyMy righteousness will not be abolished. 7Listen to MemeMe, you who know righteousness, a people in whose heart is MymyMy law; do not fear the reproach of man, nor be dismayed at their revilings. 8For the moth will devour them like a garment, and the worm will devour them like wool; but MymyMy righteousness will be forever, and MymyMy salvation to all generations."

Zion's Deliverance and Divine Power

9Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the the LORD; awake as in the days of old, the generations of long ago. Was it not YouyouYou who cut Rahab to pieces, who pierced the dragon? 10Was it not YouyouYou who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep, who made the depths of the sea a path for the redeemed to cross? 11So the redeemed of the the LORD will return and come with singing to Zion, and everlasting joy will be upon their heads; they will obtain gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing will flee away.

12"I, even I, am HeheHe who comforts you. Who are you that you are afraid of man, who dies, and of the son of man, who is made like grass, 13that you have forgotten the the LORD your MakermakerMaker, who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundations of the earth, and that you fear continually all the day because of the fury of the oppressor, as he prepares to destroy? And where is the fury of the oppressor? 14The exile will soon be set free, and will not die in the dungeon, nor will his bread be lacking. 15For I am the the LORD your God, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar—the the LORD of hosts is HishisHis name. 16I have put MymyMy words in your mouth, and I have covered you with the shadow of MymyMy hand, to plant the heavens and to found the earth, and to say to Zion, 'You are MymyMy people.'"

Jerusalem's Cup of Wrath and Its Removal

17"Rouse yourself! Rouse yourself! Arise, O Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the the LORD the cup of HishisHis wrath; you have drained the bowl of staggering. 18There is none to guide her among all the sons she has borne, and none to take her by the hand among all the sons she has reared. 19These two things have befallen you—who will grieve for you?—devastation and destruction, famine and sword. How shall I comfort you? 20Your sons have fainted; they lie at the head of every street, like an antelope in a net, full of the wrath of the the LORD, the rebuke of your God. 21Therefore hear this now, you afflicted one, who are drunk, but not with wine: 22Thus says your Lord, the the LORD, even your God who contends for HishisHis people, 'Behold, I have taken from your hand the cup of staggering, the bowl of MymyMy wrath; you shall drink it no more. 23I will put it into the hand of your tormentors, who have said to you, "Lie down, that we may pass over you." You have even made your back like the ground, and like the street, for them to pass over.'"
