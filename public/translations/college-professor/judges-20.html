Israel Assembles at Mizpah

1From Dan to Beersheba, together with the land of Gilead, all the Israelites came forth; the congregation assembled as one man before the LORD at Mizpah. 2The chiefs of all the people, every tribe of Israel, took their stand in the assembly of God—four hundred thousand foot soldiers who drew the sword.

The Levite Recounts the Crime at Gibeah

3The Benjaminites heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah. The Israelites said, "Tell us, how did this wickedness come to pass?"

4The Levite, the husband of the woman who was slain, answered, "I came to Gibeah in Benjamin, I and my concubine, to lodge for the night. 5The men of Gibeah rose against me and surrounded the house by night, intending to kill me; they violated my concubine, and she died. 6So I seized my concubine, dismembered her, and sent her through all the territory of Israel, because they had committed a shameful outrage in Israel. 7Behold, all you Israelites, give here your counsel and advice."

8All the people arose as one man, saying, "None of us will go to his tent, and none of us will turn aside to his house."

9"Now this is what we shall do to Gibeah: we shall go up against it by lot. 10We will take ten men of every hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, a hundred of every thousand, and a thousand of every ten thousand, to fetch provisions for the people, so that when they come to Geba of Benjamin they may requite all the folly that has been wrought in Israel." 11So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, knit together as one man.

Benjamin Rejects Israel’s Demand and Prepares for War

12The tribes of Israel sent men throughout the tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has happened among you?"

13"Now therefore deliver up the worthless men who are in Gibeah, that we may put them to death and purge the evil from Israel." But the Benjaminites would not listen to the voice of their brothers, the Israelites. 14The Benjaminites gathered from their cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the Israelites. 15On that day the Benjaminites mustered from their cities twenty-six thousand swordsmen, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who mustered seven hundred select men. 16Among all this people were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed; every one could sling a stone at a hair and not miss. 17The Israelites, apart from Benjamin, mustered four hundred thousand swordsmen, all men of war.

The Campaign Against Benjamin

18The Israelites arose and went up to Bethel and inquired of God, saying, "Who shall go up first for us to battle against the Benjaminites?"

The LORD said, "Judah shall go up first." 19The Israelites rose in the morning and encamped against Gibeah. 20The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and they drew up in battle array against them at Gibeah. 21The Benjaminites came out of Gibeah and struck down to the ground twenty-two thousand men of Israel on that day.

22But the people, the men of Israel, took courage and again set the battle in array in the place where they had arrayed themselves the first day. 23The Israelites went up and wept before the LORD until evening, and they inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I go up again to battle against my brother Benjamin?"

The LORD said, "Go up against him." 24The Israelites drew near to the Benjaminites on the second day. 25Benjamin went out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day and struck down to the ground eighteen thousand men of the Israelites; all these were men who drew the sword.

26Then all the Israelites, the whole people, went up and came to Bethel and wept, and sat there before the LORD, and fasted that day until evening, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD. 27The Israelites inquired of the LORD—for the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days, 28and Phinehas the son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, stood before it in those days—saying, "Shall I go up yet again to battle against my brother Benjamin, or shall I cease?"

The LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give them into your hand." 29So Israel set ambushes around Gibeah.

30The Israelites went up against the Benjaminites on the third day and drew up against Gibeah as before. 31The Benjaminites went out to meet the people and were drawn away from the city; then they began to strike down the people and kill them, as on previous occasions, along the highways—one ascending to Bethel and the other to Gibeah in the open country—about thirty men of Israel.

32The Benjaminites said, "They are struck down before us, as at first."

But the Israelites said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways." 33All the men of Israel rose from their place and drew up at Baal-tamar, and the ambush of Israel burst forth from its place at Maareh-geba. 34Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came against Gibeah, and the battle was fierce, but the Benjaminites did not know that disaster was close upon them.

35The LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel, and the Israelites destroyed on that day twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin, all of them swordsmen. 36The Benjaminites saw that they were defeated. The men of Israel gave ground to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

37The ambush hurried and rushed against Gibeah; the ambush drew itself out and struck all the city with the edge of the sword. 38The appointed signal between the men of Israel and the ambush was that they should make a great column of smoke rise up from the city.

39The men of Israel turned in the battle, and Benjamin began to strike and kill about thirty men of Israel, for they said, "Surely they are struck down before us, as in the first battle." 40But the column of smoke began to rise from the city in a pillar, and Benjamin looked behind him, and behold, the whole city was going up in smoke to Heavenheavenheaven.

41Then the men of Israel turned, and the Benjaminites were dismayed, for they saw that disaster had come upon them. 42So they turned their backs before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and those who came out of the cities destroyed them in their midst. 43They surrounded the Benjaminites, pursued them, and trampled them down with ease as far as a point opposite Gibeah toward the sunrise. 44Eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell, all of them men of valourvalourvalor.

45They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they cut down five thousand of them along the highways, and pursued them as far as Gidom, and struck down two thousand men of them. 46So all who fell that day of Benjamin were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword, all of them men of valourvalourvalor. 47But six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon and remained at the rock of Rimmon four months. 48The men of Israel turned back against the Benjaminites and struck them with the edge of the sword—the city, the cattle, and all that they found; moreover they burned with fire all the cities that they found.
