Opposition and Intrigue Against the Work

1Now when Sanballat and Tobiah and Geshem the Arab and the rest of our enemies heard that I had built the wall and that no breach remained in it—though up to that time I had not set doors in the gates— 2Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, let us meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono." But they intended to do me harm.

3I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work cease while I leave it and come down to you?"

4Four times they sent to me in this manner, and I answered them in the same manner. 5The fifth time Sanballat sent his servant to me with an open letter in his hand.

6In it was written: "It is reported among the nations—and Gashmu says—that you and the Jews intend to rebel; therefore you are building the wall, and you will be their king according to these words." 7"Moreover, you have appointed prophets to proclaim concerning you in Jerusalem, 'There is a king in Judah.' Now this will be reported to the king according to these words. Come now, let us take counsel together."

8I sent to him, saying, "No such things as you say have been done, but you are fabricating them out of your own imagination."

9For they all were trying to frighten us, thinking, "TheirtheirTheir hands will drop from the work, and it will not be done." But now strengthen my hands.

The Conspiracy to Intimidate Nehemiah

10I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah son of Mehetabel, who was confined, and he said, "Let us meet together in the house of God, within the temple, and let us close the doors of the temple, for they are coming to kill you; by night they are coming to kill you."

11I said, "Should a man of my station flee? Or should one such as I enter the temple merely to preserve his life? I will not enter."

12I perceived that God had not sent him, but that he had prophesied against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him. 13For this purpose he was hired: that I should be afraid and act and sin, and so they might have an evil report with which to reproach me.

14Remember, O my God, Tobiah and Sanballat in accordance with these deeds, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who were seeking to intimidate me.

The Wall Completed and Its Aftermath

15The wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty-two days. 16When all our enemies heard of it and all the surrounding nations saw it, their self-assurance collapsed, for they perceived that this work had been accomplished by our God.

17Moreover, in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and letters from Tobiah came to them. 18For many in Judah were under oath to him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Johanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah. 19Moreover, they kept reporting his good deeds to me and relaying my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.
