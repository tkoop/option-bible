4In the haughtiness of his countenance the wicked does not seek God; all his thoughts are, “There is no God.” 5His ways prosper at every turn; YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are exalted beyond his ken; he sneers at all his adversaries. 6He says in his heart, “I shall never be shaken; throughout the generations I shall know no calamity.” 7His mouth is brimming with cursing, deceit, and oppression; beneath his tongue lurk mischief and iniquity.

8He lurks in the hiding places of the villages; in secret he slays the innocent; his eyes are ever on the watch for the helpless. 9He lurks in his covert like a lion in his thicket; he lurks to snatch away the afflicted; he seizes the afflicted as he drags them into his net. 10The helpless are crushed and brought low; they fall by his might. 11He says in his heart, “God has forgotten; HeheHe has hidden HishisHis face; HeheHe will never behold it.”
