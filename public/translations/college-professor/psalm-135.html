An Exhortation to Extol the LORD

1Extol the LORD! Extol the name of the LORD! Extol HimhimHim, O servants of the LORD, 2who stand within the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God. 3Extol the LORD, for the LORD is good; sing praises to HishisHis name, for it is delightful. 4For the LORD has chosen Jacob for HimselfhimselfHimself, Israel as HishisHis own treasured possession.

The LORD’s Universal Sovereignty and Redeeming Acts

5For I know that the LORD is great, and that our Lord is exalted above all gods. 6The LORD accomplishes all that HeheHe pleases, in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth, in the seas and in all their depths. 7He makes the clouds ascend from the ends of the earth; HeheHe fashions lightning for the rain and brings the wind forth from HishisHis storehouses. 8He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both human and beast. 9He sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants. 10He struck down many nations and slew mighty kings— 11Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan— 12and gave their land as a heritage, a heritage to HishisHis people Israel. 13O LORD, YouryourYour name endures forever; YouryourYour renown, O LORD, through all generations. 14For the LORD will vindicate HishisHis people and have compassion on HishisHis servants.

The Impotence of Idolatry

15The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of human hands. 16They have mouths, yet they cannot speak; they have eyes, yet they cannot see. 17They have ears, yet they cannot hear; indeed, no breath resides in their mouths. 18Those who fashion them will become like them, as will all who place their trust in them.

A Concluding Call to Bless the LORD

19O house of Israel, bless the LORD! O house of Aaron, bless the LORD! 20O house of Levi, bless the LORD! You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD! 21Blessed be the LORD from Zion, HeheHe who dwells in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!
