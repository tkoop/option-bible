A Plea for Vindication and Integrity

1Vindicate me, O the LORD, for I have conducted myself in integrity; I have trusted in the LORD, and I shall not waver. 2Examine me, O the LORD, and put me to the proof; refine my inmost being and my heart. 3For YouryourYour lovingkindness stands before my eyes, and I have walked in YouryourYour truth.

Repudiation of the Wicked and Devotion to the Sanctuary

4I have not sat with men of falsehood, nor shall I consort with dissemblers. 5I have hated the assembly of evildoers, and I will not sit with the wicked. 6I cleanse my hands in innocence, and I make my circuit about YouryourYour altar, O the LORD, that I may proclaim with the voice of thanksgiving, and recount all YouryourYour wondrous deeds. 78O the LORD, I have loved the dwelling of YouryourYour house, and the place where YouryourYour glory abides.

Petition for Preservation and Final Praise

9Do not gather my soul with sinners, nor my life with men of blood, 10in whose hands is depravity, and whose right hand is full of bribes. 11But as for me, I will walk in my integrity; redeem me, and be gracious to me. 12My foot stands upon an even plain; in the great assemblies I will bless the LORD.
