The Quail and the Plague at Kibroth-hattaavah

31Then a wind went out from the LORD and drove quail in from the sea, and scattered them over the camp to a depth of about two cubits on the ground, for about a day’s journey in every direction around the camp.

32And the people rose up all that day and all that night and all the next day and gathered the quail; the one who gathered least gathered ten homers; and they spread them out for themselves all around the camp.

33While the meat was still between their teeth, before it was consumed, the anger of the LORD burned against the people, and the LORD struck the people with a very great plague.

34So the name of that place was called Kibroth-hattaavah, because there they buried the people who had craved.

35From Kibroth-hattaavah the people journeyed to Hazeroth, and they remained at Hazeroth.
