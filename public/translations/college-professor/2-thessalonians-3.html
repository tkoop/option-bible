Apostolic Exhortation and Prayerful Concern

1Finally, brothers, intercede on our behalf, that the word of the Lord might run swiftly and be glorified, even as it has been among you, 2and that we may be rescued from perverse and malevolent persons, for faith is not the possession of all. 3Yet the Lord is faithful, and HeheHe will establish you and guard you from the evil one. 4Moreover, we have confidence in the Lord regarding you, that you are both practisingpractisingpracticing and will continue to practiseto practiseto practice the injunctions we lay upon you. 5And may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the patient endurance of Christ.

Withdrawal from the Disorderly and the Principle of LabourLabourLabor

6Now we charge you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw from every brother who conducts himself in a disorderly manner and not in accordance with the tradition you received from us. 7For you yourselves know how fitting it is for you to emulate us, because we did not conduct ourselves disorderly among you. 8Nor did we partake of anyone's bread gratis, but labouredlabouredlabored with toil and hardship night and day, lest we should burden any of you. 9Not because we lack authority, but in order to present ourselves as a paradigm for you, that you might emulate us.

10For even when we were among you, we enjoined upon you this precept: “If anyone is unwilling to work, let him not eat.” 11For we hear that certain individuals among you are conducting themselves in a disorderly fashion, doing no work, but meddling in others' affairs. 12Now we charge and exhort such persons in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and to eat their own bread. 13But as for you, brothers, do not lose heart in doing good. 14And if anyone refuses to heed our instruction conveyed through this epistle, mark that person and do not consort with him, so that he may be put to shame. 15Yet do not esteem him as an adversary, but admonish him as a brother.
