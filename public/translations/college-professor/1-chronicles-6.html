Levitical Lineages and the Priestly Succession

1The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 2The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 3The sons of Amram were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam; and the sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 4Eleazar fathered Phinehas, and Phinehas fathered Abishua. 5Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi.

6Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, and Zerahiah fathered Meraioth. 7Meraioth fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub. 8Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz. 9Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan. 10Johanan fathered Azariah, who served as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

11Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub. 12Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum. 13Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah. 14Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak. 15Jehozadak went into exile when the LORD carried Judah and Jerusalem into exile by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

Levite Families and Their Service

16The sons of Levi were Gershom, Kohath, and Merari. 17These are the names of the sons of Gershom: Libni and Shimei. 18The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 19The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites according to their fathers’ houses.

20For Gershom: Libni his son, Jahath his son, Zimmah his son, 21Joah his son, Iddo his son, Zerah his son, and Jeatherai his son. 22The sons of Kohath: Amminadab his son, Korah his son, and Assir his son; 23Elkanah his son, Ebiasaph his son, and Assir his son; 24Tahath his son, Uriel his son, Uzziah his son, and Saul his son. 25The sons of Elkanah were Amasai and Ahimoth.

26From Ahimoth the line ran Elkanah, Zophai, and Nahath; 27then Eliab, Jeroham, and Elkanah. 28The sons of Samuel were Vashni the firstborn and Abijah. 29The sons of Merari: Mahli, Libni his son, Shimei his son, and Uzzah his son; 30Shimea his son, Haggiah his son, and Asaiah his son.

David’s Appointment of the Singers

31These are the men David appointed to direct the music in the house of the LORD after the ark came to rest. 32They ministered with song before the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, until Solomon built the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, and thereafter they served according to their prescribed order.

33Among the sons of the Kohathites stood Heman the singer, son of Joel, son of Samuel, son of Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Eliel, son of Toah, son of Zuph, son of Elkanah, son of Mahath, son of Amasai, son of Elkanah, son of Joel, son of Azariah, son of Zephaniah, son of Tahath, son of Assir, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, son of Israel. 39His brother Asaph stood at his right hand: Asaph, son of Berechiah, son of Shimea, son of Michael, son of Baaseiah, son of Malchijah, son of Ethni, son of Zerah, son of Adaiah, son of Ethan, son of Zimmah, son of Shimei, son of Jahath, son of Gershom, son of Levi.

44On the left stood their brothers, the sons of Merari: Ethan, son of Kishi, son of Abdi, son of Malluch, son of Hashabiah, son of Amaziah, son of Hilkiah, son of Amzi, son of Bani, son of Shemer, son of Mahli, son of Mushi, son of Merari, son of Levi. 48Their fellow Levites were assigned to all the service of the tabernacle of the house of God, 49while Aaron and his sons were charged with the work of the most holy place: they offered on the altar of burnt offering and on the altar of incense, and they made atonement for Israel, according to all that Moses, the servant of God, had commanded.

50The sons of Aaron were Eleazar, Phinehas, Abishua, Bukki, Uzzi, Zerahiah, Meraioth, Amariah, Ahitub, Zadok, and Ahimaaz.

Cities and Pasturelands Allotted to the Priests and Levites

54These were their settlements within their territories: to the sons of Aaron of the Kohathite families, for the lot fell to them, they gave Hebron in the land of Judah with its surrounding pasturelands. 56The fields and villages of that city, however, were given to Caleb son of Jephunneh. 57To the sons of Aaron they gave the city of refuge, Hebron, together with Libnah, Jattir, Eshtemoa, and their pasturelands, 58as well as Hilen, Debir, 59Ashan, and Beth-shemesh with their pasturelands.

60From the tribe of Benjamin they gave Geba, Alemeth, and Anathoth with their pasturelands—thirteen cities in all for their families. 61The remaining sons of Kohath received by lot ten cities from the half-tribe of Manasseh. 62The sons of Gershom received thirteen cities by families from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Manasseh in Bashan. 63The sons of Merari received twelve cities by lot from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.

64Thus the Israelites gave the Levites these cities and their pasturelands. 65They allotted by lot from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin the cities named above. 66Some of the Kohathite families had cities in the territory of Ephraim: 67Shechem, a city of refuge, with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, together with Gezer and its pasturelands, 68Jokmeam and its pasturelands, Beth-horon and its pasturelands, 69Aijalon and its pasturelands, and Gath-rimmon and its pasturelands.

70From the half-tribe of Manasseh they gave to the remaining Kohathite families Aner and its pasturelands and Bileam and its pasturelands. 71To the sons of Gershom from the family of the half-tribe of Manasseh they gave Golan in Bashan and its pasturelands and Ashtaroth and its pasturelands. 72From Issachar they gave Kedesh, Daberath, Ramoth, and Anem with their pasturelands; 74from Asher, Mashal, Abdon, Hukok, and Rehob with their pasturelands; 76and from Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee, Hammon, and Kiriathaim with their pasturelands.

77To the remaining sons of Merari from Zebulun they gave Rimmono and Tabor with their pasturelands. 78From Reuben, east of the Jordan at Jericho, they gave Bezer in the wilderness, Jahzah, Kedemoth, and Mephaath with their pasturelands. 80From Gad they gave Ramoth in Gilead, Mahanaim, Heshbon, and Jazer with their pasturelands.
