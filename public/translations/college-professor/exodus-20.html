The People's Terror and Moses' Mediation

18All the people witnessed the thunder and the lightning, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; and the people, seeing it, trembled with fear and stood at a distance.

19They said to Moses, “You speak to us, and we will heed; but let not God speak to us, lest we perish.”

20Moses said to the people, “Do not be afraid, for God has come to test you, so that the fear of HimhimHim may remain before you, and that you may not sin.”

21The people remained at a distance, while Moses approached the thick darkness where God was.
