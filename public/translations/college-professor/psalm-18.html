David's Song of Deliverance

1To the choirmaster: a psalm of David, the servant of the the LORD, who spoke to the the LORD the words of this song on the day when the the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. And he said, "I love YouyouYou, the the LORD, my strength. 2The ThetheThe LORD is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge; my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold. 3I call upon the the LORD, who is worthy to be praised, and I am saved from my enemies.

"4The cords of death encompassed me, and the torrents of destruction terrified me. 5The cords of Sheol surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me.

"6In my distress I called upon the the LORD; to my God I cried for help. From HishisHis temple HeheHe heard my voice, and my cry came before HimhimHim, into HishisHis ears.

The LORD's Mighty Deliverance

"7Then the earth reeled and rocked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled and quaked, because HeheHe was angry. 8Smoke went up from HishisHis nostrils, and devouring fire from HishisHis mouth; glowing coals blazed forth from HimhimHim. 9He bowed the heavens and came down, with thick darkness under HishisHis feet. 10He rode on a cherub and flew; HeheHe soared on the wings of the wind. 11He made darkness HishisHis covering; HishisHis canopy around HimhimHim was dark waters and thick clouds of the skies.

"12Out of the brightness before HimhimHim HishisHis thick clouds passed, with hailstones and coals of fire. 13The ThetheThe LORD thundered in the heavens, and the MostmostMost HighhighHigh uttered HishisHis voice, with hailstones and coals of fire. 14He sent out HishisHis arrows and scattered them; HeheHe flashed forth lightnings and routed them. 15Then the channels of the sea were seen, and the foundations of the world were laid bare, at YouryourYour rebuke, the the LORD, at the blast of the breath of YouryourYour nostrils.

"16He reached down from on high and took me; HeheHe drew me out of many waters. 17He rescued me from my strong enemy, and from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me. 18They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the the LORD was my support. 19He brought me out into a broad place; HeheHe rescued me, because HeheHe delighted in me.

The Integrity of the Righteous King

"20The ThetheThe LORD has dealt with me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands HeheHe has recompensed me. 21For I have kept the ways of the the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God. 22For all HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments were before me, and HishisHis statutes I did not put away from me. 23I was blameless before HimhimHim, and I kept myself from my iniquity. 24So the the LORD has recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in HishisHis sight.

"25With the faithful YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself faithful; with the blameless YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself blameless. 26With the pure YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself pure, and with the crooked YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself shrewd. 27For YouyouYou save an afflicted people, but the haughty eyes YouyouYou bring down. 28For YouyouYou light my lamp; the the LORD my God illumines my darkness. 29For by YouyouYou I can run against a troop; by my God I can leap over a wall. 30As for God, HishisHis way is perfect; the word of the the LORD is refined; HeheHe is a shield to all who take refuge in HimhimHim.

"31For who is God but the the LORD? And who is a rock except our God? 32It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way blameless. 33He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me secure on the heights. 34He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 35You have given me the shield of YouryourYour salvation, and YouryourYour right hand has supported me, and YouryourYour gentleness has made me great. 36You enlarge my steps beneath me, so that my feet do not slip.

Victory, Dominion, and Praise

"37I pursued my enemies and overtook them, and did not turn back until they were consumed. 38I struck them down, so that they were not able to rise; they fell beneath my feet. 39For YouyouYou girded me with strength for the battle; YouyouYou made those who rose against me sink under me. 40You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hated me I destroyed. 41They cried for help, but there was none to save; they cried to the the LORD, but HeheHe did not answer them. 42I beat them as fine as dust before the wind; I cast them out like mud in the streets.

"43You delivered me from strife with the people; YouyouYou made me the head of the nations; a people I had not known served me. 44As soon as they heard of me, they obeyed me; foreigners cringed before me. 45Foreigners lost heart and came trembling out of their fortresses.

"46The ThetheThe LORD lives, and blessed be my rock, and exalted be the God of my salvation. 47The God who gives me vengeance and subdues peoples under me. 48He delivers me from my enemies; indeed, YouyouYou exalt me above those who rise against me; YouyouYou rescue me from the violent man. 49Therefore I will give thanks to YouyouYou among the nations, the the LORD, and sing praises to YouryourYour name. 50Great salvation HeheHe gives to HishisHis king, and shows steadfast love to HishisHis AnointedanointedAnointed, to David and his offspring forever."
