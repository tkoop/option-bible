Hezekiah’s Mortal Illness and Prophetic Warning

1In those days Hezekiah became sick to the point of death, and Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, came to him and said to him, “Thus says the LORD: Set your house in order, for you are dying and will not live.”

2Then Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD. 3He said, “O the LORD, remember, I pray, how I have walked before YouyouYou in truth and with a whole heart, and have done what is good in YouryourYour sight.” And Hezekiah wept bitterly.

The Lord’s Promise of Healing and a Sign

4Then the word of the LORD came to Isaiah, saying, 5“Go and say to Hezekiah, ‘Thus says the LORD, the God of your father David: I have heard your prayer; I have seen your tears. Behold, I will add fifteen years to your days. 6I will deliver you and this city from the hand of the king of Assyria, and I will defend this city.’ 7And this shall be the sign to you from the LORD, that the LORD will do this thing which HeheHe has spoken: 8Behold, I will make the shadow that has descended on the steps of Ahaz retreat ten steps backward. So the sun’s shadow went back ten steps on the steps by which it had descended.”

Hezekiah’s Song of Thanksgiving

9A writing of Hezekiah, king of Judah, when he had been sick and recovered from his sickness: 10“I said, ‘In the very prime of my days I must enter the gates of Sheol; I am robbed of the remainder of my years.’ 11I said, ‘I shall not see the LORD in the land of the living; I shall no longer look upon man among the inhabitants of the world.’ 12My dwelling has been pulled up and removed from me like a shepherd’s tent; I have rolled up my life like a weaver; HeheHe cuts me off from the loom; from day to night YouyouYou make an end of me. 13I composed myself until morning; like a lion HeheHe breaks all my bones; from day to night YouyouYou make an end of me. 14Like a swallow, like a crane, so I chirp; I moan like a dove; my eyes grow weak as I look to the heights. O Lord, I am oppressed; be my security. 15What shall I say? He has spoken to me, and HeheHe HimselfhimselfHimself has done it. I shall walk softly all my years because of the bitterness of my soul. 16O Lord, by these things men live, and in all these things is the life of my spirit; YouyouYou will restore me and make me live. 17Behold, for my peace I had great bitterness; but YouyouYou have, in love for my soul, delivered it from the pit of destruction, for YouyouYou have cast all my sins behind YouryourYour back. 18For Sheol cannot thank YouyouYou, death cannot praise YouyouYou; those who go down to the pit cannot hope for YouryourYour faithfulness. 19The living, the living—he praises YouyouYou, as I do this day; a father makes known YouryourYour faithfulness to his children. 20The LORD is ready to save me; therefore we will sing my songs with stringed instruments all the days of our life in the house of the LORD.”

Isaiah’s Remedy and Hezekiah’s Final Question

22And Hezekiah had said, “What is the sign that I shall go up to the house of the LORD?”
