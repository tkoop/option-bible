Thanksgiving for the LORD's Enduring Mercy

1Render thanksgiving unto the LORD, for HeheHe is good; indeed, HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 2Let the redeemed of the LORD proclaim it—namely, that HeheHe ransomed them from the hand of the adversary. 3He gathered them from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.

Deliverance from Desolation, Bondage, and Affliction

4They wandered in the wilderness, in a desolate waste, finding no road to a city of habitation. 5Hungry and thirsty, their very lives were ebbing within them. 6Then they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and HeheHe delivered them from their afflictions. 7He led them by a straight path, that they might journey toward a city for habitation. 8Let them give thanks to the LORD for HishisHis steadfast love, and for HishisHis wonders to the children of men. 9For HeheHe satisfied the longing soul and filled the hungry soul with good.

10Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and in irons, 11because they had rebelled against the words of God and spurned the counsel of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 12Therefore HeheHe subdued their heart with toil; they stumbled, and there was no one to help. 13Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them out of their distresses. 14He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and burst their bonds asunder. 15Let them give thanks to the LORD for HishisHis steadfast love, and for HishisHis wonders to the children of men. 16For HeheHe has shattered the doors of bronze and cut the bars of iron in sunder.

Healing for the Sick and Calming of the Storm

17Fools, because of their rebellious way and their iniquities, were afflicted; 18their soul abhorred all food, and they drew near to the gates of death. 19Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them out of their distresses. 20He sent HishisHis word and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions. 21Let them give thanks to the LORD for HishisHis steadfast love, and for HishisHis wonders to the children of men. 22Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare HishisHis works with rejoicing.

23Those who descend to the sea in ships, who conduct their business upon the great waters— 24these have seen the works of the LORD and HishisHis wonders in the deep. 25For HeheHe spoke, and HeheHe raised up a stormy wind, and it lifted up the waves thereof. 26They mounted up to the heavens and descended to the depths; their soul melted away in their misery. 27They reeled and staggered like a drunken man, and all their wisdom was swallowed up. 28Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe brought them out of their distresses. 29He made the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof were still. 30Then they were glad because they were quiet, and HeheHe guided them to their desired haven. 31Let them give thanks to the LORD for HishisHis steadfast love, and for HishisHis wonders to the children of men. 32Let them exalt HimhimHim in the congregation of the people, and praise HimhimHim in the assembly of the elders.

The LORD's Sovereign Reversals in History

33He turns rivers into a wilderness, and springs of water into thirsty ground; 34a fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of those who dwell therein. 35He turns the wilderness into a pool of water, and dry land into springs of water. 36And there HeheHe makes the hungry to dwell, that they may establish a city for habitation, 37and sow fields, and plant vineyards, and obtain a fruitful harvest. 38He blesses them, so that they multiply greatly, and HeheHe does not let their cattle decrease.

39Yet they are diminished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow. 40He pours contempt upon princes, and causes them to wander in a trackless waste. 41But HeheHe sets the needy on high away from affliction, and makes their families like a flock. 42The upright see it and rejoice, and all iniquity shuts its mouth. 43Whoever is wise, let him observe these things, and let him consider the steadfast love of the LORD.
