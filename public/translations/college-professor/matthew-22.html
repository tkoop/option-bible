The Parable of the Wedding Feast

1Once more Jesus replied to them in parables, saying, “The kingdom of Heavenheavenheaven may be compared to a king who prepared a wedding banquet for his son. 2He sent his servants to summon the invited guests to the banquet, but they refused to come. 3Again he dispatched other servants with this message: ‘Tell those who have been invited, “Behold, my banquet is prepared; my oxen and fattened cattle have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding.”’

“5But those invitees, paying the summons no heed, went away—one to his farm, another to his business. 6The rest seized his servants, abused them, and killed them. 7The king was enraged; he sent his troops, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.

“8Then he told his servants, ‘The feast is prepared, but those invited proved unworthy.’ 9Go therefore to the intersections of the highways and invite to the banquet whomever you encounter. 10Those servants went out into the streets and gathered everyone they could find, wicked and righteous alike, so that the banquet hall was filled with guests.

“11When the king entered to inspect the guests, he noticed a man not attired in wedding garments. 12He said to him, ‘Friend, how did you gain entrance here without wedding attire?’ And the man was struck mute. 13Then the king ordered his attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness, where there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ 14For many are summoned, yet few are chosen.”
