Belshazzar's Revelry and the Ominous Inscription

1King Belshazzar prepared a lavish banquet for a thousand of his nobles, and he drank wine in their presence. 2While exhilarated by the wine, Belshazzar gave orders to bring the gold and silver vessels that Nebuchadnezzar his father had taken from the temple in Jerusalem, so that the king, his nobles, his wives, and his concubines might drink from them. 3Accordingly, they brought the gold vessels taken from the temple of the house of God in Jerusalem, and the king, his nobles, his wives, and his concubines drank from them. 4They drank wine and extolled the gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone.

5In that very hour the fingers of a human hand emerged and wrote upon the plaster of the wall opposite the lampstand, and the king beheld the part of the hand that was writing. 6Then the king's countenance was altered, and his thoughts terrified him; the joints of his loins were loosened, and his knees smote one another.

7The king cried out authoritatively for the enchanters, the Chaldeans, and the astrologers to be brought in. He addressed the wise men of Babylon, saying, "Whoever reads this writing and reveals its interpretation shall be clothed in purple, shall wear a chain of gold upon his neck, and shall exercise authority as the third highest ruler in the kingdom." 8Then all the king's wise men entered, yet they were unable to read the writing or to disclose its interpretation to the king. 9Thereupon King Belshazzar was greatly dismayed, his countenance changed, and his nobles were confounded.

The Queen's Counsel and Daniel's Summons

10Because of the king's words and those of his nobles, the queen entered the banquet hall. The queen declared, "O king, live forever! Let not your thoughts terrify you, nor let your countenance be altered. 11There is a man in your kingdom in whom resides the spirit of the holy gods; in the days of your father, illumination, understanding, and wisdom like that of the gods were found in him. King Nebuchadnezzar, your father, appointed him chief of the magicians, enchanters, Chaldeans, and astrologers. 12Because an extraordinary spirit, together with knowledge and discernment—interpreting dreams, expounding riddles, and unraveling knotty problems—was found in Daniel, whom the king named Belteshazzar, let Daniel now be summoned, and he will reveal the interpretation."

13Then Daniel was brought before the king. The king addressed Daniel, saying, "Are you Daniel, one of the exiles from Judah whom my father the king brought from Judah? 14I have heard concerning you that the spirit of the gods is in you, and that illumination, understanding, and surpassing wisdom have been found in you. 15Now the wise men, the enchanters, have been brought before me to read this writing and to make known its interpretation to me, yet they were unable to disclose the interpretation of the matter. 16But I have heard concerning you that you can render interpretations and unravel knotty problems. Now if you can read the writing and make known to me its interpretation, you shall be clothed in purple, shall wear a chain of gold upon your neck, and shall exercise authority as the third highest ruler in the kingdom."

Daniel's Rebuke and the Interpretation of the Writing

"17Let your gifts remain yours, and let your recompense be bestowed upon another; nevertheless, I shall read the writing to the king and make known to him its interpretation. 18O king, the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God granted to Nebuchadnezzar your father the kingdom, greatness, glory, and majesty. 19Because of the greatness that HeheHe bestowed upon him, all peoples, nations, and languages quaked and feared before him. Whomever he willed, he put to death; whomever he willed, he spared; whomever he willed, he exalted; and whomever he willed, he abased.

"20But when his heart was lifted up and his spirit grew insolent with pride, he was deposed from his royal throne, and his glory was taken from him. 21He was driven out from human society, his mind was made like that of the beasts, and his dwelling was with the wild donkeys. He was fed grass like an ox, and his body was drenched with the dew of Heavenheavenheaven, until he acknowledged that the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God holds sovereignty over human kingdoms and appoints over them whomever HeheHe pleases.

"22And you, his son Belshazzar, have not humbled your heart, although you knew all this. 23Rather, you have exalted yourself against the Lord of Heavenheavenheaven. The vessels of HishisHis house were brought before you, and you, your nobles, your wives, and your concubines drank wine from them. You praised the gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone—gods that neither see, nor hear, nor know—yet the God in whose hand are your life-breath and all your ways you have not glorified. 24Therefore, from HishisHis presence were sent the fingers of a hand, and this inscription was written. 25This is the inscription that was written: MENE, MENE, TEKEL, and PARSIN. 26This is the interpretation of the matter: MENE—God has numbered your kingdom and brought it to its close. 27TEKEL—you have been weighed in the scales and found deficient. 28PERES—your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians."

29Then Belshazzar gave the command, and Daniel was clothed in purple, a chain of gold was placed upon his neck, and proclamation was made concerning him that he should be the third highest ruler in the kingdom. 30That very night Belshazzar, the king of the Chaldeans, was slain. 31And Darius the Mede received the kingdom, being about sixty-two years old.
