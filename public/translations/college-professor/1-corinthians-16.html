Concerning the Collection for the Saints

1Now concerning the collection for the saints, I would have you follow the same arrangement I prescribed for the churches of Galatia. 2On the first day of every week, let each of you set aside and store up whatever prosperity permits, so that no collections may be necessitated by my arrival. 3When I come, I shall send, with letters of approval, those whom you approve to convey your gift to Jerusalem. 4If it should prove expedient that I too make the journey, they shall accompany me.

Paul’s Itinerary and Timothy’s Ministry

5I shall come to you after traversing Macedonia, for my route lies through Macedonia. 6Perhaps I shall abide with you, or even winter there, that you may speed me on my way whithersoever I proceed. 7For I am unwilling to see you now merely in transit; rather, I hope to remain with you for some interval, if the Lord permits. 8Nevertheless, I shall remain in Ephesus until Pentecost, 9for a wide and efficacious door has opened to me, though many adversaries oppose me. 10When Timothy arrives, see that he may be among you without apprehension, for he labourslabourslabors in the work of the Lord, even as I do. 11Let no one, therefore, treat him with contempt; instead, send him forth in peace, that he may return to me, for I await him with the brothers. 12Now concerning our brother Apollos, I entreated him repeatedly to visit you with the brothers, yet it was not his inclination to come at present; he will come when opportunity affords.

Final Exhortations, Commendations, and Salutations

13Be vigilant, stand fast in the faith, acquit yourselves with manly courage, and be strong. 14Let all that you do be done in love. 15You know that the household of Stephanas were the first converts in Achaia and devoted themselves to the service of the saints. 16I exhort you, brothers, to submit to such people and to every collaborator and labourerlabourerlaborer. 17I rejoice in the advent of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus, for they have supplied what was lacking on your part; 18for they refreshed my spirit and yours. Acknowledge such men. 19The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca, together with the church in their house, greet you warmly in the Lord. 20All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss. 21I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. 22If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be anathema. Our Lord, come! 23The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. 24My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
