Priestly Defilement and Mourning Rites

1The LORD said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron and his sons and say to them: 'No member of his people may render himself ceremonially unclean for a dead person, 2except for his nearest kin—his mother, his father, his son, his daughter, and his brother—or 3his sister, a virgin still bound to his household, who has never been given to a husband; on her account he may incur defilement. 4He shall not defile himself for a relative by marriage among his people, lest he profane himself. 5They shall not tonsure their heads, nor trim the extremities of their beards, nor gash their flesh. 6They shall be consecrated unto their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God, because they present the LORD's offerings made by fire, the bread of their God; hence they must be holy. 7They shall not take a wife who has been profaned by harlotry, nor one dismissed by her husband, for the priest is consecrated unto his God. 8You shall sanctify him, for he presents the bread of your God; he shall be holy unto you, because I, the LORD, am holy, and I am HeheHe who sanctifies you. 9If a priest's daughter profanes herself by playing the harlot, she profanes her father; she shall be burned with fire.

The High Priest's Exceptional Consecration

"'10ThetheThe high priest, preeminent among his brethren, upon whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to wear the garments, shall not dishevel his hair nor rend his garments. 11He shall not come near any dead body, nor shall he defile himself, even for his father or his mother. 12He shall not leave the sanctuary, nor shall he profane the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is upon him. I am the LORD. 13He shall take a wife in her virginity. 14A widow, or a divorced woman, or one profaned by harlotry—these he shall not take; but he shall take a virgin from his own people as wife, 15so that he does not defile his offspring among his people, for I am the LORD who sanctifies him.'"

Physical Blemishes and Priestly Eligibility

16The LORD said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron and say, ' 17No man among your descendants throughout their generations who has a blemish shall approach to offer the bread of his God. 18For no man who has a blemish shall approach—neither a blind man, nor a lame man, nor a mutilated man, nor a man with a withered limb— 19nor a man with a broken foot or a broken hand, 20nor a hunchback or a dwarf, nor one with a blemish in his eye, nor one suffering from scabs or scurf, nor one with crushed testicles.

"'21No descendant of Aaron the priest who has a blemish shall approach to present the LORD's offerings made by fire; because he has a blemish, he shall not approach to offer the bread of his God. 22He may eat the most holy food of his God, as well as the holy food, 23but because he has a blemish, he shall not approach the veil nor come near the altar, lest he profane MymyMy sanctuaries, for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'"

24So Moses spoke these words to Aaron, to his sons, and to all the Israelites.
