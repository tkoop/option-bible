Expiation for an Unsolved Homicide

1Should a slain man be discovered fallen in the open country of the land that the LORD your God is granting you to possess, and his killer remain unknown, 2then your elders and your judges shall go out and measure the distances from the corpse to the cities encircling it. 3The elders of the city nearest the slain man shall take a heifer from the herd that has neither been worked nor drawn under a yoke.

4The elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a rugged valley—one that has neither been plowed nor sown—and there they shall break the heifer’s neck. 5Then the priests, the sons of Levi, shall approach, for the LORD your God has chosen them to minister to HimhimHim and to bless in the name of the LORD; and by their pronouncement every dispute and every act of violence shall be adjudicated. 6All the elders of the city nearest the slain man shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley.

7and they shall declare, “Our hands did not shed this blood, nor did our eyes witness it. 8Atone, O the LORD, for YouryourYour people Israel, whom YouyouYou have redeemed, and do not charge innocent blood to YouryourYour people Israel.” Then the bloodguilt shall be atoned for them. 9Thus you shall purge the shedding of innocent blood from your midst, when you do what is right in the sight of the LORD.

Captives, Marriage, and Inheritance

10When you go out to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God delivers them into your hand and you take captives, 11and you see among the captives a woman of beautiful form, and you desire her and would take her as your wife, 12then you shall bring her into your house; she shall shave her head and trim her nails,

13and she shall remove the garments of her captivity, remain in your house, and mourn her father and mother for a full month; thereafter you may go in to her and become her husband, and she shall be your wife. 14But if you no longer delight in her, you shall let her go wherever she wishes; you shall not sell her for silver, nor treat her as merchandise, because you have humbled her.

Primogeniture and Family Order

15If a man has two wives, one beloved and the other unloved, and both bear him sons, and the firstborn son is the child of the unloved wife, 16then on the day he apportions his inheritance among his sons he may not grant the son of the beloved wife the rights of the firstborn in preference to the firstborn son of the unloved wife. 17Rather, he shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the unloved wife, by giving him a double portion of all that he possesses, for he is the firstfruits of his vigourvigourvigor, and the right of the firstborn is his.

Capital Punishment and Burial

18If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey his father’s voice or his mother’s voice, and though they discipline him he still does not listen, 19then his father and mother shall seize him and bring him out to the elders of his city and to the gate of his place.

20They shall say to the elders of his city, “This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he will not obey our voice; he is a glutton and a drunkard.”

21Then all the men of his city shall stone him to death, so that you purge the evil from your midst, and all Israel shall hear and fear. 22If a man has committed a capital offenceoffenceoffense and is to be put to death, and you hang him on a tree, 23his corpse shall not remain on the tree overnight; you shall surely bury him that same day, for a hanged man is accursed of God, so that you do not defile your land, which the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
