Jeremiah’s Twenty-Three-Year Warning to Judah

1The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah—that year being the first of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon— 2which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying:

3From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, even to this day—twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened. 4And the LORD has sent to you all HishisHis servants the prophets, sending them early and often; yet you have neither listened nor inclined your ear to hear.

5“Return, every one, from his evil way and from the evil of your deeds, and dwell in the land that the LORD has given to you and to your fathers forever and ever. 6Do not walk after other gods to serve them or bow down to them, and do not provoke MemeMe with the work of your hands; then I will do you no harm.”

7“Yet you did not listen to MemeMe,” declares the LORD, “so as to provoke MemeMe to anger with the work of your hands to your own hurt.”

The Decree of Seventy Years and the Fall of Babylon

8Therefore thus says the LORD of hosts: “Because you have not heard MymyMy words, behold, I will send for and take all the families of the north,” declares the LORD, “and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, MymyMy servant, and I will bring them against this land and its inhabitants and against all these surrounding nations. I will devote them to destruction and make them a desolation and a hissing and perpetual ruins. 10I will banish from them the voice of mirth and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the bride, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp. 11This whole land shall become a ruin and a waste, and these nations shall serve the king of Babylon seventy years.”

12“Then, when seventy years are completed, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans, for their iniquity,” declares the LORD, “and I will make it desolate forever. 13I will bring upon that land all MymyMy words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations. 14For many nations and great kings shall themselves reduce them to servitude; I will requite them according to their deeds and the work of their hands.”

The Cup of Wrath and the Nations That Must Drink

15For thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, to me: “Take from MymyMy hand this cup of the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it. 16They shall drink and stagger and go mad because of the sword that I am sending among them.”

17So I took the cup from the LORD’s hand and made all the nations drink to whom the LORD sent me: 18Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and officials, to make them a desolation, a hissing, a curse, and a ruin, as it is this day; 19Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his princes, and all his people; 20all the mixed multitude; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod; 21Edom, Moab, and the sons of Ammon;

22all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastland across the sea; 23Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who clip the hair at their temples; 24all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed peoples who dwell in the wilderness; 25all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of Media; 26all the kings of the north, far and near, one with another, and all the kingdoms of the world that are on the face of the earth. After them the king of Sheshach shall drink.

27Then you shall say to them, “Thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Drink, be drunk, vomit, fall, and rise no more, because of the sword that I am sending among you. 28And if they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, then you shall say to them, ‘Thus says the LORD of hosts: You shall drink!’”

29“For behold, I begin to bring disaster upon the city that is called by MymyMy name, and shall you go unpunished? You shall not go unpunished, for I am summoning a sword against all the inhabitants of the earth,” declares the LORD of hosts.

The LORD’s Roaring JudgmentJudgementJudgment upon the Earth

30“Therefore prophesy against them all these words, and say to them: ‘The LORD roars from on high and utters HishisHis voice from HishisHis holy habitation; HeheHe roars mightily against HishisHis fold. He shouts like those who tread grapes against all the inhabitants of the earth. 31A tumult reaches to the end of the earth, for the LORD has a controversy with the nations; HeheHe enters into judgmentjudgementjudgment with all flesh; HeheHe gives the wicked to the sword.’” declares the LORD.

32“Thus says the LORD of hosts: ‘Behold, evil is going forth from nation to nation, and a great tempest is stirring from the farthest parts of the earth. 33And those slain by the LORD on that day shall extend from one end of the earth to the other. They shall not be lamented or gathered or buried; they shall be dung upon the face of the ground.’”

Lamentation for the Shepherds and the Devastated Land

34“Wail, you shepherds, and cry out; roll in ashes, you leaders of the flock, for the days of your slaughter have come, and I will shatter you, and you shall fall like a precious vessel. 35Flight shall perish from the shepherds, and escape from the leaders of the flock. 36A voice—the cry of the shepherds and the wailing of the leaders of the flock! For the LORD is despoiling their pasture. 37The peaceful folds are silenced because of the fierce anger of the LORD. 38He has left HishisHis lair like a lion, for their land has become a waste because of the fierce anger of the oppressor and because of HishisHis fierce anger.”
