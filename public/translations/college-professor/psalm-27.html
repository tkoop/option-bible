David's Unshakable Confidence in the LORD

1The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom, then, shall I fear? The LORD is the bulwark of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? 2When malefactors draw near to devour my flesh—my adversaries and foes—they stumble and fall. 3Though a host encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war be raised against me, in this I put my trust.

The Singular Desire of the Psalmist's Heart

4One thing I have asked of the LORD, and that I will seek: that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD and to inquire in HishisHis sanctuary. 5For in the day of trouble HeheHe will conceal me in HishisHis shelter; in the covert of HishisHis tent HeheHe will hide me; HeheHe will set me high upon a rock. 6And now my head shall be lifted up above my enemies round about me; and in HishisHis tent I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make melody to the LORD.

Hopeful Waiting and Courageous Endurance
