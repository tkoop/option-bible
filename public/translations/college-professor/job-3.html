Job Curses the Day of His Birth

1Afterward Job opened his mouth and cursed his day. 2Job answered and said:

“3Let the day perish on which I was born, and the night that declared a male child had been conceived. 4Let that day be darkness; let not God regard it from on high, and let not light shine upon it. 5Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it; let a cloud dwell upon it; let the blackness of the day terrify it. 6Let thick darkness seize that night; let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not be numbered among the months. 7Behold, let that night be barren; let no cry of joy enter it. 8Let those who execrate the day execrate it, even those prepared to rouse Leviathan. 9Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it wait for light, but have none; and let it never behold the dawning of the morning. 10For it did not shut the doors of my mother’s womb, nor hide trouble from my eyes.”

The Longing for Rest in Death

“11Why did I not die at birth? Why did I not perish when I came from the womb? 12Why did knees receive me, or breasts, that I might nurse? 13For then I would have lain down and been quiet; I would have slept, and then I would have been at rest, 14with kings and counselors of the earth, who built for themselves places now in ruins, 15or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver; 16or I would have been hidden like a stillborn child, like infants who never saw light. 17There the wicked cease from turmoil, and there the weary are at rest. 18There prisoners are at ease together; they do not hear the voice of the oppressor. 19The small and the great are there, and the servant is free from his master.”

The Anguish of Unrelieved Suffering

“20Why is light given to one in misery, and life to the bitter in soul, 21who long for death, but it does not come, and seek it more eagerly than buried treasure, 22who rejoice exceedingly and are glad when they find the grave? 23To a man whose way is hidden, and whom God has hedged in? 24For my sighing comes in place of my bread, and my groanings are poured out like water. 25For the thing I feared has come upon me, and what I dreaded has befallen me. 26I am not at ease, nor am I quiet, and I have no rest; but trouble comes.”
