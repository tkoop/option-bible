Jehoshaphat’s Accession and Pious Reforms

1Jehoshaphat, his son, succeeded him and consolidated his authority over Israel. 2He stationed troops in every fortified city of Judah, and appointed garrisons throughout the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had seized.

3The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the earlier ways of David his father and did not resort to the Baals. 4Rather, he sought the God of his father and walked in HishisHis commandments, not emulating the conduct of Israel.

5Therefore the LORD established the kingdom firmly in his hand, and all Judah rendered tribute to Jehoshaphat, so that he amassed wealth and honourhonourhonor in abundance. 6His heart took courage in the ways of the LORD; moreover, he removed the high places and the Asherim from Judah.

A Nationwide Program of Instruction

7In the third year of his reign he dispatched his officials—Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in the cities of Judah. 8With them went the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, together with the priests Elishama and Jehoram. 9They instructed Judah, carrying with them the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD; they traversed every city of Judah and taught the people.

10The fear of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, so that none waged war against Jehoshaphat. 11Some Philistines brought gifts and silver as tribute to Jehoshaphat, and the Arabs brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats.

Fortifications and the Mustering of Judah’s Forces

12Jehoshaphat continued to grow in greatness; he built fortresses and store cities in Judah. 13He maintained extensive administrative and military business in the cities of Judah, and mighty men of valourvalourvalor were stationed in Jerusalem.

14Their enrollment by ancestral houses was as follows for Judah: Adnah the commander, with 300,000 mighty men of valourvalourvalor; 15next to him Jehohanan the commander, with 280,000; 16next Amasiah son of Zichri, who had volunteered for the LORD, with 200,000 mighty men; 17from Benjamin, Eliada the mighty man, with 200,000 armed with bow and shield; 18and next Jehozabad, with 180,000 equipped for battle. 19These served the king, in addition to those whom the king had posted in the fortified cities throughout Judah.
