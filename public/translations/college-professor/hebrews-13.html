Exhortations to Fraternal Love and Hospitality

1Let fraternal love abide. 2Do not be remiss in hospitality, for thereby some have entertained angels unawares. 3Remember the imprisoned as though you were shackled alongside them, and the maltreated as though you yourselves were sharing their suffering. 4Let marriage be held in honourhonourhonor among all, and let the marriage bed remain undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.

Contentment, Confidence, and Steadfast Doctrine

5Keep your conduct free from avarice and be content with what you have, for God HimselfhimselfHimself has said, “Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you.”

6Hence we may say with confidence, “The Lord is my helper; I shall not fear. What can man do to me?”

7Remember your leaders, who proclaimed to you the word of God; and, contemplating the outcome of their manner of life, imitate their faith. 8Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. 9Do not be swept away by diverse and foreign doctrines, for it is good that the heart be established by grace, not by foods, which have conferred no benefit upon those who partake of them.

The Superior Altar and Going Outside the Camp

10We possess an altar from which those who minister at the tabernacle have no authority to partake. 11For the bodies of those animals whose blood the high priest bears into the sanctuary as an offering for sin are burned outside the camp. 12Therefore Jesus likewise suffered outside the gate, that HeheHe might sanctify the people through HishisHis own blood.

13Let us therefore go forth unto HimhimHim outside the camp, bearing HishisHis reproach. 14For we have here no abiding city, but we seek the city that is to come.

Acceptable Sacrifices, Submission, and Final Blessing

15Through Jesus, therefore, let us continually offer up to God a sacrifice of praise, that is, the fruit of lips confessing HishisHis name. 16And do not be forgetful of doing good and of sharing, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased. 17Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they watch over your souls as those who must render an account, so that they may do this with joy and not with groaning—for that would be unprofitable to you.

18Pray for us, for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves honourablyhonourablyhonorably in all things. 19And I exhort you the more abundantly to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner.

20Now may the God of peace, who brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great ShepherdshepherdShepherd of the sheep, through the blood of the eternal covenant, 21equip you with every good thing to do HishisHis will, working in us what is pleasing in HishisHis sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.

22I beseech you, brethren, to bear with this word of exhortation, for I have written to you in brief. 23Know that our brother Timothy has been set at liberty; if he comes shortly, I shall see you with him. 24Salute all your leaders and all the saints; those from Italy greet you. 25Grace be with you all.
