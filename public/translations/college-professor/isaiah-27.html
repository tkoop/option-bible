The LORD's Cherished Vineyard

2In that day: “Sing of a delightful vineyard. 3I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually. Lest anyone harm it, I guard it night and day. 4I have no wrath. If only thorns and briers confronted MemeMe! The Purging of Jacob and the Ruin of the Fortified City

There the calf grazes; there it lies down and strips its boughs. 11When its boughs wither, they are broken; women come and use them for kindling. For this is a people devoid of understanding; therefore their MakermakerMaker will not pity them, and their CreatorcreatorCreator will not show them grace.

The Regathering of the Dispersed
