Daniel’s Vision of Four Beasts from the Sea

1In the first year of Belshazzar’s reign over Babylon, Daniel beheld a dream, and visions traversed his mind as he lay upon his couch; he then committed the dream to writing, distilling the essential purport of the affair. 2Daniel declared, “In my nocturnal vision I looked, and behold, the four winds of Heavenheavenheaven were churning the great sea. 3Four great beasts ascended from the sea, each distinct from the others. 4The first resembled a lion, yet it possessed the wings of an eagle; I watched until its wings were plucked away, whereupon it was lifted from the earth and made to stand upon its feet like a man, and a human heart was bestowed upon it. 5And behold, a second beast, like a bear, raised itself on one side, and three ribs were clenched between its teeth; a voice commanded it, “Arise and devour flesh in abundance.” 6After this I beheld another beast, like a leopard, with four wings of a bird upon its back; it had four heads, and dominion was granted to it. 7Thereafter, in my nocturnal visions, I saw a fourth beast, dreadful and exceedingly terrifying, possessed of great iron teeth; it devoured, crushed, and trampled the remainder with its feet, and it differed from all the beasts that preceded it, for it had ten horns. 8While I contemplated the horns, behold, another horn, small, sprang up among them, and three of the former horns were uprooted before it; and in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man, together with a mouth uttering grandiose things.

9I continued watching until thrones were set in place and the Ancient of DaysdaysDays took HishisHis seat. His garment was white as snow, and the hair of HishisHis head was like pure wool; HishisHis throne was flames of fire, and its wheels were burning fire. 10A river of fire issued forth and flowed from before HimhimHim; thousands upon thousands served HimhimHim, and myriads upon myriads stood before HimhimHim. The court convened, and the books were opened. 11I watched then because of the sound of the great words the horn was speaking; I watched until the beast was slain, its body destroyed, and given over to be burned with fire. 12As for the other beasts, their dominion was removed, though a prolongation of life was granted to them for a season and a time. 13In my nocturnal vision I continued to watch, and behold, OneoneOne like a SonsonSon of ManmanMan came with the clouds of Heavenheavenheaven; HeheHe approached the Ancient of DaysdaysDays and was brought before HimhimHim. 14To HimhimHim was given dominion, glory, and a kingdom, so that all peoples, nations, and languages should serve HimhimHim; HishisHis dominion is an everlasting dominion that shall not pass away, and HishisHis kingdom is one that shall not be destroyed.”

Daniel’s Distress and the Angelic Interpretation

15I, Daniel, was grieved in spirit, and the visions of my head alarmed me. 16I approached one of those standing by and sought from him the certain truth concerning all this, and he spoke to me and disclosed the interpretation of these matters. 17“These four great beasts are four kings who shall arise from the earth. 18But the saints of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh shall receive the kingdom and possess it forever—yes, forever and ever.” 19Then I desired to ascertain the truth concerning the fourth beast, which differed from all the others, exceedingly dreadful, with teeth of iron and claws of bronze, devouring and crushing, and trampling the remainder with its feet; 20and concerning the ten horns upon its head, and the other horn that came up, before which three fell, the horn that had eyes and a mouth speaking great things and appeared greater than its companions. 21As I watched, that horn made war against the saints and prevailed against them, 22until the Ancient of DaysdaysDays came and judgmentjudgementjudgment was rendered in favourfavourfavor of the saints of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh, and the appointed time arrived for the saints to possess the kingdom.

The Fourth Kingdom and the Triumph of the Saints

23This is what he said: “The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom that shall arise upon the earth, differing from all the kingdoms; it shall devour the whole EarthEarthearth, trample it down, and crush it. 24The ten horns are ten kings who shall arise from this kingdom, and after them another shall arise, different from the former ones, and he shall subdue three kings. 25He shall speak words against the MostmostMost HighhighHigh and wear out the saints of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh; he shall intend to alter the times and the law, and they shall be given into his hand for a time, times, and half a time. 26But the court shall sit, and his dominion shall be taken away, to be destroyed and annihilated forever. 27Then the kingdom, the dominion, and the greatness of the kingdoms under all Heavenheavenheaven shall be given to the holy people of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey HimhimHim.” 28Here is the end of the matter. I, Daniel, was greatly troubled by my thoughts, and my countenance changed; yet I kept the matter in my heart.
