The Commissioning of the Twelve and Their Mission

1Then, having summoned the twelve, HeheHe conferred upon them power and authority over every demon and the capacity to cure diseases. 2Moreover, HeheHe dispatched them to proclaim the kingdom of God and to restore the ailing to health.

6Then, going forth, they traversed the villages, evangelizing and healing everywhere.
