The Oracle Concerning the Drought

1The word of the the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the drought.

2Judah mourns, and her gates languish; they are bowed down to the ground in mourning, and the cry of Jerusalem ascends. 3Her magnates have dispatched their servants to draw water; they have reached the cisterns and found them dry; they have returned with their vessels unfilled; they have been put to shame and confounded, and have veiled their heads. 4Because the soil is fractured—for no rain has fallen upon the land—the tillers of the soil have been disgraced; they have covered their heads. 5Even the doe in the field has calved and forsaken her offspring, because no herbage remains. 6The wild asses have taken their stand upon the bare heights; they have snuffed the wind like jackals; their eyes have been consumed, because no verdure is to be found.

Jeremiah's Lament and Final Supplication

17You shall say this word to them: "Let my eyes stream with tears night and day, and let them not be still; for the virgin daughter of my people has been shattered with a great fracture, with an exceedingly grievous stroke." 18If I go forth into the field, behold, those slain by the sword; and if I enter the city, behold, those languishing with famine.
