Moses Convenes Israel and Recalls the Covenant at Horeb

1Moses summoned all Israel and declared to them: “Hear, O Israel, the statutes and ordinances that I am proclaiming in your hearing this day; learn them and be scrupulous to observe them. 2The LORD our God cut a covenant with us at Horeb. 3The LORD did not make this covenant with our ancestors, but with us—every one of us who is alive here today. 4The LORD spoke with you face to face on the mountain, from the midst of the fire. 5I stood between the LORD and you at that time to convey to you the word of the LORD, because you were terrified by the fire and did not ascend the mountain, saying:

The Decalogue: The Covenant Stipulations

Israel’s Terror and the LORD’s Mediated Word

22The LORD spoke these words to your whole assembly on the mountain, out of the midst of the fire, the cloud, and the thick darkness, with a loud voice, and HeheHe added nothing more; then HeheHe wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me. 23When you heard the voice out of the midst of the darkness, while the mountain was blazing with fire, you drew near to me—all the heads of your tribes and your elders—

24and you said, “Behold, the LORD our God has shown us HishisHis glory and HishisHis greatness, and we have heard HishisHis voice out of the midst of the fire; today we have seen that God speaks with a human being and yet that person lives. 25Now, therefore, why should we die? For this great fire will consume us, and we shall die. If we hear the voice of the LORD our God any longer, we shall die. 26For what mortal being has ever heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of fire, as we have, and survived? 27You go near and hear all that the LORD our God says; then speak to us all that the LORD our God speaks to you, and we will hear and do it.”

28The LORD heard the voice of your words when you spoke to me, and the LORD said to me, “I have heard the voice of the words of this people, which they have spoken to you; they have done well in all that they have spoken. 29Oh, that they had such a heart as to fear MemeMe and keep all MymyMy commandments always, so that it might be well with them and with their children forever! 30Go and say to them, ‘Return to your tents.’ 31But as for you, stand here by MemeMe, and I will speak to you all the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that you are to teach them, so that they may observe them in the land I am giving them to possess.”

A Final Charge to Walk in Obedience

32So be careful to do as the LORD your God has commanded you; do not turn aside to the right or to the left. 33Walk in all the way that the LORD your God has commanded you, so that you may live and prosper, and prolong your days in the land that you will possess.
