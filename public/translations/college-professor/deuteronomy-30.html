The Restoration of Israel and the Circumcision of the Heart

1When all these things have come upon you—the blessing and the curse that I have set before you—and you call them to mind among all the nations to which the LORD your God has driven you, 2you and your children shall return to the LORD your God and obey HishisHis voice with all your heart and all your soul, in accordance with all that I command you this day.

3Then the LORD your God will restore you from captivity and have compassion on you, gathering you again from all the peoples among whom the LORD your God has scattered you. 4Even if your exiles are at the ends of Heavenheavenheaven, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there HeheHe will take you. 5the LORD your God will bring you into the land your fathers possessed, and you shall possess it; HeheHe will prosper you and multiply you beyond your fathers.

6the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your descendants, so that you love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, and thereby live. 7the LORD your God will place all these curses upon your enemies and those who hate you, who have persecuted you.

8You shall turn again and obey the voice of the LORD, doing all HishisHis commandments that I command you this day. 9the LORD your God will make you abundantly prosperous in all the work of your hands, in the fruit of your womb, your livestock, and your soil, for good; for the LORD will once more rejoice over you for good, as HeheHe rejoiced over your fathers. 10This is contingent upon your obeying the voice of the LORD your God by keeping HishisHis commandments and statutes written in this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw, and upon your returning to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.

The Immediacy and Accessibility of the Commandment

11For this commandment that I am commanding you today is not too abstruse for you, nor is it remote. 12It is not in Heavenheavenheaven, that you should say, "Who will ascend to Heavenheavenheaven for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?" 13Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, "Who will cross the sea for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?" 14Rather, the word is very near you—in your mouth and in your heart—so that you may do it.

The Solemn Election Between Life and Death

15See, I have set before you today life and good, death and evil. 16If you heed what I command you today—loving the LORD your God, walking in HishisHis ways, and keeping HishisHis commandments, statutes, and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments—then you shall live and multiply, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are entering to possess. 17But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are drawn away to bow down to other gods and serve them,

18I declare to you today that you shall surely perish. You shall not prolong your days upon the land that you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess. 19I call Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth to witness against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse; therefore choose life, that you and your descendants may live, 20by loving the LORD your God, obeying HishisHis voice, and clinging to HimhimHim, for HeheHe is your life and the length of your days, that you may dwell in the land that the LORD swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—to give them.
