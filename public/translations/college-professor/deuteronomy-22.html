Restitution of Lost Property and Aid to Fallen Beasts

1Should you discern your brother's ox or sheep wandering astray, you must not avert your gaze from them; rather, you shall diligently restore them to your brother. 2If your brother does not dwell nearby, or you are unacquainted with him, then you shall bring the beast into your own dwelling, where it shall remain in your custody until your brother inquires after it; thereupon, you shall restore it to him. 3You shall extend the same care to his donkey, his garment, or any lost possession of your brother that you happen to find; you may not turn away from it. 4Should you observe your brother's donkey or ox collapsed upon the roadway, you must not disregard them; you shall assuredly assist him in raising them.

Distinctive Apparel, Agricultural Separation, and Domestic Safeguards

5No woman shall don the apparel of a man, nor shall any man array himself in woman's attire, for all who perpetrate such acts constitute an abomination to the LORD your God. 6Should you chance upon a bird's nest containing fledglings or eggs, whether in a tree or upon the ground, while the mother broods over the young or the eggs, you shall not seize the mother together with her offspring. 7You shall assuredly release the mother, yet you may take the young for your own use, so that prosperity may attend you and your days may be prolonged. 8When you erect a new dwelling, you shall construct a parapet upon its roof, lest you incur bloodguilt within your household should someone fall from it.

9You shall not sow your vineyard with heterogeneous seed, lest the entire harvest be forfeited—both the seed you have sown and the produce of the vineyard. 10You shall not yoke an ox and a donkey together in plowing. 11You shall not wear a garment woven of wool and linen combined. 12You shall fashion tassels upon the four corners of the cloak with which you cover yourself.

Marriage, Virginity, and the Adjudication of Defamation

13Should a man take a wife, consummate the marriage, and thereafter come to despise her, 14and should he fabricate allegations against her, impugning her reputation by declaring, “I espoused this woman, and upon approaching her I discovered that she was not a virgin,”

15then the young woman's father and mother shall take the tokens of her virginity and exhibit them before the elders of the city at the gate.

16The father of the young woman shall then address the elders, saying, “I gave my daughter to this man in marriage, and he has grown to hate her; 17and behold, he has imputed to her baseless charges, asserting, ‘I did not find your daughter a virgin.’ Yet these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity.” Then they shall spread the cloth before the elders of the city.

18Then the elders of that city shall seize the man and chastise him, 19and they shall impose upon him a fine of one hundred shekels of silver, which they shall give to the father of the young woman, because he has defamed a virgin of Israel. She shall remain his wife; he shall not be at liberty to divorce her for the remainder of his days. 20But if the accusation proves true, and the tokens of virginity are not found in the young woman, 21then they shall bring the young woman to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her to death, because she has committed a scandalous act in Israel by playing the harlot in her father's house. Thus shall you purge the evil from your midst.

22If a man is discovered lying with another man's wife, both of them—the man who lay with the woman and the woman herself—shall die; thus shall you purge the evil from Israel. 23If a betrothed virgin is encountered by a man in the city, and he lies with her, 24then you shall bring them both to the gate of that city and stone them to death—the young woman because she did not cry out for help though she was in the city, and the man because he violated his neighbourneighbourneighbor's wife. Thus shall you purge the evil from your midst.

25But if a man encounters a betrothed young woman in the open country, seizes her, and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her shall die. 26You shall do nothing to the young woman, for she has committed no capital sin; this matter is analogous to a man rising against his neighbourneighbourneighbor and murdering him. 27For he found her in the open country, and though the betrothed young woman cried for help, no one was present to rescue her.

28If a man finds an unbetrothed virgin, seizes her, and lies with her, and the matter comes to light, 29then the man who lay with her shall give the father of the young woman fifty shekels of silver, and she shall become his wife, because he has humbled her; he shall not be permitted to divorce her throughout all his days. 30No man shall take his father's wife, lest he expose his father's nakedness.
