A Communal Lament Recalling Past Deliverance and Pleading for Renewed Mercy

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah.

1We have heard with our ears, O God, what our fathers recounted to us—the deeds YouyouYou performed in their days, in days of old. 2With YouryourYour hand YouyouYou drove out the nations and planted them; YouyouYou crushed the peoples and cast them out. 3For they did not take the land by their sword, nor did their arm secure victory for them; rather, it was YouryourYour right hand, YouryourYour arm, and the radiance of YouryourYour face, because YouyouYou favouredfavouredfavored them.

4You are my King, O God; ordain victories for Jacob. 5Through YouyouYou we repel our adversaries; in YouryourYour name we trample our foes. 6For I do not trust in my bow, nor does my sword deliver me. 7For YouyouYou save us from our foes and put to shame those who hate us. 8In God we have boasted all day long, and we will give thanks to YouryourYour name forever. Selah

9Yet YouyouYou have rejected and humiliated us; YouyouYou no longer go forth with our armies. 10You made us retreat before the enemy, and those who hate us have plundered us. 11You gave us up like sheep to be devoured; YouyouYou scattered us among the nations. 12You sold YouryourYour people for nothing; YouyouYou gained no profit from their sale. 13You made us a reproach to our neighbors, a scorn and derision to those around us. 14You made us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples.

15All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face, 16because of the voice of the taunter and reviler, because of the enemy and avenger.

17All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten YouyouYou or dealt falsely with YouryourYour covenant. 18Our hearts have not turned back; our steps have not strayed from YouryourYour path. 19Yet YouyouYou have crushed us in the haunt of jackals and covered us with deepest darkness.

20If we had forgotten the name of our God or spread out our hands to a foreign god, 21would not God have discovered it, since HeheHe knows the secrets of the heart? 22Yet for YouryourYour sake we are killed all day long; we are regarded as sheep for the slaughter.

23Awake, O Lord! Why do YouyouYou sleep? Arise! Do not reject us forever. 24Why do YouyouYou hide YouryourYour face and forget our misery and oppression? 25For our soul has sunk to the dust; our bodies cling to the earth. 26Rise up; be our help! Redeem us for the sake of YouryourYour loving devotion.
