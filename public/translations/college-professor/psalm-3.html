David's Confidence in the LORD Amidst Rebellion

1The LORD, how my adversaries have multiplied! Many rise up against me. 2Many are declaring concerning me, “There is no salvation for him in God.” Selah.

3But YouyouYou, the LORD, are a shield encircling me—my glory and the OneoneOne who exalts me. 4I cry aloud to the LORD, and HeheHe answers me from HishisHis holy mountain. Selah.

5I lay down and slept; I awoke, for the LORD sustains me. 6I will not dread myriads of people who have arrayed themselves against me round about.

7Arise, the LORD! Deliver me, my God! For YouyouYou have smitten all my enemies upon the cheek; YouyouYou have shattered the teeth of the wicked. 8Salvation belongs to the LORD; YouryourYour blessing be upon YouryourYour people. Selah.
