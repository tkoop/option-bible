A Maskil of Asaph: Instruction and Intergenerational Memory

1Attend, O my people, to my instruction, and incline your ears to the words of my mouth. 2I will open my mouth in a parable; I will propound riddles whose origins lie in antiquity— 3things we have heard and known, and which our fathers have recounted to us. 4We shall not conceal them from their children, but shall recount to the latest generation the praises of the LORD, HishisHis might, and the wonders HeheHe has wrought.

5For HeheHe established a testimony in Jacob and instituted a law in Israel, which HeheHe commanded our fathers to make known to their children, 6so that the generation to come might know them—the children yet unborn—that they might arise and declare them to their own children, 7and that they might set their confidence in God, neither forgetting HishisHis works nor forsaking HishisHis commandments. 8Thus they would not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation whose heart was not steadfast and whose spirit was not faithful to God.

Ephraim’s Retreat and the Wilderness Provocations

9The Ephraimites, though armed with bows, retreated on the day of battle. 10They did not keep the covenant of God, and they refused to walk in HishisHis law. 11They forgot HishisHis deeds and the wonders HeheHe had shown them.

12In the sight of their fathers HeheHe performed wonders in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. 13He split the sea and led them through, and HeheHe made the waters stand like a wall. 14He guided them with a cloud by day, and all the night with a fiery light. 15He split rocks in the wilderness and gave them drink as from the great deep. 16He brought forth streams from the rock and made waters run down like rivers.

17Yet they sinned still more against HimhimHim, rebelling against the MostmostMost HighhighHigh in the arid waste. 18They tested God in their hearts by demanding food for their craving. 19They spoke against God, saying, “Can God prepare a table in the wilderness? 20Behold, HeheHe struck the rock, and waters gushed out, and streams overflowed. Can HeheHe give bread also? Can HeheHe provide meat for HishisHis people?”

21Therefore the LORD heard and was enraged; a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger ascended against Israel, 22because they did not believe in God and did not trust in HishisHis salvation.

23Yet HeheHe commanded the skies above and opened the doors of Heavenheavenheaven, 24and HeheHe rained down manna upon them to eat and gave them the grain of Heavenheavenheaven. 25Mortals ate the bread of angels; HeheHe sent them provision in abundance. 26He made the east wind blow in the heavens, and by HishisHis power HeheHe drove the south wind. 27He rained flesh upon them like dust, winged fowl like the sand of the seas. 28He let it fall in the midst of their camp, round about their dwellings. 29So they ate and were well filled, for HeheHe granted them their craving.

30But before their craving was sated, while the food was still in their mouths, 31the anger of God rose against them and slew their stoutest and struck down the chosen of Israel. 32In spite of all this they sinned still and did not believe HishisHis wondrous works. 33Therefore HeheHe consumed their days in futility and their years in terror. 34When HeheHe slew them, they sought HimhimHim; they repented and sought God earnestly. 35They remembered that God was their RockrockRock, and that the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God was their Redeemer.

36But they flattered HimhimHim with their mouth and lied to HimhimHim with their tongue. 37Their heart was not steadfast toward HimhimHim, nor were they faithful to HishisHis covenant. 38Yet HeheHe, being merciful, forgave their iniquity and did not destroy them; many times HeheHe turned HishisHis anger away and did not arouse all HishisHis wrath. 39For HeheHe remembered that they were flesh, a passing breath that does not return.

Divine Chastisement and the Exodus Deliverance

40How often they rebelled against HimhimHim in the wilderness and grieved HimhimHim in the desert! 41They turned back and tested God, and they provoked the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 42They did not remember HishisHis hand, nor the day when HeheHe redeemed them from the adversary, 43when HeheHe displayed HishisHis signs in Egypt and HishisHis wonders in the fields of Zoan, 44and turned their rivers into blood, so that their streams became undrinkable.

45He sent among them swarms of flies that devoured them, and frogs that ruined them. 46He gave their produce to the caterpillar and their labourlabourlabor to the locust. 47He destroyed their vines with hail and their sycamore trees with frost. 48He delivered their cattle to the hail and their flocks to bolts of lightning. 49He let loose upon them the fierceness of HishisHis anger—wrath, indignation, and distress—a band of destroying angels.

50He cleared a path for HishisHis anger; HeheHe did not spare their souls from death, but gave their lives over to the plague. 51He struck down all the firstborn of Egypt, the firstfruits of their vigourvigourvigor in the tents of Ham. 52But HeheHe led forth HishisHis people like sheep and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. 53He led them safely, so that they feared not, while the sea overwhelmed their enemies. 54He brought them to the border of HishisHis sanctuary, to this mountain that HishisHis right hand had acquired. 55He drove out nations before them and apportioned them an inheritance by line, settling the tribes of Israel in their tents.

Renewed Apostasy, the Rejection of Shiloh, and the Choice of David

56Yet they tested and rebelled against God the MostmostMost HighhighHigh, and did not keep HishisHis decrees. 57They turned back and acted treacherously like their fathers; they proved as unreliable as a warped bow. 58They provoked HimhimHim to anger with their high places and moved HimhimHim to jealousy with their graven images. 59When God heard this, HeheHe was furious and utterly rejected Israel, 60and HeheHe abandoned the tabernacle of Shiloh, the tent HeheHe had pitched among mortals, 61and delivered HishisHis strength into captivity and HishisHis splendoursplendoursplendor into the hand of the foe.

62He gave HishisHis people over to the sword and was enraged at HishisHis inheritance. 63Fire consumed HishisHis young men, and HishisHis maidens were not given in marriage. 64Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows could not weep. 65Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a warrior roused by wine. 66He beat back HishisHis enemies; HeheHe put them to everlasting reproach.

67He rejected the tent of Joseph and did not choose the tribe of Ephraim, 68but chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which HeheHe loved. 69He built HishisHis sanctuary like the high heavens, like the earth which HeheHe established forever. 70He chose David HishisHis servant and took him from the sheepfolds; 71from following the nursing ewes HeheHe brought him to shepherd Jacob HishisHis people and Israel HishisHis inheritance. 72So he shepherded them with integrity of heart and guided them with skilfulskilfulskillful hands.
