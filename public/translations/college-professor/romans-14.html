Receiving the Frail in Faith Without Quarreling over Opinions

1Receive the person whose faith is infirm, yet not for the sake of adjudicating contentious opinions. 2One individual is convinced that he may consume all things, whereas the feeble one subsists upon vegetables.

3The one who eats must not disdain the one who abstains, and the one who abstains must not condemn the one who eats, for God has welcomed him.

4Who are you to render judgmentjudgementjudgment upon another's servant? Before his own master he stands or falls; and he will stand, for the Lord is able to establish him.

5One person esteems one day above another, while another esteems all days alike; let each be fully persuaded in his own mind. 6The one who regards the day regards it for the Lord; the one who eats eats for the Lord, for he gives thanks to God; and the one who abstains abstains for the Lord and gives thanks to God.

Living and Dying in Devotion to the Lord

7For none of us lives for himself, and none dies for himself; 8for if we live, we live for the Lord, and if we die, we die for the Lord. Hence, whether we live or die, we are the Lord's. 9For to this end Christ died and returned to life, that HeheHe might be Lord both of the dead and of the living.

Standing Before the JudgmentJudgementJudgment Seat of God

10But why do you judge your brother? Or why do you regard your brother with contempt? For we shall all stand before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat of God. 11“As I live, says the Lord, every knee shall bow to MemeMe, and every tongue shall confess to God.” 12So then each of us will render an account of himself to God.

Abstaining from Conduct That Stumbles a Brother

13Therefore let us no longer judge one another, but rather render this judgmentjudgementjudgment: not to place a stumbling block or snare in a brother's way. 14I know and am persuaded in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself; yet for the one who regards anything as unclean, to him it is unclean.

15For if your brother is grieved on account of food, you are no longer walking in love. Do not, by your food, destroy the one for whom Christ died. 16Do not let your good be maligned.

17For the kingdom of God is not a matter of eating and drinking, but of righteousness, peace, and joy in the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit. 18For the one who serves Christ in this way is pleasing to God and approved by men.

19So then let us pursue what conduces to peace and to mutual edification. 20Do not destroy the work of God for the sake of food. All things are indeed clean, but it is evil for the person who eats and thereby gives offenceoffenceoffense. 21It is honourablehonourablehonorable not to eat meat, nor to drink wine, nor to do anything by which your brother stumbles.

22The faith that you have, hold as your own conviction before God. Blessed is the one who does not condemn himself in what he approves. 23But the one who doubts is condemned if he eats, because his eating is not from faith; and whatever is not from faith is sin.
