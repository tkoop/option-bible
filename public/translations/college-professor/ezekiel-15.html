The Oracle Delivered to Ezekiel

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying:

The Inutility of Vine Timber

2“Son of man, how does the wood of the vine surpass all other wood—the branch that grew among the trees of the forest? 3Can any useful article be fashioned from its timber? Could one even extract from it a peg upon which to suspend some vessel? 4Behold, it has been consigned to the fire as fuel: the flames have consumed both its extremities, and its core is charred. Could it still be of service for any work? 5Even when it was intact, it was not fashioned for any task; how much less, then, once fire has consumed and charred it, can it be turned to any useful purpose?”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Pronounced upon Jerusalem

6“Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Just as I have consigned the vine’s timber—standing among the trees of the forest—to the fire as fuel, so have I consigned the inhabitants of Jerusalem to the flames. 7I will set MymyMy face against them; though they come forth from the fire, the fire shall nonetheless devour them; and you shall know that I am the LORD when I set MymyMy face against them. 8I will lay the land waste, because they have acted treacherously, declares the Lord GOD.”
