Preparations for War and Confidence in Divine Aid

1When you march out to war against your enemies and perceive horses and chariots and a people more numerous than you, do not dread them, for the LORD your God, Who brought you up from the land of Egypt, is with you.

2And when you draw near to battle, the priest shall approach and speak to the people, 3saying, “Hear, O Israel: Today you are drawing near to battle against your enemies. Let not your hearts grow faint; do not be afraid, do not panic, and do not be terrified before them. 4For the LORD your God goes with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.”

Exemptions from Military Service

5Then the officers shall speak to the people, saying, “Who is the man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in battle and another man dedicate it. 6And who is the man who has planted a vineyard and has not yet partaken of its produce? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in battle and another man partake of its produce. 7And who is the man who has betrothed a woman and has not taken her in marriage? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in battle and another man marry her. 8And who is the man who is fearful and fainthearted? Let him go and return to his house, lest his comrades’ courage fail as his own.” 9And when the officers have finished speaking to the people, they shall appoint commanders of the armies to lead the people.

Terms of Peace and Siege with Distant Cities

10When you approach a city to fight against it, you shall proclaim peace to it. 11And if it answers you with peace and opens to you, then all the people found in it shall become forced labourlabourlabor for you and shall serve you. 12But if it does not make peace with you, but makes war against you, then you shall besiege it. 13And when the LORD your God delivers it into your hand, you shall strike every male in it with the edge of the sword. 14Only the women, the little ones, the livestock, and all that is in the city—all its spoil—you shall take as plunder for yourself, and you shall eat the spoil of your enemies, which the LORD your God has given you. 15Thus you shall do to all the cities that are very far from you, which are not of the cities of these nations.

Destruction of the Canaanite Peoples and Siege Regulations

16But of the cities of these peoples that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance, you shall not leave alive anything that breathes. 17Rather, you shall utterly destroy them—the Hittite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite—just as the LORD your God has commanded you, 18so that they may not teach you to do according to all their abominations, which they have done for their gods, and so you sin against the LORD your God. 19When you besiege a city for a long time, fighting against it to capture it, you shall not destroy its trees by laying an axe to them, for you may eat from them, but you shall not cut them down. For the tree of the field is not a man, that it should be drawn into your siege. 20Only a tree that you know is not a tree for food you may destroy and cut down, and you shall build siege works against the city that makes war with you, until it falls.
