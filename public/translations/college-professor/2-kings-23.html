Josiah Renews the Covenant Before the LORD

1The king dispatched emissaries, whereupon all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem were convoked before him. 2He then ascended to the house of the LORD, accompanied by every man of Judah and every inhabitant of Jerusalem, together with the priests, the prophets, and all the people, from the least to the greatest; and in their hearing he recited the entirety of the words of the book of the covenant discovered in the house of the LORD. 3Standing beside the pillar, the king solemnized a covenant before the LORD: that he would walk after the LORD and keep HishisHis commandments, testimonies, and statutes with all their heart and soul, thereby establishing the words of this covenant inscribed in the book. And all the people acceded to the covenant.

The Systematic Purging of Idolatry from Judah

4The king then instructed Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second order, and the doorkeepers to remove from the temple of the LORD every vessel fashioned for Baal, for Asherah, and for all the host of Heavenheavenheaven; he burned them outside Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron and conveyed their ashes to Bethel. 5He deposed the idolatrous priests whom the kings of Judah had appointed to offer incense upon the high places throughout the cities of Judah and in the environs of Jerusalem; likewise those who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, to the moon, to the constellations, and to all the host of Heavenheavenheaven. 6He removed the Asherah from the house of the LORD to the Kidron brook outside Jerusalem, burned it there, pulverized it to dust, and scattered that dust upon the graves of the common people. 7He demolished the quarters of the cult prostitutes that stood by the house of the LORD, where women wove hangings for Asherah.

8He convened all the priests from the cities of Judah and defiled the high places where they had burned incense, from Geba to Beersheba; he also demolished the high places at the gates, including the one at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the city governor, situated on the left as one entered the city gate. 9Yet the priests of the high places were not permitted to ascend to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem; instead, they partook of unleavened bread among their brethren. 10He desecrated Topheth in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom, so that no one could cause his son or daughter to pass through the fire to Molech.

11He removed the horses that the kings of Judah had dedicated to the sun at the entrance to the house of the LORD, near the chamber of Nathan-melech the eunuch, which lay in the precincts; and he burned the sun chariots with fire. 12The king tore down the altars upon the roof of Ahaz’s upper chamber, which the kings of Judah had erected, as well as the altars Manasseh had built in the two courts of the house of the LORD; he pulverized them and cast their dust into the Kidron brook. 13He defiled the high places on the right side of the Mount of Corruption, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians, for Chemosh the abomination of Moab, and for Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites. 14He shattered the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherim, and filled their sites with human bones.

The Destruction of Bethel’s Altar and Samaria’s Shrines

15Furthermore, he demolished the altar at Bethel and the high place that Jeroboam son of Nebat—who had caused Israel to sin—had erected; he burned the high place, reduced it to powder, and incinerated the Asherah. 16As Josiah turned, he noticed the tombs on the hill; he sent for the bones, removed them from the tombs, and burned them upon the altar, thereby defiling it, in fulfillmentfulfilmentfulfillment of the word of the LORD proclaimed by the man of God who had foretold these deeds.

17He asked, “What monument is that which I behold?”

The men of the city replied, “It is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah and proclaimed the very deeds you have performed against the altar of Bethel.”

18He said, “Leave him undisturbed; let no one displace his bones.” So they spared his bones, together with those of the prophet who had come from Samaria.

19Josiah likewise removed all the shrines of the high places in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel had built to provoke the LORD to anger, and he treated them as he had treated Bethel. 20He slaughtered all the priests of the high places upon their altars and burned human bones upon them; then he returned to Jerusalem.

The Passover Restored and the Aftermath of Josiah’s Reign

21The king commanded all the people, “Observe the Passover to the LORD your God, as it is prescribed in this book of the covenant.”

22Indeed, no Passover like it had been observed since the days of the judges who judged Israel, nor throughout the reigns of the kings of Israel and Judah. 23Only in the eighteenth year of King Josiah was this Passover celebrated to the LORD in Jerusalem. 24Moreover, Josiah eradicated the mediums, the spiritists, the household gods, the idols, and every abomination discerned in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, so that he might fulfillfulfilfulfill the words of the law written in the book Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the LORD.

25Before him there had been no king like him, who turned to the LORD with all his heart, soul, and might, in accordance with the whole law of Moses; nor did any arise after him who resembled him.

26Nevertheless, the LORD did not turn from the fierceness of HishisHis great wrath, which had been kindled against Judah because of all the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked HimhimHim. 27The LORD declared, “I will remove Judah from MymyMy sight as I removed Israel; I will reject this city Jerusalem, which I chose, and the house concerning which I said, ‘My name shall be there.’”

28The remaining deeds of Josiah, and all that he accomplished, are they not recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? 29In his days Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, marched against the king of Assyria at the Euphrates; King Josiah went out to oppose him and was slain at Megiddo as soon as he saw him. 30His servants conveyed him dead in a chariot from Megiddo, brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his own tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah, anointed him, and enthroned him in his father’s place.

31Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king; he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah. 32He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, following all that his fathers had done.

33Pharaoh Neco imprisoned him at Riblah in the land of Hamath, preventing his reign in Jerusalem, and imposed upon the land a tribute of one hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.

34Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king in place of his father Josiah, changed his name to Jehoiakim, and carried Jehoahaz away; he went to Egypt and died there. 35Jehoiakim paid the silver and gold to Pharaoh; yet to raise the money he taxed the land according to Pharaoh’s demand, exacting from each person according to his assessment in order to remit it to Pharaoh Neco.

36Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king; he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zebudah daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah. 37He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, according to all that his fathers had done.
