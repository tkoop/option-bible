Israel's Arrival at Sinai and the Covenant Summons

The People's Assent and the Promise of Divine Presence

7So Moses came and summoned the elders of the people, and laid before them all these words that the LORD had enjoined upon him. 8And all the people responded in unison, saying, "All that the LORD has spoken we will perform." And Moses conveyed the people's words back to the LORD.

9The LORD said to Moses, "Behold, I am about to come to you in a dense cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you, and may also repose enduring trust in you." And Moses reported to the LORD the words of the people.

Consecration for the Divine Descent

10The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them launder their garments, 11and let them be prepared for the third day; for on the third day the LORD will descend upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people. 12And you shall demarcate boundaries for the people round about, saying, 'Take care that you do not ascend the mountain or touch its extremity. Whoever touches the mountain shall assuredly be put to death. 13No hand shall touch him, but he shall assuredly be stoned or transfixed with arrows; whether human or beast, he shall not survive. When the trumpet emits a prolonged blast, they may ascend the mountain.'"

14So Moses descended from the mountain to the people and sanctified the people, and they laundered their garments. 15And he said to the people, "Be prepared for the three days; abstain from sexual relations."

The Theophany and the Sanctified Boundaries

16On the third day, at daybreak, there were thunderings and lightnings, and a heavy cloud upon the mountain, and an exceedingly loud trumpet blast, so that all the people in the camp quaked. 17Then Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stationed themselves at the foot of the mountain. 18Mount Sinai was altogether enveloped in smoke because the LORD had descended upon it in fire; its smoke ascended like the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mountain quaked violently. 19And as the trumpet's sound waxed louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him with thunder.

20The LORD descended to the summit of Mount Sinai, and the LORD summoned Moses to the summit, and Moses ascended. 21And the LORD said to Moses, "Descend and admonish the people, lest they break through to the LORD to gaze, and many of them perish. 22Let the priests also, who draw near to the LORD, sanctify themselves, lest the LORD erupt against them."
