Oracle Concerning Philistia

28In the year of King Ahaz's demise, this oracle was delivered: 29"Rejoice not, all you Philistia, that the rod which smote you is broken, for from the root of the serpent shall come forth a viper, and its offspring shall be a flying serpent. 30The firstborn of the poor shall pasture, and the needy shall lie down in safety; but I will slay your root with famine, and your remnant I will put to death. 31Wail, O gate; cry out, O city; dissolve in terror, all you Philistia; for smoke comes from the north, and there is no straggler in his ranks." 32What answer, then, will be given to the messengers of the nation?
