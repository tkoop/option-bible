1Hear my cry, O God; attend to my prayer. 2From the farthest reaches of the earth I call to YouyouYou when my heart is overwhelmed; lead me to the rock that rises above me. 3For YouyouYou have been my refuge, a fortified tower against the foe.

4I shall dwell in YouryourYour tent forevermore; I shall take refuge beneath the shelter of YouryourYour wings. Selah. 5For YouyouYou, O God, have heard my vows; YouyouYou have granted me the inheritance of those who fear YouryourYour name.

6You shall add days to the king; his years shall extend through generation upon generation. 7He shall sit in the presence of God forever; steadfast love and truth shall preserve him. 8So I will sing praise to YouryourYour name forever, that I may discharge my vows day by day.
