The Summoning of Jerusalem's Destroyers

1Then HeheHe cried in my ears with a loud voice, saying, "Draw near, O executioners of the city, each with his weapon of destruction in his hand." 2And behold, six men came from the direction of the upper gate, which faces north, each with his shattering weapon in his hand; and among them was one man clothed in linen, with a scribe's inkhorn at his waist. They entered and stood beside the bronze altar. 3The glory of the God of Israel rose from above the cherub where it had been to the threshold of the temple, and HeheHe called to the man clothed in linen who had the scribe's inkhorn at his waist.

The Marking of the Sighing Remnant

4And the LORD said to him, "Pass through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and put a mark upon the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the abominations committed within it."

The Execution of JudgmentJudgementJudgment from the Sanctuary

5To the others HeheHe said in my hearing, "Pass through the city after him and strike; let your eye not spare, and you shall show no pity." 6"Slay outright the old man, the young man, the virgin, the woman, and the little child, but do not touch any man upon whom is the mark; and begin at MymyMy sanctuary." So they began with the elders who were before the temple. 7And HeheHe said to them, "Defile the temple and fill the courts with the slain. Go out!" So they went out and struck throughout the city.

The Prophet's Intercession and Divine Vindication

8As they struck, I was left alone. I fell upon my face and cried out, "Ah, Lord the LORD, will YouyouYou destroy the whole remnant of Israel by pouring out YouryourYour wrath upon Jerusalem?" 9He said to me, "ThetheThe iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah is exceedingly great; the land is full of bloodshed, and the city is full of perversity, for they say, 'The LORD has forsaken the land; the LORD does not see.' 10So I will not look with pity or spare them, but I will bring their ways upon their own heads." 11Then the man clothed in linen, who had the inkhorn at his waist, reported, "I have done as YouyouYou commanded."
