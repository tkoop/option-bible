The Priestly Diagnosis of Human Leprosy

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2“When a person develops upon the skin of his body a swelling, an eruption, or a bright spot, and it becomes in the skin of his body an affliction of leprosy, he shall be brought to Aaron the priest, or to one of his sons among the priests. 3The priest shall inspect the affliction on the skin of the flesh; and if the hair in the afflicted area has turned white and the affliction appears deeper than the skin of the flesh, it is an affliction of leprosy. Upon inspecting him, the priest shall pronounce him unclean.

4“But if the bright spot is white in the skin of his body and does not appear deeper than the skin, and its hair has not turned white, the priest shall confine the afflicted person for seven days. 5On the seventh day the priest shall inspect him; and if, in his judgmentjudgementjudgment, the affliction has remained stationary and has not spread in the skin, then the priest shall confine him a second time for seven days. 6On the seventh day the priest shall inspect him again; and if the affliction has faded and has not spread in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him clean: it is merely an eruption. He shall wash his garments and be clean. 7But if the eruption spreads in the skin after he has presented himself to the priest for his cleansing, he shall present himself again to the priest. 8The priest shall look; and if the eruption has spread in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is leprosy.

9“When a man is afflicted with leprosy, he shall be brought to the priest. 10The priest shall inspect him; and if there is a white swelling in the skin that has turned the hair white, with raw flesh in the swelling, 11it is chronic leprosy in the skin of his body. The priest shall pronounce him unclean and shall not confine him, for he is unclean. 12If the leprosy breaks out abundantly in the skin, so that it covers all the skin of the afflicted person from his head to his feet, so far as the priest can see, 13then the priest shall inspect him; and if the leprosy has covered all his flesh, he shall pronounce him clean. Since it has all turned white, he is clean. 14But whenever raw flesh appears on him, he shall be unclean. 15The priest shall inspect the raw flesh and pronounce him unclean; raw flesh is unclean, for it is leprosy. 16If, however, the raw flesh turns again and becomes white, he shall come to the priest, 17and the priest shall inspect him; and if the affliction has turned white, the priest shall pronounce the afflicted person clean: he is clean.

18“If there is a boil on the skin of a person’s body and it heals, 19and in the place of the boil there appears a white swelling or a reddish-white bright spot, it shall be shown to the priest. 20The priest shall inspect it; and if it appears deeper than the skin and its hair has turned white, the priest shall pronounce him unclean. It is leprosy that has broken out in the boil. 21But if the priest examines it and finds no white hair in it, and it is not deeper than the skin but has faded, the priest shall confine him for seven days. 22If it spreads in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is a disease. 23But if the bright spot remains in its place and does not spread, it is the scar of the boil, and the priest shall pronounce him clean.

24“Or if the body sustains a burn by fire, and the raw flesh of the burn becomes a reddish-white or white bright spot, 25the priest shall inspect it; and if the hair in the bright spot has turned white and it appears deeper than the skin, it is leprosy. It has broken out in the burn, and the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is an affliction of leprosy. 26But if the priest examines it and finds no white hair in the bright spot, and it is not deeper than the skin but has faded, the priest shall confine him for seven days. 27On the seventh day the priest shall inspect him; and if it is spreading in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is an affliction of leprosy. 28But if the bright spot remains in its place and does not spread in the skin, but has faded, it is a swelling from the burn, and the priest shall pronounce him clean, for it is the scar of the burn.”

Afflictions of the Scalp, Face, and Baldness

29“When a man or a woman has an affliction on the head or in the beard, 30the priest shall inspect the affliction; and if it appears deeper than the skin, with yellow, fine hair in it, the priest shall pronounce him unclean. It is a scall, an affliction of leprosy of the head or beard. 31If the priest examines the scall and it does not appear deeper than the skin, and there is no black hair in it, the priest shall confine the person with the scall for seven days. 32On the seventh day the priest shall inspect the affliction; and if the scall has not spread, and there is no yellow hair in it, and the scall does not appear deeper than the skin, 33then he shall shave himself, though he shall not shave the scall, and the priest shall confine the person with the scall for a second period of seven days. 34On the seventh day the priest shall inspect the scall; and if the scall has not spread in the skin and does not appear deeper than the skin, the priest shall pronounce him clean. He shall wash his garments and be clean.

35“But if the scall spreads in the skin after his cleansing, 36the priest shall inspect him; and if the scall has spread in the skin, the priest need not search for yellow hair: he is unclean. 37If, however, the scall remains unchanged in his sight and black hair has grown in it, the scall is healed; he is clean, and the priest shall pronounce him clean.

38“When a man or a woman has bright spots on the skin of the body, white bright spots, 39the priest shall inspect them; and if the bright spots on the skin of the body are of a dull white, it is a benign discoloration that has broken out on the skin: he is clean.

40“If a man loses the hair of his head, he is bald; he is clean. 41If he loses the hair from the front of his head, he has a bald forehead; he is clean. 42But if there is on the bald head or the bald forehead a reddish-white affliction, it is leprosy breaking out on his bald head or forehead. 43The priest shall inspect him; and if the swelling of the affliction on his bald head or forehead is reddish-white, like the appearance of leprosy in the skin of the body, 44he is a leprous man; he is unclean. The priest shall pronounce him unclean; his affliction is on his head.

45“The leper who has the affliction shall wear torn garments, let his hair hang loose, cover his upper lip, and cry out, ‘Unclean, unclean.’ 46As long as the affliction remains upon him, he is unclean. He is unclean; he shall dwell apart, and his dwelling shall be outside the camp.”

Leprosy in Garments and Woven or Leather Articles

47“When an affliction of leprosy appears in a garment, whether a woolen or a linen garment, 48or in the warp or the woof of linen or wool, or in a skin, or in anything made of skin, 49and if the affliction is greenish or reddish in the garment, in the skin, in the warp, in the woof, or in any article of skin, it is an affliction of leprosy, and it shall be shown to the priest. 50The priest shall inspect the affliction and confine the article that bears the affliction for seven days. 51On the seventh day he shall inspect the affliction; and if it has spread in the garment, in the warp or the woof, or in the skin—whatever the skin may be used for—the affliction is a persistent leprosy: it is unclean. 52He shall burn the garment, the warp, or the woof, whether of wool or linen, or any article of skin in which the affliction is found, for it is a persistent leprosy. It shall be burned in the fire.

53“If, however, the priest examines it and the affliction has not spread in the garment, in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of skin, 54the priest shall command that the article bearing the affliction be washed, and he shall confine it for a second period of seven days. 55After it has been washed, the priest shall inspect it; and if the appearance of the affliction has not changed, though it has not spread, it is unclean. It shall be burned in the fire; the corruption is on its front or its back. 56But if the priest examines it and the afflicted area has faded after washing, he shall tear it out of the garment, the skin, the warp, or the woof. 57If it appears again in the garment, the warp, the woof, or any article of skin, it is spreading; the article in which the affliction is found shall be burned in the fire. 58But if the affliction departs from the garment, the warp, the woof, or any article of skin when it is washed, it shall be washed a second time and be clean. 59This is the law for an affliction of leprosy in a garment of wool or linen, in the warp or the woof, or in any article of skin, for determining whether it is clean or unclean.”
