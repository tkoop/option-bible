A Yearning for Unashamed Affection

1“Were you but my brother, one who nursed at my mother's breast—then, finding you in the open, I could kiss you, and none would hold me in contempt. 2I would lead you and bring you into my mother's house—there you would instruct me—and I would give you spiced wine to drink, the nectar of my pomegranate. 3His left hand beneath my head, and his right hand embraces me.”

4“Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you: do not arouse or awaken love until it so desires.”

Love's Invincible Power and the Beloved's Ascent

5Who is this ascending from the wilderness, leaning upon her beloved? I awakened you beneath the apple tree; there your mother conceived you, there she who bore you was in labourlabourlabor. 6“Set me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm; for love is as strong as death, and jealousy is as unrelenting as Sheol. Its flashes are flashes of fire, a blaze of unsurpassed intensity. 7Many waters cannot quench love, nor can rivers sweep it away; and if a man were to offer all the wealth of his house for love, he would be utterly despised.”

The Little Sister and the Vineyard

8“We have a little sister, and she has no breasts. What shall we do for our sister on the day she is betrothed? 9If she is a wall, we will build upon her a battlement of silver; but if she is a door, we will enclose her with cedar boards.”

10“I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers; then I became in his eyes as one who finds peace.”

11Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon; he leased the vineyard to keepers, and each was to bring a thousand pieces of silver for its fruit.

12“My vineyard, my very own, is before me: you, Solomon, may have the thousand, and those who tend its fruit two hundred.”

A Final Summons to the Beloved

13“O you who dwell in the gardens, your companions are listening for your voice; let me hear it.”

14“Flee, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young stag upon the mountains of spices.”
