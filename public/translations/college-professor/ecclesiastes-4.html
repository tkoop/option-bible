Oppression and the Tears of the Powerless on Earth

1I turned again and beheld all the oppression wrought on EarthEarthearth: I saw the tears of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; power resided with their oppressors, and for them likewise there was no comforter.

2Then I extolled the dead, who had already died, above the living, who yet remain alive; 3and I judged that better than both is the one who has not yet come into being, who has not witnessed the evil deed accomplished on EarthEarthearth.

Envy, Idleness, and the Measure of Contentment

4I also observed all toil and every skilfulskilfulskillful achievement, and perceived that it arises from one person's envy of another. This too is meaningless and futile.

5The fool, folding his hands in idleness, devours his own substance. 6Yet better is a single handful accompanied by tranquillitytranquillitytranquility than two full hands of toil and futility.

The Solitary Toiler and the Value of Companionship

7Again I turned and beheld meaninglessness on EarthEarthearth: 8there is one who is alone, having neither companion nor brother; there is no end to all his toil, and his eyes are not sated with riches. "For whom, then," he asks, "do I toil, while depriving myself of good?" This too is meaningless, and it is an evil business.

9Two are better than one, because they receive a good reward for their toil. 10For if one falls, his companion will raise him up; but woe to the solitary one who falls and has no second to lift him up. 11Likewise, if two lie together, they are warmed; but how can one be warmed alone? 12And though one may be overpowered, two can stand against him; a threefold cord is not quickly severed.

The Rise and Decline of a Popular Successor

13Better is a poor yet wise youth than an old and foolish king who no longer knows how to receive admonition. 14For the youth came forth from prison to reign, though he was born poor in his own kingdom.

15I saw all the living who walk on EarthEarthearth, together with the second youth who is to stand in his place. 16There is no end to all the people, to all who preceded them; yet those who come after will not rejoice in him. This too is meaningless and futile.
