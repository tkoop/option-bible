The Bride’s Nocturnal Search for Her Beloved

1Upon my bed by night I sought the beloved of my soul; I sought him, but did not find him. 2I will now arise and go about the city, through the streets and squares; I will seek the beloved of my soul. I sought him, but did not find him.

3The watchmen who patrol the city found me: “Have you seen the beloved of my soul?”

4Scarcely had I passed them when I found the beloved of my soul. I seized him and would not let him go until I had brought him to my mother’s house, to the chamber of the one who bore me.

5Daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles or by the does of the field: Do not stir up or awaken love until it pleases.

The Royal Procession Ascending from the Wilderness

6Who is this coming up from the wilderness like columns of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and frankincense, with every powder of the merchant?

7Behold, it is Solomon’s palanquin, escorted by sixty warriors from the mightiest of Israel, 8all of them grasping swords and trained in war, each with his sword at his thigh, because of the terror of the nights.

9King Solomon has made for himself a palanquin from the wood of Lebanon. 10He has made its posts of silver, its base of gold, its seat of purple; its interior is inlaid with love by the daughters of Jerusalem.

11“Come out, O daughters of Zion, and gaze upon King Solomon, wearing the crown with which his mother crowned him on the day of his wedding, the day of the gladness of his heart.”
