The LORD's Commission to Joshua in His Advanced Age

1Now Joshua was old and advanced in years, and the LORD said to him, “You are old and advanced in years, and very much of the land remains to be possessed—this is the land that remains: all the districts of the Philistines and all the Geshurites, 2from the Shihor, which lies before Egypt, to the border of Ekron northward (this is reckoned as Canaanite territory)—that of the five Philistine rulers of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, together with the Avvites; 3from the south, all the land of the Canaanites, from Mearah of the Sidonians to Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorites; 4and the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath. 5As for all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim—all the Sidonians—I MyselfmyselfMyself will drive them out before the Israelites. 6Be sure to allot this land as an inheritance to Israel, as I have commanded you.”

The Division of the Remaining Inheritance

7Now, therefore, apportion this land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 8The Reubenites and the Gadites, together with the half-tribe of Manasseh, had already received the inheritance that Moses, the servant of the LORD, had given them east of the Jordan. 9Their territory extended from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, and all the tableland from Medeba to Dibon; 10and all the cities of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, to the border of the Ammonites; 11and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan to Salecah; 12all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei—he was one of the last of the Rephaim—and Moses struck them down and dispossessed them. 13Yet the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites, so Geshur and Maacath dwell among the Israelites to this day. 14To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses gave no inheritance; the fire offerings of the LORD, the God of Israel, are his inheritance, as HeheHe had spoken to him.

The Portion Allotted to Reuben

15Moses gave the following inheritance to the clans of the tribe of Reuben: 16their territory extended from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, and all the tableland near Medeba; 17Heshbon and all its cities on the plateau—Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 18Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath, 19Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley, 20Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth; 21all the cities of the plateau and the whole kingdom of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who had reigned in Heshbon. Moses struck him down, along with the chiefs of Midian—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba—princes of Sihon who had lived in the land. 22The Israelites also killed Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, along with the others they put to the sword. 23The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan. These were the cities and villages given as an inheritance to the Reubenites, according to their clans.

The Allotments of Gad and the Half-Tribe of Manasseh

24This is what Moses had given to the clans of the tribe of Gad: 25their territory included Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, near Rabbah; 26and from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir; 27and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon—the rest of the kingdom of Sihon, king of Heshbon. The Jordan River was the border, extending to the edge of the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth on the east side of the Jordan. 28These were the cities and villages given as an inheritance to the Gadites, according to their clans. 29This is what Moses had given to the clans of the half-tribe of Manasseh: 30their territory included Mahanaim, all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og, king of Bashan, and all the towns of Jair in Bashan—sixty cities; 31half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan. These were allotted to the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh—to half the clan of Machir. 32These are the portions Moses had given them on the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho. 33To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses had given no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as HeheHe had promised them.
