Moab’s Supplication and the Davidic Throne

1DispatchDispatch, despatchDispatch a lamb to the sovereign of the land, from Sela in the wilderness, to the mountain of the daughter of Zion. 2Like a fluttering bird driven from its nest, the daughters of Moab shall linger at the fords of the Arnon.

3Bring counsel, execute judgmentjudgementjudgment; let your shadow be as night at noonday; conceal the outcasts, and do not betray the fugitive. 4Let MymyMy outcasts dwell with you; be a refuge for Moab from the destroyer, for the oppressor has ceased, devastation is ended, and the trampler has vanished from the land. 5In steadfast love a throne shall be established, and one shall sit upon it in truth in the tent of David, judging and seeking justice, swift in righteousness.

Moab’s Pride and the Prophet’s Lament

6We have heard of Moab’s pride—how exceedingly proud he is—of his haughtiness, his arrogance, and his wrath; yet his boasting is empty. 7Therefore Moab shall wail, yea, all Moab shall wail; for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth they shall mourn, utterly stricken. 8For the fields of Heshbon languish, and the vine of Sibmah; the rulers of the nations have struck down its choice vines, which reached as far as Jazer and strayed into the desert; its shoots spread abroad and passed over the sea.

9Therefore I weep with the weeping of Jazer for the vine of Sibmah; I water you with my tears, O Heshbon and Elealeh, for upon your summer fruits and your harvest the shout of joy has fallen. 10Joy and gladness are taken from the fruitful field; in the vineyards there is no singing, no shouting; the treader treads out no wine in the presses, for I have caused the vintage shout to cease. 11Therefore my inward parts sound like a harp for Moab, and my soul for Kir-hareseth.

The Futility of Moab’s Worship and the Final Oracle

12And when Moab appears upon the high place, weary, and comes to his sanctuary to pray, he shall not prevail. 13This is the word that the LORD spoke concerning Moab from of old. 14But now the LORD says: Within three years, as the years of a hired worker, the glory of Moab shall be despised, together with all his great multitude; and the remnant shall be very small and feeble.
