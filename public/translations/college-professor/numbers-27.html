The Daughters of Zelophehad and the Statute of Inheritance

1The daughters of Zelophehad—Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah—descended from Hepher, Gilead, Machir, and Manasseh, and thus belonged to the families of Manasseh son of Joseph. 2They drew near to the entrance of the tent of meeting and stood before Moses, before Eleazar the priest, before the leaders, and before the whole congregation, and they said:

“3Our father died in the wilderness, though he was not among the company of Korah that gathered against the LORD; rather, he died for his own sin, and he left no sons. 4Why should our father’s name be expunged from his clan merely because he had no son? Grant us a possession among our father’s brothers.”

5Moses brought their case before the LORD.

6The LORD said to Moses, 7“The daughters of Zelophehad speak rightly. You must certainly give them a possession as an inheritance among their father’s brothers, and you must transfer their father’s inheritance to them.

“8Moreover, speak to the Israelites and say: If a man dies leaving no son, then you must transfer his inheritance to his daughter. 9If he has no daughter, you must give his inheritance to his brothers. 10If he has no brothers, you must give his inheritance to his father’s brothers. 11And if his father has no brothers, you must give his inheritance to his nearest kinsman within his clan, who shall take possession of it. This shall be a statutory ordinance for the Israelites, as the LORD has commanded Moses.”

Moses Views the Promised Land

12The LORD said to Moses, “Go up into this Mount Abarim and survey the land that I have given to the Israelites. 13When you have seen it, you too shall be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron was gathered, 14because you rebelled against MymyMy word in the wilderness of Zin during the congregation’s contention, failing to sanctify MemeMe at the waters before their eyes”—those waters being the waters of Meribah Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin.

Moses Prays for a Successor, and the LORD Appoints Joshua

15Moses spoke to the LORD, saying, 16“the LORD, God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation 17who shall go out before them and come in before them, who shall lead them out and bring them in, so that the congregation of the LORD may not be like sheep without a shepherd.”

18The LORD said to Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the SpiritspiritSpirit, and lay your hand upon him. 19Stand him before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation, and commission him in their sight. 20Invest him with some of your authority, so that the entire congregation of the Israelites may obey. 21He shall stand before Eleazar the priest, who shall inquire for him by the judgmentjudgementjudgment of the Urim before the LORD; at his word they shall go out, and at his word they shall come in—he and all the Israelites with him, even the whole congregation.”

22Moses did as the LORD had commanded him: he took Joshua and stood him before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation. 23He laid his hands upon him and commissioned him, exactly as the LORD had spoken through Moses.
