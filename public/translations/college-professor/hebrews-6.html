Advancing Beyond Elementary Doctrine

1Therefore, leaving behind the elementary teaching concerning Christ, let us be carried onward toward maturity, without repeatedly laying a foundation of repentance from dead works and faith toward God, 2of instruction concerning baptisms, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgmentjudgementjudgment. 3And this we shall do, if God permits.

The Irreversibility of Apostasy

4For it is impossible, in the case of those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift and have become partakers of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 5who have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come, 6and then have fallen away, to renew them again to repentance, since they are crucifying the SonsonSon of God afresh for themselves and holding HimhimHim up to public contempt.

7For land that has drunk the rain frequently falling upon it and brings forth vegetation useful to those for whose sake it is cultivated receives blessing from God; 8but if it bears thorns and thistles, it is worthless and near to being cursed, and its end is to be burned.

Confidence in the Saints’ Perseverance

9But although we speak in this way, beloved, we are convinced of better things concerning you—things that accompany salvation. 10For God is not so unjust as to forget your work and the love that you showed for HishisHis name in having served and in continuing to serve the saints.

11And we desire that each one of you show the same earnestness so as to realizerealiserealize the full assurance of hope to the end, 12in order that you may not become indolent but may become imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

The Immutable Promise and the Anchor of Hope

13For when God made a promise to Abraham, since HeheHe had no one greater by whom to swear, HeheHe swore by HimselfhimselfHimself, 14saying, “Surely I will bless you abundantly and multiply you greatly.” 15And thus, having patiently endured, he obtained the promise.

16For people swear by the greater, and an oath is for them a confirmation that puts an end to every dispute. 17In the same way, when God desired to show more abundantly to the heirs of the promise the unchangeableness of HishisHis purpose, HeheHe guaranteed it with an oath, 18so that by two unchangeable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled for refuge may have strong encouragement to seize the hope set before us.

19We have this hope as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast, which enters into the inner sanctuary beyond the veil, 20where Jesus has entered as a forerunner on our behalf, having become a high priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.
