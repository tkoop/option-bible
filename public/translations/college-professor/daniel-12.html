The Final Tribulation and the Deliverance of Daniel's People

1In that hour, Michael—the great prince who stands as guardian over the sons of your people—will arise; and there will be a time of distress such as has never occurred since nations first existed until that hour. Yet in that hour your people—every one found inscribed in the book—shall be delivered. 2Multitudes who slumber in the dust of the earth will awaken: some to life everlasting, and others to shame and everlasting contempt. 3The discerning will radiate like the splendoursplendoursplendor of the firmament, and those who lead many to righteousness will shine like the stars for ever and ever. 4As for you, Daniel, conceal these words and seal the book until the time of the end; many will range to and fro, and knowledge will proliferate.

The Vision by the River and the Oath Concerning the End

5Then I, Daniel, looked and beheld two others standing—one on this bank of the river and one on the opposite bank. 6One said to the man clothed in linen, who stood above the waters of the river, “Until when shall the consummation of these wonders be?”

7The man clothed in linen, who stood above the waters of the river, raised his right hand and his left hand toward Heavenheavenheaven and swore by HimhimHim who lives forever that it would be for a time, times, and half a time, and that when the shattering of the power of the holy people had run its course, all these things would reach their consummation.

8I heard, yet I did not comprehend; so I said, “My lord, what will be the latter end of these things?”

The Sealed Prophecy and the Blessed End

9He said, “Proceed on your way, Daniel, for the words are sealed and shut up until the time of the end.” 10Many will be purified, whitened, and refined; yet the wicked will persist in wickedness. None of the wicked will understand, but the discerning will understand. 11From the moment the continual sacrifice is removed and the abomination of desolation is erected, there will be one thousand two hundred ninety days. 12Blessed is the one who waits and attains to the one thousand three hundred thirty-fifth day. 13As for you, proceed to the end; you will rest, and you will stand in your allotted inheritance at the end of the days.
