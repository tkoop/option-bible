Woe to the Land of Whirring Wings

1Woe to the land of whirring wings, which lies beyond the rivers of Cush.

2It sends envoys by sea in vessels of papyrus upon the waters. Go, swift messengers, to a nation tall and smooth-skinned, to a people feared from near and far, a nation mighty and trampling, whose land the rivers divide.

A Signal to All the Earth

3All you inhabitants of the world, you who dwell on the earth, when a banner is raised on the mountains, you will see it; when a trumpet sounds, you will hear it.

The LORD's Quiet Vigil

4For thus the LORD said to me: "I will be still and watch from MymyMy dwelling place, like shimmering heat in the sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest."

5For before the harvest, when the blossom is spent and the flower has become a ripening grape, HeheHe will cut off the shoots with pruning hooks and remove the spreading branches.

6Together the mountains will be left to the birds of prey and to the beasts of the earth; the birds of prey will summer upon it, and all the beasts of the earth will winter upon it.

A Gift Brought to Zion

7At that time a gift will be brought to the LORD of Hosts from a people tall and smooth-skinned, from a people feared from near and far, a nation mighty and trampling, whose land the rivers divide—to Mount Zion, the place of the NamenameName of the LORD of Hosts.
