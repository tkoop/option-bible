A Levite’s Concubine and the Journey to Retrieve Her

1In those days, when there was no king in Israel, a Levite was sojourning in the remote quarters of the hill country of Ephraim, and he took to himself a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah. 2His concubine was sexually unfaithful to him, so she departed from him to her father’s house in Bethlehem of Judah, where she remained for four months. 3Then her husband arose and went after her, to appeal to her affections and bring her back; he brought with him his young servant and a pair of donkeys. When she ushered him into her father’s house, the young woman’s father saw him and rejoiced to meet him. 4Her father—her husband’s father-in-law—detained him, and he stayed with him three days; they ate and drank and lodged there.

5On the fourth day they rose early, and as he prepared to depart, the young woman’s father said to his son-in-law, “Refresh yourself with a morsel of bread, and afterward you may go.” 6So the two of them sat down and ate and drank together, and the young woman’s father said to the man, “Please be willing to stay the night, and let your heart be glad.” 7The man rose to go, but his father-in-law pressed him, so he lodged there again. 8On the fifth day he rose early to depart, and the young woman’s father said, “Refresh yourself, and tarry until the day declines.” So they ate together. 9When the man rose to go with his concubine and his servant, his father-in-law, the young woman’s father, said to him, “Behold, the day is waning toward evening; please stay the night. The day is drawing to a close; lodge here, and let your heart be glad. Tomorrow you shall rise early for your journey and go to your home.”

The Road to Gibeah and the Failure of Hospitality

10But the man would not consent to lodge; he arose and departed, and came as far as opposite Jebus—that is, Jerusalem. He had with him two saddled donkeys, and his concubine accompanied him. 11When they were near Jebus, the day was far spent, and the servant said to his master, “Come, please, let us turn aside into this city of the Jebusites and lodge there.”

12But his master said to him, “We will not turn aside into a city of foreigners that does not belong to the Israelites; we will pass on to Gibeah.” 13He said to his young servant, “Come, let us draw near to one of these places and lodge in Gibeah or in Ramah.” 14So they passed on, and the sun set upon them beside Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin. 15They turned aside there to lodge in Gibeah, and he went in and sat down in the open square of the city, for no one took them into a house to spend the night.

16And behold, an old man was coming from his field work in the evening; he was from the hill country of Ephraim and was sojourning in Gibeah, while the men of the place were Benjaminites. 17He lifted his eyes and saw the travellertravellertraveler in the open square, and the old man said, “Where are you going, and from where have you come?”

18He replied, “We are passing from Bethlehem in Judah to the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim; from there I am, and I went to Bethlehem in Judah. Now I am going to the house of the LORD, and no one has taken me into his house. 19We have both straw and fodder for our donkeys, and bread and wine for me, your maidservant, and the young man with us; we lack nothing.”

20The old man said, “Peace be with you; let all your need rest upon me; only do not spend the night in the street.” 21So he brought him into his house and provided fodder for the donkeys; they washed their feet and ate and drank.

The Outrage in Gibeah and Its Grisly Aftermath

22While they were making merry, behold, the men of the city—worthless scoundrels—surrounded the house and beat upon the door. They said to the old man, the master of the house, “Bring out the man who came into your house, that we may have sexual relations with him.”

23The master of the house went out to them and said, “No, my brothers, do not act so wickedly; since this man has come into my house, do not commit this outrage. 24Behold, I will bring out my virgin daughter and his concubine; you may violate them and do with them as seems best to you, but do not commit this outrage against this man.” 25But the men would not heed him; so he seized his concubine and brought her out to them, and they had sexual relations with her and abused her all night until morning, when, at the break of dawn, they released her.

26As morning approached, the woman came and fell at the door of the man’s house where her master was, and she lay there until daylight. 27In the morning her master rose, opened the doors of the house, and went out to go his way; and behold, the woman, his concubine, was lying at the door of the house with her hands upon the threshold. 28He said to her, “Rise, and let us be going,” but she did not answer. So he took her upon the donkey, and the man went to his home. 29When he reached his house, he took a knife, seized his concubine, and cut her into twelve pieces, limb by limb, and sent them throughout all the territory of Israel. 30Everyone who saw it said, “Such a thing has never occurred nor been seen since Israel came up from the land of Egypt until this day. Consider it, take counsel, and speak.”
