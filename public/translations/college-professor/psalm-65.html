Praise, Vows, and Forgiveness in Zion

1For the choirmaster—a psalm of David, a song. Praise is YouryourYour due in Zion, O God, and to YouyouYou vows will be discharged. 2O YouyouYou who hear prayer, unto YouyouYou all humanity shall come. 3When iniquities prevail against me, YouyouYou make atonement for them. 4How blessed is the one YouyouYou choose and bring near, that he may dwell in YouryourYour courts! We shall be sated with the beneficence of YouryourYour house, YouryourYour holy temple.

The Righteous God of Universal Salvation

5By awe-inspiring acts of righteousness YouyouYou answer us, O God of our salvation, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. 6You establish the mountains by YouryourYour strength, being girded with might. 7You quell the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of the peoples. 8Thus those who dwell at the ends of the earth stand in awe of YouryourYour signs; YouyouYou make the dawn and the sunset shout for joy.

The Creator’s Bounty Upon the Earth

9You attend to the earth and saturate it; YouyouYou enrich it immeasurably; the river of God is brimful of water; YouyouYou prepare their grain, for thus YouyouYou prepare the earth. 10You drench its furrows; YouyouYou level its ridges; YouyouYou soften it with gentle rains; YouyouYou bless its burgeoning. 11You crown the year with YouryourYour bounty, and YouryourYour paths overflow with abundance. 12The wilderness pastures drip with plenty, and the hills gird themselves with rejoicing. 13The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy, they also sing.
