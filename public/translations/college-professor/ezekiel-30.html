A Lamentation over Egypt and Its Allies

1The word of the the LORD came to me: 2“Son of man, prophesy and say: Thus says the Lord the the LORD: Wail—alas for the day! 3For the day is near, the day of the the LORD is near; it will be a day of clouds, a time of doom for the nations. 4A sword will come upon Egypt, and anguish will seize Cush when the slain fall in Egypt, when her wealth is carried away and her foundations are torn down. 5Cush and Put and Lud, all Arabia and Kub, together with the people of the land that is in league with Egypt, will fall by the sword alongside them.”

The Collapse of Egypt and Her Supporters

6“Thus says the the LORD: Those who uphold Egypt will fall, and her proud might will descend; from Migdol to Syene they will fall within her by the sword, declares the Lord the the LORD. 7They will be desolated among desolated lands, and their cities will lie among cities laid waste. 8Then they will know that I am the the LORD, when I set fire in Egypt and all her helpers are broken. 9On that day messengers will go out from MemeMe in ships to terrify unsuspecting Cush, and anguish will seize them on the day of Egypt’s doom, for behold, it is coming.”

Nebuchadnezzar’s Conquest and the Desolation of Egypt

10“Thus says the Lord the the LORD: I will put an end to the multitude of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 11He and his people with him—the most ruthless of nations—will be brought in to destroy the land; they will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain. 12I will dry up the rivers, and I will sell the land into the hand of evildoers; by the hand of foreigners I will make the land desolate, together with all that fills it. I am the the LORD; I have spoken.”

13“Thus says the Lord the the LORD: I will destroy the idols and make the images cease from Memphis; no longer will a prince arise from the land of Egypt, and I will put fear in the land of Egypt. 14I will make Pathros a desolation, set fire in Zoan, and execute judgmentsjudgementsjudgments in Thebes. 15I will pour out MymyMy wrath upon Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the multitude of Thebes. 16I will set fire in Egypt; Sin will writhe in anguish; Thebes will be breached; and Memphis will face adversaries day by day.”

17“The young men of On and Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and their women will go into captivity. 18At Tehaphnehes the day will be darkened when I break there the yoke-bars of Egypt; her proud might will cease within her; a cloud will cover her, and her daughters will go into captivity. 19Thus I will execute judgmentsjudgementsjudgments on Egypt, and they will know that I am the the LORD.”

Pharaoh’s Broken Arm and Babylon’s Empowerment

20In the eleventh year, in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me:

21“Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and behold, it has not been bound up to heal it, nor has a bandage been applied to strengthen it so that it might grasp the sword. 22Therefore thus says the Lord the the LORD: Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I will break his arms—both the strong and the one already broken—and I will make the sword fall from his hand. 23I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries.

24“I will strengthen the arm of the king of Babylon and put MymyMy sword in his hand, and I will break the arms of Pharaoh, so that he will groan before him with the groanings of a mortally wounded man. 25I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, but the arms of Pharaoh will fall; and they will know that I am the the LORD when I put MymyMy sword into the hand of the king of Babylon and he stretches it out against the land of Egypt. 26I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries, and they will know that I am the the LORD.”
