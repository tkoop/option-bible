Sheba's Revolt and David's Return to Jerusalem

1A worthless fellow named Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjamite, happened to be there; he sounded the trumpet and proclaimed, “We have no portion in David, no patrimony in the son of Jesse. Every man to his own home, Israel!” 2So all the men of Israel deserted David and followed Sheba son of Bichri, while the men of Judah remained loyal to their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.

David's Concubines and Amasa's Summons

3When David returned to his house in Jerusalem, the king took the ten concubines whom he had left to keep the house and placed them in a guarded residence; he provided for them, but he did not go in to them. They were confined as though widowed until the day of their death. 4Then the king said to Amasa, “Summon the men of Judah to me within three days, and you be present here.” 5Amasa went to summon Judah, but he tarried beyond the appointed time.

Joab's Pursuit and the Death of Amasa

6David said to Abishai, “Now Sheba son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom did. You take your lord's servants and pursue him, lest he secure fortified cities and elude us.” 7So Joab's men went out after him—the Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the mighty men—and they departed from Jerusalem to pursue Sheba son of Bichri. 8When they reached the great stone in Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Joab was wearing his military tunic, with a sword in its sheath fastened at his waist; as he advanced, the sword slipped from its sheath.

9Joab said to Amasa, “Is it well with you, my brother?” and took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him. 10Amasa was not on guard against the sword in Joab's hand; Joab struck him in the abdomen, spilling his entrails to the ground, and he did not need to strike again. So Amasa died. Then Joab and his brother Abishai pursued Sheba son of Bichri.

11One of Joab's young men stood beside Amasa and cried, “Whoever is for Joab and whoever is for David, follow Joab!” 12Amasa lay wallowing in his blood in the middle of the highway. When the man saw that everyone who came by stopped, he dragged Amasa from the highway into the field and threw a garment over him. 13Once he had been removed from the highway, all the men passed on after Joab to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.

The Siege of Abel and the End of the Revolt

14He passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel of Beth-maachah and to all the Berites, who mustered and followed him as well. 15They came and besieged him in Abel of Beth-maachah, raising a siege ramp against the city until it reached the wall; all the people with Joab were battering the wall to bring it down.

16Then a wise woman called from the city, “Listen! Listen! Please tell Joab, ‘Come here, that I may speak with you.’”

17He approached her, and the woman said, “Are you Joab?”

He answered, “I am.”

She said, “Listen to the words of your servant.”

He said, “I am listening.”

18She said, “In former times people would say, ‘Let them inquire in Abel,’ and so the matter would be settled. 19I am among the peaceful and faithful of Israel. You seek to destroy a city, a mother in Israel. Why would you consume the inheritance of the LORD?”

20Joab answered, “Far be it from me, far be it, that I should consume or destroy!”

21Joab said, “The matter is not so. A man named Sheba son of Bichri from the hill country of Ephraim has raised his hand against the king, against David. Hand over that man alone, and I will withdraw from the city.”

The woman said to Joab, “Behold, his head shall be thrown to you over the wall.”

22Then the woman went to all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it to Joab. He blew the trumpet, and they dispersed from the city, each to his own home. Joab returned to the king in Jerusalem.

23Joab was over all the army of Israel; Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites. 24Adoram was over the forced labourlabourlabor; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder. 25Sheva was scribe; Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 26Ira the Jairite was also priest to David.
