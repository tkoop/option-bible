Oracle Against the Idolatrous Highlands of Israel

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, “2Son of man, fix your gaze upon the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them. 3And say, ‘Hear the word of the Lord, the LORD! Thus says the Lord, the LORD, to the mountains and hills, to the ravines and valleys: Behold, I am bringing the sword against you, and I shall annihilate your high places.’”

The Desolation of Altars, Idols, and Peoples

“4Your altars shall be rendered desolate, your incense altars shattered, and I will cast down your slain before your idols. 5I will lay the corpses of the Israelites before their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars. 6In all your dwelling places the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be desolated, so that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate, your idols broken and destroyed, your incense altars cut down, and your works blotted out. 7The slain shall fall in your midst, and you shall know that I am the LORD.”

The Remnant’s Recognition Among the Nations

“8Yet I will leave a remnant, so that you may have some who escape the sword among the nations when you are dispersed throughout the lands. 9Those of you who escape will remember MemeMe among the nations to which they are carried captive—how I was crushed by their adulterous heart that turned away from MemeMe and by their eyes that lusted after their idols. They will loathe themselves for the evils they have committed and for all their abominations. 10And they shall know that I am the LORD; I did not speak in vain when I declared that I would bring this calamity upon them.”

The Comprehensive Execution of Divine Fury

“11Thus says the Lord, the LORD: Clap your hand and stamp your foot, and say, ‘Alas!’ because of all the vile abominations of the house of Israel, for they shall fall by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence. 12The one who is far off shall perish by pestilence, the one who is near shall fall by the sword, and the one who remains besieged shall die of famine. Thus I will expend MymyMy fury upon them. 13And you shall know that I am the LORD when their slain lie among their idols around their altars—on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, under every green tree, and under every leafy oak—wherever they offered a pleasing aroma to all their idols. 14I will stretch out MymyMy hand against them and render the land desolate and waste, from the wilderness to Diblah, throughout all their habitations. And they shall know that I am the LORD.”
