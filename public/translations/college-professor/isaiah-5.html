The Song of the Beloved’s Vineyard

1Let me now sing to my beloved a song of my beloved concerning HishisHis vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a hill of exceptional fertility. 2He fenced it, cleared it of stones, and planted it with the choicest vine; HeheHe built a watchtower in its midst and hewed a winepress in it. Then HeheHe expected it to yield grapes, but it yielded only sour grapes.

The LORD’s Indictment of His Vineyard

3“Now then, O inhabitants of Jerusalem and men of Judah, judge between MemeMe and MymyMy vineyard. 4What more was there to do for MymyMy vineyard that I did not do? Why, when I looked for it to yield grapes, did it yield only sour grapes? 5And now I will tell you what I am about to do to MymyMy vineyard: I will remove its hedge, and it shall be consumed; I will break down its wall, and it shall be trampled underfoot. 6I will make it a waste; it shall not be pruned or hoed, and briers and thorns shall grow up. I will also command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.”

7For the vineyard of the LORD of Hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are HishisHis pleasant planting. He looked for justice, and behold, bloodshed; for righteousness, and behold, an outcry.

Woes against Greed, Excess, and Injustice

8Woe to those who add house to house and join field to field, until there is no more room, and you are left to dwell alone in the midst of the land.

9In my hearing the LORD of Hosts has sworn: “Surely many houses, great and fine, shall become desolate, without inhabitant. 10For ten acres of vineyard shall yield but a bath, and a homer of seed shall yield but an ephah.”

11Woe to those who rise early in the morning to pursue strong drink, who linger late into the evening as wine inflames them. 12They have lyre and harp, tambourine and flute, and wine at their feasts, but they do not regard the work of the LORD, nor do they consider the work of HishisHis hands.

13Therefore MymyMy people go into exile for lack of knowledge; their honouredhonouredhonored men are famished, and their multitude is parched with thirst. 14Therefore Sheol has enlarged its appetite and opened its mouth beyond measure; down go their glory, their multitude, their pomp, and their revelers. 15So man is humbled, and each one is brought low; the eyes of the haughty are brought low. 16But the LORD of Hosts is exalted in justice, and the HolyholyHoly God shows HimselfhimselfHimself holy in righteousness. 17Then the lambs shall graze as in their pasture, and strangers shall feed among the ruins of the rich.

18Woe to those who draw iniquity with cords of falsehood and sin as with cart ropes, 19who say, “Let HimhimHim make speed; let HimhimHim hasten HishisHis work, that we may see it; let the counsel of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel draw near and come, that we may know it.”

20Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness in place of light and light in place of darkness, who put bitter in place of sweet and sweet in place of bitter. 21Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes and shrewd in their own sight. 22Woe to those who are heroes at drinking wine and valiant men at mixing strong drink, 23who acquit the guilty for a bribe and deprive the innocent of justice.

24Therefore, as a tongue of fire devours the stubble, and as dry grass sinks down in the flame, so their root shall be as rottenness, and their blossom shall go up like dust; because they have rejected the law of the LORD of Hosts and have despised the word of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 25Therefore the anger of the LORD is kindled against HishisHis people, and HeheHe has stretched out HishisHis hand against them and struck them; the mountains quaked, and their corpses were as refuse in the midst of the streets. For all this HishisHis anger is not turned away, and HishisHis hand is stretched out still.

26He will raise a signal for nations afar off, and whistle for them from the ends of the earth; and behold, they will come with speed, swiftly. 27None is weary, none stumbles, none slumbers nor sleeps; not a waistband is loose, nor a sandal strap broken; 28their arrows are sharp, and all their bows are bent; their horses’ hooves seem like flint, and their wheels like a whirlwind. 29Their roaring is like a lion, they roar like young lions; they growl and seize the prey, they carry it off, and there is none to rescue. 30They will growl over it in that day like the growling of the sea. And if one looks to the land, behold, darkness and distress; and the light is darkened by its clouds.
