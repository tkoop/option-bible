The Conquest Assured and the Peril of Self-Righteousness

1Hear, O Israel: today you are crossing the Jordan to enter and dispossess nations greater and mightier than you, with cities great and fortified up to the heavens, 2a people great and tall, the descendants of the Anakim, whom you know, and of whom you have heard it said, ‘Who can withstand the sons of Anak?’ 3Know therefore today that the LORD your God is HeheHe who goes before you as a consuming fire; HeheHe will destroy them and subdue them before you, so that you may drive them out and annihilate them swiftly, just as the LORD has promised you.

4When the LORD your God has thrust them out before you, do not say in your heart, ‘Because of my righteousness the LORD has brought me in to possess this land,’ for it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the LORD is driving them out before you. 5Not by your righteousness or the uprightness of your heart are you entering to possess their land, but because of the wickedness of these nations the LORD your God is driving them out before you, and in order to fulfillfulfilfulfill the word that the LORD swore to your fathers—to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob. 6Know therefore that it is not by your righteousness that the LORD your God is giving you this good land to possess, for you are a stiff-necked people.

The Golden Calf and the Provocation at Horeb

7Remember; do not forget how you provoked the LORD your God to wrath in the wilderness. From the day you came out of the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebellious against the LORD. 8Even at Horeb you provoked the LORD to wrath, and the LORD was incensed with you to destroy you. 9When I ascended the mountain to receive the tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant that the LORD made with you, I remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights; I neither ate bread nor drank water. 10And the LORD gave me the two tablets of stone written with the finger of God, and upon them were all the words that the LORD had spoken with you on the mountain out of the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly. 11And at the end of forty days and forty nights the LORD gave me the two tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant.

12And the LORD said to me, ‘Arise, go down quickly from here, for your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have corrupted themselves. They have turned aside quickly from the way that I commanded them; they have made for themselves a molten image.’ 13Furthermore the LORD spoke to me, saying, ‘I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people. 14Leave MemeMe alone, that I may destroy them and blot out their name from under Heavenheavenheaven; and I will make of you a nation mightier and greater than they.’

15So I turned and descended from the mountain, while the mountain was burning with fire, with the two tablets of the covenant in my two hands. 16And I looked, and behold, you had sinned against the LORD your God; you had made for yourselves a molten calf; you had turned aside quickly from the way that the LORD had commanded you. 17So I seized the two tablets and cast them from my two hands, and I shattered them before your eyes.

18Then I prostrated myself before the LORD forty days and forty nights, as at the first; I neither ate bread nor drank water, because of all your sin that you had committed in doing evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke HimhimHim. 19For I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure with which the LORD was angry against you to destroy you; but the LORD listened to me that time also. 20And the LORD was exceedingly angry with Aaron to destroy him; so I prayed for Aaron also at that time. 21Then I took your sin, the calf that you had made, and burned it with fire and crushed it, grinding it very fine until it was as fine as dust; and I cast its dust into the brook that descended from the mountain.

Further Provocations and Moses’ Intercession

22And at Taberah and at Massah and at Kibroth-hattaavah you provoked the LORD to wrath. 23And when the LORD sent you from Kadesh-barnea, saying, ‘Go up and possess the land that I have given you,’ you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God; you neither believed HimhimHim nor listened to HishisHis voice. 24You have been rebellious against the LORD from the day I knew you.

25So I prostrated myself before the LORD forty days and forty nights, because the LORD had said HeheHe would destroy you.

26And I prayed to the LORD and said, ‘O Lord GOD, do not destroy YouryourYour people and YouryourYour inheritance, whom YouyouYou have redeemed through YouryourYour greatness, whom YouyouYou brought out of Egypt with a mighty hand. 27Remember YouryourYour servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; do not regard the stubbornness of this people, nor their wickedness, nor their sin. 28Otherwise the people of the land from which YouyouYou brought us may say, “Because the LORD was not able to bring them into the land that HeheHe had promised them, and because HeheHe hated them, HeheHe brought them out to slay them in the wilderness.” 29Yet they are YouryourYour people and YouryourYour inheritance, whom YouyouYou brought out by YouryourYour mighty power and outstretched arm.’
