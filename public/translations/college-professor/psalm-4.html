A Davidic Psalm of Evening Confidence

1For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments—a Psalm of David. Answer me when I call, O God of my righteousness; YouyouYou have granted me relief in adversity. Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

2How long, O sons of men, will you transmute my honourhonourhonor into disgrace? How long will you cleave to emptiness and pursue falsehood? Selah. 3But know that the LORD has set apart the godly for HimselfhimselfHimself; the LORD hears when I call to HimhimHim. 4Tremble, yet sin not; commune within your heart upon your bed, and be silent. Selah. 5Offer sacrifices of righteousness, and trust in the LORD.

6Many are saying, "Who will show us good? Let the light of YouryourYour face shine upon us, O the LORD."

7You have put gladness in my heart, more than in the season when their grain and new wine abound. 8In peace I will both lie down and sleep, for YouyouYou alone, O the LORD, make me dwell in safety.
