A Pilgrimage Supplication for Divine Mercy amid Scorn

1A song of ascents: I elevate my gaze unto YouyouYou, enthroned in the heavens. 2Behold, as the eyes of servants are fixed upon the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maidservant upon the hand of her mistress, so our eyes are fixed upon the LORD our God, until HeheHe grants us grace.

3Be gracious to us, O the LORD, be gracious to us, for we are sated to overflowing with contempt. 4Our soul is sated to overflowing with the derision of the complacent, with the scorn of the arrogant.
