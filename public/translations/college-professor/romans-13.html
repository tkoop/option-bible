Submission to Governing Authorities

1Every soul must submit to the governing authorities, for no authority exists save that which derives from God, and those authorities that do exist stand ordained by God. 2Consequently, whoever arrays himself against authority opposes the divine ordinance, and those who set themselves in opposition shall bring judgmentjudgementjudgment upon themselves.

3For rulers are not a terror to good conduct but to evil. Do you desire to be free from fear of authority? Practice the good, and you shall garner praise from it. 4For he is God’s minister unto your good; yet if you perpetrate evil, fear, for he does not wield the sword without cause, since he is God’s minister, an avenger inflicting wrath upon the one who practiseswho practiseswho practices evil.
