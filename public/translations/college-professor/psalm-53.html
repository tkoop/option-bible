The Folly of the Godless and the Hope of Israel

1The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They have become corrupt, and they commit abominable iniquity; there is no one who does good.

2God gazes down from Heavenheavenheaven upon the children of men, to discern whether any are wise, any who seek God. 3All have turned aside; together they have become depraved; there is none who does good, not even one.

4Do the workers of iniquity possess no understanding, they who devour my people as readily as bread and do not call upon God?

5There they were overwhelmed with dread, though no dread had been; for God has scattered the bones of your besiegers; you have put them to shame, for God has rejected them.

6Oh, that salvation for Israel would come out of Zion! When God restores the fortunes of HishisHis people, Jacob shall rejoice and Israel shall be glad.
