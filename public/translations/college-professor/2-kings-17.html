The Reign of Hoshea and the Conquest of Samaria

1In the twelfth year of the reign of Ahaz over Judah, Hoshea son of Elah ascended the throne of Israel in Samaria, and his reign endured nine years. 2He perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD, though not to the same degree as the kings of Israel who had preceded him. 3Shalmaneser, king of Assyria, advanced against him; Hoshea became his vassal and rendered tribute. 4Yet the king of Assyria detected a conspiracy in Hoshea, who had dispatched envoys to So, king of Egypt, and who had ceased to remit the annual tribute. Accordingly, the king of Assyria seized him and incarcerated him in a prison house. 5Then the king of Assyria overran the entire country, ascended to Samaria, and besieged it for three years. 6In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported Israel to Assyria, resettling them in Halah, in Gozan by the Habor River, and in the cities of the Medes.

Israel's Apostasy and the Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment of Exile

7All this calamity befell Israel because the Israelites had sinned against the LORD their God, who had liberated them from the land of Egypt and from the domination of Pharaoh, king of Egypt; moreover, they had venerated other gods. 8They had conformed to the statutes of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed before the Israelites, and to the innovations introduced by the kings of Israel. 9The Israelites surreptitiously perpetrated deeds contrary to the LORD their God; from watchtower to fortified city, they erected high places in every one of their cities. 10They raised up sacred pillars and Asherah poles upon every lofty hill and beneath every luxuriant tree. 11They burned incense at every high place, emulating the nations that the LORD had expelled before them, and they committed iniquitous acts that provoked the LORD. 12They served idols, although the LORD had admonished them, “You shall not do this thing.”

13Yet the LORD had admonished Israel and Judah through all HishisHis prophets and seers, declaring, “Turn back from your evil ways, and observe MymyMy commandments and statutes in accordance with the entire LawlawLaw that I commanded your fathers and transmitted to you through MymyMy servants the prophets.” 14Nevertheless, they refused to hearken, and they stiffened their necks like their fathers, who had not placed faith in the LORD their God. 15They spurned HishisHis statutes and the covenant HeheHe had ratified with their fathers, along with the decrees HeheHe had given them; they pursued vain idols and thereby became vain themselves, imitating the surrounding nations, although the LORD had commanded them not to emulate them. 16They abandoned all the commandments of the LORD their God and fashioned for themselves two calves of molten metal; they fabricated an Asherah, prostrated themselves before the entire host of Heavenheavenheaven, and served Baal. 17They consigned their sons and daughters to the fire, practisedpractisedpracticed divination and sorcery, and sold themselves to perpetrate evil in the sight of the LORD, thereby provoking HishisHis wrath.

18Therefore the LORD was exceedingly incensed with Israel and banished them from HishisHis presence; only the tribe of Judah remained. 19Yet even Judah did not observe the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the customs that Israel had instituted. 20Consequently, the LORD rejected the whole line of Israel, afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of plunderers, until HeheHe had cast them out of HishisHis presence. 21For when HeheHe had rentletrent Israel from the house of David, they elevated Jeroboam son of Nebat to kingship; Jeroboam diverted Israel from following the LORD and induced them to commit a grave sin. 22The Israelites persisted in all the sins that Jeroboam had committed; they did not desist from them. 23until the LORD finally removed Israel from HishisHis presence, precisely as HeheHe had foretold through all HishisHis servants the prophets. Thus Israel was exiled from its native soil to Assyria, where it remains to this day.

The Assyrian Resettlement and the Emergence of Syncretistic Worship

24Thereafter the king of Assyria imported people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the cities of Samaria in lieu of the Israelites; they took possession of Samaria and dwelt in its cities. 25At the inception of their residence, they did not revere the LORD, so the LORD dispatched lions among them, and the beasts slew some of them.

26So they addressed the king of Assyria, saying, “The people you have resettled in the cities of Samaria are ignorant of the law of the god of the land; hence HeheHe has sent lions among them, for they are ignorant of the law of the god of the land.”

27Then the king of Assyria issued a command: “Send one of the priests you carried off from there; let him go and reside there, and instruct them in the law of the god of the land.”

28So one of the priests who had been carried away from Samaria came and took up residence in Bethel, and he instructed them how to revere the LORD. 29Nevertheless, every nation continued to fabricate its own gods and installed them in the shrines that the Samaritans had constructed on the high places, each nation in the cities where it dwelt. 30The Babylonians made Succoth-benoth, the Cuthites made Nergal, and the Hamathites made Ashima. 31The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, while the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim. 32Thus they revered the LORD, yet they also appointed priests from their own ranks to officiate for them in the shrines of the high places. 33They revered the LORD, but they also served their own gods in accordance with the customs of the nations from which they had been deported. 34To this day they persist in their former practices: they do not revere the LORD, nor do they observe the statutes, ordinances, laws, or commandments that the LORD gave to the descendants of Jacob, whom HeheHe designated Israel.

35When the LORD covenanted with them, HeheHe charged them: “Do not revere other gods, nor bow down to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them. 36Rather, revere the LORD, who brought you up from the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm; bow down to HimhimHim and sacrifice to HimhimHim. 37And you must meticulously observe the statutes, ordinances, laws, and commandments that HeheHe inscribed for you; do not revere other gods. 38Do not forget the covenant that I have made with you, and do not revere other gods. 39Rather, revere the LORD your God, and HeheHe will rescue you from the hand of all your enemies.” 40But they did not hearken; instead, they persisted in their ancestral customs. 41So these nations revered the LORD, yet they also served their carved images; to this day, their children and grandchildren continue to act as their fathers did.
