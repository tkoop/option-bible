4Their appearance is like that of horses, and like cavalry they charge. 5Like the rumbling of chariots they leap upon the mountaintops, like the crackling of a fire consuming stubble, like a mighty army drawn up for battle. 6Before them peoples writhe in anguish, and every countenance grows pale.

7They charge like warriors; they scale walls like soldiers; each advances along his own course, and they do not break ranks. 8They do not crowd one another; each proceeds along his own path; they burst through the defenses without being halted. 9They surge into the city; they run along the walls; they climb into the houses; they enter through the windows like a thief.

10Before them the earth quakes and the heavens tremble; the sun and moon grow dark, and the stars withdraw their light. 11The LORD gives voice before HishisHis army, for HishisHis camp is exceedingly great and mighty in executing HishisHis word.
