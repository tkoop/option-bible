Saul’s Anointing and the Foretold Signs

1Samuel took a flask of oil, poured it upon Saul’s head, kissed him, and said, “Has not the LORD anointed you ruler over HishisHis inheritance?”

2“When you leave me today, you will meet two men near Rachel’s tomb at Zelzah in the territory of Benjamin. They will say to you, ‘The donkeys you set out to find have been found. And now your father has ceased to concern himself with the donkeys and is anxious about you, asking, “What shall I do for my son?”’ 3Then, as you continue on from there, you will come to the oak of Tabor, where three men ascending to God at Bethel will meet you. One will bear three young goats, another three loaves of bread, and another a skin of wine. 4They will greet you and give you two loaves of bread, which you are to receive from their hands.”

5“Afterward you will come to Gibeah of God, where the Philistine garrison is stationed. As you enter the city, you will meet a band of prophets descending from the high place, preceded by harp, tambourine, flute, and lyre, and they will be prophesying. 6Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD will rush upon you, and you will prophesy with them and be transformed into a different man. 7When these signs have come to pass, do whatever your hand finds to do, for God is with you.”

8“Then go down before me to Gilgal. I will come down to you to offer burnt offerings and to sacrifice peace offerings. Wait seven days until I come to you and disclose what you are to do.”

9As Saul turned to leave Samuel, God altered his heart, and all these signs came to pass that very day.

10When Saul and his servant arrived at Gibeah, a band of prophets met him. The SpiritspiritSpirit of God rushed upon him, and he prophesied among them.

11Everyone who had previously known him saw him prophesying with the prophets, and they asked one another, “What has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?”

12A man from there replied, “Who is their father?” Hence the saying, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”

13When Saul had finished prophesying, he went to the high place.

Saul’s Return and His Uncle’s Inquiry

14Saul’s uncle asked him and his servant, “Where did you go?”

“To look for the donkeys,” Saul replied. “When we saw that they could not be found, we went to Samuel.”

15His uncle said, “Please tell me what Samuel said to you.”

16Saul replied, “He told us that the donkeys had been found.” But he did not tell his uncle what Samuel had said concerning the kingship.

Israel Convenes at Mizpah for the Selection of a King

17Samuel summoned the people to the LORD at Mizpah.

18He said to the Israelites, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘I brought Israel up out of Egypt, and I delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all the kingdoms that oppressed you.’ 19‘But you have rejected your God, who saves you from all your calamities and your distresses. You have said to HimhimHim, “No, appoint a king over us.” Now present yourselves before the LORD by your tribes and by your clans.’”

20Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin was selected by lot. 21Then he brought the tribe of Benjamin near by its clans, and the clan of the Matrites was selected. Saul son of Kish was then selected. But when they sought him, he could not be found.

22So they inquired of the LORD again, “Has the man come here yet?”

And the LORD said, “Behold, he is hiding among the supplies.”

23They ran and brought him out from there. When he stood among the people, he was a head taller than any of them from the shoulders upward.

24Then Samuel said to all the people, “Do you see the man the LORD has chosen? There is no one like him among all the people.”

And all the people shouted, “Long live the king!”

The Establishment of Saul’s Kingship and Its Early Critics

25Samuel explained to the people the rights and duties of the kingship. He wrote them in a book and placed it before the LORD. Then Samuel dismissed all the people, each to his own home.

26Saul went to his home in Gibeah, and valiant men whose hearts God had touched accompanied him.

27But some worthless fellows said, “How can this man save us?” They despised him and brought him no gift. But Saul remained silent.
