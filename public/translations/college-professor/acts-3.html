The Lame Man's Healing at the Beautiful Gate

1Peter and John were ascending to the temple at the hour of prayer, namely the ninth hour. 2A certain man, lame from his mother's womb, was being carried to the gate of the temple called the Beautiful GategateGate; he was deposited there each day so that he might solicit alms from those entering the sanctuary. 3Perceiving Peter and John about to enter the temple, he entreated them for an alms.

4Peter, fixing his gaze upon him together with John, said, “Fix your eyes upon us.”

5He fastened his gaze upon them, anticipating some benefaction from their hands.

6Peter said, “Silver and gold are not at my disposal; nevertheless, that which I do possess I give to you: in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, arise and walk.”

7Grasping him by the right hand, Peter lifted him up, and forthwith his feet and ankles were fortified. 8He sprang to his feet, stood firm, and began to walk; then he accompanied them into the temple, proceeding with walking and leaping and praising God.

9All the populace beheld him ambulating and glorifying God. 10They recognizedrecogniserecognized him as the very man who had been wont to sit begging at the Beautiful GategateGate, and they were suffused with astonishment and ecstasy at the occurrence befalling him. 11While he maintained his hold upon Peter and John, the entire crowd, convulsed with amazement, converged upon them in the portico denominated Solomon's.
