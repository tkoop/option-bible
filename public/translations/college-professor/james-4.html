The Origin of Strife within the Community

1Whence arise wars, and whence quarrels among you? Do they not spring from the very passions that wage war within your members? 2You covet and yet do not possess; you kill and envy, yet cannot attain; you contend and wage war. You have not, because you do not ask. 3You ask and receive nothing, since you ask amiss, intending to squander it upon your pleasures.

Worldliness, Enmity, and the Greater Grace

4You adulteresses! Do you not know that friendship with the world constitutes enmity toward God? Consequently, whoever resolves to be a friend of the world constitutes himself an enemy of God. 5Or do you suppose that ScripturescriptureScripture speaks in vain when it declares that the SpiritspiritSpirit, whom HeheHe caused to dwell within us, yearns with envy? 6But HeheHe gives greater grace; therefore it says, “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

Submission to God, Cleansing, and Humility

7Submit yourselves, then, to God; resist the devil, and he will flee from you. 8Draw near to God, and HeheHe will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded. 9Lament, mourn, and weep; let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to dejection. 10Humble yourselves before the Lord, and HeheHe will exalt you.

Judging One's NeighbourNeighbourNeighbor and the Folly of Boasting

11Brothers, do not speak evil against one another. Whoever speaks against a brother or judges his brother speaks against the law and judges the law; and if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge. 12There is one LawgiverlawgiverLawgiver and JudgejudgeJudge, the OneoneOne able to save and to destroy; but who are you to judge your neighbourneighbourneighbor?

13Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go to this or that city, spend a year there, trade, and make a profit.” 14You do not even know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes. 15Instead, you ought to say, “If the Lord is willing, we will live and do this or that.” 16As it is, you boast in your arrogance; all such boasting is evil. 17Therefore, to one who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.
