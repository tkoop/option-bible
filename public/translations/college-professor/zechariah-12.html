The Oracle Concerning Israel and Its Creator

1An oracle: the word of the the LORD concerning Israel. Thus declares the the LORD, who distends the heavens, establishes the earth’s foundations, and fashions the human spirit within a person.

Jerusalem as a Chalice of Stupefying JudgmentJudgementJudgment

2“Behold, I shall render Jerusalem a chalice of stupefying judgmentjudgementjudgment to all the peoples encircling it; moreover, Judah shall be caught up in the siege against Jerusalem. 3On that day I shall make Jerusalem an immovable boulder for all the peoples; every nation that attempts to lift it shall be grievously injured, and all the nations of the earth shall assemble against it. 4On that day,” declares the the LORD, “I shall strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness; I shall keep watch over the house of Judah, yet I shall blind all the horses of the nations.”

The Clans of Judah and the DefenceDefenceDefense of Jerusalem

5Then the leaders of Judah will say in their hearts, “The inhabitants of Jerusalem are my strength through the the LORD of Hosts, their God.”

6“On that day I shall make the leaders of Judah like a brazier among timber and like a flaming torch among sheaves; they shall devour all the surrounding peoples on the right and on the left, while Jerusalem shall be resettled securely in her own place. 7The the LORD will first deliver the tents of Judah, so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of Jerusalem’s inhabitants may not be magnified above that of Judah. 8On that day the the LORD will defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the most feeble among them shall be like David, and the house of David shall be like God, like the Angel of the the LORD going before them. 9On that day I shall set MyselfmyselfMyself to destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem.”

The Spirit of Grace and the Great Mourning

10“I shall pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and supplication, and they shall look upon MemeMe, the OneoneOne whom they have pierced. They shall mourn for HimhimHim as one mourns for an only child, and they shall grieve bitterly for HimhimHim as one grieves for a firstborn.”

11On that day the mourning in Jerusalem shall be as great as the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo. 12The land shall mourn, family by family, apart: the family of the house of David apart, with their wives apart; the family of the house of Nathan apart, with their wives apart; 13the family of the house of Levi apart, with their wives apart; the family of the Shimeites apart, with their wives apart; 14and all the remaining families, each with its wives apart.
