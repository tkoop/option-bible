The Vision of the Man with the Measuring Line

1I raised my eyes and beheld a man with a measuring line in his hand. 2"Where are you going?" I asked. "To measure Jerusalem," he replied, "to see how wide and how long it is." 3Then the angel who had been speaking with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him.

The Promise of an Unwalled Jerusalem and Divine Protection

4The other angel said, "Run, tell that young man, 'Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the multitude of people and livestock in it.'" 5"And I will be a wall of fire around her," declares the the LORD, "and I will be the glory in her midst."
