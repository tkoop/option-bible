Discerning the Spirits in a World of False Prophets

1Beloved, do not accord credence to every spirit; rather, subject the spirits to rigorous discernment, ascertaining whether they emanate from God, for a multitude of false prophets have gone forth into the world. 2By this you may recognizerecogniserecognize the SpiritspiritSpirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh is from God; 3yet every spirit that withholds confession of Jesus is not from God. This, moreover, is the spirit of the antichrist, concerning which you have heard that it is impending and is even now operative within the world. 4You are from God, little children, and you have overcome them, because greater is HeheHe who is in you than he who is in the world. 5They are from the world; therefore they speak as the world speaks, and the world listens to them. 6We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us, whereas whoever is not from God does not listen to us. By this we discern the SpiritspiritSpirit of truth from the spirit of error.

Love as the Ontological Signature of God

7Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 8The one who does not love does not know God, because God is love. 9In this the love of God was manifested among us: God sent HishisHis only begotten SonsonSon into the world, so that we might live through HimhimHim. 10In this is love—not that we have loved God, but that HeheHe loved us and sent HishisHis SonsonSon as the propitiation for our sins. 11Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another. 12No one has ever seen God; if we love one another, God abides in us, and HishisHis love is perfected in us.

Abiding, Confidence, and the Expulsion of Fear

13By this we know that we abide in HimhimHim and HeheHe in us: He has given us of HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit. 14And we have seen and testify that the FatherfatherFather has sent the SonsonSon as SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior of the world. 15Whoever confesses that Jesus is the SonsonSon of God—God abides in him, and he in God. 16We have come to know and have believed the love that God has in us. God is love, and the one who abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him. 17In this, love is perfected among us, so that we may have confidence in the day of judgmentjudgementjudgment, because as HeheHe is, so also are we in this world. 18There is no fear in love; rather, perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves punishment, and the one who fears has not been perfected in love. 19We love because HeheHe first loved us.

20If anyone says, “I love God,” yet hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen cannot love God whom he has not seen. 21And we have this commandment from HimhimHim: that the one who loves God should love his brother as well.
