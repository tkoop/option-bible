The Advantage of the Jew and the Faithfulness of God

1What, then, is the distinctive advantage of the Jew, or what utility does circumcision confer? 2Much in every way. For first and foremost, they were entrusted with the oracles of God. 3What if some were unfaithful? Does their unfaithfulness nullify the faithfulness of God? 4By no means! Let God be true, and every human being a liar, as it is written: “So that YouyouYou may be justified in YouryourYour words and prevail when YouyouYou are judged.” 5But if our unrighteousness commends God’s righteousness, what shall we say? Is God unjust in inflicting wrath? I speak in merely human terms. 6By no means! For how then could God judge the world? 7But if the truth of God has abounded to HishisHis glory through my lie, why am I still judged as a sinner? 8And why not say—as some slanderously charge us with saying—“Let us do evil that good may come.” Their condemnation is just.

The Universal Reign of Sin

9What then? Are we any better off? Not at all! For we have already charged that both Jews and Greeks are all under sin. 10As it is written: “There is no one righteous, not even one; 11there is no one who understands; there is no one who seeks God. 12All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; there is no one who does good, not even one.

“13Their throats are open graves; with their tongues they practice deceit. The venom of asps is under their lips. 14Their mouths are full of cursing and bitterness.

“15Their feet are swift to shed blood; 16ruin and misery mark their ways; 17and the way of peace they have not known. 18There is no fear of God before their eyes.”

19Now we know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God. 20Therefore no one will be justified in HishisHis sight by works of the law, for through the law comes knowledge of sin.

Righteousness Revealed Apart from the Law

21But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been manifested, being attested by the LawlawLaw and the ProphetsprophetsProphets, 22even the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all who believe. For there is no distinction, 23for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, 24and are justified freely by HishisHis grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus,

25whom God presented as an atoning sacrifice through faith in HishisHis blood, to demonstrate HishisHis righteousness, because in HishisHis forbearance HeheHe had passed over the sins committed beforehand, 26to demonstrate HishisHis righteousness at the present time, so that HeheHe might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus.

Boasting Excluded and the Law Upheld

27Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. By what law? By that of works? No, but by the law of faith. 28For we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law. 29Is God the God of Jews only? Is HeheHe not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too, 30since there is one God, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through that same faith. 31Do we then nullify the law by faith? Not at all! Rather, we uphold the law.
