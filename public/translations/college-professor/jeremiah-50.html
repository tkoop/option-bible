Oracle Against Babylon: The Fall of Her Idols and the Return of Israel

1The word that the LORD spoke by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, was this: 2“Declare among the nations and make it heard; lift up a standard and proclaim it without concealment: say, ‘Babylon is captured, Bel is put to shame, Merodach is dismayed; her idols are dismayed, her images are shamed.’ 3For a nation from the north has come up against her; it shall reduce her land to desolation, so that no one dwells in it; from man to beast they have fled away.”

4“In those days and at that time,” declares the LORD, “the children of Israel and the children of Judah shall come together, weeping as they go, and seeking the LORD their God. 5They shall ask the way to Zion, with their faces turned thitherward, saying, ‘Come, let us join ourselves to the LORD in an everlasting covenant that shall not be forgotten.’”

6“My people were lost sheep; their shepherds led them astray, turning them away upon the mountains; from mountain to hill they wandered, and they forgot their resting place. 7All who found them devoured them, and their adversaries said, ‘We are not guilty, for they have sinned against the LORD—the righteous habitation, the hope of their fathers.’”

The Summons to Flee and the Mustering of the Nations

8“Flee from the midst of Babylon, and go forth out of the land of the Chaldeans, and take the lead, like he-goats before the flock. 9For behold, I am raising up and bringing against Babylon a great assembly of nations from the north country; they shall array themselves against her, and from there she shall be taken. Their arrows shall be like those of a skilled warrior, and none shall return empty. 10Chaldea shall be plundered, and all who plunder her shall be sated,” declares the LORD.

11“Because you rejoiced, because you exulted, you plunderers of MymyMy heritage, because you frisked like a heifer treading grain and neighed like stallions, 12your mother shall be greatly ashamed; she who bore you shall be confounded. Behold, the last of the nations shall she be—a wilderness, a parched land, and a desert. 13Because of the wrath of the LORD she shall not be inhabited, but she shall be wholly desolate; everyone who passes by Babylon shall be appalled and hiss at all her wounds.”

14“Array yourselves against Babylon round about, all you who bend the bow; shoot at her, and spare no arrows, for she has sinned against the LORD. 15Shout against her round about; she has surrendered; her bulwarks have fallen, her walls are demolished; for it is the vengeance of the LORD. Take vengeance on her; as she has done, do to her. 16Cut off the sower from Babylon, and the handler of the sickle at harvest time; because of the sword of the oppressor, each shall turn to his own people, and each shall flee to his own land.”

Israel Restored and Babylon Reckoned

17“Israel is a scattered flock; the lions have driven him away. First the king of Assyria devoured him, and this last—Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon—has broken his bones. 18Therefore thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will punish the king of Babylon and his land, as I punished the king of Assyria. 19I will restore Israel to his pasture; he shall graze on Carmel and Bashan, and on Mount Ephraim and Gilead his soul shall be satisfied. 20In those days and at that time, declares the LORD, the iniquity of Israel shall be sought and shall not be found, and the sins of Judah shall not be discovered; for I will pardon those whom I leave as a remnant.”

21“Go up against the land of Merathaim, and against the inhabitants of Pekod; waste and utterly destroy them, says the LORD, and do according to all that I have commanded you. 22A sound of battle is in the land, and great destruction. 23How the hammer of the whole EarthEarthearth is cut down and broken! How Babylon has become a desolation among the nations! 24I have laid a snare for you, O Babylon, and you are taken, though you did not know it; you are found and seized, because you have contended with the LORD.”

25The LORD has opened HishisHis armouryarmouryarmory and brought forth the weapons of HishisHis indignation; for the Lord GOD of Hosts has a work to do in the land of the Chaldeans. 26“Come against her from the farthest border; open her granaries; pile her up like heaps, and devote her to destruction; let no remnant remain. 27Slay all her bulls; let them go down to slaughter. Woe to them! for their day has come, the time of their punishment.” 28A voice of fugitives and refugees from the land of Babylon declares in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God, the vengeance for HishisHis temple.

The Sword of the LORD and the Everlasting Desolation

29“Summon many against Babylon, all who bend the bow; encamp against her round about; let there be no escape; repay her according to her work; do to her according to all that she has done; for she has acted arrogantly against the LORD, against the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 30Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets, and all her warriors shall be silenced in that day,” declares the LORD. 31“Behold, I am against you, O arrogant one,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts, “for your day has come, the time of your reckoning. 32The arrogant one shall stumble and fall, and none shall raise him; I will kindle fire in his cities, and it shall consume all around him.”

33Thus says the LORD of Hosts: “The children of Israel and the children of Judah are oppressed together; all their captors hold them fast; they refuse to release them. 34Their Redeemer is strong; the LORD of Hosts is HishisHis name. He will surely contend for their cause, to bring rest to the land and to agitate the inhabitants of Babylon.”

35“A sword is upon the Chaldeans,” declares the LORD, “and upon the inhabitants of Babylon, and upon her princes, and upon her wise men. 36A sword is upon the boasters, and they shall become fools; a sword is upon her mighty men, and they shall be dismayed. 37A sword is upon their horses, and upon their chariots, and upon all the foreign folk in her midst, and they shall lose all courage; a sword is upon her treasures, and they shall be robbed. 38A drought is upon her waters, and they shall dry up; for it is a land of graven images, and they are maddened by their idols.”

39“Therefore the desert creatures and jackals shall dwell there, and ostriches shall inhabit it; it shall never again be inhabited, nor dwelt in from generation to generation. 40As when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, declares the LORD, no man shall dwell there, nor shall any son of man sojourn in it.”

41“Behold, a people comes from the north, a great nation and many kings stirred up from the ends of the earth. 42They shall lay hold on bow and spear; they are cruel and will show no mercy; their voice roars like the sea; they ride on horses, arrayed like a man for war, against you, O daughter of Babylon. 43The king of Babylon has heard the report concerning them, and his hands have grown feeble; distress has seized him, pangs as of a woman in labourlabourlabor.”

44“Behold, like a lion coming up from the thickets of the Jordan against the strong fold, for I will suddenly make them run away from her; and who is the chosen one whom I may appoint over her? For who is like MemeMe? and who will summon MemeMe? and who is this shepherd that shall stand before MemeMe? 45Therefore hear the counsel that the LORD has devised against Babylon, and HishisHis thoughts that HeheHe has thought against the land of the Chaldeans: Surely the little ones of the flock shall drag them away; surely HeheHe shall make their dwelling desolate over them. 46At the sound of the capture of Babylon the earth shall tremble, and a cry shall be heard among the nations.”
