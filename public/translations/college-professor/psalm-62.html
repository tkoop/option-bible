Psalm 62: Quiet Confidence in God Alone

1My soul waits in silence for God alone; from HimhimHim comes my salvation. 2He alone is my RockrockRock and my salvation, my stronghold; I shall not be greatly shaken. 3How long will all of you assail a single man, intent on murdering him—like a wall that bulges outward or a fence that totters toward collapse? 4They conspire only to thrust him down from his exalted station; they take delight in falsehood, blessing with their mouths while cursing in their hearts. Selah.

5Rest in God alone, O my soul, for my hope is from HimhimHim. 6He alone is my RockrockRock and my salvation, my stronghold; I shall not be shaken. 7My salvation and my honourhonourhonor rest upon God; my mighty RockrockRock, my refuge, is in God.

8Trust in HimhimHim at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before HimhimHim. God is our refuge. Selah.
