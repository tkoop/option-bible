Indictment of Samaria's Indulgent Matrons

1Hear this word, you indulgent matrons of Bashan on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the poor and crush the needy, who say to their lords, “Bring, and let us drink.”

The Oath of Exile and the Reversal of Fortune

2The Lord, the the LORD, has sworn by HishisHis holiness: “Surely the days are coming upon you when they will take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks. 3You will go out through the breaches, each woman straight before her, and you will be cast toward Harmon,” declares the the LORD.

Satirical Summons to Idolatrous Worship

4“Come to Bethel and transgress; to Gilgal multiply transgression; bring your sacrifices in the morning, your tithes every three days. 5Burn a thank offering of leaven, and proclaim freewill offerings—announce them! For this is what you love, O children of Israel,” declares the Lord, the the LORD.

Divine Chastisement and the Final Summons

6“I also gave you famine in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places; yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the the LORD. 7“I also withheld rain from you when three months yet remained before the harvest; I caused rain to fall on one city and withheld it from another; one field was rained upon, while the field on which it did not rain withered,” declares the the LORD.

8“So two or three cities wandered to another city to drink water, but were not satisfied; yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the the LORD. 9“I struck you with blight and mildew; the locust devoured the abundance of your gardens and vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees; yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the the LORD.

10“I sent among you a pestilence after the manner of Egypt; I killed your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses; I made the stench of your camps rise in your nostrils; yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the the LORD. 11“I overturned you as God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a brand plucked from the burning; yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the the LORD.

12“Therefore thus I will do to you, O Israel; and because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel! 13For behold, HeheHe who forms the mountains and creates the wind, who declares to man what is his thought, who makes the morning darkness, and treads upon the high places of the earth—the the LORD, the God of hosts, is HishisHis name.”
