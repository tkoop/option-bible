Israel's Encampment in Moab and Balak's Alarm

1The Israelites journeyed onward and encamped on the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan, opposite Jericho. 2Balak son of Zippor beheld all that Israel had wrought against the Amorites. 3Moab was seized with profound dread of the people, for they were numerous, and Moab was sick with anxiety on account of the Israelites. 4Moab said to the elders of Midian, "This multitude will now devour all that surrounds us, as an ox consumes the grass of the field." At that time Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab.

Balak's Summons to Balaam

5Balak dispatched messengers to Balaam son of Beor, at Pethor beside the River, in the land of his people, to summon him with these words: "Behold, a people has emerged from Egypt; it covers the face of the earth and is encamped opposite me. 6Come, I pray, and curse this people for me, for they are too mighty for me. Perhaps I may then be able to smite them and expel them from the land, for I know that whomever you bless is blessed, and whomever you curse is accursed."

7So the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian set out, bearing fees for divination, and came to Balaam and delivered Balak's message to him.

8Balaam said to them, "Remain here tonight, and I shall return to you with whatever answer the LORD gives me." So the princes of Moab stayed with Balaam.

9Then God came to Balaam and asked, "Who are these men with you?"

10Balaam said to God, "Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent to me, saying, 'Behold, the people that has come out of Egypt covers the face of the earth. 11Come now, curse them for me; perhaps I may be able to fight against them and drive them out.'"

12God said to Balaam, "YouyouYou shall not go with them; you shall not curse the people, for they are blessed."

13Balaam rose in the morning and said to Balak's princes, "Go back to your own land, for the LORD has refused to let me go with you."

14So the princes of Moab rose, came to Balak, and said, "Balaam refuses to come with us."

15Balak sent again, dispatching princes more numerous and more distinguished than the former. 16They came to Balaam and said, "Thus says Balak son of Zippor: 'Please do not let anything prevent you from coming to me. 17For I will honourhonourhonor you greatly, and whatever you say to me I will do; come, therefore, and curse this people for me.'"

18Balaam replied to Balak's servants, "Even if Balak were to give me his house filled with silver and gold, I could not transgress the word of the LORD my God, by doing anything, small or great. 19Now, please remain here tonight as well, so that I may learn what further the LORD will say to me."

20God came to Balaam by night and said to him, "If the men have come to summon you, arise and go with them; yet you shall do only what I speak to you."

21Balaam rose in the morning, saddled his donkey, and went with the princes of Moab.

The Angel, the Donkey, and Balaam's Eyes Opened

22But God's anger was kindled because he went, and the Angel of the LORD took HishisHis stand in the road as an adversary against him. Balaam was riding his donkey, with his two servants beside him. 23The donkey saw the Angel of the LORD standing in the road with HishisHis drawn sword in HishisHis hand, and she turned aside from the road and went into the field. Balaam struck the donkey to turn her back onto the road.

24Then the Angel of the LORD stood in a narrow path between the vineyards, with a wall on either side. 25When the donkey saw the Angel of the LORD, she pressed against the wall and crushed Balaam's foot against it, so he struck her again.

26The Angel of the LORD went further and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to turn, either to the right or to the left. 27When the donkey saw the Angel of the LORD, she lay down under Balaam; Balaam's anger burned, and he struck the donkey with his staff.

28Then the LORD opened the donkey's mouth, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you, that you have struck me these three times?"

29Balaam answered the donkey, "Because you have made a fool of me! If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you on the spot."

30The donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey, the one you have ridden all your life until today? Have I ever treated you this way before?"

Balaam said, "No."

31Then the LORD opened Balaam's eyes, and he saw the Angel of the LORD standing in the road with HishisHis sword drawn. Balaam bowed down and fell on his face.

32The Angel of the LORD said to him, "Why have you struck your donkey these three times? Behold, I have come out to oppose you, because your path is crooked before me. 33The donkey saw me and turned aside from me these three times. If she had not turned aside, I would have killed you and spared her."

34Balaam said to the Angel of the LORD, "I have sinned, for I did not know that you were standing in the road against me. Now, if it is displeasing in your sight, I will turn back."

35The Angel of the LORD said to Balaam, "Go with the men, but speak only the word that I tell you." So Balaam went with Balak's princes.

Balaam's Arrival in Moab

36When Balak heard that Balaam had come, he went out to meet him at the Moabite city on the Arnon, at the frontier of his territory.

37Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not send to summon you? Why did you not come to me? Am I not able to honourhonourhonor you?"

38Balaam replied, "Behold, I have come to you. But do I now have any power to say anything? I can speak only the word that God puts in my mouth."

39Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth. 40Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and sent portions to Balaam and to the princes who were with him. 41In the morning Balak took Balaam and brought him up to Bamoth-baal, and from there Balaam saw the outskirts of the people.
