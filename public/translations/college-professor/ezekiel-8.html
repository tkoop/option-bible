The Vision of the Divine Glory and the Idol of Jealousy

1In the sixth year, on the fifth day of the sixth month, I was sitting in my house, and the elders of Judah were sitting before me, when the power of the Lord GOD fell upon me there. 2I looked, and behold, a figure like that of a man: from HishisHis waist downward HeheHe was like fire, and from HishisHis waist upward HishisHis appearance was like the gleam of glowing metal. 3He stretched out the form of a hand and seized me by a lock of my head; the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up between EarthEarthearth and Heavenheavenheaven and brought me, in visions of God, to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces north, where the idol of jealousy had its seat. 4And there I saw the glory of the God of Israel, just as in the vision I had seen in the valley.

The Idol of Jealousy at the Temple Gate

5Then HeheHe said to me, “Son of man, look northward.” So I looked northward, and there, north of the altar gate, stood this idol of jealousy. 6He said to me, “Son of man, do you see what they are doing—the great abominations that the house of Israel is committing here, to drive MemeMe far from MymyMy sanctuary? Yet you will turn and see even greater abominations.”

The Secret Idolatry of the Elders

7Then HeheHe brought me to the entrance of the courtyard, and I looked, and behold, a hole in the wall. 8He said to me, “Son of man, dig through the wall.” So I dug through the wall, and behold, an entrance. 9He said, “Go in and see the wicked abominations they are committing here.”

10So I went in and looked, and behold, every form of creeping thing and detestable beast and all the idols of the house of Israel were carved all around on the wall. 11Before them stood seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing among them; each had a censer in his hand, and a dense cloud of incense was rising.

12He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in his chamber of carved images? For they say, ‘The LORD does not see us; the LORD has forsaken the land.’ 13Again, you will see them committing even greater abominations.”

Women Mourning for Tammuz

14Then HeheHe brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the LORD, and there sat women weeping for Tammuz. 15He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen this? You will see even greater abominations than these.”

Sun Worship in the Inner Court

16He brought me into the inner court of the house of the LORD, and there, at the entrance to the temple of the LORD, between the portico and the altar, were about twenty-five men with their backs to the temple of the LORD and their faces toward the east; they were bowing down to the sun in the east.

17He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen this? Is it a trivial thing for the house of Judah to commit the abominations they are committing here? For they have filled the land with violence and have provoked MemeMe to anger again and again. Look, they are thumbing their noses at MemeMe. 18Therefore I will act in wrath: I will not look on them with pity, nor will I show compassion. Though they cry out with a loud voice in MymyMy ears, I will not hear them.”
