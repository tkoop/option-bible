The Proverbs of Solomon

1A wise son makes his father glad. A foolish son makes his mother sad. 2Money from doing wrong does not help. Doing right saves a person from death. 3The LORD does not let good people go hungry. But HeheHe stops bad people from getting what they want. 4Lazy hands make a person poor. Hard work makes a person rich. 5A child who gathers food in summer is wise. A child who sleeps at harvest time brings shame.

Blessings and Words

6Good things come to a good person. But the mouth of a bad person hides hurt. 7People remember a good person with thanks. But the name of a bad person rots away. 8A wise heart takes commands. But a fool who talks too much gets in trouble. 9The person who walks in honesty walks safe. But the one who twists his ways gets found out. 10A person who winks to trick others causes trouble. And a fool who talks too much gets in trouble. 11The mouth of a good person is like a spring of life. But the mouth of a bad person hides hurt.

Love and Wisdom

12Hate stirs up fights. Love covers all wrongs. 13Wisdom is found on the lips of one who understands. But a rod is for the back of one who lacks sense. 14Wise people keep knowledge safe. But a fool's mouth brings ruin.

Money and Work

15A rich person's money is like a strong city. But poor people are ruined by having no money. 16A good person's work leads to life. But a bad person's gain leads to sin. 17The person who listens to teaching stays on the path to life. But the person who ignores correction goes the wrong way.

Speech and Value

18A person who hides hate has lying lips. And a person who spreads bad talk is a fool. 19When words are many, sin is not far away. But a person who keeps quiet is wise. 20The tongue of a good person is like good silver. But the heart of a bad person is worth little. 21The lips of a good person feed many. But fools die because they lack sense.

The LORD's Blessing

22The LORD's blessing makes a person rich. He adds no sorrow to it. 23To a fool, doing wrong is like a game. But a person with understanding has wisdom. 24What a bad person fears will come to him. But a good person's desire will be given. 25When the storm passes, bad people are gone. But good people stand firm forever.

Lazy Workers and Long Life

26Vinegar hurts the teeth. Smoke hurts the eyes. A lazy worker is like that to the people who send him. 27Fear of the LORD adds days to life. But the years of bad people are cut short. 28The hope of good people brings joy. But the hope of bad people dies away. 29The way of the LORD is safe for honest people. But it brings ruin to those who do wrong. 30Good people will never fall. But bad people will not stay in the land. 31The mouth of a good person brings out wisdom. But a crooked tongue will be stopped. 32The lips of a good person know what is right. But the mouth of a bad person is crooked.
