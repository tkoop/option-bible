God Tells Moses to Count the People

1The LORD spoke to Moses in the tent of meeting. This was in the Sinai desert. It was the second month of the second year after the Israelites left Egypt. The LORD said, 2"Take a census of all the Israelites. Count them by their families. Count every man by name. 3You and Aaron must count the men who are twenty years old or older. Count all who can serve in the army. Count them by their groups. 4One man from each tribe will help you. He will be the head of his family. 5These are the names of the men who will stand with you to help: From Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur. 6From Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. 7From Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab. 8From Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar. 9From Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon. 10From Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud. From Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. 11From Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni. 12From Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. 13From Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran. 14From Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel. 15From Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan. 16These men were chosen from the people. They were leaders of their tribes and heads of Israelite families."

17Moses and Aaron took these named men.

The People Are Counted

18On the first day of the second month, the whole community gathered. They were listed by their families and family groups. Men twenty years old or older were counted by name. 19Moses counted them in the Sinai desert, just as the LORD had commanded.

Count of Each Tribe

20The people of Reuben were counted by their families. 21There were 46,500 men. 22The people of Simeon were counted by their families. 23There were 59,300 men. 24The people of Gad were counted by their families. 25There were 45,650 men. 26The people of Judah were counted by their families. 27There were 74,600 men. 28The people of Issachar were counted by their families. 29There were 54,400 men. 30The people of Zebulun were counted by their families. 31There were 57,400 men. 32The people of Ephraim, from the family of Joseph, were counted by their families. 33There were 40,500 men. 34The people of Manasseh were counted by their families. 35There were 32,200 men. 36The people of Benjamin were counted by their families. 37There were 35,400 men. 38The people of Dan were counted by their families. 39There were 62,700 men. 40The people of Asher were counted by their families. 41There were 41,500 men. 42The people of Naphtali were counted by their families. 43There were 53,400 men.

The Total Number

44These were the men Moses and Aaron counted. The twelve leaders helped them. One leader came from each family. 45All the Israelite men who were twenty years old or older and could serve in the army were counted by their families. 46The total was 603,550 men.

Levites Care for the Holy Tent

47The Levites were not counted with the others. 48The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 49"Do not count the tribe of Levi with the other Israelites. Do not include them in the total. 50You must put the Levites in charge of the holy tent where the sacred box is kept. They will care for it and all its things. They will carry the holy tent and its things. They will serve there. They will camp around the holy tent. 51When the holy tent moves, the Levites will take it down. When it is set up, the Levites will put it up. Anyone else who comes near it must die. 52The other Israelites will camp in their groups under their flags. 53The Levites will camp around the holy tent. They will guard it. Then the LORD will not be angry with the Israelite people." 54The Israelites did everything the LORD had commanded Moses.
