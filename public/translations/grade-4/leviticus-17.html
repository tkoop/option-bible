God Speaks to Moses

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites. Tell them this is what the LORD commands. 3If an Israelite kills an ox, a lamb, or a goat in the camp or outside the camp, 4and does not bring it to the door of the meeting tent to offer it to the LORD, that man has spilled blood. He must be sent away from his people."

Bring Sacrifices to the Priest

"5This is so the Israelites will bring their sacrifices to the priest at the door of the meeting tent. They will not offer them in the open field. They will offer them as peace offerings to the LORD. 6The priest will sprinkle the blood on the LORD's altar at the door of the meeting tent. He will burn the fat. It will smell sweet to the LORD. 7They must not offer sacrifices to goat idols anymore. They used to follow them. This rule will last for them and their children forever."

Burned Offerings

"8Also tell them: If an Israelite or a foreigner living with them offers a burned offering or a sacrifice, 9and does not bring it to the door of the meeting tent to do it for the LORD, that man must be sent away from his people."

Do Not Eat Blood

"10If an Israelite or a foreigner living with them eats blood, I will turn against that person. I will send them away from their people. 11The life of the body is in the blood. I gave it to you to pay for your lives on the altar. The blood pays for life because life is in it. 12So I told the Israelites that no one among you may eat blood, and no foreigner living with you may eat blood."

Blood from Hunted Animals

"13If an Israelite or a foreigner living with them hunts an animal or a bird that may be eaten, he must pour out its blood and cover it with dirt. 14The life of every creature is in its blood. So I told the Israelites not to eat the blood of any creature, because the life of every creature is in its blood. Anyone who eats it must be sent away."

Animals Found Dead

"15If any person, Israelite or foreigner, eats an animal that died on its own or was torn by another animal, he must wash his clothes and bathe in water. He will not be clean until evening. Then he will be clean. 16If he does not wash his clothes or bathe his body, he will be guilty."
