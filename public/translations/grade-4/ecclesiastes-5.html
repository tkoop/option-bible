Be Careful in God's House

1Watch your steps when you go to God's house. It is better to listen than to give a foolish offering. Fools do not know they do wrong. 2Do not rush to speak. Do not let your heart be quick to say a word to God. God is in Heavenheavenheaven, and you are on EarthEarthearth. So let your words be few. 3Dreams come from having too much to do. A fool talks too much.

Keep Promises to God

4If you make a promise to God, do not wait to keep it. God is not pleased with fools. Keep your promise. 5It is better not to promise than to promise and not keep it.

6Do not let your mouth make you sin. Do not say to the temple worker, "I made a mistake." Why should God be angry at your words and ruin the work of your hands?

7Dreams and empty words are many. Fear God.

Do Not Be Surprised by Unfairness

8If you see poor people treated badly, and fairness and good things taken away in a land, do not be surprised. One leader is watched by a higher leader, and there are even higher leaders above them. 9The land gives food to everyone. Even the king eats from the fields.

Money Does Not Bring Peace

10People who love money are never satisfied with money. People who love having lots of things are never happy with what they get. This is also empty. 11When you have more stuff, more people use it up. What good is it to the owner, except to look at it? 12A worker sleeps well, whether he eats a little or a lot. But a rich person has so much that he cannot sleep.

13I have seen a bad thing in this life: a person saves up money, and it hurts him. 14That money is lost in a bad deal. Then he has a son, but nothing is left for him. 15Everyone comes into the world with nothing. Everyone leaves the same way. They take nothing from their hard work. 16This is also a bad thing: people come with nothing, and leave with nothing. What good is working so hard for nothing? 17All his life he eats in the dark. He has much trouble, sickness, and anger.

Enjoy God's Gifts

18I have seen what is good and right: It is good for a person to eat, drink, and enjoy the good things from all his hard work in this life. God gives him a certain number of days to live. That is his share. 19God also gives some people wealth and things. He lets them eat from it, take their share, and be happy in their work. This is a gift from God. 20They do not think much about their life, because God keeps them busy with joy in their heart.
