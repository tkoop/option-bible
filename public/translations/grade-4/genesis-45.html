Joseph Shows Himself to His Brothers

1Joseph could not control himself in front of all the people standing there. He called out, “Make everyone leave!” No one stayed with him when he told his brothers who he was. 2He cried so loud that the Egyptians heard it. Pharaoh’s house heard it too.

3Joseph said to his brothers, “I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?” His brothers could not answer him. They were too scared.

4Joseph said, “Come close to me.” They came close. “I am Joseph, your brother,” he said. “You sold me to Egypt.”

5“Now do not be upset. Do not be angry with yourselves because you sold me here. God sent me ahead of you to save lives.”

6“There has been no food in the land for two years. There are still five years with no planting or harvest.”

7“God sent me ahead of you to keep your family alive on EarthEarthearth. He saved many lives in a big way.”

8“So it was not you who sent me here. It was God. He made me like a father to Pharaoh. I am the boss of his whole house. I rule all of Egypt.”

9“Hurry and go to my father. Tell him, ‘Your son Joseph says God made him boss of all Egypt. Come down to me. Do not wait.’”

10“You will live in Goshen. You will be near me. You, your children, your grandchildren, your sheep, your cows, and all you own.”

11“I will take care of you there. There are still five years with no food. I do not want you, your family, and all you own to have nothing.”

12“You can see with your own eyes. My brother Benjamin can see too. I am the one talking to you.”

13“Tell my father about all my good things in Egypt. Tell him all you have seen. Bring my father here fast.”

14Then Joseph threw his arms around his brother Benjamin and cried. Benjamin cried too.

15Joseph kissed all his brothers and cried over them. After that, his brothers talked with him.

Pharaoh Welcomes Joseph’s Family

16The news reached Pharaoh’s house that Joseph’s brothers had come. Pharaoh and all his servants were happy.

17Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Tell your brothers to do this: Load your animals and go back to Canaan.”

18“Then bring your father and your families. Come to me. I will give you the best land in Egypt. You will eat the best food in the land.”

19“You must also tell them, ‘Take wagons from Egypt for your little ones and your wives. Bring your father and come.’”

20“Do not worry about your things. The best of all Egypt is yours.”

21So Israel’s sons did as they were told. Joseph gave them wagons, just as Pharaoh had said. He gave them food for the trip.

22He gave new clothes to each of them. But he gave Benjamin 300 pieces of silver and five sets of clothes.

23He sent this to his father: ten donkeys carrying the best things from Egypt, and ten female donkeys carrying grain, bread, and food for the trip.

24He sent his brothers away and said, “Do not fight on the way.”

25They went up from Egypt and came to their father Jacob in Canaan.

Jacob Hears Joseph Is Alive

26They told him, “Joseph is still alive! He rules all of Egypt!” Jacob felt weak because he did not believe them.

27But when they told him all that Joseph had said, and when he saw the wagons Joseph sent to carry him, Jacob felt better.

28Then Israel said, “My son Joseph is still alive! I will go see him before I die.”
