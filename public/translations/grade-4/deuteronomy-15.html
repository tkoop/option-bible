The Year to Cancel Debts

1At the end of every seven years, you must cancel debts. 2This is the rule for the release: If someone lent money to another person, let it go. Do not ask your neighbourneighbourneighbor or your brother to pay you back. This is the the LORD's special year to cancel debts. 3You may ask a person from another country to pay you back, but you must let your brother's debt go.

4There should not be poor people among you. The the LORD your God will bless you a lot in the land HeheHe is giving you to own. 5You will be blessed if you listen carefully to the the LORD your God and obey all these rules I give you today. 6The the LORD your God will bless you, just as HeheHe promised. You will lend money to many nations, but you will not borrow from them. You will rule over many nations, but they will not rule over you.

Help Poor People

7If one of your brothers is poor in any town in the land the the LORD your God is giving you, do not be stubborn, and do not refuse to help him. 8Instead, help him. Lend him what he needs.

9Be careful. Do not let an evil thought grow in your heart. Do not say, "ThetheThe seventh year is near, the year to cancel debts," and then look down on your poor brother and give him nothing. He may cry to the the LORD against you, and you will have sinned. 10Give to him freely. Do not feel bad in your heart when you give. Because of this, the the LORD your God will bless you in all your work and in everything you do.

11There will always be poor people in the land. That is why I command you to help your brother, your poor and needy people in your land.

Freeing Servants

12If a Hebrew man or woman is sold to you, they must serve you for six years. In the seventh year, you must let them go free. 13When you let them go free, do not send them away with nothing.

14Give them gifts from your sheep, your grain, and your wine. Give to them as the the LORD your God has blessed you. 15Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and the the LORD your God set you free. That is why I give you this command today.

16But if your servant says, "I do not want to leave you," because he loves your family and has a good life with you, 17then take a sharp tool and push it through his ear into the door. He will be your servant for the rest of his life. Do the same for your female servant.

18Do not think it is hard to let them go free. They served you six years, which is worth twice as much as a hired worker. The the LORD your God will bless you in everything you do.

Giving the Firstborn Animals

19Set apart to the the LORD your God every firstborn male from your cattle and sheep. Do not make your firstborn ox work, and do not cut the wool from your firstborn sheep. 20Each year, you and your family must eat them in the place the the LORD your God chooses.

21If an animal has something wrong with it, such as being lame or blind or having another bad problem, do not sacrifice it to the the LORD your God. 22You may eat it in your own towns. People who are clean and people who are not clean may eat it, like a gazelle or a deer. 23But do not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water.
