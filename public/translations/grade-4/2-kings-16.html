Ahaz Becomes King of Judah

1In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham became king of Judah. 2Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king. He ruled sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD his God. 3He followed the ways of the kings of Israel. He even sacrificed his son in fire. This was like the terrible things the nations did. The LORD had driven those nations out before the people of Israel. 4He offered sacrifices and burned incense on high places, on hills, and under every green tree.

Enemies Attack Jerusalem

5Then Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel came to fight Jerusalem. They surrounded Ahaz, but they could not win against him. 6At that time Rezin king of Aram took back Elath for Aram. He drove the people of Judah out of Elath. The men of Aram came to Elath and lived there to this day.

Ahaz Asks Assyria to Help

7Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria. He said, "I am your servant and your son. Come up and save me from the king of Aram and from the king of Israel. They are attacking me." 8Ahaz took the silver and gold found in the house of the LORD and in the king's treasure rooms. He sent it as a gift to the king of Assyria. 9The king of Assyria listened to him. He went up to Damascus and took the city. He sent the people away to Kir. He also killed Rezin.

Ahaz Changes the Temple

10King Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria. He saw an altar there in Damascus. King Ahaz sent Uriah the priest a copy of the altar and a pattern for making it. 11Uriah the priest built the altar. He made it just like King Ahaz had sent from Damascus. Uriah the priest finished it before King Ahaz came back from Damascus. 12When the king came back from Damascus, he saw the altar. He went to it and offered sacrifices on it. 13He burned his burnt offering and his grain offering. He poured out his drink offering. He sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings on the altar. 14He moved the bronze altar that stood before the LORD. It had been in front of the temple, between the other altar and the house of the LORD. He put it on the north side of the other altar. 15King Ahaz told Uriah the priest, "Burn the morning burnt offering and the evening burnt offering on the large altar. Burn the king's burnt offering and grain offering. Burn the burnt offering and grain offering for all the people of the land. Pour out their drink offerings. Sprinkle on it all the blood from the burnt offerings and all the blood from the sacrifices. But I will use the bronze altar to ask God for guidance." 16Uriah the priest did everything King Ahaz had told him to do.

Ahaz Takes Things from the Temple

17King Ahaz cut the panels off the stands. He took the bowls from them. He took the large bronze bowl down from the bronze bulls under it. He put it on a stone floor. 18He moved the covered walkway used on the Sabbath. It had been built in the temple. He also moved the king's outside entrance to the house of the LORD. He did this because of the king of Assyria. 19The other things Ahaz did are written in the book of the history of the kings of Judah.

Ahaz Dies

20Ahaz died and was buried with his ancestors in the CitycityCity of David. His son Hezekiah became king after him.
