Darius Looks for Cyrus's Order

1King Darius made a rule. Men searched the records kept in Babylon's treasure house. 2They found one scroll in the fort at Ecbatana in Media. The scroll said:

3"In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus made this order: God's house in Jerusalem must be rebuilt. It is the place where offerings are made. Its base must be strong. It must be about 90 feet high and 90 feet wide. 4It will have three rows of big stones and one row of wood. The king will pay for it. 5Also bring back the gold and silver dishes from God's house. Nebuchadnezzar took them from the temple in Jerusalem and carried them to Babylon. Put them back in God's house in Jerusalem."

Darius Tells Officials to Help

6"Now, Tattenai, governor of the land beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and their friends, stay away from there. 7Let the Jewish elders build God's house in its place.

8"I also order this: Help these Jewish elders build God's house. Pay them in full from the king's money and from taxes in the land beyond the River, so the work does not stop. 9Give them what they need: young bulls, rams, and lambs for burned offerings to the God of Heavenheavenheaven; wheat, salt, wine, and oil, as the priests in Jerusalem ask. Give it every day without fail. 10Then they can offer pleasing sacrifices to the God of Heavenheavenheaven and pray for the king and his sons. 11If anyone changes this order, a beam will be pulled from his house, and he will be hung on it. His house will be made a trash pile. 12May the God who put HishisHis name there destroy any king or people who try to change or destroy this house in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have made this order. Do it fully."

The Officials Obey Darius

13Then Tattenai, Shethar-bozenai, and their friends did exactly as King Darius had sent. 14The Jewish elders built and did well. They were helped by the prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo. They finished the house by the command of the God of Israel and by the orders of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes, king of Persia. 15The house was finished on the third day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of King Darius.

The People Celebrate the New Temple

16The people of Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the rest of the returned captives joyfully celebrated the opening of God's house. 17For the celebration they offered 100 bulls, 200 rams, 400 lambs, and 12 male goats as a sin offering for all Israel. There was one goat for each tribe. 18The priests and Levites were set in their groups to serve God in Jerusalem, just as Moses' book says.

The People Keep Passover

19On the fourteenth day of the first month, the returned people kept the Passover. 20The priests and Levites had all become clean. They killed the Passover lamb for all the returned people, for their priest brothers, and for themselves. 21The Israelites who came back from exile ate it. So did everyone who had turned from the unclean ways of other nations to seek the the LORD God of Israel. 22They kept the FeastfeastFeast of BreadbreadBread Without Yeast for seven days with joy, because the the LORD had made them joyful. He made the king of Assyria want to help them build the house of God, the God of Israel.
