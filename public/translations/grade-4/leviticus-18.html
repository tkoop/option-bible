God Speaks to Moses

1The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2"Speak to the Israelites. Tell them: I am the LORD your God.

Do Not Live Like Other Nations

"3Do not do what people in Egypt did, where you lived. Do not do what people in Canaan do. I am bringing you there. Do not follow their ways.

"4You must follow MymyMy rules and keep MymyMy laws. Live by them. I am the LORD your God.

"5Keep MymyMy rules and MymyMy laws. A person who does them will live by them. I am the LORD.

Do Not Have Sex with Close Family

"6No one may have sex with a close family member. I am the LORD.

"7Do not have sex with your father or your mother. She is your mother. Do not have sex with her.

"8Do not have sex with your father's wife. She is your father's wife.

"9Do not have sex with your sister. She may be your father's daughter or your mother's daughter. She may have been born at home or somewhere else. Do not have sex with her.

"10Do not have sex with your granddaughter, whether she is your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter. They are your own family. Do not have sex with them.

"11Do not have sex with your father's wife's daughter if she was born to your father. She is your sister. Do not have sex with her.

"12Do not have sex with your father's sister. She is your father's close family.

"13Do not have sex with your mother's sister. She is your mother's close family.

"14Do not have sex with your uncle's wife. She is your aunt.

"15Do not have sex with your daughter-in-law. She is your son's wife. Do not have sex with her.

"16Do not have sex with your brother's wife. She belongs to your brother.

"17Do not have sex with a woman and her daughter. Do not take her granddaughter, her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter, to have sex with her. They are her close family. That is very wrong.

"18Do not marry a woman and her sister at the same time. Do not have sex with one while the other is alive.

"19Do not have sex with a woman during her monthly period.

Other Wrong Acts

"20Do not have sex with your neighbourneighbourneighbor's wife. That would make you unclean.

"21Do not give any of your children to be burned for Molech. Do not misuse the name of your God. I am the LORD.

"22Do not have sex with a man the way you would with a woman. That is a terrible sin.

"23Do not have sex with any animal. That makes you unclean. A woman must not let an animal mate with her. That is wrong.

God Warns His People

"24Do not make yourselves unclean by doing any of these things. The nations I am sending away before you became unclean by doing them.

"25The land became unclean. I punished it for its sin. The land spit out the people living there.

"26You must keep MymyMy rules and MymyMy laws. Do not do any of these terrible things. This is for people born here and for strangers living among you.

"27The people who lived in this land before you did all these terrible things. That made the land unclean.

"28If you make the land unclean, it may spit you out too, just like it spit out the people before you.

"29Anyone who does any of these terrible things will be removed from his people.

"30So obey MymyMy command. Do not do these terrible customs that were done before you. Do not make yourselves unclean by them. I am the LORD your God."
