A Song to Thank God

1Shout with joy to the the LORD, all the earth. 2Serve the the LORD with joy. Come before HimhimHim with happy songs.

3Know that the the LORD is God. He made us. We did not make ourselves. We are HishisHis people. We are sheep in HishisHis care.

4Come into HishisHis gates with thanks. Come into HishisHis yards with praise. Thank HimhimHim. Bless HishisHis name. 5For the the LORD is good. His love never ends. He keeps HishisHis promises forever.
