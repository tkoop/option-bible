Paul Warns About Bad Examples

1I do not want you to miss this, brothers and sisters. Our people long ago were all under the cloud. They all went through the sea. 2They all were joined to Moses in the cloud and the sea. 3They all ate the same food from God. 4They all drank the same drink from God. They drank from the spiritual rock that went with them. That rock was Christ. 5But God was not happy with most of them. They died in the desert.

6These things happened to teach us. We should not want bad things like they did. 7Do not worship fake gods like some of them did. It is written, "ThetheThe people sat down to eat and drink. Then they got up to play." 8Do not commit sexual sin like some of them did. In one day, 23,000 people died. 9Do not test Christ like some of them did. They were killed by snakes. 10Do not complain like some of them did. They were killed by the Destroyer. 11These things happened to them to teach us. They were written for us who live now. The end of time has come to us. 12So if you think you are strong, be careful. You might fall.

13No test has come to you that is not common to people. God is faithful. He will not let you be tested more than you can handle. With the test, HeheHe will make a way out. Then you can stand it. 14So, my dear friends, run away from worshiping fake gods. 15I talk to you like smart people. Decide for yourselves if what I say is right.

The Lord's Supper and Sharing

16The cup we bless, is it not sharing in Christ's blood? The bread we break, is it not sharing in Christ's body? 17There is one bread. We are many, but we are one body. We all share that one bread. 18Look at the people of Israel. Are not the people who eat the sacrifices sharing in the altar? 19What am I saying then? Is food given to fake gods a big thing? Is a fake god a big thing? 20No. I am saying that what people give to fake gods, they give to demons. They do not give it to God. I do not want you to share with demons. 21You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You cannot share the table of the Lord and the table of demons. 22Do we make the Lord jealous? Are we stronger than HeheHe is?

23"All things are allowed," but not all things help. "All things are allowed," but not all things build us up. 24No one should look out for only himself. Look out for what helps other people.

Freedom and Conscience

25Eat any meat sold in the meat store. Do not ask questions about it. This is for your conscience. 26For "the earth is the the LORD, and everything in it." 27If someone who does not believe asks you to eat, and you want to go, eat what is put in front of you. Do not ask questions. This is for your conscience. 28But if someone says to you, "This was given to a god," do not eat it. Do it for that person and for your conscience. 29I do not mean your conscience. I mean his conscience. Why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience? 30If I eat with thanks, why am I blamed for what I thank God for? 31So if you eat, or drink, or do anything, do it all to give God glory. 32Do not do things that make people fall. Do not make Jews, Greeks, or God's church fall. 33I try to please all people in all things. I do not look for what helps me. I look for what helps many people. I want them to be saved.
