Aaron's Stick Turns into a Snake

8The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 9"When Pharaoh says, 'Show a miracle,' you tell Aaron, 'Take your stick and throw it in front of Pharaoh. It will become a snake.'" 10Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. They did what the LORD told them. Aaron threw his stick in front of Pharaoh and his servants.
