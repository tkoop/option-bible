How to Treat People in Church

1Do not speak harshly to an older man. Talk to him like a father. Treat younger men like brothers. 2Treat older women like mothers. Treat younger women like sisters. Stay pure in every way.

3Help widows who are truly widows. 4But if a widow has children or grandchildren, they should first learn to be good to their own family. They should pay back their parents and grandparents. This pleases God. 5A widow who is truly alone puts her hope in God. She prays day and night. 6But a widow who lives only for fun is dead while she lives.

7Tell people these things. Then no one can blame them. 8If anyone does not take care of his own family, he has turned away from the faith. He is worse than someone who does not believe.

Widows Who Need Help

9A widow should be put on the list only if she is at least 60 years old. She should have been married to one husband. 10People should say good things about her. She raised children. She welcomed strangers. She washed the feet of God's people. She helped people in trouble. She did every kind of good work.

11But do not put younger widows on the list. When they feel strong desires, they want to marry, and that can pull them away from Christ. 12They will be judged because they broke their first promise. 13Also, they learn to be lazy. They go from house to house. They are not just lazy. They also talk too much. They poke into other people's business. They say things they should not say.

14So I want younger widows to marry. They should have children. They should take care of their homes. Then no enemy will have a reason to say bad things. 15Some have already turned away to follow Satan. 16If a woman who believes has widows in her family, she should help them. Then the church will not have to carry the load. The church can help the widows who are truly alone.

HonourHonourHonor for Church Leaders

17Church leaders who lead well should get twice as much honourhonourhonor. This is even more true for those who work hard at speaking and teaching. 18The Bible says, "Do not stop an ox from eating while it works." It also says, "A worker should get his pay."

19Do not listen to a charge against a church leader unless two or three people say it is true. 20Tell people who keep sinning to stop. Do this in front of everyone. Then the others will be afraid to sin.

More Advice

21I ask you to do this before God, Christ Jesus, and the angels HeheHe chose. Keep these rules without favoring anyone. Do not be unfair. 22Do not be too quick to choose someone for church work. Do not share in other people's sins. Keep yourself pure. 23Stop drinking only water. Drink a little wine. It can help your stomach and your many sicknesses.

24Some people's sins are easy to see. They lead the way to judgmentjudgementjudgment. Other sins show up later. 25Good works are easy to see too. Even the ones that are not easy to see cannot stay hidden.
