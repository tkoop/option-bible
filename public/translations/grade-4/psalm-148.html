All Things on Earth Praise the LORD

7Praise the LORD from the earth! Praise HimhimHim, big sea animals and deep ocean places! 8Praise HimhimHim, fire and hail, snow and clouds, and strong winds that do what HeheHe says! 9Praise HimhimHim, mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedar trees! 10Praise HimhimHim, wild animals and all farm animals, small things that crawl and birds that fly! 11Praise HimhimHim, kings of the earth and all nations, leaders and all judges of the earth! 12Praise HimhimHim, young men and young women, old people and young people too!

He is praised by all HishisHis faithful people. They are the children of Israel, people close to HimhimHim. Praise the LORD!
