The LORD Speaks to Moses

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Tell the Israelites: If any Israelite or foreigner living in Israel gives one of his children to the false god Molech, that person must be put to death. The people of the land must throw stones at him.

3"I will turn against that man and remove him from his people, because he gave his child to Molech. He made MymyMy holy place dirty. He also treated MymyMy holy name like it was not special.

4"If the people of the land pretend not to see and do not put him to death when he gives his child to Molech, 5then I will turn against that man and his family. I will remove them from their people. That includes him and all who follow him by being unfaithful to MemeMe and worshiping Molech.

Do Not Go to Mediums

6"If a person goes to mediums or to people who say they can talk to spirits, and is unfaithful to MemeMe by following them, I will turn against that person and remove him from his people.

Be Holy to the LORD

7"Set yourselves apart and be holy, because I am the LORD your God. 8Obey MymyMy rules and do them. I am the LORD. I am the one who makes you holy.

Do Not Curse Your Parents

9"If anyone curses his father or mother, he must be put to death. He cursed his father or mother, so his death is his own fault.

Do Not Commit Adultery

10"If a man has sex with another man's wife—with his neighbourneighbourneighbor's wife—both the man and the woman must be put to death.

Do Not Commit Sexual Sins

11"If a man has sex with his father's wife, he has shamed his father. Both of them must be put to death. Their death is their own fault. 12If a man has sex with his daughter-in-law, both of them must be put to death. They have done something terrible. Their death is their own fault. 13If a man has sex with a man as he would with a woman, both of them have done something detestable. They must be put to death. Their death is their own fault.

14"If a man marries a woman and her mother, that is wicked. The man and the women must be burned with fire, so that this wicked thing will not be among you.

15"If a man has sex with an animal, he must be put to death. You must also kill the animal. 16If a woman goes near an animal to have sex with it, you must kill the woman and the animal. They must be put to death. Their death is their own fault.

17"If a man marries his sister, whether she is his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and he sees her private parts and she sees his, that is a disgrace. They will be separated from their people. He has shamed his sister, and he will be punished for his wrong. 18If a man has sex with a woman during her monthly period, he has exposed her flow of blood. Both of them will be separated from their people.

19"Do not have sex with your mother's sister or your father's sister, because that shames his close relative. They will be punished for their sin. 20If a man has sex with his aunt, he has shamed his uncle. They will be punished for their sin, and they will die without children. 21If a man marries his brother's wife, that is unclean. He has shamed his brother. They will have no children.

Keep the LORD's Laws

22"So obey all MymyMy rules and all MymyMy laws. Do them. Then the land where I am bringing you to live will not throw you out.

23"Do not follow the customs of the nations I am driving out before you. They did all these things, so I was disgusted with them.

25"So you must tell the difference between clean and unclean animals and birds. Do not make yourselves disgusting with unclean animals, birds, or anything that crawls on the ground. I have set these apart as unclean for you.

26"You must be holy to MemeMe, because I, the LORD, am holy. I have set you apart from the other nations to be MymyMy own.

27"If a man or woman among you is a medium or a person who says they can talk to spirits, they must be put to death. You must throw stones at them. Their death is their own fault."
