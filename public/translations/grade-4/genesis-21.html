Isaac Is Born

1The LORD came to Sarah, just like HeheHe said HeheHe would. The LORD did for Sarah what HeheHe had promised. 2Sarah became pregnant and had a baby boy for Abraham when he was very old. It happened at the time God said it would. 3Abraham named his son Isaac. Sarah had this baby for him. 4Abraham had Isaac circumcised when he was eight days old, just like God told him to. 5Abraham was one hundred years old when Isaac was born. 6Sarah said, "God has made me laugh. Everyone who hears about this will laugh with me." 7She also said, "Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would feed babies? But I have had a son when Abraham is old."

Isaac's Party

8The boy grew up and was weaned. Abraham gave a big party on the day Isaac was weaned.

Hagar and Ishmael Leave

9Sarah saw the son of Hagar, the woman from Egypt. Hagar had this son for Abraham. Sarah saw him making fun of Isaac. 10Sarah said to Abraham, "Send away this slave woman and her son. The son of a slave woman will not share the family blessing with my son Isaac."

11This made Abraham very sad because it was about his son. 12God said to Abraham, "Do not be sad about the boy and your slave woman. Listen to Sarah. Do everything she tells you. Your family will be named through Isaac. 13I will also make the son of the slave woman into a big group of people, because he is your son too."

14Early the next morning, Abraham got up. He took bread and a bag of water and gave them to Hagar. He put them on her shoulder. He also sent the boy with her. Hagar left and walked in the desert near Beersheba. 15When the water was gone, she put the boy under a bush. 16She went and sat down far away, about as far as an arrow can fly. She said, "I cannot watch the boy die." She sat there and cried out loud.

17God heard the boy crying. An angel of God called to Hagar from Heavenheavenheaven and said, "What is wrong, Hagar? Do not be afraid. God has heard the boy where he is. 18Get up, pick up the boy, and hold him with your hand. I will make him into a big group of people." 19God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water. She went and filled the bag with water and gave the boy a drink.

20God was with the boy as he grew. He lived in the desert and became a hunter with a bow. 21He lived in the desert of Paran. His mother found a wife for him from Egypt.

Abraham and Abimelech Make Peace

22At that time, Abimelech and Phicol, the leader of his army, said to Abraham, "God is with you in everything you do." 23Abimelech said, "Now promise me here before God that you will not lie to me, or to my children, or to their children. Be kind to me and to this land where you have lived, just as I have been kind to you."

24Abraham said, "I will promise."

25Abraham complained to Abimelech about a well of water. Abimelech's workers had taken it. 26Abimelech said, "I did not know who did this. You did not tell me. I had not heard about it until today."

27Abraham took sheep and cows and gave them to Abimelech. The two men made an agreement. 28Abraham set seven female lambs from the flock by themselves. 29Abimelech asked, "What do these seven female lambs mean? Why did you put them by themselves?"

30Abraham said, "Take these seven female lambs from my hand. They will show that I dug this well." 31So that place was called Beersheba, because the two men swore an oath there. 32They made an agreement in Beersheba. Then Abimelech and Phicol, the leader of his army, got up and went back to the land of the Philistines.

33Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba. He called on the name of the LORD, the God who lives forever. 34Abraham lived in the land of the Philistines for many days.
