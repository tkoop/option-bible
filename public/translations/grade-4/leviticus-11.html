God Gives Food Rules

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said, "Speak to the Israelites. 2Among all the animals on the land, these are the ones you may eat. 3You may eat any animal that has a split hoof in two parts and chews its food again.

Animals You May Not Eat

"4But do not eat these animals. They chew their food again, but they do not have a split hoof. The camel is one. Its hoof is not split, so it is not clean for you. 5The rock badger chews its food again, but it does not have a split hoof. It is not clean for you. 6The hare chews its food again, but it does not have a split hoof. It is not clean for you. 7The pig has a split hoof, but it does not chew its food again. It is not clean for you. 8Do not eat their meat. Do not touch their dead bodies. They are not clean for you.

Water Animals

"9You may eat anything that lives in the water. In the seas or in rivers, you may eat anything that has fins and scales. 10But anything in the water that does not have fins and scales is gross to you. This includes the small living things in the water. 11They are gross to you. Do not eat them. Their dead bodies are gross to you. 12Anything in the water without fins and scales is gross to you.

Birds You May Not Eat

"13These birds are gross. Do not eat them: the eagle, the vulture, and the black vulture. 14The kite and the falcon of any kind. 15Every kind of raven. 16The ostrich, the screech owl, the gull, and the hawk of any kind. 17The little owl, the cormorant, and the great owl. 18The white owl, the desert owl, and the osprey. 19The stork, the heron of any kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

Bugs With Wings

"20All flying bugs that walk on four legs are gross to you. 21But you may eat some flying bugs that walk on four legs. Eat the ones that have strong back legs for jumping on the ground. 22You may eat these: the locust, the katydid, the cricket, and the grasshopper. Eat each kind. 23All other flying bugs with four legs are gross to you.

Touching Dead Animals

"24These animals make you not clean. Anyone who touches their dead bodies is not clean until evening. 25Anyone who carries part of their dead body must wash his clothes. He is not clean until evening. 26These animals are not clean for you: any animal that has a split hoof but not in two parts, or does not chew its food again. Anyone who touches them is not clean. 27Any animal that walks on four legs and walks on its paws is not clean for you. Anyone who touches its dead body is not clean until evening. 28Anyone who carries its dead body must wash his clothes. He is not clean until evening. These animals are not clean for you.

Crawling Animals

"29These small animals that crawl on the ground are not clean for you: the mole, the mouse, and the great lizard of any kind. 30The gecko, the monitor lizard, the lizard, the sand lizard, and the chameleon. 31These are not clean for you. Anyone who touches them when they are dead is not clean until evening.

Dead Animals on Things

"32If one of these dead animals falls on something, that thing is not clean. It does not matter if it is wood, clothes, skin, or a sack. Any tool used for work must be put in water. It stays not clean until evening. Then it is clean. 33If one falls into a clay pot, everything inside is not clean. You must break the pot. 34Any food that water touches in that pot is not clean. Any drink in that pot is not clean.

"35Anything their dead body falls on is not clean. An oven or a stove must be broken into pieces. They are not clean, and they are not clean for you. 36A spring or a well with water in it stays clean. But anyone who touches the dead body in it is not clean. 37If part of a dead body falls on seed that is planted, the seed is still clean. 38But if water is put on the seed and part of the dead body falls on it, it is not clean for you.

Clean Animals That Die

"39If an animal you may eat dies, anyone who touches its dead body is not clean until evening. 40Anyone who eats part of its dead body must wash his clothes. He is not clean until evening. Anyone who carries the dead body must wash his clothes. He is not clean until evening.

Crawling Things Are Gross

"41Every small animal that crawls on the ground is gross. Do not eat it. 42You must not eat them, because they are gross. This includes any animal that crawls on its belly, walks on four legs, or has many legs. 43Do not make yourselves gross with these crawling things. Do not make yourselves not clean with them. Do not let them make you not clean.

Be Holy Like God

"44I am the LORD your God. Make yourselves clean and holy, because I am holy. Do not make yourselves not clean with any crawling thing on the ground. 45I am the LORD. I brought you out of Egypt to be your God. Be holy, because I am holy.

The Law of Clean and Unclean

"46This is the law for animals, birds, water animals, and all living things that crawl in the water or on the ground. 47You must learn the difference between what is not clean and what is clean. You must learn the difference between animals you may eat and animals you may not eat."
