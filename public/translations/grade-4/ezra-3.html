The People Come Together

1In the seventh month, the people of Israel were in their towns. They came together in Jerusalem like one person.

Building the Altar

2Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers, the priests, stood up. Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his brothers stood up too. They built the altar of the God of Israel. They wanted to give burnt offerings on it. This is what the LawlawLaw of Moses, the man of God, said to do. 3They put the altar on its old base. They gave burnt gifts on it to the LORD, morning and evening. They were afraid of the people in the lands around them. 4They also kept the FeastfeastFeast of Shelters. They gave the daily burnt offerings. They gave the right number for each day, as the rules said. 5After that, they gave the regular burnt offering. They also gave offerings for the new moons and for the LORD's special feasts. People also gave gifts they chose to give to the LORD. 6They started giving burnt offerings to the LORD on the first day of the seventh month. But the LORD's temple had not been built yet. 7They gave money to stonecutters and builders. They gave food, drink, and oil to the people of Sidon and Tyre. Those people brought cedar wood from Lebanon to Joppa by sea. King Cyrus of Persia had said they could do this.

Work Begins on the Temple

8In the second month of the second year, after they came to God's house in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the rest of their brothers began the work. The priests and Levites helped too. All who had come back from captivity to Jerusalem helped. They chose Levites who were twenty years old or older to lead the work on the LORD's house. 9Jeshua and his family stood together with Kadmiel and his family. The sons of Judah and the sons of Henadad stood with them too. All these Levites led the builders in God's house.

The Foundation Is Laid

10When the builders laid the foundation of the LORD's temple, the priests stood there. They wore their special clothes and had trumpets. The Levites, who were Asaph's family, had cymbals. They stood to praise the LORD. This was like what King David of Israel had done. 11They sang to the LORD. They praised HimhimHim and thanked HimhimHim. They said, "HeheHe is good. His love for Israel lasts forever." Then all the people shouted with joy. They praised the LORD because the foundation of HishisHis house was laid. 12Many older priests, Levites, and family leaders cried. They had seen the first temple. When they saw the new foundation, they cried loudly. But many others shouted with joy. 13The people shouted so loudly that no one could tell the happy shouts from the sad cries. The sound was heard far away.
