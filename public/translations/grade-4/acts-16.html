Paul and Timothy

1Paul came to Derbe and Lystra. There, he met a follower named Timothy. Timothy's mother was a Jewish woman who believed in God. His father was a Greek man. 2The believers in Lystra and Iconium said good things about Timothy. 3Paul wanted Timothy to travel with him. So Paul had Timothy circumcised. He did this because of the Jews who lived there. Everyone knew Timothy's father was Greek.

Rules from the Church Leaders

4Paul and his friends went through the cities. They gave the people the rules the church leaders in Jerusalem had decided. They told them to keep these rules. 5The churches became strong in faith. More people joined them every day.

The Holy Spirit Stops Them

6The HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit kept them from telling God's word in Asia. So they went through Phrygia and Galatia. 7They came to Mysia and tried to go to Bithynia. But the SpiritspiritSpirit of Jesus did not let them. 8They passed by Mysia and went down to Troas.

Paul's Vision

9At night, Paul had a vision. He saw a man from Macedonia standing there. The man begged him, "Come over to Macedonia and help us." 10After Paul saw the vision, we quickly tried to go to Macedonia. We knew God had called us to tell the good news there.

Sailing to Philippi

11We sailed from Troas straight to Samothrace. The next day we went to Neapolis. 12From there we went to Philippi. Philippi is the main city in that part of Macedonia. We stayed there for some days.

Lydia Believes

13On the Sabbath day, we went outside the city gate by the river. We thought people prayed there. We sat down and talked to the women who came together. 14A woman named Lydia listened. She sold purple cloth and was from Thyatira. She worshiped God. The Lord opened her heart so she could listen to Paul's words. 15She and her family were baptized. Then she begged us, "If you think I am faithful to the Lord, come and stay at my house." She made us stay.

A Slave Girl Is Healed

16One day we were going to pray. A slave girl met us. She had a spirit that told the future. She made much money for her owners. 17She followed Paul and us. She shouted, "These men are servants of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God. They tell you how to be saved."

18She did this for many days. Paul got upset. He turned and said to the spirit, "In the name of Jesus Christ, I tell you to leave her." The spirit left her right away.

Paul and Silas in Prison

19Her owners saw their hope of money was gone. They grabbed Paul and Silas and dragged them to the leaders in the market. 20They brought them to the city rulers and said, "These men are Jews. They are troubling our city. 21They teach customs that we Romans are not allowed to accept or do."

22The crowd joined against them. The city rulers ordered Paul and Silas to be stripped and beaten with rods. 23After many blows, they threw them into prison. They told the jailer to guard them carefully. 24The jailer put them in the inner prison. He locked their feet in chains.

Prisoners Sing at Midnight

25At midnight, Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God. The other prisoners listened to them. 26Suddenly a big earthquake shook the prison. The doors opened, and everyone's chains came off. 27The jailer woke up. He saw the doors open. He pulled out his sword to kill himself. He thought the prisoners had run away.

28Paul shouted in a loud voice, "Do not hurt yourself! We are all here."

29The jailer called for a light. He ran in and fell trembling before Paul and Silas. 30He brought them out and said, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

31They said, "Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your family." 32They told him and everyone in his house about the Lord.

33That same hour of the night, the jailer took them and washed their wounds. He and his whole family were baptized right away. 34He brought them to his house and set food before them. His whole family was very happy because they believed in God.

Paul and Silas Are Set Free

35When day came, the city rulers sent officers with a message: "Let those men go."

36The jailer told Paul, "ThetheThe rulers sent to release you. Now go in peace."

37But Paul said to them, "They beat us in public. We were not guilty, but they threw us in prison. We are Romans. Now they want to send us away in secret. No! Let them come here and lead us out themselves."

38The officers told the rulers what Paul said. The rulers were afraid when they heard Paul and Silas were Romans. 39They came and said they were sorry. They led Paul and Silas out and asked them to leave the city.

40Paul and Silas left the prison. They went to Lydia's house. They saw the believers there and encouraged them. Then they left.
