A Man Turns Against David

1A troublemaker named Sheba son of Bichri was from Benjamin. He blew a trumpet and said, "We have no part with David. We have no share with Jesse's son. Everyone, go to your own homes, Israel!"

2So all the men of Israel left David and followed Sheba son of Bichri. But the men of Judah stayed with their king from the Jordan River to Jerusalem.

3David came to his house in Jerusalem. The king took the ten women he had left to watch the house. He put them in a guarded house. He gave them food, but he did not sleep with them. They stayed there like widows until they died.

David Calls for Help

4The king said to Amasa, "Call the men of Judah to me in three days. Be here yourself."

5Amasa went to call Judah together. But he took longer than the time David had set.

Joab Goes After Sheba

6David said to Abishai, "Sheba son of Bichri will hurt us more than Absalom did. Take your lord's men and chase him. If he finds a strong city, he may hide there and get away from us."

7Joab's men went with him. The Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the brave men went too. They left Jerusalem to chase Sheba son of Bichri.

Joab Kills Amasa

8They were at the big stone in Gibeon when Amasa met them. Joab had his armourarmourarmor on. He had a sword in its case on his belt. As he stepped forward, the sword fell out.

9Joab said to Amasa, "Are you okay, my brother?" Joab took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him.

10Amasa did not watch the sword in Joab's hand. Joab struck him in the stomach. His insides spilled on the ground. Joab did not strike him again. Amasa died. Then Joab and Abishai chased Sheba son of Bichri.

The People Follow Joab

11One of Joab's young helpers stood by Amasa and said, "Whoever is for Joab, and whoever is for David, follow Joab!"

12Amasa was lying in his blood in the middle of the road. When the man saw that all the people were stopping, he dragged Amasa off the road into a field. He threw a cloth over him.

13When Amasa was moved off the road, everyone went on after Joab to chase Sheba son of Bichri.

Sheba Hides in a City

14Sheba went through all the tribes of Israel to Abel Beth Maacah. The people of Beri gathered and followed him too.

15Joab's men came and trapped him in Abel Beth Maacah. They piled dirt up to the city wall. The wall stood firm. All the people with Joab worked to knock the wall down.

A Wise Woman Speaks

16A wise woman called from the city, "Listen! Listen! Please tell Joab to come here. I want to talk to you."

17Joab came near her. The woman asked, "Are you Joab?"

He said, "I am."

She said, "Listen to your servant."

He said, "I am listening."

18She said, "In the old days people said, 'Ask advice at Abel,' and that settled things."

19She said, "I am one of the peaceful, faithful people of Israel. You are trying to destroy a city that is important in Israel. Why would you destroy what belongs to the LORD?"

20Joab said, "No! I do not want to destroy or ruin anything!"

21He said, "That is not it. A man named Sheba son of Bichri is from the hills of Ephraim. He has rebelled against King David. Hand him over, and I will leave the city."

The woman said to Joab, "HishisHis head will be thrown to you over the wall."

22The woman went to all the people with her wise words. They cut off Sheba's head and threw it to Joab. He blew the trumpet. The men left the city and went home. Joab went back to the king in Jerusalem.

David's Officials

23Joab was over all the army of Israel. Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites.

24Adoram was over the forced work. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the record keeper.

25Sheva was the writer. Zadok and Abiathar were the priests.

26Ira the Jairite was also a priest for David.
