How Good It Is When Brothers Live in Peace

1This is a song David wrote for going up to worship. Look, it is good and nice when brothers live together in peace. 2It is like special oil poured on the head, running down the beard, even Aaron's beard, down to the edge of his clothes. 3It is like dew from Mount Hermon falling on the hills of Zion. There the LORD gives HishisHis blessing, life that lasts forever.
