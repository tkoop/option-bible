Paul Goes to Corinth

1After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth. 2There he found a Jewish man named Aquila. Aquila was from Pontus. He had just come from Italy with his wife Priscilla. Claudius had told all Jews to leave Rome. 3Paul stayed with them and worked. They made tents. 4Every Sabbath day, Paul talked in the meeting place. He tried to convince Jews and Greeks. 5When Silas and Timothy came from Macedonia, Paul gave all his time to telling God's message. He told the Jews that Jesus is the Christ.

6But they argued and said bad things. Paul shook his clothes and said, "YouyouYou are to blame for what happens to you! I am not to blame. From now on, I will go to people who are not Jews."

Paul Stays in Corinth

7Paul left that place and went into the house of a man named Justus. Justus worshiped God. His house was next to the meeting place. 8Crispus was the leader of the meeting place. He believed in the Lord. His whole family believed too. Many people in Corinth heard Paul. They believed and were baptized.

The Lord Helps Paul

9One night, the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision. He said, "Do not be afraid. Keep speaking. Do not stop. I am with you. 10No one will hurt you. I have many people in this city." 11Paul stayed there for a year and a half. He taught God's word to the people.

Paul Goes to Court

12When Gallio was the Roman governor of Achaia, the Jews came together against Paul. They took him to court. 13They said, "This man teaches people to worship God in a way that breaks the law."

14Paul was about to speak. But Gallio said to the Jews, "If this were a real crime, I would listen to you. 15But these are questions about words, names, and your law. You take care of them. I do not want to be the judge." 16He sent them away from the court. 17Then everyone grabbed Sosthenes, the leader of the meeting place. They beat him in front of the court. Gallio did not care.

Paul Leaves Corinth

18Paul stayed many more days. Then he said goodbye to the believers. In Cenchreae he had cut his hair because he had made a promise to God. Then he sailed to Syria. Priscilla and Aquila went with him.

Paul Visits Ephesus

19They came to Ephesus. Paul left Priscilla and Aquila there. He went into the meeting place and talked with the Jews. 20They asked him to stay longer. He said no. 21He said goodbye and said, "I will come back to you if God wants." Then he sailed from Ephesus. 22He landed at Caesarea. He went up and greeted the church. Then he went down to Antioch. 23After some time, he left. He travelledtravelledtraveled through Galatia and Phrygia. He helped all the followers be strong.

Apollos Comes to Ephesus

24A Jew named Apollos came to Ephesus. He was from Alexandria. He was a good speaker and knew the ScripturesscripturesScriptures well. 25He had learned the way of the Lord. He was excited in spirit. He spoke and taught true things about Jesus. But he only knew John's baptism. 26He began to speak boldly in the meeting place. Priscilla and Aquila heard him. They took him aside and explained God's way better. 27Apollos wanted to go to Achaia. The believers encouraged him. They wrote to the followers there to welcome him. When he arrived, he helped many people. They had believed by God's grace. 28He proved to the Jews in public that Jesus is the Christ. He used the ScripturesscripturesScriptures.
