God's City on Holy Mountains

1This is a song by the sons of Korah. God's city rests on holy mountains. 2The LORD loves the gates of Zion. He loves them more than all the homes of Jacob. 3People say wonderful things about you, city of God. Selah

People from Every Nation

4I will name Rahab and Babylon to those who know MemeMe. Look! Philistia, Tyre, and Cush— "This one was born there."

5People will say about Zion, "This one and that one were born in her." The MostmostMost HighhighHigh HimselfhimselfHimself will make her strong.

6The LORD will write in the book of the nations, "This one was born there." Selah

All Joy Comes from God's City

7Singers and dancers will say, "All my joy comes from you."
