Ezra's Family Line

1After this, while Artaxerxes was king of Persia, there was a man named Ezra. He was the son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah, 2son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub, 3son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth, 4son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki, 5son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest.

Ezra Travels to Jerusalem

6This Ezra came up from Babylon. He was a scribe who knew the LawlawLaw of Moses well. The LORD, the God of Israel, had given that LawlawLaw. The king gave Ezra everything he asked for, because the the LORD his God was helping him. 7In the seventh year of King Artaxerxes, Ezra went up to Jerusalem. Some people of Israel went with him. There were priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants. 8He got to Jerusalem in the fifth month of the seventh year of the king. 9Ezra left Babylon on the first day of the first month. He got to Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month. His God was good to him and helped him on the way. 10Ezra had made up his mind to study the LawlawLaw of the the LORD, to obey it, and to teach its rules and laws to the people of Israel.

The King's Letter

11This is a copy of the letter King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest and scribe. Ezra was a scribe of the the LORD's commands and laws for Israel.

12"Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven: Peace! Now I make this rule: 13Any of the people of Israel, priests, and Levites in my kingdom who want to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

14You are sent by the king and his seven helpers to check on Judah and Jerusalem. Use the LawlawLaw of your God that you hold. 15You are to take the silver and gold that the king and his helpers freely gave to the God of Israel, whose home is in Jerusalem. 16Take all the silver and gold you find in the land of Babylon. Take the gifts that the people and priests freely give for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

17Use this money to buy bulls, rams, and lambs. Buy grain and drink for offerings. Offer them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. 18You and your brothers may do what seems good to you with the rest of the silver and gold. Do it the way your God wants. 19Give the items that were given to you for the house of your God to the God in Jerusalem. 20If you need anything else for the house of your God, take it from the king's treasury.

21I, King Artaxerxes, make this rule for all the treasurers beyond the river: Give Ezra, the priest and scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, everything he asks for. Do it quickly. 22Give up to 100 talents of silver, 100 baskets of wheat, 100 jars of wine, 100 jars of oil, and as much salt as is needed. 23Do everything the God of Heavenheavenheaven commands for the house of the God of Heavenheavenheaven. Then no anger will come on the king's kingdom or his sons.

24We also tell you that no tax, fee, or toll may be taken from the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or workers of this house of God. 25And you, Ezra, use the wisdom God gives you to choose judges and leaders. They will judge all the people beyond the river who know God's Laws. Teach those who do not know. 26Anyone who does not obey the LawlawLaw of your God or the king's law must be punished. They may be put to death, sent away, lose their things, or be put in prison."

Ezra Praises God

27Ezra said, "Praise the the LORD, the God of our fathers! 28He put this in the king's heart to make the the LORD's house in Jerusalem beautiful. He was kind to me before the king, his helpers, and all the king's powerful leaders. I was strong because the the LORD my God was helping me. I gathered the leaders of Israel to go with me."
