Amaziah Becomes King of Judah

1In the second year that Joash son of Jehoahaz was king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash became king of Judah. 2He was 25 years old when he began to rule. He ruled 29 years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

3Amaziah did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD. But he was not like David his father. He did what his father Joash did. 4But he did not take away the high places. The people still gave offerings and burned incense there.

5When his power was strong, he killed the servants who had killed his father the king. 6But he did not kill their children. He followed what is written in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses. The the LORD had told them, "Fathers must not die for their children. Children must not die for their fathers. Each person dies for his own sin."

7Amaziah killed 10,000 men from Edom in the SaltsaltSalt ValleyvalleyValley. He took the city of Sela in war. He named it Joktheel. That name is still used today.

Amaziah Challenges Israel

8Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel. He said, "Come. Let us meet face to face."

9Jehoash king of Israel sent a message back to Amaziah king of Judah. He said, "YouyouYou are like a small thorn bush, and I am like a big cedar tree. A wild animal can crush a small bush. 10You beat Edom, and now your heart is proud. Enjoy your win and stay home. Why start trouble? You will fall, and Judah will fall with you."

11But Amaziah did not listen. So Jehoash king of Israel went up. He and Amaziah king of Judah faced each other at Beth Shemesh in Judah. 12Judah lost the battle. Every man ran home.

13Jehoash king of Israel caught Amaziah king of Judah, the son of Joash and grandson of Ahaziah, at Beth Shemesh. He took him to Jerusalem. He broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim GategateGate to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate. That was about 400 cubits. 14He took all the gold and silver. He took all the things found in the house of the the LORD and in the king's store rooms. He took people captive too. Then he went back to Samaria.

15The rest of what Jehoash did is written in the book of the history of the kings of Israel. It tells about his power and his war with Amaziah king of Judah. 16Jehoash died and was buried with his fathers in Samaria. His son Jeroboam became king after him.

17Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived 15 years after Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel died. 18The rest of what Amaziah did is written in the book of the history of the kings of Judah.

19Some men made a plan against Amaziah in Jerusalem. He ran to Lachish. They sent men after him, and they killed him there. 20They carried his body on horses. He was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the CitycityCity of David.

21All the people of Judah took Azariah. He was 16 years old. They made him king instead of his father Amaziah. 22Azariah built the town of Elath and took it back for Judah. This happened after King Amaziah died.

Jeroboam Rules Israel

23Amaziah son of Joash was king of Judah for 15 years when Jeroboam son of Joash became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled 41 years. 24He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He did not stop doing the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat. That Jeroboam had led Israel to sin.

25Jeroboam took back the land of Israel from Lebo Hamath to the SeaseaSea of the Arabah. He did this as the the LORD God of Israel said through HishisHis servant Jonah son of Amittai. Jonah was the prophet from Gath Hepher.

26The ThetheThe LORD saw how much Israel suffered. Everyone was in trouble. No one was left to help Israel.
