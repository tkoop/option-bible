God Asks About Wild Goats and Deer

1"Do you know when mountain goats have their babies? Do you watch the deer have her fawn? 2Do you count the months they carry? Do you know when they give birth? 3They crouch down and have their young. They send their babies away. 4Their young get strong. They grow up in the wild. They leave and do not come back."

God Asks About the Wild Donkey

5"Who let the wild donkey go free? Who untied its ropes? 6I gave it the dry land for its home. I gave it the salty flats for its place. 7It laughs at the noise of the town. It does not hear the driver shout. 8It looks for food in the hills. It follows every green plant."

God Asks About the Wild Ox

9"Will the wild ox work for you? Will it stay by your feeding trough? 10Can you tie it with a rope to make it plow? Will it plow the fields for you? 11Will you trust it because it is strong? Will you leave your hard work to it? 12Will you trust it to bring your grain home and pile it up for you?"

God Asks About the Ostrich

13"The ostrich flaps its wings with joy. But its wings are not like a stork's feathers. 14It leaves its eggs on the ground. It warms them in the dust. 15It forgets that a foot may crush them. A wild animal may step on them. 16It is hard on its young, as if they were not its own. Its work is wasted. It is not afraid. 17God did not give it wisdom. He did not give it understanding. 18But when it runs with its wings spread, it laughs at the horse and rider."

God Asks About the Horse

19"Do you give the horse its strength? Do you put a mane on its neck? 20Do you make it jump like a grasshopper? Its proud snorting scares people. 21It paws the ground in the valley. It is glad in its strength. It runs into battle. 22It is not afraid. It does not turn back from the sword. 23Arrows rattle against it. The spear and javelin flash. 24It runs so fast it seems to eat the ground. It cannot stand still when it hears the trumpet. 25When the trumpet sounds, it says, 'Ha!' From far away it smells war. It hears the thunder of leaders and shouting."

God Asks About the Hawk and Eagle

26"Does the hawk fly because you are wise? Does it spread its wings to the south? 27Does the eagle fly high because you tell it to? Does it build its nest up high? 28It lives on the cliff. It stays on the rock. 29From there it looks for food. Its eyes see far away. 30Its young drink blood. Where dead bodies are, there it is."
