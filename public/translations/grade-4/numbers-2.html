God Gives Camp Rules

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said, 2"ThetheThe people of Israel must camp around the tent of meeting. They must stay a little way back from it. Each group must camp under its own flag, with the sign of its family group.

The East Side Camps

3"On the east side, toward the sunrise, Judah must camp with its army. Nahshon son of Amminadab is the leader of Judah's people. 4His army has seventy-four thousand six hundred men. 5Issachar camps next to him. Nethanel son of Zuar is their leader. 6His army has fifty-four thousand four hundred men. 7Zebulun camps there too. Eliab son of Helon is their leader. 8His army has fifty-seven thousand four hundred men. 9All the men in Judah's camp total one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred. They must move out first.

The South Side Camps

10"On the south side, Reuben must camp with its army. Elizur son of Shedeur is their leader. 11His army has forty-six thousand five hundred men. 12Simeon camps next to him. Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai is their leader. 13His army has fifty-nine thousand three hundred men. 14Gad camps there too. Eliasaph son of Reuel is their leader. 15His army has forty-five thousand six hundred fifty men. 16All the men in Reuben's camp total one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty. They must move out second.

The Tent in the Middle

17"The tent of meeting, with the Levites, must travel in the middle of the camps. They must move out the same way they camp. Each group must stay in its place under its own flag.

The West Side Camps

18"On the west side, Ephraim must camp with its army. Elishama son of Ammihud is their leader. 19His army has forty thousand five hundred men. 20Manasseh camps next to him. Gamaliel son of Pedahzur is their leader. 21His army has thirty-two thousand two hundred men. 22Benjamin camps there too. Abidan son of Gideoni is their leader. 23His army has thirty-five thousand four hundred men. 24All the men in Ephraim's camp total one hundred eight thousand one hundred. They must move out third.

The North Side Camps

25"On the north side, Dan must camp with its army. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai is their leader. 26His army has sixty-two thousand seven hundred men. 27Asher camps next to him. Pagiel son of Ocran is their leader. 28His army has forty-one thousand five hundred men. 29Naphtali camps there too. Ahira son of Enan is their leader. 30His army has fifty-three thousand four hundred men. 31All the men in Dan's camp total one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They must move out last, under their flags.

All the People Counted

32"These are the people of Israel, counted by their families. All the people in the camps total six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty. 33The Levites were not counted with the people of Israel, because the LORD told Moses not to count them. 34The people of Israel did everything the LORD told Moses. They camped under their flags, and they moved out. Each man went with his family group and his father's house."
