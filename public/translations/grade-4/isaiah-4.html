A Hard Time Is Coming

1On that day, seven women will hold on to one man. They will say, "We will eat our own bread. We will wear our own clothes. Just let your name be on us. Take away our shame."

The LORD's Branch Will Shine

2In that day, the LORD's BranchbranchBranch will be beautiful and great. The fruit of the land will be the joy and honourhonourhonor of Israel's people who are left. 3Everyone left in Zion and Jerusalem will be called holy. This is for everyone whose name is written down for life in Jerusalem.

4The Lord will wash away the dirt of Zion's daughters. He will clean the blood from Jerusalem. He will do this with fairness and fire.

5Then the LORD will make a cloud and smoke by day over every home on Mount Zion and over their groups. At night, there will be a shining fire. Over all the glory, there will be a cover. 6It will be a shade from the hot sun by day. It will be a safe place and a hiding place from storm and rain.
