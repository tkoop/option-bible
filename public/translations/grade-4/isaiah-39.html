Hezekiah Shows His Treasures

1At that time, Merodach-Baladan, son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah. He had heard that Hezekiah was sick and got well. 2Hezekiah was happy to see them. He showed them his treasure room. He showed them silver, gold, spices, and fine oil. He also showed them his weapons and everything in his storage rooms. He did not hide anything in his house or in all his land.

3Then Isaiah the prophet went to King Hezekiah. He asked, "What did those men say? Where did they come from?"

Hezekiah said, "They came from far away. They came from Babylon."

4Isaiah asked, "What did they see in your house?"

Hezekiah said, "They saw everything in my house. I did not keep anything back from them."

Isaiah Warns Hezekiah

5Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Listen to what the LORD of Armies says. 6Days are coming. Everything in your house will be carried to Babylon. So will the things your fathers saved up until now. Nothing will be left," says the LORD. 7"Some of your children, your own sons who will be born to you, will be taken away. They will be servants in the palace of the king of Babylon."

8Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "ThetheThe word of the LORD that you spoke is good." He thought, "There will be peace and safety while I live."
