The Water Goes Down

1But God remembered Noah. He remembered every living thing and all the animals in the boat with Noah. So God sent a wind over the earth. The water went down. 2The springs under the earth and the windows in the sky closed. The rain stopped. 3The water kept going down. After one hundred fifty days, there was less water. 4On the seventeenth day of the seventh month, the boat came to rest on the mountains of Ararat. 5The water kept going down until the tenth month. On the first day of the tenth month, the tops of the mountains could be seen.

Noah Sends Birds

6After forty days, Noah opened the window he had made in the boat. 7He sent out a raven. It flew back and forth until the water dried up on the earth. 8Then he sent out a dove to see if the water had gone down. 9The dove could not find a place to rest. So it came back to him in the boat. The water was still over the whole EarthEarthearth. Noah reached out his hand. He took the dove and brought it back into the boat. 10He waited seven more days. Then he sent the dove out again. 11In the evening, the dove came back. It had a fresh olive leaf in its mouth. Then Noah knew the water had gone down on the earth. 12He waited seven more days. Then he sent the dove out again. This time it did not come back.

The Ground Is Dry

13In the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, on the first day of the first month, the water had dried up. Noah took off the cover of the boat. He looked out and saw that the ground was dry. 14By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was dry.

God Tells Noah to Leave

15Then God spoke to Noah. 16He said, "Come out of the boat. You and your wife, your sons and their wives, come out too. 17Bring out every living thing that is with you. Bring out the birds, the animals, and all the small animals that move on the ground. They will spread out over the earth. They will have babies and grow in number."

18So Noah came out with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives. 19All the animals, all the small animals that move, and all the birds came out of the boat. They came out by their families.

Noah Offers a Gift to God

20Then Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took some of the clean animals and some of the clean birds. He burned them on the altar as a gift to the LORD. 21The LORD smelled the nice smell. The LORD said in HishisHis heart, "I will never put a curse on the ground again because of people. People's thoughts are evil from when they are young. I will never again destroy all living things the way I did." 22As long as the earth is here, there will be planting time and harvest time. There will be cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night. They will not stop.
