A Message for the Philistines

1The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet. It was about the Philistines. This was before Pharaoh attacked Gaza.

2This is what the LORD says: "An army is coming from the north. It will be like a big flood. It will cover the land and everything in it. It will cover the city and the people living there. The people will cry out. Everyone in the land will wail. 3They will hear the loud sound of horses' hoofs. They will hear chariots rushing and wheels rumbling. Fathers will not even look back to help their children. They will be too weak.

4"This will happen on the day the LORD destroys all the Philistines. He will cut off Tyre and Sidon and every helper left. The LORD is destroying the Philistines, the people left from Caphtor. 5Gaza will be ruined. Ashkelon will be destroyed. You who are left in the valley, how long will you keep mourning?

6"Oh, sword of the LORD! How long until you stop? Go back into your sheath. Rest and be still! 7How can it be quiet when the LORD has given it a job? He has set it against Ashkelon and the coast."
