Tola and Jair Lead Israel

1After Abimelech, a man from Issachar named Tola came to save Israel. Tola was the son of Puah and the grandson of Dodo. He lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim. 2He judged Israel for twenty-three years. Then he died and was buried in Shamir. 3After him, Jair from Gilead came up. He judged Israel for twenty-two years. 4He had thirty sons. They rode on thirty donkeys. They had thirty towns in Gilead. People still call those towns Havvoth Jair today. 5Jair died and was buried in Kamon.

Israel Turns to Other Gods

6The Israelites did wrong again in the eyes of the LORD. They served the Baals and the Ashtoreths. They also served the gods of Aram, Sidon, Moab, Ammon, and Philistia. They left the LORD and did not serve HimhimHim. 7The LORD was angry with Israel. He let the Philistines and the Ammonites take charge of them. 8For eighteen years, they crushed and hurt the Israelites who lived east of the Jordan River in Gilead, in the land of the Amorites. 9The Ammonites crossed the Jordan River to fight Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim. The people of Israel were very upset.

Israel Cries to the LORD

10The Israelites cried to the LORD and said, "We have sinned against you. We have left our God and served the Baals."

The LORD Answers Israel

11The LORD said to the Israelites, "Didn't I save you from the Egyptians, the Amorites, the Ammonites, and the Philistines? 12The Sidonians, the Amalekites, and the Maonites also hurt you. You cried to MemeMe, and I saved you from their power. 13But you left MemeMe and served other gods. So I will not save you again. 14Go and cry to the gods you chose. Let them save you when you are in trouble."

15The Israelites said to the LORD, "We have sinned. Do to us whatever you think is best. But please save us today."

16They put away the foreign gods from among them. They served the LORD. He felt sorry for Israel because they were hurting.

Israel Gets Ready to Fight

17The Ammonites were called together and camped in Gilead. The Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah. 18The leaders of Gilead said to each other, "Who will start the fight against the Ammonites? He will be the leader of all who live in Gilead."
