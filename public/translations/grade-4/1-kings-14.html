Jeroboam's Son Gets Sick

1At that time, Jeroboam's son Abijah got sick. 2Jeroboam said to his wife, "Please get up and change how you look. No one must know you are Jeroboam's wife. Go to Shiloh. The prophet Ahijah is there. He is the one who said I would be king over this people. 3Take ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey with you. Go to him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy."

4Jeroboam's wife did what he said. She got up and went to Shiloh. She came to Ahijah's house. Ahijah could not see, because his eyes were weak from old age.

The LORD Warns Jeroboam

5The LORD had told Ahijah, "Look, Jeroboam's wife is coming to ask you about her son, because he is sick. Say this and that to her. When she comes in, she will pretend to be someone else."

6When Ahijah heard her footsteps at the door, he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why do you pretend to be someone else? I have a hard message for you."

7"Go and tell Jeroboam that the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: I picked you up from among the people. I made you ruler over MymyMy people Israel. 8I tore the kingdom away from David's family and gave it to you. But you have not been like MymyMy servant David. He obeyed MymyMy commands. He followed MemeMe with all his heart. He did only what was right in MymyMy eyes. 9You have done more evil than all who came before you. You went and made other gods for yourself. You made metal gods to make MemeMe angry. You turned your back on MemeMe.

10"So I will bring trouble to Jeroboam's family. I will destroy every male in Jeroboam's family, both slave and free, in Israel. I will burn up Jeroboam's family and sweep it away like trash until it is gone. 11Dogs will eat anyone from Jeroboam's family who dies in the city. Birds of the sky will eat anyone who dies in the field. The LORD has spoken.

12"As for you, get up and go home. When your feet enter the city, the child will die. 13All Israel will mourn for him and bury him. He is the only one of Jeroboam's family who will be buried. This is because the LORD, the God of Israel, found something good in him among Jeroboam's family.

14"The LORD will raise up a king over Israel who will destroy Jeroboam's family. That will happen even today. Yes, even now. 15The LORD will strike Israel. They will be like a reed shaking in the water. He will pull Israel up from this good land HeheHe gave their ancestors. He will scatter them beyond the Euphrates River. This is because they made poles for the false goddess Asherah. They made the LORD angry. 16He will give Israel up because of the sins of Jeroboam. Jeroboam sinned and made Israel sin too."

17Then Jeroboam's wife got up and left. She went to Tirzah. As she stepped over the doorstep of the house, the boy died.

18They buried him, and all Israel mourned for him. This happened just as the LORD had said through HishisHis servant Ahijah the prophet.

Jeroboam's Death

19The rest of the things Jeroboam did, including his wars and his rule, are written in the records of the kings of Israel. 20Jeroboam ruled for twenty-two years. Then he died and was buried with his fathers. his son Nadab became king after him.

Rehoboam Rules Judah

21Rehoboam son of Solomon ruled in Judah. he was forty-one years old when he became king. he ruled seventeen years in Jerusalem. This is the city the LORD chose from all the tribes of Israel to put HishisHis name there. his mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. 22Judah did evil in the sight of the LORD. They sinned more than their fathers had done. They made the LORD jealous. 23They also built high places, stone pillars, and poles for the false goddess Asherah on every high hill and under every green tree. 24There were also men who sold sex at the worship places in the land. The people did all the terrible things the other nations did, the nations the LORD had driven out before the Israelites.

Shishak Attacks Jerusalem

25In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem. 26he took the treasures from the LORD's temple and the king's palace. he took everything. he took all the gold shields Solomon had made. 27King Rehoboam made bronze shields to take their place. he put them in the care of the guards who watched the entrance to the king's palace. 28Whenever the king went to the LORD's temple, the guards carried the shields. Then they brought them back to the guard room.

29The rest of Rehoboam's acts and all he did are written in the records of the kings of Judah. 30There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all their days.

Rehoboam's Death

31Rehoboam died and was buried with his fathers. his mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. his son Abijam became king after him.
