The Altar for Burnt Offerings

1He made the altar for burnt offerings from acacia wood. It was square. It was 5 cubits long and 5 cubits wide. It was 3 cubits high. 2He made horns on its four corners. The horns were part of the altar. He covered the altar with bronze. 3He made all the altar's bronze tools. These were pots, shovels, bowls, forks, and fire pans. He made every tool for it. 4He made a bronze grate like a net. It went under the ledge around the altar. It reached halfway up. 5He put four bronze rings on the four corners of the grate. The rings held poles. 6He made poles from acacia wood. He covered them with bronze. 7He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the altar. The poles were used to carry it. He made the altar hollow. It was made with boards.

The Bronze Basin

8He made the bronze basin and its bronze stand. He made them from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

The Courtyard

9He made the courtyard. The south side was 100 cubits long. It had curtains of fine twisted linen. 10It had 20 posts and 20 bronze bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 11The north side was also 100 cubits long. It had 20 posts and 20 bronze bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 12The west side was 50 cubits long. It had 10 curtains, 10 posts, and 10 bases. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. 13The east side, toward the sunrise, was 50 cubits long. 14On one side of the entrance, the curtains were 15 cubits long. They had 3 posts and 3 bases. 15On the other side, the curtains were also 15 cubits long. They had 3 posts and 3 bases. 16All the curtains around the courtyard were made of fine twisted linen. 17The bases for the posts were bronze. The hooks and bands on the posts were silver. The tops of the posts were covered with silver. All the posts of the courtyard had silver bands. 18The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was made with needlework. It was blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine twisted linen. It was 20 cubits long and 5 cubits high. It was like the courtyard curtains. 19It had 4 posts and 4 bronze bases. The hooks, bands, and tops of the posts were silver. 20All the tent pegs for the holy tent and the courtyard were bronze.

The Materials Used

21This is the list of things for the holy tent, the holy tent where God's law was kept. Moses had them counted. The Levites did the work under Ithamar son of Aaron the priest. 22Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah, made everything the the LORD commanded Moses. 23Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, helped him. He was a skilled worker and designer. He worked with blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen.

24All the gold used for the work, the gold given as an offering, was 29 talents and 730 shekels. It was weighed by the holy shekel. 25The silver from the people who were counted was 100 talents and 1,775 shekels. It was weighed by the holy shekel. 26This was half a shekel for each person. It was for everyone 20 years old and older who was counted. There were 603,550 men.

27The 100 talents of silver were used to make the bases for the holy tent and the bases for the inner curtain. There were 100 bases. Each base used one talent. 28The 1,775 shekels were used to make hooks for the posts, to cover the tops of the posts, and to band them. 29The bronze given as an offering was 70 talents and 2,400 shekels. 30With it he made the bases for the entrance to the tent of meeting, the bronze altar, the bronze grate for it, and all the altar's tools. 31He also made the bases around the courtyard, the bases for the courtyard gate, and all the tent pegs for the holy tent and the courtyard.
