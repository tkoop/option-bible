The Bronze Altar and the Sea

1Solomon made a bronze altar. It was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high. 2He also made the SeaseaSea out of cast metal. It was round. It was ten cubits from one side to the other. It was five cubits high. A line thirty cubits long went all the way around it.

3Below the rim, there were bulls all around it. There were ten bulls in each cubit. The bulls were in two rows. They were cast as part of the SeaseaSea. 4The SeaseaSea stood on twelve bulls. Three bulls faced north. Three faced west. Three faced south. Three faced east. The SeaseaSea was on top of them. Their backs faced the middle. 5The SeaseaSea was as thick as a hand is wide. Its rim was like the rim of a cup. It looked like a lily flower. It held three thousand baths.

6Solomon made ten basins for washing. He put five on the right side and five on the left side. The priests washed things for burnt offerings in them. The priests washed in the SeaseaSea.

Lampstands, Tables, and Bowls

7He made ten gold lampstands. They were made the way they should be. He put them in the temple. Five were on the right side and five on the left side. 8He made ten tables. He put them in the temple. Five were on the right side and five on the left side. He also made one hundred gold bowls for sprinkling.

The Courtyard

9He made the priests' courtyard. He also made the big courtyard and its doors. He covered the doors with bronze. 10He put the SeaseaSea on the southeast side. It was on the south side toward the east.

Huram's Work

11Huram made the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. Huram finished the work he had started for King Solomon in the house of God. 12He made two pillars. He made two bowl-shaped tops for the pillars. He made two sets of net-like work to cover the two bowl-shaped tops on the pillars. 13He made four hundred pomegranates for the two sets of net-like work. There were two rows of pomegranates on each net. They covered the two bowl-shaped tops on the pillars. 14He made the stands and the basins that went on them. 15He made the SeaseaSea and the twelve bulls under it. 16Huram-abi made the pots, shovels, forks, and all their tools for King Solomon for the house of the LORD. They were made of polished bronze. 17The king had them cast in clay molds in the plain of Jordan. This was between Succoth and Zeredah. 18Solomon made so many of these things that no one could weigh all the bronze.
