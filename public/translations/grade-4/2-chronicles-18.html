Jehoshaphat Joins Ahab

1Jehoshaphat had a lot of riches and honourhonourhonor. He joined his family to Ahab's family.

2After some years, he went down to Ahab in Samaria. Ahab killed many sheep and cattle for him and the people with him. Ahab urged him to go up to Ramoth Gilead.

3Ahab, king of Israel, said to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, "WillwillWill you go with me to Ramoth Gilead?"

Jehoshaphat said, "I am like you. My people are like your people. I will be with you in the war."

4Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Please ask what the the LORD says today."

The Prophets Answer

5The king of Israel gathered four hundred prophets. He said to them, "Should we go to war against Ramoth Gilead, or should I stay home?"

They said, "Go up. God will give it to the king."

6Jehoshaphat said, "IsisIs there still a prophet of the the LORD here? We should ask him."

7The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is one more man. We can ask the the LORD through him. But I hate him. He never says good things about me. He always says bad things. His name is Micaiah son of Imla."

Jehoshaphat said, "Don't say that, king."

8The king of Israel called an officer and said, "BringbringBring Micaiah son of Imla quickly."

The Kings Sit and Listen

9The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, each sat on his throne. They wore royal robes. They sat at the threshing floor by the gate of Samaria. All the prophets spoke in front of them.

10Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns for himself. He said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'With these horns you will push the Arameans until they are destroyed.'"

11All the prophets said the same thing: "Go up to Ramoth Gilead and win. The the LORD will give it to the king."

Micaiah Is Brought In

12The messenger who went to get Micaiah said to him, "Look, all the prophets say the same good thing to the king. Please say the same thing they say. Speak good words too."

13Micaiah said, "As surely as the the LORD lives, I will speak only what my God says."

14When Micaiah came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, should we go to war against Ramoth Gilead, or should I stay home?"

Micaiah said, "Go up and win. They will be given into your hand."

15The king said to him, "How many times must I make you swear to tell me only the truth in the name of the the LORD?"

16Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains like sheep without a shepherd. The the LORD said, 'These people have no master. Let each one go home in peace.'"

17ThetheThe king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Didn't I tell you he would not say good things about me, but bad things?"

Micaiah's Vision

18Micaiah said, "So listen to the word of the the LORD. I saw the the LORD sitting on HishisHis throne. All the armies of Heavenheavenheaven stood on HishisHis right and on HishisHis left."

19The the LORD said, "Who will trick Ahab, king of Israel, into going up and dying at Ramoth Gilead?" One said one thing. Another said another.

20Then a spirit came out and stood before the the LORD. He said, "I will trick him."

The the LORD said to him, "How?"

21The spirit said, "I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouths of all his prophets."

The the LORD said, "YouyouYou will trick him. You will also succeed. Go out and do it."

22Micaiah said, "Now look, the the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouths of these prophets of yours. The the LORD has said bad things will happen to you."

23Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came close and hit Micaiah on the cheek. He said, "Which way did the the LORD's SpiritspiritSpirit go from me to speak to you?"

24Micaiah said, "YouyouYou will see. On that day you will hide in an inner room."

25The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and send him back to Amon, the ruler of the city, and to Joash the king's son."

26He said, "Say this: 'The king says, Put this man in prison. Feed him only a little bread and water until I come back safe.'"

27Micaiah said, "If you come back safe, the the LORD did not speak through me. Listen, everyone!"

The Battle at Ramoth Gilead

28So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, went up to Ramoth Gilead.

29The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will dress like someone else and go into battle. But you wear your own clothes." So the king of Israel dressed like someone else, and they went into battle.

30The king of Aram had told his chariot captains, "Don't fight the small soldiers or the big leaders. Fight only the king of Israel."

31When the chariot captains saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "That is the king of Israel." They turned to fight him. Jehoshaphat shouted for help. The the LORD helped him. God made them turn away from him.

32When the chariot captains saw it was not the king of Israel, they stopped chasing him.

33A man shot an arrow without aiming. He hit the king of Israel between the pieces of his armourarmourarmor. The king said to his driver, "Turn around and take me out of the battle. I am hurt."

34The battle got worse that day. The king of Israel stayed in his chariot facing Aram until evening. He died at sunset.
