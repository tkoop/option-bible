The Queen of Sheba Visits Solomon

1The queen of Sheba heard about Solomon. People said he was famous because of the the LORD. So she came to test him with hard questions. 2She came to Jerusalem with a very big group. Her camels carried spices, much gold, and precious stones. She went to Solomon and told him everything on her mind. 3Solomon answered all her questions. Nothing was too hard for him to explain. 4The queen saw Solomon’s wisdom and the house he built. 5She saw his food, his servants, his helpers, their clothes, his drink servers, and his burnt offering at the the LORD’s house. After that, she was so amazed she could hardly speak.

6She said, “What I heard in my own land about your words and wisdom is true. 7I did not believe it until I came and saw it with my own eyes. People did not even tell me half of it. Your wisdom and good life are much greater than the stories I heard.

8“How happy your people are! How happy are the servants who stand before you all the time and hear your wisdom! 9Praise be to the the LORD your God. He was pleased with you and put you on Israel’s throne. Because the the LORD loves Israel forever, HeheHe made you king to do what is right and fair.”

10She gave the king 120 talents of gold. She also gave many spices and precious stones. No one ever brought so many spices again. 11Hiram’s ships brought gold from Ophir. They also brought much rare wood called almug and precious stones from Ophir. 12The king used the almug wood to make supports for the the LORD’s house and for his own house. He also made lyres and harps for the singers. No one had seen such wood before or since. 13King Solomon gave the queen everything she wanted. He also gave her gifts from his royal treasure. Then she and her servants went back to her land.

Solomon’s Great Wealth

14Each year Solomon got 666 talents of gold. 15This was besides money from traders, merchants, and all the kings of Arabia and the governors of the land. 16Solomon made 200 large shields of gold. Each one used 600 shekels of gold. 17He made 300 small shields of gold. Each one used 3 minas of gold. He put them in the House of the ForestforestForest of Lebanon. 18The king made a big throne of ivory and covered it with pure gold. 19The throne had six steps. The back was round. There were armrests on both sides. Two lions stood by the armrests. 20Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one on each side of each step. No other kingdom had a throne like it.

21All of Solomon’s drinking cups were gold. The cups in the House of the ForestforestForest of Lebanon were gold too. In Solomon’s time, silver was not worth much. 22The king had a fleet of ships. Every three years they brought gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks. 23Solomon became greater than all the kings on EarthEarthearth in riches and wisdom. 24People from all over the world came to see Solomon. They wanted to hear the wisdom God put in his heart.

People Bring Gifts to Solomon

25Every year each man brought a gift. The gifts were silver and gold things, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules.

Solomon’s Horses and Chariots

26Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He kept them in chariot cities and in Jerusalem. 27In Jerusalem, silver was as common as stones. Cedar wood was as common as sycamore trees in the lowlands. 28Solomon got horses from Egypt and Kue. His traders bought them from Kue. 29A chariot from Egypt cost 600 pieces of silver. A horse cost 150 pieces of silver. Solomon’s traders sold them to the Hittite and Aramean kings.
