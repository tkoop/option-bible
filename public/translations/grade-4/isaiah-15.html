A Sad Message for Moab

1A message about Moab. The city of Ar in Moab is ruined. It is destroyed in one night. The city of Kir in Moab is ruined. It is destroyed in one night too. 2The people of Dibon go up to their temple to cry. They cry at the high places. Moab cries loudly over Nebo and Medeba. All the men shave their heads. All the men cut off their beards.

3In the streets they wear rough cloth. On the roofs and in the squares, everyone cries loudly. They fall down and weep. 4Heshbon and Elealeh cry out. Their voices are heard as far as Jahaz. So the soldiers of Moab cry out. They are scared deep inside.

Moab's People Run Away

5My heart cries out for Moab. Her people who run away go as far as Zoar. They go as far as Eglath-shelishiyah. On the road up to Luhith, they cry as they walk. On the road to Horonaim, they shout that they are being destroyed. 6The water in Nimrim is dried up. The grass is dry. The plants are gone. The green is no more.

7So now they carry their things over the BrookbrookBrook of the Willows. 8A cry is heard all around Moab. The crying reaches Eglaim. The crying reaches Beer-elim. 9The water at Dimon is full of blood. But I will bring even more trouble on Dimon. I will send a lion after the people running from Moab and the people left in Admah.
