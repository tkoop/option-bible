Jacob and His Family

1Jacob lived in the land where his father had stayed. It was the land of Canaan. 2These are the family stories of Jacob. Joseph was 17 years old. He helped his brothers take care of the sheep. He was still a boy. He worked with the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, his father's wives. Joseph told his father about the bad things his brothers did. 3Israel loved Joseph more than his other sons. This was because Joseph was born when Israel was old. Israel made Joseph a special robe. 4When his brothers saw that their father loved Joseph more than them, they hated him. They could not speak kindly to him.

Joseph's Dreams

5Joseph had a dream. He told it to his brothers. They hated him even more.

6Joseph said, "Please listen to the dream I had. 7We were tying up bundles of grain in the field. My bundle stood up. Your bundles gathered around and bowed down to mine."

8His brothers said, "Do you think you will be our king? Do you think you will rule over us?" They hated him even more because of his dreams and words.

9Then Joseph had another dream. He told it to his brothers. He said, "Listen. I had another dream. The sun, the moon, and eleven stars bowed down to me."

10He told it to his father too. His father scolded him. He said, "What is this dream? Will I and your mother and your brothers really bow down to you?"

11His brothers were jealous of him. But his father kept thinking about it.

Joseph Goes to Find His Brothers

12His brothers went to take care of their father's sheep in Shechem.

13Israel said to Joseph, "YouryourYour brothers are taking care of the sheep in Shechem. Come, I will send you to them."

Joseph said, "I am ready."

14Israel said, "Go and see how your brothers and the sheep are doing. Then come back and tell me." So he sent Joseph from the valley of Hebron. Joseph went to Shechem.

15A man found Joseph. He was lost in the field. The man asked, "What are you looking for?"

16Joseph said, "I am looking for my brothers. Please tell me where they are taking care of the sheep."

17The man said, "They have moved from here. I heard them say, 'Let us go to Dothan.'" So Joseph went after his brothers. He found them in Dothan.

The Brothers Plan to Harm Joseph

18They saw him from far away. Before he came near, they made a plan to kill him.

19They said to each other, "Look, here comes the dreamer. 20Come on, let's kill him and throw him into one of the pits. We will say a wild animal ate him. Then we will see what happens to his dreams."

21Reuben heard this. He saved Joseph from them. He said, "Let's not kill him. 22Throw him into this pit in the desert. But don't hurt him." Reuben said this so he could save Joseph and take him back to his father.

23When Joseph came to his brothers, they took off his robe, the special robe he was wearing. 24They took him and threw him into the pit. The pit was empty. There was no water in it.

25Then they sat down to eat. They looked up and saw a group of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead. Their camels were carrying spices, balm, and perfume. They were going to Egypt.

26Judah said to his brothers, "What good is it to kill our brother and hide his blood? 27Let's sell him to the Ishmaelites. Let's not hurt him. He is our brother. He is one of us." His brothers listened.

28Some Midianite traders passed by. They pulled Joseph out of the pit. They sold Joseph for twenty pieces of silver to the Ishmaelites. The Ishmaelites took Joseph to Egypt.

Reuben Finds Joseph Gone

29Reuben came back to the pit. Joseph was not there. Reuben tore his clothes. 30He went back to his brothers. He said, "ThetheThe boy is gone! Where can I go now?"

The Brothers Trick Their Father

31They took Joseph's robe. They killed a goat. They dipped the robe in the blood. 32They sent the special robe to their father. They said, "We found this. Is it your son's robe? Please look at it."

33Jacob knew it was Joseph's robe. He said, "It is my son's robe! A wild animal has eaten him. Joseph has been torn apart." 34Jacob tore his clothes. He put on sackcloth. He mourned for his son for many days. 35All his sons and daughters came to comfort him. But he would not be comforted. He said, "I will go down to the grave mourning for my son." So Jacob wept for him.

36The Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar. Potiphar was one of Pharaoh's officers. He was the captain of the guard.
