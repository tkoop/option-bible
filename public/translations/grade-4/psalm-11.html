David Trusts the LORD

1I trust the LORD to keep me safe. Why do you say to me, "Run away to safety"?

2Look, bad people bend their bows. They put arrows on the string. They shoot from hiding at good people. 3If everything falls apart, what can a good person do?

The LORD Rules from Heaven
