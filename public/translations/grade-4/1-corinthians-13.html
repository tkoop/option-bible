Love Matters More Than Gifts

1If I speak every language that people and angels use, but I do not have love, I am just a loud, empty bell. 2If I can tell what will happen, know every secret, and know everything, and if I have so much faith that I can move mountains, but I do not have love, I am nothing. 3If I give away everything I own, and even give up my body, but I do not have love, it does not help me.

What Love Is Like

4Love is patient and kind. Love is not jealous. Love does not brag. Love is not proud. 5Love is not rude. Love does not want only its own way. Love does not get angry easily. Love does not keep a list of wrongs. 6Love is not glad when bad things happen. Love is glad when truth wins. 7Love puts up with all things. Love believes all things. Love hopes all things. Love keeps going in all things.

Love Lasts Forever

8Love never ends. But special messages will end. Speaking in new languages will end. What we know will end. 9We know only a little now. We also tell what will happen only a little now. 10When the perfect thing comes, these little things will end.

Growing Up

11When I was a child, I talked like a child. I thought like a child. I understood like a child. When I became a man, I stopped doing child things. 12Now we see things like a dark picture in a mirror. Then we will see face to face. Now I know only a little. Then I will know fully, just like I am known. 13So these three things stay: faith, hope, and love. But the greatest of these is love.
