Good People and Bad People

1People who like being corrected like to learn. People who hate being corrected are foolish. 2A good person gets kindness from the LORD. But HeheHe punishes people who make bad plans. 3No one becomes strong by doing wrong. Good people stay firm like strong roots. 4A good wife is like a crown for her husband. A wife who brings shame is like rot in his bones.

5Good people think fair thoughts. Bad people plan tricks. 6Bad people's words wait to hurt others. But honest words save people. 7Bad people are knocked down and gone. But a good person's home stays strong. 8People praise a person for being smart. But a person with a bad heart is looked down on.

Being Humble and Caring

9It is better to be humble and have a helper than to act important and have no food. 10A good person cares for his animals. But bad people are cruel, even when they try to be kind. 11A person who works his farm has enough food. A person who chases empty things has no sense. 12Bad people want what other bad people steal. But good people grow strong like roots that bear fruit.

Watch Your Words

13A bad person is trapped by his own words. But a good person gets out of trouble. 14A person is filled with good things by his good words. He gets back what his hands do. 15Fools think their own way is right. But wise people listen to advice. 16A fool shows his anger fast. But a smart person hides his shame.

17A person who speaks truth gives honest proof. A liar spreads lies. 18Some words cut like a sword. But wise words help people heal. 19True words last forever. Lies do not last. 20Bad people plan tricks in their hearts. But people who plan peace are happy.

God's Care and Good Choices

21Good people are kept safe. But bad people are full of trouble. 22The LORD hates lying lips. He is happy with honest people. 23A smart person keeps what he knows to himself. But a fool shouts his foolishness. 24Hard workers become leaders. Lazy people become servants.

25Worry makes a heart heavy. But a kind word brings joy. 26A good person helps his neighbourneighbourneighbor. But bad people lead others the wrong way. 27A lazy person will not even cook his food. But a hard worker gets valuable things. 28Doing right brings life. That road has no death.
