We Do Not Give Up

1God was kind to us. He gave us this work. So we do not give up. 2We do not hide shameful things. We do not use tricks. We do not twist God's word. We show the truth clearly. We want all people to see that we are true. God sees us too.

The Good News Is Hidden from Some

3If our good news is hidden, it is hidden from people who are lost. 4The god of this world has blinded their minds. They cannot see the light of the good news. It shows the glory of Christ. Christ is the image of God.

We Tell About Jesus

5We do not tell people about ourselves. We tell about Jesus Christ as Lord. We are your servants because of Jesus. 6God said, "Let light shine out of darkness." He made HishisHis light shine in our hearts. This light helps us know God's glory. We see that glory in Jesus Christ.

Treasure in Clay Jars

7We have this treasure in clay jars. God's great power is shown. It does not come from us. 8We are pushed hard, but we are not crushed. We are confused, but we do not give up. 9People chase us, but God does not leave us. We are knocked down, but we are not destroyed.

10We carry Jesus' death in our bodies. We do this so Jesus' life can show in our bodies. 11We who are alive are always handed over to death because of Jesus. This is so Jesus' life can show in our weak bodies. 12So death works in us, but life works in you.

We Believe and We Speak

13It is written, "I believed, so I spoke." We have the same spirit of faith. We believe too, so we speak. 14We know God raised the Lord Jesus. He will raise us too. He will bring us to stand with you. 15All this is for your sake. God's grace is growing. More and more people will thank HimhimHim. This brings glory to God.

Do Not Lose Heart

16So we do not give up. Our outer person is wearing away. But our inner person is made new day by day. 17Our troubles are light and short. They make for us a glory that lasts forever. It is far greater than all our troubles. 18We do not look at what we can see. We look at what we cannot see. What we see does not last. What we cannot see lasts forever.
