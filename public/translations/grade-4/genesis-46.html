Jacob Goes to Egypt

1Israel and everyone with him travelledtravelledtraveled to Beersheba. There he gave gifts to God, the God of his father Isaac.

2That night, God spoke to Israel in a dream. He said, "Jacob! Jacob!"

Jacob answered, "Here I am."

3God said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt. I will make you into a great nation there.

4I will go down with you to Egypt. I will also bring you back again. Joseph will close your eyes."

5Jacob left Beersheba. The sons of Israel took their father Jacob, the children, and the wives in the carts Pharaoh had sent to carry him.

6They took their animals and the things they had gained in Canaan. Jacob and all his family went to Egypt.

7He brought his sons, his grandsons, his daughters, and his granddaughters. He brought all his family with him to Egypt.

Jacob's Family Who Went to Egypt

8These are the names of Israel's sons and their families who came to Egypt. Reuben was Jacob's firstborn son. 9Reuben's sons were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

10Simeon's sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. Shaul's mother was a Canaanite woman. 11Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 12Judah's sons were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah. Er and Onan died in Canaan. Perez's sons were Hezron and Hamul.

13Issachar's sons were Tola, Puva, Job, and Shimron. 14Zebulun's sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel. 15These were the sons Leah had with Jacob in Paddan Aram. She also had a daughter named Dinah. All of Leah's sons and daughters made 33 people.

16Gad's sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli. 17Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. Their sister was Serah. Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel.

18These were the children of Zilpah. Laban gave Zilpah to his daughter Leah. Zilpah had 16 children for Jacob. 19Rachel's children were Joseph and Benjamin.

20In Egypt, Joseph had two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim. Their mother was Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera, the priest of On. 21Benjamin's sons were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.

22Rachel had 14 children and grandchildren for Jacob. 23Dan's son was Hushim. 24Naphtali's sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.

25These were the children of Bilhah. Laban gave Bilhah to his daughter Rachel. Bilhah had 7 children for Jacob. 26All of Jacob's direct family who came to Egypt were 66 people. This does not count his sons' wives. 27With Joseph's two sons born in Egypt, Jacob's whole family in Egypt was 70 people.

Joseph Meets His Father

28Jacob sent Judah ahead to Joseph to show the way to Goshen. They came to Goshen. 29Joseph got his chariot ready. He went to Goshen to meet his father Israel. He hugged him and cried on his neck for a long time. 30Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die happy. I have seen your face, and you are still alive."

31Joseph said to his brothers and father, "I will go to Pharaoh and tell him, 'My brothers and my father's family from Canaan have come to me. 32The men are shepherds. They care for animals. They brought their sheep, cattle, and all they own. 33When Pharaoh calls you, he will ask, 'What is your work?' 34YouyouYou must say, 'Your servants have cared for animals since we were young, just like our fathers did.' Then you can live in Goshen, because Egyptians do not like shepherds."
