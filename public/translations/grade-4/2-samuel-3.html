The War Between Saul's Family and David's Family

1The war between Saul's family and David's family lasted a long time. David got stronger and stronger, but Saul's family got weaker and weaker.

David's Sons Born in Hebron

2David had sons in Hebron. His first son was Amnon. His mother was Ahinoam from Jezreel. 3His second son was Chileab. His mother was Abigail, who had been Nabal's wife from Carmel. His third son was Absalom. His mother was Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur. 4His fourth son was Adonijah. His mother was Haggith. His fifth son was Shephatiah. His mother was Abital. 5His sixth son was Ithream. His mother was David's wife Eglah. These sons were born to David in Hebron.

Abner and Ish-Bosheth Argue

6While the war went on between Saul's family and David's family, Abner became strong in Saul's family. 7Saul had a woman named Rizpah. She was Aiah's daughter. Ish-Bosheth said to Abner, "Why did you sleep with my father's woman?"

8Abner was very angry about what Ish-Bosheth said. He said, "Do you think I am a nobody from Judah? Today I am kind to your father Saul's family, his brothers, and his friends. I have not handed you over to David. But today you blame me for something about this woman." 9"May God punish me if I do not do for David what the the LORD promised him. 10I will help move the kingdom from Saul's family and make David king over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba."

Ish-Bosheth Is Afraid

11Ish-Bosheth was afraid of Abner, so he could not answer him.

Abner Sends a Message to David

12Abner sent messengers to David. They said, "Whose land is this? Make a covenant with me, and I will help you bring all Israel to you."

David Asks for Michal

13David said, "Good. I will make a covenant with you. But I ask one thing. You will not see my face unless you bring my wife Michal, Saul's daughter, when you come to see me."

Michal Is Brought Back

14David sent messengers to Ish-Bosheth, Saul's son. They said, "Give me my wife Michal. I paid for her with one hundred Philistine foreskins." 15Ish-Bosheth sent men and took Michal from her husband, Paltiel son of Laish. 16Her husband walked with her and cried as he went. He followed her to Bahurim. Then Abner said to him, "Go back." So he went back.

Abner Talks to Israel's Leaders

17Abner talked with the elders of Israel. He said, "In the past you wanted David to be your king. Now do what you need to do. 18The ThetheThe LORD has spoken about David. He said, 'By the hand of MymyMy servant David, I will save MymyMy people Israel from the Philistines and all their enemies.'"

Abner Goes to David

19Abner also spoke to the people of Benjamin. Then he went to tell David at Hebron all that Israel and Benjamin's family thought was good. 20Abner came to David at Hebron with twenty men. David made a feast for Abner and his men. 21Abner said to David, "Let me go and gather all Israel for my lord the king. Then they can make a covenant with you, and you can rule over all that your heart wants." David sent Abner away, and he went in peace.

Joab Returns and Is Angry

22Just then, David's servants and Joab came back from a raid. They brought much treasure. Abner was not with David in Hebron anymore, because David had sent him away in peace. 23Joab and all his men came in. They told Joab, "Abner son of Ner came to the king, and the king sent him away in peace." 24Joab went to the king and said, "What have you done? Look, Abner came to you. Why did you send him away? Now he is gone!" 25You know Abner son of Ner. He came to trick you and learn where you go and what you do."

Joab Kills Abner

26Joab left David and sent messengers after Abner. They brought him back from the well of Sirah. David did not know about this. 27When Abner came back to Hebron, Joab took him aside at the gate to speak quietly. There Joab stabbed him in the stomach, and Abner died.

David Says He Is Not Guilty

28When David heard about it, he said, "I and my kingdom are not guilty before the the LORD for the blood of Abner son of Ner." 29He said, "May this trouble fall on Joab and his father's family. May Joab's family never be without someone who is sick, or has a skin disease, or leans on a staff, or dies by the sword, or has no bread."

30Joab and his brother Abishai killed Abner. They did this because Abner had killed their brother Asahel in the battle at Gibeon.

David Mourns Abner

31David said to Joab and all the people with him, "Tear your clothes. Put on sackcloth. Mourn for Abner." King David followed the coffin. 32They buried Abner in Hebron. The king cried loudly at Abner's grave. All the people cried too. 33The king sang a sad song for Abner. He said, "Should Abner die like a fool?" 34"Your hands were not tied. Your feet were not chained. You fell like a man killed by bad men." All the people cried again over him.

David Will Not Eat

35All the people came to David to eat bread while it was still day. But David made a strong promise. He said, "May God punish me if I taste bread or anything else before the sun goes down." 36All the people noticed this, and it pleased them. Everything the king did was good in the people's eyes. 37All Israel and all the people knew that day that the king had not wanted Abner son of Ner to die.

David Speaks About Abner

38The king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a great leader and prince died today in Israel?" 39"I am weak today, even though I am the chosen king. These men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too strong for me. May the the LORD pay back the one who does evil with the evil he has done."
