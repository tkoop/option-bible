God's Message to Joel

1This is the word of the LORD that came to Joel. Joel was the son of Pethuel.

Listen, Everyone

2Hear this, you leaders. Listen, all you people who live in the land. Has anything like this happened in your days? Did it happen in the days of your fathers? 3Tell your children about it. Let your children tell their children. Let their children tell the children after them.

Locusts Eat Everything

4What one group of locusts left, the next group ate. What that group left, another group ate. What was left after that, yet another group ate.

The Land Is Ruined

5Wake up, you people who drink too much, and cry. Wail, all you who drink wine. The sweet wine is taken from your mouths.

6A strong group of people came up on MymyMy land. They are too many to count. Their teeth are like a lion's teeth. Their jaws are like a lioness's jaws. 7They ruined MymyMy grapevines. They stripped MymyMy fig tree. They threw down its branches. The branches turned white.

8Cry like a young woman in rough cloth. She is sad for the husband she had when she was young.

9Grain and drink gifts are cut off from the house of the LORD. The priests are sad. The helpers of the LORD are sad. 10The field is ruined. The ground is sad. The grain is ruined. The new wine is weak. The oil is dry.

11Be upset, you farmers. Cry out, you grape growers. Cry for the wheat and barley. The harvest of the field is gone. 12The grapevine is dry. The fig tree is weak. The pomegranate, the palm, and the apple tree are dry. All the trees in the field are dry. Joy is gone from people.

Cry Out to God

13Put on rough cloth and be sad, you priests. Cry out, you workers at the altar. Come and lie down in rough cloth all night, you helpers of my God. Grain and drink gifts are kept from the house of your God. 14Set apart a time with no food. Call a special meeting. Bring the leaders and all the people who live in the land to the house of the LORD your God. Cry out to the LORD.

A Sad Day Is Coming

15Oh, what a sad day! The DaydayDay of the LORD is near. It comes like ruin from the All-Powerful OneoneOne. 16Our food is taken away right in front of us. Joy and gladness are gone from the house of our God.

17The seeds dry up under the dirt. The storehouses are ruined. The barns are broken down. The grain is dry. 18The animals groan. The herds of cows are confused. They have no grass to eat. Even the flocks of sheep suffer.

A Prayer to God

19I cry to YouyouYou, the LORD, because fire ate the grass in the wild land. The flame burned all the trees in the field. 20Even the animals in the field cry to YouyouYou, because the streams of water are dry. Fire ate the grass in the wild land.
