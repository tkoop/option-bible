False Gods Fall Down

1Bel falls down. Nebo bends over. Their statues are carried by animals. The things you carry are a heavy load for tired animals.

2They bend down together. They cannot save the load. They are taken away as prisoners.

God Carries His People

3Listen to MemeMe, family of Jacob. All of you who are left from Israel. I have carried you since you were born.

4Even when you are old, I will not change. I will hold you up when your hair turns greygreygray. I made you, and I will carry you. I will care for you and save you.

No One Is Like God

5Who can you compare MemeMe to? Who is equal to MemeMe? Who is like MemeMe?

6They pour gold from a bag. They weigh silver. They pay a worker to make a god. Then they bow down and worship it.

7They lift it on their shoulders and carry it. They put it in a place, and it stays there. It does not move. If someone calls to it, it does not answer. It cannot save him from trouble.

Think About What You Do

8Remember this and be ashamed. Think about it, you who do wrong.

God Alone Is God

9Remember the things from long ago. I am God. There is no other God. There is no one like MemeMe.

God's Plan Will Happen

10I tell the end from the start. I tell what will happen before it comes. I say, "MymyMy plan will stand. I will do all I want to do."

11I call a bird of prey from the east. I call a man from far away to do MymyMy plan. I have spoken, and I will make it happen. I have planned it, and I will do it.

God Will Help His People

12Listen to MemeMe, you who are stubborn. You are far from doing right.

13I bring MymyMy right way near. It is not far away. My help will not be late. I will give help in Zion and MymyMy glory to Israel.
