God Keeps Us Safe

1I look up at the hills. Where does my help come from? 2My help comes from the LORD. He made Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.

3He will not let your foot slip. The one who watches over you will not sleep. 4The one who keeps Israel will not sleep or rest.

5The LORD keeps you. The LORD is your shade on your right side. 6The sun will not hurt you in the day. The moon will not hurt you at night.

7The LORD will keep you safe from all harm. He will watch over your life. 8The LORD will watch over you when you come and go. He will do this now and forever.
