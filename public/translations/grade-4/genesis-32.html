Jacob Meets God's Angels

1Jacob went on his way. God's angels met him. 2When Jacob saw them, he said, "This is God's camp!" He named that place Mahanaim.

Jacob Sends Gifts to Esau

Jacob Wrestles with a Man

22During the night he got up. He took his two wives, his two female servants, and his eleven sons. He crossed the river at the Jabbok. 23He took them and sent them across the stream. He also sent over all that he had. 24Jacob was left alone, and a man wrestled with him until daybreak.

25When the man saw that he could not win against him, he touched the socket of Jacob's hip. Jacob's hip was put out of joint as they wrestled.

26Then the man said, "Let me go, for the dawn is coming."

Jacob Is Named Israel

27The man asked him, "What is your name?"

He said, "Jacob."

29Jacob asked, "Please tell me YouryourYour name."

30So Jacob named the place Peniel, saying, "I have seen God face to face, and my life was saved."

Jacob Limps at Sunrise

31The sun rose upon him as he passed Penuel, and he was limping because of his hip. 32So to this day the people of Israel do not eat the tough cord on the hip socket, because he touched Jacob's hip socket.
