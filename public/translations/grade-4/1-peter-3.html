Wives and Husbands

1Wives, in the same way, obey your own husbands. Then even if some do not obey God's word, they can be won over without talking. 2They will see your clean and respectful lives. 3Your beauty should not come from outside things, like fancy hair, gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry, or fine clothes. 4It should come from who you are inside. That beauty lasts forever. It is a gentle and calm spirit. That is very special to God.

5For this is how good women long ago made themselves beautiful. They trusted God and obeyed their own husbands. 6Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her master. You are her daughters if you do good and are not scared of anything frightening.

7Husbands, in the same way, live with your wives and understand them. They are more delicate. HonourHonourHonor them because they share God's gift of life with you. Then nothing will block your prayers.

Living Together

8Last of all, everyone should think the same way. Care about each other's feelings. Love each other like family. Be kind. Do not be proud. 9Do not pay back bad for bad or mean words for mean words. Instead, bless them. God called you to do this so you can receive a blessing.

10"For anyone who wants to love life and see good days should keep their tongue from evil and not tell lies. 11They should turn away from evil and do good. They should look for peace and go after it. 12The eyes of the LORD are on good people. He hears their prayers. But the face of the LORD is against people who do bad things."

Do Not Be Afraid

13Who can hurt you if you try hard to do good? 14But even if you suffer for doing right, you are blessed. Do not be afraid of their threats. Do not be upset.

15But in your hearts, make Christ your Lord. Always be ready to tell anyone who asks you why you have hope. Do it gently and with respect. 16Keep a clean heart. Then people who say bad things about you will be ashamed when they see your good life in Christ.

17It is better to suffer for doing good than for doing bad, if that is what God wants.

Christ Suffered for Us

Only eight people were saved by water.
