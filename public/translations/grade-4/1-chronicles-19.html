David Shows Kindness to Hanun

1After this, Nahash, king of the Ammonites, died. His son Hanun became king. 2David said, "I want to be kind to Hanun son of Nahash. His father was kind to me." So David sent men to comfort him about his father. David's servants came to the land of the Ammonites to Hanun to comfort him.

3The leaders of the Ammonites said to Hanun, "Do you think David is honouringhonouringhonoring your father by sending men to comfort you? Did his servants come here to look around, spy, and destroy our land?"

4So Hanun took David's servants. He shaved them. He cut their clothes in half up to their hips. Then he sent them away.

5Some men went and told David about this. He sent men to meet them, because they were very ashamed. The king said, "Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back. Then come home."

Ammonites Hire an Army

6The Ammonites saw they had made David angry. Hanun and the Ammonites sent 1,000 talents of silver to hire chariots and horsemen from Aram Naharaim, Aram Maacah, and Zobah. 7They hired 32,000 chariots. They also hired the king of Maacah and his troops. They came and camped near Medeba. The Ammonites came from their towns and got ready for war.

8When David heard this, he sent Joab and all his strong soldiers.

9The Ammonites came out and lined up for battle at the city gate. The kings who came stayed by themselves in the open field.

10Joab saw that the battle was in front of him and behind him. He chose the best soldiers in Israel and lined them up against the Arameans. 11He put the rest of the army under his brother Abishai. They lined up against the Ammonites.

12Joab said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, you will help me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will help you. 13Be strong. Let us fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. May the the LORD do what is good in HishisHis sight."

14Joab and his men moved forward to fight the Arameans. The Arameans ran away from him. 15When the Ammonites saw the Arameans running away, they also ran away from Abishai. They went back into the city. Then Joab went back to Jerusalem.

Arameans Try Again

16The Arameans saw they had lost to Israel. They sent messengers to bring more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates River. Shophach, the leader of Hadadezer's army, led them.

17David was told about this. He gathered all Israel and crossed the Jordan River. He came to them. David lined up his army for battle. They fought.

18The Arameans ran away from Israel. David killed 7,000 chariot drivers and 40,000 foot soldiers. He also killed Shophach, the army leader.

19When Hadadezer's servants saw they had lost to Israel, they made peace with David. They became his servants. The Arameans did not want to help the Ammonites anymore.
