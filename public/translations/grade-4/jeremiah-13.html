The Linen Belt

1The LORD said to me, "Go buy a cloth belt. Put it around your waist. Do not put it in water." 2So I bought the belt, just as the the LORD said. I put it around my waist.

3Then the word of the the LORD came to me again. 4He said, "Take the belt you bought, the one around your waist. Go to the Euphrates River and hide it in a crack in the rocks." 5So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, just as the the LORD told me.

6After many days, the the LORD said to me, "Go to the Euphrates and get the belt I told you to hide there." 7So I went to the Euphrates and dug up the belt. I took it from the place where I had hidden it. But it was ruined. It was no good for anything.

Judah Will Be Ruined Like the Belt

8Then the word of the the LORD came to me. 9He said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'In the same way, I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem. 10These wicked people will not listen to MymyMy words. They follow their stubborn hearts. They go after other gods to serve and worship them. They will be like this belt—useless for anything. 11For as a belt clings to a man's waist, so I made all the people of Israel and all the people of Judah cling to MemeMe,' says the the LORD. 'They were to be MymyMy people. They were to bring MemeMe praise, glory, and honourhonourhonor. But they did not listen.'"

Jars Full of Wine

12"Say to them, 'This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Every jar will be filled with wine.' They will say to you, 'Do we not know that every jar will be filled with wine?' 13Then say to them, 'This is what the the LORD says: I will make everyone in this land drunk. I will make the kings who sit on David's throne drunk, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the people in Jerusalem. 14I will smash them against each other, fathers and sons together,' says the the LORD. 'I will not pity them. I will not spare them. I will not have mercy on them. I will destroy them.'"

Listen to the LORD

15Listen and pay attention. Do not be proud, for the the LORD has spoken. 16Give glory to the the LORD your God before HeheHe brings darkness, before your feet stumble in the dark. While you hope for light, HeheHe will turn it into deep darkness and thick clouds. 17But if you will not listen, I will cry in secret because of your pride. My eyes will cry hard tears, because the the LORD's flock will be taken away.

Trouble for the King and Queen Mother

18Say to the king and the queen mother: "Come down from your throne. Your beautiful crown has fallen from your heads." 19The cities in the south are shut up, and no one opens them. All the people of Judah are carried away. They are all taken away.

20Look up and see the people coming from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you? Where are your beautiful sheep? 21What will you say when HeheHe puts over you the leaders you taught to be your friends? Will pain grab you like a woman having a baby?

Sin Has a Cost

22If you ask in your heart, "Why did this happen to me?" it is because of your great sin. Your skirts are lifted, and your feet are bare.

23Can a person change the colourcolourcolor of his skin? Can a leopard change its spots? Then you also cannot do good, because you are used to doing evil.
