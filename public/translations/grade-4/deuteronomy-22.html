Helping a Brother

1If you see your brother's ox or sheep wandering away, don't ignore them. Bring them back to your brother. 2If your brother is not near, or you don't know him, take the animal to your home. Keep it until your brother looks for it, then give it back. 3Do the same for his donkey, his clothes, or anything else he loses that you find. Don't ignore it. 4If you see your brother's donkey or ox fall on the road, don't ignore it. Help him lift it up.

Clothes and Mixed Things

5A woman must not wear a man's clothes, and a man must not wear a woman's clothes. This is because the LORD your God hates anyone who does this.

Birds and Nests

6If you find a bird's nest with baby birds or eggs, and the mother is sitting on them, don't take the mother with the young. 7You may take the young, but let the mother go. Then things will go well for you, and you will live a long time.

Building a Safe Roof

8When you build a new house, put a low wall around the edge of the roof. Then no one will fall, and you won't be blamed for their death.

Farming and Clothes

9Don't plant your vineyard with two kinds of seed. If you do, both crops may be ruined. 10Don't plow with an ox and a donkey together. 11Don't wear clothes made of wool and linen mixed together. 12Make tassels on the four corners of the cloak you wear.

Rules About Marriage

13If a man takes a wife and sleeps with her, then hates her, 14and he says bad things about her and gives her a bad name. He says, "I took this woman, but when I came near her, I did not find proof she had never been with a man."

15Then the young woman's father and mother must take proof she had never been with a man to the city elders at the gate. 16The father will say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, but he hates her. 17Look, he has said bad things about her. He says, 'I did not find proof your daughter had never been with a man.' But here is proof my daughter had never been with a man." Then they will spread the cloth in front of the elders.

18The elders of that city will take the man and punish him. 19They will fine him one hundred pieces of silver and give the money to the young woman's father, because he gave a bad name to an Israelite girl who had never been with a man. She will stay his wife, and he can never divorce her as long as he lives. 20But if the charge is true, and no proof she had never been with a man is found, 21they must bring the young woman to the door of her father's house. The men of her city will stone her to death, because she did a wicked thing in Israel. She acted like a prostitute in her father's house. Remove the evil from among you.

22If a man is found lying with a married woman, both the man and the woman must die. Remove the evil from Israel.

23If a young woman is promised to a man, and a man finds her in the city and sleeps with her, 24bring both of them to the gate of that city and stone them to death. Stone the young woman because she did not cry out in the city. Stone the man because he brought shame on his neighbourneighbourneighbor's wife. Remove the evil from among you.

25But if a man finds a promised young woman in the field, and he grabs her and sleeps with her, only the man must die. 26Do nothing to the young woman. She has done no sin that deserves death. This is like a man who attacks his neighbourneighbourneighbor and kills him. 27He found her in the field. The promised young woman cried out, but no one was there to save her.

28If a man finds a young woman who is not promised to anyone, grabs her, and sleeps with her, and people find out, 29the man who slept with her must give the young woman's father fifty pieces of silver. She will become his wife, because he brought shame on her. He can never divorce her as long as he lives.

30A man must not take his father's wife. He must not bring shame on his father.
