Children and Parents

1Kids, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is right. 2HonourHonourHonor your father and mother. This is the first command that comes with a promise. 3Then things will go well for you, and you will live a long time on the earth. 4Fathers, do not make your children angry. Instead, raise them with the Lord’s teaching and training.

Slaves and Masters

5Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear. Be honest in your heart, as if you were serving Christ. 6Do not work only when people are watching, to make them happy. Work like servants of Christ. Do what God wants, from the heart. 7Serve with a good attitude, as if you were serving the Lord, not people. 8Know this: The Lord will reward each person for any good thing he does, whether he is a slave or free. 9Masters, treat them the same way. Stop threatening them. Remember that both you and they have a Master in Heavenheavenheaven. He does not play favorites.

Be Strong in the Lord

Then you can stand against the devil’s tricks. 12We are not fighting against people made of flesh and blood. We are fighting against rulers, authorities, and powers of this dark world. We are also fighting against evil spirits in the heavenly places. 13So put on all of God’s armourarmourarmor. Then, when evil days come, you can stand your ground. After you have done all you can, keep standing.

14Stand firm. Put truth around your waist like a belt. Put on the breastplate of right living. 15Put shoes on your feet, ready to tell the good news of peace. 16Also take the shield of faith. With it you can stop all the flaming arrows of the evil one. 17Take the helmet of salvation. Take the sword of the SpiritspiritSpirit, which is God’s word.
