Fire in Lebanon

1Open your doors, Lebanon, so fire can eat your cedar trees. 2Wail, cypress, because the cedar fell. The mighty trees are ruined. Wail, oaks of Bashan, because the thick forest came down. 3Shepherds cry out, because their good land is gone. Young lions roar, because the Jordan’s thick bushes are ruined.

The LORD Tells Zechariah to Shepherd a Doomed Flock

4This is what the LORD my God says: “Shepherd the flock that is set to be killed.”

5Their buyers kill them and are not guilty. Their sellers say, “Bless the LORD, I am rich!” Their shepherds do not feel sorry for them.

6The LORD says, “I will not feel sorry for the people of this land anymore. Look, I will hand each person over to his neighbourneighbourneighbor and his king. They will ruin the land, and I will not save it from their hands.”

Two Staffs

7So I took care of the flock set to be killed, especially the poor sheep. I took two sticks. I called one FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor and the other UnionunionUnion. Then I took care of the sheep. 8In one month I got rid of three shepherds. I was tired of them, and they hated me. 9Then I said, “I will not be your shepherd. Let the dying die. Let the lost be lost. Let those left eat each other up.”

10I took my stick called FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor and broke it. I did this to break the agreement I had made with all the nations. 11It broke that day. The poor sheep who watched me knew it was the LORD’s word. 12I said to them, “If it seems good to you, give me my pay. If not, keep it.” They weighed out my pay: thirty pieces of silver.

13The LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter—the great price they put on me!” So I took the thirty silver coins and threw them to the potter in the LORD’s house. 14Then I broke my second stick, UnionunionUnion. I did this to break the bond between Judah and Israel.

A Foolish Shepherd

15The LORD said to me, “Take the tools of a foolish shepherd again. 16I am raising up a shepherd in this land. He will not look for lost sheep. He will not help young sheep. He will not heal hurt sheep. He will not feed strong sheep. He will eat the meat of fat sheep and tear off their hooves. 17Trouble for the worthless shepherd who leaves the flock! A sword will hit his arm and right eye. His arm will dry up, and his right eye will go dark.”
