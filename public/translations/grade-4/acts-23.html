Paul Talks to the Leaders

1Paul looked straight at the council and said, "BrothersbrothersBrothers, I have served God and tried to do right up to today."

2The high priest Ananias told the men near him, "Hit him on the mouth!"

3Then Paul said to him, "God will hit you, you pretender! You judge me by the law, but you break the law when you order me hit."

4The men near him said, "Do you insult God's high priest?"

5Paul said, "BrothersbrothersBrothers, I did not know he was the high priest. The Bible says, 'Do not say bad things about a ruler of your people.'" 6Then Paul saw that one group was Sadducees and the other was Pharisees. He called out, "BrothersbrothersBrothers, I am a Pharisee and the son of Pharisees. I am on trial because I hope in the rising of the dead."

7When he said this, the Pharisees and Sadducees started to argue, and the crowd split. 8The Sadducees say there is no rising of the dead, no angels, and no spirits. But the Pharisees believe in all three. 9Then a loud shout came. Some teachers of the law from the Pharisees' side stood up and argued, "We find nothing wrong with this man. Maybe a spirit or an angel spoke to him." 10The argument grew big. The commander was afraid Paul would be torn apart. So he ordered the soldiers to go down, take Paul by force from them, and bring him into the army camp.

11That night the Lord stood near Paul and said, "Be brave. You spoke for me in Jerusalem. In the same way, you must speak for me in Rome."

A Plot to Kill Paul

12The next day some Jews made a plan. They promised not to eat or drink until Paul was dead. 13More than forty men joined this plot. 14They went to the chief priests and elders and said, "We have promised not to eat anything until we kill Paul. 15Now you and the council must tell the commander to bring Paul down to you. Pretend you want to ask more about him. But we are ready to kill him before he gets near."

Paul's Nephew Warns Him

16But Paul's sister's son heard about the trap. He went into the army camp and told Paul. 17Paul called one of the army officers and said, "Take this young man to the commander. He has something to tell him."

18So the officer took him to the commander and said, "ThetheThe prisoner Paul called me and asked me to bring this young man to you. He has something to say to you."

19The commander took the young man by the hand and stepped aside. He asked, "What do you have to tell me?"

20The young man said, "ThetheThe Jews agreed to ask you to bring Paul to the council tomorrow. They will pretend they want to ask more about him. 21Do not listen to them. More than forty men are waiting to kill him. They promised not to eat or drink until he is dead. They are ready now, waiting for your promise."

22The commander let the young man go. He told him, "Do not tell anyone that you told me these things."

The Commander Sends Paul to Felix

23Then he called two army officers and said, "Get ready two hundred soldiers, seventy men on horses, and two hundred soldiers with spears. They will go to Caesarea at nine o'clock at night. 24Give them horses so they can take Paul safely to Governor Felix."

The Commander's Letter

25He also wrote a letter. It said this:

26"Claudius Lysias to the MostmostMost Excellent Governor Felix: Greetings. 27The Jews seized this man and were about to kill him. I came with the soldiers and saved him, because I learned he is a Roman citizen. 28I wanted to know why they accused him, so I brought him to their council. 29I found that he was accused about questions of their law. He has done nothing that deserves death or chains. 30When I heard about the plot against him, I sent him to you right away. I told his accusers to speak against him in front of you."

Paul Goes to Caesarea

31So the soldiers did what they were told. They took Paul by night to Antipatris. 32The next day they went back to the army camp. They let the men on horses go on with Paul. 33The men on horses came to Caesarea. They gave the letter to the governor and brought Paul to him.

34After reading the letter, the governor asked where Paul was from. He learned Paul was from Cilicia. 35The governor said, "I will listen to you when your accusers come." He ordered Paul to be kept in Herod's palace.
