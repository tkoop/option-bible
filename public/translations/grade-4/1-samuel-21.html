David Gets Bread from the Priest

1David went to Nob to see Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech came to meet David, but he was afraid. He said, "Why are you alone? Why is no one with you?"

2David said to Ahimelech, "ThetheThe king sent me on a secret job. He said, 'No one must know anything about what I am sending you to do or what I told you to do. I told the young men to meet at a certain place.' 3Now what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever you can find."

4Ahimelech answered, "I have no ordinary bread here. I only have holy bread. The young men may eat it if they have stayed away from women."

5David said, "Women have been kept away from us since I left. The young men keep themselves clean. This bread is for an ordinary trip, but today it is holy in the bag."

6The priest gave David the holy bread. There was no other bread there except the bread set before the LORD. It had been taken away to put hot bread in its place on the day it was taken. 7One of Saul's servants was there that day, held before the LORD. His name was Doeg the Edomite. He was the boss of Saul's animal keepers.

8David said to Ahimelech, "Do you have a spear or sword here? I did not bring my sword or my things, because the king's business was urgent."

9Ahimelech said, "ThetheThe sword of Goliath the Philistine is here. You killed him in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah. It is wrapped in cloth behind the priest's vest. You may take it if you want. There is no other sword here."

David said, "There is none like it. Give it to me."

David Runs to Gath

10That day David got up and ran from Saul. He went to Achish, king of Gath.

11Achish's servants said to him, "Isn't this David, the king of the land? Don't they sing about him, 'Saul has killed thousands, and David has killed tens of thousands'?"

12David thought about these words. He was very afraid of Achish, king of Gath.

David Acts Like He Is Crazy

13He changed the way he acted in front of them. He acted like he was crazy. He scratched on the doors and let spit run down his beard.

14Achish said to his servants, "Look! You see a man who is out of his mind. Why did you bring him to me? 15Am I short of crazy people? Why did you bring this man to act crazy in front of me? Should this man come into my house?"
