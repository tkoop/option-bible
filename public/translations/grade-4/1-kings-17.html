Elijah Warns King Ahab

1Elijah was from Tishbe in Gilead. He said to King Ahab, "I serve the LORD, the God of Israel. He is alive! There will be no dew or rain for years, unless I say so."

Elijah Hides by the Brook

2Then the LORD spoke to him. He said, 3"Go away from here. Turn east and hide by the BrookbrookBrook Cherith, east of the Jordan. 4You will drink from the brook. I have told the ravens to feed you there." 5So Elijah went and did what the LORD said. He stayed by the BrookbrookBrook Cherith, east of the Jordan. 6The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and evening, and he drank from the brook. 7After a while, the brook dried up because no rain fell in the land.

Elijah Goes to Zarephath

8Then the LORD spoke to him again. He said, 9"Get up and go to Zarephath near Sidon. Stay there. I have told a widow there to feed you."

10So Elijah got up and went to Zarephath. When he came to the city gate, a widow was there picking up sticks. He called to her and said, "Please bring me a little water in a cup so I can drink." 11As she went to get it, he called, "Please bring me a piece of bread too."

12She said, "I swear by the LORD your God, I have no bread. I only have a little flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am picking up two sticks so I can go home and make food for me and my son. We will eat it, and then we will die."

13Elijah said to her, "Do not be afraid. Go and do what you said. But first make a small bread cake for me from what you have, and bring it to me. Then make some for yourself and your son. 14For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'The jar of flour will not run out, and the jug of oil will not be empty, until the day the LORD sends rain on the land.'"

15She went and did what Elijah said. Then she, her family, and Elijah ate for many days. 16The jar of flour did not run out, and the jug of oil did not become empty, just as the LORD had said through Elijah.

Elijah Brings the Widow's Son Back to Life

17Later, the son of the woman who owned the house got very sick. He got so sick that he stopped breathing.

18The woman said to Elijah, "What do you have against me, man of God? Did you come here to remind me of my sin and kill my son?"

19Elijah said, "Give me your son." He took the boy from her arms, carried him to the upper room where he stayed, and laid him on his bed.

20Elijah prayed to the LORD, "the LORD my God, did YouyouYou bring trouble on this widow I am staying with by killing her son?" 21He stretched himself over the boy three times and cried out to the LORD, "the LORD my God, let this boy's life come back to him!" 22The LORD heard Elijah, and the boy's life came back to him, and he lived.

23Elijah took the boy and brought him down from the upper room into the house. He gave him to his mother and said, "Look, your son is alive."

24The woman said to Elijah, "Now I know that you are a man of God, and that the word of the LORD from your mouth is true."
