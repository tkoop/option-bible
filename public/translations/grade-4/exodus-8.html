God Sends Gnats

16Then the the LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and hit the dust of the ground. It will become gnats all over the land of Egypt.'"

17They did it. When Aaron stretched out his hand with the staff and hit the dust, gnats came on people and animals. All the dust of the ground became gnats all over Egypt.

18The magic men tried to make gnats with their tricks, but they could not. Gnats stayed on people and animals.

19The magic men said to Pharaoh, "God did this." But Pharaoh became stubborn and would not listen to them, just as the the LORD had said.
