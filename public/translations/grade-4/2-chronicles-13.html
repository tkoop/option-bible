Abijah Becomes King

1In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, Abijah became king of Judah. 2He ruled in Jerusalem for three years. His mother was Micaiah. She was the daughter of Uriel from Gibeah. There was war between Abijah and Jeroboam. 3Abijah went to war with 400,000 brave soldiers. Jeroboam got ready to fight him with 800,000 brave soldiers.

Abijah Speaks

4Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in the hills of Ephraim. He said, "Listen to me, Jeroboam and all Israel. 5You know the LORD, the God of Israel, gave the kingdom to David and his sons forever. He made a lasting promise with them.

"6Jeroboam son of Nebat was a servant of Solomon son of David. But Jeroboam turned against his master and took power. 7Some worthless men joined him. They grew strong against Rehoboam son of Solomon. Rehoboam was young and soft-hearted, so he could not stop them.

"8Now you think you can stand against the kingdom of the LORD. That kingdom is in the hands of David's sons. You have many people, and you have the gold calves Jeroboam made for you. He made them to be your gods. 9You drove away the LORD's priests, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites. You made your own priests, like other countries do. Anyone who brings a young bull and seven rams can become a priest for gods that are not real. 10But we serve the LORD our God. We have not left HimhimHim. The priests who serve the LORD are Aaron's sons, and the Levites help them. 11Every morning and evening they burn offerings and sweet incense to the LORD. They set out bread on the clean table. They light the gold lampstand each evening. We do the LORD's work. But you left HimhimHim. 12God is with us. He leads us. His priests blow the trumpets to warn you. People of Israel, do not fight the LORD, the God of your fathers. You will not win."

The Battle

13Jeroboam sent men to sneak around behind them. His army stood in front of Judah, and the hidden men stood behind them. 14Judah turned around and saw they were trapped front and back. They cried out to the LORD, and the priests blew the trumpets. 15The men of Judah shouted. When they shouted, God struck Jeroboam and all Israel in front of Abijah and Judah. 16The Israelites ran away from Judah. God gave them into Judah's hands. 17Abijah and his people hit them hard. Five hundred thousand chosen men from Israel died. 18At that time the Israelites were brought low. The men of Judah won because they trusted the LORD, the God of their fathers.

After the Battle

19Abijah chased Jeroboam and took cities from him. He took Bethel and its towns, Jeshanah and its towns, and Ephron and its towns. 20Jeroboam never got his strength back while Abijah lived. The LORD struck him, and he died.

Abijah's Family

21Abijah grew strong. He married fourteen wives. He had twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters. 22The rest of Abijah's story, his ways and words, is written in the book of the prophet Iddo.
