Advice for a Son

1My son, do not forget my teaching. Keep my rules in your heart. 2They will help you live a long and peaceful life.

3Do not let kindness and truth leave you. Keep them with you always. Remember them in your heart. 4Then God and people will like you. They will see you as wise.
