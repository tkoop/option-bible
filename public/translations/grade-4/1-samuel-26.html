Men from Ziph Tell Saul Where David Is

1The men from Ziph came to Saul at Gibeah. They said, "David is hiding on the hill of Hakilah, near the desert."

2So Saul took 3,000 chosen men from Israel. He went down to the desert of Ziph to look for David there.

3Saul camped by the road on the hill of Hakilah, near the desert. David stayed in the desert. He saw that Saul had come after him.

4David sent out spies. He learned that Saul had really come.

5David went to the place where Saul had camped. He saw where Saul and Abner, son of Ner, the army leader, were lying. Saul lay inside the camp. The army camped around him.

David and Abishai Go to Saul's Camp

6David asked Ahimelek the Hittite and Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, "Who will go down with me to Saul in the camp?"

Abishai said, "I will go with you."

7So David and Abishai went to the army at night. Saul was asleep inside the camp. His spear was stuck in the ground by his head. Abner and the soldiers lay around him.

8Abishai said to David, "God has given your enemy to you today. Let me hit him once with the spear. I will not hit him twice."

9But David said, "Do not kill him! Who can hurt the the LORD's chosen king and not be guilty?"

10David also said, "I swear by the the LORD, the the LORD may strike him down. Or his time may come, and he may die. Or he may go to war and die."

11But David said, "May the the LORD keep me from hurting the the LORD's chosen king. Now take the spear and the water jar by his head. Let's go."

12David took the spear and the water jar from near Saul's head. They left. No one saw or knew. No one woke up. They were all asleep, because the the LORD had made them sleep deeply.

David Calls to Saul's Army

13Then David crossed to the other side. He stood on top of a hill far away. There was a big space between them.

14He called to the army and to Abner son of Ner. He said, "Abner, won't you answer me?"

Abner answered, "Who is calling to the king?"

15David said, "YouyouYou are a man, aren't you? Who is like you in Israel? Why did you not guard your lord the king? Someone came to kill your lord the king. 16What you did is not good. I swear by the the LORD, you and your men should die, because you did not guard your master, the the LORD's chosen king. Look around. Where are the king's spear and water jar that were by his head?"

Saul and David Talk

17Saul knew David's voice. He said, "IsisIs that your voice, David my son?"

David said, "Yes, my lord the king. 18Why does my lord chase his servant? What have I done? What wrong did I do? 19Now, my lord the king, please listen to me. If the the LORD made you turn against me, may HeheHe accept a gift. But if people did it, may they be cursed before the the LORD. They drove me out today from sharing in the the LORD's land. They said, 'Go serve other gods.' 20Now do not let my blood fall to the ground away from the the LORD. The king of Israel came out to look for a flea, like someone hunting a bird in the mountains."

21Saul said, "I have sinned. Come back, David my son. Because you treated my life as precious today, I will not harm you again. I have acted like a fool. I was very wrong."

22David said, "Here is the king's spear. Let one of your young men come over and get it. 23The LORD gives each man what he deserves for doing right and being faithful. The LORD gave you to me today, but I would not hurt the the LORD's chosen king. 24As your life was precious to me today, may my life be precious to the the LORD. May HeheHe save me from all trouble."

25Saul said to David, "YouyouYou are blessed, David my son. You will do great things and win." Then David went on his way, and Saul went home.
