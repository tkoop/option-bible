God's Warning to Egypt

1The LORD spoke to me. He said,

"2SonsonSon of man, prophesy. Tell the people this message from the Lord, the LORD: Cry out, 'Oh no for that day!' 3For the day is near. The DaydayDay of the LORD is near. It will be a day of clouds. It will be a time of trouble for the nations.

"4A sword will come to Egypt. Cush will have great fear when many die in Egypt. Egypt's riches will be taken away. Its strong places will be torn down. 5Cush, Put, Lud, and all the mixed people will die by the sword. Kub and the people of the covenant land will die too.

"6This is what the LORD says: The people who help Egypt will fall. Egypt's proud strength will come down. From Migdol to Syene, they will die by the sword inside Egypt. This is what the Lord, the LORD, says. 7They will be empty like empty lands. Their cities will be among ruined cities.

"8Then they will know that I am the LORD. I will set fire to Egypt. All who help her will be broken. 9On that day, messengers will go out from MemeMe in ships. They will scare Cush so it does not feel safe. Great fear will come on the day Egypt is judged. Yes, it is coming.

"10This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I will stop the crowds of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. 11He and his people will come. They are the most cruel nation. They will come to destroy the land. They will draw their swords against Egypt. They will fill the land with dead people.

"12I will dry up the streams of the Nile. I will give the land to bad men. I will ruin the land and all in it by the hand of strangers. I, the LORD, have spoken. 13This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I will destroy the false gods. I will stop the idols in Memphis. There will be no more prince in Egypt. I will put fear in the land of Egypt.

"14I will ruin Pathros. I will set fire to Zoan. I will punish Thebes. 15I will pour out MymyMy anger on Pelusium, the strong place of Egypt. I will cut off the crowds of Thebes. 16I will set fire to Egypt. Pelusium will shake in pain. Thebes will be split open. Memphis will have trouble every day.

"17The young men of On and Pi-beseth will die by the sword. Those cities will be taken away. 18In Tehaphnehes, the day will be dark. I will break Egypt's power there. Then Egypt's proud strength will stop. A cloud will cover her. Her daughters will be taken away. 19So I will judge Egypt. Then they will know that I am the LORD."

Pharaoh's Arm Will Be Broken

20In the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month, the LORD spoke to me. He said,

"21SonsonSon of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh, king of Egypt. It has not been wrapped to heal. It has not been bandaged to make it strong enough to hold a sword. 22So this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I am against Pharaoh, king of Egypt. I will break both his arms, the strong one and the weak one. I will make the sword fall from his hand.

"23I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations. I will spread them out in the lands. 24I will make the arms of the king of Babylon strong. I will put MymyMy sword in his hand. I will break the arms of Pharaoh. He will cry out in pain before him, like a wounded man.

"25I will make the arms of the king of Babylon strong. But the arms of Pharaoh will fall. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I put MymyMy sword into the hand of the king of Babylon. He will use it against the land of Egypt. 26I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations. I will spread them out in the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
