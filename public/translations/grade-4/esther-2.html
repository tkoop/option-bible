King Xerxes Looks for a New Queen

1After these things, King Xerxes was not angry anymore. He remembered Queen Vashti, what she had done, and what he had decided about her.

2The king's helpers said, "Let us look for pretty young girls for the king. They should never have been married. 3Let the king choose leaders in every part of his kingdom. They can bring all the pretty young girls to the women's rooms in the fort of Susa. Put them with Hegai, the king's servant who takes care of the women. Give them things to make them beautiful. 4Then the girl who makes the king happy can be queen instead of Vashti." This idea made the king happy, so he did it.

A Jewish Man Named Mordecai

5In the fort of Susa there was a Jewish man named Mordecai. He was the son of Jair, the son of Shimei, the son of Kish. He was from the family of Benjamin. 6He had been taken from Jerusalem to Babylon. King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took him when he carried away Jeconiah, the king of Judah, and other people. 7Mordecai took care of his cousin Hadassah, also called Esther. She had no father or mother. She was lovely and beautiful. When her parents died, Mordecai took her as his own daughter.

Esther Is Taken to the Palace

8When the king's order was heard, many young girls were brought to the fort of Susa to Hegai. Esther was also taken to the king's palace and put with Hegai, who took care of the women. 9She pleased him and won his favourfavourfavor. He quickly gave her beauty care, her food, and seven special helpers from the king's palace. He moved her and her helpers to the nicest place in the women's rooms. 10Esther did not tell anyone about her people or her family, because Mordecai had told her not to. 11Every day Mordecai walked in front of the courtyard of the women's rooms. He wanted to know how Esther was and what was happening to her.

The Girls Prepare to See the King

12Before a young woman went in to King Xerxes, she had to have twelve months of beauty care. She had six months with oil of myrrh and six months with perfumes and makeup. 13When she went in to the king, she could take anything she wanted from the women's rooms to the king's palace. 14She went in the evening and came back in the morning to the second women's rooms. Shaashgaz, the king's servant who took care of the other women, was in charge there. She did not go to the king again unless he liked her and called her by name.

Esther Wins the King's FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor

15When it was Esther's turn to go in to the king, she did not ask for anything except what Hegai, the king's servant who took care of the women, told her to ask for. Everyone who saw Esther liked her. 16She was taken to King Xerxes in his palace in the tenth month, the month of Tebeth, in the seventh year of his rule. 17The king loved Esther more than all the other women. She won his favourfavourfavor and kindness more than all the other young girls. He put the royal crown on her head and made her queen instead of Vashti. 18The king gave a big party for Esther. He invited all his leaders and servants. He gave a holiday to the lands he ruled. He gave gifts generously.

Mordecai at the King's Gate

19When the young girls were gathered a second time, Mordecai was sitting at the king's gate. 20Esther still did not tell her family or people, just as Mordecai had told her. She kept doing what Mordecai said, just as she did when he raised her.

Mordecai Saves the King

21In those days, Mordecai was sitting at the king's gate. Bigthan and Teresh, two of the king's servants who guarded the door, got angry. They planned to hurt King Xerxes. 22Mordecai heard about the plan. He told Queen Esther, and she told the king that Mordecai had heard it. 23The plan was checked and found true. Both men were hanged on a pole. This was written in the book of the king's records.
