A Good King Will Come

1Look! A king will rule in a fair way. 2Leaders will rule with fairness. Each one will be like a safe place from wind and from a storm, like streams of water in dry land, like shade from a big rock in a tired land.

3Then people who see will keep their eyes open. People who hear will listen. 4A person who is quick to act will learn and understand. People who stutter will speak clearly.

5No longer will a foolish person be called good. No longer will a selfish person be called kind. 6A foolish person talks nonsense. His mind plans bad things. He does bad things. He lies about the LORD. He leaves hungry people with nothing. He keeps thirsty people from drink. 7A selfish person's ways are bad. He makes bad plans to hurt poor people with lies. He does this even when needy people ask for what is right. 8But a good person makes good plans. He stands up for good things.

Women, Listen Up

9“Get up, you women who feel safe! Listen to my voice. Pay attention to my words, you daughters who feel safe. 10In a little more than a year, you safe women will shake with fear. The grape harvest will fail. There will be no new wine.

11“Shake with fear, you women who feel safe! Tremble, you women who are too sure! Take off your clothes and put on rough cloth. 12Beat your chests in sadness for the good fields, for the vines that gave fruit.”

Trouble Will Come

13Thorns and weeds will grow on MymyMy people's land. They will grow on all the happy homes in the joyful city. 14The palace will be left empty. The city full of people will be left alone. The hill and the watchtower will become caves forever. Wild donkeys will enjoy them. Flocks of sheep will eat there.

God Will Make Things New

15This will last until the SpiritspiritSpirit is poured out on us from above. Then the dry land will become good land. Good land will seem like a forest. 16Then what is right will live in the wild land. What is good will stay in the good land. 17Doing what is right will bring peace. Serving what is right will bring calm trust forever. 18Then MymyMy people will live in a peaceful place. They will live in safe tents and safe homes.

19But hail will knock down the forest. The city will sink down to low places. 20You will be happy if you plant seeds by all the water and let the ox and donkey walk free.
