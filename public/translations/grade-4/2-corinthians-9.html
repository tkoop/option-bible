Help for God's People

1I do not need to write much about helping God's people. 2I know you are ready to give. I even brag about you to the people in Macedonia. I tell them that Achaia has been ready since last year. Your excitement made many others want to give too. 3But I sent our friends ahead. I did not want our bragging to be empty. I want you to be ready, like I said. 4What if people from Macedonia come with me and find you not ready? Then we would feel ashamed. You would too, because we trusted you. 5So I asked our friends to go first. They will help get your promised gift ready. I want it to be a real blessing, not something from greed.

Give with a Happy Heart

6Remember this: A farmer who plants only a little will gather only a little. A farmer who plants a lot will gather a lot. 7Each person should give what he chose in his heart. Do not give because you feel sad or because someone forces you. God loves a giver who is happy to give. 8God can give you more than enough kindness. Then you will always have what you need. You will have plenty to do every good work. 9The Bible says, "HeheHe gave freely to the poor. His good ways last forever."

God Gives More

10God gives seed to the farmer and bread to eat. He will give you seed too. He will make it grow. He will make your good works grow. 11God will make you rich in every way so you can share. Then many people will thank God because of us. 12This gift helps God's people. It fills what they need. It also makes many people thank God. 13This gift shows that you obey the good news about Christ. It also shows that you share with them and with everyone. People will praise God for this. 14They will pray for you. They long to see you because God has given you so much kindness. 15Thank God for HishisHis gift that is too great to tell!
