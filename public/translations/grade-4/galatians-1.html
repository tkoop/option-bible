A Different Good News

6I am amazed! You are turning away so fast from the God who called you by the grace of Christ. Now you follow a different good news. 7It is not really another good news. Some people are troubling you. They want to change the good news about Christ. 8But even if we or an angel from Heavenheavenheaven tells you a different good news, let that one be cursed. 9We said this before. I say it again now. If anyone tells you a different good news, let that one be cursed. 10Am I now trying to please people or God? If I still tried to please people, I would not be a servant of Christ.

How Paul Became a Messenger

11I want you to know, brothers, that the good news I told you did not come from people. 12No person gave it to me. No person taught it to me. Jesus Christ showed it to me. 13You heard about my old life in the Jewish faith. I hurt God's church very much. I tried to destroy it. 14I was doing better than many people my age in the Jewish faith. I worked very hard to follow the old ways of my people.

15But God chose me before I was born. He called me by HishisHis grace. 16God was happy to show HishisHis SonsonSon to me. He wanted me to tell people who are not Jews about HimhimHim. I did not go ask people what to do. 17I did not go to Jerusalem to see the special messengers who were there before me. I went to Arabia. Then I went back to Damascus.

18After three years, I went to Jerusalem. I visited Peter. I stayed with him for fifteen days. 19I did not see any other special messengers. I only saw James, the Lord's brother. 20I am writing this to you. God sees me. I am not lying.

21Then I went to the areas of Syria and Cilicia. 22The churches in Judea that belong to Christ did not know me in person yet. 23They only heard people say, "ThetheThe man who used to hurt us is now teaching the faith he once tried to destroy." 24They praised God because of me.
