Peter Talks to Church Leaders

1I am an elder too. I saw Christ suffer. I will share in the glory that will be shown. I ask the elders among you to listen. 2Take care of God's people near you. Watch over them. Do it because you want to, not because you have to. Do it with a ready heart, not to make money. 3Do not boss the people God gave you. Be a good example to them. 4When the ChiefchiefChief ShepherdshepherdShepherd comes, you will get a crown of glory that never fades.

Be Humble and Trust God

5Younger people, obey your elders. All of you should wear humility like clothes when you are with each other. God fights against proud people, but HeheHe gives grace to humble people. 6So be humble under God's strong hand. Then HeheHe will lift you up at the right time. 7Give all your worries to HimhimHim, because HeheHe cares about you.

Watch Out for the Devil

8Stay alert and be careful. The devil is your enemy. He walks around like a roaring lion. He wants to eat someone up. 9Stand up to him. Stay strong in your faith. Remember that your brothers and sisters all over the world are going through the same hard times.

God Will Make You Strong

10After you suffer for a little while, God will make you whole again. He is the God who gives all grace. He called you to share HishisHis glory that lasts forever in Christ. He will make you steady, make you strong, and give you a firm base. 11All power belongs to HimhimHim forever. Amen.

Final Greetings

12I wrote this short letter through Silvanus. I think he is a faithful brother. I am asking you and telling you that this is God's true grace. Stay in it. 13The church in Babylon, chosen with you, says hello. So does my son Mark. 14Say hello to each other with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.
