Manasseh Becomes King

1Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for fifty-five years.

Manasseh Does Wrong

2He did what was wrong in the LORD's eyes. He did the bad things that the nations did. The LORD had driven those nations out before the people of Israel.

3He built again the high places that his father Hezekiah had torn down. He made altars for the Baals. He made poles for Asherah. He bowed down to all the stars in the sky and served them.

4He built altars in the LORD's temple. The LORD had said, "MymyMy name will be in Jerusalem forever." 5He built altars in the two courtyards of the LORD's temple for all the stars in the sky.

6He burned his sons in the fire in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom. He used magic and guessed the future. He did witchcraft. He asked mediums and people who spoke to spirits. He did much wrong in the LORD's eyes. He made the LORD angry.

7He put the carved idol he made in God's temple. God had said to David and to his son Solomon, "In this temple and in Jerusalem, which I chose from all the tribes of Israel, I will put MymyMy name forever. 8I will not make Israel leave the land I gave their fathers. But they must keep all I told them through Moses. They must obey all the law, rules, and decisions." 9Manasseh led Judah and the people of Jerusalem away from the right path. They did more wrong than the nations the LORD had destroyed before the people of Israel.

The LORD Warns Manasseh

10The LORD spoke to Manasseh and to his people. But they did not listen.

Manasseh Is Taken Away

11So the LORD brought the army leaders of the king of Assyria against them. They caught Manasseh. They put hooks in him. They tied him with bronze chains. They took him to Babylon.

Manasseh Turns Back to God

12When he was in trouble, he asked the LORD his God for help. He was very sorry and bowed before the God of his fathers. 13He prayed to the LORD. The LORD listened to him. The LORD heard his prayer. The LORD brought him back to Jerusalem and gave him back his kingdom. Then Manasseh knew that the LORD is God.

Manasseh Fixes Jerusalem

14After that, he built the outer wall of the CitycityCity of David. It was on the west side of Gihon in the valley. It went to the FishfishFish GategateGate. It went around the hill. He made it very high. He put army leaders in all the strong cities of Judah. 15He took away the foreign gods and the idol from the LORD's temple. He took away all the altars he had built on the temple hill and in Jerusalem. He threw them out of the city. 16He fixed the LORD's altar. He gave peace offerings and thank offerings on it. He told Judah, "Serve the LORD God of Israel." 17The people still gave offerings at the high places. But they gave them only to the LORD their God.

More About Manasseh

18The rest of what Manasseh did is written down. So is his prayer to his God. So are the words of the prophets who spoke to him in the name of the LORD God of Israel. They are in the records of the kings of Israel. 19His prayer and how the LORD listened to him are written down. So are all his sins and wrong ways. So are the places where he built high places. So are the places where he set up poles for Asherah and idols before he was sorry. They are in the records of Hozai.

Manasseh Dies

20Manasseh died and was buried with his fathers in his palace. His son Amon became king after him.

Amon Becomes King

21Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for two years.

Amon Does Wrong

22He did what was wrong in the LORD's eyes, like his father Manasseh did. Amon gave sacrifices to all the carved idols Manasseh had made. He served them. 23He did not bow before the LORD, like his father Manasseh did. Instead, Amon added to his wrong.

Amon Is Killed

24His servants made a plan against him. They killed him in his palace. 25The people of the land killed all who had planned against King Amon. Then they made his son Josiah king.
