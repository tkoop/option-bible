Send Sick People Out of Camp

1The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 2"Tell the people of Israel to send out of the camp everyone with a bad skin disease, everyone with a body discharge, and everyone who touched a dead body. 3Send out both men and women. Put them outside the camp so they do not make the camp unclean, because I live among you. 4The Israelites did this. They sent them outside the camp. They did what the LORD said to Moses."

Make Things Right After Sin

5The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 6"Tell the people of Israel: If a man or woman does any wrong that people do, and is not true to the LORD, that person is guilty. 7He must tell about his sin. He must pay back for the wrong he did, and add one-fifth more. He must give it to the person he wronged. 8If the man has no family member to pay back, the payback goes to the LORD for the priest. This is besides the ram used to make him right with God. 9Every holy gift the Israelites bring to the priest belongs to the priest. 10Each person keeps his own holy things. But whatever anyone gives to the priest belongs to the priest."

When a Husband Is Jealous

11The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 12"Tell the people of Israel: If a man's wife turns away and is not true to him, 13and a man sleeps with her, and her husband does not know, and she becomes unclean in secret, with no one to speak against her, because she was not caught, 14and if a jealous feeling comes over the husband, and he is jealous of his wife, whether she is unclean or not, 15then the man must bring his wife to the priest. He must bring an offering for her: one-tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put incense on it, because it is a grain offering for jealousy. It helps people remember and shows guilt."

16The priest must bring her near and stand her before the LORD. 17The priest must take holy water in a clay jar. He must take dust from the floor of the holy tent and put it into the water. 18The priest must stand the woman before the LORD. He must let her hair hang loose. He must put the grain offering for remembering in her hands. The priest holds the bitter water that brings a curse.

19The priest must make her promise and say, "If no man has slept with you, and if you have not turned away to become unclean while married to your husband, then you will be free from this bitter water that brings a curse. 20But if you have turned away while married to your husband, and if you have made yourself unclean, and a man other than your husband has slept with you, 21then the LORD will make you a curse and an oath among your people. He will make your leg shrink and your belly swell. 22May this water that brings a curse go into your body, so your belly swells and your leg shrinks."

And the woman must say, "AmenamenAmen, AmenamenAmen."

23Then the priest must write these curses on a scroll and wash them into the bitter water. 24He must make the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse. The water will go into her and become bitter. 25The priest must take the grain offering for jealousy from the woman's hand. He must wave it before the LORD and bring it to the altar. 26The priest must take a handful of the offering as a special part and burn it on the altar. After that, he must make the woman drink the water. 27When she drinks the water, if she has made herself unclean and was not true to her husband, the bitter water will go into her and become bitter. Her belly will swell and her leg will shrink. She will become a curse among her people. 28But if the woman has not made herself unclean, and she is clean, she will be free and will have children.

29This is the law for jealousy. If a wife turns away while married to her husband and becomes unclean, 30or if a jealous feeling comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife, then he must stand her before the LORD, and the priest must do all this law to her. 31The man will be free from guilt, and the woman will carry her guilt.
