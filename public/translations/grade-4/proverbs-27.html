Don't Brag About Tomorrow

1Do not brag about tomorrow. You do not know what tomorrow will bring. 2Let someone else praise you. Do not praise yourself.

A Fool's Anger Is Heavy

3A stone is heavy. Sand is heavy too. But a fool's anger is heavier than both. 4Anger is mean. Rage is wild. But who can stand up to jealousy?

Honest Friends Are Better

5It is better for someone to correct you openly than to love you in secret. 6A true friend may hurt you with honest words. An enemy may say too many nice things to you.

Hungry People Like Bitter Food

7A full person does not want honey. A hungry person thinks even bitter food tastes sweet. 8A person who leaves home is like a bird that leaves its nest.

Good Advice Makes You Happy

9Oil and perfume make the heart glad. Good advice from a friend is sweet too.

Keep Good Friends Close

10Do not leave your friend or your father's friend. Do not go to your brother's house when trouble comes. A close neighbourneighbourneighbor is better than a brother far away.

Be Wise, My Son

11Be wise, my son, and make my heart glad. Then I can answer those who make fun of me.

Wise People See Trouble

12A wise person sees trouble and hides. A foolish person keeps going and gets hurt.

Be Careful with Promises

13If someone promises to pay a stranger's debt, take his coat as a promise. If he does it for a woman he does not know, hold him to it.

Don't Shout Early Blessings

14If you shout a blessing to your neighbourneighbourneighbor early in the morning, it will seem like a curse.

A Quarrelsome Wife

15A wife who always argues is like a leaky roof on a rainy day. 16Trying to stop her is like trying to stop the wind or hold oil in your hand.

Friends Make Each Other Better

17Just as iron makes iron sharp, one person can help another grow.

Care for What You Have

18If you take care of a fig tree, you eat its fruit. If you serve your master well, you will be honouredhonouredhonored.

Your Heart Shows Who You Are

19Water shows your face. In the same way, your heart shows who you are.

People Are Never Full

20Death and ruin are never full. People's eyes are never full either.

Praise Tests a Person

21Silver is tested by fire. Gold is tested by fire too. A person is tested by praise.

Fools Stay Foolish

22Even if you grind a fool like grain, his foolishness will not go away.

Care for Your Animals

23Know how your sheep are doing. Pay attention to your herds. 24Money does not last forever. A crown does not last forever either. 25When the grass is gone, new plants grow. The plants on the mountains are gathered. 26Your lambs will give you clothes. Your goats will help you buy a field. 27There will be enough goat milk for your food, your family's food, and your servant girls.
