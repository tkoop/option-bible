Eliphaz Talks to Job

“1Call out now. Will anyone answer you? Which holy one will you turn to? 2For anger kills a fool, and jealousy kills a simple person. 3I have seen a fool take root, but then I cursed his home right away. 4His sons are far from safety. They are crushed at the city gate, and no one saves them. 5Hungry people eat his crops, even from thorn bushes. Thirsty people long for his riches.

“6For hardship does not come from the dust. Trouble does not grow from the ground. 7But people are born to trouble, just like sparks fly up.

God Does Great Things

“8But I would ask God for help. I would bring my case to God. 9He does great things we cannot find out. His wonders have no number. 10He gives rain to the earth and sends water on the fields. 11He lifts humble people up high, and sad people are lifted to safety. 12He stops the plans of sneaky people, so their hands do not succeed.

“13He catches wise people in their own tricks. The plans of sneaky people fail fast. 14They meet darkness in the day and feel around at noon like it is night. 15He saves poor people from cruel words and from the hand of strong people. 16So poor people have hope, and wrong is silenced.

God Corrects and Blesses

“17Happy is the man God corrects, so do not hate the lessons of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 18For HeheHe hurts, but HeheHe also bandages. He strikes, but HishisHis hands heal. 19He will save you from six troubles. No harm will touch you in seven. 20In hunger HeheHe will save you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. 21You will be safe from cruel words, and you will not fear ruin when it comes.

“22You will laugh at ruin and hunger, and you will not fear wild animals. 23For you will have an agreement with the stones of the field, and wild animals will be at peace with you. 24You will know your tent is safe, and you will check your home and find nothing wrong. 25You will know your children will be many, and your family will be like grass on the earth. 26You will go to the grave at a good old age, like grain brought in at the right time. 27We have checked this, and it is true. So listen and know it for yourself.”
