God Speaks from the Storm

1Then the LORD answered Job from the storm and said:

2“Who is this who makes things dark with words that have no knowledge?

3“Get ready like a man. I will ask you questions, and you will answer MemeMe.

Questions About the Earth

4“Where were you when I laid the earth's foundation? Tell MemeMe, if you understand.

5“Who set its size, since you know? Or who stretched the measuring line over it?

6“What holds up its bases? Or who laid its corner stone,

7“when the morning stars sang together and all God's sons shouted for joy?

Questions About the Sea

8“Who shut up the sea behind doors when it burst out at birth,

9“when I made clouds its clothes and thick darkness its blanket?

10“When I set MymyMy rule for it and put bars and doors on it,

11“and said, ‘You may come this far, but no farther. Here your proud waves must stop’?

Questions About Light and Darkness

12“Have you ever told the morning what to do since your days began? Have you shown the dawn where to be,

13“so it could grab the earth's edges and shake the wicked out of it?

14“The EarthEarthearth turns like clay under a stamp, and its parts stand out like clothes.

15“The light of the wicked is taken away, and their strong arm is broken.

Questions About the Deep Places

16“Have you gone to the springs of the sea? Have you walked in the deep parts of the ocean?

17“Have the gates of death been shown to you? Have you seen the gates of deep darkness?

18“Have you understood how wide the earth is? Tell MemeMe, if you know all of it.

Questions About Light and Dark

19“Where is the road to where light lives? And where does darkness stay,

20“so you can take it to its land and know the paths to its home?

21“Surely you know, because you were born then, and your days are many!

Questions About Snow, Hail, and Wind

22“Have you gone into the storehouses of snow? Have you seen the storehouses of hail,

23“which I keep for hard times, for the day of battle and war?

24“Where is the road where light is split, or where the east wind is scattered over the earth?

25“Who cut a channel for the heavy rain and a path for the thunder?

26“to rain on land where no one lives, on the empty desert,

27“to fill empty and ruined places and make the grass grow?

Questions About Rain and Dew

28“Does rain have a father? Or who made the drops of dew?

29“Who is the mother of ice? Who gave birth to the frost from the sky?

30“Water becomes hard like stone, and the deep sea freezes over.

Questions About the Stars

31“Can you tie up the Pleiades? Can you untie Orion's belt?

32“Can you bring out the stars in their time? Can you lead the Bear and her cubs?

33“Do you know the rules of the sky? Can you set their power over the earth?

Questions About Clouds and Lightning

34“Can you shout to the clouds so a flood of water covers you?

35“Can you send out lightning so it goes and says to you, ‘Here we are’?

36“Who put wisdom in people's hearts? Who gave understanding to their minds?

37“Who can count the clouds with wisdom? Who can tip over the sky's water jars,

38“when the dust becomes mud and the clods stick together?

Questions About Animals

39“Can you hunt food for the lion? Can you feed the young lions,

40“when they hide in their dens and wait in the bushes?

41“Who feeds the raven when its babies cry to God and wander around hungry?”
