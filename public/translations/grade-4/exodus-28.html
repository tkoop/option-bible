Priest Clothes for Aaron

1Bring your brother Aaron and his sons to you from the Israelites. They are Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. Aaron will serve MemeMe as a priest. 2Make holy clothes for your brother Aaron. They will show his honourhonourhonor and beauty.

3Talk to all the wise men I have filled with a wise spirit. They will make Aaron's clothes. The clothes will set him apart to serve MemeMe as a priest. 4These are the clothes they will make: a chest piece, an ephod, a robe, a patterned coat, a turban, and a sash. Make holy clothes for your brother Aaron and his sons. They will serve MemeMe as priests. 5They will take gold, blue cloth, purple cloth, red cloth, and fine white cloth.

The Ephod

6Make the ephod from gold thread, blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine twisted linen. Make it with skill. 7It will have two shoulder pieces joined to its two ends. 8The belt on it will be one piece with it. Make the belt like the ephod, from gold, blue, purple, red yarn, and fine twisted linen.

The Shoulder Stones

9Take two onyx stones. Carve the names of Israel's sons on them. 10Put six names on one stone and six names on the other stone. Put them in the order the sons were born. 11A skilled worker will carve the names. Then set the stones in gold frames. 12Put the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod. They will help people remember Israel's sons. Aaron will carry their names on his two shoulders before the LORD. It will help people remember them.

13Make gold frames. 14Make two chains of pure gold. Make them like twisted cords. Put the chains on the frames.

The Chest Piece

15Make a chest piece for decisions. Make it with skill, like the ephod. Make it from gold, blue, purple, red yarn, and fine twisted linen. 16It will be square and folded double. It will be one hand span long and one hand span wide. 17Put four rows of stones in it. The first row will have a ruby, a topaz, and an emerald. 18The second row will have a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond. 19The third row will have a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst. 20The fourth row will have a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. Set them in gold frames. 21There will be twelve stones. Each stone will have the name of one of Israel's sons. Carve each name like a seal. The stones will stand for the twelve tribes.

22Make two gold chains for the chest piece. Make them like twisted cords. 23Make two gold rings for the chest piece. Put them on the two upper corners. 24Put the two gold chains through the two rings at the corners of the chest piece. 25Fasten the other ends of the two chains to the two gold settings. Put them on the shoulder straps of the ephod in front. 26Make two gold rings. Put them on the two lower corners of the chest piece, on the side that faces the ephod. 27Make two more gold rings. Put them low on the two shoulder straps of the ephod, near the front. Put them close to where the pieces join, above the belt of the ephod. 28Tie the chest piece to the ephod with blue cord. Tie it through the rings. Then the chest piece will not come loose from the ephod.

29When Aaron goes into the holy place, he will carry the names of Israel's sons on his heart. The chest piece will help people remember them before the LORD. 30Put the Urim and Thummim in the chest piece. They will be on Aaron's heart when he stands before the LORD. Aaron will always carry the means to make decisions for Israel's people before the LORD.

The Robe

31Make the robe of the ephod all of blue cloth. 32Make an opening at the top for the head. Put a woven edge around the opening, like a collar. It must not tear. 33Make shapes like pomegranates of blue, purple, and red yarn around the bottom edge. Put gold bells between them all around. 34Put a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, all around the bottom edge of the robe. 35Aaron will wear it when he serves. People will hear the bells when he goes into the holy place before the LORD and when he comes out. Then he will not die.

The Gold Plate

36Make a pure gold plate. Carve on it: Holy to the LORD. 37Tie it to the turban with a blue cord. It will be on the front of the turban. 38Aaron will wear it on his forehead. He will carry the guilt connected to the holy gifts Israel's people set apart. The gifts include all their holy offerings. The plate will help the LORD accept them.

Clothes for Aaron's Sons

39Make the tunic of fine linen. Make the turban of fine linen. Make the sash with skilled needlework. 40Make coats, sashes, and hats for Aaron's sons. Make them for honourhonourhonor and beauty. 41Dress your brother Aaron and his sons in these clothes. Pour oil on them to set them apart. Give them authority to serve. Set them apart to serve MemeMe as priests. 42Make linen underclothes for them. They must cover from the waist to the thighs. 43Aaron and his sons must wear them when they go into the tent of meeting or come near the altar to serve in the holy place. Then they will not be guilty and die. This is a law for Aaron and his family after him forever.
