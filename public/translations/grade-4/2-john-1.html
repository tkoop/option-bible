Greeting

1The elder writes to the chosen lady and her children. I love them in the truth. All who know the truth love them too. 2The truth stays in us, and it will be with us forever. 3Grace, mercy, and peace come from God the FatherfatherFather and from Jesus Christ, the SonsonSon of the FatherfatherFather. They will be with us in truth and love.

Walking in Truth and Love

4I was very happy. I found that some of your children are walking in the truth. This is what the FatherfatherFather told us to do. 5Now I ask you, lady, not for a new rule. I write this rule we have had from the start: We should love each other. 6This is love: We follow HishisHis rules. This is the rule you heard from the start. You should walk in it.

Watch Out for False Teachers

7Many liars have gone into the world. They do not say that Jesus Christ came as a real person. Such a person is a liar and against Christ. 8Watch yourselves, so you do not lose what we worked for. Then you can get the full reward. 9Anyone who goes too far and does not stay in Christ's teaching does not have God. The one who stays in the teaching has both the FatherfatherFather and the SonsonSon. 10If someone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not let him in. Do not even say hello to him. 11Anyone who says hello to him shares in his bad work.

Final Greeting

12I have much more to write to you. I do not want to use paper and ink. I hope to come and talk with you face to face. Then our joy will be full. 13The children of your chosen sister greet you.
