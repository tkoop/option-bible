Nehemiah Asks the King for Help

1In the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, wine was in front of him. I took the wine and gave it to the king. I had not been sad in front of him.

2The king said to me, "Why is your face sad? You are not sick. This is only sadness inside." I was very afraid.

3I said to the king, "Long live the king! Why should I not look sad? The city where my fathers are buried is in ruins, and its gates were burned by fire."

4The king said to me, "What do you want?" Then I prayed to the God of Heavenheavenheaven.

5I said to the king, "If it pleases the king, and if you are happy with me, your servant, send me to Judah, to the city where my fathers are buried, so I can rebuild it."

6The king and the queen sitting beside him said to me, "How long will your trip be, and when will you come back?" The king was pleased to send me, and I told him a time.

7I said to the king, "If it pleases the king, please give me letters for the governors west of the River, so they will let me pass until I reach Judah."

8I also asked for a letter to Asaph, who takes care of the king's forest. He should give me wood to make beams for the gates of the strong building by the temple, for the city wall, and for the house I will live in. The king gave me these things because my God was helping me.

Nehemiah Travels to Jerusalem

9I went to the governors west of the River and gave them the king's letters. The king had sent army captains and horsemen with me.

10Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard about it. They were very upset that someone had come to help the people of Israel.

Nehemiah Checks the Walls

11I came to Jerusalem and stayed there three days.

12I got up at night with a few men. I did not tell anyone what my God had put in my heart to do for Jerusalem. The only animal with me was the one I rode.

18I told them how my God had helped me, and what the king had said to me. They said, "Let us get up and build." So they got ready to do good work.

Enemies Make Fun of the Builders

19But Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab heard about it. They laughed at us and looked down on us. They said, "Are you rebuilding the wall? Are you going to turn against the king?"
