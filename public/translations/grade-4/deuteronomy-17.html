Do Not Offer Animals with Flaws

1Do not give the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has something wrong with it. That is very wrong to the LORD your God.

Punish People Who Worship Other Gods

2If a man or woman in one of your towns does something bad in the eyes of the LORD your God, and breaks HishisHis promise, 3and goes to serve other gods, and bows down to them, or to the sun, or the moon, or all the stars in the sky, which I did not command, 4and you are told about it and hear it, look into it well. If it is true that this bad thing happened in Israel, 5take that man or woman to your city gate. Throw stones at them until they die.

6The person who dies must be put to death because two or three people saw it. No one may be put to death because only one person saw it. 7The witnesses must be the first to put him to death. Then all the people must help. Remove the bad from among you.

When a Case Is Too Hard

8If a case is too hard for you to judge, like between one kind of blood and another, or one law and another, or one hurt and another, then go to the place the LORD your God will choose. 9Go to the Levite priests and to the judge who is in office then. Ask them, and they will tell you the right answer. 10Do what they say from that place the LORD chooses. Be careful to do all they teach you. 11Follow the law they teach you and the decision they give you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left.

12Anyone who proudly refuses to listen to the priest who stands there to serve the LORD your God, or to the judge, must die. Remove the bad from Israel. 13Then all the people will hear and be afraid. They will not be so proud again.

When You Want a King

14When you enter the land the LORD your God gives you and take it and live there, you may say, "Let us set a king over us like all the nations around us."

15You must set over you the king the LORD your God chooses. He must be from your brothers. You may not set a foreigner over you, who is not your brother.

16The king must not get many horses for himself. He must not send the people back to Egypt to get more horses, because the LORD has said to you, "YouyouYou must not go back that way again."

17He must not take many wives, or his heart will turn away. He must not get too much silver and gold.

18When he sits on his king's seat, he must write a copy of this law on a scroll. He must do it in front of the Levite priests. 19It must stay with him. He must read it all his life. Then he will learn to respect the LORD his God and keep all the words of this law and these rules. He will do them.

20Then his heart will not be proud over his brothers, and he will not turn from the command to the right or to the left. Then he and his sons will rule a long time over Israel.
