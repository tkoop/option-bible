Solomon Gives Offerings

They said HishisHis loving kindness lasts forever. The priests blew trumpets. All Israel stood there. 7Solomon made the middle of the courtyard holy. It was in front of the LORD’s temple. The Festival

They had kept the altar dedication for seven days. They had kept the festival for seven days. 10On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, he sent the people to their tents.
