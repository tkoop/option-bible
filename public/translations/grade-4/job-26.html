Job Talks About How Powerful God Is

1Then Job answered and said,

2"How you helped the weak! You saved a person with no strength. 3How you advised someone with no wisdom! You shared much good advice. 4Who did you speak to? Whose breath came from you?

5The dead shake under the waters, with all who live there. 6The grave is open before HimhimHim. The place of ruin has no cover.

7He stretches out the north over empty space. He hangs the earth on nothing. 8He wraps water in HishisHis clouds. The clouds do not break under the water. 9He covers the face of the full moon. He spreads HishisHis cloud over it. 10He drew a circle on the waters, where light and dark meet.

11The pillars of Heavenheavenheaven shake. They are amazed at HishisHis warning. 12By HishisHis power HeheHe calmed the sea. By HishisHis wisdom HeheHe broke the sea monster Rahab. 13By HishisHis breath the sky became clear. His hand struck the fleeing snake.

14These are only the edges of HishisHis ways. We hear only a faint whisper of HimhimHim. Who can understand HishisHis mighty thunder?"
