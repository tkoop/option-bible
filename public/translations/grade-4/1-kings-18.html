God Tells Elijah to Go to Ahab

1After many days, in the third year, the word of the the LORD came to Elijah. He said, "Go show yourself to Ahab. I will send rain on the earth."

2So Elijah went to show himself to Ahab. The famine was strong in Samaria.

Obadiah Hides God's Prophets

3Ahab called Obadiah, who was over the house. Obadiah feared the the LORD very much.

4When Jezebel was killing the prophets of the the LORD, Obadiah took one hundred prophets. He hid fifty in a cave. He gave them bread and water.

Ahab and Obadiah Look for Grass

5Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs and streams. Maybe we can find grass for the horses and mules. Then we will not have to kill any of our animals."

6They split the land between them. Ahab went one way by himself. Obadiah went the other way by himself.

Elijah Meets Obadiah

7As Obadiah was on the way, Elijah met him. Obadiah knew him. He fell on his face. He said, "Are you my lord Elijah?"

8Elijah said, "I am. Go tell your master, 'Elijah is here.'"

9Obadiah said, "What sin did I do? Why are you giving your servant to Ahab to be killed? 10As the the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent someone to look for you. When they said you were not there, he made them promise they could not find you.

11"And now you say, 'Go tell your lord, "Elijah is here."' 12I do not know where the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD will take you when I leave you. If I go tell Ahab and he does not find you, he will kill me. But I, your servant, have feared the the LORD since I was young. 13Did no one tell my lord what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of the the LORD? I hid one hundred prophets of the the LORD. I put fifty in a cave. I gave them bread and water. 14And now you say, 'Go tell your lord, "Elijah is here."' HeheHe will kill me!"

Elijah Promises to Meet Ahab

15Elijah said, "As the the LORD of all armies lives, before whom I stand, I will show myself to Ahab today."

16Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him. Ahab went to meet Elijah.

Ahab Meets Elijah

17When Ahab saw Elijah, he said to him, "Are you the one who troubles Israel?"

18Elijah said, "I have not troubled Israel. But you and your father's family have. You left the commands of the the LORD. You followed the Baals."

Elijah Calls for a Contest

19Elijah said, "Now call all Israel to me on Mount Carmel. Also bring the four hundred fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah who eat at Jezebel's table."

20So Ahab called all the people of Israel. He gathered the prophets on Mount Carmel.

21Elijah came near to all the people and said, "How long will you go back and forth? If the the LORD is God, follow HimhimHim. If Baal is God, follow him." The people did not answer.

22Elijah said to the people, "I am the only prophet of the the LORD left. But Baal has four hundred fifty prophets."

23Elijah said, "Let us have two bulls. Let them choose one bull. They will cut it up and put it on the wood. They will not light the fire. I will get the other bull. I will put it on the wood. I will not light the fire."

24Elijah said, "Then you will call on the name of your god. I will call on the name of the the LORD. The God who answers by fire, HeheHe is God."

All the people said, "That is good."

25Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "YouyouYou are many. Choose one bull. Make it ready first. Call on the name of your god. Do not light the fire."

26They took the bull that was given to them. They made it ready. They called on the name of Baal from morning until noon. They said, "Baal, answer us!" But there was no voice and no answer. They danced around the altar they had made.

27At noon Elijah made fun of them. He said, "Call louder! He is a god. Maybe he is thinking. Maybe he is busy. Maybe he is on a trip. Maybe he is asleep and must wake up."

28They called louder. They cut themselves with swords and spears. This was their way. Blood ran on them.

29After noon they kept on prophesying until the time for the evening gift. But there was no voice. No one answered. No one listened.

Elijah Rebuilds the Altar

30Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me." All the people came near to him. He fixed the altar of the the LORD that had been torn down.

31Elijah took twelve stones. There was one stone for each tribe of Jacob's sons. The the LORD had said, "YouryourYour name will be Israel."

32With the stones he built an altar in the name of the the LORD. He dug a ditch around the altar. It was big enough to hold two baskets of seed.

33He laid the wood in order. He cut up the bull. He put it on the wood.

34He said, "Fill four jars with water. Pour it on the offering and on the wood." They did. He said, "Do it again." They did it again. He said, "Do it a third time." They did it a third time.

35The water ran around the altar. It even filled the ditch.

Elijah Prays to God

36At the time of the evening gift, Elijah the prophet came near. He said, "O the LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known today that YouyouYou are God in Israel. I am YouryourYour servant. I have done all these things by YouryourYour word."

37"Answer me, the LORD! Answer me, so these people may know that YouyouYou are the the LORD their God, and that YouyouYou have turned their hearts back."

38Then the fire of the the LORD fell. It burned up the offering, the wood, the stones, the dust, and the water in the ditch.

39All the people saw it. They fell on their faces. They said, "ThetheThe the LORD, HeheHe is God! The the LORD, HeheHe is God!"

40Elijah said to them, "Catch the prophets of Baal! Do not let one of them get away." They caught them. Elijah brought them down to the Kishon ValleyvalleyValley. He killed them there.

Rain Is Coming

41Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up. Eat and drink. I hear the sound of much rain."

42Ahab went up to eat and drink. Elijah went up to the top of Carmel. He bent down to the ground. He put his face between his knees.

43Elijah said to his servant, "Go up and look toward the sea." The servant went up and looked. He said, "There is nothing."

Elijah said, "Go back." He did this seven times.

44The seventh time, the servant said, "Look! A small cloud, like a man's hand, is rising from the sea."

Elijah said, "Go tell Ahab, 'Hitch up your chariot and go down. The rain must not stop you.'"

The Rain Falls

45The sky grew dark with clouds and wind. There was a heavy rain. Ahab rode and went to Jezreel.

46The hand of the the LORD was on Elijah. He tied up his coat. He ran ahead of Ahab all the way to Jezreel.
