Jeremiah's Letter to the People Taken Away

1These are the words of the letter Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem. He sent it to the older leaders, the priests, the prophets, and all the people Nebuchadnezzar had taken from Jerusalem to Babylon. 2This was after King Jeconiah, the queen mother, the royal officials, the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, and the skilled workers had left Jerusalem. 3Jeremiah sent the letter with Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah. King Zedekiah of Judah had sent them to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. The letter said:

God Tells the People to Settle Down

4The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says to all the people I took from Jerusalem to Babylon: 5"Build houses and live in them. Plant gardens and eat their fruit. 6Get married and have sons and daughters. Find wives for your sons, and give your daughters to men to marry. Let them have sons and daughters too. Grow in number there. Do not become few. 7Look for peace in the city where I sent you. Pray to the LORD for that city, because when it is at peace, you will be at peace."

Do Not Listen to False Prophets

8The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: 9"Do not let your prophets and those who tell the future trick you. Do not listen to the dreams you dream. They prophesy lies to you in MymyMy name. I did not send them, says the LORD."

God's Good Plans

10The LORD says: "After seventy years in Babylon are over, I will come to you. I will keep MymyMy good promise to you and bring you back to this place. 11I know the plans I have for you, says the LORD. They are plans for peace, not for harm. I want to give you a good future and hope. 12You will call to MemeMe and come and pray to MemeMe, and I will listen to you. 13You will look for MemeMe and find MemeMe when you look for MemeMe with all your heart. 14I will be found by you, says the LORD. I will bring you back from captivity. I will gather you from all the nations and places where I sent you, says the LORD. I will bring you back to the place I sent you away from."

God Warns the People Left in Jerusalem

15The LORD says: "You say, 'The LORD has given us prophets in Babylon.' 16But this is what the LORD says about the king who sits on David's throne and about all the people who live in this city. They are your brothers who did not go with you to Babylon."

17The LORD of Hosts says: "I am sending war, hunger, and sickness on them. I will make them like bad figs that are too bad to eat. 18I will chase them with war, hunger, and sickness. I will make them a terrible warning to all kingdoms on EarthEarthearth. People will curse them, be shocked by them, mock them, and shame them among all the nations where I sent them. 19This is because they did not listen to MymyMy words, says the LORD. I sent MymyMy servants the prophets to them again and again, but you did not listen, says the LORD."

Listen to God's Word

20But you, listen to the word of the LORD. All of you who were taken from Jerusalem to Babylon, listen.

False Prophets in Babylon Will Die

21The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah: "They prophesy lies to you in MymyMy name. I will hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will kill them while you watch."

22The LORD says: "Because of them, all the captives from Judah in Babylon will use a curse. They will say that the LORD should make you like Zedekiah and Ahab, whom the king of Babylon burned in the fire. 23This is because they did a terrible thing in Israel. They were not faithful to their neighbors' wives. They also said false words in MymyMy name, words I did not tell them to say. I know what they did, and I am a witness, says the LORD."

Shemaiah Sends a False Letter

24The LORD also told Jeremiah to send a message to Shemaiah the Nehelamite. 25The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Because you sent letters in your name to all the people in Jerusalem, to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, saying:"

26"The LORD has made you priest instead of Jehoiada the priest. You are to be in charge in the LORD's house. You must arrest any person who acts crazy and says he is a prophet. Put him in chains. 27Why have you not stopped Jeremiah from Anathoth? He says he is a prophet for you. 28He sent us a letter in Babylon. He said it will be a long time. He told us to build houses and live in them, plant gardens, and eat their fruit."

29Zephaniah the priest read this letter to Jeremiah the prophet.

God Judges Shemaiah

30Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. He said, "Send this message to all the people who were taken away: 31'This is what the LORD says about Shemaiah the Nehelamite. He prophesied to you, but I did not send him. He made you trust a lie. 32So I will punish Shemaiah and his family. None of his family will live among these people. He will not see the good I will do for MymyMy people, says the LORD, because he spoke against the LORD.'"
