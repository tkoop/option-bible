Hezekiah Invites All Israel to Passover

1King Hezekiah sent word to all Israel and Judah. He also wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh. He asked them to come to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. They were to keep the Passover for the LORD, the God of Israel.

2The king, his leaders, and all the people in Jerusalem decided to keep the Passover in the second month. 3They could not keep it at the usual time. The priests had not made themselves ready, and the people had not come together in Jerusalem.

4This plan looked good to the king and all the people.

5So they decided to send word through all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan. They told people to come to Jerusalem to keep the Passover for the LORD, the God of Israel. Many people had not kept it the way God's LawlawLaw said.

6The king sent runners with letters. They went through all Israel and Judah. The letters said, "People of Israel, come back to the LORD. He is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel. Come back to HimhimHim. Then HeheHe may bring back the people who are left. They got away from the kings of Assyria.

7"Do not be like your fathers and brothers. They were not faithful to the LORD, the God of their fathers. So HeheHe let them be destroyed. You can see it.

8"Now do not be stubborn like your fathers. Give your hearts to the LORD. Come to HishisHis holy place. He made it holy forever. Serve the LORD your God. Then HeheHe will not be angry with you.

9"If you come back to the LORD, your brothers and children will find mercy. Their captors will show them mercy. The LORD your God is kind and caring. He will not turn away from you if you come back to HimhimHim."

Some Laugh, Some Come

10The runners went from town to town in Ephraim, Manasseh, and Zebulun. People laughed at them and made fun of them. 11But some men from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun were sorry for their sin. They came to Jerusalem.

12God's hand was on Judah. He gave the people one mind. They did what the king and leaders asked, because the LORD had spoken.

23All the people agreed to keep the feast for seven more days. So they kept it for seven more days with joy.

24King Hezekiah of Judah gave 1,000 bulls and 7,000 sheep for the people. The leaders gave 1,000 bulls and 10,000 sheep. Many priests made themselves ready.

Everyone Is Happy

25All the people of Judah were happy. The priests and Levites were happy too. So were all the people who came from Israel. People from other lands who came from Israel and people living in Judah were happy too.

26There was great joy in Jerusalem. Nothing like it had happened in Jerusalem since the time of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel.
