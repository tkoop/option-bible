More Wise Words from Solomon

1These are more wise sayings from Solomon. Men who worked for King Hezekiah of Judah copied them.

God and Kings

2It is a great honourhonourhonor for God to keep a thing secret. It is a great honourhonourhonor for kings to find things out. 3The sky is high, and the earth is deep. In the same way, a king's heart is hard to understand.

Clean Silver and Clean Rule

4Take the junk out of silver. Then the metal worker can make something good. 5Take bad people away from the king. Then his rule will be fair and strong.

Do Not Show Off

6Do not act big in front of the king. Do not stand where great people stand. 7It is better to hear, "Come up here," than to be put down in front of a leader.

Do Not Rush to Argue

8Do not rush into a fight. What will you do later if your neighbourneighbourneighbor shames you? 9Talk out your problem with your friend. Do not share another person's secret. 10Someone may hear it and shame you. Then your bad name will stay.

Words Like Gold

11A word said at the right time is like gold apples in a silver dish. 12A wise warning to a listening ear is like gold earrings or fine gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry.

A Faithful Helper

13A faithful messenger is like cool snow on a hot harvest day. He brings joy to those who sent him.

Empty Promises

14A person who brags about gifts he does not give is like clouds and wind with no rain.

Patient Words

15With patience, a ruler can be won over. Gentle words can win over a stubborn person.

Honey and Visits

16If you find honey, eat just enough. If you eat too much, you will throw up. 17Do not go to your friend's house too often. He may get tired of you and hate you.

Lies and Bad Friends

18A person who tells lies about a friend is like a club, a sword, or a sharp arrow. 19Trusting a person who turns on you in trouble is like a bad tooth or a sore foot.

Do Not Sing to Sad Hearts

20Singing happy songs to a sad person does not help. It is like taking off a coat in the cold.

Be Kind to Enemies

21If your enemy is hungry, give him food. If he is thirsty, give him water. 22In this way, you will make him feel ashamed. And the LORD will reward you.

Wind, Wives, and News

23The north wind brings rain. A gossiping tongue brings angry faces. 24It is better to live on a corner of the roof than to share a house with a wife who always argues. 25Like cold water to a tired person is good news from far away.

Stay Strong

26A good person who gives in to a bad person is like a muddy spring or a dirty well. 27It is not good to eat too much honey. It is not good to brag about yourself. 28A person who cannot control himself is like a city with broken walls.
