Give Your Lives to God

1So, my brothers and sisters, I beg you to give your bodies to God. Because God is so kind, give yourselves as a living gift. Make it holy and pleasing to God. This is the right way to worship HimhimHim. 2Do not be like the people of this world. Let God change you inside. He will make your mind new. Then you can know what God wants. You will know what is good and pleasing and perfect.

Think Clearly About Yourself

3God has been kind to me. So I tell each of you: Do not think you are better than you really are. Think of yourselves in a wise and careful way. God has given each person a share of faith.

We Are One Body

4A body has many parts. Not all parts do the same job. 5In the same way, we are one body in Christ. We are many people, but we belong to each other.

Use Your Gifts

6God has given us different gifts. Use them well. If you can tell God's message, speak with faith. 7If you can serve, serve well. If you can teach, teach well. 8If you can cheer people up, cheer them up. If you give, give freely. If you lead, lead with care. If you show kindness, be happy to do it.

Love and Serve Others

9Love must be real. Hate what is bad. Hold on to what is good. 10Love each other like family. Try to honourhonourhonor each other more than yourselves. 11Do not be lazy. Be excited in your spirit. Serve the Lord. 12Be happy because you have hope. Keep going when life is hard. Keep praying. 13Share with God's people when they need help. Try hard to welcome strangers.

Bless and Get Along

14Bless the people who hurt you. Bless them, and do not curse them. 15Be happy with happy people. Cry with people who cry. 16Think the same good thoughts toward each other. Do not be proud. Be friends with people who are not rich or important. Do not think you know everything.

Do Not Get Even

17Do not pay back evil with evil. Try to do what is right in front of everyone. 18If you can, live in peace with all people. 19My dear friends, do not try to get even. Let God be angry. He will punish. For the Bible says, "I will pay them back. I am the one who punishes," says the Lord. 20But if your enemy is hungry, give him food. If he is thirsty, give him a drink. If you do this, you will make him feel ashamed. 21Do not let evil win. Win over evil by doing good.
