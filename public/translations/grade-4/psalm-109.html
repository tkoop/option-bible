A Prayer When People Tell Lies About Me

1God, YouyouYou are the one I praise. Please do not stay quiet. 2Bad people with tricky mouths speak against me. They tell lies about me. 3They surround me with mean words and fight me for no reason. 4I loved them, but they accuse me. I keep praying to YouyouYou. 5They pay me back with bad for good, and hate for love.

David Asks God to Punish His Enemy

6Put a bad man over my enemy. Let someone accuse him at his right side. 7When he is judged, let him be guilty. Let his prayer be a sin. 8Let his days be few. Let someone else take his job. 9Let his children lose their father. Let his wife become a widow. 10Let his children wander and beg. Let them look for food from ruined homes. 11Let money collectors take all he has. Let strangers steal what he worked for. 12Let no one be kind to him. Let no one feel sorry for his children who have no father. 13Let his family line end. Let their name be erased from the next generation. 14Let the LORD remember the wrongs of his fathers. Do not erase his mother's sin. 15Let these wrongs stay before the LORD always, so HeheHe can erase their memory from the earth.

Why David Asks for This

16He did not remember to be kind. He chased poor and needy people and those with broken hearts to kill them. 17He loved to curse others. Let curses come to him. He did not want blessing. Let blessing stay far from him. 18He wore curses like clothes. They soaked into him like water, into his bones like oil. 19Let curses cover him like clothes. Let them be the belt he always wears. 20This is how the LORD will pay back my accusers, those who speak evil against me.

My body has grown thin. 25I am a joke to them. When they see me, they shake their heads. 26Help me, the LORD my God. Save me because of YouryourYour faithful love. 27Let them know that YouryourYour hand did this. You, the LORD, did it. 28They may curse, but YouyouYou bless. They stand up, but they are put to shame.
