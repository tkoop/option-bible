Jeremiah Is Put in Prison

1The LORD spoke to Jeremiah. This was in the tenth year that Zedekiah was king of Judah. It was also the eighteenth year that Nebuchadnezzar was king of Babylon. 2At that time, the army of the king of Babylon was all around Jerusalem. Jeremiah the prophet was locked up in the prison yard of the king's palace. 3Zedekiah king of Judah had put him there. He asked, "Why do you say the LORD says HeheHe will give this city to the king of Babylon? He will capture it." 4Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape the Babylonians. Zedekiah will stay there until I come to him, says the LORD. You may fight the Babylonians, but you will not win.

9So I bought the field from my cousin Hanamel in Anathoth. I weighed out seventeen pieces of silver. 10I wrote it down and sealed it. I had people watch.
