Nineveh Is Guilty

1How terrible for the city of blood! It is full of lies. It is full of stolen things. It never stops hurting people. 2Whips crack. Wheels rumble. Horses run fast. Chariots jump. 3Horsemen charge. Swords flash. Spears shine. Many people die. Dead bodies pile up. There is no end to them. People trip over the dead.

4This will happen because the city acted like a woman who sells herself. She looked pretty. She used magic. She tricked nations and families.

The LORD Will Shame Nineveh

5“I am against you,” says the LORD of Armies. “I will show your shame to the nations. I will show your shame to the kingdoms.” 6“I will throw garbage on you. I will treat you as worthless. I will make people stare at you.”

7Everyone who sees you will turn away. They will say, “Nineveh is destroyed! Who will feel sorry for her? Who will comfort her?”

Thebes Fell Too

8Are you better than Thebes? Thebes sat by the Nile River. Water was all around it. The sea was like a wall around it. 9Cush and Egypt made it strong. Put and Libya helped it. Their help had no end. 10But Thebes was taken away. Its people became slaves. Its babies were killed in the streets. Soldiers drew lots for its leaders. Its important men were tied up in chains.

Nineveh Will Fall Too

11You will be like a drunk person. You will try to hide. You will look for a safe place from your enemies. 12Your strong cities are like fig trees with ripe figs. If someone shakes them, the figs fall right into his mouth. 13Your people are weak and afraid. Your gates stand open to your enemies. Fire has burned your gate bars.

Get Ready for War

14Store water for the attack. Make your forts strong. Go into the mud and mix clay. Hold the brick mold. 15There fire will burn you. The sword will cut you down. It will eat you up like locusts eat plants. Even if you become as many as locusts or grasshoppers, it will not save you. 16You have more traders than stars in the sky. But like locusts, they strip everything and fly away. 17Your guards are like locusts. Your leaders are like swarms of locusts. They sit in the walls on a cold day. When the sun comes up, they fly away. No one knows where they go. 18King of Assyria, your leaders are asleep. Your nobles sleep too. Your people are scattered on the mountains. No one gathers them. 19No one can heal your wound. Your injury will kill you. Everyone who hears about you claps their hands. Who has not suffered from your endless cruelty?
