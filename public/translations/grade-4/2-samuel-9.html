David Wants to Help Saul's Family

1David asked, "IsisIs there anyone still left from Saul's family? I want to show kindness to them because of Jonathan."

2They called Ziba, a servant from Saul's house, to come to David. The king asked him, "Are you Ziba?"

He answered, "Yes, I am your servant."

3The king said, "IsisIs there anyone left in Saul's family? I want to show God's kindness to him."

Ziba said, "Yes, Jonathan has a son. Both his feet are hurt, so he cannot walk."

4The king asked, "Where is he?"

Ziba said, "HeheHe is at the house of Machir son of Ammiel in Lo-debar."

5King David sent men and brought him from there.

6Mephibosheth, son of Jonathan and grandson of Saul, came to David. He bowed down with his face to the ground.

David said, "Mephibosheth!"

He answered, "Here I am, your servant."

7David said, "Do not be afraid. I will show you kindness because of your father Jonathan. I will give you back all the land that belonged to your grandfather Saul. You will always eat at my table."

8Mephibosheth bowed down and said, "Who am I, your servant, that you would care about a nobody like me?"

David Gives Mephibosheth Saul's Land

9Then the king called Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "I have given your master's son everything that belonged to Saul and his family. 10You, your sons, and your servants must work the land for him. Bring in the crops so your master's grandson has food to eat. But Mephibosheth, your master's grandson, will always eat at my table."

Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

11Ziba said to the king, "YouryourYour servant will do everything my lord the king tells him to do."

So Mephibosheth ate at David's table like one of the king's sons.

12Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica. All who lived in Ziba's house became servants to Mephibosheth.

13Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, because he always ate at the king's table. He could not walk because both his feet were hurt.
