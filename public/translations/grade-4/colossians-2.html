Paul Works Hard for the Church

1I want you to know how hard I work for you. I work for the people in Laodicea too. I work for all who have never met me face to face. 2I want their hearts to be strong. I want them to love each other. I want them to understand God's big secret. That secret is Christ. 3In Christ are all the hidden treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

4I say this so no one tricks you with smooth words. 5I am not there in body, but I am with you in spirit. I am glad to see your good order and your strong faith in Christ.

Walk with Christ

6You received Christ Jesus as Lord. So walk with HimhimHim. 7Grow deep roots in HimhimHim. Build your life on HimhimHim. Stay strong in what you were taught. Be full of thanks. 8Watch out! Do not let anyone take you away with empty ideas. Do not follow human rules. Do not follow the world's basic ways. Follow Christ. 9In Christ lives all of God's full power in a body. 10You are full in HimhimHim. He is the head over all rulers and powers.

Alive with Christ

11In HimhimHim, you had a circumcision not done by hands. It took away the old body of sin. 12You were buried with HimhimHim in baptism. You were raised with HimhimHim through faith in God's power. God raised Christ from the dead. 13You were dead in your sins. Your old life was far from God. But God made you alive with Christ. He forgave all our sins. 14He erased the record of debt against us. He took it away and nailed it to the cross. 15He took power from the rulers and powers. He made them look foolish. He beat them through Christ.

Do Not Let Others Judge You

16So do not let anyone judge you about food or drink. Do not let them judge you about a holiday, a new moon, or a Sabbath day. 17These things are only a shadow of what was coming. The real thing is Christ. 18Do not let anyone take your prize. Some people act humble and worship angels. They talk about what they have seen. They are proud for no reason. Their minds are weak. 19They do not hold on to Christ, the Head. From HimhimHim, the whole body grows. God makes it grow through its joints and ties. 20You died with Christ. You are free from the world's basic rules. So why do you act like you still live by them? 21"Do not touch. Do not taste. Do not handle." 22These things do not last. They are used up and gone. They come from human rules and teachings. 23They look wise. They make people worship their own way. They make people be hard on their bodies. But they do not stop wrong desires.
