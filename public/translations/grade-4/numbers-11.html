The People Complain

1The people complained, and it sounded bad to the LORD. He was angry. Fire from the LORD burned among them and burned the edge of the camp. 2The people cried out to Moses. Moses prayed to the LORD, and the fire stopped. 3So they named that place Taberah, because the LORD's fire had burned among them.

The People Want Meat

4A mixed group among them wanted better food. The Israelites cried too. They said, "Who will give us meat to eat? 5We remember the fish we ate for free in Egypt, and the cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic. 6But now we are tired of this food. All we see is manna."

7Manna looked like tiny seeds. It shone like a clear stone. 8The people went around and picked it up. They ground it in mills or beat it in a bowl. They cooked it in a pot and made cakes from it. It tasted like cakes made with oil. 9When the dew fell at night, the manna fell on it.

God Sends Quail

31A wind from the LORD brought quail from the sea and dropped them all around the camp. They were about three feet deep on the ground, in every direction as far as a day's walk. 32The people got up and gathered quail all that day, all that night, and all the next day. The one who gathered the least gathered ten large baskets full. They spread them out around the camp.

The People Are Punished

33While the meat was still between their teeth, before they chewed it, the LORD became angry with the people. He struck them with a very bad sickness. 34So that place was called Kibroth Hattaavah, because there they buried the people who had wanted the meat.
