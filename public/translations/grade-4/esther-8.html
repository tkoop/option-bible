Mordecai Gets the King's Ring

1On that day, King Ahasuerus gave Queen Esther Haman's house. Haman was the enemy of the Jews. Mordecai came to the king. Esther had told the king who Mordecai was to her. 2The king took off his ring. He had taken it from Haman. He gave it to Mordecai. Esther put Mordecai in charge of Haman's house.

Esther Asks the King to Help

3Esther spoke to the king again. She fell at his feet and cried. She begged him to stop Haman's bad plan. Haman the Agagite had planned to hurt the Jews. 4The king held out his gold sceptresceptrescepter to Esther. She stood up and stood before the king.

5She said, "If it pleases the king, and if I have found favourfavourfavor with him, and if this seems right to the king and good to him, then write a new letter. Take back the letters Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite wrote. He wrote them to kill the Jews in all the king's lands. 6I cannot bear to see my people hurt. I cannot bear to see my family ruined."

7The king said to Esther and Mordecai, "I have given Esther Haman's house. They hanged Haman on a pole because he hurt the Jews. 8Now you may write a letter in my name about the Jews. Write what seems right to you. Seal it with my ring. A letter written in my name and sealed with my ring cannot be changed."

The New Letter Is Sent

9On the 23rd day of the third month, Sivan, the king's writers were called. They wrote everything Mordecai told them. They sent it to the Jews and to the rulers in all 127 lands, from India to Ethiopia. Each land got the message in its own writing. Each people got it in their own language. The Jews got it in their own writing and language. 10The letters were written in the name of King Ahasuerus. They were sealed with the king's ring. Messengers on fast horses carried them. They rode the king's best horses.

11The king let the Jews in every city gather together. They could fight to save their lives. They could destroy any army that attacked them. They could also take the enemy's things. 12This was to happen on one day in all the lands. It was the 13th day of the 12th month, called Adar. 13A copy of the letter was sent to every land. It was made known to all people. The Jews were to be ready on that day to fight back against their enemies. 14The messengers hurried out because the king commanded it. The law was also given in the city of Susa.

The Jews Celebrate

15Mordecai left the king wearing royal clothes. He wore blue and white. He had a big gold crown and a purple robe made of fine cloth. The city of Susa shouted with joy. 16The Jews had light, joy, and honourhonourhonor. 17In every land and city where the king's order reached, the Jews were happy. They had parties and special days. Many people became Jews, because they were afraid of the Jews.
