Give God the Glory

1Not to us, the LORD, not to us. Give glory to your name because of your love and your truth.

2Why do the nations ask, “Where is their God?”

Our God Is in Heaven

3Our God is in Heavenheavenheaven. He does whatever HeheHe wants.

4Their false gods are made of silver and gold. People made them with their hands. 5They have mouths, but they do not talk. They have eyes, but they do not see. 6They have ears, but they do not hear. They have noses, but they do not smell. 7They have hands, but they do not feel. They have feet, but they do not walk. They do not make a sound with their throats. 8People who make them become like them. So do people who trust them.

Trust in the LORD

9Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. 10Family of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. 11You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield.

The LORD Blesses His People

12The LORD has remembered us. He will bless us. He will bless the family of Israel. He will bless the family of Aaron. 13He will bless those who fear the LORD, the small and the great.

14May the LORD give you more and more, you and your children! 15May you be blessed by the LORD, who made Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth!

16The heavens belong to the LORD, but HeheHe gave the earth to people.

17The dead do not praise the LORD. Neither do those who go down into silence. 18But we will bless the LORD from now on and forever. Praise the LORD!
