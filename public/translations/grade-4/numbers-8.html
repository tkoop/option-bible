The Lampstand

1The LORD spoke to Moses. 2He said, "Speak to Aaron. Tell him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps should shine in front of the lampstand.'"

3Aaron did this. He set up the lamps so they shone in front of the lampstand, just as the LORD had told Moses. 4The lampstand was made of hammered gold. It had a base and flowers. It was made just the way the LORD showed Moses.

The Levites Are Made Clean

5The LORD spoke to Moses. 6He said, "Take the Levites from among the Israelites. Make them clean. 7Do this to make them clean: Sprinkle the water that washes away sin on them. Have them shave their whole body. Have them wash their clothes. Then they will be clean. 8Then they must take a young bull and its grain offering. The grain offering is fine flour mixed with oil. You must also take a second young bull for a sin offering.

9Bring the Levites to the front of the meeting tent. Gather all the Israelites there. 10Bring the Levites to the LORD. The Israelites must put their hands on the Levites. 11Aaron must lift the Levites up to the LORD as a wave offering from the Israelites. Then they can serve the LORD. 12The Levites must put their hands on the heads of the bulls. Offer one bull for a sin offering. Offer the other as a burnt offering to the LORD. This will pay for the Levites' sin. 13Stand the Levites in front of Aaron and his sons. Lift them up to the LORD as a wave offering.

14This is how you set the Levites apart from the other Israelites. The Levites will be MinemineMine. 15After that, the Levites may come to serve in the meeting tent. You must make them clean first. You must lift them up as a wave offering. 16They are given to MemeMe fully from among the Israelites. I took them for MyselfmyselfMyself instead of the first baby boy born in every Israelite family. 17Every firstborn Israelite belongs to MemeMe, both people and animals. I made them holy for MyselfmyselfMyself on the day I killed every firstborn in Egypt. 18So I took the Levites instead of all the firstborn Israelites. 19I gave the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons. They come from the Israelites. They will serve for the Israelites in the meeting tent. They will pay for the Israelites' sin. Then no sickness will come on the Israelites when they come near the holy place."

The Levites Begin Their Work

20Moses, Aaron, and all the Israelites did for the Levites just what the LORD told Moses. The Israelites did it. 21The Levites made themselves clean. They washed their clothes. Aaron lifted them up to the LORD as a wave offering. Aaron paid for their sin to make them clean. 22After that, the Levites came to serve in the meeting tent in front of Aaron and his sons. They did just as the LORD told Moses.

Age for Levite Work

23The LORD spoke to Moses. 24He said, "This is the rule for the Levites: A Levite who is 25 years old or older may come to work in the meeting tent. 25When he is 50 years old, he must stop the hard work. He must not work anymore. 26He may still help his brothers. He may watch over the meeting tent. But he must not do the hard work. This is how you care for the Levites in their jobs."
