Keep Loving Each Other

1Keep loving your brothers and sisters. 2Do not forget to be kind to guests. Some people welcomed strangers and did not know they were angels.

3Remember people who are in prison. Think of them as if you were locked up with them. Remember people who are hurt, as if you were hurt too, since you have bodies too.

Keep Marriage Pure

4Marriage is good for everyone. Keep the marriage bed pure. God will judge people who do sexual wrong and people who cheat in marriage.

Trust God, Not Money

5Do not love money. Be happy with what you have. God has said, "I will never leave you. I will never abandon you."

6So we can say with confidence, "ThetheThe LORD is my helper. I will not be afraid. What can people do to me?"

Remember Your Leaders

7Remember your leaders. They taught you God's word. Think about how they lived and how they finished life. Follow their faith. 8Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.

Do Not Follow Strange Teachings

9Do not let strange and different teachings pull you away. It is good for your heart to be made strong by grace, not by food rules. Food rules did not help the people who followed them.

We Have a Better Altar

10We have an altar. The people who serve at the holy tent are not allowed to eat from it. 11The high priest brings animal blood into the holy place to pay for sin. But the animals' bodies are burned outside the camp.

12So Jesus suffered outside the city gate. He made the people holy by HishisHis own blood. 13So let us go to HimhimHim outside the camp. Let us share HishisHis shame. 14We do not have a city that lasts here. We are looking for the city that is coming.

Praise God and Do Good

15Through Jesus, let us always give God a sacrifice of praise. That means we praise HimhimHim with our words. 16Do not forget to do good and share with others. God is pleased with gifts like these.

Obey Your Leaders

17Obey your leaders and listen to them. They watch over you, and they will have to answer to God for you. Let them do this with joy, not with sadness. Sadness would not help you.

Pray for Us

18Pray for us. We believe we have a clear heart, and we want to live the right way in everything. 19I ask you even more to pray, so I can come back to you soon.

A Prayer for You

20May the God of peace make you ready to do every good thing, so you can do HishisHis will. He brought back our Lord Jesus from the dead through the blood of the forever promise. Jesus is the great ShepherdshepherdShepherd of the sheep. 21May God do in us what pleases HimhimHim through Jesus Christ. Give HimhimHim glory forever and ever. Amen.

Final Words
