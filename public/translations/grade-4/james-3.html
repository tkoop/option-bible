Watch Your Words

1My brothers, not many of you should be teachers. We know we will be judged more strictly. 2We all make many mistakes. If someone does not make mistakes with words, that person is good. That person can keep the whole body under control. 3We put bits in horses' mouths. Then they obey us. We can turn their whole body. 4Look at ships. They are big. Strong winds push them. But a small rudder turns them where the driver wants. 5The tongue is small too. But it says big things. Look how a little fire can burn a big forest. 6The tongue is a fire. It is a world of wrong. It is among the parts of our body. It makes the whole body dirty. It sets the whole life on fire. It is set on fire by HellHellhell. 7People have tamed all kinds of animals, birds, snakes, and sea creatures. 8But no person can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil. It is full of deadly poison. 9With it we praise the Lord and FatherfatherFather. With it we curse people made in God's likeness. 10Praise and cursing come from the same mouth. My brothers, this should not be. 11Can sweet water and bitter water come from the same spring? 12My brothers, can a fig tree make olives? Can a grapevine make figs? No. Salt water cannot make fresh water.

Real Wisdom

13Who is wise and knows things among you? Show it by a good life and by works done with gentle wisdom. 14If you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not brag and lie against the truth. 15This wisdom is not from Heavenheavenheaven. It is earthly, natural, and demonic. 16Where jealousy and selfish ambition are, there is disorder and every evil thing. 17But wisdom from above is first pure. Then it is peace-loving, gentle, willing to listen, full of mercy and good fruit. It is fair and not fake. 18People who make peace plant peace. The fruit of right living grows.
