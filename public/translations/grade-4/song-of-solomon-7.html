The Man Praises the Woman

1How pretty are your feet in sandals, daughter of a prince! The curves of your thighs are like jewels, made by a master's hands. 2Your belly button is like a round cup. It never runs out of mixed wine. Your belly is like a pile of wheat, ringed with lilies. 3Your breasts are like two young deer, twins of a gazelle.

4Your neck is like an ivory tower. Your eyes are pools of water in Heshbon, by the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose is like the tower of Lebanon, looking toward Damascus. 5Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel. The hair of your head is like purple cloth. A king is caught in your hair. 6How pretty and sweet you are, my love, with all your delights!

7Your height is like a palm tree, and your breasts are like its clusters. 8I said, 'I will climb the palm tree and hold its fruit.' May your breasts be like grape clusters, and may the smell of your nose be like apples. 9Your mouth is like the best wine. It flows smoothly to my love and makes sleeping lips talk.

The Woman Invites Her Love

10I belong to my love, and he longs for me.

11Come, my love, let's go to the country. Let's stay the night in the villages. 12Let's go early to the vineyards. We will see if the vines have budded, if the flowers have opened, and if the pomegranates are blooming. There I will give you my love. 13The mandrakes give off their smell. At our door are all kinds of good things, new and old, that I saved for you, my love.
