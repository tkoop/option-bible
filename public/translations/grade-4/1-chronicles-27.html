David's Army Groups for Each Month

1This is the list of the people of Israel. It names the family leaders and the army leaders over thousands and hundreds. It also names the officers who helped the king. They served in groups that worked each month all year. Each group had 24,000 men. 2Jashobeam son of Zabdiel led the first group. This group worked in the first month. It had 24,000 men. 3He was from Perez's family. He was the chief army leader for the first month.

4Dodai the Ahohite led the second group. This group worked in the second month. Mikloth was the leader of his group. It had 24,000 men. 5Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest led the third group. This group worked in the third month. It had 24,000 men. 6This Benaiah was a strong fighter among the ThirtythirtyThirty. He was over the ThirtythirtyThirty. His son Ammizabad led his group.

7Asahel, Joab's brother, led the fourth group. This group worked in the fourth month. His son Zebadiah took his place after him. It had 24,000 men. 8Shamhuth the Izrahite led the fifth group. This group worked in the fifth month. It had 24,000 men. 9Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite led the sixth group. This group worked in the sixth month. It had 24,000 men.

10Helez the Pelonite, from Ephraim, led the seventh group. This group worked in the seventh month. It had 24,000 men. 11Sibbecai the Hushathite, from Zerah's family, led the eighth group. This group worked in the eighth month. It had 24,000 men. 12Abiezer the Anathothite, from Benjamin, led the ninth group. This group worked in the ninth month. It had 24,000 men.

13Maharai the Netophathite, from Zerah's family, led the tenth group. This group worked in the tenth month. It had 24,000 men. 14Benaiah the Pirathonite, from Ephraim, led the eleventh group. This group worked in the eleventh month. It had 24,000 men. 15Heldai the Netophathite, from Othniel's family, led the twelfth group. This group worked in the twelfth month. It had 24,000 men.

Leaders of the Tribes of Israel

16These men led the tribes of Israel. For Reuben, Eliezer son of Zichri. For Simeon, Shephatiah son of Maacah. 17For Levi, Hashabiah son of Kemuel. For Aaron, Zadok. 18For Judah, Elihu, one of David's brothers. For Issachar, Omri son of Michael.

19For Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah. For Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel. 20For Ephraim, Hoshea son of Azaziah. For half of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah. 21For the other half of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah. For Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner. 22For Dan, Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the tribes of Israel.

David Counts the People

23David did not count the men who were 20 years old or younger. This was because the LORD had said HeheHe would make Israel as many as the stars in the sky. 24Joab son of Zeruiah started to count the people, but he did not finish. Because of this, God was angry with Israel. The number was not written in King David's history book.

Officials Over David's Property

25Azmaveth son of Adiel was in charge of the king's storehouses. Jonathan son of Uzziah was in charge of the storehouses in the country, in the cities, in the villages, and in the towers. 26Ezri son of Kelub was in charge of the field workers who farmed the land. 27Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards. Zabdi the Shiphmite was in charge of the fruit from the vineyards for the wine cellars.

28Baal-hanan the Gederite was in charge of the olive trees and sycamore trees in the low hills. Joash was in charge of the olive oil stores. 29Shitrai the Sharonite was in charge of the herds in Sharon. Shaphat son of Adlai was in charge of the herds in the valleys. 30Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels. Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the donkeys. 31Jaziz the Hagrite was in charge of the sheep. All these men were officials who took care of King David's things.

David's Advisors

32Jonathan, David's uncle, gave advice. He was a wise man and a writer. Jehiel son of Hachmoni helped teach the king's sons. 33Ahithophel was the king's advisor. Hushai the Archite was the king's friend. 34After Ahithophel, Jehoiada son of Benaiah and Abiathar advised the king. Joab was the leader of the king's army.
