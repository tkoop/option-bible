Moses Talks to Israel

1Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel. 2He said, "I am 120 years old today. I can't lead you anymore. The LORD has told me, 'YouyouYou will not cross the Jordan River.' 3ThetheThe LORD your God will go ahead of you. He will destroy these nations, and you will take their land. The LORD your God goes with you. He won't leave you or give up on you."

Moses Talks to Joshua

7Moses called Joshua. In front of all Israel he said, "Be strong and brave. You will go with these people into the land the LORD promised their fathers. You will give them that land. 8The LORD goes before you. He will be with you. He won't leave you or give up on you. Don't be afraid or worried."

The Law Is Given to the Priests

9Moses wrote down this law. He gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the LORD's agreement. He gave it to all the elders of Israel too.

10Moses told them, "Every seven years, at the special time, the year debts are forgiven, during the FeastfeastFeast of Shelters, 11all Israel must come to the place the LORD your God chooses. Then they will respect the the LORD your God and obey all the words of this law. 13Their children, who don't know it yet, must hear it and learn to respect the the LORD your God as long as you live in the land you are crossing the Jordan to take."

Moses Writes the Song
