The LORD Speaks to Moses

1The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. He said,

The Sabbath Year

2"Speak to the people of Israel. Say this: When you come into the land I give you, the land must have a Sabbath rest for the the LORD. 3For six years, plant your field. Trim your grapevines. Gather the crops. 4But the seventh year is a Sabbath rest for the land. It is a Sabbath for the the LORD. Do not plant your field. Do not trim your grapevines. 5Do not gather the grain that grows by itself. Do not pick grapes from vines no one trimmed. It will be a year of rest for the land."

6"The land's rest will give food to you, your male servant, your female servant, your hired worker, and the stranger who lives with you. 7Your livestock and the wild animals in your land may eat all its crops."

The Year of Jubilee

8"Count seven groups of seven years. That is forty-nine years. 9On the tenth day of the seventh month, on the DaydayDay of Forgiving Sins, blow a trumpet through all your land. 10Make the fiftieth year holy. Call for freedom in the land for everyone who lives there. It is your Jubilee. Each person must go back to his own land. Each person must go back to his family. 11The fiftieth year is your Jubilee. Do not plant. Do not gather grain that grows by itself. Do not pick grapes from vines no one trimmed. 12It is holy. You may eat what the field grows by itself. 13In this Jubilee year, each person must go back to his own land."

Buying and Selling Land

14"When you sell land to your neighbourneighbourneighbor, or buy land from your neighbourneighbourneighbor, do not cheat each other. 15Buy from your neighbourneighbourneighbor by counting the years after the Jubilee. He will sell to you by counting the years of crops. 16If many years are left, pay more. If few years are left, pay less. He sells to you based on the number of harvests. 17Do not cheat one another. Fear your God. I am the the LORD your God."

18"Obey MymyMy rules and laws. Then you will live safely in the land. 19The land will give its fruit. You will eat until you are full. You will live safely there."

20"You may ask, 'What will we eat in the seventh year? We did not plant or gather our crops.' 21I will send MymyMy blessing in the sixth year. The land will grow enough food for three years. 22When you plant in the eighth year, you will still eat from the old crops. You will eat them until the ninth year, until the new crops come in."

23"The land must not be sold forever. It is MinemineMine. You are only strangers and guests with MemeMe. 24In all the land you own, you must allow the land to be bought back."

Buying Back Land

25"If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his land, his nearest relative must come and buy it back for him. 26If a man has no one to buy it back, but he gets enough money, he may buy it back himself. 27He must count the years since he sold it. He must pay back the extra money to the man who bought it. Then he can go back to his land. 28If he cannot get enough money to buy it back, the buyer keeps it until the Jubilee year. In the Jubilee, it is set free, and he goes back to his land."

29"If a man sells a house in a walled city, he can buy it back until one year after he sold it. 30If it is not bought back before the year is over, the house in the walled city belongs to the buyer and his family forever. It will not be set free in the Jubilee. 31But houses in villages without walls are treated like fields. They can be bought back. They will be set free in the Jubilee."

32"The Levites can always buy back the houses in their cities. 33If a Levite buys back a house, it will be set free in the Jubilee. This is because the houses in Levite cities belong to them. They are among the people of Israel. 34The fields around their cities must not be sold. They are theirs forever."

Helping Poor Brothers

35"If your brother becomes poor and cannot take care of himself, you must help him. Help him like a stranger or guest, so he can live with you. 36Do not take interest or extra money from him. Fear your God, so your brother can live with you. 37Do not lend him money for interest. Do not sell him food for profit. 38I am the the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God."

39"If your brother becomes poor and sells himself to you, do not make him work like a slave. 40He will be with you like a hired worker or a guest. He will work with you until the Jubilee year. 41Then he and his children will leave you. He will go back to his family and to his father's land. 42They are MymyMy servants. I brought them out of Egypt. They must not be sold as slaves. 43Do not rule over him harshly. Fear your God."

Slaves from Other Nations

44"You may buy male and female slaves from the nations around you. 45You may also buy them from the children of the strangers who live with you. You may buy their families who were born in your land. They will be your property. 46You may leave them to your children after you. They can own them forever. But do not rule harshly over your brothers, the people of Israel."

47"If a stranger or guest with you becomes rich, and your brother becomes poor and sells himself to that stranger or guest, or to a member of his family, 48he may be bought back after he is sold. One of his brothers may buy him back. 49His uncle or his cousin may buy him back. A close relative from his family may buy him back. Or if he gets enough money, he may buy himself back. 50He and his buyer must count the years from the year he sold himself to the Jubilee year. The price will be like the pay of a hired worker for those years."

51"If many years are left, he must pay back more of his price. 52If few years are left until the Jubilee, he must pay back less. 53He will be with the buyer like a hired worker year by year. The buyer must not rule over him harshly while you watch. 54If he is not bought back in these ways, he and his children will be set free in the Jubilee year."

55"The people of Israel are MymyMy servants. They are the servants I brought out of Egypt. I am the the LORD your God."
