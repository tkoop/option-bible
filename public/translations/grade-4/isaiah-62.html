God Will Make Zion Shine

1For the good of Zion, I will not be quiet. For the good of Jerusalem, I will not rest. I will keep talking until her goodness shines like the morning. I will keep talking until her rescue burns like a bright torch. 2Other nations will see your goodness. All kings will see your honourhonourhonor. You will get a new name. The LORD will give it to you. 3You will be a beautiful crown in the LORD’s hand. You will be a royal crown in your God’s hand. 4People will not call you Left Alone anymore. They will not call your land Empty anymore. Instead, they will call you MymyMy Delight. They will call your land Married. This is because the LORD is happy with you. He will take your land as HishisHis own. 5A young man marries a young woman. In the same way, your sons will marry you. A groom is very happy with his bride. In the same way, your God will be very happy with you.

Guards on Jerusalem’s Walls

6Jerusalem, I put guards on your walls. They watch day and night. They never stop. You people who pray to the LORD, do not rest. 7Do not let HimhimHim rest until HeheHe makes Jerusalem strong. Do not let HimhimHim rest until HeheHe makes the whole world praise her.

God Promises to Protect His People

8The LORD made a strong promise by HishisHis right hand and by HishisHis mighty arm: “I will never again give your grain to your enemies for food. People from other lands will not drink the new wine you worked hard to make. 9The people who harvest it will eat it and praise the LORD. The people who gather it will drink it in MymyMy holy place.”

Get Ready for God’s People

10Go out! Go out through the gates! Make a road for the people! Build up the road! Build it up! Clear the stones away! Lift up a flag for all nations! 11Look! The LORD has spoken to the whole world. He says, “Tell DaughterdaughterDaughter Zion: Your rescue is coming. His reward is with HimhimHim. His pay goes before HimhimHim.” 12They will be called the HolyholyHoly People. They are the ones the LORD set free. You will be called Looked For. You are a city that is not left alone.
