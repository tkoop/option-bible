God Calls All Nations to Listen

1Come near, you nations. Listen. Pay attention, you peoples. Let the earth hear. Let all that fills it hear. Let the whole world hear, and all its people.

The LORD Is Angry with the Nations

2The LORD is angry with all the nations. He is furious with all their armies. He will destroy them all. He will give them over to be killed.

3Their dead will be left on the ground. The smell of their bodies will rise up. The mountains will be soaked with their blood.

The Sky Will Roll Up

4All the stars in the sky will fade away. The sky will be rolled up like a scroll. All the stars will fall like dry leaves from a grapevine. They will fall like leaves from a fig tree.

The LORD’s Sword Will Fall on Edom

5My sword has been used in Heavenheavenheaven. Now it will come down on Edom. It will bring judgmentjudgementjudgment on the people I set apart to be destroyed.

6The sword of the LORD is covered with blood. It is full of fat. It has blood from lambs and goats. It has fat from the kidneys of rams. For the LORD will make a sacrifice in Bozrah. It will be a great killing in Edom.

7Wild cattle will fall with them. Young bulls will fall with the strong bulls. Their land will be soaked with blood. Their soil will be rich with fat.

A Day of Payback

8For the LORD has a day of payback. It is the year HeheHe pays back those who hurt Zion.

Edom Will Burn

9Edom’s streams will turn to tar. Its soil will turn to sulphursulphursulfur. Its land will keep burning.

10The fire will not go out day or night. Its smoke will rise forever. From one generation to the next, the land will stay empty. No one will ever walk through it again.

Wild Animals Will Live There

11Owls and other night birds will live there. Ravens will live there too. He will mark the land to make it empty and ruined.

12Its leaders will have no kingdom left to rule. All its princes will be gone.

13Thorns will grow over its forts. Weeds and thorny plants will grow in its strong places. It will be a home for wild dogs and a place for ostriches.

14Wild animals of the desert will meet hyenas there. One wild goat will call to another. A night animal will rest there and find a safe place.

15The owl will build a nest there and lay eggs. She will hatch them and keep them in her shade. Hunting birds will gather there, each with its mate.

God’s Word Will Come True

16Look in the book of the LORD and read. Not one of these animals will be missing. Not one will be without its mate. His mouth has commanded it. His SpiritspiritSpirit has gathered them.

17He has chosen their places. His hand has measured it out for them. They will own it forever. From one generation to the next, they will live there.
