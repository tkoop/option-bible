Land for Joseph's Sons

1The land for Joseph's family started at the Jordan River near Jericho. It went east to the water of Jericho. It went through the dry land that goes up from Jericho to the hills near Bethel. 2It went from Bethel to Luz. Then it crossed to the border of the Archites at Ataroth. 3It went down west to the border of the Japhletites. That was as far as lower Beth Horon and Gezer. It ended at the sea. 4So Joseph's sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, got their land.

Ephraim's Land

5This was the land for Ephraim's family groups. Their border went from Ataroth Addar in the east to upper Beth Horon. 6It went out toward the sea. From Michmethath on the north, it turned east to Taanath Shiloh. Then it passed by it to Janoah on the east. 7It went down from Janoah to Ataroth and Naarah. It touched Jericho and came out at the Jordan. 8From Tappuah the border went west to the BrookbrookBrook of Kanah. It ended at the sea. This was the land for Ephraim's family groups.

Canaanites in Gezer

9Ephraim also had cities and towns set apart for them inside the land of Manasseh's people. 10But they did not drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer. So the Canaanites live among Ephraim's people even now. They are forced to work.
