Be Good Citizens

1Remind them to obey leaders and people in charge, and to be ready to do every good thing. 2They should not say bad things about anyone. They should be peaceful and kind, and show true gentleness to everyone.

3We were once like that too. We were foolish. We did not obey. We were tricked. We served many wrong wants and pleasures. We lived in badness and jealousy. People hated us, and we hated each other.

Stay Away From Foolish Talk

9But stay away from foolish questions, family histories, fights, and arguments about the law. They do not help. They are useless. 10Warn a person who causes trouble once or twice. Then stay away from him. 11Know that this kind of person is turned away from truth and keeps sinning. He brings judgmentjudgementjudgment on himself.

Paul's Final Words

12When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, try hard to come to me at Nicopolis. I have decided to stay there for the winter. 13Help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their trip. Make sure they have what they need. 14Our people should also learn to do good works and help people in real need. Then they will not be useless. 15Everyone with me says hello to you. Say hello to those who love us because of faith. May grace be with all of you.
