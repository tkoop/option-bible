Reuben's Family

1Reuben was the first son born to Israel. But Reuben lost his first son rights because he slept with his father's wife. So his first son rights went to the sons of Joseph, son of Israel. Reuben was not counted as the first son. 2Judah was the strongest of his brothers. A leader came from him. But Joseph got the first son rights.

Reuben's Sons

3The sons of Reuben, Israel's first son, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

4Joel's son was Shemaiah. Shemaiah's son was Gog. Gog's son was Shimei. 5Shimei's son was Micah. Micah's son was Reaiah. Reaiah's son was Baal. 6Baal's son was Beerah. Beerah was the leader of the Reubenites. The king of Assyria, Tiglath-pileser, took him away. 7Their relatives were listed by family groups. Jeiel was the leader, and Zechariah was with him.

Where They Lived

8Bela was the son of Azaz. Azaz was the son of Shema. Shema was the son of Joel. Bela lived in Aroer, Nebo, and Baal-meon. 9He lived eastward to the edge of the desert, starting from the Euphrates River. His animals were many in Gilead. 10In Saul's time, they fought the Hagrites. The Hagrites lost to them. Then they lived in tents across the east side of Gilead.

Gad's Family

11The Gadites lived across from them in Bashan, as far as Salcah. 12Joel was the leader. Shapham was second. Janai and Shaphat were in Bashan. 13Their brothers by father's houses were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jachan, Zia, and Eber. That is seven. 14They were sons of Abihail, son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz. 15Ahi was the son of Abdiel, son of Guni. He was the leader of their fathers' houses. 16They lived in Gilead, Bashan, and its towns. They also lived in all the pasture lands of Sharon. 17They were all listed in the time of Jotham, king of Judah, and Jeroboam, king of Israel.

The Eastern Tribes Go to War

18The Reubenites, Gadites, and half of Manasseh had 44,760 soldiers. They were brave men. They carried shields and swords. They could use bows. They knew how to fight. 19They fought the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab. 20They cried to God in the war. They trusted in HimhimHim. God listened to them and helped them. The Hagrites and all with them were given into their hands. 21They took 50,000 camels, 250,000 sheep, 2,000 donkeys, and 100,000 people as prisoners. 22Many died because God helped them win the war. Then they lived there until they were taken away.

Half of Manasseh

23Half of Manasseh was very large. They lived in Bashan, Baal-hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon. 24Their fathers' leaders were Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel. They were brave, famous men.

Why They Were Taken Away

25But they sinned against the God of their fathers. They worshiped the gods of the people in that land. God had destroyed those people before them. 26So the God of Israel stirred up Pul, king of Assyria, and Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria. They carried away the Reubenites, Gadites, and half of Manasseh. They took them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river Gozan. They are still there today.
