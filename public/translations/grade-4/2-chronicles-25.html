Amaziah Becomes King

1Amaziah was 25 years old when he became king. He ruled 29 years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem. 2He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, but not with all his heart.

3When his kingdom was strong, he killed the servants who had killed his father, the king. 4But he did not kill their sons. The book of the law of Moses says what the LORD commanded: "Parents must not die for their children. Children must not die for their parents. Each person must die for his own sin."

Amaziah Hires Soldiers

5Amaziah gathered the people of Judah. He chose leaders for groups of 1,000 and 100. He counted all the men of Judah and Benjamin who were 20 years old or older. He found 300,000 strong men who could fight. They held spears and shields. 6He hired 100,000 brave soldiers from Israel. He paid 100 talents of silver.

A Man of God Warns Amaziah

7A man of God came to him and said, "KingkingKing, do not let the army of Israel go with you. The LORD is not with Israel. He is not with the people of Ephraim. 8But if you go, be strong for the fight. God will make you fall before your enemies. God can help or make you fall."

9Amaziah said to the man of God, "What about the 100 talents of silver I gave to the Israel troops?"

The man of God said, "ThetheThe LORD can give you much more than that."

10So Amaziah sent the troops from Ephraim home. They were very angry with Judah. They went home in a rage.

Amaziah Beats Edom

11Amaziah became strong. He led his people to the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. He killed 10,000 men from Seir. 12The men of Judah captured 10,000 men alive. They brought them to the top of a rock. They threw them down. All of them died. 13The troops that Amaziah had sent home raided cities in Judah. They went from Samaria to Beth-horon. They killed 3,000 people. They took many valuable things.

Amaziah Worships False Gods

14After Amaziah came back from killing the Edomites, he brought the gods of the people of Seir. He set them up as his own gods. He bowed down to them. He burned incense to them. 15The LORD was angry with Amaziah. He sent a prophet to him. The prophet said, "Why do you look for gods that could not save their own people from you?"

16While he spoke, the king said to him, "Did we make you an adviser to the king? Stop! Why should you be hit?"

The prophet stopped. Then he said, "I know that God has planned to destroy you. You did this, and you did not listen to my advice."

Amaziah Fights Israel

17Amaziah, king of Judah, asked for advice. Then he sent a message to Joash, son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel. It said, "Come. Let us fight."

18Joash, king of Israel, sent a message back to Amaziah, king of Judah. It said, "A small thistle in Lebanon sent a message to a big cedar tree in Lebanon. It said, 'Give your daughter to my son for a wife.' A wild animal in Lebanon walked by and stepped on the thistle. 19You say, 'I beat Edom.' YouryourYour heart makes you proud. Now stay home. Why stir up trouble? You and Judah will fall."

20Amaziah did not listen. This was from God. God wanted to let the enemy win. They had looked for the gods of Edom.

21Joash, king of Israel, went up. He and Amaziah, king of Judah, fought each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 22Judah was beaten by Israel. Every man ran home. 23Joash, king of Israel, captured Amaziah, king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh. He brought him to Jerusalem. Amaziah Dies

25Amaziah, son of Joash, king of Judah, lived 15 years after the death of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel. 26The rest of the things Amaziah did, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel. 27From the time Amaziah turned away from the LORD, people in Jerusalem made a plan against him. He ran to Lachish. They sent men after him to Lachish. They killed him there. 28They brought him back on horses. They buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah.
