The Last Days

1At that time, Michael will stand up. He is the great leader who watches over your people. There will be a hard time. No time has ever been that hard since nations began. But at that time, your people will be saved. Everyone whose name is in the book will be saved. 2Many people who sleep in the dust of the earth will wake up. Some will live forever. Others will be shamed forever. 3The wise people will shine like the bright sky. The ones who help many people do right will shine like the stars forever and ever.

4But you, Daniel, keep these words secret. Seal the book until the end time comes. Many people will go here and there. Knowledge will grow.

Daniel by the River

5Then I, Daniel, looked. I saw two other people standing there. One stood on this side of the river. The other stood on the other side.

6One of them said to the man in linen clothes, who was above the river water, "How long until these amazing things end?"

7I heard the man in linen clothes, who was above the river water. He raised his right hand and his left hand to Heavenheavenheaven. He made a promise by HimhimHim who lives forever. He said, "It will be for a time, times, and half a time. When the power of the holy people is broken, all these things will end."

8I heard it, but I did not understand. So I asked, "MymyMy lord, what will happen at the end?"

9He said, "Go your way, Daniel. These words are secret and sealed until the end time."

10Many people will be made clean and pure. But bad people will keep doing bad things. None of the bad people will understand. But wise people will understand. 11From the time the daily offering is stopped and the terrible thing that brings ruin is set up, there will be 1,290 days. 12Happy is the person who waits and reaches the end of the 1,335 days.

13But you, go your way until the end. Then you will rest. You will stand up and get your reward at the end of the days.
