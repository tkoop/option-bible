A Prayer When Good People Are Gone

1Help, the LORD, because good people are gone. People who can be trusted are gone from us. 2People lie to each other. They use nice words, but they do not mean them.

3May the LORD stop all smooth talkers and every proud tongue.

4They say, “Our words will make us strong. Our lips are ours. Who is our master?”

5“Because poor people are treated badly and needy people cry out, now I will get up,” says the LORD. “I will keep safe the one who wants help.”

6The LORD’s words are pure. They are like silver cleaned in a fire on the ground, made pure seven times. 7You, the LORD, will keep them. You will guard us from these people forever.

8Wicked people walk around everywhere when bad things are praised among people.
