Nahash Attacks Jabesh-gilead

1Nahash was the king of the Ammonites. He went up and camped near Jabesh-gilead. The men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Make an agreement with us, and we will serve you.”

2Nahash the Ammonite said to them, “I will make an agreement with you, but I must take out the right eye of every one of you. Then all of Israel will be ashamed.”

3The leaders of Jabesh said to him, “Give us seven days. We will send messengers all over Israel. If no one comes to save us, we will come out to you.”

Saul Hears the News

4The messengers came to Gibeah, where Saul lived. They told the people the news. All the people cried out loud and wept. 5Saul was coming in from the field behind his oxen. He asked, “Why are the people crying?” They told him what the men of Jabesh had said. 6When Saul heard this, the SpiritspiritSpirit of God came on him with power. He became very angry.

Saul Calls Israel to Fight

7Saul took a pair of oxen and cut them into pieces. He sent the pieces by messengers all over Israel. He said, “This is what will happen to the oxen of anyone who does not follow Saul and Samuel into battle.” The fear of the LORD fell on the people, and they all came out together as one man. 8Saul counted them at Bezek. There were 300,000 men from Israel and 30,000 men from Judah.

Help Comes to Jabesh-gilead

9They told the messengers to tell the men of Jabesh-gilead, “Tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, you will be saved.” The messengers went and told the men of Jabesh, and they were glad.

10The men of Jabesh said, “Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you can do to us whatever seems good to you.” 11The next day Saul split the army into three groups. They attacked the Ammonite camp in the early morning. They fought until the sun was hot. The Ammonites who were left ran away. Not even two of them stayed together.

Some Men Question Saul

12The people said to Samuel, “Who said, ‘Saul should not be our king’? Give us those men, and we will put them to death.”

13Saul said, “No one will be put to death today, because today the LORD has saved Israel.”

Saul Is Made King Again

14Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and make Saul king again there.” 15All the people went to Gilgal. There they made Saul king before the LORD. They offered peace offerings to the LORD. Saul and all the men of Israel were very happy there.
