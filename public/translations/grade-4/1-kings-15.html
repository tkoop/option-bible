Abijam Rules Judah

1In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam began to rule over Judah. 2He ruled in Jerusalem for three years. His mother was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom. 3He did the same bad things his father did. His heart was not fully for the LORD his God, like David's heart was.

4But because David served the LORD, the LORD let David's family keep ruling in Jerusalem. He let David's son rule after him, and HeheHe kept Jerusalem strong. 5David did what was right in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn from any command all his life, except the wrong he did with Uriah the Hittite.

6There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all through Abijam's life. 7The rest of what Abijam did is written in the book of the kings of Judah. There was also war between Abijam and Jeroboam. 8Abijam died and was buried in the city of David. His son Asa became king after him.

Asa Rules Judah

9In the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa became king of Judah. 10He ruled in Jerusalem forty-one years. His mother was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom. 11Asa did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, like David his father.

12He sent away the men who served false gods in the temple. He took away all the idols his fathers had made. 13He also removed his mother Maacah from being queen mother. She had made a horrible idol for Asherah. Asa cut down the idol and burned it in the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 14The hilltop worship places were not taken away. But Asa's heart was fully for the LORD all his life. 15He brought into the house of the LORD the silver, gold, and vessels that he and his father had set apart for God.

Asa and Baasha Fight

16There was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days. 17Baasha king of Israel attacked Judah. He built up Ramah. He wanted to stop people from going to or coming from Asa king of Judah.

Asa Asks Aram for Help

18Then Asa took all the silver and gold left in the treasures of the house of the LORD and the king's house. He gave them to his servants. He sent them to Ben-Hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, king of Aram, who lived in Damascus. He said, 19"There is an agreement between me and you. There was one between my father and your father. I have sent you silver and gold. Go and break your agreement with Baasha king of Israel. Then he will leave me alone."

20Ben-Hadad listened to King Asa. He sent his army leaders against the cities of Israel. He struck Ijon, Dan, Abel Beth Maacah, and all the land of Chinnereth and Naphtali. 21When Baasha heard this, he stopped building Ramah. He stayed in Tirzah. 22Then King Asa called all Judah. No one was excused. They carried away the stones and wood of Ramah that Baasha had built. King Asa used them to build Geba in Benjamin and Mizpah.

23The rest of all that Asa did, his power, and the cities he built are written in the book of the kings of Judah. In his old age he got sick in his feet. 24Asa died and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father. His son Jehoshaphat became king after him.

Kings of Israel: Nadab and Baasha

25Nadab son of Jeroboam became king of Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah. He ruled over Israel two years. 26He did evil in the eyes of the LORD. He walked in his father's way and in his sin, which made Israel sin. 27Baasha son of Ahijah, from the family of Issachar, made a plan against him. Baasha struck him at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines. Nadab and all Israel were attacking Gibbethon. 28Baasha killed Nadab in the third year of Asa king of Judah. He became king in his place.

29When Baasha became king, he struck down all the house of Jeroboam. He did not leave Jeroboam anyone who breathed. He destroyed them, as the LORD had said through HishisHis servant Ahijah the Shilonite. 30This happened because of Jeroboam's sins and the sin he made Israel do. He made the LORD, the God of Israel, angry. 31The rest of what Nadab did is written in the book of the kings of Israel. 32There was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.

Baasha Rules Israel

33Baasha son of Ahijah became king over all Israel in Tirzah in the third year of Asa king of Judah. He ruled twenty-four years. 34He did evil in the eyes of the LORD. He walked in the way of Jeroboam and in his sin, which made Israel sin.
