Think About Heaven

1If you have been raised with Christ, look for things above. Christ sits at God's right hand. 2Think about things above. Do not think only about things on EarthEarthearth. 3You died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God. 4When Christ, who is your life, shows HimselfhimselfHimself, you will show too. You will be with HimhimHim in glory.

Put Away Bad Things

5So put to death the bad things in your life on EarthEarthearth. Stop sexual sin, dirty acts, bad feelings, wrong wants, and greed. Greed is like worshiping fake gods. 6God's anger comes because of these things. It comes on people who do not obey. 7You used to live this way. You did these things. 8But now you must put away all these things: anger, hot temper, mean acts, bad talk, and dirty words from your mouth. 9Do not lie to each other. You have taken off the old you with its bad acts.

Put On the New You

10Put on the new you. It is made new to know God. It is like the OneoneOne who made you. 11Here there is no Greek or Jew. There is no one who is marked or not marked. There is no person from a far place. There is no slave or free person. Christ is all, and HeheHe is in all. 12You are God's chosen people. You are holy and loved. So put on kind hearts, good acts, humble thoughts, gentle ways, and patience. 13Be patient with each other. Forgive each other. If someone has a problem with another person, forgive them. Forgive like the Lord forgave you. 14Above all, put on love. Love holds everything together in a perfect way.

Let Peace Rule

15Let Christ's peace rule in your hearts. You were called to peace in one body. Be thankful. 16Let Christ's word live richly in you. Teach and warn each other with wisdom. Sing Bible songs, hymns, and songs that praise God. Sing with grace in your hearts to God. 17Whatever you do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus. Give thanks to God the FatherfatherFather through HimhimHim.

Rules for Home Life

18Wives, follow your husbands' lead. This fits in the Lord. 19Husbands, love your wives. Do not be mean to them. 20Children, obey your parents in all things. This pleases the Lord. 21Fathers, do not make your children angry. Do not make them lose heart. 22Slaves, obey your earthly masters in all things. Do not work only when they watch. Work with a true heart. Fear the Lord. 23Whatever you do, work hard. Work as for the Lord, not for people. 24Know that you will get your reward from the Lord. You serve Christ the Lord. 25The one who does wrong will be paid back for the wrong. The Lord does not play favorites.
