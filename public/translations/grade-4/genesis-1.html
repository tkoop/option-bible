God Makes the World

1In the beginning, God made the sky and the earth. 2The EarthEarthearth was empty and had no shape. It was dark. Deep water covered everything. God's SpiritspiritSpirit moved over the water.

God Makes Light and Dark

3God said, "Let there be light." And there was light. 4God saw that the light was good. He separated the light from the dark. 5God called the light "day." He called the dark "night." Evening came, and then morning came. That was the first day.

God Makes the Sky

6God said, "Let there be a sky in the middle of the water. Let it split the water in two." 7God made the sky. He split the water below the sky from the water above the sky. And it was so. 8God called the sky "Heavenheavenheaven." Evening came, and then morning came. That was the second day.

God Makes Land and Seas

9God said, "Let the water under the sky come together in one place. Let dry land show." And it was so. 10God called the dry land "EarthEarthearth." He called the water "seas." God saw that it was good.

God Makes Plants

11God said, "Let the earth grow plants. Let plants make seeds. Let trees make fruit with seeds inside. Each kind should make its own kind." And it was so. 12The EarthEarthearth grew plants and trees. Each kind made its own seeds. God saw that it was good. 13Evening came, and then morning came. That was the third day.

God Makes Sun, Moon, and Stars

14God said, "Let there be lights in the sky. They will split the day from the night. They will be signs for special days, years, and seasons. 15Let them shine on the earth." And it was so. 16God made two big lights. The bigger light rules the day. The smaller light rules the night. He also made the stars. 17God put them in the sky to shine on the earth. 18They rule the day and the night. They split the light from the dark. God saw that it was good. 19Evening came, and then morning came. That was the fourth day.

God Makes Fish and Birds

20God said, "Let the water fill with living things. Let birds fly above the earth in the sky." 21God made the big sea animals. He made every living thing that moves in the water. He made every kind of bird. God saw that it was good. 22God blessed them and said, "Have many babies. Fill the seas. Let birds grow in number on the earth." 23Evening came, and then morning came. That was the fifth day.

God Makes Land Animals

24God said, "Let the earth make living things. Let there be animals, farm animals, and things that crawl." And it was so. 25God made the wild animals, the farm animals, and everything that crawls on the ground. Each kind was its own kind. God saw that it was good.

God Makes People

26God said, "Let UsusUs make people in OurourOur image. Let them rule the fish, the birds, the animals, and all the earth. Let them rule everything that crawls on the ground." 27So God made people in HishisHis own image. He made them male and female. 28God blessed them and said, "Have many children. Fill the earth and be in charge of it. Rule the fish, the birds, and every living thing on the earth."

29God said, "Look, I give you every plant that makes seeds. I give you every tree that makes fruit with seeds. They will be food for you. 30I give every green plant to the animals, the birds, and everything that crawls on the earth. It will be food for them." And it was so. 31God saw all that HeheHe had made. It was very good. Evening came, and then morning came. That was the sixth day.
