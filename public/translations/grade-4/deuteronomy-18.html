The Priests and Levites

1The priests from the tribe of Levi will not get land with the rest of Israel. They will eat the food offerings given to the the LORD. That is their share. 2They will not have land with their brothers. The the LORD is their share, just like HeheHe said.

3This is what the priests get from the people, from those who bring animals to offer: If it is an ox or a sheep, they give the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the stomach. 4Give him the first of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the first wool from your sheep. 5The the LORD your God chose him and his sons from all your tribes to stand and serve the the LORD all their lives.

6If a Levite comes from one of your towns, from anywhere in Israel where he lives, and he wants to go to the place the the LORD chooses, 7he may serve in the name of the the LORD his God, like all his brother Levites who stand there before the the LORD. 8They will eat the same share. They can also keep money from selling their family things.

Stay Away from Magic

9When you enter the land the the LORD your God gives you, do not learn to do the bad things those nations do. 10No one among you may burn his son or daughter as an offering, or tell fortunes, or read signs, or do magic. 11No one may cast spells, ask a medium or fortune-teller, or try to talk to the dead. 12Anyone who does these things disgusts the the LORD. Because of these bad things, the the LORD your God will drive those nations out before you. 13You must be blameless before the the LORD your God.

14The nations you are taking the land from listen to fortune-tellers and sign readers. But the the LORD your God has not allowed you to do that.

A Prophet Like Moses

15The the LORD your God will raise up a prophet like me from among you, from your brothers. You must listen to him. 16This is what you asked the the LORD your God for at Horeb on the day the people met together. You said, "Do not let me hear the voice of the the LORD my God again. Do not let me see this great fire again, or I will die."

17The the LORD said to me, "They have spoken well. 18I will raise up a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put MymyMy words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him. 19Anyone who does not listen to MymyMy words that he speaks in MymyMy name, I will hold him responsible. 20But a prophet who dares to speak a word in MymyMy name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must die."

How to Test a Prophet

21You may say in your heart, "How will we know if the the LORD did not speak?" 22If a prophet speaks in the the LORD's name and it does not happen, the the LORD did not speak that word. The prophet spoke on his own. Do not be afraid of him.
