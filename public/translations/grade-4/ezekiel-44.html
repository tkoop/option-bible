The East Gate Stays Shut

1He brought me back to the outer gate of God's holy place. It faced east. The gate was shut. 2The LORD said to me, "This gate will stay shut. It must not be opened. No one may come in through it, because the LORD, the God of Israel, came in through it. So it must stay shut. 3Only the leader may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD. He will come in through the porch of the gate and go out the same way."

God's Glory Fills His House

4He brought me to the front of God's house by the north gate. I looked and saw the LORD's bright glory fill God's house. I fell face down.

A Warning for Israel

5The LORD said to me, "Person, pay attention. Look closely with your eyes. Listen closely with your ears to everything I say to you. Remember all the rules and laws of the LORD's house. Pay close attention to the way in and the ways out of God's house. 6Tell the stubborn people of Israel, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: You have done enough bad things, people of Israel. 7You let people from other lands come in. They do not follow God in their hearts or bodies. They made MymyMy house not holy. You gave MemeMe fat and blood as offerings. You broke MymyMy promise with all your bad things. 8You did not take care of MymyMy holy things. You let other people take care of MymyMy holy place for you.' 9ThetheThe Lord the LORD says, 'No person from another land may come into MymyMy holy place. Not even one who lives with Israel's people. No one whose heart and body are not right with MemeMe may come in.'"

Levites Who Went Wrong

10The Levites who left MemeMe when Israel went the wrong way will pay for their sin. They left MemeMe to follow false gods. 11But they will still work in MymyMy holy place. They will guard the gates of God's house and work there. They will prepare the burnt offerings and sacrifices for the people. They will stand and help the people. 12They served false gods. They caused Israel to sin. So I made a serious promise that they will pay for their sin, says the Lord the LORD. 13They must not come near MemeMe to be priests. They must not come near MymyMy holy things or the most holy things. They will carry their shame and their bad sins. 14But I will put them in charge of all the work in God's house and everything done there.

Priests Who Stayed True

15But the priests who are Levites and come from Zadok's family stayed faithful. They took care of MymyMy holy place when Israel went the wrong way. They may come near to serve MemeMe. They will stand before MemeMe and give MemeMe fat and blood offerings, says the Lord the LORD. 16Only they may come into MymyMy holy place. Only they may come to MymyMy table to serve MemeMe. They will do what I ask.

Rules for God's Priests

17When they come into the inner court, they must wear linen clothes. They must not wear wool when they work at the inner gates or inside God's house. 18They must wear linen hats on their heads and linen underclothes around their waists. They must not wear anything that makes them sweat. 19When they go out to the outer court to be with the people, they must take off those clothes. They must leave them in the holy rooms and put on other clothes. Then they will not make the people holy with their clothes. 20They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long. They must trim their hair neatly.

21No priest may drink wine when he goes into the inner court. 22They must not marry a woman whose husband died or a divorced woman. They must marry a woman who has never been married, from Israel's people. Or they may marry the widow of a priest. 23They must teach MymyMy people the difference between what is holy and what is not holy. They must show them the difference between what is clean and what is not clean. 24When people do not agree, the priests must judge them. They must judge by MymyMy rules. They must keep MymyMy laws and MymyMy rules for all MymyMy special days. They must keep MymyMy Sabbaths holy.

25A priest must not make himself unclean by going near a dead person. But he may do so for his father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or sister who never married. 26After he is clean again, he must wait seven days. 27On the day he goes into the holy place, into the inner court, to serve there, he must bring a sin offering, says the Lord the LORD.

What the Priests Receive

28I am what they receive from MemeMe. Do not give them land in Israel, because I am their land. 29They will eat the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering. Everything in Israel given to the LORD will belong to them. 30The best of all first crops and every gift from all your offerings will belong to the priests. You must also give the priests the first of your dough, so a blessing may rest on your house. 31The priests must not eat any animal that was found dead or torn by wild animals.
