God Tells Joshua Not to Be Afraid

1The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid. Do not give up. Take all the fighting men with you, and go up to Ai. See, I have given the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his land into your hand. 2Do to Ai and its king what you did to Jericho and its king. But you may take the things and animals. Set a trap behind the city."

Joshua Plans the Attack

3Joshua and all the fighting men got up to go up to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand brave men and sent them out at night. 4He told them, "See, you are the hidden men behind the city. Do not go far from the city. All of you stay ready. 5I and all the people with me will go near the city. When they come out to meet us, as before, we will run away from them. 6They will chase us until we draw them away from the city. They will think we are running from them, as before. Then we will run away. 7Then you will get up from your hiding place and take the city. the LORD your God will give it into your hand. 8When you take the city, burn it. Do as the LORD told you."

Joshua Leads the People to Ai

9Joshua sent them to the hidden place. They stayed between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai. Joshua stayed that night with the people. 10Joshua got up early in the morning. He counted the people. He and the leaders of Israel went up in front of the people to Ai. 11All the fighting men with him went up. They came near the city and camped north of Ai. There was a valley between them and Ai.

12Joshua took about five thousand men and set them in the hidden place between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. 13They set all the army north of the city and the men at the back west of the city. That night Joshua went into the middle of the valley.

The People of Ai Come Out

14When the king of Ai saw this, he hurried. Early in the morning, the men of the city went out to meet Israel in battle. The king and all his people went to the place in front of the open plain. He did not know that hidden men were behind the city. 15Joshua and all Israel let the men of Ai push them back. They ran toward the desert.

16All the people in the city were called to chase them. They chased Joshua and were drawn away from the city. 17No man was left in Ai or Bethel who did not go after Israel. They left the city open and chased Israel.

The LORD Tells Joshua to Raise the Spear

18Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Hold out the spear in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand." Joshua held out the spear in his hand toward the city.

19As soon as he held out his hand, the hidden men got up quickly from their place. They ran into the city, took it, and hurried to set it on fire. 20The men of Ai turned and looked. They saw smoke rising from the city into the sky. They had no strength to run this way or that. The people who had run toward the desert turned back against those who chased them.

21Joshua and all Israel saw that the hidden men had taken the city and that smoke was rising. They turned back and struck the men of Ai. 22The men who came out of the city were now in the middle of Israel. Israel struck them until no one was left alive.

The City of Ai Is Destroyed

23They caught the king of Ai alive and brought him to Joshua. 24Israel finished killing all the people who lived in Ai. They chased them in the fields and in the desert. All of them fell by the sword until they were gone. Then Israel went back to Ai and struck it with the sword. 25All the people of Ai who fell that day were twelve thousand, both men and women.

26Joshua did not pull back his hand that held the spear until all the people of Ai were completely destroyed. 27Israel took only the animals and the things of the city, as the LORD had told Joshua. 28Joshua burned Ai and made it a pile of ruins that is still there today. 29He hung the king of Ai on a tree until evening. At sunset Joshua told them to take his body down from the tree. They threw it at the gate of the city and put a big pile of stones over it. It is still there today.

Joshua Builds an Altar

30Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal. 31He did this as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had told the people of Israel. The altar was made of whole stones that no iron tool had touched. On it they offered burnt offerings to the LORD and sacrificed peace offerings.

32There, in front of the people of Israel, Joshua wrote on the stones a copy of the law of Moses. 33All Israel stood on both sides of the covenant box of the LORD. The priests and the men who carried the box stood there too. Half of the people faced Mount Gerizim, and half faced Mount Ebal. This was as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had first commanded, to bless the people of Israel.

34After that, Joshua read all the words of the law, the blessings and the curses, just as they were written in the book of the law. 35Joshua read every word that Moses had commanded. He read it in front of all the people of Israel, the women, the little children, and the foreigners who lived among them. Not one word was left out.
