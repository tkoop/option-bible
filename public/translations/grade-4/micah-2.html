Trouble for Greedy People

1Trouble is coming for people who plan bad things and do wrong while they are in bed. When morning comes, they do those bad things because they have the power to do them. 2They want fields and take them. They want houses and take them too. They are mean to a man and his home. They take away a man's family land.

The LORD's Warning

3So the LORD says, “I am planning trouble for this family. You cannot get out of it. You will not walk with proud heads anymore, because it will be a bad time.”

4In that day people will make fun of you. They will cry a sad song: “We are ruined! He has changed the share of my people. How HeheHe took it from me! He gave our fields to people who turned against us.” 5So you will not have anyone among the LORD's people to divide the land.

False Preachers

6They say, “Do not preach!” They say, “Do not preach those things. We will not be shamed.”

God Will Bring His People Back

12I will surely gather all of you, people of Jacob. I will surely bring together the rest of Israel. I will put them together like sheep in a pen, like a flock in a field. There will be many people. 13The one who breaks open the way will go up before them. They will break out, go through the gate, and go out.
