A Prayer for a Fair King

1God, give the king YouryourYour fair rules. Give YouryourYour good and right ways to the king’s son. 2May he judge YouryourYour people in a right way. May he judge YouryourYour hurting people fairly. 3May the mountains bring peace to the people. May the hills bring good and fair ways. 4May he help the people who are hurt. May he save the children of poor people. May he stop the ones who hurt others.

5May people honourhonourhonor YouyouYou while the sun shines. May they honourhonourhonor YouyouYou while the moon shines, for all time. 6May he be like rain on new grass. May he be like showers that water the earth. 7While he rules, may good people grow strong. May peace fill the land for all time.

The King Rules Everywhere

8May he rule from sea to sea. May he rule from the river to the ends of the earth. 9May the desert people bow to him. May his enemies fall down and give up. 10May kings from faraway lands bring gifts. May the kings of Sheba and Seba bring their gifts. 11May all kings bow to him. May all nations serve him.

The King Helps the Poor

12He will save the poor who cry for help. He will help those who hurt and have no one to help them. 13He will care for the poor and needy. He will save their lives. 14He will rescue them from harm and wrong. He sees their lives as very precious.

15May he live a long time. May gold from Sheba be given to him. May people always pray for him and bless him all day. 16May there be lots of grain in the land. May it grow on the tops of mountains. May its fruit grow like the trees in Lebanon. May it grow like grass in a field. 17May his name last forever. May it grow as long as the sun shines. May all nations be blessed because of him. May they call him blessed.

Praise to the LORD

18Praise the LORD God, the God of Israel. He alone does amazing things. 19Praise HishisHis glorious name forever. May all the earth be filled with HishisHis glory. Amen and AmenamenAmen. 20This ends the prayers of David, the son of Jesse.
