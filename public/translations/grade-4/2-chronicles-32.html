Sennacherib Attacks Judah

1After all this, Sennacherib, king of Assyria, came to Judah. He camped near its strong cities. He wanted to take them. 2Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come. He knew Sennacherib wanted to fight Jerusalem.

3Hezekiah talked with his leaders and brave men. They decided to stop the water from the springs outside the city. The men helped him. 4Many people came together. They stopped all the springs. They also stopped the stream that ran through the land. They said, "Why should the kings of Assyria come and find lots of water?"
