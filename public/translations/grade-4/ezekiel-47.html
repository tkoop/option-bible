Water Flows from the Temple

1He brought me back to the door of the house. I saw water coming out from under the door. It flowed east. The house faced east. The water ran down from the right side of the house, south of the altar.

2He led me out by the north gate and around to the outside gate that faces east. Water was trickling from the right side.

3The man went east. He had a measuring line in his hand. He measured one thousand cubits. Then he led me through the water. It was up to my ankles. 4He measured another thousand and led me through the water. It was up to my knees. He measured another thousand and led me through the water. It was up to my waist.

5He measured another thousand. It was a river I could not cross. The water was too deep. I would have to swim. No one could cross it.

6He said to me, "SonsonSon of man, do you see this?" Then he led me back to the bank of the river.

7When I got back, I saw many trees on both sides of the river.

8He said to me, "This water flows to the east country. It goes down to the Arabah. It enters the sea. When it reaches the sea, the water will be healed. 9Wherever the river flows, living things will live. There will be many fish. The water that comes there makes things well. Everything will live where the river goes.

10"Men will fish by the sea. They will stand from En-gedi to En-eglaim. They will spread nets. The fish will be many kinds, like the fish of the great sea. 11But the swamps and marshes will not be healed. They will be left for salt. 12By the river, on both sides, trees will grow for food. Their leaves will not dry up. Their fruit will not stop. They will make new fruit every month, because the water comes from the holy place. The fruit will be for food. The leaves will be for healing."

The Land for the Tribes

13The Lord the LORD says this: "This is the border of the land you will take for the twelve tribes of Israel. Joseph will get two parts. 14You will share it equally. I promised to give it to your fathers. This land will be yours.

15"Here is the north border. It goes from the great sea by the road to Lebo-hamath, to Zedad. 16It goes through Hamath, Berothah, and Sibraim, between Damascus and Hamath. It reaches Hazar-hatticon by the border of Hauran. 17So the border goes from the sea to Hazar-enan, north of Damascus and Hamath. This is the north side.

18"On the east side, the border runs from between Hauran and Damascus, along the Jordan, between Gilead and the land of Israel, to the eastern sea. Measure this to the east. 19On the south side, it goes from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, along the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt, to the great sea. This is the south side. 20On the west side, it is the great sea, from the border to across from Lebo-hamath. This is the west side.

21"Divide this land among the tribes of Israel. 22Share it for your own and for the strangers who live among you and have children. Treat them like people born in Israel. They will share the land with you among the tribes of Israel. 23Give the stranger his share in the tribe where he lives," says the Lord the LORD.
