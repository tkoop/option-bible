Zedekiah Rules in Jerusalem

1Zedekiah was 21 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. His mother was Hamutal. She was the daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah. 2He did what was bad in the eyes of the LORD. He did like Jehoiakim had done. 3The LORD was angry with Jerusalem and Judah. He would send them away from HimhimHim. Zedekiah also turned against the king of Babylon.

The City Is Attacked

4In the ninth year of his rule, in the tenth month, on the tenth day, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came with all his army. They camped around Jerusalem. They built ramps against the city. 5The city was under attack until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah. 6By the ninth day of the fourth month, there was no food in the city. The people were starving.

Zedekiah Is Caught

7Then the city wall was broken through. The Babylonian soldiers were all around. At night, the king and his soldiers ran away through the gate between the two walls near the king's garden. They went toward the desert. 8The Babylonian army chased King Zedekiah. They caught him on the plains of Jericho. All his soldiers ran away from him. 9They took the king to Riblah in the land of Hamath. There the king of Babylon judged him. 10At Riblah, the king of Babylon killed Zedekiah's sons while Zedekiah watched. He also killed all the leaders of Judah. 11Then he put out Zedekiah's eyes. He tied him with bronze chains. He took him to Babylon. Zedekiah stayed in prison until he died.

The City Is Burned

12In the fifth month, on the tenth day, in the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuzaradan came to Jerusalem. He was the captain of the guard who worked for the king of Babylon. 13He burned the house of the LORD. He burned the king's house. He burned all the big houses in Jerusalem. 14The Babylonian soldiers with him tore down all the walls around Jerusalem. 15Nebuzaradan took away the rest of the people who were still in the city. He took the people who had given up to the king of Babylon. He took the rest of the workers. 16But Nebuzaradan left some of the poor people. They were to take care of the grape fields and farms.

Temple Things Are Taken

17The Babylonians broke the bronze pillars in the house of the LORD. They broke the bronze stands and the big bronze bowl called the SeaseaSea. They took all the bronze to Babylon. 18They took the pots, shovels, bowls, and all the bronze tools used for the temple. 19They took the cups, fire pans, bowls, pots, lampstands, pans, and small bowls. Some were gold and some were silver. 20The two pillars, the SeaseaSea, the twelve bronze bulls under the stands, and all the things King Solomon made for the house of the LORD were too heavy to weigh. There was so much bronze. 21Each pillar was 18 cubits high. It was 12 cubits around. It was four fingers thick and hollow. 22The bronze top on each pillar was 5 cubits high. There was bronze lattice and pomegranates all around the top. Both pillars were the same. 23There were 96 pomegranates on the outside. There were 100 pomegranates all around the lattice.

More People Are Taken

24Nebuzaradan took Seraiah the chief priest. He took Zephaniah the second priest. He took the three men who guarded the door. 25From the city he took one officer who was in charge of the soldiers. He took seven of the king's advisors who were found there. He took the army commander's secretary. He took 60 other men from the city. 26Nebuzaradan took them to Riblah. They went to the king of Babylon. 27There the king of Babylon killed them in Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Judah was taken away from its land.

How Many People Were Taken

28This is how many people Nebuchadnezzar took away: In the seventh year, 3,023 Jews. 29In the eighteenth year, 832 people from Jerusalem. 30In the twenty-third year, Nebuzaradan took 745 Jews. In all, 4,600 people.

Jehoiachin Is Set Free

31In the thirty-seventh year after Jehoiachin, king of Judah, was taken away, on the twenty-fifth day of the twelfth month, Evil-Merodach, king of Babylon, showed kindness to Jehoiachin. He brought him out of prison. 32He spoke kindly to him. He gave him a seat above the other kings in Babylon. 33Jehoiachin took off his prison clothes. He ate bread every day for the rest of his life. 34Every day, the king of Babylon gave him food until he died.
