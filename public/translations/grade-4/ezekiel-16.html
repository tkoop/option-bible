The LORD Speaks to Ezekiel

1The word of the LORD came to me. 2He said, "SonsonSon of man, tell Jerusalem about her bad ways."

Jerusalem's Early Days

3"This is what the Lord, the LORD, says to Jerusalem: You come from Canaan. That is where you were born. Your father was an Amorite, and your mother was a Hittite. 4On the day you were born, no one cut your cord. No one washed you with water. No one rubbed you with salt. No one wrapped you in cloth. 5No one felt sorry for you or did any of these things to care for you. You were thrown into a field. You were hated on the day you were born.

The LORD Cares for Jerusalem

6"I passed by you and saw you kicking in your blood. I said to you, 'Live!' Yes, I said to you, 'Live!' 7I made you grow like a plant in a field. You grew up and became a young woman. Your breasts were formed, and your hair grew. But you were still naked.

I put bracelets on your arms and a chain around your neck. 12I put a ring on your nose and earrings in your ears. You wore a beautiful crown on your head. 13You wore gold and silver. Your clothes were fine linen, silk, and pretty cloth. You ate the best flour, honey, and oil. You were very beautiful, and you became a queen. 14People in other nations heard about your beauty. Your beauty was perfect, because I gave it to you, says the Lord, the LORD.

Jerusalem's Punishment Begins

You made a high place in every street. 25You built high places at the top of every road. You made your beauty dirty. You made high places in every street. But you gave gifts to all who loved you. You paid them to come to you from all around because you were not faithful. 34You were the opposite of other women. No man came to you for pay; you paid them. So you were different.

The LORD's JudgmentJudgementJudgment

35"So, you prostitute, listen to the word of the LORD. 36This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Because you showed your bad ways and uncovered yourself to your lovers and your idols, and because you gave them your children's blood, 37I will gather all your lovers you were happy with, and all those you loved, and all those you hated. I will gather them around you. Then I will uncover you for them to see all your nakedness. 38I will punish you like women who are not faithful and who kill. Jerusalem and Her Sisters

53"I will bring back Sodom and her daughters and Samaria and her daughters from being captives. I will also bring back your captives with them. 54Then you will carry your shame.
