Two Women Bring a Problem to the King

16Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king. They stood before him.

17One woman said, "Please, sir, this woman and I live in the same house. I had a baby while she was there. 18Three days after my baby was born, she had a baby too. We were alone in the house. No one else was there, just the two of us. 19During the night, this woman's baby died because she lay on it. 20She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while I was sleeping. She put him in her arms and put her dead baby in my arms. 21When I got up in the morning to feed my baby, he was dead. But when I looked closely, I saw he was not my baby."

22The other woman said, "No! The living baby is mine, and the dead one is yours."

But the first woman said, "No! The dead baby is yours, and the living one is mine." They argued in front of the king.

23The king said, "OneoneOne woman says her baby is alive and the other woman's baby is dead. The other woman says the opposite."

26The woman whose baby was alive loved her son very much. She said to the king, "Please, sir, give her the living baby. Do not kill him."

But the other woman said, "HeheHe will not be mine or yours. Cut him in half."
