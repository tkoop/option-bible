Daniel Is Chosen to Lead

1It made King Darius happy to choose 120 rulers to help run the whole kingdom. 2He put three leaders over them. Daniel was one of them. These leaders checked the rulers' work so the king would not lose anything. 3Daniel did so well that the king planned to put him in charge of the whole kingdom.

Men Look for a Way to Trap Daniel

4The other leaders and rulers tried to find something wrong with Daniel in his work for the king. But they could not find any wrong or bad thing. He was faithful. No careless or bad thing was found in him. 5So they said, "We will never find anything wrong with Daniel unless we find something against him about the law of his God."

They Ask the King for a New Law

6Then these men went to the king together. They said, "O King Darius, live forever! 7All the king's top helpers, rulers, and leaders have agreed. The king should make a new law. For thirty days, anyone who prays to any god or man, except you, O king, will be thrown into the pit with lions. 8Now, O king, make this law and sign it. Then it cannot be changed, like the laws of the Medes and Persians, which cannot be taken back."

9So King Darius signed the written law.

Daniel Keeps Praying

10When Daniel learned that the law was signed, he went to his house. His upstairs room had windows that opened toward Jerusalem. Three times a day he got down on his knees. He prayed and thanked his God, just as he had done before. 11Then these men went as a group and found Daniel praying and asking his God for help.

Daniel Is Accused

12So they went to the king and said, "O KingkingKing, did you not sign a law? For thirty days, no one may pray to any god or man except you, O king. If someone does, he will be thrown into the pit with lions."

The king answered, "This is true. It is the law of the Medes and Persians, and it cannot be changed."

13Then they said to the king, "Daniel, who is one of the people taken from Judah, does not care about you, O king. He does not care about the law you signed. He still prays three times a day."

The King Tries to Save Daniel

14When the king heard this, he was very upset. He wanted to save Daniel. He worked until sunset to rescue him. 15Then the men went to the king together. They said, "Remember, O king, that no law the king makes can be changed. That is the law of the Medes and Persians."

Daniel Is Thrown to the Lions

16So the king gave the order. Daniel was brought and thrown into the pit with lions. The king said to Daniel, "May your God, whom you serve all the time, save you!"

17A stone was brought and put over the mouth of the pit. The king sealed it with his own ring and with the rings of his important men. This was so nothing could be changed about Daniel.

The King Waits

18Then the king went to his palace. He did not eat. No music or fun was brought to him. He could not sleep.

The King Goes to the Pit

19At the first light of morning, the king got up. He hurried to the pit with lions. 20When he got near the pit, he called out in a worried voice, "O Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God, whom you serve all the time, been able to save you from the lions?"

Daniel Is Safe

21Then Daniel spoke to the king: "O king, live forever! 22My God sent HishisHis angel and shut the lions' mouths. They did not hurt me. I was found not guilty before HimhimHim. I have not done anything wrong against you, O king."

The King Is Happy

23The king was very happy. He ordered Daniel to be lifted out of the pit. When Daniel was lifted out, no hurt was found on him. He had trusted in his God.

The Men Are Punished

24Then the king ordered the men who had told lies about Daniel to be brought. They were thrown into the pit with lions, along with their children and wives. Before they reached the bottom, the lions grabbed them and crushed all their bones.

The King Praises God

25Then King Darius wrote to all the people, nations, and languages in the land: "May your peace and plenty grow! 26I order that in every part of my kingdom, people must fear and respect the God of Daniel. He is the living God. He lives forever. His kingdom will never be destroyed. His power will never end. 27He saves and rescues. He does amazing things in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth. He saved Daniel from the power of the lions."

Daniel Does Well

28So Daniel did well during the rule of Darius and during the rule of Cyrus the Persian.
