David Runs to Jonathan

1David ran away from Naioth in Ramah. He came to Jonathan. He said, "What did I do? What is my wrong? What sin did I do against your father? Why does he want to kill me?"

2Jonathan said, "No! You will not die. My father does not do big things or small things without telling me. Why would he hide this from me? He would not."

3David said again, "YouryourYour father knows I found favourfavourfavor in your eyes. He said, 'Do not let Jonathan know this, or he will be sad.' But as the LORD lives, and as you live, I am only one step away from death."

4Jonathan said to David, "What do you want me to do? I will do it."

David and Jonathan Make a Plan

5David said to Jonathan, "Tomorrow is the new moon. I should sit with the king to eat. Let me hide in the field until evening on the third day. 6If your father asks about me, say, 'David asked me to let him run to Bethlehem, his city. He has a family feast there for all his family.' 7If he says, 'That is good,' then I am safe. But if he gets angry, you will know he wants to hurt me. 8Please be kind to me. You brought me into a promise with you before the LORD. If I have done wrong, kill me yourself. Why take me to your father?"

9Jonathan said, "No! If I knew my father planned to hurt you, I would tell you."

10David said, "Who will tell me if your father answers you harshly?"

11Jonathan said, "Come, let us go out to the field." So they both went out to the field.

12Jonathan said to David, "I promise by the LORD, the God of Israel. Tomorrow about this time, I will find out what my father thinks. If he is kind to David, I will send word to you. 13But if my father wants to hurt you, may the LORD punish Jonathan. I will tell you and send you away in peace. May the LORD be with you, as HeheHe was with my father. 14While I am still alive, show me the LORD's kindness, so I will not die. 15And do not stop being kind to my family forever, not even when the LORD removes all of David's enemies from the earth. 16So I make this promise with David's family. May the LORD hold David's enemies responsible."

17Jonathan made David promise again because he loved him. He loved him as much as his own life.

18Jonathan said, "Tomorrow is the new moon. You will be missed, because your seat will be empty. 19On the third day, go down quickly to the place where you hid before. Stay by the stone Ezel. 20I will shoot three arrows off to the side, as if I am shooting at a target. 21Then I will send a boy and say, 'Go find the arrows.' If I say to the boy, 'The arrows are on this side of you; pick them up,' then come. It is safe. I promise by the LORD, you are safe, and there is no danger. 22But if I say to the boy, 'Look, the arrows are beyond you,' then go. The LORD has sent you away. 23As for the thing we talked about, the LORD is between you and me forever."

David Hides in the Field

24So David hid in the field. When the new moon came, the king sat down to eat.

25The king sat in his usual place by the wall. Jonathan stood up. Abner sat by Saul. But David's place was empty.

26That day Saul said nothing. He thought something had happened and David was not clean.

Saul Asks About David

27The next day, the second day of the month, David's place was still empty. Saul said to Jonathan, "Why has the son of Jesse not come to eat yesterday or today?"

28Jonathan answered Saul, "David asked me to let him go to Bethlehem. 29He said, 'Please let me go. My family has a feast in the city. My brother told me to come. Now, if I have found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, please let me go see my brothers.' That is why he has not come to the king's table."

Saul Gets Angry at Jonathan

30Then Saul got very angry with Jonathan. He said, "YouyouYou are a stubborn, rebellious son! I know you have chosen the son of Jesse. You bring shame to yourself and to your mother! 31As long as the son of Jesse lives on the earth, you and your kingdom will not last. Now send and bring him to me, for he must die."

32Jonathan answered his father Saul, "Why should he be put to death? What has he done?"

33Then Saul threw his spear at Jonathan to hit him. Jonathan knew his father really wanted to kill David.

34Jonathan got up from the table in great anger. He did not eat on the second day of the month. He was sad because his father had shamed him about David.

Jonathan Sends the Signal

35In the morning, Jonathan went out to the field to meet David. A small boy was with him.

36He said to his boy, "Run and find the arrows I shoot." The boy ran, and Jonathan shot an arrow past him.

37When the boy came to the place where Jonathan's arrow had fallen, Jonathan called, "ThetheThe arrow is beyond you!" 38Jonathan called again, "Hurry! Do not stop!" The boy picked up the arrow and brought it to his master.

39The boy did not know what it meant. But Jonathan and David knew.

40Jonathan gave his things to the boy and said, "Take them to the city."

David and Jonathan Say Goodbye

41As soon as the boy left, David got up from the south side. He fell on his face to the ground and bowed three times. The two men kissed each other and cried. David cried the most.

42Jonathan said to David, "Go in peace. We swore in the LORD's name that the LORD will be between me and you, and between our children forever." Then David left. Jonathan went back to the city.
