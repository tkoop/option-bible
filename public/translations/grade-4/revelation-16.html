God Sends the Seven Angels

1I heard a loud voice from the temple. It said to the seven angels, "Go and pour out the seven bowls of God's anger on the earth."

The First Bowl

2The first angel went and poured his bowl on the earth. Painful, bad sores came on the people who had the beast's mark and who worshiped its picture.

The Second Bowl

3The second angel poured his bowl into the sea. The sea turned to blood, like the blood of a dead person. Every living thing in the sea died.

The Third Bowl

4The third angel poured his bowl into the rivers and springs. They turned to blood.
