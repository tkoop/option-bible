Esau's Family Line

1These are the family lines of Esau. Esau is also called Edom. 2Esau married women from Canaan. His wives were Adah, the daughter of Elon the Hittite; Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite; 3and Basemath, the daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth. 4Adah had a son named Eliphaz for Esau. Basemath had a son named Reuel. 5Oholibamah had three sons: Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These sons of Esau were born in Canaan.

Esau Moves Away

6Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, and everyone in his house. He took his animals and all he had gained in Canaan. Then he went to another land away from his brother Jacob. 7They had too many things to live close together. The land where they stayed could not hold them all because of their animals. 8So Esau, who is also called Edom, settled in the hill country of Seir.

Esau's Sons and Leaders

9These are the family lines of Esau, the father of Edom, in the hill country of Seir. 10These are the names of Esau's sons: Eliphaz, the son of Esau and Adah; and Reuel, the son of Esau and Basemath. 11The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz. 12Timna lived with Eliphaz, Esau's son. She had a son named Amalek. These grandsons came through Adah, Esau's wife. 13The sons of Reuel were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These sons came from Basemath, Esau's wife. 14The sons of Oholibamah, Esau's wife and daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, were Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

15These were the leaders among Esau's sons. From Eliphaz, Esau's firstborn son, came leaders named Teman, Omar, Zepho, and Kenaz. 16Leaders named Korah, Gatam, and Amalek also came from Eliphaz. These leaders of Eliphaz lived in Edom. They came from Adah, Esau's wife. 17From Reuel came leaders named Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These leaders of Reuel lived in Edom. They came from Basemath, Esau's wife. 18From Oholibamah, Esau's wife, came leaders named Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. 19These were the sons of Esau, who is also called Edom, and these were their leaders.

The Family of Seir

20These were the sons of Seir the Horite, who lived in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 21Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These were the leaders of the Horites. They were sons of Seir in Edom. 22The sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam. Lotan's sister was Timna. 23The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. 24The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah. This Anah found hot springs in the desert while he was taking care of his father Zibeon's donkeys. 25The children of Anah were Dishon and Oholibamah, Anah's daughter. 26The sons of Dishon were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. 27The sons of Ezer were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. 28The sons of Dishan were Uz and Aran.

29These were the leaders of the Horites: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 30Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These leaders of the Horites led in Seir.

Kings of Edom

31These were the kings who ruled Edom before Israel had a king. 32Bela son of Beor ruled Edom. His city was Dinhabah. 33When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah ruled after him. 34When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites ruled after him. 35When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad ruled after him. Hadad defeated Midian in the field of Moab. His city was Avith. 36When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah ruled after him. 37When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the river ruled after him. 38When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor ruled after him. 39When Baal-hanan died, Hadar ruled after him. His city was Pau. His wife was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred and granddaughter of Mezahab.

Leaders of Edom

40These were the names of Esau's leaders, by their families and places: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, 41Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, 42Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, 43Magdiel, and Iram. These leaders of Edom lived in the places they owned. He is Esau, the father of Edom.
