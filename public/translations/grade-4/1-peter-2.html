Live Good Lives Among Other People

Obey Human Leaders

13For the Lord's sake, obey every human rule. Obey the king, because he is the top ruler.

15God wants you to do good. Then you will stop foolish people from saying bad things, because they do not know.

16Live like free people. But do not use your freedom to cover up bad things. Live like servants of God.

Servants and Hard Times

18Servants, obey your masters with all respect. Obey not only the good and kind ones, but also the mean ones.

19It is a good thing if someone suffers pain for doing right, because he wants to please God.

20What good is it if you are hit for doing wrong and you take it? But if you suffer for doing good and take it, that is a good thing in God's eyes.
