Abraham's Other Family

1Abraham married another woman named Keturah. 2She had Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah for him. 3Jokshan had Sheba and Dedan. Dedan's sons were Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim. 4Midian's sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were Keturah's sons.

5Abraham gave everything he owned to Isaac. 6While he was still alive, Abraham gave gifts to his other sons. He sent them east, away from Isaac, to the east country.

Abraham Dies

7Abraham lived 175 years. 8He died old and full of years. He had lived a long, good life. Then he died and went to rest with his people.

9His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah. It was in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, east of Mamre. 10Abraham had bought that field from the Hittites. Sarah, his wife, was buried there too.

11After Abraham died, God blessed Isaac. Isaac lived near Beer Lahai Roi.

Ishmael's Family

12These are the children of Ishmael, Abraham's son. Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's servant, had Ishmael for Abraham. 13These are the names of Ishmael's sons, by their family groups: Nebaioth, the firstborn, then Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam. 14Also Mishma, Dumah, and Massa. 15Also Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. 16These were Ishmael's sons. They had towns and camps. There were twelve leaders over their tribes.

17Ishmael lived 137 years. Then he died and was gathered to his people. 18His family lived from Havilah to Shur, east of Egypt, toward Asshur. They settled near all his brothers.

Isaac's Family

19These are the children of Isaac, Abraham's son. Abraham was Isaac's father. 20Isaac was 40 years old when he married Rebekah. She was the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan Aram. She was also the sister of Laban the Aramean. 21Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife, because she could not have children. The LORD answered Isaac, and Rebekah became pregnant.

22The babies fought inside her. She said, "If this is how it is, why am I here?" So she went to ask the LORD.

23The LORD said to her, "Two nations are in your womb. Two groups of people will come from your body. One will be stronger than the other. The older will serve the younger."

Jacob and Esau Are Born

24When her time came, she had twins. 25The first baby came out red and hairy all over, like a coat. They named him Esau. 26Then his brother came out. His hand held Esau's heel. They named him Jacob. Isaac was 60 years old when they were born.

27The boys grew up. Esau became a skilled hunter and a man of the field. Jacob was a quiet man who stayed in the tents. 28Isaac loved Esau, because Esau brought him food from hunting. Rebekah loved Jacob.

29One day Jacob was cooking stew. Esau came in from the field. He was very tired. 30Esau said to Jacob, "Let me eat some of that red stew. I am very tired." That is why he was called Edom.

31Jacob said, "Sell me your right to be the first son today."

32Esau said, "I am about to die. What good is that right to me?"

33Jacob said, "Promise me today." Esau promised. So he sold his first son's right to Jacob. 34Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. Esau ate and drank. Then he got up and left. In that way, Esau did not care about his first son's right.
