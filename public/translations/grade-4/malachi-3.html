The Messenger Is Coming

1"Look, I am sending MymyMy messenger. He will clear the way in front of MemeMe. Then the Lord you look for will suddenly come to HishisHis temple. The messenger of the promise you want is coming," says the the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD Will Make His People Pure

2"But who can live through the day HeheHe comes? Who can stand when HeheHe shows HimselfhimselfHimself? He is like a fire that cleans metal. He is like soap that washes clothes. 3He will sit like one who cleans silver and makes it pure. He will make Levi's sons pure. He will clean them like gold and silver. Then they will bring gifts to the the LORD in the right way. 4Then the gifts from Judah and Jerusalem will please the the LORD. They will be like they were long ago."

The LORD Will Judge

5"Then I will come close to you to judge you. I will quickly speak against people who do magic, people who are not faithful in marriage, people who lie under oath, people who are mean to widows and children with no father, people who do not pay workers, and people who treat strangers unfairly. They do not fear MemeMe," says the the LORD of Hosts. 6"For I, the the LORD, do not change. That is why you, Jacob's children, are not destroyed."

Come Back to the LORD

7"For a long time, you have turned away from MymyMy rules. You have not obeyed them. Come back to MemeMe, and I will come back to you," says the the LORD of Hosts. "But you say, 'How can we come back?' 8Can a person steal from God? But you are stealing from MemeMe. But you ask, 'How are we stealing from YouyouYou?' YouyouYou are stealing from MemeMe when you do not give your tithes and gifts. 9So you are cursed, because all of you are robbing MemeMe. 10Bring all the tithe into the storehouse, so there will be food in MymyMy house. Test MemeMe in this, says the the LORD of Hosts. I will open the sky for you and pour out so much blessing that you will not have room enough for it. 11I will stop the pests that eat your crops. They will not destroy the food your land grows. Your grapevines in the field will not lose their fruit, says the the LORD of Hosts. 12Then all the nations will say you are blessed, because your land will be a land people love, says the the LORD of Hosts."

People Complain to God

13"Your words have been mean things against MemeMe," says the the LORD. "Yet you say, 'What have we said against YouyouYou?' 14YouyouYou have said, 'It is useless to serve God. What good is it to obey HishisHis rules? Why should we act sad before the the LORD of Hosts? 15So now we call proud people blessed. People who do wrong get rich. They even test God and get away with it.'"

The LORD Remembers His People

16Then the people who feared the the LORD talked with each other. The the LORD listened and heard them. A book to remember them was written in front of HimhimHim. It was for the people who feared the the LORD and honouredhonouredhonored HishisHis name.

17"They will be MinemineMine," says the the LORD of Hosts, "on the day I do this. They will be MymyMy special treasure. I will be kind to them, like a father is kind to his son who serves him. 18Then you will see again the difference between good people and bad people, between people who serve God and people who do not."
