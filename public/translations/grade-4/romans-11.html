God Has Not Rejected His People

1So what do I say? Did God push HishisHis people away? No way! I am an Israelite too. I come from Abraham's family and from Benjamin's tribe. 2God did not push away the people HeheHe knew before. Don't you know what the Bible says about Elijah? He prayed to God against Israel.

3He said, "Lord, they killed YouryourYour prophets and tore down YouryourYour altars. I am the only one left, and they want to kill me."

4But what did God say? He said, "I kept seven thousand men for MyselfmyselfMyself. They did not bow to Baal."

5So it is now too. A small group is saved by grace. 6If it is by grace, it is not by works. If it were by works, grace would not be grace.

7What then? Israel did not find what it wanted. But the chosen ones did. The rest became stubborn. 8The Bible says God gave them a spirit of deep sleep. Their eyes cannot see, and their ears cannot hear. This is still true today. David says:

9"May their table be a trap and a snare. May it make them fall and pay them back. 10May their eyes be dark so they cannot see. May their backs be bent forever."

Israel's Fall Brings Help to Others

11Did they trip just to fall? No! Because they sinned, salvation came to people who are not Jews. This was to make Israel jealous. 12If their sin brought riches to the world, and their loss brought riches to people who are not Jews, think how much more their full return will bring!

13I am talking to you who are not Jews. I am an apostle to people who are not Jews. I honourhonourhonor this work. 14I hope to make my own people jealous and save some of them.

15If God turning them away made the world right with HimhimHim, what will bringing them back be? It will be life from the dead. 16If the first part of the dough is holy, all of it is holy. If the root is holy, the branches are holy too.

A Warning to New Believers

17Some branches were broken off. You are like a wild olive branch. You were joined to them. Now you share the rich root of the olive tree. 18Don't brag about the branches. If you brag, remember this: you do not hold up the root. The root holds you up.

19You may say, "Branches were broken off so I could be joined." That is true. 20They were broken off because they did not believe. You stand because you believe. Don't be proud. Be afraid. 21If God did not spare the natural branches, HeheHe may not spare you either.

22See how kind and how strict God is. He is strict with those who fell. He is kind to you, if you keep in HishisHis kindness. If not, you too will be cut off. 23And if they stop refusing to believe, they will be joined in again. God can join them in again. 24If you were cut from a wild olive tree and joined to a good olive tree, even though that is not natural, how much more can these natural branches be joined to their own tree!

God's Plan for All Israel

25Brothers, I don't want you to miss this secret. Then you won't think you are so smart. Part of Israel has become stubborn until all the people who are not Jews have come to God. 26And then all Israel will be saved. The Bible says, "ThetheThe OneoneOne who saves will come from Zion. He will turn Israel from sin. 27This is MymyMy promise to them. I will take away their sins."

28As far as the good news goes, they are enemies for your sake. But as far as God's choice goes, they are loved because of the fathers. 29God does not take back HishisHis gifts or HishisHis call.

30Just as you once disobeyed God but now have HishisHis mercy because they disobeyed, 31so now they have disobeyed. This is so they can get mercy through the mercy shown to you. 32God put everyone in the same place of not obeying, so HeheHe can have mercy on everyone.

Who has been HishisHis helper? 35Who gave HimhimHim something first, so HeheHe has to pay it back? 36Everything comes from HimhimHim, through HimhimHim, and to HimhimHim. Glory to HimhimHim forever!
