Leaders in the Church at Antioch

1In the church at Antioch, there were prophets and teachers. They were Barnabas, Simeon (called Niger), Lucius from Cyrene, Manaen (who grew up with Herod the ruler), and Saul.

On the Island of Cyprus

4So the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit sent them out. They went down to Seleucia. Then they sailed to Cyprus. 5When they came to Salamis, they told God's message in the Jewish meeting places. John helped them. 6They went all over the island to Paphos. There they met a Jewish man who used magic. He was a false prophet named Bar-Jesus. 7He was with Sergius Paulus, the Roman leader. Sergius was a wise man. He sent for Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear God's message. 8But Elymas the magician (that is what his name means) tried to stop them. He wanted to turn the Roman leader away from believing. 9But Saul, also called Paul, was full of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit. He looked hard at Elymas. 10Paul said, “You son of the devil! He walked around looking for someone to lead him by the hand. 12The Roman leader believed when he saw what happened. He was amazed at the Lord's teaching.

Paul and Barnabas Go to Antioch in Pisidia

Some Believe, but Others Reject the Message

42As Paul and Barnabas left, the people asked them to say these things again the next Sabbath. 43When the meeting ended, many Jews and people who worshiped God followed Paul and Barnabas. Paul and Barnabas talked with them and urged them to keep trusting God's grace. 44The next Sabbath, almost the whole city came to hear the Lord's message. 45But when the Jews saw the crowds, they became jealous. They argued against Paul and said bad things about him.

46Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly: “God's message had to be told to you first. But you push it away. You show you are not worthy of eternal life. So we are going to the people who are not Jews. 47The Lord commanded us, ‘I have made you a light for the nations, so you can bring salvation to the ends of the earth.’”

48When the non-Jewish people heard this, they were glad. They praised the Lord's message. All who were chosen for eternal life believed. 49The Lord's message spread through the whole area. 50But the Jews stirred up the respected women and the city leaders. They started trouble against Paul and Barnabas and made them leave the area. 51Paul and Barnabas shook the dust off their feet to show they were done with that place. Then they went to Iconium. 52The believers were full of joy and the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit.
