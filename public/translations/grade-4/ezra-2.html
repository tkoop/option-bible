The People Who Came Back from Babylon

1These are the people from the land who came back from being held captive in Babylon. King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon had carried them away. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah. Each person went to his own town. 2They came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispar, Bigvai, Rehum, and Baanah. This is the list of the men of Israel.

Families Who Returned

3The family of Parosh had 2,172 people. 4The family of Shephatiah had 372 people. 5The family of Arah had 775 people. 6The family of Pahath-moab, from the sons of Jeshua and Joab, had 2,812 people. 7The family of Elam had 1,254 people. 8The family of Zattu had 945 people. 9The family of Zaccai had 760 people. 10The family of Bani had 642 people.

11The family of Bebai had 623 people. 12The family of Azgad had 1,222 people. 13The family of Adonikam had 666 people. 14The family of Bigvai had 2,056 people. 15The family of Adin had 454 people. 16The family of Ater, from Hezekiah, had 98 people. 17The family of Bezai had 323 people. 18The family of Jorah had 112 people. 19The family of Hashum had 223 people. 20The family of Gibbar had 95 people.

21Bethlehem had 123 people. 22Netophah had 56 people. 23Anathoth had 128 people. 24Azmaveth had 42 people. 25Kiriath Jearim, Chephirah, and Beeroth had 743 people. 26Ramah and Geba had 621 people. 27Michmas had 122 people. 28Bethel and Ai had 223 people. 29The family of Nebo had 52 people.

30The family of Magbish had 156 people. 31The other family of Elam had 1,254 people. 32The family of Harim had 320 people. 33Lod, Hadid, and Ono had 725 people. 34Jericho had 345 people. 35Senaah had 3,630 people.

Priests

36The priests: the family of Jedaiah, from the house of Jeshua, had 973 people. 37The family of Immer had 1,052 people. 38The family of Pashhur had 1,247 people. 39The family of Harim had 1,017 people.

Levites, Singers, and Gatekeepers

40The Levites: the families of Jeshua and Kadmiel, from the family of Hodaviah, had 74 people. 41The singers: the family of Asaph had 128 people. 42The gatekeepers: the families of Shallum, Ater, Talmon, Akkub, Hatita, and Shobai had 139 people in all.

Temple Servants

43The temple servants included the families of Ziha, Hasupha, and Tabbaoth. 44The families of Keros, Siaha, and Padon. 45The families of Lebanah, Hagabah, and Akkub. 46The families of Hagab, Shamlai, and Hanan. 47The families of Giddel, Gahar, and Reaiah. 48The families of Rezin, Nekoda, and Gazzam. 49The families of Uzza, Paseah, and Besai. 50The families of Asnah, Meunim, and Nephusim. 51The families of Bakbuk, Hakupha, and Harhur. 52The families of Bazluth, Mehida, and Harsha. 53The families of Barkos, Sisera, and Temah. 54The families of Neziah and Hatipha.

55The sons of Solomon’s servants included the families of Sotai, Hassophereth, and Peruda. 56The families of Jaalah, Darkon, and Giddel. 57The families of Shephatiah, Hattil, Pochereth-hazzebaim, and Ami. 58The temple servants and the sons of Solomon’s servants had 392 people in all.

Those Who Could Not Prove Their Family

59These people came up from Tel Melah, Tel Harsha, Cherub, Addan, and Immer. They could not show their family or family line, or prove they were from Israel. 60The families of Delaiah, Tobiah, and Nekoda had 652 people. 61Among the priests were the families of Habaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai. Barzillai had married a daughter of Barzillai the Gileadite, so he was called by that name. 62They looked for their family records but could not find them. So they were not allowed to serve as priests. 63The governor told them not to eat the most holy food until a priest could use the Urim and Thummim.

The Whole Group

64The whole group had 42,360 people. 65Besides these, there were 7,337 male and female servants and 200 male and female singers. 66They had 736 horses, 245 mules, 67435 camels, and 6,720 donkeys.

Gifts for God’s House

68When they came to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, some family leaders gave freely to build God’s house again in its place. 69They gave as much as they could: 61,000 gold coins, 5,000 pieces of silver, and 100 priest clothes.

They Settle in Their Towns

70The priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, and some of the people settled in their towns. All Israel lived in their towns.
