David Asks God to Judge Him

1Judge me, O the LORD, because I have lived in an honest way. I trusted in the LORD and did not turn away. 2Test me, O the LORD, and try me. Look deep into my heart and mind. 3Your love is always in front of me, and I have lived by YouryourYour truth.

David Stays Away from Bad People

4I have not sat with people who lie, and I do not go with people who pretend to be good. 5I hate groups of bad people. I will not sit with evil people.

David Worships God

6I wash my hands to show I am clean. I walk around YouryourYour altar, O the LORD. 7I speak out with thanks. I tell all the amazing things YouyouYou do. 8O the LORD, I love YouryourYour house. It is where YouyouYou live and where YouryourYour glory is.

David Asks God to Save Him

9Do not take me away with people who do wrong. Do not take my life with people who hurt others. 10They use their hands for bad plans. Their right hands are full of money to cheat. 11But I will live in an honest way. Save me and be kind to me. 12My feet stand on flat ground. In the big group I will praise the the LORD.
