Sing to God with Joy

1Sing out loud to God. He is our strength. Shout to the God of Jacob. 2Start the song. Play the tambourine. Play the sweet harp. 3Blow the trumpet on the new moon. Blow it on the full moon. Blow it on our feast day. 4This is a rule for Israel. It is a law from the God of Jacob.

God Helped His People

5He made this rule for Joseph. Joseph went out from Egypt. I heard a voice I did not know:

6"I took the heavy load from his shoulders. His hands were free from the baskets. 7You called to MemeMe when you were in trouble. I saved you. I answered you from a hidden thundercloud. I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

8"Hear MemeMe, MymyMy people. I will warn you. Israel, if you would listen to MemeMe! 9Do not have any strange god among you. Do not bow down to a god from another land. 10I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt. Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it. 11But MymyMy people did not listen to MymyMy voice. Israel did not want MemeMe. 12So I let them go their own way. They followed their own plans. 13If only MymyMy people would listen to MemeMe! Israel would walk in MymyMy ways. 14I would quickly beat down their enemies. I would turn MymyMy hand against their enemies. 15Those who hate the LORD would bow low before HimhimHim. Their trouble would last forever. 16I would feed them the best wheat. I would fill you with honey from the rock."
