Solomon Becomes King

1Solomon was David's son. He grew strong as king. The LORD his God was with him. He made Solomon very great. 2Solomon spoke to all Israel. He spoke to leaders of thousands and hundreds. He spoke to judges. He spoke to every leader in Israel. He spoke to the heads of the family groups.

3Solomon and all the people went to the high place at Gibeon. They went there because God's tent of meeting was there. Moses, the LORD's servant, had made it in the desert. 4David had already brought God's holy box from Kiriath-jearim. He put it in the place he made in Jerusalem. He had set up a tent for it there. 5The bronze altar was there too. Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, had made it. It stood in front of the LORD's tent. Solomon and the people went there to worship the LORD. 6Solomon went up to the LORD at the bronze altar. It was by the tent of meeting. He gave 1,000 burnt offerings on it.

Solomon's Wealth

14Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He kept them in chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem. 15The king made silver and gold as common as stones in Jerusalem. He made cedar wood as common as sycamore trees in the lowlands. 16Solomon's horses came from Egypt and Kue. The king's traders bought them from Kue for a price. 17They brought a chariot from Egypt for 600 pieces of silver. They brought a horse for 150 pieces of silver. They did the same for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram. Their hands brought them out.
