The Tribes of Reuben and Gad Ask for Land

1The people of Reuben and Gad had many animals. They saw the land of Jazer and Gilead. That place was good for animals. 2They came to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the people.

3They said, "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon. 4The land the LORD helped Israel take is good for animals. We, your servants, have animals. 5If you are pleased with us, please give this land to us, your servants. Do not make us cross the Jordan River."

Moses Warns Them

6Moses said to the people of Gad and Reuben, "WillwillWill your brothers go to war while you stay here? 7Why do you make the people of Israel not want to go to the land the LORD gave them? 8Your fathers did this when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to see the land. 9They went up to the ValleyvalleyValley of Eshcol and saw the land. Then they made the people of Israel not want to go to the land the LORD gave them. 10That day the LORD was very angry. He made a promise. 11He said, 'The men twenty years old and older who came out of Egypt will not see the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. They did not follow MemeMe all the way.' 12Only Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun will see it, because they followed the LORD all the way. 13The LORD was angry with Israel. He made them walk in the desert for forty years, until all the people who did evil in the LORD's eyes were gone. 14Now you, a new group of sinful men, have taken your fathers' place. You are making the LORD even more angry with Israel. 15If you turn away from following HimhimHim, HeheHe will leave them in the desert again. You will destroy all these people."

They Make a Promise

16The men came near and said, "We will build sheep pens here for our animals and cities for our children. 17But we will get ready and go in front of Israel's people. We will help them until they reach their land. Our children will stay in the safe cities, away from the people who live there. 18We will not go back to our homes until every Israelite gets his land. 19We will not share their land on the other side of the Jordan. Our land is here, east of the Jordan."

Moses Agrees

20Moses said to them, "If you do this, if you get ready to fight for the LORD, 21and every soldier crosses the Jordan in front of the LORD until HeheHe drives out HishisHis enemies, 22and the land is taken for the LORD, then you may go back. You will be free from blame before the LORD and Israel. This land will be yours to keep before the LORD. 23But if you do not do this, you have sinned against the LORD. Know that your sin will find you. 24Build cities for your children and pens for your sheep. Do what you promised."

25The people of Gad and Reuben said to Moses, "We, your servants, will do what you say. 26Our children, our wives, and all our animals will stay in the cities of Gilead. 27But your servants will cross over, ready for battle, to fight for the LORD, just as you said."

28Moses told this to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the family leaders of Israel. 29Moses said, "If the sons of Gad and Reuben cross the Jordan with you, ready for battle for the LORD, and the land is won for you, then give them Gilead to keep. 30But if they do not cross over ready for battle, they must live with you in Canaan."

31The people of Gad and Reuben answered, "We will do what the LORD has said. 32We will cross into Canaan ready for battle for the LORD. Our land will stay here east of the Jordan."

Cities Built East of the Jordan

33Moses gave them the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and Og king of Bashan. He gave them the whole land with its cities and borders. He gave it to Gad, Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph.

34The people of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 35Atroth Shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 36Beth Nimrah, and Beth Haran. These were strong cities and sheep pens. 37The people of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim, 38Nebo, Baal Meon (they changed its name), and Sibmah. They gave new names to the cities they built.

39Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead and took it. He drove out the Amorites who were there. 40Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he lived there. 41Jair son of Manasseh went and took their towns. He called them Havvoth Jair. 42Nobah went and took Kenath and its towns. He called it Nobah after himself.
