The Sheep Gate

1Eliashib the high priest and the other priests got to work. They rebuilt the SheepsheepSheep GategateGate. They made it holy. They put in its doors. They built up to the TowertowerTower of the Hundred. They made that tower holy too. They built up to the TowertowerTower of Hananel.

2The men of Jericho built the next part. Zakkur son of Imri built next to them.

The Fish Gate

3The sons of Hassenaah rebuilt the FishfishFish GategateGate. They put in its beams, doors, locks, and bars.

4Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, fixed the wall next to them. Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, fixed it next to him. Zadok son of Baana fixed it next to him.

5The men of Tekoa fixed the wall next to them. But their leaders would not help with the work.

The Old Gate

6Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah fixed the Old GategateGate. They put in its beams, doors, locks, and bars.

7Men from Gibeon and Mizpah fixed the wall next to them. Melatiah the Gibeonite and Jadon the Meronothite were among them. They worked for the governor of the land across the Euphrates River.

8Next to them, Uzziel son of Harhaiah, a goldsmith, fixed the wall. Next to him, Hananiah, a perfume maker, fixed it. They rebuilt Jerusalem up to the BroadbroadBroad Wall.

9Next to them, Rephaiah son of Hur fixed the wall. He was the leader of half of an area of Jerusalem.

10Next to them, Jedaiah son of Harumaph fixed the wall across from his house. Next to him, Hattush son of Hashabneiah fixed it.

11Malkijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab fixed another part. They also fixed the TowertowerTower of the OvensovensOvens.

12Next to them, Shallum son of Hallohesh fixed the wall. He was the leader of the other half of an area of Jerusalem. His daughters helped him.

The Valley and Dung Gates

13Hanun and the people of Zanoah fixed the ValleyvalleyValley GategateGate. They rebuilt it. They put in its doors, locks, and bars. They also fixed about 1,500 feet of wall up to the DungdungDung GategateGate.

14Malkijah son of Rechab fixed the DungdungDung GategateGate. He was the leader of the area of Beth-haccerem. He rebuilt it. He put in its doors, locks, and bars.

The Fountain Gate

15Shallun son of Col-hozeh fixed the FountainfountainFountain GategateGate. He was the leader of the area of Mizpah. He rebuilt it. He put a roof on it. He put in its doors, locks, and bars. He also fixed the wall by the PoolpoolPool of Siloam, by the king’s garden, up to the stairs that go down from David’s city.

16After him, Nehemiah son of Azbuk fixed the wall. He was the leader of half of an area of Beth-zur. He fixed it up to a spot across from David’s tombs. He fixed it up to the man-made pool and the House of the Warriors.

17Next to him, the Levites fixed the wall under Rehum son of Bani. Next to him, Hashabiah fixed it. He was the leader of half of the area of Keilah. He fixed it for his area.

18Next to him, the other Levites fixed the wall under Binnui son of Henadad. He was the leader of the other half of the area of Keilah.

19Next to him, Ezer son of Jeshua fixed another part. He was the leader of Mizpah. He fixed it from a spot across from the road up to the weapons house to the corner.

20Next to him, Baruch son of Zabbai worked hard. He fixed another part. He fixed it from the corner to the door of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

21Next to him, Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, fixed another part. He fixed it from the door of Eliashib’s house to the end of it.

22Next to him, the priests from the nearby area fixed the wall.

23Next to them, Benjamin and Hasshub fixed the wall in front of their house. Next to them, Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, fixed it next to his house.

24Next to him, Binnui son of Henadad fixed another part. He fixed it from Azariah’s house to the corner.

25Palal son of Uzai fixed the wall across from the corner and the tower that sticks out from the king’s upper palace. This was by the guard’s courtyard. Next to him, Pedaiah son of Parosh fixed the wall.

26The temple servants living on the hill called Ophel fixed the wall up to a spot across from the Water GategateGate to the east and the tower that sticks out.

27Next to them, the men of Tekoa fixed another part. They fixed it from the big tower that sticks out to the wall of the hill called Ophel.

The Horse Gate

28Above the Horse GategateGate, the priests fixed the wall. Each one fixed it in front of his own house.

29Next to them, Zadok son of Immer fixed the wall across from his house. Next to him, Shemaiah son of Shecaniah fixed it. He was the guard at the East GategateGate.

30Next to him, Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, fixed another part. Next to them, Meshullam son of Berechiah fixed the wall across from his room.

Finishing the Wall

31Next to him, Malkijah, a goldsmith, fixed the wall up to the house of the temple servants and the merchants. This was across from the InspectioninspectionInspection GategateGate and up to the upper room at the corner.

32Between the upper room at the corner and the SheepsheepSheep GategateGate, the goldsmiths and the merchants fixed the wall.
