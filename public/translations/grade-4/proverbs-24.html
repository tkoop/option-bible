Do Not Want to Be Like Bad People

1Do not be jealous of bad people. Do not want to be with them. 2Their hearts think about hurting others. Their lips talk about trouble.

Build Your Home with Wisdom

3By wisdom a house is built. By understanding it is made strong. 4By knowledge the rooms are filled with things that are special and nice.

Be Strong and Wise

5A wise man is strong. A man who knows things grows stronger. 6By wise plans you fight a war. You win when you have many helpers who give advice.

Foolish Plans Are Bad

7Wisdom is too hard for a fool to understand. He does not speak at the gate. 8A person who plans to do bad will be called a troublemaker. 9Foolish plans are sin. People do not like a person who makes fun of others.

Be Strong and Help Others

10If you are weak in trouble, your strength is small. 11Save those who are being led to die. Hold back those who are stumbling toward being killed. 12If you say, "We did not know," doesn't HeheHe know? He keeps your soul. He pays back each person for what he does.

Eat Honey and Learn Wisdom

13Eat honey, my son, because it is good. Sweet honey tastes good in your mouth. 14Learn wisdom for your soul. If you find it, you will have a future. Your hope will not end.

Do Not Hurt Good People

15Do not set a trap, you wicked person, against the home of a good person. Do not ruin his resting place. 16A good person may fall seven times and get up. But wicked people stumble into trouble.

Do Not Be Happy When an Enemy Falls

17Do not be glad when your enemy falls. Do not let your heart be happy when he stumbles. 18The LORD may see it and be unhappy. He may turn HishisHis anger away from him.

Do Not Worry About Bad People

19Do not be upset because of people who do bad. Do not be jealous of wicked people. 20A bad person has no future. The lamp of wicked people goes out.

Fear the LORD and the King

21My son, fear the LORD and the king. Do not join people who cause trouble. 22Their trouble comes suddenly. Who knows what trouble will come to them?

Be Fair in Court

23These are also sayings for wise people: It is not good to be unfair in court. 24Whoever says to a bad person, "YouyouYou are good," will be cursed by people. Nations will hate him. 25But those who correct bad people will be happy. A good blessing will come on them. 26An honest answer is like a kiss on the lips.

Finish Your Work First

27Get your work ready outside. Get your field ready. Then build your house.

Do Not Lie About Others

28Do not be a false witness against your neighbourneighbourneighbor. Do not lie with your lips. 29Do not say, "I will do to him what he did to me. I will pay back the man for what he did."

Learn from the Lazy Man

30I walked past the field of a lazy man and the vineyard of a man with no sense. 31Thorns had grown all over it. Weeds covered its face. Its stone wall was broken down. 32I saw it and thought about it. I learned a lesson from what I saw. 33A little sleep, a little rest, a little folding of the hands, 34and being poor will come on you like a robber. You will be in need like a man with a weapon.
