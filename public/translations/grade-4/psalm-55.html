David Asks God for Help

1God, please listen to my prayer. Do not hide from me when I ask for help. 2Pay attention to me and answer me. I am upset, and I groan. 3I hear my enemy. Wicked people hurt me. They bring trouble on me, and they hate me in anger. 4My heart pounds in me. I am scared I might die. 5I am afraid and I shake. A deep fear covers me.

6I said, “I wish I had wings like a dove. Then I would fly away and rest. 7I would go far away. I would stay in the desert. Selah. 8I would hurry to a safe place away from the wild wind and storm.”

9Lord, mix up their words. I see hurting and fighting in the city. 10Day and night they walk around its walls. Trouble and evil are inside it. 11The city is full of trouble. Lies and cheating are in its streets.

A Friend Turns Against David

12If an enemy made fun of me, I could take it. If someone who hates me came against me, I could hide from him. 13But it is you, a man like me. You are my friend, my close friend. 14We used to enjoy being together. We walked with the crowd to God's house. 15Let death grab them. Let them go down alive to the grave. Evil is in their homes and hearts.

16But I will call on God, and the LORD will save me. 17Morning, noon, and night, I will complain and cry out, and HeheHe will hear me. 18He saved my life and gave me peace from those who fought me, because many were against me. 19God will hear and bring them down. He has been king forever. They do not change, and they do not fear God. Selah.

20My friend has turned against his friends. He broke his promise. 21His words are smooth like butter, but he wants war in his heart. His words are soft like oil, but they are like sharp swords.

Trust in God

22Give your worries to the LORD. He will take care of you. He will never let good people fall. 23But YouyouYou, God, will bring them down to a deep pit. People who hurt others and lie will not live long. But I will trust in YouyouYou.
