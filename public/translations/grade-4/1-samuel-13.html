Saul Becomes King

1Saul was one year old when he became king. He ruled Israel for two years.

Saul Picks Soldiers

2Saul chose three thousand men from Israel. Two thousand stayed with Saul at Michmash and in the hills near Bethel. One thousand stayed with Jonathan in Gibeah in Benjamin. Saul sent the rest of the people home.

Jonathan Attacks the Philistines

3Jonathan attacked the Philistine camp at Geba. The Philistines heard about it. Saul blew a horn all over the land. He said, "Let the Hebrews hear this!"

4All Israel heard the news. They said, "Saul attacked a Philistine camp. Now the Philistines hate Israel!" Then the people were called to join Saul at Gilgal.

The Philistines Gather

5The Philistines gathered to fight Israel. They had thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and many soldiers. There were as many soldiers as sand on the seashore. They camped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.

6The men of Israel saw they were in trouble. Their army was weak. So they hid in caves, bushes, rocks, strong places, and holes in the ground.

7Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan River. They went to the land of Gad and Gilead. Saul stayed at Gilgal. All his soldiers were very scared.

8Saul waited seven days for the time Samuel set. Samuel did not come to Gilgal. The soldiers began to leave Saul.

Saul Offers a Sacrifice

9So Saul said, "BringbringBring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." Then he offered the burnt offering.

10Just as Saul finished the burnt offering, Samuel came. Saul went out to meet him and greet him.

11Samuel asked, "What have you done?"

"I saw the soldiers leaving me. You did not come at the time you promised. The Philistines were gathering at Michmash. 12I thought the Philistines would come down to fight me at Gilgal. I had not asked the LORD for help. So I felt I had to offer the burnt offering."

Samuel Rebukes Saul

13Samuel said, "YouyouYou acted foolishly. You did not obey the command the LORD your God gave you. If you had obeyed, the LORD would have kept your kingdom strong forever.

14But now your kingdom will not last. The LORD has found a man who wants to please HimhimHim. He has chosen that man to rule HishisHis people. This is because you did not obey the LORD's command."

Saul Counts His Men

15Then Samuel got up and left Gilgal. He went up to Gibeah in Benjamin. Saul counted the soldiers with him. There were about six hundred men.

16Saul, his son Jonathan, and the men with them stayed in Geba in Benjamin. The Philistines camped at Michmash.

The Philistines Send Raiders

17The Philistines sent out soldiers to attack. They came in three groups. One group went toward Ophrah in the land of Shual.

18Another group went toward Beth-horon. The third group went toward the border that looks over the ValleyvalleyValley of Zeboim, toward the desert.

Israel Has No Weapons

19There was no metal worker in all Israel. The Philistines said, "ThetheThe Hebrews must not make swords or spears."

20So all Israel went down to the Philistines. They went to sharpen their plows, hoes, axes, and sickles.

21They charged two-thirds of a shekel to sharpen plows, hoes, and forks. They charged one-third of a shekel to sharpen axes and fix goads.

22So on the day of battle, none of the soldiers with Saul and Jonathan had a sword or spear. Only Saul and Jonathan had them.

23A group of Philistines went out to the mountain pass at Michmash.
