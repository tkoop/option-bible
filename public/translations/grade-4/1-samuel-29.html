The Philistines Get Ready for War

1The Philistines brought all their armies to Aphek. The people of Israel camped by the spring in Jezreel.

2The Philistine leaders marched by hundreds and by thousands. David and his men marched last with Achish.

The Leaders Complain About David

3The Philistine leaders said, "What are these Hebrews doing here?" Achish said to the Philistine leaders, "This is David. He served Saul, the king of Israel. He has been with me for days or years. I have not found anything wrong with him since the day he came to me."

4The Philistine leaders were angry with Achish. They said, "Send the man back. Let him go to the place you gave him. He must not go with us to battle. He might turn against us in the fight. He could win back his master's favourfavourfavor by killing our men." 5"Isn't this David? The people sang about him in dances. They said, 'Saul killed thousands, but David killed tens of thousands.'"

Achish Sends David Home

6Achish called David and said to him, "I swear by the LORD that you have been good and right in my eyes. I would be glad for you to go out and come in with me in the army. I have found no wrong in you from the day you came to me until now. But the leaders do not approve of you. 7Now go back. Go in peace. Do not make the Philistine leaders angry."

8David said to Achish, "What have I done? What wrong have you found in your servant since I came before you until today? Why can't I go and fight the enemies of my king?"

9Achish answered David, "I know you are good in my eyes, like an angel of God. But the Philistine leaders said, 'He must not go up with us to battle.' 10So get up early in the morning. You and your master's servants who came with you should leave when it is light."

11David and his men got up early. They went back to the land of the Philistines. The Philistines went up to Jezreel.
