Ahaziah Becomes King

1The people of Jerusalem made Ahaziah king. He was the youngest son of Jehoram, king of Judah. The older sons had been killed by a group of raiders who came with the Arabs to the camp. 2Ahaziah was forty-two years old when he began to rule. He ruled one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah. She was the daughter of Omri.

Ahaziah Follows Bad Advice

3He followed the bad ways of Ahab's family. His mother told him to do wrong. 4He did what the the LORD saw as bad, like Ahab's family did. After his father died, they gave him bad advice. That advice ruined him.

5He listened to their advice. He went with Joram, the son of Ahab and king of Israel, to fight Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth Gilead. The people of Aram hurt Joram. 6Joram went back to Jezreel to get well. He had been hurt at Ramah while fighting Hazael, king of Aram. Ahaziah, son of Jehoram and king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Joram, because Joram was sick.

God Brings Ahaziah Down

7God caused Ahaziah to be destroyed when he went to see Joram. When he got there, he went out with Joram to meet Jehu, the son of Nimshi. The the LORD had chosen Jehu to destroy Ahab's family. 8While Jehu was punishing Ahab's family, he found the leaders of Judah. He also found the sons of Ahaziah's relatives who helped Ahaziah. He killed them.

9Jehu's men looked for Ahaziah. They caught him while he was hiding in Samaria. They brought him to Jehu and killed him. They buried him because they said, "HeheHe is the son of Jehoshaphat, who followed the the LORD with all his heart." After that, Ahaziah's family had no strength to rule.

Athaliah Takes the Throne

10When Athaliah, Ahaziah's mother, saw that her son was dead, she took over. She killed all the royal family of Judah. 11But Jehoshabeath, the king's daughter, took Joash, Ahaziah's son. She hid him while the other royal children were being killed. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom. Jehoshabeath was King Jehoram's daughter and the wife of Jehoiada the priest. She was Ahaziah's sister. She hid Joash from Athaliah so Athaliah would not kill him. 12Joash stayed hidden with them in God's house for six years. During that time, Athaliah ruled the land.
