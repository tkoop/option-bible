Judah Leaves His Brothers

1At that time, Judah left his brothers. He went down to stay with a man named Hirah. Hirah was from Adullam.

2There, Judah saw a Canaanite woman. Her father was named Shua. Judah married her and slept with her.

3She became pregnant and had a son. Judah named him Er.

4She became pregnant again and had another son. She named him Onan.

5She had one more son. She named him Shelah. He was born in Chezib.

Judah's Sons and Tamar

6Judah found a wife for Er, his oldest son. Her name was Tamar.

7Er was very bad in the the LORD's eyes. So the ThetheThe LORD put him to death.

8Judah said to Onan, "Go and sleep with your brother's wife. Be a husband to her. Have children for your brother."

9Onan knew the children would not be his own. So when he slept with his brother's wife, he made sure she did not get pregnant. He did not want to give children to his brother.

10What Onan did was bad in the the LORD's eyes. So the ThetheThe LORD put him to death too.

11Judah said to Tamar, his daughter-in-law, "Live as a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up." He was afraid Shelah might die too, like his brothers. So Tamar went home and lived with her father.

Judah Goes to Timnah

12After a long time, Judah's wife died. She was the daughter of Shua. Judah was comforted. Then he went up to Timnah with his friend Hirah from Adullam. They went to see the men cutting wool from his sheep.

13Someone told Tamar, "YouryourYour father-in-law is going up to Timnah to cut wool from his sheep."

14Tamar took off her widow's clothes. She put on a veil and covered her face. Then she sat by the road at Enaim, on the way to Timnah. She did this because she saw that Shelah was grown, but Judah had not given her to him as a wife.

15Judah saw her. He thought she was a prostitute, because she had covered her face.

16Judah turned to her by the road and said, "Come, let me sleep with you." He did not know she was his daughter-in-law.

She said, "What will you give me to sleep with me?"

17Judah said, "I will send you a young goat from my flock."

She said, "WillwillWill you give me something to keep until you send it?"

18He said, "What should I give you?"

She said, "Give me your seal, your cord, and the staff in your hand." He gave them to her. Then he slept with her, and she became pregnant.

19Tamar got up and left. She took off her veil and put on her widow's clothes again.

20Judah sent the young goat by his friend from Adullam. He wanted to get his things back from the woman. But he could not find her.

21He asked the men who lived there, "Where is the prostitute who was by the road at Enaim?"

They said, "No prostitute has been here."

22He went back to Judah and said, "I could not find her. The men who live there said no prostitute has been there."

23Judah said, "Let her keep the things. We do not want to be ashamed. I sent this goat, but you did not find her."

24About three months later, someone told Judah, "YouryourYour daughter-in-law Tamar has been a prostitute. She is pregnant."

Judah said, "BringbringBring her out. Burn her!"

25As she was being brought out, Tamar sent a message to her father-in-law. She said, "I am pregnant by the man who owns these things. Please see whose seal, cord, and staff these are."

26Judah knew them. He said, "She is more right than I am. I did not give her to my son Shelah." He did not sleep with her again.

Tamar's Twins

27When it was time for her to have her babies, there were twins in her body.

28While she was giving birth, one baby put out his hand. The woman helping her took a red thread and tied it on his hand. She said, "This one came out first."

29But he pulled his hand back. Then his brother came out. The woman said, "YouyouYou broke out!" So he was named Perez.

30Then his brother came out. He had the red thread on his hand. He was named Zerah.
