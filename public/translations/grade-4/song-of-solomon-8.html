The Bride Speaks to Her Beloved

1"If only you were like a brother to me, one who drank milk from my mother! Then if I found you outside, I would kiss you, and no one would look down on me. 2I would lead you and bring you to my mother's house. She taught me there. I would give you spiced wine to drink, and sweet juice from my fruit. 3His left arm holds my head, and his right arm hugs me. 4Daughters of Jerusalem, I ask you to promise: do not wake up love until it is ready."

Coming Up from the Desert

5"Who is this coming up from the desert, leaning on the one she loves?"

"Under the apple tree I woke you up. There your mother became pregnant with you. There she gave birth to you."

Love Is Stronger Than Death

6"Put me like a seal on your heart, like a seal on your arm. Love is as strong as death. Jealousy is as hard as the grave. It burns like a big fire, like a strong flame. 7Many waters cannot put out love. Rivers cannot wash it away. If a man gave all the money in his house for love, people would look down on him."

The Little Sister

8"We have a little sister, and her breasts are not grown yet. What should we do for our sister when someone asks to marry her? 9If she is a wall, we will build silver towers on her. If she is a door, we will cover her with cedar boards."

10"I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers. So I have become in his eyes like one who brings peace."

The Grape Field

11"Solomon had a grape field in Baal Hamon. He let farmers use it. Each one had to bring a thousand pieces of silver for its fruit. 12But my grape field is mine to give. The thousand pieces of silver are for you, Solomon, and two hundred are for the men who care for the fruit."

The Beloved Speaks

13"You who live in the gardens, with friends listening for your voice, let me hear it."

14"Come quickly, my love, and be like a deer or a young deer on the mountains of spices."
