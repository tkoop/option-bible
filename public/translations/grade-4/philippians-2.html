Timothy

19I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon. Then I can be cheered by news about you. 20I have no one like him. He truly cares about you. 21All the others look out for themselves. They do not care about Jesus Christ. 22But you know Timothy. He served with me like a son with a father. He helped me share the good news. 23So I hope to send him as soon as I see how things go with me. 24I trust in the Lord that I will come soon too.
