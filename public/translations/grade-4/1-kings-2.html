David Tells Solomon What to Do

1When David was close to death, he told his son Solomon what to do.

2"I am going to die, like everyone does. So be strong. Be a man. 3Keep what the LORD your God asks you to do. Walk in HishisHis ways. Keep HishisHis rules, HishisHis commands, HishisHis laws, and HishisHis teachings. They are written in the LawlawLaw of Moses. Then you will do well in all you do, wherever you go. 4Then the LORD will keep HishisHis promise to me. He said, 'If your sons walk in truth before MemeMe with all their heart and all their soul, then you will always have someone from your family on Israel's throne.' 5YouyouYou also know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me. He killed Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. They were the leaders of Israel's armies. He killed them when there was peace. Their blood stained the belt around his waist and the sandals on his feet. 6So use your wisdom. Do not let him die peacefully in old age. 7But be kind to the sons of Barzillai from Gilead. Let them eat with you. They helped me when I ran from your brother Absalom. 8Watch Shimei son of Gera. He is from Benjamin and lives in Bahurim. He said awful things about me when I went to Mahanaim. But he came down to meet me at the Jordan River. I promised him by the LORD, 'I will not kill you with a sword.' 9So do not let him go free. You are a wise man, and you will know what to do to him. Make sure he is killed."

David Dies and Solomon Rules

10Then David died. He was buried in the CitycityCity of David. 11David ruled Israel for forty years. He ruled seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. 12Solomon sat on his father David's throne. His kingdom was strong.

Adonijah Asks for Abishag

13Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. She asked, "Do you come in peace?"

"Yes, in peace," he said.

14"I have something to say to you."

"Speak," she said.

15"You know the kingdom was mine. All Israel wanted me to be king. But the kingdom went to my brother. The LORD gave it to him."

16"Now I have one request. Please do not say no."

"Speak," she said.

17"Please ask King Solomon to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife. He will not say no to you."

18"Very well. I will speak to the king for you."

19Bathsheba went to King Solomon to talk about Adonijah. The king stood up to meet her and bowed to her. Then he sat on his throne. He had a throne brought for his mother, and she sat at his right hand.

20"I have one small request. Please do not say no."

"What is it, my mother? I will not say no to you."

21"Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to your brother Adonijah as his wife."

22"Why do you ask for Abishag for Adonijah? You might as well ask for the kingdom for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab son of Zeruiah!"

23Then King Solomon promised by the LORD, "May God punish me, and even more, if Adonijah does not pay with his life for this request!"

24"Now, as sure as the LORD lives, HeheHe made me strong and set me on my father David's throne. He gave me a family, as HeheHe promised. So Adonijah will die today!"

25So King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada. Benaiah killed Adonijah, and he died.

Solomon Deals with Joab and Abiathar

26The king said to Abiathar the priest, "Go to your fields in Anathoth. You deserve to die. But I will not kill you now. You carried the ark of the Lord the LORD before my father David. You shared in all my father's troubles."

27So Solomon removed Abiathar from being the LORD's priest. This happened just as the LORD had said at Shiloh about Eli's family.

28Joab had joined Adonijah but not Absalom. When he heard the news, he ran to the LORD's tent and held on to the altar's horns.

29King Solomon heard that Joab had run to the LORD's tent and was by the altar. So he sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada and said, "Go kill him."

30Benaiah went to the LORD's tent and said to Joab, "ThetheThe king says, 'Come out!'"

"No, I will die here!" Benaiah went back and told the king what Joab said.

31The king said, "Do what he said. Kill him and bury him. Then I will be free from the blame for the innocent blood Joab spilled. 32The LORD will make Joab pay for the blood he spilled. He killed two good men better than himself. My father David did not know about it. The men were Abner son of Ner, leader of Israel's army, and Amasa son of Jether, leader of Judah's army. 33Their blood will come back on Joab and his children forever. But the LORD will give peace to David, his children, his family, and his throne forever."

34So Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and killed Joab. Joab was buried at his own house in the desert.

35The king made Benaiah son of Jehoiada leader of the army in Joab's place. He made Zadok the priest in Abiathar's place.

Shimei Breaks His Promise

36The king sent for Shimei and said, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem. Live there. Do not leave and go anywhere else."

37"On the day you leave and cross the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley, you will surely die. Your blood will be on your own head."

38Shimei said to the king, "That is good. Your servant will do what my lord the king says." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem for a long time.

39After three years, two of Shimei's slaves ran away to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Someone told Shimei, "Look, your slaves are in Gath."

40So Shimei saddled his donkey and went to Gath to look for his slaves. He found them and brought them back from Gath.

41Solomon was told that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and come back.

42The king sent for Shimei and said, "Did I not make you promise by the LORD? Did I not warn you, 'On the day you leave and go anywhere, you will surely die'? And you said, 'That is good. I have heard.'"

43"So why did you not keep your promise to the LORD and the command I gave you?"

44The king also said to Shimei, "YouyouYou know in your heart all the bad things you did to my father David. Now the LORD will bring that bad back on your own head."

45But King Solomon will be blessed, and David's throne will stand before the LORD forever.

46Then the king ordered Benaiah son of Jehoiada. Benaiah went out and killed Shimei, and he died. So Solomon's kingdom was safe.
