Paul Sails to Tyre

1After we left them, we sailed straight to Cos. The next day we went to Rhodes. Then we went to Patara. 2There we found a ship going to Phoenicia. We got on it and sailed. 3We saw Cyprus, but we left it on our left side. We sailed to Syria and landed at Tyre. The ship was there to unload its cargo. 4We found some followers of Jesus there. We stayed with them for seven days. Through the SpiritspiritSpirit, they told Paul not to go to Jerusalem. 5When the week was over, we left. Everyone, with their wives and children, walked with us out of the city. We knelt on the beach and prayed. 6We said goodbye to each other. Then we got on the ship, and they went home.

Paul Visits Philip

7After we finished our trip from Tyre, we landed at Ptolemais. We greeted the brothers and stayed with them one day. 8The next day we left and went to Caesarea. We went into the house of Philip. He was the man who told the good news. He was one of the seven. We stayed with him. 9He had four daughters who were not married. They told what God would do.

Agabus Warns Paul

10We stayed there many days. A prophet named Agabus came from Judea. 11He came to us. He took Paul's belt. He tied his own feet and hands. Then he said, "ThetheThe HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit says, 'The Jews in Jerusalem will tie up the man who owns this belt. They will hand him over to people who are not Jews.'"

12When we heard this, we and the people there begged Paul not to go up to Jerusalem. 13Paul answered, "Why are you crying? Why are you breaking my heart? I am ready not only to be tied up, but also to die in Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus."

14He would not listen. So we stopped. We said, "ThetheThe Lord's will be done."

Paul Goes to Jerusalem

15After these days, we got ready and went up to Jerusalem. 16Some followers from Caesarea went with us. They took us to stay with Mnason. He was from Cyprus. He was an old follower. 17When we got to Jerusalem, the brothers welcomed us with joy. 18The next day Paul went with us to see James. All the leaders were there.

Paul Tells What God Did

19Paul greeted them. He told them one by one what God had done among people who are not Jews. He did this through his work. 20When they heard it, they praised God. Then they said to Paul, "Brother, you see how many thousands of Jews believe. They all care deeply about the law. 21They have heard that you teach Jews who live among people who are not Jews to turn away from Moses. They say you tell them not to circumcise their sons and not to follow our customs. 22What should we do? They will hear that you have come.

23"So do what we tell you. We have four men who made a promise to God. 24Take them with you. Make yourself clean with them. Pay for them so they can shave their heads. Then everyone will know that what they heard about you is not true. They will know that you keep the law too. 25As for the people who are not Jews and believe, we wrote to them. We said they should stay away from food offered to false gods, from blood, from meat of animals that were strangled, and from sexual sin."

Paul Is Caught in the Temple

26The next day Paul took the men. He made himself clean with them. Then he went into the temple. He told when the days of cleaning would end. This was until an offering was brought for each man. 27The seven days were almost over. Some Jews from Asia saw Paul in the temple. They stirred up the whole crowd. They grabbed him. 28They shouted, "Men of Israel, help! This man teaches everyone everywhere against our people, our law, and this place. He even brought Greeks into the temple. He made this holy place unclean." 29They had seen Trophimus from Ephesus with Paul in the city. They thought Paul had brought him into the temple. 30The whole city was upset. People ran together. They seized Paul and dragged him out of the temple. The doors were shut. 31They were trying to kill him. The leader of the soldiers heard that all Jerusalem was in an uproar. 32He quickly took soldiers and officers. He ran down to them. When the people saw the leader and the soldiers, they stopped beating Paul. 33The leader came near. He took Paul and ordered him tied with two chains. He asked who Paul was and what he had done. 34Some people shouted one thing. Others shouted something else. The leader could not learn the truth because of the noise. So he ordered Paul taken to the army camp. 35When Paul got to the steps, the soldiers had to carry him. The crowd was so rough. 36The crowd followed and shouted, "Take him away!"

Paul Speaks to the Commander

37As Paul was about to be taken into the camp, he said to the leader, "May I say something to you?"

The leader asked, "Do you know Greek? 38Are you not the Egyptian who started trouble before this? Did you not lead four thousand men with daggers into the desert?"

39Paul said, "I am a Jew. I am from Tarsus in Cilicia. It is not a small city. Please let me speak to the people."

40The leader said yes. Paul stood on the steps. He waved his hand. When the people were very quiet, he spoke to them in the Hebrew language.
