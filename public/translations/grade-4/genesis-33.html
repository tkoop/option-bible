Jacob Meets Esau

1Jacob looked up and saw Esau coming with 400 men. 2He split the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two female servants. He put the servants and their children first. He put Leah and her children next. He put Rachel and Joseph last.

3Jacob went ahead of them. He bowed to the ground seven times as he came near his brother.

4Esau ran to meet him. He hugged Jacob and kissed him. They both cried.

5Esau looked up and saw the women and children. He asked, "Who are these with you?"

Jacob said, "They are the children God gave your servant."

6The female servants and their children came near and bowed down. 7Leah and her children came near and bowed down. Then Joseph and Rachel came near and bowed down.

8Esau asked, "What do you mean by all this group I met?"

Jacob said, "I sent them so you would be pleased with me."

9Esau said, "I have enough, my brother. Keep what you have."

10Jacob said, "No, please. If I found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, please take this gift from my hand. I saw your face, and it was like seeing God's face, and you welcomed me. 11Please take the gift I brought you, because God has been kind to me. I have everything I need." Jacob kept asking, and Esau took it.

12Esau said, "Let us travel together. I will go ahead of you."

13Jacob said, "MymyMy lord knows the children are young and weak. The sheep and cows have new babies. If they are pushed too hard in one day, all the animals will die. 14Please let my lord go ahead of his servant. I will travel slowly with the children and animals until I come to my lord in Seir."

15Esau said, "Let me leave some of my people with you."

Jacob said, "Why? I only want to find favourfavourfavor in my lord's eyes."

16That day Esau went back to Seir.

Jacob Buys Land and Builds an Altar

18Jacob came safely to the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan. He set up his camp near the city. 19He bought the piece of land where he had set up his tent. He bought it from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for 100 pieces of silver. 20There he built an altar and called it "God, the God of Israel."
