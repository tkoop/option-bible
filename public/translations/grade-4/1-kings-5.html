Hiram Hears That Solomon Is King

1Hiram, king of Tyre, heard that Solomon was made king in place of his father. Hiram had always been David's friend. So Hiram sent his servants to Solomon.

Solomon Writes to Hiram

2Solomon sent this message to Hiram:

3"You know that my father David could not build a house for the the LORD his God. He had wars all around him. The ThetheThe LORD did not let him rest until HeheHe put his enemies under his feet. 4But now the the LORD my God has given me peace on every side. There is no enemy and no trouble. 5So I plan to build a house for the the LORD my God. The ThetheThe LORD promised my father David:

'I will put your son on your throne after you. He will build a house for MymyMy name.'

6So please command your men to cut cedar trees for me from Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants. I will pay your servants whatever wage you set. You know we do not have anyone who can cut wood like the people of Sidon."

Hiram Praises the LORD

7Hiram was very glad when he heard Solomon's words. He said, "Praise the the LORD today! He gave David a wise son to rule this great people."

8Hiram sent this message to Solomon: "I heard what you sent to me. I will do all you ask for cedar and cypress wood. 9My servants will bring the wood down from Lebanon to the sea. I will tie it into rafts and float it to the place you choose. There I will break it apart, and you can take it. You can pay me back by giving food for my house." 10So Hiram gave Solomon all the cedar and cypress wood he wanted.

Solomon Pays Hiram

11Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand baskets of wheat for food for his house, and twenty jars of pure oil. Solomon gave this to Hiram every year. 12The ThetheThe LORD gave Solomon wisdom, just as HeheHe promised. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made an agreement.

Workers Build the Temple

13King Solomon made thirty thousand men from all Israel work for him. 14He sent them to Lebanon in shifts. Ten thousand men went each month. They worked one month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was in charge of this work. 15Solomon also had seventy thousand men who carried loads and eighty thousand men who cut stone in the hills. 16He had three thousand three hundred bosses who watched the workers. 17The king told his workers to cut large, expensive stones to lay the foundation of the temple. 18Solomon's builders, Hiram's builders, and the men from Gebal cut and prepared the wood and stones to build the temple.
