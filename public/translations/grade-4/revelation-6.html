The Lamb Opens the First Seal

1I saw the LamblambLamb open one of the seven seals. I heard one of the four living things say in a voice like thunder, "Come!" 2I looked, and there was a white horse. The rider had a bow. He was given a crown. He went out to win, and to keep on winning.

The Second Seal

3When the LamblambLamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living thing say, "Come!" 4Another horse came out. It was red. The rider was given power to take peace from the earth, so people would kill each other. He was given a big sword.

The Third Seal

5When the LamblambLamb opened the third seal, I heard the third living thing say, "Come!" I looked, and there was a black horse. The rider held a scale in his hand. 6I heard a voice among the four living things say, "A quart of wheat costs a whole day's pay. Three quarts of barley cost a whole day's pay. Do not hurt the oil or the wine."

The Fourth Seal

7When the LamblambLamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth living thing say, "Come!" 8I looked, and there was a pale horse. The rider's name was Death. Death was followed by the grave. They were given power over one fourth of the earth. They could kill with a sword, with hunger, with sickness, and with wild animals.

The Fifth Seal

9When the LamblambLamb opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of people who were killed for God's word and for the truth they told. 10They cried out in a loud voice, "How long, holy and true Lord, until you judge the people on EarthEarthearth and pay them back for killing us?" 11Each one was given a white robe. They were told to rest a little longer. More servants and brothers who would be killed like them still had to come.

The Sixth Seal

12I saw when the LamblambLamb opened the sixth seal. There was a big earthquake. The sun turned black like rough cloth. The whole moon turned like blood. 13The stars in the sky fell to EarthEarthearth, like figs that fall from a tree when a strong wind shakes it. 14The sky was rolled up like a scroll. Every mountain and island moved from its place.

15The kings of the earth, the rulers, the army leaders, the rich, the strong, and every slave and free person hid in caves and among the rocks of the mountains. 16They said to the mountains and rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the face of the OneoneOne who sits on the throne and from the anger of the LamblambLamb." 17For the great day of their anger has come, and who can stand?
