Micah and His Mother

1There was a man named Micah. He lived in the hill country of Ephraim. 2He said to his mother, "YouyouYou had 1,100 pieces of silver taken from you. You cursed about it. You said, 'I have the silver.' But I took it."

His mother said, "May the LORD bless my son!"

3Micah gave the silver back to his mother. She said, "I set this silver apart for the LORD for my son. It was to make a carved statue and a metal idol. Now I give it back to you."

4So Micah gave the silver back to his mother. She took 200 pieces of silver and gave them to a man who worked with silver. He made a carved statue and a metal idol. They were kept in Micah's house.

Micah Makes Idols

5Micah had a small place to worship. He made an ephod and some household gods. He picked one of his sons to be his priest.

No King in Israel

6In those days Israel had no king. Everyone did what he thought was right.

A Levite Comes to Micah

7There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah. He was a Levite. He was living among the people of Judah. 8He left Bethlehem in Judah to find a place to live. He came to Micah's house in the hill country of Ephraim.

9Micah asked him, "Where do you come from?"

The man said, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah. I am looking for a place to live."

Micah Hires the Levite

10Micah said, "Stay with me. Be like a father to me and be my priest. I will give you ten pieces of silver each year, clothes, and food." So the Levite went with him.

11The Levite agreed to stay with the man. The young man became like one of his sons.

12Micah made the young Levite his priest. The Levite lived in Micah's house.

13Micah said, "Now I know the LORD will be good to me. I have a Levite as my priest."
