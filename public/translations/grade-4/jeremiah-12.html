Jeremiah Talks to God

1"You are right, the LORD, when I talk with YouyouYou. But I want to ask YouyouYou about what is fair. Why do bad people do well? Why do people who break trust feel safe? 2You planted them, and they took root. They grow and bear fruit. You are always on their lips, but far from their hearts. 3But YouyouYou know me, the LORD. You see me and test my heart for YouyouYou. Drag them off like sheep to be killed. Set them apart for the day they will be killed. 4How long will the land be sad and all the grass in the fields dry up? The bad things people do have caused this. Even the animals and birds are gone, because they say, 'He will not see how we end.'"

God Answers Jeremiah

5"If you ran with men on foot and they wore you out, how can you race with horses? If you trip in a safe land, how will you do in the thick woods by the Jordan? 6Even your brothers and your father's family have turned against you. They have called out after you. Do not trust them, even if they say good things to you."

God Speaks About His Land

7"I have left MymyMy house. I have given up MymyMy land. I have put the one I love into the hands of her enemies. 8My land has become like a lion in the forest to MemeMe. She roared against MemeMe. So I hate her.

9"Is MymyMy land like a spotted bird to MemeMe? Birds of prey all around are against her. Come, gather all the wild animals. Bring them to eat her. 10Many shepherds have ruined MymyMy vineyard. They trampled MymyMy field. They turned MymyMy special land into a dry, empty place.

11"They made it a ruin. It is sad before MemeMe. The whole land is ruined, because no one thinks about it. 12Destroyers have come to all the bare hills in the desert. The sword of the LORD destroys from one end of the earth to the other. No one is safe. 13They planted wheat but picked thorns. They worked hard for nothing. Be ashamed of your crops because of the LORD's fierce anger."

God's Plan for the Nations

14"The LORD says: 'All MymyMy bad neighbors touch the land I gave to MymyMy people Israel. I will pull them up from their land. I will pull the people of Judah out from among them.' 15After I pull them up, I will come back and show mercy to them. I will bring each one back to his own land.

16"If they learn MymyMy people's ways and promise by MymyMy name, 'As the LORD lives,' just like they taught MymyMy people to promise by Baal, then they will be built up among MymyMy people. 17But if they do not listen, I will pull up that nation and destroy it, says the LORD."
