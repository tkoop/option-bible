1After this, Absalom got a chariot and horses. He also had fifty men run ahead of him.

2Absalom would get up early and stand beside the road to the gate. Whenever someone had a problem to bring to the king for judgmentjudgementjudgment, Absalom would call out to him, "What city are you from?"

He would answer, "YouryourYour servant is from one of the tribes of Israel."

3Then Absalom would say, "Look, your case is good and right, but the king has no one to hear you."

4Absalom would add, "If only I were made judge in the land! Then everyone with a complaint or case could come to me, and I would give them justice."

5Also, when anyone came near to bow down to him, Absalom would reach out his hand, take hold of the person, and kiss him.

6Absalom did this to all the people of Israel who came to the king for judgmentjudgementjudgment. In this way, Absalom won the hearts of the men of Israel.

Absalom Goes to Hebron

7After forty years, Absalom said to the king, "Please let me go to Hebron to keep a promise I made to the the LORD."

8"For your servant made a promise while I lived at Geshur in Aram. I said, 'If the the LORD brings me back to Jerusalem, then I will worship the the LORD.'"

9ThetheThe king said to him, "Go in peace." So he got up and went to Hebron.

10Then Absalom sent spies through all the tribes of Israel with this message: "As soon as you hear the sound of the horn, you shall say, 'Absalom is king in Hebron.'"

11Two hundred men from Jerusalem went with Absalom. They had been invited, and they went without knowing what was happening.

12While Absalom was offering sacrifices, he sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, David's advisor, from his city Giloh. The plan grew strong, and the people who followed Absalom kept growing.
