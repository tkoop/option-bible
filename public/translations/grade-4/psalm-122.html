A Song for Going Up to the House of the the LORD

1I was glad when people said to me, "Let's go to the house of the the LORD." 2Our feet stand in your gates, Jerusalem.

3Jerusalem is built close together, like one city. 4The tribes go up there. They are the tribes of the the LORD. They tell Israel about HimhimHim. They thank the the LORD.

5There are seats where leaders judge. They are the seats of David's family.

Pray for Peace in Jerusalem

6Pray for peace in Jerusalem. May those who love you do well.

7May there be peace inside your walls and good things inside your big houses.

8For my brothers and friends, I will now say, "Peace be with you."

9For the house of the the LORD our God, I will want good things for you.
