The Wall Is Finished

1After the wall was built and I put the doors in place, I chose gatekeepers, singers, and Levites. 2Then I put my brother Hanani in charge of Jerusalem. I also put Hananiah in charge. He was the leader of the fort. I chose him because he could be trusted, and he feared God more than most people. 3I told them, "Do not open the gates of Jerusalem until the sun is hot. While the guards are on duty, keep the doors shut and locked. Also choose people who live in Jerusalem to guard. Some can stand at their posts, and some can guard near their homes." 4The city was big and wide, but few people lived there. The houses had not been rebuilt yet.

The People Are Counted

5Then my God put it in my heart to gather the leaders, the officials, and the people so they could be listed by family. I found the list of the first people who came back. I found these words written in it: 6These are the people from the area who came back from being captives in Babylon. King Nebuchadnezzar had taken them away. They came back to Jerusalem and Judah. Each person went to his own town. 7They came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bigvai, Nehum, and Baanah. This is the number of the men of Israel:

Families Who Came Back

8The family of Parosh had 2,172 people. 9The family of Shephatiah had 372. 10The family of Arah had 652. 11The family of Pahath-moab, through Jeshua and Joab, had 2,818. 12The family of Elam had 1,254. 13The family of Zattu had 845. 14The family of Zaccai had 760. 15The family of Binnui had 648. 16The family of Bebai had 628. 17The family of Azgad had 2,322.

18The family of Adonikam had 667. 19The family of Bigvai had 2,067. 20The family of Adin had 655. 21The family of Ater, through Hezekiah, had 98. 22The family of Hashum had 328. 23The family of Bezai had 324. 24The family of Hariph had 112. 25The family of Gibeon had 95. 26The people from Bethlehem and Netophah had 188. 27The people from Anathoth had 128. 28The people from Beth Azmaveth had 42.

29The people from Kiriath Jearim, Chephirah, and Beeroth had 743. 30The people from Ramah and Geba had 621. 31The people from Michmas had 122. 32The people from Bethel and Ai had 123. 33The people from the other Nebo had 52. 34The people from the other Elam had 1,254. 35The family of Harim had 320. 36The people from Jericho had 345. 37The people from Lod, Hadid, and Ono had 721. 38The people from Senaah had 3,930.

Priests Who Came Back

39The family of Jedaiah, through Jeshua, had 973 priests. 40The family of Immer had 1,052. 41The family of Pashhur had 1,247. 42The family of Harim had 1,017.

Levites, Singers, and Gatekeepers

43The Levite family of Jeshua, through Kadmiel and Hodevah, had 74 people. 44The singers from the family of Asaph had 148. 45The gatekeepers from the families of Shallum, Ater, Talmon, Akkub, Hatita, and Shobai had 138.

Temple Servants

46The temple servants were from these families: Ziha, Hasupha, and Tabbaoth. 47They were also from Keros, Siaha, and Padon. 48They were from Lebanah, Hagabah, and Shalmai. 49They were from Hanan, Giddel, and Gahar. 50They were from Reaiah, Rezin, and Nekoda. 51They were from Gazzam, Uzza, and Paseah. 52They were from Besai, Meunim, and Nephisim. 53They were from Bakbuk, Hakupha, and Harhur. 54They were from Bazluth, Mehida, and Harsha. 55They were from Barkos, Sisera, and Temah. 56They were from Neziah and Hatipha.

57The servants of Solomon were from these families: Sotai, Sophereth, and Perida. 58They were also from Jaala, Darkon, and Giddel. 59They were from Shephatiah, Hattil, Pokereth-hazzebaim, and Amon. 60In all, the temple servants and the servants of Solomon numbered 392.

People Who Could Not Prove Their Families

61The following people came from Tel Melah, Tel Harsha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer. They could not prove that their families came from Israel. 62They were from the families of Delaiah, Tobiah, and Nekoda, and they numbered 642. 63Some of the priests were from the families of Hobaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai. Barzillai had married a daughter of Barzillai the Gileadite and took his name. 64These men looked for their family records, but they could not find them. So they were not allowed to serve as priests. 65The governor told them not to eat the most holy food until a priest could ask God using the Urim and Thummim.

The Whole Group

66The whole group had 42,360 people. 67This did not include their 7,337 male and female servants and 245 male and female singers. 68They had 736 horses and 245 mules. 69They had 435 camels and 6,720 donkeys.

Gifts for the Work

70Some family leaders gave money for the work. The governor gave 1,000 gold coins, 50 bowls, and 530 sets of clothes for the priests. 71Other family leaders gave 20,000 gold coins and 2,200 pieces of silver for the work. 72The rest of the people gave 20,000 gold coins, 2,000 pieces of silver, and 67 sets of clothes for the priests.

The People Settle Down

73So the priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and all the rest of Israel settled in their own towns. By the seventh month, the people of Israel had settled in their towns.
