Ben-Hadad Attacks Samaria

1Ben-Hadad was the king of Aram. He brought all his army. Thirty-two kings came with him. He also had horses and chariots. He went up, surrounded Samaria, and fought against it.

2He sent messengers into the city to Ahab, the king of Israel.

3The message said, "Ben-Hadad says, 'YouryourYour silver and gold are mine. Your best things, your wives, and your children are mine too.'"

4ThetheThe king of Israel answered, "As you say, my lord the king, I am yours, and all I have is yours."

5The messengers came back and said, "Ben-Hadad says, 'I sent word to you. I said you must give me your silver, gold, wives, and children.'"

6"But tomorrow at this time, I will send my servants to you. They will search your house and your servants' houses. They will take everything you like best."

7The king of Israel called all the elders of the land. He said, "Please see how this man wants trouble. He sent for my wives, children, silver, and gold. I did not refuse him."

8All the elders and the people said to him, "Do not listen to him. Do not agree."

9So Ahab said to Ben-Hadad's messengers, "Tell my lord the king, 'I will do all you asked at first. But I cannot do this new thing.'" ThetheThe messengers left and told him.

10Ben-Hadad sent another message: "I swear by the gods that I will make Samaria into dust. There will not be enough dust for all my men to carry."

11The king of Israel answered, "Tell him, 'A man should not brag while putting on his armourarmourarmor. He can brag only after he wins.'"

12When Ben-Hadad heard this message, he was getting drunk in the tents with the kings. He said to his servants, "Get ready." They got ready to attack the city.

The LORD Promises Help

13A prophet came to Ahab, the king of Israel. He said, "This is what the LORD says: 'Do you see this big crowd? Today I will give them into your hand. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

14Ahab asked, "Who will start the fight?"

The prophet answered, "This is what the LORD says: 'The young leaders of the regions will start it.'"

Then Ahab asked, "Who will lead the army?"

The prophet said, "YouyouYou will."

15Ahab counted the young leaders of the regions. There were 232. After them, he counted all the people of Israel. There were 7,000.

16They went out at noon. Ben-Hadad was getting drunk in the tents with the 32 kings who helped him.

17The young leaders went out first. People told Ben-Hadad, "Men have come out from Samaria."

18Ben-Hadad said, "If they come for peace, take them alive. If they come for war, take them alive too."

19Those young leaders and the army behind them came out of the city.

20Each man killed his enemy. The army of Aram ran away. Israel chased them. Ben-Hadad, the king of Aram, escaped on a horse with some horsemen.

21The king of Israel went out and struck the horses and chariots. He killed many men in Aram.

22Then the prophet came to the king of Israel and said, "Go, make yourself strong. Think about what you must do. Next year the king of Aram will come back against you."

23The servants of the king of Aram said to him, "TheirtheirTheir gods are gods of the hills. That is why they were stronger than us. But if we fight them on the flat land, we will be stronger than them."

24"Do this: Take the kings away from their places. Put governors in their places instead."

25"Get an army as big as the one you lost. Get the same number of horses and chariots. Then fight them on the flat land. We will be stronger than them." Ben-Hadad listened to them and did it.

The Second Battle

26The next year, Ben-Hadad counted the men of Aram. He went up to Aphek to fight Israel.

27The people of Israel were counted and fed. They marched out to meet them. They camped across from them. They looked like two small flocks of goats. But the army of Aram filled the land.

28A man of God came to the king of Israel and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'The Arameans said, "ThetheThe LORD is a god of the hills, not a god of the valleys." So I will give all this big crowd into your hand. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

29They camped across from each other for seven days. On the seventh day the battle started. The Israelites killed 100,000 Aramean foot soldiers in one day.

30The rest ran into the city of Aphek. The wall fell on 27,000 men who were left. Ben-Hadad ran into the city and hid in an inner room.

31His servants said to him, "We have heard that the kings of Israel are kind. Let us go to the king of Israel with sackcloth around our waists and ropes around our heads. Maybe he will spare your life."

32They put sackcloth around their waists and ropes around their heads. They went to the king of Israel and said, "YouryourYour servant Ben-Hadad says, 'Please let me live.'"

ThetheThe king said, "IsisIs he still alive? He is my brother."

33The men watched Ahab's words. They quickly said, "Yes, your brother Ben-Hadad!"

Ahab said, "Go get him." Ben-Hadad came out. Ahab had him ride in his chariot.

34Ben-Hadad said to him, "I will give back the cities my father took from your father. You can have streets in Damascus, as my father had in Samaria. Then I will let you go." Ahab made a peace treaty with him and let him go.

A Prophet Warns the King

35At the LORD's word, one of the prophets said to his friend, "Hit me, please." But the man would not hit him.

36The prophet said, "Because you did not obey the LORD's voice, a lion will attack you as soon as you leave me." Then he left. A lion found him and killed him.

37The prophet found another man and said, "Hit me, please." The man hit him and wounded him.

38Then the prophet went and stood by the road. He waited for the king. He disguised himself with a bandage over his eyes.

39As the king passed by, the prophet cried out to him. He said, "YouryourYour servant went into the battle. A man brought another man to me and said, 'Guard this man. If he gets away, you will die, or you will pay a large amount of silver.'"

40"While your servant was busy here and there, the man got away."

The king of Israel said to him, "That is your sentence. You decided it."

41The prophet quickly took the bandage off his eyes. The king of Israel recognizedrecogniserecognized him as one of the prophets.

42The prophet said to him, "This is what the LORD says: 'Because you let go the man I set apart for destruction, your life will be taken instead of his life, and your people instead of his people.'"
