Babylon Has Fallen

1After these things, I saw another angel come down from Heavenheavenheaven. He had great power. The EarthEarthearth shone with his glory. 2He cried out in a loud voice, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She is now a home for demons. She is a prison for every bad spirit. She is a prison for every dirty bird. She is a prison for every dirty and hated beast." 3She led all nations to sin. The kings of the earth did wrong with her. The sellers of the earth got rich from her fancy life.

God Calls His People Out

4Then I heard another voice from Heavenheavenheaven. It said, "Come out of her, my people, so you do not share in her sins or get her troubles." 5Her sins piled up to Heavenheavenheaven. God remembers her bad deeds.

Babylon Gets Paid Back

6Pay her back for what she did. Give her twice as much trouble for her bad work. Mix her a double cup of what she mixed. 7She gave herself glory and lived in luxury. Give her that much pain and sadness. She says in her heart, "I sit as queen. I am not a widow. I will not be sad." 8So her troubles will come in one day: death, sadness, and hunger. She will be burned with fire. The Lord God is strong, and HeheHe judges her.

Kings and Sellers Cry

9The kings of the earth did wrong and lived fancy with her. They will cry and be sad when they see her smoke. 10They stand far away because they are afraid of her pain. They say, "Oh no! Oh no! The great city! The strong city Babylon! In one hour, your punishment has come." 11The sellers of the earth cry and are sad. No one buys their goods anymore.

12Their goods are gold, silver, pretty stones, pearls, fine cloth, purple cloth, silk, red cloth, all kinds of special wood, things made of ivory, things made of the best wood, bronze, iron, and marble. 13They also sell cinnamon, spice, sweet smells, perfume, frankincense, wine, oil, fine flour, wheat, cattle, sheep, horses, wagons, and even people's lives.

14The things you wanted most are gone from you. All the rich and bright things are lost from you. They will never be found again.

Sellers Stand Far Away

15The sellers who got rich from her stand far away. They are afraid of her pain. They cry and are sad. 16They say, "Oh no! Oh no! The great city! She wore fine cloth, purple, and red. She was covered with gold, pretty stones, and pearls."

17In one hour, all that wealth was ruined. Every ship captain, every sailor, and everyone who works on the sea stood far away. 18They cried out when they saw her smoke. They said, "Who is like the great city?" 19They threw dust on their heads. They cried and were sad. They said, "Oh no! Oh no! The great city! In her, all who had ships on the sea got rich from her costly things. In one hour, she was ruined."

Heaven and God's People Rejoice

20Rejoice over her, Heavenheavenheaven! Rejoice, saints, apostles, and prophets! God has judged your case against her.

Babylon Is Thrown Down

21Then a strong angel picked up a stone. It was as big as a heavy grinding stone. He threw it into the sea. He said, "So Babylon the great city will be thrown down with force. She will not be found anymore." 22No one will hear music, flutes, or trumpets in you anymore. No worker of any kind will be found in you anymore. No mill sound will be heard in you anymore. 23No lamp light will shine in you anymore. No bride or groom will be heard in you anymore. Your sellers were the great men of the earth. All nations were led astray by your magic. 24In her, blood of prophets and saints was found, and blood of all who were killed on the earth.
