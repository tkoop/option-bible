Trouble for Damascus

1This is a message about Damascus. Look! Damascus will stop being a city. It will become a pile of broken stones. 2The towns of Aroer will be empty. They will be for sheep. The sheep will lie down, and no one will scare them.

3The strong city will be taken from Ephraim. The kingdom will be taken from Damascus. The few people left in Syria will be like the greatness of the people of Israel, says the LORD of Hosts.

Israel Will Be Small

4In that day, Jacob's greatness will be small. His body will get thin. 5It will be like when a worker cuts ripe grain. His arm cuts the heads of grain. It will be like picking grain in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 6A few olives will be left. It will be like shaking an olive tree. Two or three olives will be on the top branch. Four or five will be on the other branches. This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says.

People Will Look to God

7In that day, a man will look to his MakermakerMaker. His eyes will look to the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 8He will not look at the altars his hands made. He will not look at the Asherah poles or the incense altars his fingers made. 9In that day, his strong cities will be like empty woods and empty hilltops. People left those places because of the people of Israel. The land will be empty.

Israel Forgot God

10You forgot the God who saves you. You did not remember HimhimHim. You did not remember the RockrockRock who keeps you safe. So you plant pretty plants. You plant grapevines from a faraway land. 11On the day you plant them, you make them grow. In the morning, your seeds bloom. But the harvest will be a sad pile on a day of grief and pain that cannot be healed.

God Will Punish the Nations

12Oh no! Many, many people are loud. They sound like the sea. Nations rush like mighty water. 13Nations rush like many waters. But HeheHe will tell them to stop. They will run far away. They will be blown away like dust on hills before the wind. They will be like a rolling leaf before a storm. 14In the evening, look! There is fear. Before morning, they are gone. This is what happens to those who steal our things. This is what happens to those who rob us.
