Do Not Worship False Gods

1Do not make false gods for yourselves. Do not set up a cut-out shape or a stone pillar for yourselves. Do not put a carved stone in your land to bow down to it. I am the LORD your God. 2Keep MymyMy rest days. HonourHonourHonor MymyMy holy place. I am the LORD.

Good Things for Obeying

3If you walk in MymyMy rules and keep MymyMy commands and do them, 4I will send rain at the right time. The land will grow food. The trees will give fruit. 5Your grain will be so much that you will still be picking grain when it is time to pick grapes. Picking grapes will last until planting time. You will eat all you want and live safely in your land. 6I will give peace in the land. You will lie down, and no one will scare you. I will take away wild animals from the land. No sword will go through your land. 7You will chase your enemies. They will fall by the sword in front of you. 8Five of you will chase a hundred. A hundred of you will chase ten thousand. Your enemies will fall by the sword in front of you.

9I will look at you with favourfavourfavor. I will make you grow and have many children. I will keep MymyMy promise with you. 10You will eat old grain from last year. You will bring out the old to make room for the new. 11I will put MymyMy tent among you. I will not hate you. 12I will walk among you. I will be your God. You will be MymyMy people. 13I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt so you would not be slaves. I broke the heavy yoke on you. I let you walk tall.

Hard Times for Not Obeying

14If you do not listen to MemeMe and do not do all these commands, 15if you hate MymyMy rules and are sick of MymyMy laws, so you do not do any of MymyMy commands and break MymyMy promise, 16then I will do this to you: I will bring sudden fear on you. I will bring sickness that makes you weak and sad. You will plant your seed for nothing. Your enemies will eat it. 17I will turn MymyMy face against you. You will be beaten by your enemies. Those who hate you will rule over you. You will run even when no one chases you.

18If you still do not listen to MemeMe, I will punish you seven times more for your sins. 19I will break your strong pride. I will make the sky like iron and the ground like bronze. 20Your strength will be used up for nothing. Your land will not grow food. The trees will not give fruit.

21If you walk against MemeMe and will not listen to MemeMe, I will hit you seven times more, as your sins deserve. 22I will send wild animals against you. They will take your children and kill your animals. There will be fewer of you. Your roads will be empty.

23If these things do not teach you to obey MemeMe, and you walk against MemeMe, 24then I will also walk against you in anger. I will punish you seven times more for your sins. 25I will bring a sword against you that pays back MymyMy broken promise. You will gather in your cities. I will send sickness among you. You will be given into the hand of the enemy. 26When I take away your bread, ten women will bake bread in one oven. They will weigh out your bread. You will eat but not be full.

27If you still do not listen to MemeMe, and you walk against MemeMe, 28I will walk against you in MymyMy burning anger. I will punish you seven times more for your sins. 29You will eat the flesh of your sons and daughters. 30I will destroy your worship hills. I will cut down your idol poles. I will throw your dead bodies on the dead bodies of your idols. I will hate you. 31I will make your cities empty. I will ruin your holy places. I will not accept your sweet gifts. 32I will make the land empty. Your enemies who live there will be shocked by it. 33I will scatter you among the nations. I will send enemies with swords after you. Your land will be empty. Your cities will be ruins.

34Then the land will enjoy its rest. All the time it is empty, you will be in your enemies' land. Then the land will rest and enjoy its rest. 35All the time it is empty, it will rest. It did not rest on your rest days when you lived there.

36The people left among you will be scared in your enemies' lands. The sound of a leaf will make them run. They will run like running from a sword. They will fall even when no one chases them. 37They will trip over each other like running from a sword, even though no one chases. You will not be able to stand before your enemies. 38You will die among the nations. Your enemies' land will destroy you. 39The people left among you will waste away because of their sin in your enemies' lands. They will waste away for their fathers' sins too.

God Will Not Forget His People

40They must tell about their sin and their fathers' sin. They were not faithful to MemeMe. They walked against MemeMe. 41So I will also walk against them in anger. I will bring them into their enemies' land. Then if they stop being proud, they will pay for their sin.

42Then I will remember MymyMy promise with Jacob. I will also remember MymyMy promise with Isaac and with Abraham. I will remember the land. 43They will leave the land. It will enjoy its rest while it is empty. They will pay for their sin. This is because they hated MymyMy rules and were sick of MymyMy laws.

44Even then, when they are in their enemies' land, I will not hate them or destroy them. I will not break MymyMy promise with them. I am the LORD their God. 45I will remember MymyMy first promise. I brought them out of Egypt so all people could see. I did this to be their God. I am the LORD.

The Laws God Gave

46These are the rules, laws, and teachings the LORD gave between HimselfhimselfHimself and the Israelites through Moses on Mount Sinai.
