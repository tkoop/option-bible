Elisha Makes an Axe Head Float

1The group of prophets said to Elisha, "Look, the place where we live with you is too small for us."

2They said, "Let us go to the Jordan River. Each of us can take one log. We will build a place to live there."

Elisha said, "Go."

3One of them said, "Please come with your servants."

Elisha said, "I will go."

4So he went with them. They came to the Jordan and cut down trees.

5As one man was cutting a log, the iron axe head fell into the water. He cried out, "Oh no, my master! It was borrowed!"

6Elisha asked, "Where did it fall?" The man showed him the place. Elisha cut a stick and threw it there. The iron floated.

7Elisha said, "Pick it up." The man reached out his hand and took it.

Elisha Helps Israel's King

8The king of Aram was at war with Israel. He talked with his servants. He said, "We will camp at a certain place."

9But the man of God sent a message to the king of Israel. He said, "Be careful. Do not pass by that place, because the Arameans are coming down there."

10The king of Israel sent men to the place the man of God warned him about. He kept watch there. This happened more than once or twice.

11The king of Aram was upset about this. He called his servants and said, "Tell me! Which of us is on the side of the king of Israel?"

12One servant said, "None of us, my lord the king. Elisha the prophet in Israel tells the king of Israel the words you say in your bedroom."

13The king said, "Go and see where he is. I will send men to get him."

They told him, "HeheHe is in Dothan."

14So he sent horses, chariots, and a strong army there. They came at night and surrounded the city.

15Elisha's helper got up early. He went out and saw an army with horses and chariots all around the city. He said, "Oh no, my master! What shall we do?"

16Elisha said, "Do not be afraid. Those who are with us are more than those who are with them."

17Elisha prayed, "ThetheThe LORD, please open his eyes so he can see." The LORD opened the young man's eyes. He saw the hill full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.

18When the Arameans came down to him, Elisha prayed to the LORD. He said, "Please strike this nation with blindness." The LORD struck them with blindness, just as Elisha said.

19Elisha said to them, "This is not the right road, and this is not the right city. Follow me. I will lead you to the man you are looking for." He led them to Samaria.

20When they reached Samaria, Elisha said, "ThetheThe LORD, open their eyes so they can see." The LORD opened their eyes. They saw they were in the middle of Samaria.

21The king of Israel saw them. He said to Elisha, "MymyMy father, shall I strike them down? Shall I strike them down?"

22Elisha said, "Do not strike them. You would not strike men you captured with your sword and bow. Put bread and water in front of them. Let them eat and drink. Then let them go back to their master."

23The king made a big feast for them. They ate and drank. Then he sent them back to their master. After that, small groups of Arameans did not come into Israel again.

Aram Attacks Samaria

24Later, Ben-Hadad king of Aram gathered all his army. He went up and surrounded Samaria.

A Terrible Famine

25There was a terrible famine in Samaria. The city was under attack. A donkey's head cost 80 pieces of silver. A small cup of dove droppings cost 5 pieces of silver.

26The king of Israel was walking on the wall. A woman cried out to him, "Help, my lord the king!"

27He said, "If the LORD does not help you, how can I help you? I have no food from the threshing floor and no wine from the winepress."

28The king asked her, "What is wrong?"

She said, "ThetheThe other woman told me to give her my son. She said we could eat him today and eat her son tomorrow."

29She said, "So we cooked my son and ate him. The next day I asked her to give me her son so we could eat him, but she hid her son."

30When the king heard the woman's words, he tore his clothes. He was walking on the wall. The people saw he wore sackcloth under his clothes.

The King Blames Elisha

31He said, "May God punish me if Elisha son of Shaphat still has his head today!"

32Elisha was sitting in his house. The elders were sitting with him. The king sent a man ahead. Before the messenger came, Elisha said to the elders, "Do you see how this son of a murderer has sent someone to cut off my head? When the messenger comes, shut the door. Hold him at the door. Don't you hear his master's feet behind him?"

33While he was still speaking, the messenger came down to him. The messenger said, "This trouble is from the LORD. Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?"
