Elijah Runs from Jezebel

1Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah had done. He told her how Elijah had killed all the prophets with a sword. 2Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah. The messenger said, "I swear by the gods that by this time tomorrow you will be dead like those prophets."

3Elijah was afraid. He got up and ran to save his life. He went to Beersheba in Judah. There he left his servant. 4Elijah walked one day into the desert. He sat under a bush. He asked to die. He said, "I have had enough, the LORD. Take my life. I am no better than my fathers."

God Takes Care of Elijah

5Elijah lay down under the bush and slept. An angel touched him and said, "Get up and eat." 6Elijah looked up. He saw bread baked on hot stones and a jar of water by his head. He ate and drank. Then he lay down again. 7The angel of the LORD came a second time. He touched Elijah and said, "Get up and eat. The journey is too long for you." 8Elijah got up. He ate and drank. Then he walked for forty days and forty nights. He reached Mount Horeb, the mountain of God.

God Talks with Elijah

9There Elijah went into a cave and stayed. The LORD spoke to him. He said, "Elijah, what are you doing here?"

After the wind came an earthquake. But the LORD was not in the earthquake. 12After the earthquake came fire. But the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire came a soft, quiet voice.

13When Elijah heard it, he pulled his cloak over his face. He went out and stood at the mouth of the cave. Then a voice said to him, "Elijah, what are you doing here?"

God Gives Elijah New Work

15The LORD said to him, "Go back the way you came. Go to the desert near Damascus. When you get there, make Hazael king over Aram. 16Also make Jehu son of Nimshi king over Israel. And choose Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah to be prophet after you. 17Jehu will kill anyone who escapes from Hazael. Elisha will kill anyone who escapes from Jehu. 18But I will leave seven thousand people in Israel. They have not bowed down to Baal. They have not worshiped him."

Elisha Follows Elijah

19Elijah left there. He found Elisha son of Shaphat. Elisha was plowing with twelve teams of oxen. He was with the last team. Elijah walked by him and threw his cloak on him.

20Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah. He said, "Let me say goodbye to my father and mother. Then I will follow you."

Elijah said, "Go back. I did not force you to come."

21Elisha went back. He took his team of oxen and killed them. He cooked the meat over a fire made from the yokes. He gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he got up and followed Elijah. He helped Elijah.
