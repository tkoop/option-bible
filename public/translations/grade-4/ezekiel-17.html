God Gives Ezekiel a Riddle

1The word of the the LORD came to me. He said,

2"SonsonSon of man, tell the people of Israel this riddle and story.

3"Say to them: This is what the Lord the the LORD says. A big eagle with big wings and long feathers came to Lebanon. It took the top of a cedar tree. 4It broke off the top shoot and carried it to a land of traders. It planted it in a city of traders. 5It also took a seed and planted it in good soil. It put it by plenty of water, like a willow tree. 6The seed grew into a low vine. Its branches turned toward the eagle. Its roots stayed under it. It became a vine and grew branches and shoots.

7"But another big eagle with big wings and many feathers came. This vine turned its roots toward him. It sent its branches to him. It wanted him to water it. 8It was planted in good soil by plenty of water. It could grow branches and fruit. It could become a fine vine.

9"Say: This is what the Lord the the LORD says. Will it grow well? No. He will pull up its roots. He will take off its fruit. It will dry up. All its leaves will dry up. It will not take a strong arm or many people to pull it up by the roots. 10It is planted, but will it grow well? No. The east wind will hit it, and it will dry up. It will dry up where it grew."

The Meaning of the Riddle

11Then the word of the the LORD came to me again. He said,

12"Tell the stubborn people: Do you not know what these things mean? Tell them: The king of Babylon came to Jerusalem. He took its king and its leaders. He brought them to Babylon. 13He took a man from the king's family. He made an agreement with him. He made him promise to be faithful. He also took the leaders of the land. 14He did this so the kingdom would stay weak. It could not rise up again. The king had to keep the agreement to stay alive.

15"But the king turned against Babylon. He sent men to Egypt to get horses and many soldiers. Will he succeed? Will a man who does this get away? Can he break the agreement and still get away? 16As surely as I live, says the Lord the the LORD, he will die in Babylon. He will die in the land of the king who made him king. He did not respect that king's promise. He broke his agreement. 17Pharaoh and his big army will not help him in war. They will build ramps and walls to attack the city and kill many people. 18He broke his promise. He said he would keep it, but he did all these things. He will not get away.

19"So the Lord the the LORD says: As surely as I live, I will punish him for the promise he did not respect and the agreement he broke. 20I will spread MymyMy net over him. He will be caught in MymyMy trap. I will bring him to Babylon. I will judge him there for turning against MemeMe. 21All his soldiers who run away will die by the sword. The rest will be scattered everywhere. Then you will know that I am the the LORD. I have spoken."

A New Tree

22"This is what the Lord the the LORD says: I will take a small branch from the top of a cedar tree and plant it. I will break off a soft shoot from its top branches and plant it on a high mountain. 23I will plant it on the high mountains of Israel. It will grow branches and fruit. It will become a great cedar tree. Birds of every kind will nest under it. They will rest in the shade of its branches. 24Then all the trees in the field will know that I am the the LORD. I bring the tall tree down and make the short tree tall. I dry up the green tree and make the dry tree grow. I, the the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it."
