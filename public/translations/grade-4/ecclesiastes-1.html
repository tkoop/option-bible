Words from the Teacher

1These are the words of the TeacherteacherTeacher. He was David's son. He was king in Jerusalem. 2The TeacherteacherTeacher says, "Everything is empty! Yes, everything is empty!"

3What good does a person get from all his hard work under the sun? 4One group of people comes, and another goes. But the earth stays forever. 5The sun rises. The sun goes down. Then it hurries back to where it rises. 6The wind blows south. Then it turns north. It goes around and around. Then it comes back again. 7All rivers run to the sea, but the sea is never full. The water goes back to where rivers start. Then it flows again. 8Everything is tiring. No one can say it all. The eye is never full from seeing. The ear is never full from hearing.

Nothing Is New

9What happened before will happen again. What was done before will be done again. Nothing is new under the sun. 10Someone may say, "Look! This is new!" But it was already here long ago, before we lived. 11No one remembers people from long ago. People later on will not be remembered by those who come after them.

The Teacher Searches for Wisdom

12I, the TeacherteacherTeacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem. 13I decided to use my mind to seek and search out wisdom about everything done in the world. God gave people a hard job. They must work at it. 14I have seen everything done under the sun. It is all empty. It is like chasing the wind. 15A bent thing cannot be made straight. A missing thing cannot be counted. 16I said in my heart, "I have grown in wisdom more than anyone who ruled Jerusalem before me. My mind has learned much wisdom and knowledge." 17I set my mind to know wisdom. I also wanted to know foolish and silly things. I saw that this too is like chasing the wind. 18Much wisdom brings much trouble. More knowledge brings more sadness.
