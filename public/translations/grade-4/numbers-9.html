The Passover

1In the first month of the second year after the Israelites left Egypt, the the LORD spoke to Moses in the Sinai desert. 2He said, “Tell the Israelites to keep the Passover at the right time. 3Keep it on the fourteenth day of this month, in the evening. Keep it by all its rules.”

4So Moses told the Israelites to keep the Passover. 5They kept the Passover in the Sinai desert. They did it on the fourteenth day of the first month, in the evening. They did everything the the LORD had told Moses.

6Some men could not keep the Passover that day because they were unclean from touching a dead body. 7They came to Moses and Aaron that day. They said, “We are unclean because of a dead body. Why should we be kept from bringing the the LORD’s gift at the right time with the other Israelites?”

8Moses said to them, “Wait here. I will find out what the the LORD tells us to do.”

9Then the the LORD spoke to Moses. He said, “Speak to the Israelites. 10If any man or any of his family in the future is unclean because of a dead body, or is far away on a trip, he may still keep the Passover for the the LORD. 11He must keep it on the fourteenth day of the second month, in the evening. He must eat it with bread made without yeast and bitter herbs. 12He must not leave any of it until morning. He must not break a bone. He must keep the Passover by all its rules. 13But a man who is clean and not on a trip, but does not keep the Passover, must be cut off from his people. He did not bring the the LORD’s gift at the right time. He will be guilty. 14If a foreigner living with you keeps the Passover to the the LORD, he must keep it by the same rules. There is one rule for the foreigner and for the person born in the land.”

The Cloud Over the Meeting Tent

15On the day the meeting tent was set up, the cloud covered it. At night the cloud looked like fire over the tent. It stayed that way until morning. 16The cloud always looked like that. At night it looked like fire.

17When the cloud rose from the tent, the Israelites moved on. Where the cloud stayed, the Israelites camped. 18The Israelites moved at the the LORD’s word, and they camped at the the LORD’s word. They camped as long as the cloud stayed over the tent. 19Even if the cloud stayed over the tent for many days, the Israelites did what the the LORD said and did not move. 20Sometimes the cloud stayed over the tent for only a few days. Then at the the LORD’s word they camped, and at the the LORD’s word they moved. 21Sometimes the cloud stayed from evening to morning. When it rose in the morning, they moved. Whether by day or by night, when the cloud rose, they moved. 22Whether the cloud stayed for two days, a month, or a longer time, the Israelites stayed camped and did not move. When it rose, they moved. 23They camped at the the LORD’s word, and they moved at the the LORD’s word. They did what the the LORD said, just as the the LORD had told them through Moses.
