1After these things, King Ahasuerus made Haman very important. Haman was the son of Hammedatha. He was an Agagite. The king gave him a high seat. It was above all the other leaders with the king.

2All the king's workers at the gate bowed down to Haman. They did this because the king said to do it. But Mordecai did not bow down or worship Haman.

3The king's workers at the gate said to Mordecai, "Why do you disobey the king's rule?"

4Day after day they said this to him. He did not listen. So they told Haman. They wanted to see if Mordecai would keep refusing. Mordecai had told them he was a Jew.

5Haman saw that Mordecai did not bow down or worship him. Haman was very angry.

6They told Haman who Mordecai's people were. Haman did not want to hurt only Mordecai. He wanted to destroy all the Jews in King Ahasuerus's land.

Haman Plans to Destroy the Jews

7In the twelfth year of King Ahasuerus, in the first month, Nisan, they cast lots. This was called Pur. They cast them before Haman. They did this day after day and month after month. The lot chose the twelfth month, Adar.

8Haman said to King Ahasuerus, "There is one people. They are spread out among the people in all your lands. Their laws are different from all other people. They do not obey the king's laws. It is not good for you to let them stay. 9If it pleases the king, write an order to destroy them. I will pay ten thousand talents of silver into the king's treasury. The king's workers can take it."

10The king took his special ring from his hand. He gave it to Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite. Haman was the enemy of the Jews.

11The king said to Haman, "ThetheThe silver is given to you. The people are given to you too. Do with them what seems good to you."

12On the thirteenth day of the first month, the king's writers were called. They wrote all that Haman told them to write. They sent it to the rulers of each land. They wrote to each land in its own writing. They wrote to each people in its own language. They wrote it in the name of King Ahasuerus. They sealed it with the king's ring.

13Letters were sent by messengers to all the king's lands. They told the people to destroy, kill, and wipe out all the Jews. This included young and old, women and children. It was to happen in one day. That day was the thirteenth day of Adar, the twelfth month. The people could take the Jews' things.

14A copy of the writing was given as a law in every land. It was shown to all the people. They were to be ready for that day.

15The messengers went out quickly. They did what the king said. The law was given in Susa. The king and Haman sat down to drink. The city of Susa was confused.
