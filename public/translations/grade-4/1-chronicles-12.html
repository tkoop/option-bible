Men Who Joined David at Ziklag

1These are the men who came to David at Ziklag while he was still hiding from Saul son of Kish. They were strong helpers in war. 2They could shoot arrows and throw stones with either hand. They were from Benjamin, Saul's tribe.

3Ahiezer was the leader, and Joash, sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Beracah; and Jehu the Anathothite. 4Ishmaiah the Gibeonite was a strong fighter among the thirty and their leader. Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite. 5Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite. 6Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam the Korahites. 7Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham from Gedor.

Gadite Warriors

8Some Gadites joined David at the stronghold in the desert. They were strong soldiers, ready for war. They could use shields and spears. Their faces were like lions' faces, and they were as fast as deer on the mountains. 9Ezer was first, Obadiah second, Eliab third. 10Mishmannah fourth, Jeremiah fifth. 11Attai sixth, Eliel seventh. 12Johanan eighth, Elzabad ninth. 13Jeremiah tenth, Machbannai eleventh. 14These Gadites were army leaders. The least could fight one hundred men, and the greatest one thousand. 15These are the men who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was full and overflowed its banks. They chased away everyone in the valleys, to the east and to the west.

Benjaminites and Judahites Come to David

16Some Benjaminites and Judahites came to David at the stronghold.

17David went out to meet them and said, "If you come in peace to help me, my heart will be one with yours. But if you come to trick me and hand me over to my enemies, even though I have done no wrong, may the God of our fathers see it and judge."

18Then the SpiritspiritSpirit came on Amasai, the leader of the thirty, and he said, "We are yours, David! We are with you, son of Jesse! Peace, peace to you, and peace to those who help you! For your God helps you." So David welcomed them and made them leaders of his army.

Men from Manasseh

19Some men from Manasseh joined David when he went with the Philistines to fight Saul. But they did not help the Philistines, because the Philistine leaders sent David away. They said, "HeheHe may turn against us and go back to his master Saul." 20When David went to Ziklag, these men from Manasseh joined him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, leaders of thousands in Manasseh. 21They helped David fight the raiders, because they were all strong, brave men and leaders in the army. 22Day after day men came to help David until his camp was as big as God's camp.

Leaders Come to Hebron

23These are the numbers of the leaders who came to David at Hebron to turn Saul's kingdom over to him, as the LORD had said.

24From Judah: 6,800 men ready for war, carrying shields and spears. 25From Simeon: 7,100 strong soldiers. 26From Levi: 4,600. 27Jehoiada, leader of Aaron's family, came with 3,700 men. 28Zadok, a brave young man, came with 22 leaders from his father's family. 29From Benjamin, Saul's relatives: 3,000. Most of them had stayed loyal to Saul's family until then. 30From Ephraim: 20,800 strong soldiers, famous men in their families. 31From the half tribe of Manasseh: 18,000 men chosen by name to make David king. 32From Issachar: 200 leaders who understood the times and knew what Israel should do. All their relatives followed their advice. 33From Zebulun: 50,000 soldiers ready for war, with all kinds of weapons. They were loyal and not divided. 34From Naphtali: 1,000 leaders, with 37,000 men carrying shields and spears. 35From Dan: 28,600 men ready for war. 36From Asher: 40,000 soldiers ready for war. 37From east of the Jordan, from Reuben, Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh: 120,000 men with all kinds of weapons.

Israel Makes David King

38All these soldiers came to Hebron with one heart to make David king over all Israel. The rest of Israel also agreed with one heart to make David king. 39They stayed there three days, eating and drinking, because their relatives had prepared food for them. 40Also, people near them, as far as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. They brought flour, fig cakes, raisins, wine, oil, cattle, and sheep. There was great joy in Israel.
