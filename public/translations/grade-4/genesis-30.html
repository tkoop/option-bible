Rachel and Leah Want Children

1Rachel saw that she had no children for Jacob. She was jealous of her sister. She said to Jacob, "Give me children, or I will die."

2Jacob got angry with Rachel. He said, "I am not God. He kept you from having children."

3Rachel said, "Here is my servant Bilhah. Sleep with her. She can have children for me. Then I can have a family through her."

4So she gave Bilhah to Jacob as his wife. He slept with her.

5Bilhah became pregnant. She had a son for Jacob.

6Rachel said, "God has helped me. He heard my voice and gave me a son." So she named him Dan.

7Bilhah became pregnant again. She had a second son for Jacob.

8Rachel said, "I have fought hard with my sister, and I won." She named him Naphtali.

9Leah saw that she had stopped having children. She took her servant Zilpah and gave her to Jacob as his wife.

10Zilpah had a son for Jacob.

11Leah said, "What good luck!" She named him Gad.

12Zilpah had a second son for Jacob.

13Leah said, "I am happy! Women will call me happy." She named him Asher.

14During wheat harvest, Reuben found mandrake plants in a field. He brought them to his mother Leah. Rachel said to Leah, "Please give me some of your son's mandrakes."

15Leah said, "YouyouYou took my husband. Now you want my son's mandrakes too?"

Rachel said, "Then he can sleep with you tonight for the mandrakes."

16Jacob came from the field in the evening. Leah went out to meet him. She said, "YouyouYou must sleep with me tonight. I paid for you with my son's mandrakes." So he slept with her that night.

17God listened to Leah. She became pregnant and had a fifth son for Jacob.

18Leah said, "God has given me my reward, because I gave my servant to my husband." She named him Issachar.

19Leah became pregnant again. She had a sixth son for Jacob.

20Leah said, "God has given me a good gift. Now my husband will honourhonourhonor me, because I have given him six sons." She named him Zebulun.

21Later she had a daughter. She named her Dinah.

22Then God remembered Rachel. He listened to her and opened her womb.

23She became pregnant and had a son. She said, "God has taken away my shame."

24She named him Joseph. She said, "May the LORD add another son to me."

Jacob Wants to Go Home

25After Rachel had Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, "Send me away. Let me go to my home and my land. 26Give me my wives and children. I worked for you for them. Let me go. You know how hard I worked for you."

27Laban said, "If you are pleased with me, stay. I know the LORD has blessed me because of you."

28He said, "NamenameName your pay, and I will pay it."

29Jacob said, "YouyouYou know how I worked for you and how your animals have done while with me. 30You had little before I came. Now you have much more. The LORD has blessed you because of me. But when can I take care of my own family?"

31Laban said, "What should I give you?"

Jacob said, "Don't give me anything. If you do this one thing for me, I will keep taking care of your flocks. 32Let me go through all your flocks today. I will take every spotted and speckled sheep, every dark lamb, and every spotted or speckled goat. That will be my pay. 33In the future, you can check my honesty. Any goat that is not spotted or speckled, or any lamb that is not dark, if it is with me, you can say I stole it."

34Laban said, "Good. Let it be as you say."

35That day Laban took out the male goats that were streaked or spotted. He took all the speckled or spotted female goats, every one with white on it, and all the dark lambs. He gave them to his sons.

36Then he put a three-day journey between himself and Jacob. Jacob took care of the rest of the flocks.

Jacob's Plan for the Flocks

37Jacob took fresh branches from poplar, almond, and plane trees. He peeled white stripes in them, so the white inside showed.

38He put the branches in the water troughs where the flocks came to drink. They mated when they came to drink.

39The flocks mated in front of the branches. They had young that were streaked, speckled, and spotted.

40Jacob set the young animals apart. He made the flocks face the streaked and dark animals in Laban's flocks. He kept his own flocks separate. He did not put them with Laban's flocks.

41Whenever the strong animals were ready to mate, Jacob put the branches in the troughs in front of them. They mated near the branches.

42But when the animals were weak, he did not put the branches there. So the weak young belonged to Laban, and the strong ones belonged to Jacob.

43Jacob became very, very rich. He had large flocks, female and male servants, camels, and donkeys.
