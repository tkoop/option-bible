Solomon's Best Song

1This is Solomon's best song.

The Woman Speaks

2"Kiss me with your lips. Your love is better than wine. 3Your oils smell sweet. Your name is like perfume poured out. So young women love you. 4Pull me along with you, and let's run. The king brought me into his rooms. We will be glad and happy in you. We will praise your love more than wine. It is right that they love you. 5I am dark and beautiful, young women of Jerusalem. I am like Kedar's tents and Solomon's curtains. 6Do not stare at me because I am dark. The sun has burned me. My mother's sons were angry at me. They made me guard the grape fields, but I did not guard my own. 7Tell me, my love, where you feed your flock. Where do you let them rest at noon? Why should I be like someone hiding her face near your friends' sheep?"

The Man Speaks

8"If you do not know, most beautiful of women, follow the sheep's tracks. Feed your young goats near the shepherds' tents. 9My love, you are like Pharaoh's horse among chariots. 10Your cheeks are pretty with earrings. Your neck is pretty with bead necklaces. 11We will make gold earrings for you with silver beads."

The Woman Speaks Again

12"While the king rests on his bed, my perfume gives its sweet smell. 13My love is like a little bag of sweet perfume resting on my chest. 14My love is like a bunch of flowers from the gardens of En Gedi."

The Man Speaks Again

15"Look, you are beautiful, my love. Look, you are beautiful. Your eyes are like doves."

The Woman Speaks Once More

16"Look, you are beautiful, my beloved. You are so pleasant. Our bed is fresh and green. 17The beams of our house are cedar. Our roof is made of pine."
