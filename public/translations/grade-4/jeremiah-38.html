Jeremiah Warns the People

1Four men named Shephatiah, Gedaliah, Jucal, and Pashhur heard what Jeremiah was saying to all the people. 2He said, "This is what the LORD says: Anyone who stays in this city will die by sword, hunger, or sickness. But anyone who goes out to the Babylonians will live. He will keep his life like something taken in war, and he will live. 3This is what the LORD says: This city will be given to the army of the king of Babylon. They will take it."

Some Leaders Want Jeremiah Killed

4Then the officials said to the king, "This man should be put to death. He is making the soldiers and the people lose heart by saying these things. He does not want good for the people. He wants to hurt them."

5The king said, "HeheHe is in your hands. I cannot stop you."

Jeremiah Is Thrown into a Pit

6So they took Jeremiah and threw him into the pit of Malchijah, the king's son. It was in the courtyard of the guards. They let him down with ropes. There was no water in the pit, only mud, and Jeremiah sank in the mud.

A Servant Helps Jeremiah

7Ebed-Melech the Cushite was a servant in the king's palace. He heard that they had put Jeremiah in the pit. The king was sitting at the Benjamin GategateGate. 8Ebed-Melech went out from the palace and spoke to the king.

9He said, "MymyMy lord the king, these men did wrong to Jeremiah the prophet. They threw him into the pit to die there of hunger, because there is no more bread in the city."

10The king told Ebed-Melech, "Take thirty men from here and pull Jeremiah the prophet out of the pit before he dies."

11So Ebed-Melech took the men with him. He went to a room under the storehouse in the palace. He took old rags and old clothes and lowered them on ropes to Jeremiah in the pit. 12He called down, "Put these rags and old clothes under your arms to pad the ropes." Jeremiah did so. 13They pulled Jeremiah up with the ropes and lifted him out of the pit. Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guards.

The King Talks with Jeremiah

14King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah the prophet and had him brought to the third entrance of the the LORD's house. The king said to Jeremiah, "I want to ask you something. Do not hide anything from me."

15Jeremiah said, "If I tell you, you will kill me. And if I give advice, you won't listen to me."

16But King Zedekiah swore to Jeremiah secretly, "As sure as the LORD lives, who gave us breath, I will not kill you or hand you over to those men who want to kill you."

Jeremiah Gives a Warning

17Then Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, "This is what the LORD, the LORD All-Powerful, the God of Israel, says: If you go out to the officials of the king of Babylon, you will live. This city will not be burned. You and your family will live. 18But if you do not go out, this city will be given to the Babylonians. They will burn it, and you will not escape."

19Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "I am afraid of the Jews who joined the Babylonians. The Babylonians might hand me over to them, and they might hurt me."

20Jeremiah said, "They will not hand you over. Obey the LORD's voice in what I say. Then things will go well for you, and you will live. 21But if you refuse to go out, this is what the LORD has shown me: 22All the women left in the king's palace in Judah will be brought out to the Babylonian officials. Those women will say about you, 'Your friends tricked you and got the best of you. Your feet sank in the mud, and they turned away.' 23All your wives and children will be led out to the Babylonians. You will not escape. You will be caught by the king of Babylon, and this city will be burned."

The King Keeps It Secret

24Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "Do not tell anyone about this talk, or you will die. 25If the officials hear that I talked with you, they may come and say, 'Tell us what you said to the king and what he said to you. Do not hide it, or we will kill you.' 26Then you are to say, 'I was just asking the king not to send me back to Jonathan's house to die there.'"

27When all the officials came to Jeremiah and asked him questions, he told them everything the king had told him to say. They said nothing more to him, because no one had heard the talk.

Jerusalem Is Taken

28Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guards until Jerusalem was taken. That is how it happened when Jerusalem was taken.
