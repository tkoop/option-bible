The LORD Speaks After Aaron's Sons Die

1The LORD spoke to Moses after two of Aaron's sons died. They had come too close to the LORD, and they died.

2The LORD said to Moses, “Tell your brother Aaron not to go into the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace behind the curtain at just any time. He must not go before the mercy seat on the ark, or he will die. I will appear in a cloud over the mercy seat. 3This is how Aaron must come into the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. He must bring a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering.

4“Aaron must put on the holy linen clothes. He must wear a linen shirt, linen underclothes, a linen belt, and a linen turban. These are holy clothes. He must wash his body with water and then put them on. 5From the people of Israel, he must take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

6“Aaron must bring the bull for his sin offering. He must make things right for himself and for his family. 7He must take the two goats and bring them to the LORD at the door of the meeting tent.

8“Aaron must draw lots for the two goats. One lot is for the LORD, and one lot is for Azazel. 9Aaron must bring the goat that gets the lot for the LORD. He must offer it as a sin offering. 10The goat that gets the lot for Azazel must be brought alive to the LORD. It will be used to make things right. Then it will be sent into the desert for Azazel.

11“Aaron must bring the bull for his sin offering. He must make things right for himself and his family. Then he must kill the bull. 12He must take a fire pan full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD. He must take two handfuls of fine incense. He must bring them behind the curtain. 13He must put the incense on the fire before the LORD. The smoke from the incense must cover the mercy seat over the ark. Then he will not die.

14“He must take some of the bull's blood. He must sprinkle it with his finger on the front of the mercy seat, on the east side. He must sprinkle the blood seven times in front of the mercy seat. 15He must kill the goat for the people's sin offering. He must bring its blood behind the curtain. He must do with it as he did with the bull's blood. He must sprinkle it on the mercy seat and in front of the mercy seat.

16“He must make things right for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. The people of Israel have made it unclean with their sins. He must make things right for the meeting tent too. It stays among them even when they are unclean. 17No person may be in the meeting tent when Aaron goes in to make things right in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. He must stay out until Aaron comes out. Aaron must make things right for himself, his family, and all the people.

18“Then Aaron must go out to the altar before the LORD. He must make things right for it. He must take some of the bull's blood and some of the goat's blood. He must put it on the horns of the altar all around. 19He must sprinkle some of the blood on the altar with his finger seven times. This will clean the altar and make it holy. It will clean it from the sins of the people of Israel.

20“When Aaron has finished making things right for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, the meeting tent, and the altar, he must bring the live goat. 21Aaron must put both hands on the head of the live goat. He must tell God about all the wrong things the people of Israel have done. He must tell about all their sins. He must put their sins on the goat's head. Then he must send the goat into the desert with a man who is ready for this job. 22The goat will carry all their wrongs to a faraway place. The man will let the goat go in the desert.

23“Then Aaron must go into the meeting tent. He must take off the linen clothes he wore in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. He must leave them there. 24He must wash his body with water in a holy place. Then he must put on his own clothes. He must come out and offer his burnt offering and the people's burnt offering. He must make things right for himself and for the people.

25“He must burn the fat of the sin offering on the altar. 26The man who sent the goat away for Azazel must wash his clothes. He must wash his body with water. Then he may come back into the camp.

27“The bull and the goat for the sin offering must be taken outside the camp. Their blood was brought into the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace to make things right. Their skin, meat, and waste must be burned with fire. 28The person who burns them must wash his clothes. He must wash his body with water. Then he may come back into the camp.

29“This is a rule that lasts forever. On the tenth day of the seventh month, you must make yourselves humble. You must not do any work. This is for people born among you and for strangers living among you. 30On that day, I will make things right for you. I will make you clean. You will be clean from all your sins before the LORD. 31It is a Sabbath day of complete rest. You must make yourselves humble. This is a rule that lasts forever.

32“The priest who is chosen and set apart to serve in his father's place must make things right. He must wear the holy linen clothes. 33He must make things right for the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, the meeting tent, and the altar. He must make things right for the priests and all the people. 34This is a rule that lasts forever. Once a year, make things right for the people of Israel for all their sins.”

Aaron Obeys the LORD

Aaron did what the LORD told Moses.
