A Bad Thing I Saw

1I saw something bad in this world. It is a heavy load for people.

2God gives a man money, things, and respect. He has all he wants. But God does not let him enjoy them. Someone else enjoys them instead. This is empty and very sad. 3A man may have a hundred children and live many years. But if he is not happy with good things and does not get a good burial, I say a baby who dies before birth is better off than he. 4That baby comes into the world with no life and goes away in darkness. His name is hidden in darkness. 5He never saw the sun or knew anything. But he has more peace than the man who lived long. 6Even if a man lives two thousand years but does not enjoy his good things, all people still go to the same place.

Work and Wanting More

7A man works to eat, but he is never full. 8What good is a wise man more than a foolish man? What does a poor man gain by knowing how to act among living people? 9It is better to enjoy what the eyes see than to chase what the heart wants. This is also empty and useless.

God Is Stronger

10What is has already been named. What a person is has been known. No one can fight the OneoneOne who is stronger. 11Many words make things more empty. What good does that do for a person? 12Who knows what is good for a person in life, in the few days that pass like a shadow? Who can tell him what will happen after him in this world?
