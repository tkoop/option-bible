The Story of the Vineyard

1Jesus began to speak to them in stories. A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it. He dug a wine press. He built a tower. He rented the vineyard to farmers. Then he went away. 2At harvest time, he sent a servant to the farmers to get some fruit from the vineyard.

3The farmers took the servant, beat him, and sent him away with nothing.

4Then the man sent another servant. They hit him on the head and treated him badly.

5He sent another one, and they killed him. He sent many more. Some they beat. Some they killed.

6The man had one son left. He loved his son. He sent him last. He thought, “They will respect my son.”

7But the farmers said to each other, “This is the one who will get everything. Come, let’s kill him. Then the vineyard will be ours.”

8So they took him, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.
