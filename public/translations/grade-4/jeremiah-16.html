The LORD Tells Jeremiah Not to Marry

1The word of the the LORD came to me. 2The LORD said, “Do not get married. Do not have sons or daughters here. 3This is what the the LORD says about the children born here, and about their mothers and fathers in this land: 4They will die of bad sickness. They will not be cried for or buried. They will be like waste on the ground. They will die by war and by hunger. Their bodies will be food for birds and wild animals.”

Do Not Go to Houses of Sadness or Parties

5The LORD says, “Do not go into a house where people are sad for someone who died. Do not go to cry for them. Do not feel sorry for them. I have taken away MymyMy peace, MymyMy faithful love, and MymyMy mercy from these people, says the the LORD. 6Both important and unimportant people will die in this land. They will not be buried. No one will cry for them. No one will cut their skin or shave their heads for them. 7No one will bring food to comfort them because someone died. No one will give them a drink to comfort them for their father or mother. 8Do not go into a house where people are having a party to eat and drink.”

Joy Will Stop in This Place

9The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says, “While you watch, I will stop the happy sounds in this place. You will not hear the groom or the bride.”

The People Ask Why

10When you tell the people all this, they will ask you, “Why did the the LORD say such a big trouble will come to us? What have we done wrong? What sin have we done against the the LORD our God?”

Jeremiah Tells Them Why

11Then say to them, “Your fathers left MemeMe, says the the LORD. They followed other gods. They served them and bowed to them. They left MemeMe and did not obey MymyMy law. 12You have done worse than your fathers. Each of you follows his own stubborn, evil heart. You do not listen to MemeMe.”

The LORD Will Throw Them Out

13The LORD says, “So I will throw you out of this land into a land you and your fathers did not know. There you will serve other gods day and night. I will not be kind to you.”

The LORD Will Bring Them Back

14“So the days are coming, says the the LORD, when people will no longer say, ‘As surely as the the LORD lives, who brought the people of Israel up from Egypt.’ 15Instead, they will say, ‘As surely as the the LORD lives, who brought the people of Israel up from the north and from all the lands where HeheHe drove them.’ Then I will bring them back to their own land, which I gave to their fathers.”

The LORD Will Send Fishermen and Hunters

16The LORD says, “Look, I am sending many fishermen. They will catch the people. After that, I will send many hunters. They will hunt them from every mountain, hill, and crack in the rocks.”

The LORD Sees Their Sin

17“For MymyMy eyes see all their ways. They cannot hide from MemeMe. Their sin cannot hide from MymyMy eyes. 18First, I will pay them back double for their sin and guilt. They made MymyMy land unclean. They filled MymyMy land with dead bodies of their hated idols and their disgusting things.”

Jeremiah Prays to the LORD

19“The LORD, YouyouYou are my strength and my safe place. You are my help in trouble. Nations will come to YouyouYou from the ends of the earth. They will say, ‘Our fathers only had lies. Their idols were worthless and could not help.’”

The LORD Will Make Them Know His Name

20“Can people make gods for themselves? They are not real gods. 21So I will make them know. This time I will show them MymyMy power and MymyMy strength. Then they will know that MymyMy name is the the LORD.”
