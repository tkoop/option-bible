Hezekiah Is in Trouble

1When King Hezekiah heard this, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and went into the house of the the LORD. 2He sent Eliakim, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the writer, and the older priests. They were wearing sackcloth. He sent them to Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz. 3They said to him, "Hezekiah says today is a day of trouble, blame, and shame. We are like a mother ready to have a baby, but she has no strength to push. 4Maybe the the LORD your God heard what Rabshakeh said. The king of Assyria sent him to make fun of the living God. Maybe the the LORD your God will show he is wrong. So pray for the people who are left."

5King Hezekiah's servants came to Isaiah.

God Answers Through Isaiah

6Isaiah said to them, "Tell your master the the LORD says this: Do not be afraid of the words you heard. The servants of the king of Assyria made fun of MemeMe. 7Look, I will put a spirit in him. He will hear news and go home. Then I will make him die by a sword in his own land." 8Rabshakeh heard that the king of Assyria had left Lachish. He went back and found the king fighting against Libnah.

Sennacherib Threatens Hezekiah

9He heard that Tirhakah, king of Cush, was coming to fight him. So he sent messengers to Hezekiah. 10They said, "Tell Hezekiah, king of Judah: Do not let your God fool you. You trust in HimhimHim, but HeheHe says Jerusalem will not be given to the king of Assyria. Do not believe HimhimHim. 11Look, you have heard what the kings of Assyria did to all the lands. They destroyed them. Do you think you will be saved? 12Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed save them? Did they save Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar? 13Where is the king of Hamath? Where is the king of Arpad? Where is the king of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?"

14Hezekiah took the letters from the messengers and read them. Then he went up to the house of the the LORD and spread them out before the the LORD.

Hezekiah Prays to the LORD

15Hezekiah prayed to the the LORD. He said, 16"the the LORD of Hosts, God of Israel, YouyouYou sit above the cherubim. You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth. 17Please listen, the the LORD, and hear. Please look, the the LORD, and see. Hear all the words Sennacherib sent to mock the living God. 18It is true, the the LORD. The kings of Assyria have destroyed all the nations and their lands. 19They threw their gods into the fire. Those were not gods at all. They were made by people. They were made of wood and stone. So the Assyrians destroyed them. 20Now, the the LORD our God, save us from his hand. Then all the kingdoms of the earth will know that YouyouYou alone are the the LORD."

The LORD's Answer to Sennacherib

21Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah. He said, "This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Because you prayed to MemeMe about Sennacherib king of Assyria, I have heard you. 22This is what the the LORD says about him: The young woman of Zion looks down on you and laughs at you. The people of Jerusalem shake their heads at you. 23Who have you mocked and made fun of? Who did you shout at? You raised your eyes against the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel! 24By your servants you mocked the Lord. You said you took your many chariots up to the high mountains, to the far sides of Lebanon. You said you cut down its tallest cedars and its best cypress trees. You said you went to its farthest height and its thickest forest. 25You said you dug wells and drank water. You said that with the soles of your feet you dried up all the rivers of Egypt. 26Have you not heard? I did this long ago. I planned it long ago. Now I have made it happen. You turned strong cities into piles of rocks. 27The people who lived there were weak. They were afraid and put to shame. They were like grass in a field and green plants. They were like grass on a roof and grain burned before it grows. 28But I know when you sit down, when you go out, and when you come in. I know how angry you are at MemeMe. 29Because you are angry at MemeMe and your proud words reached MymyMy ears, I will put MymyMy hook in your nose and MymyMy bridle in your lips. I will make you go back the same way you came."

30"This will be a sign for you: This year you will eat what grows on its own. The second year you will eat what grows from that. But in the third year you will plant and harvest. You will plant grapevines and eat their fruit. 31The people left in Judah will grow roots down and bear fruit up. 32For a people will go out from Jerusalem. Survivors will come from Mount Zion. The the LORD of Hosts will do this."

33"So this is what the the LORD says about the king of Assyria: He will not come into this city. He will not shoot an arrow here. He will not come near it with a shield. He will not build a ramp against its walls. 34He will go back the same way he came. He will not come into this city," says the the LORD. 35"I will protect this city and save it. I will do it for MymyMy own sake and for the sake of MymyMy servant David."

The Assyrian Army Is Destroyed

36Then the angel of the the LORD went out. He struck 185,000 men in the Assyrian camp. In the morning, people got up early. They saw that all of them were dead. 37So Sennacherib king of Assyria left. He went home and stayed in Nineveh. 38While he was worshiping in the house of Nisroch his god, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him with a sword. They ran away to the land of Ararat. His son Esarhaddon became king after him.
