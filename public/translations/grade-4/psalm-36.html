A Song by David

1For the music leader. A song by David. David served the LORD. A thought came to my heart about bad people's sin: "They do not fear God."

2He thinks he is too good. He does not see his sin or hate it. 3His words are mean and full of lies. He stopped being wise and good. 4Even in bed he plans bad things. He walks a bad road. He does not say no to evil.
