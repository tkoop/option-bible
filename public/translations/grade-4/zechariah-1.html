God Tells His People to Come Back

1In the eighth month of the second year of King Darius, the LORD spoke to the prophet Zechariah. Zechariah was the son of Berechiah and the grandson of Iddo. The LORD said:

2"The LORD was very angry with your fathers. 3So tell them to come back to MemeMe, and I will come back to them. That is what the LORD of Hosts says. 4Do not be like your fathers. The old prophets told them to stop their bad ways and bad deeds, but they did not listen or pay attention to MemeMe. 5Where are your fathers now? Do the prophets live forever? 6But MymyMy words and MymyMy rules, which I gave to MymyMy servants the prophets, caught up with your fathers. They turned back and said that the LORD of Hosts did to them just what HeheHe planned, because of their ways and their deeds."

A Man on a Red Horse

7On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in the second year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Zechariah, the son of Berechiah and grandson of Iddo.

8I saw a vision at night. A man was riding a red horse. He stood among myrtle trees in a low place. Behind him were red, brown, and white horses.

9I asked, "What are these, my lord?"

The angel who talked with me said, "I will show you what they are."

10The man standing among the myrtle trees answered, "These are the ones the LORD has sent to walk through the earth."

11They answered the angel of the LORD who stood among the myrtle trees, "We have walked all through the earth. Look, all the earth is sitting still and at rest."

12Then the angel of the LORD said, "O the LORD of Hosts, how long will YouyouYou not show mercy to Jerusalem and the towns of Judah? You have been angry with them for seventy years."

13The LORD answered the angel who talked with me. He gave him good and comforting words.

14The angel who talked with me said, "Shout this message: The LORD of Hosts says HeheHe cares very much for Jerusalem and Zion. 15He is very angry with the nations that feel safe. He was only a little angry, but they made things worse. 16So the LORD says HeheHe has come back to Jerusalem with mercy. His house will be built there, says the LORD of Hosts. A measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem. 17Shout again: The LORD of Hosts says HishisHis towns will grow and do well again. The LORD will comfort Zion and choose Jerusalem again."

Four Horns and Four Builders

18I looked up and saw four horns.

19I asked the angel who talked with me, "What are these?"

He answered, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem."

20Then the LORD showed me four builders.

21I asked, "What are these coming to do?"

He answered, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, so no one could even lift his head. But these builders have come to scare those nations and knock down their power. They lifted their power against Judah to scatter it."
