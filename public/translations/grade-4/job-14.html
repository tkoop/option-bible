Job Talks About How Short Life Is

1"A man born to a woman lives only a short time and has lots of trouble. 2He grows like a flower and then fades away. He is like a shadow that quickly goes away. 3Do YouyouYou look at someone like that? Will YouyouYou bring me to court with YouyouYou?

4"No one can make something clean from something dirty. Not even one person. 5You decide how many days he will live. You know his months. You set his time, and he cannot go past it. 6Please look away from him and leave him alone. Then he can have some peace in his day, like a worker at the end of his job.

A Cut-Down Tree Can Grow Again

7"There is hope for a tree. If it is cut down, it can grow again. Its new shoots will not stop. 8Even if its roots are old in the ground and its stump dies in the dirt, 9it can grow again when water is near. It grows branches like a new plant.

10"But a man dies and is laid low. He takes his last breath, and then where is he? 11Water can leave a sea. A river can dry up and be gone. 12So a man lies down and does not get up again. He will not wake up until the sky is gone. He will not rise from his sleep.
