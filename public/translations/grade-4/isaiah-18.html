Trouble for a Faraway Land

1Trouble is coming to the land with buzzing wings, far past the rivers of Cush! 2That land sends messengers by sea in boats made of reeds. Go, fast messengers, to a tall people with smooth skin. They are feared far and wide. They are a strong nation with a strange language. Rivers split their land.

Everyone Will See the Sign

3All you people of the world, you who live on the earth, when a flag is raised on the mountains, you will see it. When a horn blows, you will hear it.

The LORD Speaks

4This is what the LORD says to me: "I will stay quiet and watch from MymyMy home. I will be like heat waves in the sun, and like a cloud of dew in the hot harvest time."

The Vine Is Cut Back
