About Spiritual Gifts

2You know that when you were not believers, you were led to worship fake gods that can't speak.

3So I tell you: no one who speaks by God's SpiritspiritSpirit says, "Jesus is cursed." And no one can say, "Jesus is Lord," unless the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit helps them.

Different Gifts, Same Spirit

4There are different kinds of gifts, but the same SpiritspiritSpirit gives them. 5There are different kinds of work to do, but the same Lord gives them. 6There are different kinds of power at work, but the same God works all of them in everyone. 7The SpiritspiritSpirit gives a gift to each person to help everyone.

8To one person the SpiritspiritSpirit gives wise words. To another, the SpiritspiritSpirit gives knowledge. 9To another, faith by the same SpiritspiritSpirit. To another, gifts to heal people by the one SpiritspiritSpirit. 10To another, power to do miracles. To another, the gift to speak for God. To another, the gift to tell good spirits from bad ones. To another, the gift to speak in new languages. To another, the gift to explain those languages.

11One and the same SpiritspiritSpirit does all these things. He gives each gift to each person the way HeheHe wants.

One Body, Many Parts

12A body is one, but it has many parts. Even with many parts, it is still one body. It is the same with Christ.

13We were all baptized by one SpiritspiritSpirit into one body. It doesn't matter if we are Jews or Greeks, slaves or free. We all received the one SpiritspiritSpirit.

14A body is not just one part. It has many parts.

15If the foot says, "I am not a hand, so I am not part of the body," it is still part of the body.

16If the ear says, "I am not an eye, so I am not part of the body," it is still part of the body.

17If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If the whole body were an ear, where would smell be?

God Puts the Parts Together

18But God put each part in the body just as HeheHe wanted.

19If all were one part, where would the body be?

20But now there are many parts, but one body.

We Need Each Other

21The eye can't say to the hand, "I don't need you!"

The head can't say to the feet, "I don't need you!"

22In fact, the parts that seem weaker are needed.

23We give more honourhonourhonor to parts we think are less important. The parts we keep covered get more respect.

24The parts that look fine don't need this. But God put the body together. He gave more honourhonourhonor to the part that lacked it.

25This is so the body does not break apart. Instead, the parts take care of each other.

26If one part hurts, all the parts hurt. If one part is honouredhonouredhonored, all the parts are glad.

The Body of Christ

27You are the body of Christ. You are parts of it.

Jobs in the Church

28In the church, God chose first apostles, second prophets, third teachers. Then HeheHe chose people who do miracles, people with gifts to heal, people who help others, people who lead, and people who speak in new languages.

29Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all do miracles?

30Do all have gifts to heal? Do all speak in new languages? Do all explain them?

31But you should want the greater gifts. And I will show you a way that is even better.
