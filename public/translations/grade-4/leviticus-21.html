Rules for Priests

1Then the LORD said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron's sons, the priests. Tell them: A priest must not make himself unclean by touching a dead person among his people. 2He may do it only for close family: his mother, father, son, daughter, or brother. 3He may also do it for his unmarried sister who is close to him, since she has no husband. 4But he must not make himself unclean for his wife's family. That would make him unclean. 5Priests must not shave their heads. They must not shave the edges of their beards. They must not cut their bodies. 6They must be holy to their God. They must not ruin the name of their God. They bring gifts burned in fire to the LORD. They bring the food of their God. So they must be holy. 7They must not marry a woman who sold herself for sex or a woman whose husband sent her away. A priest is holy to his God. 8You must treat him as holy, because he brings the food of your God. He will be holy to you, because I, the LORD, am holy. I make you holy. 9If a priest's daughter makes herself unclean by selling herself for sex, she brings shame on her father. She must be burned with fire.

Rules for the High Priest

10"The high priest is the one above his brothers. Oil was poured on his head to make him priest. He was chosen to wear the holy clothes. He must not let his hair hang loose. He must not tear his clothes. 11He must not go near any dead body. He must not make himself unclean, even for his father or mother. 12He must not leave God's holy place. He must not make God's holy place unclean. The oil that made him priest is on him. It belongs to his God. I am the LORD.

13"He must marry a woman who has never been married. 14He must not marry a widow, a divorced woman, or a woman who sold herself for sex. He may marry only a woman who has never been married, from his own people. 15Then he will not make his children unclean among his people. I am the LORD, who makes him holy."

Priests with Body Problems

16Then the LORD said to Moses, 17"Speak to Aaron and tell him: For all the years to come, no man from your family who has a body problem may come near to bring the food of his God. 18No man with a body problem may come near. Not a blind man, a lame man, a man with a scarred face, or a man with a body part that is too big or too small. 19No man with a broken foot or hand. 20No hunchback or dwarf. No man with a bad eye, a skin sore, scabs, or crushed private parts. 21No priest from Aaron's family who has a body problem may come near to bring gifts burned in fire to the LORD. He has a body problem. He must not come near to bring the food of his God. 22He may eat the most holy food of his God, and the holy food too. 23But he must not go near the curtain or the altar, because he has a body problem. Then he will not make MymyMy holy places unclean. I am the LORD, who makes them holy."

24So Moses told these things to Aaron, his sons, and all the people of Israel.
