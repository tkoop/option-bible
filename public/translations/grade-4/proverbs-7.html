Listen to Your Father

1My son, remember my words. Keep my rules with you. 2Keep my rules and live. Guard my teaching like your own eyes. 3Tie them on your fingers. Write them on your heart. 4Say to wisdom, “You are like a sister to me.” Call good sense your close friend. 5Wisdom and good sense will keep you safe from a woman who is not your wife. They will keep you from a woman from far away who uses sweet words to trick you.

A Young Man in Danger

6I looked out the window of my house. I looked through the bars. 7I saw some young men who did not think. I noticed a young man among the boys. He had no sense. 8He walked down the street near her corner. He took the road to her house. 9He went when it was getting dark, in the evening. It was the middle of a very dark night.

10Then a woman came to meet him. She was dressed like a woman who sells love, and her heart was tricky. 11She is loud and will not listen. She does not stay at home. 12She walks the streets and town squares. She waits at every corner to trap someone.

She Grabs Him and Speaks

13She grabbed him and kissed him. She looked bold and said to him:

“14I made my peace offerings today. I paid what I promised to God. 15So I came out to meet you. I looked for you, and I found you. 16I put nice covers on my bed. They are covers made of fine cloth from Egypt. 17I put perfume on my bed: myrrh, aloe, and cinnamon. 18Come, let us enjoy love until morning. Let us be happy together. 19My husband is not at home. He went on a long trip. 20He took a bag of money with him. He will come home when the moon is full.”

He Follows Her

21With her many words, she led him away. With her smooth talk, she made him go with her. 22He followed her right away, like an ox going to be killed. He was like a deer stepping into a trap. 23He kept going until an arrow hit his liver. He was like a bird rushing into a trap. He did not know it would cost him his life.

Listen, My Sons

24Now listen to me, my sons. Pay attention to my words. 25Do not let your heart turn to her ways. Do not wander on her paths. 26She has hurt many people. She has killed strong men. 27Her house leads down to the grave. It leads to the rooms of death.
