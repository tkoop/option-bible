God Sends Men to Punish the City

1Then I heard a loud voice say, “Bring the men who will punish the city. Each one has a weapon that can destroy in his hand.” 2Six men came from the upper gate that faces north. Each one had a weapon to kill in his hand. One man among them wore linen clothes and had a writer’s case at his waist. They came and stood by the bronze altar.

God’s Glory Moves to the Doorway

3The glory of Israel’s God had moved from the winged creature above it to the doorway of the house. He called to the man in linen clothes who had the writer’s case at his waist.

A Mark for Those Who Sigh and Cry

4The LORD said to him, “Go through the city, through Jerusalem, and put a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and cry because of all the bad things done there.”

5Then HeheHe said to the other men while I listened, “Follow him through the city and strike. Do not let your eye feel sorry, and do not pity anyone. 6Kill old men, young men, young women, women, and children, but do not touch anyone who has the mark. Start at MymyMy holy place.” So they started with the older men who were in front of the house.

The House Is Made Unclean

7Then HeheHe said to them, “Make the house unclean and fill the courtyards with dead people. Go out!” So they went out and hit people in the city.

Ezekiel Asks God for Mercy

8While they were killing, I was left alone. I fell on my face and cried out, “Ah, Lord the LORD! Will YouyouYou destroy all the people left in Israel when YouyouYou pour out YouryourYour anger on Jerusalem?”

God Will Not Show Mercy

9He said to me, “The people of Israel and Judah have done very, very bad things. The land is full of blood, and the city is full of violence. They say that the LORD has left the land and that the LORD does not see. 10So I will not look with pity or show mercy. I will let what they did come back on them.”

The Man in Linen Obeys

11Then the man in linen clothes who had the writer’s case at his waist came back and said, “I have done what YouyouYou told me.”
