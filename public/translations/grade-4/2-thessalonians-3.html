Ask People to Pray for Us

1Finally, brothers and sisters, pray for us. Pray that the Lord's word will spread fast and be praised, just as it was with you. 2Pray that we will be saved from bad and evil people. Not everyone trusts in God. 3But the Lord can be trusted. He will make you strong and keep you safe from the evil one. 4We trust in the Lord that you are doing what we tell you. We also trust that you will keep doing it. 5May the Lord help your hearts love God and wait with patience for Christ.

Stay Away from People Who Will Not Work

6Brothers and sisters, we tell you this in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. Stay away from any brother who lives in a wrong way and does not follow the teaching we gave you. 7You know you should copy us. We did not live in a wrong way when we were with you. 8We did not eat anyone's food for free. We worked hard day and night. We did this so we would not be a heavy load to any of you. 9We had the right to ask for help. But we worked to give you a good example to follow. We did this so you would follow it.

10When we were with you, we told you this: “If someone will not work, then he should not eat.” 11We hear that some of you live in a wrong way. They do no work. They only get into other people's business. 12In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, we tell and ask those people to work quietly. Then they can eat their own food.

Keep Doing Good

13Brothers and sisters, do not get tired of doing good.

How to Treat People Who Will Not Listen

14If someone does not obey what we say in this letter, mark that person. Do not spend time with him. Then he may feel ashamed. 15Do not treat him like an enemy. Warn him like a brother.

Final Words

16May the Lord of peace give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you. 17I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is a sign in every letter. This is how I write. 18May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.
