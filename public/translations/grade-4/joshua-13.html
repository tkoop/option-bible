Land Still to Take

1Joshua was old. He had lived many years. The LORD said to him, “You are old and have lived many years. But much land still needs to be taken.”

2This is the land that is left: all the land of the Philistines and the Geshurites. 3It goes from the Shihor River near Egypt to the border of Ekron in the north. This land counts as Canaanite land. It has the five Philistine rulers of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron. It also has the Avvites. 4To the south is all the land of the Canaanites. It goes from Mearah of the Sidonians to Aphek, up to the border of the Amorites. 5It has the land of the Gebalites and all Lebanon to the east. It goes from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.

6“All the people who live in the hills from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, and all the Sidonians, I will drive out before the Israelites. Just give the land to Israel as a gift, as I have told you. 7Now divide this land as a gift among the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”

Land East of the Jordan

8The other half of Manasseh, the Reubenites, and the Gadites had already received their land. Moses gave it to them east of the Jordan. He was the servant of the LORD. 9Their land went from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the flat land of Medeba up to Dibon. 10It included all the cities of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, up to the border of the Ammonites. 11It also included Gilead, the land of the Geshurites and Maacathites, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan up to Salecah. 12It included all the kingdom of Og in Bashan. Og ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei. He was one of the last of the Rephaim. Moses beat them and took their land.

13But the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites. So Geshur and Maacah still live among the Israelites today.

14But to the tribe of Levi, Moses gave no land. The gifts burned for the LORD, the God of Israel, are their land, just as HeheHe had promised them.

Land Given to Reuben

15This is what Moses gave to the family groups of the tribe of Reuben:

16Their land went from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the flat land near Medeba. 17It had Heshbon and all its cities on the flat land—Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 18Jahaz, Kedemoth, and Mephaath, 19Kiriathaim, Sibmah, and Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley, 20Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth.

21It had all the cities of the flat land and all the kingdom of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon. Moses had beaten Sihon and the leaders of Midian—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. These were the princes of Sihon who lived in the land. 22The Israelites also killed Balaam son of Beor, the fortune teller, with the sword, along with the others they killed. 23The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan. This was the land given to the family groups of Reuben, including the cities and towns.

Land Given to Gad

24This is what Moses gave to the family groups of the tribe of Gad:

25Their land was Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites up to Aroer, near Rabbah. 26It went from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir. 27In the valley, it had Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon. It also had the rest of the kingdom of Sihon, king of Heshbon. The Jordan was its border on the east.

28This was the land given to the family groups of Gad, including the cities and towns.

Land Given to Half of Manasseh

29This is what Moses gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh, that is, to the family groups of the half-tribe of Manasseh:

30Their land went from Mahanaim through all Bashan—all the kingdom of Og, king of Bashan. It included all the towns of Jair in Bashan, sixty cities. 31It had half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the royal cities of Og in Bashan. This was for the family groups of Machir son of Manasseh, for half of the family groups of Machir.

32These are the lands Moses gave on the plains of Moab, east of the Jordan, across from Jericho. 33But to the tribe of Levi, Moses gave no land. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their land, just as HeheHe had promised them.
