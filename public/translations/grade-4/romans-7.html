Released from the Law

1Don't you know, brothers? I talk to people who know the law. The law has power over a person only while that person is alive. 2A married woman must obey the law about her husband while he lives. But if her husband dies, she is free from that law. 3So if her husband is still alive and she marries another man, people call her a cheater. But if her husband dies, she is free from that law. She is not a cheater if she marries another man. 4My brothers, you also died to the law through Christ's body. Now you belong to another. You belong to HimhimHim who rose from the dead. This is so we can do good things for God. 5When we lived for our own wants, our sinful wants worked in our bodies. The law made them grow. They led to death. 6But now we died to what held us. We are free from the law. Now we serve God in a new way by the SpiritspiritSpirit. We do not serve in the old way of written rules.

The Law and Sin

7So what do we say? Is the law sin? No! But I would not have known sin without the law. I would not have known wanting what others have is wrong, if the law had not said, "Do not want what belongs to someone else." 8But sin used the command to make me want all kinds of things. Without the law, sin is dead. 9I once felt alive without the law. But when the command came, sin came alive, and I died. 10So the command that was meant to give life brought death to me. 11Sin used the command to trick me. It killed me through it. 12So the law is holy. The command is holy, right, and good. 13Did the good thing become death to me? No! Sin used what is good to bring death to me. This showed sin is truly sin. The command made sin very sinful.

Struggle with Sin

14We know the law is spiritual. But I am only human. I was sold to sin as a slave. 15I do not understand what I do. I want to do good, but I do not do it. I do the thing I hate. 16If I do what I do not want, I agree the law is good. 17So now I am not the one doing it. Sin lives in me. 18I know nothing good lives in me. I mean, nothing good lives in my sinful self. I want to do good, but I cannot do it. 19I do not do the good I want. I keep doing the bad I do not want. 20If I do what I do not want, it is not really me. Sin living in me does it.

21So I find this rule: When I want to do good, bad is right there with me. 22Deep inside, I am happy with God's law. 23But I see another rule in my body. It fights the law in my mind. It makes me a prisoner to the rule of sin in my body.

Who Will Save Me?

24What a sad man I am! Who will save me from this body that leads to death? 25Thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord! So I serve God's law with my mind. But with my sinful self, I serve the law of sin.
