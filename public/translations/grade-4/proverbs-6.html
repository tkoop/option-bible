Warning About Promising to Pay

1My son, if you promise to pay for your neighbourneighbourneighbor, or shake hands to back a stranger's debt, 2you are trapped by your own words. You are caught by what you said. 3Do this now, my son, to get free. You have fallen into your neighbourneighbourneighbor's hands. Go and humble yourself. Ask your neighbourneighbourneighbor to let you go. 4Do not let your eyes sleep. Do not let your eyelids rest. 5Get free like a deer from a hunter's hand. Get free like a bird from the hand of a bird catcher.

Learn From the Ant

6Go to the ant, you lazy person. Watch what it does. Learn from it and be wise. 7It has no boss, no leader, and no ruler. 8But it stores food in summer. It gathers food at harvest time. 9How long will you lie there, you lazy person? When will you get up from your sleep? 10A little sleep, a little rest, a little folding of your hands to sleep, 11and poverty will come to you like a robber. You will not have enough, like a man with a weapon.

The Bad Man

12A bad man is a troublemaker. He walks with twisted words. 13He winks with his eyes. He taps his feet. He points with his fingers. 14He has tricks in his heart. He always plans bad things. He stirs up fights. 15So his trouble will come on him fast. In a moment he will be broken, and no one can fix him.

Seven Things the LORD Hates

16There are six things the LORD hates. There are seven things HeheHe hates. 17He hates proud eyes. He hates a lying tongue. He hates hands that kill people who did no wrong. 18He hates a heart that makes bad plans. He hates feet that run fast to do bad things. 19He hates a false witness who lies. He hates a person who stirs up fights between brothers.

Listen to Your Parents

20My son, keep your father's rules. Do not leave your mother's teaching. 21Tie them to your heart always. Wear them around your neck. 22When you walk, they will guide you. When you sleep, they will watch over you. When you wake up, they will talk to you. 23The rules are like a lamp. The teaching is like a light. The hard words that correct you show the way to life.

Stay Away From the Bad Woman

24They will keep you from the bad woman. They will keep you from the smooth words of a woman you do not know. 25Do not want her beauty in your heart. Do not let her eyes catch you. 26A bad woman can be bought for a piece of bread. But a married woman hunts for a man's life. 27Can a man carry fire in his lap and not burn his clothes? 28Can a man walk on hot coals and not burn his feet? 29So is the man who goes to his neighbourneighbourneighbor's wife. No one who touches her will go free. 30People do not hate a thief who steals because he is hungry. 31But if he is caught, he must pay back seven times. He must give all the money in his house. 32A man who sleeps with another man's wife has no sense. He hurts his own life. 33He will get hurt and shame. His shame will never go away. 34A husband is very jealous. He will not be kind on the day he gets even. 35He will not take any money to let it go. He will not be happy even if someone gives him a big gift.
