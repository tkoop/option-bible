The Day of the LORD Is Near

1Blow a trumpet in Zion. Shout on God's holy hill. Let everyone in the land shake with fear. The day of the LORD is coming. It is near.

2It will be a day of darkness and gloom. It will be a day of clouds and thick darkness. A huge, strong army will come, like the morning light spreading over the mountains. There has never been an army like it. There will never be one again, not for many years. 3Fire burns everything in front of them. Flames burn everything behind them. The land in front of them is like a garden of Eden. The land behind them is a dry, empty desert. Nothing escapes them.

4They look like horses. They run like horsemen. 5They jump on the mountain tops like chariots. They sound like fire eating dry grass. They are like a strong army lined up for war.

6People shake with fear when they see them. Every face turns pale. 7They run like strong warriors. They climb walls like soldiers. Each one goes straight ahead. They do not turn from their path.

8They do not push each other. Each one stays in his own place. They break through weapons, but they are not stopped. 9They run through the city. They climb the walls. They go into houses. They come in through windows like a thief.

10The EarthEarthearth shakes before them. The sky trembles. The sun and moon grow dark. The stars stop shining. 11The LORD shouts before HishisHis army. His camp is very large. The ones who carry out HishisHis word are strong. The day of the LORD is great and terrible. Who can live through it?

God Asks His People to Come Back

12"Even now," says the LORD, "come back to MemeMe with all your heart. Come with fasting, crying, and sadness." 13Be truly sorry in your hearts. Do not just tear your clothes. Come back to the LORD your God. He is kind and full of mercy. He is slow to get angry. He has great love. He may choose not to send the hard times. 14Who knows? He may turn back and be kind. He may leave a blessing behind. Then you can bring grain and drink offerings to the LORD your God.

15Blow a trumpet in Zion. Set apart a time to fast. Call a meeting. 16Bring the people together. Set apart the group. Gather the older people. Bring the children and even babies. Let the groom leave his room. Let the bride leave her tent.

17The priests who serve the LORD cry between the porch and the altar. They say, "LORD, please spare YouryourYour people. Do not let other nations shame what belongs to YouyouYou. Do not let them rule YouryourYour people. Why should people ask, 'Where is their God?'"

God Will Help His People

18Then the LORD cared deeply for HishisHis land. He felt sorry for HishisHis people. 19The LORD answered HishisHis people. He said, "Look, I am sending you grain, new wine, and oil. You will have enough. I will not let other nations shame you again." 20The LORD will move the army from the north far away from you. He will push it into a dry and empty land. Its front will go into the eastern sea. Its back will go into the western sea. Its bad smell will rise. Its stink will go up, because the LORD has done great things.

21Do not be afraid, land. Be glad and happy, because the LORD has done great things. 22Do not be afraid, you wild animals. The dry fields are turning green. The trees are growing fruit. The fig trees and grapevines are strong again.

23Children of Zion, be glad. Be happy in the LORD your God. He gave you the early rain at the right time. He sent rain early and late, just like before. 24The grain bins will be full of grain. The wine jars will overflow with new wine and oil.

25The LORD will give back the years the bugs ate. He sent the big army of bugs that ate your crops. 26You will eat plenty and be full. You will praise the name of the LORD your God. He did amazing things for you. His people will never be put to shame again. 27You will know that the LORD is with Israel. He is the LORD your God. There is no other. His people will never be put to shame again.

30He will show wonders in the sky and on the earth. There will be blood, fire, and columns of smoke. 31The sun will turn to darkness. The moon will turn to blood. This will happen before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes. 32Everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved. On Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be safety. The LORD said some people will survive. He will call them.
