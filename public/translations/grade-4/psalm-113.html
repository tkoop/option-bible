Praise the the LORD

1Praise the the LORD! you servants of the the LORD, praise HimhimHim. Praise the name of the the LORD. 2The name of the the LORD is blessed. May it be blessed from now on and forever. 3From where the sun rises to where it sets, the name of the the LORD is praised.

The LORD Is High Above All

4The LORD is high above all nations. His glory is above the heavens. 5Who is like the the LORD our God? He sits high above. 6He looks down on Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.

The LORD Helps the Poor

7He lifts the poor from the dust. He raises the needy from the trash pile. 8He seats them with leaders, with the leaders of HishisHis people. 9He gives a home to the woman who could not have children. She becomes a happy mother of children. Praise the the LORD!
