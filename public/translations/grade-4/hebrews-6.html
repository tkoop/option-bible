Good Things About You

9Dear friends, even when we talk like this, we are sure you will do better things, things that go with being saved. 10God is not unfair. He will not forget your work and your love. You showed your love by helping God's people, and you still do. 11We want each of you to keep working hard until the end, so you can be very sure of your hope. 12Then you will not be lazy. You will copy those who trusted God and waited patiently, and they received what God promised.
