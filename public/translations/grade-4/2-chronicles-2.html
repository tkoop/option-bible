Solomon Counts the Workers

17Solomon counted all the foreigners in Israel. This was after his father David had counted them. He found 153,600 men. 18He made 70,000 of them carry loads. He made 80,000 cut stone in the mountain. He made 3,600 watch over the people and make them work.
