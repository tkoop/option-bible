Job Wonders Why Bad People Are Not Punished

"1Why doesn't the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty set times for things? Why do people who know HimhimHim not see HishisHis days? 2They move land markers. They graze stolen sheep. 3They chase away the donkey of a child with no father. They take a widow's ox as a promise to pay. 4They push poor people off the road. They make the poor hide.

Poor People Work Hard but Have Little

"5Poor people go out to work like wild donkeys in the desert. They look for food. The dry land gives food to their children. 6They gather grass in the fields. They pick leftover grapes from bad people's vineyards. 7They sleep with no clothes. They have nothing to keep them warm. 8Mountain rains soak them. They hide by rocks because they have no shelter.

Bad People Hurt the Poor

"9They snatch babies from their mothers. They take a poor baby as a promise to pay. 10They walk around with no clothes. They carry grain but still go hungry. 11They press olives for oil. They press grapes, but they are thirsty. 12People in the city groan. Hurt people cry out. But God does not blame anyone for it.

Bad People Hide in the Dark

"13Some people turn away from the light. They do not know its ways. They do not walk in its paths. 14When the sun goes down, a killer gets up to kill poor people. At night he is like a thief. 15A man who is not faithful to his wife watches for evening. He thinks, 'No one will see me.' HeheHe hides his face. 16In the dark they break into houses. By day they stay inside. They never see the light. 17To them, deep dark is like morning. They are friends with scary dark.

God Will Judge the Wicked

"18They are like foam on water. Their part of the land is cursed. They do not go to the vineyards. 19Just as dry heat melts snow water, the grave takes sinners. 20Their mothers forget them. Worms eat them. No one remembers them. So wrong is snapped like a tree. 21They hurt women who cannot have children. They are not kind to widows.

God Is Strong and Sees All

"22But God drags away strong people by HishisHis power. When HeheHe stands up, no one feels safe. 23He lets them feel safe, and they trust it. But HishisHis eyes watch their ways. 24They are high for a short time, then they are gone. They are brought low and gathered like all others. They are cut off like heads of grain. 25If this is not true, who can prove me wrong? Who can show my words are empty?"
