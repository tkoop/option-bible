A Song of David

1God stands up. His enemies scatter. The people who hate HimhimHim run away. 2Smoke blows away. Wax melts near fire. In the same way, bad people are destroyed when God is near. 3Good people are happy. They cheer before God. They are glad and happy. 4Sing to God. Sing about HishisHis name. Praise the OneoneOne who rides on the clouds. His name is the LORD. Be happy before HimhimHim.

God Cares for People in Need

5God is a father to children with no parents. He stands up for women whose husbands have died. He lives in HishisHis holy home. 6God gives lonely people a home. He leads prisoners out with happy songs. But people who fight HimhimHim live in a dry, empty land.

God Led His People

7God, when YouyouYou went out ahead of YouryourYour people and walked through the empty land, the earth shook. 8The sky poured rain because of YouyouYou. This happened at Mount Sinai, before the God of Israel.

9You sent plenty of rain. You made YouryourYour land strong when it was tired. 10Your people lived there. God, in YouryourYour kindness, YouyouYou gave food to poor people.

God Gives Victory

11The Lord gave the command. Many women told the good news. 12Kings and their armies ran away. The woman at home shared the treasure.

13Even when God's people rest among sheep pens, they shine like a dove with silver wings and gold feathers.

14When God MostmostMost HighhighHigh scattered the kings, snow fell on Mount Zalmon.

God's Mountain

15God's mountain is Mount Bashan. It has many high peaks. 16Why are you jealous, you high mountains? This is the mountain God chose for HishisHis home. The LORD will live there forever. 17God has thousands and thousands of chariots. The Lord is with them in HishisHis holy place, at Mount Sinai.

God Saves His People

18You went up high. You took prisoners. You received gifts from people, even from those who fought YouyouYou, so the LORD God could live there. 19Praise the Lord. He carries our load every day. He is the God who saves us. 20Our God saves us. The Lord helps us escape death.

21God will crush the heads of HishisHis enemies. He will punish the one who keeps doing wrong.

22The Lord said, "I will bring them back from Bashan. I will bring them back from the bottom of the sea."

23God's people will walk in the blood of their enemies, and their dogs will get a share.

God's People Celebrate

24People saw YouryourYour march, God. They saw YouyouYou, my God and KingkingKing, march into YouryourYour holy place. 25Singers walked in front. Musicians followed. Young women played tambourines in the middle. 26Praise God with the big group. Praise the LORD, people of Israel. 27There is Benjamin, the youngest tribe, leading them. There are leaders from Judah, Zebulun, and Naphtali.

28God has given strength to HishisHis people. God, make strong what YouyouYou have done for us. 29Because of YouryourYour temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring gifts to YouyouYou.

God Stops His Enemies

30Stop the wild animal in the reeds. Stop the crowd of strong bulls with their young. They love silver. Scatter the people who love war. 31Leaders will come from Egypt. The people of Cush will quickly raise their hands to God.

All People Praise God

32Sing to God, all kingdoms of the earth. Sing praises to the Lord. 33Praise the OneoneOne who rides on the highest heavens, the heavens that have been there since long ago. Listen, HeheHe speaks with a strong voice. 34Give praise to God for HishisHis strength. His glory is over Israel. His strength is in the sky. 35God is amazing in HishisHis holy place. The God of Israel gives strength and power to HishisHis people. Praise God.
