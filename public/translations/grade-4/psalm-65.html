A Song of Praise to God

1People in Zion wait to praise YouyouYou, God. They will keep their promise to YouyouYou. 2You hear prayer. Everyone will come to YouyouYou. 3My wrongs are too much for me, but YouyouYou forgive our sins.

4Happy are the people YouyouYou choose and bring near to live in YouryourYour courts. We are full of the good things in YouryourYour house. Your holy temple is wonderful. 5You answer us with amazing and good works. You are the God who saves us. People everywhere hope in YouyouYou, even far across the seas.

God Made the Earth and Calmed the Seas

6You made the mountains with YouryourYour power. You are full of strength. 7You calm the loud seas and their waves. You calm the noise of the nations. 8People far away are amazed by YouryourYour signs. Morning and evening shout for joy because of YouyouYou.

God Gives Rain and Crops

9You visit the earth and water it. You make it very rich. Your streams are full of water. You get the grain ready, and YouyouYou get the earth ready. 10You water its rows. You smooth its bumps. YouyouYou soften it with rain. You help its plants grow. 11You fill the year with good things. Everywhere YouyouYou go has more than enough.

12The wild fields are full. The hills are full of joy. 13Meadows are full of sheep. Valleys are covered with grain. They shout and sing for joy.
