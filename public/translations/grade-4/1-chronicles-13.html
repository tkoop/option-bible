David Plans to Bring the Ark

1David talked with the leaders of thousands and hundreds and with every chief.

2Then David said to all the people of Israel, "If you think this is good, and if it comes from the LORD our God, let us send word to our brothers who are left in all the lands of Israel. Let us also send word to the priests and Levites in their towns with fields. Ask them to come join us. 3Let us bring back the ark of our God to us. We did not ask about it in the days of Saul."

4All the people said it was good. The whole group wanted to do it.

5David gathered all Israel from the Shihor in Egypt to Hamath. He did this to bring the ark of God from Kiriath Jearim.

6David and all Israel went up to Baalah, which is Kiriath Jearim, in Judah. They went to bring up the ark of God, the LORD, who sits between the winged angels. His name is called there.

7They put the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Abinadab. Uzzah and Ahio were driving the cart.

8David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all their strength. They sang and played harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

Uzzah Touches the Ark

9When they came to the grain floor of Kidon, the oxen stumbled. Uzzah put out his hand to hold the ark.

10The LORD was angry with Uzzah. He struck him because he put his hand on the ark. Uzzah died there before God.

11David was angry because the LORD had punished Uzzah. So that place is called Perez Uzzah to this day.

David Is Afraid and Stops

12David was afraid of God that day. He said, "How can I bring the ark of God to me?"

13So David did not take the ark to the CitycityCity of David. He took it instead to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.

14The ark of God stayed with the family of Obed-Edom in his house for three months. The LORD blessed Obed-Edom and all that he had.
