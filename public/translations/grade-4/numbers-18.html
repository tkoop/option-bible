The LORD Gives Rules for the Priests and Levites

1The LORD said to Aaron, "YouyouYou and your sons must carry the blame for wrongs in the holy place. You and your sons must carry the blame for your priest work. 2Bring your brothers too. They are from Levi's family, your father's family. They will join you and help you while you and your sons stand in front of the tent where God's rules are kept. 3They will watch over you and the whole tent. But they must not come near the holy things or the altar. If they do, they and you will die. 4They will join you and watch over the tent where people meet God. They will do all the work of the tent. No one else may come near you. 5You must watch over the holy place and the altar. Then God's anger will not come on the people of Israel. 6I have taken your brothers, the Levites, from among the people of Israel. I give them to you as a gift. They are given to the LORD to do the work of the tent where people meet God. 7You and your sons must keep your priest work. You will care for the altar and the room behind the curtain. I give your priest work to you as a gift. Anyone else who comes near must die."

Gifts for the Priests

8Then the LORD spoke to Aaron, "Look, I have given you charge of the gifts brought to MemeMe. I give all the holy things of the people of Israel to you and your sons. This is a gift that lasts forever. 9This is yours from the very holy gifts, kept from the fire: every gift they bring, every grain gift, every sin gift, and every guilt gift. They give these to MemeMe. They are very holy for you and your sons. 10Eat them in a very holy place. Every male may eat them. They are holy for you. 11This is also yours: the gift from what they give, all the wave gifts of the people of Israel. I give them to you, your sons, and your daughters. This lasts forever. Anyone clean in your house may eat them. 12I give you the best oil, the best wine, and the best grain. These are the first and best parts they give to the LORD. 13The first ripe fruits from their land, which they bring to the LORD, will be yours. Anyone clean in your house may eat them. 14Everything in Israel that is set apart fully for God will be yours. 15The first baby born to a person or an animal, that they give to the LORD, will be yours. But you must buy back the firstborn son of a person. You must also buy back the firstborn of an animal that is not clean. 16Buy them back when they are one month old. Pay five silver coins, by the holy weight. Twenty small coins make one shekel. 17But do not buy back the firstborn of a cow, sheep, or goat. They are holy. Sprinkle their blood on the altar. Burn their fat as a gift that smells good to the LORD. 18Their meat will be yours. It is like the breast that is waved and the right leg that is given. 19All the holy gifts that the people of Israel bring to the LORD, I give to you, your sons, and your daughters. This lasts forever. It is a promise that lasts forever before the LORD, for you and your children after you."

The Levites' Share

20The LORD said to Aaron, "YouyouYou will not receive land in their country. You will not have a share among them. I am your share and your land among the people of Israel. 21I give every tenth part in Israel to the Levites. It is their land share. It pays them for the work they do, the work of the tent where people meet God. 22Then the people of Israel will not come near the tent where people meet God. If they do, they will carry blame and die. 23The Levites will do the work of the tent where people meet God. They will carry their own blame. This rule will last for all time, for all your children after you. They will not receive land among the people of Israel. 24I give the Levites the tenth part that the people of Israel bring to the LORD. That is why I said they will not receive land among the people of Israel."

The Levites Give a Tenth to the Priests

25The LORD spoke to Moses and said, "Speak to the Levites and tell them this: 26When you take the tenth part from the people of Israel, the tenth part I gave you from them as your land share, you must give part of it to the LORD. Give a tenth part of the tenth part. 27Your gift will count for you like grain that has just been gathered and like full jars from the grape press. 28So from all your tenth parts, which you receive from the people of Israel, give the LORD's gift to Aaron the priest. 29From all your gifts, give every part that belongs to the LORD. Give the best part of it. It is holy. 30Tell them this: When you have set aside the best part, the rest will count for the Levites like grain that has just been gathered and like grapes from the grape press. 31You and your families may eat it in any place. It is your pay for your work in the tent where people meet God. 32You will not be blamed for it, if you give the best part. Do not treat the holy things of the people of Israel as if they are not holy. If you do, you will die."
