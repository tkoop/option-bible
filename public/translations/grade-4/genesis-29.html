Jacob Goes to the East

1Then Jacob went on his way. He came to the land of the people in the east. 2He looked and saw a well in a field. Three flocks of sheep lay by it. The flocks drank from that well. The stone on the top of the well was big. 3When all the flocks came together there, the men rolled the stone from the top of the well. They gave water to the sheep. Then they put the stone back.

Jacob Meets Rachel

4Jacob said to them, “My brothers, where are you from?”

They said, “We are from Haran.”

5He asked, “Do you know Laban, Nahor's son?”

They said, “We know him.”

6He asked, “Is he well?”

They said, “He is well. Look, Rachel his daughter is coming with the sheep.”

7Jacob said, “It is still early in the day. It is not time to bring the animals home. Give water to the sheep and take them to eat grass.”

8They said, “We cannot, until all the flocks come together and they roll the stone from the well. Then we water the sheep.”

9While he was still talking with them, Rachel came with her father's sheep. She took care of them.

10When Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and Laban's sheep, he went near. He rolled the stone from the well and watered Laban's flock.

11Jacob kissed Rachel and cried out loud.

12Jacob told Rachel that he was Rebekah's son, her father's nephew. She ran and told her father.

13When Laban heard about Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him. He hugged him and kissed him. He brought him to his house. Jacob told Laban all these things.

14Laban said, “You are my own family.” Jacob stayed with him a month.

Jacob Works for Rachel

15Laban said, “Just because you are my relative, should you work for me for free? Tell me what your pay should be.”

16Laban had two daughters. The older one was Leah. The younger one was Rachel. 17Leah had weak eyes, but Rachel was pretty and had a nice shape.

18Jacob loved Rachel. He said, “I will work for you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.”

19Laban said, “It is better that I give her to you than to another man. Stay with me.”

20Jacob worked seven years for Rachel. They seemed like a few days to him because he loved her.

21Jacob said to Laban, “Give me my wife. My time is finished, and I want to be with her.”

22Laban gathered all the men of that place and made a feast.

23In the evening he took his daughter Leah and brought her to Jacob. Jacob slept with her.

24Laban gave his servant Zilpah to his daughter Leah as her maid.

25In the morning Jacob saw it was Leah. He said to Laban, “What have you done to me? Did I not work for Rachel? Why did you trick me?”

26Laban said, “In our place we do not give the younger daughter before the older one.”

27Laban said, “Finish this week with her. Then we will give you Rachel too, if you work for me seven more years.”

28Jacob did so. He finished the week with Leah. Then Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife.

29Laban gave his servant Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as her maid.

30Jacob slept with Rachel too. He loved Rachel more than Leah. He worked for Laban seven more years.

The LORD Blesses Leah

31The LORD saw that Leah was not loved. He let her have children. But Rachel could not have children.

32Leah had a son. She named him Reuben. She said, “The LORD has seen my trouble. Now my husband will love me.”

33She had another son. She said, “The LORD heard that I was not loved, so HeheHe gave me this son too.” She named him Simeon.

34She had another son. She said, “Now my husband will stay close to me, because I have had three sons for him.” She named him Levi.

35She had another son. She said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” She named him Judah. Then she stopped having children.
