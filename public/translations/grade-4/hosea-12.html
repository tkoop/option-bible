Ephraim's Wrong Ways

1Ephraim wastes all day chasing empty things. He keeps lying and hurting people. He makes a deal with Assyria and sends oil to Egypt. 2The LORD also has a complaint against Judah. He will punish Jacob for the way he lives. He will pay him back for what he has done. 3Even before he was born, he grabbed his brother’s heel. When he was a man, he wrestled with God. 4He fought with the angel and won. He cried and begged God to be kind. He found God at Bethel, and there God spoke to us. 5The LORD is the God of armies. The LORD is HishisHis name.

6But you must come back to your God. Keep love and fairness. Always wait for your God.

A Trader Who Cheats

7A trader likes to cheat with false scales. 8And Ephraim brags,

“I am very rich. I found wealth for myself. With all my work, they can’t find any sin in me.”

God Speaks to His People

9“But I am the LORD your God from the time you were in Egypt. I will make you live in tents again, like in the days of the special feast. 10I spoke to the prophets. I gave them many dreams. I taught with stories through the prophets.”

God Remembers Israel

11Is there sin in Gilead? The people there are worthless. They kill bulls in Gilgal. Their places of worship are like piles of rocks in the fields. 12Jacob ran away to Aram. Israel worked for a wife. Yes, he watched sheep for a wife. 13But the LORD used a prophet to bring Israel out of Egypt. He also used a prophet to keep Israel safe. 14Ephraim made his Lord very angry. So his Lord will leave his guilt on him. He will pay him back for his shame.
