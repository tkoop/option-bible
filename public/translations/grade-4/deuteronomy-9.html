Moses Speaks to Israel

1Listen, people of Israel. Today you are crossing the Jordan River. You will go in and take land from nations that are bigger and stronger than you. Their cities are big, and their walls reach up to the sky.

2These people are big and tall. They are the Anakites. You know about them. You have heard people say, "Who can fight the Anakites and win?"

3But today you need to know this. The LORD your God is the OneoneOne who goes before you. He is like a fire that burns everything up. He will destroy those people. He will make them fall before you. You will drive them out and destroy them fast, just like the LORD promised you.

Why God Gives the Land

4The LORD your God will push them out before you. Do not say in your heart, "I am so good. That is why the LORD brought me here to take this land." It is because those nations are bad that the LORD is pushing them out before you.

5You are not going in to take their land because you are good or have a clean heart. You are going in because those nations are bad. The LORD your God is pushing them out before you. He is keeping the promise HeheHe made to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 6Know this. The LORD your God is giving you this good land. It is not because you do right. You are a stubborn people.

Do Not Forget the Past

7Remember this. Do not forget how you made the LORD your God angry in the desert. From the day you left Egypt until you came here, you kept saying no to the LORD. 8At Mount Horeb you made the LORD angry. He was so angry HeheHe wanted to destroy you.

9I went up the mountain to get the stone tablets. They were the tablets of the agreement the LORD made with you. I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. I did not eat bread. I did not drink water. 10The LORD gave me the two stone tablets. God wrote on them with HishisHis finger. They had all the words the LORD spoke to you from the fire on the mountain on the day you all came together. 11After forty days and forty nights, the LORD gave me the two stone tablets. They were the tablets of the agreement.

12The LORD said to me, "Get up. Go down from here fast. Your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have ruined themselves. They quickly turned away from what I told them to do. They made a metal god for themselves." 13The LORD also said to me, "I have seen these people. They are very stubborn." 14"Leave MemeMe alone. I will destroy them. I will wipe out their name from under the sky. Then I will make you into a nation bigger and stronger than they are."

Moses Breaks the Tablets

15So I turned and went down the mountain. The mountain was on fire. I had the two tablets of the agreement in my hands. 16I saw that you had sinned against the LORD your God. You had made a metal calf for yourselves. You quickly turned away from what the LORD told you to do. 17So I took the two tablets. I threw them down. I broke them in front of you.

18Then I fell down before the LORD for forty days and forty nights, just like before. I did not eat bread. I did not drink water. I did this because of all the sin you did. You did bad things in front of the LORD. You made HimhimHim angry. 19I was afraid of the LORD's anger. He was so angry HeheHe wanted to destroy you. But the LORD listened to me that time too.

20The LORD was very angry with Aaron. He wanted to destroy him. But I prayed for Aaron at that time too. 21I took the sinful calf you made. I burned it in the fire. I crushed it. I ground it into dust. Then I threw the dust into the stream that ran down the mountain. 22You also made the LORD angry at Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth Hattaavah.

23When the LORD sent you out from Kadesh Barnea, HeheHe said, "Go up and take the land I gave you." But you went against what the LORD your God told you. You did not believe HimhimHim. You did not listen to HimhimHim. 24You have been going against the LORD since the day I met you. 25So I fell down before the LORD for forty days and forty nights. The LORD had said HeheHe would destroy you.

Moses Prays for the People

You brought them out of Egypt with a strong hand. 27Remember YouryourYour servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Do not look at how stubborn these people are. Do not look at their bad ways or their sin.

28"Otherwise the people in the land YouyouYou brought us from will say, 'The LORD could not bring them into the land HeheHe promised. He hated them. So HeheHe brought them out to die in the desert.'

29"But they are YouryourYour people. They are YouryourYour special treasure. You brought them out with YouryourYour strong hand and YouryourYour mighty arm."
