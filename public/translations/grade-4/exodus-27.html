The Altar for Burnt Offerings

1Make an altar from acacia wood. It should be square. Make it five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high. 2Make horns on its four corners. The horns should be part of the altar. Cover the altar with bronze. 3Make bronze tools for it: pots to carry away ashes, shovels, bowls, forks, and fire pans.

4Make a bronze grate like a net. Put four bronze rings on its four corners. 5Put the grate under the edge of the altar, halfway up. 6Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with bronze. 7Put the poles through the rings on two sides of the altar. Use them to carry the altar. 8Make the altar hollow, out of boards. Make it just as it was shown on the mountain.

The Courtyard

9Make a courtyard around the holy tent. On the south side, put fine linen curtains one hundred cubits long. 10Use twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The posts should have silver hooks and bands. 11Do the same on the north side: curtains one hundred cubits long, twenty posts, twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands. 12On the west side, put curtains fifty cubits long, with ten posts and ten bases. 13The east side should also be fifty cubits wide.

14On one side of the gate, put curtains fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 15On the other side, put curtains fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 16For the gate, make a screen twenty cubits long. Use blue, purple, and red yarn, and fine linen. Four posts and four bases should hold it. 17All the posts around the courtyard should have silver bands. Their hooks and bases should be bronze. 18The courtyard should be one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide. The curtains should be five cubits high, with bronze bases. 19All the tools used in the holy tent, and all the tent pegs for the holy tent and the courtyard, should be bronze.

Oil for the Lamp

20Command the people of Israel to bring pure olive oil, pressed from olives, for the lamp. It should burn all the time. 21In the tent of meeting, outside the curtain in front of the box that holds the law, Aaron and his sons should keep the lamp burning before the LORD from evening to morning. This is a rule for the people of Israel for all time.
