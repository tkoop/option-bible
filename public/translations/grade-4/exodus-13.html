God Sets Apart the Firstborn

1The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2"Set apart for MemeMe every firstborn male. The first baby born to every mother among the Israelites belongs to MemeMe. This is true for people and for animals."

Moses Reminds the People

3So Moses said to the people, "Remember this day. It is the day you came out of Egypt, out of the place where you were slaves. The LORD brought you out of there by HishisHis great power. Nothing with yeast may be eaten. 4Today, in the month of Abib, you are going out.

The Feast Without Yeast

"5The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites, and Jebusites. He promised your fathers HeheHe would give you this land. It is a land full of good things. So keep this special service in this month. 6For seven days, eat bread without yeast. On the seventh day, have a feast for the LORD. 7Eat bread without yeast for all seven days. Do not let bread with yeast be seen among you. Do not let yeast be seen anywhere in your land.

A Sign to Help You Remember

"8On that day, tell your son, 'It is because of what the LORD did for me when I came out of Egypt.' 9It will be a sign for you on your hand and a reminder between your eyes. This is so the LORD's teaching will be in your mouth. By HishisHis great power, the LORD brought you out of Egypt. 10So keep this rule at the right time, year after year.

Give the Firstborn to God

"11The LORD will bring you into the land of the Canaanites. He promised you and your fathers this land, and HeheHe will give it to you. 12You must give the LORD the first baby born to every mother. Every firstborn male among your animals belongs to the LORD. 13You must buy back every firstborn donkey with a lamb. If you do not buy it back, break its neck. You must also buy back your firstborn sons.

Answering Your Child's Question

"14In the future, when your son asks you, 'What does this mean?' you must tell him, 'ThetheThe LORD brought us out of Egypt by HishisHis great power. He brought us out of the place where we were slaves.' 15When Pharaoh refused to let us go, the LORD killed every firstborn in Egypt. He killed the firstborn of people and of animals. So I sacrifice to the LORD the first male born to every mother. But I buy back my firstborn sons. 16This will be a sign on your hand and a reminder between your eyes. The LORD brought us out of Egypt by HishisHis great power."

God Leads the People Another Way

17When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them on the road through the land of the Philistines. That road was shorter. God said, "If the people face war, they might change their minds and go back to Egypt." 18So God led the people around, through the desert toward the Red SeaseaSea. The Israelites left Egypt ready to fight. 19Moses took Joseph's bones with him. Joseph had made the Israelites promise, "God will surely take care of you. Carry my bones with you when you leave this place."

God Goes Before Them

20The people left Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the desert. 21The LORD went ahead of them. During the day, HeheHe was in a pillar of cloud to show them the way. During the night, HeheHe was in a pillar of fire to give them light. So they could travel day and night. 22The pillar of cloud did not leave them during the day. The pillar of fire did not leave them during the night.
