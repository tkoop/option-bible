Israel Is Like a Grapevine

1Israel is like a grapevine that grows well and makes fruit. The more fruit he had, the more altars he built. The better his land was, the more stone pillars he set up. 2Their hearts are not true. Now they must pay for their wrong. the LORD will break their altars and smash their stone pillars.

Israel Will Have No King

3Now they will say, "We have no king. We did not fear the LORD. What can a king do for us?" 4They say empty words. They make deals with false promises. So God's punishment grows like bad weeds in the rows of a field.

The Calf Idol Will Be Taken Away

5The people of Samaria will be afraid for the calf idol at Beth-aven. Its people will be sad for it. Its false priests will cry over it, over its glory, because it was taken away. 6It will be taken to Assyria as a gift to the king. Ephraim will be ashamed. Israel will be ashamed of its own plans. 7Samaria and her king will be destroyed. They will be like foam on the water.

Israel Will Be Punished

8The hilltop worship places at Aven will be destroyed. That is Israel's sin. Thorns and thistles will grow on their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, "Fall on us!" and to the hills, "Cover us!" 9Israel, you have sinned since the days of Gibeah. You stayed there. Will the battle at Gibeah not catch the bad people? 10I will punish them as I choose. Nations will gather against them and put them in chains for their double sin. 11Ephraim is like a young cow that has been trained and likes to crush grain. So I will put a heavy yoke on her neck. I will make Ephraim pull. Judah will plow. Jacob will break up the ground.

Turn Back to God

12Plant what is right for yourselves. Reap love. Break up your hard ground. It is time to seek the LORD, until HeheHe comes and sends good like rain on you. 13You planted wrong and reaped unfairness. You ate the fruit of lies. You trusted in your own plans and in your many strong men. 14So the noise of war will rise against your people. All your forts will be destroyed. It will be like Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel in battle, when mothers were killed along with their children. 15So Bethel will do this to you because of your great wrong. At dawn, the king of Israel will be destroyed.
