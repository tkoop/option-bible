Paul Visits Thessalonica

1They travelledtravelledtraveled through Amphipolis and Apollonia. Then they came to Thessalonica, where the Jews had a meeting place.

2As was his way, Paul went into the meeting place. For three Sabbath days he talked with them from the ScripturesscripturesScriptures.

3He explained and showed that the Christ had to suffer and rise from the dead. He said, "This Jesus I am telling you about is the Christ."

4Some of them believed and joined Paul and Silas. Many Greeks who worshiped God believed too, and quite a few leading women.

Trouble in Thessalonica

5But the Jews were jealous. They got some bad men from the market. They made a crowd and upset the city. They came to Jason's house and looked for Paul and Silas, to bring them out to the crowd.

6When they did not find them, they dragged Jason and some other believers to the city leaders. They shouted, "These men have caused trouble all over the world. Now they are here too! 7Jason has taken them in. They all break Caesar's rules. They say there is another king, Jesus."

8Hearing this, the crowd and the city leaders were upset.

9They took money from Jason and the others, then let them go.

Paul in Berea

10That night the believers sent Paul and Silas to Berea. When they arrived, they went to the Jewish meeting place.

11The people in Berea were more willing to listen than the people in Thessalonica. They gladly received the message. They checked the ScripturesscripturesScriptures every day to see if these things were true.

12Many of them believed. Many respected Greek women and men believed too.

13When the Jews in Thessalonica heard that Paul was telling God's word in Berea, they came there too. They stirred up the crowds and caused trouble.

14Then the believers quickly sent Paul away to the sea. Silas and Timothy stayed there.

15The men who travelledtravelledtraveled with Paul took him to Athens. Then they returned with a message for Silas and Timothy to come to him as soon as they could.

Paul in Athens

16While Paul waited for them in Athens, he was very upset to see that the city was full of idols.

17So he talked in the meeting place with the Jews and with people who worshiped God. He also talked every day in the market with anyone who was there.

18Some Epicurean and StoicstoicStoic thinkers talked with him.

Some said, "What is this talker trying to say?"

Others said, "HeheHe seems to be telling about strange gods," because he was telling the good news about Jesus and the rising from the dead.

19They took him to a place called the Areopagus. They said, "Can we know what this new teaching is that you are telling? 20You are bringing strange things to our ears. We want to know what they mean."

21All the Athenians and visitors there spent their time talking about and listening to the newest ideas.
