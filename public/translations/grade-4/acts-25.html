Paul Is Taken to Jerusalem

1Three days after Festus came to the area, he went from Caesarea to Jerusalem. 2The chief priests and Jewish leaders told him their case against Paul. They asked Festus to do them a favourfavourfavor. 3They asked him to bring Paul to Jerusalem. They planned to kill Paul on the road. 4Festus answered that Paul was being kept in Caesarea. He said he would go back soon. 5So he said, "If there is anything wrong with this man, let the leaders among you go down and charge him."

Paul Stands Before Festus

6Festus stayed with them no more than eight or ten days. Then he went down to Caesarea. The next day he sat on the judge's seat and ordered Paul to be brought in. 7When Paul came, the Jews from Jerusalem stood around him. They brought many serious charges, but they could not prove them.

8Paul said in his defencedefencedefense, "I have done nothing wrong against the Jewish law, the temple, or Caesar."

9Festus wanted to do the Jews a favourfavourfavor. So he asked Paul, "Do you want to go up to Jerusalem and be judged there by me about these things?"

10Paul said, "I am standing before Caesar's judge's seat. I must be judged there. I have done nothing wrong to the Jews, as you know very well. 11If I have done wrong and deserve death, I will not ask to be saved from death. But if none of these charges is true, no one can hand me over to them. I appeal to Caesar."

12Festus talked with his council. Then he said, "YouyouYou have appealed to Caesar. You will go to Caesar."

Festus Tells King Agrippa About Paul

13After some days, King Agrippa and Bernice came to Caesarea to greet Festus.

14While they stayed there many days, Festus told the king about Paul. He said, "There is a man here who was left as a prisoner by Felix. 15When I was in Jerusalem, the chief priests and Jewish elders told me about him. They asked me to judge him guilty. 16I told them it is not the Roman way to hand over a man before he has a chance to face his accusers and answer the charge. 17So when they came here, I did not wait. The next day I sat on the judge's seat and ordered the man to be brought in.

18"His accusers stood up, but they did not bring any charge of the bad things I thought they would. 19Instead, they had questions about their own religion and about a dead man named Jesus. Paul said Jesus was alive. 20I did not know what to do with these questions. So I asked if he wanted to go to Jerusalem and be judged there. 21But Paul appealed to be kept for Caesar's decision. So I ordered him to be kept until I send him to Caesar."

22Agrippa said to Festus, "I would like to hear the man myself."

Festus said, "Tomorrow you will hear him."

Paul Is Brought Before Agrippa

23The next day, Agrippa and Bernice came with great show. They went into the meeting hall with the army leaders and important men of the city. Festus ordered Paul to be brought in.

24Festus said, "King Agrippa and all the men here with us, you see this man. All the Jewish people came to me about him, both here and in Jerusalem. They shouted that he should not live any longer. 25But I found that he did nothing that deserves death. Since he appealed to Caesar, I decided to send him. 26But I do not have anything sure to write to Caesar about him. So I brought him before all of you, and especially before you, King Agrippa. Then, after we ask him questions, I may have something to write. 27It does not make sense to send a prisoner without also telling what he is charged with."
