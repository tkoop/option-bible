Jehoshaphat Goes Home in Peace

1King Jehoshaphat of Judah went home in peace. He went to his house in Jerusalem.

A Prophet Warns the King

2Jehu was the son of Hanani. He was a prophet. He went out to meet the king. He said, "King Jehoshaphat, should you help bad people? Should you love people who hate the LORD? Because of this, the LORD is angry with you. 3But some good things are found in you. You took away the poles used to worship a false god. You made your heart ready to look for God."

Jehoshaphat Brings People Back to God

4Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. He went out among the people. He went from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim. He brought them back to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

Judges in Judah

5He put judges in the land. He put them in every strong city of Judah. 6He said to the judges, "Look at what you are doing. You do not judge for people. You judge for the LORD. He is with you when you decide a case. 7Now let the fear of the LORD be on you. Be careful and do it. With the LORD our God, there is no wrong. There is no playing favorites. There is no taking bribes."

Judges in Jerusalem

8Jehoshaphat also put Levites, priests, and leaders of Israel in Jerusalem. They were there to judge for the LORD. They were there to settle arguments. They came back to Jerusalem. 9He told them, "Do this with faith. Do it with a whole heart. Do it in fear of the LORD. 10If your brothers in their cities bring a case to you, it may be about blood, or about God's law, or about rules and decisions, warn them. Then they will not be guilty before the LORD. Then anger will not come on you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not be guilty. 11Look, Amariah the chief priest is over you in all things about the LORD. Zebadiah son of Ishmael is the leader of Judah in all things about the king. The Levites and officers are before you. Be strong and do it. May the LORD be with those who do good."
