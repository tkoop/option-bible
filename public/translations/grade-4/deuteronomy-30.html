God Will Bring His People Back

1When all these things happen to you—the good and the bad that I put before you—and you think about them in all the nations where the LORD your God sends you, 2and you and your children turn back to the LORD your God and listen to HishisHis voice with all your heart and all your soul, doing all I tell you today, 3then the LORD your God will bring you back from being held captive. He will be kind to you. He will gather you from all the nations where the LORD your God sent you. 4Even if you are sent to the far edge of the sky, the LORD your God will gather you and bring you back from there. 5The LORD your God will bring you to the land your fathers owned. You will own it. He will make you do well and make you many more than your fathers.

God Will Change Hearts

6The LORD your God will change your heart and your children's hearts. Then you can love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, so you can live. 7The LORD your God will put all these bad things on your enemies, on those who hate you and chase you. 8You will turn back and listen to the LORD's voice. You will do all HishisHis commands that I give you today. 9The LORD your God will help you do well in all your work. He will bless the children you have, your animals, and your crops. The LORD will be happy to do good to you again, like HeheHe was happy to do good to your fathers. 10This will happen if you listen to the LORD your God and keep HishisHis commands and rules written in this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw. It will happen if you turn back to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.

God's Word Is Near

11The command I give you today is not too hard for you. It is not far away. 12It is not up in Heavenheavenheaven. You should not say, "Who will go up to Heavenheavenheaven to get it for us and tell it to us, so we can do it?" 13It is not across the sea. You should not say, "Who will cross the sea to get it for us and tell it to us, so we can do it?" 14The word is very close to you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so you can do it.

Choose Life

15Look, today I put before you life and good, and death and bad. 16I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in HishisHis ways, and to keep HishisHis commands, rules, and laws. Then you will live and grow. The LORD your God will bless you in the land you are going to take. 17But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are pulled away to bow down to other gods and serve them, 18I tell you today that you will be destroyed. You will not live a long time in the land you are crossing the Jordan to take. 19I call Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth to speak against you today. I have put before you life and death, good and bad. So choose life, so you and your children can live. 20Love the LORD your God. Listen to HishisHis voice. Stay close to HimhimHim. He is your life. He will give you many years in the land the LORD your God promised to give your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
