Rules for People Who Make a Special Promise to God

1The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2"Speak to the Israelites. Tell them, 'When a man or woman makes a special promise to the LORD as a Nazirite, to set himself or herself apart for the LORD,

"'3he or she must not drink wine or strong drink. He or she must not drink vinegar made from wine or strong drink. He or she must not drink grape juice. He or she must not eat fresh grapes or dried grapes. 4During all the days of this special time, he or she must not eat anything from a grapevine, not even the seeds or skins.

"'5For all the days of this special promise, no razor may touch his or her head. The person must be holy until this special promise to the LORD is done. He or she must let the hair on the head grow long. 6During all the days of this special promise to the LORD, he or she must not go near a dead body. 7He or she must not make himself or herself unclean for a father, mother, brother, or sister when they die, because he or she has made a special promise to God. 8All the days of this special promise, the person is holy to the LORD.

"'9If someone dies suddenly near him or her, and the special promise on the head becomes unclean, the person must shave the head on the day he or she becomes clean. That is the seventh day. 10On the eighth day, he or she must bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the door of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 11The priest must offer one bird for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering. This pays for the sin of being near a dead body. On that day the person must set the head apart again. 12The person must start the special promise to the LORD again and bring a one-year-old male lamb as a guilt offering. The first days do not count, because the special promise became unclean.

"'13This is the law for the Nazirite when the days of the special promise are done. The person must be brought to the door of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 14He or she must bring an offering to the LORD: one perfect one-year-old male lamb for a burnt offering, one perfect one-year-old female lamb for a sin offering, and one perfect ram for a peace offering. 15He or she must also bring a grain offering and a drink offering, and a basket of bread made without yeast. The bread should be fine flour mixed with oil, and thin cakes spread with oil. 16The priest must bring them before the LORD and make the sin offering and the burnt offering. 17He must offer the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, along with the basket of bread without yeast. The priest will also make the grain offering and the drink offering. 18Then the Nazirite must shave the special hair at the door of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. He or she must take the hair and put it on the fire under the peace offering. 19The priest must take the boiled shoulder of the ram, one piece of bread without yeast from the basket, and one thin cake. He must put them on the Nazirite's hands after the head is shaved. 20The priest must wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. They are holy for the priest, along with the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the gift. After that, the Nazirite may drink wine. 21This is the law for the Nazirite who makes this offering to the LORD for the special promise. It is in addition to anything else the person can afford. The person must keep the promise he or she made, following the law of the special promise.'"
