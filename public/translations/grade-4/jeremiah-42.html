The People Ask Jeremiah to Pray

Pray to the the LORD your God for us. Pray for this small group that is left. We are only a few left from many, as you can see. 3May the the LORD your God show us the way to go and what to do."

The LORD Answers Jeremiah

7After ten days, the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah. 8So he called Johanan son of Kareah, all the army leaders with him, and all the people, from the least to the greatest. 9He said to them, "This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says. You sent me to bring your request to HimhimHim.

10"If you stay in this land, I will build you up and not tear you down. I will plant you and not pull you up. I am sorry for the trouble I brought on you.

11"Do not be afraid of the king of Babylon. You are afraid of him now, but do not be afraid of him, says the the LORD. I am with you to save you and rescue you from his power.

12"I will make him show you mercy. He will be kind to you and bring you back to your own land.
