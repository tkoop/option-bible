The Bones Will Be Taken Out

1"At that time," says the the LORD, "they will take the bones of Judah's kings, leaders, priests, prophets, and the people of Jerusalem out of their graves. 2They will spread them in the sun, moon, and stars. They loved and served these things. They followed, asked them for help, and bowed down to them. No one will pick them up or bury them. They will be like waste on the ground. 3Wherever I have sent them away, the people left from this bad family will want to die more than live," says the the LORD of Hosts.

The People Will Not Listen

4"You must tell them this is what the the LORD says: 'Do people fall down and not get up? If someone turns away, do they not come back?' 5Why then do these people turn away? Why does Jerusalem keep turning away? They hold tight to lies. They will not come back. 6I listened and heard. They do not say what is right. No one is sorry for his bad ways. No one says, 'What did I do?' Everyone runs his own way, like a horse running into battle.

7"Even the stork in the sky knows her times. The dove, swallow, and crane know when to go. But MymyMy people do not know what the the LORD wants. 8How can you say, 'We are wise, and we have the the LORD's rules'? But the writers' pens have made lies. 9The wise will be ashamed. They will be scared and trapped. They turned away from the the LORD's word. So what wisdom do they have?

10"So I will give their wives to other men. I will give their fields to new owners. From the least to the greatest, all want money. From prophet to priest, all tell lies. 11They act like MymyMy people's hurt is small. They say, 'Peace, peace,' but there is no peace. 12Were they ashamed of the bad things they did? No, they were not ashamed at all. They did not even know how to blush. So they will fall with others who fall. When I punish them, they will fall down," says the the LORD.

No Harvest Left

13"I will take away their harvest," says the the LORD. "There will be no grapes on the vines. There will be no figs on the trees. Even the leaves will dry up. I gave them things, but I will take them away."

The People Run to the Cities

14"Why do we sit here? Come, let us go to the strong cities and die there. The the LORD our God has let bad things happen to us. He gave us poison water to drink, because we sinned against the the LORD. 15We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for healing, but there was only fear."

The Enemy Is Coming

16We hear the horses snort in Dan. The whole land shakes at the sound of the strong horses. They come to eat up the land and all in it, the city and the people in it.

Snakes Will Bite Them

17"I will send snakes among you. They are snakes no one can charm. They will bite you," says the the LORD.

The Prophet Is Sad

18My joy is gone. I am full of grief. My heart is sick.

19Listen to the cry of my people from far away:

"Is the the LORD not in Zion anymore? Is her KingkingKing not there anymore?"

"Why did they make MemeMe angry with their carved statues and empty idols?"

20"The harvest is past. Summer is over. But we are not saved."

21I am hurt because my people are hurt. I am sad. Fear holds me.

22Is there no medicine in Gilead? Is there no doctor there? Why then are my people not healed?
