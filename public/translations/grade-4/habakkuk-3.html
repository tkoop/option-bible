The LORD Judges the Nations

8Were YouyouYou angry with the rivers, the LORD? Was YouryourYour anger against the rivers? Did YouyouYou ride on YouryourYour horses? Did YouryourYour chariots bring help?

Habakkuk Trusts God

16I heard this, and my body shook. My lips trembled at the sound. My bones felt weak. I shook all over. I will rest in the day of trouble. The enemy will come against the people. He will attack them with his army.

17Even if the fig tree does not bloom, and the vines have no grapes, and the olive trees give no oil, and the fields grow no food, and the sheep are gone from the pen, and there are no cows in the stalls.

18Yet I will be glad in the LORD. I will rejoice in God who saves me.

19The LORD God is my strength. He makes my feet like a deer's feet. He makes me walk on high places. For the music leader, with stringed instruments.
