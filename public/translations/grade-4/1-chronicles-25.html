David Chooses Singers for God's House

1David and the army leaders chose some of Asaph's, Heman's, and Jeduthun's sons to serve. They spoke God's words and played harps, lyres, and cymbals. They were counted as workers for this job. 2From Asaph's sons were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. Asaph led them. He spoke God's words as the king told him. 3From Jeduthun's sons were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah. There were six in all. Their father Jeduthun led them. He sang thanks and praise to the the LORD and played the harp.

4From Heman's sons were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth. 5All these were sons of Heman, the king's prophet. He spoke God's words to make God's power great, as God said. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

6All these men were under their fathers' lead. They made music in the the LORD's house with cymbals, harps, and lyres for God's house work. Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman were under the king's lead. 7With their brothers, all trained and good at singing to the the LORD, there were 288 of them.

They Draw Lots for Their Jobs

8They drew lots for their jobs. Young and old took part together. Teachers and students took part together. 9The first lot was for Asaph. It went to Joseph. The second lot went to Gedaliah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 10The third lot went to Zaccur, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 11The fourth lot went to Izri, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 12The fifth lot went to Nethaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 13The sixth lot went to Bukkiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 14The seventh lot went to Jesharelah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 15The eighth lot went to Jeshaiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men.

16The ninth lot went to Mattaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 17The tenth lot went to Shimei, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 18The eleventh lot went to Azarel, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 19The twelfth lot went to Hashabiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 20The thirteenth lot went to Shubael, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 21The fourteenth lot went to Mattithiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 22The fifteenth lot went to Jeremoth, with his sons and brothers—twelve men.

23The sixteenth lot went to Hananiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 24The seventeenth lot went to Joshbekashah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 25The eighteenth lot went to Hanani, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 26The nineteenth lot went to Mallothi, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 27The twentieth lot went to Eliathah, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 28The twenty-first lot went to Hothir, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 29The twenty-second lot went to Giddalti, with his sons and brothers—twelve men.

30The twenty-third lot went to Mahazioth, with his sons and brothers—twelve men. 31The twenty-fourth lot went to Romamti-ezer, with his sons and brothers—twelve men.
