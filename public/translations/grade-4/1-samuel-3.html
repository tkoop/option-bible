Samuel Helps at God's House

1Samuel was a young helper of the the LORD. He served the the LORD near Eli. Back then, the the LORD's word was rare. People did not have many visions. 2One day, Eli's eyes were weak. He could not see well. He was lying in his room. 3Before God's lamp went out, Samuel was lying in the the LORD's house. The ark of God was there.

The LORD Calls Samuel

4Then the the LORD called Samuel. Samuel said, “Here I am.”

5Samuel ran to Eli. “Here I am,” he said. “You called me.”

“I did not call you,” Eli said. “Go back and lie down.”

So Samuel went back and lay down.

6The the LORD called again, “Samuel!” Samuel got up and went to Eli. “Here I am,” he said. “You called me.”

“My son, I did not call you,” Eli said. “Go back and lie down.”

7Samuel did not know the the LORD yet. The the LORD had not shown him HishisHis word.

8The the LORD called Samuel a third time. Samuel got up and went to Eli. “Here I am,” he said. “You called me.”

Then Eli knew the the LORD was calling the boy.

9Eli said to Samuel, “Go and lie down. If HeheHe calls you, say, ‘Speak, the LORD, for YouryourYour servant is listening.’” So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

10The the LORD came and stood there. He called as before, “Samuel! Samuel!”

Samuel said, “Speak, for YouryourYour servant is listening.”

God's Message for Eli

11The the LORD said to Samuel, “Look, I am about to do something in Israel. It will make both ears of everyone who hears it ring.

12“On that day, I will do to Eli's family all that I said about his house. I will start it and finish it.

13“I told him I would judge his family forever. He knew his sons were speaking against God, but he did not stop them.

14“So I swore to Eli's family that their wrong will never be forgiven by sacrifices or offerings.”

Samuel Tells Eli

15Samuel lay until morning. Then he opened the doors of the the LORD's house. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli what he had seen.

16Eli called Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son.”

Samuel said, “Here I am.”

17Eli said, “What did HeheHe say to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do to you and more if you hide any word from me that HeheHe said to you.”

18So Samuel told him everything. He did not hide anything.

Eli said, “He is the the LORD. Let HimhimHim do what seems good to HimhimHim.”

Samuel Grows Up

19Samuel grew up. The the LORD was with him. None of his words failed.

20All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew that Samuel was a true prophet of the the LORD.
