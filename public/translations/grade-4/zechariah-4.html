The Gold Lampstand and Olive Trees

1The angel who had been talking to me came back. He woke me up like a man waking from sleep.

2He asked me, "What do you see?"

I said, "I see a gold lampstand. It has a bowl on top, and seven lamps on it. 3There are seven spouts for the lamps on top, and two olive trees by it. One is on the right of the bowl, and one is on the left."

4I asked the angel who was talking to me, "What are these, my lord?"

5The angel who was talking to me answered, "Do you not know what these are?"

I said, "No, my lord."

The Two Olive Trees

The Two Chosen Men
