The Ark Stays with the Philistines

1The ark of the LORD had been in the land of the Philistines for seven months.

The Philistines Ask the Priests

2The Philistines called their priests and people who tell the future. They said, "What should we do with the ark of the LORD? Tell us how to send it back home."

The Priests Tell Them What to Do

3They said, "If you send back the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty. Send a gift to show you are sorry. Then you will get better. You will know why God has not stopped punishing you."

4The Philistines asked, "What gift should we send?"

They said, "Make five gold sores and five gold rats. Make one for each Philistine leader. The same sickness hit you and your leaders. 5Make models of your sores and models of your rats that are ruining the land. Give honourhonourhonor to the God of Israel. Maybe HeheHe will stop punishing you, your gods, and your land. 6Why should you be stubborn like the Egyptians and Pharaoh? God punished them. Then they let the people go."

7"Now get a new cart. Hitch two mother cows that have never pulled a load. Tie the cows to the cart. Take their calves away and keep them in a pen. 8Put the ark of the LORD on the cart. Put the gold things you send back to God as a sorry gift in a box beside it. Then let it go. 9Watch. If it goes up the road to Beth-shemesh, then God did this bad thing to us. If not, then we will know God did not hurt us. It was just chance."

They Send the Ark Back

10So the men did it. They took two mother cows, hitched them to the cart, and kept the calves in a pen. 11They put the ark of the LORD on the cart. They also put the box with the gold rats and models of their sores on it. 12The cows went straight toward Beth-shemesh. They stayed on the road and mooed as they walked. They did not turn right or left. The Philistine leaders followed them to the edge of Beth-shemesh.

The Ark Reaches Beth-shemesh

13People in Beth-shemesh were gathering wheat in the valley. They looked up and saw the ark. They were glad to see it. 14The cart came to the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh. It stopped by a big rock. The men split the wood of the cart and burned the cows as a gift to the LORD.

15The Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the box with the gold things. They put them on the big rock. That day the men of Beth-shemesh burned gifts and made sacrifices to the LORD. 16The five Philistine leaders saw this. They went back to Ekron that same day.

The Gold Gifts

17These are the gold sores the Philistines sent back to the LORD as a sorry gift: one each for Ashdod, Gaza, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron. 18The number of gold rats matched the number of Philistine cities of the five leaders. It included strong cities and country towns. The big rock where they put the ark of the LORD is still there today in the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh.

God Punishes the Men of Beth-shemesh

19But God struck down some men of Beth-shemesh because they looked into the ark of the LORD. He struck down seventy men and fifty thousand men. The people were sad because the LORD had hit them with a big blow.

20The men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who can stand before the LORD, this holy God? Where should the ark go from here?"

They Ask Kiriath-jearim to Take the Ark
