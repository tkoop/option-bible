Wise and Foolish Words

1A soft answer turns anger away. But harsh words make people mad. 2Wise people say things that help us learn. Foolish people just talk nonsense.

God Sees All

3The LORD sees everything. He watches both bad people and good people. 4Kind words help people live. But lying words hurt the heart.

5A foolish child hates being corrected. But a wise child listens and learns. 6Good people have rich homes. But bad people's money brings trouble. 7Wise people share what they know. But foolish people do not have good hearts.

God Hates Wrong

8The LORD hates gifts from bad people. But HeheHe likes the prayers of good people. 9The LORD hates the way bad people live. But HeheHe loves people who do what is right.

10Hard trouble comes to people who leave the right way. People who hate being corrected will die. 11Even death and the grave are open to the LORD. So HeheHe surely sees our hearts. 12People who make fun of others hate being corrected. They will not go to wise people.

A Happy Heart

13A happy heart makes your face smile. But sadness breaks your spirit. 14A smart heart wants to learn. But a foolish mouth eats up nonsense. 15Sad people have hard days. But happy people always feel like they are at a feast.

16It is better to have a little and respect the LORD than to have lots of money and trouble. 17It is better to eat plain food with love than a big feast with hate. 18Angry people start fights. But patient people calm fights down.

19A lazy person's way is full of thorns. But a good person's path is clear. 20A wise son makes his dad happy. But a foolish man looks down on his mom. 21Foolish people are happy doing wrong. But wise people stay on the right path.

22Plans fail when no one helps. But with many helpers, plans work. 23It feels good to say the right thing at the right time. 24The path of life goes up for wise people. It keeps them from going down to death.

God Helps People

25The LORD knocks down proud people's homes. But HeheHe protects widows' land. 26The LORD hates bad thoughts. But kind words are pure to HimhimHim. 27Greedy people ruin their homes. But people who hate bribes will live.

28Good people think before they answer. But bad people just spill out evil. 29The LORD is far from bad people. But HeheHe hears good people's prayers. 30Bright eyes bring joy to the heart. Good news makes the body strong.

31People who listen to life-giving correction live among wise people. 32People who ignore correction hurt themselves. But people who listen gain wisdom. 33Wisdom teaches us to respect the LORD. Being humble comes before honourhonourhonor.
