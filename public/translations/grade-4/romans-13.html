Obey the Leaders

1Everyone should obey the leaders who are over them. 2God is the only one who gives power, and the leaders here were put there by God. So if you fight against them, you are fighting against God's plan. People who do that will bring punishment on themselves.

3Leaders do not scare people who do good. They scare people who do bad. Do you want to not be scared? Do good, and they will praise you. 4They are God's helpers to do good for you. But if you do bad, be afraid. They have the power to punish, and they do not use it for nothing. They are God's helpers who punish people who do bad.

5So everyone must obey them. Do it not just because of fear of punishment, but because it is right. 6That is why you pay taxes. The leaders work for God, and they keep doing this work. 7Give everyone what you owe them. Pay taxes to the one who collects taxes. Pay what is due to the one who should get it. Love One Another

8Do not owe anyone anything, except to love each other. The one who loves others has done what God's rules ask. 9God's rules say, do not cheat on your husband or wife, do not murder, do not steal, and do not want what is not yours. Wake Up and Live in the Light

11You know what time it is. It is time to wake up from sleep. We are closer to being saved now than when we first believed. 12The night is almost over. The day is near. So let us stop doing the things of the dark. Let us put on the things of the light.

13Let us live good lives in the day. Do not have wild parties or drink too much. Do not do wrong sexual things or be out of control. Do not fight or be jealous. 14Instead, live like the Lord Jesus Christ. Do not plan to do the wrong things your body wants.
