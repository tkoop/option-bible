So I ate it, and it tasted sweet like honey in my mouth.

God Sends Ezekiel to Israel

4Then HeheHe said, "SonsonSon of man, go to the people of Israel and speak MymyMy words to them. 5You are not being sent to people who talk in a strange, hard language. You are being sent to the people of Israel. 6You are not being sent to many nations that speak strange, hard languages you cannot understand. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you. 7But the people of Israel will not want to listen to you, because they do not want to listen to MemeMe. All the people of Israel are stubborn. They will not change their minds. 8Look, I have made your face as strong as their faces. I have made your forehead as strong as their foreheads. 9I have made your forehead like a diamond, harder than rock. Do not be afraid of them. Do not be upset by them, because they are a stubborn people."

Ezekiel Is Sent to His People

10Then HeheHe said, "SonsonSon of man, listen well to all the words I say to you. Take them to heart. 11Go to your people who were taken away. Speak to them. Tell them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: They may listen, or they may not.'"

Ezekiel Hears a Loud Sound

12Then the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up, and I heard a loud rumbling behind me. It said, "Praise the glory of the the LORD where HeheHe is!" 13It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures touching each other. It was also the sound of the wheels beside them. It was a loud rumbling. 14So the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up and carried me away. I went with a bitter and angry heart. The strong hand of the the LORD was on me.

Ezekiel Sits With the Captured People

15I came to the people who were taken away at Tel Abib, by the River Kebar. I sat there with them for seven days. I was very upset.

God Makes Ezekiel a Lookout

16At the end of seven days, the word of the the LORD came to me. He said, 17"SonsonSon of man, I have made you a lookout for the people of Israel. When you hear a word from MymyMy mouth, warn them for MemeMe. 18If I say to a bad person, 'You will die,' and you do not warn him to turn from his bad ways so he can live, that bad person will die because of his sin. But I will blame you for his death. 19But if you warn the bad person and he does not turn from his bad ways, he will die because of his sin. But you will have saved your life. 20If a good person turns from doing good and does wrong, and I put something in his way that makes him fall, he will die. Since you did not warn him, he will die for his sin. The good things he did will not be remembered. But I will blame you for his death. 21But if you warn the good person not to sin, and he does not sin, he will live. He listened to your warning. You will have saved your life."

Ezekiel Must Be Quiet

22Then the hand of the the LORD was on me there. He said, "Get up and go out to the open field. I will speak to you there." 23So I got up and went out to the open field. I saw the glory of the the LORD standing there. It looked like the glory I had seen by the River Kebar. I fell facedown. 24Then the SpiritspiritSpirit came into me and stood me on my feet. He spoke to me. He said, "Go and shut yourself inside your house. 25You, son of man, people will tie ropes on you. They will bind you with them. You will not be able to go out among them. 26I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth. You will not be able to talk. You will not be able to tell them they are wrong, because they are a stubborn people. 27But when I speak to you, I will open your mouth. You will say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Whoever wants to listen, let him listen. Whoever refuses, let him refuse. They are a stubborn people.'"
