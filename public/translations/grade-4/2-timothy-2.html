Be Strong in God's Kindness

1So, my child, be strong in the kindness that is in Christ Jesus. 2Also, tell faithful people what you heard me say with many people watching. They must be able to teach others.

Share in Suffering

3Suffer with me, like a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 4A soldier does not get busy with everyday work. He wants to please the leader who picked him. 5Also, an athlete does not win a prize unless he follows the rules. 6The farmer who works hard should get some of the crop first. 7Think about what I say. The Lord will help you understand all of this.

Remember Jesus

8Remember Jesus Christ. He rose from the dead. He came from David's family. This is the good news I tell. 9I suffer for it. I am in chains like a bad person. But God's word is not chained. 10So I keep going for God's chosen people. I want them to be saved in Christ Jesus and have glory that lasts forever.

A True Saying

11This is a true saying: "If we died with HimhimHim, we will live with HimhimHim. 12If we keep going, we will rule with HimhimHim. If we turn away from HimhimHim, HeheHe will turn away from us. 13If we are not faithful, HeheHe stays faithful. He cannot turn away from HimselfhimselfHimself."

Teach the True Word

14Remind people of these things. Tell them before God not to fight about words. It does no good. It only hurts the people who listen. 15Try hard to show God you are a good worker. Then you will not be ashamed. You will teach the true message the right way.

Stay Away from Empty Talk

16Stay away from empty talk that does not honourhonourhonor God. People who talk like that will become worse and worse. 17Their talk will spread like a sickness. Hymenaeus and Philetus are two of them. 18They missed the truth. They say people already rose from the dead. They are turning some people away from faith.

God's Strong Base

19But God's strong base stands firm. It has this seal: "The Lord knows who belongs to HimhimHim," and, "Everyone who says the Lord's name must turn away from wrong." 20In a big house, there are gold and silver things. There are also wood and clay things. Some are for special use. Some are for everyday use. 21So if a person makes himself clean from these wrong things, he will be a special tool. He will be holy and useful to the Master. He will be ready for every good work.

Run from Wrong Wants

22Run from the wrong wants of youth. Run after right living, faith, love, and peace. Do this with people who call on the Lord with a clean heart. 23Stay away from foolish and silly questions. You know they start fights. 24The Lord's servant must not fight. He must be kind to everyone. He must be able to teach. He must not get angry fast. 25He must teach those who are against him in a gentle way. Maybe God will help them change their hearts. Then they will know the truth. 26Then they may wake up and get free from the devil's trap. The devil has caught them to do what he wants.
