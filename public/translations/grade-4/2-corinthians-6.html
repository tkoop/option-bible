Working Together with God

1We work with God. We beg you not to take God's gift for nothing.

2God says, "I heard you at the right time. I helped you on the day I saved you." Look, now is the right time. Look, now is the day God saves you.

3We do not want to upset anyone, so no one can blame our work.

4But as God's servants, we show who we are in every way: in great patience, in troubles, in hard times, and in worry. 5We show it in beatings, in jail, in fights, in hard work, in sleepless nights, and in hunger.

6We show it in pure living, in knowing what is true, in being patient, in being kind, in the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, and in true love. 7We show it in true words, in God's power, and in the tools of right living for our right and left hands.

8We are praised and shamed. People speak badly of us and speak well of us. They call us liars, but we tell the truth. 9People do not know us, but God knows us. We seem to be dying, but look, we live. We are punished, but not killed. 10We are sad, but always happy. We are poor, but we make many people rich. We have nothing, but we own everything.

11Corinthians, we have spoken openly to you. Our heart is wide open to you. 12You are not held back by us. You are held back by your own feelings. 13I speak to you as to children. Open wide your hearts too.

Do Not Team Up with Unbelievers

14Do not be tied to people who do not believe. What do right living and wrong living share? What do light and dark share? 15What agreement can Christ have with Belial? What part does a believer have with an unbeliever?

16What agreement can God's house have with false gods? We are the house of the living God. God said, "I will live in them. I will walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be MymyMy people."

17So the Lord says, "Come out from among them and be separate. Do not touch anything that is not clean, and I will take you in." 18He says, "I will be a FatherfatherFather to you, and you will be MymyMy sons and daughters," says the Lord AlmightyalmightyAlmighty.
