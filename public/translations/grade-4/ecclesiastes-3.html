A Time for Everything

1There is a time for everything. There is a time for every thing people do on EarthEarthearth. 2A time to be born, and a time to die. A time to plant, and a time to pull up what was planted. 3A time to kill, and a time to heal. A time to tear down, and a time to build. 4A time to cry, and a time to laugh. A time to grieve, and a time to dance. 5A time to throw stones, and a time to gather stones. A time to hug, and a time to let go. 6A time to look for things, and a time to lose them. A time to keep, and a time to throw away. 7A time to tear, and a time to sew. A time to be quiet, and a time to speak. 8A time to love, and a time to hate. A time for war, and a time for peace.

God Gives Work to People

9What good does a worker get from all his hard work? 10I saw the hard work God gave people to do. 11God made everything beautiful at the right time. He put the idea of forever in people's hearts. But people cannot fully understand what God does from start to finish.

Enjoy God's Gifts

12I know there is nothing better for people than to be happy and do good while they live. 13It is God's gift when a person can eat, drink, and enjoy good things from all his hard work. 14I know that everything God does lasts forever. No one can add to it, and no one can take away from it. God does this so people will honourhonourhonor HimhimHim. 15What is now has already happened. What will happen has already happened. God brings back what has passed.

God Will Judge Everyone

16I also saw something on EarthEarthearth. In the place where fairness should be, there was evil. In the place where right should be, there was evil. 17I said to myself that God will judge good people and bad people. There is a time for everything, and for everything people do. 18I said to myself about people that God is testing them. He wants them to see that they are like animals.

Death Comes to All

19People and animals have the same end. One dies, and so does the other. They all breathe the same air. People are no better than animals. Everything is like a breath that quickly fades. 20Everything goes to the same place. Everything came from dust, and everything goes back to dust. 21Who knows if the spirit of people goes up, and the spirit of animals goes down into the earth? 22So I saw that there is nothing better for a person than to be happy in his work. That is his share. Who can bring him to see what will happen after him?
