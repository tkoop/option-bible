Balaam Sees Israel

1Balaam saw that the LORD was happy to bless Israel. He did not go to look for magic signs like before. He turned to face the wild land. 2Balaam looked up and saw Israel camped by its family groups. The SpiritspiritSpirit of God came on him.

Balaam's First Message

3Balaam spoke this message: "This is the word of Balaam son of Beor. This is the word of the man whose eyes are open. 4This is the word of one who hears God's words. He sees what the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty shows him. He falls down, but his eyes stay open.

5"How good your tents are, Jacob! How good your homes are, Israel! 6They spread like valleys. They are like gardens by a river. They are like sweet-smelling trees the LORD planted. They are like cedar trees by water. 7Water will pour from their buckets. Their crops will have plenty of water. Their king will be greater than Agag. Their kingdom will be lifted up. 8God brought them out of Egypt. He is like strong horns of a wild ox for them. He will eat up enemy nations. He will crush their bones. He will shoot them with arrows. 9They crouch and lie down like a lion, like a lioness. Who will wake them? Blessed are the ones who bless you. Cursed are the ones who curse you."

Balak Gets Angry

10Balak got very angry at Balaam. He clapped his hands. He said to Balaam, "I called you to curse my enemies. But look, you have blessed them three times! 11Now go home! I said I would honourhonourhonor you, but the LORD has kept you from honourhonourhonor."

Balaam Answers Balak

12Balaam answered Balak, "Did I not tell the messengers you sent me that if Balak gave me his whole house full of silver and gold, I could not do anything beyond what the LORD says, good or bad, on my own? I will say only what the LORD says. 13Now I am going back to my people. 14Come, let me warn you what this people will do to your people in the days to come."

Balaam's Next Message

15Balaam spoke this message: "This is the word of Balaam son of Beor. This is the word of the man whose eyes are open. 16This is the word of one who hears God's words. He knows what the MostmostMost HighhighHigh knows. He sees what the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty shows him. He falls down, but his eyes stay open.

17"I see him, but not now. I look at him, but not near. A star will come from Jacob. A ruler's stick will rise from Israel. He will defeat Moab and all the sons of Sheth. 18Edom will belong to him. Seir, his enemy, will belong to him too. But Israel will be strong and win. 19A ruler will come from Jacob. He will destroy the people left in the city."

Words About Amalek

20Balaam saw Amalek. He spoke this message: "Amalek was first among the nations, but in the end it will be destroyed."

Words About the Kenites

21Balaam saw the Kenites. He spoke this message: "Your home is strong, and your nest is set in a rock. 22But the Kenites will be destroyed. How long until Assyria takes you captive?"

Words About Other Nations

23Balaam spoke this message: "Oh no! Who can live when God does this? 24Ships will come from Kittim. They will trouble Assyria and Eber. But they will also be destroyed."

25Then Balaam got up and went back to his home. Balak also went his way.
