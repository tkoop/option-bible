God Tells His People to Be Comforted

1“Comfort, comfort MymyMy people,” says your God. 2“Speak kindly to Jerusalem. Tell her that her hard work is done. Her sin is forgiven. She got twice the punishment from the LORD for all her sins.”

Get the Road Ready for the LORD

3A voice calls out, “Get the road ready for the LORD in the wild land. Make a straight path for our God in the desert.” 4Every valley will be raised up. Every mountain and hill will be lowered. The crooked ground will be made straight. The rough places will be made flat. 5The LORD's glory will be shown. All people will see it together. The LORD has spoken.

People Are Like Grass

6A voice says, “Cry out!”

I asked, “What should I cry?”

“All people are like grass. All their beauty is like a flower in the field.

7“The grass dries up. The flower fades when the LORD's breath blows on it. Yes, people are like grass. 8The grass dries up. The flower fades. But the word of our God stands forever.”

Good News for Zion and Jerusalem

9Go up on a high mountain, O Zion, you who bring good news. Raise your voice with strength, O Jerusalem, you who bring good news. Raise it. Do not be afraid. Say to the towns of Judah, “Here is your God!” 10See, the Lord the LORD comes with power. His arm rules for HimhimHim. His reward is with HimhimHim. His payment goes before HimhimHim. 11He will feed HishisHis flock like a shepherd. He will gather the lambs in HishisHis arm and hold them close to HishisHis heart. He will gently lead the mother sheep.

No One Is Like the LORD

12Who measured the water in the hollow of HishisHis hand? Who marked off the sky with HishisHis hand? Who held the dust of the earth in a small cup? Who weighed the mountains on a scale and the hills on a balance? 13Who has told the LORD's SpiritspiritSpirit what to do? Who has given the LORD advice? 14Who did HeheHe ask for help? Who taught HimhimHim what is right? Who taught HimhimHim what HeheHe knows? Who showed HimhimHim the way to understand? 15The nations are like a drop in a bucket. They are like dust on the scales. See, HeheHe lifts up the islands like fine dust. 16Lebanon is not enough wood for a fire. Its animals are not enough for a burnt offering. 17All the nations are nothing to HimhimHim. They are like nothing and empty.

Idols Are Not Like God

18Who can you compare God to? What picture can you compare HimhimHim to? 19To an idol? A worker makes it. A goldsmith covers it with gold and makes silver chains for it. 20A poor man who cannot give a big gift chooses wood that will not rot. He finds a good worker to set up an idol that will not fall.

God Made Everything

21Do you not know? Have you not heard? Has it not been told to you from the start? Have you not understood since the world was made? 22He sits above the circle of the earth. The people on it are like grasshoppers. He stretches out the sky like a cloth. He spreads it like a tent to live in. 23He makes leaders come to nothing. He makes the judges of the earth useless. 24They are planted and sown. They take root in the ground. But HeheHe blows on them, and they dry up. A storm carries them away like straw. 25“To whom will you compare MemeMe? Or who is MymyMy equal?” asks the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne. 26Look up at the sky. Who made all these? He brings out the stars and calls each one by name. Because HeheHe is so strong and powerful, not one is missing.

The LORD Gives Strength to His People

27Why do you say, O Jacob, and why do you speak, O Israel, “my way is hidden from the LORD, and my God ignores my case”? 28Do you not know? Have you not heard? The LORD is the God who lives forever. He made the ends of the earth. He does not get tired or weary. No one can understand HishisHis wisdom. 29He gives power to tired people. He gives strength to people with no power. 30Even young people get tired and weary. Young men trip and fall. 31But people who wait on the LORD will get new strength. They will fly high like eagles. They will run and not get tired. They will walk and not get weary.
