The LORD Ruins the Earth

1Look! The LORD is making the earth empty. He is ruining it. He is twisting the ground. He is scattering the people who live there. 2Everyone will be the same. People and priests will be the same. Workers and bosses will be the same. Buyers and sellers will be the same. Lenders and borrowers will be the same. 3The EarthEarthearth will be completely emptied. It will be robbed clean. the LORD has said this.

Everyone Is Guilty

4The EarthEarthearth is sad and dying. The whole world is weak and fading. Even the proud leaders of the earth are weak. 5The EarthEarthearth is dirty because of the people who live on it. They broke the LORD's laws. They changed HishisHis rules. They broke HishisHis promise that lasts forever. 6So a curse is eating up the earth. The people who live there are guilty. That is why EarthEarthearth people are burned up, and only a few are left.

Sadness and Empty Cities

7The new wine is sad. The grapevines are weak. All happy people are groaning. 8The happy sound of drums stops. The noise of party people stops. The joy of the harp stops. 9They do not sing while they drink wine. Strong drink tastes bitter to those who drink it.

10The broken city is in ruins. Every house is closed. No one can enter. 11People cry in the streets because there is no wine. All joy has turned sad. Happiness has left the earth. 12The city is left empty. Its gate is broken down.

A Few Are Left

13So it will be in the middle of the earth among the nations. It will be like shaking an olive tree. It will be like picking the last grapes.

Praise from Far Away

14They lift up their voices. They shout for joy. From the west they praise the LORD's greatness. 15So praise the LORD in the east. HonourHonourHonor the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the faraway islands. 16From the ends of the earth we heard songs of praise to the Good OneoneOne. But I said, “I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Oh, no! Traitors have been bad! Traitors have been very bad!”

Trouble for Earth People

17Terror, a pit, and a trap are waiting for you who live on the earth. 18The person who runs from the sound of terror will fall into the pit. The person who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the trap. The windows of Heavenheavenheaven are opened. The foundations of the earth shake. 19The EarthEarthearth is broken into pieces. The EarthEarthearth is split apart. The EarthEarthearth shakes hard. 20The EarthEarthearth wobbles like a drunk person. It sways like a small hut. Its sins are heavy on it. It falls and does not get up again.

The LORD Rules at Last

21On that day the LORD will punish the armies in Heavenheavenheaven above and the kings on EarthEarthearth below. 22They will be gathered like prisoners in a pit. They will be shut in a cell. After many days they will be punished. 23The moon will be ashamed. The sun will be embarrassed. This is because the LORD of Armies will rule on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem. His leaders will see HishisHis glory.
