Good Things for Listening to the LORD

1If you listen well to the voice of the the LORD your God and keep and do all HishisHis commands that I give you today, the the LORD your God will put you high above all nations of the earth. 2All these blessings will come to you and catch up to you, if you listen to the voice of the the LORD your God. 3You will be blessed in the city, and blessed in the field. 4Your children, your crops, and your animals will be blessed. Your calves and lambs will grow. 5Your basket and your bowl will be blessed. 6You will be blessed when you come in, and blessed when you go out.

7The the LORD will make your enemies who rise against you run away in fear before you. They will come out against you one way, but run away seven ways. 8The the LORD will send blessing on your barns and on all you do. He will bless you in the land the the LORD your God gives you. 9The the LORD will set you apart as HishisHis holy people, as HeheHe promised you, if you keep the commands of the the LORD your God and walk in HishisHis ways. 10All the peoples of the earth will see that you are called by the name of the the LORD, and they will be afraid of you.

11The the LORD will give you plenty of good things: children, animals, and crops in the land the the LORD promised your fathers to give you. 12The the LORD will open the heavens, HishisHis good storage place, to give rain to your land at the right time, and to bless all the work of your hands. You will lend to many nations, but you will not need to borrow. 13The the LORD will make you the head, not the tail. You will be only on top, not on the bottom, if you listen to the commands of the the LORD your God that I am giving you today, to keep and do them. 14Do not turn away to the right or left from all the words I command you today, to follow other gods and serve them.

Bad Things for Not Listening to the LORD

15But if you do not listen to the voice of the the LORD your God, to keep and do all HishisHis commands and rules that I give you today, all these curses will come on you and catch up to you. 16You will be cursed in the city, and cursed in the field. 17Your basket and your bowl will be cursed. 18Your children, your crops, your calves, and your lambs will be cursed. 19You will be cursed when you come in, and cursed when you go out.

20The the LORD will send on you trouble, panic, and punishment in all you do, until you are destroyed and quickly lost, because of the evil things you did when you left HimhimHim. 21The the LORD will make sickness stick to you until HeheHe has removed you from the land you are going in to take. 22The the LORD will hit you with wasting disease, fever, swelling, burning fever, war, blight, and mildew. They will chase you until you die. 23The sky above your head will be like metal, and the ground under you will be like iron. 24The the LORD will make the rain of your land turn to dust and powder. It will come down on you from the sky until you are destroyed.

25The the LORD will let you be defeated by your enemies. You will go out against them one way, but run away seven ways. You will become a scary sight to all the kingdoms of the earth. 26Your dead body will be food for all the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth, and no one will scare them away. 27The the LORD will hit you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, rash, and itch that cannot be healed. 28The the LORD will hit you with madness, blindness, and a confused heart. 29At noon you will feel around like a blind man feels around in the dark. Nothing you do will go well. You will only be hurt and robbed all your life, with no one to save you.

30You will promise to marry a woman, but another man will sleep with her. You will build a house, but not live in it. You will plant a grape field, but not use its fruit. 31Your ox will be killed in front of you, but you will not eat it. Your donkey will be taken from you and not come back. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and no one will save you. 32Your sons and daughters will be given to another people. Your eyes will get tired looking for them all day, but you will have no power to help. 33A people you do not know will eat the fruit of your land and all your hard work. You will only be hurt and crushed all your life. 34You will go crazy from what your eyes see.

35The the LORD will hit you with a bad sickness that cannot be healed, from the bottom of your foot to the top of your head. 36The the LORD will lead you and your king, whom you set over you, to a nation you and your fathers have not known. There you will serve other gods made of wood and stone. 37You will become a scary thing, a warning story people tell, and a joke among all the peoples where the the LORD sends you. 38You will plant much seed in the field, but gather little, because locusts will eat it. 39You will plant and care for grape fields, but you will not drink the wine or gather the grapes, because worms will eat them.

40You will have olive trees in all your land, but you will not get to use the oil, because your olives will drop off. 41You will have sons and daughters, but they will not stay with you, because they will be taken away. 42Locusts will take over all your trees and the fruit of your land. 43The stranger among you will rise higher and higher, and you will go lower and lower. 44He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.

The Curses Will Not Stop

45All these curses will come on you, chase you, and catch you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of the the LORD your God, to keep HishisHis commands and rules that HeheHe gave you. 46They will be a warning sign for you and your children forever. 47Because you did not serve the the LORD your God with joy and a glad heart when you had plenty of everything, 48you will serve your enemies whom the the LORD sends against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and lack of everything. He will put a heavy iron collar on your neck until HeheHe destroys you.

49The the LORD will bring a nation against you from far away, from the end of the earth, flying like an eagle. It will be a nation whose language you do not understand. 50A fierce nation that will not respect the old or show mercy to the young. 51It will eat the fruit of your animals and your land until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine, or oil, no calves or lambs, until it ruins you. 52It will surround you in all your towns and all your land, until your high and strong walls that you trust in fall. It will surround you in all your towns and all your land that the the LORD your God gave you.

53You will eat your own children, the sons and daughters the the LORD your God gave you, in the trouble your enemy brings when he traps you. 54The tender and delicate man among you will turn against his brother, his wife, and his other children he has left. 55He will not give any of them the flesh of his children that he is eating, because he has nothing left in the trouble your enemy brings when he traps you in all your towns. 56The tender and delicate woman among you, who would not even put her foot on the ground because she was so dainty, will turn against her husband, her son, and her daughter. 57She will secretly eat her afterbirth and her children she gives birth to, because she has nothing left in the trouble your enemy brings when he traps you in your towns.

58If you do not carefully keep all the words of this law written in this book, to fear this great and amazing name, the the LORD your God, 59then the the LORD will make your plagues and your children's plagues terrible—great and lasting plagues, and bad and lasting sicknesses. 60He will bring back on you all the diseases of Egypt that you feared, and they will stick to you. 61The the LORD will also bring on you every sickness and every plague not written in this book of the law, until you are destroyed.

62Because you were as many as the stars of Heavenheavenheaven, but you will be left few, because you did not listen to the voice of the the LORD your God. 63Just as the the LORD rejoiced over you to do you good and make you many, so the the LORD will rejoice over you to destroy you and wipe you out. You will be pulled up from the land you are going in to take. 64The the LORD will scatter you among all peoples, from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods of wood and stone, which you and your fathers have not known.

65Among those nations you will find no rest, and your foot will have no resting place. There the the LORD will give you a worried heart, weak eyes, and a sad heart. 66Your life will always be in danger. You will be afraid night and day, and you will not feel safe about your life. 67In the morning you will say, "If only it were evening!" and in the evening you will say, "If only it were morning!" because of the fear in your heart and what your eyes see. 68The the LORD will send you back to Egypt by ship, by the way I said you would never see again. There you will try to sell yourselves to your enemies as slaves, but no one will buy.
