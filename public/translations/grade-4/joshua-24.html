Joshua Calls Israel Together

1Joshua brought all the tribes of Israel to Shechem. He called the elders, judges, and leaders of Israel. They stood before God.

2Joshua said to all the people, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Long ago, your fathers lived on the other side of the Euphrates River. Terah was the father of Abraham and Nahor. They served other gods. 3I took your father Abraham from the other side of the river. I led him through all the land of Canaan. I gave him many children. I gave him Isaac. 4I gave Isaac Jacob and Esau. I gave Esau the hill country of Seir to own. Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.

5"I sent Moses and Aaron. I punished Egypt with trouble. After that, I brought you out. 6I brought your fathers out of Egypt. You came to the sea. The Egyptians chased your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Red SeaseaSea. 7Your fathers cried to the LORD. He put darkness between you and the Egyptians. He brought the sea over them and covered them. Your eyes saw what I did in Egypt. You lived in the desert for many days.

8"Then I brought you to the land of the Amorites. They lived on the other side of the Jordan. They fought you. I gave them into your hands. You took their land. I destroyed them before you. 9Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, rose up and fought Israel. He sent for Balaam son of Beor to curse you. 10But I would not listen to Balaam. He blessed you instead. I saved you from his hand.

11"You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho. The people of Jericho fought you. So did the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites. I gave them into your hands. 12I sent hornets ahead of you. They drove out the two Amorite kings before you. It was not by your sword or your bow. 13I gave you land you did not work for. I gave you cities you did not build. You eat from vineyards and olive trees you did not plant.

14"So now, respect the LORD. Serve HimhimHim with all your heart and with truth. Get rid of the gods your fathers served on the other side of the river and in Egypt. Serve the LORD. 15If it seems bad to you to serve the LORD, choose today whom you will serve. You may serve the gods your fathers served on the other side of the river. Or you may serve the gods of the Amorites, whose land you live in. But my family and I will serve the LORD."

The People Promise to Serve the LORD

16The people answered, "We would never leave the LORD and serve other gods! 17The LORD our God brought us and our fathers up from Egypt. He brought us out of slavery in Egypt. He did great miracles we could see. He kept us safe on the whole road we travelledtravelledtraveled. He kept us safe among all the peoples we passed through. 18The LORD drove out all the peoples before us. He drove out the Amorites who lived in the land. So we will serve the LORD too, for HeheHe is our God."

19Joshua said to the people, "YouyouYou cannot serve the LORD. He is a holy God. He is a jealous God. He will not forgive your sins and wrongs if you turn away. 20If you leave the LORD and serve foreign gods, HeheHe will turn against you. He will hurt you and destroy you, even after HeheHe has been good to you."

21The people said to Joshua, "No! We will serve the LORD."

22Joshua said, "YouyouYou are witnesses against yourselves. You have chosen the LORD to serve."

The people said, "We are witnesses."

23Joshua said, "Then get rid of the foreign gods among you. Give your hearts to the LORD, the God of Israel."

24The people said to Joshua, "We will serve the LORD our God. We will obey HishisHis voice."

Joshua Makes an Agreement

25That day Joshua made an agreement with the people at Shechem. He set rules for them.

26Joshua wrote these words in God's LawlawLaw BookbookBook. He took a large stone and set it up there under the oak tree near the LORD's holy place. 27Joshua said to all the people, "This stone will be a reminder for us. It heard all the words the LORD spoke to us. It will be a reminder for you, so you will not deny your God."

28Then Joshua sent the people away. Each man went to his own land.

Joshua Dies

29After these things, Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died. He was 110 years old. 30They buried him at Timnath Serah in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

31Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua. They also served the LORD all the days of the elders who lived after Joshua. These elders knew all the things the LORD had done for Israel.

Joseph's Bones and Eleazar

32The bones of Joseph, which the people of Israel brought up from Egypt, were buried at Shechem. They were buried in the piece of land Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father. Jacob paid 100 pieces of silver for it. It became the land of Joseph's children. 33Eleazar son of Aaron died. They buried him at Gibeah, which belonged to his son Phinehas, in the hill country of Ephraim.
