Job Was a Good Man

1There was a man in the land of Uz. His name was Job. He was good and honest. He respected God and stayed away from bad things. 2He had seven sons and three daughters. 3He owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 pairs of oxen, and 500 donkeys. He had many workers. He was the greatest man in the east. 4His sons took turns having parties at their homes. They invited their three sisters to eat and drink with them. 5After the parties, Job would call his children and make them clean. He got up early and gave burnt offerings for each one. He thought, "Maybe my children did wrong. Maybe they cursed God in their hearts." Job did this every time.

Job Praises God

20Then Job stood up. He tore his robe. He shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped. 21He said, "I was born with nothing. I will go back with nothing. The LORD gave, and the LORD took away. May the LORD's name be praised." 22In all this, Job did not sin. He did not blame God.
