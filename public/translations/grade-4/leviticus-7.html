Rules for the Guilt Offering

1This is the rule for the guilt offering. It is very holy. 2The guilt offering must be killed where the burnt offering is killed. The priest must splash its blood all around the altar.

3He must bring all its fat. He must bring the fat tail and the fat that covers the inside parts. 4He must bring the two kidneys and the fat on them near the lower back. He must bring the part of the liver near the kidneys. He must take it off.

5The priest must burn these on the altar. They are a gift made by fire to the LORD. This is a guilt offering.

6Every male priest may eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place. It is very holy.

7The guilt offering is like the sin offering. The same rule is for both. The priest who uses it to make up for sin keeps it.

8If a priest gives a burnt offering for someone, the skin of that burnt offering belongs to that priest.

9Every grain offering baked in an oven, cooked in a pan, or made on a flat pan belongs to the priest who gives it. 10Every grain offering, dry or mixed with oil, belongs to all of Aaron's sons in the same way.

Rules for the Peace Offering

11This is the rule for the peace offering that a person may give to the LORD.

12If he gives it to say thank you, he must bring flat bread with no yeast mixed with oil, thin bread with no yeast spread with oil, and cakes of good flour mixed with oil. He must bring them with the thank offering.

13He must also bring bread with yeast with his peace offering of thanks.

14He must bring one of these as a gift to the LORD. It belongs to the priest who splashes the blood of the peace offerings.

15The meat of the thank offering must be eaten on the day he gives it. None of it may be left until morning.

16If the offering is a promise gift or a free gift, it may be eaten on the day he gives it. What is left may be eaten the next day.

17But any meat left until the third day must be burned up.

18If any meat from his peace offering is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not count for the person who gave it. It will be a very bad thing. The person who eats it will be guilty.

19Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten. It must be burned up. But meat that is clean may be eaten by anyone who is clean.

20But if anyone who is unclean eats the meat of the peace offering that belongs to the LORD, that person will be removed from his people.

21If anyone touches anything unclean, whether a person's uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean, gross thing, and then eats the meat of the LORD's peace offering, that person will be removed from his people.

Do Not Eat Fat or Blood

22Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 23He said, "Speak to the Israelites and say, 'YouyouYou must not eat the fat of an ox, a sheep, or a goat.'"

24ThetheThe fat of an animal that died or was torn by wild animals may be used for other things. But you must not eat it.

25Anyone who eats the fat of an animal given to the LORD as a gift by fire will be removed from his people.

26You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal, anywhere you live. 27Anyone who eats blood will be removed from his people.

The Priest's Share

28Then the LORD spoke to Moses. 29He said, "Speak to the Israelites and say, 'When anyone gives a peace offering to the LORD, he must bring to the LORD a gift from his peace offering. 30He must bring it with his own hands. He must bring the fat and the breast. He must wave the breast before the LORD. 31The priest must burn the fat on the altar. But the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons. 32You must give the right thigh to the priest. It is a gift from your peace offerings. 33The priest who gives the blood and fat of the peace offering gets the right thigh as his share. 34I have taken the breast that is waved and the thigh that is given from the Israelites' peace offerings. I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons. This is their share from the Israelites forever.'"

35This is the share of the gifts burned for the LORD for Aaron and his sons. It was given on the day they were brought to serve as priests to the LORD. 36On the day oil was poured on them, the LORD commanded that the Israelites give this to them. It is a share that lasts forever for all the years to come.

A Summary of the Offerings
