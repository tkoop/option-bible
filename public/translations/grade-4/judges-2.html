The LORD's Messenger Speaks to the People

1The angel of the LORD went up from Gilgal to Bochim. He said, "I brought you out of Egypt. I led you into the land I promised to your fathers. I said, 'I will never break MymyMy promise to you.' 2YouyouYou must not make a promise with the people who live in this land. You must tear down their altars. But you did not listen to MemeMe. What have you done? 3So now I say I will not drive them out in front of you. They will cause you trouble. Their gods will trap you."

The People Cry and Offer Sacrifices

4After the angel of the LORD said these words to all the people of Israel, they cried loudly and wept. 5They named that place Bochim. There they gave offerings to the LORD.

After Joshua Leads the People

6Joshua sent the people of Israel away. Each man went to his own land to take it. 7The people served the LORD while Joshua lived. They also served HimhimHim while the older leaders lived after Joshua. Those leaders had seen all the great things the LORD did for Israel. 8Joshua son of Nun was the the LORD's servant. He died at 110 years old. 9They buried him in his land at Timnath-heres, in the hills of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

10After that whole group died, a new group grew up. They did not know the LORD. They did not know the things HeheHe had done for Israel.

Israel Worships False Gods

11Then the people of Israel did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD. They served gods called Baal. 12They left the LORD, the God of their fathers. He had brought them out of Egypt. They followed other gods, the gods of the people around them. They bowed down to them. They made the LORD angry. 13They left the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.

14The LORD was very angry with Israel. He let raiders rob them. He let their enemies capture them. They could not beat their enemies anymore. 15Everywhere Israel went, the LORD worked against them and brought trouble, just as HeheHe had promised. So they were very upset.

The LORD Sends Judges

16Then the LORD raised up judges. The judges saved them from the raiders. 17But the people would not listen to their judges. They ran after other gods and bowed down to them. They quickly left the way their fathers had walked. Their fathers had obeyed the the LORD's commands. They did not do the same. 18When the LORD raised up a judge for them, HeheHe was with that judge. The judge saved them from their enemies while the judge lived. The LORD felt sorry for them when they cried out because their enemies hurt them. 19But when the judge died, the people did even worse than their fathers. They went after other gods to serve and bow down to them. They did not stop their bad ways. They stayed stubborn.

The LORD Leaves Some Nations

20The LORD was very angry with Israel. He said, "This group broke MymyMy promise. I told their fathers to keep it. They did not listen to MemeMe. 21So I will not drive out the nations that Joshua left when he died. 22I will use them to test if Israel will follow the LORD's way, like their fathers did." 23So the LORD let those nations stay. He did not drive them out quickly. He did not give them to Joshua.
