The Feast of Trumpets

1On the first day of the seventh month, you must have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work. It is a day to blow trumpets. 2As a pleasing smell to the LORD, bring one burnt offering: one young bull, one ram, and seven one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 3Bring grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah with the bull, two-tenths with the ram, 4and one-tenth with each of the seven lambs. 5Also bring one male goat as a sin offering to make things right for you. 6These are in addition to the monthly burnt offering and its grain offering, and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings. They are a pleasing smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

The Day of Forgiveness

7On the tenth day of this seventh month, you must have a holy meeting. You must be humble and not do any work. 8Bring to the LORD a burnt offering that smells good: one young bull, one ram, and seven one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 9Their grain offerings are three-tenths of an ephah with the bull, two-tenths with the ram, 10and one-tenth with each of the seven lambs. 11Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the sin offering for forgiveness and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings.

The Feast of Shelters

12On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you must have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work. Celebrate a feast to the LORD for seven days. 13As a pleasing smell to the LORD, bring a burnt offering made by fire: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 14Bring grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah with each of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths with each of the two rams, 15and one-tenth with each of the fourteen lambs. 16Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

Offerings for Each Day

17On the second day, you must bring twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 18Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 19Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings.

20On the third day, bring eleven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 21Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 22Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

23On the fourth day, bring ten young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 24Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 25Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

26On the fifth day, bring nine young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 27Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 28Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

29On the sixth day, bring eight young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 30Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 31Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings.

32On the seventh day, bring seven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 33Bring grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 34Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

The Eighth Day

35On the eighth day, you must have a holy meeting. Do not do your regular work. 36As a pleasing smell to the LORD, bring a burnt offering made by fire: one bull, one ram, and seven one-year-old male lambs. They must have nothing wrong with them. 37Bring grain and drink offerings for the bull, the ram, and the lambs, matching their number, as the rules say. 38Also bring one male goat as a sin offering. This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

Other Offerings

39You must bring these to the LORD at your special feasts. This is in addition to your promised offerings and free-will offerings. These can be burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings, or peace offerings. 40So Moses told the Israelites everything the LORD had told him to do.
