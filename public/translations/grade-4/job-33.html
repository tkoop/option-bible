Elihu Talks to Job

1But now, Job, listen to my words. Hear all my words. 2Look, I open my mouth. My tongue speaks. 3My words show my heart is right. My lips speak what I know. 4God's SpiritspiritSpirit made me. The breath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty gives me life. 5Answer me if you can. Put your words in order. Stand before me. 6Look, I am like you to God. I was also made from clay. 7Do not be afraid of me. I will not press hard on you. 8I heard what you said. I heard your words.

Job Said He Was Clean

9You said, “I am clean. I have not sinned. I have no wrong in me. 10He finds reasons to be against me. He counts me as HishisHis enemy. 11He puts my feet in chains. He watches all my ways.”

Elihu Says God Is Greater

12But you are not right in this. I will answer you, because God is greater than man. 13Why do you argue with God? You say, “He will not answer people's words.” 14God speaks in one way, and even in two ways, but people do not notice.

God Speaks in Dreams

15In a dream, in a vision at night, when deep sleep falls on people, while they lie on their beds, 16then HeheHe opens people's ears and scares them with warnings. 17He does this to turn people from what they do and to hide pride from them. 18He keeps their soul from the grave. He keeps them from dying by the sword.

God Corrects Sick People

19A man may be corrected with pain in bed. His bones may hurt all the time. 20He may hate bread. He may not want good food. 21His body wastes away until no one can see it. His bones stick out. 22His soul comes near the grave. His life comes near those who kill.

An Angel Speaks for a Man

23If there is an angel for him, one messenger out of a thousand, to tell the man what is right, 24then God is kind to him and says, “Save him from going down to the grave. I have found a price.” 25His body becomes fresh like a child's. He goes back to his young days.

God Forgives and Saves

26Then the man prays to God. God accepts him. He sees God's face with joy. God gives back his right standing. 27Then he sings before people and says, “I sinned. I did wrong. But it was not paid back to me. 28God saved my soul from the grave. My life will see the light.”

God Does This Again and Again

29Look, God does all these things two or three times with a man. 30He does this to bring his soul back from the grave, so he can shine with the light of life.

Elihu Asks Job to Listen

31Pay attention, Job. Listen to me. Be quiet, and I will speak. 32If you have words, answer me. Speak, because I want to show you are right. 33If not, listen to me. Be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.
