David Calls Israel’s Leaders

1King David called all of Israel’s leaders to Jerusalem. He called the leaders of the tribes, the army leaders who served the king, the leaders of thousands and hundreds, and the men in charge of the king’s and his sons’ things and animals. He also called the palace workers, the strong men, and all the brave soldiers.

David Speaks to the People

2King David stood up and said, “Listen to me, my brothers and my people. I wanted to build a home where the box of God’s promise could rest. It would be a place for our God’s feet. I got ready to build it.”

3But God said to me, “You are not to build a house for MymyMy name, because you are a man who fought wars and spilled blood.”

4David said, “Yet the LORD, the God of Israel, chose me from all my father’s family to be king over Israel forever. He chose Judah to lead. From Judah’s family HeheHe chose my father’s family. From my father’s sons HeheHe was happy to make me king over all Israel.”

5“Of all my sons—for the LORD has given me many sons—HeheHe chose my son Solomon to sit on the throne over the LORD’s kingdom in Israel.”

6God said to me, “Solomon your son is the one who will build MymyMy house and MymyMy yards. I chose him to be MymyMy son, and I will be his FatherfatherFather.”

7“I will make his kingdom last forever, if he is strong to do MymyMy commands and MymyMy rules, like he does today.”

8David said, “So now, with all Israel watching and with our God listening, keep and look for all the commands of the LORD your God. Then you may own this good land and leave it to your children after you, forever.”

9“And you, Solomon my son, know the God of your father. Serve HimhimHim with a whole heart and a willing mind. The LORD looks at all hearts and understands every thought. If you look for HimhimHim, you will find HimhimHim. But if you leave HimhimHim, HeheHe will leave you forever.”

10“See now that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the holy place. Be strong and do it.”

David Gives Solomon the Plans

11Then David gave his son Solomon the plans for the front porch of the temple, its buildings, its storage rooms, its upper rooms, its inner rooms, and the room for the cover of the box.

12The plans had everything he had in mind for the yards of the LORD’s house, for all the rooms around it, for the storage rooms of God’s house, and for the storage rooms for the holy things.

13They also had plans for the groups of priests and helpers, for all the work in the LORD’s house, and for all the tools used in the LORD’s house.

14He set the weight of gold for all gold tools used in every kind of work, and the weight of silver for all silver tools.

15He set the weight of gold for the gold lampstands and their lamps, and the weight of silver for the silver lampstands and their lamps, for the work each one did.

16He also set the weight of gold for the tables that held the special bread, and the weight of silver for the silver tables.

17He gave pure gold for the forks, the sprinkling bowls, and the pitchers. He set the weight of gold for each of these. For the silver things, he set their weight of silver.

18He set the weight of pure gold for the altar where sweet spices were burned, and for the gold seat with angels whose wings spread out and cover the box of God’s promise.

The Plans Come from the LORD

19David said, “All of this is written down for me. The LORD gave me these plans. He helped me understand every part.”

David Encourages Solomon

20Then David said to his son Solomon, “Be strong and brave. Do the work. Do not be afraid or sad. The LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you or leave you until the LORD’s house is finished.”

21“The groups of priests and helpers are ready to work in God’s house. Every skilled worker who wants to help will be there for you. The leaders and all the people will follow your orders.”
