The Two Witnesses

1I was given a measuring stick like a rod. A voice said, "Get up and measure God's temple and the altar, and count the people worshiping there." 2But do not measure the outer courtyard. Leave it out, because it was given to the nations. They will walk all over the holy city for 42 months.

3I will give power to my two witnesses. They will speak God's message for 1,260 days. They will wear rough cloth. 4These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.

5If anyone tries to hurt them, fire comes from their mouths and eats up their enemies. Anyone who tries to hurt them must be killed. 6They have power to shut the sky so rain does not fall while they speak God's message. They have power to turn water into blood. They can hit the earth with any plague whenever they want.

7When they finish speaking, the beast that comes up from the deep pit will fight them. It will beat them and kill them. 8Their bodies will lie in the street of the big city.
