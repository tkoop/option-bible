Twenty-Five Men at the East Gate

1Then the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up. He brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD. It faces east. At the gate, I saw twenty-five men. I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah. They were leaders of the people.

God Tells Ezekiel to Speak Against the Leaders

2He said to me, "SonsonSon of man, these men plan to do wrong. They give bad advice in this city. 3They say, 'Isn't it time to build houses? This city is like a pot, and we are the meat inside.' 4So speak against them. Speak, son of man."

5Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came on me. He said, "Speak. Tell them this is what the LORD says: You have said these things, people of Israel. I know what you think. 6You have killed many people in this city. You filled its streets with dead bodies. 7So this is what the Lord GOD says: The dead you put in the city are the meat. The city is the pot. But I will take you out of it. 8You are afraid of the sword, so I will bring the sword on you, says the Lord GOD.

"9I will take you out of the city. I will put you in the hands of strangers. I will punish you. 10You will die by the sword. I will judge you at the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 11This city will not be your pot. You will not be the meat in it. I will judge you at the border of Israel. 12Then you will know that I am the LORD. Ezekiel Cries Out to God

God Speaks About Ezekiel's Brothers

14Then the word of the LORD came to me. He said, 15"SonsonSon of man, your brothers, your family, and all Israel are the ones the people in Jerusalem talk about. They say, 'They are far from the LORD. This land is ours to keep.'

"16So tell them this is what the Lord GOD says: I sent them to many nations. I spread them in other lands. But for a short time I have been a safe place for them where they went. 17So tell them this is what the Lord GOD says: I will gather you from the nations. I will bring you together from the countries where you were scattered. I will give you the land of Israel. 18They will go there. They will take away all the bad idols and wrong things.

"19I will give them one heart. I will put a new spirit in them. I will take away their hard heart.
