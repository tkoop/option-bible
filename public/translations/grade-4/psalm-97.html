The LORD Rules the Earth

1The LORD rules. Let the earth be happy. Let the faraway islands be glad. 2Clouds and thick darkness are around HimhimHim. Right and fair hold up HishisHis throne. 3Fire goes before HimhimHim. It burns HishisHis enemies all around. 4His lightning lights up the world. The EarthEarthearth sees it and shakes. 5The mountains melt like wax before the the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth. 6The sky shows HishisHis right ways. All people see HishisHis glory. 7People who worship statues are put to shame. Those who brag about fake gods are put to shame. All gods, worship HimhimHim.

God's People Are Glad

8Zion hears and is glad. The people of Judah are glad because of YouryourYour fair rules, O the LORD. 9For YouyouYou, the the LORD, are highest over all the earth. You are lifted up far above all gods. 10You who love the the LORD, hate what is bad. He keeps HishisHis people safe. He saves them from bad people. 11Light is planted for good people. Joy is planted for those with honest hearts. 12You who do right, be glad in the the LORD. Thank HishisHis holy name.
