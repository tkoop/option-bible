Love and Spiritual Gifts

1Follow love. But you should also want gifts from the SpiritspiritSpirit. Most of all, you should want to speak God's message. 2A person who speaks in an unknown language is not talking to people. He is talking to God. No one understands him. He speaks secret things by the SpiritspiritSpirit. 3But a person who speaks God's message talks to people. It helps them grow. It gives them courage. It gives them comfort. 4A person who speaks in an unknown language helps himself grow. But a person who speaks God's message helps the whole church grow.

5I want all of you to speak in unknown languages. But I want even more for you to speak God's message. The one who speaks God's message is greater than the one who speaks in an unknown language. That is true unless he explains what he said. Then the church can grow.

6Now, brothers and sisters, if I come to you speaking in an unknown language, how will that help you? It will not help unless I also speak to you. I could share a truth God showed me. I could share what I know. I could speak God's message. Or I could teach.

Speaking Clearly

7Even things without life make sounds. A flute or a harp makes a sound. But how will anyone know the tune if the notes are not clear? 8If a horn makes a sound no one can tell, who will get ready for battle? 9It is the same with you. If you do not speak clear words, how will anyone know what you said? You will just be talking to the air.

10There are many kinds of languages in the world. All of them mean something. 11If I do not know what the language means, I will seem like a foreigner to the speaker. And the speaker will seem like a foreigner to me.

12It is the same with you. You want gifts from the SpiritspiritSpirit so much. So try hard to help the church grow. 13So a person who speaks in an unknown language should pray. He should pray that he can explain it.

14If I pray in an unknown language, my spirit prays. But my mind is not helped. 15So what should I do? I will pray with my spirit. I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit. I will also sing with my mind.

16If you praise God only with your spirit, how can someone new say “Amen” to your thanks? He does not know what you are saying. 17You may thank God well. But the other person is not helped.

18I thank God that I speak in unknown languages more than all of you. 19But in church I would rather say five words people can understand. Then I can teach others. I would rather do that than say ten thousand words in an unknown language.

20Brothers and sisters, do not think like children. But be like babies when it comes to doing wrong. In your thinking, be grown up.

21The LawlawLaw says, “I will speak to these people with strange languages and foreign lips. But even then, they will not listen to MemeMe,” says the Lord.

Strange Languages and God's Message

22So unknown languages are a sign. They are for people who do not believe. They are not for believers. But speaking God's message is a sign. It is for believers. It is not for people who do not believe.

23So if the whole church meets together and everyone speaks in unknown languages, what will happen? If new people or people who do not believe come in, won't they say you are crazy?

24But if everyone speaks God's message and a person who does not believe or a new person comes in, he will be shown he is wrong. Everyone will show him. 25The secrets of his heart will come to light. Then he will fall on his face and worship God. He will say, “God is really here with you.”

Order in Church Meetings

26So what should we do, brothers and sisters? When you meet, each person has something. One has a song. One has a teaching. One has a truth God showed. One has an unknown language. One has an explanation. Do everything to help the church grow.

27If anyone speaks in an unknown language, only two or three should speak. They should take turns. And one person should explain it. 28If no one can explain it, the speaker should be quiet in church. He can speak to himself and to God.

29Two or three prophets should speak. The others should decide if it is right. 30But if God shows something to another person who is sitting there, the first speaker should stop. 31All of you can speak God's message one by one. Then everyone can learn. Everyone can be encouraged. 32The spirits of the prophets are under the prophets' control. 33God is not a God of trouble. He is a God of peace. This is true in all the churches of God's people.

Women in Church

34Women should be quiet in the churches. They are not allowed to speak. They should follow, just as the LawlawLaw says. 35If they want to learn something, they should ask their own husbands at home. It is not right for a woman to speak in church.

36Did God's word start with you? Or did it come only to you? 37If anyone thinks he is a prophet or has a spiritual gift, he should know this. What I write to you is a command from the Lord. 38If anyone does not accept this, he will not be accepted.

Final Words

39So, my brothers and sisters, want to speak God's message. And do not stop people from speaking in unknown languages. 40But everything should be done in a good and orderly way.
