The LORD Gives Rules for Skin Spots

1Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

2“If a person has a raised spot, a scab, or a bright spot on the skin, and it may be a skin disease, he must be brought to Aaron the priest, or to one of his sons who is a priest. 3The priest must look at the spot. If the hair in the spot has turned white and the spot looks deeper than the skin, it is a skin disease. The priest will say the person is not clean.

4“If the bright spot is white but does not look deeper than the skin, and the hair has not turned white, the priest must keep the person away from others for seven days. 5On the seventh day the priest must look again. If the spot has not changed and has not spread, the priest must keep him away for seven more days. 6On the seventh day the priest must look again. If the spot has faded and has not spread, the person must wash his clothes. Then he is clean.

7“But if the scab spreads on the skin after he was shown to the priest to be made clean, he must be shown to the priest again. 8If the priest sees that the scab has spread, he must say the person is not clean. It is a skin disease.

A Long-Lasting Skin Disease

9“When a person has a skin disease, he must be brought to the priest. 10The priest must look. If there is a white raised spot, and the hair has turned white, and there is raw flesh in the spot, 11it is an old skin disease. The priest must say the person is not clean. He must not keep him away, because he is already not clean.

12“If the skin disease spreads all over the skin, from head to foot, as far as the priest can see, 13the priest must look. If it covers all his skin, he must say the person is clean. It has all turned white, so he is clean. 14But if raw flesh shows up, he is not clean. 15The priest must look at the raw flesh and say the person is not clean. Raw flesh is not clean. It is a skin disease. 16If the raw flesh turns white again, he must come to the priest. 17The priest must look. If the spot has turned white, the priest must say the person is clean. Then he is clean.

Spots After a Boil

18“If a person has a boil on the skin and it heals, 19and then a white raised spot or a reddish-white spot appears where the boil was, he must be shown to the priest. 20The priest must look. If it looks deeper than the skin and the hair has turned white, it is a skin disease that has broken out in the boil. The priest must say the person is not clean. 21If the hair has not turned white, and it is not deeper than the skin, but it is faded, the priest must keep him away for seven days. 22If it spreads on the skin, the priest must say it is not clean. It is a skin disease. 23If the spot stays in its place and does not spread, it is only the scar from the boil. The priest must say the person is clean.

Spots After a Burn

24“If a person has a burn on the skin, and the healed burn has a reddish-white or white spot, 25the priest must look at it. If the hair in the spot has turned white and it looks deeper than the skin, it is a skin disease that has broken out in the burn. The priest must say the person is not clean. It is a skin disease. 26If the hair has not turned white, and it is not deeper than the skin, but it is faded, the priest must keep him away for seven days. 27On the seventh day the priest must look. If it has spread on the skin, the priest must say the person is not clean. It is a skin disease. 28If the spot stays in its place and does not spread, and it is faded, it is only the scar from the burn. The priest must say the person is clean.

Spots on the Head or Beard

29“If a man or a woman has a spot on the head or in the beard, 30the priest must look at it. If it looks deeper than the skin, and the hair in it is yellow and thin, it is a scaly skin disease on the head or beard. The priest must say the person is not clean. 31If the spot does not look deeper than the skin, and there is no black hair in it, the priest must keep the person away for seven days. 32On the seventh day the priest must look. If the spot has not spread, and there is no yellow hair, and it does not look deeper than the skin, 33the person must shave, but not the spot. The priest must keep him away for seven more days. 34On the seventh day the priest must look. If the spot has not spread and does not look deeper than the skin, the priest must say the person is clean. He must wash his clothes, and he is clean.

35“But if the spot spreads after he was made clean, 36the priest must look. If it has spread, the priest must say the person is not clean. He does not need to look for yellow hair. The person is not clean. 37If the spot stays the same and black hair grows in it, the spot has healed. The person is clean. The priest must say he is clean.

Other Skin Spots

38“If a man or a woman has white spots on the skin, 39and the priest sees that the spots are dull white, it is only a rash on the skin. The person is clean.

Bald Heads

40“If a man loses his hair and becomes bald, he is clean. 41If he loses hair at the front of his head, he is bald in front, but he is clean. 42If there is a reddish-white spot on his bald head or forehead, it is a skin disease breaking out. 43The priest must look at it. If the raised spot is reddish-white, like a skin disease on the skin of the body, 44the man is sick with a skin disease. He is not clean. The priest must say he is not clean, because of the spot on his head.

A Person with the Disease

45“The person with the skin disease must wear torn clothes. His hair must be loose. He must cover the lower part of his face. He must call out, ‘Not clean! Not clean!’ 46He is not clean as long as he has the spot. He must live alone. He must stay outside the camp.

Spots on Clothes

47“If a garment has a spot that may be a skin disease, in wool or linen, 48or in woven or knitted cloth of linen or wool, or in leather, or in anything made of leather, 49and the spot is greenish or reddish, it must be shown to the priest. 50The priest must look at the spot and keep the garment away from others for seven days. 51On the seventh day the priest must look. If the spot has spread in the cloth, in the woven or knitted part, or in the leather, or in anything made of leather, it is a spreading mildew. It is not clean. 52He must burn the garment, the woven or knitted cloth, the wool or linen, or any leather thing that has the spot. It is a spreading mildew, so it must be burned.

53“If the priest sees that the spot has not spread in the cloth, in the woven or knitted part, or in the leather, 54the priest must order the spot to be washed. Then he must keep the garment away for seven more days. 55After it is washed, the priest must look. If the spot has not changed, even if it has not spread, it is not clean. It must be burned. It is a bad mildew, on the front or back. 56If the priest sees that the spot has faded after washing, he must tear it out of the cloth, the leather, the woven part, or the knitted part. 57If it shows up again in the cloth, in the woven or knitted part, or in the leather, it is spreading. The spot must be burned. 58If the spot is gone after washing, the cloth, the woven or knitted part, or the leather must be washed again. Then it is clean. 59This is the law for a spot that may be a skin disease in wool or linen cloth, in woven or knitted cloth, or in leather. It tells how to make it clean or not clean.”
