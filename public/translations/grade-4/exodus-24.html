The LORD Calls Moses Up

1Then the LORD said to Moses, "Come up to the LORD, you and Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel's leaders. Worship HimhimHim from far away." 2Only Moses may come close to the LORD. The others must not come close, and the people must not go up with him.

Moses Tells the People

3Moses came and told the people all the words of the LORD and all HishisHis rules. All the people answered together, "We will do everything the LORD has said." 4Moses wrote down all the words of the LORD. Early in the morning, he got up and built an altar at the foot of the mountain. He set up twelve stone pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel.

Offerings and the Covenant

5He sent some young men of Israel. They offered burnt offerings to the LORD and sacrificed bulls as peace offerings. 6Moses took half of the blood and put it in bowls. He sprinkled the other half on the altar. 7Then he took the BookbookBook of the Agreement and read it aloud to the people. They said, "We will do everything the LORD has said, and we will obey." 8Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people. He said, "Look! This is the blood of the agreement the LORD has made with you, based on all these words."

Leaders See God

9Then Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel's leaders went up the mountain. 10They saw the God of Israel. Under HishisHis feet was something like a floor made of clear blue stone, as clear as the sky. 11He did not hurt the leaders of Israel. They saw God, and they ate and drank.

Moses Gets Stone Tablets

12The LORD said to Moses, "Come up to MemeMe on the mountain and stay there. I will give you stone tablets with the law and the commands I have written, so you can teach them."

Moses and Joshua Go Up

13Moses got up, and Joshua, who helped him, went with him. Then Moses went up to God's mountain. 14He said to the leaders, "Wait here for us until we come back to you. Aaron and Hur are with you. If anyone has a problem, he can go to them."

The Cloud Covers the Mountain

15Moses went up the mountain, and the cloud covered it. 16The glory of the LORD rested on Mount Sinai. The cloud covered it for six days. On the seventh day, HeheHe called to Moses from inside the cloud. 17To the people of Israel, the glory of the LORD looked like a fire that burns everything, on top of the mountain. 18Moses went into the cloud and up the mountain. He stayed there forty days and forty nights.
