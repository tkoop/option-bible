David Shows Kindness to Hanun

1After this, the king of the Ammonites died. His son Hanun became king. 2David said, "I will be kind to Hanun son of Nahash, just like his father was kind to me." So David sent men to comfort him. They went to the land of Ammon.

The Ammonite Leaders Doubt David

3The leaders of the Ammonites said to Hanun, their lord, "Do you think David is honouringhonouringhonoring your father? He sent men to comfort you. But David may have sent them to look around the city, spy on it, and destroy it."

4So Hanun took David's men. He shaved off half of each man's beard. He cut their clothes in half up to their hips. Then he sent them away.

David's Men Come Home

5People told David what happened. He sent men to meet them, because they were very ashamed. The king said, "Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back. Then come home."

Ammon Hires Soldiers

6The Ammonites saw that David was angry with them. So they hired soldiers from Aram. They got 20,000 foot soldiers from Beth Rehob and Zobah, 12,000 men from Maacah, and men from Tob.

David Sends His Army

7David heard about it. He sent Joab and all his strong soldiers. 8The Ammonites came out and got ready to fight at the city gate. The Arameans from Zobah, Rehob, Tob, and Maacah stayed out in the field by themselves.

Joab and Abishai Make a Plan

9Joab saw that the enemy was in front of him and behind him. He picked the best soldiers of Israel and lined them up against the Arameans. 10He gave the rest of the men to his brother Abishai. They lined up against the Ammonites.

11Joab said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, you come help me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will come save you." 12He also said, "Be strong! Let us be brave for our people and for the cities of our God. And may the LORD do what is good in HishisHis eyes."

The First Battle

13Joab and his men moved forward to fight the Arameans. The Arameans ran away. 14When the Ammonites saw that the Arameans had run away, they also ran from Abishai. They went back into the city. Then Joab left the Ammonites and went back to Jerusalem.

Aram Tries Again

15The Arameans saw that Israel had beaten them. So they came together again. 16Hadadezer sent for more Arameans from across the river. They came to Helam. Shobach, the leader of Hadadezer's army, led them.

David Fights at Helam

17Someone told David. He gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan River, and went to Helam. The Arameans lined up against David and fought him.

David Wins the Battle

18The Arameans ran from Israel. David killed 700 chariot soldiers and 40,000 horse soldiers. He also hit Shobach, the army leader, and Shobach died there.

Aram Makes Peace

19All the kings who served Hadadezer saw that Israel had beaten them. They made peace with Israel and served Israel. The Arameans were afraid to help the Ammonites again.
