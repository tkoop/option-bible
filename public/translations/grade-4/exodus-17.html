2The people argued with Moses. They said, "Give us water to drink." Moses said to them, "Why do you argue with me? Why do you test the the LORD?"

3The people were thirsty. They complained to Moses. They said, "Why did you bring us out of Egypt? Did you bring us here to die of thirst, us and our children and our animals?"

4Moses cried out to the the LORD. He said, "What should I do with these people? They are almost ready to throw stones at me."

Amalek Attacks Israel

8The Amalekites came and fought Israel at Rephidim. 9Moses said to Joshua, "Pick some men for us. Go out and fight Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on top of the hill. I will hold God's stick in my hand."

10Joshua did what Moses said. He fought Amalek. Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill. 11When Moses held up his hand, Israel was stronger. When he put his hand down, Amalek was stronger.

12Moses' hands got tired. So they put a stone under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur held up his hands. One stood on one side, and one on the other side. His hands stayed up until the sun went down. 13Joshua beat Amalek and his people with the sword.
