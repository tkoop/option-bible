God Tells Noah to Get in the Ark

1The LORD said to Noah, "Come into the ark. You and your whole family come in, because I see that you are good. 2Take seven pairs of every clean animal. Take a male and a female. Take one pair of every animal that is not clean. 3Take seven pairs of birds too. This will keep life going on the earth. 4In seven days I will make it rain for forty days and forty nights. I will wipe out every living thing I made from the earth."

5Noah did everything the LORD told him. 6Noah was 600 years old when the flood waters came on the earth. 7Noah, his wife, his sons, and their wives went into the ark to get away from the flood water.

8Clean animals and animals that are not clean came too. Birds and all small animals that move on the ground came. 9They came to Noah in the ark two by two, male and female. This was just as God had told Noah. 10After seven days, the flood water was on the earth.

The Flood Starts

11Noah was 600 years old. It was the second month, day 17. On that day, springs under the earth burst open. The windows in the sky opened too. 12Rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights. 13On that same day, Noah went into the ark. His sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth went too. Noah's wife and the wives of his three sons went with them. 14They went with every kind of wild animal, every kind of farm animal, every kind of small animal that moves on the ground, and every kind of bird. 15They came to Noah in pairs, two of every living thing that breathes. 16They came in as God told Noah. Then the LORD closed the door.

The Water Covers the Earth

17The flood lasted forty days. The water got higher and lifted the ark up off the ground. 18The water kept rising. The ark floated on top of the water. 19The water rose so high that it covered all the tall mountains under the sky. 20The water rose about 22 feet above the mountains.

21Every living thing on EarthEarthearth died. Birds, farm animals, wild animals, small creatures, and all people died. 22Everything on dry land that breathed through its nose died. 23Everything on the ground was wiped away. Only Noah and the people with him in the ark were left. 24The water stayed high for 150 days.
