God Sends Locusts

1Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh. I have made his heart and his helpers' hearts stubborn. I want to do these signs of MinemineMine among them. 2Then you can tell your children and grandchildren how I punished Egypt. Tell them about the signs I did there. Then you will know that I am the LORD."

3So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh. They said, "This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: 'How long will you refuse to obey MemeMe? Let MymyMy people go, so they can worship MemeMe. 4If you do not let MymyMy people go, tomorrow I will send locusts into your land. 5They will cover the ground. No one will see the dirt. They will eat what the hail left. They will eat every tree in your fields. 6They will fill your houses and your helpers' houses. They will fill all the houses in Egypt. Your fathers and grandfathers never saw anything like this. It has never happened since people first lived here.' " Then Moses turned and left Pharaoh.

7Pharaoh's helpers said to him, "How long will this man cause trouble for us? Let the people go so they can worship the LORD their God. Don't you see that Egypt is ruined?"

8So Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh. He said, "Go, worship the LORD your God. But who is going?"

9Moses said, "We will go with our young and old. We will go with our sons and daughters. We will go with our sheep and cows. We must have a feast for the LORD."

10Pharaoh said, "May the LORD be with you if I ever let you go with your little ones! You are planning something bad. 11No, only the men may go and worship the LORD, since that is what you are asking for." Then he made them leave.

12Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch your hand over Egypt. Let locusts come over the land. Let them eat every plant. Let them eat everything the hail left."

13So Moses held his stick over Egypt. The LORD sent a strong east wind all day and all night. By morning, the wind had brought the locusts. 14The locusts came over all Egypt. They settled in every part of the country. There had never been so many locusts before, and there will never be so many again. 15They covered the ground, and the land became dark. They ate every plant and all the fruit on the trees that the hail had left. No green thing was left on any tree or plant in Egypt.

16Pharaoh quickly called Moses and Aaron. He said, "I have sinned against the LORD your God and against you. 17Please forgive my sin this one time. Pray to the LORD your God. Ask HimhimHim to take this deadly trouble away from me."

18Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD. 19The LORD changed the wind to a very strong west wind. It carried the locusts away and blew them into the Red SeaseaSea. Not one locust was left anywhere in Egypt. 20But the LORD made Pharaoh's heart stubborn. He did not let the Israelites go.

Darkness Covers Egypt

21Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch your hand toward the sky. Let darkness spread over Egypt. Let it be darkness so thick it can be felt."

22So Moses raised his hand toward the sky. Thick darkness covered Egypt for three days. 23No one could see anyone else. For three days, no one got up from where they were. But all the Israelites had light where they lived.

24Then Pharaoh called Moses. He said, "Go, worship the LORD. Even your little ones may go with you. But your sheep and cows must stay here."

25But Moses said, "YouyouYou must also give us animals to sacrifice and burn as gifts to the LORD our God. 26Our animals must go with us. Not one hoof will be left behind. We need some of them to worship the LORD our God. We do not know what we will need until we get there."

27But the LORD made Pharaoh's heart stubborn. He would not let them go.

28Pharaoh said to Moses, "Get away from me! Be careful. Do not come see me again. The day you see my face again, you will die!"

29Moses said, "As you say. I will never see your face again."
