God Gives Job Twice as Much

10After Job prayed for his friends, the LORD gave Job back twice as much as he had before. 11All his brothers and sisters and old friends came. They ate with him in his house. They felt sorry for him and comforted him for all the trouble the LORD had brought on him. Each one gave him a piece of silver and a gold ring. 12So the LORD blessed Job's later life more than his early life. He had 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 pairs of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.

Job's New Family

13He also had seven sons and three daughters. 14He named his first daughter Jemimah, his second Keziah, and his third Keren-happuch. 15No women in all the land were as beautiful as Job's daughters. Their father gave them a share of the family land along with their brothers.

Job's Long Life

16After this, Job lived 140 years and saw his children and their children to the fourth generation. 17Then Job died. He was old and had lived a long life.
