David Gets Ready for Battle

1David counted the people with him. He put leaders over thousands and leaders over hundreds. 2David sent out the army in three groups. One group was led by Joab. One was led by Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother. One was led by Ittai from Gath. The king said to the people, "I will go out with you."

3But the people said, "No, you should not go. If we run away, they will not care about us. Even if half of us die, they will not care. But you are worth ten thousand of us. It is better for you to help us from the city."

4The king said to them, "I will do what seems best to you." So the king stood by the gate. All the people went out by hundreds and by thousands.

David Tells His Men to Be Gentle

5The king told Joab, Abishai, and Ittai, "Be gentle with the young man Absalom, for my sake." All the people heard the king say this about Absalom.

The Battle in the Forest

6The army went out to fight Israel. The battle was in the forest of Ephraim. 7The men of Israel lost to David's servants there. That day many people died. Twenty thousand men died. 8The battle spread over the whole land. That day the forest killed more people than the sword did.

Absalom Gets Caught

9Absalom met David's servants. He was riding a mule. The mule went under the thick branches of a big oak tree. Absalom's head got stuck in the oak. The mule kept going. So he hung between the sky and the ground.

10A man saw it and told Joab. He said, "I saw Absalom hanging in an oak tree."

11Joab said to the man who told him, "YouyouYou saw him? Why didn't you strike him to the ground? I would have given you ten pieces of silver and a belt."

12The man said to Joab, "Even if I had a thousand pieces of silver in my hand, I would not hurt the king's son. We heard the king tell you, Abishai, and Ittai, 'Watch over the young man Absalom.'" 13"If I had killed him, it would not have stayed hidden from the king. And you would have turned against me."

Absalom Dies

14Joab said, "I will not wait here with you." He took three sharp sticks in his hand and stabbed them into Absalom's heart while he was still alive in the oak.

15Ten young men who carried Joab's weapons came around Absalom and hit him and killed him.

16Joab blew the trumpet. The people stopped chasing Israel. Joab held back the people.

17They took Absalom and threw him into a big hole in the forest. They piled a very big heap of stones over him. Then all Israel ran away, each to his own tent.

18While he was alive, Absalom had set up a stone pillar for himself in the King's ValleyvalleyValley. He said, "I have no son to keep my name alive." He named the pillar after himself. It is called Absalom's pillar to this day.

David Hears the News

19Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, "Let me run and tell the king the good news. The LORD has saved him from his enemies."

20Joab said to him, "YouyouYou are not the man to bring news today. You may bring news another day. But today you must not bring news, because the king's son is dead."

21Joab said to the Cushite, "Go tell the king what you saw." The Cushite bowed to Joab and ran.

22Ahimaaz son of Zadok said again to Joab, "Whatever happens, let me run too, after the Cushite."

Joab said, "Why do you want to run, my son? You will not get a reward for this news."

23Ahimaaz said, "But let me run." So Ahimaaz ran by the way of the plain. He passed the Cushite.

24David was sitting between the two gates. The watchman went up to the roof of the gate by the wall. He looked up and saw a man running alone.

25The watchman called out and told the king. The king said, "If he is alone, he brings good news." The man kept coming closer.

26The watchman saw another man running alone. He called to the gatekeeper and said, "Look, another man is running alone. This one is also bringing good news." The king said, "This one also brings good news."

27The watchman said, "I think the first runner is Ahimaaz son of Zadok." The king said, "HeheHe is a good man. He comes with good news."

28Ahimaaz called out to the king, "Peace!" He bowed down to the ground before the king. He said, "Praise the LORD your God! He has given you the win over the men who lifted their hands against my lord the king."

29The king said, "IsisIs it well with the young man Absalom?"

Ahimaaz said, "When Joab sent your servant, I saw a big crowd. But I did not know what it was."

30The king said, "Turn aside and stand here." So he stood there.

31Then the Cushite came. He said, "MymyMy lord the king, good news! The LORD has saved you today from all who rose up against you."

32The king said to the Cushite, "IsisIs it well with the young man Absalom?"

The Cushite said, "May the enemies of my lord the king, and all who rise up against you to do harm, be like that young man."

David Cries for Absalom

33The king was very upset. He went up to the room over the gate and cried. As he went, he said, "MymyMy son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! I wish I had died instead of you, Absalom, my son, my son!"
