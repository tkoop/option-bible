Stand Strong in the Lord

1So, my dear brothers and sisters, whom I love and miss, you are my joy and my crown. Stand firm in the Lord, my dear friends.

Agree and Help Each Other

2I ask Euodia and Syntyche to agree with each other in the Lord. 3Yes, I ask you, my true friend, to help these women. They worked hard with me to share the good news. Clement and my other helpers worked too. Their names are in the book of life.

Be Joyful and Pray

4Always be joyful in the Lord. I say it again: Be joyful! 5Let everyone see your gentle spirit. The Lord is near.

6Don't worry about anything. Instead, pray about everything. Tell God your needs, and thank HimhimHim. 7Then God's peace, which is greater than we can understand, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

Think About Good Things

8Finally, my brothers and sisters, think about things that are true, good, right, pure, lovely, and kind. If something is excellent or worth praise, think about these things. 9Do what you learned and received and heard and saw in me. Then the God of peace will be with you.

Paul Thanks His Friends

10I was very happy in the Lord because you care about me again. You cared before, but you had no chance to help. 11I am not saying this because I need something. I have learned to be happy with whatever I have. 12I know how to live with little, and I know how to live with plenty. I have learned to be full or hungry, to have much or little. 13I can do anything through HimhimHim who makes me strong.

14But it was good of you to share my troubles. 15And you Philippians know that when I first preached the good news and left Macedonia, only your church shared with me in giving and receiving. 16Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs more than once.

17I am not asking for gifts. I want the good reward that comes to you from giving. 18I have received full payment, and more. I have all I need. The gifts you sent by Epaphroditus are like a sweet smell to God. They are a sacrifice that pleases HimhimHim.

19And my God will meet all your needs from HishisHis glorious riches in Christ Jesus. 20To our God and FatherfatherFather be glory forever and ever. Amen.

Final Greetings

21Greet all God's people in Christ Jesus. The brothers and sisters with me send you their greetings. 22All God's people here send greetings to you, especially those in Caesar's house. 23May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
