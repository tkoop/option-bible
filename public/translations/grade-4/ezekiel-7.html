God Says the End Is Near

1The LORD spoke to me.

“2You, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says to the land of Israel: The end has come! The end is here for all four corners of the land.

“3The end is here for you. I will send MymyMy anger on you. I will judge you by your ways. I will punish you for all your sinful things.

“4I will not feel sorry for you. I will not hold back. I will pay you back for your ways. Your sinful things will stay among you. Then all of you will know that I am the LORD.

“5This is what the Lord GOD says: Bad trouble is coming, one after another. Look, it is coming.

“6The end has come. The end has come. It has woken up against you. Look, it is coming.

“7Your time is up, people in the land. The time has come. The day is near. It will be a day of noise and trouble, not a happy day on the mountains.

“8Soon I will pour out MymyMy anger on you. I will use up MymyMy anger on you. I will judge you by your ways. I will punish you for all your sinful things.

“9I will not feel sorry. I will not hold back. I will pay you back for your ways. Your sinful things will stay among you. Then all of you will know that I am the LORD who punishes.

The Day of Trouble Is Here

“10Look, the day is here! Look, it is coming! The end has come. The stick has budded. Pride has bloomed.

“11Violence has grown into a rod of wickedness. None of them will be left. Their money and things will not stay. No one will cry for them.

“12The time has come. The day is here. The buyer should not be glad. The seller should not be sad. My anger is on all the crowd.

“13The seller will not get back what he sold, as long as he lives. The warning is for all the people. It will not turn back. Because of their sin, no one will stay alive.

“14They blew the trumpet and got everything ready. But no one goes to war. My anger is on all the people.

War, Hunger, and Sickness

“15Outside is the sword. Inside are sickness and hunger. Anyone in the field dies by the sword. Anyone in the city is eaten by hunger and sickness.

“16The ones who get away will run to the mountains. They will be like sad doves in the valleys. They will all cry, each for his own sin.

“17All hands will be weak. All knees will feel like water.

“18They will put on rough cloth. Fear will cover them. Shame will be on all faces. All heads will be shaved.

Money Cannot Save Them

“19They will throw their silver into the streets. Their gold will be like trash. Their silver and gold cannot save them on the day of the LORD's anger. They will not fill their stomachs. They will not be happy in their souls. Their sin made them trip and fall.

“20They used their pretty things to show off. They made horrible statues and bad gods from them. So I will make those things dirty to them.

“21I will give those things to strangers to take. I will give them to the wicked people of the earth to steal. They will ruin them.

“22I will turn MymyMy face away from them. Bad men will come in and ruin MymyMy special place.

Chains and Punishment

“23Make a chain! The land is full of bloody crimes. The city is full of violence.

“24I will bring the worst nations to take their houses. I will stop the pride of the strong. Their holy places will be ruined.

“25When trouble comes, they will look for peace. But there will be none.

“26One disaster will come after another. One rumourrumourrumor will follow another. They will look for a vision from the prophet. But the teaching from the priest will be lost. The advice from the elders will be lost.

“27The king will cry. The prince will be filled with fear. The people's hands will shake. I will do to them what they deserve. I will judge them by their own rules. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
