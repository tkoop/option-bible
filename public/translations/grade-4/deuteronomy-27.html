Moses Tells the People to Obey God

1Moses and the elders of Israel told the people, "Keep all the rules I give you today.

Stones and an Altar

2On the day you cross the Jordan River into the land the LORD your God gives you, set up big stones and cover them with plaster. 3Write all these rules on them after you cross over. Then you can go into the land the LORD your God gives you. It is a land with lots of milk and honey. The LORD, the God of your fathers, promised it to you. 4When you cross the Jordan, set up these stones on Mount Ebal, like I tell you today, and cover them with plaster. 5Build an altar there for the LORD your God. Make it from stones. Don't use iron tools on them. 6Build the altar of the LORD your God with whole stones. Offer burnt gifts on it to the LORD your God. 7Offer peace gifts there, eat them, and be happy before the LORD your God. 8Write all the words of this law clearly on the stones.

The People Belong to God

9Then Moses and the priests from Levi spoke to all Israel. They said, "Be quiet, Israel, and listen! Today you have become the people of the LORD your God. 10Obey the LORD your God. Do what HeheHe says. Keep HishisHis rules and laws. I am giving you these commands today."
