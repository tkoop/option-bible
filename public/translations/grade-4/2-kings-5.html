Naaman Has a Skin Disease

1Naaman was the leader of the army for the king of Aram. He was a great man. His master liked him very much. The LORD had used him to help Aram win. He was a brave soldier, but he had leprosy. 2Bands of soldiers from Aram had taken a young girl from Israel. She worked for Naaman's wife.

The Girl from Israel Has a Plan

3She said to her mistress, "I wish my master could go to the prophet in Samaria. He could heal him of his leprosy." 4Naaman went to his master and told him what the girl had said.

The King of Israel Is Afraid

5The king of Aram said, "Go. I will send a letter to the king of Israel." So Naaman went. He took ten bags of silver, six thousand gold coins, and ten sets of clothes. 6He took the letter to the king of Israel. It said, "I am sending my servant Naaman to you. Please heal him of his leprosy."

7When the king of Israel read the letter, he tore his clothes. He said, "Am I God? Can I kill people and make them alive again? Why does he send a man to me to heal his leprosy? He must be trying to start a fight with me."

8Elisha, the man of God, heard that the king tore his clothes. He sent a message to the king: "Why did you tear your clothes? Send him to me. Then he will know there is a prophet in Israel."

Elisha Sends Naaman to the River

9So Naaman came with his horses and chariots. He stood at the door of Elisha's house. 10Elisha sent a messenger to him. The messenger said, "Go wash in the Jordan River seven times. Your skin will be like new, and you will be clean."

Naaman Is Angry

11But Naaman was angry. He left and said, "I thought he would come out to me. I thought he would stand and call on the name of the the LORD HishisHis God. I thought he would wave his hand over the spot and heal the leprosy." 12"Are not the rivers of Damascus, the Abana and the Pharpar, better than all the water in Israel? Could I not wash in them and be clean?" He turned and left in anger.

Naaman Listens to His Servants

13His servants came to him. They said, "Sir, if the prophet had told you to do something big, would you not do it? So you should do this. He only said, 'Wash and be clean.'" 14So Naaman went down. He dipped in the Jordan River seven times, just as the man of God said. His skin became like a little child's skin. He was clean.

Naaman Thanks God

15He went back to the man of God. He and all his men stood before him. Naaman said, "Now I know there is no God in all the earth except in Israel. Please take a gift from your servant."

16Elisha said, "As the the LORD lives, I will not take anything." Naaman urged him to take it, but Elisha said no.

Naaman Asks for Soil and Forgiveness

17Naaman said, "If you will not take it, please let me take two loads of dirt. I will not burn gifts or give sacrifices to other gods anymore. I will give them only to the the LORD." 18"May the the LORD forgive me for this one thing. When my master goes into the temple of Rimmon to worship, he leans on my arm. I have to bow there too. May the the LORD forgive me when I bow in the temple of Rimmon."

19Elisha said, "Go in peace." So Naaman left him and went a short way.

Gehazi Tries to Get Money

20Gehazi, Elisha's helper, said, "MymyMy master let Naaman go. He did not take what Naaman brought. As the the LORD lives, I will run after him and get something from him."

21So Gehazi chased Naaman. Naaman saw him running. He got down from his chariot to meet him. He said, "IsisIs everything all right?"

22Gehazi said, "Everything is all right. My master sent me. He said, 'Two young men from the group of prophets came from the hills of Ephraim. Please give them one large piece of silver and two sets of clothes.'"

23Naaman said, "Please take two pieces of silver." He urged him. He tied two pieces of silver in two bags. He gave them and two sets of clothes to two of his servants. They carried them for Gehazi. 24When Gehazi came to the hill, he took the things from the servants. He put them in the house. Then he sent the men away, and they left.

Gehazi Is Punished

25Then Gehazi went in and stood before his master. Elisha said, "Where have you been, Gehazi?"

Gehazi said, "I did not go anywhere."

26Elisha said, "MymyMy heart went with you when the man got down from his chariot to meet you. Is this a time to get money, clothes, olive fields, vineyards, sheep, cows, and servants?" 27"So Naaman's leprosy will stick to you and your children forever." Gehazi left. His skin was white like snow.
