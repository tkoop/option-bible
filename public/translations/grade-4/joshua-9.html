The Kings Join Together

1When all the kings on the other side of the Jordan heard what Joshua did, they came together. They lived in the hills, in the lowlands, and along the big sea near Lebanon. They were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 2They agreed to fight Joshua and Israel together.

The Gibeonites Trick Joshua

3The people of Gibeon heard what Joshua did to Jericho and Ai.

4The Gibeonites used a trick. They went and got old, worn sacks for their donkeys. They took old wine skins that were torn and tied up. 5Their sandals were old and patched. Their clothes were old. All their food was dry and moldy.

6They went to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal. They said to him and to the men of Israel, "We come from a far country. Please make a peace agreement with us."

7The men of Israel said to the Hivites, "Maybe you live near us. How can we make an agreement with you?"

8They said to Joshua, "We are your servants."

Joshua asked, "Who are you? Where do you come from?"

9They said to him, "YouryourYour servants have come from a very far country. We came because of the name of the the LORD your God. We heard about HimhimHim. We heard all HeheHe did in Egypt. 10We heard all HeheHe did to the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan. He beat Sihon, king of Heshbon, and Og, king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth. 11Our leaders and all the people in our land told us to take food for the trip and go meet you. They told us to say, 'We are your servants. Please make an agreement with us.'"

12"Look at our bread. It was warm when we packed it at home on the day we left to come to you. Now it is dry and moldy. 13These wine skins were new when we filled them. Now they are torn. Our clothes and sandals are very worn from the long trip."

Joshua Makes a Peace Agreement

14The men took some of their food. But they did not ask the the LORD what to do. 15Joshua made a peace agreement with them. He promised to let them live. The leaders of the people swore an oath to them.

Israel Learns the Truth

16Three days later, the Israelites heard that the Gibeonites lived nearby. 17The Israelites travelledtravelledtraveled and reached their cities: Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath Jearim. 18But the Israelites did not attack them, because the leaders had sworn an oath to them by the the LORD, the God of Israel. All the people grumbled against the leaders.

19All the leaders told all the people, "We swore an oath to them by the the LORD, the God of Israel. Now we cannot hurt them." 20"This is what we will do. We will let them live. Then the the LORD will not be angry with us for breaking the oath we made." 21The leaders said, "Let them live." So they became woodcutters and water carriers for all the people, just as the leaders said.

Joshua Punishes the Gibeonites

22Joshua called the Gibeonites and said to them, "Why did you trick us? You said, 'We live very far from you,' but you live near us. 23Now you are cursed. You will never stop being servants. You will cut wood and carry water for the house of my God."

24They answered Joshua, "We were told clearly that the the LORD your God commanded HishisHis servant Moses to give you all this land and to destroy all the people living in it before you. We were very afraid for our lives, so we did this. 25Now we are in your hands. Do to us what seems good and right to you."

26So Joshua saved them from the Israelites. He did not let them kill the Gibeonites. 27That day Joshua made them woodcutters and water carriers for the people and for the altar of the the LORD. They serve there to this day, at the place the the LORD chooses.
