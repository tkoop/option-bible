Joseph Cries for His Father

1Joseph fell on his father's face. He cried over him and kissed him. 2Joseph told his servants, the doctors, to prepare his father Israel's body for burial. They did. 3It took 40 days to prepare his body. That is how long it took. The people of Egypt cried for him for 70 days.

4After the days of crying, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh's people. He said, "If you are kind to me, please tell Pharaoh this. 5My father made me promise. He said he was dying and wanted to be buried in the grave he dug in Canaan. Now let me go up and bury my father, and then I will come back."

6Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, just as he made you promise."

7Joseph went up to bury his father. All of Pharaoh's servants went with him. The leaders of his house and all the leaders of Egypt went too. 8Joseph's whole family went too, and his brothers and his father's family. They left only their little children, sheep, and cows in Goshen. 9Chariots and horsemen also went with him. The group was very big.

10They came to a grain floor at Atad, across the Jordan. There they cried very hard for a long time. Joseph led them in mourning for his father for seven days. 11The people who lived in Canaan saw the mourning at Atad. They said, "This is a very sad time for Egypt." So they named that place Abel Mizraim, across the Jordan. 12His sons did for him what he had told them to do.

13His sons carried him to Canaan. They buried him in the cave of Machpelah. This was in the field near Mamre. Abraham had bought the field from Ephron the Hittite to use as a burial place. 14After Joseph buried his father, he went back to Egypt. His brothers and all who went with him went back too.

Joseph's Brothers Are Afraid

15Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead. They said, "What if Joseph is angry with us? What if he pays us back for all the bad we did to him?"

16They sent a message to Joseph. They said, "Before your father died, he told us to ask you to forgive us. 17We did bad to you. Please forgive us, your brothers. We are servants of your father's God." Joseph cried when he heard this.

18His brothers also went to him. They fell down in front of him and said, "We are your servants."

19Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid. Am I in God's place? 20You planned to hurt me, but God planned it for good. He did it to keep many people alive today. 21So do not be afraid. I will take care of you and your children." Then he comforted them and spoke kindly to them.

Joseph's Last Years

22Joseph stayed in Egypt with his father's family. He lived 110 years. 23Joseph saw Ephraim's children and grandchildren. He also saw the children of Machir, Manasseh's son. He held them on his lap as babies.

Joseph's Last Words

24Joseph said to his brothers, "I am dying. But God will take care of you. He will bring you out of this land to the land HeheHe promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 25Joseph made the people of Israel promise. He said, "God will take care of you. When HeheHe does, carry my bones up from here."

26Joseph died at 110 years old. They prepared his body and put it in a coffin in Egypt.
