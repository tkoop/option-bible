Warning to People Who Feel Safe

1Oh no! People in Zion feel safe. People on the hill in Samaria feel safe too. They are the big leaders of the best nations. Israel goes to them. 2Go see Calneh. Then go to big Hamath. Then go down to Gath, where the Philistines live. Are those places better than your kingdoms? Is their land bigger than your land?

3You act like trouble is far away. But you bring hurt close. 4You lie on fancy beds with ivory. You lounge on your couches. You eat baby sheep from the flock. You eat young cows from the pen.

5You sing silly songs with harps. You make up new instruments, like David did. 6You drink wine from bowls. You put on the best oils. But you do not care that Joseph is being ruined.

7So now you will be the first to go away as captives. Your parties will end.

God's Promise

8The Lord, the LORD, promised by HishisHis own life. The LORD, the God of armies, says, "I hate the pride of Jacob. I hate his big houses. I will give up the city and everything in it."

9If ten men are left in one house, they will die.

10A family member will come to take the bones out of the house. He will ask, "IsisIs anyone still with you?"

The answer will be, "No."

Then he will say, "Hush! We must not say the name of the LORD."

11Look, the LORD will give the order. The big house will break into pieces. The little house will crack.

12Do horses run on rocks? Do people plow rocks with oxen? But you turned justice into poison. You turned good into something bitter.

13You are happy about Lo-debar. You say, "We took Karnaim by our own power."

A Nation Will Attack

14Look, I will bring a nation against you, people of Israel, says the LORD, the God of armies. They will crush you from Lebo-hamath to the BrookbrookBrook of the Arabah.
