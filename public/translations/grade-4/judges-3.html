Nations Left to Test Israel

1These are the nations the LORD left. He left them to test Israel. He tested all the people who had not fought in the wars in Canaan. 2He did this to teach war to the new groups of Israel who had not fought before. 3There were the five rulers of the Philistines. There were also the Canaanites, Sidonians, and Hivites. They lived in the Lebanon mountains, from Mount Baal Hermon to Lebo Hamath. 4They were left to test Israel. The LORD wanted to see if Israel would obey HishisHis rules. Moses had given these rules to their fathers.

5The people of Israel lived among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 6They married the daughters of those people. They gave their own daughters to the sons of those people. They served the gods of those people. 7The people of Israel did wrong in the eyes of the LORD. They forgot the LORD their God. They served the false gods named Baal and Asherah. 8The LORD was very angry with Israel. He let Cushan-Rishathaim, king of Aram Naharaim, rule over them. Israel served him for eight years.

Othniel Saves Israel

9Then the people of Israel cried out to the LORD. The LORD gave them a helper. His name was Othniel. He was the son of Kenaz. He was Caleb's younger brother. He saved Israel. 10The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came on Othniel. He became Israel's judge. He went to war. The LORD let Othniel beat Cushan-Rishathaim, king of Aram. 11Then the land had peace for forty years. After that, Othniel son of Kenaz died.

Israel Turns from the LORD Again

12Again the people of Israel did wrong in the eyes of the LORD. So ThetheThe LORD let Eglon, king of Moab, become strong against Israel. This was because Israel did wrong in the eyes of the LORD. 13Eglon got the Ammonites and Amalekites to join him. He came and attacked Israel. They took the CitycityCity of Palms. 14The people of Israel served Eglon, king of Moab, for eighteen years.

Ehud Saves Israel

15Again the people of Israel cried out to the LORD. He gave them a helper named Ehud. Ehud was the son of Gera. He was from Benjamin. He was left-handed. Israel sent Ehud to Eglon, king of Moab, with a gift. 16Ehud made a sword with two sharp sides. It was about a foot and a half long. He tied it to his right leg under his clothes. 17Ehud gave the gift to Eglon, king of Moab. Eglon was a very fat man. 18After Ehud gave the gift, he sent home the men who carried it.

19But at the stone statues near Gilgal, Ehud turned back. He said, “I have a secret message for you, KingkingKing.”

The king said, “Quiet!” Then all the men standing near him left.

20Ehud came to the king. The king was sitting alone in the cool room upstairs. Ehud said, “I have a message from God for you.” The king stood up from his chair.

21Ehud reached with his left hand. He took the sword from his right side. He pushed it into the king's stomach.

22The handle went in too. The king's body emptied. Ehud did not pull the sword out. The king's helpers went out the back.

23Then Ehud went out to the porch. He shut and locked the doors of the room upstairs.

24After Ehud left, the king's helpers came. They saw the doors were locked. They said, “He is probably using the bathroom in the cool room.”

25They waited a long time. He did not open the doors. So they took a key and opened them. They saw their king dead on the floor.

26While they waited, Ehud got away. He passed the stone statues and went to Seirah.

27When he got there, he blew a trumpet in the hills of Ephraim. The people of Israel came down with him from the hills. He led them.

28He said, “Follow me. The LORD has given Moab, your enemy, into your hands.” So they went down after him. They took the crossings of the Jordan River near Moab. They did not let anyone cross.

29At that time they killed about ten thousand men of Moab. All were strong men. Not one man got away. 30That day Moab was brought under Israel's rule. The land had peace for eighty years.

Shamgar Saves Israel

31After Ehud came Shamgar son of Anath. He killed six hundred Philistines with a sharp stick used to drive oxen. He also saved Israel.
