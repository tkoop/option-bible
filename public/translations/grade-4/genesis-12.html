God Tells Abram to Go

1The LORD said to Abram, “Leave your country, your people, and your father’s home. Go to the land I will show you. 2I will make you into a big nation. I will bless you. I will make your name great. You will be a blessing. 3I will bless anyone who blesses you. I will curse anyone who curses you. All people on EarthEarthearth will be blessed because of you.”

Abram Goes to Canaan

4So Abram went, just as the LORD had told him. Lot went with him. Abram was 75 years old when he left Harran. 5He took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, all the things they owned, and the people who were with them in Harran. They left for Canaan and arrived there. 6Abram travelledtravelledtraveled through the land to the big tree at Moreh, near Shechem. At that time, the Canaanites were living in the land.

God Promises the Land

7The LORD appeared to Abram and said, “I will give this land to your children.” So Abram built an altar there for the LORD, who had appeared to him. 8From there he went to the hills east of Bethel and set up his tent. Bethel was to the west and Ai to the east. There he built an altar for the LORD and prayed to the LORD. 9Then Abram kept travellingtravellingtraveling south.

Abram Goes to Egypt

10There was very little food in the land. So Abram went down to Egypt to stay for a while, because the famine was very bad. 11When he was almost in Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, “I know you are a very beautiful woman. 12When the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘She is his wife.’ Then they will kill me, but they will let you live. 13Please say you are my sister. Then things will go well for me because of you, and I will live because of you.”

14When Abram came to Egypt, the Egyptians saw that Sarai was very beautiful. 15Pharaoh’s helpers saw her too. They told Pharaoh how beautiful she was. Then she was taken into Pharaoh’s house. 16Pharaoh was good to Abram because of her. Abram got sheep, cattle, donkeys, servants, and camels.

17But the LORD made Pharaoh and his family very sick because of Abram’s wife Sarai. 18So Pharaoh called for Abram. He said, “What have you done to me? Why didn’t you tell me she was your wife? 19Why did you say, ‘She is my sister,’ so I took her as my wife? Now take your wife and go!” 20Then Pharaoh told his men to send Abram away. They sent him and his wife and all he had.
