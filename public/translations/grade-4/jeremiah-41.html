Ishmael Kills Gedaliah

1In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, came to Mizpah. He was from the king's family. Ten men came with him. They ate with Gedaliah son of Ahikam. 2Then Ishmael and his ten men stood up. They killed Gedaliah with a sword. Gedaliah was the man the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land. 3Ishmael also killed all the Jews who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah. He killed the Babylonian soldiers who were there too. 4The next day, no one yet knew Gedaliah was dead.

Men Come to Worship

5Eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria. They had shaved their beards and torn their clothes. They were sad and had cut themselves. They brought gifts and incense to the house of the LORD. 6Ishmael went out from Mizpah to meet them. He was crying. He walked toward them and said, "Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam." 7When they came into the town, Ishmael and his men killed them. They threw the bodies into a deep hole in the ground. 8But ten men said to Ishmael, "Do not kill us! We have wheat, barley, oil, and honey hidden in a field." So he did not kill them. 9The deep hole was one King Asa had made long ago. He made it to hide from Baasha, king of Israel. Ishmael filled it with dead bodies.

Ishmael Takes Captives

10Ishmael took all the people left in Mizpah as captives. This included the king's daughters and the other people. Nebuzaradan, the guard leader, had put Gedaliah in charge of them. Ishmael took them and started to go to the Ammonites.

Johanan Rescues the People

11Johanan son of Kareah and the army leaders with him heard what Ishmael had done. 12They took their men and went to fight Ishmael. They caught up with him near the big pool in Gibeon. 13The people with Ishmael saw Johanan and his leaders. They were glad. 14All the people Ishmael had taken turned and went to Johanan. 15Ishmael and eight of his men got away from Johanan. They ran to the Ammonites.

The People Go Toward Egypt

16Then Johanan and his leaders took all the survivors from Mizpah. These were the people he had saved from Ishmael. They were soldiers, women, children, and servants. He brought them from Gibeon. 17They went and stayed at Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem. They were on their way to Egypt. 18They were afraid of the Babylonians. This was because Ishmael had killed Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land.
