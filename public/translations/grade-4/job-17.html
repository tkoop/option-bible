Job Speaks About His Trouble

1My spirit is broken. My days are cut short. The grave is ready for me.

2People who make fun of me are all around me. My eyes have to watch their mean words.

3Please, God, promise to help me. Who else will stand up for me?

4You have kept their hearts from understanding, so YouyouYou will not lift them up.

5A person who turns in friends for money will see his children's eyes grow weak.

6He has made me a laughingstock to everyone. I am someone people spit on.

7My eyes are weak from sadness. My whole body is just a shadow.

8Good people are shocked by this. Innocent people get angry at those who do wrong.

9But good people will keep doing right. Those who are innocent will get stronger.

10But all of you, come back. I will not find one wise person among you.

11My days are over. My plans are broken. The things I hoped for in my heart are gone.

12They make the night seem like day. "Light is near," they say, "even in the dark."

Job Talks About the Grave

13If I think the grave will be my home, if I make my bed in the dark,

14if I say to the pit, "YouyouYou are my father," and to the worm, "YouyouYou are my mother and sister,"

15then where is my hope? Who can see hope for me?

16Will my hope go down with me to the grave? Will we sink into the dust together?
