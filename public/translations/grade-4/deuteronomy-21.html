When a Dead Body Is Found

1If someone is found dead in a field in the land the LORD your God gives you to own, and no one knows who killed him, 2your leaders and judges will go out. They will measure the distance from the dead body to the cities around it. 3The city closest to the dead body will be found. The leaders of that city will take a young cow that has never worked. It has never pulled a heavy wooden bar. 4The leaders of that city will bring the young cow down to a valley with running water. No one plows or plants there. They will break the cow's neck in the valley.

5Then the priests, the sons of Levi, will come near. The LORD your God chose them to serve HimhimHim and to bless in the name of the LORD. They will settle every argument and every fight. 6All the leaders of the city closest to the dead body will wash their hands over the young cow whose neck was broken in the valley.

7They will say, "OurourOur hands did not spill this blood. Our eyes did not see it happen. 8Please, the LORD, forgive YouryourYour people Israel, whom YouyouYou saved. Do not let innocent blood stay among YouryourYour people Israel. Then the guilt for this blood will be forgiven for them."

9So you must remove the guilt of innocent blood from among you. Do what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

A Woman Taken in War

10When you go to war against your enemies, the LORD your God will put them in your power. You will take prisoners. 11If you see a beautiful woman among the prisoners, and you want her, you may take her as your wife.

12Bring her to your home. She will shave her head and cut her nails. 13She will take off the clothes she wore as a prisoner. She will stay in your home and cry for her father and mother for one full month. After that, you may marry her, and she will be your wife.

14But if you do not want her, let her go where she wants. Do not sell her for money. Do not treat her like a slave, because you have shamed her.

Sons Who Get Property

15If a man has two wives, and he loves one but not the other, and both wives have sons for him, the oldest son is from the wife he does not love. 16Then when he gives his sons what he owns, he must not give the son of the loved wife the special firstborn share instead of the son of the unloved wife. The unloved wife's son is the true oldest son. 17Instead, he must give the oldest son, the son of the unloved wife, twice as much as the other sons. That son was the first proof of his strength. The oldest son's right stays with him.

A Son Who Will Not Obey

18If a man has a son who is stubborn and refuses to obey, who does not listen to his father or mother, and who will not listen even when they punish him, 19his father and mother will grab him and take him to the leaders of his city, to the city gate.

20They will say to the leaders, "This son of ours is stubborn and refuses to obey. He does not listen to us. He eats too much and drinks too much."

21Then all the men of his city will throw stones at him until he dies. You must remove the evil from among you. All Israel will hear about it and be afraid.

A Body Hung on a Tree

22If a man does a sin that deserves death, and he is put to death, and you hang his body on a tree, 23do not leave his body on the tree overnight. Bury him that same day. Anyone who is hung is under God's curse. Do not make your land unclean. the LORD your God is giving it to you to own.
