The LORD Is My Shepherd

A song by David. 1The LORD is my shepherd. I will not need anything. 2He makes me lie down in green fields. He leads me by quiet water. 3He gives me new strength. He leads me on the right path to show HishisHis goodness.

4Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, because YouyouYou are with me. Your rod and YouryourYour staff comfort me.

5You set a table for me where my enemies can see. You pour oil on my head. My cup is full and running over. 6Goodness and love will follow me all my life. I will live in the LORD's house forever.
