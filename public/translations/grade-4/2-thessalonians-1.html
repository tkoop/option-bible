Paul Writes to the Church

1Paul, Silas, and Timothy write to the church in Thessalonica. This church belongs to God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ. 2Grace and peace to you from God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ.

3We must thank God for you, brothers. This is right. Your faith is growing a lot. The love that each of you has for the others is growing too. 4So among God's churches, we tell about you. We are proud of how you keep going and keep trusting God while you face trouble and hard times.

He will punish people who do not know God and who do not obey the good news about our Lord Jesus. 9They will be punished forever. They will be far from the Lord and from HishisHis great power.

10This will happen on the day HeheHe comes. His holy people will praise HimhimHim. All who believed will be amazed at HimhimHim. You believed our message, so this includes you.

Paul Prays for the Church

11So we always pray for you. We ask God to count you worthy of HishisHis call. We ask HimhimHim to use HishisHis power to help you finish every good thing you want to do and every work that comes from faith. 12Then the name of our Lord Jesus will be praised among you.
