God's Message About Babylon

1This is the message the LORD gave to Jeremiah the prophet. It was about Babylon and the land of the people of Babylon.

Babylon Will Be Taken

2"Tell the nations. Let them hear. Raise a flag. Do not hide it. Say, 'Babylon is caught. Bel is put to shame. Merodach is scared. Babylon's false gods are shamed. Their statues are scared.' 3A nation from the north will attack her. It will make her land empty. No one will live there. People and animals will run away."

God's People Will Return

4The LORD says, "In those days, the people of Israel and the people of Judah will come together. They will go and cry. They will look for the LORD their God. 5They will ask the way to Zion. They will look that way. They will say, 'Come, let us join the LORD. Let us make a promise that lasts forever. It will not be forgotten.'"

Israel Was Like Lost Sheep

6"My people were like lost sheep. Their leaders led them the wrong way. They made them wander in the hills. They went from hill to hill. They forgot their safe place."

7Everyone who found them hurt them. Their enemies said, "We are not guilty. They sinned against the LORD. He is the home of what is right. He is the hope their fathers trusted."

Leave Babylon

8"Run away from Babylon. Leave the land of the people of Babylon. Be like strong goats that lead the flock."

A Big Army Will Come

9"I will bring a big group of nations from the north to attack Babylon. They will line up against her. They will take her. Their arrows will be like a skilled fighter's arrows. None will miss. 10The people of Babylon will be taken like treasure. Everyone who takes from her will have enough," says the LORD.

Babylon Was Proud

11"You were happy. You were glad. You took what was MinemineMine. You were like a fat young cow in the grass. You made noise like strong bulls. 12Your mother will be very ashamed. The one who had you will be confused. See, Babylon will be the last of the nations. It will be a dry, empty place."

Babylon Will Be Empty

13"Because of the LORD's anger, no one will live there. It will be empty. Everyone who passes by will be shocked. They will make fun of all her hurts. 14Line up against Babylon all around. Everyone with a bow, shoot at her. Do not hold back arrows. She sinned against the LORD. 15Shout against her all around. She has given up. Her walls have fallen down. It is the LORD's payback. Pay her back. Do to her what she did. 16Stop the farmers in Babylon. Stop the people who cut the grain. Because of the enemy's sword, everyone will turn to their own people. Everyone will run to their own land."

Israel Was Scattered

17Israel is like a sheep that was scattered. Lions chased it away. First the king of Assyria hurt it. Now Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, has broken its bones.

God Will Bring Israel Home

18The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "I will punish the king of Babylon and his land. I punished the king of Assyria the same way. 19I will bring Israel back home. He will eat in Carmel and Bashan. He will be full on the hills of Ephraim and Gilead. 20In those days, says the LORD, people will look for Israel's wrongs, but find none. They will look for Judah's sins, but not find them. I will forgive the people I leave. 21Go up against the land of Merathaim. Attack the people of Pekod. Destroy them completely, says the LORD. Do all I tell you."

War in the Land

22A sound of war is in the land. There is big destruction. 23Babylon was like a hammer for the whole EarthEarthearth. Now it is broken. Babylon is empty among the nations.

Babylon Is Caught

24"I set a trap for you, Babylon. You were caught, and you did not know it. You were found and caught because you fought against the LORD." 25The LORD opened HishisHis storehouse. He brought out HishisHis angry weapons. This is the work of the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, in the land of the people of Babylon.

Destroy Babylon Completely

26"Come against her from far away. Open her storehouses. Pile her up like heaps of ruins. Destroy her completely. Leave nothing of her. 27Kill all her young bulls. Trouble is coming for them. Their time has come."

People Escape Babylon

28People run away and escape from Babylon. They tell in Zion that the LORD our God has paid Babylon back. He has paid back for HishisHis temple.

Pay Babylon Back

29"Call the bowmen against Babylon. Set up camp around her. Let no one escape. Pay her back for what she did. Do to her what she did. She was proud against the LORD, against the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 30So her young men will fall in her streets. All her soldiers will be killed that day," says the LORD.

God Is Against Pride

31The Lord, the LORD of Hosts, says, "Look, I am against you, proud one. Your day has come. I will punish you. 32The proud one will trip and fall. No one will help him up. I will start a fire in his cities. It will burn all around him."

God Will Save His People

33The LORD of Hosts says, "ThetheThe people of Israel and Judah are hurt together. Everyone who took them keeps them. They will not let them go." 34Their SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior is strong. His name is the LORD of Hosts. He will fight for them. He will give rest to the land. He will shake up the people of Babylon.

A Sword for Babylon

35"A sword is coming for the people of Babylon, says the LORD. It is coming for their leaders and their wise men. 36A sword is coming for the braggers. They will act like fools. A sword is coming for her strong men. They will be scared. 37A sword is coming for their horses, chariots, and all the mixed people inside her. They will be weak. A sword is coming for her treasures. They will be stolen. 38Her waters will dry up. It is a land of carved idols. The people are crazy for their idols. 39Wild animals will live there. Ostriches will live there. It will never be lived in again. No one will live there from family to family. 40I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities near them, says the LORD. In the same way, no one will live there."

An Army from the North

41The LORD says, "Look, a people is coming from the north. It is a big nation. Many kings will come from faraway places. 42They will hold bows and spears. They are mean and will not show mercy. Their noise will roar like the sea. They will ride horses. They will line up for war against you, daughter of Babylon. 43The king of Babylon heard about them. His hands became weak. Fear took hold of him. Pain came on him like a woman having a baby. 44Look, he will come up like a lion from the thick bushes by the Jordan River to the safe place. I will chase them away from her quickly. Who can stand before MemeMe?"

Babylon Will Shake

45So listen to the LORD's plan against Babylon. Listen to HishisHis thoughts against the land of the people of Babylon. Even the smallest sheep will drag them away. He will make their home empty. 46When Babylon is taken, the earth will shake. The nations will hear the cry.
