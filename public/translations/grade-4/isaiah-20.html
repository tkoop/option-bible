A Sign and Warning for Egypt and Cush

1In the year when Tartan came to Ashdod, Sargon, the king of Assyria, sent him. Tartan fought Ashdod and took it.

2At that time the LORD spoke through Isaiah son of Amoz. He said, "Go take off the rough cloth from your waist and your sandals from your feet." Isaiah did it. He went without clothes and without shoes.

3The LORD said, "Just as MymyMy servant Isaiah has gone without clothes and without shoes for three years, this is a sign and warning for Egypt and Cush. 4So the king of Assyria will take the people of Egypt and Cush away as prisoners. Young and old will go without clothes and shoes. Egypt will be put to shame. 5The people who hoped in Cush and bragged about Egypt will be afraid and ashamed.

6"On that day, the people who live by the sea will say, 'Look! This is what happened to the people we hoped in. We ran to them for help to get away from the king of Assyria. How can we get away now?'"
