Nathan Tells David a Story

1The LORD sent Nathan to David. Nathan went to him and said, "There were two men in one city. One was rich, and one was poor. 2The rich man had many sheep and cows. 3The poor man had nothing but one little lamb. He bought it and raised it. It grew up with him and his children. It ate his food, drank from his cup, and slept in his arms. It was like a daughter to him. 4A travellertravellertraveler came to the rich man. The rich man did not want to take one of his own sheep or cows for the guest. He took the poor man's lamb and cooked it for the man who came to him."

David Gets Angry

5David was very angry with that man. He said to Nathan, "I swear by the LORD, the man who did this should die! 6He must pay back the lamb four times, because he did this and had no pity."

Nathan Says David Is the Man

I saved you from Saul. 8I gave you your master's house and his wives. I gave you the people of Israel and Judah. If that was not enough, I would have given you even more.

David Says Sorry

13David said to Nathan, "I have sinned against the LORD."

Nathan said to David, "ThetheThe LORD has taken away your sin. You will not die. 14But because you made the LORD look bad by this thing, the baby born to you will die."

The Baby Gets Sick

15Then Nathan went home. The LORD struck the baby that Uriah's wife had for David, and the baby became sick. 16David prayed to God for the baby. He did not eat. He went in and lay on the ground all night. 17The older men of his house stood over him to help him up, but he would not get up. He did not eat with them.

The Baby Dies

18On the seventh day the baby died. David's servants were afraid to tell him. They said, "While the baby was alive, we talked to him, but he would not listen. How can we tell him the baby is dead? He might hurt himself."

19David saw his servants whispering. He knew the baby was dead. He said to them, "IsisIs the baby dead?"

They said, "HeheHe is dead."

20David got up from the ground. He washed, put on oil, and changed his clothes. He went to the LORD's house and worshiped. Then he went home. He asked for food, and they gave him bread, and he ate.

21His servants said to him, "Why did you do this? You did not eat and you cried while the baby was alive. But when the baby died, you got up and ate."

22He said, "While the baby was alive, I did not eat and I cried. I thought, 'Who knows? The LORD may be kind to me, and the baby may live.' 23But now he is dead. Why should I keep fasting? Can I bring him back? I will go to him, but he will not come back to me."

David and Bathsheba Have Solomon

24David comforted his wife Bathsheba. He went to her and slept with her. She had a son. He named him Solomon. The LORD loved him. 25The LORD sent a message through Nathan the prophet to name the baby Jedidiah, because the LORD loved him.

David Takes Rabbah

26Joab fought against Rabbah of the Ammonites. He captured the royal city. 27Joab sent messengers to David. He said, "I have fought against Rabbah. I have also captured the city by the water. 28Now bring the rest of the people. Camp around the city and capture it. If I capture it, it may be named after me."

29David gathered all the people. He went to Rabbah. He fought against it and captured it. 30He took the crown from their king's head. It was gold and very heavy. It had an expensive stone. They put it on David's head. He also took many valuable things from the city. 31He brought out the people who were there. He made them work with saws, iron picks, and iron axes. He made them work at the brick ovens. David did this to all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all the people went back to Jerusalem.
