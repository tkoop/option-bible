Job Answers His Friends

1Then Job answered:

2"I know this is true. But how can a person be right with God? 3If someone wanted to argue with God, he could not answer even one question out of a thousand. 4God is wise and strong. Who has stood against HimhimHim and won?

Job Says He Cannot Answer God

Job's Days Are Short
