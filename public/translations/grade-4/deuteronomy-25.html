Rules for Fair Punishment

1If two men have a fight and go to court, the judges will decide who is right and who is wrong. They must say the good man is right and the bad man is wrong. 2If the bad man should be beaten, the judge will make him lie down. He will be beaten in front of the judge. The number of hits must match his wrong. 3He may hit him up to forty times, but not more. If he hits him more, your brother would be put to shame before you. 4Do not stop an ox from eating while it works.

When a Brother Dies

5If brothers live together and one of them dies and has no son, his wife must not marry someone outside the family. Her husband's brother must marry her and do what a brother should do for her. 6The first son she has will carry the dead brother's name. Then his name will not be wiped out in Israel.

7If the man does not want to marry his brother's wife, she must go to the elders at the city gate. She will say, "MymyMy husband's brother will not keep his brother's name alive in Israel. He will not do his duty for me."

8The elders of his city will call him and talk to him. If he stands there and says, "I do not want to marry her,"

9then his brother's wife will go to him in front of the elders. She will take off his sandal and spit in his face. She will say, "This is what happens to a man who will not build his brother's family."

10In Israel, his family will be called "the family of the man whose sandal was pulled off."

Fighting and Punishment

11If two men are fighting, and one man's wife comes to save her husband from the man hitting him, and she grabs the other man's private parts, 12then cut off her hand. Do not feel sorry for her.

Use Fair Weights

13Do not keep two different weights in your bag, one big and one small. 14Do not keep two different measures in your house, one big and one small. 15You must use honest weights and measures. Then you will live a long time in the land the the LORD your God gives you. 16The LORD your God hates everyone who does these wrong things.

Remember What Amalek Did

17Remember what Amalek did to you on the way when you came out of Egypt. 18He met you on the way. He attacked you from behind when you were tired and weak. He killed the people who were falling behind you. He did not fear God. 19When the the LORD your God gives you rest from all your enemies around you in the land HeheHe gives you to own, you must wipe out the memory of Amalek from the earth. Do not forget.
