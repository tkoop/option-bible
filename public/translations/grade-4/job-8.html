Bildad Speaks to Job

1Then Bildad the Shuhite replied:

2"How long will you talk like this? Your words are like a big wind. 3Does God make things unfair? Does the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty twist what is right? 4Your sons sinned against HimhimHim, so HeheHe let them face the trouble they made. 5If you look to God and ask the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty for help, 6if you are clean and good, then HeheHe will come to help you and make your home good again. 7You started small, but your future will grow big.

"8Ask the old people. Listen to what their fathers learned. 9We are new here and know little. Our days on EarthEarthearth pass like a shadow. 10Won't they teach you and tell you what they know in their hearts?

"11Can a water plant grow without a swamp? Can reeds grow without water? 12While they are still growing, they dry up before other plants do. 13That is what happens to people who forget God. The hope of people who do not follow God will die. 14His trust is weak. His safety is like a spider's web. 15He leans on his house, but it falls. He holds it tight, but it does not last. 16He is like a plant with lots of water in the sun. Its branches spread over the garden. 17Its roots wrap around rocks. It tries to find a place among the stones. 18If it is pulled up, it is gone. Its place does not remember it. 19This is how his life goes. But others will grow from the dust.

"20Look, God does not turn away a good man. He will not help bad people. 21He will fill your mouth with laughter and your lips with shouts of joy. 22Your enemies will be covered with shame. The home of bad people will be gone."
