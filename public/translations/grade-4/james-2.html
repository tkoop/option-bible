Do Not Treat People Better Than Others

1My brothers and sisters, you believe in our great Lord Jesus Christ. Do not treat some people better than others. 2Imagine a man comes to your meeting. He wears a gold ring and nice clothes. A poor man comes in too. His clothes are dirty. 3If you give the rich man a good seat and say, “Sit here, please,” but you tell the poor man, “Stand there,” or “Sit by my feet,” 4then you have treated people unfairly. You have become judges with bad thoughts.

God Chose Poor People

5Listen, my dear brothers and sisters. Hasn't God chosen poor people in this world? He makes them rich in faith. He gives them the kingdom HeheHe promised to people who love HimhimHim. 6But you shame poor people. Aren't rich people the ones who treat you badly? They take you to court. 7They say bad things about the good name you were given.

Love Others as Yourself

8If you follow the great rule in the Bible, “Love your neighbourneighbourneighbor as you love yourself,” you do well. 9But if you treat some people better than others, you sin. The law says you broke its rules.

10A person who keeps all of God's law but breaks one small part is guilty of breaking all of it. 11God said, “Do not be unfaithful in marriage.” He also said, “Do not kill.” If you do not cheat in marriage but you kill someone, you have broken God's law.

Live Ready for God's JudgmentJudgementJudgment

12Talk and act like people who will be judged by the law that gives freedom. 13God will judge without mercy people who did not show mercy. But mercy is stronger than judgmentjudgementjudgment.

Faith Needs Good Works

14My brothers and sisters, what good is it if someone says he has faith but does no good works? Can that faith save him? 15Suppose a brother or sister has no clothes and not enough food for the day. 16If one of you says, “Go in peace. Stay warm and eat well,” but you do not give them what they need, what good is that? 17So faith alone, with no good works, is dead.

18But someone may say, “You have faith, and I have good works.” Show me your faith with no good works. I will show you my faith by my good works. 19You believe God is one. Good! Even the demons believe that, and they shake with fear. 20You foolish person, do you want proof that faith with no good works is useless?

21Wasn't our father Abraham made right with God by what he did? He offered his son Isaac on the altar. 22You see that his faith worked with his actions. His faith was made complete by what he did. 23The Bible came true. It says, “Abraham believed God, and God counted it as right.” Abraham was called God's friend.

24So you see that a person is made right with God by good works, not by faith alone. 25In the same way, wasn't Rahab the prostitute made right with God by her actions? She welcomed the spies and sent them out another way. 26Just as a body with no life is dead, faith with no good works is dead.
