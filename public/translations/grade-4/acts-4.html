Peter and John Are Arrested

1Peter and John were talking to the people. The priests, the temple guard leader, and the Sadducees came to them. 2They were upset because Peter and John taught the people. They also said that Jesus rose from the dead. 3They grabbed Peter and John and put them in jail until the next day. It was already evening. 4But many people who heard the message believed. The number of men grew to about 5,000.

Peter Speaks to the Leaders

5The next day, the rulers, elders, and teachers of the law met in Jerusalem. 6Annas the high priest was there. Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and others from the high priest’s family were there too. 7They made Peter and John stand in the middle. They asked, “By what power or by what name did you do this?”

8Peter was filled with the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit. He said to them, “Leaders and elders of the people, 9today we are being checked because we did a good thing for a sick man. You want to know how he was healed. 10Let all of you and all of Israel know this. This man stands healthy in front of you. He was healed by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. You nailed HimhimHim to a cross. But God raised HimhimHim from the dead. 11Jesus is the stone that you builders did not want. Now HeheHe is the most important stone. 12No one else can save us. There is no other name that God has given to people. We must be saved by Jesus.”

The Leaders Cannot Answer

13The leaders saw that Peter and John were brave. They also saw that Peter and John were not trained in school. They were amazed. They knew Peter and John had been with Jesus. 14They saw the healed man standing with them. They could not say anything against them.

The Leaders Warn Peter and John

15The leaders told Peter and John to leave the meeting. Then the leaders talked with each other. 16They said, “What should we do with these men? Everyone in Jerusalem knows they did a big miracle. We cannot say it did not happen. 17But we do not want this to spread to more people. Let us warn them not to talk to anyone using that name.” 18They called Peter and John back in. They told them not to speak or teach at all in the name of Jesus.

Peter and John Will Not Stop

19Peter and John answered them, “You decide if it is right for us to listen to you instead of God. 20We cannot stop talking about what we saw and heard.” 21The leaders threatened them more. Then they let them go. They could not punish them because the people praised God for what happened. 22The man who was healed was more than 40 years old.

God Answers Their Prayer

31After they prayed, the place where they were meeting shook. They were all filled with the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit. They spoke God’s word bravely.

The Believers Share Everything

32The group of believers were of one heart and mind. No one said that their things were their own. They shared everything. 33The apostles told people about the rising of the Lord Jesus with great power. God’s grace was on all of them. 34There were no poor people among them. People who owned land or houses sold them. They brought the money from the sales. 35They put the money at the apostles’ feet. Then it was given to each person who needed it.

Barnabas Gives Money

36Joseph was a Levite from Cyprus. The apostles called him Barnabas. That name means “son who gives comfort.” 37He sold a field he owned. He brought the money and put it at the apostles’ feet.
