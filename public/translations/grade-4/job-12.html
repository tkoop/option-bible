Job Answers His Friends

1Then Job answered:

2"You think you are the only smart people. You think wisdom will die with you. 3But I have a mind like yours. I am not less than you. Everyone knows these things. 4My friends laugh at me. I called to God, and HeheHe answered me. I am a good and honest man, but they still laugh at me.

5"People who feel safe look down on people in trouble. Trouble is waiting for people who slip. 6Robbers live in peace. People who make God angry feel safe. They trust their own power.

Even Animals Know God's Power

7"But ask the animals. They will teach you. Ask the birds in the sky. They will tell you. 8Or talk to the earth. It will teach you. The fish in the sea will tell you too. 9All of them know that the the LORD did this.

God Holds All Life

10"He holds the life of every living thing. He holds the breath of all people. 11Ears test words, just like mouths taste food. 12Old people are wise. Long life brings understanding.

God Is Wise and Strong

13"God has wisdom and power. He has good advice and understanding. 14If HeheHe tears something down, no one can build it back. If HeheHe locks someone in, no one can open it. 15If HeheHe holds back the water, it dries up. If HeheHe sends it out, it floods the earth. 16He has strength and true wisdom. Both the tricked and the trickster belong to HimhimHim.

17"He leads advisors away with nothing. He makes judges look foolish. 18He takes power from kings. He makes them weak. 19He leads priests away with nothing. He knocks down strong men. 20He takes away the words of trusted people. He takes away the wisdom of old leaders. 21He brings shame on leaders. He takes strength from strong men.

22"He shows things hidden in darkness. He brings dark secrets to light. 23He makes nations great, then destroys them. He grows nations, then leads them away. 24He takes away the sense of the leaders. He makes them wander in a place with no path. 25They feel around in the dark with no light. He makes them stagger like a drunk person."
