Joshua Leads the People to the Jordan

1Early in the morning, Joshua got up. He and all the people of Israel left Shittim. They went to the Jordan River. They stayed there before they crossed. 2After three days, the leaders went through the camp. 3They told the people, "When you see the priests from Levi carrying the ark of the the LORD your God, leave your place and follow it. 4But stay far away. Keep about two thousand cubits between you and the ark. Do not go near it. Then you will know which way to go. You have never been this way before."

5Joshua told the people, "Make yourselves clean. Tomorrow the the LORD will do amazing things among you."

6Joshua said to the priests, "Pick up the ark of the covenant and go ahead of the people." So they picked it up and went ahead of the people.

God Promises to Help Joshua

7The ThetheThe LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will make you great in the eyes of all Israel. Then they will know that I am with you, just as I was with Moses."

8The the LORD said, "YouyouYou must tell the priests who carry the ark, 'When you reach the edge of the Jordan, stand in the river.'"

9Joshua said to the people of Israel, "Come close and listen to the words of the the LORD your God. 10This is how you will know that the living God is among you. He will drive out the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites from before you. 11Look! The ark of the the LORD of all the earth will go into the Jordan ahead of you. 12Choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one from each tribe. 13As soon as the priests who carry the ark of the the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, put their feet in the Jordan, the water flowing down from above will be cut off. It will stand up in a heap."

The People Cross the Jordan

14When the people broke camp to cross the Jordan, the priests carrying the ark went ahead of them. 15The Jordan was full over all its banks during harvest time. When the priests reached the Jordan and their feet touched the water, 16the water coming down from upstream stopped. It piled up in a heap far away at Adam, near Zarethan. The people crossed over opposite Jericho. 17The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All Israel passed by on dry ground until the whole nation had crossed the Jordan.
