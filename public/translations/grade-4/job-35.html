Elihu Says God Is Above Us

1Then Elihu answered and said:

2"Do you think this is fair? You say, 'I am more right than God.' 3But you ask, 'What good is it for me? What do I gain if I do not sin?' 4I will answer you, and your friends too. 5Look up at the sky and see. Look at the clouds high above you.

6"If you sin, does it hurt God? If you sin many times, what does it do to HimhimHim? 7If you do right, what do you give HimhimHim? What does HeheHe take from your hand? 8Your sin hurts people like you. Your good helps people like you.

9"People cry out when they are pushed down. They call for help when strong people hurt them. 10But no one says, 'Where is God, my MakermakerMaker, who gives songs at night?' 11HeheHe teaches us more than the animals. He makes us wiser than the birds.

12"There they cry out, but HeheHe does not answer. This is because bad men are proud. 13Surely God does not listen to empty cries. The AlmightyalmightyAlmighty does not pay attention to them. 14How much less will HeheHe hear you when you say you cannot see HimhimHim! Your case is before HimhimHim, and you must wait for HimhimHim.

15"Now, because HeheHe does not punish in anger, and HeheHe does not pay much mind to foolish talk, 16Job opens his mouth for nothing. He says many words with no knowledge."
