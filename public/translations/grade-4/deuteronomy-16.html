Rules for Passover

1Watch for the month called Abib. Celebrate the Passover for the LORD your God. In that month, the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt at night. 2Offer the Passover to the LORD your God. Use cattle or sheep. Do it at the place the LORD chooses for HishisHis name to stay. 3Do not eat bread with yeast with it. For seven days eat flat bread with it. This bread is bread of hard times. You left Egypt fast. Eat it so you remember the day you left Egypt all the days of your life. 4For seven days, no yeast should be seen anywhere in your land. Also, the meat you kill on the first evening must not stay until morning.

5You cannot offer the Passover in just any town the LORD your God gives you. 6Offer the Passover in the evening at the place the LORD your God chooses for HishisHis name. Do it at the set time you left Egypt. 7Cook it and eat it at the place the LORD your God chooses. In the morning, go back to your tents. 8Eat flat bread for six days. On the seventh day, have a special meeting for the LORD your God. Do no work that day.

Feast of Weeks

9Count seven weeks. Start counting when you first cut the grain. 10Celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of WeeksweeksWeeks for the LORD your God. Give a free gift from your hand. Give as much as the LORD your God has blessed you. 11Be glad before the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses for HishisHis name. Be glad with your son and daughter, your servants, the Levite in your town, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow among you. 12Remember you were a slave in Egypt. Keep and obey these laws.

Feast of Shelters

13Celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Shelters for seven days. Do it after you gather your grain and your wine. 14Be glad at your feast. Be glad with your son and daughter, your servants, the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow in your town. 15Celebrate for seven days for the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses. The LORD your God will bless all your crops and all your work. You will be very glad. 16Three times a year all your men must come before the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses. Judges and Fair Rules

18Appoint judges and officers for your tribes in all your towns the LORD your God gives you. They must judge the people fairly. 19Do not twist justice. Do not favourfavourfavor one person over another. Do not take a bribe. A bribe blinds wise people and changes the words of good people. 20Follow only what is right. Then you will live and keep the land the LORD your God gives you.

Do Not Worship Idols

21Do not plant a tree for a false god next to the altar you make for the LORD your God. 22Do not set up a stone pillar. The LORD your God hates it.
