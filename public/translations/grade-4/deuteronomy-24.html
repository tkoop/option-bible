Rules for Ending a Marriage

1If a man marries a woman and he is not pleased with her because he found something wrong in her, he writes a paper to end the marriage. He gives it to her and sends her away from his home. 2She leaves his home, goes away, and becomes another man's wife. 3If that second man does not like her, he writes a paper to end the marriage, gives it to her, and sends her away. Or if that second man dies after he marries her, 4her first husband, who sent her away, may not take her back to be his wife after she has been made unclean. That is wrong before the LORD. Do not sin against the land the LORD your God gives you as a gift.

A New Husband Stays Home

5When a man takes a new wife, he must not go to war. He must not be given any other hard job for one year. He stays home and makes his new wife happy.

Do Not Take a Millstone

6Do not take a millstone as a promise for a loan. That takes away a person's way to live.

Punishment for Kidnapping

7If someone is caught stealing a person from the Israelites, working him hard, and selling him, that thief must die. Remove the evil from among you.

Be Careful with Skin Disease

8Be very careful about the skin disease leprosy. Do everything the priests and Levites teach you, just as I commanded them. 9Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the way when you came out of Egypt.

Rules for Lending

10If you lend something to your neighbourneighbourneighbor, do not go into his house to take something as a promise. 11You must stand outside. The man you lent to will bring the promise out to you. 12If he is poor, do not keep his promise item overnight. 13Give the promise back to him by sunset. Then he can sleep in his coat and bless you. That will be right for you before the LORD your God.

Pay Workers on Time

14Do not cheat a poor worker, whether he is one of your people or a stranger living in your land or town. 15Pay him his wages the same day, before the sun goes down. He is poor and needs the money. If you do not, he may cry to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.

Fair Rules for Punishment

16Fathers must not be put to death for their sons' sins. Sons must not be put to death for their fathers' sins. Each person must die for his own sin. 17Do not be unfair to strangers, orphans, or widows. Do not take a widow's coat as a promise for a loan. 18Remember that you were a slave in Egypt. The LORD your God saved you from there. That is why I command you to do this.

Leave Food for Others

19When you harvest your field and forget a bundle in the field, do not go back to get it. Leave it for the stranger, the orphan, and the widow. Then the LORD your God will bless you in all your work. 20When you shake your olive tree, do not pick every olive. Leave some for the stranger, the orphan, and the widow. 21When you pick grapes from your vine, do not go back over the vines. Leave some for the stranger, the orphan, and the widow. 22Remember that you were a slave in Egypt. That is why I command you to do this.
