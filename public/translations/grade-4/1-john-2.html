Jesus Helps Us

1My dear children, I write this to you so you won't sin. But if anyone does sin, we have Someone who speaks for us to the FatherfatherFather. He is Jesus Christ, and HeheHe is always right. 2He is the one who paid for our sins. He did not pay for only our sins. He paid for the sins of the whole world.

How We Know God

3We know we know HimhimHim when we obey HishisHis commands. 4If someone says, "I know HimhimHim," but does not obey HishisHis commands, that person is a liar. The truth is not in that person. 5But if someone keeps HishisHis word, God's love is truly made complete in that person. This is how we know we are in HimhimHim. 6If someone says they stay in HimhimHim, they should live like HeheHe lived.

Love One Another

7Dear friends, I am not giving you a new rule. I am giving you an old rule you have had since the start. The old rule is the message you heard. 8But I am also giving you a new rule. It is true in HimhimHim and in you. The dark is going away, and the true light is already shining. 9If someone says they are in the light but hates their brother, they are still in the dark. 10Anyone who loves their brother stays in the light. There is nothing in them that makes them fall. 11But if someone hates their brother, they are in the dark. They walk in the dark. They do not know where they are going, because the dark has blinded their eyes.

Words to Fathers, Young Men, and Children

12Dear children, I write to you because your sins are forgiven because of HishisHis name. 13Fathers, I write to you because you know HimhimHim who has been there from the start. Young men, I write to you because you have beaten the evil one. Children, I write to you because you know the FatherfatherFather. 14Fathers, I wrote to you because you know HimhimHim who has been there from the start. Young men, I wrote to you because you are strong. God's word stays in you. You have beaten the evil one.

Do Not Love the World

15Do not love the world or the things in it. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in them. 16All that is in the world is not from the FatherfatherFather. It is wanting things for our bodies, wanting what we see, and being proud of what we have. These do not come from the FatherfatherFather. They come from the world. 17The world and its wants are passing away. But anyone who does what God wants will live forever.

Watch Out for False Teachers

18Children, this is the last hour. You heard that the enemy of Christ is coming. Now many enemies of Christ have come. So we know this is the last hour. 19They left us. But they were not really with us. If they had been with us, they would have stayed with us. They left so it would be clear that not all of them were with us. 20But you have a special gift from the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne. You know these things. 21I write to you, not because you do not know the truth, but because you do know it. No lie comes from the truth. 22Who is the liar? It is the one who says Jesus is not the Christ. That person is the enemy of Christ. They say no to the FatherfatherFather and the SonsonSon. 23Anyone who rejects the SonsonSon does not have the FatherfatherFather. Anyone who accepts the SonsonSon has the FatherfatherFather too.

Stay in Him

24Keep in your hearts what you heard from the start. If you keep it in your hearts, you will stay in the SonsonSon and in the FatherfatherFather. 25He promised us life that never ends. 26I write this to you about people who try to trick you. 27But the gift you got from HimhimHim stays in you. You do not need anyone to teach you. His gift teaches you about everything. It is true and not a lie. Stay in HimhimHim, just as it taught you. 28Now, dear children, stay in HimhimHim. Then, when HeheHe comes, we can be brave. We will not be ashamed when HeheHe comes. 29If you know HeheHe is good, then you know this: anyone who does what is right has been born from HimhimHim.
