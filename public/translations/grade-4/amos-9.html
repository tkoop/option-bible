The Lord Stands by the Altar

1I saw the Lord standing by the altar. He said, "Hit the top of the pillars so the door frames shake. Break them on the heads of all the people. I will kill the rest with a sword. No one will get away. No one will escape. 2If they dig down to the place of the dead, MymyMy hand will grab them there. If they climb up to Heavenheavenheaven, I will bring them down from there. 3If they hide on top of Mount Carmel, I will look for them and take them. If they hide from MemeMe at the bottom of the sea, I will tell the snake to bite them there. 4If they are taken away as prisoners by their enemies, I will tell the sword to kill them there. I will watch them to hurt them, not to help them."

The Lord Is the Maker of All

5The Lord GOD of all armies touches the earth, and it melts. Everyone who lives on it cries. The whole land rises like the Nile River and sinks like the Nile in Egypt. 6He builds HishisHis rooms high in Heavenheavenheaven. He sets HishisHis foundation on the earth. He calls the sea water and pours it on the land. the LORD is HishisHis name.

The Lord Judges Israel

7The LORD says, "Are you not like the people of Cush to MemeMe, people of Israel? Did I not bring Israel up from Egypt? Did I not bring the Philistines from Caphtor? Did I not bring the people of Aram from Kir?" 8"Look, MymyMy eyes are on the sinful kingdom. I will destroy it from the earth. But I will not completely destroy Jacob's family," says the LORD.

9"Look, I will give the command. I will shake the people of Israel among all the nations. It will be like shaking grain in a strainer. Not one small stone will fall to the ground. 10All the sinners among MymyMy people will die by the sword. They say, 'Trouble will not come near us or find us.'"

11"On that day I will raise up David's fallen tent. I will fix its broken walls. I will raise up its ruins. I will build it like in the old days. 12Then they will take over what is left of Edom and all the nations that are called by MymyMy name," says the LORD, who does this.

The Lord Will Restore His People

13"Look, the days are coming," says the LORD, "when the farmer who plows will catch up to the worker who cuts grain. The one who crushes grapes will catch up to the one who plants seed. The mountains will drip sweet wine. All the hills will flow with it. 14I will bring back MymyMy people Israel. They will rebuild ruined cities and live in them. They will plant grape fields and drink the wine. They will make gardens and eat the fruit. 15I will plant them in their land. They will never again be pulled up from the land I gave them," says the LORD your God.
