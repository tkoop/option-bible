Church Leaders

1This is a true saying: If anyone wants to be a church leader, he wants a good job. 2A church leader must be a good man. He should have only one wife. He should stay calm and think clearly. He should act in a nice way. He should welcome guests. He should be able to teach. 3He should not drink too much. He should not hit people. He should be gentle and not fight. He should not love money. 4He should lead his own family well. His children should obey him, and they should be respectful. 5If a man does not know how to lead his own family, how can he take care of God's church? 6He should not be a new believer. Then he will not become proud and fall into the devil's trap. 7He must also have a good name with people outside the church. Then he will not fall into shame and the devil's trap.

Church Helpers

8Helpers in the church should also be respected. They should not lie or say two different things. They should not drink too much wine. They should not be greedy for money. 9They should hold on to the secret truth of faith with a clean heart. 10First, they should be tested. Then, if no one can blame them, they may serve. 11Women should be respected too. They should not talk mean about others. They should stay calm and be faithful in everything. 12Helpers should have only one wife. They should lead their children and homes well. 13Those who serve well gain a good place and strong courage in their faith in Christ Jesus.

Why Paul Wrote This

14I hope to come to you soon. But I am writing these things to you now. 15If I am slow to come, you will know how people should act in God's house. That house is the church of the living God. It is the strong support and base of the truth. 16The secret of living for God is very great: He was shown in a body. He was made right by the SpiritspiritSpirit. Angels saw HimhimHim. He was preached to all nations. People believed in HimhimHim all over the world. He was taken up into glory.
