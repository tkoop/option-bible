Enemies Make Fun of the Builders

1When Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he got very mad. He made fun of the Jews. 2He spoke to his brothers and the army of Samaria. He said, "What are these weak Jews doing? Can they make the city strong again? Will they give gifts to God? Will they finish in one day? Can they make good stones from piles of burned stones?"

3Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him. He said, "Even a fox could knock down their stone wall."

Nehemiah Prays to God

4Hear us, our God, for we are treated badly. Make them pay for their mean words. Let them be carried off as prisoners in another land. 5Do not hide their sin. Do not take away their guilt from before YouyouYou, because they made the builders angry. 6So we built the wall. We joined all of it together up to half its height, because the people wanted to work.

Enemies Plan to Attack

7When Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites, and the people of Ashdod heard that the wall of Jerusalem was being fixed and the gaps were closing, they got very angry. 8They all made a plan to come and fight against Jerusalem and cause trouble. 9We prayed to our God, and we set guards day and night to watch for them.

10The people of Judah said, "ThetheThe workers are getting tired. There is too much broken stone. We cannot build the wall."

11Our enemies said, "They will not know or see us coming. Then we will kill them and stop the work."

12The Jews who lived near them came ten times and said, "Wherever you go, they will attack us."

Nehemiah Gets Ready to Fight

13So I put people behind the lower parts of the wall, at the open spots. I put them in family groups, with swords, spears, and bows. 14I looked around, stood up, and said to the leaders, the officials, and the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord. He is great and amazing. Fight for your brothers, sons, daughters, wives, and homes." 15When our enemies heard that we knew their plan, God ruined it. Then we all went back to work on the wall.

The People Work and Watch

16From that day on, half of my servants worked on the building, and half held the spears, shields, bows, and armourarmourarmor. The leaders stood behind all the people of Judah. 17The builders and the carriers worked with one hand, and held a weapon in the other hand. 18Each builder had a sword tied at his side. The man with the trumpet stayed beside me.

Nehemiah Gives Instructions

19I said to the leaders, the officials, and the rest of the people, "ThetheThe work is big and spread out. We are far apart on the wall. 20Wherever you hear the trumpet sound, run to us there. Our God will fight for us."

Working Day and Night

21So we worked from early morning until the stars came out. Half of us held spears. 22At that time I said to the people, "Let every man and his servant stay in Jerusalem at night. Then they can be guards at night and work during the day."

23Neither I, nor my brothers, nor my servants, nor the guards with me took off our clothes. Each man kept his weapon, even when he went for water.
