Paul Remembers His Visit

1You know, brothers and sisters, that our visit to you was not a waste. 2You know we had already been hurt and treated badly in Philippi. But even with much trouble, God gave us courage to tell you HishisHis good news.

3Our message did not come from a mistake, or from bad thoughts, or from a trick. 4We speak the way God wants. He tested us and trusted us with the good news. We do not try to please people. We try to please God, who checks our hearts.

5You know we never used sweet talk. We did not make up a reason to get money. God is our witness. 6We did not look for praise from people, from you or from others. As messengers of Christ, we could have been a heavy load to you, but we were not.

7Instead, we were gentle with you, like a mother who takes care of her own children. 8We loved you so much that we were glad to share God's good news with you. We even shared our own lives with you, because you became very dear to us.

9Remember, brothers and sisters, how we worked hard day and night. We did not want to be a load to any of you. We told you God's good news. 10You are witnesses, and God is too. We lived in a good, fair, and without fault way with you who believe.

11You know we treated each one of you like a father treats his children. 12We cheered you up, comforted you, and told you to live in a way that honourshonourshonors God. He calls you into HishisHis kingdom and HishisHis greatness.

Paul Thanks God for the Church

13We never stop thanking God. When you heard God's word from us, you accepted it. You did not treat it like human words. You treated it as God's word, which it really is. That word works in you who believe.

14Brothers and sisters, you became like God's people in Judea who belong to Christ Jesus. You suffered the same things from your own people that they suffered from the Jews. 15Those people killed the Lord Jesus and God's prophets. They drove us away. They do not please God. They work against all people. 16They stop us from speaking to people from other nations so those people can be saved. In this way, they keep adding to their sins. But God's anger has finally come on them.

Paul Wants to See Them Again

17Brothers and sisters, we were taken away from you for a short time. We were not with you in person, but we were with you in heart. We wanted very much to see you again. 18We wanted to come to you. I, Paul, tried again and again, but Satan stopped us.

19What is our hope or joy or crown we are proud of? Is it not you? You will stand before our Lord Jesus when HeheHe comes. 20You are our glory and our joy.
