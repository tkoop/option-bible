Peter and John Heal a Man Who Could Not Walk

1One day Peter and John went to the temple to pray at three in the afternoon. 2A man who could not walk since he was born was carried to the temple gate called Beautiful. People put him there every day to beg from those going into the temple.

3When he saw Peter and John about to go in, he asked them for money. 4Peter and John looked at him and said, "Look at us!" 5So the man looked at them. He thought he would get something from them.

6But Peter said, "I don't have silver or gold. But what I have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ from Nazareth, get up and walk!"

7Peter took him by the right hand and helped him up. Right away his feet and ankles became strong. 8He jumped up and stood. Then he began to walk. He went into the temple with them, walking and jumping and praising God.

9All the people saw him walking and praising God. 10They knew he was the man who used to sit and beg at the Beautiful GategateGate. They were full of wonder and surprise at what had happened to him.
