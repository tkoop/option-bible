The Eighth Day Begins

1On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron, Aaron's sons, and the elders of Israel. 2Moses said to Aaron, "Take a young bull for a sin offering. Take a ram for a burnt offering. They must have no flaws. Offer them to the LORD."

3Moses said, "Tell the Israelites, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering. Take a calf and a lamb. They must be one year old. They must have no flaws. They are for a burnt offering.' 4Take an ox and a ram for peace offerings. Sacrifice them to the LORD. Take a grain offering mixed with oil. Today the LORD will show HimselfhimselfHimself to you."

5The people brought what Moses said. They came near and stood before the LORD. 6Moses said, "This is what the LORD told you to do. Then the glory of the LORD will show itself to you."

Aaron Makes the Offerings

7Moses said to Aaron, "Go to the altar. Make your sin offering and burnt offering. Pay for your sins and the people's sins. Make the people's offering. Pay for their sins, just like the LORD said." 8Aaron went to the altar. He killed the calf for his sin offering.

9Aaron's sons brought him the blood. He dipped his finger in it. He put it on the horns of the altar. He poured the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar. 10He burned the fat, the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver from the sin offering on the altar. He did this just like the LORD told Moses. 11He burned the meat and skin outside the camp.

12He killed the burnt offering. Aaron's sons gave him the blood. He sprinkled it all around the altar. 13They gave him the burnt offering cut into pieces, with the head. He burned them on the altar. 14He washed the inside parts and legs. He burned them on the altar with the burnt offering.

15He brought the people's offering. He took the goat for the people's sin offering. He killed it. He paid for sin with it, just like the first one. 16He brought the burnt offering. He did it the right way. 17He brought the grain offering. He filled his hand with it. He burned it on the altar, besides the morning burnt offering.

18He killed the ox and ram for the people. They were peace offerings. Aaron's sons gave him the blood. He sprinkled it all around the altar. 19He took the fat from the ox and ram. He took the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver. 20They put the fats on the chest pieces. He burned the fats on the altar. 21Aaron lifted the chest pieces and the right thigh toward the LORD. He waved them, just like Moses said.

22Aaron raised his hands toward the people and blessed them. Then he came down from making the sin offering, burnt offering, and peace offerings. 23Moses and Aaron went into the meeting tent. They came out and blessed the people. The glory of the LORD showed itself to all the people.

Fire from the LORD

24Fire came from the LORD. It burned up the burnt offering and the fats on the altar. All the people saw it. They shouted with joy and fell on their faces.
