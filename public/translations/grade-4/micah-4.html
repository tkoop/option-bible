All Nations Will Come to God's Mountain

1In the last days, the mountain where the house of the LORD stands will be higher than all the other mountains. It will stand above the hills. People from many lands will come to it. 2Many nations will come. They will say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of Jacob's God. He will teach us HishisHis ways, and we will walk in HishisHis paths." God's law will go out from Zion. The word of the LORD will go out from Jerusalem.

3He will settle fights between many people. He will decide for strong nations far away. They will hammer their swords into farm tools. They will turn their spears into tools to trim trees. One nation will not fight another. They will not learn war anymore. 4Each man will sit under his grapevine and fig tree. No one will scare him. The LORD All-Powerful has spoken. 5All people follow their own gods. But we will follow the LORD our God forever and ever.

The LORD Will Gather His People

6On that day, says the LORD, "I will gather the ones who limp. I will bring together the ones sent away. I will bring back the ones I hurt. 7I will make the ones who limp into the people who are left. I will make the ones sent far away into a strong nation. The LORD will rule over them on Mount Zion from now on and forever." 8And you, tower near the sheep, hill of Zion's people, your old rule will come back to you. The kingdom will come to Jerusalem's people.

Zion Will Hurt but Be Saved

9Why do you cry so loud now? Is there no king in you? Has your wise helper died? Pain has grabbed you like a woman having a baby. 10Twist and groan, Zion's daughter, like a woman having a baby. Now you will leave the city. You will live in the field. You will go all the way to Babylon. There you will be saved. There the LORD will buy you back from your enemies.

11Now many nations have come together against you. They say, "Let her be ruined, and let us look at Zion." 12But they do not know what the LORD is thinking. They do not understand HishisHis plan. He has gathered them like grain to be beaten. 13Get up and beat them, Zion's daughter, for I will make your horn like iron. I will make your feet like bronze. You will crush many people. I will give their gains to the LORD and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.
