A Message About Tyre

1In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me. He said, 2"SonsonSon of man, Tyre said about Jerusalem, 'Ha! The door for all nations is broken. It swings open to me. Now that she is ruined, I will be full.'"

The LORD Is Against Tyre

3"So the Lord the LORD says: 'Look, I am against you, Tyre. I will bring many nations against you, like the sea brings its waves.' 4'They will break down your walls and tear down your towers. I will scrape your dust from you and make you a bare rock.' 5'YouyouYou will be a place to spread nets in the sea, because I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD. You will be taken by the nations.' 6'YouryourYour daughters in the field will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.'"

Nebuchadnezzar Will Attack Tyre

7"For the Lord the LORD says: 'Look, I am bringing Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, king of kings, from the north. He comes with horses, chariots, horsemen, and many people.' 8'He will kill your daughters in the field with the sword. He will set up a siege wall against you. He will pour out a ramp against you. He will lift up shields against you.' 9'He will hit your walls with his battering rams. He will break down your towers with his axes.' 10'His many horses will cover you with dust. He will come through your gates like men break through a broken wall. Your walls will shake at the sound of horsemen, wheels, and chariots.' 11'With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword. He will bring down your strong forts to the ground.'"

12"They will take your riches and steal your goods. They will break down your walls and tear down your nice houses. They will throw your stones, wood, and dust into the water. 13I will stop your happy songs. No one will hear your harps again. 14I will make you a bare rock. You will be a place to spread nets. You will never be built again, because I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.'"

Nations Will Mourn Tyre

15"The Lord the LORD says to Tyre: 'Will not the islands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan and people are killed inside you?' 16'Then all the leaders of the sea will step down from their thrones. They will take off their robes and their fine clothes. They will put on shaking fear. They will sit on the ground. They will shake every moment and be shocked at you.' 17'They will sing a sad song about you. They will say, "How you are destroyed, famous city, where sailors lived! You were strong on the sea, you and your people. You made everyone who lived there afraid."' 18'Now the islands will shake on the day you fall. The islands in the sea will be scared because you are gone.'"

19"For the Lord the LORD says: 'I will make you a ruined city, like cities no one lives in. I will bring the deep water over you, and the great waters will cover you.' 20'I will send you down with those who go down to the deep hole, to the people of long ago. I will put you in the lowest parts of the earth, like old ruins, with those who go down to the deep hole. You will never live there again. I will bring beauty to the land of the living.' 21'I will make you a terror, and you will be gone. People will look for you, but they will never find you again, declares the Lord the LORD.'"
