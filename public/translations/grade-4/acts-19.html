They said, “No. We have not even heard that there is a HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit.”

3Paul asked, “Then what baptism did you have?”

They said, “John’s baptism.”

4Paul said, “John baptized people to show they were turning from sin. He told them to believe in the OneoneOne who would come after him. That OneoneOne is Jesus.”

5When they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. 6When Paul put his hands on them, the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit came on them. They spoke in new languages and told God’s messages. 7There were about twelve men in all.

Paul Teaches in the Synagogue

8Paul went into the synagogue. He spoke boldly for three months. He talked with people and tried to convince them about God’s kingdom.

9But some people were stubborn. They did not believe. They spoke badly about the Way in front of the crowd. So Paul left them. He took the followers with him. He talked every day in the school of Tyrannus. 10This went on for two years. So everyone who lived in Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the Lord’s word.

God Does Great Things Through Paul

11God did amazing things through Paul’s hands. 12Even handkerchiefs and aprons that had touched his skin were taken to sick people. Their sickness left them, and evil spirits went out of them.

The Sons of Sceva

13Some Jewish men travelledtravelledtraveled around and tried to make evil spirits leave people. They tried to use the name of the Lord Jesus over people who had evil spirits. They said, “I command you to leave by Jesus, whom Paul preaches.” 14Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, did this.

15But the evil spirit answered, “I know Jesus, and I know Paul, but who are you?”

16The man with the evil spirit jumped on them. He overpowered them and hurt them. They ran out of the house naked and wounded.

17Everyone in Ephesus, both Jews and Greeks, heard about this. They were afraid. The name of the Lord Jesus was honouredhonouredhonored.

Many People Turn from Magic

18Many believers came. They admitted their sins and told what they had done. 19Many who had done magic brought their books and burned them in front of everyone. They counted the cost. It came to fifty thousand pieces of silver. 20So the Lord’s word grew strong and won.

Paul Plans to Go to Jerusalem

21After this, Paul decided, led by the SpiritspiritSpirit, to go to Jerusalem. He would pass through Macedonia and Achaia. He said, “After I go there, I must see Rome too.” 22He sent two helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia. He stayed in Asia for a while.

A Big Uproar in Ephesus

23About that time, there was a big uproar about the Way. 24A man who worked with silver named Demetrius made silver temples of Artemis. This gave much work to the workers.

25He called the workers and others who did this kind of work together. He said, “Men, you know that we make our money from this work. 26You see and hear that not only in Ephesus but almost all Asia, this Paul has convinced many people and turned them away. He says gods made by hands are not gods. 27So our business is in danger. Also, the temple of the great goddess Artemis may be counted as nothing. Her greatness, which all Asia and the world worship, may be destroyed.”

28When they heard this, they were full of anger. They shouted, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” 29The whole city was confused. They rushed together into the theater. They grabbed Gaius and Aristarchus, Paul’s travel friends from Macedonia.

30Paul wanted to go in to the crowd, but the followers would not let him. 31Some of the city leaders who were his friends sent a message. They begged him not to go into the theater.

32The crowd was confused. Some shouted one thing, and some shouted another. Most did not know why they had come together.

33The Jews pushed Alexander forward. He motioned with his hand. He wanted to explain things to the crowd. 34But when they knew he was a Jew, they all shouted the same thing for about two hours: “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”

35The city clerk quieted the crowd. He said, “Men of Ephesus, who does not know that the city of Ephesus guards the temple of the great Artemis and the stone that fell from the sky? 36Since these things cannot be denied, you should be calm. Do not do anything quick and foolish. 37You brought these men here. But they did not rob temples. They did not speak against our goddess. 38If Demetrius and the workers with him have a charge against anyone, the courts are open. There are judges. Let them bring charges against each other. 39If you want anything more, it will be settled in the lawful meeting. 40We are in danger of being blamed for a riot today. There is no reason for this. We cannot explain this crowd.” 41After he said this, he sent the crowd away.
