Money for God's People

1Now about the money you collect for God's people. Do what I told the churches in Galatia to do. 2On the first day of each week, each of you should set aside some money. Save it up if things go well for you. Then I will not have to collect money when I come. 3When I get there, I will send people you choose with letters. They will take your gift to Jerusalem. 4If it is right for me to go too, they can go with me.

Paul's Travel Plans

5I will come to you after I travel through Macedonia. Yes, I am going through Macedonia. 6Maybe I will stay with you for a while, or even spend the winter. Then you can help me on my way to where I go next. 7I do not want to see you now for just a short time. I hope to stay with you longer, if the Lord lets me. 8But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost. 9A great chance to do good work has opened up for me there, but many people are against me.

Timothy and Apollos

10If Timothy comes, make sure he is not afraid with you. He does the Lord's work, just like I do. 11No one should look down on him. Send him on his way in peace, so he can come to me. I am waiting for him with the brothers. 12Now about our brother Apollos. I asked him many times to come to you with the brothers. He did not want to come now, but he will come when he has time.

Stand Strong and Do All in Love

13Be on guard. Stand strong in your faith. Be brave. Be strong. 14Do everything in love.

Respect Those Who Serve

15You know that Stephanas and his family were the first in Achaia to believe. They gave themselves to serving God's people. I ask you, brothers and sisters, 16to follow the lead of people like them, and of everyone who works hard to help. 17I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus came. They made up for what you could not do. 18They made my spirit and your spirit feel better. So respect people like them.

Greetings

19The churches in Asia send you greetings. Aquila and Priscilla send you many greetings in the Lord. So does the church that meets in their house. 20All the brothers and sisters send you greetings. Greet each other with a holy kiss. 21I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.

Final Blessing

22If anyone does not love the Lord, let that person be cursed. Our Lord, come! 23May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. 24My love is with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
