Ruth Goes to Work in the Fields

1Now Naomi had a relative. He was a strong man from Elimelech’s family. His name was Boaz. 2Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, “Let me go to the fields. I will pick up leftover grain. Maybe someone will be kind to me.”

Naomi said, “Go, my daughter.”

3So Ruth went to the fields. She picked up grain behind the workers. By chance, she came to a field that belonged to Boaz. He was from Elimelech’s family.

Boaz Comes to the Field

4Just then Boaz came from Bethlehem. He said to the workers, “The LORD be with you!”

The workers said, “The LORD bless you!”

5Boaz asked the boss of the workers, “Who is that young woman?”

6The boss answered, “She is the Moabite woman who came back with Naomi from Moab.”

7She asked, “Please let me pick up grain and tie it into bundles behind the workers.” She has worked since morning. She only rested a little in the house.

Boaz Is Kind to Ruth

8Then Boaz said to Ruth, “Listen, my daughter. Do not go to another field to pick grain. Do not leave this field. Stay close to my young women. 9Watch the field where they cut grain. Follow them. I told the young men not to bother you. When you are thirsty, go to the water jars. Drink the water the men drew.”

10Ruth bowed down to the ground. She said, “Why are you so kind to me? Why do you notice me? I am a foreigner.”

11Boaz answered, “I have heard all you did for your mother-in-law after your husband died. You left your father and mother and your home country. You came to a people you did not know. 12May the LORD repay you for what you have done. May the LORD, the God of Israel, give you a full reward. You have come to HimhimHim for safety under HishisHis wings.”

13Then Ruth said, “My lord, you have comforted me. You have spoken kindly to me, your servant. I am not even like one of your servant girls.”

14At mealtime, Boaz said to her, “Come here. Eat some bread. Dip your piece in the vinegar.” So she sat with the workers. Boaz gave her roasted grain. She ate until she was full. She had some left over.

15When Ruth got up to gather grain, Boaz told his workers, “Let her gather even among the bundles. Do not shame her. 16Also pull out some grain from the bundles for her. Leave it so she can pick it up. Do not scold her.”

Ruth Brings Grain to Naomi

17So Ruth gathered grain in the field until evening. Then she beat the grain out of the stalks. She had about a big basket of barley. 18She carried it to town. Her mother-in-law saw what she had gathered. Ruth also gave her the food left from her meal.

19Her mother-in-law asked, “Where did you gather today? Where did you work? Blessed is the man who noticed you!”

Then Ruth told her mother-in-law about the man she worked with. She said, “The man I worked with today is named Boaz.”

20Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, “The LORD bless him! He has not stopped showing kindness to the living and the dead.” Naomi also said, “That man is a close relative of ours. He is one of our family redeemers.”

21Then Ruth the Moabite said, “He also told me to stay close to his workers until they finish all his harvest.”

22Naomi said to Ruth, “It is good, my daughter, that you go with his young women. Then no one will bother you in another field.”

23So Ruth stayed close to Boaz’s young women. She gathered grain until the barley and wheat harvests were done. Then she lived with her mother-in-law.
