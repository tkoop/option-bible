Moab Breaks Away from Israel

1After King Ahab died, Moab broke away from Israel.

Ahaziah Gets Hurt

2King Ahaziah fell through the screen in his upstairs room in Samaria. He was badly hurt. He sent messengers to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, "WillwillWill I get well from this sickness?"

The Lord Sends Elijah

3But an angel of the the LORD said to Elijah the Tishbite, "Go and meet the messengers of the king of Samaria. Say to them, 'Is there no God in Israel? Why are you going to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron?'"

4"So this is what the the LORD says: You will not get up from the bed you went to. You will die for sure." Then Elijah went.

The Messengers Go Back

5The messengers went back to the king. He asked them, "Why did you come back?"

6They said, "A man came to meet us. He told us, 'Go back to the king who sent you. Tell him, "This is what the the LORD says: Is there no God in Israel? Why do you send men to ask Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron? So you will not get up from the bed you went to. You will die for sure."'"

Elijah Goes to the King

Ahaziah Dies

17So Ahaziah died, just as the the LORD had said through Elijah. He had no son, so his brother Jehoram became king after him. This was in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah.

18The other things Ahaziah did are written in the book of the kings of Israel.
