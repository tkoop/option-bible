Jesus and the Sabbath Day

1On a Sabbath day, Jesus was walking through grain fields. His followers picked grain heads and rubbed them in their hands to eat.

2Some Pharisees said, "Why are you doing what is not allowed on the Sabbath?"

3Jesus answered, "Have you not read what David did when he and his men were hungry? 4He went into God's house. He took the holy bread. He gave it to his men, and they ate it. Only priests were allowed to eat that bread. 5The SonsonSon of ManmanMan is Lord of the Sabbath."

6On another Sabbath, Jesus went into the meeting place and taught. A man was there. His right hand was weak and dry.

7The teachers of the law and the Pharisees watched Jesus. They wanted a reason to accuse HimhimHim. They watched to see if HeheHe would heal on the Sabbath.

8Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said to the man with the weak hand, "Get up and stand in the middle." The man got up and stood there.

9Jesus said to them, "I ask you: Is it right to do good on the Sabbath or to do bad? Is it right to save a life or to destroy it?"

10Jesus looked at all of them. He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." The man did it, and his hand was made well. 11They were very angry. They talked with each other about what to do to Jesus.

Jesus Chooses His Twelve Apostles

12In those days, Jesus went up on a mountain to pray. He prayed to God all night. 13When day came, HeheHe called HishisHis followers. He chose twelve of them and named them apostles. 14They were Simon, whom HeheHe named Peter, and his brother Andrew; James and John; Philip and Bartholomew; 15Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Simon called the Zealot, 16Judas son of James, and Judas Iscariot, who later turned against Jesus.

Jesus Teaches the People

17Jesus came down with them and stood on a flat place. A large crowd of HishisHis followers was there. Many people came from Judea, Jerusalem, and the seashore near Tyre and Sidon. 18They came to hear HimhimHim and to be healed from their sicknesses. People troubled by evil spirits were healed. 19Everyone in the crowd tried to touch HimhimHim, because power came from HimhimHim and healed them all.

20Jesus looked at HishisHis followers and said, "BlessedblessedBlessed are you poor, because God's kingdom is yours. 21Blessed are you who are hungry now, because you will be filled. Blessed are you who cry now, because you will laugh. 22Blessed are you when people hate you, push you away, say mean things to you, and say your name is bad because of the SonsonSon of ManmanMan. 23Be glad on that day. Jump for joy! You have a big reward in Heavenheavenheaven. Their fathers did the same things to the prophets."

"But trouble is coming for you rich people, because you already have your good things. 25Trouble is coming for you who are full now, because you will be hungry. Trouble is coming for you who laugh now, because you will be sad and cry. 26Trouble is coming for you when all people say good things about you. Their fathers did the same to the false prophets."

Love Your Enemies

27"But I say to you who listen: Love your enemies. Do good to those who hate you. 28Say good things about people who curse you. Pray for those who are mean to you. 29If someone hits you on the cheek, let him hit the other cheek too. If someone takes your coat, do not stop him from taking your shirt. 30Give to everyone who asks you. If someone takes your things, do not ask for them back. 31Do to others what you want them to do to you."

32"If you love people who love you, what good is that? Even sinners love people who love them. 33If you do good to people who do good to you, what good is that? Even sinners do that. 34If you lend to people you hope will pay you back, what good is that? Even sinners lend to sinners to get the same back. 35But love your enemies. Do good. Lend, and do not hope to get it back. Then your reward will be big. You will be children of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh, because HeheHe is kind to people who are not thankful and to bad people. 36Be kind, just like your FatherfatherFather is kind."

37"Do not judge others, and you will not be judged. Do not blame others, and you will not be blamed. Forgive others, and you will be forgiven. 38Give, and it will be given to you. People will give you a good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over. They will pour it into your lap. The way you measure for others is the way it will be measured to you."

39Jesus also told them a story: "Can a blind man lead a blind man? Will they not both fall into a hole? 40A student is not above his teacher. But when a student is fully trained, he will be like his teacher."

41"Why do you see the tiny piece in your brother's eye, but you do not see the big stick in your own eye? 42How can you say to your brother, 'Let me take the tiny piece out of your eye,' when you do not see the big stick in your own eye? You faker! First take the stick out of your own eye. Then you will see clearly to take the piece out of your brother's eye."

Good Fruit and Bad Fruit

43"A good tree does not make bad fruit. A bad tree does not make good fruit. 44Each tree is known by its own fruit. People do not pick figs from thorn bushes. They do not pick grapes from a briar bush. 45A good man brings good things out of the good stored in his heart. A bad man brings bad things out of the bad stored in his heart. What a person says shows what is in his heart."

Two House Builders

46"Why do you call MemeMe 'Lord, Lord,' but you do not do what I say? 47I will show you what everyone is like who comes to MemeMe, hears MymyMy words, and does them. 48He is like a man who builds a house. He digs down deep and sets the base on rock. A flood comes. The river hits that house. But it cannot shake it, because it was built well. 49But the one who hears and does not do is like a man who builds a house on the ground with no base. The river hits it. It falls right away. And the house is badly broken."
