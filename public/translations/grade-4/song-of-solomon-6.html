The Friends Ask About the Beloved

1"Where did your loved one go, most beautiful of women? Which way did he turn? We want to look for him with you."

The Bride Answers

2"My loved one went down to his garden, to the spice beds. He feeds in the gardens and picks lilies." 3"I belong to my loved one, and my loved one belongs to me. He feeds among the lilies."

The Groom Praises His Bride

4"You are beautiful, my darling, like the city of Tirzah. You are lovely like Jerusalem. You are amazing like an army with flags." 5"Turn your eyes away from me. They are too much for me. Your hair is like a flock of goats coming down from Gilead." 6"Your teeth are like a flock of sheep coming up from the wash. Each one has a twin, and none is missing." 7"The sides of your face are like a piece of pomegranate behind your veil." 8"There are sixty queens and eighty other wives, and young women too many to count." 9"But my dove, my perfect one, is the only one. She is the only daughter of her mother. She is the favouritefavouritefavorite of the one who gave birth to her. The young women saw her and called her blessed. The queens and other wives praised her."

The Friends Admire the Bride

10"Who is this looking down like the morning light? She is beautiful like the moon. She is bright like the sun. She is amazing like an army with flags."

The Bride Visits the Garden

11"I went down to the nut grove to see the flowers in the valley. I wanted to see if the vines had buds or if the pomegranates were flowering." 12"Before I knew it, my heart put me with the royal chariots of my people."

The Friends Call Her Back

13"Come back, come back, dear Shulammite. Come back, come back, so we can look at you. Why would you look at the Shulammite like the dance of two armies?"
