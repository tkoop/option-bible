Paul Travels to Macedonia and Greece

1After the trouble ended, Paul called the followers to him. He cheered them up and said goodbye. Then he left to go to Macedonia. 2He went through those places. He talked a lot to cheer the people. Then he came to Greece. 3He stayed three months. The Jews made a plan to hurt him. He was about to sail to Syria. So he decided to go back through Macedonia. 4Sopater from Berea went with him. Aristarchus and Secundus were from Thessalonica. Gaius was from Derbe. Timothy went too. Tychicus and Trophimus were from Asia. 5These men went ahead and waited for us at Troas. 6After the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread, we sailed from Philippi. It took five days to reach them at Troas. We stayed there seven days.

Paul Preaches in Troas

7On the first day of the week, we met to break bread. Paul was going to leave the next day. He talked with the people. He kept talking until midnight. 8There were many lamps in the upstairs room where we were meeting. 9A young man named Eutychus was sitting in the window. He was very sleepy. Paul talked for a long time. Eutychus fell into a deep sleep. He fell from the third floor. They picked him up. He was dead. 10Paul went down. He fell on him. He hugged him. He said, "Don't worry. He is alive." 11Paul went back upstairs. He broke bread and ate. He talked with them until morning. Then he left. 12The people were very comforted. They took the boy home alive.

Paul Travels to Miletus

13We went ahead to the ship. We sailed to Assos. We were going to pick up Paul there. He had planned it that way. He wanted to walk. 14When he met us at Assos, we took him on board. We came to Mitylene. 15We sailed from there. The next day we were across from Chios. The next day we stopped at Samos. The day after that, we came to Miletus. 16Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus. He did not want to spend time in Asia. He was in a hurry to get to Jerusalem for Pentecost, if he could.

Paul Speaks to the Church Leaders

17From Miletus, Paul sent to Ephesus. He called for the church leaders. 18When they came to him, he said, "YouyouYou know how I lived with you. I was with you the whole time from the first day I came to Asia. 19I served the Lord with a humble heart. I served with tears. I served through hard times when the Jews made plans against me. 20I did not hold back anything good for you. I taught you in public and in your homes. 21I told Jews and Greeks to turn to God. I told them to have faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.

"22Now I am going to Jerusalem. The HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit is sending me. I do not know what will happen to me there. 23I only know this: in every city, the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit tells me that chains and troubles are waiting for me. 24But I do not care about my own life. I want to finish my race. I want to finish the work the Lord Jesus gave me. That work is telling people the good news of God's grace.

"25Now I know that none of you will see me again. I preached God's kingdom to all of you. 26So I tell you today: I am clean from the blood of all people. 27I did not keep back any of God's plan from you. 28Watch yourselves and all the sheep. The HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit made you leaders to take care of God's church. He bought it with HishisHis own blood.

"29I know that after I leave, fierce wolves will come among you. They will not spare the sheep. 30Even some of your own men will stand up. They will say wrong things. They will try to pull followers away to themselves. 31So stay awake. Remember that for three years, I never stopped warning each of you. I did it night and day, and I cried. 32Now I give you to God. I give you to HishisHis word of grace. It can build you up. It can give you a share with all God's holy people.

"33I did not want anyone's silver, gold, or clothes. 34You know these hands worked for my needs. They worked for the people with me. 35In everything, I showed you that we must help weak people. We must remember the words of the Lord Jesus. He said, 'It is better to give than to get.'"

Paul Prays and Says Goodbye

36After he said this, he knelt down with all of them. He prayed. 37They all cried a lot. They hugged him. They kissed him. 38They were very sad. They were sad most because he said they would not see his face again. Then they walked with him to the ship.
