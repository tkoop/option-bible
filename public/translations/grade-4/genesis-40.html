Joseph in Prison

1After this, the king of Egypt's cupbearer and baker did wrong to their master, the king of Egypt. 2The king of Egypt was angry with two of his helpers. He was angry with the head cupbearer and the head baker. 3He put them in jail. It was in the house of the guard captain. It was the same jail where Joseph was held. 4The guard captain put Joseph in charge of them. Joseph took care of them. They stayed in jail for a while.

Two Dreams

5The king's cupbearer and baker were in jail. One night, each man had a dream. Each dream had its own meaning. 6In the morning, Joseph came to them. He saw they looked sad. 7He asked the king's helpers who were in jail with him, "Why do you look so sad today?" 8They said, "We had dreams, but no one can tell us what they mean." Joseph said, "Only God can tell what dreams mean. Please tell me your dreams."

9The head cupbearer told Joseph his dream. He said, "In my dream, I saw a grapevine in front of me. 10The vine had three branches. It seemed to bud and bloom. Then its clusters made ripe grapes. 11Pharaoh's cup was in my hand. I took the grapes. I squeezed them into Pharaoh's cup. Then I put the cup in Pharaoh's hand."

12Joseph said, "Here is what it means. The three branches are three days. 13In three more days, Pharaoh will give you your job back. You will put Pharaoh's cup in his hand, like you did before. 14But when things go well for you, please remember me. Be kind to me. Tell Pharaoh about me. Get me out of this place. 15I was stolen from the land of the Hebrews. I have done nothing wrong here. But they put me in this prison."

16The head baker saw that the meaning was good. He said to Joseph, "I also had a dream. In my dream, I had three baskets of bread on my head. 17The top basket had all kinds of baked food for Pharaoh. But birds came and ate the food from the basket on my head."

18Joseph said, "Here is what it means. The three baskets are three days. 19In three days, Pharaoh will put you to death. He will hang you on a tree. Birds will eat your body."

The Dreams Come True

20Three days later was Pharaoh's birthday. He made a feast for all his servants. He brought out the head cupbearer and the head baker in front of them. 21He gave the head cupbearer his job back. He put the cup in Pharaoh's hand. 22But he hanged the head baker, just as Joseph had said. 23The head cupbearer did not remember Joseph. He forgot him.
