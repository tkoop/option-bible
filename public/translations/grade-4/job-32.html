Elihu Gets Angry

1These three men stopped answering Job because Job thought he was right. 2Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram, became very angry. He was angry at Job because Job said he was right instead of God. 3He was also angry at Job’s three friends. They had no answer, but they still blamed Job. 4Elihu waited to speak to Job because the other men were older than him. 5When Elihu saw that the three men had no answer, he became very angry.

Elihu Speaks

"6I am young, and you are old. So I was afraid to tell you what I know. 7I thought older people should speak. I thought many years should teach wisdom. 8But it is the spirit in people. The breath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty gives understanding. 9Not all people are wise. Not all old people understand what is right. 10So listen to me. I will tell you what I know.

"11I waited for your words. I listened to your ideas until you were done talking. 12I listened closely. None of you proved Job wrong. None of you answered his words. 13Don't say, 'We found wisdom. God will push him down, not a person.' 14Job has not spoken against me. So I will not answer him with your words. 15They are shocked and have no more words. They stopped talking. 16I waited because they stopped talking. They stood there and did not answer again.

"17Now I will answer too. I will tell what I know. 18I have so many words. The spirit inside me pushes me to speak. 19I feel like I will burst with words. It is like new wine ready to spill. 20I will speak so I can feel better. I will open my mouth and answer. 21I will not favourfavourfavor anyone. I will not flatter any person. 22I do not know how to flatter. If I did, my MakermakerMaker would soon take me away."
