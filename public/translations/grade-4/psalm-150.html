A Song of Praise

1Praise the LORD. Praise God in HishisHis holy place. Praise HimhimHim in the strong sky. 2Praise HimhimHim for HishisHis mighty acts. Praise HimhimHim for how great HeheHe is.

3Praise HimhimHim with a trumpet. Praise HimhimHim with a harp and a lyre. 4Praise HimhimHim with a tambourine and dancing. Praise HimhimHim with strings and a flute.

5Praise HimhimHim with loud cymbals. Praise HimhimHim with crashing cymbals. 6Let everything that breathes praise the LORD. Praise the LORD.
