The Macedonians Give with Joy

1Now we want you to know about the grace God gave to the churches in Macedonia. 2Even in hard trouble, their great joy and deep poverty led to rich giving. 3I can tell you they gave as much as they could, and even more. They chose to give on their own. 4They begged us to let them share in this grace and help God's people. 5They did more than we hoped. First they gave themselves to the Lord, and then to us, by God's will.

6So we asked Titus to finish this grace among you too, since he started it. 7You do well in everything: faith, speaking, knowledge, eagerness, and love. So do well in this grace too. 8I am not giving an order. I am testing if your love is real by comparing it with how eager others are.

9You know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. He was rich, but HeheHe became poor for you, so that by HishisHis poverty you might become rich. 10Here is my advice: it is good for you. Last year you were first not only to do this work but also to want to do it. 11Now finish it, so that your eager desire to do it is matched by your finishing it, with what you have. 12If the eagerness is there, the gift is accepted based on what you have, not on what you do not have. 13I do not mean that others should have an easy time and you should have a hard time. I mean fairness. 14Right now, your plenty can meet their need, so that later their plenty can meet your need. Then there will be fairness. 15It is written, "ThetheThe one who gathered much did not have too much, and the one who gathered little did not have too little."

Titus and His Helpers

16Thanks be to God, who put the same eagerness for you into Titus's heart. 17He not only accepted our urging, but because he was very eager, he went to you on his own. 18We sent with him the brother who is praised in all the churches for the gospel. 19Not only that, but the churches chose him to travel with us with this grace we manage, to bring glory to the Lord HimselfhimselfHimself and to show our eagerness. 20We are careful so that no one will blame us about this large gift we manage. 21We want to do what is right not only before the Lord but also before people. 22We sent with them our brother whom we have often tested and found eager in many things. Now he is even more eager because of his great trust in you. 23As for Titus, he is my partner and helper for you. As for our brothers, they are messengers of the churches and bring glory to Christ. 24So show them, in front of the churches, proof of your love and of our praise for you.
