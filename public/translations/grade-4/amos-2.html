God Says Moab Did Wrong

1The LORD says, "Moab has done wrong again and again. I will not take back its punishment, because Moab burned the bones of the king of Edom and turned them to powder.

2"So I will send fire on Moab. It will burn the forts of Kerioth. Moab will die in the loud noise of battle, with shouts and trumpet blasts.

3"I will take away its ruler. I will kill all its leaders with him," says the LORD.

God Says Judah Did Wrong

4The LORD says, "Judah has done wrong again and again. I will not take back its punishment, because Judah turned away from the law of the LORD and did not keep HishisHis rules. They believed lies that their fathers believed.

5"So I will send fire on Judah. It will burn the forts of Jerusalem."

God Says Israel Did Wrong

6The LORD says, "Israel has done wrong again and again. I will not take back its punishment, because they sell good people for silver and poor people for a pair of sandals.

7"They walk on the heads of poor people like dust on the ground. They are not fair to people who need help. A man and his father go to the same young woman. They make MymyMy holy name unclean.

8"They lie down by every altar on clothes they took from poor people. In the house of their God, they drink wine that came from fines.

9"Yet I destroyed the Amorites before them. The Amorites were as tall as cedar trees and as strong as oak trees. I destroyed their fruit above and their roots below.

10"I brought you up from Egypt. I led you for forty years in the desert. I did this so you could take the land of the Amorites.

11"I chose some of your sons to be prophets. I chose some of your young men to be special servants called Nazirites. Isn't this true, children of Israel?" declares the LORD.

God Will Punish Israel

12The LORD says, "YouyouYou made the Nazirites drink wine. You told the prophets not to speak God's message.

13"So I will crush you where you are. I will crush you like a heavy cart full of grain crushing what is under it.

14"Fast runners will not get away. Strong men will lose their strength. Warriors will not save their lives.

15"Archers will not stand their ground. Fast runners will not escape. Horse riders will not save their lives.

16"Even the bravest warrior will run away with nothing on that day," declares the LORD.
