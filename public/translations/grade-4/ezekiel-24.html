The Day Jerusalem Was Surrounded

1On the tenth day of the tenth month in the ninth year, the LORD spoke to me. 2He said, "SonsonSon of man, write down today's date. Today the king of Babylon has surrounded Jerusalem."

The Pot of Meat

3Tell this stubborn people a story. Say,

"Put the pot on the fire. Pour water into it. 4Put in the meat pieces—all the best pieces, the thigh and shoulder. Fill it with the best bones. 5Take the best sheep. Pile bones under the pot. Boil it well. Cook the bones in it too."

6So this is what the Lord GOD says:

"Oh, city full of blood! You are like a pot with grime stuck inside. The grime has not come out. Take the meat out piece by piece. No one chose the pieces. 7Her blood is in her. She poured it on bare rock. She did not pour it on the ground to cover it with dust. 8To make MemeMe angry and pay her back, I put her blood on bare rock, so it would not be covered."

Heat it until its bronze glows. Let its grime melt inside. Let its grime be burned away. 12She worked hard, but her grime stayed. The fire did not take it out."
