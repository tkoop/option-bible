God Tells Moses About the Last Plague

1Now the LORD said to Moses, "I will bring one more trouble on Pharaoh and Egypt. After that, he will let you go from here. When he lets you go, he will push you out completely. 2Tell the people that men and women should ask their neighbors for silver and gold things."

3The LORD made the Egyptians kind to the people. Moses was very important in Egypt. Pharaoh's officials and the people thought highly of him.

Moses Warns Pharaoh

4So Moses said, "This is what the LORD says: 'About midnight I will go all through Egypt. 5Every firstborn in Egypt will die. This includes Pharaoh's firstborn son on his throne, the firstborn son of the slave girl at the mill, and even the firstborn of the animals. 6There will be loud crying all over Egypt. There has never been crying like this, and there never will be again. 7But among the Israelites, not even a dog will bark at a person or an animal. Then you will know that the LORD treats Egypt and Israel differently. 8All your officials will come to me and bow down. They will say, 'Go, you and all the people who follow you!' After that I will leave." Then Moses, very angry, left Pharaoh.

God Says Pharaoh Will Not Listen

9The LORD had said to Moses, "Pharaoh will not listen to you, so that MymyMy wonders may be multiplied in Egypt."

Pharaoh Still Refuses

10Moses and Aaron did all these miracles before Pharaoh, but the LORD made Pharaoh stubborn, and he did not let the Israelites leave his land.
