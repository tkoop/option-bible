Ezekiel Sees a Strange Vision

1In the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day, I sat in my house. The leaders of Judah sat in front of me. Then the hand of the Lord GOD came on me there. 2I looked and saw a shape like a man. From HishisHis waist down, HeheHe looked like fire. From HishisHis waist up, HeheHe shone like bright metal. 3He reached out what looked like a hand and took me by the hair of my head. The SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me between the ground and the sky. He brought me in a vision to Jerusalem. He brought me to the north gate of the inner yard. There was a false god there that makes God jealous. 4There I saw the glory of the God of Israel. It looked like the vision I saw in the valley.

The False God at the Gate

5He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, look toward the north.” I looked north. By the altar gate, at the entrance, stood that false god that makes God jealous. 6He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, do you see what they are doing? The people of Israel are doing terrible sins here. They drive MemeMe away from MymyMy holy place. But you will see even worse sins than these.”

A Hole in the Wall

7Then HeheHe brought me to the entrance of the yard. I looked and saw a hole in the wall. 8He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, dig through the wall.” I dug through the wall and found a door.

A Hidden Room Full of Idols

9He said, “Go in and see the terrible things they do here.” 10I went in and looked. I saw all kinds of crawling things and gross animals. I also saw all the false gods of Israel carved all around the wall. 11Seventy leaders of Israel stood in front of them. Jaazaniah son of Shaphan stood among them. Each man held a small pan. Sweet-smelling smoke rose up.

12He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, do you see what the leaders of Israel do in the dark? Each man is in his own private room. They say, ‘The ThetheThe LORD does not see us. The the LORD has left the land.’ 13You will see them do even worse sins.”

Women Cry for Tammuz

14Then HeheHe brought me to the north gate of the the LORD’s house. I saw women sitting there, crying for Tammuz, a false god. 15He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, do you see this? You will see even worse sins than these.”

Men Worship the Sun

16Then HeheHe brought me into the inner yard of the the LORD’s house. At the entrance to the the LORD’s temple, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men. Their backs were toward the the LORD’s temple. Their faces looked east. They bowed down to the sun. 17He said to me, “Son of ManmanMan, have you seen this? Is it a small thing for Judah to do these terrible sins here? They fill the land with hurt and keep making MemeMe angry. Look! They are insulting MemeMe. 18So I will act in anger. I will not feel sorry for them. I will not spare them. Even if they shout loudly to MemeMe, I will not listen.”
