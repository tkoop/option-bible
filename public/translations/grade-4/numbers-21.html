Israel Wins at Hormah

1The Canaanite king of Arad lived in the south land. He heard that Israel was coming on the road to Atharim. He fought Israel and took some people captive. 2Israel made a promise to the LORD. They said, "If YouyouYou give these people to us, we will destroy their cities." 3The LORD heard Israel. He gave the Canaanites to them. They destroyed them and their cities. They named that place Hormah.

The People Complain

4The people left Mount Hor. They went toward the Red SeaseaSea to go around Edom. The people grew tired on the way. 5They spoke against God and Moses. They said, "Why did you bring us out of Egypt to die in this desert? There is no bread and no water. We hate this bad food." 6So the LORD sent poisonous snakes among the people. The snakes bit them, and many Israelites died. 7The people came to Moses and said, "We sinned. We spoke against the LORD and against you. Pray to the LORD to take the snakes away from us." So Moses prayed for the people.

8The LORD said to Moses, "Make a snake and put it on a pole. Anyone who is bitten can look at it and live." 9Moses made a bronze snake and put it on a pole. If a snake bit someone, that person looked at the bronze snake and lived.

Israel Travels On

10The Israelites moved on and camped at Oboth. 11Then they moved from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim. It was in the desert east of Moab. 12They moved from there and camped in the Zered ValleyvalleyValley. 13Then they moved and camped beyond the Arnon River. It was in the desert that came from the Amorites. The Arnon was the border between Moab and the Amorites.

14That is why the BookbookBook of the Wars of the LORD says, "Waheb in Suphah, and the streams of the Arnon, 15and the slope of the streams that goes down to Ar, and leans toward Moab's border."

16From there they went to Beer. That was the well where the LORD said to Moses, "Gather the people, and I will give them water."

17Then Israel sang this song: "Spring up, O well! Sing to it! 18The leaders dug it. The important men of the people dug it with their sticks and staffs." Then they went from the desert to Mattanah.

19From Mattanah they went to Nahaliel. From Nahaliel they went to Bamoth. 20From Bamoth they went to the valley in the field of Moab. It was at the top of Pisgah, looking over the empty land.

Israel Asks to Pass Through

21Israel sent messengers to Sihon, king of the Amorites. They said, 22"Let us pass through your land. We will not go into the fields or vineyards. We will not drink water from your wells. We will stay on the king's road until we pass through your land."

23But Sihon would not let Israel pass through his land. He gathered all his people and went out to fight Israel in the desert. He came to Jahaz and fought Israel. 24Israel beat him with the sword. They took his land from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River, up to the border of the Ammonites. The border of the Ammonites was too strong. 25Israel took all those cities. They lived in all the cities of the Amorites, including Heshbon and its towns. 26Heshbon was the city of Sihon, king of the Amorites. He had fought the first king of Moab and took all his land up to the Arnon.

27That is why the poets say, "Come to Heshbon! Let the city of Sihon be built and set up! 28For fire came out of Heshbon. A flame came from the city of Sihon. It burned up Ar of Moab and the lords of the hilltops of the Arnon. 29Oh, Moab! You are ruined! You people of Chemosh are lost! He gave his sons as runaways and his daughters as captives to Sihon, king of the Amorites. 30We have shot at them. Heshbon is gone to Dibon. We laid waste to Nophah, which reaches to Medeba."

31So Israel lived in the land of the Amorites.

Moses Sends Men to Jazer

32Moses sent men to spy out Jazer. They captured its villages and took the land from the Amorites there. 33Then they turned and went up the road to Bashan. Og, king of Bashan, came out to fight them. He and all his people came to Edrei to battle. 34The LORD said to Moses, "Do not be afraid of him. I have given him, his people, and his land into your hand. Do to him what you did to Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon." 35They struck him, his sons, and all his people. They left no one alive. Then they took his land.
