Cyrus Lets God's People Go Home

1In the first year that Cyrus was king of Persia, the LORD did what HeheHe promised through Jeremiah. The LORD stirred up Cyrus, the king of Persia. Cyrus told everyone in his land and wrote it down too. He said:

2"This is what Cyrus, king of Persia, says: The LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, has given me all the lands of the earth. He has told me to build a house for HimhimHim in Jerusalem, in Judah.

3If any of you belong to HishisHis people, may his God be with him. Go up to Jerusalem in Judah and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

4People who live near those who are left should help them. Give them silver, gold, things, and animals. They should also give gifts for God's house in Jerusalem."

The People Get Ready to Go

5So the family leaders of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and Levites, got ready. God had stirred their hearts to go up and build the LORD's house in Jerusalem.

6All the people around them helped too. They gave silver and gold things, other things, animals, and nice gifts. They also gave extra gifts.

The Temple Things Are Returned

7King Cyrus brought out the things from the LORD's house. Nebuchadnezzar had taken them from Jerusalem and put them in his god's house.

8Cyrus had Mithredath, the money keeper, bring them out. He counted them and gave them to Sheshbazzar, the leader of Judah.

9There were 30 gold dishes, 1,000 silver dishes, and 29 silver tools. 10There were 30 gold bowls, 410 silver bowls, and 1,000 other things. 11In all, there were 5,400 gold and silver things. Sheshbazzar brought all of them when the people came back from Babylon to Jerusalem.
