The Ark of the Covenant

1Bezalel made the ark from acacia wood. It was two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high. 2He covered it with pure gold inside and out. He made a gold trim around it. 3He made four gold rings. He put them on its four feet. He put two rings on one side and two rings on the other side. 4He made poles from acacia wood. He covered them with gold. 5He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark. The poles were used to carry the ark.

6He made the cover from pure gold. It was two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 7He made two cherubim from hammered gold. He put them at the ends of the cover. 8He made one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end. The cherubim were made as one piece with the cover. 9The cherubim had wings spread upward. Their wings covered the top. Their faces looked at each other. They looked down toward the cover.

The Table

10He made the table from acacia wood. It was two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high. 11He covered it with pure gold. He made a gold trim around it. 12He made a frame around it one hand wide. He made a gold trim around the frame.

13He made four gold rings. He put them on the four corners by its four legs. 14The rings were near the frame. They held the poles for carrying the table. 15He made poles from acacia wood. He covered them with gold. The poles were used to carry the table. 16He made plates, dishes, bowls, and pitchers from pure gold. They were used on the table. Some were for pouring drink offerings.

The Lampstand

17He made the lampstand from pure gold. It was hammered work. The base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers were all one piece. 18Six branches came out from its sides. Three branches were on one side, and three were on the other side. 19Each branch had three cups shaped like almond flowers. Each cup had a bud and a flower. This was true for all six branches.

20The shaft had four cups shaped like almond flowers. They had buds and flowers. 21A bud was under the first two branches. A bud was under the next two branches. A bud was under the last two branches. 22The buds and branches were all one piece with the lampstand. It was hammered pure gold. 23He made seven lamps. He also made wick trimmers and trays from pure gold. 24He used one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its parts.

The Incense Altar

25He made the altar of incense from acacia wood. It was square. It was one cubit long, one cubit wide, and two cubits high. The horns were made as one piece with it. 26He covered the top, sides, and horns with pure gold. He made a gold trim around it. 27He made two gold rings below the trim. They were on two opposite sides. They held the poles. 28He made poles from acacia wood. He covered them with gold.

The Holy Oil and Incense

29He made the holy anointing oil. He made pure incense with sweet spices. A perfume maker made them.
