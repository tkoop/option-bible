Elisha Sends a Prophet to Make Jehu King

1The prophet Elisha called one of the younger prophets. He said, "Tie up your clothes and take this little jar of oil in your hand. Go to Ramoth-gilead. 2When you get there, look for Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Bring him away from his friends. Take him to a private room. 3Pour the oil on his head. Say, 'ThetheThe LORD says, "I have chosen you to be king of Israel."' Then open the door and run. Do not wait."

4The young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead.

He found the army leaders sitting together.

"Captain, I have a message for you."

Jehu asked, "For which one of us?"

5The prophet said, "For you, captain." 6Jehu stood up and went inside. The prophet poured oil on his head. He said, "ThetheThe LORD, the God of Israel, says, 'I have chosen you to be king over the LORD's people, over Israel.'"

7ThetheThe prophet said, "YouyouYou must destroy the family of Ahab, your king. I will punish them for killing MymyMy prophets and all the LORD's servants. Jezebel is to blame. 8Every man and boy in Ahab's family will die. I will destroy every one of them, slave or free, in Israel. 9I will make Ahab's family like the families of Jeroboam son of Nebat and Baasha son of Ahijah. 10Dogs will eat Jezebel in the field at Jezreel. No one will bury her." Then he opened the door and ran away.

The Soldiers Make Jehu King

11Jehu went out to his lord's servants. They asked, "IsisIs everything all right? Why did this crazy man come to you?"

Jehu said, "YouyouYou know the man and his talk."

They said, "That is not true! Tell us."

12Jehu said, "HeheHe said this to me: 'ThetheThe LORD says, "I have chosen you to be king of Israel."'"

13They hurried. Each man took off his coat and put it under Jehu on the steps. They blew a trumpet and shouted, "Jehu is king!"

Jehu Plans to Meet King Joram

14Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, had joined together against Joram. Joram and all Israel had been guarding Ramoth-gilead from Hazael, king of Aram. 15King Joram had gone back to Jezreel to get well from wounds the army of Aram gave him while fighting Hazael, king of Aram. Jehu said, "If you are with me, do not let anyone leave the city to carry news to Jezreel." 16Jehu rode to Jezreel, because Joram was there. Ahaziah, king of Judah, had come down to see Joram.

17The watchman on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu's men coming. He said, "I see a group coming."

Joram said, "Send a horseman to meet them. Ask if they come in peace."

18A horseman rode out and said, "ThetheThe king asks, 'Do you come in peace?'"

Jehu said, "This is not your business. Fall in behind me."

The watchman said, "ThetheThe messenger reached them, but he is not coming back."

19Joram sent a second horseman. He came to them and said the same thing.

Jehu answered, "Fall in behind me."

20The watchman said, "HeheHe reached them too, but he is not coming back. The way the chariot is driven looks like Jehu, son of Nimshi. He drives like a crazy man."

Jehu Kills King Joram of Israel

21Joram said, "Get ready." His chariot was made ready. Joram, king of Israel, and Ahaziah, king of Judah, each rode out in his chariot to meet Jehu. They met him at the field of Naboth the Jezreelite.

22When Joram saw Jehu, he asked, "Do you come in peace, Jehu?"

Jehu answered, "How can there be peace while your mother Jezebel keeps sinning and using evil magic?"

Joram turned his chariot to run. He shouted to Ahaziah, "It's a trap, Ahaziah!"

2324Jehu pulled his bow as hard as he could and shot Joram between his shoulders. The arrow went out through his heart. Joram sank down in his chariot.

25Jehu said to Bidkar, his officer, "Pick him up and throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember when you and I rode together behind Ahab, his father. 26The LORD said this against him: 'I saw the blood of Naboth and his sons yesterday,' says the LORD. 'I will pay you back in this same field,' says the LORD. Now throw him into the field, just as the LORD said."

Jehu Kills King Ahaziah of Judah

27Ahaziah, king of Judah, saw what happened. He ran toward Beth-haggan. Jehu chased him and said, "Shoot him too!" They shot him in his chariot on the road up to Gur near Ibleam. He got away to Megiddo and died there.

28His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem. They buried him with his fathers in the city of David. 29Ahaziah became king of Judah in the eleventh year of Joram, son of Ahab.

Jezebel Dies

30Jehu came to Jezreel. Jezebel heard about it. She painted her eyes, fixed her hair, and looked out the window.

31When Jehu came through the gate, she said, "Do you come in peace, Zimri, killer of his master?"

32He looked up at the window and said, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three servants looked out at him. 33He said, "Throw her down!" They threw her down. Her blood splashed on the wall and on the horses. The horses ran over her.

34He went in, ate, and drank. Then he said, "Take care of this cursed woman and bury her, for she was a king's daughter."

35They went to bury her, but they found only her skull, her feet, and her hands.

36They came back and told him. He said, "This is the word of the LORD spoken by Elijah the Tishbite: In the plot of Jezreel dogs will eat Jezebel's flesh. 37Jezebel's body will be like waste on the field in the plot of Jezreel, so no one can say, 'This is Jezebel.'"
