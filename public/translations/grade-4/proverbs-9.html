Wisdom Builds Her House

1Wisdom built her house. She cut seven pillars. 2She prepared her meat. She mixed her wine. She also set her table. 3She sent out her helpers. They called from the high places in the town.

Wisdom Invites Everyone

4She said, "Whoever is simple, come in here!" She said this to the one who needs understanding. 5"Come and eat my bread. Drink the wine I mixed. 6Leave your simple ways and live. Walk in the way of understanding."

Wisdom Gives Advice

7A person who corrects a mocker gets insulted. A person who rebukes a wicked man gets hurt. 8Do not rebuke a mocker, or he will hate you. Rebuke a wise man, and he will love you. 9Teach a wise man, and he will be even wiser. Teach a good man, and he will learn more.

The Start of Wisdom

10Respecting the LORD is the start of wisdom. Knowing the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne gives understanding. 11Through wisdom, your days will be many. Years will be added to your life. 12If you are wise, your wisdom will reward you. If you are a mocker, you alone will suffer.

Foolishness Tries to Trick People

13Foolishness is loud. She tricks people and knows nothing. 14She sits at her door, on a seat high in the town. 15She calls to people passing by, who walk straight on their way.

16She said, "Whoever is simple, come in here!" She said this to the one who lacks sense. 17"Stolen water tastes sweet. Bread eaten in secret is nice." 18But they do not know that dead people are there. Her guests are deep in the grave.
