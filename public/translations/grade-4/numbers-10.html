Silver Trumpets

1The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,

2"Make two silver trumpets for yourself. Hammer them from one piece of silver. They will help you call the people together and tell the camps to move. 3When both trumpets blow, all the people will meet with you at the door of the meeting tent. 4If only one trumpet blows, the leaders of Israel's thousands will meet with you. 5When you blow an alarm the first time, the camps on the east side will move. 6When you blow a second alarm, the camps on the south side will move. They will blow an alarm when it is time to move.

7"When you gather the people, blow the trumpets, but do not sound an alarm. 8Aaron's sons, the priests, will blow the trumpets. This rule will last for all your families, forever. 9When you go to war in your land against an enemy who hurts you, sound an alarm with the trumpets. Then the LORD your God will remember you, and HeheHe will save you from your enemies. 10Also, on your happy days, at your feasts, and at the start of your months, blow the trumpets over your burnt gifts and peace gifts. They will help the LORD remember you. I am the LORD your God."

The Cloud Lifts

11On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year, the cloud rose from the sacred tent. 12The people of Israel left the Sinai desert. They travelledtravelledtraveled in their groups. The cloud stopped in the Paran desert. 13They moved first because the LORD told them to through Moses.

The Camps Move Out

14The camp of Judah moved first, by their groups. Nahshon son of Amminadab led its army. 15Nethanel son of Zuar led the army of Issachar. 16Eliab son of Helon led the army of Zebulun. 17The sacred tent was taken down. The Gershonites and the Merarites carried it and moved.

18The camp of Reuben moved next, by their groups. Elizur son of Shedeur led its army. 19Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led the army of Simeon. 20Eliasaph son of Deuel led the army of Gad. 21The Kohathites carried the holy things. They set up the sacred tent before the others arrived.

22The camp of Ephraim moved next, by their groups. Elishama son of Ammihud led its army. 23Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led the army of Manasseh. 24Abidan son of Gideoni led the army of Benjamin.

25The camp of Dan moved last. It guarded all the other camps. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai led its army. 26Pagiel son of Ocran led the army of Asher. 27Ahira son of Enan led the army of Naphtali. 28These were the ways the Israelites moved by their groups.

Moses and Hobab

29Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, who was Moses' father-in-law, "We are moving to the place the LORD promised to give us. Come with us. We will be good to you, because the LORD has promised good things to Israel."

30Hobab said, "I will not go. I will go back to my own land and my family."

31Moses said, "Please don't leave us. You know where we should camp in the desert. You can show us where to go. 32If you come with us, we will share with you the good things the LORD gives us."

The Sacred Box Goes Ahead

33They travelledtravelledtraveled for three days from the mountain of the LORD. The box of the LORD's promise went ahead of them for three days to find a good place to rest. 34The LORD's cloud was over them by day when they left the camp.

35When the box moved, Moses said, "Rise up, the LORD! Let YouryourYour enemies scatter. Let those who hate YouyouYou run away from YouyouYou."

36When it rested, he said, "Come back, the LORD, to Israel's many thousands."
