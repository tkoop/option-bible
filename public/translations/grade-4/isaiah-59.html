The Lord Can Still Save

1The LORD is strong enough to save us. He can hear us.

Sin Keeps Us Apart from God

2But your sins have put a wall between you and your God. Your wrongs have made HimhimHim turn away, so HeheHe will not hear you.

People Do Wrong

3Your hands are stained with blood. Your fingers are stained with sin. Your lips tell lies. Your tongue speaks bad things.

No One Does Right

4No one asks for what is right. No one is fair and true. People trust in empty things and tell lies. They plan trouble and cause harm.

Bad Plans Do Not Help

5They hatch snake eggs and weave spider webs. If someone eats one of their eggs, he dies. If an egg is crushed, a snake comes out. 6Their webs will not become clothes. They cannot cover themselves with what they do. What they do is wrong. They use their hands to hurt people.

They Hurt Others

7Their feet run to do bad things. They hurry to kill innocent people. Their thoughts are full of wrong. They leave ruin and trouble behind them. 8They do not know how to live in peace. What they do is not fair. They make twisted paths. No one who walks on them finds peace.

We Are in the Dark

9So what is fair is far from us. Right living does not reach us. We wait for light, but we see only dark. We look for bright light, but we walk in deep dark. 10We feel along the wall like blind people. We feel around like we have no eyes. We trip at noon like it is night. We are like dead people in empty places.

11We all growl like bears. We moan like doves. We look for what is fair, but there is none. We look for God to save us, but HeheHe is far away.

We Admit Our Sins

12Our wrongs are many before you. Our sins speak against us. We know our wrongs. Our sins stay with us. 13We turned against the LORD and lied. We turned away from our God. We talk about hurting people and turning away. We plan lies in our hearts and speak them.

Nothing Is Fair

14So what is fair is pushed back. Right living stands far away. Truth has fallen in the street. What is right cannot get in. 15Truth is gone. Anyone who stops doing wrong gets robbed. The LORD saw this. It was bad in HishisHis eyes, because nothing was fair.

The Lord Steps In

16He saw there was no one to help. He was amazed that no one stepped in. So HishisHis own power saved HimhimHim. His right living held HimhimHim up. 17He put on right living like armourarmourarmor. He put a helmet of saving power on HishisHis head. He wore clothes of payback. He wrapped HimselfhimselfHimself in strong love for what is right.

He Will Pay Back His Enemies

18He will pay back each person for what they do. He will be angry with those who fight HimhimHim. He will pay back HishisHis enemies, even faraway lands. 19People in the west and east will fear the LORD's name. They will see HishisHis glory. When the enemy comes like a flood, the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD will stand against him.

The Lord Keeps His Promise
