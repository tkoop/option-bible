The LORD Speaks to Ezekiel

1The word of the LORD came to me.

2He said, "SonsonSon of man, speak against the prophets of Israel. They make up messages from their own hearts. Say to them to listen to the word of the LORD."

God Warns the False Prophets

3"This is what the Lord the LORD says: Trouble is coming for the foolish prophets. They follow their own thoughts and have seen nothing. 4O Israel, your prophets are like foxes in ruined places.

5"You did not fix the broken places. You did not build a wall for Israel's house to stand in battle on the day of the LORD. 6They see false visions and tell lies. They say the LORD sent them, but the LORD did not send them. Still they hope their words come true.

7"Did you not see a false vision? Did you not speak a lying message? You claimed the LORD said it, but I did not say it. 8So this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you spoke lies and saw false visions, I am against you, says the Lord the LORD.

9"My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and tell lies. They will not be in the meeting of MymyMy people. Their names will not be written in Israel's family list. They will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord the LORD.

10"This is because they led MymyMy people the wrong way. They said there was peace, but there was no peace. When someone builds a weak wall, they cover it with plaster. 11Tell the people who cover it with plaster that it will fall. Heavy rain will come. Great hailstones will fall. A strong wind will break it open. 12When the wall falls, people will ask you where the plaster is that you spread on it.

13"So this is what the Lord the LORD says: In MymyMy anger I will send a strong wind. In MymyMy anger I will send heavy rain and hailstones to destroy it. 14I will tear down the wall you covered with plaster. I will bring it to the ground. Its base will show. It will fall. You will be destroyed in it. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 15I will finish MymyMy anger against the wall and against the people who covered it with plaster. I will say to you that the wall is gone and the people who covered it are gone. 16These are the prophets of Israel. They spoke about Jerusalem. They saw visions of peace for her, but there was no peace, says the Lord the LORD."

God Warns the Women Who Use Magic

17He said, "SonsonSon of man, turn your attention to the women of your people. They prophesy from their own hearts. Speak against them."

18"This is what the Lord the LORD says: Trouble is coming for the women who sew magic charms on their wrists. They make veils for people of every size to catch souls. Will you catch the souls of MymyMy people and keep your own souls alive?

19"You dishonoureddishonoureddishonored MemeMe among MymyMy people for a few handfuls of barley and pieces of bread. You killed people who should live. You let people live who should die. You lied to MymyMy people, and they listened to lies. 20So I am against your magic charms. You use them to catch souls like birds. I will tear them from your arms. I will let the souls go free. They are the souls you hunt like birds.

21"I will tear off your veils. I will save MymyMy people from your hands. They will not be caught by you anymore. Then you will know that I am the LORD. 22You made good people sad with lies. I did not make them sad. You made bad people strong so they would not turn from their bad ways to save their lives.

23"So you will no longer see false visions. You will no longer tell fortunes. I will save MymyMy people from your hands. Then you will know that I am the LORD."
