Boaz Meets the Family Member at the Gate

1Boaz went up to the city gate and sat down. The family member Boaz had talked about walked by. Boaz said, "Come here and sit down, friend." The man came and sat down. 2Boaz asked ten town leaders to sit there too. They sat down.

Boaz Talks About the Land

3Boaz said to the family member, "Naomi came back from Moab. She is selling the field that belonged to our brother Elimelech. 4I wanted to tell you about it. Buy it here in front of the people and leaders. If you will buy it back, buy it. If you will not, tell me. I know no one else who can buy it, and I am the next one who can buy it." The man said, "I will buy it."

Boaz Tells About Ruth

5Boaz said, "When you buy the field from Naomi, you also get Ruth from Moab. She was the wife of the man who died. You must buy her so the dead man's name can stay on his land." 6The man said, "I cannot buy it for myself. It might hurt my own land. You buy it back. I cannot."

A Custom in Israel

7This is how people in Israel made a land deal sure. A man took off his shoe and gave it to the other man. This showed everyone the deal was true. 8So the family member said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself," and took off his shoe.

Boaz Buys the Land and Ruth

9Boaz said to the town leaders and all the people, "YouyouYou are witnesses today. I bought from Naomi everything that belonged to Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon. 10I also bought Ruth from Moab. She was Mahlon's wife. She will be my wife. This keeps the dead man's name on his land. His name will not be lost among his family or at the city gate. You are witnesses today."

The People Bless Boaz

11All the people and leaders said, "May the LORD make the woman coming into your house like Rachel and Leah. They built the family of Israel. Do well in Ephrathah. Be well known in Bethlehem. 12May your family be like the family of Perez. Tamar had him for Judah. May the LORD give you children through this young woman."

Ruth Has a Son

13Boaz married Ruth. He slept with her. The LORD gave her a baby, and she had a son.

The Women Praise the LORD

14The women said to Naomi, "Praise the LORD! He has not left you without a family helper today. May his name be well known in Israel. 15He will give you new life. He will take care of you when you are old. Your daughter-in-law loves you. She is better to you than seven sons. She has given you this baby."

16Naomi took the baby and held him in her arms. She took care of him.

17The women living nearby said, "Naomi has a son!" They named him Obed. He became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.

The Family Line of David

18This is the family line of Perez: Perez was the father of Hezron. 19Hezron was the father of Ram, and Ram was the father of Amminadab. 20Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon was the father of Salmon. 21Salmon was the father of Boaz, and Boaz was the father of Obed. 22Obed was the father of Jesse, and Jesse was the father of David.
