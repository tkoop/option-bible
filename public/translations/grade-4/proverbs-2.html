Listen to Wisdom

1My son, if you listen to my words and keep my commands with you, 2listen to wisdom and work hard to understand. 3Yes, if you ask for good sense and ask for understanding, 4and if you look for it like silver and search for it like hidden treasure,

God Gives Wisdom

5then you will understand deep respect for the LORD, and you will find out about God. 6For the LORD gives wisdom. From HishisHis mouth come knowledge and understanding. 7He keeps good things for honest people. He is a shield for people who live without blame. 8He guards the path of fair people. He protects the way of HishisHis loyal people. 9Then you will understand what is right and fair and good, every good way.

Wisdom Keeps People Safe

10Wisdom will come into your heart, and knowing things will make you happy. 11Good sense will protect you, and understanding will keep you safe. 12Wisdom will save you from the ways of bad men, from men who say twisted things.

13These men left the right path to walk in dark ways. 14They like to do wrong, and they are glad to do evil. 15Their paths are crooked, and they are tricky in their ways.

Stay Away from the Wrong Woman

16Wisdom will save you from the woman who is not true to her husband, from the smooth words of a stranger. 17She left the husband she married when young. She forgot the promise she made to her God. 18Her house leads to death, and her paths go to the place of the dead. 19No one who goes to her comes back, or finds the way to life.

Good People Stay in the Land

20So you will walk in the ways of good people and stay on the paths of people who do right. 21Honest people will live in the land, and people without blame will stay there. 22But bad people will be removed from the land, and people who are not loyal will be pulled out of it.
