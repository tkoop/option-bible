People Move to Jerusalem

1The leaders lived in Jerusalem. The rest of the people drew lots. One out of every ten would live in Jerusalem, the holy city. The other nine stayed in their towns. 2The people blessed all the men who chose to live in Jerusalem.

Leaders Who Lived in Jerusalem

3These are the heads of the areas who lived in Jerusalem. In the towns of Judah, each person lived on his own land. This included the people of Israel, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants, and the sons of Solomon's servants. 4Some of the sons of Judah and Benjamin lived in Jerusalem. From Judah's sons: Athaiah son of Uzziah, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel, from Perez's family.

5Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, from Shelah's family. 6The sons of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were 468 brave men.

7From Benjamin's sons: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah. 8After him were Gabbai and Sallai. There were 928 men. 9Joel son of Zichri was their leader. Judah son of Hassenuah was in charge of the second part of the city.

Priests in Jerusalem

10From the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib; Jachin. 11Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub. He was the leader of the house of God. 12Their helpers who worked in the temple were 822 men. Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah was also there. 13His helpers, who were family leaders, were 242 men. Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer was also there. 14Their helpers were 128 brave men. Zabdiel son of Haggedolim was their leader.

Levites in Jerusalem

15From the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni. 16Shabbethai and Jozabad were two leaders of the Levites. They were in charge of the outside work for the house of God. 17Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph led the thanks and prayer. Bakbukiah was second among his helpers. Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun was also there. 18The Levites in the holy city were 284. 19The gatekeepers were Akkub, Talmon, and their helpers. They guarded the gates. There were 172 men.

People in Other Towns

20The rest of the Israelites, with the priests and Levites, lived in all the towns of Judah. Each lived on his own land. 21The temple servants lived on the hill of Ophel. Ziha and Gishpa were in charge of them. 22The leader of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Mica. He was from Asaph's family, the singers who served in the house of God. 23The king had given a command about them. The singers got food each day. 24Pethahiah son of Meshezabel was the king's helper. He was from Zerah's family, from Judah. He spoke for the people in all matters.

25Some people of Judah lived in villages with their fields. They lived in Kiriath-arba and its villages, Dibon and its villages, Jekabzeel and its villages, 26Jeshua, Moladah, Beth-pelet, 27Hazar-shual, Beersheba and its villages, 28Ziklag, Meconah and its villages, 29En-rimmon, Zorah, Jarmuth, 30Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its villages. They settled from Beersheba to the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom.

31The sons of Benjamin lived from Geba to Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its villages, 32Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah, 33Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim, 34Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat, 35Lod, Ono, and the ValleyvalleyValley of Craftsmen. 36Some Levites from Judah settled in Benjamin.
