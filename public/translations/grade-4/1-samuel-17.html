The Philistines and Israel Get Ready to Fight

1The Philistine people came together for war. They set up camp at Socoh in Judah. They camped between Socoh and Azekah at Ephes-dammim. 2Saul and the men of Israel came together too. They set up camp in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah. They got ready to fight the Philistines. 3The Philistines stood on one hill. Israel stood on the other hill. The valley was between them.

Goliath Challenges Israel

4A fighter named Goliath came out of the Philistine camp. He was from Gath. He was more than nine feet tall. 5He wore a bronze helmet on his head. He wore bronze metal clothes. His metal clothes weighed about 125 pounds. 6He wore bronze covers on his legs. He had a bronze small spear between his shoulders. 7His spear was like a big weaving stick. Its iron point weighed about 15 pounds. A man carried his shield in front of him.

8Goliath stood and shouted to the Israel army lines. He said, "Why do you come out to get ready for battle? I am a Philistine. You are Saul's servants. Pick one man and let him come down to me. 9If he can fight me and kill me, we will be your servants. But if I beat him and kill him, you will be our servants and serve us." 10Then the Philistine said, "I challenge the army lines of Israel today. Give me one man, and we will fight." 11Saul and all Israel heard these words. They were very scared.

David Goes to the Battle

12David was the son of Jesse. Jesse was from Bethlehem in Judah. He had eight sons. In Saul's time, Jesse was very old. 13His three oldest sons had gone with Saul to the war. The firstborn was Eliab. The second was Abinadab. The third was Shammah. 14David was the youngest. The three oldest had gone with Saul. 15David went back and forth from Saul to take care of his father's sheep at Bethlehem.

16The Philistine came near every morning and evening for forty days. He stood and shouted. 17Jesse said to David, "Take a big bag of roasted grain and ten loaves of bread for your brothers. Carry them quickly to the camp. 18Take ten cheeses to the army leader. See how your brothers are doing. Bring back news from them." 19Saul and all the men of Israel were in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah. They were fighting the Philistines.

20David got up early in the morning. He left the sheep with a keeper. He took the food and went, just as Jesse had told him. He came to the camp as the army was going out to fight. They shouted the war cry. 21Israel and the Philistines got their armies ready. They faced each other. 22David left his things with the man who kept the things. He ran to the army lines. He said hello to his brothers. 23While he was talking with them, the fighter Goliath came up from the Philistine lines. He said the same things again. David heard him.

David Hears About Goliath

24All the men of Israel saw the man. They ran away from him. They were very afraid. 25The men of Israel said, "Have you seen this man? He comes up to challenge Israel. The king will give a lot of money to the man who kills him. He will give his daughter to that man. He will also make that man's father's house free from taxes in Israel."

26David asked the men standing near him, "What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and takes away the shame from Israel? Who is this man who is not one of God's people? Why does he challenge the armies of the living God?"

27The people answered him the same way. They said, "This is what will be done for the man who kills him."

David's Brother Gets Angry

28Eliab, David's oldest brother, heard David talking to the men. Eliab was angry with David. He said, "Why did you come down here? With whom did you leave those few sheep in the desert? I know your pride and your mean heart. You came down just to see the battle."

29David said, "What did I do now? I only asked a question."

30He turned away from Eliab to another man. He asked the same thing. The people gave him the same answer. 31People heard the words David said. They told Saul. Saul sent for David.

David Talks to King Saul

32David said to Saul, "Do not let anyone lose heart because of this Philistine. Your servant will go and fight him."

33Saul said to David, "YouyouYou cannot go against this Philistine to fight him. You are young. He has been a fighter since he was young."

34David said to Saul, "YouryourYour servant used to keep his father's sheep. When a lion or a bear came and took a sheep from the flock, 35I went after it. I hit it and saved the sheep from its mouth. If it turned on me, I grabbed its hair and hit it and killed it. 36Your servant has killed both a lion and a bear. This man who is not one of God's people will be like one of them. He challenged the armies of the living God."

37David said, "ThetheThe LORD who saved me from the lion's claw and the bear's claw will save me from this Philistine."

Saul said to David, "Go, and may the LORD be with you."

David Gets Ready to Fight

38Saul dressed David in his own clothes. He put a bronze helmet on his head. He dressed him in armourarmourarmor.

39David put his sword on over the armourarmourarmor. He tried to walk, but he could not. He was not used to them. David said to Saul, "I cannot go in these. I have not tried them before." So David took them off.

40He took his stick in his hand. He chose five smooth stones from the stream. He put them in his bag. He had his sling in his hand. He went near the Philistine.

David and Goliath Talk

41The Philistine came closer to David. The man with the shield went in front of him. 42When the Philistine saw David, he looked down on him. David was young. He had reddish skin and was good-looking.

43The Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with a stick?" The Philistine cursed David by his gods. 44The Philistine said to David, "Come to me. I will give your flesh to the birds of the sky and to the animals of the field."

45David said to the Philistine, "YouyouYou come to me with a sword, a spear, and a small spear. I come to you in the name of the LORD of Armies. He is the God of the army lines of Israel. You challenged HimhimHim. 46Today the LORD will hand you over to me. I will hit you and take your head from you. I will give the dead bodies of the Philistines to the birds of the sky and to the animals of the earth. Then all the earth will know that Israel has a God. 47All the people here will know that the LORD does not save with a sword or a spear. The battle is the LORD's. He will give you into our hands."

David Kills Goliath

48When the Philistine got up and came to meet David, David ran fast toward the battle line to meet the Philistine. 49David put his hand into his bag. He took a stone from it. He slung it. He hit the Philistine on the forehead. The stone went into his forehead. The Philistine fell on his face to the ground. 50David beat the Philistine with a sling and a stone. David had no sword. He hit the Philistine and killed him. 51David ran and stood over the Philistine. He took the Philistine's sword. He pulled it out of its cover. He killed the Philistine with it. He cut off his head. When the Philistines saw that their best fighter was dead, they ran away.

Israel Wins the Battle

52The men of Israel and Judah got up. They shouted and chased the Philistines. They chased them all the way to the gate of Ekron. The dead Philistines fell along the road to Shaaraim, all the way to Gath and Ekron. 53The Israelites came back from chasing the Philistines. They took things from the Philistine camp. 54David took the head of the Philistine. He brought it to Jerusalem. He put the Philistine's armourarmourarmor in his tent.

Saul Asks About David

55When Saul saw David going out to meet the Philistine, he said to Abner, the army leader, "Abner, whose son is this young man?"

Abner said, "As surely as you live, O king, I do not know."

56The king said, "Find out whose son this young man is."

57When David came back from killing the Philistine, Abner brought him to Saul. David still had the Philistine's head in his hand.

58Saul said to him, "Young man, whose son are you?"

David said, "I am the son of your servant Jesse of Bethlehem."
