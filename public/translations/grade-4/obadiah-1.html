Obadiah's Vision About Edom

1This is what Obadiah saw. My Lord, the LORD, says this about Edom:

"We heard news from the LORD. He sent a messenger to the nations. The messenger said to get up and fight Edom.

2"I will make you small among the nations. You will be hated a lot. 3Your pride tricked you. You live in high rock caves. You say in your heart, 'Who can bring me down?' 4Even if you fly like an eagle and make your nest among the stars, I will bring you down," says the LORD.

5"If thieves came to you, they would steal only what they want. If grape pickers came to you, they would leave some grapes. But you will be ruined badly. 6Esau's hidden things will be found and taken. 7Your friends will push you out of your land. Your peaceful friends will trick you and beat you. People who eat your bread will set a trap. You won't know.

8"I will destroy the wise men of Edom and the smart men of Esau's mountains that day," says the LORD.

9"Your fighters, Teman, will be scared. Everyone from Esau's mountains will be killed. 10Because you hurt your brother Jacob, shame will cover you. You will be cut off forever. 11You stood by when strangers took his stuff and foreigners entered his gates and drew lots for Jerusalem. You were like one of them.

12"Don't be glad in your brother's bad day. Don't be happy when Judah's people are destroyed. Don't brag in their hard time. 13Don't enter MymyMy people's gate in their bad day. Don't be glad about their trouble. Don't take their stuff in their bad day. 14Don't stand where roads meet to kill their running people. Don't hand over their people who got away in their hard time.

The Day of the LORD Is Near

15"The DaydayDay of the LORD is near for all nations. What you did will be done to you. Your pay will come back on your head. 16You drank on MymyMy holy mountain. So all nations will drink and drink. They will drink and choke. They will be like they never were.

Israel Will Be Saved

17"But on Mount Zion, some will be saved. It will be holy. Jacob's family will get their land back. 18Jacob's family will be like fire. Joseph's family will be like a flame. Esau's family will be like dry grass. They will burn it up. No one will be left of Esau's family." For the LORD has spoken.

Israel Will Get Its Land Back

19People from the south will take Esau's mountains. People from the lowlands will take the Philistines' land. They will take Ephraim's and Samaria's fields. Benjamin will take Gilead. 20Israel's people who were taken away will take the land of the Canaanites up to Zarephath. Jerusalem's people who were taken to Sepharad will take the south cities. 21Helpers will go up Mount Zion to rule Esau's mountains. The kingdom will belong to the LORD.
