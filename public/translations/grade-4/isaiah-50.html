The LORD Speaks to His People

1This is what the LORD says: "Where is the divorce paper for your mother? I did not send her away without a reason. Or did I sell you to pay a debt? No. You were sold because of your sins. Your mother was sent away because of your wrongs. 2Why was no one here when I came? Why did no one answer when I called? Am I not strong enough to save you? Do I have no power to help? Look, I can dry up the sea with a word. I can turn rivers into dry land. The fish die and rot because there is no water. 3I can cover the sky with darkness. I can put black cloth over it."

The Servant Speaks

4The Lord the LORD gave MemeMe words to teach. He taught MemeMe how to help tired people with a word. Each morning HeheHe wakes MemeMe. He opens MymyMy ear to listen like a student. 5The Lord the LORD opened MymyMy ear. I did not say no. I did not turn back. 6I let people hit MymyMy back. I let them pull hair from MymyMy face. I did not hide MymyMy face from shame and spit. 7But the Lord the LORD helps MemeMe. So I am not ashamed. So I set MymyMy face like a hard rock. I know I will not be put to shame. 8The OneoneOne who says I am right is near. Who can argue with MemeMe? Let us stand together. Who can take MemeMe to court? Let him come near. 9Look, the Lord the LORD helps MemeMe. Who can say I am guilty? Look, all MymyMy enemies will wear out like old clothes. Moths will eat them.

A Warning and a Call

10Who among you fears the LORD and listens to HishisHis servant? If you walk in the dark with no light, trust in the name of the LORD. Lean on your God. 11Look, all of you start fires and light torches. Walk in the light of your fire. Walk by the torches you made. But this is what you get from MemeMe: you will lie down in pain.
