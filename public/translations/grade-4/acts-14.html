Paul and Barnabas in Iconium

1In Iconium, Paul and Barnabas went into the Jewish meeting place. They spoke so well that many Jews and Greeks believed. 2But some Jews did not believe. They got the other people angry. They made trouble for the believers. 3Paul and Barnabas stayed there a long time. They spoke boldly about the Lord. The Lord showed that HishisHis grace was true. He let them do signs and wonders. 4The people in the city were split. Some stood with the Jews. Some stood with the apostles. 5Then the other people and the Jews, with their leaders, tried to hurt Paul and Barnabas. They wanted to throw stones at them. 6Paul and Barnabas found out. They ran away to the cities of Lystra and Derbe in Lycaonia. They also went to the area around them. 7There they told the good news.

A Man Is Healed in Lystra

8In Lystra, a man sat there. He could not use his feet. He had been crippled since he was born. He had never walked. 9He listened to Paul speak. Paul looked at him. Paul saw that the man had faith to be healed. 10Paul said in a loud voice, "Stand up on your feet!" The man jumped up and walked.

The Crowd Thinks They Are Gods

11The crowd saw what Paul did. They shouted in their own language, "ThetheThe gods have come down to us like men!" 12They called Barnabas Zeus. They called Paul Hermes. This was because Paul was the main speaker. 13The priest of Zeus had a temple just outside the city. He brought bulls and flower crowns to the city gates. He wanted to offer a sacrifice with the crowd.

Paul and Barnabas Say They Are Only Men

14When Barnabas and Paul heard this, they tore their clothes. They ran into the crowd and shouted. 15"Men, why are you doing this? We are men just like you. We bring you good news. Turn away from these useless things. Turn to the living God. He made the sky, the land, the sea, and everything in them. 16In the past, HeheHe let all nations go their own way. 17But HeheHe did not leave HimselfhimselfHimself without proof. He does good. He sends rain from the sky. He gives good times for crops. He fills your hearts with food and joy." 18Even with these words, they could barely stop the crowd from offering sacrifices to them.

Paul Is Stoned

19Then some Jews came from Antioch and Iconium. They won the crowd over. They stoned Paul. They dragged him out of the city. They thought he was dead. 20The believers gathered around him. He got up and went back into the city. The next day, he left with Barnabas for Derbe.

They Go Back to Help the Churches

21They told the good news in that city. They made many disciples. Then they went back to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch. 22They made the believers strong. They told them to stay true to the faith. They said, "We must go through many hard times to enter God's kingdom." 23In each church, they chose leaders. They prayed and did not eat for a time. Then they gave the leaders to the Lord. They trusted HimhimHim.

They Return to Antioch

24They travelledtravelledtraveled through Pisidia. Then they came to Pamphylia. 25They spoke God's word in Perga. Then they went down to Attalia. 26From there, they sailed to Antioch. That was the city where believers had sent them out with God's help. They had finished the work. 27When they arrived, they gathered the church. They told all that God had done with them. They said God had opened a door of faith to the non-Jewish people. 28They stayed there a long time with the believers.
