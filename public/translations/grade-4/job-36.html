Elihu Keeps Speaking

1Elihu also kept speaking. He said:

"2Wait a little. I will show you. I still have words to say for God. 3I will bring my knowledge from far away. I will say my MakermakerMaker is right. 4My words are true. A person with true knowledge is with you.

God Is Strong and Fair

"5God is very strong. He does not reject people. He is strong in power and wisdom. 6He does not let evil people live on. He gives fair help to poor people. 7He always watches over good people. He puts them with kings on thrones. He lifts them up forever.

God Uses Trouble to Teach

"8If people are tied up in chains, caught by trouble, 9HeheHe tells them what they did. He shows them their sins, because they were proud. 10He helps them listen to correction. He tells them to stop doing wrong. 11If they listen and serve HimhimHim, they live good days. Their years are happy. 12But if they do not listen, they die by the sword. They die without understanding.

Wicked People Do Not Ask for Help

"13People who pretend to be good store up anger. They do not ask for help when God ties them up. 14They die while young. Their life ends among bad people. 15He saves poor people through their trouble. He helps them listen when life is hard.

A Warning for Job

"16He would lead you out of trouble to a wide, calm place. Your table would be full of good food. 17But you have received the punishment meant for wicked people. Punishment and justice have caught you. 18God is angry. Be careful, or HeheHe may take you away with one blow. No big payment can save you. 19Will your cry for help save you from trouble? Will all your strength save you? 20Do not wish for the night, when people are taken away from their homes. 21Be careful. Do not turn to sin. You have chosen this instead of trouble.

No One Is Like God

"22God is great in power. Who can teach like HimhimHim? 23Who told HimhimHim what to do? Who can say HeheHe did wrong? 24Remember to praise HishisHis work. People see it. 25Everyone can see it. People look at it from far away. 26God is great. We do not fully know HimhimHim. His years cannot be counted.

God Controls the Weather

"27He makes water drops small. Rain pours down from the mist. 28The clouds pour it out. Rain falls hard on people. 29Who can understand the clouds? Who can understand HishisHis thunder? 30He spreads HishisHis light on it. He covers the bottom of the sea. 31By these things HeheHe judges people. He gives plenty of food. 32He covers HishisHis hands with lightning. He tells it where to strike. 33His thunder shows HishisHis power. Even animals know a storm is coming."
