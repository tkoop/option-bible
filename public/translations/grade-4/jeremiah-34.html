God's Message to Zedekiah

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD. It came when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, all his army, all the kingdoms he ruled, and all the people were fighting against Jerusalem and its cities.

2The LORD, the God of Israel, says, "Go and tell Zedekiah king of Judah that I will give this city to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it down. 3You will not get away from him. You will be caught and given to him. You will see the king of Babylon in person, and you will talk with him. Then you will go to Babylon. 4But listen to the LORD's word, Zedekiah king of Judah. The LORD says about you, 'YouyouYou will not die by the sword. 5You will die in peace. People will burn spices for you, like they did for your fathers, the kings before you. They will mourn for you and call you their king. For I have said this, says the LORD.'"

6Jeremiah the prophet told all this to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem. 7At that time, the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and the other cities left in Judah, Lachish and Azekah. These were the only strong cities left in Judah.

The People Promise to Free Their Slaves

8After King Zedekiah made a promise with all the people in Jerusalem to set slaves free, 9each person was to set free his Hebrew slave, man or woman. No one was to make a fellow Jew work as a slave. 10All the leaders and all the people who joined the promise agreed to set free their male and female slaves. They obeyed and let them go. They did not make them work as slaves anymore. 11But later they changed their minds. They took back the men and women they had set free and made them slaves again.

The LORD Warns About Broken Promises

12Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah again.

13The LORD, the God of Israel, says, "I made a promise with your fathers when I brought them out of Egypt, out of the land where they were slaves. 14I said, 'At the end of seven years, you must set free a Hebrew brother who sold himself to you. He will work for you six years, and then you must set him free.' But your fathers did not listen to MemeMe. They did not pay attention. 15Recently you turned back and did what is right in MymyMy eyes. You set your neighbors free, and you made a promise before MemeMe in the place that has MymyMy name. 16But now you have turned back. You have dishonoureddishonoureddishonored MymyMy name. Each of you took back your male and female slaves after you set them free. You forced them to be slaves again. 17So the LORD says, 'YouyouYou did not obey MemeMe. You did not set your brothers and neighbors free. So now I set you free, says the LORD. I will let war, disease, and hunger come to you. I will make you a terrible thing to all the kingdoms of the earth.'"

18I will treat people who broke MymyMy promise like the calf they cut in two and walked between. They did not keep the words of the promise they made before MemeMe. 19The leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, the court officials, the priests, and all the people who walked between the pieces of the calf, 20I will give them to their enemies who want to kill them. Their dead bodies will be food for birds and animals.

21I will give Zedekiah king of Judah and his leaders to their enemies who want to kill them, and to the Babylonian army that has pulled back from you. 22I am going to give the command, says the LORD. I will bring them back to this city. They will fight against it, take it, and burn it down. I will make the cities of Judah empty, with no one living in them.
