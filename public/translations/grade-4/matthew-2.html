Wise Men Come to See Jesus

1Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea. This was when King Herod ruled. Wise men came from the east to Jerusalem. 2They asked, “Where is the new KingkingKing of the Jews? We saw HishisHis star in the east. We came to worship HimhimHim.” 3King Herod heard this. He was upset. All Jerusalem was upset too. 4He called the chief priests and teachers of the law. He asked them where the Christ would be born.

5They said, “In Bethlehem in Judea. This is what the prophet wrote: 6‘You, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are not the smallest among Judah’s leaders. A ruler will come from you. He will take care of MymyMy people Israel.’”

7Then Herod called the wise men in secret. He asked them exactly when the star appeared. 8He sent them to Bethlehem. He said, “Go and look carefully for the child. When you find HimhimHim, tell me. Then I can come and worship HimhimHim too.”

9The wise men heard the king. They went. The star they had seen in the east went ahead of them. It stopped over the place where the child was. 10They were very happy when they saw the star. 11They went into the house. They saw the child with HishisHis mother Mary. They bowed down and worshiped HimhimHim. They opened their gifts. They gave HimhimHim gold, incense, and myrrh. 12God warned them in a dream not to go back to Herod. So they went home another way.

Joseph and Mary Flee to Egypt

13After they left, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. He said, “Get up. Take the child and HishisHis mother. Run away to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you. Herod is going to look for the child to kill HimhimHim.” 14Joseph got up. He took the child and HishisHis mother at night. They went to Egypt.

15They stayed there until Herod died. This happened to make true what the Lord said through the prophet: “I called MymyMy SonsonSon out of Egypt.”

Herod Kills the Baby Boys

16Herod saw that the wise men had tricked him. He was very angry. He sent soldiers. They killed all the boys in Bethlehem and nearby. The boys were two years old or younger. This matched the time the wise men told him. 17Then the words of the prophet Jeremiah came true. 18“People heard a voice in Ramah. There was crying and loud sadness. Rachel cried for her children. She would not be comforted, because they were gone.”

Joseph Brings Jesus Back

19After Herod died, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream in Egypt. 20The angel said, “Get up. Take the child and HishisHis mother. Go to the land of Israel. The people who wanted to kill the child are dead.”

21Joseph got up. He took the child and HishisHis mother. They went to Israel. 22But he heard that Archelaus was king in Judea. Archelaus took his father Herod’s place. Joseph was afraid to go there. God warned him in a dream. So he went to Galilee. 23He came and lived in a town called Nazareth. This made true what the prophets said: “He will be called a Nazarene.”
