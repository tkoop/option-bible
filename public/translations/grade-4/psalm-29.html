Give Glory to the LORD

1A song of David. Give to the LORD, you mighty beings. Give to the LORD glory and strength. 2Give to the LORD the glory HishisHis name deserves. Worship the LORD in HishisHis holy beauty.

3The LORD's voice is over the waters. The God of glory thunders. The LORD is over many waters. 4The LORD's voice is strong. The LORD's voice is grand.

5The LORD's voice breaks the cedar trees. Yes, the LORD breaks the cedar trees of Lebanon. 6He makes Lebanon jump like a calf. He makes Sirion jump like a young wild ox.

7The LORD's voice flashes fire. 8The LORD's voice shakes the desert. The LORD shakes the desert of Kadesh.

9The LORD's voice twists the oak trees. It strips the woods bare. In HishisHis temple all cry, "GlorygloryGlory!"

10The LORD sits as KingkingKing over the flood. The LORD sits as KingkingKing forever. 11The LORD gives strength to HishisHis people. The LORD blesses HishisHis people with peace.
