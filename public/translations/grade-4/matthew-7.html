Do Not Judge Others

1Do not judge, or you will be judged. 2The way you judge others is the way you will be judged. The way you measure others is the way you will be measured.

3Why do you look at the tiny speck in your brother's eye? You do not see the big board in your own eye. 4How can you say to your brother, "Let me take the speck out of your eye," when you have a big board in your own eye? 5You hypocrite! First take the big board out of your own eye. Then you will see clearly to take the speck out of your brother's eye.

6Do not give holy things to dogs. Do not throw your pearls to pigs. They may step on them, and then turn and hurt you.
