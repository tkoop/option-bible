God Speaks Against Egypt

1In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day, the word of the LORD came to me.

2“Son of man, turn your face toward Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Speak against him and against all Egypt.

3“Speak and tell him, ‘This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I am against you, Pharaoh, king of Egypt. You are like a big monster lying in your rivers. You said the Nile is yours and that you made it.’

4“But I will put hooks in your mouth. The fish in your rivers will stick to your scales. I will pull you out of your rivers, and all the fish that stick to your scales will come out too.

5“I will leave you in the desert, you and all the fish from your rivers. You will fall on the open field. No one will pick you up or gather you. I have given you as food to the wild animals and the birds.

6“Then all the people of Egypt will know that I am the LORD. They have been like a weak stick for the people of Israel.

7“When Israel held your hand, you broke and hurt their shoulders. When they leaned on you, you broke, and their backs shook.

8“So this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I will bring a sword against you. I will destroy people and animals in your land.

9“The land of Egypt will become empty and ruined. Then they will know that I am the LORD. You said the Nile is yours and that you made it.

10“So I am against you and your rivers. I will make the land of Egypt a ruined, empty place from Migdol to Syene, all the way to the border of Cush.

11“No person or animal will walk through it. It will not be lived in for forty years.

12“I will make Egypt the emptiest land among ruined lands. Its cities will be empty for forty years among ruined cities. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations. I will send them to other lands.

13“For this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: After forty years, I will bring the Egyptians back from the nations where they were scattered.

14“I will bring Egypt back from being captured. I will bring them back to the land of Pathros, the land where they started. There they will be a small kingdom.

15“It will be the smallest kingdom. It will never again lift itself above other nations. I will make them so small that they will never again rule over other nations.

16“Egypt will no longer be a place Israel trusts. Instead, it will remind Israel of its sin when Israel turned to Egypt. Then they will know that I am the Lord, the LORD.”

God Gives Egypt to Babylon

17In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day, the word of the LORD came to me.

18“Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made his army work hard against Tyre. Every head was made bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare. But he and his army got no pay from Tyre for the work they did.

19“So this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. He will carry off its wealth. He will take its treasure and its goods. It will be pay for his army.

20“I have given him the land of Egypt as a reward for his work, because he and his army did it for MemeMe, says the Lord, the LORD.

21“On that day I will make the people of Israel strong again. I will let you speak among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
