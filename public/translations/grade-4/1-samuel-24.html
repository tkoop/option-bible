Saul Looks for David

1After Saul came back from fighting the Philistines, someone told him, "David is in the desert of En Gedi." 2So Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel. He went to look for David and his men near the rocks where wild goats live.

3He came to sheep pens by the road. There was a cave there. Saul went in to use the restroom. David and his men were far back in the cave.

4The men said, "This is the day the LORD told you about. He said, 'I will give your enemy to you. You can do to him what you want.'" Then David quietly got up and cut off a corner of Saul's robe.

5After that, David felt sorry because he had cut off a piece of Saul's robe.

6He said to his men, "ThetheThe LORD would not want me to do this to my master. He is the one the LORD chose to be king. I will not hurt him."

7David spoke firmly to his men. He did not let them hurt Saul. Then Saul left the cave and went on his way.

David Calls Out to Saul

8Then David came out of the cave. He called after Saul, "MymyMy lord the king!" Saul looked back. David bowed low with his face to the ground.

9He said to Saul, "Why do you listen to people who say, 'David wants to hurt you'?

10Today you saw with your own eyes how the LORD put you in my power in the cave. Some told me to kill you, but I did not. I said, 'I will not hurt my lord, because he is the one the LORD chose to be king.'

11"Look, my father. Look at this piece of your robe in my hand. I cut off the corner of your robe, but I did not kill you. See that I have done nothing wrong. I have not hurt you. But you are chasing me to kill me.

12"May the LORD judge between us. May the LORD pay you back for what you did to me, but I will not hurt you.

13"People have an old saying: 'Bad people do bad things.' But I will not hurt you.

14"Who is the king of Israel chasing? A dead dog? A flea?

15"May the LORD be the judge between us. May HeheHe look at my side and stand up for me. May HeheHe save me from you."

Saul Talks to David

16When David finished, Saul said, "IsisIs that your voice, David my son?" Then Saul cried loudly.

17"You are more right than I am. You were good to me, but I was bad to you.

18Today you told me the good you did for me. The LORD put me in your hands, but you did not kill me.

19When a man finds his enemy, does he let him go safely? May the LORD pay you back with good for what you did for me today.

20Now I know you will be king. The kingdom of Israel will be strong in your hands.

21So swear to me by the LORD that you will not cut off my family after me. Do not wipe out my name from my father's family."

22So David swore to Saul. Then Saul went home. David and his men went up to the safe place.
