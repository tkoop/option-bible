A lesson to remember

1Listen, my people. Hear my teaching. Turn your ears to my words. 2I will open my mouth and tell a story. I will share old secrets. 3We heard these things. We know them. Our fathers told them to us.

4We will not hide them from their children. We will tell the next group of kids about the great things the LORD has done. We will tell about HishisHis power and HishisHis wonders. 5He made a rule for Jacob's family. He gave a law to Israel. He told our fathers to teach it to their children. 6Then the next group would know it. Even kids not born yet would learn it. They would grow up and tell their own children. 7Then they could trust God. They would not forget what God did. They would keep HishisHis commands. 8They would not be like their fathers. Their fathers were stubborn and would not obey. Their hearts were not true to God. Their spirits were not faithful to HimhimHim.

9The men of Ephraim had bows, but they turned back on the day of battle. 10They did not keep God's agreement. They would not follow HishisHis law. 11They forgot what HeheHe had done. They forgot the wonders HeheHe showed them.

God's wonders in Egypt

12God did amazing things where their fathers could see. He did them in Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. 13He split the sea and led them through it. He made the water stand like a wall. 14He led them with a cloud in the day. He led them with fire all night. 15He broke rocks in the desert. He gave them plenty to drink, like water from the deep sea. 16He made streams come from the rock. The water flowed down like rivers.

The people complain in the desert

17But they sinned even more against God. They fought against the MostmostMost HighhighHigh in the dry land. 18They tested God in their hearts. They asked for food they wanted. 19They spoke against God. They said, "Can God set a table in the desert? 20He hit the rock, and water poured out. Streams rushed by. Can HeheHe also give bread? Can HeheHe get meat for HishisHis people?"

21So the LORD heard them. He was very angry. Fire burned against Jacob. His anger rose against Israel. 22They did not believe in God. They did not trust HishisHis power to save.

God sends food from Heavenheavenheaven

23But HeheHe told the clouds above to open. He opened the doors of Heavenheavenheaven. 24He rained bread on them to eat. He gave them grain from Heavenheavenheaven. 25People ate the food of angels. He sent them food until they were full. 26He made the east wind blow in the sky. By HishisHis power HeheHe led the south wind. 27He rained meat on them like dust. He rained birds like sand by the sea. 28He let them fall in the middle of the camp. They fell all around their tents. 29The people ate and were very full. He gave them what they wanted. 30But they were not done wanting. The food was still in their mouths. 31Then God's anger rose against them. He killed their strongest men. He brought down the young men of Israel.

32Even after all this, they still sinned. They did not believe HimhimHim, even with HishisHis wonders. 33So HeheHe made their days end like a breath. Their years ended in fear.

34When HeheHe killed some of them, the others looked for HimhimHim. They turned back and searched for God. 35They remembered that God was their rock. The MostmostMost HighhighHigh God was the one who saved them. 36But they only praised HimhimHim with their mouths. With their tongues they lied to HimhimHim. 37Their hearts were not true to HimhimHim. They were not faithful to HishisHis agreement.

God is kind, but they forget again

38But God was kind. He forgave their sin. He did not destroy them. He held back HishisHis anger many times. He did not let all HishisHis wrath come out. 39He remembered that they were only people. They were like wind that passes and does not come back.

40How often they fought against HimhimHim in the desert! How often they made HimhimHim sad in the dry land! 41They tested God again and again. They made the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel angry. 42They did not remember HishisHis power. They did not remember the day HeheHe saved them from their enemy. 43They did not remember the signs HeheHe did in Egypt. They did not remember HishisHis wonders in the fields of Zoan.

God's plagues in Egypt

44He turned their rivers into blood. They could not drink from their streams. 45He sent flies that ate them. He sent frogs that ruined them. 46He gave their crops to the locust. He gave the fruit of their work to the grasshopper. 47He killed their vines with hail. He killed their fig trees with frost. 48He gave their animals to the hail. He gave their flocks to lightning. 49He sent HishisHis burning anger on them. He sent wrath, trouble, and distress. He sent angels to hurt them. 50He made a path for HishisHis anger. He did not spare them from death. He gave their lives to sickness. 51He struck all the firstborn in Egypt. He struck the first sons in the tents of Ham.

52Then HeheHe led HishisHis people out like sheep. He guided them in the desert like a flock. 53He led them safely. They were not afraid. The sea covered their enemies. 54He brought them to HishisHis holy place. He brought them to the mountain HishisHis right hand won. 55He drove out nations in front of them. He gave them land to live in. He settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

56But they tested God the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. They fought against HimhimHim. They did not keep HishisHis rules. 57They turned back and were not faithful, like their fathers. They were like a bow that does not shoot straight. 58They made HimhimHim angry with their hilltop altars. They made HimhimHim jealous with their idols. 59God heard this and was very angry. He turned away from Israel. 60He left HishisHis home at Shiloh. That was the tent where HeheHe lived among people. 61He let HishisHis power be taken away. He let HishisHis glory fall into the enemy's hand. 62He gave HishisHis people to the sword. He let HishisHis anger fall on HishisHis own people. 63Fire ate their young men. Their young women did not sing happy songs. 64Their priests fell by the sword. Their widows did not cry.

65Then the Lord woke up, like a man waking from sleep. He was like a strong man shouting after wine. 66He beat back HishisHis enemies. He put them to shame forever.

God chooses David

67He turned away from the tent of Joseph. He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim. 68He chose the tribe of Judah. He chose Mount Zion, which HeheHe loves. 69He built HishisHis holy place high like the heavens. He made it strong like the earth HeheHe set forever. 70He chose David, HishisHis servant. He took him from the sheep pens. 71He brought him from caring for mother sheep. He brought him to lead Jacob, HishisHis people. He brought him to lead Israel, HishisHis own land. 72David led them with a true heart. He guided them with skilfulskilfulskillful hands.
