A Prayer for the King

1May the LORD answer you when you have trouble. May the God of Jacob keep you safe. 2May HeheHe send you help from HishisHis holy place. May HeheHe hold you up from Zion. 3May HeheHe remember all your gifts. May HeheHe be pleased with the gifts you burn. Selah. 4May HeheHe give you what your heart wants. May HeheHe make all your plans work. 5We will shout with joy when you win. We will raise our flags for our God. May the LORD give you all you ask for.

God Saves His King

6Now I know that the LORD saves HishisHis chosen king. He answers him from HishisHis holy Heavenheavenheaven. He saves him with the power of HishisHis right hand. 7Some trust in war carts. Some trust in horses. But we trust in the LORD our God. 8They fall down and lose. But we stand up and stay strong. 9The LORD, save the king! Answer us when we call!
