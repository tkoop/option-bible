People Are Hurt and No One Helps

1Then I looked at all the wrongs people do in this world. I saw the tears of people who were hurt. No one helped them. The people who hurt them had power, but no one helped the hurt people. 2I said the dead are better off than the living who are still alive. 3But even better is someone who has never lived. That person never saw the bad things people do in this world.

4I saw that people work hard and do good work because they are jealous of each other. This is also useless and pointless. 5A fool does nothing and ruins himself. 6A little peace is better than a lot of hard work that means nothing.

More Meaningless Things

7Then I looked again and saw another useless thing in this world. 8A man works alone. He has no partner, no son, and no brother. He works all the time, but he is never happy with his money. He asks, "Who am I working for? Why am I not enjoying good things?" This is also useless and a heavy burden.

Two Are Better Than One

9Two people are better than one, because they get more from their work. 10If one falls, the other can help him up. But if a person is alone and falls, no one is there to help him up. 11If two people sleep near each other, they keep warm. But one person alone cannot get warm. 12If one person is attacked, two can stand together. And three people together are even stronger.

A Poor Wise Boy and a Foolish King

13A poor young person who is wise is better than an old king who is foolish and will not listen to advice. 14He came out of prison to become king, even though he was born poor in that land. 15I saw all the people living in this world follow the young man who would take the king's place. 16There is no end to the people who came before him. And the people who come after will not be happy with him either. This is also useless and pointless.
