God Sends Jonah Again

1The LORD spoke to Jonah again. 2He said, "Get up and go to Nineveh, that big city. Tell the people the message I give you."

Jonah Obeys God

3Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, just as the the LORD said. Nineveh was a very big city. It took three days to walk across it. 4Jonah walked one day into the city. Then he called out, "In forty days, Nineveh will be destroyed!"

The People Turn to God

5The people of Nineveh believed God. They did not eat for a while, and they wore rough cloth. Everyone did this, from the most important to the least. 6The king of Nineveh heard the news. He got up from his throne. He took off his fancy robe. He put on rough cloth. He sat in ashes.

The King's Order

7He sent a message all through Nineveh. He said, "ThetheThe king and his leaders make this rule: Do not let people or animals eat or drink anything. 8Let people and animals wear rough cloth. Let them call out loud to God. Let each person stop doing bad things and stop hurting others. 9Maybe God will change HishisHis mind. Maybe HeheHe will stop being so angry. Then we will not die."

God Shows Mercy

10God saw what they did. He saw that they stopped doing bad things. So God changed HishisHis mind about the bad thing HeheHe had planned to do to them. He did not do it.
