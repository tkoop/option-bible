Asa Becomes King

1Abijah died and was buried in the CitycityCity of David. His son Asa became king after him. During Asa's time, the land was at peace for ten years.

Asa Does What Is Right

2Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God. 3He took away the foreign altars and the high places. He broke the stone pillars and cut down the Asherah poles. 4He told Judah to look for the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to keep the law and the command. 5He took away the high places and the sun images from all the cities of Judah. The kingdom was at peace under him.

Asa Builds Cities

6Because the land was at peace, Asa built strong cities in Judah. There was no war in those years, because the LORD gave him rest. 7Asa said to Judah, "Let us build these cities. Let us put walls around them, with towers, gates, and bars. The land is still open before us, because we looked for the LORD our God. We looked for HimhimHim, and HeheHe gave us rest all around." So they built and did well.

Asa's Army

8Asa had an army. From Judah there were 300,000 men with large shields and spears. From Benjamin there were 280,000 men with shields and bows. All of them were strong fighters.

Zerah Attacks Judah

9Zerah, a man from Cush, came against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots. He came as far as Mareshah. 10Asa went out to meet him. They set up for battle in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

Asa Prays

11Asa cried out to the LORD his God. He said, "the LORD, no one can help like YouyouYou, whether someone is strong or weak. Help us, the LORD our God, because we trust in YouyouYou. We came in YouryourYour name against this big crowd. the LORD, YouyouYou are our God. Do not let people win against YouyouYou."

God Gives Victory

12The LORD struck the men from Cush before Asa and Judah. The men from Cush ran away. 13Asa and his people chased them to Gerar. The men from Cush fell until they could not get up again. They were broken before the LORD and HishisHis army. Asa's men took a lot of things. 14They struck all the cities around Gerar, because they were afraid of the LORD. They took things from all the cities, because there was much to take. 15They also struck the tents where people kept their animals. They took many sheep and camels. Then they went back to Jerusalem.
