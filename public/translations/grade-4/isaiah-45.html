The LORD Talks to Cyrus

1The LORD says this to Cyrus, the man HeheHe chose to lead: "I hold your right hand. I will help you beat nations. I will take power from kings. I will open doors for you. Gates will not stay shut. 2I will go in front of you. I will make rough places flat. I will break bronze doors. I will cut iron bars."

3"I will give you treasures hidden in dark places. I will give you secret riches. Then you will know I am the LORD, the God of Israel. I call you by name. 4I do this for Jacob, who serves MemeMe, and for Israel, MymyMy chosen people. I call you by name. I give you a special name. But you do not know MemeMe."

5"I am the LORD. There is no other God. I will give you strength, even though you do not know MemeMe. 6Then people from sunrise to sunset will know there is no one but MemeMe. I am the LORD. There is no other. 7I make light. I make darkness. I bring peace. I bring trouble. I, the LORD, do all these things."

8"Rain down, heavens. Let the skies pour down what is right. Let the earth open up. Let saving help grow. Let what is right grow too. I, the LORD, made this."

Do Not Fight Your Maker

9It is bad for a man who fights his MakermakerMaker. He is like a clay pot among other pots. Will clay ask the potter what he is making? Will the thing made say he has no hands? 10It is bad for one who says to his father, "What did you make?" or to his mother, "What did you bring into the world?"

The LORD Made Everything

11The LORD, the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel, who made Israel, says this: "Ask MemeMe about what will happen. Will you tell MemeMe what to do with MymyMy children? Will you tell MemeMe what to do with the work of MymyMy hands? 12I made the earth. I made people on it. My hands spread out the skies. I told all the stars to be there. 13I will raise Cyrus to do what is right. I will make his path straight. He will build MymyMy city again. He will let MymyMy people go free. He will not ask for money or pay. This is what the LORD of Armies says."

Other Nations Will Bow to You

14The LORD says this: "Egypt's goods and Cush's goods, and the tall Sabeans, will come to you. They will be yours. They will walk behind you. They will come in chains. They will bow down to you. They will say that God is with you and there is no other God."

15Truly, YouyouYou are a God who hides HimselfhimselfHimself. You are the God of Israel. You save people.

16They will all be ashamed. The men who make false gods will leave together in shame. 17But Israel will be saved by the LORD forever. You will never be ashamed.

The LORD Made the World

18The LORD says this: "He made the heavens. He is God. He made the earth. He set it in place. He did not make it empty. He made it for people to live in. I am the LORD. There is no other. 19I did not speak in secret in a dark land. I did not tell Jacob's people, 'Look for MemeMe for nothing.' I, the LORD, speak truth. I say what is right.

20"Come together. Come near, you who ran away from the nations. They do not know anything. They carry wooden gods. They pray to a god that cannot save. 21Speak up. Make your case. Let them talk together. Who said this long ago? Who told it from the start? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no other God but MemeMe. I am a good God who saves. There is no one else.

Turn to the LORD and Be Saved

22"Turn to MemeMe and be saved, all the world. I am God. There is no other. 23I made a promise by MyselfmyselfMyself. Truth came from MymyMy mouth. My word will not change. Every knee will bow to MemeMe. Every tongue will promise to MemeMe. 24They will say that only in the LORD are right ways and strength. All who get angry at HimhimHim will come to HimhimHim and be ashamed. 25All Israel's children will be made right in the LORD. They will praise HimhimHim."
