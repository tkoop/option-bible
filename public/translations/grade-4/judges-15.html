Samson Goes to See His Wife

1Later, when people were picking wheat, Samson took a young goat to visit his wife. He said, "I will go to my wife's room." But her father would not let him go in.

2Her father said, "I thought you hated her. So I gave her to your friend. Isn't her younger sister nicer? You can have her instead."

Samson Pays Back the Philistines

3Samson said to them, "This time I can pay them back. I will really hurt them." 4Then Samson went out and caught three hundred foxes. He tied them tail to tail in pairs. He tied a torch to each pair.

5He lit the torches and let the foxes go in the Philistines' grain fields. He burned the piles of grain, the growing grain, and the grape fields and olive trees.

6The Philistines asked, "Who did this?"

They were told, "Samson did. He is from Timnah. His wife was given to his friend." So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father to death.

7Samson said to them, "Since you did this, I will not stop until I pay you back." 8He fought them hard and killed many of them. Then he went down and stayed in a cave in the rock of Etam.

The People of Judah Tie Up Samson

9The Philistines went up and camped in Judah. They spread out near Lehi.

10The people of Judah asked, "Why have you come up against us?"

The Philistines answered, "We have come to tie up Samson. We want to do to him what he did to us."

11Then three thousand men from Judah went down to the cave in the rock of Etam. They said to Samson, "Don't you know the Philistines rule over us? What have you done to us?"

Samson answered, "I only did to them what they did to me."

12They said to him, "We have come to tie you up and hand you over to the Philistines."

Samson said, "Promise me you will not kill me yourselves."

13They said, "We will only tie you up and hand you over to them. We will not kill you." So they tied him with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

Samson Beats the Philistines with a Donkey Jawbone

14As he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted to meet him. Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD came on him with power. The ropes on his arms became like burned cloth. The ties fell off his hands.

15He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey. He picked it up and hit a thousand men.

16Then Samson said, "With a donkey's jawbone I have piled them into heaps. With a donkey's jawbone I have killed a thousand men."

17When he finished speaking, he threw the jawbone away. That place was called Ramath Lehi.

God Gives Samson Water

18He was very thirsty. He called to the the LORD, "YouyouYou have given YouryourYour servant this big win. Now must I die of thirst and fall into the hands of people who do not belong to the the LORD?"

19Then God opened the hollow place in Lehi. Water came out. Samson drank, and his strength came back. He lived. So he called the place En Hakkore. It is in Lehi even now.

20Samson led Israel for twenty years during the time of the Philistines.
