A Prayer for Help

1The LORD, remember what happened to us. Look and see our shame. 2Our land now belongs to strangers. Our homes now belong to foreigners. 3We have no father. Our mothers are like women whose husbands have died. 4We have to pay for water to drink. We have to pay for wood.

5People chase us. We are tired and get no rest. 6We turned to Egypt and Assyria for food. 7Our fathers did wrong. Now we carry their trouble. 8Slaves rule over us. No one saves us from them.

9We risk our lives to get bread because of danger in the desert. 10Our skin is hot like an oven because of hunger. 11Women are hurt in Zion. Young girls are hurt in Judah towns. 12Leaders are hung by their hands. Old men are not honouredhonouredhonored.

13Young men grind grain. Boys fall while carrying wood. 14Old men no longer sit at the gate. Young men no longer sing. 15Our joy is gone. Our dancing turned to crying. 16Our crown fell from our head. We sinned, and now we are in trouble.

17Because of this, our hearts are weak. Because of these things, our eyes are dim. 18Mount Zion is empty. Foxes walk on it.
