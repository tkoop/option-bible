Job Curses the Day He Was Born

1After this, Job opened his mouth. He cursed the day he was born. 2Job said:

"3May the day I was born be lost. May the night that said, 'A boy is born,' be lost too. 4May that day be dark. May God above not care about it. May no light shine on it. 5May darkness and deep gloom take it. May a cloud cover it. May blackness fill it. 6May thick dark take that night. May it not be counted in the days of the year. May it not be counted in any month. 7May that night be empty. May no happy shout be heard in it. 8Let those who know how to curse a day curse it. Let those who can wake the sea monster do it. 9May its stars go dark. May it wait for light but not get it. May it not see the morning light. 10For it did not close the doors of my mother's body. It did not hide trouble from my eyes."

Job Wishes He Had Died at Birth

"11Why did I not die when I was born? Why did I not die as I came from my mother? 12Why were there knees to hold me? Why were there breasts to feed me? 13Now I would lie down and be quiet. I would sleep and rest. 14I would rest with kings and leaders of the earth. They built places that are now in ruins. 15Or with princes who had gold. They filled their houses with silver. 16Or why was I not hidden like a baby who died before birth? Like a baby who never saw light. 17There the bad people stop making trouble. There tired people rest. 18Prisoners rest together. They do not hear the boss shout. 19The small and the great are there. The slave is free from his master."

Job Asks Why Suffering People Live

"20Why is light given to people in pain? Why is life given to people who are bitter? 21They wait for death, but it does not come. They look for it more than for hidden treasure. 22They are glad and happy when they find the grave. 23Why is life given to a man who cannot see his way? God has shut him in. 24My sighs are my food. My groans pour out like water. 25What I feared has come to me. What I dreaded has happened. 26I have no peace. I have no quiet. I have no rest, only trouble."
