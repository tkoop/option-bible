Paul’s Third Visit

1This is the third time I am coming to you. Every wrong thing must be proven by two or three people who saw it. 2I already told you when I was there the second time. Now I tell you again while I am away. I warn the ones who sinned before and everyone else. If I come again, I will not be gentle with them. 3Since you want proof that Christ speaks through me, know this. He is not weak with you. He is strong in you. 4He was weak when HeheHe died on the cross. But HeheHe lives by God’s power. We are weak too. But we will live with HimhimHim by God’s power for you.

Test Yourselves

5Look at yourselves. Are you still in the faith? Test yourselves. Do you not know that Jesus Christ is in you? Unless you fail the test. 6I hope you know we have not failed the test. 7I pray to God that you do no wrong. I do not pray this so we look good. I pray it so you do what is right. Even if we look like we failed. 8We cannot work against the truth. We can only work for the truth. 9We are glad when we are weak and you are strong. We also pray that you be made whole. 10That is why I write this while I am away. I do not want to be hard on you when I am there. The Lord gave me power to build you up, not tear you down.

Final Words

11Finally, brothers and sisters, be happy. Fix what is wrong. Take heart. Think the same. Live in peace. Then the God of love and peace will be with you. 12Greet each other with a holy kiss. 13All of God’s holy people say hello to you. 14May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sharing of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit be with all of you.
