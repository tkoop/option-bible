Moses Speaks to All Israel

1Moses called all Israel together and said, “Listen, Israel. Today I will tell you the rules and laws I speak to you. Learn them and do them.”

2The LORD our God made an agreement with us at Horeb. 3The LORD did not make this agreement with our fathers. He made it with us, with all of us who are alive here today.

4The LORD spoke to you face to face from the fire on the mountain. 5At that time I stood between the LORD and you to tell you what the LORD said. You were afraid of the fire, so you did not go up the mountain. Then the LORD said:

God Gives the Ten Rules

6“I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of Egypt, out of the place where you were slaves. 7You must not have other gods before MemeMe. 8You must not make a carved picture or statue of anything in the sky above, on the earth below, or in the water under the earth. 9You must not bow down to them or serve them. I am the LORD your God. I want you to worship only MemeMe. I punish children for the wrongs of their fathers, down to the third and fourth family after those who hate MemeMe. 10But I show love to a thousand families of those who love MemeMe and obey MymyMy rules. 11You must not use the name of the LORD your God in a wrong way. The LORD will not let anyone go free who uses HishisHis name in a wrong way.”

“12Keep the Sabbath day holy. Rest on that day, as the LORD your God told you. 13Work for six days and do all your work. 14But the seventh day is a Sabbath for the LORD your God. On that day you must not work. You, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, your ox, your donkey, and all your animals must rest. The stranger who lives in your town must rest too. Then your male and female servants can rest like you. 15Remember that you were slaves in Egypt. The LORD your God brought you out with HishisHis strong hand and HishisHis power. So the LORD your God told you to keep the Sabbath day.”

Then you will live a long time, and things will go well for you in the land the LORD your God gives you. 17You must not murder. 18You must not cheat with someone else’s husband or wife. 19You must not steal. 20You must not lie about your neighbourneighbourneighbor in court. 21You must not want your neighbourneighbourneighbor’s wife. You must not want your neighbourneighbourneighbor’s house, field, male servant, female servant, ox, donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbourneighbourneighbor.”

The People Are Afraid of God’s Voice

22These are the words the LORD spoke in a loud voice to all your people from the fire, the cloud, and the thick dark cloud on the mountain. He did not say anything else. He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me.

23When you heard the voice from the dark, and the mountain was burning with fire, the leaders of your tribes and your elders came to me.

24You said, “Look! The LORD our God has shown us HishisHis glory and HishisHis great power. We heard HishisHis voice from the fire. Today we saw that a person can live even when God talks to him. 25But now, why should we die? This big fire will burn us up, and we will die! If we hear the LORD our God’s voice any more, we will die. 26What person has ever heard the voice of the living God speaking from the fire, like we did, and stayed alive? 27You go near and listen to everything the LORD our God says. Then you tell us everything the LORD our God tells you. We will listen and do it.”

28The LORD heard the words you spoke to me. He said to me, “I heard the words these people spoke to you. They spoke well. All they said was good.”

29The LORD said, “If only they had a heart that wanted to fear MemeMe and keep all MymyMy rules all the time! Then things would go well for them and their children forever.”

30The LORD said, “Go and tell them, ‘Go back to your tents.’”

Moses Stays with God

31The LORD said, “But you, stand here with MemeMe. I will speak to you all the commands, rules, and laws you must teach them. They must do them in the land I am giving them to own.”

32So be careful to do what the LORD your God told you. Do not turn to the right or the left.

33Walk in all the ways the LORD your God told you, so that you may live, do well, and live a long time in the land you will own.
