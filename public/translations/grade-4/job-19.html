Job Talks About His Pain

1Then Job answered.

2"How long will you make me sad? You hurt me with words. 3You have blamed me again and again. You are not ashamed to attack me. 4If I really did go wrong, that is my own trouble.

5"If you make yourselves bigger than me and use my shame against me, 6then know that God has been unfair to me. He has trapped me.

7"I cry out, 'Someone hurt me!' but no one answers. I call for help, but there is no justice.

8"He has blocked my path, so I cannot pass. He has covered my roads with darkness. 9He has taken my honourhonourhonor away. He has taken the crown off my head. 10He breaks me down on every side until I am gone. He pulls up my hope like a tree.

11"His anger burns against me. He counts me as HishisHis enemy. 12His soldiers come together. They build a ramp to attack me. They set up camp all around my tent.

13"He has pushed my brothers away from me. My friends have become strangers to me. 14My relatives have left. My close friends have forgotten me. 15My guests and my female servants treat me like a stranger. They see me as someone from another land.

16"I call my servant, but he does not answer. I beg him with my own mouth. 17My breath is bad to my wife. I am disgusting to my own family.

18"Even little boys make fun of me. When I get up, they talk against me. 19All my close friends hate me. The people I loved have turned against me.

20"I am only skin and bones. I have barely escaped. 21Have pity on me, my friends. Have pity, because God has hurt me.

22"Why do you chase me like God does? Can you never be satisfied with hurting me?

23"I wish my words were written down. I wish they were written in a book. 24I wish they were cut into rock with an iron pen and lead, so they would last forever.

25"I know my Redeemer lives. In the end HeheHe will stand on the earth. 26After my skin is destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God. 27I myself will see HimhimHim with my own eyes. I, and not someone else. My heart aches inside me.

28"If you say, 'We will hunt him down,' and, 'He is the cause of his trouble,' 29you should fear the sword. Anger brings punishment with a sword, so you will know there is a judge."
