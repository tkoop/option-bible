Joab Talks to the King

1Someone told Joab, "ThetheThe king is crying and mourning for Absalom." 2The army's win that day became sad for everyone, because the people heard the king was sad about his son. 3The people sneaked back into the city that day, like people sneak away in shame when they run from a battle. 4The king hid his face and cried out loud, "Oh, my son Absalom! Oh, Absalom, my son, my son!"

5Joab went to the king in his house and said, "Today you made all your servants feel ashamed. They saved your life today, and the lives of your sons, daughters, wives, and other wives. 6You love people who hate you, and you hate people who love you. Today you showed that your leaders and servants mean nothing to you. Today I know that if Absalom were alive and all of us were dead, you would be happy. 7Now get up and go out. Speak kindly to your servants. I swear by the LORD, if you do not go out, not one man will stay with you tonight. That would be worse for you than all the trouble you have had since you were young."

8The king got up and sat in the gate. People were told, "ThetheThe king is sitting in the gate." Then all the people came before the king. By then, Israel's soldiers had run away, each to his own tent.

The People Want the King Back

9All the people in all the tribes of Israel were arguing. They said, "ThetheThe king saved us from our enemies. He saved us from the Philistines. But now he ran from the country because of Absalom. 10Absalom, the one we chose to be over us, died in battle. So why are you not talking about bringing the king back?"

David Sends a Message to Judah

11King David sent a message to Zadok and Abiathar the priests. He said, "Speak to the elders of Judah. Ask them, 'Why are you the last to bring the king back to his house? The words of all Israel have reached the king about bringing him to his house. 12You are my brothers, my own flesh and bone. Why are you the last to bring back the king?' 13Also say to Amasa, 'Are you not my flesh and bone? May God punish me if you are not the commander of my army from now on instead of Joab.'"

14David won over the hearts of all the men of Judah. They became like one man. They sent a message to the king: "Come back, you and all your servants." 15The king came back and reached the Jordan. The people of Judah came to Gilgal to meet the king and help him cross the Jordan.

Shimei Asks for Forgiveness

16Shimei son of Gera, a man from Benjamin's family from Bahurim, hurried down with the men of Judah to meet King David. 17A thousand men from Benjamin were with him. Ziba, who had served Saul's family, was there too, with his fifteen sons and twenty servants. They rushed to the Jordan ahead of the king. 18They crossed the river to help the king's family cross and to do what pleased him. Shimei son of Gera fell down before the king as he crossed the Jordan.

19Shimei said to the king, "MymyMy lord, please do not blame me. Do not remember the wrong your servant did on the day my lord the king left Jerusalem. Please do not take it to heart. 20I know I have sinned. That is why I came today, the first of all Joseph's family, to meet my lord the king."

21Abishai son of Zeruiah answered, "Should Shimei not be put to death for this? He cursed the LORD's anointed one." 22David said, "What do you and I have in common, sons of Zeruiah? Why are you against me today? Should any man be killed in Israel today? Do I not know that today I am king over Israel?" 23The king said to Shimei, "YouyouYou will not die." The king swore to him.

Mephibosheth Explains

24Mephibosheth, Saul's grandson, came down to meet the king. He had not cared for his feet, trimmed his moustachemoustachemustache, or washed his clothes from the day the king left until the day he came back safely. 25When he came to Jerusalem to meet the king, the king said, "Why did you not go with me, Mephibosheth?"

26Mephibosheth answered, "MymyMy lord the king, my servant tricked me. Your servant said, 'I will saddle the donkey and ride with the king,' because your servant is lame. 27He has lied about your servant to my lord the king. But my lord the king is like an angel of God. Do what seems good to you. 28All my father's family were as good as dead before my lord the king. Yet you let your servant eat at your table. What right do I have to ask the king for more?"

29The king said, "Why talk more? I say you and Ziba will share the field." 30Mephibosheth said, "Let him take it all, since my lord the king has come home safely."

Barzillai Helps the King

31Barzillai the Gileadite came down from Rogelim. He crossed the Jordan with the king to send him on his way. 32Barzillai was very old. He was eighty years old. He had fed the king while the king stayed at Mahanaim, because he was a very rich man. 33The king said to Barzillai, "Cross over with me. I will take care of you in Jerusalem."

34Barzillai said to the king, "How many years do I have left to live? Why should I go up with the king to Jerusalem? 35I am eighty years old today. Can I tell good from bad? Can your servant taste what I eat or drink? Can I still hear men and women sing? Why should your servant be a burden to my lord the king? 36Your servant will go a little way over the Jordan with the king. Why should the king reward me with all this? 37Please let your servant go back. I want to die in my own city, near my father's and mother's graves. But here is your servant Chimham. Let him cross over with my lord the king. Do for him what seems good to you."

38The king said, "Chimham will cross with me. I will do for him what seems good to you. I will do for you whatever you ask." 39All the people crossed the Jordan, and the king crossed too. The king kissed Barzillai and blessed him. Then Barzillai went back home. 40The king went on to Gilgal. Chimham went with him. All the people of Judah and half the people of Israel brought the king across.

The Tribes Argue

41Then all the men of Israel came to the king and said, "Why did our brothers, the men of Judah, steal you away? They brought the king and his family across the Jordan, along with all David's men." 42The men of Judah answered the men of Israel, "ThetheThe king is our close relative. Why are you angry about this? Have we eaten at the king's expense? Or has he given us any gift?" 43The men of Israel answered the men of Judah, "We have ten shares in the king. We have more claim on David than you do. Why did you look down on us? Were we not the first to talk about bringing our king back?" But the words of the men of Judah were stronger than the words of the men of Israel.
