Israel Fights King Og

1Then we turned and went up the road to Bashan. Og, the king of Bashan, came out with all his men to fight us at Edrei. 2The LORD said to me, "Do not be afraid of him. I have given him, all his people, and his land to you. Do to him what you did to Sihon, the king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon." 3So the LORD our God gave Og, the king of Bashan, and all his people into our hands. We beat them until no one was left.

4At that time we took all his cities. There was not one city we did not take. There were sixty cities in the whole area of Argob, in Og's kingdom in Bashan. 5All these cities had high walls, gates, and bars. There were also many towns without walls. 6We destroyed them just as we did to Sihon, the king of Heshbon. We destroyed every city, including the men, women, and children. 7But we kept all the animals and the things from the cities for ourselves.

Land East of the Jordan

8At that time we took the land from the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan. It went from the Arnon River to Mount Hermon. 9The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion. The Amorites call it Senir. 10We took all the cities on the flat land, all of Gilead, and all of Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei in Og's kingdom. 11Only Og, the king of Bashan, was left of the Rephaim. His bed was made of iron. It was about 13 feet long and 6 feet wide. It is still in Rabbah of the Ammonites.

Land Given to the Tribes

12At that time we took this land. I gave it to the Reubenites and Gadites. It went from Aroer by the Arnon River and half of the hill country of Gilead with its cities. 13I gave the rest of Gilead and all of Bashan to the half tribe of Manasseh. That was Og's kingdom, all the area of Argob and all Bashan. It was called the land of the Rephaim. 14Jair, the son of Manasseh, took all the area of Argob up to the border of the Geshurites and Maachathites. He named the towns of Bashan after himself. They are called Havoth Jair to this day. 15I gave Gilead to Machir. 16To the Reubenites and Gadites I gave land from Gilead to the Arnon River, with the middle of the river as the border. It went to the Jabbok River, the border of the Ammonites. 17The border also included the Jordan River and the Arabah.
