A Man Named Elkanah

1There was a man named Elkanah. He lived in Ramathaim-zophim in the hills of Ephraim. He was the son of Jeroham. Jeroham was the son of Elihu. Elihu was the son of Tohu. Tohu was the son of Zuph. Elkanah was from Ephraim. 2He had two wives. One was named Hannah. The other was named Peninnah. Peninnah had children. Hannah had none.

Yearly Trips to Shiloh

3Every year, this man went up from his town to Shiloh. He went there to worship and give gifts to the LORD of Hosts. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests of the LORD there. 4On the day he gave his gift, Elkanah gave parts to his wife Peninnah. He gave parts to all her sons and daughters too. 5But he gave Hannah two parts. He did this because he loved Hannah. The LORD had kept her from having children.

6The LORD had kept Hannah from having children. So Peninnah made fun of her. She bothered her again and again. 7This happened every year. When Hannah went to the house of the LORD, Peninnah made her sad. Hannah cried and did not eat.

Elkanah Comforts Hannah

8Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Hannah, why are you crying? Why won't you eat? Why is your heart sad? Am I not better to you than ten sons?"

Hannah Prays for a Baby

9After they ate and drank in Shiloh, Hannah stood up. Eli the priest was sitting by the door of the LORD's house. 10Hannah was very sad. She prayed to the LORD and cried hard.

11She made a promise to God. She said, "O LORD of Hosts, please look at me. I am YouryourYour servant. Please remember me. Do not forget me. Give me a son. Then I will give him to the LORD for all his life. No razor will ever touch his head."

12Hannah kept praying to the LORD. Eli watched her mouth. 13She was praying in her heart. Only her lips moved. No one could hear her voice. So Eli thought she was drunk.

14Eli said to her, "How long will you be drunk? Put away your wine!"

15"No, my lord," Hannah said. "I am a woman with a sad heart. I have not had wine or strong drink. I have poured out my heart to the LORD. 16Do not think I am a bad woman. I have been praying because I am so sad and upset."

17Eli said, "Go in peace. May the God of Israel give you what you asked for."

18Hannah said, "May I find favourfavourfavor in your eyes." Then she went on her way. She ate. Her face was not sad anymore.

Samuel Is Born

19Early the next morning, they got up and worshiped the LORD. Then they went home to Ramah. Elkanah and Hannah were together as husband and wife. The LORD remembered her.

20In time, Hannah had a baby boy. She named him Samuel. She said, "I asked the LORD for him."

21Elkanah and his whole family went up to Shiloh. They went to give their yearly gift to the LORD. They also kept their promise. 22But Hannah did not go. She said to her husband, "After the boy is weaned, I will take him. I will bring him to see the LORD. He will stay there forever." 23Her husband Elkanah said, "Do what seems best to you. Stay here until you have weaned him. May the LORD keep HishisHis promise." So Hannah stayed. She nursed her son until he was weaned.

Hannah Gives Samuel to God

24When she had weaned him, she took the boy with her. She took three bulls, some flour, and a bottle of wine. She brought him to the LORD's house in Shiloh. 25They killed the bull. They brought the boy to Eli.

26Hannah said, "Please, my lord, as sure as you live, I am the woman who stood here praying to the LORD. 27I prayed for this boy. The LORD gave me what I asked for. 28So I am giving him to the LORD. He will belong to the LORD all his life." Then he worshiped the LORD there.
