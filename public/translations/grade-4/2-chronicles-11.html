Rehoboam Gets Ready to Fight

1Rehoboam went to Jerusalem. He called together the men of Judah and Benjamin. There were 180,000 chosen fighters. They were ready to fight Israel. They wanted to win back the kingdom for Rehoboam.

God Says, "Do Not Fight"

2The word of the LORD came to Shemaiah, a man of God. 3God said, "Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and all the people of Israel in Judah and Benjamin this: 4'This is what the LORD says: You must not go up. You must not fight your brothers. Go home. This is from MemeMe.'" ThetheThe people listened to the LORD's words. They went back and did not attack Jeroboam.

Rehoboam Builds Strong Cities

5Rehoboam stayed in Jerusalem. He built cities in Judah to keep them safe. 6He built Bethlehem, Etam, and Tekoa. 7He built Beth-zur, Soco, and Adullam. 8He built Gath, Mareshah, and Ziph. 9He built Adoraim, Lachish, and Azekah. 10He built Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron. These were strong cities in Judah and Benjamin. 11He made the strong places stronger. He put leaders in them. He stored food, oil, and wine there. 12He put shields and spears in every city. He made them very strong. Judah and Benjamin were on his side.

Priests and Levites Come to Judah

13The priests and Levites from all over Israel came to Rehoboam. 14The Levites left their fields and homes. They came to Judah and Jerusalem. Jeroboam and his sons had not let them serve the LORD. 15Jeroboam chose his own priests. They served at the high places. They served the goat idols and the calves he had made. 16People from every tribe of Israel wanted to follow the LORD, the God of Israel. They came to Jerusalem after the Levites. They came to give offerings to the LORD, the God of their fathers. 17They made Judah strong. They helped Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years. They followed the ways of David and Solomon for three years.

Rehoboam's Family

18Rehoboam married Mahalath. She was the daughter of Jerimoth son of David. Her mother was Abihail, daughter of Eliab son of Jesse. 19She had sons named Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham. 20Later he married Maacah, daughter of Absalom. She had sons named Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith. 21Rehoboam loved Maacah more than his other wives and servant wives. He had 18 wives and 60 servant wives. He had 28 sons and 60 daughters. 22He chose Abijah, Maacah's son, to be the leader. Abijah would be king after him. 23Rehoboam was wise. He sent some of his sons to cities in Judah and Benjamin. He gave them plenty of food. He found many wives for them.
