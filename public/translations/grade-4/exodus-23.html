Rules for Being Fair

1Do not tell a false story. Do not help bad people by being a false witness. 2Do not do wrong just because many people do. When you speak in a case, do not twist the truth to follow the crowd. 3Do not show special favourfavourfavor to a poor person in his case.

4If you see your enemy's ox or donkey that got lost, take it back to him. 5If you see the donkey of a man who hates you lying under its load, do not walk away. Help him get it up.

6Do not keep a poor person from fair help in his case. 7Stay away from false charges. Do not kill innocent and good people, because I will not let guilty people go free. 8Do not take money to change what is right. Money can blind people who see well and twist the words of good people. 9Do not be hard on a foreigner. You know how it feels to be a foreigner, because you were foreigners in Egypt.

Rules for Land and Rest

10For six years, plant your land and gather its crops. 11But in the seventh year, let it rest and leave it alone. Then poor people among your people can eat from the field. Wild animals can eat what is left. Do the same with your grape field and your olive trees. 12Work six days, but rest on the seventh day. This lets your ox and donkey rest. It also lets your servant's son and the foreigner rest. 13Be careful to do all I have said to you. Do not even say the names of other gods.

Feasts for the LORD

14Three times a year, celebrate a feast for MemeMe. 15Keep the FeastfeastFeast of BreadbreadBread with No Yeast. Eat bread with no yeast for seven days, as I told you. Do this at the set time in the month of Abib, because that is when you came out of Egypt. No one should come to MemeMe without a gift. 16Celebrate the harvest feast with the first crops you grow in the field. Celebrate the gathering feast at the end of the year, when you bring in your crops from the field. 17Three times a year, all your men must come before the Lord, the the LORD. 18Do not offer the blood of MymyMy sacrifice with bread that has yeast. Do not let the fat from MymyMy feast stay until morning. 19Bring the best first crops from your land to the house of the the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother's milk.

God Sends an Angel

“20I am sending an angel ahead of you to guard you on the way and bring you to the place I have made ready. 21Pay attention to him and listen to what he says. Do not go against him, because he will not forgive your sins. My name is in him. 22But if you truly listen to him and do all I say, then I will be an enemy to your enemies. I will oppose those who oppose you. 23My angel will go ahead of you and bring you to the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites. I will wipe them out.

“24Do not bow down to their gods. Do not serve them. Do not do the things they do. Instead, tear down their gods and break their stone pillars. 25Serve the the LORD your God, and HeheHe will bless your food and water. I will take sickness away from you. 26No woman in your land will lose her baby or be unable to have children. I will let you live out your days. 27I will send fear of MemeMe ahead of you. I will throw every nation you meet into confusion. I will make all your enemies turn and run.

“28I will send hornets ahead of you to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites. 29I will not drive them out in one year, because then the land would become empty, and wild animals would become too many for you. 30Little by little I will drive them out, until you grow and take the land. 31I will set your borders from the Red SeaseaSea to the Philistine sea, and from the desert to the River. I will hand over the people who live there, and you will drive them out.

“32Do not make an agreement with them or with their gods. 33They must not live in your land, or they will cause you to sin against MemeMe. If you serve their gods, it will be a trap for you.”
