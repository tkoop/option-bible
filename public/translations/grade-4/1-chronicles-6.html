Levi's Family

1The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 2The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 3The sons of Amram were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. The sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

4Eleazar was the father of Phinehas. Phinehas was the father of Abishua. 5Abishua was the father of Bukki. Bukki was the father of Uzzi. 6Uzzi was the father of Zerahiah. Zerahiah was the father of Meraioth. 7Meraioth was the father of Amariah. Amariah was the father of Ahitub. 8Ahitub was the father of Zadok. Zadok was the father of Ahimaaz. 9Ahimaaz was the father of Azariah. Azariah was the father of Johanan. 10Johanan was the father of Azariah. This Azariah served as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem. 11Azariah was the father of Amariah. Amariah was the father of Ahitub. 12Ahitub was the father of Zadok. Zadok was the father of Shallum. 13Shallum was the father of Hilkiah. Hilkiah was the father of Azariah. 14Azariah was the father of Seraiah. Seraiah was the father of Jehozadak. 15Jehozadak was taken away when the the LORD let Nebuchadnezzar carry Judah and Jerusalem into exile.

Levite Family Lines

16The sons of Levi were Gershom, Kohath, and Merari. 17The sons of Gershom were Libni and Shimei. 18The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 19The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These were the Levite families by their fathers' lines.

20Gershom's family line was Libni, then Jahath, then Zimmah. 21Then came Joah, then Iddo, then Zerah, then Jeatherai. 22Kohath's family line was Amminadab, then Korah, then Assir. 23Then came Elkanah, then Ebiasaph, then Assir. 24Then came Tahath, then Uriel, then Uzziah, then Saul. 25Elkanah had two sons, Amasai and Ahimoth. 26Elkanah's family line was Elkanah, then Zophai, then Nahath. 27Then came Eliab, then Jeroham, then Elkanah. 28Samuel had two sons. Joel was the firstborn, and Abijah was the second. 29Merari's family line was Mahli, then Libni, then Shimei, then Uzzah. 30Then came Shimea, then Haggiah, then Asaiah.

Singers in God's House

31These are the men David put in charge of music in the the LORD's house after the ark had a resting place. 32They sang before the meeting tent until Solomon built the the LORD's house in Jerusalem. Then they did their work in their right order.

33Here are the men who stood with their sons. From the Kohathites came Heman the singer. He was the son of Joel, the son of Samuel, 34the son of Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Eliel, the son of Toah, 35the son of Zuph, the son of Elkanah, the son of Mahath, the son of Amasai, 36the son of Elkanah, the son of Joel, the son of Azariah, the son of Zephaniah, 37the son of Tahath, the son of Assir, the son of Ebiasaph, the son of Korah, 38the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, the son of Israel.

39His brother Asaph stood at his right hand. Asaph was the son of Berechiah, the son of Shimea, 40the son of Michael, the son of Baaseiah, the son of Malchijah, 41the son of Ethni, the son of Zerah, the son of Adaiah, 42the son of Ethan, the son of Zimmah, the son of Shimei, 43the son of Jahath, the son of Gershom, the son of Levi.

44On the left stood their brothers, the sons of Merari. Ethan was the son of Kishi, the son of Abdi, the son of Malluch, 45the son of Hashabiah, the son of Amaziah, the son of Hilkiah, 46the son of Amzi, the son of Bani, the son of Shemer, 47the son of Mahli, the son of Mushi, the son of Merari, the son of Levi.

Work of the Priests

48Their brothers the Levites were given to all the work of the tent, the house of God. 49Aaron and his sons did all the work of the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. They burned gifts on the altar for burnt offerings and on the incense altar. They did the work to make Israel right with God, as Moses, God's servant, had told them.

50These were Aaron's sons: Eleazar, then Phinehas, then Abishua, 51then Bukki, then Uzzi, then Zerahiah, 52then Meraioth, then Amariah, then Ahitub, 53then Zadok, then Ahimaaz.

Towns for the Priests

54These were their towns with their borders. The sons of Aaron, the Kohathites, got these towns by lot. 55They were given Hebron in Judah, with its fields around it. 56But the fields of the city and its villages were given to Caleb son of Jephunneh.

57To the sons of Aaron they gave Hebron, a safe city, and Libnah with its fields, and Jattir and Eshtemoa with their fields. 58They also gave Hilen with its fields, Debir with its fields, 59Ashan with its fields, and Beth-shemesh with its fields. 60From Benjamin's tribe they gave Geba with its fields, Alemeth with its fields, and Anathoth with its fields. That was thirteen towns for their families.

Towns for Other Levites

61The rest of Kohath's families got ten towns by lot from half of Manasseh's tribe. 62The sons of Gershom got thirteen towns from Issachar's tribe, Asher's tribe, Naphtali's tribe, and half of Manasseh's tribe in Bashan. 63The sons of Merari got twelve towns by lot from Reuben's tribe, Gad's tribe, and Zebulun's tribe. 64The people of Israel gave these towns and their fields to the Levites. 65They gave by lot these towns named here from Judah's tribe, Simeon's tribe, and Benjamin's tribe.

66Some Kohath families got towns from Ephraim's tribe. 67They gave them Shechem, a safe city, in Ephraim's hill country, and Gezer with its fields, 68Jokmeam with its fields, Beth-horon with its fields, 69Aijalon with its fields, and Gath-rimmon with its fields. 70From half of Manasseh's tribe, they gave Aner with its fields and Bileam with its fields to the rest of Kohath's families.

71To Gershom's sons from half of Manasseh's tribe, they gave Golan in Bashan with its fields and Ashtaroth with its fields. 72From Issachar's tribe, they gave Kedesh with its fields, Daberath with its fields, 73Ramoth with its fields, and Anem with its fields. 74From Asher's tribe, they gave Mashal with its fields, Abdon with its fields, 75Hukok with its fields, and Rehob with its fields. 76From Naphtali's tribe, they gave Kedesh in Galilee with its fields, Hammon with its fields, and Kiriathaim with its fields.

77To the rest of Merari's sons from Zebulun's tribe, they gave Rimmono with its fields and Tabor with its fields. 78From Reuben's tribe, across the Jordan east of Jericho, they gave Bezer in the desert with its fields, Jahzah with its fields, 79Kedemoth with its fields, and Mephaath with its fields. 80From Gad's tribe, they gave Ramoth in Gilead with its fields, Mahanaim with its fields, 81Heshbon with its fields, and Jazer with its fields.
