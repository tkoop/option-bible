Rehoboam Goes to Shechem

1Rehoboam went to Shechem. All Israel had gone there to make him king. 2Jeroboam son of Nebat heard about this. He was still in Egypt. He had run away from King Solomon. He had been living there.

The People Ask for Lighter Work

3The people sent for Jeroboam. He and all Israel came to Rehoboam. They said, 4"YouryourYour father made our work very hard. Please make it lighter. Make his hard rules easier. Then we will serve you."

Rehoboam Asks for Advice

5Rehoboam said, "Go away for three days. Then come back to me." So the people left. 6King Rehoboam asked the older men. They had served his father Solomon. He asked, "How do you say I should answer these people?"

7They said, "If you are kind to these people today, and serve them, and speak good words to them, they will serve you always."

8But Rehoboam did not listen to the old men. He asked the young men who grew up with him. They were near him. 9He said, "What do you say? We need to answer the people. They said to make the load lighter that your father put on us."

10The young men who grew up with him said, "Tell these people who spoke to you that your father made our load heavy and you should make it lighter. Tell them that I am stronger than my father." 11My father put a heavy load on you. I will make it even heavier. My father hit you with whips. I will hit you with sharp whips."

Rehoboam Answers the People

12On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam. This was what the king had said: "Come back to me on the third day." 13The king answered the people in a harsh way. He did not follow the old men's advice. 14He spoke to them the way the young men said. He said, "MymyMy father made your load heavy. I will make it even heavier. My father hit you with whips. I will hit you with sharp whips."

The Kingdom Splits

15The king did not listen to the people. This happened because the LORD planned it. He wanted to keep HishisHis word. He had spoken this word to Jeroboam son of Nebat through Ahijah the prophet from Shiloh.

16All Israel saw that the king did not listen to them. So they answered the king, "What part do we have with David? We have no share with Jesse's son. Go home, Israel! David, take care of your own family." Then Israel went home.

17But Rehoboam still ruled over the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah. 18King Rehoboam sent Adoram. Adoram was in charge of forced work. All Israel stoned him, and he died. King Rehoboam quickly got into his chariot. He ran away to Jerusalem.

19So Israel turned away from David's family. They are still against David's family today. 20All Israel heard that Jeroboam had come back. They sent for him and made him king over all Israel. Only the tribe of Judah stayed with David's family.

Rehoboam Tries to Fight

21Rehoboam came to Jerusalem. He gathered all Judah and the tribe of Benjamin. There were 180,000 chosen men. They were ready to fight. They wanted to fight Israel. They wanted to give the kingdom back to Rehoboam son of Solomon.

God Stops the Fight

22But God's word came to Shemaiah, a man of God. 23He said, "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah. Speak to all Judah and Benjamin. Speak to the rest of the people. 24Say that the LORD says not to go up. Do not fight your brothers, the Israelites. Each man should go home, because the LORD planned it." The people listened to the LORD's word. They went home, just as the LORD said.

Jeroboam Makes Gold Calves

25Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim. He lived there. Then he left and built Penuel. 26Jeroboam said in his heart, "ThetheThe kingdom may go back to David's family. 27If these people go up to the LORD's house in Jerusalem to give gifts, their hearts may turn back to their king. They may turn back to Rehoboam, king of Judah. Then they may kill me and go back to Rehoboam, king of Judah."

28So the king asked for advice. Then he made two gold calves. He said to the people, "It is too far for you to go up to Jerusalem. Look! These are your gods, Israel. They brought you up out of Egypt."

29He put one calf in Bethel. He put the other in Dan. 30This became a sin. The people went to worship the calf in Dan. 31Jeroboam built places to worship on the hills. He made priests from all kinds of people. They were not from Levi's family.

Jeroboam Makes New Feasts

32Jeroboam made a feast. It was on the fifteenth day of the eighth month. It was like the feast in Judah. He offered gifts on the altar. He did this in Bethel. He gave gifts to the calves he had made. He put priests on the hill altars he had made. 33On the fifteenth day of the eighth month, a month he chose for himself, he offered gifts on the altar he made in Bethel. He made a feast for Israel. He went up to the altar to burn sweet smoke.
