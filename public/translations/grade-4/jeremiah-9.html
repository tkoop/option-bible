Jeremiah Wishes He Could Cry More

1I wish my head were full of water. I wish my eyes made tears like a spring. Then I could cry day and night for my people who were killed. 2If I had a small house in the desert, I would leave my people. They all break their promises. They are not true to God.

The LORD Says His People Are Not True

3"They use their words like bows to shoot lies. Lies win over truth in the land. They go from one bad thing to another. They do not know MemeMe. 4Be careful with your friends. Do not trust your brothers. Every brother tricks people. Every friend tells lies about others. 5Each person tricks the person next door. They do not tell the truth. They have taught their tongues to lie. They work hard to do wrong. 6You live among lies. In their tricks, they refuse to know MemeMe," says the LORD.

The LORD Will Test His People

7So the LORD of Armies says: "Look, I will clean them like metal in fire. I will test them. What else can I do for MymyMy people? 8Their tongues are like deadly arrows. They speak lies. With their mouths they say peace to a friend, but in their hearts they plan a trap. 9Should I not punish them for these things? Should I not pay them back, this nation?" says the LORD.

The Land Will Be Empty

10I will cry and wail for the mountains. I will sing a sad song for the grass in the wild places. They are burned, so no one walks there. No cows are heard. The birds and animals have run away. They are gone.

11"I will make Jerusalem a pile of rocks. Wild dogs will live there. I will make the cities of Judah empty. No one will live there," says the LORD.

Why the Land Is Ruined

12Who is wise enough to understand this? Who has heard the LORD speak, so he can tell it? Why is the land ruined and burned like a desert, so no one walks through?

13The LORD says, "They left MymyMy law, which I gave them. They did not live by it. They did not obey MymyMy voice. 14Instead, they followed their own stubborn hearts. They worshiped the false gods called Baal. Their fathers taught them to do this."

The LORD Will Scatter His People

15So the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I will feed these people bitter food. I will give them poisoned water to drink. 16I will scatter them among nations they and their fathers did not know. I will send war after them until they are gone," says the LORD.

Call the Women Who Cry

17The LORD of Armies says: "Think about this. Call the women who cry at funerals. Send for them. Let them come. 18Let them come fast. Let them cry for us. Then our eyes will fill with tears. Our eyelids will pour water. 19A crying voice is heard from Jerusalem: 'We are ruined! We are very ashamed! We had to leave the land because our homes were torn down.'"

Teach the Girls to Cry

20Now, women, listen to the word of the LORD. Let your ears hear what HeheHe says. Teach your daughters to cry. Teach each other to sing sad songs. 21Death has climbed in through our windows. It has come into our big houses. It takes children from the streets and young men from the town squares.

22Say this: "The LORD says, 'Dead bodies will fall like waste in the open field. They will be like cut grain left behind the worker. No one will pick them up.'"

Do Not Brag About Yourself

23The LORD says, "Do not let a wise man brag about his wisdom. Do not let a strong man brag about his strength. Do not let a rich man brag about his money. 24But if someone brags, let him brag about this: He understands MemeMe and knows MemeMe. I am the LORD. I show love, do what is right, and make things fair on EarthEarthearth. I am happy with these things," says the LORD.

The LORD Will Punish All People

25"Look, the days are coming," says the LORD. "I will punish all who have the outside mark but not the inside change. 26Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab, and all who live in the desert and cut the corners of their hair. All the nations are not truly changed. All Israel is not changed in heart."
