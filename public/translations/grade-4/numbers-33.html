The Israelites Leave Egypt

1Here are the places where the Israelites went. They left Egypt in groups. Moses and Aaron led them. 2Moses wrote down each place they left and each place they stopped. He did this because the the LORD told him to. 3They left Rameses on the fifteenth day of the first month. That was the day after the Passover feast. The Israelites marched out bravely where all the Egyptians could see them. 4The Egyptians were burying their firstborn sons, whom the the LORD had killed. The LORD had also punished their gods.

Their Journey Through the Desert

5The Israelites left Rameses and camped at Succoth. 6They left Succoth and camped at Etham, at the edge of the desert. 7They left Etham and turned back to Pi-hahiroth, near Baal-zephon. They camped near Migdol. 8They left Pi-hahiroth and crossed the sea into the desert. They walked three days in the desert of Etham and camped at Marah. 9They left Marah and came to Elim. There were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees there. They camped there.

10They left Elim and camped by the Red SeaseaSea. 11They left the Red SeaseaSea and camped in the desert of Sin. 12They left the desert of Sin and camped at Dophkah. 13They left Dophkah and camped at Alush. 14They left Alush and camped at Rephidim. There was no water there for the people to drink. 15They left Rephidim and camped in the desert of Sinai.

16They left the desert of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah. 17They left Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth. 18They left Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah. 19They left Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez. 20They left Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah. 21They left Libnah and camped at Rissah. 22They left Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.

23They left Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shapher. 24They left Mount Shapher and camped at Haradah. 25They left Haradah and camped at Makheloth. 26They left Makheloth and camped at Tahath. 27They left Tahath and camped at Terah. 28They left Terah and camped at Mithkah. 29They left Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah.

30They left Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth. 31They left Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan. 32They left Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad. 33They left Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah. 34They left Jotbathah and camped at Abronah. 35They left Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber. 36They left Ezion-geber and camped in the desert of Zin at Kadesh.

Aaron Dies on Mount Hor

37They left Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, on the edge of Edom. 38Aaron the priest went up Mount Hor because the the LORD told him to. He died there. This was in the fortieth year after the Israelites left Egypt. It was on the first day of the fifth month. 39Aaron was one hundred twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.

40The king of Arad was a Canaanite. He lived in the south part of Canaan. He heard that the Israelites were coming. 41The Israelites left Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.

42They left Zalmonah and camped at Punon. 43They left Punon and camped at Oboth. 44They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, on the border of Moab. 45They left Iye-abarim and camped at Dibon-gad. 46They left Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim. 47They left Almon-diblathaim and camped in the mountains of Abarim, near Nebo. 48They left the mountains of Abarim and camped on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho. 49They camped by the Jordan River from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim.

The LORD Gives Rules for the Promised Land

50The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho. 51He said, "Tell the Israelites this: When you cross the Jordan into Canaan, you must make the people who live there leave. You must break their carved statues and their metal statues. You must tear down the places where people worshiped false gods. Then you will take the land and live there. I gave this land to you to own. You will divide the land by drawing lots for your families. You will give more land to a big family and less to a small family. Each family will get the land where its lot falls. But if you do not make the people leave, the ones you let stay will be like thorns in your eyes and sharp sticks in your sides. They will trouble you in the land where you live. Then I will do to you what I planned to do to them."
