What faith is

1Faith means being sure of what we hope for. It means knowing that things we cannot see are real. 2Long ago, people were praised for trusting God. 3By faith we know that God made the world by HishisHis word. What we see was not made from things we can see.

Abel and Enoch

4By faith Abel gave God a better gift than Cain. God said Abel was right, because God accepted his gift. Abel still speaks, even though he died. 5By faith Enoch was taken away so he did not die. No one could find him, because God took him. Before that, he was known for pleasing God. 6Without faith, no one can please God. Anyone who comes to God must believe that HeheHe is real. They must also believe that HeheHe rewards people who look for HimhimHim.

Noah trusted God

7By faith Noah was warned about things he had not yet seen. He was careful and built a big boat to save his family. By doing this, he showed the world was wrong. He became right with God because of his faith.

Abraham obeyed God

8By faith Abraham obeyed when God called him to go to a place he would receive. He left, even though he did not know where he was going. 9By faith he lived in the land God promised, like a stranger. He lived in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who would share the same promise. 10He was waiting for a city with strong walls, made by God.

11By faith Sarah, even though she could not have children and was too old, got power to have a baby. She trusted God, who had promised. 12So from one man, almost as good as dead, came many people. They were as many as the stars in the sky and the sand by the sea.

They looked for a better home

13All these people died still trusting. They did not get what was promised yet. But they saw it far away and welcomed it. They said they were strangers and travelers on EarthEarthearth. 14People who say such things show they are looking for a home country. 15If they had kept thinking about the country they left, they could have gone back. 16But they want a better country, a heavenly one. So God is not ashamed to be called their God. He has made a city for them.

Abraham offered Isaac

17By faith Abraham, when tested, offered Isaac. The man who had received the promises offered his only son. 18God had said, "Through Isaac your children will be called." 19Abraham thought God could raise the dead. So he got Isaac back, like a picture of rising from the dead.

Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph

20By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau about the future. 21By faith Jacob, when dying, blessed each of Joseph's sons. He worshiped, leaning on his staff. 22By faith Joseph, near the end of his life, spoke about the Israelites leaving Egypt. He gave orders about his bones.

Moses chose God's people

23By faith Moses' parents hid him for three months after he was born. They saw he was a beautiful child. They were not afraid of the king's rule. 24By faith Moses, when grown, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter. 25He chose to suffer with God's people instead of enjoying sin for a short time. 26He thought being made fun of for Christ was worth more than Egypt's treasures. He looked ahead to the reward. 27By faith he left Egypt and was not afraid of the king's anger. He kept going because he saw the OneoneOne who cannot be seen. 28By faith he kept the Passover and put blood on the doors. Then the one who killed the firstborn would not touch them. 29By faith the people crossed the Red SeaseaSea on dry ground. The Egyptians tried it and were drowned.

More heroes of faith

30By faith the walls of Jericho fell after the people marched around them for seven days. 31By faith Rahab the prostitute welcomed the spies in peace. She did not die with those who did not obey. 32What more can I say? Time would run out if I told about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets. 33Through faith they won kingdoms, did right, got promises, and stopped lions from hurting them. 34They put out fire, escaped swords, became strong from weakness, were strong in war, and turned back enemy armies.

35Women got their loved ones back when God raised them from the dead. Others were hurt badly and did not accept freedom, so they could have a better rising from the dead. 36Others were laughed at, beaten, chained, and put in prison. 37They were hit with stones, cut in two, and killed with swords. They wore sheepskins and goatskins. They were poor, hurt, and treated badly. 38The world was not good enough for them. They wandered in deserts, mountains, caves, and holes in the ground.

God's better plan

39All these people were praised for their faith, but they did not get what was promised. 40God had planned something better for us, so they would not be made complete without us.
