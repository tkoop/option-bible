Josiah Makes a Promise to God

1The king sent for all the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem. They came to him. 2The king went up to the house of the the LORD with all the men of Judah. All the people of Jerusalem went with him. The priests and prophets went too. All the people, young and old, were there. He read aloud all the words of the BookbookBook of the Agreement that had been found in the house of the the LORD. 3The king stood by a pillar. He made an agreement before the the LORD. He promised to follow the the LORD and to keep HishisHis rules, HishisHis laws, and HishisHis commands with all his heart and all his soul. He promised to do what this agreement said. All the people agreed to keep the agreement.

Josiah Cleans the Temple

4The king told Hilkiah the high priest, the other priests, and the doorkeepers to bring out of the the LORD's temple all the things made for Baal, for Asherah, and for everything in the sky. He burned them outside Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron. He carried their ashes to Bethel. 5He stopped the false priests. Judah's kings had chosen them to burn incense on the high places in Judah's towns and around Jerusalem. He also stopped those who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, to the moon, to the stars, and to everything in the sky. 6He brought the wooden idol of Asherah out of the the LORD's house. He took it to the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley outside Jerusalem. He burned it there. He ground it to dust. He threw the dust on the graves of the common people. 7He tore down the rooms in the the LORD's house where men sold themselves for false worship. Women wove cloth there for the Asherah idol.

Josiah Stops False Worship

8He brought all the priests from Judah's towns. He ruined the high places where priests had burned incense. This was from Geba to Beersheba. He also broke down the high places at the gates. These were at the entrance of Joshua's gate. Joshua was the city leader. The high places were on the left side of the city gate. 9But the priests of the high places did not serve at the the LORD's altar in Jerusalem. They ate bread without yeast with their fellow priests. 10He made Topheth unclean. Topheth was in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom. He did this so no one would burn his son or daughter as a sacrifice to Molech.

11He took away the horses that Judah's kings had given to the sun god. They were at the entrance to the the LORD's house. They were near the room of Nathan-Melech, an officer in the courtyard. He burned the sun chariots. 12The kings of Judah had made altars on the roof of Ahaz's upper room. Manasseh had made altars in the two courtyards of the the LORD's house. The king broke them down and crushed them. He threw their dust into the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 13The king made unclean the high places east of Jerusalem. They were south of the Mount of Corruption. Solomon king of Israel had built them for Ashtoreth, the false god of the Sidonians. He also built them for Chemosh, the false god of Moab, and for Milcom, the false god of the Ammonites. 14He broke the stone pillars. He cut down the wooden idols. He filled their places with human bones.

Josiah Breaks the Altar at Bethel

15He also broke down the altar at Bethel and the high place. Jeroboam son of Nebat had made them. Jeroboam led Israel to sin. Josiah broke down the altar and the high place. He burned the high place and crushed it to dust. He burned the Asherah pole. 16Josiah turned and saw tombs on the mountain. He sent men to take bones from the tombs. They burned them on the altar. This made the altar unclean. It happened just as the the LORD had said through the man of God. That man had said these words long ago.

17Josiah said, “What tombstone is this that I see?”

The men of the city told him, “It is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah. He said these things would happen to the altar at Bethel. You have done them.”

18Josiah said, “Leave him alone. Do not move his bones.” So they left his bones alone, along with the bones of the prophet who came from Samaria.

19Josiah also took away all the shrines of the high places in Samaria's towns. Israel's kings had made them to make the the LORD angry. He did to them the same things he had done in Bethel. 20He killed all the priests of the high places there on the altars. He burned human bones on them. Then he went back to Jerusalem.

The People Keep the Passover

21The king told all the people, “Celebrate the Passover for the the LORD your God. Do it the way this BookbookBook of the Agreement says.”

22No Passover like this had been held since the days of the judges who led Israel. It had not been held in all the days of Israel's kings or Judah's kings. 23In the eighteenth year of King Josiah, this Passover was held for the the LORD in Jerusalem.

Josiah Gets Rid of More Wrong Worship

24Josiah also got rid of people who talked to spirits and mediums. He got rid of household gods, idols, and all the terrible things seen in Judah and Jerusalem. He did this to obey the words of the law written in the book. Hilkiah the priest had found that book in the the LORD's house. 25No king before him or after him was like him. He turned to the the LORD with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength. He followed all the LawlawLaw of Moses.

God Still Plans to Punish Judah

26But the the LORD did not stop being angry. His great anger burned against Judah because of all the things Manasseh had done to make HimhimHim angry. 27The ThetheThe LORD said, “I will take Judah away from MymyMy sight, just as I took Israel away. I will reject this city of Jerusalem that I chose. I will reject the house where I said, ‘My name will be there.’”

Josiah Dies and New Kings Rule

28The rest of what Josiah did is written in the book of the history of Judah's kings. 29While Josiah was king, Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt went up to fight the king of Assyria at the Euphrates River. King Josiah went to meet him. Pharaoh Necho killed Josiah at Megiddo. 30Josiah's servants carried him in a chariot from Megiddo. They brought him to Jerusalem. They buried him in his own tomb. The people of the land took Jehoahaz, Josiah's son. They anointed him and made him king after his father.

31Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king. He ruled three months in Jerusalem. His mother was Hamutal. She was the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah. 32He did evil in the the LORD's eyes. He did what his fathers had done. 33Pharaoh Necho put Jehoahaz in prison at Riblah in the land of Hamath. This kept him from ruling in Jerusalem. Pharaoh also made the land pay one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold. 34Pharaoh Necho made Eliakim, Josiah's son, king instead of Jehoahaz. He changed Eliakim's name to Jehoiakim. He took Jehoahaz to Egypt, and Jehoahaz died there.

35Jehoiakim gave the silver and gold to Pharaoh. But he taxed the land to get the money. He made the people of the land pay silver and gold, each man by his own value, to give to Pharaoh Necho. 36Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king. He ruled eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zebidah. She was the daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah. 37He did evil in the the LORD's eyes. He did what his fathers had done.
