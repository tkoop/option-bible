Hananiah Makes a False Promise

1In the fifth month of that year, early in King Zedekiah’s rule over Judah, the prophet Hananiah, son of Azzur from Gibeon, spoke to me in the the LORD’s house. The priests and all the people were there. 2He said, “This is what the the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: ‘I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon. 3In two years I will bring back to this place all the things from the the LORD’s house that King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took away and carried to Babylon. 4I will also bring back Jehoiachin son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and all the people of Judah who were taken to Babylon,’ says the the LORD. ‘I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.’”

Jeremiah Answers Hananiah

5Then the prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah in front of the priests and all the people standing in the the LORD’s house. 6He said, “Amen! May the the LORD do it! May the the LORD make your words come true. May HeheHe bring back the things from the the LORD’s house and all the people who were taken, from Babylon to this place. 7But please listen to what I say to you and to all the people. 8Long ago, prophets who came before us said there would be war, trouble, and sickness for many lands and big kingdoms. 9But a prophet who says there will be peace is known to be truly sent by the the LORD only when his words come true.”

Hananiah Breaks the Yoke

10Then Hananiah took the yoke off Jeremiah’s neck and broke it. 11He said before all the people, “This is what the the LORD says: ‘In the same way, I will break the yoke of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon off the necks of all the countries in two years.’” Then Jeremiah went on his way.

God Sends a Message to Hananiah

12After Hananiah broke the yoke off Jeremiah’s neck, the the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. 13The LORD said, “Go and tell Hananiah, ‘This is what the the LORD says: You broke a wooden yoke, but now you will have an iron yoke. 14I have put an iron yoke on the necks of all these countries. They must serve King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, and they will serve him. I will even give him the wild animals.’”

15Then Jeremiah said to Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! The the LORD did not send you. But you have made these people trust in lies. 16So the the LORD says: ‘I am about to take you away from the earth. You will die this year, because you spoke against the the LORD.’”

Hananiah Dies

17In the seventh month of that year, the prophet Hananiah died.
