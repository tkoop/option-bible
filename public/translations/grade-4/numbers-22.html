Israel Camps in Moab

1The people of Israel travelledtravelledtraveled and camped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

Balak Is Afraid of Israel

2Balak son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites. 3Moab was very afraid of the people, because there were so many of them. Moab dreaded the Israelites. 4Moab said to the elders of Midian, “This crowd will eat up everything around us, like an ox eats grass in a field.” Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.

Balak Sends for Balaam

5Balak sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor in Pethor by the river, in the land of his people. They were to call him. They said, “A people came out of Egypt. They cover the face of the earth, and they are staying near me. 6Please come and curse this people for me, because they are too strong for me. Maybe I can beat them and push them out of the land. I know that whoever you bless is blessed, and whoever you curse is cursed.”

7The elders of Moab and Midian went with money to pay for magic. They came to Balaam and spoke Balak's words to him. 8Balaam said, “Stay here tonight. I will tell you what the LORD says to me.” So the princes of Moab stayed with Balaam.

Balaam Talks with God

9God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?”

10Balaam said to God, “Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, sent them to me. 11Look, a people came out of Egypt. They cover the face of the earth. Now come and curse them for me. Maybe I can fight them and drive them out.”

12God said to Balaam, “Do not go with them. Do not curse the people, because they are blessed.”

13In the morning Balaam got up and said to Balak's princes, “Go back to your land, because the LORD will not let me go with you.”

14So the princes of Moab got up and went to Balak. They said, “Balaam will not come with us.”

Balak Sends More Men

15Then Balak sent more princes. They were greater and more important than the first ones. 16They came to Balaam and said, “Balak son of Zippor asks you not to refuse to come to him. 17He will honourhonourhonor you very much, and he will do whatever you say. Please come and curse this people for him.”

18Balaam answered Balak's servants, “Even if Balak gave me his whole house full of silver and gold, I could not go past the word of the LORD my God, to do anything small or big. 19Now please stay here tonight, so I can find out what else the LORD will say to me.”

20That night God came to Balaam and said, “If the men came to call you, get up and go with them. But do only what I tell you.”

21In the morning Balaam got up, saddled his donkey, and went with the princes of Moab.

Balaam's Donkey Sees the Angel

22But God was angry because Balaam went. The angel of the LORD stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam was riding his donkey, and his two servants were with him.

23The donkey saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a sword in his hand. The donkey turned off the road and went into a field. Balaam hit the donkey to turn her back onto the road.

24Then the angel of the LORD stood in a narrow path between vineyards, with a wall on each side.

25The donkey saw the angel of the LORD and pressed against the wall. She crushed Balaam's foot against the wall, and he hit her again.

26Then the angel of the LORD moved ahead and stood in a tight place where there was no room to turn right or left.

27The donkey saw the angel of the LORD and lay down under Balaam. Balaam got angry and hit the donkey with his stick.

28Then the LORD opened the donkey's mouth, and she said to Balaam, “What have I done to you, that you hit me these three times?”

29Balaam said to the donkey, “You made a fool of me! If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you now.”

30The donkey said to Balaam, “Am I not your donkey, the one you have ridden all your life? Have I ever done this to you before?”

Balaam said, “No.”

31Then the LORD opened Balaam's eyes, and he saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a sword in his hand. Balaam bowed down and fell on his face.

32The angel of the LORD said to him, “Why did you hit your donkey three times? Look, I came out to oppose you, because your way is wrong before me. 33The donkey saw me and turned away three times. If she had not turned away, I would have killed you and let her live.”

34Balaam said to the angel of the LORD, “I have sinned, because I did not know you were standing in the road against me. Now if it is bad in your eyes, I will go back.”

35The angel of the LORD said to Balaam, “Go with the men, but speak only the word I tell you.” So Balaam went with Balak's princes.

Balaam Meets Balak

36Balak heard that Balaam had come. He went out to meet him at a city of Moab on the border of the Arnon, at the edge of his land.

37Balak said to Balaam, “Did I not send for you? Why did you not come to me? Am I not able to honourhonourhonor you?”

38Balaam said to Balak, “Look, I have come to you. But can I say anything? I will speak only the word God puts in my mouth.”

39Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.

40Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and he sent some to Balaam and to the princes who were with him.

41In the morning Balak took Balaam and brought him up to Bamoth-baal. From there Balaam could see the edge of the Israelite camp.
