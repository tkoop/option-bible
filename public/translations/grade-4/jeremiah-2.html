God Speaks to Jeremiah

1The word of the the LORD came to me. He said,

God Remembers Israel’s Love

2“Go and tell Jerusalem this: The LORD says, ‘I remember how kind you were when you were young. I remember how you loved MemeMe when you were MymyMy bride. You followed MemeMe into the desert, into a land where no one plants.’”

3“Israel was special to the LORD. Israel was the first part of HishisHis harvest. Anyone who hurt Israel had to pay. Trouble came to them,” says the LORD.

God Asks Why Israel Left Him

4Hear the word of the LORD, family of Jacob. Hear it, all the families of Israel.

5The LORD says, “What wrong did your fathers find in MemeMe? Why did they go far from MemeMe? They followed worthless things and became worthless.”

6They did not ask, “Where is the LORD? He brought us up from Egypt. He led us through the desert, through a dry and dark land, through a land where no one lives and no one walks.”

7“I brought you into a good land to eat its fruit and its good things. But you came and made MymyMy land dirty. You made the gift I gave you into something awful.”

8The priests did not ask, “Where is the LORD?” The men who knew God’s law did not know MemeMe. The leaders turned against MemeMe. The prophets spoke for Baal. They followed things that do not help.

God Will Bring Charges

9“So I will still bring charges against you,” says the LORD. “I will bring charges against your children and your grandchildren. 10Go across the sea to the lands of Kittim and look. Send to Kedar and look hard. See if anything like this has ever happened.”

Israel Traded Its Glory

11Has a nation ever changed its gods? They are not even real gods. But MymyMy people traded the honourhonourhonor I gave them for something useless.

12“Be shocked at this, you heavens. Be very afraid and be ruined,” says the LORD.

13“My people did two bad things. They left MemeMe, the source of living water. They dug broken wells that cannot hold water.”

Israel Is Robbed and Shamed

14Is Israel a slave? Was he born a slave? Why has he been robbed?

15Young lions roar at him. They raise their voices. They make his land empty. His cities are burned. No one lives there.

16The people of Noph and Tahpanhes will hurt you and shame you.

Israel Left God and Got Nothing

17“Have you not brought this on yourself? You left the LORD your God when HeheHe led you in the way.”

18“What do you gain by going to Egypt to drink the water of the Nile? What do you gain by going to Assyria to drink the water of the Euphrates?”

19“Your own bad choices will punish you. Your turning away will correct you. Know and see that it is bad and bitter to leave the LORD your God. You do not fear MemeMe,” says the Lord the LORD of armies.

Israel Was Not Faithful

20“Long ago I took off your heavy load. I broke your chains. But you said, ‘I will not serve.’ On every high hill and under every green tree, you were not faithful to MemeMe.”

21“I planted you like a good grapevine. You were all true. How did you turn into a wild vine and become useless to MemeMe?”

22“Even if you wash with strong soap and use lots of cleaner, your guilt is still in front of MemeMe,” says the Lord the LORD.

Israel Made Excuses

23“How can you say, ‘I am not dirty. I did not follow the Baals’? Look at what you did in the valley. See what you have done. You are like a fast young camel running here and there.”

24A wild donkey used to the desert sniffs the wind when she wants a mate. Who can turn her back? All who look for her will not get tired. They will find her when she is ready.

25“Keep your feet from going bare. Keep your throat from getting dry. But you said, ‘It is no use. I love strangers, and I will follow them.’”

Israel Was Caught in Sin

26As a thief is ashamed when he is caught, so the family of Israel is ashamed. Their kings, leaders, priests, and prophets are ashamed too.

27They say to a piece of wood, “You are my father.” They say to a stone, “You gave birth to me.” They turn their backs to MemeMe, not their faces. But when trouble comes, they say, “Get up and save us!”

28“Where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them get up if they can save you when you are in trouble. You have as many gods as you have cities, Judah.”

The People Argued with God

29“Why do you argue with MemeMe? You have all turned against MemeMe,” says the LORD.

30“I hit your children for nothing. They did not learn. Your sword has eaten your prophets like a destroying lion.”

31“You people, see the word of the LORD. Have I been a desert to Israel? Have I been a land of deep darkness? Why do MymyMy people say, ‘We are free. We will not come to YouyouYou anymore’?”

Israel Forgot God

32Does a young woman forget her jewelleryjewelleryjewelry? Does a bride forget her wedding clothes? But MymyMy people have forgotten MemeMe for days without number.

33“How well you get ready to look for love! Even bad women you have taught your ways.”

34“On your clothes is found the blood of innocent poor people. You did not catch them breaking in. Yet you did all these things.”

God Will Judge Israel

35“You say, ‘I am innocent. His anger has turned away from me.’ But I will judge you because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’”

36“Why do you run around so much to change your way? You will be put to shame by Egypt, just as you were put to shame by Assyria.”

37“You will also go out from there with your hands on your head. The LORD has turned away from those you trusted. You will not succeed with them.”
