Rules for the New Land

1These are the rules and laws the the LORD your God told me to teach you. You will follow them in the land you are going to take.

2Then you and your children and grandchildren will respect the the LORD your God all the days of your life. You will keep all HishisHis rules that I give you. Then your days will last longer.

3Listen, Israel! Be careful to obey these rules. Then things will go well for you. You will grow in number in a good land. The LORD, the God of your fathers, promised this to you.

Love the LORD

4Listen, Israel! The LORD our God is the one and only the LORD.

5You must love the the LORD your God with all your heart, all your soul, and all your strength.

Keep These Words Close

6These words that I command you today must stay in your heart.

7Teach them carefully to your children. Talk about them when you sit at home, when you walk on the road, when you lie down, and when you get up.

8Tie them on your hand as a sign. Wear them on your forehead.

9Write them on the door frames of your house and on your gates.

Do Not Forget the LORD

10The LORD your God will bring you into the land HeheHe promised to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He will give you big, good cities that you did not build.

11You will live in houses full of good things that you did not fill. You will use wells that you did not dig. You will eat from vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant. You will eat and be full.

12Be careful not to forget the the LORD. He brought you out of Egypt. He brought you out of slavery.

13Respect the the LORD your God. Serve HimhimHim. Make promises using HishisHis name.

14Do not follow other gods. Do not follow the gods of the people around you.

15The the LORD your God is with you. He is a jealous God. Do not make HimhimHim angry, or HeheHe will destroy you from the land.

Do What Is Right

16Do not test the the LORD your God. You tested HimhimHim at Massah.

17Be careful to obey the commands of the the LORD your God. Obey HishisHis rules and laws that HeheHe gave you.

18Do what the the LORD says is right and good. Then things will go well for you. You will go in and take the good land the the LORD promised to your fathers.

19He will drive out all your enemies in front of you, as the the LORD said.

Talk to Children

20Later, your son will ask you, “What do these rules mean? The the LORD our God told you to obey them.”

21Then you will say to your son, “We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt. But the the LORD brought us out of Egypt with HishisHis strong hand.

“We saw the the LORD do big and scary signs and wonders in Egypt. He did them against Pharaoh and all his family.

“He brought us out of there. He led us to this land. He gave us the land HeheHe promised to our fathers.

“The ThetheThe LORD told us to obey all these rules. He told us to respect the the LORD our God. Then we will do well and live, like we do today.

“If we are careful to obey all these commands before the the LORD our God, just as HeheHe told us, then it will count as doing right for us.”
