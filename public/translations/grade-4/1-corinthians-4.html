Servants of Christ

1People should think of us as helpers of Christ and as keepers of God's secrets. 2Now those who keep things must be found faithful.

Do Not Judge Too Soon

3I care very little if you judge me, or if other people judge me. I do not even judge myself. 4My own mind is clear, but that does not make me right. The Lord is the one who judges me. 5So do not judge before the right time. Wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to light the things hidden in darkness. He will show the reasons in people's hearts. Then each person will receive praise from God.

Do Not Boast

6Brothers, I used myself and Apollos as an example for you. I did this so you can learn from us the words, "Do not go beyond what is written." Then you will not be proud of one leader against another. 7Who made you different? What do you have that you did not receive? If you did receive it, why do you brag like you did not? 8You already have all you want! You already have become rich! You have begun to rule without us! I wish you really had begun to rule, so we could rule with you!

Apostles Have a Hard Life

9It seems to me that God has shown us apostles last, like men sent to die. We have been made a show to the whole world, to angels, and to people. 10We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are honouredhonouredhonored, but we are not honouredhonouredhonored. 11Even now we are hungry and thirsty. We are poorly dressed. We are beaten. We have no home. 12We work hard with our own hands. When people say bad things about us, we bless them. When people hurt us, we put up with it. 13When people speak badly of us, we answer kindly. Even now we have become like the trash of the world, like dirt that everyone wipes away.

A Father's Warning

14I am not writing this to shame you. I am writing to warn you, because you are my dear children. 15Even if you had ten thousand teachers in Christ, you do not have many fathers. In Christ Jesus, I became your father through the good news. 16So I beg you to copy me.

Timothy Will Come

17That is why I sent Timothy to you. He is my dear and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my way of life in Christ Jesus. I teach this same way in every church everywhere. 18Some of you have become proud. You act as if I am not coming to you. 19But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wants. Then I will find out not just the words of those proud people, but their power. 20For God's kingdom is not about words. It is about power. 21What do you want? Should I come to you with a stick, or with love and a gentle spirit?
