Elisha tells the woman to leave

1Elisha said to the woman whose son he brought back to life, “Get up. Take your family and go live where you can. The LORD says there will be no food in this land for seven years.” 2The woman got up and did what God’s man said. She took her family and lived in the land of the Philistines for seven years.

3After seven years, the woman came back from the land of the Philistines. She went to the king to ask for her house and her land back.

The woman gets her land back

4The king was talking to Gehazi, the helper of God’s man. He said, “Tell me all the big things Elisha has done.” 5While Gehazi told the king how Elisha brought a dead man back to life, the woman came to ask the king for her house and land. Gehazi said, “My lord the king, this is the woman. This is her son. Elisha brought him back to life.” 6The king asked the woman about it. She told him. Then the king told one of his officers, “Give back everything that was hers. Give back all the food from her fields from the day she left until now.”

Elisha goes to Damascus

7Elisha went to Damascus. Ben-hadad, the king of Aram, was sick. People told him, “God’s man has come here.”

Hazael asks about the king

8The king said to Hazael, “Take a gift with you. Go meet God’s man. Ask the LORD through him, ‘Will I get better from this sickness?’” 9Hazael went to meet him. He took a gift with him: forty camel loads of the best things from Damascus. He stood in front of Elisha and said, “Your son Ben-hadad, king of Aram, sent me to you. He asks, ‘Will I get better from this sickness?’” 10Elisha said to him, “Go tell him, ‘You will get better.’ But the LORD has shown me that he will die.” 11Elisha stared at Hazael until Hazael felt ashamed. Then God’s man cried. 12Hazael said, “Why are you crying, my lord?” Elisha said, “Because I know the bad things you will do to the people of Israel. You will burn their strong towns. You will kill their young men with swords. You will kill their little children and hurt their pregnant women.” 13Hazael said, “I am only a dog. How could I do such a big thing?” Elisha said, “The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Aram.”

Hazael becomes king

14Hazael left Elisha and went back to his master. The king asked, “What did Elisha say to you?” Hazael said, “He told me you would get better.” 15The next day Hazael took a blanket, dipped it in water, and put it over Ben-hadad’s face. Ben-hadad died. Then Hazael became king.

Jehoram rules Judah

16In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat became king of Judah. Jehoshaphat was king of Judah then. 17Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for eight years. 18He followed the bad ways of the kings of Israel, like Ahab’s family. He married Ahab’s daughter. He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD. 19But the LORD did not want to destroy Judah. He kept HishisHis promise to David. He promised that someone from David’s family would always be king. 20While Jehoram was king, Edom broke free from Judah’s rule. They chose their own king. 21Jehoram went to Zair with all his war carts. The Edomites surrounded him and his army leaders. But he attacked at night and beat them. Then his army ran home. 22Edom is still free from Judah today. At that time, Libnah also broke free. 23The rest of what Jehoram did is written in the book of the kings of Judah. 24Jehoram died and was buried with his fathers in the city of David. His son Ahaziah became king.

Ahaziah rules Judah

25Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah in the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel. 26Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for one year. His mother was Athaliah. She was the granddaughter of Omri, king of Israel. 27He followed the bad ways of Ahab’s family. He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD, like Ahab’s family did. He was married into Ahab’s family. 28He went with Joram son of Ahab to fight Hazael, king of Aram, at Ramoth-gilead. The men of Aram hurt Joram. 29King Joram went back to Jezreel to heal from the wounds the men of Aram gave him at Ramah. He was fighting Hazael, king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went to Jezreel to see Joram son of Ahab, because Joram was sick.
