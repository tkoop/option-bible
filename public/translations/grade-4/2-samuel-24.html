David Counts His People

1The LORD was angry with Israel again. He caused David to count the people of Israel and Judah.

2The king said to Joab, the army leader, "Go through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba. Count the people so I will know how many there are."

3Joab said to the king, "May the LORD your God make the people a hundred times more. May my lord the king see it. But why does my lord the king want to do this?"

4The king's word won over Joab and the army leaders. So Joab and the leaders left the king and went to count the people of Israel.

5They crossed the Jordan River. They camped near Aroer, south of the city in the middle of the valley of Gad, toward Jazer. 6Then they went to Gilead and the land of Tahtim-hodshi. Then they went to Dan and around to Sidon. 7Then they went to the strong city of Tyre and to all the cities of the Hivites and Canaanites. Then they went out to the south of Judah at Beersheba. 8After nine months and twenty days, they came back to Jerusalem.

9Joab told the king how many people there were. Israel had eight hundred thousand brave men who used swords. Judah had five hundred thousand men.

David Is Sorry and Is Punished

10After that, David felt bad in his heart. He said to the LORD, "I have sinned a lot by doing this. Now, LORD, please take away my sin. I have acted foolishly."

11The next morning, the LORD spoke to Gad the prophet. 12He said, "Go tell David, 'The LORD says: I am giving you three choices. Pick one, and I will do it to you.'"

13Gad went to David and told him. He said, "Should seven years of hunger come to your land? Or should you run for three months while your enemies chase you? Or should three days of sickness come to your land? Now think and choose what answer I should take back to the OneoneOne who sent me."

14David said to Gad, "I am in big trouble. Let us fall into the LORD's hands, because HeheHe is very kind. But do not let me fall into people's hands."

15So the LORD sent sickness on Israel from morning until the set time. Seventy thousand men died from Dan to Beersheba.

16When the angel reached out his hand to destroy Jerusalem, the LORD felt sorry and stopped the trouble. He said to the angel who was hurting the people, "Stop! Put your hand down." The angel of the LORD was by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

17When David saw the angel hurting the people, he said to the LORD, "I am the one who sinned. I did wrong. These sheep, what did they do? Please let YouryourYour hand be against me and my family."

David Builds an Altar

18That day Gad came to David and said, "Go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

19David went up, just as Gad had said, as the LORD had told him.

20Araunah looked and saw the king and his servants coming. He went out and bowed with his face to the ground before the king.

21Araunah said, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?"

David said, "To buy the threshing floor from you, to build an altar to the LORD, so the sickness will stop hurting the people."

22Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take what he likes and offer it. Here are oxen for the offering. Here are the sledges and yokes for wood."

23Araunah gave all of it to the king. Araunah said to the king, "May the LORD your God be pleased with you."

24The king said to Araunah, "No, I will buy it from you for a price. I will not give the LORD my God an offering that cost me nothing." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty pieces of silver.

25David built an altar there to the LORD. He offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. The LORD answered the prayer for the land, and the sickness stopped hurting Israel.
