God's Agreement with His People

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 2The LORD said, "Listen to the words of this agreement. Tell them to the men of Judah and to the people who live in Jerusalem. 3Tell them that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Bad things will happen to the person who does not obey the words of this agreement. 4I gave this command to your fathers when I brought them out of Egypt. That place was like a hot iron furnace. I said, "Obey MemeMe. Do everything I tell you to do. Then you will be MymyMy people, and I will be your God." 5I made this promise to your fathers. I promised to give them a land with plenty of milk and honey. That is still true today.'"

Then I answered, "AmenamenAmen, the LORD."

6Then the LORD said to me, "Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Say, 'Listen to the words of this agreement and obey them.' 7From the time I brought your fathers out of Egypt until today, I have warned them again and again. I said, 'Obey MymyMy voice.' 8But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. Each person followed his own stubborn and evil heart. So I brought on them all the curses of this agreement that I commanded them to keep, but they did not keep them."

The People Break the Agreement

11So the LORD says, "I am going to bring trouble on them. They will not be able to escape it.
