He forgot HishisHis footstool on the day HeheHe was angry. 2The Lord did not feel sorry. He destroyed all of Jacob's homes. In HishisHis anger HeheHe tore down Judah's strong cities. He brought them to the ground and made them unclean. He ended the kingdom and its leaders. 3In fierce anger HeheHe took away all of Israel's strength. He pulled back HishisHis helping hand when the enemy came. He burned through Jacob like a fire that eats everything around. 4He bent HishisHis bow like an enemy. His right hand stood like a foe. He killed all their loved ones. In Zion's homes HeheHe poured out HishisHis anger like fire.

5The Lord became like an enemy. He destroyed Israel. He destroyed all her big houses and tore down her strong forts. He made much sadness and crying in Judah. 6He ruined HishisHis meeting tent like a garden. He destroyed HishisHis place to meet. The LORD made Zion forget her special days and Sabbaths. In fierce anger HeheHe rejected the king and the priest. 7The Lord rejected HishisHis altar. He left HishisHis holy place. He let the enemy take the walls of her palaces. They shouted in the LORD's house like on a special day.

Zion's Walls Fall

8The LORD planned to destroy Zion's wall. He stretched a measuring line and did not stop destroying. The wall and the fort both grew weak and wasted away. 9Her gates sank into the ground. He destroyed and broke their bars. Her king and leaders are among other nations. There is no law, and her prophets find no message from the the LORD.

The People Suffer

10The leaders of Zion sit on the ground and say nothing. They put dust on their heads and wore rough clothes. The young women of Jerusalem bowed their heads to the ground.

11My eyes are worn out from crying. My insides hurt. My heart is poured out on the ground because my people are broken. Little children and babies faint in the city streets.

12They ask their mothers, "Where is food and drink?" They faint in the city streets like hurt people. Their lives slip away in their mothers' arms.

No One Can Comfort Zion

13What can I say to you? What can I compare you to, Jerusalem? What can I say you are like, so I can comfort you, Zion? Your hurt is as big as the sea. Who can heal you?

14Your prophets told you false and empty things. They did not show your sin so you could come back home. They told you false and misleading things.

People Make Fun of Zion

15All who walk by clap their hands at you. They hiss and shake their heads at Jerusalem. They say, "IsisIs this the city they called the most beautiful, the joy of the whole EarthEarthearth?"

16All your enemies open their mouths against you. They hiss and grind their teeth. They say, "We destroyed her. This is the day we waited for. We lived to see it!"

17The LORD did what HeheHe planned. He kept HishisHis word from long ago. He tore you down and did not feel sorry. He let your enemies be happy over you. He made your enemies strong.

A Prayer for Help

18Their hearts cried out to the Lord. Zion's wall, let your tears run like a river day and night. Do not rest. Let your eyes overflow with tears.

19Get up and cry out in the night. At the start of the night watch, pour out your heart like water before the Lord. Lift your hands to HimhimHim for your children who faint from hunger on every street corner.

20Look, the LORD, and see. Have YouyouYou ever done this to anyone? Should mothers eat their own children, the little ones they care for? Should priests and prophets be killed in the Lord's holy place?

21Young and old lie together in the streets. My young men and girls fell by the sword. You killed them in the day of YouryourYour anger. You slaughtered them and did not feel sorry.

22You brought fears on me from every side, like on a special day. On the day of the LORD's anger, no one escaped or lived. My enemy destroyed the children I cared for and raised.
