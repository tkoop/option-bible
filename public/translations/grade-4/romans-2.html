God Judges Everyone Fairly

1You have no excuse, person. If you judge someone else, you judge yourself. You do the same things. 2We know God's judgmentjudgementjudgment is true for people who do these things. 3So when you, a person, judge people who do these things but do them too, do you think you will get away from God's judgmentjudgementjudgment? No.

4Or do you look down on God's great kindness, patience, and waiting? Do you not know that HishisHis kindness leads you to change your heart? 5But because of your hard heart that will not change, you are saving up anger for yourself. On the day God shows HishisHis fair judgmentjudgementjudgment, HishisHis anger will come.

God Will Pay Each Person

6God will pay each person for what they do. 7He will give life that never ends to people who keep doing good and want praise, respect, and a life that never ends. 8But HeheHe will give anger and strong anger to people who only want their own way, who do not obey the truth, but obey what is wrong. 9There will be trouble and pain for every person who does evil, first for the Jew, then for the Greek. 10But there will be praise, respect, and peace for everyone who does good, first for the Jew, then for the Greek. 11God does not play favorites.

People Without the Law

12All who sin without God's law will also be lost without the law. All who sin under God's law will be judged by the law. 13It is not the people who hear the law who are right with God. The people who do the law will be called right.

14When people who do not have God's law do by nature what the law says, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law. 15They show that the work of the law is written in their hearts. Their inner voice also speaks up. Their thoughts either blame them or clear them. 16This will happen on the day God judges the secret things of people through Jesus Christ. This is what my good news says.

True Jews and Circumcision

17Now you call yourself a Jew. You rest on the law. You brag about God. 18You know what HeheHe wants. You can tell good from bad. You learn this from the law. 19You think you are a guide for blind people, a light for people in the dark. 20You think you are a teacher for foolish people and a teacher for little children. You have the law, which holds what is true and what to know.

21So you teach other people, but do you teach yourself? You preach, "Do not steal," but do you steal? 22You say, "Do not cheat with someone's wife or husband," but do you do it? You hate idols, but do you steal from temples? 23You brag about the law, but do you shame God by breaking the law? 24God's name is spoken badly among the nations because of you, just as it is written.

25Being circumcised helps if you obey the law. But if you break the law, your circumcision becomes like not being circumcised. 26If a person who is not circumcised keeps what the law says, will not his not being circumcised count as circumcision? 27The person who is not circumcised in the body but obeys the law will judge you. You have the written law and circumcision, but you break the law.

28A person is not a Jew just because he looks like one on the outside. True circumcision is not just on the skin. 29A true Jew is one on the inside. True circumcision is of the heart, by the SpiritspiritSpirit, not by written rules. That person's praise does not come from people. It comes from God.
