Keep Away from Sin

4Everyone who sins breaks God's law. Sin is breaking God's law. 5And you know that HeheHe came to take away sins. There is no sin in HimhimHim. 6No one who stays in HimhimHim keeps sinning. Love One Another

11This is the message you heard from the start: we should love one another. 12Do not be like Cain. He belonged to the evil one and killed his brother. Why did he kill him? Because his own deeds were evil, but his brother's deeds were good. 13Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you. 14We know we have moved from death to life because we love our brothers. Anyone who does not love stays in death. 15Anyone who hates his brother is a murderer. Let us love by what we do and by being true.

God Hears Us
