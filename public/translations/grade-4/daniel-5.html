King Belshazzar's Big Dinner

1King Belshazzar gave a big dinner for one thousand of his important men. He drank wine with them. 2While he drank, Belshazzar said to bring the gold and silver cups. These were the cups his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken from God's house in Jerusalem. The king wanted to drink from them with his important men, his wives, and his other women. 3So they brought the gold cups from God's house in Jerusalem. The king, his important men, his wives, and his other women drank from them. 4They drank wine and praised gods made of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone.

The Hand Writes on the Wall

5Right then, fingers of a human hand came out and wrote on the wall. The wall was near the lampstand in the king's palace. The king saw the hand writing. 6The king's face changed. He was scared. His thoughts troubled him. His legs felt weak, and his knees hit together. 7The king called loudly for the wise men, the Chaldeans, and the star readers. He said to Babylon's wise men, "Whoever reads this writing and tells what it means will wear purple. He will get a gold chain for his neck. He will be third in charge of the kingdom." 8All the king's wise men came in, but they could not read the writing or tell the king what it meant. 9Then King Belshazzar was very scared. His face changed more. His important men were confused.

The Queen Speaks

10The queen came into the banquet hall because of the king's words and his important men. She said, "O king, live forever! Do not be scared. Do not let your face change. 11There is a man in your kingdom who has the spirit of the holy gods. In your father's days, he was found to have light, understanding, and wisdom like the gods. Your father, King Nebuchadnezzar, made him chief of the wise men, the magicians, the Chaldeans, and the star readers. 12His name is Daniel, but the king called him Belteshazzar. He has a very great spirit. He knows things and understands things. He can explain dreams and tell hard things. He can untie knots. Call Daniel now. He will tell you what the writing means."

Daniel Is Brought to the King

13Then Daniel was brought to the king. The king said to Daniel, "Are you Daniel, one of the Jewish people my father the king brought from Judah? 14I heard that the spirit of the gods is in you. You have light, understanding, and great wisdom. 15The wise men and magicians were brought to me. They could not read this writing or tell me what it means. 16But I heard you can give meanings and untie knots. If you can read the writing and tell me what it means, you will wear purple. You will get a gold chain for your neck. You will be third in charge of the kingdom."

Daniel Answers the King

17Daniel answered the king, "Keep your gifts. Give your rewards to someone else. He killed people when he wanted. He gave power to people when he wanted. He took power away when he wanted. 20But when his heart became proud and his spirit became hard, he was taken from his throne. His glory was taken from him. 21He was driven away from people. His mind became like an animal's mind. He lived with wild donkeys. He ate grass like an ox. Dew from Heavenheavenheaven made him wet until he knew that the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God rules over people's kingdoms. He sets over them whom HeheHe wants.

The Kingdom Is Given Away

29Then Belshazzar said to dress Daniel in purple. They put a gold chain on his neck. They announced that he would be third in charge of the kingdom. 30That same night, Belshazzar, the king of the Chaldeans, was killed. 31Darius the Mede took the kingdom. He was about sixty-two years old.
