Samuel's Sons Do Wrong

1When Samuel was old, he made his sons judges over Israel. 2His first son was Joel, and his second son was Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba. 3But his sons did not follow his ways. They chased money. They took bribes. They did not judge fairly.

The People Ask for a King

4All the leaders of Israel came together. They came to Samuel at Ramah. 5They said to him, "YouyouYou are old. Your sons do not live the way you do. Now give us a king to judge us, like all the other nations." 6But Samuel did not like it when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." So Samuel prayed to the LORD.

God Answers Samuel

7The LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to the people in all they say to you. They have not turned away from you. They have turned away from MemeMe. They do not want MemeMe to be their king. 8They have done this since the day I brought them out of Egypt. They left MemeMe and served other gods. Now they are doing the same thing to you. 9Listen to them. But warn them. Tell them what a king will do to them."

Samuel Warns the People

10Samuel told all the LORD's words to the people who asked for a king. 11He said, "This is what a king will do to you. He will take your sons and put them in his chariots and make them his horsemen. They will run in front of his chariots. 12He will make some of them leaders of thousands and leaders of fifties. He will make them plow his fields and cut his grain. He will make them build his weapons and his chariots. 13He will take your daughters to make perfume, cook food, and bake bread. 14He will take your best fields, vineyards, and olive trees. He will give them to his servants. 15He will take one tenth of your grain and your vineyards. He will give it to his officers and servants. 16He will take your servants, your best young men, and your donkeys. He will put them to work for him. 17He will take one tenth of your sheep. You will be his servants. 18On that day, you will cry out because of the king you chose. But the LORD will not answer you then."

The People Still Want a King

19But the people would not listen to Samuel. They said, "No. We want a king over us. 20We want to be like all the other nations. We want a king to judge us, to go out before us, and to fight our battles." 21Samuel heard all the people's words. He told them to the LORD.

22The LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to them. Make them a king."

Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Go home, each of you to your own city."
