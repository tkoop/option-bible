The Altar for Burning Incense

1"Make an altar for burning incense. Make it from acacia wood. 2Make it square. It should be one cubit long, one cubit wide, and two cubits high. Its horns should be one piece with it. 3Cover it with pure gold on top, on all sides, and on the horns. Put a gold strip around it. 4Make two gold rings under the strip, one on each of two sides. They will hold poles for carrying it. 5Make the poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold. 6Put the altar in front of the curtain that hangs over the box that holds the law. Put it in front of the lid that covers the law. That is where I will meet with you. 7Aaron will burn sweet-smelling incense on it. He will burn it every morning when he takes care of the lamps. 8He will also burn incense at sunset when he lights the lamps. This incense will burn before the the LORD always, for all your people to come. 9Do not burn strange incense on it. Do not burn a burnt offering, a grain offering, or a drink offering on it. 10Once a year, Aaron will use blood from the sin offering to make the altar right with God. He will do this on the horns of the altar once a year for all time. The altar is most holy to the the LORD."

Money for the Tent

11The the LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "12When you count the people of Israel, each man must give money to the the LORD to buy back his life. Do this when you count them. Then no sickness will come to them. 13Everyone who is counted must give half a shekel of silver. Use the holy shekel. A shekel is 20 gerahs. So half a shekel is the gift to the the LORD. 14Everyone 20 years old or older who is counted must give this gift to the the LORD. 15The rich must not give more, and the poor must not give less than half a shekel. This gift pays for your lives. 16Take this money from the people of Israel. Use it for the work of the tent of meeting. It will help the people of Israel remember before the the LORD. It will pay for your lives."

The Wash Basin

17The the LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "18Make a bronze bowl for washing. Make a bronze stand for it. Put it between the tent of meeting and the altar. Put water in it. 19Aaron and his sons will wash their hands and feet with the water. 20When they go into the tent of meeting, or when they come near the altar to serve, they must wash. If they do not wash, they will die. This is when they burn a gift to the the LORD. 21They must wash their hands and feet so they will not die. This rule will last for all time for Aaron and his children."

Holy Oil

22The the LORD spoke to Moses. He said, "23Take the best spices. Take 500 shekels of liquid myrrh, 250 shekels of sweet cinnamon, 250 shekels of sweet cane, 24and 500 shekels of cassia. Use the holy shekel to weigh them. Take a hin of olive oil. 25Make a holy oil for pouring. Mix it like a perfume maker would mix it. It will be holy oil for pouring. 26Pour this oil on the tent of meeting and on the box that holds the law. 27Pour it on the table and all its tools, on the lampstand and its tools, and on the altar for burning incense. 28Pour it on the altar for burnt offerings and all its tools, and on the bowl and its stand. 29Make these things holy. They will be most holy. Anyone who touches them will become holy. 30Pour oil on Aaron and his sons. Make them holy so they can serve MemeMe. 31Tell the people of Israel that this oil is holy to MemeMe. It is for all time. 32Do not pour this oil on a normal person. Do not make oil like it. It is holy, and it must be holy to your. 33Anyone who makes oil like it, or puts it on a stranger, will be cut off from his people."

Holy Incense

34The the LORD said to Moses, "Take spices: stacte, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense. Use the same amount of each. 35Make incense from them. Mix it like a perfume maker would mix it. Make it pure and holy. 36Crush some of it into a fine powder. Put it in front of the law in the tent of meeting. That is where I meet with you. It is most holy for you. 37Do not make incense like this for yourselves. It is holy to the the LORD. 38Anyone who makes it to smell it will be cut off from his people."
