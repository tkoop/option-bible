God Warns Israel

1Listen to this word. The LORD spoke it against you, people of Israel. He spoke it against the whole family I brought out of Egypt. He said, 2"I chose you alone from all the families on EarthEarthearth. So I will punish you for all your sins."

Why Trouble Comes

3Can two walk together unless they agree? 4Does a lion roar in the woods if it has no food? Does a young lion growl from its den unless it has caught something? 5Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground if there is no bait? Does a trap spring up from the ground if it has not caught something? 6If a trumpet blows in a city, won't the people be afraid? If trouble comes to a city, hasn't the LORD done it? 7The Lord the LORD does nothing without telling HishisHis secret to HishisHis servants, the prophets. 8A lion has roared. Who won't be afraid? The Lord the LORD has spoken. Who can keep from speaking HishisHis words?

God Calls Others to Watch

9Tell the big houses in Ashdod and in Egypt, "Come together on the hills of Samaria. Look at the big mess inside her. Look at the people being hurt there." 10"They do not know how to do right," says the LORD. "They store up hurt and stealing in their big houses."

Punishment Is Coming

11So the Lord the LORD says, "An enemy will come all around the land. He will take away your strength. He will rob your big houses."

12The LORD says, "Like a shepherd saves two legs or part of an ear from a lion's mouth, so the people of Israel who live in Samaria will be saved. They will have only a corner of a bed or a piece of a couch."

God Will Punish Israel

13Listen and speak against the family of Jacob, says the Lord the LORD, the God of Hosts.

14"On the day I punish Israel for their sins, I will also punish the altars of Bethel. The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground."

15"I will hit the winter house and the summer house. The houses made of ivory will be gone. Many houses will end," says the LORD.
