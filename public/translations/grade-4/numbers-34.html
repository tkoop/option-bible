The Land the LORD Gives Israel

1The LORD spoke to Moses.

2The LORD told Moses to tell the Israelites, “When you come into Canaan, this land will be yours. It is the land of Canaan with all its borders.

3“Your southern border will start at the desert of Zin, near Edom. It will begin at the east end of the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea.

4“It will turn south of the Scorpion Pass, go through Zin, and end south of Kadesh Barnea. Then it will go to Hazar Addar and cross to Azmon.

5“The border will turn from Azmon to the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt. Its end will be at the SeaseaSea.

6“Your western border will be the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea. That will be your western border.

7“This will be your northern border. From the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea, mark out Mount Hor for yourselves.

8“From Mount Hor, mark out toward Lebo Hamath. The border will end at Zedad.

9“Then the border will go to Ziphron. Its end will be at Hazar Enan. This will be your northern border.

10“Mark out your eastern border from Hazar Enan to Shepham.

11“The border will go down from Shepham to Riblah, east of Ain. It will go down and touch the slope east of the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth.

12“Then it will go down to the Jordan. Its end will be at the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea. This is your land with its borders all around.”

How the Land Will Be Shared

13Moses told the Israelites, “This is the land you will receive by drawing lots. The LORD commanded that it be given to the nine and a half tribes.

14“The tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their land.

15“The two and a half tribes received their land east of the Jordan, across from Jericho.”

Men Who Will Divide the Land

16The LORD spoke to Moses. The LORD said,

17“These are the names of the men who will divide the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.

18“You will choose one leader from each tribe to divide the land.

19“These are the men: from Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh; 20from Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud; 21from Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon; 22from Dan, Bukki son of Jogli; 23from Manasseh, Hanniel son of Ephod; 24from Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan; 25from Zebulun, Elizaphan son of Parnach; 26from Issachar, Paltiel son of Azzan; 27from Asher, Ahihud son of Shelomi; 28from Naphtali, Pedahel son of Ammihud.”

29These were the men the the LORD commanded to give the Israelites their land in Canaan.
