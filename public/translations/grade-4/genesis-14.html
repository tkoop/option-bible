Four Kings Fight Five Kings

1In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim, 2these kings made war with Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar. 3All these kings came together in the ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim, which is the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea. 4For twelve years they served Chedorlaomer. In the thirteenth year they turned against him.

5In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings with him came. They beat the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim. 6They also beat the Horites in the hill country of Seir, all the way to El-paran near the desert. 7Then they turned back and came to En-mishpat, which is Kadesh. They beat all the country of the Amalekites and the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.

8Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out. They set up for battle in the ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim. 9They fought against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar. It was four kings against five. 10The ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim had many tar pits. The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah ran away and fell into them. The rest ran to the hills.

11The winners took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food. Then they left. 12They also took Lot, Abram's nephew, and his things. Lot was living in Sodom. Then they left.

Abram Rescues Lot

13One man who got away came and told Abram the Hebrew. Abram was living near the big trees of Mamre the Amorite. Mamre's brothers were Eshcol and Aner. They had made a promise to help Abram. 14Abram heard that his relative had been taken. He led out his trained men, 318 men born in his house. He chased the enemy as far as Dan. 15At night Abram split his men into groups. He and his servants beat the enemy. They chased them to Hobah, north of Damascus. 16Abram brought back all the goods. He brought back his relative Lot and Lot's things. He also brought back the women and the people.

Melchizedek Blesses Abram

Abram gave him one tenth of everything.

Abram Refuses the King of Sodom's Gifts

21The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people. You can keep the goods." 22Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I promised the LORD, God MostmostMost HighhighHigh, MakermakerMaker of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth. 23I will not take a thread or a sandal strap or anything that is yours. You will not say, 'I made Abram rich.' 24I will take nothing except what the young men ate and the share for the men who went with me: Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them take their share."
