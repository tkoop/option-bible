The Story of the Ten Young Women

1"The kingdom of Heavenheavenheaven is like ten young women. They took their lamps and went out to meet the groom. 2Five of them were silly, and five were wise. 3The silly ones took their lamps but did not take extra oil. 4The wise ones took oil in jars with their lamps. 5The groom was late. So all the young women got sleepy and fell asleep.

6"At midnight someone shouted, 'Look! The groom is coming! Go out to meet him!' 7Then all the young women woke up and fixed their lamps. 8The silly ones said to the wise ones, 'Give us some of your oil. Our lamps are going out.' 9But the wise ones answered, 'No. There may not be enough for us and you. Go buy some for yourselves.' 10While they were gone to buy oil, the groom came. The ready ones went in with him to the wedding. Then the door was shut.

11"Later the other young women came and said, 'Lord, Lord, open the door for us!' But he answered, 'I do not know you.' 12So stay awake. You do not know the day or the hour.

The Story of the Talents

14"It is like a man who went on a trip. He called his servants and gave them his money to use. 15He gave five bags of money to one, two bags to another, and one bag to another. He gave each one what he could handle. Then he left.

16"The servant who got five bags went right away and worked with the money. He made five more bags. 17The one with two bags did the same. He made two more bags. 18But the servant who got one bag went and dug a hole in the ground. He hid his master's money there.

19"After a long time, the master came back. He settled accounts with his servants. 20The servant who had five bags came and brought five more. He said, 'Sir, you gave me five bags of money.
