Solomon Builds His Own House

1Solomon built his own house. It took thirteen years to finish all of it. 2He also built the House of the ForestforestForest of Lebanon. It was 100 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high. It stood on four rows of cedar pillars. Cedar beams rested on the pillars.

3Cedar covered the rooms above the pillars. There were 45 rooms. There were 15 rooms in each row. 4The windows were in three rows. They faced each other. 5All the doorways and posts were square. The windows faced each other in three rows.

6He made a porch of pillars. It was 50 cubits long and 30 cubits wide. There was another porch in front of them. It had pillars and a roof in front. 7He made a porch for the throne where he judged. It was called the porch of judgmentjudgementjudgment. Cedar covered it from the floor to the ceiling. 8His own house was in the other court behind the porch. It was built the same way. He also built a house for Pharaoh's daughter, his wife. It was like the porch.

9All these buildings used costly stones. The stones were cut to size. They were sawed with saws. This was true inside and outside. They went from the foundation to the top. They also went outside to the big court. 10The foundation had large, costly stones. Some were 10 cubits long. Some were 8 cubits long. 11Above them were costly stones cut to size. There was also cedar wood. 12The big court all around had three rows of cut stone. It had one row of cedar beams. This was for the inner court of the the LORD's house. It was also for the porch of the house.

Hiram Comes to Help

13King Solomon sent for Hiram. He brought him from Tyre. 14Hiram was the son of a widow from the tribe of Naphtali. His father was a man from Tyre. His father worked with bronze. Hiram was filled with wisdom, understanding, and skill. He could do all kinds of bronze work. He came to King Solomon. He did all the work.

15He cast two bronze pillars. Each pillar was 18 cubits high. Each was 12 cubits around. 16He made two bronze capitals. They went on top of the pillars. Each capital was 5 cubits high.

17He made a network of chains for the capitals. The capitals were on top of the pillars. There were seven chains for one capital. There were seven chains for the other. 18He made pomegranates in two rows around the network. They covered the capitals on top of the pillars. He did the same for the other pillar. 19The capitals on top of the pillars in the porch were shaped like lilies. They were 4 cubits high. 20There were also two hundred pomegranates in rows around each capital. They were just above the rounded part next to the network.

21He set up the pillars at the porch of the temple. He set up the right pillar. He called it Jachin. He set up the left pillar. He called it Boaz. 22The top of the pillars had lily work. So the work on the pillars was finished.

23He made the SeaseaSea of cast metal. It was round. It was 10 cubits across from rim to rim. It was 5 cubits high. It was 30 cubits around. 24Under the rim, gourds went all around it. There were ten gourds for every cubit. The gourds were in two rows. They were cast with the SeaseaSea. 25It stood on twelve oxen. Three faced north. Three faced west. Three faced south. Three faced east. The SeaseaSea was on top of them. Their backs pointed inward. 26The SeaseaSea was a handbreadth thick. Its rim was like the rim of a cup. It was shaped like a lily flower. It held two thousand baths of water.

27He made ten bronze stands. Each stand was 4 cubits long. Each was 4 cubits wide. Each was 3 cubits high. 28The stands had panels between the frames. 29On the panels were lions, oxen, and cherubim. Above and below were lions and oxen. Hanging pieces went down from them. 30Each stand had four bronze wheels. It had bronze axles. It had four feet. Under the basin, supports were cast at its four corners. These supports were beside the hanging pieces.

31The top had a round opening. It was one cubit across. The capital above it was one and a half cubits high. The opening had carvings. The frames were square, not round. 32Four wheels were under the frames. The wheel axles were part of the stand. Each wheel was one and a half cubits high. 33The wheels were like chariot wheels. Their axles, rims, spokes, and hubs were all cast metal. 34Each stand had four supports at its four corners. The supports were part of the stand.

35The top of the stand was round. It was half a cubit high. The supports and panels were part of it. 36He carved cherubim, lions, and palm trees on the plates, supports, and panels. Wreaths went all around. 37He made all ten stands the same way. They were cast the same. They were measured the same. They were the same size.

38He made ten bronze basins. Each basin held forty baths of water. Each basin was four cubits across. There was one basin on each stand. 39He put five stands on the right side of the house. He put five on the left side. He put the SeaseaSea at the southeast corner. 40Hiram made the basins, shovels, and bowls. Hiram finished all the work he did for King Solomon for the the LORD's house.

41There were two pillars. There were two bowl-shaped capitals on top of the pillars. There were two networks covering the two bowl-shaped capitals. 42There were four hundred pomegranates for the two networks. Each network had two rows of pomegranates. They covered the two bowl-shaped capitals in front of the pillars. 43There were ten stands and ten basins on the stands. 44There was one SeaseaSea and twelve oxen under the SeaseaSea. 45There were pots, shovels, and bowls. All the vessels Hiram made for Solomon for the the LORD's house were polished bronze.

46The king had them cast in clay molds. This was in the Jordan ValleyvalleyValley. It was between Succoth and Zarethan. 47Solomon did not weigh all the vessels. There were so many. The weight of the bronze was too great to measure.

Gold for the Temple

48Solomon made all the vessels for the the LORD's house. He made the gold altar. He made the gold table for the special bread. 49He made lampstands of pure gold. Five were on the right. Five were on the left. They were in front of the inner room. The flowers, lamps, and tongs were also gold. 50The cups, snuffers, bowls, spoons, and censers were pure gold. The hinges for the doors of the inner room, the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, and for the doors of the temple were gold. 51All the work King Solomon did for the the LORD's house was finished. Solomon brought in the holy things his father David had set apart. He put the silver, gold, and vessels into the treasuries of the the LORD's house.
