The LORD Speaks to Joshua

1After Moses, the servant of the LORD, died, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun. Joshua had helped Moses. The LORD said:

2"Moses MymyMy servant is dead. Now get up. You and all the people must cross the Jordan River. Go to the land I am giving to the people of Israel. 3I will give you every place where your feet walk, just as I told Moses. 4Your land will go from the desert and Lebanon to the great Euphrates River. It will include all the land of the Hittites and reach to the great sea in the west.

5"No one will be able to stand against you all your life. I will be with you, just as I was with Moses. I will not leave you or give up on you. 6Be strong and brave. You will help these people take the land I promised to give their fathers.

7"Be very strong and brave. Obey all the law that Moses MymyMy servant gave you. Do not turn away from it, to the right or to the left. Then you will do well wherever you go. 8Keep this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw with you. Think about it day and night. Then you can obey all that is written in it. You will do well and succeed. 9I have told you to be strong and brave. Do not be afraid or worried. I, the LORD your God, am with you wherever you go."

Joshua Tells the Leaders

10Then Joshua gave orders to the leaders of the people. He said, 11"Go through the camp and tell the people to get food ready. In three days you will cross the Jordan River. You will go in and take the land the LORD your God is giving you."

Joshua Speaks to the Two and a Half Tribes

12Then Joshua spoke to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh. He said, 13"Remember what Moses, the servant of the LORD, told you. The LORD your God is giving you rest. He is giving you this land. 14Your wives, your children, and your animals may stay in the land Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. But all your fighting men, ready for war, must cross ahead of your brothers. You must help them. 15You must help them until the LORD gives your brothers rest, just like HeheHe gave you. Then they will take the land the LORD your God is giving them. After that, you may go back to your own land. You will take the land Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you east of the Jordan."

The People Answer Joshua

16The people answered Joshua. They said, "We will do everything you tell us to do. We will go wherever you send us. 17We obeyed Moses in everything. We will obey you in the same way. May the LORD your God be with you, just as HeheHe was with Moses. 18Anyone who does not obey your words, or does not listen to everything you command, will be put to death. Just be strong and brave."
