Melchizedek Met Abraham

1This Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God MostmostMost HighhighHigh. He met Abraham when Abraham came back from winning a battle against kings. He blessed Abraham. 2Abraham gave him one tenth of everything. First, Melchizedek's name means "king of right living." Then it also means "king of Salem," which means "king of peace." 3He had no father or mother listed. He had no family tree. He had no beginning of days and no end of life. He is like God's SonsonSon. He stays a priest forever. 4Think about how great this man was. Even Abraham, the great father, gave him one tenth of the best things he won.

The Priests from Levi's Family

5The law says priests from Levi's family must take one tenth from the people. That means from their own brothers, even though they also come from Abraham. 6But Melchizedek did not come from Levi's family. He took one tenth from Abraham. He blessed the man who had God's promises. 7Everyone knows the greater person blesses the lesser person. 8Here, people who will die collect the tenth. But there, he is said to live. 9In a way, Levi, who collects the tenth, also paid the tenth through Abraham. 10When Melchizedek met Abraham, Levi was still in his father's body.

A Better Priest and a Better Law

11If the priests from Levi's family could make people perfect, why would we need another priest like Melchizedek, not like Aaron? The people got the law through those priests. 12When the work of the priests changes, the law must change too. 13The one these words are about came from a different tribe. No one from that tribe served at the altar. 14It is clear our Lord came from Judah. Moses said nothing about priests from that tribe. 15This becomes even clearer if another priest like Melchizedek comes. 16He became a priest not because of a family rule about birth, but because of the power of a life that cannot end. 17For it is said, "YouyouYou are a priest forever, in the way of Melchizedek." 18So the old command is set aside because it was weak and useless. 19The law made nothing perfect. But a better hope is brought in. Through it we come near to God.

Jesus Is Our Perfect High Priest

He is higher than the heavens. 27Unlike other high priests, HeheHe does not need to offer gifts every day. First for their own sins, then for the people's sins.
