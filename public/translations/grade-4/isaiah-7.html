Two Kings Attack Jerusalem

1In the days of Ahaz son of Jotham son of Uzziah king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram went to fight Jerusalem. Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel joined him, but they could not take the city. 2David's family heard, "Aram and Ephraim have joined together." The king and his people were scared. Their hearts shook like trees in a strong wind.

God Sends Isaiah to King Ahaz

3The LORD said to Isaiah, "Go out with your son Shear-jashub. Meet King Ahaz at the end of the upper pool, by the road to the washer's field. 4Say to him, 'Be careful. Stay calm. Do not be afraid. Do not let your heart get weak because of these two rulers. They are like two sticks that are almost burned out. They are angry, but they will not last. They are Rezin of Aram and Remaliah's son.'"

The Enemy's Plan Will Fail

5They have made an evil plan against you. They say, 6"Let us attack Judah. Let us scare the people. Let us break in and put Tabeel's son on the throne."

God's Answer

7But this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: "It will not happen. It will not come true."

What Will Happen to Aram and Israel

8Damascus is the capital of Aram, and Rezin rules Damascus. In 65 years, Ephraim will be broken. It will no longer be a people. 9Samaria is the capital of Ephraim, and Remaliah's son rules Samaria. If you do not believe this, you will not last.

Ahaz Is Offered a Sign

10The LORD spoke to Ahaz again. 11He said, "Ask the LORD your God for a sign. You can ask for something deep in the ground or high in the sky."

12But Ahaz said, "I will not ask. I will not test the LORD."

The Sign of Immanuel

13Isaiah said, "Listen, family of David. Is it not enough to tire out people? Do you also want to tire out my God? 14So the Lord HimselfhimselfHimself will give you a sign. Look! A young woman is going to have a baby. She will have a son. She will call HishisHis name Immanuel. 15He will eat curds and honey. He will know how to say no to bad and yes to good. 16Before the boy knows how to say no to bad and yes to good, the land of the two kings you fear will be left empty."

Hard Times Are Coming

17The LORD will bring hard times to you, your people, and your family. Hard times like these have not come since Israel left Judah. The LORD will bring the king of Assyria. 18On that day, the LORD will whistle for flies from the rivers of Egypt and for bees from Assyria. 19They will all come and rest in the valleys, in the cracks of rocks, in the thorn bushes, and in the pastures.

20On that day, the Lord will use a razor from across the River. He will shave the head, the leg hair, and the beard. 21On that day, a man will keep alive a young cow and two sheep. 22Because there will be so much milk, he will eat curds. Everyone left in the land will eat curds and honey.

23On that day, every place where a thousand grapevines grew, worth a thousand silver coins, will be full of thorns and briers. 24People will go there with bows and arrows, because the whole land will be thorns and briers. 25No one will go to the hills that people used to hoe. They will be afraid of the thorns and briers. But oxen will be sent there, and sheep will walk over them.
