A Song by David

1I wait quietly for God alone. He saves me. 2He alone is my rock. He saves me. He is my safe place. I will not be shaken.

People Who Hurt Me

3How long will you attack one man? Will all of you knock him down? He is like a wall about to fall, like a fence ready to break. 4They plan to bring him down from his high place. They love lies. They say good things with their mouths, but they curse him in their hearts. Selah

I Will Trust in God

5I tell myself to rest in God alone. I hope in HimhimHim. 6He alone is my rock. He saves me. He is my safe place. I will not be shaken. 7God saves me and gives me honourhonourhonor. He is my strong rock. I am safe in HimhimHim. 8Trust in HimhimHim at all times, people. Pour out your hearts to HimhimHim. God is our safe place. Selah

People Are Not Strong

9People are like a puff of air. Even important people are a lie. On a scale, they are too light. They weigh less than a puff of air. 10Do not trust in money you took by force. Do not hope in stolen things. Even if your money grows, do not set your heart on it.

God Is Strong and Kind

11God has said one thing. I have heard two things. Power belongs to God. 12And that YouyouYou, Lord, are kind. You will give each person what he deserves.
