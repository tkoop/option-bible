Jesus Talks About Turning from Sin

1At that time, some people were there. They told Jesus about some people from Galilee. Pilate had killed them and mixed their blood with the gifts they gave to God.

2Jesus answered, "Do you think those Galileans were worse sinners than all other Galileans, so this happened to them? 3No, I tell you. But unless you turn from your sin, you will all die the same way. 4What about the eighteen people the tower in Siloam fell on and killed? Do you think they were worse sinners than all the people in Jerusalem? 5No, I tell you. But unless you turn from your sin, you will all die the same way."

A Story About a Fig Tree

6Then Jesus said, "A man had a fig tree in his grape field. He came looking for fruit on it, but he did not find any. 7So he said to the man who cared for the field, 'Look, for three years I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree. I never find any. Cut it down. Why should it take up the ground?' 8But the man answered, 'Sir, leave it alone for one more year. I will dig around it and put plant food on it. 9Maybe it will make fruit next year. If not, then cut it down.'"
