Jeremiah Warns Judah

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah about all the people of Judah. It was in the fourth year that Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, was king of Judah. That was the first year Nebuchadnezzar was king of Babylon.

2Jeremiah spoke to all the people of Judah and everyone living in Jerusalem. He said,

"3For twenty-three years, since the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until today, the the LORD's word has come to me. I have spoken to you again and again, but you did not listen. 4The ThetheThe LORD sent you all HishisHis servants, the prophets. He sent them again and again, but you did not listen or pay attention. 5He told you to turn from your evil ways and stop doing bad things. Then you could live in the land the the LORD gave you and your fathers forever. 6He told you not to follow other gods or bow down to them. He told you not to make HimhimHim angry by what you do, so HeheHe would not hurt you. 7But the the LORD says you did not listen to HimhimHim. You made HimhimHim angry by what you did, and it hurt you."

8The ThetheThe LORD of armies says, "Because you did not listen to MymyMy words, 9look, I will send for all the families from the north. I will send for MymyMy servant Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. I will bring them against this land and its people and all the nations around it. I will wipe them out completely. I will make this place empty and scary forever. 10I will take away happy sounds and joy. I will take away the voices of brides and grooms. I will take away the sound of mills and the light of lamps. 11This whole land will become empty and ruined. These nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years. 12After seventy years, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation for their wrongs. I will make their land empty forever. 13I will bring on that land all MymyMy words that I spoke against it. I will do everything written in this book that Jeremiah said would happen to all the nations. 14Many nations and great kings will make them slaves too. I will pay them back for what they have done."

The Cup of God's Anger

15The ThetheThe LORD, the God of Israel, said to me, "Take this cup of wine from MymyMy hand. It is full of MymyMy anger. Make all the nations I send you to drink it. 16They will drink and stagger and go crazy because of the sword I am sending among them."

17So I took the cup from the the LORD's hand. I made all the nations drink it, the nations the the LORD sent me to.

18Jerusalem and the cities of Judah drank it. Their kings and officials drank it. This made them empty, ruined, scary, and cursed, as they are today.

19Pharaoh king of Egypt drank it, along with his servants, his leaders, and all his people. 20All the mixed people drank it. All the kings of the land of Uz drank it. All the kings of the Philistines drank it—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the rest of Ashdod. 21Edom drank it. Moab drank it. The Ammonites drank it. 22All the kings of Tyre drank it. All the kings of Sidon drank it. The kings of the coastlands across the sea drank it.

23Dedan drank it. Tema drank it. Buz drank it. All who cut the corners of their hair drank it. 24All the kings of Arabia drank it. All the kings of the mixed tribes who live in the desert drank it. 25All the kings of Zimri drank it. All the kings of Elam drank it. All the kings of Media drank it. 26All the kings of the north drank it, near and far, one after another. All the kingdoms of the world on the earth drank it. After them, the king of Sheshach will drink too.

27Then tell them that the the LORD of armies, the God of Israel, says they must drink until they are drunk. They will vomit, fall down, and not get up again. This is because of the sword HeheHe is sending among them. 28If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink, then say to them that the the LORD of armies says they must drink.

29The ThetheThe LORD of armies says, "Look, I am starting to bring trouble on the city that bears MymyMy name. Do you think you will go free? You will not go free, because I am calling for a sword against everyone on EarthEarthearth."

The LORD Roars Against the Nations

30Then Jeremiah prophesied these words: "The ThetheThe LORD will roar from high above. He will thunder from HishisHis holy home. He will roar loudly against HishisHis own land. He will shout like people stomping grapes, against everyone on EarthEarthearth. 31Noise will reach the ends of the earth, because the the LORD has a case against the nations. He will judge all people. He will hand the wicked over to the sword, says the the LORD."

The LORD Punishes the Nations

32The ThetheThe LORD of armies says, "Look, trouble is going out from nation to nation. A great storm is rising from the ends of the earth. 33On that day, those killed by the the LORD will lie from one end of the earth to the other. No one will cry for them. No one will gather them or bury them. They will be like dung on the ground. 34Cry out, you shepherds! Roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock! Your time to be killed has come. You will fall and break like a fancy jar. 35The shepherds will have no way to run. The leaders of the flock will have no way to escape. 36Listen to the shepherds cry! Listen to the leaders of the flock wail! The ThetheThe LORD is destroying their pasture. 37Their peaceful homes are cut down because of the the LORD's fierce anger. 38He has left HishisHis hiding place like a lion. Their land is empty because of the cruel sword and HishisHis fierce anger."
