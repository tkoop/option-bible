Samuel Talks to All Israel

1Samuel said to all Israel, “Look, I have listened to your voice in all you said to me, and I have set a king over you. 2Now look, the king walks before you. I am old and greygreygray. My sons are with you. I have walked before you from my youth until today. 3Today, here I am. Tell me if I have done wrong before the LORD and before HishisHis anointed. Did I take anyone's ox? Did I take anyone's donkey? Did I cheat anyone? Did I hurt anyone? Did I take money from anyone to look the other way? Tell me, and I will give it back.”

4“You have not cheated us,” they said. “You have not hurt us. You have not taken anything from anyone.”

5Samuel said, “The LORD is a witness against you, and HishisHis anointed is a witness today. You have not found anything in my hand.”

“He is a witness,” they said.

Samuel Remembers What God Did

6Samuel said to the people, “The LORD is the OneoneOne who made Moses and Aaron. He brought your fathers up from Egypt. 7Now stand here. I will judge you before the LORD. I will tell you all the good things the LORD did for you and your fathers. 8When Jacob came to Egypt, your fathers cried to the LORD. The LORD sent Moses and Aaron. They brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place.

9But they forgot the LORD their God. So HeheHe let their enemies beat them. He let Sisera, the army leader of Hazor, and the Philistines, and the king of Moab fight against them.

10They cried to the LORD and said, “We have sinned. We left the LORD and served the Baals and the Ashtoreths. Now save us from our enemies' hands, and we will serve YouyouYou.”

11Then the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel. He saved you from your enemies all around, and you lived in safety. 12But when you saw Nahash king of the Ammonites coming against you, you said to me, “No, a king must rule over us,” even though the LORD your God was your king. 13Now here is the king you chose, the one you asked for. See, the LORD has set a king over you.

14If you fear the LORD and serve HimhimHim, and obey HishisHis voice, and do not go against the LORD's words, then you and your king will keep following the LORD your God. 15But if you do not obey the LORD, and you rebel against the LORD, then the LORD's hand will be against you and your fathers.

God Sends Thunder and Rain

16Now stand and see this great thing the LORD will do before your eyes. 17Today is wheat harvest time. I will pray to the LORD, and HeheHe will send thunder and rain. Then you will know and see that you did a very bad thing in the LORD's eyes when you asked for a king.

18Samuel called to the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain that day. All the people were very afraid of the LORD and Samuel.

19All the people said to Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for us, your servants, so we will not die. We have added to all our sins by asking for a king.”

Samuel's Advice

20Samuel said to the people, “Do not be afraid. You have done all this wrong. But do not stop following the LORD. Serve the LORD with all your heart. 21Do not chase empty things that cannot help you or save you. They are useless. 22The LORD will not leave HishisHis people, because of HishisHis great name. It pleased the LORD to make you HishisHis people. 23I would never sin against the LORD by stopping my prayers for you. I will teach you the good and right way. 24But you must fear the LORD and serve HimhimHim faithfully with all your heart. Remember what great things HeheHe has done for you. 25But if you keep doing wrong, you and your king will be destroyed.”
