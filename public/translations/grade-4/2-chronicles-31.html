The People Remove False Worship

1After all this, the Israelites who were there went to the towns of Judah. They broke the stone pillars. They cut down the Asherah poles. They tore down the high places and altars in Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. They destroyed all of them. Then the Israelites went back to their own towns and land.

Hezekiah Sets Up Priest Groups

2Hezekiah put the priests and Levites into groups. Each group had its own work. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. They served, thanked, and praised God at the gates of the LORD’s house.

3The king gave some of his own things for the morning and evening burnt offerings. He also gave for burnt offerings on Sabbath days, new moon days, and feast days. This was written in the LORD’s LawlawLaw.

4He told the people who lived in Jerusalem to give the priests and Levites their share. Then they could give their time to the LORD’s LawlawLaw.

The People Bring Many Gifts

5When the people heard this, the Israelites brought a lot of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and all the food from the fields. They brought a large amount. They also brought a tenth of everything.

6The people of Israel and Judah who lived in the towns of Judah also brought a tenth of their cows and sheep. They brought a tenth of the holy things given to the LORD their God. They piled them in big heaps.

Gifts Pile Up

7They started making the heaps in the third month. They finished in the seventh month.

Hezekiah Praises God

8Hezekiah and his leaders came and saw the heaps. They praised the LORD. They blessed HishisHis people Israel.

9Hezekiah asked the priests and Levites about the heaps.

The Priests Answer

10Azariah the chief priest from Zadok’s family said, “Since the people started bringing gifts to the LORD’s house, we have had plenty to eat. There is much left over. The LORD has blessed HishisHis people.”

Rooms Are Made Ready

11Then Hezekiah said to get rooms ready in the LORD’s house. They did.

Faithful Workers Handle Gifts

12They brought the gifts, tithes, and holy things in faithfully. Konaniah the Levite was in charge. His brother Shimei was next in charge.

13Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were helpers under Konaniah and Shimei. King Hezekiah and Azariah, the leader of God’s house, chose them.

Gifts Are Shared Out

14Kore son of Imnah was a Levite. He kept the east gate. He was in charge of the free gifts given to God. He handed out the LORD’s gifts and the holy things.

15Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah helped him. They faithfully gave food to the priests in the towns. They gave to their fellow priests in each group, to the great and the small.

All the People Are Counted

16This was besides the males three years old and older whose names were written down. They came to the LORD’s house each day to do their daily work in their groups.

17The priests were counted by their family groups. The Levites twenty years old and older were counted by their groups and duties.

18They also gave to all their little children, wives, sons, and daughters in the whole group. They set themselves apart for God faithfully.

Shares for Priests in the Fields

19For the priests, the sons of Aaron, who lived in the fields near their towns, men were named in every city. They gave shares to every male priest and to every Levite on the list.

Hezekiah Does Right

20Hezekiah did this in all Judah. He did what was good, right, and true before the LORD his God.

21In all the work he started for God’s house, and in obeying the LawlawLaw and commands, he looked for his God with all his heart. He did well and had success.
