The Philistines Fight Israel

1The Philistines fought against Israel. The Israelites ran away from them. Many Israelites died on Mount Gilboa.

2The Philistines chased Saul and his sons. They killed his sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.

3The fight was hard around Saul. Men with bows caught up to him and shot him. He was hurt very badly.

Saul Dies

4Saul said to the man who carried his weapons, "Draw your sword and kill me with it. If not, these men who are not God's people will come and kill me and hurt me." But the man was very afraid. He would not do it. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it.

5The man who carried his weapons saw that Saul was dead. He fell on his own sword too. He died with Saul.

6So Saul, his three sons, his helper, and all the men with him died on that same day.

7The Israelites across the valley and across the Jordan saw that their army had run away. They saw that Saul and his sons were dead. So they left their towns and ran away too. The Philistines moved in and lived there.

The Philistines Take Saul's Things

8The next day the Philistines came to take things from the dead. They found Saul and his three sons on Mount Gilboa.

9They cut off his head. They took off his armourarmourarmor. They sent men all around the land of the Philistines to tell the news in the temples of their gods and to the people.

10They put his armourarmourarmor in the temple of Ashtoreth. They hung his body on the wall of Beth Shan.

The Men of Jabesh Bury Saul

11The people of Jabesh Gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul.

12All their brave men walked all night. They took the bodies of Saul and his sons from the wall of Beth Shan. They brought them to Jabesh. They burned them there.

13Then they took the bones and buried them under a tree in Jabesh. They did not eat for seven days.
