Ask God for Rain

1Ask the LORD for rain in spring. The LORD makes lightning. He gives rain to everyone. He makes grass grow in the fields. 2Idols tell lies. Fortune tellers see false dreams. They say empty words to comfort people. So people wander like sheep. They suffer because they have no shepherd.

God Will Care for His People

3“My anger burns against the shepherds. I will punish the leaders. For the LORD of Armies has cared for HishisHis flock, the people of Judah. He makes them like HishisHis strong horse in war. 4From Judah will come the cornerstone, the tent peg, the battle bow, and every ruler. 5They will be like strong men in war. They will trample the enemy in the mud of the streets. They will fight because the LORD is with them. They will put the horsemen to shame.

6“I will make the people of Judah strong. I will save the people of Joseph. I will bring them home because I care for them. They will be as if I never turned away from them. I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them. 7The people of Ephraim will be like strong men. Their hearts will be glad, like wine makes people glad. Their children will see it and be glad. Their hearts will rejoice in the LORD. 8I will whistle for them and gather them, because I have set them free. They will grow in number as they did before.

God Will Bring His People Home

9“I will scatter them among the nations. They will remember MemeMe in faraway lands. They and their children will live and come home. 10I will bring them back from Egypt. I will gather them from Assyria. I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon. There will not be room enough for them. 11They will pass through a sea of trouble. They will strike the waves of the sea. All the deep parts of the river will dry up. The pride of Assyria will fall. The power of Egypt will go away. 12I will make them strong in the LORD. They will live to honourhonourhonor HishisHis name,” says the LORD.
