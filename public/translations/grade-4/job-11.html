Zophar Answers Job

1Zophar the Naamathite answered:

Job Talks Too Much

2"Should so many words go without an answer? Should a man who talks a lot be called right? 3Do your proud words make people stay quiet? You laugh at others, and no one tells you to stop. 4You say, 'My teaching is right, and I am clean in HishisHis sight.'

Zophar Wishes God Would Speak

5"I wish God would speak. I wish HeheHe would open HishisHis mouth and answer you. 6He would show you hidden wisdom. True wisdom is much bigger than you think. Know this: God does not punish you as much as your sin deserves.

No One Can Know All About God

7"Can you understand God's deep ways? Can you find out how far the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty goes? 8God's ways are higher than the sky. What can you do? They are deeper than the grave. What can you know? 9They are longer than the earth and wider than the sea.

10"If God passes by and locks someone up, or calls a court, who can stop HimhimHim? 11He knows false people. When HeheHe sees wrong, HeheHe does not ignore it. 12But a silly man cannot become wise, just like a wild donkey cannot become a person.

Zophar Tells Job How to Be Right With God

13"If you set your heart on God and lift your hands to HimhimHim, 14if you put away the wrong in your hand and do not let sin stay in your home,

15"then you can lift your face without shame. You will stand strong and not be afraid. 16You will forget your trouble, like water that has flowed away. 17Your life will be bright like noon. Even your dark times will be like morning.

18"You will be safe, because there is hope. You will look around and rest in peace. 19You will lie down, and no one will scare you. Many people will come to you for help. 20But the eyes of bad people will grow weak. They will not escape. Their hope will be like a last breath."
