No Punishment Now

1So now there is no punishment for people who are in Christ Jesus. 2The power of the SpiritspiritSpirit, who gives life in Christ Jesus, has set you free from the power of sin and death. 3The law could not save us because our human nature was too weak. So God sent HishisHis own SonsonSon in a body like ours. He came to take away sin, and God punished sin in HishisHis Son's body. 4Now the good things the law wanted can happen in us. We do not live to please our sinful selves. We live to please the SpiritspiritSpirit.

Two Ways to Live

5People who live to please their sinful nature think about sinful things. But people who live to please the SpiritspiritSpirit think about what the SpiritspiritSpirit wants. 6Thinking only about sinful things leads to death. Thinking about what the SpiritspiritSpirit wants gives life and peace. 7A mind that thinks only about sin is against God. It does not obey God's law. It cannot obey it. 8So people who live only for sinful things cannot please God.

God's Spirit Lives in You

9But you are not like that. You live by the SpiritspiritSpirit, if God's SpiritspiritSpirit lives in you. If anyone does not have Christ's SpiritspiritSpirit, that person does not belong to HimhimHim. 10If Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, but your spirit is alive because God made you right. 11If the SpiritspiritSpirit of God, who raised Jesus from the dead, lives in you, then God, who raised Christ from the dead, will also give life to your bodies that die through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit who lives in you.

We Are God's Children

12So, brothers and sisters, we do not owe anything to our sinful nature. We do not have to live to please it. 13If you live to please your sinful nature, you will die. But if you use the Spirit's power to stop doing wrong things with your body, you will live. 14All who are led by God's SpiritspiritSpirit are God's children. 15You did not get a spirit that makes you a slave again and makes you afraid. You got the SpiritspiritSpirit who makes you God's child. With that SpiritspiritSpirit, we cry, "Abba, FatherfatherFather." 16The SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself tells our spirit that we are God's children. 17If we are God's children, then we will receive what God has promised. We will share it with Christ. But we must suffer with HimhimHim first, so we can share HishisHis glory.

Future Glory

18I think the hard things we suffer now are nothing compared to the glory God will show us later. 19All creation is waiting. It wants to see God's children shown for who they are. 20God made the world broken and empty. The world did not choose it. But God did it with hope. 21One day creation itself will be set free from decay and death. It will share the freedom and glory of God's children. 22We know that all creation has been groaning like it is in pain giving birth until now. 23We who have God's SpiritspiritSpirit also groan inside. We wait for God to make us fully HishisHis children. We wait for our bodies to be set free. 24We were saved because of this hope. But if we can see something, it is not hope. Who hopes for what they already see? 25If we hope for what we cannot see, we wait for it with patience.

The Spirit Helps Us Pray

26The SpiritspiritSpirit also helps us when we are weak. We do not know how to pray the way we should. But the SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself prays for us with deep groans that words cannot say. 27God, who looks at our hearts, knows what the SpiritspiritSpirit is thinking. The SpiritspiritSpirit prays for God's people the way God wants.

God Works for Good

28We know that God makes everything work for good for people who love HimhimHim. He does this for those HeheHe has called to follow HishisHis plan. 29God knew HishisHis people before they were born. He chose them to become like HishisHis SonsonSon. Then HishisHis SonsonSon would be the first and most important among many brothers and sisters. 30Those HeheHe chose, HeheHe also called. Those HeheHe called, HeheHe made right with HimhimHim. Those HeheHe made right, HeheHe also gave glory.

Nothing Can Separate Us

31What can we say about all this? If God is for us, no one can stand against us. 32God did not keep back HishisHis own SonsonSon. He gave HimhimHim up for all of us. So HeheHe will surely give us everything else we need, along with HishisHis SonsonSon. 33Who can accuse the people God has chosen? God is the one who makes them right. 34Who can condemn us? Christ Jesus died. He was raised to life. He sits at God's right side. He prays for us. 35Who can separate us from Christ's love? Can trouble, or pain, or being chased for our faith, or hunger, or having no clothes, or danger, or a sword? 36It is written, "For YouryourYour sake we face death all day. We are counted like sheep to be killed." 37But in all these things we win completely through HimhimHim who loved us. 38I am sure that nothing can separate us from God's love. Not death or life. Not angels or rulers or powers. Not things now or things to come. 39Not high or low. Not anything else in all creation. Nothing can separate us from God's love that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
