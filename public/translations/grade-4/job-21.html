Job Answers His Friends

1Then Job answered: 2Listen closely to my words. Let this be your comfort. 3Bear with me, and I will speak. After I speak, mock me.

4Am I arguing with a person? If so, why should I not be upset? 5Look at me and be shocked. Put your hand over your mouth. 6When I think about it, I am scared. Fear grabs my body.

Why Do Wicked People Do Well?

7Why do wicked people keep living? They grow old and get strong. 8Their children are safe with them. Their grandchildren are right before their eyes. 9Their homes are safe. They are not afraid. God does not punish them. 10Their bulls breed well. Their cows do not lose their calves.

11They send their little ones out like sheep. Their children dance. 12They sing with tambourines and harps. They are happy when they hear the flute. 13They live in comfort. Then they die in peace.

What Wicked People Say to God

14They say to God, “Go away from us. We do not want to know YouryourYour ways. 15What is the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, that we should serve HimhimHim? What do we gain if we pray to HimhimHim?”

16Look, their good things are not in their own hands. I do not follow the plans of wicked people.

God Will Judge the Wicked

17How often does the light of wicked people go out? Does trouble come on them? In HishisHis anger, does God give them pain? 18They are like straw in the wind. They are like dust blown away by a storm. 19God saves up their wrong for their children. He pays them back, so they will know. 20They will see their own ruin. They will drink the anger of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty.

21What good is their house to them after they die, when their time is cut short? 22Can anyone teach God? He judges even the great ones.

23One person dies strong and well. He is safe and calm. 24His body is full of good food. His bones are full of marrow. 25Another person dies sad and bitter. He never enjoyed good things. 26They both lie in the dust. Worms cover them.

Job Says His Friends Are Wrong

27I know what you are thinking. I know your plans against me. 28You say, “Where is the house of the great man? Where is the tent of the wicked?”

29Have you not asked people who travel? Do you not know what they say? 30Wicked people are kept for the day of trouble. They are brought out on the day of anger. 31Who will tell him his wrong to his face? Who will pay him back for what he did?

32He is carried to the grave. Someone watches over his tomb. 33He rests in the grave in the valley. Many people follow him. Countless go before him. 34So how can you comfort me with empty words? Your answers are false.
