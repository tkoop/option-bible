Joshua Sends the Eastern Tribes Home

1Then Joshua called the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh. 2He said to them, “You have kept all that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, told you to do. You have listened to my voice in all that I told you. 3For a long time you did not leave your brothers. You kept the command of the the LORD your God. 4Now the the LORD your God has given your brothers rest, just as HeheHe said. Now turn and go to your tents. Go to the land you own. Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave it to you on the other side of the Jordan. 5But be very careful to do the law and the commands that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave you. Love the the LORD your God. Walk in all HishisHis ways. Keep HishisHis commands. Stay close to HimhimHim. Serve HimhimHim with all your heart and all your soul.”

6Then Joshua blessed them and sent them away. They went to their tents. 7Moses had given half the tribe of Manasseh land in Bashan. Joshua gave the other half land west of the Jordan. When Joshua sent them to their tents, he blessed them too. 8He said, “Go home with great riches. You have much cattle, silver, gold, bronze, and iron. You have many clothes. Share the things you took from your enemies with your brothers.”

They Build an Altar by the Jordan

9So the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh left the people of Israel at Shiloh in the land of Canaan. They went back to their own land of Gilead, which they had received by the word of the the LORD through Moses. 10When they came to Geliloth near the Jordan in the land of Canaan, the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh built a big altar there by the Jordan. It could be seen from far away.

Israel Hears About the Altar

11Then the people of Israel heard, “Look, the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh have built an altar on the edge of the land of Canaan. It is at Geliloth near the Jordan, on our side.” 12When the people of Israel heard this, the whole group came together at Shiloh to go to war against them.

Leaders Go to Ask Why

13The people of Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the land of Gilead. He went to the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh. 14They also sent ten leaders. There was one leader from each tribe of Israel. Each man was head of a family group among the clans of Israel. 15They went to the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead. They said to them,

“16This is what the whole group of the the LORD says: ‘Why did you turn away from the God of Israel by building an altar for yourselves today? 17Was the sin at Peor not enough for us? We have not made ourselves clean from it to this day. A bad sickness came on the group of the the LORD then. 18And now you would turn away from the the LORD? If you turn against the the LORD today, HeheHe will be angry with all the people of Israel tomorrow. 19If your land is not clean, come over to the the LORD’s land. The the LORD’s tent is there. Take land among us. But do not turn against the the LORD or against us by building an altar for yourselves other than the altar of the the LORD our God. 20Did not Achan son of Zerah sin against the things set apart for God? Anger came on all the people of Israel. He was not the only one who died for his sin.’”

The Eastern Tribes Answer

21Then the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh answered the leaders of Israel.

“22The LORD, God of gods, the LORD, God of gods, HeheHe knows, and Israel will know too. If we did this to turn against the the LORD or to be unfaithful to HimhimHim, do not save us today. 23If we built this altar to turn away from the the LORD, or to burn gifts or grain on it, or to make peace gifts on it, may the the LORD HimselfhimselfHimself ask us to pay for it. 24But we did this because we were worried. We thought that someday your children might say to our children, ‘What do you have to do with the the LORD, the God of Israel?’ 25For the the LORD made the Jordan a border between us and you. You people of Reuben and Gad have no share in the the LORD. Then your children might make our children stop fearing the the LORD.

“26So we said, ‘Let us build an altar. It is not for burnt gifts or sacrifices. 27It is a witness between us and you, and for the children after us. We want to serve the the LORD in front of HimhimHim with our burnt gifts, sacrifices, and peace gifts. Then your children will not say to our children, “You have no share in the the LORD.”’ 28And we thought, ‘If they say this to us or to our children later, we will say, “Look at the copy of the the LORD’s altar that our fathers made. It is not for burnt gifts or sacrifices. It is a witness between us and you.”’ 29We would never turn against the the LORD or turn away from HimhimHim today by building an altar for burnt gifts, grain gifts, or sacrifices other than the altar of the the LORD our God, which is in front of HishisHis tent.”

The Leaders Are Pleased

30When Phinehas the priest and the leaders of the group heard what the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh said, they were pleased. 31Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the people of Reuben, the people of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh, “Today we know that the the LORD is with us. You have not been unfaithful to the the LORD in this. Now you have saved the people of Israel from the the LORD’s anger.” 32Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders went back from the people of Reuben and Gad in the land of Gilead to the people of Israel in the land of Canaan. They brought back a report. 33The people of Israel were pleased with the report. They praised God. They did not talk about going to war against them or destroying the land where the people of Reuben and Gad lived.

34So the people of Reuben and Gad named the altar WitnesswitnessWitness. They said, “It is a witness between us and you that the the LORD is God.”
