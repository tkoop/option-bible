Ezekiel's Hair Sign

"1You, son of man, take a sharp sword. Use it like a barber's razor. Shave your head and your beard. Then get scales and weigh the hair. Divide it into parts. 2When the siege is done, burn one third of the hair in the fire in the city. Take another third and cut it with the sword all around the city. Scatter the last third to the wind. I will send a sword after them. 3Take a few hairs from there. Tie them in the edge of your robe. 4Then take some of those hairs and throw them into the fire. Burn them up. A fire will come from them to all the people of Israel.

Jerusalem's Sins

"5This is what the Lord the LORD says: This is Jerusalem. I put her in the middle of the nations. Lands are all around her. 6She turned MymyMy laws into wrong. She was worse than the nations around her. She did not follow MymyMy laws. She did not keep MymyMy rules. 7Because you were more trouble than the nations around you, you did not follow MymyMy laws. You did not keep MymyMy rules. You did not even act like the nations around you.

The LORD Punishes Jerusalem

"8So now I am against you. I will punish you right among you, where the nations can see. 9Because of all your bad things, I will do to you what I have never done. I will never do it again. 10So fathers will eat their sons among you. Sons will eat their fathers. I will punish you. I will scatter all that is left of you to every wind.

The LORD's Anger

"11This is what the Lord the LORD says: As I live, you have made MymyMy holy place dirty with all your bad things and your ugly idols. So I will cut you down. I will not look away. I will not show you pity. 12One third of you will die from sickness. One third will die from hunger among you. One third will fall by the sword all around you. I will scatter one third to every wind. I will chase them with a sword. 13I will use up MymyMy anger on them. Then MymyMy anger will rest. I will be calm when MymyMy anger is done. Then they will know that I am the LORD. I said this in MymyMy jealousy.

Shame for Jerusalem

"14I will make you a ruin. The nations around you will shame you. Everyone who passes by will see. 15You will be shamed and mocked. You will be a warning and a shock to the nations around you. I will punish you in anger and fury. I am the LORD. I have spoken.

"16I will send bad arrows of hunger to destroy you. I will bring more hunger on you. I will break your food supply. 17I will send hunger and wild animals against you. They will take your children. Plague and blood will pass through you. I will bring a sword against you. I am the LORD. I have spoken."
