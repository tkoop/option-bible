The Workers and Their Gifts

1The LORD gave wisdom and understanding to Bezalel, Oholiab, and all the skilled workers. They knew how to do all the work for the holy place. They did everything the LORD commanded. 2Moses called Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled worker whose heart the LORD had filled with skill. They came to do the work. 3They took all the gifts the people of Israel had brought to Moses. The gifts were for the work of the holy place. But the people kept bringing more gifts every morning.

The Gifts Are Enough

4All the skilled workers who were doing the holy work came. Each man came from the work he was doing. 5They said to Moses, "ThetheThe people are bringing more than we need for the work the LORD commanded."

6Moses gave a command. They told everyone in the camp, "No man or woman should bring any more gifts for the holy place." So the people stopped bringing gifts. 7They had enough to do all the work. In fact, they had some left over.

Making the Holy Tent Curtains

8All the skilled workers made the holy tent. They made ten curtains of fine linen. The curtains were blue, purple, and red. They had cherubim woven into them with skill. 9Each curtain was 28 cubits long and 4 cubits wide. All the curtains were the same size.

10They joined five curtains into one piece. They joined the other five curtains into another piece. 11They made blue loops on the edge of the last curtain in the first set. They did the same on the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 12They made fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the other. The loops matched each other. 13They made fifty gold hooks. They used the hooks to join the two sets of curtains. So the holy tent became one piece.

Making the Goat Hair Tent

14They made curtains of goat hair for a tent over the holy tent. They made eleven of these curtains. 15Each curtain was 30 cubits long and 4 cubits wide. All of them were the same size. 16They joined five curtains into one piece. They joined the other six curtains into another piece.

17They made fifty loops on the edge of one curtain. They made fifty loops on the edge of the other curtain. 18They made fifty bronze hooks. They used them to join the tent into one piece. 19They made a covering for the tent from ram skins dyed red. They made another covering from fine leather skins on top.

Making the Frames

20They made frames of acacia wood for the holy tent. 21Each frame was 10 cubits long and 1 1/2 cubits wide. 22Each frame had two pegs joined to it. They did this for every frame.

23They made twenty frames for the south side of the holy tent. 24They made forty silver bases for the twenty frames. Each frame had two bases under it, one for each peg. 25They made twenty frames for the north side of the holy tent. 26These frames had forty silver bases, two under each frame.

27They made six frames for the back of the holy tent, on the west side. 28They made two frames for the two corners at the back. 29These corner frames were joined at the bottom and the top with a ring. They did this for both corners. 30So there were eight frames in all, with sixteen silver bases, two under each frame.

31They made five bars of acacia wood for the frames on one side. 32They made five bars for the frames on the other side. They made five bars for the frames at the back on the west side. 33They made the middle bar to go all the way through the frames from one end to the other. 34They covered the frames with gold. They made gold rings to hold the bars. They covered the bars with gold too.

Making the Veil and Entrance Screen

35They made the curtain that separated the rooms. It was blue, purple, and red fine linen. Cherubim were woven into it with skill. 36They made four acacia pillars for it. They covered them with gold. They made gold hooks for them. They made four silver bases for them.

37They made a screen for the entrance of the tent. It was blue, purple, and red fine linen, with pretty designs sewn into it. 38They made five pillars for it. They put hooks on them. They covered the tops and bands with gold. They made five bronze bases for them.
