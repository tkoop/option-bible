Jeremiah Speaks at the Temple

1At the start of King Jehoiakim's rule, this message came from the LORD. Jehoiakim was the son of King Josiah of Judah. 2The LORD said, “Stand in the courtyard of the LORD's house. Speak to all the cities of Judah that come to worship there. Tell them all the words I tell you to say. Do not leave out a word. 3Maybe they will listen and turn from their bad ways. Then I will not bring the trouble I planned for them. I planned it because of their bad deeds. 4Say to them, ‘This is what the LORD says: If you do not listen to MemeMe and follow MymyMy law that I gave you, 5and if you do not listen to the prophets who serve MemeMe—whom I send to you again and again—though you have not listened, 6then I will make this temple like Shiloh. I will make this city a curse to all the nations.’”

The People Get Angry

7The priests, the prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah say these things in the the LORD's house. 8When Jeremiah finished saying all that the the LORD told him to say to the people, the priests, the prophets, and all the people grabbed him. They said, “You must die!”

9They said, “Why did you say in the the LORD's name that this house will be like Shiloh? Why did you say this city will be empty and ruined?” All the people gathered around Jeremiah in the the LORD's house.

10The leaders of Judah heard this. They came from the king's house to the the LORD's house. They sat down at the entrance to the NewnewNew GategateGate.

11The priests and prophets said to the leaders and all the people, “This man should die. He spoke against this city. You heard it with your own ears.”

Jeremiah Answers

12Jeremiah said to all the leaders and all the people, “The the LORD sent me to speak against this temple and this city. I said all the words you heard. 13Now change your ways and your actions. Listen to the the LORD your God. Then the the LORD will not bring the trouble HeheHe said HeheHe would bring. 14As for me, I am in your hands. Do to me what you think is right. 15But you should know this: If you kill me, you will bring guilt on yourselves, on this city, and on the people who live here. The the LORD truly sent me to say all these words to you.”

The People Change Their Minds

16The leaders and all the people said to the priests and prophets, “This man should not die. He spoke to us in the name of the the LORD our God.”

The Elders Speak

17Some of the older leaders stood up. They spoke to all the people. They said, “Micah from Moresheth prophesied in the days of King Hezekiah of Judah. 18He said, ‘This is what the the LORD of Armies says: Zion will be plowed like a field. Jerusalem will become a pile of rocks. The hill where the temple stands will become a forest.’ 19Did King Hezekiah and all Judah kill him? No. Hezekiah feared the the LORD. He prayed to the the LORD. The the LORD did not bring the trouble HeheHe had said HeheHe would bring. But we are about to bring great trouble on ourselves.”

A Prophet Named Uriah

20There was also a man named Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath Jearim. He prophesied in the the LORD's name. He said the same things about this city and this land that Jeremiah said. 21King Jehoiakim and his soldiers and leaders heard his words. The king wanted to kill him. Uriah heard about it. He was afraid. He ran away to Egypt. 22King Jehoiakim sent Elnathan son of Achbor and some other men to Egypt. 23They brought Uriah out of Egypt. They took him to King Jehoiakim. The king killed him with a sword. He threw his body into the common people's graves.

Jeremiah Is Safe

24But Ahikam son of Shaphan helped Jeremiah. So Jeremiah was not given to the people to be killed.
