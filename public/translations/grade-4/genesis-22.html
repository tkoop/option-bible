God Tests Abraham

1After these things, God tested Abraham. He said, "Abraham!"

"Here I am," Abraham said.

2God said, "Take your son, your only son, whom you love. Take Isaac. Go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will show you."

3Early in the morning, Abraham got up. He put a saddle on his donkey. He took two young men with him and Isaac his son. He cut wood for the burnt offering. Then he got up and went to the place God had told him about. 4On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the place far away.

5Abraham said to his young men, "Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there. We will worship, and then we will come back to you."

6Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering. He put it on Isaac his son. He took the fire and the knife in his hand. The two of them walked on together.
