David's Last Words

1These are David's last words. David, son of Jesse, said this. He was the man God lifted up. He was chosen by the God of Jacob. He wrote songs for Israel.

2The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD spoke through me. His word was on my tongue.

3The God of Israel spoke. The RockrockRock of Israel said to me, "A good leader rules people fairly and shows respect for God.

4He shines like the morning sun. He is like rain that makes the grass grow."

5My family is not perfect with God. But God made a promise to me. It lasts forever. It is safe and sure. He will give me help and everything I hope for.

6But bad men are like thorns. They are thrown away. No one can pick them up with bare hands. 7A man who touches them needs iron or wood. They are burned in the fire.

David's Brave Soldiers

8These are the names of David's mighty men. Josheb-Basshebeth the Tahkemonite was the leader of the ThreethreeThree. He killed eight hundred men in one battle.

9Next was Eleazar son of Dodo, the Ahohite. He was one of the three brave men with David. The men of Israel ran away when the Philistines came to fight. But Eleazar stood and fought. 10He fought the Philistines until his hand was so tired he could not let go of his sword. The LORD gave a great victory that day. The people came back only to take things from the dead.

11Next was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered at a field full of lentils. The people ran away from the Philistines. 12But Shammah stood in the middle of the field and saved it. He struck down the Philistines. The LORD gave a great victory.

Three Brave Men Get Water for David

13At harvest time, three of the thirty leaders went down to David at the cave of Adullam. A Philistine army was camped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 14David was in a safe place. Philistine soldiers were in Bethlehem then.

15David wanted water. He said, "I wish I could drink water from the well by the gate in Bethlehem!"

16The three brave men broke through the Philistine camp. They drew water from the well by the gate of Bethlehem. They brought it to David. But David would not drink it. He poured it out to the LORD.

17David said, "ThetheThe LORD, I would never drink this! It is like the blood of men who risked their lives." So David did not drink it. These three brave men did this.

18Abishai, Joab's brother and son of Zeruiah, was the leader of the ThreethreeThree. He swung his spear at three hundred men and killed them. He was famous among the ThreethreeThree. 19He was the most honouredhonouredhonored of the ThreethreeThree. So he became their leader. But he was not one of the first ThreethreeThree.

20Benaiah son of Jehoiada was from Kabzeel. He did many brave things. He killed two of Moab's best fighters. He also went down into a pit on a snowy day and killed a lion.

21He killed a big Egyptian man. The Egyptian had a spear. Benaiah went down to him with a stick. He took the spear from the Egyptian's hand and killed him with it.

22Benaiah son of Jehoiada did these things. He was famous among the three brave men. 23He was more honouredhonouredhonored than the thirty. But he did not reach the first ThreethreeThree. David made him leader of his guards.
