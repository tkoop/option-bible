David Prays in a Cave

1I cry out loud to the the LORD. I beg the the LORD to help me. 2I tell the the LORD all my worries. I tell HimhimHim how much trouble I am in.

3I feel weak inside, but YouyouYou know where I go. People hide traps where I walk. 4Look to my right and see. No one knows me. I have nowhere to run. No one cares about my life.

5I cry to YouyouYou, the LORD. I say, "YouyouYou are my safe place. You are my hope in this life." 6Listen to my cry, because I am very weak. Save me from the people chasing me. They are stronger than I am.

7Get me out of this prison, so I can thank YouyouYou. Good people will be around me, because YouyouYou will be good to me.
