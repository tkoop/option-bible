Saul Plans to Trap David

David Hides in the Desert

14David stayed in the desert in safe places. He stayed in the hills in the desert of Ziph. Saul looked for him every day, but God did not let Saul catch him. 15David saw that Saul had come out to look for him. David was in the desert of Ziph at Horesh.

Jonathan Visits David

16Jonathan, Saul's son, went to David at Horesh. He helped David trust God. 17Jonathan said to him, "Do not be afraid. My father will not find you. You will be king of Israel. I will be next to you. My father knows this too." 18The two men made a promise to each other before the the LORD. Then David stayed at Horesh, and Jonathan went home.

The Men of Ziph Tell Saul

19Some men from Ziph went up to Saul at Gibeah. They said, "David is hiding among us in the safe places. He is at Horesh, on the hill of Hakilah, south of Jeshimon." 20They said, "Now, KingkingKing, come down whenever you want. It will be our job to give him to you."

He is very tricky." 23Saul said, "Look and find all the places where he hides. Then come back to me with sure news. I will go with you. If he is in the land, I will search for him all over Judah."

David Escapes Again

24The men went to Ziph ahead of Saul. David and his men were in the desert of Maon, in the dry land south of Jeshimon. 25Saul and his men went to look for David. Someone told David, and he went down to the rock and stayed in the desert of Maon. Saul heard about it and chased David in the desert of Maon. 26Saul was on one side of the mountain, and David and his men were on the other side. David was hurrying to get away because Saul was close. Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to catch them.

27A messenger came to Saul and said, "Hurry! The Philistines are attacking the land."
