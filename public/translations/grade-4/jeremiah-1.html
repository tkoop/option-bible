Jeremiah's Family and Home

1These are the words of Jeremiah. He was the son of Hilkiah. He was one of the priests who lived in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin.

2The word of the LORD came to him. This was in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah. It was in the thirteenth year of his rule.

3It also came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. It came until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah. That was when Jerusalem was taken away in the fifth month.

The LORD Chooses Jeremiah

4The word of the LORD came to me. He said, "5Before you were born, I knew you. Before you came into the world, I made you special. I chose you to be a prophet to the nations."

6"Ah, Lord, the LORD!" I said. "I do not know how to speak. I am too young."

7The LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am too young.' YouyouYou must go to everyone I send you to. You must say everything I tell you. 8Do not be afraid of them. I am with you, and I will rescue you," says the LORD.

9Then the LORD stretched out HishisHis hand and touched my mouth. He said to me, "See, I have put MymyMy words in your mouth. 10Today I put you in charge of nations and kingdoms. You will pull up and tear down. You will destroy and knock down. You will build and plant."

Jeremiah Sees an Almond Branch

11The word of the LORD came to me. He said, "What do you see, Jeremiah?"

"I see a branch from an almond tree," I said.

12The LORD said to me, "YouyouYou have seen right. I am watching to make sure MymyMy word comes true."

Jeremiah Sees a Boiling Pot

13The word of the LORD came to me again. He said, "What do you see?"

"I see a pot of boiling water," I said. "It is tilting away from the north."

14The LORD said to me, "From the north, trouble will come to all who live in this land. 15I am calling all the families and kingdoms of the north. Their kings will come and set up their thrones at the gates of Jerusalem. They will come against all its walls and all the cities of Judah."

16The LORD said, "I will judge them for all their wrongs. They left MemeMe and worshiped other gods. They bowed down to things their hands made."

The LORD Makes Jeremiah Strong

17The LORD said to me, "Get ready now. Stand up and tell them everything I tell you to do. Do not be afraid of them. If you are, I will make you afraid in front of them."

18The LORD said, "Today I have made you a strong city, an iron pillar, and walls of bronze. You will stand against the whole land, against the kings of Judah, its leaders, its priests, and the people of the land."

19"They will fight you, but they will not win. I am with you, and I will rescue you," says the LORD.
