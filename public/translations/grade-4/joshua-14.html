The Land Is Shared

1These are the parts of land the people of Israel got in Canaan. Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the tribes gave out the land. 2They gave out the land by drawing lots to the nine and a half tribes. The LORD had told Moses to do this. 3Moses had already given land to two and a half tribes on the other side of the Jordan. He did not give land to the Levites among them. 4Joseph's family became two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites did not get a piece of land. They got cities to live in and fields for their animals. 5So the people of Israel did what the LORD told Moses. They split up the land.

Caleb Asks for His Land

6Then the people of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb son of Jephunneh, a Kenizzite, said to him, "YouyouYou know what the LORD said to Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea about you and me. 7I was 40 years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to look at the land. I came back and gave him an honest report. 8My brothers who went with me scared the people. But I followed the LORD my God with all my heart. 9That day Moses promised me, 'Surely the land your foot walked on will be yours and your children's forever, because you followed the LORD my God with all your heart.' 10Now look, the LORD has kept me alive for 45 years since HeheHe spoke this word to Moses, while Israel walked in the desert. Today I am 85 years old. 11I am still as strong today as I was when Moses sent me. I am as strong now as I was then, for war and for all I do. 12So give me this hill country the LORD talked about that day. For you heard then that the Anakites were there, with big cities that had strong walls. Maybe the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, just as the LORD said."

Caleb Gets Hebron

13Then Joshua blessed Caleb son of Jephunneh and gave him Hebron for his land. 14So Hebron belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as his land to this day, because he followed the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. 15Hebron used to be called Kiriath-arba, after Arba, the greatest man among the Anakites. Then the land was at peace.
