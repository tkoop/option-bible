God’s Spirit Comes to Azariah

1Now God’s SpiritspiritSpirit came on Azariah son of Oded. 2He went out to meet Asa. He said, “Listen to me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin. The LORD is with you when you are with HimhimHim. If you look for HimhimHim, HeheHe will let you find HimhimHim. But if you leave HimhimHim, HeheHe will leave you.

“3For many days, Israel did not have the true God. They did not have a priest to teach them. They did not have God’s law. 4But when they were in trouble, they turned back to the LORD, the God of Israel. They looked for HimhimHim, and HeheHe let them find HimhimHim. 5In those days, people who went out or came in had no peace. There were many fights among the people who lived in those lands. 6One nation crushed another nation. One city crushed another city. God made trouble for them in every way. 7But you, be strong. Do not give up. Your work will be rewarded.”

Asa Makes Big Changes

8When Asa heard these words and the message from Oded the prophet, he became brave. He took away the hated idols from all Judah and Benjamin and from the cities he had taken in the hills of Ephraim. He fixed the altar of the LORD that was in front of the porch of the LORD’s house.

The People Gather to God

9Then he gathered all Judah and Benjamin. He also gathered people from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who lived with them. Many people had come to him from Israel. They came because they saw that the LORD, his God, was with him. 10They gathered in Jerusalem in the third month. This was in the fifteenth year of Asa’s rule.

They Offer Gifts and Make a Promise

11At that time, they gave the LORD seven hundred bulls and seven thousand sheep. These came from the things they had taken in battle. 12Then they made a promise to look for the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and all their soul. 13Anyone who would not look for the LORD, the God of Israel, would be put to death. This was for small and great, man or woman. 14They shouted to the LORD with a loud voice. They shouted and blew trumpets and horns.

15All Judah was happy about the promise. They had sworn it with all their heart. They looked for HimhimHim with all their strength, and HeheHe let them find HimhimHim. So the LORD gave them rest on every side.

Asa Removes His Grandmother

16King Asa also took away his grandmother Maacah from being queen mother. She had made a hated Asherah pole. Asa cut down the pole. He chopped it up. He burned it in the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 17The high places were not taken away from Israel. But Asa’s heart was fully given to the LORD all his days. 18He brought into God’s house the silver and gold things and the tools that he and his father had set apart for God. 19There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa’s rule.
