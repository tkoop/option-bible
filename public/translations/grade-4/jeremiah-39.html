Jerusalem Is Taken

1In the ninth year that Zedekiah was king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came with all his army to Jerusalem. They surrounded the city and kept people from going in or out. 2On the ninth day of the fourth month of Zedekiah's eleventh year, the city wall was broken through.

3Then all the leaders of the king of Babylon came in and sat at the MiddlemiddleMiddle GategateGate. They were Nergal-Sharezer, Samgar-Nebu, Sarsechim, Nergal-Sharezer the Rabmag, and all the other leaders of the king of Babylon.

Zedekiah Tries to Escape

4When Zedekiah, king of Judah, and all his soldiers saw them, they ran away. They left the city at night through the king's garden. They went out through the gate between the two walls and headed toward the desert. 5But the Babylonian army chased them. They caught Zedekiah on the flat land near Jericho. They took him to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, at Riblah in the land of Hamath. There the king decided his punishment. 6At Riblah, the king of Babylon killed Zedekiah's sons while Zedekiah watched. He also killed all the important men of Judah. 7Then he put out Zedekiah's eyes. He tied him with bronze chains and took him to Babylon.

The City Is Burned

8The Babylonians burned the king's house and the people's houses. They also tore down the walls of Jerusalem. 9Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, took the rest of the people who were left in the city to Babylon. This included the people who had gone over to the Babylonians.

The Poor People Stay

10But Nebuzaradan left some of the poor people in the land of Judah. They had nothing. That day he gave them grape fields and other fields.

Jeremiah Is Set Free

11Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had given orders about Jeremiah through Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard. He said, 12"Take him. Keep him safe. Do not hurt him in any way. Do for him whatever he asks you."

13So Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, and Nebushazban, Nergal-Sharezer the Rabmag, and all the other chief officers of the king of Babylon sent men. 14They took Jeremiah out of the courtyard of the guard. They gave him to Gedaliah, son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, to take him home. So Jeremiah stayed with the people.

A Message for Ebed-Melech

15While Jeremiah was still held in the courtyard of the guard, the LORD spoke to him.

16"Go and tell Ebed-Melech, a man from Cush, that I, the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, say this: I am about to do what I said I would do to this city. It will bring trouble, not good. In that day these things will come true before your eyes.

17"But I will save you that day, says the LORD. You will not be given to the men you fear. 18I will surely save you. You will not die by the sword. You will keep your life, because you trusted in MemeMe, says the LORD."
