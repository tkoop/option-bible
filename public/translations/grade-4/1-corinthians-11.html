Follow Paul's Example

1Follow my example, like I follow Christ.

2I praise you because you remember me in all things and keep the teachings I gave you.

Head Coverings in Worship

3But I want you to know this: Christ is the leader of every man. A man is the leader of a woman. And God is the leader of Christ.

4Every man who prays or speaks God's message with his head covered brings shame on himself.

5But every woman who prays or speaks God's message with her head uncovered shames herself. It is just as bad as if her hair were cut off.

6If a woman will not cover her head, she should cut her hair short. But if it is shameful for a woman to cut her hair short or shave her head, then she should cover her head.

7A man should not cover his head. He is made like God and shows God's glory. But a woman shows a man's glory.

8Man did not come from woman. Woman came from man.

9Man was not made for woman. Woman was made for man.

10So a woman should have a sign of authority on her head, because of the angels.

11But with the Lord, men and women belong together. Neither can be without the other.

12Just as woman came from man, man is born through woman. And all things come from God.

13You decide for yourselves: Is it right for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?

14Does not nature itself teach you that long hair on a man is a shame to him?

15But long hair on a woman is her glory. Her hair is given to her like a covering.

16If anyone wants to argue about this, we have no such custom. The churches of God do not either.

Problems at Church Meals

17Now when I give this command, I do not praise you. You come together, but it is for the worse, not for the better.

18First, I hear that when you come together as a church, there are splits among you. I partly believe it.

19There must be groups among you so that the ones who are true can be seen.

20So when you come together, it is not really the Lord's SuppersupperSupper you eat.

21When you eat, each one grabs his own supper first. One person is hungry, and another gets drunk.

22Don't you have homes where you can eat and drink? Or do you look down on God's church and shame people who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I praise you? In this I will not praise you.

The Lord's Supper

23I got this from the Lord, and I gave it to you: On the night HeheHe was handed over, the Lord Jesus took bread.

24He thanked God, broke it, and said, “This is MymyMy body, which is for you. Do this to remember MemeMe.”

25In the same way, after supper, HeheHe took the cup and said, “This cup is the new agreement in MymyMy blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, to remember MemeMe.”

26For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you tell about the Lord's death until HeheHe comes.

27So anyone who eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in a wrong way will be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.

28A person should look at himself first. Then he may eat the bread and drink the cup.

29If he eats and drinks without thinking about what it means, he brings judgmentjudgementjudgment on himself.

30That is why many of you are weak and sick, and some have died.

31If we looked at ourselves, we would not be judged.

32When the Lord judges us, HeheHe trains us so we will not be punished with the world.

33So, my brothers and sisters, when you come together to eat, wait for each other.

34If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home. Then you will not come together for judgmentjudgementjudgment. I will tell you the rest when I come.
