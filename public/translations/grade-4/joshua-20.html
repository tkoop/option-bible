Safe Cities for People Who Kill by Accident

1The LORD said to Joshua, 2"Tell the people of Israel to choose safe cities for themselves. I told them about these cities through Moses. 3A person who kills someone by accident can run there. These cities will keep him safe from the relative who wants revenge. 4He will run to one of these cities. He will stand at the city gate. He will tell the city leaders what happened. Then they will let him come inside. They will give him a place to live with them.

5"If the relative who wants revenge comes after him, the city leaders must not give him up. The killer did not mean to kill his neighbourneighbourneighbor. He did not hate him before. 6He must live in that city until he goes to court in front of the people. He stays until the high priest dies. Then he can go back home to the city he ran from."

The Six Safe Cities

7So they chose Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath Arba, which is Hebron, in the hill country of Judah. 8On the east side of the Jordan near Jericho, they gave Bezer in the desert on the flat land from the tribe of Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan from the tribe of Manasseh.

9These were the cities chosen for all the people of Israel and for strangers living among them. Anyone who killed a person by accident could run there. He would not be killed by the relative who wanted revenge until he went to court in front of the people.
