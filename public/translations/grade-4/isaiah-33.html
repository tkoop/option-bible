A Warning to the One Who Destroys

1Oh no, you destroyer! You have not been destroyed yet. You traitor! No one has turned on you yet. But when you stop destroying, you will be destroyed. When you stop being a traitor, people will turn on you.

A Prayer for Help

2The LORD, be kind to us. We wait for YouyouYou. Be our strength each morning. Save us when we are in trouble.

3When YouyouYou speak, people run away. When YouyouYou stand up, nations scatter.

4Your things that were taken will be gathered up like caterpillars gather. People will rush on them like locusts swarm.

The LORD Is Great

5The LORD is great. He lives high above. He filled Zion with what is fair and right.

6He will be a strong base for your times. He gives much salvation, wisdom, and knowledge. HonouringHonouringHonoring the LORD is Zion’s treasure.

Sad News for the Land

7Look! Their brave fighters cry in the streets. The messengers of peace cry hard. 8The roads are empty. Travelers stop going. The agreement is broken. Cities are hated. People are not respected anymore. 9The land is sad and weak. Lebanon is ashamed and dries up. Sharon is like a desert. Bashan and Carmel drop their leaves.

The LORD Will Act

10“Now I will stand up,” says the LORD. “Now I will raise MyselfmyselfMyself. Now I will be great.”

11You make empty plans. You bring forth useless straw. Your own breath is like fire that will burn you up. 12People will be burned like lime. Cut thorns will be set on fire.

God’s Power Is Known

13You who are far away, listen to what I did. You who are near, know MymyMy power.

14Sinners in Zion are scared. Shaking grabs the people who pretend to be good. They ask, “Who can live with a fire that eats? Who can live with flames that never end?”

The One Who Lives Right

15The one who lives right and speaks truth refuses money from cheating. He will not take bribes. He closes his ears to plans to kill. He shuts his eyes so he does not look at evil. 16He will live high up. His safe place will be strong rocks. His food will be given. His water will be sure.

Seeing the King

17Your eyes will see the KingkingKing in HishisHis beauty. They will see a land far away. 18Your mind will think about the old fear: “Where is the one who keeps records? Where is the one who weighs? Where is the one who counts the towers?”

19You will not see the mean people anymore. They speak a language you do not understand. They stammer in words you cannot figure out.

Zion Will Be Safe

20Look at Zion, the city of our festivals. Your eyes will see Jerusalem. It will be a safe place to live. It will be like a tent that is not moved. Its stakes will never be pulled up. None of its ropes will break. 21But there the the LORD, who is great, will be for us. It will be a place of rivers and wide waters. No boat with oars will go there. No big ship will pass by.

22The LORD is our judge. the LORD makes our laws. the LORD is our King. He will save us.

23Your ropes are loose. They cannot hold the mast up. They cannot spread the sail. Then a lot of treasure will be shared. Even people who cannot walk will take stolen things. 24No one living in Zion will say, “I am sick.” The people living there will be forgiven for their sins.
