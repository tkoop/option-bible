Jehoram Becomes King of Israel

1In the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, Jehoram son of Ahab became king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria for twelve years. 2He did what was wrong to the LORD. But he was not as bad as his father and mother. He took away the stone pillar of Baal that his father had made. 3But he held on to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who led Israel to sin. He did not stop.

Moab Stops Paying Israel

4Mesha, king of Moab, raised sheep. He gave the king of Israel 100,000 lambs and the wool of 100,000 rams. 5When Ahab died, the king of Moab stopped obeying the king of Israel.

Three Kings Go to War

6King Jehoram left Samaria that day and gathered all Israel. 7He sent a message to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah. He said, "ThetheThe king of Moab has turned against me. Will you go with me to fight Moab?"

Jehoshaphat said, "I will go. I am like you. My people are like your people. My horses are like your horses."

8Jehoram asked, "Which way should we go?"

Jehoshaphat said, "Through the desert of Edom."

No Water in the Camp

9So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom went. They travelledtravelledtraveled seven days. There was no water for the army or for the animals with them. 10The king of Israel said, "Oh no! The LORD has brought us three kings together so Moab can beat us."

11Jehoshaphat said, "IsisIs there no prophet of the LORD here? We can ask the LORD through him."

One of the king of Israel's servants said, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here. He helped Elijah."

12Jehoshaphat said, "ThetheThe LORD speaks through him." So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to see Elisha.

13Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What do you want from me? Go to your father's prophets and your mother's prophets."

The king of Israel said, "No! The LORD has brought us three kings together so Moab can beat us."

14Elisha said, "As surely as the LORD of Armies lives, I would not even look at you if I did not respect Jehoshaphat, king of Judah."

The LORD Sends Water

15Elisha said, "But now bring me someone who can play music." While he played, the LORD's power came on Elisha. 16Elisha said, "This is what the LORD says: Dig many ditches in this valley. 17You will not see wind or rain. But this valley will fill with water. You and your animals can drink it. 18This is easy for the LORD. He will also give Moab to you. 19You will attack every strong city and every good city. You will cut down every good tree. You will stop all the water springs. You will ruin the good land with stones."

20In the morning, at offering time, water came from the direction of Edom. The land filled with water.

Moab Is Tricked

21All Moab heard that the kings had come to fight. So every man who could fight, and even older men, were called together. They stood at the border. 22They got up early. The sun shone on the water. To Moab, the water looked red like blood. 23They said, "This is blood! The kings must have fought and killed each other. Now, Moab, let's take their things!"

24When they came to Israel's camp, Israel's army got up and fought Moab. The Moabites ran away. The Israelites chased them and kept fighting them in the land. 25They destroyed the cities. They threw stones on the good land until it was full. They stopped up the water springs. They cut down every good tree. Only the city of Kir Hareseth was left, with its stones. Men with slings surrounded it and attacked it.

26The king of Moab saw the battle was too strong for him. He took 700 men with swords to break through to the king of Edom. But they could not. 27Then he took his oldest son, who would be king after him. He offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. The Israelites were shocked. They left and went back to their own land.
