The Family Line of Adam

1This is the family line of Adam. God made man to look like God. 2God made them male and female. God blessed them and called them Adam on the day they were made.

3Adam was 130 years old when he had a son who looked like him. He named the boy Seth. 4After Seth was born, Adam lived 800 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 5Adam lived 930 years in all. Then he died.

6Seth was 105 years old when he had a son named Enosh. 7After Enosh was born, Seth lived 807 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 8Seth lived 912 years in all. Then he died.

9Enosh was 90 years old when he had a son named Kenan. 10After Kenan was born, Enosh lived 815 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 11Enosh lived 905 years in all. Then he died.

12Kenan was 70 years old when he had a son named Mahalalel. 13After Mahalalel was born, Kenan lived 840 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 14Kenan lived 910 years in all. Then he died.

15Mahalalel was 65 years old when he had a son named Jared. 16After Jared was born, Mahalalel lived 830 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 17Mahalalel lived 895 years in all. Then he died.

18Jared was 162 years old when he had a son named Enoch. 19After Enoch was born, Jared lived 800 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 20Jared lived 962 years in all. Then he died.

Enoch Lives Close to God

21Enoch was 65 years old when he had a son named Methuselah. 22After Methuselah was born, Enoch lived close to God for 300 years. He had other sons and daughters. 23Enoch lived 365 years in all. 24Enoch lived close to God. Then he was gone, because God took him.

25Methuselah was 187 years old when he had a son named Lamech. 26After Lamech was born, Methuselah lived 782 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 27Methuselah lived 969 years in all. Then he died.

Noah Is Born

28Lamech was 182 years old when he had a son. 29He named the boy Noah. Lamech said, "This boy will give us rest from our hard work. The LORD cursed the ground." 30After Noah was born, Lamech lived 595 more years. He had other sons and daughters. 31Lamech lived 777 years in all. Then he died. 32Noah was 500 years old when he had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
