Paul Writes to the Church in Corinth

1I am Paul. God chose me to be a messenger for Christ Jesus. This was God's plan. Our brother Sosthenes is with me. 2I write to God's church in Corinth. God made you holy because of Christ Jesus. He called you to be HishisHis holy people. This letter is also for all people everywhere who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. He is their Lord and ours. 3May God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

4I always thank my God for you. I do this because of the grace God gave you in Christ Jesus. 5In HimhimHim, you became rich in every way. You are rich in all you say and in all you know. 6This shows that what Christ said is true among you. 7You do not lack any gift. You wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to come again. 8He will keep you strong to the end. Then you will be without blame on the day our Lord Jesus Christ comes. 9God is faithful. He called you to share life with HishisHis SonsonSon, Jesus Christ our Lord.

Do Not Split Into Groups

10Brothers, I ask you by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to agree with one another. Do not split into groups. Be joined together in the same mind and the same plan. 11My brothers, some from Chloe's family told me about you. They said you are arguing.

12I mean this: One of you says, "I follow Paul."

Another says, "I follow Apollos."

Another says, "I follow Peter."

Another says, "I follow Christ."

13Is Christ split into parts? Was Paul killed on a cross for you? Were you baptized in Paul's name?

14I thank God that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius. 15So no one can say you were baptized in my name. 16I also baptized the family of Stephanas. I do not remember if I baptized anyone else.

17Christ did not send me to baptizebaptisebaptize. He sent me to tell the good news. I do not use fancy words. I do this so the cross of Christ will not lose its power.

The Message About the Cross

18The message about the cross is foolish to people who are dying. But to us who are being saved, it is God's power. 19It is written, "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise. I will throw away the understanding of those who think they know." 20Where is the wise person? Where is the teacher of the law? Where is the thinker of this age? Has not God made the world's wisdom look foolish? 21The world did not know God through its own wisdom. So God was pleased to save those who believe through the foolish message we preach.

22Jews ask for signs. Greeks look for wisdom. 23But we preach Christ on the cross. This is a problem for Jews. It is foolish to people who are not Jews. 24But to those God has called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is God's power and God's wisdom. 25God's foolishness is wiser than people. God's weakness is stronger than people.

God Chose the Weak and Lowly

26Brothers, look at your own calling. Not many of you were wise by human standards. Not many were powerful. Not many came from important families. 27But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise. God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong. 28God chose things that are low and looked down on. He chose things the world counts as nothing. He did this to bring down things the world counts as something. 29So no person can brag before God. 30God put you in Christ Jesus. Christ became our wisdom from God. He became our right living, our being made holy, and our being set free. 31So, as it is written, "Let the one who brags brag about the Lord."
