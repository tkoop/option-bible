Job Says Life Is Hard

"1Life on EarthEarthearth is hard work. 2A slave wants shade. A worker hopes to get paid.

"3So I have months of empty days. I have nights of trouble. 4When I lie down, I ask, 'When will morning come?' I toss and turn until daylight.

"5Worms cover my body. Dust is on me. My skin breaks open and looks bad. 6My days go by fast, like thread on a loom. They end with no hope.

"7Remember that my life is short, like a breath. My eyes will not see good again. 8The person who sees me now will not see me again. Your eyes look at me, and then I am gone.

"9A cloud fades and is gone. In the same way, a person who goes down to the grave does not come back. 10He will not return to his house. His place will not know him anymore.
