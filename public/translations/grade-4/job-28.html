People Dig for Treasure

1People can find silver in mines. They can find gold and clean it. 2Iron comes from the dust. Copper is poured from stone. 3People put an end to the dark. They search to the farthest place, where black rock and deep shadow are. 4They break open a tunnel far from where people live. The place is forgotten by feet. People hang and swing there. 5The ground gives food. Under it, fire changes the rock. 6Its rocks hold sapphires. Its dust holds gold.

7No bird of prey knows that path. No falcon has seen it. 8Proud animals have not walked there. No lion has gone over it. 9People put their hands on hard rock. They turn mountains over from the roots. 10They cut tunnels through rock. Their eyes see every precious thing. 11They hold back streams so water does not drip. They bring hidden things out into the light.

Where Can Wisdom Be Found?

12But where can wisdom be found? Where is the place to get good sense? 13No one knows how much it is worth. It is not found where living people are.

14The deep says, “It is not in me.”

The sea says, “It is not with me.”

15It cannot be bought with gold. No amount of silver can pay for it. 16It cannot be valued with gold from Ophir, with costly onyx or sapphire. 17Gold and glass cannot equal it. It cannot be traded for fine gold. 18No one talks about coral or crystal. Wisdom costs more than pearls. 19The topaz of Cush cannot equal it. It cannot be valued with pure gold.

Wisdom Is Hidden from People

20So where does wisdom come from? Where is the place to get good sense? 21It is hidden from all living eyes. It is kept from the birds of the sky. 22Destruction and Death say, “We have only heard about it.”

God Knows Where Wisdom Is

23God understands the way to it. He knows where it is. 24He looks to the ends of the earth. He sees under the whole sky. 25He made a weight for the wind. He measured the water. 26He made a rule for the rain. He made a path for the thunderbolt. 27Then HeheHe saw it and told about it. He set it up and studied it.

True Wisdom Is to Fear the Lord

28And HeheHe said to man, “Look, the fear of the Lord is wisdom. To turn away from evil is good sense.”
