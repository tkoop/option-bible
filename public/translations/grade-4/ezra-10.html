Ezra Prays for the People

1While Ezra prayed and said he was sorry, he cried and fell down in front of God's house. A very big crowd of Israel's men, women, and children came to him. The people cried a lot.

Shecaniah Brings Hope

2Shecaniah son of Jehiel, from the family of Elam, said to Ezra, “We have not been faithful to our God. We married women from other lands. But there is still hope for Israel. 3Now let’s make a promise to our God. We will send away all these foreign wives and their children. We will do this the way you, my lord, and those who obey God's commands say. We will follow God's LawlawLaw. 4Get up! This is your job, and we are with you. Be strong and do it.”

The People Make a Promise

5So Ezra got up. He made the priests, the Levites, and all Israel promise to do this. They promised.

6Then Ezra left God's house. He went to the room of Johanan son of Eliashib. He did not eat bread or drink water. He was sad because the people who had come back had done wrong.

A Message Goes Out

7They sent out a message all through Judah and Jerusalem. It told all the people who had come back to meet in Jerusalem. 8If anyone did not come in three days, he would lose everything he owned. He would also be kept away from the group of people who had come back. The leaders and elders decided this.

The People Meet in the Rain

9In three days, all the men of Judah and Benjamin came to Jerusalem. It was the twentieth day of the ninth month. All the people sat in the open area in front of God's house. They shook because of this problem and because of the rain.

Ezra Speaks to the Crowd

10Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, “You have not been faithful. You married women from other lands. This made Israel's guilt even bigger. 11Now say you are sorry to the LORD, the God of your fathers. Do what HeheHe wants. Stay away from the people of the land and from these foreign wives.”

12All the people answered in a loud voice, “You are right! We will do what you said. 13But there are many people. It is the rainy time, and we cannot stand outside. This work will take more than one or two days. We have done a very wrong thing. 14Let our leaders stand for all of us. Let every man in our towns who married a foreign wife come at set times. Let the elders and judges of each town come with them. We will do this until God stops being so angry with us about this.”

15Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah were against this. Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them.

The Leaders Make a Plan

16The people who had come back did this. Ezra the priest chose men who were leaders of their families. He chose them by name. They sat down on the tenth day of the tenth month to look into this. 17They finished with all the men who had married foreign wives by the first day of the first month.

Men Who Married Foreign Wives

18Here are the priests who had married foreign wives: From the family of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah. 19They promised to send their wives away. They also brought a ram as a guilt offering because they were guilty. 20From the family of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah. 21From the family of Harim: Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah. 22From the family of Pashhur: Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Eleasah.

23From the Levites: Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah (also called Kelita), Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer. 24From the singers: Eliashib. From the gatekeepers: Shallum, Telem, and Uri.

25From the people of Israel: From the family of Parosh: Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eleazar, Malchijah, and Benaiah. 26From the family of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah. 27From the family of Zattu: Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza. 28From the family of Bebai: Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai. 29From the family of Bani: Meshullam, Malluch, Adaiah, Jashub, Sheal, and Jeremoth. 30From the family of Pahath-moab: Adna, Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Binnui, and Manasseh. 31From the family of Harim: Eliezer, Ishijah, Malchijah, Shemaiah, and Shimeon. 32Benjamin, Malluch, and Shemariah. 33From the family of Hashum: Mattenai, Mattattah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, and Shimei.

34From the family of Bani: Maadai, Amram, and Uel. 35Benaiah, Bedeiah, and Chelluh. 36Vaniah, Meremoth, and Eliashib. 37Mattaniah, Mattenai, and Jaasau. 38Bani, Binnui, and Shimei. 39Shelemiah, Nathan, and Adaiah. 40Machnadebai, Shashai, and Sharai. 41Azarel, Shelemiah, and Shemariah. 42Shallum, Amariah, and Joseph.

43From the family of Nebo: Jeiel, Mattithiah, Zabad, Zebina, Jaddai, Joel, and Benaiah. 44All these men had married women from other lands. Some of them had children with these wives.
