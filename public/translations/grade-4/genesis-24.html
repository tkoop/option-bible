Abraham Sends His Servant

1Abraham was very old. The LORD had blessed him in every way. 2Abraham said to his oldest servant, the one who ruled all he had, "Make a serious promise to me. 3Promise by the LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth, that you will not get a wife for my son from the Canaanite girls who live near me. 4Go to my old home and my family. 6Abraham said, "No. Do not take my son back there. 7The LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, took me from my father's house and my old home. The Servant Prays at the Well

10The servant took ten of his master's camels and left. He took many good things from his master. He went to the city of Nahor in Mesopotamia. 11In the evening, when women came to get water, he made the camels kneel by the well outside the city.

12He prayed, "the LORD, God of my master Abraham, please help me today. Show kindness to my master Abraham. 13I am standing by the spring. The young women of the city are coming out to get water. 14Let the girl who says, 'Please give me a drink,' and then says, 'I will get water for your camels too,' be the one YouyouYou have chosen for Isaac. Then I will know YouyouYou have been kind to my master."

Rebekah Comes to the Well

15Before he finished praying, Rebekah came out. She had a jar on her shoulder. She was the daughter of Bethuel, who was the son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham's brother Nahor. 16The girl was very pretty and had never been married. She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came back up.

17The servant ran to meet her and said, "Please give me a little water from your jar."

18She said, "Drink, my lord." She hurried and lowered her jar on her hand and gave him a drink.

19When she finished giving him a drink, she said, "I will get water for your camels too, until they have all they need." 20She hurried and poured the water into the water tub. Then she ran to the well again and got water for all his camels.

21The man watched her quietly. He wanted to know if the LORD had made his trip a success.

22When the camels finished drinking, the man took a gold nose ring and two gold bracelets. He put them on her hands.

23He asked, "Whose daughter are you? Tell me. Is there room in your father's house for us to stay?"

24She said, "I am the daughter of Bethuel. Milcah is his mother, and Nahor is his father. 25We have plenty of straw and feed, and room for you to stay."

26The man bowed down and worshiped the LORD. 27He said, "Praise the LORD, the God of my master Abraham. He has not stopped being kind and true to my master. The LORD led me straight to my master's relatives."

28The girl ran and told her mother's family what had happened.

29Rebekah had a brother named Laban. Laban ran out to the man at the spring. 30He had seen the ring and bracelets on his sister's hands. He had heard her say what the man said to her. So he went to the man, who was standing by the camels at the spring.

31Laban said, "Come in, you who are blessed by the LORD. Why are you standing outside? I have made the house ready and a place for the camels."

32So the man came into the house. Laban unloaded the camels. He gave them straw and feed. He gave water to wash the feet of the man and his helpers.

33Food was set before him. But he said, "I will not eat until I tell why I came."

Laban said, "Speak."

The Servant Meets Rebekah's Family

52When Abraham's servant heard this, he bowed to the ground to worship the LORD.

53The servant brought out silver and gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry and clothes. He gave them to Rebekah. He also gave nice gifts to her brother and mother.

61Rebekah and her maids got up and rode on the camels. They followed the man. The servant took Rebekah and left.

Isaac Meets Rebekah

62Isaac had come from Beer-lahai-roi. He was living in the south country. 63In the evening, Isaac went out to the field to think and pray. He looked up and saw camels coming.

64Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac. She got down from her camel.

65She asked the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?"

The servant said, "HeheHe is my master." So she took her veil and covered herself.

66The servant told Isaac everything he had done. 67Isaac brought her into his mother Sarah's tent. He married Rebekah. He loved her. Isaac was comforted after his mother died.
