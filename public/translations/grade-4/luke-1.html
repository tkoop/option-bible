Luke Writes to a Friend

1Many people have tried to write down the things that happened among us. 2They wrote what the first people who saw it and helped teach the good news told us. 3I have studied all of this carefully from the start. So I decided to write it down in order for you, Theophilus. 4I want you to know that the things you were taught are true.

Zechariah and Elizabeth

5When Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah. He was from the priest group of Abijah. His wife was named Elizabeth. She was from Aaron's family. 6Both of them did what was right in God's eyes. They followed all the Lord's commands without blame. 7But they had no children. Elizabeth could not have a baby. They were both very old.

An Angel Visits Zechariah

8One day, Zechariah was doing his priest work. His group was on duty. 9He was chosen to go into the Lord's temple and burn incense. 10The whole crowd of people prayed outside while the incense burned.

11Then an angel of the Lord stood on the right side of the incense altar. 12Zechariah saw him and was scared. Fear came over him.

13The angel said, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah. God has heard your prayer. 14Your wife Elizabeth will have a son for you. 15You will name him John. he will bring you joy and gladness. Many people will be happy when he is born. he will be great in the Lord's eyes. he will not drink wine or strong drink. he will be filled with the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit even before he is born. 16he will turn many of Israel's people back to the Lord their God. 17he will go before the Lord with the spirit and power of Elijah. he will turn fathers' hearts to their children. he will help stubborn people become wise. he will get the Lord's people ready."

18Zechariah said to the angel, "How can I know this? I am an old man. My wife is very old too."

19The angel answered, "I am Gabriel. I stand in front of God. I was sent to talk to you and bring you this good news. 20But you did not believe my words. So you will not be able to speak until these things happen. My words will come true at the right time."

21The people were waiting for Zechariah. They were surprised that he stayed in the temple so long. 22When he came out, he could not speak to them. They knew he had seen something special in the temple. He made signs to them, but he stayed unable to speak. 23When his days of service were over, he went home.

24After some days, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She stayed hidden for five months. She said, "ThetheThe Lord has done this for me. 25In these days HeheHe took away my shame among people."

28The angel came to her and said, "Greetings, Mary. You are very special. The Lord is with you." 29Mary was troubled by his words. She wondered what kind of greeting this was.

John Is Born

57When it was time for Elizabeth to have her baby, she gave birth to a son. 58Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had been very kind to her. They were happy with her.

59On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child. They wanted to name him after his father, Zechariah. 60But his mother said, "No. His name will be John."

61They said to her, "No one in your family has that name."

62They made signs to his father to ask what he wanted to name the child. 63He asked for something to write on. He wrote, "HishisHis name is John." Everyone was surprised.

John Grows Up

80The child grew and became strong in spirit. He lived in the desert until he was shown to Israel.
