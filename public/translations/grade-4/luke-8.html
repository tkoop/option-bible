They believe for a while, then turn away when trouble comes. 14The seeds among thorns are people who hear it. But as they go, worries, money, and fun things in life choke the word, and it does not grow fruit. 15The seeds in good soil are people who hear the word with a good and honest heart. They hold on to it and grow fruit with patience."

16"No one lights a lamp and covers it with a bowl or puts it under a bed. They put it on a stand, so people coming in can see the light. 17Nothing is hidden that will not be shown. Nothing is kept secret that will not come out and be seen. 18So listen well to how you hear. Whoever has will be given more.
