God's Message to Micah

1This is the word of the LORD that came to Micah from Moresheth. It came in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. Micah saw it about Samaria and Jerusalem. 2Listen, all you peoples! Listen, EarthEarthearth and everything in it! The Lord the LORD will be a witness against you. He is in HishisHis holy temple.

God Comes to Judge

3Look! The LORD is coming from HishisHis place. He will come down and walk on the high places of the earth. 4The mountains will melt under HimhimHim. The valleys will split open. They will be like wax in front of fire. They will be like water running down a hill. 5All this is because of the sin of Jacob and the sins of Israel. What is the sin of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? What is the high place of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

6So I will make Samaria a pile of rocks in a field. It will be a place to plant grape vines. I will pour its stones into the valley. I will show its foundations. 7All its carved statues will be smashed. All the things it got for sin will be burned in fire. I will destroy all its idols. She got them with money from selling herself, and that money will go back to that kind of life.

Micah Cries for His People

8Because of this I will cry and wail. I will walk barefoot and with no clothes. I will cry like a wild dog and mourn like an owl. 9Her wound will not heal. It has reached Judah. It has reached the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem.

Towns That Will Fall

10Do not tell this in Gath. Do not cry at all. In Beth-le-aphrah, roll in the dust. 11Pass on, people of Shaphir, in shame and with no clothes. The people of Zaanan do not go out. The people of Beth-ezel mourn. He will take away its help from you. 12The people of Maroth wait for good. But bad has come down from the LORD to the gate of Jerusalem.

13Tie the chariot to the fast horse, people of Lachish. She was the first to lead Zion's people into sin. The wrongs of Israel were found in you. 14So you will give goodbye gifts to Moresheth-gath. The houses of Achzib will trick the kings of Israel. 15I will bring another enemy against you, people of Mareshah. The glory of Israel will come to Adullam. 16Shave your head and cut off your hair for the children you love. Make your bald spot as wide as an eagle's. They have gone far away from you.
