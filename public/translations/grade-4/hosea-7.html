Israel's Hidden Sins

1When I wanted to heal Israel, Ephraim's sin showed. Samaria did bad things too. They told lies. A thief came in, and robbers took things outside. 2They do not think about all their wrongs. Now their bad deeds surround them. They are right in front of MemeMe. 3They make the king happy with their bad deeds. They make the leaders happy with their lies. 4They all burn with sin, like a hot oven. The baker stops tending the fire while the dough rises.

5On our king's day, the leaders got sick from wine. The king shook hands with men who mock. 6They get ready in their hearts like a hot oven. They lie in wait all night. In the morning, it burns like a blazing fire. 7They are all hot like an oven. They eat up their judges. All their kings have fallen. None of them calls to MemeMe.

8Ephraim mixes with other nations. Ephraim is like bread cooked on only one side. 9Strangers eat his strength, and he does not know it. GreyGreyGray hair shows on him, and he does not know it. 10Israel's pride speaks against him. Yet they do not turn back to the LORD their God. They do not look for HimhimHim, even after all this.

Foolish Help from Egypt and Assyria

11Ephraim is like a silly dove with no sense. It calls to Egypt and goes to Assyria. 12When they go, I will spread MymyMy net over them. I will pull them down like birds in the sky. I will punish them as their people hear.

God Warns Israel

13Trouble for them, because they ran from MemeMe! Ruin for them, because they turned against MemeMe! I wanted to save them, but they tell lies about MemeMe. 14They do not cry to MemeMe from their hearts. They only wail on their beds. They gather for grain and new wine, and they turn away from MemeMe. 15I trained and strengthened their arms, but they plan bad things against MemeMe. 16They turn, but not to God above. They are like a bad bow. Their leaders will fall by the sword because of their proud words. This will make them a joke in Egypt.
