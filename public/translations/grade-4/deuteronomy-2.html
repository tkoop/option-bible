Israel Turns Toward the Desert

1Then we turned and travelledtravelledtraveled toward the desert along the Red SeaseaSea road, just as the LORD had told me. We went around Mount Seir for many days.

2The LORD said to me, 3"YouyouYou have gone around this hill country long enough. Turn north. 4Command the people, 'YouyouYou are about to pass through the land of your brothers, the people of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be very afraid of you. So be very careful.'"

5"Do not fight with them. I will not give you any of their land, not even a small piece, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau to own. 6You may buy food from them with money, and eat it. You may also buy water from them with money, and drink it. 7The LORD your God has blessed you in all your work. He has watched over your trip through this big desert. For forty years the LORD your God has been with you, and you have not needed anything."

Israel Passes Edom

8So we passed by our brothers, the people of Esau, who live in Seir. We went along the road through the desert valley, away from Elath and Ezion-geber. Then we turned and took the desert road to Moab.

God's Command About Moab

9The LORD said to me, "Do not trouble Moab. Do not start a war with them. I will not give you any of their land, because I have given Ar to the people of Lot to own."

Old Giants in Moab

10The Emim used to live there. They were a big, strong people, as tall as the Anakites. 11People thought they were Rephaim, just like the Anakites. But the Moabites called them Emim. 12The Horites used to live in Seir. But the people of Esau chased them out, destroyed them, and settled there, just as Israel did in the land the LORD gave them.

The Desert Years End

13"Now get up and cross the Zered ValleyvalleyValley." We crossed it. 14We travelledtravelledtraveled from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered ValleyvalleyValley. It took thirty-eight years. By then, all the soldiers of that generation had died, just as the LORD had promised. 15The LORD worked against them. He troubled them until they were all gone.

16When all the fighting men had died, 17the LORD spoke to me. 18"Today you are to cross the border of Moab at Ar. 19When you come near the people of Ammon, do not trouble them and do not start a war with them. I will not give you any of their land, because I have given it to the people of Lot to own."

Old Giants in Ammon

20That land was also thought to be Rephaim land. The Rephaim used to live there. The Ammonites called them Zamzummim. 21They were a big, strong people, as tall as the Anakites. But the LORD destroyed them. The Ammonites took their place and settled there. 22The LORD did the same thing for the people of Esau who live in Seir. He destroyed the Horites before them, and they took their place and settled there. They still live there today. 23The Avvim lived in villages as far as Gaza. The Caphtorites came from Caphtor and destroyed them and settled there.

God Gives Sihon to Israel

24"Get up and move on. Cross the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley. See, I have given you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Start to take his land, and fight him. 25Today I will begin to make all people everywhere afraid of you. When they hear about you, they will shake and be scared."

Asking Sihon for Peace

26From the desert of Kedemoth I sent messengers to Sihon king of Heshbon with a message of peace. 27I said, "Let me pass through your land. I will stay on the road and not turn right or left. 28You can sell me food for money, and I will eat. You can give me water for money, and I will drink. Just let me pass through on foot. 29Let me pass through, as the people of Esau in Seir and the Moabites in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land the LORD our God is giving us."

Sihon Says No

30But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through. The LORD your God had made him stubborn and hard-hearted, so HeheHe could give him to you.

31The LORD said to me, "See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land to you. Now begin to take his land and own it."

Israel Beats Sihon

32Sihon and all his people came out to fight us at Jahaz. 33The LORD our God gave him to us. We beat him, his sons, and all his people. 34At that time we captured all his cities. We destroyed every city completely. We killed the men, women, and children. We did not leave anyone alive. 35We took only the animals and the goods from the cities we captured. 36From Aroer by the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley to the city in the valley, all the way to Gilead, no city was too strong for us. The LORD our God gave them all to us.

Land Israel Did Not Take

37But you did not go near the land of the Ammonites. You stayed away from the Jabbok River, the hill country cities, and everything the LORD our God told us to leave alone.
