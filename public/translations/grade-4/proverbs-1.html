The Book of Proverbs

1These are the wise sayings of Solomon. He was David's son. He was the king of Israel.

Why These Sayings Were Written

2They help people know what is wise. They help people learn good lessons. 3They help people learn to be fair and do right. 4They give good sense to people who are not wise yet. They give young people knowledge and good plans. 5A wise person listens and learns more. A person with understanding finds good advice. 6These sayings help people understand short wise sayings. They help people understand what wise people say. They help people figure out hard questions.

The Start of Knowledge

7HonouringHonouringHonoring the LORD is where knowing starts. But fools hate wisdom and good lessons.

Listen to Your Parents

8My son, listen to your father's teaching. Do not forget your mother's words. 9They will be like a pretty crown on your head. They will be like a necklace around your neck.

Stay Away from Sinners

10My son, if sinners try to get you to do wrong, do not agree. 11They may say, "Come with us. Let us hide and hurt someone. Let us attack a person who has done nothing wrong. 12We will make them disappear like death. We will take them down whole, like people falling into a deep hole. 13We will find many valuable things. We will fill our homes with stolen goods. 14Share your money with us. We will all share one money bag."

15My son, do not walk in their way. Keep your feet away from their path. 16Their feet run to do bad things. They hurry to hurt people. 17It is useless to spread a net where a bird can see it. 18But these people set traps for their own lives. 19This is what happens to people who chase money the wrong way. It costs them their lives.

Wisdom Calls Out

20Wisdom shouts in the street. She raises her voice in the town squares. 21She calls out where crowds gather. She speaks at the city gates. She says, 22"How long will you people who are easily fooled love being fooled? How long will you who make fun of others enjoy it? How long will you fools hate learning? 23Turn back when I warn you. Then I will pour out MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit on you. I will make my words known to you."

24"I called, but you refused to listen. I reached out my hand, but no one cared. 25You ignored all my advice. You did not want me to correct you. 26So I will laugh when trouble comes to you. I will make fun of you when you are afraid. 27When fear comes like a storm and trouble comes like a strong wind, when hard times and pain come on you, I will laugh."

28"Then they will call to me, but I will not answer. They will look for me, but they will not find me. 29This is because they hated knowledge. They did not choose to respect the LORD. 30They did not want my advice. They laughed at all my warnings. 31So they will get what their choices deserve. They will be full of their own bad plans. 32People who are easily fooled can die because they turn away. Fools are destroyed by their own comfort. 33But the person who listens to me will live in safety. That person will be calm and not afraid of trouble."
