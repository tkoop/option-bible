Rules About Promises

1Moses told the leaders of Israel's tribes, "This is what the LORD commanded."

Promises Made by Men

2If a man makes a promise to the LORD, or makes a serious promise to do something, he must not break his word. He must do all he says.

Promises Made by Young Women

3If a young woman who still lives in her father's house makes a promise to the LORD, or makes a serious promise about what she will do, 4and her father hears it and says nothing, then all her promises stay in place. 5But if her father says no on the day he hears it, none of her promises stay in place. The LORD will forgive her, because her father said no.

Promises Made After Marriage

6If she gets married while she still has promises, or makes careless words that she must keep, 7and her husband hears it and says nothing, then her promises stay in place. 8But if her husband says no on the day he hears it, he cancels her promise and her careless promise. The LORD will forgive her.

Other Rules About Promises

9Any promise made by a widow or a divorced woman stays in place for her.

10If a woman makes a promise while in her husband's house, or makes a serious promise, 11and her husband hears it and says nothing and does not stop her, then all her promises stay in place. 12But if her husband cancels them on the day he hears them, then nothing she said in her promises stays in place. Her husband cancelledcancelledcanceled them, and the LORD will forgive her.

13A husband can say yes to or cancel any promise or serious promise his wife makes to give up something. 14If her husband says nothing day after day, he says yes to all her promises. He says yes to them because he said nothing when he heard them. 15But if he cancels them after he hears them, he is to blame.

16These are the rules the LORD gave Moses for a man and his wife, and for a father and his young daughter.
