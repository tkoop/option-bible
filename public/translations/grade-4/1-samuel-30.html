David Finds Ziklag Burned

1On the third day, David and his men came to Ziklag. The Amalekites had attacked the south country and Ziklag. They attacked Ziklag and burned it down. 2They took the women and everyone else there, young and old. They did not kill anyone. They just led them away. 3When David and his men came to the city, they saw it burned down. Their wives, sons, and daughters had been taken away.

David and His Men Are Very Sad

4David and the people with him cried out loud. They cried until they had no strength left to cry. 5David's two wives were taken too. One was Ahinoam from Jezreel. The other was Abigail, who had been Nabal's wife from Carmel. 6David was very upset. The people wanted to throw stones at him. They were all very sad because of their sons and daughters. But David found new strength in the LORD his God.

David Asks God What to Do

7Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Please bring me the special vest." So Abiathar brought it to David. 8David asked the LORD, "Should I chase these attackers? Will I catch them?"

The LORD said, "Chase them. You will catch them and save the captives."

David Chases the Attackers

9So David and his six hundred men went. They came to the BrookbrookBrook Besor. Some men stayed there. 10David and four hundred men kept chasing. Two hundred men were too tired to cross the brook, so they stayed behind.

David Finds an Egyptian Helper

11David's men found an Egyptian man in a field. They brought him to David. They gave him water to drink and food to eat. 12They gave him a piece of fig cake and two bunches of raisins. He ate and felt better. He had not eaten or drunk for three days and three nights.

13David asked him, "Who do you belong to? Where are you from?"

The man said, "I am an Egyptian. I am a slave of an Amalekite. My master left me three days ago because I was sick. 14We attacked the south country of the Cherethites, the land of Judah, and the south country of Caleb. We also burned Ziklag."

15David asked him, "Can you lead me to these attackers?"

He said, "Promise me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master. Then I will lead you to them."

David Beats the Amalekites

16So he led David there. The attackers were spread out over the whole area. They were eating, drinking, and having a party. They had taken many things from the land of the Philistines and the land of Judah. 17David fought them from evening until the next evening. No one got away except four hundred young men on camels. 18David got back everything the Amalekites had taken. He also got back his two wives. 19Nothing was missing. Not one small thing or big thing, not sons or daughters, not any of the things they had taken. David brought it all back. 20David took all the sheep and cattle. His men drove them ahead of the rest of the animals and said, "This is David's share of what he took."

David Shares What He Took

21David came to the two hundred men who had been too tired to follow him. They had stayed at the BrookbrookBrook Besor. They came out to meet David and the people with him. David greeted them. 22But some bad and useless men who had gone with David said, "They did not go with us, so we should not give them any of the things we took. They can take their wives and children and go."

23But David said, "No, my brothers. We must not do that with what the LORD has given us. He kept us safe and let us beat the attackers who came against us. 24Who would agree with you about this? The man who goes to fight gets the same share as the man who stays with the supplies. They will share the same."

25From that day on, David made this a rule for Israel.

David Sends Gifts

26When David got back to Ziklag, he sent some of the things he had taken to his friends, the elders of Judah. He said, "Here is a gift for you from the things taken from the LORD's enemies."

27He sent gifts to the people in Bethel, in Ramoth in the south country, and in Jattir. 28He sent gifts to the people in Aroer, in Siphmoth, and in Eshtemoa. 29He sent gifts to the people in Racal, in the towns of the Jerahmeelites, and in the towns of the Kenites. 30He sent gifts to the people in Hormah, in Bor-ashan, and in Athach. 31He sent gifts to the people in Hebron and to all the places where David and his men had gone.
