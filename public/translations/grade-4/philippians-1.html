Paul and Timothy Write to the Church

1Paul and Timothy, who serve Christ Jesus, send this to all of God's holy people in Christ Jesus in Philippi. We also send it to the leaders and helpers. 2May God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

Paul Prays for the Church

3I thank my God every time I remember you. 4In all my prayers for all of you, I always pray with joy.

5I thank God because you have helped spread the good news from the first day until now. 6I am sure that God, who began a good work in you, will keep working in you until the day Christ Jesus comes back.

7It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, because you are in my heart. You share God's grace with me. You share it while I am in chains and while I tell others about the good news and make it strong. 8God knows how much I long for all of you with the love of Christ Jesus.

9I pray that your love will grow more and more. I pray it will grow in knowing God and in good sense. 10Then you can choose what is best. You will be pure and good until the day Christ comes. 11You will be filled with the good fruit of right living. This comes through Jesus Christ and brings praise and glory to God.

Paul's Chains Help the Good News

12Brothers and sisters, I want you to know that what happened to me has helped spread the good news even more. 13Everyone in the palace guard and all the others know that I am in chains for Christ. 14Because of my chains, most of the brothers and sisters have become brave in the Lord. They speak God's word more bravely and without fear.

15Some preach Christ because they are jealous and want to argue. But some preach Christ because they want to help. 16The ones who love preach because they know I am here to tell others about the good news. 17The others preach Christ to cause trouble. They are not pure. They think they can make things harder for me while I am in chains. 18But what does it matter? Christ is preached in every way, whether for show or for truth. I am glad about that, and I will keep being glad.

Paul Hopes to HonourHonourHonor Christ

19I know this will turn out for my good. It will happen because you pray for me and because the SpiritspiritSpirit of Jesus Christ helps me. 20I hope and expect that I will not be ashamed. I hope I will be bold, as I always am, so Christ will be honouredhonouredhonored in my body. This is true whether I live or die.

21For me, living is Christ, and dying is gain. 22If I keep living in this body, I can do more good work. But I do not know which to choose. 23I am pulled in two ways. I want to leave this life and be with Christ. That is much better. 24But it is more needed for you that I stay in this body. 25I am sure of this, so I know I will stay and keep helping you. I will help you grow in faith and joy. 26Then, when I come to you again, you will have even more to be proud of in Christ Jesus because of me.

Live Like the Good News

27Live in a way that fits the good news of Christ. Then, whether I come and see you or only hear about you, I will know you stand strong. I will know you work together as one. You will have one spirit and one mind as you believe the good news. 28Do not be afraid of those who are against you. This shows them they will be lost, but it shows you will be saved. And this comes from God. 29God has given you a gift. He has given you the chance to believe in Christ and to suffer for HimhimHim. 30You are fighting the same battle you saw me fight, and now you hear that I still fight.
