Listen to God

1Listen to MemeMe, you who want to do right. You look for the the LORD. Look at the rock you were cut from. Look at the stone pit you were dug from.

2Look at Abraham, your father. Look at Sarah, who gave birth to you. I called him when he was just one man. Then I blessed him and gave him many children.

God Will Make Zion Happy

3The LORD will comfort Zion. He will fix all her broken places. He will make her dry land like Eden. He will make her desert like the the LORD's garden. People will find joy and gladness there. They will sing songs of thanks.

God's Word Will Light the World

4Listen to MemeMe, MymyMy people. Pay attention to MemeMe, MymyMy nation. My teaching will come from MemeMe. My right way will be a light for all people. I will do it soon.

5My right way is near. My help has already gone out. My arms will judge the people. The far islands wait for MemeMe. They hope in MymyMy strong arm.

6Look up at the sky. Look down at the earth. The sky will fade like smoke. The EarthEarthearth will wear out like old clothes. The people on it will die. But MymyMy help will last forever. My right way will never fail.

Don't Fear People

7Listen to MemeMe, you who know what is right. You people have MymyMy teaching in your hearts. Don't fear people's mean words. Don't be crushed by their insults.

8Moths will eat them up like clothes. Worms will eat them like wool. But MymyMy right way will last forever. My help will last for all time.

God Wakes Up His Power

9Wake up! Wake up! Put on strength, arm of the the LORD! Wake up like in the old days, long ago. Was it not YouyouYou who cut the sea monster Rahab into pieces? Was it not YouyouYou who killed the dragon?

10Was it not YouyouYou who dried up the sea? Was it not YouyouYou who made a road in the deep water so saved people could cross?

11The people the the LORD saved will come back. They will enter Zion singing. They will have joy that lasts forever. Gladness and joy will catch up to them. Sadness and sighing will run away.

God Comforts His People

12"I, yes I, am the OneoneOne who comforts you. Why are you afraid of people who die? Why are you afraid of people who fade like grass?"

13"You have forgotten the the LORD, your MakermakerMaker. He stretched out the sky and laid the earth's foundations. You are scared all day because of angry, mean people, as if they will destroy you. But where is their anger now?"

14"The prisoner will soon be free. He will not die in the dark hole. He will not run out of bread.

15"I am the the LORD, your God. I stir up the sea so its waves roar. The LORD of Heaven's Armies is MymyMy name.

16"I put MymyMy words in your mouth. I cover you with the shadow of MymyMy hand. I made the sky and the earth. I say to Zion, 'You are MymyMy people.'"

Wake Up, Jerusalem

17Wake up! Wake up! Stand up, Jerusalem. You drank from the the LORD's hand. You drank the cup of HishisHis anger. You drank every drop of the cup that makes people stumble.

18Of all the sons she raised, none leads her. Of all the sons she grew up, none holds her hand.

19Two troubles have hit you. Who will feel sorry for you? Ruin and destruction, hunger and war. Who will comfort you?

20Your sons are weak. They lie at the head of every street, like animals caught in a net. They are full of the the LORD's anger. Your God has spoken strongly against them.

21So listen to this, you who are hurt and dizzy, but not from wine.

God Takes Away the Cup

22This is what your Lord, the the LORD, says: "See, I have taken the cup that makes people stumble from your hand. From that cup, the cup of MymyMy anger, you will never drink again.

23"I will put it in the hands of the people who hurt you. They told you to bow down so they could walk over you. You let them walk all over you, like dirt on the ground."
