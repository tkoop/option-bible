David Promises to Do Right

1I will sing about the LORD’s kindness and fairness. To YouyouYou, the LORD, I will sing praise. 2I will think about the right way to live. When will YouyouYou come to me? I will live with a clean heart in my house. 3I will not look at worthless things. I hate the bad things people do when they turn away from God. That will not stick to me. 4A bad heart will stay far from me. I will not take part in evil. 5I will destroy anyone who talks bad about a neighbourneighbourneighbor in secret. I cannot stand proud people who look down on others.

Choosing Good People

6I look for loyal people in the land. They can live with me. The one who lives the right way will help me. 7No one who tricks others can live in my house. No one who tells lies will stay before me. 8Every morning I will destroy all the bad people in the land. I will remove all who do wrong from the city of the LORD.
