Abram Leaves Egypt

1Abram went up from Egypt to the south country. He took his wife and all he had. Lot went with him. 2Abram was very rich. He had many animals, silver, and gold. 3He travelledtravelledtraveled from place to place. He went from the south country to Bethel. He went to the place between Bethel and Ai. That was where his tent had been before. 4It was where he had built an altar first. There Abram prayed to the LORD.

Abram and Lot Have Too Much

5Lot went with Abram. Lot also had sheep, cows, and tents. 6The land could not hold them both. They had too much stuff to live together. 7The men who cared for Abram's animals and Lot's animals began to fight. At that time, Canaanites and Perizzites lived in the land.

Abram Asks Lot to Go His Own Way

8Abram said to Lot, “Please do not let there be a fight between you and me. Do not let there be a fight between your workers and my workers. We are brothers. 9Look, the whole land is before you. Please go your own way. If you go to the left, I will go to the right. If you go to the right, I will go to the left.”

10Lot looked up and saw all the flat land by the Jordan River. It had water everywhere. This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. It looked like the garden of the LORD. It looked like the land of Egypt near Zoar. 11So Lot chose all that flat land. He travelledtravelledtraveled east. The two men went their own ways. 12Abram stayed in the land of Canaan. Lot stayed in the cities of the flat land. He put his tent near Sodom. 13The men of Sodom were very bad. They sinned much against the LORD.

God Promises the Land to Abram

14After Lot left, the LORD said to Abram, “Look up from where you are. Look north, south, east, and west. 15All the land you see, I will give to you and your children forever. 16I will make your children as many as the dust of the earth. If a man can count the dust, he can count your children. 17Get up and walk through the land. Walk its length and width. I will give it to you.”

Abram Moves to Hebron

18Abram moved his tent. He came and lived by the big trees at Mamre in Hebron. There he built an altar to the LORD.
