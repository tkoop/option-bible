Jehoiakim Turns Against Babylon

1While Jehoiakim was king, Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, came to attack. Jehoiakim served him for three years. Then he turned against him.

The LORD Sends Enemies to Judah

2The LORD sent groups of soldiers from Babylon, Aram, Moab, and Ammon to destroy Judah. This happened just like the LORD said through HishisHis prophets. 3This happened to Judah because the LORD commanded it. He wanted to take them away from HimhimHim. This was because of the sins of King Manasseh and all the bad things he did. 4He also killed innocent people. He filled Jerusalem with innocent blood. The LORD would not forgive.

Jehoiakim Dies

5Everything else King Jehoiakim did is written in the book of the kings of Judah. 6King Jehoiakim died and was buried with his ancestors. His son Jehoiachin became king after him. 7The king of Egypt did not leave his land again. The king of Babylon had taken all the land that belonged to Egypt, from the stream of Egypt to the Euphrates River.

Jehoiachin Becomes King

8Jehoiachin was 18 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for three months. His mother was Nehushta. She was the daughter of Elnathan from Jerusalem. 9He did what was wrong in the LORD's eyes, just like his father had done.

Babylon Takes Jerusalem

10At that time, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. They surrounded the city. 11Nebuchadnezzar himself came to the city while his men kept it surrounded. 12King Jehoiachin of Judah, his mother, his servants, his leaders, and his palace workers went out to the king of Babylon. The king of Babylon took them captive. This was in the eighth year of Nebuchadnezzar's rule. 13He took all the treasures from the LORD's house and from the king's house. He cut up all the gold things that King Solomon of Israel had made for the LORD's temple. This happened just as the LORD had said. 14He took all the people of Jerusalem away. He took the leaders, the strong soldiers, the craftsmen, and the metal workers. There were ten thousand captives. Only the poorest people were left in the land. 15He took King Jehoiachin to Babylon. He also took the king's mother, the king's wives, the officials, and the important men of the land. He led them away from Jerusalem to Babylon. 16The king of Babylon took seven thousand strong soldiers and one thousand craftsmen and metal workers. They were all strong men who could fight. He brought them to Babylon.

Zedekiah Becomes King

17The king of Babylon made Mattaniah king in Jehoiachin's place. Mattaniah was Jehoiachin's uncle. The king changed Mattaniah's name to Zedekiah. 18Zedekiah was 21 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for 11 years. His mother was Hamutal. She was the daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah. 19He did what was wrong in the LORD's eyes, just like Jehoiakim had done. 20Because of the LORD's anger, HeheHe let this happen to Jerusalem and Judah. He sent them away from HimhimHim. Zedekiah also turned against the king of Babylon.
