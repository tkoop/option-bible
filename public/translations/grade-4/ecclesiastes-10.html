Wisdom and Foolishness

1Dead flies can make perfume smell bad. A little foolishness can count more than wisdom and honourhonourhonor. 2A wise person tends to do right. A foolish person tends to do wrong.

3Even when a foolish person walks along the road, his heart has no sense. He says to everyone, “I am a fool!”

Rulers and Fools

4If a ruler gets angry with you, do not leave your place. Being calm can make big wrongs better. 5I have seen something bad under the sun. It is a mistake that comes from a ruler. 6Foolishness is put in high places, while rich people sit in low places. 7I have seen slaves riding horses, while princes walk on the ground like slaves.

Work and Danger

8Anyone who digs a hole may fall into it. Anyone who breaks through a wall may be bitten by a snake. 9A person who cuts stones may get hurt by them. A person who cuts wood may be in danger from them. 10If an ax is dull and not sharp, more strength is needed. But skill brings success. 11If a snake bites before it is charmed, the snake charmer gets no reward.

Words of the Foolish

12A wise person’s words are kind. A fool’s words can ruin him. 13His words start with foolishness and end with wicked madness. 14A fool also says many words. No one knows what will happen. Who can tell him what will come after? 15A fool’s hard work makes him tired, because he does not know the way to the city.

Kings and Lands

16How sad for you, land, if your king is a child and your leaders eat a big meal in the morning. 17How good for you, land, if your king comes from a good family and your leaders eat at the right time—for strength, not to get drunk!

Money and Secrets

18Because of laziness, the roof caves in. Because of lazy hands, the house leaks. 19People get food ready for fun. Wine makes life happy. Money can solve everything. 20Do not curse the king, even in your mind. Do not curse a rich person in your bedroom. A bird may carry your words. A flying creature may tell what you said.
