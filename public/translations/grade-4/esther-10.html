The King's Tax

1King Ahasuerus made the land and the islands in the sea pay a tax.

Mordecai's Greatness

2All of Mordecai's power and strength, and the story of how great the king made him, are written in the book of the history of the kings of Media and Persia.

3Mordecai the Jew was next in rank to King Ahasuerus. He was great among the Jews, and many of his people liked him. He worked for the good of his people and spoke peace to all his family.
