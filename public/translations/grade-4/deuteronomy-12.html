Rules for the Land

1These are the rules and laws you must keep doing all the days you are alive on the land. The LORD God of your fathers gave it to you to own. 2You must destroy all the places where the nations you will take over served their gods, on the high mountains, on the hills, and under every green tree. 3Break down their altars. Break their stone pillars. Burn their Asherah poles with fire. Cut down the carved images of their gods. Wipe out their names from that place. 4Do not worship the the LORD your God like that.

One Place to Worship

5Instead, look for the place the the LORD your God will choose from all your tribes to put HishisHis name there. Go there. 6Bring there your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tenth parts, the gifts from your hand, your promises, your free gifts, and the firstborn of your cows and sheep. 7There, before the the LORD your God, you and your families will eat and be glad in all the work your hands have done, because the the LORD your God blessed you. 8Do not do as we do here today, each person doing what seems right to him. 9For you have not yet come to the rest and the land the the LORD your God is giving you. 10You will cross the Jordan and live in the land the the LORD your God gives you to own. He will give you rest from all your enemies around you, and you will live safely. 11Then the place the the LORD your God chooses to put HishisHis name will be where you bring everything I command you: your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tenth parts, the gifts from your hand, and the best of your promises you make to the the LORD. 12Be glad before the the LORD your God, you, your sons, your daughters, your male servants, your female servants, and the Levite in your towns, because he has no land or share with you. 13Be careful not to offer your burnt offerings in any place you see. 14Offer your burnt offerings only in the place the the LORD will choose in one of your tribes. There do everything I command you.

Eating Meat

15But you may kill and eat meat in any of your towns, as much as you want, with the blessing of the the LORD your God. The clean and the unclean may eat it, like a deer or a gazelle. 16But do not eat the blood. Pour it on the ground like water. 17In your towns you may not eat the tenth part of your grain, wine, or oil, or the firstborn of your cows or sheep, or any of your promises or free gifts from your hand. 18Eat them only before the the LORD your God in the place the the LORD your God chooses. You, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, and the Levite in your towns will eat them. Be glad before the the LORD your God in all the work your hands have done. 19Be careful not to forget the Levite as long as you live in your land. 20When the the LORD your God makes your land bigger, as HeheHe promised you, and you want to eat meat, you may eat meat whenever you want. 21If the place the the LORD your God chooses to put HishisHis name is too far from you, then you may kill animals from your cows or your sheep that the the LORD has given you, as I commanded you. Eat them in your towns, as much as you want. 22Eat them like you eat a deer or a gazelle. The unclean and the clean may eat them together. 23But be strong and do not eat the blood, because the blood is the life. Do not eat the life with the meat. 24Do not eat it. Pour it on the ground like water. 25Do not eat it, so that it may go well with you and your children after you, because you do what is right in the eyes of the the LORD. 26But take your holy things and your promises, and go to the place the the LORD chooses. 27Offer the meat and blood of your burnt offerings on the altar of the the LORD your God. The blood of your sacrifices will be poured on the altar of the the LORD your God, and you may eat the meat. 28Listen and obey all these words I command you, so that it may go well forever for you and your children after you, because you do what is good and right in the eyes of the the LORD your God.

Do Not Follow Other Gods

29When the the LORD your God removes the nations before you, and you take their land and you live in it, 30be careful not to be trapped into following them after they are destroyed before you. Do not ask about their gods, saying, "How did these nations serve their gods? I will do the same." 31Do not do that to the the LORD your God, because they did for their gods every terrible thing the the LORD hates. They even burned their sons and daughters in fire for their gods. 32Be careful to do everything I command you. Do not add to it, and do not take away from it.
