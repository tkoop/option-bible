God's Sword Against Jerusalem

1The word of the LORD came to me. 2He said, "SonsonSon of man, turn your face toward Jerusalem. Speak against the holy places. Speak God's message against the land of Israel. 3Say to the land of Israel, 'This is what the LORD says: I am against you. I will pull MymyMy sword from its case. I will cut off both good people and bad people from you.' 4Because I cut off both, MymyMy sword will come out of its case against all people, from the south to the north. 5Then all people will know that I am the LORD. I pulled MymyMy sword from its case. It will not go back."

Ezekiel Shows Sadness

6You, son of man, groan in front of them. Groan with a broken heart and bitter pain. 7When they ask why you groan, say, 'Bad news is coming. Everyone will lose courage. All hands will become weak. Every spirit will faint. Every knee will shake like water. It is coming. It will happen,' says the Lord, the LORD."

The Sharp Sword

8The word of the LORD came to me again. 9He said, "SonsonSon of man, speak for God. Say, 'This is what the Lord says: A sword! A sword! It is sharpened and polished.' 10It is sharpened to kill. It is polished to flash like lightning. Should we be glad about MymyMy son's stick? The sword cuts down every tree. 11The sword is given to the sharpener to hold. It is sharpened and polished, so it can be put in the killer's hand. 12Cry and wail, son of man, for this sword is against MymyMy people. It is against all the leaders of Israel. They are given to the sword with MymyMy people. So show how sad you are. 13This is a test. What if the stick that hates all wood does not last? says the Lord, the LORD."

The Sword Strikes

14"Son of man, speak for God and clap your hands. Let the sword strike two times, even three times. It is a sword for people who are killed. It is a big sword that cuts deep into them. 15So people will lose courage and many will stumble. I set the sword against all their gates. It is made to flash like lightning. It is sharpened to kill. 16Gather together. Turn to the right. Turn to the left. Go where your face is set. 17I too will clap MymyMy hands. I will calm MymyMy anger. I, the LORD, have spoken."

Babylon's King Chooses a Road

18The word of the LORD came to me again. 19He said, "SonsonSon of man, draw two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to follow. Both roads start from the same land. Make a sign where the road splits at the city. 20Make one road for the sword to go to Rabbah, the city of the Ammonites. Make the other road go to Judah, to the strong city of Jerusalem. 21The king of Babylon stands where the two roads split. He uses magic to choose. He shakes arrows. He asks his false gods. He looks at an animal's liver. 22In his right hand is the sign for Jerusalem. He will set up big logs to break the walls. He will give the order to kill. He will shout a war cry. He will set the logs against the gates. He will pile up dirt against the wall. He will build a wall to attack. 23To them, it looks like a false sign. They have sworn many oaths. But he remembers their guilt, so they will be caught. 24So the Lord, the LORD, says: Because you bring your guilt to mind by showing your sins, your sins are seen in all your deeds. Because you are remembered, you will be caught."

The Prince Will Fall

25"And you, sinful and bad prince of Israel, your day has come. The time to punish you is here. 26This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Take off the turban. Remove the crown. Things will not stay the same. The lowly will be lifted up. The proud will be brought down. 27I will ruin it, ruin it, ruin it. It will not be fixed until the OneoneOne comes who has the right to judge. Then I will give it to HimhimHim."

God's Message to Ammon

28"Son of man, speak for God. Say, 'This is what the Lord, the LORD, says to the Ammonites and their insults: A sword! A sword is drawn to kill. It is polished to destroy. It is made to flash.' 29They see false visions about you. They tell lies when they use magic. They want to use the sword to kill bad people. Their day has come. The time to punish them is here. 30Put the sword back in its case. I will judge you where you were made, in the land where you were born. 31I will pour out MymyMy anger on you. I will blow the fire of MymyMy great anger on you. I will give you into the hands of cruel men, skilled destroyers. 32You will be fuel for the fire. Your blood will be in the middle of the land. You will not be remembered, because I, the LORD, have spoken."
