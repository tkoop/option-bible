13The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He told them to go to the Israelites and to Pharaoh, king of Egypt. He told them to lead the Israelites out of Egypt.

The Family of Moses and Aaron

14These are the leaders of their families. The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. These were the family groups of Reuben. 15The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman. These were the family groups of Simeon.

16These are the names of Levi's sons by their families: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years. 17The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei, by their families. 18The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived 133 years. 19The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These were the family groups of the Levites by their families.

20Amram married his father's sister, Jochebed. She gave birth to Aaron and Moses. Amram lived 137 years. 21The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri. 22The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

23Aaron married Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon. She gave birth to Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 24The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These were the Korahite family groups. 25Aaron's son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel. She gave birth to Phinehas. These were the leaders of the Levite families by their family groups.
