A Sad Song for Egypt's King

1In the twelfth year, on the first day of the twelfth month, the word of the the LORD came to me.

2The ThetheThe LORD said, "SonsonSon of man, sing a sad song for Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Say to him: You are like a young lion among the nations. But you are like a sea monster in the seas. You splash in your rivers. You stir up the water with your feet. You make their rivers muddy.

3"The Lord GOD says, I will throw MymyMy net over you. Many people will gather around you. They will pull you up in MymyMy net. 4I will throw you on the land. I will leave you in the open field. Birds of the sky will settle on you. Wild animals will eat their fill from you. 5I will spread your flesh on the mountains. I will fill the valleys with your body. 6I will water the land with your blood, even to the mountains. The streams will be full of you.

7"When you die, I will cover the sky. I will make the stars dark. I will cover the sun with a cloud. The moon will not shine. 8I will make all the bright lights in the sky dark over you. I will put darkness over your land, says the Lord GOD.

9"I will trouble the hearts of many people. I will bring news of your ruin to the nations, to lands you did not know. 10Many people will be shocked when they see you. Kings will shake with fear. I will wave MymyMy sword in front of them. On the day you fall, each one will shake with fear for his own life.

11"The Lord GOD says, ThetheThe sword of the king of Babylon will come against you. 12I will make your people fall by the swords of mighty men. They are the most scary men of all nations. They will destroy the greatness of Egypt and all her people.

13"I will destroy all her animals near the many waters. No human foot will muddy them again. No animal hoof will muddy them again. 14Then I will make their waters settle. I will make their rivers flow like oil, says the Lord GOD.

15"When I make the land of Egypt empty and ruined, when I take away all who live there, then they will know that I am the the LORD. 16This is a sad song. The daughters of the nations will sing it. They will sing it for Egypt and all her people, says the Lord GOD."

Egypt Goes Down to the Grave

17In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the the LORD came to me.

18The ThetheThe LORD said, "SonsonSon of man, cry for Egypt's people. Send them down. Send Egypt and the daughters of mighty nations down to the world below, to those who go down to the grave. 19You are not better than them. Go down and lie with those not circumcised. 20They will fall among those killed by the sword. The sword is ready. Drag Egypt and all her people away. 21The mighty men in the grave will speak to him. They will say that his helpers have gone down too. They lie with those not circumcised, killed by the sword.

22"Assyria is there with all her people. Graves are all around her. All of them are dead, killed by the sword. 23Her graves are in the deepest part of the grave. Her people are all around her grave. All of them are dead, killed by the sword. They once spread fear in the land of the living. 24Elam is there with all her people. They are all around her grave. All of them are dead, killed by the sword. They went down not circumcised to the grave. They spread fear in the land of the living. Now they carry their shame with those who go down to the grave. 25A place is made for Egypt among the dead, with all her people. Graves are all around her. All of them were not circumcised and were killed by the sword. They spread fear in the land of the living. Now they carry their shame with those who go down to the grave. She is put among the dead.

26"Meshech and Tubal are there with all their people. Graves are all around them. All of them were not circumcised and were killed by the sword. They spread fear in the land of the living. 27They will not lie with the fallen mighty men who went down to the grave with their weapons. Those men put their swords under their heads, but their sins stayed on their bones. They were feared as mighty men in the land of the living. 28But you will be broken among those not circumcised. You will lie with those killed by the sword. 29Edom is there with her kings and princes. In their strength they were put with those killed by the sword. They lie with those not circumcised and with those who go down to the grave. 30All the princes of the north are there, and all the Sidonians. They went down in shame with those killed by the sword. Even though they spread fear by their strength, they lie with those not circumcised, killed by the sword. They carry their shame with those who go down to the grave.

31"Pharaoh will see them and be comforted about all his people—Pharaoh and all his army, killed by the sword, says the Lord GOD. 32I put fear of him in the land of the living. Pharaoh and all his people will be laid among those not circumcised, with those killed by the sword, says the Lord GOD."
