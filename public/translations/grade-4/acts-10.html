Cornelius Sees an Angel

1In Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius. He was an army officer in the Italian group. 2He and his whole family worshiped God and respected HimhimHim. He gave many gifts to the people. He prayed to God all the time.

3One day, about three in the afternoon, he saw an angel from God in a vision. The angel came to him and said, "Cornelius."

4Cornelius looked at the angel. He was scared. He said, "What is it, Lord?"

The angel said, "YouryourYour prayers and your gifts have come up to God. He remembers them.

5"Now send men to Joppa. Bring back a man named Simon. People call him Peter.

6"He is staying with another Simon. That Simon works with leather. His house is by the sea."

7When the angel finished talking, Cornelius called two of his servants and one soldier who loved God. The soldier stayed close to him.

8Cornelius told them everything. Then he sent them to Joppa.

Peter Sees a Vision

9The next day, the men were near the city. Peter went up on the roof to pray.

10He was hungry and wanted to eat. While they made food ready, he had a vision.

11He saw Heavenheavenheaven open. Something like a big sheet came down. It was held by four corners and lowered to the ground.

12In it were all kinds of animals with four feet. There were also things that crawl on the ground and birds from the sky.

13A voice said to him, "Get up, Peter. Kill and eat."

14Peter said, "No, Lord! I have never eaten anything that is not allowed or unclean."

15The voice said to him again, "Do not call unclean what God has made clean."

16This happened three times. Then the sheet was taken back up into Heavenheavenheaven.

Peter Meets Cornelius's Men

17Peter was wondering what the vision meant. Just then, the men from Cornelius reached Simon's house. They stood at the gate.

22They said, "Cornelius is an army officer. He does what is right and respects God. All the Jewish people speak well of him. A holy angel told him to send for you. He wants you to come to his house and hear your words."

23Peter invited them in and let them stay. The next day he got up and went with them. Some brothers from Joppa went too.

24The next day they came to Caesarea. Cornelius was waiting for them. He had called his family and close friends together.

Peter and Cornelius Meet

25When Peter was about to go in, Cornelius met him. He fell at Peter's feet and worshiped him.

26Peter lifted him up. He said, "Stand up. I am a man too."

27They talked together. Then Peter went in. He saw many people gathered there.

28He said to them, "YouyouYou know it is against our law for a Jewish man to be close to a person from another nation. But God showed me that I should not call any person common or unclean.

29"So when you sent for me, I came without arguing. Now tell me, why did you send for me?"

30Cornelius said, "Four days ago I was in my house praying. It was about this same hour, the ninth hour. Then a man in bright clothes stood in front of me.

31"He said, 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard. Your gifts are remembered before God.

32"'Send to Joppa and call for Simon, called Peter. He is staying in the house of Simon the leather worker by the sea.'

33"So I sent for you right away. You did well to come. Now we are all here before God. We want to hear everything the Lord has told you to say."
