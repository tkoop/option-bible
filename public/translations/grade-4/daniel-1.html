Babylon Attacks Jerusalem

1In the third year that Jehoiakim was king of Judah, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came to Jerusalem. He surrounded the city and attacked it. 2The Lord let him take King Jehoiakim of Judah. He also took some things from God's house. He carried them to the land of Shinar, to his god's house, and put them in his god's treasure room.

Young Men Go to Babylon

3Then the king told Ashpenaz, his top helper, to bring some people of Israel. He wanted boys from the king's family and from important families. 4They had to be young men with no marks on them. They had to look good. They had to be smart in all kinds of learning. They had to know things and understand things. They had to be strong enough to serve in the king's palace. They also had to learn the books and language of the Chaldeans. 5The king gave them food from his own table each day. He also gave them the wine he drank. They were to learn for three years. After that, they would stand before the king. 6Some of these young men were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. They were from Judah. 7The top helper gave them new names. He called Daniel Belteshazzar. He called Hananiah Shadrach. He called Mishael Meshach. He called Azariah Abednego.

Daniel Will Not Eat the King's Food

8But Daniel decided in his heart that he would not make himself unclean with the king's food or wine. So he asked the top helper to let him not make himself unclean. 9God helped Daniel. He made the top helper kind and caring toward Daniel. 10The top helper said to Daniel, "I am afraid of my lord the king. He gave you your food and your drink. Why should he see you looking weak? You might look worse than the young men your age. Then I could be in trouble with the king."

11Then Daniel said to the helper that the top helper had put over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. 12"Please test us, your servants, for ten days. Give us vegetables to eat and water to drink. 13Then look at us and at the young men who eat the king's food. After that, do with us, your servants, what you think is best." 14The helper listened to them about this. He tested them for ten days. 15After ten days, they looked better and stronger than all the young men who ate the king's food. 16So the helper took away the king's food and wine. He gave them vegetables instead.

God Makes Them Wise

17God gave these four young men learning and skill in all books and wisdom. Daniel could understand all visions and dreams. 18At the end of the time the king had set, the top helper brought them to King Nebuchadnezzar. 19The king talked with them. No one was like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. So they stood before the king. 20In every question about wisdom and understanding, the king found them ten times better than all the magicians and wise men in his whole kingdom. 21Daniel stayed there until the first year of King Cyrus.
