Bildad Says No One Is Clean Before God

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered. He said,

2"God has power, and people fear HimhimHim. He makes peace in Heavenheavenheaven. 3No one can count all HishisHis armies. His light shines on everyone. 4So how can a person be right with God? How can someone born from a woman be clean? 5Even the moon is not bright enough for HimhimHim. The stars are not clean in HishisHis eyes. 6How much less a person, who is only a worm!"
