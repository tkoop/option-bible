Praise the LORD

1Praise the LORD! I will thank the LORD with all my heart. I will do this with good people who follow God, in our meeting.

God's Great Works

2The LORD does great things. People who love HishisHis works study them. 3His work is beautiful and great. He always does what is right, and that lasts forever.

God Is Kind and Caring

4He made people remember HishisHis miracles. The LORD is kind and caring. 5He gives food to those who respect HimhimHim. He remembers HishisHis promise forever.

God Helps His People

6He showed HishisHis people how strong HishisHis works are. He gave them the lands of other nations. 7His hands made things that are true and fair. All HishisHis rules can be trusted. 8They stand strong forever. They are made with truth and what is right. 9He sent help to HishisHis people. He made HishisHis promise last forever. His name is holy and wonderful.

Wisdom Starts with God

10Respecting the LORD is the start of wisdom. Everyone who follows HishisHis rules learns good sense. His praise lasts forever!
