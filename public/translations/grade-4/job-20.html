1Then Zophar, a man from Naamah, answered.

Zophar Says Bad People Do Not Last

2"So my worried thoughts make me answer. I feel upset inside. 3I heard words that shame me. My mind makes me reply. 4Do you not know this has been true since people were put on EarthEarthearth? 5Bad people win for a short time. People who do not care about God are happy for only a moment.

6"Even if his pride reaches the sky and his head touches the clouds, 7he will be gone forever, like his own dirt. People who saw him will say, 'Where is he?' 8HeheHe will fly away like a dream, and no one will find him. He will be chased away like a bad dream at night. 9The eye that saw him will not see him again. His home will not see him again.

Bad People Lose Their Money

10"His sons will pay back the poor. His hands will give back his money. 11His bones were strong when he was young, but he will lie down in the dust.

12"Even if evil tastes sweet in his mouth and he hides it under his tongue, 13even if he holds it and will not let it go, and keeps it in his mouth, 14his food will turn to poison in his stomach. It will be like snake poison inside him.

God Takes Back Bad Gains

15"He swallows riches, but he will throw them up. God will make him spit them out. 16He will suck poison from snakes. A snake's fangs will kill him. 17He will not enjoy rivers flowing with honey and cream. 18He must give back what he gained without enjoying it. He will not enjoy the money from his work.

19"For he crushed poor people and left them. He took houses he did not build. 20Because he was never happy with what he had, he will not keep his treasure. 21Nothing is left for him to eat. So his good things will not last. 22When he has plenty, he will be in trouble. Hard times will come to him.

God Punishes the Wicked

23"When he has filled his stomach, God will show HishisHis anger. He will rain trouble on him while he eats. 24If he runs from an iron weapon, a bronze arrow will hit him. 25It will come out of his back. The shiny point will come out of his liver. Great fear will come over him. 26Darkness is saved for his treasures. A fire no one fans will burn him and eat up what is left in his tent.

27"The sky will show his sin. The EarthEarthearth will rise up against him. 28The good things in his house will be carried away. They will be dragged away on the day of God's anger. 29This is what God gives a bad person. This is the part God gives him."
