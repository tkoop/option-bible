Samson Goes to Timnah

1Samson went down to Timnah. There he saw a young woman. She was from the Philistines who lived in Timnah.

2He went back and told his father and mother. He said, "I saw a young woman from the Philistines in Timnah. Get her for me to be my wife."

3His father and mother said, "Are there no young women among our own people? Why do you go to the Philistines, who are not God's people, to get a wife?"

Samson said to his father, "Get her for me. She is the one I want."

4His father and mother did not know this was from the LORD. The LORD was looking for a chance to fight the Philistines. At that time, the Philistines ruled over Israel.

Samson and the Lion

5Samson went down to Timnah with his father and mother. They came to the grape fields near Timnah. A young lion roared at Samson.

6The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came on Samson with power. He tore the lion apart with his bare hands, like a young goat. He did not tell his father or mother what he had done.

7Then Samson went down and talked to the woman. She was the one he wanted.

8After some days, Samson went back to get her. On the way, he turned to look at the lion's body. He saw a swarm of bees and honey in it.

9He scraped the honey into his hands and ate it. He gave some to his father and mother, and they ate it. He did not tell them the honey came from the lion's body.

Samson's Wedding Feast

10Samson's father went down to the woman. Samson gave a big party there. Young men did that in those days.

11When the people saw Samson, they brought thirty young men to be his friends.

Samson's Riddle

12Samson said to them, "Let me tell you a riddle. If you can solve it in the seven days of the feast, I will give you thirty linen shirts and thirty sets of clothes."

13"If you cannot tell me, then you must give me thirty linen shirts and thirty sets of clothes."

They said, "Tell us your riddle. We want to hear it."

14Samson said, "OutoutOut of the eater came food. Out of the strong came something sweet." For three days they could not solve the riddle.

The Men Threaten Samson's Wife

15On the seventh day, they said to Samson's wife, "Trick your husband into telling you the answer. If you do not, we will burn you and your father's house. Did you ask us here to rob us?"

16Samson's wife cried to him. She said, "YouyouYou hate me. You do not love me. You gave a riddle to my people, but you did not tell me the answer."

He said, "I did not even tell my father and mother. Should I tell you?"

17She cried before him for the seven days of the feast. On the seventh day he told her the answer, because she kept asking. Then she told it to her people.

The Men Answer the Riddle

18Before the sun went down on the seventh day, the men of the city said to Samson, "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?"

Samson said, "If you had not used my wife to get the answer, you would not have solved my riddle."

Samson Gets Revenge

19The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came on Samson with power. He went down to Ashkelon and killed thirty of their men. He took their clothes and gave them to the men who had answered the riddle. He was very angry, and he went back to his father's house.

Samson's Wife Is Given Away

20Samson's wife was given to his friend, the man who had been with him.
