Enemies Hear About the Temple

1The enemies of Judah and Benjamin heard that the people who had come back were building a temple for the the LORD, the God of Israel. 2They came to Zerubbabel and to the family leaders. They said, “Let us build with you. We want to worship your God too. We have worshiped HimhimHim since the time of King Esarhaddon of Assyria. He brought us here.”

The Leaders Say No

3But Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the other family leaders said, “You cannot help us build a temple for our God. We will build it for the the LORD, the God of Israel. King Cyrus of Persia told us to do this.”

Enemies Make Trouble

4Then the people of that land made the people of Judah weak. They scared them so they would not build. 5They paid people to work against them. This went on during the time of King Cyrus and until King Darius ruled Persia.

They Write to the King

6They wrote a letter against the people of Judah and Jerusalem when King Ahasuerus ruled. 7Later, in the time of King Artaxerxes, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and their friends wrote to him. The letter was written in the Aramaic language.

A Letter Against Jerusalem

8Rehum the leader and Shimshai the writer wrote a letter against Jerusalem to King Artaxerxes. 9Rehum, Shimshai, and their friends wrote it. Their friends were judges and leaders from many lands. 10The great and famous Osnappar had moved them to live in Samaria and in other places west of the Euphrates River. Now they wrote to the king.

The Letter

11This is a copy of the letter they sent: “To King Artaxerxes, from your servants who live west of the Euphrates River. 12King, we want you to know that the Jews who left you and came to us have gone to Jerusalem. They are rebuilding that city. It has been a bad city. They are fixing the walls and the bases. 13King, if they finish this city and the walls, they will stop paying taxes and money to kings. It will hurt the kings. 14We work for you, and we do not want to see you lose honourhonourhonor. So we tell you. 15Look in the records of your fathers. You will see that this city has been a bad city. It has hurt kings and lands. People have fought against the king there for a long time. That is why the city was destroyed. 16King, if this city is built again and the walls are finished, you will have no land left west of the Euphrates River.”

The King Answers

17King Artaxerxes sent this answer to Rehum, Shimshai, and their friends in Samaria and west of the Euphrates: “Peace to you. I read the letter you sent. 18I read your letter. 19I told men to search. They found that this city has fought against kings for a long time. People there have turned against kings and caused trouble. 20Strong kings have ruled Jerusalem. They ruled all the land west of the Euphrates. People paid taxes and money to them. 21Now tell these men to stop. Do not let them build the city until I say so. 22Be careful and do this right. Do not let the damage grow and hurt the kings.”

The Work Stops

23When Rehum, Shimshai, and their friends read the king's letter, they hurried to Jerusalem. They used their power to make the Jews stop building. 24So the work on God's temple in Jerusalem stopped. It did not start again until the second year of King Darius of Persia.
