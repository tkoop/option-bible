You Are God's Children

1You are the children of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves. Do not shave the front of your head for someone who died. 2For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. The LORD chose you to be HishisHis special people out of all the people on EarthEarthearth.

Food You May and May Not Eat

3Do not eat anything that is unclean. 4You may eat these animals: ox, sheep, and goat. 5You may also eat deer, gazelle, roe deer, wild goat, ibex, antelope, and mountain sheep. 6You may eat any animal with a split hoof that chews its cud. 7But do not eat the camel, the rabbit, or the rock badger. They chew the cud, but their hooves are not split. So they are unclean for you. 8Do not eat the pig. It has a split hoof, but it does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you. Do not eat its meat. Do not touch its dead body.

9You may eat anything from the water that has fins and scales. 10Do not eat anything from the water that does not have fins and scales. It is unclean for you. 11You may eat any clean bird. 12But do not eat these birds: the eagle, the vulture, and the black vulture. 13Do not eat the kite, the falcon, or any kind of hawk. 14Do not eat any kind of raven. 15Do not eat the ostrich, the screech owl, the gull, or any kind of hawk. 16Do not eat the little owl, the great owl, or the white owl. 17Do not eat the desert owl, the osprey, or the cormorant. 18Do not eat the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, or the bat.

19All flying insects are unclean for you. Do not eat them. 20You may eat any clean flying thing. 21Do not eat an animal that dies on its own. Give it to the stranger who lives in your town. He may eat it. Or sell it to a foreigner. You are a holy people to the LORD your God. Do not cook a young goat in its mother's milk.

Giving a Tenth to God

22Be sure to give one-tenth of all the crops your fields grow each year. 23Eat the tenth of your grain, new wine, and oil before the LORD your God. Also eat the firstborn of your cattle and sheep. Do this in the place the LORD your God chooses for HishisHis name. Do this so you will learn to respect the LORD your God all your days. 24If the trip is too long for you, and you cannot carry your tithe because the place the LORD your God chooses is too far away, and the LORD your God has blessed you, then... 25trade your tithe for money. Carry the money in your hand. Go to the place the LORD your God chooses.

26Spend the money on anything you want: cattle, sheep, wine, beer, or anything else you like. Eat it there before the LORD your God. You and your family should be happy. 27Do not forget the Levite who lives in your town. He has no land like you do. 28Every three years, bring a tenth of all your crops from that year. Store it in your towns. 29Then the Levite, the stranger, the orphan, and the widow who live in your towns can come and eat. They will be full. Then the LORD your God will bless you in all the work you do.
