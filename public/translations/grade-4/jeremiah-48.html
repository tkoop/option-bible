God's Message About Moab

1This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says about Moab: "Oh no! Nebo is ruined! Kiriathaim is taken and put to shame. The strong place is put to shame and broken."

2"Moab will not be praised anymore. In Heshbon, people made bad plans against Moab.

They said, "Come, let's cut Moab off so it is not a nation anymore."

"Madmen, you will be quiet too. The sword will come after you."

3A cry comes from Horonaim:

"Everything is ruined! It is a big breakdown!"

4"Moab is destroyed. Its little ones cry out. 5They go up the hill to Luhith, crying. On the way down to Horonaim, they hear cries of trouble."

6"Run! Save your lives! Be like a bush in the desert. 7You trusted in your own work and treasures, so you will be caught too. Chemosh will be taken away. His priests and leaders will go with him."

8The destroyer will come to every city. No city will get away. The valley will die, and the flat land will be ruined, just as the LORD said.

9"Give Moab wings, so it can fly away and get free. Its cities will be empty, with no one living in them."

10A person who does the the LORD's work in a lazy way will be cursed. A person who keeps his sword from blood will be cursed.

11"Moab has been safe since it was young. It stayed calm and did not move. It was not poured from jar to jar. It did not go away. So its old ways stayed in it, and its smell did not change."

12The LORD says, "DaysdaysDays are coming when I will send men to pour Moab out. They will empty its jars and break its pots."

13"Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, just like Israel was ashamed of Bethel, the thing they trusted. 14How can you say that you are strong men and brave men for war?"

15"Moab is ruined. Its cities are taken. Its best young men are going down to be killed. This is what the KingkingKing says. His name is the LORD of Armies. 16Moab's trouble is near. Its bad time is coming very fast."

17"All of you around Moab, cry for it. All of you who know Moab's name, say this:"

"How the strong stick is broken! How the beautiful rod is broken!"

18"Come down from your glory and sit thirsty, you who live in Dibon. The destroyer of Moab has come up against you. He has ruined your strong places. 19Stand by the road and watch, you who live in Aroer. Ask those who run away and escape what happened."

20"Moab is ashamed, for it is broken. Cry out and wail! Tell it by the Arnon River that Moab is ruined."

Many Cities Will Fall

21JudgmentJudgementJudgment has come to the flat land: to Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath, 22to Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim, 23to Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon, 24to Kerioth, Bozrah, and all the cities of Moab, far and near.

25The LORD says, "Moab's power is cut off, and its strength is broken. 26Make Moab drunk, because it made itself bigger than the the LORD. Moab will roll in its own sick. People will laugh at it too."

27"Was Israel not a joke to you? Was Israel found with thieves? Every time you talked about Israel, you shook your head. 28Leave the cities and live in the rocks, you people of Moab. Be like a dove that makes its nest in the sides of a deep hole."

Moab's Pride

29"We have heard how proud Moab is. Moab is very proud. We have heard about its high place, its pride, its bragging, and its proud heart. 30I know Moab's pride, says the LORD, but it is not right. Its bragging has not done what it says."

31"So I will cry for Moab. I will cry out for all of Moab. I will be sad for the people of Kir-heres. 32I will cry for you more than for Jazer's vine, you vine of Sibmah. Your branches went over the sea. They reached the sea of Jazer. The destroyer has fallen on your summer fruit and your grapes."

33"Joy and gladness are taken away from the good land and from Moab. I have stopped the wine in the presses. No one steps on grapes with a happy shout. The happy shout is gone. 34From the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh, and to Jahaz, they lift up their voices. From Zoar to Horonaim, and to Eglath-shelishiyah. The waters of Nimrim will become dry and ruined."

35The LORD says, "I will stop in Moab the one who offers sacrifice on the high place and the one who burns incense to his god. 36So my heart is sad for Moab like flutes. My heart is sad for the people of Kir-heres like flutes, because the rich things Moab had are gone."

37Every head is shaved. Every beard is cut. Every hand has cuts. Every person wears rough cloth around the waist. 38"On every roof in Moab and in its streets, there is crying. I have broken Moab like a jar no one likes, says the LORD. 39How it is broken! They wail. How Moab has turned its back in shame! Moab will be a joke and a scare to all around it."

40The LORD says, "Look! Someone will fly like an eagle and spread his wings over Moab. 41Kerioth is taken. The strong places are seized. In that day, the hearts of Moab's brave men will be like the heart of a woman in pain."

42"Moab will be destroyed and not be a people anymore, because it made itself bigger than the the LORD. 43Terror, pit, and trap are on you, you who live in Moab, says the LORD."

44"The one who runs from terror will fall into the pit. The one who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the trap. I will bring on Moab the year it must be punished, says the LORD. 45Those who ran away stand in the shadow of Heshbon, with no strength. Fire came out from Heshbon. A flame came from Sihon. It has eaten the edge of Moab and the top of the noisy people."

46"Trouble is coming to you, Moab! The people of Chemosh are gone. Your sons are taken away. Your daughters are taken away too."

Moab Will Come Back

47"But in the last days, I will bring Moab back from being taken away, says the LORD. This is the end of the judgmentjudgementjudgment on Moab."
