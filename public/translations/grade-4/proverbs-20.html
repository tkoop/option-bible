Wine Can Trick You

1Wine makes fun of people. Strong drink makes them loud. Anyone who lets them lead him is not wise.

A King's Anger

2A king's anger is like a lion's roar. Anyone who makes him angry hurts himself.

Stay Out of Fights

3It is good for a man to stay out of a fight. But every fool starts trouble.

The Lazy Worker

4A lazy man does not plow in winter. At harvest he looks for food, but finds nothing.

Deep Thoughts

5What a man thinks deep down is like deep water. A wise person can bring those thoughts out.

True Friends

6Many people say they are good friends. But it is hard to find one who can be trusted. 7A good man lives with honesty. His children are happy after him.

The King Judges

8A king who sits to judge looks around and sends evil away.

Pure Hearts

9Who can say, "I have kept my heart pure. I am clean from my sin"?

Fair Weights

10Using two different weights or two different measures is hated by the LORD.

Children's Actions

11Even a child is known by what he does. We can see if his actions are good and right.

God Made Our Senses

12The LORD made both the hearing ear and the seeing eye.

Don't Love Sleep

13Do not love sleep, or you may become poor. Open your eyes, and you will have plenty of food.

The Buyer's Trick

14The buyer says, "This is bad, this is bad!" But after he leaves, he brags.

Wise Words

15There is gold and many rubies. But wise words are a precious jewel.

Paying for Strangers

16If a man promises to pay for a stranger, take his coat. Hold it until he pays for that person.

Bread from Lies

17Bread gained by lying tastes sweet to a man. But later his mouth is full of rocks.

Plans and War

18Make plans with good advice. If you go to war, get guidance.

Gossip

19A gossip tells secrets. So do not be friends with a person who talks too much.

Cursing Parents

20A person who curses his father or mother will have his light put out in deep darkness.

Fast Wealth

21Money gained too quickly at first will not be blessed at the end.

Don't Seek Revenge

22Do not say, "I will pay back this wrong." Wait for the LORD, and HeheHe will save you.

Fair Scales

23The LORD hates different weights. Bad scales are no good.

God Guides Our Steps

24The LORD guides a man's steps. How then can a man understand his own way?

Rash Promises

25It is a trap for a man to make a holy promise too fast, and then think about it later.

The Wise King

26A wise king separates the wicked and crushes them.

The Lord's Lamp

27A man's spirit is the lamp of the LORD. It searches all his deep thoughts.

Love and Truth

28Love and truth keep a king safe. By love his throne is made strong.

Strength and Age

29Young men are proud of their strength. GreyGreyGray hair is the honourhonourhonor of old men.

Hard Hits

30Hard hits and wounds clean away evil. Beatings reach the deep parts inside.
