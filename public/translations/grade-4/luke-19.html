Zacchaeus Meets Jesus

1Jesus went into Jericho. He was passing through. 2A man named Zacchaeus was there. He was the head tax man. He was rich. 3He wanted to see who Jesus was. He could not see because of the crowd. He was short. 4So he ran ahead and climbed a tree. He wanted to see Jesus, because Jesus was coming that way.

5When Jesus came to that place, HeheHe looked up. He said, "Zacchaeus, come down fast. I must stay at your house today."

Jesus Tells a Story

11The people were listening. Jesus told a story. They were near Jerusalem. The people thought God's kingdom would come right away.

12Jesus said, "A rich man went to a far country. He went to get a kingdom for himself. Then he would come back. 13He called ten servants. He gave each one a coin. He told them to do business until he came back. 14But his people hated him. They sent a message that said they did not want him to rule them."

15When he came back as king, he called the servants he had given money to. He wanted to know what they had earned.

16The first servant came. He said, "Lord, your coin made ten more coins."

17The master said, "Good job, good servant. You were faithful with a little. So you will rule ten cities."

18The second servant came. He said, "Lord, your coin made five more coins."

19The master said to him, "YouyouYou will rule five cities."

20Another servant came. He said, "Lord, here is your coin. I kept it in a cloth. 21I was afraid of you. You are a hard man. You take what you did not put down. You harvest what you did not plant."

22The master said, "YouyouYou bad servant, I will judge you by your own words. You knew I am a hard man. I take what I did not put down. I harvest what I did not plant. 23Why did you not put my money in the bank? Then I could have collected it with interest."

24He said to the people standing by, "Take the coin from him. Give it to the one who has ten coins."

25They said, "Lord, he already has ten coins."

26He said, "I tell you, everyone who has will get more. But from the one who has nothing, even what he has will be taken. 27But bring my enemies here. They did not want me to rule them. Kill them in front of me."

28After Jesus said this, HeheHe went on ahead. He was going up to Jerusalem.
