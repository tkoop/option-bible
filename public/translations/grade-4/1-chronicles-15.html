David Gets Ready for the Ark

1David built houses for himself in the CitycityCity of David. He made a place for the ark of God. He set up a tent for it.

David Speaks to the Levites

2Then David said, "Only the Levites should carry the ark of God. The LORD chose them to carry the ark of the LORD. They will serve HimhimHim forever."

All Israel Gathers

3David called all Israel to Jerusalem. They would bring the ark of the LORD to the place he made for it. 4David gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites.

Levite Leaders

5For the Kohathites, Uriel was the leader. He had 120 brothers. 6For the Merarites, Asaiah was the leader. He had 220 brothers. 7For the Gershonites, Joel was the leader. He had 130 brothers. 8For the Elizaphanites, Shemaiah was the leader. He had 200 brothers. 9For the Hebronites, Eliel was the leader. He had 80 brothers. 10For the Uzzielites, Amminadab was the leader. He had 112 brothers.

David Calls the Priests and Levites

11David called the priests Zadok and Abiathar. He also called the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab. 12He said to them, "YouyouYou are the heads of the Levite families. You and your brothers must make yourselves clean. Bring the ark of the LORD God of Israel to the place I made for it. 13You did not carry it the first time. The LORD our God punished us. We did not follow HishisHis rules."

The Levites Get Ready

14So the priests and Levites made themselves clean. They would bring up the ark of the LORD God of Israel. 15The Levites carried the ark of God on their shoulders. They used poles, just as Moses had told them to do. This was what the LORD had said.

Music for the Ark

16David told the Levite leaders to choose their brothers to sing. They would sing with joy. They would play harps, lyres, and cymbals. The music would be loud.

17The Levites chose Heman son of Joel. From his brothers they chose Asaph son of Berechiah. From the Merarites they chose Ethan son of Kushaiah. 18With them were their brothers in the next group: Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel. They were gatekeepers.

19The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan played bronze cymbals. 20Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played harps. 21Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah played lyres. They led the music.

22Chenaniah was the leader of the Levites. He was in charge of carrying the ark. He knew how to do it well. 23Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark. 24Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer were priests. They blew trumpets before the ark of God. Obed-Edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.

The Ark Comes to Jerusalem

25David, the elders of Israel, and the leaders of thousands went to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the house of Obed-Edom. They were happy. 26God helped the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD. They offered seven bulls and seven rams. 27David wore a robe of fine linen. All the Levites who carried the ark wore linen too. The singers wore linen too. Chenaniah, the leader of the carrying singers, wore linen. David also wore a linen ephod. 28All Israel brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD up with shouts. They sounded horns, trumpets, and cymbals. They played harps and lyres.

Michal Sees David Dance

29When the ark of the covenant of the LORD came to the CitycityCity of David, Michal, Saul's daughter, looked through a window. She saw King David dancing and celebrating. She did not like him for it in her heart.
