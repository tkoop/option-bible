A Message for the Ruler of Tyre

1The word of the LORD came to me.

2"Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre this message from the Lord GOD: Because your heart is proud, you said, "I am a god. I sit on a god's throne in the middle of the seas." But you are only a man, not a god, even though you think you are as wise as a god." 3Look, you are wiser than Daniel. No secret is hidden from you.

"4By your wisdom and understanding, you made wealth for yourself. You gathered gold and silver in your storehouses. 5By your great skill in trade, you grew your wealth. Your heart became proud because of your riches.

"6So the Lord GOD says: Because you think your heart is like a god's heart, 7I will bring strangers against you. They are the meanest of nations. They will draw their swords against your beauty and wisdom. They will ruin your shine. 8They will bring you down to the grave. You will die a violent death in the middle of the seas.

"9Will you still say, "I am a god," when those who kill you stand before you? You will be just a man, not a god, in the hands of those who hurt you. 10You will die like people who are not part of God's family. Foreigners will kill you. For I have spoken, says the Lord GOD."

A Sad Song for the King of Tyre

11The word of the LORD came to me.

12"Son of man, sing a sad song for the king of Tyre. Tell him this message from the Lord GOD: You were a perfect example, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty. 13You were in Eden, God's garden. Every precious stone covered you: ruby, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald. They were set in gold. They were ready on the day you were created.

"14You were a chosen guardian angel. I placed you on God's holy mountain. You walked among stones of fire. 15You were good in all your ways from the day you were created, until bad was found in you.

"16Through your great trade, you were filled with hurt, and you sinned. So I drove you out from God's mountain. I destroyed you, the covering angel, from among the stones of fire. 17Your heart became proud because of your beauty. You ruined your wisdom because of your shine. I threw you to the ground. I made you a show before kings.

"18By your many sins and cheating in trade, you made your holy places dirty. So I brought fire from inside you. It ate you up. I turned you to ashes on the earth in front of everyone who saw you. 19All the nations who knew you are shocked at you. You have become something scary, and you will be gone forever."

A Message for Sidon

20The word of the LORD came to me.

21"Son of man, look toward Sidon and speak against it. 22Say this message from the Lord GOD: I am against you, Sidon. I will show MymyMy greatness among you. They will know that I am the LORD when I carry out punishments in her and show MymyMy holy power in her.

"23I will send sickness into her and blood in her streets. Dead people will fall inside her from swords on every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

"24Israel will no longer suffer painful problems from the people around them who look down on them. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD."

God Will Bring Israel Home

25"This is what the Lord GOD says: When I bring together the people of Israel from the nations where they were sent away, I will show how holy I am through them so other nations can see. Then they will live in their own land, the land I gave to MymyMy servant Jacob. 26They will live there in peace. They will build houses and plant grape gardens. They will live in peace when I punish all the nations around them who were mean to them. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God."
