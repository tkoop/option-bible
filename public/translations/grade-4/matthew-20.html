The Workers in the Vineyard

1For the kingdom of Heavenheavenheaven is like a man who owned a vineyard. He went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard. 2He agreed to pay them one coin for the day. Then he sent them to work in his vineyard.

3About nine in the morning, he went out again. He saw some other people standing in the town square doing nothing. 4He said to them, “You go and work in my vineyard too. I will pay you what is right.”

5So they went. He went out again at noon and at three in the afternoon and did the same thing.

6About five in the afternoon, he went out and found some other people standing there. He asked them, “Why have you been standing here all day doing nothing?”

7They said, “No one hired us.”

He said to them, “You go and work in my vineyard too.”

8When evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his helper, “Call the workers and pay them. Start with the last ones hired and go to the first.”

9The workers who were hired about five in the afternoon came. Each one got one coin.

10When the first workers came, they thought they would get more. But each one also got one coin.

11When they got their pay, they complained to the owner.

12They said, “These last workers only worked one hour. You made them equal to us! We worked hard all day in the hot sun.”

13The owner answered one of them, “Friend, I am not cheating you. Didn't you agree to work for one coin? 14Take your pay and go. I want to give this last worker the same as I gave you. 15Can't I do what I want with my own money? Or are you upset because I am kind?”

16So the last will be first, and the first will be last.
