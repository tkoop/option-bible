Give Thanks to God

1Give thanks to the LORD. Call on HishisHis name. Tell people in other lands what HeheHe has done. 2Sing to HimhimHim. Sing HishisHis praise. Tell about all HishisHis wonderful works. 3Praise HishisHis holy name. Let those who look for the LORD be glad. 4Look for the LORD and HishisHis strength. Look for HishisHis face always. 5Remember the wonderful things HeheHe did. Remember HishisHis miracles and the words HeheHe spoke.

God's Promise to Abraham

6You are the children of Abraham, God's servant. You are the sons of Jacob, HishisHis chosen people. 7He is the LORD our God. He rules all the earth. 8He remembers HishisHis promise forever. He gave HishisHis word for a thousand families. 9He made a promise to Abraham. He made a promise to Isaac. 10He made it strong for Jacob. He made it last for Israel forever. 11He said, "I will give you the land of Canaan. It will be your own land."

12They were few in number. They were strangers in that land. 13They travelledtravelledtraveled from nation to nation. They went from one kingdom to another. 14He did not let anyone hurt them. He told kings to leave them alone. 15He said, "Do not touch MymyMy chosen ones. Do not hurt MymyMy prophets."

Joseph in Egypt

16He called for hunger in the land. He took away their food. 17He sent a man ahead of them. It was Joseph, sold as a slave. 18They hurt his feet with chains. They put iron chains on him. 19He stayed there until the right time. The LORD's word tested him. 20The king sent for him and set him free. The ruler of the people let him go. 21He made Joseph the boss of his house. He put him over all he owned. 22Joseph could teach the leaders what to do. He could teach the old men wisdom.

God Brings His People Out of Egypt

23Then Israel came to Egypt. Jacob lived in Egypt. 24The LORD made HishisHis people grow in number. He made them stronger than their enemies. 25Their enemies' hearts turned to hate HishisHis people. They made secret plans against HishisHis servants. 26He sent Moses, HishisHis servant. He sent Aaron, whom HeheHe chose. 27They did HishisHis miracles among them. They did wonders in Egypt.

28He sent darkness, and it got dark. They had not obeyed HishisHis words. 29He turned their water into blood. He killed their fish. 30Frogs filled their land. They were even in the kings' rooms. 31He spoke, and flies came. Tiny bugs came all over their land. 32He gave them hail instead of rain. Lightning flashed through their land. 33He hit their grape plants and fig trees. He broke the trees in their land. 34He spoke, and locusts came. Young locusts came without number. 35They ate every plant in the land. They ate the food from the soil. 36He hit the oldest child in every home. He took the first and best of their strength.

37He brought Israel out with silver and gold. None of HishisHis people fell. 38Egypt was glad when they left. The people of Israel had scared them.

God Takes Care of His People

39He spread a cloud to cover them. He gave fire to light the night. 40They asked, and HeheHe brought quail. He fed them with bread from Heavenheavenheaven. 41He opened a rock, and water poured out. It flowed like a river in the dry land. 42He remembered HishisHis holy promise. He remembered HishisHis promise to Abraham, HishisHis servant. 43He brought out HishisHis people with joy. He brought out HishisHis chosen ones with songs. 44He gave them the lands of other nations. They took what other people had worked for. 45He did this so they would keep HishisHis rules and obey HishisHis laws. Praise the LORD!
