The Assyrian Army Attacks Judah

1In the 14th year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib, king of Assyria, attacked all the strong towns in Judah. He took them. 2The king of Assyria sent his leader, Rabshakeh, from Lachish to Jerusalem. He brought a big army. Rabshakeh stood by the upper pool on the road to the field where clothes were washed. 3Eliakim son of Hilkiah was in charge of the palace. Shebna was the writer. Joah son of Asaph wrote things down. They came out to meet him.

Rabshakeh Talks to Hezekiah's Men

4Rabshakeh said, "Tell Hezekiah this. The great king, the king of Assyria, says, 'What are you trusting in?' 5I say your plans and your power for war are just talk. Who do you trust? Why did you turn against me? 6Look, you trust in Egypt. But Egypt is like a broken stick. If a man leans on it, it will break and cut his hand. Pharaoh, king of Egypt, is like that to all who trust in him. 7Maybe you will say, 'We trust in the LORD our God.' But isn't HeheHe the God whose hilltop altars Hezekiah took away? Hezekiah told Judah and Jerusalem, 'YouyouYou must worship at this altar.' 8Now make a deal with my master, the king of Assyria. I will give you 2,000 horses if you can find riders for them. 9How could you turn back even one of my master's smallest officers? Yet you trust in Egypt for chariots and horsemen. 10I did not come here without the LORD to destroy this land. The LORD told me, 'Go up against this land and destroy it.'"

11Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah said to Rabshakeh, "Please speak to us in Aramaic. We understand it. Do not speak to us in Hebrew where the people on the wall can hear."

12Rabshakeh said, "MymyMy master did not send me to speak only to your master and to you. He sent me to speak to the men on the wall too. They will eat their own waste and drink their own urine with you."

Rabshakeh Speaks to the People

13Then Rabshakeh stood and shouted in Hebrew, "Hear the words of the great king, the king of Assyria! 14The king says, 'Do not let Hezekiah trick you. He cannot save you.' 15Do not let Hezekiah make you trust in the LORD. He says, 'ThetheThe LORD will save us. This city will not be given to the king of Assyria.' 16Do not listen to Hezekiah. The king of Assyria says, 'Make peace with me and come out to me. Then each of you will eat from his own grapevine and fig tree and drink from his own well.' 17I will come and take you to a land like your own land. It will have grain, new wine, bread, and vineyards. 18Do not let Hezekiah fool you. He says, 'ThetheThe LORD will save us.' Has any god of any nation saved his land from the king of Assyria? 19Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim? Did they save Samaria from me? 20None of the gods of these lands saved their countries from me. So ThetheThe LORD will not save Jerusalem from me."

The People Stay Quiet

21The people stayed quiet and did not answer him, because the king had said, "Do not answer him." 22Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the writer, and Joah son of Asaph, who wrote things down, came to Hezekiah with their clothes torn. They told him what Rabshakeh had said.
