Israel Comes Together at Mizpah

1All the Israelites from Dan to Beersheba, and from Gilead too, came out. The whole group gathered as one man before the LORD at Mizpah. 2The leaders of all the people stood in God's meeting. There were 400,000 foot soldiers with swords.

The Levite Tells What Happened

3The Benjamites heard that Israel had gone up to Mizpah. The Israelites said, "Tell us how this bad thing happened."

4The Levite, the husband of the woman who was killed, answered, "I and my second wife came to Gibeah in Benjamin to stay the night. 5The men of Gibeah came against me. They surrounded the house. They wanted to kill me. They hurt my second wife, and she died. 6I took my second wife, cut her into pieces, and sent the pieces to all parts of Israel. They did a terrible, foolish thing in Israel. 7Look, all you Israelites, tell me what to do here."

The People Plan to Punish Gibeah

8All the people stood up as one man. They said, "None of us will go home. None of us will go to his house. 9Now this is what we will do to Gibeah. We will go against it by drawing lots. 10We will take ten men from every hundred, from all the tribes of Israel. We will take a hundred from every thousand, and a thousand from every ten thousand. They will get food for the people. They will go to Geba of Benjamin. They will punish the foolish thing done in Israel." 11All the men of Israel came together as one man.

12The tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribe of Benjamin. They said, "What is this bad thing among you? 13Hand over the bad men in Gibeah. We will kill them and remove the evil from Israel." But the Benjamites would not listen to their brothers, the Israelites.

Benjamin Gets Ready to Fight

14The Benjamites gathered from their cities to Gibeah to fight the Israelites. 15That day they had 26,000 swordsmen from the cities, plus 700 chosen men from Gibeah. 16Among them were 700 left-handed men. Each one could throw a stone at a hair and not miss. 17The Israelites, not counting Benjamin, had 400,000 swordsmen. All of them were fighters.

Israel Asks God Who Should Go First

18The Israelites went up to Bethel and asked God, "Who should go first to fight Benjamin?"

The LORD said, "Judah first."

19The Israelites got up in the morning and camped near Gibeah. 20The men of Israel went out to fight Benjamin. They lined up against them at Gibeah.

The First Battle

21Benjamin came out of Gibeah and killed 22,000 Israelites that day. 22The Israelites got stronger and set up for battle again in the same place.

23The Israelites went up and cried before the LORD until evening. They asked, "Should we go again to fight our brother Benjamin?"

The LORD said, "Go up against him."

24The second day the Israelites came near Benjamin. 25Benjamin came out from Gibeah and killed 18,000 more Israelites. All of them were swordsmen.

Israel Prays and Gives Offerings

26All the Israelites went up to Bethel. They sat and cried before the LORD. They did not eat until evening. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings to the LORD.

27The Israelites asked the LORD a question. In those days the ark of God's promise was there. 28Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, stood before it. He asked, "Should I go out again to fight my brother Benjamin, or should I stop?"

The LORD said, "Go up. Tomorrow I will give them into your hand."

Israel Sets a Trap

29The Israelites put hidden men all around Gibeah. 30The third day the Israelites went up against Benjamin. They lined up at Gibeah as before. 31Benjamin came out to meet them. The Israelites drew them away from the city. Benjamin began to kill Israelites, about 30 men, as before. This happened on the roads to Bethel and Gibeah. 32The Benjamites said, "They are running from us as before."

The Israelites said, "Let us run and draw them from the city to the roads."

33All the Israelites got up from their place and lined up at Baal Tamar. The hidden men of Israel burst out from their place near Geba. 34Then 10,000 chosen men from all Israel came opposite Gibeah. The battle was heavy. Benjamin did not know that trouble was near.

God Gives Israel the Victory

35The LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel. The Israelites killed 25,100 Benjamites that day. All of them were swordsmen. 36Benjamin saw that they were beaten. The Israelites moved back because they trusted the hidden men at Gibeah. 37The hidden men hurried and rushed into Gibeah. They spread out and killed the whole city with the sword.

38The Israelites and the hidden men had agreed on a signal. They would make a cloud of smoke rise from the city. 39The Israelites turned back in the battle. Benjamin had begun to kill about 30 Israelites. They thought, "Surely they are running from us as before." 40But the smoke began to rise from the city. Benjamin looked back and saw the whole city going up in smoke to the sky. 41The Israelites turned back. Benjamin was afraid because they saw that trouble had come on them.

42They ran from Israel toward the desert. But the battle followed them. Men from the cities destroyed them along the way. 43They surrounded Benjamin. They chased them without rest and trampled them down east of Gibeah.

44Then 18,000 brave Benjamites fell. 45They turned and ran to the rock of Rimmon. The Israelites killed 5,000 more along the roads. They chased them to Gidom and killed 2,000. 46That day 25,000 brave Benjamites who used swords fell. 47But 600 men turned and ran to the rock of Rimmon. They stayed there four months.

Israel Destroys the Cities of Benjamin

48The Israelites went back to Benjamin. They killed with the sword every living thing in the cities, even the animals. They burned every city they found.
