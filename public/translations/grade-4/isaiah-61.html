Good News for Sad People

1The SpiritspiritSpirit of the Lord, the LORD, is on me. the LORD has chosen me to bring good news to poor people. He sent me to help people whose hearts are broken. He sent me to tell people in chains that they are free. He sent me to open the prison for people locked up. 2He sent me to tell about the year of the LORD's favourfavourfavor. He sent me to tell about the day when our God will punish HishisHis enemies. He sent me to comfort everyone who is sad. 3He sent me to help sad people in Zion. I will give them a beautiful crown instead of ashes. I will give them oil that brings joy instead of sadness. I will give them clothes of praise instead of a sad spirit. People will call them strong trees of what is right. They are plants the LORD grew to show HishisHis glory.

God Will Make Things New

4They will build up old ruins. They will make broken places new again. They will make cities new again that have been empty for many years. 5Strangers will stand and take care of your sheep. People from other lands will work in your fields and grape fields. 6People will call you priests of the LORD. People will say you serve our God. You will eat the riches of other nations. You will enjoy their wealth. 7Instead of your shame, you will get twice as much. Instead of being disgraced, they will be happy with what they receive. So they will own twice as much in their land, and they will have joy that lasts forever.

The LORD Makes a Promise

8For I, the LORD, love what is right. I hate stealing and wrong. I will be faithful to them. I will reward them for their work, and I will make a promise with them that lasts forever. 9People in other nations will know their children. People everywhere will know their families. Everyone who sees them will know that they are people the LORD has blessed.

I Am Happy in the LORD

10I am very happy in the LORD. My heart is glad in my God. He has dressed me in clothes of salvation. He has put a robe of what is right on me. He made me look like a groom with a priest's head covering. He made me look like a bride wearing her jewels. 11Just as the ground grows plants, and a garden makes seeds grow, so the Lord, the LORD, will make what is right and praise grow in front of all nations.
