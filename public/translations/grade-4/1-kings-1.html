David Grows Old

1King David was very old. His servants covered him with blankets, but he could not get warm. 2His servants said to him, "Let us find a young woman who has never married. She can stand before you, our king, and take care of you. She can lie beside you and keep you warm." 3They looked all over Israel for a beautiful young woman. They found Abishag from Shunem and brought her to the king. 4The young woman was very beautiful. She took care of the king and served him, but the king did not sleep with her.

Adonijah Tries to Be King

5Adonijah son of Haggith made himself important. He said, "I will be king." He got chariots and horsemen, and fifty men ran ahead of him. 6His father never told him no. He never asked, "Why did you do that?" Adonijah was very handsome, and he was born after Absalom. 7He talked with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest, and they helped him. 8But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David's mighty men were not with Adonijah.

9Adonijah killed sheep, cows, and fat calves. He did this near the stone of Zoheleth by En Rogel. He invited all the king's sons and all the men of Judah who served the king. 10But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.

Nathan and Bathsheba Warn David

11Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, "Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has made himself king? Our lord David does not know it. 12Now let me give you some advice. It can save your life and your son Solomon's life. 13Go to King David and tell him that he promised you your son Solomon would be king after him and sit on his throne. Ask him why Adonijah has become king. 14While you are still there talking with the king, I will come in after you. I will say the same things you said."

15Bathsheba went to the king in his room. The king was very old, and Abishag from Shunem was serving him. 16Bathsheba bowed down to the king. The king said, "What do you want?"

17She said, "MymyMy lord, you swore by the LORD your God to me that my son Solomon would be king after you and sit on your throne. 18But now Adonijah has made himself king. My lord the king, you do not know it. 19He killed many cows and fat calves and sheep. He invited all the king's sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the army leader. But he did not invite your servant Solomon. 20My lord the king, all Israel is watching you. They want to know who will sit on your throne after you. 21If you do not tell them, then when you die, I and my son Solomon will be treated like we did wrong."

22While she was still talking with the king, Nathan the prophet came in. 23They told the king, "Nathan the prophet is here." He came in and bowed with his face to the ground.

24Nathan said, "MymyMy lord the king, did you say Adonijah would be king after you and sit on your throne? 25Today he went down and killed many cows and fat calves and sheep. He invited all the king's sons, the army leaders, and Abiathar the priest. Now they are eating and drinking with him and shouting for King Adonijah. 26But he did not invite me, your servant, or Zadok the priest, or Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon. 27Did my lord the king do this? You did not tell your servants who would sit on your throne after you."

David Makes Solomon King

28King David answered, "Call Bathsheba to me." She came and stood before the king.

29The king made a promise. He said, "As the LORD lives, Who saved my life from all trouble, 30just as I promised you by the LORD, the God of Israel, that your son Solomon would be king after me and sit on my throne in my place, I will do it today."

31Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground. She honouredhonouredhonored the king and said, "May my lord King David live forever."

32King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada." They came to the king.

33The king said to them, "Take my servants with you. Put my son Solomon on my own mule. Take him down to Gihon. 34There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet will pour oil on him and make him king over Israel. Blow the trumpet and say that King Solomon should live long. 35Then come back up with him. He will come and sit on my throne. He will be king in my place. I have chosen him to lead Israel and Judah."

36Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, "AmenamenAmen! May the LORD, the God of my lord the king, say so too. 37As the LORD has been with my lord the king, so may HeheHe be with Solomon. May HeheHe make Solomon's throne greater than my lord the king's throne."

Solomon Is Made King

38So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down. They put Solomon on King David's mule and took him to Gihon. 39Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent. He poured it on Solomon. Then they blew the trumpet. All the people said, "Long live King Solomon!" 40All the people went up after him. They played flutes and were very happy. The noise was so loud that the ground shook.

Adonijah Hears the News

41Adonijah and all his guests heard it as they finished eating. When Joab heard the trumpet, he said, "Why is the city so noisy?"

42While Joab was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest came. Adonijah said, "Come in. You are a brave man. You bring good news."

43Jonathan answered, "No! Our lord King David has made Solomon king. 44The king sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites. They put Solomon on the king's mule. 45Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet poured oil on him at Gihon. They came back up happy. The whole city is excited. That is the noise you heard. 46Solomon now sits on the royal throne. 47The king's servants came to bless our lord King David. They said that the king's God should make Solomon's name greater than the king's name, and make his throne greater than the king's throne. Then the king bowed on his bed. 48The king also blessed the LORD, the God of Israel. He said that today God has given someone to sit on his throne, and his eyes have seen it."

49All of Adonijah's guests were afraid. They got up and went home.

Adonijah Gives Up

50Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up and went to the altar. He grabbed the horns of the altar.

51Someone told Solomon, "Adonijah is afraid of you, King Solomon. He has grabbed the horns of the altar. He asks you not to kill him with a sword."

52Solomon said, "If he is a good man, not one hair of his head will fall to the ground. But if he does wrong, he will die."

53King Solomon sent men. They brought Adonijah down from the altar. He came and bowed to King Solomon. Solomon said to him, "Go home."
