The LORD Speaks to King Zedekiah

1This is the word the LORD gave Jeremiah. King Zedekiah sent Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah the priest, son of Maaseiah, to him. They said,

2"Please ask the the LORD to help us. Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, is fighting against us. Maybe the the LORD will do HishisHis great works for us, and Nebuchadnezzar will leave."

3Jeremiah said to them, "Tell Zedekiah this:

4"This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'I will make your weapons useless. You are using them to fight the king of Babylon and the Babylonians who are around your city walls. But I will bring those enemies into the middle of this city.

5"'I will fight against you with a strong hand and a mighty arm. I will be very angry.

6"'I will strike the people and animals in this city. They will die from a bad sickness.

7"'After that,' says the the LORD, 'I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, and the people left in this city after sickness, war, and hunger to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to their enemies. Those enemies want to kill them. The enemy will kill them with swords. They will not spare them or feel sorry for them.'

8"And tell these people, 'This is what the the LORD says: I am putting before you the way to live and the way to die.

9"'Anyone who stays in this city will die by sword, hunger, and sickness. But anyone who goes out and gives up to the Babylonians who are around you will live. He will keep his life like a prize taken in war.

10"'I have turned MymyMy face against this city to hurt it, not to help it,' says the the LORD. 'It will be given to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.'

A Warning to the King's Family

11"Also tell the family of the king of Judah, 'Listen to the word of the the LORD!'

12"House of David, this is what the the LORD says: 'Do what is right each morning. Save the person who was robbed from the robber. If you do not, MymyMy anger will go out like fire and burn. No one will put it out. This will happen because of their evil deeds.'

13"'I am against you, you who live in the valley, you who are like a rock on the flat land,' says the the LORD. 'YouyouYou say, "Who can come down against us? Who can come into our homes?"'

14"'I will punish you for what you have done,' says the the LORD. 'I will start a fire in your forest. It will burn up everything around it.'"
