Pray for All People

1First of all, I ask that prayers be made for everyone. Ask God to help them. Pray for them. Thank God for them. 2Pray for kings and all leaders. Then we can live quiet and peaceful lives. We can live in a way that honourshonourshonors God and is good. 3This is good. It pleases God, who saves us. 4He wants all people to be saved. He wants them to know the truth.

5There is only one God. There is only one person who brings God and people together. That person is Jesus Christ, who is a man. 6He gave HimselfhimselfHimself to pay for all people. This is the message God gave at the right time. 7God chose me to tell this message. I am a preacher and a messenger for God. I teach people who are not Jews to trust God and know the truth. I am telling the truth. I am not lying.

How Men Should Pray

8So I want men everywhere to pray. They should lift their hands to God. They should not be angry or argue.

How Women Should Live

9In the same way, women should dress in a way that is modest. They should have self-control. They should not show off with fancy hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes. 10Instead, they should do good things. That is what women who love God should do.

11A woman should learn quietly and obey. 12I do not let a woman teach a man or tell him what to do. She should be quiet. 13Adam was made first. Then Eve was made. 14Adam was not tricked. But Eve was tricked. She broke God's rule. 15But women will be saved by having children, if they keep trusting God, loving others, and living holy lives with self-control.
