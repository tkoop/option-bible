Rules After a Baby Is Born

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Speak to the people of Israel. Tell them: When a woman has a baby boy, she is not clean for seven days. This is like the time of her monthly bleeding. 3On the eighth day, the boy must be circumcised. 4Then the mother must wait thirty-three days to be made clean from her bleeding. She must not touch anything holy, and she must not go into the holy place until her days of cleaning are done.

5"If she has a baby girl, she is not clean for two weeks, like her monthly bleeding. Then she must wait sixty-six days to be made clean from her bleeding. 6When her days of cleaning are done, for a boy or a girl, she must bring these gifts to the priest at the meeting tent: a one-year-old lamb for a burnt offering and a young pigeon or dove for a sin offering. 7The priest will offer them before the LORD. He will pay for her sin, and she will be clean from her bleeding. This is the rule for a woman who has a baby, boy or girl. 8If she cannot afford a lamb, she must bring two doves or two young pigeons. One is for a burnt offering, and one is for a sin offering. The priest will pay for her sin, and she will be clean."
