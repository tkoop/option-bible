A Message About Nineveh

1This is a message about Nineveh. It is the book of Nahum's vision. He was from Elkosh.

God Is Angry with His Enemies

2The LORD is a jealous God who punishes. The LORD punishes and is full of anger. The LORD punishes HishisHis enemies. He keeps HishisHis anger for those who hate HimhimHim. 3The LORD is slow to get angry, but HeheHe is very strong. The LORD will not let guilty people go free. He walks in the storm and the wind. The clouds are like dust under HishisHis feet.

4He tells the sea to dry up, and it does. He dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel dry up. The flowers of Lebanon fade. 5The mountains shake before HimhimHim. The hills melt away. The EarthEarthearth trembles when HeheHe is near. The whole world and everyone in it shakes too. 6Who can stand before HishisHis anger? Who can live through HishisHis hot anger? His anger pours out like fire. Rocks break apart before HimhimHim.

God Is Good to Those Who Trust Him

7The LORD is good. He is a safe place when trouble comes. He takes care of people who trust in HimhimHim. 8But HeheHe will destroy Nineveh with a huge flood. He will chase HishisHis enemies into darkness.

God Will Stop Nineveh's Plans

9What do you plan against the LORD? He will destroy it. Trouble will not come again. 10They will get tangled in thorns. They will be drunk with their wine. They will burn up like dry grass. 11Someone from you, Nineveh, planned evil against the LORD. He made bad plans.

God's Promise to Judah

12The LORD says, "Even if they have many friends and are strong, they will be destroyed and disappear. I hurt you, Judah, but I will not hurt you again. 13Now I will take their heavy load off your neck. I will break the chains that hold you."

14The LORD has given a command about you, Nineveh. He says, "YouryourYour name will not last. I will destroy the carved idols and metal gods in your temple. I will dig your grave, because you are worthless."

Good News for Judah

15Look! On the mountains, someone is coming with good news. He brings peace. Celebrate your special days, Judah. Keep your promises. The worthless people will never attack you again. They will be completely destroyed.
