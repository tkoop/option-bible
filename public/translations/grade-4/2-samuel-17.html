Ahithophel's Plan

1Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me pick 12,000 men. I will chase David tonight. 2I will attack him while he is tired and weak. I will scare him. All his people will run away. Then I will kill only the king. 3I will bring all the people back to you. You want all the people to come back. David is the only man you are looking for. Then all the people will be at peace." 4This plan sounded good to Absalom and to all the leaders of Israel.

Hushai Gives Different Advice

5But Absalom said, "Call Hushai the Arkite too. Let us hear what he says." 6Hushai came to Absalom. Absalom said to him, "Ahithophel gave this advice. Should we do what he says? If not, speak."

7Hushai said to Absalom, "ThetheThe advice Ahithophel gave this time is not good. 8You know your father and his men. They are strong and brave. They are like a mother bear whose cubs were taken in the field. Your father is a soldier. He will not camp with the troops at night.

9"Even now he is hiding in a cave or some place. If some of our men are killed first, whoever hears it will say, 'There has been a big loss among the people who follow Absalom.' 10Even a brave man whose heart is like a lion's heart will melt with fear. All Israel knows your father is a mighty man, and the men with him are brave.

11"So I advise that all Israel be gathered from Dan to Beersheba, as many as sand by the sea. Then you go to battle yourself. 12We will come upon him wherever he is. We will fall on him like dew on the ground. We will not leave one man, not even one. 13If he hides in a city, all Israel will bring ropes to that city. We will pull it into the valley until not even a small stone is left."

14Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "Hushai's advice is better than Ahithophel's." For the the LORD had decided to stop Ahithophel's good advice, so that the the LORD might bring trouble on Absalom.

David Gets a Warning

15Then Hushai told Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "Ahithophel told Absalom and the leaders of Israel to do one plan. I told them to do another plan. 16Now send quickly and tell David not to stay tonight in the plains of the desert. Tell him to cross over fast, or the king and all the people with him will be wiped out."

17Jonathan and Ahimaaz were waiting at En Rogel. A servant girl went and told them. They were to go tell King David, because they could not be seen going into the city. 18But a young man saw them and told Absalom. So the two hurried away and went to a man's house in Bahurim. He had a well in his yard, and they went down into it. 19The woman took a cover and spread it over the well's mouth. She spread grain on it, so nothing was noticed.

20Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house and asked, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" The woman said to them, "They crossed over the stream." The men searched and did not find them, so they went back to Jerusalem. 21After they had gone, the two came up out of the well and went to tell King David. They said to David, "Get up and cross the water fast. Ahithophel has advised a plan against you." 22So David and all the people with him got up and crossed the Jordan. By morning not one was left who had not crossed the Jordan.

Ahithophel Dies

23When Ahithophel saw that his advice was not followed, he saddled his donkey and went to his hometown. He put his things in order and hanged himself. He died and was buried in his father's tomb.

The Armies Gather

24David came to Mahanaim. Absalom crossed the Jordan with all the men of Israel. 25Absalom had made Amasa leader of the army instead of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Ithra the Israelite. Ithra had married Abigail, the daughter of Nahash and sister of Zeruiah, Joab's mother. 26Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.

Friends Help David

27When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo Debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim 28brought beds, bowls, clay pots, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils, honey, butter, sheep, and cheese. 29They brought these things to David and the people with him to eat. They said, "ThetheThe people are hungry, tired, and thirsty in the desert."
