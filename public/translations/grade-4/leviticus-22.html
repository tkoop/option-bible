Rules for Holy Food

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Tell Aaron and his sons to be careful with the holy things the people of Israel give to MemeMe. They must not make MymyMy holy name common. I am the LORD. 3Always, if any man in your family has something that makes him not clean, and he comes near the holy things that the people of Israel give to the LORD, that man must be sent away from MemeMe. I am the LORD.

"4No man from Aaron's family who has a skin disease or a flow may eat the holy food until he is clean. Also, no one who touches a dead body, or a man who has a flow from his body, may eat it. 5No man who touches any unclean thing, like a bug that makes him not clean, or a person who is not clean, may eat it. 6The person who touches these things will be not clean until evening. He may not eat the holy food unless he washes his body with water.

"7When the sun goes down, he will be clean again. Then he may eat the holy food, because it is his food. 8He must not eat an animal that died by itself or was killed by another animal. That would make him not clean. I am the LORD. 9The priests must keep MymyMy rules. If they do not, they will be blamed and die, because they made the holy things common. I am the LORD. I make them holy.

"10No one outside the priest's family may eat the holy food. A guest of the priest or a hired worker may not eat it. 11But if the priest buys a person with his money, that person and the children born in his house may eat his food. 12If a priest's daughter marries a man who is not a priest, she may not eat the holy food that is given to the LORD. 13But if she is a widow or is sent away, and she has no children, and she comes back to her father's house, she may eat her father's food like when she was young. No one outside the family may eat it.

"14If a man eats the holy food by mistake, he must add one-fifth more to it and give the holy food to the priest. 15The priests must not let the holy things of the people of Israel be made common. 16They must not let the people carry the blame for eating the holy things. I am the LORD. I make them holy."

Rules for Offerings

17The LORD said to Moses, 18"Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the people of Israel. Say: If any man of Israel or a foreigner living among you gives a gift to the LORD as a burnt offering, to keep a promise or as a free gift, 19you must give a male animal with nothing wrong with it. It can be a bull, a sheep, or a goat. Then the LORD will accept it. 20Do not bring an animal that has something wrong with it. The LORD will not accept it for you.

"21If a man brings a peace offering to the LORD, from a bull or sheep or goat, to keep a promise or as a free gift, it must have nothing wrong with it. 22Do not bring to the LORD an animal that is blind, hurt, cut, has a sore, a skin disease, or scabs. Do not put any of these on the altar as a gift burned to the LORD. 23You may give a bull or sheep that has a part too long or too short as a free gift. But it will not be accepted as a promise gift.

"24Do not bring to the LORD an animal whose body parts are crushed, broken, torn, or cut off. Do not do this in your land. 25Do not take any of these from a foreigner to give as food to your God. They have something wrong with them, so they will not be accepted for you."

More Rules for Offerings

26The LORD said to Moses, 27"When a bull, sheep, or goat is born, it must stay with its mother for seven days. From the eighth day on, it will be accepted as a gift burned to the LORD. 28Do not kill a cow or sheep and its young on the same day. 29If you give a thank offering to the LORD, give it so the LORD will accept it. 30Eat it the same day. Do not leave any of it until morning. I am the LORD. 31Keep MymyMy rules and do them. I am the LORD. 32Do not make MymyMy holy name common. I will be treated as holy among the people of Israel. I am the LORD. I make you holy. 33I brought you out of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD."
