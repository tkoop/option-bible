For the music leader. For Jeduthun. A song by David.

David Tries to Keep Quiet

1I said I will watch my ways. I will not sin with my tongue. I will keep my mouth shut with a muzzle. I will do this while bad people are near. 2I was quiet and said nothing. I did not even say good things. But my sadness got worse. 3My heart got hot inside me. When I thought about it, a fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue.

David Asks About His Life

4The LORD, show me when my life will end. Show me how long I will live. Help me know how short my life is. 5Look, YouyouYou made my life only a few inches long. My whole life is like nothing to YouyouYou. Yes, every person is like a breath. Selah. 6Yes, people walk around like shadows. They worry for nothing. They gather things, but they do not know who will get them.

David Prays for Help

7And now, Lord, what am I waiting for? My hope is in YouyouYou. 8Save me from all my wrongs. Do not make me a joke to foolish people. 9I am quiet. I do not open my mouth, because YouyouYou did it. 10Take away YouryourYour sickness from me. I am worn out by the hit of YouryourYour hand. 11You teach people with warnings when they do wrong. You melt away what they love, like a moth eats cloth. Yes, every person is like a breath. Selah.

David Cries to the LORD

12Listen to my prayer, the LORD. Hear my cry. Do not stay quiet when I cry. I am a stranger with YouyouYou. I live here for a short time, like all my fathers did. 13Look away from me, so I can smile, before I leave and am gone.
