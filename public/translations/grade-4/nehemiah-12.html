Priests and Levites Who Came Back

1These are the priests and Levites who came back with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra, 2Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, 3Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, 4Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah, 5Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah, 6Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, 7Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah. These were the leaders of the priests and their brothers in the days of Jeshua.

Levites Who Helped

8The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah. He and his brothers led the songs of thanks. 9Bakbukiah and Unni, their brothers, stood across from them in the work.

Family Lines

10Jeshua was the father of Joiakim. Joiakim was the father of Eliashib. Eliashib was the father of Joiada. 11Joiada was the father of Jonathan. Jonathan was the father of Jaddua.

Priest Family Leaders Later

12In the days of Joiakim, these were the leaders of the priest families. For Seraiah, Meraiah. For Jeremiah, Hananiah. 13For Ezra, Meshullam. For Amariah, Jehohanan. 14For Malluchi, Jonathan. For Shebaniah, Joseph. 15For Harim, Adna. For Meraioth, Helkai. 16For Iddo, Zechariah. For Ginnethon, Meshullam. 17For Abijah, Zichri. For Miniamin and Moadiah, Piltai. 18For Bilgah, Shammua. For Shemaiah, Jehonathan. 19For Joiarib, Mattenai. For Jedaiah, Uzzi. 20For Sallai, Kallai. For Amok, Eber. 21For Hilkiah, Hashabiah. For Jedaiah, Nethanel.

Levite Leaders and Gatekeepers

22In the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, the leaders of the Levite and priest families were written down. The priests were written down too, during the rule of Darius the Persian. 23The family leaders of Levi were written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles up to the days of Johanan son of Eliashib. 24The leaders of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua son of Kadmiel, and their brothers. They stood across from each other to praise and thank God. One group answered the other, as David the man of God had commanded. 25Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers. They guarded the storerooms at the gates. 26These men served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua son of Jozadak. They also served in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and writer.

The Wall Is Dedicated

27When the wall of Jerusalem was dedicated, the Levites were found in all their towns. They were brought to Jerusalem to celebrate with joy, thanks, and song. They used cymbals, harps, and lyres. 28The singers came from the area around Jerusalem, from the villages of the Netophathites, 29from Beth-gilgal, and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth. The singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem. 30The priests and Levites made themselves clean. Then they made the people, the gates, and the wall clean.

Two Choirs on the Wall

31I brought the leaders of Judah up on the wall. I set up two large choirs to give thanks and walk along the wall. One choir went to the right, toward the DungdungDung GategateGate. 32Hoshaiah and half the leaders of Judah followed, 33along with Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam, 34Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah, 35and some priests with trumpets: Zechariah son of Jonathan son of Shemaiah son of Mattaniah son of Micaiah son of Zaccur son of Asaph, 36and his brothers Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani. They played the musical instruments of David the man of God. Ezra the writer led them. 37At the FountainfountainFountain GategateGate they went straight up the stairs of the CitycityCity of David to the wall. They passed above David's house to the Water GategateGate on the east.

I stood there with half the officials. 41The priests with trumpets were Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, Hananiah, 42Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. The singers sang loudly under Jezrahiah, who led them. 43That day they offered big sacrifices and were happy. God had given them great joy. The women and children were happy too. The joy of Jerusalem was heard far away.

Gifts for Temple Workers

44That day men were chosen to be in charge of the storerooms. They collected gifts, first crops, and tenths. They brought in the shares the LawlawLaw said to give to the priests and Levites. The fields around the towns gave these shares. Judah was happy about the priests and Levites who served. 45They took care of the work of their God and the work of making things clean. The singers and gatekeepers did this too, as David and his son Solomon had commanded. 46Long ago, in the days of David and Asaph, there were leaders of song. They sang praise and thanks to God. 47In the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel gave shares to the singers and gatekeepers. They gave what was needed each day. They set apart shares for the Levites. The Levites set apart shares for the sons of Aaron.
