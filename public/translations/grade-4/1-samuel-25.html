Samuel Dies

1Samuel died. All Israel came together. They cried for him. They buried him at his home in Ramah. Then David got up and went down to the DesertdesertDesert of Paran.

A Rich Man Named Nabal

2A man in Maon had land in Carmel. He was very rich. He had 3,000 sheep and 1,000 goats. He was cutting the wool from his sheep in Carmel. 3The man's name was Nabal. His wife's name was Abigail. She was smart and pretty. But the man was mean and did bad things. 4David was in the desert. He heard that Nabal was cutting the wool from his sheep.

David Sends Men to Nabal

5David sent ten young men. He said to them, "Go up to Nabal at Carmel. Greet him for me. 6Say to him, 'Peace to you, your family, and all you have.' 7I hear you have men cutting wool. Your shepherds were with us. We did not hurt them. We did not take anything from them the whole time they were in Carmel. 8Ask your young men. They will tell you. Please let my men find favourfavourfavor with you, because we come on a good day. Give whatever you can to your servants and to your son David."

9David's young men went and said all these things to Nabal in David's name. Then they waited.

Nabal Says No

10Nabal answered David's servants, "Who is David? Who is Jesse's son? There are many servants who run away from their masters these days. 11Why should I take my bread, my water, and the meat I cooked for my wool cutters and give it to men I do not know where they come from?"

12David's men turned around and went back. They told David everything Nabal said.

David Gets Angry

13David said to his men, "Each of you strap on your sword." So each man strapped on his sword. David strapped on his sword too. About four hundred men went with David. Two hundred stayed with the supplies.

A Servant Warns Abigail

14One of the young men told Abigail, Nabal's wife, "David sent messengers from the desert to greet our master. But he yelled at them. 15Those men were very good to us. They did not shame us. They did not take anything from us the whole time we were with them in the fields. 16They were like a wall around us, day and night, while we were with them watching the sheep. 17Now think about what you will do. Trouble is coming to our master and all his family. He is such a bad man that no one can talk to him."

Abigail Brings Food

18Abigail quickly took 200 loaves of bread, two skins of wine, five sheep that were ready, five baskets of roasted grain, 100 cakes of raisins, and 200 cakes of figs. She put them on donkeys. 19She told her young men, "Go ahead of me. I will follow you." But she did not tell her husband Nabal. 20She was riding on a donkey. She was going down the mountain in secret. David and his men were coming down to meet her. She met them.

David Vows Revenge

21David had said, "I guarded all this man had in the desert for nothing. Nothing of his was missing. But he paid me back with evil for good. 22May God do the same to David's enemies, and more, if by morning I leave alive any male in his house."

Abigail Meets David

23When Abigail saw David, she quickly got off her donkey. She bowed with her face to the ground. 24She fell at his feet and said, "MymyMy lord, let the blame be on me. Please let your servant speak to you. Listen to my words. 25My lord, please do not pay attention to this bad man Nabal. His name means fool, and he acts like a fool. I am your servant. I did not see the young men you sent.

26"Now, my lord, as the LORD lives, and as you live, the LORD has kept you from killing people and from saving yourself with your own hand. Now let your enemies and those who want to hurt you be like Nabal. 27Please accept this gift that your servant has brought for you and your men. 28Please forgive your servant's wrong. The LORD will surely make your family strong and lasting, because you fight the LORD's battles. No wrong will be found in you all your life. 29If someone chases you to hurt you and take your life, your life will be kept safe with the LORD your God, like a treasure kept safe. But HeheHe will throw away your enemies' lives, like a stone thrown from a sling. 30When ThetheThe LORD does all the good HeheHe has promised about you, and makes you the leader of Israel, this will not trouble you. You will not be sorry that you spilled blood for no reason or paid back evil yourself. 31When ThetheThe LORD has been good to you, remember your servant."

David Thanks Abigail

32David said to Abigail, "Praise the LORD, the God of Israel, who sent you to meet me today. 33Praise your good sense. Praise you, because today you kept me from killing people and from paying back evil myself. 34As surely as the LORD, the God of Israel, lives, HeheHe kept me from hurting you. If you had not come quickly to meet me, by morning not one male of Nabal's people would have been left alive."

35David took from her hand what she had brought. He said to her, "Go home in peace. I have listened to you and accepted your request."

Nabal Dies

36Abigail went to Nabal. He was in his house. He was having a feast like a king's feast. He was happy and very drunk. She did not tell him anything, big or small, until morning. 37In the morning, when the wine was gone, Abigail told him everything. His heart died inside him, and he became like a stone. 38About ten days later, the LORD struck Nabal, and he died.

David Marries Abigail

39David heard that Nabal was dead. He said, "Praise the LORD, who stood up for me against the rude way Nabal treated me. He kept me from doing wrong. The LORD brought Nabal's wrong back on his own head." Then David sent word to Abigail to ask her to be his wife.

40David's servants came to Abigail at Carmel. They said, "David sent us to you to take you as his wife."

41She stood up and bowed with her face to the ground. She said, "Here I am, your servant. I am ready to wash the feet of my lord's servants." 42Abigail quickly stood up and rode on a donkey. Five young women went with her. She followed David's messengers. She became his wife.

43David had also married Ahinoam from Jezreel. Both of them became his wives. 44But Saul had given his daughter Michal, David's wife, to Palti son of Laish, who was from Gallim.
