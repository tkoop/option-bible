Pharaoh's Army Drowned

19Pharaoh's horses, war carts, and men on horses went into the sea. The LORD brought the sea water back over them. But the people of Israel walked on dry ground in the middle of the sea.

Miriam Sings

20Miriam, a woman prophet and Aaron's sister, took a drum in her hand. All the women went out after her with drums and dancing. 21Miriam sang to them, "Sing to the LORD, because HeheHe is great and strong. He threw the horse and rider into the sea."

No Water in the Desert

22Moses led Israel away from the Red SeaseaSea. They went into the desert of Shur. They walked three days in the desert and found no water. 23They came to Marah, but they could not drink the water there because it was bitter. That is why the place was called Marah. 24The people complained to Moses. They said, "What will we drink?"

God Makes Bitter Water Sweet

25Moses cried out to the LORD. The LORD showed him a piece of wood. He threw it into the water, and the water became sweet. There the LORD made a rule and a law for them. There HeheHe tested them. 26He said, "If you listen carefully to the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is right in HishisHis eyes, and listen to HishisHis commands, and keep all HishisHis rules, I will not put on you any of the sicknesses I put on the Egyptians. I am the LORD who heals you."

Rest at Elim

27They came to Elim. There were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees. They camped there by the water.
