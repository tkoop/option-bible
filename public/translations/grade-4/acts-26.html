Paul Speaks Before King Agrippa

1Agrippa said to Paul, “You may speak for yourself.” Then Paul stretched out his hand and made his defencedefencedefense.

Paul Begins His DefenceDefenceDefense

“2King Agrippa, I am happy to stand before you today. I will answer all the charges the Jewish people bring against me. 3You know a lot about Jewish ways and questions. So I ask you to listen to me patiently.

“4All the Jewish people know how I lived from the start. They know I grew up among my own people in Jerusalem. 5They have known me for a long time. If they wanted, they could say I lived as a Pharisee. I followed the strictest part of our religion.

“6Now I stand here because I hope in God's promise. God made this promise to our fathers. 7Our twelve tribes hope to reach that promise. They serve God day and night. King Agrippa, the Jewish people accuse me because of this hope. 8Why would any of you think it is hard to believe that God raises the dead?

Paul Tells About His Past

“9I thought I should do many things against the name of Jesus from Nazareth. 10I did this in Jerusalem. I got power from the chief priests. I put many of God's people in jail. When they were killed, I voted against them. 11I punished them many times. In every synagogue I tried to make them speak against Jesus. I was very angry at them. I even chased them to cities far away.

Paul Sees a Light from Heaven

“‘Saul, Saul, why do you hurt MemeMe? It is hard for you to fight against MemeMe.’

“‘I am Jesus, whom you are hurting.’

You will turn them from darkness to light. You will turn them from Satan's power to God. Then they can receive forgiveness of sins. They can receive a place among God's holy people by faith in MemeMe.’

Paul Obeys God's Vision

“19So, King Agrippa, I did not disobey the vision from Heavenheavenheaven. 20First I told people in Damascus and Jerusalem. I told all the area of Judea and the other nations too. I told them to turn from sin and turn to God. I told them to do good things that show they are sorry.

“21Because of this, the Jewish people grabbed me in the temple. They tried to kill me. 22But God helped me. So I am still here today. I tell small and great people the same thing. I say nothing except what Moses and the prophets said would happen. 23They said the Christ would suffer. They said HeheHe would be the first to rise from the dead. They said HeheHe would bring light to HishisHis people and to the other nations.”

Festus and Agrippa React

24While Paul was saying this, Festus shouted, “Paul, you are crazy! Too much learning has made you mad!”

25Paul said, “I am not crazy, most excellent Festus. I speak true and sensible words. 26The king knows about these things. I speak openly to him. I am sure nothing has been hidden from him. These things did not happen in a corner. 27King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know you do.”

28Agrippa said to Paul, “In a short time you think you can make me a Christian?”

29Paul said, “I pray to God that soon or late, not only you but everyone hearing me today would become like me, except for these chains.”

They Decide Paul Did Nothing Wrong

30Then the king, the governor, Bernice, and the others stood up. 31They left and talked to each other. They said, “This man has done nothing that deserves death or chains.”

32Agrippa said to Festus, “This man could have been set free if he had not asked to go to Caesar.”
