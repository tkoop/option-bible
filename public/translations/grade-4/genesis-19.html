Lot Meets the Two Angels

1Two angels came to Sodom in the evening. Lot was sitting at the city gate. When Lot saw them, he stood up to meet them. He bowed with his face to the ground.

2He said, "Please, sirs, come to my house. Wash your feet and stay the night. Then you can get up early and go on your way."

They said, "No, we will stay in the town square."

3But Lot asked them so much that they went with him. He made a meal for them and baked flat bread. They ate.

The Men of Sodom Want the Angels

4Before they went to bed, all the men of Sodom, young and old, came around the house.

5They called to Lot, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us. We want to sleep with them."

6Lot went out to them and shut the door behind him.

7He said, "Please, my brothers, do not do this bad thing! 8Look, I have two daughters. They have never slept with a man. Let me bring them out to you. You can do what you want with them. But do not hurt these men. They came under my roof."

9They said, "Get out of the way! This man came here to live, and now he wants to be our judge! We will hurt you more than them." They pushed hard against Lot. They tried to break the door.

10But the men inside reached out. They pulled Lot into the house and shut the door.

11They made the men at the door blind. Young and old, they could not find the door.

The Angels Tell Lot to Leave

12The two men said to Lot, "Do you have anyone else here? A son-in-law, sons, daughters, or anyone in the city who belongs to you? Get them out of here. 13We are going to destroy this place. The cry to the the LORD against its people is so loud. He has sent us to destroy it."

14Lot went out and talked to the men who were going to marry his daughters. He said, "Get up! Get out of this place. The the LORD is going to destroy the city!" But they thought he was joking.

Lot Leaves Sodom

15At sunrise the angels pushed Lot. They said, "Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here. If you do not, you will be swept away with the city."

16Lot waited. But the men took his hand, his wife's hand, and his daughters' hands. They led them safely out of the city. The the LORD was kind to them.

17When they were outside, one said, "Run for your life! Do not look back. Do not stop on the plain. Run to the mountains, or you will be swept away."

18Lot said, "No, my lords, please! 19I have found favourfavourfavor with you. You have been very kind to me. You saved my life. But I cannot run to the mountains. The trouble will catch me, and I will die. 20Look, there is a small city near here. Let me run there. It is small. Let me go there, and I will live."

21He said, "All right. I will do this too. I will not destroy that city. 22Hurry! Run there fast. I cannot do anything until you get there." That is why the city is called Zoar.

23The sun had risen over the land when Lot reached Zoar.

Sodom and Gomorrah Are Destroyed

24Then the the LORD rained down burning sulphursulphursulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah. It came from the the LORD out of Heavenheavenheaven.

25He destroyed the cities of the plain. He destroyed all the people who lived there. He destroyed the plants of the land.

26But Lot's wife looked back. She became a pillar of salt.

27Early the next morning, Abraham got up. He went to the place where he had stood before the the LORD.

28He looked toward Sodom and Gomorrah and all the plain. He saw smoke rising from the land like smoke from a hot oven.

29When God destroyed the cities of the plain, HeheHe remembered Abraham. He brought Lot out of the disaster. Lot had lived in those cities.

Lot and His Daughters

30Lot and his two daughters left Zoar. They went up to the mountains. He was afraid to stay in Zoar. They lived in a cave.

31The older daughter said to the younger, "OurourOur father is old. There is no man here to sleep with us. This is how people have children all over the world. 32Come, let us give our father wine to drink. Then we will sleep with him. Then we can have children from our father."

33That night they gave their father wine to drink. The older daughter went in and slept with her father. He did not know when she lay down or got up.

34The next day the older daughter said to the younger, "I slept with our father last night. Let us give him wine again tonight. You go in and sleep with him. Then we can have children from our father."

35That night they gave their father wine again. The younger daughter went in and slept with her father. He did not know when she lay down or got up.

36So both of Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father.

37The older daughter had a son. She named him Moab. He is the father of the Moabites today.

38The younger daughter had a son. She named him Ben-ammi. He is the father of the Ammonites today.
