David Wins Many Battles

1After this, David defeated the Philistines and brought them under his rule. He took Metheg Ammah from them. 2He defeated Moab too. He made them lie on the ground and measured them with a rope. He killed two out of three and let one out of three live. Moab became David's servants and brought gifts.

David Defeats Hadadezer

3David also defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, when Hadadezer went to take back his power by the Euphrates River. 4David captured 1,700 horsemen and 20,000 foot soldiers from him. He crippled all the chariot horses, but he kept 100 chariots.

5When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David killed 22,000 of them. 6David placed soldiers in Damascus of Aram. The Arameans became David's servants and brought gifts. The LORD gave David victory everywhere he went.

7David took the gold shields that belonged to Hadadezer's officers and brought them to Jerusalem. 8From Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, David took a lot of bronze.

David Gets Gifts

9When Toi king of Hamath heard that David had defeated all of Hadadezer's army, 10he sent his son Joram to King David. Joram came to greet David and wish him well, because David had fought and defeated Hadadezer. Hadadezer had been at war with Toi. Joram brought gifts of silver, gold, and bronze.

11King David set these gifts apart for the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had set apart from all the nations he conquered. 12He set apart gifts from Aram, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, Amalek, and from the things taken from Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

David Becomes Famous

13David became famous when he came back from killing 18,000 Arameans in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 14He placed soldiers all through Edom. All of Edom became David's servants. The LORD gave David victory everywhere he went.

David Rules Israel

15David ruled over all Israel. He did what was fair and right for all his people.

David's Helpers

16Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records. 17Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests. Seraiah was the secretary. 18Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites. David's sons were priests.
