Joseph Plans a Test

1Joseph told the man in charge of his house, "Fill the men's bags with food. Give them as much as they can carry. Put each man's money back in his bag. 2Put my silver cup in the youngest brother's bag. Put his money for grain there too." The man did what Joseph said. 3When morning came, the men were sent away with their donkeys.

4They had not gone far from the city. Joseph said to the man in charge of his house, "Go after the men. Catch up to them. Say, 'Why did you pay back good with bad? 5Isn't this the cup my master drinks from? He uses it to tell what will happen. You did a bad thing.'" 6ThetheThe man caught up to them and said these words to them.

7They said to him, "Why does my lord say these things? We would never do such a thing. 8We brought money back from Canaan. How could we steal silver or gold from your lord's house? 9If the cup is found with any of us, that one will die. The rest of us will be your lord's slaves."

10The man said, "Let it be as you say. The one who has the cup will be my slave. The rest of you can go free." 11Each man quickly put his bag on the ground and opened it. 12The man searched. He started with the oldest brother and ended with the youngest. The cup was found in Benjamin's bag. 13They tore their clothes. Each man loaded his donkey. They went back to the city.

They Go Back to Joseph

14Judah and his brothers went to Joseph's house. Joseph was still there. They fell to the ground in front of him. 15Joseph said to them, "What is this you have done? Don't you know a man like me can find things out?"

16Judah said, "What can we say to my lord? How can we prove we are right? God has found out our sin. We are all your slaves now. So is the one with the cup."

17Joseph said, "No! Only the man with the cup will be my slave. The rest of you can go home in peace to your father."

Judah Speaks for Benjamin

18Judah came close to him. He said, "Please, my lord, let your servant speak. Don't be angry with your servant. You are like Pharaoh. 19My lord asked his servants, 'Do you have a father or a brother?' 20We said to my lord, 'We have an old father. He has a young son. His brother died. This young son is the only child left from that mother. His father loves him.' 21YouyouYou said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me. I want to see him.'"

22"We said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father. If he leaves his father, his father will die.' 23But you said to your servants, 'If your youngest brother does not come with you, you will not see my face again.' 24When we went back to your servant, our father, we told him what my lord said. 25Our father said, 'Go back and buy a little food for us.' 26We said, 'We cannot go down. If our youngest brother is with us, we will go. We cannot see the man's face unless our youngest brother is with us.'"

27"YouryourYour servant, our father, said to us, 'You know that my wife gave me two sons. 28One son left me. I said he was surely torn apart. I have not seen him since. 29If you take this one from me too, and something bad happens to him, I will die in sorrow.' 30Now, if I go back to your servant, our father, and the boy is not with us, his life is tied to the boy's life. 31When he sees the boy is not with us, he will die. Your servants will make our father die in sorrow. 32I promised my father I would keep the boy safe. I said I would be guilty before my father all my life if I did not bring him back. 33Now let me stay here as your slave instead of the boy. Let the boy go home with his brothers. 34How can I go back to my father if the boy is not with me? I do not want to see the trouble that would come to my father."
