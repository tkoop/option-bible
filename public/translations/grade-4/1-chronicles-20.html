David Fights the Ammonites

1In spring, when kings go to war, Joab led the army and ruined the land of the Ammonites. He surrounded their city, Rabbah, but David stayed in Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah and tore it down. 2Then David took the crown from their king's head. It weighed a lot of gold, and it had a pretty stone in it. David put it on his own head. He also took a lot of treasure from the city. 3David brought out the people who lived there. He made them work with saws, iron picks, and axes. He did the same thing in all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all his men went back to Jerusalem.

More Battles with the Philistines

4Later, war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. Then Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, who was from the giants. After that, the Philistines were beaten. 5There was another war with the Philistines. Elhanan son of Jair killed Lahmi, the brother of Goliath from Gath. Goliath's spear was very big. 6There was still another battle at Gath. A very tall man was there. He had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot. That made 24 in all. He also came from the giants.

7He made fun of Israel. Jonathan, the son of David's brother Shimea, killed him. 8So these giant men from Gath were killed by David and his men.
