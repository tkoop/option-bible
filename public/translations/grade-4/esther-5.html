7Esther answered, "This is my wish and my request:

8"If you are pleased with me, and if it pleases you to grant my wish and do what I ask, then let the king and Haman come to the feast I will make for them. Tomorrow I will do what you say."

Haman Gets Angry at Mordecai

9Haman left that day happy and glad. But when he saw Mordecai at the king's gate, and Mordecai did not stand up or move for him, Haman was very angry with Mordecai.

10But Haman held back his anger. He went home and sent for his friends and his wife, Zeresh.

11Haman told them about his great riches, his many children, and all the ways the king had honouredhonouredhonored him. He told how the king had made him greater than the other leaders and servants.

12Haman also said, "Queen Esther did not let anyone come with the king to the feast she made, except me. And tomorrow I am invited again with the king.

13"But all of this does not make me happy, because I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king's gate."

A Plan to Hang Mordecai

14His wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Have a tall pole made, about 75 feet high. In the morning, ask the king to hang Mordecai on it. Then go happily with the king to the feast." Haman liked this idea, and he had the pole made.
