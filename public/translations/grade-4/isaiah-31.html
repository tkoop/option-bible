Do Not Ask Egypt for Help

1Oh no! Some people go down to Egypt for help. They trust in horses. They trust in many war carts. They trust in horse riders because they are very strong. But they do not look to the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. They do not ask the LORD for help.

2But God is wise. He brings trouble. He does not take back HishisHis words. He will stand against the homes of bad people. He will stand against those who help people do wrong. 3But Egypt has people, not God. Their horses are just flesh, not spirit. When the LORD holds out HishisHis hand, the helper and the one helped will both stumble. They will all fall and die together.

The LORD Will Fight for Jerusalem

4The LORD said to me, "A lion growls over its food. A young lion does too. Many shepherds may call out to it. But it is not scared by their voices. It does not care about their noise. In the same way, the LORD of Armies will come down to fight on Mount Zion and on its hill." 5Like birds flying overhead, the LORD of Armies will protect Jerusalem. He will guard it. He will save it. He will pass over it and keep it safe.

Come Back to God

6Come back to the OneoneOne you all have turned far away from, children of Israel. 7On that day, each of you will throw away your silver idols and gold idols that your own hands made.

Assyria Will Fall

8Then Assyria will fall. No man's sword will kill him. A sword not made by man will destroy him. He will run away from the sword. His young men will be forced to work hard. 9His strong rock will pass away in fear. His leaders will panic at the battle flag," says the LORD. "His fire is in Zion, and HishisHis furnace is in Jerusalem."
