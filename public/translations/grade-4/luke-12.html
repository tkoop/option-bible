He said, "Watch out for the hypocrisy of the religious leaders. 2Nothing is hidden that will not be shown. Nothing is secret that will not be known. 3What you say in the dark will be heard in the light. What you whisper in a room will be shouted from the roof.

4"I tell you, MymyMy friends, do not be afraid of people who kill the body. After that, they can do nothing more. 5I will show you whom to fear. Fear God. After HeheHe kills, HeheHe has power to throw people into HellHellhell. Yes, I tell you, fear HimhimHim. 6Five sparrows are sold for two small coins. But God does not forget even one of them. 7Even the hairs on your head are all counted. Do not be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.

8"I tell you, everyone who says he follows MemeMe in front of people, the SonsonSon of ManmanMan will say he follows HimhimHim in front of God's angels. 9But anyone who says he does not know MemeMe in front of people will be turned away in front of God's angels. 10Everyone who speaks against the SonsonSon of ManmanMan can be forgiven. But anyone who speaks against the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit will not be forgiven.

11"When people bring you to meeting places, rulers, or leaders, do not worry about how to answer or what to say. 12The HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit will teach you in that hour what you should say."

A Man Asks About Money

13Someone in the crowd said to HimhimHim, "TeacherteacherTeacher, tell my brother to share the family money with me."

14Jesus said to him, "ManmanMan, who made MemeMe judge or divider over you?"

15Then HeheHe said to them, "Watch out! Guard yourselves from wanting too much. A person's life is not made full by having many things."

A Story About a Rich Man

16Then HeheHe told them a story. A rich man's land grew a big crop. 17He thought, "What should I do? I have no place to store my crops."

18He said, "I will do this. I will tear down my barns and build bigger ones. There I will store all my grain and goods."

19"Then I will say to myself, 'YouyouYou have many good things saved for many years. Rest now. Eat, drink, and be happy.'"

20But God said to him, "YouyouYou fool! Tonight your life will be taken from you. Then who will get the things you saved?"

21This is what happens to people who save up for themselves but are not rich toward God.

Do Not Worry

22Then Jesus said to HishisHis disciples, "So I tell you, do not worry about your life and what you will eat. Do not worry about your body and what you will wear.

23"Life is more than food. The body is more than clothes. 24Look at the ravens. They do not plant seeds. They do not gather crops. They have no barns. But God feeds them. You are worth much more than birds.

25"Which of you can add even a little bit to your life by worrying? 26If you cannot do such a small thing, why worry about the rest?

27"Look at how the flowers grow. They do not work or make cloth. But I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not dressed as nicely as one of these. 28If God dresses the grass of the field so well, and it is here today and gone tomorrow, how much more will HeheHe take care of you? You have so little faith.

29"So do not keep looking for what to eat or drink. Do not worry. 30People of the world look for all these things. But your FatherfatherFather knows you need them. 31Instead, look for God's kingdom. Then these things will be given to you too. 32Do not be afraid, little flock. Your FatherfatherFather is happy to give you the kingdom.

33"Sell what you own and give to people in need. Make money bags that do not wear out. Save treasure in Heavenheavenheaven that does not run out. Thieves cannot get near it. Moths cannot ruin it. 34Where your treasure is, there your heart will be too.

35"Be ready. Keep your belts tied and your lamps lit. 36Be like people waiting for their master to come home from a wedding. When he comes and knocks, they open the door right away.

37"Those servants are happy. The master finds them awake when he comes. I tell you the truth, he will dress himself to serve. He will have them sit down. Then he will serve them. 38Even if he comes in the middle of the night or near morning, those servants are happy if he finds them ready.

39"You know this: if the owner of a house knew when a thief was coming, he would not let his house be broken into. 40So you must be ready too. The SonsonSon of ManmanMan is coming at a time you do not expect."

Who Is the Faithful Servant?

41Peter said, "Lord, are you telling this story to us or to everyone?"

42The Lord said, "Who is the faithful and wise manager? The master puts him in charge of the other servants. He gives them their food at the right time. 43That servant is happy if the master finds him doing this when he comes. 44I tell you the truth, the master will put him in charge of all he owns.

45"But suppose that servant says to himself, 'My master is taking a long time to come.' Then he starts to hit the other servants. He eats and drinks and gets drunk. 46The master will come on a day the servant does not expect. He will come at a time the servant does not know. Then he will punish him badly. He will put him with people who do not believe.

47"That servant knew what his master wanted. But he did not get ready or do it. He will be punished with many blows. 48But a servant who did not know, and did things that should be punished, will get only a few blows. Everyone who is given much will have much asked of them. More will be asked from the one who was trusted with more."

Jesus Brings Division

49"I came to bring fire to the earth. I wish it were already burning! 50I have a baptism to go through. I am very troubled until it is finished. 51Do you think I came to bring peace to the earth? No, I tell you. I came to bring division.

52"From now on, five people in one house will be divided. Three will be against two, and two will be against three. 53They will be divided: father against son, son against father, mother against daughter, daughter against mother, mother-in-law against daughter-in-law, and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law."

Read the Times

54Jesus also said to the crowds, "When you see a cloud rise in the west, you say, 'Rain is coming.' And it happens. 55When the south wind blows, you say, 'It will be hot.' And it happens.

56"You fakers! You know how to read the sky and the earth. Why can you not read this time? 57Why do you not judge what is right for yourselves?

58"When you go with your enemy to a ruler, try hard to settle on the way. If you do not, he may drag you to the judge. The judge may hand you to the officer. The officer may throw you into jail. 59I tell you, you will not get out of there until you have paid the very last small coin."
