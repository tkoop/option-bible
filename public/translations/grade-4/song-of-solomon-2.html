The Bride Talks

1I am a flower from Sharon. I am a lily in the valleys.

The Groom Talks

2"Like a lily among thorns, so is my dear among the young women."

3"Like an apple tree among the trees of the woods, so is my love among the young men. I wanted to sit in his shade. His fruit tasted sweet to me. 4He brought me to the wine house. His flag over me was love."

5"Hold me up with raisin cakes. Make me comfortable with apples. I am weak with love. 6His left hand is under my head. His right arm holds me. 7Daughters of Jerusalem, I make you promise by the gazelles and the wild deer. Do not wake up love until it is ready."

The Bride Sees Her Love

8"I hear my love's voice! Look, he is coming. He jumps over the mountains. He skips over the hills. 9My love is like a gazelle or a young deer. Look, he stands behind our wall. He looks through the windows. He peeks through the openings."

The Groom Calls His Bride

10"Get up, my dear. Come with me, my beautiful one. 11Winter is gone. The rain has passed. 12Flowers are seen in the land. It is time to sing. The dove's voice is heard in our land. 13The fig tree has green figs. The grapevines give a sweet smell. Get up, my dear. Come with me, my beautiful one.

14"My dove, in the rock's cracks, in the secret place of the cliff, let me see your face. Let me hear your voice. Your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely."

The Bride Answers

15"Catch the little foxes for us. They ruin the grape fields. Our grape fields are in bloom. 16My love is mine, and I am his. He feeds his flock among the lilies. 17Until the day starts and the shadows run away, come back to me, my love. Be like a gazelle or a young deer on the mountains of Bether."
