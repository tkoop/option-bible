A Song of Asaph

God, Please Do Not Be Quiet

1God, do not be silent. Do not be quiet. Do not rest. 2Look! Your enemies are making noise. The people who hate YouyouYou are proud. 3They make secret plans against YouryourYour people. They plot against the ones YouyouYou keep safe.

4They say, "Come, let us wipe them out as a nation. Let no one remember Israel's name again."

Many Enemies Join Together

5They agree together. They make a pact against YouyouYou. 6They are the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites. 7They are Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia and the people of Tyre. 8Assyria has joined them too. They help the people of Lot.

God, Stop Them Like Before

9Do to them what YouyouYou did to Midian, to Sisera, and to Jabin at the Kishon River. 10They were destroyed at Endor. They became like dirt on the ground. 11Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb. Make all their rulers like Zebah and Zalmunna.

12They said, "Let us take God's fields for ourselves."

Chase Them Away

13Make them like a rolling weed, my God. Make them like dust blown by the wind. 14Like fire burning a forest, like a flame setting hills on fire, 15chase them with YouryourYour storm and scare them with YouryourYour strong wind.

Let Them Know You

16Fill their faces with shame, so they will look for YouryourYour name, the LORD. 17Let them be ashamed and scared forever. Let them be disgraced and die. 18Then they will know that YouyouYou alone are the LORD, the MostmostMost HighhighHigh over all the earth.
