God Tells Moses to Punish Midian

1The LORD spoke to Moses. 2He said, "Pay back the Midianites for what they did to Israel. After that, you will die and join your ancestors."

3Moses spoke to the people. He said, "Get ready for war. Pick men from among you to fight Midian. They will carry out the the LORD's punishment on Midian. 4Send one thousand men from each tribe of Israel to the war."

5So twelve thousand men were chosen from Israel. They were ready to fight. One thousand came from each tribe.

6Moses sent them to war. He sent one thousand men from each tribe. Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest went with them. He carried the holy things and the trumpets used to sound the alarm.

7They fought against Midian, just as the the LORD had told Moses. They killed every man. 8They killed the kings of Midian. Their names were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. These were the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with a sword.

9The Israelites captured the Midianite women and children. They took all their animals and everything they owned. 10They burned all the cities where the Midianites lived. They also burned their camps. 11They took all the goods and the captives. This included people and animals.

The Soldiers Return

12They brought the captives, the goods, and the things they had taken to Moses, Eleazar, and the whole community. They were at the camp on the plains of Moab, by the Jordan River, near Jericho. 13Moses, Eleazar, and all the leaders went out to meet them outside the camp.

Moses Is Angry

14Moses was angry with the officers and the leaders of the thousands and hundreds who came back from the war. 15Moses said to them, "Why did you let all the women live? 16These women led the Israelites to sin against the the LORD. They did it because Balaam told them to. This happened at Peor. That is why the the LORD's people became sick and many died. 17Now kill every boy among the little children. Kill every woman who has been with a man. 18But keep alive all the young girls who have not been with a man. You may keep them."

19Moses said, "Stay outside the camp for seven days. Anyone who killed a person or touched a dead body must wash to be clean. Do this on the third day and on the seventh day. This is for you and the people you captured. 20Wash every piece of clothing, every leather thing, everything made from goat hair, and every wooden thing."

How to Clean the Things Taken

21Eleazar the priest said to the soldiers who had gone to war, "This is the rule of the law the the LORD gave Moses: 22Only gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead can go through fire. 23Put everything that can stand fire through the fire. Then it will be clean. But it must also be washed with the cleaning water. Everything that cannot stand fire must be washed with water. 24On the seventh day, wash your clothes. Then you will be clean. After that, you may come into the camp."

Dividing What Was Taken

25The LORD said to Moses, 26"YouyouYou and Eleazar the priest and the family leaders of the community must count the people and animals that were taken. 27Divide the things taken between the soldiers who went to war and all the people. 28From the soldiers who went to war, take one out of every five hundred people, cattle, donkeys, and sheep for the the LORD. 29Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as an offering to the the LORD. 30From the people of Israel's half, take one out of every fifty people, cattle, donkeys, sheep, and all other animals. Give them to the Levites who take care of the the LORD's HolyholyHoly TenttentTent."

31Moses and Eleazar did what the the LORD commanded.

32The things the army took were 675,000 sheep. 33They also took 72,000 cattle. 34They took 61,000 donkeys. 35And they took 32,000 women who had not been with a man.

36The half given to the soldiers who went to war was 337,500 sheep, 36,000 cattle, 30,500 donkeys, and 16,000 people.

37From the soldiers' half, the offering to the the LORD was 675 sheep. 38It was 72 cattle. 39It was 61 donkeys. 40And it was 32 people.

41Moses gave the offering to Eleazar the priest, just as the the LORD had commanded him.

The Soldiers Give a Gift

48The officers over the thousands and hundreds came to Moses. 49They said to Moses, "YouryourYour servants have counted the soldiers under our care. Not one man is missing. 50So we bring a gift to the the LORD. It is gold from what each man found: arm bands, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces. We bring it to make up for our sins before the the LORD."

51Moses and Eleazar took all the gold items. 52All the gold from the commanders and captains was 16,750 shekels. 53The soldiers had kept the things they took for themselves. 54Moses and Eleazar brought the gold into the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. It was a reminder for the Israelites before the the LORD.
