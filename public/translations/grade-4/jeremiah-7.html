God's Message at the Temple Gate

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD. 2The LORD said, "Stand at the gate of the LORD's house. Say this message there: 'Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who come through these gates to worship the LORD.'"

Better Ways and Actions

3The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says, "Make your ways and your actions better. Then I will let you live in this place. 4Do not trust in false words and say, 'This is the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD.' 5If you really make your ways and actions better, and if you really do what is right between a person and his neighbourneighbourneighbor, 6if you do not hurt the stranger, the child with no father, or the woman whose husband died, and if you do not kill innocent people in this place, and if you do not follow other gods and hurt yourselves, 7then I will let you live in this place, in the land I gave to your fathers long ago and forever."

8But look, you trust in false words that do not help. 9Will you steal, kill, cheat on your husband or wife, lie in court, burn incense to Baal, and follow gods you do not know? 10Then you come and stand in front of MemeMe in this house that has MymyMy name on it. You say, "We are safe now," but then you go and do all these bad things again. 11Has this house, which has MymyMy name on it, become a cave for robbers to you? Look, I have seen it, says the LORD.

Shiloh's Warning

12Go to MymyMy place in Shiloh. That is where I first put MymyMy name. See what I did to it because of the bad things MymyMy people Israel did. 13Now, because you have done all these things, says the LORD, and because I spoke to you again and again but you did not listen, and I called you but you did not answer, 14I will do to this house, which has MymyMy name on it, the same thing I did to Shiloh. I will do it to the place you trust in. I will do it to the place I gave to you and your fathers. 15I will throw you out of MymyMy sight, just as I threw out all your brothers, all the children of Ephraim.

Do Not Pray for Them

16As for you, do not pray for these people. Do not cry out or pray for them. Do not ask MemeMe to help them, because I will not listen to you. 17Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem? 18The sons gather wood, the fathers light the fire, and the women mix dough to make cakes for the Queen of Heaven. They pour out drinks for other gods to make MemeMe angry. 19But are they making MemeMe angry? says the LORD. They are hurting themselves and bringing shame on themselves.

God's Anger Will Burn

20So this is what the Lord GOD says: My anger and MymyMy great anger will pour out on this place. It will hit people and animals. It will hit trees and crops. It will burn and will not go out.

Offerings Are Not Enough

21This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices and eat the meat yourselves." 22When I brought your fathers out of Egypt, I did not speak to them or command them about burnt offerings and sacrifices. 23But I gave them this command: "Obey MymyMy voice. Then I will be your God, and you will be MymyMy people. Walk in all the ways I command you, so it may go well with you." 24But they did not listen. They did not pay attention. They followed the stubborn thoughts of their evil hearts. They got worse, not better. 25From the day your fathers left Egypt until now, I have sent you all MymyMy servants, the prophets. I sent them again and again. 26But they would not listen to MemeMe. They would not pay attention. They were stubborn. They did worse than their fathers.

Tell Them the Truth

27When you tell them all this, they will not listen to you. When you call them, they will not answer you. 28So you are to tell them, "This is the nation that would not listen to the LORD their God. They would not take correction. Truth is gone. It is not in their mouths anymore."

A Sad Song

29Cut off your hair and throw it away. Sing a sad song on the bare hills, because the LORD has rejected and left the people HeheHe is angry with. 30The sons of Judah have done evil in MymyMy sight, says the LORD. They have put their bad things in the house that has MymyMy name on it, and made it unclean. 31They built places to worship at Topheth in the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom. There they burned their sons and daughters in the fire. I did not command this. It never came into MymyMy mind.

Valley of Slaughter

32So the days are coming, says the LORD, when people will not call it Topheth or the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom. They will call it the ValleyvalleyValley of SlaughterslaughterSlaughter. They will bury people in Topheth until there is no room. 33The dead bodies of these people will be food for birds and wild animals. No one will scare them away. 34I will take away the happy sounds in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem. No one will hear the voice of the bridegroom or the bride. The land will become an empty land.
