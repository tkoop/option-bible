The LORD Wants Right and Fair Living

1This is what the LORD says: "Keep what is right and do what is fair. My saving help is coming soon. My right way will soon be seen." 2Happy is the person who does this. Happy is the one who holds on to it. He keeps the Sabbath day and does not ruin it. He keeps his hands from doing bad.

The LORD Welcomes All People

3A person from another land who joins the LORD must not say, "ThetheThe LORD will keep me out of HishisHis people."

A man who cannot have children must not say, "I can never have children."

4This is what the LORD says to the men who cannot have children: "If you keep MymyMy Sabbath days, choose what pleases MemeMe, and hold on to MymyMy agreement, 5I will give you a home in MymyMy house and a place inside MymyMy walls. I will give you a name better than sons and daughters. Your name will last forever. 6Foreigners who join the LORD to serve HimhimHim, to love HishisHis name, and to be HishisHis servants must keep the Sabbath and not ruin it. They must hold on to MymyMy agreement. 7I will bring them to MymyMy holy hill. I will make them happy in MymyMy house of prayer. The gifts they burn on the altar and their other gifts will be accepted. My house will be called a house of prayer for all people."

8The Lord, the LORD, says this. He gathers the people of Israel who were sent away. He says, "I will gather even more people to them."

Bad Leaders Are Warned

9Come, all you wild animals of the field. Come and eat. Come, all you wild animals of the forest. Come and eat. 10Israel's guards are blind. They know nothing. They are like dogs that cannot bark. They lie down and dream. They like to sleep. 11They are dogs with big appetites. They never have enough. They are shepherds who do not understand. They all go their own way. Each one wants more for himself. 12They say, "Come, let us get wine. Let us drink strong drink. Tomorrow will be just like today, but even better."
