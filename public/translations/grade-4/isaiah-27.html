The Lord Will Punish the Sea Monster

1In that day, the LORD will punish Leviathan, the snake that runs away, with HishisHis strong sword. He will kill the dragon in the sea.

A Song About God's Vineyard

2“In that day, sing about a vineyard full of fruit. 3I am the LORD. I take care of it. I water it all the time. I keep it safe day and night, so no one can hurt it. 4I am not angry. I wish thorns and weeds were here for MemeMe to fight! I would go against them. I would burn them all up. 5Or let them hold on to MymyMy strength. Then they can make peace with MemeMe. Yes, let them make peace with MemeMe.”

Israel Will Grow and Fill the World

6Later, Jacob will grow roots. Israel will bloom and bud. It will fill the whole world with fruit.

The Lord Corrected Israel

7Did the LORD punish Israel like HeheHe punished her enemies? Was she killed like those who killed her? 8By war and being sent away, YouyouYou fought with her. You took her away with a strong wind on a hot day. 9So Jacob's sin will be forgiven. This is the full fruit of taking away his sin: he will crush all the altar stones into small pieces. No poles for Asherah and no incense altars will be left.

The Empty City

10The strong city is empty. It is like a deserted home in the desert. A calf eats grass there. It lies down there and eats the branches. 11When the branches dry up, they break off. Women come and burn them for firewood. This is because the people do not understand. So their MakermakerMaker will not have pity on them. Their CreatorcreatorCreator will not be kind to them.

God Will Bring His People Home

12In that day, the LORD will gather you, children of Israel, one by one. He will gather you from the Euphrates River to the stream in Egypt. 13In that day, a loud trumpet will sound. The people lost in Assyria will come. The people scattered in Egypt will come. They will worship the LORD on HishisHis holy mountain in Jerusalem.
