God Tells Jeremiah to Write His Words

1In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this message came to Jeremiah from the LORD.

2"Get a scroll. Write on it all the words I have spoken to you about Israel, Judah, and all the other nations. Write from the day I first spoke to you in Josiah's time until now. 3Maybe the people of Judah will hear about all the bad things I plan to do to them. Then each person may stop doing bad things. Then I will forgive their sin."

4So Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah. Baruch wrote on the scroll all the words the LORD had spoken to Jeremiah.

Jeremiah Sends Baruch to Read the Scroll

5Jeremiah told Baruch, "I am stuck here. I cannot go to the LORD's house."

6"So you go to the LORD's house on the fast day. Read the LORD's words from the scroll. Read them to all the people of Judah who come from their towns. 7Maybe they will pray to the LORD. Maybe each person will stop doing bad things. The LORD is very angry with these people."

Baruch Reads the Scroll

8Baruch did everything Jeremiah the prophet told him. He read the LORD's words from the book in the LORD's house.

9In the ninth month of the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, all the people in Jerusalem and all the people who came from the towns of Judah said they would not eat before the LORD.

10Then Baruch read Jeremiah's words from the book. He read them to all the people in the room of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe. This room was in the upper courtyard, by the NewnewNew GategateGate of the LORD's house.

11Micaiah son of Gemariah son of Shaphan heard all the words of the LORD from the book.

12He went down to the king's house, to the scribe's room. All the officials were sitting there. They were Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.

13Micaiah told them all the words he heard Baruch read to the people.

The Leaders Call for the Scroll

14Then all the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah son of Shelemiah son of Cushi to Baruch. They said, "BringbringBring the scroll you read to the people, and come here." So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll and came to them.

15They said to him, "Sit down and read it to us." So Baruch read it to them.

16When they heard all the words, they were afraid. They looked at one another. They said to Baruch, "We must tell the king all these words."

17Then they asked Baruch, "Tell us, how did you write all these words? Did he tell you to write them?"

18Baruch said, "HeheHe spoke all these words to me. I wrote them with ink on the book."

19The officials said to Baruch, "Go and hide. You and Jeremiah must hide. Do not let anyone know where you are."

20Then they went to the king in the courtyard. They left the scroll in the room of Elishama the scribe. They told the king all the words.

21The king sent Jehudi to get the scroll. Jehudi took it from Elishama's room. He read it to the king and to all the officials standing by the king.

The King Burns the Scroll

22The king was sitting in his winter house. It was the ninth month. A fire burned in a pot in front of him.

23As Jehudi read three or four parts, the king cut them with a knife. He threw them into the fire. He did this until the whole scroll was burned in the fire.

24The king and all his servants who heard these words were not afraid. They did not show any sorrow.

25Even Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah begged the king not to burn the scroll. But he would not listen to them.

26The king told Jerahmeel son of the king, Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to arrest Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet. But the LORD hid them.

God Gives a New Scroll

27After the king burned the scroll and the words Baruch wrote at Jeremiah's mouth, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah.

28"Take another scroll. Write on it all the words that were on the first scroll. Jehoiakim, king of Judah, burned that scroll."

29"Say to Jehoiakim, king of Judah, 'This is what the LORD says: You burned this scroll. You did not want the words that said the king of Babylon will come and destroy this land and kill people and animals.'"

30"So this is what the LORD says about Jehoiakim, king of Judah: No one from his family will rule as king after him. His dead body will be left outside in the heat by day and the cold by night."

31"I will punish him, his children, and his servants for their sin. I will bring on them, on the people of Jerusalem, and on the men of Judah all the bad things I spoke about. They did not listen."

32Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah the scribe. Baruch wrote on it, at Jeremiah's mouth, all the words from the book that Jehoiakim, king of Judah, had burned in the fire. Many more words like them were added.
