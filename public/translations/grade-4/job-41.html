The LORD Talks About Leviathan

1“Can you pull in Leviathan with a hook? Can you tie his tongue with a rope? 2Can you put a rope through his nose? Can you hook his jaw? 3Will he ask you to be kind? Will he speak soft words to you? 4Will he make a deal with you to be your slave forever? 5Can you make him a pet like a bird? Can you tie him up for your girls? 6Will traders try to buy him? Will they split him up to sell? 7Can you fill his skin with sharp spears? Can you stick fishing spears in his head? 8If you touch him, you will remember the fight and not do it again! 9Look, hoping to catch him is useless. Just seeing him scares people.

10“No one is fierce enough to wake him. So who can stand against MemeMe? 11Who gave MemeMe something first, so I must pay them back? Everything under the sky is MinemineMine.

Leviathan's Body and Strength

12“I will not be quiet about his body parts, his strength, and his fine shape. 13Who can strip off his outer coat? Who can come close with a bridle? 14Who can open the doors of his face? His teeth are scary all around. 15His back has rows of shields. They are shut tight together. 16They are so close that no air can pass between them. 17They stick to each other. They hold tight and cannot be pulled apart.

18“When he sneezes, light flashes. His eyes are like the first light of morning. 19Burning sticks leap from his mouth. Sparks of fire shoot out. 20Smoke comes from his nose like a pot boiling over burning reeds. 21His breath sets coals on fire. A flame pours from his mouth.

22“Strength lives in his neck. Fear jumps up in front of him. 23The folds of his flesh stick together. They are firm and will not move. 24His heart is hard like a stone, even like a heavy grinding stone. 25When he rises up, even strong men are afraid. They step back in fear.

Nothing Can Hurt Leviathan

26“A sword that reaches him does no good. Neither does a spear, dart, or javelin. 27He thinks iron is like straw. He thinks bronze is like rotten wood. 28Arrows cannot make him flee. Stones from a sling are like dust to him. 29A club is like straw to him. He laughs at the sound of a javelin. 30His underside is like sharp pieces of broken pots. He leaves a trail in the mud like a heavy sled.

31“He makes the deep water boil like a pot. He turns the sea into a boiling pot. 32He leaves a glowing path behind him. The deep water looks white, like foam. 33Nothing on EarthEarthearth is like him. He is a creature with no fear. 34He looks down on proud people. He is king over all who are proud.”
