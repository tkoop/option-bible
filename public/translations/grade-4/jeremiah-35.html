Jeremiah and the Rechabites

1The LORD spoke to Jeremiah. This was when Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, was king of Judah. 2The LORD said, "Go to the Rechabites. Talk to them. Bring them to one of the rooms in the house of the LORD. Give them wine to drink."

3So Jeremiah took Jaazaniah. He was the son of Jeremiah and the grandson of Habazziniah. Jeremiah also took Jaazaniah's brothers and all his sons. He took all the Rechabites. 4He brought them to the house of the LORD. They went to the room of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah. He was a man of God. This room was next to the room of the leaders. It was above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, who guarded the door.

5Jeremiah put bowls full of wine and cups in front of the Rechabites. He said, "Drink some wine."

6But they said, "We will not drink wine. Our father, Jonadab son of Rechab, told us not to drink wine. He said we and our children must never drink it. 7He also said we must not build houses. We must not plant seeds. We must not plant grapevines. We must not own them. We must live in tents all our lives. Then we will live a long time in the land where we live. 8We listened to our father, Jonadab son of Rechab. We do not drink wine. This is true for us, our wives, our sons, and our daughters. 9We do not build houses to live in. We have no grapevines, fields, or seeds. 10We live in tents. We listen to our father, Jonadab. We do what he told us. 11When King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came to this land, we said, 'Let us go to Jerusalem. The armies of Babylon and Aram are coming.' So we came to Jerusalem and stayed there."

The LORD Speaks Again

12Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah again. 13The LORD of Heaven's Armies, the God of Israel, said, "Go and tell the people of Judah and the people in Jerusalem, 'Will you not learn to listen to MymyMy words?' says the LORD." 14"Jonadab son of Rechab told his sons not to drink wine. They have not drunk wine to this day. They obeyed their father's command. But I have spoken to you again and again. You have not listened to MemeMe." 15"I sent all MymyMy servants, the prophets, to you. I said, 'Turn from your bad ways. Do good things. Do not follow other gods. Serve them no more. Then you can stay in the land I gave you and your fathers.' But you did not listen to MemeMe. You did not pay attention." 16"The sons of Jonadab son of Rechab kept their father's command. But these people have not listened to MemeMe."

The LORD's Warning

17So the LORD of Heaven's Armies, the God of Israel, says, "I will bring on Judah and on everyone in Jerusalem all the bad things I said I would bring. I spoke to them, but they did not listen. I called them, but they did not answer."
