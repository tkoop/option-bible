Pharaoh's Dreams

1Two full years passed. Then Pharaoh dreamed he was standing by the Nile River. 2Seven cows came up out of the Nile. They looked good and were fat. They ate grass by the river. 3After them, seven other cows came up. They looked bad and were thin. They stood by the first cows on the riverbank. 4The bad thin cows ate the seven good fat cows. Then Pharaoh woke up. 5He went back to sleep and dreamed again. Seven heads of grain grew on one stalk. They were full and good. 6After them, seven more heads grew. They were thin and dried by the east wind. 7The thin heads swallowed the seven full good heads. Pharaoh woke up. It was a dream. 8In the morning, Pharaoh was upset. He sent for all of Egypt's magic men and wise men. He told them his dreams. No one could explain them to Pharaoh.

The Cupbearer Remembers Joseph

9Then the chief drink server said to Pharaoh, "Today I remember my sins. 10Pharaoh was angry with his servants. He put me and the chief baker in prison. The prison was in the house of the captain of the guard. 11One night we both dreamed. Each of us had a dream with its own meaning. 12A young Hebrew man was there with us. He was a servant of the captain of the guard. We told him our dreams. He explained them to us. He told each man what his dream meant. 13Things happened just as he said. Pharaoh put me back in my job, and he hanged the baker."

Joseph Is Brought to Pharaoh

14Pharaoh sent for Joseph. They rushed him out of the prison. He shaved and changed his clothes. Then he came to Pharaoh.

15Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I had a dream. No one can explain it. I heard that you can hear a dream and explain it."

16Joseph answered Pharaoh, "It is not me. God will give Pharaoh a good answer."

17Pharaoh said to Joseph, "In my dream I stood on the bank of the Nile. 18Seven cows came up. They were fat and good-looking. They ate grass by the river. 19After them, seven other cows came up. They were very ugly and thin. I have never seen such bad cows in all Egypt. 20The thin ugly cows ate the seven fat cows that came up first. 21But when they had eaten them, no one could tell. They still looked as thin as before. Then I woke up. 22I also dreamed. Seven heads of grain grew on one stalk. They were full and good. 23After them, seven more heads grew. They were thin and dried by the east wind. 24The thin heads swallowed the seven good heads. I told this to the magic men, but no one could explain it to me."

Joseph Explains the Dreams

25Joseph said to Pharaoh, "Pharaoh's dreams are one dream. God has shown Pharaoh what HeheHe is going to do. 26The seven good cows are seven years. The seven good heads are seven years. It is one dream. 27The seven thin ugly cows that came up after them are seven years. The seven thin heads dried by the east wind are seven years of hunger. 28This is what I told Pharaoh. God has shown Pharaoh what HeheHe is going to do. 29Seven years with lots of food are coming in all Egypt. 30After them, seven years of hunger will come. All the extra food in Egypt will be forgotten. Hunger will ruin the land. 31The extra food will not be remembered. The hunger after it will be so bad. 32The dream came twice to Pharaoh because God has decided it. He will do it soon. 33Now Pharaoh should look for a wise and understanding man. Put him in charge of Egypt. 34Pharaoh should choose leaders over the land. Take one-fifth of Egypt's food during the seven good years. 35They should gather all the good food that is coming. Store grain under Pharaoh's care. Keep food in the cities. 36That food will be stored for the land for the seven years of hunger. Then Egypt will not be ruined by hunger."

Pharaoh Puts Joseph in Charge

37The plan sounded good to Pharaoh and all his servants.

38Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find a man like this? He has God's SpiritspiritSpirit in him."

39Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has shown you all this, no one is as wise and understanding as you. 40You will be over my house. All my people will obey you. Only the throne will be greater than you. 41See, I have put you over all Egypt."

42Pharaoh took his signet ring from his hand. He put it on Joseph's hand. He dressed Joseph in fine linen clothes. He put a gold chain around his neck. 43He had Joseph ride in his second chariot. Men called out before him, "Bow down!" He put him over all Egypt.

44Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh. No one in all Egypt may lift a hand or foot without your permission."

45Pharaoh gave Joseph the name Zaphenath-Paneah. He gave him Asenath, daughter of Potiphera priest of On, as his wife. Joseph went out over Egypt. 46Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. Joseph went out from Pharaoh. He travelledtravelledtraveled through all Egypt. 47During the seven good years, the land made lots of food. 48Joseph gathered all the food in Egypt for seven years. He put food in the cities. He stored the food from the fields around each city in that city. 49Joseph stored so much grain that it was like sand by the sea. He stopped counting it because there was too much. 50Before the years of hunger came, Joseph had two sons with Asenath, daughter of Potiphera priest of On. 51Joseph named his first son Manasseh. He said, "God made me forget all my hard work and all my father's house." 52He named his second son Ephraim. He said, "God made me do well in the land where I suffered." 53The seven good years in Egypt ended.

The Years of Hunger

54The seven years of hunger began, just as Joseph had said. There was hunger in all lands. But in Egypt there was bread. 55All Egypt was hungry. The people cried to Pharaoh for food. Pharaoh said to all Egypt, "Go to Joseph. Do what he says." 56The hunger was over all the earth. Joseph opened all the storehouses. He sold grain to Egypt. The hunger got worse in Egypt. 57People from all over the world came to Egypt to buy grain, because the hunger was bad everywhere.
