Paul Says He Is an Apostle

1Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Aren't you my work in the Lord? 2Even if I am not an apostle to other people, I am to you. You are the proof that God sent me to do HishisHis work. 3This is how I answer people who question me.

Paul Talks About His Rights

4Don't we have the right to eat and drink? 5Don't we have the right to take a wife who believes, like the other apostles, the Lord's brothers, and Peter? 6Or are only Barnabas and I left without the right to stop working? 7Who goes to war and pays his own way? Who plants a grape field and does not eat its fruit? Who takes care of sheep and does not drink their milk? 8Am I saying this just from a human point of view? Doesn't God's law say the same thing? 9For it is written in the LawlawLaw of Moses, "Do not put a muzzle on an ox while it works." Is God really worried about oxen? 10No, HeheHe says this for us. The farmer plows hoping to get a crop. The worker threshes hoping to share in the food. 11If we planted spiritual seeds among you, is it too much to get food and help from you? 12If other people have this right from you, shouldn't we have it even more? But we did not use this right. We put up with everything so we would not slow down the good news about Christ. 13Don't you know that people who work in God's house eat from God's house? People who serve at the altar share in what is brought to the altar. 14In the same way, the Lord said that people who tell the good news should live from the good news.

Paul Works for Free

15But I have not used any of these rights. I am not writing this to ask you to give me anything. I would rather die than let anyone take away my reason to be proud. 16I cannot brag just because I tell the good news. I have to do it. I am in trouble if I do not tell the good news. 17If I do it because I want to, I get a reward. If I do it because I must, God still gave me a job to do. 18So what is my reward? It is that I can tell the good news for free, without using all my rights.

Paul Serves All People

19I am free from everyone, but I made myself a servant to everyone so I could win more people. 20To Jewish people I became like a Jewish person, so I could win Jewish people. To people under the law I became like one under the law, even though I am not under the law, so I could win them. 21To people without the law I became like one without the law. But I am not free from God's law. I am under Christ's law. I did this to win people without the law. 22To weak people I became weak, so I could win weak people. I became all things to all people so I could save some. 23I do all this for the good news, so I can share in its blessings.

Run to Win

24Don't you know that in a race all runners run, but only one gets the prize? Run so you can win it. 25Everyone who plays in the games trains hard. They do it to get a crown that fades away. We do it to get a crown that lasts forever. 26So I do not run without knowing where I am going. I do not fight like I am hitting the air. 27I keep my body under control and make it obey me. Then, after I preach to others, I will not fail the test.
