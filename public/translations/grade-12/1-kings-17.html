Elijah Declares a Drought

1Elijah the Tishbite, one of the settlers of Gilead, said to Ahab, “As surely as the the LORD—the God of Israel—lives, before whom I stand, there will be no dew or rain in these years except at my word.”

2Then the word of the the LORD came to him: 3“Leave here, turn eastward, and hide in the BrookbrookBrook Cherith, which lies east of the Jordan. 4You will drink from the brook, and I have commanded the ravens to provide for you there.”

5So he did what the the LORD had said. He went and stayed by the BrookbrookBrook Cherith, east of the Jordan. 6The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the brook. 7After some time the brook dried up, because there was no rain in the land.

Elijah and the Widow of Zarephath

8Then the word of the the LORD came to him: 9“Get up, go to Zarephath in Sidon, and stay there. I have commanded a widow there to provide for you.”

10So he got up and went to Zarephath. When he arrived at the town gate, a widow was there gathering sticks. He called to her and said, “Please bring me a little water in a jar so I may drink.” 11As she was going to get it, he called, “Please bring me a piece of bread as well.”

12But she said, “As surely as the the LORD your God lives, I have no bread—only a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am gathering a couple of sticks to go in and bake it for myself and my son, so that we may eat it and then die.”

13Elijah said to her, “Do not be afraid. Go and do as you have said. But first make me a small cake of bread from what you have and bring it to me; afterward make some for yourself and your son. 14For this is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘The jar of flour will not be used up and the jug of oil will not run dry until the day the the LORD sends rain on the face of the earth.’”

15So she went and did as Elijah had said, and there was food for Elijah, the woman, and her household for many days. 16The jar of flour was not used up and the jug of oil did not run dry, in keeping with the word of the the LORD spoken by Elijah.

Elijah Raises the Widow’s Son

17Some time later the son of the woman who owned the house became ill. His illness grew so severe that he stopped breathing.

18She said to Elijah, “What do you have against me, man of God? Have you come to me to call my sin to mind and to kill my son?”

19Elijah replied, “Give me your son.” He took the boy from her arms, carried him to the upper room where he was staying, and laid him on his bed.

20Then he cried out to the the LORD, “The LORD my God, have YouyouYou brought calamity upon this widow with whom I am staying by killing her son?”

21Then he stretched himself out over the child three times and cried out to the the LORD, “The LORD my God, please let this child’s life return to him!”

22The LORD heard Elijah’s cry, and the child’s life returned to him, and he lived.

23Elijah took the child and brought him down from the upper room into the house. He gave him to his mother and said, “Look, your son is alive!”

24Then the woman said to Elijah, “Now I know that you are a man of God and that the word of the the LORD from your mouth is true.”
