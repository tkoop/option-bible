Habakkuk’s Steadfast Faith Amid Ruin

16I heard and my heart pounded; my lips quivered at the sound. Decay entered my bones; I trembled where I stood. Yet I will wait patiently for the day of calamity to come upon the people who invade us. 17Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls, 18yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice in the God of my salvation! 19The Lord the LORD is my strength; HeheHe makes my feet like those of a deer; HeheHe makes me walk on high places.
