David’s Prayer for Rescue from Violent and Slanderous Enemies

1For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. Deliver me, O the LORD, from evil; preserve me from men of violence. 2They are those who devise wickedness in their hearts and stir up strife continually. 3They sharpen their tongues like a serpent’s; the venom of an adder rests beneath their lips. Selah

4Guard me, O the LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from men of violence who scheme to make my steps falter. 5The arrogant have hidden a snare for me; they have spread cords as a net along my path and set traps in my way. Selah

6I said to the the LORD, “You are my God.” Hear, O the LORD, the voice of my petitions. 7O the LORD, my Lord, the strength of my salvation, YouyouYou have shielded my head in the day of battle.

8Do not grant, O the LORD, the desires of the wicked, nor let their plots succeed, lest they exalt themselves. Selah

9May the mischief of their own lips engulf the heads of those who surround me. 10Let burning coals rain down upon them; let them be hurled into the fire, into miry pits, never to rise again. 11Let no slanderer gain a foothold in the land; may calamity pursue the violent man to his overthrow.

12I know that the the LORD secures justice for the afflicted and upholds the cause of the needy. 13Surely the righteous will praise YouryourYour name; the upright will dwell in YouryourYour presence.
