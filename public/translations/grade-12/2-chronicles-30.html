Hezekiah’s Summons to a National Passover

1Hezekiah dispatched couriers throughout Israel and Judah, and he also composed letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, urging them to assemble at the house of the LORD in Jerusalem and observe the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel. 2The king, his officials, and the whole assembly in Jerusalem had resolved to celebrate the Passover in the second month, 3for they could not celebrate it at the appointed time, because too few priests had been consecrated and the people had not yet gathered in Jerusalem. 4The proposal pleased the king and the entire assembly. 5So they decreed that a proclamation should be sent throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, summoning the people to Jerusalem to celebrate the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel, because they had not observed it in large numbers as prescribed.

The Messengers’ Appeal and the People’s Response

6According to the king’s command, the couriers travelledtravelledtraveled throughout Israel and Judah carrying letters from the king and his officials. They read: “Children of Israel, return to the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, so that HeheHe may return to the remnant of you who escaped the power of the Assyrian kings. 7Do not be like your fathers and your brothers, who were unfaithful to the LORD, the God of their fathers, and whom HeheHe therefore consigned to ruin, as you can see. 8Do not stiffen your necks as your fathers did. Yield yourselves to the LORD, enter HishisHis sanctuary, which HeheHe has consecrated forever, and serve the LORD your God, so that HishisHis fierce anger may turn away from you. 9For if you return to the LORD, your brothers and your sons will find mercy before their captors and return to this land. For the LORD your God is gracious and merciful and will not turn HishisHis face from you if you return to HimhimHim.”

10The couriers went from city to city through the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, as far as Zebulun, but the people laughed at them and mocked them. 11Nevertheless, some men from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem. 12Moreover, the hand of God was on Judah, giving them one heart to carry out the command of the king and the officials in accordance with the word of the LORD.

The Passover Celebration and Hezekiah’s Intercession

The priests and Levites were ashamed, so they consecrated themselves and brought burnt offerings to the house of the LORD. 16They took their positions according to the ordinance, as the law of Moses, the man of God, required: the priests sprinkled the blood, receiving it from the Levites. 17Because many in the assembly had not consecrated themselves, the Levites were charged with slaughtering the Passover lambs for everyone who was not clean, in order to consecrate them to the LORD.

18For a great many of the people—especially from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun—had not purified themselves, yet they ate the Passover contrary to what was written. But Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, “May the good LORD pardon everyone who sets his heart to seek God, the LORD, the God of his fathers, 19even though he is not cleansed according to the sanctuary’s purity.” 20And the LORD heard Hezekiah and healed the people.

An Unprecedented Season of Joy and Blessing

21The Israelites who were present in Jerusalem kept the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread for seven days with great joy, while the Levites and priests praised the LORD day by day with resounding instruments dedicated to the LORD. 22Hezekiah spoke encouragingly to all the Levites who had shown good understanding in the service of the LORD. They ate the festival food for seven days, offering peace offerings and giving thanks to the LORD, the God of their fathers. 23Then the whole assembly agreed to celebrate for another seven days, and they kept the feast for seven more days with joy.

24For Hezekiah king of Judah had contributed to the assembly one thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep, and the officials had contributed one thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep; and many priests consecrated themselves. 25All the assembly of Judah rejoiced, together with the priests and Levites and all the assembly that had come from Israel, as well as the sojourners who had come from the land of Israel and those who lived in Judah. 26There was great joy in Jerusalem, for nothing like this had occurred in Jerusalem since the days of Solomon son of David, king of Israel. 27Then the priests and Levites stood and blessed the people, and their voice was heard, and their prayer reached HishisHis holy dwelling in Heavenheavenheaven.
