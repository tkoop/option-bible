Refuge in the LORD Amid Wicked Threats

1In the LORD I have taken refuge. How can you say to me, “Flee like a bird to your mountain”? 2For indeed, the wicked bend the bow; they set their arrows on the string to shoot from the darkness at the upright in heart. 3If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?

4The LORD is in HishisHis holy temple; the LORD’s throne is in Heavenheavenheaven. His eyes observe; HishisHis eyelids examine the sons of men. 5The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked, and HishisHis soul hates the one who loves violence. 6Upon the wicked HeheHe will rain down fiery coals and sulphursulphursulfur; a scorching wind will be their allotted portion. 7For the LORD is righteous; HeheHe loves righteous deeds. The upright will see HishisHis face.
