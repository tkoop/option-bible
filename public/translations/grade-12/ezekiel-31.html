The Word of the LORD to Ezekiel

1In the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first day, the word of the LORD came to me, saying:

A Message for Pharaoh and His Hordes

2“Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his hordes:

“‘Whom are you like in your greatness?

Assyria as a Towering Cedar

3“‘Consider Assyria, once a cedar in Lebanon, with beautiful branches and forest shade, towering to a great height, its crown among the clouds. 4The waters nourished it; the deep made it grow tall, its rivers flowing around its base and its channels reaching all the trees of the field. 5Therefore it towered higher than all the trees of the field; its boughs multiplied, and its branches grew long as they spread because of abundant waters.

6“‘All the birds of the air nested in its boughs, and all the beasts of the field gave birth under its branches; all the great nations lived in its shade. 7It was beautiful in its greatness, in the length of its limbs, for its roots spread to abundant waters.

8“‘ThetheThe cedars in the garden of God could not rival it, nor could the cypresses match its boughs. The plane trees were as nothing compared with its branches. No tree in the garden of God could compare with it in beauty.

9“‘I made it beautiful with its many branches, the envy of all the trees of Eden in the garden of God.

The Fall of the Great Cedar

10“‘Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says:

“‘‘Because it towered high and set its crown among the clouds, and because its heart was proud of its height, 11I delivered it into the hand of the ruler of the nations, for him to deal with it according to its wickedness. I have driven it out. 12Foreigners, the most ruthless of nations, have cut it down and left it. Its branches have fallen on the mountains and in every valley; its boughs lie broken in all the ravines of the earth. All the peoples of the earth have departed from its shade and abandoned it. 13All the birds of the air nested on its fallen trunk, and all the beasts of the field lived among its branches. 14This happened so that no other tree by the waters would exalt itself in its height, nor set its crown among the clouds; no other well-watered tree would reach up in its height. For they all have been consigned to death, to the depths of the earth, among the sons of men, with those who descend to the Pit.’

15“‘This is what the Lord the LORD says:

“‘‘On the day it was brought down to Sheol, I caused mourning; I covered the deep over it; I held back its rivers, and its abundant waters were restrained. I made Lebanon mourn for it, and all the trees of the field wilted on its account. 16I made the nations quake at the sound of its fall, when I cast it down to Sheol with those who descend to the Pit. Then all the trees of Eden, the choicest and best of Lebanon, all the well-watered trees, were comforted in the earth below. 17They too descended with it to Sheol, to those slain by the sword. Its allies, those who lived in its shade among the nations, also descended into the earth below.’

Pharaoh’s Coming Humiliation

18“‘Which of the trees of Eden can be compared with you in glory and greatness? Yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the earth below. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his hordes, declares the Lord the LORD.’”
