Two Baskets of Figs

1After Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into exile—along with the officials of Judah, the craftsmen, and the metalworkers from Jerusalem—and had brought them to Babylon, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs placed before the temple of the LORD. 2One basket contained very good figs, like first-ripe figs, but the other contained very bad figs, so spoiled they could not be eaten.

3Then the LORD asked me, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” I answered, “Figs—the good ones very good, and the bad ones very bad, so spoiled they cannot be eaten.”

The LORD Interprets the Vision

4Then the word of the LORD came to me: 5“Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Like these good figs, so I will regard with favourfavourfavor the exiles of Judah, whom I have sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans. 6I will set MymyMy eyes on them for good, and I will bring them back to this land. I will build them up and not tear them down; I will plant them and not uproot them. 7I will give them a heart to know MemeMe, for I am the LORD. They will be MymyMy people, and I will be their God, for they will return to MemeMe with all their heart.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Those Who Remain

8“But like the bad figs, so spoiled they cannot be eaten,” says the LORD, “so I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and the remnant of Jerusalem—those remaining in this land and those living in Egypt. 9I will make them a horror and an offenceoffenceoffense to all the kingdoms of the earth, a reproach and a byword, a taunt and a curse in all the places to which I banish them. 10I will send against them sword, famine, and plague until they have perished from the land I gave to them and their fathers.”
