Land Still to Be Possessed

1Now Joshua was very old, and the LORD said to him, “You are very old, and much land remains to be possessed. 2This is the land that remains: all the regions of the Philistines and all the Geshurites, 3from the Shihor, which lies east of Egypt, northward to the border of Ekron—it is reckoned as Canaanite territory—the five Philistine rulers of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, together with the Avvites; 4in the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, to the border of the Amorites; 5and the land of the Gebalites and all Lebanon eastward, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath; 6all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim—all the Sidonians—I will drive out before the Israelites. Only allot it to Israel as an inheritance, as I have commanded you. 7Now therefore divide this land as an inheritance for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”

The Inheritances Given by Moses

8The Reubenites and Gadites, together with the half-tribe of Manasseh, had already received their inheritance, which Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave them east of the Jordan. 9It ran from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau of Medeba as far as Dibon; 10and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites; 11and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah; 12all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei—he was one of the last of the Rephaim—and Moses struck them down and dispossessed them. 13Yet the Israelites did not dispossess the Geshurites or the Maacathites, and Geshur and Maacah still live among Israel to this day. 14Only the tribe of Levi received no inheritance; the fire offerings of the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, as HeheHe had spoken to them.

The Allotted Territories of the Eastern Tribes

15Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Reuben according to its clans. 16Their territory ran from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau near Medeba; 17with Heshbon and all its cities on the plateau—Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 18Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath, 19Kiriathaim, Sibmah, and Zereth-shahar on the hill of the valley, 20Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth— 21all the cities of the plateau and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses defeated together with the Midianite leaders Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon who lived in the land. 22The Israelites also put Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, to the sword among those they struck down. 23The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan. This was the inheritance of the Reubenites, according to their clans, with its cities and villages.

24Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to the Gadites, according to their clans. 25Their territory was Jazer and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, which is east of Rabbah; 26from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir; 27and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon—the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon—with the Jordan as its border, eastward to the end of the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth. 28This was the inheritance of the Gadites, according to their clans, with its cities and villages.

29Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it was for the half-tribe of Manasseh according to its clans. 30Their territory ran from Mahanaim through all Bashan—all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan—and all the settlements of Jair in Bashan, sixty cities; 31and half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan. These were given to the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, to half of the descendants of Machir, according to their families. 32These are the inheritances that Moses gave in the plains of Moab, east of the Jordan, across from Jericho.

The Levites' Portion

33But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance; the LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as HeheHe had spoken to them.
