The Oracle Against Damascus and the Ruin of Its Allies

1An oracle concerning Damascus: Behold, Damascus will cease to be a city and will become a heap of ruins. 2The cities of Aroer will be deserted; they will become pastures for flocks, which will lie down without anyone to frighten them. 3The fortress will disappear from Ephraim, and the kingdom from Damascus; the remnant of Aram will be like the glory of the Israelites, declares the LORD of Hosts.

4In that day the glory of Jacob will fade, and the fat of his body will waste away. 5It will be as when a reaper gathers standing grain and harvests the heads of grain with his arm; it will be like one gleaning heads of grain in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 6Yet gleanings will remain, like an olive tree that has been beaten—two or three berries on the topmost branches, four or five on the fruitful boughs, declares the LORD, the God of Israel.

A Remnant Turns Back to the LORD

7In that day men will look to their MakermakerMaker and turn their eyes to the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 8They will not look to the altars their hands made, nor regard the Asherah poles and incense altars their fingers fashioned. 9In that day their fortified cities will be like abandoned thickets and high places forsaken because of the Israelites, and there will be desolation.

The Folly of Forgetting the Rock of Strength

10For you have forgotten the God of your salvation and failed to remember the RockrockRock of your strength. Therefore you plant your pleasant vineyards and set out imported vines. 11Though on the day you plant them, you make them grow, and in the morning you sow them, your seeds sprout, yet the harvest will be a heap of rubble in the day of disease and incurable pain.

The Doom of the Raging Nations

12Oh, the raging of many nations—they rage like the raging of the sea! Oh, the uproar of the peoples—they roar like the roar of mighty waters! 13The nations roar like the roar of great waters, but HeheHe will rebuke them, and they will flee far away, chased like chaff on the hills before the wind, like tumbleweed before a gale. 14In the evening, sudden terror! Before morning, they are gone! This is the portion of those who loot us, and the lot of those who plunder us.
