An Oracle Against Babylon

1This is the oracle concerning Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz saw.

The LORD Summons His Army

2Raise a banner on a bare hilltop; call out to them. Wave a hand, that they may enter the gates of the nobles. 3I have commanded MymyMy consecrated ones; I have also summoned MymyMy mighty men to execute MymyMy wrath—those who exult in MymyMy majesty. 4A sound of uproar on the mountains, like a great multitude; a sound of tumult among kingdoms, of nations gathered together. The LORD of Hosts is mustering the army for battle. 5They come from a distant land, from the ends of the heavens—the LORD and the weapons of HishisHis wrath—to destroy the whole EarthEarthearth.

The Day of the LORD

6Wail, for the day of the LORD is near; it will come as ruin sent by the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 7Therefore all hands will grow feeble, and every human heart will melt. 8They will be seized with terror; pangs and sorrows will grip them. They will writhe like a woman in labourlabourlabor. They will stare at one another in astonishment, and their faces will burn like flames. 9Behold, the day of the LORD is coming—cruel, with fury and fierce anger—to make the earth a desolation and to destroy its sinners from it. 10For the stars of Heavenheavenheaven and their constellations will not give their light; the sun will be darkened at its rising, and the moon will not shed its light. 11I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity. I will put an end to the arrogance of the proud and humble the haughtiness of tyrants. 12I will make mortal man scarcer than pure gold and mankind rarer than the gold of Ophir. 13Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth will be shaken from its place, at the wrath of the LORD of Hosts and in the day of HishisHis fierce anger.

The Fall of Babylon

14Like a hunted gazelle, or like sheep with no one to gather them, each will turn to his own people, and each will flee to his own land. 15Whoever is found will be pierced through, and whoever is captured will fall by the sword. 16Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses will be plundered, and their wives will be violated. 17Behold, I am stirring up the Medes against them, who have no regard for silver and no delight in gold. 18Their bows will dash the young men to pieces; they will have no mercy on the fruit of the womb, and their eyes will not pity children. 19And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty and pride of the Chaldeans, will be like Sodom and Gomorrah when God overthrew them. 20It will never be inhabited or lived in from generation to generation; no Arab will pitch his tent there, and no shepherds will make their flocks lie down there. 21But desert creatures will lie there, and their houses will be full of howling creatures; ostriches will dwell there, and wild goats will leap there. 22Hyenas will howl in their citadels, and jackals in their luxurious palaces. Her time is near, and her days will not be prolonged.
