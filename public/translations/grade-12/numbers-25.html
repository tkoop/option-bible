Israel's Apostasy at Shittim

1While Israel was encamped at Shittim, the people began to engage in promiscuity with the Moabite women. 2These women invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to those gods. 3Thus Israel yoked itself to Baal of Peor, and the LORD's anger burned against Israel.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment and the Plague

4The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and hang them in the sun before the LORD, so that the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away from Israel."

5So Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you must kill those men of his who have yoked themselves to Baal of Peor."

6Then an Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his relatives in the sight of Moses and of the whole congregation of the Israelites while they were weeping at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

7When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this, he rose from the midst of the congregation, took a spear in his hand, 8and followed the Israelite man into the tent, where he pierced both of them—the Israelite man and the woman—through her belly. Thus the plague was stopped among the Israelites. 9Those who died by the plague numbered twenty-four thousand.

Command to Strike Midian

16The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 17"Harass the Midianites and strike them, 18for they have harassed you with their schemes, by which they deceived you in the matter of Peor and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a Midianite leader, their sister who was slain on the day of the plague because of Peor."
