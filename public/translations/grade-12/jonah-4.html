Jonah's Anger at the LORD's Mercy

1It displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was furious.

2He prayed to the LORD and said, “Please, the LORD, was this not what I said while I was still in my own country? That is why I fled to Tarshish at the outset, for I knew that YouyouYou are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love, and that YouyouYou relent from bringing disaster. 3And now, the LORD, please take my life from me, for death is better to me than life.”

4The LORD replied, “Do you have a good reason to be angry?”

Jonah's Shelter and the Plant

5Jonah left the city and sat down east of it. There he built a shelter and sat in its shade, waiting to see what would become of the city.

6Then the LORD God appointed a plant, and it grew up over Jonah to provide shade for his head and to relieve him from his discomfort. Jonah rejoiced greatly over the plant.

7But at dawn the next day, God appointed a worm, which attacked the plant so that it withered.

8When the sun rose, God appointed a scorching east wind. The sun beat down on Jonah's head until he grew faint, and he asked to die, saying, “It is better for me to die than to live.”

9The LORD said to Jonah, “Is it right for you to be angry about the plant?”

Jonah replied, “I have a good reason to be angry, even to death.”

The LORD's Compassion for Nineveh

10The LORD said, “You had compassion on the plant, which you did not labourlabourlabor over or cultivate. It sprang up in a night and perished in a night. 11Should I not then have compassion on Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand people who cannot distinguish right from wrong, along with many animals?”
