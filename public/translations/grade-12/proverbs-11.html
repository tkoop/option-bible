Integrity, Wealth, and Divine Justice

1Dishonest scales are an abomination to the the LORD, whereas an accurate weight is HishisHis delight. 2Pride brings disgrace, but wisdom dwells with the humble. 3The integrity of the upright guides them, whereas the perversity of the treacherous destroys them. 4Wealth is useless in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from death.

5The righteousness of the blameless makes his path straight, but the wicked falls through his own wickedness. 6The righteousness of the upright delivers them, but the treacherous are ensnared by their own desires. 7When a wicked person dies, his hope perishes, and all he expected from his power comes to nothing. 8The righteous is rescued from trouble, and the wicked takes his place.

Speech, Counsel, and the Common Good

9With his mouth the godless destroys his neighbourneighbourneighbor, but through knowledge the righteous are delivered. 10When the righteous prosper, the city rejoices; when the wicked perish, there are shouts of joy. 11By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but by the mouth of the wicked it is overthrown. 12Whoever belittles his neighbourneighbourneighbor lacks sense, but a person of understanding remains silent.

13A gossip reveals secrets, but one who is faithful in spirit conceals a matter. 14Where there is no guidance, a people falls, but victory comes through many counselors.

Generosity, Security, and Retribution

15He who guarantees a stranger's debt will surely suffer, but whoever refuses to pledge security is safe. 16A gracious woman gains honourhonourhonor, but ruthless men gain only wealth. 17A kind person benefits himself, but a cruel person harms his own body. 18The wicked earns deceptive wages, but one who sows righteousness receives a true reward.

19Righteousness leads to life, but pursuing evil leads to death. 20Those with crooked hearts are an abomination to the the LORD, but those whose ways are blameless are HishisHis delight. 21Be assured that an evil person will not go unpunished, but the offspring of the righteous will be delivered.

Desire, Prosperity, and Final Recompense

22Like a gold ring in a pig's snout is a beautiful woman who lacks discretion. 23The desire of the righteous leads only to good, but the hope of the wicked brings wrath. 24One person gives freely, yet gains even more; another withholds unduly, but comes to poverty. 25A generous person will prosper, and whoever refreshes others will himself be refreshed.

26People curse the one who hoards grain, but blessing crowns the one who sells it. 27Whoever seeks good finds favourfavourfavor, but evil comes to the one who searches for it. 28Those who trust in their riches will fall, but the righteous will flourish like a green leaf. 29Whoever troubles his own household will inherit nothing but emptiness, and the fool will serve the wise of heart.

30The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and whoever wins souls is wise. 31If the righteous are repaid on EarthEarthearth, how much more the wicked and the sinner!
