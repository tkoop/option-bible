Elisha Promises Relief from the Famine

1Elisha said, "Hear the word of the LORD. This is what the LORD says: 'Tomorrow at this time, at the gate of Samaria, a seah of fine flour will sell for a shekel, and two seahs of barley for a shekel.'"

The Lepers Discover the Abandoned Camp

3Four leprous men were at the entrance of the gate. They said to one another, "Why are we sitting here until we die?"

4One of them said, "If we go into the city, the famine is there and we will die; if we stay here, we die as well. Come, let us surrender to the Aramean camp. If they spare us, we live; if they kill us, we die."

5At twilight they set out for the Aramean camp. When they reached its edge, there was no one there.

6For the Lord had caused the Aramean army to hear the sound of chariots and horses—a great army. They said to one another, "Look, the king of Israel has hired the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to come against us."

7They rose and fled at twilight, abandoning their tents, horses, and donkeys. They left the camp exactly as it was and ran for their lives.

8When those lepers reached the edge of the camp, they entered a tent, ate and drank, and carried off silver, gold, and clothing, which they hid. Then they went to another tent, took more, and hid that too.

9They said to one another, "We are not doing right. Today is a day of good news, and we are keeping silent. If we wait until morning, guilt will catch up with us. Come now, let us go and report to the king."

10They went and called to the gatekeepers, saying, "We went to the Aramean camp, and there is not a soul there—only horses and donkeys tied up, and tents just as they were."

11The gatekeepers shouted the news and relayed it to the royal household.

The King Tests the Report

12The king got up at night and said to his servants, "I will tell you what the Arameans are doing to us. They know we are starving, so they have withdrawn from the camp to hide in the countryside, thinking that when the Israelites leave the city, they can capture them alive and take the city."

13One of his servants replied, "Let a few men take five of the horses still left in the city. They are in the same condition as the rest of Israel—indeed, as those who have already perished. Let us send them out and see what happens."

14So the king sent two chariots with horses after the Aramean army, ordering, "Go and investigate."

15They followed the trail as far as the Jordan and found the whole road strewn with clothing and equipment the Arameans had discarded in their panic. The messengers returned and reported to the king.

The Prophecy Is Fulfilled

16The people went out and plundered the camp, and prices fell exactly as the the LORD had promised: a seah of fine flour for a shekel and two seahs of barley for a shekel.

17The king had put the officer on whose arm he leaned in charge of the gate, and the people trampled him there as they rushed through, and he died, just as the man of God had foretold when the king came to him.

18For the man of God had told the king, "Tomorrow at this time, at the gate of Samaria, two seahs of barley will sell for a shekel, and a seah of fine flour for a shekel."

19The officer had replied, "Look—even if the LORD opened windows in Heavenheavenheaven, could this happen?"

And Elisha had answered, "YouyouYou will see it with your own eyes, but you will not eat any of it."

20And so it happened: the people trampled him at the gate, and he died.
