A Lament for Israel’s Princes

1And you, take up a lamentation for the princes of Israel. 2And say,

“What was your mother? A lioness among lions she crouched; among young lions she reared her cubs. 3She brought up one of her cubs, and he became a young lion; he learned to tear his prey and devoured men. 4The nations heard of him, so they caught him in their pit and brought him with hooks to the land of Egypt.

5“When she saw that her hope was lost, she took another of her cubs and made him a young lion. 6He prowled among the lions; he became a young lion, learned to tear his prey, and devoured men. 7He violated their widows, laid waste their cities, and the land with all that filled it was appalled at the sound of his roaring. 8Then nations from the surrounding provinces set themselves against him; they spread their net over him, and he was caught in their pit. 9They put him in a cage with hooks and brought him to the king of Babylon; they brought him into fortresses, so that his voice would no longer be heard on the mountains of Israel.

The Vine Uprooted

10“Your mother was like a vine in your lineage, planted by the waters; she was fruitful and full of branches because of abundant waters. 11She had strong branches, fit for the scepters of rulers; her height was exalted among the clouds, and she was conspicuous in her height and in the multitude of her branches. 12But she was uprooted in fury and thrown to the ground; the east wind dried up her fruit; her strong branches were broken and withered; fire consumed them. 13Now she is planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty land. 14Fire has gone out from a rod of her branches and consumed her fruit, so that she has no strong branch—a sceptresceptrescepter to rule. This is a lamentation, and it has become a lamentation.”
