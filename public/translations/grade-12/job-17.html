Job's Broken Spirit and the Mockers Around Him

1My spirit is shattered, my days are extinguished, and the grave stands ready for me. 2Surely mockers hem me in, and my eyes must dwell on their insolence.

Job Seeks a Guarantor before God

3Please stand as my guarantor with YourselfyourselfYourself; who else will pledge himself to me? 4For YouyouYou have hidden their hearts from discernment; therefore YouyouYou will not exalt them. 5Whoever informs against friends to claim a share of the spoils will see his children's eyes grow dim. 6He has made me a byword among the peoples; I am a man in whose face they spit. 7My eyes have grown dim with grief, and my whole body is but a shadow.

The Upright Are Appalled and Job's Friends Have No Wisdom

8The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent stir themselves up against the godless. 9Yet the righteous will hold fast to his way, and he who has clean hands will grow stronger.

10But as for all of you, come back now; I will not find a wise man among you. 11My days have passed; my plans are broken; the desires of my heart are shattered. 12They turn night into day and say, "LightlightLight is near in the face of darkness."

Job's Hope in the Grave

13If I hope for Sheol as my house, if I spread my bed in darkness, 14I have called to corruption, "YouyouYou are my father," and to the worm, "YouyouYou are my mother and my sister." 15Where then is my hope? Who can see any hope for me? 16Will it go down to the gates of Sheol? Will we descend together into the dust?
