A Joyful Call to Worship

1Raise a joyful shout to the the LORD, all the earth. 2Serve the the LORD with gladness; come before HimhimHim with joyful singing.

3Know that the the LORD—HeheHe is God. He made us, and we did not make ourselves; we are HishisHis people and the sheep of HishisHis pasture.

4Enter HishisHis gates with thanksgiving and HishisHis courts with praise; give thanks to HimhimHim and bless HishisHis name. 5For the the LORD is good; HishisHis steadfast love endures forever, and HishisHis faithfulness extends to every generation.
