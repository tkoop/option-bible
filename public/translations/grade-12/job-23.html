Job Longs to Present His Case Before God

1Then Job answered and said: 2“Even today my complaint is bitter; my hand weighs heavily because of my groaning. 3Oh, that I knew where I might find HimhimHim, that I might come to HishisHis seat! 4I would lay my case before HimhimHim and fill my mouth with arguments. 5I would know what words HeheHe would answer me with, and understand what HeheHe would say to me. 6Would HeheHe contend with me in great power? No—HeheHe would pay attention to me. 7There an upright man could reason with HimhimHim, and I would be delivered forever from my JudgejudgeJudge.”

Job Affirms His Integrity

8“Behold, I go forward, but HeheHe is not there; I go backward, but I do not perceive HimhimHim. 9When HeheHe works on the left, I cannot grasp HimhimHim; HeheHe turns to the right, but I do not see HimhimHim. 10But HeheHe knows the way that I take; when HeheHe has tried me, I shall come out as gold. 11My foot has held fast to HishisHis steps; I have kept HishisHis way and not turned aside. 12I have not departed from the commandment of HishisHis lips; I have treasured the words of HishisHis mouth more than my daily bread.”

God’s Unchangeable Purpose Terrifies Job

13“But HeheHe is unchangeable, and who can turn HimhimHim? What HishisHis soul desires, that HeheHe does. 14For HeheHe will complete what HeheHe has appointed for me, and many such things are with HimhimHim. 15Therefore I am terrified at HishisHis presence; when I consider, I am in dread of HimhimHim. 16God has made my heart faint, and the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty has terrified me, 17for I am not silenced by the darkness, nor by the thick darkness that covers my face.”
