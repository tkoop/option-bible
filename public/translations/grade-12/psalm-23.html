The LORD Is My Shepherd

1The LORD is my shepherd; I lack nothing. 2He lets me rest in verdant meadows and leads me beside tranquil waters. 3He revives my spirit and guides me along righteous paths for the sake of HishisHis name.

4Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I fear no evil, for YouyouYou are with me; YouryourYour rod and YouryourYour staff comfort me. 5You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; YouyouYou anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. 6Surely goodness and faithful love will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the the LORD forever.
