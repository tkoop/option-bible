David's Praise to the LORD, His Rock and Deliverer

1Blessed be the LORD, my rock, who trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle. 2He is my loving devotion and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer, my shield, in whom I take refuge, who subdues my people under me.

3O the LORD, what is man that YouyouYou regard him, or the son of man that YouyouYou think of him? 4Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow.

A Prayer for the LORD to Descend and Scatter the Enemy

5O the LORD, part YouryourYour heavens and come down; touch the mountains so that they smoke. 6Flash forth lightning and scatter them; shoot YouryourYour arrows and rout them. 7Stretch out YouryourYour hand from on high; rescue me and deliver me from the many waters, from the hand of foreigners 8whose mouths speak deceit and whose right hands are full of lies.

9I will sing a new song to YouyouYou, O God; on a ten-stringed harp I will make music to YouyouYou. 10To the OneoneOne who gives salvation to kings, who rescues HishisHis servant David from the deadly sword, 11rescue me and deliver me from the hands of foreigners, whose mouths speak deceit and whose right hands are full of lies.

A Vision of Prosperity for God's People

12Then our sons will be like well-nurtured plants, and our daughters like pillars carved to adorn a temple. 13Our barns will be full, supplying every kind of produce; our flocks will increase by thousands, by tens of thousands in our fields. 14Our oxen will be well laden. There will be no breach in the wall, no going into captivity, and no cry of distress in our streets. 15Blessed are the people whose God is the LORD.
