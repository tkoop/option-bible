The Distinct Privilege of the Jewish People

1What advantage, then, accrues to the Jew, or what benefit does circumcision confer? 2Much in every respect! Chiefly, the Jews were entrusted with the oracles of God. 3What if some proved unfaithful? Does their unfaithfulness nullify God's faithfulness? 4By no means! Let God prove true, though every human being proves false, as it is written: “So that YouyouYou may be justified in YouryourYour words and prevail when YouyouYou are judged.”

5But if our unrighteousness commends God's righteousness, what shall we say? Is God unjust in inflicting wrath? (I speak in human terms.) 6Certainly not! For then how could God judge the world? 7For if, through my falsehood, God's truth abounds to HishisHis glory, why am I still judged as a sinner? 8And why not say—as some slanderously allege that we say—“Let us do evil that good may come”? Their condemnation is deserved.

The Universal Dominion of Sin

9What then? Are we any better? Not at all! For we have already charged that both Jews and Greeks are all under the dominion of sin. 10As it is written: “There is no one righteous, not even one; 11there is no one who understands; there is no one who seeks God. 12All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; there is no one who does good, not even one.” 13“Their throat is an open grave; with their tongues they practice deceit. The venom of asps is under their lips.” 14“Their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.” 15“Their feet are swift to shed blood; 16ruin and misery lie in their paths, 17and the way of peace they have not known.” 18“There is no fear of God before their eyes.”

19Now we know that whatever the LawlawLaw says, it speaks to those under the LawlawLaw, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God. 20Therefore no human being will be justified before HimhimHim by works of the LawlawLaw, for through the LawlawLaw comes knowledge of sin.

Righteousness Revealed Through Faith in Christ

21But now, apart from the LawlawLaw, the righteousness of God has been manifested, being attested by the LawlawLaw and the ProphetsprophetsProphets. 22Even the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ, to all who believe. For there is no distinction: 23for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, 24and are justified freely by HishisHis grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus, 25whom God presented as an atoning sacrifice through faith in HishisHis blood, to demonstrate HishisHis righteousness, because in HishisHis forbearance HeheHe had left the sins previously committed unpunished, 26to demonstrate HishisHis righteousness at the present time, so that HeheHe might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus.

27Where then is boasting? It is excluded. By what law? By the law of works? No, but by the law of faith. 28For we hold that a person is justified by faith apart from works of the LawlawLaw. 29Or is God the God of Jews only? Is HeheHe not also the God of Gentiles? Yes, of Gentiles also, 30since God is one, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through faith. 31Do we then nullify the LawlawLaw through faith? By no means! On the contrary, we uphold the LawlawLaw.
