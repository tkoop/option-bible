The Angel of the LORD at Bochim

1The angel of the LORD ascended from Gilgal to Bochim and declared, “I brought you out of Egypt and led you into the land I swore to give your ancestors. I said, ‘I will never break MymyMy covenant with you, 2and you must not make a covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you must dismantle their altars.’ But you have not obeyed MemeMe. What is this you have done? 3Therefore I now say to you, I will not drive them out before you; they will be a constant source of distress to you, and their gods will ensnare you.”

4When the angel of the LORD had spoken these words to all the Israelites, the people raised their voices and wept. 5So they called that place Bochim, and there they sacrificed to the LORD.

Israel Serves the LORD Under Joshua

6After Joshua had dismissed the Israelites, they went to take possession of the land, each to his own inheritance. 7The people served the LORD throughout the days of Joshua and throughout the days of the elders who outlived him, those who had seen all the great works the LORD had done for Israel.

8Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at the age of one hundred ten. 9They buried him in the territory of his inheritance at Timnath-heres in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash. 10After that entire generation had passed away, another generation arose who did not know the LORD or the works HeheHe had done for Israel.

The Cycle of Rebellion and Deliverance

11Then the Israelites did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and served the Baals. 12They abandoned the LORD, the God of their ancestors, who had brought them out of Egypt. They followed and worshiped the gods of the peoples around them, and they provoked the LORD to anger. 13They abandoned the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.

14Then the anger of the LORD flared against Israel, and HeheHe handed them over to raiders who plundered them. He delivered them to their enemies all around, and they could no longer withstand their foes. 15Wherever Israel went, the power of the LORD was against them to bring disaster, just as the LORD had said and as HeheHe had sworn to them. So they were in great distress.

16Then the LORD appointed judges, who rescued them from those who plundered them. 17Yet they would not listen to their judges, but prostituted themselves to other gods and worshiped them. They quickly turned from the way their ancestors had lived in obedience to the commands of the LORD; they did not do as their ancestors had done.

18Whenever the LORD appointed a judge for them, HeheHe was with the judge and rescued them from their enemies as long as the judge lived, for the LORD was moved to pity by their groaning under those who oppressed and afflicted them. 19But when the judge died, they relapsed and behaved even more corruptly than their ancestors, following other gods to serve and worship them. They refused to give up their evil practices and stubborn ways.

20So the anger of the LORD flared against Israel, and HeheHe said, “Because this nation has transgressed the covenant I commanded their ancestors and has not obeyed MymyMy commands, 21I will no longer drive out before them any of the nations Joshua left when he died. 22I will use them to test Israel, to see whether they will keep the way of the LORD and walk in it as their ancestors did.”

23So the LORD left those nations alone, not driving them out at once, and did not deliver them to Joshua.
