The Outer Sanctuary and Its Measured Dimensions

1Then he brought me to the outer sanctuary and measured the jambs: six cubits wide on one side and six cubits wide on the other. 2The entrance itself was ten cubits wide, with sidewalls five cubits thick on either side. He measured the sanctuary and found it forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. 3He then entered the inner room and measured the jambs of its entrance at two cubits; the entrance was six cubits wide, and its sidewalls were seven cubits thick. 4He measured the room at twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide, corresponding to the outer sanctuary, and he said to me, "This is the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace."

The Side Chambers, Their Foundations, and Surrounding Spaces

5He measured the wall of the temple at six cubits thick, and the side chambers at four cubits wide, all around the temple. 6The side chambers rose in three stories, thirty chambers on each level. Ledges encircled the temple wall to support the side chambers, so that they did not rest their weight on the temple wall itself. 7The chambers grew wider with each ascending story, because the wall stepped back as it rose; consequently, the building broadened from the lowest storeylowest storeylowest story to the highest through the middle. 8I observed that the temple stood on a raised platform all around, which formed the foundation for the side chambers; it measured a full six cubits.

9The outer wall of the side chambers was five cubits thick. 10Between these chambers and the temple lay an open space twenty cubits wide on every side. 11The side chambers opened onto this open area through one doorway, with one doorway facing north and another facing south; the open area itself was five cubits wide all around.

The Separate Building and the Temple's Overall Dimensions

12The building facing the separate courtyard on the west was seventy cubits wide; its wall was five cubits thick all around, and its length was ninety cubits. 13He measured the temple at one hundred cubits long; the separate courtyard, the building, and their walls were also one hundred cubits long. 14The width of the east front of the temple and of the separate courtyard was one hundred cubits. 15He also measured the building that faced the rear of the separate courtyard, together with its galleries on either side, at one hundred cubits. The inner sanctuary and the porticoes of the court were included in these measurements.

Ornamental Carvings, Doorways, and the Altar

16The thresholds, the beveled windows, and the galleries around all three sections faced the threshold; each was panelledpanelledpaneled with wood all around, from the floor up to the windows, and the windows were covered. 17From above the doorway to the inner house and to the outside, and on every wall all around, inside and outside, the work was executed according to measure. 18Cherubim and palm trees were carved, with a palm tree between each cherub; each cherub had two faces. 19One face was human, turned toward the palm tree on one side, and the other was that of a young lion, turned toward the palm tree on the opposite side; this pattern extended around the entire building. 20From the floor to above the doorway, cherubim and palm trees were carved on the wall of the sanctuary.
