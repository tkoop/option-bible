Observing Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

1Observe the month of Aviv and celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, because in the month of Aviv the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night. 2You shall sacrifice the Passover to the LORD your God from the flock and the herd at the place the LORD chooses to establish HishisHis name. 3You must not eat leavened bread with it; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, the bread of hardship, because you left Egypt in haste, so that you may remember for the rest of your life the day you departed from Egypt.

4No leaven shall be seen anywhere within your territory for seven days, and none of the meat you sacrifice on the evening of the first day shall remain until morning. 5You may not sacrifice the Passover in just any town the LORD your God gives you; 6rather, you shall sacrifice it at the place the LORD your God chooses for HishisHis name to dwell, in the evening at sunset, at the appointed time of your departure from Egypt.

7You shall cook it and eat it at the place the LORD your God chooses, and in the morning you may turn and return to your tents. 8For six days you shall eat unleavened bread, but on the seventh day there shall be a sacred assembly to the LORD your God, and you shall do no work.

Celebrating the Feast of Weeks

9You shall count seven weeks for yourself; begin counting the seven weeks from the time you first begin harvesting the standing grain. 10Then you shall celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of WeeksweeksWeeks to the LORD your God with a freewill offering from your hand, in proportion to the blessing the LORD your God has given you.

11You shall rejoice before the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite within your towns, and the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow among you. 12Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and carefully keep and obey these statutes.

Celebrating the Feast of Tabernacles

13You shall celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Tabernacles for seven days when you gather in the produce from your threshing floor and your winepress. 14You shall rejoice in your feast—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow within your towns. 15For seven days you shall celebrate the feast to the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses, because the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in every work of your hands, and you shall be altogether joyful.

They shall not appear before the LORD without an offering, 17but each shall give as he is able, according to the blessing the LORD your God has given him.

Appointing Judges, Upholding Justice, and Rejecting Idolatry

18You shall appoint judges and officers for your tribes in all the towns the LORD your God gives you, and they shall judge the people with righteousness. 19You shall not pervert justice, show partiality, or accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of the righteous. 20You must pursue justice, and only justice, so that you may live and possess the land the LORD your God is giving you.

21You shall not plant for yourself an Asherah of any kind of tree beside the altar of the LORD your God that you build, 22and you shall not set up for yourself a sacred pillar, which the LORD your God hates.
