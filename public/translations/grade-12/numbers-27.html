The Daughters of Zelophehad Petition for an Inheritance

1The daughters of Zelophehad—son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh—belonged to the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. These were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. 2They approached the entrance of the tent of meeting and stood before Moses, before Eleazar the priest, before the leaders, and before the whole congregation, saying, 3“Our father died in the wilderness. He was not among the company of those who banded together against the LORD in Korah’s rebellion; rather, he died for his own sin, and he had no sons. 4Why should our father’s name disappear from his clan simply because he had no son? Give us a possession among our father’s brothers.”

The LORD Establishes Inheritance Law

5Moses brought their case before the LORD. 6The LORD said to Moses, “The daughters of Zelophehad speak rightly. 7You must give them a possession of inheritance among their father’s brothers, and you shall transfer their father’s inheritance to them. 8And you shall say to the children of Israel, ‘If a man dies and has no son, then you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter.’ 9If he has no daughter, then you shall give his inheritance to his brothers. 10If he has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his father’s brothers. 11If his father has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his nearest kinsman from his clan, who shall possess it. This shall be a statute of justice for the Israelites, as the LORD commanded Moses.”

The LORD Foretells Moses’ Death
