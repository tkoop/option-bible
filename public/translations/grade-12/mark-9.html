The Transfiguration

9As they were coming down the mountain, HeheHe ordered them to tell no one what they had seen until the SonsonSon of ManmanMan rose from the dead. 10They kept the word to themselves, discussing what “rising from the dead” meant. 11They asked HimhimHim, “Why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”

12He said to them, “Elijah does indeed come first and restore all things. Yet how is it written of the SonsonSon of ManmanMan that HeheHe must suffer many things and be treated with contempt? 13But I tell you that Elijah has come, and they did to him whatever they pleased, as it is written of him.”

A Boy with an Unclean Spirit

14When they came to the disciples, they saw a large crowd around them and scribes arguing with them. 15Immediately the whole crowd, on seeing HimhimHim, was amazed and ran to greet HimhimHim. 16He asked them, “What are you arguing about with them?”

17One of the crowd answered, “Teacher, I brought my son to YouyouYou; he has a spirit that makes him mute. 18Wherever it seizes him, it throws him down; he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and his body stiffens. I told your disciples to cast it out, but they could not.”

19He answered them, “O faithless generation, how long must I be with you? How long must I bear with you? Bring him to MemeMe.” 20They brought him to HimhimHim. When the spirit saw HimhimHim, it immediately threw the boy into convulsions; he fell to the ground and rolled about, foaming at the mouth. 21Jesus asked his father, “How long has this been happening to him?” He said, “From childhood. 22It has often thrown him into fire and water to destroy him. But if you can do anything, have compassion on us and help us.”

23Jesus said to him, “If you can? All things are possible for the one who believes.” 24Immediately the child’s father cried out, “I believe; help my unbelief!” 25When Jesus saw that a crowd was running together, HeheHe rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, “You deaf and mute spirit, I command you, come out of him, and never enter him again.” 26It cried out and convulsed him violently, then came out. The boy became like a corpse, so that many said, “He is dead.” 27But Jesus took him by the hand and lifted him up, and he stood.

28When Jesus had gone into the house, HishisHis disciples asked HimhimHim privately, “Why could we not cast it out?” 29He said to them, “This kind can come out only through prayer.”

Jesus Foretells His Death

30They went out from there and passed through Galilee, and HeheHe did not want anyone to know, 31for HeheHe was teaching HishisHis disciples and saying to them, “The SonsonSon of ManmanMan is being delivered into the hands of men, and they will kill HimhimHim; and when HeheHe has been killed, HeheHe will rise after three days.” 32But they did not understand the saying and were afraid to ask HimhimHim.

Greatness, Inclusion, and Warnings

33They came to Capernaum. When HeheHe was in the house, HeheHe asked them, “What were you discussing on the way?” 34But they were silent, for on the way they had argued with one another about who was the greatest. 35Sitting down, HeheHe called the TwelvetwelveTwelve and said to them, “If anyone wants to be first, he must be last of all and servant of all.” 36He took a child and set him in their midst; then, taking him in HishisHis arms, HeheHe said to them, 37“Whoever receives one of these little children in MymyMy name receives MemeMe, and whoever receives MemeMe receives not MemeMe but the OneoneOne who sent MemeMe.”

38John said to HimhimHim, “Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, and we tried to stop him because he was not following us.” 39Jesus said, “Do not stop him, for no one who performs a mighty work in MymyMy name will be able soon afterward to speak evil of MemeMe. 40For whoever is not against us is for us. 41For whoever gives you a cup of water to drink because you belong to Christ, truly I say to you, he will certainly not lose his reward.”

42“And whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in MemeMe to stumble, it would be better for him if a great millstone were hung around his neck and he were thrown into the sea. 43If your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life maimed than, with two hands, to go into Gehenna, into the unquenchable fire. 45If your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life lame than, with two feet, to be thrown into Gehenna. 47If your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out. It is better for you to enter the kingdom of God with one eye than, with two eyes, to be thrown into Gehenna, 48where their worm does not die and the fire is not quenched. 49For everyone will be salted with fire. 50Salt is good, but if salt has lost its saltiness, how will you season it? Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another.”
