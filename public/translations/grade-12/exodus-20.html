The People's Fear at Sinai

18Now when all the people saw the thunder and the flashes of lightning and the sound of the trumpet and the mountain smoking, the people were afraid and trembled, and they stood at a distance. 19And they said to Moses, “You speak to us, and we will listen; but do not let God speak to us, lest we die.”

20And Moses said to the people, “Do not be afraid, for God has come to test you, that the fear of HimhimHim may be before you, that you may not sin.”

21The people stood at a distance, while Moses drew near to the thick darkness where God was.
