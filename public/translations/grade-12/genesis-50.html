Joseph Seeks Pharaoh's Permission to Bury Jacob

1Joseph fell upon his father's face, wept over him, and kissed him. 2Then Joseph commanded his servants, the physicians, to embalm his father Israel, and the physicians embalmed him. 3Forty days were required for this, for that is the customary period of embalming; and the Egyptians wept for him seventy days.

4When the days of weeping had passed, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh's household, saying, "If I have found favourfavourfavor with you, please tell Pharaoh, saying, 5'My father made me swear, saying, "I am about to die; bury me in my grave that I dug for myself in the land of Canaan." Now let me go up and bury my father; then I will return.'"

6Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, as he made you swear."

Jacob's Funeral Procession and Burial

7Joseph went up to bury his father, and with him went all Pharaoh's servants, the elders of his household, and all the elders of Egypt, 8together with all Joseph's household, his brothers, and his father's household; only their little ones, flocks, and herds were left in the land of Goshen. 9Chariots and horsemen also went up with him, so the company was very great.

10They came to the threshing floor of Atad beyond the Jordan, and there they lamented with a very great and grievous lamentation; Joseph observed mourning for his father seven days. 11When the Canaanites who inhabited the land saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad, they said, "This is a grievous mourning for the Egyptians." Therefore its name was called Abel-mizraim, beyond the Jordan.

12His sons did for him as he had commanded them. 13His sons carried him to the land of Canaan and buried him in the cave of Machpelah—the field that Abraham had purchased from Ephron the Hittite as a burial property—before Mamre. 14After burying his father, Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brothers and all who had gone up with him.

Joseph Reassures His Brothers

15When Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, "What if Joseph bears a grudge against us and repays us for all the evil we did to him?"

16So they sent a message to Joseph, saying, "YouryourYour father commanded before his death, saying, 17'Thus you shall say to Joseph: Please forgive the transgression and sin of your brothers, for they did evil to you. Now please forgive the transgression of the servants of the God of your father.'" Joseph wept when they spoke to him.

18His brothers also came and fell before him, saying, "We are your servants."

19Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid. Am I in the place of God? 20You planned evil against me, but God planned it for good, to bring about what is being done this day: to save many people alive. 21Now do not be afraid; I will provide for you and your little ones." He comforted them and spoke kindly to them.

Joseph's Death and Final Promise

22Joseph lived in Egypt, he and his father's household; Joseph lived one hundred and ten years. 23Joseph saw the children of Ephraim to the third generation; the children of Machir, son of Manasseh, were also born on Joseph's knees.

24Joseph said to his brothers, "I am dying, but God will surely attend to you and bring you up from this land to the land HeheHe swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob."

25Joseph made the sons of Israel swear, saying, "God will surely attend to you, and you shall carry up my bones from here." 26Joseph died at the age of one hundred and ten years; they embalmed him and placed him in a coffin in Egypt.
