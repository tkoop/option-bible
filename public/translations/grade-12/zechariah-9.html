An Oracle Against the Nations

1An oracle: The word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach and will rest upon Damascus—for the eyes of all people and all the tribes of Israel are upon the LORD— 2and upon Hamath, which borders it, and upon Tyre and Sidon, though they are exceedingly wise. 3Tyre has built herself a fortress; she has heaped up silver like dust and gold like the mud of the streets. 4Behold, the Lord will impoverish her and cast her wealth into the sea, and she will be consumed by fire.

5Ashkelon will see and be afraid; Gaza will writhe in agony, and Ekron as well, for her hope will wither. There will be no king in Gaza, and Ashkelon will be uninhabited. 6A mixed people will dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines. 7I will remove the blood from their mouths and the abominations from between their teeth. Then they too will become a remnant for our God; they will become like a clan in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites. 8But I will encamp around MymyMy house as a garrison against marching armies, so that no oppressor will overrun them again, for now I have seen with MymyMy own eyes.

The Coming King

9Rejoice greatly, DaughterdaughterDaughter of Zion! Shout in triumph, DaughterdaughterDaughter of Jerusalem! See, your KingkingKing is coming to you; HeheHe is righteous and victorious, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey. 10I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war will be broken. Then HeheHe will proclaim peace to the nations, and HishisHis dominion will extend from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth.

The LORD Restores His People

11As for you, because of the blood of MymyMy covenant, I will release your prisoners from the waterless pit. 12Return to your stronghold, O prisoners of hope; even today I declare that I will restore to you double. 13For I will bend Judah as MymyMy bow and fit Ephraim as MymyMy arrow. I will rouse your sons, O Zion, against your sons, O Greece, and I will make you like the sword of a mighty man.

14Then the LORD will appear over them, and HishisHis arrow will flash like lightning. The Lord GOD will sound the trumpet and advance in the whirlwinds of the south. 15The LORD of Hosts will shield them; they will devour and trample the sling stones. They will drink and roar as with wine; they will be filled like bowls, like the corners of the altar. 16On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of HishisHis people; for like jewels in a crown they will sparkle over HishisHis land. 17How lovely and beautiful they will be! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.
