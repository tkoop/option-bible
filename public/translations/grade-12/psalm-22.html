A Psalm of David: From Anguish to Universal Praise

1My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? Why are you so far from saving me, and so far from heeding my anguished cries? 2I cry out to you by day, yet you do not answer; I call by night, but find no rest. 3Yet you are enthroned as the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne, the praise of Israel. 4In you our ancestors trusted; they trusted, and you delivered them. 5They cried to you and were rescued; they trusted in you and were never put to shame.

6But I am a worm, and not a man—scorned by everyone, despised by the people. 7All who see me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads.

8"He trusts in the the LORD," they say. "Let the the LORD rescue him. Let him deliver him, since he delights in him."

9Yet you brought me forth from the womb; you made me trust in you, even at my mother's breast. 10From birth I was cast upon you; from my mother's womb you have been my God. 11Do not be far from me, for trouble is near and no helper is at hand.

Surrounded by Fierce and Mocking Enemies

12Many bulls surround me; the strong bulls of Bashan encircle me. 13They open their mouths against me like roaring lions that tear their prey. 14I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint. My heart has turned to wax; it melts within me. 15My strength is dried up like a shard of pottery, and my tongue cleaves to the roof of my mouth; you lay me in the dust of death.

16Dogs surround me; a band of evildoers encircles me. They have pierced my hands and my feet. 17I can count all my bones; people stare and gloat over me. 18They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.

19But you, the the LORD, do not be far from me. You are my strength; come quickly to help me. 20Deliver me from the sword, my precious life from the power of the dogs. 21Rescue me from the mouth of the lions; save me from the horns of the wild oxen. You have answered me.

A Vow of Praise and the Gathering of the Nations

26The poor will eat and be satisfied; those who seek the the LORD will praise HimhimHim—may your hearts live forever! 27All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the the LORD, and all the families of the nations will bow down before you, 28for dominion belongs to the the LORD and HeheHe rules over the nations. 29All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who go down to the dust will kneel before HimhimHim—those who cannot keep themselves alive. 30Posterity will serve HimhimHim; future generations will be told about the Lord. 31They will proclaim HishisHis righteousness, declaring to a people yet unborn: He has done it!
