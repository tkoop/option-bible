Isaac Commissions Jacob

1Isaac summoned Jacob, conferred his blessing upon him, and charged him, “You shall not espouse a wife from among the daughters of Canaan. 2Arise and journey to Paddan-aram, to the household of your maternal grandfather Bethuel, and take for yourself a wife from among the daughters of Laban, your mother’s brother.”

3“May God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty bless you, make you fruitful, and multiply you, until you become a multitude of peoples. 4May HeheHe bestow upon you the blessing of Abraham—upon you and your descendants with you—so that you may inherit the land of your sojournings, which God granted to Abraham.”

5Thus Isaac dispatched Jacob, and he travelledtravelledtraveled to Paddan-aram, to Laban, son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

Esau's Response to His Parents' Wishes

6Esau observed that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him to Paddan-aram to procure a wife there, and that in blessing him he had charged him, “You shall not espouse a wife from among the daughters of Canaan.” 7Jacob obeyed his father and mother and departed for Paddan-aram.

Jacob's Dream at Bethel

10Jacob departed from Beersheba and journeyed toward Haran. 11Upon reaching a certain place, he lodged there because the sun had set; taking one of the stones of that place, he placed it beneath his head and reclined there.

12He dreamed, and behold, a ladder was erected upon the earth, its summit touching Heavenheavenheaven, while the angels of God ascended and descended upon it. 13And behold, the LORD stood above it and declared, “I am the LORD, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. The land upon which you lie I will give to you and to your descendants. 14Your descendants shall become as the dust of the earth, and you shall break forth to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south; in you and in your descendants all the families of the earth shall be blessed. 15Behold, I am with you, and I will guard you in every way you go; I will restore you to this land, for I will not forsake you until I have fulfilled what I have spoken to you.”

16Jacob awakened from his sleep and exclaimed, “Surely the LORD is in this place, and I was unaware of it.” 17Stricken with awe, he said, “How formidable this place is! It is nothing less than the house of God and the gate of Heavenheavenheaven.”

18Early the next morning Jacob took the stone he had set beneath his head, erected it as a pillar, and poured oil upon its summit. 19He named that place Bethel, although the city had formerly been called Luz.
