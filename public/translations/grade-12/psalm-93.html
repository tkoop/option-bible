The LORD Reigns in Majesty

1The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty; the LORD is robed in majesty and armed with strength. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved. 2Your throne was established long ago; YouyouYou are from all eternity.

3The floods have lifted up, O the LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods have lifted up their pounding waves. 4Above the roar of many waters—the mighty breakers of the sea—the LORD on high is mighty.

5Your testimonies are fully confirmed; holiness adorns YouryourYour house, O the LORD, for endless days.
