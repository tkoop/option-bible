Restitution for Deceit and the Guilt Offering

1The LORD spoke to Moses: 2“If a person sins and acts unfaithfully against the LORD by deceiving a neighbourneighbourneighbor about a deposit, a pledge, stolen property, or by defrauding him; 3or if he finds something lost and lies about it, swearing falsely in any matter of this kind by which a person sins— 4then, once he has sinned and incurred guilt, he must restore what he stole or extorted, the deposit entrusted to him, or the lost property he found. 5He must make full restitution, add a fifth of its value, and deliver it to the rightful owner on the day he presents his guilt offering. 6He must also bring his guilt offering to the LORD: a ram without blemish from the flock, valued according to your estimate, as a guilt offering. 7In this way the priest will make atonement for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for whatever he did that made him guilty.”

Burnt and Grain Offerings

8The LORD spoke to Moses: 9“Command Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the burnt offering. The burnt offering itself is to remain on the hearth of the altar all night until morning, and the fire on the altar must be kept burning. 10The priest is to put on his linen robe and linen undergarments, remove the ashes to which the fire has reduced the burnt offering on the altar, and place them beside the altar. 11Then he must remove those garments, put on others, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a ceremonially clean place. 12The fire on the altar must be kept burning and never allowed to go out; each morning the priest must burn wood on it, arrange the burnt offering upon it, and burn on it the fat of the peace offerings. 13The fire must be kept burning on the altar continually; it must not go out. 14These are the regulations for the grain offering: Aaron's sons are to present it before the LORD in front of the altar. 15One of them shall take a handful of the fine flour and oil of the grain offering, together with all the frankincense on it, and burn this memorial portion on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 16Aaron and his sons are to eat the remainder; it must be eaten as unleavened bread in a holy place, in the court of the tent of meeting. 17It must not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion of MymyMy food offerings; it is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering. 18Every male among Aaron's descendants may eat it. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations from the LORD's food offerings. Whatever touches them will become holy.”

The Priest's Grain Offering

19The LORD spoke to Moses: 20“This is the offering Aaron and his sons must present to the LORD on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a perpetual grain offering, half in the morning and half in the evening. 21It is to be prepared with oil on a griddle; you are to bring it well mixed and present the grain offering as baked pieces, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 22The priest anointed to succeed him from among his sons shall prepare it. It is a permanent statute to the LORD; it must be entirely burned. 23Every grain offering of a priest must be entirely burned; it must not be eaten.”

The Sin Offering

24The LORD spoke to Moses: 25“Speak to Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the sin offering. The sin offering is to be slaughtered before the LORD in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered; it is most holy. 26The priest who offers it for sin shall eat it; it is to be eaten as something holy in the court of the tent of meeting. 27Anything that touches its flesh will become holy, and if any of its blood is sprinkled on a garment, you must wash that garment in a holy place. 28The earthen vessel in which it is boiled must be broken, and if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, that vessel must be scoured and rinsed with water. 29Every male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy. 30But no sin offering whose blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place may be eaten; it must be burned with fire.”
