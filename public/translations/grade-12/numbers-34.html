The LORD Defines Israel’s Borders

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Command the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land of Canaan—this is the land that will be allotted to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders—

“3your southern boundary shall be from the wilderness of Zin along the border of Edom; on the east, your southern boundary shall begin at the end of the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea. 4your boundary shall turn south of the ascent of Akrabbim, pass through Zin, and end south of Kadesh-barnea; then it shall go to Hazar-addar and pass on to Azmon. 5The boundary shall turn from Azmon to the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt, and its end shall be at the SeaseaSea. 6For the western boundary, you shall have the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea and its coast; this shall be your western boundary.

“7This shall be your northern boundary: from the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea you shall mark out your line to Mount Hor. 8From Mount Hor you shall mark out your line to Lebo-hamath, and the end of the boundary shall be at Zedad. 9Then the boundary shall go to Ziphron, and its end shall be at Hazar-enan; this shall be your northern boundary. 10you shall draw your eastern boundary from Hazar-enan to Shepham. 11The boundary shall go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain, and the boundary shall descend and reach the eastern slope of the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth. 12The boundary shall descend to the Jordan, and its end shall be at the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea. This shall be your land with its boundaries all around.”

The Land to Be Divided by Lot

13Moses commanded the Israelites, saying, “This is the land that you shall inherit by lot, which the LORD has commanded to be given to the nine tribes and the half-tribe. 14For the tribe of the Reubenites by their ancestral houses, the tribe of the Gadites by their ancestral houses, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance. 15The two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho, eastward, toward the sunrise.”

Leaders Appointed to Apportion the Land

16The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 17“These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun. 18You shall take one leader from every tribe to apportion the land for inheritance.

“19These are the names of the men: For the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh. 20For the tribe of the Simeonites, Shemuel son of Ammihud. 21For the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon. 22For the tribe of the Danites, a leader, Bukki son of Jogli. 23For the Josephites: for the tribe of the Manassites, a leader, Hanniel son of Ephod, 24and for the tribe of the Ephraimites, a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan. 25For the tribe of the Zebulunites, a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnach. 26For the tribe of the Issacharites, a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan. 27For the tribe of the Asherites, a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi. 28For the tribe of the Naphtalites, a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud.”

29These are those whom the LORD commanded to apportion the inheritance for the Israelites in the land of Canaan.
