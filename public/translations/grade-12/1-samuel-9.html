Saul Searches for His Father's Lost Donkeys

1There was a man from Benjamin named Kish, son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah, a Benjamite, a valiant warrior. 2He had a son named Saul, a choice and handsome young man; among the Israelites, no one surpassed him in handsomeness, and from his shoulders upward he towered above all the people. 3The donkeys belonging to Kish, Saul's father, had strayed. Kish said to his son Saul, "Take one of the servants with you, rise, and go search for the donkeys." 4So he passed through the hill country of Ephraim and through the land of Shalishah, but they did not find them. Then they passed through the land of Shaalim, yet they were not there; then they passed through the land of Benjamin, but they did not find them. 5When they reached the land of Zuph, Saul said to the servant who was with him, "Come, let us return, lest my father stop worrying about the donkeys and become anxious about us."

6But he said, "Look, there is a man of God in this city, and he is an honouredhonouredhonored man; everything he says surely comes to pass. Let us go there now; perhaps he will tell us about our journey on which we have set out."

7Then Saul said to his servant, "But if we go, what shall we bring the man? The bread in our sacks is gone, and we have no gift to bring to the man of God. What do we have?"

8The servant answered Saul, "Look, I have a quarter shekel of silver; I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

9(Formerly in Israel, when a man went to inquire of God, he would say, "Come, let us go to the seer," for the prophet of today was formerly called a seer.)

10Saul said to his servant, "Well said; come, let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.

11As they were going up the hill to the city, they met young women coming out to draw water, and they said to them, "IsisIs the seer here?"

12They answered, "HeheHe is; look, he is just ahead of you. Hurry now, for he came into the city today, because the people have a sacrifice today on the high place. 13As soon as you enter the city, you will find him before he goes up to the high place to eat. For the people will not eat until he comes, because he must bless the sacrifice; afterward the invited guests will eat. Now go up, for at this time you will find him."

14So they went up to the city. As they were entering the city, look, Samuel was coming out toward them to go up to the high place.

The LORD Reveals Saul to Samuel

15Now the LORD had revealed to Samuel a day before Saul came, saying, 16"Tomorrow about this time I will send to you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as prince over MymyMy people Israel. He shall save MymyMy people from the hand of the Philistines, for I have seen MymyMy people, because their cry has come to MemeMe."

17When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said to him, "Look, the man of whom I spoke to you! This is he who shall rule over MymyMy people."

Samuel HonoursHonoursHonors Saul and Reveals His Destiny

18Then Saul approached Samuel in the midst of the gate and said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."

19Samuel answered Saul, "I am the seer. Go up before me to the high place, for today you shall eat with me; and in the morning I will let you go, and will tell you all that is on your mind. 20As for your donkeys that were lost three days ago, do not worry about them, for they have been found. And for whom is all the desire of Israel? Is it not for you and for all your father's house?"

21Saul answered, "Am I not a Benjamite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and my family the least of all the families of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me in this way?"

22Then Samuel took Saul and his servant and brought them into the hall and gave them a place at the head of those who had been invited, who were about thirty men.

23Samuel said to the cook, "BringbringBring the portion that I gave you, of which I said to you, 'Put it aside.'"

24So the cook took up the leg with what was on it and set it before Saul. And Samuel said, "Look, what was kept is set before you. Eat, because it was kept for you until the appointed time, while I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel that day.

25When they came down from the high place into the city, Samuel spoke with Saul on the roof.

26They rose early, and at dawn Samuel called to Saul on the roof, saying, "Get up, that I may send you away." So Saul arose, and both of them, he and Samuel, went out into the street.

27As they were going down to the outskirts of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the servant to pass on before us, and when he has passed on, stand here a while, that I may make known to you the word of God."
