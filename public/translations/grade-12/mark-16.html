6He said to them, “Do not be alarmed. You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has risen; HeheHe is not here. Appearances of the Risen Lord

9(Now after HeheHe had risen early on the first day of the week, HeheHe appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom HeheHe had cast out seven demons.) 10She went and reported it to those who had been with HimhimHim, who were mourning and weeping. 11But when they heard that HeheHe was alive and had been seen by her, they refused to believe it.

12Afterward HeheHe appeared in another form to two of them as they were walking along on their way to the country. 13They went and reported it to the rest, but they did not believe them either.

14Later HeheHe appeared to the Eleven as they were reclining at table, and HeheHe rebuked them for their unbelief and obstinacy, because they had not believed those who had seen HimhimHim after HeheHe had risen.
