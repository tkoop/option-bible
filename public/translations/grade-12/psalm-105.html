Praise the LORD and Remember His Wondrous Deeds

1Give thanks to the LORD; call upon HishisHis name, and proclaim HishisHis deeds among the nations. 2Sing to HimhimHim; sing praises to HimhimHim, and recount all HishisHis wonders. 3Glory in HishisHis holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. 4Seek the LORD and HishisHis strength; seek HishisHis face always. 5Remember the wonders HeheHe has done, HishisHis miracles, and the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments HeheHe has spoken.

The LORD's Covenant with the Patriarchs

6O descendants of Abraham HishisHis servant, O sons of Jacob, HishisHis chosen ones, 7HeheHe is the LORD our God; HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments reach throughout the earth. 8He remembers HishisHis covenant forever, the word HeheHe commanded for a thousand generations— 9the covenant HeheHe made with Abraham, and HishisHis oath to Isaac, 10which HeheHe established as a statute for Jacob, an everlasting covenant for Israel— 11saying, “I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.”

12When they were but few in number, insignificant, and strangers in that land, 13they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another. 14He allowed no one to oppress them; HeheHe rebuked kings on their behalf, 15saying, “Do not touch MymyMy anointed ones; do no harm to MymyMy prophets.”

From Joseph to the Plagues of Egypt

16He called a famine upon the land and broke every source of bread. 17He sent a man before them—Joseph, sold as a slave. 18They bruised his feet with shackles, and his neck was placed in irons, 19until his word came to pass and the word of the LORD proved him true. 20The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free. 21He made him master of his household and ruler over all his possessions, 22to instruct his princes as he pleased and to teach his elders wisdom.

23Then Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. 24He made HishisHis people very fruitful and stronger than their adversaries. 25He turned their hearts to hate HishisHis people and to conspire against HishisHis servants. 26He sent Moses HishisHis servant, and Aaron, whom HeheHe had chosen. 27They performed HishisHis signs among them and wonders in the land of Ham. 28He sent darkness, and it became dark, and they did not rebel against HishisHis words. 29He turned their waters into blood and caused their fish to die. 30Their land teemed with frogs, even in their royal chambers. 31He spoke, and insects swarmed—gnats throughout their country. 32He gave them hail for rain, with lightning throughout their land. 33He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country. 34He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts without number. 35They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil. 36Then HeheHe struck down all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigourvigourvigor.

The Exodus and the Promised Land

37He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and none among HishisHis tribes stumbled. 38Egypt was glad when they departed, for the dread of Israel had fallen upon them. 39He spread a cloud as a covering and a fire to light the night. 40He summoned quail and satisfied them with bread from Heavenheavenheaven. 41He opened the rock, and water gushed out; it flowed in the desert like a river. 42For HeheHe remembered HishisHis holy promise to Abraham HishisHis servant. 43He brought forth HishisHis people with rejoicing, HishisHis chosen with shouts of joy. 44He gave them the lands of the nations, that they might inherit the fruit of others' labourlabourlabor, 45that they might keep HishisHis statutes and obey HishisHis laws.
