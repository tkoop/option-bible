Israel's Stubbornness and the LORD's Patience

1“Hear this, O house of Jacob—you who bear the name Israel and descend from Judah's line, you who swear by the the LORD's name and invoke the God of Israel, yet not in truth or righteousness. 2For they style themselves after the holy city and lean upon the God of Israel; the the LORD of Hosts is HishisHis name.

3“I foretold the former things long ago; they went out from MymyMy mouth, and I proclaimed them. Suddenly I acted, and they came to pass. 4Because I knew you were obstinate—your neck an iron sinew and your forehead bronze. 5I declared it to you long ago; before it came to pass I announced it to you, lest you should say, ‘My idol did this; my carved image and molten idol commanded it.’

6“You have heard; now see all this. And you, will you not declare it? I proclaim to you new things from this time—hidden things you have not known. 7They are created now, not long ago; before today you have not heard them, lest you say, ‘Behold, I knew them.’ 8You have neither heard nor known; from ancient times your ear has not been opened. For I knew that you would deal treacherously, and you were called a rebel from the womb.

9“For MymyMy name's sake I defer MymyMy anger; for the sake of MymyMy praise I restrain it for you, that I may not cut you off. 10Behold, I have refined you, but not as silver; I have tested you in the furnace of affliction. 11For MymyMy own sake, for MymyMy own sake, I will act; for how could MymyMy name be profaned? I will not give MymyMy glory to another.”

The LORD's Eternal Purpose and Israel's Deliverance

And now the Lord the LORD has sent MemeMe, and HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit.

The Call to Depart from Babylon

20“Go out from Babylon, flee from the Chaldeans! Declare with a shout of joy, proclaim this, send it out to the ends of the earth; say, ‘The the LORD has redeemed HishisHis servant Jacob!’

21“They did not thirst when HeheHe led them through the deserts; HeheHe made water flow for them from the rock; HeheHe split the rock, and water gushed out. 22There is no peace, says the the LORD, for the wicked.”
