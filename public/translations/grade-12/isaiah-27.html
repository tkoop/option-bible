The LORD's Triumph over Leviathan

1In that day the LORD will punish Leviathan the fleeing serpent with HishisHis hard, great, and strong sword; HeheHe will punish Leviathan the twisting serpent and slay the dragon that is in the sea.

The LORD's Care for His Vineyard

2In that day, “Sing of a delightful vineyard: 3I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually. I guard it night and day, lest anyone harm it. 4I have no wrath. If only thorns and briers confronted MemeMe, I would march against them in battle and burn them all together. 5Or let him take hold of MymyMy stronghold and make peace with MemeMe—yes, let him make peace with MemeMe.”

6In days to come Jacob will take root; Israel will blossom and put forth shoots, and the face of the world will be filled with fruit.

The LORD's Measured JudgmentJudgementJudgment

7Has HeheHe struck them as HeheHe struck their attackers? Or have they been slain as their slayers were slain? 8In exact measure, when you sent her away, you contended with her. He removed her with HishisHis fierce blast in the day of the east wind. 9Therefore by this the guilt of Jacob will be atoned for, and this will be the full fruit of removing his sin: when he makes all the stones of the altar like crushed chalkstones, so that no Asherah poles or incense altars remain standing.

10The fortified city stands desolate, an abandoned settlement forsaken like the wilderness; there the calf grazes, there it lies down and strips its branches bare. 11When its boughs are dry, they are broken; women come and set them ablaze. For this is a people without understanding; therefore their MakermakerMaker will not have compassion on them, and their CreatorcreatorCreator will not be gracious to them.

The Gathering of God's People

12In that day the LORD will thresh from the Euphrates River to the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt, and you will be gathered one by one, O people of Israel. 13In that day a great trumpet will be blown, and those who were lost in the land of Assyria and those who were driven out to the land of Egypt will come and worship the LORD on the holy mountain at Jerusalem.
