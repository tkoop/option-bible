The LORD's Case Against His People

1Listen now to what the LORD declares: "Arise and present your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice." 2Hear, O mountains, the LORD's indictment, and you enduring foundations of the earth, for the LORD has a case against HishisHis people, and HeheHe will contend with Israel.

3"My people, what have I done to you? How have I wearied you? Bear witness against MemeMe. 4For I brought you up from the land of Egypt and ransomed you from the house of bondage; I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam before you. 5My people, recall what Balak king of Moab devised, and what Balaam son of Beor answered him; remember the route from Shittim to Gilgal, that you may know the righteous deeds of the LORD."

The People Ask What the LORD Requires

6"With what shall I come before the LORD and bow before the God of Heavenheavenheaven? Shall I come before HimhimHim with burnt offerings, with calves a year old? 7Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousand rivers of oil? Shall I give my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?"

The LORD's Answer: Justice, Mercy, and Humility

8He has shown you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?

The LORD Indicts the City and Pronounces JudgmentJudgementJudgment

9The voice of the LORD calls to the city—and it is sound wisdom to fear YouryourYour name: "Hear the rod and HimhimHim who appointed it! 10Are there still ill-gotten treasures in the house of the wicked, and a deficient ephah under curse? 11Can anyone be innocent with dishonest scales and a bag of deceitful weights? 12For her rich men are full of violence, her inhabitants speak lies, and their tongues are steeped in deceit.

13"Therefore I will strike you with a devastating blow and make you desolate because of your sins. 14You will eat but not be satisfied, and emptiness will linger within your midst. You will hoard but not secure, and what you save I will give to the sword. 15You will sow but not reap; you will tread olives but not anoint yourself with oil; and you will press new wine but not drink it. 16For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab; and you have walked in their counsels, that I may make you a desolation, and your inhabitants a hissing; and you will bear the reproach of MymyMy people."
