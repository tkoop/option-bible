The Angel's Message About Future Kingdoms

1And as for me, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to strengthen and protect him. 2Now I will tell you the truth: three more kings will arise in Persia, and the fourth will be far richer than all the others; and when he becomes strong through his riches, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece. 3Then a mighty king will arise, who will rule with great authority and do as he pleases. 4But as soon as he has arisen, his kingdom will be broken up and divided toward the four winds of Heavenheavenheaven; it will not pass to his descendants, nor will it be as he ruled, because his kingdom will be uprooted and given to others besides these.

The Rivalry of the Kings of the South and North

5Then the king of the South will grow strong, along with one of his commanders; and that commander will grow stronger than he and rule with a great dominion. 6After some years they will form an alliance, and the daughter of the king of the South will come to the king of the North to make an agreement; but she will not retain her power, nor will his strength endure. She and her escort, her child, and the one who supported her will be given over. 7Yet a shoot from her roots will rise in her place, and he will come with the army, enter the fortress of the king of the North, and act against them and prevail. 8He will also carry off their gods, along with their metal images and their precious vessels of silver and gold, into Egypt; and for some years he will refrain from attacking the king of the North. 9Then the king of the North will invade the realm of the king of the South, but will return to his own land.

10His sons will wage war and assemble a great multitude of forces, which will advance and overflow and pass through; and they will return and wage war as far as his stronghold. 11Then the king of the South will be enraged and go out and fight against the king of the North, who will raise a great army, but it will be given into his hand. 12When the army is carried away, his heart will be exalted, and he will cast down tens of thousands, but he will not prevail. 13For the king of the North will return and raise a multitude greater than the former, and after some years he will certainly come with a great army and much equipment. 14In those times many will rise up against the king of the South; and the violent among your people will exalt themselves to fulfillfulfilfulfill the vision, but they will fail. 15Then the king of the North will come and build a siege ramp and capture a fortified city. The forces of the South will not stand; even their best troops will not have the strength to stand.

16But he who comes against him will do as he pleases, and no one will stand before him. He will stand in the Beautiful LandlandLand, with destruction in his hand. 17He will set his face to come with the strength of his whole kingdom, and upright men with him; thus he will do. And he will give him the daughter of women to bring it to ruin; but she will not stand with him, nor be for him. 18Then he will turn his face toward the coastlands and capture many. But a commander will put an end to his taunting, and will turn his taunting back on him. 19Then he will turn his face toward the fortresses of his own land, but he will stumble and fall, and be found no more.

The Contemptible King and the Desecration

20Then in his place one will arise who will send an oppressor through the glory of the kingdom, but within a few days he will be broken, neither in anger nor in battle. 21In his place a despicable person will arise, on whom royal majesty has not been conferred; he will come in a time of security and seize the kingdom by intrigue. 22The overflowing forces will be swept away before him and shattered, and also the prince of the covenant. 23And after an alliance is made with him, he will act deceitfully, and he will rise and become strong with a small people. 24In a time of security he will enter the richest parts of the province and do what neither his fathers nor his forefathers have done; he will distribute plunder, spoil, and wealth among them, and he will devise plans against fortified cities, but only for a time.

25He will stir up his power and his courage against the king of the South with a great army, and the king of the South will wage war with an exceedingly great and mighty army; but he will not stand, for schemes will be devised against him. 26Those who eat his food will destroy him, and his army will be swept away, and many will fall slain. 27As for both kings, their hearts will be intent on evil, and they will speak lies at the same table; but it will not succeed, for the end is still at the appointed time. 28Then he will return to his land with great wealth, but his heart will be set against the holy covenant; he will take action and then return to his own land. 29At the appointed time he will return and come into the South, but this time it will not be as the former or as the latter.

30For ships of Kittim will come against him; therefore he will be discouraged and return. He will be furious with the holy covenant and take action; and he will return and show favourfavourfavor to those who forsake the holy covenant. 31His forces will arise, desecrate the sanctuary fortress, and abolish the regular sacrifice; and they will set up the abomination of desolation. 32By smooth words he will corrupt those who violate the covenant, but the people who know their God will display strength and take action. 33Those with insight will instruct many, though for a time they will fall by sword and flame, by captivity and plunder. 34Now when they fall they will be granted a little help, and many will join with them in hypocrisy. 35Some of those who have insight will fall, to refine, purge, and make them white until the time of the end; because it is still for the appointed time.

The Final King and the Time of the End

36Then the king will do as he pleases, and he will exalt and magnify himself above every god, and he will speak astonishing things against the God of gods. He will prosper until the indignation is finished, for that which is decreed will be done. 37He will show no regard for the gods of his fathers or for the desire of women, nor will he regard any god; for he will magnify himself above them all. 38But instead he will honourhonourhonor a god of fortresses—a god whom his fathers did not know—with gold, silver, precious stones, and costly gifts. 39He will attack the strongest fortresses with the help of a foreign god; those who acknowledge him he will greatly honourhonourhonor, and he will cause them to rule over many and will divide the land for a price.

40At the time of the end the king of the South will collide with him, and the king of the North will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships; and he will enter countries, overflow them, and pass through. 41He will also enter the Beautiful LandlandLand, and many countries will fall; but these will be rescued from his hand: Edom, Moab, and the foremost of the sons of Ammon. 42Then he will stretch out his hand against the countries, and the land of Egypt will not escape. 43He will gain control of the treasures of gold and silver and all the precious things of Egypt; and Libyans and Cushites will follow in his steps. 44But rumoursrumoursrumors from the east and the north will disturb him, and he will go out with great fury to destroy and annihilate many. 45He will pitch the tents of his royal pavilion between the seas and the beautiful holy mountain; yet he will come to his end, and no one will help him.
