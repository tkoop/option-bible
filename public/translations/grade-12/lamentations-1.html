Jerusalem's Lonely Ruin

1How desolate sits the city once thronged with people! How like a widow she has become, she who was mighty among the nations and a princess among provinces, now reduced to vassalage. 2Bitterly she weeps through the night, tears staining her cheeks; among all her former allies, none remains to console her, for every friend has betrayed her and become an enemy. 3Judah has been carried into exile, crushed by affliction and grinding toil; dwelling among foreign peoples, she finds no rest, and her persecutors overtake her amid her anguish. 4Zion's roads mourn because no pilgrims attend the festivals; her gates stand empty; her priests sigh; her young women grieve; and the city itself is steeped in bitterness.

The City's Suffering and Enemy Triumph

5Her adversaries now dominate her; her enemies thrive, for the LORD has afflicted her because of her many transgressions; her children have been led away captive before the oppressor. 6All the splendoursplendoursplendor has departed from DaughterdaughterDaughter Zion. Her princes resemble deer searching in vain for pasture; robbed of strength, they flee before the hunter. 7In the days of her affliction and homelessness, Jerusalem remembers all the treasures she once possessed; when her people fell into enemy hands and no one came to rescue them, her foes gloated and mocked her ruin. 8Jerusalem sinned grievously, and so she became an object of scorn; all who once honouredhonouredhonored her now despise her, having seen her shame exposed; she groans and turns away in humiliation.

Jerusalem's Lament and Plea to the LORD

9Her defilement clung to her garments; she never considered her end, so her collapse astonished everyone; no one comforted her. "Look, the LORD, upon my affliction, for the enemy has triumphed!" 10The enemy stretched out his hand over all her treasures, for she watched the nations invade her sanctuary—people YouyouYou had forbidden to enter YouryourYour assembly. 11All her people groan, searching for bread; they barter their treasures for food to sustain their lives. "Look, the LORD, and take notice, for I have become contemptible." 12Does this mean nothing to all you who pass by? Look and see whether any sorrow equals mine, the sorrow the LORD brought upon me in the day of HishisHis fierce anger.

13From above HeheHe sent fire into my bones, and it overpowered them; HeheHe spread a net for my feet and turned me back; HeheHe has made me desolate and faint all day long. 14My transgressions are bound into a yoke; by HishisHis hand they are fastened together and placed upon my neck; HeheHe has sapped my strength; the Lord has delivered me into the hands of those I cannot resist. 15The Lord has rejected all my warriors within me; HeheHe has summoned a time of judgmentjudgementjudgment against me to crush my young men; the Lord has trampled the virgin DaughterdaughterDaughter of Judah like grapes in a winepress. 16Because of these things I weep; my eyes overflow with tears, because a comforter is far from me, and no one restores my spirit; my children are desolate because the enemy has prevailed.

17Zion stretches out her hands, yet no one comforts her; the LORD has commanded concerning Jacob that his enemies surround him; Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them. 18The LORD is righteous, for I rebelled against HishisHis command; hear, all you peoples, and behold my suffering; my young men and young women have gone into exile. 19I called to my allies, but they betrayed me; my priests and elders perished in the city as they searched for food to sustain themselves. 20"Look, the LORD, for I am in distress; my innermost being churns; my heart is overturned within me, because I have been utterly rebellious. Outside, the sword bereaves; inside, there is only death." 21They heard my groaning, yet no one comforted me; all my enemies heard of my calamity and rejoiced that YouyouYou have done it.
