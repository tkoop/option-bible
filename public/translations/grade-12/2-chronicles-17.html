Jehoshaphat Establishes His Kingdom

1His son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place, and he strengthened himself against Israel. 2He stationed troops in all the fortified cities of Judah, and he put garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.

The LORD Prospers Jehoshaphat

3The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the former ways of his father David and did not seek the Baals. 4Instead, he sought the God of his father and walked by HishisHis commandments rather than according to the practices of Israel. 5So the LORD established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah brought tribute to Jehoshaphat, so that he had an abundance of riches and honourhonourhonor. 6His heart was courageous in the ways of the LORD, and he removed the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah.

Teaching the Law throughout Judah

7In the third year of his reign, Jehoshaphat sent his officials—Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in the cities of Judah. 8With them were the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, along with the priests Elishama and Jehoram. 9They taught in Judah, having with them the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD, and they went throughout all the cities of Judah and taught among the people. 10And the dread of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, so that they did not make war against Jehoshaphat. 11Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat tribute and silver as a load, and the Arabs also brought him seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred goats.

Judah’s Army and Resources

12Jehoshaphat grew stronger and stronger, and he built in Judah fortresses and store cities. 13He had a great deal of property in the cities of Judah, and men of war—mighty men of valourvalourvalor—in Jerusalem. 14These are their numbers according to their fathers’ houses for Judah: the commanders of thousands, Adnah the commander, and with him 300,000 mighty men of valourvalourvalor. 15Next to him was Jehohanan the commander, with 280,000. 16Next to him was Amasiah son of Zichri, who volunteered himself to the LORD, and with him 200,000 mighty men of valourvalourvalor. 17From Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty man of valourvalourvalor, with 200,000 men armed with bow and shield. 18Next to him was Jehozabad, with 180,000 ready for battle. 19These were the men who served the king, in addition to those he stationed in the fortified cities throughout Judah.
