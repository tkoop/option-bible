Josiah Observes the Passover

1Josiah observed the Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem, and the Passover lambs were slaughtered on the fourteenth day of the first month. 2He stationed the priests in their divisions and strengthened them for the service of the house of the LORD.

3To the Levites, who taught all Israel and were holy to the LORD, he said, "Put the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David, king of Israel, built. You no longer have a burden to carry on your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and HishisHis people Israel. 4Prepare yourselves according to your ancestral houses and divisions, as written by David king of Israel and by Solomon his son. 5Stand in the holy place according to the divisions of the fathers' houses of your brothers, the lay people, and according to the division of a fathers' house of the Levites. 6Slaughter the Passover, sanctify yourselves, and prepare for your brothers, so that you may carry out the word of the LORD given through Moses."

Josiah and His Officials Provide for the Passover

7King Josiah contributed from his own possessions thirty thousand lambs and young goats, and three thousand bulls, all for the Passover offerings for all the people who were present. 8His officials contributed willingly for the people, the priests, and the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the chief officers of the house of God, gave the priests two thousand six hundred lambs and kids and three hundred bulls for the Passover offerings. 9Conaniah, along with his brothers Shemaiah, Nethanel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad, the chiefs of the Levites, gave the Levites five thousand lambs and kids and five hundred bulls for the Passover offerings.

10So the service was prepared. The priests stood in their places, and the Levites in their divisions, according to the king's command. 11They slaughtered the Passover lambs. The priests sprinkled the blood from their hands, while the Levites skinned the animals. 12They set aside the burnt offerings to be distributed to the divisions of the fathers' houses of the lay people, so that they could offer them to the LORD, as written in the BookbookBook of Moses; they did the same with the bulls. 13They roasted the Passover over the fire according to the ordinance, boiled the holy offerings in pots, cauldrons, and pans, and carried them quickly to all the people.

14Afterward they prepared portions for themselves and for the priests, because the priests, the sons of Aaron, were occupied with offering burnt offerings and fat portions until nightfall; the Levites prepared portions for themselves and for the priests, the sons of Aaron. 15The singers, the sons of Asaph, stood in their places according to the command of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun the king's seer. The gatekeepers were at each gate. They did not have to leave their posts, because their fellow Levites prepared portions for them. 16Thus all the service of the LORD was prepared that day for observing the Passover and for offering burnt offerings on the altar of the LORD, according to the command of King Josiah.

The Passover Is Celebrated

17The Israelites who were present kept the Passover at that time and celebrated the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread for seven days. 18No Passover like it had been kept in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. None of the kings of Israel had ever kept a Passover such as this one, which Josiah kept with the priests, the Levites, all Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 19This Passover was kept in the eighteenth year of Josiah's reign.

Josiah's Death at Megiddo

20After all this, when Josiah had set the temple in order, Neco king of Egypt marched up to fight at Carchemish on the Euphrates. Josiah went out to meet him. 21Neco sent messengers to him, saying, "What do you have against me, king of Judah? I am not coming against you today, but against the house with which I am at war. God has commanded me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me, so that HeheHe does not destroy you." 22But Josiah did not turn away from him. Instead, he disguised himself to fight against him. He did not listen to the words of Neco, which came from the mouth of God, but went to fight in the ValleyvalleyValley of Megiddo.

23The archers shot King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, "Take me away, for I am badly wounded." 24His servants took him out of his chariot and put him in his second chariot, then brought him to Jerusalem. There he died and was buried in the tombs of his fathers. All Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah.

25Jeremiah composed laments for Josiah, and to this day all the male and female singers speak of Josiah in their laments. They made these laments an ordinance in Israel, and they are written in the BookbookBook of Laments. 26The rest of the acts of Josiah, along with his deeds of faithful love, are written according to the LawlawLaw of the LORD. 27His first and last acts are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Israel and Judah.
