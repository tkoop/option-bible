Amaziah's Early Reign

1Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan, from Jerusalem. 2He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, but not with a whole heart. 3Once the kingdom was firmly established under him, he killed the servants who had struck down his father the king. 4Yet he did not execute their sons, because it is written in the LawlawLaw, in the BookbookBook of Moses, which the LORD commanded: “Fathers shall not die for their sons, nor sons die for their fathers; instead, each shall die for his own sin.”

Amaziah's Army and the Prophet's Warning

5Amaziah gathered Judah and assigned them by ancestral houses under commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds for all Judah and Benjamin. He mustered those twenty years old and older and found three hundred thousand chosen men able to go to war, bearing spear and shield. 6He also hired one hundred thousand mighty warriors from Israel for one hundred talents of silver.

7Then a man of God came to him and said, “O king, do not let Israel's army go with you, for the LORD is not with Israel—not with any of the Ephraimites. 8But if you go, be strong for battle; God will make you fall before the enemy, for God has power both to help and to overthrow.”

9Amaziah asked the man of God, “But what are we to do about the hundred talents I have already given to Israel's troops?”

“The LORD can give you much more than this.”

10So Amaziah dismissed the troops who had come to him from Ephraim and sent them home. They were furious with Judah and returned home in a rage.

11Amaziah strengthened himself and led his people to the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt, where he struck down ten thousand men of Seir. 12The men of Judah captured another ten thousand alive, took them to the top of the rock, and threw them down from the summit, so that all of them were dashed to pieces.

13Meanwhile, the troops Amaziah had sent back from the battle raided the cities of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon, killing three thousand people and carrying off great plunder.

Idolatry, Pride, and Conflict with Israel

14After Amaziah returned from defeating the Edomites, he brought back the gods of the Seirites, set them up as his own gods, bowed down to them, and offered sacrifices to them.

15The LORD's anger burned against Amaziah, and HeheHe sent a prophet to him, who said, “Why have you sought the gods of a people who could not deliver their own people from your hand?”

16While the prophet was still speaking, the king said to him, “Have we appointed you as counsellorcounsellorcounselor to the king? Stop! Why should you be struck down?”

“I know that God has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel.”

17After taking counsel, Amaziah king of Judah sent messengers to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come, let us meet face to face.”

18Joash king of Israel replied to Amaziah king of Judah, “A thornbush in Lebanon sent to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son for a wife,’ but a wild beast in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thornbush. 19You say, ‘Look, I have defeated Edom,’ and your heart has lifted you up in boasting. Now stay at home. Why should you provoke trouble and fall, you and Judah with you?”

20But Amaziah would not listen, for this turn of events was from God, so that HeheHe might deliver them into the hand of their enemies, because they had sought the gods of Edom.

Amaziah's Defeat and Death

21So Joash king of Israel advanced, and he and Amaziah king of Judah faced each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah.

22Judah was defeated before Israel, and every man fled to his home.

23Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem. He broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim GategateGate to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate, a distance of four hundred cubits.

24He took all the gold, silver, and vessels found in the house of God, together with Obed-edom, the treasures of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

25Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 26The rest of the acts of Amaziah, from first to last, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Judah and Israel?

27From the time Amaziah turned away from following the LORD, a conspiracy formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but they pursued him there and put him to death. 28They carried him back on horses and buried him with his fathers in a city of Judah.
