Warriors Who Rallied to David at Ziklag

1These are the men who came to David at Ziklag while he was still banished from the presence of Saul son of Kish. They ranked among the warriors who aided him in battle. 2They were archers who could shoot arrows and sling stones with either hand; they were Benjamites, Saul's kinsmen.

3The chief was Ahiezer, then Joash, sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Beracah and Jehu the Anathothite; 4Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the ThirtythirtyThirty and leader over the ThirtythirtyThirty; Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite; 5Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite; 6Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam the Korahites; 7and Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham from Gedor.

The Gadite Warriors Who Joined David

8Some Gadites defected to David at the stronghold in the wilderness. They were mighty men of valourvalourvalor, trained for war, able to handle shield and spear, whose faces were like the faces of lions and who were as swift as gazelles on the mountains.

9Ezer was the chief, Obadiah second, Eliab third, 10Mishmannah fourth, Jeremiah fifth, 11Attai sixth, Eliel seventh, 12Johanan eighth, Elzabad ninth, 13Jeremiah tenth, and Machbannai eleventh.

14These Gadites were commanders of the army; the least of them was a match for a hundred, and the greatest for a thousand. 15These are the ones who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was overflowing all its banks, and they put to flight all those in the valleys, both east and west.

16Some Benjamites and men from Judah also came to David at the stronghold.

David Tests the Loyalty of Those Who Came to Him

17David went out to meet them and said, "If you have come to me in peace to help me, my heart will be fully with you; but if you have come to betray me to my enemies, though I have done no wrong, may the God of our fathers see it and judge."

18Then the SpiritspiritSpirit came upon Amasai, chief of the ThirtythirtyThirty, and he said, "We are yours, David! We are with you, son of Jesse! Peace, peace to you, and peace to your helpers, for your God helps you." So David received them and made them leaders of his troops.

19Some Manassites defected to David when he went with the Philistines to fight against Saul. They did not help the Philistines, for the Philistine rulers sent David away, saying, "HeheHe will defect to his master Saul at the cost of our heads." 20As David went to Ziklag, these men of Manasseh defected to him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, chiefs of thousands in Manasseh. 21They helped David against raiders, for they were all mighty men of valourvalourvalor and commanders in the army. 22For day after day men came to David to help him, until he had a great army, like the army of God.

The Forces Assembled at Hebron to Make David King

23Now these are the numbers of men armed for battle who came to David at Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul over to him, as the LORD had declared:

24From Judah: 6,800 armed men bearing shields and spears. 25From Simeon: 7,100 mighty men of valourvalourvalor, ready for war. 26From Levi: 4,600, 27including Jehoiada, leader of the house of Aaron, with 3,700 men, 28and Zadok, a young mighty man of valourvalourvalor, with twenty-two commanders from his own family; 29from Benjamin, the kinsmen of Saul: 3,000, most of whom had remained loyal to the house of Saul until then;

30from Ephraim: 20,800 mighty men of valourvalourvalor, famous men in their fathers' houses; 31from the half-tribe of Manasseh: 18,000 designated by name to come and make David king; 32from Issachar: men who understood the times and knew what Israel should do—200 chiefs with all their kinsmen under their command; 33from Zebulun: 50,000 seasoned troops, prepared for battle with every kind of weapon, to help David with an undivided heart; 34from Naphtali: 1,000 commanders, accompanied by 37,000 men armed with shield and spear; 35from Dan: 28,600 men prepared for battle; 36from Asher: 40,000 seasoned troops prepared for battle; 37and from beyond the Jordan, from Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh: 120,000 armed with every kind of weapon of war.

38All these men of war, arrayed in battle order, came to Hebron fully determined to make David king over all Israel. And all the rest of Israel were of one mind to make David king.

39They stayed there with David three days, eating and drinking, for their brothers had made preparations for them. 40Their neighbors as far as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali brought food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen—abundant supplies of flour, fig cakes, raisins, wine, oil, oxen, and sheep. Indeed, there was joy in Israel.
