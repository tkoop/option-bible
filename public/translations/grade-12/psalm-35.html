A Psalm of David

1Strive, O the LORD, with my adversaries; wage war against those who war against me. 2Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise to my aid. 3Brandish spear and javelin against my pursuers; say to my soul, “I am your salvation.”

The Defeat of His Enemies

4Let those who seek my life be dishonoureddishonoureddishonored and disgraced; let those who devise evil against me be turned back and confounded. 5Let them be like chaff before the wind, with the angel of the LORD driving them away. 6Let their path be dark and slippery, with the angel of the LORD pursuing them.

7For without cause they hid their net for me; without cause they dug a pit for my soul. 8Let ruin come upon them unawares; let the net they hid ensnare them; let them fall into it, to their ruin.

9Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD and exult in HishisHis salvation. 10All my bones will say, “the LORD, who is like YouyouYou, delivering the poor from those too strong for them, the poor and needy from those who rob them?”

Betrayal by False Friends

11Malicious witnesses rise up; they ask me of things I do not know. 12They repay me evil for good, to the bereavement of my soul.

13Yet when they were sick, I wore sackcloth; I humbled my soul with fasting, and I prayed with my head bowed upon my chest. 14I went about as though he were my friend or my brother; I bowed down in mourning, as one grieving for his mother.

15But when I stumbled, they rejoiced and gathered together; attackers gathered against me without my knowledge; they slandered me without ceasing. 16Like godless mockers at a feast, they gnashed their teeth at me.

A Final Appeal for Vindication

17O Lord, how long will YouyouYou look on? Rescue my soul from their ravages, my precious life from the lions. 18Then I will give YouyouYou thanks in the great assembly; I will praise YouyouYou among many people.

19Let not my enemies gloat over me without cause, nor those who hate me without reason wink maliciously. 20For they do not speak peace, but devise deceitful schemes against those who live quietly in the land.

21They gape at me and say, “Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen!”

22O the LORD, YouyouYou have seen; be not silent. O Lord, be not far from me. 23Awake and rise to my defencedefencedefense, to my cause, my God and my Lord. 24Vindicate me by YouryourYour righteousness, O the LORD my God, and do not let them gloat over me.

25Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha! We have swallowed him up!” Let them not say, “We have devoured him!” 26May all who gloat over my distress be ashamed and confounded; may those who exalt themselves over me be clothed with shame and disgrace.

27May those who favourfavourfavor my righteous cause shout for joy and be glad; may they ever say, “The LORD be magnified, who delights in the prosperity of HishisHis servant.” 28Then my tongue will proclaim YouryourYour righteousness and YouryourYour praises all day long.
