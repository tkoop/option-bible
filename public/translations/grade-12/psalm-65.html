Praise and Vows in Zion

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A SongsongSong. 1Praise awaits YouyouYou, O God, in Zion, and to YouyouYou our vows shall be fulfilled. 2You who hear prayer, to YouyouYou all flesh will come. 3My iniquities have overwhelmed me, yet YouyouYou atone for our transgressions. 4Blessed is the one YouyouYou choose and bring near to dwell in YouryourYour courts. We shall be satisfied with the goodness of YouryourYour house, the holiness of YouryourYour temple.

God's Awesome Deeds and Sovereign Power

5You answer us with awesome deeds in righteousness, O God of our salvation, YouyouYou who are the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. 6You formed the mountains by YouryourYour power, girded with strength. 7You stilled the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of the nations. 8Thus the inhabitants of the ends of the earth fear YouryourYour signs; YouyouYou make the dawn and the sunset shout for joy.

God's Abundant Provision for the Earth

9You visit the earth and water it; YouyouYou greatly enrich it. The river of God is full of water; YouyouYou prepare their grain, for so YouyouYou have prepared it. 10You drench its furrows and level its ridges; YouyouYou soften it with showers and bless its growth. 11You crown the year with YouryourYour goodness, and YouryourYour paths overflow with abundance. 12The pastures of the wilderness overflow, and the hills gird themselves with joy. 13The meadows are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are decked with grain; they shout and even sing.
