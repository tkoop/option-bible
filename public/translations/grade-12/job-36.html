Elihu Continues His DefenceDefenceDefense of God’s Justice

1Elihu continued and said:

“2Bear with me a little longer, and I will show you; for I still have words to speak on God’s behalf. 3I will carry my knowledge from afar and ascribe righteousness to my MakermakerMaker. 4For truly my words are not false; one perfect in knowledge is with you.

“5Behold, God is mighty and does not despise anyone; HeheHe is mighty in strength of understanding. 6He does not keep the wicked alive, but HeheHe gives justice to the afflicted. 7He never withdraws HishisHis eyes from the righteous; HeheHe enthrones them with kings forever and exalts them.

God’s Discipline and Deliverance

“8If they are bound in chains and caught in the cords of affliction, 9then HeheHe declares to them their deeds and their transgressions, that they have acted arrogantly. 10He opens their ears to instruction and commands them to turn back from iniquity. 11If they obey and serve HimhimHim, they complete their days in prosperity and their years in pleasantness. 12But if they do not obey, they perish by the sword and die without knowledge.

“13The godless in heart harbourharbourharbor anger; they do not cry for help when HeheHe binds them. 14They die in their youth, and their life ends among the male shrine prostitutes. 15He delivers the afflicted through their affliction and opens their ears by adversity. 16He is even drawing you out of distress into a broad place where there is no cramping, and the table set before you is laden with rich food.

“17Yet you are filled with the judgmentjudgementjudgment due the wicked; judgmentjudgementjudgment and justice have seized you. 18Beware lest wealth entice you, and do not let a great ransom turn you aside. 19Will your riches avail you, or all your mighty strength? 20Do not long for the night, when peoples are cut off in their place. 21Be careful; do not turn to iniquity, for you have chosen it rather than affliction.

God’s Power Displayed in Creation

“22Behold, God is exalted in HishisHis power; who is a teacher like HimhimHim? 23Who has prescribed HishisHis way for HimhimHim, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? 24Remember to extol HishisHis work, of which men have sung. 25All mankind has looked upon it; men behold it from afar.

“26Behold, God is great, and we do not know HimhimHim; the number of HishisHis years is unsearchable. 27For HeheHe draws up the drops of water; they distill rain from HishisHis mist. 28The clouds pour it down and drop it abundantly upon mankind. 29Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds, the thunderings of HishisHis pavilion?

“30Behold, HeheHe spreads HishisHis lightning over it and covers the depths of the sea. 31For by these HeheHe judges the peoples and gives food in abundance. 32He covers HishisHis hands with lightning and commands it to strike its mark. 33Its thunder announces HimhimHim, and even the cattle sense what is rising.”
