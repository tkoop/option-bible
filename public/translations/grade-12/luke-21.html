The Widow’s Offering and the Temple’s Impending Destruction

1Looking up, Jesus saw affluent worshippers casting their gifts into the treasury. 2He also noticed an impoverished widow depositing two small copper coins.

3“Truly I tell you, this poor widow has contributed more than all the others combined. 4For they all contributed out of their surplus, but she, out of her poverty, contributed her entire livelihood.”

5While some were speaking about the temple—how it was adorned with ornate stones and dedicated gifts—Jesus said,

6“As for these things you are observing, days are coming when not one stone will remain upon another; all will be dismantled.”
