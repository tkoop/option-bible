Jonathan's Secret Plan

1One day Jonathan, Saul's son, said to the young man carrying his armourarmourarmor, "Come, let us go over to the Philistine outpost on the other side." But he did not tell his father.

2Saul was staying under the pomegranate tree in Migron on the outskirts of Gibeah, and the troops with him numbered about six hundred men.

3Ahijah son of Ahitub, Ichabod's brother, son of Phinehas son of Eli, priest of the LORD in Shiloh, was wearing an ephod. The troops did not know that Jonathan had left.

4On each side of the pass that Jonathan intended to cross to reach the Philistine outpost stood a cliff; one was called Bozez and the other Seneh. 5One cliff rose to the north toward Michmash, and the other to the south toward Geba.

The Attack on the Philistine Outpost

6Jonathan said to his armourarmourarmor-bearer, "Come, let us go over to the outpost of these uncircumcised men. Perhaps the LORD will act on our behalf. Nothing can hinder the LORD from saving, whether by many or by few."

7His armourarmourarmor-bearer answered, "Do all that you have in mind. Go ahead; I am with you heart and soul."

8Jonathan said, "Come on, then; we will cross over toward them and let them see us. 9If they say, 'Stay there until we come to you,' we will stay where we are and not go up to them. 10But if they say, 'Come up to us,' we will climb up, because that will be our sign that the LORD has given them into our hands."

11So both of them showed themselves to the Philistine outpost, and the Philistines said, "Look! Hebrews are coming out of the holes where they were hiding." 12The men of the outpost shouted to Jonathan and his armourarmourarmor-bearer, "Come up to us and we'll teach you a lesson." So Jonathan said to his armourarmourarmor-bearer, "Climb up after me; the LORD has given them into Israel's hands."

13Jonathan climbed up on his hands and feet, with his armourarmourarmor-bearer right behind him, and the Philistines fell before Jonathan, while his armourarmourarmor-bearer killed them as he followed. 14In that first assault Jonathan and his armourarmourarmor-bearer killed about twenty men in an area of about half an acre. 15Then panic struck the camp, the field, and all the people; the outpost and the raiding party also trembled. The ground shook, and a panic from God spread through the land.

16Saul's watchmen in Gibeah of Benjamin saw the army melting away in all directions. 17Saul said to the troops with him, "Muster the men and see who has left us." When they did, Jonathan and his armourarmourarmor-bearer were missing.

18Saul said to Ahijah, "BringbringBring the ark of God." (At that time the ark of God was with the Israelites.) 19While Saul was speaking to the priest, the uproar in the Philistine camp increased more and more, so Saul said to the priest, "Stop."

20Then Saul and all the men with him assembled and went to battle. They found the Philistines in total confusion, striking each other with swords. 21The Hebrews who had previously been with the Philistines and had gone up with them into the camp went over to the Israelites who were with Saul and Jonathan. 22When all the Israelites who had been hiding in the hill country of Ephraim heard that the Philistines were fleeing, they joined the pursuit. 23So the LORD saved Israel that day, and the battle moved beyond Beth Aven.

Saul's Oath and the Day's Battle

24Now the Israelites were in distress that day, because Saul had bound the people with an oath, saying, "Cursed be anyone who eats food before evening, before I have avenged myself on my enemies." So none of the troops tasted food. 25All the army entered the woods, and there was honey on the ground. 26They saw the honey flowing, but no one put his hand to his mouth, because the troops feared the oath.

27But Jonathan had not heard his father's oath. He reached out the end of the staff in his hand, dipped it into the honeycomb, put his hand to his mouth, and his eyes brightened. 28Then one of the soldiers told him, "YouryourYour father bound the army with a solemn oath: 'Cursed be anyone who eats food today.'" And the troops were exhausted.

29Jonathan said, "MymyMy father has made trouble for the country. See how my eyes brightened when I tasted a little of this honey. 30How much better if the troops had eaten today some of the plunder! Would not the slaughter of the Philistines have been even greater?" 31That day they struck down the Philistines from Michmash to Aijalon, and the troops were very weary.

32The troops pounced on the plunder and took sheep, cattle, and calves, and slaughtered them on the ground, and ate them with the blood. 33Then someone told Saul, "Look, the people are sinning against the LORD by eating meat with the blood." Saul said, "YouyouYou have broken faith. Roll a large stone over here before me." 34Then Saul said, "Go among the people and tell them, 'Each of you bring me your ox and your sheep, and slaughter them here and eat. Do not sin against the LORD by eating meat with the blood.'" So that night everyone brought his ox and slaughtered it there.

35Saul built an altar to the LORD; it was the first altar he built to the LORD.

Jonathan's Trial and Saul's Later Reign

36Saul said, "Let us go down after the Philistines by night and plunder them until dawn, and not leave one of them." They said, "Do whatever seems best to you." But the priest said, "Let us approach God here." 37Saul asked God, "Shall I go down after the Philistines? Will YouyouYou deliver them into Israel's hand?" But God did not answer him that day.

38Saul said, "Come here, all you leaders of the people, and find out how this sin happened today. 39As surely as the LORD lives, who saves Israel, even if the guilt lies with my son Jonathan, he must die." But no one said a word.

40Saul said to all Israel, "YouyouYou stand over there; I and my son Jonathan will stand over here." The people said to Saul, "Do what seems good to you." 41Then Saul prayed to the LORD, the God of Israel, "Give a clear answer." Jonathan and Saul were chosen by lot, and the people went free.

42Saul said, "Cast the lot between me and my son Jonathan." And Jonathan was chosen. 43Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you have done." Jonathan replied, "I tasted a little honey with the end of the staff in my hand. And now I must die?" 44Saul said, "May God deal with me, be it ever so severely, if you do not die, Jonathan."

45But the people said to Saul, "Should Jonathan die—he who brought about this great deliverance in Israel? Never! As surely as the LORD lives, not a hair of his head will fall to the ground, because he did this with God's help." So the people rescued Jonathan, and he did not die.

46Then Saul stopped pursuing the Philistines, and the Philistines went to their own land. 47After Saul had assumed kingship over Israel, he fought against all his enemies on every side: Moab, the Ammonites, Edom, the kings of Zobah, and the Philistines. Wherever he turned, he inflicted punishment. 48He fought valiantly and defeated the Amalekites, and delivered Israel from the hands of those who had plundered them.

49Saul's sons were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malki-Shua. The names of his two daughters were Merab the older and Michal the younger. 50Saul's wife was Ahinoam daughter of Ahimaaz. The name of the commander of his army was Abner son of Ner, whose father was Saul's uncle. 51Saul's father was Kish, and Abner's father Ner was the son of Abiel. 52There was fierce war with the Philistines throughout all the days of Saul, and whenever Saul saw any mighty man or warrior, he took him into his service.
