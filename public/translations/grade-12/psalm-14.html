The Folly of Denying God

1For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They have corrupted their conduct; their deeds are abominable; no one does what is good.

2The LORD looks down from Heavenheavenheaven upon the children of men to see whether any understand, whether any seek God. 3They have all turned aside; together they have become corrupt. No one does good—not even one.

4Do all the evildoers lack understanding? They devour my people as though eating bread, and they do not call upon the LORD.

5There they are, overwhelmed with dread, for God is with the generation of the righteous. 6You have frustrated the plans of the poor, but the LORD is their refuge.

7Oh, that salvation for Israel would come out of Zion! When the LORD restores the fortunes of HishisHis people, let Jacob rejoice and Israel be glad.
