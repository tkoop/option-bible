The Righteous Taken Away

1The righteous perish, and no one takes it to heart; faithful people are gathered away, with no one understanding that the righteous are taken away from the presence of evil. 2Those who walk uprightly enter into peace; they find rest in death.

Israel's Idolatry Condemned

3Draw near, you sons of a sorceress, offspring of an adulterer and a prostitute. 4Against whom do you mock? Against whom do you open your mouth and lengthen your tongue? Are you not children of transgression, offspring of deceit? 5You who burn with lust among the oaks and under every green tree, who slaughter your children in the valleys and under the clefts of the rocks.

6Your portion is among the smooth stones of the valley; they, they are your lot. To them you have poured out a drink offering; you have offered a grain offering. Shall I relent because of these? 7On a high and lofty hill you set your bed, and there you went up to offer sacrifices. 8Behind the door and the doorpost you set up your memorial. Forsaking MemeMe, you uncovered yourself; you climbed into it and opened it wide. You made a pact with those whose bed you loved, and you looked on their nakedness.

9You went to Molech with oil and increased your perfumes. You sent your envoys far away, and you descended to Sheol itself. 10You were wearied by your many ways, yet you did not say, "It is hopeless." You found renewal of your strength; therefore you did not faint.

11And of whom have you been afraid and in awe, that you lie and have not remembered MemeMe, nor laid it to your heart? Have I not been silent from of old, and you do not fear MemeMe? 12I will declare your righteousness and your works, but they will not profit you. 13When you cry out, let your gathered idols deliver you; but the wind will carry them all away, and a mere breath will sweep them away. But he who takes refuge in MemeMe will possess the land and inherit MymyMy holy mountain.

A Call to Prepare the Way

14And it will be said: "Build up, build up, prepare the way, remove the stumbling block from the path of MymyMy people."

The High and Holy One Who Heals

15For thus says the HighhighHigh and Exalted OneoneOne, who inhabits eternity, whose name is HolyholyHoly: "I dwell in the high and holy place, and also with the contrite and lowly in spirit, to revive the spirit of the lowly and to revive the heart of the contrite."

16For I will not contend forever, nor will I always be angry; for then the spirit of man would grow faint before MemeMe, and the breath of life that I have made. 17Because of his sinful gain I was angry and struck him; I hid MymyMy face in anger, yet he kept on in the way of his heart. 18I have seen his ways, but I will heal him; I will guide him and restore comfort to him and to his mourners.

19I create praise on their lips: "Peace, peace to the far and to the near," says the LORD, "and I will heal him."

No Peace for the Wicked

20But the wicked are like the tossing sea, which cannot rest, whose waves cast up mire and mud. 21"There is no peace," says my God, "for the wicked."
