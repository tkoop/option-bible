Solomon’s Construction and Fortification of Cities

1At the conclusion of twenty years—during which Solomon had constructed the house of the LORD and his own palace— 2Solomon rebuilt the cities that Huram had given him and settled the Israelites in them. 3He then campaigned against Hamath-zobah and subdued it. 4He built Tadmor in the wilderness, together with all the store cities he had established in Hamath. 5He fortified Upper Beth-horon and LowerlowerLower Beth-horon, equipping them with walls, gates, and bars. 6He also rebuilt Baalath and all the store cities belonging to Solomon, along with the chariot cities, the cavalry cities, and every other city he desired to build in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his dominion.

Conscripted Peoples and Royal Administration

7As for all the people who remained of the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—peoples not of Israelite descent— 8namely, their descendants who remained in the land after them, whom the Israelites had not wholly destroyed—Solomon conscripted them into forced labourlabourlabor, and they remain so to this day. 9Yet Solomon did not reduce any Israelite to slave labourlabourlabor for his projects, because they were warriors, officers of his elite guard, and commanders of his chariots and cavalry. 10These were the two hundred fifty chief officers who supervised the people under King Solomon. 11Solomon brought Pharaoh’s daughter up from the CitycityCity of David to the house he had built for her, for he said, “My wife shall not live in the house of David, king of Israel, because the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy.”

Worship, Priestly Divisions, and Completion

12Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of the LORD that he had built before the portico. 13He offered them according to the daily requirement, as Moses had commanded, for the Sabbaths, the NewnewNew MoonsmoonsMoons, and the three annual feasts—the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread, the FeastfeastFeast of WeeksweeksWeeks, and the FeastfeastFeast of Tabernacles. 14In accordance with the ordinance of his father David, he appointed the divisions of the priests for their service, the Levites for their duties of praise and ministry before the priests according to each day’s requirement, and the gatekeepers by their divisions at each gate; for this was the command of David, the man of God. 15They did not depart from the king’s command in any matter concerning the priests and Levites, including the treasuries. 16Thus all of Solomon’s work was accomplished, from the day the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid until it was completed. So the house of the LORD was finished.

The Voyage to Ophir

17Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth on the seacoast in the land of Edom. 18Huram sent him ships by the hand of his servants, along with servants who knew the sea; they sailed with Solomon’s servants to Ophir and brought back four hundred fifty talents of gold, which they delivered to King Solomon.
