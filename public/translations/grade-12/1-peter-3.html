Wives and Husbands in Godly Conduct

1Wives, likewise, be subject to your own husbands, so that even if some disobey the word, they may be won without a word by the conduct of the wives. 2They will be won when they observe your pure and reverent conduct.

3Your adornment must not be merely external—braiding the hair, wearing gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry, or putting on fine clothing. 4Rather, let it be the hidden person of the heart, expressed in the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is of great worth in the sight of God.

5For this is how the holy women of former times adorned themselves: they placed their hope in God and were subject to their own husbands. 6Sarah, for example, obeyed Abraham and called him lord. You have become her children if you do what is right and do not give way to fear.

7Husbands, in the same way, live with your wives in an understanding way, as with a weaker vessel, and show them honourhonourhonor as fellow heirs of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered.

Living in Harmony and Love

8Finally, all of you, be like-minded, sympathetic, loving as brothers, tenderhearted, and humble. 9Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult, but with blessing, because to this you were called, so that you may inherit a blessing.

10For, “Whoever would love life and see good days must keep his tongue from evil and his lips from deceitful speech. 11He must turn from evil and do good; he must seek peace and pursue it. 12For the eyes of the the LORD are on the righteous, and HishisHis ears are attentive to their prayer, but the face of the the LORD is against those who do evil.”

Suffering for Righteousness

13Who is going to harm you if you are eager to do what is good? 14But even if you should suffer for what is right, you are blessed. Do not fear their threats; do not be frightened.

15But in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord. Always be prepared to give an answer to everyone who asks you to give the reason for the hope that is in you, yet do it with gentleness and respect. 16Keep a clear conscience, so that those who malign your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame.

17For it is better, if it is God’s will, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil.

Christ’s Suffering and Triumph

18For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God. He was put to death in the flesh but made alive in the SpiritspiritSpirit.
