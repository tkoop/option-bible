The Vision of a Basket of Summer Fruit

1Thus the Lord the LORD showed me, and behold, a basket of summer fruit.

2And HeheHe said, “Amos, what do you see?”

And I said, “A basket of summer fruit.”

Then the the LORD said to me, “The end has come upon MymyMy people Israel; I will no longer spare them.”

The LORD Condemns Those Who Exploit the Poor

3“In that day,” declares the Lord the LORD, “the songs of the temple will become wailing. Many will be the corpses, strewn everywhere in silence. 4Hear this, you who trample the needy and do away with the humble of the land, 5saying,

“‘When will the NewnewNew MoonmoonMoon be over so we can sell grain, and the Sabbath so we can market wheat? We will reduce the ephah and increase the shekel; we will cheat with dishonest scales,

6“‘buying the poor with silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, and selling the sweepings of the wheat.’”

The Day of JudgmentJudgementJudgment and Mourning

7The the LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob: “I will never forget any of their deeds.”

8Will not the land tremble for this, and all who dwell in it mourn? All of it will rise like the Nile; it will surge and subside like the Nile of Egypt.

9“And in that day,” declares the Lord the LORD, “I will make the sun go down at noon and darken the earth in broad daylight. 10I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into lamentation; I will put sackcloth on every waist and baldness on every head. I will make it like the mourning for an only son, and its end like a bitter day.”

A Famine of the LORD’s Word and the Fall of Idolaters

11“Behold, the days are coming,” declares the Lord the LORD, “when I will send a famine on the land—not a famine of bread or a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the words of the the LORD. 12People will stagger from sea to sea and wander from north to east, seeking the word of the the LORD, but they will not find it. 13In that day the beautiful virgins and young men will faint from thirst. 14Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria and say,

“‘As surely as your god lives, O Dan,’ or, ‘As surely as the way of Beersheba lives’—

“they will fall and never rise again.”
