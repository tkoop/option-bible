The Peace Offering from the Herd

1If his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, and he brings an animal from the herd—whether male or female—he must present it without blemish before the LORD. 2He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting; then Aaron’s sons, the priests, shall sprinkle the blood all around the altar. 3From the sacrifice of peace offerings he shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the entrails, together with all the fat that is on the entrails. 4He shall also remove the two kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the fatty lobe attached to the liver, removing it together with the kidneys. 5Aaron’s sons shall burn these on the altar on top of the burnt offering that lies on the wood over the fire, as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

The Peace Offering from the Flock

6If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD is from the flock, he must present a male or female without blemish. 7If he presents a lamb as his offering, he must bring it before the LORD. 8He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting; then Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle its blood all around the altar. 9From the peace offering he shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD: its fat, the entire fat tail cut off close to the backbone, the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat that is on the entrails. 10He shall also remove the two kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, removing it together with the kidneys. 11The priest shall burn them on the altar as food, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

The Peace Offering of a Goat

12If his offering is a goat, he must present it before the LORD. 13He must lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting; then Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle its blood all around the altar. 14From his offering he shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails, 15the two kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys. 16The priest shall burn them on the altar as food, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma. All the fat belongs to the LORD. 17This is a permanent statute for your generations, in all your dwellings: You must not eat any fat or any blood.
