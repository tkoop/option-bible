The Law for Cleansing a Leper

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“This is the law for the leper on the day of his cleansing: he shall be brought to the priest. 3The priest shall go outside the camp and examine him; and if the leprous disease has been healed in the leper, 4the priest shall command that two clean living birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop be taken for the one who is to be cleansed.

5“The priest shall command that one bird be killed in a clay vessel over fresh water. 6As for the living bird, he shall take it together with the cedar wood, the scarlet yarn, and the hyssop, and dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that was killed over the fresh water. 7He shall sprinkle the one to be cleansed from the leprosy seven times, pronounce him clean, and release the living bird into the open field.

8“The one to be cleansed shall wash his clothes, shave off all his hair, and bathe in water, so that he may be clean; afterward he shall enter the camp, yet he shall remain outside his tent for seven days. 9On the seventh day he shall shave all the hair from his head, his beard, and his eyebrows—indeed, all his hair he shall shave off; he shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he shall be clean.

Offerings for the Cleansed

10“On the eighth day he shall take two unblemished male lambs, one unblemished ewe lamb a year old, three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and one log of oil. 11The priest who cleanses him shall present the man to be cleansed, together with these offerings, before the LORD at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 12The priest shall take one male lamb and offer it as a guilt offering, along with the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. 13He shall slaughter the lamb in the place where the sin offering and the burnt offering are slaughtered, in the holy place; for, like the sin offering, the guilt offering belongs to the priest and is most holy.

14“The priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot. 15The priest shall take some of the log of oil and pour it into the palm of his own left hand. 16The priest shall dip his right finger into the oil in his left hand and sprinkle some of the oil with his finger seven times before the LORD. 17From the remaining oil in his palm, the priest shall put some on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, over the blood of the guilt offering. 18The priest shall pour the rest of the oil in his hand upon the head of the one to be cleansed, and the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD.

19“The priest shall offer the sin offering and make atonement for the one to be cleansed from his uncleanness; afterward he shall slaughter the burnt offering. 20The priest shall offer the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar, and the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be clean.

A Concession for the Poor

21“If he is poor and cannot afford these, he shall take one male lamb for a guilt offering to be waved, to make atonement for him, one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and a log of oil. 22He shall also take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford, one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering.

23“On the eighth day he shall bring them to the priest for his cleansing, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, before the LORD. 24The priest shall take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

25“He shall slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering, and the priest shall take some of its blood and put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot. 26The priest shall pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand. 27With his right finger the priest shall sprinkle some of the oil in his left hand seven times before the LORD. 28The priest shall put some of the oil in his hand on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, on the place of the blood of the guilt offering. 29The rest of the oil in the priest’s hand he shall put on the head of the one to be cleansed, to make atonement for him before the LORD.

30“He shall offer one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford— 31whichever he is able to obtain—one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering, together with the grain offering; and the priest shall make atonement for the one to be cleansed before the LORD. 32This is the law for the one who has a leprous disease, when he cannot afford the means of his cleansing.”

Leprosy in a House

33The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 34“When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as a possession, and I inflict a leprous disease on a house in the land of your possession,

35“The owner of the house shall come and tell the priest that he has seen something resembling a plague in the house. 36The priest shall command that the house be emptied before he enters to examine the plague, so that all that is in the house may not be made unclean; afterward the priest shall enter to examine the house.

37“He shall examine the plague, and if the plague is in the walls of the house with greenish or reddish depressions that appear deeper than the wall, 38the priest shall go out of the house to its doorway and quarantine the house for seven days. 39On the seventh day the priest shall return and examine it; and if the plague has spread in the walls of the house,

40“the priest shall command that the stones in which the plague is found be removed and cast into an unclean place outside the city. 41He shall have the house scraped inside all around, and the plaster that is scraped off shall be poured out into an unclean place outside the city. 42They shall take other stones and put them in the place of the removed stones, and he shall take other mortar and plaster the house.

43“If the plague returns and breaks out in the house after the stones have been removed and the house has been scraped and plastered, 44the priest shall come and examine it; and if the plague has spread in the house, it is a malignant leprous disease in the house; it is unclean. 45He shall tear down the house—its stones, its timber, and all its plaster—and carry them out of the city to an unclean place.

46“Moreover, whoever enters the house during all the days it is quarantined shall be unclean until evening. 47The one who lies down in the house shall wash his clothes, and the one who eats in the house shall wash his clothes. 48If the priest comes in and examines it and finds that the plague has not spread in the house after it was plastered, the priest shall pronounce the house clean, because the plague is healed.

49“He shall take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop to cleanse the house. 50He shall kill one bird in a clay vessel over fresh water. 51He shall take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn, and the living bird, dip them in the blood of the slain bird and in the fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times. 52He shall cleanse the house with the blood of the bird, the fresh water, the living bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop, and the scarlet yarn. 53He shall release the living bird outside the city into the open field; thus he shall make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

54“This is the law for every kind of leprous disease and scall, 55for leprosy in a garment or in a house, 56and for a swelling, a rash, or a bright spot, 57to teach when it is unclean and when it is clean. This is the law of leprosy.”
