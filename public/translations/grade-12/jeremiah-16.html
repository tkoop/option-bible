The Prophetic Sign of Jeremiah's Celibacy

1The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 2“You shall not take a wife for yourself, nor shall you have sons and daughters in this place.”

3For this is what the the LORD says concerning the sons and daughters born in this place, and concerning their mothers who bore them and their fathers who begot them in this land:

4“They shall die of deadly diseases. They shall not be lamented or buried; they shall be as dung on the surface of the ground. They shall fall by sword and famine, and their corpses shall be food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth.”

Forbidden Mourning and Feasting

5For this is what the the LORD says: “Do not enter a house where there is a funeral meal. Do not go to lament, nor show sympathy, because I have withdrawn MymyMy peace from this people,” declares the the LORD, “along with MymyMy loving devotion and compassion.”

6“Great and small shall die in this land. They shall not be buried, and no one shall mourn for them, nor shall anyone cut himself or shave his head for them. 7No food will be offered to comfort those who mourn the dead; not even a cup of consolation will be given for the loss of a father or mother. 8You shall not enter a house of feasting to sit with them, to eat and drink.”

9For this is what the the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “I will put an end in this place, before your eyes and in your days, to the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.”

Exile, Restoration, and Divine Recompense

10And when you tell this people all these words, and they say to you, “Why has the the LORD pronounced all this great evil against us? What is our iniquity? What is our sin that we have committed against the the LORD our God?”

11Then you shall say to them, “Because your fathers have forsaken MemeMe, declares the the LORD, and have walked after other gods and served them and worshiped them, and have forsaken MemeMe and have not kept MymyMy law. 12And you have done worse than your fathers, for behold, every one of you walks after the stubbornness of his evil heart, so as not to listen to MemeMe.”

13“Therefore I will cast you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you shall serve other gods day and night, because I will show you no favourfavourfavor.”

14“Therefore, behold, days are coming,” declares the the LORD, “when it shall no longer be said, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt,’ 15but, ‘As the the LORD lives who brought up the children of Israel from the land of the north and from all the lands where HeheHe had driven them.’ And I will bring them back to their land that I gave to their fathers.”

16“Behold, I am sending for many fishermen,” declares the the LORD, “and they shall fish for them; and afterward I will send for many hunters, and they shall hunt them from every mountain and from every hill and out of the clefts of the rocks.”

17“For MymyMy eyes are on all their ways; they are not hidden from MemeMe, and their iniquity is not concealed from MymyMy eyes. 18And I will first repay double for their iniquity and their sin, because they have profaned MymyMy land; they have filled MymyMy inheritance with the carcasses of their detestable things and their abominations.”

The Nations' Confession and God's Final Vindication
