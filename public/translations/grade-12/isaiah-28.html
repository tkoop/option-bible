Woe to Ephraim’s Fading Crown

1Woe to the majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards, to the fading flower of his glorious splendoursplendoursplendor, to the city on the summit of the fertile valley, to those overcome by wine! 2Behold, the Lord has a strong and mighty one. Like a hailstorm, like a destructive tempest, like a flooding downpour, HeheHe will hurl it to the ground with HishisHis hand. 3The majestic crown of Ephraim’s drunkards will be trampled underfoot. 4The fading flower of his glorious splendoursplendoursplendor, on the summit of the fertile valley, will be like a ripe fig before the harvest: the moment someone sees it, he devours it while it still rests in his hand.

5On that day the LORD of Hosts will be a crown of glory and a diadem of beauty to the remnant of HishisHis people, 6a spirit of justice to him who sits in judgmentjudgementjudgment, a strength to those who turn back the battle at the gate.

Drunken Leaders and a Hardened People

7These also stagger from wine and reel from strong drink: priest and prophet stagger from strong drink and are befuddled by wine. They reel from strong drink; they err in vision, they stumble in judgmentjudgementjudgment. 8For all their tables are covered with vomit; there is not a spot without filth.

9Whom will HeheHe teach knowledge, and whom will HeheHe instruct in the message? Those just weaned from milk, those newly removed from the breast? 10For they say, “Order on order, order on order, line on line, line on line, a little here, a little there.”

11Indeed, with mocking lips and foreign tongue HeheHe will speak to this people, 12to whom HeheHe has said, “This is the place of rest, let the weary rest; this is the place of repose.” But they would not listen.

13So the word of the the LORD will come to them: “Order on order, order on order, line on line, line on line, a little here, a little there,” so that they may go and stumble backward, be broken, snared, and captured.

The Scoffers’ False Covenant

14Therefore hear the word of the the LORD, you scoffers who rule this people in Jerusalem. 15For you have said, “We have made a covenant with death, and an agreement with Sheol. When the overwhelming scourge passes through, it will not touch us, because we have made lies our refuge and falsehood our hiding place.”

16Therefore this is what the Lord the the LORD says: “See, I lay a stone in Zion, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation. The one who believes will never be shaken. 17I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the plumb line. Hail will sweep away the refuge of lies, and water will flood the hiding place. 18Your covenant with death will be dissolved, and your agreement with Sheol will not stand. When the overwhelming scourge passes through, you will be trampled. 19As often as it passes through, it will carry you away. Morning by morning it will pass through, by day and by night. Sheer terror will come from understanding the message. 20Indeed, the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the blanket too small to wrap around you.”

21For the the LORD will rise up as HeheHe did at Mount Perazim; HeheHe will rouse HimselfhimselfHimself as in the ValleyvalleyValley of Gibeon, to do HishisHis work, HishisHis strange work, and to perform HishisHis task, HishisHis unusual task.

22So now, do not mock, or your bonds will become stronger. Indeed, I have heard from the Lord the the LORD of Hosts a decree of destruction against the whole land.

The Farmer’s Patient Wisdom

23Listen and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my words. 24Does the plowman plow all day to sow? Does he continually loosen and break up the soil? 25When he has levelledlevelledleveled its surface, does he not sow caraway and scatter cumin? Does he not plant wheat in rows and barley in plots, and rye along the border? 26For his God instructs him and teaches him properly.

27For caraway is not threshed with a sledge, nor is a cart wheel rolled over cumin. But caraway is beaten out with a stick, and cumin with a rod. 28Grain is crushed, but not without limit; though he drives the wheels of his cart over it, his horses do not crush it. 29This also comes from the the LORD of Hosts; HeheHe is wonderful in counsel and great in wisdom.
