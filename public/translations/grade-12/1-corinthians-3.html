Spiritual Infancy and Divisions in the Church

1Brothers, I was unable to address you as spiritual people, but as fleshly, as infants in Christ. 2I gave you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet able to receive it; indeed, even now you are not able. 3For you are still fleshly. For where there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not fleshly, walking according to human standards?

4For when one says, "I am of Paul," are you not merely human?

And when another says, "I am of Apollos," are you not equally mere humans?

Servants of God and the Growth of His Church

5What, then, is Apollos? And what is Paul? They are servants through whom you believed, as the Lord assigned to each. 6I planted, Apollos watered, but God caused the growth. 7So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but God, who gives the growth. 8The one who plants and the one who waters are one, and each will receive his own reward according to his own labourlabourlabor. 9For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's cultivated field, God's building.

Building on the Foundation of Christ

10According to the grace of God given to me, like a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds upon it. But let each one be careful how he builds. 11For no one can lay a foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ. 12Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw, 13each one's work will be made manifest, for the DaydayDay will disclose it, because it is revealed in fire, and the fire will test the quality of each one's work. 14If the work that anyone has built survives, he will receive a reward. 15If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; he himself, however, will be saved, yet only as through fire.

God's Temple and the Folly of Worldly Wisdom

16Do you not know that you are God's sanctuary and that God's SpiritspiritSpirit dwells in you? 17If anyone destroys God's sanctuary, God will destroy him. For God's sanctuary is holy, and you are that sanctuary. 18Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you considers himself wise in this age, let him become a fool so that he may become wise.

19For the wisdom of this world is folly in God's sight. For it is written, "HeheHe catches the wise in their craftiness."

20And again, "ThetheThe Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile."

21So let no one boast in human beings. For all things are yours, 22whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come—all are yours, 23and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.
