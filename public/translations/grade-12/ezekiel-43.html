The Glory of the LORD Returns to the Temple

1Then he brought me to the gate—the gate facing east. 2And there was the glory of the God of Israel, coming from the east. His voice sounded like the roar of many waters, and the earth was radiant with HishisHis glory. 3The vision I saw was like the vision I had seen when HeheHe came to destroy the city, and like the vision I had seen by the River Chebar; and I fell on my face.

4The glory of the LORD entered the temple by the gate facing east. 5Then the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and the glory of the LORD filled the temple. 6While the man stood beside me, I heard someone speaking to me from the temple.

The LORD’s Holy Dwelling and Israel’s Defilement

7“Son of man, this is the place of MymyMy throne and the place for the soles of MymyMy feet, where I will dwell among the Israelites forever. The house of Israel and their kings must no longer defile MymyMy holy name by their prostitution or by the carcasses of their kings at their high places. 8When they set their threshold beside MymyMy threshold and their doorposts beside MymyMy doorposts, with only a wall between MemeMe and them, they defiled MymyMy holy name by the abominations they committed; so I consumed them in MymyMy anger. 9Now let them put their prostitution and the carcasses of their kings far from MemeMe, and I will dwell among them forever.

10“As for you, SonsonSon of man, describe the temple to the house of Israel, so that they may be ashamed of their iniquities. Let them measure the plan. 11And if they are ashamed of all they have done, make known to them the design of the temple—its arrangement, its exits and entrances, its entire design, and all its statutes, forms, and laws. Write it down before their eyes, so that they may keep its entire design and all its statutes and carry them out.”

12This is the law of the temple: The whole area all around the top of the mountain shall be most holy. Behold, this is the law of the temple.

Measurements of the Altar

13And these are the measurements of the altar in cubits (the cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth): The gutter shall be a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span around its edge. And this is the height of the altar. 14From the base on the ground to the lower ledge shall be two cubits, and the width one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge shall be four cubits, and the width one cubit. 15The altar hearth shall be four cubits high, and four horns shall project upward from the hearth. 16The altar hearth shall be square, twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide. 17The ledge shall be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide on all four sides, with a rim of half a cubit around it and a gutter of one cubit around it. Its steps shall face east.

Consecration of the Altar

18Then HeheHe said to me, “Son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: These are the statutes of the altar on the day it is built, for offering burnt offerings on it and for sprinkling blood on it. 19You shall give a young bull as a sin offering to the Levitical priests of the descendants of Zadok, who approach MemeMe to minister to MemeMe—declares the Lord GOD. 20You shall take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge, and all around the rim. Thus you shall purify it and make atonement for it. 21You shall take the bull of the sin offering and burn it in the appointed place of the temple, outside the sanctuary.

22“On the second day you shall offer a male goat without blemish for a sin offering, and they shall purify the altar as they purified it with the bull. 23When you have finished purifying it, you shall offer a young bull without blemish and a ram without blemish from the flock. 24You shall present them before the LORD, and the priests shall throw salt on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD. 25For seven days you shall prepare a goat for a sin offering each day, along with a young bull and a ram from the flock, both without blemish. 26For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it and consecrate it. 27And when these days are completed, from the eighth day onward the priests shall offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings on the altar, and I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD.”
