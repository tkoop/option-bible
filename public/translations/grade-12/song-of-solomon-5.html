The Bridegroom's Garden and Invitation

1"I have entered my garden, my sister, my bride; I have gathered my myrrh amid my spices; I have eaten my honeycomb with its honey; I have drunk my wine with its milk. Eat, O friends; drink, and be drunk with love, O beloved ones."

The Bride's Nightlong Search

2I was slumbering, yet my heart kept watch. Listen! My beloved knocks, saying, "Open to me, my sister, my darling, my dove, my flawless one, for my head is drenched with dew, my locks with the dampness of night."

3I have taken off my robe; how could I put it on again? I have washed my feet; how could I soil them? 4My beloved thrust his hand through the opening, and my heart was stirred for him.

5I arose to open to my beloved, and my hands dripped with myrrh, my fingers with flowing myrrh, upon the handles of the bolt. 6I opened to my beloved, but my beloved had turned and gone. My soul failed me when he spoke. I sought him, but I did not find him; I called him, but he did not answer. 7The watchmen who patrol the city found me; they struck me and wounded me; the keepers of the walls took my veil from me.

The Daughters' Question and the Bride's Praise

8"I adjure you, daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my beloved, tell him that I am sick with love."

9"What makes your beloved better than another beloved, O most beautiful among women? What makes your beloved better than another beloved, that you so charge us?"

10"My beloved is radiant and ruddy, distinguished among ten thousand. 11His head is like pure gold; his locks are wavy, black as a raven. 12His eyes are like doves beside the watercourses, washed in milk, and set like jewels. 13His cheeks are like beds of spices, towers of perfumes; his lips are lilies, dripping liquid myrrh. 14His hands are rods of gold set with topaz; his body is ivory work adorned with sapphires. 15His legs are pillars of marble set on bases of fine gold; his stature is like Lebanon, choice as the cedars. 16His mouth is sweetness itself, and he is altogether desirable. This is my beloved, and this is my friend, O daughters of Jerusalem."
