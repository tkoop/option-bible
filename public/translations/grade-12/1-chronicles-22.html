David Prepares for the Temple

1Then David said, “This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel.” 2So David gave orders to gather the foreigners residing in the land of Israel, and he appointed stonecutters to cut dressed stone for building the house of God.

3David also provided a great quantity of iron for nails for the doors of the gates and for the clamps, and bronze without weight, 4along with cedar logs without number, for the Sidonians and Tyrians had brought a great quantity of cedar logs to David.

5David said, “My son Solomon is young and inexperienced, and the house to be built for the LORD must be exceedingly magnificent, famous and glorious throughout all lands. Therefore I will make preparations for it.” So David made extensive preparations before his death.

David Commissions Solomon

6Then he called for his son Solomon and charged him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.
