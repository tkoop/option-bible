The LORD’s Command to Destroy the Nations of Canaan

1When the LORD your God brings you into the land you are entering to possess, HeheHe will clear away many nations before you—the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—seven nations more numerous and mightier than you. 2When the LORD your God delivers them over to you, you shall defeat them and devote them to complete destruction. You shall make no covenant with them and show them no mercy. 3You shall not intermarry with them, neither giving your daughter to one of their sons nor taking one of their daughters for your son. 4For he would turn your son away from following MemeMe to serve other gods. Then the anger of the LORD would burn against you, and HeheHe would swiftly destroy you. 5Instead, this is what you shall do to them: tear down their altars, smash their sacred pillars, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved images in the fire.

Chosen as the LORD’s Treasured People

6For you are a holy people to the LORD your God; the LORD your God has chosen you to be a people for HishisHis treasured possession out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. 7It was not because you were more numerous than any other people that the LORD set HishisHis love on you and chose you, for you were the fewest of all peoples. 8Rather, it is because the LORD loves you and keeps the oath HeheHe swore to your fathers that the LORD brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt. 9Know therefore that the LORD your God is God, the faithful God who keeps covenant and steadfast love with those who love HimhimHim and keep HishisHis commandments, to the thousandth generation, 10but who repays those who hate HimhimHim to their face by destroying them. He will not delay in repaying the one who hates HimhimHim; HeheHe will repay him to his face. 11You shall therefore be careful to do the commandment, the statutes, and the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments that I am commanding you today.

Blessings for Obedience

12And because you listen to these judgmentsjudgementsjudgments and keep and do them, the LORD your God will keep with you the covenant and the steadfast love that HeheHe swore to your fathers. 13He will love you, bless you, and multiply you; HeheHe will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground—your grain, your wine, and your oil—the increase of your herds and the young of your flock, in the land HeheHe swore to your fathers to give you. 14You shall be blessed above all peoples; there shall be no barren man or woman among you, nor among your livestock. 15The LORD will remove from you all sickness and will not put upon you any of the evil diseases of Egypt that you knew; instead, HeheHe will give them to all who hate you. 16You shall consume all the peoples that the LORD your God gives you; your eye shall not pity them, nor shall you serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you.

Encouragement Against Fear and Gradual Conquest

17If you say in your heart, “These nations are greater than I; how can I dispossess them?” 18You shall not be afraid of them, but you shall remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt— 19the great trials your eyes saw, the signs and wonders, the mighty hand and outstretched arm by which the LORD your God brought you out. So will the LORD your God do to all the peoples of whom you are afraid. 20Moreover, the LORD your God will send the hornet among them until those left and hiding from you are destroyed. 21You shall not be in dread of them, for the LORD your God is in your midst, a great and awesome God. 22The LORD your God will clear away these nations before you little by little; you cannot destroy them all at once, lest the wild animals of the field multiply against you. 23But the LORD your God will give them over to you and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed. 24He will give their kings into your hand, and you shall destroy their name from under Heavenheavenheaven; no one will be able to stand against you until you have destroyed them. 25You shall burn the carved images of their gods in the fire; you shall not covet the silver or gold on them or take it for yourself, lest you be ensnared by it, for it is an abomination to the LORD your God. 26You shall not bring an abomination into your house, or you will become devoted to destruction like it. You shall utterly detest and abhor it, for it is devoted to destruction.
