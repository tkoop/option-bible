The Parable of the Two Eagles and the Vine

1The word of the LORD came to me: 2"Son of man, set forth a riddle and tell a parable to the house of Israel.

3"Say: 'Thus says the Lord, the LORD: A great eagle with great wings and long pinions, full of plumage of many colourscolourscolors, came to Lebanon and took the top of the cedar. 4He broke off its topmost shoot and carried it to a land of merchants, and he set it in a city of traders. 5He took some of the seed of the land and planted it in fertile soil; he placed it beside abundant waters and set it out like a willow. 6It sprouted and became a low, spreading vine, its branches turning toward him, while its roots remained beneath it. So it became a vine and sent out branches and put forth shoots. 7But there was another great eagle with great wings and abundant plumage. Behold, this vine bent its roots toward him and sent out its branches toward him, away from the bed where it had been planted, that he might water it. 8It had been planted in good soil beside abundant waters, so that it might bear branches and produce fruit and become a splendid vine.'

9"Say: 'Thus says the Lord, the LORD: Will it prosper? Will he not pull up its roots and strip off its fruit, so that it withers—so that all its new growth withers? It will not take a strong arm or many people to pull it from its roots. 10Behold, it is planted; will it prosper? When the east wind strikes it, will it not utterly wither? It will wither on the beds where it grew.'"

The Interpretation of the Riddle

11Then the word of the LORD came to me: 12"Say now to the rebellious house, 'Do you not know what these things mean? Say: Behold, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took its king and its princes and brought them to himself in Babylon.'

13"HeheHe took one of the royal offspring and made a covenant with him, putting him under oath. He also took away the leading men of the land, 14so that the kingdom might be brought low and not raise itself up, but keep his covenant in order to stand.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Rebellious King

15"But he rebelled against him by sending envoys to Egypt to obtain horses and a large army. Will he succeed? Will he escape who does such things? Can he break the covenant and yet escape? 16As I live, declares the Lord, the LORD, surely in the place of the king who made him king—whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke—in Babylon he shall die. 17Pharaoh with his mighty army and great company will not help him in war, when siege mounds are raised and siege walls built to cut off many lives. 18He despised the oath by breaking the covenant, and behold, he gave his hand in pledge and did all these things; he shall not escape.

Then you shall know that I am the LORD; I have spoken."

The LORD's Promise of a New Cedar

22"Thus says the Lord, the LORD: I MyselfmyselfMyself will take a sprig from the lofty top of the cedar and set it out. I will break off a tender shoot from the topmost of its young twigs and plant it on a high and lofty mountain. 23On the mountain height of Israel I will plant it, that it may bear branches and produce fruit and become a noble cedar. Under it every kind of bird will dwell; in the shade of its branches birds of every sort will nest. 24Then all the trees of the field shall know that I am the LORD. I bring down the high tree and exalt the low tree; I dry up the green tree and make the dry tree flourish. I am the LORD; I have spoken, and I will do it."
