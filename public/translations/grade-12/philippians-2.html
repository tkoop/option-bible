Encouragement to Unity and Humility

1Therefore, if there is any encouragement in Christ, any consolation afforded by love, any fellowship in the SpiritspiritSpirit, or any tender affection and compassion, 2then complete my joy by being of one mind, maintaining the same love, united in spirit, and intent upon a single purpose. 3Do nothing from selfish ambition or empty conceit; rather, in humility, regard one another as more significant than yourselves. 4Let each of you attend not only to your own interests, but also to the interests of others.

Christ's Humiliation and Exaltation

5Let this mind be in you that was also in Christ Jesus: 6who, existing in the form of God, did not regard equality with God as something to be grasped, 7but emptied HimselfhimselfHimself, taking the form of a servant and being made in the likeness of men. 8And being found in appearance as a man, HeheHe humbled HimselfhimselfHimself and became obedient to death—even death upon a cross. 9Therefore God exalted HimhimHim to the highest place and bestowed upon HimhimHim the name above every name, 10so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow—in Heavenheavenheaven, on EarthEarthearth, and under the earth— 11and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the FatherfatherFather.

Working Out Salvation with Reverence

12Therefore, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed—not only in my presence, but now much more in my absence—continue to work out your salvation with fear and trembling. 13For it is God who works in you, both to will and to work according to HishisHis good pleasure. 14Do all things without complaining or disputing, 15so that you may be blameless and pure, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as luminaries in the world, 16holding fast to the word of life, so that I may boast on the day of Christ that I neither ran in vain nor labouredlabouredlabored in vain. 17But even if I am poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I am glad and rejoice with all of you. 18Likewise, you too should be glad and rejoice with me.

Timothy and Epaphroditus

19Now I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you shortly, that I too may be cheered when I learn how you are doing. 20For I have no one else of kindred spirit who will genuinely care for your welfare. 21For all the others seek their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. 22But you know his proven worth, that as a son serves his father, so he served with me in the gospel. 23Therefore I hope to send him as soon as I see how matters turn out for me. 24And I am confident in the Lord that I myself will come shortly. 25Nevertheless, I considered it necessary to send back to you Epaphroditus—my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier—who is also your messenger and minister to my need. 26For he has been longing for all of you and is distressed because you heard that he was ill. 27Indeed he was ill, nearly to the point of death; but God had mercy on him, and not on him alone but also on me, so that I would not have sorrow upon sorrow. 28Therefore I am all the more eager to send him, so that when you see him again you may rejoice, and I may be less sorrowful. 29Receive him in the Lord with all joy, and hold such men in honourhonourhonor, 30because he came near to death for the work of Christ, risking his life to supply what was lacking in your service to me.
