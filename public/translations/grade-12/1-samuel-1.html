Elkanah's Family and the Annual Sacrifice

1There was a certain man from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite. 2He had two wives: one was named Hannah, and the other Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none. 3Year after year this man went up from his city to worship and sacrifice to the the LORD of hosts at Shiloh, where Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, served as priests of the the LORD. 4On the day of the sacrifice, Elkanah gave portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters. 5But to Hannah he gave a double portion, because he loved her, although the the LORD had closed her womb. 6Because the the LORD had closed her womb, her rival provoked her bitterly and vexed her. 7This happened year after year: whenever Hannah went up to the house of the the LORD, her rival provoked her, so she wept and would not eat.

8Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Hannah, why do you weep? Why do you not eat? Why is your heart sad? Am I not better to you than ten sons?"

Hannah's Prayer and Vow at Shiloh

9After they had eaten and drunk in Shiloh, Hannah rose. Now Eli the priest was sitting on the seat beside the doorpost of the temple of the the LORD. 10In bitterness of soul she prayed to the the LORD and wept bitterly.

11She made a vow, saying, "O the LORD of hosts, if YouyouYou will indeed look on the affliction of YouryourYour servant and remember me, and not forget YouryourYour servant, but give YouryourYour servant a son, then I will give him to the the LORD all the days of his life, and no razor shall touch his head."

12As she continued praying before the the LORD, Eli watched her mouth. 13Hannah was speaking in her heart; only her lips moved, but her voice was not heard. Therefore Eli thought she was drunk.

14Eli said to her, "How long will you go on being drunk? Put away your wine from you."

15Hannah answered, "No, my lord, I am a woman of sorrowful spirit. I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but I have poured out my soul before the the LORD. 16Do not regard your servant as a worthless woman, for out of my great anxiety and vexation I have been speaking until now."

17Eli answered, "Go in peace, and may the God of Israel grant your petition that you have made to HimhimHim."

18She said, "Let your servant find favourfavourfavor in your eyes." Then the woman went on her way and ate, and her face was no longer sad.

19They rose early in the morning and worshiped before the the LORD; then they returned and came to their house at Ramah. Elkanah was intimate with his wife Hannah, and the the LORD remembered her. 20In due time Hannah conceived and bore a son, and she called his name Samuel, saying, "I have asked him of the the LORD."

Samuel's Birth and Dedication to the the LORD

21The man Elkanah and all his household went up to offer the yearly sacrifice to the the LORD and to fulfillfulfilfulfill his vow. 22But Hannah did not go up, for she said to her husband, "I will not go up until the child is weaned; then I will bring him, so that he may appear before the the LORD and dwell there forever."

23Her husband Elkanah said to her, "Do what seems best to you; stay until you have weaned him. Only may the the LORD establish HishisHis word." So the woman stayed and nursed her son until she weaned him.

24When she had weaned him, she took him up with her, along with three bulls, an ephah of flour, and a skin of wine, and she brought him to the house of the the LORD at Shiloh, and the child was young. 25Then they slaughtered a bull and brought the child to Eli.

26She said, "As you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here in your presence praying to the the LORD. 27For this child I prayed, and the the LORD has granted me my petition. 28Therefore I have given him to the the LORD; as long as he lives, he is given to the the LORD." And he worshiped the the LORD there.
