A Psalm of David When He Fled from Absalom

1O the LORD, how numerous are my foes! Many rise up against me. 2Many say of me, "God will not deliver him." Selah.

3But YouyouYou, O the LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the OneoneOne who lifts my head. 4I cried aloud to the LORD, and HeheHe answered me from HishisHis holy hill. Selah.

5I lay down and slept; I awoke again, for the LORD sustained me. 6I will not fear ten thousands of people who have set themselves against me all around.

7Arise, O the LORD! Save me, O my God! For YouyouYou strike all my enemies on the cheek; YouyouYou break the teeth of the wicked. 8Salvation belongs to the LORD; YouryourYour blessing be on YouryourYour people! Selah.
