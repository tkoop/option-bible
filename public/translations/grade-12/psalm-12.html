A Plea for Deliverance from Deceitful Men

For the director of music; upon an eight-stringed harp—a psalm of David.

1Deliver, O the LORD, for the godly have disappeared; the faithful have vanished from among the children of men.

2They speak deception to one another, flattering with their lips while concealing duplicity in their hearts.

3May the LORD cut off all flattering lips and the tongue that speaks arrogantly— 4those who say, "With our tongues we will prevail; our lips are our own—who is master over us?"

5"Because of the oppression of the poor and the groaning of the needy, now I will arise," says the LORD, "I will establish him in the safety for which he longs."

6The words of the LORD are immaculate, like silver refined in a furnace upon the earth, purified sevenfold.

7You, the LORD, will keep them; YouyouYou will preserve them from this generation forever.

8The wicked prowl on every side when vileness is exalted among the children of men.
