A Hymn of Praise to the LORD

1Hallelujah! Praise the LORD, O my soul! 2I will praise the LORD throughout my life; I will sing praises to my God as long as I draw breath.

The Futility of Trusting Mortals

3Do not place your trust in princes, or in mortal man, who is powerless to save. 4When his spirit departs, he returns to the dust; on that very day his plans perish.

The Blessedness of Trusting the LORD

The LORD's Care for the Oppressed and His Eternal Reign

The LORD loves the righteous. 9The LORD protects the sojourner.
