Memorial Stones Commanded

1When the entire nation had completed its passage across the Jordan, the LORD addressed Joshua, saying, 2“Select twelve men from the people—one from each tribe—for yourselves, and command them, 3‘Take twelve stones from this place, from the midst of the Jordan, from the very spot where the priests’ feet stood firm; carry them with you and deposit them in the camp where you lodge tonight.’”

4Accordingly, Joshua summoned the twelve men whom he had designated from among the Israelites, one from each tribe, 5and said to them, “Cross before the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan, and each of you shall lift one stone onto his shoulder, corresponding to the tribes of the Israelites, 6so that this may serve as a sign among you. When your children ask tomorrow, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’ 7you shall tell them, ‘The waters of the Jordan were severed before the ark of the covenant of the LORD. When it crossed the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were severed. These stones shall remain a memorial for the Israelites forever.’”

8The Israelites acted precisely as Joshua had commanded. They lifted twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, one for each tribe of the Israelites, just as the LORD had instructed Joshua, and they carried them to the camp and set them down there. 9Joshua also erected twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, at the place where the priests who bore the ark of the covenant had stood; and they remain there to this day.

The Crossing Completed

10The priests who bore the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until the LORD had completed through Joshua everything HeheHe had commanded him to speak to the people, in accordance with all that Moses had commanded Joshua. Then the people hastened across. 11As soon as all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the LORD and the priests crossed over before them. 12The Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over before the Israelites, armed for battle, just as Moses had spoken to them— 13about forty thousand equipped for war passed before the LORD toward the plains of Jericho. 14On that day the LORD exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they revered him as they had revered Moses throughout all the days of his life.

15Then the LORD said to Joshua, 16“Command the priests who carry the ark of the testimony to come up from the Jordan.”

17So Joshua commanded the priests, “Come up from the Jordan.” 18When the priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of their feet touched dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their course and overflowed all its banks, as they had before.

Stones Set Up at Gilgal

19The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and they encamped at Gilgal on the eastern border of Jericho. 20Joshua set up at Gilgal the twelve stones that had been taken from the Jordan.

21He said to the Israelites, “When your children ask their fathers in the future, ‘What do these stones mean?’ 22you shall make known to your children, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground.’ 23For the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you until you had crossed, just as the LORD your God did to the Red SeaseaSea, which HeheHe dried up before us until we had crossed. 24This was so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is mighty, and so that you may fear the LORD your God forever.”
