A Psalm of David: Confidence in the LORD

1The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the fortress of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? 2When evildoers advance to devour my flesh, my adversaries and my foes stumble and fall. 3Though an army encamp against me, my heart will not fear; though war erupt against me, in this I trust.

4One thing I have asked of the LORD, and this I seek: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD and to seek HimhimHim in HishisHis sanctuary. 5For in the day of adversity HeheHe will conceal me in HishisHis shelter; HeheHe will hide me in the secret place of HishisHis tent; HeheHe will set me high upon a rock. 6Then my head will be exalted above my enemies on every side.
