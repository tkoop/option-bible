The Primordial Creation of Light and Order

1In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. 2Now the earth was formless and desolate, darkness lay over the surface of the deep, and the SpiritspiritSpirit of God was brooding over the waters. 3And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light. 4God saw that the light was good, and HeheHe separated the light from the darkness. 5God called the light “day,” and the darkness HeheHe called “night.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the first day.

The Differentiation of Sky, Sea, and Land

6And God said, “Let there be an expanse in the midst of the waters, to separate water from water.” 7So God made the expanse and separated the waters beneath it from the waters above it. And it was so. 8God called the expanse “sky.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the second day. 9And God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, and let dry land appear.” And it was so. 10God called the dry land “EarthEarthearth,” and the gathering of the waters HeheHe called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.

11Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind, upon the earth.” And it was so. 12The EarthEarthearth brought forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants according to their kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good. 13And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.

The Ordination of Celestial Luminaries

14And God said, “Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky to separate day from night, and let them serve as signs, seasons, days, and years, 15and let them be lights in the expanse of the sky to illuminate the earth.” And it was so. 16God made the two great lights—the greater to govern the day and the lesser to govern the night—and the stars. 17God set them in the expanse of the sky to illuminate the earth, 18to govern day and night, and to separate light from darkness. And God saw that it was good. 19And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.

The Generation of Living Creatures and Humanity

20And God said, “Let the waters teem with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the expanse of the sky.” 21So God created the great sea creatures and every living thing that moves, with which the waters teemed, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good. 22God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the waters of the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.” 23And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.

24And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: livestock, creatures that crawl, and beasts of the earth according to their kinds.” And it was so. 25God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that crawls upon the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good. 26Then God said, “Let UsusUs make man in OurourOur image, after OurourOur likeness, and let them rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the livestock and all the earth, and over every creature that crawls upon the earth.” 27So God created man in HishisHis own image; in the image of God HeheHe created him; male and female HeheHe created them. 28God blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it; rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth.” 29Then God said, “Behold, I have given you every plant yielding seed that is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree whose fruit yields seed. They shall be yours for food. 30And to every beast of the earth and every bird of the air and everything that crawls upon the earth—everything that has the breath of life—I have given every green plant for food.” And it was so. 31And God saw all that HeheHe had made, and behold, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.
