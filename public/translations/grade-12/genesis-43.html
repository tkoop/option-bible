The Famine Compels Jacob to Send His Sons Back to Egypt

1Now the famine had grown severe throughout the land. 2When they had eaten the grain they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, “Go back and buy us a little more food.”

3Judah said to him, “The man solemnly warned us, saying, ‘You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.’ 4If you send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you. 5But if you do not send him, we will not go down, for the man said to us, ‘You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.’”

6Israel said, “Why have you dealt so cruelly with me by telling the man that you had another brother?”

7They answered, “The man questioned us closely about ourselves and our family, asking, ‘Is your father still alive? Do you have another brother?’ We answered him according to these words. How could we have known he would say, ‘Bring your brother down’?”

8Judah said to his father Israel, “Send the boy with me, and let us arise and go, so that we may live and not die—both we and you and our little ones. 9I will be surety for him; you may hold me accountable for him. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, then let me bear the blame before you for the rest of my life. 10For if we had not delayed, we could have returned twice by now.”

11Their father Israel said to them, “If it must be so, then do this: take some of the best products of the land in your bags and carry down a gift to the man—a little balm and a little honey, gum, myrrh, pistachios, and almonds. 12Take double the money with you, and carry back the money that was returned in the mouths of your sacks; perhaps it was an error. 13Take your brother as well, and go back to the man. 14May God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty grant you mercy before the man, so that he will release your other brother along with Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved.”

The Brothers Return to Egypt with Benjamin

15So the men took the gift, and they took double money with them, along with Benjamin. They set out and went down to Egypt, and they stood before Joseph.

16When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the steward of his house, “Bring the men into the house, slaughter an animal, and prepare it, for these men are to dine with me at noon.”

17The man did as Joseph had commanded and brought the men to Joseph’s house.

18The men were afraid because they were brought into Joseph’s house, and they said, “We have been brought here because of the money that was returned in our sacks the first time, so that he may attack us, seize us, and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys.”

19They approached the steward of Joseph’s house and spoke to him at the entrance to the house,

20saying, “Please, my lord, we came down the first time to buy food, 21and when we reached the lodging place and opened our sacks, each man’s money was in the mouth of his sack—our money in its full amount. We have brought it back with us. 22We have also brought down other money to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks.”

23He replied, “Peace be with you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you treasure in your sacks. I received your money.” Then he brought Simeon out to them.

24The man brought the men into Joseph’s house and gave them water; they washed their feet, and he gave their donkeys fodder. 25They prepared the gift for Joseph’s arrival at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat there.

26When Joseph came home, they brought into the house to him the gift they had with them, and they bowed down to him to the ground.

27He asked them how they were and said, “Is your father well—the old man you told me about? Is he still alive?”

28They said, “Your servant our father is well; he is still alive.” Then they bowed down and prostrated themselves.

29He looked up and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother’s son, and said, “Is this your younger brother, the one you told me about?” Then he said, “God be gracious to you, my son.”

30Joseph hurried out, because he was deeply moved with compassion for his brother, and he sought a place to weep. He entered his private room and wept there. 31Then he washed his face and came out. Regaining his composure, he said, “Serve the food.”

32They served him by himself, them by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves, because the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination to the Egyptians.

33They sat before him in order of age, from the firstborn to the youngest, and the men looked at one another in astonishment. 34He sent portions to them from his own table, but Benjamin’s portion was five times as large as any of theirs. They drank and were merry with him.
