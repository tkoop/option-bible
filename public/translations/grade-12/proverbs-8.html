Wisdom Calls Out

1Does not wisdom call out? Does not understanding raise her voice? 2At the summit of the heights beside the road, at the crossroads she takes her stand. 3Beside the gates, at the entrance to the city, at the doorways she cries aloud. 4“To you, O men, I call out, and my voice is to the sons of men. 5You simple ones, gain prudence; you fools, gain understanding. 6Listen, for I will speak noble things, and the opening of my lips will be upright. 7For my mouth utters truth, and wickedness is an abomination to my lips. 8All the words of my mouth are righteous; nothing in them is crooked or perverse. 9They are all plain to the discerning, and upright to those who find knowledge.

The Value of Wisdom

10“Take my instruction rather than silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold. 11For wisdom is better than rubies, and nothing desirable can compare with her. 12I, Wisdom, dwell with prudence, and I find knowledge and discretion. 13The fear of the LORD is hatred of evil; pride, arrogance, the evil way, and the perverse mouth I hate. 14Counsel and sound judgmentjudgementjudgment are mine; I am understanding; strength is mine. 15By me kings reign, and rulers decree what is just. 16By me princes rule, and nobles—all who judge with justice. 17I love those who love me, and those who seek me diligently will find me. 18With me are riches and honourhonourhonor, enduring wealth and righteousness. 19My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold, and my produce than choice silver. 20I walk in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of justice. 21That I may cause those who love me to inherit wealth, and fill their treasuries.

Wisdom before Creation

22“The LORD possessed me at the beginning of HishisHis way, before HishisHis works of old. 23I was established from eternity, from the beginning, before the earth. 24When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no springs abounding with water. 25Before the mountains were settled, before the hills I was brought forth. 26While HeheHe had not yet made the earth or the fields, or the first dust of the world. 27I was there when HeheHe established the heavens, when HeheHe drew a circle on the face of the deep. 28When HeheHe made firm the skies above, when HeheHe strengthened the fountains of the deep. 29When HeheHe set a decree for the sea, that the waters should not transgress HishisHis command; when HeheHe laid the foundations of the earth. 30Then I was beside HimhimHim as a master craftsman, and I was HishisHis delight day by day, rejoicing always before HimhimHim. 31Rejoicing in HishisHis inhabited world, and my delight was with the sons of men.

The Blessing of Heeding Wisdom

32“Now therefore, sons, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways. 33Hear instruction and be wise, and do not disregard it. 34Blessed is the man who listens to me, waiting daily at my gates, keeping watch at the posts of my doors. 35For whoever finds me finds life, and obtains favourfavourfavor from the LORD. 36But whoever fails to find me injures himself; all who hate me love death.”
