The Chambers Beside the Temple

1He led me out by the way to the north, to the outer court, and brought me to the chamber that faced the separate place and faced the building toward the north. 2Its entrance faced north; it was one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide. 3The galleries were set back in three tiers, corresponding to the twenty cubits of the inner court and to the pavementroadwaypavement of the outer court. 4Before the chambers ran an inner walkway ten cubits wide, with a passage one cubit wide, and their doors opened to the north. 5The upper rooms were narrower, because the terraces occupied more of their space than those below and in the middle of the building. 6Because the chambers rose in three stories like the pillars of the courts yet lacked actual pillars, their upper stories were set back from the lower and middle ones at ground level. 7Outside them, a wall fifty cubits long ran opposite the chambers along the outer court. 8The chambers facing the outer court were fifty cubits long, while the full length facing the temple was one hundred cubits. 9Below these chambers was an entrance from the east, by which one entered them from the outer court. 10Along the eastern wall of the court, opposite the separate place and the building, were chambers. 11A walkway lay before them, resembling the chambers facing north: as long as they were, so wide, with all their exits, regulations, and doors. 12Their doors matched those of the south-facing chambers: a door stood at the head of the walkway, the walkway before the garden hedge, as one entered from the east.

The Holy Chambers for the Priests

13He said to me, "ThetheThe chambers on the north and on the south, facing the separate place, are holy chambers. There the priests who are nearest to the LORD shall eat the most holy offerings; there they shall place the most holy offerings, the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering, because the place is holy."

14When the priests enter, they shall not go out from the holy place to the outer court; there, in the court, they shall lay aside the garments in which they minister, because they are holy, and put on other garments before they approach the people.

Measuring the Temple Enclosure

15When he had finished measuring the inner house, he led me out by the gate facing east and measured the area all around. 16He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds. 17He measured the north side: five hundred reeds. 18He measured the south side: five hundred reeds. 19He turned to the west side and measured: five hundred reeds. 20He measured it on all four sides. It had a wall all around, five hundred cubits long and five hundred cubits wide, to separate the holy from the common.
