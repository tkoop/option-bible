Moab Rebels and Ahaziah Is Injured

1After Ahab died, Moab rebelled against Israel. 2Now Ahaziah had fallen through the lattice of his upper room in Samaria and injured himself, so he sent messengers with this command: “Go and inquire of Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I will recover from this injury.”

Elijah Confronts the King’s Messengers

3But the angel of the LORD said to Elijah the Tishbite, “Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say to them, ‘Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to consult Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron?’ 4Therefore this is what the LORD says: ‘You will not rise from the bed on which you lie; you will certainly die.’” So Elijah went.

5When the messengers returned to the king, he asked them, “Why have you come back?”

6They replied, “A man came up to meet us and said, ‘Go back to the king who sent you and tell him, “This is what the LORD says: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are sending to consult Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore you will not rise from the bed on which you lie; you will certainly die.”’”

7The king asked them, “What sort of man was it who came up to meet you and spoke these words to you?”

8They answered, “He was a hairy man wearing a leather belt around his waist.”

The king said, “That is Elijah the Tishbite.”

Fire from Heaven Destroys the Captains

9Then Ahaziah sent a captain of fifty with his fifty men to Elijah. The captain went up to him while Elijah was sitting on the summit of the hill and said, “Man of God, the king says, ‘Come down.’”

10Elijah answered the captain of fifty, “If I am a man of God, let fire come down from Heavenheavenheaven and consume you and your fifty men.” Then fire fell from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed him and his fifty men.

11So the king sent another captain of fifty with his fifty men. The captain approached and said to Elijah, “Man of God, the king says, ‘Come down immediately.’”

12Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, let fire come down from Heavenheavenheaven and consume you and your fifty men.” Then the fire of God fell from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed him and his fifty men.

13So the king sent a third captain of fifty with his fifty men. The third captain went up, fell on his knees before Elijah, and pleaded with him, “Man of God, please value my life and the lives of these fifty servants of yours. 14See, fire has come down from Heavenheavenheaven and consumed the first two captains with their men. But now please value my life.”

15The angel of the LORD said to Elijah, “Go down with him; do not be afraid of him.” So Elijah arose and went down with him to the king.

Ahaziah’s Death and Succession

16Then Elijah said to the king, “This is what the LORD says: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are sending to consult Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore you will not rise from the bed on which you lie; you will certainly die.”

17So Ahaziah died in accordance with the word of the LORD that Elijah had spoken.
