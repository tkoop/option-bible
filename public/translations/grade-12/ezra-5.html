Prophets Urge the Rebuilding of the Temple

1The prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews living in Judah and Jerusalem, speaking in the name of the God of Israel. 2Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak arose and began to rebuild the house of God in Jerusalem, while the prophets of God stood with them and supported them.

Regional Officials Challenge the Work

3At that time Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai, together with their associates, came to them and asked, “Who issued you a decree to build this house and finish this structure?” 4They also asked them, “What are the names of the men who are building this building?” 5Yet the eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, so they did not stop them until a report was sent to Darius and an answer returned concerning the matter.

Tattenai Sends a Report to Darius

6This is a copy of the letter that Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai and his associates—the officials in the province Beyond the River—sent to King Darius. 7They sent him a report written as follows: “To King Darius: May you enjoy complete peace and prosperity. 8Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with massive stones, and timber is being fitted into the walls. The work is proceeding diligently and prospering in their hands.

“9Then we asked those elders and said to them, ‘Who issued you a decree to build this house and finish this structure?’ 10We also asked them their names, so that we might inform you and write down the names of the men who lead them.

“11And this is the answer they gave us: ‘We are servants of the God of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth, and we are rebuilding the house that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and completed. 12But because our fathers provoked the God of Heavenheavenheaven to anger, HeheHe gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean, who destroyed this house and carried the people away to Babylon. 13However, in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree to rebuild this house of God. 14Moreover, the gold and silver vessels of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple in Jerusalem and brought to the temple in Babylon, King Cyrus took from the temple in Babylon and entrusted to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he had appointed governor. 15And he said to him, “Take these vessels, go and place them in the temple in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be rebuilt on its original site.” 16Then this Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of the house of God in Jerusalem, and from that time until now it has been under construction and is not yet finished.’

“17Now therefore, if it pleases the king, let a search be made in the royal archives in Babylon to see whether King Cyrus issued a decree to rebuild this house of God in Jerusalem. Then let the king send us his decision concerning this matter.”
