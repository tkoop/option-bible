A Call to Justice and Righteousness

1Thus says the LORD: “Maintain justice and practice righteousness, for MymyMy salvation is near and MymyMy righteousness is about to be revealed. 2Blessed is the person who does this, and the mortal who clings to it—who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it and keeps his hand from doing any evil.”

The LORD Welcomes the Excluded

3Let no foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say, “The LORD will surely separate me from HishisHis people.”

And let no eunuch say, “Look—I am like a barren tree that bears no fruit.”

4For thus says the LORD: “To the eunuchs who keep MymyMy Sabbaths, who choose what pleases MemeMe and hold fast to MymyMy covenant, 5I will give them, in MymyMy house and within MymyMy walls, a monument and a name better than sons and daughters; I will give each of them an everlasting name that will not be cut off. 6And to the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD to minister to HimhimHim, to love the name of the LORD, and to be HishisHis servants—everyone who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it and holds fast to MymyMy covenant— 7these I will bring to MymyMy holy mountain and make them joyful in MymyMy house of prayer. Their burnt offerings and sacrifices will be accepted on MymyMy altar, for MymyMy house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.”

The LORD Will Gather Still More

8Thus says the Lord, the LORD, who gathers the outcasts of Israel: “I will gather still others to them besides those already gathered.”

Israel’s Blind Watchmen Rebuked

9All you beasts of the field, come and devour; all you beasts of the forest, come and feed.

10Israel’s watchmen are blind; they all lack knowledge. They are all mute dogs that cannot bark. They lie around and dream; they love to slumber. 11They are dogs with a ravenous appetite; they never have enough. They are shepherds who lack understanding; they all turn to their own way, each one to his own gain. 12“Come,” they say, “let us get wine and drink our fill of strong drink, and tomorrow will be just like today, only far better.”
