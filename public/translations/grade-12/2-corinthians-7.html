A Call to Holiness

1Therefore, beloved, since we possess these promises, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of flesh and spirit, bringing holiness to completion in the fear of God.

Paul’s Appeal and Boasting

2Make room for us in your hearts; we have wronged no one, corrupted no one, and exploited no one. 3I do not say this to condemn you, for I have already said that you are in our hearts, so that we might die together and live together. 4I have great boldness toward you; I have great boasting on your behalf. I am filled with comfort, and I overflow with joy in all our affliction.

Affliction, Comfort, and Repentance

5For even when we arrived in Macedonia, our bodies had no rest; instead, we were afflicted on every side—conflicts without and fears within. 6But God, who comforts the downcast, comforted us through the coming of Titus, 7and not only through his arrival but also through the comfort he received from you. He reported to us your longing, your mourning, and your zeal for me, so that my joy increased all the more. 8For even if I caused you sorrow by my letter, I do not regret it—though I did regret it, since I see that the letter grieved you, if only for a little while. 9Now I rejoice—not that you were grieved, but that you were grieved into repentance. You were grieved in a godly way, so that you suffered no loss from us. 10For godly sorrow produces a repentance that leads to salvation and brings no regret, but worldly sorrow produces death. 11See what earnestness this godly sorrow has produced in you: what eagerness to clear yourselves, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what vindication! In every way you have proved yourselves innocent in this matter. 12So although I wrote to you, it was not for the sake of the wrongdoer or for the sake of the one wronged, but so that your earnestness for us might be revealed to you before God.

Titus’s Refreshment and Paul’s Confidence

13Therefore we are comforted. And besides our own comfort, we rejoiced all the more at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by all of you. 14For if I have boasted to him about you, I was not put to shame. Just as everything we said to you was true, so our boasting to Titus has proved true. 15And his affection for you is all the greater, as he remembers the obedience of all of you—how you received him with fear and trembling. 16I rejoice that I have complete confidence in you.
