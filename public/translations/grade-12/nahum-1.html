The LORD's Oracle Against Nineveh

1An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

The LORD's Jealous Wrath and Power

2The LORD is jealous and avenging; the LORD is avenging and full of wrath. The LORD takes vengeance on HishisHis adversaries and reserves wrath for HishisHis enemies. 3The LORD is slow to anger and great in power; the LORD will by no means leave the guilty unpunished. His path is in the whirlwind and storm, and clouds are the dust of HishisHis feet. 4He rebukes the sea and dries it up; HeheHe makes all the rivers run dry. Bashan and Carmel wither, and the flower of Lebanon fades. 5The mountains quake before HimhimHim, and the hills melt away. The EarthEarthearth trembles at HishisHis presence—the world and all its inhabitants. 6Who can withstand HishisHis indignation? Who can endure HishisHis burning anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before HimhimHim.

The LORD's Goodness and JudgmentJudgementJudgment

7The LORD is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble; HeheHe cares for those who trust in HimhimHim. 8But with an overwhelming flood HeheHe will make an end of Nineveh; HeheHe will pursue HishisHis enemies into darkness. 9Whatever you plot against the LORD, HeheHe will bring to an end; trouble will not rise up a second time. 10For they will be entangled like thorns and consumed like the drink of drunkards; they will be burned up like dry stubble. 11From you, O Nineveh, has come forth one who plots evil against the LORD and counsels wickedness.

Deliverance for Judah

12This is what the LORD says: "Though they are strong and many, they will still be mowed down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more. 13Now I will break their yoke from your neck and tear away your bonds." 14The LORD has issued a decree concerning you, Nineveh: "Your name will no longer be perpetuated; I will cut off the carved image and cast idol from the house of your gods; I will prepare your grave, for you are contemptible." 15Look to the mountains—the feet of one who brings good news, who proclaims peace! Celebrate your festivals, O Judah; fulfillfulfilfulfill your vows. For the wicked will never again march through you; they will be utterly cut off.
