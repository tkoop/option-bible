Hidden Idolatry in the Temple Walls

7Then HeheHe brought me to the entrance of the court, and I looked and saw a hole in the wall.

8He said to me, “Son of man, dig through the wall.” So I dug through the wall and saw a doorway.

9He said to me, “Go in and see the wicked abominations they are committing here.”

10So I went in and looked, and I saw carved all around the walls every kind of creeping thing, detestable animal, and all the idols of the house of Israel.

11Standing before them were seventy men of the elders of Israel, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan among them. Each had a censer in his hand, and a thick cloud of incense was rising.

12He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in his own hidden chamber of idols? For they are saying, ‘The LORD does not see us; the LORD has abandoned the land.’”

13He said to me, “You will see them committing still greater abominations.”

Women Weeping for Tammuz

15He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen this? You will see still greater abominations than these.”

16He brought me into the inner court of the house of the LORD, and there at the entrance to the temple of the LORD, between the portico and the altar, were about twenty-five men. They had turned their backs to the temple of the LORD and their faces toward the east, and they were bowing down to the sun in the east.
