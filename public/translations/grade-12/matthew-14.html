6When Herod's birthday arrived, the daughter of Herodias danced before the company and captivated Herod, 7prompting him to swear an oath that he would grant her whatever she requested. 8Prompted by her mother, she demanded, "Give me here, on a platter, the head of John the BaptistbaptistBaptist." 9The king, though distressed, commanded that her request be fulfilled because of his oaths and his dinner guests. 10He sent orders, and John was beheaded in prison; 11his head was conveyed on a platter to the girl, who delivered it to her mother.

12Thereafter, John's disciples came, removed the body, buried it, and reported the matter to Jesus.
