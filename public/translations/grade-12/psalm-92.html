A Sabbath Song of Thanksgiving and Righteous Flourishing

1A psalm—a song for the Sabbath. It is good to give thanks to the LORD, and to sing praise to YouryourYour name, O MostmostMost HighhighHigh— 2to proclaim YouryourYour steadfast love in the morning and YouryourYour faithfulness at night, 3with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre.

4For YouyouYou, O the LORD, have made me glad by YouryourYour work; I will sing for joy at the works of YouryourYour hands. 5How great are YouryourYour works, O the LORD, how very deep are YouryourYour thoughts!

6A brutish man does not know, and a fool does not understand 7that though the wicked sprout like grass and all evildoers flourish, they will be destroyed forever.

8But YouyouYou, O the LORD, are exalted forever.

9For indeed, YouryourYour enemies, O the LORD—indeed, YouryourYour enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered. 10You have given me the strength of a wild ox, and YouyouYou have refreshed me with new oil. 11My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies; my ears have heard the ruin of my wicked foes.

12The righteous will flourish like a palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. 13Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God. 14In old age they will still bear fruit; they will remain fresh and green, 15to proclaim that the LORD is upright; HeheHe is my RockrockRock, and there is no unrighteousness in HimhimHim.
