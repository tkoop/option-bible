A Psalm of Asaph: Distress, Questioning, and Remembrance of God's Power

For the choirmaster. According to Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph.

1I cried aloud to God; I cried aloud to God, and HeheHe heard me.

2In the day of my trouble I sought the the LORD; through the night my hand was stretched out and did not grow weary, yet my soul refused to be comforted. 3I remember God, and I groan; I muse, and my spirit grows faint. Selah. 4You hold my eyes open; I am so troubled that I cannot speak.

5I have considered the days of old, the years of long ago. 6At night I remember my song; I meditate in my heart, and my spirit searches deeply.
