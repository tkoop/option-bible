The LORD's Call and Covenant Promise to Abram

1The LORD said to Abram, “Go from your country and your kindred and your father's house to the land that I will show you. 2I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you and make your name great, so that you will be a blessing. 3I will bless those who bless you, and anyone who dishonoursdishonoursdishonors you I will curse; and in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.”

Abram's Journey to the Land of Canaan

4So Abram went, as the the LORD had told him, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he departed from Haran. 5Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his nephew, and all the possessions they had acquired, and the people they had gained in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan; and they came to the land of Canaan. 6Abram passed through the land to the place at Shechem, to the oak of Moreh. The Canaanites were then in the land.

7Then the the LORD appeared to Abram and said, “To your offspring I will give this land.” So he built an altar there to the the LORD, who had appeared to him. 8From there he moved to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east. There he built an altar to the the LORD and called upon the name of the the LORD. 9Then Abram journeyed on, continuing toward the Negev.

Abram and Sarai in Egypt

10Now there was a famine in the land, so Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine was severe in the land. 11As he was about to enter Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, “I know that you are a woman beautiful in appearance, 12and when the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife,’ and they will kill me, but they will let you live. 13Please say that you are my sister, so that it may go well with me because of you, and that my life may be spared for your sake.”

14When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful. 15The princes of Pharaoh saw her and praised her to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house. 16For her sake Pharaoh treated Abram well, and Abram acquired sheep, oxen, male donkeys, female donkeys, male servants, female servants, and camels.

17But the the LORD afflicted Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife. 18So Pharaoh summoned Abram and said, “What is this you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife? 19Why did you say, ‘She is my sister,’ so that I took her as my wife? Now then, here is your wife; take her and go.” 20Pharaoh gave orders concerning him to his men, and they sent him away with his wife and all that he had.
