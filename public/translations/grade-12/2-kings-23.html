Josiah Renews the Covenant

1Then the king summoned every elder of Judah and Jerusalem. 2He ascended to the house of the LORD with all the men of Judah, all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests, the prophets, and all the people, from the least to the greatest, and he read aloud in their hearing every word of the BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant that had been discovered in the house of the LORD. 3The king stood beside the pillar and made a covenant before the LORD, pledging to follow the LORD and to keep HishisHis commandments, testimonies, and statutes with all his heart and all his soul, and to establish the words of this covenant written in this book. Then all the people stood firm in the covenant.

Josiah Purges Idolatry from the Land

4The king commanded Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second rank, and the doorkeepers to remove from the temple of the LORD all the vessels made for Baal, Asherah, and all the host of Heavenheavenheaven. He burned them outside Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron and carried their ashes to Bethel. 5He abolished the idolatrous priests whom the kings of Judah had installed to burn incense on the high places in the cities of Judah and in the environs of Jerusalem—those who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, to the moon, to the constellations, and to all the host of Heavenheavenheaven. 6He brought the Asherah pole out of the house of the LORD to the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley beyond Jerusalem, where he burned it, ground it to dust, and scattered its dust upon the graves of the common people. 7He demolished the quarters of the male cult prostitutes that were in the house of the LORD, where women wove hangings for Asherah.

8Then Josiah gathered all the priests from the cities of Judah and defiled the high places where they had burned incense, from Geba to Beersheba. He broke down the high places at the gates, including the high place at the entrance of the gate of Joshua the governor of the city, which stood on the left at the city gate. 9Although the priests of the high places did not ascend to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem, they ate unleavened bread among their brethren. 10He defiled Topheth in the ValleyvalleyValley of the SonsonSon of Hinnom, so that no one might make his son or daughter pass through the fire to Molech. 11He removed from the entrance to the house of the LORD the horses that the kings of Judah had dedicated to the sun; they stood in the court near the chamber of Nathan-melech, an official. He also burned the chariots of the sun. 12He tore down the altars that the kings of Judah had erected on the roof of the upper chamber of Ahaz, along with the altars Manasseh had erected in the two courts of the house of the LORD. He pulverized them on the spot and cast their dust into the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 13The king defiled the high places east of Jerusalem, south of the Mount of Corruption, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians, for Chemosh the abomination of Moab, and for Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites. 14He shattered the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherah poles, and filled their sites with human bones.

15He even tore down the altar at Bethel, the high place that Jeroboam son of Nebat had erected, he who caused Israel to sin. He burned the high place, ground it to powder, and burned the Asherah pole. 16As Josiah turned, he saw the tombs on the hillside; he sent for the bones from the tombs and burned them on the altar, thereby defiling it, according to the word of the LORD proclaimed by the man of God who had foretold these events. 17Then he asked, "What is that monument I see?" The men of the city replied, "It is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah and proclaimed these things that you have done against the altar at Bethel." 18"Leave it alone," he said. "Let no one disturb his bones." So they left his bones undisturbed, together with the bones of the prophet who had come from Samaria. 19Josiah also removed all the shrines of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel had made to provoke the LORD to anger; he treated them as he had treated Bethel. 20He slaughtered on the altars all the priests of the high places who were there, and he burned human bones on them. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

The Passover and Josiah's Unparalleled Devotion

21Then the king commanded all the people, "Celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, as it is written in this BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant." 22No such Passover had been observed since the days of the judges who judged Israel, nor throughout all the days of the kings of Israel and Judah. 23But in the eighteenth year of King Josiah's reign, this Passover was observed to the LORD in Jerusalem. 24Furthermore, Josiah removed the mediums, spiritists, household gods, idols, and all the abominations seen in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, in order to fulfillfulfilfulfill the words of the law written in the book that Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the LORD. 25Neither before nor after him was there a king like him, who turned to the LORD with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength, according to all the LawlawLaw of Moses. 26Nevertheless, the LORD did not turn away from the fury of HishisHis great wrath, which burned against Judah because of all that Manasseh had done to provoke HimhimHim. 27The LORD said, "I will remove Judah from MymyMy sight, just as I removed Israel. I will reject this city of Jerusalem, which I have chosen, and the temple of which I said, 'My name shall be there.'"

Josiah's Death and Judah's Decline

28As for the rest of the acts of Josiah, together with all that he did, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 29During Josiah's reign, Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt marched up to the Euphrates River to aid the king of Assyria. King Josiah went out to confront him, but Neco killed him at Megiddo as soon as he saw him. 30From Megiddo his servants carried his body in a chariot to Jerusalem, and he was buried in his own tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah, anointed him, and made him king in place of his father. 31Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem three months. His mother's name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah; she was from Libnah. 32He did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as his fathers had done. 33Pharaoh Neco imprisoned him at Riblah in the land of Hamath, so that he could not reign in Jerusalem, and he imposed on the land a tribute of a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold. 34Then Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king in place of his father Josiah, and he changed his name to Jehoiakim. But he took Jehoahaz away to Egypt, where he died. 35So Jehoiakim gave the silver and gold to Pharaoh, but he taxed the people of the land to raise the money to give to Pharaoh Neco, exacting the silver and gold from the people of the land, each according to his assessment. 36Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eleven years. His mother's name was Zebidah daughter of Pedaiah; she was from Rumah. 37He did evil in the sight of the LORD, just as his fathers had done.
