Descendants of Issachar

1The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron—four in all. 2The sons of Tola were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel—heads of their ancestral houses in the days of David. In their generations they numbered twenty-two thousand six hundred mighty warriors.

3The sons of Uzzi were Izrahiah; and the sons of Izrahiah were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Ishiah—five chiefs in all. 4Along with them, according to their generations and ancestral houses, were thirty-six thousand troops ready for war, because they had many wives and sons. 5Their kinsmen throughout all the clans of Issachar were mighty warriors, totaling eighty-seven thousand enrolled men.

Descendants of Benjamin

6The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Becher, and Jediael—three in all. 7The sons of Bela were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri—five heads of ancestral houses, mighty warriors whose enrollment was twenty-two thousand and thirty-four.

8The sons of Becher were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All these were the sons of Becher. 9Their enrollment, according to their generations and the heads of their ancestral houses—mighty warriors—was twenty thousand two hundred.

10The sons of Jediael were Bilhan; and the sons of Bilhan were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar. 11All these were the sons of Jediael—heads of ancestral houses, mighty warriors numbering seventeen thousand two hundred who went out to war. 12Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir, and Hushim was the son of Aher.

Descendants of Naphtali and Manasseh

13The sons of Naphtali were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum—the sons of Bilhah. 14The sons of Manasseh were Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore, and Machir, the father of Gilead. 15Machir took a wife for Huppim and Shuppim; his sister's name was Maacah. Another descendant was named Zelophehad, and Zelophehad had daughters.

16Maacah, Machir's wife, bore a son and named him Peresh; his brother was named Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem. 17The sons of Ulam were Bedan. These were the descendants of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh. 18His sister Hammoleketh bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. 19The sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

Descendants of Ephraim and Asher

20The sons of Ephraim were Shuthelah; Bered was his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, and Tahath his son. 21Zabad was his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead—whom the men of Gath, born in that land, killed because they came down to take their livestock. 22Their father Ephraim mourned for many days, and his brothers came to comfort him. 23He went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and he named him Beriah, because tragedy had come upon his house. 24His daughter was Sheerah, and she built LowerlowerLower Beth-horon and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

25Rephah was his son, and Resheph; Telah was his son, and Tahan his son. 26Laadan was his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son. 27Nun was his son, Joshua his son. 28Their territory and settlements were Bethel and its villages; to the east, Naaran; to the west, Gezer and its villages; and Shechem and its villages, as far as Ayyah and its villages. 29Along the borders of Manasseh were Beth-shean and its villages, Taanach and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, and Dor and its villages. In these the sons of Joseph, son of Israel, lived.

30The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. 31The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel, who was the father of Birzaith. 32Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua. 33The sons of Japhlet were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were the sons of Japhlet. 34The sons of Shemer were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubah, and Aram.

35The sons of his brother Helem were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. 36The sons of Zophah were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah. 37Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera. 38The sons of Jether were Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. 39The sons of Ulla were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia. 40All these were the descendants of Asher—heads of ancestral houses, select and mighty warriors, chiefs among the princes. Their enrollment for war was twenty-six thousand men.
