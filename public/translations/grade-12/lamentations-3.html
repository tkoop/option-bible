The Afflicted Man's Lament

1I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of HishisHis wrath. 2He has driven me and brought me into darkness, with no light. 3Surely against me HeheHe turns HishisHis hand again and again, all day long. 4He has worn out my flesh and skin; HeheHe has broken my bones. 5He has besieged me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship. 6He has made me dwell in darkness like those long dead.

7He has walled me in so that I cannot escape; HeheHe has weighed me down with heavy chains. 8Even when I cry out and plead for help, HeheHe shuts out my prayer. 9He has blocked my paths with hewn stone and made my ways crooked. 10He is to me like a bear lying in wait, a lion in hiding. 11He has turned aside my paths and torn me to pieces; HeheHe has made me desolate. 12He has bent HishisHis bow and set me as a target for the arrow.

13He has pierced my kidneys with the arrows of HishisHis quiver. 14I have become a laughingstock to all my people; they mock me in song all day long. 15He has filled me with bitterness and given me gall to drink. 16He has broken my teeth with gravel and covered me with ashes. 17My soul has been deprived of peace; I have forgotten prosperity. 18I said, "MymyMy endurance is gone, and my hope in the LORD has perished."

19Remember my affliction and my wandering, the bitterness and the poison. 20My soul remembers them and is bowed down within me. 21Yet this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope:

Hope Renewed in the LORD's Steadfast Love

22Because of the LORD's steadfast love we are not consumed, for HishisHis mercies never fail. 23They are new every morning; great is YouryourYour faithfulness. 24"The LORD is my portion," says my soul, "therefore I will hope in HimhimHim." 25The LORD is good to those who wait for HimhimHim, to the soul who seeks HimhimHim. 26It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD.

27It is good for a man to bear the yoke in his youth. 28He sits alone and keeps silent, for the LORD has laid it upon him. 29Let him put his mouth in the dust—there may yet be hope. 30Let him offer his cheek to the one who strikes him; let him be filled with disgrace. 31For the Lord will not cast off forever. 32Though HeheHe brings grief, HeheHe will show compassion according to HishisHis abundant steadfast love. 33For HeheHe does not afflict from HishisHis heart or grieve the children of men.
