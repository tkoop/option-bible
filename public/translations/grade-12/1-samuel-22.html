David at Adullam and in Moab

1David left that place and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and his father’s entire household heard about it, they went down to him there. 2Everyone who was in distress, everyone who had a creditor, and everyone who was bitter in soul gathered to him, and he became their leader. About four hundred men were with him.

3From there David went to Mizpeh in Moab and said to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and mother stay with you until I know what God will do for me.”

4So he brought them before the king of Moab, and they stayed with him throughout the time David remained in the stronghold.

5Then the prophet Gad said to David, “Do not remain in the stronghold; leave and go into the land of Judah.” So David left and went into the forest of Hereth.

Saul’s Suspicion and Doeg’s Report

6When Saul heard that David and the men with him had been discovered, he was sitting under the tamarisk tree on the hill at Gibeah, spear in hand, with all his servants standing around him.

7Saul said to the servants standing by him, “Listen now, Benjaminites! Will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make every one of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds? 8For all of you have conspired against me, and no one tells me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you is sorry for me or tells me that my son has incited my servant to ambush me, as is the case today.”

9Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing among Saul’s servants, answered, “I saw the son of Jesse come to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob. 10Ahimelech inquired of the the LORD for him, gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”

11So the king sent for Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and for all his father’s household, the priests of Nob, and they all came to the king.

12Saul said, “Listen now, son of Ahitub.”

“Here I am, my lord,” Ahimelech replied.

13Saul said to him, “Why have you and the son of Jesse conspired against me by giving him bread and a sword and inquiring of God for him, so that he rises against me to lie in wait, as he does today?”

14Ahimelech answered the king, “And who among all your servants is as faithful as David, the king’s son-in-law, captain over your bodyguard, and honouredhonouredhonored in your house? 15Have I today begun to inquire of God for him? Far be it from me! Let not the king impute anything to his servant or to all my father’s house, for your servant knew nothing of all this, whether small or great.”

16The king said, “You shall surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house.”

The Massacre at Nob and David’s Responsibility

17The king said to the guards standing by him, “Turn and kill the priests of the the LORD, because their hand also is with David, and because they knew he was fleeing and did not disclose it to me.” But the king’s servants were unwilling to stretch out their hand to strike the priests of the the LORD.

18The king said to Doeg, “You turn and strike the priests.” So Doeg the Edomite turned and struck the priests, and that day he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.

19He also put Nob, the city of the priests, to the sword—men and women, children and infants, oxen, donkeys, and sheep.

20But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, named Abiathar, escaped and fled to David.

21Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the the LORD.

22David said to Abiathar, “I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I am responsible for the deaths of all your father’s household. 23Stay with me; do not be afraid, for whoever seeks your life seeks mine; you will be safe with me.”
