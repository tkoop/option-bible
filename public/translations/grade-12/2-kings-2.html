Elijah's Ascension to Heaven

1When the the LORD was about to take Elijah up to Heavenheavenheaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were travellingtravellingtraveling from Gilgal.

2Elijah said to Elisha, “Please remain here, for the the LORD has sent me as far as Bethel.”

But Elisha said, “As surely as the the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you.” So they went down to Bethel.

3The company of prophets at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that the the LORD will take your master away from you today?”

He said, “Yes, I know it too; be silent.”

4Elijah said to him, “Elisha, please remain here, for the the LORD has sent me to Jericho.”

But he said, “As surely as the the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you.” So they came to Jericho.

5The company of prophets at Jericho came to Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that the the LORD will take your master away from you today?”

He said, “Yes, I know it too; be silent.”

6Elijah said to him, “Please remain here, for the the LORD has sent me to the Jordan.”

But he said, “As surely as the the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you.” So the two of them went on.

7Fifty men from the company of prophets went and stood at a distance, facing them, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

8Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the water; it divided to the right and to the left, and the two of them crossed on dry ground.

9When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, “Ask what I shall do for you before I am taken from you.”

Elisha said, “Please let a double portion of your spirit be upon me.”

10He said, “You have asked for something difficult; yet if you see me as I am being taken from you, it shall be so for you, but if not, it shall not be.”

11As they walked along and conversed, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated them, and Elijah ascended to Heavenheavenheaven in the whirlwind.

12Elisha saw it and cried out, “My father, my father, the chariots and horsemen of Israel!” And he saw him no more. He took hold of his own clothes and tore them into two pieces.

Elisha Takes Up Elijah's Mantle

13He picked up the cloak that had fallen from Elijah and went back and stood by the bank of the Jordan.

14He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him and struck the water, saying, “Where is the the LORD, the God of Elijah?” When he had struck the water, it divided to the right and to the left, and Elisha crossed over.

15When the company of prophets at Jericho saw him from afar, they said, “The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha.” They came to meet him and bowed to the ground before him.

16They said to him, “Look, your servants have fifty strong men. Please let them go and search for your master, lest the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD has carried him away and cast him upon some mountain or into some valley.”

But he said, “You shall not send.”

17They pressed him until he could no longer refuse, and he said, “Send them.” So they sent fifty men, and they searched for three days but did not find him.

18When they returned to him while he was staying in Jericho, he said to them, “Did I not tell you, ‘Do not go’?”

Elisha Heals the Waters of Jericho

19The men of the city said to Elisha, “Look, my lord, the city is pleasantly situated, as my lord sees, but the water is bad and the land is barren.”

20He said, “Bring me a new bowl, and put salt in it.” So they brought it to him.

21He went out to the spring of water and threw the salt into it, and said, “This is what the the LORD says: ‘I have healed these waters; from now on neither death nor miscarriage shall come from them.’”

22So the waters have been healed to this day, according to the word that Elisha spoke.

The Mockers at Bethel

23He went up from there to Bethel, and as he was going up on the way, young boys came out of the city and mocked him, saying, “Go up, you bald head! Go up, you bald head!”

24He turned around, looked at them, and cursed them in the name of the the LORD. Then two she-bears came out of the woods and mauled forty-two of the boys.

25From there he went to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.
