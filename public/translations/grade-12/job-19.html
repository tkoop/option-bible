Job Replies to His Friends

1Then Job answered:

2"How long will you torment me and crush me with words? 3Ten times now you have reproached me; you shamelessly mistreat me. 4Even if I have truly erred, my error remains my own concern. 5If indeed you would exalt yourselves above me and use my disgrace against me, 6then understand that God has wronged me and drawn HishisHis net around me."

Job Describes His Affliction

7"Though I cry out that I have been wronged, I receive no answer; though I plead for justice, there is none. 8He has blocked my path so I cannot pass; HeheHe has veiled my ways in darkness. 9He has stripped me of my honourhonourhonor and removed the crown from my head. 10He tears me down on every side until I am gone; HeheHe uproots my hope like a tree. 11His anger burns against me, and HeheHe counts me among HishisHis enemies. 12His troops advance together; they build a siege ramp against me and encamp around my tent."

13"He has removed my brothers from me, and my acquaintances have become strangers to me. 14My kinsmen have failed me, and my friends have forgotten me. 15My guests and maidservants count me as a stranger; I am an alien in their sight. 16I call for my servant, but he does not answer, though I implore him with my own mouth. 17My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to my own children. 18Even young boys scorn me; when I rise, they deride me. 19All my close friends abhor me, and those I love have turned against me. 20My bones cling to my skin and flesh; I have survived by the narrowest margin."

Job Pleads for Pity

21"Have pity on me, my friends, have pity, for the hand of God has struck me. 22Why do you pursue me as God does? Will you never be satisfied with my flesh?"

Job's Hope and a Warning to His Accusers

23"Oh, that my words were recorded, that they were inscribed in a book, 24that with an iron stylus and lead they were engraved in rock forever! 25But I know that my Redeemer lives, and in the end HeheHe will stand upon the dust. 26Even after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God. 27I will see HimhimHim for myself; my eyes will behold HimhimHim, and not as a stranger. How my heart yearns within me!"

28"If you say, 'How shall we persecute him, since the cause is found in him?' 29then you should fear the sword, for wrath brings the punishment of the sword, so that you may know there is a judgmentjudgementjudgment."
