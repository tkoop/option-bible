11Then Elkanah went home to Ramah, while the boy ministered to the the LORD in the presence of Eli the priest.

The Corruption of Eli’s Sons

12Now the sons of Eli were worthless men; they did not know the the LORD. 13The custom of the priests with the people was this: whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest’s servant came while the meat was boiling, carrying a three-pronged fork in his hand. 14He would thrust it into the pan, kettle, cauldron, or pot, and whatever the fork brought up the priest took for himself. This is what they did to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

15Moreover, before the fat was burned, the priest’s servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, “Give the priest meat to roast; he will not accept boiled meat from you, but only raw.”

16If the man said to him, “Let them burn the fat first, then take as much as you desire,” he would answer,

“No, give it now; if not, I will take it by force.”

17Thus the sin of the young men was very great before the the LORD, for they treated the the LORD’s offering with contempt.

Samuel’s Growth and Eli’s Rebuke

18But Samuel was ministering before the the LORD, a boy wearing a linen ephod. 19Moreover, his mother made him a little robe and brought it to him year by year when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

20Then Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, “May the ThetheThe LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she dedicated to the the LORD.” Then they would go home. 21Indeed, the ThetheThe LORD attended to Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the the LORD.

22Now Eli was very old, and he heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they lay with the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

23So he said to them, “Why do you do such things? I hear from all the people about your evil deeds. 24No, my sons; the report that I hear is not good, for you are causing the people of the the LORD to transgress. 25If one man sins against another, God may mediate for him; but if a man sins against the the LORD, who can intercede for him?” But they did not listen to their father, because the the LORD had decided to put them to death.

26And the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favourfavourfavor with the the LORD and with men.
