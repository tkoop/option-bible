Praise for the LORD’s Creation and Providence

1Bless the LORD, O my soul! the LORD my God, YouyouYou are immeasurably great; YouyouYou are clothed in splendoursplendoursplendor and majesty. 2He wraps HimselfhimselfHimself in light as with a garment; HeheHe stretches out the heavens like a tent. 3He lays the beams of HishisHis upper chambers in the waters; HeheHe makes the clouds HishisHis chariot; HeheHe walks upon the wings of the wind. 4He makes the winds HishisHis messengers and flames of fire HishisHis servants.

5He established the earth upon its foundations, so that it can never be shaken. 6You covered it with the deep as with a garment; the waters stood above the mountains. 7At YouryourYour rebuke they fled; at the sound of YouryourYour thunder they hurried away. 8The mountains rose and the valleys sank to the place YouyouYou appointed for them. 9You set a boundary they cannot cross; never again shall they cover the earth.

10He sends forth springs into the valleys; they flow between the mountains. 11They provide drink for every beast of the field; the wild donkeys quench their thirst. 12The birds of the heavens nest beside them; they sing among the branches. 13He waters the mountains from HishisHis upper chambers; the earth is satisfied with the fruit of YouryourYour works.

14He makes grass spring up for the livestock and plants for man to cultivate, bringing forth food from the earth. 15He gives wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil that makes his face shine, and bread that sustains his heart.

16The trees of the LORD are well watered, the cedars of Lebanon that HeheHe planted, 17where the birds build their nests; the stork has her home among the fir trees. 18The high mountains belong to the wild goats; the cliffs are a refuge for the rock hyraxes.

19He made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set. 20You bring darkness, and it becomes night, when all the beasts of the forest prowl. 21The young lions roar for their prey and seek their food from God. 22When the sun rises, they withdraw and lie down in their dens. 23Man goes out to his work and to his labourlabourlabor until evening.

24How manifold are YouryourYour works, O the LORD! In wisdom YouyouYou have made them all; the earth is full of YouryourYour creatures. 25There is the sea, vast and wide, teeming with creatures beyond number, living things both great and small. 26There the ships pass, and Leviathan, which YouyouYou formed to frolic there.

27All of them look to YouyouYou to give them their food in due season. 28When YouyouYou give it to them, they gather it up; when YouyouYou open YouryourYour hand, they are satisfied with good things. 29When YouyouYou hide YouryourYour face, they are terrified; when YouyouYou take away their breath, they die and return to dust. 30When YouyouYou send forth YouryourYour SpiritspiritSpirit, they are created; YouyouYou renew the face of the earth.

31May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in HishisHis works. 32He looks upon the earth, and it trembles; HeheHe touches the mountains, and they smoke.

33I will sing to the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have being. 34May my meditation be pleasing to HimhimHim, as I rejoice in the LORD. 35May sinners vanish from the earth and the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul! Hallelujah!
