The Seventh-Year Release of Debts

1At the conclusion of every seventh year, you must declare a remission of debts. 2This is the statute of the release: every creditor who has lent anything to his neighbourneighbourneighbor shall cancel it; he shall not demand payment from his neighbourneighbourneighbor or his brother, because the LORD has proclaimed a release. 3You may collect from a foreigner, but you must relinquish your claim against your brother.

Prosperity and Compassion for the Needy

4Nevertheless, there should be no poor among you, for the LORD will surely bless you in the land that the LORD your God gives you to possess as an inheritance, 5provided you diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God, observing to do all this commandment that I command you today. 6For the LORD your God will bless you, as HeheHe has spoken to you, and you shall lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow; you shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.

7If a poor man is found among you—one of your brothers—within any of your towns in the land that the LORD your God gives you, you must not become callous or withhold assistance from your poor brother. 8Rather, you must open your hand generously to him and lend him enough to cover whatever he lacks. 9Beware, lest a wicked thought arise in your heart, saying, “The seventh year, the year of release, is near,” so that you look resentfully on your poor brother and give him nothing; then he will cry out to the LORD against you, and guilt will rest on you. 10You shall surely give to him, and your heart must not resent giving to him, because for this the the LORD your God will bless you in all your work and in all to which you put your hand.

11For the poor will never cease from the midst of the land; therefore I command you, saying, “You shall surely open your hand to your brother, to your poor and needy in your land.”

Freedom for Hebrew Servants

12If your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, is sold to you and serves you six years, then in the seventh year you shall let him go free from you. 13And when you let him go free from you, you shall not send him away empty-handed. 14You shall furnish him generously from your flock, from your threshing floor, and from your winepress; from that with which the LORD your God has blessed you, you shall give to him. 15You shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God redeemed you; therefore I command you this thing today.

16And it shall be, if he says to you, “I will not leave you,” because he loves you and your household, because it is good for him with you, 17then you shall take an awl and thrust it through his ear into the door, and he shall be your servant for life. And to your female servant you shall do likewise. 18It shall not seem a hardship to you when you let him go free from you, for in serving six years he has been worth twice as much as a hired worker to you; and the LORD your God will bless you in all that you do.

Consecration of Firstborn Animals

19You shall set apart to the LORD your God every firstborn male that is born of your herd and of your flock. You shall do no work with the firstborn of your ox, nor shear the firstborn of your sheep. 20You shall eat it before the LORD your God each year in the place that the LORD will choose, you and your household. 21But if there is any blemish in it—if it is lame or blind, or has any serious defect—you shall not sacrifice it to the LORD your God. 22You shall eat it within your towns; both the unclean and the clean may eat it, as the gazelle and the deer. 23Only you shall not eat its blood; you shall pour it out on the earth as water.
