God as Refuge and Strength amid Cosmic Upheaval

For the choirmaster. A composition of the Sons of Korah, set to alamoth. A song. 1God is our refuge and our strength, a help exceedingly present in every affliction. 2Therefore we shall not fear though the earth be rentletrent asunder and the mountains be cast into the depths of the sea, 3though its waters roar and foam and the mountains quake before its swelling surge. Selah.

The River That Gladdens the Holy City

4There is a river whose streams gladden the city of God, the holy dwelling of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 5God is in her midst; she shall not be shaken; God will help her at the break of dawn. 6The nations rage and the kingdoms totter; HeheHe lifts HishisHis voice, and the earth dissolves. 7The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our stronghold. Selah.

The LORD’s Desolations and the Cessation of War

8Come, behold the works of the LORD, the desolations HeheHe has wrought upon the earth. 9He causes wars to cease to the very ends of the earth; HeheHe shatters the bow and breaks the spear; HeheHe burns the chariots with fire.

10“Cease your striving and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.”

11The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our stronghold. Selah.
