God Arises to Scatter His Enemies and Establish His People

1May God arise; let HishisHis enemies be scattered; let those who hate HimhimHim flee before HimhimHim. 2As smoke is driven away, so YouyouYou drive them away; as wax melts before fire, so the wicked perish before God. 3But the righteous rejoice and exult before God; they celebrate with joy.

4Sing to God, sing praises to HishisHis name; extol HimhimHim who rides upon the clouds—HishisHis name is the LORD—and rejoice before HimhimHim. 5Father of orphans and defender of widows is God in HishisHis holy dwelling. 6God settles the lonely in homes; HeheHe leads prisoners out into prosperity; but the rebellious dwell in a parched land.

The LORD's March from Sinai and His Care for His Inheritance

7O God, when YouyouYou went out before YouryourYour people, when YouyouYou marched through the wilderness—Selah. 8The EarthEarthearth quaked; the heavens poured down rain before God; even Sinai trembled before God, the God of Israel.

9You poured out abundant rain, O God; YouyouYou restored YouryourYour inheritance when it languished. 10Your flock settled in it; in YouryourYour goodness, O God, YouyouYou provided for the needy.

11The Lord gives the command; the women who proclaim the good news are a mighty host: 12“The kings of the armies flee, they flee!” and the women at home divide the spoil— 13though you lie among the sheepfolds, the wings of a dove are covered with silver, its pinions with shimmering gold.

14When the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty scattered kings there, snow fell on Zalmon. 15The mountain of God is the mountain of Bashan; the many-peaked mountain is the mountain of Bashan. 16Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountains, at the mountain God chose for HishisHis dwelling? Yes, the LORD will dwell there forever.

17The chariots of God are tens of thousands—thousands upon thousands; the Lord is among them; Sinai is now in the sanctuary. 18You ascended on high, leading captives in YouryourYour train; YouyouYou received gifts from among men, even from the rebellious, that the LORD God might dwell there.

The Triumphant Procession of God into His Sanctuary

19Blessed be the Lord, who daily bears our burdens; God is our salvation. Selah. 20Our God is a God of salvation, and to the LORD, the Lord, belong deliverances from death. 21But God will crush the heads of HishisHis enemies, the hairy scalp of the one who walks in his guilt.

22The Lord said, “I will bring them back from Bashan; I will bring them back from the depths of the sea.” 23that YouryourYour foot may be dipped in the blood of YouryourYour enemies, and the tongues of YouryourYour dogs may have their portion from the foe.

24Your procession is seen, O God—the procession of my God, my KingkingKing—into the sanctuary. 25The singers go before, the musicians follow after, and among them are maidens playing tambourines: 26Bless God in the great congregation; bless the LORD from the fountain of Israel. 27There is Benjamin, the youngest, leading them; the princes of Judah in their throng; the princes of Zebulun; the princes of Naphtali.

28Summon YouryourYour power, O God; the power, O God, by which YouyouYou have worked for us. 29Because of YouryourYour temple at Jerusalem, kings will bring gifts to YouyouYou. 30Rebuke the beast of the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the peoples, trampling those who lust after tribute; scatter the peoples who delight in war. 31Nobles will come from Egypt; Cush will hasten to stretch out her hands to God.

32Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord—Selah. 33to HimhimHim who rides upon the highest heavens, the ancient heavens; behold, HeheHe sends out HishisHis voice, a mighty voice. 34Ascribe power to God, whose majesty is over Israel, whose power is in the skies. 35Awesome is God from HishisHis sanctuary; the God of Israel—HeheHe gives power and strength to HishisHis people. Blessed be God!
