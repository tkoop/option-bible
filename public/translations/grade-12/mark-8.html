Jesus Feeds the Four Thousand

1In those days, when a large crowd had gathered again and had nothing to eat, Jesus called HishisHis disciples and said to them, 2“I have compassion on the crowd, because they have been with MemeMe now three days and have nothing to eat. 3And if I send them away to their homes hungry, they will faint on the way; and some of them have come from a great distance.”

4His disciples answered HimhimHim, “Where can anyone get enough bread to feed these people in this desolate place?”

5He asked them, “How many loaves do you have?”

They said, “Seven.”

6And HeheHe directed the crowd to sit down on the ground. And taking the seven loaves, HeheHe gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to HishisHis disciples to set before the people; and they set them before the crowd. 7They also had a few small fish. And after blessing them, HeheHe said, “Set these before them as well.” 8And they ate and were satisfied. And they took up seven baskets full of the broken pieces left over. 9And there were about four thousand people. And HeheHe sent them away.

10And immediately HeheHe got into the boat with HishisHis disciples and went to the district of Dalmanutha.

The Pharisees Demand a Sign and Jesus Warns Against Their Leaven

11The Pharisees came out and began to argue with HimhimHim, seeking from HimhimHim a sign from Heavenheavenheaven to test HimhimHim.

12And HeheHe sighed deeply in HishisHis spirit and said, “Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I tell you, no sign will be given to this generation.”

13And leaving them, HeheHe got into the boat again and went to the other side.

14Now they had forgotten to bring bread, and they had only one loaf with them in the boat.

15And HeheHe cautioned them, saying, “Be on your guard against the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.”

16And they began discussing among themselves that they had no bread.

17And Jesus, aware of this, said to them, “Why are you discussing the fact that you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or understand? Have your hearts been hardened? 18Having eyes, do you not see? Having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember?”

19He asked, “When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?”

They said to HimhimHim, “Twelve.”

20And HeheHe asked, “And when I broke the seven loaves for the four thousand, how many basketfuls of broken pieces did you take up?”

They said to HimhimHim, “Seven.”

21And HeheHe said to them, “Do you not yet understand?”

Jesus Heals a Blind Man at Bethsaida

22And they came to Bethsaida. And some people brought to HimhimHim a blind man and begged HimhimHim to touch him.

23And HeheHe took the blind man by the hand and led him out of the village. And when HeheHe had spat on his eyes and laid HishisHis hands on him, HeheHe asked him, “Do you see anything?”

24And he looked up and said, “I see people, but they look like trees walking.”

25Then Jesus laid HishisHis hands on his eyes again. And he opened his eyes, his sight was restored, and he saw everything clearly.

26And HeheHe sent him to his home, saying, “Do not even enter the village.”
