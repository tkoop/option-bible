The Law of the Red Heifer

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2“This is the statute of the law that the the LORD has commanded: Tell the Israelites to bring you a red heifer without blemish, without defect, and upon which no yoke has ever been laid.

3“Give it to Eleazar the priest, and he shall take it outside the camp and slaughter it in his presence. 4Then Eleazar the priest is to take some of its blood on his finger and sprinkle it seven times toward the front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 5Then the heifer shall be burned in his sight; its hide, flesh, and blood, along with its dung, shall be burned. 6The priest is to take cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet yarn and cast them into the fire consuming the heifer.

7“Then the priest must wash his clothes and bathe his body in water; afterward he may enter the camp, though he remains unclean until evening. 8The one who burned the heifer must likewise wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he too remains unclean until evening.

9“Then a man who is ceremonially clean shall gather the ashes of the heifer and deposit them in a clean place outside the camp. They are to be kept for the congregation of Israel for use in the water of purification; it is for purification from sin. 10The man who gathers the ashes of the heifer must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. This is a permanent statute for the Israelites and for the foreigner residing among them.”

Purification After Contact with Death

11“Whoever touches any dead body shall be unclean for seven days. 12He must purify himself with the water on the third day and on the seventh day; then he will be clean. But if he does not purify himself on the third and seventh days, he will not be clean. 13Anyone who touches a corpse—the body of a person who has died—and does not purify himself defiles the tabernacle of the the LORD; that person must be cut off from Israel. He remains unclean because the water of purification has not been sprinkled upon him, and his uncleanness still rests upon him.

14“This is the law when a man dies in a tent: everyone who enters the tent and everyone already in the tent shall be unclean for seven days. 15Any open vessel that has no lid fastened upon it is unclean. 16Anyone in the open field who touches one slain by the sword, or a dead person, or a human bone, or a grave, shall be unclean for seven days.”

The Water of Purification and Its Application

17“For the unclean person, they shall take some of the ashes of the burnt sin offering and place them in a vessel of fresh water. 18Then a ceremonially clean person is to take hyssop, dip it in the water, and sprinkle it upon the tent, upon all the furnishings, upon the people who were there, and upon anyone who touched a bone, a slain person, or a grave. 19The clean person shall sprinkle the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day; after he has purified himself on the seventh day, he must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be clean by evening.

20“But if a person who is unclean does not purify himself, he will be cut off from the assembly, because he has defiled the sanctuary of the the LORD. The water of purification has not been sprinkled upon him; he is unclean. 21This is a permanent statute for them. The one who sprinkles the water of purification must wash his clothes, and whoever touches the water of purification will be unclean until evening. 22Anything the unclean person touches will become unclean, and anyone who touches it will be unclean until evening.”
