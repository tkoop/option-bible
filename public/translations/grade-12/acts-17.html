Paul and Silas in Thessalonica

1After Paul and Silas had passed through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to Thessalonica, where a Jewish synagogue stood. 2As was his custom, Paul entered the synagogue and, over three Sabbaths, reasoned with them from the ScripturesscripturesScriptures, 3explaining and proving that the Christ had to suffer and rise from the dead, and declaring, "This Jesus, whom I proclaim to you, is the Christ." 4Some of them were persuaded and joined Paul and Silas, along with a great multitude of God-fearing Greeks and quite a few of the leading women.

5But the Jews, moved by jealousy, recruited certain worthless men from the marketplace, formed a mob, and threw the city into turmoil. They assaulted Jason's house, seeking to bring Paul and Silas out to the crowd. 6When they could not find them, they dragged Jason and some brothers before the city magistrates, shouting, "These men who have thrown the whole world into disorder have now come here as well, 7and Jason has received them into his home. They all act contrary to Caesar's decrees, claiming that there is another king—Jesus." 8Hearing this, the crowd and the magistrates were thrown into confusion. 9After taking bond from Jason and the others, they released them.

The Bereans Receive the Word

10As soon as night fell, the brothers sent Paul and Silas away to Berea; upon arriving, they went into the Jewish synagogue. 11Now these Bereans were more noble-minded than those in Thessalonica, for they received the word with all eagerness, examining the ScripturesscripturesScriptures daily to determine whether these things were so. 12Consequently, many of them believed, along with a number of prominent Greek women and men. 13But when the Jews of Thessalonica learned that the word of God was also being proclaimed by Paul in Berea, they went there too, inciting and agitating the crowds. 14The brothers then immediately sent Paul away toward the sea, while Silas and Timothy remained behind. 15Those who escorted Paul brought him to Athens and returned with instructions for Silas and Timothy to join him as soon as possible.

Paul in Athens

16While Paul waited for them in Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he observed that the city was full of idols. 17So he reasoned in the synagogue with the Jews and the God-fearing Gentiles, and in the marketplace with those who happened to be there each day. 18Some of the Epicurean and StoicstoicStoic philosophers also engaged him in discussion. Some asked, "What is this babbler trying to say?"

Others said, "HeheHe seems to be proclaiming foreign deities," because he was preaching Jesus and the resurrection. 19They took hold of Paul and brought him to the Areopagus, saying, "May we know what this new teaching is that you are presenting? 20For you are bringing some strange things to our ears, and we want to know what they mean." 21Now all the Athenians and the foreigners residing there spent their time doing nothing but telling or hearing something new.

Paul's Address on the Areopagus

22Then Paul stood in the midst of the Areopagus and said: "Men of Athens, I observe that you are extremely religious in every respect. 23For as I passed along and examined your objects of worship, I found an altar inscribed, 'TO AN UNKNOWN GOD.' Therefore, what you worship in ignorance, I now proclaim to you.
