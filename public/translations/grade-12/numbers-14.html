Israel’s Rebellion and Fear

1That night the entire assembly raised a loud cry and wept. 2All the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole assembly said to them, “If only we had died in Egypt—or even in this wilderness! 3Why is the LORD bringing us into this land only to have us fall by the sword? Our wives and children will be taken as plunder. Wouldn’t it be better for us to go back to Egypt?” 4And they said to one another, “Let us appoint a leader and go back to Egypt.”

5Then Moses and Aaron fell facedown before the whole assembly of the Israelites.

Caleb and Joshua’s Faithful Appeal

6Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had explored the land, tore their clothes. 7They said to the whole assembly of the Israelites, “The land we passed through and explored is exceedingly good. 8If the LORD is pleased with us, HeheHe will bring us into that land and give it to us—a land flowing with milk and honey. 9Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not be afraid of the people of the land, for they are helpless before us. Their protection has been removed, and the LORD is with us; do not be afraid of them.”

10But the whole assembly threatened to stone them. Then the glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the Israelites.

How long will they refuse to believe in MemeMe, despite all the signs I have performed among them? 12I will strike them with a plague and destroy them, but I will make you into a nation greater and stronger than they.”

The LORD’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment and the Failed Ascent

26The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 27“How long must I endure this wicked community that grumbles against MemeMe? I have heard the complaints the Israelites are making against MemeMe. 28Say to them, ‘As surely as I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you exactly what you said in MymyMy hearing: 29Your corpses will fall in this wilderness—all of you who were counted in the census, twenty years old or more, because you grumbled against MemeMe. 30Not one of you will enter the land I swore with uplifted hand to make your home, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun. 31As for your children you said would be taken as plunder, I will bring them in to enjoy the land you have rejected. 32But as for you, your bodies will fall in this wilderness. 33Your children will wander in the wilderness for forty years and bear the consequences of your unfaithfulness until your bodies perish in the wilderness. 34For forty years—one year for each of the forty days you explored the land—you will suffer for your sins and know what it is like to have MemeMe against you. 35I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will surely do these things to this whole wicked community, which has banded together against MemeMe. They will perish in this wilderness; here they will die.’”

36The men Moses sent to explore the land, who returned and made the whole assembly grumble against him by spreading a bad report about the land— 37those very men who were responsible for spreading the bad report about the land were struck down and died of a plague before the LORD. 38Of the men who went to explore the land, only Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh survived.

39When Moses reported this to all the Israelites, they mourned bitterly.

40Early the next morning they set out for the hill country, saying, “Here we are; we will go up to the place the LORD promised, for we have sinned.”

41But Moses said, “Why are you now disobeying the LORD’s command? It will not succeed. 42Do not go up, for the LORD is not with you, and you will be struck down before your enemies. 43For the Amalekites and Canaanites are there before you, and you will fall by the sword, because you have turned away from the LORD; therefore the LORD will not be with you.”

44Nevertheless, in their presumption they went up toward the top of the mountain, although neither Moses nor the ark of the covenant of the LORD had left the camp. 45Then the Amalekites and Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down and attacked them and beat them down all the way to Hormah.
