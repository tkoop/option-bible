The Fall of Babylon Announced

1After these things I saw another angel coming down from Heavenheavenheaven, having great authority, and the earth was illuminated by his glory. 2And he cried out with a mighty voice, saying, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a dwelling place for demons, a haunt for every unclean spirit, a haunt for every unclean bird, and a haunt for every unclean and detestable beast. 3For all the nations have drunk the wine of the wrath of her sexual immorality; the kings of the earth have committed sexual immorality with her; and the merchants of the earth have grown rich through the power of her luxury."

A Call to God's People

4And I heard another voice from Heavenheavenheaven saying, "Come out of her, my people, so that you will not share in her sins and so that you will not receive any of her plagues. 5For her sins are piled up to Heavenheavenheaven, and God has remembered her iniquities. 6Pay her back as she has paid, and repay her double for her deeds; mix a double portion for her in the cup she mixed. 7As she glorified herself and lived in luxury, give her an equal measure of torment and grief. For in her heart she says, 'I sit as queen; I am not a widow, and I will never see mourning.' 8Therefore her plagues will come in one day—death and mourning and famine—and she will be burned up with fire, for mighty is the Lord God who judges her."

The Lament of Kings and Merchants

9The kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her will weep and mourn over her when they see the smoke of her burning. 10Standing far off in fear of her torment, they will say, "Alas, alas, the great city, Babylon the mighty city! For in one hour your judgmentjudgementjudgment has come."

11And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo anymore— 12cargo of gold and silver, precious stones and pearls, fine linen and purple, silk and scarlet, every kind of citron wood, every article of ivory, every article made of the most precious wood, bronze, iron, and marble; 13cinnamon, spice, incense, myrrh, and frankincense; wine and oil; fine flour and wheat; cattle and sheep; horses and chariots; and slaves and human souls. 14The fruit for which your soul longed has gone from you, and all your delicacies and splendoursplendoursplendor are lost to you, never to be found again.

15The merchants who grew rich from her will stand far off in fear of her torment, weeping and mourning. 16They will say, "Alas, alas, the great city, clothed in fine linen, purple, and scarlet, and adorned with gold, precious stones, and pearls! 17For in one hour such great wealth has been laid waste." And every shipmaster, every passenger, sailors, and all who trade on the sea stood far off, 18and cried out as they saw the smoke of her burning, "What city was like the great city?" 19They threw dust on their heads and wept and mourned, crying out, "Alas, alas, the great city, in which all who had ships at sea grew rich from her wealth! For in one hour she has been laid waste."

20Rejoice over her, O Heavenheavenheaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, for God has avenged you against her!

Babylon Thrown Down

21Then a mighty angel picked up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, "So will Babylon, the great city, be thrown down with violence and will be found no more. 22The sound of harpists and musicians, flutists and trumpeters, will never be heard in you again. No craftsman of any trade will be found in you again, and the sound of a mill will never be heard in you again. 23The light of a lamp will never shine in you again, and the voice of bridegroom and bride will never be heard in you again. For your merchants were the great ones of the earth, and all nations were deceived by your sorcery." 24In her was found the blood of prophets and saints, and of all who have been slain on the earth.
