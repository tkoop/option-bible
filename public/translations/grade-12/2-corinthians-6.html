Paul’s Open Heart and Appeal

11Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians; our heart is enlarged. 12You are not constrained by us, but you are constrained in your own affections. 13In fair exchange—I speak as to children—open wide your hearts as well.

A Call to Spiritual Separation

14Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers, for what partnership can righteousness have with wickedness? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness? 15What harmony can Christ have with Belial? Or what share does a believer have with an unbeliever?
