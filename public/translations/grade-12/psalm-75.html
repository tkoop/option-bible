Thanksgiving and God’s Sovereign JudgmentJudgementJudgment

1We give thanks to YouyouYou, O God; we give thanks, for YouryourYour name is near. People declare YouryourYour wonders.

2“When I choose an appointed time, I will judge with equity. 3When the earth and all its inhabitants tremble, I am the OneoneOne who holds up its pillars. Selah. 4I say to the proud, ‘Do not boast,’ and to the wicked, ‘Do not exalt yourselves. 5Do not exalt yourselves on high, nor speak arrogantly.’”

6For exaltation comes not from the east or the west, nor from the wilderness, 7but from God, who judges; HeheHe lowers one and exalts another. 8For a cup is in the hand of the LORD, full of foaming wine mixed with spices. He pours it out, and all the wicked of the earth will drink it down to the dregs. 9But I will proclaim forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

10“I will cut off all the power of the wicked, but the righteous will be exalted.”
