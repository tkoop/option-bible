Pharisees and Scribes Question Jesus

5So the Pharisees and scribes asked HimhimHim, “Why do your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with defiled hands?”

The Syrophoenician Woman’s Faith

24Jesus left that place and went to the region of Tyre. He wanted no one to know HeheHe was there, and HeheHe entered a house, but HeheHe could not escape notice.

25Instead, a woman whose little daughter was possessed by an unclean spirit heard about HimhimHim and came at once, falling at HishisHis feet. 26The woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by birth, and she begged HimhimHim to cast the demon out of her daughter.

27He said to her, “Let the children be satisfied first, for it is not right to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”

28“Yes, Lord,” she replied, “even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.”

29He told her, “Because of this answer, go; the demon has left your daughter.”

30She went home and found the child lying on the bed, and the demon was gone.
