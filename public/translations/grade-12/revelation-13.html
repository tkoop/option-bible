The Beast Rising from the Sea

1And I saw a beast rising out of the sea, bearing ten horns and seven heads, with ten diadems on its horns and blasphemous names on its heads. 2The beast I saw resembled a leopard; its feet were like a bear's, and its mouth was like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave it his power, his throne, and great authority.

3One of its heads seemed to have been mortally wounded, but its deadly wound was healed, and the whole EarthEarthearth marvelledmarvelledmarveled and followed the beast. 4They worshiped the dragon because he had given authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who can make war against it?"

5The beast was given a mouth to utter great and blasphemous words, and authority was given to it to act for forty-two months. 6It opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme HishisHis name, HishisHis tabernacle, and those who dwell in Heavenheavenheaven.

7It was permitted to wage war against the saints and to conquer them, and authority was given to it over every tribe, people, language, and nation. 8All who dwell on the earth will worship it—everyone whose name has not been written, from the foundation of the world, in the BookbookBook of LifelifeLife of the LamblambLamb who was slain.

9Whoever has an ear, let him hear. 10If anyone is destined for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone is to be killed by the sword, by the sword he must be killed. Here is the perseverance and faith of the saints.

The Beast Rising from the Earth

11Then I saw another beast rising out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb, but it spoke like a dragon. 12It exercises all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and it compels the earth and its inhabitants to worship the first beast, whose deadly wound was healed.

13It performs great signs, even making fire descend from Heavenheavenheaven to EarthEarthearth in the sight of people. 14By the signs it was permitted to perform before the beast, it deceives the inhabitants of the earth, telling them to make an image for the beast that was wounded by the sword and yet lived.

15It was granted to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast could speak and cause all who refused to worship it to be put to death.

The Mark and Number of the Beast

16It also compels everyone—small and great, rich and poor, free and slave—to receive a mark on the right hand or on the forehead, 17so that no one may buy or sell unless he has the mark: the name of the beast or the number of its name. 18Here is wisdom: let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is a human number, and its number is 666.
