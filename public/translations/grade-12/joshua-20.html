The Command to Establish Cities of Refuge

1The LORD said to Joshua, 2"Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘Designate for yourselves the cities of refuge that I promised you through Moses, 3so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally and without knowing what he has done may flee there, and they will be your refuge from the avenger of blood.’"

The Procedure for the Manslayer

4He shall flee to one of these cities, station himself at the city gate, and present his case to the elders of that city; whereupon they shall admit him into the city and allot him a residence among them. 5If the avenger of blood pursues him, they shall not surrender the manslayer into his power, because he struck his neighbourneighbourneighbor unintentionally and had harbored no prior hatred toward him. 6He shall dwell in that city until he has stood trial before the congregation and until the death of the high priest then in office; only thereafter may the manslayer return to his own home, to the city from which he fled.

The Designated Cities of Refuge

7They consecrated Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali; Shechem, in the hill country of Ephraim; and Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—in the hill country of Judah. 8East of the Jordan, beyond Jericho, they assigned Bezer in the wilderness on the tableland, from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh. 9These cities were appointed for all the Israelites and for the resident alien living among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally may flee there and not perish by the hand of the avenger of blood before he has stood trial before the congregation.
