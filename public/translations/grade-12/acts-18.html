Paul Arrives in Corinth

1Following these events, Paul departed from Athens and came to Corinth. 2There he encountered a Jew named Aquila, a native of Pontus, who had recently arrived from Italy with his wife Priscilla, because Claudius had ordered all Jews to leave Rome. Paul approached them, 3and because they shared the same trade, he stayed with them and worked alongside them, for they were tentmakers by profession. 4Every Sabbath he reasoned in the synagogue, persuading both Jews and Greeks.

5When Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia, Paul was constrained by the message, solemnly testifying to the Jews that Jesus was the Christ. 6But when they opposed him and blasphemed, he shook out his clothing and said to them, “Your blood be on your own heads! I am innocent. From now on I will go to the Gentiles.” 7Leaving there, he entered the house of a man named Titius Justus, a worshiper of God, whose residence bordered the synagogue. 8Crispus, the ruler of the synagogue, believed in the Lord with his entire household, and many of the Corinthians, upon hearing, believed and were baptized.

9During the night the Lord said to Paul in a vision, “Do not be afraid, but speak and do not be silent, 10for I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you, because I have many people in this city.” 11So he remained there a year and six months, teaching the word of God among them.

Paul Before Gallio

12While Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the Jews rose up in concert against Paul and brought him before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat, 13saying, “This man persuades people to worship God contrary to the law.” 14But as Paul was about to open his mouth, Gallio said to the Jews, “If this were some injustice or wicked crime, O Jews, I would reasonably bear with you. 15But if these are disputes about words and names and your own law, see to it yourselves; I do not wish to be judge of these things.” 16And he drove them from the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat. 17Then all of them seized Sosthenes, the ruler of the synagogue, and beat him before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat; but Gallio was indifferent to all of these things.

18Paul remained there for an extended period; then, taking leave of the brothers, he sailed for Syria, accompanied by Priscilla and Aquila, having shaved his head at Cenchreae, for he had a vow. 19They arrived at Ephesus, and he left them there, while he himself entered the synagogue and discoursed with the Jews. 20When they asked him to stay for a longer time, he declined, 21but taking leave and saying, “I will return to you again, God willing,” he set sail from Ephesus.

Paul Returns to Antioch and Strengthens the Disciples

22Landing at Caesarea, he went up and greeted the church, then went down to Antioch. 23After spending some time there, he departed and travelledtravelledtraveled sequentially through the Galatian region and Phrygia, strengthening all the disciples.

Apollos, the Eloquent Teacher from Alexandria

24Now a Jew named Apollos, a native of Alexandria, an articulate man and mighty in the ScripturesscripturesScriptures, came to Ephesus. 25He had been instructed in the way of the Lord, and being fervent in spirit, he spoke and taught accurately the things concerning Jesus, though he knew only the baptism of John. 26He began to speak boldly in the synagogue; but when Priscilla and Aquila heard him, they took him aside and explained to him the way of God more accurately. 27When he wished to cross over to Achaia, the brothers encouraged him and wrote to the disciples to receive him. When he arrived, he substantially assisted those who had believed through grace, 28for he powerfully refuted the Jews in public, establishing from the ScripturesscripturesScriptures that Jesus was the Christ.
