To the LORD Alone Belongs the Glory

1Not to us, O the LORD, not to us, but to YouryourYour name give glory, for the sake of YouryourYour steadfast love and for the sake of YouryourYour faithfulness. 2Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?" 3Our God is in Heavenheavenheaven; HeheHe does whatever HeheHe pleases.

4Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands. 5They have mouths, yet they cannot speak; they have eyes, yet they cannot see. 6They have ears, yet they cannot hear; they have noses, yet they cannot smell. 7They have hands, yet they cannot feel; they have feet, yet they cannot walk; nor can they utter a sound with their throats. 8Those who make them become like them, as does everyone who trusts in them.

9O Israel, trust in the LORD; HeheHe is their help and shield. 10O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD; HeheHe is their help and shield. 11You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; HeheHe is their help and shield.

12The LORD remembers us and will bless us; HeheHe will bless the house of Israel; HeheHe will bless the house of Aaron. 13He will bless those who fear the LORD, both the least and the greatest.

14May the LORD increase you—more and more, you and your children. 15May you be blessed by the LORD, the MakermakerMaker of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth. 16The highest heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth HeheHe has given to humanity.

17It is not the dead who praise the LORD, nor those who descend into silence. 18But we will bless the LORD from this time forth and forevermore. Praise the LORD!
