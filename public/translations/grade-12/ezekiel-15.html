The Vine and the Forest

1The word of the the LORD came to me with this message: 2“Son of man, in what respect does the wood of the vine surpass every other wood—the vine branch found among the trees of the forest? 3Can wood be taken from it to fashion anything useful? Would anyone carve from it a peg on which to hang a vessel? 4Behold, it is consigned to the fire for fuel; the fire has consumed both extremities, and its core is charred. Is it now fit for any work? 5Behold, even when it was intact, it was not fashioned for work; how much less, after fire has consumed and charred it, can it be fashioned for work?”

6“Therefore, this is what the Lord the the LORD declares: Just as the vine tree among the forest’s trees, which I consigned to the fire for fuel, so I have consigned the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 7I will set MymyMy face against them; although they emerge from the fire, the fire will consume them. Then you shall know that I am the the LORD, when I set MymyMy face against them. 8I will render the land desolate, because they have acted with treachery,” declares the Lord the the LORD.
