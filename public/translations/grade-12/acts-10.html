Cornelius’s Vision and Commission

1In Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius, a centurion of the cohort called Italian. 2He was devout, fearing God with his entire household; he gave many alms to the people and prayed to God continually. 3About the ninth hour of the day, he saw clearly in a vision an angel of God enter his presence and say, “Cornelius.”

4He stared at the angel and, overcome with terror, said, “What is it, Lord?” The angel replied, “Your prayers and your alms have ascended as a memorial before God. 5Now send men to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter. 6He is lodging with Simon, a tanner, whose house stands by the sea.”

7When the angel who had spoken to him departed, Cornelius called two of his servants and a devout soldier among those who attended him. 8After explaining everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.

Peter’s Vision on the Rooftop

9The next day, as these men drew near the city, Peter ascended to the housetop about the sixth hour to pray. 10He grew hungry and desired to eat, but while they prepared food, he fell into a trance. 11He saw Heavenheavenheaven opened and an object resembling a great sheet descending, lowered to the earth by four corners. 12Within it were all kinds of four-footed animals, reptiles of the earth, and birds of the air.

13A voice commanded him, “Rise, Peter; kill and eat.”

14But Peter replied, “By no means, Lord, for I have never eaten anything common or unclean.”

15The voice addressed him again a second time: “What God has cleansed, you must not deem common.” 16This happened three times, and immediately the object was taken up into Heavenheavenheaven.

17While Peter inwardly deliberated what the vision he had seen might signify, behold, the men sent by Cornelius, having inquired for Simon’s house, stood at the gate. 18They called out and asked whether Simon, who was called Peter, was lodging there.

19While Peter contemplated the vision, the SpiritspiritSpirit said to him, “Behold, three men are seeking you. 20Rise and go down, and accompany them without hesitation, for I have sent them.”

21Peter descended to the men and said, “Behold, I am the one you seek. What is the reason for your coming?”

22They said, “Cornelius, a centurion, is a righteous and God-fearing man, well spoken of by the whole Jewish nation; he was directed by a holy angel to summon you to his house and to hear words from you.”

23So he invited them in and lodged them. The next day he rose and went out with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.

Peter Arrives in Caesarea

24The next day he entered Caesarea. Cornelius was expecting them, having assembled his relatives and close friends. 25When Peter entered, Cornelius met him, fell at his feet, and worshiped. 26Peter raised him up, saying, “Stand up; I too am a man.”

27While conversing with him, Peter entered and found many people gathered. 28He said to them, “You yourselves know how unlawful it is for a Jewish man to associate with or visit someone of another nation; but God has shown me that I should call no person common or unclean. 29Therefore I came without objection when I was summoned. I ask, then, why you sent for me.”

30Cornelius said, “Four days ago, at this very hour, I was praying in my house at the ninth hour; and behold, a man stood before me in bright clothing 31and said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your alms have been remembered before God. 32Send therefore to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter; he is lodging in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea.’ 33So I sent to you at once, and you have done well to come. Now we are all present before God to hear everything that has been commanded you by the Lord.”

Peter Proclaims Christ and the Spirit Is Given

44While Peter was still speaking these words, the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit fell on all who heard the word. 45The believers from the circumcision who had come with Peter were amazed, because the gift of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit had been poured out even on the Gentiles. 46For they were hearing them speak in tongues and magnify God. Then Peter declared, 47“Can anyone withhold water from baptizing these people who have received the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit just as we have?” 48And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay for some days.
