The Wicked Condemned and the Righteous Vindicated

For the choirmaster: to the tune "Do NotnotNot DestroydestroyDestroy." A miktam of David.

1Do you indeed speak justice, O rulers? Do you judge fairly, O sons of men? 2No, in your hearts you devise injustice; your hands mete out violence in the land.

3The wicked are estranged from the womb; those who speak lies go astray from birth.

4Their venom is like a serpent's venom, like a deaf adder that stops its ear, 5so that it cannot hear the voice of charmers or of the skilfulskilfulskillful enchanter.

6O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O the LORD, tear out the fangs of the young lions. 7May they vanish like water that runs off; when HeheHe bends HishisHis bow, may HishisHis arrows be blunted.

8Like a slug that melts as it crawls, like a stillborn child that never sees the sun.

9Before your pots can feel the heat of the thorns—whether green or dry—HeheHe will sweep them away in a whirlwind.

10The righteous will rejoice when they see vengeance; they will bathe their feet in the blood of the wicked. 11Then men will say, "Surely the righteous have a reward; surely there is a God who judges on the earth."
