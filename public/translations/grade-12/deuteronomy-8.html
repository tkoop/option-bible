Remembering God’s Discipline in the Wilderness

“1You shall be careful to do all the commandment that I command you today, so that you may live and multiply and go in and possess the land that the the LORD swore to your fathers. 2And you shall remember the whole way that the the LORD your God has led you these forty years in the wilderness, to humble you and test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep HishisHis commandments or not. 3He humbled you and let you hunger, and fed you with manna, which neither you nor your fathers had known, that HeheHe might make you know that man does not live by bread alone, but man lives by every word that comes from the mouth of the the LORD

“4Your clothing did not wear out from you, and your foot did not swell these forty years. 5Know then in your heart that, as a man disciplines his son, so the the LORD your God disciplines you. 6You shall keep the commandments of the the LORD your God, walking in HishisHis ways and fearing HimhimHim

The Good Land Ahead

“7For the the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of brooks of water, of springs and deep waters gushing out in valleys and hills, 8a land of wheat and barley, of vines and fig trees and pomegranates, a land of olive trees and honey, 9a land where you will eat bread without scarcity and lack nothing, a land whose stones are iron and out of whose hills you can dig copper. 10And you shall eat and be full, and you shall bless the the LORD your God for the good land HeheHe has given you

Beware of Forgetting God in Prosperity

“11Take care lest you forget the the LORD your God by not keeping HishisHis commandments, HishisHis ordinances, and HishisHis statutes, which I command you today, 12lest, when you have eaten and are full and have built good houses and live in them, 13and when your herds and flocks multiply and your silver and gold increase and all that you have increases—

“14then your heart be lifted up, and you forget the the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery, 15who led you through the great and terrifying wilderness, with its fiery serpents and scorpions and thirsty ground where there was no water, who brought water for you out of the flinty rock, 16who fed you in the wilderness with manna that your fathers did not know, that HeheHe might humble you and test you, to do you good in the end

“17Beware lest you say in your heart, ‘My power and the might of my hand have gotten me this wealth.’ 18You shall remember the the LORD your God, for it is HeheHe who gives you power to get wealth, that HeheHe may confirm HishisHis covenant that HeheHe swore to your fathers, as it is this day

A Warning Against Idolatry

“19And if you forget the the LORD your God and go after other gods and serve them and bow down to them, I testify against you today that you shall surely perish. 20Like the nations that the the LORD makes perish before you, so you shall perish, because you would not obey the voice of the the LORD your God.”
