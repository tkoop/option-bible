The Plague of Locusts

12Then the LORD said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts, so that they may swarm over the land of Egypt and eat every plant in the land—all that the hail has left.”

13So Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and the LORD brought an east wind upon the land all that day and all that night. By morning the east wind had brought the locusts. 14The locusts swarmed over the entire land of Egypt and settled in every territory of Egypt. Never before had there been such a severe locust plague, and never again will there be. 15They covered the face of all the ground until the land was black. They devoured all the plants of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left. Nothing green remained on any tree or plant throughout the land of Egypt.

16Then Pharaoh quickly summoned Moses and Aaron and said, “I have sinned against the LORD your God and against you. 17Now please forgive my sin once more and pray to the LORD your God, so that HeheHe will remove this deadly plague from me.”

The Plague of Darkness

21Then the LORD said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward the sky, so that darkness will spread over the land of Egypt—a darkness that can be felt.”

22So Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky, and total darkness covered the land of Egypt for three days. 23No one could see anyone else, and for three days no one left his place. Yet all the Israelites had light in their dwellings.

24Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and said, “Go, worship the LORD. Even your little ones may go with you. Only your flocks and herds must stay behind.”

25But Moses replied, “You must also provide us with sacrifices and burnt offerings to present to the LORD our God. 26Our livestock must also go with us; not a single animal will be left behind, because we will need some of them to worship the LORD our God, and we do not yet know what we will need to offer HimhimHim.”

29Moses replied, “As you have said, I will never see your face again.”
