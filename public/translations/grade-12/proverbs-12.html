The Character of the Righteous and the Wicked

1Whoever cherishes discipline cherishes knowledge, but whoever despises correction reveals himself as senseless. 2A virtuous man obtains favourfavourfavor from the LORD, but the LORD condemns the schemer. 3Wickedness establishes no one, whereas the righteous remain unshakable. 4A capable wife is her husband’s crown, but a shameful one corrodes his very bones. 5The thoughts of the righteous are just, while the counsel of the wicked is treacherous. 6The words of the wicked lie in ambush for bloodshed, but the speech of the upright delivers. 7The wicked are overturned and vanish, but the household of the righteous endures. 8A person is commended for sound judgmentjudgementjudgment, while a perverse heart is held in contempt. 9It is better to be lightly regarded yet possess a servant than to be self-important and go hungry.

Diligence, Speech, and Daily Consequences

10The righteous attend to the lives of their animals, but even the compassion of the wicked is cruel. 11Whoever tills his land will have abundant food, but whoever pursues empty schemes lacks sense. 12The wicked covet the gains of evildoers, but the righteous flourish. 13An evil person is snared by his transgressive speech, but the righteous escape trouble. 14A person is satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth, and the work of his hands returns to him. 15The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise man listens to counsel. 16A fool’s vexation is immediately evident, but a prudent person overlooks an insult. 17Whoever speaks truthfully proclaims what is right, but a false witness spreads deceit. 18Reckless words pierce like a sword, but the tongue of the wise brings healing. 19Truthful lips are established forever, but a lying tongue endures only a moment.

Truth, Peace, and the Path of Life

20Deceit fills the hearts of those who plot evil, but counselors of peace have joy. 21No evil shall befall the righteous, but the wicked are filled with calamity. 22Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD, but those who deal faithfully are HishisHis delight. 23A prudent person conceals knowledge, but the heart of fools broadcasts folly. 24The diligent hand will rule, but slackness leads to forced labourlabourlabor. 25Anxiety depresses the heart, but a good word gladdens it. 26The righteous guide their neighbors, but the way of the wicked leads them astray. 27A lazy man does not roast his catch, but a diligent man values his possessions. 28In the way of righteousness is life, and in its path there is no death.
