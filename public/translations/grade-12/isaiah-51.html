The LORD Calls the Righteous to Remember Their Origin

1“Listen to MemeMe, you who pursue righteousness, you who seek the LORD. Look to the rock from which you were hewn and to the quarry from which you were dug. 2Look to Abraham your father and to Sarah, who gave birth to you. For I called him when he was but one man, and I blessed him and multiplied him. 3For the LORD will comfort Zion; HeheHe will comfort all her ruined places and make her wilderness like Eden and her desert like the garden of the LORD. Joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving and the voice of song.”

An Everlasting Righteousness for All Peoples

4“Give heed to MemeMe, MymyMy people, and listen to MemeMe, MymyMy nation; for instruction will go forth from MemeMe, and I will establish MymyMy justice as a light for the peoples. 5My righteousness is near; MymyMy salvation has gone forth, and MymyMy arms will judge the peoples. The coastlands will wait for MemeMe, and on MymyMy arm they will hope. 6Lift up your eyes to the heavens and look at the earth beneath, for the heavens will vanish like smoke, the earth will wear out like a garment, and its inhabitants will die likewise; yet MymyMy salvation will be forever, and MymyMy righteousness will never be abolished.

7“Listen to MemeMe, you who know righteousness, the people in whose heart is MymyMy law: do not fear the reproach of men, and do not be dismayed by their revilings. 8For the moth will eat them up like a garment, and the worm will eat them like wool; but MymyMy righteousness will be forever, and MymyMy salvation to all generations.”

Awake, O Arm of the LORD, and Comfort Your People

9Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the LORD! Awake, as in the days of old, as in the generations of long ago. Was it not YouyouYou who cut Rahab in pieces, who pierced the dragon? 10Was it not YouyouYou who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep, who made the depths of the sea a pathway for the redeemed to pass over? 11Therefore the redeemed of the LORD will return and come with singing to Zion, and everlasting joy will crown their heads. They will obtain gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing will flee away.

The LORD Removes Jerusalem's Cup of Wrath

12“I, even I, am HeheHe who comforts you. Who are you that you are afraid of mortal man, of a son of man who is made like grass, 13and have forgotten the LORD, your MakermakerMaker, who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundations of the earth? You are in constant dread all day long because of the fury of the oppressor, as though he were ready to destroy. But where is the fury of the oppressor?

14“The captive exile will soon be released; he will not die in the pit, nor will his bread fail. 15For I am the LORD your God, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar—the LORD of Hosts is HishisHis name. 16I have put MymyMy words in your mouth and covered you with the shadow of MymyMy hand, to establish the heavens, to found the earth, and to say to Zion, ‘You are MymyMy people.’”

17Awake, awake, stand up, O Jerusalem! You who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of HishisHis fury, you have drunk the dregs of the cup of staggering. 18There is no one to guide her among all the sons she has borne, and no one to take her by the hand among all the sons she has brought up.

19These two things have happened to you: devastation and destruction, famine and sword. Who will grieve for you? Who will comfort you? 20Your sons have fainted; they lie at the head of every street, like a wild ox in a net. They are full of the wrath of the LORD and the rebuke of your God. 21Therefore hear this now, you who are afflicted and drunk, but not with wine.

22“Thus says your Lord, the LORD, and your God, who defends HishisHis people: ‘Behold, I have taken from your hand the cup of staggering; you will never again drink the dregs of the cup of MymyMy fury.’ 23I will put it into the hands of your tormentors, who said to you, ‘Bow down, that we may pass over you.’ You have made your back like the ground and like the street for them to pass over.”
