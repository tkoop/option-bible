Woe to the Rebellious City

1Woe to the oppressing city—rebellious and defiled! 2She has not obeyed the voice; she has not accepted correction; she has not trusted in the LORD; she has not drawn near to her God. 3Her princes within her are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves that leave nothing for the morning. 4Her prophets are reckless and treacherous; her priests have profaned the sanctuary and done violence to the law. 5The LORD within her is righteous; HeheHe does no wrong. Morning by morning HeheHe dispenses justice; HeheHe does not fail at dawn, yet the unjust knows no shame. 6I have cut off nations; their corner towers lie desolate; I have made their streets waste, so that no one passes by; their cities are destroyed, so that no man remains and no one dwells there.

The LORD’s Warning to Jerusalem

7I said, “Surely you will fear MemeMe and accept correction.” Then her dwelling would not be cut off for all that I have appointed against her. But they rose early and corrupted all their deeds. 8“Therefore wait for MemeMe,” declares the LORD, “until the day I rise to plunder. For MymyMy decision is to gather nations, to assemble kingdoms, to pour out upon them all MymyMy indignation—all MymyMy fierce anger. For in the fire of MymyMy jealousy all the earth will be consumed.”

Purification and Restoration of the Nations

9For at that time I will restore pure lips to the peoples, so that all of them may call upon the name of the LORD and serve HimhimHim with one accord. 10From beyond the rivers of Cush MymyMy worshipers, MymyMy dispersed ones, will bring MemeMe an offering.

11On that day you will not be put to shame for any of the deeds by which you have transgressed against MemeMe. For then I will remove from your midst those who rejoice in their pride, and you will never again be haughty on MymyMy holy mountain. 12But I will leave within your midst a meek and humble people, and they will trust in the name of the LORD. 13The remnant of Israel will no longer do wrong or speak lies, nor will a deceitful tongue be found in their mouths. They will feed and lie down, with no one to make them afraid.

Joy and Salvation for Zion

14Sing, O DaughterdaughterDaughter of Zion; shout aloud, O Israel! Be glad and rejoice with all your heart, O DaughterdaughterDaughter of Jerusalem! 15The LORD has taken away your punishment; HeheHe has turned back your enemy. The LORD, the KingkingKing of Israel, is in your midst; never again will you fear any harm.

16On that day it will be said to Jerusalem, “Do not fear, O Zion; do not let your hands hang limp.”

17The LORD your God is among you; HeheHe is mighty to save. He will rejoice over you with gladness; HeheHe will quiet you with HishisHis love; HeheHe will rejoice over you with singing.

18I will gather those among you who grieve over the appointed feasts, so that you will no longer suffer reproach. 19Behold, at that time I will deal with all your oppressors. I will save the lame and gather the outcast, and I will give them praise and honourhonourhonor in every land where they were put to shame. 20“At that time I will bring you in; at that time I will gather you. For I will give you fame and praise among all the peoples of the earth when I restore your fortunes before your very eyes,” says the LORD.
