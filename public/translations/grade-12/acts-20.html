Paul’s Ministry in Macedonia and Greece

1After the commotion had subsided, Paul summoned the disciples, exhorting them, bidding them farewell with an embrace, and set out for Macedonia. 2He traversed those territories, fortifying the believers through protracted discourse, and reached Greece. 3Following a three-month sojourn, he intended to sail for Syria; however, a Jewish conspiracy against him compelled his return through Macedonia. 4His companions were Sopater son of Pyrrhus, from Berea; Aristarchus and Secundus, from Thessalonica; Gaius of Derbe; and Timothy, Tychicus, and Trophimus, who were from Asia. 5These men preceded us and awaited us at Troas. 6Upon the conclusion of the DaysdaysDays of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread, we embarked from Philippi; five days later we rejoined them at Troas, where we tarried seven days.

The Miracle at Troas

7On the first day of the week, when we had congregated to break bread, Paul—intending to depart on the morrow—conversed with them and extended his discourse until midnight. 8Numerous lamps illuminated the upper chamber in which we were assembled. 9A youth named Eutychus, seated at the window, was overwhelmed by profound somnolence while Paul continued speaking; sinking deeper into slumber, he plummeted from the third storeythird storeythird story and was recovered dead. 10Paul descended, cast himself upon him, embraced him, and declared, “Do not be alarmed, for his life is in him.” 11He then ascended again, broke bread, partook, and conversed with them extensively until dawn, whereupon he departed. 12They conveyed the boy away alive and were exceedingly consoled.

Paul’s Voyage to Miletus

13We preceded them to the vessel and sailed to Assos, intending to take Paul aboard there; he had so arranged matters, since he intended to proceed by land. 14When he encountered us at Assos, we embarked him and sailed to Mitylene. 15Departing thence, we arrived the next day opposite Chios; on the subsequent day we touched at Samos; and on the following day we reached Miletus. 16Paul had determined to sail past Ephesus lest he be detained in Asia, for he was hastening to reach Jerusalem, if feasible, by the day of Pentecost.

Paul’s Farewell to the Ephesian Elders

17From Miletus he dispatched word to Ephesus and summoned the elders of the church. 18“You know how I comported myself among you from the first day I set foot in Asia, 19serving the Lord with all humility, with tears, and amid the tribulations that befell me through the machinations of the Jews; 20how I did not shrink from proclaiming to you anything advantageous, nor from instructing you publicly and from house to house, 21bearing witness to both Jews and Greeks concerning repentance toward God and faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.

22“And now, behold, I am journeying to Jerusalem, constrained by the SpiritspiritSpirit, unaware of what shall befall me there, 23save that the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit testifies to me in every city that imprisonment and afflictions await me. 24Yet I do not esteem my life precious to myself, if only I may finish my course and the ministry I received from the Lord Jesus, to bear witness to the gospel of the grace of God. 25And now, behold, I know that none of you among whom I have proclaimed the kingdom will behold my face again. 26Therefore I testify to you this day that I am innocent of the blood of all, 27for I did not shrink from declaring to you the entire counsel of God.

28“Give heed to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit has appointed you overseers, to shepherd the church of God, which HeheHe acquired with HishisHis own blood. 29I know that after my departure rapacious wolves will invade among you, sparing not the flock; 30and from yourselves men will arise, articulating distorted teachings, to entice the disciples to follow them. 31Therefore remain vigilant, remembering that for three years I did not cease admonishing each one night and day with tears.

32“And now I entrust you to God and to the word of HishisHis grace, which is able to edify you and to bestow upon you the inheritance among all the sanctified. 33I coveted no one’s silver, gold, or raiment. 34You yourselves know that these hands ministered to my necessities and to those who accompanied me. 35In all things I have exhibited to you that, by laboring strenuously, you must assist the weak, and remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how HeheHe said, ‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’”

36When he had spoken thus, he knelt with them all and prayed. 37A great lamentation arose among them all; they fell upon Paul’s neck and kissed him, sorrowing especially over the statement he had made, that they should see his face no more. 38Then they escorted him to the ship.
