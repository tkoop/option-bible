A Psalm of Thanksgiving for God’s Enduring Love

1Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 2Give thanks to the God of gods, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 3Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.

4To HimhimHim who alone does great wonders, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 5To HimhimHim who by understanding made the heavens, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 6To HimhimHim who spread out the earth above the waters, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 7To HimhimHim who made the great lights, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 8The sun to rule by day, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 9The moon and stars to rule by night, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.

10To HimhimHim who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 11And brought Israel out from among them, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 12With a strong hand and an outstretched arm, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 13To HimhimHim who divided the Red SeaseaSea in two, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 14And made Israel pass through the midst of it, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 15And overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red SeaseaSea, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 16To HimhimHim who led HishisHis people through the wilderness, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.

17To HimhimHim who struck down great kings, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 18And slew mighty kings, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 19Sihon, king of the Amorites, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 20And Og, king of Bashan, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 21And gave their land as an inheritance, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 22An inheritance to Israel HishisHis servant, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.

23He who remembered us in our low estate, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 24And rescued us from our foes, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 25He who gives food to all flesh, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 26Give thanks to the God of Heavenheavenheaven, for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.
