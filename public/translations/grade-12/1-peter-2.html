1Therefore, divest yourselves of all malice, all deceit, hypocrisy, envy, and every form of slander. 2Like newborn infants, long for the pure, spiritual milk, so that by it you may mature into your salvation, 3since you have tasted that the Lord is good.

Submission, Suffering, and Christ's Example

18Servants, submit yourselves to your masters with all reverence—not only to the good and gentle, but also to the harsh. 19For this is commendable, if, mindful of God, someone endures grief while suffering unjustly. 20What credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten, you endure? But if you suffer for doing good and endure, this is commendable in the sight of God. 21To this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, that you might follow in HishisHis footsteps. 22"He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in HishisHis mouth." 23When HeheHe was reviled, HeheHe did not revile in return; when HeheHe suffered, HeheHe uttered no threats, but entrusted HimselfhimselfHimself to HimhimHim who judges righteously. 24"He HimselfhimselfHimself bore our sins" in HishisHis body on the cross, that we, having died to sins, might live to righteousness; "by HishisHis wounds you have been healed." 25For "you were straying like sheep," but now you have returned to the ShepherdshepherdShepherd and OverseeroverseerOverseer of your souls.
