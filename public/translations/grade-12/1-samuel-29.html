The Philistines MobilizeMobiliseMobilize for War

1The Philistines mustered all their forces at Aphek, while Israel encamped beside the spring in Jezreel. 2As the Philistine rulers advanced with their contingents of hundreds and thousands, David and his men brought up the rear with Achish.

The Philistine Commanders Challenge David's Presence

3The Philistine commanders asked, “What are these Hebrews doing here?”

Achish answered, “Is this not David, servant of Saul, king of Israel? He has been with me for days and years, and I have found no fault in him from the day he defected until now.”

4But the Philistine commanders were furious with Achish and said, “Send the man back to the place you assigned him. He must not march with us into battle, or he may turn against us in the fight. What better way could he regain his master's favourfavourfavor than with the heads of our men? 5Is this not the David about whom they sing in their dances, ‘Saul has slain his thousands, and David his tens of thousands’?”

Achish Intercedes for David

6Then Achish summoned David and said, “As surely as the LORD lives, you have been upright, and your service with me in the camp has been good in my sight, for I have found no fault in you from the day you came to me until now. Nevertheless, the rulers are not pleased with you. 7Now turn back and go in peace, so that you do not displease the Philistine rulers.”

8David asked, “But what have I done? What have you found against your servant from the day I came to you until now? Why may I not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?”

9Achish replied, “I know that you are as pleasing in my sight as an angel of God. But the Philistine commanders have said, ‘He must not go up with us into battle.’ 10Now then, rise early in the morning with your master's servants who came with you, and as soon as you are up and it is light, depart.”

David Returns to Philistia

11So David and his men rose early in the morning to return to the land of the Philistines, while the Philistines advanced to Jezreel.
