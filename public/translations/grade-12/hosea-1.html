The LORD's Word to Hosea

1The word of the the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

2When the ThetheThe LORD first spoke through Hosea, the the LORD said to him, “Go, take for yourself a wife given to unfaithfulness, and children born of that unfaithfulness, for the land itself has been utterly unfaithful, turning away from the the LORD.”

3So he went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim; she conceived and bore him a son.

4Then the the LORD said to him, “Call his name Jezreel, for in a little while I will avenge the bloodshed of Jezreel on the house of Jehu and bring the kingdom of the house of Israel to an end. 5In that day I will break the bow of Israel in the ValleyvalleyValley of Jezreel.”

6She conceived again and bore a daughter, and HeheHe said to him, “Call her name Lo-Ruhamah, for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel, that I should forgive them. 7Yet I will have compassion on the house of Judah, and I will save them—not by bow or sword or war, not by horses or cavalry, but by the the LORD their God.”

8After she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son. 9And HeheHe said, “Call his name Lo-Ammi, for you are not MymyMy people, and I am not your God.”

A Promise of Restoration and Unity

10Yet the number of the Israelites will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted. In the place where it was said to them, “You are not MymyMy people,” they will be called “sons of the living God.” 11The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together; they will appoint one leader for themselves and go up from the land, for great will be the day of Jezreel.
