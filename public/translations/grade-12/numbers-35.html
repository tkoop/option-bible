Cities Allotted to the Levites

1While Israel was encamped in the plains of Moab beside the Jordan opposite Jericho, the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2"Command the Israelites to give the Levites cities to live in from the inheritance they possess, and you shall also give the Levites pasturelands around those cities. 3The cities shall be theirs for residence, and their pasturelands shall be for their cattle, their property, and all their animals. 4The pasturelands of the cities that you give the Levites shall extend a thousand cubits from the city wall in every direction. 5You shall measure outside the city two thousand cubits on the east, two thousand on the south, two thousand on the west, and two thousand on the north, with the city in the centrecentrecenter; these shall be the pasturelands of the cities. 6Among the cities you give the Levites shall be six cities of refuge, which you shall designate so that a manslayer may flee there; besides these, you shall give them forty-two cities. 7The total number of cities you give the Levites shall be forty-eight, with their pasturelands. 8The cities you give from the Israelites' possession shall be given according to the size of each tribe's inheritance: from the larger inheritance you shall take more, and from the smaller you shall take less."

Cities of Refuge for the Unintentional Killer

9The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 10"Speak to the Israelites and say to them that when you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan, 11you shall designate cities to be cities of refuge for you, so that a manslayer who kills a person unintentionally may flee there. 12These cities shall be a refuge for you from the blood avenger, so that the manslayer does not die until he stands before the assembly for judgmentjudgementjudgment. 13The six cities you give shall be your cities of refuge. 14You shall give three cities beyond the Jordan and three cities in the land of Canaan; they shall be cities of refuge. 15These six cities shall be a refuge for the Israelites, for the foreigner, and for the sojourner among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally may flee there.

Murder, Manslaughter, and Blood Guilt

16"If anyone strikes a person with an iron object and that person dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death. 17If anyone strikes a person with a stone that can cause death and that person dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death. 18If anyone strikes a person with a wooden object that can cause death and that person dies, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death. 19The blood avenger shall put the murderer to death; when he meets him, he shall kill him. 20If anyone pushes another out of hatred, or throws something at him with intent, and he dies, 21or if he strikes him in enmity with his hand and he dies, the one who struck him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer. The blood avenger shall put the murderer to death when he finds him. 22But if he pushes him suddenly without enmity, or throws any object at him without intent, 23or drops a stone on him without seeing him, and it causes death, though he was not his enemy and did not seek his harm, 24then the assembly shall judge between the one who struck and the blood avenger according to these ordinances. 25The assembly shall rescue the manslayer from the hand of the blood avenger and return him to the city of refuge to which he fled, and he shall live there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil. 26But if the manslayer goes at all beyond the boundary of his city of refuge to which he fled, 27and the blood avenger finds him outside the boundary of his city of refuge and kills the manslayer, the avenger shall not be guilty of blood. 28For the manslayer must remain in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest; after the death of the high priest, the manslayer may return to the land of his possession. 29These things shall be a statute of judgmentjudgementjudgment for you throughout your generations in all your dwellings. 30If anyone kills a person, the murderer shall be put to death on the testimony of witnesses; but no one may be put to death on the testimony of a single witness. 31You shall take no ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death; he shall surely be put to death. 32You shall take no ransom to allow a manslayer to leave his city of refuge and dwell in the land before the death of the high priest. 33So you shall not pollute the land in which you are; for blood pollutes the land, and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood that is shed in it except by the blood of the one who shed it. 34You shall not defile the land in which you dwell, where I dwell among you; for I am the LORD, dwelling among the Israelites."
