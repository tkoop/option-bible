Prohibition of Idolatry and Reverence for the Sanctuary

1"Do not make worthless idols for yourselves, nor set up a carved image or a sacred pillar, and do not place a sculptured stone in your land to bow down to it, for I am the LORD your God. 2You shall keep MymyMy Sabbaths and reverence MymyMy sanctuary: I am the LORD."

Blessings Promised for Obedience

3"If you walk in MymyMy statutes and keep MymyMy commandments and do them, 4then I will give you your rains in their season, and the land shall yield its increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit. 5Your threshing shall last to the time of the grape harvest, and the grape harvest shall last to the time of sowing. You shall eat your bread to the full and dwell in your land securely."

6"And I will give peace in the land, and you shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid. And I will remove harmful beasts from the land, and the sword shall not go through your land. 7You shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword. 8Five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall chase ten thousand, and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword."

9"I will turn to you and make you fruitful and multiply you and will establish MymyMy covenant with you. 10You shall eat provisions long stored, and you shall clear out the old to make room for the new."

11"I will make MymyMy dwelling among you, and MymyMy soul shall not abhor you. 12And I will walk among you and will be your God, and you shall be MymyMy people. 13I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that you should not be their slaves. And I have broken the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright."

Curses Pronounced for Disobedience

14"But if you will not listen to MemeMe and will not do all these commandments, 15if you spurn MymyMy statutes, and if your soul abhors MymyMy rules, so that you will not do all MymyMy commandments, but break MymyMy covenant, 16then I will do this to you: I will visit you with panic, with wasting disease and fever that dim the eyes and drain the life. And you shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it. 17I will set MymyMy face against you, and you shall be struck down before your enemies. Those who hate you shall rule over you, and you shall flee when none pursues."

18"And if in spite of this you will not listen to MemeMe, then I will discipline you sevenfold for your sins. 19And I will break your proud strength, and I will make your heavens like iron and your EarthEarthearth like bronze. 20And your strength shall be spent in vain, for your land shall not yield its increase, and the trees of the land shall not yield their fruit."

21"Then if you remain hostile to MemeMe and will not listen to MemeMe, I will continue striking you, sevenfold, for your sins. 22And I will let loose the wild beasts against you, which shall bereave you of your children and destroy your livestock and make you few in number, so that your roads shall be deserted."

23"And if by this discipline you are not turned to MemeMe but remain hostile to MemeMe, 24then I also will remain hostile to you, and I MyselfmyselfMyself will strike you sevenfold for your sins. 25And I will bring a sword upon you, that shall execute vengeance for the covenant. And if you gather within your cities, I will send pestilence among you, and you shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy. 26When I break your supply of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in a single oven and shall dole out your bread by weight, and you shall eat and not be satisfied."

27"But if in spite of this you will not listen to MemeMe, but remain hostile to MemeMe, 28then I will walk in fury against you, and I MyselfmyselfMyself will discipline you sevenfold for your sins. 29You shall eat the flesh of your sons, and you shall eat the flesh of your daughters."

30"And I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars and cast your dead bodies upon the dead bodies of your worthless idols, and MymyMy soul will abhor you. 31And I will lay your cities waste and will make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not accept your soothing aromas. 32And I MyselfmyselfMyself will devastate the land, so that your enemies who dwell in it shall be appalled. 33And I will scatter you among the nations, and I will unsheathe the sword after you, and your land shall be a desolation, and your cities shall be a waste."

34"Then the land shall enjoy its Sabbaths as long as it lies desolate, while you are in your enemies' land; then the land shall rest, and enjoy its Sabbaths. 35As long as it lies desolate it shall have rest, the rest that it did not have on your Sabbaths when you dwelt on it. 36And as for those of you who are left, I will send faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a driven leaf shall put them to flight, and they shall flee as one flees from the sword, and they shall fall when none pursues. 37They shall stumble over one another, as if to escape a sword, though none pursues. And you shall have no power to stand before your enemies. 38And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall eat you up. 39And those of you who are left shall rot away in your enemies' lands because of their iniquity, and also because of the iniquities of their fathers they shall rot away like them."

Promise of Restoration After Repentance

40"But if they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers in their treachery that they committed against MemeMe, and also in remaining hostile to MemeMe, 41so that I opposed them and brought them into the land of their enemies—if then their hardened heart is humbled and they make amends for their iniquity, 42then I will remember MymyMy covenant with Jacob, and I will remember MymyMy covenant with Isaac and MymyMy covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land. 43But the land shall be abandoned by them and enjoy its Sabbaths while it lies desolate without them, and they shall make amends for their iniquity, because they spurned MymyMy rules and their soul abhorred MymyMy statutes."

44"Yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not spurn them, neither will I abhor them so as to destroy them utterly, and break MymyMy covenant with them, for I am the LORD their God. 45But I will for their sake remember the covenant with their forefathers, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that I might be their God: I am the LORD."

46These are the statutes and rules and laws that the LORD made between HimhimHim and the people of Israel through Moses on Mount Sinai.
