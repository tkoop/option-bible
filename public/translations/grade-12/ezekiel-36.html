The ancient heights have become our possession,’ 3therefore prophesy and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Because they have made you desolate and have trampled you on every side, so that you became a possession of the rest of the nations and the subject of gossip and slander among the people,

4Therefore, O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD. Restoration of Israel’s Land

Let this be known to you. Be ashamed and disgraced for your ways, O house of Israel!

Rebuilding and Repopulation

33Thus says the Lord GOD: On the day I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will resettle the cities, and the ruins will be rebuilt. 34The desolate land will be cultivated instead of lying desolate in the sight of all who pass by. 35Then they will say, ‘This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden, and the cities that were ruined, desolate, and destroyed are now fortified and inhabited.’ 36Then the nations around you that remain will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt what was destroyed and have replanted what was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.

37Thus says the Lord GOD: Once again I will allow the house of Israel to ask MemeMe for this: I will multiply their people like a flock. 38Like the flock of holy things, like the flock of Jerusalem during her appointed feasts, so will the ruined cities be filled with flocks of people.
