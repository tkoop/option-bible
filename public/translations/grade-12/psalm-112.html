The Prosperity and Steadfastness of the Righteous

1Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD and takes great delight in HishisHis commandments. 2His descendants will be mighty in the land, and the generation of the upright will be blessed. 3Wealth and riches will dwell in his house, and his righteousness will endure forever. 4Light dawns in the darkness for the upright; he is gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

5It is good for a man to be gracious and to lend; he will sustain his cause in judgmentjudgementjudgment. 6Surely he will never be shaken, and the righteous will be remembered forever. 7He will not fear dire tidings; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD. 8His heart is secure; he will not fear until he beholds his adversaries in triumph.

9He has distributed his gifts freely to the needy; his righteousness endures forever, and his horn will be exalted in honourhonourhonor. 10The wicked will see and be incensed; he will gnash his teeth and waste away, and the desire of the wicked will perish.
