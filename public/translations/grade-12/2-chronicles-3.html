Solomon Begins Building the Temple

1Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to his father David. This was the place David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 2He began building on the second day of the second month in the fourth year of his reign.

The Temple's Foundation and Portico

3The foundation Solomon laid for building the house of God was sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide, according to the old standard. 4The portico at the front of the temple was twenty cubits long across the width of the building and one hundred twenty cubits high. He overlaid the inside with pure gold.

Ornamentation of the Sanctuary

5He panelledpanelledpaneled the main hall with cypress and covered it with fine gold, and adorned it with palm trees and chains. 6He adorned the temple with precious stones for beauty, and its gold was gold of Parvaim. 7He overlaid its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold, and carved cherubim on the walls.

He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold. 10In the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace he made two cherubim of sculptured work, and he overlaid them with gold.

11The wingspan of the cherubim was twenty cubits. One wing of one cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple, and the other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the other cherub. 12The wing of the other cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple, and its other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the first cherub. 13The wings of these cherubim extended twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, and their faces were turned toward the main hall.

14He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, with cherubim woven into it.

The Pillars of the Portico

15In front of the temple he made two pillars, thirty-five cubits high, with a capital of five cubits on top of each. 16He made interlocking chains and put them on top of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and fastened them to the chains. 17He set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and one on the left. He named the pillar on the right Jachin and the pillar on the left Boaz.
