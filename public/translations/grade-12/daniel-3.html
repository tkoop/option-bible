Nebuchadnezzar's Golden Image

1King Nebuchadnezzar made a golden image sixty cubits high and six cubits wide, and he set it up on the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon. 2Then King Nebuchadnezzar sent word to assemble the satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all the other provincial officials, so that they might attend the dedication of the image he had set up. 3So the satraps, prefects, governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, magistrates, and all the other provincial officials assembled for the dedication of the image that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up, and they stood before it.

The Royal Command to Worship

4Then the herald proclaimed with a mighty voice, “Peoples, nations, and languages, this is what you are commanded: 5As soon as you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music, you are to fall down and worship the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar has set up. 6Whoever does not fall down and worship shall be thrown immediately into the midst of a blazing furnace.” 7Therefore, when all the peoples heard the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, and every kind of music, all the peoples, nations, and languages fell down and worshiped the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

The Accusation Against the Jews

8At that time certain Chaldean astrologers came forward and maliciously accused the Jews. 9They said to King Nebuchadnezzar, “O king, may you live forever! 10You, O king, have issued a decree that everyone who hears the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music must fall down and worship the golden image. 11And whoever does not fall down and worship shall be thrown into the midst of a blazing furnace. 12There are certain Jews whom you have appointed over the administration of the province of Babylon—Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego—who pay no heed to you, O king. They neither serve your gods nor worship the golden image that you have set up.”

The Fiery Furnace and Divine Deliverance

13Then Nebuchadnezzar, in a furious rage, commanded that Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego be brought before him, so these men were brought before the king. 14Nebuchadnezzar said to them, “Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not serve my gods and do not worship the golden image that I have set up? 15Now if you are ready, when you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipes, and every kind of music, you are to fall down and worship the image I have made, very well. But if you do not worship it, you shall be thrown immediately into a blazing furnace. And what god is there who can deliver you from my hand?”

16Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered the king, “O Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to answer you in this matter. 17If we are thrown into the blazing furnace, the God we serve is able to deliver us from it, and HeheHe will deliver us from your hand, O king. 18But if not, be it known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden image that you have set up.”

19Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with rage, and the expression of his face changed toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He commanded that the furnace be heated seven times more than it was usually heated. 20And he commanded certain mighty men of valourvalourvalor in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and to cast them into the blazing furnace. 21Then these men were bound in their robes, trousers, turbans, and other garments, and they were thrown into the midst of the blazing furnace. 22Because the king's command was urgent and the furnace exceedingly hot, the flame of the fire killed those men who carried Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. 23And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell bound into the midst of the blazing furnace.

24Then King Nebuchadnezzar was astonished and rose up in haste. He asked his advisers, “Did we not cast three men bound into the fire?”

They answered the king, “Certainly, O king.”

25He said, “Look, I see four men unbound, walking in the midst of the fire, and they are unharmed; and the fourth has the appearance of a son of the gods.”

26Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the opening of the blazing furnace and said, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God, come out and come here!” Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out of the midst of the fire.

27The satraps, prefects, governors, and the king's advisers gathered together and saw that the fire had no power over the bodies of those men; the hair of their heads was not singed, their robes were not harmed, and the smell of fire had not come upon them.

28Nebuchadnezzar said, “Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who has sent HishisHis angel and delivered HishisHis servants, who trusted in HimhimHim and defied the king's command, and gave up their bodies rather than serve or worship any god except their own God. 29Therefore I issue a decree: any people, nation, or language that says anything offenciveoffenciveoffensive against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego shall be cut into pieces, and their houses shall be reduced to rubble, because there is no other god who can save in this way.”

30Then the king advanced Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in the province of Babylon.
