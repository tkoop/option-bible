A Plea to Remember Our Disgrace

1Remember, the LORD, what has befallen us; look upon our disgrace and see it. 2Our inheritance has been transferred to strangers, and our dwellings to foreigners. 3We have become orphans, fatherless; our mothers are like widows. 4We must purchase the water we drink, and our wood comes only at a price.

The Burdens of Subjugation

5Our pursuers press upon our necks; we are exhausted, and no rest is granted to us. 6We have submitted to Egypt and to Assyria merely to obtain sufficient bread. 7Our fathers sinned and are gone, yet we bear their iniquities. 8Slaves dominate us, and there is no deliverer from their hand. 9We obtain our bread at the risk of our lives, because of the sword in the wilderness. 10Our skin is scorched like an oven by the fierce heat of famine. 11Women are violated in Zion, and virgins in the cities of Judah. 12Princes are suspended by their hands, and the elders receive no honourhonourhonor.
