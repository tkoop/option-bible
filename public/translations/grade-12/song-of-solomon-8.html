The Bride’s Yearning for Her Beloved

1“If only you were my brother, nursed at my mother’s breast, I could kiss you before the world, and none would despise me. 2I would lead you and bring you to my mother’s house, where you would teach me; I would make you drink spiced wine, the rich juice pressed from my pomegranate. 3His left hand would support my head, while his right arm embraced me. 4I charge you, daughters of Jerusalem: do not rouse or awaken love until it is ready.”

Love Stronger Than Death

5“Who is this ascending from the wilderness, leaning upon her beloved?”

“Beneath the apple tree I awakened you; there your mother labouredlabouredlabored with you, and there the woman who bore you was in travail. 6Place me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm; for love is as strong as death, and jealousy is as unyielding as the grave. Its flashes are flashes of fire—the very flame of the LORD. 7Many waters cannot quench love, nor can rivers sweep it away. If a man offered all the wealth of his house for love, he would be utterly despised.”

The Brothers and Their Sister

8“We have a little sister, and she has no breasts. What shall we do for our sister on the day she is spoken for? 9If she proves a wall, we will build upon her a silver parapet; if she proves a door, we will enclose her with cedar boards.”

10“I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers. Then I became in his eyes as one who finds peace.”

The Vineyard and the Final Call

11“Solomon possessed a vineyard at Baal-hamon; he entrusted it to keepers, each of whom was to bring a thousand pieces of silver for its fruit. 12My vineyard, my own, is before me. You, Solomon, may have the thousand, and the keepers two hundred.”

13“O you who dwell in the gardens, while companions listen for your voice—let me hear it.”

14“Hasten, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young stag upon the mountains of spices.”
