Woe to the Treacherous Destroyer

1Woe to you, destroyer, though you have not been destroyed; and woe to you, traitor, though no one has betrayed you! When you have finished destroying, you will be destroyed; when you cease betraying, they will betray you.

A Prayer for Grace and Deliverance

2O the LORD, be gracious to us; we have waited for YouyouYou. Be our strength each morning, and our salvation in the hour of distress.

3At the roar of the nations, peoples fled; at your exaltation, they were scattered. 4Your spoil will be gathered as the caterpillar gathers; like swarming locusts, they will swarm over it.

5The LORD is exalted, for HeheHe dwells on high; HeheHe has filled Zion with justice and righteousness. 6Steadfastness will mark your times, a wealth of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge; the fear of the the LORD is your treasure.

7Behold, their warriors cry out in the streets; the messengers of peace weep bitterly. 8The highways are deserted; wayfarers have ceased. The covenant is broken; cities are despised; humanity is no longer regarded. 9The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is ashamed and withers; Sharon is like a desert; Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves.

The LORD Arises in JudgmentJudgementJudgment

10“Now I will arise,” says the the LORD. “Now I will be exalted; now I will be lifted up.”

11You conceive chaff, and you give birth to stubble; your breath is a fire that consumes you. 12The peoples will be burned to lime; like thorns cut down, they will be set ablaze.

13You who are far off, hear what I have done; you who are near, acknowledge MymyMy power.

The Terror of the Wicked and the Hope of the Righteous

14The sinners in Zion are terrified; trembling grips the godless: “Who among us can dwell with consuming fire? Who among us can dwell with everlasting burning?”

15He who walks in righteousness and speaks what is right, who despises gain from oppression, who shakes his hands free of bribes, who stops his ears from hearing of bloodshed and shuts his eyes against seeing evil— 16he will dwell on the heights; his refuge will be the mountain fortress; his food will be provided, and his water assured.

17Your eyes will see the KingkingKing in HishisHis beauty; they will behold a land stretching afar. 18Your heart will ponder the terror: “Where is the scribe? Where is the weigher? Where is the counter of towers?”

19You will no longer see the insolent people, a people whose speech is too obscure to comprehend, whose stammering tongue cannot be understood.

20Look upon Zion, the city of our appointed feasts! Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a tranquil habitation, a tent that will not be moved; its stakes will never be pulled up, nor will any of its cords be broken. 21But there the MajesticmajesticMajestic OneoneOne, the the LORD, will be for us a place of rivers and wide canals, where no galley with oars will go, and no majestic vessel will pass. 22For the the LORD is our JudgejudgeJudge, the the LORD is our LawgiverlawgiverLawgiver, the the LORD is our King; HeheHe will save us.

23Your rigging is slack; it cannot hold the mast firmly, nor can it spread the sail. Then an abundance of spoil will be divided; even the lame will carry off plunder. 24And no resident will say, “I am sick.” The people who dwell there will be forgiven their iniquity.
