Construction of the Temple Begins

1In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites came out of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv—the second month—Solomon began to build the house of the the LORD. 2The house that King Solomon built for the the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. 3The portico in front of the nave of the house was twenty cubits long, corresponding to the width of the house, and ten cubits deep in front of the house. 4He also made for the house windows with recessed frames. 5Against the wall of the house he built a structure around the walls of the house, both the nave and the inner sanctuary, and he made side chambers all around. 6The lowest storeylowest storeylowest story was five cubits wide, the middle six cubits, and the third seven cubits; for he made offsets in the wall around the outside of the house, so that the beams would not be fastened into the walls of the house. 7The house was built of stone finished at the quarry, so that neither hammer nor axe nor any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built. 8The entrance to the middle story was on the south side of the house; one ascended by winding stairs to the middle story, and from the middle story to the third. 9So he built the house and finished it; he roofed the house with beams and boards of cedar. 10He built the side chambers all along the house, each five cubits high, and they were joined to the house with timbers of cedar.

The LORD’s Promise to Solomon

11The word of the the LORD came to Solomon: 12“As for this house that you are building, if you walk in MymyMy statutes, observe MymyMy ordinances, and keep all MymyMy commandments by walking in them, then I will establish MymyMy word with you, which I spoke to your father David. 13And I will dwell among the children of Israel and will not forsake MymyMy people Israel.”

The Temple’s Interior and Sacred Furnishings

14So Solomon built the house and finished it. 15He panelledpanelledpaneled the walls of the house from floor to ceiling with cedar boards and overlaid the floor of the house with cypress boards. 16He built twenty cubits of the rear of the house with cedar boards from floor to ceiling and made it within an inner sanctuary, the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. 17The house—that is, the nave in front of the inner sanctuary—was forty cubits long. 18The interior of the house was carved cedar, adorned with gourds and open flowers; everything was cedar, and no stone was visible. 19He prepared an inner sanctuary within the house to place there the ark of the covenant of the the LORD. 20The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high, and he overlaid it with pure gold; he also overlaid the altar of cedar. 21Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold and drew chains of gold across the front of the inner sanctuary, overlaying it with gold. 22He overlaid the whole house with gold until the whole house was finished; he also overlaid with gold the whole altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary.

23In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits high. 24One wing of the first cherub was five cubits, and the other wing five cubits; from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other was ten cubits. 25The second cherub was also ten cubits; both cherubim had the same measurement and the same form. 26The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so was the other. 27He set the cherubim in the innermost part of the house; their wings were spread out so that the wing of one touched the wall, the wing of the other touched the opposite wall, and their wings touched one another in the middle of the house. 28He overlaid the cherubim with gold. 29He carved all the walls of the house around, both inner and outer sanctuaries, with carved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. 30He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, both inner and outer. 31For the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel and doorposts were a fifth part of the wall. 32The two doors were of olive wood, and he carved on them carvings of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, overlaying them with gold; he spread the gold upon the cherubim and upon the palm trees. 33So also he made for the entrance of the nave doorposts of olive wood, a fourth part of the wall. 34The two doors were of cypress wood, each with two folding leaves. 35He carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold, evenly applied over the carved work.

Completion of the Temple

36He built the inner court with three courses of cut stone and a course of cedar beams. 37In the fourth year the foundation of the house of the the LORD was laid, in the month of Ziv. 38In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, the eighth month, the house was finished in all its parts and according to all its specifications; so he was seven years in building it.
