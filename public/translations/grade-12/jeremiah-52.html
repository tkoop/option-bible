Zedekiah’s Reign and Jerusalem’s Doom

1Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eleven years. His mother’s name was Hamutal, the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah. 2He did evil in the sight of the the LORD, just as Jehoiakim had done. 3Because of the anger of the the LORD against Jerusalem and Judah, HeheHe finally banished them from HishisHis presence; and Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.

The Siege and Fall of Jerusalem

4In the ninth year of Zedekiah’s reign, on the tenth day of the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came with his entire army against Jerusalem. They encamped around it and built siege works against it. 5The city remained under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah. 6By the fourth month, on the ninth day, the famine in the city had grown severe, and there was no bread for the people. 7Then the city wall was breached. While the Chaldeans surrounded the city, all the men of war fled by night through the gate between the two walls near the king’s garden and made for the Arabah. 8But the Chaldean army pursued the king and overtook Zedekiah on the plains of Jericho, and his whole army scattered from him.

9They captured the king and brought him to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he pronounced judgmentjudgementjudgment on him. 10There at Riblah the king of Babylon slaughtered Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes, and he also slaughtered all the princes of Judah. 11Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, bound him with bronze shackles, took him to Babylon, and imprisoned him until the day of his death.

The Destruction of the Temple and the Exile

12On the tenth day of the fifth month—in the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon—Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, who served in the presence of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. 13He burned the house of the the LORD, the king’s palace, and all the houses of Jerusalem; every important building he burned with fire. 14The whole Chaldean army, together with the captain of the guard, tore down all the walls around Jerusalem. 15Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, carried into exile the rest of the people who remained in the city, those who had deserted to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude. 16But he left some of the poorest of the land to tend the vineyards and fields.

17The Chaldeans broke up the bronze pillars, the stands, and the bronze SeaseaSea in the house of the the LORD, and they carried all the bronze to Babylon. 18They also took the pots, shovels, snuffers, basins, pans, and all the bronze utensils used in the temple service.

19The captain of the guard took the basins, censers, sprinkling bowls, pots, lampstands, dishes, and bowls—everything made of gold or silver. 20The two pillars, the SeaseaSea, and the twelve bronze bulls beneath the stands, which King Solomon had made for the house of the the LORD, were beyond weighing; their bronze could not be measured.

21Each pillar stood eighteen cubits high; its circumference was twelve cubits, and its wall was four fingers thick and hollow. 22The bronze capital on top was five cubits high, and around it were a latticework and pomegranates, all of bronze. The second pillar was the same. 23There were ninety-six pomegranates on the outside, and one hundred pomegranates in all around the latticework.

24The captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the three doorkeepers. 25From the city he also took the eunuch who oversaw the men of war, seven of the king’s close advisors who were found in the city, the scribe of the commander of the army who conscripted the people of the land, and sixty of the common people who were found in the city. 26Nebuzaradan took them and brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah. 27There in the land of Hamath the king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death. Thus Judah was carried away captive from its land.

28This is the number of people Nebuchadnezzar carried into exile: in the seventh year, 3,023 Jews. 29In the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar, 832 people from Jerusalem. 30In the twenty-third year, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away 745 Jews. In all, 4,600 people were taken captive.

Jehoiachin Released and HonouredHonouredHonored in Babylon

31On the twenty-fifth day of the twelfth month, in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin king of Judah, Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the first year of his reign, pardoned Jehoiachin king of Judah and released him from prison. 32He spoke kindly to him and set his throne above the thrones of the other kings who were with him in Babylon. 33Jehoiachin changed out of his prison clothes and ate regularly at the king’s table for the rest of his life. 34For the rest of his life, the king of Babylon gave him a daily allowance, a portion each day, until the day of his death.
