The Ark Is Taken to Ashdod

1Now the Philistines had seized the ark of God and brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 2They carried the ark of God into the temple of Dagon and set it beside Dagon.

Dagon Falls Before the Ark

3When the people of Ashdod rose early the next morning, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD. They lifted Dagon and restored him to his place. 4But when they rose early again the following morning, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD; his head and both hands were severed at the threshold, and only his torso remained. 5Therefore, to this day, neither the priests of Dagon nor anyone who enters Dagon's temple in Ashdod steps on the threshold.

The LORD Afflicts Ashdod

6The hand of the LORD was heavy upon the Ashdodites; HeheHe devastated them and struck them with tumors, both in Ashdod and throughout its surrounding territory. 7When the men of Ashdod saw what had happened, they said, "ThetheThe ark of the God of Israel must not stay with us, for HishisHis hand is heavy upon us and upon Dagon our god."

The Ark Is Sent to Gath and Ekron

8So they sent for and gathered all the lords of the Philistines. Then they asked, "What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?"

The lords replied, "Let the ark of the God of Israel be carried to Gath." So they carried the ark of the God of Israel there.

9After it had been carried there, the hand of the LORD was against that city, producing great panic; HeheHe struck the men of the city, from the youngest to the oldest, and tumors broke out among them.

10They then sent the ark of God to Ekron. When the ark of God arrived in Ekron, the Ekronites cried out, "They have brought the ark of the God of Israel to me in order to kill me and my people!"

11So they sent for and gathered all the lords of the Philistines and said, "Send the ark of the God of Israel away; let it return to its own place, so that it will not kill me and my people." For a deadly panic had seized the whole city, and the hand of God was exceedingly heavy there.
