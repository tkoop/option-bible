Destruction of Idolatry Throughout the Land

1When all this had ended, the Israelites who were present went out to the cities of Judah and broke the sacred stones, cut down the Asherah poles, and tore down the high places and altars throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh, until they had destroyed them all; then all Israel returned to their own cities, each to his own property.

Restoration of Priestly Service and Royal Provision

2Hezekiah reestablished the divisions of the priests and Levites, assigning each to his duties—priests and Levites alike—for burnt offerings and peace offerings, for ministry, for giving thanks, and for singing praises at the gates of the LORD. 3The king also contributed from his own wealth for the morning and evening burnt offerings, as well as for the burnt offerings on the Sabbaths, NewnewNew MoonsmoonsMoons, and appointed feasts, as it is written in the LawlawLaw of the LORD. 4Moreover, he commanded the people living in Jerusalem to give the portion due to the priests and Levites, so that they could devote themselves to the LawlawLaw of the LORD.

The People's Generous Tithes and Offerings

5As soon as the command was issued, the Israelites gave generously the firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and all the produce of the field, and they brought in a great abundance—a tithe of everything. 6The Israelites and Judahites who lived in the cities of Judah also brought a tithe of their herds and flocks and a tithe of the holy things consecrated to the LORD their God, and they piled them in heaps. 7They began building the heaps in the third month and finished in the seventh month. 8When Hezekiah and his officials came and saw the heaps, they blessed the LORD and HishisHis people Israel. 9Hezekiah then inquired of the priests and Levites concerning the heaps, 10and Azariah the chief priest of the house of Zadok answered him, "Since the people began bringing their contributions into the house of the LORD, we have had enough to eat and plenty remains, because the LORD has blessed HishisHis people; this great abundance is what is left."

Stewardship, Distribution, and Hezekiah's Faithfulness

11Then Hezekiah commanded them to prepare storerooms in the house of the LORD, and they did so. 12They faithfully brought in the contributions, tithes, and dedicated gifts. Conaniah the Levite was in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second. 13Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were supervisors under Conaniah and his brother Shimei, by appointment of King Hezekiah and of Azariah the chief official of the house of God.

14Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the keeper of the East GategateGate, was in charge of the freewill offerings to God, to distribute the contributions to the LORD and the consecrated things. 15Under his authority, Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah faithfully distributed the portions to their brothers in the cities of the priests, according to their divisions, to great and small alike, 16as well as to those enrolled by genealogy—males three years of age and older—for everyone who entered the house of the LORD to perform the daily service according to their duties and divisions. 17As for the priests, they were enrolled according to their families in the genealogical records, and the Levites twenty years of age and older, according to their duties and divisions. 18The genealogy included all their little ones, wives, sons, and daughters throughout the whole assembly, for they were faithful in consecrating themselves. 19As for the priests, the sons of Aaron, who lived in the pasturelands around their cities, men were designated by name to distribute a portion to every male among the priests and to every Levite listed in the genealogical records.

20So Hezekiah did throughout all Judah, doing what was good and right and true before the LORD his God. 21In every work that he began in the service of the house of God, in the law and in the commandment, to seek his God, he did with all his heart, and he prospered.
