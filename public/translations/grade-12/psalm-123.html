A Pilgrim’s Prayer for Mercy

1A song of ascents. I lifted my eyes to YouyouYou, the OneoneOne enthroned in Heavenheavenheaven.

2Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maidservant look to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to the LORD our God, until HeheHe shows us favourfavourfavor.

3Be gracious to us, O the the LORD, be gracious to us, for we have had more than enough of contempt. 4Our soul has had more than enough of the scorn of the complacent and the contempt of the proud.
