Israel’s Zeal Without Knowledge

1Brothers, the longing of my heart and my prayer to God for them is their salvation. 2For I testify concerning them that they possess a zeal for God, yet not in accordance with knowledge. 3For, being ignorant of the righteousness of God and seeking to establish their own, they did not submit to God’s righteousness. 4For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes.

Righteousness by Faith

Israel’s Unbelief and God’s Patience

18But I say, did they not hear? Indeed they did: “Their voice has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.”
