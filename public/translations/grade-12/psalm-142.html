A Prayer from the Cave

1I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift my voice to the LORD and plead for mercy. 2I pour out my complaint before HimhimHim, and I declare my trouble before HimhimHim. 3When my spirit grows faint within me, YouyouYou know my path; in the way I walk, they have hidden a snare for me.

4Look to my right and see—no one acknowledges me. Refuge has failed me; no one cares for my life. 5I cry to YouyouYou, O the LORD; I say, “You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living.”

6Listen to my cry, for I am brought very low; rescue me from my persecutors, for they are too strong for me. 7Bring my soul out of prison, that I may give thanks to YouryourYour name; the righteous will surround me, for YouyouYou will deal bountifully with me.
