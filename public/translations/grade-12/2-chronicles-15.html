Azariah’s Message to Asa

1The SpiritspiritSpirit of God came upon Azariah son of Oded. 2He went out to meet Asa and said, “Listen to me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: the LORD is with you when you are with HimhimHim; if you seek HimhimHim, HeheHe will be found by you; but if you forsake HimhimHim, HeheHe will forsake you.

3“For many days Israel had been without the true God, without a teaching priest, and without the law. 4But in their distress they turned to the LORD, the God of Israel, and sought HimhimHim, and HeheHe was found by them. 5In those times there was no peace for anyone who went out or came in, because great turmoil was upon all the inhabitants of the lands. 6Nation rose against nation, and city against city, for God troubled them with every calamity. 7But as for you, be strong and do not let your hands grow weak, for your work will be rewarded.”

Asa Removes Idolatry

8When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the detestable things from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim; and he renewed the altar of the LORD that was before the porch of the LORD. 9He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, together with those from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who were staying with them, for many from Israel had defected to him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

The Covenant Renewal in Jerusalem

10They assembled at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa’s reign. 11On that day they sacrificed to the LORD seven hundred bulls and seven thousand sheep from the plunder they had brought. 12They entered into a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and all their soul. 13Whoever would not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, was to be put to death, whether small or great, man or woman. 14They swore to the LORD with a loud voice, with shouting, and with trumpets and horns. 15All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and sought HimhimHim with all their desire; and HeheHe was found by them, and the LORD gave them rest on every side.

Asa’s Continued Faithfulness

16King Asa also removed Maacah his mother from being queen mother, because she had made an abominable image for Asherah; Asa cut down her image, crushed it, and burned it in the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 17The high places, however, were not removed from Israel; nevertheless Asa’s heart was wholly devoted to the LORD all his days. 18He brought into the house of God the things his father had dedicated and his own dedicated things—silver, gold, and vessels. 19There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa’s reign.
