Job Replies to Bildad

1Then Job answered: 2"Truly I know that it is so, but how can a mortal be righteous before God? 3If HeheHe should choose to contend with him, he could not answer HimhimHim one time in a thousand.

God's Unsearchable Power

4"He is wise in heart and mighty in strength; who has hardened himself against HimhimHim and prospered? 5He moves mountains without their knowing it and overturns them in HishisHis anger. 6He shakes the earth from its place, and its pillars tremble. 7He commands the sun, and it does not rise; HeheHe seals up the stars. 8He alone stretches out the heavens and treads upon the waves of the sea. 9He makes the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the hidden constellations of the south. 10He does great things beyond searching out and wonders beyond number.

Job's Helplessness Before God

11"If HeheHe passes by me, I do not see HimhimHim; if HeheHe moves on, I do not perceive HimhimHim. 12If HeheHe snatches away, who can restrain HimhimHim? Who can say to HimhimHim, 'What are YouyouYou doing?' 13God does not turn back HishisHis anger; the allies of Rahab bow beneath HimhimHim.

14"How much less, then, can I answer HimhimHim and choose my words before HimhimHim? 15Though I were righteous, I could not answer; I would plead for mercy to my JudgejudgeJudge. 16If I called and HeheHe answered me, I would not believe that HeheHe was listening to my voice. 17For HeheHe crushes me with a tempest and multiplies my wounds without cause. 18He will not let me catch my breath, but fills me with bitterness. 19If it is a matter of strength, behold, HeheHe is mighty; if of justice, who can summon HimhimHim? 20If I am righteous, my own mouth would condemn me; though I am blameless, HeheHe would declare me perverse. 21I am blameless, yet I do not know myself; I despise my life. 22It is all one; therefore I say, 'He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.' 23When calamity strikes suddenly, HeheHe mocks the suffering of the innocent. 24The EarthEarthearth is given into the hand of the wicked; HeheHe covers the faces of its judges. If it is not HeheHe, then who is it?

Job's Lament and Plea

25"My days are swifter than a runner; they flee away without seeing good. 26They pass by like papyrus skiffs, like an eagle swooping down on prey. 27If I say, 'I will forget my complaint; I will change my expression and be cheerful,' 28I dread all my suffering; I know YouyouYou will not acquit me. 29I am guilty; why then should I toil in vain? 30If I wash myself with snow and cleanse my hands with lye, 31YouyouYou will plunge me into the pit, and my own clothes will abhor me. 32For HeheHe is not a man like me, that I should answer HimhimHim, that we should come together in judgmentjudgementjudgment. 33There is no arbiter between us, to lay his hand upon us both. 34Let HimhimHim remove HishisHis rod from me, and let not HishisHis terror make me afraid. 35Then I would speak without fear of HimhimHim, but that is not how I am."
