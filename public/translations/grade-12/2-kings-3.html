Jehoram’s Reign and Moab’s Rebellion

1In the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Jehoram son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned twelve years. 2He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, though not like his father and mother, for he removed the pillar of Baal that his father had made. 3Nevertheless, he clung to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had made Israel sin, and he did not turn away from them.

4Now Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder, and he paid the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs and the wool of one hundred thousand rams. 5But when Ahab died, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

The Three Kings March Through Edom

6At that time King Jehoram went out from Samaria and mustered all Israel. 7He also sent word to Jehoshaphat king of Judah: “The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me to war against Moab?” Jehoshaphat answered, “I will go. I am as you are, my people are as your people, and my horses are as your horses.” 8Then Jehoshaphat asked, “Which way shall we go up?” The reply came, “By the way of the wilderness of Edom.” 9So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom set out, and they took a circuitous route for seven days; but there was no water for the camp or for the animals that followed them.

10Then the king of Israel said, “Alas! The ThetheThe LORD has called these three kings together only to deliver them into the hand of Moab.” 11But Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there no prophet of the LORD here, through whom we may inquire of the LORD?” One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here, who served as Elijah’s attendant.” 12Jehoshaphat said, “The word of the LORD is with him.” So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to him.

Elisha Prophesies Water and Victory

13Elisha said to the king of Israel, “Why do you involve me? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother.” The king of Israel replied, “No, for the LORD has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of Moab.” 14Elisha answered, “As the LORD of Hosts lives, before Whom I stand, if I did not respect the presence of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not even look your way.” 15“But now bring me a musician.” When the musician played, the hand of the LORD came upon Elisha. 16Then he said, “Thus says the LORD: ‘Make this valley full of ditches.’ 17For thus says the LORD: ‘You shall not see wind, nor shall you see rain, yet this valley shall be filled with water, so that you, your cattle, and your livestock may drink. 18This is a small matter in the sight of the LORD; HeheHe will also give Moab into your hand. 19You shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, fell every good tree, stop up all springs of water, and ruin every good piece of land with stones.’”

The Rout of Moab and the Siege of Kir-hareseth

20In the morning, when the morning offering was being presented, suddenly water came by the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water. 21When all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, all who could bear arms, from the youngest to the oldest, were called out and stood on the border. 22They rose early in the morning, and the sun shone on the water; and Moab saw the water red as blood on the other side. 23They said, “This is blood! The kings have surely destroyed one another. Now, Moab, to the plunder!”

24But when they came to the camp of Israel, the Israelites rose up and struck Moab, so that the Moabites fled before them. Israel pursued them into the land and continued striking Moab. 25They destroyed the cities, and each man threw a stone on every good field until it was filled. They stopped up all the springs of water and felled all the good trees, until only Kir-hareseth was left with its stones; but the slingers surrounded it and struck it. 26When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, he took seven hundred swordsmen to break through to the king of Edom, but he could not. 27Then he took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. And there came a great wrath against Israel, so they withdrew from him and returned to their own land.
