A Psalm of David: Confession, Suffering, and Hope

1A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. The LORD, do not rebuke me in YouryourYour anger, nor discipline me in YouryourYour wrath. 2For YouryourYour arrows have sunk deep into me, and YouryourYour hand has fallen heavily upon me. 3There is no health in my body because of YouryourYour indignation; there is no wholeness in my bones because of my sin. 4For my iniquities have risen above my head; like a heavy burden, they weigh me down more than I can bear. 5My wounds reek and fester because of my folly. 6I am bent over and brought low; all day long I go about in mourning. 7For my sides are filled with searing pain, and there is no health in my flesh. 8I am feeble and utterly crushed; I groan because of the anguish of my heart.

Forsaken by Friends and Pursued by Enemies

9O Lord, all my longing is before YouyouYou, and my sighing is not hidden from YouyouYou. 10My heart pounds, my strength has left me, and even the light of my eyes has failed me. 11My friends and companions keep their distance from my affliction, and my relatives stand far off. 12Those who seek my life set traps for me; those who seek my ruin speak of destruction and plot deceit all day long. 13But I am like a deaf man who does not hear, like a mute man who cannot open his mouth. 14I have become like one who cannot hear, and in whose mouth there is no defencedefencedefense.

A Plea for the LORD's Deliverance

15But I put my hope in YouyouYou, the LORD; YouyouYou will answer, O Lord my God. 16For I said, "Do not let them gloat over me, those who boast against me when my foot slips." 17For I am about to collapse, and my pain is always before me. 18I confess my guilt; I am distressed over my sin. 19My enemies are strong and active, and those who hate me without cause have multiplied. 20Those who repay good with evil oppose me because I pursue what is good. 21The LORD, do not forsake me; O my God, do not be far from me. 22Come quickly to my aid, O Lord, my salvation.
