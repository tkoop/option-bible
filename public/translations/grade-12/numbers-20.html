The Death of Miriam and Water from the Rock

1In the first month, the whole congregation of the Israelites came into the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Zin, and the people settled at Kadesh. There Miriam died and was buried. 2Because there was no water for the congregation, they gathered against Moses and Aaron. 3The people quarreled with Moses and said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before the LORD! 4Why have you brought the LORD's assembly into this wilderness, so that we and our livestock should die here? 5Why did you bring us up out of Egypt to this wretched place—a place with no grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates, and no water to drink?"

6Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and prostrated themselves. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to them. 7And the LORD spoke to Moses: 8"Take the staff and assemble the congregation—you and your brother Aaron. Speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will yield its water. You shall bring water out of the rock for them and give drink to the congregation and their livestock."

9So Moses took the staff from before the LORD, just as HeheHe had commanded him. 10Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly before the rock, and Moses said to them, "Listen now, you rebels! Shall we bring water out of this rock for you?" 11Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff, and abundant water gushed out, so that the congregation and their livestock drank.

12But the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not believe in MemeMe, to honourhonourhonor MemeMe as holy in the sight of the Israelites, you shall not bring this assembly into the land I have given them." 13These are the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites quarreled with the LORD, and HeheHe proved HimselfhimselfHimself holy among them.

Israel's Request to Pass Through Edom

14From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom: "Thus says your brother Israel: 'YouyouYou know all the hardship that has befallen us— 15how our fathers went down to Egypt and we lived there many years, and the Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers. 16We cried out to the LORD, and HeheHe heard our voice and sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now here we are at Kadesh, a city on the edge of your border. 17Please let us pass through your land. We will not pass through any field or vineyard, nor drink water from any well. We will travel along the king's highway and will not deviate to the right or to the left until we have crossed your territory.'"

18But Edom said to him, "YouyouYou shall not pass through me, or I will attack you with the sword."

19The Israelites replied, "We will go up by the highway, and if we or our livestock drink your water, we will pay for it. Only let us pass through on foot; we ask for nothing more."

20But Edom insisted, "YouyouYou shall not pass through." Then Edom marched out against them with a large and powerful army. 21So Edom refused to allow Israel to pass through his territory, and Israel turned away from him.

Aaron's Death on Mount Hor

22They set out from Kadesh, and the whole congregation of the Israelites came to Mount Hor. 23At Mount Hor, on the border of Edom, the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 24"Aaron will die and be gathered to his ancestors, for he shall not enter the land I have given to the Israelites, because you rebelled against MymyMy command at the waters of Meribah. 25Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and bring them up to Mount Hor. 26Remove Aaron's garments and put them on Eleazar his son; Aaron will die there."

27Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole congregation. 28Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain. 29When the whole congregation saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.
