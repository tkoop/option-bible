Monarchs Vanquished East of the Jordan

1These are the kings of the land whom the Israelites defeated, seizing their territory east of the Jordan—from the Arnon River as far as Mount Hermon, including all the Arabah eastward. 2Sihon, king of the Amorites, who resided in Heshbon, exercised dominion from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon River, through the middle of that valley, as far as the Jabbok River—the frontier of the Ammonites—together with half of Gilead. 3He likewise ruled the eastern Arabah as far as the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth and as far as the SeaseaSea of the Arabah—the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea—eastward, along the route to Beth-jeshimoth, and southward beneath the slopes of Pisgah.

4The domain of Og, king of Bashan—one who belonged to the remnant of the Rephaim—encompassed Ashtaroth and Edrei. 5He governed Mount Hermon, Salecah, all Bashan to the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and half of Gilead, the border of Sihon, king of Heshbon. 6Moses, servant of the LORD, and the Israelites struck them down, and Moses, servant of the LORD, allotted their land as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

Monarchs Vanquished West of the Jordan

7These are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the Israelites defeated on the western side of the Jordan—from Baal-gad in the ValleyvalleyValley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which ascends toward Seir—distributed according to their tribal allotments. 8Joshua conferred that territory as an inheritance upon the tribes of Israel: the hill country, the lowland, the Arabah, the slopes, the wilderness, and the Negev, along with the lands of the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

The Thirty-One Kings Joshua Overcame

9the king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one; 10the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one; 11the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one; 12the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one; 13the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one; 14the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one; 15the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one; 16the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one.

17the king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one; 18the king of Aphek, one; the king of Lasharon, one; 19the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one; 20the king of Shimron-meron, one; the king of Achshaph, one; 21the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one; 22the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jokneam in Carmel, one; 23the king of Dor in Naphath-dor, one; the king of Goiim in Gilgal, one; 24the king of Tirzah, one—thirty-one kings altogether.
