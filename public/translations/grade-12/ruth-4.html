Boaz Negotiates the Redemption at the Gate

1Boaz ascended to the city gate and took his seat; almost immediately, the kinsman-redeemer of whom he had spoken passed by. Boaz called out, "Step aside and sit here, my friend." The man turned aside and sat down. 2Boaz then summoned ten of the city's elders and instructed them, "Sit here as well," and they complied.

3Boaz said to the redeemer, "Naomi, who has returned from the territory of Moab, is offering for sale the parcel of land that belonged to our brother Elimelech. 4I felt obliged to disclose this to you and to say that you should purchase it in the presence of those seated here and in the presence of the elders of my people. If you are willing to redeem it, redeem it; if not, tell me plainly, for no one else possesses the right of redemption ahead of you, and I stand next in line after you."

He answered, "I will redeem it."

5Boaz continued, "On the day you acquire the field from Naomi, you must also take Ruth the Moabite, the widow of the deceased, as a wife, so that the dead man's name may be perpetuated upon his inheritance."

6The redeemer replied, "I cannot redeem it for myself without jeopardizing my own inheritance. You redeem it yourself; I am unable to do so."

7Moreover, in Israel at that time, whenever a man redeemed or exchanged property, he would remove his sandal and give it to the other party; this act served as legal confirmation of the transaction. 8The redeemer therefore removed his sandal and said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself."

The Community Witnesses the Transaction

9Boaz then addressed the elders and all the people: "You are witnesses today that I have purchased from Naomi all that belonged to Elimelech, as well as all that belonged to Chilion and Mahlon. 10I have likewise acquired Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's widow, to be my wife, so that the deceased's name may be preserved upon his inheritance and not be erased from his family or from the gate of his community. You are witnesses today."

11All the people at the gate, together with the elders, responded, "May the LORD make the woman entering your house like Rachel and Leah, who together built up the house of Israel. May you prosper in Ephrathah and gain renown in Bethlehem. 12May your household be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, through the offspring the LORD grants you by this young woman."

Ruth and Boaz Build a Family

13Accordingly, Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He consummated the marriage, and the LORD granted her conception, so that she bore a son.

14The women said to Naomi, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD, who has not left you this day without a redeemer, and may his name be proclaimed in Israel. 15He will restore your vitality and sustain you in your old age, for your daughter-in-law who loves you—who is better to you than seven sons—has borne him."

16Naomi took the child, cradled him, and became his nurse.

17The neighboring women declared, "A son has been born to Naomi." They named him Obed, and he became the father of Jesse, the father of David.

The Lineage of David

18Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron, 19Hezron fathered Ram, Ram fathered Amminadab, 20Amminadab fathered Nahshon, Nahshon fathered Salmon, 21Salmon fathered Boaz, Boaz fathered Obed, 22Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.
