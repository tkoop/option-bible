Herod Persecutes the Church

1About that time, King Herod began to persecute some members of the church. 2He had James, the brother of John, executed by the sword. 3When he saw that this pleased the Jews, he proceeded to arrest Peter as well; this occurred during the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread. 4After seizing him, he imprisoned him and handed him over to four squads of four soldiers each to guard him, intending to bring him before the people after Passover.

5So Peter was kept in prison, but the church was earnestly praying to God for him.

Peter's Miraculous Deliverance

6On the very night before Herod was about to bring him out, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, while sentries guarded the entrance. 7Suddenly an angel of the Lord appeared, and a light shone in the cell. He struck Peter on the side and woke him, saying, "Get up quickly!" And the chains fell off his wrists. 8Then the angel said to him, "Get dressed and put on your sandals." And Peter did so. "Wrap your cloak around you and follow me," the angel told him. 9So Peter went out and followed him. He did not realizerealiserealize that what was happening through the angel was real; he thought he was seeing a vision. 10They passed the first and second guards and reached the iron gate leading into the city. It opened for them by itself, and they went out and walked down one street. At once the angel left him.

11Then Peter came to himself and said, "Now I know without a doubt that the Lord has sent HishisHis angel and rescued me from Herod's hand and from all that the Jewish people had been expecting."

Peter Reports to the Believers

12When he realizedrealisedrealized this, he went to the house of Mary, the mother of John (also called Mark), where many people had gathered to pray. 13He knocked at the outer entrance, and a servant girl named Rhoda came to answer the door. 14When she recognizedrecogniserecognized Peter's voice, she was so overjoyed that she did not open the gate, but ran in and announced, "Peter is standing at the gate!"

15"You are out of your mind," they told her. Yet she kept insisting that it was so. "It must be his angel," they said.

16But Peter kept on knocking, and when they opened the door and saw him, they were astonished. 17Peter motioned with his hand for them to be quiet and described how the Lord had brought him out of the prison. "Tell these things to James and to the brothers," he said. Then he left for another place.

Herod's Downfall and the Word's Advance

18When day came, there was considerable turmoil among the soldiers about what had become of Peter. 19After Herod had searched for him without finding him, he examined the guards and ordered that they be led away to be executed. Then he went down from Judea to Caesarea and remained there.

20Now Herod had been in conflict with the people of Tyre and Sidon. They came to him in unison, and having secured the support of Blastus, the king's chamberlain, they asked for peace, because their region depended on the king's country for food. 21On the appointed day Herod, wearing his royal robes and sitting on his throne, delivered a public address to them. 22The people shouted, "This is the voice of a god, not of a man!" 23Immediately, because Herod did not give glory to God, an angel of the Lord struck him down, and he was eaten by worms and died.

24But the word of God continued to spread and multiply. 25When Barnabas and Saul had fulfilled their ministry, they returned from Jerusalem, taking with them John, also called Mark.
