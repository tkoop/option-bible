David Rescues Keilah

1They reported to David, "ThetheThe Philistines are attacking Keilah and plundering the threshing floors."

2David inquired of the LORD, "Should I go and attack these Philistines?"

The LORD replied, "Go and attack the Philistines and rescue Keilah."

3David's men said to him, "We are already afraid here in Judah. How much more will we be if we go to Keilah against the Philistine forces?"

4David again inquired of the LORD.

The LORD answered him, "Get up and go down to Keilah, for I am giving the Philistines into your power."

5David and his men went to Keilah, fought the Philistines, drove off their livestock, inflicted a heavy defeat on them, and rescued the inhabitants of Keilah.

6When Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, he brought an ephod with him.

Saul Pursues David

7Saul learned that David had come to Keilah. Saul said, "God has delivered him into my power, for he has trapped himself by entering a town with gates and bars."

8Saul summoned all the people to war, to go down to Keilah and besiege David and his men.

9David knew that Saul was secretly plotting harm against him. He said to Abiathar the priest, "BringbringBring the ephod here."

10David said, "ThetheThe LORD, God of Israel, YouryourYour servant has heard that Saul is coming to Keilah to destroy the city because of me."

11David asked, "WillwillWill the citizens of Keilah surrender me to him? Will Saul come down, as YouryourYour servant has heard? The LORD, God of Israel, please tell YouryourYour servant."

The LORD said, "HeheHe will come down."

12David asked, "WillwillWill the citizens of Keilah surrender me and my men to Saul?"

The LORD said, "They will surrender you."

13David and his men, about six hundred, got up and left Keilah, going wherever they could. When Saul was told that David had escaped from Keilah, he abandoned the campaign.

14David stayed in the wilderness strongholds and in the hill country of the wilderness of Ziph. Saul sought him every day, but God did not deliver him into Saul's power.

15David realizedrealisedrealized that Saul had come out to seek to kill him. David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh.

Jonathan Encourages David

16Jonathan son of Saul went to David at Horesh and encouraged him in God.

17He said to him, "Do not be afraid, for my father Saul will not capture you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be second to you. Even my father Saul knows that."

18The two made a covenant before the LORD. David remained at Horesh, while Jonathan went home.

David Escapes at Maon

19The Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah and said, "IsisIs David not hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah south of Jeshimon? 20Now, O king, come down whenever you desire; our part is to surrender him into the king's power."

21Saul said, "May you be blessed by the LORD, for you have had compassion on me. 22Go, make certain, learn, and locate the place where he stays and who has seen him there, for I am told he is very cunning. 23See and learn all the hiding places where he hides, then come back to me with a firm report; I will go with you. If he is in the land, I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah."

24They set out and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah south of Jeshimon.

25Saul and his men went to seek David. When David was told, he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. Saul heard and pursued David into the wilderness of Maon.

26Saul was on one side of the mountain, and David and his men on the other. David was hurrying to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to capture them.

27A messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and come, for the Philistines have invaded the land."

28Saul turned back from pursuing David and went to meet the Philistines. Therefore that place was called the RockrockRock of Escape.

29David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds of En Gedi.
