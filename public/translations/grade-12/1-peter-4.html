Living in Accordance with God's Will

1Therefore, since Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves with the same resolve, for the one who has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin. 2Consequently, you no longer live the rest of your earthly life for human desires but for the will of God. 3For you have spent enough time in the past doing what the Gentiles choose to do—living in debauchery, lust, drunkenness, orgies, carousing, and detestable idolatry. 4Because of this, they are astonished that you do not plunge with them into the same flood of debauchery, and they heap abuse on you. 5But they will have to give an account to HimhimHim who is ready to judge the living and the dead. 6For this is why the gospel was preached even to those who are now dead, so that, though they were judged in the flesh as people are, they might live in the spirit according to God.

Serving with God's Varied Gifts

7The end of all things is near; therefore, be sober-minded and self-controlled for the sake of your prayers. 8Above all, love one another deeply, because love covers a multitude of sins. 9Offer hospitality to one another without grumbling. 10As faithful stewards of God's varied grace, use whatever gift each of you has received to serve one another. 11If anyone speaks, he should speak as one conveying the very words of God; if anyone serves, he should serve with the strength God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.

Sharing in Christ's Sufferings

12Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery trial that has come upon you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you. 13Instead, rejoice that you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may be overjoyed when HishisHis glory is revealed. 14If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the SpiritspiritSpirit of glory and of God rests on you. 15Indeed, let none of you suffer as a murderer, a thief, an evildoer, or a meddler. 16But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed; instead, glorify God in that name. 17For it is time for judgmentjudgementjudgment to begin with the household of God; and if it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who disobey the gospel of God? 18And, "If it is hard for the righteous to be saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?" 19Therefore, those who suffer according to God's will should entrust their souls to a faithful CreatorcreatorCreator while continuing to do good.
