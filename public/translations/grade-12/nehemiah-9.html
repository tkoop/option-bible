A Solemn Assembly of Repentance

1On the twenty-fourth day of this month, the Israelites assembled, fasting and wearing sackcloth, with dust upon their heads. 2Those of Israelite descent separated themselves from all foreigners; they stood and confessed their own sins and the iniquities of their ancestors. 3Standing in their places, they read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the the LORD their God for a quarter of the day, and for another quarter they confessed and worshiped the the LORD their God.

The Levites Lead the Assembly in Prayer

4The Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani—stood on the steps and cried out with a loud voice to the the LORD their God.

5Then the Levites—Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah—said, “Stand up and bless the the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting.
