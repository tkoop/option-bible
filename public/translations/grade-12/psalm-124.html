The LORD's Deliverance of His People

1A SongsongSong of Ascents. Of David. Had the LORD not been on our side—let Israel now declare—

2had the LORD not been on our side when our foes rose up against us, 3then they would have swallowed us alive in their burning wrath; 4then the floodwaters would have overwhelmed us, and the torrent would have swept over our souls; 5indeed, the haughty waters would have swept over our souls.

Praise for the LORD's Rescue

6Blessed be the LORD, who has not handed us over to be devoured by our foes. 7We have escaped like a bird from the fowler's snare; the snare is shattered, and we have been set free. 8Our help is in the name of the LORD, the MakermakerMaker of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.
