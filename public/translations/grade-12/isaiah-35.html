The Wilderness Blossoms and the Weak Are Strengthened

1The wilderness and the arid land will rejoice; the desert will exult and blossom like a crocus. 2It will bloom profusely and rejoice with joyful song. The glory of Lebanon will be given to it, the splendoursplendoursplendor of Carmel and Sharon. They will see the glory of the LORD, the splendoursplendoursplendor of our God.

3Strengthen the weak hands and steady the faltering knees. 4Say to those whose hearts are anxious, “Be strong; do not fear! Look, your God will come with vengeance, with divine retribution; HeheHe will come and save you.”

Miracles in the Wilderness

5Then the eyes of the blind will be opened, and the ears of the deaf unstopped. 6Then the lame will leap like a deer, and the tongue of the mute will shout for joy. For waters will gush forth in the wilderness, and streams in the desert. 7The burning sand will become a pool, and the parched ground, springs of water. In the haunt of jackals, where they rest, grass will become reeds and papyrus.

The Holy Way and the Redeemed

8And a highway will be there; it will be called the Way of Holiness. The unclean will not travel on it, but it will be for those who walk that road—even fools will not go astray. 9No lion will be there, nor will any ferocious beast come upon it; they will not be found there. But the redeemed will walk there. 10And the ransomed of the LORD will return and enter Zion with singing, everlasting joy crowning their heads. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee away.
