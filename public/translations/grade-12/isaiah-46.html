Babylon’s Idols Collapse

1Bel has bowed down; Nebo has stooped. Their idols are borne by beasts and cattle. Your carried things are burdens, a heavy load for the weary. 2They collapsed and bowed down together; they could not rescue the burden, and their own lives went into captivity.

The LORD Carries His People

3Listen to MemeMe, house of Jacob, and all you who remain of the house of Israel—you who have been borne by MemeMe from the womb and carried from birth. 4Even to old age I am HeheHe; even to greygreygray hair I will carry you. I have made you, and I will bear you; I will carry and rescue you.

The Folly of Idolatry

5To whom will you liken MemeMe, or make MemeMe equal? With whom will you compare MemeMe, that we should be alike? 6They pour gold from the purse and weigh silver on the scale; they hire a goldsmith to make it into a god. Then they bow down and worship it. 7They lift it onto their shoulders and carry it; they set it in its place, and it stands there. It does not move from its spot. If someone cries out to it, it does not answer or save him from his distress.

The LORD Alone Declares the Future

8Remember this, and take courage; bring it to mind again, you transgressors. 9Remember the former things of old, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like MemeMe. 10I declare the end from the beginning and from ancient times what is still to come. I say, “My counsel will stand, and I will accomplish all MymyMy purpose.” 11I call a bird of prey from the east, a man to carry out MymyMy purpose from a far country. Yes, I have spoken; yes, I will bring it to pass. I have planned it; yes, I will do it.

Salvation Near for Israel

12Listen to MemeMe, you stubborn of heart, you who are far from righteousness. 13I have brought MymyMy righteousness near; it is not far off, and MymyMy salvation will not delay. I will give salvation in Zion to Israel, MymyMy glory.
