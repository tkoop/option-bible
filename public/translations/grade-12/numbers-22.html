Israel Camps on the Plains of Moab

1The Israelites set out and camped in the plains of Moab, across the Jordan from Jericho. 2Balak son of Zippor saw everything Israel had done to the Amorites. 3Moab was terrified of the people, because they were numerous, and Moab dreaded the Israelites. 4So Moab said to the elders of Midian, “Now this horde will consume everything around us, as an ox grazes the grass of the field.” Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.

Balak Sends for Balaam

5He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, near the River, in the land of his people, to summon him, saying, “Look, a people has come out of Egypt. They cover the surface of the earth and are dwelling opposite me. 6Come now and curse this people for me, because they are too mighty for me. Perhaps I can defeat them and drive them from the land, for I know that whomever you bless is blessed, and whomever you curse is cursed.”

7The elders of Moab and the elders of Midian departed with divination fees in their hands. They came to Balaam and relayed Balak’s words. 8He said to them, “Stay here tonight, and I will bring you word as the LORD speaks to me.” So the princes of Moab stayed with Balaam.

9Then God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?”

10Balaam said to God, “Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent to me and says 11that a people has come out of Egypt and covers the surface of the earth; he asks me to curse them for him, so he can fight them and drive them out.”

12God said to Balaam, “You must not go with them, and you must not curse the people, for it is blessed.”

13In the morning Balaam rose and said to Balak’s princes, “Go back to your land, for the LORD refuses to let me go with you.”

14The princes of Moab rose, went to Balak, and said, “Balaam refuses to come with us.”

15Then Balak sent more princes, more numerous and more distinguished than the first. 16They came to Balaam and said, “Balak son of Zippor asks you not to refuse to come to him. 17He promises to honourhonourhonor you richly and to do whatever you say to him, and he asks you to curse this people for him.”

18Balaam answered Balak’s servants, “Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not transgress the word of the LORD my God, to do anything small or great. 19Now please stay here tonight as well, so I may learn what more the LORD will say to me.”

20That night God came to Balaam and said, “If the men have come to call you, rise and go with them; but do only the word I speak to you.”

21In the morning Balaam rose, saddled his donkey, and went with the princes of Moab.

God Opposes Balaam on the Road

22Then God’s anger was kindled because he was going, and the angel of the LORD stood in the road as an adversary against him. He was riding on his donkey, and his two servants were with him. 23The donkey saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in his hand. The donkey turned aside from the road and went into the field, and Balaam struck the donkey to turn her back onto the road.

24Then the angel of the LORD stood in a narrow path between the vineyards, with a wall on either side. 25When the donkey saw the angel of the LORD, she pressed against the wall and crushed Balaam’s foot against it, so he struck her again. 26The angel of the LORD went farther and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to turn to the right or to the left. 27When the donkey saw the angel of the LORD, she lay down under Balaam. Balaam’s anger was kindled, and he struck the donkey with his staff.

28Then the LORD opened the donkey’s mouth, and she said to Balaam, “What have I done to you, that you have struck me these three times?”

29Balaam said to the donkey, “Because you mocked me. If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you now.”

30The donkey said to Balaam, “Am I not your donkey, which you have ridden all your life until today? Have I ever been accustomed to do this to you?”

He said, “No.”

31Then the LORD opened Balaam’s eyes, and he saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with a drawn sword in his hand. He bowed down and fell on his face.

32The angel of the LORD said to him, “Why have you struck your donkey these three times? Look, I have come out to oppose you, because your way is perverse before me. 33The donkey saw me and turned aside before me these three times. If she had not turned aside from me, I would surely have killed you and spared her.”

34Balaam said to the angel of the LORD, “I have sinned, for I did not know you were standing in the road against me. Now if it is evil in your sight, I will turn back.”

35The angel of the LORD said to Balaam, “Go with the men, but speak only the word I tell you.” So Balaam went with the princes of Balak.

Balaam Meets Balak

36When Balak heard that Balaam had come, he went out to meet him at the city of Moab, on the border of the Arnon, at the farthest boundary.

37Balak said to Balaam, “Did I not earnestly send to you to call you? Why did you not come to me? Am I not able to honourhonourhonor you?”

38Balaam said to Balak, “Look, I have come to you. Now, do I have any power at all to speak anything? The word that God puts in my mouth, that I will speak.”

39Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.

40Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep and sent some to Balaam and to the princes who were with him.

41In the morning Balak took Balaam and brought him up to the high places of Baal, and from there he saw the edge of the people.
