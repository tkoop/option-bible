The Way of the Righteous and the Wicked

1How blessed is the one who does not follow the advice of the wicked, adopt the ways of sinners, or join the company of mockers. 2Rather, his delight is in the law of the the LORD, and he meditates on HishisHis law day and night. 3He is like a tree planted beside streams of water, producing fruit in its season, whose leaves never wither; everything he does prospers.

4The wicked are not like this; they are like chaff blown away by the wind. 5Therefore the wicked will not be acquitted in judgmentjudgementjudgment, and sinners will have no place among the righteous. 6For the the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked leads to ruin.
