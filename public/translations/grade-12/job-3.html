1After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed the day of his birth. 2Then Job answered and said:

Job Curses the Day of His Birth

“3Let the day perish in which I was born, and the night that said, ‘A man is conceived.’ 4Let that day be darkness; let God not regard it from above, nor let light shine upon it. 5Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it; let a cloud settle over it; let the blackness of the day terrify it.

“6As for that night, let darkness seize it; let it not rejoice among the days of the year, nor enter the number of the months. 7Behold, let that night be barren; let no joyful shout enter it. 8Let those who curse days curse it—those prepared to rouse Leviathan. 9Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it wait for light, but have none, and let it not see the eyelids of the dawn, 10because it did not shut the doors of my womb or hide trouble from my eyes.

Job Wishes He Had Died at Birth

“11Why did I not die at birth and perish as I came from the womb? 12Why did knees receive me, and why breasts, that I should nurse? 13For now I would have lain down and been quiet; I would have slept, and then I would have been at rest, 14with kings and counselors of the earth who built ruins for themselves, 15or with princes who had gold and filled their houses with silver, 16or why was I not hidden like a stillborn child, like infants who never saw light?

“17There the wicked cease from turmoil, and there the weary are at rest. 18There prisoners are at ease together; they do not hear the voice of the taskmaster. 19Small and great are there, and the slave is free from his master.

Job Questions Why Light Is Given to the Afflicted

“20Why is light given to one in misery, and life to the bitter in soul, 21who long for death, but it does not come, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures, 22who rejoice exceedingly and are glad when they find the grave? 23Why is light given to a man whose way is hidden, whom God has hedged in? 24For my sighing comes before I eat, and my groanings pour out like water. 25For the thing I feared has come upon me, and what I dreaded has befallen me. 26I am not at ease, nor am I quiet; I have no rest, for turmoil comes.”
