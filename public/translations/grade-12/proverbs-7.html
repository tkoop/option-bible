A Father’s Counsel to His Son

1My son, keep my words and treasure my commandments within you; 2keep my commandments and live, and guard my teaching as the most precious thing you protect. 3Bind them on your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart. 4Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,” and call understanding your close companion, 5so that they may keep you from the adulteress, from the seductress with her flattering words.

A Father’s Observation of a Foolish Youth

6For at the window of my house I looked out through my lattice. 7I saw among the naive, I noticed among the youths, a young man without understanding, 8crossing the street near her corner and taking the road to her house, 9in the twilight, at evening, in the blackest night and darkness.

10And behold, a woman meets him, dressed as a prostitute, with a cunning heart. 11She is loud and rebellious; her feet never remain at home. 12Now in the street, now in the squares, she lurks at every corner.

13She seizes him and kisses him, and with a brazen face she says:

“14I have made my peace offerings; today I have paid my vows. 15So I came out to meet you, to seek you eagerly, and I have found you. 16I have spread my bed with coverings, with colouredcolouredcolored linens from Egypt. 17I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. 18Come, let us satisfy ourselves with love until morning; let us delight in love. 19For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey. 20He took a purse of silver with him; he will not return until the full moon.”

21With her persuasive words she entices him; with her flattering speech she seduces him. 22Suddenly he follows her, like an ox going to the slaughter, and like a fool led to the stocks, 23until an arrow pierces his liver, like a bird darting into a snare; he does not know that it will cost him his life.

The Father’s Final Warning

24And now, my sons, listen to me, and be attentive to the words of my mouth. 25Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. 26For she has brought down many victims, and all her slain are mighty. 27Her house is the way to Sheol, descending to the chambers of death.
