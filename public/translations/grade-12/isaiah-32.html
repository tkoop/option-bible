The Reign of a Righteous King

1Behold, a king will reign in righteousness, and princes will rule with justice. 2Each will be like a shelter from the wind and a refuge from the storm, like streams of water in a dry land, and like the shade of a massive rock in a weary land.

3Then the eyes of those who see will no longer be closed, and the ears of those who hear will listen attentively. 4The mind of the rash will understand and know, and the stammering tongue will speak clearly and fluently.

5No longer will the fool be called noble, nor the scoundrel be said to be honourablehonourablehonorable. 6For the fool speaks folly, and his heart is devoted to iniquity—to practiseto practiseto practice ungodliness, to utter error concerning the LORD, to leave the hungry soul empty, and to deprive the thirsty of drink. 7The scoundrel's devices are evil; he plans wicked schemes to ruin the poor with lying words, even when the needy's plea is just. 8But the noble plans noble things, and by noble things he stands firm.

A Warning to Complacent Women

9Rise up, you women who are at ease; hear my voice. You complacent daughters, give ear to my speech. 10In little more than a year you will shudder, you complacent women, for the grape harvest will fail and the fruit harvest will not come.

11Tremble, you women who are at ease; shudder, you complacent ones. Strip yourselves bare and tie sackcloth around your waists. 12Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine, 13for the soil of MymyMy people overgrown with thorns and briers—for every joyful house and the exultant city.

The Spirit's Renewal of the Land

14For the palace is forsaken, the populous city deserted; the hill and the watchtower have become caves forever, a delight for wild donkeys, a pasture for flocks; 15until the SpiritspiritSpirit is poured out on us from on high, and the wilderness becomes a fruitful field, and the fruitful field is counted as forest.

16Then justice will dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness will abide in the fruitful field. 17And the work of righteousness will be peace, and the service of righteousness will be quietness and confidence forever. 18My people will dwell in a peaceful habitation, in secure dwellings, and in quiet resting places.

19And it will hail when the forest falls, and the city will be utterly brought low. 20Blessed are you who sow beside all waters, who let the feet of the ox and the donkey roam free.
