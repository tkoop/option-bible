A Prayer for Mercy in Times of Affliction

1For the director of music, with stringed instruments, according to Sheminith—a psalm of David. The LORD, do not rebuke me in YouryourYour anger, and do not discipline me in YouryourYour wrath. 2Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am languishing; heal me, the LORD, for my bones are in agony.

3My soul is profoundly distressed. But YouyouYou, the LORD—how long? 4Return, the LORD, and deliver my soul; save me for the sake of YouryourYour steadfast love.

5For in death there is no remembrance of YouyouYou; in Sheol who will praise YouyouYou? 6I am weary with my groaning; all night I flood my bed with tears; I drench my couch with weeping. 7My eye grows dim with grief; it wastes away because of all my foes.

8Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity, for the LORD has heard the voice of my weeping. 9The LORD has heard my supplication; the LORD accepts my prayer. 10All my enemies will be ashamed and utterly dismayed; they will turn back and be put to shame in a moment.
