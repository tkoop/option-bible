Bildad’s Second Speech

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:

The Fate of the Wicked

2“How long will you keep hunting for words? Gain understanding, and then we will speak. 3Why are we counted as cattle, and regarded as stupid in your eyes? 4You who tear yourself apart in anger—should the earth be abandoned for your sake, or should a rock be moved from its place?

5“Indeed, the light of the wicked is extinguished, and the spark of his fire does not shine. 6The light in his tent grows dark, and the lamp beside him goes out. 7His strong steps are shortened, and his own counsel brings him down. 8For his feet are caught in a net, and he walks into a snare. 9A trap seizes his heel, and a snare grips him. 10A noose is hidden in the ground, and a trap lies across his path. 11Terrors frighten him on every side and hound him at every step. 12His strength is starved, and calamity stands ready at his side. 13It devours the limbs of his body, and the firstborn of death devours his limbs. 14He is torn from the safety of his tent and led away to the king of terrors. 15Fire dwells in his tent, and brimstone is scattered over his dwelling. 16His roots dry up beneath him, and his branches wither above. 17His memory perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the land. 18He is driven from light into darkness and banished from the inhabited world. 19He has no offspring or posterity among his people, and no survivor remains in his dwelling. 20Those in the west are appalled at his fate, while those in the east are seized with horror. 21Surely such is the dwelling of the wicked, and this is the place of one who does not know God.”
