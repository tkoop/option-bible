Moses Addresses Israel East of the Jordan

1These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel in the wilderness east of the Jordan, in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab. 2It is an eleven-day journey from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by way of Mount Seir.

3In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to the Israelites according to all that the LORD had commanded him concerning them, 4after he had defeated Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth in Edrei. 5East of the Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law, saying:

The LORD Commands Israel to Leave Horeb

6The LORD our God said to us at Horeb, “You have stayed at this mountain long enough. 7Turn now and set out for the hill country of the Amorites and all their neighbors in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the foothills, in the Negev, along the coast of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea, and in Lebanon, as far as the great river, the Euphrates. 8See, I have set the land before you. Go in and possess the land that the LORD swore to give to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—and to their descendants after them.”

9At that time I said to you, “I cannot carry you alone. 10The LORD your God has multiplied you, and today you are as numerous as the stars of Heavenheavenheaven. 11May the LORD, the God of your fathers, increase you a thousand times over and bless you as HeheHe has promised. 12How can I bear your troubles, burdens, and disputes by myself?”

13Choose wise, discerning, and respected men from your tribes, and I will appoint them as your leaders. 14You answered me, “What you propose is good.” 15So I took the leaders of your tribes, wise and respected men, and appointed them as officials over you—commanders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens, and officers for your tribes.

16And I charged your judges at that time, “Hear the disputes between your brothers and judge fairly between a man and his brother or a foreign resident. 17Do not show partiality in judgmentjudgementjudgment; hear both small and great alike. Do not be intimidated by anyone, for judgmentjudgementjudgment belongs to God. Bring me any case that is too difficult for you, and I will hear it.” 18And at that time I commanded you all the things you were to do.

Israel Refuses to Enter the Promised Land

19Then we set out from Horeb and went through that vast and terrifying wilderness you saw, on our way to the hill country of the Amorites, and we came to Kadesh-barnea. 20And I said to you, “You have reached the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving us. 21See, the LORD your God has placed the land before you. Go up and possess it, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has told you. Do not be afraid or discouraged.”

22Then all of you came to me and said, “Let us send men ahead to spy out the land and bring back a report about the route we should take and the cities we will encounter.”

23The plan pleased me, so I selected twelve men from among you, one from each tribe. 24They left and went up into the hill country and came to the ValleyvalleyValley of Eshcol and scouted it out. 25They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands, brought it down to us, and reported, “The land the LORD our God is giving us is good.”

26But you were unwilling to go up; you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God. 27You grumbled in your tents and said, “The LORD hates us; that is why HeheHe brought us out of Egypt, to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites to destroy us. 28Where can we go? Our brothers have made our hearts melt by saying, ‘The people are greater and taller than we are; the cities are large, with walls up to the sky; and we have even seen the Anakim there.’”

29Then I said to you, “Do not be terrified or afraid of them. 30The LORD your God, who goes before you, will fight for you, just as HeheHe did for you in Egypt before your very eyes. 31And in the wilderness you saw how the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way you travelledtravelledtraveled until you reached this place. 32Yet in spite of all this, you did not trust in the LORD your God, 33who went before you on the journey, in fire by night and in a cloud by day, to seek out a place for you to camp and to show you the way you should go.”

God's JudgmentJudgementJudgment and Israel's Defeat

34When the LORD heard your words, HeheHe was angry and swore an oath: 35“Not one of the men of this evil generation shall see the good land I swore to give your fathers, 36except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He will see it, and I will give him and his descendants the land he has walked on, because he followed the LORD wholeheartedly.”

37The LORD was angry with me as well because of you, and HeheHe said, “You shall not enter there either.” 38Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, will enter it. Encourage him, for he will enable Israel to inherit it.

39And your little ones, whom you said would become plunder, and your children who today do not know good from evil, will enter there. I will give it to them, and they will possess it. 40But as for you, turn back and set out toward the wilderness by the way of the Red SeaseaSea.

41Then you answered me, “We have sinned against the LORD. We will go up and fight, just as the LORD our God commanded us.” And each of you strapped on his weapons of war, thinking it would be easy to go up into the hill country.

42But the LORD said to me, “Tell them not to go up and not to fight, for I am not among you; otherwise you will be defeated by your enemies.”

43So I told you, but you would not listen; you rebelled against the LORD's command and presumptuously went up into the hill country. 44Then the Amorites who lived in that hill country came out against you and chased you like a swarm of bees. They beat you down in Seir all the way to Hormah.

45You returned and wept before the LORD, but the LORD would not listen to your voice or give ear to you. 46So you stayed in Kadesh many days—the same number of days you had already spent there.
