Offerings for the Sanctuary

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Speak to the Israelites and tell them to bring MemeMe an offering. You are to receive MymyMy offering from everyone whose heart moves him to give. 3This is the offering you are to receive from them: gold, silver, and bronze; 4blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; fine linen and goat hair; 5ram skins dyed red; fine leather; and acacia wood; 6olive oil for the lamp; spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense; 7and onyx stones and other stones to be set in the ephod and breastpiece. 8Then have them make a sanctuary for MemeMe, and I will dwell among them. 9You must make the tabernacle and all its furnishings according to the pattern I will show you.

The Ark of the Covenant

10"They are to make an ark of acacia wood—two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high. 11Overlay it with pure gold, inside and out, and make a gold molding around it. 12Cast four gold rings for it and attach them to its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other. 13Make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold. 14Insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark so that it may be carried. 15The poles are to remain in the rings of the ark and must not be removed. 16Place in the ark the TestimonytestimonyTestimony that I will give you.

17"Make an atonement cover of pure gold—two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 18Make two cherubim of hammered gold at the two ends of the cover. 19Make one cherub on one end and the second cherub on the other end; make the cherubim of one piece with the cover. 20The cherubim are to spread their wings upward, overshadowing the cover with their wings, and they are to face one another, with their faces toward the cover. 21Place the cover on top of the ark, and put in the ark the TestimonytestimonyTestimony that I will give you. 22There I will meet with you, and from above the atonement cover, from between the two cherubim that are over the ark of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony, I will speak with you about all that I command you for the Israelites.

The Table for the Bread of the Presence

23"Make a table of acacia wood—two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high. 24Overlay it with pure gold and make a gold molding around it. 25Make a rim for it a handbreadth wide all around, and make a gold molding for the rim. 26Make four gold rings for it and fasten them to the four corners at its four legs. 27The rings are to be placed near the rim to hold the poles for carrying the table. 28Make the poles of acacia wood, overlay them with gold, and carry the table with them. 29Make its plates, dishes, pitchers, and bowls for pouring drink offerings; make them of pure gold. 30Place the BreadbreadBread of the PresencepresencePresence on the table before MemeMe at all times.

The Golden Lampstand

31"Make a lampstand of pure gold. Hammer out its base and shaft; its cups, buds, and blossoms are to be of one piece with it. 32Six branches are to extend from its sides—three on one side and three on the other. 33On one branch there are to be three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with bud and flower, and the same on the next branch. The six branches extending from the lampstand are to be alike. 34On the lampstand itself there are to be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with bud and flower. 35A bud is to be under the first pair of branches coming from the lampstand, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair—the six branches extending from the lampstand. 36Their buds and branches are to be one piece with the lampstand, all of it a single piece of hammered pure gold.

37"Make seven lamps for it and set them up so that they light the area in front of it. 38Its wick trimmers and trays are to be of pure gold. 39The lampstand and all its utensils are to be made from a talent of pure gold. 40See that you make them according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain."
