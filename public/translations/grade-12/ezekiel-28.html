JudgmentJudgementJudgment Against the Ruler of Tyre

1The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, say to the prince of Tyre, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: Because your heart has been lifted up, and you have said, “I am a god; I sit in the seat of the gods, in the heart of the seas,” yet you are a man and not a god, and you have set your heart as the heart of a god.

“‘3Behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you. 4By your wisdom and understanding you have gained wealth for yourself, and you have gathered gold and silver into your treasuries. 5By your great wisdom in trade you have increased your wealth, and your heart has been lifted up because of your wealth.

“‘6Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you have set your heart as the heart of a god, 7therefore, behold, I will bring strangers against you, the most ruthless of the nations; they will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and profane your splendoursplendoursplendor. 8They will bring you down to the pit, and you will die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas.

“‘9WillwillWill you still say, “I am a god,” before your killer, when you are only a man and not a god, in the hands of those who slay you? 10You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners, for I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.’”

Lament for the King of Tyre

11The word of the the LORD came to me, saying, 12“Son of man, raise a lamentation over the king of Tyre, and say to him, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: You were the seal of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

“‘13YouyouYou were in Eden, the garden of God; every precious stone was your covering: sardius, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, emerald, and carbuncle. Your settings and mountings were made of gold; they were prepared on the day you were created. 14You were an anointed guardian cherub; I placed you on the holy mountain of God, and you walked among the fiery stones.

“‘15YouyouYou were blameless in your ways from the day you were created, until unrighteousness was found in you. 16In the abundance of your trade you were filled with violence, and you sinned; so I cast you out in disgrace from the mountain of God, and I destroyed you, O covering cherub, from among the fiery stones. 17Your heart was proud because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendoursplendoursplendor. I cast you to the ground; I made you a spectacle before kings.

“‘18By the multitude of your iniquities and the dishonesty of your trade, you profaned your sanctuaries; so I brought fire from within you, and it consumed you. I reduced you to ashes on the earth in the sight of all who looked at you. 19All who knew you among the peoples are appalled at you; you have come to a horrible end and will be no more.’”
