The Arrogance and Predation of the Wicked

5His undertakings prosper at all times; YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are loftier than his vision can reach; he sneers at every adversary. 6He assures himself, "I shall never be shaken; age after age, I shall remain untouched by calamity." 7His speech brims with curses, deception, and violence; mischief and wickedness lurk beneath his words.

8He lurks in ambush near the settlements; in concealed places he murders the innocent; his eyes search furtively for the vulnerable. 9He crouches in ambush like a lion in its cover; he lurks to capture the destitute, and he drags them into his snare. 10The vulnerable are crushed; they sink down and fall beneath his power. 11He tells himself, "God has forgotten; HeheHe has turned away HishisHis face; HeheHe will never notice."
