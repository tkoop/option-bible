Jesus Heals on the Sabbath

1One Sabbath, Jesus went to eat in the home of a prominent Pharisee, and those present were observing HimhimHim closely. 2There, right in front of HimhimHim, stood a man afflicted with dropsy.

3Jesus answered the legal scholars and the Pharisees by asking, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath, or not?”

4But they remained silent. So HeheHe took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him away. 5Then HeheHe asked them, “Which of you has a son or an ox that falls into a well on the Sabbath day and will not immediately pull him out?” 6And they could not answer these things.

Teaching on Humility and Hospitality

7Noticing how the invited guests were selecting the places of honourhonourhonor, HeheHe told those invited a parable.

8“When you are invited by someone to a wedding feast, do not recline in the place of honourhonourhonor, in case someone more distinguished than you has been invited by him. 9Then the host who invited you—and the other guest as well—will come and say to you, ‘Give this man your place,’ and you will begin, with shame, to occupy the last place. 10But when you are invited, go and recline in the last place, so that when the host who invited you comes, he will say to you, ‘Friend, move up higher.’ Then you will have honourhonourhonor before everyone reclining with you. 11For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.”

12He also said to the one who had invited HimhimHim, “When you give a luncheon or a dinner, do not invite your friends, your brothers, your relatives, or rich neighbors, in case they invite you in return and a repayment is made to you. 13But when you give a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, and the blind. 14You will be blessed, because they cannot repay you; for you will be repaid at the resurrection of the righteous.”

The Parable of the Great Banquet

15One of those reclining with HimhimHim heard this and said to HimhimHim, “Blessed is everyone who eats bread in the kingdom of God.”

16“A certain man prepared a great banquet and invited many guests. 17At the hour of the banquet, he sent his servant to tell those who had been invited, ‘Come, for everything is now ready.’ 18But they all alike began to make excuses. The first said to him, ‘I have bought a field, and I must go see it; please excuse me.’ 19Another said, ‘I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I am going to test them; please excuse me.’ 20Still another said, ‘I have just married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.’ 21The servant returned and reported these things to his master. Then the master of the house became angry and said to his servant, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in the poor, the crippled, the blind, and the lame.’ 22‘Sir,’ the servant said, ‘what you ordered has been done, and there is still room.’ 23Then the master said to the servant, ‘Go out to the roads and hedges and compel them to come in, so that my house may be filled. 24For I tell you that none of those men who were invited will taste my banquet.’”

The Cost of Discipleship

25Large crowds were travellingtravellingtraveling with HimhimHim. Turning, HeheHe said to them,

26“If anyone comes to MemeMe and does not hate his father, mother, wife, children, brothers, and sisters—yes, even his own life—he cannot be MymyMy disciple. 27And whoever does not carry his own cross and follow MemeMe cannot be MymyMy disciple.

28“For which of you, intending to build a tower, does not first sit down and calculate the cost to see whether he has enough to complete it? 29Otherwise, after laying the foundation and being unable to finish, everyone who sees it may begin to mock him, 30saying, ‘This man began to build and was not able to finish.’

31“Or what king, going out to wage war against another king, does not first sit down and deliberate whether he is able with ten thousand men to oppose the one coming against him with twenty thousand? 32If he is not able, he sends a delegation while the other is still far away and asks for terms of peace. 33So none of you who does not renounce all his possessions can be MymyMy disciple.

34“Salt is good; but if salt has lost its flavourflavourflavor, with what can it be seasoned? 35It is fit neither for the soil nor for the manure pile; people throw it out. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.”
