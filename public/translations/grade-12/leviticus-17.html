1The LORD spoke to Moses: 2"Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites and tell them that this is what the LORD has commanded:

Sacrifices Must Be Presented at the Tent of Meeting

"3Any man from the house of Israel who slaughters an ox, a lamb, or a goat—whether in the camp or outside it— 4and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to offer it as a sacrifice to the LORD before the tabernacle of the LORD shall be regarded as having shed blood. He has shed blood and shall be cut off from among his people.

"5This is so that the Israelites will bring to the LORD the sacrifices they are now offering in the open field, bringing them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting and sacrificing them there as peace offerings to the LORD. 6The priest shall sprinkle the blood on the altar of the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting and burn the fat as a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

"7They must no longer sacrifice their offerings to the goat demons, after whom they prostitute themselves. This shall be a permanent statute for them throughout their generations.

Offerings by Israelites and Foreigners

"8Tell them: Any man from the house of Israel or any foreigner residing among them who offers a burnt offering or sacrifice 9and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to offer it to the LORD, that man shall be cut off from his people.

The Prohibition Against Eating Blood

"10If anyone from the house of Israel or from the foreigners residing among them eats any blood, I will set MymyMy face against that person who eats the blood and will cut him off from among his people. 11For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your souls; for it is the blood that makes atonement for the soul.

"12Therefore I have said to the Israelites: No person among you may eat blood, nor may the foreigner residing among you eat blood.

"13If any Israelite or foreigner residing among them hunts and kills a wild animal or bird that may be eaten, he must pour out its blood and cover it with dust. 14For the life of all flesh is its blood. Therefore I have said to the Israelites: You must not eat the blood of any flesh, because the life of all flesh is its blood; anyone who eats it shall be cut off.

Purification After Eating Carrion

"15Anyone, whether native-born or foreigner, who eats an animal that died naturally or was torn by beasts must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening; then he will be clean. 16But if he does not wash his clothes or bathe his body, he will bear his iniquity."
