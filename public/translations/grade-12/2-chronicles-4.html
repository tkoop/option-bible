The Bronze Altar and the Molten Sea

1Solomon made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high. 2He also made the SeaseaSea of cast metal, round, ten cubits from rim to rim, five cubits high, with a circumference of thirty cubits; a measuring line of thirty cubits encircled it. 3Beneath it were figures of oxen all around, ten per cubit, completely surrounding the SeaseaSea; the oxen were cast in two rows when the SeaseaSea itself was cast. 4The SeaseaSea stood on twelve oxen: three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east, with the SeaseaSea resting on them and all their hindquarters turned inward. 5It was a handbreadth thick, and its rim was shaped like the rim of a cup, like a lily blossom; it could hold three thousand baths.

The Basins, Lampstands, and Tables

6He made ten basins for washing and set five on the right and five on the left. In them they washed the articles used for the burnt offering, but the SeaseaSea was for the priests to wash in. 7He made ten golden lampstands according to their prescribed pattern and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left. 8He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left, and he made one hundred golden bowls.

The Court, Pillars, and Bronze Vessels

9He made the priests' courtyard, the great court, and doors for the court, and he overlaid their doors with bronze. 10He set the SeaseaSea at the southeast corner, east of the south side. 11Huram made the pots, shovels, and basins. Huram finished all the work he had done for King Solomon in the house of God. 12He made the two pillars, the two bowl-shaped capitals on top of the pillars, and the two lattice networks covering the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars. 13He also made four hundred pomegranates for the two networks—two rows of pomegranates on each network—to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars.

14He made the stands and the basins on the stands. 15He made one SeaseaSea with the twelve oxen beneath it. 16The pots, shovels, meat forks, and all their articles that Huram-abi made for King Solomon for the house of the the LORD were of polished bronze. 17The king cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zeredah. 18Solomon made all these articles in such great abundance that the weight of the bronze could not be determined.
