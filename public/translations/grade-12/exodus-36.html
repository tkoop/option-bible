The Skilled Workers and the Offerings

1Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person in whom the LORD had placed skill and understanding to know how to do all the work for the service of the sanctuary were to carry out everything the LORD had commanded. 2Moses summoned Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person whose heart the LORD had stirred—everyone whose heart moved him to come and do the work.

3They received from Moses all the contributions the Israelites had brought for constructing the sanctuary. Moreover, the people continued to bring freewill offerings to him morning after morning.

4All the skilled workers engaged in the sanctuary work came, each leaving his own task, 5and said to Moses, “The people are bringing far more than enough for the work the LORD commanded us to perform.”

6So Moses issued a command, and a proclamation was circulated throughout the camp: “Let no man or woman do any further work for the sanctuary offering.” Thus the people were restrained from bringing more. 7For the materials they had were sufficient for all the work, and more than enough.

The Tabernacle Curtains

8All the skilled workers among those doing the work made the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twisted linen, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, with cherubim skillfully worked into them. 9Each curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide; all the curtains were the same size.

10He joined five curtains to one another, and the other five curtains he joined to one another. 11He made loops of blue yarn on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, and he made the same on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set. 12He made fifty loops on the one curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set, with the loops opposite one another. 13He made fifty gold clasps and joined the curtains to one another with them, so that the tabernacle became a single unit.

The Tent Coverings

14He made curtains of goats’ hair for a tent over the tabernacle; he made eleven of them. 15Each curtain was thirty cubits long and four cubits wide; the eleven curtains were all the same size. 16He joined five curtains by themselves and the remaining six by themselves.

17He made fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set and fifty loops on the edge of the corresponding curtain in the second set. 18He made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together, so that it would be one piece. 19He made a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of fine leather above it.

The Tabernacle Framework and Veil

20He made upright boards of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 21Each board was ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 22Each board had two tenons, fitted to one another; he made them this way for all the boards of the tabernacle. 23He made twenty boards for the south side of the tabernacle. 24He made forty silver sockets under the twenty boards—two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the next board for its two tenons.

25For the second side of the tabernacle, on the north, he made twenty boards. 26He also made their forty silver sockets, two under one board and two under the next. 27For the rear of the tabernacle, on the west, he made six boards. 28He made two boards for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear. 29They were doubled beneath and joined together at the top into a single ring; he made both corners this way. 30There were eight boards with sixteen silver sockets, two sockets under each board.

31He made five bars of acacia wood for the boards on one side of the tabernacle. 32He made five bars for the other side and five for the boards at the rear on the west. 33He made the middle bar to run through the boards from end to end. 34He overlaid the boards with gold and made gold rings to hold the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.

35He made the veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, with cherubim skillfully worked into it. 36He made four pillars of acacia wood for it and overlaid them with gold; their hooks were gold, and he cast four silver bases for them. 37He made a screen for the entrance to the tent of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer. 38He made its five pillars with their hooks, overlaid their tops and their bands with gold, and made their five bases of bronze.
