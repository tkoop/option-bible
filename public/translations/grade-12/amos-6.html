Woe to the Complacent in Zion and Samaria

1Woe to those who live in ease in Zion, and to those who feel secure on the mountain of Samaria—the distinguished men of the foremost nation, to whom the house of Israel comes. 2Cross over to Calneh and observe; go from there to great Hamath; then descend to Gath of the Philistines. Are they superior to your kingdoms? Is their territory more extensive than yours?

3You postpone the day of calamity and hasten the reign of violence. 4You recline on beds of ivory and sprawl upon your couches. You feast on lambs from the flock and calves from the stall.

5You improvise music on the harp as David did, and compose songs for yourselves. 6You drink wine from bowls and anoint yourselves with the choicest oils, yet you do not grieve over the ruin of Joseph.

7Therefore, you will now be led into exile at the head of the captives, and your feasting and lounging will cease.

The LORD's Oath Against Jacob

8The Lord the LORD has sworn by HimselfhimselfHimself—the LORD, the God of Hosts, declares: “I abhor the pride of Jacob and detest his citadels, so I will surrender the city and all that fills it.”

9If ten men remain in one house, they will die.

10And if a relative who is to burn the bodies lifts up the bones to carry them out of the house, he will say to the one in the innermost recesses of the house, “Are any still with you?”

“None,” that person will reply.

Then the relative will say, “Silence! We must not invoke the name of the LORD.”

The LORD's Decree of JudgmentJudgementJudgment

11For the LORD has issued a command; HeheHe will shatter the great house into fragments and the small house into rubble.

12Do horses gallop on rocky crags? Does one plow there with oxen? Yet you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.

13You who exult over nothing and say, “Have we not seized power for ourselves by our own strength?”

14For behold, I am raising up a nation against you, O house of Israel, declares the LORD God of Hosts; and they will oppress you from the entrance of Hamath to the BrookbrookBrook of the Arabah.
