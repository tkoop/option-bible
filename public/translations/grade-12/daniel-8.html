Daniel's Vision of the Ram and the Goat

1In the third year of King Belshazzar's reign, a vision appeared to me, Daniel, subsequent to the one that had appeared to me at the beginning. 2In that vision I found myself in the citadel of Susa, in the province of Elam, beside the Ulai CanalcanalCanal.

3I looked up and beheld a ram standing beside the canal, and it had two horns. Although both horns were long, one was longer than the other, and the longer one grew up later. 4I watched the ram butting westward, northward, and southward; no beast could withstand it, and no one could rescue another from its power. It acted according to its own will and grew great.

5While I was contemplating this, suddenly a male goat came from the west, skimming across the face of the whole EarthEarthearth without touching the ground. It possessed a conspicuous horn between its eyes. 6It advanced to the ram with the two horns that I had seen standing beside the canal, and it rushed at it with fierce, powerful anger. 7I saw it reach the ram and attack it furiously, shattering both horns. Because the ram had no strength to stand before it, the goat threw it to the ground and trampled it, and no one could rescue the ram from its power.

8Thus the male goat grew exceedingly great; yet when it became powerful, its great horn was broken, and in its place four conspicuous horns grew up toward the four winds of Heavenheavenheaven.

The Little Horn and the Sanctuary

9From one of these horns came forth a little horn, which grew exceedingly toward the south, toward the east, and toward the Beautiful LandlandLand. 10It grew great, even to the host of Heavenheavenheaven, and it cast down some of the host and some of the stars to the ground and trampled them. 11It magnified itself even to the Prince of the host; it removed HishisHis daily sacrifice, and the place of HishisHis sanctuary was cast down. 12Because of transgression, the host was given over to it together with the daily sacrifice; it hurled truth to the ground and prospered in everything it did.

13Then I heard a holy one speaking, and another holy one said to the one who was speaking, “How long will the vision last—the daily sacrifice, the transgression that causes desolation, the surrender of the sanctuary, and the trampling of the host?”

14He said to me, “For 2,300 evenings and mornings; then the sanctuary will be cleansed.”

Gabriel Interprets the Vision

15When I, Daniel, had seen the vision and sought to understand it, there stood before me one who had the appearance of a man. 16I heard a man's voice between the banks of the Ulai, and it called out, “Gabriel, make this man understand the vision.”

17So he came near where I stood, and when he came, I was terrified and fell on my face. But he said to me, “Understand, son of man, that the vision concerns the time of the end.”

18While he was speaking to me, I fell into a deep sleep with my face toward the ground; but he touched me and set me upright.

19And he said, “Behold, I will make known to you what will happen in the latter part of the wrath, for it concerns the appointed time of the end.”

20The ram that you saw with the two horns represents the kings of Media and Persia. 21The shaggy goat is the king of Greece, and the great horn between its eyes is the first king. 22As for the horn that was broken, in its place four kingdoms will arise from that nation, but not with his power.

23In the latter part of their reign, when the transgressors have reached their full measure, a king of fierce countenance, skilled in intrigue, will arise. 24His power will be great, but not by his own power. He will cause astounding devastation; he will succeed and prosper; he will destroy the mighty and the holy people. 25Through his cunning he will cause deceit to succeed under his hand; he will magnify himself in his heart; in a time of prosperity he will destroy many; he will stand against the Prince of princes; but he will be broken, not by human hands.

26The vision of the evenings and mornings that has been spoken is true. But seal up the vision, for it concerns the distant future. 27And I, Daniel, was exhausted and lay sick for days. Then I got up and carried on the king's business. I was appalled by the vision, and no one could explain it.
