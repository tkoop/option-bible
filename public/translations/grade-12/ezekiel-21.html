The Sword Drawn Against Israel

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

2“Son of man, turn your attention toward Jerusalem and preach against the sanctuaries; prophesy against the land of Israel. 3Say to the land of Israel: Thus says the LORD: Behold, I am against you, and I will draw MymyMy sword from its sheath and cut off from you the righteous and the wicked. 4Because I will cut off from you the righteous and the wicked, MymyMy sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh from south to north. 5Then all flesh will know that I, the LORD, have drawn MymyMy sword from its sheath; it will not be sheathed again.

6“As for you, son of man, groan; with a broken spirit and bitter grief, groan before their eyes. 7And when they ask you, ‘Why are you groaning?’ you are to say, ‘Because of the news that is coming. Every heart will fail, every hand will hang limp, every spirit will faint, and every knee will buckle. Behold, it is coming, and it will happen,’ declares the Lord the LORD.”

The Sharpened Sword of JudgmentJudgementJudgment

8Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

9“Son of man, prophesy and say: Thus says the Lord: A sword, a sword, sharpened and polished, 10sharpened for the slaughter, polished to flash like lightning—should we then rejoice in the sceptresceptrescepter of MymyMy son? The sword despises every tree. 11It has been given to be polished, to be grasped in the hand. It is sharpened and polished, to be placed in the hand of the slayer. 12Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is against MymyMy people; it is against all the princes of Israel. They are delivered to the sword with MymyMy people. Therefore strike your thigh. 13Surely the sword has been tested, and what if the sceptresceptrescepter, which the sword despises, does not endure?” declares the Lord the LORD.

14“So prophesy, son of man, and strike your hands together. Let the sword strike twice, even three times. It is a sword for the slain, a sword for the great slaughter, piercing them on every side. 15So that hearts may melt and many may stumble, I have set the sword at all their gates. Ah, it flashes like lightning; it is drawn for slaughter. 16Unite yourself, turn to the right; set yourself, turn to the left, wherever your face is directed. 17I will also strike MymyMy hands together, and I will satisfy MymyMy wrath. I, the LORD, have spoken.”

Babylon's Divination and the Prince's Ruin

18Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

19“Son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to take; both will come from the same land. Make a signpost at the head of the road to each city. 20Mark out a road for the sword to come against Rabbah of the Ammonites, and another against Judah into fortified Jerusalem. 21For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road, at the head of the two roads, to use divination: He shakes the arrows, consults the idols, and examines the liver. 22In his right hand is the divination for Jerusalem: to set up battering rams, to give the command to slaughter, to lift up a battle cry, to set up battering rams against the gates, to build a siege ramp, and to erect a siege wall. 23It will seem like a false divination to those who have sworn an oath to him, but it will remind them of their guilt, so that they will be captured. 24Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you have brought your guilt to mind by exposing your transgressions, so that your sins are visible in all your deeds—because you have drawn attention to them, you will be taken captive.

25“And you, O profane and wicked prince of Israel, whose day has come, whose time of punishment has arrived, 26this is what the Lord the LORD says: Remove the turban, and take off the crown. The present order will not stand; exalt the lowly and bring down the exalted. 27A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it. It will not be restored until the arrival of HimhimHim to whom it belongs; to HimhimHim I will give it.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Ammon

28“Now prophesy, son of man, and say: Thus says the Lord the LORD to the Ammonites and their contempt: A sword, a sword is drawn for slaughter, polished to devour, to flash like lightning, 29while they offer you false visions and lying divinations, prophesying that you will be laid on the necks of the slain wicked, whose day has come, whose time of punishment has arrived. 30Return it to its sheath. In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you. 31I will pour out MymyMy wrath upon you; I will blow the fire of MymyMy fury against you. I will hand you over to brutal men, skilled in destruction. 32You will be fuel for the fire; your blood will be in the midst of the land. You will be no more remembered, because I, the LORD, have spoken.”
