Conduct That Accords with Sound Doctrine

1But you must proclaim what is fitting for wholesome teaching: 2older men are to be temperate, dignified, self-controlled, and sound in faith, love, and endurance; 3likewise, older women are to be reverent in demeanourdemeanourdemeanor, not slanderous, not enslaved to much wine, but teachers of what is good, 4so that they may train the young women to love their husbands and children, 5to be self-controlled, pure, industrious in the home, good, and submissive to their own husbands, so that the word of God may not be blasphemed.

6Likewise, exhort the younger men to be self-controlled. 7In everything, present yourself as a pattern of good works; in your teaching, show integrity and dignity, 8and use sound speech that is beyond reproach, so that an opponent may be put to shame, having nothing evil to say about us.
