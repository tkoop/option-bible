A Lament from the Depths of Despair

A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the director of music. According to Mahalath Leannoth. A contemplative poem of Heman the Ezrahite. 1The LORD, God of my salvation, I have cried out before YouyouYou day and night. 2Let my prayer come before YouyouYou; incline YouryourYour ear to my supplication. 3For my soul is saturated with troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol.

4I am reckoned among those who descend into the pit; I am like a man bereft of strength. 5Abandoned among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom YouyouYou no longer remember, I am severed from YouryourYour care. 6You have placed me in the lowest depths, in realms of darkness, in the abyss.

7Your wrath bears down heavily upon me, and YouyouYou have assaulted me with all YouryourYour billows. Selah. 8You have alienated my acquaintances from me; YouyouYou have made me loathsome to them. I am imprisoned and cannot escape. 9My eyes are dimmed by affliction. I have called upon YouyouYou, the LORD, daily; I have spread out my hands to YouyouYou.

10Will YouyouYou perform wonders for the dead? Will the departed rise to praise YouyouYou? Selah. 11Will YouryourYour steadfast love be declared in the grave, YouryourYour faithfulness in Abaddon? 12Will YouryourYour wonders be known in the darkness, and YouryourYour righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

13Yet to YouyouYou, the LORD, I cry out; in the morning my prayer comes before YouyouYou. 14Why, the LORD, do YouyouYou reject my life? Why do YouyouYou hide YouryourYour face from me? 15Afflicted and close to death from my youth, I have suffered YouryourYour terrors; I am in despair.

16Your burning anger has swept over me; YouryourYour terrors have destroyed me. 17They surround me like water all day long; they close in on me from every side. 18You have removed far from me friend and companion; darkness is my only companion.
