Zophar's Reply to Job

1Then Zophar the Naamathite replied:

2"Therefore, my thoughts compel me to answer, for the agitation within me will not be subdued. 3I hear a rebuke that dishonoursdishonoursdishonors me, and my understanding insists upon a reply.

The Fleeting Triumph of the Wicked

4"Do you not know that from antiquity, since humanity was placed upon the earth, 5the triumph of the wicked is ephemeral and the joy of the godless endures only a moment? 6Though his arrogance ascends to the heavens and his head touches the clouds, 7he perishes forever, like his own refuse; those who once beheld him ask, 'Where is he?' 8HeheHe vanishes like a dream and is never recovered; he is driven away like a nocturnal vision. 9The eye that saw him sees him no more, and his place no longer acknowledges him.

10"His sons oppress the poor, and his hands must restore his wealth. 11His bones, once saturated with youthful vigourvigourvigor, lie down with him in the dust.

The Poison of Ill-Gotten Gain

12"Though evil is sweet in his mouth and he conceals it beneath his tongue, 13though he spares it and refuses to relinquish it, keeping it in the roof of his mouth, 14yet his food turns sour in his stomach and becomes the venom of cobras within him. 15He swallows wealth, but he vomits it up; God compels it from his belly. 16He sucks the poison of cobras; the fangs of a viper slay him.

17"He will not see the streams—the rivers flowing with honey and curds. 18He must restore what he labouredlabouredlabored to acquire without consuming it; he does not rejoice in the profits of his commerce. 19For he has oppressed and abandoned the poor; he has seized a house he never built. 20Because his appetite remains insatiable, he preserves nothing he desired. 21Nothing remains for him to devour; consequently, his prosperity cannot endure.

Divine JudgmentJudgementJudgment and Final Ruin

22"In the midst of his abundance, he is distressed; every hand of the afflicted assails him. 23When he is about to fill his belly, God sends HishisHis fierce anger upon him and rains it down while he eats. 24Though he flees from an iron weapon, a bronze bow pierces him. 25He draws it out, and it emerges from his back; the gleaming point comes from his liver. Terrors overwhelm him. 26Total darkness is reserved for his treasures; an unfanned fire consumes him and devours what remains in his tent.

27"The heavens expose his iniquity, and the earth rises up against him. 28The increase of his house departs; his goods flow away in the day of God's wrath. 29This is the wicked man's portion from God—the inheritance appointed to him by God."
