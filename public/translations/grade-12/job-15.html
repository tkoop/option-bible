Eliphaz's Second Speech

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

2"Should a wise man answer with empty knowledge and fill his belly with empty words? 3Should he argue with unprofitable words and speeches that accomplish nothing? 4Indeed, you undermine reverence and diminish devotion before God. 5For your iniquity teaches your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the crafty. 6Your own mouth condemns you, and your lips testify against you."

The Folly of Presumption

7"Were you the first man born? Were you brought forth before the hills? 8Have you heard the secret counsel of God, that you should limit wisdom to yourself? 9What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that is not with us? 10Both the greygreygray-haired and the aged are among us, older than your father. 11Are God's consolations too small for you, even a gentle word spoken to you? 12Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash? 13so that you turn your spirit against God and pour such words from your mouth?"

The Purity God Requires

14"What is man, that he should be pure? Or one born of woman, that he should be righteous? 15If HeheHe puts no trust in HishisHis holy ones, and the heavens are not pure in HishisHis sight, 16how much less a man who is abominable and corrupt, who gulps down iniquity as easily as water! 17Listen to me; I will show you, and what I have seen I will declare, 18what wise men have declared, hiding nothing from their fathers, 19to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them."

The Doom of the Wicked

20"The wicked man writhes in pain all his days; the number of years is fixed for the ruthless. 21Sounds of terror are in his ears; in peace the destroyer comes upon him. 22He does not believe he will return from darkness; he is marked for the sword. 23He wanders in search of bread, saying, 'Where is it?' HeheHe knows that a day of darkness is prepared. 24Distress and anguish terrify him; they overpower him, like a king ready for battle.

25Because he has stretched out his hand against God and acts arrogantly against the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, 26rushing at HimhimHim with a stiff neck, with the thick bosses of his shields. 27Though he has covered his face with fat and made his loins heavy with fat, 28he will dwell in desolate cities, in houses no one inhabits, which are destined to become heaps of rubble. 29He will not grow rich, and his wealth will not endure; his possessions will not spread over the earth. 30He will not escape from darkness; a flame will wither his shoots, and by the breath of HishisHis mouth he will depart.

31Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself, for emptiness will be his reward. 32It will be accomplished before his time, and his branch will not be green. 33He will shake off his unripe grape like the vine, and cast off his blossom like the olive. 34For the company of the godless is barren, and fire consumes the tents of bribery. 35They conceive trouble and give birth to evil; their womb prepares deceit."
