Wisdom Builds Her House and Calls the Simple

1Wisdom has built her house; she has hewn out its seven pillars. 2She has prepared her meat, mixed her wine, and set her table. 3She has sent out her maidservants; she calls from the highest points of the city.

4“Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to anyone lacking judgmentjudgementjudgment. 5“Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed. 6Leave your simple ways and live; walk in the way of understanding.”

The Rewards of Reproof and the Fear of the LORD

7Whoever corrects a scoffer invites insult; whoever rebukes the wicked incurs abuse. 8Do not rebuke a scoffer, or he will hate you; rebuke a wise man, and he will love you. 9Instruct a wise man, and he will be wiser still; teach a righteous man, and he will add to his learning.
