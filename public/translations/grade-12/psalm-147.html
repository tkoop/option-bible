Praise for the LORD's Restoration and Care

1Praise the LORD! It is good to sing praises to our God; how pleasant and fitting it is to praise HimhimHim! 2The LORD builds up Jerusalem; HeheHe gathers the exiles of Israel. 3He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. 4He counts the number of the stars and calls them all by name. 5Great is our Lord and mighty in power; HishisHis understanding is beyond measure. 6The LORD sustains the humble and casts the wicked down to the ground.

7Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the harp. 8He covers the sky with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains. 9He provides food for the beasts and for the young ravens when they call. 10He takes no pleasure in the strength of a horse, nor does HeheHe delight in a man's physical strength. 11The LORD delights in those who fear HimhimHim, in those who hope in HishisHis unfailing love.

12Praise the LORD, Jerusalem; praise your God, Zion. 13He strengthens the bars of your gates and blesses your people within you. 14He grants peace within your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat. 15He sends HishisHis command to the earth; HishisHis word runs swiftly. 16He gives snow like wool and scatters frost like ashes. 17He hurls down HishisHis hail like pebbles. Who can withstand HishisHis icy blast? 18He sends HishisHis word and melts them; HeheHe stirs up HishisHis wind, and the waters flow. 19He declares HishisHis word to Jacob, HishisHis statutes and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments to Israel. 20He has not done this for any other nation; they do not know HishisHis laws. Praise the LORD!
