Request for Prayer and Confidence in the Lord

1Finally, brothers and sisters, pray for us, so that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be honouredhonouredhonored, just as it is among you, 2and that we may be rescued from perverse and wicked people, for not everyone has faith. 3But the Lord is faithful; HeheHe will strengthen you and protect you from the evil one. 4And we are confident in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will continue to do what we command. 5Now may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and the steadfastness of Christ.

Warning Against Idleness and Disorder

6Now we command you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from every brother who lives in a disorderly manner and not according to the tradition they received from us. 7For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not live in a disorderly way among you, 8nor did we eat anyone's bread without paying for it; rather, we labouredlabouredlabored and toiled night and day, so that we would not burden any of you. 9not because we lack authority, but to offer ourselves as a model for you, so that you might imitate us. 10For even when we were with you, we commanded you this: "If anyone is unwilling to work, he should not eat." 11For we hear that some among you are living in idleness, doing no work at all, but meddling in other people's affairs. 12Now we command and urge such people, in the Lord Jesus Christ, to work quietly and eat their own bread. 13But as for you, brothers and sisters, do not grow weary in doing good. 14If anyone does not obey our instruction in this letter, take note of that person and do not associate with him, so that he may be ashamed. 15Yet do not regard him as an enemy; instead, admonish him as a brother.

Final Blessing and Greeting

16Now may the Lord of peace HimselfhimselfHimself grant you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you. 17The greeting is in my own hand—Paul. This is a sign in every letter; this is how I write. 18The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.
