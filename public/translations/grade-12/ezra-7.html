The Decree of King Artaxerxes

11The following is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the LORD's commandments and statutes for Israel:

12"Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven: Greetings. 13I decree that all people in my realm from Israel, including their priests and Levites, who willingly choose to go to Jerusalem may travel with you. 14For you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to investigate Judah and Jerusalem according to the LawlawLaw of your God, which you are authorized to administer, 15and to transport the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely contributed to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem, 16together with all the silver and gold you may locate throughout the province of Babylon, as well as the freewill offerings of the people and priests for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

17"With this money you shall diligently purchase bulls, rams, and lambs, along with their grain and drink offerings, and present them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. 18You and your brothers may do whatever seems best to you with the remaining silver and gold, according to the will of your God. 19Deliver to the God of Jerusalem the vessels entrusted to you for service in the house of your God. 20Provide from the royal treasury whatever else the house of your God requires. 21I, King Artaxerxes, decree to all treasurers in the province Beyond the River that whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, requests from you, you must supply without delay, 22up to one hundred talents of silver, one hundred cors of wheat, one hundred baths of wine, one hundred baths of oil, and salt without limit.

23"Whatever the God of Heavenheavenheaven commands shall be carried out diligently for the house of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, lest wrath befall the kingdom of the king and his sons. 24We also notify you that no tribute, tax, or toll may be levied on any priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or other servants of this house of God. 25And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom your God has granted you, appoint magistrates and judges to judge all the people in the province Beyond the River—all who know the laws of your God—and instruct those who do not. 26Whoever refuses to obey the LawlawLaw of your God and the law of the king shall be punished promptly, whether by death, exile, confiscation of property, or imprisonment."
