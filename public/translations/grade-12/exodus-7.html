Aaron's Staff Becomes a Snake

8Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 9“When Pharaoh demands a miracle from you, you are to tell Aaron, ‘Take your staff and cast it down before Pharaoh,’ and it will become a snake.”

10So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did just as the LORD had commanded. Aaron cast his staff down before Pharaoh and his officials, and it became a snake. 11But Pharaoh summoned the wise men and sorcerers as well, and the Egyptian magicians performed the same feat by their magic arts. 12Each man cast down his staff, and it became a snake; but Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs. 13Nevertheless, Pharaoh grew obstinate and refused to listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

19And the LORD said to Moses, “Tell Aaron, ‘Take your staff and extend your hand over the waters of Egypt—over their rivers, canals, ponds, and all their reservoirs—so that they may become blood.’ Blood will permeate the entire land of Egypt, even in containers of wood and stone.” 20So Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded. In the sight of Pharaoh and his officials, Aaron raised his staff and struck the water of the Nile, and all the water became blood. 21The fish in the Nile died, and the river stank so severely that the Egyptians could not drink its water. Blood pervaded the entire land of Egypt.

22But the Egyptian magicians did the same by their magic arts, and Pharaoh grew obstinate; he would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said. 23Instead, Pharaoh turned away, entered his palace, and paid no heed to the matter. 24So all the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink the water from the river. 25Seven days elapsed after the LORD had struck the Nile.
