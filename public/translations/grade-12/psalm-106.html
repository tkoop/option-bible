Praise and Confession of Israel’s History

1Hallelujah! Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis loving devotion endures forever. 2Who can recount the LORD’s mighty acts? Who can declare all HishisHis praise? 3Blessed are those who uphold justice, who practice righteousness at all times. 4Remember me, the LORD, when YouyouYou show favourfavourfavor to YouryourYour people; visit me with YouryourYour salvation. 5That I may see the prosperity of YouryourYour chosen ones, that I may rejoice in the joy of YouryourYour nation, that I may glory with YouryourYour inheritance.

6We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly. 7Our fathers in Egypt did not comprehend YouryourYour wonders; they did not remember YouryourYour abundant loving devotion, but rebelled at the sea, the Red SeaseaSea.

8Yet HeheHe saved them for the sake of HishisHis name, to make HishisHis mighty power known. 9He rebuked the Red SeaseaSea, and it dried up; HeheHe led them through the depths as through a wilderness. 10He saved them from the hand of the foe; HeheHe redeemed them from the hand of the enemy. 11The waters covered their foes; not one of them survived. 12Then they believed HishisHis words and sang HishisHis praise.

13Yet they soon forgot HishisHis works; they did not wait for HishisHis counsel. 14They craved intensely in the wilderness and tested God in the desert. 15So HeheHe gave them what they asked, but sent a wasting disease among them.

16In the camp they envied Moses and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. 17The EarthEarthearth opened up and swallowed Dathan; it covered the company of Abiram. 18Fire blazed among their company; flames consumed the wicked.

19At Horeb they made a calf and worshiped a molten image. 20They exchanged their GlorygloryGlory for the image of an ox that eats grass. 21They forgot God their SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, who did great things in Egypt. 22Wonders in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red SeaseaSea. 23So HeheHe said HeheHe would destroy them—had not Moses, HishisHis chosen one, interceded before HimhimHim to turn away HishisHis wrath from destroying them.

24Then they despised the pleasant land; they did not believe HishisHis word. 25They grumbled in their tents and did not listen to the voice of the LORD. 26So HeheHe raised HishisHis hand and swore that HeheHe would make them fall in the wilderness. 27To disperse their offspring among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands.

28They bound themselves to Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to the dead. 29They provoked the LORD to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them. 30Then Phinehas stood and intervened, and the plague was restrained. 31It was credited to him as righteousness for endless generations to come.

32They angered the LORD at the waters of Meribah, and it went badly for Moses because of them. 33For they rebelled against HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit, and Moses spoke rashly with his lips.

34They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD had commanded them. 35But they mingled with the nations and learned their practices. 36They served their idols, which became a snare to them. 37They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons. 38They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan—and the land was polluted with blood. 39They defiled themselves by their actions and prostituted themselves by their deeds.

40So the anger of the LORD was kindled against HishisHis people, and HeheHe abhorred HishisHis inheritance. 41He delivered them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them. 42Their enemies oppressed them and subdued them under their hand. 43Many times HeheHe rescued them, but they persisted in rebellion and sank down in their iniquity. 44Yet HeheHe heard their cry when HeheHe saw their distress. 45He remembered HishisHis covenant with them and relented according to the abundance of HishisHis loving devotion. 46He made them objects of compassion to all who held them captive.

47Save us, the LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to YouryourYour holy name, that we may glory in YouryourYour praise. 48Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, “Amen!” Hallelujah!
