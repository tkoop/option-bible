A Fountain Opened for Cleansing

1On that day a fountain will be opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and from impurity. 2On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, “I will remove the names of the idols from the land, so that they will no longer be remembered; I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness from the land.”

The End of False Prophecy

3If anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will say to him, “You must not live, because you have spoken falsely in the name of the the LORD.” And when he prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will pierce him through.

4On that day every prophet will be ashamed of his vision, and they will no longer wear a hairy cloak in order to deceive. 5Instead, each will say, “I am no prophet; I am a farmer, for I have been a farmer from my youth.”

6And if someone asks him, “What are these wounds on your body?”

He will answer, “These are the wounds I received in the house of my friends.”

The Shepherd Struck and the Remnant Refined

7“Awake, O sword, against MymyMy shepherd, against the man who is MymyMy companion,” declares the the LORD of Hosts. “Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered; I will turn MymyMy hand against the little ones.”

8“And in the whole land,” declares the the LORD, “two-thirds will be cut off and perish, but a third will be left.” 9“And I will bring the third through the fire; I will refine them like silver and test them like gold. They will call on MymyMy name, and I will answer them. I will say, ‘They are MymyMy people,’

and they will say, ‘The the LORD is our God.’”
