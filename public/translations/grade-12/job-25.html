Bildad’s Final Speech on Divine Majesty and Human Frailty

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: 2“Dominion and awe are HishisHis; HeheHe establishes peace in HishisHis celestial heights. 3Can HishisHis troops be numbered? Upon whom does HishisHis light not shine? 4How then can a man be righteous before God, or one born of woman be pure? 5If even the moon does not shine and the stars are not pure in HishisHis eyes, 6how much less a mortal, who is but a maggot, and a son of man, who is but a worm!”
