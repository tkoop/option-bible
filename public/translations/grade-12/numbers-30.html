The LORD’s Command Concerning Vows

1Moses said to the heads of the tribes of Israel, “This is what the the LORD has commanded: 2When a man makes a vow to the the LORD or swears an oath to obligate himself by a pledge, he must not break his word; he must do everything he has promised.

Vows Made by a Young Woman in Her Father’s House

“3If a woman makes a vow to the the LORD and obligates herself by a pledge while she is young and living in her father’s house, 4and her father hears her vow and the pledge by which she has bound herself yet says nothing to her, then all her vows and every pledge by which she has bound herself will stand. 5But if her father overrules her on the day he hears of it, none of her vows or pledges by which she has bound herself will stand; the the LORD will forgive her, because her father has overruled her.

Vows of a Married Woman

“6If a woman marries while bound by a vow or by a spoken pledge by which she has obligated herself, 7and her husband hears of it but says nothing to her on the day he hears, then her vows will stand, and the pledges by which she has bound herself will stand. 8But if her husband overrules her when he hears of it, he nullifies the vow upon her or the spoken pledge by which she has bound herself, and the the LORD will forgive her. 9Every vow or obligation pledged by a widow or a divorced woman will be binding upon her. 10If a woman in her husband’s house has made a vow or put herself under an oath, 11and her husband hears of it but says nothing to her and does not overrule her, then all her vows will stand, and every pledge by which she has bound herself will stand. 12But if her husband nullifies them on the day he hears them, then nothing that came from her lips—whether her vows or her pledges—will stand. Her husband has nullified them, and the the LORD will forgive her. 13Her husband may confirm or nullify any vow or any sworn obligation by which she denies herself. 14But if her husband says nothing to her from day to day, then he confirms all the vows and pledges that are upon her; he confirms them by saying nothing to her on the day he hears of them. 15But if he nullifies them after he hears of them, then he will bear her guilt.”

Statutes Governing Vows Between Husband and Wife

16These are the statutes that the the LORD commanded Moses concerning the relationship between a man and his wife, and between a father and a daughter still in her youth in her father’s house.
