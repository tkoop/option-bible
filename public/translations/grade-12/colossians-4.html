The Duty of Masters Before Their Heavenly Master

1Masters, provide your slaves with what is just and equitable, knowing that you also have a Master in Heavenheavenheaven.

Prayer, Wisdom, and Gracious Speech

2Devote yourselves to prayer, staying alert in it with thanksgiving. 3At the same time, pray for us as well, that God may open a door for us to proclaim the word, to declare the mystery of Christ, for which I am in chains, 4so that I may make it clear, as I ought to speak. 5Walk in wisdom toward outsiders, making the most of every opportunity. 6Let your speech always be gracious, so that you may know how to answer each person appropriately.

Messengers, Coworkers, and Communal Greetings

7Tychicus will tell you everything about my circumstances; he is the beloved brother, faithful minister, and fellow servant in the Lord. 8I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know about us and that he may encourage your hearts. 9He comes with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you; they will tell you everything that is happening here.

10Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, as does Mark, the cousin of Barnabas (you have received instructions about him; if he comes to you, welcome him), and Jesus, who is called Justus. 11These alone, of the circumcision, are my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me. 12Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ Jesus, greets you. He always struggles in prayer on your behalf, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God. 13For I testify to him that he has labouredlabouredlabored earnestly for you and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis. 14Luke, the beloved physician, greets you, as does Demas. 15Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and Nympha and the church that meets in her home. 16And when this letter has been read among you, see that it is also read in the church of the Laodiceans, and that you also read the letter from Laodicea.

A Final Charge and Apostolic Blessing

17And tell Archippus, "See that you fulfillfulfilfulfill the ministry you received in the Lord." 18The greeting is in my own hand—Paul. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
