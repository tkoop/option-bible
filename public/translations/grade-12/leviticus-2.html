Regulations for the Grain Offering

1When anyone brings a grain offering to the LORD, the offering must consist of fine flour; he is to pour oil upon it and put frankincense upon it. 2He shall bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests, and the priest shall take a handful of the fine flour and oil, together with all the frankincense, and burn this memorial portion on the altar as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 3What remains of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy portion of the offerings made by fire to the LORD.

Baked, Griddled, and Pan-Cooked Offerings

4If you bring a grain offering baked in an oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers spread with oil. 5If your offering is a grain offering on a griddle, it shall be fine flour, unleavened and mixed with oil. 6You shall break it into pieces and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering. 7If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil. 8You shall bring the grain offering made of these to the LORD, and it shall be presented to the priest, who shall bring it to the altar. 9The priest shall take from the grain offering its memorial portion and burn it on the altar as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 10What remains of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy portion of the offerings made by fire to the LORD.

Leaven, Honey, and Covenant Salt

11No grain offering that you bring to the LORD shall be made with leaven, for you shall burn no leaven nor any honey as an offering by fire to the LORD. 12As an offering of firstfruits you may bring them to the LORD, but they shall not be offered on the altar for a pleasing aroma. 13You shall season all your grain offerings with salt; you shall not let the salt of the covenant of your God be missing from your grain offering; with all your offerings you shall offer salt.

Firstfruits Grain Offering

14If you offer a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you shall offer for the grain offering of your firstfruits crushed heads of new grain roasted in the fire. 15You shall put oil on it and lay frankincense on it; it is a grain offering. 16The priest shall burn as its memorial portion some of the crushed grain and some of the oil, with all its frankincense, as an offering made by fire to the LORD.
