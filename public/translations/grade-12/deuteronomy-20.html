Preparing for Battle

1When you go out to war against your enemies and see horses and chariots and an army greater than yours, do not be afraid of them, because the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, is with you. 2When you draw near to the battle, the priest shall come forward and speak to the people.

3“Hear, O Israel: Today you are drawing near to battle against your enemies. Do not let your hearts grow faint; do not be afraid, do not panic, and do not be terrified by them. 4For the LORD your God is HeheHe who goes with you to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.”

Exemptions from Military Service

5Then the officers shall address the people: “Who is the man who has built a new house but has not dedicated it? Let him return home, lest he die in battle and another man dedicate it. 6And who is the man who has planted a vineyard but has not yet enjoyed its fruit? Let him return home, lest he die in battle and another man enjoy its fruit. 7And who is the man who has betrothed a wife but has not married her? Let him return home, lest he die in battle and another man marry her.”

8The officers shall speak further to the people and say, “Who is the man who is fearful and fainthearted? Let him return home, so that his brothers’ hearts do not melt as his own has.”

9When the officers have finished speaking to the people, they shall appoint commanders over the troops at the head of the people.

Terms of Peace and War

10When you approach a city to fight against it, first offer it terms of peace. 11If it responds peacefully and opens its gates to you, then all the people found in it shall be subject to forced labourlabourlabor and shall serve you. 12But if it refuses to make peace with you and makes war against you, then you shall besiege it.

13When the LORD your God delivers it into your hand, you shall put all its men to the sword. 14But the women, the children, the livestock, and everything else in the city—all its spoil—you shall take as plunder for yourself, and you shall enjoy the spoil of your enemies, which the LORD your God has given you. 15This is how you shall treat all the cities that are very far from you and do not belong to these nations.

16But in the cities of these peoples that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, you shall not spare anything that breathes. 17Rather, you shall devote them to complete destruction—the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—just as the LORD your God has commanded you. 18Do this so that they do not teach you to follow all the abominations they practice for their gods and cause you to sin against the LORD your God.

Preserving Fruit Trees During a Siege

19When you lay siege to a city for a long time, fighting against it to capture it, do not destroy its trees by swinging an axe against them, for you may eat from them. Do not cut them down. Are the trees of the field human beings, that you should besiege them as well? 20You may, however, destroy and cut down trees that you know are not fruit trees, so that you may build siege works against the city that is waging war against you, until it falls.
