The Divisions of Aaron’s Sons

1The priestly divisions of Aaron’s sons were as follows: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 2Nadab and Abihu, however, died before their father and left no sons, so Eleazar and Ithamar carried out the priestly office. 3With Zadok, a descendant of Eleazar, and Ahimelech, a descendant of Abiathar, assisting him, David apportioned them according to their duties in the sacred service.

4Because Eleazar’s lineage produced more leading men than Ithamar’s, the apportionment comprised sixteen patriarchal heads from Eleazar and eight from Ithamar. 5They were assigned by lot, since both lineages produced officials of the sanctuary and officials of God. 6Shemaiah son of Nethanel, a Levite and scribe, recorded their names before the king, his officers, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the patriarchal heads of the priestly and Levitical houses—one household drawn from Eleazar and one from Ithamar.

The Casting of Lots for Priestly Service

7The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah, 8the third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim, 9the fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin, 10the seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah, 11the ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah, 12the eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim, 13the thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab, 14the fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer, 15the seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Happizzez, 16the nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezkel, 17the twenty-first to Jachin, the twenty-second to Gamul, 18the twenty-third to Delaiah, and the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.

19This was their appointed order of ministry for entering the house of the LORD, in accordance with the ordinance established for them by Aaron their father, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded him.

The Levitical Families and Their Lots

20Among the remaining Levites, the sons of Amram included Shubael, and Shubael’s sons included Jehdeiah. 21Of Rehabiah, among his sons, Isshiah was the chief. 22Of the Izharites, Shelomoth’s sons included Jahath. 23The sons of Hebron were Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. 24The sons of Uzziel included Micah, and among Micah’s sons was Shamir. 25Micah’s brother was Isshiah, and among Isshiah’s sons was Zechariah.

26The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi, and the sons of Jaaziah were Beno. 27Through Jaaziah his son, the Merarite line included Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri. 28Mahli’s son was Eleazar, who had no sons. 29The sons of Kish included Jerahmeel. 30The sons of Mushi were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These constituted the Levitical families according to their ancestral houses. 31These likewise cast lots, just as their brothers the sons of Aaron had done, before King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the priestly and Levitical families—the principal households alongside the junior ones.
