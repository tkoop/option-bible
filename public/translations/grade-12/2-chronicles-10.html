Rehoboam’s Journey to Shechem

1Rehoboam travelledtravelledtraveled to Shechem, because all Israel had assembled there to install him as king. 2When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard of it—he was still in Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon—he returned from Egypt.

The People Petition Rehoboam

3Then the people sent for Jeroboam and brought him before Rehoboam, and Jeroboam and all Israel spoke to Rehoboam, saying:

4“Your father imposed an oppressive yoke upon us. Now lighten the severe labourlabourlabor and the heavy burden that he placed on us, and we will serve you.”

5Rehoboam replied, “Return to me in three days.” So the people departed.

Rehoboam Seeks Counsel

6King Rehoboam consulted the elders who had attended his father Solomon during his lifetime and asked, “How do you advise me to answer this people?”

7They replied, “If today you become a servant to this people and serve them, speaking kindly to them, they will remain your servants forever.”

8But Rehoboam rejected the elders’ counsel and consulted the young men who had grown up with him and now served him.

9He asked them, “What counsel do you offer? How should we answer these people who say to me, ‘Lighten the yoke your father placed on us’?”

10The young men who had grown up with him replied, “Tell this people who said to you, ‘Your father made our yoke heavy, but you should lighten it for us,’ this: ‘I am far more severe than my father. 11My father burdened you with a heavy yoke, yet I will increase your burden. My father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with far harsher blows.’”

12Three days later, Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam, just as the king had said, “Come back to me in three days.”

13The king answered them harshly, for he had rejected the elders’ counsel.

14Instead, he spoke to them according to the young men’s advice: “My father made your yoke heavy, but I will make it heavier. My father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with far harsher blows.”

15So the king did not heed the people, because this turn of events was from God, to fulfillfulfilfulfill the word that the LORD had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

The Kingdom Divides

16When all Israel saw that the king refused to listen to them, they answered him: “What share do we have in David? What inheritance in the son of Jesse? Go to your homes, Israel! Look after your own house, David!” Then all the people of Israel returned to their homes.

17Yet Rehoboam continued to reign over the Israelites who lived in the towns of Judah.

18King Rehoboam sent out Hadoram, who was in charge of forced labourlabourlabor, but all Israel stoned him to death. King Rehoboam, however, managed to mount his chariot and flee to Jerusalem.

19So Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David to this day.
