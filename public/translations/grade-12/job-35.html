Elihu Challenges Job's Righteousness

1Elihu answered and said: 2"Do you think this is just? You said, 'My righteousness is greater than God's.' 3For you ask, 'What advantage will it be to you? How am I better off than if I had sinned?' 4I will answer you, and your friends with you.

Human Sin and Righteousness Affect Only People

5"Look up at the heavens and see; gaze at the clouds high above you. 6If you sin, what do you accomplish against HimhimHim? And if your transgressions are many, what do you do to HimhimHim? 7If you are righteous, what do you give HimhimHim? Or what does HeheHe receive from your hand? 8Your wickedness affects a man like you, and your righteousness touches a son of man.

The Oppressed Cry Out to God

9"The oppressed cry out under a heavy burden; they plead for relief from the arm of the powerful. 10Yet no one asks, 'Where is God my MakermakerMaker, who gives songs in the night, 11who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?' 12There they cry out, but HeheHe does not answer, because of the arrogance of evildoers. 13Surely God does not listen to empty pleas, and the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty does not regard them.

Elihu Rebukes Job's Empty Words

14"How much less, then, when you say you do not see HimhimHim! Your case is before HimhimHim, and you must wait for HimhimHim. 15And now, because HishisHis anger does not punish and HeheHe does not take much notice of transgression, 16Job opens his mouth in vain and multiplies words without knowledge."
