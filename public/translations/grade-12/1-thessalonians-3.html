Paul Sends Timothy to Strengthen the Believers

1So when we could no longer endure the separation, we resolved to remain alone in Athens. 2We sent Timothy, our brother and God's coworker in the gospel of Christ, to strengthen and encourage you in your faith, 3so that no one would be shaken by these afflictions; for you know that we are appointed to this. 4Indeed, when we were with you, we kept warning you that we were about to suffer affliction, and, as you know, it has come to pass. 5For this reason, when I could bear it no longer, I sent to ascertain your faith, lest the tempter had tempted you and our labourlabourlabor had been in vain.

Timothy's Encouraging Report

6But now that Timothy has returned to us from you and has brought us good news of your faith and love, and of your constant, kind remembrance of us—longing to see us just as we also long to see you— 7in all our distress and affliction we were encouraged about you because of your faith. 8For now we live, if you are standing firm in the Lord.

Paul's Prayer for the Thessalonians

9For what thanksgiving can we render to God for you, for all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our God? 10Night and day we pray most earnestly that we may see your faces and supply what is lacking in your faith. 11Now may our God and FatherfatherFather HimselfhimselfHimself, and our Lord Jesus, direct our way to you. 12And may the Lord make you increase and abound in love for one another and for all people, just as we also do for you, 13so that HeheHe may establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and FatherfatherFather at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all HishisHis saints.
