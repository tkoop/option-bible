Household Obligations in the Lord

1Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. 2“HonourHonourHonor your father and your mother”—the first commandment accompanied by a promise—“so that well-being may attend you and you may enjoy longevity upon the earth.” 4Fathers, do not exasperate your children, but nurture them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

Slaves and Masters under Christ

5Slaves, obey your earthly masters with reverent awe, and with singleness of heart, as though you were serving Christ. 6Do not render eye-service merely to ingratiate yourselves with people, but as slaves of Christ, executing the will of God from your inmost being. 7Serve with goodwill, as if you were serving the Lord rather than human beings. 8because you know that the Lord will recompense each person for whatever good he has done, whether slave or free. 9And masters, treat them in the same way. Refrain from threats, since you know that HeheHe who is both their Master and yours is in Heavenheavenheaven, and with HimhimHim there is no partiality.

Final Greetings and Benediction

19Pray also for me, that a word may be given to me when I open my mouth, so that I may boldly proclaim the mystery of the gospel, 20for which I am an ambassador in chains, that I may declare it boldly, as I ought to speak. 21Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, will make known to you everything, so that you also may know about me and what I am doing. 22I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know our circumstances and that he may encourage your hearts. 23Peace to the brothers and sisters, and love with faith, from God the FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ. 24Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.
