A Thanksgiving for the LORD's Enduring Love

1Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.

2Let Israel say, "HishisHis steadfast love endures forever."

3Let the house of Aaron say, "HishisHis steadfast love endures forever."

4Let those who fear the LORD say, "HishisHis steadfast love endures forever."

5In my distress I called to the LORD; the LORD answered me and set me free.

6The LORD is with me; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me? 7The LORD is with me as my helper; I will look in triumph upon my enemies.

8It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man. 9It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in princes.

10All the nations surrounded me; in the name of the LORD I cut them off. 11They surrounded me—yes, they surrounded me; in the name of the LORD I cut them off. 12They swarmed around me like bees; they were quenched like a fire of thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

13I was pushed back and about to fall, but the LORD helped me. 14The LORD is my strength and my song, and HeheHe has become my salvation.

15Shouts of joy and salvation resound in the tents of the righteous: "The right hand of the LORD performs with valourvalourvalor!" 16The right hand of the LORD is exalted; the right hand of the LORD performs with valourvalourvalor.

17I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done. 18The LORD disciplined me severely, but HeheHe did not give me over to death.

19Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter and give thanks to the LORD. 20This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it. 21I will give thanks to YouyouYou, for YouyouYou answered me and became my salvation.

22The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. 23This is from the LORD; it is marvellousmarvellousmarvelous in our eyes. 24This is the day that the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.

25O the LORD, save us, we pray; O the LORD, grant us success, we pray. 26Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD. From the house of the LORD we bless you.

27The LORD is God, and HeheHe has made HishisHis light to shine upon us. Bind the festival sacrifice with cords to the horns of the altar. 28You are my God, and I will give thanks to YouyouYou; YouyouYou are my God, and I will exalt YouyouYou.

29Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis steadfast love endures forever.
