The Duties of Priests and Levites

1The LORD said to Aaron, “You and your sons and your father’s house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood. 2Bring near to you your brothers, the tribe of Levi, your father’s tribe, and let them be joined to you and minister to you, while you and your sons with you stand before the tent of the testimony. 3They shall keep your charge and the charge of the whole tent, but they must not come near the holy vessels or the altar, lest they die—and you with them. 4They shall be joined to you and keep the charge of the tent of meeting for all its service, and no stranger shall approach you.

“5You shall keep the charge of the sanctuary and the charge of the altar, so that wrath does not fall on the Israelites. 6Behold, I have taken your brothers the Levites from among the Israelites; they are a gift to you, given to the LORD, to do the service of the tent of meeting. 7You and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for everything concerning the altar and for what is within the veil, and you shall serve. I give your priesthood as a gift, and any stranger who approaches shall be put to death.”

The Priests’ Share of the Holy Things

8The LORD spoke to Aaron: “Behold, I have given you charge of MymyMy contributions; I have given you all the holy things of the Israelites as your portion and your sons’ portion, as an anointing forever. 9This shall be yours from the most holy things, from the offerings made by fire: every offering of theirs, every grain offering, every sin offering, and every guilt offering that they render to MemeMe shall be most holy for you and your sons. 10You shall eat it in the most holy place; every male shall eat it. It shall be holy to you. 11This also is yours: the contribution from their gifts, all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I have given them to you, your sons, and your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute. Everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

“12All the best of the fresh oil, all the best of the new wine and grain, their firstfruits that they give to the LORD, I have given to you. 13The firstfruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house may eat it. 14Every devoted thing in Israel shall be yours. 15Everything that opens the womb of all flesh, whether man or beast, which they offer to the LORD, shall be yours; nevertheless, the firstborn of man you shall redeem, and the firstborn of an unclean animal you shall redeem. 16Their redemption price, from one month old, you shall redeem according to your valuation: five shekels of silver, by the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs. 17But the firstborn of an ox, a sheep, or a goat you shall not redeem; they are holy. You shall sprinkle their blood on the altar and burn their fat as an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 18Their meat shall be yours, as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours. 19All the holy contributions that the Israelites raise to the LORD I have given to you, your sons, and your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute. It is a perpetual covenant before the LORD for you and your offspring with you.”

The Inheritance of the Priests and Levites

20The LORD said to Aaron, “You shall have no inheritance in their land, nor shall you have any portion among them. I am your portion and your inheritance among the Israelites.”

“21Behold, I have given the Levites all the tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in return for the service they perform, the service of the tent of meeting. 22The Israelites must no longer approach the tent of meeting, lest they incur guilt and die. 23The Levites shall perform the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their own iniquity. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations, and they shall have no inheritance among the Israelites. 24For the tithe that the Israelites raise to the LORD as a contribution I have given to the Levites as their inheritance. That is why I have said of them, ‘They shall have no inheritance among the Israelites.’”

The Levites’ Tithe and Offering to the LORD

25The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 26“Speak to the Levites and say to them, ‘When you receive from the Israelites the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance, you shall present from it a contribution to the LORD—a tithe of the tithe. 27Your contribution shall be reckoned to you as grain from the threshing floor and as the full produce of the winepress. 28Thus you shall also present a contribution to the LORD from all your tithes that you receive from the Israelites, and from it you shall give the LORD’s contribution to Aaron the priest. 29From all the gifts you receive, you shall present every contribution to the LORD, all its best part, its holy portion.’ 30Say to them, ‘When you have presented its best part, it shall be reckoned to the Levites as the produce of the threshing floor and of the winepress. 31You may eat it anywhere, you and your households, for it is your reward in return for your service in the tent of meeting. 32You shall bear no sin because of it when you have presented its best part, and you shall not profane the holy things of the Israelites, lest you die.’”
