David Seeks to Show Kindness to Saul's Household

1David asked, "IsisIs there anyone still left from the house of Saul to whom I can show kindness for Jonathan's sake?"

2Now there was a servant of Saul's household whose name was Ziba, and they brought him to David. The king asked him, "Are you Ziba?"

"I am your servant," Ziba replied.

Mephibosheth Is Brought Before David

3The king said, "IsisIs there no one still remaining from Saul's house to whom I can show God's kindness?"

Ziba answered the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, and he is lame in both feet."

4"Where is he?" the king asked.

Ziba said, "HeheHe is in the house of Machir son of Ammiel, in Lo-debar."

5So King David sent for him and brought him from the house of Machir son of Ammiel in Lo-debar.

6When Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came to David, he fell facedown and paid homage. David said, "Mephibosheth!"

"Here is your servant," he replied.

David Promises Kindness and Restoration

7David said to him, "Do not be afraid, for I will surely show you kindness for the sake of your father Jonathan. I will restore to you all the land that belonged to your grandfather Saul, and you will always eat at my table."

8Mephibosheth bowed low and said, "What is your servant, that you should show regard to a dead dog like me?"

David Entrusts Saul's Estate to Ziba

9Then the king summoned Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "I have given to your master's son everything that belonged to Saul and to all his household."

10"You and your sons and your servants are to farm the land for him and bring in the harvest, so that your master's son will have food to eat. But Mephibosheth, your master's son, will always eat at my table." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

11Ziba said to the king, "YouryourYour servant will do everything my lord the king commands."

So Mephibosheth ate at David's table like one of the king's sons. 12Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica, and everyone in Ziba's household became servants to Mephibosheth. 13So Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, because he always ate at the king's table; he was lame in both feet.
