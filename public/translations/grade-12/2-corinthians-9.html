Preparation of the Offering for the Saints

1Now regarding the ministry to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you, 2for I know your readiness, of which I boast to the Macedonians, saying that Achaia has been prepared since last year and that your zeal has stirred up many. 3Yet I sent the brothers so that our boast about you in this regard may not prove empty, and so that you may be prepared, just as I said. 4For if Macedonians should come with me and find you unprepared, we—to say nothing of you—would be humiliated in this assurance. 5Therefore I judged it necessary to urge the brothers to go on ahead to you and to arrange beforehand the blessing you had promised, so that it may be ready as a blessing and not as an act of greed.

The Principle of Sowing and Reaping

6Now consider this: whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows bountifully will also reap bountifully. 7Let each one give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. 8And God is able to make all grace abound to you, so that, always having all sufficiency in everything, you may abound in every good work. 9As it is written, "HeheHe has scattered abroad; HeheHe has given to the poor; HishisHis righteousness endures forever." 10Now HeheHe who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed and increase the harvest of your righteousness, 11as you are enriched in all things for every act of generosity, which through us produces thanksgiving to God.

Thanksgiving and Glory to God

12For the ministry of this service not only supplies the needs of the saints but also overflows in many thanksgivings to God. 13Through the proof of this service they glorify God for the obedience of your confession to the gospel of Christ and for the generosity of your fellowship toward them and toward all, 14while their prayers for you express longing for you because of the surpassing grace of God upon you. 15Thanks be to God for HishisHis indescribable gift!
