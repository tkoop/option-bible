The Vision of the Measuring Line

1I lifted my eyes and looked, and behold, a man was standing there with a measuring line in his hand. 2I asked, "Where are you going?"

He said, "I am going to measure Jerusalem, to find out how wide and how long it is."

3Then the angel who had been speaking with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him.

4He said to him, "Run and tell that young man: 'Jerusalem will be inhabited without walls because of the multitude of people and livestock within it.'"

5"And I will be a wall of fire around her," declares the LORD, "and I will be the glory within her."

The LORD's Return to Dwell Among His People
