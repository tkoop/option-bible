Isaiah's Vision of the Lord's Throne

1In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and exalted, and the train of HishisHis robe filled the temple. 2Above HimhimHim stood seraphim, each with six wings: with two they covered their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew. 3And one called to another, saying, "HolyholyHoly, holy, holy is the the LORD of hosts; the whole EarthEarthearth is full of HishisHis glory." 4The foundations of the thresholds shook at the voice of him who called, and the house was filled with smoke.

5Then I said, "Woe to me, for I am ruined! For I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell among a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the KingkingKing, the the LORD of hosts."

The Prophet's Cleansing and Commission

6Then one of the seraphim flew to me, having in his hand a burning coal that he had taken with tongs from the altar. 7He touched it to my mouth and said, "Look, this has touched your lips; your iniquity is taken away, and your sin is atoned for." 8I heard the voice of the Lord saying, "Whom shall I send, and who will go for UsusUs?" And I said, "Here am I. Send me."

9He said, "Go and say to this people: 'Keep on hearing, but do not understand; keep on seeing, but do not perceive. 10Make the heart of this people calloused, make their ears dull, and close their eyes, lest they see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, and turn and be healed.'"
