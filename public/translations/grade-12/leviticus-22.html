Priests and the Sacred Offerings

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, "2Speak to Aaron and his sons, instructing them to keep away from the sacred offerings that the Israelites consecrate to MemeMe, so that they do not profane MymyMy holy name. I am the LORD. 3Say to them, 'Throughout your generations, any man among all your descendants who approaches the sacred offerings that the Israelites consecrate to the LORD while uncleanness rests upon him shall be cut off from MymyMy presence. I am the LORD.' 4No descendant of Aaron who suffers from a skin disease or a bodily discharge may eat of the sacred offerings until he is cleansed. And whoever touches anything defiled by a corpse, or a man from whom semen has issued, 5or whoever touches any swarming creature that can render him unclean, or any person whose condition renders him unclean—in accordance with every form of his uncleanness— 6that person shall remain unclean until evening and may not eat of the sacred offerings unless he has bathed his body in water. 7When the sun sets, he shall be clean, and afterward he may eat of the sacred offerings, for they constitute his food. 8He shall not eat anything that dies of itself or is torn by beasts, lest he defile himself thereby. I am the LORD. 9They shall guard MymyMy charge, lest they bear sin on account of it and die within it, because they have profaned it. I am the LORD who sanctifies them. 10No outsider may eat a sacred offering; neither a priest's guest nor a hired worker may eat a sacred offering. 11But if a priest purchases a person with his own money, that person may eat of it, as may one born in his house; they may eat his food. 12If a priest's daughter marries a layman, she may not eat of the sacred contribution. 13But if a priest's daughter is widowed or divorced, has no child, and returns to her father's house as in her youth, she may eat of her father's food; yet no layman may eat of it. 14If a man eats a sacred offering unintentionally, he shall add a fifth to its value and give the sacred offering to the priest. 15They shall not profane the sacred offerings of the Israelites, which they present to the LORD, 16and thus cause them to bear the guilt of their iniquity when they eat their sacred offerings, for I am the LORD who sanctifies them."

Acceptable Sacrifices and Vows

17The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, "18Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites, and say to them, 'Any man of the house of Israel or of the sojourners residing in Israel who presents an offering for any of their vows or freewill offerings that they present to the LORD as a burnt offering— 19it shall be accepted on your behalf: a male without blemish from the herd, from the sheep, or from the goats. 20Whatever has a blemish you shall not offer, for it will not be accepted on your behalf. 21And if a man offers a peace offering to the LORD to fulfillfulfilfulfill a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, it must be without defect to be accepted; no blemish shall be in it. 22Blind, injured, maimed, or afflicted with a running sore, eczema, or scabs—these you shall not offer to the LORD, nor present them as a food offering to the LORD upon the altar. 23A bull or lamb with disproportionately long or short limbs you may offer as a freewill offering, but for a vow it shall not be accepted. 24You shall not offer to the LORD an animal with bruised, crushed, torn, or severed testicles; nor shall you do so in your land. 25Nor shall you offer, from the hand of a foreigner, any of these as the bread of your God, because corruption is in them and a blemish is in them; they shall not be accepted on your behalf.'"

Further Rules for Offerings

26The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, "27When a bull, sheep, or goat is born, it shall remain seven days with its mother; from the eighth day onward it shall be accepted as a food offering to the LORD. 28You shall not slaughter a cow or sheep and its young on the same day. 29When you sacrifice a thanksgiving offering to the LORD, you shall sacrifice it so that you may be accepted. 30It shall be eaten on that day; you shall not leave any of it until morning. I am the LORD. 31You shall keep MymyMy commandments and do them. I am the LORD. 32You shall not profane MymyMy holy name, but I will be sanctified among the Israelites. I am the LORD who sanctifies you, 33who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD."
