David Organizes the Levites for Temple Service

1When David had grown old and lived out a full span of years, he established his son Solomon as king over Israel. 2He then convened all Israel's leaders, together with the priests and Levites. 3The Levites thirty years of age and older were enumerated, and their total by individual head count came to thirty-eight thousand men. 4Of these, twenty-four thousand were charged with supervising the work of the LORD's house, while six thousand served as officials and judges. 5Four thousand were gatekeepers, and four thousand praised the LORD with the instruments David had fashioned for praise.

The Levitical Divisions and Clan Heads

6David divided them into divisions according to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 7The Gershonites were Laadan and Shimei. 8The sons of Laadan were Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel—three in all. 9The sons of Shimei were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran—three in all. These were the heads of the fathers' houses of Laadan. 10The sons of Shimei were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah—four in all. 11Jahath was the chief and Zizah the second; Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one father's house.

The Kohathite, Merarite, and Priestly Lines

12The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in all. 13The sons of Amram were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to consecrate the most holy things, to offer incense before the LORD, to minister to HimhimHim, and to bless in HishisHis name in perpetuity. 14Moses, the man of God, had his sons counted among the tribe of Levi. 15The sons of Moses were Gershom and Eliezer. 16The son of Gershom was Shebuel the chief. 17The sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the chief; Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very many. 18The son of Izhar was Shelomith the chief. 19The sons of Hebron were Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. 20The sons of Uzziel were Micah the chief and Isshiah the second. 21The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli were Eleazar and Kish. 22Eleazar died without sons, having only daughters; their kinsmen, the sons of Kish, married them. 23The sons of Mushi were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three in all.

Duties Assigned to the Levites

24These were the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses, the heads of the fathers' houses, enumerated by number of names, twenty years old and upward, who performed the work for the service of the LORD's house. 25For David had declared, "ThetheThe LORD, the God of Israel, has granted rest to HishisHis people, and HeheHe will dwell in Jerusalem forever." 26Consequently, the Levites no longer needed to transport the tabernacle and all its vessels for service. 27By David's final instructions, the Levites were numbered from twenty years old and upward. 28Their post was beside the sons of Aaron for the service of the LORD's house, over the courts and chambers, over the cleansing of all holy things, and over the work of the service of the house of God. 29They were responsible for the showbread, the fine flour for the grain offering, the unleavened wafers, the pan, the mixed flour, and every measure and quantity. 30They also stood morning by morning to thank and praise the LORD, and likewise in the evening. 31They provided for all the burnt offerings offered to the LORD on Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed feasts, according to the required number, continually before the LORD. 32Thus they kept charge of the tent of meeting, charge of the sanctuary, and charge of their brothers the sons of Aaron, for the service of the LORD's house.
