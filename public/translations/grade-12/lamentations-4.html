The Ruin of Jerusalem's Glory

1Alas, how the gold has dimmed, the finest gold altered; the sacred stones lie scattered at every street corner. 2Zion's precious sons, once valued like pure gold, are now reckoned as earthen vessels, the work of a potter's hands. 3Even jackals offer the breast to nurse their young, but the daughter of my people has become cruel, like ostriches in the wilderness. 4The nursing child's tongue clings to the roof of its mouth for thirst; little children beg for bread, yet no one gives them any. 5Those who once feasted on delicacies perish in the streets; those reared in scarlet embrace ash heaps. 6The punishment of the daughter of my people exceeds the sin of Sodom, which was overthrown in an instant without human hands. 7Her nobles were purer than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were ruddier than coral, their forms like sapphire. 8Now their faces are darker than soot; they are not recognizedrecogniserecognized in the streets; their skin has shrivelledshrivelledshriveled upon their bones and become as dry as wood. 9Those slain by the sword are better off than those slain by famine, who waste away, pierced through, for lack of the field's produce. 10The hands of compassionate women have boiled their own children; they became food for them in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

The LORD's Wrath and the Fall of Jerusalem

He set fire to Zion, and it consumed her foundations. 12The kings of the earth and all the inhabitants of the world did not believe that foe or enemy could enter the gates of Jerusalem. 13It was because of the sins of her prophets and the iniquities of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst. 14They wandered blind through the streets, defiled with blood, so that no one could touch their garments. 15"Depart! Unclean!" people cried to them. "Depart, depart, do not touch!" The End of Hope and the Triumph of Enemies

17Our eyes still fail, watching in vain for help; from our watchtowers we watched for a nation that could not save. 18They stalked our steps so that we could not walk in our streets; our end drew near, our days were finished, for our end had come. 19Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of Heavenheavenheaven; they chased us over the mountains and ambushed us in the wilderness. 20The LORD's AnointedanointedAnointed, the breath of our life, was caught in their pits. We had said, "Under his shadow we shall live among the nations."

Edom's Reckoning and Zion's Restoration

21Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz; yet the cup will pass to you; you will be drunk and exposed. 22O daughter of Zion, your iniquity is accomplished; HeheHe will not exile you again. He will punish your iniquity, O daughter of Edom; HeheHe will expose your sins.
