The Renewal of Trees and the Finality of Death

“7At least there is hope for a tree: if it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its shoots will not fail. 8Though its root grows old in the earth and its stump dies in the dust, 9yet at the scent of water it will bud and put forth branches like a newly planted tree.

“10But a man dies and lies powerless; a human being breathes his last, and where is he? 11As water disappears from the sea and a riverbed becomes parched and dry, 12so a man lies down and does not rise again; until the heavens are no more, they will not awake or be roused from their sleep.”
