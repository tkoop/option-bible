Samuel’s Farewell to Israel

1Samuel said to all Israel, “Look, I have listened to your voice in everything you said to me, and I have set a king over you. 2Now look, the king walks before you, and I am old and greygreygray; my sons are with you. I have walked before you from my youth until today. 3Look, here I am. Bear witness against me before the LORD and before HishisHis AnointedanointedAnointed: Whose ox have I taken? Whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I defrauded? Whom have I oppressed? From whose hand have I taken a bribe to look the other way? I will make restitution to you.”

4They said, “You have not defrauded us or oppressed us, and you have taken nothing from anyone’s hand.”

5He said to them, “The LORD is witness against you, and HishisHis AnointedanointedAnointed is witness today, that you have found nothing in my hand.”

They said, “He is a witness.”

The LORD’s Faithfulness to Israel

6Samuel said to the people, “The LORD is witness, who appointed Moses and Aaron and brought your fathers up from the land of Egypt. 7Now then, stand, and I will bring a charge against you before the LORD concerning all the righteous deeds the LORD performed for you and for your fathers. 8When Jacob came to Egypt, your fathers cried out to the LORD, and the LORD sent Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place. 9But they forgot the LORD their God, and HeheHe delivered them into the hand of Sisera, commander of the army of Hazor, and into the hands of the Philistines and the king of Moab, and they fought against them. 10They cried out to the LORD and said, ‘We have sinned, because we have forsaken the LORD and served the Baals and the Ashtoreths. Now deliver us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve YouyouYou.’ 11Then the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel, and HeheHe delivered you from the hand of your enemies on every side, and you lived in safety. 12When you saw that Nahash, king of the Ammonites, came against you, you said to me, ‘No; instead, a king shall reign over us,’ though the LORD your God was your king. 13Now then, look—the king you have chosen, whom you asked for—and look, the LORD has set a king over you. 14If you fear the LORD and serve HimhimHim and listen to HishisHis voice and do not rebel against the commandment of the LORD, then both you and the king who reigns over you will follow the LORD your God. 15But if you do not listen to the voice of the LORD and rebel against the LORD, then the hand of the LORD will be against you, as it was against your fathers.”

The Sign at the Wheat Harvest

16“Now then, stand and see this great thing that the LORD will do before your eyes. 17Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call to the LORD, and HeheHe will send thunder and rain, so that you may know and see that your wickedness is great, which you have done in the sight of the LORD, in asking for yourselves a king.”

18So Samuel called to the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain that day; and all the people greatly feared the LORD and Samuel.

19All the people said to Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for your servants, that we may not die; for we have added to all our sins this evil, to ask for ourselves a king.”

Samuel’s Final Charge to Israel

20Samuel said to the people, “Do not be afraid. You have done all this evil; yet do not turn aside from following the LORD, but serve the LORD with all your heart. 21Do not turn aside after worthless things, which cannot profit or deliver, for they are worthless. 22For the LORD will not forsake HishisHis people, because of HishisHis great name, for the LORD has been pleased to make you HishisHis people. 23Moreover, as for me, it would be unthinkable for me to sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you; instead, I will teach you the good and right way. 24Only fear the LORD and serve HimhimHim in truth with all your heart, for consider what great things HeheHe has done for you. 25But if you do wickedly, you will be swept away, both you and your king.”
