A Psalm of Thanksgiving for Deliverance from Death

1I love the LORD, because HeheHe hears my voice and my petitions. 2Because HeheHe has listened attentively to me, I will call upon HimhimHim all my days. 3The cords of death entangled me, and the terrors of Sheol overtook me; I encountered trouble and sorrow. 4Then I called upon the name of the LORD: “O the LORD, I beseech YouyouYou, deliver my soul!”

5The LORD is gracious and righteous; our God is merciful. 6the LORD preserves the simplehearted; I was brought low, and HeheHe saved me. 7Return to your rest, O my soul, for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you.

8For YouyouYou have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from stumbling. 9I will walk in the presence of the LORD in the land of the living. 10I believed, therefore I spoke: “I am greatly afflicted.” 11In my alarm I said, “All men are liars.”

12What shall I render to the LORD for all HishisHis benefits to me? 13I will lift up the cup of salvation and call upon the name of the LORD. 14I will fulfillfulfilfulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all HishisHis people.

15Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of HishisHis saints. 16O the LORD, truly I am YouryourYour servant; I am YouryourYour servant, the son of YouryourYour maidservant; YouyouYou have freed me from my bonds. 17I will offer to YouyouYou the sacrifice of thanksgiving and call upon the name of the LORD. 18I will fulfillfulfilfulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all HishisHis people, 19in the courts of the LORD's house, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!
