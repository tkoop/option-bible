JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Philistines

1The word of the the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh attacked Gaza.

2“Thus says the the LORD: Behold, waters are rising from the north and will become an overflowing torrent. They will flood the land and everything in it—the city and its inhabitants. People will cry out, and every inhabitant of the land will wail 3at the thunder of galloping hooves, the rumble of chariots, and the clatter of wheels. Fathers will not even turn to their children, because their hands are too weak. 4For the day has come to destroy all the Philistines and to cut off from Tyre and Sidon every surviving ally. For the the LORD is destroying the Philistines, the remnant from the coasts of Caphtor.”

Mourning in Philistia

5“Gaza shaves its head in mourning; Ashkelon falls silent. O remnant of their valley, how long will you keep cutting yourselves in grief? 6Alas, sword of the the LORD! How long before you are quiet? Return to your sheath; rest and be still. 7How can it be quiet when the the LORD has given it a command? Against Ashkelon and the seacoast HeheHe has appointed it.”
