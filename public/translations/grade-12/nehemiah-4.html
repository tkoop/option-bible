Sanballat Mocks the Rebuilding

1When Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he became furious and greatly enraged, and he mocked the Jews. 2In the presence of his brothers and the army of Samaria, he said, “What are these feeble Jews doing? Will they restore it for themselves? Will they sacrifice? Will they finish in a day? Will they revive the stones from the heaps of rubble—burned as they are?”

3Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him and said, “Yes, what they are building—if a fox goes up on it, he will break down their stone wall.”

Nehemiah Prays for Vindication

4Hear, our God, for we are despised. Turn their reproach back on their own heads, and give them as plunder in a land of captivity. 5Do not cover their iniquity, and do not let their sin be blotted out from before YouyouYou, for they have provoked YouyouYou to anger before the builders.

6So we built the wall, and all of it was joined together to half its height, because the people were willing to work.

Enemies Plot an Attack

7When Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites heard that the repair of Jerusalem’s walls had advanced and that the breaches had begun to be closed, they were very angry. 8They all conspired together to come and fight against Jerusalem and to throw it into confusion. 9So we prayed to our God and stationed a guard against them day and night because of them.

10Then Judah said, “The strength of the burden bearers is failing, and there is much rubble, so we are not able to build the wall.”

11Our enemies said, “They will not know or see until we come among them, kill them, and stop the work.”

12The Jews who lived near them came and told us ten times over, “Wherever you turn, they will attack us.”

Nehemiah Organizes the DefenceDefenceDefense

13So I stationed people behind the wall at the lower sections and at the exposed places; I stationed the people by families, with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

14I looked, rose up, and said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, “Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord, who is great and awesome, and fight for your brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your houses.”

15When our enemies heard that it was known to us and that God had frustrated their plan, we all returned to the wall, each to his work.

16From that day on, half of my servants worked on the construction, while the other half held the spears, the shields, the bows, and the body armourarmourarmor; and the leaders stood behind all the house of Judah.

17Those who carried burdens and those who loaded them did their work with one hand and held a weapon with the other.

18Each of the builders had his sword strapped to his side while he built, and the trumpeter stood beside me.

19I said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, “The work is great and extensive, and we are separated on the wall, far from one another. 20In whatever place you hear the sound of the trumpet, rally to us there. Our God will fight for us.”

21So we labouredlabouredlabored at the work, and half of them held the spears from dawn until the stars appeared.

22At that time I also said to the people, “Let each man and his servant spend the night within Jerusalem, that they may be a guard for us by night and labourlabourlabor by day.”

23Neither I nor my brothers nor my men nor the guards with me took off our clothes; each kept his weapon even when he went for water.
