A Psalm of Asaph: The Prosperity of the Wicked and the Nearness of God

1Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. 2But as for me, my feet had nearly slipped; my steps had almost given way. 3For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

4They have no struggles in their death; their bodies are well nourished. 5They are free from common human burdens; they are not plagued as others. 6Therefore pride is their necklace; they clothe themselves with violence. 7Their eyes bulge with abundance, and their hearts overflow with schemes.

8They scoff and speak wickedly; in their arrogance they threaten oppression. 9Their mouths lay claim to Heavenheavenheaven, and their tongues take possession of the earth. 10Therefore their people turn to them and drink up waters in abundance.

11They say, "How would God know? Does the MostmostMost HighhighHigh know anything?"

12Behold, these are the wicked—always at ease, they amass wealth. 13Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure and washed my hands in innocence. 14I have been afflicted all day long, and every morning brings new chastening.

15If I had spoken thus, I would have betrayed YouryourYour children. 16When I tried to understand this, it troubled me deeply, 17until I entered the sanctuary of God; then I understood their final destiny.

18Surely YouyouYou place them on slippery ground; YouyouYou cast them down to ruin. 19How suddenly are they destroyed, completely swept away by terrors! 20They are like a dream when one awakes; when YouyouYou arise, the Lord, YouyouYou will despise their image.

21When my heart was grieved and my spirit embittered, 22I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before YouyouYou. 23Yet I am always with YouyouYou; YouyouYou hold me by my right hand. 24You guide me with YouryourYour counsel, and afterward YouyouYou will take me into glory.

25Whom have I in Heavenheavenheaven but YouyouYou? And EarthEarthearth has nothing I desire besides YouyouYou. 26My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever. 27Those who are far from YouyouYou will perish; YouyouYou destroy all who are unfaithful to YouyouYou. 28But as for me, it is good to be near God. I have made the Lord the LORD my refuge, that I may tell of all YouryourYour works.
