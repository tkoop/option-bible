Israel’s Relapse and Oppression

1After Ehud died, the Israelites once again committed what was evil in the sight of the the LORD. 2So the the LORD delivered them into the power of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera, who resided in Harosheth-hagoyim. 3Then the Israelites cried out to the the LORD, because Jabin possessed nine hundred iron chariots and had ruthlessly oppressed them for twenty years.

Deborah Summons Barak

4Now Deborah, a prophetess and the wife of Lappidoth, was administering justice in Israel at that time. 5She used to sit beneath the palm of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came up to her for adjudication. 6She summoned Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali and said to him, “Has not the the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded, ‘Go, advance toward Mount Tabor and take with you ten thousand men from the sons of Naphtali and from the sons of Zebulun’? 7I will lure Sisera, the commander of Jabin’s army, with his chariots and his multitude, to you at the Kishon River, and I will deliver him into your power.”

8Barak said to her, “If you go with me, I will go; but if you will not go with me, I will not go.”

9She said, “I will surely go with you; nevertheless, the glory will not be yours on the expedition you are taking, for the the LORD will deliver Sisera into the power of a woman.” Then Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh. 10Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali together to Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up after him; Deborah also went up with him.

Sisera Advances to the Kishon

11Now Heber the Kenite had separated himself from the Kenites, from the sons of Hobab, Moses’ father-in-law, and had pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is near Kedesh. 12They told Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor. 13So Sisera called together all his chariots, nine hundred iron chariots, and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth-hagoyim to the Kishon River.

The Battle at the Kishon

14Then Deborah said to Barak, “Arise, for this is the day on which the the LORD has delivered Sisera into your power. Has not the the LORD gone before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand men after him. 15And the the LORD routed Sisera and all his chariots and all his army before Barak by the sword. Sisera got down from his chariot and fled on foot. 16But Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Harosheth-hagoyim, and all the army of Sisera fell to the sword; not a single man survived.

Jael Kills Sisera

17But Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin, king of Hazor, and the house of Heber the Kenite. 18Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, “Come in, my lord; come in to me; do not be afraid.” So he entered her tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

19He said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty.” So she opened a skin of milk and gave him a drink and covered him. 20He said to her, “Stand at the entrance of the tent, and if anyone comes and asks you, ‘Is there a man here?’ you shall say, ‘No.’”

21But Jael, Heber’s wife, took a tent peg and took a hammer in her hand, and approached him stealthily, and drove the peg into his temple until it sank into the ground; and he died. 22Now Barak was pursuing Sisera, and Jael came out to meet him and said to him, “Come, and I will show you the man you are seeking.” So he came to her, and there lay Sisera dead with the peg in his temple.

23So on that day God subdued Jabin, king of Canaan, before the Israelites. 24The Israelites pressed Jabin, king of Canaan, with ever-increasing severity until they destroyed him.
