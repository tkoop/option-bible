The Altar of Incense

1You shall make an altar for burning incense; make it of acacia wood. 2It shall be square—one cubit long, one cubit wide, and two cubits high—with horns of one piece with it. 3Overlay it with pure gold: its top, its sides all around, and its horns; and make a gold molding around it.

4Make two gold rings for it beneath the molding on its two sides; place them on opposite sides to hold the poles for carrying it. 5Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold. 6Put the altar in front of the veil that covers the ark of the testimony, in front of the atonement cover over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

7Aaron shall burn fragrant incense on it. Each morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it; 8and when he lights the lamps at twilight, he shall burn incense on it—a perpetual incense before the the LORD throughout your generations.

9You shall not offer unauthorized incense on it, nor a burnt offering, nor a grain offering; and you shall not pour a drink offering on it. 10Once a year Aaron shall make atonement on its horns; with the blood of the sin offering of atonement he shall make atonement for it once a year throughout your generations. It is most holy to the the LORD.

The Atonement Money

11The ThetheThe LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 12“When you take the census of the Israelites according to their number, each man shall give a ransom for his life to the the LORD when you number them, so that no plague may come upon them when you number them. 13This is what everyone who is counted shall give: half a shekel by the sanctuary shekel—twenty gerahs to the shekel—half a shekel as an offering to the the LORD.

14“Everyone who is counted, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the the LORD’s offering. 15The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less, than the half shekel, when giving the the LORD’s offering to make atonement for your souls. 16You shall take the atonement money from the Israelites and devote it to the service of the tent of meeting, so that it may be a memorial for the Israelites before the the LORD, to make atonement for your souls.”

The Bronze Basin

17The ThetheThe LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 18“You shall make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. Put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it. 19Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and feet from it. 20When they enter the tent of meeting, or approach the altar to minister by burning an offering to the the LORD, they shall wash with water, so that they do not die. 21They shall wash their hands and feet, so that they do not die. This shall be a perpetual statute for them and their descendants throughout their generations.”

The Holy Anointing Oil and Sacred Incense

22The ThetheThe LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 23“You also take the finest spices: five hundred shekels of free-flowing myrrh, half as much—two hundred fifty shekels—of fragrant cinnamon, and two hundred fifty shekels of aromatic cane, 24and five hundred shekels of cassia, by the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil. 25Make from these a holy anointing oil, a fragrant blend, the work of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil. 26With it anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony, 27the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, and the altar of incense, 28the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the basin with its stand. 29Consecrate them, and they shall be most holy; whatever touches them shall become holy. 30Anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them, that they may serve MemeMe as priests. 31Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘This shall be a holy anointing oil to MemeMe throughout your generations.’ 32It shall not be poured on ordinary human flesh, and you shall not make any like it in composition; it is holy, and it shall be holy to you. 33Whoever compounds any like it, or puts any of it on an unauthorized person, shall be cut off from his people.”

34The ThetheThe LORD said to Moses, “Take spices: stacte, onycha, and galbanum—spices—and pure frankincense; let there be an equal part of each. 35Make it into incense, a perfume blended by the perfumer's art, salted, pure and holy. 36Grind some of it to a very fine powder and put some of it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you; it shall be most holy to you. 37The incense that you make must not be made for your own use according to this formula; it is to be holy to you for the the LORD. 38Anyone who makes anything like it to enjoy its fragrance shall be cut off from his people.”
