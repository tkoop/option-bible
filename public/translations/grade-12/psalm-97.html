The Sovereign Reign of the LORD

1The LORD reigns; let the earth rejoice, and let the many coastlands be glad. 2Clouds and thick darkness surround HimhimHim; righteousness and justice are the foundation of HishisHis throne. 3Fire goes before HimhimHim and consumes HishisHis adversaries on every side. 4His lightning illumines the world; the earth sees and trembles. 5The mountains melt like wax in the presence of the LORD, in the presence of the Lord of all the earth. 6The heavens proclaim HishisHis righteousness, and all peoples behold HishisHis glory.

7All who worship carved images are put to shame, those who boast in worthless idols. Worship HimhimHim, all you gods! 8Zion hears and rejoices, and the daughters of Judah are glad because of YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments, O LORD. 9For YouyouYou, O LORD, are MostmostMost HighhighHigh over all the earth; YouyouYou are exalted far above all gods. 10You who love the LORD, hate evil! He preserves the souls of HishisHis saints; HeheHe delivers them from the hand of the wicked. 11Light is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart. 12Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous, and give thanks at the remembrance of HishisHis holiness.
