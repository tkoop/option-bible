The Parable of Two Unfaithful Sisters

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2"SonsonSon of man, two women were born to the same mother.

3"They prostituted themselves in Egypt and gave themselves to promiscuity in their youth; there their breasts were caressed, and there their virginity was violated. 4The elder was named Oholah, and her sister Oholibah. They became MinemineMine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.

5"Oholah prostituted herself while she was still MinemineMine. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, her neighbors— 6warriors clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all desirable young men, horsemen riding horses. 7She lavished her prostitution upon all of them, the choicest men of Assyria, and defiled herself with every idol of everyone she coveted. 8She did not abandon the prostitution she had begun in Egypt, when men lay with her in her youth, caressed her virgin breasts, and poured out their desire upon her.

9"Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, the Assyrians she had coveted. 10They exposed her shame, seized her sons and daughters, and killed her with the sword. She became a byword among women, and they executed judgmentjudgementjudgment upon her.

11"Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she was even more depraved in her lust than her sister, and her prostitution exceeded her sister's. 12She lusted after the Assyrians—governors and commanders, warriors arrayed in splendoursplendoursplendor, horsemen riding horses, all desirable young men. 13I saw that both were defiled and had followed the same path.

14"Yet she increased her prostitution. She saw men carved upon a wall, images of Chaldeans drawn in vermilion, 15girded with belts around their waists and flowing turbans upon their heads, all resembling officers—the likeness of Babylonians, Chaldeans from their native land. 16As soon as she saw them, she lusted after them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

17"Then the Babylonians came to her bed of love and defiled her with their lust. After she had been defiled by them, she turned away from them in disgust. 18When she flaunted her prostitution and exposed her shame, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister. 19Yet she increased her prostitution, recalling the days of her youth when she prostituted herself in Egypt. 20She lusted after lovers there whose flesh is like the flesh of donkeys and whose emission is like the emission of horses. 21So you revisited the lewdness of your youth, when the Egyptians handled your breasts and caressed your youthful bosom.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Against Oholibah

22"Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to rouse your lovers against you—those from whom you turned away in disgust—and I will bring them against you from every side: 23the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans—Pekod, Shoa, and Koa—and all the Assyrians with them, desirable young men, governors and commanders, officers and men of renown, all riding horses.

24"They will come against you with an assembly of peoples, with weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will surround you on every side with buckler, shield, and helmet. I will place judgmentjudgementjudgment before them, and they will judge you according to their own standards. 25I will pour out MymyMy jealousy upon you, and they will deal with you in wrath. They will cut off your nose and your ears, and your survivors will fall by the sword. They will seize your sons and daughters, and your survivors will be consumed by fire. 26They will strip off your clothes and take away your fine ornaments. 27So I will put an end to your lewdness and your prostitution, which began in Egypt. You will no longer lift your eyes to them, nor will you remember Egypt again.

28"For this is what the Lord GOD says: I am going to deliver you into the hands of those you hate, into the hands of those from whom you turned away in disgust. 29They will treat you with hatred and take all your wealth. They will leave you naked and bare, and the shame of your prostitution will be exposed. Your lewdness and prostitution will be uncovered. 30I will do these things to you because you prostituted yourself with the nations and defiled yourself with their idols. 31You have walked in the way of your sister; therefore I will put her cup into your hand.

32"This is what the Lord GOD says: You will drink your sister's cup, a cup deep and wide; it will bring you scorn and derision, for it holds much. 33You will be filled with drunkenness and grief—a cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria. 34You will drink it and drain it; you will scrape its shards with your teeth and tear your breasts. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

35"Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have forgotten MemeMe and cast MemeMe behind your back, you must bear the consequences of your lewdness and prostitution."

The Abominations Declared

36The LORD said to me, "SonsonSon of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their abominations.

37"For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They committed adultery with their idols, and they even sacrificed to them the children they bore to MemeMe. 38They have also done this to MemeMe: they defiled MymyMy sanctuary on that day and profaned MymyMy Sabbaths. 39On the very day they sacrificed their children to their idols, they entered MymyMy sanctuary to profane it. Yes, this is what they did inside MymyMy house.

40"Furthermore, they sent for men who came from afar. When they arrived, you bathed yourself for them, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with jewelleryjewelleryjewelry. 41You sat on a stately couch with a table spread before it, on which you had placed MymyMy incense and MymyMy oil. 42The sound of a carefree crowd was there; and with ordinary men, drunkards were brought in from the desert. They put bracelets on the women's wrists and beautiful crowns upon their heads.

43"Then I said of her who was worn out by adulteries, 'Will they now commit adultery with her, and she with them?' 44Men went in to her as to a prostitute. So they went in to Oholah and Oholibah, those lewd women.

45"But righteous men will judge them with the judgmentjudgementjudgment of adulteresses and murderers, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands. 46For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will bring a mob against them and consign them to terror and plunder. 47The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn their houses.

48"So I will put an end to lewdness in the land, and all the women will be warned not to imitate your lewdness. 49They will repay you for your lewdness, and you will bear the penalty of your idolatry. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD."
