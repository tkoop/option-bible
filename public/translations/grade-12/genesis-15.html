God Promises Abram a Reward and an Heir

1After these things, the word of the the LORD came to Abram in a vision, saying, “Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your shield; your reward will be very great.”

2Abram said, “Lord the the LORD, what will YouyouYou give me, since I continue childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus?” 3Abram added, “Since YouyouYou have given me no children, one born in my house will be my heir.”

4Then the word of the the LORD came to him: “This man will not be your heir; but one who comes from your own body will be your heir.” 5He brought him outside and said, “Look now toward Heavenheavenheaven and count the stars, if you are able to count them.” Then HeheHe said, “So shall your descendants be.”

6Abram believed in the the LORD, and HeheHe counted it to him as righteousness.

The LORD Promises the Land

7He also said to him, “I am the the LORD, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans, to give you this land to possess.” 8Abram said, “Lord the the LORD, how can I know that I will possess it?”

The Covenant Ceremony

9He said to him, “Bring MemeMe a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a turtledove, and a young pigeon.” 10Abram brought all these to HimhimHim, cut them in two, and laid the halves opposite each other; but he did not cut the birds. 11When birds of prey came down on the carcasses, Abram drove them away.

12As the sun was setting, a deep sleep fell on Abram, and a great and terrifying darkness overwhelmed him.

God Foretells Israel's Future and Confirms the Covenant

13Then the the LORD said to Abram, “Know for certain that your descendants will be foreigners in a land that is not theirs; they will be enslaved and oppressed for four hundred years. 14But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will go out with great wealth. 15As for you, you will go to your fathers in peace and be buried at a good old age. 16In the fourth generation they will return here, for the iniquity of the Amorites has not yet reached its full measure.”

17When the sun had set and darkness had fallen, a smoking furnace and a blazing torch passed between the pieces.

18On that day the the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, “To your descendants I have given this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the Euphrates: 19the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, 20the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, 21the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.”
