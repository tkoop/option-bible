A Summons to the Shameless Nation

1Gather yourselves, gather together, O nation without shame. 2Before the decree is born, before the day passes like chaff, before the fierce anger of the LORD comes upon you, before the DaydayDay of the LORD comes upon you. 3Seek the LORD, all you humble people of the land who have done what HishisHis justice requires. Seek righteousness; seek humility. Perhaps you will be sheltered on the day of the LORD's wrath.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Philistines

4For Gaza will be abandoned, and Ashkelon left a desolation; Ashdod will be emptied by midday, and Ekron will be uprooted. 5Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast, O nation of the Cherethites! The word of the LORD is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines; and I will destroy you, and no one will remain. 6The seacoast will become pastureland, with shepherd encampments and sheepfolds. 7It will belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; there they will pasture and lie down in the evening within the houses of Ashkelon, for the LORD their God will attend to them and restore their fortunes.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Moab and Ammon

8I have heard the taunting of Moab and the reviling of the Ammonites, who have taunted MymyMy people and threatened their borders. 9Therefore, as I live, declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, Moab will become like Sodom and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—a land of weeds and salt pits, a desolation forever. The remnant of MymyMy people will plunder them, and the survivors of MymyMy nation will inherit them. 10This is what they will receive in return for their pride, for taunting and exalting themselves against the people of the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD's Triumph over the Nations

11The LORD will be fearsome to them when HeheHe reduces to nothing all the gods of the earth; then every nation will bow down to HimhimHim, each in its own place, even all the coastlands of the nations. 12You too, O Cushites, will be slain by MymyMy sword. 13He will stretch out HishisHis hand against the north and destroy Assyria, and HeheHe will make Nineveh a desolation, dry as the wilderness. 14Flocks will lie down in her midst, every beast of the nations; both the desert owl and the screech owl will lodge in her pillars. Their calls will echo through the windows; desolation will be on the threshold, for HeheHe has laid bare the cedar beams. 15This is the exultant city that dwelt securely, that said in her heart, "I am, and there is no one besides me." How she has become a desolation, a lair for wild beasts! Everyone who passes by her hisses and shakes his fist.
