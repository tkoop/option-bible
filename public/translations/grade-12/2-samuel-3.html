David's Sons in Hebron

1A long war raged between the house of Saul and the house of David, and while David grew steadily stronger, Saul's house grew steadily weaker. 2Sons were born to David in Hebron: his firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel; 3his second was Chileab, by Abigail, the widow of Nabal the Carmelite; the third was Absalom, son of Maacah, daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur; 4the fourth was Adonijah, son of Haggith; the fifth was Shephatiah, son of Abital; 5and the sixth was Ithream, by Eglah, David's wife. All these were born to David in Hebron.

Abner Defects to David

6While war continued between the houses of Saul and David, Abner was consolidating his power in Saul's house. 7Saul had a concubine named Rizpah, daughter of Aiah, and Ish-Bosheth said to Abner, "Why have you slept with my father's concubine?"

8Abner grew very angry at Ish-Bosheth's words and replied, "Am I some contemptible dog of Judah? To this day I have shown steadfast loyalty to the house of Saul your father, to his brothers, and to his friends, and I have not handed you over to David; yet today you accuse me over a woman. 9May God deal with Abner, and worse, if I do not do for David exactly what the LORD has sworn to him: 10to transfer the kingdom from Saul's house and establish David's throne over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba." 11Ish-Bosheth could not answer Abner a single word, for he feared him.

12Then Abner sent messengers to David, saying, "To whom does the land belong? Make your covenant with me, and I will use my influence to bring all Israel over to you."

13David replied, "Good. I will make a covenant with you, but I require one thing of you: you will not see my face unless you first bring Michal, Saul's daughter, when you come to see me."

14David also sent messengers to Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, saying, "Give me my wife Michal, whom I betrothed to myself for a hundred Philistine foreskins."

15So Ish-Bosheth sent and took her from her husband, Paltiel son of Laish. 16Her husband followed her, weeping all the way to Bahurim. Then Abner said to him, "Go back," and he went back.

17Abner conferred with the elders of Israel and said, "For some time now you have been seeking David as king over you. 18Now then, act on it, for the LORD has said of David, 'By the hand of MymyMy servant David I will save MymyMy people Israel from the Philistines and from all their enemies.'" 19Abner also spoke to the Benjaminites, and then he went to Hebron to tell David everything that Israel and the whole house of Benjamin approved.

20Abner and twenty men with him came to David at Hebron, and David prepared a feast for Abner and his men. 21Abner said to David, "Let me go and gather all Israel to my lord the king, so that they may make a covenant with you, and you may reign over all that your heart desires." Then David sent Abner away, and he departed in peace.

Joab Kills Abner

22Just then David's servants and Joab returned from a raid, bringing much plunder with them. Abner was no longer with David in Hebron, for David had sent him away in peace. 23When Joab and all the troops with him arrived, they told him, "Abner son of Ner came to the king, and the king sent him away in peace."

24Joab went to the king and said, "What have you done? Look, Abner came to you; why did you send him away so that he could leave in peace? 25You know Abner son of Ner: he came to deceive you, to learn your movements and your plans, and to find out everything you are doing."

26After Joab left David's presence, he sent messengers after Abner, who brought him back from the well of Sirah; David did not know about it. 27When Abner returned to Hebron, Joab took him aside at the gate, as though to speak with him privately, and there stabbed him in the stomach. So Abner died in revenge for the blood of Asahel, Joab's brother.

28When David heard of it later, he said, "I and my kingdom are forever innocent before the LORD of the blood of Abner son of Ner. 29Let the guilt fall on Joab's head and on all his father's house, and may Joab's house never be without one who has a discharge, or is a leper, or leans on a staff, or falls by the sword, or lacks bread." 30Joab and his brother Abishai had killed Abner because he had killed their brother Asahel at Gibeon in the battle.

David Mourns Abner

31David said to Joab and to all the people with him, "Tear your clothes, put on sackcloth, and mourn for Abner." And King David himself walked behind the bier. 32They buried Abner in Hebron, and the king wept aloud at Abner's tomb; all the people wept as well.

33The king sang a lament over Abner, saying, "Should Abner die as a fool dies? 34Your hands were not bound, and your feet were not fettered; you fell as a man falls before wicked men." Then all the people wept over him again.

35All the people urged David to eat something while it was still day, but David took an oath, saying, "May God do to me, and more, if I taste bread or anything else before the sun goes down." 36All the people noticed this, and it pleased them; everything the king did pleased all the people. 37So all the people and all Israel understood that day that it had not been the king's will to put Abner son of Ner to death.

38The king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a prince and a great man has fallen today in Israel? 39I am weak today, though anointed king, and these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too hard for me. The LORD will repay the evildoer according to his wickedness."
