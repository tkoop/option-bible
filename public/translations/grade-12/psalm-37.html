Do Not Be Vexed by the Prosperity of Evildoers

1Do not be vexed by evildoers, nor envy those who practice iniquity. 2For they will soon be mown down like grass and wither like tender herbs. 3Trust in the LORD and do good; dwell in the land and rest secure in HishisHis faithfulness.

4Take delight in the LORD, and HeheHe will grant the desires of your heart. 5Commit your course to the LORD; rely upon HimhimHim, and HeheHe will accomplish it. 6He will bring forth your vindication like the dawn and your justice like the noonday sun.

7Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for HimhimHim; do not be vexed by the prosperous wrongdoer, by the man who executes wicked schemes. 8Desist from anger and forsake wrath; do not be vexed, for it leads only to evil.

9For evildoers will be cut off, but those who hope in the LORD will inherit the land. 10In a little while the wicked will be no more; though you search for them, they will not be found. 11But the meek will inherit the land and delight in abundant peace.

The LORD Overrules the Malice of the Wicked

12The wicked plot against the righteous and gnash their teeth at them; 13but the Lord laughs at them, for HeheHe sees that their day is approaching. 14The wicked have drawn the sword and bent the bow to cast down the poor and needy, to slaughter those whose conduct is upright. 15Yet their sword will pierce their own hearts, and their bows will be shattered.

16Better is the little that the righteous possess than the abundance of many wicked; 17for the arms of the wicked will be broken, but the LORD sustains the righteous. 18The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their inheritance will endure forever. 19In times of calamity they will not be put to shame, and in days of famine they will be satisfied.

20But the wicked will perish, and the LORD's enemies, like the splendoursplendoursplendor of the pastures, will vanish; like smoke they will vanish away. 21The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous are gracious and generous. 22Surely those blessed by HimhimHim will inherit the land, but those cursed by HimhimHim will be cut off.

The LORD Establishes and Preserves the Righteous

23The steps of a man are established by the LORD, and HeheHe delights in his way. 24Though he stumble, he will not be cast down, for the LORD upholds him with HishisHis hand. 25I have been young and now am old, yet I have never seen the righteous forsaken, nor their children begging for bread. 26They are ever generous and lend freely, and their children are blessed.

27Turn from evil and do good, and you will dwell in the land forever. 28For the LORD loves justice and will not forsake HishisHis saints; they are preserved forever, but the offspring of the wicked will be cut off. 29The righteous will inherit the land and dwell in it forever.

30The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice. 31The law of his God is in his heart; his feet do not slip. 32The wicked lie in wait for the righteous and seek to slay them; 33but the LORD will not abandon them to the power of the wicked, nor condemn them when they are brought to judgmentjudgementjudgment.

The Final Deliverance of Those Who Take Refuge in Him

34Wait for the LORD and keep HishisHis way, and HeheHe will exalt you to inherit the land; you will witness the destruction of the wicked. 35I have seen a ruthless wicked man flourishing like a luxuriant tree in its native soil; 36yet he passed away and was no more; though I sought him, he could not be found.

37Consider the blameless and observe the upright, for a future awaits the man of peace. 38But transgressors will be utterly destroyed; the future of the wicked will be cut off. 39The salvation of the righteous comes from the LORD; HeheHe is their stronghold in time of trouble. 40The LORD helps them and delivers them; HeheHe rescues them from the wicked and saves them, because they take refuge in HimhimHim.
