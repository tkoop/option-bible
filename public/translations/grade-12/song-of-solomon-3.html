The Bride's Search for Her Beloved

1Upon my bed in the night watches, I sought the one I love; I sought him, but he was not to be found. 2I will arise now and go about the city, through its streets and squares, and seek the one I love. I sought him, but he was not to be found.

3The sentinels patrolling the city found me, and I asked them, "Have you seen the one I love?" 4Scarcely had I passed them before I found the one I love; I seized him and would not release him until I had brought him to my mother's house, to the chamber of her who bore me.

5Daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles and the does of the field: do not stir up or awaken love until it is ready.

The Royal Procession

6Who is this coming up from the wilderness like pillars of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and frankincense, with every fragrant powder of the merchant? 7Behold, Solomon's palanquin, escorted by sixty warriors, the mightiest of Israel. 8Each is skilled with the sword and trained for war; each man wears his sword upon his thigh because of the terrors of night.

9King Solomon made for himself a palanquin of Lebanese cedar. 10He made its posts of silver, its base of gold, its seat of purple; its interior is inlaid with love by the daughters of Jerusalem. 11"Go out, daughters of Zion, and behold King Solomon, wearing the crown with which his mother crowned him on the day of his wedding, the day his heart rejoiced."
