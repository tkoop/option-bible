Gabriel Answers Daniel’s Prayer

20While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my supplication before the LORD my God for the holy hill of my God, 21while I was still speaking in prayer, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the earlier vision, came to me in swift flight at the time of the evening offering. 22He instructed me and spoke with me, saying, “O Daniel, I have now come to give you insight and understanding. 23At the beginning of your supplications a word went out, and I have come to tell it to you, for you are highly esteemed.
