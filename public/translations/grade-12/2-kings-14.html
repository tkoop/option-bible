Amaziah’s Reign in Judah

1In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash began to reign as king of Judah. 2He was twenty-five years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan, a native of Jerusalem. 3He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, though not as David his father had done; instead, he followed in every respect the example his father Joash had set. 4Nevertheless, the high places were not removed, and the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on them.

5Once his authority was firmly established, he executed the servants who had assassinated his father the king. 6Yet he did not put the murderers’ children to death, in obedience to what is written in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses, where the LORD commanded, “Fathers must not be put to death for their children, nor children for their fathers; rather, each person shall die for his own sin.” 7He also struck down ten thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt, captured Sela in battle, and renamed it Joktheel, the name by which it is still known.

War Between Judah and Israel

8Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, with this challenge: “Come, let us meet in battle.”

9Jehoash king of Israel sent back this reply to Amaziah king of Judah: “A thistle in Lebanon sent word to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son in marriage.’ Then a wild beast of Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle. 10You have indeed defeated Edom, and your heart is lifted up with pride. Enjoy your glory and remain at home. Why provoke calamity, so that you fall—you and Judah with you?”

11But Amaziah refused to listen. So Jehoash king of Israel advanced, and the two kings met in battle at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 12Judah was defeated by Israel, and every man fled home.

13At Beth-shemesh, Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Ahaziah. He then marched on Jerusalem and tore down its wall from the Ephraim GategateGate to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate, a distance of four hundred cubits. 14He seized all the gold and silver and every vessel found in the house of the LORD and in the royal treasuries, together with the hostages, and then returned to Samaria.

15The remaining deeds of Jehoash—his accomplishments, his valourvalourvalor, and his war with Amaziah king of Judah—are recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 16Jehoash died and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel, and his son Jeroboam succeeded him.

17Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 18The rest of Amaziah’s deeds are recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.

The Assassination of Amaziah

19A conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but his pursuers followed him there and killed him. 20They carried him back on horses and buried him in Jerusalem with his fathers in the CitycityCity of David. 21Then all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and proclaimed him king in place of his father Amaziah. 22He rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah after Amaziah’s death.

Jeroboam Son of Joash Reigns in Israel

23In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned forty-one years. 24He did evil in the sight of the LORD and did not turn away from any of the sins that Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.

25He restored Israel’s border from Lebo-hamath to the SeaseaSea of the Arabah, in accordance with the word that the LORD, the God of Israel, had spoken through HishisHis servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher. 26For the LORD had seen the bitter affliction of Israel—there was no one left to help them, bond or free, and no helper for Israel. 27Yet the LORD had not decreed the obliteration of Israel’s name from under Heavenheavenheaven; instead, HeheHe delivered them through Jeroboam son of Joash.

28The remaining deeds of Jeroboam—all that he accomplished, his military exploits, and his recovery of Damascus and Hamath for Israel—are recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 29Jeroboam died and was buried with the kings of Israel, and his son Zechariah succeeded him.
