A Prayer for Deliverance from Hidden Enemies

1Hear my voice, O God, in my lamentation; preserve my life from the dread inspired by my enemy. 2Conceal me from the clandestine counsel of evildoers, from the tumult of those who perpetrate iniquity.

3They have whetted their tongues like swords; they have aimed bitter words as though they were arrows. 4From ambush they shoot at the blameless; suddenly they loose their shafts, and they entertain no fear. 5They fortify one another in wickedness; they conspire to conceal snares, saying, “Who will see us?” 6They scrutinizescrutinisescrutinize iniquities and complete a meticulous search; the inward thought of each man is profound, and his heart is unfathomable.

7But God will shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they will be wounded. 8Their own tongues will precipitate their ruin; all who behold them will recoil.
