The Violation of Dinah

1Now Dinah, the daughter Leah had borne to Jacob, went out to visit the young women of the land. 2Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the ruler of that region, saw her, seized her, lay with her, and violated her. 3Yet his soul clung to Dinah, Jacob’s daughter; he loved the young woman and spoke tenderly to her. 4Then Shechem said to his father Hamor, “Get me this young woman for a wife.” 5Now Jacob had heard that Shechem had defiled his daughter Dinah, but because his sons were with the livestock in the field, Jacob remained silent until they returned. 6Meanwhile, Hamor, Shechem’s father, went out to speak with Jacob. 7When Jacob’s sons returned from the field and heard what had happened, they were grieved and deeply angered, for Shechem had committed an outrage in Israel by lying with Jacob’s daughter—a thing that should never be done.

Hamor Proposes Intermarriage

8Hamor spoke with them and said, “My son Shechem’s heart is set on your daughter. Please give her to him as his wife. 9Intermarry with us: give us your daughters, and take our daughters for yourselves. 10You may settle among us, and the land will be open before you; live in it, trade in it, and acquire holdings in it.” 11Shechem also said to her father and brothers, “Let me find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, and I will give whatever you ask. 12Set the bride-price and gift as high as you wish; I will pay whatever you say. Only give me the young woman as my wife.”

Jacob’s Sons Answer Deceitfully

13Jacob’s sons answered Shechem and his father Hamor deceitfully, speaking as they did because Shechem had defiled their sister Dinah. 14They said to them, “We cannot do this thing—give our sister to a man who is uncircumcised—for that would be a disgrace to us.” 15“Only on this condition will we consent to you: if every male among you is circumcised, as we are. 16Then we will give you our daughters and take your daughters for ourselves, and we will live among you and become one people. 17But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter and leave.”

18Their proposal pleased Hamor and his son Shechem. 19The young man did not delay in carrying out the agreement, for he delighted in Jacob’s daughter, and he was the most honouredhonouredhonored member of his father’s household. 20Hamor and his son Shechem went to the gate of their city and spoke to the men of the city: 21“These men are at peace with us. Let them settle in the land and trade in it, for the land is spacious enough for them. Let us take their daughters as wives and give our daughters to them.” 22“But only on this condition will these men consent to live with us and become one people: every male among us must be circumcised, as they are. 23Will not their livestock, their property, and all their animals become ours? Let us only agree, and they will settle among us.” 24All who went out through the city gate listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every male was circumcised—all who went out through the city gate.

The Vengeance of Simeon and Levi

25On the third day, when the men were still in pain, two of Jacob’s sons—Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brothers—took their swords, came upon the city unawares, and killed every male. 26They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the sword, took Dinah from Shechem’s house, and departed. 27Jacob’s sons came upon the slain and plundered the city because Shechem had defiled their sister. 28They took their sheep, oxen, and donkeys—everything in the city and in the fields. 29They carried off all their wealth, their little ones, and their wives, plundering everything in the houses. 30Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me by bringing me into disrepute among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I am few in number; they will gather themselves together against me and attack me, and I shall be destroyed, I and my household.” 31They said, “Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?”
