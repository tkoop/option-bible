Laws of Justice and Compassion

1“You must not spread a false report, nor join your hand with the wicked to be a malicious witness. 2You must not follow the multitude in doing evil, nor shall you testify in a dispute so as to turn aside after the multitude and pervert justice. 3Nor shall you show partiality to a poor man in his dispute.

4“If you meet your enemy’s ox or his donkey wandering astray, you shall surely return it to him. 5If you see the donkey of one who hates you lying beneath its burden, and you would refrain from helping it, you shall surely help him with it. 6You must not pervert the judgmentjudgementjudgment due to your poor in his dispute.

7“Keep far from a false charge, and do not kill the innocent and the righteous, for I will not justify the wicked. 8You must take no bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and perverts the words of the righteous. 9You must not oppress a sojourner, for you know the heart of a sojourner, since you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.”

Sabbatical Rhythms and Sacred Festivals

The Promised Guide and the Conquest of Canaan
