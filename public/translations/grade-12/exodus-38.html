The Bronze Altar and Its Utensils

1Bezalel constructed the altar of burnt offering from acacia wood, fashioning it square—five cubits in length, five cubits in width, and three cubits in height. 2He fashioned a horn at each of its four corners, integrating the horns with the altar itself, and he overlaid the whole with bronze. 3He made all the altar’s utensils—the pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans—and he made all of them from bronze. 4He fashioned a bronze grating for the altar, a network of bronze, to be situated beneath its ledge and to extend halfway up the altar. 5He cast four rings for the four corners of the bronze grating, to receive the poles. 6He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze. 7He then inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, so that it could be carried, and he made the altar hollow, constructing it from boards.

The Bronze Basin

8He made the bronze basin and its bronze stand from the mirrors of the women who ministered at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting.

The Courtyard and Its Furnishings

9Then he constructed the courtyard. The south side measured a hundred cubits in length and was furnished with curtains of finely twisted linen. 10It had twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, with silver hooks and bands upon the posts. 11The north side was likewise a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and with silver hooks and bands upon the posts. 12The west side measured fifty cubits and had ten posts and ten bases, with silver hooks and bands upon the posts. 13The east side, toward the sunrise, measured fifty cubits. 14On one side of the entrance were curtains fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases, 15and on the other side were curtains fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases.

16All the curtains surrounding the courtyard were of finely twisted linen. 17The bases for the posts were bronze; the hooks and bands of the posts were silver; and the overlay of the tops of the posts was silver, so that all the posts of the courtyard were banded with silver. 18The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and with finely twisted linen; it was twenty cubits long and, like the curtains of the courtyard, five cubits high. 19It had four posts and four bronze bases; their hooks were silver, as were the bands and the overlay of their tops. 20All the tent pegs for the tabernacle and the surrounding courtyard were bronze.

The Inventory of the Tabernacle and Its Materials

21This is the inventory of the tabernacle—the tabernacle of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony—as recorded at Moses’ command by the Levites under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest. 22Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything that the LORD had commanded Moses. 23With him was Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, a craftsman, designer, and embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and in fine linen.

24All the gold that was employed in the work—in all the work of the sanctuary, the gold of the wave offering—amounted to twenty-nine talents and 730 shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel. 25The silver from those numbered in the congregation was 100 talents and 1,775 shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel— 26a beka per person, that is, half a shekel, according to the sanctuary shekel, from every man twenty years of age or older who was enrolled in the census: 603,550 men. 27The hundred talents of silver were cast into the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the veil: a hundred bases from the hundred talents, one talent for each base. 28From the 1,775 shekels he made hooks for the posts, overlaid their tops, and made bands for them. 29The bronze from the wave offering was 70 talents and 2,400 shekels. 30With it he made the bases for the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grating, and all the utensils for the altar, 31as well as the bases for the surrounding courtyard, the bases for the courtyard entrance, and all the tent pegs for the tabernacle and the surrounding courtyard.
