The Reigns of Jehoahaz and Jehoiakim

1The people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah and made him king in Jerusalem in place of his father. 2Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. 3The king of Egypt deposed him in Jerusalem and imposed on the land a tribute of a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold. 4The king of Egypt made Eliakim, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem, and changed his name to Jehoiakim. The king of Egypt took Jehoahaz, his brother, and carried him off to Egypt.

5Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD his God. 6Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against him and bound him in bronze shackles to take him to Babylon. 7Nebuchadnezzar also carried to Babylon some of the articles from the house of the the LORD and put them in his temple in Babylon. 8The rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, the abominations he committed, and what was found against him, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Israel and Judah. Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.

Jehoiachin, Zedekiah, and the Nation’s Decline

9Jehoiachin was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem. He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. 10At the turn of the year King Nebuchadnezzar sent for him and brought him to Babylon, along with the precious articles of the house of the the LORD, and made Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem. 11Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. 12He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD his God. He did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke from the mouth of the the LORD. 13He also rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God. He stubbornly refused and hardened his heart against turning to the the LORD, the God of Israel. 14All the leaders of the priests and the people likewise increased in unfaithfulness, following all the abominations of the nations, and they defiled the house of the the LORD that HeheHe had consecrated in Jerusalem. 15The ThetheThe LORD, the God of their fathers, sent to them again and again by HishisHis messengers, because HeheHe had compassion on HishisHis people and on HishisHis dwelling place. 16But they mocked the messengers of God, despised HishisHis words, and scoffed at HishisHis prophets, until the wrath of the the LORD rose against HishisHis people, until there was no remedy.

The Fall of Jerusalem and the Babylonian Exile

17Therefore HeheHe brought up against them the king of the Chaldeans, who killed their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and had no compassion on young man or virgin, old man or aged; HeheHe gave them all into his hand. 18All the articles of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the the LORD and the treasures of the king and his officials, he brought to Babylon. 19They burned the house of God and broke down the wall of Jerusalem; they burned all its palaces with fire and destroyed all its precious articles. 20He carried into exile those who escaped the sword to Babylon, and they became servants to him and to his sons until the kingdom of Persia came to power. 21So the land enjoyed its Sabbaths; all the days of desolation it kept Sabbath, to fulfillfulfilfulfill the word of the the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah, until seventy years were completed.

Cyrus Decrees the Return to Jerusalem

22In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to fulfillfulfilfulfill the word of the the LORD spoken by Jeremiah, the the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom and put it in writing, saying: 23“Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: The ThetheThe LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and HeheHe has appointed me to build HimhimHim a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever among you belongs to HishisHis people, may the the LORD his God be with him, and let him go up.”
