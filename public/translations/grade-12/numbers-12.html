Miriam and Aaron Speak Against Moses

1Miriam and Aaron began to speak against Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married, for he had married a Cushite woman. 2They said, “Has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Hasn’t HeheHe also spoken through us?” And the LORD heard this.

3Now Moses was a very humble man, more humble than anyone else on the face of the earth.

The LORD Summons and Rebukes Them

4Suddenly the LORD said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, “All three of you, come out to the tent of meeting.” So the three of them went out. 5Then the LORD came down in a pillar of cloud, stood at the entrance to the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam. The two of them came forward.

6The LORD said, “Listen to MymyMy words: If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, reveal MyselfmyselfMyself to him in visions; I speak to him in dreams. 7Not so with MymyMy servant Moses; he is faithful in all MymyMy house. 8With him I speak face to face—clearly, not in riddles—and he sees the form of the LORD. Why then were you not afraid to speak against MymyMy servant Moses?”

9The anger of the LORD burned against them, and HeheHe departed.

Miriam Is Stricken and Restored

10When the cloud lifted from above the tent, Miriam was leprous, white as snow. Aaron turned toward her and saw that she was leprous.

11Aaron said to Moses, “Please, my lord, do not hold against us the sin we have so foolishly committed. 12Please do not let her be like a stillborn infant whose flesh is half consumed when it comes out of its mother’s womb.”

13Moses cried out to the LORD, “O God, please heal her!”

14The LORD replied to Moses, “If her father had publicly shamed her, would she not be disgraced for seven days? Let her be confined outside the camp for seven days; after that she may be brought back.”

15So Miriam was confined outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not move on until she was brought back. 16Afterward the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Paran.
