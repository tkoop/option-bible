Hananiah's False Prophecy

1In the fifth month of that year—the fourth year, early in the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah—the prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, from Gibeon, said to me in the house of the LORD before the priests and all the people:

2"Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: 'I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon. 3Within two years I will bring back to this place all the vessels of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took from here and carried to Babylon. 4I will also bring back to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah and all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, declares the LORD, for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.'"

Jeremiah Answers Hananiah

5Then the prophet Jeremiah replied to the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests and all the people standing in the house of the LORD:

6"Amen! May the LORD do so. May the LORD fulfillfulfilfulfill your words, which you have prophesied, and bring back the vessels of the house of the LORD and all the exiles from Babylon to this place. 7But listen now to this word that I am speaking in your hearing and in the hearing of all the people. 8The prophets who preceded us from ancient times prophesied against many lands and great kingdoms, foretelling war, disaster, and plague. 9As for the prophet who prophesies peace, his words will prove true only when they come to pass; then it will be known that the LORD has truly sent him."

Hananiah Breaks the Yoke

10Then the prophet Hananiah took the yoke from Jeremiah's neck and broke it. 11In the presence of all the people, Hananiah declared, "Thus says the LORD: 'In this same way, within two years, I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all the nations.'" Then Jeremiah the prophet went on his way.

The Iron Yoke and Hananiah's Death

12After the prophet Hananiah had broken the yoke from Jeremiah's neck, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah: 13"Go and tell Hananiah, 'Thus says the LORD: You have broken the wooden yokes, but you have made iron yokes in their place.' 14For thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: 'I have put an iron yoke on the neck of all these nations, that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him; I have even given him the beasts of the field.'"

15Then the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, "Listen, Hananiah! The LORD has not sent you, and you have made this people trust in a lie. 16Therefore this is what the LORD says: I am about to remove you from the face of the earth. This very year you will die, because you have spoken rebellion against the LORD."

17And in the seventh month of that year, the prophet Hananiah died.
