All Creation's Call to Worship the LORD

1Hallelujah! Praise the the LORD from the heavens; praise HimhimHim in the loftiest realms. 2Praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis angels; praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis celestial hosts. 3Praise HimhimHim, sun and moon; praise HimhimHim, all you radiant stars. 4Praise HimhimHim, you highest heavens, and waters beyond the skies.

5Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for at HishisHis command they were created. 6He established them for all eternity; HeheHe decreed a law that shall never be revoked.

7Praise the the LORD from the earth, you great sea creatures and all ocean depths, 8fire and hail, snow and mist, stormy winds executing HishisHis word; 9you mountains and all hills, fruit-bearing trees and all cedars; 10wild beasts and all livestock, creeping things and winged birds.

11Kings of the earth and all nations, you princes and all judges of the earth, 12young men and maidens, elders and children.

13Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for HishisHis name alone is exalted; HishisHis majesty surpasses EarthEarthearth and Heavenheavenheaven. 14He has raised up a horn for HishisHis people, the praise of all HishisHis saints, of Israel, a people near to HimhimHim. Hallelujah!
