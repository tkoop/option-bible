A Final Summons to Universal Praise

1Praise the LORD! Praise God in HishisHis sanctuary; praise HimhimHim in HishisHis mighty heavens. 2Praise HimhimHim for HishisHis mighty acts; praise HimhimHim for HishisHis excellent greatness.

3Praise HimhimHim with the sound of the trumpet; praise HimhimHim with harp and lyre. 4Praise HimhimHim with tambourine and dancing; praise HimhimHim with strings and flute.

5Praise HimhimHim with clashing cymbals; praise HimhimHim with resounding cymbals. 6Let everything that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
