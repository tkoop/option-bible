Discipleship, Forgiveness, and the Nature of Faith

1"It is inevitable that stumbling blocks will come, but woe to the one through whom they come. 2It would be better for him to have a millstone hung around his neck and be thrown into the sea than to cause one of these little ones to stumble."

3"Guard yourselves carefully. If your brother sins against you, reprimand him; and if he repents, forgive him. 4Even if he sins against you seven times in a single day and seven times returns to you saying, 'I repent,' you must forgive him."

5The apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith!"

6He replied, "If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you."

7"Suppose one of you has a servant plowing or tending sheep. Would he say to the servant when he comes in from the field, 'Come along now and sit down to eat'? 8Would he not rather say, 'Prepare my supper, get yourself ready, and wait on me while I eat and drink; after that you may eat and drink'? 9Would he thank the servant because he did what he was told to do? 10So you also, when you have done everything you were told to do, should say, 'We are unworthy servants; we have only done our duty.'"
