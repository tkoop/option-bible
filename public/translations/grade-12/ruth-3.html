Ruth's Bold Request

7After Boaz had eaten and drunk and was in good spirits, he went to lie down at the far end of the grain heap. Ruth came quietly, uncovered his feet, and lay down. 8At midnight the man was startled; he turned over and discovered a woman lying at his feet.

9"Who are you?" he asked.

She replied, "I am Ruth, your servant. Take me under your protection as your wife, for you are my kinsman-redeemer."

Boaz's Promise

10"Blessed are you by the LORD, my daughter.
