5Then HeheHe said to them, “Suppose one of you has a friend and goes to him at midnight and says, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread, 6for a friend of mine has arrived on a journey, and I have nothing to set before him.’ 7And he answers from within, ‘Do not trouble me; the door is already locked, and my children are with me in bed.
