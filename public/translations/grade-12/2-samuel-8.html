David Subdues the Philistines and Moab

1After this, David defeated the Philistines and subdued them, and he took the Philistines’ principal stronghold from their control. 2David also defeated Moab. He forced its people to lie on the ground and measured them with a cord: two lengths he condemned to death, and one length he spared. Thus Moab became subject to David and paid him tribute.

Victory over Hadadezer and Aram

3David also defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as Hadadezer was going to restore his authority at the Euphrates River. 4David captured from him 1,700 horsemen and 20,000 foot soldiers, and he hamstrung all the chariot horses, sparing only a hundred for chariots. 5When the Arameans of Damascus came to assist Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down 22,000 of them. 6David then stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became subject to David and brought him tribute. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went. 7David also took the gold shields that had belonged to Hadadezer’s officers and brought them to Jerusalem. 8From Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took a great quantity of bronze.

Tribute and Dedicated Spoils

9When King Toi of Hamath heard that David had defeated Hadadezer’s entire army, 10he sent his son Joram to King David to greet him and bless him, because David had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him. Hadadezer had been at war with Toi. Joram brought articles of silver, gold, and bronze. 11King David dedicated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had dedicated from all the nations he had subdued: 12Aram, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, Amalek, and the spoil of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah. 13David made a name for himself when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 14He placed garrisons throughout Edom, and all the Edomites became subject to David. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

David’s Reign and Officials

15Thus David reigned over all Israel, administering justice and righteousness to all his people. 16Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder. 17Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar served as priests; Seraiah was scribe. 18Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites; and David’s sons were priests.
