Woe to Those Who Trust in Egypt

1Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help, relying on horses, trusting in chariots because they are many, and in horsemen because they are very strong, yet who have not looked to the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel and have not sought the LORD. 2Yet HeheHe too is wise and will bring disaster; HeheHe will not retract HishisHis words. He will rise against the house of evildoers and against the helpers of those who work iniquity. 3Now the Egyptians are men, not God, and their horses are flesh, not spirit. When the LORD stretches out HishisHis hand, the helper will stumble, the helped will fall, and all will perish together.

The LORD Will Defend Jerusalem

4For thus says the LORD to me: "As a lion growls, and a young lion over its prey—though a band of shepherds is called out against it, it is not terrified by their shouting, nor cowed by their clamourclamourclamor—so the LORD of Hosts will come down to fight on Mount Zion and on its hill." 5Like birds hovering, so the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem; HeheHe will protect and deliver it, pass over and rescue it.

A Call to Repentance

6Return to HimhimHim from whom they have deeply revolted, O children of Israel. 7For on that day each man will reject his silver idols and his gold idols—things your hands have made for you to sin.

The Fall of Assyria

8Assyria will fall by a sword not wielded by man; a sword not of human making will consume him. He will flee from the sword, and his young men will be pressed into forced labourlabourlabor. 9"His rock will pass away in terror, and his princes will be dismayed at the battle standard," declares the LORD, whose fire is in Zion and whose furnace is in Jerusalem.
