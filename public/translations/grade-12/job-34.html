Elihu Appeals to the Wise

1Then Elihu continued and said: 2"Hear my words, you wise men; listen to me, you who have knowledge. 3For the ear tests words as the palate tastes food. 4Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us determine among ourselves what is good."

Job's Accusations and Elihu's Answer

5For Job has declared:

"I am righteous, yet God has deprived me of justice. 6Should I lie about my right? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression."

7What man is like Job, who drinks up mockery like water, 8who keeps company with evildoers and walks with wicked men? 9For he has said:

"It profits a man nothing that he should delight in God."

10Therefore listen to me, you men of understanding: Far be it from God to do wickedness, and from the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty to do wrong. 11For HeheHe repays a man according to his deeds, and makes him find according to his ways. 12Indeed, God does not act wickedly, and the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty does not pervert justice. 13Who appointed HimhimHim over the earth? Or who set HimhimHim over the whole world? 14If HeheHe set HishisHis heart upon it and gathered to HimselfhimselfHimself HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit and HishisHis breath, 15all flesh would perish together, and man would return to dust."

Job's DefenceDefenceDefense Examined

31Suppose a man says to God:

"I have endured my punishment; I will offend no more. 32Teach me what I do not see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again."

33Should God repay you on your terms, when you refuse to repent? You must decide, not I; so tell me what you know. 34Men of understanding will declare to me—and any wise man who hears me— 35that Job speaks without knowledge; his words lack insight. 36Oh, that Job were tested to the utmost, for he answers like a wicked man. 37For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God.
