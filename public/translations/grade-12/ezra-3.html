The Altar Restored amid the People’s Return

1In the seventh month, after the Israelites had settled in their towns, the people congregated in Jerusalem in unanimous accord. 2Then Jeshua son of Jozadak, together with his fellow priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, together with his relatives, rose and constructed the altar of the God of Israel, so that they might present burnt offerings upon it, as prescribed in the LawlawLaw of Moses, the man of God.

3They established the altar upon its foundations, although they remained apprehensive of the peoples of the surrounding lands, and they offered burnt offerings upon it to the LORD—burnt offerings each morning and evening. 4They also observed the FeastfeastFeast of Tabernacles, as it is written, presenting the daily burnt offerings in their prescribed number, according to the ordinance governing each day. 5Thereafter they offered the continual burnt offering, together with the offerings for the new moons and all the LORD’s consecrated appointed feasts, as well as every freewill offering presented to the LORD. 6From the first day of the seventh month they began offering burnt offerings to the LORD, although the temple of the LORD had not yet been founded.

Provisions for Rebuilding the Temple

7They also disbursed money to the masons and carpenters, and furnished food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and Tyrians, so that cedar logs might be transported from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, in accordance with the authorization granted by Cyrus king of Persia.

8In the second month of the second year after their arrival at the house of God in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the remainder of their brothers—the priests and Levites—and all who had returned from exile to Jerusalem began the work; and they appointed the Levites, from twenty years of age and older, to supervise the work of the house of the LORD. 9Jeshua, with his sons and brothers, and Kadmiel, with his sons—the descendants of Judah—together with the sons of Henadad and their sons and brothers, the Levites, stood united to direct the workmen in the house of God.

The Foundation Laid amid Joy and Sorrow

10When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, the priests stood in their vestments with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD according to the ordinance of David king of Israel. 11They sang responsively, praising and giving thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good and HishisHis loving devotion endures forever toward Israel. All the people shouted with a great shout as they praised the LORD, because the foundation of the house of the LORD had been laid.

12Yet many of the priests, Levites, and heads of ancestral houses—older men who had seen the first temple—wept aloud when this temple’s foundation was laid before their eyes, while many others shouted for joy. 13The people could not distinguish the sound of the joyful shout from the sound of the weeping, for the people shouted with a great shout, and the sound was heard from afar.
