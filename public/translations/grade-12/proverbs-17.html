Wisdom for Everyday Conduct

1Better a dry morsel eaten in tranquillitytranquillitytranquility than a house filled with sacrificial feasts and strife. 2A prudent servant will rule over a disgraceful son and share the inheritance among the brothers. 3A crucible refines silver, and a furnace gold, but the LORD examines hearts.

4An evildoer heeds deceitful lips, and a liar lends his ear to a ruinous tongue. 5Whoever mocks the poor reproaches their MakermakerMaker; whoever rejoices in calamity will not go unpunished. 6Grandchildren are the crown of the aged, and the glory of children is their fathers.

7Eloquent words ill suit a fool; how much more do lying lips misbecome a ruler. 8A bribe works like a charm in its giver's hand; wherever he turns, he prospers. 9He who covers an offenceoffenceoffense seeks love, but he who repeats a matter separates close friends. 10A rebuke penetrates deeper into a discerning man than a hundred blows into a fool.

Relationships, Justice, and Self-Control

11An evil man seeks only rebellion, so a cruel messenger will be sent against him. 12Better to encounter a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his folly. 13Whoever repays evil for good will never have evil depart from his house. 14The onset of strife resembles released water; therefore abandon contention before it erupts.

15Acquitting the guilty and condemning the innocent are alike an abomination to the LORD. 16Why should a fool have money in his hand when he has no intention of acquiring wisdom? 17A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity. 18A man lacking judgmentjudgementjudgment pledges his hand and becomes surety for his neighbourneighbourneighbor.

Speech, Temperament, and Integrity

19Whoever loves transgression loves strife; whoever builds a high gate invites destruction. 20A man of crooked heart will not prosper, and one with a perverse tongue will fall into calamity. 21He who begets a fool begets grief for himself; the father of a fool has no joy. 22A joyful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones.

23A wicked man accepts a bribe in secret to pervert the course of justice. 24Wisdom is the focus of the discerning, but the eyes of a fool wander to the ends of the earth. 25A foolish son is a grief to his father and bitterness to his mother. 26It is not good to punish the innocent, nor to flog officials for their integrity.

27A man of knowledge restrains his words, and a man of understanding maintains composure. 28Even a fool is thought wise if he keeps silent, and discerning if he restrains his lips.
