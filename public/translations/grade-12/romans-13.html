Submission to Governing Authorities

1Let every soul be subject to the governing authorities, for no authority exists except from God, and those that are constituted have been ordained by God. 2Consequently, whoever resists authority opposes the divine ordinance, and those who resist will incur judgmentjudgementjudgment against themselves.

3For rulers are not a terror to good conduct but to evil. Do you desire to be without fear of authority? Do what is good, and you will receive its approval. 4For the authority is God’s servant for your good; but if you do evil, be afraid, for it does not bear the sword in vain. It is God’s servant, an avenger executing wrath upon the evildoer.

5Therefore necessity compels submission, not merely to escape wrath but also for conscience’ sake. 6For this reason you also render tribute, for the authorities are God’s public servants, attending diligently to this very office. 7Render to all their dues: taxes to whom taxes are due, customs to whom customs are due, fear to whom fear is due, and honourhonourhonor to whom honourhonourhonor is due.
