The LORD’s Hand Is Not Too Short to Save

1Behold, the hand of the LORD is not too short to save, nor is HishisHis ear too dull to hear. 2But your iniquities have separated you from your God, and your sins have hidden HishisHis face from you, so that HeheHe will not hear.

3For your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue mutters perversity. 4No one calls for justice, and no one pleads for truth. They trust in emptiness and speak lies; they conceive trouble and give birth to iniquity. 5They hatch viper eggs and weave spiderwebs; whoever eats their eggs dies, and when one is crushed, a viper breaks out. 6Their webs will not become garments, nor will they cover themselves with their works. Their works are works of iniquity, and the act of violence is in their hands. 7Their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed innocent blood; their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity. Devastation and destruction are in their paths. 8They do not know the way of peace, and there is no justice in their paths. They have made their roads crooked; no one who walks in them knows peace.

The Nation Confesses Its Guilt

9Therefore justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us. We wait for light, but behold, darkness; for brightness, but we walk in gloom. 10Like the blind we grope along the wall; we grope as though we had no eyes. We stumble at noon as in twilight; among the powerful we are like the dead. 11We all growl like bears and moan like doves. We hope for justice, but there is none; for salvation, but it is far from us. 12For our transgressions are many before YouyouYou, and our sins testify against us. Our transgressions are with us, and we know our iniquities. 13transgressing and lying against the the LORD, departing from our God, speaking oppression and revolt, and conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood.

The LORD Rises to Bring Salvation

14Justice is turned back, and righteousness stands at a distance. Truth has stumbled in the street, and uprightness cannot enter. 15Truth is missing, and whoever turns from evil becomes prey. The LORD saw it, and it displeased HimhimHim that there was no justice. 16He saw that there was no man, and HeheHe was appalled that there was no intercessor. Therefore HishisHis own arm brought salvation, and HishisHis righteousness upheld HimhimHim. 17He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation on HishisHis head. He put on garments of vengeance for clothing, and wrapped HimselfhimselfHimself in zeal as in a cloak. 18He will repay according to their deeds: wrath to HishisHis adversaries, retribution to HishisHis enemies; to the coastlands HeheHe will repay. 19So they will fear the name of the LORD from the west, and HishisHis glory from the rising of the sun. When the enemy comes in like a flood, the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD will raise a standard against him.

The Redeemer’s Covenant with His People

20"The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from transgression," declares the LORD. 21"As for MemeMe, this is MymyMy covenant with them," says the LORD. "My SpiritspiritSpirit, which is upon you, and MymyMy words, which I have put in your mouth, will not depart from your mouth, nor from the mouths of your children, nor from the mouths of your children's children, from now on and forever," says the LORD.
