The LORD Promises to Reveal Himself

9The LORD said to Moses, "Behold, I am coming to you in a thick cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you and may also believe in you forever." Then Moses reported the people's words to the LORD.

Preparation for the LORD's Descent

10The LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow; let them wash their garments. 11Be ready by the third day, for on the third day the LORD will descend upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people. 12Set bounds around the people on every side and say, 'Take heed that you do not ascend the mountain or touch its border; whoever touches the mountain shall surely be put to death. 13No hand shall touch him, but he shall surely be stoned or shot with arrows; whether man or beast, he shall not live. The LORD Descends upon Sinai

16On the third day, when morning came, there were thunder and lightning and a thick cloud upon the mountain, and a very loud trumpet blast, so that all the people in the camp trembled. 17Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they took their stand at the foot of the mountain. 18Mount Sinai was entirely wrapped in smoke because the LORD had descended upon it in fire; its smoke ascended like the smoke of a kiln, and the whole mountain quaked violently. 19As the sound of the trumpet grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him with a voice.

20The LORD descended upon Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain; and the LORD called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.
